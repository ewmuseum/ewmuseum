In Memoriam

Sean Simon Williams

1985–2018

An avid lifelong professional wrestling fan who also loved role-playing games and card games, and stood against injustice wherever he found it. He would have loved this. the Janitor

Characters

Seymour Almasy

UWF (2001–07), NFW (2000), LOW (2002), ACW (2002–13), DWO (2001), PRIME (2003), SCCW (2005) (2), GCW (gcwonline.net), GW (Dallas, 2000–2007), PTC (2002), AWC (Atlantic Wrestling Club, 2005–2006), fWo (1998), NBW (No Brand, 2013), FWrestling.com (1998)
30 title reigns (3 as part of a tag team)
17 documented awards
Thirty title reigns under a straight-faced Final Fantasy X cosplay, World Heavyweight runs in the UWF and in ACW among them, the second ACW reign won on a 2009 return whose only stated goal, by his own card there, was to have fun, a 2003 sweep of Wrestler of the Year, Best Gimmick, and Best Mic-Worker, a masked half-year as Emoticon in which he took the PTC Infinite Gauntlet from Rich Rollins before unmasking on Global Wrestling's pay-per-view, the PTC Tag Team Championship won beside his ex-wife Aerith Heartilly as Unholy Matrimony, a 2005 arrival in GCW unbeaten in singles until Rich Rollins took their Mount Gagazet Death Match, a place in the Triangles match on the Atlantic Wrestling Club's last show, a 2011 return to PRIME, the one company he had promised never to work for, that left him its final Intense Champion, and a 2012 run as Orphan through four rounds of FWrestling.com's ULTRATITLE tournament before Castor V. Strife stopped him one match short of its final four — closing a career the Museum holds sacred and frozen exactly where Sean Simon Williams left it.

The H.I.T. — Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid

UWF (2001–07), PCW (Primetime, 2000–2004), PURE (2002), PRIME (2003), SCCW (2005) (2), PWA (Primetime, 2000–2003), PTC (2002), ACW (2002–13), RPW (RUSH Pro Wrestling, 2003–2004), Epic Wrestling (2001)
10 title reigns (1 as part of a tag team)
A geometry pun played dead straight as a hardcore wrecking machine, Sean's second UWF creation alongside Almasy, with four reigns with the Hardcore title, one of them won by pinning a table. Before UWF he wrestled in IWO, whose own news of May 2000 had him promised a World Title match. At Epic Wrestling he won the North American Championship on 23 December 2001 in a Poisoned Pyramid Deathmatch of his own devising. In 2005 PRIME saw him purge the Trapezoid to wrestle as Justin Williamson, the Deville's Disciple, and win the Tag Team Championship with Angelo Deville as Manifest Destiny.

El Terrible

XUW (2001), The X (2005), UWF (2001–07)
Wrestling as XUW's straight-faced Final Fantasy VII pastiche, finishing opponents with Fallen Wings, also billed in UWF, GW, LOW, ACW, The X, and NFW Season II.

Politically Incorrect

PCW (Primetime, 2000–2004), OSW (Easton, 2002), FSW (Five Star Wrestling, 2000–02)
3 title reigns
William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft, PCW's own Tag Team Champions and part-owners, who held the FSW Tag Team Championship from 20 October to 27 November 2002 and OSW's United States Championship, as co-holders, from 25 May to 29 June 2003.

David Walter Smith

PRIME (2003), SCCW (2005) (2), fWo (1998)
Sean Simon Williams' character best known as Logic — a technical powerhouse across fWo, OCW, XUW and PRIME, head of Logical Management. As Logic, the Thinking Man's Grappler out of Orlando, he wrestled eight PRIME matches between April and June 2008 and feuded with Mega Job. As Logic he came third in fWo's 2004 Survivor IV contest, went to its OCW breakaway, and wrestled the fWo roster through 2005, beating Jeff Garvin and challenging Deacon for the World title.

Kenjiro Ito

PRIME (2003), GCW (gcwonline.net), TEAM Wrestling (2005), ACW (2002–13), fWo (1998), IGC (2012), jOlt Wrestling (2001)
2 title reigns
Sean Simon Williams' character — the Cocky Osakan, a strong-style wrestler out of Osaka who held Jolt Wrestling's Ambassador and Triple Crown titles, came into PRIME in 2006 beside Sonny Silver and twice challenged Adam there for the Internet Championship. In 2012, billed as ACW's, he teamed with Jack Harris against INFINITE's pair in IGC's first Intergalactic Championship qualifier.

Phillip Kennedy (SCCW)

PRIME (2003), SCCW (2005) (2), GCW (gcwonline.net), NFW (2000)
2 title reigns (1 as part of a tag team)
1 documented award
Sean Simon Williams' character — the Las Vegas Lariat, Sin City's longest-reigning Gateway Champion and the last of the Dead Man's Hand, who rode the stable into PRIME's war of 2008 and turned up in New Frontier four years later to win both his matches.

Sean Williams

PURE (2002)
PURE's own self-billed owner and president, Sean Williams wrestled under his own name on his own promotion's roster, a face finishing with a modified crossface he called PURE Mourning.

Louis Bourbon

thReat (2002), UWF (2001–07)
"The Sun King" Louis Bourbon, a modern Frenchman who proclaimed himself Louis XIX, was carried to the ring in a royal carriage and finished with the Revokation of Nantes and a boot on the chest; two years later he resurfaced in the UWF without the crown, a “proud Frenchman” in Seth Silver's Uprising who beat Chris Storm with a top-rope guillotine leg drop.

SOCIETY

PCW (Primetime, 2000–2004)
Experiment #67345 of the Order of Society and Logic, SOCIETY stole the Cruiserweight Championship belt in June 2003 and declared himself its champion at six foot ten and three hundred and fifty-seven pounds.

Laura Whittle

UWF (2001–07)
"The Kindergarten Conqueress" — a submission-and-cheating heel who debuted against Chris Stevens, challenged White Wolf for the UWF Television Championship at Live and Loud 3, and put opponents to sleep with Naptime, a hanging figure-four sleeper.

Aerith Heartilly

UWF (2001–07), LOW (2002), PTC (2002), AWC (Atlantic Wrestling Club, 2005–2006), ACW (2002–13)
2 title reigns (1 as part of a tag team)
Seymour Almasy's wife and, by 2005, his ex-wife: World Tag Team Champion with him for twenty-eight days in 2003, hospitalized by Jamie Shock in a storyline that sent her husband into a grief-fueled hiatus, and PTC Tag Team Champion with him again as Unholy Matrimony in April 2005, the year she also challenged him for the ACW Television Championship and, billed Laura Winters, wrestled four singles matches for the Atlantic Wrestling Club as the Queen of Submission.

Unholy Matrimony

PTC (2002), UWF (2001–07)
1 title reign
Seymour Almasy and Aerith Heartilly's divorced-couple tag team, a pairing that had held the UWF World Tag Team Championship for four weeks in 2003 before it took the name and, in April 2005, the PTC Tag Team Championship on the judges' scores for a three-way pinata match no federation ever hosted — champions no longer a month later, when they no-showed the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean.

Ryuji Kamigawa

GCW (gcwonline.net)
Ryuji Kamigawa worked five GCW cards from 4 July 2011 to 11 October 2011, billed in the style "Japanese heavyweight with a showman's flair. Also has a notable propensity for moves executed out of a full nelson" and finishing with Kamigawa no Kachi! ("Victory for Kamigawa!"); Ten Kara ("From Heaven").

Sultan Strawberry

SCCW (2005) (2), ACW (2002–13)
Billed from the Strawberry Emirate, worked six SCCW cards in the summer of 2009, finishing with the Strawberry Panic.

Capital Punishment

ACW (2002–13)
1 title reign
A former IWO wrestler and one-time prison guard who formed the tag team Team HOSS with Angel Trinidad, successfully defending the ACW Tag Team Titles against Irish Spring.

Reginald P. Lovecraft

PCW (Primetime, 2000–2004), PRIME (2003), SCCW (2005) (2), FSW (Five Star Wrestling, 2000–02), OSW (Easton, 2002)
4 title reigns (3 as part of a tag team)
Teamed with William S. Powell across a 22-card PCW run in 2002 and as co-holder of OSW's United States Championship in 2003.

Michael Vain

GCW (gcwonline.net), SCCW (2005) (2)
Signed to GCW's own roster, Michael Vain carries no dated appearance in the federation's surviving record.

William S. Powell

PCW (Primetime, 2000–2004), FSW (Five Star Wrestling, 2000–02), RPW (RUSH Pro Wrestling, 2003–2004), OSW (Easton, 2002)
3 title reigns (3 as part of a tag team)
A Des Moines, Iowa wrestler paired with Reginald P. Lovecraft across a 21-card PCW run in 2002, and with him co-holder of OSW's United States Championship in 2003.

Ariana Al-Doleh

PTC (2002)
An Iranian Free Agent who wrestled PTC's Infinite Gauntlet in hijab, billed as both a symbol of restriction and of freedom.

Benjamin Johnson

PRIME (2003)
The Lone Star from Austin, Benjamin Johnson worked six PRIME shows between September and November 2009.

The Dark Executioners

PRIME (2003)
A tag team of two unknowns, each billed Dark Executioner, finishing with the Execution.

Dark Executioner #1

PRIME (2003)
Half of The Dark Executioners, billed from Parts Unknown with every other detail kept unknown.

Dark Executioner #2

PRIME (2003)
The other Dark Executioner, as unknown on the card as the first.

Mary-Louise Aragonne

PRIME (2003)
A cheerleading manager from New York by way of Marseilles, the first person, by the card's account, to get a PRIME job by filing a lawsuit.

Ashley Winters

RPW (RUSH Pro Wrestling, 2003–2004), PWC (Pro Wrestling Circuit, 2003)
Ashley Winters worked two RPW cards from 9 November 2003 to 16 November 2003.

Designed

A promotion this person pitched or designed themselves, held in the collection on that strength alone.

Buck Brothers Wrestling

2014
A pitch for a deliberately terrible "Z-tier indy fed" run out of a flea market in Wheeling, West Virginia — and, as far as any public record shows, nothing more than that.
