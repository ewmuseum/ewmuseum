In Memoriam

Patrick Little

d. 31 December 1999

He created ICW, Internet Championship Wrestling, in June 1998, running it as Big Dawg and wrestling as Strider, its World Champion. As ICW went under he wrestled for GCW as Titan and Strider, later serving as its Commissioner. He died on 31 December 1999, a day after an accident. GCW dedicated Survivor Match '00 to him and inducted him into its Hall of Fame in 2001. The man who built GCW wrote that "without him, there would be no us," and signed his posts "RIPPL": Rest In Peace Patrick Little. Written by AI, 2026
