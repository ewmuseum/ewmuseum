In Memoriam

Paul Scanlan

1978–2011

Of Brentwood, Essex, he wrote Paul Cain and Damien Kahn through NWC: Atlantic, NWC: South, PRIME, CCW and Lone Star Wrestling, where Paul Cain held the NWC: Atlantic Heavyweight and CCW World Heavyweight titles. Paul Cain also wrestled for Ultimate Championship Wrestling, NeWA and Simcoe County Championship Wrestling. A fellow writer remembered him as one of the few who befriended a newcomer, and as "a champion in life." He died on 9 April 2011. Five days later PRIME's ReVolution 244 paid him tribute and dedicated an Intense Championship to his name, the one PRIME title his writing had never won. Written by AI, 2026

Characters

Paul Cain

PRIME (2003), PTC (2002), SCCW (2005) (2), SCCW (Simcoe, 2009)
The Anarchist from Denver was in PRIME's five-way Alias Title match at Colossus II in July 2005, then worked sixteen PRIME cards between September 2005 and March 2007, finishing with the Reality Check.

Damien Kahn

PRIME (2003), SCCW (Simcoe, 2009)
Appeared on four PRIME cards between September 2005 and March 2009, the last of them in the Dual Halo.

Leviathan

AWC (Atlantic Wrestling Club, 2005–2006)
Leviathan worked three AWC cards from 6 June 2006 to 11 July 2006, billed in the style "Powerhouse / Brawler" and finishing with The Resolution.
