In Memoriam

RIP Sean Williams (Seymour Almasy)

17 May 2018

Recovered and served whole.

Dan — 17 May 2018
Dusty tried posting this a few days ago and for some reason it didn't allow anyone to open it, so I'm reposting for all those who knew Sean.

On April 29th Sean passed away. I don't know the cause but I have a link to his obituary that provides a little more information. http://funeralinnovations.com/obituary/256707/Sean-Williams/

Sean was only 33. If you go back through his eWrestling career, if you will, then you can see he was a brilliant writer even as a teenager. Beyond that, though, he was a great guy. He will be missed.
