In Memoriam

Jeffrey Ward

1982–2026

He wrote Angelo Deville for most of a decade: a Primetime Wrestling Academy graduate of 2001, FSW's Primetime Champion in 2002, the winner of PTC's third Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, PTC's Wrestler of the Year for 2005 and, that spring, PRIME's Tag Team Champion as Manifest Destiny beside Justin Williamson, Sean Simon Williams' character. Angelo Deville also wrestled for PCW, PWC, NFW, UWF, SCCW, GCW and Global Wrestling. He died on 11 June 2026, aged 44. Written by AI, 2026

Characters

Angelo Deville

PCW (Primetime, 2000–2004), NFW (2000), UWF (2001–07), PTC (2002), PRIME (2003), SCCW (2005) (2), PWC (Pro Wrestling Circuit, 2003), FSW (Five Star Wrestling, 2000–02), GW (Dallas, 2000–2007), PWA (Primetime, 2000–2003), GCW (gcwonline.net), RPW (RUSH Pro Wrestling, 2003–2004), WIW:NG (1999), OSF (Chicago, 2003)
4 title reigns (1 as part of a tag team)
4 documented awards
Wrestling's Cadillac out of Kemptville, Ontario, who came out to "Sympathy for the Devil" and finished men with the Soprano: a Primetime Wrestling Academy graduate of 2001, FSW's Primetime Champion through the autumn of 2002 with Mob Rule at his back, the winner of PTC's third Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and its first Universal Champion, unbeaten through three rounds of Roleplay God 4 in 2004, PTC's Wrestler of the Year for 2005, and PRIME's Tag Team Champion beside Justin Williamson as Manifest Destiny that spring. He and Rich Rollins carried PRIME into GCW's NC-17 in January 2007, losing to Steven Caldera and Seymour Almasy, and he was still on PRIME's cards in 2009.

El Cocko Steelo~!

UWF (2001–07)
El Cocko Steelo~! is named on two UWF cards from 13 February 2005 to 20 February 2005, billed with El Cocko Erecto~! as Los Cocko Doomo~!, at a combined 285 pounds.
