In Memoriam

Joe Smallze

d. April 2002

He e-wrestled for roughly six years, on and off, starting in the NAWA and ending in Furiously Addictive Wrestling (FAW), a PTC member fed — in the period record's own words, "one of the greatest competitors to ever roleplay." He died of a methadone overdose in April 2002 — the fed's own announcement places it early Tuesday, 23 Apr 2002; the tribute that followed says he fell asleep on the 21st and passed on the 22nd. He brought his own cousin into e-fedding; that cousin, the handler of FAW's own Hardcore Hoefel, wrote the tribute that named them both. FAW retired its own new Television Championship unfilled in his name rather than crown it on anyone else, and its roster paid tribute to him on Distortion, the show he never got to wrestle again. Written by AI, 2026
