In Memoriam

Tim Fitzgerald

d. 20 March 2008

The creator of WrestleCrazy Entertainment and Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, and the reason — in his own community's words — that a whole family of federations existed at all: "without Tim Fitzgerald, there would have been NO WcE, WWA, eWa, SWA, WHQ, or MWE." He appeared on his own shows as Rocky Stevens and wrestled in WHQ and, in 2007, in MWE as John Austin, but the work he was loved for happened behind the scenes: welcoming newcomers other boards had chased off, coaching writers one at a time, and posting shows week after week. He died on 20 March 2008, the day MWE closed its doors. His roster rebuilt the company in his honor and marked the first anniversary with a memorial supershow — "TF" armbands in the ring, his portrait hung beside the champions he made, an annual award in his name for the craft he loved best — and a roleplay titled In Tim We Trust, the board’s own pick for roleplay of the year in 2009. Characters

Rocky Stevens

WcE (2004), MWE (2007)
Tim Fitzgerald's own on-camera character — a true wrestler of WcE's ring and the founder's face in MWE, until the on-screen sale to Richard Covell.

John Austin

WHQ (2000), MWE (2007)
The character Tim Fitzgerald wrestled as — an MWE original of the 2007 first season, and the name he carried in the sibling ring Wrestling's Highest Quality.
