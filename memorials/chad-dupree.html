In Memoriam

Chad Dupree

11 March 1975 – 27 August 2004

A mainstay on the FW circuit, he wrote as "Mr. Main Event" Rob Sampson, ran the EUWC and co-owned GXW. In a fellow handler's own words, Chad Dupree was "a phenomenal roleplayer, a dedicated federation owner, and a good friend," one who "would stick with me and give me advice when it seemed as though nobody... was on my side." After his death, EUWC named its annual tournament the Chad Dupree Memorial Cup; by 2006, the Cup was being staged across the wider circuit. Years after he died, handlers were still posting on his birthday to raise a toast in his honor. the Janitor

Characters

Chad Dupree

Global Xtreme Wrestling (2001), EUWC (1996)
The promotion's own on-screen Chief Executive and Co-Owner across its whole run, staged brokering signings, weathering a roster boycott, and fielding the fed's rename before its 2004 closure.

"Mr. Main Event" Rob Sampson

CSWA (1988), EUWC (1996), Empire Pro Wrestling (2003), Global Xtreme Wrestling (2001)
4 title reigns
The Show Stopper finished a man in under five minutes and was billed as a message to the rest of the rookie battle royal field, and his half-year in the CSWA carried on into the Presidential Championship tournament.
