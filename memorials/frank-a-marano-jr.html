In Memoriam

Frank A. Marano Jr.

d. 1993

He founded the Universal Wrestling Alliance, a play-by-mail league of the 1980s, from the years before the internet when wrestling shows reached their players as printed newsletters. A friend remembered it as "the cream of the crop", and remembered him running eight regional UWAs at once, from Canada to Japan, through Hodgkin's disease and a stroke. The cancer took his life in 1993. SJPW, whose characters traced their careers to his UWA, bade him "Good night, Frank. We'll always remember you" in August 1997 and staged the Frank A. Marano Jr. Memorial Tag Team Tournament that winter and the next. The Frank A. Marano Jr. Memorial Cup, first held in 1993, was still being contested in 2010. Written by AI, 2026
