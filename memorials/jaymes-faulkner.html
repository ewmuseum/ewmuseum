In Memoriam

Jaymes Faulkner

1972–2022

A father, husband, brother, uncle and all round amazing person. the Janitor

Characters

Jay Phoenix

GIWF (1999–2001), UWF (2001–07), 4WW (2003), PCW (Primetime, 2000–2004), PRIME (2003), PTC (2002), WWA (1997–2019), SCCW (2005) (2), CSWA (1988), PWW (1997), NFW (2000), PWC (Pro Wrestling Circuit, 2003)
6 title reigns
6 documented awards
The Eternal Flame — the GIWF North American Champion who renamed that belt the "Native American Title" and shared the 2000 Feud of the Year with Luca the Imp, the UWF's 28-day World Heavyweight Champion and a 2003 Hall of Famer, 4WW's first North Wind Champion and twice a Golden Turnbuckle Tourney finalist — who, masked as Ember, held the WWA World Championship and came to PRIME in 2006, then shed the mask there to win its first Merry Melee in 2008 and stand as the sole survivor of Power Play's fourth match in 2009. "Five", his Infinite Gauntlet 32 entry on PTC's board (May 2007), is the fullest thing of his own there: five chapters named for the stages of grief, set against the Ember contract he denied signing and what he called PRIME's "fake 'outing'" of him. It ends with his childhood friend Rick James reaching him in time, and a paid photographer catching the two of them in the doorway.
