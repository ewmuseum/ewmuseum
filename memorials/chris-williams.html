In Memoriam

Chris Williams

d. 4 May 1999

The author of "Coldblooded" Kain Threat and a former PWW executive and Television Champion who died in an automobile accident. After his death, the PWW Chris Williams Television Championship was named in his honor. For at least eight years thereafter, the wider e-fedding community and "an ever increasing circle of friends" gathered together to pay respect to his memory with an independent tournament: the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. the Janitor
