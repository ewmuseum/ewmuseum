Steven W. Pagano

  • The Steven W. Pagano Memorial Cup of 20 April 2014, the World eWrestling Alliance's own results, opening "Steven W. Pagano, you are missed brother"
  • The Steven W. Pagano Memorial Cup's own winners, 2005 to 2014, "dedicated To The Memory Of Our Fallen Friend" (its page of 2014)
  • The World eWrestling Alliance's own rule book, section 5.03: the Steven W. Pagano Memorial Cup, "created in the memory of a great friend" (23 Apr 2014)
