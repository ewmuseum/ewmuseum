RingHype tournaments

2 February 2007

Recovered and served whole.

Subject: New announcement: RingHype tournaments

Now that GTT6 has finished I am going to point everyone - once again - over towards RingHype.

The main goal of that place is to bring different efedders and different communities together in different tournaments through the year. Tournaments such as: ----- Chris Williams Memorial Cup

May 4th 1999 Chris Williams, the handler of a character known as Colblooded Kain Threat, was killed in an automobile accident.

Each year the people whose live's he touched through efedding gather together in an ever increasing circle of friends to honour his memory with a single elimination wrestling tournament.

OOC - each year the CWMC takes on a different guise. It could be a bracketed, single elimination tournament, or it could be a three matches (battle royal, guantlet and one-on-one final). Whatever format it takes it is a pure RP tournament based around a very worthy concept! ----- CountDown

Professional wrestlers all over the World are used to physical battles. Often they are called upon to put their bodies, their careers and their very lives on the line in the name of entertainment. Tables, chairs and ladders have taken centre stage and the fans bay for blood night after night, not satisfied until they get their pound of flesh.

Now that is about to change.

Where once there was extreme, now there is excellence. Where once there was blood, now there is sweat. Where once there was carnage, now there is skill.

The best of the best will gather to prove themselves. The only way to win is with a clean pinfall or submission. Inside the ropes, and inside the time-limit. Anyone can fight, anyone can brawl ... but when the pressure is on just who can out wrestle their opponent before the sand of time runs out?

Let's find out!

OOC - the premise of this is simple. While on the surface it looks like a regular single elimination tournament it has one major difference - strict word count caps! Each round you will have to write the best RP possible and stick within re-defined limits. When all things are equal who really writes the best story? ----- Face Off

Masked wrestling is a tradition in Japan and Mexico, and now it has been brought home to the States as well! Each year masked wrestlers step foot in the ring in single elimination bouts to see who truly is the Face Off champion!

OOC - Face Off is a tournament that brings new and old handlers alike together with TWO commonalities ... they ALL have to use brand new, unique characters for this tournament and they have to remain anonymous! Not until the end of the tournament will details be released of who handled whom. ----- WoW (Women of Wrestling)

For too long they have fought in an industry dominated by men.

For one night only though, the best females around gather to compete in a single elimination tournament.

The Women of Wrestling are here!

OOC - it does what it says on the tin. Single elimination tournamentwith the only rule being that all characters HAVE to be female. ----- Iron Man

Every wrestler thinks that he is the best, that he is the toughest, but how many of them have the ability to go for a solid hour against another opponent Hell bent on beating them? Let's find out!

OOC - almost opposite to Count Down this tournament will push writers to the limits with not only minimum word counts that rise each round but also minimum RPs each round. You think that you can write? Let's find out! ----- Now, the Chris William's Memorial Cup is an (almost) annual event and it is intended to be the mainstay of RingHype, but it isn't going to run until May 2007. So I am thinking about running a 'small' tournament now to kick things off.

So, would you be interested in something like that and, if so, which of the other tournaments would you like to see first?

Please use the poll at PTC or RingHype to cast your vote.
