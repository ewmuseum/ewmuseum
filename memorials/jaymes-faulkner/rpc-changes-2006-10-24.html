RPC ch-cha-changes!

24 October 2006

Recovered and served whole.

From: RPCommunity Forums
Date: Tue, Oct 24, 2006 at 7:48 AM
Subject: New announcement: RPC ch-cha-changes!

Hi guys,

apologies for the global announcement but I wanted to be sure that you all (600+ of you) were aware of some big changes to Role Play Community (RPCommunity.com)

We have been going since 2003, and in that time RPC has been used as a hub for different aspects of 'roleplaying' such as live-action, PC/console, tabletop, creative writing and of course e-fedding. We have offered hosting for feds, RP societies, gaming groups and writing contests with e-fed tournaments ranking high on the list of 'things done' as well.

From January 2007 that will all change.

RPC will no longer be involved with wrestling or e-wrestling at all, in terms of hosting, forums, or tournaments. Instead RPCommunity.com will be 'revamped' to focus on everything that isn't e-wrestling - as already stated that means things like gaming (tabletop and live-action), RP/angle feds that are based in fantasy rather than real life (so comic book, horror, sci-fi, etc) and a bigger emphasis on creative writing with a regular short story 'fanzine' being e-published called 'The Quill'.

We are not abandoning wrestling and e-wrestling entirely, however, just the opposite! We have decided to use another site - RingHype.com - exclusively for this instead. Regular, independant, tournaments will be run - which include the Chris Wiliams Memorial Cup and Face Off and more - which I hope will bring together various feds, i-feds and indy characters alike. There will also be some hosting available for feds/characters as well.

So for all of you non-e-wrestling people I hope you continue your stay at Role Play Community and enjoy the ride next year when everything changes. For all of you ring-rats please join me over at Ring Hype as we prepare to usher in some great old and new tournaments!

Jay
