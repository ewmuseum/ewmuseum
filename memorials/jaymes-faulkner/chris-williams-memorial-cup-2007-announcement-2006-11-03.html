OK, the first tournament run by RingHype will be the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. This will start in early 2007 (January or early February at the latest).

Here is some info about the tournament:

Chris Williams Memorial Cup

May 4th 1999 Chris Williams, the handler of a character known as Colblooded Kain Threat, was killed in an automobile accident.

Each year the people whose lives he touched through efedding gather together in an ever increasing circle of friends to honour his memory with a single elimination wrestling tournament.

OOC - each year the CWMC takes on a different guise. It could be a bracketed, single elimination tournament, or it could be a three matches (battle royal, guantlet and one-on-one final). Whatever format it takes it is a pure RP tournament based around a very worthy concept!

Presently there is nothing set in stone about what format this tournament will take. The first one was a bracketed, single elimination tournament with three RPs. It wasn't done in rounds, instead the overall winner was decided on the three RPs. Subsequent tournaments saw different presentations which included four fatal fourway deciding matches leading to a final fatal fourway and the last CWMC being a 'tiple match' threat event. Three matches - battle royal, gauntlet and cage - deciding the eventual winner.

There is the possibility that the upcoming CWMC will take the form of a round-by-round elimination tournament made up of brackets. This does mean that the tournament will be spread out over a longer period (rather than the two week period as usual) but would lead to more one on one battles which is always fun to watch!

I am completely open to suggestions, though, so if you have an idea for the theme/layout of the 2007 Chris Williams Memorial Cup tournament please feel free to let me know. You never know you may just be repsonsible for deciding people's fates ;)
