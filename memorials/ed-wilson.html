In Memoriam

Ed Wilson

1976–2001

The owner of the Cyber Wrestling League — in his own community's words, "a league that visually changed fantasy wrestling." A husband as well as a doctor, he was killed in the first week of May 2001, struck by a car as he left the hospital where he worked. The news reached the hobby through fantasywrestling.com's own front page, written out of fiction by that hub's own operator — one portal stopping its presses to mourn the founder of another, and a peer hub's FAQ had already counted his league's initials among the handful every newcomer was told were too well known to reuse. Written by AI, 2026

Characters

Machina

fWo (1998), CWL (2001)
A masked Lone Gunman from the federation's first card, twice a Tag Team Champion beside Rudo Insano, whom fWo's staff carried on in the story after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: he came back for one night in November 2002 to pin Jim Dudley for the World Heavyweight Championship — in the story, Penny Pureheart under his mask, winning it for the man who had worn it — came home from an island in the August 2004 special to the Lone Gunmen, and was still in the story at the federation's 2009 return.

Rudo Insano

fWo (1998), CWL (2001)
Machina's partner in the Lone Gunmen from the federation's first summer, twice a Tag Team Champion beside him and the team's first singles champion with the Cruiserweight title in March 2000, whom fWo's staff carried on after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: in the story's later years he comes back under another name as the owner of the CWL, Penny Pureheart's husband, through the end of 2005.

Sid Snow

fWo (1998), CWL (2001)
A Lone Gunman from the federation's first card in April 1998, who wrestled for the team in its tag matches through 2000, and whom fWo's staff carried on after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: he walked out again with the CWL's men in November 2002 and was brought back together with the Lone Gunmen in the story of August 2004.

Total Eclipse

fWo (1998), CWL (2001)
A Tag Team Champion beside the Chameleon in the federation's first summer of 1998, then one of the Lone Gunmen, who wrestled for the team into 2000, and whom fWo's staff carried on after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: he came back with the CWL's men in November 2002 and returned to the ring in 2005 and 2009.

Billy James

fWo (1998)
One of the Lone Gunmen from the federation's first card, who wrestled for the team through 1998 and 1999 until, in the story, he went missing at the turn of the millennium.

Penny Pureheart

fWo (1998), CWL (2001)
A reporter who came to the federation in November 1999 to write an exposé on it and kissed Machina as the century turned, and whom fWo's staff carried on after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: in the story's November 2002 she was revealed as the one under Machina's mask, winning the World Heavyweight Championship in his memory and giving it up a week later as belonging to Erik Kelly, and in its August 2004 special she is the CWL's researcher and its owner Rudo's wife, dying there of an illness.

The Lone Gunmen

fWo (1998)
Machina and Rudo Insano's team from July 1998, joined in its matches by Sid Snow, Billy James and the Total Eclipse, twice the fWo Tag Team Champions — over Heaven's Demons in October 1998 and over Ultra Cool 2000 at Countdown '99 — and brought back together by fWo's staff after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001, in the story's August 2004 special.

Kid Cool

fWo (1998), CWL (2001)
A young wrestler who called out the fWo champion and Ice in his first appearance in July 1999 and held the Tag Team titles beside Ultra Violet as Ultra Cool 2000 and the Cruiserweight title for four days in 2000, and whom fWo's staff carried on after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: a backstage hand, the federation's webmaster, from 2002 to 2004, and the acting General Manager, then owner, who ran its 2009 return.
