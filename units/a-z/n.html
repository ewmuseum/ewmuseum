StableECW Worldwide (Nevermore's diary)
Members: Nate Hatred, Chris Hero, James Mitchell

Chris Hero, who calls himself 'The Saviour of ECW', beats Chris Jericho twice in three nights, Jerry Lynn and Sal Thomaselli, and with Punk beats Jericho and Vito on Blood, Sweat and Beers. At November To Remember XV Nate Hatred, with James Mitchell, beats Sabu in the Streak versus Career Match with a Big Splash onto barbed wire and a Decapitator Lariat and spits at Sabu's offered hand, and Chris Hero beats Chris Jericho with Hero's Welcome after a Liontamer. On the Saturday show Hero beats Tommy Dreamer in a Masters of Pain qualifier when Hatred knocks Dreamer out; on Revolution on the first Monday of December Hatred and Mitchell break up Cactus Jack's qualifier with Mark Calaway, Hatred hitting both men with Decapitator Lariats so that Cactus is ruled knocked out, and he floors both Innovators with a Double Decapitator. On the Saturday show Hero beats Eddie Guerrero with Hero's Sidekick, and Hatred beats Dreamer; Mitchell announces a handicap match against both Innovators at Got Blood?, and Hatred smashes a microphone handle into his own forehead until he bleeds.
