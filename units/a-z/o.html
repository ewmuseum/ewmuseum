The O'Malleys

Tag TeamVXW (2001)
Members: Anthony O'Malley, Marcus O'Malley
Title reigns: 2

A brother tag team who, joined for one night by a returning Justin Harper, beat The Outlaw Morris Bass and Kagedo on Xtreme Prejudice VII. Chased Kagedo off Justin Harper on Setting Sun I, then challenged for the VXW Tag Team Championship against Hot Property on Christmas Comes Early in a Tornado Tag Team match that ended in a double-pin with no official decision rendered. Lost a scored Irish Street Fight for the VXW Tag Team Championship to Hot Property on New Years Eve Of Destruction, then confronted Chris Wheeling on Winter War I and helped clear his hired men from the ring during Adam Jennings's own match. Beat Chris Wheeling's own hired hands in a No DQ Tornado match on Winter War II, then, billed as the Society of Doom alongside Adam Jennings, beat Thunder Valenkov and Hot Property on Winter War III to earn a shot at the VXW Tag Team and 24 Hour Championships at Ghost Town Blues.
