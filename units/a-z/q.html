The Quest

Tag TeamMAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)
Members: Daredevil Aero, Fearless Blue

A tour-show team of two of the June 2011 signings: the diary never prints the line-up, but the signing notes pair Daredevil Aero and Fearless Blue against the others in the XDW storyline, and Aero is the man Oscar Golden pins in the team's Tag Team title challenge at Iron on 26th August, a match the narrator calls "squashy McSquash". They lose to Team XDW on 25th June and 15th September, with the West Texas Peacekeepers to Team XDW on 1st September and in a four-team match with them to VIP Wallace and Jefferson Stardust on 18th August. The pair is released with the rest of the third brand on 1st November 2011.
