Raven's Nest

StableECW Worldwide (Nevermore's diary)
Members: Raven, Paul Burchill, Goliath, Abyss, Vito Thomaselli, CM Punk, Alexis Laree, Nattie Neidhart

Raven's own stable, announced as the 'newly re-formed Raven's Nest' on the last Blood, Sweat and Beers of February 2007, when Raven beats Sabu for the World Heavyweight Championship with Paul Burchill and Goliath's help, throws Charlie Pag and Stephanie Mcintosh through the ringside tables ('time to take out the trash') and is joined by Abyss, Vito and CM Punk. In March Raven demands a tag title shot for his 'monsters' and is refused by Shane Douglas, beats Douglas at Baptism Of Fire with Punk's help to become the Revolution commissioner, and sees Paul Burchill win the Baptism Of Fire match for the Nest. In April 2007 Raven, as commissioner, fires Owen Hart, and the Nest, with the Canadian Connection's help, unmask the masked man who has been attacking them, who turns out to be Vito, while a voice on the PA says it will not be that easy; at Cyberslam Raven keeps the World Heavyweight Championship over Rhino in the Steel Cage Match with Paul Burchill as special referee, and Burchill then turns on Raven with a C-4 and leaves the Nest: on the next Blood, Sweat and Beers Burchill and Shane Douglas, the real masked man, tell how the plan ran for seven months. In May the Nest (Raven, CM Punk, Abyss, Goliath and Vito) take Kelly Thomaselli, Vito's sister, as a hostage against Burchill, and at Hardcore Heaven Raven beats Burchill when Punk threatens to throw Kelly off the stage and then does; in June the Nest face Burchill, Shane Douglas, Team England and Davey Boy Smith. In June 2007 Raven has Paul Heyman clear the arena so that the Nest can face Paul Burchill alone and beats him down with a run of finishers, then collapses when a chanting video plays; on Revolution he admits he commissioned the videos and hijacked ecw.com himself, until the Iron Saints, out of hospital, wreck the Nest with chairs. At Wrestlepalooza Vito turns on CM Punk and the Saints leave Punk and Abyss on the girders under the stage, Burchill beats Abyss, and Raven keeps the World Heavyweight Championship over both Harts and is Chokeslammed by the mystery signing, Mark Calaway. In July Calaway and Vito beat Raven and Punk and Monsters Inc, Raven turns Vito's First Blood Match into a pinfall match, shocks Calaway and Vito with cattle prods and sends the Nest after Calaway and the Saints, a Heyman eight-man tag ends in a no contest, and at Summer Assylum the Iron Saints beat Punk and Monsters Inc, Vito powerbombing Punk through a table, before Raven keeps his title over Calaway when Rhino Gores Calaway. In August Goliath loses to Vito and Raven tells him he cannot face Vito at Heatwave. In August 2007 the Nest run in on the mass brawl and on Paul Burchill and Shane Douglas; at Heatwave Raven's cage stipulation, Monsters Inc.'s key and CM Punk's GTS on Vito Thomaselli after Burchill's top-of-the-cage Corkscrew Cross Body let Raven keep the World Heavyweight Championship, and on Blood, Sweat and Beers he reads out clause 17.2a of the contract, which voids a title change after outside interference, and uses clause 24.17e, as Commissioner, to hold Burchill's and Douglas's contracts over them. In September Raven brings in Alexis Laree, 'the cream of the CZW crop', Punk keeps the International All Action Championship, Raven hits Burchill with the Raven Effect after his wins, and Raven is booked to defend against Rhino at Anarchy Rulz. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 Nate Hatred beats Paul Burchill and Burchill is fired, Raven pins Rhino after Mark Calaway's Chokeslam and is then chokeslammed by Calaway. In October Owen Hart makes Raven tap on Revolution, Raven loses a cage match to Chris Jericho and is chokeslammed by Calaway; at Guilty As Charged Raven, as Commissioner, books his own match, pins Jericho with a Raven Effect and becomes seven-time champion, and on Blood, Sweat and Beers the company's owner returns, strips him of his powers as Commissioner, gives booking power back to Paul Heyman and books Paul Burchill against him for November To Remember XV. Alexis Laree wins the first Lioness Championship, and Nattie Neidhart joins the Nest as 'the anonymous blonde'. In November 2007 Paul Heyman, back in power, tells Raven on Revolution that nobody in the arena is comfortable seeing him as their champion, has CM Punk stripped of the International All Action Championship by security guards, and says that Paul Burchill will beat Raven at November To Remember; the whole Nest assaults Burchill on the second Revolution until Chris Jericho's chair shots and a C-4 turn it, and Raven is left alone in the ring. Alexis Laree and Nathalie work together against Jazz and Kelly Thomaselli, and on the third Blood, Sweat and Beers Heyman makes Raven's match a Loser Leaves ECW Match. At November To Remember XV Raven loses the Loser Leaves ECW Match to Paul Burchill and is out of ECW; CM Punk, Alexis Laree and the others lose their leader. On the Saturday show Punk tells Burchill, Vito Thomaselli and Shane Douglas that Raven 'ensured that I was ready to take his place as the leader of the Nest' and loses to Burchill with a C-4; the Nest's run-in is cleared by the ECW Enforcers. Punk and Goliath lose on Revolution on the first Monday of December to Burchill and Vito Thomaselli by disqualification, when Punk low-blows Vito.
