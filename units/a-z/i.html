In the diary's pages for June 2012 the narrator reports that Max Mayhem's contract has lapsed and that Hugh Lee has been re-signed for three months without a partner, so the Internet Favourites are no more.

The Iron Saints

StableECW Worldwide (Nevermore's diary)
Members: Vito Thomaselli, Salvatore Thomaselli, Brandon Thomaselli, Christopher Daniels

A stable of preaching youngsters who arrive on F' The Mainstream in August 2006 vowing to rid ECW of sinners, call out one wrestler a week for punishment, and win the First Blood Match against The Acolytes at Heatwave. In February 2007 the Iron Saints, Salvatore and Brandon Thomaselli, beat the Dudley Boys, the Blue World Order and the FBI in a row, jump the Whole F**kin' Show on Blood, Sweat and Beers and throw Traci through a table, and take the Undisputed ECW World Tag Team Championships at Living Dangerously with a Spike Powerbomb on Shawn Michaels after Rob Van Dam steps off the apron; they lose the belts at Baptism Of Fire in the first Halflife Handicap match and in April beat Paul London and Brian Kendrick. In April 2007 the Iron Saints beat the Full Blooded Italians, lose to the Canadian Connection and then to Monsters Inc with the Nest's interference, after which Raven orders Vito to hit one of his own brothers with a Vito Driver. In May they object to Vito bringing their sister Kelly into ECW, side with Paul Burchill against the Nest (Salvatore is the masked man on Blood, Sweat and Beers), beat Vito and CM Punk, beat the Nest in a six-man tag with Burchill and lose to Vito and Punk on Revolution; at Hardcore Heaven they beat Monsters Inc with steel chairs, then are chokeslammed and taken away on gurneys after Vito turns on them. On Revolution in June 2007 Brandon and Salvatore return from hospital with chairs and wreck the Nest as Paul Burchill is about to be powerbombed through tables; at Wrestlepalooza Vito turns on CM Punk and, with his brothers, beats him in the tag match Heyman gave them. In July Raven makes them wrestle each other (Brandon wins with a T-Bone Suplex and Salvatore hugs him), Mark Calaway beats Brandon with a Chokeslam in the tag match against Vito and Calaway, and at Summer Assylum the Saints beat CM Punk and Monsters Inc. In August Brandon beats Afa and Salvatore saves him from the Samoan Gangstas. In August 2007 Brandon is beaten by Nate Hatred when James Mitchell's cane intervenes, and both brothers are at ringside with Vito when Raven's Nest turn on him. In September Sal beats Chris Jericho with a Tiger Driver, Brandon is beaten by Rhino and by Paul Burchill, Sal is beaten by Burchill, and the brothers come to Burchill's aid with Vito and Kelly. In October 2007 Sal beats Jerry Lynn with a Senton Bomb, and the brothers announce 'Thomaselli Thomaselli '08', the Green Party's candidacy for president; Brandon wins the 'Presidential Primary' Match against Yoshihiro Tajiri with a T-Bone suplex, and Sal loses to Kurt Angle's Ankle Lock. In November 2007 Brandon beats Scorpio Sky with a Crossface Chickenwing and Sal beats Teddy Hart with a Senton Bomb on the first F' The Mainstream recap; the brothers lose to the Hardcore Innovators (a Double DDT on Brandon) on Blood, Sweat and Beers, Sal loses to Shawn Michaels's Sweet Chin Music, to Chris Hero, who then chokes him out, and to Andrew Martin in a parody gimmick, and Brandon loses to Nate Hatred's Decapitator Lariat and to Martin in a second parody. At November To Remember XV Vito beats CM Punk clean with a Vito Driver and walks to the ring in Paul Burchill's entourage with his sister Kelly, Steven Regal and Shane Douglas. Sal and Brandon wrestle as Thomaselli-Thomaselli '08: on Revolution on the first Monday of December they lose the Three Way Dance for the Undisputed ECW Tag Team Titles; the F' The Mainstream recap has Jushin Lyger and Rey Mysterio Jr. beat them in 9:22, and on the Saturday show Slyk and Dick Dudley beat them with a Boston Crabs on Brandon before the Original Dudleyz chair Brandon and powerbomb Sal through a table. Vito and Burchill beat CM Punk and Goliath by disqualification on Revolution, and on the Saturday show Rhino beats Vito in a number one contendership match.
