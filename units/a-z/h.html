The Hardcore Innovators

Tag TeamECW Worldwide (Nevermore's diary)
Members: Cactus Jack, Tommy Dreamer

Cactus Jack and Tommy Dreamer, who team in April 2007 after Cactus, weeping backstage, saves Dreamer from Chris Benoit with a barbed-wire 2x4, and in May win the Hardcore Heaven match against Chris Benoit and The Sandman for the number one contendership to the World Tag Team Championship, when Cactus's Double Arm DDT pins Benoit as Dreamer drives The Sandman through a DDT on the concrete. Billed as 'The Hardcore Innovators' on Revolution in June 2007, they bring Singapore canes to the ring and win in turn over the Full Blooded Italians, over a second team on Blood, Sweat and Beers and on the next Revolution, and over Mark Briscoe, each time with a Double DDT. In June 2007 the Innovators beat Balls & Axl (Dreamer's Double DDT on Axl), the bWo (Cactus's Double Arm DDT on Stevie) and Paul London and Brian Kendrick, Cactus having been Super Kicked by Stevie in the car park; at Wrestlepalooza they lose the World Tag Team Championship Match to the Whole F**kin' Show and afterwards ask Heyman for the Dudleys. In July the Dudleys beat them for the number one contendership with Big Dick Dudley Jr.'s and Daizy's help, after attacking them with chairs and a 3D through a table; at Summer Assylum the Innovators DDT the champions after the Tables Match and are floored by Ultimo Dudley II, they lose to the champions in a non-title match on Blood, Sweat and Beers, and in August they DDT the whole second generation and challenge the Dudleys for the Dudleyville Hardcore Titles at Heatwave. In August 2007 they beat the bWo (a Double DDT on Hollywood Nova) on Revolution and Big Dick Jr. and Ultimo Dudley II (a Double DDT on Ultimo Dudley II) on Blood, Sweat and Beers, and after the Dudley Boyz beat the Briscoe Boys they cane the second generation of Dudleyz, DDT Bubba Ray and D-Von and take the Dudleyville Hardcore Title belts with them. At Heatwave they win the titles from the Dudley Boyz, whom they put through a table with a Double DDT, and the Whole F**kin' Show attack them afterwards; the diary then calls the belts the ECW Hardcore Tag Team Championship, 'newly rechristened'. In September, with Beulah McGillicutty at their side, the Innovators ask the World Tag Team Champions for a Champions versus Champions match at Anarchy Rulz to crown the Undisputed Tag Team Champions, and Tommy Dreamer pins Rob Van Dam on Revolution when Shawn Michaels's Super Kick misses Dreamer and hits Van Dam. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 they lose the Champions versus Champions match by a no contest when the Dudley Family run in; Tommy Dreamer is pinned by D-Von on Blood, Sweat and Beers. In October, with Beulah McGillicutty at their side, they ask Paul Heyman for a Singapore Cane Match, Dreamer's DDT beats Ultimo Dudley II, and at Guilty As Charged Cactus Jack and Dreamer are eliminated in the Three Way Dance (Bubba Ray pins Dreamer after Michaels's Sweet Chin Music and Cactus Jack after the Whole F**kin' Show's 420 Splash through a table), losing the Dudleyville belts, but they leave with all four tag belts and beat the second generation of Dudleyz with a Double DDT the next night before taking the belts from ringside again. In November 2007 the Innovators carry all four tag belts to the ring and beat Brandon Thomaselli with a Double DDT on Blood, Sweat and Beers; Tommy Dreamer and Cactus Jack say that at November To Remember XV they will win both tag titles and leave as the first Undisputed ECW Tag Team Champions, and Paul Heyman books the match against the Dudley Boyz to unify the titles. They beat the Second Generation of Dudleyz with a Double DDT on the last Revolution before the pay-per-view, and join the eight-man brawl that ends that show with Singapore canes. At November To Remember XV the Innovators come to the ring with Singapore canes and lose to the Dudley Boyz, Tommy Dreamer taking a Dudley Death Drop through a flaming table while Cactus Jack and Beulah McGillicutty lie beaten; the Dudley Boyz raise the Undisputed ECW Tag Team Titles. Cactus Jack's promo that night accuses Nate Hatred of maiming the men who paved the way for him and warns that 'when you insult one Hardcore Innovator, you insult them both'. Dreamer loses his Masters of Pain qualifying match to Chris Hero on the Saturday show when Hatred knocks him out with a Decapitator Lariat; on Revolution on the first Monday of December Cactus Jack is ruled knocked out in his qualifier with Mark Calaway after Hatred hits both men with Decapitator Lariats, and Hatred floors both Innovators with a Double Decapitator. On the Saturday show Hatred beats Dreamer, and James Mitchell books a handicap match against both at Got Blood?; Cactus's promo calls himself 'the cripple' who 'actually enjoys that pain'.
