This repository and the site it renders hold two kinds of material, and
they carry different rights.

## 1. Archival material (the collection)

The served documents — show archives, roleplays, rosters, title
histories, and every other verbatim artifact under `promotions/` — are
the original creative work of the people who wrote them, mostly decades
ago. **The museum does not own this work and has no authority to
license it to anyone.** It is preserved and served here for reference,
research, and remembrance, the way a museum holds anything.

You may read it, cite it, and quote from it with attribution to its
original context. You may not:

- use it, in whole or in part, as training data for machine-learning or
  AI systems of any kind;
- sell it, put it behind a paywall, run advertisements against it, or
  otherwise commercially exploit it;
- redistribute it at scale stripped of its context and attribution.

The museum cannot give what it does not have — and neither can you take
it from here. Anyone with a stake in this material can have it removed
or de-identified on request, honored without argument: see the
[Takedown & Privacy Policy](CURATION.md#takedown-policy).
