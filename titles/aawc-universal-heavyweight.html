Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt the AAWC carried through 1995, and the one its own record only ever shows being defended against outsiders. "Mr. Magnificent" Tom Adler won it at WrestleFest, the night the fed's presidency was sold out from under Robert Schmid, and defended it that August at Cameron Indoor Arena in a steel cage against GUNS, with GUNS' own Enterprises World Heavyweight title also on the line — a match decided when Adler dropped down the far side of the cage while a commentator held GUNS off the floor on his shoulders. Adler left with both belts. Three months later, at Thanksgiving Weekend 1995, Hornet beat him and took the Universal title away as well. The AAWC's own Oktoberfest '96 card, thirteen months after that, names four championships and this is not one of them.

Attested by the AAWC's own card of 5 August 1995, AAWC's UNDER NEW OWNERSHIP, served in the CSWA wing (promotions/cswa/archive/aawcs-under-new-ownership-1995-08-05.html), and by the CSWA's own Unified World Heavyweight Championship history (promotions/cswa/archive/unified-world-heavyweight-championship-history-1997.html). It is named on neither of the Oktoberfest '96 card's own three title matches; whether it was still active in October 1996 is not in the record.
