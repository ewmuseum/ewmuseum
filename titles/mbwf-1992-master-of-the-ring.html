A men's belt that the Gross Gladiator took from Scourge on 30 November 1992 on a Monday Madness show, with a defence due within 14 days, and kept until the Great Tadaka pinned him at Grapple Wars on 18 June 1993.

"Master of the Ring............ Men Only" in the league's write-up of 14 December 1993.
