SEWF World Heavyweight Championship

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Vacant through the 2000 relaunch's own tournament, the World Heavyweight Championship was first won by Jay Lange on 3 September 2000. By the fed's own title histories it passed through Johnny Sledge, Ray Hagan, Jarred Matthews and four reigns of Phah Q. Dickshine, two of which ended with Dickshine handing the belt backstage to Jeff Kane and to Archangel for an hour, before Mike Masters took it in May 2001. Art Blake held it with the United States title after a sixty-minute Iron Man Hell in a Cell at Octoberfest, gave it up, and Burt Clifton won the sixteen-man tournament for it at End Game in December 2001.

No reign is registered: the held record stages no title change. See the registry's own record for the tournament's own Round 2 results and Round 3 announced pairings.
