Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened April 2006 when Alpha & Omega won a battle royal eliminating Beer King & Rockstar to become the CWE brand's own 1st World Tag Team Champions. The Adams (Adam Colt & Adam Colt II) and Japan X (Yukio Oh & Hayao Suzuki) each hold it three times across 17 recorded reigns, the rest of the field otherwise bare team names with no bio page behind them. The Double Dragons close the ledger's own account, defeating the Farm Boys to unify the CWE World Tag Team Titles with the CWF-Pro World Tag Team Titles.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 17 reigns in full; all 17 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/cwe-world-tag-team-title.html for the underlying primary source.
