Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

One of a matched pair of regional belts, defended against any challenger of the champion's own choosing who stood inside the top tenth of the rankings. Pain held it into the 1992 hiatus. Carried back from the WeWF wing, whose own newsdesk This Week in Wrestling printed this federation's weekly title results and full rankings through the winter of 1992. The belt is VACANT in the newsdesk's own standings of 15 February 1992 — Admiral Ax had left it to take the Intercontinental Championship. Biff “the Terror” Jones fills the vacancy on 19 February (“Biff ‘the Terror’ Jones eeked a pin out over Mosh to become the new Atlantic Champion”) and Pain takes it off him on 25 February. A whole reign and a vacancy stood between this ledger's own first two reigns and are now registered between them.

Declared in the federation's own founding document, 19 November 1991 (promotions/uewf/archive/new-pbem-the-uewf-1991-11-19.html), which sets out how it was to be defended. Holder transcribed from the federation's own relaunch call, 14 May 1992. The federation's own World Championship semi-final of 28 December 1991 was announced in advance as crowning a champion either way — “the winner to move on to the finals of this tournament and the loser to be named the UEWF's first Atlantic Champion” — and Admiral Ax lost it. Pain's reign is renumbered r002 accordingly; how the belt passed from one to the other is not something any held document says. The weekly record survives second-hand, in another federation's own newsdesk, not yet served here. Nine editions of the WeWF's own This Week in Wrestling and its TTTTTT tag tournament, 30 January to 31 March 1992, plus the EMWA's own Kings Of The Ring of 25 March, are held in this project's own Google Groups dumps and are unserved. Between them they carry this federation's own weekly title results week by week, its full ranking tables with win-loss records and points, and two of its own matches staged play-by-play. What they say about THIS belt: The chain between the two reigns on this ledger is partly recoverable and is not one step. The table of 15 February 1992 reads "Atlantic Champion: vacant"; the same broadcast has Mosh promising to take the belt from Biff "the Terror" Jones "next week"; the roundup of 19 February has "Biff 'the Terror' Jones eeked a pin out over Mosh to become the new Atlantic Champion"; and the roundup of 25 February has "Pain succeeded in dethroning new Atlantic Champion Biff 'The Terror' Jone[s]." So: Admiral Ax (28 December 1991, r001 here) … vacancy … Biff "the Terror" Jones (c. 19 February 1992) … Pain (c. 25 February 1992, r002 here). At least one whole reign and one vacancy sit between the two rows this ledger carries.
