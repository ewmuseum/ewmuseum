4WW North Wind Championship

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

4 Winds Wrestling's own top singles belt from its first night. A gauntlet on the opening StormFront of July 2003 crowned Jay Phoenix as its first champion; the belt then changed hands four times in eight months, through Syxx Figures, Jaime, Todd Dream and Patti Anderson, and every one of those changes turned on outside interference the fed staged deliberately — a coal miner's glove, a run-in, a stablemate with a title belt in his hands, and finally a guest referee counting three.

The awards site's own Hall of Fame ballot names "Jay Phoenix (First Ever NWT Champion)" and "Todd Dream (Longest Ever NWT Reign)"; the reign sequence below is built from the fed's own show scripts and the fed's own Official 4WW Title History thread (promotions/4ww/archive/forum-official-4ww-title-history.html).
