LCW Mercury Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   LCW Mercury (2001)
Active   2001–2002

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Founded on the belts stripped from Evo's own Dean Wallace and Frank Heimdal in the split that made Mercury, and put up in a first-Fusion match the Trent Brothers won. The titles changed hands eight times across the promotion's own run — twice on Sloan's own turns, twice inside EEI's own shifting membership, once when Beaumont simply reassigned a vacated half without a match — before settling on Vincent Picasso and Johnny Glamour as the Elite, still champions when the record runs out.

Already the recognized champions by the 29 March 2002 Fusion and by the federation's own champions.html (captured 16 April 2002: "Tag Team Champions: EEI (currently Picasso & Dirge)"); the exact date of the reassignment is not stated and is bounded only between the 6 February 2002 title change and 29 March 2002 — recorded here as a presumed midpoint, not a fact.
Retained against The Trents and The Heat Seekers in a triple threat on 29 March 2002 and against the same two teams again on 5 April 2002.
The Elite (Vincent Picasso, Johnny Glamour)19 April 2002—Dirge relinquished his own half of the tag titles to Picasso on air on 12 April 2002 ("I give up my half of the tag team titles, and the chapter of our lives known as EEI...is closed"), dissolving EEI; Johnny Glamour returned the following week and resumed as Picasso's own tag partner, reforming as the Elite. The federation's own brief-history recap dates the return to 19 April 2002; that show's own results page was never captured.
Still champions when the surviving record runs out: retained against The Trents in a Salt Lake City Streetfight at Suicide (5 May 2002) and billed against Rush & Killjoy on the 10 June Climax-predictions thread's own card.
