Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's North Beach Championship: 11 reigns are served here, across 10 different champions, the first beginning on 20 March 2011 with Alioth Starre and the last on 25 August 2019 with Agustin Gates. By the dates served here the longest is Michael Lynch's, 257 days from 23 June 2013, which the fed's own table counts as 54 days; of the reigns whose dates fit the table's own lengths, the longest is Alex Castellanos's, 59 days from 9 June 2011. Of these, 3 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). It carries the fed's own North Beach table from 2011; the Affliction and NOX belts before it are listed beside it. It was unified into the Undisputed Championship on 7 March 2014 and crowned a new champion in August 2019.
