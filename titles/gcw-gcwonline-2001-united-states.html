Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GCW's United States Championship was first won by Thirteen on 22 April 2001. The fed's own record carries 39 reigns on it, across 33 different champions. The longest of known length is Karina Wolfenden's, 314 days from 20 November 2010. Two of its reigns ended outside a match (vacated, forfeited). Aimz held it at the end of the surviving record.
