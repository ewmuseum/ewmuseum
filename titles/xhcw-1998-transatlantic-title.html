Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Pico Flush lost it to Adam Simon in a Texas Death Match at Retribution, 7 January 2002. Simon lost it to Brad Jones in a three-way Falls Count Anywhere Match at the Silverdome, 2 February 2002. Jones lost or vacated the title in a match no surviving document records; by Affliction, 28 April 2002, it stood vacant for an eight-man tournament, which Vincent Lyons won by beating Fear in the final. Lyons lost the title in turn, undocumented, to a radically changed Ramsus Palantiri, who dropped it to the mat himself at the opening of Hardcore Harvest, 24 October 2002, declaring it beneath him. No document shows anyone taking it up afterward.
