Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt whose whole recorded lineage runs across four months. Sonny Williams beat Vega Knight for it on 12 December 2008, on the final edition of Friday Night Genesis. Zak Warner took it from him on the 26 January 2009 Monday Night Mayhem, and Paige beat Warner for it at Highway to Hell on 29 March 2009. The ledger then listed it vacant, to be decided at Tribulation — and at Tribulation on 24 May 2009 it was: Sinc Mercier won a Scavenger Hunt match to unify it with the United States Championship under his own name, and this title's own reign ledger ends there. See the United States Championship's own epitaph for the unified lineage onward.
