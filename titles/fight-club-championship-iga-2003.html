International Grappling Association IGA Fight Club Championship

aka IGA Fight Club Title

Placard

Promotion   International Grappling Association (2001)
Active   2001–2002

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A bronze belt on a red strap, defended anywhere but the arena: parking lots, bars and locker rooms. Malkav won it at the Last Chance Bar & Grille on Devil's Night, 30 October 2001.
