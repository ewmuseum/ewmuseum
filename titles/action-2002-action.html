Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Action!'s Action Championship was first won by Chris Fear on 20 March 2002. The fed's own record carries 9 reigns on it, across 8 different champions. The longest is Jeff Garvin's, 143 days from 31 August 2003. One of its reigns ended outside a match — stripped from the holder; another changed hands off-page, by the fed's own owner's award rather than a match. Phil Atken held it at the end of the surviving record, his own status left open twice on the fed's own last show.

Pre-history residue, 2002-03-31 ('Resentment' special): see action-001's note -- an overturned title-change finish, not a reign, with no title-log row. Two mechanical conflicts on this belt are resolved by legacy log rows 2538/2539 (action-007/action-008); see their notes. Jeff Garvin's second reign ends off-page: Reed Young hands the belt to Phil Atken on Pressure Point 90 (2004-01-21), no strip shown; Atken's own reign is filed open to the fed's own record's end (Pressure Point 92, 2004-04-07, leaves his status 'unclear' twice, and the fed's own last word follows 9 July 2004) rather than given an invented loss.
