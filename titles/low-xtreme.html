LOW Xtreme Championship

aka LOW Extreme Championship, LOW X-Treme Championship, Xtreme Title

Placard

Promotion   LOW (2002)
Active   2003–2004

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt that carried the fed's own tagline — Lights Out Wrestling billed itself "Xtreme Has Returned" and named a championship after the word. Annihilation, Reality/Mayhem, Iron Lung, Angelus Sorrow, Psymon, Juvian Ramorez, Dark Haze and Eric Russo all held it across three years. Iron Lung defended it on Realm 48 in a thirty-count Warrior Match and lost it three weeks later to Angelus Sorrow in an Ultimate Taipei Match with broken glass glued to the ropes; Ramorez took it off Psymon at CATASTROPHIC in a two-out-of-three-falls match the fed's own commentary called stolen.
