Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PRIME's Global Title was first won by Alex Caine on 6 July 2003. The fed's own record carries 10 reigns on it, across 8 different champions. The longest is Ignatius Lisieux's, 152 days from 23 February 2004. Two of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. PRIME renamed it the Universal Title on 26 September 2004.

PRIME's own Title Histories page, its 'GLOBAL TITLE (DEFUNCT)' section: ten numbered reigns, 6 July 2003 – 26 September 2004; the Universal section continues its numbering from '1. (11.)'.
