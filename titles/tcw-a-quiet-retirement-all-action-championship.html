Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt the narrator wants to turn into a test of a rising wrestler, with the weight limit removed. Sammy Bach holds it when the diary opens and loses it at Total Mayhem 13 to the Syndicate's Edd Stone. Edd Stone defends it at Excessive Force in June 2008 in the Hangtime Match of his own devising, against Sammy Bach and Acid, and keeps it. Edd Stone keeps it through July 2008, beating Hell Monkey at Summer Showdown with the Party's Over; Hell Monkey won the shot in a three-way ladder match on Badge of Honor. Edd Stone keeps it through August 2008: Art Reed, the fans' choice in a web poll, beats him at Hotter Than Hell only by count out, so the title stays with Stone. Edd Stone keeps it through September 2008: he beats the Cannonball Kid on Badge of Honor, when the Kid tries a powerbomb, and Art Reed in the rematch at Destructive Energy. Edd Stone loses it at Crunch Time in October 2008: Frankie Perez, who won the number one contendership in a three-way match on Badge of Honor, beats him with the P-Clutch after a mule kick to the head, taking his first TCW gold. Frankie Perez keeps it in his first month with it: at A Little South Of Sanity, after entrants chosen on Badge of Honor, he beats Art Reed, Greg Keith and Freddy Huggins in a four-way match, Greg Keith tapping to the P-Clutch. Frankie Perez keeps it through December 2008, beating Hell Monkey, who wins the ladder match for the contract, with the P-Clutch at Psycho Circus after Perez has abandoned Chris Rockwell and allied with Remo Richardson and Phil Vibert.
