EUWC International Tag Team Championship

aka EUWC Unified International Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   EUWC (1996)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Killers Inc retained it at Ultra Brawl in August 1996 over the Missouri Connection, then apparently lost it — unrecorded — to Badd Company before that October’s Blood Bath, where Badd Company retained over a team the card spells "Kiillers Incorprated." At Ultra Brawl II the following August the Midnight Massacre won it from Cause & Effect in a "Unification Match," becoming, per the card’s own words, the "Unified International Tag Team Champions."

Every reign recovered from the Ultra Brawl and Bloodbath/Blood Bath event-history pages; no dedicated title-history ledger page survives for this belt. Only reigns with a recorded WIN are structured (Killers Inc’s and Badd Company’s "retain" defenses are recorded as matches, not reign rows, since no won-date survives for either).
