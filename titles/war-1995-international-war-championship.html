WAR! International WAR! Championship

Placard

Promotion   WAR! (1995)
Active   1995–1997

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WAR!'s original secondary belt, crowned in an inaugural tournament won by Bludgeon days after the promotion's own launch and passed through Johnny Thunder and Butch Williams before Jerry "Goon" Gordon's October 1995 win made him, per the show's own text, the first wrestler to hold two WAR! titles at once; Booster Dunne, Williams again, and "Magnum" Biff McGriff followed across 1996, McGriff winning it off a slow three-count the show's own text calls "tainted... but a victory nonetheless."
