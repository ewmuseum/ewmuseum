Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt of the CHZ's June 2002 relaunch under its new owner, listed on the fed's title page with no champions and on its champions page as vacant, the contenders to be announced. Jake Schultz was crowned "the first CHZ World Heavyweight Champion" at Mind Games on 31 August 2002, powerbombing Matt Hunt through a table in a barbed wire tables match, and lost it when the fed's new owner stripped every champion on 11 September 2002.

Named on the fed's own 2002 champions and title-history pages (/promotions/chz/archive/champions-2002-06.html; /promotions/chz/archive/title-history-2002.html) and billed "the first CHZ World Heavyweight Champion" on its 11 September 2002 front page (/promotions/chz/archive/front-page-2002-09.html). The 2003 World title is filed on chz-heavyweight (see its notes).
