EUWC European Championship

Placard

Promotion   EUWC (1996)
Active   2004–2008
Unified into   EUWC International Championship 9 July 2008 — Cobris Grayson, the reigning European Champion, won this four-way Unification Match at Blood Bath to become International Champion, folding the European title he already held into the International title he won in the same match.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EUWC European Championship's own ledger opens in July 2004 with Jack "The Ripper," whose two reigns both came against opponents billed from a partner promotion, RBW — one of the clearest surviving traces of EUWC's own cross-federation booking. The Monster, Jacob Jacobs, Apocalypse, T. Waring and the Odinist cycled the belt through RBW-billed defenses before the Odinist's own retirement, when the RBW itself closed, vacated it in August 2005; T. Waring's second reign, the Monster's second, and John Doe carried it into the fed's short fifth-era hiatus around Blood Bath 2005. Chris Comet's 460-day reign, the longest recorded on the belt, ran from June 2006 to Ultra Brawl IX in September 2007, when Cobris Grayson won it. Grayson's own reign carried the belt to its own unification, into the EUWC International Championship (his own second title), in the Unification Match at Blood Bath, 9 July 2008.
