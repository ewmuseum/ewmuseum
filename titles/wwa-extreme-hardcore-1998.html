Promotion   WWA (1997–2019)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A short-lived 1998 title, crowned via a dedicated tournament (semifinals wwa-1998-12-26, final wwa-1998-12-27) and won outright by New Star. Not confirmed to be the same lineage as the modern wwa-extreme title (whose own epitaph traces its recovered ledger continuously only from 2003's "GenX" billing, with no mention of an earlier belt) — filed as a distinct, likely-discontinued early incarnation rather than merged, absent evidence tying the two.
