Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

FAW's top belt, and the one the fed kept through every rebuild it went through. Its first champion, HaHaKlown, is recorded as never having lost it — he handed it to his own sidekick Deadman and became president instead. Pyro took it at Breakaway 2001, American Psycho took it from Pyro, and from there it moved almost monthly: Spider, Spawn, Jim ‘JLC’ Phoenix, Skipster, The Omega Fro twice, The British Bomber, Chris Thunder for nine days in January 2002, Tony Vigilante, Rzarector through the summer of 2003, and Violator from Deceit on 9 November 2003. The fed's own paperwork survives in snapshots rather than as a ledger, so the order above is what its champions pages and its Hall of Fame between them attest, not a continuous chain.

A separate, earlier champions snapshot of 8 April 2002 (promotions/faw/archive/faw-champions-april-2002.html) names The Omega Fro as World Champion, International Champion EDH, European Champion Shifty, Hardcore Champion Xander, and Tag Team Champions Foam World Order (Neo & 9th Wonder Of Foam); all five corroborate reigns already recorded as open-ended at that date and do not themselves change any recorded date.
