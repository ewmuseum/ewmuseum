GIW's EPW Evolve Championship: 2 reigns are served here, across 2 different champions, the first beginning on 5 October 2010 with Christopher Darren Erns and the last on 19 October 2010 with Chris Armstrong. The longest is Chris Armstrong's, from 19 October 2010 to about 11 November 2010 (24 days in the fed's own history); of the reigns with both dates exact, the longest is Christopher Darren Erns's, 14 days from 5 October 2010. Of these, 1 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). It took this name on 31 August 2010 and became the GIW Evolve Championship on 11 November 2010.
