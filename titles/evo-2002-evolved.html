Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

eVo's eVolved Championship was first won by Seara on 15 January 2003. The fed's own record carries 8 reigns on it, across 8 different champions. The longest is Scott Evans's, 98 days from 29 June 2003. One of its reigns ended outside a match — retired with the belt. eVo unified it into the United Championship on 10 December 2003.

ReignWonLostDetail
Seara15 January 200330 March 2003
Seara countered Sean Mason's powerbomb into the Last Caress and pinned him, unifying the High Impact and Rising Star titles as the eVolved Championship with herself as its first champion. Defences on record. New Year's eVolution 2003, 26 January 2003: Seara beat Xander in a table match by pushing a ladder over so that Xander's face went through the table, to retain the eVolved Championship. Legends XXV, 12 February 2003: Seara retained the eVolved Championship by pinning Lee eXodus with the Last Caress through a table in a San Jose Street Fight. Tainted Love 2003, 23 February 2003: Seara retained the eVolved Championship over Xander in a two out of three falls match, escaping over the top in the deciding cage fall as the cage door was slammed on Xander.
Xavier Citizen30 March 200325 May 2003
Xavier Citizen made Seara tap out to the Questionable Sanity to win the eVolved Championship. Defences on record. Legends XXXI, 3 April 2003: The Syndicate beat the Omen Brotherhood by count-out in a match billed Non-Title, and the ring announcer ruled that Xavier Citizen, billed Malcolm Luther Xavier, kept the eVolved Championship since it cannot change on a disqualification. Legends XXXIV, 23 April 2003: Malcolm Luther Xavier (Xavier Citizen) retained the eVolved Championship by pinning Hedges with a school boy after Fiona Preston and Xero interfered. End of Days 2003, 27 April 2003: Malcolm Luther Xavier (Xavier Citizen) retained the eVolved Championship by pinning Fiona Preston in a roll-up with his feet on the middle rope. Legends XXXV, 7 May 2003: Malcolm Luther Xavier (Xavier Citizen) beat Fiona Preston in a Glass Coffin match by putting her through the coffin, to retain the eVolved Championship. Legends XXXVII, 21 May 2003: Malcolm Luther Xavier (Xavier Citizen) retained the eVolved Championship in a triple threat with a Standing STF and dragon sleeper combination on Seara, who passed out as the referee's hand fell.
Hedges25 May 20035 June 2003
Hedges pulled the eVolved Championship down in a four-way ladder match after Morpheus Omega drove a truck into the ring and knocked Malcolm Luther Xavier off the ladder. The fed's own title history names the loser as Malcolm Luther Xavier, who is Xavier Citizen in the write-up, not Luther Wisdom.
Hedges and Luther Wisdom5 June 200321 June 2003
The same match's third fall made Hedges and Malcolm Luther Xavier (Luther Wisdom) co-eVolved Champions. A defence is on record. Legends XL, 18 June 2003: Jason Jameson beat Thug roC with Perfection, Malcolm King refereeing, and Luther Wisdom and Hedges remained eVolved and Tag Team Champions. The write-up bills Luther Wisdom as Malcolm Luther Xavier through this match; the fed's own title history names Luther Wisdom.
Eric Knight21 June 200329 June 2003
Eric Knight won the eVolved Championship from the co-champions by pinning Luther Wisdom with the same rolling cradle, ending the co-championship.
Scott Evans29 June 20035 October 2003
Scott Evans won the eVolved Championship when Eric Knight tapped out, after Hedges had attacked Knight before the bell. Defences on record. eVo1: One Year Anniversary Show, 3 July 2003: Scott Evans kept the eVolved Championship in a triple threat table match with Hedges and Drake Nathaniel Dukes by putting Hedges through the tables with a german suplex. Legends XLIII, 16 July 2003: Scott Evans pinned Eric Knight with a frog splash off the stage to retain the eVolved Championship in a Falls Count Anywhere match. ReVelations 2003, 27 July 2003: Scott Evans retained the eVolved Championship by locking the Death Trap on Hedges until the referee stopped it, in a No Holds Barred match.
Aaron Justice5 October 20039 November 2003
Aaron Justice covered a bloodied Matt Hunt to win the eVolved Championship in a triple threat, after chair shots from Rimian and distractions by Tok'n and QBX; champion Scott Evans was not pinned. A defence is on record. Legends LIV, 22 October 2003: Aaron Justice pinned Scott Evans with The Legacy after Shane Wethers hit Evans with a chair, to retain the eVolved Championship.
Matt Hunt9 November 200310 December 2003
Sources disagree — The fed's own title-history table and page carry no line for this match; the fed's own pages for the eVolved, United and retired titles carry the unification. Matt Hunt made Aaron Justice tap out to The Package sharpshooter in a steel cage match to win the eVolved Championship. The eVolved Championship ends when Matt Hunt, its champion, beats Thug roC for the United Championship and the two titles are unified.
