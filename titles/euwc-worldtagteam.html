EUWC World Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   EUWC (1996)
Active   1996–2008

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EUWC World Tag Team Championship's own ledger opens with the Specialists (Nate Augustine & Roger Thorpe) on 28 July 1996, the same Summer Sizzler card that crowned the fed's first World Heavyweight Champion, and changed hands more than a dozen times across the founding two years — the Beantown Bruisers, the Outsiders, the Black Panthers (twice each), the Surf Patrol, the Glamour Boys (twice), the Hellraisers, Team PSD and Harlem Connection among the teams — with three separate vacancies along the way (a controversial finish, the Outsiders' own contract lapsing, the Black Panthers' retirement) before Harlem Connection's reign carried it into EUWC's first hiatus in April 1998. Team PSD's own second reign reopened it in October 1999, and a run of ad hoc pairings and named teams — the Salvation, the Dark Angels, the Kool Klub, American Mafia, the Blood Brothers, Alliance of Violence, the Kings, Extreme Lightning and the Wannabes among them — carried it through EUWC's second era into its second hiatus. New Pimps on the Block reopened it in September 2001; Diabolic Forces, the Enders, the Motley Crew and a returning American Mafia carried it through the third era before a third hiatus. Nero & Big G (billed under Lynch Garrisson's own tag-team nickname "Big G") won it three separate times across the fourth era, trading it with Scott Samson & T-Charger, the Action Faction, Buck Naked & Samuel Roundtree, the Dream Team (twice), Shock Therapy, the Dominion (twice), U.S. Express, the Rebellion and Diabolic Forces, before Team Danger's own reign absorbed a fourth hiatus (240 days deducted).
