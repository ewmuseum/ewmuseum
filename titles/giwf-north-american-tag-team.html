Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Added September 1999, briefly disbanded after The Dark Lords' own reign and revived by tournament seven months later. The Phenomenons held it three times, the second a 112-day reign, the belt's own longest; Street Science's own win over the Phenomenons and the Bringers of Darkness, 4 July 2001, is the fed's own last recorded change of this title.
