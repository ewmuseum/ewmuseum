XHCW World Tag Team Championship

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Jolted and Frost lost it to John Sebastion and Scotty Fitzpatrick in a Double Dog Collar Match at Retribution, 7 January 2002, after the promotion's own president made the match a title match on the spot; Sebastion and Fitzpatrick kept it once more by simply leaving the arena with the belts. Adam Simon and 2Short, billed as "Short and Long," won it from them in a Weapons Match at Counterstrike, 9 February 2002, and never defended it again on any surviving card; it stood vacant by Hardcore Harvest, where Moose and Anthony Dolarhyde won a four-way dance for it, 24 October 2002, with their manager Paul Wade's own interference.
