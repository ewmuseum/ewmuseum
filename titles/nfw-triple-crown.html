Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The last singles championship NFW made, and it was made by consolidation rather than by tournament: President Eddie Mayfield ordered the Pure, National and Elite Championships merged into one belt at Supercrash IV in January 2012, and Rook Black won it at Reloaded 11 that April by pinning Deacon in a three-way that also had the reigning Pure Champion in it. He carried it through the promotion's last touring year — Portland, the Gorge, Sydney, Helsinki — turning back the Mighty Impala, Akita and Leyenda de Ocho, and was still holding it at the last card the archive keeps.

Created by presidential order inside the fed's own fiction, announced at Supercrash IV and first awarded at Reloaded 11; the fed's own championship ledger records it only as the name the National Championship became.
