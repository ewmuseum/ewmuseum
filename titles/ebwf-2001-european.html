Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Title History opens this belt with “Started W/The Rock” on Smackdown, 18 March 2004 — the same night it retires the Multicontinental US Championship with The Rock holding that one too. Twenty-three reigns follow in two and a half years: Mark Jindrak, Booker T, Ric Flair, Petey Williams, Shane Helms and Kane each take it twice. At the Destiny Pay-Per-View on 24 September 2006 the promotion renamed it the Iron Man Championship, on the same night Kane won it, and his reign carries into the new name's record.

Source: the promotion's own Title History page (/promotions/ebwf-2001/archive/titlehistory.html). The Title History writes this belt's own name only as “European Title”, in the rename entry. Whether the Multicontinental US lineage passed into this belt on 18 March 2004, or simply ended as this one began, is not stated anywhere read.
