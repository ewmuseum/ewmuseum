Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Rhett Locke won OSW's first International Championship at Crowning Glory, the fed's debut PPV, pinning Drew Hostyle on 10 March 2002, and defended it against Tom Walczak and Corg before Propaganda beat him for it at Slam! 14 on 4 May 2002. Propaganda was defending it through June and July until 'Angeldust' Nick Casey won a four-way over him, Lilith and Vampir Nosferatu at Slam! 26 on 27 July 2002. Casey beat Propaganda to keep the belt at By Any Means Necessary on 1 September and held it until 7 September, when the Jane Says segment at Slam! 32 announced Frankie Bones as the new champion; Bones held it at the end of the surviving record. The fed's results archive index tells the July stretch differently, listing Tom Walczak and Rhett Locke as champions with no Casey reign, but the fed's own write-ups of those two cards read both bouts as World Heavyweight title matches and have Propaganda defending the International on both nights.
