WWA World Heavyweight Championship — WWA (1997–2019)

aka WWA Championship, CWC Northeast Heavyweight Championship, CWC Northeastern Championship, Northeast Championship

Placard

Promotion   WWA (1997–2019)
Active   1997–2010

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned at WWA's own 12/12/97 founding tournament, when Mark Lynch outlasted the field to become the fed's first champion — a tournament the served card itself bills throughout as "the CWC Northeast Heavyweight Championship," WWA's own later title-history ledger folding it into "WWA Championship" retroactively from this exact reign. From Lynch's own coronation the belt changed hands dozens of times across WWA's continuous run, carried by four-time champion Tracer Bullet, two-time champions Damian Thorne, Craven Moore, and Armaan, and a seven-month reign for Ian Tense rivaled only by Nik Segier's own early run. The fed's own last recovered capture (September 2010) leaves Trendkiller as champion, having just outlasted RJ Stone, Jack Griffiths, Chris Bagwell, and Jaymz Watkins inside the Ultimate Glory match.
