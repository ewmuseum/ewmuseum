Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Declared with the federation itself and defended, by its own rules, against the highest-ranked woman who had not had a shot that month. The division was not run again after the spring of 1992 — the president's own relaunch call says so outright. Its first champion was crowned in a ladies' battle royal on the second night of the Summit of E-Wrestling, 28 December 1991: eight women, elimination over the top rope only, and Laurie Liberty, who drew last, threw the Angel of Death out to win it. Carried back from the WeWF wing, whose own newsdesk This Week in Wrestling printed this federation's weekly title results and full rankings through the winter of 1992. Laurie Liberty is champion at 5-0-0 on 30 January and 6-0-0 on 15 February 1992. On 19 February the newsdesk rolls the finish as tape: Sarah Jackson heaves her over the top rope, Rhonda Rhodes gets in cheapshots on the outside, and “JAckson covers......ONE!....TWO.....THREEEE!!!! We've got a new champion”. Jackson keeps it on 25 February when Rhodes interferes again. The row that said “the reign is left open” is corrected.

Declared in the federation's own founding document, 19 November 1991 (promotions/uewf/archive/new-pbem-the-uewf-1991-11-19.html), which sets out how it was to be defended. The discontinuation is stated in the federation's own relaunch call, 14 May 1992: "I will not be running a ladies division." Eight women, two starting and six entering at one-minute intervals, elimination over the top rope only; Laurie Liberty drew last and won it. The weekly record survives second-hand, in another federation's own newsdesk, not yet served here. Nine editions of the WeWF's own This Week in Wrestling and its TTTTTT tag tournament, 30 January to 31 March 1992, plus the EMWA's own Kings Of The Ring of 25 March, are held in this project's own Google Groups dumps and are unserved. Between them they carry this federation's own weekly title results week by week, its full ranking tables with win-loss records and points, and two of its own matches staged play-by-play. What they say about THIS belt: This reign ended, and the held record both dates and stages it. The roundup of 19 February 1992 cuts to a clip of the ladies title match and closes it on "JAckson covers......ONE!....TWO.....THREEEE!!!! We've got a new champion"; the roundup of 25 February has "Sarah Jackson kept her Women's belt, when Rhonda Rhodes interfered saving Ms. Jackson from a pinfall at the hands of Laurie Liberty." Sarah Jackson — one of the eight women of the battle royal this belt's first reign came out of — took it from Laurie Liberty in the third week of February 1992 and was still holding it a week later. No reign is minted from an unserved document; the row is corrected instead.
