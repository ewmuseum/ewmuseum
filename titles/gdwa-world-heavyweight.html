Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The top belt of the line, and the one the fed dated its own founding by: its ledger opens with a tournament final on 5 April 1996, held under the two federations GDWA grew out of, and then records the same wrestler being inaugurated GDWA World Champion at the same building on 21 April. Seven reigns follow it across two and a half years — one ended by a stripping for breach of contract, one won in a three way dance decided by a pinfall and a disqualification at once, one settled by a tournament final in the Fukuoka Dome. The last change the fed recorded put it on Black Lotus at Founder's Day Tradition II in August 1998. Its own site announced in January 2000 that a pay per view called Glory Days would crown a new and undisputed champion; nothing held says it did.

Transcribed from the fed's own Lineages pages (promotions/gdwa/archive/lineages-current.html, promotions/gdwa/archive/lineages-1997.html); the Glory Days announcement is the scrolling marquee on its own Dragon's Lair page (promotions/gdwa/archive/dragons-lair.html).
