Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Cellrock was the first CHZ Intercontinental Champion, beating Triple D in a Last Man Standing match at Caged Fury 1 on 1 August 1999 and winning fifteen straight before the CHZ closed. The belt ran on through the FWF and SWF years — Kryptosonic, who retired undefeated; JT Fringe and Jaguar-F trading it in February 2000; Staz ending Jaguar-F's career in a Loser Leaves Town cage match. In the New CHZ, Black Dragon, Don Rossi and Merlin held it through 2001; Chazzy 2 Cool took it from Merlin on 12 December 2001, Brent Brewer from Chazzy 2 Cool on 9 January 2002, and Kaanen Thorne from Brewer on 18 March 2002. Thorne is billed to defend it in a three-way at NBK Pride, with no result surviving.

The fed's own Intercontinental title history (/promotions/chz/archive/intercontinental-title-history.html) dates every change from 1999; Brewer won the title from Chazzy 2 Cool on 9 January 2002.
