Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt the fed sold on what it could do to a body: eight inches of glass table, a thirty foot cage, a pit of acid. Switchblade won it on 23 May 2002 and defended it with both men's hands taped and doused in glass. Luke Lawless is a former champion by that autumn, Chryste holds it in the spring of 2003, and Kuff McSlade takes it in the first week of July and keeps it a week later in the main event, barely. By the end of September it is on Sean Quinn and being put on the line beside a title Callisto Bane has just forced another man to vacate.

The fed's own June 2002 Champions page lists the belt without a contender; the same page lists Lethal against the Heavyweight title and Lana Day against the Women's.
