WWA Rookie Championship

aka Rookie Ring

Placard

Promotion   WWA (1997–2019)
Active   1999–2002

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A developmental title for the fed's own newer talent, active from at least 1999 and repeatedly retired and reinstated across the surviving record — "the Rookie Ring is retired, once again" reads one of its own ledger entries, followed months later by a full relaunch. Mal Somers's win over Barrett Sinclair at Birthday Brawl 2002 closes out the fed's final recovered reign.
