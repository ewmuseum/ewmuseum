Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

fWo's International Championship: the earliest reign served here begins on 19 February 1999, held by Black Panther. The fed's own record carries 24 reigns on it; 24 of them, across 15 different champions, are served here so far. The longest of those is Eron the Relentless's, 94 days from 31 October 1999. One of them ended outside a match (retired).
