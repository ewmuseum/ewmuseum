Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Won by Michael Manson on 29 March 2003 as the man left unpinned in the three-way final for the World Heavyweight Title. Rat Fink stole the belt in April, and Manson won it back in a ladder match at the first pay-per-view in May. Scotty Michaels took it from Manson in a steel cage at the Spirit of NGEN on 16 August 2003, after Luster struck Manson with a pipe, and kept it against Copycat at Almost Live. Richard Gideon took it from Scotty Michaels in a three-way with Felix Red at Ghoulish Games on 15 November 2003, when Michaels passed out in his figure-four leglock, and kept it against Psycho at Turkey Day Terror on 8 December when Psycho was disqualified for using a fork. Gideon kept it in a three-way against Copycat and Psycho at Great Expectations on 18 February 2004. Psycho took it from Gideon at the Superbowl of Wrestling on 18 April 2004, two falls to one in a match of submissions only, the Four Corners of Hell and a Texas Death Match. Psycho kept it against Rat Fink, Pulsar and El Arco Iris at Cherry Blossom Chaos II on 15 August 2004 and against Golem at Paradise Lost on 20 September 2004.

Introduced beside the World Heavyweight Title as the fed's second belt; the Vox Hiberionacum introduction says both resume the lineage of the old NGEN World Heavyweight Title, a claim of the fed's own. While Rat Fink held the stolen belt, the broadcasts went on calling Michael Manson champion. The fed's own post of its North American champions, headed "NGEN/WFW North American Title History" (December 2003, kept up in later years), begins with Michael Manson at Vox Hiberionacum and carries no NGEN reign; like the World title's list, it dates each change a few days after the first post of the broadcast that told it. The same list dates the Psycho change to 26 April 2004, the day the post telling the deciding fall went up; this wing's rows use the date of the first post of the broadcast, 18 April 2004.
