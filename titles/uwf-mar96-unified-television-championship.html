Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Marcus Nuit, the reigning Rampage Champion, beat Meltdown Champion Tigress by pinfall (the 51st Drop) in a title unification match on 22 June 2005 to become the belt's first champion, then retained it by disqualification over "Violent" Victor Frost in its own first defense on 6 July 2005 after Sabbath's own interference.
