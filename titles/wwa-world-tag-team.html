WWA World Tag Team Championship

aka CWC Northeast Tag Team Championship, CWC Northeastern Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   WWA (1997–2019)
Active   1998–2008
Retired by the fed   1 May 2003 — The fed's own ledger: "Matt Warr, in his infinte wisdom retires the WWA World Tag Team Titles." Revived one month later, 1 June 2003, when Havoc defeated X-Corp for the reinstated title.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Defended across dozens of pairings from 1998 onward, including four-time champions The Veteranz and a run for The Franchise Players (Tracer Bullet and Denrol). The fed's own title history has the front office retire the belt on 1 May 2003; it was revived exactly one month later, on 1 June 2003, when Havoc defeated X-Corp for the reinstated title, and continued changing hands for years afterward.
