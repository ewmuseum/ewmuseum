ICWC World Heavyweight Championship

Promotion   ICWC (2008)
Active   2008–2009
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Vacant at the January 2008 launch, filled when Dean Malenko won an eight-man tournament final over Owen Hart. Hulk Hogan, Jeff Jarrett and Kurt Angle held it in turn, before Edge defeated champion Kurt Angle, Sid Vicious and Jeff Jarrett in a Fatal Four Way. Edge's second reign unified in the XWF World Heavyweight Championship, defeating XWF Champion Brock Lesnar and Hulk Hogan in a Triple Threat at ICWC Capital Combat (2009).
