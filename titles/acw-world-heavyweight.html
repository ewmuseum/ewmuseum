Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The top of All-Star Championship Wrestling's card, and the belt the fed kept taking away from people. Osyrus was its first champion of the television age, in September 2002, and it changed hands four more times before the fed folded in December 2003. “Superstar” Vince Jacobs won it from Alias at King of Ages in August 2004 in the third and, at the time, final match of a rivalry ACW dated back a decade; Jacobs and Khristain Keller pinned Alias at the same time at breakOUT the following February, and on the next Courage SilverHAWK vacated the belt. Keller took it at End Game, Max Danger took it from Keller on Courage 86, 19 January 2006, and Seymour Almasy took it from both of them in a three-way at Legends II on 26 January 2006. He defended it through the spring, twice in one night at Honor & Glory after being made to qualify for his own main event, and Andy Sharp pinned him for it at Courage 100 on 6 July 2006. When the fed came back in 2009 he won it again, beating Andy Sharp in the final of a tournament for the vacant title on the Courage of 9 April 2009, and never lost it in a match: on the night of Legends III, 4 June 2009, he was found unconscious in a straight jacket, and Max Danger won it in a battle royal. After Max Danger, Andy Sharp, Alias, Trey Vincent and Keith Scott Zimmerman held it through 2010, and Z won it at End Game in Rome on the last night of 2010 and was champion when that run ended. On the fan board's run, Tyson XL, The Amazing Gabriel and Andy Sharp held it into 2013.

Courage 107, 18 March 2010: Alias retained the World Heavyweight Championship when his partner Jesse Ramey pinned Khristain Keller with the Hangover while Keller winked at him, repaying a favour Keller said Alias owed. Courage 108, 27 March 2010: Alias retained the World Heavyweight Championship by pinning Sharc. Courage 109, 1 April 2010: Alias kept the World Heavyweight Championship when his match with Keith Scott Zimmerman dissolved into a brawl after Shawn Jessica Hart rang the bell across the referee's face, and Trey Vincent's three count on Alias with no referee counted for nothing. Courage 110, 8 April 2010: Alias retained the World Heavyweight Championship by pinning High Flyer after a Super A-Bomb, with a split-open forehead and a Cliffhanger onto a ladder behind him.
