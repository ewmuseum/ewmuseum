EWF's own top title from its founding show onward: 16 structured reign changes from Kraven's win of the founding battle royal (2001) to a three-way rights transfer settled at the fed's fourth PPV (2005) and Kraven's third reign (2007).

The title-history table credits this reign to Kraven alone. This wing's own newly served show script for the same date (Wrestlepalooza LXXXVIII, 1 April 2007) instead depicts Sandman, Kraven and Game jointly pinning defending champion Tool together -- the same three-way-co-championship booking pattern already seen at the 17 April 2005 PPV. The two of the fed's own documents disagree on the resulting champion(s); this row is left as the title table itself states it, per the standing rule against over-interpreting one primary source against another.
