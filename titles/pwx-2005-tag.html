The fed's own title history lists 21 numbered reigns of its tag titles, from Shocker and Mark O'Neal on 16 December 2005 to Corey Gein and Micheal Norcia on 28 October 2007, with the numbers 16 and 18 missing from the page. The last row records the titles vacated when PWX announced its closure in December 2007.

The page says only the top tag teams in the world compete for these titles. Its heading reads "Campeones de Parejas".
