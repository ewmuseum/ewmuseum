Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned in the same 23 March tournament final (Jason Starr over PO Box), the belt passed through John Stillwater and PO Box before Mike Garvey took it at Heat Wave '97; renamed on-air to the New York Championship around mid-October under its reigning champion, Sam Adams, with the lineage carried unbroken under its new name (see inwi-new-york).
