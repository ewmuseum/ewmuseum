Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

An annual tournament rather than a belt, and by the fed's own Accolades page its marquee one, run at times with sixteen entrants and at times with twenty and settled on its own show. Khristain Keller won the 2003 edition on 7 September 2003, beating Vince Jacobs in the final, and his bio card notes he was still waiting on the title shot it was supposed to carry. The 2004 edition ran its qualifiers through July and August — Lancett, Simian Kade, SilverHAWK, Quinton May and God's Forgotten Son all fought through them — and God's Forgotten Son beat May in the final on 22 August 2004, which also made him United States Champion. The third ran through five Courage cards in 2010, from a qualifying battle royal to a three-man finale at King of Ages on 26 August, in which Jesse Ramey beat Z and Keith Scott Zimmerman, and its prize was new: the King's Choice, which its holder could use for any purpose.

Won in the fed's own tournament, at most once a year (2003, 2004 and 2010), and never defended between editions. Its own characters call it a title (Khristain Keller, 26 August 2004: "I am the reigning King of Ages, never having lost the title in the first place"); no defence of it survives in the fed's own record.
