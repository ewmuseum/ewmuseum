Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A crown created at Battle of the Warriors 2006, in a four-way won by Myst, with Scavenger announcing that once a month someone would challenge the king or queen of AW. Its champions page lists a Monarch for each month through November 2006 and notes no pay-per-view in March, ten holders in all.
