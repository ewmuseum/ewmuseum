Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Civil Disobedience (Seth Masters & Trevor Hawke) won OSW's own first Tag Team Championship at Crowning Glory, OSW's debut PPV, and lost it the same night three weeks later, 30 March 2002, to Heresian Bloodlust (Hessian & Azrael Cain) — who beat back two challenges from Riptah & Short Circuit before dropping the belts to that same team on 23 June 2002, closing the chain across the fed's own first three reigns. Riptah and Short Circuit then held the belts for a month that summer. The Throwbacks ('Old School' Dave Gibson & Clay Vendetta) held the titles by a September 2002 champions snapshot. By early 2003 the champions were Heaven & Hell (Hessian & Propaganda — Hessian's second tag reign with a second partner), who retained against a crossover challenge from PCW's own Politically Incorrect in a Falls Count Anywhere match, 9 Feb 2003.
