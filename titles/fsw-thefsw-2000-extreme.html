Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The first run's hardcore championship. Mike Marvelous, refereeing, awarded it to Jacob Jackson at High Impact on 27 August 2000 when time ran out, though Dysan believed he had won. Jack Miller held it three times; at Final Destination on 26 November 2000 he ran Hardcore Camo over with a car in the parking lot and took the belt on the same night Camo had won it. Jack Brooze won it vacant at Redemption on 26 August 2001. A Paranoia write-up of October 2001 has P.S. Mad pin Brooze for it; at Futile Resistance in November 2001 Brooze is billed champion again and PS Mad wins it from him, together with the Rising Star title, and the fed's champions page still listed him at the end of the year.

Sources: the fed's own title history on thefsw.com, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 8 April and 31 October 2001; the first run's show write-ups, among them an undated Paranoia results page of October 2001; the Futile Resistance preview card ("Jack Brooze(c)"); the champions page captured on 28 December 2001. The October 2001 title history ends with Jack Brooze and carries no line for the Paranoia change.
