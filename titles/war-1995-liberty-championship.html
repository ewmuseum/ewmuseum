WAR! Liberty Championship

Placard

Promotion   WAR! (1995)
Active   1995

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A title born at the Season 2 relaunch opener, when The Mandarin won it from a debuting Joey the Clown (the future DeathJester), then lost it within the week to Jerry "Goon" Gordon's same-week double-title run; a hot-potato stretch followed — Joey the Clown regaining it under his own future ring name, then Sid Starr, then "008" Jim Bondo twice in one night after a retiring Butch Williams signed it over by contract clause — before it settled, by late 1996, on DeathJester and then "Dirty" Doug Simpkins.
