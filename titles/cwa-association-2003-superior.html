Placard

Promotion   CWA (Association, 2003)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Inter-Continental title of the CWA’s first summer, in 2003, which absorbed the Hardcore title on 5 July to become the Superior belt. Mach, Q, Shannon Skanes and Vigilante are its holders on the title history, and the cards of 2004 show The Moose taking it on 23 February, SinmaN on 26 April and Tommy Air on 24 May.
