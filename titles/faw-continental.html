Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Named on FAW's own December 2002 title-history index as one of four belts whose recorded history began on 1 December 2002, and named again in the 2003 list of titles the fed stopped defending. The only holder the surviving record identifies is Dave Tronick, billed as a former Continental Champion on his own return at Diabolical Day 2003; the index page's own linked histories were never captured.

FAW's own Title History index, December 2002 (promotions/faw/archive/faw-title-history-index.html), FAW's own champions page, December 2003 (promotions/faw/archive/faw-champions-december-2003.html) and FAW's own Diabolical Day 2003 recap (promotions/faw/archive/diabolical-day-2003-2003-07-13.html).
