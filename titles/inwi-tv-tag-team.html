Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The consolation half of the founding tag final (the Peeler Brothers), this belt bounced hardest of all -- the Friendship Express and Solid Gold traded it repeatedly across the spring and summer before Solid Gold's own reign from mid-July ran uninterrupted through the rest of the wing's own served record.
