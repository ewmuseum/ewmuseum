Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own served title-history page runs a fully dated 15-reign chain for this belt, from Bolo Yeung's founding win at NEWF: The Beginning (21 June 1998) onward. The title-history page's own footnote records the belt vacated the same day, "when Speed Racer dumped it to pursue the World Title." The fed's own served Pandemonium PPV recap, dated the same day, does not itself narrate a vacating — it shows Speed Racer defending and retaining the U.S. Title in a straight match against Sinister Sean Blake that same night (Commissioner Clayton's own on-air announcement calls him "the U.S. Champ" going into it), and its own introduction for the later World Title main event billed Most Holy — not Speed Racer — as "The U.S. Champion," a discrepancy between the two served primary sources the held record does not otherwise resolve.
