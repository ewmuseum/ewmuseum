Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Awarded from the founding tournament's own final on 23 March 1997 (Pimp Daddy Brown over John Stillwater), the top prize churned through four champions across the year -- Brown to Brody Thunder at SuperCard I's Steel Cage main event, Thunder to the Springer in the 1997 Ringmaster PPV main event, and the Springer to Tony Houston by the following show, Houston still champion at Winter Storm '97.
