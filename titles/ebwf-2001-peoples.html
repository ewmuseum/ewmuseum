Promotion   EBWF (2002)

Renamed   EBWF Television Championship 24 September 2006 — The Title History's own entry: “People's Title renamed Television Championship - PPV: Destiny - Sunday, September 24, 2006”.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Kane held it first, from 15 April 2002. John Cena took it five times between 2003 and 2005 and Booker T four in 2002 and 2003; Kurt Angle and Chris Jericho — billed on this belt's record as Y2J Chris Jericho — traded it five times between them across the winter of 2004. Two of its forty-nine reigns began on the same night, 4 October 2004, when Christian and then The Undertaker each held it, and The Undertaker's own retirement emptied it two days later. At the Destiny Pay-Per-View on 24 September 2006 the promotion renamed it the Television Championship, with Alex Shelley's own reign running through the change.

Source: the promotion's own Title History page (/promotions/ebwf-2001/archive/titlehistory.html). The Title History writes this belt's own name only as “People's Title”, in the rename entry.
