Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own title history lists 80 entries for the World Heavyweight Championship, from Vampiro to Mark Jindrak. 13 of them carry dates, from 28 October 2006 to 11 May 2008; the rest carry only their number. Its footnote 8 says: 'Sting unifies WCW and WWF world titles'. Its footnote 16 says: 'Edge unifies WCW and WWE world titles by defeating Chris Jericho (WWE world champion)'. The TNA fan federation's news of 8 June 2008 says it acquired the 'Big Gold' Championship with WCW's property.

The fed's own WCW belt as its title history lists it, never the real belt.
