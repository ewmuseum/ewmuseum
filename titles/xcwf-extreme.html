Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Born at ResurreXtion in a four-way ladder match that opened with three men colliding in midair before it even properly started, and closed with Freak climbing past a tornado DDT'd Justin Pearce to unhook the belt. It is the fed's own richest lineage — twenty reigns in eleven months, three of them changing hands in a single week between Bayamon and Cancun, and three more on one Cancun night alone. Royce Mathers was its last champion the ledger records, crowned in Richmond in July 2002.
