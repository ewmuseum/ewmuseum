AW Xtreme Championship

Placard

Promotion   AW (Anarchy, 2005)
Active   2005–2006

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt for the company’s high-flyers and brawlers alike, awarded vacant to Hoshi Yakamoto on 21 December 2005 and passed through a battle royal, a table-filled rumble, an open challenge and a hardcore main event. Hoshi holds it three times by its own champions page, which dates Frank Merritt’s first reign to 7 October 2006 though the 23 September Insurgence shows him winning it a week earlier.
