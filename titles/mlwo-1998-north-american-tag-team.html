MLWO North American Tag Team Championship

aka VPWF North American Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   MLWO (1996)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A tag team title that Da Ghetto Blasters lost to the Two Dudes With Attitude on the Wednesday Washout of 6 November 1996. ER, Michael Jeffries and Andrew Saunders, carried it in December 1996 and January 1997, and the fed's president announced in January 1997 that it would be unified with the world tag belts.
