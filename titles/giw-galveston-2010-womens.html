Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's Women's Championship: 5 reigns are served here, across 5 different champions, the first beginning on 11 July 2010 with Violet Vanity and the last on 26 August 2010 with Kacie Church. The longest is Kacie Church's, from 26 August 2010 to about 11 November 2010 (78 days in the fed's own history); of the reigns with both dates exact, the longest is Alexii Ravynova's, 18 days from 4 August 2010. Of these, 2 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). Its cards call it the Divas title and the Galveston Island Women's title side by side in 2010; it was retired on 11 November 2010.
