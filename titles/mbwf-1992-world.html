MBWF World Championship

aka World Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   MBWF (Swansea)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The league's top belt, open to men, and vacant when the league opened on 10 October 1992; Death Croc was champion by 19 October. Wolverine took it from him at Friday Ferocity #2 on 26 February 1993 and lost it back to him at Grapple Wars on 18 June 1993.

Named in the league's founding post ("M.B.W.F. World Champion"), its update of 19 October 1992, the newsletter of 18 March 1993 and Grapple Wars 1; "World Heavyweight Champion.... Men Only" in its write-up of 14 December 1993.
