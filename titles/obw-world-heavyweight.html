Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

OBW's second men's flagship singles title, attested only through incidental mentions on other wrestlers' own bios and the federation's own last-touched champions list; no dedicated title-history page survives for it, unlike the Vixens belts. MVP Lux is the earliest attested holder, named only as the champion Chad Bowens dethroned; the record does not say how or when Lux won it, though it does record him earning an open-ended championship opportunity in an Elimination Chamber match at Death Trap (30 July 2012). Bowens won the title from Lux at an Execution television taping ("Execution 86", by his own account 25 June 2013), with help from an outside beatdown of Lux by four other wrestlers, but was forced to vacate it after an in-storyline injury. How or when the vacancy was filled is not recorded; the wiki's own champions list, last touched 21 February 2014, names M-Star as champion by then.

Ledger source: promotions/obw/archive/obw-champions.html; reign chain also drawn from promotions/obw/archive/chad-bowens.html and promotions/obw/archive/mvp-lux.html. Distinct from the wing's other men's flagship, the OBW Championship (Sidster/Krmi/Bowens/Ice/Punisher lineage), which is reconstructed separately.
