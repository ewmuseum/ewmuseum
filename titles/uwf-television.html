UWF Television Championship

Placard

Promotion   UWF (2001–07)
Active   2001–2006
Cadence   standing
Retired by the fed   18 January 2004 — The belt was put up in a unification match against the World Heavyweight Championship and went dark when its holder won that match and left with the World title instead. The card of that night calls the match on its way to the ring — “One way or the other, the UWF TV Title’s dead after this match” — and the fed's own 2004 title-history revision carries “Title Suspended.” in the holder's place. A battle royal on 25 Apr 2004 brought the belt back.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The hot-potato belt of the UWF era, exactly as it had been in the GIWF — the 2004 revision's own Quick Facts credit Shadow Blade with the most reigns (four) and, by its last update, Hot Mask with the longest (79 days). Seymour Almasy held it three times across the run. Several rows carry names the crew would not perform today, marked under the Bum Boozler rule; State Trooper Roger Rogers's own reign ends with him vacating the title and throwing it into a river rather than face Ruff Ryder. After the 2004 revision the belt passed from Silvio Rochelli to James von Doom in the Clash's Television Title Jubilee on 6 March 2005, then to Red Rock, whose reign was the fed's longest to that point, and on through James Mason to Sledge at the Regal Rumble in September.
