Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The other half of the split Spirit of ACW, and the belt Seymour Almasy came to the fed to win. Its own belt page carries a staged history of thirteen reigns from January 1999 to July 2002 before ICU won it on the Courage of 13 October 2002. Alias won it on 16 March 2003 and vacated it on 1 June 2003 after an undefeated reign; Quinton May won the battle royal for it at Glory and held it until the fed folded that December, then won it back in a ladder match on 8 April 2004; Fejona Min took it from him on 7 October 2004 in a match that put the Scorpion Fighting Championship on the same shoulder. Almasy beat both her and Kelly Flawless for it at breakOUT, defended it through the spring and summer of 2005, and lost it to Andy Sharp on Courage 79. Sharp was carrying it on 26 January 2006 when ACW announced from the stage that it was being folded back into the returned Spirit of ACW; the show's own text leaves him looking down at it, “the last in a long storied list of champions.” It came back on Courage 106 in Madrid on 11 March 2010, when Trevor Wilson declared himself its owner and SilverHAWK let it stand, and Midnight Cowboy retired it for good at World.Wide.Wrestlecide 001 on 30 April 2010, eight days after winning it at Legends IV.

ICU13 October 20022 February 2003
ICU hit Aaron Ward with Critical Condition and pinned him to become the first Television Champion of the televised era. Defences on record. breakOUT 2002, 10 November 2002: ICU kicked Ron Williams low, then climbed the ropes and pulled the chicken down from its peg to retain the Television Title in the Chicken on a Pole match. End Game 2002, 22 December 2002: ICU retained the Television Title by pinning ToK after ToK slid out of Critical Condition and ICU hit him with the Depression clothesline.
Scott Perry2 February 200316 March 2003
Scott Perry pinned ICU after A*Dubbs hit ICU with a steel chair and Ethan Winters shoved the referee into the ring to count the three, and Winters gave Perry the Television Title. A defence is on record. Apocalypse 2003, 16 February 2003: Scott Perry retained the Television Title over Alias when the referee, who had been knocked down during Osyrus and Ethan Winters' interference, recovered and counted the three.
Alias16 March 20031 June 2003Won on the Courage of 16 March 2003, when the referee stopped the match with the champion caught in Anarchy's Lullaby.
Glory's own text: "The Television Title had been vacated since June 1st when Alias ended his reign as the champion." The fed's belt page dates the win 9 March 2003 and counts 84 days to that vacancy; the show carrying the win falls a week later, on 16 March, which makes the reign 77 days. On 1 June Alias hands the belt across SilverHAWK's desk; SilverHAWK does not become champion. Alias's own card (captured 3 November 2005) also counts the reign at 84 days ("undefeated; 84 day reign"), the belt page's count.

Seymour Almasy6 February 20052005-09-08 (circa)Submitted champion Fejona Min with an ankle lock in a three-way, after putting Kelly Flawless off the top rope to the floor. His first championship in ACW.

Andy Sharp pinned him for it on Courage 79; he did not move after the pin and was carried out.

Trevor Wilson11 March 201022 April 2010
Trevor Wilson, also known as Rejection and at the time the Spirit of ACW Champion, declared himself the owner of the retired Television Title in the ring and SilverHAWK let it stand on condition that he defend it that night. A defence is on record. Courage 106, 11 March 2010: Rejection, billed as Trevor Wilson, retained the Television Title in his first defence when Kati covered Buddy Saxon for the three count.
Midnight Cowboy22 April 201030 April 2010
Midnight Cowboy pinned Rejection, billed as Trevor Wilson, after Voices After Midnight and took the Television Title with the Spirit of ACW Championship. A defence is on record. World.Wide.Wrestlecide 001, 30 April 2010: Midnight Cowboy survived the six-man elimination match, last facing JP Boudreau, to retain the Television Title. Midnight Cowboy retired the Television Title after retaining it in the six-man elimination match, eight days after winning it at Legends IV.
