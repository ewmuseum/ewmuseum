Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Created by Brian Hardy on 15 May 2000 and awarded to Brian Hardy by Brian Hardy the same day, and defended in hardcore matches almost exclusively for the rest of its life. Fifty-seven numbered reigns, one of them vacated with the note "Title Vacated when the site was deleted"; the fed retired the belt.

The full reign ledger, built from /promotions/ngw/archive/untamed-title-history.html, is transcribed into this registry. The belt is listed on the fed's own retired-titles page (/promotions/ngw/archive/retired-titles-2004-09.html) despite a 2004 revival under 24/7 rule the dedicated ledger itself records.
