Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The UWF's top belt, restarted at the September 2001 Live and Loud tournament that closed the GIWF and opened the new fed. Its own Quick Facts, as of the 2004 revision, credit Seymour Almasy with the longest reign (138 days) and a three-way tie for most reigns among Danny Jaye, Almasy, and Tsunami Oni; Ruff Ryder's own 0-day reign — champion and ex-champion inside a single SAS — is its shortest. Six Hall of Famers held it before the fed's own last title-history revision; a museum-compiled continuation past that point carries it on through Kenny Mercury and Jamie Shock in 2005 (Shock won it at FANdamonium DOS in May and lost it to Misfit in a three-way at the Regal Rumble in September), then Killer Dude, Misfit, and Clyde, to a final recorded loss at the independently-hosted Sins of the Past, August 2006.
