GCW Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   GCW (gcwonline.net)
Active   2001–2010
Cadence   standing
Retired by the fed   30 October 2005 — The federation's own card, Devil's Night 2005 (30 Oct 2005), in the words of the champion Joshua Curtis: "It has been explained to me that the Tag Team Division here in GCW has flat lined and there is no way to revive it and that effective immediately the tag division has died with myself and Ami being the last champions of record." WorldWide 31 (6 Nov 2005) confirms it ("when the tag division got shut down"). The belt was later revived: the federation's own Tag Team Title History gives its reign No. 16 (21 Mar 2006) as won from 'Vacant'.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GCW's Tag Team Championship was first won by Wallstreet Hustlers on 3 April 2001. The fed's own record carries 29 reigns on it, across 28 different champions. The longest is Blood Games's, 1113 days from 21 March 2002. Four of its reigns ended outside a match (two vacated, one departed, one retired). Bryan Mayhem held it at the end of the surviving record.
