Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The federation's own top belt, and one a returning legend claimed on air had only been renamed: the JCWF's own world title, he said, carried under new letters since the merger that swallowed his federation. It changed hands twice in twenty-two days in the spring of 1996 — taken off 187 at Leprecharnage with a chair slid in by that same returning legend, and taken back on the next Blow-Out in a match the federation's own president cleared both entourages out of, to see who the better man was. Its earlier line is only partly recoverable: a 600-pound champion in May 1995, and a masked former champion out of Guadalajara still being billed as one a year later.

The claim that it was formerly the JCWF's own championship is made on air by Freddy Flame at Leprecharnage and is not corroborated elsewhere in the held record: “you can change the name of the title, but that doesn't mean a two bit thug can still wear it.” Recorded as the fed's own on-air claim, not as an established lineage.
