Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The LCW World Hardcore Title (billed the World Extreme Title in the fed's own rulebook) survives from a pre-history holder, "The Stalker" Steve Caslu, whose own reign predates the ledger's own 4 August 1998 opening entry, through 17 more recorded reigns to "The King of Slack" Chris Egerton's own 16 April 2000 win over Cuchulain, its own final entry in the surviving record.

Full reign-by-reign chain (17 title changes, plus the ledger's own pre-history holder, 4 Aug 1998 – 16 Apr 2000) structured from the fed's own served ledger (promotions/lcw-1997/archive/hardcore-title.html).
