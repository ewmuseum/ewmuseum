Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned by a 16-man tournament in the fed's own first two months, the CWAA's top title changed hands three more times -- twice by decision, once by unmasking -- before the held record ends with its reigning champion revealed as Marc Thomas.

Vacated by AEWA ruling, 22 June 1997 (sJ2Kd6hfi3AJ).
