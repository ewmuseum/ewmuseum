Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

CSW's own tag belt, contested from January 1996 as the "CSW Tag Team Title" and renamed "CSW World Tag Team Title" that August. The Raxton Brothers won it first, throwing the Pretty Boys out of a battle royal, and dominated its whole independent-era run — three of nine recorded reigns by the ledger's own count, plus a fourth spell the ledger pointedly does NOT number, restored to them after the CSW itself stripped UBH's disputed house-show win and handed the belts back. (Zamza, one half of that UBH team, tells it differently on his own page: the match never happened, and the promotion closed before the pay-per-view could.) This ledger keeps more of the promotion's texture than any of the other three: the only reign length CSW's entire record preserves — the Kennel Killers' 22 days, its longest, by a team whose members are named nowhere at all — and the note that Crippler, one half of "Rico Suave & Crippler," was the belt's own first Black champion. The Raxton Brothers were still champions in February 2004 when the acquisition stripped the lineage and folded it into CWF-Pro's own tag belt.

Source: csw-title.html/csw-national-title.html/csw-royal-title.html/csw-world-tag-team-title.html's own shared line: "Title is stripped by Pres Burns who has Acquired the CSW Efed".
