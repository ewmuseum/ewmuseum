Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Thirteen recorded reigns and eight teams in thirteen months: the Dynamic Machine, the Leaping Lords, the Maniacs, Flint and Steel three times, the Intoxicators, the Law three times, the Highlanders, the Hoosiers twice and RnR — the Law's own third win in February 1992 counted on air, and this ledger's count of their reigns matches the broadcast's exactly.
