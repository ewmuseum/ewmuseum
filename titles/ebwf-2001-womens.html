Promotion   EBWF (2002)
Active   2002–2008

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Molly Holly held it first, from 15 April 2002. Torrie Wilson took it eight times over four years, Lita and Stacy Keibler five each, across fifty-two reigns. Lita's last ended without a match: the Title History records her retiring with the belt at Backlash on 30 April 2006 and giving it to Kristal, who is listed as champion from that December. The reign before hers ended in the one vacancy the page explains by a handler leaving the promotion. Jillian Hall's win at Aftermath on 27 April 2008 is the last change recorded, and the promotion's own Champions page still names her champion.
