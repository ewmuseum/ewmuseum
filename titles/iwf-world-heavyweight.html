Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The top belt of the founding fed, passing through three men across the fed's recovered 1998 show recaps: Rob Williams's early two-time reign under his “Real Deal” ex-Navy-SEAL gimmick, a five-minute reign for Grim Reaper that ended on a simple clothesline, and Alien (kayfabe Chris Phillips of the Insane Crew) carrying it into a Buried Alive match at Christmas Clash '98, the year-end story those recaps close on. The site's own homepage, captured early the following year, names Lew Spectre the reigning champion by then — the last title change the surviving record shows before the fed handed over to the GIWF.
