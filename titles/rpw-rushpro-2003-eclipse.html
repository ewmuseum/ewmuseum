Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RPW's Eclipse Title was first won by Karina Wolfenden on 9 November 2003. The fed's own record carries 5 reigns on it, across 4 different champions. The longest is Ilan Levin's, 28 days from 23 November 2003. Gideon Poe held it at the end of the surviving record.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Karina Wolfenden9 November 200323 November 2003
Karina Wolfenden retrieved the belt from above the ring in a six-person ladder match to become the first Eclipse Champion, ahead of Lumi, Ilan Levin, A.J. Trinidad, The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid and El Diablo. A defence is on record. Adrenaline 2, 16 November 2003: Karina Wolfenden hooked The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid's legs after pulling him in over the top rope with a Huricanrana from the apron and pinned him to retain the Eclipse Title.
Ilan Levin23 November 200321 December 2003
Ilan Levin pinned Karina Wolfenden to win the Eclipse Title while El Diablo was on the outside, on Adrenaline 3, a show whose write-up has not been loaded; the record is the recap in Adrenaline 4. A defence is on record. Adrenaline 4, 30 November 2003: El Diablo hit Ilan Levin with the Brain Disorder as the time limit expired before the referee finished the three count, so the match was ruled a time limit draw and Levin kept the Eclipse Title.
Karina Wolfenden21 December 2003between 2004-01-25 and 2004-03-21 (circa)
Karina Wolfenden hit Ilan Levin with the Three Sixty Alien Drop and pinned him in a three way with Brianna Washington to win the Eclipse Title back. Defences on record. Adrenaline 9, 18 January 2004: Karina Wolfenden hit The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid with the Whirlwind Romance, a tornado seatbelt DDT, onto a chair and pinned him to retain the Eclipse Title in the first match under the new Eclipse Rules. Adrenaline 10, 25 January 2004: Karina Wolfenden sprang off the ropes with a Huricanrana that drove Violence Jack's head onto a chair and pinned him with his legs locked to retain the Eclipse Title, after Magnum Wolf had hit Jack with a chair while she was tied in the ropes.
Carysbetween 2004-01-25 and 2004-03-21 (circa)30 May 2004
Carys became the Eclipse Champion after Karina Wolfenden; the change of hands is on no card that is held. Defences on record. Adrenaline 20, 11 April 2004: Carys hit Quinton Dufresne in the face with a shovel and pinned him to retain the Eclipse Title. Adrenaline 21, 18 April 2004: Carys covered Tessa Windsor and got the three after Chris Storm hit Tessa in the back with the Mid South belt as she came off the ropes, so Carys retained the Eclipse Title in a no disqualification match.
Gideon Poe30 May 2004—
Gideon Poe spiked Carys with the Unholy Confession, a cradle tombstone piledriver, and pinned her in a five way to win the Eclipse Title.
