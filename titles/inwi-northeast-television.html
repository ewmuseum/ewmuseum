Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

One of two Northeast-branded belts awarded the same tournament weekend (23 March), crowned on Brody Thunder before a run of short reigns -- the Springer, Mike Garvey, Firestar, Vito Rizzotto, Bill Bob Woijicowski -- settled with Tony Houston from mid-August on.
