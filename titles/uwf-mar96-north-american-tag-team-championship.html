Won by the Ghost Dancers from Team Canada with stereo scorpion deathlocks on 25 October 2003, after a contract clause meant only this title, not the World Tag Team Championship, was actually on the line in the match.

The Ghost Dancers ("Screaming Drillbit" Jason Keening, "Banshee" Michael Keening)25 October 200315 November 2003
Won the UWF North American Tag Team Championship from Team Canada; the same match's own fine print kept Team Canada's separate UWF World Tag Team Championship off the line.
The Sons of Cacaphony (Erik Grimsson, Chad Grimsson)15 November 2003—
Won the UWF North American Tag Team Championship inside the Tower of Doom, a four-team cage-escape match, taking the belts from their own side's teammates and the reigning champions, the Ghost Dancers, by being the first complete pair out of the cage. Retained against the Universal Powers (Todd Franklin and Bobby Lincoln) on 21 March 2004, the Sky Hammer combination finishing Bobby Lincoln after both teams traded dives to the arena floor. Lost the title to the Universal Powers (Bobby Lincoln and Todd Franklin) in a street fight on 28 August 2004, an unidentified masked interloper knocking Erik Grimsson out from inside a locker-room closet before Todd Franklin's superkick finished him for the pin.
The Universal Powers (Bobby Lincoln, Todd Franklin)28 August 2004—
Won the UWF North American Tag Team Championship from the Sons of Cacaphony in a street fight with the help of an unidentified masked interloper, whose identity was not revealed this show; the Privateers separately ambushed Chad Grimsson at ringside during the same match, unrelated to either tag team's own title challenge. Lost the title to the Amazing Romanis (Niklos and Ravnos Romani) by pinfall on 20 October 2004, Niklos pinning Lincoln with a neckbreaker off his own brother's shoulders after a returning Vyolynce's own ringside chair distraction.
The Amazing Romanis (Niklos "Ricochet" Romani, "The Wild Card" Ravnos Romani)20 October 2004unknown
Won the UWF North American Tag Team Championship from the Universal Powers under a new team name, having previously wrestled together in this wing as the Arcana. Retained again over the Prophets of Rage (Derek Rage, Shadoe Rage) by pinfall (Without A Net) on 17 November 2004, aided by a mystery burst of pyrotechnics and an eyeball graphic that rattled Shadoe Rage ahead of the finish. A defense against the Hands of Death (Serge Annis, Caliban) on 1 December 2004 was thrown out as a no-contest after the arriving Prophets of Rage provoked a weapons brawl; the champions were hung by dog collars afterward, and both the Hands of Death and the winner of their own Rise to Power dog collar match with the Prophets of Rage were subsequently challenged to a title match by the champions themselves. Lost the titles to the Prophets of Rage in a bout not read by this collection, per the promotion's own billing on UWF Saturday Night Rampage, 18 March 2006.

Lost the title to the Ghost Dancers on 25 October 2003 in a rematch a fine-print contract clause secretly limited to this belt alone, keeping the team's own UWF World Tag Team Championship out of the match.
