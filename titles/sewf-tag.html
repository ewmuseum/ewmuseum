SEWF World Tag Team Championship

Promotion   SEWF (ShawWorld, 2000–02)
Active   2000–2001

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Filled at ShawCade 3 in September 2000 by the Invisibles, the Tag Team Championship ran through nine reigns by the fed's own title histories between September 2000 and May 2001, three of them Tommy Kain and Burt Clifton's, then passed to Sex Stable 2001 at the June relaunch and to the Flaming Jet Planes for a second time on 1 July. The Kasai Contingent won it in Tag Team Turmoil at End Game in December 2001.
