SWF: Generation Supreme SWF Generations Championship

Promotion   SWF: Generation Supreme (Bigpapa42's diary)
Active   2008

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A new belt for Generation Supreme, shown off by Richard Eisen and Jack Bruce on the second Friday show of the diary and won by Ace Newton in a ten-man battle royal at The World is Watching. Robbie Retro takes it from Newton on Supreme TV in June 2008. Retro keeps it against Sean Deeley on Supreme TV in Week 1 of July 2008, while he and Newton each claim to be the face of Generation Supreme and both are left off the card of The Supreme Challenge 28. Retro keeps it against Ash Campbell on Supreme TV in Week 3 of July 2008 but loses it to Ace Newton in a Threefold Threat with Jay Chord a week later, Newton pinning Retro with his feet on the ropes; in Week 1 of August the Four Kings and Playboy Jake Sawyer give Newton a new belt, which Richard Eisen calls the new official Generations Championship. Newton keeps it against Kirk Jameson on Supreme TV in Week 3 of August 2008 and, in the SWF.com recaps of Generation Supreme, against El Leon and Swoop McCarthy, and is billed against Gino Montero for it in Week 2 of September.
