Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A championship NFW's own ledger never gave a lineage to. Bloodhunt unveiled the belt at the 2010 opening night in Philadelphia after losing to Sean Stevens, crowned Stevens its first champion on the spot, called him drug-free and demanded Olympic-style testing of anyone who wanted it — an argument aimed squarely at the World Champion of the day. Stevens held it into 2011 and was suspended with it; Troy Windham then had Bobby Jack Windham named champion as one of the terms of the Supercrash III deal, on the grounds that no Windham was purer. Legion won it in a four-corners match at Reloaded X by falling on an unconscious champion, and Stalker took it from him at Supercrash IV in a chain match while Legion's own valet worked against him. Three months later it stopped existing, merged into the Triple Crown. The fed's own championship history lists none of that: the belt appears there once, in another title's closing note.

Named by the fed's own championship ledger only in passing, in the National Championship's own closing note; the belt has no lineage of its own on that page, and the reigns below come from the fed's own served show documents instead.
