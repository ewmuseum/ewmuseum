LTD Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   LTD (2001)
Active   2002

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Contested in a tournament seeded at Winter's Wrath from eight teams; the final is never captured, and HaVoC (billed Ruthless Inc. once Maxx Vain joined) are the first attested champions, holding the belts into 19 March 2002. Baine and Parker Louis, wrestling as Global Warning, won them off Ruthless Inc. that night in the same six-man that kept Myrddin's LTD Championship, and were still champions — billed back down to a two-man HaVoC challenge for Genesis — when the surviving record runs out.
