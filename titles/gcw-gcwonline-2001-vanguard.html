Placard

Promotion   GCW (gcwonline.net)
Active   2007–2011
Cadence   standing
Unified into   HDTV Championship 3 June 2007 — At Warpath 2007 the federation staged the Television and Vanguard Championship unification match, "contested under Vanguard Rules... which means the winner of this match can be declared only by 5 second knock-out or submission": Jay Terror beat Vanguard Champion Katie O'Clare, and the timekeeper tossed both belts into the ring for him. Source: the fed's own card, Warpath 2007 (3 Jun 2007).
Unified into   Hardcore Championship 11 October 2011 — At Rampage on 11 Oct 2011 the federation staged a one-title-leaves match between Vanguard Champion Shaman — "the last remnant of the Hardcore division, save for the current Hardcore Champion" who "brought back The Vanguard title" — and the Hardcore Champion, Brad Cruz, who won it on the Cruzifix Slam: "Andrews: Here is your winner...the GCW Hardcore Champion...The SoCal Prophet...BRAAAAAAD! CRUUUUUUUUUUUUZZZZZZZ!!!" and "Bryan: What a war that was, and we've got a SOLIDIFIED Hardcore Champion!" The Vanguard title is named nowhere after this night; the card of 10 Jan 2012 still calls the belt 'HARDCORE/VANGUARD' in its billing and its commentary corrects that on air to the Hardcore title. Source: the fed's own card, Rampage of 11 Oct 2011.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GCW's Vanguard Championship's first recorded champion is 'The Silent Assassin' Peter Vetra. The fed's own record carries 3 reigns on it, across 3 different champions. GCW unified it with the Television Championship at Warpath 2007, on 3 June 2007, when Jay Terror beat Katie O'Clare, and Shaman reinstated it in 2011. The federation unified it into the Hardcore Championship on 11 October 2011.

The inaugural Vanguard reign. Two defences are held: 9 Mar 2007 (by knockout) and 26 Mar 2007 ('and STILL GCW Vanguard Champion').

Sources disagree — the fed's own title list names Katie O`Clare the Vanguard champion, but Warpath 2007 (3 Jun 2007) stages the Television and Vanguard unification match, in which Jay Terror beat her under Vanguard Rules and the timekeeper tossed both belts into the ring for him, and the fed's own title log records Shaman as the Vanguard champion going into 11 Oct 2011. Both readings are recorded; the reign ends at the unification match, which the card states, and the list, whose entry was not kept current (the same is true for the Hardcore belt), is not taken as evidence. The belt then leaves the fed's own title log for four and a half years.

Sources disagree — two dates, both the federation's own, and both recorded. On 28 Jul 2011 Shaman declares himself champion of a belt he has reinstated with no authority behind him, and the commentary treats it as unsettled ("Yale: Is it even official that he is Vanguard Champion yet?"). On 15 Aug 2011 the Commissioner's letter makes it official and the belt is handed to him. This reign files the official date; the eighteen days before it are his own claim and nobody else's. The belt was not being defended between 2007 and 2011: the fed's own word for what happened in July 2011 is 'reinstated'.
