The federation's own tag-team prize, left vacant entering the 2004 comeback season and decided in a four-way elimination match at the federation's own first pay-per-view.

Attested as vacant from the federation's own earliest 2004 news bulletins onward; the four teams that filled its own inaugural four-way match were Youth Gone Wrong, the Boys Next Door, the Natural Criminals and the True All Stars.
