Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Philippe LaFleur won it in February 2001 with a referee Rick Sturgis had paid, and Justin Keith was carrying it by May, when Taurus took it from him two falls to one in the Detonation main event. Keith won it back at Ragnarok that September as the first three-time champion the fed had billed, then lost it to "Deadly" Derek Irvin in a ladder match at Ascension, with his late brother's honorary belt hanging beside it.
