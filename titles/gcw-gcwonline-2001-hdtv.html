Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GCW's HDTV Championship was first won by Capo on 20 May 2001. The fed's own record carries 48 reigns on it, across 42 different champions. The longest of known length is Jay Terror's, 157 days from 10 May 2007. Sexton Hardon held it at the end of the surviving record, as the Unified Television and Hardcore Champion after the Hardcore Championship was unified into it on 10 January 2012.

Triumph Frost19 August 20102 October 2010
The federation's own Television Title History page stops at Cheap Labor's reign of 25 Nov 2009 and carries nothing after it. The fed's own title log brackets this win between 1 May and 19 Aug 2010; the text of the 19 Aug card dates it exactly. One successful defence is held: High Stakes of 2 Sep 2010, billed 'Luck of the Draw TV Title Match', against Tempest — won by disqualification after Frost brought a steel chair into the match and she DDT'd him onto it ("Here is your winner by disqualification, and stiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiill GCW Television Champion...TRIUMPH FROOOOOOOOOSSSSTT!!!!"); he kept both the TV and Hardcore belts leaving the ring that night. He lost the TV belt one month later, 2 Oct 2010, to a challenger billed only as '? ? ? ? ?' and revealed in the text as Dominic Wall, tapping to a Guillotine Choke after Frost blinded the referee with a title-belt shot and the referee (Josh Briggs) recovered late; the Hardcore title was not contested in that match and stayed his.
