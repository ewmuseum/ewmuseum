Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The WWO's own busiest championship by the ledger's own count — the Business Men, World Leaders, Lethal Weapons and Mean Mason/DOC traded it four times inside eleven days in December 1997 alone, and World Leaders' own three total reigns lead every team the ledger names. Uncivil Disobedience won it from Don't You Wish You Were Us in Los Angeles on 21 October 1998, and by 2001 the belts had passed to Physicians Advocating Innovative Neoprocedures, who took them from Vegas Connection in Washington, D.C. on 18 June 2001 and lost them back to the same team in Milwaukee just six weeks later, on 29 July 2001 — the ledger's own last recorded change. The federation's own notes name Thunderstorm, an earlier holder, as "the final WWO tag team champions of 2000," leaving the 2001 Vegas Connection/P.A.I.N. exchanges as a coda the notes themselves never reconcile against that claim.
