Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt Ric Beauty created for his Beautiful Wrestling Federation faction, diamond and jewel encrusted, and awarded to Corey Fuller in a match he refereed himself. G-Gangsta took it from Fuller three weeks later; by December it was Classy Joey Boy's, with the faction's new president, Sebastian, demanding it back.

The title of the Beautiful Wrestling Federation, Ric Beauty's faction inside the VWF, first awarded on 3 October 2001.
