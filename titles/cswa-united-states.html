CSWA United States Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   CSWA (1988)
Active   1988–2006

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

One of the three belts the fed built in its first year, and the one that changed hands most. Jay Youngblood won the founding tournament in 1988 and lost it to Hornet on the very next card; Safari Sakan took it off Mark Windham in 1989 and Windham won it straight back. Eleven years later it was still the belt a run got built on: Kevin Powers won a tournament for it at FISH FUND XII in 1998, Eli Flair took it in a Triangle match in January 1999, and Nemesis won it twice in the same year, the first time out of a Barbed Wire Ladder Match he came into with a concussion. Lawrence Stanley and Havoc carried it through 2000.

It is corroborated by two counts the fed keeps of him: FISH FUND XI of 6 September 1996 says the belt "is one that Mark Windham has held on at least four separate occasions", and CSWA PRIMETIME in New Orleans of 27 April 1997 bills him "a former four-time United States Heavyweight Champion" — four only if this reign is counted alongside the three the chain already carried.
