Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

fWo's World Championship: the earliest reign served here begins on 24 April 1998, held by Jim Dudley. The fed's own record carries 66 reigns on it; 50 of them, across 24 different champions, are served here so far. The longest of those is Erik Kelly's, 187 days from 27 April 2002. Five of them ended outside a match (stripped, vacated).
