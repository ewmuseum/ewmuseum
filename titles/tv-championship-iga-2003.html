Promotion   International Grappling Association (2001)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A silver belt on a white strap, defended at least three times a month. Maxime St. Jean won the first title match, a six-man elimination at The Battle for Fort Knox, and held it through the rest of 2001.
