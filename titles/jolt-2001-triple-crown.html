Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The 2005 site's top singles prize: three belts, the 2001 jOlt World Heavyweight Title, the 2002 jOlt World Championship and the new Triple Crown Title, decided by a tournament through February 2005. Brandon Youngblood won its final from Jeff Garvin at WrestleSault I on 27 February after fifty-four minutes; on the 15 March Tuesday Night Intense he threw the belt down to save Melissa Garvin from Garvin's Hammer Jammer, and the page ends asking whether he had forfeited it. Kenjiro Ito's own later biography cards say he won it on Jolt's last show.

Sources disagree on the last champion: Kenjiro Ito's PRIME card (/promotions/prime-2003/archive/kenjiro-ito-bio.html) and GCW card (/promotions/gcw-gcwonline-2001/archive/kenjiro-ito-bio.html) name him; fWo's own text of 21 September 2005 (/promotions/fwo-fans-1998/archive/fwo-fans-1998-disney-s-wednesday-warfare-2005-09-21.html) bills Carson Nash 'the last Jolt Triple Crown Champion'.
