Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A title of thReat's 2003 run. Vertigo won it from Phil Atken on Sunday Night Flaw 16; Phil Atken took it back at Malevolence 2003 over Vertigo and Tyler Davidson, and Tyler Davidson won it from Atken at Payback 2003 and was still champion when the title history ends.

thReat's title history (threat.theewe.net/cgi-bin/superstars.cgi?titlehistory), served at /promotions/threat/archive/threat-title-history.html.
