Active   2008

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The lowest-ranked singles belt, which the diary's characters call the least important in the promotion. Marc DuBois holds it through ladder matches against Enygma and Antonio Marquez until Darryl Devine wins it at The World is Watching. Darryl Devine keeps it through the ladder match with Enygma at Master of Puppets 2008 and on SWF Legends in June 2008, when Phil Vibert names it the title of a new Sky Club Division for the high-fliers. Devine keeps it in the three-way ladder match at Times of Trouble 2008 but loses it to Kid Toma on SWF Legends in June 2008, after the Samoan Family's interference; Toma keeps it against Brett Biggz and American Elemental and then against Jacob Jett, and the belt is not on the card of The Supreme Challenge 28. Toma keeps it against Remmy Skye on SWF Legends in Week 4 of July 2008, the night Eric Tyler hands him a redesigned belt, and Bart Biggz earns a shot for Welcome to the Jungle 2008 by beating Akima Brave by disqualification in Week 1 of August. Acid, a masked high-flier new to the SWF, pins Kid Toma in a Threefold Threat with Bart Biggz at Welcome to the Jungle 2008 and takes the belt; he keeps it against El Leon and Mainstream Hernandez on SWF Legends in Week 3 of August, Remmy Skye in Week 4 and John Greed and Mainstream Hernandez in Week 1 of September, while Bart Biggz asks for a singles match.
