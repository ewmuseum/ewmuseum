RUSH Network Championship

Promotion   RUSH (2002)

Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RUSH's Network Championship was first won by Marvin Harris on 2 May 2002. The fed's own title log carries 4 reigns on it, across 4 different champions, and ends with Livewire's, from 12 September 2002; PCW's cards of December 2002 and January 2003 carry a fifth, Matt Hunt's, won about 10 December 2002 and still held at Shanghaied III on 26 January 2003. Livewire's, of about three months, is the longest; Scott Cage's, 63 days from 16 June 2002, is the longest with both dates exact. Matt Hunt held it at the end of the surviving record.

No length is given for the previous reign or this one's start, because of the disagreement. Two defences are on record, neither in the fed's own title log. First, Adrenaline 18 (27 Sep 2002), announced "for the RUSH NETWORK TITLE": the billed challenger Stimpo never appeared and Joe Bananas attacked the champion instead; the card gives no result and none is claimed, and the belt did not change hands. Second, Rising From the Ashes (13 Oct 2002; the fed's own pay-per-view script, served as a document): Sheila Tyrone announces "the RUSH Network Title", Livewire beats Joe Bananas with the Electricity ("Winner: Livewire", "LIVEWIRE KEEPS THE NETWORK TITLE!"), and the fed's own newsdesk has it in Bananas's own words, "Ah lost ta Livewire at Rising From the Ashes". The reign ended after the fed's own record: the fed's own title log carries no later Network change and its own title list (about October 2002) still names him champion, but the PCW card of Fusion 101 (20 Dec 2002) has Matt Hunt walk out with "his newly acquired Network Title", saying he won it "last week on Turmoil", and the commentary calls Livewire "past his prime" (reign r005); how Livewire lost it is on no served card.

The fed's own title log, whose page dates from about October 2002, ends with Livewire's reign, before this one; this reign is on PCW's cards of December 2002 and January 2003. Four defences are on served cards: Turmoil 104 (17 Dec 2002, Matt Hunt (c) against Ariz Ona, Gideon Poe special referee, announced as "for the RUSH Network Championship"; Poe stops it when Hunt uses a foreign object, "This match is a no contest. DDT hangs on to his title."), Decimation 2002 (22 Dec 2002, a German rules Christmas ladder match against Gideon Poe, "THE WINNER AND STILL RUSH NETWORK CHAMPION…MATT HUNT!!!"), Turmoil 107 (7 Jan 2003, against Stimpo, a count-out win for Stimpo: "STILL NETWORK CHAMPION, MATT HUNT!") and Shanghaied III (26 Jan 2003, against Stimpo, "YOUR WINNER AND STILLLLL RUSH NETWORK CHAMPION.... MATTHIAS KUNTZ-HUNT!"). On Turmoil 108 (14 Jan 2003) he is introduced as "the RUSH Network Champion" against Ebola III, the Syndicate champion, in a match billed for neither belt. The card of Sunday Adrenaline, 19 Jan 2003, announces that Stimpo has "a big Network Title Match one week from tonight". No PCW card dated after Shanghaied III (26 Jan 2003), from the Turmoil of 28 Jan 2003 to the cards of April 2004, bills a RUSH Network or Syndicate title or names its champion. The reign therefore carries no end date and none is claimed.
