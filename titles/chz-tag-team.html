Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Millennium and Canon, the original Outlawz, were its first champions at Caged Fury 1 on 1 August 1999; Sex and Violence — Dirk Diggler and The Crow — won it five times across the CHZ, FWF and SWF years, trading it with the Detroit Boyz in the autumn of 1999 and retiring after their last reign in 2000. Boylan's Boys held it longest, from November 2000 to May 2001. In the New CHZ the Disciples of Thunder held it from May 2001 and Hell's Fury (Diablo and Fire) from July; Son's of Thunder (James and John, managed by Simon) won it from Hell's Fury in the fed's first tables match on 9 November 2001, and by 23 January 2002 the Disciples (Atlas and Mountain) are billed the reigning champions, with no show recording the change. The Disciples were fired and the titles went vacant on 1 March 2002, and the Midnight Wonders won them in a four-corners elimination match on the 13 March 2002 Wild; they are billed to defend against Soul Stormer and 'Psycho' Cody Hawk at NBK Pride, with no result surviving. The fed's June 2002 relaunch under a new owner listed a Tag Team Championship with no champions and crowned its own champions (see chz-tag-team-2002). The titles were reinstated in 2003 after a tournament to crown new winners: the AAOD, Lucifer and Diablo, won them by beating the Reaper and Archangel, billed Heaven and Hell, at May HayDay 2 on 14 May 2003 and kept them on 21 May.

The fed's own Tag Team title history (/promotions/chz/archive/tag-team-title-history.html) gives the Disciples of Thunder (2) one reign, 9 November 2001 to 1 March 2002 ("DOT was fired after a period of inactivity"), and dates the Midnight Wonders' win 18 March 2002; the 9 November 2001 Friday Night Wild crowns the Sons of Thunder and the 13 March 2002 Wild stages the four-corners match. Sources disagree. The 2003 reign is filed on this belt, not on the June 2002 relaunch's (chz-tag-team-2002): the new era that opened at BreakOut 2002 carried the fed's 2001-02 belts on (see chz-heavyweight's notes), and the 14 May 2003 booth calls the titles "reinstated" (/promotions/chz/archive/may-hayday-2-2003-05-14.html).
