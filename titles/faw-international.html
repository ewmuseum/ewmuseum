Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's secondary singles belt, and one of the three it kept when it cut its championship list down in 2003. Sean Mason defended it in Chris Thunder's own pay-per-view debut at Breakaway 2001; Jim ‘JLC’ Phoenix took it after eight months on the roster and called it the breakthrough of his career; Chris Thunder won it in a triple threat at Taking Hardcore To The Extreme in October 2001, in the match that started the xCw invasion, and lost it inside the month. Shifty held it in the summer of 2002. Sniper beat The British Bomber for it on the 6 July 2003 Panic and was still champion at the fed's last surviving snapshot that December.
