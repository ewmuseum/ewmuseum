Placard

Promotion   WIW:NG (1999)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own history opens the World Title on 17 May 1999, when Tap Out won a battle royal to become its first champion, and lists 36 reigns across its on-and-off eras to The End's of 18 August 2002. Michael J was its first three-time champion, forfeiting his third reign the next card; Shane Slade won it three times in 2002 and lost the last on 18 August. The November 2002 champions list names Chris Storm.
