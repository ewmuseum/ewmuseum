Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AWC's Relentless Championship was first won by Crimson O'Malec on 7 June 2005. The fed's own record carries 7 reigns on it, across 7 different champions. The longest is Red Rock's, 178 days from 7 March 2006. One of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. Jack Murphy held it at the end of the surviving record.

Crimson O'Malec7 June 20051 July 2005
Crimson O'Malec hit Pact in the head with his own kendo stick and pinned him in a four way to become the first Relentless Champion. A defence is on record. Fresh!, 21 June 2005: Crimson O'Malec reversed Christopher Ashton's Fisherman's suplex from the top turnbuckle into a roll-up to retain the Relentless Championship.
Steven Xandrous1 July 200519 August 2005
Steven Xandrous hit Crimson O'Malec with two chairs while referee Lars Larsson was down from a Hate chokeslam, then landed a senton bomb onto a chair and pinned him to win the Relentless Championship. A defence is on record. Fresh!, 9 August 2005: Steven Xandrous lifted The Farmer onto his shoulders for the Homicidal Wreckage and pinned him to retain the Relentless Championship.
Ian English19 August 200530 September 2005
Ian English, who had kept away from the fights, won the Giant Haystacks Invitational when a haystack fell on Steven Xandrous, the last man eliminated, and took the Relentless Championship.
Mike Wade30 September 200528 February 2006
Mike Wade, blinded by Ian English's Tea Mist, kicked him in the stomach and hit the TFW to pin him and win the Relentless Championship. Defences on record. Fresh!, 11 October 2005: Mike Wade hit James von Doom the Handler with the TFW into shards of glass on the canvas and pinned him to retain the Relentless Championship in the weapons match. Triangles 2005, 11 November 2005: Mike Wade kept the Relentless Championship at the top of the pyramid when the clock ran out in the Pyramid Scramble; Chainz, who held a Sharpshooter on Alexa Kendericks through the buzzer, was named the new number one contender. Fresh!, 6 December 2005: Mike Wade hit Hate with the TFW onto a steel chair and pinned him to retain the Relentless Championship. Winter Warfare 2005, 23 December 2005: Mike Wade clubbed Chainz on the back of the head with his mouth over the cup and filled it with his blood to win the Cup Of Blood match and retain the Relentless Championship. Bloodlust 2006, 17 February 2006: Mike Wade hit Paddy O'Shea with the TFW off the top rope onto the ring steps, then dragged him into the ring and pinned him in a Loser Leaves Town match, retaining the Relentless Championship and ending O'Shea's AWC contract. Mike Wade laid the Relentless Championship in the centre of the ring, held up his Triangles briefcase and said he would use it on Jack Murphy for the Transatlantic title; there was no match.
Red Rock7 March 20061 September 2006
Red Rock came out from under the boardwalk holding the belt after he threw Anton Assault back out, and won the vacant Relentless Championship in the six man Beneath The Boardwalk match. Defences on record. Twilight Of The Gods 2006, 24 March 2006: Red Rock and Vince Jones fell 25 feet through a second floor window with Red Rock still holding the RDT, and Red Rock rolled an arm over the unconscious Jones and pinned him to retain the Relentless Championship in the Back To School match. Fresh!, 16 May 2006: Red Rock wrapped a chain around Anton Assault's neck and swung him down head first with the RDT, then pinned him to retain the Relentless Championship. Fresh!special: Day Of The Devil, 6 June 2006: Red Rock pinned Chainz with a roll-up out of a sunset flip counter to retain the Relentless Championship in the Double Dog-Collar match, and Chainz caned him afterwards. Divide And Conquer 2006 (pay-per-view), 23 June 2006: Red Rock hit Jack Murphy with a kendo stick, landed a body splash from the top of a ladder and pulled him into a pin by his knee pad to retain the Relentless Championship. Fresh!special: Live On Huntington Beach, 1 August 2006: Red Rock hit Aimz with a rolling spinning heel kick and the Elevated Inverted DDT, the RDT, after a barbed wire bat match and pinned her to retain the Relentless Championship.
Gabriel Afeaki1 September 200624 October 2006
Gabriel Afeaki made Red Rock tap out to the Fijian Leg Lock in the Vortex disco on the ship, eliminating him from the East vs West match and taking the Relentless Championship. Defences on record. Fresh!, 19 September 2006: Gabriel Afeaki hit Captain Suleimon in the ankle with a chair and locked on the Fijian Leg Lock, and Suleimon tapped out, so Afeaki retained the Relentless Championship. Untouchable 2006, 13 October 2006: Gabriel Afeaki kept kicking a downed Teresa Tomas in the ribs, referee Lars Larsson stopped the match and it was declared a no contest, so Afeaki kept the Relentless Championship.
Jack Murphy24 October 2006—
Jack Murphy dropped a shield on Gabriel Afeaki's face and double stomped it from the top rope, with his feet on the ropes for the pin, to win the Relentless Championship. Defences on record. Fresh!, 7 November 2006: Jack Murphy landed on Red Rock's arms after a missed top rope drop and pinned him to retain the Relentless Championship. Fresh!, 14 November 2006: Jack Murphy hit Teresa Tomas with the Fall From Grace after the man in grey hit her with a chair from under the apron, and pinned her to retain the Relentless Championship. Triangles 2006, 24 November 2006: Jack Murphy locked in the STF on Darcy Crisis after Crisis kicked out at two, and Crisis tapped out, so Murphy retained the Relentless Championship.
