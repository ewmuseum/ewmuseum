A classic-era-only mid-card title (2005-2010), never revived after the 2010 closure as far as the surviving record shows. "Dirty" Don Travelli held it across at least two separate reigns per the served corpus, trading it with Lobo; the fed's own Sept 2010 rankings sidebar confirms Chris Casino as champion at the classic era's own close, though the hand-off from Travelli's own last confirmed reign (Apr 2006) isn't in the held corpus — years of real, unreconstructed gap.
