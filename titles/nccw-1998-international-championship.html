Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own secondary heavyweight title, defended more often than the World Titles: "GQ" Geoffrey Quartz won it at the fed's own first dated show, and Purple Pride is its own last recorded champion, winning it eight days before the fed's own record breaks off.
