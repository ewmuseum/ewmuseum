AICW World Championship

aka AICW World Heavyweight Championship, American Internet Championship Wrestling World Championship

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned in an eight-man ladder match at Apocalypse '97, where "The Demon" Kyle Bane became the fed's own first-ever World Champion. "Hard Line" Byron Brady dethroned him in a steel cage at New Year's Revolution; a masked debutant billed only as The Saint won it from Bane a second time at the Tokyo Thunder Festival, in a match decided by Byron Brady's own disguised interference as the Demonweb's "Legion"; Brady reclaimed it from The Saint at Steel City Meltdown, the belt's own last recorded change in the surviving record.

The belt's own name drifts between "World Championship" and "World Heavyweight Championship" across the held record without the fed itself ever marking a rename; recorded as one continuous lineage.
