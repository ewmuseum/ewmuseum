Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Made in early 1996 out of the North American title's own abolition — a four-way match for a new belt, on a night the federation said it wanted to be more competitive — and then the most-travelled championship in the surviving record: three changes in thirty-six days, to a visiting EWF light heavyweight at Leprecharnage, to a masked former world champion three weeks later in three minutes, and to a trumpet-carrying former light heavyweight champion three weeks after that.

The federation's own account of the four-way that created the belt contradicts itself inside a single sentence — it names Haplo as the reigning Television champion at the time AND Justice D. Nyde as the current World Television champion, then says Haplo retained — while every other document bills Nyde as the champion going into Leprecharnage. The contradiction is recorded rather than resolved, and no reign is written for that match.
