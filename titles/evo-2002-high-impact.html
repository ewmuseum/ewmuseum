Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

eVo's High Impact Championship was first won by Quasar the Great on 14 August 2002. The fed's own record carries 5 reigns on it, across 5 different champions. The longest is Thug roC's, 63 days from 9 October 2002. Two of its reigns ended outside a match — retired with the belt or stripped from the holder. eVo unified it with the Rising Star Championship into the eVolved Championship on 15 January 2003.

Thug roC strips Scott Frost of the High Impact Championship under the threat of security guards, and Frost throws the belt over the top rope; a match between Seara and Lee eXodus is booked for it.
