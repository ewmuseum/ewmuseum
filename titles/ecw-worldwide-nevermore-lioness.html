Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The first Lioness Championship, crowned at Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 in a Lioness' Den Match between Jazz, Kelly Thomaselli and Alexis Laree: Alexis Laree wins by rolling up Kelly Thomaselli and hooking her tights, and the diary's own title history lists her as the champion, 'present'. She keeps the belt on Revolution and Blood, Sweat and Beers over Daizy Dudley, Beulah McGillicutty, Traci and Mika Akino, and at Guilty As Charged over Jazz when a new member of Raven's Nest, Nattie Neidhart, runs in and the two of them hit a Double DDT; she is still the champion when the pages of October 2007 end. In November 2007 she and Nathalie lose a Lioness Pack Match to Jazz and Kelly Thomaselli, and on the last Revolution before November To Remember XV she pins Jazz in a non-title match after Nathalie hits Jazz with the belt; Kelly Thomaselli challenges her for the title, and the diary's preview of the card bills Kelly Thomaselli against Alexis, the champion. At November To Remember XV Alexis beats Kelly Thomaselli in the Lioness match with Nathalie's belt shot, and on Revolution and the Saturday show of the first week of December over Mika Akino; Kong, back 'from APW' with Prince Nana, flattens her with a Sit-out Powerbomb, and the diary's next preview books Kong and Akino against Alexis and Nathalie.

Title reigns

Alexis Laree28 September 2007—Alexis Laree wins the first Lioness' Den Match at Anarchy Rulz against Jazz and Kelly Thomaselli: Jazz kicks out of two Alexis Effects, hits her Fisherman Brainbuster on a chair, and Kelly Thomaselli breaks up the cover and hits Jazz with the chair; Alexis rolls Kelly up and hooks her tights for the three, and Joey Styles calls it 'underhanded'. She keeps the belt over Daizy Dudley on Blood, Sweat and Beers, over Beulah McGillicutty and Traci on Revolution and Blood, Sweat and Beers, and over Jazz at Guilty As Charged, when Nattie Neidhart, the 'anonymous blonde', runs in and the two hit a Double DDT; she is still the champion when the pages of October 2007 end. In November 2007 Jazz and Kelly Thomaselli beat Alexis and Nathalie in a Lioness Pack Match, Alexis jumps Kelly after Kelly beats Nathalie, and on the last Revolution before November To Remember XV Alexis pins Jazz in a non-title match after Nathalie hits Jazz with the belt and the two land a Double DDT onto it; Kelly then clears both with the belt and challenges for the title, and the diary's preview of the card bills Kelly Thomaselli against Alexis, the champion. At November To Remember XV Alexis beats Kelly Thomaselli in the Lioness match (the preview bills it with Alexis as the champion) with an Alexis Effect when Nathalie slides in with the Lioness belt and hits Kelly from behind, then Alexis and Nathalie beat Kelly down until Jazz clears them (C). On Revolution on the first Monday of December she beats Mika Akino with an Alexis Effect (D+) and on the Saturday show again (E+, 5:42); Kong, back 'from APW' with Prince Nana, then flattens her with a bodycheck and a Sit-out Powerbomb. The diary's next preview books Kong and Akino against Alexis and Nathalie in a Lioness Pack Match.
The history lists the reign as 'present', so no end date is filed: the diary's record ends with her still the champion.
