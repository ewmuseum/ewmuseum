A hub-wide hardcore championship of the National eWrestling Alliance, defended from February 1999. Six reigns survive in this collection's own record; the last, Frag Sinister from October 1999, is this title's own final attested champion -- also the hub's own "Roll Call of Champions" naming him current as of November 1999, with no later document naming a successor.

Source: the hub's own title-history page. No claimed real-world lineage.
