Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Picked up in February 2004 when Pres Burns, having acquired the CSW Efed outright, stripped the belt from its last independent-era champion and folded it into his own CWF-Pro hierarchy — the independent-era lineage (fourteen reigns, Mercury through Tom Cage) is held on the CSW wing's own title row. Scott Nash became its first CWF-Pro-era champion, defeating Kevin Hall in a tournament final; the fed's own numbered ledger records Billy Lee as its last holder, unifying the CSW National Title with the CWF-Pro Intercontinental Title.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 16 reigns in full across both eras; only the two post-acquisition reigns are structured here (the fourteen independent-era reigns are structured on the CSW wing's own title row, csw-national-title) — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/csw-national-title.html for the complete numbered ledger.
