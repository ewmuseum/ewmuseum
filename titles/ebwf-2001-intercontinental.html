EBWF Intercontinental Championship

Placard

Promotion   EBWF (2002)
Active   2002–2008
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

HHH opened it on 11 April 2002 and held it five separate times through December 2002, more than anyone else took it. Vampiro's four reigns run from November 2004 to February 2007, the last of them won in a tournament after the belt had been vacated. Christian took it three times and Randy Orton three, and Bret Hart held it twice in 2003. The Miz's win at Death Before Dishonor on 25 May 2008 closes its own record, and the promotion's own Champions page still names him champion.
