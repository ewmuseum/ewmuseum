Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The federation's top belt, and the one it opened with: its own ledger's first two matches are a double-elimination tournament, and the winner of the first was awarded the World Championship while the loser took the United States title as consolation. Five reigns follow across the ledger's own sixty-eight matches. Doctor Death took it first and lost it to a man who hit him with a stick; Annihilus won it by submission and lost it to an inside cradle; Titan Morgan held it until Death took it back with the Vulcan Death Grip on 15 April 1992, the one change of it the surviving record holds a report of. By January 1993 the federation was billing its holder as "PWA/FWF World Heavyweight Champion" — the belt had been unified with another federation's at the Clash of the Masters, and Death had won that too.

Transcribed from the federation's own singles ledger (promotions/fwf-1992/archive/fwf-singles-record-1993-01-08.html).
