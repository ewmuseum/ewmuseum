Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The junior tag belts, held by Carnage into the 1992 hiatus and still recognised at the relaunch. Carried back from the WeWF wing, whose own newsdesk This Week in Wrestling printed this federation's weekly title results and full rankings through the winter of 1992. The Armed Forces hold this belt at 10-1-0 in the newsdesk's own standings of 30 January 1992, with Carnage ranked first behind them; Carnage hold it at 8-2-0 by 15 February and defend it twice on the record, over the Annihilation Alliance on 19 February and over the Ruffians on 25 February. An earlier reign this ledger did not carry is now registered.

Declared in the federation's own founding document, 19 November 1991 (promotions/uewf/archive/new-pbem-the-uewf-1991-11-19.html), which sets out how it was to be defended. Holders transcribed from the federation's own relaunch call, 14 May 1992. Nothing in the Summit's own record bears on when this reign began. The weekly record survives second-hand, in another federation's own newsdesk, not yet served here. Nine editions of the WeWF's own This Week in Wrestling and its TTTTTT tag tournament, 30 January to 31 March 1992, plus the EMWA's own Kings Of The Ring of 25 March, are held in this project's own Google Groups dumps and are unserved. Between them they carry this federation's own weekly title results week by week, its full ranking tables with win-loss records and points, and two of its own matches staged play-by-play. What they say about THIS belt: Carnage hold it throughout — defending against the Annihilation Alliance on 19 February 1992 and against the Ruffians on the 25th — and the WeWF's own TTTTTT tournament of 24 March 1992 still bills them into its ring as "the UeWF InterContinental Tag Team Champions, CARNAGE!" Nothing in the held record says when the reign began.
