TCW: A Quiet Retirement TCW World Heavyweight Championship

Active   2008

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

TCW's top belt, 'a prettier piece of gold the industry doesn't have', defended last on the card. Tommy Cornell holds it when the diary opens and through the first ten shows, beating Troy Tornado at Where Angels Fear To Tread and losing to him only by disqualification at Total Mayhem 13. Troy Tornado takes it from Cornell at Excessive Force in June 2008 with the Star Maker, ending Cornell's reign. Troy Tornado keeps it through July 2008, beating Chris Rockwell and Wolf Hawkins in a three-way match at Summer Showdown with the Star Maker. Troy Tornado keeps it through August 2008: at Hotter Than Hell he beats Brent Hill and Wolf Hawkins in a three-way match, pinning Hawkins after the Star Maker. Troy Tornado is still champion at the end of September 2008: he beats Tyson Baine, Sammy Bach in a ladder match and Chris Rockwell and Eddie Peak, and at Destructive Energy, in a three-way match with Johnny Bloodstone and Tommy Cornell, he and Bloodstone together make Cornell submit; the match ends with both men celebrating, Mayhem Midden saying he will have to talk to the Board, and the commentary saying only that Cornell is not the World Heavyweight Champion. The diary now shows two belts in October 2008: Tommy Cornell walks off with the belt Mayhem Midden is carrying, the Board bars him from title matches for six months, and Troy Tornado and Johnny Bloodstone each appear with a new custom belt, the prediction key billing Tornado as the Standing Champion and Bloodstone as champion of the Canadian Belt. At Crunch Time Tornado keeps his belt, beating Jeremy Stone and Remo Richardson in a three-way match with the Star Maker, and Bloodstone keeps the Canadian Belt, beating Brent Hill with the Bloodstone Mutilation. The Board recognises both belts as championships in November 2008: Mayhem Midden announces that Troy Tornado's and Johnny Bloodstone's belts are recognised, with the proviso that TCW cannot have two World Heavyweight Champions, and orders the two men, or whoever has beaten them for a belt in the meantime, to meet in the Malice in Wonderland main event in January to unify the belts. At A Little South Of Sanity Tornado, barring Tommy Cornell from a title shot, beats him in a non-title match with the Star Maker, and Bloodstone keeps the Canadian Belt, beating Chris Rockwell in a half-hour match. The two belts stay apart through December 2008, the Board's edict that they be unified at Malice in Wonderland in January standing: Johnny Bloodstone keeps the Canadian Belt, beating Edd Stone and, in a non-title match, Merle O'Curle, and Troy Tornado, as Triple-Threat Champion, picks the men for a six-man match whose winner takes the third place in his defence. At Psycho Circus, after Tommy Cornell hits Tornado and Eddie Peak with the retired TCW belt, Tornado beats Peak and Jeremy Stone in a three-way match with the Star Maker, and the diary's preview makes the winner of that match the man who goes on to face Bloodstone at Malice in Wonderland to unify the belts.

Title reigns

Troy Tornado2008-06 (month)—Won from Tommy Cornell at Excessive Force, coming off a failed top-rope attack with the Star Maker, after Painful Procedure ringed the match to cut off Syndicate help; Edd Stone's missile dropkick from the Hangtime structure and a Savate Kick from Steve Gumble on Cornell came first. He keeps it through July 2008, beating Chris Rockwell and Wolf Hawkins in a three-way match at Summer Showdown with the Star Maker. He keeps it through August 2008, beating Brent Hill and Wolf Hawkins in a three-way match at Hotter Than Hell. He is still champion at the end of September 2008; at Destructive Energy, in a three-way match with Johnny Bloodstone and Tommy Cornell, he and Bloodstone together make Cornell submit, and Mayhem Midden says he must take the result to the Board. He keeps it through October 2008, carrying a new custom belt as the Standing Champion, beating Sammy Bach & Wolf Hawkins with Koshiro Ino and drawing with Sam Keith against Jeremy Stone & Tommy Cornell; at Crunch Time he beats Jeremy Stone and Remo Richardson in a three-way match with the Star Maker. He keeps it through November 2008: on Total Wrestling in the first week the Board's edict recognises his belt and Johnny Bloodstone's and orders the two unified at Malice in Wonderland in January; he beats Jeremy Stone with the Star Maker, beats Tommy Cornell & Tyson Baine with Rick Law and loses a six-man match with John Anderson and Johnny Bloodstone to Chris Rockwell, Tommy Cornell & Wolf Hawkins; at A Little South Of Sanity, with the belt not at stake, he beats Tommy Cornell with the Star Maker. He keeps it through December 2008, drawing with Eric Tyler in twenty minutes, fighting Tommy Cornell to a no contest when Jack Bruce returns and, at Psycho Circus, beating Eddie Peak and Jeremy Stone in a three-way match with the Star Maker after Cornell has attacked him and Peak with the retired TCW belt.
Johnny Bloodstone2008-10 (month)—Holder of the second World Heavyweight Championship belt the diary shows from October 2008, the Canadian Belt, after he and Troy Tornado together made Tommy Cornell submit at Destructive Energy and the Board left both men with a claim; the prediction key bills him '(c)'. He keeps it at Crunch Time, beating Brent Hill with the Bloodstone Mutilation, and the diary says the belts will be reunited sooner or later. He keeps it through November 2008: on Total Wrestling in the first week the Board's edict recognises his belt and Troy Tornado's and orders the two unified at Malice in Wonderland in January; he beats Sean Deeley on Badge of Honor, beats the Elite Express with Sam Keith and loses a six-man match with John Anderson and Troy Tornado to Chris Rockwell, Tommy Cornell & Wolf Hawkins; at A Little South Of Sanity he beats Chris Rockwell after half an hour, pinning him in an improvised pin attempt. He keeps it through December 2008, beating Edd Stone with the Bloodstone Mutilation on Total Wrestling and Merle O'Curle in a non-title match on Badge of Honor; the diary's Psycho Circus preview treats the match that decides who faces him at Malice in Wonderland as a title unification match.
Tommy Cornellunknown2008-06 (month)Champion when the diary opens, and still champion after Total Mayhem 13, where Troy Tornado wins by disqualification; the diary puts his win about seventeen months before May 2008 in one place and fifteen months before April 2008 in another, and dates it nowhere. He loses it to Troy Tornado at Excessive Force in June 2008, when Tornado catches him coming off the top rope with the Star Maker.
