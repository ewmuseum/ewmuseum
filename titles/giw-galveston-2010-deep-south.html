Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's Deep South Championship: 16 reigns are served here, across 15 different champions, the first beginning on 24 January 2011 with Madison Seton and the last on 30 August 2015 with Geneva Lost. The longest of those is Christopher Darren Erns's, 99 days from 22 July 2014. Of these, 5 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). It was unified into the Undisputed Championship on 17 February 2011, retired at the 2013 closure, brought back in 2014, unified again on 29 October 2014 and brought back once more on 15 March 2015.
