AW Heavyweight Championship

aka EWA Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   AW (Anarchy, 2008)
Active   2007–2010

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The top belt, listed as Tier I. Kaige won it in a three-way at HalloWicked 2K7 and Alexander Caine vacated it on 13 December 2007. The fed calls Morrigan, who won it in the All Nite Action 1 cell on 29 July 2008, its first champion since Kaige; Jason Lovell took it at Cold Fall on 21 September 2008. The 2010 page lists it vacant on 1 January 2010 and Dave Wilson champion from the Rabbithole of Idus Martias.
