Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt built out of three other belts and then folded into a fourth. The AAWC merged three of its own lesser championships into a Unified Championship, and Hornet took it from Mike Randalls at the fed's own World War Games in March 1995 — a win GUNS answered by attacking him, which is where a three-year feud across four promotions started. Two months later the AAWC merged this title with its own World Championship at WrestleFest '95 to crown a single Universal Champion, in a round robin the surviving account describes as GUNS, Hornet, Dr. Silver and Flic Rair, the first man to beat his three opponents in consecutive matches taking the belt. Nobody else is recorded as holding it.

Attested by a CSWA retrospective on the Hornet/GUNS feud (promotions/cswa/archive/cswa-spotlight-hornet-vs-guns-1998.html), which describes it as "created from a previous merge of three of the AAWC's lesser titles" and records its own end: at WrestleFest '95 it was merged with the AAWC World Title to create the AAWC Universal Championship.
