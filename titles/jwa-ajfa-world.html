Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Keith Wildwing won the title in the fed's own debut PPV, "And Justice For All" (22 August 1999) — a twenty-man battle royal feeding into a ladder match, its own closing pinfall lost to the surviving transcript, but confirmed by "Blind Justice"'s own opening recap package, which shows Wildwing "his body tired and worn" holding the belt. That same recap frames Jon Clark as having then taken the title from Wildwing off-page — a reign this ledger does not structure, since no document dates or stages it directly. At "Blind Justice" itself, Bryan Mercy outlasted Clark in a Triple Threat Ladder Match to become champion, and successfully defended the belt against War at "Dark Justice" (14 November 1999) — the held record's last-attested title change. Two reigns anchor the ledger here: the founding and the last-attested.
