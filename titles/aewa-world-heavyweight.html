Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The AEWA's own top belt changed hands six times in 1997 alone. Shane Matthews carried it into the year off a battle-royal win the previous November, lost it to Deranged on a chair shot in January, and watched Deranged lose it to "Crimson" Joe Reed at the promotion's own Kickoff '97. Reed's first reign lasted less than two months: Heavy Metal took it off him in a four-way at AEWA Extreme in April, and was himself dethroned within weeks by "Superman" Mike Stewart in a match the AEWA's own record does not carry at all — it survives only as footage replayed on a rival federation's pay-per-view. Reed took the belt back from Stewart at Battle Klash II that summer, in a show that briefly made him champion of two federations at once, and was still champion in the fed's own newsletter as late as 30 November 1997. A rival's own Top 200 write-up records that Randall Osbourne finally ended that reign sometime in 1998, past the edge of the wing's own 1997 record.

Six lineage changes traced through 1997 from the fed's own broadcasts and newsletters; the top belt of the promotion, defended on both of its 1997 pay-per-views.
