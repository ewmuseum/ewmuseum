Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Continued the same TTW-era lineage as UPW's other belts, dark since TTW's own 30 November 2015 closure until the Warriors — DeAwn Carter and Kel Robertson — won it in the main event of UPW's very first Showdown. Soldiers of the Sun took it from them in a Fatal Four-Way at Survival and held it through the rest of the promotion's own life.

The served ledger (world-tag-team-title-history.html) also carries an unbroken TTW-era reign chain back to Slamfest I, May 2011; those reigns are not individually registered, for the same reason given on the World Heavyweight Championship's own row.
