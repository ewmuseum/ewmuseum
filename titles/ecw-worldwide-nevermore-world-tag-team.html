Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The pair of belts the Dudley Boys hold in the diary's first ten shows, in a reign its own title history dates from 23 May 2006 to 2 August 2006. They beat the Hardy Boys for them on the first show and again on the eighth, and the ring announcer bills both as title matches. The Hardy Boys win the belts in the opening match of the first July post, with Brock Lesnar's help, and the Dudley Boys win them back for what the commentary calls their tenth reign; after Heatwave Raven and Paul Burchill take them, and Big Stevie Cool and Hollywood Nova take them from Raven and Burchill. The Blue World Order keep the belts through the autumn, beating Burchill and Abyss at Anarchy Rulz, until Abyss and Goliath, as Monsters Inc., take them from the bWo at Guilty As Charged. The Whole F**kin' Show, Rob Van Dam and Shawn Michaels, take the belts from Monsters Inc. at November To Remember on 26 November 2006 by the diary's title history, keep them against Monsters Inc. at Got Blood? and on Revolution in February, and are still champions when the pages of February 2007 end. The Iron Saints, Salvatore and Brandon Thomaselli, take the belts from the Whole F**kin' Show at Living Dangerously on 28 February 2007 by the diary's title history, with a Spike Powerbomb on Shawn Michaels after Rob Van Dam steps off the apron, and lose them at Baptism Of Fire on 29 March 2007 in the first Halflife Handicap match, when Rob Van Dam and Shawn Michaels win with a Five Star Frog Splash on Salvatore; the Whole F**kin' Show are champions when the pages of April 2007 end. At Cyberslam the Whole F**kin' Show keep the belts over Los Increibles in the Ladder Match when Rob Van Dam pulls them down; at Hardcore Heaven they keep them over the Canadian Connection, who won the three-way dance for the number one contendership at Cyberslam, when Shawn Michaels, bloodied and alone while Rob Van Dam runs to help Traci, pins Christian with a third Sweet Chin Music; the champions quarrel through June but are still champions when the pages of June 2007's second week end. At Wrestlepalooza in June 2007 the Whole F**kin' Show keep the belts over the Hardcore Innovators with simultaneous covers; the Dudley Boyz, returned to ECW, win the number one contendership, and at Summer Assylum the champions beat them in the Tables Match with Shawn Michaels's Sweet Chin Music through a table; they are still champions when the pages of August 2007's first week end, with the history's 26 October 2007 date for the reign's end still ahead. After Heatwave in August 2007 the Whole F**kin' Show attack the new holders of the Dudleyville Hardcore Titles, the Hardcore Innovators; in September they keep the belts over the Dudley Boyz on Revolution, and the Innovators ask for a Champions versus Champions match at Anarchy Rulz, with the history's 26 October 2007 date for the reign's end still ahead. At Guilty As Charged in October 2007 the Dudley Boyz win the Ladders, Chairs, Canes and Flaming Tables Three Way Dance from the Whole F**kin' Show, whose partners fall out when Shawn Michaels's Super Kick takes down Rob Van Dam, and from the Hardcore Innovators; the history lists the Dudley Boyz's reign, their ninth, from 26 October to 4 November 2007. In November 2007 the Innovators still carry all four belts to the ring; Paul Heyman announces the Undisputed ECW Tag Team belts and a match at November To Remember XV in which the Dudley Boyz and the Hardcore Innovators unify the titles, and the diary's preview of the card bills that match with the Dudley Boyz as the champions. At November To Remember XV (30 November 2007 in the history) the Dudley Boyz beat the Hardcore Innovators and raise the Undisputed ECW Tag Team Titles, the belts the diary calls the first unified ones; on Revolution on the first Monday of December the titles change hands in a restarted Three Way Dance: Slyk Tayshaun Dudley and Big Dick Dudley Jr. win them when Slyk makes Bubba Ray tap to the Boston Crabs, and they keep them over Thomaselli-Thomaselli '08 on the Saturday show. The history lists the Second Generation of Dudleyz as the champions from 4 November 2007, a date that the text does not agree with: the order of the shows is the text's, the dates are the history's.
