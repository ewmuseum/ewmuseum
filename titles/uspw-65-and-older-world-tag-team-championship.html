Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The pair of belts The Towers of Power hold until the third show of the diary. They pass to The Dudez!!!1! in January 2007, then to the Canadian Crusties a week later, who defend them against the Lords of War in March. The Dudez!!!1! win the belts back in a three-way match on the third show of March 2007 and keep them against The Demons of Rage and Veteran Mentality, with another defense against the Demons of Rage set for the first Family Night. The Dudez!!!1! keep the belts in June 2007 against the Demons of Rage at the first Family Night and Veteran Mentality at the fourth; Trent Shaffer then carries the belts off after a chair attack, and the diary records no change of champions. The Dudez!!!1! beat Trent Shaffer and Chance Fortune, billed as the dudes, at the Family Night of Thursday, Week 3, July 2007 and take the belts back from the pair, and beat them again in August. Team Seduction, Human Arsenal and Fumihiro Ota with Seduction, take the belts from The Dudez!!!1! at Summer of Love in August 2007; the Dudez are given a rematch, set as a ladder match for Blaze of Glory. Team Seduction keep the belts against The Dudez!!!1! in a ladder match at Blaze of Glory in September 2007; Veteran Mentality are named the #1 contenders for A Haunting in America. Team Seduction keep the belts against Veteran Mentality at A Haunting in America in October 2007. The Valentines take the belts from Team Seduction at Pilgrimage in November 2007 when Triple D turns on Human Arsenal, and lose them minutes later to Divinity's new members The Hotshots, who keep them against The Valentines on the Monday of Week 3. The Hotshots keep the belts against Veteran Mentality on the Thursday of Week 4, November 2007 and against The Dudez!!!1!!! on the Thursday of Week 1, December 2007; Maxmillion Productions are booked against them at Winter Epiphany.
