Unified into   XCWF InterUnited Championship 30 June 2002 — Menace, Intercontinental Champion since 28 April 2002, won the InterUnited Championship's own inaugural match on this date; the InterUnited title-history page names its own first match a unification bout, and this is the reign that carries forward.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Won first by Da Masta on Total HaVok, and carried by Mimic into a two-out-of-three-falls ladder match at Desperation that Kamikazi took in the deciding fall after a masked ally shoved the champion from the ladder. Menace won it in a tournament final in Cancun and carried it into the InterUnited Championship's own creation two months later; PTC's own dated profile of the fed names a different Intercontinental Champion for the week before that, a disagreement the surviving record does not resolve.
