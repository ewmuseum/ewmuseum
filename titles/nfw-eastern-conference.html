The prize for winning NFW's own Eastern half of the 2007 Ultratitle playoffs, crowned by the ring announcer on the spot rather than staged as a title with a history behind it — the fed's own text does not say whether the belt existed before this show or who, if anyone, held it previously. Yori Yakamo Jr. no-sold a second Kooter Cruise chokeslam, bodyslammed him onto Felix Red, and pinned Red with the Yori=Awesome to become the first and, in the surviving record, only NFW Eastern Conference Champion, earning the berth against the Western Conference's own champion at Wrestlebowl.

Crowned once in the surviving record, on NFW's own served card of 15 March 2007, feeding the Ultratitle Final at Wrestlebowl II.
