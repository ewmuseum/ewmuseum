AGW World Title

Placard

Promotion   AGW (1997)
Active   1997–2000

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AGW's flagship championship, first won 7 October 1997 at the fed's own founding and defended through 26 recorded reigns before the ledger's own last word, Mark Payne's reign beginning 30 January 2000, running into the fed's own hiatus. Kid Nitro's own reign ended not by a loss but by his death in a quadrangle Bridge match in the EEMWF, vacating the belt (the fed's own title-history ledger, footnote 2) -- the one reign this ledger records ending in kayfabe death rather than defeat.
