Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The promotion's top title changed hands controversially again and again across this collection's own holdings: won, vacated by presidential decree, won again in a rematch marred by the same interference, won a year later in a battle royal, won again from a returning ex-convict in a riot that collapsed the ring, and taken one last time in the promotion's own final match.

Source: DWO Wrestle Mayhem (30 Nov 1996, part 3) and DWO Holiday Bash (24 Dec 1996, parts 1 and 5).
