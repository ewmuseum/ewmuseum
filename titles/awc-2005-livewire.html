Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AWC's Livewire Championship was first won by The Educator on 5 July 2005. The fed's own record carries 6 reigns on it, across 6 different champions. The longest is The Educator's, 49 days from 5 July 2005. One of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. AWC disbanded it on 24 December 2005 and reinstated it in September 2006. Scott Taylor held it at the end of the surviving record.

The Educator5 July 200523 August 2005
The Educator took the AWC Frontier and Livewire belts from a drawer in David Harber's office at the end of Fresh!; there was no match. Defences on record. Fresh!, 2 August 2005: Pearl agrees in The Steal Deal to make The Educator's Livewire reign official, backdated to the day he took the belt, in return for the AWC Frontier belt. The Battle Of Britain 2005, 19 August 2005: The Educator hit Kris Krimzon with Detention, a jumping DDT from the turnbuckle, and pinned him to retain the Livewire Championship in a three way, with Jonny Sake out cold on the outside.

Steven Xandrous pinned Andy M. with a lateral press after Murray dropped to the mat, winning the first fall and the Livewire Championship from The Educator, who was absent from the fall.
Red Rock13 September 20051 November 2005
Red Rock reversed Steven Xandrous's headlock into a bridging back suplex and pinned him early to win the Livewire Championship. Defences on record. Fresh!burst, 25 September 2005: Red Rock low-blowed Jonny Sake while the referee was clearing a chair, then hit the RDT, a running spinning heel kick into an elevated DDT, and pinned him to retain the Livewire Championship. Testimony 2005, 30 September 2005: Red Rock put The British Bomber in a sleeper hold and hit the RDT through a table to win the table match and retain the Livewire Championship.
Jack Murphy1 November 200529 November 2005
Jack Murphy pinned Butterfly Hamada, then Red Rock with a school-boy roll-up to take the Livewire Championship, and finished The British Bomber with the Fall From Grace to win the four way elimination match. A defence is on record. Fresh!, 22 November 2005: Jack Murphy hit Alexa Kendericks with a Bull Charge that sent her off the apron into the guardrail while Chainz held her leg, and she was counted out, so Murphy retained the Livewire Championship. Jack Murphy declared the Livewire belt his own Platinum Title, said no one could strip it, and dropped the Livewire championship at David Harber's feet; there was no match.
Johnny Lexicon13 October 200624 November 2006
Johnny Lexicon broke up Jack Murphy's cover on AgentDash, hit Murphy with a DDT and then pinned AgentDash to win the Livewire Championship in a triple threat match. A defence is on record. Fresh!, 7 November 2006: Johnny Lexicon hit Blue Ruin with an elbow drop from the top rope and the Pattern Perfect for the third fall to retain the Livewire Championship in the first to three match.

Scott Taylor hit AgentDash with a charging super kick and pinned him, the first successful pin in the green triangle after Johnny Lexicon was disqualified, to win the Livewire Championship.
