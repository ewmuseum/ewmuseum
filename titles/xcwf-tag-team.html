Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Held first by the Immature Playaz and defended, vacated and re-vacated across thirteen reigns — a team named Total HaVok shares its own name with the fed's March pay-per-view, a Tag Team Turmoil Match crowned Shannon York and Unfitt Survivor after the belts went dark, and Predator once held them as a solo champion after beating Donovan Simmons in the straps' own first singles defense. Big 'N' Bad were the fed's last champions the ledger records, having already held the belts once before that summer.
