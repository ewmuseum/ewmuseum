Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened February 2006 when D40 (Jones & D-Bo) won a battle royal eliminating the Bad Asses to become the DTW brand's own 1st World Tag Team Champions. Mac Daddy & Daddy Mac hold it four times across 17 recorded reigns, the rest of the field otherwise bare team names with no bio page behind them. The Insyders close the ledger's own account, defeating Two White Boys to unify the DTW World Tag Team Titles with the CWF-Pro World Tag Team Titles.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 17 reigns in full; all 17 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/dtw-world-tag-team-title.html for the underlying primary source.
