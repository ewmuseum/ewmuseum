Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The newsdesk's own singles belt, wrestled for across federation lines: Mr. B held it into 1992 and kept it on a disqualification he was himself the subject of, the Hangman of the IWF took it from him with his own noose on 15 February, and defended it against the UeWF, the WeWF and a bout the championship committee annulled.
