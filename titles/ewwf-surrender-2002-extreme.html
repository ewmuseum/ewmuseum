Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

First attested champion Despair won the title from Randy Love in a parking-lot ladder match on 16 April 2002. He lost it around 10 July to Homeless Joe Boxcar (shown only in a later recap) and regained it the same night, 17 July, after Trinity interference. Ashlyn Summers held it undocumented by 8 August, retaining in a hardcore match that night and again at Kingdom of the Wicked; Harvester of Sorrow held it undocumented by 5 December, having already assaulted Summers before that broadcast.

The fed's own broadcasts also call this belt the “Surrender Extreme Championship” and, coarsely, the “Sex Championship”/“EWWF SEX Championship” — the same title under three names in the promotion's own words, confirmed explicitly in its own commentary (“she holds the Surrender Extreme Title...the SEX title”); the registry uses its fullest attested name.
