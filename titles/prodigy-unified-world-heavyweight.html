CSWA PRODIGY Unified World Championship

Placard

Promotion   CSWA (1988)
Active   1993–2007

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Not one federation's belt but the whole board's, and the CSWA is where its record survives. It began as the CSWA's own Unified Championship, built up across 1992 by Hornet challenging the champions of the other leagues in the Enterprise Superleague, and on 24 October 1993 the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS at the CSWA's LAST CALL II merged the IWCCW World, the AAWC World and that ten-title CSWA belt into one. Over the next three months Hornet added the UWL Superleague's half and the AR Superleague's title to it. It was retired and re-won twice — a sixteen-man tournament in 1994 that ended with a masked man unmasking himself as Mark Windham, a 128-man ULTRATITLE in 1996 that made Paul Pierce champion — and by 1997 the fed's own page describes it as more than forty championships merged into one and sanctioned by PRODIGY. A five-member committee decided its matches on votes for roleplay quality. Mike Randalls won the last of it on 21 January 1997, the last reign the CSWA's own ledger records. A different fed's broadcast, New Frontier Wrestling's own Wrestlebowl II a decade later, separately bills the same-named belt in a comedic "Monkey Championship Wrestling" interlude — Professor Tremendous, already champion, pinning the last of three billed monkey challengers and unifying it with three more belts. No document joins the two readings; both stand.

Filed under the CSWA because the CSWA is where it started and where its ledger survives; the belt itself was contested across the PRODIGY Wrestling BB's own federations and administered by the Unified Championship Committee, not by the CSWA alone.
