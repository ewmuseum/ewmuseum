EUWC International Championship

Placard

Promotion   EUWC (1996)
Active   1996–2008

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EUWC International Championship's own history table opens with Ahmed the Powerhouse winning a tournament final the night EUWC opened, 28 July 1996, and changed hands some fifteen times through the fed's founding two years — X.Plosion, Don Hall, The Crow, Consequence, HICAL and Dan Sokolov (twice) among the holders — before Dan Sokolov's own vacating reign carried it into EUWC's first hiatus in April 1998. Mid-lineage, on 28 September 1997, HICAL unified the North American Championship into this belt. Fred Rogers, Dan Sokolov, Triple T, Erik Kelly, Ray Santana, Cuchulain, The Chameleon, Kritical Kondition and STEALTH carried the title through its second era; Sledgehammer, T-Charger, Simon, HICAL, Larry Serson, Triple T, Neighborhood Sniper, Gavin Glass, David Harten and Virus carried it through the third, before a third hiatus vacated it in June 2002. Nick Marshall, Damian Stone, Cole Justice, Sean Taylor, Jim Roberts, Apocalypse, Danny Collins and Lynch Garrisson cycled it through the fourth era — Apocalypse's own loss to Danny Collins staged at a show the Uprising event-history page dates a full year earlier than the title ledger itself, an unresolved discrepancy between the fed's own documents — before Sean Taylor's second reign absorbed a fourth hiatus (230 days deducted) and Tyrone Walker reopened it at Superiority II in July 2005. After a run the fed's own record does not otherwise explain, the ledger resumes in the mid-2000s with Rocko Daymon, Hawkeye (twice), Sevyn, Jonathan Darkstar and Cobris Grayson carrying it through EUWC's final fwrestling.com-hosted years.
