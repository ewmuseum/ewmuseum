Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Five Star Wrestling's tag team championship, in both of its runs. The Psycho Brotherz won it first in a four-corner elimination match at High Impact on 27 August 2000. It was stripped or vacated more than once in the first run, and P.S. Mad and Sound, who won it at Love Hurts on 26 February 2001, declared the belts the Hardcore Tag Titles. At Futile Resistance in November 2001 Human took the title from Blake in a parking lot and then held it alone. In 2002 Ming and Michel Krieg won it vacant at Genesis on 18 August, Bad Frotitude took it in a three-way on 26 September, and at Bad Moon Rising on 20 October the masked Dead Presidents won it from Bad Frotitude in a chain match and unmasked as Politically Incorrect, William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft. Rites To Pain, Jason McAllister and Neurosis, won it from them on 27 November 2002, the last show the surviving record holds.

Sources: the fed's own title history on thefsw.com, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 8 April and 31 October 2001; its 2002 Title Histories page; the show write-ups of both runs; the champions page captured on 28 December 2001. The 2002 page has Ming and Michel Krieg beat Bad Frotitude at Genesis, where the write-up has them beat A Tag Team; it dates the Jacksons' title 29 November 2000 (Fallen, 29 October, on its own World list) and Bad Moon Rising 21 October 2002.

Sources disagree — The fed's 2002 Title Histories page dates this "21st October 2002"; the fed's own booking gives Sunday 20 October 2002, which this reign follows. Kennedy pinned Asa Fountain in the chain match, the Dead Presidents were handed the Tag Team titles, and the masks came off: Kennedy was William S. Powell, Nixon was Reginald V. Lovecraft, and the masked man at ringside was their campaign manager, Roger Schultz. The ring announcer called the team the new FSW Tag Team Champions, Politically Incorrect. Defences on record. Glory 16, 30 October 2002: Magnum Wolf pulled the referee out of the ring at the count of two, and Politically Incorrect kept the Tag Team titles. Glory 17, 6 November 2002: The bell rang before the referee's count of three on Politically Incorrect, and the timekeeper ruled them the winners and still Tag Team Champions on the 20-minute time limit. Glory 18, 13 November 2002: Roger Schultz hit the referee with a tag title belt, and Jesse Ramey and John Jupiter won by disqualification, but Politically Incorrect kept the Tag Team titles.
Rites To Pain27 November 2002—
Jason McAllister rolled William S. Powell over and hooked the leg, though Powell was not the legal man, and Rites To Pain won the Tag Team title from Politically Incorrect.
