The federation's two-man championship, opened on CHAOS INC. on 24 December 1999. Fifteen reigns are recorded across fourteen teams, The Isolators, CHAOS INC., The Avator & Biohazard and Iceman & Myst each taking two. The Brainiacs' win of 18 August 2000 is the last it recorded, and they are the champions on the federation's own final Champions page.
