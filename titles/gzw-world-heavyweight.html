The fed's own served title-history page runs an unbroken chain of twelve champions from "The First" Paul Spartan through a late-era Kage — Zachary Sharp, "The Wrestling Franchise" Monarch, "Career Killer" Maxx Pain, Zac Sharp, "Smooth Operator" Billy Bond, Deacon Kane, Magic, Sincere, T-Rex (carried over from the fed's own predecessor, EWO), Firefly, T-Rex again, and Kage among them — none of it dated on the page itself. Two reigns anchor the ledger here: Paul Spartan's founding reign, per that page's own opening entry, and Zion's win over Above Average Josh Reetz at Autumn of Armageddon, the fullest dated PPV card the corpus preserves. A separate roster page elsewhere in the corpus calls Josh Reetz, not Paul Spartan, the promotion's "first-ever GZW World Champion" — a contradiction the surviving record itself does not resolve, recorded here rather than silently picked. A third reign is now structured from the corpus's own Dead Zone PPV card: Crucyfix defeats Zion in the corpus's own first Crucyfixion Match, strapping him to a burning cross after Black Sunshine's own interference. The fed's own separately served "GZW Champions" snapshot page instead lists Zion, not Crucyfix, as the reigning champion at the time of that page's own writing — a further, unresolved contradiction against the corpus's own record, preserved rather than reconciled.
