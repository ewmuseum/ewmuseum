Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's Hardcore Championship: 4 reigns are served here, across 4 different champions, the first beginning on 18 July 2010 with Billy Spizatoni and the last on 21 September 2015 with George Stark. The longest of those is Jamie Vaccaro's, 32 days from 21 July 2010. Of these, 3 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). It was unified into the Legacy Championship in 2010; the fed's own history then has it retired, reinstated by David Sinclair and vacated at the 2015 hiatus.
