Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A hub-wide championship of the National eWrestling Alliance, contested by wrestlers drawn from its member territories from March 1998 onward and, per the title's own account, absorbing two retired regional heavyweight lineages (United States and Canadian) into itself in 1999. Eight reigns survive in this collection's own record, Roddy Piper through Agent Mandell, the last still champion as of the hub's own latest surviving document.

Source: the hub's own title-history page. Unlike this hub's own flagship World Heavyweight title (held under the wccw wing), this title's own document claims no real-world ancestor lineage -- its first reign (Roddy Piper, 8 Mar 1998) is billed on the page's own terms as "the first board recognized champion." A 1999 unification (footnote on this title's own page) folds two other retired titles -- United States Heavyweight and Canadian Heavyweight, both also named as retired on the hub's own "Roll Call of Champions" -- into this one via Adam Fierce's win. Eight reigns are structured, the record ending mid-chain (Agent Mandell, still champion per the hub's own latest-held document, the Rankings page dated 4 March 2000).
