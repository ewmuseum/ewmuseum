NPWA North American Championship

Placard

Promotion   NPWA (1993)
Active   1997–1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Created for 1997 exactly as the fed's own 1996 FAQ had promised ('a third singles belt on the way early in '97'), first won by 'Sadistic' Steve Stone in a 15-man King of the Hill gauntlet at the fed's own Valentine's Day Massacre '97, and defended in turn by 'Breakfast' Bobby Toast and Mustafa before the Cougar carried it into 1998.
