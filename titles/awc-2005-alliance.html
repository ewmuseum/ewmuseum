Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AWC's Alliance Championship was first won by Emerald Isle on 1 July 2005. The fed's own record carries 6 reigns on it, across 5 different champions. The longest is Emerald Isle's, 151 days from 1 July 2005. One of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. The Coalition held it at the end of the surviving record.

Emerald Isle1 July 200529 November 2005
Paddy O'Shea climbed the ladder and unhooked both belts, so Emerald Isle beat Krimzon Karnage Inc. in the ladder match and won the first Alliance Championship on league points. Defences on record. Fresh!burst, 14 August 2005: Paddy O'Shea announced that The Farmer was the third member of Emerald Isle and a champion, with Crimson O'Malec out of action; there was no match. Fresh!, 23 August 2005: Paddy O'Shea hit Liam Martin with the Top Of The Morn onto The Farmer's knee and pinned him, so Emerald Isle II retained the Alliance Championship over The Furious Fists Of God. Fresh!, 11 October 2005: The referee called the bell after Mike Wade pulled The Farmer out of the ring during a three count on John of T.T.S., so the Alliance Championship match ended without a winner and Emerald Isle II kept the titles.
The Unfuckables29 November 20057 February 2006
Mike Wade pinned Owen O'Shea with La Majistral after Adam Dick hit him with the Estrada Explosion and Estrada Lock behind the referee's back, so The Unfuckables won the Alliance Championship from Paddy O'Shea's team.
The Furious Fists Of God7 February 200617 February 2006
Liam Martin hit Adam Dick with To the Gates and Tim Martin punched him with the Fist of God, and Liam pinned Dick to win the Alliance Championship for The Furious Fists Of God, while Tim threw Mike Wade out of the ring with 6 Feet Under.
Celestial Fury17 February 20064 June 2006
Sources disagree — The fed's own title history has the Alliance vacant from 4 June to 23 June 2006; the write-up states the vacancy in passing on 6 June 2006, with no show on 4 June. The date here is the fed's own. The Green Grappler knocked Tim Martin off the ladder into the pit of coals in the Baptism Of Fire match, so Celestial Fury won the Alliance Championship from The Furious Fists Of God. Defences on record. Fresh!, 14 March 2006: Celestial Fury won the Alliance Championship match by disqualification when Adam Dick hit The Green Grappler and Butterfly Hamada with the timekeeper's chair; the match is only summarised and the summary does not mention the belts. Fresh!, 30 May 2006: The Alliance Championship match between Celestial Fury and Kip and Teresa Tomas ended in a double count out, and James Brunt announced that Celestial Fury retained. Dave Kern says, before Adam Dick's handicap match with The Furious Fists Of God, that Celestial Fury have walked out on their contracts and the Alliance titles are vacant.
The Furious Fists Of God23 June 200613 October 2006
Tim Martin powerbombed Angelus Sorrow onto Liam Martin's knee to eliminate the last other team, so The Furious Fists Of God won the vacant Alliance Championship in the Sudden-Death Invitational Alliance Rumble. A defence is on record. Coast To Coast 2006, 1 September 2006: Liam Martin power bombed Lacuna Debris onto Tim Martin's knee with the Book Of Revelations and pinned her, so The Furious Fists Of God retained the Alliance Championship over The New Black.
The Coalition13 October 2006—
Sources disagree — The fed's own Alliance history ends with The Furious Fists Of God (2) from 23 June 2006, with no end, and its championships page lists them; the write-up has The Coalition win the titles at Untouchable on 13 October 2006. Phil Allen curb stomped Liam Martin onto a concrete slab while referee Selena Sumner was turned away, and Wesley Reno covered for the fall, so The Coalition won the Alliance Championship.
