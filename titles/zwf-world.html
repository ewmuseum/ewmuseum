Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own served World Title page runs a "Last 5 title changes" ledger across five reigns from 1 July to 12 August 2002: Brock Lesnar filled a vacancy by defeating Jerry Lynn by default, The Undertaker beat Lesnar eighteen days later, Sting beat Undertaker, the belt sat vacant again until Jerry Lynn beat Lesnar to reclaim it, and Undertaker beat Lynn for his second reign — the fed's own current champion as of its latest content. A served Summerslam broadcast separately stages Undertaker defending the belt in a steel cage against Triple H, winning clean before Sting attacks him after the bell and drapes the title over him — an echo of the ledger's own Sting reign, never explicitly confirmed as the same event.
