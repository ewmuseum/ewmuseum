FoB Tag Team Championship

Promotion   FoB (2003)
Active   2004–2005
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Held by exactly one team in its first life — Chris Harvard with Justin Credible, then with Shelton Benjamin after Credible left mid-reign — sixty-seven days that ran to 25 May 2004 and stopped. It came back with the promotion in September 2005 and moved three times in eighty-one days: The Butlers won a four-team tournament for it, the Detroit Street Thugs took it off them, and The Fear took it off the Thugs on 8 December 2005, the last change the lineage records.

Transcribed complete from the fed's own published Championship Lineage (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-supercard-schedule-2005-09-29.html). The tournament final The Butlers won was a fatal four-way against the Marcello Family, the Sonic Death Monkeys and Alpha Gamma Sigma; none of the three is minted, their names standing in the served ledger itself.
