WWA United States Championship

aka CWC New England Championship, New England Championship

Placard

Promotion   WWA (1997–2019)
Active   1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A secondary singles title running alongside the World Heavyweight Championship from the fed's own earliest recovered years, contested under the New England Championship name in WWA's own NCW predecessor era — Johnny Blaze wins it in an over-the-top battle royal the served card bills as "the CWC New England Championship Title" on 2 January 1998, the same event WWA's own later title-history ledger folds into "WWA United States Championship" retroactively, and is billed "the New England Champion" on the fed's own served card of 9 January 1998. From Blaze's ledger-tracked win the title ran on, held across long runs by Steihl, Tracer Bullet (three times), and Grunge, whose 6 June 2003 – 30 November 2003 reign is credited in his own bio page as the longest in the title's history.
