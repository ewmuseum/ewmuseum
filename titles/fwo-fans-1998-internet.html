Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

fWo's Internet Championship: the earliest reign served here begins on 8 May 1998, held by Austin Davis. The fed's own record carries 50 reigns on it; 38 of them, across 26 different champions, are served here so far. The longest of those is Scott Slugger's, 277 days from 23 February 2002. Five of them ended outside a match (retired, stripped, vacated). The Flying Frenchie held it at the end of the surviving record.
