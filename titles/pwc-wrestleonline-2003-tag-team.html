Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PWC's Tag Team Championship was first won by Penance on 8 February 2003. The fed's own record carries 3 reigns on it, across 3 different champions. The longest is Penance's, 56 days from 8 February 2003. Chandler Tsonda held it at the end of the surviving record.

Penance8 February 20035 April 2003
Penance became the first PWC Tag Team Champion, gorilla-pressing Porky Bunson over the top rope for the final elimination in a battle royal after flooring him with a jumping sit-down piledriver. His partner refused the second belt, leaving Penance the sole champion. Defences on record. PWC TV, 15 February 2003: Penance retained the PWC Tag Team Championship in his first defence, pinning delivery boy Sammy Bello after dropping him with a tombstone piledriver onto his own tag partner, Chuck D'Andrea. PWC TV, 22 February 2003: Penance retained the PWC Tag Team Championship, pinning both members of 2GVC at once, dropping Chandler Tsonda with a sit-out piledriver onto the already-piledriven Jimmy Nguyen. PWC TV, 15 March 2003: Penance retained the PWC Tag Team Championship, chokeslamming Jim Axtell onto a folded steel chair for the pin after escaping Axtell's Axe to Grind. Axtell's own partner, Jimmy Nguyen, blinded him with cologne passed in by Chandler Tsonda, letting Penance recover to finish him. PWC TV, 22 March 2003: Penance retained the PWC Tag Team Championship, eliminating all three challenging teams in a gauntlet match: pinning Sammy Bello of Special Delivery with the Ideological Crucifixion, chokebombing Scourge of Total Violation, and finishing with a tombstone piledriver on Gunner Gojoh of the Deathmatch Samurai.
2GVC5 April 2003between 2003-06-14 and 2003-06-15 (circa)
The Second Generation Viet Cong (Jimmy Nguyen and Chandler Tsonda) won the PWC Tag Team Championship from Penance in a Boiler Room Brawl, Tsonda diving into the ring first with both belts after Nguyen dragged Totakoxe Salvador back off the ring apron by the foot. Penance had abandoned the match to carry off Jim Axtell on Jacob Solomon's orders, leaving no one behind to stop 2GVC's dash. Defences on record. Capitol Punishment, 13 April 2003: 2GVC retained the PWC Tag Team Championship in a four-way defence, Chandler Tsonda pinning Ryo Kazaki of the Deathmatch Samurai with the Model Citizen off the top rope as Charlie Loc held Kazaki down. Jimmy Nguyen ran in to superkick Kazaki and turn the numbers back in 2GVC's favor after Tsonda had accidentally sprayed hairspray in referee Gil McGoosen's face. PWC TV, 26 April 2003: 2GVC retained the PWC Tag Team Championship, Chandler Tsonda pinning Colossus of the Roman Empire with the Model Citizen moonsault off the top rope. Jimmy Nguyen blindsided Romulus with a spinning calf kick just before he could break up the count.
Chandler Tsondabetween 2003-06-14 and 2003-06-15 (circa)—
Chandler Tsonda became the sole holder of the PWC Tag Team Championship, the belts the show calls the "Alliance" titles, when Ron Starr released his injured partners, Jimmy Nguyen and Charlie Loc, after Tsonda ran them down with Loc's own car in the parking lot the night before. A defence is on record. Last Man Standing, 15 June 2003: Chandler Tsonda retained both PWC Tag Team Championship belts, billed as the Alliance belts, alone in a handicap match, pinning the Masked Mullet with the Model Citizen moonsault off the top rope. No match decides the hand-off: it happens off the cards, between the shows of 14 June and 15 June 2003. 2GVC's reign ends there with no loss on record.
