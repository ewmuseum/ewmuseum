Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

jOlt's top belt, first won by Brandon Blade at a2o: Alpha To Omega on 24 June 2001. The fed's own title history carries it through Blade, HaVoK, Blade again and erØn, who left holding it and had it vacated at Rise of the Legends on 28 October 2001, and gives it to The Don at Cataclysm on 30 December. The fed's other accounts also name Kodiak Vic Creed its champion: Unwanted's opening video of 2002 shows him among the past champions, the 2005 site replays the Cataclysm match among its 'jOlt Vintage Classics' and ends it with 'new jOlt World Heavyweight Champion: Kodiak Vic Creed', and its Mardi Gras write-up says the re-make of the belt served 'for Kodiak Vic Creed and The Don’s reigns'. The second season began in May 2002 by putting the belt up again: at Unwanted on 28 May 2002 Erik Rave won a thirty-man battle royale and with it 'a NEW jOlt World Championship'. The revival of 2012 opened with Brandon Blade as the jOlt Champion on iNtense 35; Damien Lee fired him on iNtense 36, and the belt was contested at Divide & Conquer, whose first review is dated 30 July 2012: Sylo beat Superstar Vince Jacobs there for it, in a main event that also ended the Legacy title. Derecho won it at Power Struggle in June 2013 and lost it to Aran Thompson at Thieves' Honor that August; Jeremy Ryan was its champion in 2015, and Death Wish, the final show, books him against Omega in a steel cage for it and the Fearless belt.
