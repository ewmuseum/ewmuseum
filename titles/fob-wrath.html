Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Foundation of Brutality's second belt, crowned the same night as its first: Ozena beat Tony “The Marvel” Mason for it on 6 December 2003, then quit. Two of its four 2004 reigns started as vacancies and a third was simply handed to the number one contender, and it went dark with Tatewaki Kuno's on 25 May 2004 — the fed's own lineage lists it thereafter as a retired belt whose banner it no longer had. It was reinstated on 8 December 2005 with a division of its own, entered by beating a member of it, and Baxter Baxter beat Gene Snitsky to be its fifth champion.

Transcribed complete from the fed's own published Championship Lineage (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-supercard-schedule-2005-09-29.html), which marks the belt “[Retired Belt, Banner Not Available.]”. The reinstated division's own rules are set out in the Sacrifice of 13 October 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-sacrifice-2005-10-13.html).
