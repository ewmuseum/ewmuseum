Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt passed through fourteen recorded reigns and ten men between January 1991 and March 1992 — Volcano and Juggernaut inside one turn, then the General four separate times, Frank Steel, DiMadMan, the Big Kahuna, Bjorn “the Sweedish Pretzel” and “Irish” Mike Flint inside a single week in May, and Mr. Roddy, Steelgrave, the Sandman and Metal after it.
