Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A hardcore-rules title of the promotion's final months: Gods Tears proposed it to majority owner Kingston Cash on Holiday Havoc II and was first billed as its champion on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), the deciding bout having taken place off the page; its record runs only as far as the surviving broadcasts.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Gods Tearsunknown—Already champion entering New Years Kick Off, defends no title match that night but is shown assaulting backstage vendor Spaz and special guest referee Sidious The Clown on the arena's own big screens with a lead pipe until new VWF guardian John Tyler orders him to stop under threat of a double title strip and suspension.
Not billed as contested in the VWF Hardcore and Submission Championship match on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), where the champion instead loses the VWF Submission Championship. Defended together with the VWF Submission Championship against Sidious The Clown and Lark Fenriz in a Scaffold Match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005); Fenriz hits the ground first and the overall match goes to Sidious, but the broadcast states neither title changes hands, confirming Gods Tears retains the VWF Hardcore Championship. Retains against Roadkill Rick at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), a draw when neither man answers a ten count after a Cobra Clutch Suplex through a table. Retains again against Fast Eddie Cutlass at End Of March (30 March 2005), a time-limit draw. Retained without a change at The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005): on the line, alongside the 24 Hour and Submission Championships, in the same eight-man elimination match, champion Gods Tears is disqualified against Roadkill Rick in a chair-and-brass-knuckles standoff, a finish that does not transfer the title.
