Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A hardcore belt first awarded to Romeo Salvador at Hardcore Hell on 10 May 2003 and held by Leech and The Moose before it was merged into the Inter-Continental on 5 July. A second hardcore belt appears from 20 October 2003, with Max Cameron and Dante trading it and Leech holding it from 19 January 2004 until Shannon Skanes won it in a three-way on 22 March 2004.
