Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's Galveston Island Championship: 5 reigns are served here, across 4 different champions, the first beginning on 3 June 2010 with Aspen Chaud and the last on 13 February 2011 with Chris Jericho. The longest of those is Wake Walker's, 98 days from 22 August 2010. Of these, 2 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). The fed's own Undisputed history carries it as the first seven lines of one belt; on 17 February 2011 it became the GIW Undisputed Championship.
