Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A pair of tag belts Eric Tyler, the new General Manager of SWF Legends, unveils on SWF Legends in July 2008 in his first 'Mr. Tyler Says' segment, so that the Sky Club division's tag teams, tied to the Legends brand, have belts of their own instead of the World Tag Team Championships of the Supreme brand. Tyler sets a four-team tournament with its deciding match at Welcome to the Jungle 2008 among The Upper Class, High Concept, The Tokyo Underground and Pure Energy; High Concept and The Tokyo Underground qualify on SWF Legends in Week 2 of August 2008 by beating The Upper Class and Pure Energy, and High Concept, Elmo Benson and Groucho Bling, beat The Tokyo Underground at Welcome to the Jungle 2008 to become the first champions. They keep the belts against Double Deuce on SWF Legends in Week 4 of August and lose a non-title match to Rocky Golden and Bloodstone in Week 1 of September.
