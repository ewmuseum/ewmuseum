Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A tournament staged across two 1998 On The Road cards (3 February, 19 March) reached its own final round -- Angry Chef against Jack Emerald -- which the held record never shows being contested; by the 5 October 1998 Topeka show Chef is billed the reigning champion, retaining the belt twice in one night against Commander Cody and then Emerald himself, still billed the #1 contender. He also defended it against Dane Thompson at an untranscribed "GPWA Slim Pickens" show, breaking Thompson's ribs. By the site index's own later, undated snapshot the belt had changed hands again, to "Sideshow" Tad Detmer -- another of the wrestlers the fed's own news names for a different, September 1998 tournament draw (the vacant ICBINR U.S. Heavyweight Championship's own 4-Corners) -- with no document in the held record showing how.

The tournament final's own result (Chef over Emerald, presumably, per the bracket the 19 March 1998 card sets up) is never shown on the page; Chef's own reign is receipted from the Topeka Tabloid's "ANGRY CHEF RETAINS TITLE" headline treating the reign as already standing, not from a documented title-winning match.
