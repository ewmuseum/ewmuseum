PTC Global Championship

aka Primetime Central Global Champion, PTC Title

Placard

Promotion   PTC (2002)
Active   2002–2007
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The alliance's top prize, defended in an open 128-competitor tournament (eighty signed in the first seventy-two hours) and, by the record's own count, held by sixteen different superstars whose reigns the record places before it was unified with the PTC Extreme Championship on 30 April 2007. Its lineage runs from Vampir Nosferatu's tournament win over Jay Phoenix at PCW's Friday Fusion, through Obsidian, Donny Diamond, three reigns for Seymour Almasy, Chris Storm, Jesse Jamester (who won it from Storm in a four-way crossover title match XUW's own Reborn 2004 show hosted, representing Global Wrestling against Apocalypse and Patti Anderson of Furiously Addictive Wrestling and Four Winds Wrestling), Hessian, Saij, the Latch Key Kid, Emilio Rage, Karina Wolfenden (who vacated it the night she won it), Rich “The Renegade” Rollins, Uber Judge, Shadow and Kimbusa, to Clinton Sage, whose reign ended when Jason Snow won the match that unified the belt with the PTC Extreme Championship. That unified belt, the PTC Unified Championship, now has a record of its own. The sources do not agree on everything: PTC's own title log and PTCpedia date Vampir Nosferatu's win to the final shown on 23 November 2001, where the alliance's news places his reign in February 2002, and the log dates Almasy's win of the title at PTC Survival to 9 November 2004, the day the results were posted, where the show's own billed date is 22 August. The news also has Ignatius Lisieux, who “defeated the dreaded Global Title Curse”, as champion by April 2004, and speaks of a second, unnamed Global Title Tourney winner; PTC's own title log lists neither, so those two reigns stand as the news gives them. Jay Phoenix reached this title's own tournament final once more and lost the judged decision by three points — the closest he came to the biggest prize the whole alliance offered.
