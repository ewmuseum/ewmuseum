Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PCW's Television Championship was first won by Revenant on 22 October 2000. The fed's own record carries 46 reigns on it, across 38 different champions. The longest is Teresa Tomas's, 84 days from 22 December 2002. Four of its reigns ended outside a match — handed over or vacated. Tessa Windsor held it at the end of the surviving record.
