Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The first belt the CSWA made, and the one it kept. Joey Melton is defending it on the second card of 1988 and Hornet takes it from Purple Hayes the year after; a decade later it is still the fed's own main event, won out of an eight-man tournament at FISH FUND XII in August 1998 and carried through steel cages and IRONMAN round-robins until the record thins out. Its 1999 is the year that reads hardest: Hornet wins it in San Antonio in April and is stripped of it by a Co-Commissioner at BATTLE of the BELTS XVI, Eli Flair takes the vacancy out of the ANNIVERSARY IRONMAN, and Deacon takes it from Flair at THANKSGIVING WEEKEND SPECTACULAR and holds it for most of a year. The fed's own Champions page ends that reign with a sentence it does not explain further: the title was relinquished, in November 2000, on a diagnosis. It was still being defended on the board in 2004.

Called the CSWA World Championship on the 1988 and 1989 cards and the CSWA World Heavyweight Championship on the Champions pages from 1998; the same belt throughout.
