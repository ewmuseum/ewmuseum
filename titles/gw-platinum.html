Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt that spent the spring of 2003 being taken rather than won. Venom Alexander was carrying it on 31 March when the arena screen cut to Leviathan going through his locker room and walking out with it, which Venom watched from the ring in the middle of a tag match. Mark Kullypso has it by WrestleCade III five weeks later, with nothing held saying how, and loses it there to Jay Robson and The Next Element — after which four Nevermore men lock the new champion in the ropes with a stack of chairs and Derek Frost comes down the ramp with a skateboard.
