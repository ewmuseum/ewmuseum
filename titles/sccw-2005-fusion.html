Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Fuse Fusion Championship's first recorded champion is Ethan Frost, who was already carrying it when the fed's own record opens on him. Its Fusion Rumble of 10 February 2007 ended with Frost and Fred Fusion eliminated together and the two of them declared co-holders. The fed's own record carries four reigns on it, across three champions: Frost alone, Frost and Fusion twice, and The Legacy. Frost and Fusion held it at the end of the surviving record, by then billed under a name its own authority figure refused to recognise.

Self-awarded on the federation's own word: FUSE's own commentary opens the double-title ladder match of Afflicted Glory 2007 (31 May 2007) with "It's time for our first title match of the night! True, both belts up for grabs are unsanctioned, but both of these teams feel passionately about their belts". The same broadcast stages the FUSION straps as belts hung above the ring that the fed itself had not sanctioned for two months and puts them up against the Fatal Affliction straps the reigning team had made for itself, the losing set "banned" from FUSE; the preceding segment, "FUSION Wrestling: RIP", bills the pair as "the Fatal Affliction or FUSION Wrestling titles" and shows the Reeds wearing their own custom straps.
