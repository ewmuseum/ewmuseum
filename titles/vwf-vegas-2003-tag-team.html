Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A tag title that changed hands off-page between two attested reigns; the surviving record does not show the actual title match. A second, undocumented change of hands followed before the wing's own 12 Days Tournament finale, and a third change split one outgoing pairing's own two belts between two different men in a single ladder match.

Team Brittania (Simon Davidson, Justin Davidson)17 March 2005—Debuting Team Brittania inserted themselves into a defense already underway between champions Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov and challengers Frank O'Shea and Billy Bones, turning it into a three-way; Justin Davidson pulled Valenkov from the ring to stop a cover on O'Shea, and Simon Davidson pinned O'Shea for the titles without either champion having been pinned.
Retains by disqualification against Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov at End Of March (30 March 2005) after the returning Hot Property (Stevie Knight and Austin Lee) run in and help finish McQueen. A legal challenge filed the same night argues the win itself does not count, since Team Brittania was never a named, signed participant in the St Patricks Day match; incoming VWF ownership is left to decide the championship's own status. On The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), Team Brittania defends the titles, billed with a question mark over their own legitimacy, against a returning Henry Fish (billed Scott Starring) and an unidentified stand-in wrestling as the absent Van Wild, winning by pinfall; the team's own hired ring announcer, not the broadcast's regular ring announcer, declares them champions afterward. The same night the previous champions, Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov, billed "the unlikely tag team champions," lose their own tag match to Hot Property when Valenkov turns on McQueen; the VWF's incoming ownership still has not ruled on the championship's own disputed status.
