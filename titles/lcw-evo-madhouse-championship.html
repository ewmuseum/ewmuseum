LCW Evo Madhouse Championship

aka Hardcore Title

Placard

Promotion   LCW Evo (2001)
Active   2001–2002

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

LCW's own Hardcore Championship, carried into the split and renamed the moment neo opened. Duel held it from the very first day, defending it through the winter of 2001-02 against Gryfon and, on a card whose own results were never captured, Frank Heimdal — until Heimdal finally took it off him in a cage at Dawning, the wing's own second Madhouse Champion.
