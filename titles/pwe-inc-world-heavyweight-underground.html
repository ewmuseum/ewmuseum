Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PWE Underground's own top belt — a second, brand-specific championship the surviving record never once shows crossing paths with Revenge's own Heavyweight/World Championship. Chaos carried it into Underground Network on 10 October 2005 and lost it there to Bulldozer, who won it with a sledgehammer shot once the referee was knocked cold; a community wrap-up column the next day corroborated the same result while airing a dispute over the two writers' own RP agreement. A departing champion's own promo names Kurt Angle, ZerO and Tex Graham himself among its past holders, alongside Deuce Jackson before Chaos — spoken evidence only, no dates or documents behind any of it.

Distinct from pwe-inc-heavyweight (Revenge's own belt, Ryan Styles to Nicholas Pryde): the corpus stages what read as two parallel Heavyweight/World title lineages, one per brand, never shown unifying or resolving against each other in any served document. Kept separate rather than merged on guesswork — see the wing's own tier_source note.
