First held by Masanobu Fuchi from 1 March 1988, the page's own history runs 29 reigns and ends with Jerry Lynn's reign closing on 1 October 2002 (retired per the page's own footnote).

Filed from the alliance's own title page. The page says the alliance's title histories are based on the World Wrestling Alliance fantasy league's; its champions are real professional wrestlers and the league's own wrestlers side by side in that inherited history.
