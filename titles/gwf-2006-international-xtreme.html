GWF International Xtreme Championship

aka GWF Undisputed International Xtreme Championship

Placard

Promotion   GWF (2006–09)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Falcon McKenzie retained it against Michael Smart in a bloody hardcore defense on 27 April 2008; by the 2009 reunion tour it belonged to Baron Trotter, who fended off a returning Mark Madison in a billed-but-uncaptured defense and carried it to the fed's own last night. At 20113, Baron unified it with the Co-Cruiserweight Championship in a triple threat against XO Malone and Debolt Dragonsbane, becoming the Undisputed GWF International Xtreme Champion for GWF's own final record.
