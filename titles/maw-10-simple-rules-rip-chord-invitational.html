Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MAW's annual tournament, whose winner the diary's own title records list under "Rip Chord Invitational" and whose champion the text treats as guaranteed a title shot. Antonio is the 2008 winner; at The Rip Chord Invitational in January 2009 Kid Arachnid beats Fumihiro Ota in the final and is unmasked as Rip Chord's son Jay (the record line reads "Kid Arachnid (Jay Chord)"). Jay Chord, the 2009 winner, cashes in the title shot the Invitational guaranteed him at The Rip Chord Invitational in January 2010, after losing his quarter-final to Erik Strong. Primus Allen beats Strong in the 2010 final with the Running Powerslam and becomes the champion; the diary's own title records list him as "Primus Allen" under January 2010. Primus Allen says at Eleventh Hour in November 2010 that he will not defend the Invitational crown at the 2011 tournament but cash in the guaranteed title shot it carries, a year after his win, against Aaron Andrews. Kirk Jameson wins the 2011 tournament on 21st January 2011 by beating Antonio, Firebird and Hugh de Aske on the one night, after a field that took in qualifiers at Remember November, Season's Beatings, End Of Days and New Year, Old Rivalries and a quarter-final on a tour show; Primus Allen, the holder, has by then spent his crown's title shot on Aaron Andrews. The diary's own title records list Jameson under January 2011. Kirk Jameson spends the guaranteed title shot of his crown on Aaron Andrews at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2011 and loses. The 2012 tournament is a field of eight, with qualifiers at Season's Beatings, End Of Days and New Year, Old Rivalries and the rest on 12th January 2012, when Kid Arachnid beats Jared Johnson, Firebird and JD Morgan on the one night and is unmasked as KC Glenn; the diary's own title records list him as "Kid Arachnid (KC Glenn)" under January 2012, and the narrator notes that tew.com now rates the show of Above Average importance, the company's first. In the diary's pages from June to July 2012 KC Glenn, the holder, cashes in a guaranteed title shot against Aaron Andrews at Summer Showdown on 12th June and loses; after Beach Blast on 12th July he leaves MAW.
