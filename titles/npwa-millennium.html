NPWA Millennium Championship

Placard

Promotion   NPWA (1993)
Active   1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Created for the NPWA's own Millennium Division sometime before the held record's June-August 1998 gap, first held by "Untouchable" Randy Gant before "Superstar" Steve Sampson took it in a chaotic Coal Miner's Glove three-way at Mayhem, 29 Sep 1998.
