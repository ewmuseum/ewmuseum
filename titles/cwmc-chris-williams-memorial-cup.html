Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A floating interfederation tournament rather than any one promotion's own institution, named for Chris Williams, a community handler killed in an automobile accident on 4 May 1999. The Cup's own roll of past champions (its organiser's own "CWMC 'Hall of Fame'" posting, 16 May 2007) opens by naming Jay Phoenix its first-ever champion, of what the roll calls the Chris Williams Television Title, at PWW — a promotion Chris Williams himself worked for as an executive. Three further reigns follow on the same roll, in order and undated: TimeBomb, Cutthroat Chris Hatchett, and Gurgles. No year survives for any of the four. The roll's fifth name, Saij, is the one the surviving record also dates: PTC's own news of 2 December 2003 reports him "winning the Chris Williams Memorial Cup" that year — the same year Jay Phoenix's own PCW and PRIME biography cards instead claim a Cup championship of his own, and the same claim PRIME's ReVolution 197 and SCCW's own record separately inflate to a two-time championship. None of Jay Phoenix's three claims is dated to a specific staging or corroborated by any other federation's record; all three stand recorded against Saij's own dated win, unsettled. A third running, staged by GIWF in 2001 and billed inconsistently on-air as both its second and third annual, names no surviving winner. The tournament's only staging fully held is 2007's: run in Flagstaff, Arizona and organised in-fiction by Jay Phoenix as "the first ever PTC Indy Event," six rounds and a Second Chance Battle Royal wildcard across thirty-four entrants, decided by The Illustrious Face-Eater over Digital Mortality in the final, three judges to none.
