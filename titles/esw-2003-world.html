ESW World Championship

Placard

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's top belt. Eddie won it as the NYCW World title at the first Kick Ass Inc on 3 March 2003, and it runs through the change of name to the ESW and forty-one reigns in the fed's table to Craig Williams on 15 September 2010, among them Slipstream's five and Darien Sunn's record 230 days. The table's footnote says it was merged with the CWA and BLPW World titles to become the Triple Crown Championship.

The page's footnote: 'The ESW World title was merged with the CWA World title and the BLPW World title to become the Triple Crown Championship.'
