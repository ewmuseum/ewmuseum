INWI Television Championship

Promotion   INWI (1997)
Active   1997

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Won in the founding tournament final by Vito Rizzotto, the belt changed hands to Mr. Apples within days and stayed with him for the bulk of the spring and summer before a fast rotation through Harvey Thunder, Ultimate Perversion, Pig Pen and Jason Chandler carried it to year's end.
