VXW Spin The Wheel Television Championship

Placard

Promotion   VXW (2001)
Active   2005

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Its own stipulation is set by a spin of a wheel before every defense; Mike Bisignano won it from Valec Corso through a table at Birthday Bash 2005 and carried it through ladder match defenses at Vegas Violence V and Fear Is Coming II, before losing it to Lark Fenriz inside the Rage In The Cage cage match at Phear Phantastic 2005. Fenriz retained it against Justin Harper on Winter War I after a disqualification reversal, then lost it to Harper on Winter War II inside a triple threat also involving Kagedo. Harper retained the title in a scored Taipei Death Match against Roman Anderson at St Patricks Day Massacre.

Mike 'The Biz' Bisignano24 August 2005—Pinfall, a frog splash off the top rope through a table (The Delmar Dive), after the wheel landed on Hardcore Rules.
Successfully defended against Blake Hamilton in a No Disqualification match on Prelude To Violence III, then in a ladder match against a masked enforcer for Valec Corso at Vegas Violence V, then again in a ladder match against Robbie Caldwell at Fear Is Coming II, before losing the title to Lark Fenriz inside the Rage In The Cage cage match at Phear Phantastic 2005 after a masked interloper (revealed afterward as Classy Joey Boy) struck both men with a canoe paddle.
Lark Fenriz22 November 2005—Pinfall over Mike Bisignano inside the Rage In The Cage cage match, after a masked interloper (revealed afterward as Classy Joey Boy) struck both men with a canoe paddle from above.
Won as one of four falls inside a single ten-man cage match that also awarded a new fifth title and saw the 24 Hour Championship successfully defended. Retained on Winter War I against Justin Harper: Kagedo's own chair interference let Harper score an apparent pinfall, but promotion co-owner Rod Beauty overturned the result to a disqualification win for the champion. Lost the title to Harper on Winter War II inside a triple threat also involving Kagedo, when Kevin Simmons's own interference knocked Kagedo out cold and let Harper pin him for the title.
Justin Harper27 January 2006—Pinfall over Kagedo, not champion Lark Fenriz, inside a triple threat, after Kevin Simmons's own interference knocked Kagedo out cold and Harper hit a Spinebuster on Fenriz before covering the fallen Kagedo for the three count.
Harper's own first title win in the held record. His own next match on record, against Roman Anderson on Winter War III, was explicitly billed non-title and is not treated as a defense. Retained the title in a scored Taipei Death Match against the same challenger, Roman Anderson, at St Patricks Day Massacre, a stipulation decreed by new 49 percent owner Lark Fenriz. Retained again in a twenty-minute time-limit draw against Omega at Hotter Than Hell I. Fought John Manning to a further time-limit draw at Sin City Fights August 31, in a match the broadcast's own commentary questioned the title status of after co-owner Spartacus's earlier title-stripping spree reportedly overlooked Harper; co-owner Ric Beauty publicly recognized Harper as champion on Sin City Fights September 19, setting a defense against Lance Voltar (winner of the TV Title Open Match's own Ring Two) at Vegas Violence. Retained again over 'The Big V' Lance Voltar at Vegas Violence 2006, opening the show with a Wipeout after kicking out of a top-rope corkscrew elbow.
Cobra24 October 2006—Pinfall over the beaten John Manning in a triple threat that also involved champion Justin Harper, after Roman Anderson, disguised as the match's own replacement referee, knocked Manning out with brass knuckles.
Cobra's first VXW Spin The Wheel Television Championship win, ending Justin Harper's own reign without Harper landing the deciding fall himself. Retained the title over Justin Davidson with the Snakebite at Sin City Fights November 18, after brother Simon Davidson's own ringside run-in with a cane broke Davidson's own focus. Contested again in the same three-way at New Year's Eve of Destruction 2006, Miles McKinley eligible to win only this title from Cobra; the match ended without a decision when the referee called for the bell amid a mass brawl, Cobra still champion.
