Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The CHZ's top belt, first won by Millenium in a four-man elimination battle royal at Caged Fury 1 on 1 August 1999. Its own title history carries it through the fed's years as the FWF and the SWF — Cellrock relinquishing it when the CHZ closed in November 1999, JT Fringe winning the FWF title at that fed's opening, Rufus Blaq becoming the first SWF World Champion — and through a six-month vacancy while the SWF was closed in 2000. Kris Snyder, later Chazzy 2 Cool, won it in the New CHZ on 27 May 2001; Staz took it from him on 4 July and left North America with the belt at the end of August. After five months vacant, Matt "The Franchise" Helms won it at Caged Fury 2 on 30 January 2002, beating Diablo and then Don Rossi, The Count and Chazzy 2 Cool in a two-cage final, and is billed to defend it against Keyser Soze at NBK Pride, with no result surviving. The fed's June 2002 relaunch under a new owner listed a World Heavyweight Championship with no champions and crowned its own first champion (see chz-world-heavyweight). The belt's 2003 shows, after the fed's new era opened at BreakOut 2002: Gus Theo is billed champion on 5 March 2003, Matt Helms holds it again by 26 March after Caged Fury 2, and Harry Manback beat Helms for it at May HayDay 2 on 14 May 2003, keeping it on 21 May when a second referee's count of Drayton MacPhereson was ruled void.

The chain follows the fed's own Heavyweight title history (/promotions/chz/archive/heavyweight-title-history.html), which runs through the fed's FWF and SWF years exactly as the fed kept it; the Hall of Fame, the news archive and the held show scripts corroborate it. The 2003 reigns are filed on this belt, not on the June 2002 relaunch's (chz-world-heavyweight): the new era that opened at BreakOut 2002 carried the fed's 2001-02 belts on (Don Rossi, North American champion since February 2002, defends that title at BreakOut, "A title mind you that Don Rossi held twice before", /promotions/chz/archive/breakout-2002-12-18.html; Mad Max, Hardcore champion since March 2002, defends it on 26 March 2003), and on 26 March 2003 Matt Helms, this belt's champion of January 2002, says "Not only do I once again possess the CHZ World title" (/promotions/chz/archive/wnw-2003-03-26.html).
