Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

"GQ" Geoffrey Quartz won this title at CCW's own "First Night" debut show (18 January 1997); by the fed's first RSPWF broadcast that July his brother "IQ" Ian Quartz held it instead, an unrecorded transition, and Ian was still champion when the surviving record breaks off.

"GQ" Geoffrey Quartz18 January 1997between 1997-03-31 and 1997-07-07 (circa)Won the four-man West Coast Championship tournament at CCW's own "First Night" debut show, 18 January 1997, pinning Ricky Revolution for the title.
Billed to defend the title against Bill MacRagge at Explosion!! (30 March 1997); that result does not survive. Lost it to his brother Ian at FanFest after more than a minute in the Equation deathlock, and was out injured with his back on Slamfest, 9 July 1997.
"IQ" Ian Quartzbetween 1997-03-31 and 1997-07-07 (circa)—Beat his brother, "GQ" Geoffrey Quartz, for the title at CCW FanFest, by submission to the Equation deathlock.
Won a future title shot for Ricky Revolution by losing a non-title match to him on 9 July 1997; the shot itself is not resolved in this corpus.
