Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AWC's Countdown Trophy was first won by Teresa Tomas on 23 June 2006. The fed's own record carries 4 reigns on it, across 4 different champions. The longest is Vince Jones's, 35 days from 4 July 2006. One of its reigns ended outside a match — retired with the belt. AWC discontinued it on 1 September 2006.

Teresa Tomas locked Captain Suleimon in Southern Comfort in the third five minute interval and Suleimon tapped out, so Tomas won the first Countdown Trophy.

Vince Jones threw referee Richie Travis into Teresa Tomas in mid-air, hit her in the head with a baseball bat and covered her with Lars Larsson counting, to win the Countdown Trophy in the sudden death round. Defences on record. Fresh!east, 11 July 2006: Vince Jones stood over Leviathan, who was out cold beside a broken bat, and referee Aaron Davies counted the sudden-death fall, so Jones retained the Countdown Trophy. Fresh!west, 18 July 2006: Vince Jones hit Mikey O'Reilly with The Silencer, an inverted brainbuster, and pinned him before the time limit to retain the Countdown Trophy. Fresh!east, 25 July 2006: Vince Jones pinned Aimz, who was knocked out and bleeding, after Shawn Harris hit him with Natural Causes, to retain the Countdown Trophy. Fresh!special: Live On Huntington Beach, 1 August 2006: Garbage Bag Johnny burned Vince Jones out of the Ruby's Surf City Diner match with a flaming mop; Jones was still announced as the winner of the Countdown Trophy match and kept the trophy.

Chainz took the baseball bat from Jasmine, hit Vince Jones with it and then the Chain Reaction, two power bombs, and pinned him to win the Countdown Trophy. A defence is on record. Fresh!west, 15 August 2006: Chainz tapped to Teresa Tomas's Stretch Muffler only after the time-limit bell, and after Tracy Stanton hit Tomas in the back with a steel chair he pinned her with two power bombs to retain the Countdown Trophy.
