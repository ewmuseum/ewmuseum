Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Graphic Violence held this belt into 1997 before losing it to the Assassins at the promotion's own Kickoff '97. It stopped existing as its own belt on 16 April 1997, at AEWA Extreme, when the Assassins lost a dedicated unification match to the World Tag Team champions, the Machines, merging the two lineages into the AEWA Unified World Tag Team Championship.

Retired into the AEWA Unified World Tag Team Championship on 16 April 1997.
