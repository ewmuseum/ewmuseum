Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Chris Jacobson lost it to Moose at the Metrodome, 13 January 2002. Moose lost it to "Sadistic" Steve Liermann in Counterstrike's own "Final Battleground Match," 9 February 2002. Liermann defended it into the spring before losing a "Career vs. Title" match to Duff Man at Affliction, 28 April 2002. Duff Man lost the title to Kryptonic in a match no surviving document records, and Kryptonic lost it in turn to Fudgey McGlone at Hardcore Harvest, 24 October 2002.
