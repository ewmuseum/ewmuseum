Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New ERA of Wrestling's second-tier singles title, contested across the federation's whole run from an ambiguous 2005 change through a merger-era reign four years later, with no results document ever confirming a change of hands directly.

MWG is billed champion by the federation's own 17 September 2005 RAPTURE card, defending against Mr. Entertainment; the immediately preceding International Intrigue pay-per-view (9 June 2005) put him against the title's own prior #1 contender, Jason Payne, with no titleholder marked on either man, suggesting the title changed hands at that show, though no results document confirms it directly. A later champion, Cameron Cruise, is billed against Erik Black on the federation's own Destrucity II card (23 January 2010), more than four years later, with nothing in the held record's text bridging the two reigns; recorded as an honest gap rather than a guessed chain.
