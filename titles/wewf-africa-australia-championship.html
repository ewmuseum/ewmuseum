Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

One of three Continental belts, and the only one whose reigns the reports date almost end to end: the Crusher, the General, Mr. Roddy, Tom “the Animal” Brown, DiMadMan twice and WindWarrior, before DiMadMan vacated it on winning the World Championship and Bjorn “the Sweedish Pretzel” won the tournament that filled it across three nights of one turn.
