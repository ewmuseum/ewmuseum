Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Tag Team belt of the CHZ's June 2002 relaunch under its new owner, listed on the fed's title page with no champions and on its champions page as vacant, the contenders to be announced. A tag team title tournament ended on 14 August 2002 with Redemption Song, Jim Surge and Ben Campbell, beating the Flying Vergomovs in a falls count anywhere final; they kept the titles against Gold Rush on 21 August, the Mind Games cage match against the Mystery Assailants ended in a no contest, and the fed declared the titles vacant on 2 September 2002 as Redemption Song moved on.

Named on the fed's own 2002 champions and title-history pages (/promotions/chz/archive/champions-2002-06.html; /promotions/chz/archive/title-history-2002.html); not the belt of the fed's own 2001-02 Tag Team title history (/promotions/chz/archive/tag-team-title-history.html). The Tag Team titles reinstated in 2003 are filed on chz-tag-team, the belt that era carried on (see its notes).
