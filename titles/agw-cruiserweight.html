AGW Cruiserweight Title

Placard

Promotion   AGW (1997)
Active   1997–2000

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AGW's weight-limited championship (a 240-pound maximum per the ledger's own footnote 19), first won 7 October 1997 and defended through 55 recorded reigns before the ledger's own last word, Jason Star's reign beginning 30 January 2000, running into the fed's own hiatus. Matt Grendel's own reign (19 January 1999) was struck from a front-office scheduling error that gave the 247-pound Grendel a title shot over the weight limit; President Speed Racer stripped him days later and awarded him a TV Title shot instead, though the ledger still counts Grendel a past champion.
