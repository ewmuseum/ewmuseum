Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NCCW's top title from the fed's own 1998 opening bell: "Dominator" Don Cameron became its first-ever World Heavyweight Champion and held it twice more inside the fed's own first year, the belt passing through six further champions across the fed's own dated record before Cameron reclaimed it a final time, undated, and spent the fed's final stretch defending it against a mounting challenge from Lew Spectre.
