Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own served title-history page runs a full eighteen-champion chain across the promotion's WWC era (1997-2001), before the WWC name itself gave way to UGA — seventeen of the eighteen reigns are structured here, the page's own numbering skipping from a 14th to a 16th champion, an archival gap in the source itself, never filled. "The Franchise" Shane Stud held the title five times, more than any other champion the ledger names; the chain closes on his own fifth and final recorded reign, won at Carnacage III (31 December 1999, billed the last-ever Carnacage) over Disturbed Youth, the man who had beaten him for the belt eleven days earlier in the WWC's own first-ever Canadian title change.
