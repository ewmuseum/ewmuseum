Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Founded April 1997 as the CWF-Pro Tag Team Titles (renamed CWF-Pro World Tag Team Titles in May 2001), when the Double Dragons won a battle royal eliminating the Insyders to become its 1st CWF-Pro Tag Team Champions, unifying the UBH World Tag Team Titles into it the same night. The Double Dragons return again and again across the belt's own 149 recorded reigns — 30 of them, more than a fifth of the whole run — with the Johnson Boys and the Flash Brothers (9 each) the only other teams to approach that mark; like the fed's other flagship belts it absorbed dozens of rival promotions' own tag titles one by one over its own decade-plus life. The Williams Brothers hold it at the ledger's own last recorded line, with no closing note. Nearly half its recorded champions — 71 of 149 — survive only as bare team names, no bio page behind them.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 149 reigns in full; all 149 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/cwf-pro-world-tag-team-titles.html for the underlying primary source.
