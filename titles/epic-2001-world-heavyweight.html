Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Heavyweight Championship of Epic Wrestling's revival, vacated in its story when Wayne Carter's career ended and won by Seth Anson in a tournament on 3 December 2001. Ben Archer took it from him at Vengeful Hearts on 24 February 2002 and is its last champion in the surviving record. The federation's own title history also lists reigns from 1983 to 1995: the invented past of its story, served on its page.

Source: the federation's own title history (/promotions/epic-2001/archive/epic-2001-title-history.html); the champions page of 1 March 2002 (not served: a two-line list) names Ben Archer and Sam Potright.
