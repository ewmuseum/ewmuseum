Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A championship of the fed's first run. Cyle Chaos became the first FSW Intercontinental Champion by climbing a ladder for the belt at High Impact on 27 August 2000. Big Daveylicious, Mike Marvelous, Colin Baird, Jacob Jackson, Aggonizer and Johnny Mayhem were among its holders before Painkiller, the boss, made Mayhem lay the belt down on 6 April 2001. Punisher 2000 won it at Hallowed Knights on 7 October 2001, and Jonny Trip pulled it down in a ladder match at Futile Resistance in November 2001; the fed's champions page still listed Trip at the end of that year. The 2002 run did not bring the title back.

Sources: the fed's own title history on thefsw.com, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 8 April and 31 October 2001; the first run's show write-ups; the Futile Resistance preview card ("Punisher 2000(c)"); the champions page captured on 28 December 2001. The October 2001 page names Carlton Carter beaten on 15 May 2001, where its previous row makes neXt the champion.
