Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

CSW's own third singles belt, contested from March 1996 — the only one of the four whose header does not start with the promotion. Kevin McFly won it first, eliminating Miguel Sanchez in a battle royal ("He did become the 1st Royal champion," his own bio agrees, in a career he says he otherwise made no name in). Eight independent-era reigns survive on the fed's own numbered ledger, all recorded here, eight different men — the only CSW belt no one ever won twice. Facing Death's own bio credits the stable rather than the man for his: "The UBH did help him win the CSW Royal title. His only title he ever won." Mike Hart was defending it in February 2004 when the acquisition stripped him of it, though the prose on his own page names a different belt than his own card does.
