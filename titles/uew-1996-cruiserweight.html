Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The UEW's newest belt, born out of a fifteen-man international battle royal and successfully defended twice inside its first three months by the man who won it.

Introduced as an international invitational battle royal at the fed's own 1999 New Year's supercard; billed at its introduction as the European Cruiserweight Grand Prix Invitational Championship before settling into everyday use as the Cruiserweight title.
