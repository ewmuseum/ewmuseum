The wing's own top title, defended in falls-count-anywhere and Stretcher-match stipulations across its attested run; its third Classy Joey Boy reign ended in vacancy after an in-storyline attack rather than a defense.

His third VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship reign; the loss returned Randall Crane to an in-storyline suspension both men had been serving since Vegas Violence III's own post-match assault. The Maquilladora's own scheduled challenge on the Kickoff Card (9 January 2004) was called off by order of new co-owner Classy Joey Boy, who ruled he had already beaten The Maquilladora and would not face him again; Demonic and The Maquilladora wrestled a 24 Hour Contender Match instead, which The Maquilladora won. An unseen figure attacked the champion near a creek before Ghost Town Blues's own Pentagon match, keeping him from a scheduled title defense against the match's own winner; a stand-in triple threat settled that night's own live crowd stakes without changing the title. On Firestorm (26 Feb 2004), 50% owner Rob Beauty ruled the champion out at least six weeks with a concussion and declared the title vacant pending a Gauntlet tournament; Lark Fenriz later confessed on the same broadcast to being the attacker.
The Equalizer12 October 2004—Pinned champion Lark Fenriz, freshly chair-shotted by guest referee Flash, after stablemate Orion pulled him clear of a Jason Storm cover attempt earlier in the three-way and restored the match's own original referee.
Retains against The Maquilladora and Classy Joey Boy in a triple threat on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), pinning Classy Joey Boy with the Face Plant after Sebastian's own interference and a guest-referee stint from majority owner Kingston Cash. Retains again over Classy Joey Boy and The Maquilladora in a Wheel Of Chaos match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) after a doctor secretly working for Kingston Cash injects both challengers mid-match; the doctor is revealed afterward as Kingston Cash's own brother, Terrance Cash. A scheduled defense against Kagedo at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) never begins: Gods Tears attacks Kagedo with a tire iron during his own entrance, and John Tyler books a bonus tag match in its place, in which The Equalizer and Gods Tears are disqualified against Kagedo and Roadkill Rick. Retains again at The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), entering the wing's own closing Gauntlet battle royal first by his own request rather than last as tradition dictates, and surviving as the sole man left in the ring after Thunder Valenkov, sent into a top rope that Hot Property forgets to pull down, goes over instead.
Lark Fenrizunknown12 October 2004Champion by the promotion's own billing after the February 2004 vacancy; the winning match is not in the held record.
Recorded from the promotion's own repeated billing, on the same footing as the wing's other off-air title changes; the crowning show is absent from the record. Continues to be billed champion on Dog Days 2 (22 June 2004), scratched from his own three-way match by majority owner Kingston Cash before the bell; returns to competition, without the title on the line, in an eight-man singles scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004). Retains the title in a clean pin over Daniel Sinz on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004). The Heavyweight Championship main event on Wrestle Style V.1 (16 August 2004) ends without a finish when a returning Classy Joey Boy interferes; Kingston Cash announces a random-draw title tournament running each week until Vegas Violence, with the champion defending every time he is drawn and the title changing hands on count-outs and disqualifications during it. Retains cleanly over a returning Billy Bones, a shell of his former self after months in an alcoholic rehabilitation facility, in the tournament's opening round on Wrestle Style V.3 (3 September 2004). Wrestles The Predator Kevin Simmons to a time-limit draw in the tournament's second round on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004); majority owner Kingston Cash rules the count-out and disqualification stipulation does not cover draws, and both men advance. Retains in a fatal four-way on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004) over Classy Joey Boy, Sebastian and The Assassin Marcus O'Malley (self-eliminated to save a kidnapped Mark McQueen), pinning Sebastian with the Sammael after Sebastian tricks Classy Joey Boy into a sledgehammer disqualification; the win sets a Vegas Violence main event against tournament runner-up The Equalizer. Signs the contract for that match on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), where a video appearance by Jason Storm inserts him into the match as a third man and the champion declares he no longer cares about the title, only about denying The Equalizer. Loses the title to The Equalizer in a three-way also involving Jason Storm at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), pinned after a chair shot from guest referee Flash; Orion's own interference in the match, and a referee dispute between guest referees Classy Joey Boy and Flash, both factor into the finish.
