Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GCW's Cruiserweight Championship was first won by Chameleon on 22 April 2001. The fed's own record carries 11 reigns on it, across 10 different champions. One of its reigns ended outside a match (retired with the belt). Tessa Windsor held it when the record ends; a card of March 2007 calls the belt retired.
