Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Filled by a six-team tournament on the opening night and defended almost every week after it. Seven reigns in four months, two of them by the same team twice over, and the belts finished the record on the team that lost the very first final.

Declared in the federation's own recruitment announcement of 8 April 1991 — “the PWGA Tag-Team champions, who will defend against the No.1 contenders” — and filled by tournament on 22 April. One reign begins inside the unheld Report #11 and is chained across that gap at month precision.
