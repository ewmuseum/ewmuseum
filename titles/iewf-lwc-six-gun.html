IEWF/LWC LWC Six Gun Championship

Placard

Promotion   IEWF/LWC (1997)
Active   1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A singles title on the promotion's own LWC side. Mark Cutter lost it to Mark Wugger (Glenn Hudson) by 18 October 1998, and Rob James took it from his partner at Last Tangle In Texarkana on 15 November 1998; he was billed as its champion in April 1999. Past holders named on air include Casey James, Brody Thunder and J.W. Hardin.
