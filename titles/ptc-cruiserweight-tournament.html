Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Open to cruiserweights only, run twice: CW1 in 2004 (forty-two entrants across four brackets, free agents permitted alongside PTC and member-fed competitors) and CW2 in 2005, the smallest tournament in the alliance's history at thirty-two entrants. Seymour Almasy's CW1 win extended a run that reached fourteen straight tournament victories across the PIT, CW1 and GTT4 before ending in GTT4's fourth round.
