Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Hardcore Championship's own lineage under a new name, renamed at the Destiny Pay-Per-View on 24 September 2006 with Raven still holding it. Raven's reign ran on until the belt was emptied over his own inactive status, and Sting won it from the vacancy in a battle royal at Aftermath on 29 April 2007. Triple H took it from him at Death Before Dishonor that July and it was vacated again on Warfare on 15 October 2007. Lacey's win at Survivor Series on 25 November 2007 is the last change recorded, and the promotion's own Champions page still names her champion.
