Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RUSH's Syndicate Championship was first won by Statuz Quo on 23 May 2002. The fed's own title log carries 2 reigns on it, across 2 different champions, and ends with El Spiffy!'s, from 18 August 2002; PCW's cards of December 2002 and January 2003 carry three more: Stimpo's (about 1 December 2002), Ebola III's (about 8 December 2002) and The Masked Mullet's, won from Ebola III at Shanghaied III on 26 January 2003. El Spiffy!'s is the longest, from 18 August to about 1 December 2002, which Decimation 2002's commentary counts as 107 days; Statuz Quo's, 87 days from 23 May 2002, is the longest with both dates exact. The Masked Mullet held it at the end of the surviving record.

Statuz Quo23 May 200218 August 2002
The champion renamed the belt, in character, for the length of his reign. On the card of Adrenaline 5 (30 May 2002) he announces it himself: "I deserve not da 'Syndicate title' but my own! Datz why I'm officially renamin' da title to da \"Quo title\" fo' as long as my reign as champion!!", with a custom platinum belt unveiled on camera and a champion-declared stipulation, "from now on, diz title will only be defended in no disqualification matches". He repeats it on Adrenaline 10: "I am the QUO CHAMPION!!". This is not a second belt and not a rename by the federation: the announce team calls it degrading the Syndicate title, the fed's own title list keeps the name Syndicate Championship, the page heads its list "Syndicate Championship", and the next champion's own words are "how long it took for El Spiffy! to get the Quo-stink off of the belt". Three defences are on held cards, none in the fed's own title log: Ignition 2002 (16 Jun 2002, beat Christopher Shackelton in a match announced "for one fall with no disqualifications and is for the Syndicate title", his own declared stipulation honoured), Adrenaline 8 (27 Jun 2002; he lost the match by disqualification and kept the belt, the card's words being "Winner by DQ: Steve Brent. Statuz Quo retains his Syndicate Title") and Adrenaline 11 (25 Jul 2002, beat Caleb McGlocklan by pinfall). A fourth match is explicitly not a title match: on Adrenaline 10 Sheila Tyrone introduces "a non-title match", which Statuz Quo lost to William 'Biohazard' Perry. The page's "Longest Title Holder: Statuz Quo-87 days" agrees exactly with 23 May to 18 August.
El Spiffy!18 August 20022002-12-01 (circa)
Five defences are on held cards, none in the fed's own title log, each announced as a Syndicate title match: Adrenaline 14 (29 Aug 2002; he lost the match and kept the belt, the ring announcer's words being "Your winner, due to interference, Scott Cage! But still the Syndicate Champion, El Spiffy!"), Adrenaline 15 (5 Sep 2002, beat Wes Outlett), Adrenaline 16 (12 Sep 2002, beat Chris Matthews), Adrenaline 17 (19 Sep 2002, a four-way over Chris Matthews, The Masked Mullet and Caleb McGlocklan) and Adrenaline 19 (4 Oct 2002, retained when Stimpo was disqualified). The belt itself was stolen twice and the championship never moved: Stimpo impersonated him to hand himself the belt on Adrenaline 18, the CEO Rush Mason took it back and voided it on screen ("it's a good thing you were just joking about that whole 'giving the belt away' thing... So why don't you just keep this belt for now"), the real El Spiffy! was found tied up backstage, and Stimpo laid him out and took the belt again on Adrenaline 19. A sixth defence is the WCW Hardcore match at Rising From the Ashes (13 Oct 2002), booked on the card of Adrenaline 19 (he challenges Stimpo "to a match at the Pay-Per-View! For HIS Spiffy-cate title"); the fed's own pay-per-view script, served as a document, has Sheila Tyrone announce "here is your winner, and STILL RUSH Wrestling Syndicate Champion, EL SPIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIFFYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY" and James Williams say he returned "JUST A DAY AFTER BEING HOSPITALIZED FOR A BACK INJURY" to defend it. The reign ended after the fed's own record, which carries no later Syndicate change and whose title list (about October 2002) still names him champion: the PCW card of Turmoil 102 (3 Dec 2002) has Stimpo "winning his first title, the Syndicate Title on Sunday Adrenaline defeating both Ebola III and Spiffy! in their triple threat match" (the Adrenaline of Sunday 1 Dec 2002, on no capture), and Decimation 2002's commentary says "Even when he lost his belt, Stimpo pinned Ebola in the triple threat to end it!" and that he held "the belt for 107 days"; 18 Aug to 1 Dec 2002 is 105 days; the commentary's count and the calendar differ, and no length is claimed.

The fed's own title log ends with El Spiffy!'s reign (Crossroads, 18 Aug 2002), before this one; this reign, the next two and the end of El Spiffy!'s are on PCW's cards of December 2002 and January 2003. Fusion 100 counts it among four champions: "Three weeks ago, RUSH had only two Syndicate Champions in its history. By now, that number has doubled." Decimation 2002's commentary says of El Spiffy! "Even when he lost his belt, Stimpo pinned Ebola in the triple threat to end it!" How the belt left him is on no served card. On Fusion 100 Stimpo tells El Spiffy! "you didn’t have it for only a few days…you had it for months!" and wants "my rematch" to "win back my precious", and Xavier Kannon complains of "this little belt-trading scheme you’ve got going". No length is claimed (both dates are inferred from the Sunday card and the cards around it).
Ebola III2002-12-08 (circa)26 January 2003
Defences on served cards, none in the fed's own title log (which ended before this reign): Fusion 101 (20 Dec 2002, "Mordred vs Ebola III (RUSH Syndicate Championship)", Ebola III won); Decimation 2002 (22 Dec 2002, a Double Jeopardy match for the Syndicate title in which he beat El Spiffy! and then, after Marvin Harris and Stimpo double counted out, was announced "HERE IS YOUR WINNER AND STILL RUSH WRESTLING SYNDICATE CHAMPION, EBOOOOOLAAAAAAAAAAAA III"); Fusion 103 (3 Jan 2003, Tyler Valentine did not appear for the Syndicate title match and the card rules "Ebola III wins by disqualification"). The cards of 24 Dec 2002 and 14 and 21 Jan 2003 introduce him as the Syndicate champion. The Masked Mullet pinned him with an inverted backslide at Shanghaied III.
