Promotion   NPWA (1993)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned by a 16-man tournament in late 1996, the title changed hands six times across 1997 -- the Southern Rocker to Jurisprudence to the Mighty Boss to 'Bad Boy' Mark Bagwell to 'Blackheart' Brian Masters to the Silent Striker to Dark Justice, who carried it into 1998's own Death Row Match.
