MWE Tag Team Championship

aka MWE Undisputed Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   MWE (2007)

Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt MWE crowded most: its first recorded champions, Project Revolution, won it at Turmoil in a six-team match, and the pair who took it from them, Maxxamillion Mustafa and the Grand Mystique, won it the same way. Darius and Boyd beat the Hollywood Hooligans for it on the 24 November 2008 Genesis, lost it to Project Revolution on the 12 January 2009 Monday Night Mayhem — a match the fed booked as a unification with a separate World Tag Team Championship — and the belts passed to Tex-Mex that March and to the Black List Mafia at Highway to Hell on 29 March 2009. The fed billed them the Undisputed MWE Tag Team Championships from the unification onward. Black List Mafia never lost the belts in the ring: Leon Roberts vacated them himself on 22 June 2009 to chase the vacant United States Championship instead, and no further reign is held in the held record.
