LVW Las Vegas Championship

Placard

Promotion   LVW (2005)
Active   2006

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A secondary title decided for the first time by a bracket of poker-table gimmick spots, awarded to whoever could put an opponent through the right table.

El Gordo Grande won the first Las Vegas Championship, a bracelet, in the Final Table Match at Supershow On The Strip I and lost it to Erik Mateo (billed there as Eric Mateo) at Hard 8 (17 June 2006). No later card shows it change hands; in August 2009 Mateo says he left LVW still holding it. The bracelet The Captain holds in LVW's own "4:18" (posted 4 August 2009) is the separate Strip Championship he won from Olvir Arsvinnar at Snake Eyes (1 June 2009).
