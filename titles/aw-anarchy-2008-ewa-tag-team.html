Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EWA's tag belts, listed as Tier IV: Devestation Inc. won them at HalloWicked 2K7, Dog House took them on 12 November 2007 and defended them against Damage Co. on 2 December, and Beauty & the Beast took them on 13 December 2007. No later tag belt carries them on; the Chaos Theory Championships began vacant.
