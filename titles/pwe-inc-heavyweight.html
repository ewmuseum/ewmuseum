Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt at the top of PWE's Revenge brand, and the one its own paperwork keeps changing the name of: the front page called it the PWE World Championship in the spring of 2005 and the Heavyweight Championship by the autumn. Ryan Styles carried it out of Saga III in May and lost it in the main event of Deadly Game at Madison Square Garden on 27 November 2005, to Nicholas Pryde, by submission. Eddy Guerrero had challenged for it a month earlier and Sting a year before that; the fed's own record of who held it before Styles is not in the surviving record.
