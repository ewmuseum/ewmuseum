Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned in a ten-team, two-ring clash at the promotion's own first pay-per-view, this title's lineage runs clean through early 1997 and tangles across August to November, where this collection's own holdings carry two teams both claiming it at once.

The August-November 1997 stretch (a Midsummer Madness change to the Syndicate, a Golden Grapple lyric crediting Cold Spell) is the messiest run in the fed's own record and is registered here as partially unresolved rather than silently picking one line.
