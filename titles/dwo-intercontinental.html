Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Third belt down the line and, by its own page's account, no less wanted for it. Angelus won it on 25 March 2002, the earliest dated reign anywhere in the DWO's record. Chryste held it twice and was offered the chance to be a triple champion with it against Adam Stryker's World title; Noah White carries it into 2003; by that July the fed vacates it and books four men to fill it at the pay-per-view. It ends on Damian, and it ends by decree — Callisto Bane made him choose between the belt and a Heavyweight title shot on the fed's own Relentless of 25 September 2003, and he threw it down.

The fed's own Champions page spells the belt Intercontiental; its own show scripts spell it Intercontinental and call it the IC Title.
