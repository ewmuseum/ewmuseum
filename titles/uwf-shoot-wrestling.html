Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The THC's own shadow belt, created when hardcore wrestling was banned in December 2001 and the THC itself briefly went dark; ran independently until 16 February 2003, when it was absorbed back into the reunified THC. Seymour Almasy is among its recorded holders.
