New Era Old School Wrestling's second-tier title, created through a four-man tournament across its January and February 2001 shows. Amuro Balsa won that tournament on 18 February 2001; Messiah held the title by 11 April; Matt Saunders beat Messiah at Storm Warning on 7 May 2001 and was still champion on 26 June.
