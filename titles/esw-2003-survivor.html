ESW Survivor Championship

aka NYCW Survivor Championship

Placard

Promotion   ESW (Empire State, 2003)
Active   2003–2010

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Steve Sinclair created the Survivor title on 10 November 2003, when the NYCW Tag Team titles were dropped, and appointed Kid Krazy its first champion; the fed's rules call it 'a gimmick themed title'. Its table runs through forty-six reigns to Amber LeBeau on 15 September 2010, among them Tommy Purr's 242 days in 2009, the longest run of any belt in the fed, and Steve Sinclair's own reign in September 2008.
