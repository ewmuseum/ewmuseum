Kevin Blair17 May 200412 October 2004Pinned Thunder Valenkov with a back body drop onto Valenkov's own cane, Lucy, during the Gauntlet battle royal.
Attacked Valenkov, Ric Beauty and Rob Beauty with Lucy after his own earlier tag match, then won the title from Valenkov in the closing battle royal the same night. Retained the title by count-out against Flash on Dog Days 2 (22 June 2004) after leaving the ring with the belt rather than answering the referee's count, which eliminated him from the Dog Days tournament; a further challenge from Daniel Sinz on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004) ended with both men walking away from the ring before a decision, the title again not changing hands. Majority owner Kingston Cash declares the championship no longer recognized by the promotion on Wrestle Style V.1 (16 August 2004) and tries to confiscate the belt; Blair refuses and strikes Cash down, keeping the belt in his own possession with no successor named. Still clutching the same belt, Blair loses to a returning Classy Joey Boy by disqualification in the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament's opening round on Wrestle Style V.3 (3 September 2004) after attacking the referee rather than give it up. Eliminated early from a VWF 24 Hour Championship battle royal on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004) after fleeing to the back with the belt; Adam Jennings wins the battle royal and is announced champion by the ring announcer, but Kingston Cash refuses to recognize the change unless Jennings physically takes the belt from Blair. Blair fails to appear for a signed follow-up against Jennings on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004), ruled a no contest; Cash instead sends his own new bodyguard G-Gangsta after Jennings, no bell rung. Blair again fails to appear for a defense against Fast Eddie Cutlass on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), another no contest, and runs in with Lucy to chair Jennings after Jennings beats Gangsta later the same show; Cash books Blair against Jennings in an I Quit match for the title at Vegas Violence. Loses the title to Adam Jennings in an I Quit match at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), knocked into the referee before physically knocking Jennings around while refusing to let go of the belt himself; finally says the words after a powerslam over the top rope with both men still gripping the title, then speared, sedated and stretchered out.

Retains again over Robbie Caldwell in a Bring Your Own Weapons match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), finishing him with the Hyper Driver moments after ringside legend Johnny "Hands Of" Stone reclaims his own prosthetic leg, which Caldwell had used as a weapon on the champion. Retains against Paddy O'Shea at March To Madness (2 March 2005) by disqualification after Frank O'Shea's own interference. Retains against the debuting Mad Bomber at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), the Bomber disqualified for refusing to let a bomb-sniffing dog search his pockets. Retains again over the Mad Bomber at End Of March (30 March 2005), pinning him while he is stuck in a bulky training suit. Retained without a challenge at The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005): on the line, alongside the Submission and Hardcore Championships, in an eight-man elimination match, but champion Adam Jennings is the final survivor for his own side and is never eliminated.
