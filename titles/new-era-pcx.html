New ERA of Wrestling New ERA PRODIGY Classic Xtreme Championship

Placard

Promotion   New ERA of Wrestling (2004)
Active   2005–2009

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A cross-promotional hardcore title, crowned and re-crowned across the federation's own run in tournaments whose own outcomes this corpus never directly records.

First seen contested at a tournament final on the federation's own International Intrigue pay-per-view (9 June 2005, Chaos vs. Dallas Carter), with no result recorded. Jason Payne is billed champion on two 2009 cards, the Almost Live special (26 June) and Unplugged (18 July); nothing in the held record's text connects his reign back to the 2005 tournament. The title is vacated and recontested in a new single-night tournament on the federation's own Destrucity II card (23 January 2010, John Doe, Ashe Corvin, Trevor Cane and Chaos), again with no result recorded; recorded as an honest gap rather than a guessed chain throughout.
