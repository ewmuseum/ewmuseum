Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Created at Glory on 6 July 2003, when SilverHAWK decreed that the winners of a three-team, sixty-minute Iron Man match would become the first ACW Tag Team Champions: El Janitors won it, defended the belts three times, and were stripped of them on 7 December 2003, when the fed folded. They returned with the 2004 revival — theNormans took them at Relentless on 30 September 2004 and kept them past Splink on Courage 65 by disqualification; Splink took them from theNormans at breakOUT on 6 February 2005 and went missing, and the fed retired the belts that May. The Heirs of Wrestling won them out of vacancy at The Wake on 17 September 2009, and nine more changes followed by February 2011; Big E. Smalls, Orcs and Humans and Team HOSS held them in 2012 and 2013.
