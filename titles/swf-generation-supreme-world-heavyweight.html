Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The top belt of the diary's SWF. Jack Bruce holds it when the diary opens, a reign the diary's later posts date from last August, and he keeps it through Brandon James at Awesome Impact and Remo at The World is Watching, where Vengeance's interference ends the second match by disqualification. At Master of Puppets 2008 he beats Vengeance in a Supreme Cell match, and the diary's June posts book him against Eric Eisen for it at Times of Trouble 2008. Bruce beats Eric Eisen at Times of Trouble 2008, keeps the belt against Antonio Marquez and John McClean on Supreme TV in Week 3 of June and against Jay Chord in Week 2 of July, and sees Richard Eisen pick him in the brand split, which ties the title to the Supreme brand even if he loses it. Eisen then announces Supreme Double Jeopardy for The Supreme Challenge 28, in which Bruce must defend the belt twice in one night, first against Vengeance, with Eric Tyler added to that first match by Phil Vibert and the second opponent left for Eisen to choose on the night. Bruce beats Eric Tyler and Vengeance in the first Supreme Double Jeopardy match at The Supreme Challenge 28, then loses the belt to Rich Money in the second after Richard Eisen's indecision lets Money into it, ending a reign the SWF.com review puts at some eleven months. Money refuses to defend the belt anywhere but pay-per-view, makes Bruce and Randy Bumfhole each beat Remo for a shot, swaps the belt for a black one set with diamonds on Supreme TV in Week 4 of July 2008, and, when Bruce and Bumfhole fight to a double disqualification in Week 2 of August, sees Richard Eisen make Welcome to the Jungle 2008 a Threefold Threat among the three. Money keeps the belt at Welcome to the Jungle 2008 in a Threefold Threat against Jack Bruce and Randy Bumfhole, pinning Bruce with Bruce's own New York Minute, and Remo hands him his custom belt. He beats Guide on Supreme TV in Week 3 of August with Bruce watching from the ramp and, on a video from the Las Vegas casino he part owns, turns down Bruce's challenge for Under Control 2008; Pistol Pete Hall, who has asked for a match, is billed against him for the belt on Supreme TV in Week 2 of September.
