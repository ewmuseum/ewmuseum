CWF-Pro World Title

Placard

Promotion   CWF-Pro (1997)
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Founded May 2004 as a second CWF-Pro World-branded belt running alongside the existing CWF-Pro Title, when Zebakiah won a battle royal eliminating Weirdo to become its 1st CWF-Pro World Champion. For roughly its first third the two World titles moved together, six wrestlers in a row winning both belts on the same night before the ledgers begin to diverge; Mike Powers holds it 22 times across the belt's own 89 recorded reigns, far ahead of the Icon Miguel Sanchez's 6. Jimmy Lee holds it at the ledger's own last recorded line, defeating Jason Lee and Billy Lee in a 3-way dance.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 89 reigns in full; all 89 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/cwf-pro-world-title.html for the underlying primary source.
