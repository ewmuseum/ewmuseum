Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

UCWF's own top title changed hands six times across the surviving record, twice on the strength of a bare rollup rather than a clean finish: Johnny Johnson loses it to Bobby Linken at Wrestle Insanity while distracted by a fabricated news report naming him alongside a dying convicted murderer, and The Notorious Don Joe Giovanni takes it from Johnson at Resurrection on a quick count by special guest referee Mumrat, the fed's own owner. Giovanni's own reign is the longest the record shows in outline: he survives a 60-minute Hell In A Cell Ironman against Ace Of Spades at Fury 2003-06-20, three falls to two, only to lose the belt to the same man weeks later in a Breakout dome-cage match, kicked off a 20-foot ladder short of the roof hatch Ace pulls himself through alone. Ace Of Spades is the last champion the surviving record shows.

Billed once as the “Heavyweight Championship” at Wrestle Insanity's own finish (promotions/ucwf/archive/wrestle-insanity-2003.html); every other appearance calls it the World Championship.
