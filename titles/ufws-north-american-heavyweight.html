Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The federation's second singles belt, and the only one filled by losing: the runner-up in the opening night's World title tournament took it as a consolation. Its own announcement said the champion would defend against any of the top five contenders, chosen by the president himself so that different people got shots — and it changed hands seven times in four months, including once at the Summit of E-Wrestling and once inside an issue that does not survive.

Declared in the federation's own recruitment announcement of 8 April 1991 — “the PWGA North American champ, who will defend against any one of the top 5 contenders (I will make the choice myself, so as to give different people title shots)” — and filled on 22 April by the tournament final's losing side. One change happened at the Summit of E-Wrestling (its own wing, id `summit-of-e-wrestling`) and one inside the unheld Report #11; both are chained at month precision with the reason given on the reign.
