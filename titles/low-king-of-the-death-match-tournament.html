Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

An annual crown rather than a defended belt, and the one honour Lights Out Wrestling carried in its masthead banner alongside its five championships. Adam Stryker is recorded as the 2004 King of the Death Match and Donny Diamond as the 2005; no other year survives in the fed's own record.

Won once a year in the fed's own tournament — a bracket entered by invitation, run over rounds with byes and a final, and settled on the night — and never defended between crownings. Its own masthead banner carried the reigning king in the same strip of champion portraits as its five championships, and one of its own bio cards lists the crown among a wrestler's LOW titles. No defence of it survives anywhere in the fed's own record.
