Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The PWF Challenge Title's own dated ledger runs three reigns deep and closes mid-chain: Crusader Chrome won the title's own first-ever match — a fatal four-way over Theros Macalvia, The Fro, and Maxwell Powers — at Saturday Night Havoc, 24 March 2001; Power G won it from him on 7 April; Rye Hazwaki won it from Power G on 18 April, the ledger's own last entry.

The fed's own four remaining named titles (European, Hardcore, Extreme, Tag Team) are named on the Records page but no ledger, reign, or dated mention survives for any of them in this capture. The World and US Titles are also unledgered here, but their own reigns are independently documented from other primary sources — see pwf-powersite-world and pwf-powersite-us.
