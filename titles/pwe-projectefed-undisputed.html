Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PWE's own top belt, and the only one it ever crowned in a ladder match. Four men climbed for it at New Years Resolution on 25 January 2003 and Scott Curtis came down with it, helped up the ramp afterwards by the president who had just knocked one of the other three through the ropes. Five days later Lynx put the champion's head through a vending machine backstage and asked for an inferno match for it at the next pay-per-view; the fed was still running a tournament for shots at it in April, and the answer to Lynx's question is not in the surviving record.
