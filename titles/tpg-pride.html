Retired by the fed   2004 (year) — The fed's own roleplay text of August 2004 has the belt already gone — “Only the Pride Title has been demolished; taken away” — with no date and no closing match recorded. It was defended again by 2007, so the event records what happened, not a permanent state.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AJ Styles held it seventy-nine days and was still arguing about it in August 2004, by which point the fed had taken it away — “the TPG Pride Title is dead now,” one of his own opponents told him, “demolished; taken away.” Sixteen months later the fed's founder came back to Madison Square Garden asking why an International Championship was being paraded around with the Pride Title sitting on the sidelines, and the chairman offered to burn the International belt in front of him and bring this one back instead. It came back: the last line the record ever writes about the promotion has Montel Vontavious Porter defending it in May 2007.

Both the reign and the retirement come from one served document: AJ Styles's own signature record and the promo exchange around it (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/iron-man-battle-royal-2004-08-16.html). The board's own last capture, of 27 May 2007, carries a news line reading “TPG Showcase: Pride Title: Montel Vontavious Porter © d. The Mask”, which is held in the workshop and not served and is registered here as a second reign. The belt the fed's own chairman offered to bring back at Madison Square Garden in December 2005 was defended again, under the same name, eighteen months later.
