Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AICW's own first title, awarded at a seven-man gauntlet on Fright Night to "Swingin'" Chandler Stone. The belt changes hands repeatedly across the surviving record — to Elvis, to Stephon T. Davis, to "Pretty Boy" Troy Walcott, to "The Convict" Oswald Bates, and, after an off-page saga involving a voided win and a Commissioner's own fiat award, to Legion and then Zeek Monkey — several of its own transitions never shown on any served card.

One document (Steel City Meltdown) names a physical belt "the AICW Extreme title"/"jobber title" in what its own surrounding text treats as this same lineage; not clearly resolved in the surviving record, so recorded here rather than as a separate title.
