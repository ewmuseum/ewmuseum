Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

EPW's Tag Team Championship was first won by Blackheart and Mephisto on 24 August 2003. The fed's own record carries 4 reigns on it, across 4 different champions. The longest is Mike Stryker and Devildriver's, 24 days from 28 August 2003. One of its reigns ended outside a match — retired with the belt. EPW did away with it on 8 June 2004 until a tag team division could be established.
