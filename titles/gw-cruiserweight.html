Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Jay Robson held it into the spring of 2003 and lost it to Sage Sabbot in a ladder match in the last week of March, which is the whole of what the record says about how it moved. Sabbot was still carrying it at WrestleCade III five weeks later, using it as a weapon on the man who had just taken the Platinum title off Kullypso, and the ring announcer introduced him for the International title match with it in his hands. Roz Blayze won the right to challenge him on the same night, in a four-way ladder match for a replica of the belt.
