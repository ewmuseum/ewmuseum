CSWA Enterprise World Tag Team Championship

Promotion   CSWA (1988)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A tag championship the CSWA ran alongside its Unified belts for a single documented year. Generation X-Press won the EN World/US tag tournament final over the FORCE at BATTLE OF THE BELTS XIV in July 1996; the Japanese Assault took it from Troy Windham with the Shogun Slam in a unification match at FISH FUND XI that September, and the fed's own record carries nothing further.
