Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

TEAM's own top prize, held for most of its first year by an underdog Canadian one-night-tournament winner before a real-world firing forced the league to strip it and start over.

Established between this wing's own first and second eras (the era map's own title-history document dates it to shortly after the first TEAM Invitational Tournament); the title's own establishment and its first champion's own coronation are not captured in this era's own page set, only D!'s standing reign by the time of SuperShow I. Declared vacant at the 2006 Dupree Cup Finals after D! was fired by New Alberta Professional Wrestling for backstage conduct; a vacant-title match (Mr. Amazing! vs. Jake Hix) was set for SuperShow II, outside this era's own window.
