Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A championship that outlived three of the promotions that carried it. It began in eWo when Johnny Legend won a ladder match at Bound for Glory, and passed through Gabe Shelley, Lawrence Jarvis and Seth Thomas before the promotion closed in December 2004 with nobody left to compete for it. Shelley, its last eWo champion, took it to the Blazenwing Wrestling Federation, which bought the rights to it outright and defended it until spring 2006; px:w's chairman Paul Reynolds then purchased it and activated it there; when px:w closed it went back to BWF for a short while. In the fed's own words it "is one of a select few championships that has gone through many federations," and "many believe it lost it's prestige and it has since been abandoned."
