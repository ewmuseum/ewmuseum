Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The revived era's own tag belt, carrying a four-month reign by the touring outside act the Phenomenons — a four-way at Tagstravaganza to Flavour Explosion — before Those That Would Inflict Ill took it over and were still champions as of the January 2015 roster snapshot the record ends on.

The Dudesunknown—Confirmed World Tag Team Champions by the fed's own Sept 2010 rankings sidebar, the classic-era record's own last word on the title before its 2013 revival restart. The classic-era tag chain before this point is a real, largely unbuilt gap — the served show corpus documents at least the New & Improved D-X, the Celtic Assassins, and the Delivery Men each holding the belts across 2005-2007, but the exact reign-by-reign chain isn't reconstructed.
The revived era's own 2014+ chain (the Phenomenons onward) is a separate, later restart of this same title id, not a continuation of this reign.
