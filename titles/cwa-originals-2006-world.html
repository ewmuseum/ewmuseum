The CWA World Heavyweight title as the CWA Originals’ own page lists it, from Yuri Jolnecov in January 1984 to Noah Skanes’s eighth reign, dated May 2009 and still running. Its list runs through 2003 to 2005 with different holders from the CWA World belt of those years in the other CWA wing: the two histories cannot both be the same belt.
