WWO World Title

Promotion   WWO (1995)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The WWO's top belt, run through a tournament to crown King $$$ Perfect after its own earliest history and on through Tony Tobis, "Ravashing" Ric Studd and "The Master and Ruler of the World" before Randy Bryson won a tournament final on 1 May 1997 after the previous champion's retirement. Andros, Zac Schneider, Iron, Bellamy and Lord Robert d'Artois traded it through 1997 and 1998 — Bellamy's own reign ending in a cage match billed as the Valentine's Day Massacre — before Fury took it and held it 369 days, the longest total reign the ledger's own "Quick Facts" table records, ending only when Fury was forced to retire from professional wrestling. Ultimate Thrasher and a wrestler billed as Roundhouse then traded it five times across 1999 alone, before "Mr. Ratings" Warren Hayes took it in a battle royal on 15 January 2000, knocking out Mike Sebastian to end it — the ledger's own last recorded champion. The held record then goes dark: by the time the revived WWO's Superstar Showcase resumes coverage on 24 August 2001, Tyrone "Purple" Hayes is already champion, defending against Steve Dumars, and nothing the museum holds explains how the belt passed from Warren Hayes to him.
