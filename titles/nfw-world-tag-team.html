Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NFW's tag championship, renamed the Everette Memorial Tag Team Championship when it returned in 2008 — for Avery Everette, the figure the fed's own history names as the first owner of the UWA, the Prodigy-era federation NFW says it grew out of. The Showstoppers were the inaugural champions, beating The Oblivion Express and Hellmachine in a three-way dance at Crash 5 on Christmas Day 2000. Hellmachine took the belts at Banned 4 Life and held them 440 days; The Original Showstoppers won a six-team playoff at Futureshock in August 2002 and the titles were retired at the end of that night, unreinstated until Supercrash I in February 2008. The Hollywood Wrecking Crew won the renamed belts in a tournament, and the ledger's own last reign is the Superfly Express, who beat Jack Bryant and Malik Anderson in the final of the Avery Cup.
