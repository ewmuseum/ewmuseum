EUWC World Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   EUWC (1996)
Active   1996–2007

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EUWC World Heavyweight Championship is the fed's flagship title, first won by STEALTH at Summer Sizzler on 28 July 1996 — the same week EUWC's own Ultra Brawl series began. The belt changed hands rapidly through its founding two years — Fred Rogers, Don Hall (three times), Genesis Taylor, Dan Sokolov, X.Plosion, HICAL (twice), YAHOO and Chet Jenkins all held it before HICAL's own refusal to defend it at When Worlds Collide helped trigger EUWC's first hiatus in April 1998. HICAL, STEALTH, Dan Sokolov and Erik Kelly kept it moving through the fed's second era, into a run of short reigns (Ray Santana, Triple T and Neighborhood Sniper trading it four times inside two months in 2000) before David Harten's win, at the same show the ledger bills "Caged In!," carried it into a second hiatus that November. Cuchulain, Jordan Lockhart, Buck Naked, STEALTH and Rob Sampson cycled it through a third short era before a third hiatus in June 2002; Jordan Lockhart's second reign reopened it in April 2003, and Paul Doom, Lord Alucard, Angel of Death, Jay Smash, Holocaust (twice), Damian Stone, Danny Collins, Sean Taylor and Stephen Greer carried it across a fourth hiatus the fed's own ledger buries inside Sean Taylor's own 2004–2005 reign, deducting 220 days from its recorded length rather than pretending the promotion never went dark. STEALTH carried it across several further reigns spanning more than a decade, the last coming at the 4th Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup on 28 October 2007, a sixteen-man tournament held after the belt was declared vacant amid the chaos that followed Ultra Brawl IX. Through 2006 and 2007 the belt changed hands rapidly among the fed's own later roster — Jay Smash three times, Adam Benjamin, Nero and "Classy" Mike C among them — before STEALTH's own final win closed out the surviving ledger.
