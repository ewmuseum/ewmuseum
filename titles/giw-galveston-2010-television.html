Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's Television Championship: 28 reigns are served here, across 23 different champions, the first beginning on 13 February 2011 with Joshua Acquin and the last on 7 September 2015 with Alexis Terry. By the dates served here the longest is Joshua Acquin's, 77 days from 4 December 2011, which the fed's own history counts as 11 days; of the reigns whose dates fit the history's own lengths, the longest is Henry Malkin's, 70 days from 19 February 2012. Of these, 7 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). It was retired twice by the fed, in December 2011 and July 2012, and reinstated on 15 March 2015.
