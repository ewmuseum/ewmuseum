Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The AAWC's own tag team championship, held across the fed's two surviving cards by two different teams and never once seen changing hands. In August 1995 it was on THE ARMED FORCES, Battleship and Submarine, led by their manager TTL, who kept it at Cameron Indoor Arena when the referee ruled a double disqualification on a challenge by ARROGANCE — Joey Melton and Peyton Wright, with Teri Melton at ringside — after Steven Flair and GUNS both got involved. By Oktoberfest '96 it was on "Showtime" Shawn Matthews and "Flannel Animal" Kurt Taylor, booked to defend against the Dirty Rotten Scoundrels, a team the broadcast booth spent the first night pretending to be frightened of. The Vegas Connection had come close to the belts earlier that year, and the Untouchables won their way into the second night's match on the first. When and from whom either reign began, and whether either ended, is not in the surviving record.

Attested by the fed's own Oktoberfest '96 (Night 1) card of 25 October 1996 (promotions/nfw/archive/news-1996-10-25-aawc-oktoberfest-96-night-1.html).
