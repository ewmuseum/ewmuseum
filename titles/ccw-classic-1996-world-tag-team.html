Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Murder Inc. became CCW's first tag team champions by beating the Devestation Express, and were billed to defend the belts against Fire & Ice at the fed's own "Explosion!!" pay-per-view (30 March 1997). By the fed's first RSPWF broadcast that July, Fire & Ice held them, by a change no surviving document records. When CCW returned in 2001, its own cards called Fire & Ice the last CCW Tag Team Champions.
