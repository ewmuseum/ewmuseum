Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The other Northeast-branded belt from the founding weekend (Jonathan Sunset), its lineage is the wing's most volatile: it passed through Brody Thunder, a vacancy-filling battle royal (Mr. Ed Rogers), Dell Pickle, Zack Sharp, the Springer (twice, trading with Sharp), Rogers again, King Apples, and finally Bill Bob Woijicowski, who won it at Winter Storm '97 wrestling in disguise as a mystery entrant.
