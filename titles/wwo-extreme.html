Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Formed by merging the ERW Television Title into the WWO's own championship picture once the two promotions came together, and unified away again within a year: the ledger runs Christopher Stonebreaker's own first reign against Derrick Michaels through American Eagle, Jack "Tower of Terror" Harper, "Big Daddy" Justin Fischer, Vader X and "Prime Time" Phill Massacre — vacated once along the way by Fischer's own departure from the federation — before its own notes record it merged into the International People's Championship. The Extreme Title's own life, start to finish, spans less than a year and a half of the fed's own held record.

Ledger source: promotions/wwo/archive/title-history-extreme.html. The ledger's own two entries for American Eagle and Christopher Stonebreaker carry a date conflict the source itself never explains — recorded as printed, not resequenced.
