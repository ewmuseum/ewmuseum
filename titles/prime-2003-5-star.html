PRIME 5 Star Title

Placard

Promotion   PRIME (2003)
Active   2003–2011
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PRIME's 5 Star Title was first won by Mark Blaze on 23 June 2003. The fed's own record carries 31 reigns on it, across 25 different champions. The longest is Tyler Rayne's, 275 days from 30 January 2008. Three of its reigns ended outside a match — stripped from the holder. Roxy Phoenix held it at the end of the surviving record.
