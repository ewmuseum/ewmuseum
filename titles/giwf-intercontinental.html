Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Twenty-eight reigns, headlined by Corporal Punishment's own three separate runs and Lyle Wile's 113-day reign, the longest in the belt's history; Jay Phoenix held it 53 days in 2001, between Celest's two reigns. Jaxx's own second reign is the final Intercontinental Championship the GIWF's own record names. Restarts as the UWF's own Intercontinental Championship.
