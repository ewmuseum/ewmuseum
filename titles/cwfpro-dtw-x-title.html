Promotion   CWF-Pro (1997)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened February 2006 when OPG won a battle royal eliminating “156” to become the DTW brand's own 1st X Champion. OPG (6), X (4) and Eighty Ninety (4) return most often across 35 recorded reigns, though the great majority — 29 of the 35 — survive only as bare ledger names. Jimmy Lee closes the ledger's own account, defeating Street Fighter to unify the DTW X Title with the CWF-Pro Intercontinental Title.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 35 reigns in full; all 35 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/dtw-x-title.html for the underlying primary source.
