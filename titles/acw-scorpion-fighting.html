ACW Scorpion Fighting Championship

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

ACW's hardcore belt, and the one belt in the fed whose fate a champion got to decide out loud. It entered ACW on 11 May 2003, when Vincent Pembridge won a six-person fight to become its first champion, and Quinton May took it from him at King of Ages that September. Quinton May, Phil Atken, Chris Messiah and Azrael Asesino held it through 2004; Fejona Min held it alongside the Television Championship from Relentless on 30 September, and when Seymour Almasy won the Television title at breakOUT the fed's owner handed him this one too and left it to him whether the two stayed merged. On Courage 72 Almasy announced they would not — “the two titles will REMAIN SEPARATE” — and set his own rule for which belt went up in which kind of match: the Scorpion in anything with a stipulation, the Television title in a straight one. He defended it that same night in a kendo stick match, and lost it at End Game 2005, where Lowell Dot Com pinned him first in a five-way elimination match for both belts. Coral Avalon, Brandon Youngblood, Calypso and Jimmy Cain held it through 2006, and the fed's belt page vacated it that July. Alias won it when the fed came back in 2009, and Iceman, Landon Stevens, Omega, Fejona Min and Tyson XL held it through the 2010 tour, Fejona Min carrying it into the bout at King of Ages 2010 that its own title page says unified it with theAsylum's Black Title as the ACW [Black] Scorpion Title. Khristain Keller won it on 27 January 2011, on a card that is not served, and on the fan board's run Keith Scott Zimmerman and GoldenHAWK, billed as BlackHAWK, carried it into 2013.

The fed's own title page, note *13: at King of Ages 2010 "there was something of unification bout that involved the ACW Scorpion Fighting Title and the infamous Black Title from theAsylum. The result was the birth of the ACW [Black] Scorpion Title"; Fejona Min won it. The Black Title's reigns before that night are theAsylum's.
