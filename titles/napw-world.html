Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NAPW's own top title from its first night on — The Plague won it in a match at the very debut show, 31 Oct 2005 — through a densely-chained run of classic-era champions (Patrick Bickle, "Perfection" Evan Cartwright, and two Ravager reigns, the second a ten-defense run ended by Donovan Astros on a date the fed's own broadcast called live, 31 Jul 2007) into an overseas ECFW/REBEL tour and a three-plus-year Astros reign confirmed still standing by the fed's own Sept 2010 rankings, the classic era's own last word. Into the promotion's 2010 closure and 2013 revival, the title restarted vacant and ran through five more champions — Anton Petrov, Clancy King, Abbey Graves, Jay Deschain, and Declan Black, the revived era's own last-recorded champion — a chain the fed's own title-history ledger keeps complete from Nov 2013 on. One real, named gap remains in the classic-era chain: exactly when and to whom Ravager first lost the title, sometime between his Feb 2006 win and Bickle's Oct 2006 win, isn't in the held record.
