Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MWE's secondary singles belt, and the one its ledger records changing hands most often. Sinc Mercier beat James Sullivan for it at MWE Turmoil on 14 September 2008. Alejandro Rodriguez took it from him at Wrestle Wars II that October in a three-way with Mason Resoan and was stripped of it once the fed reviewed the tape of the finish. Mercier won it back that November, lost it to Resoan at Adrenaline Rush II and beat him for it again at Friday Night Overdrive on 13 February 2009 in a Ladder Match. The ledger then recorded it vacant, to be decided at Tribulation; the belt was still being defended in Shattered Glass and Iron Man Table matches through the year, Mason Resoan and Leon Roberts fighting for the vacant title at Independence Day that July. At Tribulation on 24 May 2009, Sinc Mercier won a Scavenger Hunt match to unify this belt with the Intercontinental Championship under his own name — his fourth US title, his first IC title, both at once.
