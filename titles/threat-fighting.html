Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A championship thReat ran in both of its runs. Quincy Brown won it from Amp Canon at ReLoad 2002 and held it until the belt was retired with the company's closure on 23 June 2002. Vincent Pembridge won it from Jason Semps in a cage on Sunday Night Flaw 18 in 2003 (the fed's title history dates the win to Flaw 17 and counts ninety days), Jere Casarella took it from him on Sunday Night Flaw 25, and psycho-Rio won it from Casarella in a Fight To The Death at Double Or Nothing 2003, still champion when the title history ends.

thReat's title history (threat.theewe.net/cgi-bin/superstars.cgi?titlehistory), served at /promotions/threat/archive/threat-title-history.html.
