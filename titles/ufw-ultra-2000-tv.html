Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The federation's most-traded championship: twenty-three reigns in eight months, opened on The Body on 24 December 1999. Iceman held it three times and B.A. Bruiser three, Bloodshed, JiN and Anguish twice each. Nemesis' win of 22 August 2000 is the last title change of any kind the federation's own record records.
