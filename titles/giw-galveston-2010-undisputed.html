Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's Undisputed Championship: 18 reigns are served here, across 14 different champions, the first beginning on 17 February 2011 with Eric Sparrow and the last on 18 August 2019 with Bronx Valescence. By the dates served here the longest is Alexis Terry's, 257 days from 23 June 2013, which the fed's own history counts as 54 days; of the reigns whose dates fit the history's own lengths, the longest is Alioth Starre's, 155 days from 29 May 2011. Of these, 4 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). It is the Galveston Island Championship under the name it took on 17 February 2011; it took in the Deep South belt on that date and again on 29 October 2014, and the North Beach belt on 7 March 2014.
