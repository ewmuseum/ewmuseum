IEWF/LWC LWC Rio Grande Championship

Placard

Promotion   IEWF/LWC (1997)
Active   1998–1999

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A title fought over in extreme-rules gimmick matches, changing hands at the promotion's own South Laredo Rodeogrounds. Steve Spector held it through the autumn of 1998 and beat Adam Craig in two out of three falls at Last Tangle In Texarkana; Thantasia and Johnny Rage held it in early 1999.
