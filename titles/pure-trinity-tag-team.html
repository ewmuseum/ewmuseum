Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The three-man tag team championship PURE instated when it re-opened in January 2003, in place of the two-man tag titles it retired at the same time. Shaun Styles and the Sushi Crew were its first champions, beating the Black Dragons for the belts. The Suburban Ninja Clan took them at Budokan Hall on 9 March 2003, and eight days later answered with an open challenge to any three-man team in the federations PURE shared a host with, offering to defend the belts on those feds' own shows. The surviving record carries no later change.

Instated on PURE's own studio show Axcess, in the fed's own words: "PURE has now instated the brand new PURE Trinity Tag Team titles. Puro wrestling is quite famous for their 6 man tag team action and it was only a matter of time before we embraced that staple of the culture." Its lineage sits at the tail of the same title-history page that carries the two-man tag titles' (newsdata board 78, the page world.shtml renders): "#3 - Trinity Tag Team Championship declared. Sushi Crew / Shaun Styles become new champions over Black Dragons." Source: Argyle's PURE deposit (collection 0159).
