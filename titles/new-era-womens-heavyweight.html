Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The one championship of New ERA of Wrestling's women's division, won by Karla Starr at the first BattleBRAWL in December 2004 and held by her for two years until Juliet Marceau announced it over on RAUCOUS of 19 December 2006 and Starr took off its faceplate to reveal the VENUS World Heavyweight Championship underneath.

The whole of this title's run is the one reign. The BattleBRAWL 2 pay-per-view (19 September 2006) has Starr retain over Foxx, and the broadcasts of 2006 show her defending it or putting it up against her challengers; its video package dates her win 19 December 2004 where Marceau and the BattleBRAWL card give 3 December. The same belt carries on as the VENUS World Heavyweight Championship.
