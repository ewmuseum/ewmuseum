Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

First attested already held by Headbuster as of LTD's own December 2001 front page, and defended clean through Winter's Wrath. Headbuster lost it to Scott Royal at an uncaptured show sometime before 19 March 2002, and Royal was still champion — and still defending it, in a three-way against Humpy and Poltergeist that both men earned by eliminator wins the same night — when the surviving record runs out.
