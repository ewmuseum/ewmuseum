UFW World Title

Placard

Promotion   UFW (Ultra)
Active   1999

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The federation's top championship, first won by Executioner on 19 November 1999 and changing hands seventeen more times over the nine months the fed ran. Layeth the Smack Down held it three times and Myst three times, the two of them trading it across January and again in the closing summer; Roody Poo, the fed's own house jobber, held it for two days in April after counting his own pinfall as the match's referee. Myst's third win, 20 August 2000, is the last reign it recorded.
