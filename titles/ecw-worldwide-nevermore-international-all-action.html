ECW Worldwide ECW International All Action Championship

Active   2006–2007

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt exclusive to Revolution, announced on the first Revolution of August 2006 for a month-long tournament of fast-paced wrestlers; Jushin Lyger, Ultimo Dragon, Yoshihiro Tajiri and Psicosis are the names the commentary gives for it. Tajiri beats Lyger in the final at Heatwave to become the first champion and keeps the belt over Super Dragon on Revolution after Heatwave. Tajiri keeps the belt until Anarchy Rulz, where Jushin Lyger wins the Triangle Match with AJ Styles and him; The Great Sasuke takes it from Lyger at Guilty As Charged and loses it to Vito on F' The Mainstream a week later, and Vito keeps it over Chris Daniels on Revolution. Vito loses the belt to Christopher Daniels at November To Remember in the Stairway To Hell Match with Super Crazy, when Daniels hangs on to the harness as the ladder collapses and drops to the floor with the belt; Daniels keeps it over Michael Shane at Got Blood? and is still champion when the pages of February 2007 end. The diary's own title history omits Daniels's reign and lists Vito as champion until 28 February 2007, so the history and the text of the shows disagree; both are recorded, and the reign dates follow the text. At Living Dangerously on 28 February 2007, the date the history gives for CM Punk's win, Punk takes the belt from Christopher Daniels in the Triangle Match with Michael Shane when Raven hits Daniels with a chair, so on that date the history and the text of the shows agree even though the history leaves Daniels out; Punk pins Daniels again with a low blow on Blood, Sweat and Beers and is champion when the pages of April 2007 end, with Lance Storm, who won an International All Action title shot in the Stairway To Hell match at Baptism Of Fire, set to challenge him at Cyberslam. At Cyberslam CM Punk keeps the belt over Lance Storm in the No Way Out Match with a GTS while the masked man traps Abyss and Vito outside, and at Hardcore Heaven he keeps it over Storm again with a GTS after Monsters Inc chokeslam Storm onto Abyss's knee; Punk is champion when the pages of June 2007's second week end. On Revolution in June 2007 CM Punk knocks out Paul Burchill and Shane Douglas with the belt, and at Wrestlepalooza and Summer Assylum Vito Thomaselli turns on him and powerbombs him through a table; the belt is not defended in the range, and Punk is champion when the pages of August 2007's first week end, with the history's 29 September 2007 end still ahead. At Heatwave in August 2007 CM Punk hits Paul Burchill with the belt and Vito Thomaselli with a GTS; in September he defends the belt for the first time in months, keeping it over Lance Storm, Big Sasuke Cool and Eddie Guerrero, and Vito Thomaselli is booked against him at Anarchy Rulz, with the history's 29 September 2007 end still ahead. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 Vito Thomaselli pins CM Punk with a Crucifix Roll-Up (29 September in the history); at Guilty As Charged CM Punk wins the Last Man Standing Match when referee Jim Molineaux stops it, with Monsters Inc. at ringside (26 October), and he keeps the belt over Lance Storm on Blood, Sweat and Beers. On the first Revolution of November 2007 Paul Heyman has about fifteen security guards strip Punk of the belt ('it ain't yours anymore', 3 November in the history) and starts the International All Action Title Tournament, open to 'hard working, respectful talent'. The first-round winners are Ricky Marvin (over Kensuke Sasaki), Teddy Hart (over Psicosis), Slyk Tayshaun Dudley (over Scorpio Sky) and Excalibur (over Paul London); in the semi-finals Slyk beats Teddy Hart and Ricky Marvin beats Excalibur, and the final, Ricky Marvin against Slyk Tayshaun Dudley, is on the card of November To Remember XV. Ricky Marvin beats Slyk Tayshaun Dudley in the final at November To Remember XV (30 November 2007 in the history) after Big Dick Jr. interferes against his own cousin, and is the new champion; the Code of Diminishing Extremes sets the title to lucha libre rules, with a twenty count and two falls out of three.

The history dates the win 29 September 2007, a day after the show's date (the show is filed under Friday, week 4, September 2007); the history's dates are the ones filed.

Ricky Marvin30 November 2007—Ricky Marvin, the winner of the International All Action Title Tournament's semi-final over Excalibur, beats Slyk Tayshaun Dudley in the final at November To Remember XV: Slyk comes back in the fifth minute until Big Dick Jr. slides in and hits his cousin with a Total Penetration, and Marvin pins him with a Shining Wizard in a match the diary rates C-; he dances round the ring with the belt. The Code of Diminishing Extremes read on the Saturday show sets the title to lucha libre rules, a twenty count when a wrestler is outside the ring and victory on two falls out of three.
The history lists the reign as 'present', and the record of these pages ends with Marvin still the champion.
