Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt Chris Jericho carries through the diary's first ten shows, in a reign that its own title history dates from 23 November 2005 to 8 August 2006. He keeps it over Lance Storm, Doug Williams, Bryan Danielson and Cactus Jack, and again on the tenth show, where the commentary counts his reign as entering its seventh month. Chris Jericho keeps the belt through the pages of July, then loses it to Rhino on 8 August 2006 by the diary's title history, wins it back a week later, and loses it again to Rhino in the Triangle Match with Christian at Heatwave. Rhino keeps the belt until Anarchy Rulz, where the returning Shane Douglas beats him for it; Kensuke Sasaki takes it from Douglas at Guilty As Charged and still holds it when the pages of November 2006 end. Vito Thomaselli takes the belt from Kensuke Sasaki on the debut Blood, Sweat and Beers on 4 December 2006 by the diary's title history, when Francine lowblows Sasaki; Vito keeps it through December and January with Francine's help and then the New Age Enterprise's, until Bret Hart beats him for it with the Sharpshooter on Blood, Sweat and Beers on 6 February 2007; Bret Hart is champion when the pages of February 2007 end. Paul Burchill takes the belt from Bret Hart at Living Dangerously on 28 February 2007 by the diary's title history, with a clean C-4 after he sends the New Age Enterprise away, and loses it back to Bret Hart on Blood, Sweat and Beers on 22 March 2007 when his knee gives way; Bret Hart loses it to Chris Jericho in the Triangle Match with Christian on Blood, Sweat and Beers on 15 April 2007, and Jericho is champion when the pages of April 2007 end, with Rob Van Dam booked to challenge him at Cyberslam. At Cyberslam on 24 April 2007 by the diary's title history Rob Van Dam takes the belt from Chris Jericho in the Lumberjack Match, when Shawn Michaels Superkicks Jericho off the ropes while Christian is held at ringside and a Five Star Frog Splash wins; at Hardcore Heaven on 29 May 2007 AJ Styles, who debuted in May, takes it from Van Dam with the Canadian Connection's hockey sticks and a Styles Clash while Michaels is away from ringside; Styles is champion when the pages of June 2007's second week end, having beaten Rey Mysterio Jr. with a low blow and lost a non-title match to Juventud Guerrera. On Revolution in June 2007 AJ Styles beats Juventud Guerrera with a low blow and the Styles Clash, and at Wrestlepalooza he keeps the belt over Guerrera with another Styles Clash after pushing Guerrera's foot off the rope while the referee does not see it; at Summer Assylum he keeps it a third time when Francine's pretended turn lets Guerrera hit Styles with a low blow and Christopher Daniels hits Guerrera with the belt; Styles is champion when the pages of August 2007's first week end, with Guerrera's challenge for Heatwave still unanswered and Ultimo Dragon billed against him on the next Revolution, so the history's 28 September 2007 end is still ahead. At Heatwave in August 2007 AJ Styles keeps the belt in the Stairway To Hell Match, in which Juventud Guerrera, challenging with his mask at stake, loses the mask; in September Styles beats Jerry Lynn on Blood, Sweat and Beers, and Ultimo Dragon is booked against him at Anarchy Rulz, so the history's 28 September 2007 end is still ahead. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 Ultimo Dragon pins AJ Styles with an Asai DDT after Francine's interference (28 September); at Guilty As Charged AJ Styles wins the four-way Stairway To Hell Match by pulling the belt from the strap above the ring (26 October), and Juventud Guerrera storms in with a chair. In November 2007 Styles keeps the belt over Juventud Guerrera on Blood, Sweat and Beers when the referee misses Guerrera's foot on the rope, and over Rey Mysterio Jr. on Revolution, and loses it on Revolution on the last Monday of the month (26 November) when Guerrera rolls through his Spiral Tap into a pin, for his first title in ECW. Paul Heyman has announced a newly designed belt and a title match at November To Remember XV against the winner of a three-way match, Ultimo Dragon, who is hurt in it (an EMT says 'a cracked sternum'); in a backstage scene the company's owner and Heyman ask Rey Mysterio Jr. to wear Dragon's mask and compete as Ultimo Dragon (PDF pp.1654-1655), and the history lists Guerrera as the champion, 'present'. Juventud Guerrera keeps the belt at November To Remember XV over Ultimo Dragon, whom Francine's lowblow and DDT leave open to a 450 Splash, over The Great Sasuke on the Saturday show and over Eddie Guerrero on Revolution on the first Monday of December; Christopher Daniels is booked against him next, and the history lists Guerrera as the champion, 'present'.

Juventud Guerrera26 November 2007—Juventud Guerrera pins AJ Styles on Revolution in the rematch that Paul Heyman has booked for the show before November To Remember XV: Guerrera hooks Styles for a Juvi Driver, Styles slips over his shoulder, Guerrera hits an Enziguiri and Styles climbs for a corkscrew leg drop, the Spiral Tap, but Guerrera hooks his leg and rolls through into a pin, for his first title in ECW. Ultimo Dragon, the number one contender after a three-way match on Blood, Sweat and Beers, appears at the entrance with Francine, points at her and then at his own waist, and the champion's first defence is the World Television Championship Match against him at November To Remember XV. Juventud Guerrera keeps the title at November To Remember XV over Ultimo Dragon, who pulls Francine into the champion's chair swing and later knocks Guerrera out with the belt; Francine breaks up the cover, lowblows Dragon and hits a DDT, and Guerrera hits a 450 Splash for the pin (B), receives the new belt from Jim Molineaux, and kisses Francine. On the Saturday show he beats The Great Sasuke with a Juvi Driver in the twelfth minute and, on Revolution on the first Monday of December, Eddie Guerrero with a Juvi Driver (B); the diary's next preview books him against Christopher Daniels.
The history lists the reign as 'present', and the pages through the first Saturday of December show the champion keeping the belt (the diary's own preview of November To Remember XV and its Saturday show call him a new champion, a week after the win on 26 November); no end date is filed, and the record of these pages ends with Guerrera still the champion.
