Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Nuke and Trent Malady fought the vacant title to a 30-minute draw in the fed's own tournament final; Malady won it outright the following week in a Triple Threat, and retained it by DQ against Axon Blade before Da Messiah's own interference handed it to Nuke off-page. Kontra took it from Nuke in a Scaffold Match staged as payback for that same interference, and stands as the fed's own last-attested champion on its served Rankings page.
