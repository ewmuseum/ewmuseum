MWE X-Television Championship

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MWE's own junior-division belt, and the only one in its ledger to pass through a mask. Aeron Beowulf beat J.K. Valentino for it at Crowning Glory II on 10 August 2008; Vega Knight took it that October and was stripped of it on being drafted to Genesis. A tournament final at Adrenaline Rush II settled it as a Mask vs Career match between the masked Mysterious G and Samuel Steele, and the winner pulled his own mask off afterwards to reveal Eric Derf. Anderson surrendered the belt on air that May rather than defend it, and the fed's own text records no result for the "NEW, and PROPER Champion" match Dick Malibu promised in its place. The belt was still being defended at the October 2009 Turmoil.
