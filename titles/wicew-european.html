Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Volcano won the WICeW European Title from Sean "Iceman" Stevens on 8 November 1998, an upset the broadcast called unpredictable; Kyle Barrows held it by the fed's own 10 December 1998 championship snapshot, the transition between the two unrecorded.

The fed's own first-ever title match for this belt was billed for an unheld Thursday Night House Show after the 4 October 1998 card (Arch Angel vs. "Superstar" Steve Taylor); its own result is not recorded, and Sean "Iceman" Stevens's own path to holding the belt by 8 November is likewise unrecorded.
