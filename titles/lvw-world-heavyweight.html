LVW World Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   LVW (2005)
Active   2006–2009

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

LVW's top championship, crowned for the first time in a 60-minute Ironman match and, after an interval of untracked reigns, fought over again in a 30-man battle royal.

Later referred to in the fed's own text simply as the "LVW Championship" and "LVW Title."
