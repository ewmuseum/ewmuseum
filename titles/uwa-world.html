Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The UWA's own top prize, and the reason the promotion staged the Random Rumble at all. The belt was vacant going into 24 February 1997 — the card bills it as “up for grabs” and its own commentary calls it “one of the MOST prestigious World titles in wrestling history” — and thirty entrants were called to a Royal Rumble-style match to decide it, with the last two standing set to meet in a ladder match. Commissioner Sullivan tore that up when the final four hit the floor at the same time, ruling instead for a four-way King of the Hill ladder match: the first man to pull the belt off a hoop fifteen feet above the ring. Winston Steele, Nathan Storm, Carlos Canyeta and Javid Dones went back for it. Who came out with the belt is not in the surviving record — the one held copy of the card ends during the entrances, and no later UWA document survives to say. Eight years on, NFW's own continuity stages a revival of this same physical belt as a storyline of its own — see nfw-uwa-world for that record; it is NFW's fiction about this title, not a continuation of the real UWA's own, unresolved history.

Attested by the fed's own Random Rumble card of 24 February 1997 (promotions/nfw/archive/news-1997-02-24-uwa-2-0-random-rumble.html); no winner survives in the record. NFW's own later continuity stages a “resurrected” revival of this same belt as one of its own storylines — see nfw-uwa-world, a separate title under NFW's own promotion, not a continuation of this ledger.
