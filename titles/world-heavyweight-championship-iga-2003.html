International Grappling Association IGA World Heavyweight Championship

aka IGA World Title, IGA World Championship

Placard

Promotion   International Grappling Association (2001)
Active   2001

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The promotion's top title, first decided in a round robin at its first pay-per-view, The Battle for Fort Knox, where Matt Allen beat Mark Davenport in the deciding match. Todd Bishop took it from Allen in a scaffold match at Requiem for Pain on 31 October 2001 and held it through the year's end.
