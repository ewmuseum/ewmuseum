Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New ERA of Wrestling's own top championship, first crowned in a Fatal Fourway Battle Royal at the federation's founding show, carried through a 2009 merger and unification with a second promotion's own world title, and renamed the New ERA Championship when the federation switched to a season-based format in 2011 -- the held record's last captured card, in 2015, still finds it standing at the center of an unresolved open challenge.

Jean Rabesque was billed champion by January 2005; the Phantom Republican's own reign, begun around 12 January 2006, is now structured (see reigns) and independently corroborated by an 18 April 2006 cross-federation posting naming him champion and Jean Rabesque his immediate predecessor. A co-championship split with Rocko Daymon by mid-2009 after a merger with WFW is attested only by billing on cards served elsewhere in the held record. New ERA's own rules document, dated 15 January 2011 in its own text, states that when the federation switched to a season-based format its reigning World Heavyweight Champion "became" the first champion of a renamed New ERA Championship -- the same title continuing under a new name, not a new belt -- and eliminated the Television and PRODIGY Classic Xtreme Championships, folding their own champions into the new title's bankable-shot system. The corpus does not capture who held the belt at that exact transition, though its own next captured card already bills a champion (see reigns r004) who names, in his own promo, the rival he took the belt from. The title changed hands at least three more times across 2011 (see reigns r004-r007), with a final open challenge left unresolved when the held record's last captured card ends without a results document.
