NPWA World Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   NPWA (1993)
Active   1996–1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Traced back to the fed's own 1993 founding (Bash, its first champion), the belt changed hands six times across 1997 alone -- Amoral to Locutus to Col. Buddy Lee Jarrett to 'Superstar' Steve Sampson to 'Blackheart' Brian Masters, a November vacancy, and finally 'Hollywood Heartthrob' Dave Diamond's win in a 14-man Pinfall Royale at the fed's own year-end Xmas Gory '97 -- along the way surviving a stolen-belt cliffhanger left unresolved on a satellite cut-off.
