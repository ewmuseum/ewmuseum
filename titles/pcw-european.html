Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PCW's European Championship was first won by The Chef on 9 March 2001. PCW retired it on 8 April 2001, under its only champion.

A belt PCW ran for thirty days in the spring of 2001 and that his database holds no row for anywhere: PCW's own title list lists the other seven and nothing else, and all three of its matches carry title_id 0 on their own match record. Created when the fed scrapped the Rising Star Championship for it and retired when the fed brought the Rising Star Championship back, so its lineage is recorded as restarted from title 13. Receipts: PCW's own results pages for March Madness 2001 (4 Mar 2001), Turmoil 24 (6 Mar 2001, 442.txt), Fusion 25 (9 Mar 2001, 375.txt), Sabotage 1 (11 Mar 2001, 305.txt), Fusion 26 (16 Mar 2001, 376.txt), Turmoil 26 (20 Mar 2001, 444.txt), Sabotage 3 (25 Mar 2001, 299.txt) and Spring Fling 2001 (8 Apr 2001, 511.txt).

The Chef9 March 20018 April 2001
The only PCW European Champion — he won it in the belt's own first match, a four corners against Hans Kruger, Slade and Chris Metal, and the reign ends with the belt retired under him rather than lost. The belt was announced at March Madness 2001 of 4 Mar 2001, where Hans Kruger challenged for "the first ever PCW European Title Match" on the strength of PCW's new European television deal, and created at Turmoil 26 of 6 Mar 2001 in the fed's own voice. Fusion 25 of 9 Mar 2001 crowned The Chef in a four corners match — "Clay Clayborne: The following contest is a four corners match for the PCW European Championship!" … "Winner and European Champion: The Chef Time: 9:56" … "So Chef is PCW's first European Champion" — and Sabotage 1 of 11 Mar 2001 corroborates it ("We see Chef win his match to become the first ever PCW European champion"). Two defences survive: Fusion 26 of 16 Mar 2001 ("Winner and still Euro Champion: The Chef Time: 7:15") and Turmoil 26 of 20 Mar 2001, a no-DQ falls-count-anywhere four corners the champion restipulated himself ("Winner and still European Champion: The Chef Time: 5:01"). Sabotage 3 of 25 Mar 2001 ran a seventeen-man battle royal for the number-one contendership, won by "The Graduate" Jay Gavin, and Spring Fling 2001 of 8 Apr 2001 is that title match — The Chef retained ("Winner: The Chef Time: 3:00") and the belt was retired in the same segment. Back to Old School 2001 of 16 Sep 2001 still bills him "PCW European champion" in his own ring introduction, among his other titles.
