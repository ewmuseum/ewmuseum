Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Eddie and Kid Krazy won the first Tag Team titles at Kick Ass Inc on 30 June 2003, and Steve Sinclair put the belts on the shelf on 10 November 2003 when the two stopped being partners. They came back as a yearly one-night tournament held at the Bruise Cruise, the way the fed's own history says the King of the Ring is run, and its table lists eight champions from 2003 to the Gary Barber and Shawn Sanders pairing of 2010.
