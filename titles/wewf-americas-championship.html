WeWF Americas Championship

Placard

Promotion   WeWF (1990)
Active   1991
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

One of three Continental belts. It changed hands seven times in fourteen weeks, was declared vacant when Frank Steel found himself holding three belts at once, was settled in an eight-man tournament, and ended on a Boston crab at a Supercard — after which the Summer League simply did not carry it.
