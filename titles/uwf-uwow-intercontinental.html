Held by Kurt Angle at the September 2006 restart, vacated when Angle pursued the World title, then won by Scott Steiner over Raven in a tournament final. Ultimate Warrior took the belt from Steiner at Fallout 2007 and, per the fed's own ledger, carried it with him to ICWC when he moved federations — continuing as the ICWC Intercontinental Championship.
