Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own second belt and, by its own commentary, the one both of its last two champions won out of a tournament — each of them going through David D to get there. Wil the Thrill retained it in a main event against Carlito Caribbean Cool in the spring of 2005; Edge held it after him; JC Sinn carried it into December. On 9 October 2005 the fed ran three first-round matches for it in one night, and its founder came back at Christmas to demand it be dropped for the Pride Title instead.

No title history survives: the pinned TPG: Title History thread is listed on the fed's own roleplay forum, posted 4 December 2005, and was never captured (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/forum-tpg-aggression-132.html). An unresolved claim sits between this belt and the other: FoB's own Rebirth of 20 September 2005 has its commentary announce an interfering wrestler as “THAT'S MORTIS! THE TPG CHAMPION!!” (FoB's own Rebirth of 20 September 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-rebirth-2005-09-20-9684.html)), and the fed's own record names no belt he could have been carrying. Both of this promotion's singles championships are candidates and no document names one; the claim is recorded on both rows and settled on neither.
