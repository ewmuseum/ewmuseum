Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RUSH's World Championship was first won by Cassius Deamont on 16 June 2002. The fed's own title log carries 3 reigns on it, across 3 different champions, and ends when the belt was vacated on 18 October 2002; the card of Decimation 2002 (22 December 2002), the pay-per-view RUSH and PCW staged together, crowns a fourth champion, Tyler Valentine, in the match for the vacant title. The longest reign with both dates on record is Cassius Deamont's, 63 days from 16 June 2002. One of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. Valentine was still billed champion at Shanghaied III on 26 January 2003, and the surviving record carries no later change.

The fed's own title log stops at the vacancy of 18 October 2002 and its own title list names no World champion after Brock Newbludd. The pay-per-view card of Decimation 2002, staged by PCW and RUSH together, has the match for the vacant title: the announcer's introduction above, the commentary "Two and a half months without a champion and we're finally going to have our rightful leader!", Tyler Valentine beating McGlocklan, and the announcer's closing call. The cards that follow bill him champion. Fusion 103 (3 Jan 2003) stages "Tyler Valentine's RUSH World Championship coronation" and introduces him as "the RUSH World Champion"; Turmoil 107 (7 Jan 2003) introduces him as "the RUSH World Heavyweight Champion"; Shanghaied III (26 Jan 2003) bills "Tyler Valentine (c) vs Marvin Harris" for the RUSH World title, the announcer introduces him as "the RUSH World Heavyweight Champion", and the match ends with the announcer's "YOUR WINNER, AND STILL RUSH WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAM—" cut off by Valentine, who finishes the call himself. No card on file shows the belt leaving him: none of the PCW cards on file dated after Shanghaied III (the Turmoil and Fusion cards of 28 Jan 2003 onward, March Madness, Spring Fling and Supremacy 2003, Shanghaied IV and the Turmoil cards to 17 Apr 2004) names him or the RUSH World title, so the reign carries no end date and none is claimed.
