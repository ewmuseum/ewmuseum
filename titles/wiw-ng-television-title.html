WIW:NG Television Title

Placard

Promotion   WIW:NG (1999)
Active   2000

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Introduced on 31 January 2000 when Benshaw beat Eric Austin, the Television Title was retired after Chris Storm's reign of July 2000 and revived on 1 December 2000 in a fatal four way. The fed's own history lists 29 reigns to Christian Niels's of 18 August 2002, among them Angelo Deville's of 13 to 27 May 2002 and Bryan Statis's of 30 July to 18 August. The November 2002 champions list names Johnny Fame.

Reign list and footnotes from the fed's own Television Title history, captured 31 August 2002; later holder from its champions list of 29 November 2002.
