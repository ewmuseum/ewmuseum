EUWC Hardcore Championship

Placard

Promotion   EUWC (1996)
Active   1999–2003

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EUWC Hardcore Championship is attested only through the Ultra Brawl, Bloodbath and Aftershock event-history pages — no dedicated title-history ledger for it survives, and several hand-offs between its eight recorded reigns are unrecorded gaps rather than invented continuity. Neighborhood Sniper won it in a Ladder Match at Ultra Brawl III in October 1999; T-Charger, Johnny Hardcore, Kaine, Marshall Snow, Sevyn and Bibyl carried it in turn across the fed's second, third and fourth eras, the title going dark for stretches the surviving record never explains. Bibyl's win over Sevyn at Blood Bath, 30 November 2003, is the last EUWC Hardcore Championship reign attested anywhere in the held record. Distinct from the 1997 EUWC Hardcore Television Championship, a separate, one-match-only belt from the fed's founding era.
