WU One Shot Championship

aka Contender Championship

Placard

Promotion   WU (Wrestling Unplugged, 2008)
Active   2008

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Called the Contender Championship at its start (Chad Vargas was the first Contender champion at See No Evil), then the One Shot Championship: Skull Daze, Twilight Rayn, Troy Gafgen and CJ Gates held it from October to December 2008. Five defences earned a World title shot; the only cash-in on record is Skull Daze's, helped by four defences the owner awarded him.

The fed's page lists the first reign under the Contender name; the two names are taken as one belt because the page heads the list One Shot.
