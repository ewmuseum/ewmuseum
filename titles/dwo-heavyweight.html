Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The top of the fed, and the belt its own Champions page described as brutal to reach and worth beatings to keep. John Miro won it on 21 April 2002 and the record loses it almost at once: AJ Trinidad is champion by that June, Adam Stryker by the autumn, Jackson Rust by the end of October, and none of the three changes is dated anywhere held. Lexis Bane's shutdown emptied it, and a World Title tournament through December 2002 put it back on Kaiowas at the first Travesty of 2003. He lost it on 20 April 2003 at Primetime Central's own New Era supercard, to Donny Diamond, inside the DWO Asylum — the only change in the whole lineage the record dates to the day — and he had it back by 10 July, when the fed's own commentary calls him the DWO World Champion in the middle of losing a non-title match.

Billed the DWO Heavyweight Championship on the fed's own 2002 Champions page and by Primetime Central's own New Era results, and the DWO World Title in the fed's own show scripts from the autumn of 2002 onwards.
