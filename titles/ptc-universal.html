PTC Universal Championship

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Made for Angelo Deville after he won GTT3, PTC's third Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, in 2002. PCW's Alliances card of 24 November 2002 bills it as the WT Universal Championship; Deville kept it there against Xavier Kannon, then against Syko Arsonist on a DWO special event, and lost it to Alan Kriegman on OSW's Saturday Night Slam, four judges' votes to three. A round robin to find Kriegman a challenger was called off unfinished. PTC's history of its Global Championship leaves this belt out.

Angelo Deville's PRIME biography card; PCW's Alliances 2002 card; PTCpedia's GTT history; PTC forum posts 132383, 141903, 170608 and 120404 (Deville's own account), 297925 (Syko Arsonist: "the only PTC vs WT match for the Universal Title") and 241787 (the PTCpedia editor: the Universal and Global titles "were separate belts").
