Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Vacant at the January 2008 brand-extension launch, filled when Samoa Joe won a King of the Mountain reverse-ladder match. Big Show and finally Brock Lesnar (in a Triple Threat over Big Show and Rob Van Dam) held it on the surviving ledger, before Lesnar's reign was unified into the ICWC World Heavyweight Championship at ICWC Capital Combat (2009).
