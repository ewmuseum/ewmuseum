WAR! World WAR! Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   WAR! (1995)
Active   1996

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A tag title that changed hands at least five times across this pull's own read shows, from the Brotherhood through the Star Smashers, the Aderondac Brothers and Industrial Revolution to the Annihilators, the last won with a moonsault off the roof of a parked van in the WARena parking lot.

The Brotherhood26 April 1996—Won via a surprise contractual title match sprung on the Ghetto Boyz immediately after the latter unified the tag belts.
Retains against the Ghetto Boyz by double disqualification in a "loser leaves town" match on "Tuesday Night WAR!fare" (6 May 1996). No document in the held record records the loss of the title; the Star Smashers hold it a week later.
Star Smashers6 May 1996—Title change not recovered in the held record.
Lose the World WAR! Tag Team titles to the Aderondac Brothers on "Monday Night WAR!fare" (13 May 1996).
Aderondac Brothers13 May 1996—Won by pinfall over the Star Smashers.
No document in the held record records the loss of the title; Industrial Revolution holds it by Dec 1996.
Lost Boys24 May 1996—Won a Street Fight by pinfall.
Ends the existing Aderondac Brothers reign. Lost Boys's own first tag reign.
Theatre of Pain21 June 1996—Won by pinfall.
Also billed "Total Destruction" on the era's arena-card results (e.g. "WAR! IN WINNIPEG `96", 23 Jun 1996, retaining by DQ). Loses the title back to the Lost Boys at an unrecovered date.
Lost Boys1996-08 (circa)—Title change not recovered in the held record.
Lost Boys's own second tag reign. Retains through 22 Oct 1996 (Wendy Addams's own interference against Industrial Revolution).
Industrial Revolution29 October 1996—Title change not recovered in the held record.
Loses the World WAR! Tag Team titles to the Annihilators on "Nuclear WAR!fare" (7 Dec 1996).
The Annihilators7 December 1996—Won a falls-count-anywhere match against Industrial Revolution.
Lost Boys28 January 1997—Won by pinfall, ending the Annihilators' own reign. The Lost Boys' own third World WAR!
This reign's own end is not recovered.
Tyler Stevens & Ricky Deathwish (Tyler Stevens, "The Mangler" Ricky Deathwish)31 March 1999—Won by pinfall, aided by a corrupted guest referee.
