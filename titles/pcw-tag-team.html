Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PCW's Tag Team Championship was first won by Goovil on 22 October 2000. The fed's own record carries 51 reigns on it, across 35 different champions. The longest is E2's, 274 days from 25 May 2003. Three of its reigns ended outside a match — stripped from the holder or vacated. Mega Soaring Arrows held it at the end of the surviving record.

A TAG RESULT IS NOT A TITLE CALL, so the win is bracketed 9–12 Oct rather than dated to the Turmoil 51 tourney match, which carries no title stipulation and no crowning. THE SECOND READING, recorded and not followed: that Red October is the tournament FINAL and the "(c)" is the card's own error, which would date the win 21 Oct 2001 exact. It is not followed because the fed's own ring announcer names them champions BEFORE the bell and Fusion 54 bills them champions nine days earlier, while nothing but the tournament's own name argues the other way; Dave's closing line is "DA WESTSIDAZ ARE THE FIVE TIME TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS", never "new".
