Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

X-Kalibur's reign was cut short by Leo in May 2001, and Leo kept it through a sixty-minute triple threat Iron Man match at Detonation. By August it was "Deadly" Derek Irvin's until John O'Riordan beat him, and that autumn a tournament decided it, won by Shrapnel over Tyson Bryson at The Process of Weeding Out.
