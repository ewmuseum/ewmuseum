Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The consolation prize of the founding tournament and the busiest belt on the ledger: six reigns across five men, with a vacancy in the middle when its holder won the World Championship and had to give it up. Damien Flayer held it twice, John Lawless twice, and Joe Reed took it off Flayer with a small package on 9 April 1992. Titan Morgan's win over Lawless on 14 October 1992 is where the ledger leaves it; a month later he defended it to a double countout and then carried it into the Clash of the Masters against the PWA's own United States Champion, and lost by countout.

Transcribed from the federation's own singles ledger (promotions/fwf-1992/archive/fwf-singles-record-1993-01-08.html).
