Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own 4 April 1999 card and Title History page together trace a long, mostly undated chain: Titan won it in a Four Man Battle Royale at Manchester Colosseum, then Chris Mann, The Wraith, HICAL (twice, the title held up between), and Titan (a second time) each held it in turn, none of it dated. Rowdy Piper then beat Titan in an I Quit Match at Gaslaw Gala, 16 May 1999 — the fed's own broadcast of that night receipts the change directly. Lord Ravager beat Rowdy Piper the following day, 17 May 1999, per the fed's own Title History page alone — the last champion this holding attests.

The fed's own 4 April 1999 card bills the newly-crowned champion as "the NEW NeWA European / BCW National Champion" in one breath — the same belt carrying a hub-level name alongside its own promotion name, per the fed's own text, not a separate title and not evidence of any join to another wing.
