Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

EPW's Hardcore Championship was first won by Blackheart on 1 August 2003. The fed's own record carries 3 reigns on it, across 3 different champions. The longest is King Hardkore's, 131 days from 24 August 2003. One of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. The belt was vacated on an unrecorded date after Tom Conway's reign, and the surviving record carries no later change.
