WWA Television Championship

aka New York City Championship, NCW New York City Championship

Placard

Promotion   WWA (1997–2019)
Active   1997–2008

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Contested from the fed's earliest recovered years under the New York City Championship name — a New York City Title tournament crowns Randy Cross champion at the RIC Athletic Complex on 21 November 1997, and its own served card explicitly bills the belt as "the New York City Title" straight through Johnny Blaze's own steel-cage title win a fortnight later. WWA's own later title-history ledger folds this same lineage into the "WWA Television Championship" retroactively, dating Cross's own tournament win to 14 November 1997 — a week earlier than the served card's own internal dateline ("Friday, November 21, 1997"), and the served show is preferred here as the stronger primary source (see the reign's own note). From there the belt passed through dozens of hands across the surviving record to the 2008 rebuild; Nicholas Adams's own bio credits him with four reigns, more than any other champion in the title's history, and the belt saw multi-time runs for Mantis, Shaman, and Vik Ventressa.
