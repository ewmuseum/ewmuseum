Promotion   IEWA (1996)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

First held by Fast Eddie at the fed's own founding; held by the Vulture entering 22 September 1997 undefeated since March, and lost that night to Leeb in sixteen seconds after an Armageddon ambush, the shortest title match on the fed's own record.
