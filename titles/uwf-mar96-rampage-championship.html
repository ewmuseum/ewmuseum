"Showtime" Rick Marley16 October 20042005-05-19 (circa)
Won the UWF Rampage Championship from Jamie Underwood in his own first UWF match, catching the champion looking away from the match. The upset touched off Underwood's own violent falling-out with his "son," who turned out to be the biological child of a returning Sabbath. Retained again over Brian McKenzie by countout on 30 October 2004, after McKenzie abandoned the match to save tag partner Chris O'Brien from a backstage beating by Michael Bonn. Retained again over "The Walking Contradiction" Sabbath in a no-contest on 27 November 2004, after Jamie Underwood interfered with a steel chair. Retained again over "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas by pinfall (the Limelight) on 11 December 2004, aided by Jason Keening's own distracting presence at ringside.
Marcus Nuit2005-05-19 (circa)22 June 2005
Recorded as a reign of record from the promotion's own billing and commentary; no surviving document in the held record states the match in which Nuit won the title from Rick Marley at the Rise to Power pre-show. Retained the title over "The Walking Contradiction" Sabbath by pinfall (a headscissors takedown into a cradle) on 28 May 2005, his own first Rampage title defense, declaring himself the "Renegade of Rampage" beforehand. Retained again over the Mercenary by pinfall (a senton splash) on 11 June 2005, the title's own last defense before its unification with the Meltdown Championship the following week. Marcus Nuit beat Meltdown Champion Tigress by pinfall (the 51st Drop) in a title unification match on 22 June 2005; the Rampage Championship was retired into a new Unified Television Championship, with Nuit becoming its first champion.

Uriah Hawkinsunknown—

Retained against "Dead End" Derek Martin on 24 March 2004 by pinfall (a small package). Retained again on 15 May 2004 in a three-way dance against Troy Grace and Quinn Brown, catching both men together with a double DDT for the pin. Retained again at Gold Rush 2004 (5 June 2004) in a title-versus-contract match, pinning Pierce Bleed with a Suicide DDT after special referee T.D. Lost the title to Jamie Underwood by pinfall (a small package) on 28 August 2004, blindsided moments earlier by a run-in Papa Legba's own Ragga Bomb.
