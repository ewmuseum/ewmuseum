VWF Triple Threat Championship

aka T3 Championship, Triple Threat Title

Placard

Promotion   VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)
Active   2001–2002
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A title decided and defended in three-way matches. G-Gangsta won the first one in August 2001 and held it into October, when Zell Strife took it; in November Strife handed the belt to Thomas Rainka, who defended it to the year's end.

Named in the promotion's own 2001 cards as the Triple Threat Title, shortened on air to the T3 Title.
