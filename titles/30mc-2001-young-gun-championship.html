30MC Young Gun Championship

Placard

Promotion   30MC (2001)
Active   2001

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Unified from two old Young Guns belts on the promotion's first card and changed hands four times across its served run, ending with a champion counted out of the ring in a finish the broadcast itself never explains, and a belt burned on camera the same night.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Billy Blaze4 August 2001—Defeated Lars (his own Pretty in Pink partner and the belt's other former co-champion) with a schoolboy rollup, unifying the promotion's two old Young Guns titles into one. (ended: lost the title)
The title's first attested reign; its prior history as two separate belts is not served here.

Angel10 November 2001—Defeated Flaxen with a flying somersault neck breaker. (ended: lost the title)
Harry Palmer7 December 2001—Awarded the match, and the title, when referee Orly Diaz counted champion Angel out of the ring at ten -- a finish the broadcast never explains.
The same broadcast closes with a previously unseen wrestler, Malicea McDeath, burning the Young Gun belt in a garbage can on camera; no further title match is served after this reign.
