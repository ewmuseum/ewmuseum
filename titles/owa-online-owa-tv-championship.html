OWA TV Championship

Placard

Promotion   OWA (1995)
Active   1996–1997

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The OWA's television title, changing hands repeatedly across 1996 between Amazon Woman, Mariko, Masked Mayhem, Johnny Fate, and Asian Invasion.
