MLWO Television Championship

Placard

Promotion   MLWO (1996)
Active   1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A title created in January 1998 from the former PCW Northwestern belt, with its first champion, Matt Harris, crowned in a torture cage match on the Saturday Slam of 24 January 1998. Vince Valor says Harris handed it to him, and Kevin Legend was later billed as the final MLWO Television champion.
