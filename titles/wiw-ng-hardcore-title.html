WIW:NG Hardcore Title

Placard

Promotion   WIW:NG (1999)
Active   1999–2002

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own history opens the Hardcore Title on 4 June 1999 when Larry Wood beat Rey Mysterio Jr. in a hardcore match, and lists 51 reigns to Da D'Mon's, won from Larry Stone on 23 July 2002. Raven held it from 29 November 1999 to 13 January 2000, and Trauma and RaZeR were declared co-champions after escaping the Wrestlefest II junkyard together on 9 June 2002.

Reign list and footnotes from the fed's own Hardcore Title history, captured 31 August 2002; its champions list of 29 November 2002 also names Da D'Mon.
