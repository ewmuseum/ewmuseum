Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The CWA’s top belt in 2003 and 2004: Dizz is its first listed champion on 21 June 2003 and wins it three times that year, and the owner’s own character, Shannon Skanes, holds it briefly in October. Vigilante has it from 19 January 2004, the last holder its title history shows; the cards of 2004 then show Max Cameron taking it at Terror in the Aisles on 29 March and losing it to Leech at Outrage on 26 April, and Ash winning it at Blind Rage on 24 May and keeping it on 7 June.
