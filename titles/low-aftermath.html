LOW Aftermath Championship

Placard

Promotion   LOW (2002)
Active   2003

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The championship of Aftermath, the midweek show Lights Out Wrestling ran alongside Realm. Iron Lung, Pax, Jason Warrior, Tyrone Johnson and Kimi Aikohana are the champions the fed's own record names; Jason Warrior's own bio card dates his reign precisely, from the In The End pay-per-view of 11 June 2003 to Wakeup Call on 31 August 2003, and says he relinquished it rather than lost it. Pax's own bio card lists "LOW Aftermath Champ[Creator]" among the LOW titles it gives, a claim to the belt's creation, and Iron Lung's own manager came back at Shellshock for a belt he said he had won himself.
