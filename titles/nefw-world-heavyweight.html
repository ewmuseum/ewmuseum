Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NEFW's top championship. Tommy McKnight took it from Blade on the February 2002 Nemesis, drew with him at State of Shock, and lost it back to Blade six days later, Blade's fourth reign by the broadcast's own count. Senton held it by the Body Count pay-per-view of June, keeping it against his own brother Corona. The federation's own September handbook renamed the belt the NEFW Title, calling it representative of the Champion of the Federation rather than a claim to world status, and that October's tournament for it ran through a Doctor Melancholy-Hannibal final the served cards do not carry the result of.
