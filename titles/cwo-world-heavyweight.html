Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The CWO's own top belt, tracked week by week across the fed's self-published Rankings ledger from the moment the 1997 corpus opens (Master of Darkness Damion, carried over from 1996) through Lord Hile Troy's title-changing swerve at the CWO Celebration on 14 December 1997, when the operator's own on-screen persona turned on the man he had spent the match seconding.
