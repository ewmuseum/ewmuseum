Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own numbered ledger for this belt runs continuously from an early "European Championship" naming (its first tournament winner, The Psycho, crowned 12 April 2003) through 4 March 2004, when Rocky Adams's win is recorded, in the fed's own words, as making him "the last ever EWA Champion and the first ever SCWF Champion" — the same night the European Wrestling Alliance closed and the Squared Circle Wrestling Federation launched from its ashes. Armaan took the belt from Adams on 21 March 2004, carrying the first SCWF Championship into the brand's later absorption back under the unified WWA banner.

The fed's own numbered ledger (title id 7) traces this belt continuously from an earlier "European Championship" billing to the "SCWF Championship" name, with no break in the sequence — only the champion's own billing changes mid-record. Its earliest surviving row (Desolation, stripped of a pre-existing reign "while still holding the championship" upon his promotion to the WWA roster) predates the belt's own numbered start and carries a corrupted source date; no WWA-side win is recorded for that reign, so it is not counted among these rows.
