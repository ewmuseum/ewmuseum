Billed by the fed's own rules as its 'Ironman' belt, defended weekly. Its captured lineage runs from Ray Simmons through Capital Punishment, Suicide King, Tommy Grayson and Roadkill across the fed's 1998-99 run, and was vacant again by the 3 June 1999 rankings page.
