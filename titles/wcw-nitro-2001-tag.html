The fed's own title history lists 30 entries for the World Tag Championship, from Hardy Boys to Elite (Robert Roode & Lance Cade). 21 of them carry dates, from 24 March 2002 to 14 January 2008; the rest carry only their number. Its footnote 1 says: 'Titles retired 3-24-03'. Its heading note says the history of the champions before the first listed team was erased.
