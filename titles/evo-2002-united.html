Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

eVo's United Championship was first won by Thug roC on 31 August 2003. The fed's own record carries 2 reigns on it, across 2 different champions. The longest is Thug roC's, 101 days from 31 August 2003. Matt Hunt held it at the end of the surviving record.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Thug roC31 August 200310 December 2003
Thug roC pinned Ryan Mason with the roCing Horse after a chair shot to win the Continental and ULWN titles together and become the first United Champion. Defences on record. Legends XLIX, 10 September 2003: Thug roC pinned Yoshii Tanaka with the roCing Horse to retain the United Championship in its first defence. Legends L, 17 September 2003: Thug roC retained the United Championship when Aaron Justice ran in and pushed Scott Evans's shoulders down during a small package, and the referee counted three. Legends LI, 24 September 2003: Thug roC pinned Tok'n with the roCing Horse after the Lethal Injection to retain the United Championship, in a match the header billed Non-Title. Digital Destruction 2003, 5 October 2003: Thug roC saved the United Championship in the second fall by clotheslining QBX and hitting him with the tag belt before the referee saw Shaft-Man tap, then pinning him. Legends LIII, 15 October 2003: Scott Evans's interference gave Shane Wethers the win by disqualification over Thug roC, who kept the United Championship. Faith 2003, 9 November 2003: Thug roC pushed the ladder over, hit Scott Evans with the roCing Horse onto it and unhooked the belt to retain the United Championship in a ladder match. Legends LVII, 26 November 2003: Thug roC pinned Matt Hunt with the roCing Horse to retain the United Championship in a four corners match.
