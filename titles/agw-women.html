Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AGW's women's championship, its earliest recorded reign (Tessa, a tournament final) undated in the fed's own ledger; 20 further recorded reigns follow before the ledger's own last word, Goth Chick's reign beginning 26 January 2000, running into the fed's own hiatus. The belt sat vacant from 13 January 2000 "due to injury" -- the only vacancy in this ledger the fed's own text attributes to a wrestler's health rather than a stripping, no-show, or administrative gap.
