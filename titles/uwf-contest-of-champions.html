Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

An annual December tournament, run four times on the surviving record: Tsunami Oni won the inaugural in 2002 over runner-up Lacuna Debris, Ruff Ryder the second in 2003 over runner-up Ezra Kazee, Hot Mask the third in January 2005 over runner-up Kenny Mercury, and Clyde the fourth in January 2006 over runner-up Chris Storm.
