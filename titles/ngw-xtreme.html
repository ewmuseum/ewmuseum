NGW Xtreme Title

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Made as the NGW Cage Title and renamed on 6 April 2000, and it kept the shape of the first name: its own ledger is a run of Hell in a Cell, graveyard, buried alive, anarchy and mega mall brawl matches, fifty numbered reigns deep. Perry Saturn won the first one out of a triple Hell in a Cell with the belt hung from the roof of the third cage; John Hitchin broke the fed's own title-reign record holding it in July 2004, and it was vacant when the last title page went up that November.

The full reign ledger, built from /promotions/ngw/archive/xtreme-title-history.html and cross-checked against /promotions/ngw/archive/champions-and-title-history-2004-11.html, is transcribed into this registry.
