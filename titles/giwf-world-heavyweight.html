Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Twenty-four reigns across the promotion's own run, opening on Akira's tournament win and closing on Gurgles's second, 89-day reign — the longest of the belt's GIWF life and its final one on record. The lineage restarts as the UWF's own World Heavyweight Championship at the September 2001 tournament that opened the new fed.
