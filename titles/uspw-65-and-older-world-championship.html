USPW: 65 and older get in for free USPW World Championship

aka USPW World Title

Placard

Promotion   USPW: 65 and older get in for free (2007)
Active   2007

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

USPW's top belt. Bruce the Giant holds it when the diary opens and loses it to Freddie Datsun at Stars, Stripes and Slams in January 2007; Datsun defends it against William Hayes, Peter Valentine and Umaga into March, with a burst appendix after Bruce's chokeslam off the stage keeping him out for part of February. Umaga takes the belt from Datsun in a steel cage match on the third show of March 2007, keeps it against Datsun, Trent Shaffer, Muffin and Datsun again through the end of May, and is set to defend it against Triple D at the diary's first Family Night. Umaga beats Muffin on the fourth Saturday of May 2007 and keeps the belt in June against Triple D at the first Family Night, Freddie Datsun and Triple D again on the fourth Family Night, still champion at the end of June 2007. Umaga keeps the belt against William Hayes at the Family Night of Thursday, Week 4, July 2007 and loses it to Darryl Devine in a steel cage match at the Family Night of Friday, Week 1, August 2007; Devine is champion at the end of the diary's second week of August. Darryl Devine keeps the belt against Bruce the Giant in August 2007, against William Hayes at Summer of Love, which the narrator calls the best match in his history, and against Freddie Datsun and Jim Force in September; he is champion in the first half of Blaze of Glory, where Muffin is set to challenge him. Darryl Devine keeps the belt against Muffin at Blaze of Glory in September 2007, in a match restarted after the referee counted her pin, and beats her down in a cage match on the Monday of Week 4; he is champion at the end of the range, set to defend it against Sam Strong at A Haunting in America. In October 2007 Darryl Devine keeps the belt against Frankie Perez, Bruce the Giant, Sam Strong, back from retirement at A Haunting in America, and Davey Wayne; Freddie Datsun wins the number one contendership and takes the belt away from him in a brawl on the Thursday of Week 3, and the two are set to meet at Pilgrimage on 22 November 2007. In November 2007 Darryl Devine keeps the belt against Umaga on the Monday of Week 1, in a match the narrator rates an A*, and against Freddie Datsun at Pilgrimage, when the Masked Man hits Datsun and Danny Jillefski counts the pin; he is champion at the end of the range. Through December 2007 Darryl Devine is still champion with no title match booked; the Masked Man is revealed as Jack Giedroyc on the Thursday of Week 2 and is booked against him at Winter Epiphany.

He keeps it against Bruce the Giant at the Family Night of Friday, Week 2, August 2007, against William Hayes at Summer of Love, against Freddie Datsun at the Family Night of Thursday, Week 1, September and against Jim Force at the Family Night of Thursday, Week 2; he is still champion at the end of this range, with Muffin set to challenge him at Blaze of Glory. At Blaze of Glory, Saturday of Week 2, September 2007, he beats Muffin in a match the narrator rates an A, after the referee counts her pin and Seduction tells him Devine's foot was on the rope, and Danny Jillefski has the match restarted; on the Monday of Week 4 he leaves her bloodied in a cage match, an angle with no result; he is still champion at the end of this range, set to defend against Sam Strong at A Haunting in America. In October 2007 he beats Frankie Perez on the Monday of Week 2 with the Hotshots' help, beats Bruce the Giant on the Thursday when Seduction counts the pin, and keeps the belt against Sam Strong, back from retirement, at A Haunting in America on Saturday of Week 2 by pulling the tights, then against Davey Wayne on the Monday of Week 3; on the Thursday of Week 3 Freddie Datsun wins the number one contendership and walks off with the belt in a brawl without the title changing hands, and Devine is still champion at the end of this range, set to defend against Datsun at Pilgrimage on 22 November 2007. In November 2007 he keeps the belt against Umaga on the Monday of Week 1, in a match the narrator rates an A*, after Danny Jillefski, Big Cat Brandon and a low blow turn it and while the Masked Man's video threatens Seduction and Muffin, and against Freddie Datsun at Pilgrimage, Saturday, 22 November, when the Masked Man hits Datsun with the Slick Trick and Jillefski, in a referee's shirt, counts the pin; he is still champion at the end of this range. Through the Monday of Week 4, December 2007 no title match is booked: he beats Casey Valentine, Fumihiro Ota, Human Arsenal, Rick Law, Wolf Hawkins and Reaper in non-title and singles matches, learns that the Masked Man is Jack Giedroyc, and the card for Winter Epiphany lists Giedroyc against him for the World Championship; he is still champion at the end of this range.
