Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Vacant when Legend Pro Wrestling reopened in March 2002 and filled within two weeks: the Shadowlords won it on 12 March, the Oriental Assassins took it from them on 9 April after an unseen figure's darts, and the Puerto Rican Slayers won it from the Assassins on 13 April in a Final Confrontation superfight that sent the Assassins out of the promotion. The Slayers still held it on 3 May 2002.
