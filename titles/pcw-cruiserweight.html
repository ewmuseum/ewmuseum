Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PCW's Cruiserweight Championship was first won by Nick Taylor on 22 October 2000. The fed's own record carries 44 reigns on it, across 30 different champions. The longest is Chris Chase's, 56 days from 9 January 2002. Three of its reigns ended outside a match — handed over, stripped from the holder or vacated. Miss Moonlight held it at the end of the surviving record.
