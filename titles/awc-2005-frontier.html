Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AWC's Frontier Championship was first won by Tim Shipley on 9 August 2005. The fed's own record carries 12 reigns on it, across 10 different champions. The longest is Ellis Nash's, 91 days from 24 March 2006. One of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. Jack Murphy held it at the end of the surviving record.

Tim Shipley hit The Illustrious Face-Eater with the Chaos Theory and then the Chi-Squared Drop from the second turnbuckle onto a chair, and Michael Ryan counted to ten to give Shipley the AWC Frontier Championship. Defences on record. Fresh!, 25 October 2005: Tim Shipley locked The Farmer in Newton's Paradox, then hit the Eternal Hope and pinned him to retain the AWC Frontier Championship. Winter Warfare 2005, 23 December 2005: Tim Shipley hit Red Rock with the Eternal Hope and pinned him early to retain the AWC Frontier Championship, then left with the belt. Jessica O'Grady told Jack Murphy in a backstage talk that the AWC Frontier title had been declared vacant after Tim Shipley's run-in with a villain from a bad action film; there was no match.
Mike Wade31 January 200624 March 2006
Mike Wade hit Red Rock with a DDT onto a steel chair and pinned him in a four way to win the vacant Frontier Championship, so The Unfuckables held all the gold. A defence is on record. Bloodlust 2006, 17 February 2006: Mike Wade hit Paddy O'Shea with the TFW off the top rope onto the ring steps, then dragged him into the ring and pinned him in a Loser Leaves Town match, retaining the AWC Frontier Championship and ending O'Shea's AWC contract.
Ellis Nash24 March 200623 June 2006
Ellis Nash hit three moonsaults and Mike Wade kicked out, then she hit the Decree and pinned him to win the AWC Frontier Championship. Defences on record. Fresh!, 9 May 2006: Aimz won by disqualification when security pulled Adam Dick and Ellis Nash off her after they beat her with a chair; Nash kept the AWC Frontier Championship. Fresh!, 23 May 2006: Ellis Nash hit Aimz with a snap suplex and a split-legged moonsault from the top rope and pinned her in a No Escape match to retain the AWC Frontier Championship.

Fresh!east, 25 July 2006: Anton Assault hit Teresa Tomas with a Dragon Suplex, the Fate Of Ravaillac and a palm strike to the back of the neck, and pinned her to retain the AWC Frontier Championship.

Tony Little grabbed Anton Assault's leg, and Darcy Crisis hit the Mind Crisis and locked in his finishing hold, and Assault tapped before Billy Mays could break it up, so Crisis won the AWC Frontier Championship. Defences on record. Fresh!, 3 October 2006: Darcy Crisis held the Darcinator on Johnny Lexicon, who tapped while the referee looked away, and the referee called the bell only when Commissioner Wade came out, so Crisis retained the AWC Frontier Championship. Fresh!, 7 November 2006: Darcy Crisis turned an ankle lock into the Darcinator on Aimz, who tapped, so Crisis retained the AWC Frontier Championship. Fresh!, 14 November 2006: Darcy Crisis retained the AWC Frontier Championship by disqualification when the man in grey ran in and dropkicked Jack Murphy as Murphy was close to a fall.
