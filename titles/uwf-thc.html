Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Spelled out letter by letter, always — the weed-abbreviation gag deliberate. Born at Ground Zero 2001, retired weeks later when hardcore wrestling was banned, then carried back to life by Gurgles on the physical belt he had personally salvaged from the GIWF's own hardcore lineage, re-sanctioned under Gary Clark, and merged with the Shoot Wrestling Championship on 16 February 2003. The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid — Sean's own creation alongside Almasy — held it four times, more than anyone else on record; Seymour Almasy held it twice. The 2004 Quick Facts credit Nick Persia with the longest reign, 103 days. After the fed's 2004 title-history revision, Misfit took it from Clyde on 13 February 2005; Clyde had it back by July, lost it to Nick Persia at the Regal Rumble in September, and won it again in the Live and Loud 5 Foundry match, where it was unified with the Insert-Name-Here title.

Source: uwf.org.uk's own title-history page (three versions, May–Oct 2002, predating the 2004-revision ledger this record otherwise draws on) — “Title taken over by the THC championship belt Gurgles salvaged from the GIWF. UWF refuses to sanction title... Gary Clark officially sanctions title during his reign.”
