MAW: 10 Simple Rules MAW All Action Championship

aka MAW All Action Title

Placard

Promotion   MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)
Active   2011–2012

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MAW's newest belt, announced by Rip Chord at Eleventh Hour on 12th November 2011 as the replacement for the Trios titles he has just retired: held by the man best able to adapt to changing conditions, it is to be defended in randomly selected matches whose stipulation the champion learns only just before the bell. JD Morgan wins it that night in a ten-man battle royal (the diary's own title records say "Won in a Battle Royal") and keeps it against Ernest Youngman in a submission match at Farewell Tour on 22nd November. At Remember November on 26th November the belt is up for grabs all night, a pin or submission anywhere being enough, and it passes through Kid Arachnid, Black Eagle, Wanda Fish (the first woman to hold it), Knuckles and Amazing Fire Fly before JD Morgan wins it back with a chair. He keeps it against Jonnie Perez and Providence at End Of Days and Riley McManus at New Year, Old Rivalries, Bradford Peverell at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February 2012, in a straight wrestling match, Wanda Fish and Fumihiro Ota in a Triple Threat at All Or Nothing on 12th February, Kashmir Singh at Third Time's The Charm on 5th March and Kirk Jameson in a submission match at March Madness on 12th March, and at April Showers on 5th April survives the six-champion Gold Rush match and takes the Traditional title from Primus Allen as well. Donnie J takes the All Action belt from him in a ladder match at South East Crisis on 8th April; Aaron Andrews wins it from Donnie J in a title-versus-title match at May The Force Be With You on 5th May and loses it back to him at Great Lakes Madness on 9th May in a two-out-of-three-falls match, and Donnie J keeps it in the diary's pages from June to August 2012 against Father Johan in a pole match at High Noon on 5th June, Firebird in a submission match at South East Madness on 9th June, Eddie Powell and Citizen X at Summer Showdown on 12th June, Firebird again at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, JD Morgan in a thirty-minute draw for this and the Traditional title at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July, Powell and Rudy Velasquez at Summernova on 5th August and Providence in a ladder match at South East Grand Parade on 9th August. At Iron on 12th August Rip Chord says the title can change hands anywhere, and Black Eagle, helped by Firebird, pins Donnie backstage for the belt; Firebird turns on Eagle in a corridor and Donnie, after hitting Firebird with a metal briefcase, pins Eagle to take it back the same night. The diary's own title records list "Black Eagle (2) August 2012 3" and "Donnie J (3) August 2012 0". Donnie J is still the champion in the diary's pages for September 2012. The diary's own title records list "JD Morgan (2) November 2011 7".

Donnie J8 April 20125 May 2012Won from JD Morgan in a ladder match at South East Crisis, which Rip Chord orders so that Donnie can feel at home, the belt hung from a hook fifteen feet over the ring: Morgan topples the ladder from the top, J lands on the top rope, slingshots himself back on a second ladder, grabs the belt and hangs from it with a wide smile until the catches give way and he drops to the mat holding it, as Haley Buck screeches at ringside. Aaron Andrews5 May 20129 May 2012Won at May The Force Be With You in the title-versus-title match that Rip Chord announces, the All Action belt being, in his words, a little spicier than the others, one he can put on the line at any time: Andrews, who already holds the Heavyweight title, beats Donnie J in twenty-two minutes by blocking a dive from the top rope with a standing leap, turning it into a superplex and winning with the Twisting Face Crusher, and so becomes a double champion. He loses the All Action belt back to Donnie J at Great Lakes Madness four days later.
Donnie J9 May 201212 August 2012Regained at Great Lakes Madness, after he asks for the rematch his contract gives him, in a two-out-of-three-falls match for the All Action title only (Rip Chord will not give him a Heavyweight title match after losing a week before): Andrews takes the first fall in under ten minutes with a Twisting Face Crusher, Donnie levels it with the Death On Miami Beach after almost fifteen minutes, and in the slower third fall he catches Andrews's leg, drills him with a dragon whip and rolls up into a bridging cradle for three. At Mother, May I? on 12th May he fails to win the Heavyweight title from Andrews in a thirty-minute Iron Man match; he keeps the belt against Father Johan in a pole match at High Noon on 5th June 2012, Firebird in a submission match at South East Madness on 9th June, Eddie Powell and Citizen X at Summer Showdown on 12th June, Firebird again at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, JD Morgan in a thirty-minute draw for this and the Traditional title at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July, Powell and Rudy Velasquez at Summernova on 5th August and Providence in a ladder match at South East Grand Parade on 9th August, and loses it to Black Eagle, who pins him backstage with Firebird's help, at Iron on 12th August.
Black Eagle12 August 201212 August 2012Won backstage at Iron when Rip Chord declares that the title can change hands anywhere so long as a qualified referee is there to make the call: Donnie J is ambushed from both sides by the Dark Raptors, Firebird throws him into a stack of crates, and when Eagle seizes his partner from behind and throws him headfirst into Donnie, Eagle dives on top of the champion and gets a three count, taking the title from round Donnie's waist. Amazing Fire Fly's Cutter through a table brings a two count that Firebird breaks up, and Lassana Makutsi's challenge is met with kicks; Firebird then turns on Eagle in a darkened corridor, and Donnie J, who blasts Firebird with a metal briefcase, pins the gasping Eagle with a Death From Miami Beach to take the belt back the same night.
Donnie J12 August 2012—Regained the same night at Iron, in a darkened backstage corridor, after Firebird turns on Black Eagle: Donnie hits Firebird in the face with a metal briefcase, scores with a Death From Miami Beach on the still-gasping Eagle and makes the three-count, then winks at Sienna LeNoir and asks whether it reminds her of anyone she used to know. He is still the All Action champion at the end of this range, standing with Kashmir Singh and Brandon Smith to protect Sienna at Back To School on 5th September.
