Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Elite Wrestling Foundation put its own top belt up as a tournament before it had a champion at all. The first round ran on the fed’s first Immortality, 9 March 2003, and the final closed WrestleFest: Turbulence beat “The Turnbuckle” Jay to be handed the belt and, in the fed’s own commentator’s words, to become “the very first EWF Champion”. He was still carrying it at the last capture of the champions page, six weeks later, and the fed left nothing dated after that.

The fed’s own rules page describes a quite different set of belts — a World Title, an Intercontinental Title, an EWF Title and World Tag Team Titles — which its champions page never uses. Both are held; the rules read as a template the fed never brought into line with what it crowned.
