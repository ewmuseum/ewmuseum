Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Total Conquest, Sepiroth Du Luc and Persephone, won the vacant belts at iNtense 22 and lost them at Thieves Honour to Fueled by V.I.A.G.R.A., who lost them at Rise of the Legends to V.I.A.G.R.A., High Flyer and Tony Davis. Chris Titan and Mike Extreme won them at Cataclysm by the fed's own title history; Team V.I.A.G.R.A. took them back on 3 February 2002, and Derecho and Blazer won them in the Tag Team Turmoil Gauntlet at Unwanted on 28 May 2002. In the 2012 revival the Heirs of Wrestling won the belts in a four-corners match at Divide & Conquer and kept them against Sylo and Ray Chavez on Warriors 05.
