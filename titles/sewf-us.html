Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Filled at ShawCade 3 in September 2000 by Simon Southward, the United States Championship went on to Wyatt Wallace, Tommy Grayson, Adam Mitchell, Grayson again and Art Blake, whose 119-day reign from January 2001 the fed called its longest, before Tom Collins, Mitchell again, Mike Bright and Simon Solkoff held it through the summer. Blake held it again at Octoberfest and gave it up in November to a five-way Thunderdome brawl, won at End Game by Trineo Del Abanico, who unmasked as Johnny Sledge.
