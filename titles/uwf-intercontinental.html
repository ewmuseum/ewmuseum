Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The number-two belt, and the one the North American Championship was twice folded into and pulled back out of — merged 18 January 2004, separated again that August. Its own 2004 Quick Facts credit Joshua Dunn with the longest reign (140 days) and Digital Mortality with the most (three); Seymour Almasy won it once, in a 3-Title/3-Ladder match, and was stripped immediately. Past the fed's own ledger, Richard Hayes renamed it the “Insert-Name-Here Championship” in January 2005, leaving each champion to name the belt and set its rules; Kenny Mercury held it first, then Ruff Ryder and Jamie Shock in the spring, and Clyde from June until James von Doom took it from him in an August unification match with the North American belt, Clyde winning it back in October. Tessa Windsor's own win over Chris Storm at Sins of the Past (2006) is staged in-show as unifying it with the North American belt, though the fed's own site kept tracking both lineages separately afterward.

The ledger this Museum holds ends here. The belt was presented to Kenny Mercury as the Insert-Name-Here Championship by then-UWF-Owner Richard Hayes (promotions/uwf/archive/sas-2005-01-30.html; uwf.rpcommunity.com/champions.shtml, Aug 2006), each champion naming the belt and its rules. Ruff Ryder, Jamie Shock, Clyde, James von Doom and Clyde again follow him, then Tessa Windsor.
Ruff Ryder2005-04 (circa)24 April 2005The match that gave him the title is not in the served record; Kenny Mercury is the last champion before him on the record.
The 24 Apr card gives the champion the naming of the belt: "TITLE NAME AND STIPULATION MUST BE ANNOUNCED BY RYDER". The Clash on 6 Mar, the only show of that stretch in the served record, does not stage the change.
Jamie Shock24 April 20052005-06 (month)Took the title from Ruff Ryder — Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, 24 Apr 2005.
He named the belt at FANdamonium DOS, where the card lists the title as the "UWF Kenny Mercury Appreciation Championship", Jamie Shock against Mike Montgomery (https://forum.ewmuseum.org/t/uwf-presents-fandamonium-dos/13902/1); the 2 Jun card bills him "Insert-your-own-title Champion". He held the World Heavyweight title alongside it from that show. Neither the 24 Apr nor the 12 Jun show is in the served record.
Clyde2005-06 (month)21 August 2005Took the title from Jamie Shock as the THC champion of the 12 Jun 2005 SAS unification main event; the show is not in the served record and the export does not state the result, only that Clyde is billed with both belts by 26 Jun.
Billed "THC/Nightmare Champion" on 5 Aug 2005 (https://forum.ewmuseum.org/t/feedback-uwf-presents-the-special-sas/14919/1) — the champion chose the belt's name. He held the Hardcore title (THC) throughout.
James von Doom21 August 200523 October 2005Defeated Clyde in the Insert-Name-Here/North American unification match — Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, 21 Aug 2005; the show is not in the served record.
The 28 Aug card says "The North American Championship is merged" (https://forum.ewmuseum.org/t/predictions-uwf-presents-sunday-afternoon-supremacy-09-28-05/15110/1), yet he stays North American champion: the 6 Sep Regal Rumble card books him for the Insert-Name-Here Championship against Killer Dude (https://forum.ewmuseum.org/t/uwf-presents-regal-rumble-prediction-thread/15234/1), the same post's synopsis calls it a North American defence (https://forum.ewmuseum.org/t/feedback-uwf-presents-regal-rumble/15373/1), and the fed's own North American history runs unbroken through his reign (promotions/uwf/archive/nahistory.html).
Clyde (2)23 October 2005unknownAnnounced the winner and new champion of the return match after his father, Dark Gimmick, took over the bout and wrecked the champion — Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, 23 Oct 2005.
At Live and Loud 5 (30 Oct 2005) Clyde saved both the THC and this belt in the Foundry match, so the two titles were unified in his hands.
Tessa Windsorunknown—The show corpus references, but does not stage, the actual title change: Clyde is described losing the Insert-Name-Here belt to Tessa Windsor "the same forklift Misfit parked on Clyde to make him lose the INH belt to Tessa Windsor." Unified with the North American Championship at Sins of the Past when Windsor defended both against Chris Storm.
Corroborated as her holding by uwf.rpcommunity.com/champions.shtml (captured 27 Aug 2006, rpcommunity mine), which lists her as the sitting champion of the renamed Insert-Name-Here Championship alongside the North American title, tracked as two separate lineages on the fed's own site despite the in-show "unification" framing.
