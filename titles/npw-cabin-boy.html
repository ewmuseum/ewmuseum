Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Named once, years after the fact, in a bath-time conversation between a UWF champion and his girlfriend two federations away — Tsunami Oni, explaining why a young opponent had impressed him in the first round of a cruiserweight tournament, remembers that the kid had been Cabin Boy Champion for No Pirates Wrestling. It is the only championship the promotion is ever recorded as having, and the joke of it is the whole promotion's joke: a fed founded on hating pirates named its bottom-rung belt after the lowest rank on a pirate ship.

The sole receipt is one line in PTC's own Cruiserweight Tournament 2 roleplay board, 24 April 2005: "I guess that's why he was Cabin Boy Champion for No Pirates Wrestling." No date, no match, no defence and no second holder survives; the one holder it names, Mickey D, is a PTC/CW2 competitor who has no registry row of his own yet, so the reign below carries him as an honest unminted holder.
