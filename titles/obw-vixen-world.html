OBW Vixen's World Championship

Placard

Promotion   OBW (2006)
Active   2012–2013
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Debuted 1 February 2012 on the first night of Wednesday Night Catfight, the federation's own Vixens-only brand, when Cream Stylez beat Jade Mystique in a match its ledger records as merging the OBW Divas Championship and the Execution Woman's Championship into one belt. Ten reigns follow in fourteen months, none of them longer than Mystique's own 92 days, and the belt travels through pay-per-views the federation named Heart of Wrestling, Alcatraz Aggression, Wheel of Torture, Last Man Standing and Xtreme Xmas. The ledger's last word is Ayden Starr-Malice's second reign, won in a falls-count-anywhere five-way at Heart of Wrestling 2013 and still running at 63 days when the record stops.
