LOW World Championship

aka LOW World Title

Placard

Promotion   LOW (2002)
Active   2002–2004

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Lights Out Wrestling's own top prize, and the belt its whole first year of storyline ran through: CADE was billed as the first man to hold it, Mike Meyhu took it and the presidency together after turning on Trigger, and Tristan Belmont, Jason Ortiz, Jason Kage and Donny Diamond carried it across the fed's second run. Annihilation won it in an Asylum Match at CATASTROPHIC in October 2004 and hanged himself on the wire that lowered it to him in the same segment; the fed's own masthead banner shows the belt vacant six weeks later. No title ledger of LOW's own survives; what is known of the lineage comes from the fed's own bio cards, its served shows and the champion banner it stamped into the masthead of every page.
