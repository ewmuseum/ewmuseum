Promotion   GWO (Global, 2004)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The GWO’s tag belts, won by the Regulators on 2 August 2004 and passed to Thunder and Marla Storme, to the Smooth Kriminals, vacated after a double pin on 8 November, awarded to Beyond Control in an open match on 29 November and won by Thunder Storme in January 2005.
