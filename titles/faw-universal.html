Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Sat above the World Championship on FAW's own earliest surviving site. The Olympian held it first, into a March 2001 I Quit Match against Lex Luger; Luger carried it into a four-way battle royal at Extreme Beating and lost it there to British Bulldog, then held it again by the fed's own FAW is War card at month's end. A Tables, Ladders and Chairs rematch against Bulldog for it at May Day Massacre was called off on air for injuries to both men, and FAW's own president scrapped the belt that night — though the fed's own later site still names a champion for it into 2003.

FAW's own champions page, dated March 2001 and captured 21 April 2001 (promotions/faw/archive/faw-champions-2001-03.html); its own cards page, same snapshot (promotions/faw/archive/faw-cards-2001-03.html); Tuesday Torture, 20 March 2001, Friday Night Fear, 23 March 2001, Extreme Beating, March 2001, FAW is War, 30 April 2001, and May Day Massacre, 5 May 2001 (promotions/faw/archive/tuesday-torture-2001-03-20.html, friday-night-fear-2001-03-23.html, extreme-beating-2001-03.html, faw-is-war-2001-04-30.html, may-day-massacre-2001-05-05.html and may-day-massacre-card-2001-05-05.html). FAW's ten registered titles do not include this belt. This crawl's own full 2003 read (When Worlds Collide 2003, Panic (February and 6 April), Extreme Beating Three) turned up Sean Mason billed champion throughout but found no textual support anywhere for this note's own earlier claim that Xander was separately billed champion -- that claim could not be corroborated and may be an error in this row's own prior research pass.
