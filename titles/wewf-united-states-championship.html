Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Created for the 1991 Summer League and settled in one night: a sixteen-man tournament in which Bjorn “the Sweedish Pretzel” beat BudMan in an all-Four-Corners final, cleanly enough that both announcers remarked on it — and then held the belt undefeated through five defenses.
