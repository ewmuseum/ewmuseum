New Era Old School Wrestling's top title. Steve Dumars won it at the promotion's year-end supercard in December 2000, retained it against Matthew Reason at Snowed In on 18 February 2001 and again at Storm Warning on 7 May 2001, and was still champion in the promotion's report of 26 June 2001.

The promotion's own top title, first awarded at its December 2000 year-end supercard (already served).
