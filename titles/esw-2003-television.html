ESW Television Championship

aka NYCW Television Championship, ESW TV Championship

Placard

Promotion   ESW (Empire State, 2003)
Active   2003–2010

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Devo won the first Television title in a fifteen-man over-the-top battle royal at the second Kick Ass Inc on 7 April 2003. Its table runs through forty-two reigns to Vise on 28 September 2010, and the holders include Rayne, the first to hold both the World and Television titles, Exodus, whose 189 days stood as the record, and Kid Krazy, who held it four times.
