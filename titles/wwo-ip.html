Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Absorbed the WWO Extreme Championship's own lineage into itself in a title-unifying match, then ran nine more reigns before the federation's first closure: Tyrant, Ultimate Thrasher, Darkness, Night's Harbinger, "Latin Lightning" Al Perez, "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson and "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian each held it in turn. Detson took it from Perez in Atlanta on 22 July 1999 and lost it to Sebastian in Salt Lake City that November — the ledger's own last recorded change before the belt goes dark for the mid-era gap. Along the way it was held up once after "Prime Time" Phill Massacre was fired from the federation, and Ultimate Thrasher's own reign ended not in a loss but in his failure to appear and defend it. The revived WWO revived the belt itself sideways rather than head-on: its own Cruiserweight Championship, reintroduced by tournament in May 2001, was converted into this title on 18 June 2001 with "Problem Child" Chris Stringer's own cruiserweight reign carrying over — Stringer is still defending it, by countout over Ultimate Thrasher, at the 24 August 2001 Superstar Showcase.
