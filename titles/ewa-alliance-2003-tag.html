Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The tag belts, with fourteen reigns on its page from a survivor match at the TTTTT2 on 5 July 2003 to Brett Cotter and Michael Bendis, The Regulators, on 14 September 2004. The page numbers two different reigns 6; its date for the last reign sits ten days after the news of 4 September 2004 reports The Regulators winning the titles from Gekido.
