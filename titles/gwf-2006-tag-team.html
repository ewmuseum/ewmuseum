Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A well-traveled belt: SheltonSplash and Kevin McAuliffe lost it to XO Malone and Damian DeNiro's own Movement in a match the record keeps only as a flashback; by April 2008 The Number 4 and Michael Toaster's own Numbered Toast held it, walking out on their own title defense at that month's War Games rather than lose it clean; by 2013 it belonged to Dave Shadow and Kevin McAuliffe's own Neo-Evolution, who lost it in the fed's own last hours to Danny Mainer and Bryan Daniels — the final GWF Tag Team Champions.

The Billionaires Club (Wolfenstein and John Beanshaw Wrathfield)'s own reign, named only in a 28 July 2008 Hall of Shame retrospective with no result or date recovered, likely precedes all five dated/flashback reigns above; recorded on this same title id rather than split, given no evidence of a distinct belt.
