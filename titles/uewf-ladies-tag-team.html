Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The HellKats and the Valkyries are the only two teams ever recorded holding these belts — the HellKats from around 30 January 1992 until the Valkyries beat them for the titles on 19 February 1992 — and the division closed with the federation's 1992 relaunch with no further holder on record.

Declared in the federation's own founding document, 19 November 1991 (promotions/uewf/archive/new-pbem-the-uewf-1991-11-19.html), which sets out how it was to be defended. The discontinuation is stated in the federation's own relaunch call, 14 May 1992. That is the only sighting of this belt being contested anywhere in the record, and it is a sighting of a card that does not survive. The weekly record survives second-hand, in another federation's own newsdesk, not yet served here. Nine editions of the WeWF's own This Week in Wrestling and its TTTTTT tag tournament, 30 January to 31 March 1992, plus the EMWA's own Kings Of The Ring of 25 March, are held in this project's own Google Groups dumps and are unserved. Between them they carry this federation's own weekly title results week by week, its full ranking tables with win-loss records and points, and two of its own matches staged play-by-play. What they say about THIS belt: A holder does survive, in a document this collection holds and does not serve. The roundup of 19 February 1992 reads "The Valkyries defeated the Hellkats to become the new UeWF ladies tag team champions," and the EMWA's own Kings Of The Ring of 25 March 1992 has two TWIW women watching tapes of UeWF cards and calling the HellKats "one hell of a team." So the division ran, the belts were contested, and at least two teams held or challenged for them — none of which is minted here, because neither document is served and neither team has a row anywhere in the registry.
