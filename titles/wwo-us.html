WWO United States Championship

Placard

Promotion   WWO (1995)

Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Ran through a tournament final to crown its first champion, The Heckler, and passed through King $$$ Perfect and Andros before Lord Keeblor won it from "Prime Time" Michael Fulton in Auckland on 1 October 1997 — the first of two reigns the ledger credits him with. Underground Kingpin's own two reigns bracket a CJ Gesner title win in between, and "The Prodigy" Tracy Hudson took the belt from "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian in Atlanta on 22 July 1999 before losing it back in the same city, to Lord Keeblor a second time, on 17 March 2000. The ledger's own notes record the federation closing on that same day.
