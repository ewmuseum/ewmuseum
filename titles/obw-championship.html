Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

OBW's own men's flagship singles title, tracked only through incidental mentions on other wrestlers' bios and the federation's own last-touched champions list rather than a dedicated title-history page. Sidster is the earliest attested holder, backed by the newly formed stable the Flock to a defense at Rebirth (30 April 2012); the record does not say when his reign began or ended. Super Krmi held the belt next, by the time Chad Bowens dethroned him on the Storm episode after Storm's 54th — a reign Bowens's own bio credits him with holding twice in total, though only this one surfaces by name in the surviving record. Bowens lost the title the same night to a Money in the Bank-style cash-in by Ice, whose own caption calls it his only reign. How or when Ice lost the belt is not recorded; the corpus resumes the men's title story in early 2013 with Punisher already the Storm-side rival of Execution's own M-Star, and the wiki's own champions list, last touched 21 February 2014, still names Punisher, leader of the stable the Prophecy, as champion.

Ledger source: promotions/obw/archive/obw-champions.html; reign chain also drawn from promotions/obw/archive/chad-bowens.html and promotions/obw/archive/james-parker.html.
