Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's North Beach Championship: 3 reigns are served here, across 3 different champions, the first beginning on 17 June 2010 with Apollo Calix and the last on 22 August 2010 with Chris Armstrong. The longest of those is Chris Armstrong's, 44 days from 22 August 2010. This is the 2010 belt, not the North Beach Championship of 2011 to 2019 (a separate belt that shares the name); it became the EPW Evolve Championship on 31 August 2010.
