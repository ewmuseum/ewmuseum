TEAM Wrestling TEAM Challenge Championship

Placard

Promotion   TEAM Wrestling (2005)
Active   2007–2009

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A prize purpose-built to test its holders under a new gimmick every time it's on the line, held throughout its first year by one man.

Contested under a different gimmick match each time it changes hands (a steel cage at its debut, later falls-count-anywhere and lumberjack matches).
