The CWA’s own title inside Anarchy Wrestling’s 2006 run, decided by a tournament whose final The Nameless won over Trinity Holmes on 28 May 2006. The Moose won it on 1 July, defended it in a steel cell at Wars of the World and lost it to Rated R in the octagon at Dead By Dawn.
