Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A hardcore-stipulation title from the start — a Thumbtacks Match, a Flaming Hell In A Cell, a Deathmatch, a Barbwire Beat Down — that “QBz Finest” Blaze loses to Wes Easton in the first of those at Fury 2002-09-08. Flashing Thunder holds it through an unrecorded turnover into a draw against The Fox at Wrestle Insanity, then loses it, also unrecorded, to Kevin Murray by the time Bryon Wade Osbourne makes his own UCWF debut at Fury 2003-06-20 and wins the title in a Deathmatch with his first opponent. Osbourne survives a draw against Sean Johnson (and a mid-match elimination of Flashing Thunder) at Breakout — the last the surviving record shows of the belt.
