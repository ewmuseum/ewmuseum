WWA Diablo Championship

Promotion   WWA (1997–2019)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The LDA brand's own title, created when the WWA briefly split into three brands (Meltdown/NWC, Fiesta/LDA, and SCWF) in 2004. Jack Griffiths won the first Diablo reign on 17 May 2004, surviving a hectic singles bout against Odium and then four more men in a cage-match final; Odium took the belt back from Griffiths one show later, on 14 June 2004, in a match the fed's own title-history ledger never recorded. The lineage ends with the fed's own words: Odium — "the second Diablo Champion," by his own farewell — left the WWA on 11 August 2004, and five days later the belt was folded into the Extreme Championship's relaunch, never defended again.
