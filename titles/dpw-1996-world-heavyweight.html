DPW World Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   DPW (1996)
Active   1996–1997

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Won by an 8-man tournament in 1996 and defended almost continuously into 1997: Steven "The Edge" Spector gave way, untracked, to "Devastator" Mick Morton by New Year's Day 1997, who lost it in turn to "Superstar" Steve Sampson at the uncaptured Broken Hearts PPV (14 February 1997). Sampson was still champion, having just survived a Captain Midnight attack, when this wing's own served record breaks off (8 March 1997); the fed's own founder later stated it closed its doors the following April, merging into PCW.
