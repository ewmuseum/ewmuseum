WAR!Lord Championship

Placard

Promotion   WAR! (1995)
Active   1996–1999

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A title contested through a running succession involving Big Duke, CrocMaster, American Patriot and Robski, the last "regaining" it in a cage match at the fed's own Dec 1996 supercard after an earlier, off-page reign of his own.
