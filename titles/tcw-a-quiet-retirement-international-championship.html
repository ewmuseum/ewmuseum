Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt of the middle of the card. Wolf Hawkins holds it when the diary opens, keeps it against Koshiro Ino at Where Angels Fear To Tread by interference, and loses it to Ino in a no-disqualification match at Total Mayhem 13. Ino keeps it through June 2008, beating Sam Keith on Total Wrestling in Week 2 and, at Excessive Force, losing to Chris Rockwell only by disqualification. Ino keeps it through July 2008: Tommy Cornell's win on Total Wrestling comes only by disqualification, and at Summer Showdown Ino beats Sammy Bach with the Kobra's Bite. Ino keeps it through August 2008 until Hotter Than Hell, where John Anderson beats him with the Ammo Dump and the diary says TCW has a new International Champion. Anderson keeps it through September 2008: Wolf Hawkins beats him only by count out, and he beats Frankie Perez, Acid and, at Destructive Energy, Eric Tyler, each with the Ammo Dump. Anderson keeps it through October 2008: he draws with Sean Deeley on Total Wrestling when time runs out and beats Jack Marlowe with the Ammo Dump. At Crunch Time he makes an open challenge and Sean Deeley beats him by count-out, a result the diary does not treat as a change of champion. Anderson keeps it through November 2008: he beats Texas Pete on Total Wrestling after Wolf Hawkins interferes, and at A Little South Of Sanity he beats Pete and Hawkins in a three-way match, pinning Hawkins with the Ammo Dump after the Enforcers have beaten him down. Anderson keeps it through December 2008: he beats American Buffalo after Wolf Hawkins's Full Moon Rising lands on the Buffalo, draws with Hawkins at the time limit on Total Wrestling and at Psycho Circus beats Hawkins in a match the diary rates an A, with the Ammo Dump after fifteen minutes.
