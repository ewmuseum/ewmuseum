Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The pair of belts the Machines hold when the diary opens, for a full year by May 2008. They retain against Painful Procedure at Where Angels Fear To Tread and against the Young Guns at Total Mayhem 13. The Machines lose the belts to the New Wave at Excessive Force in June 2008. The New Wave keep the belts through July 2008, beating the Young Guns at Summer Showdown with the Special Force, after losing a non-title match to the School of Tradition on Badge of Honor. The New Wave keep the belts through August 2008, beating the Easy Riders, the British Lions and, at Hotter Than Hell, the School of Tradition. The New Wave keep the belts through September 2008: a disqualification loss to Freddy Huggins and Sammy Bach does not cost them the titles, and they beat the Keith Brothers on Total Wrestling, the Enforcers on Badge of Honor and, at Destructive Energy, the School of Tradition with Scout's Special Force. The New Wave keep the belts through October 2008, the Stone Brothers drawing with them in a non-title match on Total Wrestling; at Crunch Time they beat the Easy Riders, the tournament winners, only by disqualification. The New Wave keep the belts through November 2008, beating the Elite Express and the British Lions and drawing with the Keith Brothers on Total Wrestling when the Easy Riders interfere; at A Little South Of Sanity they beat the Easy Riders and the Stone Brothers in a three-team elimination match. The New Wave keep the belts through December 2008 until Psycho Circus, where they lose them in a hardcore match to the Easy Riders: Billy Fonda and Peter Hopper beat down Scout, take him to the corner and loop a chain, which Carl Batch holds, round his neck to anchor him to the turnbuckle, and finish Guide with the Emergency Stop onto a steel chair. The diary's closing notes call the fall of the New Wave a long reign ended and doubt that the Easy Riders will hold the belts as long.
