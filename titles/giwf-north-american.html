Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Briefly renamed the “Native American Title” during Jay Phoenix's own reign — the fed's own Quick Facts crediting its creation to “Jay Phoenix's overwhelming egoti—*shot*” — and, in an earlier stretch, the belt Lew Spectre vacated by throwing it in a trash can, only for Daneel McCord to retrieve it three days later; also carried, in later use, as the “European Title.” Restarts as the UWF's own North American Championship.
