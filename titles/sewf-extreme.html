SEWF Extreme Championship

Promotion   SEWF (ShawWorld, 2000–02)
Active   2000–2002

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Filled at the end of October 2000 by Phah Q. Dickshine in a three-way, the Extreme Championship was held by eleven champions in turn by the fed's own title histories before the summer of 2001, three of them in March and April alone as Twisted Chaos and Ian Blade traded it. Renamed the Extreme Television Championship by the autumn, it was defended in a Hardcore Hell match of barbed-wire ropes and light bulbs and in a handicapped weapons match, and Smoove won it back at End Game in December 2001.
