Dublin Pub Wrestling DPW Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   Dublin Pub Wrestling (1997)
Active   2004–2005

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned in a tag tournament at the federation's first New Year's card and, after an unrecorded change of hands, won again by the Wolfpack3000's own Mongs, who held it through the spring of 2004.
