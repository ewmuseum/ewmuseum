Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The second belt on the card, and the one whose own name did not survive the fed's last era intact. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens won it out of a three-way against Gobstopper and Ace Hawk at Revolution; a year earlier the alliance's own profile page named The Valiant One the champion in its champions block and Brad Lamen the champion in its own top ten, on the same page, in the same month. Both readings are kept.

Two years later the fed's own rulebook lists an Atlantic Championship instead, among five belts, with no Trans-Atlantic Championship beside it and no explanation of a rename. That belt is recorded separately, as ccw-atlantic; nothing held joins the two lineages.
