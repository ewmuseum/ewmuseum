MAW: 10 Simple Rules MAW Trios Championship

aka MAW Trios Titles

Placard

Promotion   MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)
Active   2010–2011

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MAW's trios belts, announced by Rip Chord at All Or Nothing in February 2010 after Ultimate Phoenix's lucha experience and his trouble with The Firm. The Watch meet The Firm at March Madness to decide the first champions, and The Firm (Jean Cattley, Darryl Devine and Ryan Powell) win; they keep the belts against the Internet Favourites and Fumihiro Ota at April Showers. The diary's own title records list the first reign as "Cattley, Allen & Powell". The Firm keeps the belts against Shane Nelson, Amazing Fire Fly and Riley McManus at Summer Showdown in June 2010, with Primus Allen standing in for Darryl Devine, and loses them to Fumihiro Ota, Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh at Hotter Than Hot in July; the new champions keep them against Ricochet, Sharp and Toxin at Summernova and against Jared Johnson, Nelson Callum and Roderick Remus at Eleventh Hour in November 2010. The diary's own title records list the two reigns as "Cattley, Allen & Powell March 2010" and "Smith, Ota & Singh July 2010". Fumihiro Ota, Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh lose the belts to The Triple Threat (Jared Johnson, Roderick Remus and Nelson Callum) at Season's Beatings on 12th December 2010, when Firebird distracts Ota; the new champions keep them at End Of Days and on four tour shows. The diary's own title records list the reign as "Johnson, Remus & Callum December 2010 7". The Triple Threat keep the belts on the May and early July tour shows until Kid Arachnid, Chance Fortune and Randy Bumfhole, billed as The Firm, take them at Summernova on 12th August 2011; The Firm keep them against The Fallen at Back To School. The diary's own title records list the new champions as "Arachnid, Fortune & Bumfhole August 2011 1". The Second Sons, Casey Valentine, Cameron Vessey (on loan from USPW) and Antonio, take the belts from The Firm at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2011, Valentine pinning Kid Arachnid; at Eleventh Hour on 12th November Rip Chord says he does not like championships that let two men rely on a third and retires the Trios titles, creating the All Action title in their place. The diary's own title records list the last champions as "Vessey, Valentine & Antonio October 2011 - Retired November 2011".
