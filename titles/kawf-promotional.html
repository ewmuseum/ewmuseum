Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The federation's own second-tier singles prize, described in its own recruitment document as the one fans see most often; crowned vacant on the 2004 comeback broadcast.

The federation's own recruitment document (8 April 2004) describes it as defended at least every 30 days and, on overseas tours, every other show.
