Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The federation's own second singles belt, defended weekly against the second-ranked contender. Admiral Ax held it into the 1992 hiatus and was still recognised as champion at the relaunch. Carried back from the WeWF wing, whose own newsdesk This Week in Wrestling printed this federation's weekly title results and full rankings through the winter of 1992. Mr. B holds this belt at 10-1-1 in the newsdesk's own standings of 30 January 1992; by 15 February he has the World Championship and Admiral Ax has this one at 10-0-0. Ax then defends it twice on the record — over Lord Plunder on 19 February and over Rage on 25 February — and is 14-1 in the last standings the newsdesk printed.

Declared in the federation's own founding document, 19 November 1991 (promotions/uewf/archive/new-pbem-the-uewf-1991-11-19.html), which sets out how it was to be defended. Holder transcribed from the federation's own relaunch call, 14 May 1992. Where this belt's own first reign began is still not on the record. The weekly record survives second-hand, in another federation's own newsdesk, not yet served here. Nine editions of the WeWF's own This Week in Wrestling and its TTTTTT tag tournament, 30 January to 31 March 1992, plus the EMWA's own Kings Of The Ring of 25 March, are held in this project's own Google Groups dumps and are unserved. Between them they carry this federation's own weekly title results week by week, its full ranking tables with win-loss records and points, and two of its own matches staged play-by-play. What they say about THIS belt: Admiral Ax holds it throughout — 10-0-0 in the table of 15 February 1992, beating Lord Plunder to retain on the 19th, beating Rage to retain on the 25th, and 14-1-0 in the table that week. His single loss in that span is the World Championship match he lost to Mr. B, not a defence of this belt. Nothing in the held record says when the reign began.

THE HELD-BUT-UNSERVED RECORD CARRIES THIS REIGN RUNNING, THOUGH NOT ITS START. The WeWF's own broadcasts of 15, 19 and 25 February 1992 have this holder as Intercontinental Champion at 10-0-0, beating Lord Plunder to retain, and beating Rage to retain, ending that week at 14-1-0. So the reign is continuous from at least mid-February to the relaunch call's recognition in May. Its beginning is still nowhere in the record, served or unserved.
The reign is now bounded at both ends of the winter: Mr. B holds this belt on 30 January 1992 and this holder holds it by 15 February, defending it over Lord Plunder on 19 February and over Rage on 25 February, and standing at 14-1 in the last rankings the newsdesk printed.
