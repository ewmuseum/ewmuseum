Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Won in one night, by one man, in the only tournament the fed's record holds end to end. Six men were bracketed for it at WrestleCade III on 2 May 2003; Deacon Holmes came through a triple threat and Leviathan through a triangle match, and Leviathan took the final by refusing to let go of an octopus hold until Holmes passed out inside it. Its own name had been in the fed's rumour mill a season earlier — a match for it between Johnny Dollar and the Enigma was supposed to have happened at the previous pay-per-view and does not appear on that card.

Vacant before the tournament: the front page's own championship row shows the PrimeStar belt paired with the site's own mysteryman silhouette rather than a champion's headshot. The fed's own front page hangs its six championships in a row as belt graphics that swap, on hover, for the current champion's own headshot; three of the six pairings are independently corroborated by the fed's own show text of the same week, which is what makes the mechanism readable at all.
