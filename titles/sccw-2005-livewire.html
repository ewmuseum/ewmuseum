The SCCW LiveWire Championship was first won by Evan 'Damage' Jackson on 12 November 2005. The fed's own record carries 34 reigns on it, across 31 different champions. The longest is Jadian Bridden's, 161 days from 27 September 2009. Two of its reigns ended outside a match — stripped from the holder or vacated. Mitchell Quinlan held it at the end of the surviving record.
