MLWO North American Championship

aka VPWF North American Championship

Placard

Promotion   MLWO (1996)
Active   1996–1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's second singles title. Angel of Pain lost it to Jim Mongol on the Wednesday Washout of 6 November 1996. Jak Martin, Sean Torrington and Danny Ice followed; Steve Sampson won it from Ice at Ground Zero on 28 June 1997, and the runner-up of the Torture Cage at Torture Chamber 2 took it under the match's own stipulation. Steve Spector won it from Matt Harris at Winter Inferno 97, and Tommy Marauder, the Man From Heck and Kevin Legend held it in 1998.
