EUWC United States Championship

Placard

Promotion   EUWC (1996)
Active   1997–2008
Unified into   EUWC International Championship 9 July 2008 — Cobris Grayson (entering as European Champion) defeated Texas Lightning (United States Champion, among others) in a four-way Unification Match at Blood Bath to become International Champion and unify the belts.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EUWC United States Championship changed hands more often than any other EUWC belt on record. Its ledger opens with YAHOO winning a tournament final on 25 May 1997 and cycled rapidly through Don Hall, The Crow, Stephon Kardell and Kris Blade before the promotion's first hiatus in April 1998; once EUWC returned it kept the same pace, naming dozens of reigns across every era through the promotion's second hiatus (Angel of Death, Ray Santana and Tony DeLucci among the early holders), its third (Cuchulain, T-Rex, David Harten and Bibyl), its fourth (Larry Serson, Rob Sampson, Gavin Glass, Marshall Snow and Virus), and its fifth (Ryan Baker, Samuel Roundtree, Major Action, T-Charger and Scott Samson) — Samuel Roundtree's second reign, in June 2005, absorbing the EUWC 52 Wild Championship outright in a fatal four way Unification match. Angel of Death carried the belt three separate times; Adam Benjamin lost the belt off-page, exact date unrecorded, to Texas Lightning, who carried it into the title's own final unification, into the EUWC International Championship, in the Unification Match at Blood Bath, 9 July 2008.
