Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's tag-team belts, introduced on the night of All Nite Action 2 when Jon Tees handed them to Matt Ward and Suriel N. Crowlie. Vinny Vegas and Maxx Kalifornia are said by the All Nite Action V card to have won them at Cold Fall; the belts were vacant on 1 January 2010, and Scott Owens and Morrigan won them by beating Parental Advisory in the thumbtacks, ladders and wire match at Idus Martias.

The Idus Martias card says Owens and Payne once 'cost Parental Advisory the championships'; no page says whether Parental Advisory ever held them, and the 2010 champions page lists the belts vacant from 1 January 2010 until that match.
