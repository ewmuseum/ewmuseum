Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Chad Dupree Memorial Cup's one roll of winners across both hosts: EUWC's four runnings, 2004 to 2007 (Jordan Lockhart, Rocko Daymon, Nero, STEALTH), and TEAM Wrestling's two, 2006 and 2007 (New Era of Wrestling, The A-List). The hosts number it differently: EUWC's 2006 card is the third annual, TEAM's 2006 final the inaugural, and EUWC's event history heads 2006 and 2007 alike “3rd Annual” against the 2007 card's “4th Annual.” Each stands as its page gives it.

Jordan Lockhart2004 (year)—Won the 2004 Chad Dupree Memorial Cup, defeating STEALTH in the final (semi-finalists: Havoc, Beast). EUWC's running of the Cup.
Rocko Daymon2005 (year)—Won the 2005 Chad Dupree Memorial Cup, billed the second annual, defeating Michael Lennox in the final (semi-finalists: Malik Johnson, Samuel Roundtree). EUWC's running, announced for 25 September 2005 as a sixteen-man bracket with competitors from the BWA, SOC and WCCW named beside EUWC's own, its roleplay posted on the WWC's forums, its first round on EUWC's Main Frame and its later rounds at the Cup's own pay-per-view.
Nero15 October 2006—Won the 2006 Chad Dupree Memorial Cup, billed the third annual, defeating Violent K in the final (semi-finalists: Cobris Grayson, Sevyn), at University Arena, Albuquerque, New Mexico. An eight-man bracket of four wrestlers from each of EUWC's two brands, Main Frame and Blackout.
New Era of Wrestling (HAL, MWG, Mr. Entertainment, Chaos)9 December 2006—Won TEAM Wrestling's 2006 Dupree Cup, which the broadcast bills the inaugural Cup, beating Message Board Entertainment two matches to one in the final. Twelve federation teams of four, in three groups of four, entered the roleplay tournament; New Era of Wrestling won its group unbeaten. The final's four were HAL, MWG, Mr. Entertainment and Chaos; the sign-in roster of 14 August 2006 had listed Larry Tact, Mr. Entertainment, Chaos and HAL. HAL and The Spoiler shared the Most Valuable Wrestler award.
STEALTH28 October 2007—Won the 2007 Chad Dupree Memorial Cup, billed on its card the fourth annual, defeating Rocko Daymon in the final (semi-finalists: Nero, Angel of Death), at the United Center, Chicago, Illinois. A sixteen-man single-elimination tournament for the vacant EUWC World Heavyweight Championship; the final made STEALTH champion, his seventh World Heavyweight reign by EUWC's own count.
The A-List6 February 2008—Won TEAM Wrestling's 2007 Dupree Cup, the second running by TEAM's count, beating A1E in a WarGames final at SuperShow VII. The team was entered as Danny Ferguson, Chandler Tsonda, The Illustrious Face-Eater and Mike Wade; in the final, Ferguson's bodyguard Dametreyus Fuquieawytas was in the cage as its fourth man. Danny Ferguson, the last man standing, took the Rob Sampson Memorial Most Valuable Wrestler Trophy.
fwrestling.com's community voting list of 6 January 2008 (/promotions/fwrestling/archive/fwrestling-com-wrestler-voting-list-2008-01-06.html) credits The Illustrious Face-Eater, under “Leagues: TEAM,” with having “Won Chris Williams Memorial Cup” in 2007. That is the other memorial Cup, which he won outright in its final of 12 September 2007 (show ptc-cwmc-2007-final); it is not this Cup, and the list does not mention this one.
