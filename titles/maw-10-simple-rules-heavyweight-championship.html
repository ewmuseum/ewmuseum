MAW: 10 Simple Rules MAW Heavyweight Championship

aka MAW Heavyweight Title

Placard

Promotion   MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)
Active   2007–2010

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MAW's top belt. Erik Strong holds it when the diary opens and defends it at nine of the first ten shows (Spring Fever's main event is a tag match), against Jean Cattley, Findlay O'Farraday, Citizen X, Ricky Douglas, Raphael, Antonio, Davis Wayne Newton and Jacob Jett, before losing it to Jean Cattley in a three-way with Black Eagle at Where It All Begins Again in October 2008. Jean Cattley keeps it against Erik Strong in a best-of-three-falls rematch at Remember November, then against Black Eagle, Ultimate Phoenix twice, Fumihiro Ota, the Masked Patriot, Stevie Grayson, Jacob Jett and Trent Shaffer, and fights Steven Parker to a time-limit draw at Iron in August 2009; the diary's own title records give the reign twelve defences. Cattley and Steven Parker draw again at Fall Of The King in September 2009; Parker then beats him for it in a thirty-minute Iron Man match at Where It All Begins Again in October 2009, keeps it against Darryl Devine and Cameron Vessey, and loses it at The Rip Chord Invitational in January 2010 to Jay Chord, who cashes in the title shot guaranteed to the previous year's Invitational winner; Jay keeps it against Thom Morgan, Stevie Grayson, Amazing Fire Fly and Kirk Jameson and fights Aaron Andrews to a time-limit draw at April Showers. The diary's own title records give Parker's second reign ten defences and Jay Chord's sixteen. Jay Chord keeps the belt through the summer of 2010 against Erik Strong, the Masked Patriot, Fumihiro Ota and Firebird in a three-way, Brandon Smith, Steve Flash and Trent Shaffer, until Aaron Andrews beats him in a thirty-minute Iron Man match at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2010, by two falls to one, after Rip Chord's bell and a referee's reversal of the last fall; Andrews keeps the belt against Joey Poison (Toxin) at Eleventh Hour in November. The diary's own title records list Andrews's reign as "Aaron Andrews October 2010 41". Aaron Andrews keeps the belt against Hugh de Aske at Remember November, Matthew Gauge at Season's Beatings, Jay Chord in a street fight at New Year, Old Rivalries on 12th January 2011 (the match the narrator calls MAW's best ever), Primus Allen at The Rip Chord Invitational, Firebird at March Madness and JD Morgan at April Showers; Tommy Cornell's threat to pull him back to TCW fails when he signs a new deal for a full year. In the diary's pages to September 2011 he keeps the belt against Black Eagle on 12th May, Erik Strong on 26th May, Kirk Jameson, Hugh de Aske, JD Morgan, Firebird and Jay Chord in a six-man Gold Rush match on 12th June, in which he turns on Jameson with a Standing Hot Shot onto an exposed turnbuckle, Fumihiro Ota on 12th July, Hugh de Aske on 26th July, Steve Flash on 12th August, Chance Fortune on 26th August and Ernest Youngman on 12th September. In the diary's pages to January 2012 he keeps the belt against Brandon Smith on 26th September, Randy Bumfhole in a time-limit draw on 12th October, Kirk Jameson, who cashes in the guaranteed shot of the Invitational title, on 26th October, Chance Fortune, Steve Flash and Fumihiro Ota, having written into a new contract the right to refuse challengers, and Randy Bumfhole again at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January 2012. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he keeps the belt against Jared Johnson at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February, Kashmir Singh at All Or Nothing on 12th February, Wanda Fish on 2nd March, in her last match, Brandon Smith on 5th March and Jim Force on 12th March; at April Showers on 5th April, one of the six champions in the Gold Rush match, he gets Randy Bumfhole thrown out of the field by using his right to refuse a challenger and comes through without being pinned. He keeps it against Steve Gumble on 8th April, Randy Bumfhole in a steel cage match on 12th April, which the narrator counts as his thirtieth title defence, and Donnie J in a title-versus-title match on 5th May, which wins him the All Action belt as well, and again in a thirty-minute Iron Man match on 12th May, which he wins by two falls to one, by the skin of his teeth. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he keeps the belt against Lassana Makutsi at High Noon on 5th June, which he chooses to open the show, Fumihiro Ota at South East Madness on 9th June, in a thirty-minute match the narrator counts as his thirty-fourth defence, KC Glenn at Summer Showdown on 12th June, who cashes in a guaranteed title shot, Bradford Peverell at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, in what the narrator calls the worst main event in years, Kid Toma at Beach Blast on 12th July and Rich Money at Summernova on 5th August, in a thirty-minute draw. At Summer Showdown he swings the belt into Kirk Jameson's head and joins The Fallen, and at Back To School on 5th September he loses a non-title steel cage match to Money. He is still the champion in the diary's pages for September 2012.
