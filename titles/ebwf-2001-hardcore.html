Renamed   EBWF No Limits Championship 24 September 2006 — The Title History's own entry: “Hardcore Title renamed No Limit Championship - PPV: Destiny - Sunday, September 24, 2006”.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Tommy Dreamer held it first, from 15 April 2002, and took it three times in its opening year. Fifty-seven reigns ran through it in four and a half years, faster than any other EBWF belt changed hands: The Hurricane took it four times across 2004 and 2005, Cactus Jack, Chris Benoit and David Flair three each. At the Destiny Pay-Per-View on 24 September 2006 the promotion renamed it the No Limits Championship, with Raven's own reign running through the change and on into the new name's record.

Source: the promotion's own Title History page (/promotions/ebwf-2001/archive/titlehistory.html). The Title History carries this lineage under the No Limits name, which it writes as “Hardcore Title” only in the rename entry itself; no page read gives the belt a longer name than that.
