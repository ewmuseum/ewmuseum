CSWA Presidential Championship

Placard

Promotion   CSWA (1988)
Active   1994–2005

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt tangled up with the office it is named for. The fed's own newspaper describes it as previously retired and revived at THANKSGIVING WEEKEND SPECTACULAR '99, when a returning Co-Commissioner ousted the other one from the commissionership and put the title back on a wrestler. Randy Harders lost it to a returning Alex Wylde in an impromptu match in Philadelphia; Aaron Douglas took it from Cameron Cruise at ANNIVERSARY 2000 on a three-count made by Hornet himself. By April 2001 the fed's own Champions page carries it as vacated, to be filled at ELVIS LIVES XII, and does not say why.
