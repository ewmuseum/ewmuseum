Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A championship of the fed's first run. Shelldog won it first in a four-way at High Impact on 27 August 2000, and Falken and D. Jaxx followed; on 13 October 2000 the fed's title history records it stripped from D. Jaxx and retired. It came back at Love Hurts on 26 February 2001, when Ron Jacobs won it in a three-way, and passed through T.Q. Smooth, who was stripped of it in March, Andy Overdose and Matt Senate to Chris Taylor on 4 May 2001. By the end of October 2001 the title history marked it retired again.

Sources: the fed's own title history on thefsw.com, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 8 April and 31 October 2001; the Love Hurts write-up; the champions pages captured on 8 April and 28 December 2001.
