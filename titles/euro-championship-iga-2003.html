aka IGA European Championship, IGA Euro Title

Placard

Promotion   International Grappling Association (2001)
Active   2001

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Introduced on 7 November 2001, when Estrago outlasted nine other men in an elimination match to become its first champion; he defended it against Nick Hart and Marco Avery.
