Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The first champion on the recovered pages is Tails, from 1 February 2009; 4 reigns are recorded, the last held by Brenton Cyrus and still running when the record ends.

The one-year show of 18 January 2010 bills The Notorious K.I.D as 'Heavyweight and Undisputed Champion'; which heavyweight belt that is, and when he won it, is not on the recovered pages.
