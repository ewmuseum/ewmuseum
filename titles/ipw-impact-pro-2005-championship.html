The fed's own title history lists five reigns of the IPW Championship, from 1 July 2005 to 9 April 2008, with the title twice retired for the fed's closings and once more on its last night, and a footnote saying it was renamed the UCW Championship while UCW held it in 2005.

The page's footnotes record that the title was retired three times: when UCW closed in 2005, when IPW closed in November 2006, and on the last night in April 2008.
