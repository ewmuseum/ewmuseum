MAW: 10 Simple Rules MAW Traditional Championship

aka MAW Traditional Title

Placard

Promotion   MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)
Active   2009–2012

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MAW's second singles belt, announced by the narrator at All Or Nothing in February 2009 "in honour of the great success stories of the past". Stevie Grayson wins it in the ten-man Mayhem Battle Royal at March Madness, keeps it against Jay Chord at Spring Fever and Curtis Jenkins at Summer Showdown, and loses it to Darryl Devine at Beach Blast; Devine keeps it from Ultimate Phoenix at Iron. Stevie Grayson regains it from Darryl Devine at Where It All Begins Again in October 2009 and loses it to Hugh de Aske at End Of Days in December; de Aske keeps it against Fumihiro Ota, Hugh Lee, "Bulldozer" Brandon Smith (a time-limit draw) and Kirk Jameson until Aaron Andrews wins it at Spring Fever in April 2010, and Andrews keeps it against Antonio at Mother, May I?. Aaron Andrews keeps it against Casey Valentine and Jared Johnson in a triangle match, Firebird and Toxin until Primus Allen beats him at Iron in August 2010; Allen keeps it against Kirk Jameson and Joe Benning and loses it at Where It All Begins Again in October to Jonnie Perez, who keeps it on the November tour shows. The diary's own title records list Allen under August 2010 and Perez under October 2010. Jonnie Perez keeps it on the December and early January tour shows and against Matthew Gauge at End Of Days, until Gauge wins it at The Rip Chord Invitational on 21st January 2011; Gauge, leaving for Japan, loses it to Hugh de Aske in a three-way with Perez at Leap Into The Unknown on 12th February, and de Aske keeps it against Perez, Jean Cattley and "Regular" Joe Benning. The diary's own title records list Gauge under January 2011 with no defence and "Hugh de Aske (2)" under February 2011. Hugh de Aske keeps it against Fumihiro Ota, Providence, Black Eagle and, in a fifteen-minute draw, Jay Chord, until Primus Allen beats him at Summernova on 12th August 2011 with a Sitout Spinebuster; Allen keeps it against Ernest Youngman and, in a three-way, Steve Flash and Erik Strong. The diary's own title records list "Primus Allen (2)" under August 2011. Primus Allen keeps it against Bradford Peverell at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2011, Riley McManus at Season's Beatings, Jared Johnson in a fifteen-minute draw at End Of Days, Kid Arachnid on 5th January 2012 and Kirk Jameson at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January, KC Glenn, the Invitational champion, in a time-limit draw at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February 2012, Randy Bumfhole at Third Time's The Charm on 5th March and Lassana Makutsi at March Madness on 12th March, and loses it at April Showers on 5th April to JD Morgan, who pins him in the six-champion Gold Rush match. JD Morgan keeps it against Bradford Peverell and Lord Geoffrey Windameer in a three-way at Mother, May I? on 12th May, and keeps it in the diary's pages from June to September 2012 against Steve Gumble in a draw at High Noon on 5th June, Amazing Fire Fly at Summer Showdown on 12th June, Gumble at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, Donnie J in a thirty-minute draw for the Traditional and All Action titles at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July, Black Eagle at Beach Blast on 12th July, Sean Deeley at South East Grand Parade on 9th August and Rudy Velasquez at Iron on 12th August; he is pinned by Deeley in a six-man match at Back To School on 5th September, a non-title match, and is still the champion in the diary's pages for September 2012. The diary's own title records list "JD Morgan April 2012 12 Won in a Gold Rush match".

He keeps it against Fumihiro Ota at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, Providence on 26th May, Black Eagle on 26th June and, in a fifteen-minute draw, Jay Chord on 12th July, is no longer available for the touring shows now that, in the narrator's words, he has "wised up to his true worth", and loses the belt to Primus Allen at Summernova on 12th August 2011.
Primus Allen12 August 20115 April 2012Won from Hugh de Aske at Summernova with a Sitout Spinebuster, after throwing the champion into a bank of lockers backstage and carrying the match through a flying lariat that seemed to give Hugh the answer; the second reign of his career. He keeps the belt against Ernest Youngman at Iron on 26th August, an extended squash, and against Steve Flash and Erik Strong in a three-way at Back To School on 12th September, Bradford Peverell at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October, Riley McManus at Season's Beatings, Jared Johnson in a fifteen-minute draw at End Of Days, Kid Arachnid on 5th January 2012 and Kirk Jameson at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January, KC Glenn in a time-limit draw at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February 2012, Randy Bumfhole at Third Time's The Charm on 5th March and Lassana Makutsi at March Madness on 12th March, and loses it at April Showers on 5th April 2012 in the six-champion Gold Rush match, when JD Morgan hits him with A Shot Of JD and pins him.
JD Morgan5 April 2012—Won in the six-champion Gold Rush match at April Showers, which Rip Chord revives from the year before with every champion entered and a title lost to any champion who is pinned or made to submit: with Haley Buck screaming at him from the outside to make the cover, Morgan turns Primus Allen round into A Shot Of JD and pins him, so keeping the All Action title and taking the Traditional title as well. He keeps it against Bradford Peverell and Lord Geoffrey Windameer in a three-way at Mother, May I? on 12th May 2012, putting Peverell away with the Cross Atlantic Stretch after a half nelson suplex, and keeps it in the diary's pages from June to September 2012 against Steve Gumble in a draw at High Noon on 5th June, Amazing Fire Fly at Summer Showdown on 12th June, Gumble at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, Donnie J in a thirty-minute draw at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July, Black Eagle with the Mid Atlantic Stretch at Beach Blast on 12th July, Sean Deeley at South East Grand Parade on 9th August and Rudy Velasquez at Iron on 12th August; he is pinned by Deeley in a non-title six-man match at Back To School on 5th September, and is still the champion at the end of this range.
