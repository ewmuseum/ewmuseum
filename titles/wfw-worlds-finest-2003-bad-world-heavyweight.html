Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Separated from the WFW World Heavyweight Title on 16 August 2003, when Shawn Hart handed it to Diablo. Rat Fink took it from Diablo in Central Park, helped by The American and Fink Bot, and kept it against Psycho in a no-disqualification brawl at Almost Live. Rat Fink kept it against Pulsar at Ghoulish Games, but Felix Red took it from him at Turkey Day Terror on 8 December 2003 with the help of two fans, and won a tag match beside Richard Gideon at Merrython. Felix Red kept it against Psycho at Mind Over Matter on 22 January 2004, and Minion took it from him in a falls-count-anywhere match at Great Expectations on 18 February 2004; the line-up announced at Vox Hiberionacum Part Deux on 27 March says Minion vacated it with a torn ligament. Jared Wells won the vacant title in a four-man falls count anywhere elimination match at the Superbowl of Wrestling on 18 April 2004 and kept it against Mike Lane at BOMB Voyage on 9 June 2004. Jared Wells kept it against Iron Lion at Cherry Blossom Chaos II on 15 August 2004. The fed's own history of the belt has Steve Johnson take it from Wells on 17 April 2007, ending a reign of almost three years, and Wells beat Johnson at Unplugged on 31 December 2009; the fed carried the title into its NGEN relaunch with Wells as champion on 20 January 2010.

The fed presents this as the title of the old BAD promotion and, until 16 August 2003, held it together with the WFW World Heavyweight Title; that night Shawn Hart, who had just won both, handed it to Diablo to be defended "hardcore style" under falls-count-anywhere, no-disqualification, no-countout rules. No earlier BAD reign is carried onto this title. The fed's own post, headed B.A.D. World Heavyweight Title History (first posted 6 January 2005 and kept up to 2010), repeats the reigns of the unified title before the 16 August 2003 split and dates the later changes a few days after this wing's show rows (Shawn Hart's win over Doc Silver on 20 August 2003, Rat Fink's on 7 September, Felix Red's on 10 December, Minion's on 23 February 2004, Jared Wells's on 22 April) and Minion's vacating on 10 March 2004 at Road to Glory, where this wing's row dates it from the line-up of 27 March; this wing's rows use the dates of the broadcasts and, for the 2007 and 2009 changes, which only the list tells, the list's own dates.
