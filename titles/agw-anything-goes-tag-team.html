AGW Anything Goes Tag Team Titles

Placard

Promotion   AGW (1997)
Active   1999–2000
Renamed   AGW U.S. Tag Team Titles 27 March 1999 — picks up the U.S. Tag Team Titles' own lineage per that title's discontinuation event.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AGW's tag championship successor to the discontinued U.S. Tag Team Titles, its first reign the finals of a 16-team tournament (footnote 22) beginning 27 March 1999; 21 recorded reigns follow before the ledger's own last word, New Death Sensation's reign beginning 30 January 2000, running into the fed's own hiatus.
