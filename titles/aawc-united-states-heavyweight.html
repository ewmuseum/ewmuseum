Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The AAWC's secondary singles belt, and the one with the longest memory in the fed's own commentary. Marcus Price held it in the mid-1990s and lost it to Dr. Silver, who "outsmarted Price two years ago to beat him for the very same AAWC United States title"; Mike Randalls held it at some point too, billed as a former champion in his own right on a night the record does not date. Price won it back in Pittsburgh the week before Oktoberfest '96 and was booked to defend it on both of that event's nights — first against Randalls, then against "Marvelous" Mike Chain. The Randalls defence is the one the record holds: a bloodbath in which Randalls jabbed a broken table leg into his own forehead to shake off the Fashion Statement, and Price tried to walk up the aisle and was sent back by the fed's own President. The one surviving copy of the card ends mid-count on a Price small package, so who left New Orleans with the belt is not recorded. Nine months after that, in July 1997, the belt was on Anarky, booked to defend it at Wembley Stadium in a rematch with "Marvelous" Mike Chain.

Attested by the fed's own Oktoberfest '96 (Night 1) card of 25 October 1996 (promotions/nfw/archive/news-1996-10-25-aawc-oktoberfest-96-night-1.html).
