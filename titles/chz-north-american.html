Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Created in the New CHZ in 2001: Don Rossi won it on 2 May 2001, beating Chester Park in the final, and lost it three weeks later to Thor. Steve Santos made Thor submit for it in July and kept it through the autumn shows, against Gazz Maybury on 21 September and Matt Helms on 9 November, until he retired; Matt Helms won it from Brian Nash at the 12 December 2001 Wild and lost it to Violent Dave on 9 January 2002; Rossi took it back from Dave on 6 February 2002, the first two-time champion. Rossi retains it against Diablo by disqualification on 22 March 2002 and is billed to defend it again at NBK Pride, with no result surviving. Rob Lewis beat Don Rossi for it at BreakOut 2002 on 18 December 2002; Lucifer is billed its champion on 5 March 2003, when he does not come to work for a defence against Bloodlust, and Keyser Soze was its champion by 26 March 2003 and kept it through a count-out loss to Chris Holler on 9 April.

The fed's own North American title history (/promotions/chz/archive/north-american-title-history.html): Santos lost it to Matt Helms on 21 September 2001; Violent Dave took it from Helms on 11 January 2002 and Rossi from Dave on 6 February 2002.
