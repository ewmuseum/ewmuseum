Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The alliance's tag team championship, first won at the end of a tournament that ran across its member feds for the better part of a year and finished at New Era on 20 April 2003 — Shane Stone and the Bum Boozler, billed as Homo/Phobic, over the Shadow Riders of Lights Out Wrestling. They held it until 3 February 2004, when the Bum Boozler withdrew from tag team competition and the titles were declared vacant. Souls of the Black Lotus won the vacant titles in a three-way on 19 May 2004 and vacated them on 9 July, when the team retired; the Shadow Riders won them at PTC Survival on 22 August 2004 and lost them around 21 December to Clique Clique BOOM~! in a steel cage match decided on the roleplays. The Masked Monkeys of UWF won them on 24 March 2005 and lost them on 26 April to Unholy Matrimony, Seymour Almasy and Aerith Almasy of UWF, in a three-way decided on the judges' scores for the roleplays, which no federation would host and which was never written; the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean took them from Unholy Matrimony uncontested on 28 May, when the champions did not roleplay, and held them until 4 September, when they were vacated because the team was no longer active in PRIME. The record's last word on the belt is the opening match of PTC vs Archibald MacGregor on 15 September 2006, where Unite, billed as the current GCW Tag Team Champions, won the vacant title over the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean; they were the last champions, and the title was retired on 6 January 2007. PTC's own title log dates the Shadow Riders' win to 9 November 2004, the day the results were posted, where the show's own billed date is 22 August.

The fed's own pages style the belt three ways on one night — "PTC Tag Team Titles Match" on the card, "PTC World Tag Team Title Match" as the results' own match header, and "PTC Tag Team Champions" in the commentary that crowned them. Recorded under the shortest form, with the others as aka.
