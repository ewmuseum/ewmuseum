EUWC North American Championship

Placard

Promotion   EUWC (1996)
Active   1996–2008
Unified into   EUWC International Championship 28 September 1997 — HICAL (International Champion) defeated Chet Jenkins (North American Champion) at And The Winner Is... Part V: War Games to unify the belts. The North American title lay dormant until a November 2005 revival.
Unified into   EUWC International Championship 9 July 2008 — Cobris Grayson (entering as European Champion) defeated Braden Kincaid (North American Champion, among others) in a four-way Unification Match at Blood Bath to become International Champion and unify the belts.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EUWC North American Championship's own history table opens with STEALTH winning it in EUWC's first-ever 20-man Blood Bath, 27 October 1996, then changed hands seven more times across the fed's founding year before Chet Jenkins carried it into a unification match at And The Winner Is... Part V: War Games on 28 September 1997, where HICAL (as International Champion) absorbed it into the International Championship. The belt itself lay dormant for eight years — the ledger's own "Defunct" — before Lt. Hawkeye's win over Cuchulain and Tony DeLucci at Blood Bath, 20 November 2005, "resurrected thanks to the roster split"; due to a short hiatus that same night, the reign's own active clock is recorded as beginning 9 February 2006. Angel of Death, Cuchulain, Artur Arina and Braden Kincaid carried it into the fed's own final active years. Braden Kincaid's own reign carried the belt to its second and final unification, into the EUWC International Championship, in the Unification Match at Blood Bath, 9 July 2008 -- Kincaid himself was never pinned in the fall.
