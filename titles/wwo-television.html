Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Absorbed the WWO Cruiserweight Championship's own lineage in 1999, then changed hands five times in ten months: "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian took it from Night Stalker in Manchester, Apex took it from Sebastian in Orlando, Latex Lex took it from Apex in Roanoke, Dark Soul took it from Lex in Huntington, and "Smooth as Silk" Miguel Quesada took it from Dark Soul in Salt Lake City on 25 November 1999 — the same night, by the ledger's own dating, the International People's Championship also changed hands. The belt's own earlier history records a Lancaster Bomber reign ended by his own firing and a Wuzho reign forfeited when its holder was arrested in Mexico. Quesada is the ledger's own last word: the federation closed with him still champion.
