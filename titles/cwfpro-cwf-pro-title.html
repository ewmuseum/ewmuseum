Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened April 1997 as the CWF-Pro World Heavyweight Title (renamed the CWF-Pro Title that December), when the Icon Miguel Sanchez won a battle royal eliminating Gator at the fed's own “2nd TNF” to become its 1st CWF-Pro World Champion, unifying three developmental belts (UBH, AIWA, DBWF) into it the same night. Across 143 recorded reigns Mike Powers (22), Sanchez (18) and Beastman (14) dominate the ledger, and dozens more changes each fold in a rival promotion's own top belt — the EWF, UCWF, WWWWF, IWWA, IWF, NWO and AWI World Titles among many others — as the belt absorbed CWF-Pro's acquired predecessors and in-house developmental leagues one by one. The ledger's own printed numbering carries two genuine irregularities, preserved exactly as the source prints them: two reign changes are marked “X” instead of a sequential number — one restores Major Disorder to the belt while reprinting his own earlier “(2)” reign-count suffix rather than advancing it, the other restores Seven — and two further numbers, 49 and 133, are simply missing from the fed's own printed list. Eric Michaels holds the belt at the ledger's own last recorded line; 16 of the 143 recorded champions survive only as bare ledger names.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 143 reigns in full; all 143 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/cwf-pro-title.html for the underlying primary source.
