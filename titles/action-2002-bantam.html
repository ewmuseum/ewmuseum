Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Action!'s Bantam Championship was first won by Joey Malone on 10 July 2002. The fed's own record carries 8 reigns on it, across 8 different champions. The longest is Joey Malone's, 81 days from 10 July 2002. Keith Scott Zimmerman held it at the end of the surviving record.

Pre-history residue, 2002-03-31 ('Resentment' special): a ladder match for a Bantam Championship the fed's OWN OWNER character retracts on air minutes later ('it's way too soon to bring out the B Title'), before the belt's real creation by tournament on 2002-07-10 (bantam-001). No reign, no championship existed to change hands; see bantam-001's note. Two mechanical conflicts on this belt are resolved by the legacy log row 2546 (bantam-007); see its note.

No championship existed to change hands: this is a non-event, not a reign, and not a defence of anything (the real Bantam Championship is created by tournament four months later, at Joey Malone's win above).
