VXW Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   VXW (2001)
Active   2006

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Held by Hot Property, "All That" Austin Lee and "Hotstuff" Stevie Knight, surviving a draw against Team Brittania, a defense against The Rhombus, and a three-way title match at Vegas Violence V that Team Brittania won by disqualification without taking the belts. Survived a publicly rejected stripping rumor on Setting Sun I and a double-pin, no-decision Tornado Tag Team defense against The O'Malley Brothers on Christmas Comes Early, then a scored Irish Street Fight against the same challengers on New Years Eve Of Destruction, before losing the belts to the O'Malley Brothers at Ghost Town Blues in a Tables Ladders and Chairs match; the O'Malleys retained the titles in their own first defense, against Team Brittania at St Patricks Day Massacre, on a disqualification.

The O'Malley Brothers27 February 2006—Anthony O'Malley unhooked the suspended title belts while Stevie Knight fell from the other ladder, in a Tables Ladders and Chairs match, ending Hot Property's own reign.
The O'Malley Brothers' own first VXW Tag Team Championship win, after a No DQ Tornado loss to Wheeling's Thugs and a scored Irish Street Fight loss to the champions earlier in their own pursuit. Retained the titles in their first defense, against Team Brittania at St Patricks Day Massacre, on a disqualification after Anthony O'Malley struck Simon Davidson with a chair. Lost the titles to Team Brittania at The Hype III, Justin Davidson pinning Marcus O'Malley with the Kiss of Death after valet Kelly DeClair's own ringside interference.
Team Brittania17 April 2006—Pinfall, Justin Davidson's Kiss of Death on Marcus O'Malley, after valet Kelly DeClair threw makeup in Marcus's eyes and low-blowed Anthony O'Malley from the apron.
Team Brittania's own first VXW Tag Team Championship win, after a four-way draw against champions Hot Property at Birthday Bash 2005 and a disqualification win over The O'Malley Brothers at St Patricks Day Massacre that did not change the title in their own earlier pursuit of it; wins the titles outright this time from The O'Malley Brothers, who had held them since Ghost Town Blues. Retained the titles in their first defense, against Purgatory at Hotter Than Hell I, on a disqualification after Synn accidentally kicked the referee. Lost the titles to The O'Malleys at Vegas Violence 2006 in a six-team scramble, Marcus O'Malley pinning Justin Davidson with a small package after Davidson nearly stole the title on a hot cover of a beaten-down Marcus.
The O'Malleys5 October 2006—Pinfall, Marcus O'Malley's small package on Justin Davidson, in a six-team scramble that also saw The Dierdorfs and Purgatory disqualified.
The O'Malleys' second VXW Tag Team Championship win (their own pre-match promo claimed a third reign overall, a count the held record's documented history does not fully support), ending Team Brittania's own reign at Vegas Violence 2006. A returning Blake Hamilton and Lance Voltar attacked the match's own last team standing, The Outlaw Morris Bass, moments before the finish. Lost the titles, together, when The Outlaw Morris Bass clotheslined Marcus and Anthony O'Malley through the two-story cage's own wall during Phear Phantastic 2006's own Rage In The Cage main event, pinning both men at once to claim both belts alone.
The Outlaw Morris Bass6 November 2006—Pinfall over both Marcus and Anthony O'Malley at once, clotheslining them together through the two-story cage's own wall to the arena floor during the Rage In The Cage main event.
The Outlaw Morris Bass's first VXW Tag Team Championship win, credited as a solo champion after the broadcast's own referee awarded him both title belts; revealed immediately afterward as an ally of a returning Kevin Simmons, who chair-shotted Marcus O'Malley from outside the ring. Retained by disqualification over The O'Malleys at Sin City Fights November 27 after Simmons again chair-shotted Marcus O'Malley, then helped Simmons put both O'Malleys down with a Durham Deathdrop and a Widowmaker after the bell. Lost the title to Roman Anderson and John Manning, billed afterward as The Outcasts, at New Year's Eve of Destruction 2006, John Manning's schoolboy landing after a returning Adam Jennings distracted Bass from the entrance stage.
The Outcasts (Roman Anderson, John Manning)25 January 2007—Pinfall, John Manning's schoolboy on champion 'The Outlaw' Morris Bass, after a returning Adam Jennings appeared on the entrance stage and distracted Bass mid-cover.
Roman Anderson and John Manning's own first VXW Tag Team Championship win, together billed afterward as The Outcasts, ending 'The Outlaw' Morris Bass's own solo reign. A Sin City Fights February 7 angle framed the returning Dierdorfs' own long pursuit of a title shot, but the bout it produced was fought as a non-title singles match between Anderson and Kris Dierdorf, which Anderson won by disqualification after Brian Dierdorf and valet Delila jumped into the ring; Manning cleared the ring afterward with his own title belt.
Hot Propertyunknown—Unknown — the record opens with the team already champions.
Retained through a four-way draw against Team Brittania at Birthday Bash 2005, a defense against The Rhombus on Prelude To Violence I, and a three-way title match against Purgatory and Team Brittania at Vegas Violence V that ended by disqualification with no title change. Confirmed still champions on Setting Sun I after the promotion's own management publicly rejected a stripping rumor stemming from Austin Lee's own Rage In The Cage cover of his tag partner Stevie Knight at Phear Phantastic 2005, then survived a Tornado Tag Team title defense against The O'Malley Brothers on Christmas Comes Early that ended in a double-pin with no official decision rendered. Retained again on New Years Eve Of Destruction, beating The O'Malley Brothers 12-9 in a scored Irish Street Fight after two of Chris Wheeling's own hired men drove Marcus O'Malley through a table. Lost the title to the O'Malley Brothers on Ghost Town Blues in a Tables Ladders and Chairs match, Anthony O'Malley unhooking the belts while Stevie Knight fell from the other ladder.
