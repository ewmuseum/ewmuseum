Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Christian held it first, from 15 April 2002, and Lita took it from him that May — the one reign a woman holds on this belt's record. Rey Mysterio Jr. and The Hurricane traded it through 2003, three reigns to four, and it was emptied twice in 2002, at Unforgiven that September and on Smackdown that November. At the Destiny Pay-Per-View on 24 September 2006 the promotion renamed it the X-Division Championship, with Syxx's own reign running through the change and on into the new name's record.

Source: the promotion's own Title History page (/promotions/ebwf-2001/archive/titlehistory.html). The Title History writes this belt's own name only as “LHW Title”, in the rename entry, and no page read spells the abbreviation out.
