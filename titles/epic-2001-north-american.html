Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Epic Wrestling's North American Championship, won by Jason Winters on 26 November 2001 and taken from him by the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid in a Poisoned Pyramid Deathmatch at Epic Warfare VI on 23 December 2001. Sam Potright beat the Trapezoid for it at Vengeful Hearts on 24 February 2002 and holds it at the record's end. The federation's own title history also lists reigns from 1983 to 1995: the invented past of its story, served on its page.

Source: the federation's own title history (/promotions/epic-2001/archive/epic-2001-title-history.html); the champions page of 1 March 2002 (not served: a two-line list) names Ben Archer and Sam Potright. The history prints the last change 'February 24, 2001'; Vengeful Hearts is dated 24 February 2002 by its own 2002 event list and by the Trapezoid's own reign, which began on 23 December 2001.
