Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Kurt Angle won it vacant on 15 April 2002, the earliest date the promotion's own record carries, and it changed hands forty-eight times across six years. Raven took it nine times between June 2002 and April 2004, more often than anyone else; Christian took it seven times, once in 2002 and six more across 2005 and 2006. It emptied four times — in June 2002, after the King of the Ring in June 2004, in November 2005, and on 1 September 2006, the day after Chris Jericho won it, when his own retirement ended a reign one day old. Triple H's win at Wrestlemania 7 on 30 March 2008 is the last change recorded, and the promotion's own Champions page still names him champion.
