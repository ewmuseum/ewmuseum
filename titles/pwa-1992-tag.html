Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Won on the same June tournament card as the singles belts and defended through a summer of run-ins and DQ finishes, changing hands only once on camera — an upset by the Dogs of War — before it reached Crime & Punishment, its last PWA-billed holders, in time for the Clash of the Masters.
