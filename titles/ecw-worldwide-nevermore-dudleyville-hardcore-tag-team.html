Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Dudley Boyz's own belt, which they declare on Blood, Sweat and Beers the night after Summer Assylum in July 2007, when the Dudley family bring out the old World Tag Team Championships, "back from when Bubba and I were dominating anything that stood in our path", and D-Von says they are rechristened the Dudleyville Hardcore Titles, because Paul Heyman will not give them a fair crack at the Whole F**kin' Show's belts; the diary's own title history (PDF p.8, 'ECW/Dudleyville Hardcore Tag Team Championship') does list it, dating the Dudley Boyz's first reign from 28 July 2007. The Dudley Boyz make their first defence on Revolution in August 2007, beating the bWo with a 3D on Hollywood Nova after Chessman and Oz hit the bWo with the belts, and beat Balls & Axl, who beat the Samoan Gangstas to earn the match, with a 3D on Axl on Blood, Sweat and Beers; the Hardcore Innovators challenge for them at Heatwave on 24 August 2007 and Los Guerreros are billed as third challengers, so the Dudleyz are still the holders when the pages of August 2007's first week end. At Heatwave in August 2007 the Hardcore Innovators, Cactus Jack and Tommy Dreamer, win the belts from the Dudley Boyz in the Dudleyville Hardcore Championship Match, the Dudleyz having defended them earlier that month against Los Guerreros, the Samoan Gangstas and the Briscoe Boys; Joey Styles says the Dudleyville Hardcore Championship has become the ECW Hardcore Championship, and the diary's later posts call the belts the ECW Hardcore Tag Team Championship, 'newly rechristened'. The Innovators ask the holders of the World Tag Team Championship for a Champions versus Champions match at Anarchy Rulz and are still the holders when the pages of September 2007's fourth Monday end. At Guilty As Charged in October 2007 the Dudley Boyz win the Three Way Dance for the tag titles and take the Dudleyville belts back from the Hardcore Innovators (26 October); the Innovators leave the arena with all four belts anyway, and the history lists the Dudleyville belt's unification with the World Tag Team Championship on 30 November 2007, still ahead. In November 2007 the Innovators still carry the Dudleyville belts with the others; Paul Heyman announces that they are to be unified with the World Tag Team Championship at November To Remember XV. At November To Remember XV the Dudley Boyz beat the Hardcore Innovators, and the Dudleyville belts are unified with the World Tag Team Championship on 30 November 2007, the date the history gives.

The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray and D-Von) (Buh Buh Ray Dudley, D-Von Dudley)28 July 200724 August 2007The Dudley Boyz's only reign in this range: they say on Blood, Sweat and Beers that the belts are the old World Tag Team Championships from when they dominated the division and that these are now the Dudleyville Hardcore Titles, with the second generation of Dudleyz at their side; they make the first defence on Revolution in August, beating the bWo with a 3D on Hollywood Nova after Chessman and Oz hit the bWo with the belts, beat Balls & Axl with a 3D on Axl on Blood, Sweat and Beers, and are challenged by the Hardcore Innovators for Heatwave and by Los Guerreros. In August 2007 they keep the belts over Los Guerreros, the Samoan Gangstas and the Briscoe Boys, with their relatives' help; the Hardcore Innovators cane them after the last of these and walk off with the belts, and at Heatwave Cactus Jack and Tommy Dreamer win the Dudleyville Hardcore Championship Match, putting D-Von through a table with a Double DDT: the reign ends there.
The belt is the Dudleyz's own, made and named by them on screen; no match is wrestled for it. The diary's own title history (PDF p.8) lists it and dates this reign 28 July 2007 (the Saturday show the night after Summer Assylum) to 24 August 2007, Heatwave (Friday, week 4, August 2007), where the Hardcore Innovators win the belts.
The Hardcore Innovators (Cactus Jack and Tommy Dreamer) (Cactus Jack, Tommy Dreamer)24 August 200726 October 2007Cactus Jack and Tommy Dreamer beat the Dudley Boyz for the belts at Heatwave: the Innovators cane the second generation of Dudleyz on the outside, Cactus Jack's Double Arm DDT catches Bubba Ray as he rebounds, Dreamer rakes D-Von's eye, and the two put D-Von through a table with a Double DDT before Cactus covers (1...2...3). Joey Styles says the Dudleyville Hardcore Championship has become the ECW Hardcore Championship, the diary's later previews call the belts the (newly rechristened) ECW Hardcore Tag Team Championship, and the Whole F**kin' Show attack the new champions after the match. In September, with Beulah McGillicutty at their side, the Innovators ask the World Tag Team Champions for a Champions versus Champions match at Anarchy Rulz to crown the Undisputed Tag Team Champions; they are still the holders when the pages of September 2007's fourth Monday end. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 the Innovators lose the Champions versus Champions match by a no contest when the Dudley Family run in; in October, with Beulah McGillicutty, they ask Paul Heyman for a Singapore Cane Match, and at Guilty As Charged they are eliminated in the Three Way Dance (Bubba Ray pins Tommy Dreamer, then Cactus Jack): the reign ends there, 26 October 2007, though they leave the arena with all four belts and take them again from ringside after beating the second generation of Dudleyz on Blood, Sweat and Beers.
The belt is the Dudleyz's own, made and named by them on screen and won from them here. The diary's own title history (PDF p.8) lists it and dates this reign 24 August 2007 (Heatwave) to 26 October 2007 (Guilty As Charged). The diary's text calls the belts the ECW Hardcore Tag Team Championship after this match; the title's own page keeps the name the Dudleyz gave it.
The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray and D-Von) (Buh Buh Ray Dudley, D-Von Dudley)26 October 200730 November 2007The Dudley Boyz win the Three Way Dance at Guilty As Charged, which the card bills as the match 'to unify the tag team championships', and take back the Dudleyville belts from the Hardcore Innovators; the Innovators leave with the belts, and retrieve all four again on Blood, Sweat and Beers. In November 2007 Paul Heyman announces the Undisputed ECW Tag Team belts and a match at November To Remember XV in which the Dudley Boyz and the Hardcore Innovators unify the tag titles. At November To Remember XV the Dudley Boyz beat the Hardcore Innovators and raise the Undisputed ECW Tag Team Titles; the Dudleyville belts are unified with the World Tag Team belts there, on the date (30 November 2007) the diary's history gives.
The history lists this belt separately and gives the reign as running to 30 November 2007, when it is 'unified with the World Tag Team Championship', and the text agrees: the unification is the Undisputed Tag Team Championship Match at November To Remember XV (PDF pp.1706-1707); the World Tag Team history treats the Dudley Boyz as holders of the undisputed titles from 26 October, and each history's date is filed in its own title.
