Action!'s Action! "Television" Championship's first recorded champion is Keith Scott Zimmerman. The fed's own record carries 5 reigns on it, across 4 different champions. The longest is Keith Scott Zimmerman's, 21 days from 2003-01-06. Sweet Aroma held it at the end of the surviving record.

The '(t)' marker on the Pressure Point 59 match Dirk $eller vs. Sweet Aroma (5 May 2003) is the fed's own champion tag for it, exactly as (A!)/(b)/(c)/(D) mark its four filed belts -- treated here as a real lineage the record carries, served as an unfiled belt with its lineage.
