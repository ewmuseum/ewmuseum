CSWA World Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   CSWA (1988)
Active   1988–1995

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The first tag championship, and the one act that runs through the whole of the fed's first two years. The C.S. Express beat the Big Connection to become the first champions in 1988 and then defended it on every card the archive lists: a lumberjack two-out-of-three falls against the Dirty Duo, a double bullrope match against the Indian Warriors, a scaffold match against Armed and Dangerous. They were still holding it at the end of 1989.
