Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

TCW's newest belt: Mayhem Midden unveils it on Total Wrestling in Week 2 June 2008 and makes every match for it No Holds Barred (no run-ins, no weapons, no disqualification for using only your body). Eddie Peak, Sam Keith, Tyson Baine (who beats American Buffalo in a Strap Match) and Ricky Dale Johnson (who beats Frankie Perez on Badge of Honor) qualify, and Sam Keith wins the first championship in a four-way match at Excessive Force. Sam Keith keeps it through July 2008: he wins by disqualification over Tyson Baine on Total Wrestling, after Tommy Cornell's chair, and beats Ricky Dale Johnson at Summer Showdown with the Proton Lock. Sam Keith keeps it through August 2008: he beats Sammy Bach on Total Wrestling and, in a No Holds Barred match at Hotter Than Hell, Tommy Cornell. Sam Keith keeps it through September 2008: he draws with Chris Rockwell on Total Wrestling when the time limit runs out and, in a No Holds Barred match at Destructive Energy, beats Jeremy Stone with the Proton Lock. Sam Keith keeps it through November 2008, beating JD Morgan with the Proton Lock on Total Wrestling before Sammy Bach, who has taken Wolf Hawkins' contendership as a favour, hits him with a chair; at A Little South Of Sanity he beats Bach with the NeutronPlex. Sam Keith keeps it through December 2008: at Psycho Circus, in a No Holds Barred match, Sammy Bach wins by disqualification when Texas Pete hits him with a chair, a result the diary does not treat as a title change.
