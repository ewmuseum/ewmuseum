CWF-Pro Intercontinental Tag Team Titles

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Founded July 1997, when Destruction Inc (Larry & Donny) defeated ICP in a tournament final to become the belt's own 1st CWF-Pro Intercontinental Tag Team Champions. The Double Dragons hold it three times and Destruction Inc twice across 19 recorded reigns; one further reign ended only when Todd the Godd retired out of the Hammerman & Todd the Godd pairing. The Double Dragons close the ledger's own account in November 2006, unifying the Intercontinental Tag Team Titles into the CWF-Pro World Tag Team Titles — the ledger records the Intercontinental Tag Team Titles themselves as no longer used from that reign on.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 19 reigns in full; all 19 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/cwf-pro-intercontinental-tag-team-titles.html for the underlying primary source.
