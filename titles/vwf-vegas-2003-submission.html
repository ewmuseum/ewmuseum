VWF Submission Championship

Placard

Promotion   VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)
Active   2003–2004

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned by a submission-only qualifying tournament at Vegas Violence III; its first champion also went on to unify it briefly with the promotion's own 24 Hour Championship before losing it during that November's 12 Days Tournament.

Sidious The Clown27 January 200511 April 2005Awarded the VWF Submission Championship after the referee, guided through the finish by a bleeding Lark Fenriz, mistook Fenriz's own hand slapping a concourse counter for champion Gods Tears's tap-out during a three-way brawl that spilled from the ring through the backstage area.

Defended together with the VWF Hardcore Championship against Gods Tears and Lark Fenriz in a Scaffold Match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005); wins the overall match by outlasting both other men on the scaffold, but the broadcast states neither title changes hands. Loses the title to Roadkill Rick's own Texas Speedbump in an eight-man elimination match at The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005) after Gods Tears attacks him from behind before the bell.
