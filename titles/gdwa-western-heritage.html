GDWA Western Heritage Championship

Placard

Promotion   GDWA (1996)
Active   1996–1998
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt decided twice by Royal Rumble and defended, at the end, three times in six weeks. It was crowned at the first Summer Supercard in May 1996 when Sachie Yokoyama last-eliminated Radhi Ananda, and changed hands eleven times over the two years after: knocked away at a Fall Moonsault, vacated in October 1997 by a champion who had just won a second belt, won again out of a Rumble, and finally taken by Daisy Butterfly in the match that unified it with the Intercontinental title. The fed's own handler's page of 21 October 2000 has it live again, three days out from crowning a brand new champion on its first card out of Tacoma.

Transcribed from the fed's own Lineages pages (promotions/gdwa/archive/lineages-current.html, promotions/gdwa/archive/lineages-1997.html); the 2000 revival is on its own handler's page (promotions/gdwa/archive/handlers-page-2000-10-21.html).
