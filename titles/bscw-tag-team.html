Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Hellz Angelz won it from Da Old Timerz at Viking Hall in February 2001; Gemini held it through Detonation, and the Q's lost it to Debonair in a loser-leaves-town triple threat at Ragnarok. Whiskey Jakk and Derek Irvin became champions by surviving the Thunderdome at Hostile Intentions, and Hell On Earth took it from them at The Process of Weeding Out when Jakk left his partner to be pinned.
