Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

2Short lost it to Kletus Casady in a Cage Match at Retribution, 7 January 2002. Chris Cage won it from Casady in a Weapons Match twelve days later and defended it through the winter. Ramsus Palantiri took the title from him in a match no surviving document records, held it past Unleashed and a 30 March Steel Cage main event, then lost it back to Cage, again undocumented. Cage's second reign ended at Hardcore Harvest, 24 October 2002, when a mystery opponent, revealed as a new challenger, Spiral, appeared already holding his belt.
