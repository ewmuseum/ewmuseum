Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A championship for the bottom of the card, restricted by the federation's own rules to wrestlers built on 145 power points or fewer, and the first belt it ever awarded — Baron Von Klaw won the All-Jobber final before either of the other tournaments reached its own. It changed hands seven times, was held for one week by a man who was North American Champion at the same time, and ended the record on a Soviet technician the standings had classed a jobber all summer.

Declared in the federation's own recruitment announcement of 8 April 1991 — “the PWGA All-Jobber champion, a title restricted to wrestlers with 145 power points or less (not counting the 25 free power points for class moves)” — and filled by a six-man tournament on 22 April. The belt is called both the All-Jobber and the Junior Heavyweight title in the federation's own text, sometimes in the same issue. One reign begins inside the unheld Report #11 and is chained across that gap at month precision.
