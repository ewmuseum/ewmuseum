Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened April 1997 when Joe Sanders won a battle royal eliminating Steel to become the EWF brand's own 1st United States Champion. Sanders (6) and Steel (3) are its most frequent holders across 19 recorded reigns, nearly two-thirds of them otherwise bare ledger names. Jackson closes the ledger's own account, defeating Sanders to unify the EWF United States Title with the CWF-Pro Intercontinental Title.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 19 reigns in full; all 19 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/ewf-united-states-title.html for the underlying primary source.
