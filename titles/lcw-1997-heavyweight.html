The LCW Heavyweight Title changed hands across 29 recorded reigns, from a 14 February 1997 inaugural tournament — won by Tower — through Johnny Hardcore's own 14 May 2000 tournament win over a vacant title, its own final entry in the surviving record; HICAL alone held it four separate times.
