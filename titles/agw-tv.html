AGW TV Title

Placard

Promotion   AGW (1997)
Active   1997–1999

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AGW's television championship, first won 7 October 1997 and defended through 55 recorded reigns before the ledger's own last word, Jason Star's reign beginning 9 January 2000, running into the fed's own hiatus. Won by Atlas on the fed's own earliest surviving results card, 15 September 1998 -- the one AGW title change this wing holds a served results document for.
