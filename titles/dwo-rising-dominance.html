Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt for the fed's own lower divisions, filled by bracket: the second set of brackets to name the last two competitors ran on Travesty of 11 April 2003. Carys held it that May. It is the one DWO title whose end the record actually names, and the naming comes from outside — a Lights Out Wrestling bio card of July 2004 lists its wrestler's other-federation honours and calls him the DWO Rising Dominance Champion, last ever.

The last-ever claim is Angelus Sorrow's own LOW bio card (promotions/low/archive/angelus-sorrow-bio-card.html), not a DWO document; the DWO record itself never records the reign.
