Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's top belt, first won by Celeste LaRue at GoldRush on 16 June 2007 and passed through nine reigns in ten months, among them a tournament crowning Hallow Wicked on 12 November 2007; the champions page ends 'Championship Vacant due to BLW Closer'.

Also called 'the Universal Championship' in the eXposed of November 2007 and a World title on the champions page.
