Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own tag title, already active when the held documents begin. Defended once on-panel (Shwag over With Authority, Spring Slams) before changing hands twice on-panel across the record (the Best of the Best over Team Elite, Brawl For It All; the Goths over the Best of the Best, Danger Zone '98) and once more at the one-year Anniversary Special's own eight-team tournament (Teen Angst over the Goths and the Best of the Best in turn). Two further changes (the Lethal Leaders to Shwag; Shwag's retirement handing the belts to Team Elite) are attested only by the fed's own title-history sheet, off-panel.
