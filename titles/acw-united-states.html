Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

One of the two belts ACW's own in-fiction history says was made by splitting the Spirit of ACW in half, and one of the two it retired to put that belt back together. ICU was its first champion of the television age, in September 2002, and it changed hands twice at Relentless that October. Dante Inferno won it inside a steel cage at Glory in July 2003; God's Forgotten Son won it at King of Ages in August 2004 and defended it at breakOUT; Kelly Flawless took it in a battle royal on Courage 71 and lost it to Alias on Courage 81. At Legends II on 26 January 2006 the fed announced from the stage that the title was being retired and that Alias — “the Original Pulp Hero,” whose own card claims a 1997 reign under a different ring name — would be its last champion. It came back in 2010 as the All-Star Academy's top prize and ended with the Academy that December.
