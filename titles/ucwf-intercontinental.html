Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Scorpion, of the stable American Bad Boyz, opens the surviving record's own account of this title by taking it from Crash Paradise at Immortality 2002 and defending it at Fury 2002-09-08 — Johnny Johnson steals the belt off him afterward with a kendo stick, an angle beat the record never turns into an actual title change. The lineage that follows shows three more unrecorded turnovers before landing on Notorious, then “QBz Finest” Blaze, who takes it from him at Wrestle Insanity, then “The Reality” A.J. Simmons, who loses it to Rich Bitch at Resurrection — the last champion the surviving record shows.
