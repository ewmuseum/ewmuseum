Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own title history lists 19 reigns of the PWX Championship, from Michael Norcia on 30 September 2005 to Brian Hollywood's second, read as 25 January 2010. The title was vacated when PWX announced its closure in December 2007 and was next decided on 18 October 2009, at Never Say Die 2009, when Ness won it from Jackson.

The page calls it the one title every wrestler strives for, PWX's equivalent of a world title though PWX does not recognize it as a world championship.
