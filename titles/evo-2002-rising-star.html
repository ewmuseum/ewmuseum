Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

eVo's Rising Star Championship was first won by Asis on 14 August 2002. The fed's own record carries 3 reigns on it, across 3 different champions. The longest is Sean Mason's, 52 days from 24 November 2002. Two of its reigns ended outside a match — retired with the belt or stripped from the holder. eVo unified it with the High Impact Championship into the eVolved Championship on 15 January 2003.

Asis14 August 200225 September 2002
Asis kicked Shodan in the head and pinned him with the Asisnation to win the first Rising Star Championship in a triple threat with Brad Kelley. A defence is on record. Digital Destruction 2002, 18 September 2002: Asis retained the Rising Star Championship by pinning Jason Jameson with the Asisnation after Eric Badger attacked Jameson and left.
Matt Boone25 September 200216 October 2002
Matt Boone locked a cobra clutch on Asis, who had been kicked unconscious by a masked man in black, and the referee called for the bell, giving Boone the Rising Star Championship. A defence is on record. Legends XI, 9 October 2002: Matt Boone pinned Sean Mason with the Natural Causes after Cori Simmons threw Mason throat first onto the middle rope, to keep the Rising Star Championship; Tyler Davidson then reversed the decision to Mason but left the belt with Boone. Daniel S. strips Matt Boone of the Rising Star Championship and fires him and Cori Simmons for attacking Tyler Davidson, and announces an eight man tournament for the vacant title.
Sean Mason24 November 200215 January 2003
Sean Mason made Seara tap out to the Enigma in the tournament final to win the vacant Rising Star Championship. Defences on record. Legends XVII, 4 December 2002: Sean Mason kept the Rising Star Championship over Eric Knight when Morpheus Omega and Thug roC stormed the ring and the referee ruled it a disqualification win for Mason. Nightmare Before Christmas 2002, 22 December 2002: Sean Mason retained the Rising Star Championship over Morpheus Omega with the Enigma on the knocked-out Omega, the referee dropping his arm three times. The Rising Star Championship is retired into the new eVolved Championship when Seara beats Sean Mason in the unification match.
