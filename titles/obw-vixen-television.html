Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Debuted 28 February 2012 at the federation's own Big Shot pay-per-view, where Kitten beat three others in a sudden-death four-way to become the first champion. Its own article and its own ledger disagree about the belt's size — the prose says six reigns shared between five wrestlers, the table lists ten across nine — and both are recorded here rather than resolved. Leda's 82 days is the longest reign on it; Mahlia won hers in what the ledger calls the first ever Vixens Lockdown.
