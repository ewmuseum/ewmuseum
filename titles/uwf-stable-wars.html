The GIWF's own annual stable tournament, continued under the UWF banner starting with a fourth edition: Generic Faces of Doom in 2002, WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 in 2003, and Mike Montgomery's own Player's Club at a sixth edition run from Madison Square Garden in 2004.
