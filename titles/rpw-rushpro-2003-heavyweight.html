RPW's World Heavyweight Title was first won by Gideon Poe on 11 January 2004. The fed's own record carries 3 reigns on it, across 3 different champions. The longest is Gideon Poe's, 98 days from 11 January 2004. Karina Wolfenden held it at the end of the surviving record.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Gideon Poe11 January 200418 April 2004
Gideon Poe hit El Diablo with the Cadaver Theory and pinned him to become the first RPW World Heavyweight Champion at Genesis, a show whose write-up has not been loaded. Defences on record. Adrenaline 10, 25 January 2004: Gideon Poe hit Peter Maxwell with the Cadaver Theory, then flew off the turnbuckle with the Suicide Anthem and pinned him, with Jason O'Neil counting as special guest referee, to retain the World Heavyweight Championship. Adrenaline 18, 28 March 2004: Gideon Poe kicked Nascosto Nerezza in the throat and covered him without hooking the leg to retain the World Heavyweight Championship. Adrenaline 20, 11 April 2004: Gideon Poe dropped to his knees with Christian Alexander Kannon over his shoulder for a backdrop piledriver and pinned him to retain the World Heavyweight Championship in a triple threat with Jason O'Neil.

Sources disagree — Argyle's booking record dates Adrenaline 23 as 25 April 2004; the show's own page dates it 9 May 2004 from the fed's own news posts, and this history follows the page. Invincible Gabe won the World Heavyweight Championship in a six way when Christian Alexander Kannon restarted the match as sudden death, because Gideon Poe had tapped out to Magnum Wolf's Triangle Hold while Gabe's pin on Violence Jack was being counted, and Gabe hit Wolf with the Apocalyptic Chartbusting Last Dancing Stone Gabe Stunner and pinned him. A defence is on record. Adrenaline 23, 9 May 2004: Invincible Gabe retained the World Heavyweight Championship by count out after Gideon Poe left the ring to save Quinton Dufresne from Peter Maxwell's chair and was still on the ramp at the count of ten.

The date window rests on the first account, and both are recorded. Argyle's booking record dates Adrenaline 30 as 4 July 2004; the show's own page dates it 11 July 2004 from the fed's own news posts, and this history follows the page. Argyle's booking record dates Adrenaline 32 as 18 July 2004; the show's own page dates it 25 July 2004 from the fed's own news posts, and this history follows the page. Karina Wolfenden took the World Heavyweight Championship from Invincible Gabe at The Kannon Effect, a pay-per-view whose card is not held; the write-up of the next show has Gabe say he lay down for her. Defences on record. Adrenaline 30, 11 July 2004: Magnum Wolf swatted Tessa Windsor off the top rope with a steel chair and the bell rang, so the match ended with no decision and Karina Wolfenden kept the World Heavyweight Championship. Adrenaline 32, 25 July 2004: Karina Wolfenden hit Brian Arson with a Pump Dropkick and caught him in a Small Package for the three after Christian Alexander Kannon had a replay show Magnum Wolf knocking her foot off the rope and the match continued, so she retained the World Heavyweight Championship.
