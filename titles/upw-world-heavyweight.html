Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

UPW's own World Heavyweight Championship continued an older title lineage from a predecessor league the corpus names only as "TTW," whose reign ledger runs back to Slamfest I in May 2011 before recording that TTW itself "closed down" 30 November 2015. The belt sat dark through UPW's own founding until a Fatal Four-Way on the promotion's fifth Showdown crowned Aaron "All Day" Lewis its first UPW-era champion; Derek Rage took it from him at Survival, UPW's own first pay-per-view, and held it through the rest of the promotion's own life.

The served ledger (world-heavyweight-title-history.html) also carries an unbroken TTW-era reign chain back to May 2011; those reigns are not individually registered — their holders wrestled for a promotion this museum does not hold (a predecessor never expanded beyond the initials "TTW" anywhere in the held record), and no UPW tenure could honestly be dated that early.
