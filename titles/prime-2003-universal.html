Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PRIME's Universal Title was first won by Black Angel on 26 September 2004. The fed's own record carries 22 reigns on it, across 17 different champions. The longest is Jason Snow's, 401 days from 13 March 2009. One of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. Hessian held it at the end of the surviving record.
