Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PCW's Extreme Championship was first won by Scott Naket on 6 October 2000. The fed's own record carries 72 reigns on it, across 54 different champions. The longest is Skitzo's, 65 days from 14 February 2003. Shaun Styles held it at the end of the surviving record.
