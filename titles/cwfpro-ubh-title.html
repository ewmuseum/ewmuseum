Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

UBH's own title, founded February 1996 — the single earliest-dated document anywhere in the held record, five months ahead of CWF-Pro's own April 1997 founding. Jenny Love awarded the belt outright to the Icon Miguel Sanchez to make him its 1st UBH Champion; Mercury held it as UBH Champion in between, per his own served bio; Sanchez reclaimed it and, that April, folded it into the newly-founded CWF-Pro Title by winning a battle royal over Gator, unifying UBH's own lineage into CWF-Pro's flagship belt the same night both titles' fates were decided.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 3 reigns in full; all 3 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/ubh-title.html for the underlying primary source.
