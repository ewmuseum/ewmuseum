Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Won first by Mark Massacre in Ames, Iowa, and changed hands nine times — once, in Nashville, the same night twice over, when Al Magz handed it to Freak and won it back before the night was through. Al Magz beat Sumo for it in a hardcore brawl at ResurreXtion; Stryder won the vacant belt from Ninja at Desperation on a Raven Storm run-in. Rage was its last champion the ledger records; the belt was later folded into the InterUnited Championship, though the ledger does not show how it got there from him.

The XCWF InterUnited Championship's own title-history page names this belt as one of the two it unified — but its own last recorded champion, Rage (won 26 May 2002), is not the InterUnited belt's own first champion, Menace, and the ledger records no reign between them. The unification fact stands; how this belt's own lineage actually reached it does not.
