The champions page lists a Grand Prix belt crowned at GoldRush on 16 June 2007 under the Prime Time heading; the entries from 16 December 2007 name the Prime Time title without a prefix, and the last holder was Dave Johnson on 6 April 2008.

The Grand Prix and the Prime Time entries share one list on the champions page and are filed as one belt.
