CWF-Pro Ultra Extreme Hardcore Title

Placard

Promotion   CWF-Pro (1997)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened November 1998 as the CWF-Pro Extreme Hardcore Title (renamed CWF-Pro Ultra Extreme Hardcore Title in December 2006), when Ticking Time Bomb won a barbwire battle royal eliminating Mike Hart to become its 1st CWF-Pro Extreme Hardcore Champion. Hardcore Pimp towers over its own 220 recorded reigns, holding the belt 45 separate times against R@ven and Buffo's 10 apiece, and its late-life stretch settles into a running Hardcore Pimp/Beer King trade the ledger records dozens of times over with no method given beyond the name change. Like the fed's other flagship belts it absorbed a long run of rival promotions' own hardcore titles one by one — the IWWA, IWF, TDW No Limits, KWF, PWE, XPWA, Phoenix Promotion, BMWF, Fantasy Brawl, EIWZ and UEH Barbwire Titles among them — and the fed's own printed numbering repeats several positions outright (78 four times over, 79 and 94 twice each) rather than being silently corrected here. The ledger's own last recorded line names Hardcore Pimp as champion, with no closing note; 30 of the 220 recorded champions survive only as bare ledger names.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 220 reigns in full; all 220 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/cwf-pro-ultra-extreme-hardcore-title.html for the underlying primary source.
