The fed's own served title-history page runs a dated lineage from SmirtDogg's Battle Royal win on 15 January 1998 to Bret Hart's win on 13 October 1999, the belt briefly outliving the fed's own "closed" ledger note (recorded vacant from 20 April 1999) via a late-1999 revival chain; the same page's own notes record the title briefly renamed "North American" after a unification with a Canadian belt (note 8) and renamed back to United States when all EWA titles were declared vacant (note 15). Ten of the belt's own reigns are reified here, chaining the founding and closing anchors through Erik Draven's own four reigns, SmirtDogg's second, KG's, Stevie Richards's, Kanyon's, and "Cold" Harding Cash's own reigns; the remaining stretches (Prodigy, The Golden Child, Dallas Page, Raven, Justin Hicks, The Wyldsyde, Vic Deakins, and others' own reigns) still ride the title's own served page, pending those wrestlers' own future minting.
