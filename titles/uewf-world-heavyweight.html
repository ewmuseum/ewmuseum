Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The federation's own top belt, defended every week against the highest-ranked contender who had not already had a shot that month. The Ultimate Lanny held it when the federation went dark in the spring of 1992, and was still recognised as champion when it reopened that May; no match for the start or the end of that reign survives. Carried back from the WeWF wing, whose own newsdesk This Week in Wrestling printed this federation's weekly title results and full rankings through the winter of 1992. The belt's own winter is now dated end to end. Lord Plunder is champion in the newsdesk's own standings of 30 January 1992, at 11-2 — and the same issue's rumour mill says why: “The recent ‘administrative errors’ in the UeWF which allowed Lord Plunder to obtain the World Championship were a direct result of the bribing of the UeWFs chief officer - Harvey Wallbanger.” Mr. B is champion by 15 February at 11-2-1; he hands Admiral Ax the first loss of his career to retain it on 19 February; and on 25 February the Ultimate Lanny beats him with a superplex, called move for move — “ONE! TWO!!.........THREEEEE!!!!!! We've got a new World Champion!!!!!” Three reigns are registered here on that record.

Declared in the federation's own founding document, 19 November 1991 (promotions/uewf/archive/new-pbem-the-uewf-1991-11-19.html), which sets out how it was to be defended. Holder transcribed from the federation's own relaunch call, 14 May 1992. The federation's own inaugural World Championship tournament ran two rounds on 27 December 1991 and both semi-finals on the 28th, and its final — Mr. B against Lucifer — was advertised for a fifth night that no held document carries. The Ultimate Lanny went out of it in the FIRST ROUND, beaten by Lucifer. Whoever won that final, this reign did not begin at the Summit, and the record still does not say when it did. The weekly record survives second-hand, in another federation's own newsdesk, not yet served here. Nine editions of the WeWF's own This Week in Wrestling and its TTTTTT tag tournament, 30 January to 31 March 1992, plus the EMWA's own Kings Of The Ring of 25 March, are held in this project's own Google Groups dumps and are unserved. Between them they carry this federation's own weekly title results week by week, its full ranking tables with win-loss records and points, and two of its own matches staged play-by-play. What they say about THIS belt: the standings table of 15 February 1992 has Mr. B as World Champion at 11-2-1; the roundup of 19 February has Mr. B beating Admiral Ax to retain it, handing Ax the first loss of his career; and the broadcast of 25 February stages the title change itself, play-by-play — The Ultimate Lanny beating Mr. B with a superplex, "We've got a new World Champion!!!!!" — and prints the new table, "World Champion: The Ultimate Lanny (15-3-1) 89". The reign the May 1992 relaunch call recognises therefore begins in the last week of February 1992, and at least two earlier reigns exist that this ledger does not carry: Mr. B's, and whatever Lord Plunder held when the newsdesk's own rumour mill of 30 January 1992 reported "the recent 'administrative errors' in the UeWF which allowed Lord Plunder to obtain the World Championship."
