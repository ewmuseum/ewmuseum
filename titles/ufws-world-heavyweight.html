Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Filled on the federation's opening night, 22 April 1991, by a twelve-man tournament whose final also decided the North American belt: the winner took the world title, the loser the second. Sargent Sledge Hammer won it and held it for seven weeks, and the record's last word is that he holds it still. Between those two facts it changed hands five times in ten weeks, once when a whole stable beat the champion unconscious behind the referee's back.

Declared in the federation's own recruitment announcement of 8 April 1991 — “The PWGA World Champion, who will defend against the No.1 Contender each week” — and filled by tournament on 22 April (promotions/ufws/archive/pwga-by-e-mail-1991-04-08.html, promotions/ufws/archive/pwgea-report-1-1991-04-22.html). One reign begins inside the unheld Report #11 and is chained across that gap at month precision.
