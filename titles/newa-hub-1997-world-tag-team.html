Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A hub-wide tag-team championship of the National eWrestling Alliance, claiming descent from the real NWA/WCW World Tag Team Championship before diverging into the alliance's own invented continuity from March 1998. Eleven reigns of that continuity survive in this collection's own record -- "The Crazy Ones" the title's single most frequent titleholder, across four separate reigns -- the last still held as of the hub's own latest surviving document.

Source: the hub's own title-history page. The page's own table opens with a claimed real-world lineage borrowed from the real NWA/WCW World Tag Team Championship (Terry Gordy & Steve Williams, 12 Jul 1992, through Pat Anderson & C.W. Anderson, 14 Sep 1996-"1997," vacated "due to inactivity") -- consistent with this hub's own convention, seen on the flagship World Heavyweight title (held under the wccw wing), of claiming a real historical lineage before diverging into the fed's own invented continuity. That claimed real lineage is described here, not structured reign-by-reign; only the fed's own post-divergence reigns (Fear & Adam Riggs, 24 Mar 1998, onward) are structured in the registry's own reign record, the same discipline the wccw wing's own flagship title applies. Eleven reigns are structured, the record ending mid-chain (a fourth reign for "The Crazy Ones," attested only by the hub's own Rankings page, 4 March 2000 -- not itself numbered by the title's own page, whose own table ends at a third, bracketed reign).
