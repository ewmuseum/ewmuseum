Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own title history lists 24 reigns of the Hybrid Championship, from John Pariah on 2 September 2005 to Terrell Odom, who won it on 7 December 2009 and was stripped of it for injury on 18 January 2010. The page says it can be contested under any rules and by wrestlers of any style.

The page calls it the fed's most unique title: contested under any rules and fought for by wrestlers of any style, hence "Hybrid" wrestling.
