Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A second singles championship the fed introduced at Supercrash I in February 2008, decided in a double-elimination tournament that Teresa Quaranta won over Rook Black. Steve Knox took it that August and Rook Black got it back at Supercrash II, holding it 923 days. Supercrash III in San Antonio put three reigns on it in one night: Thirteen won it and was unmasked on the spot as High Flyer, Doc Curiosity took it from him, and Rook Black took it back. The ledger closes on that second Rook Black reign with a note that the belt became the Triple Crown Championship, merged with the Pure and Elite Championships, after he beat Deacon and Stalker at Reloaded 11 in April 2012.

Transcribed from the fed's own championship ledger (promotions/nfw/archive/a-guide-to-nfw-championship-history.html).
