Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own champions page lists 4 reigns of the LoC Elite Championship, from Randy Orton (21 April 2007) to Tyler Black. Its page says the title was vacated or deactivated on 24 June 2007 when the league shut down, and that at the restart of 6 December 2008 Ricky Steamboat gave previous champions their titles back as if the reigns had never ended.

The fed's own belt as its champions page lists it, never a real company's belt.
