GDWA Ironwoman Square Dance Tournament

aka Ironwoman Crowning

Placard

Promotion   GDWA (1996)
Active   1996–1997
Cadence   contested annually

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Not a belt but a crowning, contested once a year at Fall Moonsault. Daisy Butterfly took the first at the Carolinas Stadium in September 1996; Andrea Chandler took the second at the Sky Dome in Toronto in November 1997, and the fed named her Wrestler of the Month for it. The pay-per-view calendar bills Ironwoman Square Dance Matches as a headline attraction of the show.
