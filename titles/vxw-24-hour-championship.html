VXW 24 Hour Championship

Placard

Promotion   VXW (2001)
Active   2006

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Held by Thunder Valenkov, defended successfully against Robbie Caldwell and, in a match that ended The Maquilladora's own career, at Vegas Violence V, then again against Adam 'The Hype' Jennings on New Years Eve Of Destruction, before losing it to Jennings at Ghost Town Blues inside a Russian Cage Match; Jennings retained the title in a rematch against Valenkov at St Patricks Day Massacre.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Adam "The Hype" Jennings27 February 2006—Pinfall (the Hyperdrive, a reversal of Thunder Valenkov's own attempted Thunder Bomb) inside a Russian Cage Match.
Jennings's own first VXW Twenty-Four Hour Championship win, after a New Years Eve Of Destruction title match loss to Valenkov decided by Hot Property's own ringside interference. Retained the title in a rematch against Valenkov at St Patricks Day Massacre, pinning him with the Hyper Drive after the returning "Outlaw" Morris Bass's own interference backfired. Suspended and reported to have left for an outside opportunity sometime before Sin City Fights August 31; the title was declared vacant and contested across a mid-year tournament culminating at Vegas Violence 2006.
Thunder Valenkov5 October 2006—Pinfall, a Thunder Bomb, for the title left vacant by Adam Jennings's own suspension, at the end of a mid-year tournament (a Second Chance Battle Royal and four quarterfinals narrowing to a Final Four, then a Vegas Violence final).
Valenkov's second documented VXW 24 Hour Championship win (this broadcast introduces him as 'the three time former 24 Hour Champion,' a claim the held record's documented history does not fully account for). Omega's own manager Richard Masterson secretly conspired with Valenkov's manager Chris Wheeling, saying something to Omega mid-match that left him open to the Thunder Bomb; Valenkov refused Wheeling's demand to attack the beaten Omega afterward. Manager Chris Wheeling wore the belt himself in a mock celebration and continued to lead Valenkov to the ring wearing it through Sin City Fights October 16 and October 24, without a title defense on either broadcast. Still without a defense, Wheeling gave Valenkov a night off from wearing the belt to the ring on Sin City Fights November 18 in order to wrestle a non-title match himself in Valenkov's place, then had Valenkov stand ringside without it again on Sin City Fights November 27. Contested again in a three-way match against Television Champion Cobra and challenger Miles McKinley at New Year's Eve of Destruction 2006, under special rules barring Valenkov from losing the title to McKinley; the match ended without a decision when the referee called for the bell amid a mass brawl, Valenkov still champion.
Thunder Valenkovunknown—Unknown — the record opens with him already champion.
Defended successfully against Robbie Caldwell at Birthday Bash 2005 and against The Maquilladora on Prelude To Violence I, then again against The Maquilladora at Vegas Violence V in a match that ended the challenger's own career. Defended again on New Years Eve Of Destruction against Adam 'The Hype' Jennings, Hot Property's own ringside interference deciding the finish after the match's own first referee was knocked out. Lost the title to Adam Jennings on Ghost Town Blues inside a Russian Cage Match, Jennings pinning him with the Hyperdrive after reversing Valenkov's own Thunder Bomb.
