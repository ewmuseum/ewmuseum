Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Founded March 1997 as the UBH Royal Title, absorbed into CWF-Pro that April, when Beastman won a battle royal eliminating R@ven to become its 1st UBH Royal Champion; the fed's own ledger restarts its own numbering at “1” from that point, a fresh count for the CWF-Pro-branded belt though the title runs continuous. Hardcore Pimp (5) and Extreme Adam-X (4) are its most frequent CWF-Pro-era holders across 83 recorded reigns — 19 of them bare ledger names — which fold in a long run of rival promotions' own belts, from the EWF Television Title to the SCW Cruiserweight Title. The Golden One closes the ledger's own account in December 2007, unifying the Royal Title together with the Canadian Title into the Intercontinental Title in a 3-way dance.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 83 reigns in full; all 83 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/cwf-pro-royal-title.html for the underlying primary source.
