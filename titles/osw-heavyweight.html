Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Sebastian "Switchblade" Toomes won OSW's own first Heavyweight Championship in March 2002, at the fed's debut PPV, Crowning Glory. Doug Troy won it from him on Slam 16, 22 May 2002 -- and was immediately Polish Powerbombed through a table by Tom Walczak in the title's own aftermath. Howard Porter held the belt by a September 2002 champions snapshot; a later ledger (per the mining digest) records him as a corrupt Commissioner who won the title through office rather than the ring.
