Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own served Title History page runs one of the richest dated chains in this collection's own holdings, fully chained here across fifty-nine recorded reigns from Tundra (EMT)'s tournament win at "Chaos" (30 March 1999) through Aura's win over Jamie Havoc at "Thriller" (14 November 2002), the corpus's own last-attested change. Sirus Moran alone holds it five separate times, and Showtime five more; the belt is vacated and re-tournamented on several occasions, including once when its own champion (Ashram) "leaves for personal reasons," and splits into a two-holder belt three times running in the summer of 2002 before Fallen Angel unifies it. A handful of holders survive only as bare names on the fed's own page (King Agony, Kevin Cool, Jamie Havoc) with nothing else in the held record to build a fuller character record from.
