Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Vacant since TTW's own closure, the Television Championship was filled by Felix Sandoval in a ladder match on Showdown; he held it through a controversial time-limit-draw defense against Brandon Franklin before losing it to Franklin at Survival, and Franklin lost it in turn to Windham Brody on UPW's own last served Showdown.

The served ledger (television-title-history.html) also carries an unbroken TTW-era reign chain back to Showdown, May 2011; those reigns are not individually registered, for the same reason given on the World Heavyweight Championship's own row.
