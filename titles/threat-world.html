Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

thReat's top championship, held across both of its runs. Xaxon won it at ReLoad 2002 from Disciple X and held it until the company closed on 23 June 2002, when the belt was retired. When thReat reopened, General David Sanders beat Xaxon for it at Payback 2003, and was still champion when the title history ends.

thReat's title history (threat.theewe.net/cgi-bin/superstars.cgi?titlehistory), served at /promotions/threat/archive/threat-title-history.html.

Defences in the text: Sunday Night Flaw 24 (Phil Atken won by disqualification and Sanders kept the title), Flaw 25 (Carson Nash), Flaw 26 (haTE), Double Or Nothing 2003 (The Harbinger Of Revenge) and House Show 02 (Vincent Pembridge, a draw).
