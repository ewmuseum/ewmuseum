4WW East Wind Tag Team Championship

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

4 Winds Wrestling's tag titles, crowned at Maelstrom II: Eruption in December 2003 at the end of a gauntlet and a tournament final. The division they were built for kept emptying: in February 2005 the fed put the belts to a members' poll asking whether to keep them as tag titles, suspend them, or turn them into a ladies', pure-wrestling or cruiserweight championship, and its own owner argued to keep them on the ground that killing the tag division would cost 4WW its place in Primetime Central's.

The poll and the argument are in the fed's own East Wind Title Situation thread (promotions/4ww/archive/forum-east-wind-title-situation.html).
