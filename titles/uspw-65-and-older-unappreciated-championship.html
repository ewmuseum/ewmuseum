Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt the card for Blaze of Glory bills in September 2007 as the "USPW Unappreciated Championship", fought for in a four-way between Bulldozer Brandon Smith, Brett Hall, "The Trademark" Thomas Morgan and Insane Machine, the lower-card wrestlers the narrator says the crowd could not care less about. Bulldozer Brandon Smith wins the match in the first half of the show, and the diary names no earlier holder. Bulldozer Brandon Smith draws with Brett Hall by double disqualification in September 2007 and beats Brendan Idol in October; Hall is set to challenge him at A Haunting in America. Bulldozer Brandon Smith keeps the belt against Brendan Idol and Insane Machine and against Brett Hall in a street fight at A Haunting in America in October 2007; Idol earns a Pilgrimage challenge by beating Hall on the Monday of Week 3. Bulldozer Brandon Smith keeps the belt against Brendan Idol at Pilgrimage in November 2007 with a Backdrop Driver; he is champion at the end of the range. Bulldozer Brandon Smith beats Mean Jean Cattley in November 2007 and has his belt stolen by the Mantis in December, who is booked against him at Winter Epiphany.

Bulldozer Brandon Smith2007-09 (month)—Won in the four-way at Blaze of Glory, Saturday of Week 2, September 2007: he promises Thomas Morgan his back, drops him with a Backdrop Driver, drops Insane Machine with an Inverted Piledriver and pins Brett Hall; still champion at the end of this range. He draws with Brett Hall by double disqualification on the Monday of Week 3, September 2007, and beats Brendan Idol with a Backdrop Driver on the Monday of Week 1, October; he is still champion at the end of this range, with Hall set to challenge him at A Haunting in America. In October 2007 he keeps the belt against Brendan Idol and Insane Machine on the Monday of Week 2 and against Brett Hall in a street fight at A Haunting in America on Saturday of Week 2; he is still champion at the end of this range, with Idol, who beats Hall by disqualification on the Monday of Week 3, set to challenge him at Pilgrimage. He keeps the belt against Brendan Idol at Pilgrimage, Saturday, 22 November 2007, with a Backdrop Driver after Idol's springboard crossbody; he is still champion at the end of this range. He keeps the belt against Mean Jean Cattley on the Family Night of Thursday, Week 3, November 2007 in 7:41 with the Dozer Driver; in December 2007 the Mantis, a masked newcomer, steals the belt from his shoulder in the halls and challenges him for it at Winter Epiphany, and the Mantis's messenger The Trademark is knocked out by his chain-wrapped fist; he is still champion at the end of this range.
