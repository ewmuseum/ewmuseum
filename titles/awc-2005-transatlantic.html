Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AWC's Transatlantic Championship was first won by Alexander Strider on 6 May 2005. The fed's own record carries 10 reigns on it, across 7 different champions. The longest is Pierce Lavelle's, 140 days from 30 August 2005. One of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. Darcy Crisis held it at the end of the surviving record.

Alex Strider made Pierce Lavelle submit with Life Support in the Zero To Hero final to win the first Transatlantic Championship. A defence is on record. Pierce Lavelle hit Paddy O'Shea with Whiplash and pinned him to win back the Transatlantic Championship from The Educator in a three way. Defences on record. Fresh!, 6 September 2005: Pierce Lavelle turned James von Doom's spike tornado DDT into a camel clutch and von Doom tapped out, so Lavelle retained the Transatlantic Championship for AWC in the interpromotional match. Testimony 2005, 30 September 2005: Hate hit Pierce Lavelle with the Eleventh Commandment and Paddy O'Shea counted three, but Lavelle had kicked out and no bell rang; the cage then plunged with O'Shea and Mike Wade clinging to it and the show ended with no result. Fresh!, 18 October 2005: Pierce Lavelle climbed over the cage wall and dropped to the floor ahead of Hate to win the Escape match and retain the Transatlantic Championship. Triangles 2005, 11 November 2005: Pierce Lavelle reached the Transatlantic belt on the red triangle's roof a moment before Jack Murphy, slid under him and grabbed it to retain the Transatlantic Championship in the Triangles match. Fresh!, 13 December 2005: Pierce Lavelle flipped over Jack Murphy into a DDT and rolled into a pin to retain the Transatlantic Championship in a title for title match, where the loser's belt was to be desanctioned. Winter Warfare 2005, 23 December 2005: Pierce Lavelle countered Paddy O'Shea with Whiplash and pinned him to retain the Transatlantic Championship.
The Illustrious Face-Eater17 January 200617 February 2006
Sources disagree — The fed's own title history gives Adam Dick one Transatlantic reign, 17 January to 1 September 2006, footnoted as his crown running beside the belt; the write-ups have the belt change hands on 17 February, 24 March and 1 August, and have him throw the belt down on 4 July. This history follows the write-ups; the crown is not part of the belt's chain. Adam Dick hit Pierce Lavelle with the Shooting Star Press and pinned him, knocking Lavelle's foot off the bottom rope and using the second rope for leverage while the referee missed it, to win the Transatlantic Championship; James Brunt would not announce it.
Jack Murphy17 February 200624 March 2006
Sources disagree — The fed's own title history lists Jack Murphy from 17 February to 24 March 2006 and footnotes that Adam Dick never lost his crown, so the loss of the belt is "inconsequential"; the write-up has Murphy win the belt from Dick at Bloodlust. Jack Murphy kicked Aimz off Adam Dick in the middle of a pin, then hit Dick with the Fall From Grace and pinned him to win the Transatlantic Championship at Bloodlust; Dick's crown is not part of the finish. A defence is on record. Fresh!, 7 March 2006: Jack Murphy faked being knocked out on the third drop of the referee's hand, took Mike Wade down with a drop toe hold into the STF, and Wade tapped out, so Murphy retained the Transatlantic Championship.
The Illustrious Face-Eater24 March 20064 July 2006
Sources disagree — The fed's own title history ends Jack Murphy's reign on 24 March 2006 and lists no belt reign for Adam Dick after it (his line, the crown, runs from 17 January to 1 September 2006); the write-up has Dick win the belt from Murphy at Twilight Of The Gods. The fed's own title history has no vacancy (Adam Dick from 17 January to 1 September 2006, with Pierce Lavelle's third reign from 1 August concurrent); the write-up has Dick throw the belt down on 4 July 2006 and the belt left vacant. Adam Dick lifted Jack Murphy and dropped him on his head with Murphy's own Fall From Grace and pinned him to win the Transatlantic Championship; James Brunt refused to announce it. Defences on record. Fresh!, 9 May 2006: Adam Dick faked an injury after falling out of the ring, kicked out of Darcy Crisis's pin and hit the Eaterplex '05 to pin him and retain the Transatlantic Championship. Fresh!, 13 June 2006: Adam Dick hit Tim Martin with the Eaterplex '05 and pinned him while Ellis Nash kicked out of Liam Martin's cover, and James Brunt announced that Dick was still Transatlantic champion. Divide And Conquer 2006 (pay-per-view), 23 June 2006: Adam Dick hit Mike Wade with the Finger Poke Of Doom and covered him, and the two Unfuckables embraced after the count; Pearl then sent Michael Ryan home and ordered the match run again with Jack Murphy as referee. Divide And Conquer 2006 (pay-per-view), 23 June 2006: Mike Wade kicked out of Adam Dick's Eaterplex '05, then Dick reversed Wade's roll-up into a pin, with Jack Murphy as referee, to keep the Transatlantic Championship. Adam Dick threw the Transatlantic belt on the ground in front of Pearl and kept the crown as his own; the belt was left vacant and there was no match.

Pierce Lavelle powerbombed Ellis Nash and locked her in the submission of the Whiplash II, and the bell rang, so Lavelle won the vacant Transatlantic Championship.
Garbage Bag Johnny1 September 200624 November 2006
Garbage Bag Johnny dived off the roof of a semi truck onto Pierce Lavelle with the Dumpster Dive, both men crashed through the trailer roof, and Michael Ryan counted Lavelle out, so Johnny won the Transatlantic belt and crown as one title. A defence is on record. Untouchable 2006, 13 October 2006: Garbage Bag Johnny held Paddy O'Shea off in the Countdown Tally match, 3 falls to 2 at the 30 minute mark, to retain the Transatlantic Championship.
