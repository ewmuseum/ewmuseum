Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Named inconsistently by the fed's own hand — "International" on its dedicated title-history page and in the 25 June 2000 "Slamfest" script that crowned Gorilla its new champion, "Intercontinental" in the two 1999 pay-per-views that billed Kirk as its holder. Both readings are recorded rather than picking one. How and when Kirk's own reign ended and the title was declared vacant before Gorilla's win is likewise not in the surviving record.
