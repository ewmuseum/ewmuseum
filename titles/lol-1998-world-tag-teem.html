LOL World Tag Teem Titties

Placard

Promotion   LOL (1998)
Active   1999

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's only tag team title, per the founding FAQ (21 July 1998), which notes a third title may be added later "should we develop a big enough roster." Unclaimed in every document held before the fed's 1999 return, it was finally awarded on 3 June 1999 to the Real American Zeroes by tournament; they lost it eleven days later to a pairing of Bubbolicious Bob and Van Halent, whose own partnership then broke up, and the fed's on-screen Chancellor held the belts up rather than let them be defended by a team that could not work together. A rematch to crown new champions, announced for 28 June 1999, falls after the end of the surviving record.
