FoB Heavyweight Championship

aka FoB Championship

Placard

Promotion   FoB (2003)
Active   2003–2005
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Created by tournament in December 2003 and won by Jamie Noble over Kraven in the final. It changed hands four times inside six months and twice by vacancy — John Lennex gave it up on a call-up to Revenge, and Chaos won the five-way that replaced him — then stopped dead with Robbie Crockett's reign on 25 May 2004. The fed brought it back sixteen months later and put thirty-one men in a Rebirth match for it; Christopher Daniels won, and was still champion when the record ends.
