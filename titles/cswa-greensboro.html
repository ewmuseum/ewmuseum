CSWA Greensboro Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   CSWA (1988)
Active   1988

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A promotion naming a championship after the city it was invented in, and then defending it for thirteen years. Big Wesley Slack won the founding tournament in 1988; Mark Windham took it from him and then retired it when he won the CSWA Heavyweight Championship, and Jay Youngblood was defending it again by 1989. It goes dark for years and comes back: the fed's own newspaper records Aaron Douglas as the first Greensboro Champion since 1997 when he beat Apocalypse in the tournament final at ELVIS LIVES XI in January 1999. From there it moves fast — Randy Harders, Douglas again, Blade, Wicked Sight, Sky Suicide, Evan Aho — and the fed's own Champions page still has a holder for it in April 2001.
