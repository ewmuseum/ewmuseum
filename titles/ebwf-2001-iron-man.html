Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The European Championship's own lineage under a new name, renamed at the Destiny Pay-Per-View on 24 September 2006, the night Kane won it. Triple H took it from him on Aggression that October, and lost it at Survivor Series on 26 November 2006 without wrestling for it: the Title History records Rene Dupree beating Shawn Michaels, who was defending the title on Triple H's behalf. Dupree held it for the rest of its run, until the belt was retired on Aggression on 1 November 2007.
