Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt named for the midweek show 4 Winds Wrestling ran for one month and then dropped. Tempest the programme was cancelled in August 2003 because a midweek deadline did not fit the roster's own schedules; the name came back as a championship, and the fed's own title historian could not find dates for its first two holders.

The cancellation is announced in the fed's own Federation News of 2 August 2003; the missing early dates are stated as missing in the fed's own Official 4WW Title History thread (promotions/4ww/archive/forum-official-4ww-title-history.html).
