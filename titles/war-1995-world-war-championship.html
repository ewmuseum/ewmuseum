WAR! World WAR! Championship

Placard

Promotion   WAR! (1995)
Active   1995

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own top prize, crowned in a 16-man tournament won by Brutus Noir at the promotion's own second-ever show and changing hands at least nine times since — through Inner City Firmer, Seth Blitzspear, Noir again, Randy Blake, J.R. Death, Stein "Ice" Berg, and Tonnage — before Jason Midnight was crowned via a separate UWC interfed tournament and carried it into the fed's own Dec 1996 supercard.
