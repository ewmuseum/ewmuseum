IEWF/LWC LWC Silver City Championship

Placard

Promotion   IEWF/LWC (1997)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own post-rename regional title. Dallas "Perfection" Reed held it in 1997 and Alex Wallace, a two-time champion, lost it in the four corners main event of Last Tangle In Texarkana on 15 November 1998 to Tex Violence, who still held it in February 1999.
