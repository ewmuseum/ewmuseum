Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GWF's own top prize, worn by Wolfenstein as the fed's first Undisputed Champion in its earliest days, then by James Murphy, then Michael Toaster, then Chris O'Malley after Locked N' Loaded 2007, then SheltonSplash after Valentine's Vengeance 2008, then Don Cicinni after three Omerta superkicks in a match recovered only as a flashback. The belt's own last night is the fed's own last night: on 20 January 2013's closing show, "20113," Chris O'Malley pinned Robert Garland in a four-way lumberjack match to become GWF's final champion — the title, like the promotion around it, going dark undefeated.

"GWF Global Championship" is the name a single flashback promo gives the belt Don Cicinni won from SheltonSplash (promotions/gwf-2006/archive/rp-the-living-fairytale.html); every other surviving reference calls it the GWF Championship or GWF World Championship. Recorded as one title under the more common name rather than split, per the standing REBIRTH rule's own default-to-one-record posture, absent evidence of two distinct belts.
