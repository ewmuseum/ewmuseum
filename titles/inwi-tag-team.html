Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The founding tournament's tag final (23 March) crowned the MacDaddies as the first Heavyweight Tag Team Champions; the Big Brothers, the Terminators, the Comforters and, at year's end, the ad hoc pairing of James Pillow and Vinny Vitrone all held it before the returning Friendship Express -- disguised as 'the Masks' -- won it at Winter Storm '97.
