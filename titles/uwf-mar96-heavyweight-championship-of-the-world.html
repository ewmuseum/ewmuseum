Won the UWF Heavyweight Championship of the World from Michael Augustine in the Gold Rush 2004 main event triple threat, also involving Edmond Winston IV, capping a year-long rise from a non-wrestling authority-figure role to the sport's top title; embraced manager Tesla St. James in the ring afterward. Retained over Jamie Underwood by disqualification on 18 September 2004 after owner Edmond Winston IV struck him with a steel chair, then superplexed him from the top rope through a steel ladder propped against the ring apron; Tesla St. James was knocked out trying to fight Winston off.
