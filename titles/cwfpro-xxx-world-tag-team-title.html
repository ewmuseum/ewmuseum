Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened alongside its XXX 2nd counterpart in January 2006, when Jason Porn & Jason Sexton won a battle royal eliminating Tuff Luck to become its 1st XXX World Tag Team Champions. The Flash Brothers dominate the belt's own short life, recorded as champions in 17 of its 38 ledger reigns; the Kelly Boys (5) and Corey Matthews & Shawn Hunter (7) account for most of the rest. Beastie Boys II (Beastman & Weirdo) close the ledger's own account, defeating the Flash Brothers to unify the XXX World Tag Team Titles with the CWF-Pro World Tag Team Titles. Just over half its recorded champions — 21 of 38 — survive only as bare team names, no bio page behind them.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 38 reigns in full; all 38 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/xxx-world-tag-team-title.html for the underlying primary source.
