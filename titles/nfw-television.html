Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own working belt, twice discontinued and twice brought back. Tempesta Del Fuerte won it out of a one-night sixteen-man tournament at Impact 4 in July 2002; Steel Viper took it at Futureshock the following month and the title was retired at the end of that same night. It returned at Wrestlebowl II in May 2007 with co-champions — Shawn Jessica Hart and Joe The Plumber simultaneously won the battle royal that decided it, Hart disappeared the next night, and Joe was declared sole champion. Impulse won the vacancy in a Sears Tower match, Castor Strife gave it up to enter the World Title tournament, and Jack Bryant won the vacancy in a five-man ladder match. Its last chapter is a best-of-five series against Dorchester Stratton that the ledger tracks match by match and leaves with Stratton holding it.
