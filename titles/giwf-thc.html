Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Added January 2000 and, by the fed's own admission, “never officially recognized by the GIWF” — its shortest reign lasted roughly seven minutes (Lew Spectre's tournament win, immediately “defeated” by Ace Thunder), its longest 95 days (Gurgles), and its last GIWF holder, L.U.C.A.B.O.T., “to this day has never been able to rid himself of the title.” Restarts as the UWF's own THC, later salvaged as a physical belt by Gurgles after the UWF's own hardcore title was banned.
