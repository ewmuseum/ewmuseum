Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GCW's World Heavyweight Championship was first won by Octavian on 22 April 2001. The fed's own record carries 34 reigns on it, across 22 different champions. The longest of known length is Andy Murray's, 269 days from 21 June 2009. Three of its reigns ended outside a match (two vacated, one ended when the holder departed). Johnny Borealis held it at the end of the surviving record.
