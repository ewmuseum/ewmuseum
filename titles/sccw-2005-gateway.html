Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The SCCW Gateway Championship's first recorded champion is Jonathan Rhine. The fed's own record carries 14 reigns on it, across 14 different champions. The longest is Phillip Kennedy's, 195 days from 11 January 2009. Baron von Blackberry held it at the end of the surviving record.
