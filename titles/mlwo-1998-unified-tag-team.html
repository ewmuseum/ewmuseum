MLWO Unified Tag Team Championship

aka MLWO World Tag Team Championship, MLWO Unified Tag Team Titles

Placard

Promotion   MLWO (1996)
Active   1996–1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's tag title, billed as the World Tag Team Championship in 1996 and unified with the North American tag belts in 1997. The Mobb and Da Ghetto Blasters held it in the autumn of 1996 and the Rednecks and the Vegas Fighters in 1997; the Rednecks won it from the Vegas Fighters in a ladder match on May Madness: The Anniversary on 25 May 1997. The Good Knights defended it at Torture Chamber 2 on 26 October 1997 and lost it to the Rich and Infamous at Winter Inferno 97, whose retirement from the titles on the Saturday Slam of 28 March 1998 was a hoax. The Natural Born Annihilators, masked as Team LWC, won it in a three-way at May Madness 98 on 25 May 1998.
