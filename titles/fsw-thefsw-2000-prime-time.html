Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Mike Marvelous became the first FSW Prime Time champion at High Impact on 27 August 2000. Marc North won it from him in a three-way at Total Annihellation and vacated it at Fallen on 29 October 2000, calling it the Piece of Trash title. It changed hands often after that, through Kay Kash's win in a three-way at Defiance on 15 July 2001, and by the end of October 2001 the fed's title history marked it retired. The 2002 run put it up for grabs again at Genesis on 18 August 2002, as the Primetime Championship, and Angelo Deville won it; he kept it through every defence the surviving record holds, the last on 20 November 2002, when he also won Cassius Deamont's FSW contract.

Sources: the fed's own title history on thefsw.com, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 8 April and 31 October 2001; its 2002 Title Histories page; the show write-ups of both runs. The 2002 Title Histories page numbers Angelo Deville's reign fourteenth, after the thirteen of 2000–01, where the Genesis write-up puts the title up "for the first time".

Sources disagree — The Genesis write-up puts the title up "for the first time", and the fed's own title history of October 2001 had marked it retired, while the fed's 2002 Title Histories page numbers this reign 14th, after the thirteen of 2000 and 2001, and Mike Marvelous is billed a former Primetime Champion; the title keeps one lineage, as the fed's own title history numbers it. Glory 8, 22 August 2002: Angelo Deville made John Jupiter submit to the Soprano to keep the Primetime title. Glory 10, 4 September 2002: The masked Dead Presidents broke up Angelo Deville's match with Jesse Ramey and the referee called for the bell, and Deville left still Primetime Champion. Bad Moon Rising, 20 October 2002: Ming let Angelo Deville pin Azala Zameer and the referee called for the bell on a count the text calls a shoulder-up, and Deville kept the Primetime title. Glory 16, 30 October 2002: Magnum Wolf tapped out to the Soprano at once, and Angelo Deville kept the Primetime title. Glory 19, 20 November 2002: Angelo Deville made Cassius Deamont submit to the Soprano, keeping the Primetime title and winning Deamont's FSW contract.
