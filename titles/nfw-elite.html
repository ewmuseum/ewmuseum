Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

An interfed belt whose whole custody the record can only half account for. Steve Knox is already carrying it when NFW's own cameras first find it, at Crash TV 46 in August 2008; the show's own account of how he got it has him beating three other promotions' champions to do it, none of it staged on an NFW card. Wyatt Connors took it off him at Supercrash II two months later, in a segment the broadcast reported rather than showed. After that the belt goes quiet: by July 2010 the fed is describing it as vacated and crediting the vacancy back to Knox rather than to Connors, and running an interfed tournament to fill it whose first round is the only round that survives. It was revived on paper in January 2012 for the sole purpose of being merged into the Triple Crown Championship three months later. Where the chain breaks, it breaks in the fed's own record, not in this one.

Named by the fed's own championship ledger only in passing, in the National Championship's own closing note; the belt has no lineage of its own on that page, and what survives of its custody comes from the fed's own served show documents.
