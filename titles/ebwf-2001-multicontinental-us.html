Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Rock held it first, from 11 April 2002, and last: he took it a third time at Wrestlemania on 14 March 2004 and the Title History retires the belt with him four nights later, on the same Smackdown it opens the European Championship with him. Twenty-one reigns run between: Stone Cold Steve Austin took it three times in 2002 and 2003, Bret Hart and Christian twice each. It is one of three EBWF belts whose whole record ends before the promotion did.

Source: the promotion's own Title History page (/promotions/ebwf-2001/archive/titlehistory.html). Two stretches of this belt's own history are printed out of their own date order (August 2002 and June 2003); the rows follow the dates the page itself gives. Its August 2002 entries place two Christian reigns back to back with no loss between them, which the page does not resolve.
