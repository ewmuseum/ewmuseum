Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A championship of the fed's first run. Its first champion was crowned at High Impact on 27 August 2000: The Riddler in the show's own write-up, Aggonizer in the fed's title history. Red Hurricane won it at Total Annihellation that October, Colin Baird took it in a Hell In A Cell ladder match at Fallen, and neXt, champion from March 2001, handed the belt to Max Danger on 10 April 2001, saying he was moving on to higher things. Jack Brooze won it at Hallowed Knights on 7 October 2001, and PS Mad pinned him for it and the Extreme title together at Futile Resistance in November 2001.

Sources: the fed's own title history on thefsw.com, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 8 April and 31 October 2001; the first run's show write-ups; the Futile Resistance preview card ("Jack Brooze(c)"). The High Impact and Total Annihellation write-ups have The Riddler win the title and lose it to Red Hurricane; both captures of the title history give those two lines to Aggonizer. The 15 July 2001 row names the card Paranoia; the same page's other rows of that date, and the fed's booking, say Defiance.
