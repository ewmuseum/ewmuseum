Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt the fed built its own last spring around, and it changed hands three times in six weeks without a single one of those changes happening in a GW ring. Maverick Reno lost it because Salaok fired him, and the co-owner handed it to Silent Wind, who said publicly that he felt cheated of it. Silent Wind carried it out of Global Wrestling altogether on 20 April 2003 and defended it on Primetime Central's own New Era supercard against Christopher Camero, in a traditional square ring the commentary said its own wrestlers had stopped being used to; Camero beat him with a superplex DDT and was still carrying it a fortnight later, when Jason Dynasty asked him for a shot and was told he was not at that level. Salaok is billed a five-time holder of it and Ben J. Pailthorpe a two-time one; neither reign is anywhere in the surviving record.

The fed's own newsdesk of 30 March 2003 separately calls Lance Daniels "the GW Heavyweight Champion" in the same week its own news post of 28 March calls Silent Wind the new World Champion. Both readings stand; no seventh belt is recorded anywhere in the fed's own front-page championship row, which lists six.
