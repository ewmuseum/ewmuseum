AGW World Tag Team Titles

Placard

Promotion   AGW (1997)
Active   1997–2000

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AGW's flagship tag championship, first won 7 October 1997 and defended through 38 recorded reigns before the ledger's own last word, E.H.B./McKnight's reign beginning 16 January 2000, running into the fed's own hiatus. One reign (Conveyors Of Pain over The Cowboys, 8 August 1998) lasted three hours -- the shortest recorded on this wing.
