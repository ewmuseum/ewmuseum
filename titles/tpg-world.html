Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt the fed's own commentary said had returned to The Proving Grounds when Kris Adams won it at Madison Square Garden on 17 December 2005, beating JC Sinn with a frog splash thrown as a tribute. Before that the record is thin and out of order: PWE's own roster records name Chris Watts a former TPG World Champion without a date, and the chairman who had it carried to the ring that night, Mikel Sweeten, is never called champion in words. The fed billed the same belt as its Heavyweight Championship in the weeks either side.

Sources disagree on who held it going into End of an Era III: the show's own announcer crowns a NEW World Champion, and the show's own staging has Mikel Sweeten walk out with the belt over his shoulder without ever being introduced as its holder. Both readings are recorded; no reign is written for Sweeten. An unresolved claim sits between this belt and the other: FoB's own Rebirth of 20 September 2005 has its commentary announce an interfering wrestler as “THAT'S MORTIS! THE TPG CHAMPION!!” (FoB's own Rebirth of 20 September 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-rebirth-2005-09-20-9684.html)), and the fed's own record names no belt he could have been carrying. Both of this promotion's singles championships are candidates and no document names one; the claim is recorded on both rows and settled on neither. The fed's own booking blurb of 10 December 2005 calls it “the returning 'TPG Heavyweight Championship'” a week before it was won (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/standard-match-2005-12-10.html).
