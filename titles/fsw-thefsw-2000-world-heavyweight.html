Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Five Star Wrestling's top championship, in both of its runs. Steve Johnson won it first, beating Matthew Morrison in the final of a tournament at High Impact on 27 August 2000. It changed hands often over the next year and was stripped from its champion more than once; Chris Fear won it at Hallowed Knights on 7 October 2001, and Blitz pinned Fear for it at Futile Resistance in November 2001. When the fed returned in 2002 the title was vacant: a four-way for it at Genesis on 18 August 2002 was ruled a no contest, and Timo Bolamba won it at Bad Moon Rising on 20 October 2002, pinning Mike Marvelous in the final of the Dead or Alive tournament. Bolamba beat Chris Chase in his first defence on 27 November 2002, the last show the surviving record holds.

Sources: the fed's own title history on thefsw.com, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 8 April and 31 October 2001; its 2002 Title Histories page; the show write-ups of both runs; the August 2000 tournament bracket. The 2002 write-ups bill Bolamba's belt as new and call its first holder the first FSW World Champion, while the 2002 Title Histories page numbers his reign sixteenth, after those of 2000–01, and Bad Moon Rising bills Mike Marvelous a former World Heavyweight Champion. The October 2001 page dates Johnny Mayhem's first win "4/17/02"; its own order, its Intercontinental row and the April 2001 calendar put that Glory on 17 April 2001. The 2002 page dates Bad Moon Rising 21 October 2002; the show and its advertisements give Sunday 20 October.
