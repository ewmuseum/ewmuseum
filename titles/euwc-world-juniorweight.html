Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Discovered only through EUWC’s full-card event-history pages rather than any dedicated ledger: the Perfect Pugilest regained it at Ultra Brawl in August 1996, Sweet Daddy Bad Ass regained it again that October at EUWC’s first Blood Bath, Michael Foreman won it fresh at Ultra Brawl II in August 1997, and Ender won a Four Corners match for it at Blood Bath 1997. The fed’s own text names it inconsistently, sometimes "Juniorweight Championship" and sometimes "World Juniorweight Championship" — the same belt, one lineage.

Every reign recovered from the Ultra Brawl and Bloodbath/Blood Bath event-history pages (/promotions/euwc/archive/ultrabrawl9-ultrabrawl-history.html, /promotions/euwc/archive/bloodbath2007-bloodbath-history.html); no dedicated title-history ledger page survives for this belt.
