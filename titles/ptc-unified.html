PTC Unified Championship

aka PTC Unified Title

Placard

Promotion   PTC (2002)
Active   2007–2008
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt PTC made on 30 April 2007 by unifying its Global and Extreme Titles, in a match decided by judges on the roleplays. Jason Snow won it that day; Clinton Sage, Tchu and Lindsay Troy held it after him, each winning it on another federation's card. Lindsay Troy was the last champion: the title was retired in May 2008.

PTC's own title log dates the retirement to a forum post of 13 May 2008; PTCpedia gives 14 May.
