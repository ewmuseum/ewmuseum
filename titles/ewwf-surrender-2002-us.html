EWWF United States Championship

Placard

Promotion   EWWF (Surrender, 2002)
Active   2002

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Held by Smoove when the surviving record opens, changing hands on 16 April 2002 to Thomas Casey. The title cycled repeatedly across the spring and summer among Casey, Jimmy Slade, Axel Reed and Randy Love before Slade's 8 August win; it later passed undocumented to Randy Love and was ruled to Sammy Paulison by disqualification on 10 October, then passed undocumented again to Larry Spikowski, who retained against Ragnarok on 5 December.
