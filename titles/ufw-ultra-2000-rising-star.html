Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A championship for the federation's newer members, opened on The Gimp on 26 March 2000 — four months into the run — and traded fifteen more times in five months, The Gimp and Havok each holding it twice. "The Scholar" Howard Porter's win of 19 July 2000 is the last change it recorded for it; the federation's own final Champions page bills him as holding this belt and the US Title at once.
