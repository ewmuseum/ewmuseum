Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Phenomenons' own first tag title — three reigns across the fed's run, the last an 89-day hold — in a lineage that also crowned the Dragoons, the Fomorians (won in what the record calls “the Dublin pub brawl”), and, at the close, Street Science's own 89-day final reign. Restarts as the UWF's own World Tag Team Championship.
