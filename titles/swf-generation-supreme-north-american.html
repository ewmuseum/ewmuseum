Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt Rich Money holds in Big Money Incorporated when the diary opens, defended against Eric Tyler, Randy Bumfhole and Christian Faith before Steve Frehley takes it at The World is Watching and then refuses to defend it on Supreme TV, leaving Richard Eisen to announce its future. Eisen then sets up an eight-man Grand Prix for it, which Marc DuBois wins over Eric Eisen at Master of Puppets 2008, and DuBois defends it into June 2008. He keeps it in a three-way against Runaway Train and Antonio Marquez at Times of Trouble 2008, against Valiant on Supreme TV in Week 4 of June and, when Chris Morrisette wins by disqualification after DuBois hits him with the belt, in Week 1 of July 2008; the card of The Supreme Challenge 28 puts the title in a Threefold Threat against Zimmy Bumfhole and Antonio Marquez. DuBois keeps it at The Supreme Challenge 28 in a Threefold Threat against Zimmy Bumfhole and Antonio Marquez, when Darryl Devine's interference lets him throw the referee in front of Marquez's shooting star press; Welcome to the Jungle 2008 is set as another Threefold Threat, with Devine, who has complained to Richard Eisen, and Marquez, who wins the Supreme TV three-way against Zimmy Bumfhole and Sean Deeley for the place. DuBois keeps it in the Threefold Threat at Welcome to the Jungle 2008 when Darryl Devine taps to the Model Solution sleeper, beats Zimmy Bumfhole on Supreme TV in Week 4 of August and Scout of The New Wave in Week 1 of September, and is billed against Art Reed in Week 2.
