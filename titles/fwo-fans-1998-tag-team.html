Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

fWo's Tag Team Championship: the earliest reign served here begins on 26 June 1998, held by Punisher Squad (Jim Dudley & Chris Kidman Jr.). The fed's own record carries 68 reigns on it; 46 of them, across 31 different champions, are served here so far. The longest of those is Austin Davis & Boston Strangler's, 358 days from 30 December 2001. Two of them ended outside a match (vacated). The Entourage held it at the end of the surviving record.
