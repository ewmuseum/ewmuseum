MAW: 10 Simple Rules MAW Tag Team Championship

aka MAW Tag Team Titles

Placard

Promotion   MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)
Active   2007–2012

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MAW's tag belts. The Canadian Blondes hold them when the diary opens, Perez & Shaffer win them in the Beach Blast ladder match in July 2008, and Trent Shaffer carries them into the autumn with a new partner, Stevie Grayson. The Gauge Brothers take the belts from Shaffer & Grayson at The Rip Chord Invitational in January 2009, Morgan & Benning take them at All Or Nothing in February and keep them against C-V-2 and the Canadian Blondes, and C-V-2 win them at Iron in August 2009. C-V-2 keep the belts against the Internet Favourites, the Masked Patriot and Kashmir Singh, Riley McManus and Shane Nelson, and Ultimate Phoenix and Kashmir Singh; Cameron Vessey leaves for USPW in March 2010, and Casey Valentine and Jared Johnson carry the belts on, keeping them in the April Showers three-way, against Morgan & Benning at Spring Fever and against Shaffer & Grayson at Mother, May I?. The diary's own title records list the whole reign as "Valentine & Johnson", with nine defences. Casey Valentine and Jared Johnson keep the belts against the Internet Favourites, Morgan & Benning and, in a three-way, the Internet Favourites and Ricochet & Sharp, until the Canadian Blondes beat them in a three-way at Fall Of The King in September 2010 for a record third reign; the Blondes keep them in a four-team ladder match at Where It All Begins Again in October and against Brandon Smith and Joe Benning on a November tour show. The diary's own title records list the Blondes' reign as "The Canadian Blondes (3) September 2010". The Canadian Blondes keep the belts against Smith & Singh at Remember November, the Internet Favourites on 11th December, the Bailey Boys at Season's Beatings and Smith & Singh on 25th December, until Kashmir Singh and Jean Cattley, a pair thrown together when the Blondes injure Brandon Smith, beat them at New Year, Old Rivalries on 12th January 2011. Cattley and Singh keep the belts against Antonio & Raphael, the Blondes and Thrash and Firebird, until the Canadian Blondes beat them at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 and Jean Cattley loses a Loser Leaves Town match to Casey Valentine and leaves for the SWF. The diary's own title records list the reign as "Cattley & Singh January 2011 3". The Blondes keep the belts against the Young Americans, Smith & Singh with the Dark Raptors, Kid Arachnid and Amazing Fire Fly, the West Texas Peacekeepers, Smith & Singh with Ford & Speed, Leftie Wilkes and Ben Williams, The Quest and the Young Americans again, the Young Americans and Smith & Singh in a three-way on 26th September, the Young Americans again and the Internet Favourites, until Smith & Singh take the belts from them at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2011, ending the fourth reign at six months. Smith & Singh keep them against the Second Sons, in a three-way with the Dark Raptors and Ford & Speed, against Ford & Speed, Firebird and Citizen X and, at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January 2012, in a three-way with Ford & Speed and Lockdown, and then beat the Canadian Blondes, who cash in their rematch four months after losing the belts, at Great Lakes Crisis on 2nd March 2012, and keep the belts against the Second Sons at March Madness on 12th March and May The Force Be With You on 5th May. The Second Sons earn their shot by winning a four-team match at April Showers on 5th April. At High Noon on 5th June 2012 Smith & Singh keep the belts in a three-way against the Second Sons and Ford & Speed, and a week later, at Summer Showdown on 12th June, they lose them to Firebird and Black Eagle, The Dark Raptors, when Firebird pins Singh with the Phoenix Firebird Splash. The Dark Raptors keep them against the West Texas Peacekeepers at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July, the XX Factor at Summernova on 5th August and Spectrum Infinity at Iron on 12th August, and lose them at Back To School on 5th September to Team Honor, Jared Johnson and Lassana Makutsi, when Black Eagle taps out to the Carolina Crossface. The diary's own title records list The Dark Raptors as "June 2012 3" and Team Honor as "September 2012 4". The diary's own title records list their reign as "The Canadian Blondes (4) April 2011 11".

They keep the belts in a four-team ladder match against the Bailey Boys, Morgan & Benning and Antonio & Raphael at Where It All Begins Again in October 2010, with Flash Savage retrieving them, and against Brandon Smith and Joe Benning on the 11th November tour show, against Smith & Singh at Remember November in November 2010, when Flash Savage pulls down the top rope and Smith is beaten with a Canadian Crab, against the Internet Favourites on the 11th December tour show, the Bailey Boys at Season's Beatings and Smith & Singh on the 25th December tour show, and lose them to Kashmir Singh and Jean Cattley at New Year, Old Rivalries on 12th January 2011, when Singh names Cattley as his partner in place of the injured Brandon Smith.
Cattley & Singh12 January 201123 April 2011Won from the Canadian Blondes at New Year, Old Rivalries when Kashmir Singh, using the clause that a winning team would get a title match without naming Brandon Smith, brings in Jean Cattley as his partner and Cattley scores with the Mood Swing on Flash Savage. They keep the belts against Antonio & Raphael at Leap Into The Unknown, the Blondes at Third Time's The Charm and Thrash and Firebird at April Showers, until the Canadian Blondes take the belts from them at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, Oscar Golden pinning Cattley with the Golden Shower; Cattley then loses a Loser Leaves Town match to Casey Valentine and leaves for the SWF.
The Canadian Blondes23 April 201126 October 2011Won from Cattley & Singh at Spring Fever, ten minutes in, when the champions' lack of cohesion lets them down once more and Oscar Golden scores the pin on Jean Cattley with the Golden Shower; the narrator notes that the Blondes have more than a hundred matches together as a team. They keep the belts against the Young Americans on 12th May, Smith & Singh and the Dark Raptors in a three-way on 26th May, Kid Arachnid and Amazing Fire Fly on 12th June, the West Texas Peacekeepers on 26th June, when they debut the O, Canada!, Smith & Singh and Ford & Speed in a three-way on 12th July, Leftie Wilkes and Ben Williams on 26th July, The Quest on 26th August and the Young Americans on the 4th September tour show, the Young Americans and Smith & Singh in a three-way at Fall Of The King on 26th September, the Young Americans on the 3rd October tour show and the Internet Favourites on 24th October, until Smith & Singh take the belts from them at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2011, Kashmir Singh catching Oscar Golden with the Cal-Cutter, and so end the reign at six months.
Smith & Singh26 October 201112 June 2012Won from the Canadian Blondes at Where It All Begins Again, ending their fourth reign at the six-month mark: Kashmir Singh plays face-in-peril for the Blondes' showboating until Flash Savage crashes and burns on a springboard senton and Brandon Smith bulls his way through the champions, and Singh catches a rising Oscar Golden with the Cal-Cutter. They keep the belts against the Second Sons at Remember November, in a three-way with the Dark Raptors and Ford & Speed at Season's Beatings, against Ford & Speed at End Of Days, against Firebird and Citizen X at New Year, Old Rivalries (Smith finally beating Citizen X) and, at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January 2012, in a three-way with Ford & Speed and Lockdown, when Singh pins Greg Ford with a Cal Cutter after Lockdown have been put out; at Great Lakes Crisis on 2nd March 2012 they beat the Canadian Blondes, who have cashed in their rematch, Smith finishing Flash Savage with a Backdrop Driver, at March Madness on 12th March they beat the Second Sons, Singh pinning Antonio with a Bombay Duck, and at May The Force Be With You on 5th May they beat the Second Sons again with Smith's Backdrop Driver on Casey Valentine; at High Noon on 5th June 2012 they keep the belts in a three-way against the Second Sons and Ford & Speed, Smith catching Casey Valentine with an Inverted Piledriver, and at Summer Showdown on 12th June they lose them to The Dark Raptors, Firebird pinning Singh with the Phoenix Firebird Splash.
The Dark Raptors12 June 20125 September 2012Won from Smith & Singh at Summer Showdown, on the night Black Eagle returns from his spinal surgery: Eagle, freshly healed, avoids and evades the champions' early attacks before bringing Firebird in, Firebird wears Singh down with precision strikes, and when Eagle ambushes Smith and takes him over the top rope with a leg lariat, Firebird gets Singh down on the mat and scores with the Phoenix Firebird Splash. They keep the belts against the West Texas Peacekeepers on 9th July 2012, the XX Factor on 5th August and Spectrum Infinity on 12th August, and lose them to Team Honor at Back To School on 5th September, when Jared Johnson's MDK Powerbomb leaves Eagle vulnerable to Lassana Makutsi's Carolina Crossface.
Team Honor5 September 2012—Won at Back To School from the champions Firebird and Black Eagle, whom they were not much fancied to beat: Jared Johnson matches the Dark Raptors hold for hold while Lassana Makutsi takes to the air as gracefully as the masked men before outbrawling them on the mat; Eagle turns to Sienna LeNoir for support and gets none, walks into Johnson's MDK Powerbomb, and Makutsi drills Firebird with a Lassana Stunner to stop his interference and locks the Carolina Crossface on Eagle, who taps out. They are still the champions at the end of this range.
