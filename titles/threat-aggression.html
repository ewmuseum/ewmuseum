Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The most travelled of thReat's belts. Riot won it from Lennox Dawson on Sunday Night Flaw 04 and handed it to Lawrence Clementia six weeks later; Rejection beat Clementia for it and held it to the company's closure on 23 June 2002, when the belt was retired. In the 2003 run Steve Solex won it at Malevolence, Wolf took it from him at Payback, and Ernie French took it from Wolf at Double Or Nothing and was still champion when the title history ends.

thReat's title history (threat.theewe.net/cgi-bin/superstars.cgi?titlehistory), served at /promotions/threat/archive/threat-title-history.html.
