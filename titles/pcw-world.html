Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PCW's World Championship was first won by Kremmen on 22 October 2000. The fed's own record carries 35 reigns on it, across 28 different champions. The longest is Skylar Green's, 265 days from 25 May 2003. Two of its reigns ended outside a match — stripped from the holder or vacated. The belt left Inphino Blitz on 23 February 2004, stripped from the holder, and the surviving record carries no later change.

This reign runs through PCW's own closure and restart, which is why it has no recorded defence between Supremacy 2003 of 25 May 2003 and Turmoil 132 of 21 Dec 2003. The fed says so in its own voice on its return: "Skylar Green is back with PCW after 6 months of the federation's hiatus, still holding the World Title. That's how he left, that's how he returned", and again — "He can't be forced to give the title back. He can't be stripped of the title. He must lose it in a match" (PCW's own results page for Turmoil 132, 21 Dec 2003). The same page carries a "NEW PCW World Champion... 'The Buzz' Cal Roberts!" line, which is Cal Roberts quoting his own prediction back at the standing champion in a backstage promo, not a crowning; the page bills Skylar Green champion throughout. Turmoil 133, 7 Jan 2004 — no contest; the fed's own log records the outcome as undetermined.

Inphino Blitz23 February 200423 February 2004
Shanghaied IV, 23 Feb 2004 — the belt's last recorded night, and three things happen to it. Cal Roberts VACATES it himself in an in-ring promo ("So as of right now I officially vacate my World Championship title!"); the fed's own executives overrule him on the same page ("Hey Buzz you can't just give up a title you moron, it takes lots of paper work. You're still the champion and your match with Blitz is for the title"); and Inphino Blitz then beats him for it — "And the winner... and NEW PCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION... THE BLACK DRAGON MASTER... INPHINOOOOOO BLITZ!!!" Minutes later Dick James and Alan Zoober take it off him in the aisle: "you, Inphino Blitz, just MIGHT get into the records books as the SHORTEST REIGNING PCW WORLD CHAMPION EVER!! Cause of right now, YOU'RE STRIPPED!!!", adding "no matter the winner... the belt was getting vacated IMMEDIATELY AFTER" (PCW's own results page for Shanghaied IV, 23 Feb 2004). The fed uses both its words in one segment — STRIPPED for the act and vacated for the result — and the reign's end is filed under the word for the act. Blitz then lays the belt down himself rather than fight for it. His reign is the shortest of the belt's recorded reigns. The fed's own title log records the outcome of the match as undetermined (its own champion-after value left blank); corrected below.
