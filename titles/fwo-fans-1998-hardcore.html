Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

fWo's Hardcore Championship: the earliest reign served here begins on 12 June 1998, held by Falcon. The fed's own record carries 86 reigns on it; 69 of them, across 41 different champions, are served here so far. The longest of those is Eli Flair's, 187 days from 27 April 2002. Three of them ended outside a match (handed over with no match, vacated).
