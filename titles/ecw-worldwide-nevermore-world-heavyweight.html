Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The top belt of the diary's ECW. Its own title history opens on Shane Douglas's second reign, won in March 1994 and running when the game world leaves real events in July 1994, and the shows of the first ten are set inside Raven's reign that ran from 23 November 2005 to 26 September 2006; Raven is the billed champion at Wrestlepalooza, where he keeps it over Goldberg. Raven keeps the belt through the pages of July, August and September 2006: he retains at Summer Assylum when Shawn Michaels interferes against Paul Burchill, and at Heatwave he sides with Charlie Pag and Paul Burchill and keeps it over Goliath with their help. Raven's reign ends at Anarchy Rulz on 26 September 2006 by the diary's title history, when Shawn Michaels superkicks him in a five-on-five brawl and pins him; Michaels loses the belt back to Raven at Guilty As Charged, in the Triangle Match with Rob Van Dam, after Stephanie Mcintosh lowblows him. At Masters Of Pain on 29 December 2006 by the diary's title history Sabu takes the belt from Raven with a Triple Jump Moonsault after Zakk Wylde punches the champion; Sabu keeps it over Cactus Jack at Got Blood? and is still champion when the pages of February 2007 end. At Living Dangerously Sabu keeps the belt over Steve Austin and Raven then hits him with an Evenflow DDT; the next night on Blood, Sweat and Beers Raven beats Sabu for it with Paul Burchill's and Goliath's help (the diary's own history prints Sabu's reign as ending, and Raven's sixth as starting, on "29 February 2007", which is not a calendar date, so the change is filed at month precision), and keeps it over Shane Douglas at Baptism Of Fire on 29 March 2007 with CM Punk's interference; Raven is still champion when the pages of April 2007 end, with Paul Burchill, who won the Baptism Of Fire match, due a title shot at Hardcore Heaven. At Cyberslam Raven keeps the belt over Rhino in the Steel Cage Match when Paul Burchill, the special referee, counts fast and calls a submission Rhino never gave, after which Burchill turns on Raven with a C-4 (on the next Blood, Sweat and Beers Burchill and Shane Douglas, the masked man, tell how the plan ran for seven months); at Hardcore Heaven, in a match that is not for the title, Raven beats Burchill after CM Punk threatens to throw Kelly Thomaselli off the stage, so that Burchill may never challenge for the belt; Raven's sixth reign continues in the diary's history and is still going when the pages of June 2007's second week end, with Owen Hart booked to challenge him at Wrestlepalooza and Bret Hart in a Triangle Match for the belt. At Wrestlepalooza in June 2007 Raven keeps the belt in the elimination Triangle Match with Bret and Owen Hart, then is Chokeslammed by the mystery signing, Mark Calaway; on Blood, Sweat and Beers Paul Heyman forces Raven into a non-title match against Vito Thomaselli, and Calaway's Chokeslam lets Vito pin the champion. At Summer Assylum (Friday, week 4, July 2007) Raven keeps the belt over Calaway when Rhino Gores Calaway; Raven is still champion when the pages of August 2007's first week end, with the history's date for the reign's end, 1 October 2007, still ahead. At Heatwave in August 2007 Vito Thomaselli beats Raven in the Barbed Wire Cage Match and is presented with the belt, but on the next Blood, Sweat and Beers Raven reads out a clause of the contract that voids any title change after outside interference, and says the belt is still his; Rhino wins the first Buck Stops Here Match at Heatwave and is booked against Raven at Anarchy Rulz, with the history's date for the reign's end, 1 October 2007, still ahead. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 Raven beats Rhino with Mark Calaway's help and is then chokeslammed by Calaway; on the first Revolution of October Owen Hart makes him tap to the Sharpshooter after Chris Jericho's chair shot and is the champion (1 October). Owen retires after Guilty As Charged, where Chris Jericho wins the Ultimate Submission Match on a ruling that his tap came after the time limit and loses the belt minutes later when Raven, using the Commissioner's right to book one match of his choosing at a pay-per-view, pins him with a Raven Effect (26 October); the company's owner then strips Raven of his powers as Commissioner and books Paul Burchill against him for November To Remember XV. In November 2007 Paul Heyman, back in power, tells Raven on Revolution that nobody in the arena is comfortable seeing him as the champion and that Paul Burchill will beat him at November To Remember; on the third Blood, Sweat and Beers he makes the match a Loser Leaves ECW Match, with anyone outside it who touches either man to be fired, and says that the man who stays will be the Undisputed ECW World Heavyweight Champion. Raven is still the champion when the diary's preview of the card ends. Raven loses the Loser Leaves ECW Match to Paul Burchill at November To Remember XV (30 November 2007 in the history) and leaves ECW; Burchill is the new champion and, on the Saturday show, defends under the Code of Diminishing Extremes' modified British rules (twelve five-minute rounds, two falls out of three); CM Punk, now the leader of Raven's Nest, loses to him in a non-title match, and Rhino, who gores Burchill twice and beats Vito Thomaselli in a number one contendership match, is booked against him at Masters of Pain.
