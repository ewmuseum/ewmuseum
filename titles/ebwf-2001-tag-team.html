Promotion   EBWF (2002)

Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Hulkamaniacs — The Undertaker and Hulk Hogan — held it first, from 15 April 2002, lost it at Backlash that month and took it back a week later. Team 3:16, The Rock and Stone Cold Steve Austin, took it three times in 2002 alone. Six of its forty-five reigns changed hands on storyline rather than in a match: four between August 2005 and March 2006, as injuries and a retirement moved half the championship between Shannon Moore, Wes Ikeda, Jeff Hardy and Scotty 2 Hotty, and two more in 2007, when Syxx and then Jeff Hardy vacated their own halves and the other holder kept his. D-Generation X's win at Death Before Dishonor on 25 May 2008 is the last change the whole Title History records, of any EBWF belt.

Source: the promotion's own Title History page (/promotions/ebwf-2001/archive/titlehistory.html). Two reigns are printed with one name on both sides (“Raven & Raven”, 14 December 2003; “Bret Hart & Bret Hart”, 25 May 2003); they are recorded as printed.
