ZWF Eurocontinental Championship

Promotion   ZWF (2002)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Chris Jericho's own second Summerslam title, defended the same night as his Light Heavyweight loss: he retained against Booker T, reversing a near-fall Bookend into a Bulldog and a Lionsault. The fed's own commentary names the belt "the Eurocontinental title"; the same broadcast separately introduces Booker T carrying "both Eurpean Title and Intercontinental Title," a wording the served text never reconciles.
