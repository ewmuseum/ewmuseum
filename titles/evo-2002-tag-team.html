eVolution Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   eVolution (eVo)
Active   2002–2003
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

eVo's Tag Team Championship was first won by The Unlimited on 24 November 2002. The fed's own record carries 16 reigns on it, across 11 different champions. The longest is The Syndicate's, 76 days from 3 July 2003. One of its reigns ended outside a match — stripped from the holder. St. Violence held it at the end of the surviving record.

Daniel S. refuses to sanction Twisted Reality or their titles for their 30-day hiatus and commissions a second set of Tag Team belts for a three-team WarGames Match Beyond at Unbreakable.

From The Ashes 2003, 25 May 2003: Twisted Reality retained the Tag Team Championship in Hell in a Cell when Sky Night covered Big Daddy Dreamer for three.
Hedges and Luther Wisdom5 June 200321 June 2003
Hedges pinned The HeartBreak Chick at the same moment Sky Night tapped out, and the referee ruled the Tag Team Championship to Hedges and Luther Wisdom, billed Malcolm Luther Xavier. A defence is on record. Legends XL, 18 June 2003: Jason Jameson beat Thug roC with Perfection, Malcolm King refereeing, and Luther Wisdom and Hedges remained eVolved and Tag Team Champions. The write-up bills Luther Wisdom as Malcolm Luther Xavier through this match; the fed's own title history names Luther Wisdom.

St. Violence won the Tag Team Championship when Hedges abandoned his partner and Eric Knight pinned Luther Wisdom with a rolling cradle after Deacon's Supper DDT.
The Syndicate3 July 200317 September 2003
The Syndicate won the Tag Team Championship when Big Daddy Dreamer powerslammed Eric Knight off a ladder through the ring and The HeartBreak Chick speared Deacon, and the referee counted three. Defences on record. Legends XLII, 9 July 2003: The Syndicate kept the Tag Team Championship when The HeartBreak Chick pinned Blackman1, in a match the header billed Non-Title. Legends XLIII, 16 July 2003: The Syndicate kept the Tag Team Championship in an Iron Man match against The BodySnatchers by having won more falls when the time limit expired. ReVelations 2003, 27 July 2003: The Syndicate retained the Tag Team Championship in a War Games match when Big Daddy Dreamer made Dolo Bentswinger tap out to a dragon sleeper. X-Rated 2003, 31 August 2003: The Syndicate retained the Tag Team Championship when Luther Wisdom, billed Xerxes Democritus, turned on his partner Jason Relms and lay down for Big Daddy Dreamer's pin. Legends XLIX, 10 September 2003: The HeartBreak Chick rolled up Scott Frost in a small package, holding his tights, after a masked Strictly Prohibited (Jason Jameson) chaired Frost, to keep the Tag Team Championship for The Syndicate.
Morpheus Omega17 September 200317 September 2003

The Industry17 September 20035 October 2003

The Industry5 October 20039 November 2003

Legends LIII, 15 October 2003: Tok'n pinned Shaft-Man and won the ten count on him to keep the Tag Team Championship for The Industry in a handicap Cell match against Thug roC and Shaft-Man.
