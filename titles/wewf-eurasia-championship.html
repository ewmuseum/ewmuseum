Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

One of three Continental belts, held in turn by the Lethal Lemming, “Irish” Mike Flint twice, Destruction, AquaDemon, BudMan and the Millionaire; the last of those won it eight days before the league downsized for the summer and never defended it.
