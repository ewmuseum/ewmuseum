Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Triple H is the earliest-attested champion, defending against Sting at "Sweet Surrender" (27 June 1999) in a steel-cage main event with President NateDogg as special guest referee — Sting dethrones him there, Scorpion Death Drop and a NateDogg fast count. By the fed's own next served broadcast, "Saturday Night Massacre" (14 August 1999), Shawn Michaels is billed as champion against Turney, an undocumented change; Turney in turn is dethroned by Canyon at "Barbed Wire" after a masked-man run-in (revealed as Chaos and Shawn Michaels) let Canyon steal the pin. Three reigns are structured here on staged evidence; the two intervening changes of hands ride the record as unstructured, attested-but-undocumented gaps.
