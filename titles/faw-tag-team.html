Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Reinstated in the summer of 2001 and held by nearly every stable FAW ran. Dark Generation took the belts and were stripped of them when both champions were injured; Skipster and The Omega Fro, Chris Thunder with three different partners, the Mass Murderers, the Foam World Order and New School all held them across 2002. They were vacant going into Diabolical Day 2003, where a triple threat gave them to the Vice Squad — who were handed the belts and put on the floor with chairs in the same minute.
