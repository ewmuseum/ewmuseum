Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned in a three-way at SeVeN — reduced to a two-way mid-match when Starter was written out by an in-story lightning strike — and won by Mr. X, who pinned Hardcore Z with his own legs on the ropes. The Redzne-2 card bills him defending it against Sean Manning in a Dumpster Match ten days later; that show's own results were never captured, so the belt's own history ends there, mid-defense, in the surviving record.

Source: the SeVeN show script, which headers the match "CWF Anarchy Championship — Anarchy Rules" and ring-announces Mr. X the winner (promotions/cwf/archive/seven-2003-02-01.html); the Redzne-2 card, which bills Mr. X "(Champ)" defending against Sean Manning in a Dumpster Match (promotions/cwf/archive/redzone-2-card-2003-03-11.html).
