Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The SCCW Universal Championship's first recorded champion is Evan 'Damage' Jackson. The fed's own record carries 21 reigns on it, across 12 different champions. The longest is Aimz's, 421 days from 2 August 2008. Two of its reigns ended outside a match — stripped from the holder or vacated. The belt left Lane Stevens on 13 June 2010, stripped from the holder, and the surviving record carries no later change.
