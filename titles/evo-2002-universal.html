eVolution Universal Championship

Placard

Promotion   eVolution (eVo)
Active   2002–2003
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

eVo's Universal Championship was first won by Sovereign on 14 August 2002. The fed's own record carries 9 reigns on it, across 8 different champions. The longest is Xander's, 98 days from 25 May 2003. James Kimson held it at the end of the surviving record.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Sovereign14 August 200231 October 2002
Sovereign unhooked the belt from the top of the ladder after Jamal Kage was left down, to win the eight-man tournament and the vacant Universal Championship, the first held in eVolution. Defences on record. Legends VI, 28 August 2002: Sovereign retained the Universal Championship over Matt Boone in a cage match, beating him with chair shots and Sovereignty before walking out through the cage door. Legends VIII, 11 September 2002: Sovereign retained the Universal Championship by pinning Troy Pierce with the Last Breath after Pierce threw Matt Boone into the guard rail. Digital Destruction 2002, 18 September 2002: Sovereign retained the Universal Championship by pinning Matt Boone with the Last Breath in a four-way match. Legends IX, 25 September 2002: Sovereign retained the Universal Championship when Alias tapped out to the Last Breath (the commentary calls the champion William here).
Sovereign and L2J31 October 200224 November 2002
Sources disagree — The fed's own title history dates this ruling to Faith, 27 October 2002, in the same line as the double knockout; the ruling is made on Legends XIII, 31 October 2002, and Faith's own write-up leaves the champion unresolved. This reign follows the write-up. Daniel S. ruled L2J and Sovereign both Universal Champion after their double knockout at Faith, to hold it until one of them loses it, when they would both lose it.
L2J24 November 200222 December 2002
L2J climbed the ladder on the cage roof with one good arm and pulled the belt down ahead of Sovereign to become sole Universal Champion, ending the co-championship.
Shocka22 December 200223 February 2003
Shocka pinned L2J with the #1 Stunna, off the ropes after a low blow and a distraction from Thug roC, to win the Universal Championship. Defences on record. New Year's eVolution 2003, 26 January 2003: Shocka retained the Universal Championship in a Last Man Standing match when Scott Frost could not answer the ten count after the burning table spot. Legends XXIV, 5 February 2003: Shocka retained the Universal Championship when Morpheus Omega, wearing a referee's shirt, ran in and counted the fall on Scott Frost, and Shocka left with the belt.
Scott Frost23 February 200319 March 2003
Scott Frost poked Shocka in the eye and pulled the Universal Championship down from above the Cell, after a powerbomb through a burning table, to win it in Hell's Fury. A defence is on record. Legends XXVIII, 12 March 2003: Scott Frost retained the Universal Championship by pinning Erin Christoph with the Frost Bite after the Frost Effect, in a no-disqualification match.
Jason Jameson19 March 200330 March 2003
Jason Jameson elbowed his way out of Scott Frost's setup for the Cold Front and pinned him with Perfection to win the Universal Championship.
Scott Frost30 March 200325 May 2003
Scott Frost pinned Jason Jameson with the Frost Effect to win the Universal Championship back, making him its first two-time holder in eVolution. A defence is on record. End of Days 2003, 27 April 2003: Scott Frost hit James Kimson with the Frost Effect, rolled onto Erin Christoph and pinned him to retain the Universal Championship in a no-disqualification triple threat.
Xander25 May 200331 August 2003
Xander pinned Scott Frost in a hardcore match to win the Universal Championship, after the ropes snapped and his foot landed on Frost. Defences on record. Legends XXXIX, 11 June 2003: Xander retained the Universal Championship by pinning Sean Mason with the Chaos Reign, after a shove into the referee and a monkey wrench shot. A New Dawn 2003, 29 June 2003: Xander retained the Universal Championship over Tyler Davidson by pinning him after a low blow and a wrench across Davidson's forehead. eVo1: One Year Anniversary Show, 3 July 2003: Xander retained the Universal Championship by pinning Seara with Chaos Reigns. The Genesis Pit 3, 5 July 2003: Xander pinned The Tone with Chaos Reigns to retain the Universal Championship in a surprise match on a house show. ReVelations 2003, 27 July 2003: Xander retained the Universal Championship over James Kimson by disqualification after Asis laid Xander out with his own wrench, which the referee counted against Kimson.
James Kimson31 August 2003—
James Kimson broke up Xander's pin on Asis, hit Xander with the monkey wrench and pinned him with History in the Making to win the Universal Championship. Defences on record. Legends L, 17 September 2003: James Kimson retained the Universal Championship by pinning Scott Frost with History in the Making after Shocka's #1 Stunna and a chair shot from Jason Jameson. Digital Destruction 2003, 5 October 2003: James Kimson retained the Universal Championship by pinning Shocka with History in the Making after a superplex, in a Falls Count Anywhere match. Legends LIV, 22 October 2003: Jason Jameson was declared the winner by count-out over James Kimson after Shocka's chair shot left Kimson down, but the Universal Championship did not change hands. Legends LVI, 5 November 2003: James Kimson pinned Machine with History in the Making in an impromptu title defence announced in the ring by Leya Forth. Faith 2003, 9 November 2003: James Kimson retained the Universal Championship by pinning Shocka with History in the Making after Leya Forth's chair shot missed Kimson and hit Shocka.
