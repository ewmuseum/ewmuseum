Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's hardcore-stipulation belt. Ronny Wonder was announced champion on 22 November 1998, the same broadcast that also announced Richard Riley as champion in what the show's own text plays as a joke rather than a clean handoff; Cyris held the belt clearly from 6 December 1998 through at least the end of that year. It was vacant again on the fed's own 3 June 1999 rankings page.
