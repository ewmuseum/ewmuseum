LCW Mercury World Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   LCW Mercury (2001)
Active   2001–2002

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Founded with a fourteen-man single-elimination tournament in the promotion's own opening weeks, closed on a bloody "I Quit" match at Mercury Rising. Harbinger held it as the promotion's flagship champion into the winter, lost it to Pariah in a double-down shootfight the referee himself had to call, and Pariah carried it into Oblivion's own opening assault at Suicide, where Gol pinned him to become the group's first champion. Geoffrey Slate took it from Gol on an uncaptured show days later — the shortest reign in the belt's own short life, by the roster's own reckoning — and was still defending it when the surviving record runs out.
