Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Anarchy Wrestling’s top singles belt, awarded vacant to The Nameless on 21 December 2005 and carried in turn by Rated R, Gerard Angelo and Tommy Purr before Halloween Jack took it from Tommy in a four-way ladder match at Wars of the World on 30 September 2006. Tommy won it back at Dead By Dawn under a guest referee, and Battle of the Warriors 2007 bills Rated R as champion; the champions page, captured in April 2007, ends with Tommy’s second reign.
