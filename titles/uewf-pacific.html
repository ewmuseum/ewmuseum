Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Atlantic Championship's opposite number, defended on the same terms. Lord Plunder held it into the 1992 hiatus. Carried back from the WeWF wing, whose own newsdesk This Week in Wrestling printed this federation's weekly title results and full rankings through the winter of 1992. T'ien “the Master” Lung holds the belt in the newsdesk's own standings of 30 January, 15 February and 19 February 1992, and defends it over the Shropshire Slasher; Lord Plunder takes it back on 25 February with an international object. The first reign on this ledger DID NOT run unbroken to May 1992, and the row that said so is corrected.

Declared in the federation's own founding document, 19 November 1991 (promotions/uewf/archive/new-pbem-the-uewf-1991-11-19.html), which sets out how it was to be defended. Holder transcribed from the federation's own relaunch call, 14 May 1992. The federation's own other World Championship semi-final, on the same night, carried the same double purpose — “The Winner will advance to face in the Championship round and the loser will be be awarded the Pacific Championship Title” — and Lord Plunder lost it to Lucifer on 28 December 1991. The reign the relaunch call recognised five months later begins there. The weekly record survives second-hand, in another federation's own newsdesk, not yet served here. Nine editions of the WeWF's own This Week in Wrestling and its TTTTTT tag tournament, 30 January to 31 March 1992, plus the EMWA's own Kings Of The Ring of 25 March, are held in this project's own Google Groups dumps and are unserved. Between them they carry this federation's own weekly title results week by week, its full ranking tables with win-loss records and points, and two of its own matches staged play-by-play. What they say about THIS belt: This belt changed hands twice inside the reign this ledger carries as one. The table of 15 February 1992 has T'ien "Master" Lung as Pacific Champion at 12-1-1, with Lord Plunder first among the contenders; the roundup of 19 February has T'ien beating the Shropshire Slasher to hold it; and the roundup of 25 February has "Lord Pluder beat Pacific Champion T'ien 'The Master' Lung by hitting him with an internation object," with the table that week reading "Pacific Champion: Lord Plunder (12-4-0)." The reign this ledger dates to 28 December 1991 therefore did NOT run unbroken to the May 1992 relaunch: T'ien Lung held the belt in between, and Lord Plunder's second reign begins in the last week of February 1992.

Four days after the relaunch call the same president wrote that all the champions were returning "with the exception of 2" — which two is not on record, so no reign here is ended on that sentence. THIS REIGN IS NOT CONTINUOUS, AND THE HELD RECORD SAYS SO. The sentence above has this reign running from the Summit to at least 14 May 1992. It did not. The WeWF's own broadcasts of 15 and 19 February 1992 — held, unserved — have T'ien "The Master" Lung as this federation's Pacific Champion, at 12-1-1 and defending successfully against the Shropshire Slasher, with this holder first among the contenders; the broadcast of 25 February then has this holder win the belt back, "Lord Pluder beat Pacific Champion T'ien 'The Master' Lung by hitting him with an internation object," and prints "Pacific Champion: Lord Plunder (12-4-0)". So the reign dated here to 28 December 1991 ended before mid-February 1992, and the holder the relaunch call recognises in May is in a SECOND reign that begins in the last week of February. The row is not renumbered and no second reign is minted, because the documents that carry it belong to another wing and are not served; the correction is written here so nothing in this ledger reads as settled when it is not.
