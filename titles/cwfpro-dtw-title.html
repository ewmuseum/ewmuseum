Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened February 2006 when Ganxsta won a battle royal eliminating Mike Unit to become the DTW brand's own 1st Champion. Eminem (5) and Jason X (4) are its most frequent holders across 22 recorded reigns, better than two-thirds of them otherwise bare ledger names. Monster closes the ledger's own account, defeating Jason X to unify the DTW Title with the CWF-Pro Title.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 22 reigns in full; all 22 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/dtw-title.html for the underlying primary source.
