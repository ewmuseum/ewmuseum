Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Listed as Tier V in 2008 and retired on 1 January 2010: a belt whose holder had to defend it at every event. James Caine, Madison Divera and Spaz Blud held it in November and December 2007; Suriel N. Crowlie won it at Noitcerusser I and The Diablo Slayer took it from him on 12 August 2008. The Iron Man and No Limit belts were combined into the Untamed Championship at Cold Fall on 21 September 2008.

No holder is listed between Spaz Blud in December 2007 and Suriel N. Crowlie in July 2008.
