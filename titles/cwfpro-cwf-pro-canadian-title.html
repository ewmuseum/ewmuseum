CWF-Pro Canadian Title

Placard

Promotion   CWF-Pro (1997)
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened December 1997 as the CWF-Pro Canadian Heavyweight Title (renamed CWF-Pro Canadian Title by March 1998), when Mike Hart won a tournament final over Ticking Time Bomb to become its 1st Canadian Champion. Across 81 recorded reigns White Zombie (6), Mr. Universe (4) and Jimmy Lee (4) return most often, and a long run of its own title changes double as unification nights — rival promotions' own belts, from the TPW United States Title to the CWE Canadian Title, folded into it one by one as CWF-Pro absorbed each fed's own top titles. The Golden One closes the ledger's own account in December 2007, unifying it together with the Royal Title into the Intercontinental Title in a 3-way dance. 22 of the 81 recorded holders (27%) survive only as bare ledger names.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 81 reigns in full; all 81 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/cwf-pro-canadian-title.html for the underlying primary source.
