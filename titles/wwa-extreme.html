Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opens under "WWA GenX" billing, its first champion "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion crowned 7 March 2003 in a title unification with the Cruiserweight Championship — but Zion already held the Extreme belt itself at that point, having won it solo from an untraced "Rage In A Cage 6" after a brief joint reign with Chance, who in turn had won it back from Enigma in a 2-on-1 handicap match on 6 January 2003; Enigma took the belt clean off Chance on 9 December 2002. Chance's own reign before that loss, and Shaman's own four reigns predating Chance's (billed on-air, December 2002 and again February 2003, as "Four time former WWA Extreme Champion"), remain a genuine, honest gap — no opponents or dates for either survive in the recovered corpus. The belt's own recap prose shifts to "Extreme Championship" branding by early 2004 without a break in the numbered ledger. "Bonafide" James Curzon, Shaman, and Jonathon Winters all held it more than once across the run; the title was folded into the Television Championship on 26 March 2006, "merged with the TV Title" in the ledger's own words, before Eddie Van Dorn and Tarin Ballard closed out the fed's final recovered Extreme-title reigns in 2007.

The fed's own numbered ledger (title id 9) traces this belt continuously from an earlier "Gen X Championship" billing to the "Extreme Championship" name, with no break in the sequence — only the champion's own billing changes mid-record.
