Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The record's own account of this belt opens on a finish nobody could call: Ace Of Spades and challenger Jinx both have their shoulders down for referee Gangsta Mac's own three-count at Immortality 2002, and the announce team simply refuses to name a winner. The lineage that follows is a chain of retains broken by unrecorded turnovers — CM2, The Krazy Kid and Mr. Wacko each hold it into a show where the record shows someone else already champion — until “QBz Finest” Blaze takes it from Wacko at Resurrection and The Fox wins it off Blaze in a six-man Six Pack Challenge at Breakout, the last title change the surviving record shows for this belt.
