RPW's Tag Team Title was first won by Invincible Gabe and Rampage, between 2004-03-14 and 2004-03-21. The fed's own record carries 2 reigns on it, across 2 different champions. The Forsaken held it at the end of the surviving record.

Invincible Gabe and Rampage won the Tag Team Titles at an Adrenaline show before 21 March 2004 that is not held; the write-up names Sweet Illusion among the teams they beat. Defences on record. Adrenaline 18, 28 March 2004: Scott "Rampage" Matthews lifted James Edwards in the Electric Chair and Invincible Gabe hooked the leg for the three, so Invincibleness~! retained the Tag Team Titles in their first defence against Christian Crawford and Edwards. Adrenaline 20, 11 April 2004: Invincible Gabe and Rampage retained the Tag Team Titles by count out when the three men of The Phantom Swerve~! ran to the back and Mike Matslap counted to ten.
