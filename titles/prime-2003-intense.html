PRIME Intense Title

Placard

Promotion   PRIME (2003)
Active   2003–2011
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PRIME's Intense Title was first won by Rock Startling on 6 July 2003. The fed's own record carries 31 reigns on it, across 28 different champions. The longest is Vangelus Olsig's, 361 days from 24 July 2005. Five of its reigns ended outside a match — stripped from the holder or vacated. Its last champion, Seymour Almasy, vacated it on 1 August 2011.
