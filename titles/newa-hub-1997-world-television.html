Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A hub-wide television championship of the National eWrestling Alliance, defended continuously from April 1998. Nineteen reigns survive in this collection's own record, several lasting only days; the last (Harbeneth Rookens, from February 2000) carries an unresolved later complication in the hub's own record rather than a clean end.

Source: the hub's own title-history page. No claimed real-world lineage; the page's own footnote numbering continues directly from the World Heavyweight and World Tag Team pages' own footnotes, confirming the three documents were maintained as one continuous site.
