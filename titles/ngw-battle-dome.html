Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt the fed's own thirty-man Battle Dome match played for, and Time Bomb was carrying it at the last title page. Border Line 2004 ended with the referee handing it to John Hitchin on the turnbuckles.

The reign ledger is served at /promotions/ngw/archive/battle-dome-title-history.html; it is not yet transcribed into this registry.
