Battling Ring Angels Celestial Championship

Placard

Promotion   Battling Ring Angels (1999)
Active   2001

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

BRA's own top singles title, changing hands repeatedly across the promotion's run (Sherry Ann, Yvette Malreaux, Evonne Carmikel, Lisa Dream, Tiffany Lane and Lindsay Locke among its recorded holders); a 2001 “Celestial Tournament” closes out the surviving record with a Heaven-and-Hell cage final between Akira Hagawa and Casey Gaines whose result does not survive.
