Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A 24/7 belt with rules of its own: anyone on the roster except the World Champion could challenge for it at any time by sending the word “Challenge” to the front office, first come first served, and it was defended out of a board only its own champion could read. El Pablo won it on Shatter Point in May 2011; the fed's own record shows it changing hands twice more inside two months, to Chris Johnson at Retaliation and then to a surprise-entrant Waylon Krews mid-way through an unrelated tag match on Shatter Point 7/5/11. The fed called it innovative in its own recruiting copy.

The fed's own Hall of Champions index links an Underground Championship history page (ewaefed.ucoz.com/index/underground_championship/0-34) that was never captured.
