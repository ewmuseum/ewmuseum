PTC Extreme Championship

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The alliance's hardcore championship, first won on 21 July 2002 by Menace of the XCWF, who beat Jackson Rust in the final of the R.E.B.E.L. tournament the XCWF ran with PTC's backing and lost it in August to Rust; Rust was stripped of it when he spoke of retiring. The record then reaches the New Era card of 20 April 2003, where the belt already had a history — the results page calls that night only the second defence in the title's own life. Terry Woods came out of semi-retirement to defend it inside the Death Trap, a suspended ring strung with barbed wire over a burning pit, and lost it to Maelgrim in Maelgrim's own debut match. The title was later vacated when Maelgrim and his challenger Kai Shinobi both failed to show for a title match. Tristan Belmont was champion by 27 September 2003 and lost it to Fathom in a hide and seek match on 5 December; Mike Marvelous took it from Fathom in the Lion's Den at WWA's Fiesta on 19 January 2004, Sean McClancy from Marvelous in the Irish Pub Brawl on 21 April, and Moraless from McClancy on UWF's Sunday Afternoon Supremacy on 23 May, before giving it up in July. Joey Vendetti won the vacant title in the Elimination Chamber at PTC Survival on 22 August 2004, Shadow took it from him in a three-way on 22 February 2005, and Nova took it from Shadow in a scaffold match at PRIME's Overkill on 15 May; Nova vacated it on 31 August. Hoyt Williams won the vacant title, while Rich “The Renegade” Rollins took the vacant PTC Global Championship, in a Scavenger Hunt Match at PTC's Super Series I on 6 November 2005. Archibald MacGregor, a free agent, took it from him in a judged title match (the forum's own results are dated 4 June 2006; the fed's later write-up says March) and held it until 29 December 2006, when he dropped out of his next defence and the title went to Nova, the first man to hold it twice. Dusk won the last Extreme Title match, an eight-way, on 15 February 2007, and lost the belt to Jason Snow on 30 April 2007, when it was folded into the PTC Unified Championship. PTC's own title log dates three of these wins differently from the hosts' own cards and PTC's own records of the matches — Marvelous's to 21 January 2004, McClancy's to 20 April and Vendetti's to 9 November 2004, the day the results were posted — and puts MacGregor's win on an AWC card of 30 May 2006; the dates above are the cards' and records' own. Who held it before Woods, and how Woods won it, the record does not settle.

The belt's first two reigns are recorded: Menace won it on 21 July 2002 in the final of the R.E.B.E.L. tournament, and lost it about two weeks later to Jackson Rust, who was stripped of it when he spoke of retiring. Which held record Jackson Rust is has not been settled, so his reign carries the name as PTC's own title log bills him. Nothing survives about who held the title, or how, between his reign and Terry Woods's, which begins at a date nobody has located. At New Era, the results page has Rust at ringside helping Maelgrim, taking revenge for "his title loss, which he didn't actually lose".
