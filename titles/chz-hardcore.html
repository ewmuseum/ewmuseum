Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Count won the first CHZ Hardcore Championship at Caged Fury 1 on 1 August 1999, in the match since named the Transylvania Hardcore Match, and carried it through the CHZ and FWF mergers. The SWF years passed it among Staz, Freddy Landstrom, the Outlawz and BlaQ Inc.; The Count won it twice more and was kept as a non-fighting champion until the New CHZ held a final, which Diablo won over his partner Fire on 21 September 2001. Extreme Chancelz took it from Diablo in the fed's first barbed wire match, 14 November 2001; Black Dragon, Lucifer and Madd Maxx followed in early 2002, Maxx winning it on 6 March 2002 and billed to defend it against Wild Dog and Lucifer at NBK Pride, with no result surviving. Lucifer beat Mad Max for it on 26 March 2003; Xear was champion by 23 April, and Zen Kyosaku beat him for it in a three-way at May HayDay 2 on 14 May 2003.

The fed's own Hardcore title history (/promotions/chz/archive/hardcore-title-history.html): Black Dragon took it from Extreme Chancelz on 24 January 2002, Lucifer from Black Dragon at No Luv on 27 February, and Madd Maxx from Lucifer on 6 March 2002. The 21 September 2001 Friday Night Wild bills Diablo's win "for the right to be the First Hardcore Champion" and calls him "the first ever CHZ hardcore champion" (/promotions/chz/archive/fnw-2001-09-21.html), where the title history carries the belt back to The Count on 1 August 1999: sources disagree.
