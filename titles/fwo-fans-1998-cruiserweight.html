Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

fWo's Cruiserweight Championship: the earliest reign served here begins on 15 May 1998, held by Ultra Violet. The fed's own record carries 67 reigns on it; 48 of them, across 30 different champions, are served here so far. The longest of those is Tempest's, 288 days from 23 February 2002. Five of them ended outside a match (handed over with no match, stripped, vacated).
