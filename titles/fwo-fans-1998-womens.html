Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

fWo's Women's Championship: the earliest reign served here begins on 29 October 1998, held by Briana. The fed's own record carries 18 reigns on it; 17 of them, across 10 different champions, are served here so far. The longest of those is Briana's, 268 days from 24 December 1999. One of them ended outside a match (handed over with no match). James Armstrong held it at the end of the surviving record.
