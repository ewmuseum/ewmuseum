LOL Galactic Title

Placard

Promotion   LOL (1998)
Active   1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Billed in the fed's own founding FAQ (21 July 1998) as "the big one; the most prestigious title offered by the LOL," reserved for the very best. Won by Van Halent at the fed's own SantaSlam '98 season finale in November 1998, per its own later recap; Van Halent carried the belt through the fed's 1999 return, retaining it against every challenger the surviving record shows.
