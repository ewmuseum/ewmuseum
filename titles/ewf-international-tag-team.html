Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Blood Man and J won this belt vacant at WrestleFest 2003, eliminating John Dumas and Chaos in a tag team elimination match — Blood Man pinned Dumas behind the referee’s back with a chair, then the pair finished Chaos with Blood Man’s Bleeding Blood and J’s own J Splash. They were still champions, by the fed’s own commentary and ring announcement called simply the “Tag team championship,” when Jay Krazy, Dumas and Chaos jumped them celebrating the win, and were still holding it six weeks later at the champions page’s last capture, there billed the “International Tag Team” champions. No other holder appears anywhere in what the Elite Wrestling Foundation left.
