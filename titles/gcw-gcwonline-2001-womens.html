Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GCW's Women's Championship's first recorded champion is Icebabe. The fed's own record carries 6 reigns on it, across 5 different champions. One of its reigns ended outside a match (stripped from the holder). The federation's own Record Book lists it among the defunct titles.
