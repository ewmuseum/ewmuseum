ACW Gateway Championship

Placard

Promotion   ACW (2002–13)
Active   2011–2012

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Made for a new division on Courage 131, 20 January 2011, when Jeremy Hunt, ACW's new Overlord, unveiled the belt and awarded it to Billows; Shawn Jessica Hart pinned Billows for it two weeks later and held it through the last card of that run. Ryan Sully submitted champion Dude In Mask for it at ACW's first World Wide Wrestlecide on 3 November 2011, and Chris Hopper held it by mid-2012. The fan board's own account calls it "now-defunct" by February 2013, retired sometime after a match Jack Harris lost for it at that year's End Game -- a card not otherwise recovered in the held record.
