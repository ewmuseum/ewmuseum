Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The LHW Championship's own lineage under a new name, renamed at the Destiny Pay-Per-View on 24 September 2006 with Syxx still holding it. Thirteen reigns follow in under two years, seven of them inside 2008 alone — one of those won by Val Venis at a house show on 8 January, two days after the belt had been vacated at another. Ron Killings and Paul London took it twice each that spring. Rob Van Dam's win at Death Before Dishonor on 25 May 2008 is the last change recorded, and the promotion's own Champions page still names him champion.
