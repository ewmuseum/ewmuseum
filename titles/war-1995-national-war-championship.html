WAR! National WAR! Championship

Placard

Promotion   WAR! (1995)
Active   1997

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Born from a 14-man battle royale during the fed's own August 1997 relaunch under new ownership in Manhattan, contested twice in three weeks and still active when the fed's own 'Old War!'/'New War!' rosters reunified that October.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Tommy Walks-With-Thunder24 August 1997—Won the title's inaugural match by countout.
The title itself is created for the fed's own August 1997 relaunch in Manhattan under new ownership (see the digest's own Old War!/New War! ruling); its first two challengers were the finalists of a 14-man battle royale on "War!Zone - All But One". Loses the title to Nomad on "War!Zone - OverKill" (8 Sep 1997).
Nomad8 September 1997—Won by pinfall with stablemate interference.
Retains against Tommy Walks-With-Thunder by disqualification-style stoppage (Juggernaut interferes again, unmasking is threatened but not completed) on "War!Zone - Bloody Allies"; also billed defending against Kevin Slater on "THIS IS WAR!"
Jerry "Goon" Gordonunknown—Recorded from the promotion's own repeated billing; the title-winning match itself is not attested.
Retains against "The American" Butch Williams by pinfall on "War!Zone: Beyond Hell" (31 March 1999); still champion at the close of the held record's 1999 pull. The title is carried on the same record as the National WAR! Championship (war-1995-national-war-championship), on-air renamed the Manhattan Championship per the promotion's own 5 November 1999 Players Guide.
