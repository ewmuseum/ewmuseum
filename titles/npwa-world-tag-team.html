NPWA World Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   NPWA (1993)
Active   1996–1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A lineage reaching back to the NWC era, the belts changed hands four times across 1997 alone -- the New Cadavre Connection to the Muslim Massacre (their challengers flying Vegas to London on the Concorde mid-show to turn) to the Lords of the Ring to the Asian Triad -- before a further change and a forfeiture carried the title to Drama by year's end.
