Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The United Kings — the English Gent and Pete Hardy, billed together out of the land of warm beer and bad weather — are the only team the record ever places with it. They were booked to defend it against the Tragic Kingdom at Clash of the Titans in February 2003, and the fed's own front page was still pairing the tag belts with their photograph in April. The night that page was captured they had wrestled Venom and Cobra in a non-title match, and lost it by disqualification with the English Gent bleeding through a bandage at ringside.

The fed's own front page hangs its six championships in a row as belt graphics that swap, on hover, for the current champion's own headshot; three of the six pairings are independently corroborated by the fed's own show text of the same week, which is what makes the mechanism readable at all.
