Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's hot-potato belt — 68 changes of hands across the run, more than any other GIWF title. Jay Phoenix held it 13 days in 2000 under the ring name Inferno. Blade's own final reign, the fed's last recorded TV Championship change, closes on a vacancy: he “vacated the title before it was retired.” Restarts as the UWF's own Television Championship.
