WeWA World Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   WeWA (Alliance, 2010)
Active   1988–2014

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

First held by Pete Jackson from 29 April 1988, the page's own history runs 29 reigns and ends with "The Influence of Wrestling" Jason Cashe's reign from 20 April 2014, printed as current.

Filed from the alliance's own title page. The page says the alliance's title histories are based on the World Wrestling Alliance fantasy league's; its champions are real professional wrestlers and the league's own wrestlers side by side in that inherited history.
