VXW Spin The Wheel Championship

Placard

Promotion   VXW (2001)
Active   2005–2006

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A new title created on the spot at Phear Phantastic 2005, awarded to the first non-champion who pinned another non-champion inside the Rage In The Cage cage match; Damian Payne won it in his own second VXW appearance, pinning Justin Harper with Kagedo's own illegal assistance. Retained in a Table For Two match against Omega on Christmas Comes Early, before losing it to Cobra at Xtreme Machine I in an escapes-only steel cage match and regaining it from Cobra twelve days later at St Patricks Day Massacre.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Damian Payne22 November 2005—Pinfall over Justin Harper (a small package) inside the Rage In The Cage cage match, with Kagedo rolling through Harper's own attempted pin to put Payne on top.
The inaugural champion, in a title created on the spot for the first non-champion to pin another non-champion inside the same cage match. Retained the title against Omega on Christmas Comes Early in a Table For Two match, both men crashing through the same table together before the referee ruled Payne on top. Lost the title to Cobra at Xtreme Machine I, escaped from an escapes-only steel cage.
Cobra8 March 2006—Cage escape inside an escapes-only steel cage match, pushing off with his boots to send Damian Payne backward through the open door.
Cobra's own first VXW Spin The Wheel Championship win, ending Damian Payne's own reign as the title's inaugural champion. Lost the title back to Payne at St Patricks Day Massacre.
Damian Payne20 March 2006—Pinfall, the Last Stop followed by a Chokebomb.
Payne's own second VXW Spin The Wheel Championship win, regaining the title he lost to Cobra twelve days earlier at Xtreme Machine I. Cobra repeatedly claimed the championship on air across the promotion's later broadcasts, defending it in matches billed as title defenses against Mr. Jones (The Hype II), Mr. Sal (The Hype IV) and John Manning (Hotter Than Hell I), but no surviving card ever bills or records a title change away from Payne. Payne remains this championship's last documented holder through the promotion's final surviving broadcast, Sin City Fights February 7, 2007, where he wrestles a non-title match unrelated to the championship.
