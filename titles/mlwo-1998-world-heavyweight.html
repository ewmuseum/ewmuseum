MLWO World Heavyweight Championship

aka VPWF World Heavyweight Championship, MLWO Heavyweight Championship of the World

Placard

Promotion   MLWO (1996)
Active   1996–1998

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's top singles title. It was billed as the VPWF World Heavyweight Championship in the autumn of 1996, when Mauler carried it and Guy LaChance won it and lost it back. Marcus Renge and Jak Martin held it before Steve Sampson pinned Martin at Deadly Games on 22 March 1997; Martin won it back in a steel cage on 26 April, the first man to hold it twice. Marcus Renge won the five-man Torture Cage of Torture Chamber 2 on 26 October 1997, and Mister Robinson took it from Martin in the ladder match of Winter Inferno 97. Captain Midnight held it after February 1998 until it was handed to Steve Spector and dropped, leaving it vacant; Midnight won it back in a ladder match at May Madness 98 on 25 May 1998.
