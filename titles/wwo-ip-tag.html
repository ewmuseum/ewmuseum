WWO International People's Tag Team Titles

Placard

Promotion   WWO (1995)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Won first by Rocky Johnson and Rusty Bowman in a battle royal, the WWO's own International People's Tag Team Titles passed from Continental Connection through the World Leaders, the Bionic Team and Thunderbolts — vacated once when Thunderbolts lost a member to a departure from the federation and sat on hiatus — before Uncivil Disobedience simply stole the belts and declared themselves champions. The ledger's own last word on them: the federation closed with Precious Moments recognized as its final holders.
