Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A mid-card singles belt through 2001 and 2002 — Jim ‘JLC’ Phoenix twice, Spawn, Skipster, Baz, Kryptonite, Shensui Yusuki, Shifty and Cyrus Skar all held it, and Joe La Fontaine was champion at the fed's own June 2002 snapshot. It is one of the five titles FAW's own 2003 champions page names as no longer worth keeping.
