Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's Tag Team Championship: 12 reigns are served here, across 10 different champions, the first beginning on 28 November 2010 with Technical Mechanica and the last on 19 July 2015 with The Setons. The longest of those is Perfect Combination's, 77 days from 3 May 2015. Of these, 6 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). It was retired by the fed in September 2011 and February 2012 and brought back in April 2015.
