Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Vacant at the September 2006 restart, filled by an eight-man tournament Abyss won over Kurt Angle with outside interference from owner Matthew Hopkins and Chyna-Doll. The belt changed hands six more times on the surviving ledger — through Kurt Angle, Brock Lesnar, Sting, Christian Cage (in an elimination chamber over Sting, U.S. Champion Hulk Hogan, Paul Wight, Bill Goldberg and Ric Flair), Kurt Angle again and Ric Flair — before Matthew Hopkins stripped Flair over a real-world dispute involving Vince McMahon and the Stamford Outsiders; the tournament called to fill the vacancy never finished after a riot closed the arena. Randy Orton won the last-recorded reign in a Triple Threat against John Bradshaw Layfield and Flair.
