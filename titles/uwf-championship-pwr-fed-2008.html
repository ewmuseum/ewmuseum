Billed as held by Jeff Hardy from Summer Party (28 September 2008), where he retained against Edge. By the Sacrifice brand's own launch (February 2009), John Cena is the billed champion instead; no surviving document records how the title passed from Hardy to Cena. Edge earned the next challenge for it by winning Sacrifice #1's own Elimination Fatal-4-Way, aided by Randy Orton's interference; that match's own result closed with Cena, Orton, Mr. Kiz and Edge in open conflict, setting up a promised BlockDown title match not captured.
