Chapter One promised a tournament for 'the first-ever LOC Elite Champion'. Edge won it in a four-way over Austin Starr, Desmond Wolfe and Christopher Daniels at the end of the one-night tournament on Chapter Three, 7 May 2010; the champions page, dated before that night, still lists the title as vacant.

The fed's own belt as its champions page lists it, never a real company's belt.
