Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own served title-history page runs a fully dated chain of 17 reigns from K.C. Colossus's tournament win on 8 November 1997 through Rob Holiday's tournament win on 21 October 2001 -- both tournament finals billed on the page itself for the "World/North American Championship," a compound name used only at those two bracket notes and nowhere else in the lineage, distinct from the fed's own separately-defended RCW North American Championship. The Executive holds the belt four separate times, K.C. Colossus three. The page's own ledger records the title vacant from 6 October to 21 October 2000, the last entry before that stretch carrying its own note "Federation closed," and then vacant again for a full year before Rob Holiday's own win -- an unexplained year-long gap in the source's own stated dates, recorded as it stands. The fed's own served Royal Rampage V recap (18 June 2000) corroborates one reign mid-chain, with The Executive successfully defending against Jack McGriff in a nine-fall main event.
