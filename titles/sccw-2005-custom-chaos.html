Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Fuse Custom Chaos Champion was first won by The Animal on 6 November 2005. The fed's own record carries 12 reigns on it, across 12 different champions. The longest is Evan 'Damage' Jackson's, 73 days from 6 September 2006. Two of its reigns ended outside a match — vacated. Jonathan Rhine held it at the end of the surviving record.
