Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Decided at the promotion's own first pay-per-view, where "The Natural Wonder" Max Maxwell won a tournament final over the Mad Goat to become the fed's first-ever World Heavyweight Champion, successfully defending it against "Lucky" Lambert at the next and last card this record holds. A fellow Usenet participant's own signature line, five weeks later, names Bobby "Bad Apple" Durango as champion instead; no card documenting how the title changed hands survives.
