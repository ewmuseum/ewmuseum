Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

OWA's top belt, created February 2016 and won on the federation's own first night: Tyler Chandler took an open battle royal on the 2 March 2016 Anarchy to become the inaugural champion. Its own ledger records exactly one reign, still running at 327 days when the record stops, and its own article calls it “the top-tier championship title in Orlando Wrestling Alliance.” Chandler defended it against Alex Madsen on the January 2017 return show and was billed to defend it against Hunter Gage inside a cage at Rage in the Cage.
