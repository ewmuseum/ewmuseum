Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt Apocalypse held six times. Skipster held it twice, Jim ‘JLC’ Phoenix twice, Baz twice, and Spider, Bayne and The Omega Fro once each; Violator was champion at the fed's own June 2002 snapshot. It was one of the titles dropped in the 2003 cut.

FAW's own champions snapshot of 6 February 2003 (promotions/faw/archive/faw-champions-february-2003.html) names "Golden Nine" Jeff Harris as Hardcore Champion, narrowing the undocumented transition between Damien Ryan's 29 September 2002 win and Harris's own reign to on-or-before 6 February 2003 -- previously the transition was bounded only by Harris's confirmed title on 30 March 2003. A same-week Panic card (promotions/faw/archive/faw-panic-card-2003-02-02.html) bills a Hardcore Title triple threat, Jeff Harris vs Rzarector vs Skipster, corroborating the same snapshot; no results page for that card survives.
