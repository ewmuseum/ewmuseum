EWA Heavyweight Championship

aka EWA Undisputed Heavyweight Championship, EWA Undisputed World Heavyweight Championship

Placard

Promotion   EWA (Alliance, 2003–10)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's top belt of 2003 and 2004. Camaro won its title tournament at Kingdom Come on 10 May 2003 and its page counts ten reigns to Brett Cotter on 27 November 2004. The page's sixth row carries a note that the WWO Championship and the EWA Championship were unified into the EWA Undisputed Heavyweight Championship on 27 March 2004, under a reign dated 17 January 2004; the Sinners card of May 2004 bills the title as the EWA Undisputed World Heavyweight Championship.
