Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The promotion's tag titles, defended once a month. Bill Dynamite and Czecher held them in the first run, Dylan Dunn and Scott Royal twice; AJ Donavon and Bobby Dynamite, Xaria Linette and Alexis Sykes, and C.M. Vyrus and Ryley Wynters as Nuclear Wynter all held them afterwards. The Takeover were defending them on the 11 November 2007 card, and Nuclear Wynter had them back by April 2008 — with a shirt of their own in the fed's own store. The Iconoclast were the champions by the Hell and Back card, billed against the former champions The Takeover.
