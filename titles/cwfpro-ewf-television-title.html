Promotion   CWF-Pro (1997)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened April 1997 when Ghost won a battle royal eliminating Wildman to become the EWF brand's own 1st Television Champion. Half Man Half Amazing (5), Joe Sanders (3) and Pornstar (3) are its most frequent holders across 21 recorded reigns, roughly half of them otherwise bare ledger names. Kevin Hall closes the ledger's own account, defeating Business Man to unify the EWF TV Title with the CWF-Pro Royal Title.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 21 reigns in full; all 21 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/ewf-television-title.html for the underlying primary source.
