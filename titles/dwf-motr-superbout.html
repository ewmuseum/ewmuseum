Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

An annual grudge-match attraction bout, fought outside the tournament bracket; "Bad Eye" McBaine's seventh-edition win over Eddie Jacobs made him a two-time champion.

Jason Keening is billed on the seventh broadcast as the third SuperBout's own champion, and McBaine as a two-time champion; the second and third editions now survive, contested on the same July 2000 broadcast as the Master of the Ring III tournament final, so three of the seven editions' reigns are recorded.
