Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A hub-wide cruiserweight championship of the National eWrestling Alliance, defended continuously from January 1999. Twelve reigns survive in this collection's own record, changing hands rapidly through late 1999; the last (El Scorcho, from February 2000) carries an unresolved later complication in the hub's own record rather than a clean end.

Source: the hub's own title-history page. No claimed real-world lineage.
