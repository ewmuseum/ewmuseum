Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned in a tournament whose final the record never resolves: the fed's own Clash of the Titans card of 28 February 2003 stages the final as its main event under two silhouettes, and its own newsdesk reports a fortnight later that an International Champion was crowned there. Derek Frost is carrying it a month afterwards, tagging with Jay Robson as ColdBlood and booked to defend it against Roz Blayze on 7 April. By WrestleCade III it is Tragic Hero's, and he keeps it in a four-way elimination match that Quartz walks into to spear the last man standing so that Tragic Hero can put him in the Perfect Circle.
