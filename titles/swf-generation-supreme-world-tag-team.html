Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The pair of belts held by Sexual Aggression, who took them from The Amazing Bumfholes at Nothing to Lose in February 2008 and defend them on Supreme TV, at Awesome Impact and at The World is Watching. They keep them against The Amazing Bumfholes at Master of Puppets 2008, and Valiant & Giedroyc take them from Sexual Aggression on the first SWF Legends in June 2008, after Joe Sexy walks out on Angry Gilmore mid-match. Valiant & Giedroyc keep the belts when Giedroyc's stoppage win ends the four-way at Times of Trouble 2008, then against The Heartbreakers on Supreme TV in Week 1 of July and The Upper Class in Week 2; the card of The Supreme Challenge 28 sets them against The New Wave and Badd Kompany in a Threefold Threat. Valiant & Giedroyc keep the belts at The Supreme Challenge 28 when Valiant pins Joss Thompson in a Threefold Threat against The New Wave and Badd Kompany, and are booked against Sin Inc for Welcome to the Jungle 2008. Valiant & Giedroyc keep the belts at Welcome to the Jungle 2008 against Sin Inc when Giedroyc rolls up John McClean, beat The Upper Class in Week 3 of August and The Heartbreakers in Week 1 of September, and Richard Eisen names The New Wave the next challengers, for Under Control 2008.
