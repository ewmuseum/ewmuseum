CSWA Unified World Tag Team Championships

Placard

Promotion   CSWA (1988)
Active   1994

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own tag championship after the merges, carried by two men rather than renamed for each of them. Eddy Love and Kevin Powers won it at BATTLE of the BELTS XVI in May 1999; by December 2000 the fed's Champions page has Foreign Exchange holding it, and by April 2001 Simply Stunning, since PRIMETIME in Paducah. The fed's own page writes it plural — Championships, two belts — throughout.

The fed's own Champions page header reads "Unified World Tag Team Championships", plural. Whether and how it descends from the 1988 CSWA World Tag Team Championship is not settled by any page held here.

Three defenses are recovered from the fed's own 2001 cards, none of which moves the belts: a fifteen-minute time-limit draw against The Professionals at PRIMETIME in Tacoma on 6 October 2001, announced as a draw after the referee had already counted three for the champions; and the champions still billed and defending as of PRIMETIME in New Orleans (11 November) and PRIMETIME in St. Louis (26 December), where they challenge the whole tag division to a gauntlet match at ANNIVERSARY. A FOURTH defense, dated to the day, is the one that challenge was building to: the four-way HOUSE OF PAIN cage match at ANNIVERSARY 2001 on 31 December 2001, in which the champions RETAIN. Michael Hardy takes most of the match's punishment, is spike-tombstoned and busted open, and still scores both of his team's falls -- cradling WJ Mills out of a FrankenParsons to eliminate Raw Deal, then splashing both Professionals off the top of the cage on a Rocket Launcher from Simon Wilcox for the win, with the CS Express on the floor to stop Eddie Mayfield's chairshot first. The fed's own cards call this belt the Unified Tag Team Championships in their section headers and the CSWA World Tag Team Titles in their commentary, within the same document; both readings are recorded and neither is resolved. One of the champions puts the length of this reign at "six months or so" on 26 December 2001, which does not sit with the reign's own recorded start at PRIMETIME in Paducah in February 2001; that disagreement is recorded too. The ANNIVERSARY card adds a THIRD spelling to that first disagreement and resolves nothing: its own section header reads "the CSWA Unified Tag Team Titles" and its commentary "the Unified Tag Team Championships".
