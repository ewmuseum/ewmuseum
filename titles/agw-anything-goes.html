AGW Anything Goes Title

Placard

Promotion   AGW (1997)
Active   1998–2000

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AGW's namesake singles championship, first won 15 February 1998 (in a tournament final, footnote 11) and defended through 33 recorded reigns before the ledger's own last word, E.H. Brawler's reign beginning 30 January 2000, running into the fed's own hiatus. Its footnote 23 reign changed hands in a 4-way Flaming Barbed Wire Baseball Bat Match.
