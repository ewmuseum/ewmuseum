New Era Old School Wrestling's title for wrestlers under 215 pounds. Sushi Tsunami held it when the promotion's January 2001 preshow opened, and Psicopa won it from Sushi Tsunami in a ladder match at Storm Warning on 7 May 2001, still champion on 26 June.

The promotion's own weight-restricted (under 215 lbs) title; already held when this record's own January 2001 preshow opens.
