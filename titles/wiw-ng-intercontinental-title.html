Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own history opens the Intercontinental Title on 17 May 1999 with The Punisher and lists 46 reigns to Chris Storm's of 26 July 2002, among them three by Jack Death and three by Shane Slade. The End won it in a Wrestlefest II cage match on 9 June 2002 and lost it to Storm on 26 July. The November 2002 champions list names Golden Hammer.
