Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PCW's Continental Championship was first won by Alan Kriegman on 22 October 2000. The fed's own record carries 36 reigns on it, across 31 different champions. The longest is Totally Tubular Tommy's, 84 days from 22 December 2002. Four of its reigns ended outside a match — handed over, stripped from the holder or vacated. Skitzo held it at the end of the surviving record.
