NCCW Television Championship — NCCW

Placard

Promotion   NCCW (1998)
Active   1998–2000

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NCCW's mid-card belt, defended most weeks on the fed's own Saturday Matinee per its 1998 recruitment ad, changing hands eight times (two by forced vacancy) before "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre won it, defended it once on pay-per-view, and held it for over eighteen months without a single loss, forfeiting it only when the promotion folded around him.
