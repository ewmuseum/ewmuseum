Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The People's Championship's own lineage under a new name, renamed at the Destiny Pay-Per-View on 24 September 2006 while Alex Shelley held it. Randy Orton took it from him at the King of the Ring in June 2007 and Lance Storm took it at Destiny that September. Storm held it until the belt was retired on Aggression on 1 November 2007, the same night as the Iron Man Championship.
