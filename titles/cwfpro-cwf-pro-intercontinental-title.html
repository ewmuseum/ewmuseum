CWF-Pro Intercontinental Title

Placard

Promotion   CWF-Pro (1997)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Opened April 1997 as the CWF-Pro Intercontinental Heavyweight Title (renamed CWF-Pro Intercontinental Title that December), when Gator won a battle royal eliminating Sebek to become its 1st Intercontinental Champion. Hero (6), Jackson (4) and Billy Lee (3) are its most frequent holders across 106 recorded reigns, and like CWF-Pro's other flagship belts it absorbed a long list of rival promotions' own titles one by one — the EWF United States, CSW National, SCW Internet, BMWF Intercontinental, WWWCW Intercontinental, IWWA Intercontinental, XXX 2nd, EIWZ North American, TDW International, NWO TV, IWF Intercontinental, CWE National and AWI Television Titles among them. The ledger's own last recorded line names OPG as champion, with no closing note; 20 of the 106 recorded champions survive only as bare ledger names.

CWF-Pro's own served title-history ledger records 106 reigns in full; all 106 are structured into the registry's own reign record — see promotions/cwfpro/archive/cwf-pro-intercontinental-title.html for the underlying primary source.
