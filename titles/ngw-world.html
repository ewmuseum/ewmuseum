NGW World Title

aka NGW World Heavyweight Title

Active   2000–2004
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Crowned at NGW's first pay-per-view, Out Cold I on 25 February 2000, when Excalibur beat Chris Jericho in a no-disqualification steel cage match, and vacated four days later when the man who took it off him quit. The fed's own ledger runs the belt through thirty-six numbered reigns; Scottywood's own is the longest thing on it, seven defences deep through the spring of 2000, and Scott King was carrying it when the fed last published a title page in November 2004.

The full reign ledger is served at /promotions/ngw/archive/world-title-history.html and /promotions/ngw/archive/champions-and-title-history-2004-11.html; it is not yet transcribed into this registry.
