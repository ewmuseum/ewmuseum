Unified into   HDTV Championship 10 January 2012 — The federation unified the Hardcore Championship into the Television Championship in a three-way match between the two champions and a challenger. Its own voice, before the bell: "Bryan: As per the higher ups, the Television and Hardcore title are being unified and, albeit for a short time, one person will leave here as the first double champion since Triumph Frost." And after: "Andrews: Your winner...and the NEW GCW Unified Television and Hardcore champion.....SEXXXXXXXXX-TONNNNNNN......HARRRRRRRRRRRDDDDDD-ONNNNNNNNNNNNN!!!!!" / "Bryan: Hardon pulled through to become the first ever Unified Hardcore and Television champ in GCW history". The direction is the Television title's: the fed's own title log files this match and the belt's next and last match (Dangerous Games of 17 Mar 2012, 'Television Championship Match') under the Television title, and the fed's own title list carries the champion of record. The federation's own name for the belt from this night is the Unified Television and Hardcore Championship; Yale spends the entrance segment failing to coin a shorter one and Bryan settles it — "Let's just stick with the Unified Television and Hardcore title for now". Source: the fed's own card, WorldWide of 10 Jan 2012 (billed 'TELEVISION & HARDCORE/VANGUARD CHAMPIONSHIP UNIFICATION MATCH').
Retired by the fed   between 2005-05-20 and 2007-03-09 (circa) — The federation's own words, in the closing line of the Vanguard Championship's first title defence: "Vetra has the retired Cruiserweight and Hardcore titles on his form like a gold vest while he holds his Vanguard Title up in the air." The last Hardcore reign the page carries begins 19 May 2005, so the retirement falls between that date and this card. The reversal has the federation's own word too: a news column of 17 Mar 2010 ('The WorldWide Web #1') says "We knew after GCW Radio that the Tag Team Titles had been suspended, which drew the ire of Bryan Mayhem. But Mayhem was satisfied; he gets credit for a Tag Title reign, and now he's the sole holder of the REINSTATED GCW Hardcore Title." Source: the fed's own card, WorldWide of 9 Mar 2007, and the fed's own news post of 17 Mar 2010.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GCW's Hardcore Championship was first won by Toad on 21 March 2001. The fed's own record carries 41 reigns on it, across 26 different champions. The longest is Trey Tremendous's, 1693 days from about 28 July 2005, a span that takes in the belt's retirement and reinstatement. Twice in 2005 the belt left him and came back the same night, at Warpath and at Dangerous Games. One of its reigns ended outside a match (vacated). GCW unified it into the Television Championship on 10 January 2012, at WorldWide, in a three-way match won by Sexton Hardon. A card of March 2007 calls the belt retired, and a news column of March 2010 speaks of the 'REINSTATED' Hardcore Title.

Three retentions inside the span are on cards: WorldWide of 3 Apr 2011 (a Rampage Group Stage Hardcore Match against Jimmy Thunder, which ends "Bryan Mayhem did it! He retains!" after a belly-to-belly suplex through a broken table), WorldWide of 4 May 2011 (against The Idol, "The winner of this match, and STILL GCW Hardcore Champion, Bryan MAYHEM!") and WorldWide of 22 May 2011 (against Shaman, "Bryan: Mayhem retains, Mayhem retains!" and "the ref hands Mayhem the hardcore title"). So the title was still his on 22 May 2011. The belt was then vacant from 28 Jul 2011 until 15 Aug 2011, when the Commissioner awarded it.
