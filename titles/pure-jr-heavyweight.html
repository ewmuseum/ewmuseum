PURE Jr. Heavyweight Title

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The first belt PURE awarded, and the only one it carried through both of its runs. The End won it on 28 April 2002 at Ikusa II in Matsumoto, beating The H.I.T. and Kensuke Suzahara in a three-way the fed billed as the crowning of its first ever champion. He lost it seven weeks later at Ikusa V in the Sapporo Dome, pinned by The H.I.T. in a tag match the fed put his title on, with The H.I.T. announced in the ring as the new champion, and won it back that summer in a fatal four-way over Inphino Blitz that PURE's own title history places on a show called Ikusa 6 and does not date. It left him for good on 28 July 2002, in another federation's ring: Charlie Loc took it in the first fall of a two-fall match at PCW's Wrestle Vessel that carried the PURE Junior Heavyweight and PCW Cruiserweight titles at once, and was billed afterwards as the first man to win a PURE title outside a PURE ring. He lost it on 13 October 2002 to a masked DJ Nygma, unmasked in the ring that night as El Romantico, who was still champion and under contract to another federation when PURE re-opened. PURE decided the belt again by tournament in January 2003, and Radiant won it on the 26th at Hakatanomori Football Stadium, beating Isao Asai in thirty minutes and fifteen seconds. He beat Asai twice more, then wrestled El Mesias to a draw at Budokan Hall on 9 March 2003, and the surviving record carries no later change.

Attested by the fed's own Ikusa IV card, which bills a defence (/promotions/pure/archive/ikusa-4-card.html), and by its own division rankings (/promotions/pure/archive/columns.html). Its whole lineage is the fed's own: a complete Junior Heavyweight Championship history of six numbered reigns, served at /promotions/pure/archive/junior-heavyweight-championship-history.html. Its champions page carries only the holder of the moment — "Champion: El Romantico" in its January 2003 state, "Champion: Radiant" in its last — and Radiant's own roster card carries the only dated list of defences any PURE belt has. The history and the show scripts disagree twice, and both stand each time: the history names Ikusa III for the change of 16 June 2002 that the Ikusa V script carries, and it has Charlie Loc pin The End for the belt on 28 July 2002 where PCW's own Wrestle Vessel script has him pin Alexander Miyagawa for it. Source: Argyle's PURE deposit (collection 0159).
