Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Contested twice on the same December afternoon in 1997 by an entity that claimed a five-year history nobody in the newsgroup believed: a 16-man battle royal crowned Stonecold, and the same day's DEATHMANIA — billed as the fed's first pay-per-view — staged an entirely separate match for the same championship, won by Emortal Soldier, with no acknowledgment that Stonecold had just won it hours earlier. Neither the announcer nor either script explains the discontinuity; both results stand as the fed's own text gives them.
