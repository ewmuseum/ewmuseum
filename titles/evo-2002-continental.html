eVolution Continental Championship

Placard

Promotion   eVolution (eVo)
Active   2002–2003
Cadence   standing
Unified into   United Championship 31 August 2003 — The fed's own title history records the belt unified with the ULWN Heavyweight Title and renamed the United Title.

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

eVo's Continental Championship was first won by Erin Christoph on 10 July 2002. The fed's own record carries 10 reigns on it, across 9 different champions. The longest is Asis's, 109 days from 5 February 2003. Three of its reigns ended outside a match — retired with the belt or vacated. eVo unified it into the United Championship on 31 August 2003.

Erin Christoph10 July 200228 August 2002
Erin Christoph reversed Xander's roll-up into a pin, holding his tights, after a Falcon Arrow Suplex, to beat him, The HeartBreak Chick and Eric Badger in a four corners match and become the first Continental Champion. Defences on record. Legends II, 17 July 2002: Erin Christoph retained the Continental Championship in a cage match by low-blowing and powerbombing Xander off the cage wall and climbing out. X-Rated 2002, 14 August 2002: Erin Christoph retained the Continental Championship by pinning Malcolm King after Xander turned on King with the Victimizer and embraced Christoph, in a Tiers of eVolution match.

Sources disagree — The fed's own title history logs the change as Legends VII, 28 August 2002; that date is Legends VI, whose own dateline says so. The date agrees. Shocka pinned Erin Christoph to win the Continental Championship after Tyler Davidson hit Christoph in front of the referee. Defences on record. Digital Destruction 2002, 18 September 2002: Shocka retained the Continental Championship over Shodan with a sunset flip after Thug roC hit Shodan with the belt, in the restarted match. Legends X, 2 October 2002: Shocka retained the Continental Championship by putting Python in a camel clutch after a jackknife powerbomb, and the referee called for the bell.
Shodan27 October 200227 October 2002
Shodan won the Continental Championship by low-blowing Shocka and hitting the End.Game DDT onto a steel chair in a Three Stages of Hell match. Shodan threw the belt down at Shocka's feet after winning it, leaving the Continental Championship vacant.
Jason Jameson31 October 200228 November 2002
Jason Jameson pinned The HeartBreak Chick with the Final Sin off the top rope to win the vacant Continental Championship, after Big Daddy Dreamer hit her with bolt cutters.
Shodan28 November 20028 January 2003
Sources disagree — The fed's own title history has no vacating line between Legends XVI and Legends XXII; the write-up dates it to Legends XX, 8 January 2003. Shodan slid down the back of Jason Jameson's jackknife powerbomb attempt and pinned him with the Deep Sleep to win the Continental Championship. A defence is on record. Nightmare Before Christmas 2002, 22 December 2002: Shodan retained the Continental Championship over Jason Jameson when their 60-minute Iron Man match ended 4-4 and was ruled a No Contest. Shodan walked out on the show, leaving the Continental Championship draped across the ring, vacant, and a four-man tournament was ordered.
Big Daddy Dreamer22 January 20035 February 2003
Big Daddy Dreamer pinned Asis with the Dreamanator, after Sean Mason trapped Asis's head between a steel chair and the ring post, to win the vacant Continental Championship. A defence is on record. New Year's eVolution 2003, 26 January 2003: Big Daddy Dreamer kept the Continental Championship when Asis hit Sean Mason with a steel chair as Mason had the Enigma locked in, and the referee called for the bell.
Asis5 February 200325 May 2003
Asis pinned Big Daddy Dreamer with the Asisination 2k3 to win the Continental Championship. Defences on record. Tainted Love 2003, 23 February 2003: Asis retained the Continental Championship by knockout, putting the tazzmission on an unconscious Big Daddy Dreamer outside the Cell. Legends XXIX, 19 March 2003: Asis retained the Continental Championship by pinning Troy Pierce with the Asisination 2k3 in a Career versus Championship match, ending Pierce's career. End of Days 2003, 27 April 2003: Asis retained the Continental Championship in a TLC match by climbing and unbuckling the belt ahead of Sean Mason after putting Mason through a table. Legends XXXVII, 21 May 2003: Thug roC was first announced the winner and new Continental Champion, then the referee reversed it on the replay to a disqualification win for him after Jason Jameson and Sean Mason interfered, and Asis kept the championship.
Sean Mason25 May 200327 July 2003
Sean Mason pinned Asis with the Midas Touch after driving a chair into Asis's face to win the Continental Championship. Defences on record. eVo1: One Year Anniversary Show, 3 July 2003: Sean Mason retained the Continental Championship by pinning Malik Allah Roland with the Midas Touch. Legends XLIV, 23 July 2003: Sean Mason kept the Continental Championship when Thug roC ran in and hit him with the roCing Horse and the referee disqualified Carl Cash.
Ryan Mason27 July 200331 August 2003
Ryan Mason, wrestling masked as the champion Sean Mason, pulled the Continental Championship down in a ladder match with Thug roC, who then unmasked him. The fed's own title history names Ryan Mason; the write-up has the champion of record, Sean Mason, unmasked as his brother Ryan after the match.
Thug roC31 August 200331 August 2003
Thug roC pinned Ryan Mason with the roCing Horse after a chair shot to win the Continental and ULWN titles in a double title match, the last change of the Continental Championship.
