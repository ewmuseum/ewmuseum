Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Commissioner Becky Byers announced the title the week before, framing it as ending Meltdown's own status as a secondary program; Papa Legba won the inaugural tournament on 17 December 2003, beating Paul Wong in the finals with the Black Magic superbomb.

Title reigns
ReignWonLostDetail
Papa Legba17 December 2003—
Won the inaugural UWF Meltdown Championship, surviving a fifteen-man battle royal as one of its own final four, beating Victor Frost in the semi-finals with an inverted DDT, then Paul Wong in the finals with the Black Magic superbomb. Retained against Alex Kidd on 10 March 2004 by pinfall (Ragga Bomb) with an assist from Tigress and Randall Osbourne. Attacked Alex Extreme and Randall Osbourne with a chair before the bell of a scheduled three-way defense on 21 March 2004, then postponed the match to a cage bout the following week without it ever starting, citing his own father's recent death. Retained again in the rescheduled steel cage match on 5 May 2004: Osbourne never entered after Alex Kidd attacked him before the bell, and Legba climbed out of the cage to the arena floor ahead of the remaining challenger, Alex Extreme, to win by escape. Retained again on 19 May 2004, teaming with Tigress to beat Simon O'Neal and Donna Tetreault in an intergender tag after a top-rope splash caught both opponents for the pin.
Collision Holly25 August 2004—
Won the UWF Meltdown Championship from Papa Legba in a career-first title win, seconded to ringside by Rampage Champion Uriah Hawkins, who had encouraged him beforehand to treat the match seriously rather than as a showcase for the champion. Lost the title to Tigress by submission (the Marjara Mallaka) on 22 September 2004, after Vyolynce, the match's own advertised third participant, declined to compete and left the ring before the bell.
Tigress22 September 200422 June 2005
Won the UWF Meltdown Championship from Collision Holly in what was billed as a three-way submissions match; Vyolynce refused to participate and left the ring before the bell, leaving Tigress to beat Holly alone. Retained again over Youth Gone Wild by submission (an inverted dragon sleeper) on 6 October 2004. Retained again over Bobby Nash by pinfall (the Sumatran Headhunter, using the ropes for leverage) on 20 October 2004. Retained again over "Nighthawk" Michael Bonn by disqualification on 3 November 2004, after a UWF tryout group led by a disguised T.D. Swagger ran in. Retained again over Scorpio by submission (the Marjara Mallaka) on 17 November 2004. A defense against Scott "Hotspot" Daniels on 1 December 2004 ended in a double disqualification draw after both wrestlers fouled each other simultaneously; the title did not change hands. A defense against Marissa Monet on 25 May 2005 also ended in a double disqualification, after both wrestlers shoved the referee; the title did not change hands. Retained again over Marissa Monet by submission (a modified Marjara Mallaka surfboard) in a No DQ rematch on 8 June 2005, the title's own last defense before its unification with the Rampage Championship the following week. Lost the title to Rampage Champion Marcus Nuit by pinfall (the 51st Drop) in a title unification match on 22 June 2005, ending the Meltdown Championship's own separate history.
