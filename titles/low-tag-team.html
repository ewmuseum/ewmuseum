LOW Tag Team Championship

Placard

Promotion   LOW (2002)
Active   2002–2004

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Lights Out Wrestling's own tag team championship, held for long stretches by one pairing under two names: Eric Russo and Jason Kage won it in August 2002 and held it again from August 2003 as the Metallo Zords, and The Gold Ranger and The Silver Ranger — the Zords' own two members, named as such on the team's own bio card — record the same reigns on their own cards. The Saikyo System took the belts off them up a ladder on the fiftieth Realm, and Delta Upsilon Iota, a fraternity act of Colby Korver and Hank Cobb, held them through CATASTROPHIC.

Sources disagree on who the Metallo Zords were. Jason Kage's and Eric Russo's own bio cards each record a Tag Team Championship reign "as the Metallo Zords" from 18 August 2003; The Gold Ranger's and The Silver Ranger's own cards record a Tag Team Championship reign of their own across the same period. Both readings are recorded rather than resolved. The team's own bio card settles who the Metallo Zords were even where it does not settle whose reign this was: its kayfabe Real Name field reads "Gold Ranger and Silver Ranger".
