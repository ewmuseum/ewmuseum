Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Thirty-three numbered reigns from January 2000, and twice inside the first year they changed hands without a tag match: once on the stipulation of a six-man at Xtrememania I, and once when Rey Jr. simply gave his half of them to Triple H. Vacant on the fed's own last title page.

The full reign ledger, built from /promotions/ngw/archive/tag-team-title-history.html and cross-checked against /promotions/ngw/archive/champions-and-title-history-2004-11.html, is transcribed into this registry.
