Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

It was fought for with barbed wire. The one title change held is Revolution's opener, where Enigma beat Jack Manson in a match whose props were a trash can wrapped in barbed wire and a board of nails pulled out of a bin bag, and the belt changed hands on a referee's decision Manson spent the rest of the night calling a screw job. Scorpion held it in the spring of 2002 and Brad Douglas was billed the champion in the first week of March 2003; nothing held says how it travelled between any of them.

Named in the fed's own 2004 rulebook among five championships, two years after the alliance's profile page and one year after the only change of it the record holds.
