Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Machines held this belt into 1997 and defended it against the West Coast Connection at the promotion's own Kickoff '97. It stopped existing as its own belt on 16 April 1997, at AEWA Extreme, when the Machines beat the National Tag Team champions, the Assassins, in a dedicated unification match and the two lineages became one — the AEWA Unified World Tag Team Championship.

Retired into the AEWA Unified World Tag Team Championship on 16 April 1997.
