Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GIW's Legacy Championship: 8 reigns are served here, across 7 different champions, the first beginning on 22 August 2010 with Jamie Vaccaro and the last on 15 August 2012 with Aspen Chaud. The longest of those is Alexis Terry's, 105 days from 15 January 2012. Of these, 1 ended outside a match (stripped, vacated or retired). It was made at Blackout on 22 August 2010 by the unification of the Hardcore and Affliction belts, and retired in November 2012.
