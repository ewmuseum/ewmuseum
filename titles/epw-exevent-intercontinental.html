Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

EPW's Intercontinental Championship was first won by Hudman, before 2003-08-15. The fed's own record carries 6 reigns on it, across 6 different champions. The longest is Chapel's, 168 days from 9 January 2004. Ace held it at the end of the surviving record.
