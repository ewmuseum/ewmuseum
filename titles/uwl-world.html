Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own served title-history page runs a fully dated chain of fifteen reigns across fourteen holders, from Masked Menace's founding reign (11 January 1997) through Chris Jericho's win on 29 October 1999 — Black Shadow alone holding it twice, in April and again from July to September 1997 — mixing original characters (Moonbeam Kid, Trasher, Big Daddy Cool Zader, Punishing One) with real-wrestler names and gimmicks (Bret Hart, Tommy Dreamer, Chris Jericho). The fed's own Summer Blast '99 results page corroborates one link in the chain from the other side — Psycho defending against Punishing One that night — though its own text breaks off before showing the finish the title ledger independently records. The ledger's own last entry records the title vacant from 1 January 2000, with no further champion attested.
