PRIME Tag Team Title

Placard

Promotion   PRIME (2003)
Active   2003–2004
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PRIME's Tag Team Title was first won by Mark Blaze & Andy Magnum on 14 September 2003. The fed's own record carries 34 reigns on it, across 31 different champions. The longest is Crudely Civilized's, 134 days from 12 March 2004. Four of its reigns ended outside a match — handed over or vacated. Change in Spades held it at the end of the surviving record. PRIME suspended its tag division after Culture Shock 2010.
