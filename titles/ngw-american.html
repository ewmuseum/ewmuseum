Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Won first out of a seven-man battle royal by one of the fed's own founders four days after it opened, vacated a week later, and carried through thirty-five more reigns. It was vacated again in August 2003 after John Hitchin was written into a limo accident, and Pr0t0typ3 — later fired and put on the fed's own public blacklist — was holding it when the last title page went up.

The full reign ledger, built from /promotions/ngw/archive/american-title-history.html and cross-checked against /promotions/ngw/archive/champions-and-title-history-2004-11.html, is transcribed into this registry.
