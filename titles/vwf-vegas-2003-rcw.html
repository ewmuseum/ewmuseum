Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt the RCW invaders crowned for themselves rather than one the promotion handed out. G-Gangsta won it in a four-way on 17 January 2002 by outlasting Demonic, Blaze and the Real Deal, then defended it in a Last Man Standing match through three tables and a bloody 2 out of 3 falls brawl through the streets of an old Nevada ghost town that left both men and the referee knocked out.

Crowned by the RCW, an invading faction of former outside wrestlers (G-Gangsta, Demonic, Blaze and the Real Deal) inside the VWF in January 2002; the broadcast itself questions whether the VWF recognizes it as an official title.
