Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fed's own #2 title, billed as the North American 'Cyber' Championship in its early weeks before the qualifier dropped. It changed hands four times across the captured 1998-99 run — Lonewolf, then The Train (Wayne Rothschild), then The Multinational Rebel, then Johnny Sledge — and was vacant again on the fed's own 3 June 1999 rankings page.
