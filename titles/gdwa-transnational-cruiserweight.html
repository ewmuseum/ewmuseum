Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A belt with a rulebook of its own: defended every four weeks, open to any cruiserweight, and any contender above.500 who challenged had to be given a shot. It was crowned in a tournament final at the US Air Arena in Baltimore in July 1997, and its only recorded champion held it for more than a year while adding two other titles to it — the first wrestler in the fed to hold two belts at once, then three. It was vacated at Founder's Day Tradition II in August 1998. In January 2000 the fed announced a tournament to crown a new one.

Transcribed from the fed's own Lineages pages (promotions/gdwa/archive/lineages-current.html, promotions/gdwa/archive/lineages-1997.html); its defence rules are set out in the fed's own FAQ sheet (promotions/gdwa/archive/faq-sheet.html) and the new tournament announced on the Dragon's Lair marquee (promotions/gdwa/archive/dragons-lair.html).
