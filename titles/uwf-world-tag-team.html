Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Local NVA Company are its defining team, five reigns, more than any other; the UWF era's own longest reign belongs to the Resurrection (Digital Mortality & Moraless, 84 days). On 5 March 2003, Lyle “In Style” Wile won the belts partnered with a VR T-Shirt — the first inanimate object to hold a UWF title.
