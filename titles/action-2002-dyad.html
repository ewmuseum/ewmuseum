Action!'s Dyad Championship was first won by Clit 'n Candy on 25 August 2002. The fed's own record carries 7 reigns on it, across 7 different champions. The longest is Arcade & Son's, 126 days from 25 May 2003. The International Workrate Connection held it at the end of the surviving record.
