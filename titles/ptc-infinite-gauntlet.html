PTC Infinite Gauntlet Championship

aka Infinite Gauntlet Title, IG Championship

Placard

Promotion   PTC (2002)
Active   2005–2010
Cadence   standing

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The prize of PTC's Infinite Gauntlet, the forum's perpetual roleplay competition, in which each round's winner stayed on as champion. The Uber Judge took it in the first round on 4 June 2005; twenty-six more reigns followed, twenty-five champions in all, until the last round on 20 December 2010, when the host's replies named James von Doom the last champion. Alcaeus was stripped of it in May 2006, and two champions vacated it.

The fed's own table of champions counts 26 reigns by 24 champions and ends with Eegor; the twenty-seventh reign here is read from the host's replies of 20 December 2010 and is graded presumed.
