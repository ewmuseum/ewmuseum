Billed as held by Michelle McCool on WAR #7 (8 September 2008), where she retains by disqualification against Candice Michelle. Beth Phoenix won the title outright from McCool's own team in an 8-Diva Tag Team Ladder match at Summer Party (28 September 2008), and is still billed champion on the Sacrifice brand's own later champions list (February 2009). A WAR #16 commentary tease (5 April 2009) separately promises "Beth Phoenix defende [o título] contra Candice Michelle", suggesting Phoenix's reign continued at least that far, though under a title name this document does not itself use.
