Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The revived era's own third championship, restarted vacant in a May 2014 five-way melee that Sammy Devine won. Changed hands between Devine and Bruiser Breton twice across the Phenomenons' own touring season, then again to a name the fed's own title-history ledger never recorded a transition for — the ledger's own last word (28 Dec 2014, Devine) and the 2014 Year End Awards' own later snapshot (Feb 2015, Prince Wadjethotep) simply disagree, a gap in the surviving record, not a resolved handoff.

A "NAPW Heritage Championship" is also named once in the classic-era served corpus (Stone Zellor, Anniversary Assault II, 31 Oct 2007); whether it shares a lineage with this belt is unresolved.
