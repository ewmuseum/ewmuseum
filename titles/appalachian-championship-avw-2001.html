AVW Appalachian Championship

Placard

Promotion   AVW (2001)
Active   2001

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The promotion's second singles title, vacant at its launch in January 2001 and held by Dan Debacle by the end of February. His defence against Leevon Parks on 26 February 2001 was broken up by Seth Alexander's chair, and Alexander and Parks were booked to fight for the number-one contendership at Bluegrass Bash.
