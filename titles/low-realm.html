LOW Realm Championship

aka Realm Title

Placard

Promotion   LOW (2002)
Active   2003–2004

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Named for Realm, the weekly show it was defended on, this was Lights Out Wrestling's own mid-card championship and the one its record tracks most closely: Eric Russo, Jason Ortiz, Cyrus Skar, Malik Katsuya, The Demond, Ron Diamond and Brody Dahmer all held it. Cyrus Skar's own bio card records him as Realm Champion "until LOW closed for the 1st time" — the belt outlasting the fed's own first run — and he lost it on the fiftieth Realm without being pinned, stripped mid-match by the fed's own president for attacking his valet.
