Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

eVo's Cell Championship was first won by Jinx on 14 August 2002. The fed's own record carries 6 reigns on it, across 4 different champions. The longest is Damon O'Halloran's, 59 days from 7 August 2003. Two of its reigns ended outside a match — retired with the belt or vacated. eVo retired it on 9 November 2003.
