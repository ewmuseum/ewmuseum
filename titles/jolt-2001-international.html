Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The belt Erik Rave won at iNtense 10, which passed to Bane Loneheart, Azrael Ravenell, Reno Davis, who vacated it the night he won it at Thieves Honour, and Rush before Azrael Ravenell took it again at Rise of the Legends on 28 October 2001. He defended it into 2002; J-Con won the title from Trey Vincent by submission at Unwanted on 28 May 2002. The revival of 2012 opened with Rush as its champion; Jonathan Conspiracy beat him for it on Warriors 02, whose first review is dated 28 June 2012, and lost it to The Nashvillain at Divide & Conquer, where it was absorbed into the Relentless title.
