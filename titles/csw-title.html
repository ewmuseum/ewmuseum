Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

CSW's own top belt, contested from January 1996 as the "CSW World Title" and renamed the plain "CSW Title" that August. Tank won it first, eliminating Mercury in a battle royal, and said so himself afterwards — "Tank was a rookie when he came too the csw and became the 1st world champion." Nine independent-era reigns survive on the fed's own numbered ledger, all recorded here; six men held it, Mr. Ravishing twice. The Heartbreaker's own bio insists he was the second of them and Tank the man he beat, where the ledger puts Ace Stevens between the two — a disagreement kept rather than settled. Shane Curton was defending it in February 2004 when Pres Burns, having acquired the CSW Efed outright, stripped it from him and folded the lineage into his own CWF-Pro hierarchy; the belt's later CWF-Pro-branded life is recorded on CWF-Pro's own title row.

Source: csw-title.html/csw-national-title.html/csw-royal-title.html/csw-world-tag-team-title.html's own shared line: "Title is stripped by Pres Burns who has Acquired the CSW Efed".
