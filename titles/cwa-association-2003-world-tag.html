Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The CWA’s World Tag Team belts in 2003: Eternal Nightmares won a four-team match on 17 May, and the titles passed through BOD twice, Full House, Northern Exposure and Los Especialistos before Dark Forces took them on 1 December and Reality and Dark Ice Symphony followed in January 2004; the cards then show Dark Forces taking the belts back on 22 March 2004 and keeping them on 24 May.
