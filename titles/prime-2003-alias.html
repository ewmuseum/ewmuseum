Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PRIME's Alias Title was first won by JTF on 3 March 2004, who became its Xposure Champion; its first four champions carried it under names of their own — Xposure, roC-TV, PRIMEal Rage and PRIMEtime — and the fed's own cards and title log also bill it the Pulse Title and the Internet Title. The fed's own record carries 14 reigns on it, across 14 different champions. The longest is The Illustrious Face-Eater's, 189 days from 16 November 2005. Three of its reigns ended outside a match — retired with the belt or vacated. PRIME merged it into the 5 Star Title on 30 May 2007.
