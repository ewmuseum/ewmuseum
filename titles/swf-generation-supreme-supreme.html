Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A second top championship Phil Vibert announces on SWF Legends in June 2008, after the terms Richard Eisen put into the brand split attach every singles title to one brand and so tie the World Heavyweight Championship to Supreme TV. Vibert unveils the gold-and-platinum belt on SWF Legends in Week 4 of June 2008, calls it the most expensive in professional wrestling history, and sets the first champion to be decided at The Supreme Challenge 28, where the card puts Christian Faith against Brandon James for it. Christian Faith wins it at The Supreme Challenge 28, beating Brandon James with the Test of Faith sleeper, and Phil Vibert takes the belt from the referee to present it. Faith keeps it against Bloodstone and Eric Eisen on SWF Legends in Week 3 of July 2008, Troy Tornado in Week 4 and Texas Pete in Week 1 of August; Vengeance is booked against him for Welcome to the Jungle 2008, and Brandon James and Steve Frehley meet there for a shot at the belt at Under Control 2008. Faith keeps it against Vengeance at Welcome to the Jungle 2008, after which Vengeance puts him through the timekeeper's table, and against Bryan Holmes on SWF Legends in Week 3 of August; in Week 4 he keeps it by disqualification in a Threefold Threat with Steve Frehley and Rocky Golden, and Under Control 2008 is billed as a Threefold Threat for it among Faith, Brandon James and Steve Frehley.

Christian Faith2008-07 (month)—The first champion, who beat Brandon James with the Test of Faith sleeper at The Supreme Challenge 28 and received the belt from Phil Vibert. He kept it in a Threefold Threat against Bloodstone and Eric Eisen on SWF Legends in Week 3 of July 2008, against Troy Tornado on SWF Legends in Week 4 of July and against Texas Pete on SWF Legends in Week 1 of August 2008, and is booked against Vengeance for Welcome to the Jungle 2008. He beat Vengeance at Welcome to the Jungle 2008, after which Vengeance put him through the timekeeper's table, beat Bryan Holmes on SWF Legends in Week 3 of August 2008, and kept the belt by disqualification in a Threefold Threat with Steve Frehley and Rocky Golden in Week 4; he is booked in a Threefold Threat with Brandon James and Steve Frehley for Under Control 2008.
