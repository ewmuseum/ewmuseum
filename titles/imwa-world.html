A title that moved three times across one week in the surviving record: Amundson beat a seven-time champion, Bolt, for the belt; Mr Arse took it from Amundson on the Friday of the two-night Road to CWZ card; and the very next night Mr Arse lost a separate Loser Leaves iMWA match and left the belt vacant on his way out the door to the CWZ.
