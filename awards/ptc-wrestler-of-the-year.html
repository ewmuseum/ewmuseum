PTC Wrestler of the Year

Placard

Promotion   PTC (2002)

Year-by-year
YearRecipientDetail
2004Seymour Almasy, Saij
Over AWS Man (also known as Bill)
Shared. Seymour Almasy and Saij tied on 13 votes each of the 43 cast, and were declared co-holders of the award. Argyle took nominations by private message through November 2004 ("Character, not handler"; one to three picks, each with reasons), short-listed the five characters nominated more than once, and counted a private ballot sent to him by private message. Third was AWS Man (also known as Bill), then Jason Hughes, then Chris Storm. Each winner was promised a splash image in the site's random rotation.
2005Angelo Deville
Chosen from 21 self-nominated characters by a ballot sent privately to Argyle, each vote required to give reasons including at least one feat the nominee had not listed; voting closed at midnight on 31 December 2005. Deville put his own case forward on the forum's 2005 nomination thread on 9 December 2005, and the result was billed for PTC Radio's first 2006 edition, in mid-January 2006. Fellow nominee Rich Rollins campaigned for the award in character with a hype video, posted on 30 December 2005 and re-uploaded to YouTube in 2007 as "PTC WOTY 2005 Rollins"; its claim of the award for Rollins is his own campaign's, and the ballot went to Deville.
2003Chryste
The first PTC Wrestler of the Year, decided by members' vote in the 2003 PTC Awards; by her own count, she took 11 votes, one more than double the runner-up's. The award earned her a splash image in the site's random rotation.
2006
No official award. Self-nominations opened on 16 February 2007 and closed at the end of that month; Argyle later wrote that there was no official 2006 Wrestler of the Year because the nominations were too few and too thin, and that Doctor Curiosity's handler "ended up awarding it to himself". Doctor Curiosity crowned himself PTC Wrestler of the Year 2006 in character on 2 May 2007, and PTCpedia carries him as the unofficial winner. Tchu disputed the claim in character in December 2007.
2007Clinton Sage, Snow (nominated)
Nominations by private message to Argyle opened on 2 December 2007. No winner was declared: PTCpedia, captured in March 2008, gives 2007 as "To be determined". Clinton Sage's own handler posted on 28 March 2008 that Clinton Sage and Jason Snow "swept the votes", and in May 2008 that the two had "'unofficially' tied" for the award; that claim is his own.
