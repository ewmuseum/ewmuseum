MWE Roleplay of the Week

Placard

Promotion   MWE (2007)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MWE's award for the week's best roleplay, voted from the relaunch in August 2008 through December 2009 and carrying its own escalating prestige level, which reached Level 4 ("Excellent") in May 2009 on "In Tim We Trust" — a roleplay one of its own winners later called his personal pick for roleplay of the year. Ties were common and the award was routinely split two and, once, three ways.
