Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy newsgroup's own year-end poll, counted every January and posted back to the group: two hundred wrestlers placed in order, with a written entry for the top twenty-five in most years. Eight editions survive, ranking 1996 through 2004, and the compiler of the first two cross-referenced them himself — the 1997 edition names a wrestler's 1996 place thirty-two times, twice getting the number wrong. What the poll is worth now is not the order it put people in but the census it took by accident: 1,610 placings, and in every year but the first, each one tagged with the federation the wrestler was writing for.
