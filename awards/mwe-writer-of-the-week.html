MWE Writer of the Week

Placard

Promotion   MWE (2007)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The staff-side award, given for the best-written match on the week's card and named for the writer rather than the wrestlers. It replaced Match of the Week in September 2008, kept that award's habit of naming the match honored in its own opening line, and carried its own escalating prestige level.
