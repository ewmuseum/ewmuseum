MWE Wrestler of the Week

Placard

Promotion   MWE (2007)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Voted every show week from the relaunch in August 2008 to the last Monday Night Mayhem of 2009, and carried its own escalating prestige level — an award that climbed a rung whenever the staff judged a win worth it, reaching Level 4 ("Excellent") by the summer of 2009. Sinc Mercier, Nic Kraven, Chris Saint Blaise and Zack Crash each took it three times or more.
