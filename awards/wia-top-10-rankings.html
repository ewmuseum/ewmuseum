Promotion   WIA (2002)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WIA’s own staff ranked its top ten wrestlers with a paragraph of commentary apiece — praising roleplays, noting who’d stepped up and who was still waiting to debut. One clean edition survives whole, dated January 2003. A second, undated Top 10 sidebar survives on the fed’s own later news/front page (Wayback-captured 26 March 2003, bounded no earlier than 20 January 2003): eight names carry over, Spastic and AAH drop out, and David Valdez and Hunter Tazi appear for the first time — consistent with Spastic’s own move from wrestler to owner on 26 January — but nothing dates that second list precisely enough to ledger it as its own year-bestowal alongside the first, so it stands as prose here rather than a second row.
