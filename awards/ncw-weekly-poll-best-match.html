The fed's own recurring reader poll, voted weekly from 18 March to 21 October 1997 -- not an idle popularity contest but the engine of the fed's rankings: winners earned a Victory Point, and Victory Points decided who entered the Free-for-All. Structured here from 11 of the series' 13 known editions.
