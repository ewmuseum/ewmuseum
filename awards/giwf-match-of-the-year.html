1999Curt Raines, Hamish McGrubber
31%, the World Title Cage Match — a three-way tie for third at 23% each (Studog Escobar, Twiztid, the Dark Lords, Team Esco, Nephilim, Ace Thunder); no second place recorded in the ceremony's own recap table. The ceremony's own script names the match "the World Title Cage Match at Fear the Raine"; the following year's ceremony instead calls the same pairing's win a match "at March Maelstrom II" — the two GIWF ceremony scripts disagree with each other on the event name, not just with any outside source; recorded as an open discrepancy between two primary documents rather than silently resolved. CORRECTED from an earlier registry entry that credited the-phenomenons here, sourced only from a later, self-attested Hall of Fame induction boast. The actual GIWF ceremony transcript — a live, contemporary primary source — settles it: the Phenomenons placed in that three-way tie for third at 23%, not first. See giwf-2000-match-of-the-year's own provenance note for where the Phenomenons' real Match of the Year win actually landed.
2000The Phenomenons, Al Crud, Tom the Christmas Tree
47.06%, Venetian Home Rumble I — the Phenomenons vs. Al Crud & the joke gimmick Tom the Christmas Tree, fought at Christmas Clash '99 — over Slasher/CA$H's THC Title War (23.53%), the Nightmare Reflections Match (17.65%), and Al Crud/Pete Steele's Regal Rumble main event (11.76%). PROVENANCE NOTE, resolving a dating discrepancy: a Phenomenons Hall of Fame induction speech elsewhere self-attests a "1999 GIWF Match of the Year" win for this same Venetian Home Rumble; the actual match happened at a Dec 1999 show, but the GIWF's own annual-awards cycle crowned it at the ceremony covering "year 2000" (the 1999 ceremony's own Match of the Year, held only weeks earlier, had already gone to Curt Raines & Hamish McGrubber — see giwf-1999-match-of-the-year). Read together, the two contemporary ceremony transcripts show the HOF speech's own claim as misdated by one ceremony cycle, not fabricated: right match, wrong year named. Both rows stand, each receipted to its own ceremony's own primary transcript. Independently corroborated by a later UWF retrospective ("the battle... that had won the 2000 Match of the Year award") and a Christmas Clash 2000 preview calling Rumble I the favorite for the award.
