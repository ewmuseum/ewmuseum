GDWA Wrestler of the Month Award

Placard

Promotion   GDWA (1996)

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Given in two-month blocks rather than monthly, and always with the fed's own reason attached — a title won, a streak, a knee wrestled on. Ten citations survive across 1996, 1997 and 1998, twice naming two wrestlers for the same block.
