Web-recovery campaign.

A first tranche of early-1990s federations documented through material posted on the fantasy-wrestling newsgroup and its parent group, plus the newsgroup's own institutional record. It brought 7 wings into the museum, active 1989-2020, which together hold roughly 378 documents today.

Held under this accession

  • GCW (1992–95) — Global Championship Wrestling
  • NeWA (Nippon, 1991–1992) — Nippon Electronic Wrestling Association
  • UeWF (1991) — Universal Electronic Wrestling Federation
  • WeWF (1990) — World Electronic Wrestling Federation
  • RSPW (1989) — rec.sport.pro-wrestling
  • RSWA (1990) — RecSport Wrestling Association
  • SJPW (1997) — Super Japan Pro-Wrestling
