Accession

0159

Private donation · Contributed by Argyle.

A former owner's own archive, handed over rather than found on the public web, apart from the parts filled in from the Wayback Machine's and Common Crawl's copies of the federations' own sites, among them all of the Fans Wrestling Organization's roleplays and biographies: the backstage database the federations ran on — Primetime Championship Wrestling, PRIME, Sin City Championship Wrestling, Global Championship Wrestling, Global Wrestling, Old School Wrestling, Action! Wrestling, RUSH Wrestling, New Frontier Wrestling, Five Star Wrestling, PURE, Pro Wrestling Circuit, RUSH Pro Wrestling, the Underground Brawler's Club, ABF, All-Star Championship Wrestling, the Atlantic Wrestling Club, eVolution, Evolution Pro Wrestling, thReat and Primetime Central's tournaments — and the backups kept of the family's and allied federations' websites.
