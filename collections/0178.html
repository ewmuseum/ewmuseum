Web-recovery campaign.

A Wayback recovery of RUSH Pro Wrestling's own sites: its news desk on its first site, rpw.sheepsden.com (November 2003 to May 2004), and on its later address, rushpro.net (May to July 2004), read as the fed's own news posts; its write-ups of its weekly television show, Adrenaline (November 2003 to July 2004), on rpw.sheepsden.com, www.angrypete.net and rushpro.net, read as the fed's own record of each show; six posts of its roleplay board on rushpro.net (May and June 2004); and three of its own site pages: its About page, its TV Archive and Champions page of December 2003 and its closing notice of September 2004. The donated backstage database (lot 0159) does not hold the news or the site pages, and holds the write-ups and the six roleplays only as copies taken from these captures. It stands behind the RPW wing's served news archive, its served shows, six of its served roleplays and its site pages.
