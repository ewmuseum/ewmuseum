Web-recovery campaign.

The 1992 Usenet record, read out of a set of subscription CD-ROM discs holding an early newsgroup's own traffic — the fantasy federations already active three years before the group had one of its own. It brought 13 wings into the museum, active 1988-2020, which together hold roughly 160 documents today.
