A request from the museum. We will never ask you to support this site. But when we lose one of our own, we will ask you to support the people they leave behind. Jeff Ward wrote Angelo Deville for most of a decade, and was PTC's Wrestler of the Year for 2005. He died in June, aged 44. His family has a fundraiser; if his work meant something to you, or you just want to support one of our own, please consider giving.
