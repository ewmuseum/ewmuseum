Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A federation calling itself simply RAW announced its own first broadcast on 5 March 2000, a magazine-style recap show framed as the return of three earlier e-federations merged into one operation. Its own FAQ, posted two weeks later, named the roster it had already assembled and set a cap of roughly twenty-five wrestlers, run out of an affiliated e-wrestling school.

Two shows anchored the weekly schedule: Sunday Night Main Event Live, a full play-by-play card, and RAW in Retrospect, a bi-weekly recap and interview program filling the off weeks. Three singles titles were named at launch — a top championship, a secondary title, and an entry-level title for newer talent — with a tag division and a hardcore title planned once interest allowed.

The federation's first Pay-Per-View, Spring Beatings, was posted complete in three parts at the start of May 2000, followed in July by Battlezone, a single-elimination tournament finishing inside a three-level steel cage. A weekly results column, RAW at WAR, carried the promotion's own storylines from midyear through the rest of the year, alongside a September Pay-Per-View, Internecine.

By November 2000 the operation described itself as a franchise: RAW UK, opened that May, was joined that month by a new Australian branch, RAW-stralia, with two further regional siblings announced as forthcoming. The federation framed the expansion as proof of its own health after a brief scheduling gap earlier that autumn.

Through 2001 and into 2002 the federation ran its weekly business through a members' digest on its own mailing list, renamed Let Me Tell You Something: roleplays, results written up as the RAW Report, and a European tour that took its cards to San Marco Square in Venice and the San Siro Stadium in Milan, home of the Aggression pay-per-view of 13 January 2002.

Its last documents are February 2002 digests building toward a Ring Dreams card. By April the federation had closed; a sister territory's FAQ records that with RAW's closing its regional promotions regrouped as an NWA-style alliance, carrying the RAW name on without it.
