RAW (EWU, 2000–2002) — Roleplays

The promo RAW doesn't want you to see

14 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No one else wants to see it either ... but here it is ... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [RAW World Champion, the "Outlaw" Travis LaGrange, stands in front of a RAW banner, black cowboy hat pulled down over his head in a black-and-white "Suicide is your only option" T-Shirt and his trademark black duster, blue jeans and cowboy boots. He slowly lifts a mic to his mouth. He seems somewhat depressed, but willing to tell the story of what happened on RAW Aggression.]
LaGrange: Last week, I got my shoulders pinned to the mat for the first time here in RAW ... most people might call that a miracle ...
some might even say it was a fluke ... but that's besides the point ... I got beat, plain and simple ... It wasn't the fact that it happened that has me here today ... no, I've been beaten before by a lot of people in a lot of other places ... you see, it was the *reaction* that the loss got, that made me sick to my stomach ... the cheering, the love, the adulation ... for the Devil ... for Douglas McCray.
[Travis pauses to recollect. Reasoning with himself, to explain McCray's fan reaction.] You know, I could understand a little bit why you hate me so much, because a little more than three months ago, I sold out. I admit it.
And for that, I'm sorry. I'm sorry to each and every fan who bought a ticket. I'm sorry to the boys in the back and I'm sorry to my family.
The fact is, I became the "Sexy Boy" for personal gain. I made an ass out of myself ... again for personal gain. Never mind the fact that it made me more money that I can possibly ever spend ... never mind it saved a few years of my health ... you people weren't interested in that ... you never really wanted to hear the truth, did you?
You people would come up to me on the street and ask 'Why Travis? Why? Why don't you just drink, chew tobacco, kick ass and be the Outlaw?' [Almost to himself, with contempt, but very audible to the microphone.] Why don't you just be the Outlaw ... be the Outlaw, you'd say ... well, ya got the Outlaw. [Back to normal speaking voice.] Ya got the carnage, ya got the hatred, you got the lack of respect for authority and you got the unpredictability ... You got it, and now, it's a "why" of a different breed ... Hmmph ... Why ...
"Why", is a word that has been used many times when describing my career ... few people who ask the question, ever really comprehend what they are truly asking, and few people want to hear the truthful answer to the question when they ask it.
But ... in order to truly understand me, and the question you ask, let's go back a little bit, shall we?
[LaGrange pauses to collect his thoughts. Then philosophically begins. Almost like a history professor.] You know ... RAW has had a storied existence ... the champions ... the legends ... the stars ... you can name them all ... "Kodiak" Vic
Creed, OCD, Tonnage, William Bennett, the Andrazoons, Neil McDougall, Stevie Ray Murdoch, Kirk Maclean, the Singapore Bandits, Spikyjim, Despair, Deadboy, Eddie Drysdale, John Williams .... all names, all made an impact in this sport ... RAW has been around for two years now, and although it hasn't always been that way, RAW is, without a doubt, one of the most prestigious wrestling federations on the planet
... people from around the world want to compete here ... it's said that if you really want to find out how good you are, you go to RAW
... If you really want to test your intestinal fortitude, if you really want to see if you can sustain a workrate ... go to RAW.
It's the talk of the wrestling world, they say! And do you want to know why? It's because of me. [LaGrange tilts his cowboy hat back, continuing his philosophical tone.] There's the truth ... That's right, face facts, before I came here ... RAW was the punch-line to a joke. Vik Ventressa, Chase Williams, the Deities, Mike Slater, the American Patriot, Indiana Cassanova, the Gods of Destruction ... the eight-man ladder matches in a public pay toilet ... the mindless decisions ...
the lackluster pay-per-view numbers ... the half-filled houses ... over-booking that would make the Enron accountants look like choirboys ... T.V. ratings that would make watching two frogs copulate look like a hit show ... Yep, before I came ... RAW ... [Travis explodes.] ... WAS A JOKE!! [Travis quickly back to calm disgust ... pauses to catch his bearings.] That's right, I said it, RAW was a joke that the wrestling world pointed and laughed at ... And back then ... perhaps the biggest joke of them all ... the one who got the biggest guffaws and howling laughter ... was your champion ... A chain-smoking idiot who of all things put horns on a title belt.
Horns? On a title belt? ... A man ... who *thought*, he was more important than he actually was. A *man* who, when he had forsaken the friendly confines of this then *minor* league known as RAW, found out how "dominant" he really was ...
[Travis chuckles all too briefly as if he really didn't think what he said was funny.] You see, Doug McCray would have you believe that he invented hardcore wrestling ... smoking cigarettes ... cussing and drinking ... along with the light bulb, the telephone the home computer and several other essential items we as civilized humans need to get along ... the dominant all-knowing champion .... but we all know the truth, don't we
Douglas? We all know the real story ... the truth ... ... The whole wrestling world knew the truth ... it was on display for everyone to see in a World-wide Pay-per-view event called Masters of the Ring ... you remember it, don't ya, Doug McCray? You remember the story ...
You should remember, 'cause it's short and easy to remember ... but you don't and the fans don't, because it's the truth and not some damn fairy tale ...
Anyway, so this grand story goes ... in this world-wide, pay-per-view extravaganza ... Doug McCray, RAW's most "dominant" so-called champion ... the Devil Himself ... drank whiskey, pissed on graves, made a general ass of himself and got his ass beaten TWICE!! IN THE
SAME DAMN MATCH!!! In front of the whole damn world, he got beaten twice, in the same_damn_match. And when it happened .... [almost pleading for people to understand] ... THEY LAUGHED AT YOU, RAW!!! They laughed a huge belly laugh and looked at you with some sort of sh*t-eating grin ... they knew something you didn't, they cackled and they pointed ... look at the minor-league champion, they said! Look at the *minor-league* federation, they said!
But instead of rebounding and buckling down like the true champion he so thinks he is. What did he do? Just *what* did your beloved RAW champion do after suffering such a crushing, humiliating defeat? What did your champion *do* when he was a beaten man?
C'mon, Doug, you know the answer ... you *know* the truth ... Did he stand up and take his game up a notch? Did he charge back into the ring and renew his dedication?
NO!!! He folded like a cheap WAL-Mart card table under the weight of Big Gay Bill and Gigantor having loving prison sex on top of it!!! That's right. You heard me. He packed it in. He quit. The Doug McCray you thought you knew, thought he was too good for RAW ... the Devil, thought he was too good for its wrestlers ... he *thought* he could take months off at a time without fear of repercussions, [sarcastic] 'cause he was soooooh much better than every one else. He carried the belt with a cowardice and talent usually reserved for a Possum ... and then ... then our Hellhero, when there came a champion greater than him, a man who got more reaction than he ever did, ran like a scaled dog. And now, he has the utter audacity to say the belt meant more when he had it? How I'm just a pale imitation of the Devil
Himself? BULLSH*T DOUG!!! BULLSH*T LIES!!!! But you people don't want to hear the truth, do you? You people don't want to hear the truth, 'cause, quite frankly, you people loathe it
... that's right, you *want* to be lied to, you want to embrace the very Devil, that six months ago you loathed ... you don't want the truth, 'cause you can't stomach it ... BUT BY GAWD, I'M GOING TO
FORCE FEED IT TO YOU!!! [Exploding. Hat pops off.] THE TRUTH IS ... I fought every card, every pay-per-view, against anyone and EVERYONE ... EVERY GAWDDAMN DAY DOUG!! EVERY GAWDDAMN DAY!!
THE TRUTH IS ... I AM A STAR!!! In house shows, at TV tapings, on LIVE PAY-PER-VIEW!! Every single event, not missing a beat, never getting pinned, over and over and over-again!! Time and time again I walked out the champ ... a fighting champ! A champ that was and still is, revered the world over!
I WAS NEVER PINNED!!! I WAS INVINCIBLE!!! I was a man of my word ... I beat Tonnage. I beat Bennett. I destroyed ... AND BURIED WAR!!! I AM_THE_MAN!!! But you see even with the Devil at our doorstep ... all was not lost ... no, no, not at all ... Because I showed up and set the fed on its ear ... Doug was too good for me then, so I grabbed the first title I saw. You all remember what happened next ... I ripped through the competition, he ran for the hills, I won the World Title and ya know what?
[Almost waiting for an invisible person to answer.] The laughing stopped ... the jokes about RAW stopped ... it wasn't funny to bash RAW anymore ... the fed wasn't a joke ... don't you see?
I made the laughing stop ... the laughing stopped ... [Smiles. Almost beaming.] .... and the accolades began. Need proof?! [LaGrange counts off each on his fingers.] Travis LaGrange was number six in the wrestling world! RAW was one of the best wrestling federations IN THE WORLD!!! Houses were filled!!
Television ratings were almost astronomical!! Pay-per-view buy rates shot through the GAWDDAMN roof!! Eric Helmroy made money hand over fist ... every wrestler made money HAND OVER FIST ...
Because of who? [Anger boiling.] BECAUSE OF ME!!! ME GAWDDAMIT!! What a glorious federation RAW was! What a champion Travis LaGrange is! What a geniuses those bookers are!! What a show Aggression is!!
Why? [Explodes.] BECAUSE OF ME!!! GAWDDAMN YOU!!! ME!!! [Gasping for breath within his anger. Almost pleading his case again.] Not Douglas McCray ... not the Devil ... but Travis "GAWD-DAMN" LaGrange!! Hell, much unlike Douglas, even when I went outside of RAW, I WAS STILL THE GAWDDAMN MAN!! I kicked ass, chewed tobacco AND TOOK NAMES!!! People cheered! People who never talked about RAW before couldn't keep their gawddamn mouths shut!! Did ya see? Did ya see what
Travis LaGrange is doing in RAW? Did ya see the Outlaw, the Gawddamn Redneck Assassin, the Walking Target, the Homicidal Artist?!! I, and I ALONE, took this gawd-forsaken fed out of a burning [heh] hell ... and took it to the very pinnacle of the largest mountain in the business. [LaGrange pauses, matter-of-factly] And just where was Douglas McCray while I was ruling the wrestling roost? [Snap angry] LICKING HIS WOUNDS THAT'S WHERE!!! [Ranting, voice picking up serious tempo] Licking his wounds and crying over a loss he was destined to have ... a loss which he deserved ... a loss that revealed the Devil only to be a punch-line to an old joke that had long since died when I became the champion.
And ya know what?! I turn my back for only a split-second, and the cruel, Devilish joke had returned ... and the laughing started again ... but they weren't laughing at RAW anymore ... oh no ... they *sure* as hell weren't laughing at *me* ... they were laughing at Douglas McCray ... they were *laughing* in the face of the Devil ... again.
Yep after a season he's loosed on RAW *again* .... with [sarcasm] GREAT F*CKING FANFARE ... and you ... YOU mindless idiots in the stands embrace him like he's some kind of demonic savior ... he steals my fans ... he steals my friend Neil ... he steals my GAWDDAMN legacy
... he cheats me out of a life-long mark on his girlfriend's pathetic pasty white ass, he cheats me out of attention ...he lies about me ...
he lies, he cheats, he steals, just like the Devil, and you people lap it up and laud his return like he's some kind of f*cking conquering hero!
He's no hero! He's a JOKE!! He's no conquerer! HE'S A GAWDDAMN LOSER!!! I'm the champion, dammit! I'M THE CHAMPION!! So you beat me, Doug ... whoo-hoo, so what ... I bust my ass every day, every show .. and the fans, the fans of RAW piss all over me just because you get a measly win in a measly tag match?!
WELL YOU AND THE RAW FANS CAN GO TO HELL!!! You and your families, your pathetic insignificant lies and your pissant little jobs. All to hell.
I may not act like it, but I've got feelings to ya know ... I BLED for you!! I BUSTED MY ASS for you!! I almost DIED for you!! But that's not enough ... it's never enough, is it? Doug just can't be satisfied to take the fans from me with his lies, oh no, he has to top it all off, by having the nerve to say that you were RAW's best champion?! You may be able to fool all of the fans all of the time, Douglas ... but ya can't fool me ... you can't fool those outside of your little RAW occult ...
[Travis laughs a crazed chuckle.] You see ... I know, we know, that the Devil lies. It's his nature. He lies about how great a champion he was, then he neglects to mention how little he actually defended the belt. He lies about how good he was, how "dominant" he was, then fails to mention how a certain member of the Keenan clan wiped his ass all over the mat. That's right, Doug
... now you're exposed ... exposed to the world ... and guess what ... THE DEVIL LIES!! HE LIES about Travis LaGrange and what a paper champion he was, how he's just holding the belt for HIM ... How I used the system ... used the system?! USED THE SYSTEM?!!
[LaGrange stops ... toning down, but not hiding his anger.] You wanna say I used the system to become champion, Douglas? Like it's some damn big secret?! Fine, I used the system, guilty as charged ...
But if I used it, Doug, you ABUSED it. You spit in its face, you SCREWED its wife, you KILLED it and you PISSED on its GAWDDAMNED GRAVE !!! ... YOU HAD NO DAMN RIGHT TO!! NO RIGHT!!! YOU DIDN'T EARN IT!! YOU DIDN'T EARN THE RIGHT TO BE CALLED RAW'S BEST CHAMPION!!! I DID!! I DID DAMMIT!! ME!! [Flashing between emotions again.] You lied, Doug ... you lied to the fans ... you lied to me ... You lie and piss and you moan ... and YOU ABUSED ... you abused the hard-working wrestlers and its staff, just for your own amusement and ego ...AND THEY STILL LOVE YOU!!! YOU ABUSED the fans of this federation ... AND THEY STILL CHEER YOU!!!
Like mindless lemmings, they leap to their deaths in tribute to a man who is no more worthy of their worship than Mike Slater himself.
Forget what the Outlaw has done ... forget that he's the best this fed has ever seen! Forget that he's respected around the world! Forget that he MADE this fed!! Here in RAW, I'm a distant second to the lyin', cheatin', stealin' and chain-smokin' Devil ...
[sad, frustrated] Damn you. Damn you, Doug McCray ... damn you to hell ... damn you and the lies you tell ...
[Exasperated.] All the lies ... the deceptions ... you deserve the belt? Deserve? De-serve?!! HOW DARE YOU!!! YOU GOT EVERYTHING YOU DESERVED, YOU EGOMANICAL BASTARD!!! You got everything you deserved and then some ... You DESERVED to be humiliated in front of a World-wide audience at a supercard!! You DESERVED the broken leg Garland and I gave you! You
DESERVED every, single beatdown Spiky and I give you! Leah Maynard *deserves* to get her ass lit, to suffer in pain and abject agony, to be scarred for life .... JUST FOR BEING IN THE VICINITY OF SUCH A LOW-DOWN DIRTY LYING SON-OF-A-BITCH LIKE YOU!! And by GOD, you *deserve* to get your ass handed to you by me at Ring Dreams .... [Clears his throat, almost mocking.] Douglas McCray. Some great champion ... some great performer ... [Travis spins and puts two tin foil horns on the belt, then dances around like an idiot and singing to the tune of Devil with the Blue
Dress] Devil with the Horned Belt, Horned Belt, Horned Belt ... Devil with the Horned Belt on! YEAH!! Look at the idiot with the horned belt ...
Mike Slater, Vik Ventressa ... let the parade of flatbacks begin!! [Screaming at the top of his lungs as if he's arguing his point.] DON'T ALL OF YOU GET IT?!! YOU ... WERE ... A ... DAMN ... JOKE RAW!!!! A JOKE!!! A GAWDDAMN JOKE!! [Calms himself quickly, with a manical chuckle, almost eerily continues.] And not even a good one ... [Screaming again, pleading for the camera to understand] WHAT'S THE MATTER WITH YOU PEOPLE??!! DON'T YOU SEE??!! CAN'T YOU SEE?!! [Betrayed, quiet whisper.] ... can't you see the truth ... [Flabbergasted.] Are you tired of success? Are you tired of being number one? Have the accolades become too much for you to handle?! Are you sick of finally being able to admit that you're a RAW fan in public?
Do you *really* want to dip back down into the depths of apathy after tasting interest for this long? .... Back to the days of the
Ventressas, Slaters, American Patriots and RAW-ayl Rumbles on every other card?! The guys who wrestle ladder matches with broken legs, twisting in the wind?! Girls who miraculously heal from a third-degree, scarring wound in less than two weeks?!! Hiring of physicians who make Star Trek's doctors look like Old West hack artists?!!
How about a 14-man, best two-out-of-three falls, bowing, martial-arts, respect-fest, over-booked, ladder match, boiler room, napalm, exploding C4 board, brawl for old times sake, huh?!
[LaGrange opens arms wide, gesturing, speaking grandiose, getting frustrated, angry all emotions merging into one ball of hate.]
IS THIS WHAT YOU WANT?!! TO BE THE JOKE OF THE WRESTLING WORLD?!! AGAIN?!! IS THIS WHAT YOU WANT?!! IS IT?!! [LaGrange stops to catch his breath, breathing heavily. Hate morphs to a combination of depression, betrayal and sadness.]
Fans ... you ungrateful pieces of crap ... I single-handedly dip you out of the muck and sh*t of your mindless lack-luster existence ...
and you want to run back and woller in it?! Is that what you want? To duck your head in the sh*t?!! 'Cause if it is ... if *this* truly is what you, the fans, want ...
[LaGrange snaps to crazed emotion. Talking almost too fast to be sane. Almost accommodating] ... Then I'll be glad to step aside and hand *your* Devil the belt ... I'll be glad to do that ... I'd be glad to *hand* him the belt ... [Snap mild angry, ranting] Because the whole wrestling world knows gawddamn well that I'd beat his ass until my neck hurt!! Then I'd beat him again for makin' it hurt!!! I'd force his boney, pasty white, little shoulders to the mat for a three count after a well placed Traildriver or have the poor bastard, howling in pain in a Brazos Valley Backbreaker, and tappin'
the mat so fast that he'd burn a hole in it!! [Back to depression-sad-betrayed] But no ... you don't want that do you ... you don't want to see your Hellhero in abject pain ... you don't want reality, you want fantasy ... you don't want the truth, you want lies. You don't want the light, you want the darkness ... you don't want quality or dominance. You want the horns back on the belt.
You want the RAW World Championship to go from a respected piece of wrestling gear, held with the utmost prestige ... to a damn pissant novelty act suited for a tin-can alley operation that operates in back-water high school gyms, Elks Lodges and f*cking pool halls.
No, you don't want the Outlaw anymore ... you want the f*cking Paper Demon Champ. [Flabbergasted. Almost swinging back and forth, denying.] Do you *really* want that RAW? Do you really want to be a joke again? [Almost like a spurned lover, who doesn't want to admit that he's hurt.] 'Cause I've got other options RAW ... I've got other suitors ... I've got people callin' me every day and night ... people that covet Travis
LaGrange ... people that desperately want me to spike their ratings and buy-rates ... BIG, BIG PEOPLE IN THIS BUSINESS!!! People so powerful, it'd make your gawddamn head explode ...
I don't need *you* RAW ... you need *me* ... you *need* me ... You *need* me and you *need* Spikyjim. You *need* the Suicide Kings. You need us to give you legitimacy. Greatness. Someone to look up to ... Because without us, you know down deep in your heart, that you're nothing more than a bad comedy act ... a black-faced, vaudevillian impersonation that has long outlived it's entertainment value ... an old joke that everyone is in on ... except you, RAW ... except you ...
[LaGrange pauses and takes a long, long look at the RAW World Title. Almost sad.] But if your wish is to embrace your past ... if you want to welcome home the the-eliminator-danny-williams with open arms ... far be it for me to stand in the way of someone so damn foolish.
It's your call RAW ... you can't have us both. Because at The Big Card ... it's not going to be about World Championships ... it's not about revenge or respect ... it's damn sure not about some insignificant piece of ass ...
It's about a choice. A choice only you, the fans of RAW, can make. And that choice is, quite simply ... Him ... or me. [LaGrange takes another long look at his RAW World Championship belt, then throws down the belt and walks off the stage. The camera zooms into to frame the belt ... then the screen fades to black.]
