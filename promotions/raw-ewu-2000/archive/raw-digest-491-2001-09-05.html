(Part One) Flashbulbs continued to pop as RAW's world champion, Travis LaGrange, paused on the ramp being supported by "Kodiak" Vic Creed. The pair ducked through the curtain to thunderous applause, but were immediately met by a throng of people behind the curtain.
The people began to surround LaGrange and Creed, almost suffocating the new champ, touching him and asking him a barrage of questions.
"What's all of this?" LaGrange thought as the mass of humanity closed around him and his head began to swirl.
"Travis, do you have representation?" asked one. "Mr. LaGrange," said another. "We need to talk to you!" "Travis! My room number is 512!" shouted a young woman. "Outlaw! How does it feel to be the World Champion?!" questioned a reporter. LaGrange began to get physically sick as blood was running down the back of his throat, thanks in part to a busted nose which made swallowing hell on earth. The blood on his face was beginning to crust, his legs were completely dead and his head hurt a little more than usual after a match.
Creed picked up the pace as Travis reached and felt the back of his skull. The Outlaw quickly got a little queasy when he felt the dull side of several thumbtacks. He knew where the sharp side of the push-pins were, and he also knew that it wasn't going to be a piece of cake getting them out.
"Heh, and some people think they have headaches," LaGrange thought. "This is definitely not Excedrin headache number nine." The pair made a quick turn to the office of the doctor on duty and entered the door. Creed sat LaGrange down on the table and with one motion, the Alaskan
Juggernaut shoved the sea of humanity surrounding the door aside, exited and closed the door.
"Let's see what we got here, Travis," said resident RAW physician Dr. Wayne Gillman. Dr. Gillman probed the cuts on LaGrange's face as the Outlaw gritted his teeth in pain. Travis coughed several times from the swallowed blood as an aide handed him a beer. LaGrange choked down several gulps as he propped himself up.
"Travis," said Gillman. "This eyebrow is going to need a new set of stitches and I'm going to have to get a set of pliers to pull those tacks out, they're embedded pretty deep."
LaGrange nodded in agreement, but couldn't help but wonder to himself. Where was the champagne? Where was the celebration?
This was definitely not what he saw on television as a kid, but then again, no one ever used tacks or fire back then. And in his current condition, he knew he couldn't properly enjoy it anyway.
As Travis' mind wondered, the door popped open quickly,and in poked the head of Eric Helmroy, a member of RAW's prestigious board of directors.
An all around slimey human being, in Travis' opinion, Helmroy had never taken an interest in LaGrange since he had joined the company, he just thought he was another redneck wrestler.
Now that Travis was champ, and along with William Bennett, had just popped the company's largest Pay-per-View buy-rate ever, that changed rather quickly. LaGrange knew Helmroy was already salivating for the rematch. Travis, on the other hand, was sick enough already, he didn't need this.
"Hey! Travis!" shouted Helmroy in his usual happy, sing-songy fashion. "What?" LaGrange said, barely over a whisper. "The board needs to see you after you get done in here," said Helmroy. "It's important." Travis nodded reluctantly as Dr. Gillman began stitching up his eyebrow. His eyebrow was throbbing and it hurt as each needle stroke went through his skin, but this was nothing compared to the pain he was going to encounter in that Board of Directors meeting, he chuckled to himself.
"You okay, Travis?" Dr. Gillman asked as the champion's eyes began to glaze over. "Uhhhh," was Travis' only reply as he leaned beside the table and threw up in a bucket. Gillman stopped the stitches and rushed over to look at Travis' eyes to check for a concussion.
"I'm alright," lied LaGrange as the doctor finished up his stitches. "Leave me alone. I'm alright."
Gillman relented and for what seemed like hours, he wrapped Travis' head, cleaned cuts, removed thumbtacks and treated burns. The rubbing alcohol's smell filled every corner of the room and made him feel like his whole body was on fire.
Blood stained and shredded and burned, his ring attire was ruined, but Travis chuckled, since it was only blue jeans and a T-Shirt and could easily be replaced.
He was sure he could give it to Ricky to sell on e-bay for a hefty price. Travis knew Ricky meant no harm with his failed attack and would quickly forgive him after a severe, rage-induced tongue-lashing. Wait a second, LaGrange thought. Where is Rick? Is he okay?
"He took a hell of a bump," thought Travis of his diminutive, multiple personalitied cousin. "I hope he's alright."
Dr. Gillman finished up LaGrange's dressing as the Outlaw walked over to his locker to put on a new set of clothes. "Alright, Trav," said Gillman. "That's it. You're done. Anything else?" His head was still swirling from what he was now sure was a concussion, but that could be looked at later.
A personal one-on-suit visit from the board didn't happen to a young wrestler every day, so LaGrange wanted to show for the meeting without a delay.
Heavily bandaged, Travis quickly slapped on his clothes, and mentally prepared himself to meet with
Helmroy and the rest of the suits. "It's good to be King," Travis said to himself as he laughed out loud. ________________________________________________________________________ ________________________________________________________________________ Message: 3 Date: Tue, 4 Sep 2001 07:45:51 -0700 (PDT) From: Travis LaGrange < [detail withheld] > Subject: It's Good to Be King? (Part Two) The new RAW World Champion, Travis LaGrange, had just finished dressing and had exited the door to the
make-shift physician's room. His head was wrapped in gauze, and blood was crusted in his hair, but his Houston Astros' jersey and new blue jeans made him feel clean somehow and at least presentable to the Board of Directors. He turned the corner and saw the hallway loaded with people.
"Shit," LaGrange thought to himself as he reversed field. He turned to go the other direction as few people noticed him in his bandages and his new set of clothes. As he turned, however, he was immediately startled with a light impact on his frame.
"Travis! I'm so glad you're okay!" shrieked his girlfriend Tanya Maynard as she gave him a huge bear hug.
"I'm okay, I'm okay," LaGrange replied reassuringly. How many times did he have to tell this lie tonight? He just wished people would quit asking altogether. Travis smiled as he returned the hug the best way he could since his energy was completely gone. He put his arm around Maynard and steadied himself as the pair walked to the conference room where he would meet with
Helmroy and the rest of the Board. "I gotta meet with Eric and the rest of the board," LaGrange said as the two came to a stop in front of the conference room. "Will you check on Rick and
Tiny?" "Sure, Travis," said Maynard. "Then what?" "Wait here for Vic, Gar and Ronny and we'll go out later." "Are you sure you'll feel like it?" "If I don't," LaGrange laughed. "I'll be dead." LaGrange turned and opened the door to the conference room and entered. Seated around an oval table were
Helmroy and several other people Travis had never seen before. "Have a seat, Travis," Helmroy said quickly. Travis laid his belts down on the table and plopped down in a chair, focusing his brain. He knew he had to keep up with what is going on and he knew he had to hide his injury.
There was no way in the world he was going to compromise his current, newfound position in the company. This time, he had the ball in his court, and he wasn't about to blow it.
"We wanted to talk to you about your career," smiled Helmroy. "As you know, you're three-month contract is up a week from now and we consider it a priority to sign you to a long-term deal ... But first, we'd like to discuss some changes."
"Changes?!" exclaimed LaGrange. "What changes?! I just popped a damn huge buy-rate for you bastards. Our ratings are goin' through the roof, no thanks to you dumbasses. You boys were wantin' me ta hold me down! I made my own spot and you know it!"
"Whoa there, cowboy," laughed Helmroy. "Just calm down. We know your work and we admit our mistake.
These changes are nothing major, trust me ... Fred?" A board member stands, opens his briefcase and clears his throat. "Mr. LaGrange," started the man known as Fred. LaGrange would later find out he was the head of marketing for RAW. "You, in three short months, have become one of the most popular wrestlers in the federation. You're hitting the key 18-25 demographic and with this defender of the downtrodden act, you're numbers in that area are going through the roof."
"It's not an act," LaGrange scowled. "You people don't get it. It's me. 'Sides yer just blowin' smoke up my ass."
"No really," Fred replied. "And you're numbers with 18-25 year-old women is downright outstanding. You're bringing us those viewers in droves. They love this underdog persona. According to exit polls at our events, they find something attractive about you, they just can't put their finger on it."
"In fact," said Helmroy. "We want to capitalize on this. And if you'll do it, you'll be handsomely rewarded with a [name withheld], long-term contract."
"I don't know," said LaGrange. "How much are we talking about here?" "Well, let's just put it this way," said Helmroy. "We'll give you one that will put the Time Warner contracts to shame." LaGrange gulped. He knew this was a lot of money and he also knew it was for a lot less work. How could you lose? But wait, he thought, they could be pulling the old bait and switch. And what about the promises he made to his New Breed teammates?
He owed it to them to get as much money and power as he could, and if that meant listening to a Board push, then so be it ...
"So ... what about this change?" quizzed LaGrange. ________________________________________________________________________ ________________________________________________________________________ Message: 5 Date: Tue, 4 Sep 2001 13:48:57 -0400 From: [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > Subject: Tag Challenge [The scene opens shortly after the conclusion of this week's RAW-C TV broadcast as two-time RAW-Carolina Tag Team Champion Mark Adams and his manager,
