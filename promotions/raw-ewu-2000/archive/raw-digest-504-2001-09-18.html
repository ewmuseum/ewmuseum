He is sitting in the corner staring at a tape of the last RAW show. The main event has just ended and a great man has left us.* Despair: Perhaps it's time to make slight amends for past deeds. I have bled every man that dared cross me. I have been the man to run two other men out of RAW, destroy the Power title and then brutalized the man that founded it.
I make my secret about, I enjoy every moment. But you see, in the light of the past few weeks things have changed. I will not repeat them for I'm certain we all know what has occured. Pity, so many innocents mangled in with those that truely deserved to perish. So many trying to right the wrong dying in the process. And then on the homefront one of our own has passed on as well. It is a retrospective moment to be assured, even for me.
*Despair runs his hand over his now bald head. The skin is still burned from his last match with SpikeyJim.* Despair: Kudos Jim. You did two things few to no one has ever done. First was you sent me to the hospital for injuries-something only a handfun have ever done in my career. Second you managed to make me into a human torch. I must admit, I've been burned but never like that. . . . And the funny thing
I rather enjoyed it. It was quite the experience and the pain was simply wonderful. But you see, perhaps I've over looked something. For the first time I feel as though I made an error in my judgement. I have regrets
Jim about the funny thing is that usually don't regret anything. The way I live I can't afford to have regret. And this is something that must be mended immediatly and swiftly. But I wouldn't bother looking over your shoulder or in dark corners. This time, I'll be coming at you straight ahead.
*Despair takes a look down at his arms and body and stares at the scars.* Despair: Perhaps the future is bright after all. . . . For some anyway. Just wait and see what occurs Jim, after all the future is yet to be written.
