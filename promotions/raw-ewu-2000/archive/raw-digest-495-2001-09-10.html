Getting there... (We open to a panoramic shot of Albany, New York's capital city. The camera is on top of a building, looking down on the city below. Little
Louie is there, leaning on his knee, his foot perched on the low wall at the edge of the roof. He's dressed down today, a black sport jacket over a blue T-shirt and dark slacks. He glances over his shoulder at the camera, but doesn't look at it as he speaks.)
[L] Maclean, you're just not making this easy. I've gone out of my way to clear a path for you, given you all the opportunities in the world to prove you're not the ignorant rookie I took you for, and you continue to waste them. You're taking the easy way out, and that doesn't speak well of your status as a self-proclaimed professional.
This whole retirement business is a whitewash. I know, you promised it before your match with Midnight, but our business came before that.
There's something else going on here, something you don't want to talk about. Let's talk about the match first; maybe that will shed some light on this. I heard, yet again, a sob story from you about Midnight getting "help". If that's how you're representing yourself as a professional, Maclean, it might just be better if you *do* retire. Go back and look at the footage of that match. Exactly what "help" did Midnight receive? Were you referring to me showing up at ringside? You must have seen something I didn't, because I never laid a hand on anyone. In fact, the only problem you had on my account was a short attention span. It does prove a suspicion I had, however. Cara, and yourself. You didn't have the fortitude to keep your attention where it counts, or the common sense to leave your wife at home until she learns to take care of herself. Of course, the mere fact that you assumed
I'd be petty enough to attack her speaks volumes as well. It's become painfully obvious that you're confused, Maclean. I can see why
Adler wanted to take you under his wing. Whatever potential you have is squandered because you can't get your priorities straight, or even find an identity for yourself. I know who I am, and where I come from, but you're still trying to find out basic things like that. You teamed up with people who clearly won't work for your best interests, and then reinvent yourself in what appears to be my image. I'm sure you don't understand what I mean, or at least will claim you don't, so I'll state it plainly. You've wanted to be treated with the same kind of respect that I've earned, and I don't blame you, but you don't know how yet. So, you drop a moniker that someone else has taken, and corrupted, to your mind, only to imitate me. It's a delicious irony, isn't it, that you choose a theme song that is a copy of the one I've used for years? If one were to assume you didn't know about that ahead of time, "ironic" is exactly the term I'd use.
However, I think you knew that exactly, Maclean. Perhaps you thought this carbon copy of my entrance music would piss me off, but I don't think so.
I think the idea of facing me has you so perplexed, that you've been trying to walk in my footsteps, to gain an understanding of what you're up against. Your "retirement" has been cleverly staged, but really, are you just going to walk away from the biggest opportunity of your life? If you're anything like you say you are, there's no chance of that. You need time to consider what this could mean, and whether you're truly ready to step into the big leagues. You aren't. As long as you let others keep your mind from completely focusing on the business, as long as you blame others for mistakes you've clearly made yourself, you'll never reach the level I'm at. Now, what you have to do is prove me wrong.
I said it before, Maclean, I'm waiting for you. Nothing's keeping you from trying your luck against the premier technical wrestler in the sport, someone who's aching to return to action. The only thing stopping you is your own hesitation. You've seen what happened with LaGrange. A few matches with people of my calibre, and he's world famous. Imagine what someone with *talent* could achieve in his place. Imagine what you could do, if you give yourself the chance. I've heard a lot of talk from you,
