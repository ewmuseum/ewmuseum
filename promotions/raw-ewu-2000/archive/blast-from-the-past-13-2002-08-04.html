Well y'all from RAW had to know this was coming. The 4-way for the RAW
North American Title, held by Mike Masters previous to this match.
Chosen as the fifth best match in RAW history (Though not all (Or most) of the old timers voted).
Either way, although the spacing is screwed up, I still think this is the best all match psychology work I've done to date, and the action is pretty cool too. (Though that one I wrote for NEO's Crimson Glory coming out may be better. Eh. We'll see.)
So...this is probably a World title calibur match for the DWF...though
I can promise you it won't be this long. But just as good. However, the action is succinctly EX/TV style for the most part, with Masters showing his "Heritage" style throughout the match. (Especially with
Starman and Beauty)
All in all, these four guys put out a ton of effort for this four way feud, and you can still see Creighton and Beauty in RAW, and Masters and Starman...uh...doing porno. No wait...then they'd be in the EWWF!

Wicked...and of COURSE...the opening of the DWF August 20th.
