Thanks go to Pete K, who did the first 3 matches.] Alan Mangan vs. Simon Underwood: Before the match, Underwood had some words for all his opponents. [A dirtied figure in black clothes holds a microphone behind the "RAW Internecine" background and begins to speak.] Simon Underwood: Greed...Ambition...Dreams...Love... Take these feudal hopes and remove them from your strain of consciousness... They are no longer needed...For survival is upon us this day... Internecine beckons the call of the Hero... But the call will go unfulfilled, For tonight... The darkness prevails. Underwood dominated this match, although Mangan hit a quick back drop and powerslam to start things. After not being able to even get a two count, Mangan tried a suplex, but Underwood floated over and hit a reverse DDT, and then took over the match. Underwood worked over Mangan's neck quickly to set up his finisher, hitting a swinging neckbreaker and using a rear naked choke to take Mangan down. Simon then hit a powerslam and followed with the Transcendent Exposure to get the victory.
Whirling Dervish vs. American Patriot: AP came out of the gates strong, bodyslamming the Dervish and following with a series of elbow drops. A dropkick and side Russian leg sweep put the Dervish down, and got AP a 2 count. Dervish came back with an inverted atomic drop and a running knee lift. A big float over DDT got him 2. They traded some more basic offense back and forth, with the Dervish in control most of the time, until AP came back with a flying headscissors that got the crowd going, seeing as it was performed by a man that is 6-11! AP hit a flying clothesline off the top for 2. Dervish came back again with some heel tactics, raking the eyes and then putting on an abdominal stretch, using the ropes for leverage. He then tried to slam the big man, but couldn't put him down. AP countered, and gave Dervish a vicious powerbomb to the mat, and then went to the top rope. However, Dervish shoved the ref into the ropes, crotching AP up top. Dervish scaled to the top behind the Patriot and got on his shoulders, and then hit a top rope victory roll to get the
1-2-3! Little Louie vs. George "Buzzsaw" Stevens: Stevens started the match out strong, hitting several lariats. Louie came right back, hitting a running chop block to Stevens' knee, and then a springboard leg drop across the knee. Louie worked over
Stevens' knee for some time, and was in control of almost all of the offense in this match. Louie hit a springboard dropkick, followed by an eye gouge (!) and then applied the Indian
Deathlock, but Stevens still did not submit. Louie continued the onslaught with some stiff savate kicks and some more knee work.
Stevens finally made a comeback with some basic power moves, and then went for the Rocky Gulch Bomb, but
Louie slipped over, kicked Stevens in the leg, and then hit the Lambaster to get the 1-2-3! `Deadly' Derek Irvin vs Darren Walker: This was an awesome match, with both men showing the prowess which brought them to RAW. However, outside interference blighted the result, which came about after 15 hard fought minutes. As the two fought on the inside, Alan Mangan and Power O One charged down to ringside to attack Walker's retinue, blasting them with steel chairs. Walker, distracted, looked to make the save, but Irwin shoved him to the ropes and scored the victory with a roll up.
Simon Underwood vs Whirling Dervish: This was bizarre, to say the least, with what started out as an all- out brawl turning into a mixture of basic technical wrestling, although nothing special, from Underwood, and weird foreign objects from Whirling Dervish, who nearly scored the three after making his opponent. However, the win came for Simon Underwood, who ducked a
Dervish shot with a fake prosthetic leg before nailing him with the Transcedent Exposure DDT for the 3 count. `Deadly' Derek Irvin vs Little Louie: This was the match of the night so far, going 20 minutes, with both men getting incredibly close to a victory on more than one occasion.
Louie came especially close twice, first sidestepping Irvin's corner clothesline attempt and kicking him in the back of the head for good measure before turning Irvin around and drilling him across the chest with a vicious chop, then propping one of Irvin's legs up around the second rope. With the knee exposed, Louie lashed out, landing a series of crushing kicks on the leg, then he leant back, plants one hand on the mat, and both of his feet left the ground. One kicked
Irvin in the knee again, and the other hit him right between the eyes. That got two and three quarters for Louie, but the second was even closer. Louie landed a beautiful kick flush on Irvin's jaw, but as he tried for another, Derek caught his leg and tried to give him a dragon screw leg whip. Louie got pulled over, but instead of landing flat on his back, he cartwheeled out of the hold, using his free foot to snap Irvin's head back with anotherkick under his chin. Louie then snapped a kick into the side of Irvin's knee, and gave Irvin a dragon screw leg whip of his own, following up with a powerbomb for two and seven eighths. However, Irvin then mounted a wonderful comeback, hitting a space flying tiger drop and then the Gobsmacker for the 3.
