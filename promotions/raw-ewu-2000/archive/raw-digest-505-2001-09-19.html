________________________________________________________________________ ________________________________________________________________________ Message: 2 Date: Tue, 18 Sep 2001 15:21:42 -0700 (PDT) From: [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > Subject: A Promise for Simpkins [He's taken a sabbatical, having returned to the comfort of the Phoenix apartment, before leaving for RAW. Priest sits at his oak desk. Its surface is bare, save a slender lamp and an ashtray containing a burning cigarette. The lamp serves as the room's only illumination; smoke twists in and out of its dim white beam.]
JP: I've got a bone to pick. I've been reading the dirtsheets, reading the so-called "experts" opinions on RAW at WAR: Requiem for an Outlaw.
Most of them didn't agree on their picks, but one thing they did agree on was this. Judas Priest isn't in Doug Simpkins league.
[He picks up the cigarette, just long enough for a quick puff and exhale. He removes a sheaf of folded papers from his shirt pocket.] JP: Every. Last. One. Kuczynski put it this way. "Simpkins. Nobody cheats better." Guess he forgot, I'm the sunuvabitch that locked the good
Reverend in a coffin and lit that sumbitch on fire. [He takes the top paper and slowly, methodicly shredds it. The shreds slowly drift to the ground.]
JP: [name withheld]. "Dirty Doug kicks ass...lots of ass. He wins. Apparantly he missed the fact that DD is 50 years old and about 10 years past his asskickin' days.
[Another paper, another shredding, more drifting.] JP: [name withheld]. "JP is good, but Simpkins is just too sneaky for him." [Shred. Drift. Stare.] JP: Sneaky. That pretty much sums it up. All the dirtsheets, all the internet experts are pickin' old DDS over me because of that. Dirty
Doug is just too "sneaky". [Puff. Exhale.] JP: Well folks, guess what. After I lock Doug into The Offering, how is cheating gonna help him? After I break Dougs legs, how much is ass is he gonna kick? When he'll be lucky to spend the rest of his life in a damn wheelchair, how the F-CK is being "sneaky" gonna help him?
[He flicks the cigarette toward the camera, watching the ember sail past the lens. He takes a moment to compose himself, before speaking for one last time.]
JP: I tell you how. It won't. I don't know if I'm gonna win, gonna lose, or what. All I do know is I'm gonna hurt someone. That someone is Doug
Simpkins. Doug, let me promise you something. [He leans forward, letting the camera zoom in tight on his grizzled face.] JP: Doug, I promise you. Win. Lose. You will not walk out of that arena. You will ride out of there on a stretcher. That ring will be your last stop, before you arrive at the County Hospital. This, I promise you.

He is lying on the broken bed in the hideous pink trailer]
Ramirez: Raw Report. My Show. My little stalker, Zensation against that misfit, Murdoch. Why did I attack Stevie Ray with a chair?
Simple. That dickwad came out and ruined my exhibition at the last raw at war. I beat him then and just wanted that little moron to realise that you don't mess with a New Breeder.
[ Ronny looks at the latest Raw at War magazine] Ramirez: A double page spread on The Flying Scotsman. How pathetic. Neil, once upon a time we were friends but then you got too damn cocky and helped to keep me down. Something had to give. You joined the Raw forces against The New Breed. Yes we both hate WAR! and your words about Travis were damn right insulting. When did you ever care about him or The New Breed? You see Vik, Garland and myself can see right through your stupid game. This match is at Travis's tribute show and you dare to ruin his memory like this. You and Stevie Ray are bit two fools who are in above their heads.
[ Dramatic pause] Ramirez: Vik spoke for me very well when he said we would never team up with you litle pissants. We are The New Breed for a reason : We are New Breed of rasslers unlike you stupid ingrates who just try to please the ungrateful fans. This match aint about Raw anyhow. It aint about us or WAR! It is Kodiak and The Icon dishing out some overdue punishment to Tonnage. Creighton wasn't even in WAR! so he can get the hell out. Tonnage, you played your part in what happened to
Travis. You didn't do a damn thing to stop Jugger. I am gonna mash your face against that steel cage until your flesh grates like figgin cheese. It is my mission, Our mission to take you out.
[ Ronny walks out of the trailer as we fade] ________________________________________________________________________ ________________________________________________________________________ Message: 4 Date: Wed, 19 Sep 2001 01:59:46 -0400 From: milhouse < [detail withheld] > Subject: Larry Meets The Locals [The scene starts in lovely downtown Strasburg, North Dakota. Pulling up to the local Feed Store in a shinny green Gremlin with an oversized CB antenna (Yup, our hero decided to buy out the rental agency and get a whole fleet of them for himself) is the President of Raw Nature Boy Larry Spikowski. The
Natural One picks up the car’s CB microphone.] [Spikowski] Pretty lady, this is Mambo Stud One. Over?....I said Pretty lady, this is Mambo Stud One. Come back now?... [A woman with a thick Brooklyn accent finally comes on the air] [Secretary] Hello? [Spikowski] Pretty Lady, Mambo Stud One ain’t coming to the fort right now. He’s got to drain the lizard, shove some groceries down his neck and meet some of the local Indians.
[Secretary] Who is this? [Spikowski] ....For God sakes, Ethel, it’s me Larry! [Secretary] Why are you talking so funny? [Spikowski] It’s CB talk. I thought I put out a memo about it being mandatory for everyone to watch "Smokey and the Bandit".
[Secretary] Well, maybe if someone bothered to get us e-mail instead of these cheap shit used electric typewriters, they would have seen it.
[Spikowski] Whatever. Look Ethel, I’m not coming into the office right now. I’m stopping at one of these local yokel places to get a bite to eat.
[Secretary] Gee, what will we do when you’re gone? The whole place is going to fall apart. [Spikowski] Very funny, Ethel. That’s a big ten-four. [Secretary] What? [Spikowski] Good-bye, Ethel! [The Nature Boy gets out of the car. He nods to a farmer and an elderly gentleman sitting outside of the Feed Store chewing the fat. He walks past them, up the steps and goes inside. A moment later, the Nature Boy walks back out the front door. He looks a bit confused. He addresses the farmer.]
[Spikowski] Ah....Excuse me? [Farmer] ...Ye-up. [Spikowski] This might sound a bit stupid. Where’s the restaurant? [Farmer] Come again? [Spikowski] The restaurant. Is it around back? [Farmer] .....This here’s a Feed Store. [Spikowski] Yeah, I’m hungry. But there’s nothing inside to eat but a bunch of grain. [Farmer] .....Yee-up. [The Nature Boy isn’t quite sure what to make of the man’s last "Yee-up".] [Spikowski] So you’re saying I can get something to eat then? [Farmer] ....Yee-up. [Spikowski] So where do I place an order? [Farmer] .....You can get something to eat if you like grain. [The farmer’s companion leans forward with his hand over his ear. The man is obviously hard of hearing.]
[Old Man] What? [Farmer] Says he wants to eat some grain! [Old Man] What for? [Farmer] Dunno! [Spikowski] No, I don’t want to eat some grain. I want a sandwich. [Old Man] What’d he say now? [Farmer] Says he wants a grain sandwich! [Spikowski] No, I didn’t say that. [The Nature Boy leans towards the deaf man] [Spikowski] I said I want to go to a restaurant! [Old Man] If you want a grain sandwich, you should probably go to the Feed Store. [Farmer] Ye-up. [Spikowski] No, that’s not what I meant! Never mind! [The Nature Boy remembers his manners and decides to introduce himself. He extends his arm to shake hands.]
[Spikowski] I’m Larry Spikowski by the way. I’m the president of Raw. You might have seen the new building we’re constructing across from the
Lawrence Welk museum. We’re moving everything from Manhattan right to your home town. [Old Man] What? [Farmer] Says he’s from Hollywood! One of those actor types like Mel Torme! [Spikowski] Manhattan! We’re from Manhattan! And it’s wrestling not acting! [Old Man] I never liked those Hollywood types! [Spikowski] No, we’re the RAW Wrestling Federation! We’re from New York! You’ve probably seen us on TV!
[Farmer] Don’t watch TV. Not since "He" passed away. [Spikowski] Oh, you mean Lawrence Welk. Yeah, he was great. [Farmer] No, I mean Don Knotts. Used to love Three’s Company. [Spikowski] Don Knotts? Don Knotts isn’t dead. [Old Man] What? [Farmer] He said he gave Don Knotts head! [Old Man] Why would he go and do that? [Farmer] Dunno [The Nature Boy backs up towards is Gremlin. He’s starting to look a little freaked out] [Spikowski] Look gents, I better be going. It was real nice talking to you. [Old Man] What? [Farmer] Says he hates Jews! [Old Man] What’s up with his hair? [Farmer] Looks typical of Hollywood anti-Semite queer folk if you ask me! [Old Man] I say we run ‘em out of town on a rail! [The Nature Boy quickly gets into his Gremlin and locks the doors. Hurriedly, he drives away.] [Farmer] Jesus, that was funny, Bill. [Old Man] Let’s go hang out in front of the barber shop and see if any more city folk come along.
