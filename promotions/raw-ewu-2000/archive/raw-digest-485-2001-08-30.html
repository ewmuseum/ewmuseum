["Gelid" Garland Frost sits in a rather non-descript room, staring intently into the camera.]
Frost: So, my opponent finally speaks! And lo and behold, the genetic abnormality is as eloquent as he is freakishly large. Tonnage, your propensity to wax poetic for seemingly hours on end is surpassed only by your ability to simulaneously say nothing of substance at all. Sound and fury, signifying nothing...
[Frost stands.] Frost: And to make fun of me by feminizing my name... truly the last gasp of a desperate man. But I cannot blame you, my gigantic friend, for you are about to step into the ring with not only the hottest young prospect to ever set foot in RAW, but you are also facing the New Breed, and your pathetic, desperate rantings against Travis LaGrange only serve to betray the fear that you and everyone else is feeling right now, knowing that the New Breed is poised to assume control of this entire operation...
[Garland begins walking towards the door.] Frost: One last thing, Tonnage... you say no one else has been able to use my proposed strategy against you, that your prodigious strength and superior reach will counter any attempts of mine to tire you out... that may be all true, Igor, however...
[Frost reaches out and opens the exit door.] Frost: ...you've never faced me before. This is the way your world ends, Tonnage... not with a bang, not with a whimper... ...but with a 1-2-3. This isn't about impressing anyone or living up to expectations for me Neil, its about more than that. But perhaps you can't relate.
*Despair takes a turn down the street and begins to walk past an old church. He pauses for a moment to spit on the steps of it and continues on.* Despair: Perhaps you never knew what it was like to strive for excellency just to spite others. For you success was for praise. For me, it was to belittle those that would have rubbed it in my face. So many never though I would be here. They claimed I was too small, I wouldn't survive my own actions. How excellent it was to see one of those doubters delivering pizza to support himself. I take it his career is off and running.
** Despair: I am here despite all things and now, now I am given the chance to tear down what some consider an icon, a hero. And to merely think of that makes my mouth water for the carnage that will soon follow. Have you been enjoying life recently Neil, taking care to note the small things? Have you taken the time to go for a walk or enjoy a good meal just in case you can never experience them the same again? I all fairness I would suggest that you do. Right now is the preverbal eye of the storm. The calm in the middle of all hell breaking lose. Would you like me to clarify what that means to you? It means that if this is the last time I walk under my own power so be it.
