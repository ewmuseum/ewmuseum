RAW Aggression: Know Your Enemy Saturday 24th November Nippon Budokan (Tokyo) WORLD TITLE MATCH: LaGrange vs Spikyjim - These two faced off in the tag match this week, with LaGrange coming out on the winning side. But will he be able to successfully defend his title against RAW's Light Heavyweight champion, and one of RAW's other representatives on the big MotR VI card, The Human Energizer Bunny? Also, if Spikyjim wins the match, as no RAW competitor can hold two singles championships simultaneously, the RAW Light Heavyweight Title will be VACATED!
Douglas McCray/[name withheld] vs Thomas Creighton/Big Gay Bill
Guest referee: Ronny Ramirez - McCray was set to nail LaGrange at the end of last week's Aggression, when BGB made the save. As McCray pursued, he was attacked by McDougall and Creighton, both annoyed at his interference in their earlier NA title match - as was Ramirez.
Will McCray and McDougall settle their differences in time to work as a team? Will Creighton and BGB rise from the ashes of the WAR!
invasion to form a coherent partnership? And, since Ronny hates just about EVERYONE in this match, will he call it down the middle? Lots of questions... but only one way to get the answer.
Tonnage vs Despair - Despair got the huge victory over the number 1 contender to the RAW World title in the tag match, although only after Spikyjim had accidentally hit his partner with a fireball. Will he be able to pull off a repeat one on one?
Gigantor vs Omega - Gigantor lost his first match this week, falling to Ernest 'Brick' Wall via DQ when Omega pointed out to the ref the use of a foreign object. But the monster still hasn't been pinned or submitted, and has Omega bitten off more than he can chew?
Non title match Stevie Ray Murdoch & Kirk Maclean vs Ric Beauty & OCD - The alliance between Beauty and OCD seems to be reformed, although it didn't save Ric from a trip to the hospital at Aggression. Maclean, on the other hand, despite being sneak attacked, managed to return and team with
Murdoch to pull off a shock Tag team title win. Simpkins/Wall Vs. Starman/Midnight - Wall and Starman are two of our best newcomers for a long while, whilst Jay Mid and Simpkins are guys who've been around for eons - and spent most of them attacking each other. Doug and Ernest are mat technicians cut from the same cloth, while Starman and Midnight aren't exactly two of a kind... although I don't think ANYONE'S similar to Tony Quint. Who'll work together best?
'Deadly' Derek Irwin vs Mike Masters - Masters' manager Narcissa was somewhat less than impressed by Irwin's debut promo. This will be the in-ring debut of both men on Aggression, since Masters' match with
Maclean this week never went ahead, since Maclean was attacked by Gigantor, Styles, and Masters before it could get underway. George Stevens Vs. Khan - The former WC RAW champ couldn't pull off a win in his three way match this week, but has looked impressive in RAW. How will he deal with Nick Demola's protege, George 'Buzzsaw' Stevens? Adam Mitchell Vs. Simon Underwood vs Valec Corso - Mitchell was strangely silent this week, and ended up getting pinned by Starman in the triple threat match to open last week's card. Corso also hasn't had the best of starts in RAW, but has shown enough talent to make most people sure that better things are around the corner. The unknown quantity here is Simon Underwood, making his Aggression debut.
