Raw's only summary card show... ********************************************* RAW WRESTLING FEDERATION proudly presents RAW Report From the Pan American Centre, Las Cruces, New Mexico October 25th ********************************************* The show opened to fireworks and ticker tape and balloons for the fans in
Las Cruces, along with a tribute to the short but impressive career of Mike
Slater, sadly ruined by Douglas McCray, Jon Smythe and Doug Simpkins.
However, before the night even began, footage was displayed on the Raw-a-tron of events backstage. As William Bennet, The Juggernaut pounds on a Super Candy Machine outside the locker rooms to get his nickel back, Vik
Ventressa jumps him from behind with a swing of the shovel to the back of the skull, his head smashing through the glass, and ironically, all the change falling out of the machine. Ventressa is laughing and grabs an OCD
Energy Bar (Chocolate, nuts and caffeine), before the raising the shovel once more.but Tonnage appears and chases Ventressa off. Bennett is then taken to the hospital, Tonnage going with him.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 'KILER' JEFF JACKSON VS. GLITTERIS <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Both men came out to a positive reception, but Glitteris moreso, his reputation more established with the amazing Andrazoons. Jackson who may have a pinfall over McCray under his belt but little else so far in RAW really needed to win this match.
It was looking decidedly dangerous for Jackson to begin with, since Glitteris had brought Succubus along with him - and their other halves. And dangerous it was for Jackson as Glitrreris, despite looking a little bit out of sorts being in singles competition truly seemed the superior man in this match, dealing with Jackson's technical attacks well, and responding in kind with arm-drags to the outside, twisting shooting star presses, top rope bulldogs and springboard missile dropkicks from the security rail.
Glitteris made this an outstanding match to watch and showed promise of a . glittering . singles career. Victory was not to be his. With Jackson having been on the receiving end for most of the match, he capitalised when Glitteris attempted a 2nd rope tornado DDT. Jackson countered with a super-northern-lights suplex, and from there the match was his, finishing it with the Instant Death, which is
a not so instant series of 5 german suplexes. Succubus would have come to his aid, but Lolita, Jon Smythe's gal, had come down to ringside and started a fight with Tiffi Twisted that Succubus just had to watch. A much needed victory to Jackson.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><> JESSIE MULDOON Vs. PIGEONCHEST <><><><><><><><><><><><><> The new member of the Sex Stable, Jessie Muldoon, seemed to be in no mood to mess about. The boos from the crowd only served to make him more efficient in disposing of Pigeonchest. The muscular Londoner barely broke a sweat as he tore through PigeonChest. He was totally sadistic and used his power to great effect to be as vicious as possible. This match he finished with the
Manly Obsession, a very impressive top rope move. Muldoon continues to impress and despite his set-back against Knick Man at Raw At War, could be challenging for gold very soon.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> KNICK MAN VS. EDDY WHO <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> As with Jessie Muldoon in the previous match, this contest was little more than an exhibition for Knick Man who displayed his array of moves with little counter-attack. The New Yorker was showing a sharper attitude and was getting extra cheers from the fans for it, who he often rolled out of the ring to sign autographs for. The fans were treated to a display of the top-rope piledriver that is The Slam Dunk, the move getting Knick Man an easy victory.
<><><><><><><><><><><><> COPPERHEAD VS. DEACON PAGE <><><><><><><><><><><><> Copperhead has gotten into quite a feud with the Holy Alliance, and it didn't stop in this match that was somewhat of a brawl. Page was accompanied only by Bishop Righteous, who certainly did his best to be a counterweight for the size and power advantage held by Copperhead. Judas Priest & Lady Pain were at ringside to counteract the Bishop. Copperhead, with a much more aggressive attitude, has been enjoying cheers of late, moreso than usual. He won the crowd over early on as the giant leapt over the top rope before the bell had even begun as Page made his way into the ring. The flying clothesline started a brawl outside the ring before Page threw him back in. Mid-match Copperhead began to dominate the fight and started to slip in more powerful moves, the most notable a devastating crucifix powerbomb followed by a legdrop from the second rope.
For a man of such size, he is incredibly agile. It's a wonder he isn't more successful yet.
The match concluded in chaos. Lady Pain was getting into Page's face,
Bishop Righteous came to his aid. As this was happening, Copperhead spun
Page round and hit him with the Snakebite. Copperhead then put in position for a high-lift powerbomb, as Judas flew off the top rope for a reverse bulldog-powerbomb combo by the two. The move destroyed Page, and Copperhead
got the pin. Copperhead showed great understanding with Priest, and surely a Deacons Vs. Copperhead/Priest match is on the cards. <><><><><><><><><><><><> THE MOCK VS. RONNY RAMIREZ <><><><><><><><><><><><> Ramirez did not come out to make friends in this encounter. The charismatic
Mock was fighting a man who cared little for the rules, as Ramirez ripped up the rulebook and employed underhand tactics. The match was over quickly,
Ramirez making a point that he isn't going to mess around. The pin came following a Crucifix powerbomb.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> SCOTT WYLD Vs. JASON MIDNIGHT <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Jason Midnight was first out, to a tremendous reception from the fans in New Mexico. Then Wyld's music kicked in - but there was no Scott Wyld. The PA tried and second and third time, but he failed to show up, the match being awarded to Jason Midnight by default.
However, if he thought his night was going to be over easily, as he walked up the aisle he was jumped from out of the crowd by Ronny Ramirez who undertook a vicious assault on Midnight, until American Patriot came to the rescue, running Ramirez off with the Stars And Stripes Flag.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> STEVIE RAY MURDOCH Vs. SHAWN FURY <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> With the two of them scheduled to combat at Raw At War in tag-team action, this was a good show of things to come. Murdoch, betrayed by Simpkins, was more than happy to go against Shawn Fury, a man happy to be working alongside Simpkins, Smythe and McCray.
Murdoch, aware of their connections, was accompanied to the ring by Eddie
Drysdale and Jeff Jackson, his partners for Union. Their presence seemed to do the trick - no sign of their enemies throughout the entire match.
Shawn Fury is known for bringing out the best in his opponent, and Stevie
Ray Murdoch, as crazy as he is, improves by the week. This match lasted all the way to the fifteen minute mark, with both men showing great talent.
Murdoch's willingness to damage himself to hurt his opponent was incredible, as was Fury's cold, methodical approach. It was almost a great contrast, of
spontaneity meeting calculation. Fast flowing, hard hitting and damn exciting, that was this match, and SRM did wonders for increasing his popularity. The fans were chanting for 'Stevie, Stevie', and that apparently kept him in the match when otherwise all else was lost.
There cheers carried him on.and on.and then to victory, getting Shawn Fury with the 'Kickstart My Heart'. Victory then was undeniable, and Stevie Ray
Murdoch pinned up his first victory since mid-August the cheers of the fans.
Shawn Fury was bitterly disappointed, but with Drysdale and Jackson at ringside, wasn't going to do anything about it.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> VIK VENTRESSA W/TARA VS. OBSESSIVE COMPULSIVE DISORDER <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> The fans were hyper for this match, as one of the most loved and one of the most hated wrestlers clashed. Ventressa surprised everyone by coming to the ring dressed like Tonnage, and consequently mocked Tonnage throughout the entire match.
Vik fought a wily match against OCD, cheating like hell and doing whatever he could to break momentum from OCD - rolling out of the ring, hiding in the
ropes, behind the referee. OCD was finding it very frustrating. OCD is nothing if not a crazy son of a gun determined to hurt his opponent however, and he did manage to pull out some great moves. Early on in the match,
Ventressa was knocked cold with a Steve Jobs chair shot, but there was more to warm the fans.
Ventressa did well to null a lot of OCD's momentum, but not all of it. A rolling lariat, a guillotine choke with a face-claw, a twisting doctor bomb and a face first powerbomb onto the ring steps all put OCD in a very strong
position. However, Ventressa isn't a world title contender for nothing, and he weathered each storm, fighting back harder and dirtier, controlling long periods of this 22minute match with wear-down holds, again, designed to break momentum.
Vik continued his mocking of Tonnage, even hitting the gigaton crush on OCD, which OCD kicked out of. In the final minutes, OCD began to build momentum again. However, in a moment with Vik distracting the referee, Tara Ventressa pepper-sprayed him in the eyes. Vik turned him round, hit the heart punch and made the cover for the 1 - 2 - 3.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> NON-TITLE MATCH 'BLACKSMITH' JON SMYTHE Vs. EDDIE DRYSDALE <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Before the match start, Vik Ventressa came down to join the UK Internet broadcasting table, taking the opportunity to rip on Tonnage, asking where he is, saying he wants Tonnage face to face now.
Tonnage is of course has a healthy relationship with Eddie Drysdale who challenged Jon Smythe to a non-title contest just to shut Smythe up a bit.
Eddie Drysdale, as usual, emerged to a tremendous pop from the crowd, whilst
Smythe, accompanied by the diabolic Doug Simpkins found himself even less popular than ever following his part in the retirement of Mike Slater.
The match between Smythe and Drysdale turned out to be, on the whole, a good one, with Smythe showing great improvement from when he first started in
RAW, but Drysdale continued to display his world class credentials. It was a fast moving match of suplexes, slams and a wide variety of hard-hitting moves, with the match punctuated by many near-falls from both men. In short, a really good main event that Eddie Drysdale would have won if it had gone to points.
Drysdale was clearly trying to eliminate Simpkins from the equation, pressing Smythe above his head early on and throwing him onto Simpkins. It didn't eliminate Simpkins though, and he played a big part in distracting the ref so that Drysdale couldn't finish the match early on.
Simpkins played an important part in the end of the match. Kicking the
Capital Championship belt to Smythe whilst distracting the ref, Smythe swiped at Drysdale, but Drysdale was fast with a legsweep, and Smythe fell forward into the ref, knocking him over the rope and out of action.
At that moment, Vik Ventressa left the Internet team and ran into the ring, flooring Eddie with a heart punch. He, Smythe and Simpkins beat on Drysdale until Tonnage's music kicked in, returning from the hospital. Rushing to the ring, Tara rolled into the ring to get in her way. As he grabbed her,
Vik sprayed him in the eyes then shoved Drysdale at him. In his confusion,
Tonnage thought that Drysdale was Ventressa, and Drysdale, already out cold, was hit with the devastating Gigaton Crush.
The ref rolled back into the ring, and saw Drysdale laid out, and Tonnage in the ring with Smythe. Drawing his own conclusions, the ref called for the
bell, dq-ing Jon Smythe. Shawn Fury & McCray came down to the ring to join their team-mates for Union, beating down on the destroyed Drysdale whilst Tonnage struggled to regain his eye-sight. The show ended as
