Midnight: You know when I won this Capital Title at BattleZone I expected a huge ovation when I walked to the back. And yeah, a few folks shook my hand, but mostly people just looked at me funny.
Why? Because I had just retired a man. Now we all know that retirement doesn't mean all that much in this business, but still I had just done the unthinkable, I had taken away a man's ability to eat, even if only last until he can find a way to sneak back in. See we wrestlers hold many deep dark secrets, tops amoung them is that the main reason we do this is because we just wouldn't be good at anything else. Most of us started this as soon as we finished high school, getting our bodies battered in front of 10 or
15 people just so we could have a little bit of experience. We have dealt with promoters so shady that they make Eric Helmroy look like
Gandhi. But we do it all to make it to a place like this, a place where the money is good the fame is high and the risk we take can be justified. Once you make it to a place like this it's sort of an unwritten rule that you can do what you want to a man inside the ring, just make sure he can come back from it eventually. Temporary injury is fine, permanent is a no-no. So when you actually take a man out this business, like I did Kirk MacLean, people start to wonder what else your capable off. You go from becoming a dangerous man in theory to a dangerous man in fact.
That's when they start coming after you. As if they didn't have enough reason to hunt me down now they have this Capital Title. I talked earlier about what it means to be a champion but it is almost impossible to describe what it is really like. I mean when I walk into the locker room with this strap in my bag it immediately puts everybody on notice. Everybody without a belt, which means 95% of the federation, starts to eye you, looking for any sign of weakness. Do you have a cold that day? Or you thinking about something your wife or girlfriend said before the show? Are you even the slightest bit distracted from the task at hand? If so they go running to the agent in the back and beg for their shot at the title.
So the match is signed and now everybody is watching because these titles carry a whole lot of stroke with them and everybody wants that.
You've got the guy in the ring gunning for you and then you have every person in the back hoping that your opponent reveals some type of flaw in your style that can be used against you. It's a crazy thing. And why all the fuss over these pieces of gold? Because they do your talking for you.
I don't have to say that I am now one of the top three in this game, the belt says that. And I don't have to ask to be booked in a high profile match because the belt does that. This belt can ask for first class plane tickets, fancy hotel rooms, the whole nine. And it always gets what it wants.
And what's the price we champions pay for all this? The same we paid for everything else. Our blood, our bodies, except now we have to do it at a much higher level then everyone else because the whole world is watching. But that's o.k., I love the attention. And I always give the people what they want. And that my friends, is why I am going to be at the top of this business for a very long time.
[Fade out, and back to the studio with Brad Holmes.] BH: Well fans, Jason Midnight has certainly got a point there. How do you change theory into fact, what is the price of fame? What does glory represent.. is pride enough in this game anymore? It all depends on the man your asking. I think Jay Mid is a man with reason, and a calling.
