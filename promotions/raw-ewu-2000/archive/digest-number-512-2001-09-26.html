Despair made Lance Tortsven submit to the Human
Torment...Little Louie defeated SuperRay with the Lambaster...American
Patriot defeated 'Taliban Terror' Jimmy Stars with the Patriot
Missile...'Dirty' Doug Simpkins defeated Oil with the Dirty Doug Death Drop. The main action of the evening kicked off with 'EUPHORIA' LUKE LUCENT debuting against wrestling prodigy DRAKE ZENSATION. Zensation has appeared sensational in all his matches, displaying great ability but never quite being able to finish his opponents off. He was hoping that tonight Lucent would be the first to fall.
For a newcomer, and relatively unknown at that, Lucent got a great reception...not least because he is a Texan himself. The fans loved him, and his exciting brawling/high flying style got him over with the crowds while Zensation had to put up with the jeers of the crowd.
Zensation started off the strongest, and the fastest, whipping Lucent to the ropes and hitting him with a sambo suplex, then nailing him with an overhead belly to belly suplex. Lucent fought back - literally. His brawling style was a bit too much for Zensation and he almost got Drake in the fourth
minute following a Death Valley Driver. Zensation was resilient enough to kick out. The match continued with the men increasingly hitting more devastating moves on each other and getting that bit closer to the victory. A cherry blossom suplex and a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker almost got Zensation the victory while
Lucent's corkscrew plancha had everyone believing he had the victory.
A clean victory did come about in this match. As Zensation beat down on
Lucent, the crowd support carried Lucent from the grasp of defeat and on to
victory, Zensation went for the Wing on a prayer, but Lucent countered with a jawbreaker then hit with his Wraith Interloper Moonsault. He hooked the leg and got the 1-2-3. A close match, but victory for Lucent who could be a good challenger for the Capital title.
ALAN MANGAN, the powerscouse, who, under the guidance of Power O' One is firmly on a singles career path now, was scheduled up against one of the most powerful men wrestling has ever seen - former RAW World Champion,
'KODIAK' VIC CREED. The match began before the bell. As Creed walked down the ramp, Magan ran down the ramp and attacked from behind, bulldogging Creed into the ramp. He needed that advantage as a brawl broke out between the two of them and
Mangan soon realized that Creed was too good a fighter to simply brawl with.
The heat against Mangan was incredible, his comments on Raw At War clearly inciting the fans. Creed was, in one of those rarer moments of his career, actually getting cheers whenever he got a big move in. Creed was very good at that, but so too was Mangan. After several minutes of pure brawling and powermoves, Creed and Mangan decided to do a greco-roman knuckle lock to determine who was the stronger. Naturally, Mangan, under the guidance of Power O' One, cheated, by kicking Creed in the groin area then proceeded to unleash on Creed.
The onslaught didn't last long, as Vic Creed soon found his way back to his feet and almost destroyed Mangan with a running powerslam. Creed continued to inflict damage until Mangan caught a second wind with the assistance of
Power O' One. Creed however, managed to hit the Powerdriver after reversing a whip to the ropes. He went to make the cover, but once again the Masked
Attacker pulled him off Mangan and hit him with the Descent to Madness before swinging Mike Slater the Steel Chair into Creed's face. The referee dq'd Mangan for it, leaving both he and Creed far from happy.
RONNY RAMIREZ defended his North American title against 'WEAPON OF CHOICE' MARCUS WILSON and JUDAS PRIEST. Ramirez was getting a lot of negative vibes from the crowd for his attempt to kill Rose and Junior Bennett. Wilson and
Priest, however, both RAW, got great receptions from the crowd, especially
Priest who has been showing in recent weeks exactly what he is capable of.
Priest and Wilson, for the start of the match, teamed up well to soften up
Ramirez with their double martial-arts/high-flying attack, but dissension soon broke out between the two with Priest going for the cover and Wilson pulling him off.
Wilson and Priest then engaged in some low-level karate, as well as wrestling moves, to create a very exciting match. Ramirez, however, brave and wise as he is, stood back, waiting for them to weaken, then pounced,
flooring them both with his Iconic Gesture. Ramirez then pinned the legal man - Marcus Wilson - and got the 1-2-3. The main event of the evening saw a classic 30minute iron man match between
THOMAS CREIGHTON, GARLAND FROST, STEVIE RAY MURDOCH and SPIKYJIM. Before the match, Garland Frost had this to say...
[Frost grabs the mic from the ring announcer after the introductions, and gets right in the face of Spikyjim.]
Frost: The following wrestling clinic is brought to you by the letters F and
U, and the number 2. "F U too" you blue-haired freak.
[Frost drops the mic and stands toe to toe with the Light Heavyweight Champion, daring him to make a move.] The crowd blew the roof off for the entrances of Stevie Ray Murdoch and
Spikyjim, two men who, several weeks ago, fought in a classic match at
Battlezone 2. Frost, and especially Creighton, got the jeers that they deserved from the crowd.
This was a match much as everyone could expect, but somehow perhaps being more. Spikyjim and Stevie Ray Murdoch were their usual reckless selves,
Frost cemented his position as one of the top technical wrestlers in RAW pulled out a huge array of moves and working very hard throughout the match...mixed with some underhand tactics...while Thomas Creighton tried to spend the first ten minutes on the outside, coming in only to attempt to steal pins or break up pins.
All four put in tremendous effort to win this match in their own way, and there were no friends. Alliances would form and break and be betrayed in seconds. It all made for very exciting action as four of the best put on their best, displaying many of their great moves and breaking up a lot of pinfall attempts. Frost was a particularly bas thorn in Spikyjim's side, breaking off many of Spiky's pin attempts.
The first fall of the night went to Thomas Creighton. Spikyjim hit the
Living End on Stevie Ray Murdoch. He went to make the cover but Frost pulled him off and the two of them began to slug it out...or rather,
Spikyjim punched the hell out of Frost until Frost raked his eyes to stop him. Creighton rolled into the ring, having been hiding on the outside, and hit the Dirty Deeds knee-splash om Murdoch, securing the 1-2-3 at 8:38
Creighton 1
Second fall saw Creighton laid out on a table outside the ring, with Stevie
Ray Murdoch and Spikyjim each on a top turnbuckle. The two of them leapt off with flying elbow drop, Creighton moved and thw two of them crashed through the ring announcer's table. Frost grabbed an unsuspecting
Creighton, hit the Frostplex on the concrete, rolling Creighton into the ring and got the 1-2-3 at 13:20
Creighton 1 Frost 1 Third fall came to Spikyjim, getting the Living End on Frost. With Murdoch and Creighton tangled up outside the ring after another crazy dive from
Murdoch, Spikyjim had no one interfering to stop his 1-2-3 at 19:04
Creighton 1 Frost 1 Spikyjim 1 The fourth fall saw Frost pin Creighton once again, this time rolling out of the way of the Dirty Deeds, then rolling him up and hooking the tights to get the 1-2-3 at 22:20 while Murdoch and Spikyjim recovered from a tumble from the top turnbuckle when neither could get an advantage during an attempted superplex.
Creighton 1 Frost 2 Spikyjim 1 Fifth fall, and all men very tired saw Creighton get disqualifed at 24:28, hitting Stevie Ray Murdoch with a wrench from his toolbox.
Creighton 1 Frost 2 Spikyjim 1 Murdoch 1 Sixth fall and Creighton got revenge on Frost, pinning him via the Dirty
Deeds splash at 26:11.
Creighton 2 Frost 2 Spikyjim 1 Murdoch 1 Seventh fall and Stevie Ray Murdoch was going for his Kickstart my Heart onto Creighton. Spikyijm came from behind and nailed him with a dropkick to the back of the head, saving Creighton and going for a pin himself but
Murdoch kicked out. At the same time, Frost came behind and nailed
Creighton with a series of rolling german suplexes. He went to make the cover but out of nowhere Spikyjim landed a guillotine legdrop to the back of
Frost's head. He covered, 1-2-3. 28:40 gone. Creighton 2 Frost 2 Spikyjim 2 Murdoch 1 With only eighty seconds left it seemed unlikely that anyone would get a final fall in that time...and they didn't. But the referee declared that there must be a winner. The four men, tired, bloodied, but perhaps proud of putting on such an exciting battle, carried on, and the ending couldn't have been closer, with Frost covering Spikyjim after a Frostplex and Creighton having Murdoch covered after countering a flying headscissors with a powerbomb. The referee saw Creighton first, and counted the 1-2-3, Frost furious on missing out on a victory.
