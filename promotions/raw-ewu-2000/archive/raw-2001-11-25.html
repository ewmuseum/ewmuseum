Y'see, the thing is, you're bored of ewrestling aren't you? You're tired of those feds where everyone is a tough, tweener bad-ass. You're tired of reading what is pretty much the same roleplay content rewritten in the same way over and over again. You're tired of cards being horrendously late.
You're tired of being in feds which aren't that entertaining. You're tired of fedheads that don't care. You think you've had enough of feuds that begin because someone hits someone else with a chair. You're tired of words of war that essentially come down to this:
'I'm tougher than you.' ' No, I'm tougher than you' ' No, actually I'm tougher than you' ' You're mistaken, I'm tougher than you'. You're tired of feds that just don't seem to be alive, that go into a coma yet stall get called a great fed. You're tired of the politics. You're tired of the egos. You tired to the extent that you can't get the motivation to roleplay or raise your game.
I'll let you into a secret. It's because you're not in RAW. RAW WRESTLING FEDERATION Make It Happen www.raw-empire.co.uk There are 6 messages in this issue. Topics in this digest: 1.
