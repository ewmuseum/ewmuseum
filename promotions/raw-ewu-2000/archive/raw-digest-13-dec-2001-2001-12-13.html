'THE RISE AND FALL OF RONNY RAMIREZ' by Craig Heffer For those of you who saw Raw's Internecine II ppv, you would have seen the man many believe to be a few steps away from main event heaven....Ronny Ramirez. You would also have seen the 'gaybomb'
inflicted by 'BGB' William Bennett from the skybox of the Tokyodome. At the time of writing we hear that Ramirez is still unconscious. This is a sad time for all and due to this I want to take a look back at the history of the ever evolving Ronny Ramirez.
He started out as a skinny teenager with a penchant for the girls, sadly nothing come of this. He was bullied and then one day a coach by the name of Elijah Anderson stopped the fighting. He saw that even though he was being bullied that, the young Ramirez, had the body frame and the look to make him a huge wrestling star. Elijah trained and tutored Ronny for the next three years.
Over those three years Ronny lost his spots, gained muscle and his trademark long, curly, black hair. He had girls devoting themselves to him. He graduated and instead of getting a scholarship at Michigan
University decided to go to the independent leagues. With Elijah by his side he grew to win countless titles and tournaments , but always you sensed he had great doubts about his abilities, primarily his promo skills. He traveleld to Mexico and Japan and had gruelling C4, exploding ring matches, house of fire and sidewalk slam matches. He then left and went back to America.
Ronny was soon picked up by the WAR! federation run by Eric Helmroy out of New York City. This was Ronny's big break. He started off slowly. Not knowing much about his opposition and his promo skills were not that great. After a string of losses he fired Elijah and decided to go on his own. He realised that the ladies loved him and brought on 'The Nihilist' gimmick with it came the 'I'm Just Too
Cool, Baby' persona. He finally got on track with his skills and worked to make them even better. At this time he had beaten Southern
Man, The Dutch Boy and the biggest feather in his cap, Seth Blitzpear. He beat Seth with a bag of quarters, behind the ref's back. This gave him amazing heel heat, the heel heat that has followed him to this day. He went on to win the 'WarLord' title, his very first national televised title from Max Doom.
He then was poached by the Armageddon federation which was much closer to Ronny's home of Las Vegas, Nevada. Ronny had his last match in front of WAR! fans and had a rematch in a steel cage with Max Doom for the WarLord title. Ronny won his last ever match in WAR! and went to Armageddon.
In the federation, as always with him, he started slowly but improved so much, to the extent that he won the Television title twice, the
BattleZone ( extreme) title twice, the U.S title three times, and eventually won the World title but was later stripped of it. He did this all in six months. The fans loved to hate him and most of the locker room just wanted to kick his ass. Due to being stripped of the title for no showing at title match he was stripped. He tried his all to get back to that title match but was held down by his nemesis,
Commissioner Apollo DeMarco. Ronny decided to powerbomb the commish through his desk and was fired.
Ronny again sauntered around the world, wrestling, giving women babies, gaining a massive fan base. He was then called into the new federation called the BattleZone wrestling federation or BZWF as it was called. There he won the tag team titles and the TV title. In somewhat of a surprise, he revealed himself as BZWF champion
Insomnia. He fooled them into giving him a title shot and beat Steve Swanson , The Butcher and another to win the gold. Quickly his hatred , that had grown since the days of WAR!, started to ebb out.
His hatred for Larry Spikowski. They had an I quit match in which Ronny was robbed of his win by Wolfgang.He left the BZWF forever Ronny was seen in a few federations around that time, CWF, NGWF, PCWF to name a few. Then Raw picked up his services and thought that seeing as that Ronny had already won a tag team title before he'd be put in the tag ranks with newcomer Abe Chrome. This was a terrible duo. They stank the joint. Ronny was obviously not trying and wanted singles action. Ronny connived with Indiana Casanova and a few others to 'kill' Chrome. Ronny joined the sex stable and had a terrible
Union ppv due mainly to the bookers. He then went about finding someone he hated in the locker room and dismantle them. That man was a near legend, that man was The Knick Man.
After a good feud Knick Man won and Ronny was on his own again. Even though he lost the feud, he seemed mentally stronger. He went on to win The Capital title off Jon Smythe due to interference from Neil
McDougall. His reign was immense, especially for him. He beat his opponents and lost a few non title matches. Feuds with William
Benentt and Larry Spikowski all the way to lacklustre feuds with Wolfgang to his New Breed stable mates. Ronny lost his Capital title to Travis LaGrange. He had the last several months as 'the greatest ever North American champion' until the Internecine ppv.
There are now serious doubts whether 'The Icon' will ever fully recover, let alone wrestle again. We all wish him a spedy recovery and a happy, if not early and obvious, retirement.
