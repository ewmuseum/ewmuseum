RAW at WAR Report 8/9/00

9 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This weeks RAW at WAR was held at the Municipal Auditorium in
Nashville, TN. The matches were booked by Bishop Righteous, who's protegé, the Anointed One, won an eight-man tournament at the previous Pay-Per-View to give him the right to book the event. The card was also special as it was the RAW at WAR debut of the new voice of RAW, Sean O'Brien and his cynical counterpart Buddy Robins.
The first showdown was a 4-Way "Baptism" Match in which the participants had to "baptise" an opponent in a ringside pool. In the match were David Hagen, Mike Slater, Kent Schmiddt and John
Smythe who was filling in for the departed Meursault Simmons.
The Sex Pistol and the Andrazoons interfered, the former taking
Hagen from the ring to backstage, the latter drained the pool.
When Scmiddt and Slater were inside the pool, it refilled and the match was awarded to Smythe. RAW President Miles Silverberg ordered the match to continue and Slater won shortly after, putting Smythe into the pool.
Former WAR! Champion, Jason Midnight, made his way to the ring after the match-up, signalling his desire to make life for Dirty
Doug Simpkins miserable.
The next match was an "Anointing" Tag Team match where oil placed in the middle of the ring had to be used on opponents in order to win. The teams were Deadboy and Joel Murphy against
Obsessive Compulsive Disorder and the Flying Scotsman. It just so happens that Deadboy and OCD are usually tag team partners and the Scotsman and Murphy and hated enemies. Murphy was the first to be anointed, by OCD. OCD's partner, Deadboy, sent him into the oil, leaving Deadboy and the Scotsman the only participants not to be oiled. OCD returned the favour by putting his partner in the oil, rendering OCD and Scotsman the victors.
In the first non-gimmick match on the card, Young Blood Champion "Nature Boy" Larry Spikowski faced Vik Ventressa. The match was fairly even, Spikowski doing his usual cocky heel routine and Ventressa unleashing amazing power moves.
Spikowski's protegé, Kent Schmiddt was rendered unconscious after he interfered and Ventressa sent him flying to the outside. David Hagen came out sent Spikowski flying into Kent, and whilst he and Ventressa brawled, both contestants were counted out.
The fourth match was recently uncrowned RAW World Champion, Vic
Creed against the Rock N' Rap Connection and the Gods of
Destruction in a 4 on 1 handicap match. Creed won when the
Deities manager, Appollo came to ringside and Rock N' Rap connection chased him out. Slade Wilson, Creeds new manager distracted the ref whilst Creed hit the Kodiak Crippler on the
Big Man.
Next-up was technically a handicap match, as perennial fan favourite Indiana Casanova took on Scott Wyld, who's fellow members of the Sex Stable, the Sex Pistol and Larry Spikowski were Special Referee and Timekeeper respectively. Back and forth match, Casanova hitting some trademark highflying moves and
Wyld with his orthodox technical/brawling style. Casanova was pinned when Wyld's valet, Sarah distracted him and allowed Wyld to deliver the Evenflow DDT for the win.
The loser of the next match was never to wrestle a single match again, Knick Man was to take on Thomas Creighton. The Sex Pistol and Claire came out, the Sex Pistol was accidently moonsaulted onto by Knick Man and Claire was powerbombed through a table by
Creighton. Whilst the official was attending to Claire, Pistol snuck in and blasted Creighton with a chair, giving the win to Knick Man after the ref came back in. Is this the end for
Creighton?
The next match was a huge 6-Man, where the World Champion "Corruptor" Douglas McCray teamed with Tag Champions, the Deities against the combination of Anointed One and the
Deacons, Bishop Righteous' stable. All championships were on the line in this affair. The Champions all retained when McCray put
Righteous in an armbar, Righetous hitting the mat three times in pain as he had refused to count the pin in the middle of the ring.
