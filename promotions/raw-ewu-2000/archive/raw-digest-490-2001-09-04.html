Seated on a bench is the former Heavy Weight Champion of the World. A medical technician has finished stitching up his head and is using a pair of pliers to pull tacs out of his back. The big man, almost impervious to pain, doesn’t even flinch. He stares into the camera]
[Jugger] Travis LaGrange, the plot finally unfolds. Not one mystery partner, but two. Congratulations, Travis, you have become the third person in the history of wrestling to go through hell and back and score a pinfall over the Juggernaut.
[The Juggernaut grins. There is still blood in his teeth.] [Jugger] The question is, can you do it again? You may have the titles
LaGrange, but you didn’t win this match, not by a long shot.
I’m not down, I’m not broken, I’m not even tired. I’m just mad. Tonight, LaGrange, you had the element of surprise on your side. You had Vic Creed and Ronny Rameriz waiting in the wings. Now all the players have been revealed, it turns out you’re just a pawn in a much bigger game. The New Breed isn’t the gang of undercard rookies you led us to believe. All this time my own son in law Ronny
Rameriz has been cozying up to me and playing nice nice while a bitter
Kodiak Vic Creed has been waiting on the sidelines. Both of them with one goal, and that’s to take me out so they can have the World
Heavy Weight Championship all for themselves. [The technician begins swabbing alcohol on the Juggernaut’s back] [Jugger] Yeah, I saw Creed raise your hand too, LaGrange. But don’t kid yourself. You need to look at the big picture. All
Creed has done is set up someone whose just dumb and naive enough to believe he can actually defend those belts himself. You give Vic Creed enough time and he’ll be sweet talking you for a title shot.
Sort of a favour for a favour. Then, LaGrange, you won’t know what hit you. Think about it, Travis. Why the cover-up? Creed wont fight me head on. Vic Creed could have come back anytime to face the Juggernaut. Being the former RAW Champ, I would have given him a title shot in a second.
But Vic Creed knows I am the original Juggernaut. He saw what I did to
Doug McCray; the very man that sent him running away from this federation last year with his tail between his legs. You see, Vic
Creed, isn’t the Alaskan version of me, he’s just some low rent imitation that’s been living in my shadow for the last four years. He’s just setting you up to take away what’s rightfully mine because he knows he can’t take it from me himself.
[The technician takes out a scalpel and begins to cut away at the
Juggernaut’s back to pull out a piece of buried metal.]
[Jugger] But let’s not forget Ronny. The ever ambitious Ronny Rameriz. Now that you’re champ, LaGrange, maybe Ronny sees a little World gold in his future too. Maybe he thinks he’ll beat
Creed to the punch and get to you first. And, Ronnie, after all I’ve done for you in the past six months. You just had to turn around aand stab me in the back. You really disappoint me, son. I’ve feed you, clothed you, taught you table manners, even rid you of that habit of cutting your toenails at the kitchen table...No wait a second, that was me.
Come on, Ronny. The presents, the quality father/son time, the hooray for the WAR! Coalition cheerleading. Don’t tell me I didn’t see this turn coming a mile away. It was just a question of when. And to be honest, I am a little disappointed in the new company you’ve decided to keep. Really, Ronny, you could have done much better than Creed and LaGrange.
You see, your memory fails you, Rameriz. That’s your weak spot. Just like the Nature Boy always said. You’re too stupid to learn. You crossed me once and I put you through eight months of hell.
And yeah, I know you like to brag behind my back about how you supposedly kicked my ass. But let’s not kid each other. Just how many times did I send you to the hospital back in February? Hell, son, during our little feud you didn’t take me off my feet once. I seem to remember beating your brains out night after night, throwing you through plate glass windows, through tables and using your head as a battering ram on a vending machine. I let you win, Ronny. I let you have the count out victory back in Febuary. All for marrying my daughter. Call me sentimental. But that’s the way I am.
Though, I have to admit, Greaseball, the last eight months were a real hoot. You really made me laugh. That’s what I like about you.
Bringing home your little undercard title belts. Acting all macho and shit, telling everybody how tough you are when deep down you know the thought of stepping in the ring against me again a second time makes you piss your pants.
I made you my pet, Ronny. You were the Bennett family dog. And my daughter may love you with all her heart and soul. Hell, my son, may love you with all his heart and soul. But ol’Yeller, you’ve gotten mean, you’ve turned on your master. Now it’s time I put you to sleep despite what my family thinks.
[The medical technician takes some thread and begins to stitch up the
Juggernaut’s back] [Jugger] LaGrange, this ain’t over. Not by a long shot. One by one, I’m going to take out each member of the New Breed until that leaves just you and me. Now that the masks are off, your two partners have no where to hide. Creed and Rameriz’s days in this federation are numbered.
[The technician pats Bennett on the back. He indicates he’s done patching him up. Bennett suddenly has a revelation.]
[Jugger] Gee, Travis, I guess it’s kind of funny. Isn’t it? Now you finally know how it feels to be on the other side. The hunter has now becomes the hunted. Except instead of getting to hunt a big ol’preditor like myself, all I get is a wittle bunny wabbit to step on.
Think you can handle another war with me, Outlaw? I sure as hell don’t. (Prologue) 9:50 p.m. Blood pouring down his face and nearly exhausted, Travis LaGrange knew he was in trouble as he was high in the clutches of another Juggerbomb. This time he knew it was going to hurt a whole hell of a lot more than the last one.
"Dammit, those tacks were REALLY a bad idea," LaGrange thought to himself as he sat on the broad shoulders of
William Bennett, the man known as Juggernaut. Bennett tossed him toward the silvery thumbtack-covered mat as
LaGrange braced for the invevitible. "Here it comes," He thought. "The sharp pain ... the complete darkness ... time elapsing with out me knowing it. I just hope I don't die."
Bennett released the at the apex harder than he had in the past. The look on his face was one of complete hatred. Both their eyes locked, it seemed like forever, until Travis impacted the mat.
There it is ... sharp pain ... darkness. 10:05 p.m. "A light," LaGrange thought as he slowly regained conciousness. "WHOA!!! WHAT IN THE HELL!!!"
He was taken a back by the rather large arms lifting him up off the mat like he was a sack of feathers. The arena was in a state of complete bedlam. He had never heard such cheering in his entire life and strangely, it was music to his ears.
"Thank God," He thought to himself. "I'm alive." LaGrange looked up to see a blurry blonde-haired figure with sunglasses and a smile. He could barely make out the features through the mask of blood on his face, but somehow, he was sure he knew the man holding him up.
Travis could barely stand. All of his energy was sapped from his legs and the concussion he had suffered at some point in the match had his head swimming. Somehow, in all the commotion, he nearly forgot why he was here in the first place.
"What happened?" Travis asked the huge blonde man. "You won, kid," responded the man known to wrestling fans around the world as "Kodiak" Vic Creed. "You won."
Creed steadied Travis as the referee draped the RAW World Title around one shoulder and the WAR! World Title around the other. Was this some sick joke? Or was he really the World Champion? Travis mustered up the strength to look back as Creed helped the Outlaw out of the ring. He saw Juggernaut, face up in a pile of thumbtacks, tables broken, turnbuckles melted and bodies scattered.
"Did I do that?" quizzed LaGrange. "Well, most of it," responded Creed. LaGrange laughed to himself as the adulation continued to rain down on the new champ. The plan had worked! He had finally done it. Six long years. The pain, the training, starving, the constant promises, the bad payoffs, the crooked promoters ... and it was all worth it.
Just to hear one simple sentence ... "And NEW Heavyweight Champion of the World! "The Outlaw" Travis LaGrange!" He was the champion of the world. All of his goals he had strived for all of his life had been reached. He was as happy as a man could possibly be and was sure that when everything had sunk in, he would be even happier.
