RAW-at-WAR Report 8/28/00

29 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This weeks RAW at WAR, from the Coliseum, Richmond, VI began with "The Nihilist" Ronny Ramirez intellectualising, and I use that term lightly, his tag-team match against his arch- nemesis, "Nature Boy" Larry Spikowski and partner
Kent Schmiddt. The cameras shifted to the locker room as a mystery man nailed Spikowski, the man revealing himself as the new manager of the
Masters of Disaster, the very team that Ramirez forms half of. In the first match-up, the Masters of Disaster beat the team of Larry Spikowski and Kent Schmiddt, after Spikowski walked out on his nemesis, Abe Chrome making the cover. In a six-man encounter, Mike Slater teamed with the Merseysiders to take on "Blacksmith" John
Smythe and the Andrazoons. Mid-way through the match, crack RAW reporter Toxic Shock, uncovered footage of RAW President Miles Silverberg instructing the Merseysiders to give no help to
Slate whatsoever. This was evident in the end, Smythe getting the pin on Slater, who had been left in a 5 on 1 situation. Next-up was supposed to be a handicap cage match, but Dirty Doug Simpkins was scratched after an arm injury, leaving his protegé Stevie Ray
Murdoch to face Simpkins' hated enemy, Jason Midnight. Back and forth action here, Midnight getting the victory after falling to the outside when he and Murdoch were perched on top of the cage. In an insanely suicidial spot, Murdoch leapt off the top in a shooting star press shortly after the conclusion, leading to another
post-match beating of Midnight, administered by Murdoch and Simpkins. In a triangle match, Obsessive Compulsive Disorder, Sex Stable member Scott Wyld and ex-Sex Stable member the Sex Pistol were to battle. Midway through the match however, Wyld and Pistol revealed that their supposed split up was a set- up, working together against OCD. OCD came out victorious after Indiana Casanova interfered,
DDTing the Sex Pistol, allowing OCD to make the cover. Miles Silverberg came out afterwards and said that he couldn't reverse the decision, but he would force OCD and Casanova to face Wyld and
Pistol later in the evening. Bishop Righteous' Deacons were to get a Tag Team title shot against the Deities next. Thomas
Creighton brought an end to the match when he ran to ringside he nailed Bishop Righteous with a sledgehammer, giving the DQ win to Deities.
Last week Indiana Casanova won the RAW Power Title from Scott Wyld, who was defending the title for "The Flying Scotsman" Neil McDougall who was overseas, and this next match was an opportunity for the Scotsman to reclaim his championship. Scott Wyld came to ringside during the match, distracting McDougall, allowing
Casanova to score a quick win over the unlucky Scotsman. Thomas Creighton came to ringside net and demanded that he be given a handicap match against Bishop Righteous' stable, the Holy
Alliance. Righeous came out, soon to be followed by Knick Man who also demanded a shot at Righteous' men. Miles Silverberg made a compromise by announcing that Creighton and Knick Man would team up against the Holy Alliance at the next RAW-at-WAR, only with on more stipulation... Bishop Righteous would be particpating with his Holy Alliance.
David Hagen and Vik Ventressa were matched up in an attempt to settle their differences. This proved futile as the two brawled to the outside, the match ending in a double count-out. Douglas
McCray, the World Champion, one of Ventressa's opponents in the triangle match at the next PPV, came out and attacked Ventressa, a move that shocked the live audience.
Even though he was agruably deserved of a World Title shot, Eddie Drysdale was relegated to a North American title shot this week, against Knick Man. Drysdale won by count-out after he back dropped Knick Man from the ring and face first into the guard rail.
The next encounter was the match Miles Silverberg announced earlier in the night, Indiana Casanova and Obsessive Compulsive Disorder taking on Scott
Wyld and "The Sex Pistol" Jaimé Roberts. OCD deserted his partner when Casanova, who desperately wanted a piece of Scott Wyld, didn't tag out. Casanova chased after OCD and they engaged in fistacuffs, the pair losing the match by count-out.
The main event featured the World Champ, "Corruptor" Douglas McCray, against the man who he took the title from, Vic Creed. After back and forth action with several near- falls, McCray scraped the win out of the barrel,
Creed losing control and punching the referee, leading to a disqualification victory for the "Corruptor". Vik Ventressa appeared on the video screen after the match, in McCray's locker room, holding his fiancé Marie by the hair. When
McCray ran to aid her, Creed clocked him from behind and Creed-bombed him, whilst Ventressa laid a huge slap on the face of Marie, as
RAW-at-WAR concluded. Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
