RAW at WAR Report 9/7/00

10 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The latest RAW at WAR, from the Civic Center, Charleston, WV, began with Shawn Fury hosting a talk show-esque segment. He had some strong words regarding Zymas Meraten, and the other four participants in the
Five-Man Boiler Room match at Internecine, the upcoming PPV.
The first match was Joel Murphy vs Copperhead. In a short match, Murphy got the submission victory after Copperhead tapped-out to the Figure
Four.
Stevie Ray Murdoch and bluechipper Mike Slater were up next. Mid match,
Jason Midnight appeared on the video screen, telling Murdoch that he was going to feel pain later on in the night. Whilst Murdoch was distracted Slater knocked him into the ring steps a count-out victory.
After that match, Shawn Fury flattened-out Joel Murphy and we also saw a Dirty Doug Simpkins vignette, about old school athletes. Dirty Doug visited the commentary booth after the video, to be the color commentator for the Jon Smythe and Jason Midnight clash.
Midnight had most of the offense in the early stages of this match, but
Smythe got the upperhand after interference from his valet, Lolita and
Doug Simpkins. This backfired however, with Simpkins accidently nailing
Smythe, Midnight soon there after picking up the win.
Before his match with the Flying Scotsman, Indiana Casanova spoke with
Toxic Shock backstage, claiming that he'd go into Internecine as the
RAW Power Champion. Scotsman had other plans however, and picked up a moral victory, via count-out, after Jason Midnight took out Casanova.
OCD and Scott Wyld met in what was another of their brutal match-ups. With back and forth action, and several near falls, OCD took the win with open arms after Wyld allowed him to hit the Descent into Madness
(Death Valley Driver).
The Deities didn't have to defend their tag team belts against reasonably new on the scene Masters of Disaster. It disintergrated into an all-out slugfest after the Rock and Rap Connection and the League
Against Stupidity interfered, the match ending in a double disqualification as the four teams brawled.
David Hagen was to fight his Sex Stable enemy, Larry Spikowski next-up. Hagen hit some brutal high-risk offense before the Ronny Ramirez, disguised as a hot dog vendor, attacked Spikowski. The match seemingly ended in a no-contest as Thomas Creighton and Vik Ventressa engaged in a beat-down on both competitors.
Creighton returned soon after, to team with Knick Man against Bishop
Righteous, the Deacons and the Anointed One, otherwise known as the
Holy Alliance. Creighton and Knick Man picked up the win after Vik
Ventressa nailed all members of the Holy Alliance with a picket sign.
Mike Slater when he was leaving the arena, Shawn Fury being his accomplice. At the time at least it was unforseen whether he had sustained a serious injury or not.
"The Sex Pistol" Jaimé Roberts, burst onto the main-event scene, teaming with Eddie Drysdale to take on the previous World Champion, Vic
Creed and Vik Ventressa in the main event. Creighton came out early, only to have RAW President Miles Silverberg disallow him to be at
ringside. "The Double Vs" squeaked out the win, and post-match Creed, Creighton and Ventressa continually stomped on Drysdale until Mike
Slater, who made a miraculous recovery from the earlier incident, and the World Champ, Doug McCray came out to make the save.
The Sex Pistol tried to talk as he made his way up the ramp to McCray but he hit him with the Devil's Deal, ending any discussion that might have occurred. Mike Slater offered his hand in friendship to McCray, but Slater was the next victim of the Devil's Deal. As RAW at WAR concluded McCray and Drysdale were dominating Ventressa and Creed.
