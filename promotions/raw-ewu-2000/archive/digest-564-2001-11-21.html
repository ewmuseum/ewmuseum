===== OOC- For those of you who know me and the Mark Adams character, you'll know he was originally created as a throwaway character for use in an SWF angle that was only meant to span a month or two.
Well, somehow that month or two kept getting stretched out until, as of his "death" on November 18, 2001, Mark Adams' two month stint as an e-wrestler had managed to last almost 26 months. Along the way, Adams made more comebacks (from injuries sustained in Japanese Table Death Matches, Loser Leaves
Matches, Hardcore Barbed-Wire Baseball Bat Matches, and the list goes on) as a hardcore wrestler than one might consider normal and, somehow, he managed to win more titles in more federations (SWF, HIWF, UWF, RAW-C, RAW-A, RAW-UK, and
RAW) than both my other singles characters combined, ending his existence as the most decorated tag team champion in the history of RAW (if my historian spies are correct).
Not bad for a throwaway character, eh? Anyhow, there was never any question that the only way to end Adams' run was to make sure he COULDN'T come back and, despite the outcries of "Jesus, John, kill another character why don't you!!", the career of "Iceman" Mark Adams, the self-proclaimed "Hardcore Sensation," is now officially...over.
My thanks to Chris for creating the Mark Adams character in the first place, Sean for taking part in this angle, Frank for helping to make Adams' curtain call a spectacular one, and Mark Adams...for being an interesting part of the last two years of my e-wrestling career.

Kirk gets another win here, and the Maclean bandwagon seems to have found the rails again, with merchandising sales and success in the ring both booming after he reverted to the ‘Hurricane’ persona. Despair looked impressive as ever, but it’s fairly obvious that Spiky and Despair’s
‘family’ are weighing heavy on his mind. As for Khan, rumour has it that he’s headed back to Windy City while he would undoubtedly be a big fish in a small pond there, it’s unclear as to whether he has the drive to get to the top of RAW itself, although the talent is unquestioned.
OMEGA Vs. RIC BEAUTY OMEGA and RIC BEAUTY have had their problems in recent weeks, with Omega joining with Lucifer Hex in attacking Beauty at RAW Aggression 15 days ago, and then again at last Aggression, this time hospitalizing him. Beauty has reformed his alliance with OCD, but hasn’t quite found his footing in RAW yet.
Beauty also had problems, clearly not having recovered from his beatdown at Aggression, and many fans expressed doubt at whether or not he should be wrestling. That said, he started off pretty well, matching Omega hold for hold in a bout which was certainly one of the better technical matches seen in RAW recently, and managing to get the big man down in an ankle lock, although the hold had to be broken when his opponent got to the ropes fairly quickly. Beauty also showed that he could fight dirty if necessary, getting out of a pounding by using an eye gouge, and hitting a low blow to avoid being suplexed.
Unfortunately for the ‘Twenty Four Hour Man’, though, Omega’s stamina is nigh on legendary, and unlike Ric, he hadn’t just got out of hospital. Taking control of the match at the six minute mark, he blasted Beauty with a couple of powerbombs, working on re-injuring his back, then hit a great sequence of moves culminating in a Beatdown, before putting on the Omegatron Crossface for a submission win.
So, a good victory for Omega, but Beauty will take heart from his performance – better results will follow if he wrestles like that all the time.
NORTH AMERICAN TITLE MATCH RONNY RAMIREZ (C) Vs. STARMAN Prior to this match, TONY 'STARMAN' QUINT had a very impressive record in RAW, winning four out of four matches. But would the streak which had taken him past guys like Valec Corso, Adam Mitchell, and Khan, be enough to carry him to victory against arguably (and in the eyes of [name withheld] and Tommy
Creighton, especially, VERY arguably), RAW’s greatest ever NA Champion, RONNY RAMIREZ? Ramirez kept his belt at Aggression due to a no contest in the four way match which degenerated into a huge brawl, but was looking to get back to winning ways here.
The crowd were, unsurprisingly, hugely behind Quint here, wearing replica Starman T-shirts containing mottoes such as ‘I HAVE HUGE FACE HEAT... IS WHAT I WOULD SAY IF I WAS LYING FACE DOWN ON A RADIATOR’. However, it seemed like they’d be disappointed from the off, as Ramirez proved a tougher guy to deal with than any of Starman’s opponents yet in RAW. Ronny took it straight to the goofy face with a barrage of suplexes, piledrivers, and powerbombs.
However, Quint quickly managed to adjust to the tougher opposition, and came back with some great moves of his own, including a flying cross chop and a somersault kick which had fans of a certain age reminiscing about a video game.
Ronny managed to reverse a Quint attempt at a superplex, though, dropping him face first into the mat, and then tried to finish things off with an
Iconic Gesture. Quint ducked, however, not once, but twice, and got a crucifix to get an oh-so-close 2 and 3/4 on the champion. Unfortunately for Starman, this served to rile Ronny more than anything, and he scooped up the challenger, and nailed him with the Iconhilation... only for Starman to kick out on 2 and 7/8!
Ramirez, at this point, went berserk, going to the outside, and charging back in with a chair... but Starman ducked a chair shot, dropkicked Ronny into the corner, and as Ramirez staggered out, the challenger caught him with
RAW’s smallest package for the three! A HUGE win for the RAW rookie Quint, of course, getting the NA belt at such an early stage in his career. However, defending it will no doubt test him to the limits, just as it did Ronny. As for Ronny, he lost the belt, but after holding it so long, there can be no question of his talent and even without the gold, we can expect to see him at the top of the card for some time to come.
WORLD TITLE MATCH 'THE OUTLAW' TRAVIS LAGRANGE (C) Vs. LITTLE LOUIE RAW Report rarely gets to see World title defences, but the Japanese fans of RAW want to see as much of the hot 24-year old RAW Champion while they can. The truth of the matter is, Lagrange is the big draw now. In a matter of months he has shot from the relative obscurity of being a PCWF title holder to being at the very top of the top. Big matches and money are now his to grasp.
But big opponents are in waiting too, not least of whom is Tonnage, the 7ft, 525lb beast of pure muscle from Albany, New York, who is waiting to face Travis Lagrange at Internecine. Would it be for the world title? Not if Little Louie, Tonnage's manager, good friend and RAW light-heavyweight competitor had anything to do with it. Here on Raw Report, the sklled 5'8, 221lb Louie was given the chance to become the champion of the world.
The Japanese fans were firmly behind Lagrange. Louie to them is an unknown to many of them, but Lagrange is a man with traits that the Japanese fans have seen recently and admired. They were expecting a walk in the park for
Lagrange, and maybe Lagrange was too, but that wasn't what he got fromt he resolute Little Louie who matched Lagrange move for move and then some when they engaged the first five or six minutes of the match as a demonstration of knowledge of wrestling holds and maneuvers. The bottom line was, Little Louie was getting much the better of Lagrange, knowing more moves, more counters and apparently being a few thoughts ahead of Lagrange in the game.
Lagrange changed his gameplan, and the game itself changed drastically. Little Louie is only 5'8, short for a wrestler, and isn't much of a hardcore wrestler. Lagrange started attacking those weaknesses, getting hardcore with Louie, throwing him around. Louie kept on fighting back, getting pinfall attempts when possible but Lagrange moved into champion mode and there wasn't much doubt as to what would happen. Sure enough, at the twelve minute mark, Lagrange hit Little
Louie with the Lagrange Trail Driver, to the chants of 'L-T-D' from the crowd, and got the three count.
The match was over. Little Louie has put up a strong challenge to Lagrange, but it wasn't enough. The fight wasn't over yet though. The fans demanded another L-T-D ... and Lagrnage was more than happy to oblige. Still the fans cried out for another L-T-D...and this time Lagrange did it outside the ring, on exposed concrete. Little Louie was out cold. At this point Tonnage started to come down the aisle to help his friend, but Tiny was on hand to nail
Tonnage from the side with a chair shot to the side of the head...three times to get the big man down. The medics came down to the ring, put Little Louie on a stretcher and began to take him away, but the World Champion pulled the unconscious Louie off the stretcher again, yelled 'Are you watching Tonnage?' and hit the L-T-D on Little
Louie for a fourth time, this time planting his skull onto the RAW World title belt. The message to Tonnage was loud and clear. But maybe the message between the lines is that Lagrange knows that Tonnage is the biggest threat to his title reign yet.
