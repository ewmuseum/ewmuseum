RAW WRESTLING FEDERATION http://ewu.efeduniverse.com/RAW RAW REPORT Fargodome, Fargo, North Dakota Wednesday May 16th, 2001. ISAAC TOGO Vs. THE MOCK The Mock seemed to be making a run for legitamacy, with a win over Jaime Roberts. However, he fell flat on his face last week against Jay Lyons. Togo is an unknown quantity, and the front office will be watching his performance carefully here. WOLFGANG Vs. GLITTERIS In the first of two matches featuring the Androzoons in singles action, Glitteris will take on the giant Wolfgang, making his RAW in- ring debut TOMMY PAYNE Vs. SUCCUBUS Will the Androzoons be able to defeat their opponents in singles matches? Tommy Payne will do his best to say NO! TOMMY LIONHEART Vs. JASON MIDNIGHT Jason Midnight has finished his war against John Williams, and now faces Tommy Lionheart, who should be an interesting challenge for > Midnight SPYKE & KARL MERCURY Vs. BEDFORD & ROBERTS Coming off of their match against the Androzoons at R@W..., Bedford and Roberts face Spyke and Muldoon, and if Bedford and Roberts are tag champions, the titles WILL be on the line. ARRON IRON Vs. JERRY GORDON The Goon faces the Carribean Sensation in one on one competition. THE BINKIES Vs. THE WAR MACHINES The Killer Clowns have been quiet of late, and will have quite a handful with the newcomers, the War Machines. JEFF KEENAN Vs. JESSIE MULDOON As it has been said in other places at other times, this could main event in any arena in the country. Jeff Keenan will make his RAW Debut against Jesse Muldoon, who is desparately trying to find a place for himself in RAW ******************************* * MAIN EVENT! * * NORTH AMERICAN TITLE MATCH * * 'FLYING SCOTSMAN' * * [name withheld] * * Vs.
