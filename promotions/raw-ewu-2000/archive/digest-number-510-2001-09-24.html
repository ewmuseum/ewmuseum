["The Sexy Boy" Travis LaGrange lays on his bed reading a wrestling sheet. The sheets are pulled up to his abdomen and the four girls that were in Travis'
limo earlier are trying to sort out their clothes and are getting dressed. It seems the former Outlaw is selling everyone out, including his girlfriend Tanya
Maynard.] Girl #1: C'mon Travis, just one more time? Girl #2: Yeah, we've got a few more minutes before we have to go back to work. LaGrange: Woman, I'm not a machine ... 'sides I need my rest, gotta big match against [name withheld] at R@W this week ... hell, I wasn't even supposed to have sex, ruins my focus.
Girl #3: You seemed pretty focused to me just an hour ago. [Girls giggle and finish getting dressed, they begin to leave, but are met at the door by ... TANYA
MAYNARD!!! Ooops. How's the champ gonna get out of this one?] Maynard: TRAVIS!!! LaGrange: Whoa, whoa. It ain't what it looks like ... these young women are just ... um ... accountants from the RAW offices, they were ... going over my assets.
Girl #1: You bet we were and he's got a nice one too. [Girls giggle again and close the door.] Maynard (on the verge of being upset): I can't believe you ... LaGrange: Hey, hey ... I'm insulted! You think I'd cheat on you?! C'mon, whaddya take me for?
Maynard: A male whore. LaGrange: Oooh, that was low, Tanya. Low. How come ya cain't trust me? Have I ever lied to you?
[Silence.] LaGrange: Now then. C'mere. [Maynard sits down on the bed and Travis hugs her. Smarmy bastard.] LaGrange: We got to stay together ... Neil's trying in his own little way to break us apart and I'm tellin'
ya now, it ain't gonna work ... I refuse ta believe that some little midget in a kilt is gonna take the most important thing in my life right now ...
Maynard (fooled): Oh Travis ... I'm sorry ... LaGrange: Yeah, these World Titles mean everything to me ... [SLAP!!!] Maynard: You bastard. [Maynard runs out of the hotel room and slams the door.] LaGrange: Geez, wonder what her problem was? [Door opens ...] LaGrange: Tanya, I'm ... [It's Eric Helmroy, Travis exhales ... then begins to get riled up.] LaGrange: Eric, I've been readin' this wrasslin' sheet and it says here you fired William Bennett ... why?
Helmroy: He killed you Travis. LaGrange: Um, Eric, hello? I'm right here ... you ain't been talkin' to Ricky, have ya? Helmroy: He was just getting too violent and we're steering RAW into a more family friendly-type of programming. William Bennett, is not capable of playing ball like you are, Trav. We just can't control him.
LaGrange: What?! You can't control him? Aren't I the World Champion? Helmroy: Yes. LaGrange: Didn't I kick his [Helmroy glares] ... butt? Helmroy: Yes. LaGrange: Well, what makes ya think I can't do it again? Helmroy: Travis I don't ... it's just ... LaGrange: It's just what? Helmroy: You really want at him don't you? LaGrange: Hell yeah, I want at him! Helmroy: Man, Travis you're one tough ... LaGrange [interrupts] Yer blowin' a multi-million dollar payday for me at The Big Card!! Good lord,
Helmroy, I can't believe you'd be that stupid! [Travis gets up out of bed and Helmroy cringes, but whew, no homoerotic situation, LaGrange is wearing bike briefs.]
LaGrange: I mean we gotta think of ME here. Right? Helmroy: So what are you saying? LaGrange: Maybe I can best express my feelings in a poem ... [Oh GOOD GOD!! He has sold out.] [Delivered in the classic Ali style.] "To Jugger" You thought you killed me, And that was cool. But I'm back from the dead, And I pity the fool. I need the buy-rate for money, to buy a new car. So reinstate Juggy, AT RAW AT WAAAR!!! [LaGrange begins dancing around, fancying himself like Ali as Helmroy looks on and smiles. Seated at the head of the table is the Nature Boy wearing one of his polyester/wide tie combos. In front of him are two stacks of files. A lawyer conducts his presentation.]
[Lawyer] For your convenience, Mr Spikowski, I have arranged this month's lawsuits by category.
[The Nature Boy anxiously rubs his hands together. He may be the boss, but it's not very often Eric Helmroy (the real boss) actually lets him do anything.]
[Spikowski] Let's go with the big pile first. Lay it on me. [Lawyer] We call this the William Bennett pile. [Spikowski] That's some sort of joke right? [Lawyer] No. [The Lawyer picks up the first file and reads it out loud] [Lawyer] Ralph Thompson vs. RAW Wrestling Federation. Apparently, Bennett attack Mr Thompson in an Orlando airport washroom last month and stole his tie clip.
[Spikowski] That crazy, Juggs. What a character. What next? [Lawyer] Rebbeca Slater vs RAW Wrestling Federation. Ms. Slater is accusing Bennett of attacking her in a grocery store and taking a package of Mentos from her purse. [Spikowski] Next. [The Lawyer retrieves another file] [Lawyer] Bernard Kovolski vs RAW Wrestling Federation. Bennett killed his dog. [Spikowski] Will's never been good with animals. [Lawyer] Ned Chow vs RAW Wrestling Federation. Bennett attacked a waiter in a Cantonese restaurant. Apparently, not enough plum sauce for his egg roll.
[Spikowski] Wait a second. Why are we being sued for Will assaulting people? They should be suing him. [Lawyer] Oh, that. [The Lawyer reaches into this coat pocket and retrieves a card. He hands it to the Nature Boy]
[Lawyer] Apparently, after Mr Bennett has finished his assault, he's been handing this card out with your name on it.
[Spikowski reads the card out loud] [Spikowski] ....Attention: This person is legally not responsible for his actions. For all damages and liability claims please contact Larry Spikowski at the RAW Wrestling Federation.
[Spikowski puts down the card] [Spikowski] This is ridiculous. Who gave him permission to do this? [Lawyer] I understand he had the cards made up at his own expense. Not to mention he stole a bunch of our stationary before he left the building last week. Next month's Bennett pile should be a doozy.
Shall I skip the rest of the assaults and move on to the copyright infringements? [Spikowski] Copyright infringements? [Lawyer] Yes, apparently there is an issue with the Disney people. [Spikowski] But I just spoke to them on the phone yesterday. They said Juggsy vacated the Magic Kingdom Castle with out any incident. [Lawyer] Yes, but as you recall they originally agreed to let Bennett stay in the park because of his uncanny resemblance to their Quasimodo character.
They felt his presence would largely be ignored at the park as just another one of the lovable Disney characters.
[Spikowski] So what's the problem? [Lawyer] He looks more like Shrek which is a Dream Works Studio character. [Spikowski] Let me guess. We're being sued by them too. [Lawyer] Michael Eisner is pissed. [The Lawyer skips to the next file] [Lawyer] That brings us to the Bee Gees. [Spikowski] The Bee Gees? What the hell do they want? [Lawyer] They are suing us for stealing Juggernaut's "Saturday Night Fever" entrance music. [Spikowski] But Jugger swore to me that it was a sample and we weren't breaking any laws. [Lawyer] Apparently, Mr. Bennett and the Bee Gees had a special arrangement that was nullified when you terminated Mr. Bennett from RAW.
[Spikowski] What was that? [Lawyer] As long as the Bee Gees didn't sue, Bennett promised not to fly to England and punch Barry Gibb in the head. [The Nature Boy rubs his temples. He can feel a headache coming on.] [Lawyer] What do you want to do? [Spikowski] Settle out of court. What's in the next pile? [The Lawyer picks up the second set of files and sets them down in front of the Nature Boy]
[Lawyer] We call this one the Spikowski pile. [Spikowski] What? [Lawyer] Apparently the good news is this is a class action lawsuit. [Spikowski] Then what's the bad news? [Lawyer] The bad news is your vasectomy is leaking again. You're being sued for seventeen counts of child support.
[Spikowski] Crap.
