[ You'd think Ivan Kinski would change out of his dirty, blood stained boiler suit, wouldn't you? But no, even in the Hotel Swanky in Rome,
Kinski doesn't change, nor does he care about the stares he gets as he walks along the corridor to his booked-well-in-advance penthouse suite with
Lulu, the hardcore Llama...] IK(to elderly couple): Yeah, I'm going to my hotel room with a Llama. Big whoopin' deal? Wanna fight about it?
[ The elderly couple scuttle off ] IK: You know what Lulu, I'm proud to come from New Jersey... [ Big fan pop from behind a closed door - "Yay East Rutherford". Kinski stops and goes to the door... ]
IK: ...But Rome is a bee-yoo-tee-ful place to visit while we're here. [ He pulls open the door suddenly and a 7ft woman is squatting in a cleaners cupboard wearing a dress made of bricks ]
Woman: What? What do you want? IK (puzzled): Nothing...just...checking to make sure...all the...umm...chemicals are stored...safely.
[ Kinski closes the door, shrugs his shoulders and carries on walking down the corridor with Lulu his hardcore Llama ]
IK: You know, you get some really odd people staying in hotels...ah, here it is, room 238, my favorute number and coincidentally the only room in all of Italy with facilities especially for Llamas. How lucky is that? I don't know what we'd do if we hadn't got this room. Apparently we've got to share with someone, but that's cool with me
[ Kinski opens the door...the suite is in a complete mess, and sitting there on the sofa, quite at home, is...]
IK: BARBARA BUSH?...No, I mean... ELI STEERPIKE? What the whoop are you doing in MY room? And what the whoop are you doing dressed...or undressed like that?
[Steerpike smirks at Kinski, laying on the couch wearing a tattered pair of jeans. He's obviously taking quite a bit of amusement in Kinski's displeasure.]
ES: Your room? No no Ivan, I'm afraid you're mistaken. You see if you'd bothered to check with the hotel staff, you'd know that I booked this room well in advance.
Naturally you weren't bright enough to do that. [Steerpike smirks and Kinski balls up his fists and advances on Steerpike. At this point two large gentlemen in business suits appear to block Kinski's path, much to Steerpike's delight.]
ES: I wouldn't do that Kinski. I'm afraid my two friends here would take it rather poorly. You see I knew you'd try to take the room back, since no other hotel room in Rome has facilities for your llama. Therefore I contacted a couple of friends, local business men you know, to protect my interests.
[Steerpike sneers at Kinski, flicking a peanut at Lulu the Hardcore Llama. The llama bellows in annoyance and Steerpike laughs. Kinski's resulting attempt to strangle Eli is once again blocked by the suited men and the brawl is on. The fight seems about even, at least it does before a couple more suited gentlemen rush out to join the fray.]
ES[laughing as Kinski is outnumbered]: Wonderful friends you meet here in Rome. Too bad you don't seem to appreciate them though. Vinnie, Luigi, have your men show this buffoon and his beast out.
[Kinski struggles violently but the suited men are far to numerous for him or his llama to overcome. As he's dragged from the room however, he manages a few shouted comments.]
IK: You haven't heard the last of this Steerpike. You'd just now managed to whoop off East Rutherford's favourite son.
ES (mockingly): Big whoopin' deal IK (walking off with Lulu): Hey Steerpike... ES: Yeah? IK (a vengeful look in his eyes): Where's your mamma gone? [Styles listens out to the sound of echoes...] "HE never had to run for his life just to bring back enough food so he wouldn't get beaten at home. HE never had to suffer through scorching hot summers running 'errands' for those he despises"
Rick Styles: (begins to laugh) Are you trying to compare your life to Gigantor's?? Oh please. You may have had it rough in your civilization, Omega, but Gigantor knows very little about "civilization". For he has only known two lives. The Jungles of a third world country...and that same land's prisons.
[Styles shakes his head in disbelief] Rick Styles: You stole food to eat around your table, Omega? When you are standing in that ring, you will have a man staring back at you who hunted his own food and ate it raw, right in the spot that he killed it in order to survive. But this isn't about who had it rough. This is about who will have it worse-off come January 6th.
[Styles listens out to the sound of echoes...] "Gigantor only knows how to survive. You've been teaching him how to survive in the ring, but we aren't fighting by those rules, we are fighting by the rules I know."
Rick Styles: Wrong, Omega. The rules to this match are made by ME, Rick Styles. That was the agreement...a foolish bargain on your part. As far as the role of yourself and Gigantor...survival, the very thing you claim won't help him on that night.
[Styles reaches down and picks up a pebble. He tosses it with all his might over the cliff and watches it fall.]
Rick Styles: My suggestion to you, Omega, is to take what little time you have left and realize what you must do if you want to be able to walk away from your experience with Gigantor. Survival IS your only option, so I suggest you embellish the way your opponent does. It may be your ONLY chance in order to live and fight again.
[Styles turns his back on the cliff and walks towards his car, opening the driver's seat. He slowly climbs in, the look of confidence never leaves his face.]
Rick Styles: Know the rules, Omega? The rules are MINE to make. Be assured the deck is stacked. Survival is your ONLY option.
