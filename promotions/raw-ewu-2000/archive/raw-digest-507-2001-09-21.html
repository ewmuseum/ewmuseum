(The scene opens with a shot of a city street corner. It's busy, lots of vehicles passing by, and no small number of pedestrians availing themselves of the sidewalks in the area. Rather than examining the area, the cameraman focuses on the block across the street from him. A high black fence of wrought iron bars surrounds lush park grounds, with large plots of flowers and extensive greenery of all sorts. At the corner of the fence, nearest the cameraman, stands a gate, and over it is a metal sculpture of a fleur-de-lis, or some similar shape. The words "PUBLIC GARDENS" are emblazoned across the top of the gateway.
Just inside the massive iron doors, former WAR! superstar, and current RAW newcomer, Little Louie is sitting on a bench in the grass. He's traded in his suit for a light spring jacket and sweat pants; he seems to have been jogging sometime lately. He nods the cameraman over.)
[CM] This is a little off the beaten path, isn't it? [L] We were just here a week or so ago. [CM] That was a house show. [L] You don't come to house shows? Too good for them, are you? [CM] No, they just don't have much use for me there. [L] Can't argue with that. Anyway, why don't you tell the kids at home where we're at? [CM] Isn't that your job? [L] No, my job is to dismantle people in the ring. That, and do the taxes. Your job is to conduct the interview, and you can start by informing the folks about this beautiful place.
[CM] Alright, this is the Public Gardens, in downtown Halifax, Nova Scotia. [L] Yes, Halifax. Home of the former ECWF, my alma mater in the business; home of the Citadel, just about a block up the road from here; and home to a lot of people who can't get enough of myself and the Beast.
[CM] You don't seem very popular elsewhere... [L] There's a lot of reasons around that. People don't understand what's going on, and so they judge us by what they see. If they had all the background information, understood the motives behind the motions, I think they'd be a lot more appreciative of our efforts. Of course, the big man's not the easiest guy to get along with, so that's probably part of it.
[CM] *Most* of it, if you ask me. [L] Whatever. The point is, people around here remember us with great fondness. In fact, the roar of the crowds when we showed up was positively deafening. Bet you didn't know that. [CM] I can't say I really *believe* it, either. [L] Guess you had to be there. Anyway, I don't live in the past, whatever others might want to think of me. This is the present, and it's the present of which we'll speak. [CM] You going to add your condolences for the people in New York and Washington? [L] Don't forget those poor folks outside of Pittsburg. Actually, I thought I'd skip the kind of emotive response I've heard from others. Others have said what I'm feeling, so I'd just be repeating the sentiment in different words.
I'll say I was very blessed, in that none of my own loved ones were involved in any of the horrors last week, but that's no consolation to me.
I hope I don't sound crass, but I've shed my tears for those people. Really, I have no choice. It may seem cruel and unfair, but my line of work doesn't permit me the leisure of time to mourn. In my private time, I've allowed all those feelings of anger, guilt and sorrow to come out, and now, I have to go on with my work. Events have been staged, matches have been signed, and tickets have been sold, and if only five people show up for RAW at War tonight, so be it. I'm still duty bound to give one hundred and ten percent, and I will do just that.
[CM] What about our retired Capitol champion, Kirk Maclean? [L] I honestly don't understand the man. I mean, I *do*, and I don't. Clearly, he's confused, so much so he's confusing me, and a lot of other people, from what I can tell.
Here is a young man, only a few years under his belt, who's been unbelievably fortunate. Not only has he had the opportunity to work in a high class organization before, but was actually allowed to "graduate" to RAW after a brief stint in the Carolinas. He's held titles in all three of the federations in question, and yet, what is he doing now? He's sitting on the sidelines, making everyone chase him for an answer. How many people have been provided the kind of opportunities he's had, and multiple times to boot?
How many of those people had the ability to run with them? Not many, you can be sure of that.
Maclean, I've been where you were at. I was at the top of the game, and everybody wanted to be me. That never stopped me from looking for new challenges. What are you doing? You're taking the easy way out. You're squandering chances that a lot of really talented people need, and probably won't receive because of you. Well, *I'm* not wasting the one I've been given this time, and my only message to you is this. If you don't have the guts to do what you claim you want to do, get the hell out and make room for people that do have them.
I'm tired of listening to your watered-down comments. You want to be half- assed? Go sell shoes. Obviously, you aren't prepared to deal with life in the pros, and all that big talk you were spouting was as empty as your chest.
You have no heart, Maclean, and that's why, despite all the good things that have come your way, you still end up falling by the wayside. If people out there want to jeer me for telling you that, screw them. You're the one who looks in the mirror every morning, and sees the mediocre in your face, and
I'm not going to waste any more time with you. Accept the challenge, or bow out and stay gone. Be a man, or be a mouse, but make up your mind and be quick about it.
[CM] I guess this the feud he was talking about... [L] There's no feud between us. If anything, it's between him and Midnight. He and I have a battle to fight, certainly, but our feud is already over. Even before the terrible stuff last week, I'd let all that crap slide. I had my reasons for doing what I did, and he had his reasons for retaliating.
However, this business between us isn't over. I hate to use the word "closure", but we won't have it until this match takes place. If he's half the man he claims to be, he'll get back here and face me. I don't care about the Capitol belt; it can be non-title, he can give it back to Midnight, whatever. Let's just see him in the ring with me.
Anyway, ask me about Omega. [CM] Okay, what about Omega. [L] Glad you asked! (Dead silence.) [L] So much for humour. Omega is the quintessential wannabe. He *wants* to be a star, he *wants* to be respected, but really, he's neither. Right now, he's nobody special, so he spends his energy trying to get noticed. I'm sure it stems from being one of those pit fighters for all those years. He expects everybody to treat him the same as he was treated before, except that in this business, he's nothing. He thinks he's going to change that at my expense. He'd better think again.
Omega, we're somewhat alike. We've been involved in our respective careers for most of our lives, and we've developed pretty fearsome reputations with some people who know our work. However, I'm not switching businesses here.
I'm coming back to the one I've always been good at, while you're just getting started. Now, a lot of people like to think I'm past my prime, that
I'm just clinging to my dreams of yesteryear, and that I have no place among these young, virile studs of the modern wrestling scene. I hope you believe that along with them, because it'll make putting you on your face a lot easier. I'm not an old fuddy duddy. Hell, I'm not even old. I promise you,
I'm in as good, if not better shape as I've ever been, and more importantly, I'm hungry. Oh, I heard your brilliant speech about the Beast "sending me in to soften you up". You must not have been paying attention, pal. If there's anybody out there who's in less need of someone to soften somebody up for him than
Tonnage, I've yet to lay eyes on him. Moreover, he didn't arrange this little encounter. Nobody interceded on my behalf, unless you count the booking committee. They heard I wanted back in the ring, and took it upon themselves to set this up. Obviously, they thought this would be some sort of continuation of whatever you and Tonnage have going on. They're quite full of it, of course.
[CM] So, this doesn't involve your friendship with Tonnage? [L] Only so far as those two want a piece of one another. For me, this is just about getting my feet wet again, and for you, Omega, it should be about staying healthy enough to worry about the Beast ripping your lips off.
Everybody's saying I don't have a prayer. Everyone's calling me the underdog. That's just the way I like it. I've watched the big man tear smaller guys limb from limb, so I can appreciate how unpleasant that experience is. Hell,
I've *fought* the big guy, so trust me, I know. If you and the rest of the world think you're going to walk all over me, Omega, that's just peachy. You and they are going to be given a little history lesson. Fifteen years of training, schooling, and fighting don't get flushed down the tubes, simply because your opponent is larger than you are. If that was the case, the
Beast would never lose, and guys my size might as well retire. I'm not retired yet, Omega, but if you let everyone else's talk swell your head, you may well be after I'm finished with you. To you, this is probably just another paycheck. To me, it's coming back to where I belong, and I promise you, give me one opening, and I'll make you suffer in ways those fans will be cringing about long after we're both dead and gone.
[CM] He is a big, powerful man. How do you plan to stop him? [L] I don't. I plan to let him stop himself. More importantly, I plan to not stop myself. Sure, he's huge, but if he can't lay a hand on me, he's just a walking bullseye. I'm taking aim, Omega. Right between the eyes.
(Louie pulls a small box of popcorn out of his pocket, and begins feeding some ducks that waddle nearby. And with that, we return to your regularly scheduled programming, or something... :)
