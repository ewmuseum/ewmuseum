See HELP WANTED at the end of the FAQ. ______________________________ ~I~ General Information ______________________________ Welcome to RAW! We offer: - a fed-wide storyline - many years of tradition - the finest staff around - matches booked and written by humans - flashes, etc. sent out several times per day (grouped together) - moderated mailing list used to keep crap out of your mailboxes - fictional wrestlers only - an available character doctor - personal rapport with each handler - strictness yet fairness History lesson: This is RAW's third reincarnation. Originally founded by [name withheld] and [name withheld] in early 1996, RAW has always been the place for top notch competition. This time around, the competition will be even tougher, as we take it to the cutting edge of survival of the fittest. If you think you've got the goods, put them on the table in the elite RAW. If you make it, you make it. If you don't, go someplace else. There are several decent feds out there.
RAW started as the sister fed to the legendary e-fed WAR!. Now, RAW could be considered the sister fed to every other fed around. This RAW has been influenced and inspired by such great feds as the IWA, UUW, Armageddon,
WAR!, TOWL, AWA, and numerous others, and has learned from their successes and mistakes. Now RAW has re-emerged after Tyler Stevens (current RAW President) purchased the BattleZone Wrestling Federation from then owner James Powers (aka
MainFrame) for $80million. Powers has decided to use the money to found his "E-Wrestling University" ( http://www.ewu.org.uk ) as Stevens has plans of making RAW the greatest federation ever. Other things: RAW has a semi-open flash system. That is, flashes are moderated through the eGroups ( www.egroups.com ) mailing list, and will come out all together, probably about twice a day. Send your flash any time you want and don't worry about it not being sent out. Since we look over every message sent to the mailing list, we'll be able to remove handlers' junk mail, improper advertisement, and messages sent accidental to the list before it reaches your mailbox. No messages will be modified!
When it comes to rules, RAW uses this philosophy: THINK. Use common sense. Before you send out a possibly offensive roleplay, re-read it and try and see if anybody will be bothered by it. Racism, bigotry and such will NOT be tolerated. Other rules...
If you have a problem, direct it to the fed heads, NOT the group. We moderate postings anyways, and if we see you've sent something to the group that shouldn't have been (and it obviously wasn't in accident), you should prepare an explanation before we'll contact you. The fedheads will be more than willing to listen to your complaints and do their best to fix the problem or provide more information on the subject.
There will be NO abuse of the mailing list.. we'll see to it. Never, e-e-ever use anothers person's characters in a roleplay, in-ring, etc without the handler's permission!!! Example, don't have Wrestler X's valet appear in your RP if Wrestler X's handler hasn't approved it. There will be punishments handed down for this.
You can not attack any RAW staff (commentators, interviewers, etc) without the permission of the President. Chances are that we'll always give you the
OK but we need to know about it first! ______________________________ ~III~ Participation ______________________________ RAW plans to focus heavily on storyline development. We want stories and we want heat. They co-exist. Plain and simple. We want wrestlers who can deliver that heat. In order to do that, you must roleplay. RP'ing helps your chances of winning enormously. In fact, if you don't RP, you don't
win. To sum up, you MUST RP. But why wouldn't you? It`s all part of the fun, and if you don`t want to be part of the fun, what the hell are you doing here? Roleplays are to be sent to
[detail withheld] and we'll send them all out 2-3 times a day. If you really want to win a match, strats and in-rings (for the card) are important. In-rings are not necessary but strats are. They help the match writers piece together the match. Sending strats and in-rings should go like this:
Card Lineup is released. The handler sends a brief "outline/summary" of how he/she would like the match to go. (To be done as soon as possible within a two-day period following the released lineup.) Then the handler may send an elaborate strat-- more exact information that elaborates on the brief outline/summary. There will be a STRAT DEADLINE set when the lineup is released; follow this deadline!
Any time between the released lineup and the card itself, the handler may send an in-ring. An in-ring is a roleplay that is done in the ring (simple enough) or around the ring during the card. It primarily involves speaking into a microphone. (Please be reasonable in sending these in. Don't send it five minutes before the card is to be sent.)
NOTE: If you fail to send strats, you will have no say in how the match proceeds. If that's OK with you, fine, but be warned that it may lead to the disappointment of your expectations.
Going back to the issue of heat, it is important to create angles for your character. The fedheads would be glad to try and book you into a program with someone if you need assistance. But do try to get involved in an angle, even if it's the big one that everyone seems to be a part of. Don't forget, we have an ongoing angle that involves the entire fed. If you need to take a break from individual angles, are stumped, or want to develop the fed's angle, this is the one storyline to enter.
______________________________ ~IV~ Cards, PPV's, Etc. ______________________________ As of today, there are two shows in RAW, the big-daddy of them being "Sunday Night Main Event Live!". For the first few weeks, Sunday Night Main Event Live! (SNMEL) will be a bi-weekly card of @8 full play-by-play matches, written by some of the best writers in e-wrestling.
There is also a recap/highlights/dark match/interview show called the Retrospect aired every week that there is no SNMEL. This is where you get to find out if your wrestler made ____ of the Week, rumors and future events, updated roster and rankings, and awesome interviews. The show is still developmental so it may take until the first Pay Per View for it to go on the air.
Speaking of Pay Per View, the schedule looks like this... --April--Spring Beatings The "crown jewel" Pay-Per-View. @10-13 Top-Quality matches. All titles on the line! --June--BattleZone In the quest to see who the toughest man really is, eight men advance to BattleZone to fight a grueling single-elimination tournament. The first round matches are done under normal conditions... second round matches, are in steel cages. The two men left after the first two rounds enter a three level steel cage, with wooden floors and weapons everywhere... more details later.

______________________________ ~VI~ What Can You Win? ______________________________ There are three major singles titles in RAW. Those titles are as follows... World RAW Championship - The main title of the federation. Only the best of the best can vie for this title.
RAW North American Championship - The secondary title of the federation. Men who have proven themselves to be better than most, but can't quite seem to cut it at the top belong here.
RAW Young Blood Championship - The first step towards the top. Developed with the "newbie" in mind. This title goes to the most promising young talent RAW has to offer.
There is also an intricate ratings system (IRS) that is currently being developed (by [name withheld] and [name withheld]). This will help to identify who can compete for what title. In RAW, you MUST climb the ladder. You'll start out in the
Young Blood level, so don't expect to automatically be a contender for the World RAW Championship. You have to earn that. You'll hear more about the rankings in the future.
There is also going to be a Tag Team Division (if interest is high enough). We'll bring out the official World RAW Tag Team Titles once we have about eight teams signed. A Hardcore Title (of sorts) may appear as well if interest is high enough.
______________________________ ~VII~ Original Aspects of RAW ______________________________ RAW prides itself on being original. Here are just a couple of our "original ideas"... ~~Referees~~ The referees will be an invaluable portion of RAW. The referee will be vital to whether you win or lose. Referee's may be biased, paid off, or just incompetent. Each referee will have an indidual identity. More on this as we get closer to the grand opening.
~~Developmental Deals~~ Basically these are tryouts. When we want to weed out the good and the bad from our applicants, we'll put them on a developmental deal. What that means is they'll get booked against another Developmental Talent and the two must rp/strat to win (as usual). But it's stiff competition to get accepted. The "developmental" matches will be shown as a dark match on
SNMEL, and each Developmental Talent (DT) gets two chances. If they win their first match (dark match), they stay with the Developmental Deal until their second match (still against a different Development Talent). If they win that match, they are officially accepted. This will probably begin after the "Spring Beatings" PPV and the fed has really taken off. If they lose their second match, the three fedheads will evaluate them and decide.
If they lose their first match, they'll get a second match and if they lose that one, they're immediately not accepted (unless the fedheads decide otherwise). If they WIN their second match (but lost the first), they get an evaluation. If a DT wins both matches, he's automatically in, and no more dark matches! Get it?
Note: This idea may be replaced with the e-Wrestling Univeristy. ~~Earned Entrances and other things~~ Mid-carders and some semi-superstars shouldn't have entrances as fancy as the main eventers. The people vying for the World Title get fancy entrances and other special rewards for being top notch e-wrestlers. The people going for the NA Title are one step down. They get decent entrances and a few special things. The people going for the Young Blood Championship don't get a fancy entrance. Just theme music and maybe a video. Except the champion.
The YB Champion can get a fancy entrance, cause really, he's part of the NA Title crowd. All champions get an entrance a notch above the rest. This should entice wrestlers to work harder and reap the benefits of victory...
Basically, the veterans get treated better than the rookies. This will be made clearer later.
~~Scored Insult Session~~ If it's possible to get two wrestlers online with each other, RAW will host a session where wrestlers trade comments back and forth. This will build up feuds exponentially. To keep insults from getting too rude or too long, everything will be scored. There's a maximum # of points that can be awarded for every rebuttal so going too long is pointless. Insults regarding sex or race will lower chances of scoring points and may even lead to the removal of points. Witty and original insults get points and overall points are given at the end of the whole thing. These sessions (one or so per card) will act as a match-- but will really serve as storyline and feud development. Besides, this is something that people will really want to read... A similar example using real-life wrestling would be when The Rock and Chris Jericho traded insults when Jericho debuted.
RAW has many other plans, but those are just a couple. Once the federation officially opens up, everything should be finalized...
As with everything in life, changes occur. If something we`re doing doesn`t work, we`ll drop it. If we think of something new and good to try, we`ll give it a go. Nothing is set in stone, except people who cross the mafia.

Many of those I have informally trained have gone on to great things…now I and those involved are going to do it professionally. We will be in association with a reborn e-fed called RAW, so there`ll be plenty of chance to practice theory in a fed filled with great veteran players.

The ideal applicants will be: -Aged 14 upwards -Been involved with e-wrestling for, at the very most, no more that a year. -Have a good sense of humour -Keen to learn what it takes to become an e-wrestling legend On a final note, I`d like to make a request to two groups of people.
