Matches signed for WrestleWorld so far... Jan 22nd - Yankee Stadium, Bronx, New York Card subject to change Scott Wyld Vs. Takisuma Ogama Scott Wyld is still coming back into form, whilst the impressive Ogama makes his RAW PPV debut. If both stars perform to their best, it should be a superb match. It may be the opening match of the card, it may have no history behind it, but it should be a great encounter.
Joel Murphy Vs. Jessie Muldoon Joel Murphy & Jessie Muldoon's paths have continued to cross over recent weeks. These two are certainly future champions, and this battle should give a glimpse of how good that future will be.
TAG-TEAM TABLE MATCH! CAPITAL CHAMPIONSHIP ON THE LINE! Ronny Ramirez & 'Blacksmith' Jon Smythe Vs. Knick Man & Stevie Ray Murdoch Stevie Ray Murdoch and Jon Smythe have been enemies for a long time - ever since Murdoch was eschewed for Smythe by his mentor, Doug Simpkins. Ronny
Ramirez and Knick Man have a genuine deep and personal dislike for one another that has been escalating for some time. Here, it all explodes, with
Smythe's championship on on the line. If he gets pinned, or submits, he loses the title! LOSERS LEAVE RAW! Coperhead & Judas Priest Vs. The Deacons & The Anointed One Copperhead and The Anointed One have a history, as the two giants of RAW gravitated towards one another. It escalated out of control with The
Deacons getting involved and literally digging up the past of Judas Priest, Copperhead's friend and manager. The Deacons consider Priest and Copperhead heathens, and now something has to give. RAW ain't big enough for both of them...
THREE WAY NORTH AMERICAN & RAW POWER TITLE MATCH Eddie Drysdale Vs. Jason Midnight Vs. 'Flying Scotsman' [name withheld] Midnight and Drysdale have been the most vocal of their dislike for one another, ever since Midnight helped Drysdale to a victory, and Drysdale didn't appear grateful enough for Midnight's liking. Midnight & McDougall have engaged in a bitter series of hardcore matches, and McDougall would love to get his Raw Power title back - or even better, get the North
American championship. Both titles are on the line here in what could be the match of the evening.
LADDER MATCH 'Sex Pistol' Jaime Roberts Vs. Thomas Creighton The history behind these two is too deep to do justice to in any short depth. Needless to say, nearly a year ago, Roberts screwed Creighton over for money he owed for services rendered. Ever since then, these two have been the most mortal enemies in RAW, and now, for the first time, they meet in a ladder match, the winner being the person to retrieve a briefcase at the top, symbolic of the money Roberts screwed Creighton out of.
TEXAS DEATH CAGE MATCH Indiana Casanova Vs. Obsessive Compulsive Disorder Like Roberts and Creighton, their wars have been brewing for a long time. These two will love the chance to destory each other in a sealed cage, defeat coming by way of being pinned for a 5 count.
Vik Ventressa Vs. 'The Corruptor' Doug McCray Time and time again these two have encountered, this is the only time they've met one on one, and it should be great as the two darkest and most vicious competitors in RAW finally reach the pinnacle of their tete-a-tete, of trying to outdo the other. Now it will simply be a case of one outdoing
the other in a wrestling ring. This is the one that everybody is waiting for. TAG-TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH - CANES ARE LEGAL! The Andrazoons Vs. The Singapore Bandits Then two best tag-teams RAW has ever seen collide in the tag-team match that everyone has been waiting for. The Singapore Bandits are returning on a wave of support, Andrazoons are causing trouble wherever they go. With teams this talented, this is going to be special.
THREE WAY WORLD TITLE MATCH - FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE- PINFALLS ONLY! Larry Spikowski Vs. David Hagen Vs. Vic Creed One girl ties these three men - Elecrta. Has she maneuvered this perfectly so that David Hagen has to face Spikowski and Creed? Is she seeing Creed just to destroy Hagen and Spikowski, two men who have caused her hurt.
Hagen thinks Spikowski is an unworthy champion, that it needs to belong to someone younger and stronger like himself. Creed sees Spikowski as tarnishing the belt he made great and looks to regain the strap to restore that glory once more, no longer with any concern for family, just himself.
