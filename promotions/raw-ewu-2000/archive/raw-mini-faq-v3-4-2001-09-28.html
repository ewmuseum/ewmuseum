RAW Mini-FAQ V3.4

RAW Wrestling Federation Mini-FAQ V3.4 www.raw-empire.co.uk Q: What is RAW Wrestling Federation? A: It is a play by email roleplaying game based on the world of wrestling using characters created by the players, not characters who already exist. The player then users their character by working and developing storylines with flashes and strats all towards the aim of...well, whatever their aim is. It is e-wrestling.
Q: What is e-wrestling? A: Not going to answer that here, and chances are, if you're reading this, you know what it is already. However, if you don't, try
E-Wrestlung Universe at http://ewu.efeduniverse.com Q: You website it awful. Why? A: It's designed to require the minimal amount of maintenance yet still provide access to all the up to date information.
Q: Okay, so RAW is another one of those efeds out there. I've got a character I want to use. Why should I join RAW when there are so many other feds out there?
A: Well, RAW has been running, if you include the fed it respawned from, for 2years, so for starters, there is stability. We've got a nice-sized active roster. We're not going anywhere. It's a fed filled with loyal players, and we're one of the few feds out there that do not struggle for match writers. Cards are pretty much out on time and are of good quality. They're not so big you have to give up your whole weekend to read them, and the excitement of the matches and storylines tends to be rather high. We've also got a great range of friendly, egoless team-players from the very intense to the very humourous.
Q: Hold on, isn't RAW that fed that has the Open Flash System - that one where emails are sent out individually rather than in packets?
A: Yes...and no. RAW can be whatever you want it to be. Because we use Yahoogroups, *ALL* our posts are archived. In addition, if you want to use it as a web-based roleplay board, you can. If you want the emails to come to you individually, as they're sent, you can have that too. And if you like your flashes in packets, then there is the option to have daily collections of the flashes come through to you.
Q: Yeah, but it isn't very realistic to have wrestlers be able to speak at any time, any day.
A: This is ewrestling. We're not constrained by TV. Sure, people can try and make their shows as much like TV as they want. However, by providing an option to allow people to roleplay as and when they want, storylines can progress all the time at varying speeds. We don't restrict people.
Q: Doesn't this mean I get a lot of crap into my mailbox? A: No. RAW makes sure all of it's players are of a good standard and are responsible. All players are moderated for the first few months until they know the RAW system and then if they lapse, we moderate them again. We place high standards on our players.
Q: Heck, you're just another one of those federations where the person who flashes the most wins.
A: Far from it. Actually, to the contrary. Vic Creed and Doug McCray, who held the RAW World title for a year between them, probably had some of the lowest activity rate. However, their flashes and strats, when done, were of the utmost quality.
Q: I'm a big name. I've won lots of titles. Do I go straight into the main event? A: No. Everyone works their way up. Some get up there quicker than others, but everyone pays their dues. No egos here. All are judged on merit. Q: I hear most of people's strats are used. Is this true? A: Guilty as charged. You send a strat and we tend to use at least
80% of it. Q: So where is RAW based? A: RAW is a world-wide organisation, spending much of the year touring America, and going overseas several times a year. The headquarters are, at the moment, in Strasburg, North Dakota. Don't ask why!
Q: Why? A: Don't ask! Q: What's with the regional federations? Are they feeder feds? A: Yes and no. RAW Carolina, Windy City RAW and RAW Atlantic are RAW affiliated feds and they run along the same operating rules as RAW, but tend to be report style cards which means card consistency. The quality of players is not very different, just the style of cards which give a regional feel. If players want to move on to RAW, RAW will certainly consider it, and vice versa.
Q: So how often are you cards then? A: We have one main card, RAW AT WAR (full play by play), once every ten days, RAW Report (report style) five days after every RAW AT WAR, plus occassional house shows. This format keeps the momentum going but also gives people that bit of extra time to flash or strat of build angles with other handlers. Works very well. Once again, RAW does not limit itself to standard ideas of once every week or two weeks.
Q: Is RAW a 'big' fed? A: We have, at this moment, 107 addresses on our mailing list. It's increasing every week. We also have a collection of great players who you will have heard of throughout the ewrestling world. It's not up to us to say whether we're big or not, but we do have a significant following.
Q: RAW doesn't have a tag-team scene as far as I can tell. What is with that? A: We shifted focus from the tag-team scene to building a good strong Light-Heavyweight division. Q: What title are up for grabs in RAW? A: We've got World, North American, Capital and Light-Heavyweight championships. Q: What is the future for RAW? A: It looks good, it looks fun. Why don't you join up and be a part of our fun little community. We've got places in RAW and all our regionals. The future's bright, the future is...RAW

Q: Hold on, why are you open for applications if you're so good? A: We're going because we eliminate any space-wasters on our roster. If you don't participate, you're gone. See, we ain't all flowers and honey :) We've got a few openings, here's your chance.
