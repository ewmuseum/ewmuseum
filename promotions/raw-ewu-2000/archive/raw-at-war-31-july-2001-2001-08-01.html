Jose Roberts to be exact. Jose sits comfortably on the sofa looking on the nearby, Jose sips from a mug of hot coffee, when suddenly...]
Voice: While he nodded, nearly napping, suddenly there came a tapping.. [Jose is stratled, and spits the coffee from his mouth in a moment of fright, he stares up at where the voice is coming from.]

Only this, and nothing more."
[Jose places his mug on the coffee table infront of him, never taking his eyes off the speaker.]
Voice: He scarcely more than muttered, "other friends have flown before- On the morrow he will leave me, as my hopes have flown before." Then the intruder said, "Nevermore."
[Jose attempts to stand, suddenly, a figure leaps into sight, landing on the coffee table, this person kicks the mug aside, and then pushes
Jose down ever so lightly, so that he lands back to his seat on the sofa. This figure we see, suddenly drops down, crossing their legs and sitting on the coffee table, at eye level with Jose'.]
Voice: You were not expecting my company? [Jose, still a little shaken up, seems to be calming down.] Jose: No.. nothing like this.. what is the meaning of this? Akuma.. what are you doing, scaring the hell out of me like that?! [Indeed, it is the masked figure of Akuma. Wearing all black, as per usual. Akuma doesnt move, he stares straight at Jose.]
Akuma: Believe me Jose, it was not intended.. you know how I like to make a big entrance. Jose: Yes, quite.. but you still havent answered my question.. why are you here? Akuma: Dont get comfortable Roberts.. this is not a social call. This is all business. Jose: Usually these things are organised over the phone. Akuma: I like to look into a persons eyes when I speak to them.. you understand, dont you? Besides, it is perfect timing, your not busy..
and I’m sure as hell not! Jose: Well then.. what is it that you want to talk about?? Akuma: Jose.. I have an addiction.. Jose: Christ Akuma! How in the hell did you fool the drug tests? Akuma: Ahhh, yours is such a feeble mind Jose. Jose: I beg your pardon?! [Akuma shakes his head, laughs to himself.] Akuma: Jose.. my addiction is to no mere drug. My addiction is to something very uncommon, something hard to find.. something that everyman fears.
Jose: And that is.. Akuma: The destruction of my fellow man. Jose: Oh.. that is, quite.. uncommon. Akuma: And I need your help to feed my addiction! Jose: ME?! Wha-- what can I do? Akuma: What I want from you is simple.. I think I deserve the right to spread my pain and anger through RAW. Through the offices, through the staff, through the roster.. and even through the fans. But with all the rules and regulations, it makes this a hard objective.
Jose: Sooo.. Akuma: So you announce to the word that there will be no man spared or shown mercy in my path.
Jose: And that means... Akuma: Simply.. I want written word from you that states, never shall the restrictions of rules apply to me, never will I be limited to the squared circle.
Jose: So.. you want some sort of.. no rules factor in all your matches? Akuma: Not no rules.. Kamikaze Rules.. Jose: I see.. and that means? Akuma: The match can begin anytime, anyplace.. there are no rules to be followed, anything is legal.. and I do mean anything. The only salvation for my victims is the pinfall at the end of my reign.
Jose: Okay.. so.. no rules.. no restrictions.. and the only way a match can end is through pinfall.
Akuma: See, thats what I like about you Jose.. yours so understanding. [Jose thinks hard for a moment.] Jose: Well.. lately, RAW has seen a change in you.. and quite frankly,
I think it is a change for the better. Your in ring performance and the attention of the fans has really been raised lately. So.. Im going to give you this, 'Kamikaze Rules'. Because it might very well be for the good of the company.
[Akuma reaches into the inside pocket of his coat, and pulls out a piece of paper. Jose pats down himself.]
Jose: I dont have a pen.. [Akuma suddenly takes out a switchblade, grabs Jose firmly by the wrist, and holds it out above the paper on the table.]
Jose: What the-- hell.. are you doing?!! [Akuma cuts deeply into the palm of Jose's hand with the blade, Jose cries out. Akuma then squeezes his hand firmly, blood quickly drips down onto the page.]
Akuma: No ink can seal this agreement.. your blood, will bind this contract. For it is a part of you.
[Akuma points to the page.] Akuma: Now sign it! [Jose is a little frightened, but he nods, still trembling, and writes his signature with his finger-tip in the small pool of blood. Akuma then cuts his hand open, and some of his blood drips onto the page, he then writes his signature on the page, along with 'Kamikaze Rules'. He takes it carefully in his hand, so the blood doesnt run.]
Akuma: This is sufficient. Thank you, Mr. Roberts. [Akuma salutes Jose, and makes for the door. He stops, and turns back.] Akuma: Oh, and by the by.. you might want to put a bandage or something on that.. we wouldnt want it to get infected now would we?
[Akuma cackles wickedly, slamming the door behind him. Leaving Jose in a bewildered and painfull state.]
BATTLEZONE FIRST ROUND MATCH DESPAIR vs. KHAN (WC-RAW) The first match of the night was also a BattleZone first-round match-up pitting the sadistic, self-proclaimed "Last Sane Man on
Earth" DESPAIR versus Windy City RAW's Franchise, KHAN.
Despair had suffered mightily in his previous two matches, but immediately went on the offensive as Khan entered the ring, stomping on Windy City's star, driving him to the mat. The Last Sane Man on
Earth continued on the offensive and took the match to the outside at the 4:25 mark.
Khan was reeling on the floor and Despair took full advantage with a running Liger Bomb on the exposed concrete. Despair wasn't quite through as he acended to the top rope and dove off with a senton bomb on to a prone Khan on the concrete floor to a gigantic pop of
work-rate appriciation from the crowd. The suicidal move damaged both men, but it appeared Despair, due to the punishment he has suffered in the past two outings, seemed to have suffered the most. Khan was the first to his feet, although slowly, and begin to take over the match at the mid-way mark.
The Windy City star tossed the final RAW Power Champ to the ropes and delivered a vicious powerslam followed by a piledriver that left
Despair in considerable pain. Khan continued to toss the reeling RAW hardcore maniac around the ring for the next few minutes, until
Despair caught Khan celebrating a bit too early and delivered a sick
Poisioned Frankenstiener to the WC RAW's franchise to more thunderous
high-spot applause. Both men, suffering from serious punishment, took time to recover, but it was Despair crawled to his feet first and attempted to lock in his finisher, the Human Torment, but Khan countered with a kick to
Despair's groin and collapsed. Again both men were exhaused and battered on the mat. That's when things got ugly. Tony Gravano, another Windy City RAW wrestler, stormed the ring and began to put the boots to an already battered and now 2-on-1 disadvantaged Despair. Gravano continued the assault, handcuffing
Despair to the top rope. He was then joined by the revived Khan, who delivered a picture perfect Snakecharmer (super kick) that seemed to snap Despair's head back.
The referee, who had been knocked out a couple of minutes previous when Khan ducked a clothesline and collided with the referee, was out of it, the two Windy City stars kept putting the boots to RAW's resident lunatic. That is until the lights went out and "Superbeast"
by Rob Zombie hit the air. A dark figure appeared on the stage and let out a gothic yell as flames shot up from the stage. It was the satanic
D.O.A himself.
D.O.A. rushed to the ring and immediately caught a hold of Khan as
Gravano bailed. D.O.A. the delivered the Deathscythe to Khan. The lights went out as "Superbeast" faded away. The lights came on and
D.O.A. had disappeared, leaving the Windy City RAW franchise laid out in the ring with a handcuffed and spent. The ref has woken just in time to see the attack on Khan, and promptly disqualified Despair.

Murphy fought back until Akuma was pinned against the wall by Murphy, who flailed away at Akuma with some solid blows to the mid section, before winding up with a bit right hook, which Akuma ducks, and Murphy nailed the wall instead. Cradling his limp hand and crying out in pain it only gets worse from there, as he turned, Akuma delivers a little combo of his own!
Several kicks starting at the lower body and making their way up to the chest. Akuma attempted to finish it early with a 'Kamikaze Kick',
Murphy caught Akuma as he leapt in the air, dumping Akuma on a nearby table, which crashed under him.
The intense and extreme battle continued throughout the back, wowing the fans, Akuma attempting to leap off a raised platform with a flying clothesline, Joel Murphy met him in mid-air with a snappy chair shot to the face! But Akuma got up. He kept on coming at Joel Murphy and grabbed the singapore cane, raising it up to nail Joel, who gave Akuma a swift kick to the mid-section, and set Akuma up for a powerbomb, no, aRUNNING SIT-DOWN POWERBOMB ON THE FLOOR! It looked like the end, but
Akuma, still with the singapore cane in his hand, nailed Joel right between the eyes with it!
The two men, hurt to an acute degree, no longer trying to win,just trying to be more extreme, clashed on, Akuma getting the better of it mostly, the fans getting more and more behind him. Near the fifteen minute mark Akuma reversed the 'Major Depression' Fishermans Buster into his 'Dark Acension' onto a setup chair!
Dragging Murphy to the aisle entrance stage, he flung him Murphy off, then jumped down, set Murphy on a table with a chair on top. (double rotation)! He crashes onto Joel, through the table, with the added effect of the steel chair sealing a spectacular and impressive victory for the talented Akuma.

RAW threw the one of the best it had to offer at WAR! It didn't even begin to be enough now that the WAR! legend of legends proved to be alive and dangerous in RAW 2001.
