[CM] But, I'm already here... [T] Trust me, little man, this is *not* the time to piss me off. [CM] Oh, uh, um, I didn't- [T] Nevermind. Let's just get this over with. I'm not going to get into this today. If you want a synopsis of what I was going to say, fine, you can have it. Otherwise, take a hike.
[CM] Synopsis? But- (The big man's eyes narrow even further.) [T] I'm sorry, did you have something to add? [CM] Who me? Oh no, not a thing! [T] I thought not. First, I was going to speak on Pleb's drivel. I was going to tell him that nobody though his knuckles were dragging because of his size, but because of his prominent brow ridge and pointed head. I was going to tell him that I plan to cudgel his sorry ass so hard that his cavernous maw will catch up to the backs of his ankles, and he'll eat himself to death. I was also going to explain to his stupid ass about this whole jealousy nonsense. See, I could only be jealous of him if he had something I wanted, and couldn't have. Well, let's do the math. I'm bigger, more successful, more respected, and certainly more feared. While he is forced to occupy his time with scabrous ditchpigs that'll open their legs to any filthy letch that'll buy them a hot meal, I've been married for ten years, to a woman who's classy, smart and independent, not to mention hot as all Hell. I still don't understand how that little girl and Richard's wife are related to this peon; there must be a lot of good, recessive genes at work. Anyway, I was going to explain to this moron that anything he's tried to do, I was doing five times better five years ago. Hence, the notion of him imitating my immense talent, and failing miserably. Of course, it makes no difference to me. Many have tried to be as great as I am, and they've all fallen short of the mark. Sadly for him, he's attracted my attention, and now, he's going to suffer for it.
Second, I was going to put Crappy Smurf straight. He's not "two and zero" against anybody. In the first case, it wasn't him and me, it was him and me and O-Maggot. In the second case, it was my match to lose, not his to win.
I made the mistake, I paid for it. I only claim to be *almost* invincible. Hell, even a broken clock is right twice a day, and here was the Smurf's two times. I'm not surprised that he doesn't want another go. He knows that he's no match for me in a real wrestling match, where none of his garbage
"wrestling" applies. Moreover, he's already paid the price for his "victories". If he wants to consider it a win when he's suffered more punishment in ten minutes than he has in the previous ten *months* and only gotten one lousy, lucky pinfall to show for it, hey, be my guest, chump.
Third, I was going to inform O-Meager that he should mind his own business. He's been given ample warning. I do what I like, when I like, and if he doesn't like it, tough noogies. Come and stop me, half-pint. I'll be happy to beat you like an old rug. Then again, that assumes you survive past Louie, who I hear is just *jumping* at the chance to get in the ring again. You'll make a mighty fine appetizer for him.
Last, and definitely least, Greaseball Ramirez. If that moron bothered to clean the Crisco out of his ears and pay attention, he'd have heard that it was Juggernaut who put LaGonad to sleep, not me. However, he'd also hear this. His idle threats matter as much to me as his company with the WAR!
clan. He was never of any use to me in the first place, and now, I have all the reason I need to flog his sorry ass like I wanted to before. Come and get it, greasemonger. Your pain is my pleasure, and no, I don't mean that the way LaGross did.

[CM] But what about Travis LaGra- (The name hasn't even cleared the man's lips before the massive Tonnage leaps to his feet and grabs him by the throat, in a surprising show of speed. [T] Don't you *dare* mention that piece of [BLEEP!]'s name! [CM] But he's- [T] Pushing up daisies? So what! If I hear that name again, I'll pop your pin head like a zit! (He drops the man contemptuously, and sits again.) [T] Get this through your skull. I don't give a damn about LaGrease. [CM] You don't feel anything about what happened? [T] Apparently, you just slept through the past week, dillweed. Do you honestly think I give a rat's ass about some slimy cretin getting his just desserts from the Juggernaut, in the face of innocent people getting killed like that?
You'd better wake the hell up. Those people never did a damn thing to anybody to deserve what happened to them. They weren't saints, I'm sure, but so what? They were typical, decent folk, whose only crime was being American. The dirtbags that did this...
well, let's just say that Bin Laden and his cronies are going to go through a whole new world of hurt. Can you say, "scorched earth"? In the face of these gutless scumbags doing this, I have no intention of giving any thought to that miserable misfit, LaGrime.
Let's put this into perspective. I never cried when I head ol' Robski was dead. He was a thorn in my side some years back, and when one of his buddies was whining about him croaking, well, it didn't bother me in the least. You think I'm going to shed any tears over the "loss" of this slackjawed yokel, in what amounts to a gain for everybody else in the universe? I think not.
He can rot in Hell, for all I care. Besides, you know this is a ploy. [CM] What?! [T] Don't give me that phony surprised tone. You know exactly what I mean. This puke is trying to sell us snake oil. Sorry, LaGrunt, I'm not buying. I know you're going to come out at some event in a couple of weeks with a shopping bag over your head, calling yourself the Blue Buzzard or some other inane moniker. Then, a few events after that, you make your "miraculous" return by unmasking. Whoopee. Well, take some advice from somebody who knows a helluva lot more than you do, dumbass. Stay dead. Open a bait shop in
Florida with Elvis. Don't appear at gas stations in Arkansas, or have your face pop up in a jelly donut in Michigan, and whatever you do, don't let me see you again, or I promise I'll finish you for real. I don't have to kill anyone to take them out, but if it happens, it'll be a bonus. You saw how useful your bumchums are when the time comes. Keep that in mind when you're considering your little Lazarus imitation.
[CM] Uh... [T] Just hold it. I've got something else to say. I'm mad as Hell. I'm madder than I've ever been in my life over these terrorist assholes, having the gall to come into our country and attack us like this. The decimation of our landmarks, the killing of our citizens, and the attack on our way of life is despicable, to say the least. I can promise those lackwit cowards that organized this, they're going to pay for this outrage one hundred fold. We're going to hunt you down like rabid dogs, and put a bullet through every one of your brain pans.
However, that doesn't change what's going on here. We lost a lot of good people, and I'm sick inside just thinking about it. These are my neighbours that were slaughtered, people that could just as easily been my friends or my loved ones. Worst of all, there's not a damn thing I can do about it. I sit here, and stew in my own juices, thinking about the scuzzballs who did this, hiding somewhere in the woods laughing to themselves. I get more and more infuriated over it, and there's nothing I can do. One thing I certainly won't do is dedicate my matches to them. I pummel people and rack up victories for myself; I earn the money to be a productive member of society.
I won't denigrate the memories of those people by making false promises. That brings me to my final act today. I'm giving advanced notice to any pissant stupid enough to get into the ring with me. Don't. Don't even consider it. From this day forward, I'm taking all this crap out on you. I can't cross the ocean and hunt down the people responsible, but I can sure as
Hell pound my frustration out on any of you. Do yourselves a favour, and go into temporary retirement. Until this is over, none of you is safe.
I'm tired of this. Go home, camera jockey, I'm heading out. [CM] Where to? [T] The only place I can feel useful right now, the Red Cross. The least I can do is give blood; I've got a couple of pints to spare.
