Well, the way things look at the moment, we're no course to match the activity for last August, which was spectacular. August is going to see the build up to Battlezone, which will take place on either August 30th or September 10th - most likely the latter. This will mean Internecine, which was on
September 23rd last year, will see action in mid-November, and then two months later, WrestleWorld. Wow, can't believe it has all come around so quickly, and it's great to see RAW going so well.
- Our fedhead, [name withheld], has been experiencing some line problems (no, not cocaine - telephone problems) but will be back on soon. GLORY at all. - Remember the basic golden rule of ewrestling - Participation = fun = Success - Things we're seeing but things we still want to see more of - heat for matches and stories involving the characters beyond the ring. - I don't know if you've heard...but is Larry Spikowski the new President of RAW? Check out the Buffalo Wings series of flashes to find out more. - A quick request, and I request this every so often. Players, could you please begin your roleplays not with extensively detailed descriptions of surroundings but rather with providing a brief reminder or backdrop for the roleplay, so that everyone else can find it easier to follow your angle, even if it is just 'Bob has just arrived in RAW and wants to make a name for himself'.

Talk to the fedheads so we can help get your storyline sorted out. - Battlezone is coming and it looks like it'll be a twelve match card - at least!
