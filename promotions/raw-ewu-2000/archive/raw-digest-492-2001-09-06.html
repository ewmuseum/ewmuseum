(Finale) Travis LaGrange is asleep in his bed, head wrapped, when he suddenly springs up in a cold sweat. LaGrange looks around the room, then next him, where two shiny gold belts rest. He exhales and smiles broadly.
"So it wasn't a dream," he thought to himself. Like a little kid, he hops out of bed in his boxers and admires his new toys in the mirror. He wraps one belt around each shoulder and begins to mock his boyhood hero by dancing and posing in front of the mirror.
The sound of laughter stops LaGrange dead in his tracks, embarrassed he drops the belts and a broad grin crawls across his face.
It was his girlfriend Tanya Maynard peeking in. "Heh," said LaGrange. "I was just ... um ... tryin' them on." "Yeah," chuckled Maynard. "I see that." "So how's Rick and Tiny?" "Tiny's okay, but they've scheduled him for reconstructive surgery ... on his nose." "Heh." "Ricky's a little more serious. They said he suffered his 16th concussion and he's in tests. They said we can't see him until they finish."
"Damn, that boy's been hit on the noggin more times than anyone I know of." "So how did last night go?" Travis thought back and tried to remember, but everything was a blur. Winning the title, seeing the
RAW doctor, meeting with Eric Helmroy and the board of directors, partying with Creed, Gar and Ronny ...
better not tell her about that one, LaGrange thought. "Okay. You don't have to answer. You were with Vic and the boys," said Maynard with a grin. "How about the meeting with Eric? Did that go okay?"
"Um, yeah," LaGrange's mood quickly changed. "I just signed a 30-day contract." "What?!" "They wanted me to change completely, baby," Travis exclaimed. "I told them I had ta think about it. The deal can be restructured before that. They tossed all these numbers at me and I just didn't want to jump into somethin'. It sounded good ... I just want to make sure."
The phone rings interrupting the conversation. Travis picks up the receiver. "HEY THERE CHAMP!!" Helmroy. Jesus, is this guy ever depressed? "What do ya want, Helmroy?" "You got a match this weekend, champ." "WHAT?!!" LaGrange yelled angrily into the receiver. "You told me I could have some time off to heal!" "Sorry Trav, we had some unexpected departures and we needed an after Pay-per-view showstopper. You and
Creed versus Jugger and Tonnage. Spikyjim and Midnight are doing it, so can you." "F*ck you, Helmroy. You're only doin' this to me, 'cause I didn't sign that long-term deal, ain't ya?" Silence on the other end. LaGrange knows Eric is making up a lie. "No, no, champ. We just need your help on this one. Just this once." Travis relents, knowing his deal is only for 30 days and is at his current salary, not the big money deal he had discussed with the Board. As long as he plays within the foul lines, he might be able to get more creative concessions.
"Okay," responded the Outlaw. "I'll do it. But we do things my way ... by my rules." boy Travis. He caught RAW resting on our laurals. Managed to get himself a few other hungry men. Took his chance. Made it pay off. Can't say as I blame him. I will say, you've earned it, boy. Earned some respect at
BattleZone. Anybody that can put away The Jugger is double tough. But I can't let you keep going Travis, not like this. Speaking of BattleZone....
[Drag] JP: What a show. What a motherf*ckin' show. Every last thing RAW ever had...gone. Gone in the count of three. WAR or The New Breed own every major title in this company. Tragic Kingdom indeed. Every last title, every last thing RAW had, flushed down the toilet...or was it?
[Exhale. Judas slides his duster to the side, revealing a leather strap, a large golden emblem attached. Emblazoned on it are the words...]
JP: RAW Tag Team Championship. The last title that's still owned by a true RAW man. A very good friend of mine gave this to me before BattleZone. This is our rallying point. RAW at WAR is the day we strike back. It's the day we say that we will not be denied. We will not be stopped. If WAR, or

This IS our time. [JP stares out over the water one last time as he flicks his cigarette into the water. It sizzles as it sinks slowly beneath the surface.]
JP: Rally round the flag, boys. Rally round the flag. [Fade To Black]
