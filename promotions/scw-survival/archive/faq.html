Cut An Interview Form Sorry, no java browser FAQ'S Q: Do you deal with affiliations from other e-feds? A: Not at the moment. Go to www.neutralzonewrestling.homestead.com if you have any questions about affiliations. Q: Who actually manages NZW? A: SCW is owned and operated by [name withheld] and [name withheld]: Two former e-fed wrestler role players. Q: Do you write or sim your matches? A: All matches are hand written. We don't like simulators. Q: May I use other wrestlers or their property in my roleplays? A: No, unless you have permission from the wrestler or SCW superiors. Q: I want to join the SCW Staff. What do I need to do? A: Generally, we only promote staff who have been in the fed for awhile or have a lot of experience and loyalty. If you have not been involved in SCW long, it is unlikely you will be considered for a staff position. Q: What would happen if an RPer posted a sexual or racist comment on the OOC or RP board? A: That person would be suspended for one week. If it continued, he/she would be terminated and be blocked from the board. Q: I lost my match. Who can I complain to? A: Complain to yourself, your Mom, your dog, whoever. Just not to us. We will not allow it. Q: You mentioned that you can win prizes. How is this achieved? A: If your roleplay gets a grade of 95% or higher, you will win a prize. You will be e-mailed a list of prizes to choose from. This gives RPers encouragement to write real spectacular role plays. Q: I used to be in SCW and quit or was fired. Now I want to return. Am I automatically accepted? A: No, you are not. You must send in another application and we will drop you an email.
