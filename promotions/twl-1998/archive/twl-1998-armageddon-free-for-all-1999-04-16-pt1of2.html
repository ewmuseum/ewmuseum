Armageddon Free-For-All

We see Bobby McCarville and Dan Cody seated next to each other behind a large black desk. After getting a quick close-up of both men, the camera moves back and around the studio, which has the words "TWL" on all the 20 or so monitors, as well as Armageddon on a few. The camera then goes back to Bobby, who's wearing a blue blazer and a red and white tie. Bobby's hair is cut short, and his eyes show focus as he's concentrated on the camera. Dan is next to him, and looks very similiar. He has a black tuxedo on, and his hair is slicked back. Both men look ready, and then smile as we begin.] Bobby McCarville: Hello everyone! As I'm sure you all know, we are half an hour away from "ARMAGEDDON"!! Dan Cody: Yes we are. And I don't know about you Bobby, but I'm _really_ excited about all of this. Bobby McCarville: Undoubtedly, Dan. It's a Pay-per-view, and not many federations can hold pay-per-views like the TWL can. Dan Cody: Got that right! You're all currently tuned in to the Free-For-All! This will be 30 minutes of showing you what these matches are all about, as well as giving you 30 more minutes of proof as to why you should order this PPV if you haven't already. Bobby McCarville: Let's get straight to our matches. Our first match of the night will feature 4 men taking each other on in a "Barbed Wire Chair" match. Dan Cody: That should be a great way to start off this night -- Jason Hansen, Lance Storm, Micheal Mars and John Aries in a tag match where a Barbed wire chair is on top of a pole and may be used in the match if reached! Bobby McCarville: Quite a bit of history between these men. And their rivalry has been going on for quite a while now. Dan Cody: Jason Hansen looks like he may be going up in the TWL if he keeps working his hardest, but the same could definitely be said for the Dangerous Duo. As for Storm, he's made quite a large impact for the amount of time he's been here. Bobby McCarville: But let's take you back to how this all started! Over two months ago, we saw Micheal Mars take on Jason Hansen. And here's what happened... [Cut to a scene of Mars with control in his match against Hansen. A caption reads "TWL Monday Massacre -- February 8th, 1999".] Dan: Mars is viciously applying the pressure to Hansen's neck! One: That thing is about ready to snap! Mars: GIVE UP!! OR I'LL BREAK THE DAMN THING!! One: DO IT MIKE!! Dan: Mars getting frustrated by the second! Hansen just won't give up! Oh wait... Mars is now picking him up... and he delivers a Front End Suplex!!! The cover... Ref: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! {DING, DING, DING} Shylock: Your Winner... MIIIIIIIIIIIIKKKKKKKKKKKKEEEEEE MAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRSSSSSSSSSS!!!!! Dan: Aries gets back into the ring.... One: Look at him! He's stomping at Hansen with Mike! {Ding, Ding,Ding} Dan: The bell is ringing but they continue to stomp away at Jason! One: Ringing the bell never stops people... {DING, DING, DING} Dan: Aries is coming over to the table... One: Hey, what are you doing up here? Dan: Mars pulling Hansen over to the apron... {DING,DING,DING} One: Mars is there too. He setting him up for a powerbomb... Dan & One: OH SHIT!!!! [We hear their mics drop again. They run from the table in the nick of time, as Mars delivers a Powerbomb to Hansen on top of the Announcer's table, with Aries throwing Hansen down on top of his Barbwire Chair. The table doesn't break. Hansen looks in like he's in pain. Immediately, the Paramedics come down to ringside, and Mars and Aries are still laughing at "Unstoppable." Dan looks at the two with disgust as we see One smiling with a big grin. The Paramedics take Hansen away... Mars and Aries also leave. The final shot of Hansen being taken away by the Paramedics stays on the screen before we go back to Dan and Bobby.] Bobby McCarville: Jason Hansen was really hurt after that one! And because of the henious act by the Dangerous Duo, he was out for a while. Dan Cody: Yep -- but a while later, there WAS some retribution. [Cut to a scene of a post-match Dangerous Duo match. A caption reads "TWL Monday Massacre -- March 8th, 1999".] One: WAIT!! WHO IS THAT?! Dan: IT'S "UNSTOPPABLE" JASON HANSEN!! HANSEN IS OUT HERE!! One: We haven't seen him for weeks - Micheal Mars and John Aries took him out with an injury! Dan: HE JUMPS INTO THE RING, AND ATTACKS MARS FROM BEHIND! One: But Aries jumps on Hansen, and now HE'S GETTING DOUBLE-TEAMED! I sense another injury coming on! Dan: They're going for that Powerbomb/Neckbreaker they took him out with last time! Uh-oh!! One: They're setting him up-- Dan: WHO IS THAT?! SOMEBODY IS RUNNING DOWN THE RAMP, AND THAT PERSON JUST KNOCKED DOWN MARS! Now he starts wailing away on Aries - HE'S HELPING HANSEN! One: Who IS it? Dan: I DON'T KNOW! He's wearing a Tee shirt with Lance on the front and Storm written across the back - who IS this? One: I don't know, but he just cleaned house and helped out Hansen right here! Dan: _WOW!_ [Fade out again to Dan and Bobby.] Bobby McCarville: And that marked Lance Storm's entrance into the TWL. He's on a mission, and that's to help Hansen take on the Dangerous Duo. Dan Cody: Certainly an interesting situation there! This should be a great way to open things off! [We then fade out to a black-and-white clip of Hansen nailing an unsuspecting victim with his "Unstoppable Crash", and then a clip of Lance Storm running in and nailing Micheal Mars, taking him down.] "Unstoppable" Jason Hansen... Lance Storm. One of the TWL's growing stars, and a newcomer who's caused waves quickly! [A clip of Hansen single-handedly wrestling a 6-man tag for his team back in November at Helloween.] Two men with a mission... [Clip of Storm running in and helping out Hansen.] That mission, to take out... [A picture of the Dangerous Duo comes up.] The Dangerous Duo. A team that is climbing the tag team ranks in the TWL... [Clip of them beating the Liquadaters with ease.] And climbing the tag team ranks fast. [Clip of them taking on Sex Sells in a spirited match.] But they have to get through Hansen and Storm first. [A clip of the Dangerous Duo standing outside the ring, to Hansen and Storm who are staring out at them.] JASON HANSEN & LANCE STORM DANGEROUS DUO TAG TEAM BARBED WIRE CHAIR MATCH [An Armaggedon logo replaces that graphic, and then we fade back to Bobby and Dan.] Bobby McCarville: Let's move on to our second match of the evening. Dan Cody: Ah. I've been thinking about this one, and it has endless possibilities! Bobby McCarville: It certainly does. We've got God's Justice, a tag team that's gone through all their opponents thus far with ease, and then we have... well, what DO we have? Dan Cody: We have a mystery team. Bobby McCarville: Remember how these guys showed up? Dan Cody: Let's get a refresher... [Cut to a clip of after a God's Justice win. A caption reads "TWL Spotlight -- March 4th, 1999".] MARIO: You forgot the cool part! JACK: What cool part? MARIO: When God's Justice got jumped by those two guys wearing black! JACK: Oh. [Rolls eyes.] THAT. MARIO: Yep- two guys jumped the railing and attacked God's Justice while they were celebrating! JACK: Those guys looked like the ones in that weird interview earlier! MARIO: Yeah, they did! Anyway, they jumped in and both smacked a member of GJ with a chair each. JACK: Then when it looked like they were helping them up, the first masked man delivered a DDT to Jackson! MARIO: But Johnson fortunately managed to intervene before any more serious damage! JACK: The security came into the ring, and the two masked men fled. [Fade back to Dan and Bobby.] Bobby McCarville: And that's how the whole world met the Mystery Team. Dan Cody: Yes. And this is just another example of the TWL being loaded with surprises. Anything really CAN happen. Bobby McCarville: Speaking of which, let's see what happened in that SAME week during a God's Justice match! [Cut to a clip of the God's Justice/Dangerous Duo match. A caption reads "TWL Monday Massacre -- March 8th, 1999".] Dan: Great Jackson charges in and lifts Michael Mars up and nails an Inverted Atomic Drop! Michael Mars is hunched over. Great Jackson from the ropes and nails a __MEGA__ Clothesline! One: That's the prelude to their finisher, Angelic Promise! Great Jackson is fixing to lift Mars onto his shoulders, but wait! Look by the ring! Who is that?! [The two mysterious guys jump out from under the ring. One guy has a hockey stick!, while the other has a chair. They jump in the ring and nail both members of God's Justice. By now, the crowd are booing their heads off but the two continue to beat on members of God's Justice. The guy with the hockey stick is wearing a red suit with a black mask on. His hair is shoulder length and is noticeable. The other guy with the chair is wearing all black with a black mask as well. He is about 6'2 but is well built. He continues to beat God's Justice with the chair while the other guy jams the hockey stick in their throats. Officials come out to break things up but they escape before the refs arrive. They are shown running up the steps in the crowd area. They get to the top and stop. One has a mic and he speaks!] Masked Man #1: Listen up God's Justice! You have felt the wrath of us! We will not reveal ourselves to the world just yet. We will be a force to be wreck end with and you two imbeciles have been chosen to fall first victims to us! (He hands the mic to the second man who speaks in a heavier tone.) Masked Man #2: We have a proposition for you God's Justice if you guys are still conscious and can hear us! Meet us Next Sunday at the PPV. Meet us in the ring! We want you to fall yet even further in our grasps! And to make things interesting for you guys.........if we lose, which we will not, we will unmask! [Fade back to Bobby and Dan.] Bobby McCarville: And that challenge was promptly answered by God's Justice -- and we need not tell you what the answer was. Dan Cody: You know Bobby, one would have to think that God's Justice are at a terrible disadvantage here. Bobby McCarville: I have to agree -- the Mystery Team obviously knows quite a bit about God's Justice, but they know nothing in return. This means that the Mystery Team will obviously have an edge going in. Dan Cody: But we can never count the bible-quoting team out. They've already proven that they're not a one-shot deal, and they'll be looking to cement that at this PPV. Bobby McCarville: I have no doubt about that. This could be one of the better matches of the night! [We fade to a black-and-white picture of the Mystery Team wearing their masks.] The Mystery Team... [A clip of them in an interview, talking about the TWL.] We know nothing about them. [Clip of the Mystery Team taking out God's Justice.] Will that change after Armageddon? It's up to... [A picture of God's Justice comes up.] God's Justice. [Clip of them battling the Dangerous Duo.] Undefeated in the TWL so far. [Clip of them getting attacked by the Mystery Team but still managing to win a match.] Will they remain undefeated? Or will they fall victim? [A still of two dark silhouttes next to the picture of God's Justice.] MYSTERY TEAM GOD'S JUSTICE TAG TEAM MATCH! [That's slowly replaced by an Armageddon logo, and then we fade back to Dan and Bobby.] Bobby McCarville: Our next match will be a revenge match -- we'll see Asylum take on Aviator. Dan Cody: I remember when these two were in the same stable as a part of Dominance -- and then things went wrong. Bobby McCarville: After an unofficial break-up of Dominance, Aviator and Asylum stayed as friends... until Aviator joined New Breed and viciously attacked Asylum! [Cut to a scene of Exodus/Asylum. A caption reads "TWL Monday Massacre -- February 22nd, 1999".] Dan: Asylum picks Exodus up ... and he rolls him back into the ring. Exodus isn't moving. Dan: Wait, what's this? Niki has jump onto the ring apron!! The referee rushes over to admonish her! What's this all about? One: Women have no place in wrestling. They only cause problems, as you can see. Dan: Niki basically saving Exodus from a certain defeat! Whoa! What's this? LOOK!! IT'S THE AVIATOR!! One: What the?? Dan: The Aviator is coming down to help his former stable-mate!! One: This should even things up! Dan: The referee has been distracted. Aviator slides into the ring!! He knocks down ASYLUM?! One: HEE-HEE!! Dan: Asylum down after that flying forearm!! Aviator pulls him back up and drops him down with a double arm DDT!! One: Niki still has the referee preoccupied! Dan: Aviator ascends to the top turnbuckle and comes down at high speed!! FROG SPLASH ONTO ASYLUM!! ASYLUM IS OUT!! AVIATOR SLIDES OUT OF THE RING!! One: This is a real travesty! Dan: Exodus makes his way over to the fallen Asylum. He slumps down and drapes his arm over the chest of the knocked out giant. Niki hops down off of the apron! The referee sees the cover! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! EXODUS GOT 'EM!! Dan: NO!!! One: Wow. New Breed just pulled it off in the end! And they've recruited their newest member - the AVIATOR! [Fade back to Bobby and Dan.] Bobby McCarville: What a night that was! Many people were truly shocked in that one. Dan Cody: They definitely were. And then the next week on Massacre, during a 9-man 3-way tag match, we saw this. [Cut to a scene of Exodus/Asylum. A caption reads "TWL Monday Massacre -- March 1st, 1999".] One: Look! Aviator's going to the top rope! Dan: AVIATOR NAILS A MISSILE DROPKICK ON TRITAN!! One: Tritan goes down and unfortunately, he falls with all his weight on Asylum! Ouch! Dan: Wait, that's a cover!! Higgins counts ONE!! NO!! NOT QUITE!! Aviator breaks up the pin and puts the boots to Asylum! One: And for a moment it looks like Tritan and Aviator are cooperating as they put the boots to Asylum, who is looking not in good shape... Dan: But Aviator dropkicks Tritan! One: He follows it up with a nice snap suplex, then shows a good deal of strength as he hits a frankensteiner on the big man, followed by a series of elbow drops Dan: Then goes to the top rope!! One: AVIATOR HITS A DOUBLE CROSS BODY ON BOTH TRITAN AND ASYLUM OFF THE TOP ROPE!! [Fade back out to Bobby and Dan.] Bobby McCarville: That's just all the more reason for Asylum to come after Aviator at the PPV. Dan Cody: I don't know about you, but I'd think that the Aviator better watch out, because Asylum will be coming for him after the PPV. Bobby McCarville: The hatred between Asylum and New Breed is also well-documented. That looks like it'll continue here. [Fade to a black and white picture of Asylum.] Asylum. One of the TWL's oldest stars. [Clip of Asylum wrestling Flood in his first match.] He's seen some of the toughest the TWL has to offer. [Clip of Zulkim the Philosopher urinating on Asylum's wife's grave.] He's been in some of the toughest matches... [Clip of him and Palimetius battling in the 4-way Scaffold match at Helloween.] He's tried his hardest. [A clip of Asylum trying to get Sandman to submit to his "Straight Jacket".] But all that came to a screeching halt when he was attacked... [Fade to a logo of New Breed.] The Aviator One of the TWL's stars. [A clip of the Aviator nailing an unsuspecting opponent with the 747.] He's a high-flyer with no limits. [Clip of him nailing some high-flying moves on faceless opponents.] But when he turned on Asylum... was he ready for what would happen? [Clip of Aviator nailing Asylum and Tritan with a high-flying maneuver.] We'll find out! AVIATOR ASYLUM REVENGE! [Fade from that, to an Armageddon logo, and then back to Bobby and Dan.] Bobby McCarville: And then we get to our Cruiserweights. Dan Cody: Oh, this one will be a __dandy!!__ Bobby McCarville: Naturally. I mean, we have a huge Cruiserweight Title mach -- but this isn't a normal Cruiserweight Title match! Dan Cody: Explain to everyone why this is special, Bobby. Bobby McCarville: Well, first off, it'll be wrestled under "Mexican Street Fight" rules. But not only that, it'll feature XeRo as the ref!! Dan Cody: Talk about crazy! We've seen XeRo ref before, and it definitely wasn't pretty. Bobby McCarville: Maybe not pretty, but it _was_ interesting! Dan Cody: Of course. And the history of XeRo and Puerco - the man challenging Anthony Levert for the title started quite a while ago... [Cut to a scene of El Puerco/XeRo. A caption reads "TWL Monday Massacre -- February 22nd, 1999".] One: Puerco taps Xero on the shoulder, Xero turns around catches a kick to the gut and.......PUERCO PLEX!! Joe Higgins: ONE .. .. .. .. .. TWO .. .. .. .. .. THREE??? .. .. .. .. .. NO!!! HE KICKED OUT, MY GOD XERO CAN'T BE HUMAN!! Dan: Puerco looks frustrated! He picks up Xero, XERO WITH A KICK TO THE GUT!!!! One: XERO DROP!!!! AND XERO FELL ON TOP OF PUERCO!!! Higgins: ONE .. .. .. .. .. TWO .. .. .. .. .. THREE??? .. .. .. .. .. THREE!!!! [DING, DING DING!!!] Shylock: The winner of this match.... X E R O !!!!!!! One: THAT WAS A FAST-COUNT!!! OUTRAGE!! [Cut back to Dan and Bobby.] Bobby McCarville: And it was quite apparent that El Puerco didn't like that. Dan Cody: Why do you say that? Bobby McCarville: Because that _very_ week, XeRo was in action on TWL Spotlight -- and after his match, we saw this go down... [Cut to a scene of after XeRo defeats John Smith. A caption reads "TWL Spotlight -- February 25th, 1999".] JACK: Anyway, the night wasn't over for XeRo just yet! MARIO: Yeah, the RETARD got what was coming to him. JACK: Stop using that word! Anyway, the lights went out and began to blink. MARIO: What's up with that stuff anyway? Can't the TWL people even control that? JACK: ANYWAY. . . when the lights came back on. . . XERO WAS DRENCHED IN A "BEAN BATH"!! MARIO: Yeah, the RETARD was covered with refried beans from HEAD TO TOE! JACK: This is my last warning - stop using it! Anyway, he also had a bloody El Puerco mask in his hands. "REVENGE" was scrawled on the forehead of the mask... IN GUACAMOLE!! MARIO: Gotta love the Puerco! JACK: After that, a Spanish sounding voice came over the PA system and said, "Prepare to fall ... at the hands of my ARMY OF PIGS!!" [Back to Bobby and Dan.] Bobby McCarville: Quite a character, that El Puerco. Dan Cody: And so is XeRo. But let's not forget about the current Cruiserweight Champion, and the defender in this match! Bobby McCarville: That's right. It's none other than Anthony Levert -- let's take you back now to how that happened! [Cut to a scene of Anthony Levert/Chuckie Martinez. A caption reads "TWL Monday Massacre -- March 8th, 1999".] Dan: Chuckie Martinez now just back on his feet. Chuckie then just scoops up Levert and then with a bodyslam, Levert is sent crashing to the canvass. Chuckie then goes to set Levert up for his finisher. One: Looks like Chuckie Martinez is going to retain his title here tonight. Dan: Chuckie Martinez then sets up Anthony Levert, by accident, Levert bumps into the referee. The referee then stumbles to the canvass. As Chuckie then goes to execute his patented finisher, the lights just suddenly fade out. One: What the.. What is going on? Did the TWL forget to pay the electric bill? [The familiar cry of "VIVA EL PUERCO!" is heard. The lights come back on and El Puerco is standing right in front of Martinez, who is too busy trying to execute his finisher to notice.] Dan: What is he doing here? Can't he see we have a match going on? One: I guess he wants a part of the action. Dan: Just as Martinez goes to execute his finisher, El Puerco shoves Martinez away from Anthony Levert and then with a high impact face buster, sends Martinez crashing to the canvass! El Puerco then then goes over to a Anthony Levert who begins to stir. Levert is the with a powereful rocker dropper, sent crashing to the canvass. One: What carnage we are seeing from El Puerco or The Pig! The referee is still down and it looks as if El Puerco is going to make this match into an even worse situation then it has already gotten to. Dan: El Puerco pulls Levert over and places his arm across Martinez's chest and alerts the referee before he leaves the ring and disappears into the crowd. D I N G ! ] Carl: Ladies and Gentleman, the winner of the match via a pinfall at a time of 18:07 and therefore NNNNNNNEEEEEWWWWWWWW TWL CRUSIERWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WORLD.. A N T H O N Y L E V E R T!!!!! [The crowds with an astronomical pop as "Faith" by Limp Bizkit begins to blare throughout the arena. Levert then celebrates and then he just exits the ring with Chuckie Martinez shocked and confused as to what has just occured. We then fade back to Bobby and Dan.] Bobby McCarville: So El Puerco played a huge role in the winning of Levert's Cruiserweight Title -- but that meant _nothing_ along the lines of friendship. [Clip of him sacrificing Dr. Marvin Davis.] To win the TWL Cruiserweight Title. [Clip of Levert and his former partner D'Arby winning a tag battle royal to win the titles.] But will that mean anything? [Clip of Levert getting the win over Chuckie Martinez.] Will he overcome Puerco and prove himself as a legitimate champion? [Clip of Levert putting a helpless opponent into the Levert Clutch.] With XeRo as the ref... [A still of Levert and Puerco standing side by side, overlapped by a CW Title is shown.] EL PUERCO ANTHONY LEVERT CRUISERWEIGHT TITLE! [Fade from that, to an Armageddon logo, and then back to Bobby and Dan.] Bobby McCarville: And this brings us to our second title defense of the evening. Dan Cody: Yep! This is a bit early on the card, but it should still be one of _THE_ best matches on the whole thing! And Davidson is more of an easy-going type, and him joining Group Sex pretty much gives you any proof you might need about that. Bobby McCarville: People have also been wondering why Blades doesn't take a step up instead of stay at Television Title level. Most people believe he has what it takes to challenge for the WORLD Title! Dan Cody: Well, I don't think he'll have much else but defending that strap on his mind this weekend. [Fade to a black and white shot of Dave Davidson flexing his muscles.] "Delicious" Dave Davidson. Definitely one of the fastest-rising stars in the TWL. [Clip of Dave battling it out with 19 others at the Battle Royal during ACDIH.] A member of the hottest group in the TWL right now -- Group Sex. [A clip of Dave letting Hutchinson pin him for the victory during the 6-man tag.] Definitely champion material. [Clip of him performing the "Delicious Press".] We'll see if he can really prove himself... [Clip of Dave walking to the ring is eclipsed by one of Blades doing the exact same thing -- almost like a "morph".] Adrian Blades The TWL Television Champion. [A picture of the title comes onto the screen, and we can see a reflection in the center of the belt -- it's the face of Adrian Blades.] He's fought the best. [Clip of Adrian wrestling BJM, and Q-Ball.] He's defeated many. [Clip of him performing the "Smashing Bomb".] But this may be his toughest challenge yet... "DELICIOUS" DAVE DAVIDSON ADRIAN BLADES TELEVISION TITLE! [Fade from that, to an Armageddon logo, and then back to Bobby and Dan.]
