TWL: Recruiting

3 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The TWL, which has been dubbed one of the "hot" feds on the 'net recently, is currently on a recruitment drive. Our singles roster is full at the moment (but feel free to fill out an app, we never turn away top talent), however, our tag roster has several spots open.
TWL is a completely booked , original wrestler fed, with a weekly flash show entitled Flash Forward. We do have a one-flash-per-week limit, which some people may dislike, but we feel that this rule is necessary in order to keep quantity from prevailing over quality. We also have one weekly live show, called Monday Massacre, which features between six and nine matches.
So, if you want to compete with the likes of Sex Sells, who finished eighth on the RSPWF Tag Team 200, and the world reknowned God's Justice, visit the webpage or reply to this post for more infomation.
