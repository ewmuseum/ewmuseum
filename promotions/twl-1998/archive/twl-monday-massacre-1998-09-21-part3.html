Monday Massacre (part 3 of 4)

21 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JOHN: This next match-up is for a highly-sought-after title here in the TWL. The Cruiserweight Division here is one of the hottest in the sport. SM: You said it-I am in complete agreement-FOR ONCE. Of course, these little guys are really no match for the BIG BOYS-like ZULKIM! BOB: Can we have ONE MATCH without you mentioning him? SM: Nope. BOB: I didn't think so! Anyway, we have our current Cruiserweight Champion here in TWL, the Sandman, versus the Main Man. JOHN: The Main Man is a SERIOUS contender for this belt-he has really turned it up a notch here in the TWL. Sandman will be ready for this challenge, though, as he always is. Sandman has to be considered a top contender for ANY title here in the TWL! BOB: And the Main Man is making people take notice of his skills as well! SM: But this match is really sad since it's a family thing. . . BOB: HUH? What do you mean? SM: Sandman and Main Man-they have the same last name! Maybe they are brothers? JOHN: Good grief! Let's get our match underway! The ring announcer is getting ready to call this one. . . SHYLOCK: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN!!! This contest is for the TWL Cruiserweight Championship! (CROWD POPS!!!) SHYLOCK: First, our challenger! He hails from Seattle, Washington and weighs in at 220 pounds. He is the self-proclaimed number one contender for the Cruiserweight Title, he is MAAAAAAAIIIN MAAAAAAAAN!!! *He walks to the ring as the song "Iris" is being played and he walks out to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He is carrying 3 roses and he hands them to women in the crowd and kisses them on the cheek as he continues to walk to the ring.* JOHN: Well, apparently the Main Man is trying to get the crowd behind him on this one, but they are usually solidly behind the Sandman! They don't call the TWL "The House the Sandman Built" for nothing! And it looks like he's getting ready to start this one, where is the Sandman? SM: Maybe we're lucky and the dufus forgot how to get here! ( "Smells Like Teen Spirit" by Nirvana plays over the PA, the crowd is buzzing, as this is Wood's Music. Then, on the Screen you see the inside of a plane. Sandman is wearing a white jumpsuit, and a parachute. He stands up.) Sandman: Main Man, Your just an object standing in my way, And tonight, I will dispose of You!!! Have A Nice Day! ( He then leaps out of the plane. The camera zooms in, and watches him fall effortlessly. Then, out comes his parachute. The camera Changes to one inside the Arena. The crowd pops as he enters the arena from the sky. They have forgotten about him entering to Wood's theme, and they are deafening. He floats down, right at the Main man, He then does a Twisting Hurracanrana, OUT OF A PLANE! The Main Man rolls out of the ring. Sandman floated into the middle of the ring, Mic in hand, and looks at The Main Man.) Sandman: get back in here, you coward. You are a great athlete, but I, the People's champ( gets a huge pop at this) am going to destroy you. SM: HOW THE HELL DID HE DO THAT? HE STOLE THAT OFF OF ALEX EXTREME, DIDN'T HE??? JOHN: Who knows? It was pretty incredible, though! He looks to have pissed off the Main Man! Sandman iss discarding the sky-diving gear and he is wearing his wrestling stuff underneath. . . BOB: And he's too slow! Main Man rushes in and takes him down with a double-leg! He is POUNDING on Sandman, with rights and lefts to the face! He is taking it to the Sandman here! SM: YEAH! Give him one for me, Mainy! JOHN: Now he's standing, and Sandman is still trying to get the jumpsuit off! He is lifted by Main Man, who whips him into the corner! Sandman flips up-THE JUMPSUIT IS HUNG UP ON THE RINGPOST! BOB: Now he's like a hanging punching bag! Big kicks to the midsection of Sandman and he’s feeling it here! SM: YEAH! He's taking it to him-Sandman might have made a cool entrance, but he is paying for it now! What's that ref doing? BOB: His JOB! He's making Main Man back off so that Sandman can discard the suit! The ref helps him out of it as he untangles him, and Sandman drops to the mat! JOHN: And a seated dropkick by Main Man keeps him there! Now Main Man lifts him by his hair, and Sandman elbows him in the gut! Main Man is staggered, and Sandman goes into the ropes to try to nail Main Man with a running lariat. . . SM: BUT NO! Main Man catches him with an arm-drag takedown! Sandman back up quickly, and ANOTHER arm-drag has him on the mat again! Sandman staggers to his feet, and Main Man is there and brings him down AGAIN with a flying headscissors takedown! JOHN: Sandman is looking pretty bad right now-he has to get something going! Main Man has caught him off guard! Sandman may not have been taking his opponent seriously enough! BOB: I agree! And now Sandman doesn't seem to know where he is! He looks glassy-eyed! As he stands, Main Man is going up top! OH! SM: BIG MISSILE DROPKICK! He knocks down Sandman! Sandman is OUT OF IT! What a MASSACRE! JOHN: Now Sandman is laid out! Main Man motions to the crowd, and he is going for that standing moonsault. . . BOB: AND SANDMAN ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY! NOTHING BUT MAT! SM: MAIN MAN, YOU IDIOT!!! BOB: I can't believe it! Sandman looked half-dead, but he rolled out of the way and Main Man took the damage! Now Sandman rolls over on top of Main Man and hooks the leg! . 1. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . . SM: YES! A kick out! Main Man is still going! BOB: He's still in this! JOHN: And both men are on their feet! SM: YES! Main Man rakes the eyes of Sandman! Sandman is still in trouble here! Main Man is putting on a VERY impressive display tonight! BOB: And he whips the Sandman into the ropes, and as Sandman comes back, he is NAILED with a big clothesline! Now Main Man is rolling out of the ring! JOHN: He grabs Sandman's foot and pulls HIM out! They are both outside, and the referee is warning them! CLANG!!! SM: YEAH! Sandman was just WHIPPED into the steps with authority! He grabs Sandman again. . . CLANG!!! JOHN: And Main man is whipped into the guardrail! Nice reversal from the Sandman! This is what Sandman needs to do! He has to HURT Main Man and make this thing equal again! BOB: He picks up a chair-Sandman goes for a Van Daminator! OH! He just missed it! Main Man ducked and Sandman is on the floor! Main Man is back up in the ring-and he goes to the top-oh no! He's going for it! SM: He's going for a big splash to the outside! He's UP! . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . BANG!!! BOB: HE MISSED! HE MISSED! SANDMAN ROLLED OUT OF THE WAY!!! JOHN: Now Sandman lifts up Main Man, and throws him into the ring, and he follows him! He covers the Main Man! . 1 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . SM: MAIN MAN KICKS OUT!!! BOB: Incredible! What a display! Now both men are on their feet! JOHN: And Sandman rushes Main Man-LOW BLOW! MAIN MAN NAILS SANDMAN DOWN THERE!!! SM: That's why I LOVE the Main Man! JOHN: Now Main Man is ready to finish this off! He goes behind Sandman and hooks up a DRAGON SLEEPER!!! BOB: Look at Sandman! He is in TERRIBLE pain! He might submit! He looks like he's going to submit! SM: WAIT! Sandman is riding back on the hold and he flips his legs up! BOB: He floats over the shoulder of Main Man! He flips and lands behind the Main Man! Sandman grabs him by the waist! BIG GERMAN SUPLEX AND A BRIDGE INTO A PINNING COMBINATION!!! . 1 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . SM: Main Man is trying to get his shoulder up! . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . BOB: He can't get it up! . . . . . . . . . . . . . 3 . !!! JOHN: IT'S OVER! SHYLOCK: Your winner at 8:12 and STILL TWL Cruiserweight Champion, THE SAAAAAANNNNNNDMAAAAAAAN!!! (POP!!!) JOHN: What a match! Incredible! I think I'm not alone when I say that the Main Man DEFINITELY has earned a re-match! Sandman is just able to steal a victory and retain his title! Bob: You’re not alone on that one John! Main Man put up one HELL of a fight! Now Sandman’s getting up... John: Here comes Thunder Blair!! Bob: Blair jumps into the ring, and starts pounding on Sandman. After the match he’s just had, Sandman must really be hurting now! John: Main Man gets up, and starts beating on Sandman too! Bob: Blair drags Sandman to his feet... a DDT!! John: Now here comes Prince Geatar! SM: YES! We’ve got a party, and everyone is invited to beat the crap out of Sandman! Bob: Prince Geatar gets into the ring and heads over to the three, he goes towards Sandman, and moves him out of the way of a leg drop! Now he clotheslines Blair!! SM: What the hell is he doing? John: I don’t know, but Wood is coming down too! What is going on? Bob: Wood and Geatar are in the ring, and they’re back-to-back, while Blair and Main Man leave, but the damage has been done - Sandman is on the ground, and Wood and Prince Geatar help him up. The three of them are standing in the ring together. SM: YAY! Three losers are together. John: I don’t know what you’re talking about Strong Man, because the Television and Cruiserweight Champions are both in that ring! Bob: Have they united? What is going on? Why did Sandman enter to Wood’s music? We have a lot of unanswered questions folks! John: Hopefully we’ll get some answers, but for now we’re getting ready for the MAIN EVENT! Shylock: The following contest is a special match, and will be for the TWL World Title, and the TWL Intercontinental Title! The rules: First man may only be eliminated over-the-top, the second must be pinned, and the third must submit! The winner of this bout receives the TWL World Title, with the runner-up getting the TWL Intercontinental Title. Introducing first, from Port aux Basques, Newfoundland, Canada, weighing in, at 275 lbs., he is a member of the Dominators, BRILLIANT BRRRRUCE!!! ("Blood Bath In Paradise" blasts over the speakers. Brilliant Bruce comes out with Quick Cat to 5 loud bangs and many fire works. Brilliant Bruce is quickly making his way to the ring. He seems to be waiting for the other three opponents cause he has an evil look on his face. Quick Cat is in the ring with him and he looks to be pissed as well for some reason. Brilliant Bruce wants a mic and is granted one. He signals for Quick Cat to leave the ring so he can speak seriously.) Bruce: Ray Robertson, if you're the next one to walk through those curtains, I am coming right after you. Billy Joe, if you come out through those curtains, you're going to get you ass kicked too!!!! So come on out whom ever is coming out. I feel like winning back a title tonight!!! (He throws down the mic and waits the arrival of the next opponent.) Shylock: And one of his opponents, from Atlanta, Georgia, the CURRENT TWL Intercontinental Champion, weighing in at 246 lbs., he is "The Natural" RAY ROBERTSOOOOOON!! ("Machine Gun" by Jimi Hendrix blares throughout the stadium, as Ray Robertson steps out to a huge ovation. Robertson's face is very serious, as he slowly walks towards the ring, ignoring the fans on the way there. The crowd is going nuts, but Ray seems to block them out, and is totally focused.) Shylock: And one of their opponents--- John: Bruce attacks Ray, and now they’re exchanging blows! Bob: I guess he just couldn’t wait! Shylock: And one of their opponents, from Tulsa, Oklahoma, weighing in at 294 pounds, he is a two-time TWL champion of the WORRRRRRR-LD!!!....BILLY .... JOE .... McCALLISTER!!! [``Sympathy for the Devil," by Guns-N-Roses begins over the PA as the entire arena goes dark. Only the light from the jumbotron can be seen, which is showing past McCallister conquests. Suddenly, fire shoots down in two neat rows on the entrance ramp, all the way to the edge of the ring, catching Bruce and Robertson's attention. Laser lights point from the rafters of the dome to the corners of the ring, almost surrounding the other combatents.] John: What in the hell is going on here? Bob: We've seen some changes in McCallister lately on the TWL Interview show, but this is something! And now Bruce and Ray have also stopped fighting slightly, they’re watching the lights. [The video screen turns to clips of Zulkim's actions on Thursday night Terror against Asylum. The crowd boos loudly. Two explosions go off at the top of the ramp, sending sparks flying to the rafters. Out from a large, ominous blue light come 12 expressionless men. Six are dressed as Catholic priests, the other six are dressed as Satan, complete with pitchforks. The crowd is in stunned amazement as McCallister appears at the ramp in the light, carrying a small book in his right hand.] SM: There's the little bastard!! John: I wouldn't tick him off right now Strong Man!!! Bob: I wouldn't come anywhere near him if I didn't have to!!! [McCallister takes three steps toward the ring so all the fans can see him. He then waves his arms frantically, stopping the lights, the music, the fire and calming the fans to a halt.] John: Holy..... [McCallister pulls a microphone from the pocket of the white robe he is wearing over his wrestling gear. The 6 priests and 6 devils stand quietly, in a line on either side of McCallister, equal distance apart from each other.] BJM: [In a deep, godly voice] WHAT YOU SEE HERE, BROTHERS AND SISTERS, IS THE BEGINNING OF A NEW REVOULTION!!! BOW AND PRAY FOR YOUR OWN WELL-BEING IF YOU BELIEVE!!! [the crowd, almost as one, bows as much as their seats will allow them. The arena is quiet. Robertson and Bruce are perplexed and looking around nervously.] BJM: IT IS FROM YOU, MY CRUSADERS, THAT I SHALL GATHER THE STRENGTH TO DEFEAT THE T.A.K.E.O.L. INSURGENCE!! BUT FIRST, YOU TWO [pointing at Robertson and Bruce] MUST PERISH!!! RISE UP, CRUSADERS!!! [The fans come to their feet in unison, cheering McCallister on as he sheds the robe, hands the book to the last priest, the microphone to the last Satan and runs toward ringside, opening an assault on both Robertson and Brilliant Bruce. The 6 priests and 6 devils come toward ringside slowly, encircling the ring.] John: This is the scariest thing I've ever seen. SM: Even scarier than Thursday night??? John: I think quite possibly....Yes! Bob: McCallister, Robertson and Bruce, are all battling each other now! (Shylock is outside the ring now, for safety reasons) Shylock: And the final opponent, from "The Path to Enlightenment" weighing in at 256 lbs., he is the CURRENT, TWL CHAMPION OF THE WORLD! ZULKIM... THE PHILOSOPHER!!! [CONTINUED...]
