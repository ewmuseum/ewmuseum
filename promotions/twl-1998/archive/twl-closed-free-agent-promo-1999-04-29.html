"Damn, TWL Closed....so, Free Agent"

29 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yeah, with the closing of TWL :( , I must once again test the free agent market....here is a promo and a sample rp, both I used for the WWO :)
----------------------- [The camera fades into a shot of the Lady of Justice statue, it looks as if it has seen years of weathering, due to all the cracks and breaks all over the frame of it. It then begins to strobe flash scenes with a man wearing a cop uniform walking towards the camera, his face is still blackend to the camera.]
V/O: "For 14 years, he's given one hundred percent night in and night out, for 14 years, he's sacrificed everything that he has known. Now, answering the cry-out for justice and reform, he stands tall against outsiders and a spoiled breed who would change the face of professional wrestling into a gang war.....of course he stands alone...."
[The camera switches completly to the man walking towards the camera, who stands there, he is wearing a tan police uniform and a pair of sunglasses. His hair is buzzed around the sides and a short flat-top, his top us unbuttoned and underneath he has a plain white t-shirt. He shows little emotion as he pulls off his sunglasses....."Bad to the Bone" begins to play.]
[The camera cuts to scenes of him throwing the STF onto an opponent who screams out for mercy, but gets none.]
[It the shows him standing in the face of 7 foot monster and showing no back down in his eyes.]
[Another clip shows him laying out members of a stable with a nightstick as he stands over an injured wrestler laying on the mat.]
V/O: "To this man, justice isn't blind, justice is swift and is always carried out to his liking...."
[It shows the front row of a crowd hanging onto him as he rests against the barricade, with a smile on his face.]
[Again the statue of Lady Justice is shown, but this time it crumbles into a heap of rocks. A single black boot steps onto a few of the pebbles of the fallen statue. The camera pans up to once again show the man in the police uniform, his eyes show no emotion and he is pounding the nightstick into one of his hands. A flash of light and the shadow on the wall is that of the Lady
Justice Statue standing tall once again. The words....]
"..........OFFICER STEELE IS ON PATROL..........." [....flash across the scene as the camera fades.] [The camera fades into a small interview room, about 8x8 feet wide and a couple of feet taller then that. The walls are covered with the "Officer Steele" and "WWO" logos. There is a small light hanging from the ceiling, giving it that almost prison setting to the whole thing. There standing underneath the hanging light is Officer Steele himself, Eric Van Steele. He is decked out in full police grab, with the tan uniform top unbuttoned about halfway down and a plain white t-shirt underneath. He has got his nightstick in hand and a pair of aviator sunglasses on, he begins to beat the nightstick into one hand, then speaks with a confident tone in his voice.]
"Yes....I am that guy. I am that man who made wrestling a creedo for thousands and thousands of fans in the midwest. I changed the indy fed circuts and made myself a legend....so be it. Now, the time is right, I have picked my spot here in the WWO and I don't plan on doing anything less then being what I have always been, a legend. Yet, you say, where does this guy get off acting as if he has a God complex? Well, I'll tell ya....You see, the EVS train to fame started 14 years ago and it hasn't stopped since, so you can say what you want about this guy and that, but the fact of the matter is, when EVS gets into the ring, somebody is going to meet God face to face."
[Off from the side steps out "The Head Nurse" Rachel, she is wearing a skimpy nurses uniform and has her long red hair pulled up top, she throws her arms around EVS and speaks with a soft lovely voice.]
"Eric, aren't you sending the wrong message out about yourself?" [He gives a quick smile to her and continues to speak the way that only EVS can..] "But don't get me wrong, EVS is the man of the people, EVS will put butts in the seat, and EVS will sell your merchandise, why? Cause EVS, doesn't let up for Jimmy "the Farmer" Fatass...no sir, he will kick "The Farmer"'s ass around the ring, just as if he was standing in the ring with the World
Champion! And the people know this, and that's why the people come to see the hardest working man in professional sports today, Eric Van Steele. But for every EVS out there, there is some arrogant punk, some no talent hack who is talking his talk and letting others do the walk for him. I will personally make it my goal to weed these people out, then plant my 16 size shoe halfway up, well you get the picture. It is a crime what wrestling fans are getting out of today's cards, half of the time it's a wrestler who didn't graduate high school trying to talk about God knows what. Call me a throwback to the old days, but I don't mind seeing two guys beat the crap out of each other so that one can call himself a winner. In my mind, that is what this outfit represents, justice.
Justice for those that have sacrficed long hard years just to see them burned up righ before their very own eyes."
[Rachel runs her hand down the inside of the uniform and runs her other hand around the back of the neck of EVS. He rocks his hand back and gives a nice smile for the camera.]
"You know Eric, most of these big timers have never even heard of you?" [He quickly snaps his neck forward and almost shrugs off Nurse Rachel. His smile quickly disappears and his voice has more of an anger overtone to it.]
"And you know what Rach? I doubt they even care. But, does that get to me? ....NOT A CHANCE!.... Do you think I care if some spoiled brat thinks or doesn't think about me? ....NOT A CHANCE!..... But after they get in the ring with me, will they still have the same opinion of me? ....NOT A CHANCE!..... [Rachel, quickly jumps back around the side of EVS and holds her arms around his chest, she begins to play with the pockets on his shirt and looks up and down.]
"Mmmm...you know, you are pretty sexy when you are angry Eric, and damn don't you look rough and tough in that uniform of yours."
[He looks at her up and down and quickly comes back with a sarcasim tone in his voice.] "Please mam, not while I'm on duty [They both look at eachother and begin to laugh.] Baby, I am all yours. And as for the WWO, well, I think that the long and strong arm of the law is going to make this place almost acceptable....."
[Rachel grabs the nightstick from EVS's hand begins to walk off of the visible screen, just as the camera begins to fade, her arm shoots back and you can hear her say.]
"Don't forget those cuffs...."
