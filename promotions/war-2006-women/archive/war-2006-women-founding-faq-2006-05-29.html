WAR — Women's Athletic Revolution FAQ

29 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The WAR FAQ CONTENTS: 1. What is WAR? 2. So I don't have to write roleplays or strats? 3. Who runs it in real life? 4. Who are the on-screen personalities? 5. So how does this work? 6. Will there be any titles? 7. Is there anything I'm forgetting? 8. Do I have to fill out an application to get my character on the roster? 1. What is WAR? WAR stands for Women's Athletic Revolution and is a women's wrestling promotion based in Los Angeles, CA. All of their shows are taped from the WAR Arena in L.A. and are sold as dvds and digital downloads from WAR's website. Tapings are usually done twice a month. Out of character, for now WAR is a minimal summary fed in the same sense as joshi puro feds like Beauty Angels Japan, KOKORO, and Project BATTLE*DREAM. It will consist of important news articles, wrestler comments, future card line-ups, as well as results with summaries of what happened. WAR is a side project of mine, while not working on BAJ or writing flashes/role plays as a handler. However I someday hope to make it into a full-fledged, handler-friendly fed. 2. So I don't have to write roleplays or strats? That's right. If you want to, knock yourself out. I'll include 'em on the cards. But it's not mandatory. I write everything myself for the entertainment of the fed's readers. I also hope to build a roster around other people's characters. While they aren't required to write roleplays or strats for their matches, they are welcome to send me ideas for possible future angles or feuds. I believe this will also help with our eventual transition into a handler-friendly promotion if the fed is full of characters created by other people. 3. Who runs it in real life? That would be me, [name withheld]. I've been in this game since Fall of '99 and I've handled a vast number of characters. I've also run Beauty Angels Japan for a couple of years now. I also tried a something similiar to this with CLAW, which didn't get off of the ground. But this will not require as much work and is the chance to start small and become big, so I'm hoping that it will be successful. 4. Who are the personalities? PRESIDENT/CEO: "The Goddess" Nina Larue - She's been in the wrestling business for a couple of years now and is currently a wrestler in Mighty Bastard Championship. She purchased the old WAR from SHIORI, after her CLAW venture fizzled, and created this promotion because she believes strongly in the importance of having somewhere for women in North America to train and compete. She will be a completely off-camera presence. HEAD REFEREE: Vicki Task - A former wrestler for CAT and referee for the old Battling Ring Angels Promotion. Vicki isn't afraid to call things down the middle and is more than willing to stand up to the heels, when they get out of hand. BACKUP REFEREES: Avery O'Neil and Savannah Blaze - Avery's also a big fan of women's wrestling and got his start as a referee for BRA. Savannah is a former wrestler and became a referee in the old WAR. COMMENTATORS: Kenny Harbor and Sandra St. James - Kenny's a former commentator for BRA, computer geek, and women's wrestling aficionado. He astounds many with his complete knowledge of women's wrestling. Sandra is a former wrestler for both IFWF and FAWN. Like Kenny, she calls things down the middle but shows preference for the more face type wrestlers. INTERVIEWER: Jenna Jamestown - She's also a former BRA staff member. Jenna's perky and sweet but completely professional, although she has been known to be intimidated by some of the heels. TRAINERS: Eduardo Perez and Reina Kato - They're also former wrestlers and pretty harsh task masters. But the results of their hard work can't be denied. 5. So how does this work? As mentioned previously, things will start slow, so there are no plans for super cards yet. Tapings occur twice a month and are subject to change based upon wrestler's availability. Right now, roster size is open although I am aiming for 20 to start. WAR accepts almost all styles of fighter: from Strong Style, high flyer, brawler, shootfighter, etc. Although the company may make use of much more violent matches from time to time, they place a high value on real wrestling talent. 6. Will there be any titles? Yes. The Queen of WAR Title - WAR's top prize. Will be defended every 60 days. The WAR World Tag Team Titles - WAR's top prize for tag team wrestlers. Will also be defended every 60 days. 7. Is there anything I'm forgetting? If you're interested in joining or following the fed, be sure to sign on to our Yahoo Group mailing list to keep up with the happenings in WAR - http://games.groups.yahoo.com/group/Womens_Athletic_Revolution/ 8. Do I have to fill out an application to get my character on the roster? Preferably, yes. Send all applications to [detail withheld]. The information I need is listed below. Put "WAR App" in the subject. Wrestler Name: Height: Weight: Hometown: Bio(Feel Free to Keep Short): Entrance Music: Movelist: Finisher: If you have one, attach a picture of who you use as your wrestler's pic rep too. Again, send your applications to [detail withheld]. Thanks to [name withheld] for the use of his KOKORO FAQ.
