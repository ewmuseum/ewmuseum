The GCW is Looking for New Members

The Global Chaos Wrestling federation is loking for new talent. The GCW is a fed with a difference. We don't accept children and we don't accept bad roleplayers.
Right now we only have one card a week but with more wrestlers applying we may begin to have 2 cards a week. The matches are determined by way of flashes and booking. We use an email based flash system. The better your flashes the better you do.
If you want a challenge and like a well developed E-fed check out the GCW
