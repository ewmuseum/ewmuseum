Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

SEFW opened on 9 September 2001, its own operator announcing it after five years spent in another federation and promising it would go active within ten days — “i just started my own after five years in another. we have not gone active yet, but we will in the next ten days.”

It made good on that promise: a post of 23 September 2001 reports that SEFW “held our first card on 9-20, crowning our first world champion,” with about ten active wrestlers on the roster and more still wanted.

The federation's own record then goes quiet until 1 March 2002, when its operator wrote to say SEFW was “back in business” after admitting “we got off track in September.” That return post reported a membership of “58 handlers strong,” insisted the promotion was “by no means a dead fed, a stillborn fed, or a flash in the pan,” and announced that its first card of 2002 would carry an SEFW World Title match and a weapons battle royal to crown a Confederate States champion, holder of the CSA Belt.

That card was set for 7 March 2002, and a post the same day confirmed the roster had been updated with spots still open, setting the next card for 14 March.

No document survives past that 7 March 2002 update. Whether the 14 March card was held, whether the SEFW World Title match and the CSA Belt battle royal it promised for its 2002 return ever took place, and how or when the federation finally ended aren't in the surviving record.
