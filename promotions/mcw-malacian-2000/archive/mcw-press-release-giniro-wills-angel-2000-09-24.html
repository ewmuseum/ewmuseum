MCW Press Release: Giniro, Wills, Angel Sign

24 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yesterday, the MCW had the pleasure of signing Free Agent Rio D. Giniro to it's MCW Lineup. D. Giniro is a RSPWF Legend, and his signing certainly ushers in the new MCW era, that of being an Elite oriented mail fed rather than a open to everyone message board fed. Another RSPWF signing to the MCW Lineup is Johnny Wills. His bad ass, don't give a f*ck about anybody and ultra heelish attitude is a huge addition to MCW, since most of the best wrestlers are faces, with only Alysian Scottsfield as a dominant heel. MCW also resigned one of its veterans, Mike Angel. Angel is a mainstay in the MCW Hardcore division and he should remain so for a good moment. Since making the Message Board to E-Mail switch, MCW have gained trememdous credibility and slowly gets away from the "Fed For Kids" label. Our roleplayers are experienced, dedicated and very talented. MCW also benefits to the efed world as guys unheard of on RSPWF are getting some spotlight. We expect MCW Wrestlers to break out on the RSPWF Rankings soon, and we expect guys on the RSPWF Rankings to sign with MCW. The Message Board MCW had 40 Plus superstars. As only the best are allowed to join the E-Mail MCW, the lineup was cut down to originally 7 wrestlers. Now, we had 3 signings, which brings our total to 10. Talents known as Lord Mojo Of The Quaganites, Class Christopher, Lord Micheal Of Northumbria and Johnny Devine all have been invited to join the E-Mail MCW, since their performance in the Message Board MCW was very good. While Mojo, Class Chris and Lord Micheal are veterans, mainly of the PWF, Johnny Devine is a young gun (Ironically, He was a part of tag team named "The Young Guns" in a fed named the AWA when MCW hired him), and is certainly the future of MCW, along with the likes of Eric Taylor and Lewis Crowe. Ravnos and Zodiac have been sent down to the MCWX. Lack of abilities was the reason. They are unlikely to be ever called up, and if they do not show some dedication in MCWX, they will probably be released. MCWX have talents like Oz, Khalif Mohammed Assim, Anggun and Steve Corino (In character evaluation) and these guys will have the maximum of air time they need to get better.
