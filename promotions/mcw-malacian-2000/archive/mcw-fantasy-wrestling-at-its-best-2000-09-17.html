Fantasy Wrestling at its best... JOIN NOW!

17 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Maple Championship Wrestling is an e-fed based out of Canada, with incredible talented wrestlers from all over the world. We are a booked fed, and we run using e-mail flash packets. We've been open since June, and there are no signs of the MCW slowing down. The MCW is excellent because of its great storylines, competition and results. Our e-wrestlers' roleplaying talents are second to none, with big names such as Phil Raven, Lord Mojo of the Quaganites, Max Caschera, Class Christopher, Alysian Scottsfield, Lord Michael of Northumbria and T. Read. Our results are jam-packed with action and excitement, whether it be Thunder or a Pay-Per-View. And our e-wrestlers are great because they work together to do the best that they possibly can. You simply can't ask for better than the coolest fantasy wrestling federation on the Internet! The MCW is run by two legendary roleplayers and experienced e-fed presidents, Rob and Paul. The roleplaying environment here is excellent, everyone is very friendly, and we're always looking for more dedicated roleplayers. So, if you want a challenge, if you want a fed that delivers nothing but top-quality, or if you just want to have fun, and your wrestler is a free agent at the moment, we'd be interested in your wrestler being hired by the MCW. What are you waiting for? Join the coolest fantasy wrestling federation on the Internet TODAY! http://www.crosswinds.net/~maplewrestling or http://mcw.icewolf.net And if you're looking for a more beginner to intermediate e-fed, you can go to the MCW Extreme, an awesome e-fed for beginners and those who aren't ready for the MCW level yet. But don't be fooled: this e-fed rules and has lots of great wrestlers and results, so don't delay, join today! http://mcwextreme.icewolf.net
