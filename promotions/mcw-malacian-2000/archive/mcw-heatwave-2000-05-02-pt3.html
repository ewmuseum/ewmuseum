DF: "So, how's your mind-set going into a match with a man who knows almost every angle of submission style wrestling?" SB: "Well, ya know I don't really care about that the fact is I'm going to go into that ring give it my all, and hopefully by the end of the night Barjuk "Bad" Mojo will be out like a light! Compliments of the Bourdominator! I'll be back!" Barjuk is up again, whipped again, backdropped again -- and again goes even higher into the air -- and falls even farther to the canvas with an even more resounding... thud! Huge face Pop as Bourdo picks Mojo to his feet one more time, whipping him shortside and charging -- Bourdo clotheslining Bourdo over the top rope, over the apron and all the way to the floor! Big face Pop!] DF: "Barjuk not giving up, and now Bourdo slides outside the ring in no mans land! And Bourdo's caught off gaurd with a huge diving forearm smash! Barjuk lifts Bourdo to his feet and whips him into the steel gaurd rail though it's reversed! WAIT! Barjuk reversed it again! And Bourdo hits the steel railing in the kidney's!" EF: "He'll feel that tommorow!" DF: "Barjuk attempted a almost suicide dive manuever though Bourdo saw it coming a mile away and move, now Mojo slain over the gaurd rail, which is somehow still standing." [Bourdo pulls Barjuk by the air and slides him into the ring and rolls in himself just before a ten count, he goes onto the apron springboards over it and leg drops the head of Barjuk. Huge face pop!] DF: "Bourdo showing us his high-flying ability." EF: "I've seen a lot better." DF: "Bourdo only got a one count, and he lifts Barjuk to his feet and a samoan drop, Barjuk "Bad" Mojo quickly rolling out the ring." [Heel pop!] DF: "Mojo calling for the referee, looks like he's saying Bourdo is cheating.. wait! Who's that, an old man rolls into the ring and a double arm DDT on Stephen C. Bourdo! Who was that old man!" DF: "He had more wrinkles then.. then.. _YOU_ Dave!" [Barjuk rolls into the ring covering, getting an easy three count. Ding ding ding!] VL: "And your winner and advancing to round two of the Malacian Heritage Tournament -- BARJUK "BAD" MOJO!!!" [Heel pop.] [Commercial break.] ################################## MMMM MMMM CCCCCCCCC WW WW MM MMMM MM CCC CC WW WW MM MM MM CCC WW WW WW MM MM CCC CC WW WWWW WW MM MM CCCCCCCCC WWWW WWWW *Malacian Championship* "Sweet" Jarred Bombay (c) versus. Chris Masters ################################## [Cut to Vince Lawson in the squared circle. The announcer raises his microphone.] VL: "Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is for the MALACIAN CHAMPIONSHIP!" [Pop!] VL: "And it is scheduled for one fall, with a forty-five minute time limit." [Pauses.] VL: "Introducing first the champion, weighing in at 267 pounds and standing at 6'3" here is.. " S W E E T " J A R R E D B O M B A Y ! ! ! " [Tribal drum beats can be heard over the PA. The lights go out in the arena.] DF: "Oh now what?" EF: "I think we are going to see a little of good ole BOMBAY." DF: "What kind of stunt is he going to pull this time?" [The lights center at the entrance way and beautfiul women dressed in flower skirts and cocanut bras.] EF: "I don't know but I like this one so far. What do you think?" DF: "Too early to tell." [The women all have buckets in there hands and they slowly walk to the ring grabbing flower petals out of the buckets and tossing them on the ground and at the fans.] DF: "Flowers? what is he up to?" [Behind the women appear 10 guys dressed in tribal gear. And perched on their shoulders is a huge wooden stand. And on that stand is a throne with Jarred Bombay sitting on it.] DF: "This is insult to injury right here." EF: "What? Jarred is just trying to relate to the people. He is there champion, and should be treated as such." DF: "He might be their champion, but he isnt freakin Ceaser." EF: "To the Malacians he is equvilant to a Ceaser or maybe even a Hitler." DF: "Oh for heaven's sake." [The 10 men carry Bombay down to the ring, Bombay has the MCW title over his shoulder and is blowing kisses in the air to the fans. The women all get into the ring and they all kneel.] EF: "Looks like all these women are on their knees for Bombay." DF: "Please this is a family show." EF: "Dave, your mind is always in the gutter." DF: "Anyways." [Bombay reaches the ring and gets off of his petistal and into the ring. He waves the men off except for one who comes in the ring with him. Bombay then puts out his left hand and each of the women get up and come over and kiss his left hand and leave the ring.] EF: "Bombay sure has his way with the women." JB: "All my fellow Malacians please rise and sing with me the Malacian National Anthem." DF: "Will you please sit down, Edwin?" EF: "No I'm paying tribute to the Malacians." [Bombay lets the translator speak and then Bombay starts to sing.] JB: "Oh canada, Oh Canada..." [The Boos from the crowd are so loud Bombay stops and looks around.] JB: "Fine I won't sing your anthem. But just hear me out my fellow Malacians. Tonight I am going up aginst an imposter. A fraud." DF: "Who? Chris Masters a fraud?" EF: "Hells yeah, I heard he is being investigated by the IRS as we speak." JB: "Chris Masters claims he is out for justice for the Malacians. For you people. Well tonight I'm going to prove that Masters is just using you people for his own cause." [The translator speaks.] DF: "What language is this? why does Bombay continue to believe that he needs a translator to speak to these people?" EF: "Have you even been paying attention these last couple of weeks? Bombay is trying to communicate with the Malacians." DF: "I think he is trying to drive them nuts." JB: "Chris Masters just can't cut it as a regular old wrestler. He wasn't getting in the spotlight as plain old Chris Masters. Now all of a sudden he starts bad mouthing your champion. Me Jarred Bombay. Saying that I am ridiculing you Malacians. Then claiming that he is going to defeat me and restore justice to you people. Do you people actually believe this man?" [Crowd cheers.] JB: "Honestly, all this man is doing is using you people as his pawns, and he is positioning himself to put me in check mate. And you know what is going to happen if he does indeed do that. He will turn his back on you. See I am out here for you people, and tonight I am going to show you that by destroying Chris Masters and keeping the Malacian Islands in order. So Chris come on out and get your justice." VL: "Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is for the MALACIAN CHAMPIONSHIP!" [Pop!] VL: "And it is scheduled for one fall, with a forty-five minute time limit." [Pauses.] VL: "Introducing first the challenger, weighing in at 295 pounds and standing at 6'6" here is.. C H R I S M A S T E R S ! ! ! " [The lights go down as the theme "Sharp Dressed Man" by Z.Z. Top plays over the arena P.A. system. A large, white man wearing a pair of black wrestling tights and a white shirt with the sleeves cut off. Down the legs of the tights reads "Malacian Comfort" in gold jagged letters and his white cut off shirt reads "Malacian Justice". It zooms in showing Masters angered face as he begins running full speed into the ring..] * DING * DING * DING * DF: "Masters starting off with a huge left hook." [Bombay grabs at Masters huge left arm, which is locked firmly under his jaw. With his right hand, Bombay reaches up and tries to grab at Chris short hair, but he is warned by the referee. Desperate to break the hold, Bombay finally claws wildly with his right hand, and manages to rake Masters eyes, forcing the hold to be broken. [Bombay gets to his feet as Masters does, the bigger man disorientated for a moment by the pain in his eye; Bombay whips Masters into the ropes, and fires back with a clothesline of his own, taking the big man off his feet. Big heel pop!] DF: "Bombay much more agile out-manuevering Chris "Malacian Justice" Masters..." EF: "Ya think, now my boy; Jarred Bombay stomping at that punk Chris Masters." DF: "Your friend?! Wasn't he the guy who shoved you in the locker two days ago?" [Edwin blushes.] [Masters suddenly charges full-speed at Bombay who tries to move back but stumbles and is tackled by Chris Masters.] DF: "A football tackle! Chris Masters with a snap suplex into a odd head-to-head headlock.. though not very effective, Bombay kips-up and easily gets out the much slower Chris Masters taking longer to get to his feet, Bombay meets him half-way and catches Masters with a big spinning heel kick!" EF: "YEAH!" [Masters uneffected by the attack gets up Bombay attempts a hesitant punch, now Masters glares at Bombay, who begins to quaver a bit. He stumbles backward, falling into the turnbuckle, he looks around nervous as Masters walks on smiling a sadistic smile, as he charges Bombay with a flurry of right's and left's.] DF: "WOW! Master's snapped! He does a little mud-hole stomping on Bombay, who is at an awkward angle in the corner, and a knee to the face!" [Masters decimating Bombay circle throws Bombay out of the corner and into the center of the ring. He begins choking Bombay until the referee breaks it up.] DF: "I guess Masters evidently upset about the verbal attacks, and the way he humiliated the Malacians, a little redemption, yeah baby!" EF: "Ahh, shut-up! He was just trying to get in touch with their culture." DF: "Their culture my fanny!" [Masters rolls outside the ring looking for some sort of weapon, suddenly Scott Cairns appears with a chair, and gives it to him right smack on the forehead, and the referee didn't even see it, he rolls Masters into the ring for the three count.] DF: "TWO CHEAP WINS! Sheesh!" EF: "Yeah, baby! HAHA!" [Bombay begins celebrating as Cairns rolls into the ring with a microphone in hand, Bombay about to hug Cairns though Cairns pushes him aside.] SC: "You see Bombay, I saved your sorry carcass time after time! And it is all in vain! I've never once gotten a title shot when I was at your side!" JB: "What are you talking about?" SC: "You know what I'm talking about! You're holding me back!" DF: "Battle of the ego's." SC: "Right here right now! Me and you!" [Pop!] JB: "You want it you got it!" [From the back emerges Salvador Malacia, with a microphone in hand.] SM: "Save it you two, why don't you settle it at the upcoming Pay-Per-View: TURMOIL?!" [The two nod smiling.] SM: "Good, it's settled then." DF: "We've got another blockbuster folks! Former friends battle it out!" EF: "NO! Break up of the century!" [The curtains part and out comes Blake Feldman chasing Malacia around the ring as the scene fades out.]
