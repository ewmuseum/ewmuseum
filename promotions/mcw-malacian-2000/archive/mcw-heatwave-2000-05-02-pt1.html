Heatwave, Hour One

2 May 2000

Part 1 of 3, posted in order.

[The opening graphics slow ascend in lucidity through to an exterior shot of the MCW Azure Arena, the canopy bearing the logo of "HEATWAVE" illuminated by two huge searchlights mounted on the purpose-built building. Over these scenes comes the voice of David Fernadez:] DF: "Welcome everyone to _HEATWAVE_!!" [Abruptly, each ringpost explodes with a huge flare of a different color: orange, red, yellow and blue! "The Real Slim Shady" by Eminem blasts over the public announcing system adding on to this excitement.] DF: "WHAT A NIGHT IT'LL BE FOR THE MCW!" EF: "Sup folks! I'm Eddy "Shagalot" Fernadez! And over the course of these next few hours you will be staring at my posterior... err... umm... take it DAVE!" DF: "Anyways, folks, over these next few hours you'll be seeing some of the finest wrestling has to offer.. hopefully.. hee hee, scared ya didn't I? No... I didn't? Really, not even a little?" [Fernadez speaks in almost a Dr. Evil voice.] EF: "You're humor is killer, literally." DF: "Folks, anyways, tonight is a major night here in MCW history, tonight is where "Sweet" Jarred Bombay is champion though Chris Masters may leave with the Malacian Title wrapped around his waist." EF: "Don't count on it, Dave. I mean c'mon this is Jarred Bombay we're talking about here do you think he'll lose to some inbred, hick who put on a couple of sparklies? Want me to answer that? NO!" DF: *Sighs.* "You're so naive, anyways folks right now we're about to go to ringside where "Rugged" Sterling Van Rage will be taking on Blake Feldman with Salvador Malacia as the special guest referee, almost a handicap match." EF: "What are you talking about? I'm sure my buddy Sal will be totally impartial!" DF: "Yeah, like the day is short." EF: "What a coincidence the day is short! And it's a matter of opinion, so in a sense I'm right!" DF: "Yeah, yeah.." ################################## MMMM MMMM CCCCCCCCC WW WW MM MMMM MM CCC CC WW WW MM MM MM CCC WW WW WW MM MM CCC CC WW WWWW WW MM MM CCCCCCCCC WWWW WWWW *Single's Match* Special Referee: Salvador Malacia "Rugged" Sterling Van Rage versus. Blake "INSIDIOUS" Feldman ################################## VL: "Folks, this match is scheduled for one fall at a thirty minute time limit.." [Pauses.] VL: "Introducing first, the president of the MCW..." ["Now Is The Time" by Charlie Parker plays over the announcing system, at a melodic beat, and not very loud, as the curtains part and Malacia emerges in a sleeveless tee-shirt, which overlaps a long sleeved black shirt, and wearing the normal black khaki pants and black dressy shoes to match the uniform, the crowd cries out in derision he taunts back flicking them off as he demands Vince to hold the rope as he steps through.] DF: "Folks, that's a perfect example of a power hungry president." VL: "Now introducing, weighing in at 286 lbs and standing at 6'10" here is... "R U G G E D" S T E R L I N G V A N R A G E ! ! ! " [A course of jeers swarms the arena as Rage jogs out the ring, wearing a cotton hooded shirt, with blue jean shorts and black army boots he slides in the ring, standing arm and arm with the almight president himself.] VL: "And now.... introducing, weighing in at 275 lbs. and standing in at 6'7" here is... B L A K E " I N S I D I O U S" F E L D M A N ! ! ! " ["Thong Song" the remix by Sisqo blares over the speakers, as Feldman jogs out to cheers, for once. Small sparklers shoot out from the aisle, reaching only a inch high and quickly dying out, he attempts to ignore it but it only enrages him as he slides into the ring and quickly begins brawling with Rage.] * DING * DING * DING * DF: "Wow... did you see that, Malacia purposely attempted to humiliate Feldman." [Feldman fakes a right hook then teases a uppercut and uses a knee strike to the stomach of Rage as he doubles over Feldman runs across the ropes and executes a swinging neckbreaker.] EF: "He hasn't been cheating yet, now has he?" DF: "Not yet, but you can trust me, he will.." EF: "Rage down, and Feldman covers.." *SLAP* *ONE* [Malacia makes an extremely slow count, Feldman easily had Rage down for the three-count.] DF: "What a slow count!" EF: "It wasn't slow, it was just a bit delayed, yet, that's it!" DF: "Feldman pulls Rage to his feet, and a atomic drop! Sterling Van Rage has shown no opposition thus far in this matchup.. Feldman stomping Rage.." EF: "A sign of a true coward, stomping a man when he's down." DF: "As you put it so boldly before, all is fair in love and wrestling." [Malacia pushes Feldman away, and the two glare at eachother, though the stare down is quickly broken up when Sterling low-blows Feldman, before the cheap shot when down, Malacia turns his head as if somebody had called him.] DF: "LOW BLOW! What a cheap shot!" EF: "He thought somebody had called him not _his_ fault." DF: "Don't give me that! Sterling lifts Feldman to his feet.. whips him into the ropes though countered by Feldman as he put a knee deep into the sternum of Sterling Van Rage, who now falls on both knees, Feldman runs across the ropes and executes a low flying clothesline which connects with Sterling, things not looking good for Sterling Van Rage so far." EF: "He'll come back, I mean look at Sterling and then look at Blake." [Feldman lifts Sterling up sets him up on the top rope and executes a fall away slam, the crowd gives a huge face pop for that one.] DF: "What's Malacia doing? He has a bat and he aims for the head of Feldman, who luckily ducks before being decapitated." EF: "Ahh... he was just doin', ya know a few practice swings, I hear he has a big game Saturday.." DF: "Oh brother, are you really that hard-headed or do you take classes." EF: "I am naturally hard-headed, but you know I take classes to bring out my potential." [Feldman looks almost psychotically at Malacia, the crowd erupts with cheers, he grabs Malacia by the collar of his shirt, and lifts him off the ground.] DF: "Oh boy, here we go!" EF: "Don't touch him! THAT'S YOUR BOSS!" [Rage stands to his feet picks up the bat and swings at Feldman who somehow, some way saw it coming and ducked just in time, though unfortunately Malacia wasn't so lucky. Feldman turns around catching Rage off-gaurd and executes a pull away slam, he covers grabbing Malacia's hand.] *SLAP* *ONE* *SLAP* *TWO* *SLAP* *THREE* DF: "YES! YES!" EF: "HE CHEATED! No fair!" [From the back emerges the "special enforcer" who executes a spinning heel kick. It lands with a thud on the back of his head. He lifts up the bat and sticks it in the gut of Feldman who grimaces in pain. The special enforcer carries off Malacia to the back, to tend his wounds.] [Commecial break.] DF: "We're back folks, and an unusual turn of events, Malacia was womped with that bat thanks to Sterling Van Rage, hee hee.. though it was intended for Blake Feldman, though somehow he ducked just in time." EF: "Don't worry, Dave, those two especially Feldman are gonna get what's coming to them! OH YEAH!" [Cut to back. Malacia is seen a dripping blue icepack resides over most of his face as he reclines back on a leather black chair, the special enforcer standing right next to him.] SM: "ARGH! No fair! Feldman is gonna pay! Yeah!" [The special enforcer whispers something in his ear, Malacia smiles and then nods to the door as the enforcer disappears.] DF: "Where's he going?!" EF: "I dunno but I wished that cameraman would follow him." DF: "Our loving president probably won't let him." [Cut to a wide-angle shot of the Azure Arena. Fade.]
