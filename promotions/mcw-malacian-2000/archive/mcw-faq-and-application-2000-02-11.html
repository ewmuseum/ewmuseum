Application ########################################### PART ONE: a.) The geography of the Malacian Islands: The Malacian Islands is almost all beaches and woodlands, the trees and assorted foliage were brought back from South America since the climates are very similar. This is considered a continent, think of this kind of like another world (i.e. Pokemon, Batman, Book of Lost Tales) it is roughly 425 miles making it the smallest continent, there are though; arenas and housing areas, including market places, malls, theater's and other things scattered all over the Malacian Islands. Salvador, is already a multimillionaire and with the MCW and the Malacian Islands, he hopes to become a multibillionaire. b.) Who runs the MCW? Salvador Malakia born in Los Angelas, California runs it, with the combination of power and dedication, he will set out to make this federation known World-Wide. c.) Origin of the MCW: After a large underwater volcanic eruption of the coast of Mexico formed, and a tycoon; Salvador Malacia, found out, he quickly claimed it in the name of his family, the Malacian Islands. He set-out to get people to come to this small, almost insignificant island, with its past-time being wrestling, he wanted it to be the first place where it was a wrestling federation, of course with the exception, of a few factories, and farmland. PART TWO: a) MCW Cards: 1.) Heatwave Heatwave: This is MCW's only card, which is an institution, it usually holds atleast 4-5 matches, and a few interviews, this is where most titles are defended. 2.) Houseshows MCW holds the occasional houseshow, only when there has been a massive recruiting spree, though if, Salvador, has his wish, and the MCW begins large, and known World-Wide, it will be tryouts for new recruits, to test their limits. 3.) Pay-Per-Views A Pay-Per-View is held once every month, usually towards the end of month, to build up heat, and angles. b.) MCW Titles: 1.) Singles titles MCW Malakian Championship Malakian Championship, this means; you're the greatest wrestler throughout the Malakian Islands, known and whispered amongst the men who compete in this federation/island we call, the Malakian Islands. MCW Malignant Championship In commerence of the psychotic, homicidal, suicidal guys out there, who will go through almost anything for this title, this title is certainly deemed worthy, and anybody who clutches this title, certainly has some guts. MCW Lightweight Championship This is for all the guys who are under 240 pounds. The MCW, loves the high-flying action, this little tikes have to offer, especially since this is based in a place where the style of wrestling flourishes. 2.) Tag Team: ACW TAG TEAM TITLES These belts are held by the greatest duo of two men OR woman in the MCW PART THREE: a.) Rules to abide by: Obviously the MCW based a certain style of RPing, like NET style, which means; no colors, macros, pictures, etc., this makes it much easier for me to send flashes out in packets, instead of individually, I believe this decreases spam mail, and members only have to check their mail boxes every other day, instead of everyday. Second, No attacking of announcers, officials, etc., in role-plays. This really makes no sense to me. Most flashes should take place somewhere on the island, I know this seem's odd, you are probably thinking; 'well, I don't know anything about the Islands, well I do... but not too much.' This is where the fun part comes in, you get to decide about the islands, everybody adds a little charactor to the Malacian Islands, how they want to show it, that's what makes the MCW so innovative, and fun. Finally, no swearing, you know what I am talking about, and if you do happen to decide to use curses in your flashes, than be sure to censor it. b.) Strategies: I believe deeply in strategies, and I think everyone should strat, beside's putting you ahead in your match, since they are considered like flashes, it adds a little quality and depth to your matches, which benefits you, as well as the federation. c.) Angles: Angles, bring heat to you and your charactor, angles are storylines, but you make them, along with some modifying done by me, the fed-head, angles also help you win matches, and a lot of times bring a bit of depth into your charactor.
