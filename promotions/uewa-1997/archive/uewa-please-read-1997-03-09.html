[UEWA] Please Read., 9 March 1997

9 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, I know you guys are probably getting tirred of people asking you to join a new fed. So I'll be nice and ask you to join an up and running one. :) If you like booked feds and are a good roleplayer than you should try out UEWA. If yo are interested I have copied a creation sheet in here and just mail it to [detail withheld] and you will be in the Universal E-Wrestling Association. UEWA Creation sheet (please use one sheet for each wrestler, unless in a tag team, then you will list them side by side) Your name: Do you use IRC(y/n): What is your Nick: Your E-mail address: Wrestler's name: Weight (between 150-700 lbs.): Height (between 5'8" - 8'): Choose 1: 1) Face (fan support) 2) Heel (no fan support) 3) Neutral Stlye of Wrestling (choose from the bottom): a) Aerial (Jushin Liger) b) Mat Wrestling (Steve Regal) c) Lucha Libre (Rey Misterio Jr.) d) Technical (Dean Malenko) c) Brawling (Public Enemy) e) All Around (Sting) d) High Risk Flying (Sabu) f) Technical/Brawling (Steve Austin) g) Power (The Giant) Choose a weekness of your wrestler: a) Endurence b) Speed c) Strength d) Size What can bring your wrestler down? a) Speed b) Strength c) Size Gimmick (i.e. Public Enemy are street fighters): What is his entrance music: Finisher's name + Discription: Manager (OPTIONAL): Manager's discription(include what he looks like and what he does): Background on him(if he has any): In this space, make up an entrence for your wrestler using RA: for the ring announcer and put light changes and all that in between **'s: Tag Team Information -------------------- (please fill out a singles sheet for BOTH of your tag team members) Tag Team name: Members: Finisher's name + discription: Manager + Discription: Theme music: Background on them(if THEY have any): [name withheld] Head of the Universal E-Wrestling Association
