Welcome to the EWA Archives. We are going to ensure that if you are new to the fed you have all the information on past matches. As such we are going to put all cards that are televised here in this Archive Section. Here are all the cards that make the EWA the extreme place it is today. Thanks bunches, and we hope you enjoy. EWA's very first card ever. The memorable HEAVEN AND HELL And then our second card which was even better than the first Wednesday Night Warfare WNW#1 Monday Night War. The Pre PPV Spectacular Monday Night Madness The Wild the Wonderful the Wacky Wednesday Night #3 Bite My shorts and read our card Oh My God it's Wednesday Night #4 I want my two dollars. Wednesday Night #5 Bite My shorts. Memorial Day Madness Read it and Weep. Damn it, everyone sucks on Wednesday Polly want a drunken orgy. Stables... no, not the ones for horses. Now go away, or I shall have to taunt you a second time!! Stop the insanity... Kill your children. Ahhh... another week, another death. Nobody expects the Spanish Inquisition. Oh my God!!! They killed Kenny!!! Hey... pull my finger. I got a surprise for ya!!
