em1214 December 14, 1998 EWA Presents Live From the America West Arena in Phoenix, AZ Richard Riley: "Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to the jam-packed America West Arena in sunny Phoenix, Arizona. We have a huge card headed up by the main event which we see three time and newly crowned EWA Global Hardcore champion Cyris take on former friend and stablemate Pimp Daddy J in a ladder match. Joining me tonight or at least for the time being will be the Executive VP of the EWA and everyone's favorite cripple, Wayne Rothschild." VP Rothschild: "Thanks for the intro there Ricky boy but watch the cripple jokes or maybe there we'll be having two cripples doing commentary here next week." Richard Riley: "Try to catch me, wheelchair boy." VP Rothschild: [quickly grabs Riley by the neck] "I may not be able to run after you but then I again I don't have to chase you. [Pats Richard on the back] Rich, I'm not here to argue with you. I'm here to talk about the great night of action we have planned for the fans." Richard Riley: "And what a night it is. We are set to see for three big title matches and we also get to see the debut of three new wrestlers here tonight. Speaking of debuts, it looks like we are ready for the first match for the night. Any last words on the match, Mr. Rothschild?" VP Rothschild: "This fury looks like he could make an impact in the EWA given some time. He will have a tough test in his first match here against another man that once showed a lot of promise. Unfortunately, I have to leave right now. I rejoin the show later but I have some business to attend to [Rothschild leaves the booth]" Richard Riley: "Alright! As I sit here by myself let's get to the ring for the first match." Myke Fury vs. Mark Johnson Mark Johnson is waiting in the ring when Metallica’s "Disposable Heroes" begins playing. Surprisingly enough, there is some positive reaction for the rookie, Myke Fury, as he comes out from the back. Fury takes a few steps down the aisle and stops, almost in shock that he is hearing cheers for him. Fury is carrying a chair in his left hand, and a VCR in his right hand. He runs down to the ring, and throws the chair at Johnson, who is waiting in the ring. He ducks and the chair and it flies over his head bounces off of the ropes. Fury slides the VCR into the ring, and he shortly follows. The ref calls for the match to begin, now that both guys are in the ring. Johnson grabbed the chair that Fury threw into the ring, and takes a swing at Fury, who has not gotten all of the way up yet. He lands the chair shot and Fury quickly drops back to the mat. Johnson throws the chair out of the ring, and begins to just stomp on Fury. Johnson picks up the EWA newcomer and sets him up for a vertical suplex. He lifts Fury up, and holds him for a moment before dropping him to the mat. Johnson floats over for the pin. 1… not even close!!! Fury kicks out strong, and Johnson tries to pound him down to the mat. Fury this time though, is blocking the punches of Johnson. Fury finally gets to his feet and is punching back on Johnson. He is rocking Johnson back with left after left. Johnson appears to be out on his feet, and Fury throws him off of the ropes and catches him with a wicked spine buster. Fury does not let up and picks up Johnson and whips him hard into the corner. Johnson stumbles backward, with his back facing Fury, and Fury takes a few steps back before taking a running start at Johnson. Fury then hits a spinning heel kick at full speed onto the back of the head of Johnson. He falls right to the mat face first, and Fury quickly gets back to his feet, and lets out a loud war cry. He points over to the VCR that he brought into the ring and positions it in the center of the ring. Fury then grabs the virtually out cold Johnson and scoops him up onto his shoulders, and then delivers Fury’s Hell onto the VCR!!!! The VCR shatters into pieces and you can see that Johnson has been busted wide open because of it. Fury makes the cover. 1… 2… 3!!! We define Extreme (crowd starts chanting "EWA, EWA")! Starting in 1999, not only will the EWA be a major force in US wrestling but also in the world. With new talent flooding the EWA every day, we now number over thirty top stars not including tag teams. We have reached a point where we can no longer provide ample competition to the many great wrestlers that call the EWA their home. To correct this, we have decided to add a new weekly show. Starting next week, Thursday nights will now be home to two great hours for wrestling action as the EWA presents Thursday Night Slaughter. With all this growth comes opportunity and also with growth comes a need to hire additional staff. President Artful and I have decided after long hours of discussion that there is a need to hire another VP. With the death of JD Freeman and VP Derek being away on other important assignments, we have decided to add another member to our executive committee. After long and extensive research and interviews, we were able to find the man that we think will help take the EWA into the new millennium and beyond. I would like to introduce the newest VP of the Extreme Wrestling Association, the one, the only, DIRK RYAN!
