Overview It's been a long time, but the EWA is back, and better than ever! As always, we are commited to entertaining all of our loyal fans (both of them), as well as anyone else who wants to check us out. With that in mind, we've decided to do things a little bit differently this time around. In an effort to keep things entertaining and fun for everyone, all you have to do is read and enjoy. It's that simple. No egos, no hard feelings, no deadlines, just cheep entertainment.
+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 2. Who is in charge of the EWA? The EWA is headed by three people, Johnny Sledge, Art Blake, and Wayne
Rothschild. Art has held his portion of ownership of the EWA all this time but hasn't had majority control to do anything with the fed. Sledge thought he had bought a portion of Wayne's ownership, but it turned out that Arron Michaels screwed both Johnny Sledge and Wayne
Rothschild and sold it away to some Dutch multi-national corporation who "thought it would be really fun" to own an American wrestling organization, so he could pay off his gambling debts.
Wayne decided to sue the corporation, but since he used most of his money to originally buy shares from Aaron Michael, he needed help. That's where Johnny Sledge and his cheap ways came in handy. He offered his friend help with the lawsuit for a split in the remaining two-thirds of the company. The company decided to settle out of court (they saw a nice Austrian Chocolate Factory that was going under) and simply sold the rest of the EWA to the Sledge and
Rothschild, thus making Sledge, Blake, and Rothschild equal partners in the newest incarnation of the EWA.
+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 3. How do I win matches? Everyone wins! That's the beauty of it all. You, as a reader, do not have to do anything. We'll be doing all of the work so you can sit back and enjoy the story. You won't have to spend hours each day reading Role Plays, nor will you have to spend any time writing them. You can read our weekly shows, if you have the time. It's a win/win situation.
+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 4. Where is the home base of the EWA? In the past the EWA was based out of Rochester, NY. It will stay that way, but expect tours across the country, and maybe all over the world.
+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 5. How many wrestlers can I control? To be perfectly honest, the only people that will control anything, are the people in charge. They have the final say as to what appears in the weekly shows. We are, however, open to suggestions about types of wrestlers you would like to see in the EWA and storylines. Keep in mind that just because you submit an idea, does not mean it will be used. We reserve the right to make changes to any suggestions.
+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 6. What titles are in the EWA? For now there will only be 3 Titles in the EWA. They are the Global,
Television, and Tag Team Championships. More belts can be added if we get more people than we expect.
+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 7. When are the shows? There will only be one show each week: - Extreme Measures is the EWA's big Friday night extraviganza. Look for there to be a lot of action on this show. - There will also be one Pay Per View event every month. They will probably on Sundays. Dates and times will be announced as the events are planned. +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 8. How do I submit a wrestler?
