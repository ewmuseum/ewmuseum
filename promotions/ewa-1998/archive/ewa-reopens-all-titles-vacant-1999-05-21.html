EWA Reopens — All Titles Vacant

21 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The EWA recently opened it's doors again after a three month hiatus. We will be having our first card Monday, May 24th.
Just to give you a little bit of information about our fed, we are a booked fed that only uses original wrestlers and roleplays are done through e-mail. We employ a mixture of traditional and hardcore wrestling and we have competition for both the experienced veteran as well as the rookie.
The EWA opened in May of 1998 and operates for about 9-10 months until it had to be closed temporarily. Our roster was up to over 50 members and we had a great reputation for quality cards.
As of now, we have a roster of approximately 20 members including about 5 cruiserweights. We also have two seperate tag teams. We are looking for wrestlers (heavyweight, cruiser or tag) that will be consistant and make an impact on our fed. All our titles are currently vacant which presents a great opportunity for any wrestler.
