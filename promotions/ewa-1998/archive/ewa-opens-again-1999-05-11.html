EWA Opens Again After a Three-Month Hiatus

11 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWFers, The Extreme Wrestling Association is once again open for business. The EWA operated very successfully for close to a year before becoming too much for one man to handle. Now after a three month layoff the fed is back.
We are looking for committed e-fedders that are looking for some new competition. I expect to have a good number of the original members back however I would like to get some "new blood" in as well. It really doesn't matter what level of experience you are at currently. Just like it's first run through, the EWA has competition for all levels of ability. The only thing I am looking for is for people who will join, stick around for a while and most importantly RP consistantly.
To tell you a little about the fed, it is a booked fed that uses only original wrestlers. Contrary to the name, the fed is not strictly an all hardcore fed.
It will basically be somewhat simliar to the WWF in that there will be a seperate Hardcore title and that extreme matches will be reserved for settling feuds, PPV, etc. They will not be the norm although there will be plenty of hardcore action for people who like that stuff.
Below is a list of the titles that will be in the EWA: Global Heavyweight Title - The EWA's world title. This is for the best of the best. Must be defended at least once a month (always at PPVs) or whenever the exec committee deems it necessary.
North American Title - The #2 title in the EWA. Comparable to the Intercontinental or US titles. The NA champ is traditionally the #1 contender to the Global title. Defended at least twice a month or whenever the exec committee deems it necessary.
TV Title - This could also be called the Ironman title since the Champion will have to defend the belt at least once a week.
Hardcore Title* - This is for the toughest of the tough. Matches will always be decided with hardcore stipulations as well as three way and four way matches. Defended typically twice a month
Cruiserweight Title* - Only wrestlers weighing under 230 lbs will be eligilbe for this title. The champ is typically ranked somewhere between the TV and
North American champion.
Global Tag Team Titles* - As the name implies, this is only for tag Teams. The title is defended at least twice a month and is somewhere around the North
American title in prestige.
