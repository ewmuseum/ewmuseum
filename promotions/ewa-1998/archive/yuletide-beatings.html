Whatever format we choose, Tommy Grayson will be a part of it. Now be a good boy and hand the belt over." Grayson turns around. His face is red and you can see his fists begin to clunch at his sides. VP Rothschild: "Tommy! Give me the belt NOW!" Tommy Grayson: "You want the belt? Here it is!" Grayson spins around and nails Rothschild in the side of the face with the belt. Wayne is knocked out of his chair and Grayson begins to stomp on his head. Cyris, who was at the end of the ramp turns around and starts to come back to the ring. Grayson continues to stomp of the VP. Cyris just stands there with a big smile on his face. Grayson sees Cyris standing there and turns towards him. Cyris motions to Grayson almost as if telling him to continue. Grayson turns back towards Rothschild who is trying to drag himself back into his wheelchair. As soon as Grayson has turned, Cyris grabs him and pulls him off of Rothschild. Grayson lets out a scream of rage and runs at Cyris who quickly back drops Grayson out of the ring. Grayson starts to climb back into the ring but Cyris is waiting for him. Grayson decides not to go into the ring and starts to head back to the lockers to the loud boos from the fans. Cyris is standing over Rothschild. He leans down and surprisingly picks up Rothschild and places him in his wheelchair. Rothschild stared coldly at Cyris but suddenly Cyris holds out his hand for Wayne to shake. After a short moment, Wayne extends his hand and the two men shake. Cyris grabs a mic. Cyris: WAYNE WAS D.I.. BEFORE YOU TOMMY and for that YOU CROSSED THE LINE, cause even though he may have been against D.I., he WAS still D.I.!!!! You were never D.I. material and you'll never be the Global champion. Wayne and Cyris shake hands one more time as the credits begin to roll.
