Thursday Night Slaughter, 21 January 1999

1999-01-21
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
tns012199 1/21/1999 EWA Presents Taped at the Tacoma Dome In Tacoma, WA Peter Damien vs. Ray Anthony The match started off slowly with neither man looking to hook up immediately. Anthony hit an arm drag followed by a dropkick and Damien decided to take a break on the out side of the ring. Anthony attempted a suicide dive but Damien anticipated it and Anthony make contact with the ring railing. After some extra-curricular activity by Damien, the match returned to the ring. Damien hit a clothesline, then slowed the match down by locking on an arm bar and working over Anthony's shoulder. Anthony fought back with elbows to Damien's midsection and then changed the momentum of the match with a spine buster. Anthony looked to end the match his finisher, the Raybomb but Damien rolled out of the lift for a sunset flip and near pin. Damien nailed a belly-to belly suplex and then quickly scaled the ropes for a missile dropkick. Damien then locked on Fade to Black but before Anthony would submit, Da Playa rushed the ring, leading to a DQ. DP rocked Damien with a chair shot to the back and then the DP Drop before spitting on Damien and leaving the ring. Winner: Peter Damien via DQ at 6:42 "Warlord" John Davis vs. X X attacked "Warlord" before the bell and nailed him with repeated blows to the head and upper chest area. X attempted to keep Davis in the corner but he fought back with reverse knife-edge chops and then a short clothesline. The two huge men locked up in a test of strength. X gained the upper hand initially due to his slight height advantage but the "Warlord's" superior strength begun to win out and the momentum shifted. Davis applied the pressure but X attempted an Irish whip, which was reversed and let to a big boot to the face from Davis. "Warlord" worked over X's back and then locked on a camel clutch. X was able to reach the ropes, causing a break. X fought back with a few power moves but ultimately the "Warlord" was too much for him. After a devastating clothesline and powerbomb, the match was over when X submitted to the Total Domination. Winner: "Warlord" John Davis via Total Domination in 3:56 Bunghole Boys vs. Lasting Impression The match started out with Hayes and Pop Ali in the ring. Hayes tried to keep the match slow but Ali was too fast for his opponent. Ali kept the match moving by bounding off the ropes and nailing dropkicks and flying head scissors to keep the bigger man off his feet. Ali tagged in his partner Torso Man, who went to work on Hayes with more power based moves. Torso hit a clothesline followed by a suplex and then quickly tagged back in Ali. Ali entered the ring via the top rope and nearly scored a pin with a frog splash. James Head ran into the ring to break up the count but Torso Man ran in and rocked Head with a flying back elbow. The Bunghole Boys worked their men into opposite corners and then brought then out with a pair of bulldogs. Both men begun to work over Head and then knocked him out of the ring with a double clothesline. Ali whipped Hayes into the ropes and Torso Man nailed a powerslam. The fans began to cheer as the Bunghole Boys hit the Fart knocker Double Inverted Powerbomb for the win. Winners: The Bunghole Boys via The Fart Knocker Double Inverted Powerbomb at 5:15 Merlin the "g-Crusher" vs. John Tang g-Crusher started off the match strong with several arm drags and then a drop kick that sent Tang to the floor. G-Crusher nailed a baseball slide as Tang attempted to re-enter the ring and then jumped up on the apron for a centon splash. G-Crusher continued to work over Tang on the outside, smasking his opponent several times into the ring steps. The match returned to the ring and Tang gained some momentary momentum with a poke to the eye. Tang went on the offensive with several kicks and chops to g-Crusher. Tang worked g-Crusher into a corner and went to whip him into the opposite corner but g-Crusher reversed it and then nailed a belly-to-back suplex for a near pin. The match went g-Crusher's way as he used the ropes to his advantage and nailed several clotheslines followed by a flying head scissors. G-Crusher started to work over the back of Tang. After a Boston crab, g-Crusher went for another Irish whip but Tang caught g-Crusher unaware and nailed a quick DDT. Tang looked to try to end the match with the Tangy Sensation but g-Crusher was only playing possum. G-crusher rolled out of the way and quickly applied the g-Crusher. Tang immediately tapped out to give the win to g-Crusher. Winner: Merlin the "g-Crusher" via the g-Crusher at 7:22 "Superstar" Jason Moore vs. "Extremist" JT Welsh Welsh tried attacked Moore before the bell but Moore was prepared nailed a drop toe fold that caused Welsh to hit the turnbuckle face first. Moore slammed Welsh's head into the top turnbuckle several times before nailing a Russian leg sweep. Moore whipped his opponent into the ropes and nailed a very accurate drop kick that dropped Welsh. Welsh attempted to fight back but Moore would not let up. He stayed moving and rocked Welsh with a flying back elbow. Welsh thumped Moore in the eye and attempted an Irish Whip but Moore reversed it and nailed a devastating T-bone suplex. Moore planted Welsh on the top turnbuckle and quickly dispatched his opponent with the Kansas Jam for a very impressive debut victory. Winner: "Superstar" Jason Moore via the Kansas Jam in 2:45 Daddy Desire vs. Shawn Arrows Daddy Desire played to the women at the start of the match. Arrows wanted to lock up immediately but Desire was more concerned with giving the ladies in the arena a little pump and grind exhibition. Arrows finally got Desire's attention when he grabbed Desire for a belly to back suplex that almost ended the match. Arrows pounded on Desire with boots to the back. After a scoop slam, Arrows slowed the match down with a rear chin lock. Desire fought his way back to his feet but got caught with a knee off of an Irish whip. Desire quickly left the ring but Arrows followed right behind. Arrows slammed Daddy Desire's head into the ring railing. The match changed when Arrows attempted to whip Desire into the ring post but Daddy reversed it and then nailed an inverted DDT as Arrows staggered backward. Desire rolled his opponent and then climbed the ropes to deliver the Love Tap and a win. As soon as the match ended, Matt Allen ran down to the ring and nailed a celebrating Daddy Desire with a chair. He then picked up Desire for his patented piledriver onto the chair. Allen then fled the ring as Daddy Desire's brother "The Real Deal" Kenneth Morlock stormed the ring with a chair of his own. Winner: Daddy Desire via the Lover Tap at 4:26 "Super Duper" Gary Cooper vs. Joker It was fan participation night as fans threw styrafoam rocks into the ring. Joker began to mock Cooper but the joke was on him as Cooper smashed one of the styrafoam rocks in Jokers face. Cooper started to nail kicks to Jokers back and then floored him with a tilt-a-whirl side salto slam off of an Irish whip. The match continued to go downhill for Joker as Cooper threw him around the ring. The audience enjoyed the match thoroughly as Cooper taunted his opponent and made fun of him throughout the match. After a surprise huricanranna from Joker that almost cost him the match, Cooper got a little more serious. Cooper nailed a back drop followed by a gutwrench power bomb on still winded Joker. Joker fought back and hit a spring board bulldog. Joker made the critical mistake when he went up to the top rope. Cooper recovered, climbed up after Joker and then nailed the Super Duper Slam for the win. Winner: "Super Duper" Gary Cooper at 6:25 Westside Walavan vs. "Untouchable" Tommy Lane Walavan came into the match looking very unhappy. He used his size advantage to dominate his smaller opponent. Lane attempted to fight back with a few chops but Walavan ended that pretty quickly with a boot to the face. Walavan then lifted up his opponent for a monstrous press slam. After dropping several elbows onto Lane, Walavan blasted his opponent with a clothesline. Walavan picked up his opponent for a grueling bear hug and then slammed the man to the mat when he refused to submit. Walavan then screamed out to the fans, then gave Lane a merciful end to his beating by nailing the Westside Bomb for a pin. Winner: Westside Walavan via Westside Bomb at 1: 49 Main Event Simply Irresistible vs. Elite From the Street The match started out with "Pretty Boy " Paul Stevens and Da Playa. DP attempted to keep the match fast but Stevens kept the place slow with a headlock. DP fought off the hold and nailed a dropkick to the bigger man. Stevens locked up again but this time lifted his opponent for a huge press slam. Stevens dragged DP over to his corner to tag in his partner "Stunning" Simon Spencer. Spencer kept DP in his corner and Simply Irresistible used double teaming when Walker tried to interfere. Spencer began to work over DP's back and one point nailing a backbreaker. Spencer then locked on a camel clutch but DP was able to reach the ropes. Spencer continued to keep the match slow. He locked on a bear hug but DP countered with a head clap and then a flying head scissors to take the bigger man down. Spencer was knocked down again when DP nailed an enziguri. DP then tagged in his partner, "Gangsta" Jimmy Walker. Walker ran in and nailed a clothesline to Spencer and then one to Stevens, who had run into the ring. DP came back into the ring and started duking it out with Spencer. Walker began to lay the boots to Stevens in one corner as DP nailed Spencer with chops to the chest in the other. Walker made the mistake of whipping his man into the opposite corner. Stevens nailed DP with a splash but also nailed his partner. Both DP and Spencer go down and out of the ring and Walker nailed a bulldog to Stevens for a near pin. Walker next attempted a clothesline but Stevens ducked under and nailed a clothesline of his own. DP and Spencer continued to fight on the outside but DP used his speed to cause Spencer to nail the ring post. In the ring, Stevens had Walker set for a suplex but DP quickly scaled the tope rope and nailed a missile dropkick that knocked Stevens down and Walker landing on top of him and a pin. As soon as the match ended, Death Row stormed the ring and began to work over the two members of Elite from the Street. Drifter and Tyrant worked over Da Playa as VD and Dawgbone nailed Walker. When all was said and done, Death Row walked away, leaving the DP and Walker laying in the wring beaten. Winners: Elite From the Street at 9:36 [image: setstats]
