em1230 12/30/98 EWA Presents Live from the Arrowhead Pond in Anaheim, CA Richard Riley: "Good evening everyone. Welcome to Anaheim, California and the home of the Mighty Ducks of the NHL. We've got a great show as always with three big title matches. With me as always is the Executive Vice President of the EWA, Wayne Rothschild." Wayne Rothschild: "Thank you, Richard. In addition to the three big title matches we also have the first round of the tag team title tournament. It's possible that one of these teams tonight will walk out of the ring at Yuletide Beatings with some gold strapped around their waist." Richard Riley: "Speaking of beatings, how are you feeling? You took one hell of a beating at the end of last week's Extreme Measures!" Wayne Rothschild: "I'm doing ok. I had a bit of a concussion. Grayson did nail me pretty good. I guess he showed everyone what a big man he is by attacking a man confined to a wheelchair. Grayson and I may never have been friends but I always respected him as a wrestler but after what he pulled last week, I've lost all respect for the man." Richard Riley: "Very understandable and we have the whole show to discuss this but right now let's get to the ring for out first match."
