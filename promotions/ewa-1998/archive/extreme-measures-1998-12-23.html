em1223 December 23, 1998 EWA Presents Live from L.A. Forum in Los Angeles, CA The camera opens to the rear entrance of the L.A. Forum. We see Travis Keaton, John Tang, and Xavier walking into the arena with duffle bags in hand. Keaton's leg is in a cast from what appears to be a broken leg. Suffered during a recent match. The three men are joking around, laughing and all appear to in a good mood. The men are about to enter the building when Cyris and Irish Rebel appear in front of them. The men turn the other way and then the two masked men standing behind them. The men turn back to Cyris , who now has a bat in his hand. WHACK!!!!!! Cyris slams the bat right across Keaton's broken leg. Keaton falls in agony and is rolling around holding the damaged appendage. Cyris continues to pummel on Keaton's leg as the three other members of DI beat on Xavier and John Tang. Cyris starts to rip the cast apart and throw pieces of it in Keaton's face. The menbers of DI continue to beat on the three now defenseless men. Here comes SECURITY! All 4 members of D.I. hastily leave the scene as EMT's rush to tend to the injured men and security cordon off the area. Richard Riley: "My God! What a way to open up the program. I think Cyris is trying to make DI's presence felt here tonight." Wayne: "It looks that way. DI has been too quiet as of late. I guess they are back to there old, dirty tricks once again. They really disgust me!" Richard Riley: "Pardon me for being so frank but have you taken too many chair shots to the head? You used to be a member of DI and if memory serves me correctly, you were probably the most vicious, sadistic member of the group. That was only like three months ago." Wayne: [looks a little ashamed] "You are absolutely right. I was a vicious and terrible man but that was in the past. I'm a VP now and I'm here to do what is best for the organization and in my mind that is ridding the EWA of Devastation, Inc. once and for all." Richard Riley: "How do you plan on doing that? It's not like you can run down to the ring with a chair and nail Cyris." Wayne: "Ever hear the phrase 'The pen is mightier then the sword!'?" But enough about that. We have a show to start. I think we are almost ready for the first match of the evening. Up and coming Myke Fury takes on the veteran Dawgbone. Any thoughts on the match up?" Richard Riley: "Fury looked pretty impressive in his debut match here but he will have a good challenge from Dawgbone who is coming off of a big win last week. Let's head down to the ring for this opening match!" Dawgbone vs. Myke Fury Metallica’s "Disposable Heroes" begins playing and out comes Myke Fury. He is carrying a baseball bat with him as he sprints down to the ring. Fury gets to the ring, and hops up on the ring apron and climbs over the ropes to get into the ring. He first stops on the top rope and lets out a battle cry before hopping into the ring. The music fades into "Murder was the Case" by Snoop Doggy Dog and Fury’s opponent, Dawgbone, comes down to the ring. He is carrying a dog chain with him as he goes down to the ring. He slides into the ring and Fury takes a swing at DB. DB rolls out of the way just dodging the quick falling bat. He gets up and swings the bat at Fury and Fury block it with the bat. The chain wraps around the bat and DB pulls the chain back and the bat goes with it. The bat launches out into the aisle and DB lets go of the chain as well and it wraps around the to rope. The ref signals for the match to begin, and the two of them quickly begin brawling. They throw lefts and rights in the center of the ring and eventually DB begin to gain the advantage. He starts punching Fury into the corner, and quickly begins pounding on him with elbows and kicks. Fury sinks into the corner but DB does not let up. DB just keeps on kicking Fury in the chest for a bit longer, before he figures out his next plan of attack. He slides out of the ring, and grabs Fury’s leg and spins him around. DB tries to crotch Fury on the corner, but Fury reverses it and pulls DB into the pole face first. DB falls to the floor and Fury gets to his feet. He goes over to the other side of the ring and calls for a chair. One is thrown up to and he goes back over to the side where DB is. He sets up the chair between the bottom rope and the ring apron, and backs up. As DB gets up Fury runs toward him and baseball slides the chair into the face of DB. Fury exits the ring and goes over to the beginning of the aisle and grabs his bat that he brought to the ring. He goes over to where DB is, and as he gets up again and he takes a wild swing at DB. DB ducks out of the way and saves himself again from a homerun swing from Fury. The bat shatters off of the turnbuckle pole and DB lunges at Fury tackling him to the floor. Fury hits DB in the top of the head with the butt of the bat until he lets go. Fury picks up DB and rolls him into the ring. Fury then grabs the chair that he used in DB and climbs the ropes. DB realizes that his chain is still on the top tope and he reaches up and grabs it. It shakes the ropes and Fury gets crotched on the top rope. The chair falls into the ring, and Fury shortly follows. DB makes a quick cover and the ref dives into position to make the count. 1… 2… no!!! Fury gets his foot on the bottom rope, and the ref taps DB on the shoulder and is about to tell him that it was only a two count. DB jumped up before the ref could tell him, and when the ref tells him he becomes enraged over this and starts screaming at the ref. This gives Fury the chance to shake some of the cobwebs out and he rolls DB up and the ref goes to make check for the pin. The ref doesn’t notice Fury has a handful of tights as he makes the count. 1… 2… And a kickout by Dawgbone!!! The two get back to their feet and are trading punches again. The two give this up and the now bounce off of the ropes and Fury drops down and DB jumps over him. They do this a few times before Fury finally smartens up and clotheslines an oncoming DB. This stuns him long enough for Fury to throw him chest first into the corner. Fury turns the stunned DB around and hoists him up on his shoulders and drops him in the Fury’s Hell. He falls on top of DB and the ref makes the count. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner: Myke Fury via Fury’s Hell Richard Riley: "Fury comes away with a big win. This should really give his career a big push. Dawgbone wrestled a good tough match but in the end he couldn't match the power of young Myke Fury. Wayne: "You know, Dawgbone is one of those guys who wrestles hard week in and week out and some weeks he comes away with the win and other times he loses.
