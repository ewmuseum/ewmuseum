WrestlerName: Wrestler Name: Supernova Real Name: Arthur Dodge Manager: Bobo the Anti-Social Chimp Height: 6'7" Weight: 275 lbs Hometown: Vancouver, BC Overall Appearance: Nova is a tall, well built (though not overly so) young man (mid 20's). His attire generally consists of grey cargo pants hastilly tucked into a pair of green Doc Marten's. He also wears a maroon coloured Oxford style shirt made of a durable 100% cotten flanel over a plain white t-shirt. His hair is of a forest green colour that comes very close to matching the colour of his boots. He is well known for getting inside the heads of his opponents with his antics outside of the ring, relentlessly persuing anyone who has the misfortune of getting on his bad side. Despite his behavior outside of the ring, he is one of the most fair, and honorable wrestlers in the business, and is not known for outright cheating. He will stretch the rules as far as he can without actually breaking them. Entrance Music: "Codine, Glue and You" by Chemlab Titles Held: None worth mentioning Finishing Maneuver: Novabomb (Top Rope Powerbomb) as well as various leg submission holds Moves he likes using: 1. German Suplex 2. Verticle Suplex 3. Belly-toBelly Suplex 4. Russian Leg Sweep. 5. Slingshot Suplex. 6. Clothesline 7. Roundhouse Kick. 8. Shoulder Block 9. Headbut 10. Boot to the head 11. Elbow to the Nose 12. Drop Toe Hold 13. DDT Type of wrestler: Technical/Power with a little Aerial Special Quote: "Pogue mahone." and/or "Toten uber alles." Other Information: Nova has wrestled in several feds, though has never realy been interested in winning titles. He gets far more satisfaction out of the competition involved. Nova's main goal in life is to entertain. If he wins a title in the process, that's fine, if not, oh well. [image: setstats]
