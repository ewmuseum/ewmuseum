UWA Spotlight 7/18/97 http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4913/main.html ======================================================= (The UWA theme music http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4913/ACDCSHK.MID begins to play as clips of UWA superstars are shown on the screen.) - Wrestlingman stuffing Crimson Sin and Crimson Crystal into a casket - The Preacher attacking Wrestlingman after his match and then joining the DIA, stunning the crowd. - Lord Zero delivering the Soulcrusher on Taime Downe from the top rope - Bad Habbit hitting Chad 'the specimen' Blake in the knee with a bat - Big Butch Thorton executing the Thorton Slam on a UWA official - President T.N.T. suspending Big Butch Thornton and the rest of the Fearsome Foursome. - A patrol of police officers standing at ringside to keep anyone from interferring in the main event at Patriot games between Suicide and Lord Zero - The UWA logo . ####### ####### ####### ##### ####### ####### ####### ####### ####### ####### ####### # ######### ####### ####### ####### ### ##### ##### ####### ####### ####### ##### ##### ##### ####### ####### ####### ####### ##### ##### ####### ####### ####### ######### ################# ######## ######## ####################################### ######## ######## ########### ########### ####### ################### ######### ######### ####### ################# ####### ####### ####### ############### ##### ##### ####### Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome the president of the UWA, T.N.T (POP!) and his spotlight cohost, Rex Roseman.(Boos!) (The studio audience cheer for T.N.T. and boo Rex Roseman as they approach the Spotlight set. The set itself is a wrestling ring with ropes surrounding three sides leaving an opening in the front. There are four, black leather chairs arranged in a semi-circle as T.N.T. and Rex Roseman take their seats on the two chairs farthest to the right. T.N.T. is wearing a gray suit with the UWA logo attached just below his left shoulder. His above seven foot height towers over Rex and his long brown hair is tied back neatly into a pony tail. Rex Roseman is wearing jeans and an official UWA "I love Rex Roseman t-shirt." His black hair is spiked with a streak of gray on the side.) T.N.T: Thank you for that great ovation and as always, welcome to UWA Spotlight. In case some of you havn't heard, the UWA has been on hiatus for the last two weeks because our old network refused to keep UWA Saturday Night's Main Event in a saturday time slot. Well, another network jumped at the chance to have the UWA in their programming lineup so here we are. Rex Roseman: What a bunch of morons they have at the old network, I just know that we will totally crush them in the ratings next saturday and I hope that some people over there will be getting pink slips very soon. Didn't they know what kind of ratings I pulled in. T.N.T: If it wasn't for your little contract stunt that you pulled with the old UWA president, I would be handing you a pink slip this very moment, but I am afraid that we will have to put up with you for quite some time now. But we have more important things to talk about then you Rex. Comming up later on in the program, Mike Miller has a huge announcement to make concering a UWA superstar in his News and Rumors section. Also, Doug Jones will conduct an interview with a UWA champion, just stay tuned to find out who it will be. But first, we will be reviewing the matches scheduled for our first return card which is next saturday, July 26th. During that card, a lot of new UWA wrestlers who have come to where the real men wrestle will be making their debuts. The first match we will be looking at is the Fallen Angel versus Johnny Sharp. Lets see what each of them have to say. (T.N.T. and Rex Roseman both turn to the huge video screen to their right.) ########################################################################## PROMO: The Fallen Angel v. Johnny Sharp ########################################################################## ############################# The Fallen Angel ############################# (The scene is inside an abandoned church. Candles are lighted around an alter. A figure is scene walking to the alter. He turns around and reveals the face of The Fallen Angel.) The Fallen Angel: Let me welcome myself to the UWA. I am currently co-European Tag Team champion in the ESWP. As you see I am not new to wrestling and I am also not knew to beating chumps like you down. I will teach you the meanings of grace and salvation and when I am through you will worship me... the one true God of wrestling. FA: The first soul that will learn is Johnny Sharp. You better hope that you are going to be sharp, because you will have to be at the top of your game to beat me. Are you ready to walk into the shadows of the valley of death? FA: The reign of The Fallen Angel in the UWA has begun and no one is safe. (Lightning flashes in the background and The Fallen Angel disappears.) ######################## Johnny Sharp ######################## (scene opens with the "Suplex Machine" Johnny Sharp inside a wrestling ring, by him self, in an empty arena) "Suplex Machine" Johnny Sharp: Well, everyone will see the debut of Johnny Sharp on the 26th at Satuday Night's Main Event, and it seems that I will be facing the Fallen Angel on that night. I want to be at the top of the UWA, so I'm going to wrestle my butt off and pin the Fallen Angel right in the middle of the ring. I'm not even going to give him a chance, because the only thing that's going to happen inside the ring on that night is me Suplexing him all over damn ring! I don't care if I have to be Suplexing him trough tables or just smacking steel chairs up against his head, as long as I can show the UWA what I got. Once I put on the Sharper Image on the Fallen Angel right in the middle of the ring, I will come out of that match with the Johnny Sharp's first win in the UWA out of many more other win's to come! ############################################################################ END PROMO ############################################################################ Rex Roseman: Great, The Fallen Angel, just what we need, another wierdo freak who thinks he can wrestle. T.N.T: If that's the case then the both of you are two peas in a pod. In both cases. But I think that I will hold off on any comments until I get a chance to see the both of them inside the ring. But I have seen the wrestlers in our next match Scorp and Malibu Mike in competition. Lets see what the both of the had to say. ############################################################################ PROMO: Scorp v. Malibu Mike ############################################################################ ############################## Malibu Mike ############################## (Malibu sits in his new corvette. He is listening to Third Eye Blind. He turns to the camera and smiles) Malibu: Nice to see another camera guy AGAIN. Why do you guys keep bugging me, you wanna keep up with the real deal Malibu Mike, don't you. Well this Saturday I will be having the first of 3 matches this weekend. Scorp, you might think I need luck but I don't, I have more skill than you have streaks in your undi's. I don't know much about you Scorp, but I consider that your a good wrestler because you are fourth ranked in the UWA and that has got to be a great accomplishment. Well come Saturday I will give it my all and pull out on top and I don't know where that will put me, in 4th ranked our most likely one of the top 10 because I am the REAL DEAL and everybody knows that. (He turns the music down and starts to speak again) Malibu: Tomorrow I will start my big workout for my three matches, my mind is on the one against Suicide, but it looks like you will be a great opponent to deal with. We will see Saturday Night who is the better of the two you, Scorp or the REAL DEAL Malibu Mike. Scorp, you just stay with your family in Ireland in the cardboard box and I will stay in the United States making million dollar contracts and living in my Mansion. See you Saturday. (Fades out as Malibu turns the Music back up) ######################### Scorp ######################### [ Scene opens with Scorp at McDonald's and he is eating a triple cheeseburger. He also has a sprite in his right hand/ He is wearing a black suit and sitting on leather seats in his 1997 Bronco. He see's alot of people staring at him. Then he thinks about the man they call Malibu Mike. He thens notice's that a cameraman is coming his way. He drops the triple cheeseburger. Then he cleans up. Then the cameraman comes towards him. Scorp speaks!! ] Scorp: Damn! You scared the crap out of me. What are you doing here? Cameraman: I am from the UWA and is here to talk about your upcoming match against the man they call Malibu Mike. They sent me to do a interview with you. Scorp: Why didn't you contact my agent? He could have told you how to get in touch with me. He didn't contact me. Cameraman: Look do you want to shoot the interview or not. Scorp: Okay!! But let's make it good. Cameraman: Let are men set it up. You can change clothes in you need. Scorp: I will be back. I am going in to change. ( Scorp walks into McDonald's! He goes to the bathroom and spends a long time in there. Then when he is on his way out the camera zooms up on him. ) Scorp: Malibu Mike nice to see you again. On the 26th me and you are going to lock it up. You are the sixth rankings and you expect to fight a man with such quality's. You better get your mind straight. You haven't even fought in one match yet. I don't care if you held a title in UWA. Well it isn't going to have nothing to do with are upcoming match. Here's the bottom line. When me and you lock it up. There's is going to be no stopping us to. You might think well he drop in the rankings so that can only mean worse. But when I step in that ring. It's all business my friend and pleasure. ( Scorp walks towards his Bronco as his fans are smiling as they see his sexy body. Scorp walks toward his Bronco and his hair blowing in the wind. ) Scorp: Mike you and your tramp better get the steppin. Because I am going all the way to the top. I am a cruiserweight wrestler. But that doesn't mean I can't hold the world title. Because everyone is going to get that golden chance. And when I do the hold world will know. Espically the new generation of Universal Wrestling Alliance. I can't not wait. Time is ticking and we are 240 hours away from me beating the crap out of you. UWA this was some beauitful in the making. And Malibu Mike you are not going to mess it up. ( Scorp gets into his jeep and looks at the camera. ) Scorp: Malibu Mike you and your bitch better come to the ring prepared. Because if you don't I will. To all my little Scorp's have a great week and weekend. And I will see you sooner than you think. Let's get it on.
