UWA News - 10/29/97 - from [detail withheld] Everyone! Be sure to check out WWF RAW next week for DAN KIRK 3:16!!!! We will be holding a contest and the winner will recieve the chance to create another wrestler. What you need to do is create logos for Friday Night Fights and/or Sunday Spotlight. (They need to be about the same size as the Tuesday Night WAR logo that appears at the beginning of the card) DPK will be sending everything out Mondays through Thursdays. I am getting Todd to help me out on Friday through Sunday. Thanks a lot. Everyone! Include your wrestler's name and who your flash is about in your flashes! If you want to write a card or a talk show, mail me and we'll get something started. You can write one of my cards or you can start you're own. All matches must be okayed by me. I will give you the results and gameplans that I want put to use in the cards.
