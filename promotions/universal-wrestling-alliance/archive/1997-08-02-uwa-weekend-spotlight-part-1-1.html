T.N.T: What makes you think Wrestlingman will not pull off another upset and win the cruiserweight title. Rex Roseman: It's a matter of common sense, which obviously is something that you don't have much of. You see, Wrestlingman beat Suicide in a non title match. His match tommorrow on the Main Event is for the belt. "The specimen" has a lot more to lose then Suicide did, therefore he will dedicate himself totally towards staying champion. Besides, I think you've forgetting the fact that "the specimen" has already defeated Wrestlingman. T.N.T: All the more to believe that Wrestlingman will be the victor. Since he as wrestled Blake before, he has come a long way, and if Blake isn't prepared, we may very well have a new cruiserweight champiom. ====================================================================== PROMO: Chad "the specimen" Blake(cc) v. Wrestlingman ====================================================================== Rex Roseman: Well, I think that "the specimen" would beg to differ. He came by UWA studios today and droped off a tape that I found very entertaining. Let's have a look. T.N.T: Tape? What tape? Tonight, Chad welcomes the President of the UWA....TNT!!! Also featured are all FOUR members of the FEARSOME FOURSOME, and also comedian JIMMIE WALKER!!!!! Now, your host, whose political inspirations are .....mmm, mmmm, feeling good.... the UWA CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION, CHAD "THE SPECIMEN" BLAKE!!!!!!!! (MUSIC: "This is How We Do It" by Montell Jordan) (Canned applause, as the TV audience discovers this is being filmed in Chad's apacious Victorian style living room and there is no audience) CHAD BLAKE: Thank you, thank you, all you lovely people! Well, I had a bunch of jokes ready to go tonight, ladies and gentlemen, but unfortunately Wrestlingman and Ashley couldn't be here tonight. So instead let's introduce my co-host, the always lovely ERIN!!!!! (Erin walks onto sight of the camera, and she and Chad walk to a desk in the mkiddle of the room where the camera follows.) Let's proceed to my top 10 list! Tonight's top 10 list eminates from the home office here in my mansion, and the subject is: The Top Ten Newest Stables of the UWA!!! Should be a very informative list, eh, Erin? ERIN: You said it, Chad!!!! CHAD BLAKE: All-rightey, then.... 10. Kentucky Fried Freaks 9. Scorp and the rest of his AA Chapter 8. Once corporate sponsporship takes over: The Turtle Wax Squadron of Fear and the NUmber One New Stable to enter the UWA 1. It doesn't really matter, because CHAD BLAKE WILL CRUSH THEM ALL!!!!!!! Well, well, I haven't had so much fun since I placed booby-traps at the Special Olympics! Oh, Lord, that was great. Anyway, my first guest proves today how a man who dropped out of fourth grade and is illiterate can one day kiss enough butt to become the president of a major wrestling federation. Please welcome TNT!!!! (A haggard looking drunk stumbles onto camera, dressed just like TNT. He is wearing a t-shirt that has TNT drawn up like Alfred E. Neuman saying "What, Me Think?") CHAD: Hey, TNT, thanks for breaking up engagements with your pals Jack Daniels and Jim Bean and showing up tonight. CHAD: Oh, TNT, you ruddy-faced gump, just have a little patience. OK, buddy? Thanks so much. So anyway, I heard you reinstated the Fearsome Foursome recently.... FAKE TNT: Uhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh....yeah.......*fumbles in inner jacket pocket for 3X5 card* Errr....."That is becuzzzzz I have no spin, uh, SPINE, and I am an Ooooooh-terrrr.... CHAD: Er, that's "utter", TNT, "utter." FAKE TNT: Yeahhhhhhhh.....utter....uhhhh, what's this word? *shows Chad card* CHAD: Hmmmm.......you know TNT I think that is "imbecile." FAKE TNT: Emmmmm-beck-see-lay..... CHAD: No, you drunken piece of......oh never mind, we'll let it slide for ya, TNT. Lord knows you need the slightest of reasons to deny me another title shot against Suicide. FAKE TNT: *half paying attention* Suiciiiideeeeee....is BAD. That's how Rattkind died... Say guys, since you admire my craft so much, why don't you come over round here (heads to front of the desk) and kiss my specimen feet?
