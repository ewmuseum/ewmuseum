LordZero, dressed in a black trench coat and wearing his belt over his shoulder, is sitting with LadyZero, who is in black shorts and a black bikini top. Bad Habit is sitting with Desire, who has a leather strap in her hand. Hell's Angels are sitting together, their belts on their laps. LordZero is holding a VCR remote.) LordZero: Well, you might be wondering WHY we're all gathered here together. Well, we figured that every week, after seeing the Spotlights, we'd go over them. So, without further adieu, here is the second installment of "Legion's Spotlight Replay"! <<PLAY>> Malibu: Well I am back now and I never had a chance to comment on my lose a week ago. Lordzero...you knew I had Crusher and you feared that I would win and take that belt away from you. Well if you wanna play rough then so beit. I saw the next PPV is on the 24th and thought that maybe I should just show up and get me a match. WEll I have been thinking of two people I would like to face. Crusher Stevenson or Lordzero. Well I want both so here it is a TRIANGLE Triple Threat match for the International Ttile in a Steel Cage for NO interference. How do you like that. Malibu vs. Crusher vs. Lordzero for the International Title at Summer Showdown on August 24th. <<STOP>> LordZero: Let's get one thing straight Mr. Malibu. Firstoff, I will admit, I knew that you had Crusher Stevenson beat. But my reason for coming out was not that I feared that you would beat me. In fact, that was probably the LAST reason that I would do such a thing. You really want to know why we attacked? Well, that's simple. You thought you could rely on the Legion, and you counted on us to show up if the Fearsome Foursome came out. That really pissed us off, ESPECIALLY because you aren't a Legion member! As far as your little triangle match in a steel cage, I have to decline it. That is because I am going to be in the World Title match. Like I said, I will either be DEFENDING the UWA World Title, or WINNING the UWA World Title! I'm planning on going down in the UWA history books as the first 2-Title wrestler! And there's NOTHING any UWA wrestler can do to stop me! Not you, not Big Butch, and not even that punk-ass Suicide! <<PLAY>> "Rejector" Ronnie Korbine: Anotha stupid non-original idea is Legion of Darkness. Okay, I think I've heard the letters L.O.D. somewhere before. Ha! More like the Legion of Dipsh-- Oh yeah, we can't cuss. Dat right there iz gay by itself. I really hate stables, cuz that means they can't stick up for themselves and have to rely on yo' crew to watch yo' back. Dat iz weak right there. Okay, now the Ministry of Justice. Okay, whateva. <<STOP>> LordZero: RETARD ALERT! RETARD ALERT! This guy thinks that making fun of EVERY wrestler in the UWA will help his chances of winning! (laughs) Rejector, let me tell you a little something. When I first came into the UWA, all I did was make fun of the other wrestlers. And you know what it got me? MY ASS KICKED! Then I realized that actually getting up off my ASS and TRAINING would help my chances. And look what it's got me...UWA GOLD baby! You can hate stables all you want my friend, but this right here is FAMILY! From the looks of your house, it looks like you don't have a family! So, keep flapping your mouth, because it's all talk! You better watch YOUR back my friend, because you're just another name in the Legion's hit list... (LordZero gets up and turns off the television. He then sits back down.) LordZero: Well, that's all for our show today. At least that's all for the video portion of the show. Now, we have comments from our wrestlers... (camera pans to Bad Habit) Bad Habit: All I have to say is that Malibu Mike is a DEAD MAN this upcoming Sunday Night! And, unfortunately, so is his manager Sara! Malibu, this Sunday night you're gonna learn what the Legion is all about! Once you step in that ring, your worst nightmares will come true! I'm planning on hurting you. None of this technical wrestling crap. I'm a BRAWLER, and that's what you can expect...a BRAWL! Which move will I use on you? Will it be the Habit Breaker...or the Eraser? I guess you'll find out come Sunday! Sara, I hope you're ready too! Desire: That's right valley girl! That leather strap is gonna be plastered to your skin once I whip you! You'll be so mangled that even your PARENTS won't recognize you! Be prepared for HELL sweetie! (Camera then pans to Hell's Angels.) Hate: Where's the damn talent in the fed? When is some sort of a CHALLENGE going to come into the UWA? I mean come on! We've already beaten every team on the roster... Anger: Wait, no we haven't... Hate: Who's left? Ahh...the Ministry of Justice! We haven't kicked YOUR asses yet! Maybe we can set up a match with you 2. It all depends...maybe you will face us at Summer Showdown! Anger: Well, that's IF they're #1...but they look to be the best talent out there. No matter, we've PROVEN ourselves as champions! What's our record here again? Hate: Well...umm...we haven't lost yet! Anger: That's right, we haven't. Ever since we won the titles last May, we haven't lost at all! What does that say about the talent...NOT MUCH! (Camera finally pans to LordZero) LordZero: Crusher...Sunday Night you prepare for Ground Zero. You were lucky we softened Malibu Mike up for you...just consider it a favor. Now you get a shot at the champion. I don't know about the betting...but I'd say the odds are in my favor. I can't wait to SoulCrush you. Once I lay your ass out in the ring, you'll wake up knowing that you're NOT the champ! There's no more to say, so that's the end of "Legion's Spotlight Replay". I guess we'll see everyone on Weekend Spotlight! Remember... IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR! (screen fades) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Malibu Mike about: Bad Habbit, Lord Zero =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (Malibu Mike is swimming in his pool. He only has a pair of tight swimming trunks on. Sara is on the diving board wearing a swim suit. She has Silver stars around her nipples and a string bikini. Malibu sees the Camera and comes over towards it) Malibu: Well...I just found out I will be facing Bad Habit this weekend. Well he is a LOD member and he deserves to be put away like the dog that he is. They cost me my match last week. Dont think for one second I forgot about that. Lordzero...you ass is mine..I dont know when but VERY soon I will meet you and strip you of that title. (Sara sees the camera and comes towards it. The camera guy just stares.) Sara: That asshole, Lordzero thinks he is just going to get away with this and have us forget about it...well we have good memories and after Malibu spills that runt Bad Habit were coming for you. (Camera still on Sara) Malibu: Lordzero, your going to pay VERY VERY soon.....I dont know when it will be but it will be soon. And dont even think of coming down to my match and interfereing because it will only get you deeper. God dammit...im talking put the camera on me. (Malibu grabs the camera and turns it toward him) Malibu: Bad Habit, you will be the first part of my defeat over LOD. Your runt ass is going to be put down. You may claim to be a bad ass but truly you are nothing compared to the REAL DEAL Malibu Mike. Sara: Oh yeah...what is this about a strap between me and Desire. Well trust me I may not be a fighter but I sure in hell am tricky. So watch your back. Malibu: Bad Habit, why dont you talk about me not some whipping...you afraid I know you are. Your cruiserweight ass is going to get the biggest beating off its life this Weekend. (Malibu and Sara laugh as the camera fades out) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Johnny "BadBoy" Coals and Sean Goldz about: Chad Blake, The Fearsome Foursome =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (Scene opens inside of a limo with the camera facing Johnny "BadBoy" Coals, Sean Goldz and their manager, Moneyz.They're all enjoying some wine and all dressed in gray Armani suits. Johnny and Sean are also wearing Ray Bans) Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals: Whazup,whazup,whazup! Yo, Chad "The Specimen" Blake, you heard what we gots to say on The Tuesday Spotlight and we here to up the anti, baby!We all know that you're giving up 50 G's for this match,and we respect dat cuz we like guys that are willing to throw up the cash! But since we business men, we'll throw in this deal for YOU!If you choose us for your team and we somehow lose, then we will return 25G's! Sean Goldz: That's right, Chaddy, we'll give you a %50 rebate if we lose, but hey we ain't goin' to lose so don't even sweat it! Just cut the check and let's bust some Fearsome Foursome A$S!! What everybody in UWA is looking at here, is the best two wrestlers that money can buy! We stand together and baby,we always win! Moneyz: So Mr.Blake, make the right decision and make the Bad Boyz we got up in here your partners cuz believe me you won't regret, but the Fearsome Foursome WILL! Johnny"Bad Boy"Coals:But enough of this BS, we headin' up to Colorado now to hook wit our boy Larry Walker, so if ya want us, give us a call and we'll hookup and destroy those Fearsome flamers! HAHAHA! After my match with Marduk I should be called Billy "Tadpole" MacNee. NOT ANY MORE!! I was pathetic out there in front of thousands of people and got my clock cleaned, so until I win my first match, I wont go by TigerShark. I`ll go by Tadpole!! I acted like it out on the mat so I need to pay!
