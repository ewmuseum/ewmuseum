Brian Pillman's death was untimely...shocking the wrestling world...as was John Denver's death on Sunday...affecting the world at a more global level. Let's go to our ring announcer Michael Cook. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...at this time in recognition of the untimely deaths of both Brian Pillman and John Denver...and in respect to their families...we observe a moment of silence and 5 rings of the time keeper's bell. (Silence) Ding! (Silence) Ding! (Silence) Ding! (Silence) Ding! (Silence) Ding! Michael Cook: Thank you. DC: Well...two names that you just don't hear together in the same sentence. John Denver and Brian Pillman...dead in October of ‘97.
