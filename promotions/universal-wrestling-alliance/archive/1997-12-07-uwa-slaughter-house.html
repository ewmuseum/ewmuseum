UWASlaughter House

7 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UWA Saturday Slaughterhouse December 6, 1997 Civic Center - Mobile, AL LIVE!!!!! ("10 Seconds Down" plays as scene opens up to thousands of screaming fans filling the Civic Center in Mobile, Alabama...zooming in to Dan Crawford and Rex Roseman at the commentary booth) DC: Hello out there all and welcome to the Saturday Slaughterhouse! Some big contests coming up this afternoon. I'm really looking forward to them. And of course as you know...there's been several ejections made by President Gordon this week. RR: Look out BBA! Look out Solid Gold! Gordon's on the rampage! He'll have us all on food stamps by next spring! DC: You can say goodbye to the Angels of the Apocalypse...Thugs For Life...the Asswhupper..."Diehard" Devon Randall...and others that I won't mention. I understand that there's been some discussion about the whereabouts of Malibu Mike who we have not seen since he brawled with Flaming Bob at WrestleFest...and Vile Vince Viper has been in and out of the main office all week. He's NOT a happy man. RR: Would you be!? VVV was RIPPED OFF! DC: Let's not get into that Rex...let's start the action. RR: No...not the Horsemen. DC: And here's Scott May now in the ring...having a few words with Horsmen manager/valet Kelly O'Dell. RR: She prances around like she's Dazzling Danielle or something Crawford. DC: Talk about Danielle...did you see that Degeneration X interview this week without the sensors? RR: Ha ha ha! I taped that interview! Now that is what sells Crawford! DC: Well the UWA has been contacted by the FCC and scolded. And it seems that they're going to try and get some harsher ratings put on the UWA broadcasts. We DON'T need harsher ratings. RR: No but we need more fleshy interviews from Dazzling Danielle! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...currently in the ring and to my right...representing the Horsemen...SCOTT MAY!!!!!! (boos) DC: Scott May getting a typical ovation from the crowd. RR: He's used to it I think. DC: He should be. ("Harvester of Sorrow" by Metallica) (crowd cheers) DC: Listen to this crowd. RR: They know talent when they see it. Chris Simmons is in a class of his own! (Simmons appears on the jumbo-tron. He's standing before the entranceway.) Simmons: Cut the music! (music stops) Simmons: I am a member of Solid Gold now! (some boos) So start playing Solid Gold's music! ("More Human then Human" by White Zombie starts playing.) (More boos. Simmons walks out, staring straight at Scott May.) DC: The fans don't like this action by Simmons at all. RR: So what? Who cares what they think? DC: Obviously not Simmons. He's walking down to the ring, eyes fixed on May. What? Listen to the crowd chant! (YOU SOLD OUT! YOU SOLD OUT! YOU SOLD OUT! YOU SOLD OUT!) DC: Simmons looks out to the crowd, but his facial expression hasn't changed one bit. He's now entering the ring. Again, his eyes fixed on Scott May. (music stops) RR: Ha ha ha! I love it! DC: And I cannot believe what I'm hearing. This is a guy that really came in and hit it off big with fans all over the world...and now he's one of the most hated! RR: Hate is bad. Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: Simmons attacking May from behind! (boos) Sending him face first into the turnbuckle...and now rolling him up! 1 2 no! Kickout just in the nick of time! RR: This is the way to start a match! May is up and surprised...Simmons lifts him high into the air! Military press slam to the canvass! Incredible strength! DC: Chris Simmons...classified as a cruiserweight has some real heavyweight attributes as well. May is up...clothesline by Simmons...and another clothesline! (boos) Simmons down...hooking May into an arm lock. RR: Ha ha ha! This guy is great! I wasn't so sure when he first showed up at Hellstorm...but now I have drawn my conclusion. DC: And what's that? RR: HE DA' MAN! And Scotty Daniels...Johnny Tambo...this is one you've GOT TO KEEP! DC: Ha! I'm sure they'll keep him Rex. RR: They ditched Troy and Stevenson...and that I can understand. Those guys have no class...but Simmons is a whole different number. DC: Whatever you say Rex. May somehow has found his way out of the arm lock and he's got Simmons twisted up...but Simmons with a hip toss! (boos) Going to the ropes...May down for a backdrop...DOUBLE ARM DDT!!!!!!! (mixed reaction) 1 2 3!!!! Put it in the books! This one's history! ("More Human Than Human" by White Zombie plays) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner..."CRUSHER" CHRIS SIMMONS!!!!!!!!! (chants of "YOU SOLD OUT! YOU SOLD OUT!") DC: Simmons the winner...and right up in the face yelling at Scott May...and he's setting him up...ANOTHER DOUBLE-ARM DDT!!!! RR: He's out to end careers Crawford! DC: And I'm inclined to believe it! Simmons now getting out of here as Dazzling Danielle helps her man out of the ring. A good one to begin today's special...the Saturday Slaughterhouse. Lots more action to come. ("Tales From the Darkside" theme plays) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is a tag team match. The winner of this match will be named #1 contenders and will receive a shot at the UWA Tag Team titles later in this broadcast. First...lead down to the ring by the Painmaster...Frank N. Stein and the Wolf Mann....THE NIGHTMARES!!!!!!!!!!! (boos) DC: Here they come...Painmaster has Wolf Mann on that leash as always. And Frank N. Stein beating Wolf with a bone! RR: They're going to beat Wolf Mann to death before the match starts! DC: Wolf Mann just roaring and walking on...and now they've got him in the ring...Wolf standing on the ring apron...Painmaster holding his leash. And Frank N. Stein is in the ring now. RR: I didn't think they got any crazier than Frank...not until they brought this Wolf Mann along. And what else could they have down in that basement? DC: I don't even want to know that one. ("Sharp Dressed Man" by ZZ Top plays) Michael Cook: Their opponants...Rick Silver...Michael Gold....SILVER & GOLD!!!!!!!! (cheers) DC: And the crowd really likes this team! An impressive showing and a victory over Crusher Stevenson and Big Butch Thornton earlier this month. Silver & Gold have success written all over them. RR: But what about these Nightmares? The Nightmares defeated the Big Boyz...and how many teams can say that they did that? DC: True...very true. Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: Rick Silver now starting the match as Mike Gold goes to the ring apron. And both men seem very wary of the Wolf Mann. RR: Yeah, Frank Stein is scarry...but Wolf Mann is too horrible to even think about Frank. DC: Big round house right by Stein ducked by Silver...and Silver goes under...tackling Stein to the mat! (cheers) Working him over with rights and lefts. Now lifting him to his feet...sending him to the ropes...Stein holds on...and neither man move. RR: We got a quick start here! Now both men stopping...taking it easy...collecting their thoughts. DC: Lock up...and Stein with the power advantage...hooks Silver into a hiptoss! (boos) Silver flies high into the air and almost completely across the ring! (boos) RR: Ha ha ha! Stein taking a bow! DC: Silver now tagging out to Michael Gold. And now Gold is in the ring...locking horns with Stein. Stein again winning the strength contest...pushing Gold into the corner...and with a knee lift into the abdomen. And a big right hand to the head...Irish whip...right into the corner of the Wolf Mann!!!! (boos) RR: Woah! Wolf Mann holding him in there! And he's biting his face! DC: Referee stop this! Silver to the outside...he's got a chair...over the back of the Painmaster...over the back of Wolf Mann...WOAH! PAINMASTER JUST TURNS AND LOOKS AT HIM...NO EFFECT!!!!!!! RR: Wolf has dropped Gold on the inside...and Gold is bleeding from the forehead! Painmaster has dropped the leash...and he's going after Rick Silver! DC: Look at this! Wolf Mann in the ring...and clawing at Michael Gold! Frank N. Stein is trying to pull him off! Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: Painmaster just smacked a steel chair over the head of Rick Silver...and it's lights out! DC: Painmaster now has got that leash...pulling him off of Michael Gold! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winners as a result of a disqualification...and #1 contenders for the UWA tag team championship....SILVER & GOLD!!!!!!!! RR: Gold is bleeding from the forehead...look at that gash! DC: It's a nasty one Rex...and now they still have to face the Demolishers later in the broadcast. Painmaster is pulling Wolf Mann out of there...and I bet he'll get a beating tonight. RR: I'd feel sorry for the guy if he wasn't a savage beast that would chew my head off without a second thought. DC: Silver & Gold now leaving here...they win the match...but they get the hurt too. RR:"Yes now it's time for The Big Guy to make his debut in the UWA,and he is one big guy,and I can't wait to see him kick butt against Dork Man!" DC:"Oh be quiet Rex!Let's head down to ringside!" (Michael Cook is in the ring,with the microphone) Michael Cook:"This match is scheduled for one fall!" ("Welcome to Planet Motherf^&*er"by White Zombie,plays over the PA,and the fans boo loudly) Michael Cook:"Entering the ring first,from Parts Unknown,weighing in at 330lbs,at a whooping 7"5 1/2,here is making his debut in the UWA,the second tallest man in the UWA,The Mutinizer!"(The fans boo loudly) (The Mutinizer comes out,and the fans all are shocked at this man,ie.his height,size.The Mutinizer is wearing a green wrestling singlet,green gloves,green boots,and his hair is tied back in a ponytail.The Mutinizer grabs onto the ropes,climbs up onto the apron,and over the ropes.The Mutinizer raises his arms in the air,and green fireworks go off in the building,as all of the fans continue to boo loudly) Michael Cook: And now, hailing originally from Perth, Western Australia and now residing in Miami, Florida. Weighing in at 247lbs and standing 6' 4", Here is the Thunder from Down Underrr........DEEEEEEE MAAAAAAANNNNN!!!!!! (The crowd erupts as "THUNDERSTRUCK" by AC/DC begins to blast through the speakers and the lights go out. Numerous spotlights of "D"s are shone in to the crowd around the arena, including one shone down in to the ring and one on the curtain at the aisle entrance. As the curtain is thrown open and D-Man bursts through, the lights come back on around the arena. D-Man is wearing his usual attire of black tights with large golden "D"s on both front and back of his top. He walks to ringside pumping his fist in the air to the crowds chants of "Thunder". He high fives a few fans along the aisle. As he approaches the ring he runs and slides into the ring.) RR: And Mutinizer attacks D-Man before the bell! DING-------DING-------DING DC: Mutinizer stomping away on D-Man who sits against a turnbuckle! And now the ref finally separates them. RR: D-Man is struggling to get up. He appears hurt after that! DC: Mutinizer quick on the attack again. Pounding away with forearms to the back. Grabs him and gut-wrench suplex by Mutinizer. And now he's kicking him in the face. RR: D-Man's gonna have a headache after this one. DC: Mutinizer grabs D-Man around the throat. Picks him off the canvas and still has him! Holding him in the arm with one arm. He now holds him with his other arm.....up and....CHOKESLAM! RR: That was a monster chokeslam by Mutinizer! DC: He's going for the cover.....2.........NO! D-Man kicks out! RR: That was close. It was almost over for D-Man. DC: Mutinizer whips him to the ropes, D-Man ducks the close and comes off with e drop kick! Mutinizer still up. D-Man with another drop kick, he's still up! RR: Ha. D-Man can't get him down! DC: D-Man going to the top rope...FLYING DROP KICK! HE'S STILL UP! RR: He's still up after a flying drop kick by D-Man. DC: D-Man back on top...jumps off...Mutinizer catches him into a bearhug! RR: He's gonna squeeze the life out of D-Man. DC: D-Man showing signs of life...chops at Mutinizers head. And again...and again... RR: Mutinizer still has him. DC: Poke to the eye by D-Man and Mutinizer releases the hold. D-Man against the ropes...closeline by Mutinizer, and D-Man FLIPS on mid air! RR: Wow! D-Man is really hurting after that one. DC: Mutinizer picking D-Man up...lifts him up...tombstone coming up? Yes. Tombstone Piledriver by Mutinizer. D-Man looks out of it. RR: It's over! D-Man is motionless. Come on, Mutinizer, get the pin! DC: Mutinizer gloating over D-Man. He looks out to the crowd! RR: He turns around, what's he doing. Get the pin! DC: D-Man turning over, he sees Mutinizer. RR: Turn around Mutinizer! DC: From behind, D-Man rolls Mutinizer up! 2........3!!! IT'S OVER! DING-----DING-----DING DC: He got him!! RR: No! Michael Cook: The winner of this match, DEEEE-MAAAAAN!!!! (crowds cheers!) RR: He had the tights! DC: Let's go to the replay here. RR: I'll take this one. Here you see Mutinizer pick D-Man off the canvas by the throat with one hand! Hold him up there for awhile until finally chokeslaming him. Here's that wonderful tombstone piledriver by Mutinizer. But it was all for nothing because here, Mutinizer takes his eyes off D-Man, D-Man rolls him up, clearly with a handful of tights, and gets the win. DC: He's just fighting fire with fire. RR: So what? He cheated, Mutinizer deserves the win! DC: Anyway, let's get ready for the next match. Stay tuned...
