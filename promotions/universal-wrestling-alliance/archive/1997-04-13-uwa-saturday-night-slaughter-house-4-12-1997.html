(UWA) Saturday Night Slaughter House 4/12/1997

13 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------------------------------------ The Universal Wrestling Association proudly presents............. Saturday Night Slaughter House Live! From the Tokyo Dome in Tokyo, Japan ------------------------------------------------------ ( UWA Theme Song (nWo) begins to play ) ( Shot of Scorpion and Dark Scorpion brawling ) ( Shot of One Man Warrior landing One Way Out on 1-2-3 Boy ) ( Shot of Chris Comwell rolling up Radon ) ( Shot of Immorality hitting Stone Cold Crazy over the head with his guitar ) ( Shot of Gunloc and Suicide falling off the UWA trailer ) ( The camera fades into the Saturday Night Slaughter House graphic. It then cuts to a wide shot of the thousands of screaming fans in the Tokyo Dome subtitled "Saturday Night Slaughter House! Live! From the Tokyo Dome in Tokyo, Japan". This is followed by a montage of all the fans wearing UWA merchandise and holding signs that read such things as "Welcome UWA to Japan", "American Patriot Sucks", and "The DIA owns the FA". The camera cuts to another wide shot as red, white, and blue fireworks explode over the ring and orange fireworks shoot up from the turnbuckles. Then above the ring the UWA logo lights up. The camera finally cuts to the announcer's table where Dan Crawford who is dressed in a UWA issued suit and Rex Roseman dressed in jeans, a sports jacket, a VVV hat, and a VVV T-shirt. Roseman also has a VVV coffee cup sitting in front of him ) Dan Crawford: Welcome to UWA Saturday Night Slaughter House!!!!!!!!!!! We are in Japan and this is the last spot on our European/Asian Tour!!!!! I am Dan Crawford and with me is the cheap shot of artist of the decade Rex Roseman. Rex Roseman: What do you mean Crawford? Me and VVV won fair and square on Monday. Dan Crawford: I had the "Don't Call Me Crawfish" on you and then those midgets distracted me and you hit me over the head with a harp. Rex Roseman: Who Me?
