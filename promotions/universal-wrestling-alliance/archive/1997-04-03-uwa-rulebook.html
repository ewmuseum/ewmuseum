----------------------------------------------- UWA 1997 Season Rule-Book and Reference Guide ----------------------------------------------- Welcome to the UWA 1997 Season. The UWA has gone all long way since the 1996 year. I wound up closing it down and then becomming the pres of the a new UWA which inturn allowed me to merge the two together by bringing in some of the very best from my UWA. So I expect the UWA 1997 year to be one of the very best as we have some great members. Inturn to allow you to recieve the most enjoyment from our fed we have put together this new member rule-book and referance guide. So let's begin the journey to you becoming a UWA member! The Rules We here at the UWA has very few rules and I will try to describe them all right now - You must roleplay at the very least once a week - All wrestlers must be original *That means no rip offs* - Cursing is allowed in roleplays only and it must be masked - No complaining of any sorts about loses will be tolerated as we feel we are very fair - Never put down another member outside of a roleplay - Please do not send mail to all the fed unless it is a flash - All gameplans must be aproved by the President and Vice-President - No Out Of Character comments allowed - No Out of Character insult directed at holders - No in character insults directed at holders - You may not advertise for your or another fed with my mailing list - No insults directed at the President of Vice-President will be in any way tolerated Our rules are simple as I said earlier, and there is no punishment for all of them except the no cursing and the gameplan rules. If you brake it once it will result in a warning. The next time it may be suspension for up to two weeks. This is here to protect the members as most Internet providers have strict rules on cursing. UWA Cards and Pay Per View Events We host weekly cards and monthly pay per view events. These are commentated by our lovelly commentators Dan Crawford and Rex Roseman. When we need more commentators we call upon Mike Miller and Scott McBane. The weekly UWA line up consists of the following shows: UWA Monday Night Mayhem UWA Thursday Night Thriller UWA Saturday Night Slaughter House UWA Sunday Night Extreme Our yearlly Pay Per View events scheduale has now been reformed and will go as follows: UWA New Years Knockout - January 1st UWA Ney York Terror (On the Woodstock farm land) - Febuary 15th UWA March Madness - March 15th UWA Royal Rumble - April 15th UWA Superfight ( UWA's Wrestlemania ) - May 15th UWA Blood Bowl - June 15th UWA Firestrom - July 4th UWA Summer Madness - Agust 15th UWA Out of Town ( Out of the U.S ) - Semptember 15th UWA Total Wreck - October 9th UWA Wrestlefest - November 1st UWA Christmas Chaos - December 15th *Note: Cards are subject to change* UWA Rankings In order to keep track of all our members and who deserves title shots we have developed a fair method for awarding and subtracting point from eachwrestlers record depending on the win/loss outcome of each match. I have listed all the possible match outcome situations and their point value here in the UWA for your convinience. Win by submission = 7 points Win by pinfall = 5 points Win by disqualification = 2 points Win by countout = 2 points Loss by submission = -7 points Loss by pinfall = -5 points Loss by disqualification = -2 points Loss by countout = -2 points Double disqualification = 0 points Double Countout = 0 points There are other matches we hold in the UWA that are different and deserve a closer look. For instance in a Survivor Series match. Each person is awarded points by eliminating someone and how he does it. That one is pretty simple. Which brings me to the next big match the Royal Rumble. The Royal Rumble victory points are awarded to the winner only and losers do not lose points here is how it works in a 30 man rumble: Number 1 = 10 points Numbers 2 - 5 = 9 points Numbers 5 - 10 = 7 points Numbers 10 - 15 = 5 points Numbers 15 - 29 = 3 points Number 30 = 1 point For bigger rumbles the point value is changed and the system is edited, but I will not go into this as the amount f members in a royal rumble can be endless. UWA Belts The UWA has many belts some may be non-sanctioned and belong to the creator. These may change hands under any win, but our belts change hand only on a submission or pinfall. The UWA Belts are: UWA Heavyweight Championship Belt UWA Tag Team Championship Belts UWA Six-Man Tag Team Championship Belts UWA International Championship Belt UWA Lightweight Championship Belt ( 250 pounds and under only ) UWA Stable King Belt *Tournament Belt may not be lost and is striped before next years tournament* How Match Winners Are Decided Roleplay is how we decided to pick the match winners. We tried a big old system of complex things, but roleplay seems like the only fair way to pick the match winners. So that's the way it's going to be. You roleplay and do a bang up job you win. If not then too bad for you. The Application Depending on where you are reading this you have an application in right down below. Please fill it out if you still intend on joining the UWA. Also we do not allow wrestlers over 7 feet in height and 600 pounds in weight as they are very unrealistic. They would be very slow or very very fat. The Finisher, Combined Finisher area should not only have the name of the move, but also a description of the move. While the Other Notes area should describe their pysichal description, ring attire, and their personallity. Please also remember that when filling out a tag team app to include name for both wrestlers and all their stats in that one app it will save us time.
