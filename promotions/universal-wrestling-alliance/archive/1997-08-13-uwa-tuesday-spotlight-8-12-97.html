MYSTERIOUS VOICE: The following message has been paid for by the GREATEST CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION OF ALL TIME, CHAD "The Specimen" BLAKE. VOICE-OVER: Are you SICK of those Midwestern yokels THE FEARSOME FOURSOME???? (Shot of Butch THorton chasing after Balke) VOICE-OVER: Have you had it up to here with their tyrannical reign over the UWA? (Shows Thorton interfering in Blake's title loss to Wrestlingman) VOICE-OVER: and would you like to be paid $50,000? (Extreme close up of 50,000 dollars in a suitcase, which pans up to Chad Blake with his lovely valet Erin by his side.) CHAD BLAKE: Then join BLAKE'S ARMY!!! I need three able-bodied men to sit there on the ring apron while I singlehandedly dispatch of those loathsome creeps from Chi-Town. Belive you and me, it would a privledge to wrestle alongside such a caliber wrestler as myself FOR FREE, but heck, I have money floating all over the place, so why not share the wealth? VOICE-OVER: To join Blake's Army, please send a written or videotaped message of intent to the UWA offices. *in quick whispered tones* Entering does not automatically infer membership. Offer void in the state of Montana. Wrestlingmen need not apply. CHAD BLAKE: Don't miss your chance to be WAAAAAYYYY TOOOOO MUCCCCH FOR A NIGHT!!!!!!!!! (Commercial ends) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Bad Habbit about: Malibu Mike =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (Scene: The Legion of Darknesses' training facility. Bad Habit and Desire are in the ring. Bad Habit is coaching Desire, who is strapped to a plastic dummy that looks like Malibu Mike's manager Sara.) Bad Habit: (to Desire) Whip that strap harder! (a loud *snap* can be heard as the strap smacks against the dummy) Hear that? Desire: Yes... Bad Habit: That sound is PAIN! That sound means that you just whipped the hell outta Sara! How did that feel? Desire: Good...I can't wait until I can whip the real thing! (Bad Habit unstraps Desire, then the 2 walk towards tha camera.) Bad Habit: It looks like TNT finally made a good decision. He decided to put me up against Malibu Mike, and strap Desire and Sara together with a leather strap! Malibu Mike, first your manager gets kidnapped, and now she's gonna be strapped to my manager! Even if I lose the match, just the satisfaction of seeing your precious Sara get whipped will be enough! Malibu, I am the #1 contender for the Cruiserweight title, and that's how I intend it to stay! Come Saturday, when the Habit Breaker is locked, and I break your legs, I'm gonna keep it locked! You thought you were Legion material...but only a pansy stable would accept such a loser. Maybe you should try the FBI? (laughs) Desire: Sara, it's only a matter of days until I get to be strapped to you. I can't wait to beat you bloody. I hope you wear something protective, cause I'm gonna be unrelenting! Then you can take you valley-girl ass back to where you came from! (screen fades) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Malibu Mike about: Lord Zero, Suicide, Crusher Stevenson, Big Butch Thornton =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (Malibu Mike and Sensational Sara are sitting on a couch outside in front of the pool. Malibu is wearing street clothes and Sara is wearing a cut off shirt and cut off jeans.) Malibu: Well I am back now and I never had a chance to comment on my lose a week ago. Lordzero...you knew I had Crusher and you feared that I would win and take that belt away from you. Welll if you wanna play rough then so beit. I saw the next PPV is on the 24th and thought that maybe I should just show up and get me a match. WEll I have been thinking of two people I would like to face. Crusher Stevenson or Lordzero. Well I want both so here it is a TRIANGLE Triple Threat match for the International Ttile in a Steel Cage for NO interference. How do you like that. Malibu vs. Crusher vs. Lordzero for the International Title at Summer Showdown on August 24th. TNT.... give me this match it is all I am asking for or for next weekends Main Event I dont care just give me that match. I know I can beat those two goons and win that Title. Another subject is Stables. I now know that I should get some people to watch my back and I am doing that right now....soon you will see one of the best Stables that will take the UWA by suprise. I know I promised to meet Suicide at Summershowdown. There is still a chance that can happen. Heres another way I could go. Malibu Mike vs. Big Butch Throton for the #1 contender spot the winner meets Suicide at Summershowdown and this match can take place this weekend. I dont care which match it is the triple threat or the numer 1 contender spot...its all up to the President. Sara: We told everybody that Malibu would be getting a title and soon it looks as though he will....trust me. (Fade out as they go back to talking) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Sean Goldz, Johnny Coals about: Chad Blake, The Fearsome Foursome =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (The camera looks upon a big white gate which surrounds a massive 30 bedroom mansion in the Oakland Hills.A man is seen coming down the walkway from inside the gate.As he comes closer,a noticable glare is coming off his teeth.This is no other than Sean Goldz!With a cell phone in one hand and the other hand punching in the password to open the gate,Sean looks cool and fly as usual.He has on a white tank top,some khaki shorts,a pair of Black Oakleys,a pair of Nike sandals and his trademark 2 gold front teeth.As he motions for the camera man to enter the premise,he continues his conversation on the cell all the way to the front door.Then he motions of the cameraman to sit down,as he walks into another room.A few minutes later,he comes out....) Oh damm,sorry man,did you want sumpin' to drink?...Yeah,sure you do.YO DELORES! (A lovely young women walks into the room) Sean Goldz:Yo hook up Mr.Cameraman here wit a scotch on da rocks! Damm! He's up there flashin' his 50 grand and you know,me,Johnny and Moneyz(Manager of Sean Goldz and Johnny"Bad Boy" Coals)can use some extra green up in our pockets.So Chad,me and Johnny, we both up for the challenge,so if you want the two baddest hitmen to watch your back, hook up wit us cuz believe me,you dealin' wit da best right here.You can take both of us or either,as long as you put up da greenz we watch your back for life!Cuz we... (Then Moneyz and Johnny"Bad Boy"Coals enters the room) Johnny Coals: Yo whazup kid!What's this you doin'? Sean Goldz: Yo,this is dat UWA sh!t,we tryin' to hook up wit Chad Blake right?So now I'm settin' it up! Johnny Coals: Cool,yo Chad,you see right here,you dealin' wit da real playas right here,we're the best the money can buy and if you got money,then you got us!We are the Chicago Bulls of wrestling!We simply da best!Just consider me Michael and my man Sean,Scottie,da sky's da limit.... (As Johnny flings his arms in the air,he bumps into Delores who just enteredthe room to bring back a drink for the cameraman.Johnny knocks the drink right onto Sean's lap) Johnny Coals:Damm!Hey,Delores,what are you doin'!You messed him up!You want dat takin' outta your pay!F#ck,man,I swear,you gotta be the clumiest Mother... Sean Goldz:Yo,J,take it easy,it's all right,it's nothin',it's cool... Johnny Coals:No it's not COOL!Delores get your @ss outta here before I get mad! MOVE IT! Moneyz:Yo UWA and especially Chad Blake,get a good look,look at how mad Johnny gets mad over a spilled drink!Now,Chad,we down and you know dat we da best thing goin' so hook up wit us and dat Foursome will be taken care of! (Camera fades as Moneyz makes hand signs at the camera,Johnny"Bad Boy"Coals is still yelling and livid,as Sean Goldz walks away to change) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Damien Dredd about: The UWA =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (Damien Dredd is seated on a patio overlooking an isolated beach, complete with palm trees, the sound of crashing waves, and the occasional seagull. A TV and VCR can be seen to one side. Dredd's locks hang over the back of his chair as he holds a tanning shield under his face. Dredd turns to face the camera) DD: Just lazin the days away, waiting for someone to take me up on my open contract. I come to realise that it's not gaan happen anytime too soon, BRAA... I got no gold and the wrestlers inna UWA don wanna face anyone that's gaan give em a fight. They'd rather step inna ring with the likes of jobbers, BRAA! Lots of others wait for thier chance too. The Rasta keeps his ear to the ground, and I hear whispers that some may be attackin others from behind just to get some attention! We'll that's not my way... Mebbe I can make a place for myself watching some backs... THEN let a fool come an cross my path BRAA! The Rasta been lookin at other feds around the world. I wanna show ya what I been findin... (DD turns on the TV and flips a tape into the VCR) <begin tape> "... and the ACEW champion is choking the Purple-helmeted Warrior in the middle of the ring!" "SS: This is hardcore." "SR: this is real hardcore." "The Purple-helmeted Warrior swells up with fury. He digs a plastic spoon out of his tights and starts gouging the eyes of the champion!" "SS: This is so hardcore." "SR: This is real hardcore." (Dredd, with a look of disgust on his face, flips off the TV) DD: Makes me glad to be inna UWA BRAA! A lotta good talent here! Malibu Mike, I seen ya work inna other federations... Ya not too bad for a pretty boy, BRAA! I got a proposition for ya! Give ya woman a treat an bring her down ta Jamaica. Soak up some sun and sand and get a Good Vibration goin on before ya get back to work! (Dredd goes back to his tanning) See ya round, BRAA! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Her Majesty's Secret Service about: All UWA tag teams =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (It's a cold night at Flanigan's Gym, an upper NY City gymnasium that has seen better days. After about 10 seconds, a spotlight shines upon a television set, which looks like it is floating. The television turns on, and the Legion's motto "It's an all out war!" appears on the screen. A voice starts to speak.) Voice: The Legion of Darkness was suspended last week on UWA's Main Event... (Screen shows LordZero SoulCrushing Malibu Mike as he started to apply the Scorpion Death Lock. (Shot of Crusher Stevenson in the ring. Shot of the Crusher Breaker, which fades into LordZero hitting him with his bat. LordZero: Crusher, you thought what the Legion did to you last week was bad? You ain't seen nothing yet! You may have the Crusher Breaker, but I have the SoulCrusher. IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Screen fades, and the television turns off. A black aluminum bat spraypainted with "Fearsome Foursome: Terminated" slams into the television, crushing it.
