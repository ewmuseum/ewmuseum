Micheal Cook: It doesn't say who your partner i* (Vile Vince grabs the microphone out of Cook's hands...) Vile Vince Viper: Man...you made a big missstake letting me clossse to a live microphone! Sssee there are going to be one or two changesss around here. You sssee I look at Wressstle Fessst 2...firssst off the myssstery man. That guy wasss massssssive!!! How could anyone missstake me for that guy? Now I'll tell you sssome people that did fit that guy'sss build...TNT, Immorality, Damon Zex...the guy wasss wearing a sssuit but ssstill. Now here isss a hypothetical sssituation for you...presssident Gordon findsss hisss money maker isssn't allowed to wressstle on the Pay Per View. He and hisss butt-buddy TNT put together thisss myssstery man ssshit to come out and lay down for Wressstling Man! Well, I manage to get a temporary licenssse I get the match I sssigned for to ssstart...what happensss? Well firssst the PC run down, then Hell on Earth ssshow up. Then realizing I ssstill have a chance of winning it they sssend Butch Thorten down to make sssure Gordon can look ssshocked. Well Gordon, thingsss are different...you sssee you complain about how I ssscrew people out of winsss? I got counted out so you could keep sssuicide! <TripleV cracks a smile then continues...> Vile Vince Viper: Man I can't believe you allowed that ssshit head to go undefeated...HaHaHa. Now I'm allowed to compete in a four way match for MY UWA title? How convenient it isss on the same day that I challenged TNT to a match. Now I get to compete for my preciousss title? No deal boyz. Now...letsss sssee I win the title what will happen? I will get it ssstripped jussst ssso it can be put up in New Yearsss Knockout? No dice. DC: Strong words from Mr. Viper. RR: Well I'll have to give him and amen and a hallelujah because he's right on the money Crawford! I'm tired of seeing Gordon cheating legends like Vile Vince Viper! ("The Mirror" by Dream Theater blasts over the P.A.) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...introducing first...from the Urbish Mines...standing at 6'11"...weighing 326lbs...REX OVERLORD!!!! (Crowd erupts as Overlord walks through the curtain in a black singlet, black boots, black knee pads, and a black leather vest. The back of the vest simply says "Overlord" in metallic gray lettering. A look of intensity is upon his face...he stops just outside the entranceway and raises a closed fist high into the air (ala NOD) in salute to the crowd as they begin to cheer louder. Enjoying his renewed fanfare...nodding his head and grinning as he slaps hands with a few fans...then eyes meeting VVV's in the ring...pointing a finger in his direction and spewing unheard profanity...he climbs in the ring and leans in the corner...awaiting the presence of his partner) DC: Overlord looks great tonight. He looked good at WrestleFest...and I think that must have really geared him up for this one. RR: Well this is big Crawford. This is PPV worthy action that we're seeing here tonight. Michael Cook: Hailing from Detroit, Michigan, standing at 6"10 and weighing 345 lbs., here is the former UWA HeavyweightChampion........... BIG BUTCH THORNTON!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The crowd explodes as Michael Cook announces the name of Big Butch Thornton to the arena. "Enter The Sandman" continues to play and as the opening part of the song ends, Big Butch Thornton walks out with a VERY determined look on his face. Looking for a fight, he is wearing a black Fearsome Foursome t-shirt, blue jeans, and black boots. He also is bringing back out his trademark black razor blade Oakley's. Butch walks to the ring very focused, not paying attention to anything of non-importance.) DC: To say Butch isn't ready for this match is a damn lie. I wouldn't want to be in the shoes of Mr. Vile Vince Viper right now. RR: Oh please. Thornton is finally gonna get the beating he deserves after VVV and his mystery partner destroy him and Rex Overlord! HAHAHAHA DC: I doubt that will be the case. (Thornton steps inside the ring and patiently waits for the match to begin, tossing his Oakley's aside and burning a hole through the curtain at the entrance way.) DC: Both men here curious I must say as to who will join Vile Vince Viper. Overlord turning from Viper to Butch...and saying something to Butch Thornton. RR: These guys are going to get in to it! DC: Overlord is yelling at Thornton! He thinks that Thornton got in there to turn on him! Overlord pointing a finger and BBT slapping it away! WAIT! FROM BEHIND! CLIPPING OVERLORD'S KNEE! WHO IS THAT!? RR: That's Kami Kazi! DC: Kami Kazi...the guy that we saw at WrestleFest...oriental cruiserweight superstar extraordinaire! RR: You got it! DC: Overlord rolling outside the ring...and Kama Kazi going out right after him...as VVV has attacked BBT in the ring. Going crazy with rights and lefts...but having no effect on BBT! (cheers) BBT pushes him off...and out of the corner with a big lariat! (cheers) RR: Kazi has Overlord on the outside...he just busted a steel chair over his head! And he's cuffing Overlord's foot to the guardrail! DC: He is! And this is a disgrace! Overlord and BBT once again falling into a VVV trap! And Kazi back in the ring...to the top rope...jumping onto BBT's back! And he's got him in a sleeperhold! RR: Yes! That's the way to handle it! It's not brawn...it's all brains! DC: VVV slugging BBT as BBT struggles with Kama Kazi! And Overlord trying to get up...but those handcuffs really tightening as he tries to pull away from that guardrail! RR: BBT just backed hard into that corner! And Kazi breaks the hold! VVV now backing up...and BBT trying to get his consciousness back! DC: BBT with rubbery legs...and Overlord angry...this is disgusting. RR: Kazi witha dropkick from behind on BBT! And BBT goes right into VVV...and VVV powerslams him! For the pin.... 1 2 no! DC: Unbelievable...these antics. If I were Gordon I'd bar this guy for life. RR: Ha ha ha! But you're not! Fortunately for all of us! Gordon knows where the money lies! And the money is with Vile Vince Viper...not his two punk opponants! DC: And Kama Kazi now off the top rope with a flying legdrop!!!! Connects!!!! Into a pin... 1 2 no! RR: That key just flew out of Kazi's pocket...and flew outside the ring! DC: And close to Overlord's hand! But can Overlord get to it!? RR: I don't even think Kazi or VVV knows it! DC: And oh no...here comes Flaming Bob. (boos) Flaming Bob with blow torch...putting the blow torch aside and stomping Overlord in the abdomen... RR: Ha ha ha! Bob to the rescue! And he's got that key now! Holding it high above Overlord! Ha ha ha! DC: And now...BBT is up...but VVV is holding him back...and Kama Kazi hitting him with an assortment of karate chops! RR: Oooh...I hear those! DC: But now Butch...high kick to the face of Kazi...and a back elbow knocks VVV back! RR: Flaming Bob with vicious kicks to the ribs of Overlord! DC: And Butch Thornton...he's got VVV by the throat! (cheers) THORNTON SLAM!!!! (crowd erupts) This referee has lost all control! Counting pinfalls with two guys in the ring! RR: Here comes President Gordon! (cheers) DC: Down to ringside now! And he's wearing the black and white stripes! We've got a referee! RR: No! DC: Gordon confronting Flaming Bob...and he's ordering Bob to unlock those cuffs! From the crowd is Bella Donna Di Notte...she's got a chair...SMACK! Woah! RR: I've never seen that from a female manager! DC: Getting revenge for last years "Shot Heard Round the World"! Bob is bent over...and BBT outside the ring...putting Bob's head in for powerbomb position...and he gets it!!!! IT'S JUDGEMENT DAY!!!!! (deafening cheers) RR: Donna Di Notte is in Bob's coat! She's trying to steal his wallet! DC: Give me a break! She's got that key...and she unlocks Overlord's cuffs! (crowd goes nuts) RR: I can't believe it! DC: Overlord is up...and in the ring...POWERBOMBS Kama Kazi!!!!! (cheers) RR: Who's the legal man? DC: BBT and Vile Vince Viper are legal! VVV now getting out of there as BBT rolls back in...and Gordon in the ring as well! RR: VVV is setting a table up! DC: And BBT climbs the turnbuckle...reaching out for a hand full of VVV's hair....FROM BEHIND KAMA KAZI WITH A DROPKICK....AND BBT FALLS OUT...CRASHES HEAD FIRST INTO THAT TABLE... RR: HE SPLINTERED IT!!!!! DC: And Gordon doing the ref's job...counting him out... RR: Overlord has Kazi by the back of the neck...headbutt....INVERTED CHOKESLAM!!!! (cheers) By the back of the neck! Have mercy! DC: VVV now with Flaming Bob...pummeling Big Butch Thornton! Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: Yes! BBT got counted out! DC: And Gordon now talking to the ring announcer...as Overlord outside with a chair...smashes it over the back of Flaming Bob...and VVV scrambles out of the way! RR: Flaming Bob is down...Kama Kazi now out of there and joining VVV. He got chokeslammed and powerbombed! DC: VVV with his hands upraised...enjoying the victory. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner...as a result of a disqualification....BIG BUTCH THORNTON AND REX OVERLORD!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (crowd rises...deafening cheers) RR: WHAT!? NO!? NO! NO! HOW!? NO!!!!! DC: Gordon gives the match to BBT and Overlord! As Overlord is helping BBT to his feet. Folks...that's all of our time. Tune in for the Spotlight.
