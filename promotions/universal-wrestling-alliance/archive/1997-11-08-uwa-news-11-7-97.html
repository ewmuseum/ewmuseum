UWA News - 11/7/97 - from [detail withheld] Friday Night Fights will not be on tonight. The guy that was helping me write the card had something come up and couldn't get me the matches on time. My apologies. I realize that I've been sending a lot of late cards here these past few weeks. Look for the UWA to be listed on several e-mail wrestling indexes next week. I don't know how people are finding the site now, but as far as I know it is no longer linked anywhere. >From the UWA Chairman -DPK-: In order to improve the organization of the UWA mailings, I ask that everybody begin adding the following format before each flash. Please incluse your wrestlers name,(manager, etc.) on the first line and what the flash is about on the second line. ===================================== Name Of Your Wrestler About: What ever the flash is about ===================================== Remember, do not stretch this new format more then halfway accross the page. If you want to include a title of your flash instead of the 'About' format then please do so; just make sure the title covers what will be talked about. There is a gameplan form at the bottom of this sheet. Please keep the latest copy of the news so you will have access to this without having me to send you one individually. If you have any problems with it then let me know. If you want to write a card or a talk show, mail me and we'll get something started. You can write one of my cards or you can start you're own. All matches must be okayed by me. I will give you the results and gameplans that I want put to use in the cards.
