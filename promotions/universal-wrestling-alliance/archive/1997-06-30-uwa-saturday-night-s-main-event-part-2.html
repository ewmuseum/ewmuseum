UWA Saturday Night's Main Event (part 2)

30 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***break*** commercials ***break*** Dan Crawford: Welcome back, our next match is caught up in the middle of some confusion right now. Thats right, I'm talking about the Hell's Angels against New Men At Work. Hell's Angels wished to have a non- title, closed locker room braw. The stipulation was that if New Men At Work won, they would recieve a title shot at Patriot Games. Rex Roseman: Thats right, but the grand moron T.N.T. said and I quote " New Men At Work are the number one contenders as chosen by the UWA commitee and therefor they will recieve a title match against the tag team champions who happen to ne Hell's Angels. The winner of this title match will then take on Her Majesty's Secret Service at Patriot Games." Can you believe that bonehead. Dan Crawford: You're the bonehead, I think that it is a very wise decision. The number one contenders deserve a shot without all of the politics that Hell's Angels wanted to throw at them. So New Men At Work will get the shot right now. Announcer: The next contest is a locker room brawl for the tag team championship. (crowd cheers) Introducing first. (Construction noises come on over the P.A. as the crowd cheers.) Announcer: Weighing in at a combined weight of 596 pounds, managed by Sergent T. Baker. Here are the challengers, Sid, Michell......New Men At Work. (The crowd cheers loudly as the videoscreen shows New Men At Work in the locker room.) Announcer: And their opponents, weighing in at a combined weight of 555 pounds. Here are Hate and Anger....... Hell's Angels! (crowd gives a mixed reaction as the screen shows Hell's Angels run into the locker room and attack New Men At Work.) Dan Crawford: And Hate and Anger waste no time as this locker room braw is underway. Rex Roseman: Now this is my kind of match. Dan Crawford: I'm sure it is Rex....And Hate just through Sid into a locker..... Anger had Michell.. and he just battle ramed him into the steel door Rex Roseman: This is GREAT..... Come on...... head in the toilet. Dan Crawford: You ae disguisting....... And OH NO...... Hate just pushed the row of lockers onto Sid..... He had to be hurt......... And Anger had Michell by the throat........... Michell rakes his eyes.....scoops him up... And down Anger goes........Michell picks up a wooden bench.... Anger is gettin up slowly....... Rex Roseman: AHHHH... did you see that. Dan Crawford: I sure did.... Michell just swung that bench like a baseball bat smack into Angers head....... And HAte clobbers Michell from behind...... He picks up the bench and... OH MY... he just droped it onto Michell's head..... This is dispicable. Rex Roseman: This is a classic match.... but what happened to Sid? Dan Crawford: He's trying to pick himself up from underneath the lockers.... And Hate is helping Anger up after that huge bench swing from Michell..........Sid from behind and he clotheslines Anger to the back of the head Rex Roseman: Look at all the blood on the floor. Dan Crawford: I can see it..... This isn't WCW..... We arn't afraid to show blood on camera.... T.N.T. is no panzy like Bishoff....... And now SId is dragging Anger to the toilet stalls. Rex Roseman: But thats off camera.... I can't see,... tell T.N.T. to hire some real cameramen. (sound of a toilet flushing can be heard as the crowd burst into laughter) Dan Crawford: AhhhHAHAHA. Sounds like Anger got flushed..... Sid is comming out of the stall...... And Hate is waiting for him...a kink to the groin.... Hate drags Sid over to the sink and rams his head into it face first Rex Roseman: Ouch.......The sink had blood everywhere. Dan Crawford: Hate goes for the cover on the cold floor 1 Here comes Michell.... And he's met by Anger 2 3 Dan Crawford: And it's all over...... Hell's Angels keep that belts in a brutal match as they leave the battered New Men At Work........ Sergent Baker trying to help up his team..........And Sid has just pushed the Sergent..... And Michell had grabbed the Serge by the back of the neck.... OH NO... I DON'T BELIEVE IT.... THEY ARE BEATING ON THE SERGE Rex Roseman: YES!!! This is even better then I thought it would be. Dan Crawford: And Sis had just DDT'd the Serge in the cold, hard floor. I just don't believe whaat is hapenning here.... What's next Rex Roseman: Maybe they will bite his ear off or something. Dan Crawford: Oh HA HA you tasteless imbisill.... Lets see if we can get Mike Miller back there to fing out what made Sid and Michell do something like this. ***break*** commercials ***break*** Dan Crawfprd: Well, If you just tuned in you mssed something totally uncalled for...... You are luckly that you didn't have to subject yourself to the behavior of New Men At Work. Rex Roseman: WHAT!.... Don't listen to him...... If you just tuned in you should smack yourself upside the head for missing Sid and Michell attack Sergent T. Baker Dan Crawford: OK... I've just recieved word that Mike Miller has New Men At Work Now... Mike Mike Miller: Believe me Dan... I am just as appalled as you are. (Mike Miller turns to SID) Mike Miller: Sid..... WHY Sid: Why we did it? You want to know? Alright, we'll tell you! Ol' Mitch and I are sick and tired of the empty promises made by the Sarge! When he first contacted us, he promised us the golds! He said that we would continue the Men At Work legacy and become the new UWA World Tag Team Champions! But after two title matches, we are still without the titles! As our manager, he should be doing more instead of standing there and watch the Legion of Darkness cheat their way out! If we had someone better, we would have already be the champions long time ago! So who is to blame for the failure to capture the tag golds? It's him! Mitchell: We have proven to be the best tag team in the UWA but it's his poor management ability that costed us two title shot failures! I've had enough of him and I don't want to have anything to do with Sarge! Sid and Mitchell will be leaving this UWA to start anew in another where real talents like us will be treated like champions! Now get the camera out of our way! Mike Miller: Dan...Rex...back to you Dan Crawford: Well at least we won't have to see them again...but we are a little behind on time so lets get to the next match. Rex Roseman: I just don't understand why Brad Force would put his title on the line one week before Patriot Games.... If he loses the international title then Lord Zero takes his place at Patriot Games in the world title match against Suicide. Dan Crawford: Well, I guess he is sure that he can win. Announcer: This contest is scheduled for one fall...and is for the UWA International Championship!!!!!!! Let's announce the combatants... ("...And Justice For All" by Metallica plays) Announcer: Making his way down the aisle, accompanied by his manager, LadyZero, and the rest of the Legion of Darkness. Standing 6 feet 11 inches...and tipping the scales at 285 pounds...here's the challenger...LORDZERO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Smoke rises from the wrestlers entrance as LordZero and the Legion come out to a mixed crowd. The camera focuses on a child, wearing an "It's an all out WAR: UWA" T-Shirt. The entire Legion is wearing black trench coats, and carrying black aluminum bats. LordZero also wears a silver and black Undertaker hat) (Bret Hart's Theme plays) Announcer: And now the Champion. Accompanied to the ring by Sarge T. Baker, he stands 6 feet 5 inches and weighs 280 pounds...here is BRAD FORCE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Brad Force makes his way to the ring without Sarge T. Baker. He has tape wrapped around his body, with emphasis on his back. All of a sudden, a masked man comes up from behind him, and starts to beat him with a bat. Many referees and security guards come out, and they finally get the masked man out of the arena. Brad Force just lays there) DC: What in the hell was that? RR: That, my friend, was a perfectly mastered plan by the Legion! DC: Why would they do such a thing? (As Force gets up, the camera switches to LordZero, standing in the ring. He has a look of concern on his face, and he is shrugging his shoulders and mouthing "it wasn't the legion!" Force finally makes it to the ring) DC: Well, now I think we're gonna be underway. The bell sounds...and here we go...Force trying to hit Zero with a clothesline right from the start...but his back is making him move slower...Zero ducks and gives Force an inverted atomic drop...now LordZero...on the attack...whips Force to the ropes...and gives him a tilt-a-whirl slam... RR: That's gotta hurt his back...poor baby! (laughs) DC: I hope that's how people treat you in a time of need! At least Force has enough guts to wrestle after that! Anyway...Zero picks up Force...and gives him an abdominal stretch...the ref checks as Force screams out in pain...Zero grabs Bad Habit's hand for extra leverage...and the referee checks...and Zero lets go of Habit just in time...the ref going back to check Force...and Habit once again with some help...this time the ref catches Zero...and the hold is broken...Force hits the mat in considerable pain... RR: Well, at least the title will be in good hands after this Crawford! DC: Don't be so quick to judge...Zero now...whipping Force against the ropes...and a big clothesline...Zero going to the top rope...and connecting with a leg drop...LordZero with a cover...1...and a surprising kickout by Force...Zero looking very surprised...picks Force up...and Force now with a rake to the eyes...Force...whipping Zero against the ropes...and a BIG belly to belly suplex...that must have taken all of his energy to do... RR: Yeah, the little energy that the weakling had! DC: Now both men down on the mat...Zero gets up...and so does Force...Force with a spinning wheel kick to the face of LordZero...who hits the ground hard...Force now on the offensive...picking up Zero...and whipping him into the turnbuckles...he runs...and Zero moves out of the way!!! Force walking dazed...and LordZero with his SOULCRUSHER!!! RR: IT'S ALL OVER! 1... 2... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Announcer: The winner...and NEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWWWWWWW UWA International Champion...LORDZERO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! RR: YES! I can't believe he did it! World Title bound!!!!!!!!!!!! DC: That means LordZero has the shot at Patriot Games against Suicide. But LordZero has a match against Scorp in a cage that day...how's TNT gonna solve this one? (camera switches to the ring. LordZero is celebrating as the referee hands him the belt. He holds it triumphantly over his head. He then sees Force, who is in a lot of pain. LordZero calls for medics. He helps the medics to get the stretcher to the ring. Force pushes the medics away from him. Force gets up on his own. He stares at LordZero. LordZero extending his hand. Force, looking at him funny, shakes his hand. Force and the Legion end up walking out together, with Bad Habit and LordZero helping Force leave) Dan Crawford: Well at least some people have good sportsmenship. Rex Roseman: Good sportsmenship makes me sick. Dan Crawford: We are just about out of time. Dont forget to tune in next Saturday Night for the very first edition of UWA Spotlight hosted by T.N.T. and then on Sunday for Patriot Games.... See you there.
