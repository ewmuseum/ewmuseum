In the background, the noise of a busy gym can be heard. Sitting on the couch is Big Butch Thornton. He is wearing a white Champion tanktop, blue jeans, and black boots. He is watching UWA wrestling from the past couple months. He turns the TV off and begins to speak.) Big Butch Thornton: Sunday Night's Main Event. The main reason I came back to the UWA is so close I can feel it, The UWA World Title. But, like all things in life, getting there isn't gonna be clear cut. To get a shot at World Champion Suicide I have to go through Chad Blake. It's about time. (Stands up) When I came back to the UWA months ago, 2 things were on my mind. Number 1, The UWA World Title. Number 2, KICKING CHAD BLAKE'S ASS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! And isn't this match Sunday something. If I get to fufill my goal of KICKING CHAD BLAKE'S ASS.....I am ONE step away from the UWA World Title. It's about time. (Walks to the trophy case and looks at all the belts and trophies won by himself and the rest of the Fearsome Foursome) There's something missing there. Not for long though. Chad Blake, me and you certainly haven't seen eye to eye on things the past few months. But there has been one thing in common between us.....Our Lust For the UWA World Title. Only one of us can get the chance at Summer Showdown to face Suicide and you can bet your bottom dollar it's gonna be ME!!!!! Sunday Night Chad Blake, me and you were on the opposite sides of battle for the second time. But for the second time it wasn't a singles match. The match everybody has been waiting for is gonna happen Sunday with some major implications for Summer Showdown at stake. Only last week, were you pinned on the mat 1....2....3 by Big Butch Thornton. I know it was total bullshit but it's something that you better get used to because this Sunday it's gonna happen again!!! The TIME has come Chad Blake, it's you and me, ONE on ONE. Call up your doctor because after I'm through with your arrogant carcass, it's gonna be pretty messy. I GUARANTEE IT!!!!!!!!!!!!!
