An idea I had for a new magazine kind of show. Basically, a collection of the main events from feds interested. For this example, I`ve nabbed a few main events off newsgroup cards,but in future, perhaps whoever will run Main Event will get non Newsgroup matches as well. Main Event will help raise the profile of wrestlers and feds. It can only be a good thing, right? I won`t be able to run this, but somebody should take it up. Below is an example of what Main Event could be like. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by Patrick King and Justin 'Big Daddy' Fischer, weighing in at 264 pounds from Detroit, Michigan, this is "THE ASSASSIN" TONY TOBIS!!! The crowd erupts in cheers as Tobis appears from behind the entranceway, and "Enter the Sandman" by Metallica plays over the intercom system. Tobis just nods at a few of the fans as the trio continues down toward the ring. The cheers immediately turn to boos as "Money Talks" by AC/DC starts to play. [JC] And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by the Master and Ruler of the World, weighing 225 pounds, here is ANDROS!!!!! Andros and Master make their way down toward the ring, and the crowd continues a very loud chorus of boos. Niether man seems phased, and before tehy get in the ring, Andros takes off his U.S. championship title and hands it Master. Inside the ring the two men go at it immediately with a standard lockup. Andros gets the early advantage with a number of low blows, and he tries keeping Tobis slowed down with an array of headlocks and armlocks. Andros and Tobis swap positions of advantage a number of times early in the match, when Andros tosses Tobis out to the ring, where Master immediately starts pounding on Tobis. Before Fischer can get over to help his friend, Master sends Tobis back into the ring, and Andros takes over with a slingshot suplex and an attempted pin, but he can't hold it for more than 2. Andros then caught Tobis in a sidewalk slam, and again went for a pinfall, but again, Tobis managed to kick out. Andros sent Tobis to the corner, and walked in, but Tobis used what reserves he had at the moment to catch Andros with a boot to the midsection, and then hoisted Andros up into a piledriver. Being slow to cover, Tobis was only able to get a one and a half count out of it, and Andros was fairly quickly back up to his feet. Tobis however began a charge, and he literally helped Andros visit all four turnbuckles to the joy of the crowd. A "Warrior of Fear" chant immediately began filling the Garden, much to the surprise of the announcers. Tobis then after scoring an atomic drop and a full clothesline, attempted to execute the figure four, but Master, up on the ring apron kept the referee occupied. Fischer came over to help out, and pulled Master down off the apron. Cordon then ordered Fischer back to his side of the ring, and while doing so, Andros was tossed a pair of knuckle dusters from Master, and put them to use on Tobis, literally knocking the Assassin out. Andros then covered for the pin and got the victory. Fischer was immediately up in the ring, and Patrick King trying to explain what happened to Cordon when Mean Mason came running down from the entrance, and slid in the ring, and literally ripped the knuckles out of the pocket of Master and nailed Andros with them. Fischer then went after Master while Tobis, getting helped back up by King joined in on the attack on Andros. Andros and Master were finally able to get away from the ring, and Fischer, Tobis, and Mason all shook hands and walked off together out of the ring.
