Kevin Flair =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= writer: DPK ----------------------------------------- Announcer Michael Cook: Ladies and Gentlemen.... Welcome to the hottest, most watched wrestling show on the planet (crowd pop!) The first match this evening on the MAIN EVENT is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, already inside the ring, making his UWA debut.... KEVIN FLAIR! (The crowd boos as Kevin Flair struts across the ring) Kevin Flair: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Rex Roseman: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Dan Crawford: GIVE....... ME....... A....... BREAK Rex Roseman: OK Crawford, take thirty, I'll keep everything under control. Michael Cook: And his oppenent... ("Enter The Sandman" by Metallica blasts over the loud speakers. Big Butch Thornton walks out with a angry look on his face. Big Butch comes to the ring wearing a pair of NWO style black Oakleys. He wears a black t-shirt saying "Big Butch F’N Thornton" in white letters on the front and on the back it say’s "Judgement Day Is Upon Us" in white letters. His wrestling attire consists of the Kevin Nash type pants, shoulderstaps thing. It is completely black except for the initials "BBT" in red on the stomach part. He wears white tape around his wrists and his red elbowpads. His boots are black. He stops in front of the ring and smirks at Flair. He takes his shirt and glasses off as he gets in the ring.) Michael Cook: From Detroit, Michigan, at 6"10 and weighing 345 lbs., here is BIG BUTCH THORNTON!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Crowd goes nuts) Dan Crawford: Big Butch Thornton looks ready to take on one Mr. Kevin Flair! Rex Roseman: Whooo!!! Kevin Flair is gonna take care of this joke Thornton tonight once and for all!!! Dan Crawford: What are you on?? After the comments Kevin Flair made to Butch and the Fearsome Foursome, I wouldn't be suprised if he is crippled after tonight! Rex Roseman: Whatever Crawford. (bell rings) Dan Crawford: And they lock up..... And surprisingly, Flair takes him down with a hiptoss. Butch looking a little upset. Rex Roseman: WHOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Dan Crawford: They lock up again, and again Flair takes Big Butch back down with the same move. (Kevin Flair begins to strut accross the ring.) Kevin Flair: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! Rex Roseman: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! Dan Crawford: And now Butch looking a slight bit more angry as Flair is surprising everyone here. Rex Roseman: Expect for me. WHOOOOO!!! Dan Crawford: They lock up again, Flair with an irish whip, Thornton into the ropes.... back off.... Flair goes for the hiptoss.... Rex Roseman: WHOOO...?What? Dan Crawford: Butch blocks it... And he reverses it and now it's Flair who is down. Big Butch Thornton: How's that you woooing piece of trash.. WHOOOOOO!!! (crowd laughs.) Dan Crawford: Flair getting up, and he's met by a clothesline from Butch that sends him right back to the mat..... Butch follows up with an elbow... and connects.......... Rather silent arn't we Rex. Rex Roseman: Don't worry, Flair is just playing possum. Dan Crawford: He's about to become a possum on the side of the road if he doesn't do something fast... But he's letting Kevin Flair get up on his own.. and Flair is punching in every direction... Look at this, Butch is laughing...Flair has absolutly no clue where he is at..... swinging aimlessly....OH NO... He just clobbered the ref.. Rex Roseman: Here we go!!! Dan Crawford: And Butch approaches Flair as he rools out of the ring... How did he know Butch was comming for him if he can't see. Rex Roseman: I told you Crawford. Dan Crawford: And Flair is strutting outside the ring... But doesn't see Butch climbing the ropes..... OH MY!... Butch just droped an elbow to the back of Flair's head.... And he grabs a ring announcers chair... Big Butch Thornton: How many of you want to see this piece of trash get hit with a chair. (crowd cheers!) Dan Crawford: Butch gets the OK from the crowd... swings the chair.... And Flair ducts... And he's strutting again..... But Butch nails him from behind... And Flair is laying motionless as Butch picks him up and throws him back into the ring... And the ref finally getting up... Butch grabs Flair by the throat... Here comes the Thornton Slam... NO..lowblow by Flair... And Flair now with the upper hand... scoops Butch up.. and slams him down.. and follows up with a quick legdrop. Rex Roseman: Put him away Kevin. Big Butch Thornton: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO.... It's time to put this nature boy wannabee away. Rex Roseman: Ahhh, I think Flair's playing possum again. Dan Crawford: Butch grabs him by the throat.... THORNTON SLAM!!! Butch pulls Flair back up... And here it comes..... JUDGEMENT DAY!!... Thornton just powerbomoed Flair almost through the mat... He goes for the cover.. ONE!!! TWO!!! THREEEEE!!!!!!
