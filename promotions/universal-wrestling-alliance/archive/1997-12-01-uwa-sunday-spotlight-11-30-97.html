UWA Sunday Spotlight 11/30/97

1 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=================== UWA Sunday Spotlight Hartford Civic Center Hartford, CT November 30, 1997 Live!!!!!! =================== ("Eye of the Tiger" by Survivor plays over the P.A. as scene opens to the Hartford Civic Center in Hartford, Connecticut...panning a capacity crowd...zooming in to Chris Fair and Rex Roseman) CF: Hello out there to all! Welcome to the UWA Sunday Spotlight! RR: Hey...good to be back in the booth with you Chrissy! CF: Come now Rex...let's not start already! RR: Ha ha ha! Shut up Fair! You're just Crawford's fill in. Everyone knows that I've got the biggest voice in wrestling...and I'm going to win that award for best announcer too! Ha ha ha! CF: I don't know about that. You know I've been nominated for that too. RR: Why would they vote for you? CF: You may be surprised who votes for me Rex. Before we get to the ring...let's go to "Live In New York", which by some technical difficulties did not air yesterday. But here it is in full color...yesterday's interview. Enjoy. (scene cuts to the Live In New York tape...Ron Donovan & Jonathan Tell enter as usual to a rousing ovation) RD: Thank you, thank you as always and welcome to Live In New York, I'm Ron Donovan. (cheers) JT: And I'm Jonathan Tell (cheers). And tonight we have a guest which, I gotta tell ya, I just don't know what to make of. RD: I know what you mean, Jon. He came to the UWA with an acid tongue, putting down some of the true greats of this organization, and then...he showed us all he could back up those words. JT: He has stated many times that he has no love for the fans, yet, he is developing QUITE a fan following. RD: As are all the members of the group he is affiliated with, Jon. Whether you love them or you hate them, you pay attention to them. JT: Without any further adieu, please give a warm welcome to tonight's guest, Mr. "Hollywood" Johnny Tambo! (the crowd gives a mix of cheers and boos, but the cheers seem to be the greater voice. Tambo walks out from behind the curtain in a VERY expensive Armani outfit. He has the UWA International Title draped over his shoulder. He comes over to Ron and Jonathan, shakes their hands, and has a seat. He looks perplexed at the response he is getting) RD: Well, Mr. Tambo. Sounds like you are getting a few more cheers than when you first arrived on the scene. HJT: I hear that, but as far as I see it, I really don't care if they like me or not. I'm their International Champion, and they gotta deal with it. (this gets a few more boos, but still some cheers) JT: Hollywood, your team of Solid Gold is having a really tough time lately with members of your rivals, Natural Currency. (again more cheers and boos) What is your thoughts on that team? HJT: Jon, baby, its like this. You take a guy like Casturated Troi. He has a sparse bit of talent, and is lacking in coherant interviewing skills. You all saw that rediculous crap he was spewing out about that homo thing. RD: Mr. Tambo, I was there the night that Sharon Stone was in your dressing room and the two of you went out on the town after your match. I can tell ANYONE who doubts you that they are sadly mistaken. HJT: You got that right, Ronnyboy. I have since dumped her, but I'm sure she'll rally. I bet she will show up in the camp of Natural Currency next. Thats where all leftovers and deviots end up. (the crowd says "Whooooah!") JT: At the last pay per view, a man from your past interfered in the match against Scott Daniels. What can you tell us about "Devine" Dave Stein? HJT: Some time ago, there was a federation called the RWF. It held a tournament for the vacant RWF World Title. I don't know who held the belt beforehand, or anything else prior to that tournament because I was busy in another federation called the GWL. Anyway, it seems that our pal, Dave Slime WON that tournament and became the champion. Well, a week or two after he won that thing he had the misfortune of stepping into the ring with the better man. That being yours truly. I pinned Slime in the center of that ring to win the World Title, and in every rematch we had, he could never bring himself to beat me. He got VERY mad about it, but also very desperate. In fact, here is something that only a handful of people know. Slime somehow got the unlisted phone number of "The Flamboyant One" and left a message on his machine stating that "He was in! and was anxious to become a part of Solid Gold." Well, you can imagine our surprise when he informed us that we wanted him in the group. Of course, we took the misunderstanding out on his hide. If I remember correctly, he and Cannonbald Maxwell took on me and either Scotty or Casturated. Memories of the end of that federation are rapidly diminishing thanks to my success in the UWA. RD: You have been very successful here in the UWA, too. Shortly after your arrival you challenged then International Champion, Crusher Stevenson (cheers from crowd) ...to a match for the title. To everyone's amazement, particularly Crusher's, you pinned him cleanly to win his belt. HJT: That was a great accomplishment for me. Scotty had been in this league a bit longer than I had. He had been telling me what was going on in this league and that the Fearsome Foursome were the top dogs and that Stephensuck was an arrogant ass who didn't deserve the gold. Well, I finished up my other commitments in Tinseltown and I came right to the UWA offices and signed up. The rest is, well I won't call it history since (holds up belt) as you see it still continues. (a few more cheers) JT: There have been alot of people saying that you have some kind of affiliation with President Gordon and that you have been given special favors and advantages because of them. HJT: I've heard that from a few of the copycat artists who reside in this league too, and you know what, its as bogus as their contracts. The reason that I am the International Champion and continue to be on top of the world here is that I am possibly the hardest worker in professional wrestling. I put my heart into my interviews and matches alike. When I go off on one of those ripoff putzes because they are stealing from already established stars, its for real. I can't stand that these unimaginative dinks have to use other people's styles and music, and what have you. Hollywood Johnny Tambo doesn't need that kinda crap to get where he is. THAT, my friends, is a major reason why I'm a champion and they are not! Cuz they are trying to be people who they are NOT!!! (many more cheers for that one) RD: In fact, you claim that Big Butch Thornton (CHEERS!)... You claim that he is the biggest of all ripoffs! (boos) HJT: Yeah, boo if you want, ingrates. But look at the facts. His theme music "Enter The Sandman" by Metallica. Gee, I wonder who has been using that music for YEARS! A guy whose name is IN the title. If the song were "Enter The Big Bitch" I wouldn't care that he used it. (laughs from the crowd) His t-shirt says "Big Butch F'N Thornton". Ya know, I have a t-shirt at home, given to me by the genius Paul E. Dangerously, that says EC F'N W on it. Any bets WHICH phrase was introduced first? Then on the back of his shirt it says "He's Not For Everybody". Another phrase from the back of an ECW shirt. Tommy Dreamer's shirt, in fact. Oh sorry it says "IT'S not for everybody". So I guess his slogan IS original! JT: While we are on the subject of Big Butch Thornton, you recently had a terrific match against him. HJT: Yeah, thats the one where I beat him from pillar to post, just as he asked. RD: I'll have to admit, you showed that you could hang with the former world champion. Something that not many can say. HJT: Well, its simple. When your best moves are power moves, you're lost. If you have no finese or no skill, you're a sitting duck to a REAL wrestler. Get the big lummox off his feet and he's as helpless as Gamera on his back. JT: I loved the Gamera movies on "Mystery Science Theater 3000". HJT: Same here, Tell. I bet if I sent a copy of a Thorndyke match to them they could work wonders with it. His and Stephensuck's match against Silver And Gold was a big enough stinkburger for Mike and the 'Bots to have a go at. RD: We are about out of time, Mr. Tambo, but I wonder if you could give us some thoughts in closing about the upcoming Four Way Dance for the vacant UWA World Title. HJT: Well, I touched on this a little bit in my interview after the big show last Sunday. Its an opportunity of a lifetime, and definitely a long time coming. Lemme just say that its great to be International Champion. But Hollywood Johnny Tambo is World Champion material. He's been there before, and he'll be there again. Rest assured of that. (crowd cheers, then a girl in the audience rushes on stage and gives Tambo a kiss) JT: Wooooooooahhh!!! A little early Christmas gift for our guest today! HJT: Can't wait to unwrap it! RD: Thank you, Mr. Tambo, for being our guest today and good luck in your quest for the world title. HJT: Thanks, Ron. Its been a pleasure. (the three shake hands as the lights dim...then switches back to Chris Fair and Rex Roseman in the commentary booth) CF: Strong words from...Ol' HJT, "Hollywood" Johnny Tambo. RR: What a busy schedule "Hollywood" has coming up! Take a look! He's facing the Eliminator this Tuesday...Sabbath on the 9th and Christopher Maxwell on the 12th! Two of those are title defenses! CF: Well...Johnny Tambo has enjoyed some great success here in the UWA. A couple successful defenses against "Malibu" Mike Madison. A pinfall victory over Crusher Stevenson...retained the title after a big match with Big Butch Thornton. Really has had some success... RR: And he humiliated the Horsemen/Brotherhood! Let's not forget about that? CF: How can I? Let's get to Michael Cook for the formal ring introductions. Ding! Ding! Ding! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following matchup is scheduled for one fall. ("Ride of the Valkery" by Wagner blasts over the P.A.) Michael Cook: First...from Bucharest, Romania...standing at 5'9"...weighing 259lbs...he is a former UWA Heavyweight champion....and accompanied to the ring by Caliban....VILE VINCE VIPER!!!!!!!!!! (boos) RR: How can the fans boo this guy!? CF: Not everyone is. Take a look at the VVV rooting section. RR: Yeah...and take a look! (holds up a green book to the camera) The unauthorized biography of Vile Vince Viper!!! 457 pages of Vince...a forward by VVV himself telling of his time in his mothers womb...ending in an afterward by yours truly of the future of Hell On Earth! You've got to read this! I can't put it down! CF: What! What is that on the cover? I can't believe this...they allow this cover in public book stores? RR: Well...in the back room away for the kiddies. This book is a complete history...and it tells everything. Vince's family life, sexuality, wrestling career... CF: Hold on...I don't want to hear anymore. VVV in the ring now and Flaming Bob has just crawled from under the ring...Kama Kazi on the other end. He's got some help down here. RR: Ha haha! ("Enter the Sandman" by Metallica plays over the P.A.) Michael Cook: His opponant...from Detroit, Michigan...standing at 6'10"...weighing 345lbs...BIG BUTCH THORNTON!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (crowd erupts) CF: And here comes the big man! With intensity in his eyes! Butch Thornton will not be denied revenge...and I can tell you that for sure! RR: Look at this! (another crowd pop) Rex Overlord out of the crowd! CF: VVV going crazy with panic in there! Flaming Bob and Kama Kazi are now backing away as Overlord has folded a chair and backed them up! RR: Thornton in the ring...now realizing the presence of Overlord! Ding! Ding! Ding! CF: This match has begun...and VVV running at BBT...but BBT with a boot up to the gut...THORNTON SLAM!!!!!!!!!!!!! (deafening cheers) RR: No! No! No! Stop the match! CF: Overlord on the outside with a wild swing of the chair...but Flaming Bob ducks it and tackles Overlord to the concrete! Both men double teaming Overlord! RR: In the ring...BBT is covering VVV!!!!!!!! NO!!!!!!!!!!! CF: 1 2 Foot on the ropes! I can't believe that Vile survived a Thornton Slam! RR: Now...BBT is up...going to the ropes...dropping a big elbow on VVV...and up again...saluting the crowd!!! (loud cheers) CF: Look at this! (all eyes move to the locker room entrance) IT'S SABBATH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (deafening cheers) RR: WHAT'S HE DOING DOWN HERE!!! CF: Overlord now fighting off Flaming Bob...and going toe to toe with the Cruiserweight Kama Kazi! And in the ring...what's this? VVV has just deposited a chunk of metal to the outside...and Butch Thornton is down and out cold in the middle of the ring! RR: Flaming Bob now turns around...AND HE SEES SABBATH!!!!!!!!!! (crowd eruption) CF: SABBATH WITH A SHORT CLOTHESLINE....and Overlord whips Kazi in Sabbath's direction...ANOTHER CLOTHESLINE!!!!!!! (cheers) RR: This is unfair! Where's Gordon!? He's never down here when his guys are winning the match! CF: They're only winning on the outside. On the inside VVV is staggering over to BBT to is down and out in the middle of the ring. But VVV falls on top of him... 1 2 Overlord grabs VVV's leg and pulls him off!!!! VVV is upset! He goes for the cover again! 1 2 Another interference by Overlord...and the ref is yelling at Overlord! RR: Look at this! Flaming Bob and Kama Kazi are up on the other side of the ring! And Bob has just put on a black whig and sunglasses....Kama Kazi in a blonde whig and they're in the ring beating on BBT!!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! CF: They were trying to dress up like Sabbath and Overlord...but it didn't work! Where they got those whigs I don't know! But...jeez...who would be stupid enough to fall for that!? RR: Overlord and Sabbath in the ring kicking Kazi and Bob off of BBT...and VVV has scrambled out of there! This is terrible! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner...as a result of a disqualification...BIG BUTCH THORNTON!!!!!!!!! ("Enter the Sandman" plays as fans cheer loudly) CF: Thornton, Overlord, and Sabbath now walking around the ring as members of Hell On Earth circle...and this is far from over! For Rex Rosemen...I'm Chris Fair...saying good afternoon! (fades to black) 1997 UWA Sports™ All Rights Reserved
