(UWA) Weekend Spotlight part 2/2

Do you honest think that you can beat the Monster tomorrow night. And then to further insult him, you haven't even been training at all. I can't wait for this one. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== PROMO: Mark Doom v. Damien Dredd ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== T.N.T: And neither can I as the Monster will get what he deserves, I can assure you that. But lets get to a more competitive match as Mark Doom takes on Damien Dredd. Rex Roseman: Dredd and Doom, they sound like two tag team partners or something. T.N.T: Both wrestlers have been rather quiet as of late. Could one of them be up to something, It's the quiet one ya got to watch you know. ==--==--==--==--==--==--== Mark Doom ==--==--==--==--==--==--== (The scene opens in a dark room. All of a sudden a match lights and is thrown into the background. A huge fire blares revealing a hunched over cloaked figure. The man wears black silk robes with a black silk hood which is large enough to cover most of his face. The man is covered in shadows and rests, unmoving. In the background the fire appears to have been lit on a pile of logs doused in gasoline. The room appears to be the main floor of an old wooden house. The walls that are visible appear to be rotting and decaying but no specific facts can be given due to the darkness. All of a sudden the cloaked figure begins speaking in a low voice) Man- One...Two...he's coming for you.... Three...Four...better lock your door.... Five...Six...grab a crucifix.... Seven...Eight...Stay awake.... Nine...Ten...MARK DOOMS BACK...AGAIN!! (As the cloaked man began reading "Mark Dooms back again" he flipped his hood up. Because of the shadows there were only two distinguishable features,his eyes. In the light they shone a greyish-blue colour. As the mans evil laughter fills the room the camera fades away. The words "MARK "THE CREEPING" DOOM... COMING SOON TO THE UWA" appear on the screen) (The scene opens in a dark room. Torches line the wall revealing a dirty cellar, covered with spider webs and rats. In the center of the picture stands a hooded figure. He wears a black silk robe with a hood that is big enough to cast a shadow over his face) Doom: UWA the time is getting near for Mark Doom to debut. I've sat around and watched long enough....now is time for action. I've been paying attention to the recent UWA cards and I am quite displeased with many of your so called superstars. Steve Aus- errr Chad Blake....you get on my nerves. Chad, wrestling is not your sport. You go and play house or make believe with your four fat friends and your drunken father and leave wrestling to people who know how. (Finally Mark Doom lifts his head a little and you can see the faint outline of a sneer settled in on his lips. His greyish blue eyes seem to pierce the darkness now as they stare at the camera with pure hatred) Doom: Now Wrestlingman...what kind of a name is Wrestlingman? And Malibu Mike? That's all I really want to say about those two. Down to the point though,I'm new here,I don't know any other wrestlers(besides the flamboyantly idiotic ones as I previously mentioned) and I need a match! Therefor I am taking on this Damien Dredd, what a joke. (Mark Doom slowly begins walking towards the camera until his face almost covers the whole scene) Doom: Beware of the Creeping Doom (Fade out) ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== END: Mark Doom Flash ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== T.N.T: I don't know what to say to that, just that if Doom is really serious about what he says he will do, we will find out tomorrow night. Rex Roseman: Just what we need, another demented freak who has nowhere to go so he comes to the UWA looking for sympathy. Just like Malibu Mike. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== PROMO: Malibu Mike v. Bad Habit ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== T.N.T: That explains the reason for your being here Rex. Rex Roseman: I'm here because the UWA needed a great comentater and that is that. But Mark Doom has proved that demented freaks have their say on this program, so lets get to Malibu Mike. ==--==--==--==--==--==--== Malibu Mike ==--==--==--==--==--==--== (Malibu Mike is sitting on a chair in his living room. He just got done watching Thursday Spotlight and begins to speak) Malibu Mike: Lordzero....I admit you are right I tried to rely on the Legion. Hell, your the one who wanted me to come in the UWA a few months back and join the Legion of Darkness. Dont even say you did because I have it on tape. You know your going to lose your match with Suicide so just take my offer and have the Triangle match...but I forgot your scared shitless of me. WEll I have a little suprise coming for you. (Malibu takes a sip of his Dr. Pepper and sits it back down) Malibu: Oh yeah....lets not forget about that little runt Bad Habit. I dont know why he keeps thinking he can beat the best. Well Bad Habit your little runty ass is going back to Lordzero and he can see what is waiting for him soon. (Sara walks in and sees the camera...she sits down and begins to speak) Sara: Desire, the only place that lether strap is going is up your ass. I got a suprise for you...and I am certain you wont like it one bit. (Camera "Pans" to Malibu) Malibu: Lordzero after Bad Habit your next. I will climb up the rankings so you will be FORCED to give me a title shot. and that belt is going to be around MY waist VERY soon. (Fade out as Malibu and Sara start to watch Family Matters) ==--==--==--==--==--==--== END: Malibu Mike Flash ==--==--==--==--==--==--== Rex Roseman: See, I told you so, now what kind of demented freak watches a show like Family Matters. If Steve Erkel were in the UWA, then even he could probably beat Malibu Mike. T.N.T: Well, I think that he is here, and he's sitting Rex Roseman: I know exactly what you where about to say, and I in no way find it amusing. But what will be amusing is when Maliu Mike's carcass is thrown around the ring by this man, Bad Habit ==--==--==--==--==--==--== Bad Habbit ==--==--==--==--==--==--== (camera pans to Bad Habit) Bad Habit: All I have to say is that Malibu Mike is a DEAD MAN this upcoming Sunday Night! And, unfortunately, so is his manager Sara! Malibu, this Sunday night you're gonna learn what the Legion is all about! Once you step in that ring, your worst nightmares will come true! I'm planning on hurting you. None of this technical wrestling crap. I'm a BRAWLER, and that's what you can expect...a BRAWL! Which move will I use on you? Will it be the Habit Breaker...or the Eraser? I guess you'll find out come Sunday! Sara, I hope you're ready too! Desire: That's right valley girl! That leather strap is gonna be plastered to your skin once I whip you! You'll be so mangled that even your PARENTS won't recognize you! Be prepared for HELL sweetie! ==--==--==--==--==--==--== END: Bad Habbit Flash ==--==--==--==--==--==--== Rex Roseman: You tell him Bad Habbit. First, Bad Habit is going to whip Malibu, and then Desire is going to whip Sara. It's going to be a great Main Event. Not to mention the the Monster is going to send T.N.T. running back into retirement, Lord Zero crushing Crusher Stevenson, and finaly, Chad "The Specimen" Blake destroying Big Butch Thornton and the Fearsome Foursome in the eight man tag match. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== PROMO: The Fearsome Foursome v. Chad Blake w/ three mystery partners ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== T.N.T: I would have to disagree with your prdiction Rex, but this is our main event of the evening and it is shore to be a great matchup. Rex Roseman: Well, Chad has told me that he will introduce his three team mates tonight, so lets find out just who they are. ==--==--==--==--==--== Chad Blake ==--==--==--==--==--== (Our camera finds former UWA Cruiserweight champ Chad "The Specimen" Blake outside his private gym in his luxerious mansion. His lovely valet Erin is in the background, in a lovely lycra one-piece that faults her great figure.) BLAKE: Oh great, you're finally arrived! Well, world, get ready, because you're about to see the best team $150,000 can buy! Let me assure you that the men that I have chosen that are currently training BEHIND THOSE DOORS (points at closed doors) are not in this for the money. I have screened them EXTENSIVELY, and let me tell me, they're as committed as I am to make those Fearsome Froggies look like the third-stringers they are!!! So let's take a look at Blakecorps, Limited, shall we? (Blake opens the door, and thge camera closes in on three men doing extensive bodybuilding.) BLAKE: First, let me introduce you to two of my newest friends. They seem to enjoy the high life as much as I do, and they are MEN enough to put half of their bounty up in the unforseen occasion we should fail on our mission. This here is Johnny Coals *points at him* and over there is *points* Sean Goldz. (Both WAVE to camera) Right now, I'm keeping them silent to concentate on their workout. I told them they can add their own thoughts if they feel the need to, so cameraman you may need to stick around afterwards. And this time, saty away from Erin's room, you perv.... Anyway, this here *walking to large masked man* is a fellow who ius known as the Masked Avenger. He contacted me and told me he had his own score to settle with the Fearsome Foursome and that the Foursome's current existence is in peril. Very succint. I like that. And quite powerful, too. I hate to think of why the Foursome would make a man like this upset. Well, I guess what you reap is what you sow. So FEARSOME FOURSOME, when you are getting your TAIL whipped by these three gentlemen here and myself, keep in mind that unions as corrupt and FILTHY as yours, however, WILL BE VANQUISHED!!!!!!! And and also, *in whispered tones towards the camera* getting athletes this great together for a mere $50,000 is WAAAYYYYY TOOOOOO MUCCCCCHHHHHHHH!!! (turning back toward group) All right, guys, keep it up! Say, who many of ya want shrimp or fillet mignon tonight?...." (With that, the door closes and takes the camera with it....) ==--==--==--==--==--== END: Chad Blake Flash ==--==--==--==--==--== T.N.T: So, Blake has gone out and agreed to terms with the two newcommers, Sean Goldz and Johnny Coals. On the outside of the ring are "Superstar" Scott Storm and his tag team partner Buzz Craven. Butch is wearing black sweatpants and a pair of black boots. Crusher Steveson is wearing red Nike shorts and a pair of black and red Asics wrestling shoes. "Superstar Scott Storm has on a plain navy blue t-shirt, tan khaki shorts, and a pair of Adidas sandles. Buzz Craven has black and yellow Iowa Champion jacket and black warmup pants with the Iowa Hawkeyes logo on the upper righthand corner. He has a pair of Nike shoes on also. Butch calls a break as the trainees head out of the ring. He begins to speak.) Big Butch Thornton: First off, Kevin Flair, next time you decide to step inside the squared circle with Big Butch Thornton you better bring some friends. You wanted to see what me and the Fearsome Foursome were all about, well it sure it you in the face!! (Butch swipes his hair out of his face and gets closer to the camera) Well Chad Blake, I have to give you credit where credit is due. You had the balls to challenge not only me, but these 3 asskickers to a 8 man tag match. It might have been gutsy, but it was pretty stupid. In Denver, Colorado, the night we finally are in the same ring fighing in the same match and you will have 3 partners you don't even know. Look at these 4 guys... (Points to Crusher, then to Scott and Buzz) These guys are like brothers to me. I know I can trust them. (Points his finger to the camera) Blake, I don't give a FLYIN' FUCK who the hell you bring to the ring with you Saturday Night. It doesn't matter Blake who your hired hands turn out to be because I know one thing is for sure.....their gonna get the ass kicking of a lifetime!!! I GUARANTEE IT!!!!!!!!!! Crusher Stevenson: It looks like it is gonna be a busy night for me Saturday. First I have the chance to fufill something that has been eating away at me for a long long time and that is to win the International Title. Lord Zero, you might have been able to get by everybody else here in the UWA, but I'm different. I don't plan on getting double teamed by your bat weilding thugs. I'm smarter than that. It's just gonna be you and me, one on one. Think long and hard these next few days. Think about what life without the UWA International Title will be like. You might not have to think about it too long, it will be a reality Sunday!! (Starts to scratch his chin) And second of all on Sunday I go into battle with Butch, and the D-Crew against that pompous piece of crap Chad Blake and whoever his partners turn out to be. I know Butch wants to get his hands on you and I wouldn't mind either. Blake.....PREPARE TO BE CRUSHED!!!!!!!!! "Superstar" Scott Storm: So me, Buzz, Butch, and Crusher have to take on Chad Blake and his partners Saturday Night. Well Chad Blake is a fine athelete. The self proclaimed "Specimen. Former UWA Cruiserweight Champion. Blake, you see me and you are alot alike. We're both 2 cocky SOB's. But you see the difference between me and you is that I speak the truth and you are way too FULL OF SHIT!!!!! (Scott laughs and gives a cocky smile) Me and my 3 partners make up the greatest collection of wrestling talent on God's Green Earth!! Sunday Night....Denver Colorado...The hottest ticket in town, the Fearsome Foursome, are taking no prisoners. You can bank on it!! HAHAHAHAHAHAHA Buzz Craven: After that bogus loss against the Ministery of Justice Sunday Night, it's gonna be nice to tear apart somebody. And who better then Chad Blake. Blake, you better surround yourself with 3 guys that will watch out for your carcass because we're going into Sunday like a bunch of crazed dogs. But we're not gonna have fun like L.T. would say, we're gonna kick ass first, and if we break some bones..maybe we'll have fun! Total Devastation is coming.......
