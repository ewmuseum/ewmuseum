UWA Saturday Night's Main Event: part1/2

28 July 1997

Saturday Night's Main Event 7/26/97 =============================================================== (The scene opens to a shot of all nine planets rotating around the sun. The camera slowly zooms into the third planet at an incredable speed. As the planet gets larger the blue and white of the earth can be seen. The camera zooms through the clouds overtop a night time view of Washington D.C. We are shown many sights including the White House, the Washington Monument, and finily the Lincoln Memorial where a UWA tsirt has been placed over Aberham Lincoln. The begining of the National Anthem begins to play for only a short period of time until it is interupted by the UWA theme music - http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4913/ACDCSHK.MID - The statue explodes as the scene changes over to an arena of screaming fans with fireworks exploding everywhere.) Dan Crawford: WELCOME EVERYONE. What an unbelieveable crowd we have here in the nations captial. Thousands upon thousands of screaming fans have jam packed the U.S Air arena to get a chance to see their favorite UWA superstar in action. They are all here for the return of Saturday Night's Main Event. Rex Roseman: And even I would have to admit that it's exciting to be back on live television. I have recieved thousands upon thousands of letters from all the members in my own fan club. Dan Crawford: The Rex Roseman fan club, good one. You only have one member in that fan club and I don't think that I need to tell everybody just who that is. Do you really think that anyone is going to pay you $99.95 just for a t-shirt. You couldn't even get members if you were the one paying them. But we certainly don't have the time for antics. We have six action packed matches lined up involving a lot of newcomers. Rex Roseman: [Yawn} Oh goody! ("Hot Blooded" by Forienger blast over the PA to a huge heel pop) Rex Roseman: OH GOODY!!! Dan Crawford: Oh No! Here comes the cruiserweight champ, Chad "the specimen" Blake. He isn't even scheduled to be here tonight. Rex Roseman: What a talent that Blake is! Dan Crawford: Hold on a second, he's walking down the isle pointing to us with Erin. What the hell does he want? Chad Blake: Give me a damn mic! Rex Roseman: Here you go Chad. (Blake takes the mic and climbs into the ring.) Chad Blake: Thanks Roseman, your the only one around here that has brains. Now as all you the fans here and at home know, I am absolutly the greatest wrestler in the UWA. Where is the competition??? I am getting sick of wasting my time with jokes. I am the finest talent in this rinky dink operation. Why don't TNT get his lazy butt off the couch back there and tell me where all this supposed competition is!! (Crowd Boos) Dan Crawford: The specimen is making a big mistake poking fun at the UWA president. He had better watch himself. Rex Roseman: Blake speaks the truth. There is nobody at his level able to give him a challenge in this federation! (Huge face pop!!!) Dan Crawford: Here Comes T.N.T. now. The you know what is about to hit the fan. (The UWA president, T.N.T, makes his way down the isle looking very angered. He is wearing a black UWA issued suit with is long, brown hair tied back into a pony tail. He walks up the ring steps and climbs over the top rope with ease.) TNT: I have heard about enough out of you Blake. Now go in the back before you say something you will regret. Chad Blake: Regret?? You listen here you has been, I am the future of the UWA. That guy I just fought barely gave me a sweat. Suicide is overated. Wrestlingman just sucks. Thornton is scared and probaly skipped the country because of- TNT: Blake, you just gave me the perfect idea. If competition is what you want, competition is what you will get. Cmon out Butch! Chad Blake: H....Ho....Hold up a second there. I ran Thornton out of this fed. He is long gone by now!!! ("Enter The Sandman" by Metallica blasts through the arena as the fans EXPLODE. Big Butch Thornton walks out with a angry look on his face. He is wearing a black sleeveless UWA t-shirt, blue jeans, and black boots.) Dan Crawford: Will you look at that! Chad Blake and his valet Erin have high tailed it right over the guard rail through the crowd. Blake wants no part of Big Butch Thornton!! TNT has the mic, let's listen... TNT: As of 2:30 this aftertoon, the suspensions of Big Butch Thornton, Crusher Stevenson, Buzz Craven, and Scott Storm have been lifted. They are back in the UWA!! (crowd Pop) Big Butch Thornton: It's been tough the past month with not being able to compete in the UWA. Wrestling is in my blood, and the best wrestling is here in the UWA!! (Fans start to cheer) Without being able to wrestle in the UWA was tough. But now I am back and I want to get in the ring tonight!!!!! (Crowd Cheers) TNT: But there isn't anybod- Big Butch Thornton: I don't give a damn who you can scramble up. I'm already in the ring and I plan on kicking some ass and taking some names!!! TNT: I'll see what I can do. Dan Crawford: TNT is going back into the dressing room as Big Butch Thornton is waiting rather impatiently in the ring. Rex Roseman: The last few minutes has been enough to make me puke! Dan Crawford: Well that's good to here. Just take your business away from the booth! Rex Roseman: Why I oughta... Dan Crawford: Do not go away! Beacuse as soon as we come back, Big Butch Thornton will wrestle his first match since his suspension. *break* commercials *break* Dan Crawford: Thornton is in the ring standing looking to the entranceway. We have been told that T.N.T has found some contenders. Lets go to ringside. =============================== Big Butch Thornton v. Zack Folley and Dyken =============================== ("MMM Bop" by Hanson plays throughout the arena as the crowd boos like crazy. A very unmuscular, clutz of a man named Zack Fooley walks out acting real cocky. He is in the isle dancing and falls down as the crowd laughs at him. He finally makes it to the ring) Who in the hell is that?? Thornton is calling for the mic... Big Thornton: If that is the best they can F'N do, send out somebody else!! (A very odd looking man walks out to no music. He is wearing a Pee Wee Herman t-shirt as the crowd is booing heavily. He is trying to act tough but the crowd isn't buying it) Rex Roseman: Who is that?? Dan Crawford: I am being told this guy calls himself "Dyken". So it looks like Big Butch Thornton will take on the team of Zack Fooley and Dyken! Rex Roseman: (sarcasticly) Butch is gonna get killed! Dan Crawford: (Looks at Rex) The bell has rung and the match is underway. Fooley and Dyken are charging at Thorton...He has them by the necks!! THORNTON SLAM!!! He chokeslammed both of those men at one time!!! Butch is laughing at these 2 men. Rex Roseman: They are probaly just late starters. Dan Crawford: .......Thornton is picking up Dyken, it looks like he is going for Judgement Day!! He has him up, he is tossing him out of the ring onto the concrete floor!!! Fooley is getting up and starting to punch Butch. I don't think those punches are having any effect though. Rex Roseman: Look what Butch is doing to them!! Dan Crawford: Zack Fooley is crying!! For god's sakes, a grown man is crying!!! Butch doesn't seem to mind though and is setting him up for Judgement Day...He hit it!!!! Fooley has just pissed in his tights!!!! Butch is putting one foot on him and going for the pin... 1 . . . . . 2 . . . . . . 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Butch Thornton has beaten the duo of Zack Fooley and Dyken! Butch is calling for the mic again... Butch Thornton: Those 2 prelim jokes weren't the competition I had in mind. But it was good to kick a little ass on my first day back. Blake, your days of vacation here in the UWA are over. Your worst nightmare is back!!!! ("Enter The Sandman" by Metallica starts up as Big Butch Thornton leaves the ring as the crowd is going wild) Dan Crawford: Well Big Butch Thornton is back in the UWA and one Chad Blake is in trouble! Rex Roseman: Thornton, you better watch your back. Chad Blake is gonna tear you apart! Dan Crawford: We'll see about that. (The crowd stans up and turns thier head) Dan Crawford: What now! (A large man dressed in blue jeans and a plaid shirt makes his way through the crowd and takes a seat in the front row.) Rex Roseman: Oh ya. I forgot to mention that Jean Guy Lavelle would be stopping by to check out some of the competition before he steps into the ring. Dan Crawford: Is there anything else that you.... FORGOT.. to mention. Rex Roseman: Ummmm... I forget. ====================================== "The Suplex Machine" Johnny Sharp v. The Fallen Angel ====================================== writer: JW ====================================== Crawford: Well, next up tonight is...(pauses) Fallen Angel taking on "The Suplex Machine" Johnny Sharp. Both of these wrestlers are making their debut tonight. This promises to be an exciting match. Both wrestlers are... Roseman: Both wrestlers are idiots! One is a Christian reject, while the other is just a Suicide wanna-be! UWA fans, now is a perfect time to go get some snacks, or take a bathroom break! Crawford: Maybe in your opinion, Rex. But I think most UWA fans are excited to see this new talent! Let's go to the ring and Michael Cook... (Camera cuts to the ring. Michael Cook is standing in the middle holding a notecard in his left hand, and a microphone in his right hand) Michael Cook: This next match tonight is scheduled for one fall! Let's introduce the wrestlers for this match... (Hard Rock music plays, and the fans cheer) Michael Cook: Making his way down the aisle, hailing from Las Vegas, Nevada. He is accompanied to the ring by his manager, JB. He is 6 feet 6 inches tall, and weighs in at 276 pounds. Here is "THE SUPLEX MACHINE" JOHNNY SHARP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Fans cheer a little more loudly, and Sharp makes his way to the ring. He and his manager JB slap a few hands, and then gets into the ring. He stands up on one of the top turnbuckles, and raises his arms, while the crowd cheers.) Crawford: Wow, the fans are giving The Suplex Machine quite a reception! Surprising for this being the kid's first match! (All of a sudden, the arena goes totally black. "The God That Failed" by Metallica plays. A faint spotlight appears up in the rafters. The crowd stares in amazement as Fallen Angel is being lowered down to the ring. His arms are outstretched like Jesus' on the cross. His eyes, a dark black, stare almost transfixed on Johnny Sharp.) Michael Cook: And his opponent, from parts unknown. He is 6 feet 11 inches, and weighs 325 pounds, here is FALLEN ANGEL!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Fallen Angel finally makes it into the ring. The fans are still in awe, and so is the referee, Roseman and Crawford. There is some cheering, but mostly silence. Fallen Angel takes off his cords, and goes right to the attack,) Crawford: Wow, Fallen Angel wasting no time! He just hit Sharp with a big clothesline that sent him to the mat. Angel, picking up Sharp and whipping him against the ropes...another clothesline! Sharp is dazed on the canvas. Roseman: Maybe I was wrong about this guy! Fallen Angel looks like he has SOME potential, but not as much as I do, of course! Crawford: Yeah yeah Rex. Now Angel, going to the top rope. He measures Sharp up, and hits Sharp with a big elbow. Now Sharp, rolling outside the ring. Looks like he needs to regroup and set a new gameplan with JB. Roseman: Come on Angel! Go get that loser! Crawford: Looks like he's listening to you. Angel bouncing off the ropes, and HITTING SHARP WITH A SUICIDE DIVE! Both men are on the outside, and it looks like JB even got hit! Now there's 3 men on the floor! The referee is starting his count one... two... three... four... and both men starting to get up. JB is still on the ground, holding his leg. Roseman: I just received the background on JB, Sharp's manager. Apparently, he is an ex-wrestler who was forced to retire after being hit repeatedly in the leg with a metal chair. This guy might need surgery, his leg looks like it's hurting! Crawford: You're right on that one. Now Angel is up first, and he picks up Sharp. WOW! Angel just whipped Sharp into the steel post! The ref is still counting seven... eight... and now Angel getting back in and out of the ring, saving the count. He picks up Sharp, and tries whipping him into the post again. NO, Sharp reversed it! Angel went face first into that steel post! That had to hurt! Now both men are out on the outside again. But this time Sharp is up. He picks up Angel and throws him into the ring. Now Sharp looking for some payback. He picks up Angel, and a belly to back suplex! Roseman: I guess that's why they call him the suplex master! Crawford: Sharp, measuring Angel, and an elbow to the chest. Now Sharp, pulling Angel to the steel post again, and slams his leg against the post! Angel looks like he is in pain! (While the 2 men wrestle, the fans begin to stand up and cheer. They point towards the entrance, where LordZero and Bad Habit are standing there.) Roseman: What's going on? OH MY GOD! The Legion of Darkness has arrived! Crawford: What? We haven't seen them since...since Patriot Games! What are they doing here? Sharp, picking Angel up, whips him to the ropes, and a BIG tilt-a-whirl slam! (LordZero and Bad Habit make their way to the ring. They walk slowly, and wear the traditional black leather trench coats and carry the aluminum bats. Sharp sees the 2 men, and begins to taunt them. They just stand outside the ring, pointing to the 2 men that are wrestling.) Crawford: I still have no idea what they're doing, but it looks like Johnny Sharp wants a piece of them. LordZero and Bad Habit are getting up on the ring apron! The referee is going up to them, to make them get off. Oh my God! What's this? (Hate and Anger come out from underneath the ring, with their bats. They pull Johnny Sharp outside of the ring, and begin to pound him with their bats. JB hobbles over to the 2 men, and he gets beaten too.) Roseman: I think the Legion have something up their sleeves! Crawford: You never know with the Legion. Now LordZero and Bad Habit get down from the ring, as Hate and Anger join them. The referee sees that Sharp is outside the ring, and he begins to count one... two... three... Angel is getting up, dazed. He is looking around, but he doesn't see Sharp anywhere. He's looking around, and the Legion is telling him to stay in the ring. eight... nine... TEN!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Crawford: Wow, he got counted out! Let's go to Michael Cook... Michael Cook: The winner of the match, as a result of a countout...FALLEN ANGEL!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (with that, LordZero, Bad Habit, Hate and Anger come into the ring. LordZero asks for a microphone.) LordZero: Hello UWA!!!!!!!!!! Jeez, some of you actually thought we wouldn't be back! Well, let me tell you, the Legion will NEVER leave the UWA! As the great Dan Patrick says, "You can't stop us, you can only hope to contain us!" Now, Fallen Angel, we have been watching you. Both your interviews and your wrestling tonight has proven that you would be a worthy adversary. We invite you to become a member of the elite...the Legion of Darkness!!!!!!!!!! (Wrestlingman makes his way to the ring, carrying a black trench coat and a black aluminum bat.) LordZero: We offer you the traditional clothing of the Legion. Take it, and with it, you will help the Legion DOMINATE this federation! Fallen Angel: A great light has shone upon me this day. I hold a tremendous amount of respect for the Legion. It would be a great honor to join you, and with you by my side, we will conquer all that dare face us!!!!!!!!!! (With that, Fallen Angel puts on his trench coat and picks up his bat. As he does, the entire Legion gathers around him.) LordZero: UWA...your doom is evident. IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The Legion all raise their fists, as a great flame engulfs the ring. As the fire goes out, it can be seen that the Legion, with Fallen Angel, is gone. Medics are rushing onto the scene, to help out Johnny Sharp and JB.) Rex Roseman: The Legion just keeps growing stronger. Who will be next? And will anyone be able to stop them? Dan Crawford: Well, there are the F.B.I run by Suicide, who will be wrestling later on. And there is the Fearsome Foursome who we just saw reinstated by T.N.T. And Mike Miller tells me that he just might have some info on more stables making their way to the UWA during his News and Rumors segment on the next Spotlight Weekend. Rex Roseman: There is only one reason to watch that show...... ME!.... I am the star. It is dull without me. Dan Crawford: You just may be right. I was excited when T.N.T. almost beat the crap out of you last night. But he would rather have that kind of action right here on the Main Event. Let's get down to ringside for our next match.
