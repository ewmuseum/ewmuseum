Spotlight Wednesday 7/22/97 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ========================================================= Lord Zero about: Scott Daniels, Johnny Walker, Suicide, Wrestlingman ========================================================== LordZero: What? The UWA? I'm still on vacation there! Well, I guess I could talk to the UWA. After all, I am the International Champion! Cameraman: Are you ready sir? 3...2...1...ACTION! LordZero: Well, Legion of Darkness fans, your International Champion is back! You may have been wondering where the Legion has been. Well, Bad Habit, Desire, Hate, Anger, LadyZero and I had some..."business" to attend to! What business you may ask? That's for me to know...you know the little quip. All that matters is that I'm back, and I'm ready to return and dominate! Now, I've been watching the UWA since I've been away, and I have to say...WHERE DID THE COMPETITION GO? Brad Force? Oh yeah, I hurt his back, and he isn't able to compete anymore! But he and I are on good terms, and he's doing fine. Anyway, as I was saying, I've seen some of these new wrestlers. Men such as "The Flamboyant One" Scott Daniels, and Johnny "Low Blow" Walker. Well boys, you have a lot to learn in the UWA! First lesson...the Legion is the DOMINATING force here! Now, there's 2 ways to learn this lesson. Option #1: You can accept it, and stay the hell out of our way. OR Option #2: Get in our faces, and try to take us down, in which case we'll beat the living hell out of you! Suicide, don't think I've forgotten you! You and your little FBI-ripoff friends are headed into a collision with the Legion! They may have changed their names, but underneath, they are still pussies! And you, Mr. Never Defend My Title, I have a little "proposition" for you...Since our little match at Patriot Games kinda got out of hand, there was never a winner declared. Sooooo, I've been thinking. On a date TBA, it's gonna be LordZero vs. Suicide...everyone barred from the ring...no countouts...no disqualifications...winner takes ALL...which means BOTH UWA Chammpionship Belts! Well buddy, are you up to it? And if you're not...TOO BAD! I'm already in negotiations with TNT! This match WILL be signed! Lastly, to our other Legion member Wrestlingman...GREAT JOB! I commend you for working alone here in the UWA, while we were away! It's nice that you got some payback on The Preacher...we'll be in touch my friend! Well, this interview is getting old. So, in closing, I will say one thing... IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ========================= Bad Habbit about: Chad Blake ========================= (scene: Chicago, Illinois. Bad Habit and his manager, Desire are in attendance at the Chicago White Sox game. While they are watching, a UWA Cameraman approaches them...looking for an interview) Bad Habit: You want an interview? You got it! Here's the scenario...Chad Blake, I'm gonna keep fighting you and fighting you till I get a clean match! I'm the #1 Contender for that belt of yours, and I intend to win it! I'm not even interested in that piece of *bleep* belt as it is! Cameraman: Umm...Mr. Habit...you can't say that on TV! Bad Habit: Too goddam bad! The truth hurts...live with it! Anyway, as I was saying, all I want is the satisfaction of knowing that I kicked your *bleep*! You go on UWA Spotlight, and talk your little *bleep* off about how you're the greatest and all kinds of *bleep* like that. Well, the fact of the matter is that you've never faced me and won a match cleanly! Which means, either you're a *bleep* that can't fight fair, or your *bleep* is scared! Now I choose the latter... So, I don't care who's *bleep* I have to kick, but I want a match! Now, if I have to fight Scorp, I will! If I have to fight Suicide, I will! If I have to fight my own guys, I sure as hell will! TNT, give me a *bleep*ing match, or else! And that's the bottom line, and it ain't cause I *bleep*ing said so! Now, can I get back to this game? Cameraman: But...Mr. Habit... Bad Habit: I said...GET THAT THING OUTTA MY FACE MOTHER *bleep*ER!!!!!!!!!! (Bad Habit grabs the camera and slams it on the concrete. As he does, he is recognized in Comiskey Park as being in attendance. The fans cheer as they see Bad Habit crush the camera. The screen fades...) =============================================== Hell's Angels about: Ministry of Justice, The D Crew, HMSS =============================================== (scene: Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Hate and Anger are at Rock Bottom, a popular bar in downtown Milwaukee. They are sitting drinking beer. A man, looking about 24 or so, asks Hate and Anger for an autograph. They oblige, and also give him a shirt. A UWA Cameraman catches this on camera, and Hate and Anger begin to talk) Hate: Hey, cameraman, get over here! Now, you may think we're all soft because we just gave a fan an autograph and a shirt. Well, guess what? We did it because we now have a FOLLOWING! People seem to be attracted to gold... (Hate and Anger open their trench coats and reveal their UWA Tag Team Belts) Anger: So, Ministry of Justice, HMSS, D-Crew...you guys still want a shot? Well, before we go any further, let's go to the tape... (video of UWA's Saturday Night Main Event is shown. Hell's Angels are fighting the New Men at Work in a locked locker room. Hate is shown hitting Sid into a sink. Anger is shown hitting Mitchell with a bench. Hate's pin on Sid is shown, and tape stops) Anger: After seeing that...do you guys think you can handle us? Now, for the new guys...Ministry of Justice...a little lesson. In order to "earn" a Title Shot...you must prove yourselves! Hate: Which means, making your way up the ranks first. Then, if you succeed, you will get a shot at us...an EXTREME match of OUR choosing...and NON-Title. If, and I mean IF, you succeed, THEN you will get a Title shot! Anger: So boys, and all UWA Tag Teams in general...you want a piece of us? Come and get it! We've held the Title since the UWA was re-instated, and we plan to KEEP it that way! (screen fades) ============================== Ministry of Justice about: The Fatal Clan ============================== [Screen image is enhanced…two men stand amidst the moonlit darkness a mighty river drifting behind them. They stand beneath a forsaken rail road bridge. There is little light, the men can barely be seen. The one farthest from the camera begins to speak. The voice is easily discernible…it is Judge Jones. He is accompanied by his partner, Minos.] JUDGE: Words, just chirographic gestures scribbled with transient ink on unwinding cotton fibers. Voices, just stuttered mumbles that fall on deaf and sleeping ears. The words I speak, my voice I hear…things soon forgotten. We make threats…idle or otherwise. We tell lies…blatant or trivial. We make promises…earnest or deceitful. But, we do not remember the threats, the lies, or the promises that we make…we remember their consequences. "I will beat you. I will destroy you! Blah, blah, blah." No one cares, don't you realize that? Can't you see? Or is the truth too blindingly obvious? I watch the interviews, I read the flashes…and they make me ill. Men with no hearts, no souls, no minds. They hide behind their words. They cower underneath their tattoos. They cry for their mommies from within their mesomorphic shells. AAAAAHHHHH! [a startling sound of breaking glass pervades the night.] MINOS: A match is what we request. We do no designate a team. The men, any men, who step into the ring are our enemy. One would assume that we have the right to challenge the Fatal Clan, and so it has to come to pass…a challenge has been made. JUDGE: Fatal Clan, I don't know if you're some militant faction of the KKK, or just a couple of Wu Tang style jackals…neither of which really concern me. That is our challenge. That was our flash. MINOS: Mala…in se. [screen fades to black.] ========================= Grave Tavern about: The UWA ========================= <Grave is walking around the streets of LA at night..> Grave: UWA...To understand the darkness that is my soul..I guess that you have to realize where I come from. I was your normal kid...but a life time of despear and pain came..and renched my happy existance. Time after time...people that I loved...died..right in front of me...making me colder and colder...till eventully..I didn't give a damn anymore. When I thought that life couldn't get anyworse..it did..and I was so cloaked in darkness..that I could not be reached... but then.. The Dark Angels desended onto me..giving me the power to turn my pain and despear into power.. This is the power that I use..day in..day out to crush my opponents in the ring. Other wrestler's pain..makes me happy. So now I am The DarkSider..and I have come to the GraveYard of the UWA..and I must addmit...it's pretty bare..and The Dark Angels have requested that I fill it up...and that is what I'll do. The time has come UWA.. you now have The Champion of The DarkSide Grave Tavern...in your ranks..and I asure you...Your Federation will never be the same!! But what you have to ask yourself is..who will be the first victem....who will be the fisrt to fill in the void of The GraveYard... I don't know...if anyone would like to step up and try it..they are more then welcomed...they are insisted. It's time that The Light of the UWA shine into the DarkNess..and when all The Dark Angels RISE...The Light of Day will turn into a Dark Cloud...and The DarkSide..will have control of the world...as we once did before. I challenge any..UWA Superstar to a match...or maybe the President would like to make me a match...I really don't care who..see I haven't been here long enough to make and enemies.. In other feds...I have enemies...and most of them have been crushed..and the others are hiding behind their words... I hide behind nothing..I am Grave Tavern..and I am the Enforcer of DarkNess..and I will turn the UWA..into my Dark Paradise.. So...UWA...Can you handle it? The DEAD Man COMETH!!! ========================== Scorp about: Malibu Mike ========================== {Scene opens with Scorp in New York, New York,. He is in a gymnasium jump roping.
