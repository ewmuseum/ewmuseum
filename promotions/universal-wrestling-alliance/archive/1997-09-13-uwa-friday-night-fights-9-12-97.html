UWA Friday Night Fights 9/12/97

13 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(‘The One' by White Zombie plays as footage of UWA superstars comes over the screen) (Malibu Mike and Crusher Stevenson facing off at Summer Showdown) (Johnny ‘Bad Boy' Coals climbing the cage) (Rex Overlord powerbombing the Sergeant) (The Royal Family standing confident in the ring) (Wrestlingman executing ‘The Complete Finish' on an unfortunate opponant) (TNT walking to ringside) (D-Man dropping off the top of the steel cage to the concrete arena floor) (The Sports Arena jam packed to the rafters with UWA fans) Friday Night Fights The Sports Arena Los Angeles, CA Live! Dan Crawford: Here we are again for Friday Night Fights! (Cheers) Hello everyone, I'm Dan Crawford along with my colleague ‘Red' Rex Roseman!! And we have quite a show for you tonight! ‘Red' Rex Roseman: That's right! Tonight ‘Hollywood' Johnny Tambo will put a hurtin' on Crusher Stevenson for certain! Ha ha ha! DC: Tomahawk will debut tonight against TCK Justin Allmond who defeated Crusher Stevenson in his debut on Tuesday. That's sure to be one tough match! Also tonight, Malibu Mike will face another victor from TNW, I'm talking about the big logger, Jean Guy Lavelle! RR: Well that'll be great Crawford. But what I'm waiting for is the debut of ‘Golden Boy' Lawrence Stevenson! And I'm confident that he's going to whipe the mat with Rex Overlord...cutting the length of Rex's comeback by a thread!! DC: Well, we know that the Golden Boy can talk the talk. Tonight we see if he can walk the walk here on FNF! Everyone here knows that Rex Overlord can do both! RR: Rex got an easy victory at Summer Showdown Crawford. But who was he up against? Sergeant Billy Bob Brown or whatever his name is! That guy is a loser, and tonight Rex Overlord will be too! DC: Let's get to ringside. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the first contest is scheduled for one fall and set for a 20 minute time limit. (‘Man In the Box' by Alice In Chains plays) Michael Cook: And now...from Chicago, Illinois...standing at 6'5" and weighting 265lbs...CRUSHER STEVENSON!!!!! (Crowd erupts) DC: Well, Crusher looks pretty good Rex. That's something that we couldn't say for TNW. RR: Big deal Crawford. I'm sure that ‘Hollywood' Johnny Tambo looks even better! (‘More Than Human' by White Zombie plays over the sound system) Michael Cook: And now...introducing...the self-proclaimed number one box office draw of Tinseltown...wrestlings greatest superstar...(rolls his eyes)...the toast of the coast...from Hollywood, California...standing at 5'10" tall and weighing 250 lbs...'Hollywood' Johnny Tambo!!!! (boos) (Arena lights go red...blue spotlights race around the arena) DC: And there he is! RR: Ha ha! Hollywood baby!!! DC: I'm reminiscant of Hollywood Thunder Rex. RR: That guy was a loser...this is the real deal man!! DC: Well, Hollywood making a rather mythodical approach...and now rolling under the bottom rope. And blowing kisses to the crowd! (Boos) RR: They love him! DC: You'd better get a hearing aid Rex. This guy is just too much. RR: Isn't he great? Ha ha ha! DC: Why don't you ask him that Rex? Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: This match is underway...and ‘Hollywood' Johnny Tambo standing on the middle buckle...and blowing more kisses to the crowd! (Boos) Crusher Stevenson is approaching from behind...and he's got him by the trunks! (Cheers) And he just pulled him down off the buckle now! RR: Hey, Hollywood's not ready! Can't he see that? DC: Crusher now has Hollywood backed into a corner...and he just spit right in his face!! That's disgusting!!! (cheers) RR: Your right Crawford...but these morons love it! DC: Tambo now ducking under the top rope...and Crusher is going to have to back off. But no! He's got Tambo by the legs...and he's pulling him out into the middle of the ring...Tambo on his back...Crusher holding his legs!! (cheers) RR: Ring the bell! Disqualify him! DC: Crusher looking left and right for the crowds favor...and they're giving it to him!!! And Crusher just put the boot to the abdominals! RR: Low blow! Below the belt! That's an automatic DQ! DC: I think it was high enough to be acceptable Rex...but Tambo holding himself at the groin...and maybe you're right! (Boos) RR: I am! DC: No, I think Tambo is making more of it than it really is! Crusher with an elbow drop...and he rises again...Tambo is down at his feet! (cheers) And Crusher going to the corner...and he just sat down in the corner of the ring!!! (Louder cheers) Ha ha! This is just too easy! RR: Get up Crusher! Fight him like a man! DC: And he's doing just that! RR: No! Stay down! Give Johnny a breather! DC: Now Johnny Tambo up to his knees...and he racks Crusher Stevenson! (Boos) I don't think the referee saw it!!! But now...Crusher falling to his knees...and Tambo with an elbow smash to the head of Crusher! RR: Brilliant! Sheer brilliance Crawford! Tambo setting him up...oooh....piledriver! Ha ha ha! It's over! Cover him! DC: Tambo does cover him! 1... No! Kickout with authority by Crusher! And Crusher is getting to his feet again...but a thumb to the eye by Hollywood Tambo. And a big right hand! RR: That was a forearm Crawford! DC: I think I know better Rex. Tambo sending him to the ropes...and catches him with a knee to the gut on his way back! (Boos) That was called a kitchen sink! RR: Huh? DC: But now...a kick to the gut by Tambo...and Crusher down again! Tambo is ruthless! RR: ‘Hollywood' Tambo...he's setting him up again...for the piledriver...up...up...up...come on... DC: No! A backdrop instead by Crusher Stevenson turns the tide! (Cheers) And a legdrop by Crusher! Crusher pulling him to his feet...and he sends him to the ropes...up in the air!!!! Military press!!! (cheers) Slam!!! RR: Get up Hollywood! Get up! DC: And Crusher has hooked him in the CRUSHER BREAKER human torture rack!!!! (Wild cheers) RR: Look Johnny Tambo has the rope!! DC: Oh my...Tambo holding onto the top rope...and Crusher stumbles backward...and he tumbles right over the top rope!!! (Boos) And Crusher is hurt on the outside...while Tambo on the ring apron...sliding back in the ring. And what's Johnny doing? RR: He's checking the turnbuckle! I think Crusher loosened it earlier! DC: I don't think so! Johnny Tambo is loosening the turnbuckle pad!!! Turn around ref!!! RR: The referee is counting out Crusher Stevenson...he doesn't see anything! DC: But now...Crusher getting back inside the ring at the 8 count...and Johnny Tambo is right there to meet him with a boot to the midsection! And an Irish whip to the corner...with no turnbuckle pad....oh my!!! RR: That'll teach Crusher to take off the turnbuckle pad! DC: Crusher Stevenson out on his feet...and Johnny Tambo is setting him up on the top turnbuckle...hooking him for a superplex... RR: That's not a superplex...it's his brain buster finisher! Ha ha ha! DC: You are correct Rex...HOLLYWOOD OR BUST!!! And it was perfectly executed!! Tambo for the pin! 1... 2... 3... DC: I can't believe my eyes!! RR: Me neither...err...I know it all the time!! ‘Hollywood' Johnny Tambo is on a one way road to the top!!! Ha ha ha! (‘More Human Than Human' by White Zombie plays again) DC: And Johnny blowing more kisses to the crowd...and they aren't liking him one bit! RR: ‘Hollywood' Johnny Tambo...hats of to you! You really pulled it off! Ha ha ha! DC: And Tambo now out of the ring...OH NO!!! It's the Royal Family running to ringside!!! The Royal King, Royal Lawyer, and Royal Consultant!!! And they're stomping the unconscious Crusher Stevenson!!! RR: They're making him pay!!! Crusher should never have taken that turnbuckle pad off! DC: But from behind...it's ‘Superstar' Scott Storm and Buzz Craven!!! And the Royal Family is out of here!!! (Crowd pops) RR: Those guys have no business out here! DC: And the Royal Family does? RR: Crusher needed to be taught a lesson. DC: Well, Crusher was lucky to have had someone here to watch his back tonight. RR: Yeah...right. DC: Scott Storm and Buzz Craven are helping Crusher Stevenson out of here...and let's get to Michael Cook with the next match. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is a non-title match...and scheduled for one fall. Introducing...standing at 6'8" tall and weighing 321lbs...from the Logging Camps of Quebec...Jean Guy Lavelle! (Some cheers) DC: Jean Guy Lavelle had a big victory of the debuting Chris Simmons on TNW...and tonight we'll see if he can keep that winning streak going. (HBK's music plays) Michael Cook: From Malibu, California...standing at 6'6" tall and weighing 275 lbs...he is the UWA International Champion...and is accompanied to the ring by Sensational Sarah...he is Malibu Mike! (Cheers) DC: Who's your pick Rex? RR: I'm going with Jean Guy Lavelle. Don't ask why. But I just feel that Jean Guy is the better of the two. Malibu fought Lord Zero and Crusher Stevenson for his title. He didn't fight Brad Force, or Immorality, or anybody good. He fought those two idiots. I have no confidence in him. DC: We shall see. Malibu Mike approaching ringside with Sensational Sarah...and she's looking...nice tonight. Malibu popular with the ladies as well. RR: Look Crawford! Jean Guy Lavelle just climbed the ropes...and he just jumped out of the ring on top of Malibu Mike! Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: This match has started...but both men are on the concrete...and I'm not sure who got the worst of that one. Malibu Mike or Jean Guy Lavelle? RR: Lavelle is too big for stuff like that! DC: I'm in agreement with you Rex. Malibu Mike is getting up now...and the referee can't count them out...because they haven't been in the ring yet! But Malibu has got a chair! Lavelle rising to his knees...metal chair right across the back! And Lavelle is back down! RR: That was good thinking by Malibu! They're on the outside...not being counted out...it was a good time for a chair! DC: Malibu now in the ring...and Lavelle is limping along behind. Lavelle hurt himself Rex...he hurt himself more than he hurt Malibu. That's just a warning to all of you big guys out there that are trying to move like a cruiserweight. RR: Lavelle in the ring now...and Malibu brings a leg down on the back of his head as he rolls in...and now he's got him by the collar of his flannel...dragging him into the middle of the ring! DC: Malibu has got him up now...jumping DDT and he's back down! (Mixed reaction) RR: And look at Sarah...she knows how to support her man! DC: Sensational Sarah pounding on the mat...really rooting her man on. And Malibu Mike is taking shots at that knee of Lavelle's. He knows which one is injured. RR: Malibu really has had an easy time of it. He took one big hit in the beginning but since then he's dominated. I really don't care who wins this Crawford. But I do think that Lavelle is a little stupid for doing what he did. DC: Well obviously Lavelle thought he could jump a little bit further than what he did...because he only hit Malibu enough to knock him down...so Lavelle took the harder hit. RR: Lavelle now is getting to his feet! (Some cheers) DC: And the crowd is cheering him on for his efforts! This man made a bad mistake...a mistake that rookies sometimes make...and he's probably going to lose the match because of it. And oh my...Lavelle couldn't keep his balance...he just tumbled again. RR: Beat him already! DC: Well...I thought for a minute that Malibu was just going to pin the guy...but he's raising him up...and he is going to finish him. Malibu hooking the head...going for the cradle suplex...turning it into a brainbuster! Wham! And that's called the WIPEOUT! 1... 2... 3... Ding! Ding! Ding! (HBK's music plays as the crowd cheers Mike on) DC: Well, I think Lavelle would have taken the pin without the finisher...but it doesn't matter now. Malibu Mike is the winner...and he looks good for TNW...the Royal Rumble on September 16. RR: He's looking good Crawford, but he's going in there with 29 other men. Even being the size that he is, the chances of winning that one is small. DC: You are correct. The chances are small for Malibu Mike...and for everyone that entered. Right now, let's get to Michael Cook for tonight's third matchup. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is scheduled for one fall...with a 15 minute time limit. First...standing at my left...he is TCK Justin Allmond!!! (boos) RR: Here's the man Crawford! The man that pinned Crusher Stevenson 1-2-3 on TNW! Nobody but me predicted it! DC: Well it was a shock to us all. But could he beat Crusher Stevenson twice? I have my doubts. And can he beat his opponant tonight? We're about to see. (Faces of Fear music plays) Michael Cook: From Pensicola, Florida. Standing at 6'8", and weighing 295 lbs...Tomahawk!!! (Mixed reaction from the crowd) DC: And wow...here he comes. This guy is a monster. RR: He is. He doesn't look quite this big on TV. DC: Tomahawk...held 3 AWF tag team titles. Now he's here for singles competition. I'm not sure that he needs a partner Rex. RR: Well we have seen upsets before. Justin Allmond caused one just the other night with Crusher Stevenson. I think this is a pretty even match. DC: I'm not sure that I can say the same Rex. Tomahawk now, getting in the ring. Here we see no fancy outfits, no glitz, no glamour. This guy has come out here to fight. But now...Tomahawk has got the microphone! Tomahawk: Tonight, my opponent is TCK Justin Allmond. I know nothing of this man, save one thing; tonight, I will make my enterence into the ranks of the UWA and he will be my sacrafice to the spirits. Tonight, the road to my destiny begins. TCK Justin Allmond, you know not what your future holds, but I can tell you, nothing but pain is forseen in your future. Tonight, The Tribe arrives in the UWA. (Mixed reaction) RR: Is this guy for real? DC: It certainly appears that way Rex. Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: There's the bell...and a lockup...Justin Allmond applying the pressure...but Tomahawk breaks out of that with a kick to the abdomen. Twisting the arm of Justin Allmond...and a big fist right to the back of Allmonds neck! RR: Wow. DC: Tomahawk now...lifting him up...atomic drop! (Some cheers) Tomahawk paying no attention to the crowd. But to the ropes now...big clothesline from behind knocks Allmond off his feet! RR: He's working on Justin Allmond's neck it seems. And he's hitting him hard. DC: Justin Allmond now just rolled out of the ring...the referee starting the count...but Tomahawk goes out too. Tomahawk has Allmond by the hair...and he scoops him...and he slams him on the concrete! Woah! (Mixed reaction) RR: Knocking the breath right out of Justin Allmond. The referee has stopped the count as Tomahawk just rolled back in the ring. DC: But now Tomahawk back out of the ring...and he throws justin Allmond back in. Now Tomahawk in...but Allmond to his feet...and he kicks Tomahawk! Tomahawk stops that quickly...with a leg sweep takedown. RR: This has the potential to be a great match. These guys are about the same size...really great. DC: Tomahawk now whips Allmond to the ropes...but a reversal by Allmond sends Tomahawk in...Tomahawk off and a BIG powerslam by Allmond!!! My oh my!!! 1... 2... Kickout! RR: Allmond just almost got another upset victory! DC: Well the near pin was obviously due to the shock of the impact rather than the pain of the maneuver...because Tomahawk is up and doesn't seem to be feeling it. RR: And Allmond is gaping! This Allmond is a newcomer to the ring! It's not fair putting him in the ring with this guy! Tomahawk with a chop across the chest of Allmond...and another...knocking him back against the ropes....now a headbutt! And Tomahawk to the ropes...coming off...big running tomahawk chop! (Cheers) DC: Allmond is down! And Tomahawk has got him by the hair...pulling him up...hooking his arms...and oh my! That was the Pedigree! RR: Who is that? DC: I don't know Rex! For all of you watching at home...we see another man walking to the ring...in moccasin pants and boots...and the warpaint. This guy is one of the Tribe. RR: He must be! DC: Tomahawk now has got his opponant up...and he whips him to the ropes...and the man on the outside just hit Allmond right in the back with that walking stick!!! (Boos) The ref didn't see it!!! RR: Allmond down...and Tomahawk is rolling him over! Now he's going to the top! I can't watch anymore! DC: Fist drop off the top rope! Tomahawk calls it the TOMAHAWK DROP...and fittingly I might add. Tomahawk with the cover. 1... 2... 3... Ding! Ding! Ding! (Faces of Fear music plays) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...your winner...Tomahawk!! DC: And now...Tomahawk and his partner in the ring...and they just hit Allmond with that walking stick again...beating this man down! RR: Well, I like their attitude Crawford. DC: Tomahawk now...with his assistant...leaving the ring...and he is victorious. RR: Just like I knew he would be. DC: Well, I'm impressed Rex. How about you? RR: I have no opinion right now Crawford. But I need an interview with that guy. DC: Okay, I'm getting word of something going on in the locker room. We're going to get our camera's back there. (Camera's show the locker room) DC: Oh! It's ‘Golden Boy' Lawrence Stevenson! He has dropped Malibu Mike with a chair...and now he's stomping him down into the concrete! RR: ha ha ha! I believe Malibu Mike has found himself a new arch-rival! DC: Here comes Rex Overlord and Wrestlingman! And Stevenson takes off! Wrestlingman getting down to look after Malibu Mike...and Rex Overlord is following Stevenson! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the next contest is our main event!!! (cheers) Introducing first...from... DC: Look! Lawrence Stevenson has just run from the locker room...and he's running to ringside before his music could even play! RR: What the... DC: And here comes Rex Overlord!!!! (crowd erupts into cheers) RR: And we're about to get this thing rolling! DC: Michael Cook doing to smart thing and getting out of there! These guys don't need introductions! They're ready to go! RR: Rex Overlord diving under the bottom rope! Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: And Stevenson stomping Rex Overlord...not letting him up...and a fist drop to the back...a big kick to the gut...and Overlord is to his knees...Stevenson throws a punch...but Overlord catches his fist!!! (cheers) RR: But Stevenson kicks him instead! Ha ha ha! A lot of guys would have panicked right there but not Lawrence Stevenson! DC: Overlord once again doubled over and on his knees...Lawrence Stevenson going to the top...and Overlord rises...double axe-handle hits Overlord straight in the forehead...and sends the big man staggering! RR: Stevenson going to the top on the other corner...coming off...and a big bulldog takes Overlord down!! Yes!!! This is the first time I've seen the big man in pain since his match with Vile Vince Viper in January!!! DC: Overlord down and maybe out! (Boos) I can't believe this! I've never seen a man who has been able to drop Rex Overlord so soon in the match! And a big legdrop to the back of the head...and a cover! 1... 2... Kickout with authority sends Lawrence Stevenson flying out of the ring! (Cheers) RR: And Rex Overlord follows him out! He's hurt Crawford! DC: Well so is Stevenson...now getting up off the concrete...and a big right hand by Overlord sends Stevenson back into the railing! And Rex throws him into the ring post! RR: Ouch! And Rex has got a chair! Stop this match!!!! DC: Rex Overlord smashing the chair over Lawrence Stevenson's head! And he rolls him back in the ring again! And Overlord covers him! 1... 2... No! (Boos) RR: Overlord will have to do better than that to get my man Lawrence down! DC: Rex Overlord has got him up...and he slams him down! (Cheers) Raising him up again...big right hand sends Stevenson back against the ropes...and a big boot to the face sends him down to the mat! The cover... 1... 2... No... RR: And Overlord has him by the hair again...raising him to his feet...and here comes Malibu Mike! DC: Malibu Mike running to ringside...and Overlord just let Stevenson drop...and he's getting out of the ring! And Stevenson saw him...and he's getting out of the ring! Malibu Mike has jumped Lawrence Stevenson but Rex Overlord just pulled him off...and a big right hand sends Malibu down! RR: What was that for? DC: Malibu obviously angered about Stevenson's attack...and Overlord just threw Stevenson back in the ring! Chokeslam on the Stevenson! It's over!!! 1... 2... No!!!! I can't believe it! Stevenson wouldn't go down to the chokeslam! RR: Ha ha! DC: And Malibu Mike is in the ring again...and he just attacked Lawrence Stevenson!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: Lawrence Stevenson wins!!!! DC: Malibu Mike now going to the top rope...big elbowdrop on Stevenson...and Overlord has just backed into the opposite corner...obviously frustrated. RR: And he has a right to be! But it doesn't matter! Stevenson wins! Rex loses! Just like the Golden Boy said he would! Ha ha ha! DC: And Overlord is out of the corner...and he pushes Malibu Mike away. And Rex is telling him that he wants to do the beating. Malibu backs away...and Overlord is setting him up for the Spineslayer powerbomb!!! But here comes the Royal Family!!!! RR: Yes! Save Lawrence! DC: And Wrestlingman from behind in the locker room just drops the Royal Lawyer...and he's hammering him with left and rights in the aisle! Malibu Mike and Rex Overlord are trying to keep the Royal Family from getting in the ring! And the Ballplayer just fell to the floor! Now...the Royal King standing on the ring aron...and Overlord and Mike are punching and kicking him off...and the Royal King falls to the concrete! RR: But Lawrence Stevenson is out of there at least...with a victory. DC: Let's get the official word. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...your winner...as a result of a disqualification....'Golden Boy' Lawrence Stevenson!!!! (Boos) DC: And the Royal Family along with Lawrence Stevenson are going back to the locker room...and he's got to be satisfied at least in part that he got out of there. Wrestlingman has joined Malibu Mike and Rex Overlord in the ring. And Rex walks between them...and he raises their hands into the air!!!! (crowd cheers) (‘The Mirror' by Dream Theater plays) RR: How sickening can you get? DC: Well, I believe an alliance is in the making. All these guys know what it's like to have gold. A fitting alliance it is. RR: Blah blah blah blah blah blah blah. DC: Well...as the three leave the ring...we leave you as well. RR: I'll see you on Sunday Spotlight along with Chris Fair! DC: And remember...September 16th, Royal Rumble, Tuesday Night War! The winner will meet Big Butch Thornton at Stampede. Get your entries in if you haven't already...and order this pay per view if you haven't already...it's sure to be a spectacular event. Alright everyone, Rex will see you on Sunday. I'll see you right here on Tuesday for TNW. Until then, goodnight from The Sports Arena! 1997 UWA Sports™
