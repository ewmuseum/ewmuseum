PT. 2 UWA Slaughter House

7 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
("The Mirror" by Dream Theater blasts over the P.A as the fans rise from their seats cheering wildly) Michael Cook: Introducing...from the Urbish Mines...standing at 6'11"...weighing 326lbs....he is accompanied to the ring by the lovely La Bella Donna Di Notte...here is the former UWA Heavyweight champion.......REX OVERLORD!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Donna Di Notte emerges from the curtain wearing black jeans and a black Christmas Chaos '96 T-shirt. She walks ahead...long brown hair down her back...giving a small smile to the fans. Rex Overlord emerges next in a black singlet, black boots, black elbow bands. He follows Donna...then stops and salutes the crowd ala N.O.D....the crowd cheers and he continues to the ring...his face one of intensity...his look being one of stone) Micheal Cook: And his opponent..... ("Elimination" by Overkill starts playing. The Stalker comes out. He walks down to the ring.) Micheal Cook: Weighing in at 315 pounds.....The Stalker! (The Stalker grabs a mic) Stalker: Ladies and gentlemen...I'd like to welcome back an old friend. Returning to the corner of the Stalker...he is Mr. Tanaka!!!! (the crowd cheers as Mr. Tanaka walks from the curtain and down the aisle...slapping hands with a few fans...he gets in the ring and shakes hands with the Stalker...turning to wave to the crowd once more and goes to the outside) DING-----DING-----DING DC: There's the bell and we're underway. RR: The lock up...Stalker with a knee the midsection and spinning NECKBREAKER! DC: Takes Overlord down quickly. But Overlord back up...and now Overlord with a belly-to-belly suplex on the Stalker. RR: And now Stalker is back and they're eyeing each other. DC: Lock up. Overlord whipped into the ropes, comes off and nails Stalker with a knee to the midsection, keeps going, off the ropes and massive elbow drop by Overlord! RR: But, again, Stalker is back up! DC: Lock up...Stalker now getting the upperhand. Stalker with a hiptoss. Overlord up, but is sent back down with a boot to the head. Overlord back up...Stalker picks him up and POWERSLAM! RR: Stalker is in control, going for the pin, 1........2....no. Overlord kicked out. DC: Both back up, Overlord grabs Stalker and just pushes him to the canvas. Overlord sitting back against the ropes, catching a breather. RR: Stalker back up and going after Overlord. Overlord with a kick to the midsection and a forearm to the head. Stalker stumbles back. DC: Overlord charges and nails him with a closeline! RR: Stalker now in trouble. DC: Overlord picks Stalker up and POWERSLAM! Picks him up again and vertical suplex. RR: He covers...1.......2....NO! Stalker got out of it. DC: Overlord has Stalker up...SHORT CLOSELINE! RR: Stalker is hurt! DC: Overlord picks up Stalker, knee to the midsection....POWERBOMB! RR: It's OVER! DC: 1...........2...............3!! DING-----DING-----DING Micheal Cook: The winner of this match, Rex Overlord! DC: Short match as Overlord destroyed Stalker in the end. RR: When you have two guys live Overlord and Stalker fight it out, you know it's gonna be short and action packed. DC: Here's the replay. Here's Overlord charging at Stalker, nailing him with the closeline. Here's Stalker picking up Overlord and powerslamming him. And here's the finish, Overlord with a powerbomb for the one, two, and three. You're winner Rex Overlord. RR:"Yes its now time for the greatest International champion this league ever had taking on the biggest loser this league ever had,and Hollywood is going to kick some BLEEP!" DC:"Well we don't know that yet,Rex,but he just might!" RR:"No I know for sure,Crawfish,that he is going to walk out still champ!Hahahahahahahaha!" DC:"Let's head back down to ringside,for the intro's!" Michael Cook:"This match is scheduled for one fall,and is for The UWA International Belt!"(The fans boo loudly) (The fans all hold up signs showing their disapproval of both participants in this match) Michael Cook:"Coming to the ring at this time he stands 6"3 and weighs in at 235lbs he is the self proclaimed greatest wrestler in the world Eliminator. (Who shot ya by the Notorious B.I.G starts to play as Eliminator and Kelly o Dell come out. Eliminator has a big grin on his face as he walks toward the ring, and stops half way staring into the crowd with a smile on his face. He stands there for ten seconds, and continues to the ring, enters, and grabs the mic.) Eliminator:I have a big surprise here tonight for our pal Johnny Tambo, but first. (He takes off his Horsement shirt to reveal a Solid gold t shirt,then imitates Johnny Tambo and Scott Daniels, and he starts running around the ring like a mad man. Then he takes the solid gold t shirt off, and burns it.) Eliminator:That's what I think of those idiots. But now for my surprise. (Eliminator points into the crowd signaling someone to the come to the ring, a huge man wearing a solid gold shirt and a hood over his head come down to the ring) Eliminator: Oh my who could this be? We will find out later. (He drops the mic) ("More Human Than Human"by White Zombie,plays over the PA,and the fans boo loudly) Michael Cook:"And his opponent,from Hollywood,California,5"10,weighing in at 250lbs,here is The UWA International Champion,"HOLLYWOOD"JOHNNY TAMBOOOO!"(The fans boo loudly) ("Hollywood"Johnny Tambo comes out,wearing his usual attire."Hollywood"Johnny Tambo comes down to the ring,shaking his head at the fans,while smiling.Then Johnny Tambo climbs up the steps,climbs into the ring,and raises his arms in the air,as the fans continue to boo loudly."Hollywood"Johnny Tambo removes his pre-match attire,he hands it out to the ring attendant,and he removes his belt,and hands it to the ref.The referee raises the belt in the air,and then he hands it out to the ring attendant) Ding Ding Ding RR:"Kick his BLEEP bigtime Hollywood,for him making fun of you guys,and don't worry about that mystery guy,because he isn't nothing but a wuss,I bet!" DC:"Well the match is now underway!They both lock up,and The Eliminator shoves Johnny Tambo down to the mat,and he is now flexing his muscles,with his back turned to Hollywood!Johnny Tambo is now up,and he turns The Eliminator around,and now Johnny is jabbing The Eliminator with rights,and lefts!Johnny Tambo grabs The Eliminator,and down with a side russian legsweep,the cover... 1... kick-out,by The Eliminator!" RR:"Now that was a way to slow count,by the dumb ref,come on ref get with it!"Hollywood"Johnny Tambo is now up,he grabs The Eliminators legs,he pulls them apart,and he drops his head down into The Eliminators you know what,if he has one,which I bet he doesn't!Yeah come on Hollywood,kick his BLEEP!Johnny Tambo picks up The Eliminator,he sets up between his legs,and he powerbombs The Eliminator back down to the mat,and another cover by the man... 1... 2... oh come on ref,your slow counting again,get with it,or I'll ref the match,punk!" DC:"Oh for god sake Rex,he's counting at a perfectly normal speed!Johnny Tambo picks up The Eliminator he chucks him to the ropes,The Eliminator comes back,and Johnny Tambo now has a sleeperhold on The Eliminator!The Eliminator is going down,it looks like this match is going to end,and fast!The ref lifts The Eliminators arm,and it drops,again,and it drops,a third time,but Johnny Tambo let the hold go,and he touche's The Eliminator's head with his pinky,and The Eliminator sinks down to the mat,fast asleep!Another cover,by the champ... 1... 2... 3,no,close one,but The Eliminator got out of it!Johnny Tambo is back up,and he climbs up to the top rope,as The Eliminator is slowly getting up!The Eliminator turns around,and Hollywood jumps off of the top rope,and he does a missle dropkick on The Eliminator,no,no,no,The Eliminator grabs Johnny Tambo by the legs,and he is spinning him around!What the hell is Hollywood doing?Hollywood jumps up into the air,and he does a hurricarana on The Eliminator!HOLY COW,WHAT A MOVE BY THE INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION!" RR:"Yeah way to go Johnny,kick his butt!Hollywood is now gloating to the fans,as the ref is checking on The Eliminator!What the heck,The Mystery Man just grabbed Johnny Tambo,and he is bringing him outside the ring,as the ref is still checking on The Eliminator!Oh come on ref,get with it!The Mystery Man is now removing the protective pads,he sets up Johnny Tambo,between his legs,and he piledrives him down to the concrete floor!The Mystery Man is now pulling off his hood,holy BLEEP!It's Mortikus!Mortikus is now stomping down at Johnny Tambo,but yes,yes,yes,yes!Here comes Scott Daniels,and Chris Simmons with chairs,and their running down to ringside!They both nail Mortikus in the back of his knees with the chairs,and Mortikus crumples to the floor!Scott Daniels,and Chris Simmons are now stomping down on Mortikus,as Johnny Tambo is getting up!Hollywood rolls into the ring!" DC:"Johnny Tambo is now shaking the cobwebs off,and he picks up The Eliminator!Johnny Tambo puts The Eliminator onto the top rope,and he climbs up to the top rope,as well!Johnny Tambo picks up The Eliminator,and he does The Hollywood or Bust on The Eliminator,brainbuster off of the top rope!Tambo with the cover... 1... 2... 3!!!" Ding Ding Ding ("More Human Than Human"by White Zombie,plays over the PA,and the fans boo loudly) Michael Cook:"The winner of the match,by pinfall,and Still UWA International Champion,"Hollywood"Johnny Tambo!"(The fans boo loudly) DC:Scott Daniels,and Chris Simmons are now in the ring,raising Johnny Tambo's arms in the air!The ref is helping The Eliminator,and Mortikus to the back,and they are now gone through the curtains!WAIT A MINUTE,JOHNNY'S MUSIC JUST STOPPED!" (DX's Music,plays over the PA,and the fans boo like mad) RR:"What the hell do they want?Here comes "Devestating"Danny Daravo,The Mutinizer,and The Demolishers!They all get in a row,and they just did the double crotch chop to Solid Gold,and Danny Daravo is continuing to do it,to Johnny Tambo!Danny Daravo is making the sign that he wants Hollywood's belt around his waist!Oh come on,Danny,there is no way in hell your going to beat Hollywood,because no one can,and no one will!" (Degeneration X leave through the curtains,with their arms in the air.Solid Gold all climb out of the ring,and run to the back,after DX) DC:"Well we have just been informed that DX has left the building,so Solid Gold won't be able to get them tonight!But why does Danny Daravo want the International Belt?" RR:"I don't know?But he isn't going to win it,not while Hollywood is the champ,and that's for sure!" Michael Cook:"This match is scheduled for one fall!"(The fans go nuts) ("Ravishing"Rick Rude's Music,plays over the PA,and the fans boo loudly) Michael Cook:"Entering the ring first from Miami,Florida,weighing in at 265lbs,6"5,accompanied to the ring by "Dazzling"Danielle,here is the Past,the Present,and the Future of the UWA,"Devastating"Danny Daravo!"(The fans boo loudly) DC:"Well that's a very original introduction,huh?" RR:"Oh shut-up Crawfish,because if you don't shut-up Big Bitch Thornton won't be the only one getting his BLEEP whooped!" ("Devastating"Danny Daravo walks out of the curtains,by pushing the curtains aside,as "Dazzling"Danielle follows."Devastating"Danny Daravo is wearing his blue robe,which has Devastating on the back,blue pants on under,which have Devastating down the left leg,and Danny Daravo down the right leg,blue boots,and his hair is slicked back."Dazzling"Danielle is wearing a blue dress,blue high heels,and her hair is tied back in a ponytail.They both climb up the steps,then "Devastating"Danny Daravo holds the bottom rope open for "Dazzling"Danielle as she climbs into the ring,and then he climbs into the ring after her."Dazzling"Danielle removes "Devastating"Danny Daravo's robe,she hands it out to the ring attendant,and she climbs back out of the ring,as Danny Daravo does the double crotch chop to the fans) DC: "Devastating" Danny Daravo definitely looks ready but he has possibly the biggest matchup in his young UWA set against the former champion and a man who won't be happy unless the title is around his waist again in Big Butch Thornton. RR: Thorndyke is a never-has-been Crawford. I see a big win for Danny tonight! DC: I am sure this sold out crowd will beg to differ. ("Enter The Sandman" by Metallica starts to blast over the loud speakers.) DC: Here we go! Michael Cook: Hailing from Detroit, Michigan, standing at 6"10 and weighing 345 lbs., here is the former UWA Heavyweight Champion........... BIG BUTCH THORNTON!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The fans explode as Michael Cook yells out the name of the former champ. "Enter The Sandman" continues to play and as the opening part of the song ends, the curtains ruffle and Big Butch Thornton steps out from behind them, with a angry look on his face. He is wearing a black t-shirt that reads "Big Butch F'N Thornton" in white letters on the front and in firery writing on the back it say's "The Original Asskicker", the black pants/black singlet thing ala Kevin Nash with BBT written in red on the chest, black and red elbowpads, and black boots. He points to Daravo in rage as he is halfway down the aisle then sprints to the ring and gets inside.) DC: oh my, here we go. DING-----DING----DING DC: Thornton already in the ring pummeling Daravo! RR: Thronton doesn't stand a chance against Daravo! DC: Thornton picks Daravo up and body slam. Picks him up and another body slam. Here we go! ANOTHER BODY SLAM! Daravo is holding his back. Three powerful body slams by Thornton. RR: Thornton with Daravo, whips him into the ropes and big boot to the head! DC: Thornton drops a knee across the head of Daravo, and he retreats outside to catch a breather. RR: Daravo's thinking out there. That's how he'll with this match, by using his brain, something Thronton can't do. DC: Daravo rolls back in the ring...Thornton grabs him and sends him across the ring with a hiptoss! Thornton follows after him. Grabs him and tosses him into the corner. Thornton now pummeling Daravo with punches to the head an stomach! RR: Come on Daravo! Hit him! DC: Thornton now placing Daravo on the top turnbuckle. He's up on the SECOND TURNBUCKLE! SUPERPLEX BY THORNTON! UNBELIEVABLE!! He did a superplex off the second turnbuckle and the power behind it sends Daravo rolling to the outside again! Thornton posses for the crowd as the go wild!!! (Crash and Bash come to ringside.) RR: Ha ha, here come the calvary! haha. DC: What's going on here!? (Crash and Bash are over with Daravo, making sure Thornton doesn't come outside and attack Daravo.) RR: See. They're just helping out Daravo. DC: For now, but how long will it take before they get involved. RR: Darav now back up on the apron. DC: Thornton comes over, Daravo drills his shoulder into the stomach of Thornton. Over the top rope into a sunset flip. Thornton just standing there! Thornton drops down but Daravo moves! Daravo up and kicks Thornton in the back of the head! RR: here we go, it's the end for Thornton! Daravo stomping away at Thornton. Thornton on his knees. Daravo grabs his arm...spins around into some kind of roll-up...1......damn! DC: Thornton easily kicked out of that roll-up. Thornton back up, Daravo LOW BLOW! Come on ref! Thornton goes down as Daravo kicked him low!. RR: Daravo going to the top......MOONSAULT! COVER.....ONE........... .....TWO......KICKED OUT! DC: Almost as Thornton able to kick out. Daravo up and he's stomping away at Thornton! RR: He's pulling Thornton towards the turnbuckle. He goes under the ropes and pulls Thornton's legs to the ring post! DC: He's setting him up......FIGURE FOUR LEGLOCK AROUND THE RING POST!! RR: HAHA! Thornton is yelling! DC: The ref is counting! If Daravo doesn't let go he'll get counted out...he let's go and get's back in just in time. And now he's stomping away at Thornton who's trying to get up.
