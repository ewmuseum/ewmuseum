DC: Hello Greensboro! And hello to all our viewers! Welcome to WAR!!!! (cheers) I'm Dan Crawford and this is "Red" Rex Roseman at my left. We've got a HUGE card for you here tonight. RR: Oh yeah...Vile Vince Viper will come back after his FNF fluke loss and humiliate that moron Ice-Pick! Goliath and Scott Daniels will go at it! Johnny Coals will permanently injure those ribs of Malibu Mike...sending him back to the WOWA where the UWA sends those guys that just don't cut it! DC: Ha ha ha! RR: Crusher Stevenson will defend the International title against Johnny Tambo and in doing so will lose the International title. Anything and everything is going to happen...right here...tonight!! DC: Well, this is the big one. Tuesday Night War airing from Greensboro, North Carolina. Let's get to Michael Cook who's going to bring us the first match. Ding! Ding! Ding! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is a submission match scheduled for one fall! (Cheers) ("Ride of the Valkery" by Wagner plays over the sound system) DC: And that music can only mean one thing! RR: Vile Vince Viper!!!!! Ha ha ha! DC: Before we bring you this... RR: The footage!!!! DC: I'm getting to it Rex. Many of you watched the Sunday Spotlight yesterday afternoon. It ended with Big Butch Thornton winning and successfully defending his title against Mage. But what you did not see was the following. ----(Screen shows footage from Sunday Spotlight)---- DC: Okay...we've got Vile Vince Viper coming down to the ring here. RR: Roll the cameras! DC: I think...I don't know. I think maybe they've cut to commercial. In case...in case you can see us at home...if your still tuned in...Vile Vince Viper has come from the crowd here and is climbing in the ring. RR: We're not on Crawford. DC: Hello? Am I on? (Vile Vince Viper walks out wearing an "Icon of Extreme" T-shirt and some black snakeskin pants. He slides into the ring and grabs a microphone.) RR: Roll the cameras! Hey! Moron! Turn on the camera! Vile Vince Viper: Rex get up here I want to conduct an interview! (Rex Rosemen gets up and slides into the ring tripping on the bottom rope and stumbling into TripleV...Dan Crawford takes off his headset and steps out of the booth) RR: Hey Vinc...* VVV: Thats Vile Rex...even you aren't above the rules. RR: Yes Vile. Any particular question you want me to ask you? VVV: Nah...I just have something I want to say and I figure I might as well have you around to keep it legal. Now everyone is wondering....who is this ICON OF EXTREME? Well I think it should be fairly obvious...who else beats children to thicken a plot twist? In any case all these wonderful people... (Spins around pointing at the audience.) Vile Vince Viper: Are my fans!!! Vileapolloza BABY!!! THE GREATEST SHOW ON EARTH!!! NOW MANY UWA WRESTLERS HAVE ASKED 'CAN I BE A VIPERTEER?'....THE ANSWER? SURE! I got the name Icon of Extreme for my brutal street fights...Overlord welcome to a new hell! HaHaHaHa... (Thornton who is nearing the exit to the locker room is seen watching Vile Vince Viper in the ring) Vile Vince Viper: "Hey Thorten, you got some sort of crush on Overlord? Give me a title match and I will make sure my match at PPV is for the title!". (Thornton looks back...points at VVV...mutters something...then exits...Vile Vince Viper drops the microphone and climbs out of the ring) ----(Footage ends)---- Michael Cook: Approaching the ring area...from Bucharest, Romania...standing at 5'9"...weighing 259lbs...here is Vile Vince Viper!!!!! (boos) DC: And there he is...clothed head to toe in snake skin. Mr. Viper...who has wrestled in some fantastic wrestling organizations throughout his career has made his way back to the top...the UWA. RR: Yes indeed...but Viper still has yet to claim the strap again...the one that was stolen from him by the Godfather back at New York Terror in February. DC: Stolen? RR: Yeah! You saw it Crawford! You know...speaking of Vile Vince Viper merchandise... DC: We weren't. RR: Well...anyway...(holds up a green neck tie) Vile Vince Viper neckties...comes in designer blue, red, or green as you see here. Only $19.95 plus shipping and handling...and it's great. It has a picture of Vile Vince Viper right on it...and it's autographed...perfect for all you UWA merchandise collectors...or any old neck tie nut. Order now...and treasure it for all time. DC: Wow...put me down for about 20 of those Rex. (Sarcastically) RR: Whatever you say. DC: Vile Vince Viper now in the ring and accompanied by Caliban, the loyal little guy. This guy is a real showman. RR: What!? WHAT!? This is hardcore!!!! Vile Vince Viper...the Icon of Extreme!!! Watch who you're calling a showman Crawford! ("Blood Runs Cold Plays") Michael Cook: And now...from Parts Unknown...standing at 7'0"...weighing 345lbs...accompanied to the ring by Miss Kryonic...ICE-BLAAAADE!!!!!!!!!! (crowd erupts) DC: Alright...we should really see a matchup here...Ice-Blade who Vile Vince Viper has antagonized for so long now gets his shot at VVV. And I'm sure he's been back there dying to get his hands on him... RR: No Ice-Pick... DC: Ice-Blade maybe not quite as anxious as we thought...still has not entered. RR: I know what it is. He's scared of Overlord coming down and having to save the day again. DC: I doubt that. Though I'm not going to get into who's the better between Overlord and Ice-Blade...I know that Ice-Blade went head to head with Overlord...didn't blink or back down. And I thought the two were going to go at it right then and there. Didn't happen...thank God. RR: Ha ha ha! That is something that I'd pay to see! DC: Alright...Ice-Blade still is not entering. And Vile Vince Viper looking a little annoyed here...resting in the corner. RR: Hey...this match is no DQ! If Ice-Blade doesn't come down then we have to sit here all night and wait for VVV to give up on his own will! Ha ha ha! Ice-Blade is smarter than I took him for! He did the smart thing...chickened out! DC: I seriously doubt that is what happened...and I think that the match would be stopped rather than forcing VVV to submit to save television time. As we look right now.... (The jumbotron screen over the entrance comes on...Ice-Blades face can be seen on the screen...every light in the Greensboro Colisseum goes off) Ice-Blade: So VVV, "The Icon of Extreme", you actually thought I'd come down and wrestle you? No my friend, not here, not now. You may call me cowardly, you may call me scared. But the fact of the matter is that there's too much tension surrounding tonight. I am watching MY back, and because of this, I am going to not wrestle tonight. You may ask what is in store for you tonight. You stand in the ring, alone, without anyone to help you. Not Goliath, not Rex Overlord, and not any of the other "legends" of the UWA. So, without further adieu... (The jumbotron turns off...the lights in the colisseum still dark) DC: Woah...what's going on here? RR: Where is your hand? DC: In my pocket. RR: That's my pocket! (A burst of flame spouts from the ring...but all remains dark...a metal smacking sound...the crowd is silent as the lights come on and Vile Vince Viper lays motionless in the ring) DC: Woah!!! What happened? RR: Here comes Overlord! DC: Overlord rushing onto the scene...and the crowd has exploded here. Not in his wrestling gear here...Overlord in the ring and nudging Viper with his boot. (Lights go out) RR: What the... DC: The lights... (Lights turn on and a shadowy figure in a long black cloak stands holding a chair...Overlord senses someone behind him...he turns around wide-eyed to see the cloaked man) DC: Who is this? What's going on!? (The man in the cloak delivers a boot to the stomach of Rex Overlord...then smacks the chair over his back) RR: Woah!!! He's attacking Overlord!!! DC: Overlord holding his back...almost dropped by that huge blow!!! REVERSE DDT!!!!!!! RR: WOW!!!! The dark man drops Overlord too!!!! (The dark man raises his hands into the air...the lights shut off...then come on again within only a few seconds...Overlord and Vile Vince Viper lay motionless in the ring) DC: I can't believe this. What do we have here? (Mixed reaction from the crowd) RR: Overlord trying to save VVV for all the wrong reasons! And he gets dropped...by an Ice-Blade goon!!! DC: La Bella Donna Di Notte now is rushing to ringside...and Caliban is already in there trying to revive Vile Vince Viper. RR: Yowza. DC: Donna Di Notte on her knees...trying to awaken the Consigliori of the Syndicate...the monster Rex Overlord who was...Out-monstered tonight. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the referee calls this match...a no contest!!! (boos) DC: The fans not happy with the outcome. And Vile Vince Viper is up in one corner...Overlord getting to his feet in the other. RR: I think Overlord is being taken in by Vile's dark side! DC: Ha! Overlord out of here now with the Syndicate's first lady...a lady who has stood in the corner of two former UWA champions...Donna Di Notte. And in the meantime...Vile Vince Viper leaving with...Caliban. RR: ...who is a great trainer as well! Michael Cook: The following contest...also with special stipulations is scheduled for one fall. It can be won by knockout only...and has a 30 minute time limit. Ladies and gentlemen...coming down to the ring area...is the tallest man in UWA history...weighing 325lbs....GOLIATH!!!! (boos) DC: I notice that they never give Goliath's height measurements. RR: When you're the tallest man in the UWA then I guess it really doesn't matter. DC: Alright...Goliath approaching ringside...with a sort of dumb confidence about him as always. And he's in the ring now...right in the center...awaiting the entrance of his opponant... You see, I'm a much better athelete than you'll ever be!!! Me and you getting into the ring together is like compairing rich choclate to cheezy cheddar. It just can't happen!!... but I'm still gonna give you a shot ya big lug. I have randomly selected 4 other men to wrestle tonight, and if you can defeat them all I'll step into the ring with you!!! ....and to make things more interesting.... Shut up Rex. RR: I... DC: Shut up Rex. #4 clotheslines Goliath down on that metal ramp! And now...putting the boots to him! RR: That's it...Goliath can forget the money right now. DC: The big guy has got Goliath up by the hair...tossing him into the ring...and now getting in himself...BIG ELBOWDROP! (Boos) And Another! And...yet another! And big #4 going to the top turnbuckle! (some cheers) Goliath to the ropes...coming off with a big clothesline...and #4 goes out of the ring!!! Now crashing into the railing! RR: And we see some life in Goliath!! DC: Goliath out of the ring...and he's lifting the guy up...HIGH ABOVE HIS HEAD!!!!!! (cheers) And he drops him throat first across the railing!!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: What's the bell for? DC: I guess the referee figured that was enough! And he's raising Goliath's hand into the air!!! Goliath has survived Scott Daniels four best! RR: Daniels has to fight him now?
