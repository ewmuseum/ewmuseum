UWA Sunday Night's Main Event - 2/4

12 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dan Crawford: And welcome back, incase you are wandering, president T.N.T. just scheduled the match between the Fearsome Foursome and Chad Blake's team for next week on the Main Event. Rex Roseman: I can't wait for Blake to show those four imbesils how to wrestle. Dan Crawford: Well up next we have Billy "Tiger Shark" MacGee taking on the Mystery Man, just who could it be? Lets get down to Michael Cook now and find out. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Billy "Tiger Shark" MacGee v. Mystery Man =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= writer: DPK =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Michael Cook: This next contest is scheduled for one fall, introducing first, already at ringside, making his UWA debut, here is BILLY "TIGER SHARK" MACGEE!!! (some cheers.) And his oppenent... ('Swan Lake scene' plays over the PA....the lights dim and blue spotlights zoom around the room...about ten seconds into it...) Rex Rosemen: NO!...it's not... (The UWAtron shows film footage of Marduk nailing Assault, frog spashing Vile Vince Viper and DDTing "Flaming" Bob. Marduk "The Magnificent" walks out in his blue leather tights, much to the approval of the fans...he smiles and raises his arms in the air...the fans begin to chant 'Marduk' but he gestures for them to stop as he is enjoying his entrance music to much...tip toeing down to the ring...he high fives a few fans and climbs into the ring...then the music stops and he raises his arms in the air....the fans chant Marduk and he smiles...) Audience: "Marduk...Marduk...Marduk" Michael Cook: Making his return to the Universal Wrestling Alliance... Here is MARDUK "THE MAGNIFICENT"!!! Rex Roseman: I can't believe this, he was supposed to be gone for good. Dan Crawford: Well he looks like he's back and here to stay. Although I must say that the scars on his face are barely noticeable now from the incident with Flaming Bob. (bell rings) Dan Crawford: And we're underway, they go to lock up..... and MacGee puts Marduk in a headlock..... Marduk throws MacGee into the ropes..... he comes back off... they collide and the tiger shark goes down hitting the mat hard.... And Marduk didn't even move an inch..... Tiger Shark getting back up.... and Marduk meets him with a big elbow that sends him right back down to the mat...The cover ONE And he kicks out.... Marduk pulls MacGee up... irish whip.... BIG dropkick that sends MacGee down again.... Marduk going up top...... Rex Roseman: He's wasting too much time... Dan Crawfors: Legdrop from the topppp...... HE CONNECTS... And the cover ONE! Msrduk lets MacGee up.... What is he doing now..... He's going up top again.... WITH MACGEE..... Sets him up foe maybe a superplex..... Wait..... He's going for the I'm Sorry..... OH!!! A DDT from the top rope... WHat a move... This has to be it.. ONE TWO THREEE.... And this one is over... the tiger shark didn't even have a chance.... But what an impressive return for Marduk.... No ringrust what so ever..... But why is he back in the UWA... is it titles that he wants or does he just want to kick some but. Rex Roseman: You mean get his but kicked.... This match was misleading match, anyone could have beaten that loser. Dan Crawford: Well, as Mike Miller told everyone yesterday on Spotlight, There is a lot of new talent comming to the UWA... And right now we are about to see one of the these newcomers. (The camera pans to the UWA-tron as a huge, very ugly man appears. He begins to talk in a very deep voice.) Man: The UWA.... HA! What a joke... I've been around... held titles in many federations and I have to say.... I could easily beat the hell out of every single person here. When you meet the likes of the Monster, you will beg for mercey! (The Monster looks down and back up again.) The Monster: I don't care who it is, weather it be Suicide.... Lord Zero...... Chad "The Wuss" Blake..... Big Bully Thornton.... Whomever... the truth IS! That I could end the career of each and every on of you pathetic worms..... So this is why I am not going to wrestle any of you..... Next week I will be wrestling someone who's career is already over with... finished... Someone who sits on his fat a-- all day, therefor it won't matter if I cripple him... YES! T.N.T..... I'm talking to you.... Next Week... right here on the Main Event..... The Monster is going to make his debut.... And you will be my first victim. (Scene fades.) Rex Roseman: YES! I can't wait... T.N.T is going to get his a-- kicked by this guy.... It's going to be great.... I wouldn't miss it for the world. Dan Crawford: I would seriously doubt that T.N.T would step into the ring in such short notice, especially considering that he is retired... Wait a minute,, I'm told that T.N.T is standing by with a response .. Lets see what he has to say. (The camera again pans over to the UWA-tron where T.N.T is standing wearing a gray UWA issued suit with a black UWA shirt inderneath. crowd cheers.) T.N.T: The Monster.... Here we go again.... another guy with a big body and some muscles that thinks he will be comming into the UWA and taking over... If I could only count all of the wrestlers who made the same mistake..... But in response to you challenge.. Monster.. If you want to wrestle T.N.T next week on the Main Event.... Then that is exactly what you are going to get.. Toughguy!! (Scene fades as the crowd goes wild.) Rex Roseman: YES!!! I just cant wait for this one... T.N.T is finally going to get what is comming to him. Do you have any idea how long I've waited for this!!! Dan Crawford: Wow! I have to admit that I am a bit surprised to hear of T.N.T's decision. T.N.T will finaly be in the ring for the first time since he retired as UWA champion after winning the Royal Rumble. It's been an eventful night thus far, so don't go away. ***break*** commercials ***break***
