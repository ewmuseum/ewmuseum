UWAFriday Night Fights Part 2

22 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
("Too Sexy" plays over the P.A.) Michael Cook: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Approaching the ring...from Austin, Texas...standing at 6'1" and weighing 258lbs..."THE MONEY MAN" CASTOR TROY!!!!!!!!!! (boos) RR: Good! This crowd has some sense I see. DC: How is that? RR: Jeering for Castorated Troy. DC: I see. Troy approaching ringside in that zebra skin leather jacket and stirrups. RR: This guy is such a punk. DC: It's amazing Rex...with as much as this guy has been in the UWA headlines...he's only wrestled one match. That was against "Cannon" Christopher Maxwell. RR: You can't even call that a match...because it stopped before it was even started! Troy joined Maxwell and betrayed Solid Gold! ("The Green Machine" plays) Michael Cook: His opponant...from Hollywood, California...standing at 6'7"...weighing 269lbs..."THE FLAMBOYANT ONE" SCOTT DANIELS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (boos) RR: I gave them too much credit! Shut up you morons! DC: In the ring now...removing his own robe...down to the black tight pants..."The Flamboyant One" written right across the backside. RR: Daniels is going to whoop him...you just wait and see. Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: Alright...this contest is underway. Daniels and Troy locking up. RR: You know this is a great way to kick off the big Solid Gold vs. Natural Currency match this Sunday at WrestleFest II. Stable leader against stable leader. DC: It is...but don't think that the outcome of this match will have too much of an effect on that one. Scott Daniels with a big knee lift into the gut of Troy. Daniels being bigger...certainly more powerful...and a little less showy in this one. RR: Troy and Natural Currency have to be showy to make up for their lack of talent. DC: Interesting comment from my colleague "Red" Rex Roseman. Daniels now with Troy in a side headlock...but Troy sends him to the ropes...Daniels off..."BILLION DOLLAR BLAST"!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (superkick). RR: No! DC: But Daniels rolling to the outside...and collapsing on the concrete! Troy just tried to end this one quick! RR: Troy off the ropes...running...DIVING TO THE OUTSIDE..........NOBODY HOME!!!!!!!!! (some cheers) DC: Oh my...Troy just landed in a row of some metal chairs as Daniels ducked out of the way. He's still got some sense left in him after that Billion Dollar Blast. Now rolling inside as the referee continues to count Troy out...but Troy now staggering...rolling back inside the ring at an 8-count. RR: I'm surprised that he got up from that. DC: Daniels meeting him...sending him to the ropes...SPINEBUSTER BY DANIELS!!!!!!!!!! 1 2 no! Kickout at the last second! RR: Daniels is applying the hurt! DC: Both men applying the hurt...Daniels keeps rubbing that lip...as I think I see some blood. RR: I knew it! Troy has something in his boot! DC: The ref not a bit suspicious. And neither am I Rex. RR: I sure am! DC: Daniels and Troy...been in there together for nearly 5 minutes now...but they're hurting like they've gone 20 I must imagine. Daniels...SNAP SUPLEX ON TROY! And for the cover.. 1 2 no. RR: Daniels...woah! Troy just gouged him in the eye! Ring the bell! DC: Daniels has been a little down on his luck lately...trying to get back to the top after his loss to the now silent Asswhupper. RR: Troy is punching him! Closed fists! That's cheating! DC: Both men down...and Troy rolling over Daniels for the cover.. 1 2 just short... RR: He'll never get Daniels. I'll bet this months paycheck on it. DC: Troy now is up...as is Daniels. Troy going to the ropes...hooking Daniels head...JUMPING DDT!!!!!! (boos) RR: Oooh...no. DC: And now going to the top...Daniels down and barely able to move....MOONSAULT...BEAUTIFUL FORMATION...CONNECTS!!!!!!!!!! 1 2 3!!!!!!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: No! Two ripoffs in one night! DC: We have a winner.. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner...CASTOR TROY!!!!!!!!!! DC: Unexpected...a part of me was really believing that Daniels would kick out. But he didn't. And Troy now rollnig out of there...no showboating for him. And Daniels trying to collect himself...still just laying on the canvass. RR: This is terrible. Terrible. DC: Chapter 1...it goes to Natural Currency. RR: Wait till WrestleFest! You'll see Solid Gold back in the saddle...with Johnny Tambo and two mystery partners! DC: Alright...Daniels out of there now...and not happy. Let's get to Michael Cook for the final match of the evening. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the UWA Cruiserweight Championship!!!!!!!!!!!!! (cheers) The challenger for this UWA Cruiserweight Title Match,weighing in at a ready 215 pounds,accompanied by his manager Moneyz,former UWA Cruiserweight Champion,representing the Bad Boy Alliance.....JOHNNY"BAAAAD BOY"COALS!! (Lights dim as "Guess Who's Back" by Rakim blasts over the speakers!Moneyz walks out first to a round of boo's.Moneyz is waving a "Bad Boyz" towel,Moneyz is dressed in a fine BOSS suit and Ray Ban shades.As Moneyz gets halfway down the aisle,he turns around and points towards the entrance.Suddenly a huge smokebomb explodes in the entrance and out of the smoke comes Former UWA Cruiserweight Champion,Johnny"Bad Boy"Coals!Johnny is dressed in black ninja pants with "Bad Boy" written down the sides in white.Johnny is also wearing a headband which has BBA written on it.As both Moneyz and Johnny Coals enter the ring,fireworks explode from all 4 corners of the ring.Then as the lights come back on and the music dies out,Moneyz exchanges a thumbs up with Red Roseman and then he throws the towel into the face of Dan Crawford!) DC: Hey! (throws the towel back...Moneyz dodges it and laughs) RR: Hehehe...this match could make up for the whole evening. Bring it on home daddi'o! ("Come Out and Play" by the Offspring plays) Michael Cook: And now...introducing the UWA Cruiserweight champion...from Bayonne, New Jersey...standing at 6'2" and weighing 225lbs...SUICIDE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (boos) DC: Here he comes! Suicide walking out in those purple baggy pants...carrying the UWA Cruiserweight belt. This match has got me curious. Just who is better? RR: Easy...Johnny Coals. DC: Suicide in the ring...and asking for the mic. Suicide: This fed sucks...and you suck Johnny Coals. I quit. (drops the cruiserweight belt as the fans boo and begin to throw trash...Crawford and Roseman remain silent) (Coals grabs the title and hugs Moneyz as Suicide steps out of the ring...flipping off the fans. Then from beneath the ring comes a fog...and stepping out of it is a man in full length black tights and a black mask. Down one leg is the letters MWK and down the other is P.K. in white lettering. Suicide turns around to see what the crowd is cheering about. Suicide appears startled...watching the man who is known as Pedro Kozaro step toward him...methodically. From out of the locker room walk dozens of midgets lead by Flaming Bob...a big man in a pink bunny suit...and the midget Caliban. Suicide turns to face them but then shrinks back again as he sees Bob holding a blow torch. Then charging Kozaro...the two men brawling...Kozaro knocks Suicide into the guardrail...then taking him by the hair and throwing him into the ring steps...taking a chair and beating it repetitively over the body of Suicide) DC: Somebody stop this! RR: I can't believe it! Hell On Earth is out here! Pedro Kozaro is out here! Coals and Moneyz are in the ring with the title...they don't know what to do! And Kozaro smacking that chair against the head of Suicide! (mixed reaction) DC: Suicide bleeding...from that already scarred and burnt face...courtesy of Flaming Bob and a host of other wrestlers! Somebody stop this! RR: Kozaro just threw the chair aside...and it's got blood all over it! DC: Now dragging Suicide up the aisle by the hair...and at the feet of Flaming Bob. Those midgets are picking Suicide up...and they're carrying him out of here! RR: Woah! DC: I can't believe this...the second time that Suicide has done this to the UWA. What is this guys problem? RR: Suicide doesn't cut it in the heavyweight ranks anymore...that's his problem. DC: Good riddence then. Pedro Kozaro disappearing through the crowd as Flaming Bob leads the Hell On Earth crew out of here. And here comes President Gordon... RR: Another busy night for him... DC: he's got a mic...and he's in the ring... Gordon: Alright...give me that belt Coals. (cheers) RR: What!? DC: Coals doesn't want to give it up! Gordon: If this belt were going to anyone by forfeit then it'd be the #1 contender. You're not at the top Coals...so give me the title. DC: Coals drops the title at Gordon's feet...and getting out of the ring with Moneyz...disgusted look on his face. Gordon: Alright...this upsets me. Suicide...in the past you've been allowed to come and go as you pleased. One week notice you give me until you're quitting. Well I'm tired of this crap. You're fired...forget about the one week notice and don't show your face in my office again. (cheers) I could hand this belt out to the #1 contender right now...but I'm not going to do that. I'm declaring this title vacant...and to remain vacant until Christmas Chaos in which there will be a match organized in which all eligible cruiserweights can have a shot at it. That's all I've got to say at the moment. (drops the mic) DC: Great call by President Gordon. RR: Firing Suicide is good...but that belt belongs to Coals! DC: Well...it's not going to Coals. RR: Drats. DC: Well...our time is up. I certainly hope that you enjoyed FNF. Remember...WrestleFest II airs this Sunday...only on pay per view. Call your local cable operators if you haven't already and order. For Rex Roseman...I'm Dan Crawford. Goodnight. (fades to black) 1997 UWA Sports™ All Rights Reserved
