UWA Tuesday Night War - part 2/2 - 10/7/97

8 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DC: Its now time for the Main Event of the evening, Rex, and we have been looking forward to this one since Crusher Stevenson won the International Title. RR: Ever since Crusher won the gold, everyone knew who would be his biggest challenge and tonight is the night that we'll find out who is the best of the best in the UWA. DC: And Michael Cook is in the ring, so lets take you up for the ring announcements... MC: Ladies and Gentlemen, our next contest of the evening is the MAIN EVENT! (crowd cheers loudly and "More Human Than Human" begins playing. crowd changes its gears and BOOS!) RR: What's wrong with these people? This guy is gonna carry the UWA into the next century, don't they appreciate TRUE talent? DC: I suppose not. MC: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome, Tinseltown's favorite son, and the (rolls eyes) heir apparant to the UWA International Title...The #1 Box Office Draw in Professional Wrestling AND the Motion Picture Industry, the one...the ONLY....HOLLYWOOOOOOOOOOOD JOHNNNNNNNNNYYYYYYYYY TAAAAAAMMMMMMBOOOOOOOOO!!! (crowd boos big time at the intro of HJT) RR: Here he comes, my new favorite athlete! DC: Who did it use to be? RR: OJ... DC: And the fans along the runway are giving him a tough time, but Tambo seems unphased. In fact, he's giving it right back to them. And...oh no, here comes the Brotherhood. Looks like they're joining us here at the announcing booth. Eliminator: That's right, Danno. Give a nice warm welcome to Zodiac, Mr. Tananka, The Stalker, and myself. DC: No, I couldn't. RR: You're a twit, Crawdaddy. Zodiac: Got something cocky to say to me today, Crawford? DC: I got nothing to say to you, Zodiac. Z: That is wise. RR: I would like to have seen him smack you. DC: Thanks, Rex, you're a pal. Stalker: If you boys are about done, I think our soon to be new stablemate has something to say. DC: Looks like the Stalker is right, Tambo has grabbed a microphone.. Tambo: Hello my adoring public. (boos get louder) Your hero, good ol' HJT is just moments away from winning the first of two titles I intend to claim in the UWA! (more boos) So sit back down, wipe the drool off your chin, and get ready for some West Coast Offense! (Boos getting even louder) DC: Can Tambo get any more cockier?? He is fighting the UWA International Champion, Crusher Stevenson, in a matter of minutes!! RR: Stevenson is a joke, plain and simple. DC: And he has the belt because?? RR: No comment. Tanaka: He has the belt because none of us have faced him yet. Stalker: Not yet. But he is about to face one of our newest members. DC: You're sure he is going to join you? Eliminator: As sure as I have been about everything in my life. DC: So you're not sure then. E: Shut up. DC: Let's get down to Michael Cook for the introduction of Crusher Stephenson. MC: And now the Champion.... ("Man In The Box" by Alice In Chains starts to play over the loud speakers as the crowd goes nuts) MD: From Chicago, Illinois...Standing at 6'5 and weighing in at 265 pounds, here is the UWA International Champion... CRUSHER STEVENSON!!!!!!! (Crusher walks out as the crowd arupts. He is wearing a pair of black tights with "Crusher" in electric blue on the back. He wears black kneepads and boots. He also has a goatee. The UWA International Title is around his waist. He looks very focused and walks about 3/4 of the way to the ring. He stops and points to Tambo, then rushes to the ring and starts fighting with HJT) DC: AND HERE WE GO!!!! RR: Crusher and Tambo are exchanging lefts and rights, but Tambo isn't dishing out the best of em. Elim: Stevenson is a powerful man, but Tambo will win on his smarts alone! Zodiac: Thats a field where Crusher's seed will find no purchase. All: Huh??? Zodiac: Never mind. Stalker: Tambo is down on one knee and Crusher continues with fists. DC: And Tambo is on the mat face first. Crusher turns around and poses for the crowd and they respond! This place is defeaning! Tanaka: What's wrong with these idiots? They should be cheering Hollywood! RR: Stevenson picks up the challenger by his hair. Don't do that! He has beautiful hair! Elim: He should shave it off. I think I'll suggest that to him. DC: You don't really want him in your group, do you... Elim: I was kidding! DC: And Crusher lifts Tambo up above his head and slams him hard to the mat. Tambo is groaning and favoring his lower back. Zodiac: Get up, Hollywood. You're not hurt. You're just playing. DC: Zodiac obviously living in a dream world. Zodiac: What? DC: Stevenson with a well placed kick to the small of Tambo's back. Its obvious in the first five minutes of the match what Crusher's gameplan is. He's gonna try to break Hollywood's back. RR: How much more can he withstand? Stevenson continuing with kicks to the back of the challenger. Tanaka: How dull. Lets see something different. DC: You may get your wish Mr. T., Crusher is picking him back up above his head and DEPOSITS HIM ON THE ARENA FLOOR!!! Tambo in BIG touble now! Elim: Stephensuck is following him to the floor. DC: Borrowing Tambo's nickname for the Crusher? Elim: Artistic license. RR: Crusher picks up a chair and smacks it against Tambo's back!! That's illegal! Referee stop that man!! DC: Let met get this straight, Rex, YOU are complaining that someone is doing something illegal??? RR: I've seen the light. DC: I think its the light in your refridgerator. (Rex looks at his midsection) RR: You think I'm getting fat? DC: Crusher now drops the chair and picks Tambo up above his head again and THROWS HIM through the ropes and back into the ring. Zodiac: This isn't looking too good for our man, Tambo. Elim: Don't worry. I discussed some strategies with him. He'll turn this around. DC: You discussed strategies with Tambo? I thought he hasn't talked to you guys yet. Elim: Dammit, don't call a man's bluff on national TV!! RR: Look at this guy. He's posing for the crowd again. Whatta jerk! DC: And he better watch out cuz Tambo is on his knees approaching him from behind. OOOH!! A well placed forearm from beneath and Crusher is gasping for air. Speaking of which, the concession stands are open outside the arena and I hear they are selling NutterBusters. RR: That was actually funny, Danny Baby. DC: True praise indeed. Tambo struggles to his feet and looks down at the fallen champion and drops a big elbow on his head. Elim: Here we go, now Tambo's got it under control. Stalker: Go Tambo! DC: Thanks for the descriptive analysis, Stalker. Stalker: Welcome. DC: Can we get any more people here in the booth? RR: Well actually... DC: FORGET IT! Now its Tambo with several kicks to the head of Crusher Stevenson. Hollywood picks up the champion by his head and lifts him WAY UP for a suplex. And he hits it. Tambo is getting his energy back now. Elim: Maybe he should try a new battery. DC: Do you even think you are remotely funny? Elim: Remotely. DC: And Tambo rolls Crusher out of the ring. Crusher gets to his feet and looks quite groggy. And Tambo runs against the ropes,DIVES OVER THE TOP ROPE AND DOWN ONTO STEVENSON!!! RR: WOW, what a move by Tambo, and did you see Crusher's head hit the concrete floor??!! DC: I did indeed, and now he's at the mercy of Hollywood Johnny Tambo. And Tambo throws the champ back into the ring. Now he's setting a table back towards the railing. What's THIS?? Zodiac: I think someone's going through the table! DC: Do ya think? Zodiac: Yup. Stalker: Check it out, Tambo's on the ring apron. He's setting Crusher up for a suplex...he's gonna suplex Crusher through the table!! DC: And Crusher is lifted into the air. Tambo is falling backward! UNREAL!!! He just shifted his weight and put Crusher through the table with a BRAIN BUSTER!!! RR: That was a "Get ready for the Hollywood Or Bust!!" from HJT. Elim: That move took a lot out of Tambo too. But look, Crusher is busted wide open. He's juicin' man! DC: Crusher was just midly hurt before, now he's in dire trouble. The more blood pours from that wound in his head, the weaker he'll get! RR: And his face is a crimson mask right now. And Tambo is finally back up. He picks Crusher back up to his feet and STANDING DROPKICK! Tanaka: This Tambo has got some great moves. He'll make a fine addition to the BrotherHood. DC: You are all so sure that he's going to join your happy little organization. Why do you think he even NEEDS you? He's undefeated on his own without help from ANYONE! Zodiac: Don't question us, Crawfish. We want Tambo, and Tambo wants us! RR: Let them alone, Dan, just call the damn match. DC: You guys need serious help. But I'll play nice and drop the subject. Elim: Good idea. DC: Tambo rolls back into the ring to avoid a countout, and he makes sure that the referee doesn't count out Crusher either. Stalker: I counted out Stevenson awhile ago. Elim: Same here. Zodiac: Me too. Tanaka: Here here. RR: Can't argue with logic. DC: Tambo rolls back outside the ring and SLAMS Crusher's head into the guardrail. Look at the blood left on the railing. Elim: Two things are slipping away from Stevenson right now, his blood and his title! DC: You may be right, Eliminator. Its looking bleak for the champ. Tambo puts Crusher back in the ring and, takes time to jaw with the fans again. Stevenson paid the price for interacting with the fans and ignoring his opponent, now Hollywood is making the same mistake. RR: And Crusher is on his feet behind Tambo. NO! Tambo turns around with a discus punch and back down goes the champ. Zodiac: The belt will safely be in the camp of the BrotherHood in a matter of minutes. Tanaka: And then we're coming after Thornton! DC: Big Butch Thornton has officially been warned by the BrotherHood. But back in the ring, Tambo has climbed the top turnbuckle and comes off down towards Crusher... RR: Stevenson rolls out of the way and Tambo's knees impact with the canvas! Crusher still trying to recover from the beating that HJT handed out to him. The champ wipes the blood out of his eyes and gets to his feet. Stalker: Stevenson still doesn't have his bearings straight. But he picks up Tambo...NECKBREAKER!! DC: Crusher is working on all parts of the body, Rex. He wants every square inch of Hollywood's body to be in a great degree of pain. RR: Got that right, DC, Tambo is holding his neck, his back, his head,his knees. Elim: Tanaka, Zodiac, Stalker, I think you should get to ringside, lend our friend some expertise. All 3: Aye! DC: Now come on guys, stay out of the match. There is no need for your services. Elim: Don't worry about it, Dannybrook, we wouldn't dream of affecting the outcome of this match. RR: Three members of the BrotherHood converge on the ring, but true to their word, they are staying away from the match. DC: We'll see how long that lasts, and wait a second, look who's coming down the runway. Elim: Craven, Thornton, and Storm are walking down towards the ring. DC: Its become a game of checks and balances as both teams want to make sure that neither one has an advantage during this match. Elim: Get them outta here! They have NO REASON to come to ringside! DC: They have just as much right as YOU guys do! RR: You better go join your boys, Eliminator! Elim: I think you're right, Rex. HEY! Sandman, come on out! We need ya at the ring! DC: Eliminator of the Brotherhood calling for the Sandman to to come to ringside. I wondered where he was. RR: Here he comes, too. Now the entire Brotherhood and Fearsome Foursome are present. And we still have a match going on! DC: Thats right, and Crusher Stevenson notices that his friends have joined the crowd at ringside. He seems happy now! RR: And he puts Tambo up in a backbreaker! The CRUSHER BREAKER!!! He has Tambo up in his submission hold! DC: Tambo could be done at any minute, nobody has ever escaped this hold! RR: And suddenly here comes more people from the lockerrooms! DC: Here comes the "Golden Boy" Lawrence Stevenson, and also Wrestlingman, and Caster Troy, and the "Flamboyant One" Scott Daniels. RR: They all wanna watch this match! Here comes the Bad Boyz Alliance, and Donovan Black. The arena floor is filling up! DC: But back up in the ring, Tambo will NOT give up! Crusher Stevenson looks EXTREMELY mad! And he drops HJT! Tambo didn't submit, but then again he didn't escape the hold either! RR: Crusher is leaning against the ropes and exchanging some words with the BrotherHood, now turns back to Tambo and drops a leg across his throat. DC: Something I've realized here...none of the enemies on the ring floor are even talking to each other, they are simply watching this brutal match. RR: As well they should, and you are right, this one is DEFINITELY BRUTAL! DC: Stevenson picks Tambo back up and puts him in a bearhug! RR: More damage done to the back of Hollywood. And Tambo looks limp in the arms of the Crusher! DC: The crowd is chanting "Crusher!! Crusher!!!" RR: But the fans aren't 100% behind the champ. Tambo has his following too! DC: He does indeed, but nothing even close to the mass popularity of Crusher Stevenson and the ENTIRE Fearsome Foursome for that matter. RR: But popularity doesn't win you titles, SKILL does! DC: And the Fearsome Foursome owns TWO of the major UWA Titles. RR: I had a point somewhere there. DC: Tambo claps his hands behind Crusher's head, and makes the champ's ears ring. He drops Hollywood, but Tambo isn't putting up much more of an offensive. RR: Tambo may be a little too hurt to do so. DC: Tambo manages a chop across the chest of the Crusher. RR: Not very effective, but Tambo with another series of chops to the chest. And a fist to the head, Tambo seems to be gaining some degree of momentum. DC: Now Tambo picks up Stevenson and slams him back to the mat. And where's he going? RR: Tambo climbing the to the top turnbuckle and looks down at Crusher. Now he's turning his back to the champ and stares out at the crowd. He jumps.... DC: MOONSAULT ON CRUSHER STEVENSON!!! That took alot out of HJT, but even more out of the UWA International Champion. RR: And now its Stevenson who is grasping for breath as Tambo is back on his feet. And the Fearsome Foursome looks rightfully concerned as their man is writhing on the mat. DC: Tambo now goes to the outside of the ring and grabs a chair! RR: Time for a little payback! Hollywood back into the ring. He lays the chair on top of Crusher's face. Whatever Tambo is doing, this is gonna hurt! DC: Hollywood runs into the ropes and comes back, BAM! Drops a leg across the chair and Crusher is hurt some more! Now Tambo picks the chair up and turns around to yell at the crowd. AND CRUSHER STEVENSON IS GETTING UP!!! RR: He just dropkicked the chair into TAMBO'S FACE!! Now Hollywood is busted wide open!! And HJT is staggering! DC: Crusher throws the chair out of the ring and continues to work on the bleeding forehead of Hollywood Johnny Tambo. Fist after fist impacts with the bloody pulp once known as Tambo's head. Stevenson picks Tambo HIGH above his head for a gorilla slam and SLAMS him back down. RR: But Stevenson is relentess. Tired, but relentless. He grabs a handfull of hair and lifts Tambo once more to his feet. He puts Tambo in position for what will most DEFINITELY be a bonecrunching suplex, and up goes Tambo. DC: And DOWN goes Tambo. Crusher goes for the pin. 1.... 2.... NO!! RR: Tambo kicks out of the first pinning attempt in this contest. Now Crusher picks him back up and sets HJT up for a piledriver! And...BOOM!!!! He hits it! Here's the pin... 1.... 2.... Th....NO!!!! DC: Tambo gets his shoulder up off the canvas again. But it doesn't look like Tambo will be able to do it again. One more power move and it should be over. RR: I hate to admit it, but I think you're right, the power of Crusher Stevenson is just plain too much for Hollywood Johnny Tambo to overcome. DC: Now Crusher sets up Tambo for yet another suplex. And up goes Tambo again. RR: BUT WAIT...Tambo just shifted his weight again, and rolls up Stevenson for the pin.. 1... 2... 3!!!!!!! RR: OH MY GOD I DON'T BELIEVE IT!!! DC: Hollywood Johnny Tambo has just captured the UWA International Title from Crusher Stevenson! And he is now 2-0 against the Crusher! RR: Not only that, Don, but he is UNDEFEATED in the UWA!!! DC: Crusher can't believe what has just happened! The referee holds up the limp arm of HJT and Stevnson charges the new champion! RR: But here comes the Brotherhood for the save! Crusher is wailing away on an almost unconscious Hollywood Johnny Tambo, but the members of the Brotherhood grab him and throw him off to the other side of the ring, and the Fearsome Foursome have entered the ring as well. DC: But they are holding Crusher back! I just picked up from the ringside mic that they are telling him it was a clean pin and he should just calm down. RR: Big Butch Thornton telling Crusher Stevenson that there will be a rematch. And the Crusher is settling down. He shakes his head in aquiesence and is exiting the ring. The Fearsome Foursome are returning to the locker room TEN POUNDS lighter! DC: The rest of the wrestlers are leaving as well, except "The Flamboyant One" Scott Daniels, he is just standing there. RR: And the BrotherHood lifts Tambo to his feet, and Stalker and Sandman are holding him up. DC: The Eliminator puts the UWA International Title over the shoulder of Hollywood Johnny Tambo and grabs the mic... Elim: Hollywood Johnny Tambo, we like your style. DC: Of course they do, he just won a major title! Elim: and we offer you a membership in the BrotherHood. RR: The Eliminator unravels a BrotherHood T-shirt and holds it up to Tambo. Elim: Do you accept? DC: The Eliminator is handing the microphone to our new International Champion. Here comes the moment we've been waiting for. Tambo: Eliminator, Stalker, Sandman, Tanaka, and Zodiac. You guys are, in my opinion, the best group that CURRENTLY resides in the UWA. The only thing that you lack is a world title...well, Eliminator... (pauses to catch his breath) RR: Come on Hollywood, out with it. Tambo: Eliminator, you no longer lack a title in your camp. It would be an honor to join your elite fighting force. DC: THERE IT IS, LADIES AND GENTS! Hollywood Johnny Tambo, in ONE NIGHT, has defeated Crusher Stevenson for the International Title, AND has joined the BrotherHood! RR: But wait a second, Scott Daniels is getting in the ring. DC: And he has taken the microphone from Hollywood. What the hell is going on? Daniels: Awwwwwwwwwwww baaaaaaayyyyybbbbeeeeeeeee!!! (the crowd boos ever more than before) DC: This guy is just a cocky as Tambo. The fans just hate these men! Daniels: First of all I want to congratulate you on your title victory. (the Brotherhood acknowledges the compliment) Daniels: Second of all, I think its obvious that I deserve a shot at your gold! I'm undefeated TOO ya know! The Flamboyant One deserves to wear a little solid gold! DC: Tambo smiles at that one. He must find Daniels funny. RR: That could be a mistake. Never underestimate an opponent,especially one as talented as Scott Daniels. Daniels: Whatta ya say, Tambo. Do you feel lucky? (does a Dirty Harry impression) Elim (grabs the mic): Listen you little maggot, give the guy a change to recoup. He was just through HELL!! Tambo (takes the mic): Tell ya what, Daniels, I'll give you a shot if you can do ONE thing! I want you to pick two partners of your choice. I could care less who they are. Take two of the Fearsome Foursome if you like. And I want you three to wrestle ME....ELIMINATOR....AND STALKER in a 6 MAN TAG TEAM MATCH! I want this added to the match on Sunday Spotlight on the 12th. Thats right, I want Sunday Spotlight to have TWO matches this time around! What do YOU say, Daniels??? Daniels (is thrown the mic): Tell ya what buddy, I accept! And I know just the two guys to ask. They'd LOVE a chance to take apart the BrotherHood! You're on!! DC: Daniels throws down the mic and exits the ring. Check out the smirk on this guys face!! RR: I wonder who he will come up with for the match. It won't be Big Butch Thornton or Rex Overlord, OR Tomahawk. I bet its gonna be Crusher Stevenson and Scott Storm! DC: It very well may be! And Eliminator has a little more to say. Elim: There's one more thing I'd like to say...and this goes out to YOU, R.A.D. After we finish with you, you'll be known as S.A.D.! Cuz we're gonna rip you to pieces! (the crowd erupts as R.A.D. runs to ringside with a baseball bat, followed by the Goddess) DC: And speak of the devil, here comes R.A.D. RR: He's swinging that bat wildly at the members of the BrotherHood, and they are making a hasty retreat. DC: As they go up the runway, Tambo is holding the belt high. RR: Now its R.A.D. who has something to say... RAD: Today, I am declaring WAR against the BrotherHood. DC: The BrotherHood stops at the top of the runway. They wanna hear what R.A.D. has to say. RAD: Yeah, I'm talking to YOU, idiots! (RAD points the bat the the members of the BrotherHood as he says their names) RAD: Stalker, Zodiac, Eliminator, Mr. Tanaka, Sandman, Tambo... and even YOU Rex Roseman. RR: What the hell's this guy's problem? What did I do to him??!! DC: Maybe he's sick of your acid tongue too. RR: Your wife isn't. DC: WHAT!???!! RAD: Hey Eliminator, I know your brain is like that commercial where the egg is frying in the pan, but listen up! I signed a match to meet you next Tuesday, not your boyfriend over there, Sandman. Eliminator, that shows me that you are a chicken (Elim looks indignant on the runway) and you have a yellow line running down your back. Eliminator, you are a big baby and you're scared of me. That's why you have to have your boyfriend do your dirty work. DC: Tanaka is running down to ringside and tries to get in the ring, but RAD is pointing that bat right at him. RAD: Yeah, go ahead and get in the ring and I'll knock you into the 30th row. (crowd cheers) Tanaka, the Goddess has something to say to you. Goddess: Mr. Tanaka, I've heard rumors that you have been spreading the word that I want you. That is pure BULL! Tanaka, you want ME?! You can't HAVE me, because my man is standing right next to me and believe you me, has something that I know you don't have (embraces RAD).So Tanaka, you can just keep dreaming because the only woman you make out with are in your wet dreams. (crowd cheers loudly as Tanaka looks irritated) RAD: Mr. Tanaka, if you put a finger on her I will shove this baseball bat so far up where the sun don't shine that your grandkids will feel it! (crowd roars) (RAD throws down the mic and swings the bat at Tanaka, who returns to the BrotherHood, who disappear behind the curtain) DC: Do you think R.A.D. will be one of the two men that Daniels picks as his partners for Sunday night?? RR: It's a good possibility. He has no loveloss for the BrotherHood. DC: Well, folks, that wraps it up for this week's edition of Tuesday Night War. We will see you again on Friday for Friday Night Fights. Where "Bad Boy" Johnny Coalz defends his Cruiserweight Title against Wolf in a Steel Cage Ladder Match. RR: That should be spectacular! And R.A.D. takes on Ice-Blade in a 2 outta 3 Falls match. DC: Plus Scott Daniels will take on Wrestlingman, and a No Holds Barred, Falls Count Anywhere match with The Advisor against Suicide. We'll see ya then!! (fades to black) Special thanks to [detail withheld] for Johnny Tambo vs. Crusher Stevenson... 1997 UWA Sports™ All Rights Reserved
