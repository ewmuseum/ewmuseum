UWA Sunday Night's Main Event 8-18-97 2/4

19 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dan Crawford: Welcome back to Sunday Night's Main Event. What an eventful night thus far as Timothy N Turner from the IIWF surprised every person around the world as he took TnT's place absolutly destroying the debut of the Monster. Rex Roseman: .................... Dan Crawford: But we still have a lot more of action still to come so let's go to Scott McBane who is standing by in the back with Big Butch Thornton!! (Camera cuts to Scott McBane who is standing beside Big Butch Thornton in the lockeroom area. Scott has on a nice grey suit and Butch has on his normal wrestling attire.) Scott McBane: Thanks Dan. Right here with me I have Big Butch Thornton who later tonight will team will lead the Fearsome Foursome into battle against Chad Blake and his team the Blakescorps. Butch, do you have any comments? Big Butch Thornton: Yeah. Chad Blake, last week, you got on that microphone and challenged me, Crusher Stevenson, and the D-Crew to a 8 man tag match. You knew what you were getting into, now it's time to face the consequences of your actions. Later tonight, one way or another you will be in the ring with the Original Asskicker. I'm ready...Are you??? Scott McBane: Butch, what do you think of the 3 mystery men that Chad Blake first introduced last night to fight alongside with him?? Has it been difficult training for this match when you and your partners only knew who one of the opponents were? Big Butch Thornton: You know Scott, I could give a rat's ass who his mystery men are. They'll feel the pain and suffering of Judgement Day just like every other wrestler. And anyway, I like suprises. Just makes kicking their ass even more fun! Scott McBane: (Jokingly) One last question, do you have a prediction for the match?? Big Butch Thornton: I'm not a betting man, but I would lay all the money I have on the line that the Fearsome Foursome is coming out on top tonight! Scott McBane: Dan, Rex, back to you! (camera cuts back to Dan and Rex) Rex Roseman: Ya..... RIGHT! The only way the Fearsome Foursome can win this match is if TnT tries to pull the same kind of crap that we just saw earlier tonight. Dan Crawford: That match ofcourse is out main event, right now we have to get to our next matchup, take it away Mike. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== Bad Habit v. Malibu Mike ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== writer: Doug D ---------------------------------- (Michael Cook is in the ring wearing a tuxedo) Michael Cook:"This match is scheduled for one fall!"(The fans cheer) ("Bad Habit"by The Offspring blares over the PA System as the Legion fans go crazy) Michael Cook:"Coming down the aisle...hailing from Chicago,Illinois but making his residence in Las Vegas,Nevada...accompanied by his manager Desire...here is BAD HABIT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Bad Habit comes out of the entrance.He is wearing his black leather trench coat,and is weilding a black aluminum bat.He points to the ring with the bat,and then points to the entrance.Desire comes out in ring attire.She has on a black top and silver wrestling pants.She holds a leather strap in her hand.The two make their way to the ring,slapping a few hands here and there.Bad Habit climbs up into the ring,removes his trench coat,and hands it to the ring attendant,along with his bat.Desire stays outside the ring) (HBK's Music plays as the fans go nuts) Michael Cook:"And his opponent....Coming down the aisle,accompanied by the Sensational Sara,from Malibu California,weighing in at 275 and standing at 6 FEET 6......MMMMAAALIBU MIKKKKE [Fans roar as Malibu Mike comes out wearing his Silver tights with silver boots,and silver strings on the boots.Sara is right beside him wearing a glittery dress.Malibu flexes and Silver fireworks go off at the entrance.Sara has a mace bottle with her.They both slap hands as they come down to ringside.Malibu Mike climbs up into the ring,and Sara stays out of the ring) Ding Ding Ding Dan Crawford:"The ref is strapping Desire to Sensational Sara,and we are going to have two fights!" Rex Roseman:"Yeah bitch fight!" Dan Crawford:"Bad Habit is taunting Malibu Mike in the ring,and Malibu Mike is getting ticked off!Malibu Mike charge's Bad Habit,but Bad Habit trips up Malibu Mike,and Bad Habit is on top of Malibu Mike and is punching him in the face,Mike is getting pounded on!" Rex Roseman:"Who care's about that match,I'm watching the match outside the ring!Desire is whipping Sensational Sara with the strap,continuously,now this is what I like to see, a major cat fight!" Dan Crawford:"Okay Rex,let's just commentate on the match inside the ring!Bad Habit does a stepover toehold on Malibu Mike!Bad Habit covers Malibu Mike... 1... kick-out,by Malibu Mike!There's no way in the world Bad Habit is going to get a win that fast!Bad Habit pick's up Malibu Mike,and chuck's him to the ropes,but Malibu Mike duck's under Bad Habit,and Mike stops!Malibu Mike does a bulldog on Bad Habit!Malibu Mike pick's up Bad Habit,and does a Side Russian Legsweep on Bad Habit!Malibu Mike covers Bad Habit... 1... 2... kick-out by Bad Habit!" Rex Roseman:"Desire is still whipping Sara outside the ring,and Sensational Sara is now bleeding quite badly!Wait a minute what is Desire doing she pick's up Sara,and is bringing her around to a table,beside us,and she just threw Sara onto the table,and Sensational Sara is now bleeding in the face!What the hell Malibu Mike is now shouting at Desire,and Desire is looking up at him!Bad Habit is back up,Malibu Mike turns around,and Bad Habit clips his knee,and again,and again,and again!Bad Habit pick's up Malibu Mike,and chuck's him to the ropes,Bad Habit comes back and does a flying lariat on Malibu Mike!Bad Habit now has a texas cloverleaf on Malibu Mike,and Mike is in alot of pain,yeah end this match,so we can see more of the two women fight!" Dan Crawford:"Malibu Mike is in alot of pain,but he is crawling to the ropes,and he grab's them,and the ref force's the break!Bad Habit cover's Malibu Mike... 1... 2... 3,no Mike just got a shoulder up!Bad Habit pick's up Malibu Mike,but Malibu just DDT'ed Bad Habit back down to the mat!Malibu Mike is now climbing up to the top rope,and he is on the top rope,this could be it here if he hit's this move!Malibu Mike jump's off,and oh my gosh did a Summersault SummerLegDrop on Bad Habit!But Malibu is not pinning Bad Habit which is a mistake!Mike pick's up Bad Habit,and does a neckbreaker on Bad Habit,now he cover's him,and that should be it... 1... 2... 3,no,no,no Bad Habit just got a shoulder up!What's this on the outside,Desire is going to throw Sara into a guardrail,but Sensational Sara reverse's it,and she just chucked Desire into the guardrail,and now both women are bleeding on their faces!Sara now has her mace bottle,and she just sprayed Desire in the eyes,and Desire can't see at all!Sara is now whipping Desire with the mace bottle,and Desire now has a cut on both arms!Malibu Mike is looking out of the ring,with a big smile on his face!" Rex Roseman:"Who care's about Bad Habit,or Malibu Mike,I just want to see the two women fight!But wait Wrestlingman is coming down to ringside!Malibu Mike see's him,and he is shouting down at Wrestlingman,who is just staring back in the ring!Bad Habit is getting back up!Malibu Mike turns around,and he chuck's Bad Habit to the ropes,but Mike reverse's it!Wrestlingman isn't looking,but he just tripped up Bad Habit,instead of Malibu Mike!Bad Habit is now staring out at Wrestlingman!Bad Habit turns around,Malibu Mike boots him in the gut,and Mike just did The Wipeout(He starts out like a Perfect Plex but when the opponent is directly above the mat.Malibu drops so the opponents head hits)on Bad Habit!Malibu Mike covers him... 1... 2... 3!!!" (HBK's Music,plays over the PA,and the fans go nuts) Michael Cook:"The winner of the match,Malibu Mike!"(The fans go nuts) Rex Roseman:"Oh man the ref is outside the ring now,and he just unstrapped Sara,and Desire,but they both are bleeding pretty badly,which is what I like to see!Wait a minute what is Malibu Mike doing,he just got a chair,and he threw it inside the ring,and he rolls back in himself!Malibu Mike is now laughing at Bad Habit,he just threw the chair in the air,and yes go Malibu go,he just dropkicked the chair into Bad Habit's face,and he said Lord Zero is next!Go Malibu Go,Go Malibu Go!He sure kicked Bad Habit's ass!Hahahahahaha,that weiner Wrestlingman has disappeared back to the dressing room!" Dan Crawford:"Oh be quiet Rex,he didn't mean to trip up Bad Habit!Malibu Mike is now outside the ring,helping up Sensational Sara,and is helping her to the back,as the fans go nuts!The doctor's are coming down,and are putting both Bad Habit,and Desire on stretcher's this isn't good for The Legion of Darkness,they could have lost two great people in their stable for sometime to come!The doctor's are pushing the stretcher's to the back!" Rex Roseman:That moron Wrestlingman best keep his nose out of Bad Habit's business. He just cost him the match and Bad Habit ain't going to be two happy about it. Dan Crawford:"Maybe Rex,just Maybe!We'll be back in a few moments!" ***break*** commercial ***break***
