UWA Saturday Night's Main Event part 4/4

3 August 1997

Dan Crawford: Welcome back Rex Roseman: ................ Dan Crawford: It's as if Rex Just found out that Sants Clause doesn't... errr.... nevermind. Lets just get to out next match. Could we see yet another title change ===================================== Tag Team Title Match ===================================== Hell's Angels(c) v. The Fatal Clan ===================================== (The announcer is in the ring) Michael Cook:"This match is scheduled for one fall,and is for The UWA Tag Team Championships!"(The fans go nuts) (The Fatal Clan,Menace,and Mayhem are already in the ring,whereing their usual attire,Suicide is with them,and so is Riot) Announcer:"Already in the ring,with Suicide,and Riot,two part's of The FBI(Fatal Brothers Incorporated),here is Menace,and Mayhem,collectively known as The Fatal Clan!"(The fans give a mixed reaction) (Menace,and Mayhem climb up to the top turnbuckles of two ropes,and raise their arm's in the air,and make it like they want the belt's around their waist.Suicide,and Riot climb out of the ring) Michael Cook: And now, introducing the champions... ("Bow Down"by The Westside Connection blares as the fans start to cheer) Announcer:"Coming down the aisle,along with the rest of The Legion of Darkness...hailing from parts unknown but making their residence in Las Vegas, Nevada...The UWA Tag Team Champions...Hate & Anger, collectively known as HELL'S ANGELS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Smoke rises at the entrance as the rest of The Legion members come out, all wearing black leather trench coats and carrying black aluminum bats.They all pair up in two's and stand on opposite sides of each other.They all raise their bats as Hate & Anger step out of the entrance. They also have bats, and wear black leather trench coats. They open their coats, and reveal their Tag Team belts. The fans are going crazy. Hate and Anger, and the rest of the Legion slap hands on the way to the ring.) Rex Roseman:"I really do hate those loser's,and hope that The Fatal Clan kick their asse's!" (Hell's Angels climb in the ring,as the other Legion member's stay outside the ring. Hate & Anger remove their belts, and hand them to the ref, who hold's them in the air, and then he hand's them out to the ring attendant. Hell's Angels remove their other clothing, and hand it out to the ring attendant. Mayhem and Anger climb out of the ring) Dan Crawford:"This should be a great match!It will be Hate,and Menace starting off!They both lock-up,and Menace get's Hate in a headlock,and is wrenching it on tight!Hate get's out of it and throws Menace to the ropes,but Menace come's back and bodycheck's Hate to the mat!" Rex Roseman:"Yeah,way to go Fatal Clan!D-oh Hate just got up,he didn't feel that check,and Menace is gloating to the fans!Hate turn's Menace around,and nail's with him a punch to the nose,and the ref give's him a warning!Disqualify him ref,that's not fair!Hate chuck's Menace to the ropes,Menace come's back,and is powerslammed to the mat!Hate cover's Menace,no way... 1.... Menace kicks-out!" Dan Crawford:"Hate pick's up Menace by the hair,but Menace drop's down and low blow's Hate!The ref warn's Menace,Menace goes over and tag's in Mayhem!Mayhem is now in the ring,and he pick's up Hate,and he chuck's him to the ropes,but Hate ducked under a clothesline attempt,and he tag's in Anger,but Mayhem doesn't know it,and Mayhem spinebuster's Hate!Anger is climbing up to the top rope,and Mayhem is still punching Hate!Anger jump's off,and flying dropkick's Mayhem to the mat,a very good move there by Anger!" Rex Roseman:"I didn't think so,I thought it really sucked!Anger pick's up Mayhem,and chuck's him to the ropes,and Anger runs to the other ropes,come's back and does a bulldog on Mayhem!Come on Mayhem get the tag ,and let Menace in to kick some ass!" Dan Crawford:"Watch the language Rex,we're on live tv,where little kid's are watching us!" Rex Roseman:"Kid's,Shmids!Anger pick's up Mayhem,and piledriver's him back to the mat!Anger is climbing up to the top rope,but wait Menace goe's over to where he is,and he grab's Anger's foot,and trip's him up off the top rope,and throws him back into the ring!Now that's one great move there by Menace!Hate is trying to come into the ring,but the ref is forcing him back out!Hahaha,this is great,pretty soon,we're going to have New UWA Tag Team Champions!Both wrestler's are down inside the ring,and the ref is starting to count them out... 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... Mayhem is getting up,and he is making his way over to where Menace is,yes,but damn Anger is up too,and he's making his way over to where Hate is,and Mayhem make's the tag,but so does Anger!" Dan Crawford:"This has been a great match so far!Hate jump's over the ropes,and he flying clothesline's Menace to the mat,Mayhem is back up,and he charge's Hate,but Hate powerslam's him to the mat!Menace is back up,but Hate suplex's him to the mat!Mayhem is back up,and he charge's Hate again,but Hate hiptosse's him out of the ring,by The FBI,who are helping Mayhem up!Wait a minute Lord Zero is distracting the ref,and Wrestlingman is over arguing with Suicide,Riot,and Mayhem!Anger is back in the ring,and Hell's Angels are double teaming Menace!They both chuck him to the ropes,and they double clothesline Menace!They both pick up Menace,and double suplex him back down to the mat!Anger climb's back out of the ring,Lord Zero get's down off the apron,stops distracting the ref,and Wrestlingman goes back over to where The LOD is!Hate tag's in Anger who goes up to the top rope,Anger is going to do a moonsault!" Rex Roseman:"Anger jumps,and yes,yes,yes,yes,yes,yes,yes,yes,Menace moved,and Anger hit the mat hard,and he is hurting badly!Menace is getting up now,and he covers Anger,yes we got new champs... 1... 2... 3,no,crap,Anger got a shoulder up in the nick of time!Menace pick's up Anger,and he is going to Powerbomb him,yes,do it,do it,Menace powerbomb's Anger back down to the mat!Menace go's over,and tags in Mayhem,but Menace is staying in the ring,and Hate is trying to get the ref to get Menace out of the ring,but Hate is distracting the ref instead,haha,what a geek!Menace,and Mayhem are both stomping down,and kicking down on Anger!They both pick up Anger,and do a double ddt!Menace is climbing up to the top rope,and Mayhem is setting Anger up for a piledriver,now this will get The Fatal Clan the victory if they get this!Mayhem pick's up Anger,and Menace jump's off the top rope,and they do a spike piledriver on Anger,and Menace climb's out of the ring,and so does Hate!Mayhem covers Anger,new champs... 1... 2... 3,no,damn it,so close again,to new champs,but Anger kicked-out again,that dirty little bastard!" Dan Crawford:"Rex,watch your language,because if you don't President TNT might fire you,which would be great for me!Mayhem is going up to the top rope,and he is going to do a 360 Degree Abdominal Splash on Anger,and this will definitely end the match if he connect's with this!Mayhem jumps off the top rope,and he just did the 360 Degree flip,now he's on his way down,but Anger moved,and Mayhem hit the mat hard!Both wrestler's are down,again,and the ref is counting them out... 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... 8... Anger is getting up,and he go's over and tag's in Hate,and the fans go nuts!Menace climb's into the ring,but Hate dropkick's him out of the ring,by The LOD,which is not good for Menace!Anger is back up,and Hate tag's him in!Hate has just given the sign for The Flame Thrower,and he hoists Mayhem up onto his shoulders,and Anger is up on the top rope!Anger jumps off,and clotheslines Mayhem down to the mat,and he covers him... 1... 2... 3!!! Rex Roseman:"But wait this ain't fair Lord Zero just chucked Menace back into the ring,and Hate is stomping down on him,and Anger is stomping down on Mayhem!But yes,go Suicide,and Riot!Suicide,and Riot are up on the top ropes,and they jump off and do flying clothesline's on Hate,and Anger!Lord Zero,Wrestlingman,and Bad Habit roll into the ring,but they left their bat's outside the ring!Menace,and Mayhem are now back up,and The Fatal Clan,and Hell's Angels are slugging it out!Wrestlingman,and Suicide are punching,and kicking at each other!Lord Zero,and Bad Habit,are going at it with Riot!Hell's Angels just clotheslined The Fatal Clan out of the ring!Bad Habit grabs Riot,no no no,don't do it,Bad Habit does The Eraser(Stone Cold Stunner)on Riot,and rolls him out of the ring!Lord Zero just cheapshotted Suicide in the back of the head with a forearm!Wrestlingman grabs Suicide by the leg's,no don't tell me,no get some help for Suicide!Wrestlingman hooks The Submitor on Suicide,and Lord Zero,Bad Habit,Hate,and Anger are stomping on Suicide,and Wrestlingman is not letting go!Yes finally some loser's have decided to help Suicide!The officials are now all rolling in the ring,and Wrestlingman finally let's go!The officials help up Suicide,and are helping him to the back,and they are also helping Riot,Menace,and Mayhem to the back,as well!The Legion of Darkness are now all in the ring,with their little gay jackets,and little pansy bats,and just listen to these loser fans there cheering these punks!" ("Bow Down"by The Westside Connections blares as the fans go nuts) Announcer:"The winners of the match,and Still UWA Tag Team Champions,Hell's Angels,Hate & Anger!"(The fans go nuts) (All of The LOD climb out of the ring,and go to the back,slapping hands with the fans) Dan Crawford:"What a great match,and what a brawl afterwards!Hell's Angels we're lucky to get that win,but they did,and their still the champs!" Rex Roseman:"Yeah but they won't be for long,because The Fatal Clan will get The FBI's revenge,and they will kick Hate & Anger's asse's,and will win that re-match,and then they will become The New UWA Tag Team Champions,which will be great to see!Hahahahahahahahaha!"
