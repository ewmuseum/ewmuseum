PT. 3 UWA Slaughter House

7 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DC: "Well folks its now time for the tag team match between The Fearsome Foursomes Crusher Stevenson,and Scott Storm against Sabbath and Eric Valentine,and this will be a great one!" RR:"No it won't this will be a snoozer,because all of these guys suck,and are nothing compared to Johnny Tambo,and the other Solid Gold Members!" DC:"Sure Rex,sure!Let's head down to ringside!" (Michael Cook is inside the ring,with the microphone) Michael Cook:"Ladies and Gentlemen this match is scheduled for one fall!"(The fans go nuts) (The arena goes dark, and three spotlights shine,in a semi-circle, about five feet from the curtain. "Code Blue" by T.S.O.L. blares over the loudspeakers. From the curtain comes a young, pretty oriental lady, wearing street clothes,with her waist long hair flowing behind her body. She takes a position in the center circle.) Michael Cook:"Ladies and gentlemen, making their way down to the ring area, at a total combined weight of four-hundred and ninety four pounds... (Fireworks explode as "Holiday"Eric Valentine and Sabbath make their way to Sayaka's left and right, holding a crowbar and sheathed. She takes their arms, and raises them as more fireworks rock the arena, and the crowd pops loudly.) MC: ...here are "Holidayyyyyyy"....ERIC VALENTIIIINE(crowd pop) and his tag team partner...the "Walking Contradiction"...SAAAABBAAAATH!!!(Another crowd pop)Together...they are... (The trio make their way to ringside, both dressed in typical attire. Both Valentine and Sabbath slap the fans hands. Valentine jumps up on the ring apron, raising his arms to the roaring crowd, as Sabbath hands his trenchcoat and sunglasses to Sayaka.) MC: DEATH TAKES A HOLIDAY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!(HUGE crowd pop) (Sabbath joins Valentine in the ring, and whispers in his ear, perhaps going over some last minute strategy.) ("Welcome to the Jungle"by Guns N' Roses,plays over the PA,and the fans go nuts) Michael Cook:"And their opponents,here are Crusher Stevenson,and "Superstar"Scott Storm!"(The fans go nuts) (Crusher Stevenson,and "Superstar"Scott Storm come out,wearing their usual attire.They both slap hands with the fans as they come down to ringside.Then they both climb up onto the apron,and into the ring,talking to each other,for a minute or two) Ding Ding Ding DC:"Well it's going to be Sabbath,and Crusher Stevenson starting it out!" RR:"Wake me up when it's over,because this is going to be boring!" DC:"No it won't Rex,just watch the match,and help me commentate!" RR:"Oh alright,you big baby!" DC:"Here we go!They both lock up,and Crusher Stevenson shoves Sabbath into away from him,and he spits down at Sabbath,and that was unnecessary!Sabbath is back up,and he looks ticked off!They both lock up again,and Sabbath puts Crusher into a headlock,but Crusher chucks him to the ropes,Sabbath comes back,and Stevenson bodychecks him to the mat,Crusher runs to the ropes,he comes back,jumps over Sabbath,Sabbath is back up,and he does a hurricarana on Stevenson,and Crusher is hurt!Sabbath with the cover... 1... kick-out by Crusher!" RR:"Your not going to get him that fast,idiot!Sabbath picks up Crusher Stevenson,he sets him up,and he pildrives him back down to the mat!Sabbatch picks up Stevenson,and he tags in Eric Valentine!They both chuck Stevenson to the ropes,he comes back,and they double clothesline him down to the mat!NO!Crusher ducked undered it,he runs to the ropes,comes back,and he double clotheslines both of them down to the mat!Now this is what I want to see four geeks kicking BLEEP against each other!All three are down inside the ring,and the ref is counting Crusher,and Eric Valentine out,as Scott Storm is clapping his hands,and the dumb fans are clapping with him... 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... Crusher Stevenson is up,and he is making his way towards Superstar,but both Eric and Sabbath grabs his legs!Crusher is dragging them with him though,he dives,and makes the tag to Scott Storm!Scott jumps over the ropes,and he gives Eric a big right hand knocking him down to the mat,and he dropkicks Sabbath out of the ring!" DC:"Now its Scott Storm dominating this match,for their team!Scott Storm picks up Eric Valentine,he sets him up between his legs,he hooks the arms,and double underhook powerbomb on Holiday,and the cover... 1... 2... no,quick kick-out by Eric Valentine!Scott Storm picks up Eric Valentine,he chucks him to the opposite turnbuckle,he charges Holiday,but Eric puts the boot up,and he catches Superstar,and Scott is now down on the mat!Valentine picks up Superstar,and he puts him onto the top rope!Eric Valentine climbs up onto the second turnbuckle,he picks up Scott Storm,and he superplexe's him down to the mat,and both men are now down,and out!The ref is now counting them out... 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... Both men are getting up,but Eric Valentine made the tag quicker,and Sabbath comes in,and he drags Scott Storm into the center of the ring!But Crusher Stevenson just climbed into the ring,and he is charging Eric Valentine,but Holiday turns around,and he back bodydrops Crusher Stevenson out of the ring,right onto those pads,and Crusher is hurting badly!" RR:"Yeah kick each other BLEEP's!Sabbath just gave the signal for his finisher,the Black Sabbath!Sabbath sets up,and he now has the Black Sabbath(inverted powerbomb...then full nelson...but instead of locking the the fingers he jabs his thumb into the pressure points under the jaw) on Scott Storm,and the ref is asking if Superstar gives up!Yes,he does!Finally the snoozer of a match is over!" Ding Ding Ding ("Code Blue"by T.S.O.L.,plays over the PA,and the fans go nuts) Michael Cook:"The winners of the match,via submission,the team of Sabbath,and "Holiday"Eric Valentinnnneeee!"(The fans go nuts) DC:"Sayaka is now in the ring raising Sabbath's,and Eric Valentine's arms in the air!The threesome are now all leaving together,slapping hands with all of the fans!Crusher Stevenson is now up,and he rolls back into the ring!Crusher helps up Scott Storm,and he is helping him to the back,as the fans cheer them on a good effort!" RR: Well I think this may have been the first time that Scott Storm has given up...in any match! DC: Could have been...all four teams are out of here now...but I'm understanding that something big is going down in the locker room. (jumbotron shows Sabbath laying unconscious in the center of a broken table...Eric Valentine laying further down the hallway in a pool of blood..) DC: Woah! What the hell is going on here!? RR: Somebody knocked out Death Takes a Holiday! (crowd looks to the locker room entry...rising and booing...throwing trash) DC: OH NO! FLAMING BOB HAS GOT SAYAKA! RR: Sayaka looks barely conscious! She's just staggering along behind him! And LOOK AT THAT! BOB JUST FLASHED A HYPODERMIC NEEDLE TO THE CROWD AND DROPPED IT! DC: This is terrible...Bob now rolling her in the ring...and now getting in himself...with a mic. Bob: So what was that I heard you say..."Walking Contradiction?" I'd never get hold of your little wife eh? (Bob grabs Sayaka by the arm and pulls her to her feet...she stands with her eyes half open...he puts an arm around her shoulders) Bob: Well who's baby is she now? Ha ha ha! DC: Somebody get down here...help Sayaka! Bob: I don't want you're wife Sabbath... (lets go of her...leaving her standing in the middle of the ring) RR: What? Thank God. DC: NO! BOB HAS JUST THROWN A CHAIR IN THE RING...AND HE'S IN THERE WITH THE CHAIR!!!!!! ****************SMACK***************** (Bob smacks the hair over the back of Sayaka's head...she falls into a heap on the mat...crowd awes...growing silent) DC: No... Bob: She won't be much good to either of us now. RR: Here comes Big Butch Thornton! Bob: See you at Chaos. DC: And Bob just high tailed it out of the ring and into the crowd...and Big Butch Thornton is in the ring...dressed in jeans and cowboy boots... RR: Let's get some medical people down here! DC: Stretchers...paramedics are making their way down to the ring...and here comes Rex Overlord as well...with a chair. RR: That chair is covered with blood! DC: Yes it is...but Overlord drops it...and he's now in the ring...and Donna Di Notte is down there...she's kneeled beside Sayaka. This is a disaster. RR: Last time Bob did this to Donna Di Notte...but not in the head! DC: And Overlord yelling something in the face of Butch Thornton...Butch Thornton yelling right back...pointing fingers... RR: Hell On Earth gets the last laugh...always. DC: Are you condoning the actions of Flaming Bob!? RR: Not at all! DC: At least...you know there is a good side. Bob didn't take Sayaka...and he had the opportunity. But by no means does that leave Sabbath in debt to him or anything. We see now that Butch Thornton is leaving the ring...and Overlord leaning back into the corner... RR: LOOK AT THIS! DC: SABBATH RUNNING LIKE A MADMAN FROM THE LOCKER ROOM! RR: He almost knocked BBT down! DC: Right past BBT...and in the ring with Sayaka...look at the tears. These medical people are strapping Sayaka down...and she's not responding...and from the locker room...it's President Gordon... RR: We've got an all-star cast out here! DC: Gordon is in the ring now...and looking on... RR: Eric Valentine is in the aisle now having a few words with BBT...and he's bleeding from the back of the head. And Gordon has the mic now... Gordon: Sabbath...you've got your Singapore Slaughter match at Christmas Chaos. Until I get a medical report on Sayaka...well I can't take any further action against Bob until then. DC: Gordon dropped the mic...and now Overlord is out of the ring... RR: They're getting Sayaka out of here...carrying her stretcher back... DC: And Sabbath is following them...not a bit concerned about the whereabouts of Flaming Bob... RR: Look at this! Eric Valentine is pointing to that bloody chair in the aisle! DC: Is that the chair that Overlord carried out? RR: Yeah...and now he's looking at Overlord. Valentine and BBT are blaming Overlord! Ha ha ha! DC: And Overlord looking more confused than ever... RR: Overlord did it Crawford! DC: Never in my life have I ever known Rex Overlord to attack a woman...or allow a woman to be attacked. I think you're wrong Rex. He's saved Donna many times...Sensational Sara...and she was the manager of his enemy! RR: He did it! He did it! DC: No way...I won't buy it. BBT and Valentine it seems now are assured as they all just shook hands. And I believe we've got our Anti-Hell On Earth team. Rex Overlord, Big Butch Thornton, Sabbath, and "Holiday" Eric Valentine. RR: Whatever you say Crawford. DC: Our time is up. I send my best wishes to Sayaka...hoping she gets better...and soon.
