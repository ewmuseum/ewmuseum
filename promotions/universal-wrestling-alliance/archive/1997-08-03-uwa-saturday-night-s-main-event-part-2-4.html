UWA Saturday Night's Main Event part 2/4

3 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dan Crawford: Welcome back. What a night it has been here in the Igloo so far. If you have just joined then you missed the true great one, Mario Lemieux scare off the two UWA court jesters, Zack Fooley and Dyken when they attacked Jean Guy Lavalle after his debut victory. Rex Roseman: I do have to admit Crawford, Fooley and Dyken are two bafoons, they may as well jump ship to ACEW where they can get their asses whiped on a regular bases by Purple Power and "Galloping" [name withheld]. If they arn't out of business by the time they get there. Dan Crawford: But lets get back to the action of the fastest growing wrestling federation anywhere in the world. Up next we have a match for the right to become the number one contender for the Internaional title. The winner will recieve a title shot against Lord Zero. Take it away Mike. ========================================== Winner recieves International title shot ========================================== Crusher Stevenson v. Malibu Mike ========================================== (cut to the ring, where the announcer stands in the middle) Micheal Cook: This match is for one fall...and is for the chance to wrestle for the UWA INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (crowd cheers!!!) Micheal Cook: Introducing first... (HBK's entrance music plays) Announcer: COMING DOWN THE AISLE, ACCOMPANIED BY THE LOVELY SENSATIONAL SARA, STANDING AT 6 FOOT 6 AND WEIGHING IN AT 275 POUNDS....MALIBU MIKE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Fans cheer as Malibu Mike makes his way down the ring with Sensational Sara at his side. Malibu is wearing Silver tights and Silver Boots and Sara is wearing a Silver Gown.... Malibu Flexs as Silver fireworks shoot off behind him to a roar by the fans) Crawford: I know the ladies are definately behind one Malibu Mike as he is already in the ring. But he has his hands full with this guy coming out right now! ("Man In The Box" by Alice and Chains blasts through the arena as the crowd arupts in cheers. Crusher Stevenson walks out with a determined look on his face. He is wearing black shorts tights with "Crusher" written in electric blue on the back. He is wearing black kneepads and boots. He walks to the ring, not taking his eyes of Malibu Mike for one second, with intensity in his eyes.) Micheal Cook: Introducing his opponent...6'5" and 265 pounds...here is CRUSHER STEVENSON!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (fans cheer) Roseman: Listen to all those morons cheer for this loser Stevenson. I could take him on any day of the week! Crawford: About the only guy you could take on are those 2 prelims Big Butch Thornton fought last week! Roseman: No way, those guys were tough! Crawford: Anyway....Crusher Stevenson looks ready for his oppurtunity to get a shot at the International Champion, Lord Zero. This should be a great matchup! (bell rings) Crawford: And we're underway...Malibu trying to shake hands with Crusher...Crusher obliges...now they lock up...Malibu sends Crusher to the ropes...lays down and Crusher again against the ropes...now a leapfrog by Mailbu...WAIT Crusher catches him...REVERSE ATOMIC DROP...that looks like it hurt...Malibu's gonna feel that one in the morning...Crusher bounces against the ropes...and a flying knee into Malibu's back...Malibu hits the mat... Roseman: Where's the Legion? I thought they'd be out by now! Crawford: Why don't you pay attention to the match? Now Crusher...has Malibu on his face...drives his knee into Malibu's back...Malibu is screaming in pain as Sara looks worried...Crusher drives the knee into Malibu's back again...and now picks him up...whips Malibu to the ropes...BIG bear hug...the ref is checking Malibu...and he shakes the ref off...Malibu elbows Crusher in the back of the head...and again...Crusher lets go of the hold...and now Malibu gets a chance for some payback...Malibu has Crusher groggy...BULLDOG...Malibu covers... 1...and a kickout from Crusher...Malibu looks frustrated as he asks the ref to count faster... Roseman: We have a visitor! (From the entrance, LordZero comes out with a Pittsburgh Steelers jersey on and carrying an aluminum bat. He signals to the back, and Bad Habit, Hate and Anger come out. Bad Habit has on a Pittsburgh Penguins jersey, and Hate & Anger are in their wrestling attire. Bad Habit has his bat, and Hate & Anger carry their belts. They just stand there, talking to each other.) Roseman: Make it visitors! Maybe NOW we'll see some action! Crawford: Now Malibu smiling as he knows he has backup...picks Crusher up...and a tombstone piledriver...Malibu goes for another cover... 1...2...and Crusher kicks out...now Malibu signaling for the Wipeout...sets Crusher up for his patened Perfect-Plex variation...but he can't get Crusher up...tries again...but still no luck...Crusher reverses...THE CRUSHER BREAKER...Malibu is in pain in that torture rack...the ref checks and Malibu says no...wait...Sara trips Crusher...he breaks the hold...he is going after Sara!!! Roseman: Kick her a...er butt! Crawford: Crusher doesn't see Malibu getting up...OUCH...Malibu hits Crusher with a forearm to the back of the head...now he turns Crusher over...SCORPION DEATH LOCK!!!!!!!!!! (LordZero, seeing that Malibu might win, runs down to the ring...followed by Bad Habit, Hate & Anger.) Crawford: Crusher is in pain as Sara smiles...Crusher says no...wait...Bad Habit is on the ring apron...he gets the refs attention...LordZero in the ring...SOULCRUSHER ON MALIBU MIKE!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Malibu is out for the count...now Hate & Anger come in...start beating on Crusher with the bats!!!!!!!! The ref turns...and he signals for the bell!!!!!!!!! Announcer: The referee has declared this match a NO CONTEST!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Roseman: I knew that the Legion wouldn't accept that puke! I love it! Crawford: Well, now LordZero has no +-one to face...WAIT!!!!!!!! (fans go crazy as "TNT" by AC/DC blares over the speakers. TNT is walking to the ring, in a nice black suit, and a UWA tie. He grabs a microphone as he comes into the ring.) TNT: I've had enough of you guys! You four have caused enough trouble! LordZero, one more stunt like that, and you're gonna forfeit that title you so treasure. And guess what? You think that this match is over? Well, I'm the president, and I say what goes! This match is to continue until there is a WINNER! LordZero: *bleep* YOU! LET THE DAMN MATCH CONTINUE! I'LL JUST KEEP COMING IN HERE AND KICKING @$$ UNTIL BOTH OF THEM ARE FINISHED! TNT: Try it, and you four will be asking for it! (LordZero picks Malibu Mike up and gives him another SoulCrusher) LordZero: How about that? What are you gonna do now loser? TNT: Well, since Hell's Angels have a title defense tonight, I can't do anything today. (LordZero smiles and the Legion laughs) BUT...starting Monday, you guys are SUSPENDED! You 4 are hereby SUSPENDED for 1 week...and FINED $100,000! Now, get the hell outta here, otherwise I'll make it longer! (Security escorts LordZero, Bad Habit, Hate & Anger as LordZero yells "You're going down TNT...IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR!" TNT leaves, and the bell rings again) Crawford: Finally, TNT lays down the law! Roseman: TNT that loser! Now what fun am I gonna have? Crawford: Well, Malibu and Crusher are back up...Crusher whips a groggy Malibu to the ropes...and a gorilla press slam...he signals for the Crusher Breaker...he's got Mailbu in the rack again...the ref checks Malibu...and he's shaking it off...Malibu is screaming... Roseman: Oh who cares! Get it over with! Crawford: And that's it, the ref signals for the bell. (ding ding ding) Micheal Cook: The winner fo this match...CRUSHER STAVENSON (Crusher raises his arms to cheers from the crowd. Malibu Mike slowly gets up and shakes Crusher's hand and then leaves the ring with Sara.) Dan Crawford: I guess Malibu Mike never got over the two Soulcrushers he gave him.) Rex Roseman: He never got over the first one. But I am sure that he will be out for revenge. Dan Crawford: In any case, it was a great display of athleticism from both competitors and the effort should be applauded. Rex Roseman: Yippe Ya Wo! Dan Crawford: I guess I was right about you knowing the Hooked On Phonics number by heart. Rex Roseman: Ahhh, we have to go to break now, so stay tuned for two exciting title matches. ***break*** commercials ***break***
