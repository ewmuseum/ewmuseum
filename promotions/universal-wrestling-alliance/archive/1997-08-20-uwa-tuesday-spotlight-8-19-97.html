People are walking up and down the strip looking to buy food, taking a walk over lunch and talking with friends. Trucks are being loaded and unloaded with fresh produce. In the middle of the crowd a young man with Dark Brown hair wearing a Iron City Beer shirt, blue jeans and cowboy boots walks out of Wholey's market. He puts on his dark sunglasses and starts to walk down the strip. The camera focuses in on the man.) R.A.D: Let me introduce myself, my name is R.A.D. and this is my hometown Pittsburgh, PA. Isn't this town great. (The camera pans over the skyline of Pittsburgh) Great food, great sports teams, great people and a great wrestling town. I always went to the Civic Arena to watch WWF matches when I was a kid and when I saw my first match I was hooked. (R.A.D. starts to walk and the camera follows him.) R.A.D: I've been wrestling for about two years and in those two years I've gain valuable wrestling experience. I became the GFWWF World T.V. champ and two time S.W.A. World Heavyweight champ. Though they were minor wrestling leagues I made an impact on them and I would like to do the same in the U.W.A. I not going to come out and say that I'm going to kick everyones butt and become the U.W.A. World champion, I'm not that type of guy. I'm a hard worker who puts blood and sweat into everything he does. I'm looking forward to the competition in the U.W.A. I've been to a couple of the events and I was impressed. The fans in the U.W.A. will finally get to see the R.A.D. man in action and believe me you won't be disappointed. But for know I'm going to help my buddy Scott unload some Watermelons that just came in. (R.A.D. walks over to a truck that is loaded with Watermelons. "Hey R.A.D." says Scott as both of them shake hands and begin to unload Watermelons. The only source of light is an array of flames that burns in the North West corner of this darkened and empty building. The sky is overcast, shielding the light from the moon and stars of this dreary night. One man, wearing a black hooded sweatshirt with khaki pants and black boots, stands against a fatigued and fractured cement wall. The other, wearing camouflage pants, tan boots, and the new official "Ministry of Justice" tee shirt, sits upon a wooden box crate. Judge Jones begins to speak.] JUDGE: If I may be so humble as to beg for the forgiveness of our fans and colleagues for mine and Minos' absence this past week. You see, after our glorious victory at Sunday Night's Main Event two weeks ago, I celebrated a little too much with the drink. "How much?" you may ask. Well, let's just say it was enough to have me visit the detoxification ward of the Mount Sinai Medical Center for a few days. Nevertheless, business for the Ministry remains unchanged. [pushing himself from the wall, Minos approaches the camera and begins to speak.] MINOS: Heavy the head that wears the crown. The ultimate prize…for the ultimate price. Obsequious and enslaved to the mandates of smiley glad hands in gray suits; jackals to the totalitarian machine that is the UWA bureaucracy. Hate, Anger, my friends, what has become of you? Are not kings the masters of their own destiny? Is that not even true for the lowliest of the monarch's humble and philistine serfs? But why, then, are the world champions subject to the edicts of men in gray suits who have nothing to do with the course of events in the arena of battle? You call yourselves champions…shame, damn shame…you're nothing more than lackeys. [sitting up from the crate, Judge Jones walks toward the camera.] JUDGE: A week from this Sunday, Summer Showdown, the Ministry of Justice versus Hell's Angels. Hate, Anger, can't you see?...Justice is no longer blind.
