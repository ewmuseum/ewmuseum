UWATuesday Night War 11/11/97

12 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE UNIVERSAL WRESTLING ALLIANCE PRESENTS... ========================================================= TUESDAY NIGHT ========================================================= ##### ##### ######## ############## ##### ##### ########## ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ### ##### ##### ##### ############### ######## ######## ################ ############## ####### ####### ##### ##### ##### ##### ###### ###### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ========================================================== DATE: November 11, 1997 LOCATION: Knickerbocker Arena - Albany, GA ========================================================== ("Ten Seconds Down" plays as scene opens to a jam packed Knickerbocker Arena) Dan Crawford: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to UWA Tuesday Night War!!!! Take a look at this capacity crowd! I'm Dan Crawford and this is "Red" Rex Roseman! "Red" Rex Roseman: Yeah...Roseman...not Rossman, Eliminator! Stupid punk! DC: Now Rex... RR: I'm chilled...I'm chilled... DC: Alright...take a look!! I've never seen such a rowdy crowd as we've got here tonight! RR: Look at that!!! Ha ha ha!!! That sign says "Bring back 'Dangerous' D.C. Doug"!!!!! Ha ha ha! What a moron! DC: D.C. Doug...he was one of the all time greats Rex... RR: He was one of the all time losers! Wait! No wonder! That's the Eliminator holding the sign!! Ha ha ha! DC: Give me a break! It is not! RR: Ease up Crawford! Take that coat hanger out of your jacket and hang loose my man! DC: Well...let's get this thing underway. ("The Green Machine" plays) Micheal Cook: THIS MATCH IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL! CURRENTLY COMING = DOWN THE AISLE, HAILING FROM OAKLAND CALIFORNIA, WEIGHING IN AT 257 LBS, AND STANDING 6' 4", "THE EXTREME MISFIT" GAR KRYGER. (Gar Kryger comes down to the ring wearing black tights with Extreme in red on one side of his right leg and Misfit on the left side of his left leg. He has on black boots, with red laces and on the butt of his tights is a big ball of fire. He is wearing a half shredded t-shirt that says "The Extreme Misfit" on the front and Gar Kryger on the back. He steps into the ring and props himself onto the turnbuckle.) DC: There's Gar Kryger...entering to the music of Solid Gold...so you know he must be... RR: He's dripping with it Crawford!!! Ha ha ha! This guy's great. DC: Wait...Kryger putting his fingers in his ears. He doesn't want to hear any more! And Kryger is asking for the mic...and he's got it... Kryger: I will NEVER be Solid Gold 4 Eva, so shut that damn music off. (Music comes to a stop) Kryger: There's only one stable in the UWA for Gar Kryger...and it's not these wannabes. Natural Currency...Gar Kryger is here if you need him. If we can do business...great. If not...then it's been fun. (throws the mic outside to Michael Cook) RR: Grrrr...punk! You're a punk Kryger! DC: Kryger turning his back on Solid Gold just like that...and I certainly didn't expect to see this. ("In the Summertime" by Mungo Jerry plays) Michael Cook:Hailing from Manhattan, New york, Standing at 5"10 and weighing 227 pounds, Here is the man who always has a firework up his sleeve.."Holiday" Errrriicccc Vaaaallllleeeeeeeennnnntttttiiiinnnneee! (Fireworks light up the entranceway shooting above the crowd and past the ring. "Holiday" Eric Valentine walks out in his black leather jacket and red pants with green stripes down the side with Ray-ban shades on his eyes. He walks down the aisle smiling and slapping hands with the fans before reaching the ring. As he reaches it, he takes off his shades and tosses them into the crowd before sliding into the ring) DC: This match will be very interesting...a great way to kick off TNW this week. RR: Well I was mistaken about Kryger. He's a loser. DC: So you're going for Valentine? RR: I hate these goody-two-shoes guys that come out here and smile like howdy doody. No, I'm not voting for him. I'm voting that Solid Gold comes out here and kicks both of their butts! DC: Well to my knowledge Solid Gold isn't in the building...and neither is Natural Currency for that matter. RR: How can you have a show without Solid Gold? Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: Both men staring each other down...and Kryger backing into the corner...Valentine back into his...standing on the second turnbuckle...playing the fans! (cheers) Wait...from behind it's Gar Kryger...but Holiday saw it coming...he jumps over his head...and Kryger crashes into the buckle...Holiday rolling him up... 1... 2... no. Not enough. RR: Have you seen Johnny Tambo's new movie Crawford? DC: I must say that I haven't seen a movie in some time. RR: Well you should see this one. You'll laugh your butt off! It was great! DC: I didn't think Tambo's movie was a comedy Rex. RR: Of course it was. DC: I heard it was suspense/thriller. RR: Don't be silly Crawford! DC: Kryger and Holiday up...big slap across the face by Kryger...but Holiday tackles him back against the ropes...going at him with lefts and rights. Kryger now trying to avoid those swings...and Holiday off allowing a clean break...Kryger up...BIG right hand delivered by "Holiday" Eric Valentine!!! (cheers) And Kryger goes down! RR: Did you see how the Jaguars took the Chiefs yesterday Crawford? That shocked me at first...but when I heard that Grbak had been injured and Rich Gannon had started then I understood. DC: Why don't you pay attention!? RR: It's boring!!! DC: Holiday picking Kryger up now...by the hair...throwing him to the ropes...BEAUTIFUL DROPKICK!!!!!!!!!! And Kryger grabbing his nose...now rolling under the bottom rope. Holiday...grabbing the top rope...JUMPING UP AND OVER....TACKLING KRYGER FROM THE AIR!!!! (cheers) RR: Can you get anything else on this thing? (pushing buttons on the monitor) DC: Get real. Kryger and Holiday both down...and it took the wind out of them both. Holiday now is up...and he's sending Kryger back in. Holiday up now on the apron...but Kryger up...and hooking Holiday for a suplex! he's going to suplex him back into the ring! RR: Hey! I found WOWA Tuesday Jerkoff!!! ha ha ha! DC: No! Holiday reversing and suplexing Kryger out of the ring...KRYGER'S BACK HITTING HARD RIGHT OVER THAT RAILING AS HOLIDAY CRASHES TO THE FLOOR!!!!!!!!! (cheers) RR: Ha ha ha! Look at this. Their titles look like tin foil! DC: Will you stop? The WOWA doesn't even have a show on Tuesday...I don't think. RR: It's right here on the boob tube Crawford! DC: Pull your head out of your butt Rex! You're watching the WOWA when you should be paying attention to this spectacular match of rookies!!! Kryger now is hurt...and he can't even stand up. But Holiday holding him by the hair and helping out...now pulling him backward over that railing. Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: Woah!!! Both men have been counted out! I didn't even notice! RR: Ha ha ha! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the referee has ruled this match a double count out! (boos) DC: Holiday now just walking away as Kryger is hurt in the floor...but now getting to his feet as well. You missed a great match Rex. RR: Blah. DC: Blah. RR: Blah. DC: Alright...no holds barred...falls count anywhere. The last time for this was Suicide vs. The Adviser. And that was a great match. Let's go to Michael Cook. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is scheduled for one fall! No disqualifications...no count outs...and falls count anywhere! (cheers) ("Harvester of Sorrow" by Metallica plays. Chris Simmons walks out. He's carrying a baseball bat with him.) DC: He's got a baseball bat!! RR: No rules in this match, remember? (Simmons walks up the steps and into te ring. He walks to the other side of the ring, leans against the turnbuckle, and waits for Nick Willams to come out.) Michael Cook: Now approaching the ring area...from Camden, NJ...standing at 5'11"...weighing 228lbs..."CRUSHER" CHRIS SIMMONS!!!!!!!!!!!! (cheers) DC: And this crowd is really behind Simmons. RR: Well...he and Williams gave a tremendous effort at Hellstorm. But you know Williams is the tougher of the two... DC: No, I don't know that. RR: Oh come on Crawford! DC: That's what this match is for Rex! Their last match both men collapsed and couldn't continue! RR: Well... ("Burden in my Hand" by Soundgarden plays over the P.A. system) Micheal Cook: From the Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, Nick "The Punisher" Williams!!!!!! DC: And Jessica Cox has come out...manager of The Punisher. But...she's looking around. I don't think she knows where Williams is! RR: She's just standing there and looking back at the entrance. I think I need to go comfort her Crawford. Hehehe... DC: I think you need to sit there and do your job. Simmons now getting a little impatient...hollering at the ref. And...some guy in an Eagles jersey is stepping over the railing... RR: IT'S RODNEY PEETE!!!!!!! DC: NO! IT'S NICK WILLIAMS!!!! (boos) Williams just tossed a metal trash can full of boards...and...a guitar...woah! He's got the full arsenal!!! Simmons walking over and sticking his head out...grabbing Williams by the hair...but Williams has a chair in his hand...SMASHES IT OVER THE HEAD OF SIMMONS!!!!!! RR: WOAH! DC: And now...Simmons has fallen...holding onto that baseball bat...and Williams getting in the ring...grabbing one of those boards. Simmons jabbing the end of that ball bat into Williams throat...and hitting him in the head with it!!! Williams goes down...holding his jaw! Ouch! RR: Ha ha ha! DC: And Simmons is up with that ball bat...and SPLINTERING IT OVER THE BACK OF WILLIAMS!!!!! RR: Woah! He should have used aluminum! DC: Williams is down and not having a good time here. Simmons battering him with a piece of that baseball bat over the back of his head...and blood now flowing freely from the hair of Nick Williams. Jessica Cox is completely astonished on the outside. RR: Come on Williams! DC: Williams now trying to get up and away...but Simmons stomping him in the lower back...and now pulling him out into the center of the ring. Simmons props a steel chair over the head of Nick Williams. WE'VE SEEN THIS BEFORE!!!!!!! Flying off the top...CATCHING NICK WILLIAM'S BOOT IN THE FACE!!!!! (cheers) RR: And Simmons up now...sinking back into that corner...grabbing his face! And Williams getting up to his feet now...slowly...blood running down...staining that green and white Eagles jersey. Oooh! DC: Williams has got that guitar...SHATTERING IT OVER THE HEAD OF CHRIS SIMMONS!!!!!!! (boos) And Simmons goes down...Williams for the pinfall... 1... 2... foot on the ropes!!! (cheers) Williams now...rolling out of the ring...and setting up that table!!! Now...into the ring...he's nudging Simmons out onto the ring apron... RR: Oh no...something original. That always means blood Crawford! Ha ha ha! DC: Well Nick Williams constantly feeling the back of his head...blood running down his neck and back. And he's out on the ring apron...lifting Simmons...hoisting him on his shoulder...RUNNING POWERSLAM...JUMPING OFF THE APRON AND POWERSLAMMING CHRIS SIMMONS THROUGH THE TABLE!!!!! He pins him!!! 1... 2... Not quite! RR: It looked like he got him to me Crawford. I'd bet my bottom dollar on it. DC: And it took some unbelievable willpower to kick out of that. Simmons laying limp on the concrete...and Williams now getting that guitar which is broken in two...now sitting Simmons up and wrapping those guitar strings around his neck! have mercy! RR: Ha ha ha! DC: But Simmons with a thumb in the eye breaks it up...and Simmons now...up to his knees...jabbing Williams with the neck of that guitar...and RIGHT INTO THE TEETH!!!! RR: Call Polydent! Ha ha ha! DC: Simmons now is up to his feet...he and Williams exchanging lefts and rights. And...Simmons...tackling Williams...RIGHT BACK INTO THOSE STEEL STEPS!!!! RR: That hurts just looking at it Crawford. Simmons jumping in with a knee...RIGHT INTO THE RIBS OF WILLIAMS!!! DC: And Simmons now with a chair...SMACK IN THE FACE OF WILLIAMS!!!!!!! RR: Oh...holy chickencasserole!!! He's going to need plastic surgery!!! And Simmons dragging him off the steps...and going for the pin... 1... 2... 3...forget it Crawford! Ding! Ding! Ding! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner..."Crusher" Chris Simmons!!!!! (cheers) ("Harvester Of Sorrow" by Metallica plays) DC: And beautiful job by Chris Simmons...he's one up on Nick Williams. After some beautiful manuevers...Simmons walks out on top. We see now that Simmons is out of here...Williams having some trouble keeping his balance...but walking none the less...covered in blood. RR: Let's go to Michael Cook. DC: Uh..my line. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following match is a handicap match. It is scheduled for one fall...and is for a 15 minute time limit. ("The Earth Died Screaming" by Tom Waits plays) Michael Cook: From parts unknown....he is "the tallest man in the UWA"...and weighing 325lbs...GOLIATH!!!!!!! DC: Here comes the big man...wearing a Godfather t-shirt? RR: Maybe that's Godfather's Pizza. DC: I doubt that. Goliath pointing to that t-shirt and saying something...I don't know what. Now stepping into the ring...over the top...awaiting the presence of the Untouchables. Since one of the Untouchables has been thrown out, it will be interesting to see what we have here. ("The Godfather's Waltz" plays) Michael Cook: Now...representing the Syndicate...they are Willy Ciccio and the Adviser...THE UNTOUCHABLES!!!!!!!!! (cheers) DC: And here they come. It will be the Adviser filling in for Brazzi... RR: This is unfair...two on one! DC: And I see no Donna Di Notte...WAIT!!! IT'S REX OVERLORD WITH A CHAIR....OVER THE BACK OF THE ADVISER!!!! (mixed reaction) RR: What's going on here!? DC: Overlord has dropped the Adviser...and Ciccio now has just backed off. These guys are stable mates...and this is unbelievable. Overlord going to ring...and Goliath has just...HE'S RUNNING OUT THROUGH THE CROWD TERRIFIED!!!! RR: THIS IS INSANE!!! DC: And now...Overlord with the mic. Overlord: Cicci...I told you that the Syndicate was finished. I told you the history of Frank Luchese, the Adviser. Now clear out you guinea son of a [BLEEP]. DC: Woah. RR: Ciccio has taken his cigarette out of his mouth...he just dropped it on the Adviser...and he's stamping it out on the Adviser... DC: I can't figure this out. The Adviser...he's got a dirty past. But...what's going on here? Is the Adviser going bad again? RR: It's Overlord... DC: And now...walking out of here. This match is not going to happen. I can't believe what has gone down.
