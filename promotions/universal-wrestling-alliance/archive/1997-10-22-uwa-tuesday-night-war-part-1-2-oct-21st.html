UWA Tuesday Night WAR!!! part 1/2 - Oct 21st

22 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
. THE UNIVERSAL WRESTLING ALLIANCE PRESENTS... ========================================================= TUESDAY NIGHT ========================================================= ##### ##### ######## ############## ##### ##### ########## ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ### ##### ##### ##### ############### ######## ######## ################ ############## ####### ####### ##### ##### ##### ##### ###### ###### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ========================================================== DATE: October 21st, 1997 LOCATION: Miami Arena - Miami, FL ========================================================== (‘10 Seconds Down' plays as screen shows footage of UWA superstars) (The Demolishers strapping the UWA tag team belts around their waists) (D-Man dropping from the top of a steel cage to the concrete floor) (Donovan Black putting his feet on the ropes to pin Danny Daravo) (Malibu Mike clotheslining "Hollywood" Johnny Tambo) (Rex Overlord standing in the ring with Donna Di Notte & the Untouchables) (R.A.D. pointing a baseball bat at "Red" Rex Roseman) (The Adviser suplexing Suicide into a bed of tacks) (The mysterious face of Ice-Blade appearing on the UWA jumbo monitor) (Vile Vince Viper waiting in the ring for the appearance of the Godfather) (Big Butch Thornton sitting down on Mage to get the pinfall victory) (Scene opens to thousands of cheering UWA fans...exploding fireworks...signs and T-shirts of top UWA superstars) Dan Crawford: Good evening, and welcome to War! "Red" Rex Roseman: Listen to these morons! Stuff it you idiots! DC: I'm Dan Crawford along with Roseman...as always. We want to apologize to all you fans out there that were expecting a Sunday Spotlight. We were expecting one too...and had received word that the Godfather would be in attendance. But he was a no-show. The Syndicate was a no-show. Vile Vince Viper did show up...and created quite a lot of chaos. Of course we could not play that live due to content...but we will try and provide you with an edited taping of some of the antics of Vile Vince Viper. Viper got the win by disqualification...and the Godfather's contract has been tossed out the window I understand. RR: And just as it should be. Now I don't know what's going on with the Godfather and the Cosa Nostra Express...but we haven't heard from him in weeks. We've seen Overlord address the situation. We've seen Donna Di Notte address the situation. We've seen the Untouchables address the situation. But what we need are some straight answers. Where is the Godfather? What is he doing? And why? Why isn't he showing up? DC: I don't think the Syndicate knows either Rex. And we've seen Overlord's reaction. It seems that the Syndicate is not too happy about what's going on either. I draw that conclusion. RR: Add the Godfather to the list of the Missing In Action UWA superstars. The Bad Boy Alliance has not been around in weeks. And one of them is the cruiserweight champion. Crusher Stevenson is gone. Who knows where? I don't...and I don't care...as long as he stays gone. Now the Godfather. And... DC: And? RR: That reminds me...(pulls out a kleenex and whipes his eye) Today President Gordon announced that Chad Blake was no longer on the UWA payroll. (Weeps quietly) DC: Well Blake's another one that we just don't know about. But...in time perhaps we'll see some of the things that are happening here in the UWA. Strange....phenomenon...I guess you could say. But now it's time for tonight's opening match. Let's go to Michael Cook. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is scheduled for one fall... ("Come Out and Play" by the Offspring plays over the P.A.) Michael Cook: From New York...standing at 6'2"...and weighing 225lbs...here is the former UWA heavyweight champion...SUICIDE!!!!!!! (boos) DC: The fans have made up their mind I believe that it doesn't matter who comes out of the locker room next...they won't be cheering for Suicide. RR: Hey...how about that match with the Adviser? Impressive huh? DC: Impressive is the understatement of the year for that match. I hardly got any sleep that night. RR: Ha ha ha! DC: Suicide now in the ring...without manager...all by himself tonight...and awaiting the presence of the bizarre one... (‘Dance of Death' plays) Michael Cook: And now...from the Underground...standing at 6'6"...weighing 276lbs....FRANK N. STEIN!!!!!! (Boos) DC: And neither to they like this guy...hideous to say the least. I wouldn't want to turn this guy loose with a chemistry set. RR: Give it up with the witty stuff Crawford. DC: And now...Stein is to the ring....turning to look at the disgusted fans...when...flying from the ring...it's Suicide with a flying body press on Stein to the outside! Suicide right on target...and Stein hitting hard on his back. Suicide now getting back in the ring...running to the ropes...Stein is up...but moves!!! But Suicide doesn't make the jump!!! Holding onto the ropes... RR: Ha ha ha! Suicide is too smart for that one Crawford. DC: Stein now is in the ring...and Suicide hooks him in a side headlock...into a snap mare takedown...and a chinlock on Stein... RR: Suicide is fast...and Stein is hurt...so this is a good time for a chin lock. DC: Suicide up...holding onto the chin lock...dropping a knee into the back of Stein. And now...hooking his legs around the head of Frank N. Stein! RR: What do you call that? DC: I don't know. But really applying the pressure...and Stein now flipping over and out of it...to his feet...diving clothesline on Suicide!!! RR: Woah! He almost knocked his head off! DC: And Stein drops an elbow...yanking Suicide up by the hair...double underhook suplex!!! RR: Oooh! DC: Suicide hits the mat and nearly falls out! Stein is up...to the ropes...dropkick...and Suicide does fall out!!! (Some cheers) Stein going out after him...and to the outside. Now with a chair...smashing it down into the leg of Suicide...and again! Now tossing it to the side...he's got Suicide by the leg...and kicking him in the knee...dropping an elbow down on that leg! (Some cheers) RR: They're going to be counted out if they don't get back in there. DC: Stein is in the ring to break the count...now with Suicide by the hair...throwing him inside the ring...and rolling back in himself. Now Stein...dropping another elbow on the back of the head...and hooking the leg...into a...a figure four! (Some cheers) RR: Woah! Stein is technical baby!!! DC: And I don't know...I think Buzz Craven may be underestimating Frank N. Stein...as all of us have in the past. Suicide not having fun here...screaming to be released. RR: Owww...oooch. DC: And...Stein releases him. Why? RR: He's going to beat him up some more. DC: Well...Stein lifting Suicide by the hair...but Suicide rolls him up in a small package! 1.. 2... No! RR: Woah! Near pinfall! Suicide up and so is Stein! DC: Suicide with a running clothesline and Stein goes down...now bouncing from the other side...Stein is back up...INVERTED ATOMIC DROP BY STEIN!!!! RR: Come on... DC: Stein now is going to the top...and I think he's going for that ‘Acid Drop' leg drop!! (Crowd rises to their feet) But Suicide has stood up...and Frank N. Stein down with a double axe handle...sending Suicide down! He pins him! 1... 2... Kickout but barely!!! RR: Oooh! Suicide's undefeated record is hanging by a hair! DC: Stein to the top again...but Suicide has risen again...Stein dropping a double axe handle...BUT SUICIDE MEETS IT WITH A SUICIDE KICK!!!!!!!!! He pins him!!!! 1.... 2.... 3!!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! ("Come Out and Play" by Offspring plays) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...your winner...SUICIDE!!!!!!!! DC: Suicide going 4-0...and he could be a top competitor for Thornton's title by WrestleFest! RR: It could happen! That is if Johnny Tambo isn't champion by then... DC: Or R.A.D... RR: Nah...RAD's a loser. DC: Suicide now going back to the locker room...with Frank N. Stein not far behind. Excellent matchup. I was really impressed by Stein...but lady luck was not riding his shoulders tonight...as she was in the corner of Suicide. *"Horror Business" by the Misfits blares over the loudspeakers as the building darkens. A lone spotlight shines brilliantly at the entranceway. The crowd, not knowing who, or what, to expect, merely watches.* RR: What the [BEEP!]? *A young oriental beauty makes her way through the curtain, dressed in black jeans, and a black T-shirt with "EBOLA ZAIRE" written in large white letters on the front, and "The Walking Contradiction" on the back. She pauses, and looks thoughtfully at the crowd, then smiles and turns to the curtains, where a tall man in a black leather trenchcoat, mirrored John Lennon sunglasses, black jeans, and the same T walks out. His flowing raven hair is tied into a ponytail. He stops next to the lady, and raises his arms, followed by booming fireworks. The crowd, still unsure, gives a decent pop to this, but remains generally quiet.* DC: That's Sabbath! RR: Who?! DC: Sabbath...newest addition to the UWA. RR: Oh...*THAT* guy. He thinks he can come in here take over. Well, he's wrong! DC: Well, he's coming over here, for what reason I don't know. Welcome to the announce position Sabbath. MP: Thanks Dan...*nods cautiously at Rex.* RR: Yeah, whatever. DC: Well, you obviously have something on your mind, or else you wouldn't be here. MP: Exactly. I want to know why Mage still hasn't responded. Sure, he's accepted, but I've done all but insult his little goldfish that had to be flushed down the toilet when he was three, and he's been quiet. I don't like to be ignored. As you can see, when I have to, I'll come out here and stop the show. SP: Don't get us wrong, we don't want to interrupt. We just want to get recognized. That'll come sooner or later. We aren't the type to jump anyone, unless they attack us first. Micheal is a man of honor; he's not looking for a war. He's just here for the competition. Mage, when you and my husband battle, know this: we don't want to hurt you. We want a clean fight if possible, and no interefernce from outsiders. Sound fair? Good. MP: Well, we hate to just barge in here unnanounced, but we had to get that done . See you the 31st Mage! Get ready for the fight of your life!!! *"Horror Business" plays again as the two head back to the dressing rooms to a moderately better reception than before.* DC: Most strange. RR: Yeah yeah...get out of here preacher boy. DC: Why don't you say that louder Rex? RR: Are you kidding? He's standing right over there! DC: As Sabbath makes his way out of here...let's go to Michael Cook. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is scheduled for one fall... ("Elimination" by Overkill plays) Michael Cook: Approaching the ring...standing at 7'2" tall...weighing 315lbs...the Stalker!!! (boos) RR: Boo! Even these Miami idiots know that these guys are losers! DC: Here they come...the Brotherhood. The Stalker is accompanied to the ring by the Sandman, Zodiac, and Miss Kelly O'Dell. (Zodiac grabes the mic) Zodiac; I would like to take this time to bid fare well to Eliminator who has retired due to medical conditions and i would like to introduce the founder of the BrotherHood 3 time UWWF world champion Legendary Scott May. (Scott takes the mic) Scott: thanx i know my boys have been making some mistakes here and i appoligize for there actions. Now i have come out of retirement to take theBrotherHood back to the top once again thank you. (Boos) RR: Ha ha ha! It doesn't matter! Nobody likes the Brotherhood! DC: Not a friend in the world...the Brotherhood. Call them what you will...cowards...turncoats...these guys have caused real chaos in the UWA. (Michael Cook grabs the microphone back) Michael Cook: And now... ("The Mirror" by Dream Theater plays over the P.A.) Michael Cook: He is accompanied to the ring by the Adviser, Bella Donna Di Notte, Rabecca Carlotti, and the Untouchables...standing at 6'11" and weighing 326lbs...he is a former UWA heavyweight champion...REX OVERLORD!!!!!!! (crowd erupts) RR: Ha ha ha! I'll even vote Overlord over this Brotherhood moron! DC: Wait! The Brotherhood has charged the Syndicate! But the Untouchables meet them half way! (Cheers) Ciccio now slamming the Zodiac into the railing...and Brazzi slamming Sandman to the concrete! Scott May running with a briefcase...but the Adviser ducks it! Turning it around with a clothesline on May!!! (Cheers) And the Syndicate...recomposing themselves walk to the ring! RR: They've disposed of the garbage! Now it's time to get down to business! DC: Overlord in the ring now...back in trademark black singlet...facing off with the 7'2" Stalker...with the Syndicate spreading themselves at the outside of the ring. This has turned into a lumberjack match! RR: And everybody out there is an Overlord fan! Ha ha ha! DC: Stalker has got Overlord by the throat...and Overlord has Stalker by the throat! RR: Somebody's going to get chokeslammed! DC: This reminds me of the Overlord vs. T.N.T. battle for the heavyweight title that we saw almost a year ago. In that case I don't think either man got the chokeslam. But OVERLORD GETS THIS ONE!!! (cheers) The pin... 1... 2... NO!!! RR: Ooh...I don't think Stalker's used to that kind of treatment! DC: The Brotherhood is up...but the aisle is being guarded by the Untouchables! Zodiac and Sandman charge...and we've got a brawl here!!! RR: Scott May just clotheslined the Adviser! DC: He did! And he came from nowhere...dropping the Adviser to the ground...as now the Untouchables have beaten down Zodiac and Sandman again! In the ring...Overlord has got Stalker in the corner...kick to the gut...into a...POWERBOMB!!!!!!! RR: SPINESLAYER CITY!!!! DC: And he's going for the pin! Forget about it!! 1!!!! 2!!!! 3!!! It's over! Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: Stalker isn't moving after that one! DC: Overlord out of the ring...and he's got Scott May from behind. We see now...the Adviser has got that metal briefcase...right into the stomach of Scott May! (Cheers) ("The Mirror" by Dream Theater plays) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner...REX OVERLORD!!!!!!!!
