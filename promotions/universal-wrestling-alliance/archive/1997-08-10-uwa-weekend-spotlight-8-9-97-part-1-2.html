UWA *Weekend Spotlight* 8-9-97 part 1/2

10 August 1997

Weekend Spotlight 8/9/97 http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4913/main.html ======================================================= (The scene opens as the camera pans over hundreds of screaming UWA fans wearing UWA merchendise and holding signs advertiseing their favorite UWA superstar. Amonug some of the more noticable signs are, 'Suicide Rules The World' 'Rex is a wuss' and 'TNT for president'. The camera then pans toward a wrestling ring with red, white, and blue ropes surrounding only three sides of a gray canvass, leaving the front open to be viewed by the audience. Inside the ring are two, black leather chairs and a brown table with a wood-stained finish.) Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen. UWA fans of all ages. Welcome to the weekend edition of UWA Spotlight. (crpwd pop) And now, here are your two cohost, Rex Roseman and president T.N.T. (The crowd gives no reaction as Rex Roseman walks out but then begin to cheer loudly as T.N.T approaches the set. Rex is wearing jeans and a 'Rex Rules' shirt. He has spiked, red hair with a patch of gray on the side. T.N.T is wearing a navy, UWA issued suit with a olive colored shirt underneath. His long, brown hair is tied back neatly into a ponytail. Both men take their seats.) T.N.T: Hello, and as always, welcome to the weekend edition of UWA Spotlight. Our big story this week is ofcourse, the crowning of a new cruiserweight champion as Wrestlingman pulled of an incredible upset over Chad "The Specimen" Blake. It was the first title change since Superfight, almost two months ago. Rex Roseman: Thats funny, I thought the big story would have been something else... T.N.T: REX!!! Rex Roseman: Oh ya, we arn't supposed to talk about that. Anyway, Wrestlepuke doesn't deserve to be near that belt, let alone be wearing it. And if it wasn't for Big Butch Thornton's interferrence, Chad Blake would still own what is rightfully his. But I have talked to Chad and he assures me that he has bigger fish to fry in this lake called the UWA, and Wrestlingman is just a guppy. T.N.T: Say what you want about Wrestlingman, but he has come a long way since entering the UWA. He has grown up fast being involved with such veterns as Blake, Thorton, Suicide, and Lord Lero just to name a few. The fields of Nebraska have grown one hell of a cornhusker, and he will be around for quite sometime. Rex Roseman: Here we go again. T.N.T thinks that he is Bob Costas or something. Let's just quit babbleing about the past and talk about the future. T.N.T: I belive that you mean tomorrows card. And we will get to that in a moment. But first, Mile Miller is standing by with his News and Rumors segment. Rex Roseman: I heard that I will be getting a raise soon, maybe he knows something about that. ================================================================= NEWS AND RUMORS w/ Mike Miller ================================================================= (T.N.T and Rex both turn toward the huge video screen to their right as Mike Miller appears wearing black pants and a gray sports jacket with a UWA tshirt underneath. His black hair is parted and combed neatly.) Mike Miller: Well, I did hear a rumor about your pay Rex. And it's about to double........... When cows fly! But on to the important stuff. As T.N.T mentioned earlier, the big story this week is the cruiserweight title changing hands from Chad Blake over to Wrestlingman. With that all said and done, the question that remains is where each of the two are headed. Wrestlingman has gone on record with saying that Blake will not be getting a rematch and that he will be concentrating his efforts on UWA superstars who actually deserve a shot. Some names that I here popping up are the likes of Scorp and possible the new signing of a world reknown cruiserweight. From the looks of things, Wrestlingman will be having his hands full and he best stay focused if he wants to keep the title. And what about "The Specimen" Chad Blake? Where is he headed? Some say that he only cares about getting revenge on Big Butch Thortan while others are saying that he is aiming high and will be going after Suicide and the UWA heavyweight championship. We will just have to wait and see how everything plays our over the next few weeks. The top rumor this week involves a possible singing of a superstar from the fed that many are calling the best around. The scoop on this is that T.N.T has something to do with it but I am hearing the name of T.N.T an awfully lot which is causing a bit of confusion. But other then that there is nothing much going on unless you want to talk about that big argument going on. Let me tell you about this one.... T.N.T: And thank you Mike but thats all the time we have for you right now. ===================================================================== END: NEWS AND RUMORS w/ Mike Miller ===================================================================== Rex Roseman: So Mr. President, what is this rumor about you that Miller just told us about. T.N.T: I have no idea what he is talking about.... Thats why it's called a rumor you know. Rex Roseman: Hey look over there , I think that I see a cow flying!!! T.N.T: You mean to tell me that your wife is no longer afraid of airplanes... Good for her. (Crowd Laughs) Rex Roseman: I don't have to take this kind of abuse from you. Sooner or later I am going to walk out of the UWA and never come back. (Crowd Cheers) ===================================================================== PROMO: Big Butch Thornton v. Kevin Flair AND The D-Crew v. MoJ ===================================================================== T.N.T: I will let the audience do the talking for me on that one. But enough of this Rex bashing because we must talk about the kind of bashing that will be taking place tomorrow night on the Main Event. Our first match involves the UWA's number contender as he takes on the newcommer Kevin Flair. Rex Roseman: Well, I know Butch sucks, but I have never seen this Kevin Flair character, either way, he should kick Butch's a--. ============================================== Big Butch Thornton w/ The Fearsome Foursome ============================================== (The theme music from the movie Pulp Fiction starts up) (Scene opens into the apartment of Big Butch Thornton in Detroit, Michigan. Butch is sitting down on the couch wearing a grey Boston Red Sox t-shirt and black Nike workout shorts. The camera zooms into him as he begins to speak.) Big Butch Thornton: (In a soft voice) Kevin Flair. The newest kid on the block here in the UWA. He's just like a new boy in school. Wants to prove he's the man by saying things he might come to regret later on in life. Well Flair, you are gonna live to regret that little tirade you pulled a few weeks ago. You'll be regretting it Sunday night!!!!!! (Scene switches into downtown Chicago. The streets are bustling with people as clubs and bars are getting crowded. Walking in the streets are "Superstar" Scott Storm and Buzz Craven...The Devastation Crew. Scott is wearing a blue polo shirt, tan khakis, and a pair of brown loafers. Buzz is wearing a black "No Fear" t-shirt, blue jean shorts, and a pair of Nike shoes. Scott is walking around with a big smile on his face as Buzz Craven has no expression whatsoever.) "Superstar" Scott Storm: So good ole Mr. Jones and Minos are looking for the respect they so richly deserve. (Starts to laugh) Well Mr. Jones, start looking elsewhere big guy. We are much more than guys just playing on the beach and getting our "butter tans". Do we even do that Buzz?? (Buzz gives him a pissed off look back) This Sunday Night Ministery of Justice, you'll have to get that respect you are so longing for later. Me and Buzz are starting a path only mere mortals tread upon....Working our way up to the UWA World Tag Team Titles!!! Buzz Craven: Ministery of Justice, who the hell are you fooling? We aren't no pushovers. You better swig some more of that whisky down because it's gonna be one rough night for you 2!!! (Scene cuts to the Fearsome Foursome Training Facility in Chicago, Illinois. Weights can be heard crashing together as Crusher Stevenson is working out on the Universal system, sweating bullets. He is wearing a soaked white UWA t-shirt, forrest green Champion shorts, and a pair of Nike shoes. He stops working out and begins to speak) Crusher Stevenson: Lord Zero, you better be glad that you have a suspension hanging over your head. Sooner or later you will have to deal with me. Your gonna have to face a man that has been through hell and back in his wrestling career. I'm at the highest point of my career so far and I'm not gonna stop climbing. (Scene cuts back to Big Butch Thornton at his appartment.) Kevin Flair, you can Whoo and Strut all you F'N want!! But once you step into the ring with the One Man Wrecking Crew, your fate lies in my hands. Don't expect an easy match. Your nothing more then a stepping stone for me to the UWA Title. Next it will be Blake. And then you Suicide. (Camera cuts back to Crusher Stevenson) Lord Zero, it's only a matter of time before we face each other in the squared circle. It's gonna be my time to shine. If it takes fighting your hold group of thugs, I will. Whatever it takes in wearing that IC Title around my waist. Lord Zero....PREPARE TO BE CRUSHED!!!! (Camera cuts back to Scott and Buzz) Scott Storm: Ministery of Justice, at SNME, your chance to shine will be available. But you are going up against the hottest commodity in town. Me and Buzz are the best tag team in the UWA and we'll prove it by beating you 2 and then the Hell's Angels. You can bank on it!! Buzz Craven: Scott about summed it up. Get ready for TOTAL DEVASTATION!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Camera cuts back to Butch) The UWA Title is my #1 priority here in the UWA. But if cracking some skulls like Kevin Flair and Chad Blake happen to be in the way, then that's fine with me. I'll just do what I do best........KICK ASS AND TAKE NAMES!!!!!!!!!! UWA, Judgement Day is Upon Us.....I F'N GUARNATEE IT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The music ends as the camera fades to black) =================================== END: Big Butch Thornton Flash =================================== continued on part 2
