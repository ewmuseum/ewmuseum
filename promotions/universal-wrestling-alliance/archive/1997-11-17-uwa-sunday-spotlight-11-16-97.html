UWASunday Spotlight 11/16/97

17 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
("Eye Of the Tiger" by Survivor plays as screen shows clips of UWA superstars) (Silver & Gold performing the "Pick axe" on Crusher Stevenson) (Flaming Bob burning the face of Suicide with a blow torch) (Frank N. Stein sitting emotionless on top of a 25ft scaffold) ("Suicide" Johnny Pain clotheslining "Extreme Misfit" Gar Kryger) (Rex Overlord powerbombing Malibu Mike to the canvass) (Big Butch Thornton running angrily to ringside) (Sabbath standing over the fallen Thugs For Life) (Wrestlingman hitting the Asswhupper with a cross body block) (Vile Vince Viper battering Junior with the UWA Heavyweight belt) =================================== | UWA Sunday Spotlight | | November 16, 1997 | | The Mecca - Milwaukee, WI | | Live!!!!!!!!! | =================================== (scene opens to a jam packed Mecca arena...zooming in on Dan Crawford and "Red" Rex Rosemen in the commentary booth) DC: Welcome one and all... ("Who Shot Ya" by Notorious BIG starts to play as Eliminator comes down to the ring wearing a Horsemen shirt and shorts and sunglasses. He enters the Ring and grabes the mic.) Eliminator, Yea you all know ya love me. You all saw the Horsemen take out D man thats only the begining of my chaos here in the UWA. But right now if there is any one there in the back who wants to wrestle the greatest wrestler in the world come on out. (boos) RR: Oh brother! DC: Eliminator making his presence known already in this event. And...I don't know if the president is going to let this happen. But the Eliminator wants a challenger! (Crowd chants "D-MAN! D-MAN! D-MAN! D-MAN!") DC: Woah! The crowd wants the D-Man! RR: D-Man isn't even in the building! Eliminator: I knew no one could handle me. (Eliminator starts to leave when "On top of the world" starts to play....at the entrance way is a tall figure...he steps out of the shadows revealing that he is the Stalker...a suprised and nervous look comes over Eliminators face...crowd cheers...Stalker pulls out a mic) "Stalker" Toni Gills: Eliminator you want a challenge? Well you got one little man! (cheers) DC: We're going to have a fight here! RR: Come on Stalker! Kick his little hiny! (Stalker drops the mic...enters the ring and goes afetr Eliminator...but Eliminator jumps out of the ring and begins to climb over the railing into the crowd...) DC: Eliminator is heading out! RR: Coward! DC: WAIT! In the front row! Complete in wrestling gear! It's Rex Overlord! (crowd erupts) He's got the Eliminator by the throat! Now climing over the railing...and throwing the Eliminator back into the ring!!!! (cheers) RR: Yes! Beat him Stalker! DC: Stalker has got him by the hair! Headbutt! And...big bodyslam! (cheers) Now a legdrop! RR: Overlord getting in the ring! DC: Stalker now hooking his legs...turning him over...STALKER DEATH LOCK!!!!!!! (cheers) He's got him!!!! 7'2" of the Stalker...sitting right down on the back of the Eliminator! RR: Woah! DC: And now...the Stalker releasing Eliminator...as the Eliminator tries to roll out...but Stalker holding on to his legs! RR: Look at this! Overlord is going to the outside...and he's got a chair! (cheers) DC: Now getting back in with that chair...the Stalker holding Eliminator up...and Overlord aiming that chair... RR: SMACK!!!!!! Holy LellaniKai!!!!!! He just knocked is lights out! DC: Eliminator down...and now moving...Overlord now looking at Stalker...both men going face to face. RR: No way... DC: I know that the Stalker has expressed the fact that he respects Rex Overlord greatly...even requesting to be his partner... RR: No way... DC: Overlord grabbing Stalker's hand...and lifting it high into the air!!!! (cheers) Overlord pointing to the Stalker as the crowd cheers him on!! We've got a team!! RR: I can't believe this!!! ("On Top of the World" plays) DC: This is hard to believe...the Stalker now...really enjoying getting the fans cheers again...Overlord just looking on... RR: I would have never thought this possible... DC: WITH THAT CHAIR......SMACK!!!!!!!!!! RR: HA HA HA HA!! DC: HE JUST DROPPED THE STALKER STONE COLD INTO THE MIDDLE OF THE RING!!!!!!! (boos) RR: YES!!!!!! DC: And the crowd...expressing their hate...this is disgusting. RR: What? I thought you liked Overlord? DC: BBT look out...the man that wants you're partnership cannot be trusted. RR: Oh...you're overreacting Crawford. What Overlord did was completely necessary. DC: This is sick...sick...sick. Overlord dropping the chair and walking out of here...and let's get the camera off him. I don't know what happened...but he's snapped. RR: Don't you love it? DC: What!? Are you kidding me? RR: No! I love it Crawfish! Ha ha ha! DC: The ring has been cleared...so lets go back to Michael Cook... Michael Cook:The challengers for the UWA World Tag Team Title Matchup,at a combined weight of 680 pounds,representing the Bad Boy Alliance accompanied by Johnny"Bad Boy"Coals,Chauncey Faulks and Moneyz....HERE ARE SEAN GOLDZ AND G-REAL...THE BIG BOYZ!!! (Lights dim as spotlights flash through the crowd."Awww Sh!t" by the Alkaholiks begins to blast out of the speakers as the entire Bad Boyz contingent makes their way to the ring.Johnny Coals is in white khaki's,a blue North Carolina football jersey and a baby blue New York Yankees hat,Chauncey Faulks is in baggy blue jeans,a Mecca shirt and a green New York Yankees hat.Moneyz is decked out in another one of his fine BOSS suits,and Oakley shades.G-Real is in black trunks and a white sleeve shirt with "BBA" written on the back.His partner Sean Goldz is in black tights with "Pure Gold" written down the sides. They enter the ring and fireworks explode out of all four corners of the ring as a Bad Boy Alliance logo is etched out in sparkling fireworks behind them[ala Sid]) RR: Here come da boyz! Ha ha ha! I smell gold on those boys! DC: This afternoon...a wonderful opportunity for them to pick up some more gold. But the Demolishers of course might object...long time UWA tag team champions...in the midst of their second reign. RR: Well...they're good too. Michael Cook:"And their opponents,6"11,7"0,at a combined weight of 550lbs,from Parts Unknown,being accompanied to the ring by "Dazzling"Danielle,here is The UWA World Tag Team Champions,Crash,and Bash,The Demolishers!"(The fans boo loudly) (Demolition's Music,plays over the PA,and the fans boo loudly.The Demolishers come out,accompanied by "Dazzling"Danielle.Crash is wearing a black leather vest,which has DD on the back,black trunks which have DD on them,black boots,his face is painted orange,and black,and he has his UWA World Tag Team Belt strapped around his waist.Bash is wearing a black leather vest,which has DD on the back,black trunks which have DD on them,black boots,his face is painted blue,and black,and he has his UWA World Tag Team Belt strapped around his waist."Dazzling"Danielle is wearing a gold robe,gold high heels,and her hair is down her back.The Demolishers stick their tongue out at the crowd,as "Dazzling"Danielle struts down to the ring."Dazzling"Danielle goes over to The Demolishers corner,as they climb up into the ring.Crash,and Bash remove their black leather vests,and hand them out to the ring attendant,then they hand their belts to the ref) DC: And the ring is surrounded. We've got Danielle and Daravo on one end. Moneyz, Coals, and Faulks on the other. This is going to be a great matchup. RR: It is Crawford...and for the UWA tag team titles. Big match. Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: We're underway...big G-Real starting it off with big Crash. And these two tag teams...huge. RR: In most cases we see the real power and size going to the Demolishers. But now I'm not so sure. DC: G-Real locking up with Crash...in a power struggle. Who'll come out on top? Crash with a knee to the gut...and twisting G-Real's arm into a hammer lock...now backing into his own corner...tag to Bash. Bash in...big right hand to G-Real who was wide open...and an elbow smash right to the forehead. RR: Woah! And those can stun. I think G-Real might have forgotten where he was for a minute! DC: Bash...the bigger of the two Demolishers...holding G-Real in that hammer lock...big knee to the gut...now twisting him out...sending him to the ropes...both men collide!!!! (cheers) Nobody moves! Bash with a big right swing...ducked by G-Real...and countered with a huge clothesline by G-Real!!! And Bash goes to the mat! RR: He dropped him! And they're going power for power here. DC: Elbow drop by G-Real...into a cover... 1... big kickout! RR: Woah...just a little soon. DC: G-Real now pulling Bash to his feet by the hair...big right hand...now Irish whip into his own corner...and Sean Goldz has pinned him back in the corner...and...big knee lift by G-Real into the corner!!! Bash almost flew out of the ring! RR: They're taking him apart piece by piece...great strategy. But Crash and Bash are tough...maybe too tough for this. DC: Bash in the armringer...G-Real tags in to Goldz...who flies off the top with a double axe handle! And Bash staggers and collapses to the mat! Goldz with a boot to the face...smearing that paint all over...and now lifting by the hair...hooking the head...DDT!!! (mixed reaction) And for the pin... 1... 2... kickout...and a tired one. RR: Bash is taking a lot of punishment in there...he needs to make the tag Crawford. DC: Without a doubt...but this capacity crowd remains unbias...just enjoying the match and waiting to see who comes out on top...so neither team receives much backing. Bash crawling to his corner...but Goldz now grabbing his leg and pulling him back into the Big Boyz corner. RR: Big boot from Bash! DC: Woah! Bash just raised that boot into the face of Goldz...sending him tumbling back...but Goldz up in a hurry...dropping an elbow into the lower back of Bash...and now hooking him in a camel clutch! RR: It's been a long time since I've seen that one! DC: Age doesn't mean that it hurts any less. Bash trying his hardest to get back to his corner...and Danny Daravo cheering him on with Danielle! In the ring is Crash now...BIG CLOTHESLINE BY CRASH SENDS GOLDZ SPRAWLING!!!!!!! RR: Ha ha ha! Blatant cheating! I love it! DC: And now Crash is out of the ring...reaching for the tag...as Goldz reaches for G-Real...BOTH TAGS ARE MADE!!! (cheers) RR: Get ready for all out war! DC: Goldz falling back into the ropes...G-Real charging Crash...but Crash going to work with hard rights and lefts...but an eye gouge by G-Real slows him down! (boos) And G-Real throws Crash to the ropes...right towards Sean Goldz...AND GOLDZ BACKDROPS CRASH OVER THE TOP ROPE!!! (mixed reaction) RR: Oooh...not a good place to land! DC: Coals, Faulks, and Moneyz going crazy over there on Crash with kicks...but Danny Daravo from behind...SMACK!!!!!! Right over the head of Chauncey Faulks...and he backs the Bad Boyz away!!! RR: Woah! Faulks had better be careful! He got the same on FNF from Frank N. Stein! DC: He's had a rough weekend and that's for sure. But now Sean Goldz is on the outside...rolling Crash in...and G-Real pulling him to his feet...suplex...INTO A BRAINBUSTER!!! OH MY!!! FORGET IT!!! 1... 2... WAIT A MINUTE!!!! (deafening cheers) RR: What the... DC: Danielle on the apron has shed that gold robe...and she's parading back and forth on the ring apron...wearing nothing but a tiny bikini top...and the smallest thong I've ever seen in my life...oh have mercy!!!!! (crowd cheers loudly) RR: And she's smiling from ear to ear! Look at those cute rosy cheeks! DC: Knock it off Rex! The ref has ordered her to get off the apron...but he's bug eyed...just as Rex is...and myself. And...over the guardrail!!!!!! A MONSTER!!!!!! HE'S IN THE RING AND HE'S GOT GOLDZ AND G-REAL BY THE THROAT...DOUBLE CHOKESLAM!!!!!! RR: THAT GUY IS ALMOST 20 FEET TALL!!!! DC: Over 7 feet is more realistic Rex...but he is a monster...and there's no way that the ref could have missed that... Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: Ha ha ha! These teams are great! They're masters of their art! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winners of this contest as a result of a disqualification...the BIG BOYZ!!! (mixed reaction) But...since the titles can only change hands by pinfall or submission...still UWA Tag Team Champions...THE DEMOLISHERS!!! (more cheers) DC: And the Demolishers are out of there...walking back to the locker room with that monster of a man...Danny Daravo...and Danielle flipping that robe around...flashing her assets to this capacity crowd. RR: Hehehe...this has been a great match Crawford. Look...Goldz and G-Real are up...nobody seriously hurt physically or in the ratings...two of my favorite teams here. I'm really happy with how this turned out. DC: Yeah...and especially seeing another side of Danielle. RR: Oh...get off it Crawford! You're the one that keeps talking about it. DC: You were the one that got excited Rex. RR: I don't get excited over thongs. I've seen more of her than you'll ever know. DC: Give me a break. Danny Daravo...I hope you're listening. Why don't you do me a favor and come break this guys teeth? RR: You don't want that Crawford. DC: Well...no Spotlight next Sunday. Because the big one will be here. RR: WrestleFest II. DC: Indeed. RR: I can hardly wait. I remember WrestleFest from last year. Dungeon of Destruction vs. Hollywood Thunder and team...Venom Corp vs. Death's Associates...UNDF vs. Scorched Earth...Syndicate vs. Apocolypse Alliance. And of course a great main event...TNT vs. Rex Overlord. And you know what we get to see this year? Wrestlingman in a heavyweight title match. Is that the biggest blah you've ever heard in your life? DC: You're not giving Wrestlingman the credit he deserves. I'm expecting some big surprises out of that man in the future. But for now we're out of time. Folks...thanks for tuning in. We'll see you on Tuesday Night War! Happy hunting! (scene fades to black) 1997 UWA Sports™ All Rights Reserved
