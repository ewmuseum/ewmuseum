UWA Saturday Night's Main Event part 3/4

3 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The break ends as the scene shows the screaming fans in the arena. Fireworks begin to go off above the ring as flames shoot out from the turnbuckels.) Dan Crawford: Welcome back to a completly sold out Igloo here in Pittsburgh where the action is really starting to heat up and this place is on the verge of melting. Rex Roseman: And I didn't even bring my swimtrunks. Dan Crawford: And now it's time for the first of out title matches as Chad "The Specimen" Blake defends the cruiserweight title against Wrestlingman, who was deemed number one contender after scoring an upset win over heavyweight Suicide last week in a non-title matchup. Rex Roseman: As we all know, "The Specimen" is one of my favorite wrestlers here in the UWA. He is just WAAAAAYYYY TOOOOO MUCH. HA HA! ========================================== Cruiserweight Title Match ========================================== Chad "The Specimen" Blake(c) v. Wrestlingman ========================================== (Real Amercian Hero Starts to play over the PA) Micheal Cook: This next matchup is for the UWA Cruiserweight championship. Introducing first, the challenger. From the fields of Nebraska. Led to the ring by his manager Ashley. Here is the number one contender for the cruiserweight title. WRESTLINGMAN!!! (Huge pop from the crowd as Wrestlingman walks down the isle with Ashley. He high fives some of his fans on the way down.) Dan Crawford: Wrestlingman has reached a huge state of popularity over the last few weeks here in the UWA. The fans just can't seem to get enought of him.) Rex Roseman: Every second of this fool is Way To Much. HA HA HA!!! (Suddenly, Chad Blake appears on the 4-sided jumbotron above the ring as the crowd boos heavily) Blake: About time you put me on! I was starting to break a sweat in this hot as hell arena. Anyway, I've been rather silent on the Wrestlingman front, only because I am SICK TO DEATH about talking about him! So he got a so-called lucky break against Suicide. SO WHAT??? It also goes to show what I've been saying all along; Suicide is a paper champion who has to slap on Depends when he thinks about me, cuz that's how scared he is. Instead of battling worthy opponents such as myself, Suislob instead gets beat by the worst wrestler in the league! All of a sudden, this merits another shot with ME for MY belt? Wrestlingman, I beat you once for the belt, once in a regular match in about six seconds, and then did it again in a cage and damn near BUSTED YOUR HEAD OPEN!!!! Now this is your fourth damn shot at the UWA CRUISERWEIGHT BELT!!! It's appearing obvious that UWA prez TNT wants to feed me a steady diet of you and Bad Habit until one day one of you get lucky on your 10,000th try! If you wnat the belt SOOOOOOOOO friggin much, I should just let you beat me so I can stop having to stare at your ugly mug every other damn week! But I'm sure the instant I lose the match they'd find a reason to deny me a rematch and lock me out of the title picture, because they'd much rather have a guy in a STABLE with a belt than an INDIVIDUALIST who THINKS FOR HIMSELF!!!!! So Wrestlingman, instead I'm gonna do it this way: tonight, you better bring your whole family; your mom, your dad, your cousins, your sister, your wife -- oh yeah, the last two are probably one and the same, I heard all you Nebraskan losers are into that stuff. Get em all in the front row, give em all video cameras and Polaroids and what have you. To celebrate your Cruiserweight title victory? No siree Bob. TO WATCH YOUR LAST MATCH!!! I'm going to the ring with two objectives: KEEP THE BELT and PUT YOU OUT OF WRESTLING!!!!!!!! [Chad Blake turns to leave, and then turns back to the camera] Blake: And if Wrestlingman's lil Gang wanna help their man win the belt, bring it on. In fact, why don't all you little pussy posses come to ringside and watch the man who is WAAAAYYYY TOOOOOOOOOO MUCCCCCHHHHHHHH!!!!! take it to the next level! In fact, if you show up early, I'll give away Wrestleslug's limbs to the FIRST FOUR PEOPLE TO RINGSIDE!!!! [Blake makes a cut signal and the camera abruptedly turns off] Rex Roseman: You tell these morons Chad. But I doubt that the Legion of Darkness will be anywhere near ringside. T.N.T wants a new car and might fine them even more. The reason why the first fine was so steep is because the only thing that he fits in is a tractor, and he wants one custom made with leather seats to plop down his fat ass on. ("This is How We Do It" by Montell Jordan starts over the PA as the crowd begins to boo extremly loud. Rex Roseman stands up and starts to dance to the music.) Dan Crawford: Will you just listen to this crowd boo Blake. Rex Roseman: This is how I do it..... This is how I do it..... Micheal Cook: And now for the champion... being led to the ring by his valet Erin. From Phoenix, Arizona. Here is CHAD "THE SPECIMEN" BLAKE. (Chad Blake enters, wearing a glittery silk robe that has "WAAY TOOO MUUCCCCHH" embroidered on the back. Erin follows, in a dark purple strapless gown. The crowd jeers Blake and Erin as they head to the ring.) Dan Crawford: The specimen drops his belt and charges the ring.... Only to be met by the foot of Wrestlingman.... It doesn't even phase him.... He stands straight up and begins to whale away at the face of Wrestlingman. Kicks em in the midsection... And big DDT...... And now a knee to the head.... and another.... and now a stomp to the gut. Blake seems to be really upset with Wrestlingman. Rex Roseman: He is sick and tired of defending that belt against crap like Wrestlingman. C'mon, give him the speciplex so we can get this over with. Dan Crawford: Blake pulls the sluggish Wrestlingman up..... holds him up in suplex position... and spins around..... finally droping him. Rex Roseman: What strength the specimen is showing. Dan Crawford: Blake off the ropes... lefdrop... did you see the height on that one. Rex Roseman: No biggie, it's the specimen. Chad Blake: Suicide lost to this pile of dog shi#. When is MY nontitle match. Dan Crawford: Blake now climbing up top..... Flying elbow. NO! Wrestlingman rools out of the way... And now both men are hurting as the ref begins the count. Ref: 1...2...3...4 Dan Crawford: No sign of movement from either the specimen nor Wrestlingman. Ref: 5...6...7... Dan Crawford: And finally Wrestlingman slowly gets up the one knww as the ref stops the count. Rex Roseman: C'mon Blake, get up and beat his brains in. Dan Crawford: Wrestlingman finally up on two feet as the specimen is on one knee.... Wrestlingman pulls Blake up the rest of the way... swinging neckbreaker and the tides have turned oh so quickly. (Crowd begins to cheer loudly) Rex Roseman: What's going on... Chad hasn't won yet. Dan Crawford: LOOK!!! It's Big Butch Thornton comming down the isle... amd he looks steamed.... I guess he's still upset about Blake getting him suspended. Rex Roseman: Get security down here before the specimen knocks him unsenceless. Dan Crawford: And the specimen rolls outside the ring unaware of Thornton.....The ref is trying to get Erin off of the apron as she is yelling about Thornton. He walks around to the side and OH! Thornton grabs him by the throat... THORNTONSLAM!!! (CROWD POP!!!) Rex Roseman: Ahhhhh Dan Crawford: Thornton tosses the specimen back into the ring... Wrestlingman with the cover One Twooo THREEEE!!!!! It's over. We have a new cruiserweight champion and his name is Wrestlingman. Big Butch has left ringside and Wrestlingman has been givin the belt. (Real Amercian Hero Starts to play over the PA as the crowd go's nuts) Rex Roseman: NOOOOOOOOOO!!! Dan Crawford: And we have out first title change since Superfight. The specimen has finally lost. We have to take a break so stay tuned for yet ANOTHER title matchup. ***break*** commercials ***break***
