UWA Saturday Night's Main Event part 1/4

3 August 1997

The Universal Wrestling Alliance proudly presents... ========================================= S A T U R D A Y N I G H T' S ========================================= ### ### #### ###### ### ## #### #### ## ## ## #### ## ## ## ## ## ######## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## #### ## ## ## ## ## ###### ## ### ###### ## ## ###### ### ## ###### ## ## ## ## #### ## ## ###### ## ## ###### ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## #### ## ###### ### ###### ## ### ## ================ Pittsburgh, PA 8/2/97 ================ (The UWA theme music http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4913/ACDCSHK.MID begins to play while the scene opens to a wide shot of an old, rusty looking steel mill located directly next to a very wide river. As the camera begins to zoom in, long streaks of fire can be seen shooting up from the cylinder-like smoke stacks extending from the top of the mill. We are taken all the way into the flame as the scene quickly switches to the inside. Very muscular men are working hard molding hot, yellow and red liquid steel into formations before it is cooled. It is evident that the setting is extremly hot and muggy as the camera shows the sweat rolling off of each man's face. The scene erupts into a ball of fire as we are shown the UWA logo. . ###### ###### ###### ####### ###### ###### ###### # ######### ###### ###### ###### ### ##### ##### ###### ###### ###### ##### ##### ##### ###### ###### ###### ####### ##### ##### ###### ###### ###### ######### ################# ####### ####### ###################################### ####### ####### ########## ########## ###### ################# ######## ######## ###### ############### ###### ###### ###### ############# #### #### ###### http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4913/main.html Voice: Welcome to the steel city of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Where the old steel mills are producing night in and night out. But tonight, there is another source of heat. TONIGHT, the Universal Wrestling Alliance invades the Pittsburgh Civic Arena; Officially known as the Igloo. Where the action will get so hot, that this place just might begin to melt. (The scene then swithces over to thousands of screaming UWA fans waving their signs and displaying their favorite wrestler on their shirts. Then over to Dan Crawford who is wearing a UWA issued suit with his hair comed very neatly and sitting next to Rex Roseman who is wearing bluejeans and an "I love Rex Roseman" t-shirt; The gray streak in his hair being clearly visible across his spiked, red top.) Dan Crawford: OVER 18,000 screaming fans have jam-packed the igloo here in the STEEL CILY of PITTSBURGH. Each and every fan hoping to get a glimpse of their favorite UWA superstar. Rex Roseman: ME! And isn't it nice that we are right next to the ring where everyone in this hell hole can get more then a glimpse. After all, I am the UWA's best known personality. Dan Crawford: I'd have to agree with you there Rex. Everyone throughout the wrestling world knows you as being "the huge jack@$$ that complains anytime that he gets a chance to." And I am sure that you will get many of chances to do that tonight as we have an action packes card scheduled. We will be seeing the cruiserweight champ Chad "The Specimen" Blake defend his belt against the number one contender Wrestlingman AND we will also be seeing The Fatal Clan challenge Hell's Angels for their strap as well. In addition, Crusher Stevenson will be taking on Malibu Mike as the winner will be deemed a shot against Lord Zero and his International title. But next up is the long awaited debut of Jean Guy Lavalle. We got to see him last Saturday, as he sat at ringside watching the action. He will be facing The Canadian Kid, who is also making his debut tonight. Roseman: Oh great, 2 Canadians in one ring. I wonder if they'll join forces and talk bad about the USA, like some stable from another fed... Crawford: Let's go to the ring... =================================== Jean Guy Lavalle v. The Canadian Kid =================================== (Camera cuts to the ring, where the announcer is standing.) Announcer Michael Cook: This contest is scheduled for one fall... ("Big Me" by Foo Fighters plays) Michael Cook: Introducing first...from Woodstock, New Brunswick, Canada...making his debut in the UWA...6'2" and 249 pounds...THE CANADIAN KID!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The fans cheer as The Canadian Kid makes his way to the ring. He is wearing a Montreal Canadians t-shirt, and blue jean shorts. His long brown hair is tied back in a ponytail. He slaps a few hands as he makes his way to the ring.) Michael Cook: And his opponent... (The lights darken, and a spotlight is pointed at the entrance. No music plays) Michael Cook: From the logging camps of Quebec...also making his debut in the UWA...6'8" and 321 pounds...JEAN GUY LAVALLE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Jean Guy Lavalle is carrying a hockey stick as he moves quickly to the ring. He asks the announcer for his microphone.) Lavalle: Well hello to all da fans of UWA. Like I tell ya, I have a special surprise. I tell it to you that I have someone special I gonna introduce to you. Please turn your attention to the dressing room entrance. (A spotlight moves to the entrance, as the fans begin to talk about who it might be) Lavalle: Here is someone that put Pittsburgh on the map. He is good friend of mine. Everyone please stand and cheer for numero soixante six...number 66...MARIO LEMIEUX!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The fans are going crazy as Lemieux makes his way to the ring. Lemieux slaps hands, and even gives out a few hockey pucks to some of the younger fans. The camera closes up on one of the lucky kids, and he has a big smile on his face. Lemieux gets into the ring, and shakes Lavalle's hand. Lavalle lifts Lemieux up, and turns him around for all the fans to see, as the fans cheer even louder. He puts Lemieux down, and takes a seat at ringside.) Crawford: Wow! What a treat for these fans as their hometown hero makes an appearance on Saturday Night's Main Event! Roseman: Ah, what have the Penguins done? Everyone knows that the Red Wings are the REAL team in the NHL! Crawford: You never cease to amaze me, Rex... (bell rings) Crawford: And we're underway...Canadian Kid with a rake to the eyes right away as he tries to capatalize...Canadian Kid is much smaller than Lavalle...Kid moves Lavalle to the turnbuckles...and gives him a chop to the chest...that didn't phase Lavalle as he pushes Kid off of him...Kid hits the mat hard...Lavalle picks him up and a big power slam...now Lavalle going to the top rope...and hits a REVERSE MOONSAULT...that's dangerous for a man of his size...now Lavalle going for the cover... 1... And a kickout by the Kid...Lavalle picks the Kid up again...and whips him to the ropes...BIG clothesline...and the Kid to the canvas again...Lavalle is quite the brawler... Roseman: Yeah, just another Suicide wanna-be. There's too many of those in the UWA. Suicide should take them all on at once, now there would be a challenge! Crawford: Only in your dreams...now Lavalle on the offensive once again...sets the Kid up on the turnbuckles...runs to the opposite side...and runs back crashing into the Kid...the Kid is totally dazed... (the fans begin to cheer and point as LordZero comes out with a Pittsburgh Steelers jersey on. He is carrying a hockey stick.) Roseman: YEAH! The Legion's leader here to take care of business! Crawford: LordZero is making his way to ringside...he is just standing there...he now begins to walk over to Lemieux...and pulls a marker out of his pocket Roseman: He wants an AUTOGRAPH?!? I thought he was here to kick some ass... Crawford: AHEM! He's still here, isn't he? (LordZero shakes Lemieux's hand. He stands and watches the match a little longer, and points to Lavalle with the hockey stick. He then turns around and walks back to the locker room.) Roseman: That was stupid...he didn't even hit anyone with that hockey stick! Crawford: Well back in the RING...Lavalle is totally dominating...he picks up the Kid...and hits him with a DDT...he goes for another cover... 1... 2... And another kickout by the Kid...he's sure taking a lot of punishment...Lavalle picks the Kid up again...and sets him up on the ropes...he climbs up to the ropes...SUPERPLEX...this one's all over!!!!!!!!!! 1... 2... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Announcer: The winner of this match...JEAN GUY LAVALLE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Lavalle raises his arm in victory as The Canadian Kid comes up and shakes his hand. The Canadian Kid leaves the ring, and the lights go dark.) Roseman: What the hell? What's going on? (The lights come back on to reveal Lavalle in the ring with 2 nerdy looking guys.) Roseman: That's...that's... Crawford: "Zinger" ZACK FOOLEY AND DYKEN!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Zack Fooley and Dyken start to attack Lavalle, with little success. Although Lavalle is worn from his match, he is fighting back.) Crawford: HERE COMES LEMIEUX!!!!!!!!! (Lemieux comes into the ring weilding a hockey stick. He breaks it on Dyken's back. Zack Fooley sees this and tries to revive Dyken. Lemieux takes the broken stick, and hits Fooley with it. The two men, though dazed, run away and exit through the crowd, as the fans laugh at them.) Roseman: Maybe Lemieux will sign with the UWA. He's got talent... Crawford: Zack Fooley and Dyken with yet another "foolish" appearance here on the Main Event. Lavalle and Lemieux leaving, and Lavalle has Mario on his shoulder. The fans here are going absolutely bezerk! ***break*** commercials ***break***
