The city of Pittsburgh, Pennslyvania is still after the aftermath of UWA Saturday Night's Main Event hours ago. Big Butch Thornton and Crusher Stevenson are standing in front of the Roberto Clemente statue, inside the gates of Three Rivers Stadium. Big Butch Thornton is wearing a black Fearsome Foursome t-shirt, blue jeans, and black boots. Crusher Stevenson is wearing a white Gold's Gym Chicago t-shirt, blue Nike shorts, and a pair of Nike shoes. Crusher looks pissed off as Butch begins to speak) Big Butch Thornton: So Blake, how did you like the first Big Butch Thornton show?? It was pretty innovative, happened right during your match with the Wrestlingman. (Butch starts to get a look of anger in his eyes) I didn't cost you your title to get back at you for the little stunt you pulled on your "talk show", I just wanted to give you a wake up call. The last couple of months by the way you talk, you have had it easy in the UWA. Well NOT ANYMORE!!! I came back to the UWA after being suspended for one simple reason...To win the UWA World Heavyweight Title. And if kicking your cocky ass is one of the steps in accomplishing that goal, then SO BE IT!!! (Points his finger to the camera) And one more thing. Kevin Flare, if you want to put your money where your mouth is, step up to the plate Saturday Night against the "Original Asskicker" of the UWA. Mr. Flare, you'll never say the word "Whoo" again after I am finished with your sorry ass. I GUARANTEE IT!!!!!!! Crusher Stevenson: (Shaking his head) Well I beat Malibu Mike tonight. Now I get to face the Internation Champion Lord Zero. But for some reason I'm not happy. Not happy because a bunch of bat wielding thugs changed the look of the whole match. (Rubs his chin) Lord Zero, Legion of Darkness, LISTEN UP!!! You might have tried to take me out tonight, but it didn't work. I still made Malibu Mike submit. Now Lord Zero, your next. Bring all your baseball equipment to the match because your gonna need it. It's gonna take a army to stop me from taking that IC Title from you. And when I do win that IC Title, the first person that will get a shot is you Malibu Mike. Lord Zero.....Prepare to be CRUSHED!!! Big Butch Thornton: Lord Zero, I would suggest you tell all your thugs to keep away from Crusher's match against you. Just a fair warning for in advance. Judgement Day is Upon Us...... (Butch points to the statue of Roberto Clemente, looks up to the sky, and smiles. Him and Crusher walk away as the the camera fades to black) ===================================================================== Wrestlingman about: ===================================================================== (Wrestlingman with the Cruserweight belt around his waist with Ashley) Wrestlingman: HAHAHA, Blake.
