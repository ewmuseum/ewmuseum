UWAFNF 11/8/97 pt.1

9 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ UWA Presents.... FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Date: November 7, 1997 - taped Location: Joe Louis Arena - Detroit, MI ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ( www.geocities.com/colosseum/4913/main.html ) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ("10 Seconds Down" plays as scene opens...revealing thousands of fans filling the seats of the Joe Louis Arena) DC: Ladies and gentlemen...welcome to FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS!!!!!! I'm Dan Crawford alongside my colleague..."Red" Rex Rose- RR: That's "Red" Rex Roseman...Dannyboy...the reason that this show still a mile ahead of the WOWA's Friday Night Jerkoff!!! DC: Will you stop? I'm not going to tell you again! RR: Relax Crawford. DC: Well...the WOWA for the past little bit has been considered one of the basketcase feds of the wrestling world...as least by those of the UWA. But Rex...UWA officials took a little time and they took a long look at the WOWA. And you wouldn't believe the roster that they have over there... RR: Is Mikey paying you to slobber over his fed? DC: No, of course not Rex. But all the same...they've got the UWA of 1996 over there. Meaning that they're just a little bit more in this mainstream than we thought. RR: Oh please...just a bunch of saps. DC: Well...none the less I want to apologize to WOWA fans everywhere for Rex's comments. Rex...you need to take a look. RR: Let's get on with the card! I can't believe you're saying this! ("Phenomenon" by LL Cool J plays over the P.A.) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...approaching the ring area...from...(stops a moment and strains his eyes to read the card) "where ever he damn well feels like livin"...he stands at 6'3"...weighs 250lbs...(straining his eyes again)..."the man that whipped Scott Daniels ass on Sunday Spotlight"...he is...the ASSWHUPPER!!!!!!!!!!!! RR: Ha ha ha! How do you like that announcement? DC: Interesting I must say. Asswhupper coming out...followed by Mama. RR: Isn't she gorgeous! DC: Go get your eyes checked Rex. Mama is a D-O-G. RR: You don't have to be cruel about it. DC: Asswhupper in the ring...shouting obscenities to the crowd. This guy is just too much. ("War Machine" by KISS plays) Michael Cook: His opponant...from Athens, Georgia...standing at 5'9"...weighing 230lbs...he is WRESTLINGMAN!!!!!!!! (crowd erupts) DC: Here comes the #1 contender! RR: Give me a break Crawford. "Hollywood" Johnny Tambo has been the #1 contender since mid-October...but Gordon keeps finding loopholes from giving Tambo the shot. DC: Well Tambo has been a busy man lately. He's got Solid Gold to worry about...Crusher Stevenson...Malibu Mike is still on his tail. But why are we talking about Tambo? Let's get back to Wrestlingman who looks to be in the greatest shape of his life...in the ring with the Asswhupper. Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: And Asswhupper now locking up with Wrestlingman...former Cruiserweight champion...lost that one unfortunately to Johnny Coals in a ripoff match. RR: Get over it Crawfish! Let Coals alone! DC: Asswhupper with the weight advantage...and an armdrag takedown...into an armbar...(boos) RR: Ha ha ha! Wrestlingman may know 5,367 holds...but you can bet that the Asswhupper has a counter for every one of them! DC: I don't bet that...all I've seen thus far is an armdrag and armbar. RR: Well... DC: Wrestlingman maneuvering out of that...springing to his feet...twisting the arm of Asswhupper...reverse knife edge chop...now...an Irish whip into the corner...Wrestlingman running in...but Asswhupper leapfrog while holding onto the top rope...Wrestlingman stopping short...and catching him on his shoulder...RUNNING POWERSLAM!!!!!! (cheers) 1.. 2... No...Asswhupper just too tough for that. RR: Asswhupper is just toying with Wrestlingman. You see...this is his style. We go way back...so I know the guy. DC: I just bet. Wrestlingman now...up...and SOMERSAULT LEGDROP!!!! RR: WOAH! DC: And I've seen that used quite a few times before by stable mate Malibu Mike...but never by Wrestlingman. RR: They get together before matches and practice holds. You should have seen it Crawford. I walked in one day... DC: Rex...no. Please...I don't want to go there. RR: In other words..."tell me during commercial Rex". DC: Ladies and gentlemen...boys and girls..."Red" Rex Roseman is a compulsive liar. RR: ha ha ha! DC: Asswhupper now in a whole lot of trouble as Wrestlingman boots him in the head... RR: That's a crude maneuver. DC: Okay. Wrestlingman running to the ropes as Asswhupper raises to his feet...WRESTLINGMAN HOOKING THE HEAD...REVERSED INTO A SIDEWALK SLAM BY ASSWHUPPER!!!!!!! RR: Ha ha ha! What did I tell you Crawdad? DC: Asswhupper now going for the cover... 1... 2.. kickout...and Wrestlingman is in this with a ways to go. RR: Yeah...but Wrestlingman is feeling fatigue. And that's understandable. Him and Malibu were up awful late... DC: Shut up Rex! Shut up or your out of here! RR: Alright...alright... DC: Give me a break! Asswhupper up...legdrop...into a pin... 1... 2... no. RR: Ha ha ha! Sooner or later Wrestlingman has to go down. DC: Or it could work just the opposite. The fans now chanting 'Wrestlingman' over and over...clapping their hands. They're really behind this guy. RR: Well...he's better than Malibu I guess. And lots better than Ice-Pick. DC: Asswhupper up...going to the ropes...coming off with a clothesline...ducked by Asswhupper...Asswhupper off the other side...leapfrog by Wrestlingman...NO...FRANKENSTEINER!!!!! AND A PIN!!!!!!! 1... 2... no! (awes) RR: Ha ha ha! DC: Wrestlingman lifting Asswhupper by the hair...Irish whip...and BIG ELBOW SMASHES ASSWHUPPER INTO THE CORNER!!!!! (cheers) Asswhupper staggered and ready to go down...AND HE DOES!!!! (cheers) RR: WAIT! LOOK! DC: MAMA IS IN THE RING! AND MAMA WITH THAT LEATHER BELT...WHACKS WRESTLINGMAN RIGHT IN THE FACE WITH IT!!!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: HEY! That's not cheating...it's called motherly love! DC: Mama is Asswhupper's girlfriend Rex. RR: Oh, she is. (pauses) Ewwwww. DC: Wrestlingman has gotten out of the ring and he's not too happy. Asswhupper and Mama are in the ring...shouting obscenities. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner as a result of disqualification...WRESTLINGMAN!!! ("War Machine" by KISS plays) RR: What a ripoff! DC: Yes indeed it was...but an excellent match before Mama got involved. Both men have made their ways out of here...and what's this? (R.A.D. , the Goddess and the two mystery men make there way from behind the curtain and walk down to the ring. No introduction and no music. R.A.D. is wearing a street clothes which consist of blue jeans, white t-shirt, cowboy boots and a black leather jacket that has A.O.A. written on the back. R.A.D. is also carrying an all white baseball bat. The Goddess is wearing blue jeans, boots and a black top. The two mystery men are wearing identical white ninja outfits with white boots. All three enter the ring and R.A.D. gets the mic.) R.A.D.: I know I don't have scheduled time out here tonight but I don't care I have some things to say about those slugs from Natural Currency, The Brotherhood and the Bad Boy Alliance. (Crowd cheers.) R.A.D.: First off I'm going to talk about the Brotherhood. Zodiac after the Goddess came back to me last week she told me what you did to her and man did that tick me off. Do you know how hard it was for me to sit their and listen to the Goddess tell me the things you did to her. It was HELL! After she was done I wanted to get into my car and hunt you down like a dog and finish you off. Zodiac you have signed your death notice. The contract for a steel cage between you and me is in Gordon's office so you have no choice but to sign it Zodiac. I'm going to get revenge at what your did to her and my revenge is going to be bitter sweet. Zodiac you see these two men standing beside me well take a good look because if you don't sign the match I'm sending these two after you because I put a boundy on your head. I take that back I'm sending them after you if you do or don't sign the match. I gave you the chance to sign the match but since your not I'm putting these guys after you. And I don't care what you look like or if your still breathing when they get done with you but you better pray that they finish you off because when I get a hold of you, you are going to be breathing your last breath. (Crowd Cheers) R.A.D.: Now onto topic number two and that being Natural Currency. Here we have a bunch of guys who don't know what stable they are apart of. I mean one week their this stable and the next week they are another stable I mean come on. Hey who is in the stable now, Moe, Curly and Larry. This stable has no talent just look at Johnny Pain. I kicked his butt on Tuesday didn't I. (Crowd says YES!) R.A.D.: Then the big boy comes on the Bottom line and says that he had a easy victory and for me to check my apple. Pain I embarrassed you so bad on Tuesday that I can't even think of how you can come out here and say that you won the match. You were in Pain on Tuesday you fool. That low life scum your boyfriend Johnny Chamber maid or so-called retired wrestler, I guess his PMS was bothering him so bad he had to retire, had to come in and hit me on the head with a steel chair. Chamber maid I signed a contract to meet you in the ring and you backed out. That tells me and the wrestling world that you have a yellow streak running all the way from the back of your neck down your spine. Chambers if you think that getting a cheap win over me is going to shut me up and make me go away well your wrong. Chambers I also have a bounty on your head pal. If you aren't going to wrestle anymore because your too old, your hair is falling out or some stupid excuse that doesn't mean that I can't get even with you Chambers. I decided to give my boys here some more fun and have them come after you too. Kinda think of it as multi-plexing Chambers if you know what that means. I have another contract right here and its a open contract to face any member of Natural Currency. Troy, Maxwell hell even Pain if he wants his butt kicked again the contract is on the table. To me all you guys are just all talk and no action so get off your butts and get in the ring with me and I'll show you what I wrestler is all about. (Crowd Cheers) R.A.D.: And finally my third topic and I know I have to get this over with so Rex can get home to his mommy so he can be in bed by ten o'clock because we all know how cranky he can get if he doesn't get enough sleep. (Crowd laughs) RR: Hey! You suck RAD!!!! R.A.D.: Hey Rex shutup or Mr ball bat here is going to make you see stars. As I was saying my third topic is the Bad Boy Alliance or should I say the Sad boy Alliance. You guys are real men let me tell ya. You were supposed to be at one of the biggest Pay Per View of all time in the UWA but you skipped town. You were supposed to face my friends King Suicide, Judas and Johnny Walker in a six-man tag but you skipped town. Mr Moneyz shows he ugly and I mean ugly face a couple times crying about how he was treated like dirt and given no respect. I personally shed alot of tears over your speeches Moneyz....like hell I did. As I'm concerned Sad boy Alliance and is this is coming from me and from everyone else in the UWA who is sick of you, all of you guys can kiss my A@@! (Crowd Cheers) R.A.D.: King Suicide, Judas and Johnny Walker want you in the ring and I and my men want you in the ring so we can shutup those loud mouths of yours. I don't care if its single, tag team or six man I want a match. And if you guys sign the match, oh I forgot you can't write or speak the english language then get someone to sign it for you, you better show up or their is going to be more hell to pay for you. (Crowd Cheers) R.A.D.: And I would also like to take this time to say that my men and myself of course and hopefully another member of the A.O.A. are entering the eight man war at Wrestlefest II. It doesn't matter how you team us up because the A.O.A. is coming out on top of that eight man war. (Crowd Cheers) R.A.D: And one last thing before I go. I'm getting sick of seeing wrestlers not showing up for matches and from now on if you no show you are going to answer to me. I don't care who it is your going to answer to me and you will not like it at ALL! Michael the mic is yours and I'm outta here. (Crowd Cheers) (R.A.D., the Goddess and the mystery men walk back to the dressing room.) DC: Well...said with integrity. RR: He sucks!! DC: Well, we're getting set for the match between Malibu Mike and Johnny Walker. ("Harvester of Sorrow" by Metallica starts playing.) RR: What's this? (Chris Simmons walks in, mix reaction from the crowd. He walks to the ring.) DC: Looks like he wants a mic. RR: Then give him one! (Someone hands Simmons a mic and the music stops.) Chris Simmons: First off, there's a man named Nick Williams shooting his mouth off about how I won't even put up a fight at our Camden Street Fight! (Simmons smiles) Simmons: Camera man, get a close up. (Camera closes in on Simmons' face. He runs his finger along his hairline, a scar is visible.) Williams, you did this to me. (Simmons now holds his hand up. Deep red scars are visible all over his palm.) You also did this. Plus numerous other scars. When we get into that ring at Tuesday Night Wars, it's goin' be a battle, but son, you don't know what your getting yourself into. You talk about how VVV can't win the title in a straight match, one on one. Son, the ends ALWAYS justify the means, a lesson you will surely learn this coming Tuesday. ("Harvester of Sorrow" by Metallica starts playing) DC: Some strong words from Chris Simmons. RR: I wonder what he meant when he said he's gonna teach him the ends justify the means. DC: We're just gonna have to wait for Tuesday, and now maybe we can get on with our match. (Shawn Micheals music begins to play) DC: Here comes Malibu Mike. (Malibu Mike comes out with Senasational Sara.) RR: Malibu Mike, with him is Sensational Sara. DC: We're sure to have a great match up, as he's set to face Johnny Walker. RR: That's right, both men are going to hae to work for this one! Micheal Cook: The next match is scheluded for one fall. Introducing first, from Malibu, California, weighing in at 260 pounds, MALIBU MIKE! (The music stops as Malibu Mike reaches the ring. Sara enters the ring then Malibu Mike.) ("The Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson starts playing. Johnny Walker comes out, along with Judas, King Suicide, Gina B. Fine and Robert Mulligan.) Micheal Cook: And his opponent, hailing from Hollywood, California, and weighing in at 285 pounds! JOHNNY WALKER! RR: He's bringin' the whole crew with him! DC: Like the old saying goes, 'Power in numbers', and it looks like Walker is a full beleiver in that. (Johnny Walker reaches the ring. Sensational Sara is now out of the ring and the members of the AOA stay out at ringside.) RR: Well, it's about 5 on 2 here. DC: And it's supposed to be a singles match! --DING------DING------DING DC: There's the bell and were underway. RR: They quickly lock up and it's Walker with an arm drag. DC: They lock up again and this time Mike pushes Walker into the corner. The ref is calling for a break. RR: Mike backs up but then nails him with a thumb to the eye! DC: Dirty tactics there by Malibu Mike! RR: Walker now stunned. DC: Mike goes on the attack, punches and kicks him to the ribs. Sets him up and whips him into the ropes. RR: Mike nails him with a closeline, quick cover but could only get one. DC: Both are now up and Walker kicks Mike to the midsection and nails him with a European uppercut which staggers Mike. RR: Walker now nails Mike with a closeline of his own! DC: Walker picks Mike up and hits him with a vertical suplex! RR: Walker now going to the top! He jumps and MISSES a flying elbowdrop! DC: Mike quick to his feet and goes on the attack. He stomps Walker's head and drops an elbow. (boos from the crowd) DC: The crowd isn't liking what it sees. RR: Walker on his knees but Mike kicks him back down. DC: Mike now going to the top rope! He poses for the crowd a little! RR: He's wasting time! DC: Jumps and MISSES with a flying elbowdrop of his own! RR: Unbeleivable! DC: Walker to his feet, staggering a bit though. Against the ropes and drops a leg on Mike. RR: Now picks Mike and nails hime with a closeline. Mike goes right back down. DC: Walker picks him up again and looks like he's going for a piledriver. RR: No. He can't get it! DC: Mike backdrops Walker OVER THE TOP ROPE! Walker is now on the floor and he is hurt! RR: AOA trying to go protect Walker but the ref stops them! DC: What's Sara up to? She's going towards Walker. RR: From behind she rakes the eyes of Johnny Walker! (crowd boos) DC: The fans don't like that at all! RR: Mike now on the floor throws Walker back in the ring and follows right behind him. DC: Walker is holding his eyes. He was really hurt but the racking of his eyes. RR: Mike grabs his head....BULLDOG! Mike quickly back up and goes to the top! DC: SUMERSAULT LEGDROP! RR: It's over! DC: Goes for the cover! ONE......TWO......KICKOUT! RR: SO CLOSE! DC: Malibu Mike grabs Walker....he's setting Walker up. RR: Walker goes up.....MAILBU DRIVER! DC: THIS ONE'S OVER! RR: ONE....TWO.....THREE!!! --DING------DING------DING (Shawn Micheals' music plays) DC: Malibu wins it and the crowd is booing! Micheal Cook: The winner of the match, MALIBU MIKE! RR: Mike grabs a chair, but the AOA members are right there, protecting Johnny Walker. Stay tuned for Part 2... ---------------------------------------------------------------------
