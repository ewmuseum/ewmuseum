UWA (1996) Card Archive

UWAFNF pt. 2 11/8/97

9 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
("Hit 'em Up" by Tupac starts playing. Thugs for Life come out.) Micheal Cook: The following match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring, weighing at a combined weight of 505 pounds, hailing from South Pheonix, Arizona, THUGS FOR LIFE!! (Thugs for Life enter the ring. The music stops) *The arena darkens, and the air temperature drops.* Micheal Cook: Ladies and gentlemen, making his way down the aisle... *"Horror Business" by the Misfits, begins to play.* Micheal Cook: Hailing from London England, weighing in at 267 lbs... *Fireworks streak across the arena as the crowd pops loudly. Sabbath steps out from behind the curtain, Sayaka close behind. His long raven hair is loose and unkept, his eyes are blank, devoid of any emotion. His black leather trench coat flows behind him like a cloak. Underneath, he's wearing a black t-shirt with "Ebola Zaire" written in large white letters. On his back hangs a Spanish steel broadsword. He's wearing long black tights with " Sabbath" stitched down both legs in dark red. He pauses, gazing at the roaring crowd, then slowly makes his way to the ring, ignoring the fans' extended hands, caring only for his opponants destruction.* Michael Cook: The Walking Contradiction.... *He climbs the stairs leading to the ring, and hands his coat, shirt and glasses to Sayaka, then stands in the center of the ring. He lifts his arms high, but with the same cold expression, and more fireworks shake the building.* Micheal Cook: SAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAABBAAAAAAAAATH!!!!!!!!! *Sabbath calls for the mic.* Sabbath: Ladies and gents...the time has arrived. Please give a warm welcome to my partner... *Sabbath flashes an evil smile to the camera* Sabbath: THE ADVIIIIIIIIIIIIIISEEEEEEEEEEEEERRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!! *Sabbath points to the entranceway.* ("Paisley" by Ministry plays as the Adviser comes through the curtain in a black tux...followed close behind by Donna Di Notte, who is wearing a long blue evening gown...her hair falling long and brown down her back. Willy Ciccio and Fabrizio bring up the back...both in tux and hats.) *Sabbath high fives the Adviser and the two get ready for the match* --DING-----DING------DING DC: Sabbath is going to start this match along with Eastside. RR: Eastside tries to punch Sabbath in the chest but has no effect, Now a punch to the head has no effect. DC: Eastside goes flying from a palm blow to the chest by Sabbath. RR: Eastside tags to Westside, let's see what he can do. DC: Westside comes running and tries a dropkick! RR: He just bounced off Sabbath, NO EFFECT! DC: Standing drop kick by Sabbath sends Westside accross the ring! RR: This man is incredible! DC: Now has Westide up and POWERBOMB! RR: He knocked the wind out of Westside, and now we get to see what the Advisor can do! DC: Advisor now in, takes Westside and DDT! RR: And now he takes him and backbreaker! DC: Westside is hurtin' now! Tag brings Sabbath back in. Sabbath has Westside and hits him with a roundhouse kick. And he quickly tags Advisor back in. RR: Very good teamwork being shown by Sabbath and the Advisor. DC: Advisor now has Westside in a sleeperhold! RR: Sabbath still not out of the ring and the ref is being distracted by him. DC: Eastside comes in with a chair and NAILS Advisor in the head. RR: Eastside now out of the ring and Sabbath finally gets out! DC: Westside able to get over and tag! Eastside comes in and grabs the Advisor. PILEDRIVER! RR: And now a legdrop. and an elbowdrop! DC: Eastside really taking it to the Advisor. Picks him up and suplex! RR: Westside now comes in and a DOUBLE DROP KICK! DC: Westside stays in, I don't beleive there was a legal tag there. RR: Westside now with a NECKBREAKER. He drops the knee across The Advisor's head. He's going to the top.......... DC: MISSES with a flying headbutt! His head BOUCED off the canvas! He's out!! Advisor trying to make a tag....... RR: He gets over there and SABBATH now in the ring as Westside makes a tag. Eastside charges at Sabbath....Sabbath catches Eastside and closelines him over the top rope!! DC: He picks Eastside up and throws him against the ropes.....SPINEBUSTER!! RR: Weastside and Advisor are both laying in their corners, no one is going to get help!! DC: Sabbath picks up Eastside. HOUSE OF FIRE, he's got him in that submission hold he calls the HOUSE OF FIRE!!!!! RR: Eastside is out!!!!!!!!!!!! DC: Ref checks...he calls it!!!!!! IT'S OVER!!!! --DING------DING------DING DC: SABBATH AND THE ADVISOR WIN IT!! Micheal Cook: The winners of the match. SABBATH AND THE ADVISOR!! (Sabbath raises his arms in victory, high fiving the Adviser, but still keeping an eye on the Thugs, or any other signs of a sneak attack. Sayaka comes up and hugs him tight, and shakes the Adviser's hand. He calls for the microphone again.) Sabbath: Well, what'd I tell you? We beat you Slugs. Easy as pie. (Sabbath shakes the Adviser's hand heartily, and raises it one more time to the cheering crowd. He then smiles, and nods to the Adviser along with any other Syndicate members at ringside, as he makes his way to the back, slapping the fans' hands.) DC: Well...I thought maybe we'd see something long and drawn out...but not at all. The Adviser and Sabbath really taking care of business out there tonight. That was incredible. RR: Well Sabbath is impressive...can't deny that. DC: Oh no.... (Who shot ya by Notorious BIG starts to play as Eliminator Scott May and Kelly o Dell Come to the ring. Eliminator is wearing Boss jeans a t shirt that says good to be bad on the front and Eliminator on the back. Scott May is wearing a suit and tie and his hair is in a pony tail and he is carying his brief case. Kelly o Dell is wearing cut up jeans and has a Horsemen shirt on. And a leather jacket. They enter the ring and Eliminator grabs the mic.) Eliminator; Tambo get your sorry Bleep down here lets not wait lets give these people what they want to see me kicking your Bleep all over the arena. Hell i am not leaving till i wrestle some one what about you Rossmen. I am so sick of your mouth week after week. Are you going to come up here or am i gonna have to drag your sorry Bleep up here. Kelly takes the mic. Kelly; They all are afraid of you Eliminator none of them has the balls to step in the ring with you. (Scott takes the mic) Scott; Where are you at Tambo we have all night. Well this only proves Tambo is afraid of you Eliminator he has been trying to avoid you now every one here knows he is afraid of you. RR: I'm going up there. DC: What? RR: Watch this. (Rex Roseman drops the headset...walks to ringside and takes Michael Cook's microphone) RR: Hey...Eliminator. Open your eyes boy...take a look around. (crowd falls into a hush as the Horsemen begin to look around the arena) DC: What is he doing? RR: Do you hear that? No...I don't either. You boys don't have any fans. You bring your 2 cent whore Kelly O'Dell...you're freak of nature in a hockey mask...you bring your big money man...and you come on here week after week and say the same old crap. DC: Somebody shut his mic off. RR: But what do you hear? Certainly not cheers. Sure...you're not popular with the fans. But...neither was the Venom Corp. Neither was the Dungeon of Destruction...two of the greatest UWA stables in history. But these guys got SOME reaction when they came down. YOU on the other hand...DON'T GET ANY! (cheers) The bottom line is...YOU SUCK! (cheers) Nobody loves you...nobody hates you...nobody loves to hate you. There's not a wrestler in the UWA that wants to waste his time coming out here to whip your BLEEPs...BECAUSE YOU SUCK!!!!! (cheers) NOW GET THE HELL OUT OF HERE!!!! (crowd erupts...standing to their feet) (Scott May drops the mic and the Horsemen exit as Rex Roseman takes his seat back at the booth.) DC: I hope you're proud of yourself. RR: Oh yeah Crawford...I'm proud of myself! Ha ha ha! DC: Let's go to Michael Cook. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is for the UWA Heavyweight Championship!!!!!!!!! (crowd erupts) ("Ride of the Valkery" by Wagner plays over the P.A.) Michael Cook: First...requesting the first entrance...he is from Bucharest, Romania...standing at 5'9"...weighing 259lbs...the UWA Heavyweight champion...VILE VINCE VIPER!!!!!!!!!! (boos) RR: Yes! DC: And...oh no...what is this? Caliban and Goliath...with a host of midgets...they're carrying that full body cast. Did VVV get hurt again? RR: I don't know Crawford...I hope not. DC: And Caliban looking under the ring now as Goliath and the other midgets set that body cast up facing the entrance... ("The Mirror" by Dream Theater plays) Michael Cook: And now...from the Urbish Mines...standing at 6'11"...weighing 326lbs...accompanied to the ring by Willy Ciccio and Fabrizio...REX OVERLORD!!!!!!!!!! (crowd cheers loudly) DC: Here they come...members of the Syndicate...Rex Overlord a former UWA heavyweight champion as well...and now Ciccio and Fabrizio stand examining that full body cast...but Overlord walks on by... RR: No! DC: Overlord looking under the ring... RR: Woah!!! The body cast just knocked out Frabrizio and Ciccio!!!! And now it's going after Overlord!!! DC: That's VVV!!! And VVV bulldogging Overlord on the concrete!!! That body cast not slowing him down at all!!! RR: Woah! DC: Overlord getting right back up...but not without some pain...and the thumb tacks have been spilled in the ring. This IS a thumb tack match...and Michael Cook did not announce that for whatever reason. But Overlord is up on his feet now after rolling over one of those tacks... RR: Ha ha ha! VVV is wearing a body cast! What can those tacks do to him? DC: I never thought of that Rex. But now VVV is in there while Fabrizio and Willy Ciccio are to their feet on one end of the ring...Vile Vince Viper's marauders on the other. That's some hunk of humanity... RR: Yeah...well pay attention Crawford. VVV sending Overlord to the turnbuckle...and crashing in with a big splash.... DC: NO EFFECT!!!! (cheers) Overlord ripping that very sloppy cast off the head of VVV...and now wrapping it around VVV;s neck! RR: Count him out ref! DC: The ref forced Overlord to break it...but Overlord now...big right hand to the head of VVV...going to the ropes...HE SLIPS!!!!! TRIPPING ON THOSE Tacks...Overlord lands flat on his stomach...tacks sticking out of him! RR: Ouch! That's got to hurt. DC: And now...VVV with a big splash over the back of Overlord...sending those tacks even deeper...and now...kick right to the face!!! RR: Overlord was stupid to come down here in that singlet! DC: I won't use the word stupid...but I must agree with you Rex that he could have dressed a little more for the occasion. RR: A little more for the occasion...I got that one Crawford! Ha ha ha! DC: Okay...Overlord up now...and VVV delivering several hard rights and lefts...now sending Rex to the ropes...clothesline! And that hard plaster on the cast hitting Overlord right in the chest...knocking the wind right out of him. I don't know why the ref is letting this go. RR: Because this is the UWA Crawford! DC: That's no excuse. Overlord sinking into the corner...and VVV with several rights and lefts to the chest...but now...OVERLORD RETALIATING WITH A HEADBUTT!!! (cheers) But VVV going down...WOAH!!! HE JUST SLAMMED REX OVERLORD ONTO THOSE THUMB TACKS!!!! RR: YES! VILEAPOLLOZA BABY!!!! DC: I DIDN"T THINK HE HAD IT IN HIM!!! Vile Vince Viper showing tremendous strength...really sending Overlord crashing into those tacks... RR: Overlord's got more pins in him than a porcupine! DC: And now...bleeding down the back in several areas. This match has never been a pretty one. I can remember Slayer back in the old XCW and some of his thumb tack matches. Those were incredible. RR: Well...Slayer felt no pain until he came into the UWA. DC: That's true enough...but even then...I've never seen a man so vicious in there. But that's the past...and right now we've got two vicious atheletes in there who won't take a back seat to anybody. Overlord and VVV...both men so relentless...you'd think they should be friends instead of enemies...but it didn't work out that way... RR: Overlord is getting up...but VVV hooks him around the waist...BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX!!!! (boos) DC: And take a look...Overlord's flesh has been torn away...and he is an open wound. Last year at about this time he feuded with former two time UWA heavyweight champion TNT...but TNT never did anything like this to him... RR: VVV is looking great in there! He's actually beating Overlord in his own game! DC: Well these fans have not given up...chants of Overlord can be heard all through this Joe Louis arena... RR: V-V-V! V-V-V! V-V-V! DC: And now...VVV pulling Overlord up by the hair...knee to the gut...bending Overlord over...and now...hooking his head under...I can't believe this...HE'S GOING FOR A POWERBOMB...BUT IT's REVERSED BY OVERLORD...AND VVV TUMBLES INTO THOSE TACKS!!!!! RR: NO!!! DC: Overlord...dropping a big leg!!! (cheers) And putting even more tacks into himself...I don't understand that...while the tacks are just sticking in that cast of VVV's...and VVV is getting to his feet... RR: He didn't feel a thing Crawford!!!! DC: Overlord bleeding down his legs...some bad punctures in his leg. And VVV is to his feet...but Overlord with a boot to the gut...but VVV won't feel it through that cast!!! RR: Ha ha ha! DC: Overlord sending him to the ropes...BOOT TO THE FACE!!!!! (crowd erupts) AND OVERLORD BENDS HIM OVER...HEAD BETWEEN THE LEGS...INTO A...A PILEDRIVER!!!!! RR: No!!! DC: He just piledrived the top of VVV's head into those tacks!!! But meanwhile...taking more tacks in the legs and knees... RR: Oh no...look at this... DC: Fabrizio now has taken one side of the ring...Ciccio on the other...and Fabrizio is calling to conference Overlord... RR: This can't be good. DC: Fabrizio has taken some brass knuckles out of his coat...and he's going to give them to Overlord...BUT NO!!!!!! RIGHT INTO THE FOREHEAD OF REX OVERLORD!!!!!!! THE REFEREE DOESN'T SEE IT!!!!!!!!! RR: WOAH!!!!! DC: And Ciccio on the other side...he's been knocked out by Goliath!!! And those midgets are stomping Ciccio!!!!!!!! HERE COMES THE ADVISER!!!!!!!! (loud cheers) But Goliath meeting him halfway...AND PUTTING HIM IN THE FRUNDS!!!!!! RR: Look!!! Look!! DC: VVV is covering Overlord... 1.. 2... 3...I can't believe this. Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: YES!!!!! YES!!!!!!! YES!!!!!!! DC: Rex Overlord has never been pinned twice by one man until now...and this is his first pinfall since January of 1997...this is despicable. RR: Who cares about him? The UWA Heavyweight champion is STILL...VILE VINCE VIPER!!!!!!!! Ha ha ha! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner...and STILL UWA heavyweight champion.......VILE VINCE VIPER!!!!!!!!!!! DC: And Vile Vince Viper now in the ring...with Fabrizio...and Adviser still in the Frunds. This is terrible. RR: Hey...Fabrizio took off his hat. Since when does he have brown hair? DC: And VVV has got the mic. VVV: "I thought only snakes shed there skin." DC: VVV...he's ripping...ripping a mask off of Fabrizio...OH MY GOD...THAT'S FLAMING BOB!!!!!!!!! RR: HOLY SH[BLEEP]!!!!!! DC: Unbelievable. (Flaming Bob tears off the jacket and shirt...revealing a bare chest...he kneels at VVV's feet) Bob: To the glory of Hell On Earth...I am at your service my master... DC: And VVV...he's moving aside...he's...oh no. Flaming Bob has gone to the top rope...we're going to see a ....NO!!!! MOONSAULT OFF THE TOP ROPE....INTO THE TACKS!!!!!!!!!!! RR: WOAH!!!! HOLY MAGNIFICENTMOOLAH!!!!! HE JUST POKED HIMSELF FULL OF TACKS!!!!!!!! DC: And VVV looking on...laughing...as Flaming Bob rises from the tacks...he's covered in blood...and Rex Overlord is still out... RR: THIS IS CRAZY!!! DC: Flaming Bob just intentionally moonsaulted onto a ring full of thumb tacks...and he seems to be enjoying that pain... (crowd all turns to the entrance and break into cheers) DC: IT'S SABBATH!!!!!!! SABBATH WITH A CHAIR....OVER THE BACK OF GOLIATH!!!!!!!! (cheers) AND THE ADVISER HAS BEEN RELEASED FROM THE FRUNDS!!!!!! RR: WOAH!!!! DC: Sabbath now charging the ring...but Bob and VVV are out of there...into the capacity crowd...getting out of here as quickly as they can...and the midgets are following. RR: Overlord's up. DC: He is...still bleeding badly. And now the Adviser is up...and the Adviser is staggering to the ring...and rolling under the bottom rope. Sabbath now in the ring as well. Are we going to see a new union? RR: Please...nothing like that. DC: Willy Ciccio now is in the ring as well...and the Adviser is talking with Overlord. And...Overlord now I suppose being relaxed... RR: ...and they all lived happily ever after. DC: Well...we see now the Adviser patting Overlord on the shoulder...so everything must be okay...BOOT TO THE GUT BY OVERLORD!!!!!! AND HE POWERBOMBS THE ADVISER!!!!!!!!!!!!! RR: WOAH!!!!!! DC: And...the Adviser is down in those tacks...not moving. What is going on here? RR: Ciccio is down on his knees...checking on the Adviser...and Overlord just standing there and looking on... DC: Sabbath now...he's not sure what to do in there. Ciccio rising to say speak to Overlord...put Overlord just pushing him out of the way...and now leaving the ring. Not a happy man. RR: I think it's safe to say that there's some problems in the Syndicate. DC: I think...well I don't know. (mixed reaction from the crowd) Overlord walking backward down the aisle...keeping his eye on what's going on in the ring. Folks...our time here is up. We'll see you on the Spotlight. Goodnight.
