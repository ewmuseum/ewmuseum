Bad Habit: Well, Chad Blake is a very good wrestler, there's no denying that. But, with his cocky and snobby attitude, not many people like him. Now I'm just a middle class adult...I was raised middle class...I will probably always be middle class. I don't doubt Suicide's ability, I mean, he is the champ and all. But after what he did to me, I lost all respect for him. It became personal once I was stabbed in the back. That back stabbing became the basis of our motto. And come Sunday, it actually WILL be an all out war. Jason: OK, one more question. Yes, you in the black dress... Reporter #4: This is for Hell's Angels. You guys had one hell of a brawl last week. What made you decide to make such a challenge? Also, you have to defend your titles against Her Majesty's Secret Service, who have only wrestled one match in the UWA. What plans do you have to take them down? Hate: In answer to your first question, we've ALWAYS liked extreme matches. Before we joined the UWA, we did a lot of extreme fighting in Singapore and Japan. We've had matches in many places, but never in a locker room. We figured the best way to punish the Men at Work was to beat them in our home court! Sid and Mitchell, although they're not in the UWA anymore, put up one hell of a fight. Jeez, I've never seen such blood. But, we're still the holders of the Title. Anger: Like you said, the only match that HMSS has had was against Shades and Bad Habit. Those 2 men had never tagged before, and were very unskilled at teamwork, as you saw. (Bad Habit smiles) Now, they face us, who have been tagging for 10 years now. We can read each other's moves like a book. The way we see it, the HMSS are just 2 guys that brawled a lot in England. But now they're on America soil, and they're gonna get an American welcome! (camera focuses back on Jason) Jason: Well, I'd like to thank all of you once again for coming. We'll see everyone at Patriot Games! Remember...
