(UWA) Saturday Night Slaughter House *Part 2*

20 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- Continued from Part 1 -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- ( A strange man who looks familiar walks down the isle and takes a seat at the broadcast booth ) Dan Crawford: Who the hell are you? Man: I am Matt Macbeth remember me UWA!!!!!! Dan Crawford: Oh shit Matt Macbeth: I told you punks I wasn't dead. Boy, was that moron Gunloc wrong. ( "Come out and play" by Offspring plays as the fans boo ) Chris Anderson: And their opponents at a total combined weight of 1020 pounds they are Homicide, Brutality, and Mutiny, the current UWA Six Man Tag Team Champions the Masters of Mayhem!!!!!!!!! ( The fans boo and throws trash as the Masters of Mayhem enter. They are all pratically identical except for their size differences. They all have brown hair and are wearing baggy purple pants. They give the finger to the fans as they make their way to the ring. They take off their belts and climb into the ring ) Matt Macbeth: It's the Masters of Masterbation Dan Crawford: Hey, you can't say that. Matt Macbeth: I can say anything I fucking want by the way I am under contract. Dan Crawford: And I thought Rex was bad. Matt Macbeth: Hey, you shut your hole Crawfish. Ding Ding Ding Dan Crawford: Scott Storm and Mutiny will start off. Matt Macbeth: Who cares they both suck. Dan Crawford: Mutiny kicks the Superstar in the mid-section and then follows it up with a DDT. Matt Macbeth: What the hell kind of name is the Mutiny? Dan Crawford: Mutiny now with an elbow drop followed by a cover 1 2 Dan Crawford: A kick up. Matt Macbeth: Big fucking deal Dan Crawford: Mutiny tags in Homicide who steps over the top rope and whips Scott Storm into the ropes and nails him with a boot to the head. Matt Macbeth: Boot to the head NAH NAH Just give him a boot to the head. Dan Crawford: Homicide now presses Storm over his head and drops him face first to the mat. Homicide with an elbow drop, but Storm moves out of the way and tags in Crusher Stevenson. Matt Macbeth: Is it a fact we have the Masters of Masterbation vs. the Masterbation Crew. Whoa! Dan Crawford: Stevenson, comes in and lands a big clothesline on Homicide. Matt Macbeth: Who is this Fatal Corp's leader Suicide? What does that mean everytime he gets in the ring he commits Suicide. What the fuck kind of name is that. Dan Crawford: Crusher Stevenson now whips Homicide into the ropes and nails him with an elbow to the jaw. Matt Macbeth: Nighty, Night ( Macbeth appears to have fallen asleep ) Dan Crawford: Stevenson covers 1 2 Dan Crawford: A kick up. Matt Macbeth: Come on Pamela, take your clothes off. Dan Crawford: This guy his sick. ( Macbeth wakes up ) Matt Macbeth: What the fuck are you talking about? Dan Crawford: Back to the match Buzz Craven is now in there for the Devastation Crew and Brutality is now in there for the Masters of Mayhem. Matt Macbeth: Buzzed Craven and Beastilty are in the ring WOW Dan Crawford: They tie up and a suplex by Brutality. Brutality waits for Craven to get to his feet and he kicks him in the stomach and lands a neck breaker on him. Brutality covers 1 2 Dan Crawford: A kick up. Matt Macbeth: Is that all you know how to say "A kick up" Huh Huh a kick up Dan Crawford: Brutality whips Craven into the ropes, but Craven reverses it and a powerslam by Craven. Craven tags in Crusher Stevenson. Matt Macbeth: This is so exciting NOT Dan Crawford: Mutiny and Homicide hit the ring, but they are cut off by Craven and Storm who attack them and those four fall to the outside and are battling it out. Matt Macbeth: Damn, the UWA needed me back. Ha Ha Ha Ha Dan Crawford: Crusher Stevenson now applies the Crusher Breaker on Brutality, Matt Macbeth: Big deal Dan Crawford: Vic Weston is distracted by Craven and Storm who are beating down Homicide. Mutiny now has a bell in his hand. ( The fans explode as Big Butch Thorton runs down to ring side ) Matt Macbeth: Big Butt Thorny what is he doing here?? Dan Crawford: Mutiny sneaking up behind Crusher. He raises the bell in the air. Thorton snatches the bell and clocks Mutiny over the head with it. Thorton now slips to the outside as Weston turns around and checks on Brutality and he calls for the bell Ding Ding Ding Chris Anderson: The winners of this match the Devastation Crew!!!!!! ( The Hollywood Blonde's music plays as the fans go nuts. The Crew get the belts and they hold them in the air. Storm then goes up to the camera and says "Next time the belts are coming back to Chicago!" They then leave the belts and exit ) Dan Crawford: The Devastation Crew now gets a title shot on Thursday Night. Matt Macbeth: Whoop did doo Dan Crawford: You got problems Matt Macbeth: I ain't the one who has problems the UWA has problems now that I AM BACK!!!!!!!!! Dan Crawford: The cage is being lowered now for our main event!!!!! ( The UWA Theme Music "nWo" plays as Fireworks shoot from the turnbuckles as a roofed steel cage is lowered over the ring. The fans are going wild ) Matt Macbeth: I thought this was the UWA not WCW Dan Crawford: Shut up. Matt Macbeth: Crawford, you know I beat Scorpion once before. Dan Crawford: What?? Matt Macbeth: Yeah, I fucked his shit up. Dan Crawford: Okay whatever. ( The camera shifts to the outside of the cage where Chris Anderson stands. The referee Jason Drake is inside the steel cage ) Chris Anderson: The following contest is our main event. It will be a no way out cage match. As you can see there is a roof on this cage and the door will be locked. So the competitors can not escape. ( The fans explode ) Chris Anderson: Introducing first from Chicago, Illinois. He weighs 337 pounds and stands at a height of 6' 11" tall. He is a former UWA International Champion here is Immorality!!!!!!!!!!! ( The light's dim as "Blur the Technicolor" by White Zombie plays. The song starts out slow but all of a sudden gets intense as fire shoots up from the floor near the entrance area. Up on the video screen, highlight's of Immorality's matches play and on another screen a Immorality logo is shown. Immorality walks out looking straight at the cage ahead of him. Immorality is wearing his black leather trench coat and orange boots and carrying a gold electric guitar. He ignores the fans and heads straight into the cage. He takes off his coat to reveal his black leather sleeveless shirt and long black leather pants. ) Dan Crawford: Immorality is prepared with a new look and entrance. Matt Macbeth: Immorality what kind of name is that. He should have a good name like Eternity or Mercury. Dan Crawford: Let me guess you managed those guys Matt Macbeth: How did you guess?? The Industry still lives OH YES it does Dan Crawford: Oh jeeze ( The Theme From Candyman plays ) Chris Anderson: And his opponent from a Large Estate in New England, he weighs 290 pounds and stands at a height of 7' 1" tall here is Damon Zex!!!!!!! ( The DIA fans give a wierdo's pop as Damon Zex walks through the curtain with Mister Sinister and "Vile" Vince Viper. Viper carries the box that had spiders in it on Thursday Night. Damon Zex is dressed a lot like Pinhead from Hellraiser. He has a black star painted across his face with a white background plus he has his hair tied back in two pony tails ) Dan Crawford: Immorality leaps out of the cage and chases VVV around the ring. Immorality throws his guitar at Viper and it hits him in the head. Matt Macbeth: Isn't that Viper guy GAY??? Ding Ding Ding Dan Crawford: Immorality now climbs back in the cage and is attacked by Zex. Another ref Steve Sable locks the cage from the outside. Matt Macbeth: Zex scares me and that isn't easy to do. Dan Crawford: Zex continues to pound on Immorality. He backs him into the corner and chops him in the throat. Matt Macbeth: The UWA has real gotten dull without me. Dan Crawford: Damon Zex now whips Immorality into the other turnbuckle and charges in and nails Immorality with an avalanche. Matt Macbeth: Avalanche, that reminds me I have to steal some Avalanche tickets tonight. Dan Crawford: Damon Zex now sets Immorality up on the top rope and he applies a claw hold to the face of Immorality and leaps off the top rope and slams Immorality's face to the mat. What shall I call that a flying claw hold. Matt Macbeth: I don't give a shit what you call it. Dan Crawford: Damon Zex covers 1 2 Matt Macbeth: He kicks up what the fuck else did you expect me to say. Dan Crawford: Why don't they censor you? Matt Macbeth: They can't censor me dumb ass this is LIVE Dan Crawford: Zex now with a heart punch on Immorality and another cover 1 2 Dan Crawford: A kick up. Matt Macbeth: Look at those morons over there with the DIA T-Shirts they don't know how stupid Gunloc really is. Dan Crawford: Damon Zex whips Immorality into the cage and a back body drop. NO a neck breaker by Immorality. Matt Macbeth: Finally some offense from Immoronality. Dan Crawford: Immorality now gets on top of Zex and lays into him with hard right hands to the jaw. Immorality now drags Zex by the pony tail to the cage and slams his head into an Zex's face paint turns from white to blood. Matt Macbeth: I bet you think you are pretty funny well your NOT!!!!!!!! Dan Crawford: Immorality now with a piledriver on Zex and a cover 1 2 Dan Crawford: Zex kicks up. Matt Macbeth: I can't wait till Johnny Sick shows up. Dan Crawford: Immorality now sets Zex up on the top rope and a superplex by Immorality. A cover 1 2 Dan Crawford: A kick up. Matt Macbeth: I think I am going to have to get Smith to get me on all of the shows. Dan Crawford: Immorality now going for the Euthanasia and he lands it. Matt Macbeth: The Epidydimus WHAT?? Dan Crawford: A cover 1 2 Dan Crawford: Immorality is distracted by Mister Sinister who is hanging off the cage. Matt Macbeth: Isn't that Pretty Boy Floyd??? Dan Crawford: Zex from behind with a double axe handle followed by a back suplex. Matt Macbeth: It is he is the original faggott of wrestling. Dan Crawford: Zex now climbing the cage he gets all the way to the top and he is climbing across the roof of the cage. This man is like well, a spider!!!! Matt Macbeth: Big deal Dan Crawford: Zex now releases his grip and lands on Immorality with a splash. Zex now with the Spider Claw on Immorality. Matt Macbeth: Does he have to puke like that?? Dan Crawford: Drake calls for the bell Ding Ding Ding Chris Anderson: The winner of this match by submission Damon Zex!!!!!!! ( The Theme From Candyman play as Zex celebrates on the outside with his associates. While Immorality sits in the cage pissed off ) Dan Crawford: Well, this has been one crazy night. Good night and see you Monday. I hope. Matt Macbeth: I am BACK!!!!!!
