Quarterback John Elway steps back for a pass, scrambles left... scrambles right... ducts a tackle.... winds up and throws a long bomb. The ball flys fifty yards threw the air as the UWA theme music begins to play. - http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4913/ACDCSHK.MID - The camera follows the football as it spirals high above the endzone, and then the goldpost, and out of the stadium. The ball is then shown flying high above the downtown skyline of Denver and then begins it's downward trek towards Denver Collesium. Just before the football reaches the top of the arena, the scene is transformed inside with thousands of screaming UWA fans. Fireworks begin to explode above the wrestler entranceway and underneath the four-sided videotron above the ring. The sea of fans can be seen displaying UWA merchandise and homemade signs; showing their like and dislike for many UWA superstars. Some examples are as follows: 'TnT returns' 'Blakescorps Will Go Down' 'Rex Is A Wuss'. The camers then pans over to commentater Dan Crawford and Rex Roseman.) Dan Crawford: Welcome to the hottest wrestling program in the world today. The fans here in Denver are going berzerk as we have an incredible card here on Sunday Night's Main Event. We have former two time heavyweight champion and UWA president, TnT comming out of retirement to battle the Monster. Rex Roseman: Like I said last night on Weekend Spotlight, I can hardly wait to witness TnT finally getting just what he deserves. Do you know just how long I have been waiting for this. Dan Crawford: We will have the International title on the line as champion Lord Zero defends his belt against Crusher Stevenson. Cruiserweight Bad Habit will be steping up in weight to take on Malibu Mike. In a double debut, Mark Doom will be going against Damien Dredd. And in our main event, The Fearsome Foursome with Big Butch Thornton, Crusher Stevenson, and the D-Crew will be staging war with Chad "The Specimen" Blake and his self titled Blakescorps with Sean Goldz, Johnny Coals and the Masked Avenger. Rex Roseman: This is shaping up to be the greatest Main Event in UWA history. In one night, I get to witness TnT, Crusher Stevenson, Malibu Mike and the ENTIRE Fearsome Foursome get their a--es kicked, and I have the best seat in the house Crawford. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== TnT v. The Monster ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== Dan Crawford: Lets get down to ringside and Michael Cook. Michael Cook: The first match this evening will showcase the former two time UWA heavyweight champion!!! (Crowd cheers loudly.) Michael Cook: Introducing first... (Eeiry music begins to play over the P.A.) Michael Cook: From parts unknown, standing over seven feet tall and weighing in at 500 pounds... Here is the MONSTER!!! (Crowd bood loudly as the Monster makes his way toward ringside. He is wearing brown cloth like attire draped over his body. His head is greenish and many scars can be seen etched into his deformed face and bald skull. He enters the ring with no emotion what so ever.) Michael Cook: And his oppenent... ('TnT' by AC/DC begins to play over the PA as the crowd rises to their feet and begin to cheer wildly.) Michael Cook: Hailing from the steel city of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. He is a former two time UWA heavywieight champion making his return to the squares circle for the first time since retirement.... HERE IS T N T!! (The crowd erupts as TnT walks through the curtain wearing a UWA issued suit.) Dan Crawford: Wait a minute, TnT does not look ready for a wrestling match at all. Rex Roseman: I KNEW IT! He chickened out. He didn't want the Monster to embarse him. (TnT enters the ring and grabs the mic.) TnT: I know I said that TnT would be wrestling here tonight. And I don't want to let everyone down. Rex Roseman: Wuss! TnT: That is what I said and that IS what is going to happen. Come on out Timothy. ('The Good Life' by Tony Bennett begins to play as the crowd looks on in confusion. An incredibly good-looking GQ type with short-cropped red hair walks down the isle. He wears simple red and white bike-style tights which can be seen peeking out of an ornate robe, also in red and white, with TNT emblazoned on the back.) Rex Roseman: Nooooo!!!!! Dan Crawford: Wait a minute, That's Timothy N Turner from the IIWF. What is he doing here. TnT: I've invited a friend og mine from another organization, Timothy N Turner, aka.... TnT! Show em what you can do Timothy. Rex Roseman: This can't be happening, TnT from the UWA is supposed to be in there, not this guy. (Bell Rings!) Dan Crawford: And we're underway. They approach each other..... And Turner with a kick to the stomach of the Monster, and an upercut to the throat....... he grabs the Monster by the head..... Nicely executed suplex....... Turner off the ropes.... Big Leagdrop... This match isn't even close. Rex Roseman: This is BULL----!!! The other TnT should be in there getting the crap kicked out of him. Dan Crawford: And Turner already singles for his finishet as he climbs to the top...... And there it is....The big elbow drop.. I believe he calls that TNT... The cover... ONE!!! TWO!!! THREEE!!! And this one is history. What an impressive match for Timothy N Turner. A huge superstar in out sport today. TnT shakes TnT's hand and they both leave ringside together. Rex Roseman: This is absurd, How many times do I have to hear the letters TNT. I can't take much more of this kind of crap from the president. Dan Crowford: Well, we are going to take a break. But their is much more action still to come, so don't go away. And Rex... Maybe you can't get yourself straightened out. ***break*** commercials ***break***
