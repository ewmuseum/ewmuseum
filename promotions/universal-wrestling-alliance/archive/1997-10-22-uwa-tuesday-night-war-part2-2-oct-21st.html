UWA Tuesday Night WAR!!! part2/2 - Oct 21st

22 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UWA TUESDAY NIGHT WAR PART 2/2... RR: Ha ha ha! Tonight the Brotherhood sleeps with the fishes! DC: Incredible it is...the Syndicate out of here without a wrinkle or a crease in their brand new tux. The Untouchables still looking for opponants this Friday. And of course we'll see the other two in action at Hellstorm ‘97. And what an event that will be. RR: Oh yeah. DC: Syndicate out of here...and the Brotherhood gathering their senses now...and they're upset. But right now...we've got tag team action. Let's go to Michael Cook. ("Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson plays) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...accompanied to the ring by Robert Mulligan and Gina B. Fine...they are Judas and King Suicide...the Angels of the Apocalypse! (Cheers) DC: AOA becoming one of the top stables around here just a short time ago. Of course they're not making as big of headlines now as Solid Gold. RR: They've been quiet for the past little while. This match should be interesting. DC: Well here they come...same two guys. RR: Well who were you expecting? DC: That music from the P.A. is terrible Rex. What is that noise? RR: It's not music...and that's for sure. DC: The Angels of Apocalyspe look ready inside the ring and they are gonna have to be as the long awaited return of the Devastation Crew is gonna take place in just a moment!! Let's take it back down to Michael Cook. RR: Please. Michael Cook: And their opponents....... ("Welcome To The Jungle" by Guns N' Roses starts to play over the loud speakers) Michael Cook: At a total combined weight of 479 pounds....making their return as a team here in the UWA...."Superstar" Scott Storm....Buzz Craven.....THE DEVASTATION CREW!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The crowd goes wild as "Superstar" Scott Storm and Buzz Craven walk out. Scott is wearing a pair of navy blue tights with "Superstar" written in gold on the back, boots, and kneepads. He has stylish looking blond hair and long sideburns. Buzz has a pair of black tights with "Buzz" written in electric blue on the back, black boots, and black kneepads with a hint of electric blue on them. White tape are around his wrists and on his right bicep is a tatoo of the word "Buzz" with barbed wire wrapped around it. He has a VERY short brown cut and a brown goatee. Scott is all smiles and walking confidently to the ring pumped as as Buzz has a cold stare on his face, adjusting the tape around his wrists. Scott gets in the ring and jaws with the AOA while Buzz stares them down coldly) (Camera cuts back to Crawford and Roseman doing Beavis and Butthead headbanging to G'N'R) RR: We're on. DC: Ah-ha! Welcome to WAR! (Trying to get his composure) RR: Ha ha ha! DC: Shut up! The Devastation Crew has made their presence felt...and in the ring now. I can't wait for this one. Ding! Ding! Ding! RR: You won't have to! The bell has rang and Buzz Craven is in the ring with Judas! DC: And Craven saying something to Judas...something that Judas obviously doesn't like very much. Now Craven...shoving Judas back...and Judas with a roundhouse right that Craven ducks! Craven with an atomic drop on Judas! And to the ropes...big clothesline and Judas hits the mat like a ton of blocks! RR: A ton of blocks? DC: Craven now with a quick tag to Storm...but not before Judas can tag in King Suicide. King in...with short jabs to the face of Storm...knocking Storm back against the ropes...but Storm catches a fist...swings it around into an armbar...tagging Craven...who goes to the top turnbuckle...and brings a fist down into the arm of King Suicide! RR: Ouch! He's softening him up! DC: Craven hooks King for a suplex...but King blocks it...into one of his own!!! RR: Woah!!! Buzz Craven just got suplexed over the top rope! DC: Big move...and now flying over the top...King Suicide right on top of Buzz Craven!!!! RR: And this guy knows how to turn things around! DC: Scott Storm with a double axe handle from the ring apron drops King Suicide...tossing him in...and now helping Buzz Craven in. Craven with a tag to Storm who leaps inside...dropkick to King Suicide! And now...big jumping elbow drop! Into a pin... 1... 2.. No. RR: Hehehe... DC: Storm now up...kicking King Suicide right in the face! RR: Holy tenochtitlan! DC: Storm just scored a field goal with King Suicide's head I think...where from behind is Judas with a clothesline! (Some cheers) RR: Somebody had to break it up. DC: Storm is down now...as is King Suicide who is holding his nose...which appears to be bleeding. Storm makes a tag...and in comes Buzz Craven knocking Judas off the ring apron! Storm to the outside...and Craven lifting King Suicide by the hair...throwing him to the ropes...cross body press by King Suicide! Into a pin...that's reversed by Buzz Craven!!! 1... 2... 3!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! ("Welcome to the Jungle" by G'N'R plays) DC: Devastation Crew...going 1 - 0 in their first match back! RR: Good for them. I guess if they have to beat somebody then it might as well be the A.O.A. DC: Scott Storm to the second turnbuckle...saying he's number one. Buzz Craven to the camera...giving the throat cut sign... RR: What did he say? DC: He said "Frank N. Stein...the Buzzer's gonna get ya." RR: Yeah...and I just bet that Frank is scared. DC: Both teams now are out of here... RR: Hey Crawford, how about this music? (They begin to headbang as the music fades out) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is our main event...and is for the UWA Heavyweight Championship!!!!!!! (crowd pops) ("Elimination" by Overkill plays) Michael Cook: The challenger is from parts unknown...standing at 6'1"...weighing 245lbs...he is accompanied to the ring by "Legendary" Scott May and Kelly O'Dell...a representative of the Brotherhood...the SANDMAN!!!! (boos) RR: Here comes more Brotherhood. Didn't you guys humiliate yourselves enough already? DC: Sandman is down here...now getting in the ring. As we saw earlier...the Brotherhood is under some new management. Mr. Tanaka has been dismissed and this Scott May has been brought in. RR: Never heard of him. Michael Cook: And his opponent........ ("Enter The Sandman" by Metallica starts to blast over the loud speakers.) Michael Cook: Hailing from Detroit, Michigan, standing at 6"10 and weighing 345 lbs., here is the UWA Heavyweight Champion..............BIG BUTCH THORNTON!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The crowd is going nuts as "Enter The Sandman" continues to play. As the opening part of the song ends, Big Butch Thornton walks out holding the Heavyweight Titles with a eager look on his face. He is wearing a black Fearsome Foursome t-shirt with a Fearsome Foursome logo on the front. His wrestling attire consists of the Kevin Nash type pants, singlet thing. It is completely black except for the initials "BBT" in red on the stomach part. He wears white tape around both of his wrists and red elbowpads. His boots are black. He has long brown hair with a mustache and beard. He walks about halfway to the ring, then runs to the ring, going right at it with Sandman.) Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: Alright! We've got the main event! RR: And what a main event it is! BBT against some midcarder...disguised with all the glory of a heavyweight championship belt. I'd rather have seen Suicide vs. Frank N. Stein...or the tag team match. DC: Well...BBT sending Sandman to the ropes...BIG BOOT TO THE FACE! Drops Sandman to the mat...BIG LEGDROP!!! Oooh! And these manuevers of Butch Thornton...carrying so much impact. RR: He kind of reminds me of Overlord back when he was still good. DC: What? RR: You know. DC: I know nothing of the kind. BBT with the pin.. 1... 2... 3..no! RR: Ha ha ha! He almost got him with a boot to the face and a legdrop! DC: Butch Thornton lifting him by the hair...into...JUDGEMENT DAY!!!!! (Crowd erupts) RR: BIG POWERBOMB!!! IT'S OVER!!! DC: BBT dropping another big legdrop...but not going for the pin. He's lifting him by the top of the mask...JUDGEMENT DAY!!!!!! RR: Holy parcheesy! DC: Still not going for the pin...he's up...leg drop! RR: Oh...this hurts just watching it. DC: Well...Butch looks bored now. He's up and he's got Sandman by the mask...CHOKESLAM!!!! (Crowd roars in approval) RR: Still no pinfall? DC: Butch is really getting all the good out of this that he can. Sandman has not mounted any offense...not since the brawl with the Untouchables earlier in the day. RR: This really isn't fair. Should Butch get points for beating up this scrub? DC: That's the idea Rex. Butch now...has got Sandman up...MILITARY PRESS...THROWS HIM TO THE OUTSIDE RIGHT ON TOP OF SCOTT MAY!!! (cheers) And here comes the Zodiac and Stalker! RR: Ha ha ha! DC: But they're keeping their distance. As Butch rolls Sandman back in the ring...and now in himself. Butch with...a legdrop to the back of the head! And rolling him over. For the pin... 1... 2... 3...forget about it. Ding! Ding! Ding! ("Enter the Sandman" plays) DC: BBT now...he's rolling Sandman out of the ring with his foot...and raising his arms in victory! (Crowd pops) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner...BIG BUTCH THORNTON!!!!!! DC: There's your champion UWA...live and in color. Since August 30th this man has set the standard. And not only has this guy gone through some huge obstacles...this guy has dominated them. At the Summer Showdown it was Chad Blake. At Stampede it was Crusher Stevenson. And now at Hellstorm in less than a week he'll be meeting R.A.D. And personally I think R.A.D. will be the biggest challenge of them all. Folks I'm Dan Crawford. RR: And I'm "Red" Rex Roseman! We're out of here! DC: We'll see you on Friday Night Fights! So long! (Fades to black) 1997 UWA Sports™ -All Rights Reserved-
