UWA Sunday Night's Main Event - 3/4

12 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dan Crawford:" Welcome back, and now it's time for the match between Jean Guy Lavelle and The Eliminator, what do you think about it Rex?" =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Jean Guy Lavalle v. The Elimanator =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= writer: Doug D ----------------------------------- Rex Roseman:"This will be a boring match, because both of these wrestler's suck majorly, none of them come close to the talent of the specimen!" Dan Crawford:"I wonder what is the next bandwagon you will be joining Rex, let's go down to ringside!" (The Eliminator is already in the ring, whereing his usual attire. The Announcer, Micheal Cook is also in the ring) (There is a sound of electric saws and the lights darken... the spot lite moves to the entrace from the dressing room... Jean Guy Lavelle moves towards the ring.. he has his mind set on the match.. yet he acknowledges the crowd with a few quick waves.. he looks at the ring and moves that way) Michael Cook: And now entering the ring... from the logging camps of Northern Quebec.. weighing in at 321lbs.. the French Lumberjack.. Jean Guy Lavelle!!!!""" (The crowd cheers and boos.. as he is neither a good guy nor bad.. so a mixed reaction is heard.. just as he is about to enter the ring.. a spot lite travels back to the dressing room entrance.. a man about 6'6 and 300 lbs stands in the dark.. lit only by the light of the spot.. he is dressed in black.. a blackmask and nothing to distinguish him by) (bell rings) Dan Crawford:"Who is that man at the dressing room area? Nobody knows who it is, not even us?" Rex Roseman:"Maybe it's the pizza man dressed in disguise,with my pizza I ordered like last week, which I never got!" Dan Crawford:"I doubt it Rex, I really doubt it!The match has begun, neither wrestler knows what to expect from the other so their just circling each other!" Rex Roseman:"Boring,boring,boring! I'm going to sleep!" (Rex puts his head down on the desk,and falls to sleep) Dan Crawford:"Oh well, peace and quiet without him! Both wrestler's are still circing each other, and let's hope this isn't going to go on for a lot longer now, folks! I wonder why Jean Guy keeps looking out at Mr.Tanaka, it's like he's expecting him to interfere or something!" (Rex get's up) Rex Roseman:"I can't sleep because your little high pitched voice Crawford!" Dan Crawford:"Oh put a sock in it, Rex, or I'll knock you out to make you sleep!" Rex Roseman:"Ooooh I'm scared, hahahahaha! Finally their starting to fight!" Dan Crawford:"Jean Guy Lavelle slap's The Eliminator in the face, and again, and again, and again, The Eliminator is getting ticked off! The Eliminator charge's Lavelle, but Lavelle grab's him, and powerslams him to the mat! The cover... 1... 2... kick-out, by The Eliminator!" Rex Roseman:"Hahahahahaha, what a loser he went right into Jean Guy Lavelle's trap, he set up The Eliminator, nice move there by Mr.Lavelle! Jean Guy is now stomping down on The Eliminator, now he pick's up The Eliminator, and he now has him in a bear hug, yes go Jean Guy I like this guy, because he want's to injure that dumb Eliminator forever, go Jean go, go Jean go!" Dan Crawford:"I thought you would like Jean Guy after those moves! Jean Guy grab's The Eliminator's legs, and he lock's on a figure fourleglock, and The Eliminator is in pain, but he is not submitting, and he is in the center of the ring! The Eliminator is starting to move towards the ropes, and he just grabbed one, the ref is telling Jean Guy to let go, but he is not, and now he finally does!" Rex Roseman:"Go Jean Go, Go Jean Go! Jean Guy pick's up The Eliminator, and he doesn't know where he is, he's just wobbling around, Jean Guy is climbing up to the top rope! Jean Guy jump's off and does a flying double axehandle on The Eliminator, and again, and again, and again! Yes go Jean Guy! Jean Guy is climbing up to the top rope again, and The Eliminator is out of in the by the ropes! Jean Guy jump's off, and does a big splash on The Eliminator! Jean Guy Lavelle covers him, and hooks the legs, and tights... 1... 2... 3!!!" Ding Ding Ding (Sounds of electric saws,plays over the PA,and there is a mixed reaction) Announcer: "The winner of the match, Jean Guy Lavelle!"(Mixed Reaction) (The man in black disappears into the dressing room.. Lavelle turns to Tanaka.. points and motions for him to come into the ring.. Tanaka does not.. Lavelle just points at The Eliminator and laughs at him ... he then moves to the ropes where he stands on the middle rope and waves to the crowd.. !!! moves to the other side of the ring and repeats the wave to the crowd.. he leaves the ring area.. waving quickly as he leaves.. not making any contact with the crowd... no hand slapping or high fives.. just straight out to the dressing room) Rex Roseman:"That Jean Guy Lavelle guy isn't that bad, I kind of like him , of course not as much as I like da man Blake! But man oh man does that Eliminator geek suck, or what!Hahahahahahaha!" Dan Crawford:"You can't say he suck's Rex, but he was just not on his game for some reason tonight, better luck next time he fight's! And we have too take another break, but comming up next is the main event of the evening, stay tuned." ***break*** commercials ***break***
