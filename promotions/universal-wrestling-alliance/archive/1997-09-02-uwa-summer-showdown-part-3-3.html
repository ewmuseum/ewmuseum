SUMMER SHOWDOWN 3/3 Dan Crawford:"Now it's time for the triangle match,for The UWA International Title,between Malibu Mike,Crusher Stevenson,and the current champ Lord Zero,and all of these guy's hate each other,so this will be a war!" Rex Roseman:"Yeah it will be Crawford,it will be a war,which will have Lord Zero kick the other loser's a---'s,and walk out still UWA International Champion!" Dan Crawford:"Maybe Rex,maybe!Let's head down to ringside!" ======INTERNATIONAL TITLE MATCH====== ===================================== Lord Zero(c) v. Crusher Stevenson v. Malibu Mike ===================================== writer: Doug D ===================================== (Michael Cook is inside the ring,with the microphone) Michael Cook:"This match is a triangle match,scheduled for one fall,and is for The UWA International Belt!"(The fans go nuts) (HBK's Music,plays over the PA,and the fans cheer) Michael Cook:"Entering the ring first,weighing in at 275 lbs,6'6,from Malibu,California,Malibu Mike!"(The fans cheer) (Malibu Mike come's down as a whole load of firework's go off in the building.He come's out and flexe's as silver fireworks go off behind him.Malibu Mike is wearing his usual silver tights and all and red fireworks go off the ringposts once he gets in the ring) Dan Crawford:"The first challenger Malibu Mike is already waiting in the ring!Let's take it back to Michael Cook for challenger #2!" Michael Cook:"Introducing 2nd.......... ("Man in the Box"by Alice in Chains starts to play over the loud speakers as the crowd goes nuts) Michael Cook:"From Chicago,Illinois...Standing at 6"5 and weighing in at 265 lbs,here is Crusher Stevenson!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Crusher Stevenson walks out as the crowd erupts.He is wearing a pair of black tights with "Crusher" in electric blue on the back.He wears black kneepads and boots.He also has a goatee.He walks to the ring slapping hands with the fans but not taking his eyes of Malibu Mike.He is very focused and ready for the match of his life) Michael Cook:"And finally.......... ("And Justice For All"by Metallica,plays over the PA,as the fans go nuts) Michael Cook:"From Parts Unknown,but now making his residence in Las Vegas,Nevada,weighing in at 305 lbs,6'11,The UWA International Champion,Lord Zero!"(The fans go nuts) (Lord Zero comes out.Lord Zero is wearing his black leather trenchcoat,a black hat like The Undertaker's,he has his black aluminum bat,and he has The UWA International Belt strapped around his waist.Lord Zero climb's up into the ring,take's off his belt,and hand's it to the ref.Lord Zero then takes off his black leather trenchcoat,and black hat,and hand's them out to the ring attendant.Lord Zero is wearing a more faded and torn version of The Undertaker's ring attire) (Ding Ding Ding) Rex Roseman:"Go Lord Zero go,kick the other loser's asse's,and I bet he will too!Hahahaha,it will be great!" Dan Crawford:"You never know Rex,Malibu Mike,or Crusher Stevenson,might win The International Title!It will be Malibu Mike,and Lord Zero starting it out,as Crusher Stevenson climb's out of the ring!Lord Zero and Malibu Mike are having a big staredown,and oh my Lord Zero just slapped Mike in the face,and you can see a big red mark on Malibu's face,and he's ticked off now!" Rex Roseman:"That was great,Lord Zero just slapped that bitch Malibu Mike,and I bet Mike is now going to cry,because he's a baby!Hahahahahahahahaha!" Rex Roseman:"Yeah way to go Lord Zero kill the big baby!Lord Zero run's to the rope's he come's back and does a flying leg on Malibu Mike,and yes this is great Lord Zero is taunting Crusher Stevenson outside the ring!But damn it Malibu Mike is back up,and he pushe's Lord Zero into Crusher Stevenson,and Crusher Stevenson punche's Lord Zero in the face,come on ref,that's cheating,and you know it,peon!Come on Lord Zero get up!Oh crap Malibu Mike just tagged out to Crusher Stevenson,and Crusher Stevenson look's very angry,come on Lord Zero get up,and kick his gay ass bigtime!Crusher Stevenson pick's up Lord Zero,and he chuck's him to the rope's,Lord Zero come's back and Crusher powerslam's him back down to the mat!Stevenson with the cover,come on champ... 1... 2... phew,only a two count!Crusher Stevenson is up again,but yes,yes,yes,yes,Lord Zero just low blowed Crusher Stevenson,and Crusher Stevenson is now on the mat in pain!Lord Zero make's his way over,and tag's Malibu Mike in!I hope these two geek's kill each other!" Dan Crawford:"Crusher Stevenson is on a roll right now folk's,and it look's like he's going to win the belt!Crusher Stevenson cover's Lord Zero,we got a new champ... 1... 2... 3,no Lord Zero just got a shoulder-up!Malibu Mike is shouting in at Crusher Stevenson,and now their having an argument!Lord Zero is back up,and he grab's Crusher Stevenson,and he oh my gosh,and Lord Zero just did The SoulCrusher(Diamond Cutter)on Crusher Stevenson,and both men are out!Malibu Mike is now in the ring though,and the ref is trying to put him out,but he can't!Mike pick's up Lord Zero,and he does The Wipeout(He starts out like a Perfect Plex but when the opponent is directly above the mat.Malibu drops so the opponents head hit's)on Lord Zero,and now both men are definitely out,and Malibu is dragging Crusher Stevenson over to his corner!Mike climb's out of the ring,and he grab's Stevenson's hand inside the ring,and he hit's it,and the ref's letting it be a tag!Malibu roll's Crusher out of the ring,and he climb's back into the ring!Mike pick's up Lord Zero,and he does another Wipeout on him,and that look's like it's it for Lord Zero's title reign!" Rex Roseman:"No it isn't,because I know Lord Zero has the strength to get up,and he will,come on Lord Zero!" Dan Crawford:"Malibu Mike cover's Lord Zero... 1... 2... 3!!!" Ding Ding Ding (HBK's Music,plays over the PA,and the fans cheer) Michael Cook:"The winner of the match,and Newwwwww UWA International Champion,Malibu Mike!"(The fans cheer) Dan Crawford:"The ref raise's Mike's hand in the air,and hand's him the belt,and Mike hold's it over his head,and holy cow he's now beating Lord Zero with it,but Crusher Stevenson roll's back into the ring,and Mike stop's beating Lord Zero!Malibu Mike is now celebrating as Crusher Stevenson look's on in disappointment!The ref help's up Lord Zero,and is helping him to the back,as Crusher leave's not too long after,with his head held low and his hands on his hips,he's upset that he lost the match,but better luck next time!" Rex Roseman:"Malibu Mike is a big fluke,and he know's he is!Malibu Mike is now leaving with the belt strapped around his waist,slapping hands with the fans,yeah get out of here you big fluke!Lord Zero should still be champion,and everyone know's it,and he better get a re-match with Mike the geek!" Dan Crawford:"Maybe he will Rex,and maybe he won't,no one know's yet!
