New Fed by Proven Fed-Heads

30 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TJ3 Entertainment in conjunction with Double A Productions presents: Universal Wrestling Alliance From 1995 to early 1996 [name withheld] and [name withheld] combined to create one of the greatest turn arounds in e-wrestling history by taking the often mocked and seldom respected RSPCW, and turning into the highly sucessful FWLI. Autrey & Jones proved what solid booking and good talent could do. Now this duo has re-united to bring you what will be "The New Age of Old School Wrestling". Taking it's cue from the NWA era. The Universal Wrestling Alliance will stress no-nonsense wrestling, while straying away from the mindless violence of so-called "hardcore" wrestling. Yes, there will be blood, but not just for the sake of blood. Yes, there will be stipulation matches, but only in feud situations where they are justified. No, there will be no
Garbagemen, Grim Reapers, Bankers, Clowns, or other such nonsense characters. What we are is a federation that stresses substance over style. We want no-nonsense competitors, with as little gimmick as possible. Remember if a character does have a gimmick it must be in the background, it will not the focus of the character. And now without further ado, the character creation sheet.
Wrestler Name: Handler E-Mail: Height: Weight: Age: YrsPro: Build: Alignment: (Heel/Face - NOTHING ELSE!!) Entrance Music: Ring Entrance: Ring Attire: Manager/Valet: Style: Tactics: 10 Common Moves: Finisher & Set-Up Moves: Strengths: (List Three w/ Explanations) Weaknesses: (List Three w/ Explanations) Background: Titles Held: And now for some brief comments on character creation. These characters must be completely realistic, no 8 foot tall 500 pound wrestlers.
Having an enormous character will not help you win. An good 200 pound aerial wrestler can beat a a mediocre 350 pound monster, keep this in mind. Also, most wrestlers do not have a manager or valet, don't use one them unless absolutley necessary. Also, Faces and Heels are two distinct groups in UWA, faces of course work together and there is a certain amount of "honor amoung theives" within the ranks of the heels. You're not going to see almost any heel v. heel or face v. face feuds. As you complete your character please try to keep all these things in mind. Also, look over the following checklist, these are some of the guidelines we use when deciding who we'll let in and who we won't. If you make sure your character fits into these you'll save yourself quite a bit of time.
1) Does the character closely mimmick a real wrestler? If so, he won't be accepted. We don't want any rip-offs. 2) Will the characters gimmick over-shadow his in-ring performance? If so, you need to tone down the gimmick or find a different one. 3) Ring music may not be music used by a purely by a wrestler, for instance we don't want to see "The Giant's ring music from WCW" as your ring music. Yes, there is a reason for this, and it has a lot to do with all the stuff we're trying to work up for the Web Page.
4) Is this character interesting? This is pure necessity if you want to reach any kind of stardom within the federation. 5) Can you write regular, interesting interviews with this character? Once again, a necessity. Push counts a lot towards how well you do in IWA. While this doesn't cover every reason a character could be refused, it covers most of them, and I think it's enough for you to get a picture of what we want. Now onto how the federation will be run.
Every week there will be three tv shows. These shows initially will be match summary style. (Summary meaning 1 to 3 paragraphs describing the match not just one sentence) We will also have one major full-commentary card each month and one PPV every three months. The reason we're holding commentary to minimum to start with is so that we don't get burned out. Doing the matches this way we can make sure that they are always out on time and that there is always action going on.
The shows will work like this: Monday - UWA Live! - 6 to 8 Matches Wednesday - UWA Wednesday Night Warriors - 4 to matches Friday - UWA Friday Night Replay - 4 summary matches + 1 full commentary version of a match from the previous week.
(Selected by vote of the participants.) Our first PPV will take place at the end of July entitled: UWA Ultimate Survival '96 - The Iron Man Challenge On this PPV the four semi-finalists of our title tourney will battle it out for the Universal Heavyweight Championship in a Iron Man competition.
The card will also include various grudge matches. Well, that pretty much sums it up. If you're looking for the style of competition we have to offer, then get involved as soon as possible.
