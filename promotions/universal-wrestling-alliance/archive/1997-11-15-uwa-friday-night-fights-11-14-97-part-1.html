As D-Man makes his way to ringside he waves his fist to the crowd's chants. He high fives a few of the fans along the aisle. As he nears the ring he runs and slides under the ropes into the ring. He then approaches Michael Cook and asks for the microphone.) D-Man: OK, stop the music for a sec. (The Music stops). As most of you know, Eliminator has given me his word that the Horsemen will not interfere in this match. As most of you know, Eliminator is about as trustworthy as a horse's rectum. (Crowd Cheers ).
