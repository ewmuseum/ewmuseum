UWA Sunday Night's Main Event 8-18-97 3/4

19 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== Damien Dredd v. Mark Doom ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== writer: Jason F Wnuk -------------------------------------- Crawford: Next up UWA fans is the debut of 2 up and comers. Damien Dredd finally gets his shot in the ring against Mark Doom. Damien Dredd has been constantly giving interviews on Spotlight, and now finally gets his contract filled. Mark Doom, on the other hand, hasn't really been seen much. Roseman: Maybe that's because he was smoking with Damien Dredd, if you know what I mean! I think both men will sit in the ring and smoke instead of wrestle. What a couple of clowns! Why can't someone else do these matches? Crawford: If you don't want to do them I'm SURE that TNT could find a replacement. Roseman: Thats OK, I'm sure if I left he's hire someone else with the same name. (Camera switches to ringside, where ring announcer Michael Cook is standing.) Cook: Ladies and Gentlemen...this match is scheduled for ONE fall...already in the ring...here is MARK DOOM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (A small pop as Doom raises his hand.) Cook: And his opponent... ("Voodoo Child" by Jimi Hendrix starts up as the lights in the arena dim. Clouds of smoke billow from the entrance illuminated from behind by spotlights). Michael Cook: Coming to the ring... at 6'2" 265 pounds... from the Isle of Jamaica... the Lion of Judah-- DAMIEN DREDD!!! (Dredd emerges from the smoke as a shadow oulined against the backlighting. The arena lights come on as he makes his way down the aisle, ignoring the fans reaching out to touch him, focused on the ring. Dredd climbs up to the apron, then vaults over the top rope.) Crawford: Damien Dredd looks quite prepared for this match. Looks like the Lion of Judah is ready to be a contender in the UWA! Roseman: Yeah, just wait until he faces REAL men such as Chad Blake! [DING DING DING!] Crawford: And we're underway... [Doom wastes no time with Dredd. He runs over and starts to chop and punch him. Dredd looks stunned, but there is a look of rage in his eyes. Doom then whips him to the other turnbuckle, and tries a Stinger Splash. Dredd moves, and Doom hits the mat...HARD] Roseman: See, what did I tell you? [Dredd with fire in his eyes picks Doom up. He gives Doom a WICKED headbutt that sends Doom reeling to the canvas. Dredd picks him up again, and ANOTHER headbutt. Doom stands for a moment, then hits the canvas.] Crawford: Looks like someone's gonna have quite a headache! [Dredd picks up Doom again, whipping him to the turnbuckles. He executes a near-perfect handspring elbowsmash. Doom hits the mat, and Dredd is looking at the crowd. He locks the figure four sleeper on Doom!] Crawford: And Dredd has that sleeper LOCKED! The referee is checking Doom... [The referee raises Doom's hand once...then twice...he raises it again, but before it drops, Dredd lets go of the hold.] Roseman: What is he doing? Has all that smoke gone to his brain? Finish the match already! [Dredd signals for his finisher. The crowd is cheering as Dredd goes to the second turnbuckle.] Crawford: There it is! Jamaican Spike! (Shooting Star Press, landing with a headbutt.) [The referee counts...1...2...3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Cook: The winner of this match...The Lion of Judah...DAMIEN DREDD!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Dredd throws Doom out of the ring, and lets the referee raise his arm in victory. He then exits the ring, slapping hands as he leaves to the exit. The fans are cheering.] ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== International Title Match ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== Lord Zero(c) v. Crusher Stevenson ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== writer: Jason F Wnuk -------------------------------------- Dan Crawford: What an impressive debut for Damien Dredd. Next up is LordZero putting his UWA International Title on the line against Crusher Stevenson. He won the right to wrestle against LordZero 2 weeks ago, when he defeated Malibu Mike. Let's see what happened... (Camera plays a tape of Malibu Mike vs. Crusher Stevenson. On the bottom right hand corner, it says "UWA SNME 8/2/97".) Crawford: Crusher doesn't see Malibu getting up...OUCH...Malibu hits Crusher with a forearm to the back of the head...now he turns Crusher over...SCORPION DEATH LOCK!!!!!!!!!! (LordZero, seeing that Malibu might win, runs down to the ring... followed by Bad Habit, Hate & Anger.) Crawford: Crusher is in pain as Sara smiles...Crusher says no...wait...Bad Habit is on the ring apron...he gets the refs attention...LordZero in the ring...SOULCRUSHER ON MALIBU MIKE!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Malibu is out for the count...now Hate & Anger come in...start beating on Crusher with the bats!!!!!!!! The ref turns...and he signals for the bell!!!!!!!!! Announcer: The referee has declared this match a NO CONTEST!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! *** Crawford (voice over): But as fate may have it, the Legion got what was coming to them... *** (fans go crazy as "TNT" by AC/DC blares over the speakers. TNT is walking to the ring, in a nice black suit, and a UWA tie. He grabs a microphone as he comes into the ring.) TNT: I've had enough of you guys! You four have caused enough trouble! LordZero, one more stunt like that, and you're gonna forfeit that title you so treasure. And guess what? You think that this match is over? Well, I'm the president, and I say what goes! This match is to continue until there is a WINNER! LordZero: *bleep* YOU! LET THE DAMN MATCH CONTINUE! I'LL JUST KEEP COMING IN HERE AND KICKING @$$ UNTIL BOTH OF THEM ARE FINISHED! TNT: Try it, and you four will be asking for it! (LordZero picks Malibu Mike up and gives him another SoulCrusher) LordZero: How about that? What are you gonna do now loser? TNT: Well, since Hell's Angels have a title defense tonight, I can't do anything today. (LordZero smiles and the Legion laughs) BUT...starting Monday, you guys are SUSPENDED! You 4 are hereby SUSPENDED for 1 week...and FINED $100,000! Now, get the hell outta here, otherwise I'll make it longer! (Security escorts LordZero, Bad Habit, Hate & Anger as LordZero yells "You're going down TNT...IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR!" TNT leaves, and the bell rings again) *** Crawford (voice over): Though the Legion was suspended, Malibu Mike wasn't the same after LordZero's vicious attack... *** Crawford: Well, Malibu and Crusher are back up...Crusher whips a groggy Malibu to the ropes...and a gorilla press slam...he signals for the Crusher Breaker...he's got Mailbu up again...the ref checks Malibu...and he shaking it off...Malibu is screaming... Roseman: Oh who cares! Get it over with! Crawford: And the ref signals for the bell!!! It's all over!!! (Camera comes back to live action.) Crawford: So a tainted victory for Crusher, but a title shot nonetheless. Roseman: A title shot that is going to result in a loss! (laughs) LordZero is gonna pummel that scrawny loser! Crawford: Let's go down to Michael Cook who's ready to announce tonight's title match... (camera switches to ringside, where Michael Cook stands in the middle of the ring.) Cook: This match is scheduled for one fall...and is for the UWA INTERNATIONAL TITLE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (HUGE crowd pop!) Let's introduce the challenger... From Chicago,Illinois...Standing at 6"5 and weighing 265 lbs., here is CRUSHER STEVENSON!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ("Man In The Box" plays over the loud speaker as Crusher Stevenson walks out to a huge ovation. He is wearing a pair of black shorts tights with "Crusher" in electric blue on the back. He wears black kneepads and boots. He has a mustache/beard thing like alot of the wrestlers nowadays have (SCSA, Taz, Saturn, etc.) Crawford: Crusher Stevenson looks in the best shape of his career. Hold on....Crusher is calling for the mic. Let's listen. Crusher Stevenson: Well I figured on LordZero having all of his goons to watch his back at ringside. Well I prepared myself. Cmon out fellas!! Crawford: Crusher is calling some guys out! ("Enter The Sandman" by Metallica blast through the arena as Big Butch Thornton and the Devastation Crew walk down to ringside. Butch is in his wrestling attire with a black Big Butch F'N Thornton t-shirt on. Scott Storm has his wrestling attire on with a white UWA t-shirt. Buzz has his wrestling attire on as well.) Roseman: NO NO NO!!! Crawford: Big Butch Thornton and the Devastation Crew!!!!!!!! Things are a little more even now! (The Fearsome Foursome stand and pose in the ring while the fans go nuts.) Cook: And now, introducing the UWA International Champion... ("Beautiful Disaster" by 311 plays.) Cook: From parts unknown, but making his residence in Las Vegas, Nevada...he is 6 feet 11 inches...he weighs 305 pounds...accompanied to the ring by his manager LadyZero and the Legion of Darkness...here is the UWA International Champion...LORDZERO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (LordZero's voice is heard over the PA system.) LordZero: Bring out anyone that you damn well please Crusher! Because after tonight, you'll be lucky to wrestle EVER! Ground Zero is upon you!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Smoke fills the entrance as the arena goes black. The fans are going absolutely crazy as a dark shadow is cast upon the ring entrance. There, 2 bursts of flame are going off on each side. LadyZero is the first to come out. She is dressed in a long black dress, with the Legion's logo on the front. LordZero follows, with his traditional Legion gear, a black leather trench coat and a black Undertaker hat. The rest of the Legion follows, every member carrying the traditional black aluminum bat. They make their way to ring, LordZero's eyes transfixed on Crusher.) Roseman: It's time for Ground Zero! I love it! (laughs) Crawford: All 10 men and 2 women are in the ring. It's a big stare down as the referee is trying to keep some sort of order. The Fearsome Foursome leaves the ring first, followed by the Legion on the other side. [DING DING DING!] Crawford: And we're underway! [LordZero and Crusher lock up. LordZero puts Crusher in a headlock, and Crusher pushes him to the ropes. LordZero comes back, and hits Crusher with a clothesline that sends him to the mat. LordZero picks up Crusher, who pokes LordZero in the eye. LordZero grabs his eyes.] Roseman: Disqualify him! Crusher shouldn't be allowed to do that! Crawford: The Legion comes out weekly and sneak attacks people, and you want a DQ because of a rake to the eyes? [Crusher whips LordZero to the turnbuckles. He gives LordZero a HUGE chop that can be heard through the entire arena. LordZero screams in pain. Crusher follows with another vicious chop. LordZero grabs Crusher and turns him around. He returns the favor by giving Crusher a LETHAL chop. Both Crusher's and LordZero's chests are red. LordZero then gives Crusher a headbutt that sends him to the mat. LordZero covers...1...2...kickout.] Crawford: Now some action outside the ring as Wrestlingman and Bad Habit are taunting Big Butch and the D-Crew! They seem to have put the earlier incident beside them. [Big Butch and the D-Crew are coming towards Bad Habit and Wrestlingman. Hell's Angels come up from behind and NAIL them with their UWA Tag Titles. The four men are laughing as they go to the other side of the ring.] Roseman: Ding dong! Anyone home? (laughs) [Back in the ring, LordZero has the upper hand. He whips Crusher to the ropes and puts a sleeper hold on Crusher! Crusher is fighting the hold, but the more he fights, the more he loses his breath. He slowly falls to the mat, but a jawbreaker by Crusher sends LordZero reeling to the mat. Crusher goes for the cover...1...2...thr...kickout! Crusher grabs LordZero by the hair, and pulls him up. He sends LordZero to the ropes, and NAILS LordZero with a spinning wheel kick.] Crawford: And a BIG kick to the face of LordZero! Crusher with the cover...1...2...3 no kickout! And now there's MORE action on the outside! [Big Butch is going after LadyZero. She runs around a corner, and is met with a clothesline from Hate! Then the D-Crew runs up from behind and clotheslines Bad Habit and Wrestlingman! The 8 men are all brawling outside the ring. Bad Habit and Fallen Angel are fighting Big Butch, and Hate, Anger, and Wrestlingman are working on the D-Crew.] Roseman: It's chaos! Just what the Legion likes! Take em out Legion! [Back in the ring, Crusher is setting LordZero up for a Superplex. He gets LordZero up, but LordZero blocks it. Crusher tries it again, but LordZero blocks and throws Crusher to the mat. LordZero, with Crusher on the mat, turns around and executes a REVERSE ASAI MOONSAULT!] Crawford: WOW! WHAT A MOVE! I didn't think that the big man had that left in him! [The fans point as Malibu Mike is running to the ring, a chair in his hand.] Roseman: NO! MALIBU MIKE! Crawford: He's looking for some payback for last week! [Malibu Mike is stopped by Big Butch Thornton, but Big Butch is hit with the chair. Wrestlingman tries to grab the chair, but Malibu Mike kicks him in the stomach. Malibu Mike makes it into the ring. LordZero is picking up Crusher, and signaling for the SoulCrusher. He sees Malibu Mike swinging the chair, and he ducks, and Malibu Mike NAILS Crusher with the chair! LordZero is laughing, but he is also met with the chair to the face! The referee is signaling for the bell, and the Fearsome Foursome and Legion of Darkness are going after Malibu Mike.] Crawford: The 8 men are trying to get a hold of Malibu Mike, but he escapes into the crowd. The fans are cheering at Malibu Mike! [Malibu Mike grabs his crotch like Syxx of the nWo does, as he grabs a mic.] Malibu Mike: HAHAHAHA! Who's laughing now? I told you payback was gonna be a bitch! Legion, Foursome, you know where you can go! Crawford: Malibu Mike with some well-deserved payback! Now the 10 men are in the ring! LordZero and Crusher are back on their feet, and they stare each other down. The Foursome and the Legion are ready to strike at the command of either man. LordZero sticks out his hand. Crusher cautiously shakes his hand, and the two point at Malibu Mike as the two stables leave the ring area. Well folks, don't go away because our main event here on the Main Event is still to come. ***break*** commercials ***break***
