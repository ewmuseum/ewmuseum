Thursday Spotlight 7/31/97 http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4913/main.html ======================================================= ================================================================== The Legion of Darkness about: Suicide, Crusher Stevenson, Butch Thornton, Malinu Mike, Ministry of Justice, Fatal Clan, Devastation Crew, HMSS, Chad "the specimen" Blake, Wrestlingman ================================================================== (Scene: Miami, Florida. The Legion is sitting in their locker room after WOWA's Tuesday Night Hell House. LordZero, Bad Habit, and Hell's Angels are sitting with 8 other men. The UWA Camera pans over the group, which is immense. This group is the WOWA's Legion. The camera goes over to LordZero, who is still in his wrestling gear. He has just come off of a win against Side Winder. LadyZero sits next to him.) LordZero: Well, what a surprise! I didn't expect a UWA camera to be at a WOWA event! No matter, I suppose that I should talk about the upcoming matches this Saturday. There is one that I am interested in. That is the Malibu Mike vs. "Crusher" Stevenson match. Now, TNT has stated that the winner of that match will get a shot at the UWA's International Title. Well, as I have CLEARLY stated time and time again, I will NOT defend my title until I get a shot at Suicide! Butch Thornton, I realize that you are a great competitor in this sport. But the fact of the matter is that at Summer Showdown, there will be 2 things that will be evident. Either I will be the UWA World Champ, or I will be wrestling for the UWA World Title. Now, I'm not about to go out and hit you with my bat, because that wouldn't be fair now...would it? You can go on and on blowing hot air about how we use bats and you guys are much tougher. We use bats because WE CAN. (swings bat) If this wasn't a trademark of the Legion, then we'd use our fists as well. Mailbu Mike, you may be asking why I came to the ring on Saturday. Well, the answer is simple...scouting the talent. I've seen your work in the HCW as well as the WOWA, and I wanted a close up view. There was nothing more than that. Stevenson, Malibu, be prepared for the battle of your lives! IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (camera pans over to Hate & Anger, who are also in their ring attire. They have just come off of a debut win over the Wild Pokers. They are holding the UWA Tag Team Belts.) Hate: Ministry of Justice...Fatal Clan...Devastation Crew...HMSS. Some great talent we have here in the UWA. It's nice to know that we are the men to beat, and not busting our asses to make our way to the top! Now, TNT has told us that it's time to defend the belts against a team that we've already beaten. Anger: Fatal Clan, you may have changed your names, but your wrestling skills haven't changed! You are still the men that we beat back in our first weeks in the UWA. We beat you then, and we are gonna beat you come Saturday Night! Boys, be prepared for TOTAL anihilation! (camera pans over to Bad Habit, who is in his Legion gear. He was not scheduled to wrestle, but he was in attendance. He is sitting with his manager, Desire.) Bad Habit: So, Saturday Night we're gonna see if Wrestlingman can beat the Speciwoman. I sure as hell hope Wrestlingman kicks the crap outta him. Cause if he doesn't, I'm gonna have to win the gold next week! Blake, like I said, you better watch yourself come Saturday. Because after your match, be it a win or loss, I'll be looking for your punk-ass! You better learn how to take a fast shower, and get the hell outta there! Your ass will be TERMINATED! (screen fades as "It's an all out war!" is shown on the screen) ================================================================== Malibu Mike about: Lord Zero, Scorp, Suicide, Crusher Stevenson ================================================================== Malibu is sitting in his little red corvette listening to some music. He spots a UWA Cameraman and turns the music down.) Malibu: Oh Hello, and how is the UWA this morning. Great, alrighty then. Well the whole UWA saw me beat Scorp one, two, three like I said I would. I haven't heard from him so I have no idea how his feelings are on this whole Lordzero incident. I didnt mind Lordzero being there but I defintely dont need his help. I mean look at me I can beat anybody in this fed. Except for one man....that man is Suicide. I have known Suicide for a LONG time. He knows who I am and I know who he is. We have met more than 3 times in the WOWA. Two out of those 4 being loses and the other one being a sweet win. Well I will soon like to even that status with the UWA World Championship. But now if I beat Crusher Stevenson then I get a shot at Lordzero for his IC Title. (Turns the music a bit more down) Malibu: If Lordzero actually thinks im going to join his little Legion of Darkness than hes got another thing coming. I came to finally get something I wanted 5 months ago, a UWA Title. When I was in here TNT was Champion and Doomsday was IC Champ. You dont see me being a freak and pulling out my hair, you dont see me sit up everytime somebody kicks my ass, you dont see me flashing my body around, wait, I take that back I do flash my body around. The point is I dont need all that bullshit to help me win I just go out ther give the fans what they want and outwrestle my opponent in every single way they can possibly think of. Trust me when I say that tha IC Belt is going to be mine sometime in August and I will hold that belt for as long as I possibly can.. UWA I told you I wasn't some mid carder, I am a champion and I seek for one thing only the UWA World Championship.. I may not get it soon but some day it will be mine. (Fade out as MAlibu turns the radio back up) ================================================================== The Fatal Clan about: The Legion of Darkness ================================================================== ("Triumph" by Wu Tang Clan blast in the background. Scene opens with the Fatal Clan, Menace and Mayhem at FBI headquarters. They have some weird looking smiles on their faces. They look into the camera and speak..) Mayhem: I see that we have a match this Saturday night. Menace: Yes indeed we do. Mayhem: So who are we going against? Menace: Just the tag champions, nothing big. Mayhem: What? We are going against the best tag team in the UWA? Oh no! Now we have to go and train for the rest of this week! They are great! I don't know if we are good enough to beat them! After all they are apart of the biggest and best stable the LOD! President TNT, me and Menace can not handle this big task that you have singed us up for! We are going to have to bail out of this one! (Menace has a confused look in his face. Mayhem puts his head down and looks up laughing like a mental patient.) Mayhem: HAHAHAHAHAHA!.....Now lets get serious! We are the best team ever assembled here in the UWA! We are former CWF and WOWA Tag Team Champions, and former UWA Six Man Tag Team Champs, so what makes you think that you have one chance in hell to beat us? Menace: You two morons are going to pay the price for that screw job that your stable mate Wrestlingfag got over Suicide! He has sent us on a mission! And on that mission we are to go out and kick the ass of every LOD member! You think you guys are all big and bad because you are the Tag Champions well your not! After we get done taking your belts, you'll both be running home crying to your fat slut mothers! Mayhem: We are the best tag team in this shit hole of a federation and we are going to prove that to everyone, by sending you little girls home in an ambulance! You are the tag champions now, but after Saturday night the only thing you'll be is two fagot ass queers! Menace: Get the wax out and shine those belts up real nice and good for us! Mayhem: Then when you are done shinning our belts, you can wax your little dickies up and get each other up the ass some more! Menace: HAHAHA! Mayhem: After Saturday night, we will be the NEW UWA WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!!!! (Screen fades to black.) =================================================================== Grave Tavern about: The UWA =================================================================== < Grave is sitting on his Black/Red Harley Davidson...> Grave: The more things change... the more they stay the same. What do I have to do to get a match around here!?!?!? I want to fight.. I'm sick of being on the side lines.. what do I have to do attack people from behind to get attention..
