Spotlight Tuesday 7/22/97 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ============================= Grave Tavern about: The UWA ============================= <We See Grave Tavern sitting in his Stone Skull Chair..he has a Raven on his shoulder..> Grave: The jungle has called to me...for I am now in The UWA... I'm sure none of you that are here will welcome me with open arms...but yet..closed fist. That is fine with me. I have arrived in this Federation for one reason. .to BE THE MAN!! I have to work my way to the top and that is exactly what I am going to do. I don't care who you all think you are its time that The Champion of The DarkSide get's his turn to shine here in the UWA. I have fought in many Federations..From the small times of the SCW and the FEW...To the GLAMOR WORLD..of the BAWF and UWF. But I have arrived here...I still am in other Feds...The RMW.. AND THE UWF!! But The UWA is new to me..and I intend to make an impact. For those of you that wish to make me an alley...that's fine..but for you that want me as an enemy..I will be happy to do so..but it's your funeral! So I dare anyone to step up to the plate...Grave Tavern awaits... Hey...UWA..THE DEAD MAN COMETH!!!! =========================== Malibu Mike about: Scorp =========================== (Malibu is in the middle of the ring in the mansions gym. HE is wrestling a young man about the age of Malibu. Malibu executes a Bodyslam and flexes into the camera. Malibu picks him up and delivers a Jumping DDT. Malibu drops with a Leg Drop but the man moves. They are both up and Malibu kicks him in the stomach and hooks his leg... The Wipeout!! Malibu isn't finished though, he goes to the top and jumps..... Summersault Legdrop!!!. Malibu picks him up again and The Malibu Driver. Malibu pins him and Sara counts.... ONE TWO THREE. Malibu gets up and sits on the top turnbuckle and begins to speak) Malibu: Well Scorp, nice to here from you again. You have to understand one thing... you arnt going to beat me by dreaming about it......you actually have to beat me in REAL Life, which I think you cant do. (Malibu looks at the man who is still unconsious and laughs) Malibu: You are right I am sixth ranked in the UWA.. and whats one way to move up by beating number five...and who is number five? Well that would be you Scorp. You see I set a goal to myself, that goal is that I WILL beat everybody from today until Summer Showdown and do my best to get that first ranked position to meet Suicide. The only thing that is standing in my way is YOU. And when something stands in my way I make sure its knocked out of the way. Don't think I am saying you arn't a good wrestler because you are, I am just saying you cant cut it to beat Malibu Mike. (The man gets up shakes Malibu's hand and gets out of the room) Malibu: I am preparing as you can see, I am not one bit taking you lightly, I know you can do a good job in the ring. You and your little Scorp's can do what ever they want but this Saturday I will be stomping a big hole on your ass. (Fade out as Malibu gets out of the ring and leaves the room) ===================================== "The Flamboyant One" Scott Daniels about: Bouncer ===================================== (Fade out as Malibu gets out of the ring and leaves the room) ("The Flamboyant One" Scott Daniels dining at one of the finest restaurants in Miami, along side him are two beautiful ladies) Scott Daniels: AAAAAAAAAWWWWWWWWW BBBBBBAAAAAAAAAAAYYYYYBBBBYYYYY!!!!!!!!!!! Its ME!!! The one the women wanna SEE!!!!!!!......come at ya live!!!!....so I got Bouncer come Saturday Night's Main Event.....woooohhhh.... I'm shakin in my Armani Suit!!!.... You see Bouncer, my man, I don't care if you are the 5 time champion of the planet Mars... your still gonna bounced by "The Flamboyant One".... Those belts mean nothin here big daddy!!....for all I know, you moms coulda made you it!!! ..... You claim wresltin ain't "bout stylin and profilin"... but you know somethin..... money is what make the world go round!!!!!.... and a in a part of this world stands the UWA bbbbaaayyybbbyy!!!!.....and in the UWA is a guy who has enough money to buy a country, "The Flamboyant One" Scott Daniels.......and remember...what "The Flamboyant One" wants, "The Flamboyant One" gets!!!!!! AAAAAAAWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW YYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!! (camera fades) =========================== Wrestlingman w/ Ashley about: Suicide, Miss Havoc =========================== Wrestlingman with Ashley both in their new look and they are in the Leigons training facility) Wrestlingman: Well, Suicide, it looks like you can speek after all. To tell you the truth it really suprises me.Now on to the subject. I really do not care who you beat in the past,Suicide. You know why? BECAUSE IT IS IN THE PAST! You old timers should start living in the present. Nobody cares who you beat in your prime. The fact of the matter is I am here I am a hell of a lot better than you think punk. You also seem to think that I am a pice of crap on your shoe? Well it would be pretty imberassing what crap kicks your a$$ in to oblivian. I also think you had better work out a better game plan boy. You will never be able to kick my teeth in because you can't lift your fat leg above your knees. I will not try to think of ways to stop you. You know why? Well, because I know i could block anything you try to do to me.You must think i will have a short carrere. WRONG. I know i could accomplish more in one week than you could in a entire year. I know Mr. Blake has even accomplished more in one day in a bed with your wife than you could ever do in a year. But I am not one for in sults. HAHA. Now, Havoc or what ever your name is, do not even think of hitting my WIFE. If you do i am not afraid to hit anybody. I do not like to hit woman but if that's what has to be done, so be it.Here Ashley i think you have something to say. Ashley: I have a lot to say Hubby. Miss Havoc or what ever the hell you call your self now, do not even think of comming after me. If you think you could stick your fist down my throght you are wrong. If you even try, you find i am more that just the most beautiful woman in the UWA. I am not one to gossip but, tell me, is the rumor about you and RIOT true? The one about the holiday in and that "Bisnuess" trip you took with him?If it is just tell me I will keep a serect. Well that is all i have to say dear. Wrestlingman: Thanks Babby. Now TNT, I am going to ask again. PLEASE make this match for the TITLE. If Suicide thinks he can beat me so fast I think he should be able to do it with the Belt on the line. TNT I know i am only the number 2 contender for it right now but, I think i could win it as i am.One last point before i get back on the subject. Rex Rossman. You can ask all the questions you want and say any thing you want becase the bottom line is your time will come. Now, Suicide, it does not matter if the belt is on the line but i will give you the fight of your life. So, Suicide, if you have the balls put the belt on the line if you are so dam* good. Now, here it comes. Suicide, LIVE IN FEAR!!! AND REMEMBER IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR!!! (Instant Black)
