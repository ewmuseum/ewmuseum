UWAFriday Night Fights Part 1

22 November 1997

(scene quickly cuts to the locker room where Scott May, Eliminator, "Golden Boy" Lawrence Stevenson, and Kelly O'Dell can be seen argueing) RR: Woah! Trouble in Horsemenadise! DC: Sounds like...but take a look at this capacity crowd. Biggest FNF crowd that I remember having in quite some time. RR: This crowd is deafening! I haven't heard this much noise since Tuesday night! DC: Well...this is the big one. The last regular card of the month before WrestleFest II. I can't wait for WrestleFest II. It's going to be great. RR: Yeah it is. But right here tonight...5 matches! Usually four...tonight there's five! DC: Just like another TNW...let's go to Michael Cook with the ring introductions. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following match is scheduled for one fall and is set for a 20 minute time limit... ("Im Too Sexy" starts to play as Kelly o Dell and Scott May come out. Scott May is wearing a white robe that says The Legend on the back and is flashing 1,000 dollor bills to the crowd) Michael Cook: Coming to the ring at this time...2 UWWF world champion and the Icon of wrestling (rolls his eyes)...Legendary Scott May! (boos) RR: Ha ha ha! Did you see Michael Cook! DC: I'm sure President Gordon isn't too thrilled about May adding that 2-time world champion thing to his intro. Let me assure you that he was never champion of this league. RR: Hehehe...the day he's champion is the day that I'll retire. ("That's the way I like it" from K.C. and the Sunshine band begins to play. The arena goes dark and a single spotlight focuses on the curtains. The Goddess walks out first wearing and cut-off white t-shirt, black mini skirt and high heels. The Goddess walks to the end of the entrance ramp and raises her hands. The Goddess then turns around and points to the curtains and R.A.D. slowly walks out. R.A.D. is wearing all red pants with R.A.D. written in black on each leg, red boots and a black leather jacket with A.O.A. written on the back. R.A.D. is carrying an all black baseball bat in his right hand. R.A.D. raises the bat in the air and fireworks go off around the entrance ramp. R.A.D. points the baseball bat to the ring then puts it at his side. R.A.D. follows the Goddess to the ring. R.A.D. slaps hands with the fans along the way.) Michael Cook: Now approaching the ring...from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania...standing at 6'1" and weighing 265lbs...accompanied to the ring by the lovely Goddess..............RRRRRRRAAAAAAD!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (crowd erupts) RR: Wow...they really like this bum! (R.A.D. climbs up to the apron and rubs his boots on the mat. R.A.D. looks at Rex Roseman with a devilish smile on his face then enters the ring. R.A.D. takes off his leather jacket and hands it and the baseball bat to the Goddess on the outside. R.A.D. begins to do some warmup stretches. The arena lights come back up.) DC: Really good having RAD back in action here in the UWA. The last time that we saw RAD was on the fourth of the month. He took an unfortunate loss to "Suicide" Johnny Pain after some rule-breaking went on. But back now and gearing up for WrestleFest...RAD. Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: Who's your pick Rex? RR: Well...since May has absolutely no skill at all I'm leaning toward RAD. DC: We don't know that May has no skill. RR: Come on Crawford...he's won almost as many WOWA titles as Malibu Mike. DC: Ha! Scott May has NEVER been a WOWA champion. I can almost guarantee it. RAD and May locking up...RAD with a greater strength..but May with an eye gouge...and a big right hand...sending RAD to the ropes...big right fist right into the jaw...(boos) RR: Woah! That almost dropped RAD! This Scott May may surprise us! DC: May now hooking RAD in a side headlock...leading him to the corner...raising his feet to the top turnbuckle...and pushing himself off...DDT ON RAD!!!!!!!!!! RR: Hold on right there! That was a direct copy of Chad Blake! DC: It was indeed Chad Blake's finisher! RR: I'm reporting him on charges of copyright infringement! DC: Well executed though...as RAD is down and hurt. The Goddess now on the outside taunting Kelly O'Dell! We could see the beginnings of that women's division that Sayaka was mentioning! RR: The only time I'll watch women's wrestling is when they're doing it in teeny weeny bikinis that accidentally fall off pretty easily. DC: Give mea break. Scott May not lifting RAD by the hair...tossing him to the ropes...DROPKICK RIGHT IN THE TEETH! AND RAD GOES DOWN AND OUT THROUGH THE ROPES!!!!!!! RR: And May is taking a bow...(boos) This is making me sick. DC: May now to the ropes as RAD is trying to get in...but RAD grabs May by the legs...and he's pulling him under the bottom rope! (cheers) Big rights and lefts from RAD...and he hrows him right into the guardrail! (cheers) RR: I knew this domination thing from May couldn't last long. I mean...any guy that aligns themselves with the Horsemen...you've got to know that they are really really terrible. DC: RAD now rolling him in the ring...and rolling right in after him. May meets him with a rake to the eyes...and now...both men raising to their feet...May sending RAD to the ropes...AND RAD OFF WITH A FLYING FOREARM!!!!!!! RR: YES!! HA HA HA! DC: For the cover... 1... 2... no. RR: Woah...RAD is up and hungry for blood I think. DC: Looks like it...RAD up high in the air...BIG FIST DROP RIGHT TO THE FOREHEAD! RR: And those can hurt... DC: But not quite like a "Euthanasia" from Immorality can hurt, eh Rex? RR: Shut up Crawford! If there's one thing that makes the UWA a better place this November than it did last...it's the fact that Immorality tucked his tail between his legs and made a run for it when the chips were down. DC: Well...I don't know if that's exactly true... RR: It is Crawford...just believe. DC: RAD now...lifting May by the hair...Irish whip to the corner...following it in with a huge elbow smash into the corner...but NO!! May moved his head and the elbow of RAD hit the turnbuckle...and May with an inverted atomic drop by on RAD! (boos) RR: May is going to the top! DC: He is indeed...but RAD is getting to his feet. RR: May could be a good wrestler...but he's a little short on brains I think. DC: RAD now...launches him off the top rope...hard onto the canvass!!! (cheers) And now going to the top himself...WAIT! RR: It's "Crusher" Chris Simmons out of the crowd...in the ring and beating Scott May with lefts and rights! Ha ha ha! (boos) Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: And I don't know what or why he is doing this. But RAD now just sitting on the top turnbuckle..obviously upset. RR: Simmons sends May to the ropes...BIG CLOTHESLINE!!!!!!!!! HA HA HA! DC: And RAD is back in the ring with a chair! (crowd pops) SMACK!!!!!! Into the back of Chris Simmons! SMACK! Right over the head of the fallen Scott May! RR: And here comes the Eliminator from the locker room! DC: But following close behind..."Stalker" Toni Gills tackles Eliminator in the aisle...and he's working him over with lefts and rights! (cheers) RR: Woah! Mortikus, Lawrence Stevenson, and Junior just put an end to that! They're stomping Stalker into dust! DC: Mortikus is charging ringside...up on the apron...SMACK! His head meets RAD's chair...and Mortikus drops to the concrete! (cheers...all eyes turn to the entrance as D-Man and the D-Security charge out and begin to brawl with members of the Horsemen) Woah! RR: It's all out war here! DC: RAD to the outside...charging with that chair...nailing Eliminator in the back of the head! (cheers) RR: Here comes security...and they're breaking this up. DC: D-Man, D-Security, and RAD walking out of here as the Horsemen peel themselves off the concrete. RR: Ha ha ha! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner...as a result of a disqualification...SCOTT MAY!!!! (boos) DC: Well...now that they're out of there...the cage is being constructed. RR: This should be good. DC: D-Man vs. Eliminator. Originally scheduled to meet back in September...the D-Man was injured after a match with Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals and was replaced by R.A.D. Now the D-Man is back in top condition and has a couple more victories under his belt. I now that he's looking forward to getting in the ring with the Eliminator. RR: I wonder if D-Man appreciated that chair shot that RAD gave the Eliminator across the head. DC: I really don't know. The Eliminator shocked the world this Tuesday with a pinfall victory over Goliath. Of course he had the help of Ice-Blade who would later turn on him. RR: Best career move that good old Ice-Pick has ever made. DC: Well...they've got it up. And every nut and bold has been checked. There will be no repeat of Coals vs. Wrestlingman at Stampede '97. The cage will hold. Michael Cook: Coming down to the ring at this time he is the self proclaimed greatest wrestler in the world...THE ELIMINATOR!!!!!!!!!!! (no music plays as Eliminator comes out alone. He has a very angry look on his face and is acting unusually. He walks to the cage and enters and leans against the ropes awaiting D mans entrance) RR: Aww...what could be wrong with Eliminator? Do you think his puppy got sick? DC: Don't go there. Michael Cook: And now, about to come to ringside, weighing in at 247lbs and standing 6' 4", here is the Thunder from Downunder.......DEEEEEEEEEEE MMMAAAAAAANNNNNN!!!!!!!! (The crowd erupts as the lights fade and "THUNDERSTRUCK" by AC/DC begins to blare out of the speakers. A large spotlight of a "D" can be seen in the ring rotating. Another spotlight begins to randomly zoom around in the crowd. Eventually it settles at the entrance to the aisle where D-Man is standing with his hands on his hips, looking around at the crowd and nodding his head to their chants of "THUNDER". He is his usual attire of black tights with large golden "D"s on both front and back. He then sprints to ringside with the spotlight following him and jumps up on to the outside of the cage. He holds on with one hand as he now pumps his fist with the crowd's chants. He climbs down and enters the cage as the lights come back on.) DC: D-Man and Elimator go right for each other! Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: Grappling in the corner...D-Man swinging rights and lefts while Eliminator does the same thing! RR: These guys are full of hate for one another. DC: And Eliminator rams D-Man's head into the cage! Punches to the kidneys...and hooking him around the waist...BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX!!!!!!! BEAUTIFUL EXECUTION!!!!!!!!! RR: It was...for the Eliminator! Ha ha ha! DC: You've got to give credit where it's due Rex. RR: Sure...sure... DC: Eliminator now climbing to the top...and going up...D-Man is up...but Eliminator leaping to the top of the cage...and quickly jumping off the top with a corkscrew dive...CONNECTS!!!!!!!! (boos) RR: Holy Jumpingbombangels!!!!!!!!!! That's a 20 foot drop!!!!! DC: Close...not quite 20 feet. But the D-Man is down in the ring. And the Eliminator is rising. (crowd looks to the entry and boos as Junior leads Scott May, Lawrence Stevenson, and Mortikus to the ring) DC: Oh no...I thought we'd get this. RR: The Eliminator can't do anything without help. DC: I'm beginning to believe that. Eliminator now...pulling D-Man to his feet by the hair...throwing him hard into the metal bars!!!!!! RR: Oooh! DC: And D-Man falling to the canvass again as Horsemen members look on from the outside. Eliminator pulling D-Man to his feet again...and now sitting him on the top rope. RR: I know what he's going to do. DC: Eliminator climbing the top...standing behind him...and he's getting ready for that "Devestator" powerbomb off the top. But D-Man reaching behind...grabbing his head...MANHATTEN DROP OFF THE TOP ROPE!!!!!!!!!! (cheers) RR: Or otherwise known as the Stone Cold Stunner. DC: And really blowing a hole in Eliminator's plans. D-Man now crawling to the door...but Scott May just locked it!!! And D-Man is looking at May...but May now is smiling and showing the key to D-Man!!! RR: LOOK!!! It's D-Security out of the front row! I never even saw them sit down there! DC: Climbing over the railing...and approaching the Horsemen...the Horsemen backing down the aisle as D-Security members ask for the key! In the ring...Eliminator with a double eye gouge on D-Man...and now...big right hand. RR: D-Security is beating down the Horsemen! Ha ha ha! DC: And one of them with the key now...unlocking the cage door. RR: I get tired of this. Every cage match somebody locks the freakin door. DC: What's good for the geese is good for the gander I guess Rex. RR: You're starting to sound like Monsoon! Spare me the day when I'll have to go through that again. DC: D-Man now is up...and he's got Eliminator in the corner...now twisting the arm...short clothesline!!!!! (cheers) And now...down on his knees...ramming Eliminator's head into the steel...again and again and again and again!! (deafening cheers) RR: Enough! DC: The Horsemen back at ringside...D-Security on the other. As D-Man lifts Eliminator to his feet...sends him to the ropes...going back himself...DOUBLE CROSS BODY BLOCK!!!! BOTH MEN COLLIDE IN MID AIR!!!!!! (cheers) RR: WOAH! Incredible! DC: But Eliminator who landed on top...rising to his feet...but staggering into the corner. D-Man crawling to the bottom of the cage...and he's beginning to climb!!! RR: Come on! DC: Eliminator off the ropes...DROPKICKS D-MAN RIGHT INTO THE CAGE!!!!! (boos) And D-Man drops off the side to the mat! RR: Now Eliminator is trying it...to the opposite side...his Horsemen looking on! Eliminator to the top... DC: And D-Man now beginning to ascend again...Eliminator crawling over the top...and pausing as the D-Man is up the side of the cage like greased lightning!!!!!!! RR: He's gonna do it!!!!!!! DC: Eliminator now half way down the other side...AND D-MAN DIVES OFF THE TOP OF THE CAGE.......RIGHT ON TOP OF 2 of the 3 MEMBERS OF D-SECURITY!!!!!!! RR: WOAH!!! UNBELIEVABLE!!!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: D-Security collapsed beneath is weight...and the third member now is helping D-Man up who took the least of the pain. And now...another D-Security man rising...and there's the third...who is obviously limping. Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner...D-MAN!!!!!!!!!!!!! (crowd erupts) ("Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays) DC: D-Man turning to get a good look at the Eliminator...and now with a big grin on his face...celebrating with D-Security. Congratulations D-Man. RR: Look at this. Lawrence Stevenson and Junior trying to talk to Eliminator...but Eliminator pushing them away. He's not happy Crawford. DC: And I wouldn't be either. Both men out of here. Let's go back to ringside. ("The Mirror" by Dream Theater blasts through the P.A.) Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the following match is scheduled for one fall...approaching the ring area...from the Urbish Mines...6'11"...326lbs...accompanied by Donna Di Notte...he is REX OVERLORD!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (mixed reaction) DC: And here comes that madman...that psychotic turncout...Rex Overlord. RR: I don't think you're treating Overlord fairly Crawford. He said himself that he's always done what it takes to come out on top...and if you're in the way...friend or enemy...then he'll set you straight. You loved the guy when he was with the Syndicate. But now you act like he's somehow changed. DC: He has Rex. RR: You've changed Crawford. Let's go Rex! DC: Overlord now out of the locker room...black singlet...black boots and pads...wrist bands and elbow pads. Looking in top condition...as always. And the crowd doesn't quite know what to think of him. RR: Hehehe...if they're waiting for Overlord to make some sappy apology or something then they're stupid. DC: We shall see just what goes on as Overlord is in the ring...and Donna Di Notte at his side...in my book a turncoat as well... RR: Look at this! President Gordon is coming down! (cheers) DC: And what could he want? Still awaiting the presence of Gar Kryger. Gordon now in the ring and discussing something with Michael Cook as Overlord leans in the corner looking on. And now...he's leaving. RR: What's going on? Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the President has just informed me that "the Extreme Misfit" Gar Kryger is not in attendance here tonight. Therefore...Rex Overlord's opponant... (HBK's entrance theme plays as the crowd erupts) Michael Cook: From Malibu, California...standing at 6'4"...weighing 260lbs...accompanied to the ring by Sensational Sara....MAAAAAAAAAALIBU MIKE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (crowd cheers loudly) DC: IT'S MALIBU MIKE!!!!!!! RR: I CAN'T BELIEVE THIS!!!!! GORDON REALLY PULLED A SLICK ONE THIS TIME!!!!! DC: Here comes Mike...to the ring...looking straight ahead. And I've not seen Overlord this bewildered since the Godfather introduced Immorality as their mystery partner last WrestleFest!!! RR: Either way...Overlord out of the ring...and Mike charging him!!!! We've got a slugfest!!!!! (deafening cheers) DC: Overlord's blows a little bigger...knocking Mike back...and now throwing him into that guardrail!!!!! Overlord grabs Mike by the hair and throws him in the ring... RR: That's "Malibu" Mike Madison. DC: Yeah...so? RR: hehehe...goofy name Crawford. DC: No it's not. Malibu now getting up and recieving Overlord as he rolls in. Both men going toe to toe...both men going to the ropes...and colliding!!!!!!! But Mike goes down hard!!! (cheers) Overlord in the face of Mike...slapping his face and shouting obscenities!!!! RR: Ha ha ha! I think we've got a pro-Overlord crowd here! DC: Mike now is up...but Overlord meeting him with big forearms to the back...and hooking him into a front face lock now...but Mike picking him up...back suplex!!!!! (cheers) RR: Beautiful counter by Malibu. But it's going to take more than that to get the job done. DC: I must admit...I had not been looking forward to seeing Overlord in action against Gar Kryger. That's a gimme for Overlord I think. But Malibu Mike makes this so much better. RR: Yeah...but anybody is better than Kryger! DC: Malibu now lifting Overlord up...running powerslam!!!!!!! (cheers) 1 2 no! RR: You're getting good with that! DC: Knock it off Rex. Mike now grabbing a handful of hair on Overlord...lifting him to his feet...but hooking the head...suplex...but reversed by Overlord...suplex and Malibu hits hard!!!! (cheers) RR: The crowd can't make up it's mind! DC: Overlord up...BIG LEGDROP!!!! And a pin... 1 2 kickout. Not even close. RR: He got two out of three! DC: You really love this new Overlord don't you Rex? RR: I do. ha ha ha! DC: Don't count your chickens before they hatch Rex...he's been known to change. RR: Just let me live my own life Crawford! DC: Overlord now sitting on Mike's back...and he's got him in a camel clutch! I've never seen him do that move! RR: But a smart move. Malibu's ribs can't be 100% yet...and...oh...I thought I just heard one snap! Ha ha ha! DC: You're a sick man Rex. Mike trying to get to the ropes...but Overlord has it clamped on hard...but now releasing! RR: No! DC: Still sitting on Mike's back...pounding the back of his head with lefts and rights...and now pulling his hair back and raking him in the eyes! This guy is relentless! RR: Yes! Ha ha ha! DC: Off him now...up and kicking him hard in the ribs! What you said is true Rex. He's trying to hurt Mike's ribs. RR: Donna Di Notte is really enjoying this. That woman is such a beauty. I've never admitted that before. DC: She is...and I've said so many times before. But I've got to wonder what her frame of mind is. You talk about beauties...take a look at Sara. RR: Is that spittle that I see coming from the corners of your mouth Crawford? Quit lusting and call the match! DC: As you wish...Mike clutching his chest...and Overlord now with a knee drop...lifting him to his feet...BIG UPPERCUT TO THE THROAT!!!!!!! RR: And Mike falling back into the corner....Overlord is right on him...choking him out in the corner!!!! DC: Mike fighting it...Overlord holding Mike by the nose...big right hand into the teeth!!! RR: Woah! Ha ha ha! Just when Malibu gets over his lisp...looks like he's right back where he started! DC: But Mike...with a knee to the midsection of Overlord!!! (cheers) Overlord doubled over and moving away! And Mike...bleeding from the mouth...he hooks Overlord's head...jumping DDT!!!!! (cheers) And that one had to hurt! RR: Get up! Get up! DC: Mike now staggering...to his feet...and lifting Overlord with a hand full of hair...but Overlord with a big right hand to the gut doubles Mike over! And Overlord...hooking his head between his legs!!!! RR: We're going to see the SPINESLAYER!!!!!!!!!!!! DC: No! Mike raises up...picking Overlord up off his feet...Overlord slung over his shoulder as Mike holds his legs...still in powerbomb position...only Mike is on his feet! Mike...jumps and swings Overlord back over...hard onto his back!!! And for the pin... 1 2 3!!!!!!! (crowd pops) RR: I CAN'T BELIEVE IT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! DC: THE FIRST TIME THAT REX OVERLORD HAS BEEN PINNED FAIRLY TO THE MAT...SINCE HIS LOSS TO GARRET JAX OVER A YEAR AGO!!!!!!!!!!!! Michael Cook: Ladies and gentlemen...the winner....MAAAAAAAAAAALIBUUUUUUUUUUUUU MIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIKE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (deafening cheers) RR: No! No! No! DC: He's done it! Malibu in the corner with hands uplifted...receiving the praises of the crowd...as he has done something thought to be impossible here!!! RR: Overlord can't believe it! Look at the look on his face! DC: Beautiful maneuver by Malibu...I've not seen it since the Big Boss Man reversed a Jauques Rogeau piledriver at SummerSlam '91! Knocked the wind right out of Overlord...Mike is really having a great time here...leaving ringside with Sensational Sara. Happy Thanksgiving...nothing that I know could make it any better. A win like this...to me it would mean even more than winning the UWA heavyweight gold. RR: What!? Are you crazy? DC: Considering who has the belt now...no I don't think that I am. VVV is a disgrace! RR: I don't want to hear it Crawford! DC: Overlord now leaving the ring...and I've never seen such a look. Not angry...not upset...just surprised. And Donna Di Notte helping him out of here. Oh...what a matchup... Stay tuned for part 2...
