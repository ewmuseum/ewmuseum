Amonug some of the more noticable signs are, 'Suicide Rules The World' 'Rex is a wuss' and 'TNT for president'. The camera then pans toward a wrestling ring with red, white, and blue ropes surrounding only three sides of a gray canvass, leaving the front open to be viewed by the audience. Inside the ring are two, black leather chairs and a brown table with a wood-stained finish.) Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen. UWA fans of all ages. Welcome to the weekend edition of UWA Spotlight. (crpwd pop) And now, here are your two cohost, Rex Roseman and president T.N.T. (The crowd gives no reaction as Rex Roseman walks out but then begin to cheer loudly as T.N.T approaches the set. Rex is wearing jeans and a 'Rex Rules' shirt. He has spiked, red hair with a patch of gray on the side. T.N.T is wearing a navy, UWA issued suit with a olive colored shirt underneath. His long, brown hair is tied back neatly into a ponytail. Both men take their seats.) T.N.T: Thank you all very much, and as always, welcome to Weekend Spotlight. I'm T.N.T. and the runt sitting to my right goes by the name off Rex Roseman. Rex Roseman: Runt! HA! We will see who the runt is tomorrow night when you finally step into the squared circle for the first time since retirement and get the living crap beat out of you by the Monster. I can hardly wait. But I can't possibly see how someone as out of shape as you are can compete woth the likes of the Monster. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==- PROMO: International Title Match - Lord Zero(c) v. Crusher Stevenson ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==- T.N.T: I'm just going to say this about that match. Tune into the Main Event tomorrow night and you will see a very much, in shape T.N.T make short of the Monster. But enough about the shorter matches. One match that will defintly not be short will be between Lord Zero and Crusher Stevenson. Lord Zero has put the Internation title on the line against the number one contender Stevenson. This looks to be a match full of potential, as long as any outside interferrence can be kept under control. Rex Roseman: We all know that I can care less about Lord Zero and the Lefion of Darkness, but I really despise The Fearsome Foursome. And just who happens to be in the Fearsome Foursome..... Crusher Stevenson. And we all know that the entore Foursome can't wrestle if their life depended on it, so I am predicting Lord Zero to make short work of Crusher. T.N.T: Well, I'm sure that Crusher would disagree with you, lets hear what he has to say about the whole deal. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== Crusher Stevenson ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== (Noontime. It's Friday and the usual things that go on in Chicago, Illinois around this time can be heard throughout the city. Sirens going off, people talking about going to a Cubs game, and life in general. Walking through the streets of the rougher part of Chicago is Crusher Stevenson. He is wearing a forrest green Nike t-shirt, blue jeans, and a pair of dark brown Timberland boots. He is in good spirits as the camera moves in closer to him. He begins to speak as he is walking along.) Crusher Stevenson: Chicago, Illinois. I grew up in these streets. I wasn't a product of the crime and violence, I got away from that. My dream to become a wrestler started right here. (Points to a Gold's Gym. He opens the door and walks in. The man behind the desk looks up and smiles saying hello the Crusher. Some of the men and women training point and wave to him.) While all the other kids on the street were dealing drugs and stealing cars, I was working out here, getting away from the outside world. The thought never crossed my mind of becoming a wrestler. See that... (Points to a picture of Chicago legends, Hawk and Animal, The Road Warriors. In the picture they are standing beside a young, fairly muscular teenage boy) See that boy. That boy was me. I didn't have anybody to look up to. I barely knew my dad. Hawk and Animal were my role models. I looked up to them. They believed that I could become a wrestler.....and I did. (Waves goodbye to the folks at the gym and leaves. He starts walking again along the streets.) It wasn't easy at first though. Things weren't handed to me on a silver platter. I got beat around, I wasn't a wrestling sucess at first. But I kept on working harder and harder. I got right back up after I got knocked down. I overcomed all adversity and made it to the big time, the UWA. But even then I still had to work hard. I got roughed up quite a bit at the beginning. Didn't even win a match. Got knocked down again. But I got right back up. I worked harder and harder. That's gotten me to where I am today..... fighting for the UWA International Title. (Stops by a barber shop and points to a old barber in there. He smiles and gives him a hug. One of the men getting a haircut yells "Kick LordZero's ass Crusher!" Crusher laughs and waves goodbye and leaves the shop. He continues walking again.) LordZero, Sunday Night is drawing closer and closer. The dream to win gold in the UWA is drawing closer and closer. And the chance to prove all my critics one is drawing closer and closer. (Stops walking and the camera moves close to Crusher.) Sunday Night might be my only chance ever to win a title in the UWA. I'm not gonna risk anything to lose that chance. I am in the best shape of my life. I am more focused then I have EVER been before! And I know deep down inside me that I will become the NEW UWA International champion. (Gets a determined and angry look on his face) LordZero, get ready. I'm not Wrestlingman. I'm not Malibu Mike. I'm CRUSHER STEVENSON!!!! And I'm the man that is gonna take that title away from you. Get ready LordZero, the Crusher is coming. (Crusher walks away as the camera fades to black) ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== END: Crusher Stevenson flash ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== Rex Roseman: (sniffleing) Isn't that nice. While poor littler Crusher Stevenson was working his butt off in Gold's Gym and losing match after match, everyone was laughing and poking fun at him. That is just sooooo sad... But not for me. He will just come around full circle again come Sunday Night. And I will be there to witness his disapointment. T.N.T: You might be a witness tomorrow night Rex, but I doubt that you will be witnessing a Crusher defeat to the man who we are about to hear from now. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== Lord Zero ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== (Scene: The *original* Legion members are once again sitting in their living room. LordZero turns the TV on, and their local channel shows "UWA's Monday Spotlight". In addition to seeing their own faces, they see many others that talk about the Legion. After it's over, the Legion laughs. LordZero turns to the camera...) LordZero: Jeez, I'm happy I taped that show. There's so many wrestlers that are JEALOUS of us! (in an announcer's voice) "LET'S GO TO THE TAPE!" <<PLAY>> Crusher Stevenson: (Shaking his head) Well I beat Malibu Mike tonight. Now I get to face the Internation Champion Lord Zero. But for some reason I'm not happy. Not happy because a bunch of bat wielding thugs changed the look of the whole match. (Rubs his chin) Lord Zero, Legion of Darkness, LISTEN UP!!! You might have tried to take me out tonight, but it didn't work. I still made Malibu Mike submit. Now Lord Zero, your next. Bring all your baseball equipment to the match because your gonna need it. It's gonna take a army to stop me from taking that IC Title from you. And when I do win that IC Title, the first person that will get a shot is you Malibu Mike. Lord Zero.....Prepare to be CRUSHED!!! Big Butch Thornton: Lord Zero, I would suggest you tell all your thugs to keep away from Crusher's match against you. Just a fair warning for in advance. Judgement Day is Upon Us...... <<STOP>> LordZero: Crusher, let me explain something. Malibu Mike wanted to have the Legion come down to ringside to back him up in case your boys came down. We felt like he was depending on us to come down, so we did. AND WE KICKED HIS ASS! (laughs) Not only that, but since we don't like you either...we kicked your ass too! Granted, we got suspended, but you still got the win, didn't you? An army huh? Well, as I've stated TIME & TIME AGAIN, this belt is MINE! You won't get a shot at this belt until I get a match with Suicide. Speaking of being crushed...when I slap the SoulCrusher on you, that's what you'll be feeling! But wait...maybe I'll do something else! You never know...I'm a VERY surprising man! Big Butch, what can your little suck ass foursome do to us? Guess what? It's perfectly LEGAL for me to bring the Legion down...see anywhere that says we don't? Try it Butch, try picking a fight with us...cause in the end, your ass will be "crushed", just like your friends! <<PLAY>> Damien Dredd: The UWA... Where real men WRESTLE! And that's what REAL men do, BRAA! They don't need to bring bats or hockey sticks inna ring. They don't need to bring someone else to get the job done for them! (Clips of the LOD's attack during Saturday Night's Main Event projected on the background) Damien Dredd: These are the things that violate the purity of our sport, a Blasphemy in the Eye of Jah! And yet they do these things and only get a slap on the wrist... They won't learn until they step inna ring with someone like the Lion of Judah; but even so, there's a growing list of those with scores to settle with the likes of the LOD. The Will of Jah be done! <<STOP>> LordZero: Ooh! Hay Mon! Your ass must still be high on all that weed, cause you're hallucinating! You think you have a chance against US? Take a good look at us...Bad Habit, Hate, Anger, Wrestlingman, Fallen Angel, and myself. This is the DOMINANT force in the UWA my rasta friend! If you want a piece...bring your weed smoking ass to the ring! <<PLAY>> Bad News: Hay Duce!I was just wonderin what you think about the great champions {laughs} of the U.W.A. Duce: I'll tell ya what I think of um, they suck!Suicide is a punk who couldn't hold my jock and lord zero he sucks so bad he dont even matter.I tell ya what the Bad News I heard why he's called lord zero, because thats how many times his pencil been sharpend if ya know what i mean. <<STOP>> LordZero: Yeah dawg! Why don't you take yo punk azz to da crib and chill mofo! (laughs) Did I do that right? Now, Mr. Duce, you're right about Suicide. He is a punk, and he's gonna get what's coming to him soon enough. I have a question for you BRO...if I suck so bad, WHY DO I HAVE A BELT?!? Bring your sorry ass into the ring, and I'll kick your ass gangster style! As for my luck with the ladies, why don't you ask you mom and women about how many times my pencil's been sharpened? You suck, and you'll never amount to anything once I get you in the ring...BET ON IT! <<PLAY>> Bad News: So all you tag teams.... who are we kiddin' we talkin' to thoes faggs otherwise know as Hell's Angels, you better watch ya back because it mibht be me and Duce maby Duce and Hoodlum or hell it could be all us because the minute you turn ya grill away bam we all jackin' ya ass! <<STOP>> Hate: These guys MUST be joking! They want to tell US about sneak attacking people?!? Thug Life...the Legion is the ULTIMATE dirty playing stable! If you want to make threats, bring your asses into the ring! Anger: You threaten us? Well, just think about this...if you attack us...you start a war beyond any that you have ever imagined. With this troop of 6 HARDCORE individuals, you would be up to more than you could handle. That's all we have to say to you guys! <<PLAY>> MR TANAKA: Here i have a contract for the Legion of Darkness to sign if they will accept the Eliminator. LOD, we will make it worth your while cause the Eliminator is one bad s o b. <<STOP>> LordZero: Eliminator...you need to realize something. You don't choose the Legion...the Legion chooses you! You could be the baddest SOB that has ever walked this earth, but if you're not chosen, you're not a member. However, we take this offer as a compliment. I will consider your offer. For now, concentrate on wrestling, and we'll be in contact as soon as we reach a decision! Now, UWA, since we're still suspended, we don't wanna talk anymore! So, until next week... IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (screen fades) ==--==--==--==--==--==--== END: Lord Zero Flash ==--==--==--==--==--==--== T.N.T: Some strong words from Lord Zero and the Legion. They must be declaring war on the entire UWA. That's not to smart if you'd ask me. Rex Roseman: Well, No on did ask you. But what the Legion lacks in intelegence, they make up by just plain a-- kickings. T.N.T: Anyway you look at it, it's sure to be an exciting match on an exciting card. In addition to an international title match and T.N.T taking on the Monster, we will also have a four man tag match between The Fearsome Foursome and Chad "The Specimen" Blakes team of four. Just who is amoung this "elite" team? Lets go over to Mike Miller and see if he can clear things up for us. ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== News And Rumors w/ Mike Miller ==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--==--== Mike Miller: Thanks Mr. President. According to Blake, applications hace be comming in left and right and his decision was a very tough one. He assures me that only the finest athlets worth 50,000$ will be accepted. Switching subject now, on to the match involving T.N.T and the Monster. My sources have assured me that T.N.T has not even lifted a finger to train for this match, and he is really confident that he can defeat the Monster with ease. Rumor has it, that he know something that we don't know, maybe he can explain exactly what this means to us? On another note, a world reknown superstar is expected to make an appearence on tommorows Main Event. I haven't heard any wrestler names, but the IIWF might have something to do with it. ANd speaking of appearences, Summer Showdown, which will be taking place in two weeks, is expected to be a show full of surprises as a lot of new AND old superstars may be making their debuts. My time is up, I will try to have more detailed info on these stories next week, T.N.T, Rex, back to you. < continued on part 2 >
