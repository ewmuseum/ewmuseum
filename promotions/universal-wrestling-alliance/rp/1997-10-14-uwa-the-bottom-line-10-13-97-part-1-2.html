Chambers: (pausing the film at Stephenson's each move) Pathetic...Pathetic...and nothing other than pathetic...so pathetic that it's almost as pathetic at the amount of money that Castor Troy has in his wallet!!!...Nada!!!! So what's gonna be your BIG surprise that's gonna shock the wrestling world Stephenson??? It certainly can't be bigger than that oversized A** ya' got!!! (Chambers chuckles) Now, I ain't exactly sure about one thing...what makes ya' think that you're the best wrestler on the planet??? Your mom givin' ya' applause as you battle for your life in the squared circles in the back row???? (all laugh hysterically) I'm gonna give ya' the lesson of your life so bad that your decendants millions of years from now are gonna feel the wrath!!!! (Camera crew can't help but chuckle) Chambers: This is "Mr. Money" signin' off!!!!! M-R. M-O-N-E-Y!!!!! Tambo: Well, A/V Boy, welcome to your first new interview with a member of the elite fighting force known as Solid Gold. I have a few things I'd like to clear up. First off, BrotherHood, don't stay in hiding too long. We wanna wipe you off the mat as soon as possible. So, lets show some spirit, boys. Second of all, Wrestlingman, learn to read...at the PPV, I will defend my title against the #1 Contender in a match of MY choice. Point A: You are NOT the #1 Contender for my title. In fact, you smell like #2! Point B: What is a Taipei Glove?? Is that what you wear while trying to match up two identical Chinese symbols? A/V Boy: Wow, a joke. Tambo: Leave Wrestlingman alone, A/B Puke, I will be the one to bring him farther down on the totem pole...IF he becomes the #1 contender. You have less than a month to try to ascend to that level. Third point of order is Malibu Man. You just don't know when to quit, and that's alright. It's ok to get your butt kicked as a hobby. You probably already know that though. I mean, when you burn the fries at Mickey D's, does the paper hat manager yell at you? Or do you tell him, "I'm Malibu Mike and..". You see what I'm getting at.
