It's gonna be the night the Forces of Orient of explode in the UWA! Joseph: That is, if we have opponents...in case you didn't notice, it's still an open contract... Jeffrey: So? Somehow someone will have to put their names down and fight us! Joseph: Wonder who... I don't think the tag team champions will be facing us, considering the fact that we're a new tag team... Jeffrey: So it will be one of the other tops teams in the UWA... and they will see that the Forces of Orient definitely deserve to be known as one of the top tag teams in the UWA! Joseph: Sooner or later, the Forces of Orient will climb to the top and then the whole world will know that the East has risen! Jeffrey: And that'll be so cool! (Picture fades to black) __________________________________________________ Fearsome Foursome About: VVV, Rex Overlord, "Devastating" Danny Daravo, Sabbath, Eric Valentine (Scene opens inside a local sports bar in downtown Chicago, Illinois. Sitting down at the bar and watching the Monday Night Football game between the Vikings and the Packers are Big Butch Thornton, Crusher Stevenson, and "Superstar" Scott Storm. Butch is intently watching the game and wearing a grey Detroit Lions sweatshirt, blue jeans, and a pair of black boots. Sitting to his right talking to the bartender is Crusher Stevenson. He is wearing his Chicago Bears #51 long sleeved vintage jersey of Dick Butkus, blue jeans, and a pair of brown Timberland boots. "Superstar" Scott Storm is sitting on Crusher's right and is flirting with a cute brown haired waitress. He is wearing a 2 button navy short sleeved shirt, tan docker khakis, and a pair of brown loafers. Butch turns away from the game and looks toward the camera as he begins to be speak.) Big Butch Thornton: Some things are not meant to be. William Jennings Bryan winning the presidency, Susan Lucci winning a Daytime Emmy, the Buaffalo Bills winning a Super Bowl; all our things that come to mind. It's sad but true that when mentioning that subject the thought of Rex Overlord never getting to exact his revenge on VVV comes to mind. And slowly but surely it looks like I am being added to that equation. [Chuckles as the Vikings turn the ball over] VVV, since our path's have crossed, you have been nothing more then a thorn in my side, sinking to new depths time and time again to gain the upperhand. But people were saying after Sunday Night "Well Butch, you won by DQ!". F**K DQ's!!! (Rubs his beard) I don't bust my *ss 365 days a year to win by a DQ. A win and _ONLY_ a win will satisfy my competitve hunger. Rex Overlord, we were played like a fiddle at the hands of Vile Vince Viper and his minions and I take part of the responsiblity for that happening. But what's in the past is in the past. Me and you, side by side must concentrate on taking down the menace known as Vile Vince Viper and if that means taking him down with me, then so be it. (Breathes hard then gives a angry look towards the camera) "Devastating" Danny Daravo, now is not the time to cross paths with the "One Man Wrecking Crew". I have other things on my mind but for one night I will set them aside and concentrate on shoving you back in the hole where you came from. I could care less about the rubbish that will come out of your mouth about are match because the fact of the matter is once the opening bell rings, all the words that were said are meaningless. Remember this Daravo, there will be no place to hide once it is time for hell to break less. Not only you should heed those words but also you Vile Vince Viper. Judgement Day Is Upon Us....... (Butch goes back to watching the game as Crusher turns around and faces the camera) Crusher Stevenson: At Friday Night Fights, me and "Superstar" Scott Storm are going into the ring with 2 up and coming talents in the UWA...Sabbath and Eric Valentine. First of all Sabbath, I respect alot of the things you have done in the UWA thus far. From taking on VVV's minions head on to just signing on the dotted line with me and Scott shows the fighting spirit you have. This will be a battle of 4 great atheletes in 1 ring together and may the best men one and hopefully that will be us. (Crusher nudges Scott Storm who is flirting with another woman, this one a pretty blond at the bar.) "Superstar" Scott Storm: Oh sorry, didn't think we were up yet. [Snickers] Seriously, every match I fight now, I am thinking of Buzz Craven in the back of my mind and whether or not he will come back from his career threatening injuries. It won't be any different Friday Night. Sabbath, Eric Valentine, I am taking nothing away from you when I say this but me and Crusher _WILL_ win on Friday. When I walk down that aisle on Friday I might not be apart of the greatest tag team that ever lived in the Devastation Crew but I am sure me and Crusher will make a damn good team. We'll find out Friday if that last statment comes true. You can bank on it! Through the windows is darkness...but inside...Donna Di Notte sits in silence on the hearth before the large fireplace...a fire raging within. Her face is not visible...she stares ahead. Camera moving through the kitchen...opening a creaking door...a staircase. Down the staircase into darkness...we see a dark corridor...the sound of dripping water can be heard. Moving down the corridor...toward the opposite wall...and on that wall...on the floor...no...it couldn't be...) Voice: Better to knock friend... (the cameraman is startled...the camera wobbles and turns where Rex Overlord...author of the voice stands closely behind) Overlord: Always better to knock... Cameraman: Sorry sir...I just... Overlord: Nonsense. I caught you at this once before. A third time would be a great mistake on your part. Cameraman: Yes sir.. Overlord: You want a word. And that I can give you. "Stalker" Toni Gills. You've turned from your wicket ways...seen the light and stepped back onto the straight and narrow. Congratulations. But if you think that your change of heart will bear any significance with our match then you're sadly mistaken. I've beaten you once before...and again you will feel the wrath...and then your stubbornness must me satisfied. Now...on to other things. Johnny Tambo, you seem very comfortable in your position at this time. International champion...in the UWA heavyweight title match at Chaos. But my friend, there is a strong IF involved. You sit back and smile about your title shot...but it only happens IF you are the #1 contender. But by that time I intend to replace you as #1 contender. And it will be me walking out of the cage the Universal Wrestling Alliance's heavyweight champion of the world. And Vile Vince Viper...I beg you to change your plans. I beg to you step inside the cage. TNT is your goal...but you're looking at the man that pinned TNT's shoulders to the mat at WrestleFest '96 and ended his one true title reign. But is it fear the leads you away from the cage. Fear of me...fear of Thornton...fear of fate. Butch Thornton...not even you will be able to stop me in my quest for gold. Though we have become trusted allies...and you have a great desire for the gold yourself. It will be me that emerges from the cage with hands raised in victory...and it will be me that leaves with the UWA heavyweight title. So sayeth the master...and destroyer of worlds. Now get out.
