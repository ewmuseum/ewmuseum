UWA (1996) — Roleplays

UWALive in New York

7 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live In New York - Episode 11 *A siren "whirrrs" as "War Pigs" by Black Sabbath plays. Welcome to New York! The Big Apple! The City that Never Sleeps! Fade in to a glamourous helicopter shot of the sprawling metropolis, home of the Mets, the Knicks, and the Jets! But who cares about that? Because tonight...the UWA is...* Voiceover: LIVE IN NEW YORK!!!! *Cut to a shot right outside UWA studios, where a momentous event is to occur. The last few tickets are being snatched up by a few lucky fans wearing a mosaic of UWA merchandise! Malibu Mike t-shirts! Rex Overlord jerseys! Flaming Bob jockstrap! (NOTE: Flaming Bob jockstrap not available in UWA catalog. Obviously some looney made it himself!) But most noticable amongst the teeming throng is appearal supporting tonight's guest.* *Cut to inside shot of the "Live in New York" studio as the music fades, where Ron Donovan and Johnathon Tell await at their respective positions for the man who everyone is waiting for to make his entrance.* Ron Donovan: Welcome, everyone, to UWA's Live in New York!!! We've got a great show on hand for you tonight, with two very special guests. They're newcomers to the UWA scene, but have already made a noticable impact! Jon Tell: Well, that's your opinion! Personally, we could've used a more well established superstar like our last guest, "Hollywood" Johnny Tambo...maybe Flaming Bob or one of Natural Currancy. RD: Please! Anything but one of Triple V's boys. Perhaps tonight will be less chaotic than his appearance on the show two weeks back... *Cut to clips of VVV taking over, mooning the crowd.* JT: He scares me y'know? RD: Me as well! But it seems our guest isn't afraid of the Big Bad Viper, as he recently defended Big Butch Thornten against Hell on Earth with the help of Rex Overlord. So, without any further ado, let's bring him out...he is the self professed "Walking Contradiction", the Man in Black! Here is, accompanied by his lovely manager *AND* wife, Sayaka... *"Horror Business" by the Misfits plays and the audience cheers. He steps from behind the curtain, dressed in his classic attire: black jeans, black boots, black "Ebola Zaire" t-shirt, long black leather trench coat, and mirrored "John Lennon" sunglasses. Sayaka follows, casually dressed, with her beautiful waist long hair trailing behind her slim figure.* RD: SAAAAAAAAABBAAAAAATH!!! *Cheers!!! Sabbath waves to the crowd, and shakes hands with a few fans sitting in the front row. He makes his way over to the guest's couch, where he sits with his arm around lovely Sayaka.* RD: Welcome Sabb... *Sabbath holds his hand up.* Sabbath: Please, call me Micheal. RD: Okay, welcome to Live in New York Micheal, Sayaka. We're pleased to have you here aren't we Johnathon? JT: Yeah, sure...glad to have ya. So Sayaka...seen Bob lately? RD: John! *Sabbath stands upright, obviously angered at the comments made by Tell. Sayaka blushes, embarassed.* RD: Whoa! Easy big guy...I'm just kidding. Simmer down...no harm done! Sabbath: Damn straight. And if you know what's good for you, you won't ask questions like that again? (coughs, in a more respectable tone of voice) Now, shall we begin? RD: Sure...okay, first tell us how you got into this sport? I mean, up until you first joined the UWA, I'd never even heard of you? Sabbath: Well, Ron, my first wrestling experience was with a promotion called the "UFWA", based out of Canada, headed up by Micheal Hames. I never got the chance to wrestle at first, as the UFWA collapsed under financial debt. Andy Little took it over, renaming it the UCWF, and that's where my first matches took place. *Clip of Sabbath making the legendary Tommy Nightmare submit.* Sabbath: That was my first major victory, over a man who went for 7 and a half hours with another legend, Assaf Levey. But anyway, the UFWA recieved money from an unknown source, and was restarted under the leadership of Micheal Hames and Bart Brewer. However, it has once again went under when Vice President Brewer stole some forty MILLION dollars from the UFWA reserves, thus causing President Hames to file for bankruptcy. But now... *Sabbath pauses for dramatic effect.* Sabbath: I'm where the REAL action is...THE UWA!!! *Garners a modest response from the crowd.* JT: Well, that's just all nice isn't it. I'll admit though, you have been quite impressive during your tenure here, with victories over Mage, the Thugs for Life and Ice-Blade. RD: You've seemed unstoppable, with the exception of the match with Ice-Blade. Sabbath: My luck with big men has never been, shall we say, exceptional. I don't know why. But hey, I beat him, and that's what counts. Sayaka: Yes, and we will continue this winning streak, first by teaming with the outstanding, but severely underrated Eric Valentine, then going after John Tambo, and finally...at Christmas Chaos... *She can barely say the name of her beloved husband's hated rival for her affections.* Sayaka: Flaming Bob. Sabbath: Speaking of Flaming Bob, how did it feel to be on the receiving end of the Walking Contradiction? You, Kami Kaze, Triple V, or whoever wants to get in my way will suffer the consequences, needless to say. Big Butch Thornten, Rex Overlord, know this: I'll watch your back against Hell on Earth if all possible. *Sabbath chuckles.* Sabbath: And Bobby Boy? Come Christmas Chaos...well, let's just say that there will be a "requiem" written, and it won't be for yours truly! Sayaka: Mike Madison, thank you for helping me in my situation with Bob at the Pay Per View. Who knows what he would have done to me if you hadn't shown when you did? If I or my husband can help you in any way, please don't hesitate to let us know. *Sabbath smiles and nods at the camera, then removes his trenchcoat, placing it over the back of the sofa.* Sabbath: Mike, nice name by the way (grin), she's right. We don't know you, but you were a gentlemen, no matter what your motives were. RD: Well...now that we've reached the subject of Flaming Bob. We have on closed circuit...Flaming Bob. *Jumbotron revolves on the back wall behind the studio audience and Flaming Bob's face can be seen.* Sabbath: What the hell? RD: Bob...welcome to Live In New York. Bob: Many thanks. RD: And you know Sabbath. Bob: Yeah...nice seeing you again Sabbath. Your wife is looking delicious today. Sabbath: Bite me, fanboy. Bob: Hee hee! JT: The first time face to face ever for you two I guess. And it won't be the last, as the two of you will be going head on at Christmas Chaos. What are your thoughts on that Bob? Bob: Well I've been doing a lot of thinking on that very issue. Now Mike...we're reasonable men...and we've been known to take risks in our careers. Am I right? Sabbath: Risks? Yes. Reasonable? No, not you at least Bobsy. Bob: Christmas Chaos: Rage In the Cage...Flaming Bob vs. Sabbath. Sounds so bland. So what I was thinking was adding a special stipulation to maybe spice things up. RD: Oh no. Sabbath: Stipulation? (Raises an eyebrow quizzically) Bob: Yeah...you put the love of your life on the line. (boos) Sabbath: Hmmmmmmmmmm...let me think about that...ahh..no. Reason? One, this ain't the WWF. Two, she's my wife. Three, I've given you a stipulation to spice things up...Singapore Slaughter. Take it or leave it. Bob: Very well...to your stipulation I say no. Put Sayaka up...I'll beat you with a Singapore Cane to your heart's content. Otherwise...no. Understand Sabbath that I don't care about you. I don't care about your dead daddy. Whether you live a thousand years eating oysters or die cleaning toilet bowls...it makes no difference to me. My sole purpose is to get my hands on your little angel...and that I will do. So let me make this very clear to you...I WILL HAVE YOUR WIFE. Sabbath: Your deal. Thing is, you ain't gettin' my wife. Because, effective IMMEDIATELY, she will not be at ringside...not even in the same town...that you are. *All look at Sabbath with shock.* Sabbath: You heard me. She's my all...and I won't risk her. Bob: *grins* If you think that... *Sabbath reaches behind his chair, producing a laptop.* Bob: Whatcha doin' Mikey? Sabbath: This conversation... *Sabbath hits the 'Enter' key, and the signal is cut, causing the Jumbo Screen to go dark.* Sabbath: Is over. RD: How'd you do that? Sabbath: Simple matter. I was tipped off that he would be on here tonight, so I just...well...ripped off the passwords for the Windows NT network that runs here, and it was easy from there on to get a route to the live feed, which I could cut at my will. JT: Interesting. Well, what else is on the agenda for tonight? Ah, yes...Micheal...you've proven to be an avid fan of "hardcore" rock 'n' roll. Just out of curiosity, what got you into this genre? I'm sorry that this question isn't pertinent to the UWA, but I'm just curious, as many wrestlers tend to stay away from hardcore. Sabbath: I dunno...it's just what I like. But at the same time, I love classical, jazz, and especially the blues. The blues are so soulful! Especially old Mississippi Delta slide blues. I'm not sure if you're familiar with Robert Johnson? RD: The name sounds familiar. Sabbath: Well, he's my favorite bluesman. There's a legend that he went to a crossroads outside of Yazoo City, MS, and sold his soul to the devil for his talent...died at the age of twenty-seven. Ironic eh? Kurt Cobain, Jim Morrison, Jimi Hendrix, Johnson, and Janis Joplin have all died at age 27. Coincidence? I think not...but enough on that. RD: What's the deal with your fascination the occult? It says here you have a library of books relating to supernatural occurances. Sabbath: Again, it's just what I like. I'm a rich man, as I've inherited money from my late father... JT: Sorry to interrupt, but I'd like to talk about your father in a little while if that's okay. Sabbath: No probs. Anywoo, I've had a lot of time on my hands from the time I came to America again until I began wrestling. During that five year period, I just went on a learning splurge. I taught myself the UNIX operating system inside and out, as well as the ESS phone system. I taught myself to read music and play guitar, piano, drums and bass, I've learned several languages...ranging from Hokkian, the primary dialect spoken in Singapore, to Latin. And I also learned about religion. It's a fascination of mine...especially so-called "darker" faiths like Wicca. JT: Interesting. RD: Alrighty, tell us about dad. You've mentioned him in passing, but we don't know the full story. Sayaka: I'll field this one, if I may, as Micheal was very young at the time, and it gets difficult for him to talk about this. *Sayaka clears her throat. She puts her hand on his, looking into his eyes. He sighs, and nods.* Sayaka: When he was three, his family made a trip to Singapore for holiday...they were quite wealthy. While walking through a bad side of town, they were accosted by two men...and shot. Right in front of his eyes. He just sort of wandered around for a few days, in a daze...until I found him when I was out playing. He looked like he hadn't eaten in a few days. We couldn't really understand one another as he only spoke English, and I was only learning the language...my parents were strict on education, even though they were poor. Still, they took him in, and we grew up together...I was six...he was three. Anyway, one thing they did teach us was how to fight, as we lived in the slums. My father, however, eventually lost his meager job and we were forced to either steal, or go hungry...my parents weren't in well enough health to steal, so that was up to Micheal and I. This was in our teen years...when we first started feeling an attraction for one another. We were never caught, luckily. At age...fifteen? sixteen?...we took up bloodsport...and we were a moderate success. We won enough money for my aging parents to live on. Then, one day...a man by the name of Tsutomo Kamiari approached us. He hired us for his organization...an assassins guild. JT: Waitasec...Tsutomo Kamiari? The wealthy businessman? *HE* hired you to be a paid killer? Sayaka: The one and the same. It's not well known of the travesties he's caused over here in American, but in the orient he's infamous. His head assassin...known only as "Antiseen" (OOC: This is important here. Keep this in mind for the future.) trained us for induction into the "Black Rose"...but on our first mission, Micheal took the approximately one million US dollars offered us by Kamiari to join him and we fled the country...we're not murderers. To this date, Kamiari hasn't caught up to us, as even entering the States would be dangerous...he has a lot of enemies over here. And that's our story...by the way...at the time, my parents had eventually died, so they can rest in peace without threat from the Black Rose. *Camera turns to Sabbath, who's crying, crystal tears running down his cheeks. Sayaka tries to comfort him and the camera goes back to Tell and Donovan.* RD: Micheal?...Micheal?...are you okay...let's take a break. ****************************Commercials****************************** *Commercial for "The Best of 'Welcome Back Kotter'" video collection.* *Commercial for UWA Christmas Chaos.* *Commercial for Happy Fun Ball! Yay! *Commercial for a ladies' hemorroid cream, by Vagisil.* *Commercial for The Body Condoms/Murder Junkies show at the A7 Club.* ********************************************************************* *Open back up to LiNY, where Sabbath looks much better, some audience members still look choked up though.* JT: We're back and everything seems peachy keen with our guests. For those of you just joining us, we're with Sabbath and Sayaka! RD: Micheal, how are you? Sabbath: I'm fine now...I'm sorry for that. It's just... RD: No problem. I understand. Think you can continue? Sabbath: Sure...I think so. We'll just keep away from that topic from now on. JT: Sure thing. Sorry for bringing it up. Sabbath: No biggie...it helps to get that stuff out. RD: So tell us now, about "Ebola Zaire." You wear that...I dunno... slogan on all your shirts. What does it mean? I know it's a virus and all. Sabbath: There's two reasons Ron. Number one, being I have an interest in virology, and as soon as I get some time off, I'll be taking a course on the subject at the University of Cambridge. Such "killer virii" usually send the public into a panic. Take AIDS for example. But Ebola makes AIDS look like a common cold...people should be more aware that there's something out there that could literally wipe the planet out. JT: So a lot of what you do is...well...I guess, social commentary? Sabbath: Exactly. JT: And the second reason? Sabbath: There's a comic book I like called "Poison Elves." One charcter from that book had a shirt like my Ebola Zaire one. So, I wanted one. (grins.) Sayaka: He gets so carried away at times. *smiles sweetly.* Sabbath: Like when I met you. (Crowd "Awwwwwws.") RD: Well, folks, it's time we wrapped this up. Any last comments Sabbath? Sabbath: Bob...you'll never get your hands on my wife. NEVER. JT: Bold words. Bold words. Sabbath: Bold words my [BEEP!]...that's it! *Sabbath gets up from his seat, and goes over to Tell.* JT: Now Sabbath...c-calm down... RD: We've gotta go...I have to make sure my collegue doesn't die here! For Johnathon Tell, Sayaka, and Sabbath, I'm Ron Donovan, and this has been UWA LIVE IN NEW YORK!!! *Fade to dark as we see Sabbath chasing Tell off the set.*
