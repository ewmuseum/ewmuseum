Stein | ===================================================================== (Scene opens inside the lounge of the Fearsome Foursome Training Facility in Chicago, Illinois. Playing some pool is Big Butch Thornton. He is wearing a grey UWA "Summer Showdown" t-shirt, blue jeans, and a pair of black boots. All the way on the other side of the lounge watching TV our "Superstar" Scott Storm and Buzz Craven. Scott is sitting on the couch wearing a light green plain t-shirt, black shorts, and a pair of brown loafers. Doing pushups on the floor is Buzz Craven. He is wearing a black Nike University of Iowa t-shirt and gold shorts with a Iowa logo on the left leg. The News is on TV.) "Superstar" Scott Storm: Hey Butch, look at this on TV. (Butch puts down the pool stick on the pool table and walks over to the TV.) (The camera zooms into the TV as the local news is on. Tom Myers is the news anchor.) Tom Myers: In other news, UWA Superstar and Chicago Native Crusher Stevenson has been missing for the past 72 hours. He was last seen leaving the Greensboro Colisseum in Greensboro, NC Tuesday Night after his match on the Tuesday Night War card and was said to be very upset when seen. Sources say he was supposed to arrive in Chicago the following day for a autograph session but never showed up. Friends don't have any idea where he is right now so if you have seen this man... (Crusher's picture flashes on the screen) Please call the Chicago Police Dept. or the Greensboro Police Dept. for information. (Camera pans back to Scott, Buzz, and Butch) Big Butch Thornton: Hopefully some people will see him. I knew he was really upset after the match Tuesday when I talked to him backstage breifly but didn't realize he was this upset. I've known Crusher for awhile, he always calls. I don't like this one bit... (Buzz stops doing pushups and sits on his knees) Buzz Craven: He'll be back, just needs to let some steam off? Big Butch Thornton: The question is when will he be back though? Buzz Craven: The only person that knows that is Crusher himself. (Buzz goes back to doing pushups) "Superstar" Scott Storm: Guys, I know Crusher is on our minds right now but we have to focus on what is going on in the UWA. If we don't, everything we have worked so hard to earn will be down the drain like Marv Albert's career!! Big Butch Thornton: Hehehe. Scott's right, we have to move on for the time being. If Crusher wants to come back he will. But I have to worry about the UWA right now. And first on my agenda is Tomahawk and his litle mystery man..... (Swipes the hair out of his hace) Tomahawk, it doesn't matter to me one F'N bit who you grab as your partner. It could be a pal back at the reservation, a wrestler in the UWA, a newcomer, Steve Urkel, I DON'T GIVE A DAMN!!! All I care about is 2 things on Sunday....A- You and your little buddy show up and B- You 2 are prepared for a Royal A**kicking!!! Tommyboy, if you watched last Sunday Spotlight, I hope you saw what I am capable when I set my mind to something. I'm gonna be setting my mind to something else the Sunday as well, and that is seeing you are your partners bloddy carcasses lying on the mat after getting beating from pillar to post by UWA Heavyweight Champion, Big Butch Thorton and Rex Overlord. Speaking of Rex... (Lowers his voice) Rex, your a man that I respect alot. But personally I don't care for you bringing the movie trilogy "The Godfather" to the UWA. At Sunday Spotlight Rex, me and you team up for the first time. I'm anticipating alot of unexpected suprises during the match, one by a guy who I am gonna address in just a second. Whatever takes place, it won't change the result of the match though. We are teaming up for one reason and one reason only...TO WIN!!! And win we will. Just make sure your associates stay out of the match..... (Rubs his hands together) Vile Vince Viper, you didn't even need to waste your time coming out after my match with Mage to ask for a shot. I have said this time and time again. I'm not a man to back down from challenges. You want a match?? All you have to do is ask!!! (Picks up a piece of paper) Now VVV, looking at the schedule of UWA events, there isn't a opening on a card till the last Friday Night Fight of the month, a few days after the PPV. And that date is October 31st, Halloween Night. Is there any better night then that night for me and you to finally lock up in the Center of the ring?? This wouldn't be no ordinary match either, a match picked out by me that would be the only suitable match to determine who is the most "Hardcore" of the 2 of us. Think about it VVV, and you too Gordon. I know PPV is where the money is but this _CANNOT_ wait!!!! (Scott Storm gets up) "Superstar" Scott Storm: Man oh man. The offers are coming in by the bushel to fight the hottest ticket in the wrestling business today..."Superstar" Scott Storm!! First that West Coast Gangster.. (Butch snickers) Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals wants me in the ring, and now that wannabe Extreme Icon the Sandman is asking the take me on. Big Butch Thornton: Are the Brotherhood gonna have anybody left after I take care of Elimanator and you take out Sandman?? "Superstar" Scott Storm: Yep, the Brotherhood isn't gonna be looking so hot in a few weeks. Sandman, I don't know who you are trying to be but you aren't Scott Storm. That's all you need to know. People don't come to watch you and your buddies, they come to see the "Future of Pro Wrestling" Scott Storm. Sandman, anytime anyplace my man. And Johnny Coals my brother. A match between me and you would be tight. The response after that match would be PHAT! (Scott starts to laugh) Please. Take your ebonics speaking carcass out of my sight. Fighting you and the Sandman will be nice warmups for when me and Buzz start to show the whole UWA what being the Best Tag Team In The World is all about!!! (Buzz stops doing pushups and stands up.) Buzz Craven: Frank N. Stein, on the 26th of October me and you fight in a Acid Spill match. I have no idea what that type of match is not do I care what it is. All I care about is ripping you apart limb by limb for what you did to me. At Hellshock, the Buzzsaw is gonna take care of business, and that means putting Frank N. Stein out of comission!! (Scene fades to black.) ================= UWA =================== | Sabbath | | The "Walking Contradiction" is here!!! | ========================================= *Scene opens in a recreation park. Small children are running amok, playing carelessly in the cool autumn breeze. Everything is peaceful, nothing could go wrong!* Voice: In a perfect world...this would be possible. *Scene fades to a home, at Christmas time. A mosaic of wrapping papered presents is under the tree, beckoning. Lying by the fireplace are two children, weary and groggy, but still wanting to wait up for Santa.* Voice: In a perfect world...all children would have Christmas. *Scene fades again to a party of teenagers of all races, laughing, enjoying life. It seems that no alcohol or drugs are present. It's just people having a good time, without violence.* Voice: In a perfect world...everyone would love one another. *Scene fades to a slum, probably in a third world country. Children, their bones showing through their tender flesh, stare hazily at the camera with wide eyes which are almost popping out of their shrunken heads.* Voice: However... *Darkness, then we open to a castle top. About fifteen feet away, a man about 6'6" perches on the edge of a battlement, gazing off into the distance wistfully. Beside him, on the more stable roof, an oriental beauty has her arms around him.* Voice: This isn't a perfect world. I should know...I've experienced all the rot, all the scum, all the filth...that society can dredge up, and I've lived. I've fought for my very survival for twenty years. *He takes notice of the camera, pauses in thought for a moment, then speaks again.* Voice: My apologies...I havne't introduced myself. My name is Micheal Price, but I'm known to the world as Sabbath. This lovely creature... *He points to the lady to his right.* Sabbath: Is my one true...Sayaka. Isn't she lovely? *He smiles at her proudly.* Sabbath: Well UWA...here we are. Now...what was I talking about? Ah yes...the scum of society. I've been on both sides of the fence, you see? I'm not particularly proud of my past, but I admit I can't change it. Anyway...I forgot what my point was. Oh well... Sayaka: It doesn't really matter. What does matter, is that we're in the UWA now. And, frankly...that's bad news for those of you here who wish to stop our onslaught. Sabbath: We'll be honest though, we know very little about you. That will change soon. We aren't stupid...we're not going to come in here and challenge just anyone. Oh no. I have the rankings right here. I'm going to start at the *VERY* bottom. Mage...I don't know you. I won't pretend to know yo . I won't take you lightly though. You seem to be a very able competitor; you wouldn't be here if you weren't. Now, don't think I'm running smack on you, I'm not. I have a lot of respect for you and I have nothing against you. I just have to start somewhere...but I'll be nice and let you choose the stipulations, if any. Sayaka: We've made the mistake before of going after the big dogs before we learned the ropes...we won't do so again. My love is still relatively a rookie, as he's only been wrestling for seven months. He has, however, been fighting since the age of five. Sabbath: It's still no reason to get cocky. Mage could surprise me, and I'm sure he will. I'm not going to say I'll win every match. Only a fool would do so. And a fool... *The camera zooms in and we see his iceblue eyes and evil grin* Sabbath: ...I am not. *He hops off the battlement to the roof, and as the camera goes dark, we hear his evil laughter.* *"Black Sabbath" by Black Sabbath begins to play and a bleeding Celtic cross fades in.* ========= UWA ========= | Wrestlingman & Magma | | About: The UWA | ======================= (Seen Opens to Wrestlingman and Magma in a small room) Wrestlingman: You see daniels, i told you that you need to be taught a lesson. I told you i would teach you that lesson. What happend? Well i kick your butt so hard you were not able to get back into the ring. See Daniels, the only thing i want to get across to you is that you do not mess with one of the best here in the UWA. YOu do not just come in and demand title shots and say you are the best. YOu must earn it. On to other events lastnight. I heard something too framilliar. Way to familliar. I heard a phrase spoken lastnight that i thought i would never have to hear again. I heard the phrase "IT'S AN ALL OUT WAR" That phrase changed my life. Lastnight it brought back some bad memories, and some views about some things to come.What is it? Well, i will tell you it is full of darkness. A darkness i was once a part of. One if need be i would be willing to do again. Lets not dwell on the past though. On th the future. Johnny Tambo, i have not heard from you about my offer. What is it going to be? Hell, I do not care what you think. Mr. Adair, sign the match for me vs. Hollywood Johnny Tambo for the title at the PPV. What type of match? I have a little match i would like to be in with tambo. It is called a tipae golve match. WHat is it? Both men have gloves on that have small broken pieces of glass on them. I told you if need be i would be extreme, i could be technical, i can be a high flyer. It just so happens that this glove match is one of the worst matches you can get into with me. One last thing about last night in which i forgot to mention. Adivisor, you had a good match last night, too bad you lost. I think me and you need to do some talking. You know how to reach me? I am always in the gym so get me there.In closing for me, Tambo, you better start praying because in the glove match, there is no being a baby. Now, Magma, you got any thing to say??? Magma: You are dam* right i do. First off, I would like to say that i will now take your spot in the Urbish mines because you have a match with Johnny Tambo. YOu see, the urbish mines will be no problem for me. You know as well as i know, that i have been in things like this before.I will walk into urbish mines a new commer. I may walk out with the title shot, but for sure i will be walking out, not a newcommer, but i will walk out with enough experence to take on anybody in the UWA. SOmething is weird to me though. I made a open challenge to anybody here in the UWA, and nobody was man enough to challenge me. SO i am making another open challenge. So anybody who thinks they are man enough, LET'S GET IT ON!!! There BUD. Wrestlingman: At the PPV Tambo, COME PREPAIRED OR DON'T COME AT ALL!!! Magma: COME FACE THE MAN AS BIG AS A MOUNTAIN, BUT BE PREPAIRED FOR A ERUPTION!!! (Instant Black) ====== UWA ====== | Suicide | | About: The UWA | ================= (Scene opens in a dark room. The camera goes around the room, but nothing is visible. The camera then goes down, in which we see Suicide passed out on the floor, still wearing his ring attire, covered in blood. The camera man then bends down and starts trying to wake Suicide up.) Camera Man: Mr. Suicide, are you alive? (Suicide slowly lifts his head up, he opens his mouth and blood drips out. Blood is also dripping from a large cut across his forehead. He slowly wipes the blood away from his mouth and starts to clap....CLAP...CLAP...CLAP. He looks into the camera and speaks.) Suicide: Advisor, good show, I didn't think you had it in you. Who knew that two men could get so vicious? But I did what I said I was going to do, and that was beat you! I don't say this very often so listen good because you won't hear it again! Advisor you showed me that you weren't as big as a punk as I thought, so I must say that I...respect you... Now I must move on to my next opponent, that being Chauncey Faulks? Wait a second, who the hell is this guy? Camera Man: Umm, I believe he is some tag team wrestler here in the UWA. Suicide: Oh yes, I remember his comments the other day. Well Chauncey I may be bruised and hurt, but I will once again get a another check in the win column after I beat you! Thats right folks I am going to guarantee I win for myself! Because we all know that little Chauncey is no where near as good as me. So here is some advice for you Faulks, go back and study my tapes, learn my moves, bring your little friends down to ringside, because you are going to need all the help you can get when you step in the ring with me. Shall I comment on my match with Tomahawk? Nah! I think I will wait on that, because I have something even better to talk about and that is.....MALIBU MIKE! Now this rip-off of a video game character keeps mentioning the WOWA, SO I will to. Malibu you keep talking about how you beat me, but you are wrong, for you have never pinned me in your life and you never will! Dreamchild is the one who beat me in the ladder match! Then I beat you with the help of chair, then our last meeting in the WOWA I beat you 1-2-3 in the middle of the ring! Enough said about that, your a loser and I will continue to kick your ass every time we wrestle! Then you ramble on why I left the WOWA and UWA. Well lets see I left the WOWA the first time to come back to the UWA, then the second time I left is because I became UWA Champion and didn't have enough time to kick ass in more then one fed! Next you say I left the UWA because I was afraid of you! Well Like I said before I left to go to ICEW, money talks and ICEW had the right price!... And you say I am bitter towards you, but you got things backwards! You are bitter towards me because I wouldn't join that piece of crap fed WOWA! You gave me like ten contract offers, but I turned them all down because you and the WOWA SUCK! You here that? S-U-C-K, SUCK! Now I am going to make a little challenge that you Malibu can pass along to those WOWA morons! I challenge all the WOWA wrestler's to come here and step in the ring with me! Now don't take this the wrong way, it won't be UWA against the WOWA, it will be SUICIDE against those WOWA fairies! Lets just wait and see how many of those chuckle heads have the balls to come into my domain and get their asses kicked! I said I wasn't going to talk about my match with Tomahawk, well I lied! Listen here you little Indian moron, you better go back and hide in your little tent because I am going to scalp your fat ass! Once I get done with you, you are going to wish that Columbus never found America! So go and pray to God that I don't hurt you to bad! And bring the rest of those nature loving idiots, so I can give them a beating to! Folks, the "Heat Machine" is back and I am on an ass kicking spree! So be smart and just get out of my way! (Suicide finally gets up from the floor and stands up and raises both hands in the hair, as the screen fades to black!)
