UWAThe Bottom Line 11/8/97 pt2

9 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=================================================== Natural Currency: Maxwell & Pain About: S. Daniels, Asswhupper, J. Tambo, N. Williams. ==================================================== (The UWA cameraman is taken into the personal office of Natural Currency members. It is a large building nearly 24 floors. The Office is located on the 24th floor in which in gold states: "Natural Currency" on the outside. The UWA cameracrew approaches the Natural Currency secretary. It is Analysis, "The Cannon's former manager.) Cameraman: Hi, we are here on request to have a few words with "The Cannon" and "Suicide" for todays "THE BOTTOM LINE". Would it be possible if we can talk to them??? Analysis: Just a moment... (Analysis pages inside the office to "Suicide" and "The Cannon") Analysis: Chris and Mr. Pain, the UWA cameracrew is here asking for some words with you... (There is no response.) Analysis: I apologize for the inconvenience gentlemen, right this way please... (Analysis leads them into a room in which in the front has a gold plate with the words "Natural Currency" engraved in it. They then enter the room and circles the room in search for Maxwell and Pain. Circling the room is spotted two large black chairs and behind them there is money toppling over eachother. The two chairs spin around in sequence revealing smirking faces of Maxwell and Pain, camera zooms in.) Pain: I beg your pardon gentlemen, we were just performing a money check...you know, the usual, checking exactly where we are spending our money.... (Pain grabs a thick pad of paper and scrolls down it.) Pain: Lets see...5 bucks spent on Daniels mowing our lawn, Asswhupper *CLEANING* our toilets for ass comfort, Nick Williams doing our laundry, and...oh...oh yes, another 8 for Tambo flipping burgers at our personal restaurant, MANIFIQUE Tambo, the burgers are absolutely delicious... Maxwell: tsk, tsk, tsk, Natural Currency is just to genorous which is more than I can say for what we are capable of doing in the ring...(both men chuckle) Lets start on the top of the list, ok, we first have Daniels...like usual, nothing can be said about Mr. Flamboyancy other than that he's recieved too much of an asswhupping as of late...jobbers don't mean anything Natural Currency Daniels, so you're off the NC list, lucky you... Pain: And then there's Asswhupper, you open your mouth like you have enough money to do so...guess who does??? (Pain flashes several $1000 bills.) You stressed impressiveness against Daniels, but then again, that ain't really impressive, afterall...it's Daniels!!! (both burst into laughter) Now, only several words that I can say about you... if you're as good in the ring as the goodness and quality as your flashes, than you're definitely in trouble. Maxwell: Then there is Nick, "The Punisher", Williams. It appears that you seek your very own punishment, the way you talk about me. Nick, I've fought once, only one time, and that happened about two cards ago, what you mean by I haven't wrestled in a long time??? First, there's a problem with the competition and that includes yourself, and then there's the acceptance trouble by all the wrestlers of the UWA. Pain: What you expect Chris??? Maxwell: None other than that. (both chuckle) Now, Tambo, I've stressed enough as much I dislike Solid Gold and how they think they own the place~~ Pain: Guess who does??? (flashes thousand dollar bills again.) Maxwell: (chuckling) Tambo, you've been much *TOO* quite with that Intercontinental belt around your waist. So I issue a challenge to you in any "open contract" slot that you wish with the intercontinental title on the line. Am I ready for round three of our bout??? Pain: As you business counter-part would say.... Maxwell: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAWWWWWWWWWW YEEEEEEAAAHHHHHHHHH BBBBAAAAAABBBBBBBBBYYYYYYYYYYYY!!! (instant black.) ____________________________________________________ ======================================= Caster Troy About: Solid Gold and WrestleFest II ======================================= <A UWA stands outside the a dresing room door with the name Natural Currency printed across the plaque on the door. The camera then cuts inside and pans around to reveal the biggest, most luxurious dressing room ever seen. Caster Troy sits in the hot tub surrounded by beautiful women> Caster Troy: Well, Well, WWWWWWEEEEEELLLLLLLLL BBBBBAAAABBBYY!!!!!!!!!! Its the man whos always gots the masta plan comin at ya live and exclusive from Natural Currency's very own dressing room!!!!!! Now later on tonight I will be joined by the rest of Natural Currency but my buddies Maxwell and Pain have some business to attend to right now!!!!!! Speakin of business lets get down to it cause time is money and you know how I love my money baby!!!!!!!! <Caster turns on the big screen T.V. and presses play on the laser disc player remote. The screen shows Daniels and Tambo beating down Stephenson> Caster Troy: Don't you love it when you can play with somebodies mind and drive them so crazy with fear that they start attacking their own boys? <Caster chuckles> Caster Troy: Solid Gold baby we keep tellin you again and again that you got the wrong guy but for some reason you don't belive us! What ever made you think that we would want a piece of garbage like Stephenson anyway? Solid Gold there is still one cool cat in your organization that is about to follow in my footsteps and stab you in the back! It may be at WrestleFest II it may be a week from now and it may even be tonight at sometime the point is baby that sooner or later one of you is gonna wish that you listen to ol daddy Caster when you gots one hell of a headache and not a friend to turn to baby!!!!!!!! NC is the way to be!!!!!!!!!!!!!! NC4eva!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
