UWA -Live In New York- Episode #1 9/3/97

4 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live In New York - Episode 1 September 3, 1997 ('House of Pain' by Van Halen plays from somewhere) (Camera shows a helicopter view of New York City at midnight...men and women walking briskly down the sidewalks...a cab pulling over to pick up a woman scantily clad in a short leather skirt...the bright lights...a view of the Empire State building...then a birds eye view...homing in on another skyscraper...to the entrance of the building...showing a sign saying...Tonight - Live In New York!...people getting from their cars and lining up to enter the building...inside the building is a large auditorium with every seat full...the applause is deafening as the announcers voice is heard over the loud speaker) Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen...welcome! (applause) This is 'Live In New York! (louder applause) Let me now introduce you to the host... Ron Donovan! (crowd roars in approval as Donovan enters through stage left and waves to the crowd...he is a young man of approximately 30...he wears a gray suit with a bright red neck tie...his hair is short, brown, and nicely slicked back on his head...he wears also a large smile...standing at center stage before the audience) Donovan: Welcome to Live In New York...I'm certainly glad to see all of you here. It's good to be back after a season of rather low ratings. I am your host...Ron Donovan...replacing Fred Dubois. We have a great show for you tonight...but first let me introduce my co-host...yes folks...he's here in the flesh...after 6 months of ring inactivity...the Adviser! (the crowd boos the entrance...a man of no more than 35 years old walks in through stage left...taking a long look at the audience...he is approximately 6'4" tall and looks to weigh around 235 lbs...wearing a pair of spectacles...a black suit with a burgundy tie...he stands beside Donovan to the dissaproval of the crowd) Donovan: What a reception...any words for the crowd? Adviser: Yeah...seven of them...but that's what got us cancelled last year. Donovan: I can remember. Adviser: Guys like TNT...Doomsday...the lame duck DIA. Donovan: Your innocent of the bad language bit I suppose. Adviser: Of course. I know that there are children all over the world right now...waiting up and watching this show. And I believe we should set good examples for our youth. Donovan: Oh yes, I'd forgotten how versatile you could be. Adviser: You trying to get on my case Donovan? Donovan: Not at all...I'm just saying that... Adviser: Shut up. (to the camera) Don't touch that remote. We'll be right back...Live In New York. (the LINY band plays as the scene cuts to commercial) ****Advertisement**** The upset... (showing Garret Jax pin Rex Overlord with a backslide) The power... (D.C. Doug powerbombing the Specialist) The mayhem... (The Dirty Deeds battling the Guardian Angels) And the glory... (The New Hampshire Wrecking Crew escorting Garret Jax to the locker room with the UWA Heavyweight title) It's the Universal Wrestling Alliance's debut...Patriot Games 1996! Only $19.95! Order now! ****Advertisement ends**** (scene opens to LINY band playing and then fading out as Donovan takes his seat at the desk and the Adviser sits at the sofa to his right in a 'Tonight Show' type setting) Donovan: Welcome back to Live In New York. As you all know, Summer Showdown was just Sunday night, and what an incredible card it was. Adviser: The card actually surprised me. I haven't seen a UWA pay per view since I wrestled Stone Cold Crazy for the National title in February. I didn't think these new UWA guys had the talent. Donovan: But they did. 3 grueling hours of great wrestling action. We saw a couple title changes. Of course Wrestlingman successfully defended the Cruiserweight belt against Bad Habitt, but how about those tag team belts? Adviser: Yeah, great match I must admit. Both teams very talented... but the Ministry of Justice took the belts. And I have to say, it didn't shock me. Donovan: So you're saying that you had bet on the Ministry. Adviser: No no, not at all. But I think that the Ministry and Hell's Angels are equally skillfull. I think there's a good chance that Hell's Angels might beat the Ministry next time. Donovan: We shall see...perhaps a rematch for the tag straps are in the near future for Hell's Angels. How about that International title match? Adviser: One of the better triangle matches that I've seen. And I think that the belt is in good hands...that of Malibu Mike's. But I think that Mike has the potential to get a little cocky. And he can't afford to get cocky, because Crusher Stevenson wants that belt. Donovan: And what of Lord Zero? Adviser: He's retarded. Donovan: Uh...okay. Later in the night...to the shock of everyone... the UWA heavyweight champion Suicide walked out and surrendered the title before his match even began. Adviser: And that was one of the sorriest things that I've seen in quite some time. Suicide has been quiet for quite some time...who knows what his problem was. But I think that he must have gotten the pre-match jitters. And when you're going to go one on one with a guy like Big Butch Thornton...who wouldn't? Donovan: Good point...but Suicide has taken on all comers in the past. It's hard to imagine that it was his Summer Showdown title defense that scared him off. Adviser: Yes it is. Donovan: I've heard rumors that the return of Rex Overlord shook up a good many people Sunday night. It's possible that when Suicide saw Rex walk out of the locker room...that he knew it was time to run for greener pastures. Adviser: It's possible Donovan. And Overlord did shock the world Sunday night Nobody expected him to be at Summer Showdown. I mean we heard Rex Roseman and Dan Crawford debating it before the match. Roseman thought Vile Vince Viper. Crawford thought TNT. And there were other possibilities...but Overlord was probably the least expected other than the deceased Garret Jax...and he surprised a lot of people. Donovan: You think Rex is ready to be champion again? Adviser: No. Not a chance. He's obviously in great shape. It's obvious that he's been training. But the ring rust is there...and that's something that he's going to have to shake off. And I don't care what anybody says...Rex Overlord is not ready to be champion again. People have said that Overlord is going to take the UWA by storm...show some of these new guys how to wrestle...but that's not the case. You see...people that say this are stuck on the UWA of 1996. It's people like TNT that say these things...and it's not true. The UWA has changed...but the talent that it lost has been replaced. Overlord is a legend...but there's not a chance that he's going to jump in the ring and whip Big Butch Thornton after 7 months on the inactive list. Donovan: Do you think Rex Overlord knows this? Do you think he's walking into this overconfident? Adviser: I don't know Donovan...but I've managed the man and I do know that he's a man that likes to take shortcuts. He'll powerbomb his girlfriend if it means getting a UWA title shot the next day. Overlord needs to take his time...needs to work his way up the ladder if he wants to ever have a chance at becoming UWA champion. A Rex Overlord vs. Butch Thornton match would be a major ticket seller. I mean we're talking about a TNT vs. Immorality or Venom Corp vs. Syndicate calibur feud here. Donovan: You could be right. Adviser: I am, I assure you. Donovan: Only time will tell...I'm sure we all agree. On Sunday night Suicide surrendered the UWA belt to Butch Thornton...but TNT came to ringside and told the world that there would have to be a match for the belt. TNT introduced "The Specimen" Chad Blake to replace Suicide...a man who had lost to the Masked Avenger earlier in the night. What do you think Adviser? Adviser: I think it was a bad call by TNT. I don't think that Blake deserved the shot...especially after he had already lost one match earlier in the card. But TNT had the right idea in getting an opponant for Butch Thornton. Donovan: And who do you think would have been a better opponant? Adviser: I don't know Donovan. But I think that Thornton deserved the title that he won...and I think he's way out in front of all the other UWA competitors. Right now he's the man...it's undisputed...and it could stay that way for a long time. Donovan: Well, in a rundown of the title matches, Wrestlingman successfully defended the cruiserweight belt against Bad Habit. Malibu Mike won a triangle match with Lord Zero and Crusher Stevenson to become the International champion. The Ministry of Justice took the tag team belts from Hell's Angels...and Big Butch Thornton pinned Chad Blake to become the new UWA Heavyweight champion. Great pay per view. Adviser: It was. Donovan: Now folks, the reason for this show...the up close interview. I'm here with the Adviser tonight...seems like a good place to start in our interviewing. Adviser, if I might ask you some questions? Adviser: Certainly. Donovan: We all know that you were once a very large name in the wrestling world...having been one of the originals of the Apocolypse Alliance with former UWA International Champion and WEW International Champion, King Cobra, and with Blitzkrieg, Colossus, and Rex Overlord. Adviser: Correct. Donovan: Now...you took the Apocolypse Alliance from King Cobra back in the Fall of 1996...some legal maneuvering of some sorts. Adviser: My bread and butter. Donovan: Later you would recruit new members. Jack Vigo, Sandman, and Thrash are just a few of the names that later became part of your stable. The stable turned on Rex Overlord in November, leaving you the Apocolypse Alliance's head man. Adviser: Yes. Donovan: Then in return...when the chips were down...you sold your own stable down the river at WrestleFest '96. Correct? Adviser: I have no comment. Donovan: The Apocolypse Alliance had become nothing more than a group of thugs...completely outclassed by their WrestleFest opponants...the Syndicate which consisted of the Godfather, Rex Overlord, Immorality, the Boogeyman, and Dino Romeo. Adviser: I was injured Donovan. You saw the match...replay it all you want to. I was a mystery man representing the Venom Corp against Death's Associates and I was injured in that match. Donovan: And that same night, you introduced the 7'8" Dominus as your replacement in the 5 on 5 and then left the arena. After the Apocolypse Alliance was slaughtered, you had found a comfortable desk job with the Venom Corp. Adviser: Well...I... (flustered) Danovan: Later the Venom Corp would face bankruptcy...they would lose most of their talent including Immorality, Pesticide, and Carnage. Adviser: Pesticide and Carnage blew up in their car. So what does that have to do with me? Donovan: It means that either you are a crummy business man...making mockeries of two legendary stables...or perhaps a genius...intentionally making mockeries of two stables. Adviser: Well...the latter of course. Donovan: Hmmm. Later you would wrestle in singles competition...returning to singles action full time for the first time since you began with the Apocolypse Alliance. You would do very well for yourself...going on to feud with Assault for the National title...one that you weren't quite good enough to get. Adviser: Don't tell me that I wasn't good enough Donovan. This guy Assault...he was crazy. Flipping through tables, ball bats wrapped in barbed wire, blow torches, that was his thing. Mine was technical wrestling...in a ring. Donovan: And your point is? Adviser: The UWA never gave me a regular match with Assault. It was always a no-DQ or a falls count anywhere. That's not my kind of match Donovan. You're a smart guy...I shouldn't have to be explaining this. And to the fans across the world...my fans...I would be wearing the UWA National belt today...had I been treated fairly by the UWA. Donovan: You have fans? Adviser: I have friends in high places. Donovan: Any specifics? Adviser: No. All I can tell you is that they live in a big white house over on the east coast. Donovan: I see. Well, tell me, do you intend to get back in the ring any time soon? Adviser: It's a possibility Donovan. In no way am I old...or even out of shape. From what I see now...the heavyweight division could use some new talent...and the cruiserweights too. Donovan: Now, things have changed radically since you were one of the UWA big names. D.C. Doug, Gunloc, TNT, Immorality, Assault, and many other big names you just don't come across anymore. How would you compare the UWA of '96 and the UWA of '97? Adviser: Well the UWA of 1996 was huge...probably over 100 wrestlers under contract. Now, well things have fallen off Donovan. Donovan: Fallen off? Adviser: There's been a lot of big name talent that has disappeared. Take the Godfather for instance. He and the Syndicate had money problems. The Venom Corp broke down...Immorality was canned for making trouble in the fed...Gunloc and the DIA lost the war and have retreated...taking big name talent with them...TNT has retired...and the Adviser has retired. Donovan: Well, I think it was high time that the DIA went on their way...because there was no way that they could have won...and the UWA executives know just how much trouble they were to the fed. As for Immorality...he would cause his share of problems too...and it is clear that the roster has fallen down...a restructuring that I feel has been most needed...something that not Gordon Adair, Brian McVey, Venom, or John Smith could do. Dan Kirk is the man in charge now...and the UWA is great here in 1997. Adviser: Whatever Donovan. Donovan: Folks, we're out of time. We want you to tune in next time to Live In New York. Join the Adviser and I as we introduce our first special guest in this new season. For the Adviser...I'm Ron Donovan...goodnight.
