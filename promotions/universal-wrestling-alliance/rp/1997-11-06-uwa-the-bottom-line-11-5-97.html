(Scene opens with Suicide at Lynch Mob Headquarters(wowa stable), in his office sitting at his desk. He is wearing a black Armani Suit, and has on a black and gold tie. He looks into the camera and speaks...) Suicide: Oh how this federation gets so boring so fast. I was real excited to make my return here a month ago, but now things just plain suck! How unexciting can this fed be? I am actually starting to find the WOWA to be a worth wild fed! ...Now I got a match with Vile Vince Viper, barbed wire, oh joy(sarcastically)! That should really spice things up(rolls his eyes). I already beat VVV before and I know I can do it again. But I am gonna come right out an say it, I'll probably lose this Sunday, but the funny thing about that is I don't even care! World Title Match, big deal! I held that piece of tin before and it's nothing but idiotic challenges from the same worthless pieces of crap each week. I think I'll just stick with my piece of tin foil the UWA Cruiser Weight Belt, because so far no one has challenged me! Thats fine with me, I get paid no matter what! But back to VVV. He is a washed up over rated moron. He talks with an annoying lisp, which makes it so no one can understand what the hell he is saying, then when he says something understandable he's talking about some matches he had with wrestlers from like six months ago! Well Mr. Viper nobody cares! Why don't you take your midget ass and worry about the present? VVV win or lose this is gonna be a hell of a match! I just hope you are prepared for a bloody match to the death! A little square surrounded in barbed wire, just the way I like it, I just hope you don't disappoint me! But enough of you VVV, I can't talk anymore about you, Because I am about to fall asleep from the boringness of this fed. (He closes his eyes and falls face first into his desk, pretending he is sleep. He then lifts his head up and shakes his head with a little grin on his face.) Here is some advice for Mr. Adair take your wallet and pull some money out and hire some decent wrestlers, and stop with the lisp speaking midgets, and the mid carder rip-offs! If you fail to do so and I have to keep beating on the same people week after week, then you can get ready to brush up on your Spanish and say ADIOS to SUICIDE!
