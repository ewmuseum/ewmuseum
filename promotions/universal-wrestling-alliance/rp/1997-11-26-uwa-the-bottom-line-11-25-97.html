It's a video of Wrestlefest II. It just got over.) Silver: Wo, Micheal, what a Wrestlefest. But you know if we were there, we would have stole the show. Gold: That's right Rick. We are the best tag-team in the UWA, and will take on anyone. Silver: The Nightmares seem to want a shot at us there, Micheal. Gold: That they do. Nightmares, we'll take you, anyplace, anytime. There's no way to stop us. (A very beautiful woman steps into the picture with a 2 drinks in her hands.) Woman: Drink boys? Silver: Thanks, babe. (Silver and Gold take their drinks. They take a sip.) Silver: Nightmares, we are not a afraid to take on any challange. Therefore, we accept. Just sign the dotted line, and you got yourselves a match. Gold: By the way, Nightmares, we're not afraid of the dark, and we're certainly not a afraid of you. So when we have our match, you guys better train long and hard because we are the best. (fade out) ____________________________________________________ Bad Boy Alliance aka Bad Boyz,BBA About:UWA Cruiserweight Title,UWA Heavyweight Title,D-Man,Wrestlingman (Scene opens inside a white room with a wooden table situated under a single light bulb.Sitting at the table is Johnny"Bad Boy"Coals and Chauncey Faulks.Both are wearing white tanks tops and black pants.Chauncey has on a green NY Yankees hat.Behind them is a "BBA" Banner.) Johnny"Bad Boy"Coals:Gordon Adair,you open your ears and you listen closely because I've got something to say!I came back to UWA 3 weeks ago and it was insinuated that I would get a shot at the Cruiserweight Title that was STRIPPED from me during our little contractual hassles.Well,you did indeed give me a shot,but then Suicide forfeits the title and therefore I SHOULD be the rightful UWA Cruiserweight Champion!I had to do it and Suicide got the title!But now I have to participate in some stupid match that come up with to prove once again and I am the only man deserving of that title?Besides Chauncey Faulks of course! But let's see who we have in the Cruiserweight division,Wreestlingman,well he's up there in the Heavyweight Championship category,but I'll have more to say on him later!D-Man,the number one contender?I don't think so!I beat his @ss before and I should be ahead of him!But if I have to do it again,so be it because it's obvious that the UWA is trying to bruy me again!But Gordon,open your eyes!it's not a coincedence that the ratings go up when I compete!People either love me or hate me,it don't matter to me,but it should to you and UWA Sports Inc.!So you create your little match at Christmas Chaos and I'll be there to take the UWA Cruiserweight Title off your hands and this time,I'm not giving it back!But before we get there,I want D-Man in a match next Tuesday,just one on one and I'll show him and everybody else that I am the undisputed number one contender once again!But I also want to throw out something else,this one to those punks who attacked us after we humiliated the AOA,R.A.D. and Wrestlingman!You fools think you can just attack us from behind and have no retribution come your way?I don't think so!Sean Goldz suffered a concussion because of your ass and so on the behalf of Sean Goldz,he wants you to know that this Friday he along with myself and Chauncey Faulks will take you and whoever your mystery partners are out and out for good!As for Wrestlingman,you little piece of trash,you think you're all big and bad because you got the Main Event at WrestleFest?Well you didn't deserve it!I should be on the Main Event!You are a disgrace,I beat you once and I'll do it again!You whine and whine about me the cage breaking,but hey it could've happened to me!But if you want,me and you one on one in another King Cage Match 2 Sunday's from now on Sunday Spotlight!No interference,we have UWA officials,security and technition surrounding the ring,no loose parts,no outside help,just me and you!Just so I can prove to you that the first time wasn't a fluke but it was a fluke that you survived the first time!Now Gordon Adair,I have another question for you,I beat Wrestlingman and yet he gets a shot at the Heavyweight Title just weeks later!Well I want my due!I want in on that 4 way UWA Heavyweight Championship Match!You give me matches and I'll rack up the points!In fact,if you give me matches,I will guarentee you that I will go undefeated and I will become the number one contender for the UWA Heavyweight Title!But first off,I want D-Man on Tuesday and Wrestlingman on Sunday Spotlight in a few weeks and next Friday I'll take on that rat who put out my good friend Sean Goldz last night,I want R.A.D!You three sign on the dotted line as I make my way to the top and I'll put a week of hell on UWA that nobody as ever seen!It's time for Johnny"Bad Boy"Coals to get the recognition he deserves and it all starts NOW! Chauncey Faulks:That's right,the REAL Superstar in the UWA,Johnny Coals!VVV,Wrestlingman and Big Butch Thornton couldn't step to Johnny because he'll whup all their asses!I have learned from the best and it's time for me to step it up!I'm 1 and 0,even though the UWA insists on making me 1 and 1 even though when I didn't show for that match against Suicide,I WASN'T under contract to UWA!Don't matter though,come Christman Chaos,whatever match da damm prez thinks up,I'm in!Me and Johnny will rip BLEEP up and everybody will see why da boyz call me the "Future of Professional Wrestling!".As for Friday Night against R.A.D. and his boyz,we'll take them out like we took out the AOA!Together,nobody can beat the BBA!But I want that chump,Crusher Chris Simmons!Chris,you may be ranked in front of me,but there's no way in hell that you're better than me and I'll prove it to your sorry little @ss!The BBA has been put through political BLEEP and we're sick of it!The BBA Reign of Terrer is on patrol! About: - D-Man, Devastating Demolishers, BBA, Chris Simmons, Scott May & Goliath - ===================================== (Scene opens as R.A.D. is sitting alone in his hot tub outside behind his house on the Southside of Pittsburgh. The weather is around 35 degrees and with some light snow flurries. You can se the steam rise from the hot tub. ) R.A.D.: Well, Well and double well. Man was Wrestlefest II the event or what! First off D-Man your are the man. I couldn’t believe the way we worked as a team in the ring. If you ever need a tag team partner in the future you have my number. Devastating Demolishers you guys were demolished on Sunday. Do you think by sending in this big seven foot guy that it was going to be a piece of cake? Huh I took the oversized goof down myself and also half of the UWA world tag teams champs. Was it Bash or was it Crash? Hell I don’t know but the bottom line is that my team came out of WrestlefestII on top. (Pause) R.A.D. : Now I know Danny is going to be complaining about losing to my team and that I should get off my lazy butt and do something. Well my question for you Danny and your Road Warrior want a bees is why don’t you guys get off your butts and defend those UWA titles against me and one of my mystery men? What do you say Danny boy. I know you have that big work load and your defending those titles every night of the week four weeks a month. So Danny put up or shutup pal. (Pause) R.A.D.: BBA I had to interfere after your match with the A.O.A. to give a welcome present for you coming back to the UWA. Anytime you punks want to get in the ring with a wrestling machine then sign the dotted line. Though Sunday was great for me Friday wasn’t so great. As every match I had in November I beat my opponent from pillar to post but in the end I have outside interference or someone causes a D.Q. and it costs me the match. The lastest wanker is Chris Simmons. Chris let me make this perfectly clear so you can understand. DON’T GET IN MY FACE! The next time you interfere little man I going to kick your butt so hard…well I can’t say that on television. I did have some pleasure in hitting you with a steel chair along with that so-called greatest wrestler in the universe Scotty Potty Motty May. May I pulled you in early in the match and then let you have the beating of your life. May your nothing in my book. I was going to demand a rematch in a steel cage but I don’t think May has the guts. Speaking of having no guts that brings me back to the BBA. This Friday me and my mystery men are going to hurt you three or how many of you come to the ring. You may have won a battle on Sunday but the A.O.A. is going to win the war. I guess those are my thoughts for right now. Goliath the contract is in Gordon’s office for you to sign because I believe the UWA needs a enforcer. I know a real Horsemen by the name of Arn Anderson was and is the true Enforcer but the UWA needs someone to stand up to these other idiot wrestlers who think they can do whatever and whenever. Gordon I applaud you for what you did at WrestlefestII by holding the Crusierweight and World Titles vacant. Everytime I see the world title defended I think I’m at the circus. R.A.D. signing off.
