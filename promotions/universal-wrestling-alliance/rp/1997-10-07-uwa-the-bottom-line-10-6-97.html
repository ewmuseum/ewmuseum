[Both cars speed off, leaving the cameraman standing alone in the parking lot.] ============================== UWA ================================= | Hollywood Johnny Tambo | | About: Christopher Maxwell, Crusher Stevenson, and The Brotherhood | ==================================================================== (Camera cuts to Hollywood Johnny Tambo playing billiards) Tambo (notices camera): A/V boy, you sure show up at inopertune moments! (throws down poolstick) Alright, since you've already interrupted my concentration, I'll give you the facts. First off, Christopher Maxwell, I don't seem to remember you having anything to do with my winning of the RWF Title. Dave Slime, if you are out there, you tell them! The only thing I remember about you and the RWF Title is that you lost BOTH chances I gave you at winning it. You wanna shot at my International Title? A/V Boy: You haven't won that one yet. Tambo: Good thing you said YET, A/V pig. Anyway, just ask for it, and I'll give you a crack at the belt. But use that good quality wax when you polish it for me. I don't want it to look as bad as my boots when you polish THEM! A/V Boy: What in the world are you talking about? Tambo: You best be quiet. Now then, Crusher Stephensuck, my last word for you...sorry. Yup, sorry. I'm sorry you are about to receive the same treatment that Malibu Mucus was handed down from the high and mighty HJT! Don't worry though, once I've won your belt, I'll leave you alone. Then again, maybe I won't. Maybe I'll run you out like I did to those stupid spaghetti slurping cretins in the GWL. A/V Boy: I remember that. Tambo: Finally, I see that this group called the Brotherhood wants me to join their clan. Hmmmmm. Be a part of such a prestigous group as the Brotherhood. I'll consider it. I'll seriously consider it. Tell ya what Eliminator, I'll give you my answer this week.Maybe on Tuesday, maybe on Friday. Very lucritive idea, though. Thanks, man. Well, I wanna finish my game, so, A/V Boy, you know the routine. (camera cuts out) =============== UWA ================= | Rex Overlord | | About: Ice-Blade & Vile Vince Viper | ===================================== (scene opens to Rex Overlord sitting in a large leather recliner...surrounded by darkness...the only light being that of the fireplace in the background. It's light is partially blocked by the shadow of the Godfather who sits at his desk gazing into the flames) Overlord: Ice-Blade...you must understand this now. Vile Vince Viper claims that he ended the career of Rex Overlord...and he tells the truth. For seven months I've trained...I've prepared myself to meet Vile Vince Viper again. I've returned to the UWA...and I've found the Viper coiled and ready to strike...but this time I am ready. At Hellstorm '97 it must be Rex Overlord vs. a prepared Vile Vince Viper. It cannot be any other way. (starts to loosen his tie...leaning forward in the recliner...the light of the fireplace highlight his eyes and mouth) Overlord: You must understand that I have a score to settle...and that I have come for revenge. And my revenge cannot be tainted. Not by you, not by stipulation, and not even by my Godfather. I will have my untainted revenge. You will not stand in my path Ice-Blade. I warn you now...be very careful. You take the risk of fighting Vile Vince Viper in a submission match...but even worse is the risk that I will be there...watching...and making sure that you don't lose your head. (sits back again...the shadow in the background has shifted...the chair before the fireplace is empty...Overlord's eyes remain focused on the camera) Overlord: Vile Vince Viper...in three weeks time you will learn a very painful thing...something that not anyone in the history of the world has been unfortunate enough to learn. The sweet revenge of Rex Overlord...and I will leave you a pulp...a fate worse than death. It's coming Viper. Because some call him man...some call him monster...but all will call him master in the end...he is Rex Overlord. And he is coming for Vile Vince Viper.
