Somewhere, we can hear music playing. The camera pans to the left and we see the words "Proud Larry's" inscribed on a door, and underneath, a poster with "Tonight Only: THE MURDER JUNKIES!!!".* Voice: Are you sure this is where we're supposed to be? Voice2: This is where we were told to meet him...hey...we get in the club for free though. That's a good thing. Voice: But the "Murder Junkies?" What would he be doing here? Voice2: How should I know? We just go in there and do our jobs okay? Voice: Oh all right. *A hand reaches out and pushes the door open. A bouncer just inside glares disdainfully at the newcomers, but remains silent. The music has suddenly increased exponentially in volume, and as we turn a corner, we see four men up onstage. One, barking vocals like a rabid hound into the mic, stands over six feet tall, and rips at his flesh. On guitar, a second blares out cruchy power chords, while a man with 8 inch chin spikes and Hitler mustache pounds away on a vintage, stickered Fender Precision bass. Rounding out the quartet on drums is, well, a naked drummer.* Voice: Oh my God. I don't wanna be here! These people scare me. Voice2: Let's just get it over with. Better to be here than lose our jobs. *The music comes to a close, and the lead "singer" (growler?) speaks.* Mike Denied: ALRIGHT!!! Now...we got someone up here...his name's Sabbath. He's gonna play guitar for all you ****s. *Sabbath climbs up onstage replete in a long black leather trench coat, black jeans, and "EBOLA ZAIRE" t-shirt with a jet black Gibson SG strapped on. He shakes Mike Denied's hand, then Merle's (the bassist), but respectfully declines the meathook of everyone's favorite drummer Dino.* Sabbath: Okay...what do you people wanna hear? *The crowd begins to chant "GG! GG! GG! GG!"* Merle: Ya wanna hear some of my brother's ****? You got it! ONE TWO THREE FOUR!!!! *The band kicks in, and Sabbath plays along, occasionally yelping into Merle's mic the word's "BITE IT...YOU SCUM!", then solos at the appropriate point. Afterwords, he walks off the stage to more chants, and intercepts the cameramen.* Sabbath: Sorry guys...I didn't think you'd like to come here, but I had to see these guys. But let's get someplace where we can actually hear one another okay? *Both camera men agree heartily to this notion, and the camera fades. Seconds later, we open again in a limo, where Sabbath's once mussed hair is pulled into a long tight pony-tail. Sayaka has joined us, dressed in long black boots, and matching jeans and "UWA" T.* Sabbath: Well, I see I haven't made much of an impression on the denizens of the UWA just yet. They choose to overlook the "Walking Contradiction." That, my newfound compadres, is your first mistake. I'm not someone to be taken lightly. Don't make me come for your sorry hides, cause I'll do it! Still, I assume most of you see me as a flash in the pan rookie. Guess what? You're wrong! I'm here to stay. Y'know why? I got news for you! And it ain't good news! Cuz you ain't nothing! You ain't nothing but the poop on the bottom of my shoe! I'm gonna scrape you off on the curb...I'm gonna scrape you on the curb...like a piece of dog poo...like a piece of bubble gum! *Sabbath looks at the camera, his stonefaced visage nearly cracking into maniacal laughter.* Voice: Ummmmm....... Sabbath: Nevermind. It's not important. What *IS* important is that we get some business taken care of. Mage, you haven't responded yet. I don't like to wait. I'm not used to waiting. If you don't take my challenge soon, then I'll just forget you and head for bigger game. Call me arrogant tonight, well, now you know why they call me the "Walking Contradiction!" You *NEVER* know what I'll be like! But you see, that's an advantage, the way *I* see it. You can't guess what I'll do next. I'm a looney-psycho-squirrel in yo pants-homicidal-suicidal- whateveridal-gonna get into your pants and suck your soul-freak-o-rama- groovy-smooth-spifferama-ding-dang-skippy-spiff-banana-fanna-fo-fanna-kickin ' yo bootie from here to Amory Mississipi where no one'll ever find you-son of a bu-bo! Yeah...that and a *BAG O' CHIPS!* There's gonna be PLENT o' pain and suffering as long as I'm here! *He's panting now, his hair now undone and wild. He then brushes it back, as if nothing happened.* Sabbath: What's everyone looking at me like that for? I'm on a adrenaline rush okay? Sayaka: Lover, lay off the sugar and caffiene. It's not healthy. *She flashes a seductive grin at him.* Sabbath: Okily-dokily. *He takes in a *LOOOOOOOOOONG* breath.* Sabbath: Well then! Let's see...I haven't watched the cards too closely, but that will change now that you kids know I'm out there. Just don't come crying to me when I beat you. Now, that's it, I'm headin' back to the show. *Sabbath exits the limo with Sayaka.* *"Horror Business" by the Misfits begins to play and a bleeding Celtic cross fades in.* _______________________________________________________
