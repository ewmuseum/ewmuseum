Man, I'd like to say good job on kicking the crap out of those losers, Johnny Walker: But right now, I'm sick of this... come over here Rob. [Robert Mulligan walks over to Johnny with a peice of paper.] Johnny Walker: This is a contract, Johnny Walker vs. Johnny Coals!!! No-Holds-Barred, Falls Count Anywhere.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ UWA MATCH CONTRACT Johnny Walker vs. Johnny Coals Falls Count Anywhere. No Holds Barred. x Johnny Walker x ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Johnny Walker: Sign the match, if you dare!!! King Suicide: And then, come Hellstorm, three of us will take on the Bad Boyz!! Cameraman: Can you give us any hints as to who's going to be on team AOA? Judas: Get the hell outta here!!!! [Camera fades.] =================== UWA =================== | Big Butch Thornton | | About: The Syndicate, Eliminator, VVV | =========================================== (Scene opens inside the apartment of Big Butch Thornton in Detroit, Michigan. Sitting down on the couch watching TV is Big Butch Thornton. He's wearing a plain white Tank-Top and a pair of silverish blue Detroit Lions Champion shorts. A small bandage is on his forehead. He zaps the TV off and begins to speak.) Big Butch Thornton: Alot of things are going on in the UWA. People doublecrossing each other, a influx of talent, and people blindsiding others. Well, a little of everything went down Sunday Night. Rex, you were a good partner. I know if all the unexpected occurances didn't take place, we wouldn't have gotten a cheap win. I have no problem with you.....But Syndicate...LISTEN UP!!!! (Cracks his knuckles) I don't really care if I know you or not. The first time I saw you I knew right then and there I want nothing to do with you!! You might be chummy with Rex, but when the match with me and Rex teaming up took place, I didn't want to see you guys sticking your nose in my business!! Now I'm not in a good mood right now. I still haven't heard from Crusher and I'm getting sick with the crap going on in the UWA. If you Mafioso's want to make a enemy for yourself right off the bat, it would be a privelage. If not...THEN STAY THE HELL AWAY FROM ME!!!!! (Walks over the fridge and opens it up. He grabs a beer and slams the fridge shut. Butch opens the beer and chugs some of it down.) Now, moving on. Well it looks like the match I wanted to be signed......Me Vs. My close personal friend, Vile Vince Viper was signed. Vince, I'm sure you have Overlord chomping at the bit to get a piece of you but now you have the One Man Wrecking Crew after you!!! You can go around and cheapshot ladies all you want but when it comes down to me and you in a match of _MY_ choice, the only thing Extreme about you will be bones popping out your carcass!!! VVV, I'll be suspecting any suprise you throw out at me. And a 6 pack of whoop-a$$ will waiting!! (Chugs down some more of his beer) And last but certainly not least is the Eliminator. Now I saw your interview with the rest of the Brotherhood on the All UWA Channel yesterday and I started to cry....THE HELL I DID!!! You bunch of pissants were trash before your little realization to god and your now!!! I could care how much asskissing you do to bring the 5 fans you had back to you but when we meet on the 21st, the only place you'll be touching the UWA Heavyweight Title is when I SMACK it across your head!!! UWA...Judgement Day Is Upon Us.....I GUARAN F'N TEE IT!!!!!! (Butch goes into another room and slams the door pissed off as the camera fades to black.) ======= UWA ======= | Donovan Black | | About: The UWA | =================== [Sitting in 90 degree weather, Donovan Black sits on a tree stump in the desert sand, and looks to be thinking.. as the Sunlight seems to be beating down beats on his back. He looks around, and goes back to thinking....] [The camera pans back, and it's just a studio, a bare, brown floor, as Donovan sits under a heat lamp, on a pile of sand as the giant painted backdrop rumbles as a fan's gust hits it] Donovan: Perception is in the eye of the beholder... for one who has had the gold spoon-fed to him since childhood. In my early years, I ate from the table of spoiled charity, as my parents, two rich socialites made sure only the Best came to my palate... [Wipes mouth, then stands, and kicks the tree stump over, it's plastic. He stares at the camera emotionlessly] Donovan: ...And that concludes my speech. Jackie, please elaborate. [Jackie Black slides into view, hair pulled back, chomping a Twizzler...He straightens his suit, as Donovan walks off...] Jackie: NOW let me clear the wind for the confused UWAers What we have here is a man who is constantly sssshh- You know, and even though he is the Upper Class, no one gives him the respect he deserves!! This man can outwrestle EVERYONE!! BUT NO!! Oh no, no one gives a rat's ass about that!! They just wanna see Donovan talk trash about someone's momma! Let's look at a message from Davarro Danny or whatever that loser's name is! Jackie: Now here's my impression, (cracks knuckles) NOW look here Donovan Black you fat piece of crap,no one gives a damn that you've got that fat slob of a manager Jackie Black talking for you because come Tuesday,Danny ah...sorry, Devestated Danny Davarro is gonna beat your fat butt up! [WIpes head, and smiles] Jackie: Now his slutty money digger! Ok, ok, okay!! Yes that's right Donovan Black,you fat slob,you will never beat my man because he is the devestated Danny Davarro and he can beat anyone,even that fat piece of crap Jackie Black! This Tuesday Danny Davarro is gonna beat your butt...up! [Jackie smiles, and coughs under his breath "Loser"] Jackie: MAY BE I NEED TO SPELL it out!! UWA!! What a -ok, okay... there are a distinct few who could fight their way out of a paper bag, but the majority of you midcarders are small time potatoes! You know WHY you're here? So the BIG BOYS have someone to push around! Now I admit, Donovan Black has not been here long enough to form any alliances, but once I make a few business deals, you'll realize that the talent of this man is ONLY exceeded by his presence!! NOW we can all scream the same 500 words EVERY DAY FOR THE REST OF our life...but we don't LEARN ANYTHING!! See dava... Davarro, MAYBE if you were TEN FOOT TALL and 467 pounds, people would be impressed, but all that's getting impressed is that crack whore who thinks heroin chic is a designer label! Listen, I have no problem with the UWA, as every federation I do not control has this problem, Crap ass interviewers blah blah blahing the same tired damn crap!! I AM A BUSY MAN PEOPLE! I DON'T ALWAYS have time to spew one liners like a teenager after a serious hangover! IF I see that some guy is getting too much attention for becoming a a... a freakin parrot, then hey, maybe I am not as smart as my IQ says I am!! ....But this is the States, and I haven't been here all that long... back that is! But trust me, when I do have something to say, YOU HEAR IT!! [Coughs] Jackie: Damn funeral...anyways, what have we learned today? ...if you keep the channel on, apparently NOT much! Let me sum up the next few interviews...or lack thereof.. ....I'M GONNA KICK YOUR ASS...YOU FAT SLOB... I'm THE MEANEST S.O.B. in the WORLD, and YOU ARE GOING DOWN!! ...Ha ha ha, smalltime... [Coughs....] Jackie: 11!! Your time's UP! And I, I have things to do. Waiter: Your Rack of Lamb, sir. Tambo: Thank you, boy. Now scram! (waiter leaves) You want to know what kind of match I am going to chose as our gimmick match?? Well, son, originally I was going to suggest a barbed wire match. But look at this face...do you actually think I want anything to happen to this beautiful face? Come now! No, kiddo, I'm going to select...a Bullrope Match!! Thats right, varmint, I reckon I'll hog tie you and drag you to all four corners of the ring. And since everyone is banned from ringside, I won't have to worry about your little flunkies interfering in my business. And as far as you're concerned Malibu, my business is selling wholesale beatings, and at the Big Show, you are gonna get more business from good ol' HJT than you can withstand. And, Mikey baby.... There is a crowd of school kids gathered around something in the middle of the gym. The school bell sounds and the kids gradually move away from the pack. As the pack diminishes D-Man can be seen signing autographs. As the last child leaves he turns to the camera as it approaches him.) D-Man: Well well. Don't we got some new faces around here. We got new wrestlers comin in from everywhere. Well, one of these rookies has been signed to meet the D-Man. Creed, I don't know who you are, where you're from etc. But if you think I give a flying......... Any kids still around? .....No......Good. If you think I give a flying *BLEEP* then you are thoroughly mistaken. I think I'm gonna enjoy the change of scenery in the ring. I've been here a while now and only wrestled against one guy. Creed, you can expect to be meeting a fresh wrestler ready to return to the higher rankings where he belongs. My knee's back to its best and by the time you and me get in the ring, so too will my wrestling. After that comes the Urbish Mines. I've done a bit of work in mines back n Australia so being in the dark underground poses no worries for me. The other big names in the match can claim what they want, but the only things that are gonna help you in the Urbish Mines are experience and ability to fight and baby, I got them alright. D-Man signs the paper and both leave the gym.) ======================= UWA ========================== | R.A.D. | | About : Brotherhood, Solid Gold, Fearsome Foursome | ====================================================== (Camera fades in to R.A.D. who is sitting in his hot tub out behind his house in Pittsburgh, PA. The time is around 1:00 in the afternoon and is sunny. R.A.D. has his head resting on the back of the hot tub. R.A.D. notices the UWA camera man and begins to speak.) R.A.D.: Do you ever sleep UWA camera man? Well since your here I give you and interview. Last night I wrestled the Eliminator in a steel cage match. When I entered the locker room after the match I was still wired. My energy was flowing out of control last night. Eliminator I told you to expect the beating of your life and I kept my word. At the beginning of the match you put your hand out for me to shake so we could resolve all the things you did to me. Well you got my answer for that Eliminator by a slap in the face. People have asked me why I dropped in from the top of the cage with a steel chair onto the head of the Eliminator and the answer is I don’t really know. I was so full of rage last night I couldn’t control myself. I was told that the Eliminator was moving after I left the cage and that medical attention was needed. As far as I’m concern the Eliminator has been Eliminated from the UWA. And as for Brotherhood I know they didn’t like what I did last night but that’s life. The Brotherhood attacked me numerous times and that was my revenge. Zodiac, Stalker, Sandman and Mr. Tanaka are the remaining members of the Brotherhood. Zodiac your next in line and then the Sandman. Sandman I saw you unmasked the other day but I don’t recognize you at all. You must have had a another man step in for you for the interview because I know who you really are and the mystery is no longer a mystery to me. When we meet in the tag match Sandman you will meet the man that will destroy you. (R.A.D. shifts his weight in the hot tub) R.A.D.: If your wondering my leg is a little sore from the plunge off of the top rope but no real damage was done. I have a week and a half to prepare for my match with Zodiac so that will give me plenty of time to recoup. Chambers I saw your interview and it made me sick. Oh Mr. Money spills a little coke on himself and wipes it up with a 100 dollar bill. You see Chambers I have money and lots of it. I just don’t choose to flaunt it like you do. I have my own investments here and there but I choose to life in a normal house and in a normal average working class neighborhood. Chambers well meet after Hell Storm and then I’ll shut up that big annoying mouth of yours. (R.A.D. sits up in the hot tub) R.A.D.: Speaking of Hell Storm the other day the Goddess and I were talking about me challenging the UWA heavyweight Champion Big Butch Thorton for the gold. Well after my victory yesterday I’m ranked #2 in the UWA behind Johnny, don’t call me Rambo, Tambo. So Thorton I’m challenging you for the gold at Hell Storm. Since I’ve been in the UWA our paths haven’t crossed. Both of us have been on opposite sides of things and both of us have been taking care of our own business. I understand the match will be in a steel cage with barb wire. I’ve been in steel cage and barb wires matches before but not a combined cage barb wire match. So here is the contract Hell Storm 97 R.A.D vs Big Butch Thorton x R.A.D. vs x R.A.D.: No games, no gimmicks just two of the top athletes in the UWA going at it. I also understand that you can decline my challenge Thorton but if you do I know down the road I will get a shot at whoever has the heavyweight gold around there waist. Thorton the challenge has been made and I’ll be waiting for your reply. And one last thing Roseman your a marked man brother. It doesn’t matter if your in the Brotherhood or Solid Gold your are going to face my wrath. Why? Because I’m sick of your comments during matches and you give this sport a bad name. (R.A.D. sits back down into the hot tub and rests his head on the side. Camera fades to black.) ============================ UWA =============================== | The Syndicate | | About: Stalker, Magma, Big Butch Thornton, Vile Vince Viper... | ================================================================ (scene opens to the extensive front lawn of an old house...large but not extravagant. It is surrounded by an iron black fence. It is dusk. Trees shed leaves of a gold or rusty color...which are carried about the lawn by a strong autumn breeze. Several yards up the walk is the front stoop. We see Rocco Brazzi sitting on the front step staring straight down with his hands folded between his knees. The front screen door opens and out walks Willy Ciccio in a long black trench coat and black fedora...both men dressed alike minus the hat on Brazzi. Ciccio steps onto the top step...cigarette protruding from between his lips) Ciccio: Tis the season. (Brazzi grunts in reply and continues to stare into the unknown) Ciccio: I still have yet to figure out why Butch Thornton speaks of us so badly. We come to the UWA to help Rex out, and we're condemned for it. Butch saw it last week. Some jerk comes out of the audience and hits the lady with a cane and there is no one to protect her. Butch don't do it so we have to. You see Butch, it's our obligation to see to the safety of our members. The Godfather is unavailable, so it's us who are responsible. Do you think I'm taking this wrong Rocco? (Brazzi continues to stare) Brazzi: I don't object to being hated Willy. But why does Butch hate? Butch calls us mafioso...he says he wants no part of us. Well Butch, you think we want any part of you? Don't think so. If you don't want to be bothered then that's fine. We offer you no help. And at this time, we do not threaten your position as UWA champion. The gold is not the biggest interest of the Syndicate right now. And we recognize the fact that taking the gold from you would not be easy. That's all I got to say. Ciccio: Hey Butch, is that what you want to hear? You want to hear that we won't interfere in your business? Hey, you got my word. And I speak for the whole Syndicate. We respect you Butch, and when we decide to take your title, we'll tell you first. After all, we're all reasonable men... (Ciccio drops the cigarette and stamps it out...then walks down the steps and continues down the sidewalk to the front gate...as the scene changes to a very dimly lit office. We see that Rex Overlord sits in a large leather chair that faces the desk...Bella Donna Di Notte at his side. At the desk we see a shadowy figure...his visibly facial features are recognizable...as is his voice...he is the Adviser) Donna: Any news on Rabecca? Overlord: We sent Willy Ciccio to the hospital. Rabecca's going to be alright. Donna: Good. I miss her around the place. Overlord: Yeah, we all do. And Vile Vince Viper, there's hell to pay for it. Donna: Let's not get into that today Rex. VVV knows that he's going down. Overlord: You're right. Stalker, I feel as though I've overlooked you. And the truth is, I have. I don't like you, and I don't like your Brotherhood. Eliminator, I respected you at Stampede. You fought on my side with Ice-Blade and brought us a great victory over the Royal Family. But that time is gone. Stalker, when I'm pummeling you into the floor, just know that it's the Eliminator's face that I'm seeing, and that yours is of little importance. Ice-Blade, know that what you did to me weeks ago has not been forgotten. And soon, I'll return the favor. (camera leaves Overlord and Donna...showing a front view of the Adviser who sits at the honorable Godfather's desk...the fireplace raging behind him...lighting the room from pitch black to a cold darkness) Adviser: Handling the business of the Godfather for now, I find it my responsibility to address the situation as a whole. But first, to Magma. Magma, I don't know who you are. I don't know what it is that you do. But let me tell you friend of Wrestlingman, that you will be pinned on the evening of the 24th of October. And on to the Urbish Mines from there. I wish you good luck, though it won't do you any good. Now, for the UWA. I hear many accusations. With the entry of the Untouchable's last Friday into the Syndicate it seems that everyone now is pretty down on the Syndicate. You're down on Rex Overlord. But look at Stampede '97. Here we saw a six man tag team match. Rex Overlord, Ice-Blade, and the Eliminator took a victory over a team that was considered great in their time. Since that match you've seen the Eliminator who bluntly turned on his fans, turned on all his friends, and turned Judgement into some 'gangsta's paradise'. The Brotherhood. And what about Ice-Blade? His actions during the past couple shows tell you all you need to know about Ice-Blade. We all thought he was a face...and he stood up tall to Overlord. But when the showdown was over, we saw him hiding in the locker room, and sending out scuzz to take care of his business for him. Now should it really be Rex and the Syndicate that your so down on? Gentlemen, stables are formed for a purpose. During the past couple weeks it has become apparent that men like Rex and valets like Rabecca Carlotti do not have eyes in the back of their head. So the Syndicate comes, and we are the third eye. Thank you... (fading to darkness) _________________________________________________________
