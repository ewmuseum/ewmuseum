Currently, no one is present, save for the man with the long raven hair in the black leather trenchcoat, black t-shirt, and jeans. He stands, one arm akimbo, the other around an attractive oriental lady, with midnight black hair down to her waist, wearing blue jeans and a tight, almost formfitting, UWA sweatshirt. Metal folding chairs are positioned neatly throughout the room, indicating that the meeting has not yet taken place. It seems that the " Walking Contradiction" has decided to say a few words before the event.* Sabbath: Welcome, here is where the contract signings for Christmas Chaos will take place...in, oh, an hour or so. I got here early so I could have a quick chat with the UWA cameracrew about some upcoming bouts. I apologize, though, as I've been quiet lately. My life has been rather hectic lately, as I've been signed to work for some other promotions as well as the UWA....they can't compete though. The UWA is where the REAL action is...but you people out there already knew that. *Sabbath grins, and removes his trenchcoat, revealing his well honed muscles, skin now pale from Winter's icy cold. His classic "Ebola Zaire" T-shirt seems a good size or two too small, as you can see every rip and contour of his massive, yet nimble frame. He places his trenchcoat on the conference table behind him, and continues to speak.* Sabbath: I'm not here to talk about my personal life though, you'll hear all about that on the upcoming UWA "Live in New York", which I'll be appearing on soon. Make sure you catch it, because you'll learn everything about the Walking Contradiction you wanted to know, but just didn't care enough to ask. Like...what does "Ebola Zaire" mean? Where did I get the name "Walking Contradiction"? What about my mysterious past? And many other questions you'd be better off missing and watching "King of the Hill" instead. Damn...I forgot... I *WILL* miss "KotH". Ah well, I'll just set the VCR to tape. *Sabbath laughs, realizing he's on a tangent. He brushes his hair out of his eyes, and sits in an adjacent chair. Sayaka sits on the table, Indian style, fiddling, quite to his annoyment, with his hair.* Sayaka: Well, I think the fans out there want to know more about you. We've been really quiet for the most part about our history and the like. You've started to get quite popular here, love, don't knock yourself. Sabbath: But it's King of the Hill! I mean, it's no "South Park", but you know. Sheesh, I gotta get on track here, or the camera's gonna leave. *Sabbath ponders, and his eyes light up with glee.* Sabbath: Ah yes! My upcoming match with "Doc Holiday", Eric Valentine. Crusher Stevenson, Scott Storm...we know little about you, but we both respect you two, as well as the rest of the Fearsome Foursome. You men are obviously enemies of Hell on Earth ...therefore, you're friends of mine. I don't think Big Butch Thornten really needed my help...he's more than capable of taking on all of Triple V's goons by himself, as is Rex Overlord...but the three of us make a good team, no? Expect more of the same, FF, if Hell on Earth tries to interfere in your business again. I'll watch your backs against them...despite my smartassed remarks towards VVV and Bobby Boy, they're dangerous men. And, no Stevenson, I'm not afraid of Hell on Earth...nor are you, I can tell. I will accept any challenge, by anyone, anytime. Hell, look at my schedule, which I'll talk more about later as well: you two with Doc Holiday, "Hollywood" Johnny Tambo, and Flaming Bob at Christmas Chaos. *Sabbath smiles an evil smile, his ice-blue eyes glowing with an almost preternatural intelligence.* Sabbath: You will not, however, have an easy time beating Valentine and I, should you lay us down in defeat at all. We're both young lions to the sport, but we have skill. And when "Death Takes A Holiday" comes for you, we'll give it all we have. I, for one, hope for a clean match, devoid of any foreign interference against either team. But if that cannot be the case, then all I can wish for is that all four of us survive with little or no injury. Eric, I look forward to teaming with you...I think we'll make a powerful force here. *He turns to Sayaka, who's still fidgeting with his hair, trying to look annoyed, but can't help smiling.* Sabbath: Stop that. Sayaka: Sorry lover. (leans over and kisses him on the forehead.) I just like to play with your hair. Now, Flaming Bob, Micheal will face you at the upcoming Christmas Chaos Pay Per View. Get ready for the fight of your life Bob. You may be the "icon of the EXTREME", but when you fight someone who has the determination, the skill, and the never-say-die-attitude like Micheal has...you can't win. And you never know...we might have a little "surprise" waiting for you. *She flashes a seductive smile to the camera.* Sayaka: And Bob...you couldn't handle me, because there's only *ONE* cowboy (wraps her arms around her husband) who can tame *THIS* (she licks her lips) little filly. *She blushes as soon as she finishes speaking. It's clear that she's used to "talkin' dirty" to Micheal alone. Micheal, on the other hand...* Sabbath: I dunno about "tame"...*I'M* the one who's having trouble walking today. *evil smile.* Sayaka: Well, that's your fault for marrying such a wild kitten. *She kisses him deeply.* Sabbath: 'twas more than worth it though, wife of mine. Sayaka: Damn straight. *sticks out her tongue.* Sabbath: Anyhow, she's right, you know, Bobby Boy? You don't mess with a man's wife, then expect him to be a pushover. The more you do, the more you rile the husband up...and I have a quick temper to begin with. I want this to be in *MY* kind of match Bobby Boy...do you have the intestinal fortitude to throw down with the "Walking Contradiction" in a Singapore Slaughter Match? Singapore Canes... closed steel cage...no ropes...only barb wire. No countout. No DQ. No rules. One winner. One survivior! I have our contract right here Bobby Boy...you'll be signing it as soon as this press conference begins. I've redrafted the stipulations, and all you have to do is agree to the new terms. Here it is Bobby Boy. ******************************************************* UWA Contract - Christmas Chaos Singapore Slaughter between "Sabbath" and "Flaming Bob" Rules: 1.) Two Singapore Canes in the ring. 2.) Steel cage, enclosed and locked. PINFALL or SUBMISSION only!!! 3.) Barb Wire around the ring. 4.) No rules! Wrestlers Signatures Sabbath - ______________________ Flaming Bob - _____________________ Managers Signatures Sayaka - ______________________ Vincent Viper - ____________________ The above signed agree to...etc...etc. ******************************************************* Sabbath: C'mon Bobby Boy. If you're so EXTREME...we'll get it on in MY style of match. *Sabbath claps his hands together.* Sabbath: "Hollywood" Johnny Tambo! I see you finally accepted my challenge. I thank you. I hope you've noticed this is a non title match though. I'm not arrogant. I just want my shot at you, to see if I'm worthy of an International title shot. If I beat you, then I'll have a title match...if I don't...back to the drawing board. You've been a great champion Tambo, but it's time for you to step aside. Sayaka: We've never been the type to brag about titles...hell we really can't. The only title Micheal has ever won was the UFWA Television belt, which he held twice. Otherwise, nothing. Kind of ironic eh? All these wrestlers go around bragging about how many belts they've held...we just get in the ring and do what we do best. Don't think just because he's never been a World Champ, he's not capable of being a success...he was the number one contender for the UFWA World Title for months upon months and never once asked for a title shot. That didn't bother us though. We just kept on kicking ass and taking names like always. Sabbath: Exactumondo! If we take that International belt, fine, if not, we'll just keep working our way up to the top until we think we're worthy of a title match. I don't think I'll have much trouble though. Look at me...I'm ranked number four here, after only a few months. If that's not success, nothing is. But I'm not the type to brag, so we'll get off that subject. *Sabbath grins.* Sabbath: Eric Valentine, we'll be teaming this Saturday. What do you think about my aforementioned name for our team? "Death Takes A Holiday"? I know, I know...we may not even keep in contact after this match...but I figure, what the hell...you're one of the most eclectic men in the UWA today...young...energetic...exuberant, the perfect tag team partner for someone like me who'll do anything from slow methodical submission wrestling, to hardcore, to highflying martial arts. We could be a huge success...maybe not. Who knows? I just think it'll be a great opportunity for the both of us. *From off camera, a male voice can be heard.* Voice: Micheal! Could ya help us out over in the Tech Room? We're having some major probs with the new fiber optic UNIX network. Sabbath *with a sour countenence*: Great...I'll be there shortly. Yeesh...some people can't even properly configure a SGI Workstation. Pathetic. Automobiles fill the streets...people fill the sidewalks. Camera aims down one of the crowded sidewalks that runs alongside many of the city's small businesses...people filing in and out of the buildings...walking back and forth up and down the walkway...from the distance is a figure...dressed in black motorcycle leather from head to toe with black shades and long jet black hair. Boys and girls can be seen flocking to him..."Sabbath! Sabbath I love you! Sabbath you're my hero!" The figure strutting down the walk turns and flips off the crowd and begins laughing as many of the children begin crying and turning away. He continues to walk down the sidewalk...nearing the camera...pushing an elderly woman off the sidewalk and into the traffic...resulting in several sounding horns. Then knocking a girl down with a shove and butting shoulders with a man with a mohawk and nearly making him lose his balance...then turning and laughing in the mans face. He approaches the camera and stops...moving his face very close and then backing away...the camera focuses as he adjusts the black whig.) Sabbath Bob: I HATE PHILLY!!!! (the crowd that has drawn boos Bob...many of them calling him "Sabbath"...as they have not gotten a good look..."Sabbath sucks!"..."Bob's gonna kick yer BLEEP!") Sabbath Bob: SHUT UP YOU FREAKIN IDIOTS!!!!!!!!! (Bob ducks inside a small restaurant...letting the cameraman follow...then shuts the door. The crowd continues shouting from outside but eventually begins to disperse. Bob laughs and removes his whig as one viewer remains...the old lady grabs her heart and gasps realizing it is Flaming Bob and backs away) Bob: Ha ha ha! Well Sabbath...just thought I should let you know that your Anti-Bob compaign is not going as well as expected. With all the television cameras on me...it must be all over the news how much of a jerk you are. And hey...some people are going to see this on the Bottom Line. But how many more are going to see the local news here in Philly? Just wanted to inform you so that you won't have a heart attack and die at Christmas Chaos when the "Go Bob" chant starts. Cameraman: What about Wrestlingman? Bob: I'm gettin to him. First let me get rid of these... (Bob throws the whig down on the floor and takes off the sunglasses...throwing them aside as well) Bob: Wrestlingman...I'm a liar. I'm a born liar. But the whopper you told yesterday on the Bottom Line really gave me the squats. You said that the last time you beat me you did it in less than 5 minutes. Well, Wrestlingpunk, you've never beaten me. In fact, we've never even fought one another. The first time I even stepped in the same ring with you was WrestleFest...and that was just to brain you with a steel chair. You've never beaten me...let's just clear that up. We weren't even in the same league together. I left in February...and you came some time later. Now I'm back...and you're telling me how you really whipped me up while I was gone. Don't think so. Nice try...but give Bobby Boy a little credit. Our match next week means about as much to me as belly button lint. I'm not going to tell you that I'm going to train or fight until the cows come home or leave home or whatever. In fact...who knows? I might not even show up. I'm funny like that you know? Bob: In the words of the legendary "Red" Rex Roseman....HA! HA! HA! HA! Cameraman: Huh? Bob: Forget it.
