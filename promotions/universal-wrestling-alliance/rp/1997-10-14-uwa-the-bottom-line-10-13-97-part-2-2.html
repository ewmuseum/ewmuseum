UWA The Bottom Line - 10/13/97 - part 2/2

THE BOTTOM LINE 2/2 ====== UWA ====== | Malibu Mike | | About: The UWA | ================= (Camera fades into Malibu Mikes Mansion. The camera is in the recreation room. In front of the camera is the ring where Malibu is wrestling some guy. To the left is the Basketball court and the HUGE swimming pool. To the right of the wrestling ring is the batting cages. And around the pool is a track. Sensational Sara is swimming with some of her friends. Sara is wearing a two piece binkini. The top is silver and the bottom is silver also! The camera pans back to Malibu in the ring. Malibu is held in a waistlock but Malibu elbows him in the face and goes off the ropes and hits a hard clothsline on the guy. Malibu picks him back up and hits a reverse knifedge. Malibu whips him to the buckles and follows up with a handspring flying elbow to the face. Malibu then grabs him by the stomach and executes a Belly to Belly suplex! Malibu covers but only gets a two count. Malibu picks him back up and puts him up on his shoulder for a running powerslam but the guy slips out and throws Malibu into the buckle and then rolls Malibu up. He gets a two count. the guy picks Malibu up and throws him to the ropes..the guy gets down for a backdrop but Malibu puts on the brakes and hits a JUMPING DDT!!!! Malibu picks him up and hits THE WIPEOUT but then climbs to the top rope jumps and hits a SUMMERSAULT LEGDROP!!! Malibu then goes to pick him up but the guy hits a SMALL PACKAGE. Two Count and Malibu kicks out and both men get up. Malibu blocks a punch and kicks the guy in the stomach and gets him up in a suplex and drops him right on his head with the MALIBU DRIVER. Malibu gets the 3 count and then gets outta the ring and grabs some spring water adn dumps it on him. Malibu then comes up to the camera and begins to talk.) Malibu: Hello..did you just see how I TOTALLY killed that man? Well thats ONLY things to come. As I am finally going to get Tambo back into the ring on Tuesday. And it wont be a good day for Tambo. You see Tambo..you got off lucky last time because I had some problems with my ribs. Well they are practically as good as new and now im going to show you what MALIBU MIKE can do! Tambo...back at Stampede you almost ended my career...so you wanna play that way then so be it because I can do what I can to INJURE YOURS! (Malibu takes a drink of the spring water) Malibu: Now to Mr. Coals..the Cruiserweight champion. You were able to defeat my partner Wrestlingman..but NOT me! I wasnt 100% and I still beat you.....doesnt that tell you something. Maybe...maybe not. But it tells me that only half of me can STILL beat you and the whole thing of me can kick your ASS. If you want a rematch just say it..I will gladly kick your ass again. Hey ill drop 30 pounds to be considered a cruiserweight and ill take that belt off of you JUST LIKE THAT! You can tell Tambo how that felt! (Malibu takes another drink) Malibu: Now to the "brotherhood". I had no problem with you guys until TAMBO joined...now me and the Perfect Coaltion are out to BREAK YOU! Hey...we will challenge you to a 3 on 3 sometime..im all for it! I want everypart of Tambo I can GET! And he knows what I can do to him. Just because hes undefeated he thinks he is the best. Well I am going to give you your first lose one way or another. And they will see why I am what I say I am..THE BEST!!!!! Now get outta here! (Fade out as Malibu gets back into the ring and hits the guy around.) ===== UWA ===== | R.A.D. | | About : UWA | =============== (R.A.D. and the Goddess are sitting on his back porch on Monday morning in Pittsburgh PA. The weather is about 65 degrees and sunny with a slight breeze. R.A.D. and the Goddess are reading the local Pittsburgh newspaper. R.A.D. is looking threw the sports section and the Goddess is looking in the business section.) R.A.D.: Man what a weekend in sports. First Penn State beats Ohio State, The Penquins behind goals from Francis, Jagr and Barnes beat the lowly Carolina Hurricanes and then the Pittsburgh Steelers beat the pitiful Colts. Well honestly the Steelers should have lost that game for having six turnovers I mean six! All that counts is another W in the Win lose column. (R.A.D. folds up the Sports section and puts it on the patio table) R.A.D.: You know over the time I’ve been in the UWA I’ve had multiple people attacking me. Either in the ring or outside the ring because everybody wants a piece of me. Case in point on Friday. There I was minding my own business with the A.O.A. in the parking lot when the Bad Boyz show up and start to run their mouths. The next thing I know the Bad Boyz are attacking me and the A.O.A. After the A.O.A. defend off the Bad Boyz the Brotherhood shows up and attacks me and the A.O.A. But in the end the A.O.A. came out on top. I guess the A.O.A. is so popular that everyone wants a piece of the best. But then later in the night I was supposed to face Ice-Blade in a best of three falls match. Of course Ice-Blade the chicken he is sends a man down to the ring dressed like him with a mask. I guess Ice-Blade knew that by getting in the ring with me he wouldn’t have a chance. So I dispose of the fake Ice-Blade and who shows up but mystery man 2356. Mystery man 2356 taps me on the shoulder then hits me with a steel chair. Ice-Blade what you did to me on Friday tells me that you are a yellow bellied, scum sucking, moms boy piece of slime. Honestly Ice-Blade I think you have PMS! Yeah that’s right you heard me Ice-Blade. I said last week to D-Man that he should join the PC but from what you did on Friday I’m taking that back. Now D-Man can do what he wants but if he joins up with that coward then something is truly wrong. Ice-Blade you just cost yourself some points in the ranking and by me winning on Friday I’m back on top. Ice-Blade your an idiot. (R.A.D. takes a drink of orange juice) R.A.D.: Eliminator tomorrow night it will be you and me in a steel cage and I can’t wait. I’ve been chomping at the bit to get you in the ring so we can finish this thing off once and for all. It seems to me on Friday that you were scared in attacking me in the parking lot. From what I heard you stood around and watched. I don’t know who is a bigger chicken you or Ice-Blade. No matter the point is tomorrow night be ready for the beating of your life. (R.A.D. takes another drink of orange juice) R.A.D.: What’s a matter Sandman a little hot under the collar? Now you know what it is like to have someone unknown to you hiding in the shadows everywhere you go. If you think that I going to give a hint on who he is I’m not. I want to see you sweat Sandman. You say your from a former fed that I was apart of. Well the past feds I was apart of I kicked everyone’s butt and I didn’t take any crap from anybody. As for your identity I know who you are so I’ll bring my mystery partner with me on Friday, Sunday, Tuesday or whenever. And by the way Sandman calling him names is not a good thing because you see he has a temper and …. Opps I almost gave away a clue. Ahhh No No No Sandman. Here’s the contract Sandman: R.A.D. Mystery Man vs Zodiac Sandman x R.A.D x Mystery Man vs x ___________ x ___________ R.A.D. & Mystery Man win Sandman has to reveal identity Zodiac & Sandman win Mystery Man has to reveal identity. R.A.D.: There you go brothers. And I can’t understand why your flashes are coming from your homes and were you grew up. I mean you keep looking for me in garbage dumps but I don’t live their you guys do. You know when I was sitting in the back on Sunday and saw Johnny ,Don’t call me Rambo, Tambo turned on you I couldn’t help but laugh. Did you really think that Rambo wanted to join your stupid Brotherhood. I saw this coming along. Goddess: Zodiac I hear your still spreading rumors about me but the only your spreading around is cow manure so Zodiac just give it up. Nothing you do can take me away from my man. (R.A.D. kicks up his feet on the patio table and cross his arms behind his head) R.A.D.: Now before I go I have heard another challenge was presented to me on Friday by a man called "Mr. Money" Johnny Chambers. Mr. Cambers says he has lots of money and that I’m not R.A.D. Well Chambers what have you done in the UWA? What’s that I can’t hear you…..Speak up….what?….Nothing just as I thought. Chamber’s your all mouth and personally I’m getting tried of people like you Chambers. Chambers all you have to do is just sign your name and the time and place. And when we meet Chambers I’m going to stuff those 100 dollar bills down your throat. Then off course take the rest and throw it in the crowd. (Camera fades to black) ======================== UWA ============================= | The Syndicate | | About: Big Butch Thornton, the Tribe, & Vile Vince Viper | ========================================================== (scene opens to a garden...the trees have turned golden...and the leaves are blowing this way and that about the yard. Sitting on a white park bench of sorts is Rex Overlord...who is joined by Bella Donna Di Notte...a gust sends the leaves rustling across the lawn) Donna: Just when you think it's safe. Rabecca Carlotti, long time trainer of Rex Overlord and a good frint of mine, returned to the corner of the Syndicate last night. Last time it was myself who took the blow. Last time a psychotic who went by the name of Flaming Bob put ME into a hospital for well over a month. But filling in the void left by Flaming Bob...is none other than Vile Vince Viper. Overlord: Vile Vince Viper...it is a mystery to me why you continue to press on...why you hit the Syndicate like you do...when you know that you have our attention. (pauses) Never in the history of my life have I ever felt such an undying hate in my gut. Many times you've come and attacked me...put me down...put me in the hospital. And I've been angry...oh yes. But when it came down to the core of the situation...I understood. What we have is a war Vince Viper...and it will be fought like a war...MAN TO MAN!! AND I'VE GONE FOR MY WHOLE CAREER WITHOUT CARING! WITHOUT TAKING THINGS PERSONAL!! BECAUSE IT'S UNPROFESSIONAL IN THE BUSINESS WORLD TO HATE YOUR ENEMIES!! IT'S UNFAIR IN THE BUSINESS WORLD TO TAKE THINGS PERSONAL!!! But no longer is this business...and no longer is this a feud between two wrestlers. Because now it's time that I get a little unprofessional...and now it's time that I quit caring about who I hurt...AS LONG AS I HURT!!! The monster awaits my friend...and you've awoken the sleeping monster. I am that monster...and on the 26th of October...you face that monster. (scene fades to black...then opens again to a dimly lit kitchen where Rocco Brazzi is sitting at the table smoking a cigarette...Willy Ciccio sitting on a stool at the kitchen counter waiting on a phone call) Ciccio: Hey Rocco...did Baker say 7 o'clock? [looks at his watch] Brazzi: Yeah...7 o'clock Willy. Ciccio: Well we've waited long enough. I've got more important business to attend. First off I want to say a word to Big Butch Thornton...and ask why he's so down on the Untouchables? We never did nothin that's any of your concern Thornton. We don't even come down to the match on Sunday. So what's the harsh words against Rocco and me? We're a tag team...and part of a stable just like you and your Devastation Crew. So give it a rest huh? You're going to have Rex and us a bad rep before we can even wrestle. Brazzi: Butch, I'm not too concerned with what you have to say about me, Willy, the Godfather, whatever. In time when you see a little bit more of the Syndicate then you'll not judge us so harshly. Now the important thing is this...Vile Vince Viper hits Miss Rabecca with a cane. And then on the other side...we see Chris Simmons jumping out of the crowd with a chair and pummeling Butch Thornton? What was the big idea Chris? You think you're going to get a title shot for that? Ciccio: Yeah, give us a break Chris. Brazzi: But Butch don't fall...and what even makes things worse is when Overlord cleans is clock and tosses him out like last weeks garbage. Chris if you want a chance then I'm sure Butch will give you one. Like he's said, he's never backed down from a challenge. So get in the ring with him...or with one of us. I saw your debut match against Jean Guy Lavelle and I must say that I was not impressed. Any time...just name the date. (telephone rings) Ciccio: Finally. (picks up the phone) Yeah. That you Baker? Yeah...there's an opening... (fades to black)
