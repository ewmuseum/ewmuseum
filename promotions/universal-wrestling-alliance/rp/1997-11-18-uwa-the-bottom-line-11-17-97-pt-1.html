Tambo, Flaming * * Bobby Boy, Ice-Blade, Buzz Craven, * * Frank N. Stein!!!!!!!!! LISTEN!!! * ************************************** *Camera fades in to the one true Walking Contradiction. The man the UWA has come to know, love...and, quite possibly, fear as "Sabbath." Around him we see a wrestling ring, workout equipment, some with more the appearance of medeival torture than modern fitness machines, and other paraphanelia. Two double doors are on either side of the room, with two German men at each, with navy blue jumpsuits labeled "Strauheim Security." Sabbath is wearing his usual wrestling attire: long black tights, with the word "Sabbath" stitched down the sides in blood red. He's wearing a wry grin on his face, almost as if he knows something you don't, which is never a good sign.* Sabbath: Welcome, one and all, to the Pit, where the members of the Harbingers of the Eternal come to become all they can be, and a little bit more. Flaming Bibbity-bobbity-bob, they say imitation is the sincerest form of flattery, and despite your rather shabby impersonation of me, I'll take it as a compliment just because I'm in a good mood today, lucky for you. Hell on Earth, Hell on Earth, yo Momma shoulda smacked you 'round at Birth! Bobby-Boy, I've been to hell and back and it's not a pretty sight, but I survived, so bring your hell to the UWA. Natural Selection is the way of pro-wrestling, and like I have all my life, I'll survive because I am the fittest. You seem to be a pretty tough hombre Bobby Boy. *Sabbath chuckles a little to himself, striking a heroic akimbo pose.* Sabbath: Welly, welly, well. So you think you could survive my type of match? A Singapore Cane, barb wire 'round the ring, closed steel cage? I call it Singapore Slaughter, but anyone who wants to face me in it can call it their Last Match. Hmm...I'll get back to you shortly Bobby-Boy. Speaking of the Singapore Slaughter match, how would you like to make our little Spin-The-Well/Make-The-Deal match at Wrestle Fest II a little more interesting Ice-Blade? By adding the SS match to that little list. Whaddaya say? It's EXTREME. Oh, Lordy is it EXTREME. I don't even know who you are Ice-Blade...I just saw an opportunity for me to show the UWA what I'm made of. So far, no one in the UWA has been able to give me a challenge. What about you? Challenges make me happy. Make me happy. And you say I was "brave enough?" What makes it brave if I fear no man? I'll get into the ring with anyone in the UWA. If I win, that's great. If I lose, so be it, I just wasn't the better man. As for the height and weight thing, it might mean something, as my record against larger men isn't the best. Six of my eight career losses have been against men your size. Of course, I have beaten some big men, just not that many. But I think in this type of match, my speed and agility will make up for it. But anyway, lets take a look at some of these matches... *Sabbath pulls the list of matches from his right boot, and unfolds it, clearing his throat.* =========================================== 12 Stipulations on the "Wheel of Fate" Sabbath: Ooooh..."Wheel of Fate." Scary... 1. Steel Cage Match Sabbath: Okay, number one: Steel Cage Match. I don't see where this is extreme, but oh, well. 2. Weapons Match Sabbath: Number Two: A weapons match. Sounds like it'll be a lot of fun, reminds me of my days back in Singapore. 3. Barbed Wire Match Sabbath: AH! Now Number Three: is more like it! 4. Strap Match Sabbath: Boooooooooooooring! 5. Texas Death Match Sabbath: Ehhh...could be interesting. 6. KO Match Sabbath: Please... 7. Submission Match Sabbath: You don't want to go into a submission match with me. 8. Boiler Room Match Sabbath: Hmmm....... 9. Table Match Sabbath: Hey, I've seen one of these go for over two hours in the past. Think we could do better? 10. Parking Lot Brawl Sabbath: Yawn... 11. Finisher Match Sabbath: Ummm. 12. Anything Goes Match Sabbath: Okay...this I like. So what say we substitute KO Match with the Singapore Slaughter? If you're the EXTREME Icon, and I don't doubt that you are, then you'll be more than willing to accept that. *Sabbath smiles into the camera, then begins walking towards the ring.* Sabbath: Now, onto some challenges. Buzz Craven, and Frank N. Stein. You still haven't responded. Sheesh! I don't get no respect! The very least you could do is decline. Okay, now, the big challenge. I'll spell this one out. *Sabbath 'ahems.'* Sabbath: "HOLLYWOOD JOHNNY TAMBO!!!!!" Yes, you the UWA International Champion. I gots a match for you. You and me, one on one, no DQ, no Countout, no outside interference. If I win, I'll get a shot at the UWA International title. Well, this really goes to whomever is the International champion at the time, but since the current champ is Tambo, the challenge goes out to him. C'mon...surely a meager rookie like me can't be too much trouble. Can I? *Sabbath hops up onto the ring apron, and pulls back on the ropes, stretching.* Sabbath: Okay, back to you Bobby Boy. "God of the Extreme?" Just because you're a sicko who likes to hurt people? Come on. I've seen plent o' people like that, and I've beaten 'em. Ever hear of a legend of in the sport of wrestling, a man called Tommy Nightmare? Had an eight hour match with some other guy. I've made him submit. TWICE. I've also beaten his stablemates, who are among the sickest people I've met. So don't come to me wanting me to throw you scraps of respect just because you jump on tacks and hurt yourself. That isn't going to work. The way to earn my recognition is to get in the ring and fight me, doesn't matter if you win or lose. Give me a good match, and I'll congratulate you. But not yet, I have too many challenges up. But keep it in mind Bobby Boy. *Sabbath leaps up to the top turnbuckle, balancing on the precarious perch, arms akimbo again.* Sabbath: UWA, I'm coming for Wrestlefest II, and Ice-Blade, I wish you the *VERY* best of luck. But you will prove to be yet another victim of the Black Sabbath, as I will take not only your dignity, but your soul. Keep in mind the words of Jesse Dupree from Jackyl: *Camera pulls in for a tight shot.* Sabbath: "Hello everybody, welcome to the show. We're headed for destruction..." *Fade to black.* Sabbath(OOV): "Don't you wanna go?" *Sabbath laughs evily as "Horror Business" by the Misfits begins to play.* ____________________________________________________ "Golden Boy" Lawrence Stevenson About: Rex Overlord Lawrence Stephenson is about to get into his shinny blue Corvette when he is approached by UWA reporters. About: Scotty May, BBA, Goliath, D-Man, VVV ================================== (The camera pans onto the house of R.A.D. in the Southern part of Pittsburgh. Its Friday evening and their is a inch of fresh snow on the ground. Snow flurries are in the air. The camera changes scenes into R.A.D.'s house. R.A.D. is sitting on his couch watching Friday Night Fights. A warm fire is coming from the near by fireplace. R.A.D. grabs the remote and truns the T.V. off. R.A.D. starts to talk to the camera.) R.A.D.: Nice weather outside huh. This is the time of the year were the weather turns and you need to fire up the fireplace to keep nice and toasty. (R.A.D. gets up and grabs a piece of fire wood from a steel barrel. R.A.D. walks over to the fireplace and places the piece of wood on the fire. R.A.D. walks back over to the couch and sits down.) R.A.D.: First I would like to make an apology to the fans and the members of the UWA.....I apologize for hitting the Eliminator so many times in the head that he can't think straight any more. I mean after I came off the top of the cage with a steel chair to the head of the Eliminator he hasn't been the same. The Eliminator comes out week after week and babbels about this and that and the fans of the UWA just ignore him.......The Eliminator has said on various Bottom lines that he has respect for the R.A.D. man. Well if I had faced an opponent who beat me sensless match after match I would have respect for him too......But in my opinion the Eliminator doesn't want any part of me. The Eliminator knows that if he gets in the ring with me he will be defeated again. The Eliminator has told me to stay out of his way.....I think it is the other way around in that the Eliminator should stay out of my way.....Now this coming Friday I will be facing Scotty Motty Potty May. I've heard you Motty Potty May run your mouth about how you are the best wrestler in the world. I must say you have confidence in yourself but you haven't wrestled the ultimate wrestling machine...that being me. This Friday Scotty Motty Potty May you better bring everything including the kitchen sink if you expect to beat me......The last time we were face to face you hit me with a brief case over my head and knocked me out....I haven't forgotten about it and this Friday I'm going to get my revenge. (The Goddess walks in and sits next to R.A.D.) R.A.D.: Tonight I see my Mystery men were looking for people that I put boundies out on. Pain you and your boys better keep a eye over your shoulder because they might be coming for you. ...The Bad Boy Alliance had some sense knocked into them by the A.O.A. after their cheap victory. I'm sorry that I wasn't there to kick some sense into Johnny Coals myself. But after Wrestlefest II you so-called hotest wrestlers in the world today are going to be facing me and my mystery men in a six man tag. So BBA you guys better get ready because we are coming into that match to hurt you. We don't care if we win or lose. The A.O.A. wants to punsh you guys at any means possible.....Speaking of Wrestle FestII it seems that the A.O.A. only has six members. I thought we had eight but we only have six. And at Wrestle FestII my team is going to be facing the Demolishers. Now only having three people on my team I need another member. And the only member in the UWA that can be on my team is the D-Man. Like I have said before he is the only wrestler in the UWA I have respect for and it would be a privlege to have him on my team. D-Man if you accept the offer great and if you don't I won't be disappointed. The offer is on the table. Goddess: What about the Goliath this Friday Night. I mean what was that. R.A.D.: A few weeks ago I came out on Friday Night Fights and told the fans of the UWA that I was sick of wrestlers not showing for matches or walking out on matches. As a man of my word Goliath you are going to answer to me. Gordon I want a match with the 7 foot freak. The fans of the UWA shouldn't be ripped off by wrestlers walking out of matches. And a second wrestler is Vile Vince Viper. At this time some other wrestlers in the UWA have your number Vile so I'm going to let you slide but not you Goliath. Well that's all for now. I have to get ready and catch my flight to Toronto to see Super Mario be inducted into the Hockey Hall of Fame.
