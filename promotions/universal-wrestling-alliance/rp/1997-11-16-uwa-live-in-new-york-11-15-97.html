UWALive In New York 11/15/97

16 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live In New York - Episode 9 (‘House of Pain' from Van Halen's 1984 album fades up from the background as the camera pans a busy 5th avenue...cars, buses, and yellow taxi cabs inching their way through town. A man in a black suit steps out of an alley and turns the corner...passing clothing stores and window shoppers...panning out...the bright lights...the big city...a view of the Empire State building...back to the man who cuts between two scantily clad and flirtacious women...into a yellow cab...the camera then shows the ‘Live In New York' studio with people filing in through two separate lines...then a yellow cab and the man in the suit stepping out...cutting through the crowd and running into a full auditorium of applauding fans...then back stage) (Scene cuts to center stage and a voice from the background) Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen...welcome to Live In New York! Here are your hosts...Ron Donovan and Johnathan Tell! (Crowd breaks into cheers as Ron Donovan enters from the left and Johnathan Tell from the right. Donovan is 6'0" with short blonde hair and looks to be about 35 years old. Tell is 6'4" and has short brown hair. He is muscular in build and looks to be in his late twenties. Both men meet at center stage and shake hands before turning to bow to their studio audience) Donovan: Well it's great to be back after a weeks absence here on Live In New York. A lot has happened since we've been gone. When I say a lot, I mean the collapse of the Syndicate, the return of Flaming Bob, but mainly...the three consecutive successful title defenses of Vile Vince Viper. Tell: And it's that just great. He rips the title off of Big Butch Thornton...cheats Rex Overlord...cheats Suicide...and then he cheats Malibu Mike. But soon his luck is going to wear out. And it will be at WrestleFest '97 against Wrestlingman. Donovan: It may very well be. Certainly a dark time here in the UWA. We saw just six days ago...the return of Flaming Bob. This guy a maniac. For those of you that don't know him...look into old UWA records. You'll find his name I assure you. He is back. Tell: If anyone is as sick and twisted as Vile Vince Viper, it's Flaming Bob. Donovan: You may be correct. But judging by Malibu Mike's claims, Rex Overlord rates right up there. Tell: Well I'd rather not get into that Donovan. I don't know what's going on with Overlord...don't know that I really want to right now. He's always been a little off his rocker as far as I'm concerned. Donovan: Alright...let's not keep you in suspense any longer. Jonathan, would you do the honors? Jonathen Tell: Ladies and Gentlemen it is my pleasure to introduce to you our very special guest. He is the showstopper, the main event, the icon of the UWA and the leader of what is becoming the greatest force in the UWA today Natural Currency! Ladies and Gentlemen "The Money Man"........CASTER TROY!!!! <Donovan sits at the desk and Tell at the sofa to it's left in a Tonight Show type setting..."Too Sexy!" begins to play as Caster Troy dances his way out onto the stage and begins stripping for the ladies who are screaming and trying to grab him the music cuts before he reveals anything private and Caster sits down on the end of the couch closest to the desk> Ron Donovan: Its good to have you hear tonight Caster Caster Troy: Well, Well, WWWWWWEEEELLLLLLL!!!!!! I am the man with the master plan and I wish i could say the same about you Donovan baby! <Tell and the audience begins to chuckle> Ron Donovan: Getting right down to business Caster last Friday Night we watched in shock as you turned your back on Solid Gold! In fact I think we have a clip of that! Roll the clip please! <The lights in the studio dim as the video wall shows Daniels being dominated in the ring by Maxwell. The ref gets knocked around on the outside and is out of it. The camera then shows Caster Troy get in the ring and back Maxwell into the turnbuckle with a chair and then spin around and clock Daniels with it! The video then cuts to Troy, Maxwell and Pain piledriving both Daniels and Tambo through tables just after showing Tambo get clocked by Troy's "Billion Dollar Blast"! As the lights turn back up the crowd is going nuts and cheering NC...NC...NC while Caster is sitting back and chuckling.> Caster Troy: That just gets better and better every time you see it don't it? Jonathen Tell: It sure does cause wether Solid Gold likes it or not they were outsmarted and outclassed! Ron Donovan: Why did you do it Caster? What made you turn on Solid Gold? Caster Troy: Its simply really baby! I couldn't stand that big mouth Scott Daniels pretendin like everybody came to see him every night and not me! He thought that he was the brains and ran the joint but I guess I proved him wrong! Jonathen Tell: Caster at WrestleFest II you and Natural Currency will meet Solid Gold in an 8 Man Elimination Tag Team Match! Everyone is wondering who will the fourth member of Natural Currency? We already now it will be you Maxwell and Pain but who will be the 8th member? Caster Troy: Thats for my and my boys to know and for you to find out daddio! All I can tell you is that Solid Gold is gonna know him real well cause he is one of theirs who is about to follow in my footsteps and come over to NC!!!! Ron Donovan: That brings us to Tuesday Night. After you stated that another member of Solid Gold was about to change their legance Daniels and Tambo fired the Golden Boy with a capital "F"!!!!! Now you guys claimed that Solid Gold got the wrong man!!!! Caster Troy: Its like I said RD NC is a couple steps ahaead of everybody else and we can't wait to see Daniels and Tambo goin nuts tryin to figure out who is the turncoat! Its already rippin them apart! They knocked the hell outta one of their own but got the wrong guy! We are taking Solid Gold apart without even stepin into the ring baby!!!!!!! Solid Gold you'll see who the turncoat is at WrestleFest II or maybe even before then!!!!!!!!!!! Jonathen Tell: Now what about this whole contract thing with Solid Gold that we saw on Tuesday Night? Solid Gold calimed that you were still under contract had you ushered out tto the ring by cops and then beat the hell outta you! Contract: Johnny daddio he who laughs last laughs best and Solid Gold NC is defenitely gettin the last laugh cause not only are we tearing you apart at the seams neither of you two idiots have the balls to take care of the problem! Your callin us liars now but one of you knows that if your wrong then your gonna get your ass kicked by the guy you trusted most baby!!!!!!!! Ron Donovan: Well thanks for being here tonight Caster and good luck to you and the rest of Natural Currency at WrestleFest II! Caster Troy: We don't nedd luck baby cause we are the showstoppers, the main event, the icon of the UWA!!!!!!!! NC is the way to be! NC4eva!!!!!!!!!!!! Donovan: Mr. Troy, thanks a great deal for appearing on the show. I'm afraid we're out of time. So...thank you everyone for watching. Be back next week for another episode. And if you're a deer hunter...good luck! Goodnight! (fades to black) 1997 UWA Sports™ All Rights Reserved
