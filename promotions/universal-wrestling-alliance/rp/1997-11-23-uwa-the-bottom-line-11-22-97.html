What better to start with, than one of the latest acquisitions of the UWA, Sabbath, and his lovely bride, Sayaka. GG: What is your response to Flaming Bob's remarks about an affair with Sayaka in France years ago. *Sabbath ponders, sarcastically, for a few moments.* Sabbath: Bull[BEEP!]. That's all Flaming Bob is. I've spent my entire life with Sayaka since I was three years old. I know what she's done, and what she hasn't. She has always been faithful to me, as I her. Sayaka: Exactly. And Bob, your comments to me during the press conference were sick! What is your problem? You're an impressive wrestler, but you had no business asking a question like that. I wouldn't touch you for the world. As I've said in the past, the only man I've had sex with in my life is Micheal. We believe in abstinence outside of marriage. *Sayaka casts a wary glance to an outside window, perhaps looking to see if Flaming Bob is somewhere waiting.* Sayaka: And what you did to that poor girl at the beach was disgusting. If you try a stunt like that with me, I'll rip your balls off and shove them down your throat. And I can do it too. *Sayaka's face is awash with a crimson glow, part anger, part embarassment. Her small form (5'7", 115, but *SURPRISINGLY* strong) presses up against Micheal's tuxedoed body.* Sabbath: Flaming Bob, don't test my patience. I don't care how impervious to pain you are. I can find ways to hurt you. If you want a fight, let's do it in the ring. *Sabbath smiles.* Sabbath: On to more pleasant matters. Ice-Blade, the day of our battle draws nearer by the day, and I hope it will be a clean one, devoid of any outside interference. Sayaka: Indeed. Miss Kryonik, this coming Sunday, we'll be looking across the ring at one another as our respective charges do battle. All I ask from you is that you treat me with respect, and so I shall you. You're seem to be a decent lady, and some of the comments made towards you are uncalled for. I, for one, will treat you with courtesy and regard, and I would wish for you to give me the same. Sabbath: Whatever the wheel lands upon, Ice-Blade, we will be resigned to settle matters with those stipulations. I know I won't back down, nor shall you. Get ready Ice-Blade. The Black Sabbath awaits!!! *Everyone looks over at Sabbath, perhaps thinking "Oooooookay."* Sabbath: Ummm...sorry. *Fade to black.* ___________________________________________________ Nick "The Punisher" Williams and X-Treme Flash About: Natural Currency, Caster Toy,Chris Simmons (Scene opens with Nick Williams watching wrestling on TV) Nick Williams: Watch this move.
