Stein, UWA * ************************************ *"Every other week I need a new address...landlord, landlord, landlord, cleanin' up the mess..." X's "We're Desperate" can be heard blaring as you approach the large wooden door with two crisscrossed Broadswords etched into it. The door swings open and you find yourself with the Walking Contradiction, Sabbath (Micheal Price) and his one true love and faithful wife, Sayaka Daiken-Price. Sabbath is sitting shirtless on a swivel chair in front of a desk, his long raven hair tied into a ponytail reaching about three inches past his shoulders. He's typing on his laptop, a Sun SPARC 150 Mhz UNIX Workstation. Sayaka is relaxing on the bed, wearing her traditional black jeans and t-shirt combo.* Sabbath: Damn...for some reason this [BEEP!]ing port scanner won't work. I've compiled it three times, but I keep getting weird errors. Sayaka: I think we have company love. Sabbath: Oh! Hi there. Welcome to Valhalla, my home. It's recently been redecorated for the Harbingers of the Eternal to make their nest here. Luckily for the UWA, however, they won't be joining me. Well. Guess what? The Adviser and I beat the Slugs for Life. But, was there ever any doubt that it would be any different? Hmmm...no, I think not. Slugs, you put up a good fight, I'll give you that. But, what do you do against a man who cannot be hurt? NOTHING! That's what! You got a little taste of us the other night as well. I think I've made my mark in the UWA now. Sayaka: Slugs, my name is "Sayaka", not Teriakie. Then again, you aren't the most intelligent human specimens on the planet. Far from it. You don't know when to shut up do you? That's twice my husband has beaten you. And you still open your fat mouths. Why don't you quit while you're ahead and go feud with someone you have a chance of winning against. The Adviser and Sabbath were just too much for your simple intellects. *Sabbath laughs, and shuts his laptop down. He then picks up his custom designed black Fender Stratocaster with mother of pearl Celtic cross inlays and starts fiddling around with a mid-fifties rock 'n' roll riff, a la Chuck Berry.* Sabbath: Frank N. Stein...Buzz Craven...why haven't you two responded yet? You saw what happened to Mage when he didn't? I don't want the same thing to happen to you. I really don't. I hate to humiliate someone. I do. Seriously. Cross my heart. But, I will. And you know it. I take this business very seriously, and it's an insult to me when my challenges are ignored. *Sabbath plays another riff, then sets the guitar down. It's easy to tell he's bored. After all, competition in the UWA has been pitiful to say the least. Perhaps he should declare war, as he has in the past? There's a certain thrill knowing that everyone is out to get you. It's not the smartest move though, especially when you're so unknown. Best to get a reputation first.* Sabbath: I need to wrestle again. If you two will not respond, then I'll simply pass you over. Maybe that's what you want. Not that I blame you. I wouldn't want to fight me either. I'm a scary guy to be in the ring with. Know what I mean? Probably not. But, oh well. *Sabbath leans back and props his feet up on his desk.* Sabbath: Adviser, you made a great tag team partner. We were fluid in that ring, almost like one. That's what makes a good team. I understand the Syndicate is having problems at the moment. Well, since you did this favor for me, I'll be more than willing to back you guys up if I'm ever needed. I had a great time out there. A bit too easy, but when two men of our caliber team up against two lower lifeforms such as the Slugs it's not going to really be a challenge. Why Rex Overlord did what he did, I don't know. I just wish I could've seen it coming, then I could've stopped him possibly. I truly apologize for not helping you at that time. Thus, I'm even moreso in your debt. *Sabbath ponders for a moment.* Sabbath: Hey, Triple V, Vile Vince Viper. You seem like a challenge. What say you and I, in the ring? Non-title match, but with these stipulations. *NO* outside interference, steel cage, Singapore Cane, barb wire in place of ring ropes match, that if I win, I'll get a UWA World's Title shot? You seem to be a hardcore individual...well, can you survive a hardcore match? I can. As a matter of fact, I'm undefeated in them: my record is winning one in thirteen seconds. I have a UFWA world's title shot coming up...it would be nice to have the UWA World's Title around my waist as well. But then, I'm just that good. Now, VVV, I have nothing against you. I'm here for competition, not to make enemies. Just tell me what you think. *Sabbath smiles evily into the camera.* Sabbath: Imagine that, not even here for a few months, and being the UWA world's champion. Fascinating isn't it? Heh heh. Even if Vince declines, I'll go for some gold soon, and very soon. I think I can do it. Be it the Intercontinental belt or whatever. Titleholders beware the coming Sabbath! Sayaka: Please excuse him...he's a little hyper at the moment. That's just the way he is coming off of a win like last night's. *Sabbath smiles at Sayaka* Sayaka: Love, are we all still going to see the Buzzcocks in London tonight? Sabbath: Yeah, I think so. We need to get going if we are. Sayaka: Okay, I'll go tell everyone, and you can finish up here. Sabbath: Alrighty lover. *Sayaka gets up and leaves the room.* Sabbath: Well, then. I've got a concert to go to. Triple V, think about my little challenge. Just remember I don't like to be kept waiting. *Sabbath grins and the scene fades to black as "Johny Hit And Run Paulene" by X plays.* ____________________________________________________ Who: Hollywood Johnny Tambo About: Big Butch Thorton,Eliminator,Natural Currency (camera fades in to the pretty secretary at Sold Gold, Inc.) Girl: And now, ladies and gentlemen, Big Butch Thornton... Big Bitch Thorndyke, you've made a habit of ripping off other people's ring entrances, theme music, and interviews, but now it comes down to this...you gotta walk that aisle...sorry, I couldn't refuse...you gotta step into that ring with someone who is confident enough of himself that he doesn't need to copy off already tried and true routines. Hollywood Johnny Tambo is a REAL man who doesn't hide behind the skirts of his stablemates. Thorndyke, this is your one and only shot at MY title. Revel in it. And as for the Eliminator, sorry that you weren't granted the shot. It appears that our good Prez Gordon has decided that you were a no-talent bum, undeserving of the opportunity. I guess he must listen to what the fans say after all. So hey, bud, don't take it out on me with your poorly created and dull interviews, go whine to Die Fuhrer. If you are still interested, though, after I soundly defeated Thorndyke, I still grant you a shot at the belt. Take that up with the Board of Commissioners, though, pally, I don't do booking... Oh, and, uh, Natural Currency. Here is knowledge...its funny that you guys are over there at your little boring embassy saying that you pulled a good one over Solid Gold's eyes with the divide and conquer routine. What you guys didn't know, and you can confirm this with my super partner, Scotty D., we were planning on throwing you two out shortly after you joined up. Your interviews were embarassing and pointless. You simply didn't live up to Solid Gold standards. The Mock Horsemen is a good home for you, by the way, Larry Stephensuck, now your can combine your ineptness and make one big package of crud. I think you boys should consider joining that federation that was advertised at the beginning of the Bottom Line. I'm sure the beginners there won't even realize that you kids have been doing this for awhile. You won't stand out there....but then...you really don't stand out in the UWA either. Sorry fellas, thats just the way it is. And Merwyn...you're gone.... (scene opens to the highway leading out of Detroit...Flaming Bob is walking...blue jeans and a denim jacket...black combat boots...he is 6'4"...265lbs...short brown hair...holding a thumb up and toward the oncoming traffic...no one stopping...no one slowing down for a closer look...Bob's look is unchanged...his pace is quick...his thumb is...tall) Bob: City to city...all over the globe...(continueing his walk). I see unhappy faces...sense broken hearts...unsympathetic eyes...a world of zombies. Society eats...like a vampire...society steals...a den of thieves. With tongues like whips...eyes like daggers...artificial smiles...painted on faces like some porcelain doll. While stars blink in the heavens...on and off...years passing like seconds...the timelessness of space...our young planet...like a child...but with all the capabilities of a raging hurricane. Call it nuclear...call it what you will. (dropping his hand to his side he walks down a slope and into a stand of trees...continueing his walk through the woods) Bob: It's what I wake up to...every day over morning coffee. It's on the television...in the papers. And Teddy says..."My you are observant Bob", and "You really have an ear for this Bob." And what can I say? With so many different voices. "Bob, you're gifted." "Bob, you're cursed." "I wouldn't want to be in you're shoes Bob." So I say, when is it Bob's time? (smiling...darkness overcomes him as he enters the thick of the woods) Bob: I have not forgotten the Pizzaman of old. I have not forgotten Marduk. And the Godfather...I have not forgotten you. I grasp my origins with all that's inside me...and I do what I can to fulfill my calling. Suicide...you're burns are not permanent...but you're scars. My burns...my burns are permanent. My life...it's a beautiful life. You're life... (fades to black) ___________________________________________________
