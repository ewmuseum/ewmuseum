Simmons, etc. =========================================================== ("Suicide" Johnny Pain and "The Cannon" Christopher Maxwell are found at a personally owned restaurant of "Natural Currency", they are seen eating various kinds of food, in which includes Shrimp, Salmon, Steak, nothing but the finest. The table cloth in which they eat upon has the "NC 4va'" symbol located in the center of it, the rims of the table cloth has money signs {$} closely lined together all around. Pain and Maxwell are dressed in formal attire. UWA cameraman approaches them, camera zooms in.) Pain: Ho, Ho, Ho...Christmas has answered to the golden girls of Solid Gold. They have an elite edition, of Chris Simmons. Simmons, I know you have high potential, expressed greatly there at Wrestlefest, it hurts to see that the likes of you would join an untalented group, such as the likes of Solid Gold. Although, there are few things I completely disagree with you, one, obviously is the fact of why you'd join jobbers as such, and secondly, I don't quite recall it being "SG 4eva'"... Am I missing something??? Simmons,...boy,...Solid Gold went out with the bell bottoms, and they damn well was lights out for them at Wrestlfest!!!! Pain: NATURALLY!!!! Now, another thing, I believe I pinned you...fair, and *SQUARE*!!! Again, I'm not gonna sit here as you claim and state that, oh..."Natural Currency did this...Naturally Currency did that!!!"...cause again, that's just the way it is!!!! Pain: NAT-UR-ALLY!!!! (chews on some steak) (a few chuckles) Maxwell: But, I am asking, nearly requesting a match against you...considering that you remain the International champion after your fued with refriger-ator, etc. is complete. Just a completely normal match up...(pauses to take a bite of salmon)...I can stress that providing the other members of Solid Gold do not interfere, neither will Natural Currency... As we will have our round three inside the ring, the final chapter to our never ending book...(takes a sip of club soda.)...*YOU* and *I*!!! Get back to me with that...and if you prove the better man, I'm not going to sit here, wine, cry, like a suck much like Malibu Mike, and the Biggest B*tch Alive, big B*tch Thorton... Pain: Oh...Yeah...Yeah...hey!! I believe I heard a few word outta' his oversized mouth in the previous Bottom Line...roll the tape please.... (The camera turns to the 60" television set located in the bar area.) ----Big Butch Thorton: One more thing before this little interview goes to a close. Natural Currency, if those kind words you directed toward us were supposed to be some sort of a piece offering, well you can shove those words right up your *ss. The respect I have for you punks is minimal to none. You and Solid Gold are nothing but a bunch of yuppies who think they can survive in the rough and tumble world of the UWA. Don't get your hopes of NC, you can't *sskiss your way out of everything....------ (Camera is paused at Big Butch Thorton with his mouth opened wide, emphasizing on the words "EVERYTHING", camera looks back at Johnny Pain whom is seen stick his middle finger up at the Television set.) Pain: Excuse me Mr. Thorton, that was no peace offering, as far as I'm concerned the peace we had were up *YOUR* ass a long time ago, cause there was never any...and never will be!!! (Pain picks up a slop of spaghetti, and whips it at the television screen. The sauce oozes down Thorton's face. The spaghetti then falls down on the bartenders glasses, that then fall to the floor and break into a billion and one pieces.) Bartender: Hey...Pain...(Pain glares at the bartender)...Sir,...I mean...Mr. Pain, my mom bought me those new glasses just last week...(almost crying)...I, I, I, I.... Pain: Shaddap!!!!!!!! All I need is another baby to whine and cry!!! We got enough of that in the UWA!!! (Throws a *LOT* of $1000 dollar bills to the bartender...) Bartender: Oh, thank you...Thank you Mr. Pain!!! (bartender admires the bills. And has a confused expression on his face.) Maxwell: B*tch, all apologize but Natural Currency does not have any time for you!!! Without that title around your waste, you're less than the paycheck of a McDonald's employee...nearly nada'!!! You're just another road block in our trip to the heavyweight belt, tag-team belts, international, cruiserweight...etc!! So we don't have time for you!!! We're going for the big thing big B*tch!!!! (Maxwell whips a whole lotta sphagetti at the still frozen Thorton on the television screen. The Spaghetti oozes down the scree and lands on the bartender's shiny bald head. Maxwell glares at the bartender, the bartender is far too scared to say anything. Maxwell and Pain the exit the restaurant.) Maxwell & Pain: NC's The Way To *BE*!!!!!! (Camera pans over to the bartender, who finally discovers that the thousand dollar bills are not *EXACTLY* thousand dollar bills, it's monopoly money. The Bartender then turns around, to busy himself shining some glasses and trying to fix his eye-glasses. On the back of his shirt can be seen a piece of paper that states "IQ of Thorton" on it.
