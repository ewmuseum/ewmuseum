About: - BBA , Christmas Chaos ===================================== (Scene opens to a outside view of a warehouse in downtown Pittsburgh. The weather is about 35 degrees and a light snow is falling from the sky. The scene changes to inside the warehouse were workers are moving about carrying around boxes with "Toys for Tots" on the sides. In the distance R.A.D. is moving those boxes from a high lift down onto a table. R.A.D. opens the box and takes a look inside. R.A.D. pulls out a G.I. joe action figure and stares at it. After about ten seconds he puts the action figure back in the box. The camera zooms in closer to R.A.D.) R.A.D.: Well if it isn’t the UWA camera man. I sure haven’t seen you in awhile. (R.A.D. closes the box and takes off his work gloves) R.A.D.: Your all wondering what I’m doing here in a warehouse full of toys aren’t you? Well to cut the suspense I’m helping out the local chapter for Toys for Tots. You know giving little tots big toys for Christmas. Not every kid out there has the luxury I had when I was young so I help out every year at this time to brighten so little tots day. (R.A.D. walks over to a stack of boxes full of toys.) R.A.D.: You see all these boxes. These are boxes full of toys for all the good little boys and girls out there. But come to think of it there are some boys that should get a lump of coal in their stockings. Those guys are the BBA. (R.A.D. taps his right leg with his right hand) R.A.D.: You goof balls thought it would be cute and smash my right left into the steel ring post last Friday. Well its still a little sore but that is not going to stop me from meeting Johnny "I the best so-called crusierweight in the world" Coals. Johnny, Johnny, Johnny. You sent your undercard bums out last Friday to take care of me so that this Friday I wouldn’t be able to face you in the ring. Well Johnny that isn’t going to happen. (A lady in here mid thirties walks into site carrying a small box of toys) Lady : Excuse me sir can I leave this toys with you R.A.D.: You sure can (R.A.D. takes the box of toys from the Lady and sets them down. The lady walks away.) R.A.D.: Like I was saying Johnny I might not be a 100 percent but believe this your going to get the fight of you life come this Friday. Now coming up in December is Christmas Chaos. Since the World Title situation is all tied up and so is the I.C. title I decided to submit an open contract for anyone to sign and meet me at Christmas Chaos. What type of match I haven’t decided yet. So if any UWA wrestler old or new wants to get into the ring with a wrestling machine, sign the dotted line. It could be a member of the BBA, Solid Gold, Natural Currency I don’t care. (R.A.D. looks at his watch and notices the time) R.A.D.: That all the time I have camera man. The next time I see you, you better bring a toy to donate to the Toys for Tots. You hear me. (Camera man shakes his head yes) R.A.D.: I got work to do (Camera fades to black as R.A.D. puts his work gloves back on and begins to move some more boxes.) _____________________________________________________ *********************************** *"Holiday" Eric Valentine * * "Today's Holiday:Skydiving" * *********************************** (Scene opens in an airplane, Several passengers aboard including "Holiday" Eric Valentine who's standing by the door.
