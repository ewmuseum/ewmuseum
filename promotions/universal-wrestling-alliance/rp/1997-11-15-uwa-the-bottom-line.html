About: The UWA, rookies - Valentine, Kryger, Slime, etc. ===================================================== (The UWA camera is taken to the 24th floor personal office of the members of UWA's sensational bigtime stable, Natural Currency. Completing the task of venturing up to the floor on a "gold" elevator, the UWA camera crew find themselves approaching Natural Currency's personal secretary, Analysis. Analysis speaks into a speakerphone.) Analysis: Yes, Mr. Gates...Mr. Johnny Pain, Christopher Maxwell, and Castor Troy are expecting their money with interest back by the 25th... (Analysis then hangs up and takes a large stack of paper and straightens them up against the desk, she then walks away although as though she is not aware of the UWA's presence. The UWA camera then makes it's way to the familiar door with "Natural Currency" engraved on a gold plate. The UWA cameraman then opens the door and pans around the room only seeing "Suicide" Johnny Pain in a business suit writing with a gold pen. They then approach him.) Cameraman: Mr. Pain, we are here on request to have a few words with Castor Troy??? Perhaps Mr. Maxwell??? Pain: Pardon your french gents, "Suicide" here. Mr. Troy and business associate Mr. Maxwell are in a business meeting at the moment, and are far to busy to clutter up their busy schedules to answer the questions and speak a whole lot to the pieces of trash, the UWA wrestlers. Cameraman: May we ask what this meeting is concerning Mr. Pain??? Pain: The meeting is concerning Natural Currency matters, son. Nothing that your little mind could be capable of understanding. But to put it simple, a few elements of the meeting is to determine new Natural Currency recruits, our hook-ups, our partners, our contracts, and my favourite, {grins} our Enemies!!!! Cameraman: Interesting, Mr. Pain... Pain: Interesting is right One-eye-willy... {laughs} Another interesting thing is the interest that Natural Currency has provoked here in the UWA. Not only to people want our heads, {laughs hysterically} but they wanna be Natural Currency!!!! Now, on the topic of "The Extreme Misfit" Gar Kryger, a fascinating gent who claims he's got the skills and profile to become a member of Natural Currency. We're scanning your profile at the moment, gent, as you can see... (Pain sports a paper that states all the strats and the profile of Kryger. On it are several checkmarks and several X's.) Pain: So we will have to get back to you on that one. Now on the other rookies of the UWA, a news flash for all, it's NC runnin' the show, cause NC is the way to be!!! And that goes for Valentine, Kryger, and even Slime!!!! And *THAT* is the bottom line!!! (Pain pushes the cameraman aside) Pain: Now, if ya' got any questions, get to my secretary... Rays of sunlight infiltrate the many windows as Randall sits in a jacuzzi, reading a newspaper. He looks at the camera and throws the paper behind him and it flops onto the soaked ground. It lands at the edge of the indoor pool as Pierce turns to the camera and adjusts his sunglasses.} "Diehard" Devon Randall: Ahhh...gotta love those Lakers. This is their year, no questions asked. And speaking of things you gotta love...let's get to me. "Diehard" Devon Randall. I've been given that nickname becuase that is exactly what I am...diehard. The only way any of you two-bit bums is gonna get a pinfall on me is if you cut off my arms with a chainsaw and beat me to death with the soggy end of one of them. Okay, so I may be exaggerating. But then again, you wouldn't know...would you? Anyways, here I am, in the United Wrestling Alliance. Or is it the Ultimate Wrestling Association? Oh, it's called the Universal Wrestling Alliance. But that doesn't matter. Because after I'm done doing what I have to do, it'll be named after me. Cocky? Yeah, I suppose. Every woman's fantasy? Just look at me {motions at body}. But a stuck-up snob you think he's better than everybody? Not nearly...even though it's a cold, hard FACT...that I truly am...the best. Sure, I have a great place to live, plenty of money, lots of girls...but that isn't all. I can also take any of you UWA idiots and kick your a**es back to the small-time federations you came from. Oh wait, that'd mean you'd still be here {chuckles}. But I guess that not all of you are indy's wrestlers working for under mininum-wage, searching the trash cans for a bite to eat. Some of you I recognize...Wrestlingman, Malibu Mike, Vile Vince Viper and Ice-Blade to name a few. But unlike you, I don't need any "friends" to take care of anyone that wants to try to block me off in the middle of my road to destiny. And I recently checked the damn card, and there seems to be no mention of my name. All I have to say is...WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON?!? {Randall steps out of the whirlpool and continues his tirade for few more seconds and the cameraman slips slightly on the wet surface before handing the wrestler a slip of paper. Randall looks it over quickly before again immersing himself into the water, wearing a smirk upon his face.} Ohhh...looks like our president was smart enough to realize why would he want the ratings for one of his weekly shows to shoot up through the roof, when he can have the buyrate for his pay-per-view go sky-high? It seems I'm facing some guy called...Flaming Bob. Hmmm...sounds like some drink one of my buddies made. Or was that an episode of {strokes chin} "The Simpsons"? Since I've only been under contract here for a few short days, I don't know if you're an established star...or a rookie here, just like me. Either way, Bobby Boy, I still come out with win numero uno to jump-start my career in the UWA. Now I bet you're wondering how I'm gonna dispatch you with the greatest of ease. Let me tell you, in two words...Extreme Eradicator. That's correct, Extreme Eradicator. Soon to be...the most devastating finisher...in all of wrestling. So Flame Man, you might be the towering inferno when we meet...but nevertheless. Because at WrestleFest II...you, my friend, are going to be extremely extinguished...by the one-and-only..."Diehard" Devon Randall! {Randall stares at a lovely young lady who walks by and smiles at him. Around it is green grass growing everywhere. Trees that are starting to have there leaves fall off. And bright red fire bushes. Also there is a playground. The camera zooms into it, a nice playground where around 30 children are playing on it. Camera spots Malibu Mike on the bench he seems to be not to good of in a mood. He is wearing blue jeans and a UWA Windbreaker. Malibu sees the camera. Malibu waves for it to come over.) Malibu: Hey, I guess you can say I have a few things to get off my chest. Well we all saw what happened Tuesday, Tuesday had to be my worst day in UWA so far, even worse than the day I lost the UWA International Title. If you saw it you saw what kind of man Vile Vince Viper, Flaming Bob, and Rex Overlord are, they are the scum of the UWA. What sickens me is the fact the PResident allowed this to happen, I think suspensions should be handed out. But hey thats just me. All of them three men, or what ever you want to call them make me sick. First off you have Vile Vince Viper. (Malibu gets a big frown on his face at the mention of his name.) Malibu: I have known Vile Vince Viper for awhile! Ever since he was in the WOWA and HCW! We have only had one match before this meeting on Tuesday. I respected the man so much until the first time we met. You people think he is "vile" now you have got to talk to this man. Once the match was announced in HCW we both had interviews like 8 times a day as I remember it. And we verbally trashed each other as if we have had a fued for a year. But we didnt, this was the first time we were to lock up. And even our wives bashed each other with VVV calling Sara a whore and me calling Demonica and slut. Pretty Childish, yep! Then that day finally came and we met..and guess what he was so scared of me taking that TV Title from him he had a stablemate, by the name of Poe come out and help him win. It wasnt a one on one match it was two on one. Well look what happened last Tuesday another unfair match, whats wrong Vince you scared of Malibu, you know he is the ONLY man in this federation who can give youa decent fight and you scared to meet me, wow big deal you beat Big Butch Thorton you would have gave that match to me I could have beat him, well actually you never know with the people working backstage! Then we finally meet Tuesday and it was for the World Title, I wasnt really prepared actually, because I wrestled in EWF and WON the EWF Hardcore Title!!! And I came to the match and you say you didnt sign the match, more reason to be scared, you go and spit some kinda stuff in my eyes. Well im ok now as you can see but Sara ISNT. What you did was took a innocent women and you physically hit her..now in my book you NEVER, I mean NEVER hit a women, well we can all see you ARE NOT in the right mind, but that gives you NO excuse! Since most of you out there are worried about Sara she was sent to the hospital and spent the night there and is back home getting better as we speak. But VVV what you did was put a scar in my mind, a scar that CANNOT be removed, you may have thought after that match in HCW was the last, now you probably think that match in UWA last Tuesday was the last! Well once you get a scar in me you never get it out so you havent seen the last of me one bit! I will be around..so dont expect me to give up! You know me too well and once I do get you in the ring for a FAIR ONE ON ONE MATCH Sara will be there and after I beat you I give her permission just to do what ever she wants and that includes killing you. Viper, just remember one thing, I can beat you, I know I can and having you spit in my eyes and get a cheap win doesnt prove much either. So dont think im threw with you one bit.
