R.A.D. is browsing the local newspaper. The UWA camera man walks up on the back porch and asks R.A.D. for some comments from Friday Night.) R.A.D.: Oh its the UWA camera man again. Well was Friday Night a psycho night or what. I mean there I was kicking the crap out the Stalker in the ring then I go outside the ring and kick the crap out of Zodiac and then low and behold superstar pajama boy comes down from the rafters and starts to beat me with a night stick. They say you are called the Sandman. I’m sorry but I don’t get it? How does the name fit the man? I don’t know and that doesn’t bother me. Does it look like I’m worried? No. Its just another wrestler who wants a piece of the best from the best stable in the UWA. Yes if you were wondering I’m a member of the A.O.A. so if anybody messes with the A.O.A. you are going to have to answer to me also. But lets talk about Pajama boy. Someone said to me when I walked to the back with the A.O.A. "Hey! Who was that masked man!" and I replied "I don’t know kemosabe but his days are numbered." That’s right Pajama boy someone other than me or the A.O.A. wants a piece of you and buy offering this certain individual money …. Well I don’t want to get into that. I can’t let the cat out of the bag. Lets just say I’ll let you sweat it out Pajama boy. Pajama boy you better keep an eye over your shoulder because you are a marked man. This Tuesday the Brotherhood, nice name by the way, will be doing color commentary at ringside. Well I hope it won’t be as bad as Johnny Tambo’s commentary because that was horrible. It made me want to puke. Speaking of Tambo the Brotherhood is offering you to join them. Hmmm another annoying wrestler to join the Brotherhood. Let me guess the Brotherhood is going to send Johnny Tambo after me right because the Eliminator can’t get it done, The Stalker can’t get it done and Pajama boy can’t get it done. I guess this stable is going to be a revolving door on who thinks they can take me out. So the Eliminator is giving up his opportunity to face me so that Pajama boy can have the chance. Man I must have really hit you hard on the head because I think you only have two brain cells left and they aren’t functioning properly. What’s at matter Eliminator does the Steel Cage scare you? Afraid that I will break your leg or something? Eliminator go suck on your thumb and go back to your mammy so she can take care of her little boy. Pajama boy next Tuesday it will be you and me in the steel cage but I’m not going to be satisfied with you I really want the Eliminator. Zodiac you sure have a mouth but how did it feel when I smacked you with a steel chair on Friday? You want a match with me and the winner gets the Goddess. Don’t make me laugh Zodiac. The Goddess thinks that you are self centered, materialistic and a waste of flesh. Well I agree on the waste of flesh. So how about I meet you on the following Tuesday Night in two weeks and I’ll accept your condition but, oh wait there is another condition Zodiac, if I win the match I get a enclosed Steel Cage match with the Eliminator and no substitutions. That means there is only one way in the cage and one way out of the cage and I’m only facing the Eliminator not anyone else. Zodiac don’t think your going to win the match because what I did on Friday was just the tip of the ice burgh of what I’m going to do to the Brotherhood and the Bad Boyz. Speaking of the Bad Boyz how is your head Coals? You see I told D-Man that I was going to be in his corner and I was. I kept the other Bad Boyz away so they wouldn’t interfere in the match. D-Man I gave you the opportunity to win the match but in the end Coals pinned you for the three. D-Man your good but I think that leg injury is still bothering you. I gave D-Man my word and I lived up to my end of the deal. D-Man things are ruff in the UWA and I think you would make and excellent addition to the PC. I must say I don’t agree with PC but there is no room in the A.O.A. for you D-Man. I’m saying the PC because I think you would fit in that stable more than the A.O.A. D-Man you have watched my back and you have watched mine but its time to move on. Someday we might meet in the ring and if we do I know you will give 110 percent and so will I. Now Ice-Blade is a member of the PC and I will me meeting him on Friday in a two out of three falls match. Some time ago Ice-Blade hit me with a chair and knocked me out. Ice-Blade came down to save the Eliminator from the beating of his life. I’m wondering now what is Ice-Blade thinking on what the Eliminator did on Friday. I could come to the ring on Friday with chair in hand and tear things up but I’m not. The best revenge for me is to pin you twice in one night Ice-Blade to show you and the world that I’m the better wrestler. Don’t get me wrong I still don’t like you Ice-Blade or Malibu Mike or Wrestlingman but I’m going to show you and the world that I’m the best the UWA has to offer and I’m better than you Ice-Blade. Well its time for me to go. I have to meet an associate who has a lot of interest in Pajama boy. Pajama boy the hour glass is half way gone and in a couple of weeks the sands of time will be no more for you Pajama boy. *********************************** R.A.D. accepts challenge from Zodiac R.A.D. vs Zodiac Zodiac wins = Zodiac gets the Goddess R.A.D. wins = R.A.D. gets a enclosed Steel Cage match with the Eliminator ====== UWA ====== | Donovan Black | | About: The UWA | ================= [Jackie Black is smoking a fine Troya cigar, reclining with feet propped on a large oaken desk, long curly hair in a pony tail, as he is wearing a grey silk suit of the most recent Versace line. He has a thin white face, perfect teeth, a large gold ring on his finger, and a black tie on... The room is a giant office on the 69th floor in uptown Chicago, the overlooking a scenic yet clouded skyline... the office is large, grey and decorated with Wall size Windows. Barring classic pictures of Dali, Van Gogh, and Skot Olsen, the room is bare, save a Large Oaken desk in the center, and its accompanying high back black leather chair] Jackie: OK guys, what did I tell you? The second Donovan Black signs on the dotted line with Blackheart and re-takes Jackie Black as his chief promoter, what happens? Vile Vince Viper himself Submits to the Bulldog headlock and the naysayers hath since called my offices thousands of times offering big MONEY contracts! HA ha ha, but you know, since I am the kinda guy to say this.... I TOLD YOU SO! ...Ego? It's my business. I had been telling the plebians that my man Donovan Black could beat anyone and now they know what I have been saying for weeks. So Who's the hottest item since Suicide's return? Who gets a chance to prove himself again when he faces Devastated Danny Davarro? Who is gonna WIPE the mat with Crash the Terminating Tag Teamer? [Jackie leans back farther, and inhales the smoke, then looks at his cigar, twirling it... Jackie smells the end, and twists it around] Jackie: Ah, number 27, Natural, no Maduro wrapper for me, thanks. Like Jack Kerouac would have wrote, the earth spilled tidal waves of triumph, bellowing up from the churning belly of the Terran Soil, magnified thousands of times by the chanting in the air of a plod of heralding devils and demons. Rise and Rejoice UWA, as Donovan Black is signed to me and the UWA exclusively. The fall man is getting back up! [Turns to face camera, with cigar in mouth, arms folded on desk] Jackie: D MAN!! I hear around the net that there is some legit heat going on backstage, that you heard my spouting words of wisdom and are now panties-enflamed because I called My talent "D-MAN" whearas you seem to think you own Copyrights on your name or something...let me break it down for you real quick, then get back to important matters... D-Man, I don't care IF your Momma names you Dominos Man, Dog Faced Gremlin Man, Duracel Man or Dick Impaired Man, IF I choose to call Donovan Black Crusher or Big Butch or Malibu, I will. I don't need your permission, or otherwise to call him anything that I feel like. So ...just because you requested it, from now on in this flash to the UWA fans, I am gonna call Him D-MAN, cause his name starts with a D, and Unlike you, He's a MAN. [Leans backwards once again and swivels to face the giant windows] Jackie: Look outside fans, see the Big Picture? ...One town, one of many. Don't feel bad Vile Vince Viper, everyone goes up and down the ladder, ...so what if you were Just a mere ... stepping stone to the Top. Rest assured, you won't be the last. Everyone loses once in awhile, Donovan's been doing that for a long time, and he probably will once a year, but it happens. Don't quit- Actually, maybe it IS time to retire Triple V... I heard Romania's having a new election soon anyways, you got my vote! Ha ha ha... Jackie: DANNY DAVARRO... fool him once, shame on you, fool UWA twice, Shame on Me. It ain't gonna happen! Will lightening strike twice come the rematch for respect? Doubt it my friend... one thing though, after Donovan Black reminds the World of your plebian status, will that trick of a money digging slut still stick around to be "devastated" from your nightly performances? I don't think so either. Jackie: CRASH...I know of you buddy. Not impressed... NEXT. [Pauses, smoke circles form overhead] Jackie: HOLLYWOOD Johnny Tambo had the nerve to come on after Donovan's match and thereby drop the ratings again. Nice going there buddy, if not for Donovan/Viper, then we wouldn't have had to Narrowly beat the competiton. Almost ruined the last 15 minute bracket as well. Word is out Tambo, your living on borrowed time. If Malibu Mike doesn't clip your wings, eventually Donovan will hit you and it'll be all over before your 15 minutes of fame burn out on there own. Remember, you are on the list buddy, NEVER forget that. Same with you Walker. But first things first. It seems that the President has not found anyone willing to accept a challenge from Donovan for this Tuesday... and for once, he has nothing to do! What's he gonna do, sit back and watch "Must see TV"?? I don't think so, he will be there, as will I. WHERE is DONOVAN BLACK anyways? [Swivels around in a complete circle, then back again to face desk] Jackie: I have him in the gym. No, not because he's FAT or anything, if you must know Davarro, because he's the man... so for those of you who REALLY WANT to go back to the MUNDANE flashes of the UWA, then hey, let's break it down as to why No one is safe this Tuesday. Walker, Davarro, Tambo, Crash, You'll all find that out real soon. And hey, I may be there as well. Just for you Gordy, just because I know WAR can beat Third Rock from the Sun again, that is unless they give the the Mic back to Tambo. Anyways, this has been your manager supreme Jackie Black's state of the week address, and we now return you to your regularily scheduled plethora of blah blah blah and yabba dabba doo. [Leans back to face window, and inhales...then exhales...] Jackie: AH, Dominican Olor wrapped, smooth flavor...
