Behind Chambers are his personal bodygaurds as well two beautiful women. As usual, on the badges of the bodygaurds can be found the words "Natural Currency, Inc." Camera zooms in to a chuckling Chambers.) Chambers: Well look what we got here, the "two pennies paid each" cameracrew for their daily pleasure of interviewin' the UWA's greatest superstar..."Mr. Money" Johnny Chambers.... Looks like everyone in the federation just can't get enough of me including Lawrence Stevenson and R.A.D and all the other wrestling amateurs of the UWA...what can I say gents?? (brushes off dust speckles from his suit) I can't get enough of myself either... and would ya' look at that??? (ladies smirk) even the ladies can't control themselves!!...(Chambers then chuckles) I've did a little spectating in the past few days...spectating if you'd call it that...looking at my enemies, looking at my pals (Chambers smirks) and best of all looking at the production of "Solid Gold"...(Chambers laughs hysterically) Cameraman: Any thoughts on Solid Gold Mr. Chambers??? Chambers: Any thoughts??? Well, what can I say...I couldn't agree any better with them...it's the green that runs the team... it's a little too bad they don't got so much of it like I do... (pulls out a $1000 dollar bill and makes a paper crane) What can I say??? (chuckling) I'm very creative... Cameraman: Do you see any future conflict with the members of Solid Gold??? Chambers: (throwing the crane at the cameraman, and chuckling) Good question, bad interviewer...(chuckling again)...What comes around...goes around "A/V boy" (imitating "Hollywood" Johnny Tambo) if they pick a battle with the finest...(brushes off his suit again) I can't help it but giving them the finest A** beating... (chuckles hysterically) Cameraman: Would you like to issue any challenge whatsoever at the present time??? Chambers: yah...I would...(revealing a hidden smirk) I issue a challenge to the man whose everything that characterizes a loser...R-eal A-bsolute D-ummy...RAD...in fact heres a little printed copy of a challenge that ya' can give to UWA Prez, Gordon. ...hands the Cameraman a piece of paper. _______________________________ | UWA Match. | | | | "Mr. Money" Johnny Chambers | | vs. | | R.A.D | | | | X __Johnny Chambers__ | | X ___________________ | |_______________________________| Cameraman: I'll be sure to give him the sheet...(places paper in shirt pocket) now...A little off topic...(points to a badge that states "Natural Currency, Inc" on a bodygaurd) What is this new thing about "natural currency" does this indeed have anything to do with you in the Univeral Wrestling Association...does it promote the formation of yet another stable, tag-team??? Chambers: (looking at watch) oops...looks like you've over stayed your welcome baldy...(chuckles) so lets put it nice an plain huh boys...??? (laughing) (Chambers then puts a folded paper in the mouth of the cameraman...) Chambers: that oughta' pay for the extra time wasted... (The Cameraman leaves the room and outside spits out the paper in his mouth and unfolds it only to reveal a check made out to "Natural Currency, Inc"...Camera fades to a dollar sign. Chuckling can be heard, Audio & Video fade.) ================================ UWA ================================= | "The Cannon" Christopher Maxwell | | About: Urbish Mines, The UWA, Castor Troy, Danny Daravo, | | All members of SOLID GOLD. | ====================================================================== (The UWA camera zooms into the original scene where "The Cannon" Christopher Maxwell can be found, a beat up apartment located in the ghetto area of Los Angeles. The Camera zooms in to Maxwells face, beside him in the shadows is seen Analysis, Maxwell's manager camera zooms in.) Maxwell: (speaking in a serious tone) Urbish Mines....an event where I will show all UWA opponents exactly the business that "The Cannon" means in the UWA. A maze, that will not only show the strength of all those competing in the event, but what's inside here... (taps on head, and then begins to laugh hysterically) What do I have to worry about??? (chuckling) tell me whose got the brains in the UWA??? Guess who??? (flexes muscle) Can I help it if I've got brains and brawn??? Not my prob...(Maxwell smirks) Now unto a little topic, putting the topic of the Urbish Mines' theme aside...#1 target... My good ol' buddy Castor Troy...it'll be fun meeting you in the ring bbbbbbbbuuuuuddddddddyyyyy....it'll be more fun when I slap your head around the arena (begins chuckling)....#2 target..."Devestating"Danny Daravo...thinks he owns the world talking big about this...talking big about that...the only thing big in this league is me boy!!!! (Analysis nods her head in approval) (Maxwell chuckles) Life's beautiful ain't it Camerapeople??? Ya' come into my apartment...scared the hell outta' your pants..comin' to my room and feel relieved at the protection of yours truly..."The Cannon" Christopher Maxwell... well, it's an all out war out there boys, and guess whose causin' it??? Scum like Daravo, Castor Troy, and all the members of Solid Gold...speaking of which...I noticed the reunion...must be "be kind to dumb animals week"...having a skilled man like Mr. Tambo siding up with youngin's like Castor Troy & Lawrence Stevenson, not to mention our favourite old McDonald, Scotty Daniels...(sigh) will my laughter ever stop??? (laughs hysterically) sorry I just can't help myself...but let's get down to the dirty work...Danny Dar-crap-o...I issue a challenge to you after I demolish and clean up the ring with Castor Troy... that way, the ring'll be all nice and clean for your blood shed...(Maxwell chuckles)..."The Cannon" is out!!!! (Camera fades to a darker than usual shade of black) ===== UWA ====== | Creed | | About: D-Man | ================ [Creed is laying down on a park bench, wearing black boots, blue denim shorts, a Green Day t-shirt, shades, and a red bandanna. He spots the camera and sits up.] Creed: Well, well, well. It seems I got myself a match with D-Man. What does that stand for? Does it mean "the" Man? Or Demon? I don't really care because I haven't heard jack about this guy, so should I be impressed? No! Until I hear from D-Man, then I really shouldn't fear him because obviously he does fear me. I'll tell you what D-Man. I'm not going to underestimate you, because that's how a match is lost. But I will be coming to the ring in two weeks prepared. I'll take whatever you got and come back with something stronger. You want to wrestle technically? I can do that! How about high flying? I can do that! Brawling? Hell, ANYONE can do that! D-Man, that's about as much as I need to say to you know, until you reply. Basically, bring whatever you got to the ring, because I love a challenge. Don't think I won't be prepared D-Man, because I'm not going to underestimate you. See you in the ring!
