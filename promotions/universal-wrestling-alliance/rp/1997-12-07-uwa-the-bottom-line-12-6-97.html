=============== Rex Overlord About: "Stalker" Toni Gills =============== (scene opens to the locker room of Rex Overlord. Overlord: In just a few short moments...you "Stalker" Toni Gills will regret the time that you ever called upon my name...that you ever lifted finger to prevent my wrath.
