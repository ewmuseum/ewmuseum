The last time me and Scott teamed we left on the short end of the stick. Well we still have that stick and it's going straight up the AOA's A** this Tuesday!!! One of the AOA will be you at Hellshcok! "Superstar" Scott Storm: (Points to the camera) So Angels of the Apocalyspe, in a few days you have the greatest tag team ever on your hands.
