He is immidiately confronted by Security!!) Security: May I help you sir?? Camera man: I was hoping to get a few words with any of the members of Natural Currency!! (The Security guard goes to a telephone and comes back) Security: The Most Devine Wrestler in the World will see you!! (The Security guard escorts the Camera man to the Elevators!! The Security guard goes through the Buttons!! There are five buttons Caster Troy Penthouse, The Cannon Penthouse, The Amazing Penthouse, The Penthouse of Pain. The Guard pushes The Most Devine Penthouse!! The elevator doors open, The Security guard pushes a code and enters a room to the outside of the penthouse!! Security opens the door and lets the Camera man in.) Stein: Well, Well, Well, if it isnt the Camera man!! (Stein laughs remembering the incident at WrestlefestII) Stein: I called you here today, because I have something to say!! Valentine, what in the hell is going on!! You haven't gave an interview since I made the challenge to wrestle you on the friday!! I dont even think you know you have a match with The Most Devine Wrestler in the World!! If you forgot you are going to be suprised on Friday!! Your pethetic Valentine!! Camera man: Can you respond to Johnny Tambos Comments from last week? Stein: Johnny Tambo, I think you better think before you speak!! If you remember the RWF tournement for the Heavyweight belt!! I kicked your sorry ass to win the belt!! Before you shoot your mouth off maybe you should think of the consequenses of your actions!! Maybe thats why Solid Gold is so screwed up in the first place!! Now everyone knows that Natural Currency is the #1 Stable in the World!! Its the place to be, the cream of the crop and we are the kings of this castle!! Camera man: Your thoughts on Wrestlefest II?? Stein: It was all Natural Currency!! Tambo, lets face facts!! You have a title that doesnt belong to you!! It belongs to the man who single handely pinned your sorry ass for the three count!! And that man is The Cannon Christopher Maxwell!! You know it and we know it!! But being the coward that you are Tambo!! You wont give Maxwell a title shot, cause you are simply frightened by him!! Camera man: What do you think about Chris Simmons?? Stein: He reminds me of Richard Simmons!! (Stein starts laughing) Camera man: What do you think of Scott Daniels?? Stein: I haven't heard from Daniels since Wrestlefest!! WONDER WHY?? He must be still Dazed and Confused!! All I know is that Daniels stole the name of what USED to be the most powerful Group in Wrestling!! But since Daniels took it over, it went belly up!! WONDER WHY?? Camera man: What do you think of Aaron Aikman?? Stein: One of the most talented wrestlers in the world!! He also is the best planner in the business!! At WrestlefestII it was the first time I have seen him!! And he really is AMAZING!! Camera man: What do you think about Solid Gold?? Stein: LOSERS!!! WANNA BE's!!! (Camera fades to black) ___________________________________________________ The Nightmares About: Silver & Gold, becoming #1 contenders... The sound of Wolf Mann howling can be heard in the distance, the sound slowly grows louder and louder until Wolf Mann appears and begins to attack the cameraman. Frank N Stein and Painmaster rush in and try to restain Wolf Mann with that leash and bone. Painmaster begins to choke Wolf Mann with the leash until he is finally under control. The cameraman is shaken and has had his shirt ripped off by Wolf Mann's teeth. Frank n Stein- Painmaster I thought you said you could control him? Painmaster- I can but you see Frank he is not human and I can not always restain him. Frank- Just get him back to the dungeon and this time use stronger chains. I want him to be like this come Tuesday night when we face Silver and Gold. You see Silver and Gold this time when the Painmaster releases Wolf mann neither one of us are going to attempt to restain him. You see he got lose tonight because he wa hungary and smelled the fear of this cameraman . (Sniff sniff) i can smell the same fear coming from you two, and soon you will become the appetizers for Wolf Mann. You see we know that the winner of our match gets a shot at the tag titles, the main course for Wolf Mann. Silver and Gold I beg you to go to sleep so the Nightmares can come and haunt you for the rest of enternity. Painmaster- Right you are Frank. You see Wolf Mann is a hunter and once he smells fear he just attacks. That is why he came out tonight he smelled fear. Now then he will be nice and hungary come Tuesday night and then he will be turned loose on Silver and Gold. Then after he gets done feeding Tuesday night he will be ready for the main meal on Friday Crash and Bash the soon to be former UWA tag champions. Crash and Bash please come to ring and stick your tounges out because Wolf Mann would love to grab ahold of them with his bite and rip them along with your throats out of your mouths. Come Frank we must get Wolf Mann back to the dungeon so he can suffer and so you can prepare. Vile Vince Viper, title or not, it's come time for you to confront your paulbearers...the men who you chose to use for gaining the spotlight back in the UWA...and the men that will see your bloody carcass carried out of the UWA. I say to you...bring Flaming Bob...bring Goliath...bring your midgets. But the focus of Overlord & Thornton will be upon one man...and that will be you. Without the core...Hell On Earth collapses. But I call them weakness. Taking a look back there hasn't been one time...not one time that you've won a title or gained any major victory. It's always been someone else that's done it for you. Whether it be Goliath, Flaming Bob, Uncle Son of Sam, or whoever...soon you're going to look into your corner and find that you're alone. That night is tonight. And we're going to do the job that Wrestlingman couldn't get done...and that is crush the head of the serpent. Overlord: Butch Thornton, you doubt me. You doubt my loyalty. And what am I to tell you? I cannot tell you that I am and will remain loyal to you and the Fearsome Foursome. Because I feel no loyalty there. But the snake...it can only be killed by you and I. You have proved powerful in the past...and are most valuable in this war. But you will only be useful as long as you are effective...and the same will go for me. Should I slip behind...should I lag the destruction of Vile Vince Viper...then it will be me that sits at the wayside. It's not loyalty...it's not friendship...but it's cooporation. And thus far, our cooporation has produced. In a little over an hour we walk down that aisle...and we become team mates again. And Vile Vince Viper feels Hell On Earth most completely.
