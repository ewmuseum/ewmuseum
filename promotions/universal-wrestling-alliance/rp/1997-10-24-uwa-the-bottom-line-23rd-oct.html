Gang lingo and symbols decorate the tainted brick buildings which line the narrow walkway. Rats scurry about aimlessly in their filth and the garbage overflows from their bins. At the end, in the dark shadows, a man in a trenchcoat lurks, his raven hair tied into a lengthy ponytail. It's the man we've come to know and love as "Sabbath", aka Micheal Price.* Sabbath: Look around you. What do you see? Scum. Filth. Disease. Rot. This is how I see the world. There are times, especially now, where it seems all I can feel is rage and hate, except for two things: my God, and my love. There are times when I have to get away from the world, away from the people who have spent the better part of their lives toiling away for some higher Platonic ordeals. The people that have shunned those like myself, calling us "freaks" and "weirdos", with little intuition as to just how sharp those words can be to a man's soul. You see, the so-called "leaders" of the world have set forth certain standards of "normal" that we're all supposed to conform to. And what for those of us who do not? The life of an out-cast. Let me tell you a story. *Sabbath stands erect and clears his throat with a raspy cough. As he speaks, he flares his arms and hands around with grand gesture.* Sabbath: Back in the times of the ancient Greeks, there was a man, a philosopher, Socrates. Socrates taught Plato, who in turn, taught Aristotle. For my lack of knowledge on Socratic thought, I will overlook him and turn to his successors. Plato believed that mankind was basically evil, and should be sacrificed for the "higher goods" such as religion and government. He had a vision of his Utopia or "ideal state." A philosopher king would rule, raised from birth. Only certain forms of music, namely in the Dorian and Phrygian modes, would be acceptable. Sound familiar? It should. It's censorship. Aristotle, on the other hand, believe that man was basically good, and should be generally free to do as he wills. Which do you think the world followed? *Sabbath grins.* Sabbath: If you answered "Plato", then you win a treat. For some unknown reason, humanity has never wanted indepenence, for all they say they do. They really don't. Human's have need to be led, like sheep. After all, we're only animals. Yet, the difference? We're animals with souls. Now, don't get me wrong, I'm not some anarchist. I feel that government is a necessary evil. If all government was abolished, then there would be chaos. Anarchy wouldn't work. Why? Because people foolishly believed Plato's idea that man was basically evil. After a few years, it's true. Given free reign to do as we wish would be the ultimate mistake. However, people would soon band together, and one group would conquer, forming a monarchy, which would ultimately be overthrown and democracy reestablished. Anyway, my point is, the UWA needs a leader. And, I, Micheal Price, am that leader. I've begun my rise to the top of the UWA ladder, and one day, certainly not soon, that UWA World's Heavyweight Title will be mine! And there's nothing anyone can do about it. *Sabbath turns his back to us, and on his back is a long Spanish steel broadsword.* Sabbath: It's been awhile since I've taken this from it's home. But, I feel the time has come that it must be removed. I'm on a mission now. To clean the UWA up, by any means necessary, and become a champion. A hero. Someone the children of the world can look up to as a leader. Of course, I won't be using my sword, as that wouldn't be fair. But it's a nice effect is it not? And, considering the life I lead, it helps to have insurance. *Sabbath walks on down the alleyway, and notices a ragged, half-dead bum asleep against the wall.* Sabbath: This is what I'm talking about. People like this, who have been pushed aside. How could anyone allow this to happen. I've lived like this before, and it's not fun, that I can promise you. This man needs our help...and people just ignore him. Well, not I. *Sabbath reaches into his trenchcoat and pulls out a wad of money, and stuffs it into the sleeping man's pocket.* Sabbath: I'm sure he'll spend that on his obvious drinking habit, but who knows. He just *MIGHT* see it as a gift from God and turn his life around, and if I can save one soul from anymore pain than it's already suffered, then I've accomplished some good. *Sabbath walks to the end of the alley, and steps into a waiting vehicle, which speeds away.* *"Black Sabbath" begins to play and the bleeding Celtic cross fades in.* ========= UWA ========== Malibu Mike \ About: The UWA / ======================== (Camera fades into Malibu Mikes Mansion.
