Big Butch Thornton About: Vile Vince Viper, Rex Overlord - taped before FNF (Scene opens inside inside a hotel room near the arena where Friday Night Fights is to be held. Sitting inside his hotel room wearing a black Metallica "Relaod Tour" t-shirt, blue jeans, and black boots is Big Butch Thornton. He is packing a duffle bag getting ready for his big tag match tonight. Butch turns around and looks toward the camera and begins to speak.) Big Butch Thornton: In just a short amount of time, Chapter 2 in my war with Vile Vince Viper will unfold, this time with Rex Overlord by my side. Rex proved to be a worthy partner at Wrestlefest and he will need to do just the same tonight as Vile Vince Viper and whoever his sick mind finds as a partner will prove to be a tough test. But it's a test I _MUST_ pass and I _WILL_ pass. (Rubs his hands over his eyes) The last time I was in a match with you Vile Vince Viper, I failed. The belt was taken by me due to uncontrollable circumstances but nevertheless, I lost. Vile Vince Viper, you will suffer what I suffered, but your punishment will come in deuces, on tongiht and on Sunday. (Pauses for a moment) Whatever is left of you after me and Rex tear you and your partner apart limb from limb will have to come back and fight me at Sunday Spotlight. Can you survive after tonight Vince? Time will tell, but one thing is for certain....Payback is a bitch....I GUARANTEE IT!!!!!
