Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Heartland Wrestling Association launched on 3 June 1998 with a debut broadcast, “Wednesday Night RAGE!,” taped before roughly 3,000 fans at the National Guard Armory in Paducah, Kentucky, and aired on KITMO Cable Access. Billing itself “The Revolution Has Begun,” the show opened on a well-traveled veteran, Nash Tidal, declaring on camera that he had turned down offers elsewhere to chase one goal: becoming the promotion's first Heavyweight Champion.

That first card built its whole card around an eight-man tournament to crown that champion, its bracket narrowing by the broadcast's second half to Necropolis against Onslaught, “Dancing” Eric Stevens against IT, and Tidal against The Bomber. The same night's debut Tag Team Championship match, Generation NeXt against The Brotherhood, ended in a no-decision after two masked men ran in and attacked both teams before disappearing into the crowd.

By its next surviving broadcast, a month later, Eric Stevens had won the tournament in a three-way dance to become the promotion's first Heavyweight Champion. Tidal, denied a rematch, was instead made to face Stevens' own flunky, “The Blob,” after Stevens argued he had no obligation to give Tidal another shot.

That same episode introduced a second singles title, the HWA Mississippi River Championship, its first challengers to be drawn at random, and carried an unresolved mystery angle of its own — HWA officials publicly demanding that the wrestler who had been quietly injuring the roster show up and explain himself.

Whether that attacker was unmasked, who won the Mississippi River Title, or how the promotion itself ended isn't in the surviving record. Its 1 July 1998 episode, still running its cards on schedule, is the last surviving document.
