HWA (Heartland Wrestling Association) — Card

Wednesday Night RAGE! (part 1 of 4)

3 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Heartland Wrestling Association, The Revolution Has Begun" http://crazyproductions.hypermart.net/hwa -------------------------------------------------------------------------- (The screen starts with snow, then flashes quickly to the old fashioned count down...with a 5, then a spinning line which wipes it to 4, likewise to 3, again, to two...and then.... The screen shows the Heartland Wrestling Association logo, apparently as a backdrop to a media area. Echoing out, the listener hears "Say a prayer for the youth of America!" which is followed by an instrumental score of the same song. From the left side of the screen walks in Nash Tidal, a huge and rather well recognized man in the sport of professional wrestling. He is visible only from just below the waist up, but one can see he is wearing his usual black leather pants, along with a Chicago Cubs jersey, unbuttoned, and a pair of black mirrored Gargoyle shades. He is a mix of casual and intense...he is... about to speak.) Tidal: I have been to many places.... (The screen cuts to footage of Nash Tidal in the ring with a masked man. The ring apron, which we can see, says "Northern Japan Wrestling" with what is assumably the same words written in Japanese next to it. Tidal sends the masked man into the ropes then nails an atomic spine buster. Raising a fist to the air, Tidal then claws the man's head, as if palming a basket ball, and lifts him into the air, slamming him down with the brutal move known only as the Crimson Wave. We then fade out and back to Tidal...) Tidal: I've seen a great many things... (The camera shows Tidal at the final SEWF card, where he has just nailed the Warrior in the head, sending him to the outside. In the ring, Warrior's tag partner Phil Bishop is still fighting Fury, oblivious. Phil comes to the corner seconds later to make the tag, and is met with a fist, followed by the same claw we saw before, as Tidal lifts Bishop over the ropes and nails him, Crimson Wave style, to the concrete floor outside. Fade again to Tidal....) Tidal: I've been asked to carry other names.... (As the clips speed up, we see Tidal dressed as Diesel, walking out to a WWF ring...then a quick cut to Tidal, still dressed as Diesel, Crimson Waving a WWF jobber through a wooden table. Fade back to Tidal, again...) Tidal: To wear masks... (The camera shows Tidal during a stint in Japan where he was known as the Tsunami Dragon, the fearsome purple mask over his head. Fade back...) Tidal: Even to lay down and take the "job". (The camera shows Tidal in a livid argument with SwCW president Brian Gunch, which we can hear dialogue within: Gunch: I want you to lay down for Elwood Blues. Tidal: He's six foot one, and his interviews suck! The fans don't like him, I don't like him, and I refuse! Gunch: I'm your boss, Tidal. You take it like I tell you to. Now go lay down for my brother! Tidal: Oh...so it's a family affair, is it? Gunch: It's an employer/employee affair... I'd like to see you change it! Tidal: Deliver this to Elwood for me... Tidal then grabs Gunch and, you guessed it, Crimson Waves him through the pressboard that covers the interview area for SwCW, bringing himself and Gunch out in the middle of Elwood and Mighty Mack Blues interview, Gunch looking not at all happy.) Tidal: But at every step, I have remained true to myself, and gained my vengeance. (The screen shows Tidal in the now legendary RBW match he and Elwood Blues competed in. A barrage of clips fly, ending with the final few seconds of the match, as Tidal nails Elwood with the Crimson Wave then stuffs him in the casket at ringside, locks it, and pushes the casket off the Robert E. Lee, the ship on which all RBW matches take place. We fade back to Tidal's face as the casket floats off.) Tidal: And now I am here, in the Heartland Wrestling Association. I have been asked many questions as to why I am here...many offers have come in. I could be in the WWF, where I was promised my shot at Stone Cold Steve Austin. I was offered a World Title reign in the newly reformed SEWF. The eXw is ringing my phone off the hook. And I've heard from Paul E. three times since he saw that he was losing Al Snow. All these positions don't interest me. And I'll tell you all why. Nash Tidal wants to be in the best promotion, with the best competition, and Nash Tidal wants to earn his first actual world title reign. (The camera pans back, as Tidal's full frame is brought into view. He has a stained glass HWA logo sitting at his feet. As he lifts it up, he continues to speak...) Tidal: Among names like The Wolverine, The Bomber, Onslaught, Warren Merlin , Dr. Melancholy, Necropolis, and many others, I will reap from the HWA the two things I have never before been able to attain. One, I will gain the respect promised by the front office. I don't want favors, I just want respect. And second, among this group of elite wrestlers, Nash Tidal will climb to the highest pentacle of his career, becoming the HWA World Champion. (Tidal now holds the stained glass before his chest, starting to reach towards the camera.) Tidal: Get a good look at this face. A good look at me. I am Nash Tidal, and you are about to witness the laying of the first brick of a new wrestling empire. Are you ready for the HWA? The time is.....NOW (As the camera pulls back in a flash, the screen shows only the stained glass, which Tidal shatters, sending fragments flying towards the lens.) {Camera fades out to the HWA Intro. video} {It is early in the morning, the sun has only been up for half an hour, and there is a vast field of corn growing as far as you can see.} (The camera rotates and you can see a farmer at his barn talking to his livestock while he feeds them) "Here you go guys, now you eat up. I'm afraid it's gonna rain today." {He looks to the sky. All the sudden there is a fiery golden streak in the sky. The massive ball of fire falls into the tall corn.} "What the hell!" (The farmers voice trembles with fear) {Fire overtakes the field as the camera backs out. The farther away the camera gets, the more land the fire consumes. Eventually the camera is out far enough that you can see all of North America. The whole continent is ablaze. Across the continent the fire forms the HWA logo. Many years in the future there is a man in his twenties and his family walking where the field used to be. They are raggedly dressed. Apparently the fire ravaged the land destroying everything in its path and the continent still hasn't recovered. They are descendents of the few survivors. They unknowingly come upon the origin of the fire.} "What is that?" {He sees something glinting in the sun} "Could it be? My grandfather always told me a story about.. no way.. could it have been true? {He digs frantically in the soil unearthing a leather strap with golden plates fastened to it.} "Oh my god! This is what started the fire! The 'Great Fire'!" {As he wipes the belt off with his sleeve you can see the letters "HWA" on the main plate} "Heartland Wrestling Association....... I can't believe it." {As "Back in Black" by AC/DC begins to play the screen is then flooded by clips of Heartland Wrestling Federation matches including all the biggest stars.} {Then the screen is engulfed in flames. The areas that are burned away are replaced with the HWA logo.} Heartland Wrestling Association proudly presents.... HWA Wednesday Night RAGE! on KITMO Cable Access *************************************************************************** {The camera pans across the audience of 3,000 fans crammed into all corners of the National Guard Armory in Paducah Kentucky as AC/DC's "Back In Black" blast through the audio system. The fans have numerous signs for their favorite HWA stars. The camera centers on the announcers desk. Louie, the midget, is in his high chair on the right next to Ralph Newborn Jr. in the middle, and D.A. Johnson is seated on the left side.} RNJ: Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome to Paducah Kentucky as Heartland Wrestling Association proudly presents their first ever Wednesday Night RAGE! DAJ: And do we have a show for you, tonight we will be having the first round of the Heavyweight Title Tournament that will decide the first ever HWA Heavyweight Champion........ LUE: And... DAJ: And what? LUE: We will also see who becomes the first ever HWA Tag Team champions as The Brotherhood takes on Generation NeXt. RNJ: Let's take a look at the brackets for the tournament. {A graphic comes on the screen showing the tournament brackets as Ralph reads them aloud} RNJ: We will see Necropolis facing Shadow Runner, Jack Shepherd will be facing Dr. Meloncholy, Laserbeam will be taking on Onslaught, Warren Merlin will be facing "Dancing" Eric Stevens, we will also see a match up of Chaos taking on IT, Wolverine will take on Nash Tidal, and finally The Bomber draws a wildcard in the tournament. DAJ: Well I've never seen him before, but I have a feeling that The Bomber won't have much trouble against this A. Wildcard person. I think it will be kind of funny. LUE: That was the stupidest thing I've ever heard. That wasn't even remotely funny. I just wander why in the hell you would say something so senseless. DAJ: Hey I thought it was fun..... LUE: It wasn't. DAJ: Let me fin.... LUE: Shut up. DAJ: That's not fai...... RNJ: Ladies and gentlemen, apparently HWA Reporter Randal Cruize has something for us... hold on.... he says he spotted one of the HWA wrestlers on his way to the arena. DAJ: Well let's see it! RNJ: Ok let's see if we can get it. Randal, can you hear me? {The camera switches to a live video feed} (Darkness fills the night sky, and a thick fog is visible in the street lights. Two men are following after Necropolis, one with a camera) {Randal Cruize} Yes Ralph, I think we've stumbled upon Necropolis. We'll see if we can get an interview. {RC} You must be Necropolis.... funny meeting you all alone out here in the darkness. [Necropolis] Do I know you? {RC} You do now. My name is Randal Cruize. I'm part of the intervieing staff for HWA. We were on our way to the arena when we saw someone walking. I recognized your figure from a previous interview [Necropolis] I see. What do you want? {RC} Well, the championship committee has released its list of competitors for the world title.... you are signed for a match with.... [Necropolis] (interupting) ....Shadow Runner. I know. His name fits him well. He runs to the shadows. The shadows of those who face, and defeat him. I have no idea what he's saying about this match, but if he's saying that he's going to win, talk proves nothing. I'm a man of my word, and when I say I will do something, I go out and do it. I don't spend time blabbering about how great I will be, or how badly the opponent will lose. Keeping your word is done in the ring. And I always keep my word. Now leave me and continue your journey to the arena. (Necropolis continues down the road. Randal makes one last attempt to ask him a question, but makes no progress. Necropolis fades away in the fog, leaving Randal and his camera man staring blankly ahead) {End of Feed} DAJ: Where'd Necropolis go?! LUE: We don't know. It looks like Necropolis is ready for his match. I don't think I'd want to be Shadow Runner right now. RNJ: Speaking of Shadow Runner let's check out what he had to say. DAJ: Randal found him too? Man he sure is good at this. RNJ: No, it's just a clip from Shadow Runner. DAJ: Oh, well Randal is still pretty good. {Start Clip} [Shadow Runner is standing in an alleyway. It is about 3:00 am.] Shadow Runner: *whispering* Everyone in the HWA wants to know why I'm here. They ask "Who is this man who walks in the shadows, and why is he here?" I'm here because I have heard that this is where the toughest competition is. I came here to prove that I am the best wrestler that this sport has ever seen. *Yelling* I came here to break bones and inflict pain! Are you ready for me, HWA? I know I'm ready for you. I'm always ready for a fight, and that's exactly what I will get here in the HWA. [Shadow Runner walks into the shadows] {End of Clip} DAJ: See Necropolis was right!! Did you see him run into the shadows? He's afraid of Necropolis. Whoo hoo, He's shaking in his boots. I bet he's calling his mommy now. That was great, he ran into the shadows, just like Necropolis said. He just kinda we....... LUE: He was walking. DAJ: What? LUE: He was walking into the shadows. If he was scared he would have ran, but he was walking. He wasn't scared. I'm not sure if it is possible to scare that odd fellow. DAJ: Necropolis, you'd better run. Shadow Runner is after you. RNJ: We'll find out who's afraid of who in a few minutes, but first let's check out another up and coming, and odd superstar in the HWA, "Maniac" Mark Tucker. {Begin Clip} [ Camera cues in. The scene is by a lakefront beach.The camera pans over various women in their swimsuits and kids jumping off a dock doing cannon balls and such. Walking up a boardwalk on a horse is Mark Tucker.Mark is wearing blue swim trunks,Nike running shoes, and a white T-shirt with The words Revelation 6:8. The horse is a pale color that goes with the shirt. Mark stops the horse and climbs down. Mark looks at the camera and says.] Mark: It seems that the HWA wants me to jump into the fire as soon as I filled out the contract. Well my friends when you sign your name in blood. It is a done deal. I signed my name to the contracts and all you wannabe wrestlers now have to deal with the greatest wrestler known to man....Mark Tucker. [ The camera goes in for a close-up. Mark has an evil look on his face and says directly into the camera.] Mark: Those in the federation will have a new wrath poured onto their bodies and the lord sayeth to them sinners will come to their fates by the hand of the sword and famine. [ The camera backs up as Mark climbs back on the horse and says to the camera with his arms in the air.] Mark: Behold, a pale horse.It's rider is death and hades will follow behind it. Mark: Now all you wrestlers of the HWA. You will witness the greatest wrestling package around. That being The true M.M.T. That is "Maniac" Mark Tucker [ The camera pans over the area and three ladies walk up to Mark and giggle. They are putting their hands on his chest. Mark gets a furious look to his face and says to the ladies.] Mark: Get your hands off of me. Just because I let you touch my muscles yesterday mean that you have an invitation to do it all the time. Leave me alone. [ One of the women says something smart to him as the other two back off. Mark grabs her by the arm and kisses her then backs her up. He whispers something in her ear and she walks away with the other two.] Mark: It is truely amazing when you see women more brave then the men around here. I am coming to the Heartland and I am bringing hades with me. [ Mark turns away from the camera and the woman he kissed comes back into view. He pulls her to him as the camera fades to black.] {End Clip} RNJ: Now let's go to Ralph Newbron Sr. for the first match. {Camera cuts to the Ring} {"Dark Gauntlet" blairs through the sound system. The arena lights darken to be replaced by an eerie blue glow. The ring is surrounded by a dense fog.} RNS: The following is set to be a one fall no time limit match officiated by Tony Moon. Coming to the ring first, he stands at 7 feet and one inch and weighs in at 310 pounds. Where he hails from, no one knows. He is Necropolis! {Necropolis comes through the entrance eveloped in soft green spot lights giving an eerie feeling to the arena. Necropolis makes his way to the ring.} RNS: And his opponent. {"Stay Away" by Nirvana plays throughout the arena. Smoke fills the arena.} RNS: Hailing from The Belfrey, he stands at six feet and three inches, and weighs in at 230 pounds. He is Shadow Runner! {Shadow Runner walks to the rng wearing his leather jacket and carrying a baseball bat.} DAJ: Well here it comes. [DING DING DING] RNJ: And they lock up. LUE: You know, both of these men have fairly similar backgrounds. They are both sort of wandering loners. Shadow Warrior ran away from home as a child, and as far as we know Necropolis has been all alone forever. DAJ: How's that possible? LUE: I don't know. Why don't you ask him? DAJ: Are you crazy?
