Max Danger Biography Height 6'0" Weight 208 lbs Hometown Augusta, Maine Date of Birth August 23, 1977 Alignment Unpopular Heel Entrance Music Born of a Broken Man - Rage Against The Machine Manager/Valet Jessica Barrett-Dox Personality Max will often mock his opponent. He is a bit of a cheater. Using a lot of the old school heel tricks such as placing his feet on ropes for pins, thumb to the eyes, etc. Inside the ring, he's all business until he gains the upperhand, and that's when the showboating comes into play. Out of the ring, Max tends to be more laid back, and being a goofball. History Max Danger was born Marcus Alexander Dox on August 23rd, 1977 in Augusta, Maine, the place he still calls home today. He was the youngest of three children, and they were all very close. He lost his sister in an automobile accident, while she was in an ambulance on her way to the hospital. His father, a wrestler, died when Marcus was very young, in a plane crash. He was on his way to compete in Tennessee, when his plane went down. Marcus, began his wrestling training early on in life, and was trained by his uncle, who was also a wrestling. When his uncle died due to cancer, Max gave up wrestling awhile, until his brother started to take him with him on his wrestling tours. His brother got him enrolled in a wrestling school in Toronto, Canada, where Edge and Christian of the WWF were also trained. Marcus became very good, and started to work the indies in the Northeast. Marcus started to gain a following, so much so in fact, that the WWF signed him to a developmental contract. Marcus' brother also had been given one, and was about to debut on television when he passed away. Marcus' brother was facing a local Las Vegas wrestler at a house show, and broke his neck while taking a piledriver. This cause Marcus to leave wrestling for a good two years. When the passion for wrestling became too great, Marcus took the names of his father's and brother's characters and created Max Danger. He took to the indies once again, before being signed by the FWO. He was the best wrestler in the company, and quickly started to pick up titles left and right before it's closing in early 2000. Max Danger had also started to gain a following in the GFWA, where he came in being held the US Heavyweight Title, but a poor judgement from the creative staff soon had him jobbing in the midcard. Max would leave the GFWA and return to the newly opened FWO, where he captured the World title before it closed again. Max Danger came back to the GFWA and captured the Light Heavyweight Title, before the fed merged into the gWa. He quickly left after losing the unification match of the Lightweight Championships. Max bought the OWA around this time, and started to run a very successful company. He quickly handed the reigns over to new wife, Jessica Barrett-Dox, so he could buy and run the GFWA. Both companies were prospering, but Max got the itch to wrestle again. So he signed three contracts, with the aWa, gWa, and FSW, seeing which would fit him best. Max started on the road again, and the OWA feel apart, resulting in him having to merge his two companies, but he left his wife handle that as he had some wrestling to do. Max quickly left the aWa, where he was teaming with Shinjotani, due to a very underqualified creative staff. In the gWa, Max hasn't had success, where he is also teaming with Shinjotani, so Max is concentrating all his efforts in Five Star Wrestling. This is where he's at his best. This is the place he feels comfortable. Max has said in interviews that the FSW has made him find his love for wrestling once again, and it shows in his performances. Max has started off nicely, and got himself into a bit of a war with Johnny F*ckin' Mayhem, as he likes to call him. But Max is far from his potential in the FSW, he's still got more to show, but is waiting on the right time. Max loves the FSW, and doesn't look to be leaving anytime soon. FSW gold could be in this man's future, and when it happens, things are going to get a little more interesting around the FSW. To Max Danger the sky is the limit, and he's going to give it all he has to be a success in the FSW.
