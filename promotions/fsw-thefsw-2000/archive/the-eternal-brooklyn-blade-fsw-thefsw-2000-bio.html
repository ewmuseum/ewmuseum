'The Eternal' Brooklyn Blade Biography Real Name Brooklyn Blade Nicknames 'The Eternal', 'The Immortal' Hometown Brooklyn, New York Birthday 15th July 1979 Height 6'5' Weight 280 Fed Titles None as of yet. Fed Awards None as of yet. Biography Brooklyn Blade. Once a child. Once a kid. Once a boy. Now finally a man. But nothing is known about him. Not his hometown. Not his birthdate. Not even his name. People have assumed that he was born in Brooklyn, New York but that was never confirmed by Brooklyn the person itself. No matter where he goes, silence was always the key in his life. Brooklyn enjoyed silence. Maybe he loved it. For he always kept to himself. Nobody talked to him, nobody wants to talk to him. He's living in his own world. A world with his own rules, a world without passion. A dead world. However, he does believe in something. Something in which he holds so close to him, 'The Code'. Every since the first time people saw him, he has always been holding on to 'The Code' in which he lives his life the way 'The Code' wants him to live. He may be without a past, but his future is dominated by 'The Code'. It's like a religion to him. But maybe it is more than that. Maybe it is his life. Nobody knew Brooklyn Blade. No one knows about his past. And no one will know of his future. All that's important right now for him, is the present. A present where hell is life. A present where 'The Code' is life. What the past holds for him, nobody will know. What the future is offering him, only time will tell. Brooklyn's life is like a story. A never-ending story where chapters complicate each other to the point where each chapter seems like a different life. Only Brooklyn Blade knows what it means. And that's where he believes 'The Code' comes in. For to him, there is no past. And there will be no future as well if there is no Code. For history will repeat itself.
