Maximum Velocity, 6 April 2001 (part 3 of 3)

6 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DATE: 4/6/01 ARENA: Kiel Center - St. Louis, MO

Lynn Lane: Fans we are back. Let's go to the ring.

[Carlton Carter's music starts playing and he walks out from the back] Willy Wilson:: Making his way to the ring... Carlton Carter! [There is a mixed reaction from the crowd. Jade Black's music starts to play.] Willy Wilson: And his opponent... Jade Black !! [ Black runs down the ramp and slides into the ring. The two go toe to toe with each other.]
Sam Collins: This match is underway!!! [Black gets the upper hand early, dropping Carter with a DDT. Black gets up and starts to stomp on the fallen Carter.
He raises Carter to his feet and drops him with a dropkick. He stands and applies a texas cloverleaf to Carter. Carter scream in pain and tries to make it to the ropes.]
Lynn Lane: This match is gonna be great, I can tell. Black is out here with avengence!! [Carter drags himself and Black to the ropes and makes the break. Black doesn't let go.] Sam Collins: Jade Black better let go or he could loose this match!! Lynn Lane: I don't think he really cares!! [The ref makes Black break the hold. Black turns around and continues to stomp down on Carter. The ref tells him to stop but he doesn't listen, he just pushes him away. The ref comes back and is pushed away again.]
Lynn Lane: He better watch it or he may get DQed. [The ref comes back and Black punches him droping him down in a heap.] Lynn Lane: Now that was uncalled for!! [At the top of the ramp stands Big Daveylicious. He makes his way down to the ring.] Lynn Lane: What the hell is he doing here. He's not in this match.. [Big Daveylicious gets in the ring and grabs Jade Black from behind. He turns him around and chokeslams him to the mat. Carter gets up as lifts Black to his feet. Big Daveylicious gets out of the ring as the referee makes it to his feet. Carter DDT's Black hard into the canvas and makes the cover. 1..2..3 Ding..Ding..Ding..]
Lynn Lane: This match is over. Sam Collins: Jade Black should of won that match, but he didn't hahaha. Lynn Lane: Here is your winner,Carlton Carter !! [The referee raises Carters hand high in the air]

Lynn Lane: Is Carlton Carter apart of The Coalition now since Big D helped him out?

Sam Collins: How should I know. And if he is in then so what?

Lynn Lane: Don't get smart man.

Sam Collins: In my announcing contract it says I can do what I want when I'm at the table. So there.

Lynn Lane: Whatever let's just call this next match which involves The Coalition.

Lynn Lane:

Well fans, this next match should be a doosy. Tommy Taker and The Epitome Of Evil will be taking each other on in a No DQ match up. Fans, this one should take the word no DQ to a hole damn new level. These two men should bring us some crazy excitement.

Sam Collins:

 I have been looking forward to this one all week, I bet you the fans are ready fo this match. I want to see Taker and The Epitome Of Evil rip each other a new one and show absolutley no mercy towards each other. This one should not end until someone can not go on. Well the competitors are ready, lets get it on.

['The bell rings, DING.DING.DING. Taker and EOE lock up and begin to grapple with each other, EOE using his power advantage pushes Taker into the turnbuckle corner and kicks him in the ribs. EOE grabs Taker and lands a hip toss out of the corner. Taker gets up and charges, EOE hits a backbody drop over the top rope. Taker grabs the top rope in mid air and lands soundly on the apron, Taker climbs to the top rope with EOE's back turned, Taker launches himself and lands a perfectly executed missle drop kick on EOE. EOE flies back and Taker gets up, Taker stalks EOE and kicks him in the ribs. EOE slowly gets up and Taker backs off, both men lock up again, EOE grabs Taker by the throat and throws him into the turnbuckle corner causing the ring to shake. Taker bounces off but EOE lands a thrust to the jaw of Tommy Taker. Taker wobbles and EOE charges at him and nails Taker over the top rope with a devastating clothesline. EOE begins to celebrate to the crowd as Tommy Taker starts to get up from the floor on the ringside area. Taker slides under the ring and pulls out a table. He sets the table up on the outside of the ring and grabs a chair, Taker with the chair slides back into the ring and stares down EOE.]

Lynn Lane:

 This does not look good for The Epitome Of Evil. Tommy Taker has a chair and EOE doesn't, this seems totally un-fair. But,. all is fair in love and the FSW. If I were the Epitome Of Evil, I would back off and grab chair of my own and attack Tommy Taker. This is just brutal.

Sam Collins:

 This is going to be brutal. Tommy Taker is a mad man when he gets that steel chair and if The Epitome Of Evil is not careful, he may begin to learn why Mick Foley's brain is so scrambled. What The Epitome Of Evil must do is control all his anger and energy and set it off all at once in an explosion of power and try to take Taker out.

[The EOE stares down Tommy Taker and Taker stares back at EOE with a smirk on his face. Taker then tosses the chair to the EOE and motions at him to hit him with it. The EOE looks confused then grips the chair. He steps back and, CRACK. Tommy Taker is nailed over the head with a steel chair shot delievered by the EOE. The sound of the metal smashing Taker's Skull rings through out the arena. Taker does not fall though, his face is covered with blood and their is a large puddle of it around his feet. Taker smiles and tells the EOE to do it again. EOE looks at Taker shocked and bounces off the ropes and comes crashing down with the chair over the head of Tommy Taker. Taker falls back and flips over the top rope. Taker comes crashing down through the table that was set up. Would pieces go flying into the crowd. EOE looks stund as Tommy Taker gets up from the fall, bleeding badly from the chair shots. Taker grabs another table and sets it up over the broken one. EOE looks shocked as Taker runs full speed and slides into the ring dripping blood everywhere. EOE tries to clothesline Taker but he ducks and lands a belly to belly suplex. As this happens Big D walks out to ringside and starts to make his way towards the ring.]

Sam Collins:

 I do not like the looks of this, Big D seems to have a sinister smile on his face. Damn it, I knew a match of this calabur would never happen in the FSW, somebody always has to come out and scre it all up, this is totally out of whack. Damn Big D, he is just ruining a great match, if I were Painkiller I would fire him, But noooo.

Lynn Lane:

 Damn it, thats it, Painkiller has sent out Big D to interfear in this match up. No way is this happening, Big D is taking orders and not even thinking for himself. If Painkiller did not send him out here, then who did? This has to be the work of Painkiller, only he is this diabolical.

[Big D slides into the ring and punches EOE in the back of the head, Big D grabs the chair and smashes it over the head of EOE causing him to bleed from the back of his head. Taker climbs on top, the count, 1...2...3.... and thanks to the help of the diabolical Big D, Tommy Taker picks up a win against EOE. Big D is on the top turnbuckle celebrating as the bell goes, DING.DING.DING. but the bell is not being rung by the time keeper, Big D turns around and is nailed by EOE who is holding the ring bell. Big D goes down. Tommy Taker tries to leave the ring but EOE stops him. EOE cracks him too over the head with the bell. EOE grabs Taker and lands a powerbomb through the table as the scene goes to a commercial. It fades from a commercial break to the ringside area.]

Lynn Lane: Fans we are back and it's time for the main event. But we arean't sure who will take Tommy Taker's place as he seemed to have suffered a crack rib after the powerbomb on the table by EOE. So it will be intresting to see who comes out.

Sam Collins: I suppose. Well everyone is ready so let's do this.

Willy Wilson: The following match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring at this time, Marvelous and Johnny Mayhem.
[Mike Marvelous and Mayhem  make their way to the ring.] Willy Wilson: And their opponents, Tommy Taker and Big Daveylicious! [Big Daveylicious walks out and behind him comes Carlton Carter!  DING..DING..DING.] Sam Collins: We are now underway.  This is set to be a great matchup.

Lynn Lane: Yeah but more important then that. It seems Carlton Carter is now apart of The Coalition which makes them that much stronger. [Right away, Carter grabs Marvelous, and throws him to the ropes.  Carter goes for the closeline, but Marvelous ducks, and then hits Carter with a closeline of his own.  Marvelous then begins stomping away at Carter.  Marvelous then picks up Carter, and sends him to the turnbuckle.  Marvelous runs, leaps in the air, just as Carter moves, and Marvelous goes chest first into the turnbuckle.]
Lynn Lane: Ooh.  That had to hurt. [Marvelous falls flat on his back, as Carter goes and makes the tag to Big D.  Big D jumps into the ring, as Marvelous begins to climb to his feet.   Big D runs at Marvelous, and drop kicks Marvelous in the chest.] Lynn Lane: Marvelous sure is taking a beating to the chest, huh? Sam Collins: He sure is Lynn. [Big D, then starts stomping on Marvelous's chest, going for the weakened spot.  Big D then picks up Marvelous, and goes to slam him face into the turnbuckle, but his plan backfires, and Marvelous slams Big D' face into the turnbuckle.  Big D falls to the mat, and Marvelous uses every bit of energy that he has to make the tag to Mayhem.  Mayhem gets in the ring, and starts giving Big D fists to the face.  Big D is once again on his back in the middle of the ring.  Mayhem gets Big D in a leg lock, and keeps it there for about one minute.  Big D is hurting, but will not give up.   After the move is released, Mayhem starts stomping on the leg, and repeatedly slamming Big D' knee into the mat.  After Big D gains the energy to get up, he punches Mayhem in the head a few times, before limping over to make the tag to Carter.  Big D can barely walk on his leg.  Paramedics rush to the ring, and decide that Big D may have a broken leg, and they can not let him wrestle under that condition, so Big D is taken to the back, while his partner Carter is left to fight two people on his own.  Mayhem kicks Carter in the gut, and gives him a powerbomb.  Mayhem then climbs the top turnbuckle, and gives Carter a huge leg drop, right across the chest.  Mayhem goes for the pin.  1..2..Carter kicks out. Mayhem gets up and makes the tag in to Marvelous.]

Lynn Lane: It's two on one folks so this could be bad for Carter.

[Marvelous picks Carter up and sends him into the ropes. Marvelous telegraphs a clothesline but Carter kicks Marvelous in the face. Carter runs over and hits Mayhem in the face and that knocks him off the apron and he falls and hits the guard rail. Carter turns back into the ring and picks up Marvelous. He places Marvelous on the turnbuckle and Carter climbs up there with him. Carter lifts Marvelous in the air and throws him to the mat. Suddenly Painkiller walks out to the ring.! Carter covers Marvelous. The ref counts. ....1 ....2 ..Marvelous kicks out.]

Lynn Lane: This can't be good as Painkiller is coming to the ring.

Sam Collins: He is just making the match more entertaining.

[Painkiller enters the ring and gets the refs attention. Big D goes to the outside and he gets a couple of chairs and throws them in the ring. Big D slides back in the ring. Carter picks Marvelous up. Both Carter and Big D get the two chairs. They then repeatedly hit chair shots on Marvelous' head busting him wide open. They then swing the chairs at the same time and sandwhich Marvelou's head inbetween them. They throw the chairs outside of the ring and Carter covers Marvelous. Painkiller points the ref to the cover as Mayhem comes in the ring. Mayhem tries to breakup the cover but Big D levels him with a big foot. The ref counts. ....1 ....2 ....3 DING..DING..DING]

Willy Wilson: Here are your winners, Big Daveylicious and Carlton Carter!

[Painkiller joins in on the beating of Marvelous and Mayhem. Suddenly the lights go out. Pyro then shoots up at the entrance ramp as EOE runs down to the ring. The Coalition quickly get out ouf the ring as the FSW copywright flashes on the screen.]

Lynn Lane: Fans we are out of time. We will see you on Glory!

[Painkiller and EOE stare at eachother as the scene fades to black.]
