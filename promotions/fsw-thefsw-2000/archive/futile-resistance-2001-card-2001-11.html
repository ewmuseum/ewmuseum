Futile Resistance 2001 card

What this is The fed's own preview page for its Futile Resistance pay-per-view (the Classics section): the announced matches with a paragraph on each, as the page stood in its Wayback capture of 5 January 2002. The page carries no date of its own; the Classics index lists the show under November 2001.
|Futile Resistence-2001|


Main Event
Chris Fear(c) vs Stefan Douglas
Standard - World Title
At Hallowed Knights Stefan Douglas lost his FSW World Title to a man he thought to be his enemy. The following Paranoia he teamed with him, allying himself against the new threat: Jonny Trip. The following week, Johnny Phantom a man that was seeing the goodness in Stefan Douglas, Stefan didn't realize the hate within Johnny Phantom who teamed with the newest allegiance of Painkiller, Sean Cylear, and Jonny Trip. But that wasn't the final blow, as soon after... Chris Fear turned on him. Stefan Douglas was alone with only one person to trust, thought it was someone he never though of: Blitz. Now Stefan steps up against the leading titlest of the group and prays that for one night... he won't go home in a stretcher.

Punisher 2000(c) vs Tommy Taker vs Jonny Trip
Ladder - Intercontinental Title
At Hallowed Knights Punisher 2000 gained the IC Title in a ladder style match against Horizon and Blitz. Now he risks the chance of losing the exact same way. Through the trials and tribulations Tommy Taker now looks to make his mark upon the FSW by way of winning the IC title in a destructive Ladder Match. By the same token, Jonny Trip striving by making his way up the fame ladder in only a day prepares to try and prove his worthiness in the FSW.

Jack Brooze(c) vs PS Mad vs Sean Cylear
Rising and Extreme Titles
Jack Brooze seems to really be needing a phsyco ward instead of a title defense, but it looks like the defense will come before that. In this match, there will be two falls. The first one to make a pin or submission will win the Rising Star Title and the second to make the pin or submission will win the Extreme Title. The chances are likily for a man to walk out with two titles in one night.

Blake & Human vs Tommy Kane & Atrius
Standard Tag - Tag Team Titles
Blake and Human have shown to be a non-cooperative team as Tommy Kane and Atrius now look to work on that field with Atrius' second attempt at a tag team. Will it be successful is unknown. The first step will be to get past Tommy Kane and his imaginary friend...
