|New FSW Ownership|
Brandon E. | 07/14/01
On Thursday afternoon Painkiller sat down with six men. Five of these mens names will remain unknown at this time. But the sixth man was Brandon E.. On Thursday Painkiller signed the FSW over to these six men. They will take control of the federation after the Defiance show. Brandon has been given the day to day operations of the FSW. And he was quoted as saying, "Well with me in charge. There is going to be a lot of changes in the way things are run. You will see a new FSW. A Better FSW...." The other five men will be on a high council thing called The Beholders. They have power in the FSW along with Brandon. They will work togethor to run Five Star Wrestling.

The contracts were signed roughly around 9 pm eastern time. They met in the FSW Headquarters in Pittsburgh. Painkiller said that he wasn't thrilled signing the FSW away. Since it was his creation. But he said it would be better for the company if he gave it some fresh guys. Painkiller stills has a major stock investment in FSWE. So he is hoping that it still does good.

The new ownership will work like a checks and balances system. Brandon holds the majority of the power as a singal person. But the 5 man council has control over him. Painkiller set it up in a way that they have to work togethor. IF they can't, the FSW will surely fail. Painkiller also said that the reason he set it up this way is that there is too much coruption in FSW and other federations. With the way the owernship is setup now there will be no coruption. You have to be apart of the team if you want it to suceed.

The new owners will have control after the Defiance show goes off the air. Brandon said that he will be at the show to get to know all the wrestlers. There is also rumors that several of the wrestlers are preparing speeches for next weeks Paranoia in honor of Painkiller. Whether they do that or not is unknown at this time. More information on this as it comes in.
