S. 'Sterling' Silver Biography Height 6'0" Weight 231 lbs Hometown Las Vegas, Nevada Date of Birth 01/01/79 Alignment Heel Entrance Music Anthem for the Year 2000 - Silverchair Manager/Valet Yes Personality Sterling Silver is very, very vain, and concerned with his looks to a fault. His in-ring posing and flexing is usually his downfall in close matches, as when he has his opponents on the ropes, he will usually stop to pose for the crowd. In the ring, his resilience is annoying at best, as he always seems to get up even after suffering a power move, and always has time to make sure he hasn't mussed his perfect hair before continuing in the match. He will continually trash talk during the match, putting down his opponent's physique, clothing choices, girlfriends... whatever he can. History S. Sterling Silver did not want to be a wrestler. His first love was acting, and he was damned good at it. Sterling's father was a successful film actor and his mother a makeup artist for well-known movie stars. Silver's early childhood was spent in the comfort of upper middleclass as everything he ever wanted was fed to him on a silver spoon. In Junior High and High School, Sterling had acted in his school plays, and after his early successes on the stage, knew that this was the life for him. After graduating drama school in 1995, he spent his time auditioning for movie parts and partying with the Las Vegas crowd. It was during this hectic time in his life that he saw an opening in a new David Arquette film sequel, called "Ready to Rumble II". Intrigued, he thought he might check it out. "Ready to Rumble", of course, was a comedy set around the world of professional wrestling, and Silver was amazed at the agility and toughness of the wrestlers that were working on the set as consultants. Silver won a small part in the film, and spent the next three months rubbing elbows with the professional wrestlers working on the set. After being convinced to do his own stunts, playing the part of a struggling wrestler, Silver and the wrestlers were amazed at his natural ability to learn and execute the moves perfectly after little training. Soon, these pro wrestlers convinced Silver that he was missing out on life on the open road, travelling from city to city and performing in front of a live audience every night. Silver, already somewhat of a glory hog, missed the live performance of his youth and wanted to impress the people with his new arsenal of high-flying moves and his incredible physique. At once, he quit his acting career and set off with these wrestlers to tour the Indy circuits, where Silver's trademark 100-watt smile and his insane "Tarnisher" finishing piledriver made headlines wherever they stopped. Soon, these headlines reached the desk of the FSW owners, and history was made.
