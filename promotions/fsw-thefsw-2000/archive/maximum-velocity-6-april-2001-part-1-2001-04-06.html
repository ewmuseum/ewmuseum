Maximum Velocity, 6 April 2001 (part 1 of 3)

6 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DATE: 4/6/01 ARENA: Kiel Center - St. Louis, MO

[The scene fades to the Maximum Velocity promo. The scene fades from there into the Key Arena. Fireworks go around around the entrance and around the ring. The fans stand as the show begins. They begin to start chanting 'FSW! FSW! FSW!' The camera pans around the crowd and they hold a varitey of signs. The cameras focus on the announcers table.]

Lynn Lane: Hello everyone and we are finally back after a long two week abscene. We are glad you could tune into to Maximum Velocity this evening as we have a pretty good show lined up for you.

Sam Collins: That's right fans. We have the World champion, Mike Marvelous, here tonight and he will be in the main event with Johnny Mayhem taking on The Coaltion. Plus the Extreme, Rising Star and Prime Time titles will be on the line this evening.

Lynn Lane: Speaking of the Extreme title. It will be on the line in our first match of the evening. Shawn Davies will finally get his first shot at the FSW Extreme title here in Five Star Wrestling.
Sam Collins: Yes this should be an interesting match up, as both are very experienced and talented Hardcore wrestlers.
Willy Wilson: The following bout is scheduled for one fall, for the FSW Extreme Championship, under Hardcore rulings. Coming to the ring at this time, the challenger, The "Hardcore One" Shawn Davies!
[Shawn Davies walks to the ring as his music hits the PA System.] Willy Wilson: And the champion, Jack Miller! [Jack Miller walks down the aisle with a steel chair and enters the ring as his music hits the PA System.]

Lynn Lane: This should be a great match. [Shawn Davies wastes no time getting the match started as he hops to his feet and charges across the ring. Jack Miller is still holding the steel chair aloft when Davies smashes into him with a tackle. The champion loses his grip on the implement of doom and it falls to the concrete with a clatter. Davies continues the assault as he rains punches down on Miller's head, he then stands up, grabs Miller's legs and positions his back to the turnbuckle. Davies grins wildly as he prepares to slingshot Miller into the turnbuckle. Miller looks up at Davies with glassy eyes before latching his hands around Davies' ankle and pushing Davies' legs back with his own. Davies unable to keep his balance falls on his back as Miller sits up and follows Davies over. Miller turns the tables and begins pounding his fists into Davies' skull. Miller then climbs to the second rope and raises his fist the crowd cheers wildly as Miller hops from the second rope and drives his knee into Davies' face. Miller repeats the manoeuvre three more times before picking up Davies and whipping him into the opposite turnbuckle. Davies' head snaps back as he connects with the turnbuckle and falls forward on his face. Miller calmly strolls over to the fallen challenger and steps through the ropes and onto the apron. Davies is just getting up on his hands and knees when Miller slingshots over and drops a guillotine on the back of Davies' head.]
Lynn Lane: What a terrific match up! Both superstars have got off to a great start. [The "Hardcore One" Shawn Davies falls in a heap as the crowd begins to chant "WE WANT EXTREME! WE WANT EXTREME! WE WANT EXTREME! WE WANT EXTREME!"]
Sam Collins: Oh shut up will you! Lynn, can you tell these moron fans to shut up please? [The Champion obliges as he slides under the bottom rope and walks over to retrieve a steel chair wrapped in barbed wire from behind the announcer's table.]
Lynn Lane: Hey! Where on earth did he get that from? Sam Collins: And what was it doing by the announcer's table? This is very mysterious Lynn. [Miller then tosses his trusty sidekick into the ring before sliding under the bottom rope. He then retrieves the barbed wire and nail-encrusted chair and walks over to Davies. Miller raises the steel chair wrapped in barbed wire above his head but before he can bring the weapon to bear Davies delivers an uppercut to the family jewels. Miller drops the steel chair wrapped in barbed wire behind him as he falls to both knees and clutches his groin. Davies regains his footing and shakes his head to clean out the cobwebs he then sprints to the far rope, bounces off and delivers a viscous dropkick to the face of the still kneeling Miller. Miller is bent backwards under the force of the blow but quickly pops back up with a scream as his back connects with the now treacherous steel chair wrapped in barbed wire. Davies then lifts Miller to his feet and whips him into the ropes he follows the Extreme Champion and lays him out with a roaring elbow.]
Lynn Lane: Now that sounded nasty! [The Crowd begins to chant "LET'S GET HARDCORE!" As the "Hardcore One" Shawn Davies is more than willing to oblige as he retrieves the steel chair wrapped in barbed wire and charges at Miller, who has managed to pull himself back up with help from the top rope. Miller ducks at the last possible moment and back body drops Davies over the top. The steel chair wrapped in barbed wire flies through the air and lands a few feet away the steel steps as Davies crashes into the unforgiving concrete. Miller follows Davies to the floor with a suicide planza.] Lynn Lane: This match just seems too becoming more and more barbaric as time goes on. Sam Collins: Oh I just love it when you see two people who really hate each other, tear one another limb from limb!
[Miller reaches down and lifts Davies to his feet and whips him into the guardrail. Davies sags against the guardrail as Miller charges and attempts a leaping splash on his prone opponent. Davies slides to his right at the last moment and Miller slams into the steel. Davies follows it up by nailing a neck breaker on the dazed champion before stumbling and collapsing on the floor. Davies rolls to his feet and grasps the railing as he begins to stomp a mud hole into the champion. Davies then props Miller's body in the corner of the railing and takes several steps back from the champion. He turns and with a snarl charges Miller who at the last moment executes a drop toe hold that drives Davies' head into the railing; busting him wide open. Davies shakes his head as a stumbling Miller lays a kick into Davies' midsection.
Miller begins to lay punches into Davies' mush and Davies responds in kind as the two begin to rail each other with headshots. Miller reaches back with a wild haymaker that sends both men crashing over the railing. FSW Security quickly floods the area and separates the crowd from the hardcore combatants. The pair continue to brawl at the railing until Davies ducks under a Miller over hand right and executes a release belly-to-belly suplex sending the champion back over the railing with a thump. Davies quickly follows the champion over. Miller regains his footing only to receive a kick to the gut and a DDT from the challenger. Davies then hops up on the ring apron, takes off on a dead sprint and leaps off before delivering an elbow to the chest of the champion. He then lifts Miller and whips him into the steel steps. Miller hits the steps with a loud thunk before somersaulting over the top. Davies charges the fallen champion and attempts to springboard off the steps, only to be met with the business end of the steel chair wrapped in barbed wire. The blow causes the already cut face of Davies to erupt into a crimson mask as the challenger crashes to the floor. Miller regains his footing and moves towards the ring apron, pulling out a 10 foot steel ladder. Miller lifts the ladder and charges at Davies who is standing directly in front of a ring post. Davies sees his predicament and rolls out of the way at the last moment. The ladder slams into the post as a cursing Miller clutches his shoulder. The champion then stalks over to a still down Davies and begins to lay the boots into him. Miller gives a satisfied smile before turning and retrieving the ladder. Miller then lifts the ladder and shoves it over the top rope. The ladder hangs halfway over the rope as the champion lifts the challenger and tosses him under the bottom rope. Miller then hops onto the turnbuckle and climbs to the top before driving an elbow into his opponent. Davies rolls to his stomach but the champion will not relent and lift Davies to his feet and begins chopping away at him. Davies counters with a drop toehold that slams the champ's face into the ladder and opening up a nasty gash across his forehead. He then lifts Miller and whips him into a turnbuckle. Miller slumps as his back connects with the turnbuckle. Davies follows it up with a serious of kicks, driving the champion to the mat. Davies then ties him to the tree of woe, climbs to the second rope and plants his foot into Novak's crotch before bouncing up and down on the middle rope. Davies hops off the second rope as wounded Miller disengages himself from the top rope. Davies lifts Miller and tries whipping the champion into the ropes but Miller reverses and whips Davies in, nailing him with a sidewalk slam as the challenger rebounds back to him. Miller steps away from Davies and brings back his right hand in preparation for a standing lariat. Davies shakily regains his feet as Miller whips his arm forward. Davies ducks the lariat and counters with a boot to the gut and delivers cradle driver. He then begins to stalk towards the ladder before a wicked grin crosses his blood stained face. He looks to the crowd and points to his trashcan at ringside. The crowd erupts as Davies rolls to the floor and retrieves the trusty trashcan, as he then ascends the top rope, reaches into the trashcan and squirts lighter fluid into the can. Davies reaches into his pocket and grabs some Zippo. Davies flicks his thumb, igniting the Zippo. Jack Miller rolls to his feet and charges Shawn Davies shoving him off the top rope. Davies' Zippo falls into the trashcan and ignites the barrel as the challenger is launched forward.]
 Lynn Lane: Hey that trashcan is on fire! Sam Collins: This match just gets better and better! [Davies rolls from the wreckage and begins stumbling back to the ring as an exhausted Miller begins to ascend the ladder. Davies rolls into the ring as Miller's feet pass the middle of the ladder. Davies regains his footing and with a final burst of fury he rushes the ladder and slams his shoulder into the side of it. Miller falls from his precarious perch and slams into the guardrail. He hangs there limply as Davies heads to the outside and lifts up the ring apron. He pulls out a wooden table, to receive a huge pop from the fans. He throws the table into the ring. Davies then picks up the now half unconscious Jack Miller, and throws him into the ring as well. Davies enters the ring shortly afterwards, and begins to set up the table onto its all fours, then picking Miller back up and throwing him onto the table. Davies then sets the ladder back up and climbs the ladder at a snail's pace his body revealing the war he has been through tonight. Davies finally reaches the top rung and begins to pose to the thousands of euphoric fans, who anticipate his next move. Then suddenly Davies lands an immense frog splash from the top of the 10-foot ladder upon the FSW Extreme Champion, Jack Miller, straight through the wooden table. Jack Miller had just been split in half, as Shawn Davies made the cover, the ref counted....1.....2.....3!]
Willy Wilson: And here is your winner, and new FSW Extreme Champion.Shawn Davies! Lynn Lane: Shawn Davies has won the title!!! The "Hardcore One" has won the title!!! He has become the FSW Extreme Champion! Sam Collins: Yeah he finally did it. Well good for him. Lynn Lane: Pandemonium has just been witnessed by the thousands of spectators here at ringside, as they were amazed at what both these athletes would go through to become victorious.
Sam Collins: Well I'm just amazed at both these athlete's stupidity! Lynn Lane: Well it looks like the former three time FSW Extreme Champion Jack Miller is severly injured, as the EMT's are now on their way to ringside.

Sam Collins: And while they are doing that we will be taking a commercial break.

[The scene fades to a commercial break. It fades to the backstage area. Ryan 'Mush' Wilson is seen talking with a few FSW wrestlers. The scene fades back to the ringside area.]

Lynn Lane: Well fans the man you just saw is our newest staff member here at the FSW. Ryan 'Mush'Wilson. He should prove to be a great asset.

Sam Collins: I guess. But we were doing find without him. Let's just go to the next match.

Lynn Lane: I was getting to that. Don't be in such a rush. This next match will be for the Rising Star title. Willy Wilson is standing by in the ring.

Willy Wilson: The following match is scheduled for one fall. It is for the Rising Star title. Coming to the ring at this time, S. Sterling Silver!

[S. Sterling Silver makes his way to the ring.]

Willy Wilson: And his opponent, Next!

[Next's music comes on and he walks down the aisle and enters the ring.]

Lynn Lane: Here comes Next. He seems to be in excellent condition.

[Next enters the ring and as he does Silver runs at him and kicks him in the head sending him to the mat. Silver starts to kick Next in the back as the ref rings the bell. DING...DING...DING]

Sam Collins: A sneak attack by Silver and this match is underway!

[Silver picks Next up and sends him into the ropes. Next hits the ropes and Silver goes for a clothesline but Next ducks it. Next turns around as Silver does and Next throws a right hand that sends Silver to the ground. Next picks Silver up. Next kicks Silver in the stomach. Next jumps in the air and hits a jumping DDT on Silver. Next quickly covers Silver. The ref counts. ....1 ....2 ....Silver kicks out. Next picks Silver up and sends him into the corner. Silver htis the corner and as he stumbles away from it Next connects with a clothesline and Silver hits the mat.]

Lynn Lane: What a viscious clothesline from Next.

Sam Collins: Yeah that almost took Silver out of his boots. Next is picking up the pace now.

[Next goes through the ropes and he starts to climb the turnbuckle. Silver slowly gets to his feet as Next stands up on the turnbuckle. As Silver looks up Next jumps off and Next connects with a missle drop kick that sends Silver to the mat. Next gets up and he covers Silver. The ref counts. ....1 ....2 ....Silver kicks out. Next gets up and he whips Silver into the ropes but Silver reverses it and Next hits the ropes. Silver telegraphs a clothesline but Next jumps over him and does a sunset flip. Silver trys to keep his balance but Next finally pushes him over. The ref counts. ....1 ....2 ....Silver kicks out.]

Lynn Lane: Oh that was close.

Sam Collins: Yeah I thought Next was going to win that one.

[Both men get up and Next runs at Silver. Next swings a punch but Silver ducks it. Silver then gets Next in a full nelson. Next fights it but Silver tightens the hold. Silver then lifts Next in the air and he slams him to the mat with a full nelson slam. Silver falls to his knees and covers Next. The ref counts. ....1 ....2 ....Next kicks out. Silver argues with the ref about the count. Silver then picks Next up again. Silver holds Nexts head and then he hits Next in the face and Next falls to the ground. Silver picks Next up again and he sends him into the ropes. Silver swings for a clothesline but Next slides under Silver's legs. Silver turns around and Next kicks him in the stomach. Next then executes a double arm DDT. Next runs towards the ropes and he jumps onto the ropes and he bounces back and lands a moonsault on Next. Next climbs up to the topturnbuckle as Silver slowly gets to his feet. Next jumps off and he lands a bull dog and Silver's face gets slamed into the mat. Next rolls Silver over and the ref counts. .....1 .....2 .....Silver kicks out.!]

Lynn Lane: Silver is hanging in there.

[Suddenly Lance Klipped walks down the aisle.]

Sam Collins: What the...

Lynn Lane: We haven't seen this guy in months. What's he doing here?

[Klipped runs down the aisle and he enters the ring. He runs up behind Next and levels him with a clothesline. Klipped starts pounding Next. The ref sees it and calls for the bell. DING..DING..DING]

Willy Wilson: The result of this match is a no contest!

Sam Collins: This match is out of control.

[Klipped continunes to beat on Next as Silver gets up. Suddenly Christian Diablo climbs over the guard rail and rushes into the ring.]

Lynn Lane: Christian Diablo? Is he under contract here?

[Diablo runs at Klipped but he gets out of the ring. Silver rushes  Diablo but Diablo connects with a superkick that sends Silver to the mat. Diablo then rolls Silver out of the ring. Next gets up as Diablo raises his hand. The scene then fades to a commerical break. It fades back from a commercial break to the ringside area.]
