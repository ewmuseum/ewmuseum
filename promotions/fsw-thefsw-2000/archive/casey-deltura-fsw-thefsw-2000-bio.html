Casey Deltura Biography Real Name Same Nicknames The Tree Hugger, Nature’s Best Friend, Greenpeace Member of The Year, 1989 Hometown Boston, Mass. Birthday Undisclosed Height 6'3' Weight 229 Biography ”The History of Greenpeace Star, Casey Deltura” by Zack Reid. Well, the history of this man is quite a story, I must admit. From humble beginnings in Boston, Mass., Casey grew up as part of a traditional 2.4 children family, his other sibling being his younger brother James, an age gap of 4 years separating the two Deltura brothers. But the brother’s of the family were not the problem in what appears to be a loving American family: it was the parents. Mike and Lisa Deltura were a young couple with two children running around the house and excessive bills to pay. Due to this, their property suffered from severe lack of care, and slowly but surely, the Deltura house became a pit...But not the garden. From a young age, the now 15-year old Casey had taken a shine to looking after the garden and enjoyed working with things natural. Even though the rest of the house was a mess, the garden remained the focal point of all positive comments from outsiders, “People used to walk by and stop for a few minutes, just looking at the beautiful flowers and flourishing plants as I pottered away,” says Casey in a recent interview, “It was like their were enthralled by how the garden contrasted to my parent’s house,” Still deep in debt and paying for as many bills as the postman could fit through the letter box, Mike and Lisa began to stress out over their problems, and more often than not, Casey’s garden bore the brunt of their anger. Plants were ripped from their roots, flowers were chopped apart with blunt knifes, soil was thrown around like snow. On seeing the destruction his parents had caused, Casey confronted them, “I marched right on into the house and demanded to know why they’d ripped apart my beautiful garden,” explains Casey. Now slightly intoxicated on cheap liquor, the only pleasure they appeared to have during their money-tight years, Casey’s parents became infuriated at Casey’s disrespect, or what they though was disrespect, for them and started to bawl at him, “By now I’d had enough. I stormed to my room, grabbed a bag and stuffed it with as much clothes and underwear as I could lay my hands on. I left the house that day, not to return for a good few years,” The 16-year old Casey Deltura was now free of his parent’s debts and anger and did the only thing he thought was worth doing: join Greenpeace. Having been a part-time member during his teenager years, Casey knew just about everything their was to know about the organisation and quickly fitted in. Within a year, Casey had gained a steady job PR’ing for Greenpeace around the US and even abroad, and had started to live the lifestyle of a typical Greenpeace member, using organic products and washing a little less every day, or week to be correct. The year was 1989 and Casey was quickly becoming a rising star in the Greenpeace organisation. That very year, votes from over 300, 000 members worldwide culminated in Casey being awarded the 1989 Greenpeace Member Of The Year award. Filled with delight, Casey accepted the award and in his acceptance speak, actually thanked his mother and father for forcing him to leave... Fast Forward to 2002... Now 29, Casey was a board member of the Greenpeace group but still worked like he did back in the 80’s, on the streets promoting the cause and protesting for the good of nature. He gained the nickname “Tree Hugger” back in 1996 when he stopped construction of a small housing estate in Los Angelos by chaining himself to an Oak tree and refusing to move until they stopped work, which eventually happened. As he came into his small suburban office in his hometown of Boston, the television flicked with an advert for some wrestling show, FSW Glory, and the man’s eyes were suddenly filled with the same hatred he felt when his mother and father destroyed his garden years ago... The wrestlers on the television were eating some sort of energy bar and the slogan claimed it to be “Natural and organic”, which obviously wasn’t true...And Casey would have none of it. Working out in the gym, Casey’s figure didn’t change all that much so he decided to just dive in head-first and apply for this Five Star Wrestler he saw on television... Enter “The Tree Hugger” Casey Deltura to FSW...
