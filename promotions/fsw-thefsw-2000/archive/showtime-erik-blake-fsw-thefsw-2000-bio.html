'Showtime' Erik Blake Biography Real Name Eric Davidson Nicknames ShowTime, The Real Deal Hometown Hollywood, California Birthday 11/03/77 Height 6'4 Weight 246 Biography This bio contains verbal content that may not be suitable for people under the age of 99.9. So if your under that age...I suggest you click out. On the day of November 3rd, 1975 a disaster was born. Eric Davidson, who later turns out to be the filthiest-mouthed nasty guy to ever wrestle. But to explain what happened in between... September 4th, 1980 little Eric was ready for kindegarten. Before in Pre-school he was such a well-mannered boy and had his first kiss...on the cheek. Little Eric went to school and had a magnificant first day. He made lots of new friends and became very friendly with the teachers. But when Little Eric was walking home...disaster struck. His mother and father...brutally murdered on little Eric's bed. This traumatized little Eric into not speaking for 2 years. He was admitted into a Foster home and quickly established in with Foster Parents. When Eric was in second grade he'd hear so much about the little kids talking about their real mother and father. How nice they are and anyone without a mother and father should be shipped to kookyland with diseased monkies. This angered little Eric so much that he yelled right out of his deaf mute and beat the living hell out of every boy in the class. The teachers were puzzled at Eric's behavior. For the next couple of years Eric was considered a bad child. A very very Troubled child. When he was in 5th grade he got suspended 10 times out of the whole school year. The school didn't want to expel him because they knew he was a good kid and was mad about his parents. So his Foster parents agreed to beat Eric until he learned his lesson. They had agreed to only beat him when he did something severely bad...but that changed. Eric started getting beatings for every little thing now. Eating on the couch, coming in the house 5 minutes late, and he got constant beatings from his drunk Foster father. He started to get very attached to his foster mother who had stop beating him and started loving him…but his foster father continued his mean beatings. One night when Eric was 11, he was in his room half-sleep when he heard his foster mother screaming in pain. He ran out and saw his foster mother lying there in a pool of blood. Then the next thing he knows…he wakes up in a hospital with a pair of new set of Foster parents. He was still mourning over the lost of his parents and Foster mother. He attempted many suicide attempts until his new foster parents seeked counseling. Eventually he went back to school and his temper soared higher that he was kicked out of a school for putting a kid in a coma that called him a freak because he was in counseling. He managed to control his temper long enough until he got into 9th grade. Where traditionally the upper-classmen are supposed to make the New Freshman's life a living hell...but Eric didn't want that. So he beat up on the upper-classmen but he always got blamed for it so he was suspended for half of the year. At the end of the year they deided he could not come back. So he transferred into another school...the same thing happened yet again. Then in the middle of the school year he was arrested for assault and battery when a guy accidentally bumped into him while he was running. He was punished for 2 years in jail with a chance of probation. 2 years later he could not go back to school because he'd be a 18 year old in Sophmore year. So he hung out on the streets...selling weed, occasionally meet some chickenhead and leave her. It was apparent that Eric was taking the life of a thug. So he change his name to Blake, he thought Eric was too soft. So Blake started to do real good for himself...he had a nice car...a nice house...plenty of hookers...and he had a job besides selling weed. But Blake was not totally happy. He liked to fight and pumble people. So he was sad one day and watched television as he saw a wrestling promotion shoot some promos. Then the idea struck Blake...He could be a wrestler! But Blake was still a little on the chubby side. So he went to the local gym and started bulking up. While he was bulking up he developed a slight ego as he was working out. It turned into a lot of brawls at the local gym so he was kicked out of that gym forever. But the next day the guys from the gym caught Blake in the streets and beat him senseless and threw him in front of a moving car. Blake was in a coma for 3 and a half weeks. He woke up with a flashback from his childhood…where he walked into a room and saw his original mother and father lying in a pool of blood on his own bed…and the terror as the screamed to the top of his lungs in fear. The doctor came in and told him he had re-occurrence flashback syndrome. It is where a person relives the bad things happened to him/her…but nothing changes…it is just a memory re-lived. So Blake had to deal with his newly discovered disease for the rest of his life. Blake eventually saw a huge t.v. ad for a wrestling powerplant, he jumped to the idea quickly as he enrolled in the wrestling powerplant the next day. He worked so well in there he was given the nickname “ShowTime”. He liked the name and changed his name to “ShowTime” Erik Blake. And that is the legacy of Erik Blake.
