Azala Zameer Biography Real Name Azala Zameer Nicknames The African Nightmare Hometown A small village 35 kilometers north of Blantyre, Malawi in Africa Birthday October 21, 1973 Height 6'10' Weight 478 Biography Azala Zameer was born on a cool October evening to a woman in a tribe who had no husband, and upon his birth night was removed from his mother’s clutches, and she was led out of the small town of Blantyre, Malawi and banished for infedelity. She was seen in the local’s eyes as an unfit creature of evil and was never seen or heard from again. Azala, being seen as nothing more than the spawn of this woman was left alone by a rivers edge to die, but was found 2 days later and allowed a second chance at life by a native tribal leader of a small village just north of the city where he was born. He was raised by the daughter of the village chief and participated in every right of passage that a normal Winatu boy would on his quest to manhood. Azala grew to a great size at a very young age and was immediately declared a “gift from the gods”, for his worth to the village was unparalleled. His normal day consisted of traveling the jungle collecting small trees and hunting just as the other men did, but his practiced speed and maneuverability offered him a heightened ability to catch his prey with practiced precision. His pursuit of knowledge was also far greater than any of the other children in the village and oft times would spend entire nights listening to the stories of a European white man who had traveled to live with the village. Mr. Smith was a Green Peace field worker who had stumbled upon the Winatu tribe by accident and adopted them, just as they did him. He taught Azala how to read and write and the young giant became quite knowledgeable of modern economics, math, and history. His vast knowledge in these fields however never supplied Azala Zameer with much to say as he most times kept his thoughts to himself, he was a quiet child at heart, yet something inside him burned, a great yearning to explore the world and become a man, one whom his mother could be proud of as well as the entire village. Azala Zameer could no longer be held in his native village, and in the dark of night snuck off with no money, into the jungle, looking to find what it was that would make him a man. The 18-year-old male traveled for almost six months, making good of the teachings he had from Mr. Smith in navigation, traveling south, sleeping alone under cover of the thick jungle canopy, until one day he had stumbled upon a small camp. The camp was fairly heavily guarded and just as Azala was about to make his way up to the front gate, he felt a sharp, piercing pain in his lower back. When Zameer came to, he was inside a small hut, with one other man, a small white man dressed in military garb, carrying a small switch under his right arm. The man was none other than a military defector of the South African Armed Unit, a man whose beliefs didn’t quite mesh with the peaceful order of his government at the time. This is the man who would mold Azala Zameer into the machine of destruction through propaganda and hateful literature. Lt. Fredric W. Mandela took Azala in and fed him full helpings of hate and rage, preaching about the sins of Babylon, which he referred to the United States by. Lt. Mandela focused on the persecution of the African land by the “White Devils” of Babylon as well as falsely accusing them of an attempt to eventually overcome all African resistance and repopulate the vast African jungle terrain with commerce and industry. His teachings reached the easily influenced Zameer and eventually infected his soul with the same hatred of the United States and their Zionistic populous, so much so that after 9 years of heavy training Lt. Fredric W. Mandela and his warrior molded Azala Zameer finally made their first active move towards the Americans. ~The complete and total domination and eventual destruction of a fighting federation known as Five Star Wrestling.~ Their arrival in the United States now complete, the fear of the African Nightmare will soon be present in all of the men they encounter. “Get out of the way, or be demolished!” Wrestling Style Power wrestler Finisher Move Ivory Stampede Finisher Description Double handed choke slam, then whilst retaining his grasp on the throat of his opponent, Zameer pushes himself up into a handstand, dropping his knee down onto the chest of his fallen opponent Finisher Setup Move The Spear of Zameer Setup Description Running Spear Special Moves Inverted Bear Hug into Tombstone Piledriver, Jacknife Powerbomb, Iron Claw, The Nightmare (under-arm sleeper hold where Zameer applies sleeper hold and lifts opponent off the ground), Samoan Drop, Body Splash off the second rope (or standing over opponent) Entrance Music Tribal African drums with chanting
