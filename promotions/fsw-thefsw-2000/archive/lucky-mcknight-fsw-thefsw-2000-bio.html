Lucky McKnight Biography Height 6'6" Weight 254 lbs Hometown Dublin, Ireland Date of Birth April 20, 1974 Alignment Popular Face Entrance Music No Cigar - Millencolin Manager/Valet Lucas The Leprachaun Personality His personality, he has many strange quirks. Loves fried food, women, and loves to pull practical jokes. But when the time comes his a business man and a fierce fighter. Also known for his advanced speech patterns and his many catch phrases. Believes strongly in philosphy, and loves to write poetry in his spare time. An intelliletcual at heart and has a strong affirmation of his private beliefs, which he discusses on occasion. Loves his Irish upbringing and hertiage, a hard worker. But always willing to do what it takes to get the job done. During an average match he typically tries to get the crowd into the match and make it move like the waves of the ocean. Goes from punch,kick, etc into a specific move. Utilizes the top rope highflying moves in combination with strong ground moves. Often throws his opponents into the ropes. Goes for high risk and high crowd reaction maneveurs. Known to on occasion get off his opponent during a pin to do more damage, thereby enraging the fans. History Lucky worked as a barhand at a local bar in dublin, worked tables. His pracitcal jokes whoever got him into alot of trouble. One day at age 15 he poured hotsauce into the soup of a man, the guy in turn got up and thrown through a table. Suddenly Lucas, a Leprachaun appeared in front of the 15 year old boy before the man did anymore damage. All of the people surrounded them and then Lucas spit in his face and told him to leave him alone. By chance a wrestler by the name of Slick Mik, a fellow Irishman was in the room at the time and got up and did a couple of wrestling moves on the guy and took the boy and the Leprachaun out of the bar into a nearby countryside. From that day an instant friendship between Lucas and Lucky ensued. Also, after meeting Slick Mik and becoming good friends with him, Lucky became fasinated by wrestling. At age 18 he joined the Slick Mik Wrestling School in a town outside of Dublin. At age 20 Slick took his friends Lucas and Lucky on a tour with a small indy fed to give him a feel for the real deal. Lucky trained hard and got into the indy fed to compete in front of a large amount of people for one of the few times in his life. He saw the enourmous crowd reaction he got from his odd and distinctly irish style of wrestling. After that he trained on his mic skills, opening up the hidden talent and charisma he had with words. His education in the art of langauge and speech helped quite a bit. At age 21 after the tour he went solo and met a few friends that will most likely appear in his FSW carrer eventually. To make a long story short, he injured his shoulder in the last fed he was in and was out of wrestling for almost 3 months. But now is back and ready to bring the fans what they want.
