Futile Resistance, 3 November 2001

3 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Loose Ends] -SMACK- Tommy Taker flew against the wall smashing a small hole into it. He dropped down to his knees, as a foot kicked him in the face sending him to the ground below.

Tommy Taker screamed out in pain, "Dammit, just stop it!" A hand grasped around the neck of Tommy Taker as for the first time it had been noticed that his hands were tied behind his back. The face of Johnny Phantom moved a few inches from Tommy Taker's face.

Tommy yelled out in horror again, "I've taken care of it already!" Johnny Phantom backed up a moment with surprise in his expression. Phantom calmly remarked, "Good then… it's done." Phantom reached across in front of Tommy Taker, pulling over a suitcase, setting it next to Tommy Taker.

"Here's the money. Speak anything to even one soul… they will die as well as you will too."

Johnny Phantom stood to his feet walking to the door opening it quickly to see Victoria Tait waiting. Her eyes widened seeing Tommy Taker lying on the floor bleeding immensely. She lifted her hand to smack Johnny, but he grabbed her by the arm throwing her to the floor next to Tommy Taker. He spat upon both of them as Victoria's eyes filled with tears trying desperately to remove the ropes from the wrists of Tommy Taker. Phantom smirked stepping out of the room.

[Two For One] "Dammit it's mine!" Human raced across the parking lot as Blake moved to the side avoiding him, as Human's head hit off the front hood of a Mercedes. Human cursed into the wind as Blake took Human by the shoulders and smashed his head into the side of a Jeep Wrangler. Blake grabbed his Tag Team title lifting it up as Human turned around. Blake swung quickly, but Human made a quick move out of the way. Human reached for the muffler on the Wrangler ripping it off. Blake turned back around to face Human who smashed the muffler across the face of Blake bumping him unconscious. Human reached for the Tag Title in Blake's limp hand. Human put over his shoulder as he tapped the belt on his waist.

"I told you they were mine Blake. This title no longer belongs to you." Human leaned closer to the unmoving body of Blake. "Dammit, you couldn't just hand it to me. I had to pry it from your brittle hands. You're a foolish man."

Human spat upon the body of Blake, stepping back and walking away. What's with all the spitting? [I Want] A hand pushed the chest of Johnny Phantom, stopping in his stride. Phantom lost his breath for a moment looking up to see none other than Big Daveylicious, past Five Star Wrestling superstar making his presence known.

Johnny Phantom sighed looking up, "NO!" Big D's face reddened with anger, "To hell with you Phantom! You know that?! I worked my ass harder in this company more than anyone else. And this is how I'm treated? I simply want-"

Phantom pushed Big D aside standing in front of him, "Look you jackass. I have enough on my mind worrying about all your little wants and desires. I don't know you, nor will I ever want to know you. Understand that?"

"Yes, but-" Phantom quickly interrupted mid sentence, "See I don't believe you know! Because the damn phone calls to my home at midnight aren't helping the matter! Now get the hell out of my way. If you want your damn hall of fame spot you need to talk with someone who cares!"

Johnny Phantom released his hands from the collar of Big D, walking off. Big D mumbled to himself, "All I want is to be in the hall of fame…" The scene fades. [November 3rd] A dark figure came across the FSW Titan Tron. Soft spoken the voice began. "Tonight, on November 3, 2001, I will make everything that has gone wrong, make right. And everything that has gone right for those who go against me, will go wrong. They will perish, because today… November third. It all begins. My chance is now, but are they ready?"

The face of Stefan Douglas appeared as the lights came up. "I've waited too long, and I am well prepared to make things happen…" Suddenly, 'Ironman' by Black Sabbath began as Johnny Phantom, Sean Cylear, Jonny Trip, and Chris Fear stepped out from the back. The four men walked quickly down the ramp way looking up to the Titan Tron as Stefan Douglas stared grimly into the ring where Johnny Phantom was tossed a microphone.

Phantom smirked, looking around the ring at his compatriots, "You know Stefan for a man who has the odds against him, including the World Champion himself Chris Fear, you seem to think you have control. If you're so big and mighty why don't you show yourself now… and prove to the four of us why you aren't down here in the middle of the ring delivering this speech instead, of backstage hiding out. Come on… you want to prove yourself. Then Prove It!"

Stefan's music blasts over the PA as the fans in attendance jump to their feet with Stefan Douglas walking out. He looks into walking down the ramp way pointing at each individual. Stefan slid into the ring meeting Johnny Phantom face-to-face yelling. Phantom raises his fist and…

Turns around smashing Jonny Trip in the face! Stefan bounces off the ropes drop kicking Chris Fear out of the ring as Johnny Phantom kicks and DDTs Sean Cylear, as he slides out of the ring quickly. The fans go wild as Sean Cylear, Jonny Trip, and Chris Fear meet up at the ramp way. They yell into the ring cursing when without notice.. the fans begin to scream and yell again.

The three men turn around slowly, eyes wide open. Blitz stands behind them, clotheslining all three!

The scene fades as the three men scramble backstage past Blitz. Blitz raises his arm towards the ring as well as Stefan and Phantom do the same…

[Donde Esta Senor PK] Jonny Trip's hand smashed across the face of Sean Cylear as he tried to protect himself. "What the hell do you mean you don't know where Painkiller is?!" "Jonny… I don't know where he is-" Jonny Trip smashed his fist across the face of Sean Cylear again. "Dammit, why the hell not?! That damn turncoat, Johnny Phantom can't make up his damn mind, whether he is with us or against us! We need Painkiller… and we need him now!"

Jonny Trip raises his arm again, but Chris Fear grabbed it holding him back. Fear stepped in front of Sean Cylear, inches away, "Sean… it was your responsibility to watch dear ol' PK. Why didn't you?!"

"I thought… I thought-" Jonny Trip yelled, "That's your problem, you were thinking instead of doing what you were told!"

Jonny Trip raised his fist again, but Chris grabbed him by the arm. Chris whispered something into his ear as Trip began to smile. Trip leaned close to Sean Cylear.

"You're match tonight… is a for your job. If you don't capture at least one of the titles in your match… you're fired!"
Chris pats Trip on the back as the two walk away leaving Sean Cylear left in silence. The scene fades. [Human vs Tommy Kane & Atrius]
[Tag Titles] Human's forearm jolted across the face of Tommy Kane dropping him to one knee. Tommy wiped his lip as Human's knee came up smashing Kane is the face sending him to his back. Human left off the ropes following an elbow drop smashing Tommy Kane's chest in. Tommy rolled away holding his chest yowling in pain as his hand raised up smacking the arm of Atrius.

Atrius approached quickly clotheslining Human to the mat just as quickly as he had stepped in. Atrius followed up with punches to the skull bouncing the head of Human off the canvas.

On the outside, Tommy Kane rolls holding his chest, gulping in fast breaths, trying to regain his composure. A hand reached pushing Tommy Kane's arm aside as Tommy Kane's eyes quickly narrowed to look straight into a man of not so small figure.

Matt Daze aka 'The Fan' Matt Daze stood staring back into the eyes of Tommy Kane who had just felt the swift hand across the chest that had taken so much punishment.

Tommy Kane yelled out, "What the hell…?"

He was cut off when Matt Daze interrupted, "Shut the hell up. Did I say speak? No. Exactly. Understand Mr. Kane I am no normal fan. No, no, no. See, I'm Matt Daze, the one and only true fan of FSW. Why? Because I know what's best for this promotion. And I tell you true. You're not the thing."

Tommy Kane lifted his arm smashing the face Matt Daze with a fist.

The newest employee backed away quickly as ringside crew approached Kane stopping his onslaught of fists.

Meanwhile, in the ring Human gains advantage pinning Atrius for a 3 count. Some how managing a win, Human maintains the tag team titles, which he now holds singularly. Human slided out of the ring, while Tommy Kane curses out 'The Fan' Matt Daze.

It had only taken a few words to piss off Tommy Kane in Matt Daze's column. But now his actions were asking for it. Kane was furious as he was dragged backstage.

[Eve?] The woman simply known in the past as EOE's not only friend, but lover. The two had made a perfect pair. But now it seemed things had changed. But for the better?

Eve's hand caressed the arm of Desmond Ellis as he tried to relax relentlessly.

"Eve, I can't take it any longer!"

She tried to calm Desmond down, reassuring him, but no avail.

"Don't you understand I've… we've been waiting this god damn long and not one worthless piece of shit thing has happened! Dammit, when you last managed EOE it took merely a week to get a match. You begin to manage me and I'm-"

For the first time Eve raised her voice, "Stop it! Don't YOU understand I know that?! I'm trying my best, and I need you to remain patient."

Desmond took a deep breath. Inhaling… exhaling, "Alright. I'm sorry. I guess I'm just-"

Eve's hand crossed over the lips of Desmond, quieting him, "We're business partners. You have a right to be angry. If I were dating you I'd smack you silly", they laughed lightly, "Now try and relax. We have business to do."

Desmond hung his head low, before slowing raising it and moving his hair aside, "Think that jackass Matt Daze is going to get exactly what he wanted?"

Eve smirked, "We'll see…"

Not lovers? Business partners? Where is EOE, other than the unemployment line?

So many questions… [Jack Brooze vs PS Mad vs Sean Cylear]
[Extreme & Rising Star Titles] PS Mad bounced off the ropes clotheslining both Jack Brooze and Sean Cylear to the below mats, smacking their heads. PS Mad ran off them again flying over the top rope smashing his body into theirs.

PS Mad quickly gained the lead as he felt adrenaline filter throughout his whole body picking up Jack Brooze and whipping him into the ring post a foot away, sending Jack Brooze's insane mind bouncing back, and soon clubbed with a forearm sending him to the mat.

Meanwhile, Sean Cylear grabs a chair flexing his arms as PS Mad just realizing his opponent isn't still lying on the mat helpless, but in fact smashing a chair over his forehead. Sean Cylear dropped the chair, covering PS Mad as the ref began his count.

"1…2…"

He was cut off as Jack Brooze tackled Sean Cylear to the ground, tearing his FSW shirt off and ripping at his skin with backhands, sending echoes through the arena.

PS Mad shook his head looking to his right as Jack Brooze, like a madman tore at the skin of Sean Cylear, as he yelped in pain. PS Mad watched in amusement for a moment, before grabbing Jack Brooze from the back and German suplexing him over his head.

PS Mad released his hold smirking at the site. Again though Sean Cylear was up with a chair, but only to be met the head of a recovering Jack Brooze. Sean Cylear turned to PS Mad as he raised the chair again only to be smacked with a dropkick sending the chair back into his face.

PS Mad grabbed Jack Brooze sliding him into the ring. PS slid in close behind setting up Jack Brooze on the top turnbuckle, for what seems to be the Psycho Driver. PS Mad lifts Jack Brooze over his head, power bombing him into the middle of the ring, into a quick cover.

The Ref counts, "1…2…3!"

PS Mad wins the Rising Star and Extreme Titles as it seems Sean Cylear is out of a job…

[Comfort] Amy pushed back her hair walking quickly down the arena hallway. Her face was as bright as the sun as it was obvious that her, as PS Mad's manager, were celebrating. It was not only a win for him, but for her. Their relationship was growing, and she praying each day that her and him could be something more.

Amy's eyes diverted for a minute. She normally didn't give one damn second to another other than Paul Street, but his patheticness was intoxicating. He was nothing like her. Well dressed, while she was wearing her usual gothic look. But she was attracted, not physically though. As almost he was calling desperately to her.

Amy approached the beaten and bruised Tommy Taker, who probably looked more handsome to her at the moment with the small smear of blood on his cheek, than a few weeks earlier.

Amy set her hand upon Tommy Taker's shoulder. He didn't move.

"Vicki, look… I don't know how to explain what happened earlier tonight-", Tommy cut himself off midsentence looking up to not the face of Victoria Tait, his girlfriend. But instead the face of a concerned individual peered down with embarrassment.

"I'm so sorry you confused me for… well I have to-", Amy began.

"No wait!", Tommy Taker called as Amy stopped in her tracks. "You can… stay a while."

Amy exhaled approaching Tommy slowly, "Who gave you that awful thing?", as Amy pointed to the gash on the cheek of Tommy Taker.

"Oh this little thing.. well umm-", Amy raised an eyebrow, knowing well enough it wasn't nothing. Tommy restarted himself, "Phantom took a couple swings."

Amy covered her mouth, "He did that to you?!"

"Aye…"

"You poor thing! ", She hugged him holding him tightly. She lifted her face touching her lips against the gash as Amy slowed kissed it, before she was pushed to the side by…

Victoria Tait.

"What the hell are you doing to him?!"

"I was just comforting him, and-"

"Get the hell out of here bitch!", Victoria yelled as Amy backed up a few feet before quickly leaving.

Victoria turned her face to a pleading Tommy Taker. Victoria picked him up by his arm pushing him away, "Go to your damn match."

Tommy Taker began a word… but stopped. He walked away.

Victoria Tait would have none of this. She was going to hurt Amy… badly.

The scene fades.

[Punisher 2000 vs Tommy Taker vs Jonny Trip]
[Ladder Match]
[IC Title] P2K pulled out the ladder from the under the ring smacking Tommy Taker in the jaw, causing the pain in the side of his face to increase. P2K turned to see Jonny Trip running full throttle, as he leapt into the air bashing P2K with an elbow. P2K dropped to the mat, but slowly pulled out the rest of the ladder.

Jonny Trip picked up the ladder, grappling it from the hands of Punisher 2000. Jonny Trip lifted the ladder smashing it against the leg of Punisher 2000, as a loud snap filled the arena. The camera focused a moment on the leg of P2K as he held it in pain, yelling for medics.

The medics run full throttle to the ring as Tommy Taker slides into the squared circle as Jonny Trip began to set up the ladder in the middle of the ring. Tommy Taker pulled Jonny Trip, punching once then throwing him off the ropes, but Trip reversed with a neckbreaker bouncing the neck of Tommy off the mat.

Tommy felt his neck as Jonny Trip continued to work on the ladder, and to begin his climb. Taker slowly made it to his feet looking at trip. Tommy slid to the outside grabbing a chair and climbing the turnbuckle.

"Hey Trip!", yelled the voice of Taker as Trip reached for the IC Title. Trip turned as Tommy Taker flew off the turnbuckle smacking Trip in the forehead sending him to the mat below, bouncing twice.

The referee sat next to P2K on the outside checking on him constantly. From the arena seats Chris Fear jumped over the barricade and into the ring. Tommy Taker wobbled to his feet only to met with a suplex almost sending him out of the ring!

Chris went to Trip helping him up, and towards the ladder, until Tommy Taker fired back with punches to Chris Fear smacking twice then following up with a flatliner.

Taker looked to the ladder to see Trip slowly climbing his way up. Chris Fear reached grabbing the ankle of Tommy Taker forcing him to collapse to his chest. Taker yelled in pain as Chris Fear wrenched his leg tighter and tighter.

The ref looked into the ring as Chris Fear quickly rolled out. With one movement Trip reached for the IC title pulling it down as the ref called for the bell rewarding the match to Trip.

Taker looked to the ladder and Trip. He wanted payback… if not later.. now. Tommy bounced off the ropes and pushed the ladder as Trip on the top fell off from the top smashing his head into the announcers table, almost collapsing it.

The scene fades.

[Sickly Revenge] Victoria Tait walked down the hallway. Pissed. To say the least.

She was going to get revenge. She wasn't a very physical person. So instead she was going to make Amy as mad as she was… by get PS Mad.

Victoria looked into the room of PS Mad who stumbled around with his equipment, throwing his newly won titles into his bag. Victoria straightened her dress walking in.

Paul Street aka PS Mad gave a double take before smiling gently, "Uh.. hey. What's up?"

Victoria came closer wrapping her arms around PS Mad, "Paul… ever since the first time I met you… I wanted you. I wanted what you have. Everything and anything you want.. I'll give you."

Victoria reached her hand down, caressing PS Mad's leg as his eyes widened, then slowly shifted to face Victoria. He smiled.

-SMASH-

The door flew open as Amy walked in eyes wide open looking at the leg of PS Mad. Victoria released her grip walking past Amy, but not before blowing a kiss to PS Mad. He smiled looking around Amy.

Victoria closed the door behind her. And with that a sound echoed through the room, "FUCK!"

Victoria smiled walking away… it was only the beginning.

[Needs] Johnny Phantom and Stefan Douglas wait by the opening to the ramp way. Waiting for the single to enter. But not before Big Daveylicious could interrupt. His hand went over the shoulder of Phantom, as Phantom spun around quickly.

Phantom sighed, "Didn't I tell you to leave?"

"Yes, but-"

"And you're still here?"

The rage was building, and Big D was going to express it, "Phantom, if you don't give me a fucking chance to prove I'm worth of a hall of fame spot, expect to see your ass molded to the front of your Mustang!"

Phantom's eyes opened and took a moment. He shook his head cleverly, "Fine then.. you can have your shot. You will start proving yourself this Friday, in what I like to call 'Challenge of the Fittest'. Expect the unexpected… Now leave before I change my damn mind."

Big D nodded turning around… he was going to have his chance. But what was Challenge of the Fittest?

[Chris Fear vs Stefan Douglas]
[World Title] Johnny Phantom sat ringside as the bell rang. Chris Fear had screwed up this night already for men like Tommy Taker, but he wouldn't make it horrible for Stefan. Now that Phantom finally had his priorities straight, he was ready and set to take control of a federation that was truly his. With Painkiller out of the way, it would take months for anyone to find him. Tonight was his night.

Stefan grabbed Fear by the neck throwing him into the turnbuckle, and quickly throwing punches rallying up the crowd quickly as he threw Fear from the turnbuckle off the ropes and quickly belly-to-belly suplexed Fear. Smashing the back of his neck off the canvas. Fear jumped to his feet quickly, to only be met with vertical brainbuster.

Chris Fear had to fight back, and quickly. Something strategic needed to take place. He had to finalize his spot at the top by ridding his opposition, and the quickest way to do that was taking him out during a match.

Chris Fear rolled out of the ring as Stefan rolled out soon after. The two exchanged punches for a minute before Stefan gained the upper hand smacking Fear's skull off the turnbuckle poll.

Here was Phantom's chance. Stefan could hold him, and Phantom could take his swing. Johnny sat up from his chair, folding it. He lifted it above his head. Dammit… he was going to get what he wanted. Which was whatever he wanted.

Stefan looked over to Phantom as he slowly approached nodding his head to Stefan. Stefan picked up Fear holding him still behind him. Phantom bent back and came down full force on the top of…

Stefan Douglas' head!

Chris Fear moved out of the way as Stefan got his head smashed in. Fear rolled into the ring pulling the ref over to begin the ten count.

Phantom knelt down trying to help Stefan, but to no avail. Phantom asked for a microphone, "Ladies and gentlemen this match has now become a three way dance for the world title. Now including Blitz!"

Mozart played as finally Blitz ran down to the ring. He slid in grabbing Fear by the neck and smashing him into the turnbuckle. Jonny Trip followed soon behind Blitz sliding into the ring smashing a baseball bat to the side of Blitz.

Phantom slid into the ring grabbing Trip by the head and throwing him over the ropes. Phantom helped up Blitz as Chris Fear stumbled over, looking out to the outside of the ring asking to see if Trip needed help. Fear turned around only to met with…

Heavy Artillery!

Blitz covered his ribs holding them tightly. Blitz moved his arm and covered Chris Fear.

The ref counted, "1…..2….3!"

The ref rang the bell as Phantom handed the title to Blitz with Futile Resistance going over the air.

Had Stefan been screwed? How would Blitz affect the title? What has become of the Fear and Trip alliance?

The scene fades to black.
