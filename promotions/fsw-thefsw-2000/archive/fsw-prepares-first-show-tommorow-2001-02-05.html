FSW Prepares! First show Tommorow!

5 February 2001

What this is A post from the fed's own newsboard, the page where rumours, predictions and fan reports about its shows were posted.
|FSW Prepares! First show Tommorow!|

After a short time period of shut down the FSW is back on thier feet and with a whole new crew of wrestlers and staff members. Painkiller took the FSW down about a month ago becuase he was forced into doing everything and he just couldn't handle it. Now Painkiller has gone out and picked up some new Staff members and is hopeing that this time around the FSW can make its way back to the spot it was before the shut down. One of the Vice Presidents LoneStar was quoted as saying,

"The FSW is going to be back and its going to be a group effort behind the scenes. This time around Painkiller will be making the decisions around here like always but he won't be the only one carrying them all out now that he has a full faith and loyal set of troops behind him."

Tommorow will be the first show back on TV since the shutdown. Some people are saying that the FSW will never be back up at its game-

"The FSW will never be where it was before they shut down. I'll watch the first show but I pray they keep up to what they were doing before this shut down. Painkiller needs o get his stuff in order and I hope this stff can keep it up and I hope these new wrestlers have what the old ones did"

This was quoted from a fan before one of the few house shows before the big rebirth. Painkiller has been approached by many people about the titles being decided but everytime he has always said no comment. Some fans are waiting to see how he is going to do this and start the FSW off with a bang!

-Tim "The End" E.
