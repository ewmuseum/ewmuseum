Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens Biography Real Name Micael Stevens (Suprise suprise suprise) Nicknames The Evolution of Pro-Wrestling, Spitfire, Hometown Greenwhich Conneticutt Birthday July, 6 1980 Height 6'2 Weight 245 Biography I remember the day I realized I was different from everyone else. It was in science class, I was just twelve years old. Mrs. Owens the teacher was talking about Darwin, and the theory of evolution. She was talking about how the strong were the only ones that survived the process, she talked about how the cream always rose to the top, I saw right then and there, she was talking about me. But, I should back up, explain myself a little more before I get too far ahead of myself. I was the the bully, but I didn't just use my fists I used my mind too, I knew my family was richer than all the other kids' familes, and I knew that make me better. And I knew that they should know I was better too, so I proved it to them, anyway I had to, I always found out the dirty little secrets, I always sent kids crying home to mommy over black eyes and bruised feelings. The other kids all hated me, but I was smart, the teachers loved, I never let see what I was doing to everyone else. I ruled through fear, I ruled with my head, that was my weapon, that was my power. Kids feared me because I was different, better in someway, but I never realized it, not then. And now here was this teacher, actually telling me something I didn't already know, that was suprising in and of itself. I knew she was talking right to me, I knew she was telling me my destiny. My destiny was to make sure the world evolved to my level, I was fittest, and I would survive anything, I was the first of a newer better race of men, supermen if you will. Yeah, I'd have to say that class effected me a lot, prolly moreso than anything else those bumbling and stupid teachers ever 'taught' me. I started to do things a little differently after that, the beatings stopped, they were a little too obviously, it was time to hone my mind. It was time to make sure I was the smartest. But still, I wasn't happy, I was still making the other kids miserable, I was still the leader, but there was just something missing. I found that something I was missing four years later, I was sixteen. Wrestling, there it was, the missing piece of the puzzle, that would make me, whole. My high school had a great team, I was of course the star all four years, I loved it, made the All State team all four years, and All American twice. It was great, physical and psychological, I could prove my dominance through skill and intellect. I'd always watched wrestling as a kid, it just appealed to me, all the larger than life characters, my young mind ate them all up. So I shouldn't really have been surprised how much I loved the High School team, but it wasn't real wrestling, it wasn't what I knew I had to do with my life. When I got outta High School, I had a choice to face. College, nah I couldn't do that more school, more work way too boring. Go to work for Daddy at Stevens Inc., hell I hated the old man, and real work was even worse than school. No, there was only one answer, I was gonna be a pro wrestler, I was going to be on TV humiliating people, these guys were supermen, and my place was with them. So I did the only thing I could, I started going to a wrestling school, it was great, I was the fastest learner, I was the star, I was the best, of course. I graduated fromt the school in less than three monthes, faster than anyone, of course what can you expect from 'The Evolution of Pro-Wrestling' as I'd started calling myself. So I started wrestling in the indies, I'd cruise down the highway, with Alkaline Trio, Bad Religion, Less Than Jake, or someother punk or ska band pumping outta the sound system heading for my next gig. Everyone saw how good I was, the promoters couldn't deny it, I was the star of the independant wrestling circuit, I'm sure you've read about me in the Torch, or the Observer. It didn't take me long to catch work in some of the bigger Indies, Eastern Wrestling Association, yeah I was three time Unified World Champion. I was their star, there savior, I coulda helped them go national, but the dumbass who was running the place went bankrupt. So I was in the Indies again, and again that didn't last long, American Wrestling Alliance was calling me this time, I was gonna wrestle with the greatest, I put them all to shame. I hurt my arm though, pretty bad, they fired me over the phone, while I still held their US title, bitches. Next up for your old pal and hero, Global Championship Wrestling, one of the other big boys. US champ again, and a tag champ to boot, I was livin the high life, until another bankrupcy, why are all promoters financial losers? I was feeling kinda down and out, I'll tell you that, after only four short years, I was feeling like hanging up my boots for good, I could live off Dad's money, who needs to work? I spent my off time, going to shows, rocking out, being a bum, then I met her, Sheena Ramone, and who'da thunk it? She was a fan. She loved everything about me, and I have to say her body had me more than willing to keep her talking. She understood everything, except me quitting. But it didn't matter to me, I was finished witht he morons in the wreslting business, most of them knuckle dragging buffoons anyway. I was done, and nothing could change my mind, or so I thought anyway. My twenty-third birthday, I got a gift that would bring me back to wrestling. It was a first edition copy of 'On The Origin of Species' by Dr. Charles Darwin. Sheena picked it up for me, she knew I was interested in this stuff, but she didn't know how much it would effect me, she couldn't have known. But there it was staring me in the face, my destiny. I couldn't just let it go, I couldn't just quit cause everyone else is dumb and slow, of course everyone is dumb and slow, I'm supposed to be the one to change that. So I told Sheena I was going back to the ring, this time she'd be at my side too, I made sure of that. She's the closest thing to an equal I have in this world, she understands the future, she understands I am the future. And so now, I leave you with a final question, are you ready to evolve?
