[Broken Trust]

As Paranoia opens we see Stephan Douglas arrving to the arena. He walks through the doors with his bag over his shoulder. He has a shocked look on his face. Just like the one he had when Chris Fear turned his back on Douglas last week on Paranoia. Mark Jones finally catches up with him.

"Hey Stephan, do you have anything to say about what happened last Friday at the end of Paranoia?", asks Jones.

Stephan stops and turns to look at Mark. "Do I have anything to say? Yeah I have a bunch to say. I have a lot of questions that need to be answered. But that will only come with time. I just though Fear and I and put our past behind us and work togethor. But obviously I was mistaken. I heard that I have another match against him tonight. Is that right?", says Douglas.

"Yes you do. It's a tag match with you and Blitz against Fear and Trip.", responds Jones.

"Sounds good. Maybe after tonight a few of those questions will be answered. And if beating them out of Fear is what it's going to take to get them. Then so be it.", says Douglas as he walks off.

Jones just stands there and looks at Douglas as he walks down the hall.

[P.S. Mad vs. Jack Brooze(c)] [Extreme Title Match]

"Hello everyone and welcome to another edition of Paranoia. I am your host, Asytn Ripp, and sitting beside me is my colleague, Craig Neil. Say hi to the fans Craig."

"I told you last week not to speak to me didn't I?", yells Craig.

"Well umm....I can't remember.", replies Ripp.

"Well I did. So just do your commentary on the match.", says Neil as he slumps in his chair.

"I'm not even going to ask. Fans our first match of the evening will be P.S. Mad against Jack Brooze for the Extreme title. This should be a hell of a match."

P.S. Mad makes his way to the ring and he has Amy by his side. She stays on the outside as P.S. Mad enters the ring.

Mad reaches to the outside for a microphone and one of the FSW crew hands one to him. Mad takes the microphone and positions himself in the middle of the ring with Amy standing next to him.
"Well, well, well. What do we have here?" Some of the fans sing along to the phrase as they remember is from Mad's earlier stint in the FSW.
"The Mad one is back and this time he's bought the only bitch mad enough herself to join me here in the FSW and to help me create as much havoc and destruction as before."
The fans cheer, some of them remembering his hardcore antics of the past. "But it seems that not everybody is happy to see me back here. I know Jack Brooze isn't exactly ecstatic to see me now even though he's never seen me before. If he's just a little nervous I can understand. It's not every day you have to face a total mad man in an extreme match, not to mention facing a mad man who's a former 2 time Extreme Champion. And you know what Jack? I'm looking to make this my third Extreme Title reign and their ain't a damn thing you can do about it. But before You get destroyed tonight I have one other person to address. Atrius."
The fans boo at the mention of his name. "Yeah Atrius. Now I don't know what possessed you to attack me last week, but if you get in my way again there will be a slightly different outcome. This time I'm ready for you and It's going to be YOUR beaten up carcass laying in the ring. Just watch your back Atrius, because you never know who could be lurking in the shadows."
Mad throws his microphone to the outside and starts warming up for his match.

Jack Brooze then walks down the ramp with the Extreme title over his head. He rolls into the ring and as Mad turns around for the start of the match, Brooze decks him with the Extreme title. The bell then rings as the match starts. Brooze takes Mad and rolls him out of the ring. Brooze then follows and exits the ring. Brooze gets a chair and whacks P.S. Mad in the back and Mad fals to the ground.

"What a cheap shot eh Neil." says Ripp.

"Shut up, it was clean and legal." yells Neil.

Mad gets up and spears Brooze down. Brooze drops the chair on the concrete floor. Amy picks it up and hands it over to Mad. Mad belts Brooze with the chair across the knees causing him to scream out in agony. Mad drops the chair and picks Brooze up off of the ground. He throws him into the side of the ring and charges in. Brooze moves out of the way. Mad crashes into the apron. He backs out and is belted with a bulldog.

"What a great move by Brooze." says Neil.

"Cheap though." responds Ripp.

"Shut up Ripp." bellows Neil.

Brooze slides in the ring. Amy helps Mad up. Mad grabs the chair and slides into the ring. Brooze runs past Mad and ducks under the bottom rope. Mad slides to the outside and belts Brooze across the face with the chair knocking him down to the ground. Brooze stumbles up and ducks a second chair shot. Mad lifts the chair over his head but is kicked in the ribs causing him to drop the chair. Brooze DDTs Mad down onto the chair and raises his arms.

"He is skilled." says Neil.

"It takes no skill to be an Extreme wrestler." replies Ripp.

"Blow me." says Neil.

"Ah., no." responds Ripp.

Brooze slides in the ring and waits for Mad. Amy runs over and helps Mad get up. Mad picks up the chair and runs into the ring. Brooze punches Mad but to no avail. Mad belts Brooze over the head with the chair. Mad picks Brooze up off the canvas. He suplexes Brooze right onto the chair. Brooze sits up holding his back in pain. Brooze gets up and is met with a bodyslam from Mad. Mad raises his arms and stomps Brooze. Mad picks Brooze up and tosses him over the top rope.

"This is entertaining stuff." says Ripp.

"No shit." replies Neil.

Brooze gets up and shakes the cobwebs out. Brooze runs into the ring and gets onto his feet. He and Mad begin to exchange blows. Mad gets the upper hand and nails Brooze hard in the face. Brooze spins around and is hit with a piledriver. Mad stomps Brooze and picks him up. Brooze is then hit again with a piledriver. Mad goes for the pin. 1...2...3 and PS Mad wins the FSW Extreme championship!

"New champion." says Neil.

"He just wanted it more." says Ripp.

"Shut up.", says Neil getting tired of Ripp's voice.

[Rejection]

The intercom flashed on and the voice of a young female secretary replaced the silence in Painkiller's office.
"Sir, Tommy Taker is here to see you" Painkiller lifted his head from the pile of paperwork in front of him and groaned before finally speaking.
"You better send him in then" replied Painkiller as he unpressed the intercom and picked up his pen not even looking at the man known as Tommy Taker who had just entered the room. Tommy just stood there looking at Painkiller who seemed to be hard at work.
"Hey PK, how you been mate?" "I've been fine Tommy, now what did you want?" said Painkiller in a rather monotonous tone.
"I just wanted to say how good it is to see you back at the helm of this fine organization, Johnny Phantom didn't know how to run the business. Now when you were in charge those were the glory days, I guess leadership is something you are born with ay"
"Maybe" "Well I was wondering if maybe I could become part of your little group?" said Tommy forgetting about the idle chit chat and had now decided to get straight to the point.
"Why do you want to be a part of my group Tommy?" "It would be just like the good old Coalition days ya know mate....." Tommy was suddenly interrupted by Painkiller who had slammed his pen down onto the desk with such force that the desk jumped a little.
"That's where you are wrong MATE, the Coalition is dead and buried and has been for over 4 months now. That's history now, a chapter in my life that has been completed. I have plans for the present and quite frankly I don't think that you fit into them you Australian jackass."
Tommy stood in front of Painkiller awe struck, there was no reply or comment to make, he was speechless as Painkiller continued his verbal onslaught.
"You were never ever any good Tommy, I can't even remember why I drafted you into the Coalition. It's obvious to everyone that you had no talent or ability inside of the ring, and I'm going to make sure that I won't be making the same mistake I did 4 months ago when I met you. Now please remove yourself from my office before I call security"
A look of anger spread across Tommy's face as he simply turned on his heels and casually walked out of the office closing the door behind him gently.
Painkiller breathed deeply and picked up his pen as he continued his work like nothing had happened

[Human vs. Carl Cash] [Standard Match]

"This should be a very good match. Wouldn't you say Neil?", asks Ripp.

Neil is just silent as Ripp continues. "Right. Human will be facing Carl Cash. My partner just told me telepathically that he thinks this match will be very close."

"They hell I did.", bellows Craig.

"Anyways. Let's get this match started.", says Ripp.

Carl Cash's music blares across the arena as he makes his way to the ring.

"Cash is looking great tonight. Wouldn't you say Craig?", says Ripp.

"No. I don't judge the looks of men.", replies Neil sternly.

Human then makes his way down to the ring. As he enters the ring the ref calls for the bell. DING...DING..DING...And the match is underway. Human and Cash lock up in the ring. Human gets a few shots in on Cash's head and then Human sends Cash into the ropes. Cash bounces off the ropes and Human goes for a back elbow but Cash ducks it. Human turns around and Cash short arm clotheslines Human to the mat. Cash picks Human up and sends him into the corner. Cash runs in and hits a back elobw and Human falls and is now sitting on the mat with his back in the corner. Cash then begins to kick Cash in the chest. Cash turns around and begins to show boat. Human slowly pulls himself to his feet. As Cash turns around Human grabs him and turns around and throws him into the corner. Human starts pounding on Cash's face. Human takes Cash and goes to Irish whip into the corner but Cash reverses the Irish whip into one of his own and Human is sent into the corner. Cash runs after him and as Human gets to the corner he jumps up on the turnbuckle and does a back flip. Cash keeps going and hits the turnbuckle as Human lands behind him. Cash stumbles backwards and Human rolls him up for a pin.

"This could be it!", exclaims Ripp. "One..two...thre...no a kickout by Cash."

"That wasn't even close. Human is going to have to do more then a little roll up.", says Neil.

Human gets up and he picks Cash up. Human sends Cash into the ropes and Human jumps into the air and goes for a standing drop kick but Cash held onto the ropes and Human lands on the mat hitting the back of his head. Cash walks over to Human and drags him close to a turnbuckle. He lifts his legs into the air and he catapults Human face first into the corner. Human stumbles out of the corner as Cash gets to his feet. Cash takes Human and sends him through the middle ropes and to the floor on the outside. Human walks goes through the ropes and stands on the apron. As Human is slowly getting up Cash turns around to face the ring. He then does a back flip and connects with Humans body sending him to the concrete floor. Cash slowly gets to his feet as Human is still on the floor.

"Did you just see that? It was amazing.", exclaims Ripp.

"Yeah. I am watching the same monitor as you are dumbass. It was pretty cool. Not anything to get excited about.", replies Neil.

Human gets up and he takes Human and sends him into the guard rail. Cash walks over and takes the steel steps and lifts them over his head. He walks towards Human and about to throw them. But Human gains his compsure and does a standing drop kick that hits Cash in the chest. Cash falls back and the steel steps fall on him.

"Oh man. Cash could be seriously hurt now.", says Ripp worrying about Cash.

"Damn that was cool. I think we need to see a replay of that.", responds Neil.

"What? Man your sick.", replies Ripp.

Human takes the steel steps off of Cash. Human then takes Cash and rolls him into the ring. Human then makes his way into the ring. Human picks Blake up and sends him into the ropes. As Cash bounces off the ropes, Human takes him down and Human begins gving Cash rights and lefts. Human picks Cash up again and leaves him standing there. Human runs and bounces off the sides ropes and he runs towards Cash and hits a spinning neck breaker. Human then picks Cash up once again. This time he gets Cash and its a reverse DDT. Human rolls over and covers Cash.

"This could be it. One....two.....three!", yells Ripp as Human wins the match.

"Decent match. Just decent.", says Ripp.

As Human leaves the ring four midgets run past him and enter the ring. They all help Cash to his feet. They help him out of the ring and take him to the back.

"What was that all about?", asks Ripp.

Painkiller, Johnny Phantom, Sean Cylear, Jonny Trip and of course the FSW World Champion Chris Fear. The five men looked at each other smirking as the crowd's chants of hatred filled the arena. The five men simply slid into the ring as Painkiller was tossed the mic.
"I'm estatic to see that you are all happy to see us here!" The fans mumble and boo chants of asshole. Painkiller tosses the mic to Johnny Phantom. "Ladies and gentlmen, I cannot hide it any longer. I am aligned with these four other men in the ring. It took one night to take this federation by storm, one night to completely change the history of a great place like this wrestling company."
"I bought this federation right out from Brandon Ellis' nose, but not without a little help from my friend here Painkiller. Who together we collected our savings and stole this federation. Of course, only my name was on the contract, fooling everyone into believing their past owner was corrupt and I would take charge and be the unforgetable savior."

"Thank you my fellow friends. And since this is my first night on the job, I figured why not making it something special. Adding Sean Cylear as the special referee for tonight's main event! And my second act of duty is that the match with Cylear and Punisher 2000 is now a non dq match. Thank you everyone, I know you are astonded by our great accomplishments and your support is thanked."
The fans boo as 'Ironman' hits the PA again as the five men exit the ring, not with just smirks, but evil diabolical laughs of control. The scene fades. Atrius follows up and he walks down the aisle and enters the ring. Both men stare eachother down as the ref calls for the bell.

[Punisher 2000 vs. Sean Cylear] [No DQ]

The announcer is already standing in the middle of the ring after Paranoia returns from a commercial break and the fans settle themselves for the next bout which is also the semi main-event between Punisher 2000 and a member of the newly formed alliance, Sean Cylear.

"Well fans. This next bout is between Punisher 2000 and Sean Cylear in a non title match.", states Ripp.

"Yeah and the new commisioner, Jonny Trip stated that this would be a no DQ match. So that should make things interesting.", replies Neil.

"Whoah. That's the first intelligent thing you said all night.", says Ripp.

"What? Man shut up talking to me for the love of god.", says Neil.

"Heh. Seems I touched a nerve with that one.", says Ripp aging Neil on.

"Yeah. You're going to be able to touch your ass with your tounge if you don't shut up talking to me.", replies Neil getting pissed at Ripp.
The fans in attendance go quiet for a moment as the announcer reads out each competitors vitals before the theme music of Punisher 2000 roars out from the speakers situated around the arena. The man emerges from behind the curtains and the crowd go crazy for the obvious god guy. Punisher gives them a friendly sort of salute before heading down to the ringside area and jumping up onto the apron. He steps through the middle rope and rests on the far turnbuckle as he awaits the entry of his opponent.
Sean Cylear’s music is the next to play and almost immediately the crowd go crazy in a bad way and practically blow the roof of the arena with booing and unflattering chants. The crowd dies down and begin looking around for the man who should have appeared just moments ago. Punisher is just as confused but is suddenly knocked down from behind with a powerful chair shot to the back by none other than Sean Cylear who has taken the early advantage with a smart little tactic indeed.

"Oh Cylear is taking advantage of this match early.", yells out Ripp.

"Yeah you usually have an advantage when you whack somebody with a chair. Dumbass!", says Neil.
Cylear rests the steel chair in the corner and begins to pummel the lower abdomen of Punisher with a flurry of powerful kicks followed up with a standing leg drop that leaves Punisher clutching his neck in pain. Sean quickly gets back to his feet and brings Punisher with via the scruff of his neck. He taunts the crowd and knocks him back down with a lightning fast clothesline. Sean looks around and once again arrogantly taunts the crowd, but Punisher seizes his first real chance of the match and brings Cylear down with a wristlock which he turns into a submission move. The move doesn’t last for very long as Cylear reaches the ropes easily and Punisher is forced to release the hold. He stands up remembering that the match is no disqualification and makes his way to the outside of the ring and lifts up the ring skirts searching for any object that could inflict pain on his opponent. Cylear sneaks around from the other side of the ring and is about to club down the Intercontinental champ with a double fist shot before Punisher swings around and whacks Cylear over the head with a fire extinguisher. Punisher pulls Cylear to his feet and throws him over the guardrail into the fans who try and grab their favourite superstars but are cleared away by the security guards.

"What else can happen in the match?", asks Ripp.

"Ask someone that cares.", snaps Neil.

Punisher 2000 takes Cylear and pulls him back over the rail and onto the floor. He picks Cylear up and sends him into the ring. Punisher 2000 walks aorund the outside and he finally picks up a chair. He takes the chair and rolls into the ring with it as Cylear is getting up. Punisher 2000 raises the chair above his head and is about to hit Cylear but Cylear jumps in the air and kicks the chair sending it back into Punisher 2000's face. Punisher 2000 falls to the ground and Cylear covers him.

"Here it is. One....Two......Three!....We have a winner.", yells Ripp.

"Yeah Cylear won. Like anybody besides you cares.", replies Neil.

Neil and Ripp continue to argue.
