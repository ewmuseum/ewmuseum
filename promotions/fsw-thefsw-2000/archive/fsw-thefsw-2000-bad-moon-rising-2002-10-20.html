The FSW logo flashes briefly up across the screen. Then, the image shifts, to a ring, cast in shadows. The only light comes from somewhere above and to the right, a thin sliver of light reflecting on the canvas. The camera follows that shaft of light to the moon, and zooms in. Voice-over: Much like the spotlight in Five Star Wrestling, the moon shines down on the just... [picture of Paul Orion superimposed over the lunar surface] and the unjust alike. [picture of Orion morphs into one of Boda] Voice-over: The spotlight shines on unstoppable creatures... [Picture of Meanstreak appears on the lunar surface] and the most dastardly of individuals. [Morphs into image of Tyler Nelson, before quickly shifting to that of Marvelous and Nelson.] [Camera quickly zooms back down, into the squared circle.] The light glints off something in the middle of the ring. Something gold. The new Five Star Wrestling World Heavyweight Championship belt. Voice-over: Tonight, scores will be settled. Careers will be made. And four men will meet for the right to become the new FSW World Champion. It all happens here... at Bad Moon Rising. Quick cut to the outdoor Salem Amphitheater. A stage has been set up, with a ramp leading to the ring. The ring lights are on a rigging of steel and cables above the ring. Fireworks shoot off the top of that structure and from behind the stage, as the cameras pan a jam-packed crowd. Lynn: THE TIME HAS DRAWN NIGH! FIVE STAR WRESTLING IS HERE, AND READY FOR BAD MOON RISING! Cut to ringside, where our usual suspects are sitting at their announce table. Lynn: Hello everybody, and welcome to the show. We’ve got a tremendous night of action on tap. Sam: That’s right, tonight’s the night we crown our new world champion! And I’ll be happy so long as it’s anyone but Meanstreak! Lynn: Plus we’ll have two other title matches tonight, as Angelo Deville defends the Primetime strap against Ming AND Azala Zameer, plus Bad Frotitude defend the tag straps against Dead Presidents in a Chain Match! Sam: Don’t forget my favorite match, Lynn! Boda against Cassius Deamont in LAST MAN STANDING! Lynn: You seem mighty quiet, Caldera... Steven: Well let me just say this, gentlemen. Never anger a sleeping giant. Lynn: O...K. With that, let’s go backstage for a interview with J-Mac! Hatred... The sensual curves of reporter Robin Nicely fill the screen, as the cameraman following her down the hall seems to be occupying his time by focusing directly on her backside. The staccato clicking of her high heels on the tile floor stop suddenly, causing poor Mr. Cameraman to look up to see what the situation is. Robin: There they are.. How's my hair? Robin adjusts a few wayward locks before straightening out her suit jacket. She motions for the cameraman to follow her around a corner. As they proceed together another noise begins to rise over the "clack-clack" of Robin's heels. The sounds are of impact, a resounding "thump" that seems to echo through the spartan hallways that fill the lower levels of the arena. At the end of the hall in what seems to a be a meeting room of some type stand four men. Jason McAllister, John Jupiter, Kyle "Velocity" Lamen, and the fourth man in khaki cargo pants and a black hooded sweatshirt. As soon as the group notices the reporter and cameraman, the fourth man pulls his hood over his head, hiding the majority of his face. In the brief seconds before, his blonde hair, and deep scars across his forehead were visable. Now, only the gristled stubble on his chin is in view. The same can be said for McAllister, whom is lashing out at a pair of Fairtex training pads worn by KVL. McAllister's grey "Property of Iowa Wrestling" is all but completely soaked with sweat. In the background John Jupiter seems to be barking orders. John Jupiter: Remember what he did. Remember what he took from you, from us. McAllister responds to the comment by increasing the intensity and fervor of his strikes. Robin: Excuse me, excuse me gentlemen.. If I could I'd like to get a word with Jason McAllister about his upcoming match. Jason finally relents in his assault on the training pads worn by Velocity. He tosses back the hood of his sweatshirt; leveling his steady, piercing gaze at Robin. Jason: Well, we ain't got all night. Fire away. Robin: Thank you. What are your feelings about this match that was demanded by Tyler Nelson after your actions on last week's Glory. Jason: I'd have ta say it just figures. That's like Nelson. Pushin' to get his way, but too yella to do the work himself. Robin: Tyler has been quoted as saying that you're jealous of him, and that is the reason you seem hellbent on following him. Jason: I'm not followin' him, I'm huntin' him. But a man like Tyler is a hard game to corner. Most o' the time he seems to slip out of things, mostly due to the fact that he doesn't have a spine. Robin: So it's definately not jealousy then. Jason: I want you to understand this perfectly clear. I HATE Tyler Nelson. Not "disagree", not "not get along". I HATE the man and everything he represents. He and his kind, they're part and parcel to a system that trades in people's lives and livings for power and wealth. There's no in between or grey area when it comes to something like that. You're either one of them or one of us. I've seen Tyler walk on the backs of good people, hard working people to get where he is. Ask the guys from NTICW how it felt when he burnt their titles just for his own personal kicks. Ask all the people from PCW that he either held down or fired when they became a threat to him. No one in this industry has done more to put people on unemployment. I'm not gonna stand for it. I'll keep beating down these guard dogs he keeps sending after me until he doesn't have anymore left to send. That's the day when the fun begins. That's the day a white collar piece of shit finds out that in between those ropes, it's a blue collar world. Robin: One man can't be held responsible for all that. Jason: The hell he can't. He did it, and as soon as I can get my hands on that parasitic bastard he'll pay for it. I hope he watches real close tonight, watches how I put down his lil Doberman named OG. Robin: But Mr. Grant is a formidable competitor, could you be taking him too lightly? J-Mac stares dead into the eyes of Robin Nicely. She instantly averts her eyes, looking to the floor for a moment. When her gaze returns to Jason, his eyes are still on her. Jason: Does it look like I'm dickin' around here ma'am? Robin: No. But.. Jason: But nothin'. Tonight is another roadblock I've got to run, an' I've run plenty of of 'em in my time. I ain't about to stop tonight. Tonight OG and Tyler and all of FSW learns why no one is safe. NO ONE!! In the background, the man in the black sweatshirt flashes a twisted grin. JJ: I don't know Miguel, do you think he's pissed enough? Miguel: It's not a matter of rage. Let's hope he has a semblance of restraint. I haven't seen him like this since that think with Arovnik. Stevens groans in agony at the hold, trying to reach toward the ropes in a rather vain attempt, before going back to writhing in pain. Blake works the chinlock, which makes the crowd get restless. Sam: This is boring...I thought the first match was supposed to pump up the crowd? Lynn: Hey, it's going to get better, it always does.. Steven: [silent] Stevens elbows Blake in the gut a few times to break the hold, only to slip around to the back of him and hit a chinlock of his own! Lynn: What the hell does he think he’s doing? Sam: He’s putting an end to this bore-a-thon of a match! Angelo and Ming pause momentarily to stare into the challenging eyes of Tyler Nelson, staring down at them with disapproval as if from heaven, then speed up their attack. Lynn: It's Nelson! FSW's only Six Star Wrestler! Steven: Yeah... But what does he want? Nelson begins swaggering down to the ring, keeping his eyes locked on the two assailants and the World Champion. Marvelous remains unmoving on the mat outside the ring, completely oblivious to everything that's transpired in the last few minutes. When Nelson is halfway to the ring Timo suddenly leaps to his feet, divine and righteous vengeance thick and heavy in his eyes. Lynn: Timo may not even need Nelson's help! Look at the champ go! Ming and Deville stand there stunned, as if unable to attack, as Timo drops the belt and rocks them both with huge shots in succession. Ming suddenly drops from one particularly devastating punch, and the next drops Deville just as Nelson hits the ring. Timo turns around as if sensing the man's presence and offers a half-smile from behind glossy eyes. Nelson returns the smile and inclines his head towards Deville and Ming, reminding Timo that there is still danger afoot. Lynn: Yes! Give those bastards what they deserve! Timo Bolamba and Tyler Nelson! Sam: This isn't right... Timo turns around to continue his just pounding and Nelson follows. Nelson abruptly pulls up short, stopping just behind Timo. His reassuring smile suddenly transforms to one of grand opportunism, and in less than a wink he has scooped Timo up into a fireman's carry. Lynn: NO! Why Nelson? Sam: whoo~ Steven: C'mon, Lynn... You act like Nelson is some kind of boyscout. How damned naive are you, anyhow? Nelson carries the Champion around on his shoulders for several seconds, absorbing and seeming to feed off the suddenly furious hatred of the audience. Ming and Deville climb to their feet and exchange a pleased nod, watching Nelson saunter around with extreme glee. Lynn: Don't tell me... Steven: Third member...? Nelson kicks out his legs and executes a death valley driver, but Timo flips his way out of it midway, landing on his feet as Tyler falls to the ground. Deville and Ming immediately launch themselves at the World Champ, and he meets them head-on, swinging with the fury only the Samoan Silencer can muster. Lynn: Timo just escaped the Six Star Experience! Sam: Yeah, but he won't escape this... Timo's effort against the two men is indeed valiant, managing to match the pair in shots thrown, but the numbers get the better of him as Nelson rejoins the fray. Marvelous slowly climbs to his feet on the outside and stares wide-eyed at the carnage in the ring before turning his back and slowly making his way up the ramp. Sam: Ha! Marvelous is abandoning Timo to the wolves! Lynn: If Marvelous leaves now he's lost my respect for good! Marvelous continues up the ramp as Nelson, Deville, and Ming pound Timo to the canvas and begin to viciously and unceremoniously stomp his fight away. Deville picks up the World Title and looks at it for a moment before turning it on its owner, savagely smashing the World Champion in the face. Lynn: This is outrageous! Damn you Deville! Damn you Ming! Damn you Nelson! And damn you Mike! We're about to get cut off here, folks, and thank god for that! This is atrocious! Sam: This is wrestling, Lynn! Entertainment! Lynn: This was supposed to be a mark-out moment with our first champion being crowned! Sam: Yeah well... The best laid plans... The unruly beating continues along with the hissing and booing of the crowd. The FSW logo appears in the bottom right hand corner of the screen as the show prepares to go off the air. Lynn: Thanks for tuning in everyone! Sam: WAIT! An multitude of cheers drowns out Sam's protests as Marvelous suddenly turns on his heel and stampedes down to the ring, head lowered for the charge. He slides into the ring behind the three men and leaps onto them, arms and legs flailing like limbs on a windmill. The four men go crashing to the ground on top of Timo, who is still valiantly trying to fight back. Lynn: Thank you, Mike! Thank you! Marvelous and Bolamba struggle against the odds of Deville, Ming, and Nelson, the five men rolling and writhing on the ground like a ball of venomous vipers, each striking with deadly intent. The crowd is on their feet in a frenzied fury, knees weak with anticipation as the two forces struggle for dominance. Lynn: Wait! Wait! Hold on! Damnit! The screen goes dead.
