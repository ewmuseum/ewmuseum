Below is a list of frequently asked questions concerning Five Star Wrestling. We advise you also check our wrestling terms and HTML Help links for additional information. These questions are in no particular order. Q: FSW seems different, are the same guys running it? A: No, FSW is under new direction, however we try and stay true to the original FSW through the NPC's and having the old members of the Hall of Fame still there, and with other ways as well. Though we still want to have a flavor of the old FSW in the new, this is still a new promotion and will be run somewhat differently. Q: Is FSW Rp or Angle based? A: FSW is an angle based fed. Your ideas for angles will determine the pushes you receive. Q: Those images of the wrestlers are cool. How do you make them? A: We use a program called Poser 4. We also use Photoshop, Paintbrush, Bryce, Studio 3D Max, Rhino 3D and programs. Q: Can I order an image of my wrestler? A: If you are a member of the FSW, OSW, or PCW family, sure. If not, then no. Well if you offer money maybe. Q: Will you make Poser images for members of my federation? A: If you pay our artists, we sure can, but they don't work cheap. Q: I applied to FSW, but received an acceptance letter to another fed. Does this mean I was not accepted to FSW? A: Yes and no. We have working relationships with other feds. Good work in one of these other feds could certainly lead to a roster spot in FSW. If we refer you to another fed, for example Primetime Wrestling Academy, we also run these organizations, so don't fret. Q: Do in card interviews count towards my RP total? A: Yes. They will especially help you if a match is close or for pushing an angle. Q: Do you write or sim your matches? A: We completely hand write every card. Q: How many wrestlers may I control at once? A: A maximum of two, and they should be a tag team. Each member of the team may have a singles career, but the duo should be a tag team first and foremost. Should the team split up, one member of the team should be dropped from the roster. Q: May I use other wrestlers or their property in my roleplays? A: No, unless you have permission from the wrestler or the FSW President. Q: May I use OSW Front office personalities in my roleplays? A: No, unless you have permission. You MAY use referees or OSW reporters in your roleplays. Injuring OSW personnel in roleplays may (and will) have serious reprisals. Q: I want to join the FSW Staff. What do I need to do? A: Generally, we only promote staff from within the ranks of our loyal player base. If you have not been involved in FSW long, it is unlikely you will be considered for a staff position. Q: I am in another fed. May I post an announcement on FSW's boards promoting the other fed or looking for members? A: You may not. If you are are a participant in FSW, you will be fired for such an offense, and yes, we have ways of knowing who you are, even if you post under another name. If you come from another federation and spam our boards, expect to be blacklisted. Q: I lost my match. Who can I complain to? A: You can complain to the mirror or talk to the hand. Take your pick. Q: I run a federation and want to affiliate my fed with FSW. How do I go about doing this? A: Drop us an email at and we will be happy to discuss the details. Q: I used to be in FSW, now I want to return. Am I automatically accepted? A: No, you are not. You must send in another application and we will drop you an email. Q: What is Primetime Central/Wrestling Tattler? A: Primetime Central is a collaboration of federations who work together for promotional purposes and fun.
