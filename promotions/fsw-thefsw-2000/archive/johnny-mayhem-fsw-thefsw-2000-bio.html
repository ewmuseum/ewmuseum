Johnny Mayhem Biography Height 6'6" Weight 270 lbs Hometown Greenville, South Carolina Date of Birth January 21, 1977 Alignment Unpopular Heel Entrance Music One Step Closer - Linkin Park Manager/Valet N/A Personality The Power Plant has a reputation for creating superior athletes. But Johnny Mayhem might represent the training facility's most gifted superstar ever. Comprised of a true legitimate heavyweight, the monstrous Johnny Mayhem displays a disarming amount of speed just as capable as the likes of Shawn Michaels and the technical technique of Bret Hart. From power moves to Senton splashes, this stellar athlete can do it all, and change strategies to fit his competition's size and style. With a heavyweight's size and strength, Johnny Mayhem wows fans with, not only his athletic ability, but also his leading man good looks, too. Wrestling aficionados have overwhelmingly dubbed him FSW's most promising young superstar. Only time will tell if the predictions come true, but we'll all be watching. Johnny Mayhem is one arrogant human being, the best description to sum up his personality. One of the cockiest guys you are likely to ever set your eyes upon. He likes to manipulate and intimidate those who oppose him. He will never take no for an answer, and always challenges for things that other people think are way out of his league. So if he does demand for a title shot, you better believe that he will eventually be provided with it. He is very ambitious and determined to be the best. He will always go to any lengths to surpass and defeat his opponent in the ring. He always strives to come out on top. Nothing is out of reach in his mind. He is quite the schemer, and very strategic. He likes to believe that he is always one step ahead of everyone else. With Johnny Mayhem, always expect the unexpected. He always has something up his sleeve, and will not hesitate to do anything if it provides him with an extra advantage over everybody else. Johnny Mayhem only looks after himself, and himself only, everything else is just for the taking. History As a young boy, Jonathon Holmes, today known to millions and millions world-wide as Johnny Mayhem, would often sit on his mother's lap and watch his idol, Jake Rockmore go to work in the SEW. He was a legend at the time, and his intensity and aggressiveness intrigued Johnny. While most kids his age were collecting comic books and watching cartoons, Jonathon Holmes was at ringside chanting his idols name along with thousands of Jake Rockmores adoring fans. The thrill of being at ringside was something Johnny would never forget. Even in his childhood days, Jonathon was quite an accomplished athlete. A football All-American in high school, an all-star in high school hockey and water polo. In the mid 1990's, at the time, Jonathon was attending the University of Michigan in Detroit. He was a star defensive lineman, contributing greatly to the team's National Championships. Jonathon graduated with a degree in Criminology. Jonathon had aspirations of working for the Secret Service out of college, but the sports-entertainment industry took hold of him. Jonathon weighed all his options, eventually deciding that the Secret Service's risks were not quite worth its rewards. It was the world of sports-entertainment, and more specifically the EFX, which was calling Jonathon Holmes to its ranks. Jonathon Holmes took the ring name of The Grater, and made his EFX debut in June 2000. Jonathons first appearance was especially notable, since it was the night after the pay-per-view, Abomination, an almost unheard of place for a rookie to try and make his mark. Amazingly, Jonathon not only survived, he actually managed to win his match in a Hardcore Four Corners Match against The Dark Prince, The Phenom and the very experienced Hardcore Champion Pazza. Immediately capturing the attention of the awe-struck crowd. The Graters impressive debut initially wowed the Federation's fans. The third generation superstar continued to rack up wins, eventually earning him a place in the number one contendership Tournament for the EFX World Championship. This was when The Grater first tasted defeat in his yet so quite remarkable and glorious career. Four of the greatest superstars in EFX at the time were chosen for this tournament, P.S.Mad, Inferno, Wilsbowski and The Grater. The first round was scheduled to be The Grater against P.S.Mad and Inferno against Wilsbowski. P.S.Mad was the most experienced wrestler at the time that The Grater had ever faced, and The Grater failed in what was the biggest test so far in his career, due to not being fully prepared. P.S.Mad went on to win the tournament against Wilsbowski, the victor over Inferno. That night, brought the end to the once known as The Grater, but a new beginning to a new side of his personality, Johnny Freeze. This man knew what he was doing, it was like a revolution in the mind of Jonathon Holmes. He wanted to start again, and he did in great fashion. He had a new finisher, Hell Frozen Over, HFO for short. His first match was against the King of Sting, in the first round of the Television title tournament, a title just being introduced to the EFX. Johnny Freezes mission now was to become the first ever EFX Television Champion of the World. Johnny Freeze defeated King Of Sting, with not much difficulty. The next round introduced a new comer, by the name of Blade. Johnny Freeze ate him up for breakfast, still striving towards his goal. Blade turned out to be a one-week wonder, as it has been said that Johnny Freeze ended his very short career. Then moving onto the final, where Johnny Freeze was up against Pazza. This match had a lot of history between the two, Johnny Freeze always coming out on top in the past. And this time turned out just like the last few occasions, Johnny Freeze defeated the most Resilient wrestler in the business, with the HFO. Johnny Freeze had done it! He was the first ever EFX Television Champion of the World! He had achieved his goal, as he celebrated his new succession big time. His first title in the EFX, hopefully to be the first of many. Johnny Freeze did in fact go on to win one more EFX championship title during his short EFX career. He went on to compete in one of the most brutal ladder matches ever witnessed in sports entertainment, in double title ladder match against his best friend Chyna. She put her Intercontinental Championship on the line, whilst Johnny Freeze put his Television Championship on the line. The outcome was Johnny Freeze victorious, the third person ever in EFX to hold two titles at once. Johnny went on undefeated till the EFX organisation finally met its demise, around a month later. Johnny still possesses both titles in his trophy cabinet, being undefeated as Johnny Freeze since then. After the EFX, Johnny Freeze entered a few low class wrestling organisations, like the FWWF and GSWF, just so that he wouldnt lose his touch, in preparation for hitting the big time, like he has now, when signing an FSW contract. Now with a new gimmick, Johnny Mayhem, some old friends, P.S.Mad and Sound, forming The Alliance of Violence, Johnny is heading into the FSW as an unstoppable force. Only the best of the best might have a chance of withstanding the wrestling ability that Johnny Mayhem has to offer, as he will cause havoc, destruction, disorder and mayhem here in the FSW. As you will soon see, mayhem is going to overrun you all, when Johnny Mayhem comes to town. And he will show you such extreme violence, that your lives will never be the same again. Johnny Mayhem is here in the FSW on a mission, and that is to become victorious and obtain the FSW World Heavyweight Championship Title, to finally achieve his dream, to be the BEST there is, and no matter what happens, he will eventually get it. The End is Near, and Mayhem is Here! General Wrestling Style All Rounder Finisher Hell Frozen Over - Mayhem follows on from a kick to the midsection, doubling over his opponent. He then hooks up the arms in a Double Underhook/Butterfly fashion, and then heaves his opponent up in the air, facing vertically. Whilst upside down, in a piledriver position. The arms are still hooked as Mayhem proceeds with a Jumping Spike Piledriver. In conclusion, the technical term for this is the Butterfly Spiked Piledriver. Power Moves Mayhem Assault - This is a submition manouvre. The technical term for it is the "Deathlock With Bridge". Mayhem sends his opponent to the mat, face first. He then wraps his left leg around their's, and then he drops to his back snapping his opponents legs backwards causing excrutiating pain, and in order to lock it in, he grabs onto his opponent's face with both arms, putting force on his neck in addition. The Last Resort - This is a top rope manouvre. Mayhem proceeds to climb to the top rope, after surpassing his opponent, leaving them lying on their back defenceless. Mayhem then executes a "Swanton" style diving leg drop, aiming the force at his opponent's neck. Strike of Fury - This is a small but effective combo. Basically a move that evolves around the momentum of the opponent. Mayhem connects with a drop toe-hold on the oncoming opponent. The opponent's face crashes to the mat, and as soon as he gets up, before he can turn round to retaliate, Mayhem delivers a jumping roundhouse kick to the back of their head. They go down to their knees, holding their head whilst reeling in immense pain. Fatal Error - This move is one of the most deadliest in the business. It has caused serious brain damage in the past, a former finisher of his back in EFX. Mayhem sets his opponent up as if going for a powerbomb, but instantly before impact with the mat is made, he switches the maneuver into an Sitdown Facebuster movement.
