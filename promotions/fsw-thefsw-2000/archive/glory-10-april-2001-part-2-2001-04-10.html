DATE: 4/10/01 ARENA: Rose Garden - Portland, OR

Lynn Lane: Well fans, let us get into the blood drawing mood, it is time for an Extreme match, infact this match is for the number one contendorship to the FSW Extreme Title currently held by Shawn Davies. So then, I am going to pick Punisher 2000 to come out on top. He is new but I feel after seeing him in action that he can surely take out Aggonier.

Sam Collins:

Punisher 2000, Punisher 2000, what about Aggonier, he has almost won the FSW Extreme title time after time after time. Sure P2K came close too, but Aggonier has showed a tremendus improve ment here in the FSW since he first started. Plus P2K only wrestles once a week. Let's get it on.

[Punisher 2000 and Aggonier lock up, P2K gets the early advantage with a swift blow to the jaw. Aggonier gets up and tries to clothesline P2K but is met with an overhead belly to belly suplex. P2K slides out of the ring and grabs a table from under the ring. He sets it up. He grabs a trashcan, trash lids, kendo sticks, chairs, baseball bats, chains and a fire extinguisher from under the ring. He begins to throw all the weapons into the ring, he slides out 2 more tables and stacks them up on top of each other. P2K slides back in the ring with a trash can lid. Aggonier grabs a Kendo stick and both men face off, Aggonier goes for a swing with the stick but P2K rolls over and smashes his knee with the trash can lid, Aggonier bends down to one knee and P2K jumps and smashes the lid over his head sending blood everywhere. P2K celebrates to the crowd and receives a wide boo from them all.]

Lynn Lane:

Amazing. Punisher 2000 really knows his stuff in the ring. Well, his Extreme stuff, actually, I have never seen him in a non Hardcore division before. Wait, he has won several world titles in other federations, but that was because hey were fixed federations.

Sam Collins:

 Punisher 2000, Punisher 2000, SHUT THE HELL UP ABOUT THAT WASHED UP HACK. Aggonier is just warming up, that is all. Just wait five minutes and you will see the true power of Aggonier, just wait and see, Lynn, just you wait and see. 20 bucks says Aggonier walks out the winner.

[P2K turns around and Aggoinizer, who is standing cracks him over the head with the fire extinguisher, P2K stands up and is busted open, Aggonier hits him again, P2K remains standing, again Aggonier hits him again, P2K still stands, Aggonier backs up and charges with the extinguisher, P2K bows down and back body drops him over the top rope and faints. The fans looked shocked and aftual chant for P2K for a while before he flips them off and they boo again. Aggonier goes under the ring and pulls out a gas can. Aggonier starts pouring a liquid over the tables and stacks on one more table and covers it with the liquid which is obviously gasoline. P2K looks at Aggonier who strikes a match and throws it on the tables, immediatly the tables are lit up. Aggonier slides into the ring with a chair and charges at P2K, P2K ducks and Aggonier slams the chair against the ropes which comes back and smashes him in the face causing him to be openend up.]

Lynn Lane:

I will take that bet Sam, I will give you odds. I also bet you before the night is done you will be obsessed about beer and Togas. And I will bet you that Punisher 2000 and Aggonier will go through those three flaming tables. And Punisher 2000 will win.

Sam Collins:

Alright then, Punisher 2000 to win, both men to go through the flaming tables and I will be drunk and obsessed about Togas. Hmmmmm, I sure could go for a beer right now, or a screwdriver, maybe some Vodka straight or some Irish Cream. No wait, I know whatI want, Rum and Coke!

Punisher 2000 then launches himself of the turnbuckle and lands a Punished DDT through all of them setting both him and Aggonier on fire. P2K goes for the flaming pin, 1...2....3..... and as P2K gets the win EMTs and Firemen rush out and put out the blaze. As P2K gets up burn't he refuses Medical Assistance as the scene

switches to the backstage area, and we see Shawn Davies, the NEW Hardcore Champion watching the TV in his VIP like locker room. He smiles a few times, then starts laughing aloud. They are laughing and give each other a high five as the walk up to Shawn and begin' to talk.]
 Stan Phear: Hey dudet, whats happenin'? Shawn Davies: Nothing, why you guys here? Sabin' Green: Uhh, we got matches Shawn remember? (Smiles) Of course you do man! Stan: Yah and so do I man. I get to face, MVP Sports! (Gives Sabin' a high:five.) So Shawn what you watchin' there?

Okay man. Sabin': Peace to the east..... aaahahahha! (Looks at Stan, extends his hand and they both yell out.) Woogidi:Woogidi! (The HEY ARNOLD! handshake!) See yah man...
