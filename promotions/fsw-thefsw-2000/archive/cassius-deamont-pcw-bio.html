Cassius Deamont Biography Real Name Cassius Deamont Nicknames None that matter any more Hometown Toronto, Canada Birthday 21st November, 1973 Height 6'4 Weight 243 Fed Titles None. Fed Awards None. Biography Former RUSH Champion, current FSW Superstar, Cassius Deamont likes to think that aside from whoever's messing for him that month, he has no enemies. Or at least, none that are a threat to him. He says it's because he's dealt with them all. Given his track record of taking people out of not just Federation's, but the entire wrestling industry itself, you'd be hard pressed to argue. It was just over three months ago when Cassius, an ex-mercenary from Los Angeles, owner of the Danse Macabre nightclub in Los Angeles, turned up on wrestling TV for the first time, on the first ever edition of RUSH Wrestling's Adrenaline. On that occasion Cassius left the show smiling, having totally dominated the night's action. It was the beginning of a trend for Cassius, as for the next six weeks he dominated RUSH en route to becoming their first World Champion. With the world belt Cassius continued his domination of RUSH, with noone being able to touch him until, at their Crossroads Pay Per View, the man who Cassius had screwed out of complete ownership of the company earlier in the night - Chris Sloboda - took it upon himself to gain revenge, running in during Cassius' title defence against Chris Matthews in a first blood match. Despite being the first to make his opponent bleed, Cassius left as the loser, as Sloboda knocked out the referee before he could see Matthews' blood. Boda then went on to physically demolish Cassius, bursting him open and costing him the RUSH Title. Soon after that both men left the company, Boda after a breach of contract and Cassius after being granted a release by the company, who were under the impression he was suffering from a brain haemorrhage. Cassius remained hidden, allowing the wrestling world to believe he had indeed suffered the injury and was either in a coma or dead, until he got a signal to return to wrestling. Chris Sloboda was in FSW as the lackey of Chip O'Meara. Cassius turned up on FSW Glory the next week, coming to the aid of CEO Paul Orion and taking out Sloboda, gaining a degree of revenge for Boda's actions at Crossroads. But the rivalry between Deamont and Boda is far from over, and only time will tell how this feud between two of the biggest names in professional wrestling will end...
