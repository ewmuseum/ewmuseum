Owner: Painkiller AIM: [detail withheld]
Painkiller has been involved in eFeds since the summer of 1997. He has ran and wrestled in many different eFederations. The eFederation he will be most noted for is the ICW which he created in October of 99. After six months the ICW fell and Painkiller has decided to unleash another eFed, the FSW. If you really want to know how Painkiller runs his eFeds then ask one of the members that has wrestled under him before. They will be more then happy to tell you.

The Man that never sleeps...Okay well maybe we're exaggerating a bit. He does sleep. However, Painkiller is constantly involved in the majority if not all Five Star Wrestling Activities. Painkiller runs all the cards, creates the Flash images we've all grown to love, and masterminded the Members Area. Needless to say, if not for the valiant efforts of this man, there would be no Five Star Wrestling!


Chief Executive Officer: Craig S. AIM: [detail withheld]
Craig S. has been in the wide-world of e-wrestling for close to two years now. He's known simply as "the idea man." Because of his constant efforts to improve everything he does, mainly angles and his writing ability. Some may know him as the EWN columnist Craig S..

However, when it comes to FSW he's all game. Always happy to say hello and answer any questions pertaining to just about anything, including his shoe size. Need some help on an idea for an upcoming angle or Roleplay, he's your man. Craig also tends to assist with the cards: booking and writing. Other duties include FSW column called [name withheld]. Also he tries his best to keep the wrestlers happy and well taken care of. As CEO [name withheld] must assume the role of cheif businessman, advertising FSW and running campaigns to keep FSW new and fresh. His personal hobby is various sorts of writing and likes to implement that skill wherever and whenever he can. He helps make and write cards when he has the time. A born multi-tasker and a good listener, that about sums it up the CEO of the FSW. On a final note, a small mystery surrounds this man, his age is unknown by anyone over internet.

Co-Vice President: Lone Star AIM: [detail withheld]
LoneStar is probably one of the senior members of this staff having been in the world of e-wrestling since 1996. LoneStar was hopping around through several big e-federation until he was signed on here. Prior to the FSW hiatus Painkiller and LoneStar were in talks and finally sealed a deal during the hiatus. LoneStar has been around a lot and knows his way around federation and can answer any questions.

As the second Vice-President of the FSW LoneStar will be putting in as much of his thoughts as he can to the others and will be helping form the angles you the wrestlers will live out. LoneStar is usually available on AIM to answer any questions you need answered. If he can't answer it he will find someone who can. He is also one of the main match writers and will be writing several of the matches you will be reading about and your wrestlers will be living out. LoneStar may not be a rookie in the business but he loves to hang with them and teach them the ropes so if you need help with a topic of rping he is one of the men to go talk to. He hopes to be a big part of the FSW and is looking forward to working here for a while.
