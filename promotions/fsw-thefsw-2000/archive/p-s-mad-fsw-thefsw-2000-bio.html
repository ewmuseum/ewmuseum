P.S. Mad Biography Height 6'10 Weight 320 lbs Hometown West Palm Beach, FL Date of Birth February 15, 1975 Alignment Heel Entrance Music Marilyn Manson - "Disposable Teens" Manager/Valet N/A Personality : P.S.Mad is not a very sane individual at the best of times, not least in a wrestling ring. That is the time when Mad feels most in control and that's when he is most destructive. Firstly, having suffered a bad childhood and many other bad years after that, being near death almost twice, he believes that he has been possessed by a force that he calls 'The Inner Madness'. he claims that it has saved his life and that he is now a new person because of it. Paul Street no longer exists, only P.S.Mad. In the ring Mad will first try to wear down his enemies. his instinct is to brawl but sometimes his technical past gets the better of him and he can sometimes outwrestle his opponent, destroying them with his finisher. But if he cannot beat them fairly he will resort to brawling and then his hardcore nature comes into play. As a last resort he will grab his coveted crowbar and try to beat his opponents to submission with it. History It is March 15th the year 1975. In the maternity ward in Saint James hospital in Leeds one more miracle was about to happen. Another child is about to be born into the world and with him will come happiness and joy. This child is 6 days late so everybody is anxious to see if he will be ok. And on the 6th hour of the 59th minute of this day the child is born. His parents are very happy; it's their first child. He's a very big and healthy baby and from this day one of the doctors said 'Expect wonderful things to come from this child.' She couldn't have been more wrong. This child that the parents would name Paul Street had one sister who was born on the 7th May the next year and lived a normal life. Well, almost normal. At the age of 10 he was a large child for his age.not particularly fat but very tall. And because of this the many children ridiculed him in his school. Day after day he would have to put up with name-calling and rejection. He was very depressed. But one person did stick up for him when things looked down. His name was Craig Fearnsides. Craig had been accepted into the social standings in the school and had many friends, but there was something about Craig and Paul together that nobody could work out. It seemed like the two could almost be brothers, but there was something more than that. At home things were ok for Paul. His parents loved him and even though he would fight with his sister sometimes they generally got along. Home was where Paul was the safest and he loved it. There the evils of school could not reach him and he was shielded by the love of his parents. It was when Paul started high school that things started to get worse. Craig and Paul ended up in the same school and the same class none the less but there were 28 new people who didn't know either of them very well. Craig as always found it easy to establish himself a group of new friends but Paul suffered the same ridicule he had put up with in primary school. Though Craig tried to stop them picking at Paul he didn't have as much influence here so they ignored him. Without Craig's help Paul was succumb to even more extreme acts of bullying. They started to beat Paul up and steal from him. Paul tried to tell the teachers but they didn't listen to him. Paul's life was starting to fall apart. He was getting lower grades in class and sometimes didn't show up at all. His parents began getting worried and pulled him out of that school putting him into another away from all contact with Craig. Here things started to go well, Paul kept himself to himself and managed to reach year 10 with a new group of friends. But with friends come foes. One fateful weekend Paul was out walking with his girlfriend, Claris. They had just been to the cinema together and were enjoying a quiet evening. Paul was walking her home when he met up with Craig. Craig looked in bad shape, like he had just been in a fight. Suddenly, from nowhere leapt 5 teenage thugs wielding weapons, all of which came from Paul's school. They looked drunk.or high on something and were obviously not in their right minds. They had just beaten Craig up, knowing that he was close to Paul, and now they were after Paul himself. "Well, well, well. What do we have here?" uttered the leader. He was called Kevin and he was smaller than Paul but held a crowbar in his hand. "If it wasn't the freak himself out with his freak of a girlfriend." Paul froze with fear, not knowing what to do. Claris held onto him crying, obviously knowing the gangs intentions. It was then that something changed inside Paul. A fire or rage was burning inside of him and he could not control it. "What do you want Kevin. Just beat me up again and be done with it." Craig and Claris looked on in shock.this was the first time Paul had said anything like this to a person like Kevin. "The freak grows some balls. But that won't save you." Kevin had an evil glair in his eye. Then quickly he swiped for Paul with the crowbar but Paul managed to get out of the way. Craig rushed at Kevin but was attacked by two of his mates, beaten with a pole and a baseball bat. Paul screamed and rushed at Kevin but was stopped by another dude with a steel pole. Paul took a massive blow to the ribs and fell to his knees. Claris dropped to her knees crying and Kevin grabbed hold of her. "You want to go out with HIM? Id satisfy you way more than he ever could." Claris tried to fight him but he was too strong. Craig leapt at Kevin, a bloody mess and managed to grab hold of him. But he lunged with the sharp edge of the crowbar stabbing Craig in the arm. Craig fell to the floor writhing in agony. Paul, who had been left for dead got up and tried attacking Kevin again. He grabbed hold of his arm and tried to get the crowbar out of his hand. One of Kevin's mates tried to grab Paul from behind but knocked him into Kevin. Paul lost grip of Kevin's arm and it swung backwards with the crowbar cutting into some warm flesh. All was silent for a few seconds then everybody realised what had just happened. Like waking from a dream Kevin looked around confused at what he had just done. He pulled the crowbar out of the flesh and it was stained with blood. Almost at once everybody looked at where the knife had been plunged then the grim reality of it all set in. Claris was on her knees holding her stomach.blood staining her clothes. The five thugs ran for their lives leaving the three victims alone, Kevin even dropped the crowbar. Craig by this time was already dead. Paul ran up to him and a tear rolled down his eye. Then he ran to grab Claris. Her body was limp but she still breathed. Paul sat holding her on the cold blood stained concrete assuring her everything was going to be ok. He did his best to stop the bleeding and then hold her again. She looked up at Paul and said, "I will always love you." Then there was no more. The life drained from her body and her breathing stopped. Then she lay dead in the hands of her boyfriend. Paul screamed an agonising scream of Pain, as two of the only people in this world who had cared for him had died. With broken ribs, a cracked skull and blood pouring from almost every oarfish in his body, Paul managed to grab hold of the murder weapon then collapsed unconscious almost certain to die like the two before him. Paul awoke two weeks later in a hospital bed. His first sight was his mother but it did nothing to soften the fact that two people he loved had died. When he recovered more from the incident he learned that he had been in a coma.it was touch or go weather he would live or die. His parents and his sister were very worried about him and were determined that nothing like this would ever happen again. When Paul had fully recovered from his injuries he and his family moved to London where Paul would spend the rest of his school life going to a boarding school. Everything seemed fine and Paul looked like he had put the past behind him. On the outside he seemed fine, but it was the inside that was suffering. The boarding school didn't do anything to help him, if anything it suppressed his emotions even farther. He made no friends and nobody dare pick on him now. As a teenager he was 6ft, the tallest in the school. They just left him to himself and he seemed happy with that. But Paul would suffer from nightmares, again and again, always the night of the incident. Even today, he hasn't forgotten that day. It was clear to see, very clear to see that Paul had changed. He had become more aggressive and could not control his anger. The first incident that triggered alarm was when one of the students accidentally bumped into Paul then waked away laughing as Paul crashed onto the floor. Paul immediately got up and ran after the buy grabbing him from behind, turning him round and punching him square in the face. Luckily for Paul there were many witnesses who saved Paul from any major punishment but several other incidents like this caused the school to take action. Paul needed something to take his anger and frustrations out on. The school came to the conclusion that Paul should take part in a contact sport of some kind. Judo and Karate were mentioned but Paul couldn't master all of the complicated moves, he was a failure. Then one day the teachers caught him watching his favourite show, Saturday Night Wrestling. A wrestling school was close to the boarding school and thy trained Paul for a number of moths. He showed great potential and things seemed to be going the schools way. Paul was becoming more confident and was making new friends. He was somewhat of a local hero taking part in wrestling events around London. Paul was very good, some would say almost too good. He dominated his opponents and destroyed them a many number of ways. But he started breaking the rules and demonstrating too much violence in the ring, and when he got suspended he craved violence outside of it. Though he could control this craving he lost many of his friends and then lost all of them after he finished in the school. Normal wrestling bored him and at the age of 18 he started fighting in underground wrestling matches. He adopted the persona 'Pissed Off' Paul Street and finished his opponents with a deadly Powerbomb which usualy injured his opponents. Soon Paul lost his appetite for this underground wrestling and grew sick of the little money he earned. He saved up some money on his own but then went to his parents for more. After hearing what he wanted to do his parents though about it, then decided to give him what he wanted. Soon after, with all the money he had gathered, Paul took off to America to peruse his dream over there. When Paul arrived in America he had already sorted out some accommodation and a job to keep him happy for a while. He became a part time sales assistant to scrape in the money he needed to pay the wrestling schools, and he lived in a shared apartment. Things were hard but finally Paul became enlisted in ECW's Monster Factory. Paul soon realised that competition in America was a lot tougher over here and loosing matches became a regular thing. Paul's 'Pissed Off' gimmick wasn't holding either and Paul looked like he needed a change. His personal life wasn't fairing too well either. The people he was sharing the apartment with moved on leaving Paul and a girl called Amy in the dark. The two fast became friends and just as soon as Paul gained a new girlfriend the dreams of the incident came back to haunt him. the closer he got to Amy the more Claris and Craig got in the way. Amy cared for Paul and wanted to help him but Paul was loosing his mind more and more each day. They had ended up in bed with each other once but it didn't seem to matter. The two had been through a lot and lived in the same apartment for over a year, but Paul changed immensely during this time. One night Paul found Amy in the bedroom crying. When he questioned her she blew up in his face, cursing Claris and Craig and wanting nothing to do with him. She them left the next day without a trace. Paul didn't notice until he got back from his training. When he did he found his crowbar.the one used in the incident laid on the bed with a note attached to it. Paul had forgotten about the crowbar. After he had recovered he was given the crowbar to try to break the anger. He was told to destroy it but he never got round to it. He was also given the locket Claris had been wearing.it had a picture of her in it. Paul read Amy's note, basically saying she couldn't sort out his demons of the past. Paul wept that night holding the crowbar up to his face and the locket of Claris in his right hand. Paul arrived at the Monster Factory one day dressed in red and wearing a steel mask. This was the start of his new gimmick, P.S.Mad. The P and the S stands for his initials and Mad is how he felt and what he was. When management took off his mask they could see that Paul had mutilated himself and that the scar was fresh. Paul pulled out of his pocked the crowbar, which was still dripping with his blood. Paul was obviously not in his usual state of mind and was sent home to think things through. He arrived at the Factory the next day not even changing clothes or sorting out his face and was taken to hospital, the cut had become infected and was life threatening. When Paul awoke from the operation something inside of him had died. He didn't feel quite the same. When the doctors called him Paul it was if he couldn't remember that name at all. Paul left the hospital loosing something but gaining something else. He gained a nasty looking scar on his face and lost Paul Street. He was now only P.S.Mad. After training in the ECW monster factory for a few years he then sent his first application to a federation named the EHWA. Armed with his crowbar he took on his first opponent on Valentines Day 1999 and became victorious after destroying his opponent with The Psycho Driver. In this federation Mad was seen as a jobber and there were many other people in the federation better than him. After loosing spectacularly to 'X' by being thrown off of a balcony through three tables he found advice from an accomplished professional named Suicide. He taught Mad how to survive in hardcore matches and how to destroy his opponents. The two were due to team up together but unfortunate money problems caused the fed to fold. Mad was bitter after the closing of his first federation. He had claimed no gold and suffered one of the worst beatings in the federation's short history. He lost all contact with his mentor Suicide and eventually decided to join a second federation named WOW. This federation had little talent and even less money. Mad competed in barbaric hardcore matches, the most memorable against the Junk Yard Dog where Mad got his first taste of gold, the WOW Intercontinental Championship. But shortly after the e-fed closed, this time doe to the president concentrating on his family life. Enraged Mad decided to pay the president a visit at home but was met by his bodyguards who left Mad for dead in the middle of the street. Mad was rushed to hospital and just as it looked like there was no hope for him he made a miraculous recovery. Things looked too good to be true.and indeed they were. Mad joined the SEW against his doctors guidance. He was not 100% mentally stable to wrestle but this did not seem to faze Mad. His first match was against a person named Death who felt the Psycho Driver and never returned. Mad took on another opponent who just happened to be the president of this federation named Bodine 3:16. The prez lost the match but he was impressed by Mad's skills and his unique attitude. He granted Mad a world Title shot if he could beat two opponents in a triple threat match. Those opponents were Chris Jericho and The Executioner. Chris Jericho was not a factor in the match and was injured early on leaving Mad and The Executioner to battle to the death. Mad kicked out of his opponents finisher then struck back with the Psycho Driver defeating him. When questioned after the match about how he kicked out of his opponents finisher Mad talked about something called 'The Inner Madness'. True to his word Mad was granted a world title shot against Madd Dawg but on the night of the match Madd Dawg did not show. The next morning he phoned the president explaining that he had quit. With a vacated title and a PPV coming around the corner the president booked the match of P.S.Mad vs The Executioner. This was a massive match, which saw a backstage brawl; both wrestlers kick out of their opponent's finishers and a controversial ending. Mad used his crowbar on Executioner knocking him out cold and claming the SEW World Champion an undefeated wrestler. In an interview the next day Mad explained exactly what the Inner Madness was and a new wrestler was introduced.his best friend named 'Vice'. The two hung around together but never became a tag team. Mad was instrumental in the success of his friend making him cruiserweight champion. One week after the PPV Mad had to defend his belt against Executioner once more. This time Mad decided the rules and made it a 'Mad Match'. A 'Mad Match' is a vicious match where you must grab a straight jacket hanging above the ring, strap your opponent in it then find an asylum van backstage and throw your opponent in the van. The looser has to stay in the asylum for one week. Executioner lost the match and lost his chance of getting another title shot. After the match Mad claimed that Without the Inner Madness he would be a nobody, like he was at the start of his career. At the next PPV Mad took on The Rock and surprisingly lost when he couldn't find his crowbar. In the same night Vice lost is Cruiserweight title. Enraged Mad went on a path of destruction interfering in the Rocks matches and beating on the rest of the Rocks stable. Mad had to take on jobbers to climb back to the top of the ladder and two weeks after he lost the belt, he claimed it once more. Even through Mad's success he was not the greatest wrestler in the fed.but a wrestler named Taker was. Mad never crossed paths with Taker but Mad admired him. Taker was an undefeated hardcore champion and tag team partners with a dude named Dead or Alive. In a title match between P.S.Mad and Dead or Alive, Taker looked on from the back and saw DoA get destroyed by the Mad One. Though taker never joined Mad as his tag team partner the two had mutual respect. After taking 2 weeks off from the e-fed, resting after defending his title against DoA Mad returned a slightly different person. He saw 2 stables fighting, The Union and Onslaught. The team of Onslaught included Executioner. The team of the Union were but 2 men and Onslaught had 4 men. Russian Freak was looking for a partner to take on Executioner and Death, and Mad accepted his invitation. The two destroyed their opponents and formed a friendship. When The Union was under attack from Onslaught, Mad and Vice rushed to the aid of the Union and an 8 man tag team match was set up for the PPV. Bodine was attacked backstage by Executioner and was substituted by a wrestler named Super Star. The team of the Union now proved unbeatable and destroyed the Onslaught with ease. But after the match Mad was attacked by a wrestler named Killer and was laid out in the center of the ring coughing up blood. The very next night was to be the SEW's last night and the last match was the most brutal. Mad defended his championship against Killer. Both The Union and the Onslaught got involved. Mad eventually won the match after Russian Freak delivered the Commie drop on Killer leaving Mad to pick up the pin. After the closing of the SEW Mad picked up several injuries and disappeared into the darkness with Vice. The other wrestlers in the SEW went there separate ways and some invited Mad to join them, but he declined and rested. Mad eventually lost all contact with Russian Freak and became bitter because of it. A few months passed and mad was healed and ready to taste championship gold once again. he joined a federation named the EFU which had just opened. The first card was a PPV and Mad entered into a 6 man battle royal for the Hardcore championship. Mad thought nothing of this match at first but little did he know it would cause one of the biggest feuds he had ever known. Highlights of the match were Mad pushing a wrestler named Kid Kray-z off of the stage in a trolley and Vice interfering in the match and setting the ring alight. Mad pinned a wrestler inside the ring whilst another wrestler named Sound pinned an opponent outside of the ring. The official verdict of the match was a draw and the EFU had joint hardcore champions later that night was a Royal Rumble main event and Mad got thrown out by a wrestler named Decimator. Kid Kray-z won the royal rumble to become EFU World Champion. In the next card Mad took on Sound to see who the undisputed hardcore champion would be. Mad won the match eventually but threw the belt down on the body of Sound quitting the federation! Mad then helped backstage but the federation died after 4 weeks. Mad went into resting again and Vice and Mr. I-Ate Him both left Mad. For one month Mad stayed in the darkness licking his wounds. But in this time he received an invitation from a former wrestler in the EFU named Grand Master Seexy. Mad discarded two invitations but after the third he took a look around and joined the EFX. Seexy had seen Mad's power in the EFU and tried to get him to join his stable named The Union. Mad refused at first and wet about things by him self. Seexy put himself in a match against Sound and set up a no.1 contender's match for the World Title against P.S.Mad and a wrestler named Justice. Mad won the match but Kid Kray-z destroyed Mad after the match, not forgetting what happened to him in the EFU. Seexy won his match then handed Mad the world title as he was the prez and could not hold titles. Mad's first title defense would be against Kid Kray-z. The two had developed quite a feud; Kray-z claimed that Mad had stolen his gimmick so Seexy set up a gimmick match between the two. During the match, Kray-z used Mad's crowbar on him and pinned Mad for the three count! Mad was confused and angry but refused to change gimmicks. Mad was sent into a few more matches (all non title matches) and destroyed all of his opponents. Kray-z continued to interfere in Mad's matches and made life hell for the Mad One. So mad did the same, interfering in Kray-z's matches. Mad came to the conclusion that he could not do things alone any more but was challenged by Grand Master Seexy to a hardcore match. It was a bitter battle with no clear winner. Confused but showing respect for Seexy, Mad joined The Union. Other members included Pazza and the Grater. The Union looked stronger than ever but Kray-z put a damper on things trapping them all inside a net and demanding Mad lost his gimmick! Seexy didn't do anything about it and in a move to try and split up a rival team to the Union named 'The Family' put Mad in a 4-man tournament. The first round match was P.S.Mad vs. Grater (Who had since joined 'The Family'). Mad won this match and in the final took on Wilsbowski who had defeated fellow Family team member Inferno. In an enormous struggle Mad finally defeated Wilsbowski but only just. After the match Wilsbowski demanded a rematch but one day before the match would take place, due to the death of his father and his many injuries, the match never came to. Kid Kray-z retuned into the scene demanding Mad changed his gimmick and Seexy had no choice but to grant his wish. Mad went into limbo becoming "Pissed Off" Paul Street but never actually had a match in this persona. After listening to POPS Kray-z decided to let him have his old gimmick back. The next EFX PPV drew closer and Seexy set up a strange match. P.S.Mad and a mystery Partner vs. Kid Kray-z and a partner of his choice if he could find one. the winning team would become joint champions of the world. Still annoyed with Seexy but also annoyed with The Family Mad planned and schemed with his mystery partner. One night before the EFX PPV The Sound of Madness was formed and Sound and P.S.Mad destroyed The Family injuring Inferno and Duke Thunder. At this moment Mad also screwed Seexy leaving the Union. When the PPV rolled along Kray-z had no partner but Mad was arrested for threatening to kill Kid Kray-z in an earlier promo. During the closing stages of the match Mad ran down from the back, delivered the Psycho Driver to Kray-z and The Sound of Madness became joint EFX Champions of the World. The next card after the PPV Seexy set up a triple threat match between The Sound of Madness and himself. Somehow Seexy won the match on a disqualification but then the next day jumped off of a building. His body was never found and the fed was without a world champion and a Prez, so Pazza became the new prez. Soon after this Kid Kray-z was ordered into a looser leaves town match with a new wrestler named Da King of Hardcore and thanks to Mad interfering in the match Kray-z lost! The Sound of Madness soon became EFX Tag Team Champions by defeating Dynamite Kid and Pazza bringing life back to this title that had not been defended for ages. Pazza with furious and demanded a rematch. Dynamite Kid wouldn't have anything to do with him so Pazza enlisted the help of a new wrestler named 'The Dark Warrior'. Pazza and The Dark Warrior won the match after two people interfered in it attacking the Sound of Madness in spectacular fashion then the Dark Warrior revealed himself as Grand Master Seexy! Three teams were now vying for the tag team titles, The Sound of Madness, Seexy & Pazza, and the Family, which included Johnny Freeze (Formally Grater) and the returning Inferno. The Sound of Madness took on The Family in a no.1 contender's match and won, but controversially. A rematch was set up but the e-fed lost many members after a re-start set up by new prez Paul Street (the prez of the EFU). Paul tried his best to try and get new members but ultimately failed. The federation then plummeted into death. A wrestler named Dynamite Kid took over but nothing was done with the federation. Mad left the federation having made a ton of new friends and enimies. This was by far Mad's greatest federation.
