Glory, 20 February 2001 (part 1 of 3)

20 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DATE: 2/20/01 ARENA: First Union Center - Philadelphia, PA

[The scene opens up and the Glory promo comes on and then the scene fades to the ringside area. The camera pans around the crowd and everyone is on their feet. Fireworks go off around the ring entrance and around the ring itself. The cameras then focus on the broadcast table.]

Lynn Lane: Welcome to Glory everyone. We are live from the First Union Center in Philadelphia tonight.We are just one week away from Love Hurts which is looking to be a spectacular show.

Sam Collins: That's right. We have a great show lined up for you this evening.

Lynn Lane: Yeah we do. Our ref Brian Jospeh is already in the ring so it's time for our first match.

Sam Collins: Wait a minute Lynn. I'm hearing that something's going on in the back. Lets go to the back.
[The camera cuts to the back where Cage and The Ripper are already going at it! The Ripper as a chair and he levels Cage with it. Cage falls back intothe curtain and ends up on the stage inside the arena!]
 Lynn Lane: WOW! What a shot! The Ripper just about took his head off with that one. The Ripper throws the chair down and picks Cage up. He hooks Cage for a DDT and drops him on the chair!
Sam Collins: Man this is crazy! The Ripper is showing no signs of letting up either. They are now making there way to the ring. Ripper stops and bashes Cage's head on the guard rail a couple of times for good measure.Ripper throws Cage into the ring and Cage gets to his feet as Ripper slidesin under the bottom rope.
[Cage kicks The Ripper in the gut as he's getting up. Cage hits the far ropes and charges at The Ripper but The Ripper takes Cage's head off with a massive clothesline! Ripper goes for the cover,......1......2.....NO He kicked out!]
Sam Collins: That was a close one. I thought The Ripper had it won with that clothesline. The Ripper picks Cage up and sets him up on the top rope. Ripper gets on the middle rope and hooks Cage for a german suplex. He picks Cage up and has him way up there! And he slams him down to the mat!! That had to hurt!
Lynn Lane: You're tellin me! I don't think Cage even knows where he is right now. The Ripper is just taking him apart.The Ripper looks out at the crowd and I beleive he is ready to finish him. He picks Cage up, and Delivers the firestarter! He goes for the pin,.....1.......2..........3!!

[The bell rings. DING..DING..DING]

Willy Wilson: Here is your winner, The Ripper Sam Collins: I would say it was a great match but it was kinda one-sided.The Ripper owned Cage! Wait a minute, what's this?! The Ripper is grabbing a chair and here comes The Reaper and Jack Miller. They each have chairs as well!"
Lynn Lane: Well this doesn't look good for Cage. The Ripper stands Cage up. Jack Miller and The Reaper are on either side of Cage as Ripper is standing in front of Cage. Ripper nails him in the head with the chair and at the same time Jack and Reaper get him from the sides! OH MY GOD!"
Sam Collins: Well I think that's about it for Cage. As our medical attendants tend to Cage The Ripper gets a mic.

[The Ripper gets a mic as he stands over the fallen Cage.]

The Ripper: This is the resurrection of The Brotherhood Of The Sanitarium!

Lynn Lane: What kind of sick group is this?

[The Ripper drops the mic and they all get out of the ring as the scene cuts to the backstage area. The scene is at Painkillers office. Then suddenly Steve Jackson walks up to the door. As he opens it Mark Jones runs up to him.]

Mark Jones: Steve I'm surprised to see you here. Are you back in the FSW?

Steve Jackson: Sorry, got some bussiness to take care of first. I'll send for you later.

[Steve then walks into Painkillers office and he shuts the door. The scene fades back to the ringside area.]

Lynn Lane: Is Steve Jackson back?

Sam Collins: Possibly. Was he the rumored FSW star coming back?

Lynn Lane: No it stated that the person returning tonight was  a hall of famer. Now Steve did tag with a  hall of famer in Jacob Jackson. Could this mean anything? Who knows.

Sam Collins: Too early to specualate I guess. Well It's time to go back to the ring as referee Toby Bullock is in the ring.

Willy Wilson: The following match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring at this time. Tommy Taker.

[Tommy walks out with an Australian flag to a weight and height introduction, hit green and gold pyros and Tommy walks down ramp. ]
Willy Wilson: And his opponent, Big Gooey Sac!! [Big Gooey Sac makes his way down to the ring. The bell rings and the action starts.] Lynn Lane: This should be a great match.

[Sac charges at Tommy and hits him with a clothesline. Taker gets back up and is met with a right hand from Sac that sends him back to the mat. Tommy Taker gets back up and is hit with another right hand sending him down. Take gets up one more time and Sac whips him to the ropes. He goes for a clothesline but Tommmy ducks it, comes off the other ropes, and hits sac with a forearm knocking Sac down to the mat!]
Lynn Lane: I bet Big Gooey Sac didn't plan on that. Tommy Taker picks Sac up and whips him into the corner. He charges at him for a clothesline but is met in the corner with a boot to the face. Sac whips Taker into the ropes and lands a back body drop. Sac plays to the crowd a bit.

Sam Collins: He better keep his eye on Tommy Take. Taker is already getting up. Sac kicks him in the ribs, sending him back down. Sac picks Tommy up and sets him up for a suplex but Take blocks it. Sac tries the suplex again but Taker blocks it and delivers a suplex of his own! Taker gets up and hits an elbow drop. Taker picks Big Gooey Sac up and whips him into the corner. Taker sets Sac up on the top rope and hooks him for a superplex. He gets Sac up and hits the superplex! Sac is down! Tommy Taker goes for the pn!.....1.....2......NO Sac kicks out!
Lynn Lane: What a match! back and forth the whole time! This is what FSW is all about! [Taker gets up, picks up Sac and whips him into the ropes. He goes for a clothesline but Sac ducks it, hits the other ropes, and hits a clothesline of his own. Big Gooey Sac picks Tommy Taker up and has him hooked for a suplex. Taker punches him in the stomach a couple times, slips around to behind Sac, and lifts him up for Instant Gratification! He holds Sac up for a second, and drops him down on his neck! Tommy goes for the pin,....1.....2......3!]
Willy Wilson: And the winner of the match, Tommy Taker! [Tommy grabs a microphone and stands in the ring as Big Gooey Sac is making his way to the back.]
Tommy Taker: I just wanted for all you fans that have been booing me, and showing me so much disrespect to know exactly how I feel about you. Personally, You can all go fuck yourselfs! I don't give a damn about any of you! Never have, never will so you can all go to hell!

Sam Collins: Fans we do not condone the actions Tommy Taker is using here.

[Tommy Taker drops the mic and is flipping the fans off and getting in their faces as he leaves the arena. The scene fades to a commercial break as Tommy Taker argues with the fans. The scene fades from a commercial break. The scene fades to the backstage area. CashnMoney is seen walking around in his locker room, preparing for his interview. CashnMoney waits in his room until he is given the cue to go on. Joe Blogs, comes in to the room about 5 minutes later. He gives CashnMoney the cue. CashnMoney starts to walk down the hallway. He gets to the entrance to the ring. The lights go out in the arena, and A dollar sign is placed on the FSW-Tron. CashnMoney walks threw the curtains. He walks down the ramp. He has a mic in hand. The lights start to flicker..In big green letters on the Wrage Vision the words "They're Here" appears.]
CashnMoney: What the hell is that!? [The Dollar sign is placed back on the FSW-Tron. CashnMoney continues to walk to the ring. He walks up the steps and slowly walks into the ring. The lights are turned back on, and the dollar sign is taken off of the FSW-Tron.]
CashnMoney: Well guys, it's been fun. I know most of you already found out. The internet is really revolutionizing the world. You can now find out, who's going where with their careers. Well the reason Im diong this is because FSW, hasn't been nice to me. I'm the greatest athlete, but they make me fight jobbers, after jobber. So I'm..doing it!
[Crowd starts to chant quit!] CashnMoney: Yup, you guys are right! I QU... [The lights start to flicker again. They interupt CashnMoney. The words "They're Here.." are placed on the FSW-Tron. "They're Here" disappears and the words "The Boogers Are Here!" slowly are running down the FSW-Tron and stop in the middle.]
CashnMoney: What the hell is this!!?? [The lights are turned off. Someone runs to the ring. The lights are turned on and A guy with overalls is behind CashnMoney. He starts to attack CashnMoney. He grabs a chair and beats on him some more! CashnMoney is bleeding. The guy grabs the mic as people rush in to take CashnMoney away.]
Guy: Hahahaha![Laughs very loudly. Suddenly stops laughing and has a straight face.] I'm Booger Man! I like wrestling, so I became a wrestler. And looks nothing hurts me!!
[Booger Man lays the chair on the mat. He jumps on the chair, back first. He gets up and begins to talk again.]
Booger Man: See I told you! Why don't you believe me!?!? [Security comes in and tackles Booger Man and takes him away.]

Lynn Lane: Fans we have no idea who the hell that is.

Sam Collins: Yeah. Well security has taken both Booger Man and CashnMoney out of the ring so it's now time for our next bout. Our referere will be Chris Glass.

Willy Wilson: The following match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring at this time, Excaliber and Justin Mills.
[Excaliber and Justin Mills make their way to the ring.] Willy Wilson: And their opponents, Rob  Knight and Anthony Brown. [Rob Knight and Anthony Brown enter the ring, as the bell sounds.   DING..DING..DING.] Sam Collins: We are now underway.  This is set to be a great matchup. [Right away, Excaliber grabs Rob Knight, and throws him to the ropes.  Excaliber goes for the closeline, but Rob Knight ducks, and then hits Excaliber with a closeline of his own.  Rob Knight then begins stomping away at Excaliber.  Rob Knight then picks up Excaliber, and sends him to the turnbuckle.  Rob Knight runs, leaps in the air, just as Excaliber moves, and Rob Knight goes chest first into the turnbuckle.]
Lynn Lane: Ooh.  That had to hurt. [Rob Knight falls flat on his back, as Excaliber goes and makes the tag to Justin Mills.  Justin Mills jumps into the ring, as Rob Knight begins to climb to his feet.   Justin Mills runs at Rob Knight, and drop kicks Rob Knight in the chest.] Lynn Lane: Rob Knight sure is taking a beating to the chest, huh? Sam Collins: He sure is Lynn. [Justin Mills, then starts stomping on Rob Knight's chest, going for the weakened spot.  Justin Mills then picks up Rob Knight, and goes to slam him face into the turnbuckle, but his plan backfires, and Rob Knight slams Justin Mills' face into the turnbuckle.  Justin Mills falls to the mat, and Rob Knight uses every bit of energy that he has to make the tag to Anthony Brown.  Anthony Brown gets in the ring, and starts giving Justin Mills fists to the face.  Justin Mills is once again on his back in the middle of the ring.  Anthony Brown gets Justin Mills in a leg lock, and keeps it there for about one minute.  Justin Mills is hurting, but will not give up.   After the move is released, Anthony Brown starts stomping on the leg, and repeatedly slamming Justin Mills' knee into the mat.  After Justin Mills gains the energy to get up, he punches Anthony Brown in the head a few times, before limping over to make the tag to Excaliber.  Justin Mills can barely walk on his leg.  Paramedics rush to the ring, and decide that Justin Mills may have a broken leg, and they can not let him wrestle under that condition, so Justin Mills is taken to the back, while his partner Excaliber is left to fight two people on his own.  Anthony brown kicks Excaliber in the gut, and gives him a powerbomb.  Anthony Brown then climbs the top turnbuckle, and gives excaliber a huge leg drop, right across the chest.  Anthony Brown goes for the pin.  1..2..Excaliber kicks out. Anthony Brown gets up and makes the tag in to Rob Knight.]

Lynn Lane: It's two on one folks so this could be bad for Excaliber.

[Rob Knight picks Excaliber up and sends him into the ropes. Rob Knight telegraphs a clothesline but Excaliber kicks Knight in the face. Excaliber runs over and hits Brown in the face and that knocks him off the apron and he falls and hits the guard rail. Excaliber turns back into the ring and picks up Knight. He places Knight on the turnbuckle and Excaliber climbs up there with him. Excaliber lifts Knight in the air and drops down to hit The Magnum Force! Excaliber covers Knight. The ref counts. ....1 ....2 ...3 DING..DING..DING]
Willy Wilson: Here are your winners, Excaliber and Justin Mills. Sam Collins: Wow!  That was a great match.

Lynn Lane: What a great tag match fans.

Sam Collins: Yeah that feud with Excalibur and Rob Knight is far from over. Maybe we can settle things this Monday at Love Hurts.

Lynn Lane: That's right. And speaking of Love Hurts. Tickects are sold out. But if you weren't forutunate to get one then you can still order live on PPV.

Sam Collins: Yeah. And we will be back after these short messages.

[The scene cuts to a Love Hurts promo. The scene fades from there to a commercial break. The scene fades from there to the ringside area.]
