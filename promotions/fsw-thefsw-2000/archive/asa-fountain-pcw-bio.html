Asa Fountain Biography Real Name Asa Joseph Fountain Nicknames 'The Most Happenin' Cat In All Of WT' Hometown Boston, Massachusettes Birthday December 3rd, 1970 Height 6' Weight 195 Fed Titles FSW Tag Team Champion with 'Bad Attitude' Jesse Ramey (1x) Fed Awards 'One Of The First Men To Urinate In The FSW Ring' Biography THE LIFE OF THE 'FRO' Asa Joseph Fountain, also know as 'Fro', was born on December 3rd, 1970 to Norm and Cindy Fountain. Norm, Asa's father, was of a Jamaican heritage, which gives Asa his dark skin. Every once in a while you'll hear Asa use the word, 'mon'. Cindy was African-American. Asa was born, and grew up in Boston, Massachusettes. He and his sister, Tess, were both good students, usually honor roll, and lived their lives like any normal children. Since Asa was born at the beginning of the seventies, he immediately latched on to the disco era. He has never cut his hair, thus creating his trademark afro! After high school, Asa had plans for college, but he decided it wasn't his place, as smart as he was. No, Asa wanted the big time! So, Asa went off to 'Funk' records to become a disco soul singer. He was accepted, and spread his jive-ful music for fourteen years. He was strictly underground, (which is why you probably never heard of him). After his 14 year singing career, he stumbled across a flyer. That flyer, said Primetime Wrestling Academy... After graduating from PWA, and having mediocre stints in New True Internet Championship Wrestling and Primetime Championship Wrestling, Asa now finds his home in Five Star Wrestling, nearing his 33rd birthday THE STORY OF JIVE Many people have asked, 'what exactly is Jive?' Well, this will set it straight. Jive is the essence. Jive makes us who we are. Example: Jive is to Asa as Mojo is to Austin Powers. It gives us the great personality, and guides us. However, there are those who lack jive. Those are the brainless fools who have no personality whatsoever and have horrible taste in music and style. In other words, 'goths'. :P But there are those who have substantial amounts of jive. Flaymin Aymin, Jordan Shabazz, The Rush, Amy Campbell, Hessian, Vampir Nosferatu and Minion The Imp (The three only goths with jive), Duke Williams, Jesse Ramey and of course, Asa. Just to name a few. ASA'S APPEARANCE Asa has a very distinct apperance. You'll always see him wearing round, polarized, blue rimmed sunglasses. He wears bright clothing usually. Lots of yellow, orange, and green. In the ring he usually wears bright white disco pants with a tight black t-shirt with 'Fromania' across the front. He's very dark skinned, and besides all that, there is the afro. All eight inches of pure fro. There is ALWAYS an afro pick stuck in it too. Asa is almost always in a good mood. When he's in a good mood, he'll have that swagger when he walks, with the occasional spin. And of course, it's the opposite when he's down. He speaks in a deep voice, much like the voice of Shaft. In short, Asa likes to keep a positive look, a look that he hopes to make people less serious all the time.
