FSW — Card

Glory 2, 10 July 2002

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene fades to black … as the haunting melody of "Orion" by Metallica starts to play, setting the backdrop for an orange text graphic. The Man Known as "The Gunslinger" … he was a man who helped mold the profession which so many watch today into what it is and what it was. The Myth Starting off in small gymnasiums and smoke filled clubs … he rose to the top of every promotion he laced up his boots in, winning matches and showcasing what he believed the sport was about … talent. The Legend Now he returns in a new form … announcing himself as the new Five Star Wrestling CEO … making sure that the business he helped create stays on track. The lights begin to flicker on the screen as the orange fades to a black and white moving picture of some very old clips of "The Gunslinger" Paul Orion … in one whipping an opponent with a cow bell and in the other, Orion slamming his opponent to the mat with his patented piledriver … the same move which was banned in the United States because of his ruthless application of the maneuver. Wrestling He struggles to hold onto what he lives for … desperately stringing to every connection he ever had and allowing himself to be more than anyone ever expected. The voice over speaks about Orion in a fashion which a legend like him deserves, the graphics lighting up the eyes of every crowd member. Pyrotechnics shoot into the air as the video switches to a scene of Orion’s first in-ring debut in FSW. "FSW is ’gonna get a healthy dose of what this business is all about!!" Violence. Raw Ability. Struggle. "I can promise that the wrestlers of FSW will be treated fairly!!" No politics. No bullshit. Straight-up Wrestling. "Five Star Wrestling … under my leadership … will be just that! Wrestling!" It all falls under one man. "Five Star Wrestling will not be a watered down version of the golden days!!" "The Gunslinger" Paul Orion…CEO of Five Star Wrestling The song begins to wrap up as the scene finally fades to black with the last chord, the only sound left is the booming voice of Paul Orion. "I’m going to make FSW what it should be … or I’ll die trying!!!!" The screen is filled with a bright flash of white light. When the light finally fades, we are now shown to be in the inside of the Hartford Civic Center. “Sad But True” by Metallica plays as the cameras pan around the arena before we cut to the announce table, where Lynn Lane and Sam Collins sit, though there is a third, empty chair. Lynn (shouting over the crowd): WELCOME TO THE SECOND EDITION OF FIVE STAR WRESTLING WEDNESDAY NIGHT GLORY!! Last week we made our official return to the wrestling world, and we’re gonna pick up right where we left off! Johnny Raindance, Timo Bolamba, Big Daveylicious, Epitome of Evil, Lo-Key, Jesse Ramey, and Tim Walden are just SOME of the superstars that’ll be in action tonight!! Sam: But you’re forgetting about the HIGHLIGHT of the show! Joining us for commentary this week will be none other than “Five Star” Mike Marvelous Himself! “Superhero” by Sky hits the sound system, and the fans quickly begin to boo. Lynn: Speak of the devil! Willy: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome this week’s special guest commentator, “FIVE STAR” MIKE MARVELOUS!!! Mike Marvelous proudly steps out onto the ramp and strikes a pose, giving his notoriously bright smile to the fans before walking down to the ring. Ignoring the hatred around him, Mike Marvelous cockily walks over to the table and takes a seat next to Lynn. Mike: And what would an FSW show be without MISTER FIVE STAR WRESTLING himself, Mike Marvelous?! Sam: It wouldn’t be, Mr. Marvelous! Lynn: Right... I’m getting word that there’s something going on in the back…let’s give it a look. The scene cuts to the backstage parking lot area where a long black limo has just arrived. The camera pans over every inch of the vehicle revealing nothing but black paint and tented windows. Suddenly the back door to the limo swings open and out steps a pair of well dressed men's legs. The camera focuses down on the slack's wearing legs as they pause for a second and then begin their walk into the arena. The cameraman follows carefully not to reveal this mystery mans face. Not more then 30 seconds later the mystery man is stopped in his tracks by an FSW official. You can see an unhappy twinkle glowing in the eye of the official. FSW Official: So I see you finally decided to show up.... Mystery Man: I don't want to hear it, Alls I want to know is if you have the tape I asked for or not? FSW Official: BUT! *Sighs* Yes follow me, its in the special locker room we had set up for your arrival. The camera follows the two men only raised high enough to see the well built shoulders of this mystery man and the sweaty bald head of the FSW official. Upon their arrival at the locker room the FSW official sets up the television set so that it will receive video programming. He then pulls a black tape from his dress jacket pocket. Sliding the video into the machine the camera pans over revealing that what was on the videotape. Interesting enough it was a copy of last weeks Glory main event. Mystery Man: You may leave now. FSW Official: BUT! *Sighs* Mystery Man: Are you still here? Mumbling something under his breath the FSW leaves the mystery man alone in the locker room watching the video. The camera pans back over the TV set once again revealing the mystery man has fast-forwarded to the final chair shot Timo has laid down upon with such force but accidentally. Mystery Man: Very interesting, very interesting indeed. *Laughs* I wonder what will come of this.... As the mystery man again begins to laugh, we cut back to the announce table. Lynn: I don’t like the looks of this... Sam: Yeah I hate mystery man crap too. Lynn: Anyway, let’s head to the ring for our first match of the night. Willy: Our first bout of the evening is a single match scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from Cleveland, Ohio, weighing in at 245 lbs... Jason O'Neil! The arena lights dim as Scarecrow begins playing and the dysfunctional Jason O'Neil jumps through the entrance and moves warily to centre stage. He walks slowly down to the ring in darkness, scanning the crowd and even the ring itself with almost friendly suspicion. Rolling into the ring and climbing to his feet as the lights return and the music stops, O'Neil trains his one good eye on the entrance and eagerly awaits his opponent. Willy: And his opponent, hailing from Kemptville, Ontario, weighing in at 259 lbs... Angelo Deville! Sympathy for the Devil hits and Angelo Deville saunters through the opening with a smirk plastered on his face. He pauses at the top of the ramp and raises a mocking hand to point at O'Neil, then flourishes it down across his own midsection to draw comparison. Laughing and shaking his head, Deville begins strolling leisurely down the ramp, exchanging words with some of the FSW fans along the way. O'Neil tenses in the ring and moves closer to the ropes as Deville reaches ringside and pauses. Feigning concern, Deville calls the ref over and yells something at it while indicating O'Neil with his arms. The referee rolls his eyes but nevertheless convinces O'Neil to back off into his own corner. O'Neil glares as Deville slides under the bottom rope, bounces to his feet, and throws his arms up into the air to accept the cheers only he hears. The music stops and the referee calls for the bell. Lynn: Like Deville has to worry about O'Neil cheap-shotting him! Sam: That O'Neil is unstable, Lynn. He's capable of anything... Right, Mike? Mike: What do I care? Are either of them even close to being Marvelous? Thought not. Without hesitation, O'Neil charges out of his corner and tackles Deville into the opposite turnbuckle, driving his shoulder into the other's stomache. O'Neil repeats this process several times before standing and lacing Deville with a vicious right hand. Sam: Come on, Deville! Pound that freak! Deville raises his arms as if to beg off, but O'Neil just grabs ahold of one and whips him across the ring. Just as Deville turns and bounces into the turnbuckle, O'Neil smashes into him with a huge clothesline that almost makes Deville flip over the ropes to the outside. Deville stumbles out of the corner, looking pretty out of it, and O'Neil seizes the opportunity to nail him with a bulldog. Mike: O'Neil's kicking his ass. Lynn: That's what happens when the fight doesn't start with a cheap shot! O'Neil gets to his feet and pulls Deville up along with him, then nails him with a vertical suplex. Flipping over, O'Neil hooks Deville's leg and the referee slides in to make the count. Lynn: This could be over! Sam: No way! 1... 2... Kickout! Deville kicks out just after two, rolling over onto his knees as O'Neil climbs to his feet and again grabs Deville by the hair. Deville throws a hand up as O'Neil lifts him, connecting with O'Neil's one good eye, causing him to release the hold and stumble back. The referee warns Deville. He stumbles over to O'Neil and picks him up in a fireman's carry, then falls backward, executing a Samoan drop. Sam: Perfect! Mike: Shut up... Deville is quicker getting to his feet than O'Neil, and he moves in to press the advantage, grabbing him by the head. He bends down and grabs O'Neil's arm to drape over his own shoulders, but O'Neil counters it with a roll-up. Sam: He's pulling the tights! Ref! Lynn: What? No he isn't. 1... 2... Kickout! Deville manages to break the hold just before three, and rolls out of the ring with a string of curses. O'Neil gets to his feet and regards Deville with his hands on his hips and a mixture of amusement and disappointment on his face. Lynn: What a coward... The crowd boos as Deville paces around the ring getting counted out by the referee. Sam: He's just gathering himself, Lynn. 1... 2... Deville takes exception to a particular piece of profanity levelled by one of the fans in the front row, and a shouting match breaks out between them. Mike: Why's he letting himself get distracted? Shit, why'd we sign this guy? 3... O'Neil decides he's had enough of this crap bolts towards the ropes. While Deville's back is turned, O'Neil grabs onto the ropes and vaults himself over, spread-eagling in mid-air as he flies down toward the preoccupied Deville. Lynn: Whoo! Sam: This is gonna hurt... The crowd heats him off by cheering, and just before impact Deville hits the deck and rolls off to the side, leaving O'Neil helpless to land on the guardrail. O'Neil tries to cover up, but it's too late and his exposed and stretched stomach connects with the top of the rail, emitting a scream of pain from the Scarecrow. He remains draped over it. Mike: No... That's gotta hurt! Sam: I never thought I'd be thanking the fans, but... Mike: ... Lynn: "But..."? Sam: But nothing... I'm not gonna start now. Deville climbs slowly and deliberately to his feet as the ref is forced to reset the count. He flashes a less-than-charming smile at the crowd as he stalks over to O'Neil and drops an elbow across his exposed lower back. Arching his back, O'Neil slides down off the railing and writhes on the floor. 1... The referee admonishes Deville, telling him to get back in the ring. Ignoring him, Deville picks O'Neil up and whips him towards the ringsteps, which he spills into with a loud crash. Deville rolls halfway into the ring and then back out, aggravating the ref and causing him to come outside. Sam: Get back in the ring, ref! Do your job! Lynn: He is doing his job! Why should he stand around and count if Deville is just gonna keep rolling in to break the count? Sam: Because that's what he's supposed to do! Mike: That's true, Lynn. I've never seen a ref adapt before. This is... Weird. The referee shouts at Deville's back as the man grabs O'Neil and hooks his arms behind his back, then throws him backwards with a double-underhook suplex. Continuing to ignore the ref, Deville once again pulls O'Neil to his feet and lifts him onto his shoulder before charging towards the ringpost. Mike: Why do people even try this ridiculous move? O'Neil slides down Deville's back and helps the other's momentum by pushing him into the ringpost. Deville's head smacks into the metal and he falls to the ground, dazed. An angry O'Neil hoists Deville to his feet and then knocks him right back down with a huge roundhouse. The referee begs O'Neil to take it back into the ring. Sam: Yeah, c'mon O'Neil! Let's try to have a wrestling match here! Paying the zebra-dressed man as much mind as Deville did, O'Neil lifts Deville up and locks him into a cross-face chickenwing. Deville struggles, but it's useless. Lynn: The Return to Darkness! Mike: Too bad he's not smart enough to do it inside the ring. Sam: Get in there, ref! The referee throws himself in between the wrestlers, now thoroughly pissed. He breaks the hold, spilling Deville to the floor and spurring a few heated words from O'Neil. Seeking refuge, Deville crawls over beside the announcer's table and collapses there. The referee goes over to see if he's alright, with O'Neil following close behind with completely opposite motives. As the referee reaches Deville, the man throws out an arm and pulls the ref's feet out from under him. Like a good referee, he must have thrown out his back or crippled himself on the way down, because he just lays there immobile. Before the confusion has time to wipe itself from O'Neil's face, Deville lunges up from the floor and spins around, smacking the ringbell against O'Neil's ear. Ding! Mike: Now that was marvelous... Er... Almost. Lynn: That was bullshit! He should be disqualified for that! Sam: For what? What are you talking about? O'Neil collapses in a heap as Deville climbs to his feet and discards the bell. He turns to the red-faced Lynn Lane and mockingly blows him a kiss, then turns to the fallen ref and starts helping him to his feet, feigning concern. Lynn: That son of a... Sam: How come O'Neil isn't a sportsman like Deville? Lynn: Shut up, Sam. The referee regains his composure and Deville picks O'Neil up, rolls him into the ring, and follows. With arrogant, assured slowness, Deville grabs the limp O'Neil by the hair and pulls him to his feet. Looking around at the crowd with a big smile on his face, Deville pulls O'Neil's arm through his legs and prepares to hoist him. The ring shakes as Deville nails O'Neil with a pumphandle piledriver. The referee falls to the ground as Deville casually drapes an arm over O'Neil's chest. Sam: It's over! Lynn: This is despicable! Deville cheated! Mike: It's only cheating if you get caught, Lynn. 1... 2... Sam: And the winner is... Shoulder up! Sam: WHAT?! Mike: I'd have kicked out, too. With perhaps a nanosecond to spare, O'Neil's shoulder leaves the canvas. Deville can't believe it. His eyes go wide momentarily, then he curses and hooks O'Neil's leg, putting all of his weight into the pin. Sam: This time for sure... 1... 2... Kickout! Lynn: Deville looks worried! Bewildered, Deville jumps to his feet and begins stomping on O'Neil with feverish intensity. Despite the relentless assault, O'Neil continues trying to climb to his hands and knees. In desperation, Deville grabs O'Neil's hair and prepares to do something, but O'Neil hooks his legs and drives forward, knocking Deville on his ass. Maintaining the hold on Deville's legs, O'Neil gets to his feet and begins trying to turn Deville over. Deville struggles frantically as O'Neil glares down at him with one lifeless, glazed-over eye. O'Neil's insanity overpowers Deville's resolve, flipping the latter into a Boston crab. Lynn: Boston crab! Sam: No! Deville screams in pain but refuses to adhere to the ref's advice of giving up. O'Neil leans back hard, gritting his teeth and clamping his eyes shut, every muscle tensed. Deville reaches for the ropes but his fingers fall a good foot short. His face drops to the mat, contorted with pain and dripping sweat. Mike: Give it up, pal... Let's get on with the show. With a sudden burst of strength, Deville lunges forward as far as he can and reaches for the ropes... And falls short. Lynn: I think we've got ourselves a winner! Mike: It's about time... O'Neil pulls Deville a few feet closer to the middle of the ring, then leans back with renewed fury. Realising the ropes are out of the question, Deville begins pushing himself up, trying to gain leverage to break the hold. Both men are straining to achieve the advantage. Just as Deville straightens his arms, O'Neil releases the hold. Sam: He broke it! He broke it! Lynn: No he didn't! O'Neil let go! Sam: Yeah... Right. Mike: Who cares? O'Neil grabs the writhing Deville and pulls him to his feet, then sends him into the ropes. As Deville comes off the ropes, O'Neil lunges forward with a clothesline. Deville manages to duck underneath it and hook his arms around O'Neil's waist from behind. Before O'Neil can react, he finds himself pitched backward with a belly-to-back suplex. Deville falls to one knee and pauses to catch his breath, but forces himself into action when he sees O'Neil already starting to get up. He runs over and grabs O'Neil's ankles and drops to a seated position as he pushes both feet into O'Neil's groin. Rolling back onto his shoulders, he extends his legs straight up into the air, supporting O'Neil by the jewels, and simultaneously pulls down with his arms. O'Neil's eyes go wide with horror, and his mouth opens in a silent scream. Lynn: What... The... Hell...?! Sam: The Soprano! The Soprano! Mike: This has to be illegal... The referee looks confused and decides not to count it as an illegal hold, opting instead to ask O'Neil if he wants to give it up. O'Neil adamantly refuses, though with a vicious shaking of his head rather than words. O'Neil spends an agonizing thirty seconds struggling to break the hold and get down. Lynn: Why isn't the ref breaking the hold?! Mike: Never mind that... How is O'Neil still in this thing? Sam: You'd have tapped, right Mike? Mike: What are you, Sam - stupid?! Of course I wouldn't... I'm Marvelous. Anyone else would, though. O'Neil looks around for a rope or some way to break the hold, but he's trapped. The pain is overwhelming. His good eye rolls back into his head while the other stares blankly forward. Lynn: I can't believe O'Neil is hanging in there. This move is sick! Deville is sick! O'Neil is a machine! O'Neil goes limp on his perch, falling backward with an arch of his back. Deville maintains the pressure, however, and the referee reluctantly lifts one of O'Neil's arms. He holds it up for a moment before releasing it, and it drops. The referee does this a second time and receives the same result. He lifts the arm up a third time and gives it a little shake. Lynn: C'mon O'Neil! Don't let this guy win! Sam: He has no choice! The referee releases the arm and it drops like a dead fly. The referee calls for the bell and Deville lets go of O'Neil's ankles, mercifully letting the man fall to the mat. Sympathy for the Devil begins playing over the speakers as Deville triumphantly climbs to his feet, being booed by the crowd. Basking in their hatred, Deville climbs out of the ring and begins making his way up the ramp. Sam: Deville wins! Deville wins! Mike: Whatever. At least it's done. How did O'Neil hang in there that long? Lynn: We've gotta take a commercial, folks. We'll be right back! FSW Wednesday Night Glory is brought to you by the Incredible, Edible Egg. Lynn: And welcome back to FSW Glory, where we will be witnessing what hopes to be an exciting battle between Magnum Wolf, and Jack Brooze! Mike: Exciting? Lane, isn’t it enough excitement for you to be sitting next to me? “Courage” by Alien Ant Farm plays over the PA system. Pyros go off down the ramp and Magnum Wolf steps onto the entrance ramp. Willy: Introducing, from Tucson Arizona standing 6’6” and weighing in at 280 pounds, Magnum Wolf! Magnum Wolf walks down the ramp and steps into the ring, jumping up and down, ready for the match. There is a mixed reaction from the fans as “Sleep Now In The Fire” by Rage Against The Machine plays over the PA system, and Jack Brooze steps out to whirling strobe lights. Willy: And his competitor, from Brooklyn, New York. Standing 6’5”, Jack Brooze! Another mixed reaction comes from the crowd as Brooze steps into the ring. He wastes no time as he quickly walks up to Wolf, and locks up with him. Brooze gets Wolf into a headlock, but is pushed away against the ropes. As Brooze bounces back he is knocked to the canvas by a shoulder block from Wolf. Sam: Look at this! Two losers who will never amount to anything going at it! *Yawns* What a waste of time… Wolf lifts Brooze up by the hair and starts raining down punches on Brooze. Wolf lifts him up and delivers a scoop slam, then immediately delivers a fall away slam short after. Lynn: Great athleticism by the Alpha Male! Mike: You think that’s great? You just watch when the fomer FSW World Champ sets foot in the ring! Wolf bounces off the ropes, but Brooze gets up, and catches Wolf and hits a spine buster. Brooze, now with the upper hand, lifts up Wolf and Irish whips him into the corner. Brooze runs at Wolf and hits a Yakuza kick. Sam: Yep, another jobber move. What else is new. Wolf falls to the floor, and Brooze makes the cover. 1…2…kick out from Wolf. Brooze yells at the ref as he turns back to Wolf, and his met with a headbutt to the gut from Wolf! Brooze stumbles away. Wolf runs and hits a bulldog to Brooze. Wolf lifts Brooze up, and delivers a powerful dragon suplex. Wolf makes the cover. 1…2…kick out by Brooze. Lynn: What a match between two talented athletes! Marvelous: Talented? Who said anything about talented? You’re lookin’ at the man who MADE the word “talented”! Wolf bounces off the ropes, Brooze stands, and delivers a power clothesline to the returning Wolf. Wolf stands, and clips Brooze from behind. Wolf kicks Brooze multiple times, lifts him up, and delivers a inverted implant DDT, which is followed up by a death valley driver. The driver creates a hard THUD, making a large “oooooh” from the crowd. Brooze lies motionless in the ring. Wolf walks away from the fallen Brooze, lifting his arms in the air. Meanwhile, Brooze stands, and kicks Wolf from behind, sending him to the floor in the corner. Brooze walks over and delivers multiple mud hole stomps onto Wolf. Brooze lifts Wolf to his feet, and irish whips him into the corner. Brooze runs at Wolf, but is met with a big boot to the face. Wolf stands keeled over in the middle of the ring. Brooze takes advantage and bounces off the ropes. He goes for a bulldog, but is grabbed by Wolf and Wolf slams Brooze with a violent spine buster. Mike: Heh, good, hopefully that got rid of at least ONE of these amateurs. Sam: Couldn’t have said it better, Mike! Wolf smiles, and the crowd cheers. Wolf lifts up the fallen Brooze, and delivers the “Point Of Authority”. Brooze hits the mat, and falls in a heap. Wolf, nonchalantly covers Brooze. He lifts his head in victory with a large smile on his face. 1…2…NO! Brooze wakens, and reverses the cover into a small package! The ref counts 1…2…3! Lynn: AND BROOZE WINS THE MATCH! Winner: Jack Brooze Sam: And another former FSW star picks up the win. Yay. Brooze jumps around in victory, as Wolf rises to his feet. Wolf looks around in disbelief that he has been defeated. A look of anger covers his face, and he walks over to Brooze. He taps Brooze on the shoulder. Brooze turns around, and is met by a full fist to the jaw from Wolf. The sound of knuckle on jaw makes an “oooooohhh” from the crowd. Brooze falls to the canvas. Wolf stomps the hell out of him with his foot. He lifts up Brooze, throws him off the ropes, catches him, and hits a sit down power bomb. He lifts Brooze once again, and hits him with a Tiger Driver ’91. Brooze lies motionless in the ring as Wolf exits the arena, his face with a look mixed of anger and satisfaction. Lynn: And Wolf destroys Brooze after a perfectly fair match. What a sore loser. Sam: Sore loser? That was great! Put that on replay! Lynn: Actually, we’ll just head to a commercial. We’ll be right back! FSW Wednesday Night Glory is brought to you by Clamato. Clamato – Finally supplying Americans with what they want… A blend of tomato juice and clams. Lo-Key stands, shifting his weight next to a much smaller Robin Nicely, who seems nervous at the big man's presence. She clears her throat and speaks. Nicely: Lo-Key, there's been plenty of speculation about why you broke up the three way match with Jesse Ramey, Asa Fountain and Ming last week, but we'd like to find out from you. Lo-Key: (snorts) Well, that's awfully big of you. I would think the answer is obvious. Here you are asking me about it after all. This time last week, I couldn't get an FSW official to return my calls, and I sure as Hell wasn't hearing from any of you fine media types. He glares down at Nicely a moment and then smirks. Lo-Key: No, this time last week, all anyone heard about me was from some asshole saying I was lame because of my name. But then, until the guy comes up with a better name than "The Marking Ground" for his column, I'd say he doesn't have much room to talk. No, all I've been hearing about are the great former stars of the FSW who are carrying the torch for the old place and on and on and on... Well, I got a question of my own. He turns to Nicely, with his hands on his hips and leans down toward her, looking her in the eye. Lo-Key: If all these guys were so great, then WHY DID THE FUCKIN' PLACE CLOSE?! Nicely jerks back, but keeps the mic close enough to catch his comments. Lo-Key: Anybody paying attention last week should know that it's the guys you don't know what to expect from that are the most dangerous. Those old timers? They're gonna become a part of the FSW's PAST one more time. Nicely: And um... speaking of the past, would you mind commenting on your past wrestling career? Word is that you were part of some other promotions before, but no one seems to know where or when. Now it's Lo-Key's turn to look uncomfortable and he starts to answer just as Ming comes running up. Ming: HEY MAN! What’s up! I’m glad I caught up with you, I’ve been trying to thank you ever since last week for the help you gave me! Lo-Key rolls his eyes, but Ming doesn’t take the hint. Ming: So, if you’ll both follow me, I’d like to present my good buddy Lo-Key with a gift as a sign of my gratitude for his help last week. Ming leads/pushes Lo-Key and Nicely towards a closed door which read “Lo-Key” on it in giant gold letters with little star stickers plastered around it. He slowly opens the door and a blinding light flows out of the room. Nicely: What the hell? Lo-Key looks inside the room, that quickly steps back out with a sneer on his face. His stares angrily at Ming, breathing hard, obviously enraged. Ming: Cool huh? The camera follows Ming into the room, revealing what induced such rage upon the form of Lo-Key. For indeed, plastered on the wall is a gigantic painting of Ming and Lo-Key holding hands, standing over the prone bodies of Asa Fountain and Jesse Ramey. The caption at the bottom reads “Ming + Lo-Key = Best Buds”. Ming: No need to thank me pal, it’s the least I could do. But I’ve got to run, I need to prepare for my match. But don’t worry good buddy, I’ll be seeing you during your match… Ming winks at Lo-Key knowingly. Lo-Key: IF YOU INTERFERE IN MY MATCH I’LL KILL YOU YOU MOTHERFUCKER! Ming: Hey, I didn’t say anything about interfering in your match. I wouldn’t dream of doing such a thing. Nope, not good old, rule following, law abiding, straight edge me! See ya later man! Ming runs out of the room just as Lo-Key snaps and tears the painting down while screaming in rage as the camera cuts back to the announce table. Lynn: Welcome back, loyal FSW fans, and we're going to be going right back to big in-ring action tonight, as "Your True Life Fantasy" Carl Blair takes on the man who was filling this third chair last week in Tim Walden, and it's going to be interesting to see if Blair has bitten off more than he can chew, or does he have more up his sleeve than just arrogance...Your thoughts, Sam? Sam: I'm insulted to even hear the implication that Blair might not be able to beat that washout Walden, I mean, he was a former PCW TV champ, how much lower can one g-- Mike: A-HEM.. Sam: As I was saying, PCW is so bad that thanks to Mike here, the TV Title actually became the most prestigious title in the company briefly, until he just stopped caring and it got stuck with jobbers like Walden here. Mike: A-men, I mean there have been a lot of examples of blights upon the face of wrestling, but I think Walden might just take the cake with his bloated ego, just because he had the privilege of getting his hands on something that my FSW reputation kicked up a few notches in his PCW-caliber worthless life. Lynn: Well, I can't say I'm surprised..Let's take it to the ring with Willy Wilson now, as the former 10% owner of the PCW takes on the somewhat controversial Carl Blair. Willy: The following match is scheduled for one fall, and has a 15 minute time limit.. "Right Now" by SR-71 hits the PA, sending the crowd into a mild frenzy from the opening riff as a small round of green pyrotechnics fire down the entranceway with the drums' entrance, climaxing in Tim Walden's emergence from the entryway and down the ramp to a decent pop... Willy: Introducing the competitors, firstly, from Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, weighing in at 226 pounds and 6 feet even, Tim Walden! Tim quickly slides in under the ropes and scrambles to his feet at a far corner, but as soon as he does, the lights dim mildly and a slow-burn of orange pyrotechnics begins, as the words "Your True Life Fantasy" appear on the FSW-Tron and "What's Your Fantasy" by Ludacris takes over the PA, the dirty groove of the song serving as an apt backdrop for Carl Blair's strutting, arrogant entrance, the look on his face saying "You're no longer on my level" as he makes his way to the ring, making a similar roll in under the ropes on the opposite corner. Willy: And his opponent, now in the ring as well, from Orlando, Florida, weighing in at 6'5" and 255 lbs., "Your True Life Fantasy" Carl Blair!
