FSW — Biography

Sound — FSW biography (2001 capture)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sound Biography Height 6'11 Weight 325 lbs Hometown London, England Date of Birth May 1, 1975 Alignment Neutral Entrance Music "The Violence" Manager/Valet N/A Personality He's a disturbed soul. Has had his family taken from him in the business and now his mission is to destroy anyone and everyone. Beliefs include that of a higher power that will one day rule the world. He says that he is a disciple of the higher power and does his bidding. Does however, have a small, kind side hidden deep within the monster. Killed a lot of people and is still wanted for those murders. He hasnt been proven guilty as of yet. History He was born in a cold, damp room on a stormy night. The storm was so extreme, it sounded as if it was right in there with him. So right away he was born into a world of sound. He was born according to historians "Sindophrenius Luceferier", a name that Sound denies having any association with. Sindophrenius was born immediately into the darkness that he talks about today. No one to bring him up. His mother died immediately after birth and the whereabouts of his father are still unknown. Sindophrenius spent his childhood alone in darkness. As he was born in the dark, with no one to tell him better, he assumed that it was the norm to live life in the dark. The young child was all alone in the cruel world. He spent his days in that remote area. We know not how he lived and fed himself as a baby but we do know that he adapted well to his life of the night. There seemed to be however, darker forces at work surrounding the child. What we know for sure is that he lived alone for at least the first five years of his life, during which, he drank and ate, taught himself to walk and talk. The mystery deepens further at this point as the child had no previous experience of verbal intercourse between any adult. What happened is still today a mystery that flies around the backrooms of many an efed. At the age that we spoke of earlier, 5, the child was abducted. Sound says he still remembers vividly, his kidnappers who would eventually go on to parent him for almost 10 years, but refuses to make any comment as to who they were. It happened during the daytime. The child was asleep. As he was startled by the abductors, he woke up to bright light. The pain of it was agonising. He had never before, seen daylight in its entirety. He was taken to a large, gothic house and again, put into a dark room. It was here that the child received the name Sindophrenius Luceferier. And it was here that the beginnings of the entity known as Sound started to come about. The child was completely un socialised. He was like an animal. Feeding on anything that dared move, caring not for any personal property. The abductors, who disappeared after Sound first joined the small organisation EWF, seemed to have a strange effect on the child. He was given strange, sinister teachings late at night in front of a huge fire in the middle of his dark room. Talks of afterlife and godly powers have crept up. Slowly but surly, Sindophrenius became "human". To what extent, we already know. There was still that, animalistic, dark side to him. A side, he still bears today. The two men, who he likes to refer to as his guardians, brought him up. They taught him all they knew and made him a mind to be reckoned with. They saw him as someone very special. Someone who would be very important in his later life. They told him prophecies of his life and his ultimate purpose of life. It was a life of evil. It had to be injected into an uprising industry at that time. Today, it is one of the biggest in the world and already we know, that Sound is an important man in the game. As the child grew wiser, he began to "see the light". No longer did he want to live by the night. This scared the Guardians who saw a turn for the wrong in him. They drastically tried to refrain him from living to full potential by locking him away. Unfortunately for them, Sindophrenius had now gotten more powerful than they. He knew how to get what he wanted, and instead, the Guardians were locked away. Sound tells us that it was only temporarily. Until he entered the industry. He didn't want them interfering anymore and potentially ruining his life. At the age of 15, Sindophrenius Luceferier joined the EWF, a move that was a really bad one for the company. Unfortunately, Sindophrenius Luceferier wasn't well received. For one, his name was instantly a flop. He was branded a fake and a phony. Chants of "We've seen it all before" echoed round arenas all round the country. He dropped the Sindophrenius, and became just Luceferier. Even though he wasn't well received, he eventually won his first match at his first PPV after a succession of losses to begin with. It was a highly demoralising time for him. Match after match he lost. He began to wonder if there was any truth in the Guardian's words. Or was it that they had to be there with him, and by locking them up, he was holding himself back. They were let free and never heard of again. A week later, he won his first match. It was in this fed, that he first met Jessica, a girl who would go on to be a new sort of guardian. They remained unspoken colleagues until the feds mysterious collapse two weeks after Sindophrenius won his first match. Along with it went his hopes of a title, and small sense of power that he had built up. Jessica first spoke to him at this time. They were both now out of a job. Jessica was out of a home too. In the first act of good heartedness ever in this human, he gave Jessica lodgings at his home. Only Sound knew the reason for the feds collapse and he held the secret to his heart. He still does today. The two grew closer together, Sindophrenius decided to stay out of efeds for the time being for personal development. He grew even more mentally stronger, to a point where it was dangerously scary. He also grew physically stronger. The result was a huge menacing man. In the process of this change, Jessica herself became aware of the darker side. To quite an extent. But she had by no account, the same knowledge of it as Sindophrenius. In the winter of 1992, at the age of 19 Sindophrenius joined the TWA with the 21 year old Jessica as his valet. It was immediately a change of environment for the young man. Gone was Luceferier. It was just Sindophrenius. The company immediately renamed him Sindo. It was here that his taste for human blood ripened and he went hardcore. It was generally an exciting time for him. Jessica had tamed the beast as it were. However, the company saw the uneasiness and refused to give him a push. As a result, Sindo was the beat up guy. The man who would take part in one helluva match but always end up on the losing side. He would win some matches, but not enough for good recognition. Then.. He switched. Jessica just disappeared from his side. Here was a reason to stop the man and unleash the beast. Sindo went on a rampage. Almost killing himself in matches, we started to see the true sinister side of Sindo. After a stunt went wrong and both Sindo and a young man named Jakk Maestro plummeted from the top of a Hell in a Cell type cage, onto the cold concrete floor, Sindo was suspended from the fed. It was his first title match. In Sindo's eyes, the stunt went fine. Nothing went wrong; it was how he intended it to go. The fed saw this and knew the only humane thing to do would be for a suspension. The terms weren't given to him. He was told not to come back until he was told to. Jakk Maestro suffered a broken neck and massive concussion. He was in a coma for three months and came out unable to speak, think, or walk for himself. The company had a lawsuit filed against it by his relatives. In a case that would go on for four years, the eventual outcome was a huge compensation loss for the firm. Sindo had still been out of action. The extent of his injuries were an still are unknown. But what is known, is that they were by no means anywhere near the amount suffered by Maestro. And where he was during these four years is a mystery. Sound says that it was a time for personal healing and reflection. Almost as quickly as he left, he returned in early 1996. The company had to make money to survive the huge loss. The only way forward was hardcore. Its what the fans wanted. And there was no one more hardcore than Sindo. The attraction was huge. But there were people who hated Sindo. The company began to fear for his safety. The figured that the moment he won the title, there could be an outrage from disgruntled fans who still remembered him from more or less killing their champion. However, there was another demand. People were gagging to see him lift the title. Sindo competed in almost every type of hardcore match known to man. Yet he managed to come out alive. It was as if there was an aura protecting him. He became annoyed by the fact that even though he had put so much into the fed, without receiving a title shot. His actions got to a point where the company feared another Maestro incident, so unwillingly had to give in. Sindo himself knew that it could be the make or break time for him so he proposed the most hellacious and satanic matches. It was the only non televised match in that entire PPV. It is still available on home video if you're lucky. It was the only one of its kind: A double Hell in a Cell, with a cage in between, Barbed wire ropes with an Inferno around the ring that made it red hot, Thumb tacks and shrapnel, Exploding C4 explosive turnbuckles with an exploding ring ladder match. The twist being that the belt was suspended high above the outer Cell meaning that there was only one way to get it... ladder on cell! Without going into too much detail, the match was as horrific as it sounds. With of course, only one man winning the title. Sindo lifted his first ever title that fateful night. It was a momentous occasion. Finally he had made a mark to go on to conquer that strange world. Little did he know that on that same night, it would all get taken away from him, and he would never again step foot into a TWA arena again. That night, something happened that mentally broke the big man's mind. The details of which are explained in his EFX days at the height of his career. Sound makes no comment as to what he did during this time. We do know exactly what happened that night and can make our own judgement. It will be told later on. Four years later a man entered the new born fed of EFU. It was just getting started and was recruiting brand new members. With the man came a young lady. Sindo had become Sound. Jessica had returned. She however, did not play a big part with the company. While she was recognised as his valet/manager, at no point was she at ringside. It seemed that the "Sindo" of old had put his past troubles behind him with the TWA and was going to make a huge impact on the fast uprising EFU. This fed was the start of the Sound you see today. The mean black heart with a slightly light side. The fed also saw the beginning of a "clique" of sorts involving the big man. This clique would remain strong long after EFU and still remains in some form today. It wasn't an alliance, but a mutual respect. This fed was one of the most important for him. Since he joined, we saw a drastic change in him. This was a new man. He showed his dark side and was using his mouth more. A real drastic change of gimmick and heart. Every time he spoke, it was as if he were preaching the words of some one higher than him. This was scary to imagine a persona more terrifying than him. The president of the fed saw great potential in him and we would find out that he saw he could relate to him. Their darker sides would explode later on. Competing wise, he had changed a bit but still wasn't making as much impact on his matches as he could have. He was getting frustrated at the outcomes were he came up on the losing side. However, this was the biggest part that he had ever played in a fed with him being pushed up to World Title status. He also continued in the hardcore division which could answer why he seemed to remain slightly underneath the top two guys, Grandmaster Seexy and Kid Kray-Z. Kray-Z and Sound had a rivalry, which would culminate at the first ever PPV for EFU. The main event was for the EFU title and was a huge battle royale. Sound would also be competing in the Fatal Four Way Hardcore match in which he would be facing a great wrestler by the name of PS Mad who for some reason or other, stayed Hardcore and wasn't put up to main event. The outcome of that was a tad controversial as both Sound and Mad pinned an opponent at exactly the same time resulting in two HC Champs. Sound had once again captured a Hardcore Title. The rumble was set to be a big one with an alliance between Seexy's "Union" and Sound seeming to come together. It got down to the last four men; Sound, Seexy, Kray-Z and Mad. The ref was busy sorting out the elimination of Mad when Sound suddenly clotheslined Seexy out of the ring accidentally. Ceasing his chance, Seexy rolled back in and knocked out Sound. The ref turned round just in time to see Sound fly over the top rope. Seexy turned round to Kray-Z but was met with a huge boot to the face. He quickly followed Sound and EFU had a new champion. Both Seexy and Sound were pissed off with each other for the events at the PPV. The president ordered a rematch between Sound and Mad to unify the Hardcore title, a match that Mad won but threw the belt at Sound saying that he didn't need a belt to tell him that he was the best. Sound did not say no to the belt and would hold on to it till his last days with the fed. The remaining time saw the three top men attempting to fight each other for the title. Seexy got the belt, Kray-Z got it back, Sound won a number 1 contender match and was set to challenge Kray-Z when... the fed closed. In a bizarre situation, the fed had closed with no one actually being told. It hung in limbo. Yet again, Sound had been robbed of an opportunity of getting to the top. It was at about this time that Grandmaster Seexy decided to break away from the limbo of EFU and create the spawning ground of the biggest power struggle to date in the world of efed. EFX was born. Eventually, Sound would go on to "become" EFX, staying in the fed longer than any one else. Including Seexy. The word spread, members of EFU started leaving to join EFX. Sound however wanted to stay with it. Who could blame him with a title match on the way.? Seexy eventually convinced him to join and Sound appeared on top of the ramp at the second show, once again with Jessica by his side. There was a mixed reaction for him but he stayed the usual, Sound was gonna go on a verbal rampage. His actions while not as extreme as TWA, were horrendous, putting out Jeff Hardy in his first match. It was a walkover, he immediately entered the EFX Title tournament and breezed through to the final. By then he had become a firm fan favourite with "the people's man" Seexy becoming the most hated man in the fed. On 26th May 2000, Sound lifted his first ever World title, against a friend of Seexy in a bloody match. It was an emotional time. He had been boosted to the top. Jessica had a big role with him. The power was almost there. Reforming a shortlived EFU tag team with Robby 2 Snobby, (GXXX), they expanded to become a huge stable that Sound became leader of. They dominated all comers in their path with Sound proving more frightening than we first thought. He had become the Vice President of the Fed after Seexy had needed help. Only Seexy stood in Sounds way of getting the utmost power.. He proved more difficult to budge than first seemed. It was at this time that Kid Kray-Z came across from EFU. He would become the biggest test of the new champion's title. Seexy quickly threw him into a match against Sound for the title. A match the fans had been waiting for since the end of EFU. Kid Kray-Z was as cocky as ever and the two quickly had some extremely heated moments. The match was declared a loser leaves town I quit match. It was as brutal as it sounds with neither man daring to speak the words. Kray-Z produced some handcuffs midway through the match and attempted to hook up Sound. It backfired and Kray-Z became locked up in the ropes. We saw Sound switch and a satanic look came over his face as he proceeded to destroy three chairs in the process of annihilating the Kid. Just as the crowd had seen enough and wanted it to end, a woman ran out and threw in the towel by saying that he had quit. The woman was the sister of Kid Kray-Z. Kray-Z was quickly forced to leave EFX. Sound had defended the title, a feat that would never be repeated by another man in EFX. We saw Seexy change his style becoming more hardcore and in depth. Since a wild chair shot that left Sound in a pool of his own blood, a first blood match had been proposed between Sound and Seexy at the first PPV. This resulted in one of the most strangest fueds ever as we found out the mystery of what happened to Jessica for that period of time at TWA and what happened to Sound that night when he won his first ever title and then disappeared. The whole world was shocked and saddened to see a tender side of the evil man come out when we learnt about Leah. Leah had been the child of Sound and Jessica. Jessica had been removed from TWA business in her pregnancy to cover it up and stayed on maternity leave for the rest of the period after. By the time of the Hell in a Cell death match, Leah was 4 years old. The night was a celebratory one. Sound had won his first ever title and the family went to a respectable restaurant to celebrate. As TWA had feared, people were absolutely disgusted with him winning the title. A crazed fan, (who would later be found out to be the brother of Jakk Maestro), burst into the restaurant with a gun and fired several shots at Sound. The shots missed and instead hit the young child sitting by herself in the corner seat. Leah was killed immediately and Sound decided to leave the efed world forever. During the EFX time where we found this out, he explained how difficult it was for him to come back to the business. That's why Jessica wasn't at his side in EFU. The story unfolded with more heartbreak and sobbing fans as Abomination drew nearer. The final instalment was the visiting of Sound to Leah's graveside and telling her that he would do it for her. At EFX Abomination, Seexy busted Sound open to give the big man his first ever loss in EFX. Not only a physical loss but a mental one. In the most controversial event to have ever taken place in EFX, a night that was supposed to have been a big one for GXXX, turned into its death as two members screwed him. We saw the return of Kid Kray-Z who should have left for good.. Sound lost badly and didn't take kindly to it. He sabotaged the headquarters of EFX for about three days during which negotiations were taking place. There were calls for Sound to have been stripped of his title (the match was a non title match up) and even calls for his firing from the company. A fed wide vote was cast by Sound himself. The result was unanimous, 40% felt that Sound should be fired. Just as things seemed to go back downhill, Seexy did the thing that he is probably regretting most right now. In a move that allowed Sound to continue to build power, he announced that Sound wasn't going to have any action taken against him. And he announced that he himself would not compete unless it was at a PPV. The New Generation had been formed by Sound after Abomination. This would see the peaking of Sound before he left to find himself. We had seen just how much power he had when he sabotaged the place. Seexy had to be careful. Inferno of the Union started moving up the ladder fast. A former ally of Sound, he quickly joined him and Kray-Z up there with a triple threat match being announced. In a match that Sound never lost, Kid Kray-Z lifted the belt to become the new champion. His title reign was shortlived as he turned his back on the Union and was pinned by Inferno in the return Triple Threat match. Sound had become an important part of EFX. If he left, it seemed as if it would crumble. We saw the title change hands many more times. Sound would beat Kray-Z and then Inferno in one night to become two time champion. He would then be forced to relinquish it. Seexy would get it and then relinquish the following night. A tournament would crown a new champion. Kray-Z would quit for good. EFX would rise high and above all other comers. It was getting to be the biggest fed around and it was all thanks to two men working co operatively who couldn't stand each other. Eventually, Sound announced his retirement from the fed. It seemed that there was something else to do with it. External influences. It was a massive blow. No one could stop him. He had too much influence. In his leaving, we saw PS Mad join EFX. Without Sound, EFX was crazy. While the quality of talent rose, fueds were shambles and no one was being able to defend the title. PS Mad won it easily. And then lost it. The list of World champions grew while others stayed low. The value of the belt dropped and Sound saw it as him still not having achieved the most one could. A fed wide tournament, which would produce the four best players in the company who would go at it on PPV to find the elite, was set up. Since Sound had nothing to do with the company, he wasn't entered. The brackets set up a match between Jessica and PS Mad.. The match was set to take place. Mad brought his crowbar. He didn't manage to strike Jessica but some how she got it off him and used it against him. Mad got it back off her and was about to hit her when suddenly the arena went black. A gong was heard and "ARE YOU SCARED????" Sound had returned. An evil, glaring, psychotic look on his face as he stood toe to toe with Mad. In a move that shocked the world, he turned and beat the hell out of Jessica. This was the last we'd ever see of her in the fed. Sound refused then and still does to make any comment when asked why he did what he did. He says its too terrible to mention. To add to his troubled life in that way would be inhumane. The world will find out when its ready. A bond seemed to be in place between Mad and Sound. But still, Sound stayed away.. Was his coming a one off? Inferno and Duke Thunder had been in a stable together. With the Union being more powerful than ever, it was only a matter of time before a meeting was set up between them. Inferno and Duke had been mutual friends with Sound prior to this. Seexy got pissed off and demanded the entire "Family" to come out. Come out they did. Inferno leading the way with the others close behind. Eventually things got nasty and a fight broke out. The Union were obviously more powerful and started to get the upper hand. It has to be pointed out that PS Mad was part of the Union. The World Title was vacant and a tag match had been set up between Wilsbowksi and a mystery partner, and PS Mad and a mystery partner. Going back, the fight continued. Family members got hurt leading to even greater domination by the Union. Once again, as it seemed as if the end was near, the arena blackened and Sound returned once again. This time he kicked Union ass. Finally it was Sound, Duke and Inferno in the ring with PS Mad as the only Union rep. Sound stood back and Inferno and Duke advanced to devour Mad. From behind, Sound destroyed the two while their backs were turned. PS Mad joined in. Inferno was stabbed with a crowbar, and Duke was chokeslammed off the Titantron, leaving with a cracked skull. Mad's mystery partner had been found. The Union were out of the picture. It was time for a new game. And the purpose of Sound being in this existence would become crystal clear. Sound and Mad won the titles. Sound was now a three time champion. Finally, he saw a return shot at Seexy. Seexy had to run the gauntlet. He had to beat two out of three men in one night to become the champion. Two of them were the champs. The mystery guy turned out to be a jobber who posed no problem for Seexy. Next was Sound. This was different from their first encounter. Sound was stronger and put up a hell of a fight and finally pinned the Grandmaster's shoulders down for the three count. Seexy went on to defeat Mad and thus ending the double run. The two then picked up the vacant tag titles and held them for sometime. It was hear that we first saw why Mad and Sound were a combination. They shared the same purpose in life. It was as if they were twins separated at birth. They were here to prepare the world. enlighten it... They were here to do the bidding of a higher force. This was the nasty, cult feeling that crept in whenever the two men were around. It was "The Sound of Madness". They were here to fix it for "him". They were his disciples. He was the only one more powerful than them... They went a long way in twisting EFX around in the search for him. We began to see the downfall of EFX. It was also at this time that Sound became mentally unbalanced. He hadn't uttered a word since he came back. He was a silent assassin. He became a killer. It got really bad. After a letter from Jessica, he pushed two people off a cliff.. Jessica continued to send letters. She was expressing her love for him and wanted him back. She was obsessed with him. Sound burned down a hotel killing all the inhabitants. Finally, she sent him the last letter in the form of an audio cassette... In the end she drove a car off a bridge. In the back was her mother.. Jessica had been pregnant with the second child of Sound... Sound snapped out of the silent assassin mode and spoke his first words in two months. After that it was the Sound of old. Words being his prime form of expression. Together, Sound and Mad controlled EFX. The Guardian's prophecies had come true. He was a leader. Plans were being drawn up for a super fed. This was the brainchild of Sound. He would be the puppetmaster if all went according to plan. It would take time. In a showdown and finale to the whole life of Sound, "The disciples" claimed to have found the higher power. He was due to be unveiled at a PPV but EFX didn't stay alive that long. It crashed and burned and Mad and Sound crumbled.. All the power they had was gone. Sound made the most difficult decision in his life to close EFX, (Seexy had relinquished all power earlier on when he left). The plans for the Superfed were dropped. They remain to some extent today but do not seem to be getting anywhere. The two men were reduced to join another fed to attempt to rebuild some of it. It was a demeaning and difficult time. Sound got quite far in getting some power back. He was set to win their World title before that fed closed too. That was the last he was ever seen in a fed
