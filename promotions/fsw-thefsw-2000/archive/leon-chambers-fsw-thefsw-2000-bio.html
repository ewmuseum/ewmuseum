Leon Chambers Biography Real Name Leon Chambers Hometown Oakland, CA Birthday 12.12.1979 Height 5'10' Weight 222 Fed Titles FSW LightHeavyweight Champion (1) Biography Leon Chambers is a Five Star Wrestling superstar who plays the game with his own set of rules. However, although he is considered as a ‘bad guy’ – the female fans cannot seem to get enough of him. His Hollywood good looks and his flamboyant wrestling style, as well as his impressive arsenal of moves, make this 5 foot 10, 222 pound high-flyer stand out in the wrestling crowd. Leon posses a threshold for pain that is second to none in the business which is very useful when he endures punishment from bigger and more powerful foes. One of the factors that make him so successful against his larger opponents is his quickness, versatility and innovation in the ring. However, his most impressive weapon is the deadly ‘lightning quick’ Shooting Star Press, which he calls the Revolving Revolution, a dazzling move made famous by Jushin “Thunder” Liger and Billy Kidman. After battling in many indy federations around the West Coast of America, Leon Chambers found himself in the FSW where he rekindled his friendship with the then FSW International Champion Cyle Chaos. Leon debuted in FSW under the moniker of ‘Falken’ and was apart of ‘The Untouchables’ with the legendary Glitch. After Glitch left the company due to unknown circumstances – Leon’s friendship with Chaos grew stronger as he turned his back on the rest of The Untouchables and formed ‘The Black & Blue Mafia’ along with Big Daveylicious. Now back in Five Star Wrestling under his real name, Leon Chambers has once again found himself in a clique of sorts. The Five Star Five along with Mike Marvelous, Epitome of Evil, Jacob Jackson and, once again, Big Daveylicious. Each man apparently with the same goal: To bring FSW back to it’s original glory by ridding it of all the untalented losers. Although his first stint with FSW was short lived, Leon managed to capture the Light-Heavyweight Title within his first month with the company, making the other workers aware of what he could do in the ring despite his size.
