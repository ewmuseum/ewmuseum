Tommy Taker Biography Height 6'2" Weight 231 lbs Hometown Illawarra, Australia Date of Birth December 4, 1976 Alignment Unpopular Heel Entrance Music My Hero - Foo Fighters Manager/Valet None Personality This guy is a party animal and believes that there is more to life then kicking asses left and right. Mainly the other things are girls and alcohol. He's a sucker for Foster's Light Ice beer and loves to have a few quick ones before his matches, in which he cheats to win if he's getting a whooping. He always manages to find himself in trouble and is a major suck up to authority figures. History Tommy was just a normal teenage kid, with embarrassing parents and a bedroom he used as a sanctuary from the outside world. Most of the turning points in his life occured in that very room. Back when he was only 8 years old he watched his very first wrestling match and instantly fell in love. When he was 10 he tasted his first alcoholic beverage, and also instantly fell in love. The room was his world, The room was where he used to hide from his overbearing parents who wanted him to become a lawyor or someone who was succesful, but all that Tommy wanted was to become a professional wrestler. One day at the age of 16 when he was dragged into the living room with his parents to discuss his future he let it slip, he commited a sin in that very household and admitted that he had signed a deal to join a professional wrestling training camp with his two friends, Ridley Reid and Robbie Hoffman. At that very moment he was disowned and cut off from his family, he was tossed onto the street with a suitcase, a wallet full of cash he manage to smuggle out of his work obsessed brother's room, and a six pack of Foster's light ice beer he also stole to keep him company. For the next couple of years he learnt the tricks and the trade of wrestling under the watchful eye of Zeik Little, a former WWF wrestler and graduated with flying colours at the age of twenty, his friend Ridley also graduated but Robbie failed miserably and decided just to tag along with them anyway. General Wrestling Style Submission, Rulebreaking, Technican Finisher Instant Gratification - Tommy gets into a German suplex position, and executes it real slow. So that when the opponent is horizontal he will be slammed on his neck with the force generated. Power Moves Aussie Suplex - Northern Lights Suplex Inverted Powerbomb Delayed Brainbuster Australian Crossface - Usually taking the opponent down by the arm, the opponent is on the mat on their stomach, while Tommy Taker is lying to the side of the opponent's body. After scissoring the arm of his opponent closest to him with his legs, Tommy reaches around his opponent's head and pulls his arm across the opponent's face, locking his hands together and pulls back, applying pressure to the opponents's neck. This move can also be described as a Candian Crossface.
