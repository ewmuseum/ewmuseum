Maximum Velocity, 6 April 2001 (part 2 of 3)

6 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DATE: 4/6/01 ARENA: Kiel Center - St. Louis, MO

Lynn Lane: Well fans we are back. And what an exciting match we just saw. The return of an ex-FSW superstar and the debut of a future FSW superstar.

Sam Collins: It was wild out there. Too bad there wasn't a winner.

Lynn Lane: Well you can't always have what you want Sam....

[Suddenly 'Chemicals Between Us' by Bush plays as Painkiller walks out followed by Big Daveylicious and Tommy Taker. They enter the ring and Painkiller has a mic.]

Painkiller: First off. I'd like to personally welcome everyone to my show, Maximum Velocity. The action you watch each and every work is my doing. So give credit where credit is due.

[The crowd boos Painkiller.]

Painkiller: That's not the kind of respect I deserve damnit!

[The crowd boos louder and 'asshole' chants start.]

Painkiller: Oh you guys want to get cute huh? Well fine, be that way. But just so all of you know, the action I am about to take is on your hands not mine. You drove me to it!...So Johnny Mayhem, get your ass on out here!

[Mayhem's music hits as he walks out with the IC title.]

Mayhem: What?

Painkiller: Don't get smart with me bi***! I just want one thing from you.

Mayhem: And that would be?

Painkiller: That piece of gold drapped over your shoulder. So just lay it down right there and head on to the back.

Mayhem: What? You can't do that!

Painkiller: And why can't I? Who am I? I'm your damn boss and you're going to do what I tell you, alright? And if you refuse then that's insubordination. Which results in you being fired. I don't play boy.

Mayhem: Fine, but by doing this you just made your life a living hell.

[Mayhem drops the title to the floor and walks off.]

Painkiller: You see. That right there is what you call power. And I have all of it. Now I must direct my attention to another person. EOE, you and I have crossed paths a lot over the past few months. And on Tuesday it will end. You and I will meet in a cage. You know the stipulations on the match. If you win you face Big Daveylicious at the next PPV for a shot at the World title. But if I win then you don't. Well I am adding one litlte stip to that. Everything is the same except when I win then you will no longer be employed by company. I hope you like the additions.

[Chemicals Between Us plays as The Coalition walks to the back as the scene fades to a commercial break. The scene fades from a commercial break to the ringside area.]

Lynn Lane: Fans we are back. and it seems the match with Painkiller and EOE this Tuesday has just gotten that more important. As EOE isn't only fighting for a World Titlte match but for his job. I am sure he won't be too pleased about that.

Sam Collins: He won't be happy about that at all. But you got to hand it to Painkiller though. He made Tuesday that much more of an important show to watch. Which is good for him.

Lynn Lane: Yeah he does make the shows entertaining. But I question the ways he does it though. Like stripping Mayhem of the IC title. No call for that.

Sam Collins: That might of been true. But it was funny though.

Lynn Lane: I see your point. In a sick and twisted sort of way though. Well fans it's now time for our next match. Let's go to the ring.

Willy Wilson: The following match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring at this time, Alex Corvis!

[Corvis makes his way to the ring.]

Willy Wilson: And his opponent, P.S. Mad.

[P.S. Mad makes his way to the ring.]

Sam Collins: This should be semi-entertaining.

[Corvis and Mad start off the match with the test of strength. Alex Corvis easily wins after a few minutes, and throws Mad against the ropes, and dropkicks him down to the canvas. He picks him up, and chops him to the chest, a few times and backs him up into the corner. He chops him some more, and whips him to the other corner and charges. He ends up with Mad's boot in his face. He turns around rubbing his face in pain. He turns around and is closelined down by P.S Mad who then starts to stomp away on Alex. He lifts him up, and snap suplex's him down.]
Lynn Lane: Nice snap suplex there by P.S Mad. Sam Collins: Did he make THAT move up? Lynn Lane: No, I think som- wait why am I answering this? Just shutup Sam. Sam Collins: Sorry. [P.S Mad climbs ontop of Corvis and starts laying in some mounted punches. The ref pulls him off, but Mad pushes him out of the way, and P.S Mad turns around and kicks Alex in the head. He lifts him up, and chops him into the corner, and starts to stomp him down to the canvas. He pulls him out from the corner, and lays him in the center of the ring. He runs at the corner, and climbs atop. He points at Alex, and he jumps off hitting a nice big splash!]
Ref: One...Two...Thr-! Two COUNT! Sam Collins: Wow he had to reach in deep to kickout of that one Lynn... Lynn Lane: Yah I know Sam! [P.S Mad lifts Alex up, kicks him in the mid-section and sets him up for a piledriver. He lifts him up, but Corvis wriggles free, and is now on his feet. Mad turns around and is superkicked down! Corvis lifts him back up, and takes him down with a vicious DDT! He bounces off the ropes, and hits a nice big legdrop to the throat! He goes for the pin hooking the leg!]
Ref: 1...2! Oh! Two COUNT! Lynn: Wow, he kicked out of a bunch of strong moves Sam, what a wrestler, what...a...wrestler!
Sam Collins: Now thats my kind of man! Lynn: Now your scaring me Sam... Sam: Sorry is it my face? Lynn: Hey look P.S mad is up! [P.S Mad staggers around, but Corvis gets him, and takes him down with a nice german suplex! P.S Mad slowly gets up, and luckily ducks a closeline, and lands a neckbreaker on Corvis! He picks Alex up, and throws him off against the ropes, and catches him in a sleeper hold. He lifts him up and takes him down with a nice sleeper drop! He goes for the cover!]
 Ref: One...Two...Thre-! Two Count! Lynn Lane: How the hell did he kick out of that Sam! Sam COllins: I dont know, but he's gonna win I know it! Lynn Lane: Yah...Sure Sam. [P.S Mad can't believe it, but he continues the match. He lifts Alex up and he backdrops him down to the canvas hard. He picks him back up, and backdrops him down hard again, then again, and then finally again. He picks him up once more, and he takes him out with a hard brainbuster onto the canvas! P.S Mad climbs to the top rope, and connects with a moonsault and goes for the pin!]
Ref: One...Two...Three! Willy Wilson: Heres your winner, P.S Mad! Lynnn: What a great match Sam. Sam: Yeah I know, I wish I was Alex, cuz he won! Lynn: He didnt win. Sam: I thought he did... Hmm *thinks* Lynn: Hey look who the hell is that coming tothe ring! [Two men, one big and on little but both strong, run out from backstage. The bigger one has a chair, and he slides into the ring while the other gets a table from underneath the ring, and slides it into the ring.]
 Lynn Lane: Those men just got contracts a few days ago! It's Sabin' Green and Stan Phear! Sam COllins: HOT action here! [Sabin' sets up the table, leaning it against the turnbuckle. P.S Mad turns around and is hit over the head by Stan hard with the steel chair! Stan throws down the chair, and picks up P.S Mad, and throws him at Sabin' who kicks him in the mid-section and drives him down with the Initionality (pedigree)! He picks him up, and he puts him in front of the table, dazed. Stan lines him up and charges and spears him through the table! The Excellence! Stan and Sabin' stand in the middle of the ring posing and flicking off the crowd. Sabin' ask's for a mic, and begns to address the crowd.]
Sabin' Green: I guess what we're trying to say is that P.S Mad better watch out, because we're the best damn team, and we're gonna kick your ass! Hit our music!
[He throws down the mic as they exit the ring and go backstage as the scene goes to a commercial. The scene fades from a commercial break to the backstage area where Max Danger is talking to Ryan 'Mush' Wilson. Suddenly Next comes out of no where and hits Danger with a chair. Next pounds on Danger for awhile but security breaks it up. The scene fades to the ringside area.]

Lynn Lane: Fans we are back and what a night we have had so far.

Sam Collins: Yeah it has been a night to remember. And we still have a few matches left. So let's go to the ring.

Willy Wilson: The following match is for the Prime Time title. Coming to the ring at this time, the challenger, Suicide!

[Sucide's music starts playing and he walks out from the back] Willy Wilson:: Making his way to the ring now, his opponent and the champion, Sean Rivers! [There is a mixed reaction from the crowd. Sean Rivers's music starts to play.] Lynn Lane: The defending Prime Time Champion- Sean Rivers !! [ Rivers walks down the ramp and grabs a chair. He raises his title up into the air and throws it into the ring. The referee picks it up and raises it signifying that the match is for the title. Sucide is almost mesmerized by the title. Rivers gets into the ring and swings the chair at Sucide's back. Sucide gets out of the way of the chair and Sean drops it.]
Sam Collins: This match is underway!!! [Sucide takes River's head underneath his arm...DDT ONTO THE CHAIR! River's head hits the steel and bounces off it. He clutches his head as Sucide gets backto his feet. He grabs a handful of hair and pulls Rivers back up again. He tosses Riversinto the ropes and picks up the chair...Rivers on the return.. Sucide swings the chair butSean ducks. Sucide spins around as Rivers comes off the ropes. Sucide swings the chair...but is met with a hard right hand sending Sucide to the mat!]
 Lynn Lane: This is an action packed match and it's only just begun!! [Rivers lifts Sucide off of the mat and puts his head under his arm. Rivers sits on the top rope and delivers a hurricane DDT to Sucide, bouncing his head off of the canvas. Rivers gets up and runs backwards into the corner where the ref is standing. The ref falls down in a heap.]
Sam Collins: Typical Sean Rivers.. Anything to win!! [Rivers looks down at the ref and smiles. He goes and picks up the fallen Sucide and whips him into the corner turnbuckle. Rivers pick up the chair and holds it in front of him. He runs as fast as he can towards Sucide...but Sucide gets a foot up in time. The chair smashes Rivers in the face sending him to the mat.]
Sam Collins: What a great move by Sucide, Rivers never saw it coming!! [Sucide picks up Rivers and ducks Rivers head between his legs. He raises him up and slams him down with a thunderous powerpomb. Rivers bounces off of the mat and looks like he is out. Sucide goes for the cover, but the ref is still out.]
Lynn Lane: Great move by Sucide but there is no ref to make the count. [Sucide makes the count for him self. 1...2...3...4...5...6... Sucide lets go of Rivers leg and crawls over to the fallen referee. He slaps the ref in the face a few times and the referee wakes up.]
Lynn Lane: Now make the cover!! [Sucide stands and walks over to Rivers. He Bounces off of the ropes and hits Rivers with a hard leg drop. He makes another cover, the ref counts. 1..2.. Rivers kicks out.]
 Lynn Lane: Two and a half. Almost a new Prime Time champion there Sam. Sam Collins: The key word there was ALMOST, ALMOST a new champion. The match isn't over yet Lynn.
[Sucide looks furious. He begins to lift Rivers to his feet but Rivers low blows him on the way up. Rivers stands and picks Sucide up. He whips Sucide into the ropes and connects with a thrust kick to the stomach. He grabs the bent over Sucide by the head and delivers his signature snap suplex to him. Sucide grabs his shoulder in pain.]
 Lynn Lane: Looks like Sucide landed oddly on that suplex Sam. I think he has hurt his shoulder.
[Rivers is quick to his feet and he looks down at Sucide who is rolling around in pain clutching his shoulder. Rivers smiles.]
Lynn Lane: Don't do it Sean, he's in pain, just make the cover. [Rivers walks over to Sucide and stomps on his shoulder. Sucide screams in agony. Rivers rolls Sucide on to his stomach. Rivers is lying to the side of Suicide's body. Rivers scissors the closest arm of Suicide's with his legs, Sean reaches around Suicide's head and pulls his arm across Suicide's face, locking his hands together and pulls back.]
Sam Collins: The Rivernater.. Rivers just locked on his finisher the Rivernater. Won't be long now.
[Sucide screams as loud as he can in agony. Rivers keeps pulling back harder and harder on Suicide's head, applying pressure to his neck and shoulder. The ref continues to check in with Sucide. Sucide says no.]
Lynn Lane: Sucide is showing a lot of guts here in this match. Showing why he got this shot at the Prime Time championship.
Sam Collins: He should just give up, let the arm heal and come back again, he's just prolonging the match and his pain.
[After three minutes in the Rivernater Sucide can take it no more. Sucide nods his head and taps at the same time. Ding..Ding..Ding]
Willy Wilson: Here is your winner and still Prime Time champion, Sean Rivers!! [The referee gives rivers his belt and he raises it high in the air.]

Lynn Lane: Sean Rivers showed why he is one of the best in buisness right there.

Sam Collins: Yeah he is good. But not as good as me.

Lynn Lane: Sam you haven't wrestled in five years. You are way out of shape too.

Sam Collins: I meant in my prime. You know I was good.

Lynn Lane: Well I know you went like three and fifty before retiring.

Sam Collins: That's a damn lie. Fans we have to go to a commercial break.

[The scene fades to a commerical break. It fades from there to the ringside area.]
