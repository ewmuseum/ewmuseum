Harsh Addictions, 29 April 2001 (part 1)

29 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 29, 2001 Arena: Alamodome - San Antonio, TX

[The show opens by fading into a deserted and empty arena, the camera's line of vision zooming inwards gradually focusing down upon a Five Star Wrestling ring. Gusts of wind fills the serene atmosphere, as suddenly an immense explosion of fire consumes the ring, leading into the intro of "Papercut" by Linkin Park that begins to play deafeningly in the background. A still slightly transparent image of the FSW Intercontinental Champion, Carlton Carter, is suspended upon a FSW logo designed background on one side of the screen, followed shortly by a still slightly transparent image of the number one contender for his title, neXt, on the other side. The Intercontinental Championship then fades from out of nowhere between the two of them. The three images then begin to materialize slowly before your eyes, as still slightly transparent images of both Tommy Taker and Johnny Mayhem enter the screen, with the FSW World Heavyweight Championship located in the centre. As "Papercut" hits its chorus, still slightly transparent images of both Big Daveylicious and EOE enter the screen replacing the previous images, followed by a smaller image of Painkiller under Big D, and Steve Johnson under EOE. The opening then begins to fade to darkness, as the music continues in the background.]
[A loud explosion is heard, as the chorus of "Papercut" prolongs onto a roll of rapid fire footage, racing through clips of Carlton Carter, neXt, Tommy Taker, Johnny Mayhem, Big Daveylicious and EOE. This then leads to certain events outside the ring, that lead them to this event extravaganza, that is Harsh Addictions.]
