Six Finger Cisco Biography Real Name Cisco Milloy Nicknames THE Main Attraction, The Deformed One, The Freak Hometown Austin, Texas Birthday 9/12/1980 Height 5'11 Weight 250 Fed Titles Just wait... Fed Awards Current Record in FSW: 2-1 Biography Born in Austin, TX to Joe and Jenn Milloy, Cisco Milloy was not your average child. Cisco from the day he came into this world up until the day that he received his diploma from Austin High was constantly ridiculed for his extra finger. Buying gloves, shaking hands, or even playing a friendly game of thumb wrestling, you name it and Cisco had a problem with it. The kids did not let things like this slip on by. Cisco, now graduated and with nothing really in his future decided to attend the Republican National convention and show his support for George W. Bush in his presidential campaign. Bush gave his speech and began going around and shaking hands with the crowd. Cisco got his chance and it was ugly. I guess Bush didn’t like being grabbed by a Six Finger handshake because he pulled away and started to cuss at Cisco. The whole crowd started laughing at Cisco and 18 years of rage built up was released in the form of a Six Finger Discount on W. When Bush came to minutes later he banned Cisco from the state of Texas and all Texas Ranger baseball games. Cisco fled the state from that scene and eventually got a job as a freak in some traveling circus that had stopped in New Mexico. It was Cisco’s big debut and the crowd was pumped to see the likes of conjoined twins, strongmen, and Shemale/dragqueens….erm..a…or is it draqkings?…Anyway the crowd was into it until Cisco came along. The audience didn’t seem to think an extra finger was enough for someone to be in the freak show. Soon the audience and even the ringmaster were making fun of Cisco. Cisco again unleashed his fury, this time on Grendel, the Shemale/dragqueen king. The ringmaster was furious and soon banned Cisco from all circuses. Cisco again fled the state. Cisco knew that he had a gift for choking people out so he figured wrestling would be the best fit for him. He soon joined the PWA where he quickly graduated to EW. When that company went bust Cisco was promoted to PCW. Here Cisco could finally prove that he could be someone. After winning his first few matches Cisco went on a terrible losing streak and quit the PCW. After nearly a year of inactivity Cisco is ready to come back and finally prove once and for all that he can be someone and wrestle in FSW.
