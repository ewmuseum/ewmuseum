Sean Rivers Biography Height 6'1 Weight 230 lbs Hometown Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada Date of Birth July 22, 1978 Alignment Heel Entrance Music American Psycho - Treble Charger Personality Have you ever wondered why Sean Rivers is so confident? Sean knows that he's the very best and doesn't believe anyone can compare to him. Sometimes that makes him take opponents too lightly, but it hasn't cost him yet. History Sean was born in Vancouver, Canada to a well-off family. His parents gave him everything he could ever dream of, right off the bat. The only detriment in his life was that his family moved around a lot. Near the age of six, Sean's parents noticed his love and sports and encouraged it in every way possible. Sean started playing sports all year around, from football to hockey, from baseball to basketball, Sean played them all. As the years passed, everyone seemed to notice Sean's remarkable abilities in any sort of physical activity. It didn't matter what Sean played, he was always the best at it. After settling down in Halifax, at the age of 15, Sean's natural abilities began to cause him problems. With everyone telling Sean how good he was, Sean thought he'd inform everyone within earshot of what he thought of himself too. This led to more fights at home, with his father. After so many years of spoiling his son, Marcus Rivers decided to try to punish him, by taking everything away. School was pretty much the same for Sean. He had always been lazy, but now that laziness was costing him. He never worked in anything, and his grades slipped at a rapid rate. His low grades made his coaches ban him from sports teams which didn't seem to faze Sean in the least. He continued along the same path, and his parents decided they couldn't put up with him anymore. They sent him over to his grandparents in Moncton, New Brunswick. In Moncton, Sean was forced to learn what hard work was all about. His grandfather had been a boxing champion, and saw the ability in Sean. So he pushed him and pushed him some more. Sean, though, didn't really love boxing in the same way his grandfather did. As the end of 12th grade approached, a wrestling promoter saw Sean working out, and gave him his card. Sean finally had motivation to attempt to succeed, and he planned on doing just that. Then, on his birthday, in 1996, his parents plane crashed on their way out to see him. After this, Sean reverted back to much of his old ways. A couple of months later, Sean headed out on his own. Sean's inheritance made it unnecessary for him to work, so he didn't. On a day in early 1998 a man attempt to mug him. Sean destroyed the man and sent him back to the hospital. Sean remembered how fun it was fight like he did, and found the card the man had given him a couple of years back. It turned out that that man now worked for one of the biggest wrestling promotions in the world, World Wide Wrestling. He gave Sean a tryout, and Sean easily destroyed his competitors in wWw. One of the fastest rising stars ever in wWw, Sean won the European title and was main eventing shows before the owner of the company went insane, and shut the federation down. Or did he? Out of the ashes of wWw grew a new wrestling promotion. wHw began where wWw left off, with even more talent. Sean dominated this federation and won their United title, before he made what may have been the biggest mistake of his career. Feeling screwed over after the last Pay-per-view, Sean confronted the owner of the company and ended up punching him in the face. Sean's career seemed to be over. Then, out of nowhere, a contract offer came from FSW. They were giving Sean his last chance, and he definitely wanted to make the most of it. Finisher Rivernater - Usually taking the opponent down by the arm, the opponent is on the mat on their stomach, while Rivers is lying to the side of the opponent's body. After scissoring the arm of his opponent closest to him with his legs, Sean reaches around his opponent's head and pulls his arm across the opponent's face, locking his hands together and pulls back, applying pressure to the opponents's neck. This move can also be described as a Candian Crossface.
