Next Biography Height 6'3" Weight 263lbs Hometown Reno, NV Date of Birth March 15th, 1980 Alignment Neutral Entrance Music When Worlds Collide - Powerman 5000 Personality He doesn't really see himself as a wrestler. Not the entertainment part, at least. He sees so deep, he realises that his life, his way of doing things, is not set in stone, so he does everything to preserve the life he loves. When speaking, he usually just voices his thoughts, tells us what's running through his mind. He never seems to be sure about anything, not even himself. He is in no way cocky. He knows his limitations, and knows that everyone else has their own. He knows its all the day, the time, the place, that determines this. History A story you've all heard, right? Some kid grows up, rough on the streets, and he's a thug for life, right? Sure, this story carries with a lot of men. And I'm not questioning the credibility of these stories, nor am I saying that this man is much different. He is set a bit apart from the rest though...Here's how it went down for him: How well do you know your parents? How much time do you spend? Dya ever go to the game with your dad? Bake something with mom? Even hang around the mall with your bros? Parents are cool, and ALMOST essential to life. But, of course, scuffles happen. Ever yelled "I never want to see you again!"? Easy to say when you know you will. 1980...A monumental year...But, for some, it holds a whole different meaning...on April 15th of this year, Ryan Banks was born. August 27th, his mother and father were shot dead. Or so it seemed. Ryan ran away from an orphanage after eight years there, at that age: Eight. He made a home out of an old abandoned building, living in the rafters. Not much in this kid's life, huh? Probably a helluva bitcher? Well, look again. He lived up to the saying that Ignorance is Bliss. He never made it to school. He knew nothing of fault, of how to push his anger into hate. He knew not even of his anger. But, he needed something in his life, and caught onto something all too many of us get addicted to...TV. Ever passed by an appliance store, and seen the TV's blaring through the windows? Well, that was this boy's savior. Rarely at first, then, with every day, increasing in occurence, he would pass by a certain "Bob's Appliance Outlet", where he could watch the beautiful pictures flash by, entertaining him for hours on end. Slowly, through this, he learned to speak, to understand, and to percieve the world around him. Now, another thing must be realised at this time. This child has NO money whatsoever. Left at a few months old, he never had money, never clothing. He had only what he could confiscate from trash barrels and overcrowded stores in the mall. The time intervals between new clothing was such that often, his clothes were tattered and disgusting, making him very unattractive to passerby. None had pity on him, and he was the center of lots of harassment from teenagers. This, became a serious problem for him. But, TV, had given him a solution. Fight. After seeing a few Karate exhibitions on TV, he saw fighting as a defence, an end to the abuse. He wandered into a YMCA one day, and from then on, until he was 17, trained day after day and eventually was chosen to participate in such events as a YMCA Tae-Kwon-Do open expo, and a sparring workshop. Though his people skills were terrible, he was beginning to know his craft quite well. When one day approached by a local wrestling promoter, and seeing a tape of this sport, he was hooked. After four years on the independant circut, and at the age of 20, he wandered into EPW, where he made his living for about 1 year. At one point though, after having the private tutor, the teaching sessions, and all that, he came to a realisation. He knew the capital of Bolivia, he knew how many cups in a gallon, yet, he knew nothing about himself. Driven almost insand by his deep thoughts, he fled EPW, and now, but one year later, has collected himself. No, he still knows nothing of himself, but he is now in good enough condition to find out. And he plans to find out, while doing, what he loves.
