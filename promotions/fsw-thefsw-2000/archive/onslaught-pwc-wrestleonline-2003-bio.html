Onslaught Biography Biography At Cornell Medical Center on the day October 14, 1976, Onslaught was born Darren Alexander Smith would be born. Darren had a very loving family. Although they were never rich, the Smith household lived around the upper middle class within the Riverdale area of the Bronx. His mother, Sheryl worked a nurse and attending medical school to fulfill her newly found urge to become a Psychiatrist and his father, Marcus was a Senior Art Director for an Advertising company. He'd later take a job as Senior Art Director for the video game company, Acclaim. Almost a year later after his birth on September 18, 1977, his mother, Sheryl gave birth to another baby boy, Darren's little brother, Trevor. By the time Trevor was 3, the two brothers grew inseperable. They enjoyed usual things kids their age liked: going to the park, watching Sesame Street, and other various cartoons. However nothing would come close to the excitement they felt while watching pro wrestling. This would be the year Darren start in Kindergarten. On his first day, not knowing what to do really, he took a empty seat in the corner. He almost fell off his chair, as a young girl walked into the room. She was beautiful. Darren became rather nervous as she walked towards his direction. He tried to fake that he was asleep, but she shook his shoulder, hoping to wake him. Darren remained motionless. Tired of standing, she decided to take a seat to him and kick at his foot for a total of a near two munutes. Against his better judgement, he began to fake waking up, acting groggy as if he didn't know where he was. She immediately asked him his name. 'Darren... Darren Smith' he responded. Following it up with 'And you?' she introduced herself. Her name was Alyssa Summers. As they sat and talked, Alyssa and Darren became rather good friends. Granted he was only four years old and didn't know much of anything, but he was aware of the relationship that existed between the male and female gender. Later in the school year on Valentine's Day, Darren would muster up his courage and ask Alyssa to be his girlfriend. His worries would be wooshed away when she delightfully accepted. For the young couple's first date, Darren invited her to his mother's graduation from medical school. In spite of putting up with silly antics such as 'Darren and Alyssa, kissing in the tree..' from Trevor and his father's humming of 'Unforgettable' which none of the three kids knew, Darren was still able to get a kiss on the cheek. The next year when Trevor started Kindergarten, Darren was spending most of his time and attention on Alyssa. In the meantime, a first grader who was the same size as Trevor, but slightly stronger to due to the age difference was bullying him around. Within the week, Darren became aware of this. Darren approached the bully and a fight ensued. The troublemaker bothering Trevor was beaten so severe, the boy's parents had their son transferred out of the school. Darren was punished for this by his parents of course, but realizing Trevor needed to defend himself and Darren needed to control his temper their father, Marcus had the two enrolled in Jeet Kune Do classes. During the sparring sessions in Jeet Kune Do, he kept getting points taken off from excessive force. Annoyed with this behavior, the instructor had informed Darren that would be sparring against him. Due to his larger size, the instructor felt he would take on the youngster while on his knees. Darren lunged at him with a Jump Spinning Back Kick, but the instructor caught his leg and applied a hold to Darren's neck with just his finger disabling any attempt an assault. Point to the instructor. Darren then attempted a punch, but the instructor used the same technique. After class, the instructor pulled him aside. He told his student that 'such misguided rage will simply hinder your potential'. Darren didn't know what hinder meant, but he knew it wasn't something positive. Regardless, Darren realized his instructor's words had truth behind them. The instructor eventually put Darren's anger in check and after a few sessions, he taught Darren the hold he did to his neck. By the time, Darren reached High School, he and Trevor had reached brown belt. Darren had taken up a liking for the sport of Football and decided to sign up for Freshman Football Team at his school, Cardinal Spellman. Darren gracefully captured the spot as the team's starting Halfback. He broke and set the school's record by running an amazing 4.2 on the Forty-Yard Dash! The team easily ram sacked all opposing competition and eventually winning the Catholic Archdiocese, City, State, and National Championships. Next year, Darren's younger brother, Trevor tried out and was accepted on the Freshman team as Starting Wide Receiver as Darren moved on to the Junior Varsity team. Alyssa, in an attempt to be around Darren more often, tried on for the Cheerleading team and made it as Captain. Both lead their teams to Catholic Archdiocese, City, State, and National Championships. In Darren's senior year, as far as the competition in football league were concerned, the worst thing possible had happened... Darren and Trevor were BOTH on the Varsity Team! To say that they annihilated the competition, would be a massive understatement. Following every touchdown, the brothers would perform slam dunks as they had done in the previous years, but they were taunting the opposition as they did them, noting how far away the defenders were. Needless to say the opposing coaches, players, and their parents weren't pleased. This later led to seventeen protests. It was because of this, spiking the ball and any touchdown celebrations of any form were banned two years later. That year the Spellman Falcons won the Catholic Archdiocese, City, State, and National Championships! At the Homecoming Game, Darren and Alyssa were named respectively, Homecoming King and Queen. Darren and Alyssa eventually graduated and were accepted to UConn on scholarships with their impressing the Dean SAT scores composed of 1560 and 1557, respectively. The next year Trevor was accepted as well also on a scholarship with a score of 1492. During college however, the brothers didn't play football to their father's disappointment. Too many things were happening around them, mainly parties. As you can probably figure out by now, Trevor was big with the women. Despite Trevor's busy schedule, the brothers were still studying Jeet Kune Do, but refused to take the test for Black Belt in order for their hands to remain unregistered as lethal weapons. Trevor was constantly stealing someone's girlfriend. Darren on the other wanted to be able to protect himself and Alyssa and perhaps somwhere down the line... a family. In his junior year, Darren had finally selected his future profession - Pro Wrestling. Unfortunately, this decision didn't hold too well with his parents especially Marcus. He'd hoped Darren would take a career in Football. They feared him being forced into an early retirement the increasing extreme and hardcore trends. Darren being trained by an indivudual who was opening this training facility as a result of his injury didn't help his arguement in the slightest. Not only was it hard to crack into the business, but cracking into the business and becoming the next Bret Hart was alot harder as proved by fWo Survivor and WWF Tough Enough. On top of that, the African American population in Pro Wrestling at that point was a extremely short list that included Vincent, Stevie Ray, Zeus, Ernie Ladd, Tony Atlas, D-Lo Brown, Kama, Ahmed Johnson, Ron Simmons, and Booker T. Regardless, he was determined to make it. During the time period of 1982 to 1995, the wrestling industry watched the growth a great superstar in Epic Wrestling known as Wayne Carter. Wayne was one of the first individuals to ever hold a World Championship five times. Carter eventually became a legend in his own time, but his career was unfortunately cut short due to a knee injury he ironically suffered in a match of his own creation. After his injury he decided on opening a wrestling academy to teach youngsters who so longed to break into the business he loved. Wayne would name the academy after himself, The Carter Academy. Darren and Alyssa shortly enrolled. In order to strengthen his students, Carter would often place them in submission holds after gagging their mouth with a hankerchief, making it impossible to tap out. Needless to say, TCA didn't have many graduates. Someone making it past a week was a rare occasion. Much to the dismay of their parents, Trevor attended in his junior year as well. Reading a Marvel Comic spectacular mini series, Darren had found his name... Onslaught! With the exception of the character's evil side, Onslaught symbolized everything he wanted to be as a wrestler: fast, strong, and intimidating. Trevor decided to pick up an issue himself was amazed by a character named Post. Trevor took the name for himself as an in ring name. Alyssa however, competed using her real name. Darren, Alyssa, and Trevor eventually graduated from UConn with exceptional grades. Taking the next step in their training, the three then began touring with Wayne and The Carter Academy. Darren made some friends at TCA, who had also spawned names from the Onslaught Comic Saga. They took the names HaVoK and Holocaust. Onslaught and Post were never ever able to beat each other, but with Trevor's tendency to constantly pose during matches, whereas Darren would leave such things out of the ring wouldn't favor to well with Wayne. This slight difference would lead to Darren graduating earlier. Alyssa, Trevor, HaVoK, and Holocaust all graduated soon after. In late 1999, Onslaught along with HaVoK, Post, Holocaust, and Alyssa Summers were recruited up by the SCW Owner Michael Mason and were placed in a stable led by Onslaught called the nWo Gold and Black. Mr. Mason paid close attention to graduates of The Carter Academy because to graduate proved you were up there with the best. Out of respect of his mentor, Wayne Carter, Onslaught used the finisher his mentor used, Courage Required. Despite being signed to a lengthy contract, Onslaught and the other nWo members were also wrestling in other leagues at the time as well. On one fateful Sunday, Darren's relationship with his parents would draw one step closer towards reconilation. He was in the midst of a serious feud with Blackula, leader of the rival stable known as the Hellions. The two feud would reach a final conclusion on Pay Per View in what would was Onslaught's first World Heavyweight Tttle shot. His mother, Sheryl had attended the show, sitting in the front row. Countering Blackula's finisher - a version of the uranage suplex, Onslaught picked up the win. Since that match, his mother slowly began to embrace and support his decision. However, he and father still couldn't see eye to eye on the subject. Later in his career, Onslaught won his second World Championship in the SCW and his 6th overall World Championship. Alyssa reached success as well, becoming the SCW's first Women's Champion! She remains the only woman to stay undefeated in SCW or Pro Wrestling for that matter and win Women's Championship, twice in one federation. After winning the SCW World Championship for a second time, Onslaught felt he was starting lived up to his mentor's aspirations as well his own, by dominating the federation in every possible way. Onslaught had the federation's largest and unmatched fan base with the nWo Gold and Black sleeveless and Onslaught 'Courage Required' t-shirts selling tremendously well. Upon this success, commentators and interviewers began refer to him as 'The Epitome of Greatness'. Soon after, Onslaught made SCW History upon being the only man to capture the SCW World Title for a 3rd Time! The nWo Gold and Black were the hottest thing in Wrestling! This an unmatched, unbelievable, and outstanding group had captured twenty plus championships in a period from September to February! The stable even successfully took over the EAWF and SCW/IWF, declaring it their own. During their unremarkable reign during the Suicidal Championship Wrestling, problems with staff ensued and the federation was shut down in Mid-December. Between January and early March, Onslaught joined numerous failed federations while gaining titles along the way. Onslaught stumbled across XCW. He joined the federation with Post to see if the place would be good for nWo Gold and Black led by Onslaught to compete in. In his first match, he defeated Angel. After winning the Tag Team Titles with Post, by defeating Y2Jay and Icon, they were stripped of them few days later. After a week Onslaught was placed in a comatose angle for unknown reasons. He soon become weary of this and resigned. Post decided to stay in the federation and with WCW pushing for money to use the nWo name due to the group's popularity, Onslaught disbanded the nWo Gold and Black. Onslaught would start his career in the IWO Leagues after being offered a contract from then IML2 President now IWO President, Thomas Ford to begin competing in the IML2. President Ford would learn of the young superstar from receiving various recommendations by likes of IML2 and IWO Competitor, Silverchair, IWO Competitor, Former IML2 Vice President and Former IML President, Gunnar Smith, IML2 and IWO Competitor, Phyre, and IML2 Competitor, JJ Nuclear. Onslaught wasn't the type to join some federation's Minor League, but the IML2 was different. They just may have had a better roster than that of the actual IWO with a roster compromising of such names as Shawn Arrows, Mike Extreme, Joey Malone, Daniel Philips, Silverchair, Kyle Kash, LiGiL, Phyre, Lance McNally, Carlos Lopez, Jack Night, Enifirno, Mac D., and Kurt Zoom. After entering the IML2, Onslaught quickly became one of the Top 10 Current Wrestlers in the IML2 during his short time there. His debut match would have him squaring off against the No. 1 Contender to the World Championship, Kyle Kash. Although Onslaught would lose the match, he proved he would be a force to be reckoned with by withstanding a never before seen or repeated 8 Too Late Facebusters (Pedigree) before he would finally go down for the 3 Count. Impressing the IML2 'Suits', in his second match, Onslaught would take on the IML2 World Champion 'Simply Perfect' Shawn Arrows on Monday Night Melee, in what was a Non-World Title Match, Onslaught had handed Arrows his first defeat in 18 Months! IWO President John Maples closed the IML2 and IML3 in an effort to improve the IWO and IML and to make them more elite. Onslaught among with few other IML2 and IML3 Wrestlers was sent to the IML. However IML2 President Thomas Ford wasn't the only one who made this decision as did Shawn Arrows who had persuaded John to send Onslaught to the IML instead of the IWO as Arrows himself went to the IWO. Onslaught entered the IML with a renewed rage. Onslaught made his presence widely known by defeating the likes of former IML2 Standouts, Geoff Paul and Mac D. and former Television Champions, Azrael Damodread or as Onslaught would call him, Azrael Damobread and Adam Wars. It didn't take long for Onslaught to capture his first Championship in the IWO Leagues, but Onslaught didn't win gold. The championship would come in the form of Platinum as Onslaught became the second and Last Internet Wrestling Organization Minor League Platinum Champion! Onslaught won the match in his homestate, the same place he as yet to lose a match, Madison Square Garden by defeating another Undefeated Man in Michael Nash. This became Nash's first and last title defense when Onslaught tossed him off a 50 Foot Scaffold. Nonetheless, to add insult to injury the losing stipulation would Onslaught then cover him in tar and feather him like a chicken! Onslaught would defend the championship against anyone foolish enough to face him, including Dean Fiore and The Reaper (Who took off his cheap booster boots and now goes by the name Sabasatian Crow). Destined for a World Championship, Onslaught's arrogance surely won him fans, but pissed off the Executive Board in the process. Due to this, he was suspended for a month. Without The Epitome of Greatness, on the cards, the IML went downhill and had it's first card on October 23th, 2000 since Onslaught was suspended which was September 30th, 2000! They eventually closed. However in what the the IML Executive Board saw as Ulimited Arrogance, the IWO saw as Unlimited Potental. After finishing his month of suspension, just as soon as he would be reinstated back into the IML, he was promoted to the Internet Wrestling Organization itself! Onslaught would debut in the IWO at their biggest pay per view of the year, Ice Age 3 and go on to defeat former US Champion, Ash Robinson in the first ever Vertical Limit Match. Onslaught would establish himself that night as a force to be reckoned with. He continued to make empressive showings even one night on the January 5th edition of Hostile Takeover, Onslaught defeated Donnie Daze, Ash Robinson, and then United States Champion, Billy Ray in a Three On One Handicap Match! During Onslaught's stint in the IML, he and Shawn had patched their IML2 differences. As their friendship grew, the two realized they'd posses a great potential as a tag team. The IWO Executive Board allowed the two to teamed up against Former IML World Tag Team Champions, IWO Intercontinental, and World Tag Team Champions, the Suicide Kings. The crowd couldn't believe it, not only did Onslaught and Arrows win they practically dominated. Despite his quick success, Onslaught grew weary of the political and backstage *censored* that happened in the IWO. Onslaught handed in his resignation to the federation. Once again, Onslaught found himself in a search for a new federation. Except this federation, wasn't so new. While Onslaught was competing in the IML, he was working at a upstart federation called Five Star Wrestling. FSW quickly grew to respected status in the wrestling community. However, Onslaught parted ways with the company due to personal issues with some of the roster. He was contacted by Co-Owner Painkiller and Vice President Ryan Lockheart, that they were starting over and with more new faces than old among those accepted, Onslaught accepted their offer. His impact is grew more and more deeper with each growing day. At Five Star Wrestling first Pay Per View back, Love Hurts, Onslaught made quick work of 'Five Star' Mike Marvelous and Former FSW World Champion, Epitome of Evil to become FSW's First Standard Bearer, Flagship, and World Heavyweight Champion of it's New Era. That night, Onslaught won his 10th World Heavyweight Championship and proposed to his longtime girlfriend, Alyssa Summers. The two hugged as she said yes. Since Love Hurts, Onslaught mowed over anyone his path. From #1 Contender to his World Champioship, Lucky McKnight to Intercontinental Champion, Johnny Mayhem. Others included FSW's First World Champion, Steve Johnson, WWR, IML, and IWO Superstar, Ian Kain, and Sadistic Madman, Ironfist. He also became the first person to ever defeat Epitome of Evil via Tap Out. The Owner, Painkiller kept sticking his nose in the champ's business and Onslaught quickly to exception to it. The two again ended up at odds with one other, with Onslaught constantly embrassing him on his own shows to delight of the crowds. Realizing he bit off more than he could chew, Painkiller found out just couldn't take it and decided to release him. During his time off from the business, Darren finally decided to get married to Alyssa. The wedding was held in St. Patrick's Cathedral. As would be expected, numerous people from the wrestling industry attending the wedding. Faces included names such as then Tag Team Partner Jason Winters, future Tag Partner Shawn Arrows, IWO Hall of Famer Phelen Kell, former jOlt Champion Brandon Blade, Fellow TCA Graduate Lance Titan, IWO Grand Slam Winner High Flyer, former HWF World Champion Lance Sterling, and BWM Host Claude Mitchell. Within a few months, Onslaught received a hefty contract to join oWa. As luck would have it, the blasted company closed before his first match. Five Star Wrestling began to take a turn for the worse. He was given numerous offers (four to be exact) to return back to the crumbling company, but rejected every offer. In Onslaught's view.. 'They had their chance and decided to end it. Now they come crawling back when the times are hard? Too bad'. Onslaught rejected numerous offers everything from wrestling organizations like IWO, GPW, WWR, HYPE, and IOW to fighting groups such as Eclipse and The Fighting Zone. He never quite gave any specific reasons. For months the industry had speculated Onslaught had called it quits. Other suspected that 'The Epitome of Greatness' hadn't taken the offers because he felt wasn't as he good as he used to be, that with the new wave of talent, he just couldn't cut it in the big leagues anymore. In November, Onslaught was contacted by a man named William [name withheld]. William revealed that he was reopening the company. his predecessor competed in, Epic Wrestling with the fianicial support of his late uncle, former Epic Wrestling World Champion, Trent [name withheld] and support by the living members of the company's former Executive Board. [name withheld] asked if he could join the federation, as part of the tag team division. Before hanging up, Onslaught just replied stating 'If I accept your offer, you'll be among the first to know.' On February 24, 2002, Epic Wrestling Pay Per View, titled Vengeful Hearts proved the speculation wrong. Onslaught had come back to professional wrestling along with former rival and close friend, Shawn Arrows. Concealing their identities to the crowd, the duo helped saved Ben Archer from another brutal beating from MALICE (Seth Anson, Steve Johnson, and Anubis). The next night on Ground Zero, the two revealed themselves to the crowd and formed a tag team known as Malevolent Youth (The same name Onslaught and Jason Winters once used). The dream team is no longer a dream. For MALICE, it's a officially a nightmare.
