Jason O'Neil Biography Real Name Jason O'Neil Nicknames Jace, J.O., The Scarecrow Hometown Cleveland, Ohio Birthday 11 Feb 1977 Height 6'2 Weight 248 Biography I’ve seen it all. Blood. Sweat. Tears. Death. Pain. Smiles. Doesn’t matter. I’m truly a scarecrow. Over and over the images dance in my mind. And yet, I feel nothing. No remorse. No sympathy. No empathy. I’ve felt more pain than most men, yet, I’ve never shed a tear. I’ve seen people die before my eyes. I’ve seen shattered necks, broken legs, and the way the face contorts in excruciating pain. And I feel nothing. I've lost everything. My wife. My daughter. My parents. My brother. My self respect. And still, I feel nothing. Pain, is all I know, and I welcome it. I crave it. I need it. Without pain, life is not what it should be. Joy is fleeting; anger is true. It's pure in it's feeling. There is no gray area. Anger is what drives me to be who I am. Retribution is coming to those who nearly destroyed me. Kimbusa, Caldera, and the others that let this happen to me, you will be entering your own personal nightmare, you will feel pain, and suffering, unequalled in the history of humanity. It makes me the scarecrow. And that makes me whole. Now can you handle...that? Other Feds GCW Other Fed Awards 3x Tag Champion.
