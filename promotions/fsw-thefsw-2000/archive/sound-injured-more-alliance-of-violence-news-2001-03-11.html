|Sound Injured + More Alliance of Violence news. |
IP: [detail withheld]

As you all saw on Maximum Velocity, Sound was subject to a brutal beating by Static and Midnight, otherwise knows as Nightmare Inc. Sound was taken to a local medical facility to undergo treatment and he is expected to be out of action for two to three weeks. So far Nightmare Inc. seems to be doing what Painkiller told them to do and that's take out the Alliance of Violence. Nightmare Inc. also seems to be growing in number each and every card. So far Static and Midnight have returned but expect more members to join them.

But the Alliance of Violence look set to fight back to prove just who is the most evil stable in the FSW. In fact, the two remaining members of the AOV: Johnny Mayhem and P.S.Mad are rumoured to have something big in store for Nightmare Inc. but are keeping a tight lip about what it is exactly.

On Glory this week, P.S.Mad will be facing Midnight in a Extreme match. He had the following to say about Nightmare Inc. and about his match:-

"Nightmare Inc, you think that your pretty smart taking Sound out like that don't you? Well it was another act of cowardice on your behalf and I would expect nothing less from you. But on Glory this week, Midnight shall feel my wrath and the wrath of the Alliance of Violence. You can't get rid of us that easily for you see, 'IT' is coming and there is no way that you will be able to stop it."

P.S.Mad will also be interviewed by Mark Jones in a special one on one interview which shall air some time before Glory.

Also on Glory, Johnny Mayhem has to fight in one of the toughest fight in his career. Johnny Mayhem has had a string of impressive victories as of late but can he beat our FSW World Champion Onslaught? Johnny Mayhem has pinned Onslaught twice in the past but doing so in a one on one match may prove to be a slightly more difficult. Though the match is a non title match a victory by Johnny Mayhem would sky rocket his career here in the FSW.
