Foreign Disaster, 27 May 2001 (part 1)

27 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 27, 2001 Arena: Hamburg Arena - Hamburg, Germany

The show opens showing FSW announcers Lynn Lane, Sam Collins and Willy Wilson standing in immigration inspection line at the International Airport in Hamburg, Germany. A small graphic in the lower right hand corner of the screen reads "Recorded Earlier."
A mass of people is surging towards the entrance officials trying to get through the entrance process as quickly and painlessly as possible.
Sam Collins: Jesus, this line is moving slowly. Lynn Lane: There's a line here? Just looks a pushing mob of people to me. Willy Wilson: [huffing] You'd think "these people" would have a good concept of how to make a line, after all those years of waiting in line for bread.
Sam Collins: That's Russia, you jackass. After a few moments, the trio make it to the entrance officer. The entrance officer is sitting behind a simple counter, wearing the green uniform and emblems of the German Army. Beyond the officer, on the other side of the entrance gate, stand two round faced, unattractive women and an older, stern faced man, also wearing army uniforms.
Lynn Lane is the first one in line. Entrance Officer: Passport. Lynn Lane smiles and hands over his passport. Entrance Officer: What is the purpose of your stay in German, Mr. Lane? Lynn Lane: Business. Entrance Officer: What kind of business? Lynn Lane: Entertainment. The Entrance Officer types some words into a computer. After a few moments hands Lynn back his passport without even looking at him and waves him through.
Lynn passes by the watchful eye of the two, round faced women and stern looking older man, then stops and waits for his colleagues.
Sam Collins is the next to approach the Entrance Officer. Entrance Officer: Passport. Sam hands over his passport. Entrance Officer: You also here for entertainment business, Mr. Collins? Sam nods. The Entrance Officer types into his computer and after a few moments hands Sam back his passport and waves him through.
The camera focuses back on Willy Wilson, who is noticeably sweating. The Entrance Officer beckons him to approach. Willy obliges.
Entrance Officer: Passport. Willy hands over his passport and begins to look up at the ceiling of the airport nervously.
Entrance Officer: Purpose of your visit, Mr. Wilson? Willy Wilson: Business. Like my two friends over there. Sam Collins: Uh, we are not your friends. Lynn elbows Sam. The Entrance Officer begins typing at his computer terminal. The camera focuses in on Willy's sweaty face, as he uses a handkerchief to dab himself off. The shot then shifts back to the Entrance Officer who seems to be waiting for information on his screen. After a moment, the Entrance Officer's eyes narrow, and he types some more keys. The camera shifts back to the eyes of Willy Wilson, which are getting glassy. Then, the camera shifts over to Lynn and Sam, who are looking at each other in confusion.
Finally the Entrance Officer looks back at the stern faced man. Entrance Officer: Jugen, schnell shnell! The older man approaches the Entrance Officer's computer terminal. The Entrance Officer points to the screen, which is facing away from the camera so we can not see it contents. The two German officials whisper back and forth rapidly to each other, and their eyes keep darting to and from Willy Wilson. Sam is shown in the background craning his neck to see what is on
the computer screen, to no avail. Finally the older man speaks. Loyal Communist Party Member Jugen [pronounced u:gin]: [thick German accent, stilted tones] I am Officer Jugen. Mista Vilson, I am sehr sorry, but sehr is a pwoblem with your entrance visa. You may not enter.
Willy Wilson: WHAT?! Why not? Jugen: Your visa was issued by our consulate in the US in ewwor. You may not pass. Willy Wilson: Why not?! Jugen: You are a known perpetwator of activities considered illegal in Germany. It is our powericy to not arrow your kind entrance.
Willy Wilson: My kind? What is THAT supposed to mean? Sam Collins [whispering to Lynn]: Maybe Germany isn't so bad after all. Lynn Lane: [angrily, whispering] Would you shut up all ready! Jugen: Prease Mista Vilson, do not make this difficult. You may not pass. Willy Wilson: [miffed, crossing his arms] Hmmph! I will pass! I want to see your manager! Jugen: I am the manager. Willy Wilson: I want to see the director of airport security! Jugen: You are looking at him. Willy Wilson: [panicking] I am an American citizen! You can not refuse me entrance! I demand to see my congressman!! I will not be oppressed!! Jugen looks back over his shoulder at the two round faced women and nods. The two women, who may or may not be twins, but either way, we know the parents that spawned each of them were not blessed with good looks, nod back then spring into action.
Lynn Lane: Uh oh! The two round faced women move towards Willy with blinding speed. One goes high, while the other goes low. Willy tosses his carry on bag at one of the women to slow her progress, but she bats it aside with her forearm, sending the bag to the airport floor causing its contents to be spilled onto the floor, revealing a tube of KW jelly, a strand of beads and a picture of Boca del Inferno, with the lower half of it digitally blurred.
The two women wrestle Willy to the ground and easily subdue the portly, openly homosexual announcer.
Willy Wilson: I demand to see my congressman!! I am an American!! The two round faced women then drag Willy off into a side room and the door slams shut behind them with a thundering metallic boom.
Jugen turns and looks to the floor, at the spilled contents of Willy's bag. His eyes show signs of disgust, as he waves over an airport janitor, and motions her to take the stuff away.
Jugen then turns to the horrified duo of Lynn Lane and Sam Collins. Slowly but surely, a facial feature probably completely unaccustomed from Jugen wrenches its way to the old man's face. A smile.
Jugen: [insincerely, to Lynn and Sam] Welcome to sie People's Republic of Germany. Enjoy your stay.
The shot focuses in on Jugen's wicked looking smile as the scene fades to black. The opening promo begins with a reel of black and white footage, revealing certain disasters throughout foreign regions in the depths of history. Disasters such as World War 1 and 2, the Hiroshima bombing, Bangladesh flooding, the Krakatau volcanoes, Californian earthquakes, and the Chernobyl nuclear disaster are shown....
"Time's running out" by Circus begins to play loudly in the background, as the scene fades to black.then in bold white lettering.
In Their Quest For. Fame. Money. Glory. GOLD. FOUR MEN SHALL JOURNEY. BEYOND THE LOOKING GLASS. THEIR JOURNEY SHALL TAKE THEM. DEEP INTO THE HEART OF. FOREIGN DISASTER. THREE FORMER FSW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONS. EPITOME OF EVIL. TOMMY TAKER. BIG DAVEYLICIOUS. MUST DEFEAT THE CURRENT FSW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION. JOHNNY MAYHEM. TONIGHT. THE MIGHTY WILL FALL. BLOOD WILL BE SPILLED. THE SURVIVOR SHALL REIGN. PREPARE YOURSELF. FOREIGN DISASTERS!!!!!! The show opening for Foreign Disasters fades in to show the skyline of Hamburg faded with FSW wrestlers in action and a waving German flag as "Lawyers, Guns and Money" from Warren Xevon acts as the audio backdrop. As the opening rolls, so do the wrestlers highlighted within. Wrestlers featured are P.S.Mad, Token Weed, Jak Nemesis, Shawn Mosely, Jack Miller,
Jake Keeton, Thrash, and the FSW World Heavyweight Champion Johnny Mayhem, as the scene fades to black whilst "Lawyers, Guns and Money" comes to an end.
The scene shifts to the inside of a jam:packed Hamburg Arena, in Hamburg, Germany as massive pyros shoot from the roof top, ring posts and entryway with little or no regard for the safety of the audience. The crowd is really alive and popping as "Party Up" from DMX hits the PA system. The camera begins to scan the crowd where hundreds of fans are holding up signs, which are completely unintelligible to the average FSW fan. The camera stops for a moment to focus on three fans who have their faces painted red. One is banging on a drum while another is blowing into a shrill sounding horn. The third red faced fan is holding up a poster showing a roasted turkey, with a cut out of Painkiller's head stuck to the front end of it. Finally, the scene shifts to a good looking German girl holding up a sign in English which reads "I would kill my mother to get a green card, so having sex with a fat slob of a foreigner is no problem. Call me! [detail withheld]."
The scene then shifts to the announce table where the Five Star Wrestling announce team, Lynn Lane and Sam Collin wait to call the action.

Lynn Lane: Well, I'm gonna have to go with the current Champ, Johnny Mayhem, this man has done so much in the last four months, I just don't think anything will stop him tonight. But anyway folks, we have a hell of a lot more matches signed up for tonight's card, and they are just as awesome!
Sam Collins: That's right. And it's now time for our first match of the evening. It will be Red Rock against Tyler Cash in a steel cage match.
