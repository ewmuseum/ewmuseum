Jack Brooze Biography Real Name Jack Brusatelli Nicknames Jack Blue and Black, Mr. Anti-Security Hometown Brooklyn, NY Birthday June 29th, 1974 Height 6'5' Weight 265 Fed Titles FSW Rising Star Title (1); FSW World Tag Title (1); FSW Extreme Title (1) Biography Born in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn, New York, 1974, to father James Brusatelli, and mother Maria. Jack's father worked on the docks, and his mother stayed home taking care of himself and his two younger siblings. Jack was part of a very tightly connected family, which was torn apart by the hands of crime. His father was arrested under the charges of first degree murder when Jack was 14. Although never proven innocent, Jack knew that his father had committed no crime. A gang of crooks with ties to the mafia had reason for Joseph's framing. Unknown to Jack or the rest of his family, his father had a past with the mafia. He changed his name at a young age, and moved from Chicago to Brooklyn to start a new life, but the mafia tracked him down, and took him out. As Jack was getting older, around 16 or so, his mother became fatally ill, contracted a serious case of pneumonia and died. Jack was left alone to take care of his two younger siblings. His father was still in jail, serving his life sentence. Jack was unable to raise both of his siblings alone, and had them set up to live in a foster home, where he was also taken. Within a week, he ran away from his foster home, with no place to go, but to live in the streets. At the age of 18 he got into gang trouble. A gang that went by the name Blitzen, meaning Thunder, a German gang, with quite a bad reputation. Jack had no place to go, and the gang became his family. A rival gang and the Blitzen had met up for, I guess you could say a little 'business'. The rival gang was actually a group of undercover cops working for the NYPD, and they busted everyone of the Blitzen gang members, except for Brooze, as they had began to call him. He ran from the scene, quick as lightning, and no one had caught him, and nearly all trace of him had disappeared. With no place to go, and a hunger to fight, Brooze looked elsewhere to please his hunger for fighting. As he was walking down an alley, someone dressed in black tried to pick a fight with him. Brooze obliterated the man, and continued on walking. Out from a corner a man in a suit approached Brooze with an offer. An offer to fight for sport. An offer, he couldn't refuse. It must be performed when the opponent is on the move. Jack may be standing or may run at the opponent for a more effective execution.
