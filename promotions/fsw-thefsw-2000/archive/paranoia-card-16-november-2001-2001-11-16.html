Paranoia card, 16 November 2001

16 November 2001

What this is The fed's own preview page for a Paranoia show (Georgia Dome, Atlanta): the announced matches with a paragraph on each, as the page stood in its Wayback capture of 6 January 2002.
DATE: 11.16.2001
LOCATION: Georgia Dome - Atlanta, GA


Tommy Taker vs. Blitz

Standard Match

During the main event last Friday on Paranoia all hell broke loose. The winning team ended up being Tommy Taker and Blitz. Now this week they will be facing each other. Blitz is some what of a newcomer to the main event scene, eventhough he is the World champ. Tommy has been in this situation many times before and is a former World champion himself. What kind of twists and surprises will happen during this match? Tune in to find out.

Seaweed and Punisher 2000 vs. Chris Fear and Jonny Trip

Standard Match

Last week Seaweed made his FSW debut by attacking both Chris Fear and Jonny Trip in their matches. This week Fear and Trip, the two members who seem to be left of that once five man alliance, will be teaming togethor to take on Seaweed and Punisher 2000. Seaweed has hatred towards both Fear and Trip and Punisher 2000 will try and get some retrobution against Trip from Futile Resistance.

Human vs. Desmond Ellis and ???

Standard - Tag Team Titles

Last week on Paranoia Human and Desmond got into a little argument. And at the end of it Desmond found himself with a chance for tag team glory. He will be facing Human in a handicap match for the tag team titles. The one question is, who will be Desmond's partner and what kind of impact will he make on the FSW.

Tommy Kane vs. Carl Cash

Standard -Rising Star Title

After lots of confusion during the opening bout of Paranoia last week Tommy Kane came away the new Rising Star champion after defeating P.S. Mad. This week he getes his first title defense against promising superstar, Carl Cash. Last week Carl talked about a big plan for this Friday with his midget friends. Does it involve this match? And what kind of impact will it have on the FSW? Tune in to find out.
