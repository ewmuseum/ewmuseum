|The Truth|
DATE: 3/16/01
Hello fans and welcome to the third instalment of The Truth with [name withheld]. Lately I have been looking around at real life jobs and saw that most have a union. Is it time e-wrestlers create a union and a set criteria for what all presidents must do? Lets think about this.

Now, if there was to become an E-Wrestling Union, how would we(the wrestlers) enforce these rules that we set. Lets say we found an e-fed and a president who were not following the set terms created by a group of e-wrestlers. Would we just let it go, or we would we be able to find a way to enforce these rules. Well, the easiest way to enforce the rules that we state is by refusing to roleplay. Now in real life, if the employer(president) fired us, then we could sue. Now in e-wrestling if we had a union, there would be no way to sue. Since we are not paid it is pointless. So what would we do, well a way we could get back at the owner is by reporting it to a major site that we could create to show people that they do not respect their wrestlers.

But who, who would be willing to start up a dot com site most likely called EWunion.com? Well let us look around. The owner of EWplanet also owns EWzine and I have heard a few others. Would he be willing to do it. Or would it have to be a subsite of another site like Steelramp or FWLnet? These are questions we can not answer unless we ask. What person would want to help us get back at other E-Fed presidents, and how would they know this is a for real deal? The site would re-quire a large staff that would have to check out every report of a bad president, then they would have to post it on a board. Then when a president finally gave in, they would have to delete the site from the listing. Then they would also need people checking to see if the president is lying or telling the truth. This is an idea that I think would be good in the near future.

But is this idea of an E-Wrestling union possible? 100 years ago men would not let women work because they thought they couldn't and that they belonged in the kitchen. Now women are working full time while some men stay home and watch the kids. Over the years times have changed and we will always have to adjust to the attitude of the era. If the possibility of an E-Wrestling union may not be feasible now, but hopefully in the near future, it will become embraced and people will be wanting to give the money to support an EWunion.com for all e-wrestlers. Now since it is my idea I will obviously support the site if it actually becomes one.

Now, I have just finished discussing this issue with long time columnist Craig S. had this to say.

Mike: I was just wondering, since u have a lot of experience writing columns and being involved in e-wrestling, do u think an EWunion is possible?

Craig: EWunion?
Mike: E-wrestlers union
Craig says: Dunno, doubt it.
Craig: Explain it more.
Mike: well basically I wrote this column on if in the future it is possible, so I figured one day, we all band together and make EWunion.com then we as a whole make set terms that certain types of e-feds must follow. then if a e-fed does not follow it, it becomes listed
Mike: We then refuse to RP on that site
Craig: Wouldn't work.
Craig: What would Wrestlers gain from the site itself?
Mike: Never being screwed over by a president who does not listen to his e-wrestlers and favours against certain guys, also a clean e-fed
Craig: lol
Craig: You think presidents would "bend to the will of a union" of course not.
Craig: They'd just say fuck off and not use those e-wrestlers.
Mike: If the site becomes big enough
Mike: True
Craig: That's the thing.
Craig: The wrestlers really don't gain much.
Mike: Ya but the new guys do
Craig: A president is always gonna favour and shit (well not really, but the ones who want to will) they'll just be forced to hide it better.
Craig says: Wrestlers quit if there prez is a dick, no need for a union.
Craig: Blacklists are extremely ineffective, because that fed would just close and a new one with a different one would open
Mike: Alright but if the site becomes listed on a site as big as EWunion could be, then when someone is joining a fed they could put the address through a search engine and check it out
Mike: Then the new fed would be caught if he didn't change
Craig: Listen I'm telling you flat out.
Craig: IT WOULD NOT.. NOT! WORK
Craig: You'd totally eliminate newbies in the process.
Mike: Alright then
Craig: Newbie fedheads that is.
Mike: You beat me Craig
Craig: People make mistakes and fuck up, that's the point.. you don't go into something knowing how to be the best, you learn.
Mike: Once they learn they are taken down
Craig: lol
Mike: People will view the site every so often the
Craig: Who are you to say they are horrible?
Mike: I'm not
Craig says: Or how fast they should learn.
Mike: I don't, the Union leader does
Craig: Leave well enough along.
Craig: If you have a union, people need money.. like unions are for IMPORTANT things where a lot of cash and labour is involved.
Craig: It's pointless and ultimately defeating itself.
Well, Craig showed me, if you want to voice your opinions on this matter post it on the FSW fans board or email me.
Well that is it, remember, always tell the truth.
