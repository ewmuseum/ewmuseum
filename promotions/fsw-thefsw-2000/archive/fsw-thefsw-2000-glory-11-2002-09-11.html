As we open up the first thing we notice are the small words in the bottom right corner of the screen reading “Earlier Today”. The scene is the backstage are of the Dome at America’s Center, where there is much hustle and bustle going on in preparation for the nights Glory event. All of the technicians, assistants, and production crews are busily scurrying about trying to make sure everything is just right for the evenings show. Trucks are being unloaded in the background where the underground parking garage is located. As we move that direction our focus shifts to a black limousine that crawls into a parking spot and parks, sitting there ominously for a few moments. The Five Star Wrestling staff and crews move about oblivious of the limousines arrival, many passing in front of it and around it. The camera moves in closer as if trying to see inside the windows, and as it does a young technician hauling several pieces of production equipment passes in front of the camera. Suddenly the young technician is shoved out of view and to the ground by a pair of hands that thrust into the picture. Panning back and slightly to the left we can see that the hands belong to the Commissioner of FSW, Chip O’Meara. He scowls at the tech and snarls in a gruff voice. Chip: Will you get the HELL out of the way already!! Don’t you know who is in that limo?! Chip: Well are you just gonna sit there or are you gonna get back to work you jackass?! The technician shakes his head in dismay as he gets to his feet and gathers the equipment up that had scattered all over the floor around him. He scampers off as O’Meara watches him intently for a moment before walking briskly up to the back door of the limo. He takes in a deep breath to calm himself, then confidently pulls the door open with a huge smile on his face. Chip: Welcome to Five Star Wrestling, Mr. Nels- O’Meara takes a swallows heavily as a massive African American man, standing 6’11” and weighing 350 pounds, steps out from inside the limo. The stranger hauls back again and delivers another briefcase shot to Meanstreak's face, opening up a gash on his forehead. Streak stumbles backwards, shakes the cobwebs out and roars with rage, but before he can do anything, he's met with another shot...and another...and another...and another...and another...and another. Finally, The Beast's eyes roll back in his head and blood pours in a mask down his face and chest, then falls limply to the steel ramp, unconscious. Standing in front of The Beast's unconscious body, grinning, is none other than... Lynn: NO WAY! NO FUCKING WAY! Sam: IT'S HIM! HE'S HERE! The man standing above Meanstreak's body, wearing an expensive suit splattered with flecks of blood is none other than The CEO, Tyler Nelson. Nelson looks down at Streak and grins, then pops open the briefcase. Several bricks come tumbling out. Lynn: THE CEO IS HERE IN FSW! TYLER NELSON IS HERE *RIGHT NOW* ON GLORY! JESUS CHRIST! Sam: HE'S KNOCKED OUT STREAK! I CAN'T BELIEVE IT! As soon as Tyler dumps the bricks from the briefcase, letting them trickle down and land on the lifeless and bloody form of Meanstreak, two figures emerge from the backstage area and head down the ramp. Lynn: HERE COME NELSON’S THUGS! Sam: OG looks like a thug, but Simmons looks like a thud. The two men arrive at Tyler’s position and he barks orders to them, pointing down at Streak. Orpheus Grant, decked out in a Dallas Mavericks jersey, blue jeans, and tan Timberland boots, reaches down and pulls the limp body of Meanstreak up. Meanwhile Mr. Simmons, in his plain black secret service-esque suit, does his best to help his associate. The two men rolls Streak into the ring underneath the bottom rope while Nelson climbs the ring steps, a victorious grin on his face. Lynn: Hasn’t he done enough already?! He has no business being here anyway! The crowd is both incensed and shocked at the same time as the grinning former CEO of Primetime Championship Wrestling stands over the unconscious form of the Monster they call Meanstreak. Orpheus Grant and Mr. Simmons flank Nelson on either side, all three gloating in Tyler’s deed. The crowd begins to chant “ASSHOLE!! ASSHOLE!!” as Nelson calls for a microphone. Mr. Simmons produces one from the inside of his plain black suit jacket and hands it over to his boss. Tyler clears his throat and begins to speak. Tyler: *Hmmm-mmmm*…..If you could keep it down please while I speak it would be appreciated. The crowd’s noise level nearly doubles as they rain down on the ring with voracious boos. Nelson snorts disgustedly. Tyler: Typical. You people have no idea what real talent is….having to watch the likes of Timo Borunda, Mike Frampton, and Big Daveylicious week after week. I imagine you are brainwashed by the trash and down right talentlessness of these second rate curtain jerkers that Orion keeps shoving down your throats. Lynn: This man has no idea what he is talking about!! Those men are some of the top stars in FSW! Sam: All I can say to that is NELSON IS HERE!! NELSON I SAY~! Tyler pauses for a second before continuing. The crowd continues to berate him, tossing debris in the ring. Orpheus Grant is nailed in the face with a soda and goes ballistic, almost jumping out of the ring. Mr. Simmons does his best to restrain the massive bodyguard. Lynn: Someone hit Nelson in the face with a baseball bat!! Tyler: Well, I’m here to change all that. I’m here to save you from having to watch massive piles of donkey shit like this loser. Nelson points down at the unconscious Meanstreak. The crowd boos then erupts into a huge “STREAK!! STREAK!! STREAK!!” chant. Nelson paces around the ring enraged, covering his ears and stomping around angrily. Lynn: Look! He’s throwing a temper tantrum!! What a big baby! Sam: These people are insane!! Nelson has more talent in his pinky finger than Meanstreak could ever dream of having! Nelson temporarily calms himself and goes back over to where Meanstreak lies, kneeling down next to the Monster. Tyler: Last week I saw this big, hairy, stinky, dirty, rotten, no good piece of wasted sperm on Glory tossing people around like rag dolls again. I think to myself, ‘Woopty doo, Meanstreak. I kicked his ass before.’ So I gave your commissioner Chip O’Meara a call… Lynn: IT WAS NELSON ON THE PHONE LAST WEEK WITH CHIP!! He’s been working on Nelson for an entire week! Sam: Negotiations take a long time with an athlete the caliber of Tyler Nelson. Tyler: …and told him that I could help him out. We met for lunch and discussed a few things. I told him what I wanted and he said he would get it for me. That jackass of a CEO, Paul Orion tried to stick his nose in our business, but he obviously doesn’t know me very well. Contrary to his beliefs, I AM wanted here in FSW by Chip O’Meara. The deal was pretty simple. FSW gets the hottest free agent in wrestling history, I get loads of money, he gets ratings through the roof, and I get rid of MEANSTREAK! Lynn: OH MY GOD!! HAS TYLER NELSON GOT A DEATH WISH?! Sam: A better question would be is he officially under FSW contract? Tyler: I’m going to make FSW bigger than PCW could ever dream to be. As the only six star wrestler in Five Star Wrestling, I plan on spearheading our journey to the top of the ratings…… Nelson looks down at Streak in disdain. Tyler: …but you don’t fit into the plans. You aren’t what ratings are made of, Ethan, and the first thing on my agenda is getting rid of your bastard ass! The crowd continues to cheer for Meanstreak to awaken and kill Nelson, but the Monster is still out cold, bleeding profusely from the head and face. Nelson shuffles around in his suit jacket until he removes some sort of document. He unfolds it and holds it up to the camera. Lynn: THAT’S AN FSW CONTRACT!! Sam: WHOOO!!! Tyler: This is a contract to wrestle for Five Star Wrestling….unsigned as of yet, but it will be shortly. I’m going to sign this, Meanstreak’s death warrant, in the Monsters own blood! Lynn: HE WOULDN’T DARE!! Nelson grabs a pen from his pocket and dips it in the blood that has spilled all over the mat. He then places the contract on Meanstreak’s back and signs his name on the dotted line. The crowd is nearly at a riot stage now, bowing in the steel guardrails and almost surging past FSW security. Once finished signing, Nelson admires the document before stuffing it back inside his suit jacket. He rises to his feet, sneering down at Meanstreak the whole time. He then looks out at the crowd, a cocky grin starting to spread across his face. Tyler: Welcome back my friends, to the show that never ends. We’re so glad you could attend. Come inside, come inside….. Lynn: TYLER NELSON HAS OFFICIALLY SIGNED WITH FIVE STAR WRESTLING!!! FANS, WE’RE OUT OF TIME FOR THIS WEEK!!! TYLER NELSON IS NOW IN FSW, AND HE’S GOT ONE THING IN MIND – HE WANTS TO ELIMINATE MEANSTREAK!!! With that “Karnevil 9” by Emerson, Lake, and Palmer hits over the PA system. New FSW Signee Tyler Nelson stands tall over the toppled beast that is Meanstreak, chuckling evilly…as the the FSW ©2002 info appears at the bottom of the screen…and we fade to black.
