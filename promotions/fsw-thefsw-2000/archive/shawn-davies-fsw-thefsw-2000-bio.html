Shawn Davies Biography Height 6'0" Weight 236 lbs Hometown Cameron, North Carolina Date of Birth June 3rd, 1976 Alignment Popular Heel Entrance Music Hello - Ice Cube feat. Dr.Dre and MC Ren Manager/Valet Yes Personality Shawn is one stuck up, bad boy, who always has an attitude. He's not that serious about regular matches, but when it's for a title or number one contendership... he is so serious, you wouldn't be able to tell him apart from Bill Gates. Shawn is very good in extreme matches, submission matches, and of course, tag-team matches. He is really mean to the fans, and is always seen with Linzie (wife). He likes to taunt people, but really never throws a threat at you, unless it's needed. Shawn is basically a heel with a huge attitude. Also very, very rich and selfish. History Shawn (Richard) Davies, was born on June 3rd, 1976 to his parents; Marie and John Davies. Shawn started having a thing for wrestling when he was probably 4 years old, because his grandfather always sat him on his lap to watch it. His grandpa, Dicky Davies was a real life New Japan Pro Wrestler for 5 or 6 years. He taught Shawn and his brother Matt how to wrestle properly, so no one would get injured. Since Japan is an all around wrestling organization, Dicky Davies knew it all. He invented the Fireman's Carry, which most people think that he didn't, but in the Davies' minds, he did. Anyways, when Shawn was 12, and Matt was 14, they got a wrestling ring, and some fake weapons, and started the XWF. They taught people all over their town how to wrestle so they could wrestle other people and put on shows for people to make money so they could keep on showing the people shows, in which the people liked to see. When Shawn finished High School, he went to college for four years, and he wanted to become a Lawyer. He dropped out, and when he turned 21 he started to wrestle all around the world. UW, CWF, IWF, MWF, CWA, AWA, CBWA, DWF, RZA, DSF, and so many more federations are on his list. He has won many titles throughout his 7 year career. Shawn thinks that when Chris (CWF partner) won the tag-team titles, and held them for 6 months, then left because the federation shut down, is his most memorable moment! That only happened last year, and out of his seven year career that's his favourite MOMENT! Well there is the time when his brother Matt and himself won the XWF Tag-Team titles (then owned by Shane Anderson) for the 28th time was cool too. And the time when he won the XWF Extreme Title for the 18th time, and when he beat his brother Matt in a ladder match for the XWF World and International Title. He was on a roll, and Shawn isn't going to quit? Do you think he will quit soon? Heck NO! He's got enough energy that he needs. Hell he's only 24 years old! Well Shawn is one extreme wrestler, and he thinks that he can become the world champion of another organization, for his SECOND TIME! He has only held a world title, in a televised, or well known fed once. He has held it 5 times in not really known and Indie federations. Shawn is an extreme wrestler, and now a technical, a high flyer, and so much more. He wants to put the Davies name back in the history books, for the 5th time throughout the Davies Family!
