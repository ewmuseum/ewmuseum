DATE: 4/4/01

Hello, and welcome to the first installment of Idolistic Views. The most popular and well known e-wrestling column, in all of e-wrestling. Okay, so maybe I'm lying, so what..sue me. No please dont. Anyway, incase if you dont know, tis I, "The Idol" Jacob Jackson, aka, VP Jacob Jackson, aka..your idol!

Okay, now that we are done with that corny introduction, let me get on to todays topic..err..that reminds me, Im not sure what it is. Okay, how about this..a lot of people lately have been writing columns about, how e-wrestling has changed since it's birth. Now there is no doubt in my mind that E-Wrestling has changed, and I also think that it has taken a downfall. Before, when I started it was about having fun. I didnt really care about winning..although I didnt care to lose. But after a bit I got used to it...=oP Anyway, now it seems that E-wrestling is all about, who's the best E-Wrestler, who's the best poser artist..which e-fed has the flashiest site, etc. Well...now let me get to my point. Im not going to ramble on and waste your time by telling you..what is wrong with E-Wrestling..what Im going to do, is ramble on, telling you how you can change e-wrestling.

First off, we need to get rid of our egos. Who cares who's the best e-wrestler? I certainly dont. Although I do know how it feels to be a successful e-wrestler, and how it feels having people look up to you. Although..I wouldn't be in e-wrestling any longer, if 1. I no longer had fun..or 2. I was able to drop the habit. Yes indeedy, e-wrestling is a very addictive e-hobby. The more you do it, the more you love it, the harder it is to stop. But just think about this..if we all continue on with our ignorant ways, sooner or later the majority will no longer love what e-wrestling has become..and therefore..e-wrestling will slowly die out. Do we want this to happen? Hell no! Please, if you are reading this, instead of immediatly saying "I dont act like that!", just take a look at yourself. First by how you treat others. If you are on of the people that go around "You suck..your fed sucks..Im GONNA HACK IT!" Please stop! Instead look at the other e-wrestlers as equals. Realise that they are just competing in the same hobby that both you and I love. Have respect, not for who holds the "gold" but instead have respect for who is a part of E-Wrestling. Because I can guarentee, as long as people continue on with this great sport, and continue to have the love for it..E-Wrestling will live on for a very long time!

Another thing that annoys me, is money. People seem to think, that if you have the best posers, the best site design, you make the most money off of your fed..you will be the best. Yes, this is actually true to a certain degree. But what you must realize, that feds like FSW, fWo, DWF, etc, all have worked their asses off. PK has worked his ass off. FSW is not the first fed that Brandon (Painkiller) has ran. So before you begin working your ass off for all the money in the world to earn Poser, just start small. I wish I would have..I when I started my second fed, wanted it to be the best. I bought Poser, and it turns out, I dont have much of a talent with it. I also, wanted the best e-wrestlers to join. I did get some great wrestlers such as Big Poppa, Razor Blade, Scott Hunter etc..but you must realize to keep your members happy you must keep your site in tip top shape. What I mean, is that if you are starting a fed, more then likely you will be a little greedy and not want much help. Let me tell you from experiance, without much help you will eventually become stressed out, and your fed will fold, and like I had, you might quit E-Wrestling. Luckily I came back for all of you =0P

Okay, Im going to cut this column short, I should have given a little more thought on my subject. But hopefully I helped you realise a bit what is needed, to keep the E-Wrestling legend living on. Happy roleplaying to all of you. And get along =o)

BTW..Sunday was my B-Day YIPPEE! Im sure you all have checks in the mail addressed to me. =oP
