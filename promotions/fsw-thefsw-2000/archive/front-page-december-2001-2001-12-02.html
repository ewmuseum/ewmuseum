BIG UPDATE
12.2.2001

Problem after problem after problem. That's all the FSW has been having lately with it's broadcasts. After searching two weeks for an arena to host Paranoia it is now finally up. The FSW is in huge finacial debt. How many more shows can it put on? Why are plans for the FSW being sold being made? Paranoia shed some light on the matter.

Poster: Brandon E. | FSW | 12.02.2001
Well shit happens. And lately alot of that has happened in the FSW. No worries though. My lazy ass is once again fully devoted to the FSW. Just some things I had to work out. Expect a new design sometime next week. Until then there will probablly be minimal updates. Not like theres been a massive amount of those lately anyways. But with I am going to atempt to do something no other e-fed has ever done in the past few months. Re-do the site with everything complete. Yes I know hearing that could give someone a heart attack. Especially for me. But I will attempt it nonetheless. When I have something else useless to say I will be sure and put it up here.

Poster: Johnny Phantom | Futile | 10.28.2001
