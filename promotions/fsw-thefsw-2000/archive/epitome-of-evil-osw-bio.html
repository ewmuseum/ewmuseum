Epitome of Evil Biography Real Name Shawn Ellis Nicknames EOE Hometown Dallas, TX Birthday 5-1-74 Height 7'0 Weight 336 Fed Titles World Champion Biography Character Biography- EOE doesn't like to discuss his past, so everytime he is asked about it he tells each person something different. But in this case he has decided to tell the truth. EOE grew up in south Dallas. But he stayed a couple of block away from all the bad things that went on around there. His parents would never let him go to the block that separated the bad neighborhood and the good neighborhood, which the basketball court was. Shawn was a preacher kid ye that's right his dad was a preacher and EOE was always in church; so good things were expected from him and at the same time he was teased by his friends. One day he was convinced to go to the had neighborhood with a couple of his friends, just to see what it was like. Alot of people was in awe at his size and he was became very popular. After seeing what it was like, Shawn came to the conclusion that his parents just had the wrong idea of the people the lived in bad part of the south dallas adn they weren't abd people. He found himself to go there even more and more without his parents knowing of course. Shawn found himself caught up in the game and with the wrong crowd. He even went as far as being in a gang. His parents and two brothers noticed his change, but there was nothing they could do, they talk and the talked to him, but he was not in the right mind so he didn't listen. Over the years he lost his friends that exposed him to the bad things in life. Which helped him, he saw it as an eye opener. He decided to take up something that would keep him out of trouble; wrestling. Before wrestling he managed to form a better relationship with his family. He told them that he would try to make wrestling a career. Everyone was cool with it except Chris. He's only person that the he doesn't get along with is he older brother Chris, who always throws the things that he's done in past in face. He gives Shawn a hard time because he didn't go through with the brotherly plan, which was Chris being a doctor, Terrell being a lawyer, and Shawn being a stock broker. Shawn was the only one who didn't go along with the plan. The middle brother Terrell is always the one in the middle, and always stick up for Shawn and encourages him. Shawn went on to try to make his dream a reality. At the age of 21 he began to wrestle for a place down in Texas. He came ot realize being a athelete on a basketball court was very different from being on in a ring. He wasn't experienced as the others so he would get flamed and laughed at by the older experinced men. They would always disrespect him simply because he was not as level they thought he should be. Day in and day out the would harass him, not knowing how what type of person he used to be. Trying to live up to the 'new way of life' Shawn let it slide, until one of the men put his hands on him in the locker room right before their match. Shawn played it cool and didn't retaliate until he got him in near ring for their match. The fight spilled to the outside, Shawn kicked him in the stomach and delivered a shattering powerbomd, which broke the man's back. The people watch in awe as the man lied there motionless. As Shawn walked to the back, one of the wrestlers stopped him in and told him that he was an big evil bastard and didn't deserve to be alive. Shawn just laughed at him and walked out of the building. Some how Shawn was let back into the ring just a short time after the incident. When he got his big break he decided to go by the allias of EOE. This kind of resulted in a problem back home, his dad is a pastor of a church and Shawn goes by the name of EOE. Alot of people don't like him because of this they say it's disrespectful to his father, but EOE just went on about his own business an pay attention to anyone, which was a smart move. Over his years in the ring EOE became very experienced and very dominate. He's been a tag champ many times, hardcore and world champ. EOE goes in and out to different wrestling promotions to mark his paths of destruction. So far every place that he's been he's gotten noticed and respected. He's called the Epitome of Evil simply because of his actions in the ring. Perhaps OSW will be the next to agree that he is the Epitome of Evil. Character Personality- EOE doesn't go around pretenting to be a worker for the devil or anything like that, he's made it clear that his name is based on what he does in the ring. Outside of the ring EOE is a cool layed back person, he cracks a few jokes every now and then. He rarely seen without he girlfriend Eve. EOE likes to chill around his house most of the time which is in Dallas. Sometimes EOE goes around being rude and cocky toward people just because he likes to do it. However, when EOE steps foot in a arena and it's time for him to compete, he's all business. When he steps in the ring he goes into this zone that is unexplained. He is always rude towards people on the same roster as he because he knows that they want to be at the top just like him and they will do anythign to knock the big man off ladder. He rarely aligns himself with anyone, if he does it's strictly business. To put it simple he doesn't like a trust anyone on the roster.
