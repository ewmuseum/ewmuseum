Jason Winters Biography Height 6'3" Weight 238 lbs Hometown Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada Date of Birth 02/10/1977 Alignment Popular Face Entrance Music How I Could Just Kill a Man - Rage Against the Machine Manager/Valet Yes Personality Perhaps Jason Winters' most outstanding characteristic is his sheer cockiness. It affects many, if not every, aspect of his life, and even gives him his belief that "winning is everything." And, Winters goes into every situation thinking he'll come out on top. He also has a bad habit of showboating and hot-dogging, which usually works against him. Despite this, the fans love him, because there's always something about him that makes you love him, whether it's his sense of humour, his ability, or his absolute hatred of heels. History When Jason Walters was born, the planets had to have been lined up, and his fate must have been written in the stars. Pure luck would dominate his fate, and choose his calling. Jason was born into the legendary Walters wrestling family, a Pacific Coast hotbed for tag team wrestling. One of the twin sons of Josef Walters, Jason and his mirror image Jack worked together from day one to be a powerful team. It seems that from day one, Josef taught the two karate and boxing, and wrestling wasn't far behind. It was there that Jason showed the most interest, and the most skill, and he stuck with it. However, wrestling only with his brother created boredom, so Jason looked for a good backyard fed. Called the Vancouver Wrestling Federation, a group of young boys, Jason included, fought in a pioneering backyard fed. Jason went undefeated. After the VWF closed, Jason began wrestling in school, and dominated the middleweight division. When he graduated from high school, it seemed like the wrestling division went dry. The NCAA wanted to pick him up, but Jason decided to go pro. However, when he tried out for Canadian Championship Wrestling, he was turned down in shock, as a liability. It turned out that his uncle has been convicted of steroid abuse. Jason was shocked, but decided to press on...with a different last name. Jason Winters was then born, albeit at the disgrace of his family. he pressed on though, and worked from fed to fed. This went on for four years, and Jason got small amounts of recognition on independent circuits, but he felt something was missing. That's when Damon Trask of the UWA approached Jason. He offered a contract to Jason, and he immediately signed to the Big 5 fed. Jason immediately made an impact, and became Hardcore Champion in just 2 months. A month later, he became a Canadian Champion in front of his own hometown. In only 3 months, Jason became one of the biggest superstars in the UWA, partly thanks to a feud with Scott Douglas. Jason felt, though, that when the feud ended, so did his push. So, when his contract expired, he left the UWA in search of better stomping grounds, bringing him to the FSW!
