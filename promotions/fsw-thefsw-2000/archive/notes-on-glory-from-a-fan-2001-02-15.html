Notes On Glory From A Fan

15 February 2001

What this is A post from the fed's own newsboard, the page where rumours, predictions and fan reports about its shows were posted.
|Notes On Glory From A Fan|

This in from FSW Fanatic, Jason M..

"The atmosphere was simply electricfying. I can't what for the Pay Per View. That'll be something to look forward to. Finally, a Pay Per View worth the money.
I smell a Onslaught and Johnson feud down the line perhaps. That is hopefully if Epitome of Evil doesn't but his big ol' self in. By the way, Steve I be sending you my fruitcake for doing America a favor and shutting up Eve."

Biggest Pops:.
> Onslaught
(Winning the match after making EOE tap out. His entrance as well.)
> Onslaught and Phoenyx
(Their awesome Frog Splash and Burning Hate combination!)
> Mike Marvelous
(Defeating Michael Gaines)
> Vision
(Winning over Aggonizer)
> Max Danger
(Constantly screwing over Mayhem and beating both of the Psycho Brothers...AGAIN! The fans weren't sure whether to cheer or boo.)
>Phoenyx
(I think everyone liked his entrance, but then it turned into boos after their they took his Celtic Cross away, making all right with the world.)

Biggest Heat:.
> The Alliance of Violence and Mike Marvelous interfering after the Main Event.
(Mikey, say ain't so?)
> Steve Johnson
(Bailing out on Epitome of Evil. That got some boos. However when he gave Eve the Cutting Edge, it was sort of a mixed reaction. There was more booing though. The guys booed that Johnson hurt a "hottie". Although I say she's ugly. The women cheered since someone finally shut her up. I'm a guy, but I gotta argee with the chicks.)
> Epitome of Evil
(Once again, the fans STILL aren't sure whether to cheer.)
