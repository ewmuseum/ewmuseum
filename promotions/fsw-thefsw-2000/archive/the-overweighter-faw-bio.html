The Overweighter Biography Real Name Rob Hurst Nicknames The American Lardass Hometown Death Valley Birthday 13/2/77 Height 7'2' Weight 535 Biography The Overweighter was born in New York. His parents were both English, His father died in America when he was a year old. Him and his mother stayed in America. His mother married again and The Overweighter grew up believing that his stepfather was his father. His stepfather was an overweight biker. The Overweighter started to grow up and acted like his stepfather. The Overweighter’s family couldn’t afford to send The Overweighter to school, so The Overweighter was uneducated. He turned 15 years old. He was 380lbs and 6’7”ft. His mother told him who his father really is. When he was 18 and The Overweighter went to England to meet some of his family he had never met. They told him that his father was one of the funniest men alive, they showed The Overweighter his very own joke book he made and The Overweighter started to act more like his real father. He started to make up his own jokes. He was becoming more and more immature. When he got home to his mother. She saw his father. His stepfather gave him his motorcycle, and The Overweighter started to get interested in wrestling. His mother and stepfather died in a house fire. The Overweighter was now living on the street and had nowhere to go. He got a job as a comedian and after a few weeks of shows he had enough to get an apartment. He started to learn how to wrestle while he was a comedian. He got fired from his job and owed money for his wrestling lessons. One day a man showed up on his doorstep, it was his brother he didn’t know about and he had been living in England. His brother was called Candy Cane. He was a wrestler for a small federation. He started to live with The Overweighter and started to teach him how to wrestle for free. The Overweighter joined the small federation, him and his brother was raking in the money. They both bought a house together. The Overweighter was 24 years old, nearly 500lbs and nearly 7”ft. The Overweighter was doing pretty well for himself, when one day Candy Cane seriously injured his knee in a car accident. Candy Cane couldn’t wrestle anymore and it was just Overweighter bringing in the money. The Overweighter won $10,000,000 from the lottery and started his own business called BiGG Mouth’s Burger Bar. He spent all $10,000,000 on the business. The business slowly went out of business because of Mc Dave’s. He was forced to sell it to Mc Dave’s. He promised himself that before he dies he would buy BiGG Mouth’s Burger Bar back. The Overweighter and Candy Cane was running low on money once again, there was a wrestling tournament called The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and Candy Cane showed The Overweighter all he could before the tournament started. The Overweighter was now 7’2” and 535 pounds. The Overweighter signed up for the tournament, he won his first few matches and got to his bracket final where he lost to Totally Tubular Tommy. He went home with no money and without a job when one day he got a phone call from the FSW owner offering him a contract. He accepted and is currently still working at FSW.
