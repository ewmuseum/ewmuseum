Big Daveylicious Biography Real Name David Nicknames The Enchilada Of Champions, Mentor Of Professional Wrestling, Deity of Entertainment, Dynasty of Charisma, Man With The Ego Bigger Than Texas, Man With 1,001 Ways Telling You How He Is Gonna Kick Your Ass, Big D, PCW's He-Man Woman Hater Hometown Miami, Florida Birthday December 19,1978 Height 6'5 Weight 246lbs Fed Titles FSW Intercontinental Title (9/19/00- 11/3/00),FSW World Heavyweight Title (11/14/00-11/26/00), FSW Hall of Famer Fed Awards Best FSW Intercontinental Champion Award, Longest Regining FSW Intercontinental Champion, 2.5 Owner of the FSW Biography Big Daveylicious came to the FSW mainly to seek revenge on mentor,Cyle Chaos from the days of tourment in the UCW. While Cyle Chaos was defending the FSW Intercontinental title against RZA. A man came to the ring with chair attacking RZA and Chaos costing them the match. The man turned out to be Big Daveylicious. The week after that on Glory Big D defeated Cyle Chaos for the FSW Intercontinental title. He later had a rematch against Chaos costing Chaos another lose to Big D and then on the pay-per-view FSW Total Anihellation. Big D would fight Chaos in a steel cage match. Big D would retain his title with a 20 feet drop off of the cage into the announcers table. That was Cyle Chao’s last match at the FSW. A month later, the deficient ICW Intercontinental Champion, Jack Duncan would enter the FSW claiming that he still is the ICW Intercontinental Champion and that he is better than the current FSW Intercontinental Champion, Big Daveylicious. They would later fight each other at FSW’s Fallen pay-per-view. Both Intercontinental titles on the line and Big D came victorious. Big D would later defend his title in a series of matches. Big D would lose the title after holding the Intercontinental title for about two months. Big D made a record of holding the Intercontinental title the longest. Big D would later have to prove himself as he got a match against then current FSW World Heavyweight Champion, Mathew Morrison at Glory. Big D would go out of the bout as the FSW World Heavyweight Champion. Creature and Marc North later went on to involved in Big D’s matches. The tension was building up. On, the FSW Final Destination pay-per-view Big Daveylicious would defend his FSW World Heavyweight title against Creature in a Hide and seek kinda of match. The winner would be the one who found the title and retrieved it would be the FSW World Heavyweight Champion. Unfortunately, for our 'Mentor' he found the title but was attacked and stabbed by a samuri by . Giving Creature the opportunity to get the FSW World Heavyweight title and become the new FSW World Heavyweight Champion. The next couple of weeks the FSW was closed for a month and reopened. A month after the reopening after the Big D was shown on Glory along with Tommy Taker as Painkillers bodyguards which later turned into the Coalition. Big D got his return match as a tag team partner for Tommy Taker for a shot at winning the FSW Tag Team titles against P.S. Mad. Turned into a no contest due to Johnny Mayhem, Midnight, and Static interfering. The 'Mean Cocky Son Of A Bitch' made his presence know as he sadistically attacked Steve Jackson, Jacob Jackson, and the referee with a lead pipe while the Jackson's were fighting each other in a match. Big D had his return victory with Tommy Taker against The Don and Brandon Blade. Another great moment was when Carlton Carter was going against Jade Black. Big D got into the ring and chokeslamed Jade Black costing Black the match and giving Carlton the win. Giving the Coalition another member. During that night Big D and Carlton defeated the current World Heavyweight Champion: Mike Marvelous, and the current Intercontinental Champion: Johnny Mayhem giving Mayhem his first loss of four to Big Daveylicious. At the pay-per-view FSW Harsh Addictions main event match up was Big Daveylicious versus former FSW World Heavyweight Champion, Epitome Of Evil with a non-disqualification rule as Painkiller was the referee, and Steve Johnson was the enforcer for the FSW #1 Contendership for the FSW World Heavyweight title. While the match was becoming one of the best matches in the FSW history, Johnson made a quick three count on Big D. The night later, Big D and Painkiller made it clear that Johnson had no right to do a three count due to the simple fact he was not the referee. Making the match a result to a no contest. So Big Daveylicious would have a FSW World Heavyweight title match at the FSW pay-per-view, Foreign Disaster against EOE, Tommy Taker, and Johnny Mayhem. Big D went through the Foreign Disaster pay-per-view nearly the World Heavyweight Champion. After, personal issues with the company he left for the PCW. Leaving him with two records on his name the only FSW star to hold onto the FSW Intercontinental title the longest and the only FSW member on the roster to stay with the FSW for 11 months without quiting. He cameback to the FSW forming an alliance between Mike Marvelous, Jacob Jackson, Leon Chambers, and Epitome of Evil to form the Five Star Five to destroy the new blood in the FSW. EOE and Big D formed a tag team and dominated the field. Unforunately, it was cut short due to EOE leaving the FSW. Leaving Big D with the renamed Five Star Five the Five Star Four. Which quickly demonished by egos of these FSW legends and Mike Marvelous's temper. Tired of being placed in angles with wrestlers who leave the company such as Tim Walden. He laid out his own speech of the current situation of the FSW in his mind: CRAP. With that speech he got fired out of the FSW for exercising his freedom of speech. Luckily, when you got Politically Incorrect as your attroney and you sue the FSW for discrimination of FSW Hall of Famers your bound to get your job back. Due to bad angles from the FSW staff. The Supreme Court was formed reveling hell bent wrestlers by the name's of Polictally Incorrect, Magnum Wolf, and Big D focused on destroying the FSW because of their bad tenure their. Now, 10 percent of the FSW is owned by the Surpreme Court. Yes, revenge is a bitch. Esspecially, with a man whom is hell bent on destroying the company that created him.
