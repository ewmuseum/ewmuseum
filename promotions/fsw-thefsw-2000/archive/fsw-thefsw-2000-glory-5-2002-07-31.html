As the camera fades in from black, a letter is brought into view. The letter is slightly wrinkled and lays on the rather small table, in the corner of the room. A hand reaches out and lifts the page from the table. The man is not revealed, however the contents of the letter are. 'I am one who knows your trouble. It must be fixed on the double. Meet me now, in the lobby. Then together we will perfect our hobby.' The shot fades into slow motion shots of FSW wrestlers peforming high risk flying maneuvers. Glory is displayed along the top in golden script as in the background a church choir performs “Carmina Burana” by Carl Orff. Suddenly a fast forwarding screech is heard and a double timed “Descending Angel” by Misfits thumps as rapid fire shots of bit time moves are flashed. As the Glory intro fades, the scene switches in the lobby area. There stands Jason O'Neil, speaking with Jose, owner of a soda stand. Jason O'Neil: So then you haven't seen anyone around here, have you? Jose: No. I surprise to see you. No wrestler come my way. Only fan. O'Neil: Strange. I got this note, and... Jose: Der. O'Neil: What? Jose: Wrestler. Der. Jason turns around at the pointing of Jose's finger. He turns right into Chris Bagwell. Slightly agonized at O'Neil bumping into him, Bagwell delivers a hellacious gleam into the eyes of Jason O'Neil. O'Neil, however, shows no signs of intimidation and stares right back. Chris Bagwell: What in the hell do you want? O'Neil: Me? What are you talking about, Bagwell? Bagwell: What the hell kinda games are you trying to play? O'Neil: You send me this... Jason raises his not and Bagwell raises his. They look at each other for a minute and switch notes. O'Neil: Wait a minute...you didn't write the note? Bagwell: Listen closely. I am not one to develop allies. I got your message to meet you here. If this was your way of trying to confront me and become friends, it ain't going to work. O'Neil looks at Chris absolutely confused. O'Neil: I didn't write any damn note. I got the same thing. I thought it was from you. And if you want to act tough, Mister Bagwell we can... Bagwell looks right back at O'Neil, not sure what to think of the situation. Another man enters the scene, interrupting Jason. He's dressed in a black hooded sweatshirt. In red, the letters 'tU' appear on the front left breast of the sweatshirt. On the back, red letters spell out 'We're on the Rise!' The man, hidden in the darkness of the camera, speaks out with a similar voice from a previous Bagwell confrontation. Man: It was I who brought you here. For without me, a victory you would not come near. You're both too egotistical to speak. So it was I who came to seek. Together, you shall let it be known and let it be sworn. Bagwell cuts off the 6'3 character and finishes his catchphrase, however only with a slight twist. Bagwell: TOGETHER...we will make sure Davidson and Deville...are T0rN! O'Neil nods his head in agreement with Bagwell and the man. The scene switches to the raucous crowd in the sold out North Charleston Coliseum. The camera begins grabbing shots of the loyal Five Star Wrestling fans. There is one fan holding a picture of resident FSW badass Magnum Wolf along with a sign that reads “The Alpha Male brings about the Omega.” Another shot is of a group of teenage guys with a sign that reads “Five Star Fairies.” Finally there is a shot of two rather attractive teenage Asian girls who hold up a sign reading “We Rike Ming.” The camera quickly pulls away from the sign that is just wrong on so many levels and finally settles on the FSW announce team for the evening, Lynn Lane, Sam Collins, and former FSW World Champion Big Daveylicious. Sam: Since Asa and Ramey- Lynn: “Bad Frotitude.” Sam: …Right, are pathetic, I should think they both have a chance, yes. Big D: They’re all chumps if you ask Big D. Lynn: I didn’t. Lynn: We also have EOE taking on A Tag Team in a handicap match. Big D: My boy EOE will make quick work of those jerkoffs. Sam: Damn right! Sam: I can’t believe they’re giving this much credit to Kid Wonder… Lynn: But to kick things off here tonight with a big tag team match between four of the brightest new stars in FSW. The Five Star Five will get to them soon enough. Sam: Even Tyler Davey? I think he would be a great addition to the Five Star Five. Big D: That punk? O’Neil breaks the pose and screams at the fans who scream back, twenty thousand strong. He points his fists out to the sides, and then at all the fans. Davidson is elected for his team and seeing this Bagwell quickly decides for his team that he will start off. Both Deville and O’Neil leave the ring as Bagwell waits eagerly for Davidson to make a move and start the match off. Without making contact though Davidson just smiles at Bagwell and walks back to his corner slapping Deville on the shoulder and tagging him into the match unexpectedly. Bagwell gives an angry glare in the direction of Davidson as Deville enters the ring and the two men finally lock up. The two men battle back and forth for the advantage in lock up but it’s Bagwell who shoves Deville down to the mat with a powerful shove. Lynn: Bagwell wants Davidson to tag in there but he’s refusing to do it. Sam: Smart move by Davidson. He’s playing mind games with Bagwell now. Big D: I didn’t think he would be smart enough to do that but he is so far. We’ll see how long it takes him to get under the skin of Chris Bagwell. Deville gets back to his feet quickly and the two men lock up once again. This time though, it’s Deville who takes the early advantage driving a knee into the midsection of Bagwell and then following it up with a thumb to the eye. Deville hooks Bagwell up and takes him quickly over with a hard snap suplex. Deville floats over after the suplex to get on top of Bagwell and once there he immediately begins choking him out. The ref tries getting in the way starting the five count on Deville but as soon as Deville gets off Bagwell he goes right after the ref with a right hand which misses as the ref ducks and falls to the mat. Lynn: Deville is already getting carried away here tonight as he takes a swing at the referee. Sam: Well the guy should have stayed out of his way. Deville turns his attention away from the referee and looks at Tyler on the apron who is calling to be tagged in now that Bagwell is down and hurt. Deville walks to the corner and tags him in as Tyler comes quickly into the ring and begins putting the boots to Bagwell. Davidson lifts Bagwell to his feet and whips him into the ropes before connecting with a dropkick to the jaw, which sends Bagwell over the top rope. He somehow manages to land on his feet though and turns around just in time to dodge an attempt at a springboard plancha by Tyler. Tyler comes crashing down hard on the mats outside and Bagwell quickly drives a boot right into his midsection before looking back up at Deville on the apron. Bagwell reaches down to the hurt Tyler and grabs his hair lifting him back to his feet. He drags Tyler over to the announce position and after a few choice words for Sam, Bagwell slams Tyler face first down into the announce table right in front of Sam. Sam: What the hell is this maniac doing? Get him away from here! Lynn: Looks like Chris is giving you an up close and personal look at Tyler in action Sam. Big D: This is all Davidson’s good for apparently. He can’t even jump the guy right in the ring. Bagwell looks back into the ring but grabs Tyler by the hair one more time and goes to slam him down again. Before Bagwell can do it though Tyler counters with an elbow into the midsection and then slams Bagwell’s head into the announce table. Tyler follows up immediately with a big right hand to the jaw of Bagwell sending him reeling. Tyler grabs hold of Bagwell’s arm and then Irish whips him right into the side of the ring causing him to smash his back against the edge. Tyler picks Bagwell back up and rolls him into the ring before hopping onto the apron. Tyler sets himself on the apron and with Bagwell laying there he slingshots over the top rope and comes down across his chest with a leg drop. Tyler quickly covers Bagwell. Deville follows it up quickly and drives a hard knife-edge chop right across the chest of Bagwell and backing him into the corner. Deville grabs Bagwell by the head and signals for a bulldog. He comes out of the corner with Bagwell but as he jumps Bagwell manages to counter it and using his weight advantage turns the move into a back suplex instead. Deville crashes down hard on the mat and Bagwell looks to his corner for the chance at a tag. Lynn: Bagwell could definitely use a tag here to get O’Neil into this match for the first time. Big D: He better hurry because Deville is right on his tail. Sam: Get him Angelo! Don’t let him get away. Bagwell is just about at his corner and going to make the tag when Deville manages to grab onto his leg and pulls him back away from the corner. Bagwell with the opportunity quickly makes his way to the corner and tags in O’Neil who comes quickly into the ring and meets Tyler Davidson being tagged in by Deville head on. O’Neil catches Tyler with rights and lefts battering him into the corner before driving a boot into his gut. O’Neil quickly follows the kick up with a DDT planting Tyler right into the middle of the ring and rolling onto him for the cover without hooking the leg.
