"Five Star" Mike Marvelous Biography Height 6'2" Weight 243 lbs Hometown Nova Scotia, Canada Date of Birth September 4, 1976 Alignment Popular Face Entrance Music Centerfold - J.Geils Band Manager/Valet None Personality When the opponent has a weapon, he is not afraid to beg for mercy. He views hardcore wrestling as a disgrace to wrestling and thinks that your talent should be measured by your in ring skills. But when it comes down to a win/lose situation, Mike has no problem with using a foreign object to his advantage. He is a hypocrit in this sense. Also, if his opponent is performing poorly, he will ask for a time out and explain to the wrestler in the middle of the ring what he is doing wrong. This will usually result in him getting knocked on his ass. He is mainly a technical wrestler, and refuses to brawl. History Mike grew up in a small town in Nova Scotia, Canada. He was always a great athlete and shunned upon bad manners. He has won countless highscool, university, provincial, and national awards in Free-style wrestling as well as Greco-Roman, and has made a great name for himself. But one day, he noticed a "Sports Entertainment" program called FSW and was totally disgusted by the bad morals and lack of in ring talent shown by the so called "wrestlers". He also noticed how they degrated his homeland of Canada, and there were no good wrestlers to back his country up. So he decided to step in and show these Americans what a Canadian can do. He has won the Prime Time Title, the Intercontinental Title in his short career, and has also won the Tag-Team Titles with his best friend, American "The Idol" Jacob Jackson. They formed the team known as the "Can-Am Connection". Mike has also been in a couple of stables, including "The People's Choice" and "Pinnacles of Perfection" with Jacob Jackson, Razor Blade, Jake Keeton, and "All-Star" Carlton Carter. He now has only one prize in his mind....The FSW World Heavyweight Championship. And this time around, it will be his. General Wrestling Style All Rounder Finisher Marvelocity - Mike charges his opponent with a Spear and converts it into a Northern Lights Suplex during mid move.
