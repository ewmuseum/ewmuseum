Canadian Criminal Biography Real Name Christopher Irving Nicknames 'C' Squared Hometown Quebec, Quebec, Canada Birthday 5/5/83 Height 6' Weight 259 Biography The only records kept on Christopher Irving are his Criminal Records. Other than them and the fact that Doozer is the nineteen year old's uncle, nothing is known about him. Doozer himself could not tell you anything about the Canadian Criminal, for Chris just recently approached Doozer with a note from his mother, Doozer's sister. She wrote the note before she shot herself in the basement of their house. The note also explained that Irving's Dad was beaten to death when trying to resist the police while they were trying to arrest him on what they found to be a false accusation. We do know that that is what lead Christopher Irving into crime and a never-ending against authority, which fit him in nicely with his uncle. The only conflict the two really had while living together was Chris rooted for the Blue Jays and Scott (Doozer) rooted for the Red Sox. Then, when Irving showed interest in the art of professional wrestling, Doozer would not let the small flame wither and die. He fed The Criminal's flame until it erupted into a fire. Yet, as Chris experienced too much success in Indy feds here and there (going undefeated), his head started to grow, much like what happened to his uncle... Only Irving started down a different path. Although Doozer did care way too much about his record and keeping it as perfect as possible, Doozer would not go as far as cheating to get a win. Actually, he was the one usually being cheated. Yet, Irving grew obsessed with keeping his record perfect and still is... He has not gone as far as to cheat yet, other than a few cheap shots here and there... But you'll have to watch 'C' Squared to see exactly what path he will follow... The path of every Canadian Wrestler? Or will he follow his Uncle's footsteps?
