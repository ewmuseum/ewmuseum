Love Hurts card, 26 February 2001

26 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed's own advance card page for its Love Hurts pay-per-view (Continental Airlines Arena, East Rutherford): the announced matches, as the page stood in its Wayback capture of 22 April 2001. A second capture of 1 May 2001 carries the same text.
|Love Hurts|

Date: February 26, 2001
Arena: Continental Airlines Arena - East Rutherford, NJ

.:MAIN EVENT:.
Onslaught vs. EOE vs. Mike Marvelous vs. Phoenyx - World Title - Elimination Match

.:OTHER MATCHES:.
Lucky McKnight vs. Max Danger vs. Johnny Mayhem - Ladder Match - Winner gets a World title shot at the next PPV.

Vision vs. Aggonizer - IC Title

Anthony Brown vs. Jimmy Smith - Prime Time Title

Excalibur vs. Rob Knight - Rising Star Title

Jack Miller vs. Big Gooey Sac - Extreme Title

Jason Rhodes vs. JJ Mizfit vs. Ron Jacobs - Lightweight Title

The Legacy vs. Tommy Taker vs. Sean Rivers

Psycho Brothers vs. P.S. Mad and Sound vs. Keith O'Neil and Jason Rhodes vs. Aggonizer and Dragon - Tag Team Titles - Hardcore Rules - Elimination Match - Both team members have to be eliminated before the team is out.
