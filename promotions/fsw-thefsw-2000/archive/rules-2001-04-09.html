The Five Star Wrestling Regulations:.
The following regulations must be read before joining Five Star Wrestling. As long as you follow these regulations you should do exceptionally well here in Five Star Wrestling.

General Information:.
After signing with Five Star Wrestling you and your e-wrestler are officially under contract with the FSW Wrestling. While under contract, we reserve the right to terminate membership of anyone at anytime. All members shall respect the entire staff, and act in an appropriate manor. We refuse to tolerate delinquents.

Information on Joining with Five Star Wrestling:.
Five Star Wrestling is only accepting ORIGINAL characters. When filling out the application, make a highly detailed History/Biography and accurate Career Highlights because they will be used for your profile on your wrestler page. Also after a wrestler name and nickname is in use no one else may use the name, but the wrestler who has it already in Five Star Wrestling. Five Star Wrestling does not accept rip offs or anything of the such. Names such as Jabroni, Rattlesnake, and The Crock are not tolerable. Don't make any un-proportional sizes for your character. The weight limit is 200-500. The height limit is 5'5"-7'5".

You can have 3 wrestlers as long as two of the wrestlers are in a tag team.This way you can better concentrate on your roleplaying abilities. On a case by case basis, we will determine if you are allowed to violate this rule and have more then three characters. Do not submit characters under different identities. If we find out you do this, all of your characters will be removed from the fed.

Note: A stable must contain three wrestlers at the least. You're not taking over, it's been done, it's old, don't have too many people in your stable. (If your stable is going to take over Five Star Wrestling, we will contact you in advance concerning the manner.)

When you sign up and fill our your contract your have a one week trial. You may leave anytime during that week and nothing else will be said to you. However, should you decided to stay after a week and quit like a month after you join and have signed for example a 5 month contract. During that time period, we can do whatever to your wrestler as we please for the rest of your five month contract.

Five Star Wrestling is mostly an angle based eFed, meaning that you may out roleplay someone but you can still lose. We will place you into a division based on your skill. So if your world title material we will place you in the main event. We will decided when and where to give you a push. However, should you abide by the rules and are easy to work with then your push will come much sooner than someone who grips and complains alot.

Roleplaying Information:.
After being accepted into Five Star Wrestling you are expected to roleplay at least two times a week. Should you fail to do so, your contract will be terminated, but you will be allowed to re-join.

Do not include the Staff members, or other wrestlers in a roleplay without prior permission. We MUST have an email with that handlers permission before it is done or it is invalid. You may use the commentators as interviewers, but you may not attack them. Also you may not submit a roleplay with characters from WWF and WCW. No Exceptions.

Swearing is allowed just dont say "fuck" every other word, but all foul language must be censored. ex. "fuck" = "f--k", "fucked" = "f---ed". See roleplay are supposed to be televised and they sure don't allow most of those words to be said on television. This is just a small request and I hope you will abide by it. Should you decide to include foul language within your roleplays, it will not be counted agaisnt you. However we contact you, telling you about it.

Roleplays must not contain any anti-semetic, racial, or other such unnecessary comment. Sexual comments are allowed on a very stringent basis (you are not allowed to continually make sexual references). Any comment that personally insults a person's race, color, or religion will be deemed null and void and the person who wrote will most likey be immediately be terminated from the e-fed.

If you lose, please don't beg and whine about it. This is pointless and quit annoying. You will eventually win unless you never roleplay or your roleplays just plain suck. If you want to get on the staff's bad side then complain a lot.

Do not demand a title shot! If you are ranked as a contender to a title, you my request a match against the champion. The champion can not refuse the challengers that are ranked and request a title match. The Owner, Chief Executive Officer, President, Vice President, or Commissioner will grant or deny title shots at there convenience.

Roleplays are judged on about 90% quality and 10% quantity. If a roleplay is going to take me more than 10 minutes to read it, WE WILL NOT READ IT! The minimum number of lines allowed is 25 and even though there is no maximum number of lines, please don't get carried away. That means 200 line roleplays usually make no sense and are very boring to read. So if you are going to make a long one, please make it interesting to read.

Here in the FSW, we try to be as creative as we can. There is an entire world out there for your character to cut a promo. Something else like at your house, at the gym, parking lot of the arena, a restruant, etc. Don't submit a roleplay anymore in an arena unless it's to be on the show. If you disobey this you will suffer extensive punishment. First offense, that roleplay is deleted. Second offense, you will serve a one week suspension. Third offense, you're fired.

If you do not roleplay in a 10 day spand you will be fired on the spot. The FSW gets many applications and it's not right to keep people from joining with certain members not roleplaying. Of course if you send me a vacation notice telling me that you are away then we won't count your days of not roleplaying against you. But we will indeed take action if you DO NOT notify us about it.

Once accepted to Five Star Wrestling, you are allowed to post on the roleplay board. ONLY Five Star Wrestling staff and members are allowed to post on the roleplay board. No spamming or advertisements are allowed on the board. If we find you are doing this you will automatically kicked out of the FSW. No Out Of Character (OOC) messages are allowed on the board as well. OOC Messages are for the OOC Board.

Important Reminder
These rules like all rules can be bent modified and adjusted. Nonetheless, if you continue to break rules, you will be fired. The Regulations are subject to change at anytime. Should we do so a note will be placed on the Main Page.
