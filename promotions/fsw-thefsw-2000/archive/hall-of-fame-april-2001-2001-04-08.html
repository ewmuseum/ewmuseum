Hall Of Fame (April 2001)

| Hall Of Fame|
Welcome to Five Star Wrestling Hall of Fame! The FSW has seen a rather large deal of great superstars, tag teams, and stables enter it's ranks. As a sign a of respect, a shrine of sorts has been created for those that have retired or have been retired, for the elite few who's talent ascended such a level that they earned the right be named as the most talented and respected wrestlers we have had the pleasure of watching!

Creature - 2/1/01
Creature, there are only a few things that can describe this man. In his short lived career he was one of hardest working men in the FSW. After weeks upon rival of the FSW he quickl rose to the top. He won the World title in the first oppurtunity he had to win it. Creature never lose the World title as the FSW went on hiatus while he had. Creature's contract expired while the FSW was on break and he couldn't be contacted so he is no longer apart of the FSW. The FSW will miss him and remember him as one of the best.

"The Idol" Jacob Jackson - 12/28/00
Jacob has earned the right to be named as the first entry to the Five Star Wrestling Hall of Fame for no other man could have deserved it more. Since the day when the FSW open it's doors to the public, Jackson showed himself a force to be reckoned with. He has one of the longest World Heavyweight Championship Reigns so far. Jacob was the Five Star Wrestling's first Extreme Champion and was a former Tag Team Champion with the Can-Am Connection, being able to be the only wrestler in FSW History to hold 3 Titles at once! Jacob retired December 12, 2000 with the Intercontinental Championship, causing it to be vacated. Jackson left us as he was, a champion!
