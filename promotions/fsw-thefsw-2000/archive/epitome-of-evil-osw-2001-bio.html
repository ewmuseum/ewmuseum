Epitome of Evil Biography Height 7' Weight 345 lbs Hometown Dallas, Texas Date of Birth May 1, 1975 Alignment Neutral Entrance Music No More Pain - 2 Pac (Instrumental Version) Manager/Valet Eve Personality Epitome of Evil is different from what many people think when they hear his name. He doesn't go around worshipping the devil or live in dark places, he is just like any other person. He simply has the name Epitome Of Evil simply because of what he does to his opponents. EOE is rude, cocky, and quick tempered, he doesnt like to be questioned or interupted. EOE likes to play mind-games with his opponent. He likes to come out while there are wrestling in another match and just mug them or Eve might even come down to play mind games. If his opponent has a habit like drinking he just may bring out a keg of beer to mess up his opponent's game plan. In all EOE is very unpredictable you never what he's gonna say or do. History Epitome of Evil didn't have one of dramatic lifes like the other wrestlers claim they had. He grew up in a nice neighborhood, and started wrestling at the age of 12. While wrestling ealier in his career people would talk down on him and some didnt respect because they said he wasn't powerful and as intimidating as he should be as a result EOE turned into one the hated people known and proved himself in the ring one day in an little wrestling promotion down in Texas when wrestling one of the men who disrespected, during the match the men did something to him which made EOE snap. EOE powerbombed the man through the ring an the man was in a coma for about 4 months. EOE from there went on to better federations The first federation he wrestled in was Cyberlsam. He was known as Phenomdemon, he didn't like the way things were ran there so he went to the HIW. Were became noticed by many people when wrestling in a match the he created, "The Hell In the Heavens" match. After leaving a path of destruction there, he went to the ICW. Which really helped his career because he worked with some really talented wrestlers. ICW closed without EOE being able to get his hands on a belt, he then took some time off wrestling in for awhile. He came back and joined the ZTW which closed before EOE could have first match. EOE became really impatient and wanted to hang it all up, but Eve the encouraged him to keep looking for a federation. He then found the FSW, were he kicked ass and won the World Title. EOE took some time off after loosing the title, but is now back and ready to do what he does best and that is created a path of destuction. He also looks to become the first ever multiple time World FSW Champion.
