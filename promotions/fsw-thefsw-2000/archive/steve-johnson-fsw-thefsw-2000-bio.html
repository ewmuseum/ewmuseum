Steve Johnson Biography Biography Around the time Steve was born, his parents were killed in a car accident. Steve had a hard time growing up with his abusive step father and careless step mother. He would most of the time be bullied at school and not getting any attention. Around the time he graduated from high school, his step father was murdered in his own home. People thought Steve did it since neither him or his step dad never got along and lead him running away from his own home so no one could find him. After around a year, the accusations have stopped and the killer was found and put behind bars. By this time, Steve was living on his own, going after many jobs and laid off from alot of them. Steve found this newspaper ad one day about a wrestling school. The trainer's name was Grandpa Jesus. Steve thought he'd give it a try and it all started a new advanture for him. After about a year of training, Steve was ready for his first match. Along the way, he met this one guy named Brian Limpsky who befriended him. Steve's first fed was called the iRWA(independent Ringside Wrestling Alliance) and captured its North American title within his second month. The iRWA was part of a huge Fed Family called Slamfest and the iRWA was a minor league of the Family and the CEW(Cyber Extreme Wrestling) was iRWA's major league. Steve was named Wrestler of the Month and moved up to the CEW, but he didn't last long there, so he was fired. Around this time, Steve met a girl named Jenna and bout a year later were married and had a daughter named Trisha. Bumping from fed to fed, Steve and Brian came across called the XCW(Xtreme Championship Wrestling). It was a success from the very beginning. Steve and Brian(who was also known as Ace) teamed up and named there team The Elite. The Elite captured the XCW Tag Team Titles 3 times and the Xtreme Tag Titles once. The XCW shut down for a month, but restarted. While still a tag team, Steve and Brian were a success in singles competition. Steve captured the XCW Internet, U.S, and TV Titles while Ace captured the Cruiserweight title. Johnson was gunning for the XCW World Title, but could never reach the top tier. During their last match as a team, Ace permanently injured his back and was never in the ring again. After about 6 months of xtreme action, the XCW shut down for good. During his course, Steve took on forces such as Shane Douglas, Ratticus, and his arch enemy, Chinkster. In the end, Steve Johnson was the only one inducted in the XCW Hall of Fame. A few months went by and Steve stumbled upon a fed called ICW(Internet Championship Wrestling). Just like in XCW, Steve was a hit at the start. His main rival was Enemia who was the ICW Intercontinnental Champion. Steve also battled the likes of 'Clean Cut' Chris Cannon and Marcus Stivers. At the Cold War PPV, Johnson defeated Enemia and became the IC Champion, but forfeited the title a few minutes after winning it cause he wants to catch a bigger fish, the ICW World Title. Cannon was the champ at the time and when Johnson was about to get his shot, the ICW closed. Painkiller, the owner of ICW, phoned Steve about him creating another fed called the FSW(Five Star Wrestling) and wanted him to join. At first, Steve declined PK's offer. After hearing some of the ICWers that are comin back, especially Chris Cannon, Steve accepted. His primary task was to go after Cannon. At the second FSW show, PK announced a 16 man tournament to determine the first FSW World Champion. This included Steve Johnson and Chris Cannon. The sparks would finally fly, or so one thought. Cannon was put behind bars for some unknown reason and forced to leave FSW. This infuriated Johnson, but none the less, he stayed so he could capture the FSW title. In his second round match with EOE, Glitch and Riddler intentionally attacked Johnson and EOE was dq'ed. EOE was the one who was tricked as the three men attacked EOE. This was the beginning of The Untouchables. Steve Johnson went to the first FSW PPV, High Impact to face Matthew Morrison in a 2/3 falls contest for the FSW World Championship. Both able to score one fall a piece, till the end, Steve Johnson used the Black Heart Plunge and became the FSW World Champion. His first world title win. After the match. everything else seemed to go downhill. Glitch retired and The Untouchables were no more. Two weeks after winning the title, Jacob Jackson defeated Steve Johnson, with the help of Mike Marvelous to capture the FSW Championship, and put Johnson out of action for awhile. He returned at the next PPV and cost Jackson the FSW title to EOE. Around this time, his wife Jenna wanted a divorce from Steve, because he was acting so crazy. After this incident, Steve could never see his daughter again. He was depressed. AT the next PPV entitled Final Destination(I think), Steve annnounced his retirement from the ring. Before leaving, he finally got his revenge on Jackson his setting his face on fire. Five months later, Johnson came out of retirement and returned tot he FSW just for a lil while and left again. Steve Johnson is now an FSW Hall of Famer and is kcikin ass over at Advantage Wrestling.
