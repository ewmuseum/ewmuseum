Mike Marvelous Biography Real Name Strangely enough, that is his real name... Nicknames 'Five-Star', 'Your Canadian Hero', 'Mr. FSW', 'The MARVELous One, The Crowned Jewel of FSW Hometown Nova Scotia, Canada Birthday 9/04/75 Height 6'3' Weight 247lbs Fed Titles FSW World Champion(1x), FSW Intercontinental Champion(1x), FSW Tag Team Champion(1x w/ 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson), and FSW Primetime Champion(1x) Fed Awards FSW Hall of Fame, Best FSW Primetime Champion of All Time, Funniest FSW Wrestler, Greatest FSW Tag Team(The Can-Am Connection w/ 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson) Biography Mike grew up in a small town in the small province of Nova Scotia, Canada. He was the middle child of a large family, but he got most the attention. From the moment he was born, friends and family 'marvelled' at what a perfect baby he was. As he grew, they were amazed with his intelligence, wit, and charm. When he got to highschool, they were inthrawled by his natural athletic ability. He joined the highschool wrestling team, and immediately overwhelmed opponents with his quickness, agility, strenth and technique. He won every tournament he went to, and got a scholarship to wrestle at St. Francis Xavier University. There he won more competitions, and after getting his degree, he was invited to the olympics to wrestle for Canada. This was his dream. He was finally going to break out into super stardom. Mike always knew he was destined for greatness, but now was his time to truly prove himself. He trained vigorously every day as the olympics grew closer. Unfortunately, he suffered an injury during training a week before the olympics and was forced to withdraw from the competition. With his dreams of fame and superstardom shattered, Mike returned to Canada. He looked to find his niche in many different job fields. He was a lumberjack, a moose hunter, and even a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police. One day when he was watching television in his office, he saw something that captivated him. Grown men beating on each other on national t.v...in front of THOUSANDS of people. The federation in question was FSW, and Mike immediately set out to FSW Headquarters. This was his chance to shine! This is the chance for superstardom that he missed out on! Upon hearing his credentials, FSW immediately offered him a lucrative contract and he began wrestling there immediately. He was undefeated at the beginning of his FSW career with a win streak of 15 wins before he finally lost. In the span of his career, he beat every FSW great and won every title there is to win (Except the Extreme Title and the Lightweight Title). He and his tag team partner(and also his best friend) 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson put a strangle hold on FSW the likes of which had never been witnessed before or since. At one time, each of them held singles gold and tag team gold consecutively. When Jacob retired, Mike went on his own and won the World Title. After doing this, he had done everything he could possibly do in FSW, so he retired and was inducted into the Hall of Fame. Soon after FSW closed... ...until now. FSW reopened and Mike returned on the very first Glory, forming an alliance with EoE, Big D and Leon Chambers...all former FSW members. A week after that, Mike's old Can-Am Connection tag team partner and best friend 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson came back as Mike's mystery partner in a tag match, and joined the stable of old school FSW members named The Five Star Five. Together, they tried to bring FSW back to the way it USED to be before Paul Orion and Yokoshima Fujiwara Corp. took over. Mike and Jacob made it to the Genesis Main Event for the FSW World Title, in a fatal fourway with Chris Irving and Timo Bolamba, but during the match, they began to fight each other and the whole match turned to hell, ending with Mike Frampton coming down to help the Can-Am Connection, but only causing the ref to throw the match out. On the special edition of Glory after the PPV, Mike turned on Jacob and ended his career with a serious neck injury, then went on to injure fellow stable mate Leon Chambers as well. With the Can-Am Connection and the Five Star Five over, Mike went on to the main event and also ended the career of 'Canadian Criminal' Chris Irving. When Mike lost his chance at the World Title, something inside him snapped, and he became a completely different man. Now, with a new 'always look out for number one' attitude and a new found mean streak, Mike is again on a journey for the FSW World Title. This time, he will get it by himself...and by any means necessary! Other Feds PCW and OSW Other Fed Awards PCW TV Champion(1x), Named PCW DA MAN for a week by Jeff Marx after winning the FSW World Title, bringing it to PCW and renaming it the For Short Wrestlers title, Voted 'Most Sexiest OSW Male Superstar' by female fans. Wrestling Style Technical/Submission/Blatant Cheating Finisher Move The MARVELocity; The MARVELock Finisher Description Spear into a Northern Lights Suplex pin; Standing Ankle Lock Finisher Setup Move TING~! Setup Description Mike blinds his opponent temporarily with his extremely bright smile Special Moves THE Five-Star Frog Splash, The Shooting Star(Overhead Belly to Belly Suplex), Canuck Kick(Superkick), Blame Canada!(Missle Dropkick), The 'Eh' Bomb(Double Underhook Powerbomb), Northern Lights Out(The Stroke), The Disconnection(Top Rope Piledriver onto an open chair. Mike used this move to end his Can-Am Connection Partner, Jacob Jackson's, career with a severe neck injury. It is named 'The Disconnection' because it ended The Can-Am Connection and 'disconnected' Jacob's neck all in one move.) Entrance Music 'Disconnected' by Dry Cell
