daZeD, second edition

10 November 2001

What this is A column from the fed's own site.
|daZeD-11.10.2001|
Yeah…well I'm back, and guess what? FSW still sucks.

Welcome to the second edition of daZeD, produced by yours truly, 'The Fan' Matt Daze…where it truly is 'Five Star' (pun intended).

Well I won't bother my ass with a long introduction this week; I'm going to be keeping it short and sweet. It seems that the FSW continues down its winding road of failure, due to its sheer incompetence when it comes to entertainment.

In the span of seven days, the exact amount of time since my last column, nothing has changed. The content still fails to entertain me…so I can only imagine the disgust that the fans of FSW feel right about now. If it weren't for the fact that I gain large sums of money from those jackasses at FSW enterprises, I wouldn't even be wasting my breath writing this column right now.

It has gotten to the stage where I already feel embarrassed to be apart of the FSW, and I have only been here a week. The continuous cycle of disorganization is increasingly frustrating…are they high when they book the cards or something? Hell…are they high, all the time?

Well, actually I lied earlier. There has been some progress…that being the additional debut of yours truly at Futile Resistance this past Sunday. But of course, you didn't see that until Wednesday…damn those delayed television broadcasts. But anyway, I was trying to do the FSW a favour, by getting rid of that imbecile Tommy Kane…but the moron hit me in the face! Can you believe that? What a spineless piece of crap, I hadn't even threatened him…he was out of order, and should be indefinitely suspended just for that alone, besides the fact that he is just plain bad for business. But don't worry, he will get his.

Also, I have got to say, this new angle involving PS Mad's Amy and Tommy Taker's Victoria is getting my blood boiling…well the look of those fine assess are at least. I guess the FSW did take some of my advice after all, as T&A is the only plausible reason why FSW programming received any ratings this past week in my opinion…if not, it has to be some of those screwed up individuals that watch Oprah. Add the voluptuous Eve to the mix (Hi Desmond), and you have a winning formula.

Other than that, I stick to my theory that FSW shall forever suck more ass than…something that sucks a lot of ass…until those buffoons up in the front office begin to read my column.

You know, I visited the FSW web site the other day…and whoa, no wonder they don't get as much hits as they used to. The site is updated like once a week…and that's when the transcript for the forever late television showings are uploaded. Its like watching paint dry when surfing through the archives is the only productive thing to do. They should put a picture of me on the site…there's a couple thousands hits guaranteed!

But back to FSW's recent pay-per-view, Futile Resistance…why in the hell is Blitz the new FSW World Heavyweight Champion? Am I the only person that sees that this man has no talent? Hell…he's not even a man…he's German! (That's right, send the hate mail!) He is the epitome of stupidity…a talentless schmuck. He's worse than even the more recent FSW World Champions, Chris Fear and Stefan Douglas put together, and that's definitely saying something!

And Phantom…put Big Daveylicious in the Hall of Fame already! Just as much as I used to love his performances in the ring…he's just way too old and over the hill now to be in FSW…and the sight of him on my television set last Wednesday night was stomach churning.

Well, to sum up this week's ranting edition of daZeD, here's my soon to be infamous 'problem and solution' time, which FSW should really take into account, if they are ever to gain any sort of credibility again.

Problem: Tommy Kane…again.

Solution: Take that assclown out…before I do.

Problem: FSW web site.

Solution: Update it…and add a picture of me for the ladies' personal pleasure.

Problem: Those damn delayed television broadcasts.

Solution: Either sort out a new contract with the company that airs them…or set them for a new time slot.

Problem: Better booking.

Solution: Come on people…in order to get ratings, at least give people a reason to watch…not even just five minutes of me can keep them entertained for the rest of the show.

Problem: The streak of disappointing FSW World Heavyweight Champions.

Solution: Put the title around my waist…now that would be something!

Well people, the time has come for my bid farewell. It looks like daZeD has once again been the main attraction by far this week for FSW. And due to popular demand, my services have been requested to make yet another FSW television appearance this week on Paranoia…so tune in to see Tommy Kane get his ass handed to him by yours truly…and then go back to whatever you were watching beforehand. Also, make sure you check out my next column…

Same time.

Same place.

Next week!

-'The Fan' Matt Daze
