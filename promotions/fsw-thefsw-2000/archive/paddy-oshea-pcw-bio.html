Paddy O'Shea Biography Real Name Patrick O'Shea Nicknames Paddy 'Irishman'; That Drunken Bastard Hometown Galway City Birthday 5/11/61 Height 5'1 Weight 230 Biography Born in 1961, O'Shea is a remarkably old wrestler at 41 and it's surprising he has only been in the wrestling profession since 2001 but O'Shea has learned to adapt and his cunning (as well as luck) has brought him to the place where he is now. A member of one of the top federations in the wrestling world. Born into a traditionally large Catholic Irish family, O'Shea spent most of his early life looking up to his two older brothers and elder sister; and looking after his two younger brothers. The reason for this is simple - his mother died of cancer when he was only 12 years old and with his father only relying on alcohol to keep him going. The children learned to help every one of them and when his father died of alcohol related disease when O'Shea was 16, the family quickly bounced back to keep the O'Shea household going. The three oldest children left dramatically at different intervals when they were in their early twenties leaving O'Shea to look after his brothers. At age 6 and 9, they could hardly survive without the now 18 year old Patrick. O'Shea found the times stressful and at intervals found peace like his father once did in alcohol. Without the love his brothers, O'Shea would surely be in an early grave like his father and his quickly snapped out of it. When the brothers were 18 and 21, O'Shea left his now nearly empty home to make up for 30 years of misspent youth. O'Shea tried quite a few jobs including docker and fisherman but known appeared appealing to him. Now travelling around Ireland, at age 35 he got into his first fight (most of the rest, his sneaking got him quickly out of it). To most on-lookers amazement, the little 5'1 'leprechaun' pummelled the menacing 6 footer using cunning and concealed strength. This victory was fresh in his mind two weeks later when a letter arrived from his sister now living in America. It made up for 15 years of no contact. She explained of how much America meant for her. O'Shea pondered the idea of living in America but his strong nationalism persuaded him from it. At age 40, O'Shea found himself in another fight with a gigantic Scotsman known he would later called friend. The giant was known as McClintok and although O'Shea was beat in every direction, he was filled with desire to show the world he wasn't all that he seemed. An on-looker from the fight known as Patrick Smith was watching the fight. Strong in mind yet very intelligent, he saw potential in O'Shea and McClintok. Before the two knew it, they were whisked off to America (O'Shea didn't need convincing this time, trouble was brewing greatly in Ireland). Smith enrolled the two into a wrestling academy. McClintok dropped out after only a few months but O'Sheas desire pushed him on until he became the fighter we know today in FSW.
