Phoenyx Biography General Wrestling Style High Flying, Kamikaze, Technican Finisher The Jagged Edge - The Jagged Edge is easily known as, one of Phoenyx's more malicious maneuvers. Phoenyx positions the opponent in a spot to take a STO. Instead, Phoenyx does 180 Degree spin about and instead of falling forward, falls backwards delivering a reverse STO. The move can also be described a 180 Degree Spinabout Downward Spiral. Power Moves Phoenyx Rising - Moonsault Suicide Driver - Phoeynx lifts the opponent up so the opponent is laying across their shoulders on their stomach. After, Phoenyx extends his arms out as a crucifix for a 4 second pause, Phoeynx positions his opponent's head down and sits/jumps/drops down, dropping the opponent on their head. The move can also be described as a Fire Thunder Driver. Crucifix Spinesnapper - Phoenyx places himself back to back with his opponent and extends his arms outward. After hooking his arms under the arms of his opponent, Phoenyx cups his hands underneath his opponent's chin and slides their neck onto his shoulder. From there, Phoenyx jumps forward, landing in a sitdown position, extensively damanging the neck of his opponent. Phoenyx can also shift himself into the move from by trapping both of his opponent's arms under his armpits in a belly to belly fashion, by twisting his body like the reverse of a Tomakazi DDT/Impaler Motion. Infernal Descent - Phoenyx pulls down his opponent's head into an Inverted Facelock as if executing a Reverse DDT. Phoenyx, keeping his opponent in that position, spins putting himself back to back with the opponent, delivers a crushing necknreaker. Screaming Plummet - Phoenyx lifts his opponent vertically for a suplex. He then drops down on his hip, planting his opponent's head to the mat with a modified brainbuster. Signature Moves Burning Hate - Top Rope Guillotine Leg Drop Butterfly Front Suplex - Phoenyx has his opponent in a double underhooked front facelock. Phoenyx then lifts his opponent as if he were going for a Butterfly Suplex, but he instead falls forward with his opponent's arms still hooked, dropping the opponent on his face and stomach.
