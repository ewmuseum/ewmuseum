"The Epitome of Greatness" Onslaught Biography Height 6'4" Weight 264 lbs Hometown Albany, New York Date of Birth October 14, 1976 Alignment Popular Face Entrance Music Disposable Teens - Marilyn Manson (Instrumental Version) Manager/Valet None Personality Always making time for the fans, Onslaught is perhaps the most intense, exciting and charismatic superstar ever to grace a wrestling ring. When it comes to in-ring talent he is without a doubt, one of the best in the bussiness. Despite the arrogance when it comes to what happens in the ropes, he's all business. When it comes down to sheer force and intensity, Onslaught is simply second to none. From the minute the bell rings, he blazes through the match like a man possessed. The man simply doesn't stop. Onslaught's strength lies in his passion to compete and to never give up helps him consistently stay a step ahead of the competition. Onslaught also has an odd habit while at the ring stage to tilt his face to his left, staring down his opponent with his usual confident grin his face before making his way to the ring. Along with his determination and in-ring knowledge, Onslaught stands as one of the true greats in the industry. He is one of the most innovative offensive wrestlers in the industry today which brings alot of interest to his matches. When given knowledge of whom his unlucky opponent may be, The Epitome of Greatness somehow displays his amazingly ability to defeat the undefeated. Onslaught researches the backgrounds of his opponents, learning about their wrestling style, and using that to his advantage by finding reversals and submissions to compensate for. Onslaught simply views a wrestling match as yet another challenge, a challenge placed upon one's self, a challenge to chart your own boundaries and then attempt to go beyond them. No matter what, he never lets up. Onslaught's uncanny ability to trash talk and psyche out even the most cool tempered opponents leaving them simply unprepared for the Wrath of the Onslaught! History At Cornell Medical Center on the day October 14, 1976, The Sole Epitome of Greatness was born into the small baby, named Darren Smith. Darren had a very loving family. Although they were never rich, the Smith household lived around the upper middle class within the Bronx. However at the Cornell Medical Center, September 18, 1977, something would happen that would change Darren Smith's life forever His mother, Natalie Smith gave birth to a baby boy, Darren's little brother little brother, Trevor Smith! Three years later, the inseparable brothers loved childish things such as going to the park, watching Sesame Street, and various cartoons. However nothing would come close to the thrill they felt while watching pro wrestling. Yet this would be the year Darren started his first day of Kindergarten. Not knowing what to do really, he took a seat next to a beautiful Hispanic-Caucasian young girl. She had a light seemingly more Caucasian complexion, long blonde hair with orange streaks which extended to her shoulders, pouty lips with crystal ice orange lipstick, and dazzling light blue eyes. Her name...Samantha Summers. Samantha and Darren became rather good friends as they sat together throughout entire day! Later in the school year on Valentine's Day, Darren would muster up his courage and ask Samantha to be his girlfriend. She delightfully accepted and two have been a couple ever since then! The next year when Trevor started Kindergarten, Darren was spent most of his time and attention on Samantha. In the meantime, a 1st Grader who was the same size as Trevor, but slightly stronger to due to the age difference would bully little Trevor. Within the week, Darren became aware of this. Darren approached the bully and a fight ensued. Darren beat the hell outta the kid so badly his parents had him transferred out of the school. Darren was punished for his by his parents, but realizing Trevor needed to defend himself and Darren needed to control his temper their father, Nathaniel Smith had the two enrolled in Tae Kwon Do classes! In their High School year, Darren and Samantha were still dating and Darren Trevor Smith both had reached brown belt. Darren played Freshman Football at Cardinal Spellman High School as the teams starting Halfback. He broke the school's record which is yet to be topped by running an amazing 4.0 on the Forty-Yard Dash! The team easily ram sacked all opposing competition, eventually winning the championships! However his studies never slowed. Darren and Trevor were both forced by their parents to take a IQ Test. Amazingly both scored a 160! Next year, Darren decided to stay on the team. However, this year Samantha, in an attempt to be around her man more often, tried on for the Cheerleading team and made it as Captain. Darren's younger brother, Trevor tried out and was accepted on the Freshman team as Starting Wide Receiver. Both lead their teams to numerous championships. With Trevor being the only available Smith brother and success on the Freshman Team caused him Trevor lost his virginity by his freshman year! He dated the entire Freshman Cheerleading Squad! However, by Darren's Senior as far as the competition in football league were concerned, the worst thing possible had happened! Trevor and Darren smith were both on the Varsity Team! That year the Spellman Falcons won the Catholic Archdiocese, City, and State Championships! Whereas Trevor spent more time on various women, Darren and Samantha studied. This hard work earned Darren Smith an outstanding 1560 in his SATs! The Beautiful, Samantha Summers would pull out a 1557. However pretty smart himself, Trevor Smith worked his way to a 1492! Considering the highest possible grade is 1600, all three did extremely well. At the Homecoming Game, Darren and Samantha were named respectively, Homecoming King and Queen. Darren and Samantha eventually graduated and went to Penn State college on Scholarships. The next year Trevor was accepted as well also on a Scholarship. During college however, the brothers didn't play football. Too many things were happening around them, mainly parties. As you can probably figure out by now, Trevor was big with the women. Most had hard schedules, but Trevor's mostly consisted of Monday-Brenda, Tuesday-Latisha, Wednesday-Linda, and Thursday-Felicia. Despite Trevor's busy schedule, the brothers were still studying Tae Kwon Do, but refused to take the test for Black Belt in order for their hands to remain unregistered as lethal weapons. During his junior year in college, Darren Smith and Samantha Summers began training under Wayne Carter, who was in retirement due to an injury. Wayne was unfortunately injured at the peak of his career. He was only 31. After the injury he opened a wrestling academy, named after himself, The Carter Academy. However, the TCA didn't have many gradates because Wayne Carter, being a former Marine used rather intense training on his students. Many students would drop out by a week! Trevor attended the year after. Trevor's ego would rub off on his older brother, perhaps making Darren as or even more arrogant that Trevor himself! However the brothers never could score a victory over one another. Reading a Marvel Comic, which sparked a spectacular mini series, that interested Darren, he had found his name, Onslaught! Trevor reading an issue himself was amazed by a character named Post. Trevor took the name for himself as a In Ring name. Samantha however, competed using her real name. Darren, Samantha, and Trevor all graduated from Penn State with exceptional grades. The three then began touring with Wayne and The Carter Academy. Onslaught founded some friends at TCA, who had also spawned names from the Onslaught Comic Saga. They were named HaVoK and Holocaust. Onslaught and Post were never ever able to beat each other, but with Post's tendency to constantly pose during matches, whereas Darren would leave such things out of the ring wouldn't favor to well with Wayne. This slight difference would lead to Onslaught becoming Wayne's best student and first graduate! Samantha, Post, HaVoK, and Holocaust all graduated soon after. In late 1999, Onslaught along with HaVoK, Post Holocaust and Samantha Summers were recruited up by the SCW Owner Michael Mason and were placed in a stable led by Onslaught called the nWo Gold and Black. Mr. Mason paid close attention to graduates of The Carter Academy because to graduate proved you were up there with the best. Out of respect of his mentor, Wayne Carter, Onslaught used the finisher his mentor used, Courage Required. Despite being signed to a lengthy contract, Onslaught and the other nWo members were also wrestling in other leagues at the time as well. Later in his career, Onslaught won his 2nd World Championship in the SCW and his 6th overall World Championship. Samantha reached success as well, becoming the SCW's first Women's Champion! She remains the only woman to stay undefeated in SCW or Pro Wrestling for that matter and win Women's Championship, twice in one federation! After that winning the SCW World Championship for a second time, Onslaught felt he was starting lived up to his mentor's aspirations as well his own, by dominating the federation in every possible way! Onslaught had the federations largest and unmatched fan base with the nWo Gold and Black sleeveless and Onslaught "Courage Required" t-shirts. Upon this success, commentators and interviewers began refer to him as "The Epitome of Greatness". Soon after, Onslaught then made SCW History upon being the only man to capture the SCW World Title for a 3rd Time! Onslaught and the rest of the nWo Gold and Black were not exclusive and the hottest thing in Wrestling! This an unmatched, unbelievable, and outstanding group captured 20+ Titles in a period from September to February! The stable even successfully took over the EAWF, declaring it their own. During their unremarkable reign during the Suicidal Championship Wrestling, problems with staff ensued and the federation was shut down in Mid-December. Between January and early March, Onslaught joined numerous failed federations while gaining titles along the way. Onslaught stumbled across XCW. He joined the federation with Post to see if the place would be good for nWo Gold and Black led by Onslaught to compete in. In his first match, he defeated Angel. After winning the Tag Team Titles with Post, by defeating Y2Jay and Icon, they were stripped of them few days later. After a week Onslaught was placed in a comatose angle for unknown reasons. He soon become weary of this and ran the Presidents, Colt, and Ken over with the nWo Monster Truck and resigned. Post decided to stay in the federation and with WCW pushing for money to use the nWo name due to the group's popularity, Onslaught disbanded the nWo Gold and Black. Onslaught was eventually found Not Guilty of all charges primarily because the trial was held in the state of New York, the state that Onslaught resides in where he as an abnormally high fan base. Although he never spent any time in jail, he was not allowed to leave New York leaving him unable to compete in the IML2 for a month. Onslaught would start his career in the IWO Leagues after being offered a contract from then IML2 President now IWO Vice President, Thomas Ford to begin competing in the IML2. President Ford would learn of the young superstar from receiving various recommendations by likes of IML2 Competitor and soon to be IWO Competitor, Silverchair, IWO Competitor, Former IML2 Vice President and Current IML President, Gunnar Smith, IWO Competitor, Phyre, and IML2 Competitor, JJ Nuclear. After entering the IML2, Onslaught quickly became one of the Top 10 Current Wrestlers in the IML2 during his short time there. His debut match would have him squaring off against the No. 1 Contender to the World Championship, Kyle Kash. Although Onslaught would lose the match, he proved he would be a force to be reckoned with by withstanding a never before seen or repeated 8 Too Late Facebusters (Pedigree) before he would finally go down for the 3 Count. Impressing the IML2 ''Suits'', in his second match, Onslaught would take on the IML2 World Champion ''Simply Perfect'' Shawn Arrows on Monday Night Melee, in what was a Non-World Title Match, Onslaught had handed Arrows his first defeat in 18 Months! Soon afterwards, IWO President John Maples closed the IML2 and IML3 in an effort to improve the IWO and IML and to make them more elite. Onslaught among with many other IML2 and IML3 Wrestlers was sent to the IML. However IML2 President Thomas Ford wasn't the only one who made this decision as did Shawn Arrows who had persuaded John to send Onslaught to the IML instead of the IWO as Arrows himself went to the IWO. Upon setting and epitomizing the term ''Greatness'' like no other before him, G.O.A.T., Incorporated, bestowed Onslaught with the earned right along with other African Americans such as Muhammad Ali (Boxing), and LL Cool J (Rapping) to call himself ''The Greatest Of All Time'' as representative of the field of Professional Wrestling! Onslaught entered the IML with a renewed rage. Onslaught made his presence widely known by defeating the likes of former IML2 Standouts, Geoff Paul and Mac D. and former Television Champions, Azrael Damodread or as Onslaught would call him, Azrael Damobread and Adam Wars. Soon after, Onslaught captured his first Championship in the IWO Leagues. Onslaught however didn't win gold for the champioship would come in the form of Platinum! Onslaught became the second and Last Internet Wrestling Organization Minor League Platinum Champion! Onslaught won the match in his homestate, the same place he as yet to lose a match, Madison Square Garden by defeating another Undefeated Man in Michael Nash. This became Nash's first and last title defense when Onslaught tossed him off a 50 Foot Scaffold. Nonetheless, to add insult to injury the losing stipulation would have Onslaught cover him in tar and feather him like a chicken! Onslaught would go on to defend the championship against anyone foolish enough to face him, including Dean Fiore and The Reaper. Destined for a World Championship, Onslaught's arrogance surely won him fans, but pissed off the Executive Board in the process. Due to this, he was suspended for a month. Without The Epitome of Greatness, on the cards, the IML went downhill and had it's first card on October 23th, 2000 since Onslaught was suspended which was September 30th, 2000! However in what the the IML Executive Board saw as Ulimited Arrogance, the IWO saw as Unlimited Potental. After finishing his month of suspension, just as soon as he would be reinstated back into the IML, he was promoted to the Internet Wrestling Organization itself! Onslaught would debut in the IWO at their biggest pay per view of the year, Ice Age 3 and go on to defeat former US Champion, Ash Robinson in the first ever Vertical Limit Match. Onslaught would establish himself that night as a force to be reckoned with. He continued to make empressive showings even one night on the January 5th edition of Hostile Takeover, Onslaught defeated Donnie Daze, Ash Robinson, and then United States Champion, Billy Ray in a Three On One Handicap Match! Despite his quick success, Onslaught grew weary of the political and backstage *censored* that happened in the IWO. Onslaught handed in his resignation to the federation. Once again, Onslaught found himself in a search for a new federation. Except this federation, wasn't so new. While Onslaught was competing in the IML, he was working at a upstart federation called Five Star Wrestling. FSW quickly grew to respected status in the wrestling community. However Onslaught parted ways with the company due to personal issues with some of the roster. He was contacted by Co-Owner Painkiller and Vice President Ryan Lockheart, that they were starting over and with more new faces than old among those accepted, Onslaught jumped at the chance. ''How Five Star Wrestling will be changed by this decision remains to be seen..'' Those were words that you were told upon his arrival, however is impact is growing more and more obvious with each growing day. At Five Star Wrestling first Pay Per View back, Love Hurts, Onslaught made quick work of "Five Star" Mike Marvelous and Former FSW World Champion, Epitome of Evil to become FSW's first Standard Bearer, Flagship, and World Heavyweight Champion of it's New Era. That night, Onslaught won his 10th World Heavyweight Championship and proposed to his longtime girlfriend, Samantha Summers. The two hugged as she said yes. At this moment, a date as yet to be worked out. What else lies in store for FSW is unknown, but one thing is for sure, Greatness has Arrived in Five Star Wrestling! Onslaught, which is what he has been handing out since becoming a wrestler. Onslaught is a 10 time World Heavyweight Champion, 4 time Intercontinental Champion, Platinum Champion, 2 time Tag Team Champion, and World Stable Champion! When Commententators, and Fans alike refer to distinguished superstar as ''The Epitome of Greatness'' and ''The Greatest Of All Time'' they aren't just blowing smoke. The distinction has definitely been earned. Even many of Onslaught's opponents have been hard pressed admit argue. Can You Stop the Onslaught? General Wrestling Style Innovative, All Rounder Finisher Courage Required - Onslaught often finishes his opponents off using unquestionably one of the most punishing submission holds to date. Onslaught applies an Elevated Boston Crab. Turning the victim over at the proper angle, Onslaught positions himself able to rest a knee across the head of opponent, making escape simply impossible! Power Moves Prelude to Extinction - The Prelude to Extinction is, definitely one of the most interesting and amazing maneuvers which has been broadcasted on television across the globe. At first glance, it appears to be a mere Pump Handle Slam, but when the opponent reaches his shoulder, Onslaught whips his opponent into the mat, head first by way of a Scooping Sit-down Piledriver aka Michinoku Driver II. Onslaught usually performs the Prelude to Extinction on opponents under 340 pounds to set them up for Courage Required. Catastrophic - Catastrophic is a without a doubt, a perfect fit into Onslaught's innovative breed of maneuvers. During a mid Somersault Hurricanranna better known as a Dragonranna, Onslaught swiftly slides off the shoulders his opponent to deliver a DDT. This move is also known in Japan as a Samurai Driver. Catastrophic is a favorite move of Onslaught, but Onslaught usually performs the move on opponents over 340 pounds to set them up for Courage Required. Swanton Bomb Seek and Destroy - Seek and Destroy is one of Onslaught's more punishing techniques. The move is usually set up by an Irish-whip or missed punch missed punch from the opponent. Onslaught applies a Downward Spiral position then whips the opponent over the hip, slamming the victim onto the back of their neck, while crashing down with the opponent as well. The move can be best described as a 180-Degree Swinging STO. No Remorse - No Remorse is a submission hold perhaps only seconded by Onslaught's own Courage Required. Onslaught lies the victim's body over the second rope and pulls their arms over the 3rd rope while digging the heels of his boots into the victim's lower back. Signature Moves Pancake Slam Consecutive Northern Lights Suplexes (3) Rolling Frog Splash Cradle Suplex - An outstanding suplex originated in Japan. Onslaught reaches under his opponents knee bringing the leg to the face of victim, trapping the victim's arm within the process. Onslaught wraps his free arm around his opponents neck, locking his hands behind the opponent. Onslaught then proceeds to tossing them overhead while falling backwards himself. Half Nelson Suplex - Another of Onslaught's outstanding, Japanese originated Suplexes. Onslaught places his opponent in a Half Nelson and wraps his free arm around the victim's gut. From that point, Onslaught tosses them overhead onto their neck while falling backwards himself. Springboard Asai Moonsault Single Arm Underhook Overhead Suplex Gory Lock Special - Onslaught lifts the opponent up over his shoulder so that the opponent is across their back and their upper back is across his shoulder. Onslaught tucks the opponent's legs around his hips so that the opponent's ankles rest on his thighs. From there Onslaught hooks the victim's arms with his own, applying pressure to the upper back of the opponent by pulling down. This move can be best applied while lifting the opponent into a Crucifix Powerbomb.
