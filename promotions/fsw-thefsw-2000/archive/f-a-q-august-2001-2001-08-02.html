F.A.Q. (August 2001)

2 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed's own questions and answers for people joining and wrestling in it.
|F.A.Q.|
Q: How many shows are there a week?
A: There are two shows a week. One on Fridays called Paranoia and one on Sunday called Short Comings for the midget wrestlers.

Q: What is a classic?
A: A classic are supercards that happenever 4 of 5 weeks. They are bigger then the Paranoia cards but not as big as Laurels. There are usually two classics in every term.

Q: What is a laurel?
A: Laurels are the big cards. The super super cards if you will. Laurel is another word for 'award' And if you are wrestling on a Laurel it's sort of an award. There are only four laurels every year as they end each term. To be main eventing a laurel means you are at the top of the FSW mountain..

Q: What is a term?
A: Just like school the FSW has terms. There are four terms a year. Each term lasts 13 weeks. And at the end of every term there is a Laurel.

Q: What is a cycle?
A: A cycle is a full year in the FSW. After a completetion of four terms a cycle will be complete. Unlike the calendar year we don't start in January. We start in Setpember as that's when the new FSW year starts. The FSW was started in August and since a 13 week period ends in August that will be the end of the FSW year.

Q: Why do you not want long roleplays?
A: Long roleplays are fine, but that's if they are entertaining. We don't want the typical 'you suck I'm going to kick your butt roleplayers' Instead we want roleplayers that develop your character and tell a story.

Q: What does "original wrestlers only" mean?
A: It means you can't be The Rock, Triple H, Jeff Jarret, or Scott Steiner. You have to create your own wrestler and your own gimmick.

Q: Will I be fired if I complain?
A: No. But if you keep complaing over and over again and especially why you lost then yes you will be fired.. If you have a problem with something just tell us what the problem is and we will try to fix it if we can.

Q: I should have won my match, that's not fair!
A: First you're warned, then you job, then you're fired! (We are not going to put up with people like that, we do not want people like that in our federation. If you lose and you honestly believe you should have won, we are probably setting up an angle for you or something of that manner.)

Q: Can you make me any images or webpages?
A: Sorry I can't. The FSW takes up a lot of my time and so I can't do work for other people.

Q: When is the deadline for roleplays?
A: 6:00 PM Central Time on the day of the event.

Q: When are you going to post the results?
A: When I get them done. We strive to post them before midnight on the day of the event.
