November 13, 2002 From American Airlines Center in Miami, Florida Glory opens with a close-up shot of Paul Orion. The CEO is sitting at his desk somewhere backstage at the American Airlines Center, his hands steepled on the fine oak surface. Orion: Good evening, and welcome to another fantastic episode of FSW Glory. Tonight, we have a number of amazing matches on tap, as well as one very special announcement. The entire wrestling world is abuzz with the pending PCW Alliances match, and that organization’s challenge to the other top federations in the business. [Grins] Tonight, FSW will answer that challenge, and announce which four men will represent Five Star Wrestling in this historic contest. The fans watching on the Jumbotron in the arena cheer wildly. An "F-S-DUB!" chant starts up, briefly forcing Orion to pause. A wolfish grin appears on the veteran’s face as he soaks in the syllables. Orion: Unlike SOME federations which can just name four talents without even making any tough choices, the FSW roster is stacked with worthy candidates to represent the federation at Alliances. As a result, we will hold a special lottery tonight to determine which four will carry the FSW banner. We are confident that whomever is chosen will represent Five Star Wrestling proudly, and honorably. Thank you, and enjoy our show! Orion smiles as the picture fades out. Briefly the FSW logo flashes up, then we fade to slow motion shots of FSW wrestlers performing their signature maneuvers. Glory is displayed along the top in golden script as in the background a church choir performs "Carmina Burana" by Carl Orff. Suddenly a fast forwarding screech is heard and the sound of the choir is replaced with "Lose Yourself" by Eminem. Wrestlers highlighted are Six Finger Cisco, Magnum Wolf, Jason O’Neil and Tyler Nelson. A dozen golden strobes storm around the internal section of the American Airlines Center like search lights, as the crowd goes apeshit. Nelson, Ming, and Chase toss Timo from the ring and prepare for the oncoming Untouchable second wave. Lynn: I DON’T KNOW HOW THE HELL THEY GOT HERE BUT…..KILL THOSE MOB RULE BASTARDS!! Cassius slides in under the bottom rope while Afwa and Azala take a more conventional route into the ring, stepping through the ropes. Nelson, Ming, and Chase meet Afwa, Cassius, and Zameer respectively and the six men stand toe to toe in battle. Nelson is quickly overpowered by the much bigger Afwa, the same with Chase and Zameer. Those four fight in opposite corners as Deamont and Ming wage war in the center. Afwa tries to choke the life out of Nelson while Zameer bits the forehead of Chase, causing blood to start down his face. Sam: Who let the fucking natives in? Cassius ducks under a wild haymaker right hand from Ming which spins The Mingster around. Deamont locks Ming up and raises him for an atomic drop. As he drops Ming down on his knee Deamont is blasted in the face by a steel chair shot from Angelo Deville. Deamont collapses to the mat. OG is in the ring as well and hammers Afwa over the head with a chair as well. The huge Samoan doesn’t feel the first shot, but the second one drops him to his knees. Grant looks at what is left of the chair, then bends it fully over Afwa’s head with another vicious shot. Deamont walks over and taps Zameer on the shoulder as he is pummeling Chase in the corner. Azala turns around and eats a mouth full of steel chair for his troubles. The shot dazes Zameer enough for Grant to charge in and land a huge splash on Azala in the corner. Chase spits blood on Azala as he falls to the mat. Lynn: The injuries from earlier in the night and the sheer numbers are just too much on this night for The Untouchables to handle. Sam: This is going to be the norm around here, Lynn. The Mob will Rule no matter what. Steven: Certainly doesn’t look very good at this point for your heroes, Lynn. Marvelous tries to climb back into the ring but is greeted by a chair shot from Deville. Nelson and Ming bring Timo to his feet and Nelson executes Seeing Six Stars. The members of Mob Rule land a few unneeded kicks to their downed opponents, then stand over them confidently. They smirk to each other and scoff at their opponents laying in the ring. Nelson wipes a stream of blood from the corner of his mouth as he glares down at Timo. Lynn: This has got to be one of the worst evenings I have been a part of in my announcing career. The five men standing in the ring are nothing more than a pack of hyenas that need to be shot. Sam: I love it! Mob Rule, standing tall! Get used to it, folks! These men... are gonna DOMINATE Five Star Wrestling! Mob Rule grandstands in the ring over the fallen heroes as the show fades to black.
