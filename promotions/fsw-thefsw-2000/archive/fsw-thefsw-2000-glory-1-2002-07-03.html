FSW — Card

Glory 1, 3 July 2002

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Words flash upon the screen, as a voiceover begins. “It started as a dream. The dream to make the top wrestling promotion in the world. One not of “top” quality, but one of Five Star quality. Thus was the birth of Five Star Wrestling. FSW quickly grew into one of the top promotions around, accompanied by a bevy of talent.” Pictures begin to flash with names. Johnny Mayhem Token Weed Creature Jacob Jackson Thrash Mike Marvelous “Undoubtedly Five Star Wrestling was the zenith of sports entertainment. The top promotion, the top wrestlers, the top executives. Unfortunately all good things must come to an end, and FSW made a drastic turn of events, and shut its doors…” UNTIL NOW! “The Boys are Back in Town” drowns out the raucous Madison Square Garden crowd as Thin Lizzy blasts over the arena PA system. The camera begins grabbing shots of the FSW faithful. The first is of a group of teenage girls holding up a poser of tonight’s main-eventer, former FSW World Champ Big Daveylicious. The poster has Big D wearing a green hat with a clover in it and standing next to a pot of gold as it reads “He’s Magically Daveylicious.” The next shot is of a man wearing a shirt of hometown hero Jack Brooze, and holding up a sign that reads “It’s About Damn Time!” The third shot is of a group of bucktoothed people in only overalls who apparently piled into the jalopy to make the drive up to New York from West Virginia to support “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey. One of the “sons of the soil” holds up a large sign which reads “Win one for Cousin Merle, Jesse, ‘Fore He Done Goes to Heaven” It isn’t soon enough before the camera switches from the Ramey family and settles on the ring, where FSW ring announcer Willy Wilson stands, clad in a tuxedo, holding a microphone. Willy Wilson: Ladies aaaaaaaand gentleman! Please help me welcome back Five…Star…WRESTLING! The crowd erupts in cheers and begins chanting. Crowd: FSW! FSW! FSW! FSW! FSW! Willy Wilson: On behalf of Five Star Wrestling, I would like to thank each and every one of you for coming out here tonight! Hopefully we can make tonight’s show everything you’ve come to expect and then some! The crowd’s cheer continues. Willy Wilson: Now without further ado, let me introduce to you your commentators for tonight, and for all the future Glories! First, the play by play commentator for the FSW announce team, he is, Lynn…..Lane! The crowd erupts for the original FSW play by play caller. Lane walks out from the back and waves to the crowd. He walks down the aisle, slapping hands with the fans, before taking his place at the announce table. Wilson: Now to introduce the man who will be illuminating the situation on each and every match, Sam……Collins! The cheers quickly resolve into boos for the long-time FSW color man. Collins struts out from the back, giving a sarcastic smirk and a mocking wave to the fans in attendance. He then strolls down the ring entrance without so much as acknowledging the fans on either side as he takes his rightful place at the FSW announce table. Wilson: And now for tonight’s third announcer… The crowd grows quieter as Wilson says this. No doubt in wonder of who the third man could be, as they are accustomed to the customary two FSW announcers. Wilson: A former member of Primetime Championship Wrestling… Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOO! Wilson: He is a former PCW Personnel Director AND Television Champ-een! Tonight’s third announcer…Tim….WALDEN! The crowd cheers as “Right Now” by SR-71 hits the announce system and the man who was unjustly fired from PCW runs out. He smiles and pumps his fist as he jogs down the entrance ramp to the announce table, high-fiving fans along the way. Wilson: Those are tonight’s commentators ladies and gentleman. Let’s get ready for a night of Glory! Cheers continue from the wild New York crowd as Willy climbs out of the ring, turning the reigns over to the FSW announce team. Lynn: HELLO EVERYONE AND WELCOME TO THE FIRST EDITION OF GLORY IN THIS RUN OF FSW! I just have to say fellas, that it’s great to be back. Sam: I wish I could say the same, but I am still stuck with you as a partner. Lynn: I missed you too Sam. Nonetheless, my colleague and I are joined by a man who was a mainstay in PCW for so very long, before he was so hurtfully cast aside, Tim Walden! Tim: Thanks Lynn, it’s great to be somewhere where I’m appreciated, and not trapped under the rueful ruling fist of that tyrant Tyler Nelson. But I’m just glad I’m out of the nightmare that is PCW, and finally in a good promotion. Walden stands up from his chair and begins and FSW chant. The crowd erupts at this display and quickly joins him. Crowd: FSW! FSW! FSW! FSW! Walden smiles as he sits down. Sam: So Tim, are you going to be a loser here too, like you were in PCW? Or just a pathetic excuse for a commentator. Tim: I’m just sitting here right now aren’t I Sam? Sam: Well either way is a disgrace, but I suppose you can do less harm with a mic as opposed to in the ring. Lynn: Ladies and gentleman we have an action-packed card for you tonight! In our opening match inaugural RPGod tournament runner-up Chris Bagwell will take on an old FSW favorite, Jack Brooze! Sam: Yeah, and he should have won it too! Lynn: Are you kidding? Our very own Johnny Mayhem mopped the floor with him! Sam: Mayhem was a Nancy boy, and didn’t deserve anything that he got. At least Bagwell is still a God compared to Brooze. Lynn: Nonetheless, we also have an exciting triple-threat match planned between “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey, Asa Fountain, and Ming! Sam: Ming is the King when it comes to weaseling out victories, he’ll have something planned. Tim: I beg to differ Sam. I know all three of these competitors rather well, and I’d have to say they are all pretty evenly matched, and any one of them could pull out the victory. Lynn: Also slated is a match between former WWCF World Champion Tyler Davidson and another old school FSW member, Leon Chambers. Sam: Chambers will mop the floor with that small-time piece of trash. This is Five Star Wrestling, not BCWF. Lynn: BCWF? Sam: Backwater Crappy Wrestling Federation. Lynn groans. Lynn: We just got here and you’re already making an ass of yourself. Collins smiles. Sam: It’s what I do best. Lynn: The next match of the evening will be between newcomer Chris Irving, known as the “Canadian Criminal,” and a mystery opponent. Sam: It better not be some lamewad. Lynn: Well we are all in the dark as to who the man, or even woman, is. It could be anybody! Tim: Yeah! Even me! Sam: I said it better not be some lamewad Walden! Lynn: And as for our main-event, Johnny Raindance takes on former FSW World Champion Big Daveylicious! Tim: Fans outside of the AWS still haven’t seen JRD’s potential tapped. It should be an exciting match, and hopefully Johnny shows us some of what made him so talked about in his tenure here in FSW. Sam: Are you joking Walden? Lamedance is a joke. He’s not even a has-been, he’s a never-was. Big Daveylicious, the man who is carrying the FSW torch will mop the floor with him. Lynn: However before our first match is underway, I am told we are going to here from our new CEO. And to be honest, many things have been kept under wraps after the change of ownership in FSW, so I have no idea who this person will be. Sam: I do, they tell the important people everything. Lynn: You do not, shut up. Sam: I do too! Lynn: Who is it then? Sam: …..it’s a secret. Lynn: Bah! Wait, wait, here he comes! "Jumpin' Jack Flash" by The Rolling Stones begins to play on the sound system. A few moments later, a tall, lithe man steps out into the arena. Tim: HEY! That's... Lynn: No way! He wears a tan corduroy suit and burnished leather cowboy boots. He has long, white hair tied back in a ponytail and a handlebar moustache of the same color. Sam: Who the hell is THAT old guy? Sam's description of "old" fits this man pretty well, as he indeed looks to be rather aged. Lynn: Show some respect... That's the Gunslinger! Sam: WHO?! Lynn: "The Gunslinger" Paul Orion! That man is one of the FOUNDING FATHERS of wrestling! Tim: Anyone who's studied the history of wrestling knows that man's face! Guys like him, Ric Flair, "Classy" Freddie Blassie, Lou Thesz, Gorilla Monsoon, Killer Kowalski...That man is a true LEGEND of the business! The old cowboy enters the ring and stands there, almost seeming to carry an air of respect around him. He pulls a mic out of his suit jacket. "The Gunslinger" Paul Orion: About a month ago, the long-closed Five Star Wrestling was purchased by a global entertainment conglomerate, the Yokimishu Fujiwara Corporation, based in Southeast Asia. It was around that time I got a call from one of their representatives, asking me to fly all the way to their headquarters, so I could be offered an opportunity that "I would regret not taking". After a little persuasive banter from the caller, I was on the next flight to Shanghai, where I met with the corporation's board of executives. Not long thereafter, I signed a contract... My name is "The Gunslinger" Paul Orion and I am the CEO of Five Star Wrestling. The crowd absolutely EXPLODES upon the announcement that the wrestling veteran will be in charge of FSW. Lynn: Holy shit!! Ladies and Gentlemen, our new CEO is none other than one of the true founding fathers of this industry, "The Gunslinger" Paul Orion! Sam: God, that old fart is gonna drive us into the freakin' ground. Orion: I'll tell you all right off the bat...I've never done this before, so you're all going to have to bear with me. Sam: See?! Orion: I've been in this business for a long, long time. Back when today's stars weren't even rookies, and yesterday's stars were still getting established. I've seen the good and the bad. I know what kind of hell this business can be. The wrestlers can count on one thing, and that is that unlike other CEOs there have been...I know what it's like, and I can promise that the wrestlers of FSW WILL be treated FAIRLY. The crowd cheers for his integrity. Tim: Well it's about time! Orion: And what promises will I make to you, the fans? Well, it seems that the new trend these days is for things to be "Old School". Though I agree that things were better back in...heh...the day, I can't say that anyone has been very successful in attaining wrestling perfection through going old school. Well I tell you all this. Five Star Wrestling will not be a watered down version of the golden days, nor will I try to turn it into some stupid gimmick federation, another fad out there... Five Star Wrestling, under my leadership, will be just that. WRESTLING. There is another burst of adoration for the old cowboy in the ring. Orion: FSW is gonna get a healthy dose of what this business is ALL ABOUT. We're gonna go back to what made it all work, and we're gonna KICK ASS while we're doing it. This old cowboy still knows how to get the job done, and dammit, I'm gonna make FSW what it should be or I'll DIE TRYING! Lynn: YEAH! Orion: I'm not the type to waste mic time, so I'm gonna head on back to my office now. Enjoy the show, folks. Lynn: I am still in shock! Our CEO is the legendary Paul Orion! “The Gunslinger” himself! Tim: This is going to be great for FSW! I can’t believe Paul Orion is here! He was my idol growing up, and one of the men I studied in becoming a wrestler! Sam: He’s an old windbag who will bore us to death, which isn’t far off for him by the way! Lynn: Excuse my partner’s ignorance folks. The man, the myth, the legend, “The Gunslinger” Paul Orion is the new CEO of Five Star Wrestling! We have to head to commercial, when we return we’ll have our first match of the evening! Five Star Wrestling is brought to you by Ford, maker of the Windstar, the only mini-van with a five star safety rating. Ford Windstar, five stars in safety. FSW, five stars in recklessness. Why plug them together then? Hey, when you’re offered millions for marketing you don’t turn it down. As we return the scene is outside of a dressing room. The placard on the door reads “Ming” in large gold letters. The camera walks inside the room slowly, revealing a luxuriously appointed locker room. Leather couches line the walls, seafood platters are arrayed on a glass table near the middle of the room, and a fine Persian rug covers the floor. In essence, it is obvious Ming paid a lot for this seeing as he has by no means earned such a dressing room based on his record with the company. Ming is lounging on a reclined leather chair, facing another man who is lying on one of the couches. This man is dressed conservatively in a black three-piece suit and wears small wire frame glasses that accentuate his hawk like nose, underneath which a finely groomed mustache rests. In fact, this man is the spitting image of another well-known man of the couch, Dr. Sigmund Freud. Ming: Well then Dr. Lloyd. I am delighted to see you decided to answer my summons upon such short notice. Lloyd: Well at the price you’re paying me I’m happy to oblige. Ming: As, well said. I’m afraid I’ve forgotten my manners Doctor. Can I get you anything? A drink perhaps? Lloyd: Thank you Ming, but no, I’d prefer to get straight to business. Ming: A man after my own heart. Straight to business it is. Are you aware of my particular situation? Lloyd: Situation sir? Ming: Yes. Tonight marks my return to the world of wrestling after a nine-month hiatus. As you can imagine, I would be very pleased indeed were it to be a pleasant night… if you catch my drift. Lloyd: I think so sir. But Ming, I’m just a psychologist. I can’t imagine that I could be of any help to you tonight. I don’t know anything about wrestling, much less the act of physical violence it would seem you are propositioning me to commit… Ming: No, no, you have it all wrong. I don’t want you to attack them physically, if I wanted that id just hire some brainless muscle for far cheaper than the consulting fee I’m paying you. No, I want you to assault them mentally. You see, one of my opponents, Jesse Ramey, is a drunken schizophrenic. I want you to… encourage this deviant behavior. With a little luck he will become completely incapacitated leaving me to focus my huge amount of energy on that insignificant bug, Asa. Lloyd: Ming, be serious, how could I possibly do that in this limited amount of time? If I understand correctly, there is only about 15 minutes until your match. If you had solicited me sooner this plan might possibly succeed, but as it is it is simply the mindless babblings of a buffoon who knows nothing of the science of the mind! Ming: GOD DAMNIT! I waste all this effort trying to be nice to you, using all this stupid “haute couture” language, I pay you an obscene amount, and that’s all you can give me? An insult? Listen up Lloyd, if that is your real name. You’re going to listen to my plan, and you’re going to damn well like it! Ming pulls out a notepad and starts scribbling and talking to a cowering Dr. Lloyd as the camera fades back to the announce team. Lynn: What kind of hair-brained scheme does that loudmouth have planned now? Sam: Whatever it is, I’m sure it’s brilliant and devious. Ming has famous relatives in the art of cooking things up you know. Lynn: Of course, members of the Ming dynasty. Sam: Ming dynasty? I was talking about the guy who runs the Chinese place over on 42nd. Lynn and Tim share a sigh. Lynn: Without further ado, it’s time for our first match of the evening! Sam: It’s too bad there had to be people like Brooze accepted that ruin the credibility of FSW. Lynn: Jack Brooze has a lot of heart and determination, I see no way in which he ruins the credibility by being an FSW mainstay! Sam: He sucks. Lynn (groaning): Let’s head to the ring. Willy Wilson: Ladies and gentleman, the first match of the evening is a singles match and is set for one fall! Coming to the ring first…from Newark, NJ, weighing in at 268 pounds…CHRIS…..BAGWELL!!! "Fight Music" by D12 blares over the public announce speakers. The arena lights black and a faded blue haze sets through the air. As a heavy beat picks up, an explosion hits the stage, bringing Chris Bagwell out from behind the curtain. Bagwell walks out and stands at the top of the entrance, arms outstretched. He flexes every muscle in his body, causing it to ripple. He then strolls to the ring, crawling in and raising his hand. As he snaps it down another explosion goes off, startling Willy. Lynn: What an entrance to get FSW started off right! Sam: Too bad Brooze has to come out now and defile it. Willy: Introducing next, from Brooklyn, NEW YORK… The crowd erupts and begins chanting the hometown FSW multi-time champion’s name. Crowd: BROOZE! BROOZE! BROOZE! BROOZE! Willy: Weighing in at a stout 265 pounds…JACK….BROOOOOOZE! The crowd’s cheers grow louder as the lights drop and four blue spotlights scan the area. An explosion of blue pyros is set off at the top of the entrance and “Testify” by Rage Against the Machine blares onto the arena sound system. As the spotlights all settle on the same place at the top of the entrance, the crowd grows deafening as there stands Jack Brooze, holding up his trademark nine-iron. He sprints down to the ring and dives in, climbing the turnbuckle and thrusting the golf club above his head to the delight of the crowd. The lights return as Brooze hops down and dispenses of the nine-iron, as the bell to start the match rings. Bagwell and Brooze begin circling each other, looking for an opportunity to attack and sizing the other up. Moving in ever-shrinking circles, they eventually meet up in the middle of the ring and lock up. Both men lobby for the upperhand for several seconds, then break the grapple and glare at one another. Tim: Looks like size isn't the only thing they have in common! Sam: You're crazy! Bagwell is just playing around... The two men lunge forward to lock up once more, but Bagwell ducks under Brooze's arms at the last second and hooks him around the waist from behind. Before Brooze has a chance to react, Bagwell plants him on the canvas with a belly-to-back suplex. Sam: That a boy Chris!!! Tim: Bagwell should have won the first RPGod, and he’s showing why here! Bagwell leaps to his feet and smiles down at Brooze, who is keeping a wary eye on him as he slowly climbs up to a vertical position. As he gets to his feet, Brooze suddenly charges Bagwell with an arm outstretched for a clothesline, but again the other man is too quick. Dropping to the canvas, Bagwell hooks Brooze's legs with his own and causes him to do a faceplant into the canvas, then scrambles over and locks him into an ankle-lock. Lynn: This could be over already! Sam: Let’s hope so, I’m sick of watching Brooze wrestle already. The referee gets down in Brooze's face and informs him how pleasant submitting would be, but the man manages to resist the temptation long enough to reach the ropes. Grudgingly, Bagwell releases the hold and gets to his feet, pulling Brooze up and flinging him into the ropes with an irish whip. As Brooze comes hobbling back, Bagwell lowers his head for a backdrop. Lynn: Bagwell made a miscalculation there! Confirming the announcer's call, Brooze pulls up short and drops an elbow on the back of Bagwell's neck, knocking him to the mat. Tim: Damn... That's gotta hurt. Bagwell, the fresher of the two, manages to get to his feet first and throws a roundhouse at Brooze, but Brooze blocks the shot and returns one of his own. Then another. And another. Brooze pummels him all the way across the ring to the ropes, then clotheslines him over the top. Bagwell lands on his head on the outside and lay there motionless. Lynn: Brooze showing some good ol’ FSW pride to his hometown! Sam: Brooze embodies everything that was BAD about FSW! Save that comment until we get to Big D’s match! Tim: Sam, I think if you ask anyone here, it’s YOU that embodies everything bad about FSW. Lynn: Amen. The referee runs over and starts counting Bagwell out while Brooze limps to the furthest corner of the ring and rests against it. Ref: One! ...Two! Tim: Wow... Looks like these guys were slacking off during the wait. There's no excuse for this type of exhaustion already! Ref: Three! Lynn: Bagwell landed on his head, Walden. Maybe someone should check on him. Ref: Four! Bagwell finally gives his head a shake and climbs to his feet with the help of the ring apron. He shoots a glare at the ref holding his hands up and getting ready to call five, then rolls into the ring and jumps to his feet. Looking a little more refreshed and steady on his feet, Brooze launches out of the corner. Tim: ‘Bout time! Lynn: Brooze is playing it smart here! Sam: More than I can say for Walden. The two wrestlers lock up and begin struggling for the advantage. Brooze gets it first and pushes Bagwell back into the corner, then nails him with a chop across the chest… Tim and Sam: Whooo! Brooze follows it up with another vicious chop, and another, then pulls Bagwell out of the corner and tries to whip him across the ring. Bagwell plants his feet hard and reverses the whip, then follows Brooze into the corner and rocks him with a huge clothesline. Brooze slumps back against the ropes and Bagwell begins nailing him with kicks to the stomach. Sam: Stomp him like the maggot he is Chris! Tim: Someone should stop you like the fa- Lynn: TIM! Tim: Nevermind. Bagwell then turns his back on Brooze and begins pandering to the crowd. They begin to boo him at his brashness, so Bagwell shrugs them off and turns his attention back to Brooze and walks back to the corner. He grabs Brooze by the hair and begins pulling him up by his hair, but a thumb jerks up and pokes him in the eye. Holding his face, Bagwell turns and staggers away, and Brooze seizes the opportunity to run up behind him and catch him in a full nelson... Which he turns into a full nelson slam! Tim: Brooze almost put him through the ring! Sam: Illegal! Lynn: How? Sam: Um… Tim: We’re waaaaiting. Sam: I’ll think of something. Bagwell admirably starts moving right away, trying to get to his feet, but Brooze intercepts him halfway and plants him with a DDT. Suddenly the lights in the arena fade to black as “Courage” by Alien Ant Farm comes on over the announce system. Lynn: Who’s coming out here and interrupting this match!?! Sam: Yeah! Bagwell wasn’t done taking Brooze to school yet! Tim: You know darn well Brooze was holding his own in there! The crowd is in a buzz, waiting to see who will come out. Upset that the match has been interrupted, but anxious to see whom the next FSW superstar to make an appearance will be. As the chorus hits, the lights come on in reds and blues. On the jumbotron, a video package begins to play. At the entrance ramp, a figure walks onto the stage. He’s standing about 6’7”, and is ripped to say the least. His long black hair falls down to his shoulders. As the lights return to normal, the fans get their first real glimpse of him. He’s wearing a pair of black brief-style trunks. On the back is what seems to be the letters “MW”. Lynn: That’s Magnum Wolf! What is he doing out here? Sam: Saving this match I hope. Magnum Wolf: Greetings fans of Five Star Wrestling! Welcome to our first show! Ok, so there was an incarnation of this great federation once before this, but I was not involved, and therefore, it doesn’t matter a bit! Hahaha! The fans begin to boo at Magnum’s downplaying of the legend of FSW. Magnum: Anyway! As you all know, my name is Magnum Wolf, and I’ll be your resident superstar for the next, oh I’d say about, year and a half. That’s usually how long it takes the competition to realize that I’m the biggest thing going, and that they’re going to need me in order to get the ratings they believe they deserve. The boos continue and smatterings of “You Suck” can be heard. Magnum Wolf: I know, I know! You’re all saying to yourself, ‘Hey Magnum! Why would you be here in FSW when you’re already a 13 time EOW champion? Why would you need to be involved with these guys after you’ve already knocked off some of the top names on some of the top PTC networks? Why bother with FSW when you have already gotten into hall of fames in EOW, EGW, AWF, ACW, and FWW?’ Well, loyal fans of everything that is Magnum Wolf, I will tell you why I’m here. I’ve done it all, right? In EOW, I was the only man to be a Grand Slam Champion. In FWW, I was the only man to go undefeated for an entire year. In EGW, I successfully won a tag team battle royal by myself. I single handedly pinned almost every member of the LCW-Mercury roster. I was chosen as one of the 64 men who participated in the third annual RPGod tournament. Yep. I’ve done it all. You all know my name… everyone in the back knows my name. Everyone on the Internet knows my name. But the one thing I haven’t done? Lynn: Win a match in a promotion that matters? Sam: Hey! Did you not just here what the man said? THIRTEEN time EOW champion! That’s a helluva lot better than being a one time Television Champion, multi-time loser like Walden. Tim: Hey! Magnum: Nothing. There’s nothing I haven’t done. So why am I here, you ask again? Why the hell not? I had so much success in LCW-Mercury, AWF, and every other place I’ve been to, that I will here too. FSW is almost a joke, and I didn’t even come here. But I figured it’d be just more easy pickings for me and more awards, and another Hall of Fame induction. Lynn: Not likely! This is Five Star Wrestling! Where it takes a little more to be a champion! Sam: Did you get that off of a clothing commercial? Tim: Maybe if the guy was in PCW, he’d automatically have success, but not here! In a fit of rage at the denouncement of Five Star Wrestling, Jack Brooze now has a microphone. Brooze: Hey punk! You think you can come out here and rag on MY fed? I don’t think so! Nothing is given in FSW, you have to EARN everything that comes to you! Wolf, I was winning FSW titles back when you were still nursing at your mother’s teet! Lynn: Amen Jack! Sam: Hey! Don’t interrupt greatness Brooze! Magnum: Ah yes. Here we have a perfect example fans, of why I will shoot straight to the top of FSW. It was people like this doofus that the promotion was built around. It’s a wonder FSW has been around as long as it has with wrestlers like this. Wait, that must be why it closed twice! The crowd showers Wolf with boos. Meanwhile in the ring, Bagwell has recovered completely and is sneaking up behind Brooze. Magnum: Luckily now that I’m here, FSW can finally stay open and reach the pinnacle of the wrestling business. I’ve done it with so many other promotions, I can do it with this one! Even with people like this dolt bringing it down! Lynn: Don’t let him talk to you like that Jack! Sam: Why not? If Magnum Wolf doesn’t call Brooze a loser, someone else will. Tim: You’re a sad, bitter little man Sam. Brooze: You want me to show you what FSW is all about? How ‘bout I come down there an-WHOA! Chris Bagwell takes the opportunity of Magnum Wolf distracting Brooze to roll him up with a schoolboy. Lynn: This match is still going on! Tim: Hey! Not fair! Sam: Yes it is you doof! He simply took advantage of the situation! 1…2….3! Winner: Chris Bagwell Lynn: Chris Bagwell has one this match thanks to Magnum Wolf distracting Jack Brooze! Sam: Well maybe if Brooze had kept his eyes on the prize he wouldn’t have lost. He should take some Tae-Kwon-Do classes and learn how to focus. Bagwell slides out of the ring and raises his arms in the air in celebration. Brooze shoots up immediately in anger and glares at Magnum Wolf, who simply laughs. Magnum: You see? If this is the kind of competition FSW offers, it will just be another promotion to cement my stake at greatness! Thank you all, you’ve been great! Magnum Wolf rescinds to the back as “Fight Music” by D12 blasts over the arena speakers signaling Bagwell’s victory. Brooze rips through the ring ropes to get out and stalks to the back in anger. Lynn: Brooze not too happy with the outcome! Sam: Losers never are. You’d think they’d be used to it, but they aren’t. Don’t you get used to it Tim? Tim: How about I smack some of that sass outta your mouth. Sam: How about I sue your pants off. Lynn: Children, please, stop your bickering and watch the segment like good little monkies. The camera cuts to Ming sitting in his locker room. He checks his watch, arches his eyebrows, smiles, then rises from his reclined position and exits the room. Ming: Five minutes until my match. Lloyd had better have done his job. I swear, you can’t find good help these days. Still, I suppose I’ll take any help I can get. Asa alone? No sweat, that stoner doesn’t deserve to bask in my glorious presence much less cause me to fear for my perfect win loss record. But as for Asa and Ramey together? That’s when things get a little hairy. Ming stops suddenly as he passes Ramey’s locker room. Ming: I wonder if how the good doctor is doing? I think I’ll take a little peek… Ming slowly opens the door. Ming: Hello? Hey, why are all the lights off… The thin Chinese man slowly makes his way into the room. Suddenly, a dark shape flashes across the screen and slams the door. Screams and crashes can be heard from within the room. Ming’s voice can be heard above the horrendous racket Ming: NOOOO! IT’S NOT TRUE! IT’S NOT TRUE! Lloyd: Yes Ming, it is. You are not rich, you are not charming, and you are not a sexy, sexy, oh so sexy man! You are merely… average! Ming: AAAAAARRRGGGHHHH! The door flies open and Ming rushes out of the room chased by the vengeful Doctor Lloyd. Lloyd: Face the facts Ming; you aren’t any better than anyone else! YOU ARE NORMAL! Sure...some men in this ring may have their differences with each other from our days in FSW, but we are all prepared to band together to drive you people out! The fans continue the booing and throw stuff in the ring. Mike Marvelous: And who's going to stop us!?! Johnny Rainance? Mike looks over at the fallen JRD. Mike: Not bloody likely! “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey? HA! Tim Walden? Tim Walden my heiney! NO ONE! NO ONE CAN STOP US BE- Suddenly the crowd erupts as a dark skinned man with a long pony tail, wielding a chair hits the ring. Lynn: TIMO BOLAMBA!!! TIMO BOLAMBA IS HERE IN FIVE STAR WRESTLING!!! Sam: He ruined a perfectly good promo! Mike quickly leaps out of the ring, out of the reach of Timo’s chair. EOE and Leon Chambers see this too and bail out as well. Lynn: Timo is cleaning house!!! Big D turns and finally sees the chair-wielding Samoan. JRD has finally come to and shakes the cobwebs off just as Timo charges at Big D. Daveylicious ducks out of the way and out of the ring just as Timo swings. All JRD can see is Timo Bolamba coming at him before he is absolutely obliterated with the chair. Lynn: OH NO!!! Timo just cleaned JRD’s clock with that chair on accident!!! Sam: Accident my ass!!! Timo did that on purpose!!! Lynn: The hell he did!!! Sam: Welcome to our team Timo!!! Mike Marvelous is laughing outside the ring at the events that have unfolded as Timo holds the ring ropes apart, beckoning for someone to enter. Mike scoffs and turns, leading the other original FSWers to the back. Timo drops the chair and turns back to the unmoving body of Johnny Raindance, crouching over it as the FSW Logo ©2002 is displayed on the bottom of the screen.
