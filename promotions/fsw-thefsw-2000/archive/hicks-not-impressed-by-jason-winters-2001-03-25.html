|Hicks Not Impressed By Jason Winters|
IP: [detail withheld]

[Note: The following is OBVIOUSLY meant for humorous purposes. DO NOT take it the wrong way.]

Upon viewing of Jason Winters' first real on-camera promo for Evil Intentions, the American Society of Hillbillies and Hicks has deemed that one of his comments was gratuitous and unnecessary, or, as they put it, "it twasnt right none."

This comes at a time when Hillbilly-Hick/Normal People tensions are at an all-time high, when most Normal People insult the people who live in Arkansas and Missouri. Most of the citizens in the area say that the comments of all non-Hillbilly Americans "taint proper fur the nashnul good."

Of course, most people don't care, and find that it's all in good fun, much in the way that Americans freely insult Canadians, Mexicans, Polacks, Frenchmen, Russians, and everyone else in the world in the same humor.

Ironically, the quotes mentioned were not actually said by Jason Winters. Winters' narrator, who seems to follow him and make very inflammatory comments made the jokes about the Sons of the Soil. Yet, that just cements the case the narrator presents.

What is your take on the matter? Nightline will air a special devoted entirely to Jason Winters' (Narrator's) comments minutes before Evil Intensions, and outside of Madison Square Garden, there will be protesting with signs that say "Haill No, Winters Must Go, Yall."

Whatever your take, Winters is already becoming a center of attention in FSW.

--FSW Person
The Person...of FSW.
