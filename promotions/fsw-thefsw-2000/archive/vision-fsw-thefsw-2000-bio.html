Vision Biography Height 6'8" Weight 312 lbs Hometown Raleigh, North Carolina Date of Birth May 22, 1972 Alignment Neutral Entrance Music Da Rockwilder - Method Man and Redman (Instrumental Version) Manager/Valet None Personality He's usually impatient. He wants to settle things when he wants to. Has a confident but not completely cocky attitude. Has some book smarts which he uses to out-wit his opponent in a match by tricking them into certain things. Bad habits include ignoring others(including the boss), breaking property when he is upset. His strategy in a match seems to be pure offensive, which sometimes gets him in trouble against bigger men. Acts as if he was a heel but the crowd seems to like him that way. History Vision, or Eric Moore grew up in Raleigh North Carolina. As a child he seemed to be book smart and physically in shape. At age 11 he was already 5'4"..He worked even at age 13 in a store with his grandfather. His grandparents had always influenced him in life. He enjoyed doing various jobs for his grandfather in the store. The store was a bread store, plain and simple. Only thing there that wasn't really bread was the drinks in the coolers. Vision enjoyed this small job. He wouldn't even call it a job. He stacked bread a little, handled the register..Everything seemed perfect in Eric's little world. Suddenly his parents had divorced. He was quite upset about it, but got over it eventually. Eric seemed to stray away from his mother and end up with his father. Eric's father eventually found his way into another relationship, a woman from San Antonio Texas. Eric moved with his father to the lone star state. He was sad to leave all his family and relatives in this one state, but he also wanted to stay with his father. Eric expected much from San Antonio, he thought it would be very fun, which it was, at first. Then Eric started going to school for some time. He didn't really get to know many people, as they seemed to not like the fact that Vision was a white male. Hispanic children taunted and teased Eric for no particular reason. Eric didn't really care, he just shrugged it off. One day the "school bully" decided to come up to Eric and ask him for money. Eric ignored him. The bully proceeded to keep asking and even dig into Eric's coat pocket. The bully crapped about 4 dollars from Eric. Eric then got furious and decked the bully. Eric was beating the bully to a pulp when suddenly the bully pulls out a pencil and jams it into Eric's right eye. Eric was escorted to the hospital where he was told that he would no longer have eyesight through his right eye, and lucky that he lived. After he came back to school Eric thought he would be harassed because of the un-usual eyepatch he wore now. This was not the case however as he had earned a new respect with the other kids. Not only due to the fact that he actually beat the bully into unconciousness, but the fact he survived losing one eye. Eric went through school without any taunting in his face, but it was obvious that people were making captain hook jokes behind his back. When Eric turned 15 years of age his Grandfather died. Eric was heartbroken to hear of this. This might have started how Vision is today. He started picking on other children himself, and always getting into something. Eric was getting into something he couldn't comprehend as well, crime. Eric thought he could control the world, he was a strong, tall young man. Once Eric got his driver license he got into drugs and various other illegal things. He was arrested for attempted manslaughter on a man who was attacking him for not paying money on a job that was badly done on his car. Eric said he was acting in self defense, the court believed it and he got away with it. This opened up Eric's eyes. He stopped the drugs and everything he did. He noticed WWF Pro-Wrestling on TV one day and saw what fun and what stardom these athletes had. He finally had a decent obsession-become a pro-wrestler. Eric met his wife Kimberly in the Gym while signing up to be a pro-wrestler. She was attempting to become one herself, but she eventually ended up not doing so. He trained hard, went through years and years of hard work, and eventually it paid off. He joined various feds. He started off as a character named Nethershade. Eventually he dropped the name as it wasn't receiving much fame, and became the man we know as "Vision"...He had a perfect match with this name! One lost eye and a goal to become the best pro-wrestler out there. He may not be the best yet, but he claims he's close and he's not going to give up. General Wrestling Style Powerhouse, Hardcore, Rulebreaker Finisher End of Sight - Crucifix Powerbomb Power Moves Choke Slam Butterfly Pancake Slam Suicidal Dreams - Upon hoisting the opponent in the Torture Rack, Vision tosses their legs over his head, delivering a crushing DDT into the mat. Anarcy Bomb - Top Rope Splash. Power Headbutt Signature Moves One Handed Slam T-Bone Suplex Atomic Drop Top Rope Elbow Drop Tilt A Whirl Backbreaker Sidewalk Slam Side Slam Belly to Back Suplex Apron Slingshot - While on the ring apron, Vision will use the ropes to slingshot the opponent out of the ring.
