Midnight Confessions: Lynn Lane and EOE

22 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An in-fiction interview page from the fed's own site: its announcer Lynn Lane talks with EOE the night before Defiance, in a chat-transcript form. The page carries no date; it is dated by its Wayback capture.
|Midnight Confessions|

Lynn Lane: Fans I am here with FSW superstar and former World champion, EOE. EOE how are you doing tonight?

EOE: Never felt better

Lynn Lane: That's good to hear. Tomrrow night. You will be facing P.S. Mad at Defiance. The winner gets a shot at the World title. How do you think you will do in that match?

EOE: Um...hello! This is EOE you're talking to. You don't expect me to say that I think PMS.....I mean PS Mad actually has a chance do you? He's going to get his ass stomped, just put it that way.

Lynn Lane: I guess you're right. This match has been called the battle of the big men. As both of you are near or at 7 feet tall. The winner will get the bragging rights to being the best big man in the FSW. That is a title you have carried since you came to the FSW. Do you think there is any chance of P.S. Mad or someone else down the road carrying that title?

EOE: Maybe when I'm old and not able to defend that name or maybe when I die. Those are the only ways.

Lynn Lane: I see. Well I am sure you have heard this by now. But Painkiller has basically sold the FSW over to six men. 5 of these men are unknown at this time. The other is Brandon E.. Have you met with the new owners yet?

EOE: No and I don't care to meet them either. If they are anything like PK it's safe for them to keep their distance from me. They stay out of my way and the won't get hurt. Anyone that thinks the have a say so in what I do, has a very big problem with me.

Lynn Lane: Well Brandon did say that things are going to change. I haven't had the chance to meet him either. I will tomorrow though when I go to Defiance.

EOE: Yea we'll see about this "change."

Lynn Lane: Hopefully it's for the good. EOE, you have waited a long time to get your hands on the World title. And once again you are closing in on getting another shot at it. What does being World champion mean to you?

EOE: Being champ. means everything to me. It means that I am the shit (which every already knows) but I puts a capiailization on it with the world title around your shoulder. I've been there before and I'll be damned if I don't get there again.

Lynn Lane: I see. There have been a lot of greats in the FSW. But they have all come and gone. You are basically standing alone if we went and looked at the roster from when the FSW first started. This brings to my next question. The two hall of famers in the FSW right now. Creature and Jacob Jackson. One has acomplished just what you have. A world title. While Jacob has done about everything you can do. When your career is over. Do you think you will be in the Hall Of Fame? Not just because of the World title you won and maybe more to win. But the fact that you have stayed with the company since almost day one.

EOE: Yea my ability to "withstand the test of time" is definately something that's sticks out. FSW has had it's ups and downs, but when you look back and you look for the true all time top FSW Superstars I'll be numero uno on the list. I mean is there anyone else who's be here as long as me? NO! I've told people before "I was here before you came, and I'll be here after you leave" hell I wasn't lying.

Lynn Lane: Well that's been true for the most part. What do you think your chances of becoming World champion in the near future?

EOE: I have a very good chance, as always. 100% at that

Lynn Lane: Yeah, well EOE it's been nice talking to you. Do you have anything else to add?

EOE: Im still seuing the FSW. You didn't ask me how my eye was from that piece of metal that fell from the cage and hit my damn near directly in my eye. One inch from my eye ball! Get some better damn CAGES FSW!

Lynn Lane: Well I am no in charge of that. I think we better end this interview right here. Goodbye EOE

EOE: And you know what's worst? I lose to Token! wTF?

Lynn Lane: Sorry EOE but this has gotten out of hand. Catch me next week folks when I interview Matt Remicho.
