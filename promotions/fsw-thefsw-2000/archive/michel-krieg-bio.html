Michel Krieg Biography Real Name Michel Andre Krieg Nicknames 'The German War Machine' Hometown Berlin, Germany Birthday 22.02.1979 Height 7'0' Weight 296 Biography Growing up in the industrial town of Berlin, Michel Krieg became a devoted, hard working, dedicated young man through his time with the Young German Military and throughout his time in education, shown that he wasn't all about brawn, and finished his schooling years at the age of 17 an 'above average' student. From there on, Michel Krieg began to work for a manufacturing firm in a nearby industrial sector near his family home. Puting his body through it's paces during the day, lifting heavy pieces of machinery, metal, etc, Krieg lived a physical lifestyle, working out for 2 to 3 hours every evening in his local gym. By the age of 19, Krieg had developed his body into a lean, mean fighting machine, and began to participate in a local wrestling federation which he saw on local television. Developing his skills with other young men through the training facilities, Krieg quickly excelled through the program and began to make appearances on local television, slowly but surely working his way up the championship ladder. By the age of 21, Krieg had became the youngest German to have competed in both the German Senior Olympics, and also gained a reputation for his immense strength and brute power. Now at the age of 23, Krieg is looking to move out of the European wrestling circuit and into the American side of the wrestling industry, but also brings his hate of Capitalism with him, adding an angrier edge to the already intimidating German War Machine... Enter OSW... Making his debut at Crowning Glory, interfering in Jesse Ramey's match against his soon-to-be Comrade, Tom Walczak, Krieg made an impact from when he stepped in the door. Aligning himself with Walczak and the mighty Ivan Stanislav, the 3 men made up the OSW's incarnation of The Red Army, a formidable Communist-based stable. For months, Krieg worked alongside his European allies before turning the tables in late June by screwing over Ivan Stanislav and siding with Kory Storm and Frankie Bones after some slight disputes between the two men over the past few weeks. Switching his allegiance from Communism to a form of Capitalism, Krieg appeared to have turned into the very person he appeared to loathe months before. Shows how times change a man, huh? After feuding briefly with Stanislav, Michel felt there was nothing left for him to accomplish in OSW that would be within his grasp, giving him an opporunity to claim his fame somewhere else and prove he wasn't just another one of Ivan Stanislav's 'boys'... That place is... FSW. Other Feds OSW, PWA, IWF. He holds them in the air for a few seconds before letting them fall down for a second and connects with a spinning brainbuster in one smooth motion.
