|Elimination Match and Investigation|

Rumors of the winner of the 4 way Elimination Match between The Legacy, Aggonizer, Vision, and Jonny Trip could very well decide who very well might be getting a good push in the FSW. These are just suspesions, but its most likely the winner could be in the long run for a match for the number one contendership with the world title.

Also the investigation with the boy and the car with a dead woman and dead k-9 in the back of the trunk is still being dealt with. The officer leading this case states "We've seen footage from a FSW tape. We've ruled out Aggonizer, Vision, and The Legacy. We're currently searching for the where abouts of Jonny Trip so that we can question him. We believe him to be the prime suspect in this case, as the other 3 wrestlers had alibis to their whereabouts. The only wrestler we have not questioned is Jonny Trip. We've also learned that Vision seemed to fight with the boys father. Vision seemed to had used a beer bottle and broke it over the man's forehead. The man did not suffer serious injuries but was knocked into a coma. No offenses were made by Vision as he was reacting in self defense. Thank you, no further comments."

Thats about all today. Looks like a great show coming up tommorow! STAY TUNED!!!
