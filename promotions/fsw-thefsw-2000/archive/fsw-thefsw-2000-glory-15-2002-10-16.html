Before he can even lift it for the third time Boda pounds his fist hard into the mat, his veins pumping full of blood, and he uses all of his last strength to pull Deamont up on his shoulders and get back to his feet. Hooking his arms the right way he turns Cassius upside down, holding onto him by his sides. The crowd let out a loud boo as Boda falls down to his knees, driving Deamont into the mat hard. He goes for a cover, hooking two legs with ease. … 1 … 2 … Kickout! Lynn: Wow! Deamont kicked out just at the last second, very close one there. Cassius Deamont, draping his arms from the apron on the ropes, looks across the ring to see Boda folding up a chair. Deamont shakes himself and drops from the apron to the outside, running around the ring to get Boda. Deamont and Orion have done it! Sam: Boo! Lynn: Be quiet you. Steven: Boo! Lynn: And you!
