News from a Hotel worker/FSW fan near Ice Palace

5 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A post from the fed's own newsboard, the page where rumours, predictions and fan reports about its shows were posted.
|News from a Hotel worker/FSW fan near Ice Palace|

This was sent in from a worker of hotel down the street from the Ice Palace who is a FSW fan:

"A huge former FSW Superstar that is not on the card for Glory has checked in there. Due to strict rules I can not tell who it is. It's kind of ironic that this superstar is in Tampa, Florida exactly at the same time that the FSW is in town. What makes it more strange is that this superstar is not staying at the same hotel that the other FSW superstars are staying at; the majority of the superstars are staying at a hotel closer to the Ice Palace.

I could not believe my eyes, it was actually him in full flesh.

Who knows maybe he's just here to enjoy the show like everyone else, then again maybe not. But if this man is in town for what I think he is in town for, then the Glory will be even better than it already looks and will be one hell of a night. I'll be there!"

FSW Fan :)
