Lo-Key Biography Real Name Louis Thorson Nicknames none you want to go repeating Hometown New York, NY Birthday 6/7/71 Height 6'4' Weight 260 Fed Titles Wait for it... Fed Awards See Above Biography Lo-Key is a bastard. This much is known for sure. The rest is speculation and observation. He's a smartass with a big mouth, a bad temper and no observable morals. A loner, he hasn't established any apparent ties in his stay in the WT, but his tenure in the PWA was brief. In that time, it was overheard that he had spent time in prison, and prior to that was a wrestler. So far, no word has surfaced on where and with whom that career might have been, but suffice to say it must have ended badly. But today Lo-Key has resurfaced in the wrestling world, with a new name, a new look and he hopes, the chance to make a new start. With an intense determination, he seems poised to force his way into the spotlight of the FSW. Considering his past, there's an excellent chance that the name Louis Thorson is assumed. Appearance: Lo-Key is a broad, muscular guy, but not hulking. He has dark brown hair, gun metal gray eyes and a handsome if troubled face. In the ring he wears black trunks with a red diagonal pattern in a sort of leather look fabric. He has two tattoos, knot work bands around each bicep in red and black. Each is very Norse looking with a wolf's head at the end of the knot work strand.
