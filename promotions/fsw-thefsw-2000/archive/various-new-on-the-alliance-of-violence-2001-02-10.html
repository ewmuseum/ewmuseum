Various new on the Alliance of Violence.

10 February 2001

|Various new on the Alliance of Violence. |

The Alliance of Violence has had quite a run in this early stage of the new FSW. Already they have caused much mayhem and destruction. The Alliance of Violence now consist of three wrestlers: Johnny Mayhem, P.S.Mad and Sound. Razor has so far been unable to compete due to personal problems. All three wrestlers come from a now closed federation named the E-Federation X. There goal in the FSW seems to be to 'teach' the ways of hardcore wrestling to everybody in the FSW.

The Alliance of Violence have so far been in a war with a group named The Triumvirates which consists of Keith O'Niel, Jason Rhodes and Lucky McKnight. So far the two factions have been fighting for supremacy. The Alliance of Violence have beaten up by the Triumvirates on several occasions, but they have set The Triumvirates locker room on fire and most recently on Maximum Velocity, gone as far to kidnap and almost torture Jason Rhodes supposidly 'teaching' him in their 'school of hardcore'. These three maniacs will stop at nothing to reach the top of the FSW.

On Max Velocity this past week Johnny Mayhem was attacked by Max Danger in an interview. And now on Glory this week the Alliance of Violence face Triumvirates in a 6 man tag team match with Max danger as the special guest referee. This will be a great match, so tune into Glory on Tuesday to see just who will survive.

The Alliance of Violence have said to be happy with there role so far in the FSW and are looking forward to the future. But they are said to be concerned about the fact that they are not being taken seriously enough. They will continue to take more extreme action until established FSW superstars take note of them. The following is a quote from Johnny Mayhem: "We are not finished with victimising people with our school of hardcore, you thought what we did to Rhodes was bad, just wait till we get our hands on our next victim!"

On a side note, P.S.Mad has been allowed to take the weekend off to visit his family in England. He should be back late on Monday and ready to compete on Glory.
