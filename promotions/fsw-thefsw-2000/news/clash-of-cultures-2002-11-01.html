Clash Of Cultures!

1 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News Acticle entitled "Clash Of Cultures!": FSW Management confirmed today the official signing of indy star "The Tree Hugger" Casey Deltura. After making a brief appearance on Glory a few weeks ago, negotiations began over Deltura's contract, and after a day of discussions, Deltura was signed to the new FSW roster. His appearance on this week's Glory, getting in the face of the ever drunken Paddy O'Shea, who signed a day after Deltura, his negotiations taking slightly longer since FSW delegates couldn't get a hold of him, due to the fact they didn't know which bar he was drinking in. Their confrontation over Paddy's mistreatment of a flower bed as a urinal flared tempers between the two, and after a shove by Deltura to O'Shea, the drunkard levelled Deltura with a tree branch, and unusual weapon to be used in a wrestling arena, but an effective one all the same! When Deltura was approached for comment on the friction between himself and Paddy, while he was being attended by EMT's might I add, the activist had much to say, which, going by his track record of protests, isn't out of the ordinary: "If that hobo Paddy O'Shea thinks he can walk around and urinate on Mother Nature's creations, especially flowers as beautiful as those, then he's got another thing coming, and it's name is Casey Deltura," claimed the Greenpeace member, reporters having to take a step or two back from him due to his 'earthly' smell. As for Paddy, he didn't have much to say as usual, but what he did say seemed to be of the calibre of a classic Irishman... "....Meh...." We will keep the FSW fans updated on this story as it develops, which seems to be a certainty!
