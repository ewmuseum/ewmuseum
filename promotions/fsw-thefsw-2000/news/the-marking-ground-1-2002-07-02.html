FSW — Column

The Marking Ground #1

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FSW Column #1 Heya kids and squids…it’s the man himself, Mark S.…and this is the inaugural FSW column…the Marking Ground. As for my history, you don’t care, so we’ll skip it. I think we should start out talking about the first Glory card, and then I’ll take a quick look at the title pictures that could be shaping up over the course of the month. First off, we have the inaugural Glory card, set to run on July 3…it looks to be, well…interesting. Chris Bagwell meets Jack Brooze in the first match of the new era of Five Star Wrestling. Brooze is a FSW old-timer, Bagwell is a heavy-duty tournament name from the past, who bombed out in PCW. The Mark’s opinion…definitely Bagwell if he shows up. Winner: Chris Bagwell Then we see what may be one of the best matches of the night, as Ming, Asa Fountain, and Jesse Ramey go all out in a triple threat match. Ramey, when on…can be very good, he DOES have a pinfall over Sebastian Toomes in OSW competition…Asa has always been good, when given to show up…but the winner here HAS to be Ming. First off, he doesn’t mind cheating. Secondly, he’s got a mean streak (no, not him)…and has always performed well in his time in the ring. Minger takes it. With STYLE! Winner: Ming Now, we see “Five Star” Leon Chambers against the self-proclaimed “Five Star Superstar” Tyler Davidson. This seems like a moderately good match, but after seeing some of Davidson’s work, I am fully convinced that he’ll take this match with some authority. Unless the former Five Star guys all huddle up and help each other out….TD should roll. Winner: Tyler Davidson Half the card’s over already? Ugh. No more short cards, alright!? Next up is the “Candian Criminal” versus a mystery opponent. Oh joy. Mystery. Yay. This guy BETTER not disappoint, because that’d be a pretty horrible thing to do on your pre-main event match. Personally, having not seen too much of Irving, I’m gonna guess with the mystery dude, considering my assumption that he BETTER be good. Winner: Mystery Opponent And finally, our main event. Mr. Potential himself, Johnny Raindance versus the FSW Icon, Big Daveylicious. This one’s tough to call. Like I said in the case of Chambers vs. Davidson, the angle could make all the difference. To be perfectly honest, I think Raindance is the superior competitor, but I think Big D has got some sort of angle up his sleeve. Therefore, my pick to win it is the D. Tenaciously. Winner: Big Daveylicious Honestly folks, I’m hoping for some really good angles. FSW’s design and basis (and staff, can’t forget the staff) as well as it’s history give it the possibility of succeeding as much or beyond OSW and PCW…but right now, FSW isn’t exactly making a big spark in the “name” department. Therefore, it’s up to the people currently on the roster to make exciting feuds. Now…Mark’s Five Star Five Words…about the entire inaugural FSW roster (that we know of). Five words or less describing my thoughts about everyone. “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey – From this to that… Tyler Davidson – First new FSW Primetime Champ. Carl Blair – Who’s fantasy was he? Leon Chambers – Step it up, and succeed. Angelo DeVille – Excellent first promo. Jason O’Neill – Straw man may catch fire. Chris Irving – Who are you? Post! Magnum Wolf – Hope he doesn’t cause problems… Lo-Key – Lame, already used name. Johnny Raindance – Five strikes, and you’re out. Ming – SWANKY! Big Daveylicious – Carrying the torch for FSW. Chris Bagwell – Please don’t disappear. Jack Brooze – Croozin’ for a broozin’. Asa Fountain – Because feds need fros. Simple. And that’s the Marking Ground! Tune in next time for wrestler profiles, and to close us out…the Five Star Top Five! Five people who will make an impact on FSW’s first card of the new era. Five Star Top Five – Who will make the biggest impact at Glory 1? 5. Tyler Davidson – When he comes to play, he’s awesome. 4. Ming – Charisma, baby! Plus, he’s SWANKY! 3. Johnny Raindance – You hit someone with a hummer and I am OUTTA HERE, mister! 2. Mystery Opponent – Hopefully, this guy will blow doors off the building. You better be good… 1. Big Daveylicious – This is THE guy to carry the torch for this fed. Either he steps up, or he’s an instant disappointment. I ALWAYS hope for the former, rather than the latter. And that’s it from the Marking Ground. Any questions, comments or complaints, please direct them to a brick wall. Later kids, and remember…nothing EVER changes!
