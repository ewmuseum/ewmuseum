Mob Rule Sighted Injuring Fan.......

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lexington, Kentucky residents were scandalised to read in their local newspaper this morning that two of the town's regular working class were admitted to hospital after having an unfriendly run-in with five FSW superstars, who are visiting for Glory this week. Angelo Deville, Tyler Nelson, Ming, Chris Chase, and Orpheus Grant were allegedly slumming at some bar when a pair of mechanics started giving them hassle about being made fools of at Alliances by RUSH, and continued on to praise Timo Bolamba and the rest of The Untouchables for "showing you classless ass-clowns how decent people act." Needless to say, FSW's dominant stable took immediate offense to these outrageous remarks, and proceeded to trash the bar, using the two mechanics as their tools of destruction. There is no word yet on a civil action being levied against Mob Rule by the victims, but bar owner John Garaguchi has already threatened to sue Five Star Wrestling for damages, both "to his bar and state of mind." "This is an outrage," Robin Nicely stated curtly when asked for comment. "This is definitely not the kind of image Five Star Wrestling wants to present to the public. Why can't they just be civil?" The original reporter on this matter approached Mr. Tyler Nelson for comment, and hasn't been seen since. His replacement was able to track down Ming and Chris Chase, but only succeeded in getting mocked, berated, and abused. After making him eat the foam cover from his microphone, the reporter alleges the two millionaires robbed him of his lunch money and made him give up his "new" shoes. Learning from their mistakes, I gave Angelo Deville a quick phone call - long distance - and asked for his comments regarding the matter. "I have absolutely no recollection of assaulting anyone on the night in question. We were simply out having a few relaxing drinks when these greasy biker types started giving us trouble. Let me state for the record that it is pure coincidence they'll spend the rest of their days swallowing only fluids." He then changed direction completely, addressing the comments themselves. "What the hell was this RUSH place, anyhow? Was it part of WrestlingTattler? Seriously? I never saw it on television or heard anything about it... Was it some kind of wrestling academy, like the PWA? Regardless, those drill-wielding jobbers better watch their backs... And their necks."
