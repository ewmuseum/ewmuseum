Boda enjoying his suspension.....

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Internet sites report Chris Sloboda, better known as Boda, has been seen at NBA Basketball games on his time off. Now for Boda, this is not unusual, but the games he was seen at were Dallas Mavericks, San Antonio Spurs, and Houston Rockets home games. When Boda was approached about why he was in Texas for these games, all he had to say was..... Boda: I LOVE THIS GAME!! With Boda being in Texas, this looks like he will definetly defy his suspension and try to show up on Glory.
