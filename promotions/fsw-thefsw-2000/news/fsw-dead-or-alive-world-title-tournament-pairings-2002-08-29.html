FSW Dead or Alive World Title Tournament Pairings

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A recap for the tag teams for the DoA tourney, which will cap off with the final two teams facing each other at FSW's next PPV Bad Moon Rising, with the members of the winning team facing off against each other with the World Title on the line later that night. Team pairings are as follows: Dead Presidents Ming and Angelo Deville Cassius Deamont and Boda "Five-Star" Mike Marvelous and Timo Bolamba "The Scholar" Howard Porter and Alan Kriegman (Special Agreement with Old School Wrestling) Azala Zameer and Mike Frampton Michael "Spitfire" Stevens and Kimbusa Bad Frotitude John Jupiter and "Showtime" Erik Blake Kyle Velocity and a Mystery Partner A Tag Team Chris Bagwell and Tim Walden Big Daveylicious and Michel Krieg "The Gunslinger" Paul Orion and Chip O'Meara Magnum Wolf and Tyler Davidson Brackets will be set up later, with Orion and O'Meara receiving a first round bye as set up by the Commish. The Dead of Alive Tournament gets underway next week on FSW Glory!
