The Man of Mystery

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the announcement of the final pairings for the Dead or Alive Tournament, questions are raised as to who the Mystery Partner for Kyle Velocity will be. Rumors running rampant are fellow former CCW members Big Alan Steele, who recently retired from PCW, and THE Multiple Man, who did likewise, along with Action! Wrestling's own Jamaal Wilson. "I'd love to give some information," said FSW spokesperson Robin Nicely, "but to be honest, I don't know anymore than anyone does. Kyle is keeping this one under his hat." We will bring you more on this story as it devlops.
