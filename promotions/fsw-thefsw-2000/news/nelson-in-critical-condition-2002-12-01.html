Nelson in Critical Condition

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On the heels of the brutal attack perpetrated by FSW World Champion Timo Bolamba, Tyler Nelson was listed in critical condition at Your Mother is Your Sister Hospital in Lexington, Kentucky. One of the attending emergency room doctors was quoted as saying that Nelson's injuries were "Purty darn bad." The attack, brought on by Nelson pushing the last button Timo had left to push, brought an end to a Glory which saw Tyler trick Bolamba into a World Title match next week. Using a local child actor as his evil assistant, Tyler got Timo to sign a contract under the guise of an autograph. This revelation caused the FSW World Champion to snap and viciously attack Nelson after his World Title defense against Nelson's fellow Mob Rule member, Chris Chase. Nelson sustained major head and neck injuries, as well as cracked cartilage in his nose. There were also numerous lacerations about the former PCW CEO's head and face. Doctors would not comment further on Nelson's condition, either at Tyler's request or because they didn't really know what was wrong with him. We will bring you more on this story as we get information.
