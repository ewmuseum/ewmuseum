Execution of one man, preperation for others

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-06-02 “Trust me, I know. I understand. Yes, yes, it’s already been done”, a voice, echoes from with in a dark, dank room. One light hangs carelessly above the lone wooden table, which itself looks as if though it was built about 50 years ago. Water is heard, yet not seen, leading one to believe that the pipes that run overhead and inside the walls are near the source of the vibrating sound. A lone telephone sits atop the table, locked into speakerphone mode, and a befuddled Lt. Mandela perches on a steel foldout chair, listening attentively to what is being said. “You’re not holding up to your end of the bargain Fredric”, the mysteriously disguised voice accuses from the safety of the other end of the communication line, “your assignment is not being completed according to the itinerary, and that concerns me.” “But things here are going better than ever expected”, replies Mandela with a bit of dissention in his voice, “Azala is tearing through the competition far easier than even I had expected, not to mention the outside help we’ve received.” “Outside help” the disguised voice ponders. “Well, there have been certain technical difficulties as of late, the damn FSW almost went under on it’s own along with all of the other federations that mean anything here in the United States”, Mandela boasts. “Besides, it’s only a matter of time before we are finished here in the FSW, I can feel our momentum growing everyday.” “You fool! You were not sent to America to pursue a wrestling career for Zameer and you knew that! There was a bigger plan at hand, a plan to better our country’s chances of relieving ourselves of the strong-arming of the United States! That is where you have faulted!” “But, but, but…..You don’t understand”, Mandela pleads “our cover here must be perfect to pull off what you wish. We’ve already eliminated one man from the picture, but we can’t lead the authorities to our trail by continuing to do so this frequently. Our reputation in FSW must hold up until any heat regarding the last mission dies down.” “That is well understood here, but it has been far too long, our people here yearn for more action in response to American Industrialized corporations demolishing our natural habitat. You know we have no power here, that is why we sent you over to the United States, to take the power away from them and make us the aggressor.” “I’m very well aware of that” admits Mandela, “ but Zameer desires to fight, in this Americanized style, that is where his heart is and it’s hard to convince him otherwise right now. I can’t tell him the truth, he’d never stand for it.” “You need to make him realize then” demands the voice on speakerphone, “make him see things our way, or he too will have to be replaced. And if we need to replace him, you too get replaced, and you know what that means.” “Yes, of course I know, and there is no need for harsh, quick judgment. Everything is running to order and your next obstacle will be eliminated with in the next week” Mandela reassures. “Until then, were you able to acquire what I asked for last week?” “I was, although it wasn’t easy explaining why it was needed, please explain it to me again.” Mandela kicks his feet up on the table rocking back in his chair before continuing. “We have seem to run into a small obstacle here in the FSW, and were lucky enough to be granted a match that works into our favor. An African Death Match.” “A what!”, the voice demands more clarification. “Just bear with me. Basically it’s my call here, so I’m going to make it the most gruesome match the FSW has ever bared witness to. The people here as well as one Mr. Mike Frampton will have to wait to see exactly what it will entail, but the sooner you can ship out my requests, the sooner I’ll feel comfortable with everything.” “No worries then Fredric, it was shipped out yesterday, and barring any difficulties through customs, you should have it by Tuesday morning.” “Excellent” replies Mandela as a smile creases his lips, “ That’s all I wanted to hear. I’ll be in touch by next weekend, please wire me the exact instructions as far as what is to be done about the GM of United Corp, and it will be taken care of, I’ll make sure of it.” “That’s all I wanted to hear, and until you prove me wrong, you’ll have all of the means necessary to thrive over in the United States.” “Understood”, replies Mandela as he clicks off the telephone. Standing up and walking to the door, he exits showing him in the back hallway of the FSW corporate headquarters, moving rather swiftly with a specific look of concern on his face. “Azala! Where are you!” Looking around into the lobby/waiting areas he finds the 500-pound man sitting on the table inside, watching up intently at something on the television. “Azala!” yells Mandela, “let’s go now, we need to…..” Seeing that he isn’t getting through to the African Nightmare he walks over to see what has his lethal weapon so entranced on the television. “American Football!” Mandela reprimands “What business does this have in our agenda here in the United States! This is just a weak game played by weak minded individuals with absolutely no merit what so ever.” Zameer scoffs up at Mandela before standing tall above him, lowering his head and exiting the room behind him. “Not only do we have to take care of Mr. Frampton this week on Glory, but we also need to worry about the constant scheming of Angelo Deville and his rival Ming, and here I find you sitting and watching a football game.” Mandela simply quickens his pace as result of his frustration with Zameer for focusing on a mere game before he comes to a door, where outside waits a large black Hummer. Getting inside Mandela shows his contempt for Zameer’s action as he scolds to the driver to just take them home.
