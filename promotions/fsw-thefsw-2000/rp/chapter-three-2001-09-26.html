|Chapter Three|
Simon Krask | 09/26/01
Simon shot up in the hospital bed, sweating heavily. He looked like he just had a nightmare as he whiped the sweat on his forehead away, and was just about to get up, but Chuku ran into the room with a worried look on his face.

"Simon are you alright?" he gasped, running to Simon's side.

"Yah...Yah... My head hurts a bit though... But I am fine, I guess." said Simon, laying back down in the bed.

A nurse ran into the room as well, and smiled as she walked over to his side.

"Well, look who finally woke up!" she exclaimed, looking at Chuku, then back at Simon.

"Yah... Look who woke up." he said, mimmicking her.

"Can I get you anything Simon?" she asked. "A sandwhich....Water?"

"No, I am alright." he said. "Ahh!" yelled Simon, gripping his leg. "My leg hurts!" he screamed, looking at the nurse.

"You were shot Simon... I would assume it...Would hurt a bit." she replied, smirking.

"What!?" Simon yelled aloud. "I was shot??? By who!" said Simon looking very worried.

"We don't know yet Simon. But we'll find that sucker and make him pay for what he did to you!" Chuku exclaimed, sitting down in the visitor chair.

"It was Krozan!" exclaimed Simon, sitting up in the bed.

"Who is this... Krozan you keep talking about Simon?" Chuku asked.

"Leader of Klapta!" I said aloud, jumping out of the bed.

"Mr. Krask, please, sit down." the nurse said to me.

"Why?" I asked her before sitting down.

"You have been diagnosed with...a Split Personality..." she said, pulling up a chair and sitting down as well.

"What?" I asked, looking confused.

"You are someone else one minute, and then yourself the next..." she said, pulling out a piece of paper from her pocket. "Here, everything about it is on this paper." she said, handing me the paper.

"Now I know why I have been saying stupid things aloud to myself!" I shouted.

"You have been saying your name is Nick when you get an attitude... Your second personality." she said, looking like she didn't want to tell me something.

"What else?" I asked her, eager to find out more about my "problem."

"Sometimes...Nick will do things to hurt you, Simon, and not him. He won't feel it but you will. Just be prepared to suffer Simon if you don't take these pills every hour." she said, handing me a bottle of pills.

"So that's why my leg wouldn't move while I crossed the road!" I exclaimed. "JUMPING CATFISH!" I yelled aloud, "Nick can seriously kill me!"

"Yes that's why you must take these pills every hour Simon.. It is very important for you do so." she said, getting up and putting the chair back to it's rightful spot.

"Thank you..." I paused for her name.

"Bertha." she said, walking over to the door.

"I will leave you two alone now. Good day." she said as she left the room.

"Man... Split personality.... What else will I ge-" My head shook, and Nick came into play. "Your gonna die bitch... Your gonna die!" Nick screamed, as he jumped onto Chuku and started to choke him.

"S-ssimon.... Get of-" he stopped, and punched me in the face.

"Ahhh!" I yelled, as blood trickeled down my nose. "What the hell was that for man?" I screamed at him, holding my nose closed.

"Simon?" he asked.

"YES!" I replied, spilling blood all over the floor.

"Sorry." he said, as he ran out of the room to get some paper towels.

"Idiot..." I mumbled as the door closed shut.
