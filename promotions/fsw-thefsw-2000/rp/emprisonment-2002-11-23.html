Emprisonment

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-23 ONE! No… please, stop. TWO! All I have to do is move my shoulder… THREE! No… How does one define helplessness? Complete and total helplessness… A feeling of vulnerability, anger, fear and fury that knows no equal. Kimbusa felt that tonight. After the suffering a shot to the skull from a steel chair wielded by Violence Jack, the entire arena had gone silent. The people were obviously on their feet and in a frenzy, but the sound was muffled by something far more powerful. As Kimbusa’s strength left him, and his body hit the mat, his first instinct was to move. All he had to do was roll over onto to stomach where he couldn’t be pinned. All he had to do was get off his shoulders. But instead, he remained motionless, completely unable to move. He tried his legs, but found his strength had left him. He tried his arms, but there was no power left. He tried to move, he tried to speak, he tried to cry out for Epiphany, to let her know he was sorry… but nothing came. It was as if his soul had left his body entirely, and he was merely watching from the inside out. If the body is merely a vessel for something greater, then Kimbusa’s vessel had sunk at the hands of Violence Jack and Mob Rule. And all he could do was listen as the referee’s hand came down to the mat three times in succession… and shattered Kimbusa’s hopes and dreams. All Kimbusa had ever wanted was the life of a simple husband. Paradise could be found in simplicity, he had reasoned. A modest home on the Japanese countryside. Herbs blooming on the south hills. A simple job as a laborer in some small town nearby… A beautiful wife to come home to. The skies would be blue and warm, the plains green and vast… the days golden and fruitful. But the sky outside is black, and what little light the moon provides is hindered by the thick layer of smog that perpetually covers the city. Maybe Caldera was right. Maybe Kimbusa could never be victorious without that hatred that had once consumed him. Maybe the demons truly had been the only thing empowering him to succeed as he had. Maybe all hope really is lost… Maybe… It seems as if everyone around is an enemy. Ming, Tyler Nelson, Violence Jack, Angelo Deville, Jason O’Neil, Mike McClure… Steven Caldera. All these enemies, and not a single ally has risen to his side. Kimbusa had watched amid a torrent of sadness as Jason O’Neil, who once had come to Kimbusa’s side, instead turned to aid Timo Bolomba when threatened by the same enemies that had only moments earlier ended Kimbusa’s dreams. Once again, Kimbusa is trapped within the confines of his padlocked apartment, left with only the dull residual effects of the beating handed down to him hours ago. The pain they had inflicted upon his body had healed immediately. Kimbusa does not feel pain, he does not allow it to rule his thoughts… but Kimbusa is not above the limitations of the human body, and so he remains motionless, unable to move, unable to breathe. Epiphany is in the hands of Violence Jack now. He has tried to plan revenge rather than contemplate the possibilities of what harm may be befalling his love at this moment, or what torture might be brought down upon her in the nights to come. Kimbusa had failed her, and he had failed himself. Steven Caldera had bailed Kimbusa out of prison, but in some ways, he had never really left. So many enemies… Deville… Ming… Nelson… Violence Jack… …Steven Caldera. 'I’ve got to get out of this cell…..'
