Gaining Support

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-02-02 'PANTHER PURGE' BY: VIRTUAL MURRELL The governing body of the Black Panther Party, which is our Central Committee, has decided that in order to preserve democratic centralism and to destroy ultra-democracy within our ranks, that it is of absolute necessity to understand the decadence of ultra-democracy. First, it must be crystal clear that ultra-democracy damages the party organization and seeks to undermine the Party. We must also point out that the petty bourgeoisie's individualistic aversion to discipline is directly related. They are not only practicing ultra-democracy, but they are opportunistic, and these individuals are not only opportunistic or counter-revolutionaries, but individuals who are renegades, jacknapes, and factionalizers who, with their obvious stupidity, try to divide our people by their very action. It is necessary for the Black Panther Party to expose those fools that go against the Party's rules. They exhibit without doubt that their personal opportunistic gain is worth more than the liberation of black people. It is necessary for the Black Panther Party, which is a People's Party, to purge members within the Party to rid ourselves of opportunism, the petty-bourgeoisie desires. You see, we believe in the teachings of our leader; the Minister of Defense, Huey P. Newton of the Black Panther Party, when he says, 'Have faith in the party and have faith in the people.' If the masses are going to have faith in the party, it's necessary and an absolute principle that we are very sincere and honest with the people, because it is necessary to restore the much needed trust and dedication which has been lacking for hundreds of years. If we, as the vanguard, failed to criticize and denounce buffoons, and simpletons, then we will be put into a position of hypocrisy. In actuality, we would be the fools for allowing it to exist without moving to remove those who aspire to opportunism, filthy reprobates. Purging is a process which must be used, as 'defined,' as a cleansing process. In view of what has happened in the past, it was very clear that it was necessary to purge those who by their very overt actions try to destroy the Party and the Party's image. One of our objectives is to build and to unite black people and to lift their level of consciousness. In conclusion, we say that all those who aspire to opportunism are directly related to the repudiation of the dictatorship of the proletariat, which simply means 'Power by the People.' It is an unmistakable sign of treason in the highest form, betrayal of black people. All Power To the People. Black Power to Black People. “What you have just heard was an old credo spoken by none other than Bobby Seale, original cofounder of the Black Panther Party”, Said a well-dressed black man whilst standing in front of handful of others, resembling the same ensemble. The eight other men in the room eagerly listen on as Khalid Muhammad; the doctor who reformed the Black Panther Party into the New Black Panther Party spoke more aggressively. “Our founding fathers had many clear points, we must weed out the individuals who desire personal gain before that of the group, and we must make constant steps to gain strength in our efforts. Our next step,” As Dr. Muhammad pauses the large oak wooden doors part and the men seated at the table peer over one another in attempt to observe who, or what it is that is entering the room. The shorter Lt. Fredric W. Mandela leads the way in, carrying himself in a very Napoleonesque manner that virtually commands respect. He stops short of Khalid Muhammad and offers a hand of friendship. The two men shake hands until Muhammad’s jaw grows agape and his body goes paralyzed by the sight of the man who is following Mandela into the room. “This, my new friend,” Mandela began, “ is my African Nightmare, the man known around this miserable nation as Azala Zameer.” All men sit back in their chairs taking in the massive size of the giant, as Muhammad stands back as well, before offering his hand to Zameer. Azala looks down to the much smaller being, dressed neatly in mostly black attire, smirks slightly and passes on this offering of peace, before stepping back to the wall and standing strong behind Lt. Mandela. “I understand you have called us here today to make an offer.” Lt. Mandela says. “An offer of sorts, yes, but more over an opportunity to unite with in our organization, be a part of what we are trying to do here in the United States.” Muhammad replies. It takes Mandela only a fraction of a second to respond, “ we want nothing to do with this United States, from what I see, they are not as United as people believe.” “But certainly you must see the simplicity of our Proposal Lieutenant” Khalid pushes forth, “to attack this country any further standing alone, as an army of one, is certain suicide. These American often times take the law into their own hands, or attempt to, and with as easy as they’ve made it brandish a firearm, you will eventually be killed.” “What exactly does your proposal entail?” Mandela asked looking rather quizzically around the room to the other men. Khalid smiled almost semi evilly, then began again to speak, “What we offer to you is your support during your campaign here while in the United States. We can offer bodies, meaning guards, support staff, whatever you desire. We have members of the Bloods and Crips working with in our organization that will provide you with whatever service you may desire, as well as doctors, accountants, and politicians just to name a few.” “You speak to me as if though we need your help” Mandela says with a slight chuckle “I know you aren’t blind men, I have all the protection I need standing right here behind me. What more could you offer.” “Sit down and I’ll explain things to you more intimately, please, I beg of you” Muhammad pleaded. Taking the man up on his offer Mandela sat down, nodding nonchalantly to the eight men listening in very attentively to the conversation between the men. Looking up to the walls one large painting catches his eye, a painting of Malcolm X with a quote written underneath saying: 'When I'm born I'm Black, when I grow up I'm Black, when I'm in the cold I'm Black, when I'm in the sun I'm Black, when I'm sick I'm Black, when I die I'm Black. And you... When you're born you're Pink, when you grow up you're White, when you're in the cold you're Blue, when you're in the sun Red, when you're sick you're Green, when you die you're Purple. And you dare to call ME a coloured....'. Malcolm X Getting Mandela’s attention back to the meeting at hand, Khalid Muhammad presses forth. “First off I believe money will be of priority, our income is based solely on supporting black owned business, resulting in a very profitable outcome the end of each month. I’ve heard you publicly refuse to take a paycheck from the Five Star Wrestling organization, as it will result in you paying money back into the American Government to which you are so opposed. I offer you fulfill any and all monetary needs you have.” Lt. Mandela just nods and listens on, as Zameer remains attentive behind him, still standing. “Number 2, we can offer travel, we have our own jet planes and road transportation where ever you may need to go. I understand that your business here in the United States requires much travel, as I also understand your refusal to support any of America’s tourist industry. We as an organization will grant you one of our own leer jets as well as a motorcade to every gala event you attend” Mandela nods towards Muhammad as if telling him without speaking a word that his proposal is being taken under careful consideration “We will also follow the events of Zameer very closely, ensuring his continued success any way possible.” Interrupting Muhammad, Lt. Mandela stands to speak, “This can’t be something for nothing, you make very valid points here, and I agree with your Black Panther Party on many points, but for me to believe that you will give us this aid for free, why that would just be arrogant and presumptuous of me.” “The free publicity that we’ll receive from backing you and Azala in your quest here in the United States is all we ask for. We in no way intend to use either of you in an disrespectful manor, but we are on the brink of some major events in the near future, and with you on board supporting what we are attempting to do, I believe it will be hard pressed to stop us. With my knowledge of government and politics, combined with your militaristic background, we will be as strong as ever.” “Very well then” Mandela said in agreement, “it is done, we will accept your olive branch of friendship.” Standing from theirs seats the other eight men begin to applaud softly as the two men at the head of the table shake hands. “Very well then,” said Muhammad pointing towards the man sitting to his right, “This is the National Chairman of the New Black Panther Party, his name is Malik Zulu Shabazz. If you would be so kind as to follow him, he’ll get you outfitted with your travel plans and associates. I look forward to seeing you in the near future Lieutenant, as I do with your Giant as well, and best of luck to you in the FSW, we’ll all be pulling for you.” With that said, Mandela follows Shabazz out of the room followed by Zameer. With this new allegiance formed, Mandela’s fight is becoming stronger and stronger every day, and in high hope of continuing their destruction in the FSW, they head out to finalize this new deal.
