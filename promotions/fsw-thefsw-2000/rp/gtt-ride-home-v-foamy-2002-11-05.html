-GTT- Ride Home (v Foamy)

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'I do not like it here.' Kimbusa registers his usual protest with a growing sense of frustration. Steven Caldera slides his nine hundred dollar sunglasses down his face slightly, as the limo turns a quick corner, before screeching to an abrupt halt. Caldera's deep blue eyes lock with Kimbusa's, whose eyes are equally dark as Caldera's are radiant. 'We're almost to the airport. Just sit tight,' says Caldera, still sounding cheerfully optimistic despite the mammoth's disagreement. Kimbusa presses Caldera further. 'I do not like your commercial airliners.' Caldera smiles and slides his glasses back over his eyes. He turns towards the bustling street beyond; New York City is in full force, still thobbing and teeming with life a year after it was devastated. 'Don't worry, I have a special treat for you tonight. You'll be home soon enough.' Kimbusa seems only momentarily satisfied. Slumping back in his leather limosuine seat, Kimbusa remains focued on Caldera. Caldera seems fit as ever, well groomed and beaming. His brown Italian suit and red tie seem to compliment a picture of a man who is truly tied to the finer aspects of life. Watching Caldera idly gaze out his dark tinted window, Kimbusa realizes that Caldera is the embodiment of everything he is fighting... Making him the perfect mentor. Caldera had aggravated Kimbusa from day one. Ever since they had first spoken over the telephone, ever since that first handshake, Kimbusa had known that Caldera was the quintisenntial American entertainer. But Caldera seemed to be a legitimately insightful human being; he may not have been the most honest or moral man in the world, not by a long shot. But Caldera was a respectable and likeable gentleman; his overbearing ways had an aura of kindness and genuine caring that seemed to keep Kimbusa at bay, and prevent him from simply taking his frustrations out on his benefactor. Caldera truly had gone out of his way to help Kimbusa. True, Caldera himself had admitted to an alterior motive, but nonetheless it had been a profitable partnership. Kimbusa had indulged in a series of battles with American wrestlers that had fed his blood lust; and Caldera would soon have another trophy for his wall. Caldera shifts slightly in his seat, either unaware that Kimbusa was still staring directly at his face, or simply doing an excellent job of hiding it. The limo continues to speed forward and jerk to a halt, battling the intense New York traffic. The day is nearing its end, and the never-ending rush hour is intensifying. Despite their close proximity to JKF Airport, they are still expecting a bit of a wait. Caldera was also obviously a very bright young man. At only twenty six years of age, Caldera has owned and operated a highly successful wrestling organization, and his star has continued to rise throughout the wrestling world. Kimbusa did not understand the industry like Caldera did, as seen in his recent arrest and incarceration. Kimbusa simply did not understand the American way of life. The streaming cars flying perilously through crowded streets filled with bustling people and honking horns were enough to drive Kimbusa mad, and he had only been on the street for ten minutes. Who could possibly wish to reside in such an ungodly city? Caldera seemed to like it here, but Kimbusa detested the city. This was perhaps the most characteristic division between the two. Kimbusa lives in a hollowed out cove made of burnt rubble and debris; Caldera lives in a posh high-rise apartment in the world's largest city. Caldera lived extravagantly; Kimbusa was a minimalist. But the two shared a common link, a symbiot; each needed the other. Kimbusa could not survive in America without guide, and Caldera could not progress in the industry without a weapon of mass destruction. Despite what others may have branded him, Kimbusa does not consider himself a tool. He is taking advantage of a unique opportunity, and anyone thinking Kimbusa is incapable of destruction without Caldera at his side is mistaken. Caldera had never once accompanied Kimbusa to a match, and Kimbusa had made it clear that he never will. Minutes pass before the limo reaches the airport. Rather than following the usual path to the terminal, the limo veers away from the main road and finds itself at a small security booth. A gate slides open and the limo pulls out onto the tarmac, stopping before Kimbusa's surprise. Both back doors open, and Kimbusa and Caldera exit from opposite ends of the limo. Caldera smiles as he beholds this gift with awe and silent appreciation; Kimbusa seems confused. He is standing beneath a large jet, whose side reads 'Global Championship Wrestling.' 'What do you think?' Caldera finally asks, breaking th odd silence. 'What is this monstrosity?' Kimbusa inquires, tilting his head slightly to the side in confusion. The jet's engines suddenly fire up and begins emiting a tremendous hissing noise, signaling it immenent departure. 'It's your ride home!' Caldera says, his voice increasing in volume to be heard over the sound of the engines. 'Call me before your next match, I'll send someone out to collect you. No more commercial flights for you, my friend. From now on, my jet takes you wherever you want to go.' Caldera smiles at Kimbusa and flashes him a goofy thumbs up. Kimbusa seems reluctant to approach the jet. He finds the vanity of owning one's own air plane to be a bit over the top, but it is a ride home, and a private one at that. This way, at least, he would be home in the quiet forest in a matter of hours, free of the constant rush of life with Steven Caldera. 'I'll talk to you soon!' Caldera shouts, before sliding back into the limo. He closes his door and the limo pulls away, leaving Kimbusa alone to study the blue and black GCW jet. After a moment he spots an attendant at the top of a small flight of stairs, beckoning him to climb into the plane's fuselage. After a deep sigh, he moves onward, heading towards the plane to spend a few more hours in painful luxury before returning to bliss of his cramped forest refuge...
