Victory Celebration, Featuring Ming, Jesus Christ, And A Brand New Ming Rhyme (TM)!

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/04/02 Director: Our scene opens upon an empty hallway in a luxuriously appointed house. Expensive paintings line the walls: Van Gogh, Monet, Warhol. To one side is a small table atop which sits a Ming vase. Yes, a MING vase. But whom could this hallway belong to? What FSW wrestler could possibly be in possession of such an exquisite sense of style? And why is “Edge of 17” by Fleetwood Mac blasting over the speakers? There can be only one answer… … … … Director: I said, there can be only one answer! … … … Damnit, would somebody go get Ming, that was his cue! [Suddenly the Mingster slides down the hall wearing only sunglasses and a dress shirt ala Risky Business. He turns towards the camera with a huge smile on his face and begins to get his proverbial groove on.] Director: Umm, Ming? In Risky Business Tom Cruise wore underwear. In other words, he didn’t flash his unit to the entire viewing audience… Ming: What? Oh, you mean my menacing member? Mini Ming? Surely my many fans would delight in just such an exhibition! Director: I’m sorry Ming, but we have to keep this PG. Well, PG-13 anyway. Besides, your dance moves are horrendous. Could you put on some pants? Please? Ming: Way to be a buzz kill you square. You can only dream of being as cool as me. Director: Fleetwood Mac isn’t exactly hip Ming. Now would you cover yourself already? [Ming pulls on a pair of pants] Ming: Fine, are you happy? Director: Very much so. Now then, lets get down to business. What did you have planned for this promo? I pray to god your ideas have gotten a little better since your last run in PCW. Those were simply pathetic. Ming: Well screw you too buddy, those things kicked ass! As for today, I was thinking I’d throw a party. A sexy party… Director: Umm Ming? No sexy parties, the censors will slap us with a huge fine. Ming: Fine. Well… I know! I’ll throw a party for my good buddy Lo Key! Director: I don’t think he’s your “good buddy” Ming, he did slam you onto the turnbuckle during your last match… Ming: Aww, that was just tough love. Besides, it was obviously a form of hero worship on his part, he just wanted to get close to my aura, to my essence, to the greatness that is Ming! Director: You’ve got to be kidding me. Ming: I kid you not! Lo Key is in awe of me, and if that awe results in wins, so be it. Anyway, I think I’m going to throw a party in his honor. There will be toasts, awards, and breasts. Lots of breasts. Director: No breasts Ming. Remember what I said about your sexy parties. Ming: Damnit! What if they were small breasts? Say… B Cup? Director: No. Ming: Well shit. Can we at least have alcohol? Director: No. Ming: Hookers? Director: No, those go in the sexy party category. Ming: So what can we do? Director: You could throw a gala banquet; maybe do a “this is your life” thing or something. Sorry, I’m just the director. Ming: Jesus Christ, why don’t I just bore holes into everyone in the viewing audience’s heads with a drill? [Jesus Christ appears] Jesus Christ: Hey, go ahead, no skin off my lip. [Jesus disappears again] Ming: What the fuck was that? Director: Nothing Ming, nothing, just the Son of God. Anyway I think that’s a really good idea. Ming: The drilling or the banquet? Director: Screw it, let’s do both. Ming: Alrighty. [The scene cuts to later in the day inside a large banquet hall. Hundreds of tables are set up, around which are seated seats specially modified with electric drills angled towards the person sitting in said chair’s head. Waiters make their rounds through the room, seating guests, serving champagne, and making sure the drills work perfectly. As the last of the guests is seated Ming ascends the steps to a stage at the head of the room. He steps behind a lectern, adjusts the microphone, and begins his speech.] Ming: Ladies and gentlemen, we are gathered here today to honor a good friend of mine. We have been lifelong pals, best buddies since childhood, blood brothers, and lovers… Director: Umm, not lovers Ming. Ming: Oh, right. Scratch that last one. What I mean to say is that I owe this man a lot. He’s always been there for me. He’s always been a fine upstanding citizen, a really great guy. However, that alone is not enough to warrant a banquet of such high cost to yours truly. Of course, when you’re a millionaire like me it’s not really a big deal, but you get the idea. Hell, I could buy and sell you all three times over. But I digress; this man has done far more than just a friend. No, this man has helped me in my professional life as well. In my debut match he thought so highly of me as to come out and ensure a notch in the W column. Of course, I would certainly have won anyway, but it’s the thought that counts. So, without further ado, let me present to you… LO KEY! Director: Ming? Ming: What? Director: Lo Key isn’t coming. Ming: What the hell do you mean Lo Key isn’t coming! Director: Well, I called him ealier to invite him like you told me to. And… things didn’t go so well. Ming: And you couldn’t have told me this sooner? It would’ve been nice to have been made aware of this information before I came on stage and made a fool of myself. What did he say? Director: Well, I don’t think… Ming: You’re damn right you don’t think, but you’d better tell me now or else you’re fired. Director: I believe his exact words were “Tell that stupid Chinese bastard that I didn’t do that to help him. I couldn’t care less who won that match, I just felt like hurting somebody. Tell him that if he does anything to fuck with my next match I will squash him like a bug. And tell him that he can take his little party and ram it up his ass because there’s no way in hell I’m going to attend.” Ming: Umm… [Ming looks around frantically as the audience starts laughing at him] Ming: Damnit! DAMN DAMN DAMN! STOP LAUGHING AT ME YOU FOOLS! [This only causes the audience to laugh harder, much to their own misfortune. Ming picks up a small remote control adorned only with a large red button.] Director: NO MING! DON’T! [Ming begins to laugh manically as he ignores his director and presses the button. The drills all bore into the audience member’s heads, blood spurts everywhere, mass carnage ensues, etc.] Ming: NOBODY LAUGHS AT MING! Director: Thank god I hired illegal immigrants as members of the audience… Ming: White illegal immigrants? Director: No, Mexicans. Ming: Good, Americans don’t give a shit about non-white people anyway, there’s no way a jury will convict me. [Jesus appears again.] Jesus Christ: It’s true. But Ming, they are all beloved in my eyes. Ming; Screw you Jesus. What the hell are you doing here anyway? You aren’t supposed to come back to earth until after the Rapture anyway. Ha! I just proved you’re not perfect! Jesus Christ: Oh yeah. Shit. [Jesus disappears in a flash of smoke, never to be seen again.] Director: Damnit Ming, now you’ve done it. Ming: No big deal, what did he ever do for me anyway? Let’s get this promo over with already. Director: How do you want to end it? Ming: I figured I’d go old school, bring back the old “Ming Rhyme”. Director: Didn’t you just steal that from Muhammad Ali? Ming: Shut the fuck up! Anyway, here goes: Jesse Ramey sucks for his penis is small. After I am through with him he will be in great pain. I will defeat him, and it will not be through luck. No, Jesse will cower in fear of my massive wang! [Ming drops his pants, flashes the audience, then runs out of the room laughing like a dumbass.] Director: Jesus Christ I need to find new work. Jesus Christ: That can be arranged. Director: Didn’t Ming just banish you from existence forever? Jesus Christ: Oh yeah. Fuck. [Jesus vanishes again as the camera fades out.]
