Gameplan

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/4/02 :: The scene opens with an establishing shot of the area. We see that we are in the living room of a small apartment. To the left we see a stairwell that obviously leads to the second level of the apartment. Straight ahead there is a wall that contains a door, and then a little ways down this wall we see an entertainment center. The entertainment center has a TV in the middle and other various electronics. To the right there is a wall with nothing on it except for a window in the middle. The near wall has an entrance to this room and just beyond that a black leather sofa. To the left of the sofa is an end table with a touch lamp and a cordless phone placed neatly on top of it. Laying on the black sofa is none other than a battered but far from defeated Six Finger Cisco.:: Cisco: Fucking Boda. :: Cisco is in a pair of Levi cut-off jean shorts. He is not wearing a shirt and a large bandage is wrapped around his chest.:: Cisco: Put me through a table will ya?! :: Cisco squints his face as he remembers the happenings of last week’s Glory.:: Cisco: It didn’t even hurt all that much. :: Cisco tries to sit up but is sent back to his horizontal position with a sharp pain in his sternum.:: Cisco: Heh. The way Boda wrestled on Glory he should be called… :: Cisco thinks hard for a moment.:: Cisco: …he should be called. BLOWda. :: Cisco starts to crack up at his own joke but stops again when the laughter causes him pain.:: Cisco: I R Boda~!? What the hell is that supposed to mean? Why not just say I R a fucking retarded ape~!? :: Cisco again laughs but stops in pain.:: Cisco: Ow! Bruised Sternum’s suck! :: Cisco slowly sits up and leans forward to watch the TV. “ The Crocodile Hunter” is on.:: TV: “ Alright folks…What we got hear is one of the most poisonous snakes in the world. This snake is also one of the deadliest in the world. Now, the reason for this is not only can it bite you and give you poison, but this lil’ bugga can spit the poison right at your face! Cisco: This guy is fucking nuts! TV: Now, I’m gonna put me glasses on so I don’t get spi…… Cisco: COOL!!!! TV: “…OW!!!! Me eyes! The lil’ bugga spat right in me eyes!! Don’t just stand there mates! Get me some water for me eyes! Cisco: What a retard. This guy must be in close relation to BLOWda. :: The TV cuts to a commercial. The commercial is for FSW Glory and hypes up the handicap match with Cisco vs. McClure and Violence Jack and Boda as the guest referee.:: Cisco: O’Meara is a dick. What the hell is he trying to do to me by putting me in this fucking handicap match? :: Cisco casually throws the remote on the sofa and sits back.:: Cisco: Well this is a guaranteed win! :: Cisco laughs to himself.:: Cisco: With BLOWda as the ref, I don’t have to worry about him counting me out, or better yet counting to three if my shoulders are down. God knows he has trouble remembering how to count past two. :: Cisco gets up and pretends to be referee Boda. Cisco is talking in a retard voice.:: Cisco: A der…..ONE! :: Cisco holds up one finger.:: Cisco: A der…TWO! :: Cisco holds up two fingers.:: Cisco: A der….um…erm…..LINE? :: Cisco stops the shenanigans and sits down. Cisco takes off the wrapping around his chest and inspects his bruised sternum.:: Cisco: Huh. Looks good. Should be just fine for Glory. :: Cisco shuts the TV off and lays back.:: Cisco: Okay. It’s strategy time. I need to think hard about this one. :: Cisco scratches his head as he thinks of a game plan for the handicap match.:: Cisco: Let’s see. McClure. Hmmm….. :: Cisco squints his eyes.:: Cisco: Never heard of him. :: Cisco disregards McClure and begins thinking again.:: Cisco: Violence Jack. What the fuck kind of name is that? :: Cisco laughs.:: Cisco: More like Violence Jack me off, if he thinks he is intimidating anyone with that lame-ass name…..But…..with him teaming with McClure, and that retarded ape as the ref…I am going to have to think of something. :: Cisco starts to worry, but continues to think. This goes on for about 10 minutes or so.:: Cisco: I’ve got it!! :: Cisco sits up:: Cisco: The key to winning this match relies on one thing and one thing only. I have to… :: Scene quickly cuts to black.::
