Family Altercations

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-24-02 The mind of a drunk is a lonely one or at least most people would say that. You’ve really got to look deep within and find out just how this person sees things. We must take the time to analyze one drunk in particular, because he isn’t lonely. He’s more a go-getter and a happy go lucky type person when he’s drunk. It’s the loneliness that gets to him when he isn’t drinking, it’s as if he needs the drink to stay happy in more than one way, but heck for a person of his stature, why would anything get him down? Where he comes from family stays in the family and drinking comes as a natural thing, unless you’re the outspoken member of the family. The more outspoken person in the family, the one who thinks that a college education will help him out in the long run more than any of the other members in the family. Today we find our slack jawed yokel in a confrontation with one of these members in the family who has just graduated from Harvard Law and has come back to throw his diploma in the face of all of his drunken family members. “So, tell me Jesse. What do you wish to accomplish out of life? Do you think that you’re just going to make it by on this drink of yours and your wrestling career?” Cousin Tom asks. Jesse shrugs his shoulders and takes a drink out of his moonshine bottle, “Alls I wanna do is my wrestling and my cousins.” Tom begins to shake his head at Jesse in disgust, “Obviously the family liked you more than me for a reason. That being because you were the most illiterate of the bunch, because look at this right here.” Tom picks up a piece of paper and unfolds it in front of Jesse’s face. “What cha gots here? Some kind of fancy rolling papers?” Jesse takes hold of the diploma in Tom’s hand and begins to analyze it. “No, you incompetent fool. That’s my diploma from Harvard Law! Don’t you people understand how much this means to me and to the world out there. Do you think just anyone is going to higher a drunken fool like yourself to work for them?” Tom asks Jesse. “I dunno, but them people over there at that wrastlin place sure do think I’m perrty good.” Jesse says tipping up his drink a couple more times. “Yes, indeed, I did get a chance to watch this so called job of yours. Very entertaining if you like to see grown men vomiting on each other and of course watch you staggering around in the ring like a complete buffoon.” Tom says with a smirk. “Ah, shucks. I doned puked in that ring cause that black feller went and patted me on the back. Got me all shook ups what he did. I couldn’t help it much, that shine was bounds to come back up sometimes or another.” Jesse says still trying to figure out what the papers that his cousin have handed him are. “This black person that you speak of is supposed to be your partner, but you are too drunk to even notice that.” Tom says making light of the matter. “You means I’ve got a partner down there in that wrastlin place? Kinda like an Indian guide or something? So, it’s going to be me and that black feller doing a sort of Swiss Family Robinson kind of thing from back there in the old west. Well shoot me high and call me Cornrolls, cause that’s going to be mighty fine. I’s been a needing me an Indian guide for a while. That wrastlin place is might hard to finds some times.” Jesse says as he takes another swig of the shine. Tom begins to shake his head, “You know I honestly don’t understand you at all. It seems that the more you drink the dumber you get and I really don’t want to spend the time talking to you about something of the magnitude, because you obviously aren’t going to be able to comprehend any of it.” “What’s you gots to use all them there big words for, cousin Tom? You’s know I done pasted the third grade just three months ago. They’s got me started on my multipelcation tables.” Jesse says. “See what I mean? You can’t even say the word multiplication right, Jesse.” Tom says in obvious anger. A look of brilliance comes across Jesse’s face, “Yah, that there be it Tom! You’s done told me the world. Multipelcation, I knows them really good.” Tom’s face turns blood red as he begins to stomp off in anger, not realizing that he has left his diploma with Jesse. “Looks like Tom done went and gave me his fancy rolling paper as a gift. Guess I gots to go learn my multipelcation tables now that I’s got something to smoke and drink with.” Jesse says as he begins to shred the diploma into pieces of paper long enough to be rolled. Jesse continues on with his moonshine jug on his side, occasionally bringing it up to take a drink. ~The End~
