Making the Scarecrow - Part I

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/30/02 Many times, a man doesn't realize his dream until it's too late. For years, I thought my dream was to sit behind a desk, attend to the phones, and answer questions about tax shelters, 401k, and tax laws. Even through the loss of my wife and daughter, I still kept going with the illusion that I was to be one of the greatest accountants that ever lived. But in the back of my mind, I knew something was amiss. I knew that I needed something else. After all, when was the last time you went to the Accounting Hall of Fame? So what lead me into wrestling? I'm just an average man. I'm not the greatest talent among all these new guys in FSW. Hell, I could be one of the worst. It took a while for me to perfect anything but a school boy. And I gotta admit, I was on pretty shaky ground for a long time, wondering if I made the right decisions. I still wonder to this day, as a matter of fact. But as time's gone on, I've gotten better, and added more than a few moves to the repertoire. I've learned I don't need 15 lbs of gold around my waist to make an impression on people. I mean I'm among names like Tyler Davidson, Jesse Ramsey, Magnum Wolf, and Big Daveylicious. And then there's Me. Jason O'Neil. I know what I'm here for. It's not titles, it's not power. It's not even fame. I'm here to equalize all that great talent. I've got all the skills, now it's up to me to make my dream of being the best I can be. No one else can help me. It's all up to me. But then again, I'm used to going it alone. My story is not unique. I grew up poor, and was made fun of as a child. I was the fat kid. Every class had one, and everyone just assumed extra pounds mean extra problems. Ever since the very first day of kindergarten, when I was caught sampling the white paste everyone else was using to make their nametags, I was somewhat of an outcast. The last one picked for kickball, the one who was only invited to the birthday parties because my parents were friends with the other child's parent. As I grew up, the hatred became more intense. I got into fights, but rather than prove myself as a growing man, I simply let my self get beaten, too scared to fight back. I talked funny. Other kids could say words like 'rutabaga', or 'mirror'. I on the other hand, sounded like an idiot. Hell, I couldn't even say my name without being laughed at. Often, when things didn't go my way, I would cry. That just encouraged people to make fun of me even more. Children would bring me baby shampoo, just so I would be able to have 'No More Tears.' Kleenex was offered to me daily. The only solace I found, was in my solitude. I had no friends. I had nothing but my solitude. And in your formative years, when solitude is your only answer, your mind plays tricks on you. I hid my rage well, but in my mind, I was the biggest, strongest, most well liked child on the block. Not a single person could do anything better than I could. I was the one all the girls wanted to kiss behind the school, and all the boys wanted to be on their team. That wasn't the case. Silently, my rage grew. Instead of fighting back, I grew increasingly sheltered. Inside, I ached to have someone to talk to. But I had no respect. No love. Every word, cut me like a knife. Every punch thrown to my head, or body, felt like a gunshot wound. Pretty soon, I was dead inside. The only feelings I felt were those of hatred. Seventh Grade. Even without being the class outcast, it was an awkward time. The boys were sprouting hair, and testosterone was flowing through their bodies like heroin in a junkie. Every boy was overdosing on the man made chemical. Fights broke out in the halls daily, for no reason. The girls, were finally finding boobs growing on their chest, knowing that all the boys stared at them in hopes of a Friday night make out with her sweater puppets being the main attraction. I was no different. I set my sights on Michelle Grey. Michelle, was one of the kinder girls in school, and all though she didn't hate me, her indifference towards me was better than nothing. She was my lab partner in science class, with Mrs. [name withheld]. Occasionally we talked, but nothing more than her asking me if I had done the assignment from the day before, and if she could borrow it to 'check her answers'. Of course I would let her. She always wore shirts that were just a little too big, and when she leaned over the desk, I could get a clear shot at her beginnings of cleavage. It was the single greatest thing I had ever seen at the time, and could not get enough. But her boyfriend, Rob [name withheld], didn't take too kindly to other guys even talking to her. I guess my toad like cubby face didn't matter to him. He saw poor pathetic Jason O'Neil as a threat to his territory. A threat he could easily eliminate. In seventh grade, we didn't have football yet, but, Rob was build like a linebacker. Already well over six feet, and moving in on 225 lbs., he struck fear in the hearts of boys around the school, because he was already a man. Couple that with my almost five feet of fat, bones and brain, and my dilemma was clear. Rob was going to kill me. I remember the day well. It was October, 17th 1990. Pizza day in the cafeteria. Outside, the weather was just starting to move towards the Ohio fall. It was fairly warm, with a hind of burning wood from the neighborhood fireplaces in the air. As I walked the three quarters of a mile to school, I met Michelle half way. She lived a few blocks away, and seemed to be running late. She caught my eye, and I hoped and prayed she wouldn't notice me. She caught up to me. 'Hey, Jason. Did you do last nights homework?' she asked. 'Huh? Uhm…yeah. I do it every night.' 'Could I borrow it again? I need to check my answers against yours again. I think I got everything, I just want to make sure I understood it.' What a lie. But, who was I to say no to her? 'Uhm. Sure. Do you want it now, or in class?' 'Now would be great. That way, I can check them before class, and give it back to you before. Meet me at my locker before class. I'll give it back then.' Her locker. The pinnacle of cool. All the cool kids hung out near her locker. Maybe this was my time to be cool too. Quickly, I opened my Trapper Keeper, I had bought to look cool with 5 dollars of my summer's allowance, and pulled the lab report out of my Cory Snyder folder. Snyder, was for science, because they both began with S. Pretty stupid I know, but hell, I was 12 at the time. 'Sure. I'll meet you there after fourth.' 'Thanks Jason. You're a sweetie.' She said as she hurried up to the school. I however stopped dead in my tracks. Sweetie. She called me sweetie. Not just Jason, or Fatso, or Chubbs. No. She called me Sweetie. I was in heaven. Hell I was higher than heaven. It couldn't have been any better. I floated the rest of the way to school. I didn't care that I was late. Michelle Grey had just called me sweetie, and that was enough to make up for 7 years of indifference. Little did I know, that in just 4 hours, I wouldn't be feeling as great. The bell ending third period rang, and I sprinted out of the classroom. I walked into the bathroom, and checked my hair. It looked like I had a chia pet growing out of the top of my head. I could never get my hair to do much of anything but lay there, and do whatever it wanted. Some parts stuck straight up, others stayed matted to my head. My glasses were crooked, and my Hypercolor shirt turned from blue, to red with the intense heat I was feeling. My Cavariccis, seemed a bit tight, and lopsided, but for all I knew, there was something Michelle saw in me that made her call me her sweetie. I walked out of the bathroom with all the confidence in the world, and started towards her locker. It was just on the other side of the science room, so, I walked slowly, like I had all the time in the world. As I turned to corner into the science wing, there it was. Her locker. She was there, waiting for me. So was Rob. They were talking. A few of the kids that hung around them like leeches were there too. I walked up to her and smiled. 'What the hell are you doing here?' Rob asked, slightly angry that I had even come into his territory. 'I have to get my…' I started. 'What? I can't understand you.' He mocked me. Anger raised into my body. The testosterone bubbled in my veins. 'I have to get my…' 'Jason, speak English, dude. I can't understand anything you're saying.' His friends laughed at him making fun of me. Rage slowly crept into my mind. I envisioned taking him down with a knee to the stomach, and then dropping him with a DDT. 'I need my lab report.' 'Here, Jason. Thanks.' Michelle spoke up, a bit angry at the immaturity Rob was showing. She opened her Trapper with the pink and purple hearts on it and pulled the report out of her unicorn folder. I knew it well. I saw it daily. 'Lemme see that.' Rob intercepted it. He opened the piece of paper and looked at it. 'Give that back!' I yelled, a tear coming into my eye. I could feel it coming on. I knew I was about to cry. But I had to fight it. I had to remain cool. I had to. I had to. 'Wow, Jason. How long did it take for your fat ass to do this?' He said. His friends echoed the fat ass, and laughed. I looked into his eyes. 'Two hours. And I want it back.' I went and tried to swipe it out of his hands. He was too fast, and put it over his head, still reading my handiwork. 'This is pretty good, Chubby. As a matter of fact, I like it so much, I think I'm going to take it and turn it in as my own. How's that sound?' I looked at Michelle. She looked at Rob and laughed. She laughed at me. She didn't want to be me sweetie. She wanted to use me. I had gone past enraged. Suddenly, I had tunnel vision. All I could see was the face of Rob laughing at me. And then I saw a few spots of red. Then a few more. I felt my body moving in ways it never had before. I felt my arms flying around, and my legs moving around. I felt my hands hitting something. I felt pain, I had never felt before. I had no idea what it was. And then I remember darkness. In the darkness, I looked inside myself for just what was happening. Something had taken control of me. I had no idea what it was. I had never felt it before. And then it came to me. It was my rage. My hate. My pain. And Robert [name withheld] was the one who was on the receiving end of my eight years of aggression. Slowly, the darkness faded into reality. I was in the nurses office. My entire body was sore. Tears streamed down my face. Blood was all over my new Shirt, and pants. The Nurse looked at me, and smiled. 'Are you ok?' She asked 'Yes. What happened?' I questioned. What did I do? 'You just got into a fight. That's all I know. Now that you're ok, you need to go see the principal. He's got some people that want to talk to you.' Talk? To me? Oh no. Rob must have beaten me good. He must be in jail now, and he's going to come back for me. I really screwed up now. The nurse escorted me to the principals office. It was during a class change, and there were a lot of people in the hall way. Most of them stared at me. The blood on my face was probably what they were looking at. Maybe I had a black eye. Maybe I busted my nose open. But the look in their eyes was different. Some people saw me, and whispered to their friends they were walking with. We got to the principal's office, and I sat in the plush chair. I had been there often, and had been on the end of a few beatings many times. It wasn't new to Mr. [name withheld] to see me bleeding, or with a black eye. But today, it was different. Two police officers, and my mother were there. This couldn't be good. 'Jason, what just happened out there?' Mr. [name withheld] asked me. My mother sat next to me, and took my hand. Tears came down my eyes again, as I looked at him, through the fogginess of my misting eyes. 'I…don't know, Mr. [name withheld]. I just remember Rob took my homework, and when I took it back, he beat me up.' 'He beat you up?' 'I think so. I think he knocked me out though. I don't remember anything. Is he in trouble?' 'Jason, in the hallway, you almost killed Rob. I have no idea how, but you stood up for yourself, and beat the tar out of him. Evan [name withheld], and Steve [name withheld] tried to get you off of him, but you beat them up too. Right now, Rob is on his way to Southwest General Hospital.' Tears streamed from my eyes, as I looked at the faces of the two officers. They had stern looks on their faces. I was in big trouble now. My mother, showed concern, and even shed a few tears sitting there, holding my hand. I was scared. In the end, everything was all right. I had to go to counseling for a few months, and learned to deal with my anger better. Rob had a broken jaw, a shattered cheekbone and nose, and some internal bleeding, but came out fine, and just finished up his third year in NFL Europe. Evan and Steve were ok too, just shocked that Chubby had beaten the hell out of them. From that day on, no one messed with me. No one called me chubby, and no one made fun of my impediment. I still had no friends, and Michelle…No matter what happened, she'll always be beautiful. Just like she was on our wedding night.
