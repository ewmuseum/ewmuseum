The Awakening Begins

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-24-02 The slow silence of the wind rolling across the porch of a small little wooden cabin, the sky filled full of nothing more than dark clouds as the thunder in the distance is heard coming even more closer and a large bolt of lightning crashes to the ground, emitting so much light that it almost sends the camera man into shock. The trees swaying in the background and the sound of bark cracking as several of the old pieces of large wood begin to topple over and come crashing down to the ground. A single two by four smacks against the side of the cabin as it’s nail has come out of place and left it just out in the wide open. The roof made completely tin metal, which is also flapping up and down crashing against the wooden frame work holding it up and exposing what little insulation that it has underneath. Inside the home we find a little lantern, the light inside is flickering up and down, almost going out as the breeze turns into heavy winds and the front door begins to slam shut and open until finally a figure steps into the room and the light goes completely out. The figure closes the door and begins making its way over near the lantern, lighting a match and turning it back on. The figure pulls the lantern up next to his face, and we find that this man is Jesse Ramey. He slowly begins to make his way into the next room of the little cabin and he sits down at a small wooden table, in which he has a bunch of papers set out on and a couple of pencils. The camera zooms in on the piece of paper that is lying on the table, dated September 17, 2002, and in turn it reads: “Although completely thought out to be the dumbest person alive, only time will tell the truth of the matter. For I am nearly out of this torment that many a man would call Hell. Even the great writer Dante would describe this place of malice and torment that I am living in to be Hell. Not only that, but for the love of all mighty God, I’ve come to realize that this person is no more than an insolent land dweller. Not one of your average one’s either, this one has enormous traits and flaws. Taken into consideration is the fact that he does not know anything, or at least from what I can gather he doesn’t. I may be able to see into his mind, but I don’t know for certain whether or not this, what he is doing, is all a mere ploy or if it’s in truth a reality that he holds dear to his own. The time is late and I’ve not much more time to speak as he slowly begins to regain power of what will soon be mine.” Jesse: (Looking down at the paper oddly) I done understand none this stuff. The clouds begin to pour down with the heavy sound of rain beating down hard upon the tin roof as the paper lying on the desk flies up into the air and twists around landing on the ground, revealing another paper dated only two days ago and it reads: “More and more, I can’t quite figure out what the problem may be with him, I’ve tried to analyze things to my fullest. Yet, he still obviously lacks common sense and this creature that he brings along with him, he’s only using him as a mere pet. Does he not realize his full potential as a war beast? Not only that, but all of this surrounding is nearly bringing me to the point of total breakdown. He has so much money, yet he doesn’t decide to do anything with it other than keep it in the local bank. I mean, I’ve seen his bankbook before and though the penmanship isn’t all that well, it’s obvious that he holds well over a couple hundred million dollars. He has a legacy to his name, but he doesn’t use it and nothing is going to change that for him. I’ve got to get back into the game here, I can’t stand just lying back and doing nothing. I’ve been dormant for nearly three months now and I’ve let him run free a little bit, so to speak, and it’s time I finally come back in to take over. Other than that, I’ve been having the strangest craving for alcoholic beverages, for some reason. Never before in the past has this ever occurred to me, but the taste is so sweet. It’s nearly dawn though, I must be going now, for soon it wakes.” Jesse: Dis’ here be some weird stuff. Jesse stands up and walks over to his little torn up bed. Jesse: I’s just gonna have to stop and sleeps on it for a little while. The scene fades to black as the thunder continues on in the background with enormous rage.
