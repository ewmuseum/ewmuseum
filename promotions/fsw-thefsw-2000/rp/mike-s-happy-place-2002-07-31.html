Mike's Happy Place

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Wednesday, July 31st, 2002 [Scene opens in the den of your Canadian Hero, 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous and 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson. The best friends are both sitting on seperate chairs, watching SpongeBob SquarePants while eating Extreme Barbecue Doritos and drinking MARVELicious Soda. Mini-Mike is sitting on his mini rocking chair and Sparky, Jacob's two-tegged steel-testicled talking wonder dog is asleep on the floor] Mike Marvelous: Who lives in a pineapple under the sea! Jacob Jackson: Sponge..Bob..Square..Pants! Mike Marvelous: This show ROCKS! Jacob Jackson: Go SpongeBob, go SpongeBob! [Sparky awakens at all this ruckus and looks up at Jacob and Mike] Sparky: What are you two morons going on about? Mike Marvelous: It's our favorite show, Sparky. You wouldn't understand. Sparky: It's a talking yellow sponge! What's to understand? Mike Marvelous: Why don't you go take yourself for a walk or something...right into traffic... Sparky: Hey...watch it! I may be a dog but I can still whup your ass! Mike Marvelous: Oh yeah! You couldn't whoop me if both my arms were tied behind my ba-- [Mike suddenly stops. His eyes bulge out of his head as he is choking on a Dorito.] Jacob Jackson: What is it, Michael? Mike Marvelous: Ggggh! Rrrrr! Jacob Jackson: What are you trying to tell us? [Mike holds his throat hoping that they'll understand he is choking then help him] Jacob Jackson: Let me guess...your neck hurts? A vampire bit you? You had an accident while doing yoga? [Mike opens his mouth and begins pointing down his throat] Sparky: You deep throated a mule? Jacob Jackson: You had a root canal? You got your tonsels taken out? Man, I hate charades... Sparky: Haha, he's starting to turn red.... Jacob Jackson: Michael, just because I can't guess what you're trying to be is no reason to get angry! [Mike begins trying to apply the hymlec maneuvar to himself] Jacob Jackson: Stop hitting yourself in the stomach, Michael! You have a match tonight! [Mike sinks back in his chair...he is now the color blue. His eyes roll back in his head] Jacob Jackson: Michael...are you high on drugs? [Mike begins twitching] Jacob Jackson: You are, aren't you! I always suspected you were keeping something from me! [Mike begins to slowly pass out, when Mini-Mike finally realizes whats wrong and runs head first into Mike's stomach. Mike's eyes bug out and the Dorito shoots out of his mouth and smashes the t.v. screen. Mike walks over to Jacob and grabs him by the shirt] Mike Marvelous: I WAS CHOKING YOU STUPID MORON! I COULD HAVE DIED IF IT WASN'T FOR MINI-MIKE! Jacob Jackson: Stop yelling, Michael! Remember what your anger management counselor said! [Mike flashes back to his anger management meeting with Dr. Sphincter] Dr. Sphincter: When you become angry...just take a deep breath and go into your happy place....haaaappy plaaaace.... [Mike opens his eyes and releases Jacob. He then takes a deep breath, closes his eyes, and drifts off...into....] BUM BUM BUM....Mike's Happy Place! [Mike awakens in his very own special happy place...in his happy place, he is riding on an open car in front of the Parliament Buildings in Ottawa waving to thousands of fans who are lining the streets, cheering for him. People are throwing flowers to him and reaching out to touch him. His best friend, Jacob Jackson, is beside him, also waving to the fans. Mini-Mike is driving the car with a little chauffeur hat on.] Mike Marvelous: You all love me! You reeeaally LOVE me! Thank you, thank you! I love me too! [Jean Cretchien, Prime Minister of Canada, walks out of the crowd and up to the car. The car stops and the Can-Am Connection get out. Jean Cretchien holds out some sort of a key to Mike] Jean Cretchien: To show our appreciation for all you've done for you country, we'd like to give you the Key to Canada. Viva le Mike Marvelous! [Jean Cretchien hands the key to Mike and shakes his hand as the audience cheers. Mike and Jacob get back into the car and continue riding and soking up the cheers of the adoring fans when a loud POP is heard. Mike and Jacob both whip their heads to the left, thinking it is a gun shot, but it is actually a little kid who just blew a bubble in his bubble gum.] Mike Marvelous: Whew, that scared me. [They continue riding when ANOTHER pop is heard, but this time, it is just a mischievous little brat popping the baloon of his little brother with a pin] Mike Marvelous: That scared me too... [More pops are heard. Mike and Jacob whip their heads to the right and it is some chubby, mentally retarded guy popping the bubbles on bubble wrap while grinning] Mike Marvelous: Mini-Mike...drive faster... [Suddenly, another pop is heard...this time from the Book Suppository. There is a pain in Mike's throat, then another pop is heard from the grassy knowl and then...] Jacob Jackson: MICHAEL! WAKE UP! Mike Marvelous: Huh! [Mike blinks and he is back in his den. Jacob, Sparky and Mini-Mike are staring at him] Jacob Jackson: What the heck happened? One minute you were yelling at me then the next minute you're screaming about a shooter or something... Mike Marvelous: Whoo...it was just a dream. But this means my happy place is no longer safe. Jacob Jackson: Remember what Dr. Sphincter said... [Scene switches again back to Dr. Sphinchter's office] Dr. Sphincter: If at some point your happy place does not work, it is because of some subconscious thing that is bothering you. Try to find out what it is, then get rid of that negative thing and your happy place will be safe again... [Mike thinks as Jacob watches, hoping he is not going to go into some sort of dreamworld again] Mike Marvelous: I've got it! I know what the problem is that's causing the problem! It's TIMO BOJANGLES! Jacob Jackson: Bolamba... Mike Marvelous: Bochumba. Jacob Jackson: Bolamba... Mike Marvelous: Bodumba. Jacob Jackson: Bolamba! Mike Marvelous: Timo! HE'S the root of my problems! Him and all his newbie friends coming in trying to take over MY federation! OUR federation! WE helped build FSW, Jacob! And they're just trying to destroy it! Jacob Jackson: I know, but once you beat Timo tonight and I win the battle royal, we won't have to worry about them anymore. Mike Marvelous: I HAVE to beat him, Jacob! The fate of FSW depends on it! If I DON'T beat him, then the newbies will take over and ruin everything we've worked so hard on! It has to be ME in the World Title match at Genesis, not HIM! Jacob Jackson: Don't worry, Michael. You'll beat him. Mike Marvelous: You're right! I'm an FSW Hall of Famer! What are you, Timo? President of Samoa? Nobody cares about you OR your stupid homeland way out in the middle of the Atlantic ocean! I say we nuke Samoa then use it for garbage storage! That's all it's good for! Tonight, it's the Canadian Hero vs. The Samoan Silencer. FSW Grandslam Champion vs.....former PCW TV Champion. So what Timo, I was PCW TV Champion before too! Everything YOU can do, I can do 10 times better! So from now on...Brush you teeth...pay your taxes...drink 6-8 glasses of milk a day...but most importantly, be like MIKE! [Scene ends as Mike walks over to the Dorito he choked on before, pops it into his mouth to eat it, but starts choking on it again. The end.]
