Genesis: Creation

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/31/02 In the beginning, Lord-Key created the heavens and the earth. And Lord –Key said, “Let there be light.” And there was light. Lord-Key saw that the light was good, and he separated the light from the darkness. Lord-Key called the light “face,” and the darkness “heel.” And there were faces and there were heels – the first day. And Lord-Key said, “Let there be an expanse between the fans and the wrestlers to separate them.” And Lord-Key made the expanse, and it was kayfabe. And there was in character and there was out of character – the second day. And Lord-Key said, “Let the wrestling world be separated into two places.” And Lord-Key separated them, and they became segments and matches. And so the makeup of individual shows was changed forever – the third day. Then Lord-Key said, “Let the shows be divided up into big and small.” And they were divided, and they became TV shows and Pay Per Views. And Lord-Key saw that it was good. And there were regular shows, and there were PPVs – The fourth day. And Lord-Key said “Let there be lights in the expanse of the stadiums to mark wrestler entrances.” And there were lights, and they were called production value. God made one great light at the top of the entrance ramp, and it was called pyro. And it was good, and so there was production value – the fifth day. And Lord-Key said, “Let the federations be formed and managed by men of power who will rule in my place.” And there were bookers, and they were a mixed lot, and Lord-Key saw that they were not all good. However, the bookers of FSW were very good, and Lord-Key was pleased. And so it came about – the sixth day. And Lord-Key said, “Let there be main from all walks of life who write competitively in this world I have created.” And there were e-wrestlers. Some were good, and some were bad. Lord-Key saw this, and he saw the mediocre wrestlers, and he sent them to train in schools. And Lord-Key also saw those of medium skill, and he sent them to develop in smaller leagues. And Lord-Key took note of those with great skill and placed them in the big leagues. And so the PWA, PTC, and WT were formed – the seventh day. So Lord-Key created e-fedding in his own image, and he created one e-wrestler in particular to rule them all. He blessed this wrestler and granted him all that resides in the e-wrestling world. And so AdaMing was created, and he was very good. And so AdaMing walked the world of e-fedding at his leisure. He was free to do as he pleased, when he pleased. He made friends with all the other e-wrestlers and gave them names. “I’ll call you Snow… and you would make a good Darkstar… you would be an excellent Howard Porter…” And so it went. AdaMing spent many many years just enjoying himself, wrestling as he pleased, partaking in the pleasures of the world that Lord-Key had created for him. That is until AdaMing hungered for something else. “Lord-Key, this is all really great and wonderful and all, but I’m starting to get bored. I’d really like to have some company here. It would be especially nice if this company utilized this fleshy thing you attached to me below my stomach. I mean, my right hand is nice, but I was thinking that maybe if someone else stimulated it…” Lord-Key, being a kind, understanding, and horny god knew exactly what AdaMing meant, and so he went about fixing the issue. Lord-Key used his celestial might to knock AdaMing out, and, while unconscious, removed AdaMing’s spine, forever cursing AdaMing to a life of intense wimpyness. Lord-Key then formed a new being much like AdaMing, however far different in certain… private areas. And so the first woman was formed from the flesh of man, and Lord-Key named her: BethanEve. “AdaMing? AdaMing? Wake up; I have someone to introduce to you…” said the Lord as AdaMing slowly rose to his feet. Ming looked around for a few moments. “But Lord, where is this person? I don’t see anyone.” “Look down AdaMing.” Ming looked down and smiled at the small female form before him. BethanEve glances at AdaMing. Her eyes go wide, then narrow to slits as she screams, “DIE DIE DIE DIE DIE DIE DIE!” BethanEve rushed towards the spineless figure of AdaMing, who quickly hightails it out of there. “Shit Lord, what is this wretched fiend? As AdaMing slowly recovered BethanEve had managed to obtain a large pointy stick and proceeded to stalk the prone man with it. Luckily for the future of humankind, AdaMing managed to rise to his feet just in time, and outran the little terror. “Phew, that was a close one.” Lord-Key looked down from his celestial perch and realized that he had forgotten something. “Oh, AdaMing, I forgot something. I am God.” “Oh, right.” And so life went on for AdaMing and his 12-year-old wife. Nothing ever got finished due to AdaMing having to be constantly on guard against attack. Nothing got done that is, until DarkSatan, the lord of all that is; well, dark, made contact with AdaMing. One day, while AdaMing was walking through the garden of wrestling, a serpent approached him. “Hey, you, come over here a second.” Hissed DarkSatan to the naïve and unsuspecting AdaMing. Oh, if only he had simply ignored these worlds, all would be right in the world of e-wrestling. But it was not to be. AdaMing walked over to the serpent and said, “Yes?” “Hello, I’ve been noticing you around the garden lately, and I thought you might want some… companionship” “Whoa, buddy, I may be a little perveted, but bestiality is where I draw the line!” “No, no. Look, I just wanted to help you out. I’ve seen that bitch stalking you, and I figured that you would probably be, how shall I put this… appreciative to whoever dealt with that little problem, eh?” “Well, yes, but I don’t want Lord-Key to get angry at me. Last time I pissed him off he smote me good.” “Don’t you mean smite?” “Whatever, my point is that I’m not making a move unless I have some assurance that this wont be traced back to me.” “Hey, no problem, in fact my plan will ultimately result in her being blamed rather than you.” “Well, ok,” uttered Ming, and so the course of wrestling history was changed forever. DarkSatan convinced BethanEver to eat from the tree of “push” (OOC: Hey, give me a break, I couldn’t think of anything better,) she convinced Ming to do so as well, and they were both cast out of e-wrestling. Ming, feeling betrayed, cursed at DarkSatan for his fate. “Damnit! What a gyp! First God gives me some stupid 12-year-old whore who wants to kill me, now DarkSatan got me kicked out of the world of e-wrestling. What’s next? Hellfire and brimstone? Jobbing to Mike Frampton? I mean, come on, give me a fucking break!” Famous last words, as soon Ming would discover a fate worse than death: fatherhood. Alan and Ed both grew up into strapping young men, raised by their bitch of a mother and asshole of a father. Now Alan grew up to resent his father, for indeed, AdaMing was a complete dick, and a pathetic father at best. He missed all of Alan’s baseball games; never spent any time with him, you know the drill. Ed on the other hand was the proverbial apple of AdaMing’s eye. They did everything together. Baseball games movies, they even double-teamed various women, all as a family. As a result, Ed was well loved by everyone: a sweet caring guy, loving, happy go lucky, and very god fearing. Alan on the other hand hated people, killed small animals for fun, and was universally reviled. One day, Lord-Key met with the two young men. “Sons of AdaMing, I have a task for you. I want each of you to make a sacrifice to me, something to show how much you love me, your creator.” Ed instantly got to work, preparing a lavish gift for his creator, for indeed, he was very god fearing. Alan on the other hand was smart enough to realize that this was really just a setup for Lord-Key to get free food. So, when Lord-Key returned to earth to collect his presents, he found a platter of meats, fruits, and all sorts of sweets gathered for him by Ed. “Very good Ed my boy, I am quite pleased. Rest assured you will be well rewarded in the future for your show of fealty.” Of course, this was all a lie; Lord-Key had no intention of ever rewarding either of the two boys. But Ed would never be made aware of this knowledge, as he would not live long past this conversation. Yay irony! “Now then Alan, let’s see what you brought me…” “I didn’t bring you shit you freeloading bastard.” “Why you little shit, I’m going to kill you!” “No, I don’t think so. You see, I rewired all of that machinery you have under your clothes that give you special powers while you were looking over Ed’s stuff.” “Umm…. Shit.” “Indeed, now then, prepare to face my wrath.” And so Alan pulled out an olive branch and brandished it at Lord-Key. Lord-Key glanced at it, and then burst out laughing. “You have got to be kidding me. An olive branch? What are you going to do? Peace me to death? Oh ho ho ho, I kill me.” “Wrong bitch, if you had looked closer you would realize that this olive branch has a skull painted on it.” “You don’t mean… No, you wouldn’t.” “Oh yes I would. Prepare for the OLIVE BRANCH OF DOOM~!” “Don’t you mean of fear?” “No, it’s supposed to be a parody of the Palmpilot of Doom, get it?” “Oh, ok.” And so Alan smote Lord-Key over the head with the OLIVE BRANCH OF DOOM! Lord-Key disappeared from the face of the earth never to be seen again, for indeed, the OLIVE BRANCH OF DOOM was extremely frightening. Meanwhile, Ed, being the hardcore icon that he is, had climbed onto a ladder that he jury-rigged while Alan was dealing with the Lord of all creation. “Hey Alan, watch this,” screamed Ed, while attempting to nail Alan with a summersault bodypress. Alan merely rolled his eyes, lifted his OLIVE BRANCH OF DOOM and impaled Ed’s body upon it. And so it came to pass that the world of e-wrestling was created, the first e-wrestler was cast out of e-wrestling nirvana, and Alan Kriegman killed Ed… Err, I mean Alan KriegCain kille Ed NovAbel, thus changing the face of e-wrestling forever. “DIE DIE DIE DIE DIE DIE!” Oh, and a little girl named BethanEve was formed as well.
