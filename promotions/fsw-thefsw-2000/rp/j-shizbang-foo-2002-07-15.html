J Shizbang, foo!

15 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, July 15th [Scene opens in the secret Canadian lair of 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous. Well...actually, it's not a lair, it is just a mansion. Anyway, Mike and Roy the Cameraman are just making their way to through the front door. They find themselves in the main foyer of Mike's house. The they step inside] Mike Marvelous- Welcome to my secret...Canadian laaaair! MOO HOO HA HA HA HA HAAAA.... Roy the Cameraman- Ummm...secret? If it's secret, then why is the mail man coming to the door right now? Mike Marvelous- Well...it's not exactly...secret. But..it's mysterious, isn't it? Roy the Cameraman- No. Mike Marvelous- Darn. Oh well. MORTIMER! [After a couple seconds, a black man in a black suit comes out of the kitchen and into the foyer. He looks like a butler...except for the gold jewelery...and teeth...and crazy dread locks.] Mike Marvelous- Mortimer...how many times have I told you that when I knock at the door, you come and open it to greet me. That's what all butlers do. And what if it wasn't me? What if it was a guest? They wouldn't have been able to get in! Another mistep like this, buster, and I'm afraid you are go-- Mortimer- Yes massa! Toby be good ni**a, massa! FOOL! What-do-I like like to you, your slave? Listen bitch! First of all, mah names not 'Mortimer'. All the homies in Compton and all the hoes call me J Shizbang, an' I be representin', white boy. So when you need to get in your crib, let your own white ass in. Don't nobody need ta be openin' the door for you. Mike Marvelous- .... Mortimer- Look at you with your mouth open like you waitin' for someone to stick their dick in it. Whassamatter? Scared of the black man? Mike Marvelous-I..I.. Mortimer- [Laughs] Quick! Call the po-lice! We got a black man here! No wait...call the army! The black man is threatenin' to take over the city! Mike Marvelous- Mortimer...this is not what I pay you for. Mortimer- Well, if you don't like the way I be butlerin', you go find someone else. But if you do...I'll be suein' your ass on the grounds of racism...and then, after we go to court...I might just give you a little visit. Mike Marvelous- Racism?! That's absurd! I have not said ONE thing to you about your race! I hired you, didn't I? That's not being racist! Mortimer- You hired me cause I had a gun to your head... Mike Marvelous- Oh yeah...I forgot about that. But I hired you nevertheless! So at least TRY to be a decent butler! Mortimer- Hey, I should be gettin' payed for just wearin' this penguin suit you got me wearin'! Mike Marvelous- Mortimer...will you please go get us a couple drinks. We'll be in the living room. [Mortimer walks away while grumbling something] Mortimer- If he calls me Mortimer one more time I'm gonna tie his nuts around his neck and hang im' upside down... Mike Marvelous- What did you say?! Mortimer- Nothing 'massa'. I'll have your drinks in a minute...ass. [Mortimer walks into the kitchen while Mike and Roy the Cameraman make their way to the living room.] Roy the Cameraman- Wow...that guy sure has a chip on his shoulder. Maybe you should hire a new butler... Mike Marvelous- Sorry, I don't feel like waking up with my throat slit thank you very much...have a seat. [Roy sits on the green couch in Mike's living room while Mike sits in his burgandy stuffed-leather chair.] Mike Marvelous- One second. I have to call down an old friend... [Mike presses a button on the arm of his chair and speaks into the chair as if it had a microphone in it] Mike Marvelous- Mini-Mike, to the living room now. Mini-Mike, to the living room now. Roy the Cameraman- Mini-Mike? [Seconds later, a midget that looks exactly like Mike...except smaller...comes trotting down the stairs. Roy stands up in shock.] Roy the Cameraman- Oh my God! It's...it's a mini-you! Mike Marvelous- Brilliant observation there, Sherlock!...Mini-Mike...hug. [Mini-Mike trots over to Mike and gives him a hug] Mike Marvelous- So Mini-Mike, what do you think of my new cameraman? [Mini-Mike looks at Roy and begins speaking in his little midget speech impediment.] Mini-Mike- Woy wooks wike he couwd use a swift kick in the bawws... Mike Marvelous- Not yet. Get to know him first. Well, let's get down to business, Roy. What are your credentials? Roy the Cameraman- Umm...I did some camera work for documentaries...sporting events...pornos. You know, that sort of stuff. I also did the camerawork for Sherry and Lamb Chops, but I got fired when I asked Lamb Chops what it was like to have someone's hand shoved up her ass. My greatest experience was filming the movie Pee Wee Herman's Big Adventure. I'm pretty proud of that one. [Roy smirks arrogantly and leans on the bookshelf, but as he does, the book pushes back and the bookcase begins to move] Mike Marvelous- NO! Don't touch th-- Roy the Cameraman- WHAT THE HELL!?! [The bookcase revolves and as it does, it pushes Roy with it, until it is completely turned around and Roy is gone] Mike Marvelous- *Sigh* What an idiot. Well, Mini-Mike...looks like we have to take an unscheduled trip...to the MARVEL CAVE! [Mike and Mini-Mike spring to their feet and run over to a statue in the living room and pull down on the statue's arm. Immediately, two sections of the wall open up to reveal two poles. Mike and Mini-Mike slide down the poles and touch down...in the secret MARVEL CAVE. There they find Roy, laying on the black rock ground of the Marvel Cave, holding his head] Roy the Cameraman- Whoa...what a trip. One minute I'm telling you my filming experience, then the next minute I'm being pushed behind a secret revolving book case and sliding down a huge slide to a secret cave... Mike Marvelous- You idiot! I told you not to touch that! Roy the Cameraman- Sorry...my bad. Mike Marvelous- *Sigh* Well...welcome to the 'secret' Marvel Cave, where I suit up to fight crime with my super powers. It also is the holding area for the Mike Mobile. Roy the Cameraman- ...Why don't you just get suited up in the bathroom and keep the car in the garage..? Mike Marvelous- Hey, Mr. Party Pooper, keep your suggestions to yourself! It's much more fun to have a secret cave beneath my mansion complete with secret entrances! Roy the Cameraman- Sorry. Well, do you want to shoot a promo for your match on Glory? Mike Marvelous- Yeah, might as well. My comments on this match are long overdue... [Roy turns on his ever present camera] Roy the Cameraman- And ACTION! Mike Marvelous- Greetings Ladies, Gentlemen and Marvelites! Tis' I! Your Canadian Hero! I'm sure you all witnessed my return on the first Glory of the 'new era' of FSW. You all saw my actions on the last two shows. And you all saw my classy, witty commentary on the 2nd Glory show. And now, on the third Glory show that is scheduled for this Wednesday, I am taking on Johnny Raindance and Timo Bojangles. Mini-Mike- Bolamba... Mike Marvelous- Babumba. Mini-Mike- Bolamba... Mike Marvelous- Chumbawumba. Mini-Mike- Bolamba... Mike Marvelous- Bo Raider. Mini-Mike- BOLAMBA! Mike Marvelous- OOOOooh...Bolamba! Yes, I am taking on Johnny Raindance and Timo Bolamba in a tag team match with a mystery partner. Let me just say this...Johnny...Timo...you don't stand a chance. When I reveal my mystery partner, it will send shockwaves through FSW. Anyway...moving on.. Johnny Raindance. That name sounds familiar...let me think.....OH! Yes, I remember. You're one of the people I beat in PCW to win the TV Title! Well, don't let that discourage you, tiger! I'll give you another chance to beat the Marvelous One. Hehe, wait a minute, I forgot. You have no chance of beating me. You never did. You never will. You are just a washed up indian and worst of all...you're on MY turf now! So why don't you go back to the reserve and sniff gasoline with the rest of your buddies and stop taking up time on MY show! Timo Bolamba. To you I have one thing to say...who ARE you!?! I have never heard of you before in my life! And believe me...with a name like Timo Badumba, I'd remember you. So, what are you doing in the main event on Glory against your Canadian Hero when I have never heard of you in my life? They say you're the Samoan Silencer...where's Samoa? I never heard of it. Since I never heard of you, since I have never heard of your home land, since I have no clue who you are, since you are obviously an unknown in the wrestling business, then this conversation is not taking place. You do not exist, so stop taking up my time. So on Glory, I am taking on a has-been and a never-was. This is the best that PCW/OSW has to offer? What are THEY doing in the main event. People like Big Daveylicious, EoE, Leon Chambers, and Jack Brooze should be in the main events. People that were in FSW in it's glory days and helped to forge it's legacy in their blood and in their sweat. But yet, we have Johnny Raindance and Timo What's-his-name sharing the spotlight with the true legends of this company. There is something wrong with this picture. Do you people think you can just barge in here and share the spotlight with us!?! Well, look at the record so far. In the last two main events, Team FSW has won, because WE know how to work as a team. We have a mutual respect for each other. You two can't even work as a team because you are so busy fighting each other! The 'team' of Johnny Raindance and Timo Bo-lamp-post has already lost to Big D and EoE, now they are going to lose to 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous and his mystery opponent. Then who will be next to challenge team FSW?! Will it be...Tim Walden? What is he gonna do...bleed on us? Oh...maybe it will be Jesse Ramey. Yeah, Jesse Ramey will step up! Too bad the only thing he's good at is getting pinned. Maybe it will be Ming! The so-called 'King of Mind Games'. Oh...I forgot...you have to have a brain to be the King of Mind Games. So...I have come to the conclusion that all the newcomers suck and that Team FSW will take over in the matter of weeks to bring FSW back to the way it USED to be. And if you don't believe me...just sit back and watch. It is also painfully obvious that I will be the first FSW World Champion of the new era. And if you don't believe THAT, just take a stroll through the Hall of Fame in the FSW Headquarters. Keep walking. You will see a picture of 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson...my best friend and former tag team partner, as well as multiple title holder. Then you will see Creature...former World Champion. Then...at the end of the hall in a big brass frame, you will see a picture of your Canadian Hero. Former Primetime Champion, Intercontinental Champion, Tag Team Champion and World Champion. It doesn't get any better than that...but it will. I have already done everything there is to do in FSW...and now I am going to do it all again. I am not just an average FSW Superstar. I AM FSW! I've been known to tell people to 'Be like Mike'. Well, I just realized something...you CAN'T be like Mike. I am one in a million. I am unequalled. I am the one and only. And to those of you who might be looking to get in my way...JUST TRY AND STOP ME! [Scene fades to black as Roy shuts off the camera]
