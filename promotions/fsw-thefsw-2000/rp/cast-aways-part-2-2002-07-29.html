Cast Aways Part 2

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, July 29, 2002 [Scene opens on the desert island where we left off.] Jacob Jackson: It's true, Michael! WE'RE STRANDED! Waaaaaa! [Jacob begins to break down crying again but Mike buts his hand out for him to stop] Mike Marvelous: Hey, we may be stranded...but at least now we have food! [Jacob's eyes suddenly light up and his frown turns into a smile] Mike Marvelous: All we have to do is cook up this seagull and voila! Dinner for two! Jacob Jackson: But Michael...how are we going to cook it without fire? Mike Marvelous: Yeah....you're right. We're going to have to find a way to make fire. Maybe if we rub two sticks together... Jacob Jackson: Michael, that only works in the movies! Mike Marvelous: Then how else are we gonna build a fire, Mr. Smarty P. Ants? Jacob Jackson: We could use a match... Mike Marvelous: And where the heck are we going to find a-- [Mike is interrupted as Jacob pulls out a book of matches] Mike Marvelous:....match. Jacob, where did you get those?...Jacob...do you smoke? [Jacob begins to sweat] Jacob Jackson: No...that's absurd, Michael!...I would never go near cigarettes! My mother taught me better! Hey, in all our years as best friends, you've never seen me smoke ONCE! So there! There's your proof! Mike Marvelous: Oh yeah? Then what's THIS in your pocket! [Mike reaches into Jacob's pocket with lightning speed and pulls out a pack of Players Light as Jacob looks on in shock, unable to react quick enough to stop him. Mike holds the package of cigarettes up and frowns and Jacob bows his head in shame] Mike Marvelous: Well, well, well Mr. Smoker! Looks like I've found your cancer sticks! Jacob Jackson: Give those back! Mike Marvelous: How pathetic. [Mike shakes his head] From now on, if you wanna smoke...you're gonna have to find some scuba gear! [Mike throws the pack out into the ocean] Jacob Jackson: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Mike Marvelous: You'll thank me one day, Jacob. I just saved your lif-- [Mike is cut off as Jacob punches him square in the jaw. Mike falls down like a sack of potatoes and Jacob jumps on him and begins strangling him] Jacob Jackson: I'll kill you! KILL YOU! Mike Marvelous: Jacob! *gasp* It was for your own *choke* good! [Jacob slowly stops choking Mike until he lets go all together and breaks down crying] Jacob Jackson: *sniff* I'm sorry, Michael...I'm just so mixed up right now. We're stranded on a frickin' island! Mike Marvelous: [rubbing his neck] I know, I know...I'm scared too. But for now, let's make the best of things and have some food, ok? Jacob Jackson: Ok. [Minutes later, there is a fire going on the beach with the aid of Jacob's matches and the plucked form of the dead seagull is impaled on a stick over the fire, roasting. Mike and Jacob sit around the fire] Mike Marvelous: Mmmm...it smells so good. Jacob Jackson: Let's it it! It should be ready! Mike Marvelous: Alright. [Mike takes the stick away from the fire and slides the roasted bird off. He tears off a piece of the meat and hands it to Jacob, who timidly pops it in his mouth] Jacob Jackson: Mmmm! It's GOOD! Mike Marvelous: [Takes a bite and chews] You're right! Who would of thought seagull would taste so much like KFC? [Jacob shrugs his shoulders and continues eating. After about 15 minutes, both of them finish stripping the bones of the precious meat and they get up] Mike Marvelous: I'm stuffed. Couldn't eat another bite. Jacob Jackson:...I'm still hungry....where's the fries, Michael? Where's the coleslaw!? Mike Marvelous: Jacob, you have to understand that we have limited food supply here on this island. You also have to get used to eating close to nothing until we die or are someday rescued. Infact, that seagull was a blessing...that's probably the best meal we'll have in our long stay on this island. Jacob Jackson: I knew it...we ARE going to die. But I'd at least like a whopper combo with a poutine and a large pepsi before I die...no, make that a double whopper. Mmmm...whopper... [Mike slaps Jacob in the face] Mike Marvelous: Jacob, snap out of it! Dreaming will only make things worse! Now let's go find shelter! It's getting dark! Jacob Jackson: Ok...mmm...whopper. [After about an hour of searching, the Can-Am Connection come across a small cavern in a big mass of rock near the beach, hidden by the trees. Mike and Jacob sit against the wall of the cave with their arms stretched out as the moon shines through a small hole in the top of the cave] Mike Marvelous: Home sweet home. This cave will make excellent shelter. Jacob Jackson: Yeah, I admit...it's nice to have something resembling home...like a roof over our heads. Mike Marvelous: Hey, we'll probably be home in no time. Too bad I have to miss my match on Glory for the World Title match at Genesis...I worked so hard to make this comeback, Jacob...and now all that work is gone...now Timo Bolimbo will be in the World Championship Match...what an embarrassement. He hasn't even been in FSW for a month. Stupid newbie. And it's all because of HIM that we're stranded here! Jacob Jackson: And I'm going to miss the battle royal for the World Title shot... Mike Marvelous: Well...what would of happened if you won, Jacob? You would have to face me for the World Title... Jacob Jackson: Hmmm....interesting indeed.... Mike Marvelous: Well, if it comes down to that, may the best man win! Jacob Jackson: I'll shake on that! [The friends shake hands] Jacob Jackson: I just forgot...the best man WON'T win because we're not going to BE there. Mike Marvelous: You never know...a miracle could happen and we could be rescued and somehow make it to Glory. Jacob Jackson: HA! Nobody's going to find us here! Even if they do, we'll be dead before they even get here! Mike Marvelous: Don't give up hope, Jacob. For tomorrow...I am going to find some way to get us off this island! Mark my words! [Mike suddenly makes a weird face as he stares off into space] Mike's mind: Timo....I know you can read my mind. Wherever you are...know this. I WILL be at Glory, and I WILL deliver a Five-Star beating on your large Samoan butt! How you like THEM apples? Timo Bolamba's voice in Mike's mind: You suck Marvelous. I'm going to whoop you like a red headed stepson! Mike's mind: We'll see about that! Jacob Jackson: Michael...MICHAEL! [Mike suddenly blinks and shakes his head] Mike Marvelous: What? Jacob Jackson: You were in some sort of a trance! Mike Marvelous: Oh...yes...a trance... Jacob Jackson:...You're weird. G'night, Michael. Mike Marvelous: G'night, Jacob. [The two best friends dose off into sleep as the moon shines down on their heads. Will they get off the island in time to be at Glory? Or will they be forever stranded?] To be continued...
