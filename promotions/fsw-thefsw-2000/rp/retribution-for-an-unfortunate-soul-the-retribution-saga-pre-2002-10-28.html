Retribution for an Unfortunate Soul - The Retribution Saga Preface

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/28/02 [Outside of a large house, an SUV pulls into the driveway. The vehicle pulls around the driveway and parks in front of the front door, pulling around the large wraparound driveway. A man gets out the other side, and looks around his large estate. In the center of the lawn, a fountain shoots water into the air, while lights shine into it. The man looks closer and sees that one of the lights seems to be out. He pulls a phone out of his pocket, and presses a button on the side.] 'Belvedere?' the man calls into the reciever. His face looks angry as he stands waiting for a reply. The phone beeps, and quickly, a smarmy english accented voice answers on the other end. 'Yes, guvna?' the man on the other end answers. 'Belvedere, there is a light out in the pond. Take care to replace it. And do it now.' 'I'm afraid I can't do that, sir.' 'Excuse me?' 'Terribly sorry, sir. But, I'm afraid I can't do that.' 'I see. I guess your job means nothing to you then?' 'Pardon, sir. It's not that, it's just...well, perhaps you should come inside, sir.' 'I'll do that, Belvedere.' The man walks to his front door, and produces a key from his pocket. He fumbles around with it, and fits the key into the slot. He turns the key, and a large explosion rocks the front door, sending the man flying 50 feet back in the air. The house catches into flames as the Man falls to the ground, his suit on fire. He rolls on the ground a little and the flames go out. He raises his head and looks at his house, now fully enveloped in flames. His nose is bleeding from the impact, and his eyes look as if no one is home. He stares at the house, as another explosion rocks the land around him. His arms dart around the back of his neck as debris showers down around him. The debris shower stops as he starts to stand up. He looks around at the carnage, and starts to dust himself off. He turns around and looks to the fountain. Red and Blue lights cascade from it as the water once shooting up in a ring around the center, turns to flame. He stands staring as the flames shoot upwards. 'What the??' the man exclaims as the flames shoot out from the pond. He blinks in disbelief that all this is happening. Suddenly, the flames shoot at least 100 feet into the air. From the center of the ring of fire, a figure walks out between them, as if unharmed by the heat of the fire. The man takes a step backwards, shocked that this stranger has invaded his home this way. The figure starts to walk across the water toward where the man is standing. Paralyzed with fear, the man stands still, staring at the man coming towards him. 'Hello, Stevens. Remember me?' 'You! You're dead! I killed you.' 'My dear Michael. You can't kill what's already dead inside.' Michael Stevens starts to turn and run away. As he does he bumps into the large figure, now standing behind him. Stevens lets out a cry of panic, looking behind hm to see the figure that was once behind him is now gone. He looks into the figure's eyes and cowers. 'What...what do you want from me?' 'What does everyone want? Retribution, Michael. That's all I want. Retribution and an apology.' Stevens attempts to throw a punch at the figure, but misses. his own momentum is used to drive him to force him to the ground, and the Figure throws a knee to the back of his neck. 'I'm sorry! Caldera put me up to it! Ever since you killed GCW, he's had everyone ready to come get you. He didn't think he could put you away so quickly. I'm sorry!' 'Good. Now was that so hard? That's part one taken care of. But tell me. If I hadn't come here, would you have said I'm sorry?' Stevens looks down at the ground obviously regretting any answer he could have given. He stays silent, and stares down at the ground. 'Just what I thought. Michael Stevens. If you had one weakness, it was your greed and desire to be better than you actually were. You're a failure Michael, and that more than nearly killing me, will haunt you for the rest of your life. Tell me, Mike. Do you still talk to Steven?' 'Are you going to kill me?' Stevens asks as he lays on the ground prepared to beg for his life. 'I'll give you anything not to kill me. Money, cars, houses, anything you want. It's yours...' 'Kill you? What do you take me for? Another one of Steven Caldera's hired goons? Am I Angelo Deville? No. I'm not going to kill you, but when I'm done with you, you're going to wish every day that you're dead. I'm going to take you to the pain. That means that instead of killing you, I'm going to cut out your eyes, destroy your throat, and cut off each and every single finger on your hand, followed by each toe on your foot.' Stevens begins to get angry. 'And then what? Cut off my ears?' 'No. I'll leave those perfect. So that everytime you walk down the street, or wheel into a store, you hear the screams of every child looking at you in horror. You'll hear the sobs of the elderly, and they remember your face til death, praying for you in church every evening. And so you can hear your poor wife Sheena reject you everytime you attempt to mount her to make love to her. No, Michael, I'm going to leave your ears perfectly intact, and undamaged. That's what Pain means, Stevens.' Stevens begins to struggle, and gets away from the man. he starts to run towards the flaming house, and along the way picks up his cell phone, and dials, as the figure moves in behind him, smiling wickedly. The figure jumps on Steven's back driving him down to the ground, as the night sky fills with smoke. 20 minutes later a limosine pulls into the driveway, and stops 50 feet short of the house. The driver gets out and opens the rear door. A woman exits, and runs to the house, watching it burn as well. She looks around, for somewhere to run and hide, but to no avail. She turns and looks at the fountain, and in the center, amid the flames, stands Michael Stevens, crucified. He's dressed like a scarecrow, with a sign around his neck...'This is for you, Steven.' The woman screams loudly, watching blood run down the man's face, as she turns and passes out.
