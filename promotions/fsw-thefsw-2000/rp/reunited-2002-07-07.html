Reunited

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/7/02 'I watched you last week. 'I was so glad to see you again, that I ran up to you and hugged you. We sat and talked about old times for a while, but I knew the tension was in the air. I felt it, and I'm damn sure you did. I needed you. I needed you bad. It had been so long since I had been without you, I just had to have you. I had to be inside you. And that skirt you were wearing did little for the imagination. 'The anticipation building, I slowly got up the nerve to put a move on you. It had been so long, but, it was a dance we hadn't forgotten, my love. I rose up and put myself inside you, and moved around. I don't think I've felt that much joy in ages as I worked myself around in you. It felt good. Real good. And what seemed like forever, I finally finished, and rolled over, satisfied that I could never feel this good ever again. I would never be this high as long as I should live. Because you were back in my life. 'I got up, took a shower, and sat back, waiting for you. But I was distracted. By a man named Angelo Deville. He took me away from you. He did awful things. Look at poor James. Shaking him down for a meager 34 dollars. He could have asked me. I would have no problem giving him 34 dollars. But then, when I tried to confront him about it, he hit me. Not once. Not twice. But over and over again. He beat the hell out of me. So bad that I forgot about you, baby. I forgot that I wanted to see you one more time that night. I ran out and looked for you, but I couldn't find you anywhere. 'When I realized that you were gone, my heart sank. I asked quite a few people, where you had gone, and they all told me you'd moved on. I needed you again. I needed to be inside you. I needed you to hold me close. I needed to feel you. But you were gone.' Jason O'Neil stands in the Hartford Civic Auditorium in the middle of the ring. He smiles at the turnbuckle, and puts his elbow onto it. Slowly, he runs his fingers down the ropes, feeling its cool touch on his warm skin. He smiles a bit more as he looks at the canvas below him, and sits down, looking at the skirt, slightly rise over the canvas before dipping down to just before the floor. Jason lays back, and curls up on the mat, as if he was holding the ring close to him. 'I knew I would see you in Hartford this week. But I found out I have to share you with Angelo Deville. And it makes me sick. Sick to know that such a human being exists in this world, my love. It makes me sick to my stomach to know that I'm going to have to soil you with his blood. I can't get the thought out of my head. Angelo Deville, bleeding right in the middle of this ring. Losing his precious life's fluid all over your clean blue canvas top. Let me apologize now for all that happens to you, on Wednesday night. It's not me wanting to do it to you. Angelo asked for it. He hit me. Over and over again. And he showed me his weaknesses. I have to exploit them. 'I hope you understand. I hope you can forgive me. Because when Angelo tries to come between you and me, I'll fight for your honor. Men like him don't belong here. Men like him, give you a bad name. He needs to be taught a lesson. A lesson in respect for you. And I'm just the man to do that. I am the Alpha, and the Omega, of all there is, was, and will forever be. And come Wednesday when we're reunited in front of 16 thousand screaming fans, he'll be flat on his back, looking up at the lights that bathe you in their perfect light. He'll see my arm, raised in victory, and I hope he never comes back to soil you again.'
