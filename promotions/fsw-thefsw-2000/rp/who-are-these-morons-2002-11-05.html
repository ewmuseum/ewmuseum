Who Are These Morons?

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday The stench of car exhaust is thick in the air, billowing fluidly from the black back ends of two parked cars like sweet smoke from the mouth of the nearest Rastafarian. Luckily, way out here in the boonies there are no tree-huggers present to raise the alarm. Aside from the ramshackle studio with its broken wooden sign and the two cars parked behind it, all that can be seen in either direction are coniferous tress and the receding gravel line of the road. It's as still as a midnight graveyard. The low, steady rumble of the limousine and the comparably high-pitched hum of the roadster are the only sounds to be heard. The driver's door of the roadster swings open silently and without warning, making way for a highly polished black dress shoe followed by a starched grey plant leg. The rest of Ming's lithe and athletic frame oozes from the darkness, a thin and irremovable smirk twisting his lips in amusement. Wispy vapours of steam escape his silk designer jacket, and as he slams his door closed he's wracked by an involuntary shiver. Instinctually casting a suspicious glance in all directions, he sidles over to the back of the limo and raps lightly on the tinted window, then rubs his hands together for warmth. The window rolls down an inch and the two cold(colour) eyes of Tyler Nelson peer out from the depths of darkness within. Ming arches an eyebrow and folds his arms tightly across his chest, only partly due to impatience. 'Alright,' Tyler says with a resigned sigh. 'Gimme a second,' he adds before the window buzzes its way closed. Several uneventful seconds pass, then the limo's driver door creaks open and a portly man in a grey suit comes stumbling towards Ming. The Mingster recoils slightly as the insipid peasant gets close, not wanting to risk any form of contact with any of the diseased underlings. The chauffeur, apparently, has either learned to take this in stride or simply doesn't notice, because he spares Ming a second glance in favour of opening the door and dutifully holding it wide. With exaggerated grandeur and precision, two legs swathed in black pants float out of the opening and light on the floor simultaneously. The rest of Tyler Nelson follows in regal dress and fashion, and the air seems suddenly more arrogant than steamy. He smoothly straightens the one crease that dared to defy his suit's immaculate perfection, then takes a step towards Ming and offers an inquiring look. The chauffeur quietly dismisses himself, closing the door and retreating back to stand before his own, awaiting his next command. 'What the hell are we doing here?' Ming is clearly exasperated about the situation, his voice heavy with frustrated impatience. His teeth try to chatter as he takes a quick breath, but he clamps his mouth shut before they get started. 'Haven't a clue,' Nelson responds off-handedly, his mind focussed on scanning their surroundings. 'Angelo called last night and said something important has come up - some emergency. Wouldn't elaborate.' A hint of agitation enters his voice at the last, and it seems to have picked up momentum when he adds under his breath, 'It better not have to do with the plan.' 'Yeah, that's the message I got, too,' Ming says with a nod. 'He told me to be here twenty minutes ago, though. Damn him... What kind of emergency could they have in Canada - beaver overpopulation?' Ming's eyes suddenly glaze over. 'Now that's a problem I wouldn't mind getting myself stuck in,' he winks, and Nelson chuckles. 'I hear ya,' Tyler grunts, his eyes still scanning the area. 'We'll give him two more minutes, then we're blowing this freezer and getting som-' He leaves the sentence unfinished as a V12's violent roar breaks through the trees. Both men turn in the direction of the noise narrow their eyes as a black Cadillac DeVille tears onto the scene, a shower of smoke and gravel spitting out behind it. The road and tires conspire to raise a grinding protest as it whips onto the narrow drive, and the two waiting men exchange a look as it jerks to a stop behind them, perpendicular to their vehicles. The engine dies almost instantly, then the door opens to admit a grinning, grey-suited Angelo Deville to the party. 'Afternoon, boys,' he greets them, paying no mind whatsoever to the cold. 'Sorry I'm late,' he lies, his deep voice ringing with the paradigm of unapologetic pride. 'Yeah, right,' Nelson acknowledges it briefly, waving his hand to brush off any other formalities. 'What's the big emergency?' He demands, and Ming's curt nod seconds the motion. 'Where the hell is Chris?' Deville ploughs forward, ignoring the question as he does a twice-over of the area with his eyes. Nelson's hands go angrily to his hips as Deville comes up beside them and stops, still looking chipper despite the scowl on his face at finding Chris Chase missing. 'He couldn't make it,' Nelson answers simply, locking eyes with Deville. 'Why not?' Angelo asks, feigning scandal as he looks from one to the other. Ming simply shrugs, seemingly more concentrated on willing his body warm than with anything else. 'Don't worry about it,' Nelson states with a humourless grin, 'he cleared it with me.' 'And what kinda good does that do me? Why would he tell you instead of me?' Angelo demands, his brow furrowing in challenge. Both men's eyes flash and go hard, and Nelson's jaw sets as the tension quickly builds. 'Because I'm calling the f-' Tyler begins through gritted teeth, but Ming jumps in between them. 'Chill out, guys,' Ming says with a chuckle, looking from one defiant face to the other. 'Let's at least get in out of the cold before we start measuring and comparing our packages, alright?' A brief and uncomfortable pause, then Ming narrows his eyes at Angelo. 'We are going inside, of course?' He asks, but the words come out more as a statement. 'Of course,' Deville answers, maintaining the staredown with Nelson for another few seconds before turning to Ming and smiling. 'Of course we're going in... Why the hell do you think we're here?' Ming opens his mouth to respond, but Tyler speaks first. 'We have no idea... You neglected to mention that on the phone.' Tyler's voice is tinged with suspicion as he continues, 'This had better actually be an emergency.' At first, Angelo only grins in response, walking around towards the front of the building and beckoning the other two to follow. Nelson instructs the chauffeur - whom he addresses as 'chauffeur' - to shut off his and Ming's cars, then he and Ming catch up with Deville. 'Oh,' Angelo perks up as they get in stride with one another, 'thanks for the car, Tyler... I always wanted a roadster. Excellent choice on the colour.' 'No problem,' Nelson responds, looking back over his shoulder at the Cadillac Deville arrived in. 'Where is it?' 'Totalled,' Deville says bluntly, his eyes set straight ahead. 'But that's a long and boring story, and it's not why we're here,' he hastens to add. 'A shame, really, but what can you do?' 'You haven't even had it for two days!' Nelson erupts. 'Why are we here, Angelo?' Ming calmly interjects as they round the front corner of the building and the glass front doors come into view. 'What's the big emergency? These rednecks owe us some money or something?' Angelo chuckles and shakes his head. 'Nah, nothing like that. Sorry about the emergency bit, but I know you guys would've given me some shit about being busy If I levelled with you in the first place.' Angelo informs the pair, and both sets of eyes widen in outrage as Deville barks a laugh. 'There's no way you guys would've come way the hell out here if I'd told you the truth.' 'Sonofa...' Ming mumbles under his breath, amazed. 'You fucking lied to me?' Nelson demands quietly, stunned almost into silence. 'Had to,' Deville justifies with a shrug. 'It got the job done, didn't it?' 'So why are we here, then, finally?' Ming implores him, his ego obviously bruised for having been duped. 'Theme music,' Deville answers with a grin as they reach the doors and stop. 'Theme music?' Ming and Nelson blurt at the same time, their unbelieving faces mirrors of one another. 'Mob Rule already has theme music,' Nelson continues on solo, speaking through pursed lips. 'Yeah well, we have to be open to alternatives, in case the current fizzles out,' Deville nods in a finalising way, as if that should end the discussion. 'That may well be,' Nelson responds, still very much vexed, 'but surely these assholes could've just sent us a tape.' 'Who are they?' Ming asks, pushing past the other two to open one of the doors and step into the warmth. Smoke pours out from the door and all three men go silent for a moment as the putrid smell of marijuana dominates the still air. The three men exchange a glance, Ming and Nelson casting doubtful eyes on Deville. 'I don't know,' Deville mumbles as he steps through the door. 'We're meeting with the band and some lady named Bea Riehl,' he pauses and looks doubtful. 'Something like that, anyhow.' 'Oh!' Ming shouts with false enthusiasm and rolls his eyes at Nelson. 'Them!' Tyler shakes his head in disbelief and follows the other two into the narrow and hot-boxed hallway. The only door is a thick wooden beast at the far end of the hall, and as the trio make their way towards it across the linoleum floor, they can't help but notice the fog growing fuller. Without knocking, Deville grasps the steel latch and turns it down, opening into a room even denser with the acrid smoke. Through the haze the three can see a wall full of equipment and several bodies stumbling around. The sound of laughter and soft R&B tunes pulse in time with the weed-induced headaches rising within, and a husky silhouette moves towards them, gaining clarity as it closes in. 'Hey,' a soft and mellow voice greets, dragging the vowel sound out like some cartoon falling off a cliff. The newcomer is much shorter than the other three men, and his pudgy frame speaks volumes for his athletic prowess. A small and uneven goatee obscures the lower part of his face, and a bright green toque is pulled down to the top of his eyes, which are red and much, much thinner than Ming's. '?Sup gents?' The man continues the greeting, holding an arm up in front of his eyes to shake hands with the men in a way obviously arcane to them. All three simply stare. 'Couldn't you have sent us a tape? Or even emailed a damned mp3?' Nelson demands in a gruff tone, folding his arms across his chest defiantly. 'And what the fuck is going on here?' Deville barges in, raising his arms to indicate the thick wall of smoke. 'Who the hell are you?' Ming finishes the onslaught, circling around the man as if seeing him from different angles may reveal his identity. 'Woah,' the stoner exudes after a moment's gaping silence. 'You guys are trippin' me out,' he continues and follows up with a sharp and at least semi-retarded laugh. Nelson, Ming, and Deville share a disapproving glance, then Nelson steps forward and places a rough hand on the stoner's shoulders. 'We'll fuckin' string you up if you don't make with the introduction, half-wit,' he growls from behind a smiling mask, serving to severely confuse the pudgy man. Another slob comes fumbling forward out of the smog and smiles stupidly at the present members of Mob Rule. The first stoner turns his blank and uncomprehending stare at the second and begins laughing hysterically. 'Yo dawg,' he cries between snorts, whipping his hand in front of himself at mach speed so his fingers snap together in what is supposed to be a celebratory and praising gesture. 'These guys are dope!' He turns back to Tyler, who isn't in the least amused. 'Yo, Mr. Nelson... Threaten m'boy here!' The second stoner grins in anticipation as Nelson looks from his buddy to him, then offers a patronising smile. Both stoners' eyes snap open to their normal level as Nelson crumples the first one with a well-placed knee to the lower abdomen. The Six Star Wrestler takes a menacing step towards the second man, who can do nothing but gape as Ming and Deville roughly pick up his friend, bitch-slapping and talking shit to him as they do. 'I'm through threatening... In fact, I'm through talking,' Nelson informs the second, that strange grin still frozen on his face. 'You have exactly two seconds to tell me who you guys are.' 'Ya mean you don't know?' The stoner asks stupidly, then leaps back at a twitch from Nelson and hurriedly adds, 'Cypress Hill! We're Cypress Hill!' 'Who?' Deville asks, releasing the wheezing first stoner and stepping towards Nelson, who shrugs. Ming laughs and shakes his head. 'You guys aren't going to be pleased,' Ming sings, absolutely delighted at the prospect. 'Yeah yeah,' the first stoner chimes in, edging close to Nelson but keeping out of arm's reach. 'I'm B. Real... We're Cypress Hill,' he wheezes. 'Right,' Deville breaks in impatiently. 'Your name is Bea? Nevermind, I don't even wanna know. Your manager told me you guys came up with a phenomenal song that fits our new alliance in FSW.' Deville looks from one stoner to the other, a doubtful and disappointed sheen in his blue eyes. 'Are you guys even a band? I mean... Can you actually make music?' 'Oh shit yeah, cuz,' the unintroduced stoner blurts out, bobbing his head to one side on the last word. 'We'll rock tha mothafuckin' house down.' 'Fo' sho',' B. Real pipes in, still making sure to stay away from Nelson. There is a moment of silence as they nod stupidly at Mob Rule. 'So get to it!' Ming suddenly snaps and Cypress Hill almost shit themselves. The two run over to the small crew sitting by the electronics, then enter the recording booth. They cast nervous glances at the three mobsters standing on the other side of the glass as they prepare, this encounter not going anything like how they anticipated. The latter are at a loss as well, consumed with doubt at the prospect of these guys making a successful living as artists. 'Yer gonna love this, Nelson,' Real laughs and nods self-assuredly at Tyler, who unconsciously snarls slightly. Everything goes deathly silent as B. Real raises his hand and begins counting down with his fingers. The equipment crew scramble madly, fidgeting with switches and toggles, as he slowly lowers his last finger. The small room is ripped apart by the overpowering sound of machine-gun fire. Deville, Ming, and Nelson hit the floor simultaneously, Deville instinctively retrieving his special from his breast pocket. The shooting continues as the three scramble behind one of the tables, seeking out cover and an opportunity to fire back. 'Stop! Stop!' The shouting can barely be heard over the ruckus, but all at once the firing stops and silence dominates the booth again. 'What the fuck happened?' B. Real asks with genuine concern, pressing his nose up to the glass. 'I was about to ask you the same thing,' Deville snarls as he and his partners climb to their feet. 'Why the hell do you have a tape of machine guns being fired? Is this some fucking joke?' 'You havin' fun with us, you filthy little sack of shit?' Nelson sneers, his eyes burning with rage. Ming simply begins reaching into his jacket, his face the epitome of murderous intent. 'What? Hey! No! That's the song!' B. Real protests, waving his hands in front of him like a shield. 'It's part of the song!' Mob Rule stops in their tracks and exchange confused looks. B. Real just shakes his head and instructs everyone to take it from the top, then begins the hand countdown once more. At zero the machine gun fire begins again, and is shortly joined by a mellow, blues-type rhythm. If it weren't for the 'musicians' hopping around like a couple of drugged up frogs, the onlookers might have a chance at liking it. B. Real flies straight up to the glass and practically smears his face into it like some five year old kid, then opens his mouth and begins shouting. 'Hit ?em, whack ?em, string ?em up... Shoot their moms... Hits from the Mob!' He takes a deep breath and prepares to unleash another verse, but Deville charges up to the control panel yelling 'Cut!' repeatedly. The music stops again and the crew looks extremely agitated at the second unnecessary interruption. B. Real comes firing out of the booth, his face stained with concern and confusion. 'What the devil was that?' Deville blares, hands on hips. 'Why the fuck are you shouting?' 'What?' Real asks, his face contorted. 'That's how we sing, man.' 'You were singing?' Ming interrupts. 'When?' The disgust evident on his face is also plain on Nelson's. Real's partner stalks out of the booth and stands beside him, a look of defiance in his face. 'Make this the only time I have to tell you guys...' Deville begins ominously. 'Don't fuck us around. Don't waste our time. We're not your little crack-slinging gangstas from the hood who think they're tough because they're poor and have nothing, we're the genuine fucking article. We have whatever we want because we're willing to take it, regardless of the consequences. We command respect.' B. Real and his associate stare at Deville, as if waiting for the punchline. 'So we're obviously not going to demean ourselves by having your fucking pseudo music chaperone us around! Stop fucking the dog and start playing real music... We don't have time to sit around committing brain suicide all day,' Deville pauses his tirade to indicate the smoke. 'We've got shit to do.' 'Trust me... Peep the whole song ?fore you judge it, D!' B. Real pleads, and his buddy nods in agreement. 'Wait a second,' Nelson rubs his chin in thought. 'You mean to tell me that the gunfire and the shouting is your music?' Both men nod. 'And you expect us to march around under that banner?' They nod again. 'You think stringing together nonsense lyrics is actually impressive?' Nelson shakes his head, glaring at Deville in absolute shock for setting this up. 'Kinda reminds you of Timo, doesn't it?' Deville quips, and Nelson shakes his head. 'Even these guys are more eloquent than the Samoan Shitfarmer,' Nelson says matter-of-factly, but we're straying from the point. 'You guys don't seem to understand,' Ming puts in in a deceptively calm voice, circling the two men with the manner and quiet intensity of a coiled spring. 'How are we, respectable and powerful warlords who are always one step ahead of the game, supposed to relate to you, mindless and quivering drug addicts who don't even know what game is being played?' 'Exactly what I was thinking,' Deville hisses with a nod. 'Why the fuck did you guys contact me? We're not out to identify with you pretend, assembly line bad guys who'd break down in tears if they got into half the shit they boast about... We're out to annihilate you.' 'Huh?' B. Real moans. 'What're you guys talking about?' 'They're clownin' on us, dawg,' his buddy throws in angrily, his glares bouncing harmlessly off the three agitated members of Mob Rule. 'We're saying that you'd better have a plan B that consists of real music, sung by another band that inspires respect instead of stifled, sympathetic giggles,' Deville growls. 'Otherwise...' He lets the threat hang unspoken on the air, glaring its message at the two stoners. 'That's right,' Ming says. 'We're not amused by your ass-clown antics, and we're supremely pissed off that you'd waste our time dragging us down here. You guys can't operate on our level... Hell, you can't even see up to our level.' 'Yo, bitch,' B. Real finally erupts, his anger having grown intense as soon as he realised he was being insulted. 'We're the real fuckin' deal up in here! Nobody clowns on our shit! You don't think we's bad?' Real laughs maniacally as if the very idea is absurd. 'Shit, homeboy... You don't watch yo' mout' and I'll... I'll... I'll break yer bones, bitch! That's how evil we are! You can't even test that!' The rage goes on, with Real storming around on the spot and wringing his doo-rag furiously. 'Damn,' Ming says with wide eyes. 'Now they really sound like Mike and Timo.' Deville snickers but Nelson pays no mind to the joke, choosing to advance on Cypress Hill instead, eliciting a frightened look from B. Real. 'Listen up, shitface,' Nelson growls, stopping just in front of them and looking at Real. 'We're willing to let you fools out of here with your lives, but it's going to cost you. My time is worth a lot of money, as is the time of my associates here, and unless immediate reparations are made, payment must be taken out of your ass.' 'As custom dictates,' Deville adds with a menacing smile as he comes up beside Nelson. Ming circles around behind the two rappers and places a less-than-comforting hand on each of their shoulders. 'You's gotta be crazy, honkies,' B. Real spits, head whipping back and forth like a Stone Cold Steve Austin jack-in-the-box. 'You think we owe you shit? We own your shit! You can't test me and my par-' The word turns into a muffled cry as Nelson backhands the stoned artist to the ground. His friend balks towards Nelson and finds himself staring down the business end of Deville's pistol. 'Have it your way, you classless monkeys,' Deville snarls, then pistol-whips the stoner to the ground as Ming and Nelson begin unleashing a fury on B. Real. The rest of the crew simply stands back out of the way, hoping they don't attract attention. 'You'll never be on our level,' Ming trashes in between agonised wails from his victim. 'So don't even think about approaching us again, capiche?' Deville pulls the man's already bloody face towards his and waits for the scared nod, then lets it drop and continues with the beating. Screams ring out as the assault rages on and on, but no one hears it in the soundproof booth and no help comes... Not that anyone would care if they did hear. So the group that got a little too big for their boots suffers dearly for their ignorant indiscretions and unfounded association. Mob Rule lets them off easily, permitting them continued use of their bodies and perhaps even their careers, simply because right now they can't be bothered completely wiping out such pathetic bottom-feeders. The message should be clear, however, that if this group continues trying to feed off the greatness of those so far beyond them, there will be a dear price to pay. Mob Rule can't be bothered wasting their time with every group that thinks they're special, so unless you're the best, stay the fuck out of the way... ...Or we'll kill you.
