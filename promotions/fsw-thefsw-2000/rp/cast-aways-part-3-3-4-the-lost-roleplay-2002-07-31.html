Cast Aways Part 3 3/4: The Lost Roleplay

31 July 2002

Date: 7-31-02 Okay...do to complications with my computer, this roleplay got deleted last night, so it should have been posted before Cast Aways Part 4, but I typed it up again tonight, so please..just pretend this was posted before Mike's last roleplay. ---- “You go on ahead Michael, I have to go to the bathroom.” Jacob said only moments after he and Mike Marvelous had set out on their search for food. “Alright Jacob. We’ll just meet back at the rendezvous point at fourteen hundred hours.” “Actually, I would rather just meet back at the cave at around 2 o’clock.” Jacob stated while holding his stomach in anticipation to release his wrath of seagull meat upon the world. “Okay Jacob. Whatever works for you.” Mike said with a smile as he continued on with his search. With a smile, Jacob turned on his heel and quickly headed in the opposite direction of his best friend. While hidden behind a tree, Jacob reached into his pocket and pulled out what seemed to be a cigarette. “Ahh…the one that didn’t get away” Jacob said with a smile while fumbling around in his pockets in search of his matches. With one quick strike a flame replaced the sulfur tip of the match. A smile quickly appeared on Jacob’s face as he lit the cigarette. The smoke coursed through his lungs as Jacob took a deep breathe of the smoke infested air. With a sigh of relief Jacob exhaled the smoke, and continued to quickly puff on the cigarette. Freedom. Jacob had managed to hide this filthy habit from Michael for so long, but now that he knew about it..Jacob could only wonder if Mike’s personal opinion of Jacob had changed. At this very moment though, not even this troubling thought could in anyway affect Jacob. He was free. Every puff of the cigarette made Jacob calmer and made him enjoy life even more. The sound of footsteps approaching made Jacob’s heart jump to his throat. He dropped the cigarette to the ground and quickly stomped it out, refusing to exhale his last drag knowing that Mike would notice the smoke, and who knows what Mike would say then. Mike quickly found his best friend standing behind a tree. “Did ya find anything?” Mike asked. Jacob wanted to reply, but he couldn’t. If he had the smoke would exhale into Mike’s face, and he could only imagine how Mike would react. Jacob shook his head furiously, “no”. “Hmmph..well I guess we have no choice but to keep on looking” Jacob nodded his head. “Jacob..?” Mike said with a concerned look. “Jacob..you’re face is turning blue!” With his eyelids fluttering and his eyes beginning to roll into the back of his head, Jacob found it hard to stand and quickly began to sway back and forth, but he still refused to exhale. “JACOB! BREATH!” Mike yelled right before smacking Jacob across the face. Mike’s eyes widened, and the anger was easily seen, as Jacob exhaled a cloud of smoke right into his face. While on his hands and knees gasping for breath, Jacob looked up in anticipation of what his friend would say. “Oh I see now…you’re back here smoking your darn cancer sticks, trying to kill the birds!” Mike yells. Suddenly a screeching sound is heard and moments later a seagull flies right past Jacob and Mike at tremendous speed and slams into a large rock, falling to the ground..dead. “You see what you did!” Mike shouts at Jacob. “Mike it was only a-“ Jacob begins but is quickly interrupted by Mike. “SHUT UP!” Mike yells while reaching into his pocket. Jacob’s heart fills with fear when Mike pulls a pocketknife from his pocket. “Well I guess if you can’t kick that filthy habit..” Mike says while unfolding the blade, “I guess I’ll just have to cut it out of you!” With that being said, the chase began. Both man take off with great speed while the knife wielding Marvelous chases Jacob. The chase however, is cut short when Jacob Jackson is clotheslined by a tree branch. ---------- “ “Smoking your cancer sticks huh?” he says” Jacob says muttering to himself. “trying to kill the birds” he says “why don’t I just cut it out of ya” well you know what Mike, you can take that knife and shove it up your AHH-“ Jacob’s muttering was cut short when he suddenly tripped over a tree root and landed face down on the ground. “owww…” Jacob whines, but his face suddenly goes from a pouting expression to an excited one, “Hey! Food!” There he lied, only inches away from a rather large patch of mushrooms. Jacob quickly grabbed a handful of the mushrooms and yanked them from their roots. After positioning himself in a sitting position, Jacob nibbled from one of the mushrooms. “MMM!” He quickly devoured three or four of the mushrooms, then began to talk to himself. “Heh, I bet Mike wouldn’t be so mad at me now if he was here.” Jacob took another bite. “Maybe I’ll save him a few.” ---------- “I wonder what’s taking him so long” Mike asked himself while sitting in front of the cave that they were supposed to meet each other at approximately a half an hour ago. “Gee..I hope he isn’t still mad at me..Maybe I should go look for him…” ---------- “Jaaaacoooob…ohhh….Jaaaacooooob” Mike called out to his friend while searching the jungle. Suddenly Mike looked down just in time to spot his good friend. He would have ran right into him if he had not glanced down at the ground for a split second. There was Jacob, sitting on the ground with his head tucked under his shirt, hugging his knees while rocking back and forth, back and forth. “Jacob..what are you do-“ Mike’s sentence was cut short when Jacob suddenly sprang to his feet holding his arms in the air. “THE COLORS MICHAEL!” Jacob said excitedly, “Look at all the pretty colors!” Mike looked on confused as his friend began to swat at invisible butterflys. “What’s wrong with you-“ Once again he was interrupted. “Whoa..Michael..” Jacob began obviously terrified, “Stop that..” “Stop what, Jacob?” “Just…Just..” Jacob began while rubbing his hands all over his face and chest, “Just…leave me alone…I need some time..” “Jacob..what has gotten into you?!” With his eyes widened and his hand grabbing at his chest in the exact location of the heart, Jacob fell to the ground..motionless. “Jacob..?” Mike asked worried, “Jacob!” Mike rushed to the side of his best friend and cradled his head in the crook of his arm. “Oh my god…what have I done..?” Mike asked as if pleading to God. Jacob’s eyelids sprang open and he yelled, “BOO!” With uncontrollable laughter, Jacob rolled on the ground, as Mike got to his feet dusting himself off. “That was sick” Jacob got to his feet and stood only a few inches away from Mike’s face. “Oh my god..Jacob..your eyes!” Mike said with a confused look, “They’re like..the size of pennies!” Jacob smiled as if taking Mike’s statement as a compliment. “You know what Michael” Jacob asked. “Yes..?” Jacob cocked his head to the side and said, “You kind of..resemble a chipmunk..” Once again Jacob erupted with laughter as his friend shook his fist in the air. “WHY I OUGHTA!” yelled an outraged Mike Marvelous. “uh..uh…uh….Do not…bite the hand that feeds you.” Said Jacob while pointing a finger at the remaining mushrooms. “FOOD!” ---------- “I like carrots..” “Once, Mother walked in on me playing with my fiddle, I thought she would be mad, but actually she was rather intrigued” There they were, sitting on the ground, heads tucked under their shirts, and hugging their knees while rocking back and forth, back and forth, carrying on a conversation with each other that obviously didn’t make much sense at all. “Once in Canada,” Jacob began, “I was raped by a mountain goat..that’s why I despise the place so much” “Ahh yes..puberty was a wonderful experience” stated Mike, “I was so excited at the age of 14 when I noticed my first pubic hair. Yes..Miracle Grow had worked wonders for me.” “Could somebody please change the channel, I’ve seen this one..” Jacob said still hugging his knees and rocking back and forth, back and forth, “Ahh..much better.” Mike began to pop his head in and out of the collar of his shirt, “Turtle..turtle…turtle..” “I had started out just caressing it” Jacob began, “Soon I began to tug at it, I didn’t know what had overcome me, when suddenly..I thought I broke it, it was only moments later when I realized..I had become a man.” ---------- “George…George..George of the Jungle, watch out for that tree!” Suddenly Jacob ran into view at full speed and ran head first into a tree. He lied on the ground holding his forehead, laughing. “Hey…TEACHER!” Mike rolled into view in the nude, rolling on the ground while singing, “LEAVE THEM KIDS ALONE!” Jacob stood up, as did Mike, Jacob began to laugh while pointing at Mike’s penis. “Haha..” Jacob laughed, while holding his index finger and thumb about 2 inches apart, “Wow…you’re big..” Mike stood tall, hands on his hips, chest puffed out and obviously proud. ---------- “I feel high as an eagle” Jacob’s voice was heard somewhere out of view. “Me too!” Mike replied. “AHHHHHHH!” The combination of both Mike and Jacob’s screams filled the air. When suddenly they fell from the air and landed face first in the dirt, obviously they had jumped out of a tree. “Heh..” “Wh-What happened?” Jacob asked while sitting up and blinking the bright sunlight from his eyes. He looked down at the ground to his sleeping friend and nudged him..nothing..he then nudged him harder..again..nothing..Jacob suddenly smiled an evil grin. He reached over towards Mike’s nose and pinched his nostrils together. After a few seconds Mike’s head began to shake back and forth until he sat up gasping for air. “Oh…you’re awake?” Jacob asked trying to hide his grin. “Wh-where are we?” Mike asked Jacob while rubbing the morning crust from his eyes. “Home.” “HOME?!” Mike asked excitedly, “A dream..it was just a dream!” “Heh..oh nevermind..I guess we’re still stranded…in the middle of NOWHERE!” “I don’t remember a thing from last night, Jacob” Mike said to his good friend. “Me neither, but boy do I feel great!” “Yeah..and hungry” Mike said as his stomach growled. “You know what Michael..why don’t we do some hunting?” Jacob somewhat asked, yet stated. “That’s a great idea Jacob! We can sharpen some sticks to make spears..we’re bound to find some sort of wildlife around here..” They got to their feet yawning and stretching their arms in the air. They then set off to find some sort of tree branch. ---------- Jacob stood holding his tree branch turned spear in hand with a branch running from the back of his pants through his shirt and above his head with leaves hanging over..camoflauge. Mike also. They looked at each other and nodded. “Ready?” Mike asked. “Yeah..hey…why don’t you go ahead..I have to go to the bathroom” Jacob said while reaching into his pocket. “Oh no you don’t!” Mike scolded while grabbing ahold of Jacob’s ear. And they were off.
