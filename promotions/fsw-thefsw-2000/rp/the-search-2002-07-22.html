Date: ????? *The scene opens up in a small apartment where Pablo is rolling a joint while Francisco is starching some jeans. As we look around the small place we can tell these guys are very dirty and uncooth to add to that as the fish tank is dirty beyond belief and the sink is full of crusty dishes. On the walls hang posters, a few 'Scarface' posters but most are that of marijuana and stoners from the hippie era.* Francisco Fumar: You almost finished rolling that joint homes? Pablo Diablo: Yeah almost man. Francisco Fumar: Shit, hurry up already..... *Pablo puts the finishing touches on the joint and smiles* Pablo Diablo: Ok man, its done Francisco Fumar: Well pass it here lemme light it up. Pablo Diablo: Nah its my turn man! Francisco Fumar: Did you pay for that?? Now gimme my joint. *Francisco grabs the joint from Pablo's hand and walks over to his broken lazy boy recliner and sits down and lights up the joint. He inhales big and lets out a cloud of smoke as the aroma fills the air. He lets out a sigh of relief and then hits it a few more times* Pablo Diablo: C'mon man quit babysitting .....pass it here. Francisco Fumar: Ahhh fine. *With that Fran-man passes the joint over to the fiending Pablo. Pablo takes a huge hit and trys to speak while lettting it out but starts to cough heavily* Pablo Diablo: Damnnnnn thats some good shit...... *Pablo smokes some more and then puts the roach out* Francisco Fumar: I need some more weed homes Pablo Diablo: Well we aint got anymore. Francisco Fumar: Dammit, Well go ask Joe if he got some. Pablo Diablo: Man I'm tired you go ask him. Francisco Fumar: Shiit homes lets just go together. Pablo Diablo: Man I'm tired, I just want to relax Francisco Fumar: Look man if we dont go now then we prolly wont score any weed. Pablo Diablo: Ahhhhhh fine c'mon lets go. *Pablo gets up out of his chair slowly and reaches his arms way out in the air and stretches. Fran-Man pulls the cord to the iron out the wall from where he was strarching his jeans and puts the iron in an up-right position. He then walks over to where the 13' Inch TV is and picks up his hat that was laying on top of it. He puts it on and slides it around his head* Pablo Diablo: You almost done farting around man. Francisco Fumar: Hey homes, I'm just trying to get the right look man. Pablo Diablo: Well c'mon lets go. *The two of them walk over to the window and slide it up and exit the apartment. They then jump over the 4 ft. tall patio wall and walk around to where their door of the apartment is* Francisco Fumar: Well it still has the lock on the door. Pablo Diablo: Man I think they want us to pay the rent. Francisco Fumar: Nah, I think Fred is just playing a joke on us. *Pablo notices a note hanging from the door handle and picks it up and reads it* [Pay your rent or get out] Pablo Diablo: Nah it says he wants us to pay the rent man. Francisco Fumar: Ahh Fred would kick us out would he?? Pablo Diablo: I dont know, lets just go get this weed from Joe. Francisco Fumar: Yeah *Pablo turns around and knocks on the apartment adjacent from his* Pablo Diablo: Damn this Joe , he better be home man. Francisco Fumar: Thats not how you knock man, you got to bang on it. *Francisco bangs on the door hard... you can hear someone yelling from inside. Suddenly the door swings open.... and a big fat man opens the door* Pablo Diablo: Hey Joe whats happening man. Joe: What the fuck do you two want? Francisco Fumar: Oh we just wanted to see if you had any bud man? Joe: Well I dont so bye. *Joe slams the door in their faces* Francisco Fumar: Damn homes, Where we gonna get some bud at? Pablo Diablo: I dont know, how about Jimmy. Francisco Fumar: You know Jimmy is dry.....hell this whole damn region is dry. I wish I was back in Mexico. Pablo Diablo: Lets just go get some from the store. Francisco Fumar: Ok. *With that Pablo and Francisco walk on over to their car...the car is probably one of the trashiest pieces of shit you have ever layed your eyes on...Pablo gets in the passenger side going through the window since the door is broken. Francisco gets in as on his side normally* Pablo Diablo: Whats wrong? Francisco Fumar: Its not starting up homes. Pablo Diablo: Well here, take this wrench. *Hands out a wrench* Francisco Fumar: What's this for? Pablo Diablo: I dont know , I just figured you could beat it. Francisco Fumar:Shiiiit Pablo your pretty dumb man..... just open the glove box and had me those wires essay. *Pablo opens the glove box and pulls out a wire shaped like a fish* Pablo Diablo: Here you go *Pablo hands Fran the wire and Fran hot wires the car....and their off.....they slowly drive off out the apartment complex and onto a small two way road... their isnt much traffic as they putter on down the road.... Fran Man puts on the radio.....and Pablo sticks his hand out the window to adjust the foil on the antenna. Fran Man comes around the corner and their radio is blaring loud spanish music from the tweeters that are in the car...Fran Man swerves in and parks the car at the store. Francisco and Pablo get out and walk in the store non chalantly* Francisco Fumar: So you ever bought any here before Pablo? Pablo Diablo: Huh? Oh nah man, but all stores sell it these days. Francisco Fumar: They do? Pablo Diablo: Yeah man you just got to know like the secret words. Francisco Fumar: Ohhhh far out homes, well hey what about him? Pablo Diablo: Nah, lets just ask him over there (points) Francisco Fumar: Ok, lets go. *Pablo and Fran walk across the grocery store to where a young man is stocking shelves... he is probably around the age of twenty five.* Pablo Diablo: Hey man, You got any bud? Man: Oh yeah the beer is on aisle 11 on your left. *Francisco looks over at Pablo and just shrugs his shoulders* Pablo Diablo: You know man, some smoke? *Pablo puts his hands by his mouth like he is smoking some weed* Man: Yeah we have cigarettes up at the front just ask the cashier. Francisco Fumar: Ok thanks man. *Francisco takes Pablo by the arm and walks off a few feet and starts conversating pointing back at that man* Francisco Fumar: You sure this guy uses those secret words? Pablo Diablo: Oh yeah man, they all do...we just probably aint catching his vibe. Francisco Fumar: Vibe? Pablo Diablo: Yeah Vibe means when... Francisco Fumar: I know what it means knuckle head....but what do you mean homes? Pablo Diablo: Well hell this is a grocery store...we probably got to talk grocery talk with him. Francisco Fumar: Grocery talk? Pablo Diablo: Yeah man, grocery talk! Francisco Fumar: Yeahhhh grocery talk!!! Well c'mon man what you waiting for. Pablo Diablo: Well what do I say? Francisco Fumar: I dont know , I thought you knew.....uhmmm think of something green....like bud man! Pablo Diablo: How about lettuce? Francisco Fumar: Hey thats good homes. Pablo Diablo: What about salad? Francisco Fumar: Yeah, yeah, whichever. *Pablo and Francisco walk back over to where the man is stocking the shelves* Pablo Diablo: Hey man.....uhmmm Do you got any salad? Man: Look I know you two idiots think its funny...and you just want to make fun of the grocery boy ....well enough is enough! Francisco Fumar: He just wants to know if you have any salad homes. Man: Yeah I got a salad....you wanna toss it? Pablo Diablo: Uhmmm hold on a second man. *Pablo and Fran walk off a few feet and get in a little huddle* Pablo Diablo: Do I want to toss it? Francisco Fumar: I dont know man do you? Pablo Diablo: Uhhhhhh Francisco Fumar: I thought you said you knew these secret words? Pablo Diablo: Not really. Francisco Fumar: Man we're never gonna score any bud. Pablo Diablo: How about I ask him what kinds? Francisco Fumar: Oh yeah man thats good. *Pablo and Francisco walk back over to the young man* Pablo Diablo: Hey man, what kind of salads you got? Man: Well I got your ceasar salad with mystery brown sauce or I got your classic Italian with chunks. *Pablo looks over at Francisco and They both have a state of confusement on their faces* Francisco Fumar: Well which one do you prefer? Man: Whats it matter they both stink like shit!!!! Francisco Fumar: Ahhh so its some stinky shit eh? Man: Oh yeah, its some STINKY SHIT!!! Francisco Fumar: Now your talking my language!!! How much for your shit? Man: Dude you guys are disgusting.....get out of here before I call the cops! Pablo Diablo: I think he's a NARC man.....lets get out of here. Francisco Fumar: Yeah god damn NARC's.....we dont want your shit anyways homes. *With that Pablo Diablo and Francisco Fumar walk off and out of the grocery store* Pablo Diablo: Man I hate that store anyways. Francisco Fumar: Yeah homes that guy had some nerve trying to bust us in a grocery store. Pablo Diablo: Well where are we gonna get our weed now? Francisco Fumar: I dont know man, Hey I did I tell you about our jobs? *Pablo and Fran get back in their car and begin to drive off* Pablo Diablo: Jobs? Francisco Fumar: Yeah man, I got us jobs today when you were busy sleeping on your ass. Pablo Diablo: Man I already told ya, I cant be mowing any lawns.....all that grass just makes me fiend. Francisco Fumar: Nah Man I got us better jobs. Pablo Diablo: Oh yeah, like what? Francisco Fumar: We're gonna be wrestlers man! Pablo Diablo: Wrestlers? I thought we were already wrestlers? Francisco Fumar: Oh yeah huh..... damn, I keep getting that damn re-occuring dream sequence shit. Pablo Diablo: Anyways, FSW is starting to seem like alot of work......how we gonna be able to do it? Francisco Fumar: I just figured we'd do it high. Pablo Diablo: Hey man this may just be a good idea....if we can score some bud. *Francisco pulls in at a local gas station and gets out the car* Pablo Diablo: What you doing man? Francisco Fumar: I'm gonna use the pay-phone. I'm supposed to call about us arriving for next Glory Pablo Diablo: Oh yeah good idea. *Fran continues on over and picks up the pay-phone after deposting some coins. He dials a few numbers and then gets through* Francisco Fumar: Yeah hey man, Im calling about our Arrival......uh huh...This is 'A Tag Team' ... Robin Nicely? ...ohhhhh ok .. *Fran man hangs up the phone and walks back over and gets into the car* Pablo Diablo: So what happened? Francisco Fumar: He said we got to meet Robin Nicely. Pablo Diablo: Oh ok......so who are we fighting? Francisco Fumar: Well we are not fighting this week. Pablo Diablo: SWEET! More time to find some bud! Hey maybe that Robin guy has some bud. Francisco Fumar: Yeah maybe. *They pull up back at their apartment and get out the car and walk back and hop over the little fence and enter through the window once again as the scene fades to black*
