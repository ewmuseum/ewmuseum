Game Plan

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, Sept. 9th, 2002 [Scene opens in the trophy room of Mike Marvelous, deep within his large home in Nova Scotia, Canada. Mike is sitting in the middle of the large room on a swiveling chair, and beside him is his ever faithful midget companion, Mini-Mike. Both stare in awe and wonder at the many achievements, as a golden hue reflects onto their faces. Mike's jaw drops and his eyes water, seemingly over-filled with joy at this moment. He could never be happier...until...] Mini-Mike: So, weady for youw match against Meanstweak on Gwowy? [Mike's eyes suddenly lose all that awe and wonder, and his pride-filled smile is suddenly replaced for a frown.] Mike Marvelous: I have a MATCH with MEANSTREAK!? Mini-Mike: Yes...on Gwowy. [Mike's breathin suddenly becomes rapid and sweat rims his brow] Mike Marvelous: That...that can't be...I...he...he's retired! Mini-Mike: He retuwned last week...didn't you see? Mike Marvelous: No...I...I never watch the show unless I'm on it... Mini-Mike: Well, you-- Mike Marvelous: What did I do to deserve this!? Why do *I* have to face Meanstreak?! I have to concentrate on the Dead or Alive tourney! I don't want to get injured! [Mike gets up out of his chair and begins pacing around while grabbing his hair and pulling like a lunatic] Mike Marvelous: What am I gonna do...what am I gonna do... Mike flashes back to the March 20th, 2001 edition of PCW Tuesday Turmoil...... ('Money' by Pink Floyd plays as 1/5 of the Five Star Five, Mike Marvelous, along with his midget look-a-like mini-Mike, makes his way out.) Clay: Making his way to the ring, being accompanied by Mini-Mike, from Nova Scotia, Canada, at a weight of 243 pounds.......MIKE MARVELOUS ('Supernova Goes Pop' by Powerman 5000 hits as the man everyone seems to fear, Meanstreak, makes his bone-chilling entrance.) Clay: His opponent, at a weight of 345 pounds.......MEANSTREAK Mike Marvelous: I already faced Meanstreak before...I remember... Mini-Mike: Yes...and we both know what happened in the end... Meanstreak catches his breath on the outside for a moment, then climbs back into the now 2-roped ring. As Streak comes back in, MM comes up with an idea. Once Streak is in, MM charges at Streak. He goes for The MARVELocity, but as he goes for the spear portion of it, he just bounces off of Streak and ends up on the mat. He immediately grabs his ugly looking shoulder. Dave: Mike Marvelous looks hurt. He could have a seperated or dislocated shoulder. Perhaps a hurt collarbone. Mike Marvelous: Yes, I remember...I went for the MARVELocity...I thought I had him! But when I hit...something wasn't right. It was like running into a brick wall or something. I suddenly lost all feeling in my left arm from the shoulder down... Mini-Mike: And what came aftew only made things wowse... Inside the ring, MM is down and in extreme pain. Streak just looks down at him and, instead of feeling bad for him, he just does as he always does; pounds his chest and roars to a mild pop. Streak then lifts MM up and drops him with AT DEATH'S DOOR!!! Jeff: You can add broken neck to that list Dave, hehe. Streak drops MM like a bad habit. The monster then pounds his chest, roars, and places his foot on the chest of MM to symbolize a pin, 1.......2........3 WINNER: Meanstreak Dave: So Meanstreak picks up a win and a ton of momentum going into Friday's #1 contender's match?!?! Jeff: Can anyone stop Meanstreak?!?! Mike Marvelous: Yes...when I regained consciousness I was in the hospital room with a separated shoulder and a sprained neck... Mini-Mike: Yup... Mike Marvelous: But I need a game plan this time! I'm not the same Canadian Hero I was when I faced that grotesque beast. I've overcome some amazing odds, Mini-Mike! Mini-Mike: You'we tewwing me...most people who get weamed never make it above a cuwtain jewker spot on the cawd... Mike Marvelous: I WAS DRUGGED! Mini-Mike: Suuuuwe. Mike Marvelous: Anyway, I have overcome EXTREME adversity! I am just INCHES away from being FSW World Champion once again, Mini-Mike! If that Timo Bodumba starts pulling his weight, maybe it will be easier...but I am not going to let MEANSTREAK come between me and the FSW Title! I've already been cheated once, and I'll be damned if I'm going to let some big oaf snatch away my dream and stomp on it! Mini-Mike: But he's huge... Mike Marvelous: I know...that's where a game plan comes in... Meanstreak stands over the grotesquely wounded Mike Marvelous, pounds his chest and lets out a roar-- Mike Marvelous: And would you STOP with the flashbacks already! Anyway...help me devise a plan, Mini-Mike... Mini-Mike: Hmmm...you couwd kick him in the bawws! Mike Marvelous: Naaw...that doesn't work on him. He no sells it EVERY time! [Mini-Mike raises his hand to his chin, seemingly deep in thought] Mini-Mike: You couwd give Homicidaw Anthony Cay a caww... Mike Marvelous: Naw, it's too short notice... [Mini-Mike goes to think again, when suddenly Mike bursts out with an idea] Mike Marvelous: I could have my legions of female fans carry me out of the arena to safety! OSW Sexiest Wrestler award, baby!!! Mini-Mike: Umm...Michaew...I don't know how to bweak this to you...but the powws just came in and Mike Fwampton beat you in the 'Most Sexiest FSW Wrestler' categowy by a 5 pewcent mawgine... Mike Marvelous: DAMN YOU FRAMPTON`! Mini-Mike: Sowwy... [Mike bows his head in defeat and sits back down on his swivel chair] Mike Marvelous: Then I just don't know, Mini-Mike...perhaps I'm doomed after a--WAIT A MINUTE! I HAVE AN IDEA! [Mike calls Mini-Mike over and whispers something in his ear, and after a few seconds, an evil grin spreads across the midget's face. Suddenly, both of them stand up and face the camera] Mike Marvelous: I AM EVER SO SMART! NOTHING CAN STOP ME! NOTHING! MUAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! MUAHAHAHAHAHAHA! LAUGH WITH ME, MINI-MIKE! MUAHAHAHAHAHA! Mini-Mike: MUAHAHAHAHAHA! Mike Marvelous and Mini-Mike: MUAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAAAA!!! [Scene fades to black as the evil laughter of both Mike and his midget companion fills the large trophy room]
