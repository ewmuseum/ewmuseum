We got your socks..and underwear too!

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today -A New Era. Five Star Wrestling. 'The Idol'- What do all these words have in common? A second chance has been dealt. Play your cards right...and you will go straight to the top. Among these words, stands a term on a level it's own. 'The Idol'. 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson. 24 years young, and he has already accomplished so much in his life. All around the wrestling world, the name is being talked about. When heard, the name reminds you of many things. Joy..Hate..Envy..Jelousy..but most importantly, when this name is spoken, even when it falls upon deaf ears, it without a doubt brings..Fear. It strikes fear in the hearts of wrestlers all around. It strikes fear in the hearts of fans accossiated with their favorite wrestling organizations. But most imporantly..when this name is spoken, on Wendesday night at Glory. When this man is being introduced to the millions of fans worldwide by, the FSW ring announcer Willy Wilson, the name Jacob Jackson, will strike fear into the heart of one particular man..'The Canadian Criminal' Chris Irving. Tragedy. The United States of America witnessed a tragedy on, September 11th 2001, as viewers worldwide watched on their television screen, as the World Trade Center collapsed due to a terrorist attack at the hands of, Osama Bin Laden. The plan was to weaken the foundation of possibly the strongest and most feared country in the world. However, due to the attack, we didn't run. We didn't give in to these terrorists..It only made us grow stronger. It only made us want to fight back. Come Wednesday night at Glory another tragedy will fall before us, however this tragedy will only have the reverse effects. Chris Irving, nephew of another legend in the sport of wrestling, Doozer. A newcomer to this sport. Apparently, 'The Canadian Criminal', believes in the quote..'To be the best..You have to beat the best.' However, before you can become the best, you have to pay your dues. You have to prove not only to the fans, but to yourself, that you are the best..that you are an Idol. Yes a tragedy..it almost brings a tear to my eye when I think that this young man's career will suddenly come to a halt as he faces FSW Hall Of Famer, 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson. Such a tragedy. ------------------ A high pitched tone breaks the silence in the bedroom of 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson. Suddenly awoken by the ear piercing scream of his alarm clock, Jacob reaches over towards his nightstand and turns if off. While rubbing the morning crust from his eyes, Jacob lets out a loud yawn. Mumbling to himself Jacob throws the blankets off of him and rises to his feet. Dressed in baby blue mesh, Nike, shorts hanging just below his knees and no shirt which exposes his muscular chest chiseled to perfection, along with a full head of beautifull thick, dark brown hair which is now a mess, he continues mumbling to himself what seems to be gibberish while making his way to the bathroom. Soon the sound of a shower can be heard, followed by Jacob singing to the tune of 'For Hes A Jolly Good Fellow', in a monotone voice. ------------------ 20 minutes later, a cleaner, and much more alive Jacob steps out of the bathroom in a hurry as the sound of a doorbell can be heard. Wearing only a towel around his waste, Jacob quickly makes his way to the door and opens it as far as the chain lock will allow. 'EEK!' Jacob exclaims as a smile slowly replaces the plain expression on his face. He shuts the door, and separates the chain lock from the door and quickly opens the door to reveal a hunched over, Mike Marvelous, who seems to be in pain. 'Gosh darn hotel beds! My necks killing me...by backs killing me...my neck..my back..my neck and my back!' Explains Mike as he slowly enters Jacob's humble house. 'Good Morning to you too...' Jacob says while shutting the front door. Mike slowly makes his way towards Jacob's large, black, leather sofa. He sits down and lets out a sigh of relief. 'Excuse me for a minute, Michael...' Jacob says while excusing himself to his bedroom to finish getting dressed. While waiting for Jacob to finish getting dressed, Mike slowly gets up from the sofa and begins to walk towards one of the windows facing Jacob's front yard. A ray of sunlight only brings more light into the house as he moves the curtain from blocking the window to reveal it is a beautifully sunny day without a single cloud in the sky. Just the sight of such a perfect day suddenly brings a smile to Marvelous' face. Mike swings open the windows and takes a deep breath, tasting the city's air. With Mike's throat stinging from the heavy stench of car exhaust and a hint of insecticide spray our Canadian Hero quickly becomes dizzy. He suddenly moves away from the window and begins chasing invisible butterflys with a far off expression across his face. A rather hoarse voice is suddenly heard, 'What the hell is wrong with you now..?' Mike looks towards the voice. There, lying on a recliner, is Jacob's two legged, steel testicled and yes..talking dog, Sparky. Sparky looks on with a hint of confusion in his puppy dog face. 'EEK!' Mike exclaims and suddenly darts towards the kitchen. He quickly swings open Jacob's refridgerator door with enough force to knock a few magnets to the floor. Mike quickly reaches in the fridge and pulls out a delicious looking pie. He glances over towards Sparky with his eyes narrowed, then back at the pie and without hesitating dips his face into the icing. Mike tosses the pie back in the fridge and shuts the door and quickly crawls behind a counter out of view. He begins to slowly peek his head over the counter but quickly darts back down when his eyes meet with Sparky's. Marvelous' hand slowly moves back up towards the countertop and begins moving around as if its 'Thing' from the Adams Family. He suddenly finds what he's looking for...a banana. In the flick of a wrist he separates the banana from the rest and his hand is out of sight. 'And people say I'm weird..' Sparky mumbles to himself, only before closing his eyes to drift back into dreams of Puppy Chow and scantily clad poodles (don't ask). When suddenly... 'Ch..ch..ch..ch...Ha..ha..ha..ha..' Sparky lets out a loud yelp as when he opens his eyes he is face to snout, with a pie-faced Mike Marvelous armed with a banana. 'BOO!' yells Mike right before smacking Sparky across the head with the banana. *POW!* Mike then flicks Sparky across his snout. *FLICK!* Sparky jumps up and runs off yelping, but not before Mike gets in one final shot. A flick on his steel testicles. *TING!* Now Sparky is out of view and Micheal looks on with a sadistic grin. He quickly unpeels the banana and devours it in two bites. He then tosses the peeling on the floor and takes a few giant steps back. He gets in a three-point stance and begins to yell out as if he were a football player. 'HUT! HUT!....HIKE!' With a kick of his foot he is off, and running in the direction of the banana peel. With a small leap he lands on the peeling and...falls flat on his face. You see...in this rather weird state of mind Mike is in, he was reminded of old cartoons where you slip and slide if you happen to walk across a banana peel. He planned on using this to his advantage, the banana peel would act as some sort of snowboard type device to help him quickly catch up to Sparky. But what Mike didn't realise is..that only works in cartoons and...is a banana peel really gonna slide across carpet? I rest my case. After only seconds of chasing birds above his head only visible to one, Mike Marvelous, Mike slowly gets up and brushes himself off. After checking to make sure he's okay, The Marvelous One, is back in persuit of Sparky. While quietly humming the tune of the popular television series James Bond, Mike cartwheels across the hall only to slam into a wall. Without missing a note, Mike continues to hum his little tune and gets back to his feet and moves down the hallway with his back against the wall. Still humming, Marvelous somersaults into what seems to be Jacob's laundry room. He slowly begins to walk on the heels of his feet keeping close to the ground. 'Oompa....Loompa' Mike quickly darts his eyes around the room, and to his surprise he's the only person in sight. He then continues with his persuit when... 'Doopedy..Doo' Mike looks back again...nothing. 'We got your socks..and underwear too!' 'Curiousity is what killed the cat' 'Curiousity is what killed the cat' The words of his mother kept whirling around in his head. He was suddenly brought back to his childhood. ------------------ A young Mike Marvelous, who is still in diapers slowly began approaching the light socket. It seemed to be calling out to him. 'Come to me child. Touch me...' Mike slowly began to crawl towards the socket.. 'Yes..Caress Me. Touch me on every hole..' Young Mike's hand began to outstretch towards the socket, his fingers finding their way to the little slits. 'That's right. Stick your infant finger in my holes. YES! YES!' *BUZZZZ!* The room was quickly filled with the screams of young Mike. Mrs. Marvelous quickly ran to her child's aid. She smothers him in her bosom after scooping him up from the floor. 'Curiousity is what killed the cat, Mike. I bet you wont do that again, aye?' ------------------ Shaking the words of mother out of his head, Mike began to follow the voice. The voices led him to Jacob's dryer. The chanting continued. After grabbing a towel from the top of the dryer and whiping the whip cream icing from his face, Mike slowly opened the dryer's door when suddenly.... ------------------ 'So I was thinkin maybe we could go out for lun...' Jacob suddenly realised he was talking to himself as he entered his empty living room. No sign of Sparky. No sign of Mike. A breeze of fresh air made the hairs on the back of Jacob's neck stand. Noticing the window was open Jacob made his way across the living room and stuck his head out the window to survey the scene. Nothing. Nothing seemed to be out of place. Jacob quickly closed the windows and began to walk back across the living room looking for any signs of his best friend. 'WHOA!' Jacob yelled out when suddenly he stepped on the banana peel left on the floor by Mike. Jacob began to slip and slide all over his living room carpet (Yeah Yeah..I know..I guess I contradicted myself). Running into walls Jacob's screams filled the house, and ironically the banana peel lead him towards his laundry room. After falling on his face, Jacob got up and looked up just in time to see Mike..staring in the middle of his dryer. 'Michael...what are you...MICHAEL!' It was too late. His best friend..Mike Marvelous, was somehow just sucked into his dryer. Frightened..and someone curious, Jacob approached the dryer. He looked inside and.. 'What the...AHHHH! ' ------------------ *THUMP! THUMP!* There they were...The Can-Am Connection, once again reunited. They looked at other with confused expressions. Jacob, wonder what had happened, and Mike..who knows what he's thinkin, maybe he's wondering just who this man next to him is. While surveying their surroundings only one question came to mind... 'Where are we?!' Both spoke up at the same time..asking the same thing. 'JINX! HAHA!' Mike proudly yelled in Jacob's face. 'Michael this is no time for ga...' *THWACK!* Jacob fell to the floor holding his jaw obviously in pain. 'What was that for?!' 'You talked on a jinx, so I get to hit you..its the rules.' Mike explained. 'First of all..you're supposed to punch me in the arm, not the jaw,' Jacob scolded while Mike rolled his eyes at his friend. 'and secondly, this is no time for games Michael. Where are we? What happened?' 'Birdie!!' Mike yelled while chasing after yet, another invisible bird. Jacob watched in awe wondering what had happened to his friend. Mike continued to chase the imaginary bird when... 'Oompa...Loompa...' Jacob got to his feet and Mike gave up the chase. They stood side by side now, looking all around for the persons that were chanting these words. 'Doopedy...Doo...' 'I dont like the sound of this Michael.' Jacob said while obviously scared. 'You've tresspassed in our land and...NOW WE'RE GONNA KILL YOU!!' Suddenly out of the shadows jumped a flock of...OOMPA LOOMPAS?! 'HAHAHA!' Both Jacob and Michael broke out in laughter at the site of the short little guys. Jacob approached one of the Oompa Loompas and started to taunt the little guy. 'So...you're the little guys makin all this big noise. HAHA! You're sooo cute. Maybe I'll take ya home as a pet. How would you like that? What could you do? NOTHING!,' Jacob continued to taunt the Oompa Loompa while poking at his chest. 'How about I just kidnap you and take you home and lock you in a cage..give you a little jester hat and only let you out when I need a good laugh? You can do your dan-' 'SILENCE!,' Interupted the Oompa Loompa, and with his arm outstretched he pointed a finger at the two and yelled..'SEIZE THEM!' To their surprise, Jacob and Michael, were quickly tackled by a whole herd of Oompa Loompas. Jacob and Mike tried to fight them off, they occasionaly were able to kick one or two off of them but to no avail they were outmatched. The last thing they saw was a rather large pair of bloomers with the 'Rosie O'D.' stitched into them. Their faces were smothered in the panties then....darkness. ...TO BE CONTINUED...
