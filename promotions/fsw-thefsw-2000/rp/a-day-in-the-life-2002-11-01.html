A Day in the Life...

1 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-01-02 5:30 AM: Wake up. It’s going to be a busy day. 5:32 AM: Shower. …A busy day indeed. 5:45 AM: Dress. So much to do, so little time. Caldera rose from bed promptly at six thirty in the morning… A mere three hours since he had fallen asleep. Business takes priority. Sleep can wait. 5:55 AM: Eat nutritionally sound, high-protein breakfast. Diet is essential not only for the body, but for the mind. One must keep their mind sharp to be competitive. And to keep a sharp mind and razor-like reflexes, one must make a habit of eating a balanced breakfast. 6:15 AM: Depart for airport. Caldera had spent the previous night on the phone, all the way back to New York. Despite the obvious inconveniences it would cause him, he had insisted on spending the night in his own bed. As a result, he flew Kimbusa home on a commercial flight and used his private jet to return to his apartment for the night’s sleep. Now, only five hours later, Caldera was back out the door, catching up on the day’s news with his copy of the Times. 7:00 AM: Arrive at airport. Traveling by road in New York City can be a long and frustrating job, but Caldera’s limo driver was up to the task. The limo pulled up exactly one hundred yards from Caldera’s jet, which sits in its usual spot on a private runway in JFK International Airport. Caldera had finally had the jet re-painted, the words “Global Championship Wrestling” forever removed from his choice mode of transportation. Now the jet is a simple black and white, with the words “Caldera Enterprises, Inc.” inscribed thoughtfully on the tail. 7:15 AM: Take off. The jet left promptly, as Caldera prefers. The hour between his front door and lift off time would be his last peaceful hour for the remainder of the day. 7:25 AM: Call to FSW Headquarters. Shortly after the plane leveled out, Caldera phoned FSW Headquarters to discuss the contract status of Violence Jack and Mike McClure. Jack had cared little for the financial aspect of the contract negotiations, and had entrusted this task to Caldera and his lawyers. Caldera had done well for both Jack and McClure, earning them top dollar for their positions. The office briefly mentioned the contract of Kimbusa, but Caldera quickly passed the discussion off to one of his associates. 7:55 AM: Call to Violence Jack. Caldera had seen to it that Violence Jack sat first class on his way home, but had learned that Jack had missed his flight. Upon phoning Jack’s home, Caldera learned that Jack had simply preferred to drive rather than sit amid the usual pretentious businessmen that frequent the first class cabin. Caldera and Jack discuss their plans for the immediate future, and Caldera informs Jack of his new contractual benefits. 8:30 AM: Coffee and business briefing. Caldera’s entourage of lawyers and accountants file into Caldera’s on-board office to discuss his current financial ventures and investments over a cup of coffee. Caldera consults his experts regarding his financial future, and as usual is pleased to hear their optimistic opinions. 9:30 AM: Call to Mike McClure. McClure had taken up Caldera’s offer of first class transportation following Glory. McClure had also been given top dollar for his services, and was pleased to hear of his financial success. McClure and Caldera discuss their intentions, and plan to meet later in the week. 10:02 AM: Call to FSW Headquarters. Caldera phones the television department of FSW Headquarters to discuss the schedule for the following week’s broadcast of Glory. 10:23 AM: Land at Houston Hobby Airport. Caldera dispatches two members of his entourage to meet with Kimbusa in his apartment, and inform him of his current legal situation, which lies firmly in Caldera’s hands. 10:37 AM: Take off. The plane quickly departs once more, not waiting for the results of the Kimbusa meeting. 10:45 AM: Call to Office. Caldera phones his personal office in Columbus, Ohio, promptly after leveling out. He discusses plans for future ventures in sports-entertainment with members of the staff of Caldera Enterprises. 11:56 AM: Call from FSW Headquarters. Five Star Wrestling phones Caldera aboard the plane and informs him of possible changes in the contractual agreement regarding Violence Jack. Caldera fights the changes and the changes are overturned in favor of the original contract. 12:27 PM: Land at Columbus International Airport. The plane sets down once again in Caldera’s hometown of Columbus, and Caldera is met by a limousine. 12:46 PM: Arrive at Caldera Enterprises, Inc. Headquarters, downtown Columbus Caldera and his assistants arrive at their Columbus office and meet with officials inside over a lunch of grilled salmon and asparagus. 2:30 PM: Call from Houston Associates. Caldera is phoned immediately following his mid-day meeting to be informed of the result of Kimbusa’s meeting. Kimbusa refused to accept the terms delivered, and when pressed further, hospitalized one member of Caldera’s entourage. Caldera grants financial aid to the injured assistant, and begins and order of house arrest on Kimbusa. 3:12 PM: Call to Governor Bob Taft. Caldera reaches Governor Taft of Ohio and plans a dinner meeting for later in the evening. 3:37 PM: Nap. 4:00 PM: Leave office for Columbus International Airport. Caldera’s limo departs the office for the airport once more. En route, Caldera and staff discuss the future roles of Violence Jack and Mike McClure within Five Star Wrestling. 4:20 PM: Arrive at Airport. Caldera and company board the Caldera Enterprises jetliner once more. 4:31 PM: Call to Violence Jack. Caldera phones Violence Jack to inform him of the failed changes in contract, but finds Violence Jack gone. Caldera leaves a message. 5:00 PM: Arrive at Cincinnati Regional Airport. The jet lands in Cincinnati, where Caldera is met by another limo. The limo transports Caldera to the local Hyatt hotel. 5:20 PM: Dinner. Caldera and Taft discuss the current state of politics in the state of Ohio and the United States as a whole. Brief reminiscing regarding Taft’s former alliances with Caldera’s grandmother quickly fades to heated differences in policy. 6:15 PM: Dessert. Caldera and Taft enjoy homemade apple pie courtesy of the Hyatt hotel staff, while further discussing the financial status of wealthy investors, and their potential in today’s economy. 6:45 PM: Depart for Cincinnati Regional Airport. Caldera and associates leave the hotel and prepare to return to the jetliner. 7:06 PM: Arrive Cincinnati Regional Airport. Caldera and company board the jetliner and return to the air. 7:18 PM: Call from Violence Jack. Jack returns Caldera’s call. Jack discusses his legitimate beliefs in the occult, and Caldera listens carefully. After several minutes of testimony by Jack, a skeptical Caldera ends the discussion. 8:00 PM: Call from Mike McClure. McClure phones Caldera once more to further discuss his status with Five Star Wrestling. Caldera and McClure further plot their intentions for the future of Five Star Wrestling. 9:00 PM: Call to FSW Headquarters. Caldera phones FSW once more to inform them of his intention to escalate the level of violence brought on by his allies. FSW warns Caldera of potential breach in contract, but Caldera remains confident in his plans. 9:52 PM: Land at JFK International Airport. Caldera arrives in New York City after a day in the air. His limo collects him at the airport and returns him to his New York Office. 10:31 PM: Arrive at New York City office. Caldera arrives at his New York office for a brief meeting with his associates there. They further discuss his plans regarding Violence Jack and McClure, and prepare for possible legal action. 11:43 PM: Depart Office. Caldera’s limo transports him back to his apartment for the evening. 12:27 AM: Arrive Home. Caldera returns to his apartment and relaxes following a busy day. 12:35 AM: Call from Kimbusa. Kimbusa phones Caldera in a state of extreme anger, but Caldera transfers the call to an associate at his office, and prepares to sleep. 12:45 AM: Sleep. It had been a busy day…
