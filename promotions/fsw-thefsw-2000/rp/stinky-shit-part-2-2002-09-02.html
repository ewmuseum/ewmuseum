Stinky Shit Part 2

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 2, 2002 Tommy walks out of the bathroom and to Chris' room and he peers in the doorway. Chris sits up after taking notice to Tommy in the doorway. He points his finger towards Tommy and screams, 'YOU'RE THE ONE WHO INVITED HIM!' Tommy shrugs, not knowing how to respond. Suddenly, a though clicks in his mind. 'Wait a second...I didn't have a dream!' Chris rolls his eyes at Tommy's stupidity. 'No, you dipshit!' Tommy begins to raise his voice, seemingly angry. 'You just wanted to make me clean up the shit all over the bathroom!' Chris claps his hands in sarcasm. 'Ding, ding, ding. And whadda we have for him, Johnny?' Tommy gets pissed at Chris' bastard attempts and has a quasi episode. 'The hell with that Chris. This is your house.' Chris looks at Tommy in disbelief. 'Are you kidding,' he says, seeing no response from Tommy. 'You invited that dirty fucking Mexican guy into my house, and YOU are cleaning up his shit.' Tommy attempts to leave. Chris springs up from the bed and tackles Tommy to the floor, just outside the room. He hears the front door creak open. Chris glances up, seeing Jose walking in. He climbs back onto his feet, leaving Tommy and walks towards Jose. 'You goddamn dirty little Mexican ELF! You shit all over my sink...my walls..my TOOTHBRUSH!' Bagwell grows furious, shaking his fist at Jose. Jose puts out his hand, 'Back! Back I say!' Chris pays no attention to his commands and delivers a rather racey comment, 'Exactly where you're going. BACK ACROSS THE BORDER!' He wraps his hands around Jose's neck and lifts him up above his head. Jose yells with a scream that sounds similar to a tight 11 year old virgin girl getting raped by an elephant with an oversize penis. 'YEA! SCREAM YOU LITTLE SONUVA BITCH!' Chris yells in Jose's face. Suddenly, a big black man walks in. He towers at over seven feet tall and weighs in at over 350 pounds. He comes straight up to Chris and gets into his face. 'Yo you bez not be messin wit mah boy Jose. I'll bust yo azz up nigga.' Chris drops Jose to the floor, getting right back up in the face of the seven foot monster. He shows no apparent fear. 'Haha! You and what army?' Jose, sitting on the floor, yaps up to Chris 'I bring my people. HAKEEM, CALL HIM EEN!' Big black Hakeem tilts his head to the side and screams, 'ABBUUUUU!!!' A sudden stench fills the room, adding to the previous smell of soggy and rotted shit. Another seven foot man walks in. But instead, he is a rather scrauny 100-150 pound man. He's wearing a 7-Eleven shirt with a small nametag that reads 'Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh'. The nametag stretches nearly across the entire shirt. Abu begins to speak, releases a foul stench from his mouth, 'I am Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh. I come from country far distant from yours.' Chris looks at Abu a bit shocked. 'Good Christ, man. Do you EAT at all?' Tommy finally stands back onto his feet after a short break on the ground, staring at the ceiling. He looks over to the doorway, seeing the 3 quite odd characters. Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh approaches Chris real close. 'I am Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh and in my country, we do not harass the little people.' Chris stares right into Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh's eyes, trying to hide any signs of weakness from that horrid odor. 'I know what the hell your name is and in this country, we shit in the toilet. NOT in the sink and all over the walls.' Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh shakes his finger at Chris, expecting him to take better consideration of other people's problems. 'Jose has anal problems. His daddy raped him severly as a little boy. His pin needle asshole was stretched enough to fit your whole foot.' Tommy's eyes widen and mouth nearly shatters from hitting the floor so hard. He makes a face as if he was going to cry from the disturbing information. Utterly shocked for stepping into the conversation at the wrong time, he decides he should better leave. Tommy turns completely around and quickly scampers into the bedroom, slamming the door behind him. Chris remains standing, motionless. He doesn't worry too much about the information he just found out, but more about the terrible stench that seems to still be inflicting into his nasal passages Hakeem suddenly bursts out in some orgasmic episode. 'OOH HAH HAH! HEH HOE HAH! NARF!' Bagwell jumps back, scared to hell, 'What in the blue hell is wrong with him?' Jose, standing back up, smiles. 'He orgasm.' Bagwell barks back, 'WHAT?' Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh nods his head in compliance, 'Everytime he sees a fly land on shit, he has an orgasm. Its his way of being sexually charged.' Chris begins to have a conniption. 'You guys really have to leave my house.' Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh shakes his head no, 'Jose said he made a mess.' Chris finally sees some worth to these guys being around. 'Sure as hell did.' Jose tugs Chris and then points to Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh. He says, 'Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh clean it. He will.' Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh complies, 'He is right. I will clean.' Chris feels a lot better and finally cracks a smile. He begins giving orders, 'Get to it.' Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh enters the bathroom and shuts the door. Chris stares, clueless as to what he's about to do. 'How does he plan on cleaning it without any sprays or towels or anything?' 'He lick,' Jose responds. Chris shakes his head, 'He's gonna do what?' Hakeem steps in, trying to clear things up a bit with a touch of ebonics, 'Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh eatz dah shee-at yo.' Chris quivers in disgust, 'This is quite the interesting day.' Jose chuckles, giving Chris the wrong impression. He tries to call him out on it, 'Lemme guess. You're wetting yourself now, too.' Hakeem steps in once more, preaching to the world, 'Nahhh, Jose be laffin at sum shee-at.' Chris just looks with confused eyes. Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh walks out of the bathroom and holds the door open. The room is sparkling clean and a fresh, beautiful smelling scent leaks from inside its contents. 'I have completed,' he says proudly. Chris smiles in gratitud, 'I can't thank you enough.' 'We go. We must,' Jose declares. Bagwell shows no attempt to change their minds. 'Well..have an interesting life. As if it weren't interesting enough.' Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh bids his farewell with some words of advice, 'Chris, you're on the brim of being DEAD. OR maybe, you just don't appreciate being ALIVE!' He pauses. Hakeem steps in to preach one more time. 'NOW GO TEAR DER ASSEZ, BROTHA!' The picture fades and the credits beging to roll. ----------------------------------------------------- Credits: • Jose 'The Little Mexican Dwarf' played by Tim Walden's penis (how else do you think we got him so small!) • Hakeem 'The Big Black Thug' played by Tyler Davidson's mother • Abu Mohammed Abdul Jabber Ali-Ak Shabazz Dahh played by the little Chinese guy in 'Ocean's Eleven' • Tommy played by some 25 cent hooker found protesting in front of a ShopRite Supermarket.
