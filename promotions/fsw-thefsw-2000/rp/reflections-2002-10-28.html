Reflections

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, October 28th, 2002 'I can't believe I lost. Darn it. Darn it. Darn it all to heck. What happened!? What happened to me!? I worked so hard! Where did I go?' Sobbing is heard from the room of FSW's Canadian Hero and Legend, 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous. The camera shows Mike's midget companion, Mini-Mike, enter the dark room with a concerned look on his little face. He slowly tip toes over to the side of the bed where his owner and friend lays, sheets over his head, face buried in the pillow. Mike's muffled sobs are interrupted as the 3'8' tall midget tugs gingerly on the bed sheet. Mike raises his head from the tear stained pillow and looks down at his midget friend through red eyes shrink-wrapped with tears. The small man looks up and speaks. 'Michaew. Awe you ok?' The FSW Hall of Famer breathes in rapidly and makes a sniffling sound, cupping his hands over his eyes as he speaks. 'I'll survive....it's just....it's just....I don't know.' Mike continues to sniffle as Mini-Mike hops up onto the bed and sits beside him. 'I've nevew seen you wike this befowe, Michaew.' 'It's because...it's because I've WASTED my entire career! I turned my back on the fans in FSW when I went to PCW, I tried to succeed there, and failed. I worked up enough courage to come back, tried to succeed, and failed again. I was made an...an embarrassment. I was a joke! I exchanged a legacy in FSW for a comedy act in PCW. And OSW was no better.' The midget waits a moment to let it sink in then replies in his squeaky, midget lisp. 'But Michaew...you've been doing GOOD! You pwoved awot of peopwe wwong. You main evented at Bad Moon Wising!!!' 'Yeah, I made it to the main event...but I got greedy. I lied, I cheated, I backstabbed...I went against everything that I once stood for. I was a Canadian Hero, Mini-Mike! A role model to children and senior citizens! I've even been a pezz dispenser!!!' Mike breaks down sobbing again. Mini-Mike consoles him by patting him lightly on the back. 'Weww...it's not too late, Michaew.' Mike covers up his face and lets out a wail of distain. 'Yes it is! I'm not a Canadian Hero anymore, I'm a Canadian ZERO! It's TIMO who they're chanting for now. HE'S on all the lunch boxes! And I don't blame them....why would they want to store their lunches in a plastic box with MY face on it?! The face of a washed-up, defeated, Americanized SELL OUT!' 'Michaew...there MUST be some way you can win back the fans! You showed the fans you still care by helping Timo aftew youw match at Bad Moon Wising when Mob Wule attacked him! That's a good stawt!' Mike's frown turns into a tiny half smile and he wipes the tears from his cheeks. 'Mob Rule...' 'Huh?' 'I will fight Mob Rule! I'll help Timo send them packing so FSW can be like it used to be!' Mike sits up on the bed and smiles as the ideas keep flowing. 'I had it all wrong, Mini-Mike. When all the new guys like Timo, Jason O'Neil and Cassius Deamont showed up, I wanted them out. I tried to convince myself that I was PROTECTING FSW by doing everything in my power to stop them from succeeding and capturing the World Title for myself. But I was wrong all along. I only ended up hurting myself, others, and FSW itself. I failed in the end...I forgot who I am. But now I realize...I AM a Canadian Hero, darn it! And there's a NEW evil in town...and this time it's not me!' Mike stands up with a proud smile on his face and fire in his eyes. 'I will fight. This time not for me, but for FSW. I will fight beside Timo. I will fight beside whoever else is willing to purge FSW of the cancer that is threatening to eat away at it's insides! You know what...I've been lost for a long time. Ever since I left FSW in the first place...I got too ambitious for my own good. Well, this time I'm fighting for my home. I'm fighting for my legacy. I'm fighting for all the little MARVELites!' Mini-Mike smiles warmly, genuinely happy to see his friend finally coming around after months of bitterness. Mike quickly puts on a pair of jeans and a sweater, then puts on his shoes and gets his keys. 'Mini-Mike, I'll be back soon. I'm not gonna stay at home all day kicking my own arse when I should be in the gym getting ready to kick some Mob Rule arse! It's aboot time I show FSW why they call me MR. FSW! Mob Rule...look out...cause MIKEY'S BACK!!!' With that, the Canadian Hero shoves on his winter coat and sprints out the door, flashes one bright smile (TING~!), then closes it, leaving behind his tear stained bed and all the wasted, torn up miseries that he's been through in his career. It's time to stand up for what's right. Stand up for FSW.
