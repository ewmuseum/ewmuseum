Endangered Rapist

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day before tomorrow 'Ah, Mr. Deville! Welcome to Costa Rica!' The greeting comes from a small man in a green business suit. He rises to his feet while he speaks, a wide smile splitting his tan face as he extends his hand to the newcomer. Long locks of curly black hair hang down in front of his eyes, but Deville notices the unmistakable shine of ultimate greed hidden in the fields of brown. His approval increases as he grips the man's hand and notes their slippery texture. Perfect, Deville's mind cheers as he takes a seat at the table. The other man nods approvingly at Deville's black Armani suit and returns to his place, his eyes lingering even more approvingly at the briefcase placed between them. One slimy hand reaches out to touch it but Deville cuts him off with a warning clearing of his throat. The man's gaze jerks up to lock on Angelo's cold blue eyes, and the anxious greed melts away under a mask of friendliness. The men are seated in the corner of a small and seedy bar whose name Deville can't even guess at. There are only four other tables spread across the rotting wooden floor, and only one of them is occupied. No waiters or waitresses bustle around to take or deliver orders, only a big old fat man with a crooked eye, thick gray hair, and a grimy Hawaaian shirt. He glares around from behind the desk he masquerades as a bar, tipsy from his perpetual habit. 'Mr. Grundy, I presume,' Angelo inclines his head at the other's nod, leaning back in his chair so that it creaks loudly. 'Thank you for meeting me on such short notice.' 'No problem, senor! None at all!' Grundy exclaims, waving his hands dismissively before him. His eyes wander to the briefcase again and he licks his lips unconsciously. 'Business is pleasure, yes?' 'Very much so, lately,' Angelo returns with a smile, carefully folding his hands atop the briefcase. 'Nice place,' He chides, casting a disparaging look around the room. 'It is good for business,' Grundy assures him. 'Your flight was pleasent?' 'Let's cut to the chase,' Deville responds curtly, growing tired of the small-talk. 'Do we have a deal... or not?' They had spoken previously on the phone, during which Deville outlined his idea. Grundy refused to say much over the phone but assured Angelo that if he came to meet with him something could definitely be arranged. Deville trusts the man less than a starving dog, but that kind of apprehension comes along with the territory. 'You are talking a lot of rainforest, Mr. Deville,' Grundy's tone is apologetic but the sympathy doesn't reach his eyes. 'Very hard for me to explain,' he looks down at the briefcase. 'Cost me much.' 'That's not a problem.' 'Good,' Grundy beams, then a curious look strikes his face. 'What do you want with so much land? Are you with McDonald's?' His eyes narrow into suspicious slits as he asks this last, his voice losing it's vibrance as he speaks the dreaded name. 'Oh no, nothing quite so sinister as that,' Deville soothes with a chuckle. 'There are animals living here that interest me greatly.' 'You want to study?' Grundy gives his head a shake, his words incredulous. 'I want to kill,' Angelo states menacingly. 'Kill what?' 'bradypus variegatus... The Three-Toed Sloth.' Angelo's voice turns to ash as Elmer pops up in his mind, head bobbing up and down as it chitters lustfully. I don't even want to think of what that little shit would've done if Ming hadn't shown up, Angelo curses, recalling Glory. Sure, we probably killed Elmer with our assault, but there are thousands more of the cursed things here, waiting for morons like Frotitude to come and adopt them. 'The sloth?' Grundy repeats, disbelieving. 'You want to buy our rainforest so you can go in and hunt down an endangered species?' His coarse laughter fills the room until he realises that Deville is indeed serious, then his face goes blank at the response. 'Too much work... I was just planning on clear-cutting,' the off-handed and innocent tone with which he says that should incite lightning from the Heavens, but Angelo receives only a scandalised stare from Grundy. 'Clear cutting? Fiendish! You would kill many just to get the sloth...' 'Yeah well... Collateral damage and all that.' 'But why do you hate the sloth? It is lazy! Like little tailless dog! It only comes down from tree once a week to use bathroom!' Grundy seems far less outraged now, his dilapidated conscience only allowing him the slightest concern. 'They are harmless!' 'Oh really?' Angelo mocks, leaning forward. 'Well, your harmless and lazy little sloth managed to rape a friend of mine. A male friend. Over ten times it's size. ...Then it tried to rape me.' His voice is emotionless as he recalls Elmer's escapades. Grundy simply sits in stunned silence, regurgitating what he's heard over and over again, trying to make sense of it. He shifts uncomfortably in his seat under Angelo's expressionless stare. 'Uh... Right,' he croaks finally, looking nervously from side to side. 'They're a perversion of nature, Mr. Grundy, and it's high time someone put PETA in their place and did away with the little ingrates. I'm going to personally see to it that all in Elmer's lineage are killed.' 'Elmer?' 'The rapist,' Angelo answers matter-of-factly. Another awkward silence dominates the room as Grundy thinks of how to appropriately respond to such an obvious madman. 'This is a very bad plan, Mr. Deville,' he shakes his head disapprovingly. 'But maybe it will look better in the briefcase, yes?' Grundy's grubby hands reach forward and give a slight tug on the briefcase, to which Angelo obediently removes his hands. A few fresh drops of perspiration gleam on Grundy's forehead as he fumbles with the clasps. As the case pops open his eyes light up like a young boy finding his father's magazine stash for the first time. He swallows hard as he shoots a quick glance as Deville, then slams the lid down and hastily locks it. 'So...?' Deville prompts after several seconds of being stared at. 'We have a deal, Mr. Deville,' Grundy licks his chops again as he pulls the briefcase down onto his lap and clutches it there like a child... A child he's trying to squeeze to death. 'Excellent,' Angelo grins as he rises to his feet. 'My people will be in touch, Mr. Grundy.' Grundy mumbles some sort of farewell as Deville leaves the bar, but he doesn't hear it. He's too busy wallowing in his success. This marks a great day for the wrestlers of FSW... No longer will they have to live in fear of being raped by a twenty pound tailless dog who sprints around at the breakneck speed of 1/4 mph. Goodbye Elmer, you three-toed endangered rapist...
