Retrospective motivation

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 2, 2002 What is it like to be sitting on the edge of stardom, perched mere inches from immortality, your name in the news and your face all over television three nights each week, famous to the public while still maintaining a cult following to rival those you had looked up to before you got your break... ...And suddenly have the seat pulled out from under you unceremoniously, banishing you, back to the obscurity that you'd hoped you could finally put behind you for good? All because of one man, one man with an abominable avarice, who used you to bring himself to the forefront by promising you the world and only maintaining his end of the bargain for as long as it was convenient? What, indeed, as the question is rather irrelevant to most, just a trivial concept, foreign to the average John Q. Public, who merely regard it as something that just plain will never happen to them, something out of their league as they'll never have the big break in the first place, never realizing the simple truth of the matter, that most every man and woman has experienced a microcosmal version of this situation, being used by someone they thought they could trust, the wounds running deep for the time being, but they'll usually heal over a couple weeks' time..... ....But I was never quite your usual case. One memory stained my mind, gave me my first lasting self-impression of my new era.. I started the long walk from my taxi back to the hotel room, tired and agonied by the beating laid down upon me that night in the Glory ring by my famed opponent, the former World Champion Epitome of Evil, glad for my own safety that those two Mexican stoner guys had showed up, even if they were steamed out of their head they still interrupted that series of powerbombs that would probably have wrecked my back all to hell.. 'Mr. Walden?'..It was Jeffrey, the bellhop, he'd obviously recalled our past conversation, and I suddenly lurched mentally, trying to remember what all I'd said to him before I left for the arena.. 'Er, hiya Jeff, how's it going tonight?' I inquired, hoping he'd give the conversation some initial direction because for the life of me, I couldn't bring up the dialogue back in my mind. He looked to the side as I'd spoken, so the acknowledgement came as a minor surprise, his head turning back toward me with a startle, 'Oh? It's going pretty well to be honest, most of the guests are either busy with conventions or already back in their rooms, what about you, heard you had a match tonight, how'd that go?' I held my back a little as I sat down on the chairs off to the side, the pain still shooting up my spine somewhat...'Ugh, I'm lucky to have walked away after that one, he took a few cheap shots and killed my momentum, ended up getting powerbombed into the mat three times by that big lug, but I got the last laugh this time...' 'How'd you manage to pull that off? I mean, you'd be halfway broken by that point, it shouldn't be hard to win at that point for him, no offense..' I nodded as he finished, not so much out of agreement as lack of objection, then I smiled. 'What's so funny?' I let out a mild chuckle at the events of the night..'Some idiots came out and distracted him, gave me enough recovery time to quickly catch him with a big reversal to win it, then I just got the hell outta dodge, I had him pissed and that Blair loser probably figured out what I'd done to his shampoo, best that I just take what I could get and live to fight next week..It's really a bit of a bother to be honest, I shouldn't really be wasting my time trying to get back at him when he plays his mind games, it's what he wants, he wants to know he's made an impact on me and I'm just helping his cause. I just hope to God that next week I get a piece of him, because I know I could beat the hell out of him so badly that he'd get the message, but it's really all the luck of the booking..' I trailed off, lacking any real continuation to my thoughts at that point. Interjection. 'You don't know who you're facing next week?' I had an answer, but I really didn't feel like bringing it up at the moment, so I decided to leave him something vague to keep him happy. 'Well, I've got a match, I know that, and Blair's there, bunch of other guys involved too in a sort of gauntlet thing, but I don't have all the details, I just hope I get lucky and get a late draw, the match is for a World Title shot on the first Pay-Per-View, so I could conceivably win it with a good draw...' Oh how wrong that one turned out to be, I really couldn't win it with a good draw, I gave it my all, I fought toward the obvious weak point when I came in and I still couldn't wear down Irving, he hung on even when I went to the move that would've had the best hope to break him and bring me to the final battle of the match, I desperately tried to change the circumstance and he used the momentum against me, it was just too much to handle..I wasn't cut out to win a World Title, I couldn't take him one-on-one after he went through 3 other men to get to me..I knew that maybe, just maybe, if I'd tried something different or just had a little more luck, there might've been a chance, but the fact remained, I had lots of luck on my side and I STILL couldn't take him. I was a failure for the World Title, but maybe it was just a sign that I needed to train a little harder, take a step back from the main-event ranks, maybe it was just that Irving was too good for now, not that I was too bad.. Meanwhile, in my memory, I started to get up from the chair, 'I should get some sleep, it's been a long day and I've got some plans for tomorrow morning, gonna get some shopping done he--' 'Wait..'Jeff's face read like an open book, he had hoped to do a little more talking at least tonight..'Why exactly do you do this? Why do you go out and damn near get hospitalized? I mean, you've got plenty of money from owning places and that contract buy-out, not to mention the Waldenbooks endorsement, why do you need to go out and brawl with people?' ........That struck close to home. Why would I do it? I could be happy as is with my substantial net worth and live off the money the rest of my life, I could just work indy feds occasionally to give young talent a nice experience or invest the money wisely, I could even go to Japan and work on my craft, why was I in Five Star Wrestling? What was there to be gained from joining them, where I would get a steady paycheck sure, but there would be no true benefit to it? But I knew darn well what my motive was, I'd obviously had to have found it to join in the first place. 'Well, Jeff, it's not just about the money for me...You can have all the money in the world, but it's just not going to matter when you're gone..I need to have a legacy, I want to feel the fame, I want to see my name up at the top of the list when it comes to 'Professional Wrestlers of the Decade', I want to feel that I've really accomplished something...And I damn near did with my first federation, I was main-eventing, I was literally owning the place, hiring and firing whomever I pleased, and then the very man who helped me attain my greatest glories there turned his back on me and threw me away, when I was just nearing the pinnacle of greatness..Ever since, I've wanted the fame still, even more than before, not merely to give myself a sense of accomplishment, but also to spite the man who's hogged the spotlight in the wrestling world for the past year and a half, to show him once and for all that his time is over, and he should've given up when he had the chance...You know who I'm talking about, you know what he did, and you know, it's why I sold out my share of the company too, because I knew that while I owned a smaller portion of the same company as him, I could never attain glory enough to eclipse him and gain my worthy recognition..That's why I do it..That's why I damn near got hospitalized tonight, not for the money or the gold, no...I did it...for Glory.' It was rather sad to think about it in retrospect, I'd totally blown my opportunity to earn my opportunity to attain the glory, and now I'd become just a footnote for the time being..Glory would have to wait, but there was still hope in a way, I had my opportunity next week to not only gain a little glory for the time being, but also to get my chance to be known in a mere matter of weeks in the writings of legend if I could get the chance, retrieve it from its suspended animation by outwrestling my opponents, and gain my career not merely rebirth at Genesis, but the opportunity to make easy my legacy of becoming the first Primetime Champion, to go down in history as a first-time holder of a secondary title, a role held by many different sorts of people in the wrestling world, from evil super geniuses to Gothic personalities and militant African-Americans, all of whom had gained great fame through it and would probably at least be rewarded with a couple of lines in the wrestling history books...It was a destiny I could still afford to fail in, but should I attain it, my road to fame would become all the easier. He nodded with an understanding, not just of what I had said and meant, but also of my intent to discontinue the conversation for tonight. 'All right, guess I'll see you around then?' 'Sure, and hopefully you'll see a lot more of me in the years to come.' I walked away, feeling confident for the moment, but knowing it would be a long haul ahead of me either way... I wish I hadn't been as right as I was.
