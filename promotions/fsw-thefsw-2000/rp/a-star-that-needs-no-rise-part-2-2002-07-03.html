A Star That Needs No Rise (Part 2)

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-3-02 -----Rise. To come up from the bottom. To ascend to greatness. To go up. To increase in rank. Some people need time to rise. Some rise from the ashes like the proverbial phoenix. Like coming from the rubble of one federation, and rising to the top of another. Some peoples' stars don't ever rise. Others need no time to rise into their place of glory. At least they think so.----- **Magnum Wolf is backstage at the latest Glory. Obviously, it's after his segment with Jack Brooze becaause he's still breathing slightly harder than usual. The smile on his face is more than enough proof that he's happy with the way things ended up. He's standing at a long buffet table covered in random goodies. Presently, he's munching on a little rolled up peice of salami with cream cheese in the middle. A young FSW ring crew worker is staring at him with a sort of bored expression. Magnum speaks right through the food he's shoveling in his mouth.** 'You know, it's really hard to believe that the fans responded the way they did out there tonight! I mean, look at me. Magnum F'N Wolf. 13 Time EOW Champion. How many of these guys in the audience, or in the back room can claim they've won 13 ANYTHING... let alone a world title?' Magnum stares at the man intently, as though he expects an answer, 'Come on, fool. How many?' 'Uhm. Probably none of them, Mr. Wolf,' answers the somewhat nerdy kid. Magnum looks positively shocked as he says, 'You're kidding! Not one of them! Well, surely, you're underestimating the crowd a little bit, there.' **Blank stare from the crewman again, as he totally misses the comedy in that last line. Wolf throws his hands up in the air in disgust, and begins to talk in a very stern voice now.** 'You've GOT to pay attention here, kid! You look like you've been watching professional soccer! Your eyes are totally glazed over! Don't you get the joke I just told?' 'Uhm,' is the only word the kid can get out to accompany his totally blank stare. 'Oh my God. FSW is so sad, they couldn't even hire an intelligent guy to put my ring together! Look, I asked you how many people in the crowd, or backstage could say they had won 13 world titles, right?' The kid nods, 'Ok. Then, when you said none of them had 13 world titles, I purposely avoided saying that the guys in the back were talented, and said that I thought you had underestimated the talents of the crowd!' **Magnum, once again, keels himself over laughing. The kid, on the other hand, continues his impression of a zombie as Magnum laughs heartily. As Wolf begins to ease up his laughter a bit, he notices that the crewman isn't laughing. Immediately, his demeanor changes from giddy to pissed off. His eyes cut through the kid now...** 'Don't just stand there and stare at me, boy. If you want to act like a vegetable, go somewhere else and do it on your own time. I was trying to make you feel good by talking to you. I'm the biggest thing to ever happen in your life, and you don't even know it. Jesus, you FSW ilk are all alike. Too brain dead to understand when a man is trying to entertain you. And now look at you... turning red... what? You want to hit me? Go ahead and try, kid. Jack Brooze tried, and got a big fat check in his L column for his troubles.' **The kid had turned red during Magnum's last speech... his fists were now clenched to the point that his knuckles were slightly whitened right at the joints. Wolf just kept egging him on by ridiculing his mother, his wife, anyone he could think of.** 'Yeah, yea... you're all the same. Big dogs until a bigger dog barks at you. Although I really shouldn't mess with you; your mom's the biggest dog I've ever met!' exclaims Magnum as he continues to bend himself at the waist with laughter at his own jokes. **In a huff, the crewman turns around, and walks away. Magnum shakes his head as he does the 'recharge' laugh. He pops a chocoltae covered cherry in his mouth, and turns back toward the kid, smiling his head off.** 'FSW. Land of the idiots. First Brooze gets himself pinned because he was about to go redneck rampage on me for making fun of 'his beloved company'. Then, this kid gets all pissed off cause I called him an idiot. That's like a cat getting pissed off because I tell it it has fur. That's like Shabazz getting pissed off cause I called him black. It's just what you are. Deal with it. Lots of pent up anger among these people that REALLY needs a place to be directed constructively. Maybe I should open a gym around here. Have punching bags made of cement... stair climbers that are 5 feet high on each step... treadmills that change speeds randomly. I could make a fortune---' **Magnum continues to ramble on without much of a direction in his speech for a few minutes. The scene fades to black as he continues to rant about how much money he could make with this gym he's coming up with. Fade to black...**
