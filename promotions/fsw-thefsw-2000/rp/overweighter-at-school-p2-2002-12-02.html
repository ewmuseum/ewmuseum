Overweighter At School P2

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/12/02 (The camera opens up. you can see a bunch of kids walking down a corridor and at the back you can spot out The Overweighter. His walking with Jeffery and Terence, they are walking towards their math’s classroom.) The Overweighter: When’s break time? Jeffery: We have 15 minute’s break now before math. The Overweighter: So why are we heading towards math when we have a break. Terence: Because people don’t let us go anywhere else. The Overweighter: Yeah well while I’m here there is going to be changes, come on. Jeffery: cool. (The Overweighter, Jeffery and Terence walk out on to the school field where there is people playing soccer. Jeffery, Terence walks up to the person who brought in the ball.) Terence: Err…Can we play please? Boy with ball: No, who let you out on the field anyway? (The Overweighter walks up behind Jeffery and Terence.) The Overweighter: I did and we want to play soccer. Boy with ball: Err how about no! The Overweighter: Either you let us play or I will pop you ball so you can’t play? Boy with ball: fine you can play, but it has to be you three vs. us lot. The Overweighter: Okay. (The boy with the ball gives it to The Overweighter and walks down the other side of the pitch. The Overweighter passes it to Jeffery, the whole of the other team charges at Jeffery and Jeffery gets scared and ducks on the floor. One of the other players on the other team gets the ball and run for the goal. Terence is in goal, the boy goes to shoot when a huge brick wall runs in front of him. He looks up its The Overweighter. He tackles him, runs up the pitch with the ball. The Overweighter is so big the other team is scared to go and tackle him. He gets into the box, kicks the ball and he kicks it so hard the goalkeeper couldn’t see the ball. The Overweighter got the ball every time and scores. One of the people on the other team gets annoyed and tries to take down The Overweighter. He runs as fast as he can and dropkicks The Overweighter in the leg, He doesn’t budge and the boy falls on the floor. The Overweighter scores another 3 goals, that boy still wants to take down The Overweighter. The boy runs as fast as he can again, he goes to dropkick The Overweighter in the leg again but he slips and The Overweighter turns around. The boy’s foot hits The Overweighter in the (Cough). The Overweighter is stumbling.) Boy: Yes I took down the fat man. (What the boy doesn’t know The Overweighter is going to fall on him. The Overweighter falls and the boy is squished. The bell goes. Everyone runs inside and The Overweighter follows while having his hand down his trousers. The Overweighter is late for math because he stopped to rub his (cough) better, he walks in late and the teacher is shouting at someone else for being late. The Overweighter suddenly becomes afraid, the other kid just got a detention and the teacher walks up to The Overweighter.) Math Teacher: Why are you late? The Overweighter: I would rather not say. (The Overweighter is still rubbing his (cough) while talking.) Math Teacher: Fair enough, its not like I can give you detention. The Overweighter: Cool. Math Teacher: Now sit down! (The Overweighter walks over to a table, sits down and looks across the table. There is the kid that he fell on when playing soccer. The Overweighter looks at the kid. His sitting in the chair shaking.) The Overweighter: Sorry about earlier mate. Kid: It was my fault, I should be sorry. Math Teacher: Okay, children and grown up. We are going to be doing pie. The Overweighter: I’m an A* student in pie. Math Teacher: ha, ha, ha. I bet you are. The Overweighter: I can write a 12-page essay about pies, cherry pie, apple pie, blackberry pie, blueberry pie, banana pie, strawberry pie, steak and kidney pie, cheese and onion pie, Chicken and mushroom pie, the new Good Fellers pizza pie. Math Teacher: Okay, that’s enough. Kid: Wow, you must like pie. The Overweighter: Umm I love pie. Kid: I’m Fred. The Overweighter: I’m Overweighter. Fred: I know who you are your in FSW, right? The Overweighter: Yeah that’s right. Fred: Have you met The Rock before? The Overweighter: Yeah… (The Overweighter thinks back into the depths of his mind. It goes black you can’t see a thing. You start to see The Overweighter remember his run in with The Rock. The Overweighter is standing in the middle of the ring, with The Rock, who is doing an in-ring promo.) The Rock: Who in the blue hell are you? The Overweighter: Um... I'm Overw.... The Rock: It doesn't matter what your weight is! The Overweighter: No, that's my nam... The Rock: It doesn’t matter what your name is! You come here to The Rock's show, Smackdown! Standin' here with your fat ass, why don't you lose some weight you sick FREAK! The Overweighter: What!!?! The Rock: Sorry mate put it there. (The Rock sticks out his hand and The Overweighter shakes it. The Overweighter lets go. The Rock sets The Overweighter up for a Rock Bottom and tries to lift him. He still can’t lift The Overweighter. He gets tired, The Overweighter farts and The Rock suddenly stops trying to lift The Overweighter.) The Rock: Man, what did you eat!!?! The Overweighter: Some pie. The Rock: Wow wow wow wow wow wow wow wow wow wow woow. Did you say pie? The Overweighter: Yeah. The Rock: It just so happens The Rock love a slice of pie. (It goes black, that’s all that The Overweighter can remember from their run in. The Overweighter is now dribbling down his top. Fred is looking at it slowly trickle down on it The Overweighter’s T-shirt.) Fred: Your dribbling on yourself. The Overweighter: Sorry I was thinking about pie. Math Teacher: Okay I'm going to come around with the textbooks. The work is on page 25. I want you all to go up to question 20 or you wont be able to go to lunch. (The Math Teacher gives them out. He gives one to The Overweighter, sits down and drinks his cup of coffee. The Overweighter opens the book, turns to page 25 looks at the first question and goes cross eyed.) The Overweighter: Hey Fred what’s the answer to the first question? Fred: It’s 5. (The Overweighter writes it down on a piece of paper.) The Overweighter: What’s the answer to question 2? Fred: It’s 72 (The Overweighter writes it down on the piece of paper.) The Overweighter: What’s the answer to… Fred: Okay now when I'm done I will give you my work to copy. The Overweighter: Yay. Math Teacher: Overweighter stop talking, move over there on your own. (The Overweighter gets up, moves to the table that the Math Teacher is pointing to and sits down. He looks at the third question, goes crossed eyed again and starts to sweet.) The Overweighter (talking to himself): Oh stupid questions. (The Overweighter rests his head on the table. He slowly falls asleep. His eyes slowly open and the Math Teacher is in front of him.) Math Teacher: It’s lunchtime now. The Overweighter: W00t! Math Teacher: But next time you have to do more questions as you only did 2 questions. The Overweighter: Okay. (The Overweighter sits up, looks around the classroom and no one else is there. He stands up, looks outside of the window and sees Jeffery and Terence getting picked on. The Overweighter runs out of the classroom, down the stairs and outside where Jeffery and Terence are getting picked on. He gets there, slows down and walks slowly behind the people picking on them. The Overweighter coughs, the kid’s turn around and look up. They see The Overweighter. They run away, without looking back and zoom around the corner.) The Overweighter: Do you guys always get picked on this much? Terence: Yeah. Jeffery: When you’re gone we will get picked on even more. The Overweighter: Okay, next time they pick on you just tell them that you will tell me and I will kick their asses Overweighter stylee. Jeffery: That wont work? Terence: They maybe dumb but they wont fall for that. The Overweighter: Fall for what? I will kick their ass’s, overweighter stylee. Jeffery: Okay we will tell them. The Overweighter: Anyway it’s lunchtime where can I get some food? Terence: You don’t want to eat the school food, unless you like eating rubber. Jeffery: Or plastic. The Overweighter: Oh, I see. So what is there to do at lunchtime? Jeffery: There is a chess club. Terence: Homework Club. Jeffery: Library, where you can read quietly. Terence: Computer room where you can go online, play games and do stuff. The Overweighter: Cool computer room it is then, do we pass the cafeteria? Jeffery: Yes. The Overweighter: Good, good. (All three of them walk towards the cafeteria. The Overweighter walks into the cafeteria and stands in the queue. He gets to the front of the queue.) The Overweighter: Hi, so what’s on the menu? Dinner Lady: Leftovers from last month. The Overweighter: Last month..? (The Overweighter starts to feel sick.) The Overweighter: Yummy. (The Dinner lady pours a giant spoon full of some brown sludge onto a plate, hands it to The Overweighter.) Dinner Lady: Next! (The Overweighter moves down the line. He gets to a counter.) Dinner Lady 2: That will be $1.00 please. The Overweighter: We have to pay for this puke? Dinner Lady 2: Well how else are we going to get paid? The Overweighter: Fine, here. (The Overweighter gives the Dinner lady the money, walks off and sits down. Jeffery and Terence walk into the room, they sit next to The Overweighter.) Jeffery: The computer room is shut. Terence: We have to find something else to do. The Overweighter: Damn it, I wanted to go on http://www.geocities.com/[detail withheld]/hall.htm. Jeffery: I know we could tell each other joke’s and who ever is the worst has to eat that. (He points at the school food.) The Overweighter: No, I paid for that and I’m going to eat it. (He picks up a spoon, he dips it in and it does penetrate the sludge. He puts his hand in it, picks it up and its solid as a rock. He hits it against the table it doesn’t break.) The Overweighter: Okay worst person gets the food. Terence: Okay, Jeffery you go first. Jeffery: Okay, What’s big hairy and dangerous? Terence: A monkey with a machine gun! The Overweighter: Me!!!! Jeffery: It was a monkey with a machine gun but Overweighter was a good answer. It’s your turn Terence. Terence: Okay… Umm. How do you sink an Irish submarine? The Overweighter: knock on the door. Jeffery: Everyone knows that one. The Overweighter: My turn, okay, get ready to laugh. How Hi is a Chinaman? Terence: 5ft? Jeffery: smaller than 5ft? The Overweighter: No, How Hi is a Chinaman. Jeffery: I don’t know. Terence: How high is a Chinaman..? Yeah but how long is a bit of string? The Overweighter: Twice half of its length, but that’s no the answer for my one. Jeffery: Okay, tell us? The Overweighter: How Hi is his name and he’s a Chinaman. Terence: I don’t get it. Jeffery: I do, Terence you don’t get most things. Terence: Okay, tell another one. The Overweighter: What does bungee jumping and hookers have in common? Jeffery: I don’t know. Terence: Tell us. The Overweighter: They both cost a $100 and if the rubber breaks, you're screwed. (Jeffery, Terence start to laugh their heads off. Terence falls off his chair. The bell goes.) Jeffery: We have to go. Terence: We have to go to our next lesson, which is… Find out what lesson The Overweighter has next in part 3 of The Overweighter at school.
