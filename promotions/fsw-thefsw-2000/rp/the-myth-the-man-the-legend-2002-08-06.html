The Myth, The Man, The Legend.

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 6th *A world that is full of light is full of evil. A world of dark is full of good. The world we live in is light and dark. How can we be forced to face the good and the evil? How can I be expected to be good when my mind is evil? What is the key? What is the key to becoming pure?* ~Flash Back~ Lynn Lane: Punisher 2000 has PS Mad right where he wants him. Sam Collins: Punished DDT! 1...2...3! Sam Collins: Punisher 2000 is the new FSW Extreme Champion! ~End~ ~Flash Back 2~ Lynn Lane: Johnny Trip has P2K in a bad way. Sam Collins: Piledriver! 1...2...3! Sam Collins: Punisher 2000 has lost the FSW Intercontinentle Title! ~End~ Mike Frampton: Memories... (The camera fades in to Mike Frampton who sits in a corner reading an old FSW Magazine.) Mike Frampton: I was the man. I was the IC Champion, the Extreme Champion and I had a damn column in this magazine. (Mike throws the Magazine into the air. Slowly the pages which are worn and torn begin to tear and seperate from the spine. They slowly flop to the ground.) Mike Frampton: Fast Forward. (The pages of the magazine burst into flames and die out.) Mike Frampton: Now what am I? Who am I? (Bad Habit by The Offspring begins to play. Frampton gets up.) Mike Frampton: I'm a mad man. I am a psycho. I am Frampton, the P to the 2 to the K. I am a washed up nobody. I am a man living in the past. What year is it? 2001. I got a bad habit, and it's driving me insane. (The music cuts as Frampton walks towards a light. The scene cuts then fades in to Frampton on a run way. The camera pans around Frampton. Suddenly a Big Daveylicious cardboard cut out pops up.) Mike Frampton: Big Davetlicious. The man. The one we all associate with the FSW. The man who casts a heavy shadow. (Mike kicks the cardboard cut out in the head and knocks it off. An EOE cardboard cut out pops up.) Mike Frampton: The Epitome Of Evil, or as I like to call him, Big Daveylicious' personal bitch. A former OSW star who returns to his roots. A spit on you. (Frampton spits on the EOE cut out then kicks him in the body snapping it.) Mike Frampton: But then there is me. (The lights cut and a spotlight shoots down onto Frampton.) Mike Frampton: Who knows Frampton? Who knows what I have done? I am the reason why we all are here. (Frampton slams the ground.) Mike Frampton: Some call me dillusional, some call me crazy. Damn it I am neither. I am the man who kept the FSW alive. When Painkiller was running the Coalition I was busy keeping the shit together. I am the reason why the FSW survived. (Frampton snickers.) Mike Frampton: I am the FSW and it is time I am rewarded for the sleepless nights and bruised bodies. (Frampton sits down.) Mike Frampton: It's my time to be crowned an FSW God! It is time that Punisher 2000 returns. It is time that Mike Frampton cancels the fusion. It's time I become what you all envy. It's time for the return of the man. (The scene fades out.)
