Frustration Pt. 2

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 26 Flashing Back Wednsday night. The door swings open. Chris stomps his way out, aggrivated and pissed. He is still in his wrestling attire as he slams the door shut behind him. Tommy Gunn walks up to him an tries to calm him. 'Chris, calm man. They blind sided us. It isn't anything major.' Tommy pleads. Bagwell shoves Gunn aside and throws his bags at him. Chris screams violently, 'YOUR RECORD WASN'T SACRIFICED! IT WAS MINE! BECAUSE OF THAT GOD DAMNED DAVIDSON.' He continues marching away from Gunn. Gunn picks up Bagwell's bags from the ground and tries to chase after him. Bagwell continues in the direction of the parking lot to his Rent-a-car. Tommy tries to reason with him, getting him to think straight again, 'So what, Chris. You lost one mother f*cking match. ONE MATCH. And it was a screwjob. Who in the hell cares.' Bagwell stops dead in his tracks. Tommy, for a second, thinks he caught his attention. He comes up on the side of Bagwell. Tommy repeats himself, trying to imprint the idea into Chris' head, 'Its just one loss. A cheap loss, at that. It hasn't tarnished anything. It doesn't CHANGE anything. You've still got your entire career ahead of you.' Chris takes Tommy by the shirt and holds tightly. He gleams down into his eyes. Tommy gleams back and can see the fire burning within his soul. He can feel the anger through his blood. He can smell the hate seeping from Chris' pores. 'It's not about what the books say. Its about the mind, ' mummers Chris softly. He releases Gunn and shoves him back a bit. Tommy sees it as a lost cause. He allows Bagwell to continue forth to his rent-a-car. The car is not shown, but instead the face of Tommy. The vehicle door can still be heard slamming shut. Tommy softly mumbles to himself, '...and still no Rebecca...' Bagwell speeds away, off into the distance. Tommy stands there, motionless...speechless. Shift. Flashing Forward A loud, annoying clammering is heard nearby. As the camera gains focus, it unveils the clammering being caused by the railway and the train touching. 'Don't make me promises, baby you never did...know how to keep them well.' The soft melody, All or Nothing, by O-Town plays throughout the moving train. The camera takes specific notice to one young girl, who sits curled in a ball on her train seat. The tears drip down her face as the song plays through and through. 'Is this how it ends? Is this the drawing lines?' Rebecca whispers to herself, aspiring that nobody would hear her cry for help. However, an older woman can't help but overhear Rebecca speaking silently to herself. She stands from her seat and repositions to Rebecca's side. Confused, Rebecca speaks to the lady, 'uhm..hello...' The lady can sense the slight uncomfort in Rebecca's voice and see it within her eyes. 'I couldn't help but overhear you talking to yourself, sweetheart. Tell mama Lucy whats the matter.' 'Nothing. I'm fine,' replies Rebecca, wondering why someone would even care. But Lucy doesn't buy into her shameful answer. 'No, a young lady like yourself doesn't have tears falling down her face over nothing. Tell Lucy whats bothering you, babes. It won't get any better if you hold it in.' Rebecca is quite hesitant over spilling her worries. Yet, her emotions overcome her to the point in which she is unable to contain them. 'Well...it basically revolves around this guy, ' Rebecca mummers. Lucy nods her head in agreement, as she replis, 'Doesn't it always, sweetheart. Men can get the best of you sometimes. You can not let them walk all over you like that.' 'Thats not it,' argues Rebecca, 'Chris is different. He fights to spare me...to save me. And he fights to promise me the world.' Not seeing clearly her point, Lucy questions, 'Then whats the trouble?' Rebecca is hesitant in answering. Lucy looks on in slight concern. Rebecca explains, 'In failing, he fails himself...not me. I urge him to stop. He has become unstable from severe blows to the head earlier in the year. He was assaulted by a group of men and was put into the hospital for weeks, trying to recover.' Rebecca reminisces and brings about a flashback. She can still remember the days...how hard it was to get through. How she visited Chris the night of the attack and saw the condition he was in. How she never dreamed he'd make it out. Her voice can be heard softly in the background, 'I remember it like it was yesterday. I remember the blood loss. The doctors were taking pints of blood from him, trying to contain it. Trying to preserve it for a possible alternative use. There was seemingly no hope left for him...' Lucy interrupts, 'But I take it he made it...' 'Yeah. He did,' exclaims Rebecca. 'Barely. But he made it, nonetheless. However, he was forever changed. He went through a few more months of rehab until finally escaping.' 'He escaped?' Lucy asks with a bit of shock behind her voice. 'Can't be good!' 'He went back into the career he left. He went back to professional wrestling. He went back to the MONSTER that nearly CONSUMED HIS LIFE!' Rebecca grows very emotional. The tears stream down her face, more and more. Lucy takes her head and rests it on her enormous fat-lady breasts. 'Its ok, hun. It's ok,' says Lucy, in attempt to comfort Rebecca. 'You don't understand though,' Rebecca pleads. 'When he loses...he goes insane. He gets crazy. The chemical inbalance in his brain makes him act uncontrollable. It lights a fury inside him that can only be put out through another rattle of his skull.' Lucy nods her head, understanding exactly what Rebecca is trying to get at, 'In other words, he'd have to get brutally assaulted again to stop the fury?' 'Yes. Yes, exactly. But you see...it doesn't work that easily. He hasn't snapped yet. He's still in one piece...for now. The doctors say he cannot continue with his actions. They say it is just a matter of time before he breaks down emotionally and gets himself killed...' Rebecca then pauses. Tears stream down her face faster. For a moment, she can't speak because of the sadness that overcame her body. '...physically!' She breaks into a heavy sob. 'Mama Lucy senses that Chris is going into a something over his head. Something which could result the loss of his life. But if he loves you so much, why does he do it?' Rebecca tries to speak and manages to utter a few words from under her sobs. 'He does...it...to try and....puh...please me...to make...me...happy.' Rebecca gives up with speaking, and remains laying there...on Lucy's breasts. Lucy tries to comfort her, 'So I take it you're headed to stop him?' Rebecca doesn't reply. Lucy persists, 'Hunnie. Talk to Lucy, dear. It doesn't work if you don't!' Rebecca shyly raises her head and stares Lucy into the eyes, 'Thank you for all the help. Thanks a bunch. I needed a talk...' Rebecca hops off her seat and runs into the next train car. She continues to the back of the train, with the intention of leaping off. Lucy watches as she exited that car. Rebecca reaches the end car of the train. Instead of exiting and leaping for faith...she pauses. A radio conversation catches her attention. The radio host advertises a local wrestling event, 'And Five Star Wrestling seems to be stealing the show again this Wednsday night. With an event stacked like none other! Angelo Deville teams up with Tyler Davidson to battle it out with Jason O'Neil and RPGod Winner, Chris Bagwell!' Rebecca whipes the tears from her face as she steps out the back door of the train. She takes notice to the increasing speed of the train and has second thoughts of jumping. She throws her body against the train door. She speaks softly, to herself, 'The chance...the spotlight...' '...what has he gotten himself into? Deville...Davidson...O'Neil...' '...I have to make it all up. I have to make it all right again.' Shift. The picture reopens with Bagwell parked in his rent-a-car, in an empty parking lot. His head is down, on the wheel, as if he were hurt. Suddenly, he shows life. He raises his head from the wheel. Chris mumbles to himself, 'Defeating Brooze. Defeating Chambers. Defeating Deville and Davidson. None of it means anything. None of it is worth it...without one to accomplish it for.' 'But she's left me...' '...and my prayer...is to her.' He tosses a piece oif paper onto the dashboard and slams his head back on the wheel, going into a deep sleep. The camera gets a closeup on the paper, as it reads... juss wen i th0ught it waz tru baby i learned i cant trust u i tried to keep u happy n neva sad i tried givin u all the luv i eva had but u left me here, a fool in luv n hurt my heart wit more than a sh0ve it was known n it was sw0rn n n0w mah heart was finally t0rn fr0m mah heart n fr0m mah s0ul tha luv i had sh0ulda neva went 0ld but n0w itz d0ne n n0w itz g0ne neva again will i make an0tha b0nd ...away in the distance... ...l0st in hurt... ...mah s0ul will rise... ...but b0dy remain in dirt... g0ne Fade.
