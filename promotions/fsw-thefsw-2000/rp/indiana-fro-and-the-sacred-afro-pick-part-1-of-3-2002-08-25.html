Indiana Fro And The Sacred Afro Pick (Part 1 of 3)

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 25th, 2002 ~A dank, misty tunnel. Stalactites hang from the cavern roof, and a bat flies overhead. We suddenly see a figure step out of the shadows and into the light, supplied by a hole in the ceiling, allowing the sunlight to enter the cavern. The figure wears a large sombrero, covering his/her face. The figure has black arms, and is wearing white pants, and a black t-shirt. The figure steps up a incline of stone steps, which seem to lead to a shiny object at the top. The figure reaches the top, and looks at the shiny object. It’s a small, black man, holding up a peace sign. Minus that, the object has a large afro on his head, and is gilded in gold. The figure picks up the object. Suddenly a low rumbling begins, and the figure looks to the side. The figure’s sombrero flips up, revealing his face.~ ~This is Asa “Fro” Fountain.~ ~Whaaaaaat!?! What do you EXPECT Asa to wear over his fro? A normal Indiana Jones hat wouldn’t fit, so he had to resort to the sombrero. Deal with it. Anyway…~ Asa: Aww shit! ~Asa runs down the steps, and bolts through the cavern. A large boulder rolls out, and follows Asa down a corridor. Asa comes upon a large split in the corridor, and leaps it with his black-like leaping abilities. He continues to run, and a bat flies into his immense afro.~ Asa: TIME OUT JIVE TURKEY! ~Asa, the bat, the rumbling, and the boulder, all stop.~ Asa: Watch the hair disco daddy. ~The bat nods, and flies away. The rumbling starts up again, and the boulder begins to chase Asa once more. Asa continues along the corrider, and finds the opening. There, in front of the opening is his partner in crime, “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey.~ Jesse: You done got it Asa! Now get on out of that there tunnel! Asa: Right on brotha! ~Asa jumps, leaps, and flies out of the cavern as the rock slams against the wall. Asa stands up slowly, and raises the golden figure into the air.~ Asa: Success baby! Success! ~Suddenly, a rock, due to the impact of the boulder, falls to Asa’s hand from the sky, smashing the golden figure.~ Asa: DAMNIT! Jesse: That there rock done smashed the gold figure! Asa: Fuck. A well, let’s head on back, jive turkey. ~Asa and his bearded partner walk off over the hillside away from the cavern.~ ~Okay, next scene. Asa and Jesse have ANOTHER partner to help them do their dirty work, but this partner is no ORDINARY partner. You’ll find out momentarily. The two walk through a small town in Niger, Africa. Little naked black people run around, carrying water, wood, or just playing. They all stare at Jesse’s whiteness as the two enter a small hut. A man sits toward the far end of the wall, doing something which we will never know.~ Asa: What’s shakin’ pops? ~The man turns around to face Asa, and his own sombrero flips up to reveal….~ ~Flaymin fuckin’ Aymin.~ Aymin: Asa! Welcome back son! Asa: Oh yeah, Jesse, this be my d… Aymin: WHO’S YOUR DADDY!!! WHO’S YOUR DADDY!!!!! Asa: *sigh* You’re my daddy. Anyway, this is my dad disco daddy. Jesse: Dad? What you be talkin’ ‘bout? ~Now, Jesse is wondering this due to the fact that Asa is very black, and Aymin is very white. VERY white, but don’t worry about that, onto the story.~ Aymin: Long story Jesse. Anyway, you get the statue? Asa: Well oysta pops, I GOT the statue, but it got all smashed up. Aymin: *snapping his fingers* Damnit. Ah well. Anyways, I have a NEW mission for the three of us. ~Aymin brings the two over to a map, showing a topographical view of The Congo.~ Aymin: Alright boys, this is the holy grail of those who have afros!!! I have finally found out that the Sacred Afro Pick lies in a large ravine in the Congo. Inside the ravine are shit loads of secret hallways, won’t be easy to find the bastard. ~Aymin rolls up the map and puts it in a backpack.~ Aymin: We’re gonna leave in the morning. Get some sleep boys, you’re gonna need it. I however will get my rest from Shantiqua here. ~Aymin pulls a good looking black woman from the back of the hut.~ Aymin: Good night boys!!! ~Aymin kicks the two out of the hut, and closes the shades.~ THE NEXT DAY ~The scene now shoots to the morning after at a small African airport. A small bi-plane is up and running, and Asa, Jesse, and Aymin are all carrying luggage onto the plane.~ Aymin: We should be there by 4 o’clock tonight! Asa: You sure you wanna go with this disco daddy? Aymin: Sure I’m sure! The sacred afro pick gives it’s user the most perfect hair possible! And we gotta make sure we got the most happenin’ hair! Jesse: What about me??? Aymin: You’ll have the most happenin’ beard my man! ~The three board the plane. The plane revs up, flies down the runway, and flies into the air away from the airport.~ ~After a few hours of flying, we now see the three tightly crammed in the small plane.~ Asa: How much longer, jive turkey? Aymin: Beats me, I didn’t bring a watch. Jesse: I gotta pee. ~Aymin hands Jesse a soda bottle.~ Aymin: Here, just make sure you keep that thing away from me. ~Jesse walks to the corner of the plane to do his business. Suddenly, a record comes flying through the side of the plane! That’s right, I said a “record”. As in a large, black, musical disc. Continuing…The record slices through the side of the plane, and slices through the other side.~ Aymin: HOLY SHIT!!! ~Another one flies through, just barely missing Asa’s leg.~ Asa: What’s up with this funky shit? ~Another one flies through, and slices the bottle, the very same bottle that Jesse happens to be peeing in at the moment, in two. Jesse looks down to see his hands holding half of a coca cola bottle, then faints.~ Asa: Lemme see this shit. ~Asa picks up one of the records. He inspects it, then frowns.~ Asa: D-Kay Calyx… ~Another record flies through the side of the plane. ~Okay, now the scene goes into slow motion. The record flies through the air slowly, spinning through the air. Aymin’s head turns to see the record flying right at him. His face turns to fear, and he slowly moves his head out of the way. However, the record just barely slices off one, I repeat, ONE, of Aymin’s afro hairs off.~ ~Okay, back to the normal. Aymin’s hair falls to the ground, and he clutches at his heart.~ Aymin: GGAAAAHHHH!!!!1 ~He falls to the floor, apparently going into convulsions. Asa rushes over to his fallen father.~ Asa: Dad! Are you alright oysta pops? Aymin: *Breathing heavily* I’m…I’m okay. Hand me my hair, please…. ~Asa picks up the fallen afro hair, and hands it to his father. Aymin takes the hair in a shaking hand, and places it against his skull. A white flash takes up the whole screen, and the hair is restored. Aymin rises in health.~ Aymin: Ah, it’s all good now. ~Suddenly a loud “Boom” can be heard, as apparently one of the records has hit a plane engine! The plane begins to dive.~ Asa: Holy shit!!! We gonna die jive turkey! We gonna die!!! Asa and Aymin: AAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ~Jesse remains silent, due to the fact that he is still unconscious because of the record nearly slicing off his pride.~ ~SHAZOOM! New scene. The scene is now on a rocky cliff ledge, overlooking the African jungle below. All that can bee seen are the two round ends of a pair of binoculars, being held by hands covered by gauntlets.~ Man: Heh heh heh… ~The hands bring down the binoculars, to reveal a man with scraggly brown hair, and a skull mask. This, is Hessian. He looks up into the sky to see the plane falling through the air.~ Hessian: All is going according to plan. Voice: Ya damn right diz shiznit is goin’ down!!!!! ~Hessian looks down to his right, to reveal the short, purple skinned Minion The Imp, flipping off the falling plane.~ Hessian: Save your strength Minion, there will be time for the Imp Beef to be unleashed. Minion: Grrrr… Hessian: Anyway, well done D-Kay. Your skills have served well. ~The screen pans over to see the DJ, D-Kay Calyx, spinning a record on his finger.~ D-Kay: Ain’t nobody who can wield the records like me! Hessian: Let us be off, there is much more to do. ~The three turn, and enter the jungle.~ WHAT WILL HAPPEN TO OUR FALLING FRIENDS???? WILL AYMIN BE SUBJECT TO ANY MORE LOSS OF PRECIOUS AFRO HAIR??? WILL JESSE EVER AWAKEN FROM HIS CLOSE ENCOUNTER TO LOSING HIS PENIS??? AND WILL MINION EVER GET TO DEMONSTRATE THE WEILDING OF HIS IMP BEEF???? STAY TUNED!!!!!!!!
