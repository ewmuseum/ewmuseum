Baptized And Cleansed

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 7th Through the dark I see. As the wool is brought down over my eyes, a hole gives me sight. A sight that I behold. A sight that gives me strength. Then as I take in all I can the hole is covered with smoke. A smoke that is thicker than oil, a smoke with power. This smoke I see could bring to negative ions together. This smoke is a power that made me fall to my knees. This smoke made me beg for mercy and forgiveness. This smoke is something I know. I have felt this. It chills me to the bone. I know this smoke. But how? As I was on my knees I was circled by this smoke as if it was observing me. Taking in all I presented. I heard a whisper in one ear then quickly the other. I turned and the smoke shot through me and began to take shape. A mirror was brought before me. I looked into the mirror. Most folks see themselves. But I saw something else. I saw my soul. I saw my mortality. I saw my black, black, heart. But most importantly, I saw my humanity. The mirror had something I never knew. It had a special gift. It was able to show me what I had never seen before. Suddenly the mirror vanished into a thick smoke once again. As fast as it came it was gone. My mind was busy taking in what I saw. I saw what was wrong. I had the eye of God. I was able to see it all. Not only did I see my soul, mortality, heart and humanity. I also saw the world through eyes nobody else has ever. I saw the eyes through a child. A special child. A child with innocense trapped in a world of hate, pain and torture. I saw what you could only believe in. I saw what people pray to. I saw belief. I saw the wind. What else could I ask for? Suddenly the smoke was brushed away by a wind storm. How could there be a wind storm? I was in a room. The wind that came was created by my old desires. The wind was a cleansy. The wind of my desire washed away the smoke of my evil. I was baptized in the wind and smoke of my evil. My world was returning to the world of a child. I was innocent again. Completion can come in many forms. In my case I was blessed. My completion occured in a way I can never explain. My completion has drawn me closer to my fellow man. My innocence restored I felt new things. The world is a beautiful. My entire life is a thing of grace. I am now an innocent being cast out into a world of hate, pain and torture. I saw the world through... I saw the world through... I SAW THE WORLD THROUGH MY EYES! My eyes that I had to earn. I saw the future through my eyes. We are all innocent. All we need to do is tap into it. I Mike Frampton am innocent and reborn. I have a second chance to be famous and loved. I have my chance at becoming a legend. I have a new chance at becoming number one. I leave you with these words; Never think it's too late, because once you do, its to late. Goodnight, Mike Frampton
