Chapter One -- Flashback

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26 A beautiful, young jezzabell dressed in a sparkling white gown fralantly gallops about throughout the splendid night. Flashing lights glimmer and shine in sequence, giving off an illustrious glow all across the city streets. The lights so bright, that they can be seen from miles away, encompassing the horizon. The young, flapper women sips another glass of alcohol further dwindling her sense of soberness. 'You're drunk, aren't you?' says the rather bulky gentlemen, dressed and suited in black with a silvery-gray vest upon a white faded and striped shirt. He approaches the beautiful young woman, with a slight look of disappointment on his face. 'Come, Rebecca, we must get you home,' he pleads. 'Home?' whines Rebecca. 'Oh I do not wish to go home just yet. The night is young and I'm so beautiful.' 'Not to mention, you're drunk!' replies the man, sharply. 'And you must get home, immediately.' The six plus foot man stares off into the crowd, looking for his friend. Finally, he catches a glimpse of that friend and tries to waive him over. 'Come over here, Thomas.' Thomas doesn't hear the request at first, thus the man yells a bit louder. 'THOMAS!' After getting his attention, the man waives Thomas over once more. 'Ahh, sorry Chris. I didn't hear what you said the first time.' 'No worries, Tommy,' replies Chris. 'I just really need you to give me a hand with Rebecca. She's being persistant.' 'In what way,' questions Tommy. 'She's arguing with me that she's perfectly fine.' Rebecca cuts in, 'But I am fine, darling. You're imagining everything.' 'She's drunk and must be sent home,' responds Chris. Tommy, knowing exactly what was going through his mind, took charge of the situation and waived over the limosuine. The driver pulled the limo close to the curb and stepped out of the driver's side door to assist Rebecca into the vehicle. 'Would you look at that, Rebecca. The limo driver agrees with us!' Chris said with a slight chuckle of laughter. 'I'll be home within a few hours, dear. Just get to sleep.' 'Chris! This is not fair! I want to enjoy the night, as well! THE NIGHT IS STILL YOUNG!' Rebecca galloped along fralantly into the limo. Although she was verbally arguementative, she seemed to coincide rather easily. Chris seemed rather content that she complied without too much more of a hassle. 'That was weird,' exclaimed Chris. 'She didn't really put up much of a fight when the limo pulled up.' Chris watched as the driver shut Rebecca's door and made his way back to the driver's side of the vehicle. He entered and slowly pulled away. Tommy replied, 'Ah well maybe she's a little more wasted than normal.' Tommy began to glance around at the groups of people still entering to the party hall and the small packs of those leaving. 'But anyway, we have to get a move on with this night. Time is of the essence.' Chris paused for a few seconds, just cycling through thoughts in his mind. Like always, its quite hard to pin point just exactly what went through the mind of the T0rN soul, but it had to be something major. He suddenly steps forward and continues walking, pushing right past Tommy. A bit worried of the situation, Tommy reaches out and grabs Chris' arm. 'Chris. Whoa. Whats wrong?' Tommy asked, questionatively. Chris had a difficult time piecing together his words, 'Nothing. I..uhh...I don't even know what just went through my head.' Yet Tommy, although confused as hell, understood in a strange fashion. He knew that something sat on the mind of his good friend, Chris Bagwell. Something more than what can be seen at the surface. Cut Forward . . . Into The Present . . . The Time is Now . . . [narrator: tommy, close friend to Chris Bagwell ] I don't know why I tried so hard to keep him in line. Through the start of our friendship, he was a bastard to me. He'd push me around and shove me to the side, always making me feel like a worthless peon. But in that same manner, he came to teach me things that I could never learn anywhere else. He gave me a certain experience that I am forever grateful for. All in all, Chris was an awesome guy who probably knew what he was doing all along. I remember that night as if it were yesterday. We had just gotten Rebecca to comply and head home. Of course, we went through the whole saga millions of times, but this time was a little bit different. This time Rebecca went along a lot smoother. A little too smooth for the complex mind of Chris. Chris didn't look at things at the surface then and he still doesn't now. He always has the habit of peeling the surface away and digging deep into the heart of the problem. And thats exactly what Chris did that seemingly perfect night. He tore into the surface of the situation and found a problem. But thats Chris for you...always looking for a problem! I showed a lot of concern that night, wondering just why he was getting so paranoid over nothing. But it wasn't long until I figured it all out. He had plenty of reason to be paranoid, for Rebecca's limo didn't make it back to the apartment! Cut Back . . . Into The Past . . . Chris sat in the phone booth, listening to the endless ringing over and over and over again. Concerned. Worried. Yet also slightly pissed. He wasn't sure exactly what he was pissed at. But then again, he never really cared to even have a reason. After fifteen minutes of searching, Tommy finally found where Chris had went. 'Chris, what the hell is wrong?' asked Tommy. Chris hesitated and then slammed the reciever down onto the hook. The thirty five cents could be heard filtering into the change dish as Chris replied, 'that was not Rebecca's limo driver.' A sudden shock filled Tommy's face. But it was soon dissolved by an idea. 'How can you be so sure, Chris? Maybe the guy was sick, even!?!' Chris, nodding his head, replied sharply, 'It was not even an official limo, Tommy. I remember glancing at the plates. Someone drugged her to make her want to go home early.' Tommy rolled his eyes in frustration. 'Chris is just blowing this out of proportion,' he though to himself. 'Oh Chris, come on. Do you really think thats the case?' 'Absolutely,' replied Chris. 'I remember smelling her breath when we got close. There was no scent of any liquor. Whatever she drank was clear with a bit of a fog and fizz. Somebody drugged her, Tommy.' Cut Forward . . . Into The Present . . . The Time Again is Now . . . [narrator: tommy, close friend to Chris Bagwell ] Do you think he might have jumped to conclusions to fast? Perhaps. But Chris always has his reasoning. He may not be one to always come right out and explain it. But at least he knows that its there. His reasoning, that is. Futhermore, that night proved to be something spectacular. Something that wasn't all that bad in the end. A little over-exaggeration if you ask me. But ah, what the hell! What can you do!?! Turns out, we were drawn to a little house only a few blocks from the party when the phone booth phone began ringing. When we get there, Rebecca was fast asleep in a room designed exactly like her apartment room that week. Obviously, there was a bit of a catch, though. Otherwise it wouldn't have been as much fun, am I right? Cut Back . . . Into The Past . . . Chris races over to Rebecca, who was laying comfortably in the bed. He gently rubs his hand through her hair and across her forhead, checking to see that she was alright. 'Well there he is! The man of the hour.' The voice lurks from behind the shadows. As Chris hears each word being muttered, he stands from the bedside and a sudden anger floods his other emotions. 'What took you so long, Chris?' The mysterious man takes a step into the light, only revealing part of his body. But thats more than enough for Chris. Bagwell lounges at the man, tackling him to the ground. As Chris raises his hand high in the air, he takes a good look at the perpetrator's face. In dissappointment, he utters the words 'you bastard!' 'Whoa! Chris, who is he?' asks Tommy. Chris stands back onto his feet, taking a few steps away. 'The sonuvabitch is Dukey Booty.' Dukey climbs back up to his feet and fixes his shirt up a bit. 'Jesus Christ, Chris. I didn't think you'd be THAT aggresive!' exclaimed Dukey, shocked at Chris' response. 'But the reason for me getting you here is to let you know about Five Star. . . . .' Cut Forward . . . Into The Present . . . The Time Again is Now . . . [narrator: tommy, close friend to Chris Bagwell ] And that pretty much summed up the day. Strange night, if you ask me. It made no sense! But it ended with Dukey addressing Five Star Wrestling and informing Chris that he'd made the cut. As I said before, a small thing blown up into something so big. But that was then. This is now. A slightly different challenge now sits in the way of my dear friend, Chris Bagwell. A chance to once again step into the light. But the same chance holds consequences in which lurk very close. The risk that Chris ruins himself once again. The risk that his entire career could come to a shattering conclusion. But hell, if I know Chris, he won't give a shit. We'll just see how he copes against the FSW legend Jack Brooze. I somehow have the feeling that we'll all let it be known and let it be sworn. And Mr. Brooze will...be...torn!!
