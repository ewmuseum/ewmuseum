Making the Scarecrow - Part III

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/2 The rains fall in Brookpark, Ohio. Strange that it had been such an unusual year for rain. It seemed like there would be none this summer. But recently, the rains have fallen a lot more. In the veil of darkness, Jason O’Neil stumbles around, carrying a flashlight, an umbrella and flowers. The winds pick up as lightning crashes. Jason looks to the heavens, and tosses down his umbrella, to let the raid fall on his face. He smiles as it falls on him. Walking slowly looking at the ground he shines the flash light onto the path. He veers off the path to the left, and kneels about 15 feet in. Slowly, he puts his face to the ground and kisses it, first to the left, then to the right. He smiles down as the rains fall. “Hello, Michelle. Hello, Samantha. It’s Jason. I haven’t been here in a while. I’m sorry about that. I’ve been out in the world, finally. I knew it was time to get on with my life, and so…I did.” Jason lays two bouquets of flowers down, one to the left, the other to the right. “I think about you both all the time. Not an hour goes by that either of you two don’t enter my mind. I miss you both…” His voice trails off as he fights back tears. Lightning strikes in the distance, illuminating the sky, as seconds later, the thunder echo runs through the valley. “…very very much. God, it seems like it was only yesterday, Sam I held you in my arms and read you Goodnight Moon. I still have your bike in the garage. I stood and stared at it today for about two hours, crying that I’d never get the chance to see you ride it. And your room is just how you left it. The housekeeper wanted to clean it, but I wouldn’t let her.” Slowly, a tear rolls from his eye. He puts his head down, as if he’s been defeated. Jason wipes his eye, and turns to the right. “And I finally paid the house off Michelle. Jenn called yesterday. She had no idea anything happened. I forgot to call her I guess. She was very upset. She’s coming in to town next week on business. We’re going to do lunch. I’ll be sure to give her your best.” Jason sits silently, looking at the ground. He touches the words Michelle Grey-O’Neil with shaky hands. A tear falls onto the grave marker. His head falls slowly, as his face scrunches up. Rain falls on his naked scalp as tears flow from his eyes. “God, it was my fault. I should have watched Michelle. I should have seen that car coming. I didn’t, and now you’re gone. Oh god…” Jason begins to cry harder. His tears drop onto the tombstone, as his forehead touches the cool stone. His body tenses up as he cries harder, pounding the ground. Suddenly, the rains stop around him. A light starts to glow. Jason lifts his eyes skyward to see two sunbeams break through the clouds, and shine upon the granite stones on the ground. “Jason, we’ll always be with you. We love you and miss you too.” “Michelle?!” Jason exclaims between the tears. A lighted figure appears in front of him almost angelic. He stares at it, as it seems to look back, hair blowing back in the wind as he stares at the figure materializing in the form of his former wife. He stands up and rushes over to her, feeling her skind touch his for the first time in three years. She throws her arms around him and kisses him. He returns the kiss, never wanting to let go again. “That’s right, love. It’s me. I love you Jason. Samantha and I miss you very much. Jason…” “Yes?” “I need you here with me. Please, join us. We need you.” “But…how?” “Look over to your left Jason…look on the ground.” Jason looks frantically, and sees a silver blade shimmer in the light. He stares at it, then back at his wife’s figure. “Go ahead. Pick it up, sweetheart. That’s it. Place the blade at your neck.” Jason does what he is told, and closes his eyes, at long last feeling almost completely pain free. This is not the Scarecrow. This is Jason O’Neil, desperate father, and husband. “Michelle…” “Don’t be scared Jason…I need you here. Please Jason! I miss you and love you.” “Ok Michelle. Ok. Ok. Ok. I love you Michelle. Here I come. I’m coming to join you!” Jason takes the blade and pushes it into his neck. Blood shoots out from the wound as he looks at the figure in front of him. The sweet angelic face of his wife contorts violently. Jason chokes as flames begin to shoot from the ground. Jason’s head starts to fall to the left as he watches the figure materialize into the face of the Devil. Angelo Deville. The Devil…Angelo. Deville. Devil. Angel. Angel… Devil… Angelo Deville Jason blinks one final time as Angelo laughs out loud. He points and laughs as demons fly up from the ground, surrounding Jason with their evil laughter. The rain falls once more and starts to burn his skin as he falls. He takes his last breath…as his head hits the ground. The last image, burnt into his mind forever, is that of Angelo Deville, standing over him, his hands raised in triumph. Screams pierce the silence. Jason O’Neil sits up in bed, clutching his throat. He screams out and violently shakes. Suddenly, he looks around, at the unfamiliar hotel room, and realizes he’s not in any trouble. His neck is fine. He’s breathing. Most importantly, Angelo’s not here tormenting him any longer. He’s safe…secure… And alone. Tears fall from as he puts his head in his hands and weeps.
