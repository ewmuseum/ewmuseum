Care For a Burger?

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-29-02 The scene opens up in the parking lot of a small McDonald’s outside of Charlotte, North Carolina. It’s a reasonably warm day, but the wind is slightly chilly as an old Ford truck, rusted on the sides and slightly filled with a silvery looking bondage material that is keeping it held together. The old rusted exhaust blasts off one fiery shot as it comes to a park in a parking space and it is then shortly after covered in black smoke from under the hood and out the back. When the sound of a screeching door is heard and coughs coming from the cloud of smoke are heard as well, until the goofy face of Jesse Ramey emerges from the smoke. Jesse: Woo wee! That there sure was a big sha’ bang! Wernt it now there Elmer? Elmer slowly emerges from the side of the truck, rolling his eyes and coughing his little lungs out. Elmer: (In little screechy voice) Be not make Elmer suck in that Hillbilly gases, or else! Jesse: (Laughing) Elmer my little friend, you be funnier than cousin Merle when he be drinking the shine. The two slowly begin to make their way across the parking lot as everyone looks over at them slowly. A small child looks at Elmer and begins pointing. Child: Look mommy! A puppy! Elmer: I’ll show you a puppy you little… (Cut off by Jesse) Jesse: Now, now there Elmer, you be good to that there kid, he done nothing wrong to you. Elmer stands up on his two hind legs and flips off the little kid as he turns and runs crying to his mom. Jesse: (Shaking his head) I done know what I’m going to do with you Elmer. Elmer snickers as he walks in through the McDonald’s door that Jesse holds open. The two slowly make their way up to the counter and come into contact with the worker. Worker: Welcome to McDonald’s, may I take your order? Jesse: I done want me a number three with extra opossum. Worker: Sir, I’m sorry, but we don’t serve opossum here. Jesse: You don’t serve opossum?! Well, how about you run back there and fetch us some Squirrel burgers. The worker now begins to look at Jesse even more weirdly until Elmer hops up onto the counter. Elmer: Listen here babs, Elmer and the hillbilly want some grub. How about you just hook us up with some Super Sized Big Mac’s and we’ll be on our way? Worker: (She reaches down and grabs her mic) Two Super Sized Big Mac combos to go. Jesse: (Placing a twenty down on the counter) Keep the change. The worker brings back the brown paper bag and the two begin to make their way out of the McDonald’s. Jesse: I’s just can’t believe they don’t serve opossum or squirrel. The scene slowly fades to black.
