I ain't huntin wabbit. (Battery series part 2)

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-06-02 (Welcome back ladies and gents to your most looked forward to read of the day. That’s right. This is when you find out everything that’s been going on in John’s life lately. And lately, there’s been a lot going on. With last week having made his debut in FSW and exacting some revenge on an old foe, he has made a new, and apparently dangerous one. One, Magnum Wolf. A man of infamous notoriety in Primetime Central at one point, as LCW-Mercury was the dueling partner of CCW. Battling it out for the number one spot in PTC at that point, were none other than our very own John Jupiter, and this new threat, Magnum Wolf. Having each held the spot once, it was a battle that neither would take lightly. They were at first scheduled to participate in an interfed battle that pitted men from both CCW and LCW-Mercury against each other in a battle of the titans. This match was cancelled, due to John restricting schedule, which as a result caused him to take an unexpected leave of absence from wrestling. As we all know, when he returned to CCW, the foundations were already shaken, and there was nothing he could do. CCW was crumbling from the bottom up. Then, from nowhere, they had closed their doors once and for all. This is when John had to take up arms and fight for a chance to get into another federation to continue with his career. The opportunity of a lifetime arrived, and John was only too eager to compete. This is how he came to be in FSW. You all know what has happened with him since then. So I will leave that story for another day. Today, we are here to see what has happened to John in the past week, as his last appearance was cut short due to a battery power failure in his digital video camera.) (The scene opens up to a now familiar one, as it is John’s Condo in Toronto. We can see from the little battery image in the top right corner that the battery is now fully recharged, but we can also tell from the timestamp in the bottom right that it was not shot later in the day. This is several days later. Something must have kept John from his business, but only he can tell that. To make up for his disappearance, he has decided to jazz up the video a bit by adding a soundtrack. The opening track … I am the Man by The Philosopher Kings. He and Gavin sit on the couch opposite the camera, letting the song play out … talking amongst themselves.) I am the man, that's what I am I'm a straight shooter, with a master plan I am the man, that's why I'm here I am the man, I am the man I am the last real cowboy I roped the soul of the world can I have your lights can I have your mic's I mean to be heard I am a radio soldier taking aim at the world mothers and fathers bring your daughters bring your girls, cause I am the man, that's what I am I'm a straight shooter, with a master plan I am the man, that's why I'm here I am the man, I am the man truth and honesty that's my game I'm gonna make it simple I'm gonna make it plain you're gonna know my face you're gonna know my name my favourite colour my favourite number you're gonna know my pain it's all in the plan I am the man, that's what I am I'm a straight shooter, with a master plan I am the man, that's why I'm here I am the man, I am the man (The song fades out as John and Gavin finish their talking. Looking up at the camera, John smiles and nods his head. Gavin sits sullenly, with his arms crossed. Obviously having been told to keep his mouth under wraps unless he is called upon.) Jupiter: Hello again. It has been a few days since I’ve been able to sit down and finish this off. For a few reasons really, it would have only been one or two days, but I decided that if I was going to delay myself, I’d have to make up for it somehow. So I was browsing through my cd collection yesterday in order to find some music that I enjoyed. And in order to find some music that conveyed my purpose here in FSW. Gavin: Why are you there again? Jupiter: What did I tell you? Gavin: (sigh) Fine. I won’t talk. Jupiter: Good, because this time, I mean to get my point across. Gavin: I don’t see why I can’t talk this time though. I mean, you have enough battery to last at least 3 hours. Jupiter: Three hours of which I’m sure that they don’t want to waste watching you and I argue about nonsensical things. Gavin: Can I at least have 5 minutes? Jupiter: No. Gavin: Please?!?! Jupiter: NO! Gavin: Not even at the end? Jupiter: Two minutes. At the end. No more, no less. Gavin: Sweet. Ok, I’ll shut up now. Jupiter: Good. I am here in FSW for a couple reasons. I am no flash in the pan, I am no upstart young guy looking to make a name for himself. I have made my mark in wrestling, and I plan to make it loud and clear that John Jupiter is here, and that he is here to stay. No matter who you bring, no matter what you bring. I mean to be heard, and I intend to be listened to. My first message was heard loud and clear, although the only person to respond, was not the one the message was intended for. I was speaking to Kid Wonder when I was out there last week at Glory 5. He knew why I was there. It’s not because I hate him, because I do respect him. I have no tolerance for what he did to not only myself, but many others backstage. We’re the good guys. We stick together. To see myself, and others treated the way we were by Kid Wonder in those days … sickened me to the core, and I have to pay him back for what he did. Make him realize that what goes around comes around. Being just the kid he is, he should remember Kindergarten when they told you to do unto others as you would have them do unto you. He missed that point. Gavin: But you made it clear for him last week. Jupiter: Thankyou Gav. That’ll be enough. Gavin: Anytime. (Smug grin) Jupiter: However, in the doing of that, I managed to get on the wrong side of Magnum Wolf. An old foe of mine from back in the days of Primetime Central when LCW-Mercury was around. I’ve never had a problem with the guy until he took a ring bell to my head. I feel for him, I really do, because he lost a match because of my grudge against Kid Wonder. So I freely accept the match that was given to me with him, mainly to accost for my mistake and more importantly, because we never got to prove the victor back in PTC. We were scheduled to fight, and then it was cancelled as I had to leave on personal business. It had and still has nothing to do with any unwillingness to face Wolf in the ring, for if it is for that reason, I would not be facing him now. I am prepared to fight him, I always have been, and I always will be. I do not doubt his talents, yet I do doubt his motives. Based on an ultimately inconclusive opinion of me from CCW, Wolf has taken it upon himself to consider me scum. Something I may have been in the long past, but am no longer. I leave that part of the family name up to my brother. Gavin: Amen to that. Jupiter: He looks upon this match as a way to beat my ass down. I look at it as a chance to finally stand toe to toe with a man I wasn’t able to compete with months ago, and am now given the second chance. I look forward to it, but if he thinks I will let it degenerate to anything but a wrestling match … he is sorely mistaken. But for now, I must bid you adieu as I must leave to prepare for my flight. Gavin wanted to say a few words, so here he is. Please don’t let his views bear any opinion on me. Gavin: Why do you say something like that? We’re brothers bro. We’re almost the same person. Except I’m better looking, and get way more women. Anyway, I didn’t want to talk about that. I wanted to talk about my bro. Even though he can’t pimp it up near as good as me, and he hasn’t yet achieved god-like status as I have, he is still one of the best there is out there, and Magnum Wolf ain’t got nothing on him. My bro has been working with me all his life, we trained together, we fought together and I can tell you right now … that if there is any man out there who can nearly parallel my ability in the ring … it’s this man right here. (John is getting a little fidgety due to all the praise he is being given by his brother, and carefully watches the clock to make sure he gets only his two minutes. When they are up, he interrupts his brother and starts up the closing music. The song is Going all the Way by the Matthew Good Band. The camera clicks off as John shuts it down just after the song finishes.)
