Lurking Death Part 2

Date: 8/5/2002 ~The scene again reopens with a view of the Davidson Estate in Connecticut. The leafless trees surrounding the house are seen blowing violently in the wind as a major thunderstorm seems to be coming through the area. Bolts of lightning and the loud blasts of thunder come more and more frequently as you are taken back into the familiar Davidson Estate Library. The old man is already seated at the desk and at first glance he seems to be sound asleep at the desk. His head is hanging forward looking over the desk and there is little movement coming from him aside from the rise and falling of his chest as he breathes. Suddenly a loud blast of thunder from outside wakens him and he lifts his head to look around the room. He seems a bit puzzled as to where he is but his senses come back to him as he finally realizes where he is seated. He picks up the pack of cigarettes at his side on the desk and pulls one out and lights it up as he puts it to his mouth. He reaches down under the desk into one of the drawers and pulls out the book as he places it back on top of the desk in front of him. He places the cigarette back down on the ashtray and rubs his eyes trying to rid himself of the last bits of sleep. He opens the book back to his previous entry and reads it all over quickly to get his bearings before he reaches out for the cigarette again and takes in another puff of smoke. He blows the smoke slowly out and grabs hold of the pen. He looks down at the previous entry again and flips over to the next page and begins the writing picking up right where he had left off the night before.~ ~The Davidson Chronicles Chapter Ten: Lurking Death Part 2 8/5/2052 by Tyler Davidson~ Part 2 Things never did get much better for me on that day. Heather as she said she would, walked off and disappeared down the hallway the same way Jess had done so leaving me standing out on the balcony alone and getting quite cold. I looked back across the street at the building opposite us and for some reason I felt as though I was being watched after all. I shook the thoughts away and looked down onto the streets far below me. It was getting to be somewhat late in the night, nearly midnight now yet far below me there were a great many signs of life. Granted most of the life was some of the usual lesser dregs of society going about their usual nocturnal and more often than not illegal activities upon the city. New York City was always a place that I had loved to come when I was younger and had the time to do so. Living in Connecticut the city really wasn’t far away at all and more often than not I would have little trouble making it out there for a night in the city. This area of Manhattan not far from Times Square wasn’t anything new to me as I had spent quite a bit of time here in the past. Back when he was still wrestling S. and I would often remain in New York during our time off from tag teaming in various independent promotions. That wasn’t the case on this trip though, nor would it be again as far as I knew at the time. It was obviously going to be quite a long night so perhaps a walk out among the streets of New York City would be good for me. Just need to remember to take myself a jacket along with me. I quickly threw on my usual black leather jacket and made my way out of the door heading directly towards the elevator shaft to take me down to the ground level. I walked out of the large building where S. has taken out a loft apartment for the past few years and looked up and down the streets. The first thing that caught my eye was a red grand prix flying by on the nearly empty streets in front of me. I got my bearings as to which way I was supposed to head and turned right beginning a long walk down the street. I saw quite a few people still out on the streets as I walked but none of them seemed to even notice me. If they did they would pay it no attention and continue on through their own business. The walk had turned into one that was supposed to last a few minutes into nearly two hours as I passed through the streets deep in thought. A light drizzle had begun to fall upon the city while I was walking and the streets were obviously quite a bit wetter than previously. I paid the rain little attention though as I walked through the city back to the apartment. There was hardly a person for me to see on the whole walk as I finally came to a stop right outside the apartment building. I looked up into the sky again and back over at the building across from me again. Not to far up was the window which I had thought I saw something in earlier. I looked now and saw nothing of any notice there and brushed off the earlier notions as my own imagination. I turned into the building and walked the hallway to the elevator shaft before riding it up to the loft. I walked into the dark apartment and fumbled for a light in the dark of the room before finally finding one and setting a little bit of light throughout the apartment. I took the jacket off and placed it on a rack next to the door before lying down on the couch which was likely to be my bed for the night anyway. I eventually dozed off into a deep sleep right there on the couch until the early hours of the morning. Jess was already up and about the apartment and had inadvertently woken me up with some of her working. It was about time for me to get up anyway as my flight to California would be leaving in about three hours or so anyway. I already had one promotional appearance scheduled in Los Angeles which would come later in the day. I got up off the sofa and poured myself a small cup of coffee before walking out onto the balcony again where Jess was. ”Sorry if I woke you,” she said while staring down across the street below us. ”Not a problem,” I replied. “I needed to get up anyway. Going to have this cup of coffee and then get out of here.” ”Aren’t you going to wait for Heather to get up?” she asked. ”I think I would rather be out of here before she does actually,” I responded. “Save us all a little bit of trouble.” ”I heard you two fighting last night,” Jess commented as she turned her gaze from the street below back up to me. “Life on the road getting hard for her it seems.” ”Yeah I noticed,” I said while taking a drink of the coffee to wake me up. “Not much that I could really do about that though.” ”She’ll come to grips with it soon enough,” Jess said. ”Yeah we’ll see,” was my less than confident reply. “Anyway I’m going to take a quick shower and then head on out.” ”Alright,” Jess said. “I’ll try to keep her occupied if she wakes up while you’re in there.” ”Thanks,” I said while walking away placing the unfinished coffee down on the kitchen table. I turned to go away when out of the corner of my eye I noticed something in the building across from us that wasn’t there yesterday. What looked to be a telescope was now sitting in the window there fully visible in the bright light of the morning. I shrugged it off as someone’s star-watching tool and walked down the hallway and into the bathroom to take the quick shower I obviously needed before making my plans to head on out. I quickly finished it up and got myself dressed again before walking back out into the living room where my bags were already. Jess had apparently stepped out so I took my jacket off the rack and picking up my bag quietly slipped out of the apartment to make my way to California. ~The scene changes back to the library room where the old man is seated. He places the pen back into the ink jar and takes another cigarette out of the pack and lights it up. He looks down at the previous pages he had written in and quickly scans over each of the pages before closing the book as the scene fades to black.~
