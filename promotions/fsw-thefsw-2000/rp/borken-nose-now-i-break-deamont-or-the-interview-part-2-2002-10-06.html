Borken Nose? Now I Break Deamont [or The Interview, Part 2]

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/06/2002 We open up in a familiar looking office. There is a mohogany desk, with a high back leather chair turned, so we can not see who, if anyone, is sitting in it. On the desk, a placard that reads CHIP O’MEARA: FSW COMMISSIONER. So, unless you’re a complete idiot, or Cassius Deamont, one would gather that this is the office of FSW Commissioner Chip O’Meara. The only evidence of someone sitting in the office, is the hard breathing that is heard. All of a sudden, the door opens to the office. “So, listen, give me a call. I’d love to hire you, and FSW could use a star like you. All right, got to go, call me.” The voice belongs to Chip himself. Upon walking into his office, he hangs up his cellphone. Chip flips on the television with a remote he removes from his pocket. “This is CNN. Our top news story at this hour, President Bush says all is a go to NUKE THE MOTHERFUCKING SHIT OUT OF SADAM HUSSEIN, and, hopefully, we can scare up that chickenshit Osama Bin Ladin, and blow HIS FUCKING HEAD OFF TOO.” “Cool. I love a good newstory like this.” Chip talks to himself. He is unattentative to the heavy breathing within his office. “Uh, damn Taco Bell, runs right through me.” Chip disappears into his executive office bathroom. As Chip does his ‘business’, the high back leather chair turns to face the camera, and sitting in the chair, is none other than Chris ‘Boda’ Sloboda. His nose covered with a plastic face protector, the former PCW World Champion tosses a magazine onto the desk. A close-up of the magazine shows a picture of Boda and of Cassius Deamont. Boda looks at the magazine, then spits on the Cassius picture. “You’re fucking dead.” Boda snarls at the magazine, “You are so fucking dead. You little piece of shit, wait, you’re not good enough to be called that........” Boda’s interupted by the sound of the toilet flushing in the executive bathroom. Chip opens the door, and emerges, almost jumping out of his pants when he sees Boda sitting in his chair. “HOLY SHIT” Chip screams. “Dammit Chris, you scared the shit out of me.” “Are you sure? Smells like you already took care of that in the john.” Boda remarks, being his usual wise-ass self. “So, you ok? Stinks like something died in you.” “Burritos, Supreme matter of fact.” Chip grabs his stomach, as if in some pain. “Does it to me every time. So, what’s up?” “Not much, just reading the interview I did last week.” Boda tosses the magazine to Chip. “You remember, the one YOU were suppose to do. But, you disappeared, and left me with that nerd.” “Oh yeah.” Chip shrugs it off. He skims the article. “Nice work. By the way.... how’s the nose?” “Broken, how you think it is?” Boda smarts off. “Cassius is going to pay.” “Well, “ Chip asks “You gonna be able to wrestle this week? You and Deamont face Timo and Mike in round 3 this week.” “Yes I will be able to wrestle.” Boda stands, and walks towards the water cooler. “But, I don’t plan on Cassius being able to even make it to the match. He’s crossed the line, and now, it’s time to show that little puke.... why I am The Big Bad Boda Daddy.” “In due time......let’s get the win this week, then it’s gauranteed walk in the park for you to become world champion” Chip tries to smooth things a bit. “at Bad Moon Rising.” At this point, Boda punches the water bottle right off the water cooler stand. Water splashes all over the floor and wall. Chip looks at Boda, who simply turns, and remarks...... “Fuck the World Title, I don’t need it. And Fuck Cassius Deamont....... he don't matter anymore.” The scene fades to black.
