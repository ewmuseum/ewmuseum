Decree of Darkness Saga: Upholding the Covenant

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 4 of 2002 A.D. NOTE: With the PURE tournament being scrapped, I'm bringing this roleplay here for sake of continuity and reference. Slightly edited for FSW, here it is. --------------------------------------- (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan) On the horizon looms Judgment Day. I can feel it in my bones and in the recesses of my blackened heart. The rage of the Old Ones has begun to spill outward in a tumultuous fury, and with this has come the great gospel that has been prophesized as a prelude to the End Times. News of asylums becoming overcrowded with the psychically sensitive has reached my joyous ears; no man can help but succumb to the maddening dream-sendings of Great Cthulhu or his glorious kin. Violence has expanded exponentially in the targeted areas, and in the vast and remote deserts of Africa a terrible beast has been observed writhing and thrashing around the dilapidated ruins of primeval G’harne. The followers of the Old Ones worldwide have taken this salvo of phenomena as a call to arms and a way to support their masters’ bidding from the abyss. My part is relatively simple, as my directive has been issued from the Crawling Chaos himself. I have merely been ordered to spread the new gospel across professional wrestling’s forlorn landscape, and crush all in my way until The Reckoning. Following a minor setback, I view the impending battles in FSW as an offering on behalf of humanity....a slew of souls to be imparted to the Great Old Ones. PCW took steps already to protect themselves, and as their only considerable means of holding me at bay at the infamous annual Mall Brawl chose my termination. Heheh....it was no deep, dark secret that the populace of the fair PCW company feared me. They feared what I am capable of should mortal ambition override my duties to the Old Ones, and that alone held me in check……make no mistake about that. Not the efforts of my ever-fearful peers or the timid front office, but my own endeavor for the time being. Let the children of Allah and the downtrodden of Hubbard have their fun. For when their time passes and the gaunt shadow of Cthulhu rises over all this miserable and desolate planet, their days of infamy shall come to pass and a new era shall be initiated across this barren world. And under the sigil of the awakened Old Ones, I, along with the devoted and the pious under the new order, shall prosper. Of this I am told by the Black Pharaoh, and through his blessings is it affirmed. Having felt the unparalleled power of which Nyarlathotep has seen fit to bequeath upon me in times past, I can only shudder as I imagine the destruction that shall be left in my wake when the order arrives. The order to take down the wrestling regime by my own hand. All things start small. I have said this many a time now, but I have yet to receive the order to expand my activities as an agent of the Old Ones. And until I do, so things will remain. I am content with my position for now. Should I be inexplicably blessed soon, then I shall indeed relish such a sanction from my glorious majesties from beyond the stars. My past in the Oriental deathmatch circuit is well-documented, and it would be false to say that has no bearing on my decision to enter such a brutal competition. I hold loyalty to the Old Ones….but it would be foolish to state that my experience would lend no effect to the bloodshed sure to follow in coming weeks. Tonight a sacrifice must be offered, for fortune and as testament to our undying dedication to those we serve. Riding on this submission is the endorsement of the Old Ones upon our sect of followers. Brother Asenath and Brother Armitage, among others, have been dispatched to claim a worthy sacrifice for the ceremony. As is customary in such rituals we have been directed to spill the blood of a female virgin as our offering to the power of the Masters. In mere hours the sacrament shall be undertaken, and our power shall grow ever greater as the time of reckoning on this Earth approaches. I only pray the Old Ones take this lamb of vile Jehovah and deem it a worthy gift to their immaculate eminence. Ia Shub Niggurath!! * * * “High Priest Shanahan…..” Brother Asenath knelt in grim reverence at the feet of Bruce, the Boston man being donned in his silver-trimmed ceremonial robes. The shadows veiled his solemn features, and did likewise with the horde of his peers who also stooped in devotion before their sullen leader amid an abundance of black cloaks. “M’lord, the ceremony awaits your blessing to begin. The maiden is in place and prepared for the consecration.” In the background howled the frenzied shrieks of one Nichole Morgan, a nubile and energetic blond who attended local New Miskatonic University of Rutland Vermont, expanded ‘sister’ campus of the now-rebuilt Miskatonic college of Arkham Massachusetts. Tears streamed down her mascara-smudged face, sobs wracking her lean, hourglass form. She had been stripped devoid of clothing and caked in a thin layer of mud before being lashed to an insidious altar, crowned with the vile effigy of Great Cthulhu molded of ebony. Spiraling around the center monolith was a ring of glyphs, wholly obscene and scribed in coal. As Shanahan approached the youthful female, his followers knelt in a staunch circle and began iterate the familiar chant to their abyssal lords. ”Ph’nglui mglw’nafh Cthulhu R’lyeh wgah’nagl fhtagn.” “Please!” cried the shuddering young woman, her face contorted in both fear and desperate appeal. “My father is a politician! He….he has connections! Whatever you want we can give….” Shanahan’s cruel smirk abruptly silenced her. “Foul whore,” snarled the mad master of ceremony. “What purpose shall money have when the Old Ones come to rule this mad world? We have no desire nor need for any petty material things you can offer us. The only entity that you can offer us of any value…..is your soul.” “NNNOOOOO!” The frantic girl shrieked yet again, wriggling desperately against the ropes which bound her to the altar, tears still trickling down her pallid skin. Around her, those of Shanahan’s sect continued their grim chanting. Boston’s Bastard Son, eyebrows arched, continued his diatribe as the grotesque mantra of the cult rose and fell around him. “The world you live in is a fabricated hoax, Miss Morgan,” proclaimed the robed Shanahan. “’The Matrix’ is a much more viable explanation of mankind’s true nature than that despicable ‘Real World’. The reality you know veils a much more sinister truth, its all a phantasmal portrait formulated by those who had seen the truth and didn’t want the whole of humanity stumbling onto those vulgar actualities. Men have been driven mad by the things I’ve been trusted to know. And alas, they still continue to do so on an almost daily basis as they unwarily unearth ancient knowledge that man was never meant to discover.” Thunder rumbled ominously in the distance, a flash of lightning obscured by low-hanging clouds. Wind began to howl through the thickets and cast dead foliage into the area of the rite. Shanahan paused his revelations and gazed skyward, closing his eyes in rapture. “They bid the time to be right. Miss Morgan.....I advise you prepare your eyes and your mind. For the sights of the abyss are macabre beyond your comprehension.” The same evil hymn as of the cult uttered from Shanahan’s lips as he withdrew a serrated dagger from his cloak, the hilt marked with more of the irreverent occult symbols as adorned the altar. The chant meshed with Law’s resumed shrieks for help, watching as the silvery blade flashed under the light of the scattered torches. Bruce raised his head again, as if imploring the heavens. “Great Cthulhu, Glorious Hastur, Creator Of All Things and Ruler Of All Space And Time Azathoth…..I give unto you now this one of virgin blood. May her death bring you glory and her soul render service unto your glorious courts for all eternity.” Thunder crashed again even closer as the wind whipped ever stronger. Shanahan hoisted the blade and directed it at the heaving breast of the squirming Nichole Morgan, again uttering forth the chant. ”Ph’nglui mglw’nafh Cthulhu R’lyeh wgah’nagl fhtagn……” “AAHHHHHH!” Bruce paused and turned to see one of his followers on the west side of the circle pitch lifelessly to the ground. Those surrounding him backed away in what was either stark horror or utter stupefaction. Silhouetted against the flashing lightning in the background was the gaunt form of a man, a sword clutched in his right hand. Shoulder-length hair brushed his burly shoulders as he passed without concern through the sea of silent cultists and to the side of Morgan, still brandishing the brilliant blade. The eyes of Shanahan met with the eyes of his detractor, the crusader sent forth from the Vatican as warned by Father Craven in his letter. Hatred flared in the eyes of Bruce, his knuckles whitening as they gripped the ceremonial dagger of Nyarlathotep. “You……”
