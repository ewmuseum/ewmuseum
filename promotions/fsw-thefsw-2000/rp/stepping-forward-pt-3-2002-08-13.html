Stepping Forward Pt. 3

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 13, 2002 A blade...pressed hard against the skin. The camera begins to film with the shining blade. As it pulls out some, it clearly shows the vain. The blade is pressed against the vein, but with not enough force to break and cause a gash. The young girl slides the blade up and down her arm. 'Life,' she utters from her sorrowful lips. She applies stronger force this time, yet still not enough to break through her skin. '...is like a coin. You can spend it any which way you choose. But...' She stands the tip of the blade to her wrist. Rebecca's past achievements and memories flash before your very eyes. She forces the blade downward, into her vein. But her skin endures. She continues, '...you can ONLY spend...it...once.' Rebecca pulls the blade from her wrist and smiles, satanically. 'I've spent my life in the manner which I chose. But the method in which I chose to run my life...was the entirely wrong method.' 'I've failed my family.' 'I've destroyed their legacy.' 'I've ruined the family name...' Tears begin to run down her face. The light reflects off the tears, making them glimmer and shine. Yet the smile remains. The satanic, evil smirk. 'I've failed not only myself. Not only my family. But those who loved me. Those who cared for me. THE ONE...who faught for me.' Rebecca slams the knife into the table. The blade cuts right through the wood, piercing a whole and proving the strength and durability. She maintains a tight grasp on the handle. 'I should have died...way back. I shouldn't have survived the massacre. I shouldn't have survived the attacks.' She pauses. 'My soul should be resting with those of my family.' In anguish, she pulls the blade up from the table. She presses it hard against her wrist and traces the vein. Tears stream down her face at a faster, constant rate. She breaks the skin. Blood begins to drip. The cold, deep redness sickening her stomach. 'I've caused much too much dismay. I've destroyed anything reputable in my life.' A sudden knock is heard at her cottage door. Rebecca looks up and tries to hold back the tears. But she can't. They continue streaming down her face. She begins to speak at a whisper, 'Why? Why must they not let me die in peace. Why must they always interrupt at the designated moment.' The door swings open. Expecting to see someone such as Chris, Rebecca is put into a sudden shock. Another young girl enters and rushes to Rebecca. Rebecca tries to speak, but her words just won't slip from her mouth. 'Don't speak, Rebecca,' says the other girl, 'Don't speak. I know why you're here.' Rebecca quickly tries hiding the blade by moving it under the table. She carefully drops it to the floor, so it makes as little noise as possible. Using her other arm, she attempts to hide the gash in her arm as well as the streaming blood. The second girl, Abigail, knows full well what is going on. 'Don't even try to hide it. I know, Rebecca. I knew this day would come. I just knew it.' Rebecca finally manages to get herself to speak, 'How?' Abigail replies, 'I knew that once you got involved with Chris, your life would change. You fell for him too soon. And he fell right back for you. He saw too much of your sister in you. You brought him closer to your sister. You brought him the comfort he once had.' 'How do you know all this, Abby?' 'I've followed, Rebecca,' says Abigail, 'I've watched the two of you prosper over the months. I've watched the two of you become more involved...I've watched you both...fall in love.' Rebecca still holds shame on herself, 'But I've brought him down. I've brought his life to new lows.' Abigail pleads with Rebecca, 'The death of your sister brought his life to new lows. Rob brought his life to new lows.' 'But...' Rebecca pleads but Abigail interrupts, 'But what?' 'It really wasn't Rob,' Rebecca replies. 'It wasn't, was it?' exclaims Abigail, 'You're finally coming to admit that?' 'Rob, although being the older brother, wasn't the stronger one,' Rebecca explains, 'Adam began to work his physique at a younger age. He started the same time Rob started, and since he was younger he had more stamina and more determination to become the better brother.' Abigail blurts in, '...and Adam forced Rob out. Adam poisened Rob and got him sick and dependent on Adam for his own survival. Thus Rob had no choice but to listen to what Adam said.' 'If you knew all this...' Rebecca stares at her in confusion, '...why did you not say it sooner?' 'Because it was not for me to say.' says Abigail, 'Your entire family was murdered over NOTHING. Imagine what would have happened to me if I voiced SOMEthing.' Rebecca seems a bit angry at Abigail's lack of informational sharing. 'So what about now? What do you know now?' Abigail pauses before saying much else. Rebecca gives her an odd look, as if questioning her again. Finally, Abigail breaks her momentary silence, 'I don't know if you recall it or not. But the attacks on Chris long, long ago. When a group of men attempted to bring him to death before a fight with Chris Cuneo and Skull Morei.' 'I do remember him saying something of it, but that was quite some time ago,' she replies. 'I was there,' Abigail says, bringing a sudden shock to Rebecca. 'I was watching. The gunman said it was the RoP. Indeed...but not the Realm of Pain in which was affiliated with Skull Morei. Morei, as well as anyone else at that current time was affiliated with the attack.' 'Then how could it truely be explained?' Rebecca pleads, 'and where does that sit in importance to TODAY?' 'Where is Dupri?' asks Abigial. 'Gone,' Rebecca replies sharply. Abigail continues, 'What about Piccillo?' Again, Rebecca replies sharply, 'Gone.' Abigail continues once more, 'Karnage?' Rebecca grows a bit frustrated, not seeing where this is going, 'Gone, as well. But what is the relevance?' 'Each and every one of them share a similar bond with Chris,' Abigail tries to explain. 'They were all united together in Ultimate Championship Wrestling in some way. Then they were risen to stardom in Platinum Wrestling. But one by one, they were perished. They were each broken down. Now, one is left remaining. That one being Chris...' Rebecca still can't see Abigail's point, 'But thats the past. Chris no longer has affiliation to those who were involved in Platinum Wrestling or UcW. Where is the connection?' Abigail immediately comes back with her answer, 'The connection...is in their blood. They all come from similar background. They all come from the same domain. They all come from a certain heritage. A similar brand. One in which certain individuals could not get to the top of. Jealousy and anger overcame them. And since, they've began to eat away at each of those who were once involved with being at the base.' 'And Chris...' says Rebecca with a sudden sadness overcoming her, '...is the next...to be relinquished?' Abigail is silent for a moment. She slightly lowers her head and softly mumbles, 'The relinquishment has already began. Someone from his past has stepped in, in some way, and are guiding Tyler Davidson to glory.' 'Davidson...' whispers Rebecca. '...Davidson,' answers Abigail. In worry, Rebecca begins to shed more tears. She lowers her head, allowing her to sulk more. Abigail continues, 'And with Chris emotionally unstable...it makes him more vunerable. Those factors, also combined with the war he'll wage with Davidson. Its all logical how Chris can quite easily be brought to his end.' Rebecca raises her head. 'I have to make things right again. I can't let this go on. I can't ruin things any longer.' She reaches to the floor and takes the blade. She raises the tip of the blade to her throat and closes her eyes. Abigail stands and watches, not making any effort to stop her. 'And you plan to go out...like that?' she says. Abigail reaches and takes the blade from Rebecca's hand. Rebecca gives no resistance. Abigail tosses the blade on the floor, and it slides aross the room. It stops after striking into a wall. She then takes Rebecca's hand and guides her to her feet. Rebecca opens her eyes. 'Come,' whispers Abigail. She guides Rebecca to the open door. Rebecca steps outside. Before leaving, Abigail peers back in and across the room. She speaks rather softly, almost as if hoping a room full of people wouldn't hear her, 'Life is only as good as you make it out to be. We've made ourselves and our lives out to be a misery. We've chosen to live in the past. We've chosen to bring hell into our presence. Our decisions were by far not the best. But they WERE, indeed, our decision.' Abigail reaches into her pocket and pulls a sheet of paper. Stapled to it is a photograph. She tosses it into the room. It floats to the floor in a way obstructing the view. 'And now...it is time to reunite ourselves with our loved ones. To return to our family and civilation.' A single tear rolls down her face. She continues in her whisper, 'Life has treated us as best it could and treat us no more. Now is our time...to pass on into our next life.' She steps out of the door and closes it on her way. Before it shuts, she utters a few last words... 'Live it day by day...' '...sometimes, there might just not be a tomorrow.' She closes the door behind her. To be continued...
