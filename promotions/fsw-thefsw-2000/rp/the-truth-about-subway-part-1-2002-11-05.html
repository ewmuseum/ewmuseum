The TRUTH...About SUBWAY!!! (Part 1)

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/05/2002 Twas a day not unlike any other in the Subway Sandwich shop on Cobequid Road in Lower Sackville, Nova Scotia, Canada. The sun was shining through a small layer of gray clouds. A light snow had fallen the night before, leaving a thin layer of white on car tops, roofs, and trees. The traffic was slightly busy at this time of day. Many people were rushing to get to their doctor's appointments, pick their kids up from school, get in some Christmas Shopping, or grab some lunch during their break. Sales were up for Subway on this cold yet sunny winter day. They were expecting a job interview. The 57 year old supervisor, Joan, was casually whiping off the counter as Rob, a 17 year old, perky, happy Sandwich Artist stocks the front with a smile on his face. The manager of the store, Nancy, just finished ringing in the last of the rush of customers when a man walked in. He was in his mid twenties, short brown hair and blue eyes. Very clean cut looking and somewhat handsome in his black dress pants, blue shirt and dark blue tie. The thing that struck her the most was that gleaming smile of his. Almost blinding in it's intense whiteness. The man walked up to the counter and introduced himself. 'Hi, I'm Marvelous...Mike Marvelous. I'm here for the interview?' Nancy fished through a stack of paper and pulled out Mike's resumé. She quickly skimmed over it through her square-shaped glasses. 'Michael Melvin Marvelous...' Mike cringes at the mention of his middle name but he sucks it up and answers with a smile. 'Yup, that's me.' 'It says here that you have a University Education at St. Francis Xavier University with a wrestling scholarship....and you've been a professional wrestler since July, 2000?' 'Right.' Nancy looks over Mike, checking if he's drunk or on some kind of drug. 'Umm...Mr. 'Marvelous'....may I ask WHY you are giving up a lucrative career in professional wrestling for a job at a local subway?' Mike shrugs and answers. 'Who says I have to give it up? To tell you the truth, I don't even want to get paid. I'm doing this because I want to give back to the people....give back to the community.' The manager scratches her head, confused. 'And you figured working volunteer work for Subway would be the best way to accomplish that?' The Canadian Hero smirks at the question as he wonders who this lady had to boink to get her job, asking a dumb question like that. 'Uuh...my question is 'why not'? People get hungry...people need to eat...and Subway is the future of the fast food industry. I would never serve greasy, cholesterol-laden burgers to my legions and legions of fans. I think it's best that I show them how much I still care...and how much I appreciate them...by serving fresh, delicious, healthy submarine sandwiches with 6 grams of fat or less.' 'Well...you might as well come in and get your uniform on. We're expecting a rush in any minute now. The code for the door is 4321. Come on in, Mike.' Mike smiles gleefully and opens the door to the bathroom halls, then punches the code on the door that says 'Employees Only'. The door clicks open and he walks into 'The Back'. Ah, The Back...where all the Subway Secrets are forged and carried out...where vegetables and meat are sliced and prepared joyfully by smiling, happy Sandwich Artists, dressed in their green shirts, black pants, aprons and visors... WELL FORGET EVERYTHING YOU 'THOUGHT' YOU KNEW ABOUT 'THE BACK'! IN FACT, FORGET EVERYTHING YOU THINK ABOUT SUBWAY! IT WAS ALL A LIE! A JOKE! A FUCKING CONSPIRACY! LISTEN UP YOU LITTLE PUKE...REMEMBER THIS STORY NEXT TIME YOU WALK UP TO THE COUNTER TO GET A FOOT LONG VEGGIE DELIGHT OR DELICIOUS COLD CUT COMBO! The Canadian Hero looks on in horror as two young girls and one young man hunch over a counter with plastic gloves, preparing roast beef. Joan, the Supervisor...pleasant and sweet as she seemed out front...is a completely different person in 'The Back'. The 50 year old Subway Veteran paces back and forth, eyeing the young employees with piercing, blood-shot eyes. She carries with her a whip...all nine spikey tailes drag on the ground behind her as she paces around. She watches as the young kids put the roast beef on the scale, weigh it, fold paper over it and put it in a stack of other pieces of roast beef. Billy, the young blonde boy, places a piece of roast beef on the scale, and the needle shoots up to 12oz. He folds the paper over and puts it on the stack, only to feel a sharp pain shoot up and down his back as all nine tails on the leather cat-o-nine tails whip dig into the flesh on his back, tearing 9 strips through the back of his shirt revealing huge gouges, which quickly fill up with blood. He howls out in pain as Joan the Supervisor grabs him by his hair and turns his head to face her. She screams at him in a demonic voice. '12 OUNCES?! ARE YOU STUPID OR SOMETHING!? IT'S 5 OUNCES! NO MORE, NO LESS! 12 OUNCES!?! THAT'S HALF A NEWBORN BABY'S WEIGHT! ARE YOU TRYING TO PUT US OUT OF BUSINESS, BOY!?' The two girls cover shield themselves from the Supervisor and cry out nervously as the small-framed boy breathes in and out heavily in instense pain and answers in a high-pitched, frightened voice as his eyes well up with tears. 'N-No, Joan. I didn't m-mean too...I'm sorry!' 'SORRY, ARE YOU!? SORRY FOR TRYING TO WASTE OUR PRECIOUS STOCK!? WASTE OUR MONEY!? WELL...YOU KNOW THE RULES!' The boy's skink suddenly grows pale with fright. 'PICK UP THAT KNIFE!' 'N-n-no! PLEASE, Joan! Don't make me do it!' 'PICK UP THAT KNIFE, BOY!' She tightens her grip on the boy's hair and he let's out another yelp, then puts out his trembling hand and picks up a small, red-handled bread knife. 'NOW...CUT OFF 7 OUNCES OF FLESH!' 'PLEASE JOAN! GIVE ME ONE MORE CHANCE!' Mike finally speaks up. He's had enough of this. 'Come on, Joan! You can't be serious...that's...that's SICK!' The whip cracks as it cuts a strip into Mike's leg. He grits his teeth and grunts as the pain shoots up his body. 'SHUT YOUR MOUTH TRAINEE! NOW, GIVE ME MY 7 OUNCES OF FLESH, BOY! NO MORE, NO LESS! THAT'S WHAT YOU OWE US FOR YOUR STUPID MISTAKE! PUT IT ON THE SCALE SO I CAN BE SURE!' The boy slowly raises the knife, lifts his shirt off, clenches his eyes shut and plunges the knife into his side. All that can be heard for the next 5 minutes is screaming and the sound of metal ripping through flesh, followed by the dripped sound of blood as he places the flesh on the scale until the metre goes up to 7 ounches. 'T-There...can I s-start working again, now?' 'NO, YOU'RE SACKED!' 'SACKED!?!' 'SACKED! GET OUT OF MY FACE YOU FOUL, SNOTTY-NOSED DELINQUINT! WE HAVE NO ROOM FOR SLACKERS HERE!' The boy bows his head in shame and walks off, holding his bloody side. He goes for the exit, but before he can make it there, 3 men in black suits swoop down on him. Two of them hold his arms while the other inserts a needle into his neck and pushes down on the plunger. The boy's eyes roll back in his head then his whole body goes limp as he is dragged away by the men in the black suits. Joan the Supervisor clears her throat to get Mike's attention. 'Still wanna be a Sandwich Artist, Trainee?' Mike frowns and prepares to tell her off, but then thinks about it. 'Yes. I came here to serve the public and serve the public I shall.' Joan shoves a bundle of clothing into Mike's hands. 'Here's the last suit you'll ever wear.' Moments later, our Canadian Hero emerges from the bathroom in his Subway uniform. It is a little small over his muscular frame, but he looks like a million bucks with his soft, brown hair sprouting out of his black visor and 'Sandwich Artist' stitched across his sleeve in white thread. He even musters a smile of pride...until Joan cracked the whip against the back of one of the girls as she stood hunched over the sink, washing a bread pan from a stack of never-ending dishes. She turns slowly and shoots a twisted grin at Mike. 'You're lucky, trainee! You go out front today! But remember...once the customers go...your ass is mine!' Mike gulps nervously as he walks around the counter and out to the front. It is finally his time to 'walk the garden' and build masterpeace upon masterpiece of Sandwich Artistry. Will he be able to endure the hardships of being a Subway employee or will he succumb to the perils of the fast food industry? What horrors await Mike as he stands before a gleaming counter filled with fresh, juicy vegetables? What will serving the public be like...will it be everything Mike expected...or something far more horrifying? Tune in next time in this special documentary...The TRUTH About Subway!!!
