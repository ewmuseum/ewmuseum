La Policia

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today :P *The scene opens up and Pablo Diablo and Francisco Fumar are driving around in their old car which putters around and puts out alot of smoke. Francisco is driving and singing along to the spanish music and puffing on a blunt. Pablo is sitting shotgun and is just nodding his head around in circles and making weird noises smoking his own joint.... Fran Man is tapping the steering wheel to the beat and constantly looking back and forth in the rear view mirror making sure he is looking 'good'. Finally Fran Man is getting nervous with pablo making those ridiculous noises and swinging his head around* Francisco Fumar: Say homes, Cut that shit out man! Pablo Diablo: (still swinging head around) Ahh lahhh loooo uhhh chaaa balhhhh. Francisco Fumar: Im serious man, You cut that shit out right now. *Pablos isn't listening to Fran Man or he just doesnt care and keeps going at it* Pablo Diablo: (still swinging head around) Ooo choo chhaaa chiii , ohhhh choooo chahhh chiii *Francisco is getting kinda mad and looks back in the mirror to glance at himself again but this time he sees red and blue lights* Francisco Fumar: Oh shit homes..... the cops!! *Pablo just thinks he is joking trying to make hm stop. Francisco pulls the car over and Pablo is still making those weird noises. Fran Man puts his blunt out on his toungue and is eating it..... blunt and all.....* Francisco Fumar: Man shut that shit up ...... here he comes!! *The cop gets out of his patrol car and is now approaching their car..... the cops comes up to Fran Man at the drivers window.* Pablo Diablo: (still swinging head around) Ooo choo chhaaa chiii , ohhhh choooo chahhh chiii Cop: What the hell is wrong with him!! *Pablo then notices that Fran Man wasnt lying and sees the cop..... Pablo sits up straight real fast and has the joint in his right hand so the cop hasnt seen it yet...Pablo then smashes it under the seat and puts it in his sock* Cop: You gonna answer me punk! Francisco Fumar: Ohhhhh him.... ohhhhh uhmmm thats my brother..... (Francisco smiles deviously) He's retarded. Cop: Retarded eh? *Francisco looks over at Pablo and Pablo is angry but knows that the only way they are gonna get out of this situation is to play along* Pablo Diablo: I'm noooot weeetoded ...... I'm spppeccciallll Cop: Yes you are man. (looking back over at Fran) Now let me see your License and Registration. Francisco Fumar: Oh yeah sure...... no problem..... *Fran Man opens up the glove box and starts throwing papers on the floor.... and emty 'Jack In The Box' bags down untill finally he finds what he is looking for.* Francisco Fumar: Here you go sir. Cop: This picture doesnt look like you! Francisco Fumar: Well I used to have long hair back then. Cop: In the 60's?? *Francisco is getting nervous* Francisco Fumar: Uhmmm yahhhh Cop: Well first off I dont know of any damn Long haired Mexican hippies..... do you? Francisco Fumar: Oh yeahhhhh man there are tons of us..... ROCK ON !!!!! Woodstock !!!!!! Cop: SHUT UP!! Francisco Fumar: Ok. Cop: Well Obviously I'm gonna have to give you a ticket for an expired drivers license.... since this is not the 1960's....(looking at the Drivers License) Mr...... Wang. Francisco Fumar: Ok sir. Cop: WANG!!!!! That dont sound Mexican! *From the sheer fact that the cop yelled the word 'Wang' Pablo started laughing* Pablo Diablo: Hahaha he said Wang!! Cop: You got a problem man!! Francisco Fumar: No sir he doesnt...... he just likes wangs (winks at Pablo) Pablo Diablo: Yahhhhhhh I looooveeeee wannnnngssss (pretending to be retarded) Cop: Yeah I bet you do! ..... Wang dont sound Mexican to me..... Are you boys Illegal??? Francisco Fumar: Who us? Cop: No the only other 2 mexicans here. Francisco Fumar: Oh he's not a Mexican sir ...... he's from California. Cop: California my ass, he looks Mexican to me..... so he is..... Francisco Fumar: But he...... Cop: HE IS!!!! Francisco Fumar: Ok he is. Cop: You boys wait right here..... Im gonna go call for back-up Francisco Fumar: Ok Officer. *The Cop goes back to his car....* Francisco Fumar: What we gonna do man! Pablo Diablo: Well I guess I can say good bye to you. Francisco Fumar: Hey that aint funny homes..... I havent got a green card..... ahhhh fuck it *With that Fran-Man puts his foot on the accelerator and floors it........ The cop behind him notices it and turns on his sirens and chases after them...... Fran Man is driving like a maniac and is about to cross an Intersection ....... traffic is going acroos it and they have a red-light* Pablo Diablo: Dont run it man....... You'll kill us both Francisco Fumar: I dont got a choice....... if I run it That pig wont follow us through it...... *They are closing in on the red light ...... Pablo closes his eyes and starts to scream* Pablo Diablo: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH *Francisco just screams with him and closes his eyes at the last second as well* Francisco Fumar: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH *They both open thier eyes to see them on the other side of the intersection and the cop is stuck but is clearing away traffic with his sirens so he can chase after them. Fran Man keeps it floored and finally pulls into a big shopping center ...... they both jump out the car and run into a pet store...... * Pablo Diablo: Man I cant believe you did that! Francisco Fumar: Niether can I homes..... but shit I had to! Pablo Diablo: Damn cops. Francisco Fumar: Yeah, Me too. Pablo Diablo: Well hey man I told the Boss that we would do this promo cutting thing for him about our match. Francisco Fumar: What the hell is promo cutting? Pablo Diablo: Hell if I know, but lets go high........ I hate work. Francisco Fumar: Sounds like a good plan to me. Pablo Diablo: Hey maybe this guy has some weed (points backwards) Francisco Fumar: Who him? I dont know man, remember the grocery store. Pablo Diablo: Man you know how Hydro(More Potent Weed) is grown! Francisco Fumar: Yeah in fish tanks..... so? Pablo Diablo: So look around you!!! *Fran-Man glances around and sees fish tanks everywhere* Francisco Fumar: Man homes, this is a pet store. Pablo Diablo: Nah mannnn this is a drug front for a pet store! Francisco Fumar: Man homes how bout I talk to this guy this time Pablo Diablo: Ok but talk pet store talk (winks eye) Francisco Fumar: Man screw all that Im just gonna ask him. *Fran-Man walks over to the counter to where an old chinese man is sitting* Francisco Fumar: Hey old timey-man ..... you got any weed? Old Timey-Man: Weed? Are you cops? Francisco Fumar: Nah man, me and my partner just trying to score some bud. Old Timey-Man: Why didnt you just say so...... this is a drug front for a pet store! Pablo Diablo: I told ya man!! Francisco Fumar: Well shit how your dime sacs look? *Man holds up a sac* Francisco Fumar: ok homes hit me up with one of those. *Fran-Man gives the guy a ten and takes the sac and him and Pablo leave the store* Pablo Diablo: I told ya man!! Francisco Fumar: Yeah, Yeah, Well c'mon lets get out of here before that damn cop finds us. Pablo Diablo: Yeah we need to get over to the Motel .... We got a camera man waiting there. Francisco Fumar: Motel? Pablo Diablo: Yeah man they got us a Motel room and everything. Francisco Fumar: Wow, we're really moving up homes. *Pablo and Fran-Man run over quickly to the car and jump in and drive off to the motel* .....:::::::A Few Minutes Later:::::::..... Pablo Diablo: Well, here it is man. Francisco Fumar: I hope they got a color television...... I aint seen a color television in a long time homes. Pablo Diablo: Well when we get our first Paychecks I say we buy one. Francisco Fumar: Yeah one of those really big ones! *Fran-Man and Pablo continue walking and talking until they get to thier room and enter, A camera man like he said was waiting there watching the color television and layed out on the bed* Pablo Diablo: See Fran, color tv. Francisco Fumar: Alllllrighttttt....... Pablo Diablo: So you gonna turn that camera on? Camera Man: Oh yeah sorry. *Turns on Camera* Pablo Diablo: Ok man.... we were kinda thinking of doing this show like 'Cops' But itstead we call it 'Teenagers'. Francisco Fumar: Oh yeah, Teenagers...... Teenagers.....What you gonna do..... What you gonna do.... What you gonna do when POT calls for you!!!!! Pablo Diablo: So you like it?? Camera Man: You guys are supposed to talk about your match coming up! Pablo Diablo: Oh........ok..... Uhmmm Francisco Fumar: Well what do we say? Camera Man: Just look in the camera and talk about it. Francisco Fumar: Well who are we fighting? Camera Man: Are you serious?? You guys dont even know? Pablo Diablo: Nope. Camera Man: You guys are fighting EOE. Pablo Diablo: EOE?. Camera Man: Yeah the guy who sold you the shizty sac. Pablo Diablo: Oh, the black guy. Francisco Fumar: Dont worry Pablo, I will start this out. OK, I'm gonna beat your ass.... and take your Grass!!! Pablo Diablo: Your gonna take my grass man? Francisco Fumar: Well nah not yours... Since you share yours with me. Camera Man: Uhmmm arent yall gonna say anything else? Pablo Diablo: Nah I think he did a good job...... well ok..... bye *The Camera Man is confused but he gets up and leaves the Motel room as Fran-Man is amazed at the color tv and the scene fades to black*
