The Return

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day before yesterday The raucous rumble of machinery ravages the barren battlefield like a giant horde of bees on Fall's final flower. A harsh wind steals within the noise, furtively twisting and distorting its essence so it roars in forcefully and then immediately retreats, like wailing waves upon a sullen shore. An eery uneasiness seems prevalent in the air of Deville Estate, an air that reeks of disease, destruction, and death. Angelo Deville stands imperiously atop one of two rolling hills, backed by a small forest of evergreens, scornfully watching workers he's never even been introduced to trying to rebuild his dynasty. He stands out worse than Aimz in a nunnery, his white suit immaculate and unruffled despite the wind, and not one golden strand of hair strays from the slicked mop. Blue eyes burn banefully down at the clumsy yellow machinery brought in to carve out and define the foundation for intricacy they could never begin to envision. And whose fault is that? A crisp voice whispers rhetorically in his mind, sadistically raking him back over the coals of damnation. His face contorts in a grimace as he mercilessly remembers what brought him here. Deville stood on this very spot and watched it all explode and burn in the flames of his own ambition. From his makeshift pedestal he watched callously as many hard-working men died, writhing and screaming under the torment they once believed they could withstand - nay, the torment they knew they could overcome. They came courageously and without fault, bringing myriad skills to the battle, but their mission was doomed to failure from the getgo. The Devil cannot be killed, Angelo assures himself symbolically, rationalising his victims' fate, but there is an itching in the back of his brain that prevents any potential for peace of mind. We won, yes, but at what cost? His eyes remain transfixed on the wreckage, even when they glaze over from the memory of watching everything destroyed... The memory of destroying everything. In a strange and inexplicable way he's actually pleased with himself. Perhaps it comes from the unwavering belief that he never once lost control of the situation. Like every mythical beast, any significant growth demands its customary virgin... To get more requires giving up some of what you have... To annihilate opposition means sacrificing a small piece of self. Sacrifice. Deville sacrificed a lot, and he knows it. His latest excursion almost cost him everything he holds dear, everything that justifies his attitude and beliefs. He knew going up against the apparent impossible would either make or break his very being, and deep within the unexplored confines of his soul he knew it would require a form of death. Parts of Angelo Deville died in his last struggle, but it was all ultimately worth it. Where the amorphous die the refined are reborn. Sometimes sacrifices must be made, Deville's mind growls as he turns his back on the fumbling construction beneath him. Sometimes things must be lost, it continues in a teasing fashion, dancing on the ridge of redemption. But now that the payment has been made and the benefits received, it's time to retake what was lost. A small smile sneaks onto his angular face. It's time to regain control of our life. ~><>...<><~ The patrons of the elegant restaurant A Little Taste of Hell would have recognised the newcomer even if he wasn't FSW Primetime Champion, the Universal Champion, and recent winner of the global Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. They'd know that cold face even if it wasn't continuously splashed on the television and national newspapers under criminal headings. They'd shirk from the menacing gaze even if they weren't absolutely positive he'd kill them without a second thought. Their collective gasp isn't due to his infamy or any real danger they feel for themselves at the newly uncharacteristic appearance of the restaurant's founder, but rather the inevitable showdown that must occur when he realises there are some whom desire to take his place. And worse, some who've already started trying. They all regard the apparition out of the corners of their eyes as he strides past with murderous purpose, willing themselves to seem disinterested but failing miserably. Every last one of them would be willing to bet their fortune he's in for a rude awakening... But on the other side of the coin, the man would make that same bet on all of them. Deville storms directly towards the doors unnecessarily marked 'management only' and pauses for the briefest of seconds. He keeps his eyes trained straight ahead the whole time, ignoring the people, the music, and the lavish decoration he himself brought here. There is no pretense in his ignorance of the individuals gawking at him, yet he'd have to be paralysed from the neck up to not choke on the tension in the air. He can feel every set of eyes in the place firmly on his back, the ones peeking through the surveillance cameras especially. Good, he thinks as he turns the golden knob and saunters into the narrow hallway. That means they're all here. He flashes a grin at the camera perched in the far corner, just above a door identical to the one he just birthed. Without hesitation he makes his way down the short hall and prepares to enter what was the first room of his office, but as he reaches for the door it opens seemingly of its own will. Angelo lets his hand float there, extended in front of his body, as the door swings all the way open to reveal a familiar face. 'Angelo! You made it!' The white-haired mobster greets him with feigned exuberance as he blocks the doorway. Beyond him Deville can see everyone he expected to, and he simply nods grimly at the task before him. The anticipation builds within faster and more intensely than Byron's sexual frustration, and when his greeter goes to shake the extended hand, Deville pulls it slowly back and makes their eyes lock. There is an exceedingly tense moment following the snub, then Deville simply pushes the man out of the way and stoically enters the room. Six Armani suits are spread about the room, the bodies inhabiting them instinctually and knowledgeably suspicious of their guest. A few reach threateningly into their breast pockets, eliciting a grin from Deville. 'So nice of you all to welcome me back like this,' Deville remarks sarcastically, crossing his arms over his chest defiantly. 'Though from the looks of it, one might think you're trying to take over instead of keeping ?er warm for me.' Two of the men have the decency to cast shamed looks at the ground, but the others simply smirk. Deville marks this with unsurprised disapproval... They think they've surpassed his importance. 'It doesn't have to be like this,' the sympathetic voice comes from behind him, from the white-haired man. He stalks around to the front of Deville and stops when their chest are merely a foot apart. 'Rather... It doesn't have to end like this.' The threat masked in the words is so apparent even Frampton would grasp it, and whe- well... Maybe not Frampton, but I digress... When the words come out of his mouth, there is a sinister and ambitious flash behind his grey eyes. Half the men in the room shift uncomfortably at this turning point, wondering whether it'll be bloodshed or beer that they share, and which they'd prefer. 'Yes it does, Deaton,' Deville answers their first question, nodding as he does so. That an unshaven and strung-out drug addict like Deaton would be the one heading this surprises him only marginally less than the fact that he's managed to rally support. The man smiles at Angelo, revealing a full set of stained teeth that would likely rend the flesh of his own mother. 'That's too bad, Angelo, because it means you're going down. The boss has alread-' Deaton begins smugly, but Angelo cuts him off with the threat of a backhand. Deaton flinches back as if a gun was flashed, then recovers and glares from his rapidly reddening face. 'You've thirty seconds to drag your mindless body out of my fucking restaurant and resolve never to return,' Angelo states coldly, scanning the entire room as he speaks to quell any confusion that the order may be restricted to one. 'Or I'll drag you out myself, bleeding your miserable life out on my plush carpets.' There is a moment of stunned silence, then Deaton barks a humourless laugh. 'You speak with power you no longer possess, Deville! This isn't your restaurant any more! It belongs to the family, now... The mob.' Deaton flashes a mockingly superior grin, then continues. 'Salvatore himself told me so.' Six heads nod in agreement behind him, and six hands pull guns out from breast pockets. Deaton's grin grows as Deville stares down six barrels. 'Suit yourself, Deaton,' Deville begins with a rueful shaking of his head, looking at no one in particular. 'But there's something you should know before you die.' 'Oh yeah?' Salvatore prompts with another gale of laughter, his white hair swaying like grass in a field. 'And what's that?' The head-shaking stops abruptly and Deville's eyes snap to attention on Deaton's, causing the man to again flinch involuntarily. A smug smile curls around the words as he speaks them: 'Salvatore isn't the mob anymore.' Deaton has no time to react as shots ring out in the small room and Deville tackles him to the ground. Three of Deaton's cronies crumple to the ground around him, blooding seeping out of their heads onto the carpet. The other three simply take a step back, smoking guns held loftily in their hands as they watch Deville, their partner, press his forearm against Deaton's throat. Deaton's eyes go wide as he gasps for breath, his arms flailing uselessly against Deville's massive shoulders. Angelo leans in real close so that all Deaton can see are his calculating eyes laughing at him, slowly fading out of existence as the tunnel-vision begins creeping in. Deville leans harder into it as tears begin falling from the corners of the formerly grandiose man's eyes, revelling in the thrill that is murder. 'Don Salvatore isn't in charge anymore,' Deville whispers in the man's last seconds. 'Salvatore is the mob no longer.' Deaton gives one final surge or struggle at this blasphemy, but resistance is futile. 'I am in charge now,' Deville hisses, his eyes almost fanatical with glee. 'I am the mob...' Deaton's eyes roll back into his head as his body shuts down completely, his hopes of climbing above his killer forever squashed. 'And it's high time to instill Mob Rule,' Deville finishes, applying more and more pressure until a sickening crack is heard. His three associates nod their approval as blood erupts and gurgles from the already dead man's mouth. The four of them know what needs to be done... And they're the only ones who can do it.
