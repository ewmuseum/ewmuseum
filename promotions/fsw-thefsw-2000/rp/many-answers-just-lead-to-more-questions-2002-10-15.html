Many answers just lead to more questions.

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tomorrow (Somewhere along the edge of consciousness, just past the dreamlike state we reach in our sleep, lies a part of our brain where we rest undisturbed, despite the noise we hear around us. The bustle of the hospital is the most common noise heard while in this phase. Sometimes drug induced, sometimes injury related ... most often, from some form of shock or trauma. All that goes on around is heard and recorded, but the ability to participate is beyond our control. Our actions are beyond us, our interactions are almost predetermined. No matter how hard you try ... you cannot escape it. For the man in question, this has been coming for some time now. No one could see it coming, not physicians, not John, no one. His condition was first noted when he went in for his yearly checkup and almost failed the reflex tests on his knee and his eyes. He was aware all along of what was going on, but he couldn't contribute in any way, shape or form. Hospitalized on and off for weeks, his condition never bettered, and yet, never worsened. He failed to contribute to his workplace in every form other than showing up for his matches. Why is it that now something has brought it to everyone else's attention? An old friend of John's who returned, Jason McAllister, approached John and noticed something was off.) (Jason approached several medical experts looking for an answer. No one could give him a straight one, or at least one that he would expect. One man did give him some hope though. Familiarity would bring him back. Something that was very forefront in his life that could stir some emotion. Something that would jar his senses, something that would demand his every fibre of being to come together and work in unison to revive his identity. It is simple to find something that could potentially do this, but it is also quite difficult, because it may not be the most obvious thing. It might just be something subtle, yet important in their lives. It might be an old flame, a location, a friend ... the real question though, wasn't what would bring him back ... it was in fact, what brought him there in the first place.)
