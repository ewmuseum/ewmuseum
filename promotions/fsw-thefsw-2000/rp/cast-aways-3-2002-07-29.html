Cast Aways #3

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, July 29th, 2002 [Scene opens in the cave where we last found Mike Marvelous and Jacob Jackson. The camera pans around, searching for them...when suddenly it focuses on something strange. A pair of skeletons sitting against the walls of the cave with tattered clothes hanging off their bones! Oh my god! It's too late! The Can-Am Connection has suffered a grim fate!] Mike Marvelous and Jacob Jackson: EEEEEEEEEEEK! Skeletons! [Whoops...it seems they're still alive. But there's STILL skeletons sitting against the walls of the cave! Stop watching this promo! Run! Run for your life!] Jacob Jackson: RUN FOR YOUR LIFE, MICHAEL! Mike Marvelous: EEEEEEEEEK!!!! [The Can-Am Connection begin to run, but they run headlong into a long spike hanging off the ceiling of the cage. They get up slowly and shake the cobwebs out.] Mike Marvelous: Ouch. Jacob Jackson: Ok...let's WALK for our lives... Mike Marvelous: Wait! What's that on the wall!? [Mike walks over to the wall of the cave where there are many drawing on the wall. Some of the drawings depict buffalos being hunted by little stick men with spears. Some of the drawings show little stick men dancing around a fire and using tools to build things.] Mike Marvelous: These must be the skeletons of the people who used to live here! Maybe cavemen... Jacob Jackson: No, it couldn't possibly be cave men, Michael! Unless they were frozen in ice. No...I'd say these are the skeletons of tribesmen or something...sometimes these islands in the middle of the ocean are home to primitive civilizations. Mike Marvelous: What if there are cannibals living here!?! Jacob Jackson: EEEEEK! CANNIBALS! Mike Marvelous: EEEEEEK! RUN AWAY! RUN AWAY! [The Can-Am Connection again run away, this time, they dodge the spikes hanging off of the ceiling of the cavern. Once they make their way to the outside of the cave, they jump in the sand and roll around, as if they have cooties on them or something] Jacob Jackson: EEEW! Dead guy cooties! Mike Marvelous: Get it off! Get it off! [Smoke begins to rise from the backs of the best friends as they roll around in the sand. They quickly spring to their feet and begin running towards the water] Mike and Jacob: OUCH! HOT SAND! HOT SAND! [They dive in the water and splash around in it for awhile] Mike Marvelous: Aaaaah...that's better. Hey...what's that floating in the water behind you? [Jacob turns around to see something black floating behind him] Jacob Jackson: It's my bag! WHOOO! It must have floated from the ship wreck! This is--....Michael....what's that floating behind you? [Mike turns around to see the dead corpse of the skipper floating face down in the water] Mike and Jacob: EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEK!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [They both run out of the water quicker than they've ever ran before, then run across the hot sand, then back into the cave] Mike Marvelous: Oh my gosh, Jacob! That was the captain! He's dead! GROSS! Jacob Jackson: Oh no...I left my bag! Mike Marvelous: Well, I'M not going back to get it! Not with that...thing...floating in the water! Jacob Jackson: But Michael...what if there's something in the bag that could save us? Mike Marvelous: Ok...we'll go together. But if he suddenly rises from the dead and tries to grab us, I'm gonna take 3 consecutive heart attacks! Jacob Jackson: Fair enough. [The camera focuses on the mouth of the cave as Mike and Jacob both stick their heads out of the cave, slowly, looking around to make sure everything is right. The body is still in the water, bobbing up and down. Mike and Jacob tip toe out of the cave and slowly walk down the beach to the edge of the beach] Mike Marvelous: I don't think El Capitain is gonna make it... Jacob Jackson: Jeez, what made you think THAT? [Jacob grabs a stick that is laying on the beach] Jacob Jackson: Time to do some fishing. [Jacob holds the stick out and tries to snag the bag with it so he doesn't have to go into the water with the corpse. He keeps trying but he just can't reach] Mike Marvelous: Here, Jacob. I'm taller. Let me try. [Jacob hands the stick to Marvelous, who after a couple tries, snags the bag and tows it to shore with the stick. Jacob grabs the bag] Jacob Jackson: There we go. Easy as pie. Now that wasn't so hard, was i-- [Suddenly, a huge shark lunges up out of the water and grabs the corpse of the captain in it's jaws.] Jacob Jackson: GOOD LORD! Mike Marvelous: You were saying? Jacob Jackson: Shut up. Ok...let's go see what we have in this bag. [Mike and Jacob begin to walk back to the cave but there's a man standing between them and the entrance. He is very skinny and has very shaggy hair. He is also holding a white volleyball with a red handprint on it with a face drawn on it. They just all stand there staring at each other] Tom Hanks: Wilson...do you think these guys are safe? Mike Marvelous: Who are you talking to? Tom Hanks: Wilson. Jacob Jackson: Who's Wilson? [Tom Hanks points to his volleyball friend] Mike Marvelous: Aaaaah...[whispers to Jacob] You distract him while I sneak up behind and hit him in the head with a rock. [Jacob nods] Jacob Jackson: So...what is YOUR name. Tom Hanks: Tom Hanks. Jacob Jackson: Oh! The famous actor! I loved your work in Sleepless in Seattle and Big! Tom Hanks:...Thanks. [While this is going on, Mike sidesteps slowly to the left, does a forward sommersault, hides behind a bush, crawls out of the bush on his stomach and then tip toes behind Tom and looks for a rock] Jacob Jackson: So...what are you doing here? Tom Hanks: I'm filming a movie. It's called Cast Away. Jacob Jackson: Umm...didn't that movie already come out like...a year and a half ago? Tom Hanks: It did? Jacob Jackson: ...Riiiiight. Anyhoo, can I have your autograph? [Jacob catches Mike out of the corner of his eye as he raises a rock above Tom's head] Jacob Jackson: MICHAEL! NO! *THUNK* [When the camera fades back in, Mike and Jacob are standing over Tom as he lies motionless on the sand] Jacob Jackson: I tried to tell you that he's a famous actor, but you wouldn't listen! Now we're responsible for the murder of Tom Hanks AND we're still stuck here! He probably could of helped us you know! Mike Marvelous: Yeah right, Jacob! You heard the guy! He was dillusional! Wilson? What kind of a crazy man talks to a stupid volleyball? Wilson: Who are you callin' a volleyball, punk? Mike Marvelous: Excuse me? Are you calling ME a pun--EEEEEEEEEK! It talked! Wilson: D'uh...of course I talked. Can't you see I have a mouth drawn on me? Jacob Jackson: You're just a volleyball! Balls can't talk! Wilson: Balls can't talk, huh? Your head is shaped like a ball. You can talk. Jacob Jackson:...He has a point. Mike Marvelous: Jacob, don't believe his lies! Wilson: You call me a liar again and things are gonna get messy. Sure, you killed Tom, but I'm gonna let that one slide. Nobody calls me a liar though. Mike Marvelous: You're a stupid volleyball! Wilson: Hey, I may be full of hot air, but I'll have you know I hold a degree from Yale. Mike Marvelous:..... Wilson: Ok, I'm joking about that one, but hey, I was once used in a girls volleyball game in the Yale court...close enough. Mike Marvelous: This is getting too weird... [Mike runs up to Wilson and kicks him into the ocean] Wilson: NOOOOOOOOooooooooooo........ Mike Marvelous: That takes care of that...so...what now? Jacob Jackson: I'm gonna check my bag to see if there's anything good in it! [Jacob opens it up and pulls out a shirt, some pants, a pair of fuzzy dice, a fire hydrant, a mountie strap on dildo, a whip....] Jacob Jackson: Hey! This isn't my bag! It must have got mixed up with someone elses! Mike Marvelous: [eyes dart side to side suspiciously] Not mine....ANYHOO! Let's go find some food! [Scene fades to black as the Can-Am Connection walk away looking for food] To be continued....
