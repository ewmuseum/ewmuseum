Trying To Train A Drunk: The Value of a Good AA Meeting (Lesson 2, Part 4)

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-17-02 The scene opens up on the outside of a huge building, when we see an older model black car pull up right out front. Everything is starting to turn dark outside, as the doors on the vehicle swing open and we see both Jesse Ramey and Asa Fountain stepping out of the vehicle. Jesse: (Closing his door) Why we’s gots to come here, Asa? Asa: Cause, cat daddy. This is going to be your first AA meeting. Jesse: I’s know, but whys I gots to come to it? Asa: Cause man, they give you good advice in these things and trust me, I’m sure it’ll help you out in our match on Sunday. Jesse: Oh, aights then. Let’s go get this here thing done and over wit. The two begin to make their way inside the building as many men and a couple women start making their way past Jesse and Asa. Some of them dressed very well, and other dressed rather scantily. Asa: (Looking at some of the people walking by him) See, Jesse, these people are just like you cat daddy. Some of the people begin looking at Asa rather weirdly as he says this, sending Asa into somewhat of a shock, because some of the people are giving him rather evil looks. Asa: (Looking at Jesse) Just forget I said that. The two continue to make their way into a large room, with a whole lot of chairs set up in it and a stage in the front of the room, where several men are sitting. Asa: (In bewilderment) I didn’t think these things had so many people attending them, but after all, this is a fairly big city. Jesse and Asa find themselves a couple of seats and sit down. Asa: Just sit still, cat daddy and I’m sure you’ll learn something from all of these fine people. Jesse: If’n you don’t mind me askin, how long you thinkin’ this be takin? Asa: Don’t worry about that, cat daddy. Just sit back and listen to what these people have to say, I’m sure they’ll have some groovy tips that you could start using. A man walks up to the podium set up on the rather large stage. Asa: Here ya go, this is going to be your first. Man: Hello, everyone, my name is Ted and I’ve been a member of this great organization for going on seven years now. Asa: (Eyes popping out of his head) What the fuck? These groups must not work to hell, my fine friend. How long you been drinking Jesse, man? Jesse: I’d done say about my whole life. Asa: That guy don’t look like he’s been drinking for more than a year and he’s been in this thing for going on seven. Jesse: Don’t they have them their twelve step thingies? Asa: Obviously not, cat daddy. This program must work on a strict twelve-year program or something along those lines. Man: This program has helped me solve a whole lot of problems in my life. Asa: Finally, now you’re going to get to hear some of the stories these people have to offer. Ted: Like that one time, when I came home and caught my wife in bed with another man. I couldn’t have done what I did that night if it hadn’t have been for the help of this great group. You gave me the confidence that I needed and you provided me with the things I need to take that scum bag mother fucker and that cheating whore out of my life. Asa: What the hell is this guy talking about? Another man walks up to the podium and just pats the man known as Ted on the back and asks him to go and have a seat. Other Man: Hello, everyone, I’m sure most of you know me as the founder of this group and the leader of this chapter and division. For those of you who don’t, I’m Winchester Alexander Jordan the Third and I asked Ted to come up here and share that story with you all on how much our chapter means to him. Jesse: What this man be talking bout? Asa: Jesse, man I don’t really know myself. This is all beginning to really bug me out. WAJ III: Now tonight we are going to be discussing the use of the AK-47. Asa’s eyes begin to bug out of his head once more as he stands up. Asa: (Yelling) Hey, hey, hey! What hell is this crap all about? WAJ III: (Looking over at Asa) What do you mean, my fine friend? Asa: Isn’t this supposed to be an AA meeting? WAJ III: A…A…? Asa: Yeah, don’t you cats know what Alcoholics Anonymous is? WAJ III: Oh, friend, I’m sorry, but you must have came into the wrong building or something of that nature. This is a meeting of the National Riffle Association. Asa: So, what you’re trying to tell me is that I brought my drunken friend into an NRA meeting? WAJ III: Yes, I’m afraid so. Asa: Oh, ain’t that some psychedelic shit. Come on Ramey, we’re getting out of this place. Jesse: (Looking over another person holding a pistol) Now, you’s say it’s aight for me to be carrying one of these here things and I’s allowed to use it on someone if they be attacking me? Man: Yes, sir. Jesse: (Turning back to Asa holding the pistol) Lookie here, Asa. I done went and got me some learning on an idea of how to win our match this week. Asa: Cat daddy, had that pistol back over to the nice man. Jesse: (With a sad look) But Asa, I done wanna give him back the perrty shiny thing back. Asa: (Leaning over to the other man) Look, groovy cat. Is that thing loaded? Man: Nope, I don’t bring loaded guns to the NRA meetings with me. Asa: (Leaning back up to Jesse) Alright, I guess you can keep it. Jesse: Woo, I done got myself a perrty little shiny thing. I bet you hit ‘em over the heads with it and then you’s win the match. Asa: Sure thing, cat daddy. Sure thing. Jesse and Asa get up and begin to make their way out of the building. Jesse: You know Asa, I done learned me a lot of things in that their meeting. I’s sure am glad you did bring me to it. Asa: Well, we’re needing to get on our way, groovy daddy. We’ve got that match and we’re needing to go ourselves ready for it. Jesse: (Waving the pistol in the air) I’ve done got myself ready for that match, I bet ya those two aren’t gonna know what hit ‘em. Asa: I’m sure they won’t, cat daddy. Asa and Jesse make their way out to Asa’s car and the scene fades to black.
