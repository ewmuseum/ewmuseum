Random Idiotic Behavior

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-05-02 ‘Lemme see now, the rear tires should be pastes into the rims…check…now I need to fit them onto the axle housing. Hmm, this could get ugly. Where did that glue go?” Timo Bolamba, The Perpetual Master of All Things Samoan sits alone in his workshop, taking a break from teaching Jake Estrada Jr. and moreover the rest of the world. He needed a day to relax in his favorite chair, listening to his favorite music, eating his favorite snacks, drinking his favorite drink, and partaking in his favorite hobby. Building cars. Well…Model cars. “Glue goes here, okay, man this is stickier than Big Daveylicious’ porn collection.” He stuck the wheels into the axle socket and the pressure of the liquid glue drove it back out, irritating him. He looked at it for several moments before trying again and getting the same result. Timo tapped his fingers on the table for several seconds before they began to stick. He pulled them up with a bit of grunting involved and cursed himself. “Okay car, I’m going to go like this…” He motions for the wheels to be slipped on the axle. “And you are going to not go like this…” He motions for them to fall off. “Got it?” Of course there is no response. Timo places the wheel on the car and it stays put. He puts it down and reaches to grab the next piece. The wheel falls off just as he grabs the next piece, the hood, with paint drying on it. Now nobody ever said Timo was a bright man, but the decision that will plague mankind forever was upon him. Drop the hood or grab the frustrating wheel? And of course, the resourceful Samoan knew exactly what to do. “Oh no you don’t!” Timo has just enough time to toss the hood in the air and grab the wheel. He smiles at himself as he sticks the opposite hand out to grab the wet hood which, of course, lands wrong side down in his hand. “Cock Knocker! Its like a Mike Marvelous spot on TV! It’s all half-assed and wrong!” The Samoan put the hood down and reached for a rag with his free hand. As he did, the wheel fell off and we begin all over again. Timo curses and plastic is heard being hurled. -One hour later- Timo now has paint all over himself, and half a dual exhaust stuck to his shirt. He doesn’t let it phase him as he reads his directions, smiling, finally having conquered the wheel that plagued him so. “Okay, put the drive shaft into the transmission, and glue into place. Follow by putting the differential on the other end. That doesn’t sound so hard, how bad can it be?” He moves to put the instructions down, but they are stuck to his fingers. Timo shakes vigorously, but to no avail. Finally he resorts to ripping the finger marks off and sighing. The Samoan rips the paper off his fingertips and reaches for the transmission piece, which is attached to the engine. “Lemme see, yeah that looks about right. Now for the drive shaft and axle.” He puts it in to test fit and it seems to work all right. So moving swiftly, he reaches for his glue, and apparently it decides it needs to get out more than he wants it to, and he feels gobs of the non toxic adhesive come out of the tube. He dabs the very end in a puddle of glue and sighs. “Damn glue, reminds me of Jake Jackson at a nudy bar. Aww man, that’s just sick now that I think of it.” He puts the parts into place and amazingly they seem to work without trouble. The relieved Samoan puts the assembly down to dry and reaches for a can of his favorite drink Shasta’s Tiki Punch. He takes a rather large gulp and lets out a sigh of relief smacking his lips. The Samoan tries to put the can down, and as you may have guessed by the foreshadowing, it is stuck to his hand. Cursing does him no good, as well as the karate chops through mid air to relieve the raw Samoan drink from his hand. He gives it one final heave ho and with a little gumption it flies off, up in the air, and behind him, striking the assembly directly on, causing it to fall apart. Then Timo screamed. -Several hours later- Timo arrives inside the house with his hands glued to his forehead, if you can figure out how the door was opened, you are a better man than I. Jake looks over his shoulder and has an expression on his face as if to say “And I’m learning from HIM?” “I don’t want to hear it, just get me some mineral spirits.” “You sure you don’t want a picture of this? “You want me to smack you Jake?” Jake laughs and taunts Timo a bit, getting in his face and sticking his tongue out at The Samoan. The young Latino man laughs and stays just in front of Timo, dancing like a buffoon. “Smack me? And how, exactly do you intend to do tha….ERGH!” Jake hits the ground holding his unit and Timo smiles, letting his leg descend from where Jake’s crotch was. “Lesson number 5, there’s more than one way to skin a cat.” He walks past Jake on the ground and on to the next room as the scene fades out. We are greeted with a view of a frustrated Timo as he stares at a giant fingerprint in the cherry paint of his model car. The Samoan just stares, nothing more. He does not speak, gripe, groan, complain, or even furrow his brow. He just stares. The model car is nearly complete now, and two large handprints reside on his forehead. He simply sighs at this point as he looks on at his creation and how it has been marred. Timo finally breaks the silence with a few words that are sure to inspire all. “This is worse than an Epitome of Evil promo.” -Several MORE hours later- “Ha…haha…HAHAHA!! I’m done! Done I say!” As The Samoan begins his victory dance, the cars back wheels fall off and he stops, staring at the small plastic vehicle as it happens in slow motion. First the right wheel comes apart from the assembly, then rolls away, followed by the left. Timo stops, and his eye twitches slightly. “TIMOSTREAK SMASH!!” The meaty Samoan paw raises up, then comes back down with force as he grunts and roars, beating his chest. The myriad of stray model paint makes him look like an African native warrior marked up for battle The giant handprints on his forehead look like tribal markings. He feels his body bulk up to the size represented by his “FSW Company Photo”, which is far from reality, and he begins to mangle the small plastic model car. He mashes the table to splinters and beats his chest. The paint bottles all at once go flying across the room with a flick of his wrist. Paintbrushes suffice as makeshift tusks as he roars like an elephant and charges across the room, running his head through a wall, and a poster with “The Five Star Five” on it, with a big X over the picture. The Wild Samoan turns as he hears a door open, and his wild eyes stare at Jake and Gwenneth looking at the beast below, and the smashed model car. “WHAT DO YOU WANT BOYO?” “Timo, what the hell are you doing?” Timo stops to gather his thoughts, and after half a second, he looks back to her and pulls the paintbrushes from his mouth. Timo looks around the room, and his inflated perception of himself drops a bit. He smiles sheepishly at the mess, and then walks over to his model car, which is completely smashed. He picks up the glue bottle and, as if they weren’t there, goes about repairing it. “Jesus Timo, why do you build those things?” “Because it relaxes me?” She throws her hands up and walks out of the room. Jake shields his unit and sticks his tongue out. Timo jumps a bit in his direction, and Jake scoots in the other direction, leaving The Samoan alone. He hears a rumbly in his tumbly and grins. “What’s that old buddy?” His stomach rumbles again. “Yeah I’d really like to help you, but we have a small problem.” His stomach grumbles at him again and he shrugs. “Yeah I know, I am kindof ashamed of it too.” One final time the stomach growls and he sighs. “Okay, okay. As soon as I get my hands unstuck from the table, we will eat.”
