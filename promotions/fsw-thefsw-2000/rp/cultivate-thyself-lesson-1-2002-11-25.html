Cultivate Thyself – Lesson 1

25 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 25 of 2002 A.D. (Excerpted from the November 17 sermon issued by High Priest Bruce Shanahan of the First Church of Their Starry Eminences) The world doesn’t care. Mankind doesn’t care. God doesn’t care. That, my friends, is the basis upon which this glorious organization was chartered. Our peers traversing this foul planet care nothing for you or your dreams. They will spit on you, walk all over you, and even take your life in order to attain social ascension. And to compensate for their shortcomings, they insist you pray to their ‘God who art in Heaven’. And what they, the whole of mortality, feel so inclined to dismiss on a whim, those same downtrodden fools claim their divine master will happily grant unto any who may beseech him. My friends, I am here to declare to you that these statements are indeed a falsehood. A broad deception designed to instill hope where there is none, and nothing more of significance. Now many of you, my followers, witnessed my actions on the November 14 edition of FSW Glory. I have had a sizable throng of you, my glorious disciples and brothers under the Great Old Ones, inquire as to the purpose of the kidnapping and subsequent emotional persecution of the innocent Epiphany. Some of you, primarily the newest of the faithful, failed to see the reasoning behind such a sadistic action. My deeds were viewed as cruel badgering of an innocent fueled by cold blooded hatred for another. That is to be expected of those who are novice in this great and ultimately noble faith. And contrary to what you may believe or be told by the heathens outside this holy fraternity, I respect your opinions. I ask now that you try to peer deeper into the core symbolism behind young Epiphany’s abduction and please try to connect it with my opening statement. You must come to grips with one key concept before you can further progress in your devotion to the glorious Old Ones. No one is innocent. That includes you, that includes me, and that above all incorporates every other wretched, despicable mongrel of God wandering the streets and the sands of our fair Earth. We have all committed crimes against our true masters beyond the stars. So what is the answer to attaining full deliverance in the eyes of Great Cthulhu, glorious Tsathoggua, the majestic demon-sultan Azathoth? I fear there is no clear answer. My children, the greatest advice I, as your highest priest, can offer, is to cultivate thyself. Wash away the dogma instilled in your minds and souls from day one by this heathen society. Forget the empty promises by the Christians and the Jews, deny the tarnished gospel of the Mormons and Muslims. And do so with pride, for with your open resentment for these false devotions shall come the blessing of the Great Old Ones! We live in a harsh and ultimately desolate world. Mankind endures an existence where life is taken on a daily basis for the sake of greed, mercilessly torn from you without warning for the fulfillment and the blackest of avarice! My friends, it is all a fruitless journey in the quest for might, money and power that repeats in unending cycle. Man lives in order to carry out a few simple capacities: we breed, and we kill. My children, do you know the most revolting aspect of that vile sequence? All of the other ‘faiths’, beliefs which preach tolerance and brotherhood and serenity among men.....they are just as guilty as those they condemn. The ancient Crusades, an edict from the Christian God, were carried out as religious genocide for the purpose of regaining lost lands. Islam condones suicide……if, in fact, enemy lives are taken as atonement. Around the world every wretched day, wars and battles occur in the name of faith. And no one is safe. Christian, Muslim, Pagan, Scientologist, Hindu, Mormon, Atheist…..none are exempt from the mass slaughter erupting across our ravaged planet. It is the eternal Jihad, and those who find themselves without alliance are no exception to casualty. We must retaliate. It is the way of the Old Ones, and so it has been decreed. Innocence is a fabrication, for in this vile and malicious race there exists no man who may be blameless. You willingly take up arms and war with your oppressors, or you die broken and without resistance. My friends, I tell you that the Old Ones offer all of us the shelter and the weaponry we need in order to sustain our survival until their glorious return onto this dying world. They give solitude for all who shall accept and worship them, and offer sacrifice unto their eminence. And they instill great power in their servants, infusing the very power to crush the heathen hypocrites surrounding us! There is no reason to mourn. And to understand why, I again ask you to look within and dispense with the creeds of which you have been conditioned into believing. Ignore those imprudent doctrines and hear me now, for my words are those of Nyarlathotep himself. There is no sin. Yes, my children, Epiphany is no more innocent than you and I. Nor is she any less. Her capture 2 weeks ago was attempted proof of that. That faithless harlot committed only one crime, the only wrong to truly exist in this life of vague commandments and debated sins. And that is her denial of the Old Ones as the one true collected Eminence. Murder, hate, perversion and violence are inherent parts of our nature. They are not flaws, my children, nor are they foreign compulsions implanted in us by a pitchfork-wielding imp. We were created with the impulse leading us to quote-unquote “sin”, and we are merely fulfilling that drive. The only real iniquity is the urge to deny the Old Ones. I ask you today, especially of those formerly devout to the common “pure” and “holy” deities whose poisonous gospel has tainted the earth…..on which side will you be standing when the end days arrive, and the shadow of Great Cthulhu eclipses the tainted landscape of the Earth? Will you deny the Great Old Ones and believe in some foolish ‘Rapture’? Go into hiding, delaying the inevitable purging of humanity’s filth? Or will you wage war on the side of your true masters from beyond the stars? I ask that you think about this query. At this time we shall partake in communion as we bow before the altar of Great Cthulhu. Brother Asenath, as we do so I ask now that you bring forth the virgin sacrifice. With this virgin blood, the glorious Master of R’lyeh may slake his hunger and sanctify us with his divine blessing....
