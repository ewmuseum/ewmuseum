Battle Rappin' (Or is it?)

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 14th, 2002 ~Wednesday’s Glory turned out to be quite a turn around for Asa and Jesse. After weeks of aggravation and loss, they finally won a match against the two potheads, Pablo and Fumar, with a little help from Ming and Krieg. Now begins a different problem. Aside from Asa and Jesse’s win, they apparently stirred some bad blood between Big Daveylicious and Leon Chambers, seeing as how they got their asses handed to them backstage at Glory. Now the fro and the drunk have a career defining match against Big D and Leon for the number one contendership for the tag team titles at Genesis. Who will come out on top? You’ll have to check out Genesis for that.~ ~Anyway, we now see Asa and Jesse walking down the streets of Boston. Asa is clad in yellow wind pants and an orange t-shirt. He picks at his fro as he walks down the street. Next to him is his partner Jesse, sporting overall jeans with red suspenders. Aside from that, Jesse is also carrying his trademark moonshine jug, which he repeatedly sips out of as they walk.~ Jesse: Are you sure bou’ where we be goin’? *Sips from jug* Asa: Sure I am jive turkey! Look, I know I left our ride somewhere beyond this block here. *Stops at a dark alley* Jesse: *Sips from jug* Then why are we stoppin’ here? We shoulda done gone around Asa. Asa: Don’t worry about that cat daddy, this be a shortcut! Jesse: I donno… Asa: Ah, quit complainin’ disco daddy, let’s go. ~Asa grabs Jesse by the arm, and they enter the dark alleyway. The alley is pretty dark.~ Jesse: Aww Asa, I can’t see. Asa: Just follow me my man. ~The two continue on, until a match suddenly strikes, lighting up the alley. Asa and Jesse stop to meet a gang of five black men, all wearing white wife beaters and black bandanas. The first four are about Asa and Jesse’s size, but one in the back is exceptionally large. The one in front walks up to the two with a sneer on his face.~ Man: What you two crackas think you be doin’ in our alley? Jesse: We be needin’ to get across this here block to get to our vehicle. *Sips from his jug* Asa: Yeah disco daddies, we got ourselves a 69’ Boss 302 waitin’ across this here block. Man: That’s your ride, cracka? Damn dawg, you shouldn’t have told me dat. I’m Tony D, and we be the blackhawks. NOBODY enters our alley fool, so you betta just hand over da keys to yo ride, unless you wanna do it da hard way. Jesse: Why, I’ma whup yo’ ass! ~Jesse lunges for Tony, but is held back by Asa.~ Asa: Let me handle this, jive turkey. ~Asa steps up in front of Tony D.~ Asa: Now listen here, jive turkey. We don’t want any trouble, so why don’t we make a deal that be a little, less violent? ~Tony D scratches his chin, then his eyes light up.~ Tony: Aight, here’s the flo. You take me on in a battle rap nigga. You win, you pass freely. We win, we get ya ride. Deal? ~Asa is silent for a moment, then reaches out his hand.~ Asa: *Shaking Tony’s hand* Deal. After you disco daddy. ~Tony’s gang lays down a phat beat, then Tony begins his rap.~ Tony: Yo, nigga, whtchoo wanna do? You try ta walk in here, Whut? You play me for a foo’? Ima slit ya fuckin’ throat, And hang yo ass from the wall, And if that doesn’t work, then it be time to ball. Ya see nigga, you ain’t got da flo ta play dis game, I’ll knock yo ass straight out, Just like it’s all a game. So turn yo’ ass around, And toss me yo keys, Cuz I be Tony D, and I do damn well what I please! ~Tony’s gang cheers as he gives high fives to each of them. Asa nods, then begins his rap.~ Asa: *gulp* Here it goes… ~Jesse begins to lay down the ugliest beat you’d ever hear. Asa tries to go along with it as he begins his rap.~ Asa: Uh, yo yo yo, what’s that smell? Uh, I’ma send you straight to…hell. Yeah, bitch. Uh huh, yeah. ~Asa shakes his head, and waves his hands around.~ Asa: Stop stop stop! Alright, you wanna play this game, jive turkey? Well you may have the flow, but I here got the soul, so I’ma do this my way. ~Tony D nods confidently, as Asa whispers in Jesse’s ear. Jesse smiles, and nods. After taking one more swig of moonshine, Jesse lays down the flyest disco beat you’d ever hear. Asa nods his head to the beat, then begins his BATTLE DISCO!!!!!~ Asa: Never in my life have I seen such a lack of sooooouuuul. Jesse: Bow wow wow wow, chicka chicka, bow wow wow wow. Asa: So if you wanna die, then I’d be glad to dig ya hoooolllee. Jesse: Bow wow wow wow, chicka chicka, bow wow wow wow. Asa: You take this alley for yourself, and do this bulllshiiit. Jesse: Bow wow wow wow, chicka chicka, bow wow wow wow. Asa: When it’s fact, and don’t forget, you’re just a crack addiiiict. ~Jesse let’s out a WOOT, as Tony’s gang’s confident look begins to fade.~ Asa: It’s a shame that you pollute the streets of Booostooon. Jesse: Bow wow wow wow, chicka chicka, bow wow wow wow. Asa: So if you don’t smarten up, I’ll kick you in the chiiiin. Jesse: Bow wow wow wow, chicka chicka, bow wow wow wow. Asa: You claim to be the man, but you don’t even knooooow. Jesse: Bow wow wow wow, chicka chicka, bow wow wow wow. Asa: That when you mess with da fro, man you gotta gooooooo!!!!!! ~The magnitude of Asa’s disco is too much for Tony and his gang to handle, so they are instantly thrown into the opposite end of the alley, do to the powerful force that is “the jive”. Jesse’s cheers as Asa’s wipes his hands together. The two walk over to the fallen gang.~ Tony: *Unngh* Oh man, you win man…*Ooof* Asa: Thanks disco daddy. ~Asa and Jesse tip off the gang as they exit the alley~ Asa: You know jive turkey, we made a pretty good team back there. Jesse: *Sipping from moonshine* Does be meanin' that we a team?? Asa: Ya damn straight cat daddy, and just the right team to beat the hell out of those bitch daddies, Daveylicious and Chambers! ~The two give each other a vicotry fist knock, and exit the alley as the scene ends.~ ~Fade Out~
