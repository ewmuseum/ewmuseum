S.T.V. (ShowTime T.V.) v. 1

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/1/02 We open up in New York City at Times Square. As usual all the lights are lit up and traffic is is packed. We cut down to the streets to see 'ShowTime' Erik Blake standing next to a tall building with a black suit on and a microphone. On the microphone it says 'S.T.V.' ShowTime raises the mic to his mouth as the camera pans in even closer. 'Hello, FSW fans! You all know who I am so I won't bother introducing myself. Ah, screw it. I'm 'ShowTime' Erik Blake and I'm the host of the new S.T.V. ShowTime T.V. S.T.V. goes deep into celebrities' lives deeper than VH1's behind the music. Also we tour cities and see how much of a dump they really are.' ShowTime does a fake T.V. chuckle like he is from Harvard or something as some New Yorkers walk past and smirk at him. 'Oh, what are ya'll smirking at? New York ain't that dayum good...whores.' ShowTime says. Suddenly the camera spots the TRL building and Carson Daley is looking outside to the ecstatic fans outside cheering for him. ShowTime grunts as the fans begin to scream louder for Carson Daley. 'Who does this guy think he is? Hogging all of the fans...I'll show him.' ShowTime says as he crosses the street and enters the building from the side where for some odd reason...security isn't seen. He heads up a flight of stairs and enters backstage. Multiple stage crewhands attempt to stop him until they think of what the ratings could do...so they let him pass. ShowTime walks in front of the TRL camera with his microphone. 'Whoa...Whoa...Whoa. Wait a cotton-pickened minute.' ShowTime says as he backs up right next to Carson Daley. 'Uh, excuse me...what are you doing?' Carson Daley says confused. 'Huh? Oh, I'm crashing your show. Now do you mind? I'm trying to run a show here.' ShowTime says as he turns to his camera totally ignoring what Carson Daley is saying. 'Wait, wait, wait. Who are you? And why are you crashing my show?' Carson Daley asks. 'Pfft,obviously you are shelled from the world. I'm ShowTime Erik Blake. I own S.T.V.' ShowTime says. 'Uh, what? S.T.V.?' Daley asks... 'ShowTime T.V. Carson! Jeez...' ShowTime says. 'Ok, why are you crashing my show?' Daley asks again. 'You know, Carson, you ask a lot of pointless questions. And you are very rude too...I'm trying to run my show and your interupting me. I don't even want to be on your show anymore...' ShowTime says as he leaves the building. '...Huh? He...he...was never invited.' Carson Daley says as the fans start to get up and leave and boo Carson Daley for being mean to ShowTime Erik Blake. We open back up outside where ShowTime is sitting on the curb outside of the TRL building. He looks up at the window at Carson Daley who is mouthing to him he is sorry but ShowTime turns his head swiftly and meanly. 'Until next time...This is ShowTime Erik Blake with version 1 of S.T.V. signing off...Holla Back.' ShowTime says as the scene fades.
