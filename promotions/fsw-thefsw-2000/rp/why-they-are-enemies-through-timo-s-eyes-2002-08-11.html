Why They Are Enemies...Through Timo's Eyes.

11 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-11-02 Timo Bolamba, the all knowing soothe sayer that sayeth soothe had to meet someone today. Not just anyone either, it was his current enemy du jour, Mike Marvelous, or MarveLOSER, as he liked to be called. They both see each other in the entry way of “Rod’s” and as they are seated, Timo looks over to the fellow FSW member. 'So, I see you did decide to show up. So why did you choose this place anyhow? The atmosphere is rather...odd.' 'Big D recommended it to me...he said it had free bread with the meal.” Timo thought about it and shrugged. “Free bread is always a good thing.” Mike eyed Timo suspiciously, and the Samoan almost had to wonder what he was going to say next. As luck would have it, he didn’t have to wait long for that to happen however, seeing as Mike was never short of words. 'They should give us free drinks too for the price of this food...sheesh, 16 bucks for a steak! They don't have ridiculous prices like this up in Canada...' 'Out in Samoa, we just carve what we want off the steak and ride the rest home.' Mike looks on strangely at Timo, getting under the Samoans skin a little. 'What? Okay, well maybe not, but I have always wanted to try.' Timo tries to force a smile as Mike replies to him. He can’t help but wonder what exactly Mike is after here, but it has something to do with the belt, of that he is sure. 'Hey...I thought you Samoans only ate...you know...raw meat.' 'Raw? No man, they only do that on Somata, which is the island like an uncle nobody talks about. Regardless, I think we have something else to talk about here, so why don’t we get on with it?' As Mike gives Timo a dirty look, The Samoan wonders what is on his mind. It would seem he did not have long to wait for the explanation. 'Yes, it seems we do have something to talk about...my title shot at Genesis for one.' Timo snorts. “YOUR title shot? I think you are mistaken my friend. You see, when it was all said and done I was the one with the arm raised, not you.” Mike seems to get huffy about Timo’s words, and quickly fires back a retort. 'What are you talking about Bojumba!? I had my arm raised by the official referee then Canadian Criminal, a man who had no right to be there, ran in and raised your arm! So therefore, I WON!' “Bojumba? I’ll Bojumba you MarveLOSER!” Timo stands up and grabs a loaf of Italian bread, swinging it in the air in a menacing manner. Just then, the waiter walks up and nervously steps in between Timo and Mike. He glances around then pipes up. “Hey, can I get you fellas anything?” Timo growls and sits back down, putting the bread away, but never keeping it out of arms reach, He looks at the menu as Mike places an order. “Umm...I'll have the moose steak with mushrooms and onions, and a baked potato instead of fries. And our tray of free bread...' “And for you sir?” Timo looks down and hastily orders. “Umm, I’ll have the Buffalo Wings, a Smirnoff Ice, and fries instead of a baked potato. Suck that you Canadian Ponce!” As the waiter nods and begins to walk away, Mike reaches over to him and tugs on his pant leg. The man turns around and looks at “Five Star”, wondering what he wants. 'And waiter...can you get my friend here a bib? I'm sure he eats like a pig, being from Samoa and all!' Timo wastes no time in defending himself, but the words that escape his mouth can seldom be construed as logic, so he ends up sounding like an idiot yet again. “Hey, I told you Marvelous, I only ate out of the trough on a dare.” Timo glares at Mike as the waiter finishes writing up and the Canadian finishes his order. 'Haw haw...anyway, can you bring me a glass of sprite too? Thank you.' The waiter turns around and leaves after writing down the order. Mike glares at Timo and frowns 'Did you have to be so rude in front of the waiter!?' Timo reaches for the French Bread with his right hand, with a menacing look in his eyes. 'Timo...why are you looking at me like that...' Without a word, the bread swings forth, toward Mike’s unsuspecting head. There is a sickening thwack as the bread hits Mike across the forehead, and he rubs it wondering what the hell just happened. 'OUCH! Did you just hit me with a loaf of bread!?!' “Who’s the monkey now?!” 'GAAAAAH!' Mike lunges across the table and begins choking Timo with both hands as the glasses and utensils rattle over the table and the cover almost falls off. Timo reaches up and pokes for Mikes eyes with the classic Rowdy Roddy Piper eye gouge. Mike's head arches back and he let's out a howl of pain. He immediately reaches for a glass of water and throws the contents into Timo's face. Timo runs around in circles rubbing his face, making a scene and knocking over tables, chairs and whatever else gets in his way. “I’M MELTING…MELTING!!!!” Mike screams at Timo as a crowd gathers. 'Take that, you louse!' Mike gets up out of his seat with a concerned look on his face. He walks slowly but cautiously over to Timo, who is writhing on the ground, and taps him on the shoulder 'Are...are you ok? I didn't mean to--' Timo stops making a scene and swings for the Royal Crown Jewels of Indonesia, striking Mike dead on and watching him drop like a bad pick up line from Big Daveyliscious’ mouth. Timo stands up and watches Mike writhe in pain. “Man, you sure don’t give a guy much of a target.” Timo begins to laugh as Mike twitches on the ground, but in his laughter, he doesn't notice Marvelous recover...and he doesn't notice the boot rocketing directly for his genital area. He suddenly feels the force of his unit being crushed, and he falls on the ground faster than Tim Walden. The Samoan rolls around, clutching his main vein, cursing in Samoan. Mike springs to his feet, grabs a chair, holds it over his head and is about to bring it down on the Samoan Silencer when...the waiter walks up with the food and places it on the table. The waiter looks at the both of the combatants like they belong in a mental institution, and shrugs, walking away. Timo stops what he is doing, as he gets a whiff of the food on the table. “Hey…….those buffalo wings look tasty…” Mike and Timo both scramble towards the table and jump on their seats. They begin eating their food, but as Timo chews the meat off the bones and Mike cuts off chunks of his steak, they never take their eyes off each other. They both stare at each other suspiciously. 'These are some good wings…” Mike glares at Timo, and Timo glares back. 'Yes...and this is a fine steak...could you pass the salt?' Timo reaches for the salt and has a moment of inspiration. “Look Mike, an eagle!” Mike quickly whips his head back to see America's official bird. Timo unscrews the top of the salt shaker just far enough, then waits for Mike to turn back. “I don’t see any eagle.” “My mistake…friend…” Timo glares mischievously as he hands Mike the tampered salt. “Here is that salt you wanted.” Mike looks at the salt, then looks at Timo, then reluctantly accepts. 'Thank you. It appears you actually have manners. I must say, I'm impressed' Mike goes to sprinkle a couple of dashes of salt on his steak and potato when the top falls off and a shower of salt falls on top of the meal, forming a white pile. Mike looks at his now-ruined meal, then at Timo, and his face begins to turn red. 'Enjoy your meal, Mike.' Marvelous lets out a long sigh, picks up the steak, and brushes some of the salt off. Timo shakes his head, smiling, then begins eating his wings again. As he eats, he sees the reflection of Mike in his glass raising his fork in the air to throw it. Timo looks up just in time to see the fork flying straight at his head, but he bends backwards Matrix style and the fork whizzes by and stick in some lady's purse. 'Are you crazy you idiot? You could poke someone's eye out with that thing!' Mike grits his teeth together 'Well, you shouldn't have dodged it, then!' Timo tries to compose himself a bit, and calms down, he looks over and holds his hands up in truce. Suddenly, he seems to notice something he likes and he speaks up. “Say Mike, that’s a nice pair of pants.” A smile replaces Mike's frown and he looks down at his pants in self admiration. 'Really? Yeah, I sort of like them too. I got em' from JC Penny's.' “Yeah? What are those made of? They look comfortable?” Mikes answer is short, and to the point. 'They are comfortable! They're 100% Polyester!' Timo continues his analysis, undaunted in his quiz of the pants. “Polyester? You must have paid a pretty penny for them.” 'Yup...for these particular pants it was 60 dollars Canadian.' Timo’s eyes light up, smiling a bit. He nods and eats a bit of bread. The Samoan points and seems he has a question. “I have one more question for you.” Mike looks confused. 'Uh...ask away.' Timo gets a shit faced grin. “Do they make them for men?” The smile suddenly drops off Mike's face. The big vein in his forehead begins to bulge out and his left eye begins to twitch. Timo can't help but chuckle to himself as Mike secretly pictures Timo being eaten alive by wild muskrats in his head. 'You think you're soooo funny. Well...I got news for you.....you're....NOT!' 'I thought it was pretty funny.' Timo laughs to himself, then seems like he has something else to say. 'You know, I saw you and Jake Jackson walking in the hall the other day and it reminded me of a Raisin Bran commercial.' A puzzled look comes over Mike's face. 'What the heck are you talking about?' Timo chuckles to himself again as he thinks about it. “Two Scoops of Fruit!!” Mike suddenly snaps and pounds his fist on the table. 'I don't know why I ever agreed to meet a savage like you in a public place! I don't know...maybe it was wishful thinking that you'd recognize that I pinned you and I deserve the title shot at Genesis. Well, it looks like I was wrong!' Timo stops laughing and sighs a bit. He looks over at Mike and nods, again holding up the truce hands. “Okay, you are right Mike, here I am making a fool out of you, and you came out here on your own will. Tell you what, I’m sorry, have a roll or something so you don’t get hungry. They really are quite exquisite.” He reaches for a Tabasco laden roll and offers it to Mike, straight faced. Mike accepts the roll and lets out a long sigh. 'I'm glad that you've decided to quit your childish ways so we can get down to business.' Mike brings the roll up to his lips and takes a bite. He chews it a couple of times, but instead of the normal expression of bliss worn by regular roll eaters, he has a look of wide eyed horror. His eyes begin to water and his cheeks begin to suck in as the Tabasco sauce works it's magic. Sweat begins to trickle down his brow. Timo breaks out laughing. 'Hey MARVELoser...hot enough for ya?' Mike suddenly spits out the roll and dumps his glass of sprite down his throat, but it doesn't do much to help. 'WA-HER! WA-HER! I EED WA-HER!' Timo jumps up and cups his hands to his mouth. 'Quick, someone, this man needs help, can someone get him a drink?' A tall man dressed in a flamboyant shirt turns around, and its apparent Tim has figured out where they are. The man walks over and wraps his arm around Mike, then speaks with a high girly voice, complete with a lisp. 'I can buy you a drink sexy.' Mike looks at the strange man with a look of cock-eyed confusement and pain, then it suddenly dons on him. There is not a woman in this place. The woman who's purse the fork got stuck in is actually a man dressed up in women's clothing wearing a wig. The male bartender is dressed in a white tank top. Mike quickly backs away from the gay man trying to buy him a drink, and suddenly, he forgets about his burning mouth 'EEEEEEEK! THIS IS A.....A....A....' The gay guy pipes up. 'Really happenin' place!' As Timo laughs at the plight, he feels a hand grab his ass, he turns around to see a 6’8” 290 lb. black man standing behind him and his smile instantly drops. “Hey there, you feel like some Alabama Blacksnake?” “Oh Dear God.” Timo looks at Mike, Mike looks at Timo and they both come to one mutual agreement with their eyes: They have GOT to get out of this place! Mike and Timo: 'EEEEEEEEE!!!!' Both men run out of the lounge without even paying the bill. Two very gay bouncers stand in their way, but they are easily dispatched by a Canadian Quick Kick and a Cave-In. They both hit the doors and book down the street with a mob of horny flaming gays after them in hot pursuit. “Run Mike, Run for your life….and your Sweet Virgin Ass!” The Canadian looks absolutely horrified. 'Oh God! Oh God! I want my mommy!' Timo looks over his shoulder and notices they have left the pack of homosexual men far behind them. He stops with a huff and puff, as does Mike and they look at one another. Timo is the first to speak. “I think its safe to say we should never speak of this incident again.” Mike doesn't bother answering, but rather nods in agreement and wipes a drip of sweat off his brow. Timo looks over at Mike and makes sure nobody has followed them. He makes a sour face and Mike looks at him trying to figure out what he is doing. “What’s the problem?” Timo hesitates to answer. “What the hell did you do now?” “You remember how we parked at the bar?” Mike’s face droops. “Yeah…” “When you weren’t looking I let all the air out of your tires.” Mike, too tired to get angry, simply bows his head and lets out a moan. “Tell you what, lets go back, I will give you a ride, it’s the least I can do.” '...Ok. Bloody decent of you.' They get back to the car and the gay men come storming out again, horny for butt loving. Timo quickly starts the car and as Mike opens the door Timo shouts to him. “Remember calling me Rotunda?” Mike looks at him like ‘Now is not the time’, but he persists. “It was Bojumba, but why does that matter?” Timo looks back nervously and then back to Mike. “Well Mikey, in this business, sometimes a wrestler needs to pay the piper…and that wrestler is you!” He hammers the gas, and the door rips from Mikes hand. Timo speeds away, leaving Mike trying to get into his car, which has gum jammed into the keyholes. Mike looks over his shoulder before running from the gay mob and screaming into the night… “TIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIMMMMMMMMMMMMOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!”
