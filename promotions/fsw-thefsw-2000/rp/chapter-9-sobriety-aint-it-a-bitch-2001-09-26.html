Chapter 9: Sobriety, Aint It A Bitch?

|Chapter 9: Sobriety, Aint It A Bitch?|
Mike P2K Frampton | 09/26/01
Chapter 9

Mike "P2K" Frampton has been sober for a long time now. But his brother, Sean "TJ Real" Frampton has been a secret alcohol abuser for some time now. But recently he has been forgetting his memory. Could this be related to all the drinking? The fact that Mike has been catching on mean Sean has become a slave to his booze?

"Sean!" said Mike. "What do you want?" bellowed Sean. "You're drunk again aren't you?" replied Mike. "What's it to you?" asked Sean. "Are you going to answer?" asked Mike. "Why do you keep answering with another question?" asked Sean. "Why do you ask?" replied Mike. "Shut up." bellowed Sean.

Mike required 10 stitches in the forehead that night. Sean needed his stomach pumped. After a bottle of Vodka it was no suprise Sean had a tremendous hangover. But he did not notice it because he spent half the day passed out in a hospital bed. But the scary thing was Mike being attacked by his own brother. But was it Sean? Or was it the Vodka who attacked Mike? These questions needed to be answered. So of course, Mike did not wait for Sean to recover. He barged into his room as Sean woke up.

"Why the fuck did you do that Sean? I want answers before I beat the living shit out of you, Brother!" attacked Mike. "What did I do?" inquired Sean. "You know damn right what you did to me." yelled Mike. "No, what did I do to you?" asked Sean. "Bastard. You threw a damn Vodka bottle at my head and caused me to get 10 stitches in the head. No, no, you can not do that. You have to get off of the shit. No more alcohol." said Mike. "I don't think I can do that." replied Sean. "You better." started Mike. "Or else I will decimate you and not leave any evidence." finished Mike.

Well that did it. Sean started the long agonzing road to Sobriety. But could he do it? Could he overcome the obsticles it proposed?
