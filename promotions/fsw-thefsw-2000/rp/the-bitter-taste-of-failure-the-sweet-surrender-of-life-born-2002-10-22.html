The Bitter taste of Failure, The Sweet surrender of Life born Anew~

22 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-22-02 ~Your family turns on you, you lose your faith, it’s time for change! But where to go from here?~ An average office, set with a desk, two chairs sitting adjacent to the desk, two lamps in each of the rear corners, and a fumbled mess thrown around the floor. The shades hang dangling from the windows keeping all light at bay, waiting patiently outside of the room for its opportunity to break in. As the wind blows softly into the shades, they break apart, and after further examining the situation, the light eerily floats, remaining outside, for the atmosphere inside the domicile is too dreary for even the brightest light to reconcile. Shattering through the deafening silence the phone rings out, offering the slightest change in atmosphere, but being ignored, as is everything else, while the fifth rings brings to life the answering machine message. “You’ve reached the office of Fredric Mandela. Your call is greatly appreciated, please leave your name, number, and a brief message and I shall get back to you as soon as possible.” ~Beeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeep~ “Lt. Mandela!” a voice echoes out in complete rage. “You know who this is and you know why I’m calling. Your mission in the United States has been compromised, and after seeing your last appearance in the FSW, we have come to the conclusion that Zameer’s wrestling career has now too become a joke!” The voice disappears for a minute’s time, as mumbling can be heard in the background, apparently giving orders to the man on the phone. “Your new orders are to return to Malawi, Africa within 4 days time, and report for your punishment, what you do with Zameer is entirely up to you, but how you handle him may inversely affect your disciplining here! You knew full well the repercussions shall you fail, now own up to your fate. I trust, no, we trust, we will see you here soon.” There is a bizarre pause before the closing of the phone call, which is simply, “goodbye”. The camera zooms in on the phone as it once again falls silent, and then pans over to the corner where it picks up the form of a battered Lt. Mandela. He sits in his customary military garb, his head, fallen heavy into his hands out of shame. Silently he sits, almost expecting, or preferring that he just fall dead any second, until Azala Zameer steps out of a dark shadow behind him. Applying a supporting hand upon the Lieutenant’s shoulder, Mandela begins to mumble into his hands. “I’ve let you down, I’ve disgraced my family, disgraced my nation, but most of all, I took an innocent child, fed him full of lies, and led him to the lamb to be slaughtered” Mandela looks up to Zameer and continues, “I have no choice now, I must return, face my maker, and pay my penance for what I’ve done, or more importantly, what I’ve failed to do. Your life is in my hands now, and for the first time, I’m compelled to allow you to make your own decision.” Looking up at Zameer through his fingers, “What is it you want to do?” Zameer, standing firm behind Mandela, still wearing a blood soaked bandage on his head from where he had received the chair shot from Ming at Bad Moon Rising, Zameer cracks his first smile in all the time he’s been in FSW before responding, “ I WANT TO FIGHT” “So be it”, Mandela responds, “Your path is now your own” Fredric Mandela stands to walk away, but Zameer grasps down more tightly on his shoulder, pulling him back, half turning him around. “For you” Zameer whispers, “I want to fight, for you.” Mandela looks back to Zameer with shame sprayed red across his face, “you heard the message, I must return to Africa, my destiny awaits me.” “My destiny lies with you”, Zameer responds still gripping Mandela’s shoulder. “If you go, so do I.” Mandela knocks Azala’s hand away, and in an almost angry tone begins to berate him, “ You can’t do that! If you return with me, you will receive the same fate as I! You must stay here in America, make a name for yourself, make a life here now, where it’s safe!” Zameer stares back to Mandela, “If I am safe here in America, then you will be as well, please stay” Zameer pleads. “I will be constantly sought after by the African government, they will track me by our FSW dates, I will be captured and returned for certain” Mandela explains. Azala peers down to Mandela one last time, pausing for a moment, then responding, “ with me, you will always be safe from danger, with me, you are untouchable, with me, we shall conquer the FSW and recapture our pride, with me, you cannot lose.” “And with me”, Mandela smiles, “neither can you.” A strange silence is had while Mandela quietly considers the extent of damage he may possibly be doing to his life, and the lives of those back home who he is close to, then snaps up realizing there really isn’t anyone back home he is close to. “It is done then, we shall both remain in FSW, together” Mandela leads Zameer out of the dank room and as the door closes behind them one can only speculate what lays ahead for these two possible terrorists turned freedom fighters, but without Mandela’s hands being tied with other responsibilities, and Zameer seemingly more focused than ever, the FSW may be heading towards a new reign. One based on morals of destruction and devastation, and it’s not overly hard to guess with whom that reign shall begin with.. ~Ming, you’ve made your choice, and determined your undoing, FEAR THE SPEAR~
