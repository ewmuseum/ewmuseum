Time for some good ole' Barbecue... St Louis Style

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/9/2002 *In theory, Boda and Cassius Deamont are a tag team to not be taken lightly. Both, former World Champions, though, it is Boda that outshines Cassius in that department. Cassius is known for holding The RUSH Wrestling World Strap, whilst, the monster himself, has held The PCW2 World Title and twice been crowned the PCW World Champion. This in itself is a great deciding factor in the pending DoA tournament. Both know what it’s like to taste the gold, and both are indeed hungry for some more. Then again.... this is Boda and Cassius we are talking about. If this was two months ago, the two would be the team to beat in this tournament. Both, friends at the time, both, wanting the same thing, to destroy Jeremy Howard and control RUSH. Boda and Cassius gelled. They were an unstoppable force within RUSH. No one could stop them, and those that tried.... got dealt with. But like I said...... that was 2 months ago. Now, these two are like water and oil. Now it’s not about controlling RUSH, or taking over FSW, it’s now about ego. Well, ego that is for Cassius. As for Boda, this is about pay back. Sure, Orion got smart, and thought he could out smart Chip. He hired the one man that irritates Boda more than Ivan Stanislav ever did. But, in effect, Orion sealed the fate of Cassius Deamont’s soon to be short career in FSW. Cassius has done nothing, besides the cowardly chair shot, to prove he is going to be a threat to Boda in FSW. Running his mouth, at every opportunity, has done nothing but make Boda chuckle. Idle threats as Boda has called them. On the other spectrum, Orion allowing Cassius to sign with FSW will prove detrimental to not only Orion, but to Deamont as well. Sure, Boda can take the trash talking, but, after the plan that Cassius unfolded last month, costing Boda HIS wrestling company, Boda has been waiting for the time, the place, to take it to Cassius like he’s taken it to no one before. And the time and place has presented itself. Now, as the two are destined to tag team in the first round of the DoA tourney, they can either beat each other, take each other apart, or they can work together, and take this tournament by storm. My money is on them beating each other senseless. With Boda coming out on top, again.* “Great article huh Chris?” Chip puts down a wrestling magazine. He looks across the table at his Hired Gun. There, lost in either thought of beating the living piss out of Cassius Deamont, or lost in thought of beating his first round opponents, sits The Monster of FSW..... Boda. Chris lets out a stretching yawn, and rolls his eyes. “Sure... but it doesn’t say anything that the real fans of FSW and wrestling don’t already know” responds Mr. Sloboda. “That being that I have always been, will always be and am by far, the better wrestler, and better man, that Casius will ever be.” “So, what you got planned for the match this week?” Chip asks as he takes a drink from his coffee, some spilling past his lips and onto the table. Boda kicks back, and looks over his shoulders. He motions for a waitress to come over. A petite, red head walks over. Her name badge glistens in the light of the lamp on the table. It reads MaryBeth. “Welcome to Smoke Masters Bar-B-Que and Fish.” MaryBeth says in a stereo typical cheery waitress voice. “My name is MaryBeth, and I will be your waitress. I see you both got your drinks already, so are you ready to order?” “Sure sweety....” Chip tries to be a stud “I’d like a pork sandwich, and fries.” “And I’ll take a rack of Baby Back Ribs” Boda tells MaryBeth “And the fried okra, french fries, yam patties, and a huge sweet tea, no lemon.” Boda folds the menu, and tosses it on the table. Chip gives a surprised look at Boda, as MaryBeth takes the menus and heads to put the food order in. “Hungry?” Chip asks his charge. “No, that’s just a snack.” Boda quirks. “The real course comes later tonight.” “Really? What you got planned?” Chip wants to know. “Some strippers? Some young female fans? What does the FSW Monster have planned for the main meal tonight?” “Well, there is a certain someone” Boda smiles a bit “That’s caughten my eye.” “Oh really? You big stud you.” Chip drinks from the coffee again. He high fives Boda across the table. “So, who is she? Do I know her?” “Actually” Boda sits up in his chair, and looks Chip in the eyes. “There are a certain some ones that have caughten my eyes. And to be honest with you.... they have been eyeing me up for the past 2 months.” “Come on, “ Chip seems a bit anxious. “Tell me. You need to tell me.” “Well, you see......” Boda seems almost shy, almost childish in his response. “There are a certain two woman, that like to escort a certain jackass to the ring. They are bi-sexual, and I know for a fact, that Cassius can’t satisfy them the way they need to be. So, instead.... they get to learn, tonight, why I am the Big Bad Boda Daddy.” “Kathryn.....” O’Meara remarks “and Erica? You mean, you’re going out tonight, with the bisexual valets of your tag partner? That ..... that......... Words can’t describe this. I mean, this is the GREATEST. But, what about Cassius? I’m sure that he’s gonna get a little mad at this.” “Do I look like I care about what Cassius thinks?” Boda’s mood suddenly changes. “Besides.... while he’s busy ‘getting ready’ to face Kimbusa and Spitfire, I’ll be substituting the ladies plastic friend.... for some Boda sausage. And believe you me, the plan I got for Glory... Cassius needs to be ready. I.........” Suddenly, Boda is interrupted by a ruckus in the front of the restaurant. There is a man, yelling at the hostess. “WHAT DO YOU MEAN, I CAN’T GET A TABLE?” The beligerant man yells angrily. “DON’T YOU KNOW WHO I AM? I AM THE GREATEST RUSH CHAMPION OF ALL TIME, AND I AM THE SAVING GRACE OF FIVE STAR WRESTLING. GIVE ME A DAMN TABLE.” “Sir, I understand who you are.” the hostess tries to be calm, but finds it hard with a big man like Cassius staring her down. “But the restaurant has been reserved by two men. And they do not want anyone else in the restaurant.” “WHAT?” Cassius is very irate. “I CAME FOR SOME ST. LOUIS STYLE RIBS, AND YOU MEAN TO TELL ME I CAN’T GET SERVED HERE?” As Cassius continues his tirade, Boda slowly makes his way to the front. As Cassius looks up, he notices Boda standing about 2 feet away from him. “What she meant to say is....” Boda smirks, “Is that no animals are allowed in this restaurant. And from where I’m standing.... you are a JACKASS, and that means you are not allowed in. Now, there is a McDonalds at the end of the street, take you pathetic carcass there. And, tell Kathryn and Erica, to be ready by 7. I’m in the mood for some sweet, hot poon.” Cassius takes a swing at Boda. Chris simply steps out of the way. Chip O’Meara runs over, and gets between the two. Boda taunts Cassius by waving his fingers at him, as if to mockingly say ‘I’m Scared’. A few members of the wait staff try to pull Cassius away. Finally, the melee calms down. Boda and Chip walk back to their table, but not without Boda getting the last word in at Cassius. “See you on Thursday.... bitch!!” Cassius walks out, punching the wall as he leaves. Boda and Chip sit down, just as MaryBeth brings the food to the table. Boda pulls the meat off a rib with his teeth, and woofs the tender, juicy meat down in one bite. “See, I’m back inside his head.” Boda tells Chip, as some of the St. Louis Rib Sauce drips from the corner of his mouth onto a white napkin. “He comes in, all tough, talking about me being his bitch, that he used me. Well, it starts this week. First Kimbusa and Stevens, then who ever comes next, then next, then next. I’m going to USE him to make myself World Champion of Five Star. And if he doesn’t like it...... Chip.... he will get dealt with. Now, pass me some salt man.” The scene fades out as Chip smiles as he hands the salt to Chris. The two are last shown, chowing down on some good ole barbecue.
