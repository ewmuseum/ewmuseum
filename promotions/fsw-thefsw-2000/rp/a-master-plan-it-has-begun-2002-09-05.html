A master plan; It has begun

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-5-02 Darkness is like death in many ways; people fear what they don’t know, it’s simply a natural part of the human condition. Just the way mankind fear death, as they are unsure of what will happen after their life ends, people fear the dark because of a natural apprehension to walk alone in the blindness of what to them is unknown. What could possibly be behind that tree, hiding so suspiciously to the left? Or what could be crouching around the next corner, holding only secrets that the experience itself could explain? Your heart begins to thump more rapidly in your chest, until it is almost impossible to determine where one beat ends and the next begins, but people continue to tread on, maneuvering towards what they only assume is frightening. > >Your mind is your own worst enemy in a situation like this, and the tricks you play on yourself are far more devilish than the tricks your peers or friends would ever scheme against you. Your own subconscious is busy creating figments of your imagination, creatures that aren’t really there, noises that appear to be real. But if you could relinquish the fear and rationalize, you would realize that they aren’t. But you’re blinded by fear, holding a shield over your eyes and letting your mind toy with you as it wills. > > >Darkness in many aspects is like blindness, and people can learn in blindness; therefore fear that comes from 'not knowing' can't come from lack of sight, but from ignorance alone of all senses. And as petrified as you may feel while walking home alone, in total darkness and seclusion, there’s always a ray of light propelling you forward to your destination, be it comfort with your surroundings, or a different self-created beacon of hope. But again, you must realize, this is all false, created solely for one purpose: getting you home and allowing you to take the next step, and then the next, and the next, until you find yourself standing in front of your house. > >In that instance, standing alone with a hand buried in your pocket and a sigh of relief escaping your once-quivering lips, it happens. You allow yourself one last look over your shoulder for reassurance and instead catch glimpse of a set of vengeful eyes, staring back in your direction from the black alleyway from across the street. No body, not one that you can see anyway. Just a set of eyes, burning a hole right through your gaze, into your soul and into your innermost fears, seeing that “it” has now become one of them. > >Fidgeting more rapidly through your pocket you eventually find your keys, hidden behind that damn money clip that always seems a little over fed. You frantically pull them free of your pocket, hurriedly flipping through until you find the key that offers you access to your home. Inserting the key and with a half-turn your front door slowly slips ajar as you think one last time to look over your shoulder to the troublesome eyes from across the way. But alas, they are gone, just another imagined vision perhaps. However you are still left with an empty void in the pit of your stomach as the memory of just how hungrily those eyes burned from the darkness beyond plays at your prayers for security and devours your sanity. > > ~ SLAM ~ > >The door closes behind you as you toss your keys in the chipped mug in the entryway, so you’ll be able to find them easily within reach tomorrow. You hang your coat on the hook behind the door, slip off your shoes and make your way to the living room, where you find comfort in your leather recliner. > >Your living room is simple and just how you left it. Quite quaint in fact, consisting of the one black leather recliner and a matching leather sofa, a short table of jet shade that runs the same length of the couch and rests directly in front of it, and a zebra skin floor rug laying in front of a 57 inch flat screen television mounted above a stereo system that easily cost you a couple thousand dollars. Windows set up on every side of your small room, but none of them offering a look inside, or out. This is what you’ve become, what you are comfortable with, and as long as you spend so little time here anyway, Why change it? > >You comfortably rest your glasses on the bridge of your nose and place your aching feet up on the retractable footstep, and bring today’s edition of the Wall Street Journal up only inches from your frowning face. With each minute that passes by, your fear of what you encountered outside subsides further, your thoughts of what you narrowly avoided become just a drifting memory, and you allow your eyes to close, blissfully nodding…….off……….to………sleep.... > > ~ tap, tap, tap ~ > >What was that?! A sound at your window, a tapping, softly clicking on the transparent pane, but consistent enough to awake you from your slumber as the events of earlier in the evening still nudge at your dreams. Your head springs up as you begin to realize that you are no longer asleep and your eyes immediately swing over to the clock that hangs along side the window. 2:48 am. You’ve been sleeping now for over 3 hours, but the time spent in rest matters not as your thoughts return to that set of eyes perched across the street, almost smiling, if eyes could actually smile, in your direction. > > ~ tap, tap, tap ~ > >There it is again. But from a different window on the east side of the house. Your head swings around to see, but you are blinded by the tan shade that keeps out these evils you now so helplessly have succumbed to, but bravery now may be your last opportunity for hope, so you stand up with knees quaking. The walls almost seem to vibrate, reflecting the dread in your own psyche as some extension of your own being, but it’s most likely the stigmatisms in your eyes, disrupted by your heart that is now again beating out of control with all the fury of a jackhammer. The hair on the back of your neck stands straight on end, and one foot slowly drifts in front of the other, then again, and again, as you slowly reach out towards the window from where the tapping sound last came. Just as you reach for the pull chord to raise the shades, you heart is sent still. > > ~ tap tap tap ~ > >Causing your heart to stop completely if even for just a second, from the same window that sinister tapping came. Your hand recoils slightly, but out of a sudden bust of courage you grasp the chord and tow it faintly in your direction and down, until you decide to just rip it open and valiantly meet your maker. With a surge of defiance you thrust open the blind, but find only the vast darkness and the mellow breeze casting leaves across the back lawn. Your hands clasp together, rested on the glass as you peer out through the night, looking as far as the next building will offer, then to the right, then the left, before chuckling softly to yourself. There’s nobody there, probably never was, must have been a dream right? Right? Ah well, the stress has been building for weeks, only natural it would culminate into an episode like this, eh? > >Why don’t you take one more look, just to make sure. This time, whilst turning your head again into your cupped hands to the window you see what you expected to see, nothing. Nothing that is until two glowing eyes appear on the other side of the glass, the pupils flaming black as coal witha hint of delirium glimmering in the sockets. There is absolutely no resolve left within this creature staring back right into your own quivering eyes, a menacing smirk dancing across his cracked lips. He has obviously arrived here at your home for a reason, and I doubt he will leave you alone until his reason has been met. You begin asking yourself questions in your head, for only you to hear: Had he been there all along, with his eyes closed? Playing your fright to his advantage? It matters not now, and as you reel backwards crashing over the coffee table set up in front of the couch, you manage one last look to the window, but again, they eyes are gone, or are they?! > >Not being to sure of anything anymore, you rush to your closet, rip open the door and search furiously for a gun, a knife, anything that can be used as a weapon, for this is the first time in your life you’ve ever felt vulnerable, ever truly felt, AFRAID. You can almost feel the fear physically wrenching at your soul as your chest heaves and your breath escapes in agonized gasps. THIS is true fear. > >Ah! A baseball bat, that will work! You clutch the heavy object in your sweat-coated palms, slide on your sneakers and make your way through the kitchen towards the back door. Once outside, the familiar heartbeat returns to your chest and your eyes inspect the landscape of your own backyard. You try to yell out, but your voice has been silenced by your fear, as if though all trepidation inside your body has gathered in your throat disenabling your ability to speak. Your steps now grow heavy and the trees and shrubs lining your back yard are now beginning to play havoc with your mind, making you see things that most likely aren’t there. > >One step, followed by the next, slowly dragging your feet towards the side of building to the area where the window is perched, until you realize that you are finally standing on the other side of the window. Looking inside for a moment, your attention turns to the wet ground beneath your feet as you glance down and become conscious that you are standing with in another being’s footprints, however, these prints dwarf your size 11 feet. Just as you think better to continue, and turn to begin making your way swiftly back inside, a very large, meaty hand grasps around your throat from seemingly out of thin air. Your trusty weapon drops to the ground along side you body that is now being raised slowly from the ground, feet kicking desperately at your deranged oppressor. Your assailant opens his eyes again, allowing you a brief feel for the pain and torture he’s experiencing. Those eyes, the same eyes from across the street, as well as the same ones from the window, now look intently into your own, almost as if though he is judging you, and why up until this point he has not revealed his original form to you, you fear him greatly as you blink and begin to sob for you life. > >Your attacker breathes loudly through his flaring nostrils as his grasp around your neck begins to tighten. Then finally you see something other than his eyes, his jaw drops slightly showing you a large mouthful of ivory white teeth, as he begins to speak in a raspy, low tone. > >“Your tyranny is over. You have been judged, and now sentenced to death.” > >The last words you would ever hear. You indeed have met the Reaper here tonight, and as his single hand clenches more tightly around your feeble neck you open your mouth with one lone attempt to save your own life. All for naught, and with one flinch of the man’s massive wrist, your neck pops out of your skull with a sickening crack. The odd thing is that you can feel the actual popping of your neck vibrate through your assailant’s palm as you fall limp to the grass, and your last breath here on this earth slowly escapes through your slightly parted lips. > >Your last recollection of being alive is the embrace of fear more powerful than any you’ve experienced in your entire life, and now it’s over. Your sins have been judged, and the depths of purgatory await your evil deeds to lastly judge your ultimate fate. > >IT HAS BEGUN~
