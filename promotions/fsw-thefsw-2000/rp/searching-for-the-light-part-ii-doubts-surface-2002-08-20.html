Searching for the Light - Part II - Doubts Surface

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Come, Jason-san. We must meditate for the answer.” I looked at James in the trunk. I couldn’t believe I had done it myself. Just a child, he was. All of 19 years old. He didn’t know better. He shouldn’t have been involved. But it just happened. Angelo had to bring him into this. It could have been easy. Fifty thousand dollars was just a small dent in his pocket book. Instead, this boy faced with an easy million dollars, took the road of the greater evil. He followed Angelo’s empty promises, and now, it cost him. Big time. I felt almost bad enough to give him the three hundred thousand dollars I made off the deal back to him. Almost… I followed Takahiro to the back of his dojo once more. There he sat, facing the three sacred scrolls on his wall. His large legs crossed in front of himself, he looked almost like a rock. I took off my shoes and sat next to him. I’d never meditated before. Taka-san was about to unlock my mind. “Empty your mind, Jason-san. Think of nothing. Imagine white. Pure white. Watch as white, fade slowly to yellow. Yellow fade to orange. Orange fade to red. Red fade to purple. Purple fade to blue. Blue fade to green. Green fade to Yellow. Yellow fade back to white.” My mind, started to float away, out of its sea of thought. Almost instantly, I felt clarity. I felt wisdom. I felt like I never had before. I watched as I looked at my self, and then over at Taka-san. I walked around the room, while I sat and meditated. I read the scrolls on the walls, and though I never spoke nor read a word of Japanese, I felt their energies, and knew what each meant. As I stared at the scrolls, I thought about James. I thought about his pain. We weren’t so different. We’d both seen our share of troubles. We had both had our lives altered by Angelo Deville forever. But it was time for him to move on to a new chapter in our lives. For James, it would be looking for a meaningless job, where he would be under appreciated, under paid, and overwhelmed. But for me, it would be far different. I would have my chance at retribution. Steven Caldera, the target of my aggression for the past 8 months, would be in a position where his friends were all gone. It would be just me, and him. Mano a mano. Caldera was there when I was nothing more than a miserable excuse for a human being. Poor, and pathetic, I entered his company a mere shell of the man I am today. Under his tutelage, came “The Dark Messiah”, Jason O’Neil, the man who instilled fear in the hearts of the GCW faithful. A man, not unlike myself today. But he was a man incomplete, a man consumed by his fear. And at the top of his popularity, a man that Steven Caldera refused to acknowledge as the cornerstone of his failing company. Times, indeed have changed for me. I’ve learned to rely less on my anger, and more on the belief that I am responsible for all my actions. That I’m the one to blame for all the wrong that’s occurred. And the one responsible for creating my own destiny. But I can not change the destiny of others. For Steven Caldera, he’s just delaying the inevitable beating he would suffer at my cold, dead hands. But, he had to make the odds tip into his favor. Being the intelligent man he was, he recruited Kimbusa, former OSW star who never really caught on. A man, not so different from myself, seeking respect and harmony in his world. But he’s a man easily corrupted by the American dream. I’m sure the green that he’s gt in his pocket more than makes up for his moral objections. And inside, he’s nothing more than a fragile mess. The lights on the walls started to dance, playfully with their shadows, making them move around. I stood and watched. As I reflected deeper, I fell deeper into the meditory bliss. Everything was so clear, so simple. “Purifying, is it not, Jason-san?” I looked over and saw Takahiro sitting still deep in his meditation, yet he was also standing smiling like only he can. “It is. I can’t believe I’ve never done this before. I feel more alive here, than I do any other place.” “It is good to feel this way, Jason-san. For those who are worldly, this is where they come for answers.” “It’s amazing. I feel no pain ready to burst from the seams of my closed mind, I feel no sorrow, and yet…I feel it all at the same time. Instead of blocking it, I can rationalize it, and understand it.” “Things not so simple back in real world, Jason-san. You will not feel like this.” “I understand. But, I’m going to enjoy it while it lasts.” “Good. Did you come up with solution for James problem?” “No. I think I’m going to have to take the kid on the road with me.” “But, Angelo said he would kill James-san if he saw him again.” “True.” “Perhaps, you leave him here. I can train him to be not so bad man.” “You think that’s a good idea?” “I will make sure of it, Jason-san.” “Good. I’m going to stay here for a while. Do you mind?” “No, Jason-san. I will go take care of James. You take care of yourself here.” I watched as Takahiro walked over to where his rested body sat. He sat down, and immediately returned to his earthly form. He looked over to where we were conversing, and smiled. He walked out of his dojo, with a bounce in his step. I heard him call out to James and he faded out. The whiteness of the room started to fade into darkness. I looked around and saw my body stir slightly. I walked over to myself, and looked at the being in front of me. “Jason…?” I heard a voice call to me. I turned quickly to see a woman standing behind me. Her tall figure, well sculpted and nude, beckoned me to her. Slowly, I walked to where she stood. She smiled at me, and seemed to float as she walked away. “Who are you?” “I’m your self-doubt, Jason. This is the form you chose for me. I like it. Thank you.” “Doubt?” “Come on Jason, even you know you can’t defeat Caldera. You’ve tried. Remember the Revolution? The Lost Souls? You’re nothing compared to him.” “Are you sure you’re my self doubt? Or a friend of Caldera’s?” “Come on. Face the facts. You tried as hard as you could, and in the end, he’s just too much for you. He’s bigger, stronger, and smarter. If I were you, which…come to think of it, I am. I would just go away, Jason. Leave. Leave FSW behind, and let the inevitable happen.” “No. There’s no way.” “Then you’re going to delay the inevitable. Caldera is going to destroy you, and there is nothing you can do about it. He changed you once, and he’ll do it again. Angelo was right. You are a puppet.” Angerly, I lashed out at the figure. It caused me to wake out of my meditation. I stood up and looked around. I stormed out of the Dojo, to where Taka and James were standing in the garden. Taka turned around and smiled at me. Quickly, his smile turned to a look of disgust as I grabbed James by the throat and slammed him to the ground. I got on top of him, and screamed into his face. “I AM NOT A FUCKING PUPPET. I WILL NOT BE HIS PUPPET. DO YOU FUCKING UNDERSTAND ME?? DO YOU?” My arm flew behind me, and launched a vicious right hand at the face of James. It caught him right under his eye. Taka moved in and effortlessly pulled me off of James. He threw me backwards and stared me down. I looked back up at him in anger. “Fuck.” It was all that could escape from my lips. Taka looked at me, still angry for my attack on James, and turned around to help the boy up. I looked at Taka, and stood up. I started to walk to my car, but turned around and walked back into the fields behind his home.
