Stepping Forward Pt. 4

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 14, 2002 Scene Shift. Chris arrives into the arena parking lot. He parks his vehicle and turns off the ignition. With his eyes closed and head pressed against the steering wheel, he mumbles a few words to himself. 'Tonight...is what its all about.' He sniffs, trying to erase any trace of sadness. 'My final chance to life...' '...my final chance to glory...' '...my final chance...' '..to love.' He raises his head from the wheel and stares forward at the wall in front of him. 'The time has come for me to be strong.' 'My legacy shall continue to live on forever.' 'I've dreamed of this night for so long.' 'And I will not fail myself again. Not today...not ever.' He opens the door and steps out. He gently breathes in the fresh air. 'Davidson. Blair. Walden. Nothing stands in my way.' He steps away from the car door, nudging it shut. He doesn't worry about locking it or taking any safety precautions. He proceeds to the building. 'NOTHING!' He comes to the arena backstage entrance door. He takes the handle and opens the door. He looks in across the corridor. Chris steps one foot through the door. 'As I step through this door, I begin the next chapter of my life.' 'Failure to succeed this time will NOT suffice.' 'Victory is in the the beholder's eyes.' 'And the opposition...can be ready to be RANDOMIZED!' Chris continues into the building. The door closes behind him. He proceeds down the corridor, passing an office room where a good majority of the staff and superstars of Five Star Wrestling are stationed. The looks on the faces of many are those of sadness. Some of the women with tears streaming down their faces. Bagwell fails to take notice. He proceeds down the hall to the locker room door reading his name. When he reaches it, he tightly grasps the handle. As he pushes the door open, the picture shifts back to the gathering room. The staff and superstars appear to be watching a news report on the television. The camera takes the television full screen, listening to the depressed female reporter. 'And the vehicle was removed immediately after reports were filed, seeing the vehicle crashing off the bridge into the water below.' Silence fills the room and is only broken by sobs. The reporter continues, 'Authorities have presumed the victims DEAD.' Anticipation is built. The listen in to the conclusion of the report, 'Abigail Williams, 25, and Rebecca Parris-Bagwell, 23.' A cold chill fills the room. 'Police checked their homes in Salem, Massachusetts and found a small suicide note and a peculiar photo.' The note is read over the air. 'For we have failed ourselves in life, and there is nobody else to blame.' 'For those in question of their future...live everyday like it is your last.' 'For it very well may be.' 'Life is only what you chose to make of it.' 'So make sure you make it something good and worthwhile.' 'For you may only spend it...once!' The picture cuts out. It opens in Bagwell's locker room. Him not knowing of the situation. He speaks aloud, yet to himself, 'For Davidson and Blair. Your time has come. For I..am..your..executioner!' The picture shifts once more. It opens in Rebecca's house. A gust of wind blows through the open door, flipping the picture into view. All that is heard is the voice of the reporter. 'Its a time of sorrow and sadness and surely a time to mourn. For the lives of these two beautiful young girls...have been torn.' The camera zooms on the picture. It is a photo of Rebecca, Abigail, and Rebecca's entire family. Holding Rebecca and her sister in his arms is Chris Bagwell. And on each side is the family friends. The brothers...Rob and Adam Jenks. The enscription on the picture reads 'The last night of true happiness.' Another gust blows, knocking the photo over as the picture fades.
