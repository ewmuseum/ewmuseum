GTT · Banzai

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: whoo~! Finally - grudgingly - the FBI has issued its warrant for the arrest of Angelo Deville. Deville's sources aren't exactly certain as to what the charges presented in the arrest license are, but there is no doubt whatsoever as to the genre of sentence it will push. As far as the man himself is concerned it doesn't really matter. Life imprisonment and execution both translate as death and defeat in his dialect, and his definition of either term is the same: unacceptable. To accept death is to accept defeat, and Deville lived his entire life flouting both. Real men don't live under the collective thumb of others, nor do they deny their passions or desires on supposed moral grounds. Real men make their own rules and feel no guilt over whatever that leads them into. Guilt is for the weak of mind. Atonement of guilt for the weak of heart. Revelling in the belief that he's neither, Angelo finds himself stretched out in a reclined leather seat on one of his personal planes, a folded newspaper in his lap. [i]There is no shame in the act of running, only in being caught at it,[/i] his mind coos, justifying his actions as it has all morning. It seems to have an infinite supply of these little gems tucked away, and releases them every so often like a numbing dose of medication. Suspecting it could be in his best interest to get a little action going when he lands, he rounded up a small group of men to accompany him on his flight. Most of them he knows and trusts, and the other few are their close friends. They are scattered about the modified chamber of the plane, most of them engaged in a loud and apparently controversial game of poker, and the other two sleep the ride away in their chairs. Ignoring the pilot's protests about smoking, at least five cigars are lit and the acrid smell is heavy on the air. Deville flips the newspaper over in his hands and smiles down at the bold headline souring the front page for the third time. He had known it would be something like this when he put the contract out, but he never dared dream it would go this well. His oceanic eyes dance and flicker in mockery of the flames captured in the picture, then he begins to read the article again. [b]Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Headquarters Destroyed[/b] [i]Washington - Another unpredictable act of terrorism caught America sleeping early yesterday morning as the twenty-story GTT HQ crumbled amidst a whirlwind of smoke and fire. According to experts with the FBI, several small bombs were simultaneously detonated at strategic places throughout the building, leading to the almost immediate collapse and the suspected loss of over two-hundred innocent lives. Authorities are not ruling out the possibility that this atrocious crime was committed by one of our own, but they strongly suspect the involvement of Islamic terrorist cells. 'The precision of the attack teamed with the senselessness of its victims leads us to believe this is another example being made by third world scum,' Gerald Ziegler, FBI spokesman, told us earlier today. The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament is a most unlikely target, as it is an annual wrestling competition renowned around the globe. Applicants from every country are eligible to compete, with all successful candidates receiving outrageous promotional . . .[/i] Angelo lets the paper fall back to his lap as his head slumps back against the headrest of his chair, his torso coalescing with laughter. [i]Thank you, Osama![/i] His mind cackles. [i]We're not even suspect because of your pathetic group of barbarians![/i] His laughter grows louder and louder until the other men on the plane are all staring at him, even those who were sleeping. The chuckles slowly choke off and die, and he glares around at them suspiciously, detesting the look reflected in all of their eyes that say he might be crazy. His face hardens as they continue staring, and like a pack of whipped dogs they all avert their eyes, the group nervously turning their attention back to the game while the other two begin fluffing the back of their seats like pillows. [i]Why shouldn't we laugh?[/i] Deville's mind probes. [i]The executive board wanted to jeopardise our chances in the tournament... Hell, they questioned our involvement in the first place. We told those stuffed-shirts long ago not to get involved in our business or there would be consequences...[/i] His glare turns to the window at his side, and for a long while he simply stares at the wistful clouds floating by. 'Hey Angelo,' the smooth voice startles Deville from his thoughts as the bearer sits down in the seat beside him, smiling the broad smile of the eager up-and-comer. 'How're you feeling?' Julian Gandolfini continues in that quiet and understanding voice. This is one of the men Angelo doesn't know personally, and on those grounds alone he is suspicious of him. 'Top o' the world, ma,' Deville quotes, feigning a grin to rival the other's, who barks a patronising laugh. Deville doesn't let his contempt show, willing his eyes to appear friendly and harmless as he continues. 'What's on your mind, Julian?' 'Oh, nothing really,' he responds, looking down and nervously picking his pants for lint that isn't there. 'I was ah, well, just wondering where we're, uh, y'know... going.' His eyes dart up to Deville's again and have the look of a hunted animal. The hint of pity begins welling in Deville's chest, but he immediately and mercilessly crushes it before it can take appreciable form. 'Japan,' he says simply, his voice still falsely assuring. 'May I ask why?' Julian presses, his brow compressing slightly as if deep in thought. 'You just did,' Deville spits, losing the battle over his calm. He hates being interrogated, which is how he views even this simplest of questioning. Perhaps it's the source of the prodding, or the culmination of his recent stress... Maybe something else altogether. 'We're going to hunt down the monster they call Kimbusa.' 'Your GTT opponent?' Julian blurts, unable to mask his surprise. He immediately looks embarrassed and continues in a more humble tone, 'sorry. It's just that with all that's happened... Well, you can't be planning to attend the match... Besides, isn't he living in America?' 'Something tells me he'll be coming back to visit,' Angelo says mysteriously, turning his attention away from the youngster. 'But even still... You can't fight him this round. The FBI will surely be laying in wait! Showing up for the match is suicide!' Julian protests forcefully, demanding Deville's attention. Deville gives it to him, smiling at him from behind a mask of nonchalance. 'Who said anything about showing up?' Deville queries coldly, arching a pleased eyebrow at Gandolfini's confused look. 'If I find the monster beforehand I'll get a bye straight to the finals... Dead men can't wrestle.' [center]~><>...<><~[/center] The rickshaw pulls to an abrupt stop, jerking Deville forward in his seat of weaved bamboo. The driver turns around after setting down the front end, somehow still in complete control of his breathing. He mumbles something in Japanese at Deville while pointing at the building beside them and nodding. 'This is it?' Deville asks doubtfully, pointing at the same building. The man nods vigorously and holds his hand out for payment. Deville narrows his eyes and regards him suspiciously for a moment, but finally relents and pulls a wad of bills from his breast pocket, which he slaps down onto the outstretched hand. The small black eyes go momentarily wide, then the head bobs quickly down and back up. The next instant the rickshaw is gone, leaving Deville alone on a crowded and busy street. His associates were told to explore the city and meet back at the hotel later on tonight. He doubted they were having any more luck than he, but hoped otherwise. The symbols gracing all of the signs and posters on the buildings make absolutely no sense to Deville, and he doubts any of the hundred little men and women speeding by would be able to offer any help. With a sigh, he turns towards the building he prays he managed to tell the rickshaw driver he wanted to get to, and begins walking for the small wooden door. If Kimbusa would be anywhere in this little town, Deville guessed this would be the place. The door opens soundlessly and Deville closes it behind him slowly, scrunching up his nose at the overwhelming smell of lavender. He finds himself in a narrow hallway that opens up into a large room about ten feet down. Finding no doors on either side of the white walls, he shrugs and stalks down the hallway in search of anyone who speaks English, or better yet, his mammoth opponent. The room is not quite so large as it appears from the hallway, and the pungent smell is much stronger here, but Deville is pleased to see a large Japanese man looming over a desk dead in the centre of the room. Several doors line the walls here, and each bears a sign that Deville cannot decipher. 'Hel-lo,' Deville calls slowly as he crosses the room to the man, pronouncing each syllable separately. 'English,' he says in that same manner, poking himself in the chest several times. 'Understand?' He asks, holding out his hands in a helpless gesture. The man smiles at him. Deville is happy to find that the man is very large, but his spirits drop at his apparent incomprehension of English. 'Looking for big fat man,' Deville continues in that slow, deliberate voice, puffing out his cheeks and holding his arms out to his sides at arm's length to impress the massive size. 'Huge,' he amends, nodding suggestively. 'You can help, yes?' 'If I help you will you promise to stop speaking like that?' The man quips in perfect English, a twinkle in his eye. Deville's jaw drops. 'Sonofabitch,' he curses under his breath, then continues in a louder and friendlier voice. 'Name's Angelo Deville... Could you tell me where I am? I think that rickshaw driver may have ripped me off.' 'Call me John,' the man introduces himself, and Deville refrains from commenting. 'And this is the International House of Sumo.' 'Excellent,' Deville exclaims, relieved. 'You're whom I've been looking for.' 'You want to be a sumo?' John asks doubtfully, regarding Deville's athletic frame with palpable apprehension. Behind him one of the doors opens up and a line of six giant men amble through, talking rapidly in animated Japanese. Not one of them is less than four hundred pounds. 'No,' Deville responds will a grin, 'of course not. I'm looking for one, though. Goes by the name of Kimbusa... Big ugly fella... Tits out to here.' Deville holds his hands out a good foot in front of his chest, and the other man bellows with laughter. 'You just described half the people here! I'm sorry, man, but I'll need more to go on.' John shrugs apologetically as the group passes by, nodding to a few of them. 'You've never heard of Kimbusa? He masquerades as a professional wrestler over in America,' Deville elaborates, hoping it will help, and a couple of the men from the group turn around and come back. 'I've heard of Kimbusa,' one pipes in broken English, smiling at Deville. 'He's in GTT,' the one beside him chimes in, nodding happily. 'There ya go, buddy... These guys know who he is.' John says, slapping the desk with his hand. 'Wait!' The first one suddenly shouts, his voice overcome with excitement. 'You're Angelo Deville! You must fight Kimbusa!' Deville smiles at being recognised and confirms the man's identification with a nod. 'That's right, and I need to find the man right away. You wouldn't happen to know where he is, would you?' Deville demands happily, thinking this is much easier than he thought it would be. Those thoughts spoil and die like a flower in winter as the men shake their heads. 'No... He go to America. Yakuza pissed off,' the second one says regretfully, shaking his head. John and the first both start, and the latter nudges him in the stomach. 'The Yakuza?' Deville asks, noting their reactions with great interest. 'Who's that? And what does he care what Kimbusa does?' The guy who let the cat out of the bag says nothing, leaving one of the others to respond. 'You'd best not worry about that, Mr. Deville,' John says calmly, shaking his head. 'Just forget about it. More trouble than someone like you wants.' 'Someone like me?' Deville asks, back immediately rigid. 'What the hell does that mean?' 'Well... you know,' John flounders. 'An American... Someone weak.' 'Weak?' Deville barks, incredulous. 'Why don't you march your fat ass out from behind that desk and test me out, Poppin' Fresh?' 'I don't mean to offend, of course,' John says, keeping an admirable level of calm. 'But an undisciplined American has no business getting caught up with the Japanese mob.' 'The Yakuza is a Japanese mob?' Deville asks with apparent disbelief, then amends the question. 'The Japanese have a mob? You fools are trying your hand at organised crime?' The incredulity is palpable now, and the disgust in his voice raises the ire of the three huge men surrounding him. 'Watch your mouth,' John warns. 'What you talking about, calling us fools?' the second one demands, jumping back in. 'Japan have most dangerous mafia in the world.' Deville reels on the man. 'You're out of your fucking mind, pal. We Italians invented the business, and we'll be damned to eating sushi and wearing big red diapers before we let you buffoons take over.' The three Japanese men gasp. 'You better leave, Deville,' John's voice is devoid of patience, and he speaks through gritted teeth. 'Or else what? The Japanese mob will come wipe me out?' He asks sarcastically, turning from one to the other in turn. The threat of violence is heavy upon the air, with the first sumo already clenching and unclenching his fists. Deville is oblivious to their anger, being so caught up in his own. 'You slanty-eyed pricks don't scare me... You're either tot goddamned small and skinny to matter, or too goddamned big and fat to move. I oughta smash the thr-' The words are choked from his throat as six large hands seize various parts of his body and effortlessly hoist him from the ground. A tremendous cry of 'Banzai!' echoes through the small room as the three sumos begin charging down the hallway, Deville positioned like a battering ram. Their heavy footfalls on the wooden floor sound like thunder gone wrong. Deville struggles in their grip but his resistance is futile... He's like a piece of raw fish already on the chop stick. The world goes temporarily black as Deville's head forces the door open with a bang. When he wakes up he finds himself laying on the road with a headache, dozens of little feet moving around him with notice. It takes him a moment to remember what happened, and another to talk himself out of running back in there for revenge. Who would've thought that 1200 pounds of man could throw him around so easily? He climbs to his feet and unsuccessfully tries to wipe the dust from his white suit, then smmothes his hair and looks around. The realisation that this is going to be a huge pain in the ass hits him then. [i]The Yakuza, huh? Kimbusa is part of the Japanese mob. That's just fucking wonderful... Another wannabe gangster.[/i]
