GTT · Bigger Than A Breadbasket...

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: whoo~! 'It's still not too late to back down, ya know,' a voice splits the near silence of the car, contending only with the soft, high-pitched hum of the engine. Though attempted with a casual air, the words come out choked and stilted and the atmosphere immediately takes ill with its fever. Frankie steals a pensive glance at the man in the driving seat, wondering if his words have fallen on deaf ears; Deville has definitely been out of his mind lately, and Frankie's futile suggestion conveys his patient understanding of the demons behind it. 'You're talking crazy,' Deville says absently, only permitting the idea to briefly brush his mind before shooing it away. The steady stream of oncoming headlights look like an alien invasion descending on them, and through the windshield they appear to Deville as stars... Stars approaching rapidly and going supernova on impact, then fading in his shadow. 'The tournament is almost over.' For a moment Frankie is stunned, and all he can do is stare at Deville's vacant - yet unmistakably determined - eyes. 'The tournament?' Frankie demands and has to prevent himself from looking out the window for a full moon. His face begins contorting with anger and his voice rises to a shout, 'you've got bigger things to worry about than some goddamned wrestling tournament!' Deville pays him no mind whatsoever, opting instead to gaze at the spectral lights approaching. They are hypnotising in a way, blinding one with their elegance while soothing and lulling him into a false sense of comfort. Frankie harrumphs loudly and slumps back in his seat, mumbling to himself about dying with crazy men. 'All that's left now is Inphino Blitz,' Deville whispers as if from a trance, 'only him. The Black Dragon Master... The Void Element... Inphino fucking Blitz. That's all. Just him.' The monotonous voice has absolutely no feeling, cut off from all worldly attachment and emotion, overseeing as a narrator. 'Inphino Blitz is the very least of your concerns, pal!' Frankie shouts, exasperated. 'Your whole goddamned world is about to come crashing down on your bloody head! We need to flee... Now! You can't win this one!' 'There can only be one,' Deville speaks through the fog, still fixing his gaze squarely in front. 'Who will it be? Only the fates can know...' His lips twist up into a disturbing smile as he says this last, and something flashes behind his eyes. Some secret that defies spoiling. One would assume that Frankie would be happy seeing some sign of life in his old friend's eyes, but the entity there is nothing to rejoice for. Somehow, it seems the man has delved deeper, down into a world of devils and treachery, of mystery and deceit. 'Angelo, please,' Frankie begs in anguish, 'to continue past here is foolish... You've proved your p-' The word mutates into a howl as Frankie's eyes bulge and he tries to become one with the seat. The headlights are dead ahead, and if they're not directly ahead of the speeding Caddy, the difference is but a degree. They coalesce and intensify, growing closer with each passing second, and over Frankie's screams comes the desperate honking of a horn. His eyes clamp shut, his white-knuckled hands grip the side of his seat and the door handle, bracing for the inevitably fatal demolition. He knew the end was coming but he didn't think it would be something like this... Some freakish accident. Deville has been out of his mind... Crazy... Frankie knew he shouldn't let the man drive. Now they're dead, because Deville dropped his guard. 'There, now would you fuckin' shut up already?' Deville's sardonic voice calls from the land of the living. Frankie's eyes jerk open and he stares with incredulity at the empty road before them. Anxiously, he spins around in his seat and looks for any sign of the phantom car... Then marvels at its brake lights slowly fading into the night. 'You're always making things out worse than they are.' Frankie can only stare out his passenger window, heart pumping like a piston, forcefully shaking with adrenaline. 'What the hell is wrong with everyone, anyhow?' Deville continues, his face suddenly vibrant and full of scandal as he steals a glance at his passenger. 'Who the hell is going to stop me, huh? How can I lose now, after so much?' '...Too powerful, Angelo...' Frankie says quietly, regretfully, almost shamefully. 'We've no more strings to pull, we're not going to get any help. This is suicide. Please... Let's just run.' The words practically drip with concern, and for one brief moment Frankie thinks Deville will surrender to sense. 'This has dragged on long enough, Frankie,' Angelo states with vehemence. 'The running stops here. We're finally going to have our final showdown.' 'Suicide... Let's postpone everything and make some adjustments behind the scenes. Make the situation a little more favourable. We're not prepared,' Frankie continues his protest, distrusting the anticipation he sees growing in Deville. 'There's your mistake,' Deville chuckles humourlessly. 'You don't honestly think I'd come this far without a plan, do you?' ~><>...<><~ Jack observes gleefully as the last papers incriminating Deville's spot in the GTT are swallowed by the small wastebasket fire. The hungry, leaping flames reflect in his eyes as if reaching in for his soul, but the search is damned to failure. Jack killed a man to retrieve these papers, an old friend of his and his boss as well, but he felt no remorse. Angelo Deville took those precious morals and squeezed the life out of them... Devoured them with a black hole of money. Well he's on his own now, Jack thinks with a triumphant rubbing together of his hands. I've done all that I can. The fire winks out, plunging the room into darkness. Whether he wins or loses, he must do it alone. ... Must he? ~><>...<><~ 'Remember,' the gruff growl crackles through the three dozen police radios scattered along the fence, 'we're taking him alive!' it commands, and the bearers of the glorified walkie-talkies all nod their heads in the darkness as they prepare for the impending assault. The tension is tangible on the black perimeter of the Deville estate, and a gentle breeze wafts unnoticed through the tumultuous calm. Inigo Kiyama lowers the radio from his lips and spits primly into the bush beside him, scanning the area around him as if trying to sense or smell out the location of his team. He is giddy with excitement at finally being able to settle this contest, and he'll be damned if he'll sink to the level of the mob to win it. Far better to live with the suffering of his ultimate defeat then to let him fall before the climax. Glowering at the silhouette of the house beyond the fence, Inigo puffs himself up to his full size and grins with pride. He is hardly noticeable in his black get-up, given away only by the three white letters adorning the back of his jacket like a badge. The FBI beams through the darkness like the personification of false morality. This piece of garbage will be forced to realise I am impervious to his pathetic resistance, just like all those who came before. He will fall to my capable hands, and on my terms. 'Alpha team, go,' he whispers suddenly, raising the radio to his trembling lips once more. His stomach goes cold and then numb as he sees shadows begin scaling the fence, making quick and silent work of the barbwire as they go. The only shame of the matter is that no one can test me... Everyone just falls so easily into their role, too weak to even make a show of standing up for themselves. 'Beta, go,' the whisper is louder now and markedly more intense. His legs tremble and a shaky hand tries to stabilise the walkie-talkie against his lips. The fight is always fun of course... 'Gamma, let's go,' he gives the final command and returns the radio to its clasp on the belt, then whips a knife out of his pocket and places it between his teeth. Nimble like smoke, Inigo begins scaling the fence, grinning around the blade as the first line of men surround the house and take their positions at doors and windows. But Deville can't possibly beat me... It's against the law. ~><>...<><~ Less than two minutes after the initial approach, all three squads are in place. The house is absolutely monstrous, with three stories, a double-level deck, half a dozen patio doors, and a five car garage. There are so many possible escapes that one team is staying behind in case the rabbit bolts out of an unguarded hole. The other two teams are divided between the ground and the rooves of the second and third floors, all crouched beside their entrances and waiting for the go. Inigo raises his right hand and those on his side of the house pull small canisters from their belts, maintaining their intense gaze on their leader from behind their gas masks. Inigo's hand drops suddenly, like the flag before a race, and the sound of breaking glass shatters the peaceful country air. Canisters go flying through the windows, smoke billowing from either end as they clank and bounce on the mansion's floors. A second later Alpha and Beta are both inside the house, treading cautiously through the haze with stun guns gripped painfully in their hands. Gamma kneels on the ground surrounding the house like radii on a circle, armed with scoped tranquiliser guns and waiting breathlessly for a target. The scene is eerily dark and quiet, smoke steaming from every orifice of the house as if from an angry cartoon character. Watching all of this from the brim of the backyard forest is Angelo Deville, accompanied by a wide-eyed and even wider-mouthed Frankie. Deville offers a sympathetic smile for his home, wishing there was some other way this could be resolved. It's too late, though. The cards have been dealt. He stops twirling the innocent pen-like object in his hand and pulls it out before his body, pointing it at the house like a wand. It menaces there for an instant, drawing a wondering stare from Frankie, before its magic is called forth. The explosion is cliched in its ferocity, temporarily bathing the house in a serene orange glow. The faces of all those watching light up in horror as the fireball momentarily engulfs the building, then shatters it in a fit of thunder and howling. Various components of the house whip through the air in all directions, trailing streaming tails like comets. 'Regroup!' Inigo implores his men in a shocked rage, but the shout is swallowed by the combined noise of the roaring fire and the wake of the explosion. Despite that fact, his men pick up and retreat under their own accord, being chased towards any type of shield they can find by the machine gun fire now buzzing around the house. After a brief pause a second gun gets in on the action, Frankie having recovered from his shock long enough to remember this is war. Inigo hits the deck and rolls so that he's facing the gunfire, instantly pinpointing its source and charting the best route to intercept it. His mind isn't thinking rationally while any of this is going on, being overcome by the atrocious assault perpetrated against him. His mind is a violent storm seeking some body to savage, and in the first pause of gunfire he leaps to his feet and begins making his crouched way toward his target. 'You think you can take me?' Deville screams maniacally at his house, spittle flying from his mouth as he takes a threatening step toward the fallen agents. Another round bursts from the business end of his gun, seizing two startled cries from the other side. He begins shouting some profanity at the house, the police, and the land in general, but it's drowned out by the continuing fire of his automatic machine gun. Of the nine agents still alive, only three are void of bullets. These lucky men storm up the hill towards their attackers, using their years of training and experience to keep them from sight. The machine gun fire is actually working to their benefit, as it allows them to move with reckless abandon. Neither Angelo or Frankie notice as they move up to either side and begin cutting off the flank. The gunfire finally ceases and Deville lets the gun drop to his side, breath entering and exiting his body in short excited gasps. His chest heaves up and down and his entire body shakes, but the physical vibration is nothing to the mental reverberation. His mind is at its pique acuity, unwholesomely alive and piercing, aware of the slightest movement or sound. All around him the glowing landscape is bare, bearing only the flickering light of the lusty fire. That'll teach those motherfuckers, Deville's mind rejoices, <who did they think they were fuc- His sense kicks in a nanosecond too late and his body is only half-turned when the body tackles him to the ground. The gun goes flying through the air and lands harmlessly ten feet from the struggling pair. Frankie charges forward to help his downed comrade, but Inigo cuts him off, deftly taking out his legs and falling on top of him. The other agent charges in from the other side. Strings of curses erupt from Deville as he struggles atop his attacker, pinning the man's shoulder to the ground with his knees. As Deville reaches into his breast pocket with a sadistic grin, the agent whips his feet up and tries to clamp them around Angelo's neck, but the mobster manages to evade. A long jagged knife flashes its warning in the firelight, then plunges deep into the agent's chest. Deville leans in to the strike, his ear pressed right beside the man's face so he may hear and savour the startled final breath. Frankie tries rolling free of Inigo, but the man is possessed, and not only with unmitigated ambition and determination, but unparalleled fighting prowess. As if swatting a dopey autumn fly from his arm, Inigo nonchalantly snatches Frankie's head in his hands and savagely ends the battle with a tendon-wrenching snap. Frankie slumps in his arms, dead, and Inigo immediately discards the corpse and moves on. 'No!' Deville's cry rapes the relative innocence of the night, heaping the anguish of his lost friend upon the heavens themselves. Tears of rage fill his eyes and he charges Inigo, knife twirling skilfully in his left hand. With the other agent hot on his heels, Deville lunges forward and connects with air as Inigo flows out of his way and strikes him from behind. Deville stumbles and hits the ground hard, but manages to roll through with a somersault and emerge on his feet. He hits the deck again instantly as the other agent comes flying in for the tackle, just barely getting out of the way. Both men scramble back to their feet as Inigo watches on, waiting impatiently for his comrade to die and yield the fight. The wait is brief, as the better-trained Angelo dodges under an impressive roundhouse and slices up with the knife, slicing the second agent from ear to ear. The crevice opens and expands like a muppet's mouth, then the corpse is on the ground, leaving only two men standing. Deville slowly turns his wild eyes towards Inigo, fervently gripping the bloody knife close to his body. 'And now, Deville, it is time you met your match,' Inigo booms, his efforts to sound calm and unruffled betrayed by the shaking and excitement in his voice. 'You murdered Frankie...' Deville exclaims, as if the idea still hasn't sunk in. 'Do not expect a quick death.' His voice is like crumbling leaves, and his stare like grim death. Inigo seems better for it, however. 'Do not try to villainize me. Your friend's death goes to balance all the lives you've taken here and for all time, as will your eternal cell of damned failure,' Inigo rattles this off as if discussing the weather, then quirks a smile. 'As for killing me... Why don't you throw that knife away and come at me with honour? Come at me without a weapon to aid you?' Deville shrugs and tosses away the knife, his malevolent eyes locked on those of his adversary. The two begin circling slowly, neither moving in or away, simply sizing the other up. The ritual finally comes to a close and Inigo takes the first step forward. In response, Deville's hand whips his special issue pistol from his breast pocket and aims it at Inigo's chest. 'I am a weapon, fool,' Deville snarls, cocking the chamber. With all the warning of a striking snake, Inigo leaps through the air and fluidly kicks the gun free from Deville's hand. Before Deville can react, the other foot slips up and slams him across the cheek, knocking him backwards. 'No, I am a weapon.' Inigo lands and pushes in to continue the assault, but Deville seizes a wrist and expertly twists it up behind his back. His knee strikes Inigo's face with surreal force, sending blood squirting across the lawn. He tries for it again, but Inigo's free arm darts up behind the knee and knocks Deville off balance, causing him to release his other arm in the process. Inigo does a nip-up and dances gracefully over to where Deville struggles to get up, then tries to stomp on his head. Deville avoids the clumsy blow and retaliates with an open-hand shot to the groin, which drops even the mighty Inigo. As Inigo writhes on the ground clutching his balls, Deville hastily runs over to collect his fallen gun, then returns triumphantly to his fallen foe. 'I'm sure you are considered a great man among your peers,' Deville snarls in a haughty and condescending tone, 'but you made the mistake of hunting that which cannot be captured. There can be no victory when facing me, only the horrible thrill of defeat or the remarkable nothing of death. In you I see remarkable nothing.' Deville's grins glows with the pulsing of the fire as he places the barrel against Inigo's head and prepares to end this nonsense. Nothing can stop me now, Deville cackles fiendishly. Nothing ever could, either, his mind tactfully amends. The sound of a round of firecrackers going off rips through the air, and Deville drops to his knees, the gun dangling uselessly from his limp hand as he jiggles and convulses. The eyes that were a moment ago so dark and tormenting are now blank and horror-stricken, tossed beyond the realm of normal pain into the unspeakable. Inigo jams the tazer into Deville's torso again, and the latter flips down onto his back in a quivering mass. Inigo climbs to his feet and stares at the fallen Deville, happy to come out of the encounter alive. His ego quickly squashes that feeling and convinces him there was never any doubt, but a small part of him knows it could have gone either way, and likely still could. Not wanting to waste even a fraction of a second, Inigo produces a pair of handcuffs and bends down to present the Crimson Angel with his lead wings. They snap around his wrists with a click reminiscent of a mighty door slamming shut, like that of a prison cell in hell. Deville doesn't realise what's going on right now, but he'll be in for one hell of a surprise when he wakes. The running has finally ended, the lying and cheating must stop, and the remorseless killing called under question. Deville has been apprehended and is now subject to all the same rules as everyone else. What's done is done, the end draws near... Now it is time for the ultimate challenge... The ultimate judgement.
