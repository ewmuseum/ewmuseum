Balls Of Clay

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 8th We aren't made of steel. We aren't impervious to pain. We aren't supermen. We are balls of clay that can be molded by our surroundings. Our molds shape is our intentions and way of life. Our mold becomes good or evil because of one event or word that we hear. The first sculptor that we face are our parents who begin with a generic piece of clay. Our parents try o create a masterpiece. But before the masterpiece is completed someone else begins to change their creation. The original creation, intended to be a creation like no other begins to be changed. The once perfect creation begins to crumble and fall apart. From a sculpture of greatness to a crumbled pile on the floor. Simple people, molded by others who also fell apart begin to destroy the creation. Then it happens. The sculpture has become so different from the original vision that the original sculptor gives up, and you are left alone. Life is breathed into the sculpture and it begins to function on it's own. Acting the way that it was molded to. Soon all the work begins to pay off or hurt. If the original creation went unharmed and had enough confidence installed by the original sculptor than the world has been giving a gift. However, if it has become corrupt and evil by others who fell apart earlier, than the world has been given a bomb. A bomb that can go off anytime, without warning. The ball of clay is an open ended thing. You can do anything with it. Make a masterpiece or make a mess and cause a long clean up in the future. By the time it hardens you hope it has a stable base. If not, it will fall and crumble because it was not created on a sound structure. This is a lesson we all need heed. Without a proper structure, you are in for a long and painful clean up. One day the mess can be cleaned up and the clay can be worked with again. But this time the clay does not form as well. This time it takes more time and effort so it doesn't fall again. More determined to get it right, you are your own sculptor. This time you must decide what to include. This time you want to do what your first sculptures tried. This time you want to make sure nobody else touches your creation. This time you want to become a masterpiece. This is where I am. Now I am the stage of re-sculpting. I am my own sculptor and I will create a winning masterpiece. Goodnight, Mike Frampton
