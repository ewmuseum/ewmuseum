GTT · What's Going On?

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: whoo~! Under the callously calculating eyes of the Crimson Angel, the sun slowly slithers beneath the horizon like a snake into its hole. An invasive wind whips across the hanging wooden balcony, caressing it much in the same way a cat would a newly captured mouse. The ruckus of the world below is but a faint suggestion up on the twentieth floor of the Sayonara Hotel, and even those whispers elude the mind of Angelo Deville. He's been braving the breeze since midday, relishing the warmth of our star as he traces its path across the heavens, symbolic of his recent descent from grandeur. In this chapter of his life, it seems the acme has come and gone without so much as a passing embrace. His success has been much too brief, and now he finds himself at the figurative end of the road, staring over the ravine before him with bloodthirsty hounds nipping at his heels. The fact that the chase must eventually end has yet to make an impact; nay, has been trying unsuccessfully to seep into his mind all day. An epic battle rages on behind his eyes as his partner retires for the night, providing not the slightest clue of what tomorrow may bring. How everything could turn so suddenly sour is beyond him, how he could turn from predator to prey in an instant an ignominious enigma. It takes his salvation many hours to surrender, but in the first twinkle of twilight Deville has a revelation; an epiphany, if you will. How can we be defeated, he lectures his inner audience, if we are still in charge of ourself? A paper has been issued, foreshadowing doom, yet... The commanding voice falters and trails off, and he runs his right hand through his hair in an unconscious gesture of nervousness. The idea that perhaps he doesn't know where he is, or what he will do next, begins fertilizing his mind with contaminated seed. Does he really have a plan? Is he in control? Or is he just dangling on the strings of some massive puppeteer, existing to validate another's boon? What can we do? His mind demands, searching fervently for any semblance of hope. If we go back right now to fight Kimbusa, it spells the end. Need more time... Gather more information... Prepare for the inevitable showdown. There is no doubt in his mind that simply going back to fight his next opponent will condemn him to an encounter with the authorities, and if that is to be his end it may as well come right here... Right now. But something tells him there is a chance. Deep in the dark recesses of his mind a buoy floats, imperceptibly flashing a beacon, its desperate design to provide guidance through the void. It calls to him on a scrambled frequency, trying to rekindle his memory and subsequently set his quest ablaze. Why am I here? a voice prods the internal oracle, and the sweeping serenity that follows reminds him of his wings... His ability to fly. He stares out over the balcony to the street below, his path opening up before him like a parched flower in the rain. A warming sensation runs through his body, making way for an ethereal smile and radiance of the eyes. He remembers what it is to soar... Float majestically above the others without heed or care. To be free. The road to his continuing freedom lies before him, graphed in his mind like a map leading to buried treasure, the X clear and defined. The next obstacle to overcome is gigantic, bigger than any other passing before, his mind warns as he turns toward the building. For now we concentrate solely on survival, and the next piece of the puzzle is ready to be cut and placed. He remembers why he came here, the next obligation to fulfill. The noble smile of awareness morphs into the malicious grin of ambition as he stalks purposefully towards his room. Time to take the next step, that innate demon giggles. Time to kill Kimbusa. ~><>...<><~ 'Poor Marjorie,' Jack sighs from his position beside the hospital bed. Here in this poor excuse for a medical establishment he seems almost sickeningly healthy, with neatly combed black hair, a spotless suit, and a child-like redness to his cheeks that almost wrestles a smile to your face. His outward innocence is the perfect costume for a man in his field, and it has led him over many unsuspecting adversaries. 'And Robert, and Colin, and Beth,' the man in the bed moans, outpouring a dab of his soul with each name. 'And... And...' He tries to continue the list but his heart prevents it, choking the words away. He turns his head away from Jack sheepishly, shamed by his immobilizing emotions. 'I know, Phil... I know.' Jack whispers, shaking his head ruefully as he examines his shoes. Spotless, like always. 'At least you survived, though,' he attempts with renewed vigour, 'these sick bastards didn't get us all.' Of the entire executive board of directors for the GTT had been killed except for these two men, the head and his right, coincidentally. Phil, head of the board, was just returning from a meeting when the bombs went off, and while he was inside for the initial explosion the blast threw him clear out of the way. Luckily, a passerby risked his own neck and carried him down a block before the debris began to fall. Perhaps even more luckily, Jack caught the flu the morning of the attack and remained at home. He seems fine now, however. Phil remains silent for a moment, then turns to Jack with bitterly satisfied eyes. 'And at least they didn't get what they wanted,' he says cryptically, his chest beginning to coalesce at a faster pace. Phil seems taken aback at first. 'The terrorists?' He flounders, eyeing his long-time friend and boss suspiciously. 'No,' he growls, fists clenching into tight balls at his sides. 'This wasn't the act of any terrorist... Not the brand you're thinking of at least. All of this death is the result of one man's greed... One man's delusional omnipotence. Satan has a new name, and it's not that far removed from the old. Deville. Angelo Deville.' Jack stares at Phil's taut expression, shock and disbelief painted all over his face. 'Deville? You can't be serious. The man's a little over the top, sure, and he talks a lot, but...' Jack argues, addressing Phil as one does a crazy man about to make some terrible mistake. 'You read the report! You read it!' Phil shouts, then goes into a vicious coughing fit, sitting bolt upright and gently thumping his chest. 'You know what he's capable of,' he wheezes, 'you know what he's done.' 'It's all alleged,' Jack responds rationally, 'none of it has ever been proven or confirmed. You're putting this man on level with... I'm not even gonna say it, that's how ridiculous it is.' 'Not proven in a court of law! Yet! You read the evidence... And look at the bombing itself. Six bombs in my office, special attention paid to filing cabinets and desks. This strike wasn't intended to create casualties... It was to destroy documents. Incriminating documents.' The shouting subsides and a tight smile crosses Phil's face. Jack regards him carefully, as one would a diseased animal. 'That makes you happy?' Jack asks, glancing uncomfortably at the content smile on the other's face. 'His failure. His failure makes me happy,' Phil mumbles under his breath. 'That evidence is still alive. He killed all those people, yet his target lives on unscathed. I had those papers with me, Jack, in my briefcase.' Jack stares in amazement for a moment, his mind a tumult of concern behind his glassy eyes. 'Do you still have them?' He asks breathlessly. 'Here? Now?' 'Of course,' Phil speaks in a hushed, menacing voice. 'Those papers follow me to my grave.' The sterile room is again awash in silence. Jack continues to stare at his wounded superior, marvelling at the accuracy of the man's last statement. ~><>...<><~ 'I've located one of their clubs,' Deville drones into his cell phone, tucking his head towards his chest to muffle some of the sounds around him. 'It's alright, don't send anyone. I'll be fine... You guys just continue searching. A quarter-tonne oaf shouldn't be too difficult to find.' He pauses for a brief moment, nods silently, and folds up the phone. It's late in the night now, and the street glows with the carnivalesque neon lights that dot every building of the strip. Ignoring the swarms of people milling about on all sides, Deville moves up to the front door of the building immediately before him and swaggers through it. Throbbing electronic music assaults his sense the second his foot touches the tiled floor, and the flashing strobes make the neon outside seem plain. Only two feet through the door he's already engulfed by the crowd, recklessly swaying with the music like the ocean in a storm. Knowing that if he sticks to a wall he'll eventually come upon something, he fights his way back to the door and begins to slowly dance his way along. A carefree hand swoops out from the crowd and strikes the back of his neck, but when he turns to retaliate there is no one to strike against. Just the amorphous face that is the crowd. We may be out of our element here, his mind shouts against the pulsating bass, struggling to be heard. Then again... What if this is our element? The question propels him, forcing him to indulge all the clumsy and supposedly accidental blows. His hand inevitably strays a few times itself, knocking more than one unsuspecting raver gratefully to the ground. The whole process is like some surreal orgy, only his partners are not those he would choose and they seem to be in it only for their personal satisfaction. Deville finally spots his oasis, in the form of a bar. Getting far more aggressive now that his goal is within sight, he knocks several people out of the way and storms up to the counter. There are several other people sitting at stools or just standing around, but they hardly allow a glance for the newcomer. The bartender follows their lead... Until Deville pulls out a wad of money and waves it in front of him like a fan. 'Hello,' the bartender shouts, grinning like the creshire cat. 'Speak English?' He's short and fat, and his glowing red nose tells of his habit to dabble in his own trade. There is a greedy air about him that Deville finds quite appealing. 'I speak every language,' Deville responds at the top of his lungs, snapping a couple of bills from the stack and plopping them into the porker's pocket. 'Very good to hear,' the bartender exclaims, nodding vigorously. 'What would like?' 'Information. Specifically, I need to speak with the Yakuza,' Deville yells this as well, leaning towards the man so that he may be heard better - and by fewer people. A look of mortal fear descends upon the bartender's eyes, and he turns sharply away from Deville, having apparently lost his knack for English. Deville shouts after him a few times, then realizes the pursuit is fruitless. He dismisses the idea of retrieving his bribe, knowing it will only lead to more grief, but the unworthy loss makes his blood boil. Instead of making a scene with the scoundrel, Deville climbs up onto one of the stools and looks around for another refuge. This seems to be the only bar in the whole place. In fact, the only thing of note that he can see are the bathrooms. Seeing his precious time melting away with the menace of a globally warmed glacier, Deville jumps down from his perch and makes a direct run for the washroom. Time is running perilously low, and if it runs down before his objective is met, everything done up until now is for naught. After another barrage of hands and bodies, Deville comes to the door with the dressless stickman, and bounds through the door. As it closes behind him the music muffles, as if his head has been submerged in water. He can hear voices speaking rapidly in Japanese, a woman caterwauling from one of the stalls, and up beside one sink a man is doing a rail of cocaine. Deville sets his course for the last, and charges forward with a different plan of attack. 'Alright asshole,' Deville says as he rips the man around by his shirt collar and pulls his shocked, powdered face close to his own. The young man's shock is mollified by the drugs, but he still has the presence of mind to curse, likely thinking he's being arrested. 'Tell me where to find the Yakuza!' The man just stares at Deville without any comprehension of his words, staring at him as Deville would the man if he started speaking Japanese. The two men who had been drying their hands and talking make hasty retreat for the door, peering back at the lunatic over their shoulders as they run. He pushes the drug-addict to the ground and scowls around at the rest of the empty bathroom, then his gaze stops on the noisy stall. With a bemused grin, Deville strides over to the closed door and disengages the hinges with one foul kick. The lady's moans turn to a horrified shriek and the man sitting on the throne looks up at Angelo with drunken red eyes. The woman struggles to get off her mount, but Deville crams them together, leaning over them in his panic to find someone with information. He knows the gang is here, he just doesn't know how to find them... He hopes it isn't like in America, where if you go looking for the mob you regret it the moment you find them. 'Where is the Yakuza?' Deville demands of the fornicators, leaning over them with a crazed look in his eye. The woman continues screaming, the decibels piercing Deville's eardrum, and he has a powerful urge to backhand the whore into silence. Instead he shouts again, hoping to intimidate them into a quick answer. 'The Yakuza! Where are they? Tell me or die!' The man begins mumbling something in Japanese, his eyes wide with excited horror, but Deville hears none of it. A lightning bolt of pain sears down through his left shoulder, and the next thing he knows he is on the ground, half in the stall. His body seems light, almost weightless, and he can't seem to focus his mind on anything. Unconsciousness is quickly stealing over his body. 'Yes, that's him,' a decidedly Japanese voice says harshly. 'Bring him,' she continues, and Deville feels himself being lugged up by the armpits. He doesn't know what they hit him with, but he tells his drowsy mind to make sure to find out when this is over. Before he slips away into the dreamland he catches a glimpse of the woman's silhouette charging off before him, long black hair cascading down to a curve-hugging blue business suit. Definitely out of our element, Deville's final groggy thoughts proclaim. We're getting dealt with by a bitch.
