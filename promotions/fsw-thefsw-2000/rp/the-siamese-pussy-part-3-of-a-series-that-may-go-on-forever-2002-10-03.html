The Siamese Pussy (Part 3 of a series that may go on forever. Heh.)

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/02 **The scene opens with Magnum Wolf walking down the streets of New York. His head is down, and his hands are stuffed into the pockets of his overcoat. Every couple of seconds, he looks up and squints into the glint of the sun... he seems slightly depressed. Obviously, his mind is wandering from topic to topic.** It had been a while since I had seen a lead in this case. A long while. I've been told by experts on this topic that sometimes you can go for months at a time without seeing a Shaven Pussy. It had been about a month since Miss Ima Shaven had come to my office searching for answers. She had come to me because I was the only man who would be able to help her find her precious Shaven Pussy... and what have I done? Nearly nothing. A bad luck streak. That must be it. **Magnum now stops in his tracks, and pulls out a small notebook. As he traces his finger down the notebook's pages, he remembers the past of FSW...** These first months have been hard on me. Seems like everytime I get involved with someone on any level, that person disappears mysteriously. Now this case... just when I thought I was finally getting back into the game... I can't even get a clue. Jack Brooze... Tyler Davidson... Chris Bagwell, that peice of monkey shit. It's always the same. **The street takes a hard left into an alleyway, and Wolf heads down that way. As he realizes he's come to a dead end, he shakes his head.** I've barely even tried here. I can't just give up on this case because of some bad luck. That's loser-talk! I'm going to find it. I'm going to find that Pussy, and I'm going to lick this bad luck... or the other way around. I'm going to solve this case, and get BACK ON TRACK! AND NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW! **As he says this, he turns around, throws his hands in the air with enthusiasm, and starts to walk out of the alleyway. Back into the city. As he gets to the end of it, he slips on a plastic bag that was laying in the middle of the alley. Crashing to the floor, Magnum lays in a heap for a few seconds. His mind not quite clear yet. As he looks at the bag, he notices that it has a large man carrying a Tiki Torch on it. He double takes, and rubs his eyes with vigor. As he opens his eyes again, he looks at the bag. It's a souvenier bag from the Statue of Liberty. A picture of lady liberty on the side...** Ok. So one thing can stop me.
