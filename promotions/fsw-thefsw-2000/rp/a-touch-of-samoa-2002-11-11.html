A Touch of Samoa

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th Nov 2002 Dark times have clouded Five Star Wrestling since the emergance of Tyler Nelson and his group of militia, known as Mob Rule. They have tried to destroy the inner foundations of the FSW, but standing in their path is one man, that man is a man who has given his all to be where he is, and a man whose sole desire is to bring down the evil that men do. This man is of course the FSW World Heavyweight Champion, 'The Samoan Silencer' Timo Bolamba. Bolamba though is not alone in his quest to stop Mob Rule. As standing by his side are FSW Hall of Famer Mike Marvelous, Azala Zameer and Cassius Deamont. And as we saw on Glory, one of the Sons of Samoa, Afwa Kilmanjaro has returned and now he stands toe toe toe alongside his best friend Timo Bolamba and together these five men shall attempt to break down the barriers known as Mob Rule. Afwa as we see him is getting prepared to stand up for what is right, stand up for the FSW and in the process Afwa shall stand up to Tyler Nelson and once again destroy Orpheus Grant, for all his bad things he das done to Afwa in the past. As we fade in upon the Islander, Afwa just stares at the cameras, as if they were a solid pane of glass. His deep emerald green eyes penetrating the cameras lense. With a grufflike tone, Afwa greets us all.. 'Talofa lava.' Afwa looks at us as if we are chimpanzees who have no concept as to what he is going on about. Though unless you understand Samoan, or you understand Afwa, you might as well be a chimpanzee eating a bannanna as that is as far as you are going to ever get. 'Afwa pleased he be here.. Afwa come to Five Star Wrestling as a desire to show that Afwa not willing to let evil take over.. Afwa know all about evil.. He was taught by the best.. Afwa smiles at the cameras.. 'Isn't that right Nelson?' Afwa smirks at us all.. 'Tyler teach Afwa how to be bad, but Afwa no like it.. Afwa rebel and Timo Afwa friend..' A smile once more falls upon the face of Afwa Kilmanjaro. 'Tyler try and get Afwa hurt Timo.. Afwa no like Tyler.. Tyler not Afwa friend..' Afwa snarls at the cameras, as Afwa shows no remorse or contempt towards Tyler Nelson and if his looks could kill, then by now Tyler Nelson would be no more a burden upon the FSW as he would surely be inside his coffin with some lillies around his grave. And Afwa standing over it, urinating onto it, as that is how much bitter hatred Afwa has for Tyler Nelson. 'Afwa get chance to destroy Tyler.. Mob Rule, no more ruin Timo.. Afwa save Timo..' Like a huge weight has been lifted off his shoulders, Afwa does a great big sigh, one which if huge relief. 'Afwa no act anymore..' Afwa grins like a Cheshire cat.. 'Tyler Nelson, all can act dumb when they want. Tyler Nelson, Afwa might appear as if he is a gentle dumb giant. But unlike that hired hitman of yours in Orpheus 'Afwa crushed' Grant, I am that bit more intellegent, even if I did kill my little pet fishee.. Afwa was sad for that, as Afwa like fishee.. Fishee remind Afwa of past, Afwa now have nothing to remind him of past, but he have something better then fishee, he have Timo.. Timo and Afwa are back..' 'Tyler Nelson, were you expecting this? Were you prepared for this? And at Glory, will the Mob be able to Rule? Or will the black cloud that hovers over the FSW float away? We shall see.. Until then.. Let it be said, Afwa ready for fight.. Afwa ready for Mob Rule.. And Afwa ready to protect Timo, at all costs.. As Timo, he be Afwa friend.. And friend's stick together, no matter what..' 'Manuia le aso, Nelson..' Upon Afwa saying to Tyler Nelson, Have a Nice Day in Samoan, Afwa then decides to head down the stairs and towards the kitchen.. The cameras then fades out as we see Afwa headed towards the fridge.. And we are left wondering.. Is Afwa really dumb again? And what could possibly be in the fridge that he desires the most. Don't tell me, 'Oh no, Afwa not want Pineapple..' We shall not know, that is until next time. Until then, we fade out..
