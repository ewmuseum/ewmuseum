GTT · Shady Business

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: whoo~! Angelo Deville kicks his feet up onto the polished wooden surface of the table as he puffs mightily on his cigar, blatantly disregarding the no smoking sign. He is at the foot of the twelve-man table, dressed in a sharp Italian suit to shame those of the eleven corporate stooges cramped in the meeting room with him. Like a team of clones, all eleven junkies wear the same suit, sport the same hairstyle, and annoy with the same uppity mannerisms. The swirling blue pattern of the wallpaper is supposed to soothe people and make them more cooperative, but the board shifts uneasily at Angelo's demeanour and becomes visibly more irritable than usual. Normally they simply feel like bickering or arguing; today a full-scale bitch-fest could be in the cards. 'You must answer the question, Mr. Deville!' A blunt elderly man sitting to the right of the head declares, almost shouting. His left eye twitches in irritation as Deville meets the demand with an off-handed smile. Deville takes another long haul on his cigar and nonchalantly blows a few smoke rings before deigning to respond. 'I don't know what happened to Blitz,' Angelo states with a shrug. 'What makes you think I had something to do with his no-showing?' He tilts his head to one side as he asks, right eyebrow quirking up in amusement. The Board of Directors for the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament have been harassing Deville for the past ten minutes, more than implying once or twice that he had something to do with Matt Blitz's mysterious disappearance from the tournament. Deville's play of innocence is met with a shuffling of feet and an exchange of hurried looks amongst the members. 'It just strikes us as odd, Mr. Deville, that Mr. Blitz would suddenly disappear after such an active and successful beginning,' a woman from the middle of the table volunteers in a rich but sultry voice. 'Especially in light of the rumours about you buying your way into the tournament,' the man beside her continues in a flat voice. She nods hurriedly in support as another takes up the cause. 'And considering who you are to begin with, of course,' the man at the head of the table begins sleepily, then his eyes go wide an he scrambles to add, 'with your line of work, and all.' Many of the board suddenly find something worth watching on the table below them as Angelo squints up the table at the head. 'Considering who I am?' Deville demands incredulously, burning his cigar into the table as he inclines his head to hear better. 'What the fuck is that supposed to mean, considering who I am'? 'Well now,' the director stammers, holding his hands up in a defensive gesture as his eyebrows reach for the ceiling. 'I'm not gonna name names... Er... That is, uh, I didn't mean anything by it. Let's just forget I said that,' he finishes with a flush, vigorously nodding his head to inspire the appropriate answer. Deville glares at him momentarily before opening his mouth to respond, but one of the younger members speaks up and saves the director the verbal lashing. 'What we mean to say, Mr. Deville,' his voice is smooth and unruffled, much like his face. Obviously a veteran in sticky situations. 'Is that the disappearance seems very suspicious in the collective light of his track record, your controversial selection as a replacement wrestler, and let's face it - your reputation.' All heads overcome the initial shock almost immediately and turn their hungry gaze to Deville, who shakes his head ruefully. 'You stuffed-shirts make me sick,' Deville declares in a low voice as he rises from his seat and glares around the room. 'Maybe Blitz caught wind of the same rumours your office is apparently abuzz with, and decided he'd rather take the easy way out. How the hell should I know? I've nothing to gain by doing away with anyone before our match... There's less risk facing them in the ring than otherwise.' 'What about your unprecedented late entry?' A young man pipes up from the seat just to the left of Angelo. There are a couple of supportive nods from the rest of his group, but most of them sit motionless. 'Can't a man get lucky?' Deville implores them. 'A name was drawn and it happened to be mine. Let's move on already... If I don't deserve to be here then the competition will make it painfully obvious.' 'Unless they end up like Matt Blitz,' the director calls in an instigative voice, then lowers his head like a turtle retreating into it's shell. 'It's not my fault if they're too scared to show up,' Deville reaffirms his position through gritted teeth, glaring at each member in sequence. 'I want competition, damnit, but if people are simply going to bow out and let me strut to the championship unchallenged, I'm not going to complain... Understood?' 'Let's just hope the same mysterious disappearing act doesn't claim Ms. Windsor,' a second lady teases mockingly, her voice dripping with the obviousness that she thinks it very well could. 'Don't you worry your pretty little head about Tessa Windsor,' Angelo says, smiling humourously. 'She'll make the match,' Angelo pauses and seems reflective for a moment. 'Her efforts won't amount to much, but you can guarantee her frail body will scrounge up some petty attempt at offense.' 'So you're telling us that you had nothing to do with Matt Blitz's disappearing act, and you're not planning anything unethical for Ms. Windsor, right?' The director asks, his voice dripping with skepticism. 'Oh no,' Angelo barks a humourless laugh and his eyes twinkle, 'I'm definitely planning to be unethical with Tessa... I'm just gonna do it in the ring.' 'But what about-?' A new voice begins whining, but Angelo cuts it off immediately. 'Enough of this!' Both the smile and the twinkle are now gone, and his patience has apparently been sapped. 'I don't have any more time to waste running around in circles with you wannabe detectives. Go join the force if you wanna solve mysteries, because there's none to be found here. There's no stopping me in this tournament, in the ring or in the boardroom. So get off my case. I never want a repeat of this horseshit... Capiche?' Eleven heads nod in unison, all faces molded into patronising smiles and expressions of goodwill. Deville's eyes burn into each one in turn, then he turns on his heel and stalks out of the room, leaving a gaping silence in his wake. The members of the board exchange worried looks with eachother for the better part of a minute before the director loudly clears his throat. 'So... We're agreed that Deville somehow strongarmed his way into the tournament?' He asks resignedly. Eleven heads reluctantly nod their agreement. 'And he definitely did something to Matt Blitz to prevent the man from showing?' He fires again in his monotonous voice, knowing the answer. Eleven heads nod again, this time more forcefully. 'And we should warn Ms. Tessa Windsor that she needs to worry about him 24/7, not just when they meet in the ring?' He proposes his final theory, and the response is as he expects. Eleven heads nod vigorously, not a single doubt to be shared out between them.
