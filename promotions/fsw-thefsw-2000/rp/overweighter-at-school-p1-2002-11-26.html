Overweighter At School P1

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/11/02 The Overweighter At School Part one (First Day.) (The camera opens up, you can see an alum clock. The time is 8:00AM. The alum goes off, you see a massive hand go in front of the camera and whacks the alum clock. The alum stops, the camera pans around to show The Overweighter half asleep. His hair is messed up, eyes barely open and huge sleet in his eyes. He gets up walks into the bathroom, he looks into the mirror.) The Overweighter: AHHH! (The Overweighter scares himself, his head hits the mirror and he begins to snore. You can hear a large vehicle heading towards The Overweighter’s house. The Overweighter hears it, he quickly looks out of the window and sees a school bus go past his house.) The Overweighter: Sh*t, I missed the bus. (He gets dressed quickly, runs out of his bedroom and down the stairs. He runs into the kitchen, he opens the fridge and gets out a huge packet of bacon. He looks at the clock and sees it’s 8:10AM. He puts the bacon back, runs out of his front door and gets on his motorcycle. He arrives at school, he parks his motorcycle in the teachers car park. He walks over to the main entrance, there are a group of kids by the door and they point at The Overweighter and start to laugh. The Overweighter walks past them, he hears one of the kids whisper to another.) Kid (whispering): Look at that fat guy…He, he, he, he. (The Overweighter ignores it, walks into the school and up to the reception.) Receptionist: Hello, are you the sub-teacher? The Overweighter: No, I’m here to learn. Receptionist: Stop being sarcastic, people wonder why kids these days are sarcastic and it’s because of people like you. The Overweighter: No I’m really here to learn, I’m The Overweighter from FSW? Receptionist: Oh sorry, we have been waiting for you. Here’s your timetable and here’s a map of the school. The Overweighter: Cheers. Receptionist: Welcome to John Smiths High School. (The Overweighter walks out of the reception and walks down a corridor that leads to social Education area. He stops, looks at his map and timetable. He walks to the room he has to be in, he walks in and the room is empty.) Social Education Teacher: What do you think your doing? (The Overweighter turns around to see a teacher.) The Overweighter: Nothing, on my timetable it says I have this lesson first. Social Education Teacher: Oh you’re the guy from FSW, you have to go to registration first. The Overweighter: Okay thanks. (The Overweighter looks at his map, the registration rooms are just down the next corridor. He walks down it, gets to the registration room it says on his timetable and he goes to walk in when the door is opened by someone inside the room. It’s the teacher.) Registration Teacher: Oh hi, are you the new kid. The Overweighter: I’m not a kid but yes I’m new. Registration Teacher: I can see that you’re not a kid. (The Registration Teacher smiles.) Registration Teacher: hang on I will call you to come in soon. (The Registration Teacher walks back into the room and shuts the door.) Registration Teacher: Everyone listen up, we have a new student with us today. Kid at the front: Oh yes a new kid, they wont be picking on me anymore. (The kid at the front has a pear of broken glasses with tape around them and spots all over his face.) Class Bully in the corner: Hey new kid you’re no longer the new kid. Boy at the front: I have been at this school for a year and a half I’m not new! Class Bully in corner: No because there is another new kid to pick on. Registration Teacher: Be quiet there wont be any picking on this new kid, okay! Whole Class: Yes Miss. Registration Teacher: Now you can come in now. (The door opens, all the kids are gazing to find out what kind of freak comes into the door. The Overweighter steps in, everyone is looking at his belly and they were expecting a head. They all look up and see his head, all their mouths drop open.) Boy at the front: Damn looks like I’m still going to get picked on! (The Overweighter walks over to the teacher.) Registration Teacher: This is…(whispers) what’s your name? The Overweighter: [name withheld] AKA The Overweighter, but just call me The Overweighter. Registration Teacher: This is The Overweighter. The Overweighter: Where do I sit? Registration Teacher: Over there. (The Overweighter walks over to where the Registration Teacher pointed, He looks down on the chair and desk. He tries to sit in it, he can’t fit so he picks up the chair and desk and tries to get into it through the top. He gets his head in, but his chest gets stuck and he pulls it down as hard as he can. It gets past his chest and gets stuck in between his chest and his belly. The Registration Teacher looks up, sees The Overweighter is having trouble getting in the chair and desk.) Registration Teacher: Okay, err Overweighter if you want you can stand at the back. The Overweighter: Okay. (The Overweighter walks over to the back, with the desk still stuck on him. All the kids in the class are sniggering at him. The bell goes all the kids go to run out of the classroom.) Registration Teacher: Hold it! Sit down you leave when I tell you to. Overweighter you may go first. (The Overweighter walks over to the door, opens it and tries to get out but the chair and desk is stopping him. He gets stuck in the doorway.) Registration Teacher: The rest of you may go. (The Registration Teacher walks out the back door that goes to the teacher’s rest room without knowing The Overweighter is stuck and no one else can get out. All the kids are pushing The Overweighter to try and get him free.) The Overweighter: Hey stop that, I have an idea. (The kids stop pushing him, The Overweighter starts to eat the wooden desk and it makes a gap for The Overweighter to get out. All the kids now can get out of the room but they have to duck under the chair and desk. The Registration Teacher come back into the room, sees the desk in the doorway and wonders what happened. The Overweighter and the rest of the class are heading towards their first lesson, which is Social Education. The Overweighter is walking down a corridor, a few of the kids from his class run up to him.) Kid: Hey, I’m Jeffery and this is Kid2: Terence Jeffery: You’re The Overweighter right, is it true your making you match debut in FSW? The Overweighter: Yeah, you watch FSW? Terence: All the time, Mr. Lardass. The Overweighter: That’s American Lardass to you. Jeffery: Cool, so why are you here? The Overweighter: I was sent here to brighten up for FSW, everyone there is well educated and me well I’m… Terence: Stupid? (The Overweighter stops walking down the corridor and looks at Terence in the eyes.) The Overweighter: Yeah. (They carry on walking down the corridor to their next lesson, they get there and Jeffery sits down at the front of the room and Terence sits next to him. The Overweighter has to sit at the back on the floor. His taller than everyone else in the class when his sitting down, the Social Education Teacher walks into the room.) Social Education Teacher: good morning class. Whole Class: Good morning Mr. Punter. Social Education Teacher: Today we’re going to be learning about human body parts, mainly in the never regions. Boy in the class: I know a lot about the never regions sir. Social Education Teacher: I bet you do. Girl in the class: Yeah, he knows that his got a small one. (Everyone laughs at the boy.) Boy in the class: Yeah but everyone knows that Jeffery has a small one, because his fat and all fat people have small ones. (The Overweighter stands up.) The Overweighter: Not all fat people, I’m fat and I have a big one. Boy in class: No, all fat people have small ones. The Overweighter: Something must have gone wrong with me then. (The boy slides down in his chair and shuts up.) Social Education Teacher: Okay that’s quite enough, I think we will skip that for a while. Lets go to girlfriends and boyfriends, does any of you have girlfriends or boyfriends? (None puts up their hand.) Social Education Teacher: okay have any of you ever had a girlfriend or boyfriend? (None one puts up their hand and the Social Education Teacher goes to say something when The Overweighter raises his hand.) Social Education Teacher: Okay tell us about it. The Overweighter: I went out with this girl that worked on Television, well she didn’t work in television she worked on the aerials. The next day she dumped me and found someone else. A week later they got married, I was invited to the wedding and I will tell you the wedding was rubbish but the reception was brilliant! (No one is laughing but the Social Education Teacher, All the kids are looking at The Overweighter in an evil way for telling a bad joke and he sits down on the floor and tries to hide from the evil looks.) Social Education Teacher: Okay I need to go to toilet I will be back in a minute you kids can talk to each other. (The Social Education Teacher walks out of the room, one of the kid’s try to grab the clock on the wall but its to high and The Overweighter walks over. He grabs the clock and gives it to him, the kid changes the clock forwards and gives it to The Overweighter to put back. He puts it back, sits back down at the front and the Social Education Teacher comes back into the room.) Social Education Teacher: Okay I’m back, time to get started. Boy in the class: Look at the time sir, you took to long on the toilet its time to go. Social Education Teacher: So it is, okay sorry about that we will get it all done next lesson. You may leave. (Everyone gets up, they walk out and The Overweighter is the last out of the door. The door slowly shuts and it fades to black. What will happen next in The Overweighter’s next lesson? And what lesson will it be, Math, English, Science or music? Find out in Part two of The Overweighter at school.
