Gather Ye Rosebuds While Ye May

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-15-02 Tick. “The hands on a clock, funny how they turn, never looking back.” Tock. “Look around you boys. This is my backyard.” Tick. “Blood. Sweat. Tears. What do you get when you mix them all together?” Tick. “Hard work, endurance, a life lived in the shadows. I’ve been there.” Tock. “You want me to name names? Okay, I can name names.” Tick. “Kremmen.” Tock. “Tyler Nelson.” Tick. “Ivan Stanislav.” Tock. “Jason Snow.” Tick. “Meanstreak.” Tock. “Jeremy Howard.” Tick. “Sebastion Toomes.” Tock. “You need more? Look around you then.” Tick. “Mike Marvelous…You are a past FSW Champion, but you have never fought a man like me for that belt.” Tock. “Jacob Jackson….The So Called “Idol”. You want to see an Idol? Take a look around, the fans don’t scream your name. You may have been the champion, but I will never let that happen again.” Tick. “Chris Irving…You followed me right up the ladder, now we stand poised to make history in FSW, yet you fight me…good luck, you are going to need more than a steel chair this time.” Tock. “I don’t doubt you are all very skilled. The absolute best of the best in FSW, but Sunday night boys, you will have to be more than the best. You will have to reach into your secret trick bags, you will have to climb the mountains of faith, you will have to search your very beings to face me.” Tick. “Sunday will come, and Sunday will go, but there will be only one man standing in the ring when it is all said and done. FSW may be yours, but soon enough, you will see I am every bit as FSW as you ever were and ever will be.” Tock. “Listen to me boys, I know what you’re thinking. You think I’m some sort of idiot don’t you? You watch me on TV and see how funny I can be. You laughed when I made fun of Ming. You chuckled when I insulted Mike, you snickered when I glued my hands to my forehead.” Tick. “But Playtime Is Over.” Tock. “This time, you have no idea what is coming. It’s big, its bad, and it only has one thing on its mind, and that is winning the FSW Championship. Being THE MAN of FSW. Showing the whole world what a true champion is.” Tick. “What a MAN is.” Tock. “I have waited my entire life to win a World Championship. I have endured pains you can only imagine, and some you cannot begin to. At this point, pain is not an issue anymore.” Tick. “Gather ye rosebuds while ye may, Gather ye rosebuds while ye may. You have trite little time to prepare now guys, and don’t think for a moment I will cut any of you slack in that ring. I feel no pain for you either.” Tock. “But Timo, aren’t you a good guy? Of course I am, but this is the end of the line, no more time for antics, no more time for goofing around. I am here to do one thing, and that is claim The FSW Title, and wear it proudly.” Tick. “Smell the ring boys, that’s history. That’s years of sweat, of blood, of men and women pouring their hearts out for the fans. That’s what will happen on Sunday. You will see the rebirth of something in that ring that has lain dormant far too long.” Tock. “You will see 100% Effort, 100% Skill, 100% Tenacity, 100% Heart. You will see me as I enter with the fans screaming my name… Tick. “And you will look up from the canvas when I am on the top rope, and the fans are on their feet.” Tock. “I AM The Samoan Silencer, and I guarantee you, for those treasured moments in that ring, you will NEVER see another show on earth quite like this match. I will come at you all as hard as I can, because that the only way I know how to fight. That’s the ONLY way I know how.” Tick. “I WILL fight you until you have killed me, but in doing so, you had better be ready to die yourselves. Because maybe all I can do is take everything you have, and in the end, when I am still standing, and you have nothing left to hit me with, you will know why I am a true champion.” Tock. “The time draws near, and I must get ready. Every drop of sweat that hits the ground.” Tick. “Every drop of blood you can spill from me body.” Tock. “Every bruise you can inflict.” Tick. “Every time you hit me and I get back up.” Tock. “Every time I stand in your face, defying your total being, your every effort to stop me…” Tick. “You will wonder one thing…” Tock. “Has time just run out?”
