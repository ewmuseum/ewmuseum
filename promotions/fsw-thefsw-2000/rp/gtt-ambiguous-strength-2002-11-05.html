GTT · Ambiguous Strength

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: whoo~! He should be dead right now, and he knows it. After a clumsy charge fitting of a criminal Byron Calver, Deville became acquainted with the reality that his journey had finally come to its conclusion. In a moment of mental malfunction, Deville lost his cool and lashed out, aggressively hunting his prey instead of consciously constructing the trap. A brief error, perhaps, but a fatal one; his enemy was laying in wait. Yet he's waking up. The enemy has made a significant mistake as well, failing to capitalise on Deville's uncharacteristic vulnerability. Sleep's heavy blanket is slowly wearing itself off, like a cumbersome cocoon. Temporary vertigo steals over Angelo as he realises he is sitting on something soft, something airy and comforting. For just an instant he feels a compelling desire to drift back to the land of dreams, to lay in the apathetic darkness of inaction and let his troubles resolve themselves. The world is dangerous outside, but in here it is safe. Better to turn inward and face only oneself than to face the reality of the day. Why not ignore the problems for now? Put everything off until the last minute... What's the point of planning and preparation? We'll deal with it when it comes. He almost succumbs to the slithering id, but clear and conscious thought finally crash through the fog, making his body tingle with alarm. He feels the energy of life, adrenaline, and anticipation course through his spirit, pure enough to be compared to being born a second time, yet conscious of the life before. The rashness that brought him here would not be repeated. Without the slightest discernable movement, Deville lets one eye drift open a hair and tries to get a visual feeling of his surroundings. With relief he finds that he isn't being restrained in any way, he was simply dumped in a velvet easy chair. A quick glance reveals that he is tucked into a corner of a relatively small and unusually dark room. A huge mahogany desk monopolises the majority of the marble floor and the walls are lined with impressionist illustrations of mythical Japan. Small blue lamps that look remarkably like American traffic lights dot the ground, and built into the back wall behind the desk is a beautiful aquarium, filled with innumerable exotic fish. Why must every one of these places have an aquarium? We're on a goddamned island, for crying out loud! Deville quickly reins his mind back to the important, which he interprets as the lithe lady between the desk an the aquarium, looking almost titanesque outlined by the water, despite her petite frame. Her silky black hair falls down around her shoulders like an imperial gown and the professional business suit does nothing to disguise her femininity - in fact, it ironically serves to make it more beguiling. She is regarding him with a wry smile, and her eyes are locked directly on his open slit, as if trying to hypnotise him. Surprisingly, there is no one else in the room. 'There is no need to pretend you are sleeping, Mr. Deville,' her voice ripples across the room, massaging him. Her English is good yet decidedly tinged with Japanese, and whether caused by the accent or not, the voice inspires a feeling of uneasiness. Deville struggles to place it for a moment, captivated, then realises with shock that it's the same characteristic that marks car salesmen and high school hot shots. He opens his eyes and offers a smile that belies his inner distrust. Something is wrong. 'Seems we must have been introduced while I was aslumber,' Deville quips, correcting his posture as he stares across the room at this unique adversary. He feigns a yawn and a stretch. Relax her... Deville instructs himself. If she's kept you alive it's for one of two reasons: fear or respect. And they have no cause to respect an apparent madman. 'You know who I am, Mr. Deville,' she laughs. 'Or at least, what you know of me is enough. If it eases your mind to label you may call me Compassion, but names usually serve as a distraction from reality. Something to hide the truth. For example, I know that your name is Angelo Deville, an altogether slick, sinister, and darkly romantic title, yet I know nothing of the man. Does the name deceive?' She has slowly made her way over to his chair, strolling with the ease of a recently retired man, pausing from time to time to raise an eyebrow or quirk her lips. Deville simply stares, enjoying the performance. 'Who are you, Angelo Deville?' 'I am many things to many people,' Deville answers vaguely, inwardly rolling his eyes at entertaining these mental shenanigans. 'But if we are assigning ourselves titles, you may call me Resilience. I am a function of my surroundings, determining my actions based on my impression of others and what the situation demands. Or perhaps more accurately, I judge my esteem of others by how I'm inclined to feel around them. Sometimes it is pleasant. Other times...' Deville trails off with a wink, revelling in the woman's struggle to keep her eyes blank. 'And what of me?' Compassion demands simply, pausing almost on top of him with hands on hips, expectant. 'You are a riddle,' he begins with a wanton grin. 'Your intents are clearly pure, and your generosity of physical comfort and appeasement is boundless. You seek to please everyone with the same set routine, donning a moral mask.' She smiles at this description, and Deville pauses to let her enjoy it. It makes the payoff better. 'Yet ever since I met you I've been plagued by a headache and a bruised ego. You make me guilty of my transgressions and derail my self-preservation. In short,' Deville lies, 'you make me uncomfortable.' 'Do I?' She coos, gingerly seating herself on the arm of the grand chair and leaning in close. 'Have you considered that it isn't me that makes you uncomfortable, but something in yourself that I bring out?' Deville stares up into her eyes for a very long moment, mesmerised. Trying to think while staring into those great brown oceans is like trying to swim through jello. 'No,' the word comes slowly, dragged as if given reluctantly. She leans in closer and runs a sleek hand through his hair, causing seductive tingles along his skull. 'No?' She whispers, allowing her pinky finger to roam down his face, tracing a line from his temple to his chin. The skin she touches immediately becomes cool and refreshed, and her pleasingly soft smell intensifies his headache as her face draws in to mere inches from his. All he can see now are those bottomless eyes... And those rejuvenating lips. Pleasure courses through their minds like an electric shock as their noses touch teasingly. 'No,' Deville responds in a dreamy voice, just as quiet as hers but much lower. He arches his neck back slowly so that their lips brush, lets his breath warm her, then abruptly pulls back. 'You have no place in my life,' he states coldly, decisively. Her face droops with disappointment, then twists to a horrible hybrid of anguish and anger. She leaps from the chair, spinning around so that Deville can only see her retreating back. 'Then why are you here?' The voice sails over her shoulder, distant and without warmth. 'What do you seek so forcefully?' 'A man,' Deville responds, standing. Compassion turns around, mouth gaping. Her eyes are wide with surprise but also sudden understanding. Deville puzzles over the reaction for a moment, then his face is a mirror of hers. 'Not like that, damnit!' He explodes suddenly, and the hurt returning to her face is momentarily visible before she hides it once more. 'This man and I have business to resolve, and I understand that he might be found in what I can only assume is your organisation.' Her back stiffens at the mention of her business, but her face is unreadable as it faces the aquarium. Deville takes a step forward and places a hand on her shoulder. 'Where may I find Kimbusa? And quickly... Time is running out.' 'Kimbusa is lost to us,' she answers immediately, harshly. 'We do not speak of him anymore. He turned his back on us. ...Though I understand from my sources that he may still be seeking me.' She turns around slowly, peering meaningfully into Deville's eyes. 'You two were involved? Or is he after you?' Deville asks, not really following her meaning. 'A little of both, really. Kimbusa fights with himself to find me, as he always has. He realises that life without me is not really life at all... It lacks substance. Clarity. He will find me someday.' Compassion pauses thoughtfully. 'You will, too.' 'Perhaps,' Deville allows, narrowing his eyes in thought. 'But before I find you I really do need to find Kimbusa. You need to tell me where he is... Now.' His voice has an edge to it now, a desperate and threatening one that shines through his eyes and his entire being. 'Why?' She asks simply, her chin raising slightly in pompous pride. 'I will know what you are planning for him beforehand. In Japan we have loyalty... Among other things. Someday we hope America adopts them.' 'I'm going to kill him,' Deville says flatly, struggling to disallow his voice from growing to a growl over the ethnic insult. 'You're what?' Compassion demands, eyebrows rising like two surrendering arms. Her eyes dart from one glacial blue eye to the other, looking for some humanity to appeal to. 'I won't! You can't do that now!' She shouts after finding none. 'I can... I must.' Deville replies calmly. 'It's what the situation demands... What my survival demands.' 'You need me more than I ever could have imagined,' Compassion moans, pressing herself up against the Crimson Angel. 'Forget about Kimbusa...' 'I do need you,' Deville admits with a nod of the head, 'but not in the way you deem. I don't need anything from you either spiritually or emotionally. I don't need you to complete who I am... I operate alone. All I need from you is information. My only use for Compassion is the knowledge she possesses.' 'You shall not have that,' she denies him, pushing herself away once more. 'I come on my terms... Advise how I see fit.' 'I don't have time to mess around here,' Deville snarls, suddenly producing his pistol from the holster on his shoulder. He is plainly amazed they left it with him, but then many things of this house are extraordinary. 'Where is Kimbusa?' He demands again, aiming the gun at her neck. 'You cannot know,' Compassion sighs, kneeling down before him and guiding the gun to her head. 'It will damn your soul. You need me, Angelo Deville. Embrace me, now or never... One's most powerful weapon is Compassion.' 'No,' Deville argues, his voice distant with resolve. 'Only Resilience can set me free... You are but a burden intent on obscuring my path,' he dismisses her coldly, then to her shock and horror, pulls the trigger. Compassion dies slowly, wretchedly, regarding Deville with uncomprehending and scandalised eyes. ~><>...<><~ 'You seriously have the papers here? In the hospital?' Jack asks, incredulous. He's still visiting with his unfortunate boss, the Director General of the GTT, known affectionately as Phil. 'You're damn right,' Phil responds through gritted teeth. 'I've got ?em right here under my pillow. I'm gonna see that Deville hanged. Tomorrow morning, as soon as this infernal place releases me.' 'What are you going to do?' Jack asks, trying desperately to hide his alarm. 'Take them to the police...' he growls. 'Then get the board to vote the man out of our tournament. We can't let this man win.' 'Definitely not,' Jack agrees, nodding his head vigorously. 'How would that look, especially once those papers are released. Say... You mind if I look them over again?' You asks innocently, as if the thought had just occurred to him. Phil groans but lifts his head from the pillow just the same, allowing Jack access. Jack reaches down and grabs the pillow, and sure enough there are a few crumpled papers stashed on the bed. He shakes his head and grabs them with his free hand. The pillow being thrust over Phil's face comes as a complete surprise to the old man, leaving him without breath to scream while there's still time. He kicks his legs and tries to bounce his attacker off of him, but Jack has both arms pressing down on the pillow with the strength of a desperate maniac. His whole body shakes visibly as he heartlessly kills his boss, mostly from adrenaline and fear, but also as a result of a joyful giddiness he never suspected lived inside of him. Jack would have told you fifteen years ago that morality is the key to success, but now, having lived life and watched others prosper from their crimes, he extols different virtues. Whoever pays the piper calls the tune, he sings in his head as Phil struggles through his final moments. And the piper only accepts money... Morals, compassion, and good intentions be damned. Money moves mountains... The sadistic inner voice continues, corrupting him even as he commits the ultimate evil. Phil's body slows down, spasms several times, then goes still. Jack holds the pillow there until the irate beeping of the machine finally sounds, ascertaining that the job is done and letting him know it's time to get the hell out of there. He crushes the papers and puts them into his coat, then bolts from the room, replacing the simple disguise he had donned on his way in. Nurses rush past him in a fury, crashing through the door with all sorts of obscure objects in hand. Jack simply smiles and tries to control his shaking. These papers will never see the light of day again. All my life I've wanted to be rich... And now I am... Now I have the power to do as I please... Who would've thought it would be so easy? He trails off as he presses the button for the elevator, not feeling the least bit remorseful about the life he stole to obtain his status. And I owe it all to Angelo Deville. ~><>...<><~ 'If you're so certain you're going to get arrested as soon as you show your face, why the hell are we going back?' The rational voice demands from Deville's side. Everyone has been hustled back onto the plane, and Deville has spent the last few hours regarding everyone suspiciously, even the clouds they pass. There is a crazed look in his eye that scares his friend, Frankie, and the man is determined to ease it somewhat. 'I need to win this tournament, Frankie,' Deville responds mechanically, keeping his bloodshot eyes focussed on the window. 'I need to defeat Kimbusa in America if I want to do that, either by finding him before the match or dealing with him in the ring. Regardless, it has to be in America. We have to go back.' 'But it's suicide!' Frankie protests with vehemence. 'You said so yourself! The FBI will probably be waiting at the tournament to arrest you.' 'I know. I have a surprise planned for them,' Deville's eyes dart quickly to Frankie, and the man is convinced he's staring straight into the mind of a lunatic. 'To be honest,' Deville says, whispering suddenly. 'The FBI agents back in America aren't the ones concerning me. The on that's got my back up is the one on this plane.' Frankie gapes at Angelo, unable to respond. Deville's eyes burn into Frankie's for a second, meaningfully, then point accusingly across the room at Julian, who is sitting across the plane reading a magazine. Frankie exaggerates a stretch, twisting and turning his body to get a quiet look at the accused, then settles back into his chair and turns sober eyes on Angelo. 'Julian?' Frankie demands, incredulous. 'Paul has known Julian for years.' 'Paul got arrested last spring,' Deville counters. 'So? Paul was clumsy,' Frankie begins to argue, then realises his argument defeats itself. 'Alright... What makes you suspect him? How do you know he's a rat?' Frankie asks in a hushed voice. 'It's my job to know...' Angelo whispers mysteriously. 'I can smell it on him... And why else would he be pretending to be reading that magazine?' Frankie is forced to take Angelo's word for it as he doesn't wager another glance. He's concerned for Deville's well-being, but he's also concerned for his own welfare. 'You can't kill the man on a hunch,' Frankie objects. 'He's a good guy. You're just stressed out with all the shit that's going on.' Deville seems to consider this, relaxing in his seat and taking a deep breath. He doesn't exactly believe it's a figment of his imagination, but Frankie could be on to something. 'Maybe you're right, Frankie... But I don't want to take any chances.' Abruptly, Deville stands up and steps over Frankie into the hallway. His tired mind hasn't choreographed how this is supposed to go down, but he knows he needs to talk to the man. He's balancing on the fence of indecision right now, and needs something to tilt him in one direction or the other. He needs a sign. 'Hey, c'mon,' Frankie calls from behind as Angelo covers the short distance to Julian. 'Show some compassion!' Angelo stops dead, heart seizing for a fleeting moment. In his deranged mind, that coincidental term is enough of a sign to destroy the fence entirely. Julian looks up from his magazine as Deville plants himself in front of him, one hand resting against his heart inside his jacket. Julian's friendly look of greeting dissolves under the harsh and malicious stare of Deville, and his eyebrows draw down in concern before he speaks. 'Uh, hey Angelo... What's go-' he begins, but Angelo cuts him off. 'Give me your badge,' Deville instructs in a monotone. He seems to have cut himself off from all emotions, or perhaps simply embraced one to the point of harm. Julian looks at him, mouth working uselessly. Frankie comes walking slowly up behind the two and the card game pauses mid-hand as everyone turns to see what the commotion is. 'Let me see it,' Deville persists, this time adding a bit of force to the voice. Julian continues to gape, then suddenly breaks out laughing. His face goes red as he convulses and doubles over in his seat, overcome with the giggles. 'Shit man, you had me going for a-' Julian manages to say through the laughter, but everything cuts off at once as Deville's hand whips out of his jacket, gripping the gun, and whips Julian across the face. He looks up at his attacker in shocked fear, blood trickling from the outer corner of his eyebrow. 'Don't you laugh at me you fuckin' pig. I've no patience for your identity crisis. Who are you? Right now. Give it up and we'll take it easy on you... Insist on being a tweener and I'll tear you a new asshole.' Angelo riddles all of this off in a calm voice, his face motionless throughout. Julian's eyes dart around the room desperately, looking for some kind of help. 'Come on guys! Tell him I'm alright!' Julian yelps, his fear doubling by the second under their uncaring and suddenly suspicious stares. His head whips back to Deville. 'Angelo, wait! Stop man! I'm a criminal, just like you! Shit, I wanted to come help you with that Yakuza business, but you wouldn't let me! Let me prove myself! I'm bad, man... Bad. I'm a monster.' Julian's voice has a desperate tremble to it, and he's managed to bring up a couple of scandalised tears... If need be, he'll shed them. 'You're a bitch,' Deville corrects him, pointing the gun at his temple. 'And I know it. So stop playing games, pretending to be something you can't even begin to comprehend, and admit your weakness. Admit you're working for the almighty force of good, once and for all, or die as a pretender.' Julian stares at Deville for a long time, not saying a word. Finally, Frankie comes forward and puts a hand on Deville's shoulder. Deville spins around, ready to attack, and Julian springs up from his seat. Deville whips back the other way to reapply the gun, but finds one pointed square between his eyes. Julian's eyes still have the same fear and panic in them, but they're concentrated now. 'Alright!' He screams at the top of his lungs. 'Nobody make a move or Deville dies,' he cries boldly, and everyone stops reaching for their guns. Frankie takes a disbelieving step backward. 'I'm an agent with the FBI, and I'm taking all of you into custody right now. Somebody tell the pilot to land the plane!' Everyone just sits there, staring murder at him. Deville's lips curl up into a knowing smile. Julian takes exception to this. 'What are you grinning about, Deville? You're mine now! How does it feel? You like having a gun to your head?' Julian demands in an enraged voice, trying to toy with the man but working himself up instead. 'What do you know of life? Of death? Of killing? You think I've never killed before?' Angelo just continues staring drunkenly into his eyes, keeping that same smile plastered on his face. This seems to have a worse effect than any words could. 'You don't know the things I've done! The things I have to live with! You have no concept of who I am! Or who you are!' Julian, the compassionate one, continues. 'I could kill you right now! You're not so bad! C'mon, tough guy, say something!' Angelo winks. 'You want to die?' Julian screams, in a complete frenzy now. 'Push me, Deville! I dare- argh!' Julian's arm is suddenly twisted up behind his back, making him release his gun and cry out like a little girl. Deville hammers him on the back with his other hand and pushes him face first into the seat. The rest of the mob comes running over, making up for their earlier impotence with grand talk. 'Let's flay this fuckin' pig,' Frankie growls. 'Flay him?' Deville asks, feigning incredulity. 'What happened to compassion?' Frankie says nothing, but looks down at the ground shamefully. 'Compassion gets you killed, Frankie. And so does talking. Power does not talk or threaten, nor throw tantrums... It acts. It forces. It destroys. 'This man's power lies in his words, in his potential to do harm... But alas it is nothing, for his heart stops him from acting on his instinct. If he were a real man he'd have taken me out when I left myself vulnerable... But since he pretends to be good... Since he practices compassion... He must die. Yet evil lives on... I live on.' Everyone remains silent in the wake of his speech, except for Julian, who is sobbing into the chair and punching it futilely with his free hand. The mood aboard the plane is very solemn, with everyone staring at the ground and casting nervous glances at eachother. Deville shatters it with a sharp laugh. 'Damn Frankie!' He cries. 'We're gonna have to toughen you up!' Everyone shares a nervous laugh, even Frankie, who's blushing like a girl who's just been told she's pretty for the first time. 'Japan's all about meditation and spiritualism, right? Floating around in dreams and meeting ghosts and whatnot, right?' Deville asks everyone and receives a bunch of non-committal shrugs and mumbled answers. He rolls on anyhow, 'that's what I thought. Well, here Frankie. You take our dearest Julian here and send him skydiving. 'In honour of our next opponent we'll see if dreams really do save, or if this man will find his epiphany at the perfect moment. Toss this do-gooder off the plane without a parachute. Let's see how well good flies.' Julian begins to protest loudly, crying and begging for his life... For compassion. There is no weakness aboard the plane, however, and there is nothing supernatural in the air. Good doesn't fly. Good isn't saved. It simply falls unremarkably to its death, loudly complaining on the way down that this isn't fair while quietly knowing it has only itself to blame. It will realise its weakness only at the last moment, tricking itself into believing it a strength for all time before, foolishly entrusting that there is power in principle. Kimbusa will be the same. One cannot excel through the limitation of his options. He must keep an open mind to everything and take advantage of whatever comes... Even if it's evil. Deville chuckles under his breath as he watches his latest opponent plunge into the darkness he sought to escape, defeated by his own inability to cope with himself. Wounded by his desire to change. Killed by his compassion.
