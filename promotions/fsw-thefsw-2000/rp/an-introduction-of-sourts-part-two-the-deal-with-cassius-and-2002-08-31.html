An Introduction Of Sourts, Part Two (The Deal With Cassius And Boda)

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st August, 2002 OK… The deal between Chris Sloboda and I. To start with, whether he realises it or not, I've been fucking with Chris Sloboda for a long time. For a long time I have played him for a fool, used him as nothing but a lackey to achieve my own ends… Kinda like how Chip O'Meara is using him now, only far more subtly. There are two kinds of people in life, leaders and followers. I am a leader. Whether Sloboda realises it or not, he is, and is forever destined to be, a follower. With that in mind, here's the actual story… A few months back, Sloboda owned a wrestling federation by the name of New True Internet Championship Wrestling… NTICW for short. That place had once been great (though it's gone on to be what it presently is, a pathetic excuse for a federation, a shadow of it's former self), having been home to the likes of Xavier Kannon, Rush Mason and the Pharaoh. Boda, while running it, aimed to make it as great as it once was. Sadly, while he did have some talented workers to help him achieve this goal - the likes of Semore Smoke, Scott Taylor and Tyler Valentine - his incompetence and lack of brains meant that he couldn't do it. Ultimately, NTICW was ran out of business by its market rivals, particularly RUSH. To put it bluntly, Sloboda was not happy to see his business go down the drain, and he blamed one man - The RUSH majority owner, Jeremy Howard. I guess he didn't realise that all along it had been I, Cassius Deamont, who had made RUSH big, who had brought it ratings, who had given it credibility. If he had, perhaps this whole feud between us would have started much sooner, but in his stupidity he blamed Jeremy. As a result, he came to RUSH as an ally to myself and the man who at the time owned 25% of RUSH - Tim Walden. No matter what you say about Walden's wrestling talents, he is not a stupid guy. Or at least, not as much so as Boda. I realised this immediately, and knew that Boda would be far easier to control. And so I talked to a few people, made a few arrangements, to take Walden out of the picture. Soon he was indeed gone, and RUSH had a new 25% owner, a man I could easily use as a puppet - Chris Sloboda. Unfortunately, Sloboda had accomplished his original purpose - ridding RUSH of Jeremy Howard - all too soon, and, despite my ability to influence things directly around me, Boda was effectively in charge. Despite his bitter dislike for me, Howard had always known how to run a fed successfully. Anyone who remembers what I said about NTICW under his control will be able to anticipate what came next… RUSH went down the drain. Sure, it didn't close, but Boda managed to make it a complete and utter laughing stock. My goal had originally been to gain complete control of RUSH. Now I just wanted to get out. My method came to me during the run up to RUSH's Crossroads PPV. Boda detailed his makeshift, badly thought out plan to me. With Jeremy Howard out of the picture - god only knows where - he would put someone he claimed was Jeremy in an asylum, and use this to gain complete control of RUSH as its minority owner. Sadly for Boda, as the longest serving member of RUSH I knew lots of things, some things that even the staff didn't know. Knowledge is a far, far more powerful weapon than the brute force Sloboda favours, and I was about to prove that. It was on the day that Crossroads was to take place that Boda signed his 25% of RUSH over to me, believing that a contract he had signed earlier had made him the owner of the remaining 75% of the company. As usual though, his lack of intelligence let him down, and he didn't read the contract he signed. For, had he done so, he'd have noticed that RUSH wasn't being signed over to him. I'd used my knowledge to cheat Boda out of RUSH, just like he'd unknowingly done to me. I notified the man who could take control of RUSH of Boda's plan, giving him a means to take control. What I had done was revealed at the beginning of the Pay Per View. Sloboda came to the ring, ready to announce his new position as the majority owner of RUSH. He brought me out too, announced me as the new minority owner. Like I was really going to continue playing second fiddle to him. Rush Mason appeared next. Boda had no idea what was going on. When he was told, it hit him like a speeding train. When he signed that contract, he'd given 75% of RUSH to… Well, Rush. Realising his mistake, Boda turned to me, asking for his 25% back. I refused to give it to him. Not that I could have if I'd wanted to. RUSH was good as dead. Why would I want to dedicate my time and money to supporting a federation on its deathbed? I couldn't give Boda his 25% back, because I didn't have it any more. Rush Mason had wanted complete control of RUSH, and I was only too happy to give it to him. Of course, Boda was angry when he found out. Threatened he'd 'get me'. To his credit, he did make good on his word later that night, running in during my first blood match for my World Title against Chris Matthews and costing me the match, though looking back that might not have been such a bad thing after all, given that it gave me my ticket out of RUSH, and allowed me to lose the belt while saving face... Boda and I were soon both gone from RUSH, him through breach of contract when he signed with FSW - not that I knew at the time that it had been FSW he signed with - and I through being released. After all, why would the company keep me? They thought I was finished. They thought my wrestling career was over. More importantly, Boda thought I was finished. Boda thought he had won. And I was willing to let him think so. Only Kathryn, Erica and I really knew the truth. That I was watching, and waiting... Waiting to discover what federation Boda had signed with. It didn't take much waiting before I found my answer, as Boda showed up on FSW Glory. Not a surprise really, to see him back so soon, Boda never could bide his time. Boda had found a nice new job for himself too... As the lackey of FSW Commisioner Chip O'Meara. With that in mind, I knew who I had to contact... FSW CEO Paul Orion. And the rest, as they say, is history... * * * * * Back to the present day, or rather Thursday... 12:30PM, Thursday 29th August 2002. Cassius Deamont sits at a table in his apartment, having just finished off the breakfast that Kathryn and Erica had made for him. Coffee, Orange Juice, toast, cereal. Sure, nothing special, but it was nice all the same. There is silence for a few moments before it is broken as Kathryn speaks. “So, Cassius, what do you have planned for today?” she asks, leaning closer to Cassius as she speaks and smiling at him. “Well,” Cassius began, after pausing for a moment to think, “I’m really not feeling up to going out or anything… I don’t want to make it common knowledge, but to be honest I still haven’t really recovered from Crossroads… So how about we stay in and see how the old company are doing without me?” “Ugh…” A strikingly beautiful redhead, sat opposite Kathryn, speaks, with a look of contempt on her face. Erica… Cassius’ other lover. “That place… Really sucks. Why on Earth would we want to watch one of their shows?” Erica’s a little younger than Cassius and Kathryn, in her early mid-twenties, but is certainly a fully developed adult, as is signified by her extraordinarily well-endowed chest. Clad in a tight, dark red sleeveless top and a pair of cut off denim shorts, she, like Kathryn, knows how to dress to best display her body. “Maybe so,” Cassius begins his response with a smirk, “But there’s one thing that might be worth watching… Chris Matthews’ first – and, I’m betting, last – match as RUSH Champion.” “I hate him,” Kathryn remarks, and by the look on her face it appears that she means it… She truly does hate Chris Matthews, passionately. “Yes, well, I’m sure you’ll enjoy watching his entire world pulled out from underneath him, as it undoubtedly will be, then,” Cassius replies with a smile. “Well, I guess if that’s why we’re watching it’s fine then,” Erica responds, “I always enjoyed watching that fucker getting his ass handed to him.” “It’s nothing less than he deserves,” Cassius responds, with shades of his old satanic expression of pleasure at the thought of another in pain creeping onto his face, “After all, he was dumb enough to fuck with me…” * * * * * The time came, we watched the show. Matthews lost, as was expected. Another example of someone fucking with me to try to turn themselves into something, and discovering afterwards that they’re nothing... Boda will be the same. I’m Cassius Deamont. I have no enemies, save for ones I’m in the process of dealing with. I still don’t want to be a good guy, I still don’t want to fight on other people’s behalf, but given that I have no choice in the matter, I guess I can live with it. But good guy or bad guy, whatever those titles truly mean these days, I’ll always be the same. I’ll always be the person that people fuck with as a prelude to their own destruction. I wouldn’t want it any other way.
