Maintaining Perspective

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday 'What the fuck is this?' Deville's voice shouts over the lyrics of Eminem's newest single, addressing the lovely Kathryn sitting at his side. They are cruising down the highway in Deville's polished grey Caddy, and he obviously let the girls pick the music. Erika sticks out her lower lip and pouts into the rearview mirror, but Deville pretends not to see her. 'I thought you said you wanted to put on some music! So turn this crap off and find some!' He glares at Kathryn out of the corner of his eye starts with surprise, then licks his lips with a hunger no food can cure. Kathryn's green eyes stare up at him from beneath her raven tresses, a sad look contorting her angelic features. It isn't this that affects Deville, though, but the item in her hand and her position over it. She grips the strawberry ice cream cone with white-knuckled intensity, her supple tongue poised over the top scoop, struggling to reach it and give it a soothing lap. It flicks the tip and Angelo clears his throat noisily, then gets back into his lane with a snort. 'Goddamn women...' he mumbles under his breath, trying valiantly to ignore their piss-poor taste in music in favour of other qualities. 'Don't know why I bothered stealing you in the first place.' Deville steals another glance at the pair's pairs and the reason returns with an intriguing rush of blood. 'Fine,' he huffs, flooring the pedal so the engine growls happily. 'We'll listen to your crap for a while,' Angelo trails off to take in their worshipful smiles, then continues in a warning tone. 'But it means I'll be in a real bad mood for Cassius later... He won't just be leaving FSW... He'll be leaving the sport altogether... For medical reasons.' The squirming that began when he started his threats peaks as he trails off, and the two girls seem almost horny with excitement. 'Oh please do, Angie,' Erika purrs from the back, tying her fingers up in her thick red hair as she does. 'Oh yes,' Kathryn moans from his side, then takes a long sensual lick of the ice cream. 'Please Angelo.' Deville cocks his eyebrow at the two nymphs in his car and suddenly wishes he wasn't so damned late. He has roughly seven hours worth of meetings to cram into the next three, and then he needs to go retire the scummy club-owner who masquerades as a wrestler. That kind of half-assed horseshit might cut it in some bottom-feeding hole like RUSH, but this is FSW. Guys like Cassius are nothing in FSW. Still, there are a few things about the man that trouble Deville. The two ladies in his car foremost among them. He clears his throat and tries to picture them as amorphous blobs, then speaks in an off-handed tone. 'So, uh, you guys never did tell me why you were so eager to stab that little sonofabitch in the back...' He leaves it open-ended and glances at the women out of the corner of his eye. Erika just laughs. 'Cassius is a sleazebag,' Kathryn offers with an angry lick of the cone. 'And lately he's become a boring sleazebag... He doesn't do anything except get beat up and whine about his individuality.' Deville, getting more of an answer than he figured the woman was capable of, nods sagely. 'I'm not surprised,' he grunts, and prepares to elaborate on his own feelings about Deamont, but Eryka runs over him. 'Yeah, and he started trying to blame us for his trouble... As if it was somehow our fault he kept screwing up.' She huffs indignantly. 'Even when he couldn't get it up, it was somehow our fault. What kind of man is that?' 'It's not,' Deville replies crisply, shaking his head. 'Those, my dear lady, are the markings of a spineless bitch.' 'That's why we like you, Angie,' Kathryn coos, 'you're so dangerous and exciting.' 'Yeah well,' Deville grins, revelling in it. 'I do what I like.' Deville lets the words sit for a moment, then he scowls down at the radio, sitting their in gleeful contradiction of his boast. 'And on that note...' Deville says gruffly, switching on a Rolling Stones CD while squashing all protests with a warning finger. 'My car means my rules, damnit. You either listen to real music...' Deville pauses to stare meaningfully at each one individually. '...Or you walk.' Deville smiles and drives contentedly under the barrage of false whiny begging, enjoying the soothing sound of their voices as they plead with him to have a heart and take pity on them. His mind isn't completely at ease, however, as there is something in their bearing that warns of trouble. Despite their masterful attempts at masquerading their thoughts, Deville senses an uneasiness. Women, Deville extols, shaking his head in amused wonder. Another thought occurs to him and he darts a weary eye at both. 'You guys know not to get involved in my match against Cassius, right?' The tone of his voice betrays his overwhelming doubt that they know any such thing. Their cold stares confirm the suspicion. 'What are you talking about? We're your valets now!' Erika exclaims from the back, the very picture of scandal. 'It's our duty!' Kathryn jumps in, looking just as startled and betrayed. 'No,' Angelo sighs, 'it's not.' There is a brief stall as Deville tries to figure out a way to dance around this. 'Cassius is unpredictable, and you shouldn't underestimate him.' 'You told us he's an unwashed joke!' Erika counters, using his own words against him. 'Well, he is,' Deville confirms, then amends, 'to me, at least. It's a different story with you guys. Scum like him will be out to get revenge against you, and when you're as feeble-minded as Deamont the only means of achieving it is violence. He's crazy.' 'But you'll be there to protect us, won't you?' Kathryn implores, batting her large teary eyes. 'Look,' Deville says, resisting their seduction as best he can. 'The amount of good you'll do doesn't make up for the amount of trouble you'll cause. Let me retire this joke, and then--' Deville stops talking suddenly, his attention riveted to the dashboard, which is emitting a consistent beeping noise. He scans all around and sees the perpetrator up ahead, standing on the road and pointing a gun straight at his head. Wincing, he looks down at his spedometer and notes that he's almost fifty over. The cop takes his shot and dashes towards the door of his car, then jumps in and hits the cherries just as the Cadillac whips past. 'Ooooh,' Eryka claps excited, apparently forgetting her dismay of a moment ago. 'A chase!' Kathryn doesn't say a word, but the exuberance is mirrored in her face. 'It's not a chase!' Deville snaps immediately, but doubt is heavy in the words. Sonofabitch! I don't have time to screw with the cops right now. He glances at his rearview mirror and curses the rapidly approaching cruiser, then uncharacteristically slows down and pulls to the side of the road. The girls gawk at him with wide-eyed disappointment. 'I don't have time for this right now, okay girls? So just sit there and keep quiet,' their mouths drop open simultaneously as the cop car screeches to a stop behind them and the officer jumps out of his car. Deville watches with mounting rage as the cop fondles his nightstick, strutting up to the car like the creator himself. Deville allows the engine to run but turns the radio down, and he only rolls the window down halfway. 'G'day, sir,' the officer greets in a chipper yet condescending voice. 'In a big rush, are we?' He comes to a rocking stop in front of Deville's window and speaks without bending down. Deville glares at the man's hands as they loop into his pockets casually. 'Not at all... officer,' Deville chokes out, willing himself to be civil in these most trying circumstances. 'What's the fine?' Ignoring him, the cop continues in his same conversational tone, 'you were doing more than 140, you know...' He bends down and pierces Deville with his judging gaze. 'You do know that, right?' 'Yes sir,' Deville growls and prepares to continue, but Kathryn pipes up before he can utter another word. 'Good thing the radar detector went off when it did, Angie! You were doing 180 for a while!' She finishes this tirade up with a charmingly innocent smile, and Deville almost dies from the overwhelming need to strangle her. Deville stares at her blankly, all thoughts burned from his mind by anger. He mumbles a couple of curses at the roof, then clears his throat to interrupt the policeman's sudden keen interest in his dashboard. 'Radar detectors are illegal, mister,' the officer informs in his haughty tone. 'I know that... I don't have one.' Deville leans further out the window and beckons the officer near, as if ready to tell the man something of grave importance and secrecy. He gives his head a slight flick backward towards Kathryn, then explain in a whisper, 'she's on meds. She doesn't know what she's saying, and half the time I don't either... Just ignore her.' The cop stares at him doubtfully and is about to question Kathryn when she speaks up on her own. 'Oh come on, Angie,' Eryka joins in from the backseat. 'Mr. Policeman here already knows that little contraption is for radar...' She pauses and holds up a black briefcase that was sitting on the seat beside her. 'Just like he knows that this suitcase is full of bribe money.' 'Shut your mouth!' Deville cries, enraged, forgetting the policeman for a moment and spinning in his seat to glare at the woman. The officer grabs the collar of Deville's suit and pushes him against the seat, then leans in and flashes the women a look of fatherly concern. 'Does he always talk to you like that?' The officer demands of the girls, his eyes switching from warm and soft for them to cold and hard for Deville in a blink. 'Not always,' Kathryn assures the officer, and Erika shakes her head in agreement. 'Not always,' she parrots, 'only when he's been drinking.' Deville and the officer both share a silent moment of shock, then the latter unhands Angelo and begins opening his door. 'Step out of the car please, sir. Now.' Kathryn and Erika stifle a giggle as Angelo shoots them a glare, then busies himself with getting out of the car. Fucking women, Deville's mind roars, always screwing ya to get their way. You bitches want excitement? Fine. You brought this on this poor sonofabitch... It's your fault I have to end everything for him. He steps out of the car and into the brisk mid-afternoon wind, his black Deville Special already gripped tightly yet effortlessly in his left hand. The officer only sees it as it whips around towards his head, and by then all he has time for is widening his eyes. The bullet tears the top off of the officers head in a flash. One second the officer is standing there, pompously and righteously hassling Deville, and the next he's missing part of his head and falling to the ground, spouting blood like a water fountain. Deville jumps out of the way so none of the blood hits him, then jogs back to the police cruiser and quickly tampers with the instruments, then removes the tapes and hauls ass back to his car. Leaping in and closing the door, he whips around to face the two women who caused the whole mess. 'What the hell is wrong with you?' He demands, his heartbeat pounding savagely in his temples. For a moment only his panting breath disrupts the silence, then he erupts again. 'What the hell do you think you're doing?' Both girls' expressions are deadly serious now, and when they speak there is none of the seduction or giddiness prevalent before. 'We're getting involved with what you do, no matter what you say,' Erika warns him in a quiet voice. 'How that help is received is up to you,' Kathryn finishes, staring down at Deville as if from a pedestal. Gunning the engine, Deville shakes his head. 'You could've picked a better way to demonstrate that! That poor sonofabitch is dead now! You made me ruin his career! You made me kill him!' Deville shouts all of this, getting more and more appalled as he goes. Neither woman pays him much mind, though, as Kathryn turns her attention back to her ice cream and Erika tends to her nails. 'So what?' Erika asks him, callously. 'You like doing this stuff, remember? You live for it.' She says this last as if it's the most obvious thing in the world, and Deville can only stare feebly at her for several long seconds. 'Oh right,' Deville says, his face clearing like the morning horizon, as if a heavy cloud of confusion has been dispelled. He grins at himself in the rearview mirror, liking the devious aura that stares back at him. 'Forgot about that,' he says simply, and drives off down the road without another word.
