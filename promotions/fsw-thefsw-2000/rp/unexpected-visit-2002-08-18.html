Unexpected Visit

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Post Genesis James, FSW snack vendor extraordinaire finally comes down off of his adrenaline rush. Soaked with sweat, he arrives at the hotel at Disney driven by a limousine. The driver gets out and opens the door for the thin man. James pulls out his briefcase, clutching it near to his cheat, and makes his way to the door, through the lobby, and into the elevator. He presses the 6 button, and starts his ascent to his room. “James…” He hears a voice call out. He looks around the small elevator, and sees nothing. He shrugs, and stares impatiently at the numbers above the door. Finally the number six lights up and the doors open. James makes a quick right down the hall, checking behind him, making sure Angelo Deville is no where to be seen. Another quick right and he’s in front of room 613. He inserts his key, and opens the room into the darkness. Quickly, he turns on all the lights he can from the front door, and looks around. No one’s there. Finally, he can relax. “James…” He hears a voice call out again. Impatiently, he searches the room, opening small drawers in the nightstand, and checking under the bed. He finds nothing. He looks at the Television, and turns it on. “All right! Free Pay Per View! Let’s watch Genesis again!” Ming and Michael Kreig are celebrating their Tag Titles. “I’ve got time, let’s grab a quick shower first.” James gets up and enters the bathroom. He closes the door behind him, and the water starts to run. James sighs as he gets under the hot water, and he relaxes further. As he soaps himself, he hears a faint noise, coming from his room. “Must be the TV.” He rationalizes. He puts his head under the faucet, and again hears a noise this time, much louder. James leaves the water running and grabs for his plush white towel. It’s not on the toilet where he left it! Nervously, he walks quietly, and nude into the other room. Staying close to the wall, he sees a shadow that wasn’t there before, standing near the bed. Tears, well up in his eyes, as he looks. He jumps out, hoping to surprise the…Oh. His suitcase fell off the bed. Not much to worry about. James rolls his eyes, and looks across the room, to see his towel on the bed, where he left it. He walks over to it, and grabs it, his heart finally slowing down from it’s momentary scare. “James…” he hears again. Angrily, James looks behind the curtains, under the bed, and under the desk. Nothing. “Someone’s messing with me, goddamnit! I hate being messed with.” He says as he storms off, back into the shower. In a few moments, all is forgotten as he stands underneath the hot water once more, washing his hair. He turns the water off, and gets out of the shower. Slowly, he dries his face, and stands looking at himself in the foggy mirror. He poses a few times, his miniscule muscles, barely moving underneath the rubbery flesh. “Did you see the way I took O’Neil out tonight? Man oh Man, what a move that was.” “Yes, James. It was quite a move.” James shrieks as he looks into the mirror and sees the face of Jason O’Neil staring back and angry snarl on his lips. O’Neil rears back and throws a fist through the mirror shattering it into little pieces all over the floor. James starts to run, but falls on the floor, and onto a few of the pieces of glass. He holds his leg in pain as O’Neil climbs over the counter, and picks him up by the hair, dragging his bleeding body across the floor. “So, James, you think you’re slick don’t you?” O’Neil says as he pulls James to a standing position. Before he can answer he throws a right hook across the cheek of James, sending him crashing down onto the bed. James screams once more as O’Neil follows him down, grabbing his face so he can’t turn away. James starts to fight out of O’Neil’s grip, but O’Neil, filled with rage, holds him down, and pulls rope from his pocket. He ties James’ hands behind his back and forces him down on the bed. He reaches down into James’ suitcase, and pulls out his used, nasty underwear. He forces it into James’ mouth, and pulls duct tape from his jacket pocket. He tapes James’ mouth shut, and then tapes his feet. He stands up, as James stares at him in fear. O’Neil pulls out a chromed .45 magnum and lays it across James’ chest. He looks down at the pathetic snack vendor, sneering and spitting, breathing hard, as he speaks to him. “You mother fucker. You son of a FUCKING Bitch. Look at me James. I’m swearing. I’m fucking swearing. You know when the last time I said a dirty word was James? Do you? Of course you don’t. It’s been years, James. Years since someone’s gotten me this angry. But you, you mother fucker, you’ve done it now. You and Angelo. That was good. You got me. I actually cared, James. I actually gave a shit about you, about your mother. I broke character for you, James. Instead of just sitting by, waiting for Angelo to make a mistake, I made one.” Jason walks over to the briefcase sitting on the nightstand. He opens it and looks inside. “Quite a little booty here, eh James? It’s a shame really. It’s a shame that Angelo knows nothing about this countries legal system. Otherwise, he would have known that he should have paid you no more than nine thousand dollars for this little venture, because you wouldn’t have to pay taxes. So, let’s take five hundred and eighty thousand out of here, just to make sure Uncle Sam knows that you’ve been paid for your fifteen minutes of fame here.” James starts to wiggle on the bed, trying to get over to his money. O’Neil walks over and smacks him across the face, sending blood flying across the duct tape, and his face from his cuts in the shower. James stops moving and tears flow from his eyes. Jason picks up the .45 and points it at James. “As I was saying. Now, being your lawyer, and this case now being settled, I’m entitled to 35 percent of your gain, James. That’s three hundred and fifty thousand dollars, James. Good bit of money I earned for taking that weak chair to the face, isn’t it? I’m glad your mother signed me to a retainer, James, otherwise, I wouldn’t be able to touch a cent of this money. Believe me, that’s a good thing. Oh, and your medical bills. These days, those are quite expensive, and since your insurance hadn’t quite kicked in yet, that’s sixty thousand dollars you owe. And, we can’t forget the funeral expenses for our dear friend Thomas Brock, can we? After all, he’s entitled to his share, isn’t he?” James starts to stir again, but O’Neil points the .45 in his direction. James stops and stares at O’Neil, crying his eyes out, moaning and blubbering like a child. “You know how much money that leaves you with, James? Take a guess. Here, I’ll give you a hint. It’s more than 50, and less than 100 dollars.” James’ eyes go wide, as he stares at O’Neil in disbelief. He starts to talk, underneath his gag, but stops when he realizes, it really doesn’t matter. “That’s right James. All this work, and what’s it gotten you? An extra seventy five dollars? Good job, son. Way to think with your head, instead of your ass.” O’Neil stands and walks over to the window. He opens the shade and looks out into the darkness. “James, tonight, you made a mistake. A big mistake. A HUGE mistake. We all do it. Hell, I did it trusting you. But no more. A wise man said ‘Learn from your mistakes.’ I indeed have. Now, you should learn, James. Never Fuck with the Scarecrow. In time, Angelo Deville will learn that lesson. Good luck to you James. You're going to need it.” O’Neil grabs James’ briefcase, and his .45. He smiles at James, and walks out the door, down the hallway, and into the elevator. A low shriek is heard, building higher and higher until it turns into a loud wailing cry. O'Neil smiles an uncharacteristic smile, and watches the doors close. Tonight belongs to the Scarecrow. He’s gotten a measure of revenge. But the battle has just begun. Angelo Deville, would have to wait for another day. It’s time to turn his sights to a larger problem.
