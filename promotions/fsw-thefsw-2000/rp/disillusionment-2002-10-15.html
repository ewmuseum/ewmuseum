Disillusionment

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Unknown It smells like slow death in here... The toilet is running again, and each subtle drip is like another small crack in the fading strength of Kimbusa's sanity. It's been days since there's been light... The cell seems to be closing inward, its walls slowly but surely sinking in to envelop Kimbusa. When will they come for me? It had been days since Kimbusa had seen another living soul, and longer since he had spoken. For a moment, he wonders if he still posesses the ability to speak the English language. The cell is so cold and dark that his mind has yet to recover from its trauma so many nights ago. He has been left in a state of total disorientation and confusion. There is no day or night in this place, no sense of time at all. Merely moments passing along in strings of insignificance, taking larger and larger pieces of Kimbusa's mind with them. Where am I? Kimbusa had taken to trying to piece together the events of the past few nights. For some reason, the stench of his foul prison cell had clouded his memory, slowly but surely stripping him of any sense of clarity. His mind had become hopelessly clouded, as if the harder he tried to think the more difficult it became to put together a complete thought. The darkness, the dizzyness, it had become maddening... The worst part seems to be that there is no telling how long he had been there, how long it would be before his release, and where he might be taken next. Still, all he could remember was his match... John Jupiter, his partner for the evening's successful bout, had assaulted him after the match with a steel chair, which ultimately induced this state of inebriation. Revenge must be taken on Jupiter. But thinking about it more, Kimbusa realizes that he can't put a face to the name. Forget Jupiter for now. Kimbusa struggles to rise to a sitting position. He had been lying on his stiff, smelly prison cot for ages now, what could very easily have been four days. Four days and nights without movement. His back has become sore, and his head begins throbbing with pain from the rapid change in position. With a groan that ripples its way down the vast coridoor beyond the steel bars concealing him, Kimbusa gradually rises to his feet and shuffles to the steel. Slowly, he wraps his stiff fingers around the ice cold steel, and peers out down the hallway. His vision reaches barely ten feet, and affords him a view of what seems to be an empty, barren concrete hallway, devoid of anything bright, joyful, or alive. How had this happened? When he started Caldera's tournament, he had been content to sit in isolation in the most remote of locales, brooding amid a sea of desperation and hatred. With each victory he claimed in the tournament, however, he had slowly gained a bit more confidence in the sport, and in himself. He made it all the way to the semi-finals of the tournament, which climaxed for Kimbusa when he realized that he had found true love in his former sensai's daughter, Epiphany. But within a matter of days, the world had come crashing down on him; gone were the bright blue skies, the singing birds, and the blossoming roses. Once again, Kimbusa had found himself in a foul, forbidding environment, not entirely unlike that of his first twenty one years of life. In fact, he seemed to be living the perfect metaphor for those years... trapped in a dark box devoid of caring, without any idea of what the future holds or where the past went wrong. Deep down, Kimbusa only wanted one thing... to be with Epiphany again. He wasn't sure what had become of her. He hadn't seen her since the day before his fateful contest against Angelo Deville. He had raced through the match with little caring, wanting only to return to Japan and run away from the control of Steven Caldera. But Caldera had caught him first, and quickly brought him back to fight in FSW... before damning him to the insanity of a week without human company. Two months ago, Kimbusa may well have felt at home. But now, the darkness is driving him mad. How long had it been? How long will it be? Kimbusa repeats the questions in his mind, as if requesting an answer from the darkness itself. But the darkness remains solid, concealing whatever may lie behind the shadows of the coridoor. What foul being lies at the end of the tunnel? There certainly is no light... With a hesitant sigh, Kimbusa painfully shuffles his way back to his cot. With an audibly moan, Kimbusa collapses upon the canvas covering again, closes his eyes, and goes back to sleep.
