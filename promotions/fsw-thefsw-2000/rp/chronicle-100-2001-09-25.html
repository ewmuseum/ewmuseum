|The Light...|
neXt | 09/25/01
-=(The light is intense. A bright, blinding light, with no apparent source, coming from everywhere. We can make out the outlines of figures moving above us, but that's it. The pain...Voices, though. Plenty of voices, nameless voices, without meaning, having meaning only in the words they speak, and who they speak them of.)=-

"You're a damn fool...You can't have this man leave his bed, let alone compete."

"You have to understand...This man, he has two matches this weekend...My, my company...You can't do this to me..."

"This MAN, was SHOT, in the HEAD! He probably won't live, let alone make it out of this bed for months. We haven't even begun to expect the problem..."

"Then what in the bloody hell have you been doing?"

"Clean up, mostly...Dried blood, ripped skin...We go in to check it out tomorrow. If he's still alive by then...If not, we give him to the autopsy ward."

-=(One of the figures bends over, and wipes a damp cloth over our view. My view. Whatever. He straightens again, and disappears into the undying light again.)=-

"Who knows. Maybe the bullet was shot offsenter, and missed any vital areas. We won't know until we go in. The examination begins tomorrow at 11. If you want to come, go ahead, but I wo-"

"Don't worry. I've been saying he needs to get his head examined for years, but I didn't count on being here for it."

"I really don't think this is the time for humor of that sort, sir..."

"Yeah, well...Yeah."

Odd...obligation? Commitment? Why do I feel this now? Hallowed Nights....I feel I owe something to him...That voice...Ah...Coming to check up on me....Thanks Johnny.

"Well...I'll leave my number with your secratary...Call me when something gets figured out."

"Will do, sir. Let me show you to the door."

-=(Ow...a sharp pain, and everything goes black for a minute...Then white...Black...Colors come in and out, flashing for split seconds...)=-

I'm on the border. I'm between life and death...Beautiful, holy, unloved life, life thrown away, life not lived to its fullest, or escape. The easy way out. A death not wanted, a death not justified....Why me? If only the eyes of that man had stared me straight, I'd know my killer...I must die a death not named...If only he could have stared me straight with those eyes...Wait..that one eye..

-=(Suddenly, all goes black. The battle is over. The boundries between life and death, which path to follow, which way to go...has been chosen.)=-
