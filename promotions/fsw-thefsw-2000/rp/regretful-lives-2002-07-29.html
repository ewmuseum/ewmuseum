Regretful Lives

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/29/02 ~The scene opens in the state of Connecticut on a very windy and cold day. As you look around the scene, the interstate going through Connecticut is visible as cars fly by and their way to and from various locations, some for business and others for personal pleasure. As you look further down to the level of the ground and off to the sides of the interstate you see what appears to be a never ending line of trees. Most if not all the trees and roads in the area are covered by a somewhat thick layer of snow. You come down to a large mansion somewhat hidden back amongst the trees. The first thing you see is a large black iron gate with the top of the gate partially covered in snow still. You can barely make out the words on it as they read “Davidson Estate”. You go further into the massive complex and notice the well kept condition of the grounds surrounding the huge mansion when you notice for the first time how much older the mansion itself appears to be then the rest of the surroundings. As you approach the house the scene slowly begins to blur and when it comes back into focus you are no longer outside but are now inside of the mansion in a dimly lit room. The room is obviously quite a large one with each wall being covered by a large bookshelf which is in turn loaded down with books as far as you can see. Sitting a few feet away from the far wall you see a large oak desk set there and at the desk there appears to be a person seated. The only light coming in through the round window across from the desk is just enough to cast the light on the person that as you come closer you can see that it’s an older man. He has thin gray hair covering his head as well as a bushy gray beard which looks to need a trimming. To his right there is a wooden cane placed up against the desk itself and on top of the desk is seated an ashtray with a freshly lit cigarette sitting in it. On the left hand side there appears to be an ink jar and an old fashioned feather pen sitting in the ink. The man reaches to the cigarette and lifts it to his mouth before breathing in a slow long drag and then slowly blowing the smoke back out. He places the cigarette back down into the ashtray and looks down at the desk right in front of him. For the first time you notice a book laying there in front of him. The book is already flipped open to some point past the start and all you see there now are blank pages. He turns the page back a bit and on all of the previous pages you see writing in a spidery handwriting. He looks over the previous four pages before turning back to the page he was at and reaches up to take the pen out of the ink jar. He brings the pen down and slowly he begins to write on the first blank page he finds.~ ~The Davidson Chronicles Chapter Ten: Five Star Wrestling 7/28/2052 by Tyler Davidson~ Where should I begin? It has been such a long time since those events have happened that even I have trouble remembering them exactly as they were. Perhaps it would be best to start with a quick description of myself. My name is Tyler Davidson and I am....was a professional wrestler. That was a long time ago though. Most of what you will be reading in this book took place over forty years ago. The earlier chapters such as the one I am about to write took place fifty years ago. Nearly fifty years ago to the day I believe. I decided to start with something that wasn't quite back at the start of my career as this will be the time where things really started to happen for me. This will be the time where I finally began to become noticed in all my achievements. $oldout Wrestling was all but dead and buried and I had left before the sink finally sank. That had brought me now to my time in FSW. A new promotion and a new day had dawned for me there. It was without a doubt something which could lead to a much larger scale of grandeur for me. FSW has long been a place where legends in this business had been born. The fact that all of those legends had turned into pale, pitiful, shadows of their former selves is irrelevant. Men such as Big Daveylicious, Leon Chambers, Mike Marvelous, Epitome of Evil, and Jacob Jackson, the Five Star Five. They were men who consider themselves legends in this business, yet were obviously terrified of losing their “spot” in the business. They were terrified by the new legends which the business had suddenly seen emerge. Men like Timo Bolamba, Lance Perfection, and myself. Even a man like Chris Bagwell could have laid more claim to being a legend than the Five Star Five at the time. A war had already begun though it was nearly invisible to the fans eye. It was more of a war which seemed to be fought in the backstage area. The boys and the locker room were seemingly divided into two groups, the old guard of the legendary FSW and the new guard which had appeared for the FSW revival. This was one battle that was not even as clear cut as faces versus heels. The battles would surpass even those notions of the wrestling business. The battle which I now had my sights set on though was one of a different sort. The issues between myself and Chris Bagwell have come nowhere near an end. Week in and week out the battles between us would continue with each of us trying to one up the other. None of that would ever change though the things me and Chris actually had in common came into startling realization I would eventually realize. We had both traveled the same road on our life but they each went in separate directions. I made the one mistake which he would certainly hope never to make. I was now spending my free time at the beach house which I still owned in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. The show was in Charlotte so it was nothing but a short drive from there for me to make it to the show. The beach house was bought by Heather before he death in New York City. It was a death which I had not been present for due to the demands of the wrestling business, and the demands of the fans. Now it’s just me at the house and alone except for the constant presence of my own thoughts and demons. How do you think it feels to have the most important thing in your life suddenly torn from your soul? That is what had happened to me and admittedly it was something I had never dealt very well with. Perhaps I should have gone for counseling on the matters like people thought but I never did. I simply threw myself further and further into the void which my jobs had created. Whether it was the reading of a new script sent to me, training for a match, or work for Davidson Enterprises. There was always someway for me to escape the realities of my life with little visible blemish upon myself. The image which had grown on the inside however remained a different story. Perhaps a bit more bitter and darker I had grown in my time immediately following the events. The death of my brother first in September of 2001, followed by the death of my fiancé in March would be enough to damage the psych of most people. It was always my travels which would save me from the realization however. Despite where my travels took me it was always the beach house which had been considered most like home for me. The family might not have been there but the people I had become friends with always were. It was a constant revolving door on some days at the house though this one did not seem to be one those days as I sat by talking with myself. “Why is there never anything to do here when you want there to be?” I asked myself as I sat on the couch watching some old wrestling tapes of Ricky Steamboat. Little did I know what kind of turn my life would be taking in the current time. I had been going for weeks now in FSW gathering up a lackluster record and displaying an even poorer work rate. The doubts and critics had already begun getting to me and making me think that the future certainly did lie away from the wrestling ring. Who was I to argue? I had given little in the way of contrast as of late. Perhaps I should have picked up one of the many scripts and accepted one while saying to hell with the wrestling business. That never was my style though. The more I was told I couldn’t do something, the more I tried to prove everyone wrong. Maybe that is why I had held on to the past for so long. Soon I could feel the past would finally be catching up to everyone, me especially. It wouldn’t happen before I had the chance to even the score with Bagwell though. That was the first thought on my mind now. Wednesday night Glory would be our first encounter in the ring and I would make the most of it no matter what I had to do. After all, what did I have to lose? Not a damn thing and the whole world would soon see why. ~The scene changes back to the library room where the old man is seated. He places the pen back into the ink jar and takes another cigarette out of the pack and lights it up. He looks down at the previous pages he had written in and quickly scans over each of the pages before closing the book as the scene fades to black.~
