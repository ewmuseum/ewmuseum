Motivation

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'That's it. I'm bringing my recliner to the show next week.' Steven Caldera rubs his lower back in pain, obviously unstatisfied with the chairs provided for the broadcasters at ringside. Kimbusa is unamused. 'I am tired. What do you have for me?' Caldera smiles. He looks around, quickly taking in the surroundings. They are in the airport parking lot, as Kimbusa has just arrived. Commercial flights have always bothered Kimbusa, and the big man seems a bit anxious to find a more solitary place. The airport itself is quite busy, and the enclosed garage is bustling with life. The roar of a large jet taking off rumbles down the terminal, causing Kimbusa to grimace in anger. 'I do not like it here,' he snaps at Caldera. Caldera's eyes have finally found their target. 'Here. It's time to go.' A black limosuine pulls up against the curb, and comes to an abrupt stop. Caldera cheerily opens the door and steps aside to allow Kimbusa to enter. Caldera gives him a goofy grin as he slides on his sunglasses, and the limo sags beneath the newly presented weight of the former sumo. Caldera adjusts his glasses and slides into the limo as well, slamming the door behind him. Kimbusa meets the other man inside with a slight air of recognition. The man is of merely average size compared to Kimbusa, but seems fit enough. He also wears an enthusiastic smile; he is Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens, the man who is to be his partner. Caldera shouts to the driver. 'Let's get out of here.' The limo begins to pull away, and Stevens extends his hand to Kimbusa. 'Welcome,' he says happily. Kimbusa does not seem impressed, and does not move to shake Spitfire's hand. Instead, he turns back to Caldera, whose attention has been momentarily diverted to a rather attractive redhead on the curb. Kimbusa speaks firmly. 'What am I doing here?' Caldera strains his neck to watch the redhead as long as he can, before snapping back to face Kimbusa. 'Strategy. I know you don't much care about your opponents, but Mike and I have plans. Remember that favor I told you about? Well, this is it. I brought Michael in to FSW because he's one of the greatest young talents I've ever had the privaledge of working with. You're here for your own reasons. Point is, you two can do some damage together, despite your conflicting motives.' Kimbusa seems displeased. Stevens has since retracted his hand and too seems a bit upset, mostly with Kimbusa's lack of enthusiasm. 'What is it you want from me?' 'I just want you to know what you're up against is all,' Caldera explains. 'You're facing two of the bigger names in the sport, my friend. Now, Spitty here can hold his own, but we're not fooling ourselves. We need you to cap off the team. I can't wrestle like I used to. We need you on our side.' 'What are you talking about?' Kimbusa asks suspiciously, eyeing Caldera. Stevens chimes in with an explaination. 'We're set to take on Boda and Cassius Deamont next week in a tag team match.' Kimbusa turns to Stevens and delivers a monstrous glare. For a while the limo is slient, and only the light hum of the tires against pavement occupies the compartment. They turn a quick corner, causing the champagne bottles in the corner to clink lightly. Caldera smiles and turns to select one, as Kimbusa continues his suspicious interrogation. 'I need no partner. I want no partner. I told you that I would wrestle at your side, Mr. Caldera, in return for the favor of my freedom. But this...' Kimbusa trails off, again looking down at Stevens. Spitfire seems to become noticeably insulted. 'Look, big man, I'm not afraid of you. I want you on my side, but if you're not game, just step out of this limo right now, and you can go back to that cold little prison cell of yours,' he says intensely. Caldera quickly jumps in to end the altercation. 'Look, gentlemen. This is your shot at the top. Kimbusa, my friend... If you two can defeat Boda and Deamont, you're certain to go all the way. Do you know what you're involved in, Kimbusa? This is a tournament for the FSW World Title. And do you know what the world title brings? The World Champion is the most wanted man in the industry. And that means each and every week you'll have a new face gunning for you... a new face to destroy.' Kimbusa's interest finally seems to have been perked, but he still seems suspicious. 'I care not for titles. But I do like the sound of your proposition. Mr. Stevens, you may have the title. All I want is the blood.' Stevens smiles in response. 'Kimbusa, this sounds like the start of a beautiful friendship.' A loud pop startles everyone in the limo. Caldera has opened the bottle of champagne, and is preparing to serve. 'Let's make a toast... to the most dominant team in Five Star Wrestling...'
