Mike and Timo's Excellent Adventure

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-05-02 The opening beat of “Break Away” by Big Pig hits as a black screen envelopes the room. The beat slowly progresses, followed by some synthesized filler. A large diamond shaped object appears through a massive hole in the ceiling, and slowly comes down toward the ground. -All my life I’ve wanted to fly, like the birds that you see way up in the sky, flying free in the morning sun, flying high until the day is done…I Can’t Break Away!- The golden shape spins around as it comes down slowly, the music picks up pace, and the lyrics continue. -Like a child living in his fantasy, punching holes in reality, All my life I’ve wanted to fly, but I don’t have the wings and I wonder why…I Can’t Break Away!- The beat continues and the form still rotates down, portending what would be the greatest journey of two very unlikely compatriots. This would forever be known as…Mike and Timo’s Excellent Adventure! -I Can’t Break Away!- Starring: Mike Marvelous as Mike S. Preston Esquire -Mama told me, when I was young, stand tall girl your day is born- Timo Bolamba as Timo Timotoga Logan -You can be what you want to be, but you can’t change the course of your destiny, I Can’t Break Away!- Scott Taylor as Deputy Scott Logan -I Can’t Break Away!- Alexander Miyagawa as Miyacrates -One is One, and Two is Two, You have me and I have you- Brandi Wilson as Missy -Black is Black, and White is White, caught between the middle of day and night, I Cant Break Away!- Ming as Mike S. Preston’s Dad -No, I Can’t Break Away!- Corg as Ludwig Von Corgoven -Yeah Yeah, Yeah!- Lance Perfection as Abraham Perfection -My Momma told me, when I was young, stand tall girl your day is born- Trash Can Man as Trash Khan Man -You can be what you want to be, but you cant change the course of your destiny, I Can’t Break Away!- Duke Williams as Dukey The Kid -No, no, no, no, no, no- Charlie Loc as Charlie of Loc -I Can’t Break Away!- Kremmen Von Smackdown as Krempolean Smackaparte -I Can’t, I Can’t, I Can’t Break Away!- White Mexican as Mexican Freud -No, No, No, No, No!- Nerezza as Himself, but Playing the part of Rufus -I Can’t Break Away!- Argyle as Random Cannon Fodder -I CAN’T BREAK AWAY!!!- With: Tyler Davidson, Semore Smoke, Totally Tubular Tommy, Heavy Metal Harry, Chris Metal, Mordred, Paul Orion, Chip O’Meara, Ivan Stanislav, and Magnum Wolf. Finally with the last resounding chorus line, the large object lands and unfurls itself, forming into a telephone booth. A bright light emits and the flash is followed by tapping of footsteps in a very dark and hollow sounding chamber. Finally a form is scene, and its none other than Nerezza, the Digital Blood Sucker himself. He stops and smiles, then proceeds to introduce the scenario. “Greetings, My most esteemed colleagues, this is Five Star Wrestling, and I’m telling you, everything is great here. The matches are clean, the feuds are hot, and the in ring spots are superb. Ratings are way up, and Jobbers are way down…but it wasn’t always like this…Before the two great ones came into play, it was a world of Turmoil, hoping to stop the Fusion of their rivals. Long drained of Adrenaline, these two most triumphant heroes would Slam! The record books forever, and bring forth a new age of tranquillity. This is their story.” Wild ring activity is heard as two men train together, slamming into the mats and bouncing off of home made ring ropes. Mike stops as he jumps onto the second turnbuckle and holds his hands up for the camera Timo holds. “I am Mike S. Preston Esquire!” Timo hands him the camera and does a pose in the middle of the ring. “And I am Timo Timotoga Logan! Together we are…” Both together: Dastyrdly Duo! They begin to really reef on the ring now, tearing it up in mighty grappling holds. Finally Timo Irish Whips Mike into the ropes, and it snaps all three, taking the ring down with it. A cloud of dust billows from the garage door as they both come out of it, coughing hysterically. “Timo, I do not believe we will succeed until we get Meanstreak in our stable.” Timo quips back to his partner. “But Mike, I do not believe we will GET Meanstreak until we have pulled a most triumphant angle!” Mike smiles. “That’s why we NEED Meanstreak!” The two are seen entering Timo’s house and sitting in the living room as they pass is none other than Deputy Scott Logan, Timo’s father. He holds his hand up for Timo to stop and smiles mischievously. “Hey Timo, I want to talk to you.” He looks over to Mike. “ALONE!” Mike makes his exit and Timo looks sheepish as if he is about to be handed his ass…mostly because he is. Scott paces back and forth, finally coming to a stop right in front of Timo. Timo tries to smile, but Scott’s adamant face makes the smile drop immediately. “I got a call from Mr. Orion today.” Timo looks to the floor. “It seems you are failing this fancy FSW School I sent you to. What have you to say for yourself?” Timo’s head pops up momentarily. “Look dad, Me and Mike…” “Oh that’s right, Mike! You and your idiot friend spend all day long planning an angle that will never happen! Look, your final test is tomorrow, if you fail the school, I’m sending you to Colonel Diablo’s PWA Wrestling Academy…in Anchorage Alaska.” Timo tries to raise a fuss, but Scott will have none of it. “But Dad!” “No Buts Timo!” He paces around the Samoan, smiling. “I spoke to Colonel Diablo, he is…anxious… to meet you.” The door slams and Timo walks out, looking displeased. Mike is waiting with a quizzical look on his face. Timo stops and is very flustered. “Dude, we have to pass that test tomorrow or my dad is sending me to Diablo’s PWA!” “WHAT?!!” “Yeah dude! We are in some serious trouble!” Mike looks down at his watch and slaps his forehead. Timo looks at him funny and as he looks back up he has a grimace on his face. “Dude, we gotta go! We’re late!” “For what?” “School dude! The scene changes to a schoolroom. Presiding over the class is Paul Orion, he stands with his arms crossed as if he is being patient. Finally he looks out and speaks. “Mike, I’m waiting.” Mike is seen trying to contort a question in his head, surrounded by classmates. He looks up at Paul and shrugs. “He’s dead?” Paul sighs and slaps his forehead. He looks at his pupil and shakes his head in disbelief. “So what you are telling me Mike, is that Ed is a Hardcore…dead…dude?” Timo leans forward and smiles at the misfortune of Mike. He slaps him on the arm and laughs. “Way to go dude, you really blew that one!” “Timo stand up!” “What?” “Stand Up!” Timo does as he is told, standing to attention, and watching Paul Orion with every move. His dumbfounded expression is that of a child with nothing more than a cool wind between his ears. “Timo, who was John Hull?” Timo looks down for a moment or two, then thoughtfully looks back up smiling as if he has the answer. “Bret Hull’s brother?” The whole class laughs as the bell rings and everyone packs up to leave. As the two are leaving, Paul stops them at the door. “Boys, come back here, we need to have a talk.” The two proceed to turn around and walk to Orion, almost sulking as they do. He sighs as he leans back. Just as he is about to speak, Mike stop him. “Mr. Orion, before you say anything, I just want to say that without you, we wouldn’t have learned anything.” He leans forward emphatically, tapping a pen on the desk and smiling. “Is that so?” Both nod stupidly. ‘Well, what have you learned?” Timo pipes up first, quoting names off the backboard. “We have learned that thanks to great wrestlers like Ludwig Von Corgoven, Trash Khan Man, and umm The Miyacratic Maneuver…this sport is full…of history.” Orion leans back and half smiles, knowing these two to be full idiots. “Boys, it seems to me the only thing you have learned is that Clyde…was an orange monkey dude.” They both look to the floor, waiting for the rest of their scolding. “Look boys, if you don’t pass tomorrow with an A+, I’m going to have to fail you. Look, go out and try to get some work done.” The two start to walk out the door when he calls out to stop them. “Boys. This promo…it better be something special.” They look at each other in disgust as the scene changes to Mike’s room. They sit inside trying to figure out how on earth to pass their test. Timo is shooting hoops on a small backboard while Mike studies in the books. “Dude, who was Krempolean Smackaparte?” “Umm, I think he was like the first champion of PCW.” Mike looks up at him. “Are you sure?” “I dunno…wait a second dude, wasn’t he in that “Hall of Champions” thing at the academy? Mike gets a brilliant look on his face, and he smiles. “Yeah dude! What did he say?” Timo holds his left hand out as if he were announcing something, and smiles. “Welcome to The Hall of Champions.” They both laugh a bit, then realize the dilemma they are in. They sit on the bed in defeat as Brandi, Mike’s step mom knocks on the door, and their mouths both drop wide open. Timo finally breaks his trance long enough to look over and smack Mike. “Dude…it’s your mom!” He shakes his head and she smiles at the boys. Brandi saunters into the room and sits on a chair, looking at the books they have in their hands. “Hello boys, what’s going on?” Mike responds first. “Nothing Brandi…I mean mom.” She grabs one of the books and flips through a few pages. Looking back up she has a worried expression on her face. “Studying History?” “Yeah…we are destined to flunk most egregiously.” She turns the book over and sees History, then smiles. “Is that Mr. Orion’s class?” “Yeah” “Tell him I said hi, maybe he will cut you some slack.” She gives a knowing wink as the two look at each other in confusion. Ming walks into the room and stands to the side of Brandi. He smiles and raises his brows at the boys. The quirky father of Mike reaches for his wallet and pulls out a twenty-dollar bill. ‘Why don’t you boys take a small break, maybe it will help. I have some business…of my own.” They take the money and their books and head out of the room as Ming gets a devious look in his eyes. The door locks behind them, and a great deal of laughing and commotion can be heard. They look back up at the room, Mike with a disgusted look, Timo laughing. “Dude, that’s in your room!” ‘Shut up timo.” “Dude, but Brandi IS hot!” “Shut Up Timo.” “Remember when I asked her to the prom?” “SHUT UP TIMO!” The scene changes yet again and the two are seen sitting outside a Circle K convenience store. The sky is cloudy and dark, and they sit alone, pondering how the hell to pass the test. An old lady walks by and Timo pipes up to her. “Excuse me ma’am, do you know when the Mongols ruled china?” She shrugs. ‘I don’t know I just work here!” Timo and Mike both sigh and hang their heads. Mike looks up at Timo and swats him on the arm lightly. “Dude, you wanna try Super America?” “Sure dude.” Just as they stand up, something odd happens. The sky begins to thunder and lighting rips across the clouds. The mass of cloud cover swirls and opens, and a wind picks up. Both boys stand looking up as the phenomenon appears. Finally, a telephone booth flies down from the sky, landing on the ground resoundingly, and leaving a trail of fire around it. Both Mike and Timo look dumbfounded. Timo is the first to speak. “Mike, strange things are afoot at the Circle K.” The door opens and out steps Nerezza, clad in all black, with shades on. He removes them and opens his arms. Timo and Mike, not sure what to do look on in pure amazement. Finally Nerezza begins to talk. “Greetings my most esteemed compatriots. I am Nerezza.” Mike and Timo both look at each other and finally Timo says something. “Do you know when the Mongols ruled China?” Nerezza adjusts his glasses, then smiles. ‘Why don’t we ask them?” Mike and Timo both have the same thought pass through their minds. “Huh?” Just then the same aura of lightning and wind blows through the sky, and another phone booth comes down from above, this one being heralded by the screaming of two guys, not unlike Timo and Mike. They both look as it hits the ground and both have the same reaction. “Whoa.” The door to the booth opens and out step Mike and Timo. The original Mike and Timo look very shocked as they walk towards themselves. Timo and Mike reach Timo and Mike, and they begin to talk to themselves, causing a lot of confusion in Timo and Mike…naturally. “How’s it going Mike and Timo?” “No” states Mike. “Way” pipes in Timo. The new Mike and Timo look at one another and smile. “YES WAY! Look you dudes are going to go on a Most Excellent Adventure. You’re going to meet some oh histories greatest wrestlers, and have a most triumphant time.” Timo holds his fingers up, and he converses with Mike. They huddle down together and try to formulate what exactly is happening tonight. Mike seems to be the first to think of something. “Dude, what do we do?” “I don’t know man, how do we know these guys aren’t lying to us?” They both think about it for a few seconds before Mike seems to have an answer. “Dude, why would we lie to ourselves?” Timo shrugs and nods, and they turn around, smiling like a pair of idiots. Mike steps forward and asks them both a question. “Okay dudes, if you’re really us, what finisher hold am I thinking of?” The two new Mike and Timo’s stop and think to each other before answering rather quickly. “The Kick of Fear Dude!” “Whoa…Excellent!” All four of them begin to do poses while they make cheering noises. Finally Nerezza walks up to them after much eye rolling, and clasps his hands on their shoulders. They turn to see him and smile. “Nerezza!” Mike turns and says to the original two. “Dudes, listen to this guy Nerezza, he knows what he is talking about!” Nerezza smiles and turns them back toward their booth, and they briefly walk away, leaving the original two to their own devices. Finally after they converse with each other for a while, Timo yells out to the original two. “Hey Timo, don’t forget to wind your watch dude!” Timo looks down and nods, then Timo talks again. “Oh yeah, and give my love to the Titles.” ‘What?” “You’ll see!” With that they new two enter the telephone booth and the sky flashes. They suck through the ground and are only left with a small square of fire to show that they were even there. Nerezza walks back to the pair and holds his hand out, beckoning for them to enter the capsule. “Gentlemen, are we ready?” They both look at each other and shrug. They enter the small booth, and Nerezza dials a number, then slips on his shades. As they look about in uncertainty, Rezz smiles. “Dude’s…we’re history.” There is a bright flash and a sudden sinking feeling as the booth flies through the ground and enters into a long series of corridors and tubes. Electricity sparks about as the two boys scream at their speed. Suddenly Mike looks over to the totally calm Nerezza and speaks up. “Dude, where are we?” Nerezza answers quite quickly. “Not where Michael, but When. We are traveling the circuits of time, and we are about to go somewhere you have only read about, and possibly dreamed about.” Timo pipes up. “Disneyland?” “No Timo, this is far more enriching.” The booth finds its way to the end of the tunnel, and it flies into a huge armada of fans swept around two men in a pit fight. One is an eccentric looking fellow with a general’s uniform on. He holds his hand inside his jacket, while the other holds a knife in his hand. They circle about a bit before the guy with the knife lunges at him and misses. The hand comes out of the jacket, and thrusts a Tazer into the side of the other man. He twitches a bit and falls to the ground. The pin is made and the triumphant wrestler puts his Tazer away. “Triumph De Krempolean!” The wrestler sees the foreign object that Mike, Timo and Nerezza are in and makes for it with a chair in hand. They turn and pile into the phone booth, and open the gates back to San Dimas, sending the booth into the ground. Somehow, the man is sucked with it, traveling along in the circuits of time. The booth flies through the circuits again, and lands in Timo’s front yard. The noise shakes up a dog, and some lights flick on, but nobody sees anything out of the ordinary, just your average flaming flying telephone booth. They door opens and out step the three guys. Nerezza stands behind while Mike and Timo high five one another. They both exclaim exuberantly. “Not Bad!” “Gentlemen, I must be going now.” They both turn in confusion. “But Nerezza, I thought you were coming with to help us?” He smiles and places his hands on their shoulders knowingly. “My esteemed amigos, I’m afraid I cant do that. This part is all up to you. Always remember one thing though, my friends. The clock here in San Dimas, it’s always running. So no matter what, you still have to be back here in time for that report.” He walks back into the booth and punches a bunch of numbers, turning back one final time to say goodbye. “Mike, Timo, good luck my friends.” The booth disappears, and a new one flies down from the sky. As it does, a comatose form falls from the tree above, landing hard on the ground. Timo and Mike both seem to have an idea form in their head as they look down at the man. “Dude, that’s Krempolean!” “Who?” “You know, the war dude who used Tazers!” ‘Oh yeah!…Hey Timo, I just got a most triumphant idea! Let’s bring him inside.” The scene changes yet again, and we find ourselves in Timo’s bedroom, with Timo, Mike, and Timo’s little brother Byron looking on at the nearly out cold Krempolean Smackaparte. Timo talks to his little brother, holding his hands out for enunciation. “Now Byron, this is Krempolean, he is very famous and important, please take care of him. Here, this is like $20, take him shopping or something.” They hurriedly run out the door, but are stopped by Timo’s dad again. He looks at Mike and immediately Mike makes for the door. Timo stands with his head dropped as Scott circles around him, shaking his head. “I can’t believe you Timo. You waste all your time with that loser, you lose my keys, and now you are going to fail in the training camp. Pack your bags Timo, you are headed to Colonel Diablo’s PWA Training Camp!” “But Dad!” “No buts! I’ve already enrolled you. Your plane leaves tomorrow night. I want you here the rest of the night, so no funny business!” Just then the phone rings and Scott makes for it. He picks up the receiver and is greeted by an unfamiliar voice. “Deputy Logan, this is Deputy Frisco down at the station.” Scott mouths out the words “Deputy Frisco”, but continues to listen. Timo looks out the window to see Mike in the phone booth, and both of them briefly pose before making a cheering noise. “Look sir, we found your keys, and if you want them, you better come get them.” Scott shakes his head and heads for his bedroom, he stops Timo and points at him. “I’m going to the station, you had better be packed to go by the time I get back.” As soon as he makes it out of the room, Timo bolts for the door, sprinting onto the lawn and to a waiting Mike. “Dude, we have serious trouble! My Dad already signed me up for PWA! We are never going to make it in FSW!” “Dude, only if we fail!” They both look at the booth, then back at each other. “No Way!” The two compatriots run for the booth, jumping inside and grabbing the phone. Mike dials a number, and smiles, lookup up at Timo, who is anxious. “Timo, lets reach out and touch someone!” The phone booth lights up and shimmers, and finally delves into the ground quickly. Scott walks out the door and stops as he sees a ring of fire on the ground. He shakes his head and heads for the squad car, oblivious. The tunnels of time lead them on, twisting about like a snake, and shooting electricity like a broken circuit. They fly down one particular path, coming to the end of the tunnel and opening a hole in the sky. The phone booth drops to the ground in the middle of The Old West, and they step out, looking at the dusty surroundings. “Dude, it’s like a John Wayne movie!” “Yeah, except you can get killed here! They make their way down the street, finally entering a tavern. The two young men walk to the bar and sit down. Mike’s attention goes to the dancer, while Timo notices the barkeep at the end of the bar serving drinks. He grabs Mikes arm and points. “I know that dude from somewhere!” “No way man, this is the old west!” The barkeep turns around and its none other than Clyde. Timo phases out and thoughts flash through his head. Pictures of The Bartender from The U.S.S. Enterprise, The Dark Age praying for jobbers to rain from the sky, The two taxi drivers playing “Points”, Being stabbed in the darkness of Russia, Charlie’s Grandpa, dining with the sadist, being pitched the idea of buying Gabe Kaplan’s Head, and the list goes on. “I think you’re right dude, I don’t remember him.” “See I told you.” Mike looks to the barkeep. “Two beers please.” Without so much as a sadistic word, Clyde pulls out two of his most potent beers and pops the tops for them. Mike and Timo both grin and turn around, tapping the bottles together. “Dude, he didn’t even card us!” Suddenly, the door blasts open and a man with great prestige stands in the doorway. He immediately commands respect, Mike and Timo look on in reverence. They turn back to the barkeep for information. “That there is Dukey The Kid, Biggest Mouth in The West. I hear he is mighty handy with a pistol too.” He walks in, the spurs on his boots jingle jangle across the floor. He stops midway through the floor and calls out to the patrons of the bar. “I need two hosses!” Everyone slides their chairs out, except Mike and Timo. They both look at each other and smile. “We’re with you Dukey The Kid!” He eyes them both up and walks forward, grabbing them by the collars. They look down then back up, smiling. “Look, here’s the deal. What I win, I keep. What you win…I keep. You boys got that?” They both look at each other before smiling and nodding. “Sounds good Mr. The Kid!” He pulls them to the card table, and the scene shifts to them playing cards. The deal goes out and Timo is all smiles. Mike looks over and smacks him on the arm. Timo looks over to see what for and Mike rolls his eyes. “Dude, you got to have a poker face like me if you want to be a heel.” Timo gets straight faced, but when the next card flies, Mike shouts. “Whoa! Three Aces!” The card players look around and then at Dukey. They all grumble, finally one speaks up. “Three aces eh? You trying to cheat us Kid?” “Cheat?” Gulp. “Me?” Dukey overturns the table, and the men go flying. A fight breaks out with Timo and Mike caught in the middle. Dukey kicks some ass, sending a guy through the window. Four men advance on Mike and Timo and just as Timo puts his dukes up, no pun intended, Mike stops him. “No way dude, you got to do it like this…Look, we are totally weak, we can’t possibly fight you! Okay Timo, now we beg for mercy!” Both Timo and Mike drop to their knees and begin to beg. The fighters grabs them and launch them down the bar. They fly through the wall and their heads enter a burlesque house. The girls all giggle and wave, and just as the two guys smile, they are pulled out. “Dukey!” “You two got me in a mess of trouble now!” Three men advance on them, and Mike points to the sky. “Look, it’s the Goodyear blimp!” The men all look up, letting him deliver an Atomic Wedgie to them all. As they run for the door, Mike looks surprised. Timo grabs his arm when they get to the phone booth. “Dude, your old man’s trick really works!” Duke pulls his pistols out and as they pull him into the booth he doesn’t know what to think. “Look Dukey, we will bring you back as soon as you speak at our report tomorrow.” They hit some buttons and the door closes, sending flames out and the booth disappears. They hit the tubes of time again, and fall from the sky in ancient Greece. Mike and Timo step out of the booth, waving smoke out of the way. They walk up a series of steps surrounded by pillars and columns. They see a figure at the top of the steps surrounded by friends. He speaks in Greek, but appears to be doing an in ring promo. The fans cheer for the goggled one. Mike looks over and smiles. “Dude, that’s Miyacrates!” “What?” “Look it up dude!” Timo gets their book out and begins to read from it. He pages through and finds Miyacrates and smiles. “Miyacrates: The one true knowledge resides in knowing that I…am…excellent….dude….that’s us!” They both do poses and make crowd noises, then walk up to where Miyacrates is standing. He looks down on them curiously. Finally Mike looks to Timo and shrugs. “The true entertainer believes in the principle of cash in a flash.” Miyacrates looks confused. Mike pulls out several coins and smiles. “Cash” He pulls them out real quick and puts them in Miyacrate’s hand. “In a flash.” Miyacrates looks as if he has just had an epiphany. He reaches out to Mike and clasps his shoulders. He spouts something off in Greek, and it is translated below. “Like the hands of an hourglass, Time is Money!” They all laugh a bit, and pull Miyacrates along. They enter the phone booth and it disappears. Mike and Timo both try to calm Miyacrates down as they fly along the temporal spectrum of time. Finally they land in feudal Europe, much to the surprise of the locals. Most of them flee in terror, one man too scared to flee. Timo looks down at him and beckons. “Dude, where might we find some personages of historical significance?” The man simply points to a huge castle, and runs away, both Mike and Timo look up at the massive building then they both look at one another. “Whoa.” They all step out, Mike formulating a plan. He tells Dukey to keep an eye on Miyacrates, and pulls Timo along with him. They step up to the edge of the woods, and through the window catch site of gold in the light. “DUDE!” “WHOAH! REAL CHAMPIONSHIP TITLES!” They start to salivate, then Mike stops Timo. “Hey man, we are here on a history assignment, not a championship run.” “But Mike…those are HISTORIC Belts!” Mike racks his brain trying to figure out how to get in the castle, and the scene changes. Two guards walk by a few suits of armor, and as soon as they are gone, The suits begin to move. Mike and Timo step forward, totally loving the idea. “Dude I cant believe this worked! Check it out…I’m Darkstar!” Timo chimes in too. “And I’m Vennemon Pendragon! Prepare to die!” They start to fight with the swords, clanging them back and forth, coming to a head near the top of some stairs. Mike falls down and Timo helps him up. “Dude these things feel like steel.” “Yeah, a steel chair!” They pose and make cheering noises. Finally Mike takes the opportunity to smack Timo with the pommel of his sword, and Timo stumbles backward, falling down a flight of stairs. Mike quickly follows downward, and stops as he sees two surfer looking guys looking over the fallen compatriot. One of them pulls him up and hits him with “The Wipeout” the other jams on an air guitar. Just then, Mike runs in and looks at Sir Totally Tubular Tommy and Squire Heavy Metal Harry. He sees his fallen friend and gets pissed off. “You did a finisher on my friend you bad rip off’s of us!” Mike runs in and does a double eye poke followed by a MarveLOCK! On Heavy Metal Harry, but he can’t fight them both. Just as Tommy grabs him in a Full Nelson, a resounding CLANG! Is heard and he falls down, victim to a chair shot. “Dude, Timo, You’re Okay!” “Yeah man! I fell out of the suit on the way down!” They walk into the next room and on the wall, shining brilliantly are the titles. Mike and Timo both begin to drool as they see them. They reach out to grab the belts, but a somehow familiar voice is heard behind them. “What exactly are you two idiots doing?” They turn around to see King Chip the VIII standing before them. Mike points at the fallen guards on the ground and in true heel fashion, delivers the perfect line. “He did it.” Chip walks around a bit, trying to figure out just what to do. Finally he seems to have a good idea. “Put them…in the Torture Rack.” They both look at each other. “Torture Rack, That’s Lex Luger’s Move…EXCELLENT!” “Execute their contracts!!” “Bogus!” The scene shifts to a large platform used to execute by way of beheading. Two executioners stand tall, holding axes. Timo looks over at Mike as they are on the chopping block and then at the crowd. “Mike, I believe our journey has taken a most non triumphant turn.” “What are we going to do Timo! We can’t die here man, we just can’t.” Chip stands up to the crowd’s delight and smiles. He runs his finger across his neck and speaks forth. “Off with their heads!” The executioners raise their axes high in the air, then as they come down they totally miss the mark and hit the ropes holding Mike and Timo down. The axes plant firmly into the wood, and the two remove their masks, revealing Miyacrates and Dukey The Kid. The four men get up and run like hell, jumping onto a cart with the telephone booth on the back. Dukey fires a round into the air and everyone hits the ground. Miyacrates whips the horses and they take off with the King’s guard in hot pursuit. “Faster Miyacrates! Mike get a number, and hurry!” “I’m trying Timo!” An advancing troop with a mace runs along side of them, and Timo kicks him in the head, knocking him off his horse. Mike yells out from inside the booth as the others scramble. “I got one guys, hurry up!” They all pile into the booth as the horses carry on wildly. As they hit the buttons and the booth disappears, the man with the mace swings in the air, narrowly missing. The booth goes almost out of control and lands in a very futuristic place. Timo and Mike step out of it and see Semore Smoke sitting on a chair. He is dressed in futuristic clothing, and wearing sunglasses a lot like Nerezza. “Mike, I don’t think we are in San Dimas anymore dude.” Smoke looks down and pulls his shades off. He looks at the two closely as they check out their surroundings. There are pictures of them all over the place, and live action shots of them hitting finishing holds. Mike and Timo look almost perplexed as several dozen muscle bound people in tights that look to be wrestlers come forward.
