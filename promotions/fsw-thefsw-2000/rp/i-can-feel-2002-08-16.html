I Can Feel...

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 16, 2002 '...i...'          '...i...'                   '...i...' '...i can feel her breathing...' '...i can feel her heart...'          '...her warmth...'                   '...her comfort...' '...i can feel her breathing...' '...i can feel her life...'          '...her soul...'                   '...her desire...' '...i can feel her breathing...' '...i can feel her mind...'          '...her passion...'                   '...her hope...' '...i can feel her...'          '...i can feel her strong...' '...is she out there?' '...is anybody out there?' '...is anybody with her?' FLASH. The pulse thickens. Pace increasing. Each breathe becomes harder than the last. All in sadness. All in sorrow. Yet at the same time, all in hatred. Hatred for a man...          ...who killed his love. But now he can see the stars smiling back, knowing that somewhere mixed among the clouds is his lost love. She watches over him. She protects him through the nights. 'She was my hero...', words whispered by a broken soul. 'She kissed away my pain...'          '...and he took...'                   '...her breathe away...' 'I'm going to stand up...'          '...and see it through...'                   '...and defend her still today...' Her loss, so great to he. But yet at the same time, a new source of mental powering to drive him to the proceeding level of intensity. 'God bless...the...'          '..U..'                   '...S..'                            '..A..' 'For the time has ended in which we mourn...'          'And it has been known and has been sworn...'                   'The country will be taken by a storm...'                            'And starting with Tyler, they WILL BE T0rN!' FADE.
