Why ask why?

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/10/02 Why? It's a question best suited to philosophers, but I can't seem to escape it. And I'm no philosopher. 'Why did you do it?' 'Why did you come out and destroy Ramey, Ming and Asa?' If you have to ask, you weren't paying attention. But I admit I thought I was being merciful. ~ 'Why?' The face of the woman who speaks is clouded with worry as she embraces a young boy, her son. He's crying quietly onto her shoulder, a tiny trickle of blood rolling from beneath his hand, clamped tightly to his face. Mother's expression changes from worry to anger as she looks up. 'You hateful, spoiled child. I don't know why you can't even play without hurting someone, breaking something, or causing a fight.' She stands and picks up the boy, walking away without a glance backward. Maybe because you never really showed me how to do otherwise. Or maybe I just liked the sound of my brother's screams. Or maybe it was yours. A rock flies toward the two figures, and then another. Mother turns, a brief flash of fear lighting her eyes... and then runs. ~ 'Why?' The skinny kid with the now broken glasses, squints up and tries not to cry. He's getting too old for that. Bad enough that the girl he likes is watching. She probably feels sorry for him. She couldn't possibly like this asshole who just smacked his head into a locker though. Could she? 'Why man? It's not like you need the damn money.' Of course not. I never needed... no, wanted for anything. Nothing you have is worth the trouble of hurting you for. But maybe it's fun to watch you squirm. Or maybe I just want Jess to see what a whining little ingrate you are. I don't care if she likes me, just as long as she doesn't like you. ~ 'Why?' The policeman stands waiting, his arms folded across his chest. All is quiet, no one answers. He doesn't really want answers. He wants to make them feel the danger of their brush with the law, helpless in it's authoritative grip. Just give me my phone call you self righteous prick. You probably drank and smoked whatever you confiscated from us anyway. At least I hope so, considering I put rat poison in it. ~ 'Why?!' She's awfully pretty, even with her face streaked with tears and screaming. She looks hurt, angry, maybe a little afraid. But only the angry part is doing the talking. 'How can you just shrug me off as long as we've been together, after I bailed you out of trouble? It's you baby too, god damn you!' Yes it is. But you won't keep it if I go and I bet the world will be a better place without more of me in it. Or maybe I just really don't give a fuck. Yeah that's more likely. ~ 'Why?' The judge's voice echoes inside the courtroom, but not so loudly as the silence that follows. 'Your honor, my client chooses not to answer on the grounds that doing so might incriminate him.' The judge stares for a moment, his gaze is like a laser focusing intently downward from the bench. 'Sir, it is exactly those people like your client who have given law a bad name. You contort the law into a shape that best protects him at the expense of genuine justice. I for one will not stand for it, and I shall do everything in my power to see that you don't enjoy the freedom the law was designed to protect, for a very long time. I choose not to say anything else on the grounds that doing so might incriminate me. ~ Nothing has really changed over the years. The question remains the same. The people wanting to know remain much the same, easily mystified by simple callousness. What's not to understand? And the answer has always been the same... Why? Because I could. And because you couldn't stop me.
