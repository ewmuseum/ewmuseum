Buried Alive on the path to Death...

27 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
|Buried Alive on the path to Death...|
Human | 09/27/01
He had been defeated. As far as the record books were concerned, he was a loser.

Despite this, Human smiled as he gazed into the clear night sky with a look of hatred in his dark brown eyes.

This was just the beginning. His word would be globally spread, regardless of any obstacles in his path.

When the smoke is cleared he is determined to be the last man standing, with his arm raised in a feeble display of superiority over his fallen victim.

“Victory truly lies in the mind of the loser.” Whispers Human.

Human smiled menacingly as the camera highlighted the gravestone by which he was perched, and focuses on the name engraved along the top – Carl Cash.

“On the second night of Hallowed Nights, the sickness will be released from within – and there is nothing that you meaningless God-believing mortals, least of all Carl Cash, can do about it.”

“I received a gift not so long ago, a very rare, uncommon gift. I discovered the truth of what lies beneath, the meaning of life. The chance to die. A human was not blessed with immortality, and the reason for this is so that they can live a true life deep in the depths of hell once they pass by this poor excuse for an existence down on earth.”

“You see I am really just doing Carl Cash a huge favour. I am sending him to an early grave, so that his soul can live a true existence in hell. A real life.”

“Rest in peace Carl, your life is just about to begin, only this time…it’s for real.”

Sweet dreams…
