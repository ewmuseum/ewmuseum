Lurking Death Part 1

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/4/02 ~The scene reopens in the Connecticut area with the shot of the Davidson Estate. The sun still hangs high in the sky though it is partly obscured by the clouds. We go into the Davidson Estate and head back to the library where the old man was last seen writing in his book. The light is shining through the nearby window and coming down upon the empty chair near the desk. As you look around the room the door slowly begins to swing open and the old man comes hobbling into the room with a wooden cane in his hand to support his weight. He slowly makes his way over to the desk and places the cane up against the right side of the desk as he pulls the chair out a bit. He slowly lowers himself down into the chair and pulls himself back in towards the desk. He pushes the ashtray resting on top off the side and reaches down below the desk perhaps in a drawer and pulls out the book which he had been writing in. He places it on top of the desk and begins flipping through some of the pages. He finally comes to his last entry in the book and silently looks the pages over again. He finishes with a satisfied nod and turns to the next empty page in the book. He reaches to the ink jar sitting near the top of the desk and pulls out the pen as he begins to write.~ ~The Davidson Chronicles Chapter Ten: Lurking Death Part 1 by Tyler Davidson~ Yet another success story can be told in my life with a victory I had achieved over Chris Bagwell in a tag team match. It had happened on July 31st, 2002. I was forced to team with Angelo Deville against my adversary Chris Bagwell and Jason O’Neil. O’Neil and Deville had been at each other’s throats for weeks now so the teaming was obvious for all concerned, regardless of whether we liked it or not. The fact was come the end of the night I had set out on a mission and done what I promised I would do. I pinned Chris Bagwell in the center of the ring one, two, and three! Bagwell had of course debated the situation as did others but I never cared for what they had to say. In my own heart and mind I knew better than anyone that I had done the job I set out to do. I beat the man that everyone said I could never beat and proven myself worthy. Worthy of what was of course up for debate. Many people did not believe me much of a threat in the business and especially inside of FSW. That was something I was determined to change though no matter what. It would be my battles with Bagwell which I would use to propel myself into that position. The similarities our lives had seemingly taken were still quite disturbing to me. It left me many times looking back to the past few months of my life. ”So how long has it been since you heard from him?” ”It’s been about a week,” replied Jess as she looked over at me. She was obviously not as calm as she was trying to pretend to be. Not many people would really be able to blame here. The life of a pro wrestler’s wife or girlfriend never can be easy. As hesitant as I was to admit it the lifestyle had in all honesty driven a bit of a wedge into my relationship with Heather as well. ”That would be right about the time of the attack wouldn’t it?” asked Heather. ”No it was actually a few days before that,” responded Jess. “He had told me he would be out of town for a few days taking care of some new business plan he had put together.” ”And then the attack had happened on Friday,” I stated. “I’m sure wherever he is right now he’s probably working on some plan of his.” ”I’m sure that’s what he’s doing but why doesn’t he let me know something?” asked Jess. “It’s been one week now and you would think he’d get in touch with me to let me know something.” ”Maybe it’s one of those unwritten rules of the wrestling business,” Heather commented. “They seem to have quite a few things that don’t work to well when it comes to the people’s personal lives.” I was certain that the comment was meant more towards me than it was Jess. Heather over the past few weeks has become more and more vocal to me about her dislikes of the wrestling business now. Her time spent with me on the road has for the most part soured her against the business no doubt. It had been quite a few weeks since she had been able to see her own family. My injuries hadn’t helped her outlook on the situation any either but still she insisted on being on the road with me wherever we went. It was for that reason which I knew she wouldn’t be happy with the decision I had made at the time. ”I’m sure whatever the reason is S. had a good one,” I said. “We both know this isn’t the first time he had done something like this. There was the time with Sovereign a few years ago also.” ”Which was also brought on as a result of his being a wrestler,” Heather retorted shooting a sharp glare in my direction. “If he wasn’t then neither S. nor Jess would have been in any danger as a result of that madman.” ”That doesn’t matter,” Jess broke in. “I knew he was alright then but just in hiding. This time I don’t even know anything at all. I don’t even know if he’s alive or not.” ”Don’t get to carried away now Jess,” I responded as I looked out across the road at a building across from us. “I know that no matter what has happened to him he’s fine. He obviously sees some need to keep his whereabouts a secret from everyone right now. There is no telling what he might know or what could be going on in his head.” There was something across the street in one of the windows on the opposite side that had caught my attention. There seemed to be a bit of a glint of light reflecting off of something from in one of the windows. What it was I couldn’t see in the dull of the night but it seemed that there was something there that normally wasn’t. I pushed the thoughts out of my mind for the being and returned my attention to the matter at hand. Jess was definitely getting to the point where she was jumping at shadows and letting her concern override here senses into believing things were worse than they really were. ”I think that you should probably try and get some rest Jess,” I said breaking the momentary silence that had fallen between the three of us. “There isn’t much you can do to change things now and it’s getting late.” ”Sleep doesn’t come very easily right now,” responded Jess. “I’m too worried to sleep very easily. If I only knew something everything would be so much easier for me.” ”Tyler’s right though,” Heather agreed. “You need to try and get some rest. The two of us will be here until the morning sometime. If you need anything you can just tell us.” ”Alright I’ll try,” Jess conceded. “I don’t think it will do much good for me though.” Jess walked off of the balcony heading back into apartment before disappearing back down the hallways leading to her bedroom. That left me and Heather out on the balcony by ourselves for the first time since arriving in New York last night. The conversation wasn’t something that I would be looking forward to as of late. Everything had seemed to begin going wrong in the past few weeks between the two of us. She of course blamed it on the lifestyle but she knew what she was getting herself into years back when we first met. This was the only chance I would have at breaking the news to her. ”I want you staying here in New York,” were the first words out of my mouth. I knew immediately that it wasn’t going to go over well with her but it had to be done anyway. ”What!?” she responded with obvious anger. ”I want you to stay here with Jess,” I replied. “She needs someone here with her now more than I need someone on the road with me.” ”So you expect me to just stay here in New York City while you go off to California for two weeks?” Heather responded. “I don’t think I like that idea very much.” ”Why?” I retorted back to her. “You have been saying the past few weeks how much you hate traveling so much. Take a couple weeks to stay here with Jess. You and Jess have been good friends for years now and she needs someone like that with her now.” ”Fine you want me to stay here then I will,” responded Heather not at all happy about the situation. “You can go run off and play your games over in California while I stay here and deal with the real issues.” ”The situation isn’t even close to being like that and you know it Heather,” I responded letting my anger get the better of me. “There isn’t anything that I can do about it. I have to work just like everyone else, so me staying here isn’t going to do us any good at all either.” ”Whatever you want then Tyler,” replied Heather obviously not even wasting any time arguing the point. “You go and do your job and just leave your friends and family behind some more.” ”Oh give me a break Heather,” I replied. “It’s not at all like I have been ignoring you or anyone else. You go every where I go all the time. Just this one time I’m asking you to stay behind for one of our friends and you throw a little fit about it. I got enough to deal with as it is when a bunch of people I don’t even know are jumping me on a weekly basis. I definitely don’t need you badgering me about some pointless crap like this.” ”Fine go and have fun in California without me then,” responded Heather. “I’ll stay right here and take care of your business for you while you go and do talk shows with Jay Leno and all that other nonsense. Now it’s late so I think I’ll be going to bed as well. I’ll see you in the morning.” ~The scene changes back to the library room where the old man is seated. He jots down the last words 'to be continued' on the piece of paper as he places the pen back into the ink jar and takes another cigarette out of the pack and lights it up. He looks down at the previous pages he had written in and quickly scans over each of the pages before closing the book as the scene fades to black.~
