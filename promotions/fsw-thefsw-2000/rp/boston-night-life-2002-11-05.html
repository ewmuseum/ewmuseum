Boston Night Life

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/05/02 ~The night: The setting for hundreds of Dracula movies, time where robbers do their business, and breeding ground for cheap prostitutes. But tonight, I’ll be speaking of the night in a particular place. Where? Boston Massachusetts baby. Only it’s a different sort of Boston. You see, people usually come to Boston to see the future World Champion Red Sox play at good ol’ Fenway park, but rarely do people see Boston in the NIGHTTIME! BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!!!! AAAAAAAAAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! *wack*~ Me: OUCH! WHAT THE FUCK! Computer: You were laughing maniacally again. Had to hit you. Me: Oh, thanks. Computer: My pleasure, just get back to work. Me: Oye to that. ~Anyway, as I was saying, nobody ever talks about Boston at night. You see, it can be a very scary place. Thus beginning the first of many, or maybe the first and only, story of Boston…AT NIGHT!!!!!~ ~The scene opens up in Boston…AT NIGHT!!!!!! People bustle around, dealing drugs, robbing women, and trying to get the best deal on an unattractive hooker. And since this is an Asa Fountain promo, it’s only reasonable that Asa appear. So we see Asa walking down the street, eating a bucket of fried chicken, sporting white disco pants and a blue t-shirt. His glasses are set on top of his from as he munches down the KFC brand of toxic waste. Asa comes to a crosswalk, and waits for the light to turn to “Walk”. Suddenly, a little gray car slowly peeks around the corner of a building. Asa turns around, and the car quickly reverses back into the ally. Asa shrugs, the light turns to “Walk” and he crosses the street. The little gray car once again peeks out, and slowly moves down the street. Asa, in the meantime, has crossed the street, and is walking down the opposite crosswalk. The car turns through the intersection, and continues to slowly stalk Asa. Asa looks back, and the car stops in the street. Asa stares at the car, raising his right eyebrow. He shrugs again, and turns to walk down the s…POW!! Asa turns around again! The car stops again. Asa moves up to the front of the car. He waves, dances, and peers into the window. Finally, Asa drops his empty bucket of chicken, looks to his right and left, and raises his middle finger to the car.~ SQQQQQQQQUEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAALLLLLLLLLLL ~Asa jumps back in an utter rush of excitement and terror as the tiny car lays a patch in the middle of the street.~ Asa: Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaawwwww shit!!!!!!!!!!!! ~Asa books it down the street, and the little gray car flies down after him. Asa looks back to see it gaining ground. Thinking quickly, he dodges into an ally way. However, the car being small can easily fit into the ally!~ Asa: Fuck no!!! ~Asa runs again as the tiny gray ’73 Gremlin scrapes it’s sides against the brick. Asa runs to the end of the ally, turns the corner, and climbs a fire escape. The car reaches the end, and moves back and forth under Asa, waiting for him to fall.~ Asa: What’s this bitch daddy doin’!?! ~Asa looks around, and there just happens to be a rope swing at the top of the fire exit! He runs, jumps, grabs the rope, and does a Tarzan across the street.~ Asa: Oooooooh shiiiiiiit!!!!!!! ~Asa just happens to swing himself into a garbage bin! He gets up, dusts himself off, jumps out, and dashes off down the street. The little gray car notices, and chases after him. Asa tosses trash cans and anything else he can get a hold of into the street, but the car has amazing maneuverability, and dodges every throw. Thinking quickly once again, Asa runs into a local Starbucks. He runs in, bends over, and pants loudly. Much to his surprise and dismay, the car busts through the front glass wall!! After the smoke settles, the car door opens, and a little old lady hops out with a walker. She slowly moves her way up to Asa, and kicks him in the shin. She turns, and walks back to her car. She gets in, backs out of the coffee shop, and drives off down the street. The customers all stare at Asa in confusion, and Asa stares as the car drives off down the street.~ FADE OUT ~A white spiral spinning off into oblivion fills the page, and I (The narrator, person sitting in front of this computer) walk in front of it with a suit and tie.~ Me: So now you see what utter horrors the night life of Boston can bring. This is one of the hundreds of cases that go on each night in Beantown, USA. Why do these things happen? Why must innocent people, like our friend here, be put through horrors such as this. File it “F” for Funky in, the twilight zone. END PROMO
