Welcome to Mc Donalds, can I take your order please?

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-06-02 “24 big macs, 12 large fries, 3 extra large chocolate shakes, 6 apples pies, and Mc Salad Shaker!?” the young pimple-faced Mc Donald’s worker exlaimes. “Did a bus full of fat people show up?” “Not a bus full, but there is one really huge black guy out there who might get kinda pissed off if we don’t get em up soon”, answers a red headed, freckle-ridden coworker from the other side of the hamburger assembly line. “Oh yeah,” taunts back the first employee, “how big could he really be?” “Well, you know your favorite wrestler in the world,” he waits for a moment til no reply, “Ming! From FSW, the friggen guy you always say is better than any other wrestler in the history of the sport”? “Hell yeah!” the pimple-faced employee responds with much excitement and vigor, “Ming owns all!” “Well, maybe most, definitely some, but nowhere near all,” chuckles back the red head, “not even close.” “Who in god’s name…..” “Azala “muthafuckin” Zameer that’s who!” The red head interrupts speaking in his best ebonics. “Booooooooo-Yeeeeaaaayyyyaaaahhhhhhh!” “He don’t look so big,” the first emplyee says, peering under the heater out towards where Zameer and Mandela are standing. “Yeah, then you go take this order out to him, and don’t forget to mention that you are a huge Ming mark,” retorts the boy covered with freckles as he plops down the last big mac on an over-stacked tray. Shrugging his shoulders the pimply Mc Donalds employee struggles to at first lift the tray, then manages to muscle it out to the counter where he clumsily sets it down, almost losing two of the big macs. He is just about to reveal the amazing price of this order when he looks up and realizes that he’s staring directly into the belly button of a hungry, 500-pound African, who apparently hates Americans. “Ummmm……it’s free I guess today Mr. Zameer.” The boy shutters in fear under his breath. “Nonsense.” Mandela sparks back, “Just take this, I hope it covers it” Taking only a second to looks down in his hand, the pimple-faced high school boy jaunts off in a dead sprint towards the back where is friend and co worker is dropping eaves from behind the microwave yelling, “500 friggen bucks! He gave us 500 bucks baby! Whoo~!” Mandela simply shrugs it off as he turns back to find Zameer already has retreated to a table with the tray filled with 24 big macs, 12 large fries, 3 extra large chocolate shakes, 6 apples pies, and Mc Salad Shaker and is beginning to tear into the first of many of the big macs on the tray. Mandela takes a seat across from Zameer, who has now already finished his first big mac, grabs his Mc Salad off of the tray, shakes it up as he is instructed to do on the side of the product, pops off the lid, looks inside, then up to Zameer, who is still happily stuffing his face full of big macs, with an occasional mouthful of French fries interrupting his lack of breathing. “Slow down Azala,” Manela murmurs through a small bite of salad, “ Your going to choke and die” Zameer looks up to Mandela and with a single grunt silences him, before his attention turns to a man now standing directly behind the Lieutenant. “What did I tell you boys? Best food you’ve ever eaten?” asks Timo Bolamba as he takes a seat next to Lt. Mandela Shuffling himself in Timo reaches across the table for a few fries off of Zameers plate and is met with another grunt from the African Nightmare. Pulling his hand back slowly Timo apprehensively begins to speak, “Easy there big fella, we’re on the same team now.” Timo looks over to Mandela and quizzically ponders, “ Does he know that we are now friends? I mean, does he get that we’re on the same team?” “He does, but you must understand, this is his first Americanized meal, his first hamburger, and definitely his first Ice Cream, “Mandela explains. Timo looks back to Zameer to study his newest stablemate just as Azala tears the lid off the first of his chocolate ice cream shakes and begins to dump it down his gullet. “So he doesn’t know about the Brain Freeze?” Timo asks Just as Timo finishes his sentence Zameer drops his extra large shake to the table and clutches to the front of his head before letting out the most horrifying shriek anyone has ever heard from the big man. Zameer proceeds to stomp on the floor with his enormous right foot as the people around the facility who haven’t already noticed the unusual trio sitting there now have no choice but to recognize. “Guess not.” Mandela says as he begins to chuckle. Timo looks to Zameer, then to Mandela, then Mandela looks to Zameer and back to Timo, as Zameer peers back down to the ground in excruciating pain before recovering briefly and looking up towards the two other men that are trying their best not to laugh at him. Timo finally can’t take it any longer and breaks out in hysteria slapping Mandela on the shoulder sending the Lieutenant into a laughing attack. Zameer stands, slamming his fist on the table, cracking it slightly, bring the two men sitting across from him to deafening silence. Towering over Mandela and Timo, Zameer looks like he’s ready to explode, then, in an odd turn of events, does explode, into laughter, launching his manager and the Samoan Silencer back into their frenzy. “See, I can make joke too,” Zameer forced out in between bites of what has to be his fifteenth big mac. The three men finally settle back down to a normal demeanor as Timo begins to work on the big mac he swiped from Zameer during his brain freeze episode. “This is a helluvalot of food here, must a set ya back a couple of bucks.” Timo questions as he reaches for another couple fries. “I just gave him five of these,” Mandela responds. “Five, five one hundred dollar bills!” Timo exclaims, “Are you serious?!” “Sure”, Mandela replies, “after the commercial we did for Pizza Hut’s all you can eat buffet, they gave me a big check. I’m starting to get used to liking this country.” “Pizza Hut commercial?” Timo wonders aloud. Mandela finishes chewing his last bite of his salad before responding to Timo’s inquiry, “Yes, a representative from the Pizza Hut approached us the other day, so I figured what the hell, might as well make a little money.” “Sounds like you two are catching on pretty quick here in the United States,” Timo admits as Zameer finishes the last bite of his last big mac, then washes it all down with his last ice cream shake. “Guess so,” replies Mandela “Well, looks like we’re almost done here, lets be on our way, we still have to catch the basketball game tonight,” says Timo, now standing from the table. “Basketball?” asks Mandela, “What is basketball?” “You’ll see,” Timo answers, “just bear with me.” Allowing Mandela to stand from the table now as well both men look back to Zameer, who is once again dumping his chocolate shake down his throat at an alarming rate before remembering what happened last time. Unfortunately for Zameer, it is too late, as the pain of the Brain Freeze sends the enormous man down to a knee, once again clutching his head with his gigantic hands as Mandela and Timo share a laugh again at Azala’s expense. “I have a feeling that introducing you two to real life in America is going to be way more fun than I previously thought……..way more fun,” Timo says to himself as he puts an arm around Zameer who is still slouched over on one knee. “Now let’s blow this Popsicle stand boyz~!”
