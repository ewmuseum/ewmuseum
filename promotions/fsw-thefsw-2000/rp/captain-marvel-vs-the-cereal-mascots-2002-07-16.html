Captain Marvel vs. The Cereal Mascots

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday, July 16 Mysterious yet Incredibly Deep and Soothing Narrative Voice: In times of turmoil...one man stands tall and saves the day... [Camera shows mild-mannered wrestler 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous in his den watching cartoons and eating Lucky Charms] Mysterious yet Incredibly Deep and Soothing Narrative Voice: One man defends Truth...Honor...Justice...and the Canadian way... [Camera shows Mike taking another spoon-full of the delicious marshmallow laden cereal and putting it into his mouth. Before he can chew, his eyes suddenly grow wide, his lip begins to quiver, and his eye begins to twitch.] Mysterious yet Incredibly Deep and Soothing Narrative Voice: One man has the Bravado...the Boldness...and the Strength to stare evil in the face and say 'Hey Mr. Evil Pants! I'm gonna kick your butt! So how do you like THEM apples?'. [Camera shows Mike springing out of his chair sending multicolored marshmallows and milk sprawling everywhere, then sprinting across the den and towards the bathroom] Mysterious yet Incredibly Deep and Soothing Narrative Voice: One man bears light when darkness shrouds our fair city... [Camera shows Mike as he takes a flying leap at the base of the bathroom door, pulls his pants down in mid air, and lands on the toilet] Mysterious yet Incredibly Deep and Soothing Narrative Voice: And one man gets explosive diarrhea when he eats the little green leprechaun hats in his Lucky Charms... [Camera shows Mike bracing himself on the toilet bowl. His eyes roll back in his head and...the camera switches to outside the closed bathroom door as many revolting sounds follow as the Canadian Hero flatulates for nearly 10 seconds straight. This sound is followed by long splashing sound. After these dreadful sounds commense, Mike walks out of the bathroom slowly, holding his stomach] Mike Marvelous: Mini-Mike! [Mini-Mike comes trotting down the stairs and stands in front of Mike] Mike Marvelous: Mini-Mike...I am about to give you the most important advice you'll ever hear in your midget life, so listen, ok? [Mini-Mike replies in his little midget speech impediment with his arms behind his back] Mini-Mike: Ok, Michaew. Mike Marvelous: Never...and I mean NEVER...eat the green leprechaun hats in your lucky charms. The green food coloring must act as a natural laxitive or something. I feel like I pooped out my guts. Mini-Mike: Thanks fow the advice, Michaew. Mike Marvelous: You're very welcome my 3-foot tall friend. Now let's go see what else is on, shall we? [Mike and Mini-Mike start to walk towards the living room but stop as they hear a telephone ring. But this is no ordinary telephone, this is a bright red telephone incases in a glass cover with a big 'Do Not TOUCH!' Sign on it. Mike looks at Mini-Mike, Mini-Mike looks at Mike. They both nod and Mike lifts the cover, picks up the phone, and speak] Mike Marvelous: Hello, Annonomous Tipster, what news do you have for me today....yes....yes....ok...good lord....good LORD.......HORRORS OF HORRORS! Thank you, I'll be there in a flash! [Mike slams the phone down dramatically, puts the cover on, then spins around quickly] Mike Marvelous: Mini-Mike...there is a new menace threatening our fair city...I can't give you details right now, but for now...we have to suit up. Mini-Mike: To the Mawvel Cave! Mike Marvelous: Hey! That's my line! Geez, Mini-Mike...how am I supposed to be a super hero if you steal my catch lines!? And besides! You're my sidekick! Side-kick! *Sigh* TO THE MARVEL CAVE! [Mike and Mini-Mike run over to a statue and pull down on it's arm to reveal one of the secret entrances to the Marvel Cave: Two poles leading down two shafts to the cave below. Mike and Mini-Mike jump onto the bars, slide down, and when the camera finds them once again, they are inside the cave. They sprint over to two phone booths that are standing there, unused on the cave floor, and when they come out of the booths, they are no longer 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous and Mini-Mike, NO! They are CAPTAIN MARVEL AND HIS SIDEKICK, MINI-MARVEL! Their costumes consist of an M-shaped mask covering their eyes and nose...disguising their identity...a cape, full body tights and knee length boots. Captain Marvel's costume is Red and White and Mini-Marvel's is Blue and Orange. Mike thrusts his hands on his hips and stands tall] Captain Marvel: Off we go, my faithful sidekick! To uncover the dark shroud that looms over our city! To shield the people from a phantom menace! TO KICK EVIL RIGHT IN THE-- Mini-Marvel: Uhh...Captain Mawvewous...don't you think we shouwd get going? Captain Marvel: Yes, I suppose you're right...but from now on...don't interrupt my bold superhero speeches! A hero has to have SOME fun! AND AWAAAAY WE GO!!! [Captain Marvel stands up straight, puts his arms on his sides, then darts straight up. He flies up...up...up...SMACK! He flies headfirst right into the ceiling of the cave and falls back to the ground with a thud! Mini-Marvel walks over to Captain Marvel, who is rubbing his head] Mini-Marvel: It works bettew when you open up the ceiwing fiwst... Captain Marvel: Right... [Captain Marvel gets up, presses a button on the wall, and the ceiling of the cave opens up] Captain Marvel: AND AWAAAAAY WE GOOOO!!! [Captain Marvel and Mini-Marvel both assume the position, then dart upwards into the air, up and out of the Marvel Cave and into the blue sky above...their destination...wherever evil resides!] ...........Meanwhile.......... [In what looks to be an abandoned factory, there is something going down...something EVIL! The camera zooms in for a closer shot to see many small creatures gathered around in a group in front of what looks to be a tiny man dressed in a green hat with a buckle on it and a shamrock sticking out of it. He also has a green blazer, a green waistcoat and undershirt, green pants, green socks and black shoes with buckles on them. He also has bright red hair on his head, and an evil, maniacal grin on his face. He speaks in a heavy Irish accent, and the other creatures listen] Lucky the Leprechaun: For years...we have been waiting patiently as our faces have been on the front of cereal boxes everywhere. For years, we've been waiting patiently...shooting happy and musical commercials to endorse our cereals...for years, we've been held captive in our own cereal factories, not receiving a dime! It was the CEO's of our cereal companies that got all the profit! They used each and every one of us to get rich! But now they are dead...and it is time for us to get what we deserve!!! [Lucky reaches into a sack and pulls out the severed head of a well-groomed man with glasses] Lucky the Leprechaun: Top o' the mornin' to ya, Mr. Spencer. Looks like you are no longer the 'head' of our company. Mwahahahahaha. Oh well, more shillings for me! You were an alright lad though...it's just that you were always tryin' to steal me fuckin' lucky charms! [Lucky drops the head and kicks it into a vat of steaming acid] Lucky the Leprechaun: Together WE will become the richest mascots in the world! And with the help of the new syrome Alpha the Wizard concocted, once people eat our cereals...they will be hooked for life! Then once they are hooked...they won't just be eating cereal for breakfast...oh no! They'll be eating it for Breakfast, Lunch and Supper! We will be rich! RICH! RICH I TELL YOU! MOO HOO HA HA HA HA HAAAA!!! [All the other cereal mascots begin to cheer. They don't notice that they are no longer alone in the factory...Captain Marvelous and his sidekick, Mini-Marvel slipped in through the window while Lucky was delivering his speech. Captain Marvel and Mini-Marvel spring out from behind a vat of cereal and take the enemy by suprise] Captain Marvel: Sorry to spoil your little celebration, evil cereal fiends, but we heard every word of your sickening plot and we are here to spoil your scheme! Lucky the Leprechaun: Oh...is that right, now? Well, Captain Marvel...it will be hard to stop us when you are DEAD! GET THEM! [The angry cereal mascots fan out and circle around our heroes. Captain Marvel and Mini-Marvel stand back to back with their fists raises, ready for action] Captain Marvel: You guys think you are so tough! Well....I eat you for BREAKFAST! C'mon! [Rabbit, the mascot for Trix, steps forward out of the pack of rabid characters] Rabbit: Silly Rabbit, Trix are for kids! That's all I've ever hear! Well GUESS WHAT! From now on, if I want Trix, I'll have em! Captain Marvel: Silly rabbit, trix are for kids! Rabbit: I HATE THAT! SHUT UP! Captain Marvel: Silly rabbit, trix are for kids! Rabbit: YEEEEEAAAARGHHH!!!! [Rabbit runs at Captain Marvel, but with marvelous agility, he falls to his knees and hits Rabbit with a punch to the gut that sends him flying in the air and out the factory window] *BAM!* [Toucan Sam flies at Mini-Marvel with his sharp sword like beak aimed directly at Mini-Marvel's heart] Toucan Sam: Like I always say...I'll just follow my nose...straight into your heart! He he he! [Mini-Marvel jumps up in the air just in time and Toucan misses, slamming head first into a steel vat] *ZEUS!* [The three elvish mascots of Kelogg's Rice Crispies run at our heroes, but Captain Marvel and Mini-Marvel hold their arms out and clothesline the elves into unconsciousness] *SNAP!* *CRACKLE!* *POP!* Captain Marvel: Stick to building toys and fixing shoes, you elvish fiends! [Count Chocula creeps up behind Mini-Marvel and grabs him. He bends Mini-Marvel's head and prepares to suck the blood out of his body with his long, sharp fangs, but Mini-Marvel delivered a snap mare then jumps up in the air, coming down on Count Chocula's groin with both feet] *MINT!* Count Chocula: Ow...my groin. Bla..bla... [The Chocoa Puffs mascot walks over to Captain Marvel and opens up his coat to reveal 10 pounds of dynamite strapped around his waist. He grins psychotically at Captain Marvel as he holds the remote detonator in his feathered Coo Coo hand] Coo Coo: Since Chocolatey Quick Cereal came out, kids no longer go Coo Coo for Chocoa Puffs! Well, I still do! EEEehehHehhEhehahAHaha! Captain Marvel: Taste my pain, you villainous Coo Coo! [Before Coo Coo can detonate the dastardly device, Captain Marvel delivers a karate chop worthy of Bruce Lee to the wrist of Coo Coo, causing him to drop the detonator. As he goes to pick it up, Captain Marvel delivers an uppercut that sends Coo Coo flying into a vat of hot marshmallow] *UNK!* [The rest of the villains circle around our heroes, who are by now tired from fending them off. They draw nearer and nearer.] Tony the Tiger: Theeey're GRRRRREAT! Kelogg's Rooster: Cock a doodle doo! Fred Flintstone: Yaaabba Daaabba Doooo! Captain Crunch: Aaaarrrr, Maties. Why don't you just surrender! Captain Marvel: Surrender? OH-HAH-HAH-HAH! That word is not in my vocabulary, Cap'n! [Captain Marvel reaches into his utility belt and pulls out...a can of MARVELicious Soda and a bag of MARVELick n' Crackle Rocks! He opens the package, puts it over the top of the can, shakes it up, and throws it at the cereal mascots] Captain Marvel: Taste delicious defeat, fiends! [Captain Marvel and Mini-Marvel dive out of the way as the can hits the ground, causing a pink foam explosion that sends the bad guys flying in all directions. When the foam has cleared, all the mascot appear to be knocked out. Our heroes stand up and admire their handy work] Captain Marvel: We've done it my short sidekick friend. We've saved the day once again. But I can't help but feel a bit sorry for them...since they've been used all these years just to make money for some fat cat CEO. Oh well...this just goes to show you that crime doesn't pay! Voice: On the contrary, laddy...sometimes crime pays off BIG! [Captain Marvel and Mini-Marvel turn around just in time to be knocked out cold by a club wielding Lucky the Leprechaun] ....2 hours later... [Our heroes awaken to find themselves bound at the hands and feet. They try to wriggle their way free but it is no use. They hear a voice again and this time they see Lucky...but he is upside down...no, he appears to be upside down because Captain Marvel and Mini-Marvel are hanging upside down suspended above a giant vat of hot marshmallow...and the chain that they are hanging from is being lowered inch by inch...] Lucky the Leprechaun: As you know, me lucky charms have 8 different marshmallows! Hearts, moons, and horshoes, clovers and balloons. Pots of gold and rainbows, and the red balloon! But now there is going to be 2 more brand new marshmallows! CAPTAIN MARVEL AND MINI-MIKE MARSHMALLOWS! Mwaaahahahahahaaa!!! Captain Marvel: Jumpin' pie yiminy! We are getting closer and closer to that vat of steaming hot marshmallow! Lucky the Leprechaun: Hahahaha...that's right. Nothing can save you! Now if you'll excuse me, I have some addictive cereal to make! Captain Marvel: You'll never get away with this, fiend! Lucky the Leprechaun: Oh, but Captain Dimwit...I already have! Hahahahaha! [Lucky walks over to the other side of the factory and pulls a switch. A loud humming is heard followed by the movement of machinery. Vats of Lucky Charms begin moving on a converer belt. The vats stop underneath a big funnel that poors some sort of liquid over the cereal. Then the vats move down the converer belt where the contents are put into red lucky charms boxes] Lucky the Leprechaun: MWAAAHAHAHAHA! I WILL BE THE RICHEST MASCOT IN THE WORLD!!!! [Meanwhile, Captain Marvel wriggles on of his hands free of the chain, and reaches into his utility belt. He pull out another can of MARVELicious Soda] Captain Marvel: [whispers]If I can just...open this can...we'll be free! [The chain lowers even more. Now our heroes are a mere 4 feet from the steaming marshmallow fluff of doom. With one last burst of strength, Captain Marvel manages to open the can. He poors the delicious soda over the chains and after a few seconds, the chains corrode away, freeing our heroes. They jump away from the vat just as the remaining chain dips into the marshmallow. They creep up on Lucky the Leprechaun, who still has his back turned, admiring his evil plot in action] Lucky the Leprechaun: By morning, all of the world will be eating the new and improved version of me lucky charms...and then they'll be hooked! Mwahaha-- Captain Marvel: On the contrary, fiend...by morning, you'll be in the slammer! Lucky the Leprechaun: CURSES! How did you-- [With the strength of 50 Canadians, Captain Marvel winds up and delivers a devastating punch to Lucky, sending him into the air and into his own vat of hot marshmallows. He screams as he sinks into the hot fluffy vat] Lucky the Leprechaun: CURSE YOU, CAPTAIN MARVEL! CURSE YO--Glub...mmph...RRRGH! [Lucky disappears under the marshmallow, never to be seen again] Captain Marvel: How ironic...in the end, Lucky became the newest marshmallow for Lucky Charms. Mini-Marvel: Cwime doesn't pay! [Sirens are heard outside and police kick in the door of the factory. They look around to see only the cereal mascots lying unconscious on the ground and our heroes standing with their arms folded in the middle of the factory] Police Chief: Looks like we're too late boys. [The chief walks over to Captain Marvel] Police Chief: I think we owe you a debt of gratitude, Captain Marvel. Captain Marvel: No, that's ok, Chief. Knowing that our city is safe from these villains is reward enough for me. Police Chief: Alright boys, let's clean up here! [The chief walks away, but then he turns back around to say something to our heroes, but they are gone] Police Chief: Nevermind. THE END! Mysterious yet Incredibly Deep and Soothing Narrative Voice: Our city is once again safe from evil....but for how long!? Tune in next week for a new MARVELOUS adventure! Same MARVEL time! Same MARVEL channel! [A big red M spins around on the screen, then it fades to black]
