Squirrel Wars!!

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/14/02 ~Ah, Boston Massachusettes, home of the Sox, Bean town. Whatever you want to call it, it’s a great place to live or visit. On this particular day, we see Asa “Fro” Fountain and his girlfriend, Melanie Royston, sitting on a bench in Boston center. Asa is wearing a yellow shirt and orange pants, while Melanie flaunts a skimpy green tube top and skirt. Both of their fros move in the wind as they sit and enjoy the scenery. However, enjoyment isn’t the word for Asa.~ Asa: Grrsonoffuckinbitchassi’llkillhimmotherfuckerbitch…. Melanie: Aww, come on now baby, you can’t stay mad forever! Asa: Sure I can foxy mama! That bitch daddy Ming and that fool Lo-Key have BOTH interfered in both my frigen’ matches! I’ll tell ya what, they just don’t want me to win, because they know I WOULD’VE won both those matches. Melanie: Oh calm down! You and that drunk Ramey have a match to focus on this Wednesday! Asa: But I don’t even know who I’m fightin’ suga mama! ~Suddenly, an acorn hits Asa on the shoulder.~ Asa: What th… ~Four more acorns hit Asa.~ Asa: What the hell! ~Asa gets up and looks into the tree that covers the two. On a branch sits a squirrel, throwing nuts at Asa.~ Asa: Whaddya want squirrel turkey! Squirrel: Chippitychipchipchip!!!! ~The squirrel appears to be hostile.~ Asa: What? You got a problem? Squirrel: Chippitychip! Asa: Then bring it!!! ~The Squirrel runs down the tree and sits in front of Asa on the grass. Melanie puts her head in her hands as Asa and the squirrel stare each other down. Asa eyes the squirrel. Suddenly the squirrel lifts up his paw, and manages to lift his tiny little middle finger at Asa. Asa leans back, confused.~ Asa: Keep talkin’ smack bitch daddy, or you’ll find that tail of yours shoved so far up your ass you’ll be tastin’ it! ~With that, the squirrel leaps, and latches onto Asa’s face. Asa runs around, letting out muffled cries as the squirrel continues to latch on. He manages to throw it off. Asa stands up, breathing deeply.~ Asa: Alright *pant* You want some? *Pant* Then bring it! I’m gonna let out my frustrations and beat the hell out of you, just like I’ma kick the shit outta the bitch daddies I’m takin’ on this Wednesday! ~Asa waves his arms around karate style, letting out little karate war cries. The squirrel leaps into the air, but is met by a karate fist from Asa.~ Asa: Hi-yaaaah! ~The squirrel hits the ground, stands up, and latches onto Asa’s leg, sinking his little squirrel teeth into it.~ Asa: Hey! No biting squirrel daddy! ~Asa shakes the squirrel off. The squirrel leaps once more, and Asa around house kicks it. The squirrel flies through the air, letting out a tiny squirrel cry, and hits the tree. It falls to the ground, and remains motionless.~ Asa: There, that takes care of that. Come on foxy lady, let’s roll. Melanie: I just don’t know about you Asa. ~Asa and Melanie leave the park. The camera comes back to the fallen squirrel. His little paw lifts up a little…~ TO BE CONTINUED…someday…
