Who I am.

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sometime between yesterday and tomorrow. 'Who am I? Well that's a tough one, but I'll give it a shot.' Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens is a man quite unlike any other. Born to a rich family, he's always been set above the place of normal men. The days of his earliest youth were spent tormenting the servants. He realized early on they were required to do whatever, he asked, whatever he demanded of them. He had the power to control their every action in his presence. It was a power he grew to enjoy, grew to love, grew to need. 'I'm a man of power, and style. Whatever I desire, I acquire, its just that simple.' Soon the young lad was off to school, private school of course, only the best for young Michael, he was a Stevens after all. King of the playground, lord and master of the peons around him. He used, and tortured the other students. Not the biggest or the strongest, he was still the best. He instinctively knew things, knew how to hurt people, knew how to use his small body to maximum effectiveness for causing punishment. And yet, no one knew. To the teachers, parents, he was nothing but a harmless little boy with an angelic smile. No one could believe he would ever cause such harm on the other children, they were simply liars, jealous over the attention young Michael received. They were all his puppets, peers, so called betters, he held them all the palm of his small hand. 'I do things my own way and always have. I stop at nothing to get what I deserve, and what I deserve my dear friends is everything, everything in this entire fucking world. I rule you, I am your better. The fans, everyone else around me, you all mean nothing. You're small weak and pathetic, you should be bowing at my feet, for I am the man of the future.' And then it was on to high school, a place where your hero and mine flourished. His mind grew and changed, and in a moment of fate he discovered his purpose. It was in biology class, talk of Charles Darwin, and the theory of evolution. Here was the key, here was his destiny. He'd always known he was better, and now he knew why, he was quite simply more evolved. He was a higher, better breed of man. He was the fittest in the battle for survival. 'In life, its nothing but survival of the fittest, dog eat dog and all that shit. The point is, I'm the fittest, I'm the greatest of all time. I'm more than man, I'm superman.' It was also in High School, that our Evolutionary role model, found wrestling, oh while its true as a child he'd watched it on Television, in High School it became a full time part of his life, when he wrestled on the schools team. Four years, four All-State teams, and two All American teams later, Michael Stevens was known to be one of the best amateur wrestlers in the country. He was sought after and recruited by almost every college in the country, some for his brain, others for his brawn, and still others for both. He was the hottest commodity, in the world, at least in the eyes of academia. And yet none of this was for our young lord and savior, no he wanted more. 'My time is too important to waste. I have one goal only and that's to satisfy my every urge and craving, and I refuse to wait. Whatever will be mine, will be mine now, not tomorrow, but today, always today. Why wait, when I can just take?' Yes, it was the path less traveled that called out to Stevens, he'd forgo college, and start wrestling for real. He'd join the ranks of elite for truly the wrestlers were the elite of earth. He'd get the attention he craved, the power he needed, and all he had to do, was be himself. For its true that he is the best, ever, the best of all time, he is Perfection Personified. It wasn't long before people in the wrestling world noticed him. He was too good, too exceptional to wrestle in dingy gyms for too long, after just a short year wrestling on the independent circuit, he was hired onto a regaional fed in hopes of using him as their big star to go national. However it was not to be, Stevens demanded too much, he knew his worth and refused to accept any less, and the promotion crumbled under his demands for higher and higher payoffs. 'I won't accept second best, because I'm not second best. I'm the man of tomorrow, and I've come today. I know how much I'm worth to you people, I know how much you all love me. And its for that reason that I demand I'm given my due. If you can't afford me, you don't get me, period, end of story. The money's not important, I have plenty already, but its the principle of the thing, and I'll never compromise my principles.' It took no time at all for Stevens to find a new home, Global Championship wrestling. It was there he met two men that would go on to shape his destiny, and bring him into his future. Those men, Steven Caldera, and Jason O'Neil. Caldera the consummate business man, a personal in all forms of the world. He saw in Stevens his crowned prince, and its safe to save it would not be long before this prince became a king. O'Neil was a kindred spirit of sorts, and man that was willing to step out of the present and prepare for the coming of that next step in human evolution. A skilled athlete and a brilliant man, he had but one fatal flaw, when faced witht he choice, bot evolve to be stay with the masses he chose the masses. He wanted to be a hero, and that earned himself a place on Stevens's hit list. 'Evolution, is a necessary evil. We must from time to time thin out the gene pool, remove the offensive elements to create the perfect being. That's my job, that's my goal. I eliminate the undesirable elements of humanity. You might not like me for it, hell you might even hate me for it, but I don't care, I'm doing you a favor after all. You can thank me later.' Caldera soon grew weary of running Global Championship Wrestling, and the company, without its mastermind, folded, and things looked almost grim for the man that is our future. He gave it all up, retired, said goodbye to the sport he had promised to rule. However, a man like Stevens could not stay away for long, and he with a little help from Sheena Ramone, his girlfriend, and fellow evolutionary elite, soon realized that. The question then became, where to go, and what to do? The answer became simple, FSW, the new home of Jason O'Neil. Yes that would be the answer, Stevens would go forth on a mission to destroy O'Neil, for he was an example of all the bad in the world. O'Neil who refused to acknowledge his place among the higher beings of the world. He'd be given a chance, one last opportunity to take his rightful place among the ruling class, and if he refused, he would be destroyed. Then came the final part of this equation, Caldera must have had the same notion, recruit O'Neil into the glory of the future, or simply destroy him. Caldera now had a young protégé in the monstrous Kimbusa. A man so different from humanity he must be given a class all to himself. Like Stevens, like Caldera, Kimbusa was a man of the future, and he was ready to become the elite. 'Why did I help destroy Jason? That's simple the bastard deserved it. He could have been so much more than a what he was. Nothing but a miserable little puppy scraping and clawing at the feet of those wretches beneath. What a misguided fool to believe that he needed the love and support of your miserable little peons. He made his choice to lie in the bed of the unwashed masses, I simply helped deliver the consequences of his choice.' And now the three men, Caldera, Kimbusa, and Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens, have come together. They are the best humanity has to offer. Each in their own way is superior to the normal everyday man, they are the next step in human evolution. It will be these three men who lead the charge into the future. And most importantly, it will be Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens, who show the entire world, once and for all, and he is absolute perfection. 'So you still want to know who I am? You're all going to find out soon enough. I'm the Evolution of Pro-Wrestling, I'm Perfection Personified. So know that you all have seen just a bit of who I am, now that you've all gotten a little taste of who exactly Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens is, I've gotta ask, are you ready to evolve? Goddamnit, ain't I great?'
