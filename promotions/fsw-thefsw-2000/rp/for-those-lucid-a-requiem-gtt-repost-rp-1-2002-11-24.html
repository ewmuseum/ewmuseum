For Those Lucid: A Requiem (GTT repost; RP #1)

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(NOTE: Like Kimbusa and Angelo, I'm going to archive a large portion of my work in the recent GTT. So the next several by me aren't original, and are meant for character reference and a sample of some of my better recorded work) ------------------------------ Sanity is, predominantly, a relative term. Who is to say what is sane and healthy, and what is demented and irrational? It’s an eternal debate, not dissimilar to the dispute of creation versus evolution or distinguishing the exact moment of life’s true beginning in the womb. Granted in the human race there exists an ascribed set of accepted morals and values; purity and generosity are beneficial qualities, while perversion and murder are deplored by the civilized. So, in that rationale, there exists a fine line. In many cases it’s merely a matter of interpretation, akin to standards of beauty and ugliness, truth and propaganda. But among all, there is a distinct separation between the deranged and the moral. Most never question where this division lies; alas, only the enlightened (and in some cases, the cursed) ever have the pleasure of severing from their own ignorance and plunging into the depths of what can be called sane and what can be called an aberration. The spiritual and devout Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan falls into that esteemed category. Unfortunately, he falls into the side labeled as the ‘cursed’. His ‘enlightenment’ is considered a bane, not a blessing. He has not convened with any grandiose beings whom have bestowed knowledge upon him for his staunch service; instead he has glimpsed first-hand into the pits of most ravenous blasphemy and madness. Having plumbed the fathomless gulfs of the dark and the profane, in recent months Shanahan has become a changed mind. And the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, considered the largest of its kind to ever be organized, is a rather bittersweet morsel of déjà vu. His current path was initiated just months ago under almost identical circumstances; the Primetime Central Global Title Tournament, the prize being a sought-after opportunity to dethrone PTC Global Heavyweight Champion, Vampir Nosferatu. The morbid set of visions haunting the jaded Boston street thug on a nightly basis were relentless and uncompromising, and by their morose and hellishly perverse nature he assumed it to be the work of the supernatural one himself, Nosferatu. Over passage of time and thorough investigation, including a long deep search within himself and his own spiritual convictions, it was discovered the roots of this evil were vastly more sinister than his previous notions. In all fact, his mind was being probed by a set of cosmic fiends who sat imprisoned across our world and dimension, rebellious immortals whom had been incarcerated by Elder Gods beyond the stars for their hideous inhibitions and defiant actions. They had used the venerable Shanahan as a sort of living plaything for their own macabre amusement to pass away the aeons of inactivity and arcane confinement. His probing of their origin only caused the nightmares to wax in terms of their gruesome imagery, and for the otherworldly demons to begin a reality-based assault by use of their then-chiefly slumbering minions. This led to his now-current alliance with the underground society known as the Blackhelm Coalition, a secretive world-wide organization of scholars and scientists who knew of the existence of the Great Old Ones and fought to drive back their Earthly minions from breeding chaos and anarchy, and his association with Captain Marcus Stryker’s Delta Fox special forces unit. He had thought the targeting to be random, and at first such was the lie fed by his vile detractors. Eventually one of his most notorious tormentors, the loathsome Crawling Chaos and Messenger of the Outer Gods, Nyarlathotep, revealed the truth: Bruce was a descendent of a powerful mortal foe of the Great Old Ones, a still-unknown to Shanahan individual named Crowley, and the price of Crowley’s unpunished sins were now to be wrought on Bruce and his missing younger brother, Derek Shanahan, who had been brought to light for a short time by ex-PCW wrestler and now FSW-star, Big Daveylicious. With the reemergence of yet another prestigious tournament, and as a heavy favorite of the gambling community this time around, Shanahan strides into battle warding off the demons of the past and the present; memories of defeat in the past at the combined hands of White Mexican and own inner doubt now haunt his thoughts and dredge up an unwanted feeling of déjà vu. With this pressure and that of his PCW obligations, the bitter walking time-bomb hurls himself into the melee facing more adversity than any time in his storied career. With Bruce Blade in his crosshairs, the PCW hardcore specialist braces himself for the inevitable war that follows. This is his story, his ode to the declining sanity in his strained psyche…… * * * The cavern walls stood fast on all sides, forged from the raging waters of ancient underground currents which had long since dissipated and ran now deeper beneath the Earth. A steady dripping resonated throughout the massive subterranean chamber as the constantly forming condensation dropped to the floor in an almost steady stream, the air cool yet humid, and saturated from the rise of moisture. Shanahan breathed in the thick yet cool air, rising gradually to his feet as he cast his eyes on all sides. His eyes were shrouded by mirrored sunglasses, a trademark of his PCW personality, veiling his beady brown eyes. He was alone, unaided and unescorted in this vile cavern system which possessed a ghastly aura that would make any typical psychic run fleeing into the night, leaving only terrified screams in their wake. Shanahan felt it, it was unavoidable; he himself held a small degree of psychic power, but it seemingly been granted by the Old Ones as a way of enhancing the game. He could feel that aura, felt the vibrations emitted within the evil atmosphere by this stronghold of the wicked. He didn’t know what lured him further down the snaking corridors of the labyrinth, the musty aroma of decay filling his nostrils and the deathly silence giving the atmosphere a Poe-like sense of impending doom. Nonetheless he stalked through the broad chamber and into a series of interconnected tunnels, where repugnant appendages of God-knows-what protruded from the walls. As he progressed further, a fetid stench consumed the previously tolerable odor of mold and decay and he nearly stumbled into the wall, the mere power of the stench feeling like he had run headlong into a physical barrier. He lifted his shirt to envelop his nose and continued further, passing through more of the winding burrows, brushing against the grisly appendages of putrefying alien flesh. Finally the disgusted Boston native staggered into another massive chamber, but was aghast to find the putrid smell appeared to be emanating from this room. The walls were etched in a plethora of irreverent symbols and glyphs, spelling out arcane what he presumed to be occult prophecies he lacked the fluency of language to interpret, and these marks were visible by a meager, esoteric light source of which Bruce couldn’t identify. He peered forward, slowly drawing off his shades, and lurched with stupefied horror at the sight before him. Hoary and pallid, drawn in a deformed ball, was the sickly outline of a decrepit fetus. It rested immobile between two looming pillars crowned with sickly red flames, the tongues of red and orange wavering like dandelions in a spring breeze. An immense obelisk towered in the background, partially obstructed by shadow unbroken by the thin flames atop the parallel columns. Bruce stared with wide eyes, horrified at the monstrosity that seemed to stir within the rancid mucus that encased it. A shadow perched atop the now-visible cap of the obelisk, its red eyes latched like spectral jaws onto Bruce. It extended its own gaping maw into what resembled a dark parody of a grin, and echoed a grim low-toned sound of which he could only imagine was a chuckle. “The Cleansing is at hand, mortal. Our agent grows restless in his slumber….but soon he shall go free, free to ravage your war-torn planet and breed mayhem and sweet, sweet chaos. Hear me well, Bruce Everett Shanahan. There is no going back now, the prophecies have begun to pass. Behold your future.” Shanahan watched with terror as the deformed blasphemy, presumably a monstrous Byakhee, began to chant forth dark alien syllables from its barbed tongue. Then, gradually altering its maddening speech into English, that horrid revelation came forth: “When the congress of five decree the time is right, He shall go forth with renewed hunger into the night. Millenia have come and millennia have gone, Now He will rise from eternity as the guise of Abaddon. Humanity will fall by toll of the clock, And onto this Earth again the Old Ones will walk.” The cryptic prophecy produced nary a sound from the distressed Shanahan, who stumbled backward another step as the curved, membrane-lined wing of the monstrous Byakhee-Lord fluttered from its perch atop the shadowy pillar. Below, the warped fetus stirred and shifted in a disturbed torpor, and now Bruce could hear distorted sounds emanating from its thin, mucus-layered shell. The red eyes of the Byakhee-Lord blinked purposefully, then turned its attention back to guarding the abhorrent thing as if placed as some abyssal babysitter. Bruce collapsed to his knees as an unearthly glow began to radiate from the unborn atrocity, a chilling blood-red which illuminated the walls which, Bruce now observed, appeared to be a nest for the foul winged devils from beyond the stars. They stared at him in unison as the light spread around the shadowy chamber, those hellish red coals burning holes deep into his tortured soul…. * * * Upon awakening, bathed in a cold sweat that saturated his bedclothes and bed, Shanahan’s first action was immediate: a phone call to Father Randall Craven. Friends since childhood and separated nearly 15 years until the dreams had caused Bruce to seek spiritual guidance, Craven had become the means of solace for the besieged street fighter. Their paths had deviated in wholly different directions in youth, but they had converged again by the nightmares beset on Shanahan by the various Old Ones. It was only two rings before a tired voice with an accompanying yawn answered. “Yeah…hello…?” “Randall, it’s Bruce. It happened again….” “I am sorry, Bruce,” muttered the priest with a blend of regret and exhaustion. “But it’s been happening for months. Why the call at 4 am?” “Abaddon, I need to know who Abaddon is.” He could detect a feeble groan from the other end, followed by the squeal of bed springs as the Catholic priest drew himself from a prone position. A hard sigh followed. “Very well then. Abaddon, Bruce, is an enigmatic Biblical figure eluded to in Revelations. He’s an enigma in that both Heaven and Hell claim him as an ally and as an enemy at various periods. Supposedly he was an angel of judgment who would destroy at the command of God, and it is said to be he who will lead the colossal horde from the Great Abyss which will devastate and plunder the Earth when Judgment arrives. He resembles both the prophet who will stir up the south and bring many to the servitude of our Lord, and also has many of the terrible qualities of the Anti-Christ. Thus it’s really hard to say. Almost two-faced in a sense I guess. Why do you ask?” Shanahan’s brow wrinkled as ideas tumbled over in his end, possibilities and identities of whoever that fetus would become upon its “birth”. Numerous prospects danced through his turmoil-ridden mind before a final realization caused him to drop the phone, leaving the ashen-white receiver to dangle listlessly from the stand. “Marcus….dammit….” The door slammed shut with a deafening bang in his infuriated wake. “Bruce? Bruce?….”
