Chris Sloboda, at home, family time...... Part One

16 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/02 “DADDYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!” The screams of his two beautiful daughters pierce the air as Chris Sloboda walks into his living room. “YOU’RE HOME!!” Indeed, after being on the road for over 3 months, Chris has finally caughten a break in his schedule of whooping the asses of unlucky wrestlers. Putting his bags down, he accepts the running hugs from his two daughters. Kenzie, the oldest at 6 years old, gives her dad a kiss on his right cheek, as Carleigh, the youngest at 3 years of age, plants one on his left cheek. Even though the girls saw daddy a week ago, to them, it seems like an eternity. Finally the release their grip of daddy, as ROCKET POWER starts on Nickelodean. The girls run away, and plop on the couch, infront of the television. “It is good to be home” quips Boda to himself as he walks towards the kitchen, where the smell of home cooked ribs permiate the air. “Smells good Stacey.” “Thanks hun, and welcome home.” Stacey, the wife we finally met last week, says to her famous husband. “So, how’s the girlfriend?” “Funny.” Boda snarls. “We discussed this last time. What I do on tv is a gimmick. It’s part of who my character is. I love you and the girls, so why would I cheat on you?” The never ending nagging. Sure, we all know Boda is realy a shauvanistic asshole, who treats women like shit, and sleeps with anything with 2 legs and a hole, but, why not. I mean sure, he’s married..... but he’s a WRESTLER. He can, and has successfully, covered up his infidelities as part of his ‘gimmick’. But, how much longer can he convince Stacey that it is indeed a ‘gimmick’, and keep her from snooping around. After all, Chris has not done anything on T.V, other than flirting, to garner attention from it being a ‘play’ and her thinking it is real life. He’s never been caught in the act of fornication on television. Turning to leave the kitchen, Stacey walks up behind her husband, and grabs him in a hug. “I know. I just get concerned at times.” Stacey tears up. “I love you, and the girls, and it’s hard with you gone all the time.” Chris turns, and hugs his wife also. The two share a tender moment until........... “I’m thirsty. I want some chocolate milk.” Looking down, beyond Stacey’s shoulders, stands Carleigh. Chris smiles, lets Stacey go, and tell her......... “I’m going to go shower, and get ready for dinner. You get Carleigh a drink. Tomorrow, I have to go do ‘BODA’ for Real Radio and The Monsters of The Midday.” And Boda walks off, out of the kitchen as the scene fades to black.
