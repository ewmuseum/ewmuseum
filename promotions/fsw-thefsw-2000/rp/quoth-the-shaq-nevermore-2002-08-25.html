Quoth The Shaq...Nevermore!

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-25-02 The Raven, as told by Timo Bolamba Once upon a midnight dreary, while Boda pondered, weak and weary Over many a large and curious volume of forgotten porn -- While he jerked it, silently tapping , suddenly there came a rapping, As of someone gently rapping, rapping outside his chamber door. ' 'Tis some visitor, ' he muttered, 'tapping outside chamber door-- Only this and nothing more.' Ah, distinctly he remember it was in the bleak November; And each separate wrestling member wrought its shadow upon the locker room floor. Eagerly he wished the morrow -- vainly he had sought to borrow From his books surcease of sorrow -- sorrow to settle the score-- For the match he had lost to him was the score-- Nameless the man was for evermore. And the bloody, sad, uncertain beatings of a single chance meeting Thrilled him -- filled him with fantastic terrors never felt before: So that now, to still the beating of his heart, he stood repeating. ' 'T is some visitor entreating entrance at my chamber door-- Some late visitor entreating entrance at my chamber door-- That it is and nothing more.' Presently his soul grew stronger: hesitating then no longer, 'Sir, ' said he, 'or Madam, truly your forgiveness I implore: But the fact is I was napping, and then I heard you come rapping, And so badly you came rapping, rapping outside chamber door, That I scarce was sure I heard you'-- here he opened wide the door—A tall black man to settle the score. Long unto the man he was peering, long he stood there wondering fearing. Doubting, dreaming dreams no mortal ever dared to dream before: But the silence was unbroken, but the jersey gave one token, And the only words there spoken were the whispered words, 'The Score' This he whispered, and an echo murmured back the word 'The Score'-- Merely this and nothing more. Back into the chamber turning, all his soul within him burning, Soon again he heard a rapping somewhat worse than before. 'Surely,' said he, 'surely tis not him at my window lattice; Let me see, then, what thereat is, and this mystery explore-- Let his heart be still a moment and this mystery explore-- 'Tis the wind an nothing more!' Open here he adjusted his collar, when, with many a hoot and holler, In there looked a stately Shaq of the saintly days of yore; Not the least obeisance made he; not a minute stopped or stayed he; But, with mien of lord or lady, strode upon my wooden floor-- Dunking upon a bust of Jordan just above his chamber door—Dunking, then smiled, and nothing more. Then this Laker man beguiling Boda’s sad fancy into smiling, By the yellow and purple decorum of the countenance he wore, 'Though thy rapping be old and whack, thou,' he said, 'art sure no hack, Ghastly grim and greedy Shaq wandering from the Pacific shore-- Tell me what thy business here is on this nights Orlando shore!' Quoth the Shaq, 'Nevermore.' Much he marveled this largish man to hear discourse so plainly, Though his answer seemingly little meaning -- little relevancy bore; For we cannot help agreeing that no living human being Ever yet was blessed with seeing Shaq upon his wooden floor—Or that he dunk upon the bust above Boda’s door, Saying such word as 'Nevermore.' But the Shaq standing lonely on the wooden planks, spoke only That one word, as if his soul in that one word he did outpour. Nothing further then he uttered, not a “What up Doc” did he mutter-- Till Boda’s heart scarcely fluttered, 'Other friends have flown before -- On the morrow he will leave me, as my Hopes have flown before.' Then the Laker said, 'Nevermore.' Startled at the stillness broken by reply so aptly spoken, 'Doubtless,' said Boda, 'what it utters is it only stock and store Coached by some unhappy master whom unmerciful Disaster Followed fast and followed faster till his songs one burden bore -- Till the dirges of his Hope the melancholy burden bore Of 'Never - nevermore.” But the Shaq still beguiling all Boda’s fancy into smiling, Straight he wheeled a cushioned seat in front of man upon wooden floor, Then upon the velvet sinking, he betook himself to linking Fancy unto fancy, thinking what this ominous man of yore-- What this grim, ungainly, ghastly, gaunt, and ominous man of yore Meant in speaking, 'Nevermore.' This Boda sat engaged in guessing, but no syllable expressing To the man, whose fiery eyes now burned into Boda’s bosom's core; This and more he sat divining, with his head at ease reclining On the cushion's velvet lining that the lamp-light gloated o'er But whose velvet-violet lining with lamp-light gloating o'er The Score shall press, ah, nevermore! Then he thought, the air grew denser, perfumed from an unseen censer Swung by seraphim whose foot-falls tinkled on the tufted floor. 'Wretch,' he cried, 'thy God has lent thee -- by these angels he hath sent thee Respite -- respite the nepenthe from thy memories of The Score! Quaff, oh, quaff this kind nepenthe and forget this lost Score!' Quoth the Shaq, 'Nevermore.' 'Laker!' said Boda, 'man of action! -- profit still, if man of Phil Jackson! Whether Tempter sent, or whatever tempest tossed thee ashore, Desolate yet all undaunted, on this desert land enchanted -- On this home by Horror haunted -- tell me truly, I implore -- Is there -- is there a chance I won? -- tell me -- tell me, I implore!' Quoth the Shaq, 'Nevermore.' 'Laker!' said Boda, 'man of Action! -- profit still, if man of Phil Jackson! By that Booker that bends above us -- by that Phantom we both adore-- Tell his soul with sorrow laden if, within the distance hazen, It shall clasp a sainted match we had whom the Smarks named “The Score” -- Clasp a rare and radiant match named ‘The Score”.' Quoth the Shaq, 'Nevermore.' 'Be that your word, lets sign this match, man or fiend!' Boda shouted, the victory surely he could snatch -- 'Get thee back into the tempest and the Night's Pacific shore! Leave no Reebok shoe as a token of that lie thy soul hath spoken! Leave my porn jerking unbroken! – quit dunking on the bust above my door! Take thy crappy rapping out from this world, and take thy form from off my floor! Quoth the Shaq, 'Nevermore.' And the Shaq, the match he would rob, still makes Boda do the Job , and is still rapping, dunking upon “His Airness” bust of Jordan just above his chamber door; And this match had they once had, with Shaq robbing, to forever make Boda Jobbing, And the Smarks world over still tell, the legend, growing larger, larger than before, And Boda’s record from out that shadow that lies floating on the floor Shall be lifted -- nevermore!
