I've got no strings...

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/8/02 “You’re nothing but a Puppet! My Puppet!” “My Puppet!” “Puppet…Puppet…” “Puppet…” Jason O’Neil sits on a chair, blood still around his mouth, his head, chest and arms. His head, is slumped into his chest. His eyes, stand open, looking off into the darkness in front of him. Suddenly, a spotlight shines on him. His head perks up and he smiles a ridiculous smile. His jaw seems to be dislocated, with two lines of blood coming from the corners of his mouth down to his chin. Freckles, dotted in blood litter his face. Slowly, the light moves upward, toward the assumed ceiling. He begins to stand, and as he does, strings pull him up. His hands go out to the sides, as he strikes a puppet like pose. Quickly he turns his head to the left, then right, and smiles. “I’ve got not strings…to hold me down, to make me fret, to make me frown!” Jason sings. A red curtain appears behind him. Lights shine up from the floor as the darkness gives way to bright light. O’Neil starts to dance as he sings the famous song from Pinoccio. His eyes, never moving. His hands, controlled by the strings the hold him up. He moves awkwardly across the stage as he moves. “I'm not tied up to anyone. They've got strings. But you can see. There are no strings on me.” Suddenly, the strings snap and O’Neil falls in a heap on the floor. His eyes, show uncharacteristic anger. He looks around, immobilized by this newfound pain. “You’re right. Angelo. I’m nothing but your puppet. I’m here, only for you. You hold my strings, and make me do whatever you want. Whenever you anger me, I try take it out on you. But you never let me. You’re always there, to make sure that I never lose my strings. You control my destiny.” Jason starts to move around a little. His fingers wave, and his leg moves. His eyes, look even more angry as he tries to stand, yet fails on each attempt. “You control me, Angelo. You’re making sure I don’t stand on my own.” O’Neil moves his arm, and again tries to stand, and can’t. His other arm comes around to the front, and pushes him up into a push up position, momentarily. He falls onto his face. Blood trickles from his nose. His face stays buried into the floor as he lies motionless again. “You’ve done it. You are my master. The Master of my destiny. I am your puppet. Control me. You want that contract? Make me want to sign it. I can’t do it myself, Angelo. I need you to do it for me. I need you to show me, that you need this just as bad as I do. Otherwise…” Jason moves his arms up once again. He struggles to move them as he pulls them to his body. He pushes up, with all the strength he can muster, and goes once again into the push up position. He curls his right knee under his chest, and pushes his spine up into a kneeling position. Slowly he starts to stand. His face turns red as he struggles with this everyday task. His eyes close as he works his entire body to stand. “I might just learn to stand on my own. I might just learn how much I should and do hate you. I might just clear the blinders from my eyes, and instead of looking at you with passive aggression, take a more proactive stance. I might just fight back, and show you all the anger I contain inside, Angelo. I just might name you my opponent for Genesis over Tyler Davidson, Chris Bagwell, or even Timo Bolamba. Sure, you're a deserving opponent, but does that even matter? Do I dare even enter a ring with you after all you've done to me? The answer to that, is no.' 'I may hate you, but you’re not worth my sweat, or my blood. You’ve shed enough of that. Go ahead. Make me change my mind Angelo. Make me want to get in the ring with you. Make me want nothing more than to destroy your very being. Do it. Or else on Glory, I’m going to go out to the thousands of people, and tell them that Tim Walden will be taking on Jason O’Neil in what’s sure to be one of the finest matches in FSW history.” Jason puts his arms out and strikes his Scarecrow-esque pose, looking to the heavens. “Act Quickly, Angelo Deville. Time’s running out for you. Can you handle…that?” Suddenly, Jason crumples to the ground once more, as the lights around him fade again. His eyes show the same anger, but laughter fills the empty stage, as he fades into the darkness..
