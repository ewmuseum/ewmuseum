Stinky Shit

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 2, 2002 'CHRIST!' Tommy swings open the door and stomps out of the bathroom. 'That smell is ABSOLUTELY HORRID!' Chris Bagwell enters in the room in response to the yelling, 'What the hell are you bickering about? He inhales and soon finds out what the commossion is. 'GOOD GOD TOMMY, DID YOU SLAUGHTER A COW OR SOMETHING?' Tommy, whiping the tears from his eyes, replies, 'It's Jose.' 'Jose?' Chris doesn't recognize the name off-hand. 'Who in the blue hell is Jose?' 'The 3'2 Mexican dwarf.' Tommy replies, as if Chris should have some kind of indication as to who Jose is. Chris simply replies with a, 'What?' Bagwell, completely confsed, walks over to the bathroom door. He grabs the door knob and jumps back when he hears a sudden scream. 'What the hell is he doing?' Tommy shrugs and says, 'I think he's trying to crap.' 'CRAP?' Chris seems astonished, 'It sounds like a 6 year old girl getting a watermelon shoved up her ass. Not some Mexican dwarf taking a shit.' 'That was kind of rogue,' Tommy replies with a slight chuckle. Bagwell seems furious at the stench, thus asks more about Jose, 'Where did this kid come from?' Tommy plays dumb for a bit, 'Jose?' 'Who else,' Chris says a bit aggrivated. 'Jose is no kid.' Tommy shakes his finger at Chris. 'He's 68 years old.' Chris grins at Tommy, finding it rather hard to believe. 'I don't give two shits how old he is. It was a saying, anyway.' He throws his hands up in frustration. 'Where did he come from?' Tommy gives a rather innocent look, not seeing what he did wrong. 'He was knocking on the door and asked to use the bathroom.' 'And you let him?' 'Well what did you want me to do?' He shrugs and gets a bit angry at Chris' lack of kindness to strangers. Chris snaps back with an answer, 'Tell him to keep his shit in his ass and find a toilet somewhere else.' Tommy can't contain his laughter. He breaks out in chuckle after that comment. Chris tries keeping a straight face to give off a pissed impression, but eventually breaks. He cracks a little smile. 'You better get in there and get his ass outta here, Tommy. That smell is horrible. It smells like rotten eggs mixed with wet three week old elephant shit. I'm gonna puke my nuts out of my mouth!' Tommy's eyes widen in shock. Is he kidding, Tommy thought. 'There ain't a chance in hell I'm going in there.' Chris glares at Tommy giving him a dastardly look. 'The hell you ain't! Get your ass in there and get him the hell out!' Bagwell grabs Tommy by the back of the head and walks him to the door. The bathroom door swings open, smacking Tommy in the head. Tommy falls to the ground, being knocked unconcious. Bagwell looks down at the Mexican swarf, Jose. 'You toilet no flush,' he mumbles. 'Damn man, you ARE short,' Chris says in amazement. 'You'd have to jump up to suck my dick!' Jose looks in confusement. 'Ha Wha?!?! You toilet no flush. I go.' Jose starts to walk away. Bagwell grabs him by his shirt and lifts him up. 'My toilet what?' he asks. Jose gives Chris a dirty look, while struggling to get out of Chris' grasp. 'It no flush. I go' Chris' fist tightens. 'WHAT?' Jose just repeats himself, again. 'It no flush. I go.' Bagwell still stands in shock and disgust. Yelling, 'WHAT?' Jose growls, still trying to escape. He snarls back with a comment, 'You deaf? I say it no flush.' Bagwell's face grows red with anger. He begins to shake. Jose shows slight concen, 'You sick? I say make toilet flush before bug come.' 'You better get your little Mexican ass back in there and make that flush.' Chris now grabs Jose with both hands. He raises him high up in the air. A look of fear begins to develop in his eyes. 'I no can. It overflow,' he says. Yet in some way, it doesn't make sense. Chris, stumped, continues scolding the little guy, 'How can a little thing like you, with an asshole the size of a needlepoint, make my toilet clog?' Jose, always having the answer, immediately tells Chris just how it is, 'I poopy and it stuck. I no can help.' Bagwell, now furious, throws Jose across the room. Jose hits the wall and falls onto a chair. He looks around, surprised to be alive. Realizing that Chris is about to pounce on him, Jose hops out of the chair and races to the front door. Jose jumps up and down, finally getting a grasp on the knob. He turns it just enough to pull open the door and run away. Just as Jose makes his way out the door, Tommy regains conciousness. He sits up, rubbing his head. Chris notices Tommy starting to kick back into it and encourages him oh-so politely to hurry along. 'Get up.' Tommy has no memory of what happened, thus he tries filling himself in, 'Damn man, what happened?' 'He opened the door and...' Chris pauses. A sudden smirk comes across his face as he eyes out the bathroom. Smiling, he continues to fill Tommy's mind '...you had a bad dream.' 'Oh.' Tommy says, still trying to piece things together. 'Odd. I really don't remember much at all.' Bagwell nods his head 'yes', and continues to push for Tommy to complete his supposed job. 'You said you were going to unclog the toilet or something and just hit your head so I left you.' Tommy nods his head in concurrence. 'Oh then let me get to it.' Chris smiles and waltzes into the next room quickly. He immediately scavanges through his dresser drawer, pulling out a small spray bottle of ETERNITY cologne. Chris sprays the cologne all around, on a rampage to dilute the horrid stench. Tommy, in the other bathroom, yells, 'Yo Chris.' Chris gets worried that Tommy remembers. Cautiously, he replies, 'What?' Tommy explains to Chris why he can't complete his task, 'The toilet isn't clogged.' 'It isn't?' Chris asks, slightly confused. What could the smell be derived from? Tommy finishes up his statement, bringing a sudden angered shock to Chris, 'No, but the sink is.' Chris drops the bottle of cologne and it shatters across the floor. Furthermore, Tommy adds a little bit of insult to injury! 'And it looks like there is shit all over your toothbrush.' 'Theres....haa whaaa....?!!?!??!' Chris goes BISERK! Tommy continues to add fuel to a fire, 'Dude man, it looks like someone cleaned their ass with your toothbrush and then drew paintings on your walls.' Chris falls back onto his bed, staring at the ceiling. It is apparent that he's pissed at life. 'That little sonuvabitch!' To Be Continued...
