There's a Stable at My Table

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-02-02 “There’s a Stable at My Table” as written by Timo Bolamba If a stable’s at my table, and their name is Mob Rule, Then the leader is a cheater, a zero and un-cool, Greedy never needy with three goons and no remorse, Then the gooney greedy cheaty is named T. Nelson, but of course! If a Samoan Man is training, moving to and fro quite fast, And his opponent in this mixed up game is a greedy tall grabass, And he wears the gold, looking bold, eccentric with much sass Then the drooling un-cooling ruling mans lesson will come to pass! If the tighties that are whitey residey in his drawer, Then the pair he does not wear are skidmarked and on the floor, True his money is his honey,a nd the girlies want no more, So the tightey whitey wearer’s unit will surely be rubbed sore! If your match is interrupted by a man with dollar signs, It’s then true, that me and you, will listen to his lines, And the hot air that will fill the room will lift us to the skies, Because the talky jabberwocky has signaled his demise! With greed knee deep, and cowards that creep, attacking from behind, Just rest assured, that with one word, they will reside in Timo’s mind, The fighting plight of the Samoan Knight will never be unjust, As Queen once said, straight from Fred, Another One Bites The Dust! If a stable’s at your table, but their name is not yet found, Because the semblence of great warriors, has yet to come around, And if they shine with mighty might, and pureness to fight wrong, Then the Shiney Mighty Fighties will fight forever on. When trouble pops its head up to see when good is gone, Then T. Nelson the greedy cheaty will find that he is wrong, If Timo Marvels Mike, and Mike Silences Timo, Then the Greedy Cheaty’s head will be discretely thumped upon! So remember friends as we may end this tirade of endless rhymes, And a stable’s at your table full of losers that will whine, The Greedy Cheatey Tightey Whitey Talky man will lose, And in the end the un-cooling man will shine Samoan Shoes!
