Wait a minute Michael...this tag reads...'Roseanne Barr'!

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/24/02 **Continued From Previous Roleplay** Questions whirled around in the heads of Jacob Jackson and Mike Marvelous. Where were they? Where ever they were, were they going to make it out alive? But the most puzzling question of all...Doesn't Rosie O'Donnell ever wash her bloomers?! Still woozy from being smothered in them disgusting panties, the Can-Am Connection tries to put the pieces to this puzzle together. Jacob studies the room in which him and his best friend, Mike Marvelous are being held captive. There they were, chained to the floor in some sort of dungeon. Allowed no more then three feet of chain to move about, so they were pretty much obligated to sit on the cold cement ground. Jacob began to occupy himself by playing with a small pebble on the ground, while Mike struggled to position himself in the most comfortable way possible so he could lie down. Suddenly two Oompa Loompas entered the room. Mike quickly sat up, and Jacob directed his attention away from the pebble and up to the Oompa Loompas. 'What do you want with us?' Jacob asked the Oompa Loompas with a hint of fear in his voice. It was ironic. In the ring, these two men feared nobody. They had wrestled the best in the business, and recieved merciless beatings from many wrestlers. But suddenly, they began to fear for their lives. They feared these Oompa Loompas, while they were not intimidated by their height or appearence in no way at all. Jacob and Mike towered over them as if they were giants, yet they feared them. It seemed as if they had somehow entered another realm. Could Jacob Jackson's dryer be a passage to another world? One of the Oompa Loompas approached the two men. It would have been so easy. Jacob could have reached out and grabbed him, he could have beat the little guy into a coma, but it was no use. Even if he had, they wouldn't make it out alive. There was too many of them. 'Tell me..,' the Oompa Loompa began, as he took a seat in front of the duo, 'Who are you?' Without hesitating one bit, Jacob and Mike began to introduce themselves as if these Oompa Loompas should have known. 'Well of course, Tis I, 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson!' Jacob replied with a smile on his face. 'And Tis I, your Canadian Hero, 'Five Star' Mike Marvelous!' 'Tis we! The Can-Am Connection!' They both said at the same time. The Oompa Loompa just stared at the two, puzzled. He had never once heard their names spoken in their world. Where had these two recieved their remarkable height from? They in no way resembled the rest of them. The Oompa Loompa continued on with his interrogation, 'Where do you come from?' 'Salt Lake City, Utah, and my friend here hails from...somewhere in Canadia.' Jacob replied. 'It's Canada you ignorant bastard..' Mike said with a hint of anger in his voice. 'MICHAEL!', Jacob scolded, 'How dare you use such a word! And how dare you call me ignorant!' 'Does it really matter anymore Jacob. Here we are, in only god knows-' Suddenly Mike was interupted by the Oompa Loompa. 'God..?' A smile suddenly crossed the Oompa Loompas face, 'Is that who sent you? Has our God sent you as a gift from up above' The two just stared at each other puzzled, pondering how to reply to this question. Suddenly without giving either of the two a chance to reply, the Oompa Loompa waved the other guard over. 'Is there a problem here Cletus?' asked the guard, approaching Cletus with a look of concern in his eye. 'No PhillipJoeBobThorton,' Cletus said obviously overwhelmed with glee, 'I believe these men were sent down from up above, as a gift..' 'You think so, Cletus? But why? What do these men have to offer us?' PhillipJoeBobThorton asked while looking towards Jacob and Mike pondering their exsistance in their world. Jacob and Mike began to 'vogue', flashing their pearly whites at the camera and flexing their well toned bodies. 'I really have no idea...' ---------------- Mike walked along with Jacob and the guards, while burrying his face in a pair of red panties, taking in huge breaths smelling them. 'MMMM...Britney Spears' Mike said with a look of extacy on his face. Jacob looked with a twinkle in his eye. 'Britney Spears?!' He snatched the panties from Mike's hands and began to bury his nose in them too. 'Wow...quite an interesting aroma. Where did you find these, Mike?' While pointing at a big pile of underwear with a sign over them reading 'Britney Spears' Mike said 'Over there..' Cletus stopped, as did the other guard. 'Ahh yes,' he began, 'Britney Spears..definately one of the most interesting of our garments we have to offer. This particular kind is perfect to ward off our enemies. The sour smell it gives off quickly incompasitates our trespassers.' Jacob begins to take in another deep breath when he notices something written on the tag. He studies the label and almost begins to vomit. 'Wait a minute Michael...this tag reads...'Roseanne Barr'!' Jacob then begins to unfold the underwear and they stretch out bigger then the size of both Jacob and Mike's egos together. Jacob and Mike quickly drop to their knees in disgust. Mike begins scraping his toungue with a stick he picked up off the ground, and Jacob tries making himself vomit by sticking his index finger down his throat. The guards decide it's time for a break. While Jacob and Mike continue their thing, the guards sit next to them. After taking a drink of water from what seems to be a sock, Cletus begins to talk. 'You see. We have been recieving these gifts from up above for many years. We find many uses for them. They vary from weapons, such as slingshots, to cantienes, to shelters.' Cletus explains while pointing to a group of Oompa Loompas standing underneath a gigantic pair of bloomers. Jacob and Mike hardly pay attention, since they are still disgusted by the fact they buried their faces in Roseanne Barr's underwear. Jacob looks up cocking his eyebrow and says, 'Huh?' Cletus sighs and says, 'Nevermind..C'mon, get up, we have a long journey ahead of us.' 'Where are you taking us?' Jacob whines. 'We are going to see the leader. He will determine if you will be of any use to us or....if you should be executed.' PhillipJoeBobThorton states bluntly. 'E-E-Execute? But I can't, I have to wrestle tomorrow night!' Jacob explains, 'And plus..I'm missing Maury!' With a look of confusement on the faces of the guards, they shrug their shoulders and lift Jacob and Mike off the ground. 'Let's go' Cletus says. ---------------- The sound of footsteps falling on concrete fills the air, accompanied by yet another guard, Jacob, Mike, Cletus, and PhillipJoeBobThorton approach a man sitting on a bundle of socks. The man is well overweight, and yet another Oompa Loompa. He's dressed in a black overcoat and black pants, and on top of his head sits a pair of underwear, briefs not boxers. Reaching from the top of his head, all the way to his back of the underwear is a large brown streak. The guards get down to one knee and bow to the man. Taking turns, the guards approach the man with the underwear on his head and take turns sniffing the long brown streak on the underwear, then take a seat on the ground in front of him. Cletus nods his head at Mike and Jacob then begins to speak to the man. 'Oh Great Leader, look what we found.' 'I see, Cletus. From where did these men come from' the leader replies. 'They just dropped from the sky your majesty. I think they could be a gift from up above..' 'Or...a plague from down below.' The leader says while studying the two men. 'Let me speak with them' The guards motion for Jacob and Mike to approach the leader. Hesitantly the two men walk up and take a seat next to the guards. 'Did you know you have a pair of underwear on your head?' Mike asks the leader. 'How dare you mock my symbol of greatness!!' the leader yells. 'Symbol of greatness?! What the heck is wrong with you people?!' Jacob pipes up. With that being said three guards holding axes walk into view. Two of the guards hold their axes up to the Can-Am Connection's throats 'How dare you speak to the leader in such a tone! Any last words before you die?' asks a guard. 'STOP!' The leader yells from the top of his lungs, 'I will handle this one myself' the leader says while grabbing an axe from one of the guards. After wobbling over to Jacob and Mike, the leader leans forward and snorts then says..'I'm....fuckin'....HUNGRY!' Suddenly, Jacob's body begins to shake and he falls into a deep black hole, he keeps falling and falling, with the same words repeating themselves, 'Im hungry! Im hungry! PUPPY CHOW!' Jacob wakes up in a sweat and he staring at the long wet snout of Sparky. 'Jacob get up, It's past noon and Im starving! I need Puppy Chow!' Sparky whines. 'What....where am I?' Jacob asks, but only answers his own question after coming to, 'Im...home! It was a dream!' Jacob suddenly sits up with enough force to knock Sparky to the floor. After doing a little jig Jacob sits back down on his bed and begins to go over the dream in his head. ---------------- 'Hello you lowlife scumbags that call theirselves fans, Tis I, your idol, 'THE Idol' Jacob Jackson!' Jacob says, hence beginning yet another historic promo for all to enjoy. With a cocky smirk spread across his face he continues talking at the lens of a camera held by none other then, Jacob's personal camera man...CAMERA GUY! 'I bet you all missed me...didn't you? What have you done without your Idol? I imagine your lives are even worse then they were when I was around? You sloppy pigs. Well now its time to turn that frown upside down, because I am back...back to show you the way! No longer do you have to seek wisdom for those who have none to give. No longer will you have to look for critisism from those who will lie to you just to spare your feelings. No..No..'The Idol' is back..and I still..hate all of you!' Jacob pauses for a moment then begins once again. 'Now to the real problem at hand. 'The Canadian Criminal' Chris Irving..ha..Tomorrow night, I will face you at Glory..and without a doubt..I will reign supreme! 'The Idol' isn't here to give lessons, 'The Idol' isn't here to give free rides, 'The Idol' is only here to prove one thing..that he's the best, and definately better then you Chris Irving! Chris, you think you can attack my partner, Mike Marvelous with a bat and cost us the match..and get away with it?' Jacob says while stomping his feet on the ground. 'Obviously Canadia's school systems are worse then I thought, because obviously only an idiot would interupt the Can-Am Connections match, and spoil my return! Last Wednesday was my night, as will be this Wednesday! I have not ever, and never will share the spotlight with anyone. Im not scared of your bat Chris..for all I care you can take a seat on that bat and ride it all the way to Glory, but I will not be intimidated by somebody who needs a weapon to bring down one of the toughest men in wrestling! Check the records, Chris. I've been holding gold since before you were conceived. I'm an FSW Hall Of Famer, and you think you can beat me? Well I tell you what Chris, you better take an extra dose of vitamans tomorrow morning because you're going to need that extra something to lift yourself off of that mat after my arm is raised in victory. If not, then you better bring a sleeping bag and plan on staying on that canvas for a long time because I will show no mercy. Is that clear..AYE?!' An infuriated Jacob Jackson is the last thing we see before.. Darkness.
