Cast Aways

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 28th, 2002 [Scene opens in the middle of the Atlantic ocean. The camera pans over the vast blue ocean then focuses on a small dot in the middle of the infinite blue waters. As the camera gets closer, the dot appears to be a large yacht. As the camera gets even closer, two men can be seen on the deck. One man is waving at the camera and the other is bent over the side of the boat doing what looks to be vomiting. The camera nears the boat enough for us to recognize Mike Marvelous(The man who is waving) and Jacob Jackson(The man who is vomiting). The captain...or as we like to call him...the 'skipper' of the boat pokes his head out of the stern to check on things] Skipper: Hey, how's your friend doing? Mike Marvelous: He's fine, El Capitain. Just a little sea sick. Skipper: Ok, just checking. Oh...and by the way...please stop calling me that. Mike Marvelous: Aye aye, El Capitain. [The skipper turns back to the wheel and shakes his head] Mike Marvelous: Ah...isn't this the life, Jacob? Fresh air...clear waters...just you, me, and the open sea! We'll be in Samoa in no time! [Jacob raises his head from over the side of the boat and wipes his mouth. His eyes are glazed over and his skin looks rather greenish] Jacob Jackson: Why are we going to Samoa again? Would you please remind me!? Mike Marvelous: Because I'm curious. I have a match with Timo Bolamba on Glory and I want to see what I'm going to be dealing with. I want to see where he is from. Jacob Jackson: But it's out in the middle of the Atlantic ocean for pete's sake! Mike Marvelous: I know, I know. That's what makes it so mysterious. But don't worry, Jacob. We're almost down. Just take your mind off the rocking of the boat. I know, it can get a little nautious to you, but I'm Canadian. I'm used to boats. I'm used to the feeling you get as the boat rocks side to side...back and forth....side to side....up and down...up and down.... [Jacob's eyes suddenly bulge out of his head as his cheeks fill up. He quickly bends over the side of the boat and releases another stream of projectile vomit into the waves below] Jacob Jackson: Enough! Enough! I don't need commentary, Michael!! Mike Marvelous: My apologies, Jacob. But don't worry, 'mate'! You'll get your sea legs sooner or later! [The skipper pokes his head out of the stern again] Skipper: Looks like we've got a storm brewin', boys! There might be some waves. Jacob Jackson: Oh great! More waves! Mike Marvelous: Ah, yes. Waves can get really big during a storm, too. Sometimes up to 50...even 100 feet! [Jacob vomits once again off the side of the boat. The skies suddenly begin to open up as rain pours all over the deck and on to Mike and Jacob.] Mike Marvelous: Here comes the rain. [The sails of the boat suddenly fill with wind] Mike Marvelous: And here comes the wind. [The sky gets dark with clouds as the rain falls even denser. The wind picks up even more and thunder begins to echoe like a cannon. Large waves begin to form. The boat bobs up and down over the large waves] Skipper: It's really startin' to pick up now! You boys better get below! Jacob Jackson: No, Michael! I don't wanna go below! I'll go insane with all this rocking and these waves! Mike Marvelous: But up here we could be swept off the deck by a wave! You don't want that to happen, do you? [As if on cue, a huge waves crashes against the side of the boat and onto the deck. Mike and Jacob are thrown across the boat, but they grab onto the safety railing to keep from falling over the other side. They scramble to their feet] Mike Marvelous: Jacob, we have to make it inside or we'll go overboard! We don't even have life jackets on! Jacob Jackson: Ok! Let's go! [They make a sprint for the cabin door but just as they are about to open it, another wave crashes onto the deck, sending them sliding across the yacht all the way into the stern where the skipper is sailing the boat through the rough waves. The captain looks down at Mike and Jacob as they struggle to their feet] Skipper: I thought I told you two to get down below! Can't you see we're in the middle of a huge storm!? Mike Marvelous: Yes, but the waves keep-- Skipper: Shut up and get down below! [Before Mike and Jacob can even make a move, a huge 20 foot wave crashes down on top of the boat. A loud crack is heard as the mast snaps in two, followed by a loud crash as it falls onto the deck, putting a huge hole in it. The force of these events cause the ship to shake violently. Mike and Jacob are thrown out of the stern of the boat and they slide down the deck on their backs. Mike grabs on to the sail with one hand and grabs Jacob with the other before he slips into the giant hole in the bow of the boat that is filling with water.] Jacob Jackson: We're gonna die! Mike Marvelous: I know! Jacob Jackson: Hold me, Michael! [The two best friends stand up and hug each other as the ship begins to sink. The skipper screams as a wave crashes through the window in the front of the ship and carries him into the sea. Mike and Jacob stand there as the boom of the sail swings violently and hits them into the water. They thrash around in the water as the ship sinks. The front goes down...then the middle....then the back of the boat slowly disappears under the water. Mike and Jacob struggle to stay above as the waves keep coming and coming, but they keep getting pushed under. The last thing the camera sees is a wave come down on top of the Can-Am Connection, then there is darkness] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Hours...even days later...Mike Marvelous opens his eyes slowly. He quickly closes them as he is blinded by a bright light. He sits up and rubs his hands over his eyes, then opens them again. Sand. He is on a beach. He looks beside him where his friend Jacob lies, clinging on to what looks to be a piece of wood from the ship. Jacob looks like he is still unconscious. Mike goes to stand up, then stumbles and lands on his behind. He is still a little shaken up from the previous events. He gets up again, this time slowly, and stands. He looks around him. He can see a long stretching beach and behind it, green vegitation. A jungle or a rain forest. Mike looks out into the ocean to see if he can spot the ship. There is nothing. Just a bunch of debris washed up onto the shore. He walks over to Jacob and nudges him with his foot. Jacob opens his eyes, shuts them, then opens them again. He slowly lifts his head off of the sandy beach] Jacob Jackson: M-Michael? Mike Marvelous: Yes, Jacob. It's me. Jacob Jackson: Are we dead? Mike Marvelous: No, we survived. [Jacob looks around, then gets up. He too rubs his eyes to make sure he is not still sleeping. After observing his surroundings, he turns to Mike and talks with a hand on his hip] Jacob Jackson: Where are we? Mike Marvelous: I have no idea. Looks like we're stranded. Jacob Jackson: Stranded!?....Oh god...this is just like them movies! The people get stranded...then they starve to death...then they die! We're gonna die, Michael! Mike Marvelous: I know! [Mike and Jacob both drop to their knees crying like idiots. They continue doing so until Mike stops and wipes his eyes] Mike Marvelous: *sniff* Well...we can TRY to survive. Jacob Jackson: But HOW!?! There's nothing here! It's a deserted island for crying out loud! Mike Marvelous: Hey, look on the bright side. At least we still have each other. It would be worse if we were alone. Jacob Jackson: That's true...but we're still gonna die if we don't have food! Mike Marvelous: We'll find food later. For now, we have to find shelter before nightfall...there could be wild animals that come out at night! Jacob Jackson: EEEK! Ones with fangs and claws!? Mike Marvelous: Yes. And horns...and scales...and red glowing eyes! [Suddenly, a loud screech is heard behind Mike and Jacob] Mike Marvelous & Jacob Jackson: EEEEEEEEEEEK!!!!!! [It turns out it's just a seagull] Jacob Jackson: Whew! I almost had a heart attack! I thought for sure that some monster was about to kill us!....Say....don't seagulls stick around mainlands? Is it possible we floated back to America? Mike Marvelous: Hey! You're right! Seagulls never fly far from the mainland! That is...unless they're gonna... [Mike is interrupted as the seagull lands on a rock, pulls a 9mm pistol out of it's feathers, holds it up to it's head and pulls the trigger. A loud blast is heard as the seagull's head disappears] Mike Marvelous:....die. Jacob Jackson: It's true, Michael! WE'RE STRANDED! Waaaaaa! [Scene fades to black as Jacob breaks down crying and Mike puts his hand on his chin, thinking on how to get out of this mess] To be continued....
