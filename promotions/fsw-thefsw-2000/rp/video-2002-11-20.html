20 November 2002

Date: now Over, and over the video plays. Jason O'Neil entering the ring. Punch to the face of Chris Chase. Punches to Ming and Deville. 4 on 1 attack by Mob Rule. Whip to the ropes. O'Neil with a double lariat to Chase and Ming. Rapture to Deville. Nelson turns and begs off O'Neil... Rewind... Nelson begs off O'Neil... Rewind... Nelson begs off... Rewind... Nelson begs... Pause. Nelson, stares deep into the eyes of Jason O'Neil and sees the fire inside. O'Neil winds back for a big punch to the face of Nelson. O'Neil stares back into the eyes of Tyler Nelson, the Six Star Superstar of FSW, and sees nothing but fear. A finger appears on the screen and touches the digital face of the Greediest Player in the Game. 'Tyler...' a voice says, seemingly distant, a slight echo reverberating. 'Tyler Nelson, begging off an attack from Jason O'Neil...' The finger disappears for a moment. In slow motion, Tyler Nelson turns around and is met with a throw over the top rope. As he hits the ground, the screen falls, and explodes into millions of tiny peices on the floor. A light turns on as a pair of boots stands over the carnage. 'Pathetic.' the voice says. 'Mike Marvelous and Zameer had been at Timo's side for weeks now, battling you, Tyler, and you didn't fear them. Yet...Jason O'Neil emerges, and takes out Mob Rule. Alone. Chris Chase, felled with a single punch, along with Ming, Angelo Deville, and you Tyler, you didn't even take a shot from me. What was it about me, Nelson? Was it my eyes? Did my eyes give away my plans for you? Could you see the hate burning inside me for everything you stand for?' Slowly, Jason O'Neil turns around and grabs a broom. He sweeps up the little bits of television left on the ground and puts them into a pile. 'I won't lie to you Nelson. Given the chance, I would have hurt you. I would have loved to make your Six-Star face into a work of art, only equaled by a Picasso, or a Dali. The feeling of your bones, breaking under the force of my anger...I can't tell you how fantastic that would have felt. But, you caught me off guard. You, Tyler Nelson, the man who's battled the likes of Xavier Kannon, Duke Williams, Clyde and others begged off Jason O'Neil. And in my moment of weakness, I missed the opportunity to destroy you with one migty blow.' 'Be thankful for that, Tyler. Just ask Angelo what the Scarecrow can do. Ask him how the Rapture feels. Or better yet, just wait. You'll feel one soon enough.' O'Neil bends down, and sweeps the dust and debris into a dustpan, and throws it in a small trash bag. The Television, a shell of what it once was, sits on the floor, broken and destroyed. A smile crosses the face of O'Neil as he looks at it. 'I was ready to pack it in. I was ready to just walk away. But Timo changed that. He sat, and talked to me. For that, I can't thank him enough. I'm sure he was as shocked as everyone else was that I ran down that ramp, and cleaned house. Timo needed me. I needed Timo. We're even now. Favor for a favor. Blood for Blood. I owe him my life, and my life is exactly what I'll give to the cause.' O'Neil throws the bag into a trash can and looks at the ground. He shakes his head taking one final look at the television and walks away. 'Tyler Nelson...begged off...'
