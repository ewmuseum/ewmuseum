Ring Mat Hero. A tribute to Ed's Career.

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-31-02 (Song is tune to “Juke Box Hero” by Foreigner) A young man stood outside of a ramshackle arena. Blood sweat and tears poured out inside, catching his interested. He couldn’t see because he showed up to late for a ticket, but he knew he had to feel the show somehow. -Standing in the rain, with his head hung low. Couldn’t get a ticket, it was a sold out show.- He fought back tears, even though they would be awash in the downpour. The youngster felt his very heart give out on him and he shook, knowing he needed something more from this night. The young man listened as he heard something. -He heard the roar of the crowd, and he could picture the scene.- He looked inside his mind and saw the two men in the ring, colliding with one another, feeding off each others energies and the energy of the fans. The sound seemed to flow from within the building and he smiled. -He put his ear to the wall, and like a distant scream, he heard One Steel Chair, just blow him away!- The sweet sound of a clanging of skull and steel livened his eyes and he smiled. The world seemed to stop for him as the rain no longer was cold and he felt his blood pumping. The young man turned and looked into the night sky with wild ambition. -Got a beat up Steel Chair, from his basement at home.- He had an old chair lying around that he had never envisioned using as a weapon, but now he had all it took to swing with the greatest of ease. To top it off he added one secret ingredient to the volatile mixture. Barbed Wire. -That One Steel Chair, he held in his hands, didn’t take long, to understand!- He swung it in the air, practicing for the big show, hoping it could propel him into the annals of wrestling glory. He had but one thing left to do, and that was name his weapon of choice. The scrawny young man with a torn flannel and wild hair could only think of one name. BOB~! -That one Steel Chair, held far up high, was a one way ticket, straight to the sky!- The young man held it aloft and the heavens thundered. Ed was born this day, his persona would never again be that of a mild mannered young man, but that of a trained steel chair ass kicking machine. He walked out the door and slammed it behind him, confident he would never return. -So he’s gonna keep rocking, and he’s never gonna stop, gonna keep on rocking, someday he has to be on top! To be a Ring Mat Hero, with stars in his eyes, he’s a Ring Mat Hero. He had One Steel Chair! Ring Mat Hero, stars in his eyes, Ring Mat Hero, He’ll come alive.- He walks endlessly, ever trying to make his name. He fights on and on, drawing and having drawn blood. The Man that was once a boy is scarred, but resilient. He will earn his nickname, whether it kills him or not. -In a town without a name, in a heavy downpour, thought he passed his own shadow, by the backstage door.- The Hardcore Legend stopped, seeing a fan and remembering his rise in the business. He looked out the door and the man was gone, but behind him he left a Tiki Torch. The Legend knew he had not seen the last of the shadow. -Like a trip to the past, to that day in the rain, how that One Steel Chair, made his whole life change.- He smiled and picked the torch up. It was merely a sign of things to come in the business, and he would surely look out for this new guy, like he had wanted so bad for someone to look out for him when he was a young man standing in the rain. -So he’s gotta keep rocking, and he’s never gonna stop, gotta keep on rocking, he has to stay on top! He is a Ring Mat Hero, with stars in his eyes, he’s a Ring Mat Hero. He had One Steel Chair! Ring Mat Hero, stars in his eyes, Ring Mat Hero, He’ll come alive, Come Alive Tonight.- Ed enters to the tune of “Food for the Brain” and the fans erupt. The Hardcore Legend smiles as he feels the whole world around him remind him of the one day he stood there, wanting this very moment. He has come full circle in this business, and nobody, but nobody is better at what they do. -Yeah, he’s gotta keep rocking, he just cant stop, gotta keep on rocking, He Will ALWAYS be on top! And be a Ring Mat Hero, got stars in his eyes, He’s a Ring Mat Hero, Ring-Mat-Hero, RING MAT HERO! Stars In His Eyes, Tonight!- In the distance, the young man carries a tiki torch, and walks in the rain. He smiles, thanking his hero for making it possible to be the essence of Hardcore. He will be missed.
