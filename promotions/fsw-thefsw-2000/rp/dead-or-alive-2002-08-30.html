Dead or Alive

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/30/02 ~The scene opens in the state of Connecticut on a very windy and cold day. As you look around the scene, the interstate going through Connecticut is visible as cars fly by and their way to and from various locations, some for business and others for personal pleasure. As you look further down to the level of the ground and off to the sides of the interstate you see what appears to be a never ending line of trees. Most if not all the trees and roads in the area are covered by a somewhat thick layer of snow. You come down to a large mansion somewhat hidden back amongst the trees. The first thing you see is a large black iron gate with the top of the gate partially covered in snow still. You can barely make out the words on it as they read “Davidson Estate”. You go further into the massive complex and notice the well kept condition of the grounds surrounding the huge mansion when you notice for the first time how much older the mansion itself appears to be then the rest of the surroundings. As you approach the house the scene slowly begins to blur and when it comes back into focus you are no longer outside but are now inside of the mansion in a dimly lit room. The room is obviously quite a large one with each wall being covered by a large bookshelf which is in turn loaded down with books as far as you can see. Sitting a few feet away from the far wall you see a large oak desk set there and at the desk there appears to be a person seated. The only light coming in through the round window across from the desk is just enough to cast the light on the person that as you come closer you can see that it’s an older man. He has thin gray hair covering his head as well as a bushy gray beard which looks to need a trimming. To his right there is a wooden cane placed up against the desk itself and on top of the desk is seated an ashtray with a freshly lit cigarette sitting in it. On the left hand side there appears to be an ink jar and an old fashioned feather pen sitting in the ink. The man reaches to the cigarette and lifts it to his mouth before breathing in a slow long drag and then slowly blowing the smoke back out. He places the cigarette back down into the ashtray and looks down at the desk right in front of him. For the first time you notice a book laying there in front of him. The book is already flipped open to some point past the start and all you see there now are blank pages. He turns the page back a bit and on all of the previous pages you see writing in a spidery handwriting. He looks over the previous four pages before turning back to the page he was at and reaches up to take the pen out of the ink jar. He brings the pen down and slowly he begins to write on the first blank page he finds.~ ~The Davidson Chronicles Chapter Thirteen: Dead or Alive 8/30/2052 by Tyler Davidson~ I had somewhat come back into my own bearings and slowly it seems regained my own composure. The visions and the dreams which had been plaguing me were slowly starting to fade away into the back of my mind once again where they had belonged all along. I could only hope that they would remain behind me long enough for me to accomplish that which I had set my sights on. All of FSW had already been in a complete and total uproar the past few weeks but it never seemed to be dying down as of late. Each week it seemed they did all that they could to surpass the one prior to it pulling surprise after surprise which left the fans and the wrestlers reeling from the repercussions. Chip O’Meara was the man who had began it all upon being appointed commissioner of the company to combat the idiocy of Paul Orion. A move which I like many of the people were grateful for but the repercussions have been non stop over the past two weeks. First former PCW Heavyweight Champion, Boda had arrived and then it was followed with the announcement of the Dead or Alive Tournament. A tournament which I later found myself paired with that imbecile Magnum Wolf. And to think that it was only the start of many things to come. What a week it had been. Just when you think you have some things figured out you find yourself abducted by a dildo toting dork like Magnum Wolf. On top of that it was even a week where Chris Bagwell decided to go Mission Impossible and pull some insane techno-gadgeted break in crap of the Davidson Estate. I suppose he had at least pleased himself with such an endeavor as I doubt there was anything of value for him that really came of it. Perhaps he had mistaken my thoughts about his missing girlfriend Rebecca. Perhaps he thought that despite all the games I had played with his poor malleable mind at Genesis that I did really have her somewhere. A naïve thought at best for Bagwell but perhaps it was just more of his wishful thinking. Perhaps deep down Chris had really hoped I did have her so it would be his one and only chance of regaining the love he had once had and so foolishly lost. Luckily for Bagwell he is no longer my concern. I embarrassed him in all the ways I possibly could and he could never keep me down. No doubt our paths would cross again one day but until then I would have bigger and better things to move onto. As much as Bagwell would hate to admit it he is nothing but a small fish in the big pond which is FSW. The next man on my agenda is a much bigger fish. Mr. CCW himself, John Jupiter. The man who had done it all in CCW had arrived with a splash in FSW. He had however proven himself to be in dire need of a shortened career when he stepped into my path. The man who won everything there was to win in CCW, a company I competed in myself for a brief period of time you would think would be smart enough to steer clear of me. Sadly that has proven not to be the case. As a result I was forced to take matters into my own hands as it relates to Jupiter. I would be more than happy to demonstrate to John Jupiter that he is no longer in the safe confines of CCW. Gone were the likes of Spider, Blayze, Cianan, and Kensuke Suzahara. Granted his little buddy Kyle Velocity has seemingly followed him here as well but that would not make enough of a difference to save him. For two consecutive weeks he had interfered in plans of my own and prevented me from achieving a final taste of retribution and revenge against Chris Bagwell. A man who had jobbed to Hank~! certainly has no business stepping into a ring against me regardless of how much he has accomplished in his career elsewhere. I could have done what he did in CCW had I wasted away years of my life in that place but I chose not too. I moved onto bigger and better things while Jupiter remained content playing in the minor leagues of wrestling. Then he comes to FSW first with a vendetta against SDMJK who he thought was Kid Wonder and upon finding out otherwise he turns his attention to me? It wasn’t the greatest move he could possibly make but I welcome the chance to put him back in his place. Jupiter is, was, and always will be an inferior wrestler to anyone with half an ounce of talent. I had every intention of proving this to the world when I came across the chance to face him one on one. Held down and buried by the politics in CCW, he was saved by pure chance but now in FSW with a new boss in town he would not be so lucky. I would get my hands on Jupiter and I will make him scream and feel the pain. I will destroy the very essence of John Jupiter and all that it was he stood for. ~The scene changes back to the library room where the old man is seated. He places the pen back into the ink jar and takes another cigarette out of the pack and lights it up. He looks down at the previous pages he had written in and quickly scans over each of the pages before closing the book as the scene fades to black.~
