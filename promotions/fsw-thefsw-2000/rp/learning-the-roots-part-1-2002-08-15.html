Learning The Roots Part 1

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 15th (The scene opens as Mike Frampton enters an adoption agency. He enters and walks up to a desk with an elderly female behind it.) Mike Frampton: Hello miss. Where can I get some files? Janice Beetnick: Files? What type of files? Mike Frampton: On a child adopted from this agency. Janice Beetnick: Those files are strictly confidential. Mike Frampton: Maybe this will change your mind. (Mike pulls out a ten dollar American bill.) Janice Beetnick: American money! One second. Mike Frampton: Alright. (Janice pulls out a file which reads American to Canadian fund translation.) Janice Beetnick: Oh my goodness! I'm rich! (Janice puts away the file.) Janice Beetnick: Now, whose file are you searching for? Mike Frampton: Myself. Michael Frampton. Janice Beetnick: Alright let me check. (Janice puts on a large pair of glasses with thick lenses. She begins to type quickly.) Mike Frampton: So how are you doing? Janice Beetnick: Well I have this rash... Mike Frampton: Never mind. (Janice finishes typing and begins to print out a file.) Janice Beetnick: Alright, Michael J. Frampton, born November 20th 1977 in Dallas Texas. Parents Paul and Mary Frampton. Adopted at the age of three here in Toronto. Mike Frampton: Can I see that please. (Janice hands over the files.) Mike Frampton: Do you happen to have details on my parents? Janice Beetnick: All people who put their children up for adoption must fill out a form. Give me a moment. (Janice types away and begins a new print job. She hands it over to Mike.) Mike Frampton: Will this have their address? Janice Beetnick: Well it may not be their current but it is at least an address they lived at. Mike Frampton: Alright thank you. (Mike places the files in a brief case and exits the door.) ~Lesson~ A child is something that needs development. In the development process things can go right or horribly wrong. If you're cautious and ready for anything than chances are the errors can be easily corrected. But if you develop a child carelessly it can become warped. Just like a virus that is sent to your computer. A malicious program causes destruction to your computer. If a child is developed the wrong way than chances are that it will become a destructive force. The development of a child depends on the developers. The developers who are the guardians can create a wonderful being who becomes a great developer themselves or the child can become a terrible and replaceable entity. If you don't care than your child will not care. As you act, you are programming the child. What you say and do will effect if the child will last for eighty years or be obsolete in twenty. To take pride in your work will always improve performance. Take pride in your child and encourage it and it will become a person of great fortune. Take little pride and criticize and in ten years you will see what you have done and wish you could go back and re-write it. Unlike a program where you can debug after release and create new versions, with a child you can not. Once it has its Intelligence programmed it will begin to learn and block out new lessons and programming. After it becomes dependent you must watch what takes effect. Parents are the original programmers. Keep aware and always try to offer a new style of programming when you see a new programmer who is creating a virus. Why? Because that new child that is being programmed could become the next evil in this world who takes lives of innocent people. I am a virus. But unlike other viruses, I can do good or evil. I can be trust worthy one hour, then destroy your programs the next. Why? I was programmed to be this way. The lesson is; Don't let people create malicious entities or else the world is doomed. Do whatever you can to prevent this evil from being written. Help. Offer them advice and make sure that the errors are corrected. Concentrate and you will succeed with helping the world.
