Inside My Mind

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-23-02 {Ever since EOE was twenty years old he always wrote down the things the he was feeling, thinking of going through it was a sort of a personal mental therapy for EOE when he had alot of things on his mind. When his friend froma gang first told him about this therapy he thought I was stupid, but when that friend died EOE went to this method and ever since he found it to keep him out of trouble, keep things out off his mind, and found it easier for him to cope with things. So every now and then we will get the opportunity to go into the deep mind of EOE.} Will they ever learn... There's two important subject matters to that question. The first is that will people like Timhoe, Johnny, and Chris learn. Will they learn that in order to ATTEMPT to defeat a force of our magnitude is going to take more than one person. It's going to take a team thing, but I don't ever see happening because for one all three of them are bitches, then you have two of them bitching over chair shots. Then you have Chris Irving, it obvious that he hasn't learned that taking a short cut in this business will shorten your career rather than shooting your straight to the top, but in due time he will pay and he will learn. It really doesn't matter if they learn the rules of the game or not because there is no way in hell that anyone in FSW can match the power of EOE, Mike, Idol, Big, or Leon Chambers. Why? Well for one just look at us everyone in the FSF have been World Champs with the exception of one, and we look damn intimadating might I add. Two we are man enough to put history behind us and do what we have to do to keep running shit and we have some history. I powerbombed Idol through a flaming table to win the world title, Mike tried to cost me the title, and me and he did other things to disturb me and Big D well let's just say we couldn't stand by each other for half of a second. As for Leon Chambers we were nice enough to let him join us. See all that there should show you that this team is so far legit. Then you have Timhoe and Johnny, they without a doubt have the fans behind them when it comes to battling us, but they also have egos the size of Texas and that's a ego. They sort of remind of Jack Brooze. Now I don't give whether with us or not, but other people have been approaching him about FSF, it's the best thing for him. But he's just like the rest of them, he still has that 'I'm going to defeat everyone on my own' frame of mind which is really a screwed up frame of mind to have in my opinion. Maybe it's because I've beating a group of people on my own after you've done it once it gives you power, but when it comes around again you learn to conserve and protect yourself. I plan on being here for a long itme I've already proved that I don't need anyone to make, now I'm just playing it smart. But I guess Brooze has never had the 'me against the world' task. But if Jack wants someone to give him some advice maybe I should let him know that he picked the wrong time to have that 'me against the world' frame of mind because there's a force that is in FSW that will not be defeated by one person. Unlike us, they can't trim their egos down and work as a team to even contend with us. Infact the only way the FSF would ever go down is if we crumble within, which brings me to my next point. The test for us is looming in less than twenty four hours, as a matter of fact it started for me Saturday when I heard from Eve that he the Gunslinger ~ahem~, announced that Mike and Big D were in the #1 Contenders match on Glory. Now I'll admit I frowned upon it just like any other person would do if their name hadn't been called, but I maintained and let it go. But there was one individual that was sitting by me that didn't make the situation any easier. Eve the person that is still not up for all of this. You would think after what Big D did for her last week she would change here mind, but no still stubborn as ever. I had to deal with comments like 'See what I mean', 'That's the shit I'm talking about', 'Are you gonna just let that happen?', 'Oh hell naw f**k that, that's not fair', and then there's 'Now you know if the CEO or anyone leso in FSW had to hand pick two people out the FSF to contend for number one contendership for the title the last person they would pick is you, because everyone hates and they know once the title is in your hands no one can get it out.' Yea she said all that. Although some of it may be true I still have to over look it and go with the flow. Another time or day I would be pissed, but not right now. Tomorrow and from that point on everyone else will find out if Big D, Mike, Leon, Idol and myself were all talk or will be able to prove the world that a force with this much power and different personalities can accomplish the golas that they set. The other day someone approached me and asked me who do I think would when, it was kind of strange cause I never had to care about anyone winning a match besided myself, but now it's different I guess. Maybe I should root for Big D since he did make the save last week and prevented Walden from executing a moonsault on Eve. Big D didn't have to do that I guess he did that just to prove how much of a team player he is or maybe he knew that I would hold him responsible because he was the closest one to her, it doesn't matter it's just good that it didn't happen. But it ain't good for Tim Walden, I hope he realizes what he's in for tomorrow I haven't been all that vocal about him attempting to hurt my girl cause of other things but if anyone knows about my past here in FSW they know what happens when someone takes it upon themselves to try to get to me by hurting Eve. Just because he didn't get the job done doesn't mean that I won't act like he got the job done. Last time someone put their hands on Eve the most horrible event that everyone had to witness went done...I set that bitch on fire. Although I've cooled down from my older days I never doubt myself on whether I still have that side in me or not. But Tim Walden must be taught a lesson and I must use him as an example, because after tomorrow everyone take not on what happens when you f**k with Eve. That's on that's on my mind today. July 23 2002 7:40 pm
