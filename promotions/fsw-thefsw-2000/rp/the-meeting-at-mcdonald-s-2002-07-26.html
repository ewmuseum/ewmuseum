The Meeting At McDonald's

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-26-02 How could the FSW let this happen? A nineteen year old, rookie... Not just a rookie to the FSW, but a rookie to all of the wrestling world... Winning his first three matches! These matches weren’t just handovers, either... A victory over Five Star Wrestling Great, Epitome of Evil. Followed by an outstanding rookie heel in Angelo DeVille. Then, just recently defeating ‘The Idol’ Jacob Jackson in his come-back match! Don’t you think it to be a little degrading to the name of the federation to allow such a thing to happen? Well, maybe... If there wasn’t one more name attached through blood to this certain wrestler... That’s right; Doozer. Known outside the ring as Scott Lavigne, Doozer could be the one reason why FSW doesn’t have its panties all up in a bunch about a man debuting to wrestling altogether and taking out some of the fed’s top names. While Five Star Wrestling has been trying frantically to get Doozer, himself, to join with the federation... They have had no such luck. Scott wants to be done with wrestling... At least for now. --- So, with his nephew still undefeated in his wrestling career, Doozer wanted to celebrate with his nephew by taking him out to eat... At McDonalds... Hey, this is Doozer we’re talking about here! The man usually doesn’t go out of his way for anybody. His nephew, Christopher Irving, knew this... And that’s why he took it as a very large compliment that his uncle would actually spend money on him... Only five bucks or so... But it’s still money! The two set to meet at the very same McDonalds that they’d be eating at, how ironic... Yet, what Christopher Irving didn’t plan on was his uncle showing up with The Dude... After the two just finished some special cookies. You know what that means... As of this very moment, Doozer thinks he is Doozerman and The Dude thinks he is Doozerman’s faithful side-kick, Dudeyman. As the two, now self-proclaimed, Superheroes... just roll into the parking lot, we see that The Canadian Criminal is already sitting down at a table in the outdoor section of the McDonalds. Doozerman and Dudeyman notice this and stealthily sneak past who they view as Purple and Green CheebaWeebas to get to Chris. Eventually, after getting scared after brushing up against one... Dropping to the ground... And crawling to the table... The two fully-grown men finally make their way to Irving... --- Dudeyman: 'Holy Crazy Canadian Kin, Doozerman!... It’s your nephew!' Chris: 'Oh God... Don’t tell me you two think you’re Superheroes...' Dudeyman: 'Think... No!' Chris throws his head back softly and takes a deep breathe of releif... Dudeyman: '... We know we are Superheroes!' Chris: 'Unc... Did you really have to-?' Doozerman: 'Sssshhh... Young one... There are... spies amongst us!' Chris: 'Let’s just order...' --- Chris gets up and starts to walk towards the front of the fast-food market to order whatever he wants... Yet, before he takes two steps, Doozerman grabs his arm with one swift motion. And with another, he takes his nephew and throws him back into the seat he was sitting in. Irving, looking completely dumbfounded by the whole thing, shot his uncle a questioning look, and shook his head, as if saying 'what the hell are you doing?' Doozerman: 'Oh no... Don’t thank me... Just look at me like I’m a damn crackhead... Who! May I add... Just saved your life!' Chris: 'Unc, can we just go up and order already? It doesn’t seem to dangerous.' Dudeyman: 'That’s just what they want you to think... Then... BOON! CheebaSlime... All over you...' Chris: 'Did you just say... Boon?' Dudeyman: '... No!...' Chris: 'Yeah you did... What the hell is Boon?' Dudeyman: 'Maybe you just heard me say Boon...' Chris: 'Yeah... I did...' Dudeyman: 'Oh... I mean... Maybe that’s just what you wanted to hear...' Doozerman: 'Dudey, you are retarded... Let’s just order... We can take on these Cheebas...' Chris: 'You could even say we’d smoke em!' Irving’s little joke sends him into an hysterical laugh. While, on the other side of the table, Doozerman and Dudeyman just look at him confused. Dudeyman: '...' Doozerman: '...' Dudeyman: '... I don’t get it...' Doozerman: 'I think we should go get our food now...' Chris: *clears throat* '... Right...' --- With that said, the three men start walking to the front of McDonalds where they can make their orders. Irving, walking nonchalantly, trying to ignore the two men who believe they are superheroes surrounded by the enemy walking like two paranoid drug addicts in a roomful of cops. Eventually, the three guys make their way to their destination and a worker, on the other side of the counter, greets them. --- McDonald’s Worker: 'Hello, sirs, how may I help you?' Chris: 'I was wondering if I could have a smile... I don’t have much money on me...' The McDonald’s Worker just stands there, rather impatiently, shooting Chris Irving a rather crude look. Irving just smiles and laughs in that immature, 'I’m a smart-ass way.' While a few people get autographs signed by both Doozerman and Dudeyman, Christopher gives his real order. He nudges Doozerman after finishing, telling him to order. Doozerman: 'Yes... What exactly are those fries made of?' McDonald’s Worker: 'Um... I’m guess... Potato?' Doozerman: 'What kind of potato?' McDonald’s Worker: 'I don’t know...' Doozerman: 'So you admit it!' McDonald’s Worker: 'Wh-what?' Doozerman: 'You people just took the potato from a random farm... Implanted deadly poisons... Cooked them into french fries... And sell them off... IN AN EVIL PLOT TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD~!' --- After Doozerman’s outrageous outburst, the fast-food place went totally silent. The only sound in the whole place was that of the machines in the back doing whatever they do back there and the buzz of electricity. Christopher Irving just looked around for a good five minutes and nudged Doozerman. He then motioned to Doozerman to get closer, so he could whisper something to his uncle. --- Chris: 'Maybe we should get outta here... Before you get arrested again...' --- Doozerman nodded his head in approval for the idea brought to him by his nephew. So the two started to exit McDonald’s, leaving Dudeyman who was standing totally still. Doozerman noticed that Dudeyman wasn’t at his side, so he stopped, turned around and shot him a 'What the hell are you doing?' look. Dudeyman slowly moved his head towards Doozerman and said, slightly under his breath... Dudeyman: 'CheebaWeebas have horrible vision... If I stand still... They won’t even notice me...' Doozerman, now knowing that he would no be able to get Dudeyman to move from his current position, didn’t know what to do. Yet, The Canadian Criminal, knew exactly what to do. Using a type of motivational speech that Doozer used on him many times during his childhood... He figured, since Dudeyman certainly was more immature than himself, he could use this on the poor man looking like a living statue. Chris: 'Well... Hm... I guess we’ll just have to go and get Doozerman a brand new side-kick...' --- That’s all it took and Dudeyman jumped out of his statue position in a split second and ran, in a full sprint to Doozerman’s side. The three men exited McDonald’s and entered their cars. Christopher waived good-bye to his Uncle as he drove off and Doozerman and Dudeyman waived back as they left as well...
