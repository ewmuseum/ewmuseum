Immortal By Blood (Part 1)

17 November 2002

Date: 17th November 2002 Wrestler Name: 'The Eternal' Brooklyn Blade Title: Immortal By Blood (Part One) “The love that once was born can not die For it has become part of us, of our life, Woven into the very texture of our being. Each of us would wish to leave some part of ourselves, So here and now we bear witness to the one we knew in life, Who now in death bequeaths a subtle part, precious and beloved, Which will be with us in truth and beauty, In dignity and courage and love To the end of our days.” - Algernon Black -------------------------------------------------------------------------- The sleep of certain men and women - mortals and immortals scattered around the world - is haunted by a vivid, mysterious dream: of twins with fiery red hair and piercing green eyes who suffer an unspeakable tragedy. It is a dream that slowly, tauntingly reveals its meaning to the dreamers as they make their way toward each other - some to be destroyed on the journey, some to face an even more terrifying fate at journey's end... I walk under a deep purple haze as the sun pulls back its warm rays, fleeing to its hiding place for one more night. For just a moment the beauty of it all is astounding, the colors richer, deeper, saturating all they touch with life. The silence is glorious, the regal night animals peering through the clear, cold air with eyes that seem to possess a supernatural glow, their shadows dancing playfully in the moonlight. This is the time before fear and superstition overwhelm me, when this vision still renders me childlike with wonder. It is pure poetry. Then the moment is ended and my heart quickens, for I feel this vision quickly slipping away from me. Dark is falling. I now know that death and the loss of memory...is the only true heaven. If only I could be dead and gone, forever lost in the darkness of forgetfulness, I could say then, though I could say nothing since I would not exist, that I would be in heaven.I once thought when I was a living being that immortality would be such a beautiful thing, that to live forever would be paradise. I could see so much, do so many things and commit such unthinkable crimes. Yes that's right. I was a criminal, a sinner some would call me, an evil sadistic bastard others would say. When it all boils down to the common denominator, I was a person who took no responsibility for his actions. I cared not who I hurt, rather I enjoyed their pain. There were many things I was into; many things of which I do not doubt would cause you to wince in horror, and say things like 'Why would God let such a man live'. Maybe you should ask yourself if there really is a God. Then again I think I know better now. Yes I would say I do. I am immortal after all and shall live through all eternity though I only wish for death. I wish for the silence of nothingness. I wish I had never been born, or to have died and found forgetfulness. An eternity of empty thoughts would be preferable to this eternity I now find myself imprisoned in. I guess you would call it hell. I do. The walls of my cell are dark and cold stones of unimaginable build and strength I have yet to be able to even chip them, or leave an impression other than my blood on them. I exist in a cell with four walls. There is no door, but somehow they get in. I don't know who they are, but it matters little, I have my ideas. There are holes in my walls, and I can see out into terribly infinite expanse of cells just like mine, with holes in their walls just like mine. I cannot see an end to this bizarre structure that we prisoners inhabit. I cannot see any walls or ceilings, for that matter I do not even know where the feint source of light comes from. I can sometimes glimpse eyes staring back at me through those holes in the walls of other cells and I see in them terror, and pain. I assume they see the same in mine. I cannot hear them, those other trapped souls in their cells, except when they scream in terror and pain, when they come to visit. I still do not know how they get into our cells. There are no doors. I do not know how long I have been here, but I believe it matters little. I am not even aware of the passage of time. TO BE CONTINUED
