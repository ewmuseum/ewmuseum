The Aftermath of the Hardest Match Of My Life...

22 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd Oct 2002 So, I lost... Right? I'm lying in a hospital bed after receiving a Bounced Czech after a 20 foot drop and going through a table. I had to have lost... Not just the match, but this entire bitter feud with Boda. Right? It's just like FSW announcer Sam Collins said... Boda beat me, and O'Meara beat Orion... Right? Wrong. I may have lost the match. My body may be broken. But do you see O'Meara in a more powerful position than he was before this whole thing began? Does O'Meara have anything on CEO Orion now? Does he have the most powerful bargaining chip there could ever be in this promotion... The FSW World Title... On one of his men? No. It was my... Shall we say, mission, to stop that from happening. I was brought here to stop O'Meara from seizing control of the company. Irrelevant of anything that has happened since, my goals were acchieved, my mission completed, the night that Boda 'screwed' me... Us... Out of our match with Timo Bolamba and Mike Marvelous. The night he left me to fall victim to a double ankle lock. Boda wanted to hurt me... And granted, I was in pain. For all of the 30 seconds it took to tap out. But after that, whether you could see it or not, I was delighted... I returned to my appartment that night and couldn't stop laughing with joy. For, despite how stupid I'd always known Boda was, despite how foolish and short sighted a rival I knew I had, I never - EVER - dreamed that I would be gifted the ultimate victory against him like that. Oh sure... Boda won't realise it, and chances are most other people won't either... But no matter how much pain I'm in right now - and don't go thinking he'll be much better - the fact remains... He merely won the battle. I won the war. And, when the time comes to let him know that, to explain to him, in simple trms he can understand, that he did my job for me, noone will be able to deny that, once more, Cassius Deamont has one upped the idiot who's crossed him. Although, that time will be a long time coming. For, while I was ultimately the victor, I'm still here. In the hospital bed. Some victory, huh? * * * * * 7AM, Wednesday 23rd October 2002. Cassius Deamont lies on his back, in too much pain to move. The ceiling above him is shiny, smooth, but filthy, with a single light bulb hanging down in the middle of it, covered by a filthy light shade. Just his luck to have nothing but this to look at... The door to the room opens, letting in a great deal of noise from outside for a few moments before cutting the sound off abruptly as it closes once more. Much as he tries, Cassius can't look to see who it is... His neck hurts far too much... Not to mention his back. Judging from the two sets of footprints he hears though, he could guess who his visitors were... Seconds later, when a soft, gentle voice spoke, his suspicions were confirmed. 'So, you're awake.' Erica. 'You were out of it for a while...' Kathryn. 'Ugghh... Yeah, I know... I remember coming round once or twice but...' 'Never for longer than 20 minutes,' Erica cut him off, taking a seat on the edge of his bed. 'This is the first time you've been in any state to talk though,' Kathryn added. 'I'm presuming I'm pretty bad?' Cassius asked, calmly. 'Not really,' Kathryn answered him, 'You and Boda were both brought here for a check over... A few bumps and bruises each, and Boda needed a few stitches in his forehead...' 'Doesn't feel like just a few bumbs and bruises to me,' Cassius began, laughing as he did so, 'I can hardly move an inch without being in agony.' 'Well yeah, you were reffered to a chiropractor for some stiffness in your back, but it's nothing too serious... You should be up again in no time,' Erica told him, reassuringly, 'Although, I have no idea what you have to laugh about,' she added, seeming confused. This prompted Cassius to laugh even louder, before beginning to cough and splutter. Eventually he stopped, and began to speak once more. 'I expect Boda's somewhere celebrating now, huh?' He asked, hypothetically. He already knew that the chances were the hired gun of FSW's commisioner was out somewhere, partying, basking in the glory of having hospitalised the man who had been a thorn in his side for the past couple of months. 'I expect so,' Kathryn answered, 'That bastard.' 'But you'll be back soon... Able to get your revenge!' Erica added. 'Heh... Maybe,' Cassius began, 'But right now I don't see what the point would be... I'm perfectly satisfied with how things turned out.' 'Satisfied that you were hospitalised? By that fucker?' Kathryn exclaimed, outraged. 'What?' Was Erica's only word in a shocked response. Cassius began to laugh once more. 'Boda's bigger than me... He's tougher. Physically, he outmatches me, there's no doubting that, and I'm not arrogant enough to convince myself otherwise. But a battle of brains... He's no match for me there. And, whether he realises it or not, that's what this truly was... A battle of brains. And I beat him there,' Cassius explained. However, his explanation didn't seem to prove good enough for the two women, who seemed just as confused as they had been before he had said anything. 'What do you mean?' Kathryn eventually managed to ask, unsure of whether or not she should. 'Oh, I'll explain it to you some time, When I have more energy. Right now though, I'd rather not be discussing wrestling, if you don't mind... Makes the pain in my back worse,' Cassius responded, tiredly. 'Well, if you're sure,' Kathryn said. 'Want us to leave you alone?' Erica asked, once more sounding concerned. 'If you wouldn't mind,' Cassius responded, 'Just for a little while.' And, as the two women left the room, Cassius let his eyelids close, and, as he finds himself blacked out to the world, our scene fades to black.
