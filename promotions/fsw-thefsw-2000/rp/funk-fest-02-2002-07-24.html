Funk Fest '02

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/24/02 (OOC Note: This rp takes place the Thursday AFTER this weeks Glory, just so ya'll know:P) ~Ah, disco. There can be no better music. There is no music that can sooth you, calm you, and bring out the jive better than disco. When one listens to disco, they are in the ultimate freedom.~ ~And that’s exactly what Asa is doing here. We see Asa, with a large smile on his face, blue jeans, a white t-shirt, and of course, with a pick in his hair. He is walking away from a small ticket booth, and holds a white ticket in his hand. He walks toward a large crowd, and the crowd stands in front of a large stage. Above the stage, in big neon lights, are the words “Funk Fest” in orange and yellow. Asa gives his ticket to a manager, and enters the crowd. The crowd is full of people. People who appreciate the essence of music. People who truly have, “the jive”. In other words, black people.~ Asa: Damn, it’s too bad Mel couldn’t make it, she’d love this stuff! ~Asa now comes to the front of the crowd, close to the stage. A Jamaican man with a mic walks out onto the stage.~ Jamaican guy: How’s it goin’ there mons and monettes? ~The crowd yells out.~ Jamaican guy: That’s good mons. Now, without any further ado, let’s get this party staarteeed!!!! Asa: Nothin’ like a good ol’ disco concert to calm a cat daddy down! ~The Jamaican guy leaves, and in his place comes…~ Asa: Nelly? ~Nelly and is crew of little rappers come parading onto the concert floor. They start singing “Hot in herre”. The crowd cheers out as they run around the stage.~ Asa: Nelly? I thought this joint was for some disco!! Not this shitty rap! Ah well, I guess it’s just black daddies in general. ~Nelly finishes his “music” to a cheering crowd.~ Asa: Alright, now to the disco!!! ~NO! Not disco, but…P. Diddy.~ Asa: What the fu… ~P. Diddy comes out playing “bad boy 4 life” with his two other guys that sing the song. The crowd cheers, of course, as they pull up their falling pants all over the stage, and grab at what looks to be their testicular area.~ Asa: What the fuck is this shit??? ~P. Diddy finishes his song, and leaves the stage. A new singer comes out, and it is no other than…~ Asa: Please, no!!!! ~Fat Joe. Joe hobbles out onto the stage, mic in hand, singing “whuts luv”. His dancers come out, and shake their bodies around all over the stage. Asa sits in the cheering crowd, leaning on his arm. This position stayed that way, as nine other rappers came through and went. Finally, the Jamaican guy comes back.~ Jamaican guy: And now, for the grande finale mon!!! Asa: Please be disco, please be disco, please be disco…. Jamaican guy: EMINEM!!!!! ~Eminem, unfortunately, runs onto the stage, singing “without me”. ~ Eminem: “Now this looks like a job for me, now everybody, just follow me…” ~Asa’s face fills with rage.~ Asa: Alright, that’s where I draw the line!!!! ~Asa runs out of the crowd, and runs back stage. A few moments later, he appears ON stage, with a mic in his hands. The crowd stops, and so does Eminem.~ Eminem: Uh, what are you doin’ man? Asa: What am I doin’? What am I doin’? Man cat daddy, I’m trying to save these people from this shit that you call “music”. ~The crowd boos.~ Asa: THIS ISN’T FUNKFEST! Man, funk fest used to be disco! Not shit, I mean, rap! This stuff hurts my ears jive turkey! Eminem: Jive turkey? Dude, disco went out 30 years ago. And, so did the afro. ~The crowd laughs, but Asa does not. He stares at Eminem.~ Asa: Now you done it, bitch daddy. I’ma kick your ass like the little bitch you are! ~Asa drops his mic, walks up to Eminem, and…~ KICKWHAMSTUNNER~! ~Eminem falls to the floor, motionless. The crowd throws things at Asa as he kicks Eminem in the gut repeatedy. Cops try to make their way through the crowd as Asa beats the hell out of Eminem. He lies down next to Eminem, slamming his head into the stage repeatedly.~ Asa: Hi! My name is…*wack!* My name is…*wack!* My name is…*wack! wack!* ASA’S BITCH! SAY IT BOY! SAY “Hi, my name is Asa’s bitch! ~Eminem’s bloody face lifts up.~ Eminem: Hi…my name *cough* is…Asa’s…bitch. Asa: I can’t hear you bitch daddy! ~Suddenly, a girl runs onto the stage with a bat in her hand. She’s wearing baggy red pants, with black flames coming from the bottoms. She has a tight, black t-shirt with “Metallica” on the front. The red hair gives it away. This is Amy fuckin’ Campbell. Eminem stands up, slowly. Amy runs up, and WHACK!!!!! Slams him square in the head with the bat. Eminem lies motionless as Amy and Asa both kick at the fallen body of Eminem. The police finally come onto the stage, and restrain the two. The crowd boos as Amy flips them off, and Asa smiles away. They are pulled away into the back as the scene ends.~
