Preparing for Boda and Kimbusa

9 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/9/02 :: The scene opens and we see that we are outside. It is the perfect day. The air is just right. Not too hot, not too cold. There isn’t a cloud in the sky, and the sun is shining bright. A gentle, soothing breeze is present. The kind of day we live for. Suddenly the scene cuts to a gigantic entrance. This entrance has two 20ft. doors made of wood, and look just like the doors that kept “King Kong” from killing everyone in “ King Kong” the movie. Across the top of these doors is a sign that reads in red lettering “ State Zoo”. The camera moves out on these doors and we see an individual staring amazingly at the massive entrance to the zoo. The individual is none other than our deformed hero, Six Finger Cisco.:: Cisco: Wow! Zoos certainly have changed since I was young! :: Cisco remembers back to the one time his parents took him to the zoo. He remembers a small backyard type zoo with sheep, ants, and donkeys as the main attraction.:: Cisco: Well, time to train. :: Cisco goes up to the doors and they slowly open. Cisco enters through the doors and the inside of the zoo is seen. A massive fence marks the entire zoo area off, surrounding it without a single hole to escape. To the left is a customer service booth, where you buy the tickets, and possibly annoy the clerks with stupid monkey sex questions. To the right is a rocky water park type thing where the water animals in this park stay. Straight ahead is a golden brick walkway that leads past many cages with animals located inside.:: Cisco: It’s like the Garden of Eden…only without that stupid Eve. :: Cisco makes his way to the customer service booth to buy a ticket. He stops at the booth window and located inside is a middle-aged woman, dressed in a safari suit gimmick uniform and a scowl on her face.:: Woman: How many? Cisco: um….how many what? :: The woman is aggravated by Cisco’s ineptness.:: Woman: How many people for admittance? :: Cisco sarcastically looks behind him showing the woman that he is the only one there.:: Cisco: One will be fine. Woman: Five dollars. :: Cisco hands the woman the five dollars.:: Woman: What the hell is that? :: The woman points to Cisco’s sixth finger. Cisco: It’s a sixth finger. Woman: Sheesh. That’s pretty gross. Cisco: Oh stop…I’m sure you say that to all the bachelors… :: The woman hands Cisco the ticket and yanks her hand away. Cisco walks away.:: Woman: (Under her breathe) Freak. Cisco: (Under his breathe) Bitch. :: Cisco looks around like he knows where he wants to go. Suddenly Cisco finds a sign with a banana on it and in yellow letters says “ Gorilla Land”. Cisco’s eyes light up.:: Cisco: Time for conditional training, part 1. :: Cisco rubs his hands together and smiles. The camera fades to black and then fades back in from black. The scene that we see now is that of a jungle atmosphere. There are trees, rocks, and various cave-like areas around this scene. Suddenly a big black Gorilla makes his way by the camera. This area I speak of is clearly the Gorilla Land mentioned on the sign. Suddenly we see an overhead shot of this area and the camera moves back to show that the Gorilla Land is about 10ft below the zoo walkway. Cisco is looking down on the gorillas.:: Cisco: Alright. When I faced Boda I had a little bit of trouble…well…except for this last week when I choked his ass out… :: Cisco laughs:: Cisco:…but anyway…Boda is one big son of a bitch. He’s almost as big, if not bigger, then these stinky, dumb, ugly gorillas. Hmmm…now that I think about it…Boda is a lot like these things. So…in order to prepare myself for the massive Boda…I will fight one of these gorillas. :: Cisco thinks this scene through in his mind.:: Cisco: Pure genius. :: Cisco looks around and then hops over the railing and down to the Gorilla area.:: Cisco: Umph! :: Cisco brushes himself off.:: Cisco: Okay…maybe I underestimated the fall juuust a tad. :: Cisco hops up and is ready to be attacked by a gorilla, but the gorillas don’t seem to care that Cisco is in there area.:: Cisco: Come on you stupid fucks! Attack me! :: Cisco is frustrated and now tries to mock the gorillas. He puts his hands under his armpits and makes gorilla noises….the gorillas do not respond.:: Cisco: Okay…fine! :: Cisco goes up to one of the gorillas and punches it in the mouth. This time the gorilla comes back and wails Cisco in the face sending him down to the ground. Cisco starts to bleed from the mouth a little and his eyes tear up from the blow.:: Cisco: That’s it?… That’s all you got.? Well what do you say to round 2 big guy?! :: Cisco punches the gorilla in the mouth again and this time the gorilla retaliates with a punch to the balls and then a punch to the face.:: Cisco: (Talking like he’s nearly knocked out) Whew! It’s getting’ windy in here!…oh, I’m sorry…was that your punch I felt?.. :: Cisco gets pissed and applies a Clutch of the Deformed(Cobra Clutch) on the gorilla. The gorilla struggles for a moment or two, flailing its massive arms like crazy,…until it eventually falls unconscious to the dirt. Cisco releases the hold.:: Cisco: Now what bitch?! :: Cisco kicks dirt on the motionless gorilla.:: Cisco: Not so tough now huh? :: People have now gathered around the gorilla area and watch in shock as Cisco mocks the knocked out gorilla.:: Kid in crowd: Mommy….did that man kill the gorilla? Mommy: erm…um…no honey…he just …um…is helping it go nuh-nights. :: Cisco notices the crowd and panics. He scurries his way out of Gorilla Land and onto the walkway. Cisco runs away from the scene. Cisco gets away from this scene and finds his way to a zoo map.:: Cisco: Okay…conditional training…part 1…a success. :: Cisco looks at the zoo map.:: Cisco: Now…to prepare for that Asian ASSassin, Kimbusted. :: Cisco looks on the map and finds what he wants. The camera fades to black and then fades in from black. We are now at the water area of the park. The camera focuses in on a sign that reads “ Hippopotamus”.:: Cisco: Perfect! If I want to prepare for that over-stuffed egg roll then wrestling a Hippo will get me prepared. :: Cisco hops over a small fence and into the water. The water is about knee high from where Cisco is standing and gradually gets deeper as you move forward. Cisco begins to look for a hippo.:: Cisco: Okay…hippo…hippo….hippo… :: Cisco continues to look when suddenly a massive beast comes at him from the deep end of the water. It charges Cisco and pounces on top of him. Cisco is being crushed by the hippo as it tries to mall him with its teeth. Cisco panics and pokes the hippo in the eye repeatedly with his sixth finger. The hippo wails out in pain and scurries away. Zoo keepers run over to Cisco and pull him out of the water and onto the walkway.:: ZooKeeper: Oh my god! Buddy are you okay?! Cisco: Um…yeah…I’m good. ZooKeeper: How did you fall in? Cisco: Fall in?…oh…um…yeah…I slipped? ZooKeeper: Oh thank heavens you are alright! :: Cisco knows he is in the clear and that the zookeepers didn’t see him hop the fence.:: Cisco: Yeah! You’re lucky! I’m just trying to have a nice day in the zoo and your fucking hippos attack me. ZooKeeper: I’m so sorry sir.. Cisco: It’s quite alright…I’ve attained the information I needed.. ZooKeeper: What? Cisco: uh…I mean….I’ve seen enough for today. :: Cisco gets up and exits the zoo. As he exits he is bleeding from the mouth and has a black eye from the gorillas, and is soaking wet with his clothes ripped from the hippo. A mother and a child are getting ready to enter the zoo as Cisco walks out.:: Little Boy: (Cries) Mommy! I don’t want to go to the zoo!! :: The scene cuts to black:: !Fin!
