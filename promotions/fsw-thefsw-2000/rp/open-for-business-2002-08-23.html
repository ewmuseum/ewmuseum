Open for Business.

23 August 2002

Date: 8-23-02 The scene opens in a dark alley. No light shines in or out of the alleyway, but you can hear the sounds of someone shuffling their feet. Also, cutting through the darkness is the small red light of a cigarette burning. The red light moves up towards where we assume a mouth is, and begins to glow brighter. Apparently, someone is smoking that cigarette. Suddenly, the red light flies across the screen, and explodes in a burst of sparks as it hits the wall of one of the buildings surrounding this character. The sound of fingers snapping can be heard, and just as it's heard, a large spotlight comes on behind the man. You can't make out his face... but you can see by the silhoutte that the man is about 6'7'. And he's wearing a detective-style hat. When he begins to talk, it's in the deep and gravelly voice of someone who is disguising their voice... 'Hello FSW. I bet you're all wondering who it is that stands before you. Well, I'm not telling you. At least not yet. It would blow my cover...' As he says this, a car pulls right into the alleyway, and the headlights show us that the man standing there is Magnum Wolf. He's clad in a trench coat and detective's hat, with a pair of goofy glasses on his face. He looks utterly disgusted now. 'Ah God dammit! I told you to park on the street, Virgil! Sweet jesus... no wonder Ted DiBiase fired your ass! No one was supposed to know who I was yet! How do you expect me to watch out for the little guy now?!' asks Magnum with fire in his throat. 'I'm sorry, boss! I didn't know you were taping yet!' Magnum throws off the hat and glasses, and the trenchcoat. Underneath, he's wearing a flowery silk shirt that is unbuttoned down to below his chest, allowing a tuft of chest hair to stick out. His hair is pulled back into the same greasy ponytail we saw on Glory. His pants are extremely tight, and bright white. Absolutely hideous, unless you came from the 80s. There's even a hint of a mustache on his upper lip. His annoyance is obvious, but he has to keep going, because the camera is rolling. 'Well, just get over there somewhere, and stay out of the way. I have a promo to do. *clears his throat* Ok, well, so you know who I am. That's ok. I'm a master of disguise, so maybe next time you see me, you WON'T know who I am. Anyway, FSW, I'm here for all of you. I'm here to watch out for you... to take care of you. If you're unlucky, I'm here to TAKE YOU DOWN... because I'm Magnum 'P.I.' Wolf, and I don't take no shit from nobody!' 'Isn't that a rather big thing to claim?' Magnum looks down at his pants where a huge bulge is trying to burst forth from beneath his white pants. He shrugs, and looks back at Virgil... 'Well, yeah, but I can handle it...' Virgil looks confused. 'Nevermind.' In the background, you hear the sounds of a police siren, and they continue to get louder. Magnum looks over at Virgil, then at the area he's in. He glances back at Virgil again... 'Is this private property?' 'It may be...' Magnum hits the ground, and rolls. He jumps up after the first roll, and continues his evasive manuevers down the alley until he comes to a door. A police car pulls up in the alleyway just as Magnum kicks down the door, and dives into the building, leaving Virgil to fend for himself. Virgil, who was looking around for the cop car, turns to where Magnum used to be. 'This is bad news--- Hey. What the?!' -----Magnum unwittingly was trespassing on private property. Luckily, he was able to evade them for the time being... unluckily, he left his compadre Virgil behind to deal with the boys in blue. What good will Magnum do with his 'new' profession? What evil will he stop? Probably he'll do nothing good at all...-----
