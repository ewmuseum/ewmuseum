Nineteen With Issues

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-7-02 To be Nineteen and wrestling in one of the better federations around... You'd think it would be a dream come true... So far, it hasn't lived up to all the hype in the eyes of Christopher Irving. Sure, he won his first match... So his record is perfect... The problem is this; his uncle, his idol, isn't giving him any respect for the win and his debut as a proud Canadian didn't win him any fans... First off, let's start with the conversation between Uncle and Nephew... Legend and Hopeful... Old School and New School, as it was last night, when it actually occurred... --- Chris just made his way home from the rather long celebration at the bar... Getting in with the assist from his fake ID... And he decided to give his Uncle a call. Doozer had just finished watching another episode of The Simpsons while drinking a beer and eating some pizza. If the moment were to be frozen into a picture, it would perfectly symbolize the differences between the two of similiar blood and only six years difference. The Canadian Criminal, young and taking in life by the cup-fulls... And Doozer... Been there and done that... Sick of it all... Only hanging around for the love of wrestling and his nephew. Getting back on topic, Chris called up his Uncle and decided to start the conversation with a humorous prank call that they always do to each other... You'd almost think the two would catch on... But then you realize exactly who you are thinking about... Christopher Irving: 'Do you have a cold?' Doozer: 'Well... I guess... But... Who's askin?' Christopher Irving: 'Is your nose running?' Doozer: 'Um... yeah... But not bad... Oh, wait! I get it... This is some dumb ass telemarketer, tryin' to sell me some cold shit...' Christopher: 'Don't get off topic... Is your nose running?' Doozer: 'Yes! My nose is running!' Christopher: 'Well, maybe it wouldn't if you weren't so butt ugly!' Doozer mumbled the words 'you little shit' under his breathe as he realized he just got pranked by his nephew. Doozer then put his beer on the stand beside him, at down the rest of his pizza quickly, and stood up straight in his chair; happy to be on the phone with his nephew. Christopher: 'What's up, old man?' Doozer: 'Not much, young gun... How ya doin'?' Christopher: 'Not bad... After that match I had... I'm feeling pretty good... Did you like that part when I-' Doozer: 'Yo, yo... Don't get too excited about it, little man... It wasn't the most respectable win there, buddy...' Christopher: 'Yeah... But I still won... Come on, weren't you excited for your first professional win?' Doozer: 'Yeah... DWF... I caught the guy tryin' to leapfrog over me and gave him a snap-powerbomb so hard I almost broke his neck.' Christopher: 'Okay, so... You're win was more respectable... At least I got the win, right? Man, I know I can take him fairly!' Doozer could then be heard letting out a long, painful sigh over the telephone line. Chris, on the other end of the phone, became enraged and stood up from his sitting position. Christopher: 'I can't believe you... You push me to win... To do my best... But win... So I do... And it's not good enough?!' Doozer: 'Look... There is winning... And there is winning fairly...' Christopher: 'No, you look... You are just a has-been that is jealous of me! Because you know that I am going to be twice the wrestler that you ever were... And you can't handle it!' With that last statement, The Canadian Criminal took the phone and slammed it onto it's holder. He then stormed off into his condo bed room and tried to get to sleep. --- And that was that... By now, surely the two have forgiven each other... They both hold that common trait... They will get hot-headed in no time... Blow up on each other (although it was mostly CC in that case)... and then forgive each other within minutes really... Watch out for the next Christopher Irving article as we discuss what I was going to discuss now, but haven't the time... How being Canadian and being yourself seemed to be a huge let down for Christopher...
