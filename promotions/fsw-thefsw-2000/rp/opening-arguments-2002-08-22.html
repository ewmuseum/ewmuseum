Opening Arguments

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Steven Caldera is a tremendous man. Not so much in size or in appearance, but in presence. His very being tends to spread over anyone near him, enveloping anyone who might be within ear's reach. Kimbusa is a tremendous man. Not in presence but in size; he is the perfect opposite of Steven Caldera. Kimbusa is a massive hulk of humanity. Caldera is a massive hulk of charisma. Steven Caldera sits in his hotel easy chair, his jacket thrown carelessly over the bed, loosening his tie. The room is over the top in its extravagance. The jacuzzi can be seen from the main room. A priceless chandelier hangs high above the dining room table, which sits on a platform above the rest of the room, looking out through the glass wall to the city lights twenty stories below. The easy chair is of the finest leather, and the television is thirty two inches. It has all the expensive amenities. Caldera begins to whistle lightly to himself as he pulls his red tie out from around his neck and tosses it on the bed. He quickly whips around in the swiveling easy chair, flips a switch, and reclines. Seventy feet in front of him stands the five hundred pound mammoth Kimbusa, who is perfectly still as he gazes out the dining room window. Caldera smiles and shouts to his accomplace. 'Champagne?' Kimbusa answers with little more than a shrug. Caldera sighs and leans back to the end table next to him, where a bottle of fine champagne sits. He also snatches up a crystal glass and pours himself a small helping of the liquid. 'I know what you're thinking. You're thinking that all this fine living isn't for you.' Kimbusa does not answer. 'That's OK. I understand. You and I hardly know each other anyway. Well, let me re-phrase that. You hardly know me.' Kimbusa's interest is now perked, and he turns slightly to catch Caldera out the corner of his eye. 'Don't think I don't know about what happened back home in little Tokyo. And don't think others won't find out. The truth is out there, Kimbusa-san, if you're willing to look for it.' Kimbusa, now a bit disturbed, turns to face Caldera in full, but remains still. 'Oh yes, beneath the Armani suits and the three hundred dollar champagne, I'm actually a bit of an intellectual, Kimbusa. I know how to get information. I know who to talk to and how to interpret the fragments I get. I know who you are, Kimbusa.' Kimbusa scowls. 'Who am I, then?' 'You are a monster.' Kimbusa is taken aback by Caldera's succinct answer. With a grim nod, Kimbusa agrees. 'I am.' Kimbusa turns back to the window, as Caldera flips his easy chair upright, and moves towards the closet. He throws the doors open and begins looking through his belongings, idly selecting his wardrobe for the following day. 'But you don't know who I am, do you? Well, I'll tell you. I was the owner of Global Championship Wrestling, until I got tired of it, that is. I got my start in a wrestling ring as well, and I was good at it. I was very good. But that's not where the glory is, Kimbusa. I'm not the type of person who can stand to be subserviant. I'm a leader, you see. I prefer to be on top of a situation. And when I finally stopped bothering with GCW, I saw an opportunity. You were incarcerated, and I was looking for someone to aid me in my arrival to FSW. At first I thought of Mr. Jason O'Neil, but he didn't like the idea. And so I bailed you out, my lawyers will take care of any remaining problems, and here we are.' Caldera finally makes his selection, pulling out a suit jacket and pants and hanging them on the TV stand. Kimbusa has been listening intently, still staring coldly at the city throbbing with life below. 'The storm clouds are brewing, aren't they, Kimbusa?' Kimbusa's eyes close, as if the monster were visualizing the weeks to come. 'They are.' Caldera smiles, pleased with this. 'I won't intrude on your business too much. But understand that you are in my debt for the time being, and I might call upon you for favors from time to time. And the first is this Jason O'Neil character.' Kimbusa's eyes snap open, growing a bit red. 'I will crush him.' 'Do whatever you want. I know this guy, Kimbusa, and he's not to be underestimated. But he's frightened deep down inside, because he knows what I'm capable of. Just watch yourself, and you'll be fine.' Kimbusa spins around to face Caldera, his face full of anger. His eyes have grown wild with rage, having suddenly turned red. Caldera stops abruptly, taken aback by Kimbusa's hatred. 'He will be destroyed! I shall show this American why I am a monster! I will show the American spectators why I am a killer! I shall drown him in my anger, and crush him beneath my spite! I will drive the breath from his lungs and make him beg for mercy! But he shall find none, for I AM A MONSTER!' Caldera seems a bit intimidated by Kimbusa's rage, but suddenly cracks a smile. Pleased with Kimbusa's anger, Caldera idly continues with his work. 'Whatever.'
