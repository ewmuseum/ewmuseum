The Cool Mountain Air

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-18-02 Crispness hangs in the air as the sunlit morning breaks. The countryside of California, mountainous and fresh faced, awakens a weary yet still Dastardly warrior from his slumber. He yawns a bit and opens his sleeping bag, then realizes it is really cold in the mountains, and quickly shuts it. His fuss has woke his wife, Gwenneth Cross, and she squirms a bit in the two person sack. Timo Bolamba, The Dastardly Lord of Your Universe, looks about semi nervously and taps her on the shoulder. She rolls over to look at him and he smiles a bit. ‘What do you want?” “Well, I was thinking…been a while since…you know…we were alone, no kids, no nothing in the way?” She thinks about it for a few moments, then rolls over to see Timo face to face. In the morning, Timo looks like most other non-dastardly men, except still just a little studlier, and the thought of just being alone in a tent with him sends most women into a swoon. So naturally his wife had little resistance… “Oh, I don’t know Timo…what about…you know who?” “Him? You mean you know?” “Yeah…HIM.” Timo sighs. “I only brought him along because he knows how to fish and I don’t. Not only that, but he is my partner, we have a sort of bond, you know? Like Bill Dance and Roland Martin.” “Who?” “Two fishing guys…they have TV shows Gwenn, where have you been for the last year?” It is now Gwenneth’s turn to sigh, and sigh she does. “You know, this isn’t really doing much to put me in the mood…” Timo smiles a bit and raises his eyebrows up and down excitedly. He reaches a hand into the sleeping bag and she purrs for him slightly. Timo pulls the sleeping bag over their heads and it begins to flail around wildly. Suddenly it stops as if something were terribly wrong, and two muffled voices are heard. “What was that noise? “It’s nothing Gwenn, trust me.” “Last time you said that, you were trapped in a giant bowl of cottage cheese for seven hours. No, there is no “nothing” that’s definitely something.” “Okay, I’ll find out what it is…” Timo pops his head out of the sleeping bag, and standing there, in all his Canadian glory, is Mike Marvelous. “Morning to you Timo, where might Gwenn be?” Timo fidgets his fingers, and looks nervous. Mike looks down at the large sleeping bag, then to Timo, then winks and nods knowingly. Timo makes a motion as if he were going to grab Mike, but he is being held down by Gwenneth, who doesn’t know Mike is standing there. “Damnit Mike…If This Tent’s A-Rockin’ Don’t Come A-Knockin!” Mike backs up slowly and turns around, walking away and muttering to himself. “Fine, ol Mike will go aboot his own business then. I’ll go line us up a boat and bait, and stuff, you just stay back there and work your Mojo…..aww crap, how come I never get to work MY mojo?” Mike’s voice trails off and Timo disappears into the sleeping bag again. The voices begin again, one agitated, and one reluctant. “That was him wasn’t it?” “No…that was…a talking bear. Yeah…” A large sigh. “And what did this bear have to say?” “Oh you know, just wanted to make sure everything was okay, going to catch him some salmon and eat some garbage…the usual.” “And why did the bear have a Canadian accent?” There is a long pause. “Because he was drinking a bottle of Molson.” There is a long and drawn out silence and finally Timo emerges from the sleeping bag, wearing nothing but a pair of Hanes boxers and his Pe’a. The Dastardly Bastard’s hair is disheveled, his eyes have enough crud to spackle a kitchen, and his joints creak like an old door. “Yup, its good to be up in the mountain air, it smells just like a car freshener shaped like a tree up here Gwenneth.” Timo puts on some clothing and tries combing his hair, but has little success. Instead he puts on a fishing hat, and an old worn vest. He unzips the door to the tent and emerges, smelling the cold mountain air for the first time in a long time. Timo looks around the campground and stretches. “Well, I suppose I could do with a walk. It’s bad enough that Meanstreak has to kick the crap out of me, I may as well limber up.” Timo begins to walk along a beaten path, as he does he finds a suitable walking stick, and a long strand of grass. The grass hangs effortlessly from the side of his mouth, and the stick taps lightly as he walks along, admiring the scenery, and the wildlife.” “Country Roads, Take me home, To the place, I belong…” “Well, if it isn’t the Samoan John Denver. Aboot time you made it oot of there.” Timo turns to see Mike walking along a trail with two fishing poles in his hands, and a couple permits. Timo extends his hand for a Tecmo Bowl High Five, but Mike denies him. “No No, not after I trek all over gods creation to find this stuff for you while you make busy with the wife. One of these days Timo, you and me, we are going to have one hell of a fight!” “So then I guess I don’t get a kiss good morning?” Mike looks at him funny. ‘And what’s that supposed to be aboot?” Timo laughs a bit and grabs a rod and a permit from Mike. The Canadian looks at the Samoan and keeps his distance. “Nothing, lets go down to the dock man, we have some fishing to do!” They walk steadily toward the dock, keeping pace with one another. Timo looks over to Mike and sighs a bit. The Canadian master looks over to his partner in crime and smack s him on the arm. “What’s up man?” “I dunno Mike, this is some pretty big stuff coming up.” “Eh, nothing we aint seen before.” Timo looks over as Mike makes the flippant remark and shrugs. The Samoan knew all to well that was the case, but something was different, it bugged him. Through his old fishing clothing that smelled musty, another gamey scent was afoot. “I don’t know Mike. Something isn’t right this time.” ‘What do you mean Timo?” “Well, let’s say we do beat Meanstreak and Nelson…” Mike looks over and shrugs. He is dressed in a flannel shirt and a pair of blue jeans. His leather boots clunk as they walk along the dusty trail, but he doesn’t seem to mind. “So what? Then we will fight for the belt.” Timo looks at the stoic look in his eyes and realizes something is indeed wrong. The man he had worked with was somehow not quite at ease with this as he let on. There was an uneasy silence between the both of them for a good distance before Timo finally broke the silence. “How about that Barry Bonds?” “Huh?” “You know, plays for the Giants, going to the world series. He always chokes in the big one though…” Mike stops and looks at Timo. Even though his friend had made a totally casual remark, somehow he knew it was some sort of a message Timo never intended to give out. Mike stopped just as they reached the dock and nodded silently. “Don’t worry man, I got a feeling The Giants will win this year.” “Yeah, maybe the guy is just due.” “Could be Timo…Hey, looks like we are at the dock, ready to go fishing man?” “Damn right, but there’s one thing…” “What’s that?” “I’ve never really BEEN fishing.” Mike sighs and shrugs. ‘Well you didn’t know much about being a heel either, but I taught you how to do that…pretty good anyhow. This should be a walk in the park, come on, let’s hit the lake!” With that, the two former enemies, now turned friends, head for the boat. What adventures lie in store for them?
