Ego Trip

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/8/02 ~The story begins in a place that is not as familiar as the Davidson Estate which you had no doubt become accustomed to seeing over the past few weeks. Even an old man who has nothing to do but write his life story and tales of fantasy and adventure, must travel out at certain times for some of the more casual things in life. In this case it’s nothing more than a small leisurely walk in a nearby park which captures the attention of the man for the majority of the afternoon. The storms which had embroiled the east coast for the past two weeks have finally cleared off and the end result was a much welcomed day with hardly a cloud in the sky and the sun beaming down on all the people below it as they went about their business without a care in the world. The one person we are searching for though has walked himself tired in only a matter of one hour being out. His age worn body was no longer used to the walking and working that his past life had entailed and nearly twenty minutes into his walk he could be seen breathing heavily and gasping for air at times. Now he finds himself seated comfortably on a park bench walking as others went about their ways. With Little kids playing in the grass across from him and the parents or older siblings watching them from the distance to make sure that no foul play was afoot, the man sits and watches it all with no sign of amusement or remembrance showing on his pale grim face. He pulls from his bag at his side a few pieces of bread and with the birds already surrounding his bench he begins breaking it into smaller pieces and tossing it out to some of them as he watches them fight over the pieces. He digs back into the bag a second time and after a bit of rummaging and shuffling things around he pulls out his book. Hardly going anywhere without it, he takes the large brown covered book and places it on his lap in front of him before tossing another couple of pieces of bread out to the birds. He grabs a pen from his inner jacket pocket and pulls it out before yanking the cap off and making a quick little brush along the inside cover of the book to make sure it had ink. Satisfied with the result he begins flipping through the many pages of the book which had been written on already and looking over some of the pages as if to get his bearings. Finally he comes to a blank sheet of paper just on the opposite side of where he had last finished with his thoughts and memories of his previous life. He looked the last page over quickly as he did the others before it and then turning to the next one he prepared to write.~ ~The Davidson Chronicles Chapter Thirteen: Dead or Alive 9/8/2052 By Tyler Davidson~ Another show has passed and perhaps to the shock of everyone I was not seen in any form on that night. Keeping a low profile this week was the best idea I could have had to avoid any more encounters with that idiotic Magnum Wolf. How that guy could ever be allowed out in public unsupervised is beyond me. He is without any shadow of a doubt a complete and total nutcase that must be avoided at all costs. Sadly my luck never seemed to hold when I arrived at one the shows and he would be the first one I would run into, probably more often than not going on one of his supposed investigative cases. Of course with all of that said it is him I find myself now paired with once again on the coming Glory. While other stars in FSW get teamed with men like Angelo Deville, Kimbusa, and Timo Bolamba, I get paired with the raving idiotic lunatic Magnum Wolf. This is the very same man who has abducted me with a dildo, and then later tried impersonating Angelo Deville to try and get “information” of some sorts out of me. Personally I would say he has just taken one chair shot to the head to many since arriving here. Hell I would rather get teamed with Tim “My Balls Are Smaller Than My Dogs” Walden than this raving maniac. But no I have to put to suffer even more than I already have in this place because of that old fart Orion more than likely. I’m sure Chip wouldn’t have done this himself as me and him have an understanding that I’ll do my best to prove myself a contender here. As though this wasn’t enough the team we are facing off against are apparently a couple of old dead guys. Richard Nixon and John F. Kennedy the newest tag team to grace the FSW rings is our opposition. Apparently Orion wasn’t happy being the oldest guy around anymore so he went and dug up two corpses before dragging them to the arena one night and maybe having that Zameer guy do some voodoo dance or something like that to bring them back to life. Who knows really? All that I know is despite how much trouble they might have caused so far for the tag champs Ming and Krieg, or Bad Frotitude that won’t be finding the same amount of success when they step into the ring against me. They will be standing in my path to greatness here and I’m going to make sure I mow right over them. I won’t be the one pulling a Chris Bagwell this time around. You won’t see me lying flat on my back in the ring being pinned by some big monster. I’m the same man who outlasted Bagwell in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and I’ll make damn sure I do it again in this Dead or Alive tournament. I’ll use this as my chance to once again prove my superiority over men like Bagwell and Jupiter who think just because of their size and their supposed legacy in this business that it makes them better than me. I’m the one who had all the talent, and I’m the one who has what it takes to make it to the top. The people may not believe or they may not like it but it was always the truth. Dead or Alive would be nothing more to me than the first step in a long journey which I had planned since day one. Even a man like Chris Bagwell or Paul Orion would not be able to hold me back any longer. It will be a time for a new breed of wrestler to step up to the top and I’m the only one who could achieve that. As for the Dead Presidents, they had better wish they remained dead. The only presidents that have ever mattered to be are the ones lining the inside of my wallet and my bank account. Conveniently enough neither Nixon nor Kennedy is doing either of those. They like the many before them will find themselves put to rest courtesy of the Five Star Superstar! ~We again change back to a shot of the old man as he finally places the pen down on the book in front of him. He picks up the last few pieces of bread that are next to him on the bench and toss them again out to the birds that quickly move in trying to grab some for their dinner. He looks over the book slowly reading the last couple of pages he had written before closing it up quietly and slipping it back into his bag. He looks out and around the park in front of him which had become less populated as the sun had already begun to set for the evening. He gathered his belongings and rising to his feet he began the long walk back home.~
