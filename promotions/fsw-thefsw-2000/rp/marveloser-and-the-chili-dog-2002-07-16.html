MarveLOSER and The Chili Dog

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-16-02 “Hmm…Maybe I have been taking this a tad bit too leniently. What do you think?” Timo Bolamba, International Man of Two-Fisted Cool looks down at his Chili Dog and smiles. So he had to face Mike Marvelous and some schmuck they probably dredged up from the FSW olden days. He also had to work with Johnny Raindance, a guy with a chip on his shoulder bigger than the state of Oklahoma. Yessir, this was going to take some fancy plans and pants to match. And well, with a name like Timo Bolamba, you had such fancy pants. “Yeah, I know I’m talking to a chili dog, and you know what, I know exactly why too.” The chili dog does not respond. “Well, I suppose that might work, but I still refuse to dress up in drag.” He tapped his fingers anxiously as he sat in the restaurant. Timo looked as if he had something to say, but just couldn’t quite get it out. His brain was often riddled with Swiss cheese style holes, and most everyone knew it, but this time it was something different. Something was really bugging him. “You have about the same intelligence level as Mikey, you know that my chili laden friend? I remember him back in OSW, damn fool ate up our company dental plan. Do you remember when he was a jobber in PCW? I cant quite recall, but I think he had the TV Belt.” Still no response from the chili dog. “I remember when I had that belt, yeah I was all sure of myself, cock of the walk and such. Too bad what happened to it…wait…whatever did happen to that belt?” Timo stares off into space, and one can only imagine what runs through his head as his eyes dance gleefully with memory. Visions of sugar plums dancing in his head are squashed by the vivid Technicolor productions his imagination often thinks up, and this was no different case. “Damned Garden Gnomes.” With that he looks back down to the dog again and examines the food sitting I front of him again. A small bead of sweat graces his brow as he thinks intently on the upcoming night, and how badly he wants to pummel Mikey. “Look Mikey, He likes it, he likes it! Yeah, he sure wont like it when my foot meets his ass!” A bit of the brown spicy topping falls off the hot dog and Timo squints, looking at it in a strange fashion. He leans down real close and wipes it up with his finger, examining the bean and meat concoction. “And where do you think YOU are going? Running like those Ass Bandits Big Davey Jones and that Evil guy…wait, wasn’t he in OSW too? Hmm, I don’t quite remember, I was too busy headlining to notice.” He nods his head briefly and eats the spilled filling, smacking his lips as he does. “Jeremy Howard, Snow, Meanstreak, Akiyama, Ivan, Rent-A-Hero, Boda, Toomes, hell I have faced them all, and walked away. My friend, my so called friend whom is slathered with onions, chili and made of pig entrails , has Mikey ever faced Meanstreak in a steel cage?” The Chili Dog drips a bit as Timo slams his fist upon the table. “Your damned right, I bet he has never been thrown through the side of a cage by Streak, or thrown across the triple tier ring at Alliances by Ivan Stanislav.” Timo gets a glazed over look in his eyes and he starts to smile. He had found his fire, and it was burning in all its Samoan fury now. “Have they ever taken Jeremy Howard to his physical limit? Have they registered three wins against Russia’s finest hero? Did any of them EVER hold the Rising Star Title TWICE?” His wicked smile grows as he remembers everything now, his professional career beings to wake as The Silencer reveals his storied history. To Timo, this was no longer about winning, it was about proving his point…it was about honor. “Have they threw Akiyama through a barb wire fence? Have they ever done anything to live up to what I have done……..wait.” Big Daveylicious. Yes it had all become very clear now, and Timo had all but forgotten how he lost the Rising Star Title. “Oh that rat bastard. I never thought I would have the chance to pay him back, but lucky me, looks like we are working in the same circles again. Poor Poor Davey.” Timo reached down and grabbed his meal, the conversational partner that was indeed no more than a mere Chili Dog topped with onions, and he held it aloft. “No, I had almost forgotten your cowardly attack. But since you run with the boys now, I guess that gives me a reason to kick all your asses.” Yep, this was turning out to be a fine day after all. Timo still had one problem though, that nagging thought in the back of his head that he just couldn’t get to. He pulled the Chili Dog closer to his mouth and prepared to make like Roseanne Barr at an all you can eat buffet. “Wait! I got it! MarveLIMPDICK!” He frowns. “MarveLETHARGIST” Hmmm. “MarveLADY” Why couldn’t it just work right? His mind would not click and as he chowed down on his meal finally it dawned on him. He had been overanalyzing the whole situation. Why overglorify an over pushed chump that had trouble tying his wrestling boots when he was in the same building as Timo? That just wasn’t the right way to go about it. He still knew there was an issue to work out with Johnny Raindance, but hell, the guy was smart, how could he not know Timo was innocent? The Wild Samoan stood up and took off his Chili Hut “I Conquered One Dozen Chili-Dog’s” bib and began to walk for the door. As he hit the door an epiphany hit him like a brick to the head. The Samoan triumphantly stuck his finger in the air as he declared: “MARVELOSER!”
