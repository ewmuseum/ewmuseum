Stepping Forward

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 2 The paper floats in the powerful wind and is brought to a young man's feet. The paper brings a familiar memory back to your mind. Flashback. The flashback opens with a past event occuring to one, Chris Bagwell. Bagwell parked in his rent-a-car, in an empty parking lot. His head is down, on the wheel, as if he were hurt. Suddenly, he shows life. He raises his head from the wheel. Chris mumbles to himself, 'Defeating Brooze. Defeating Chambers. Defeating Deville and Davidson. None of it means anything. None of it is worth it...without one to accomplish it for.' 'But she's left me...' '...and my prayer...is to her.' He tosses a piece oif paper onto the dashboard and slams his head back on the wheel, going into a deep sleep. The camera gets a closeup on the paper, as it reads... juss wen i th0ught it waz tru baby i learned i cant trust u i tried to keep u happy n neva sad i tried givin u all the luv i eva had but u left me here, a fool in luv n hurt my heart wit more than a sh0ve it was known n it was sw0rn n n0w mah heart was finally t0rn fr0m mah heart n fr0m mah s0ul tha luv i had sh0ulda neva went 0ld but n0w itz d0ne n n0w itz g0ne neva again will i make an0tha b0nd ...away in the distance... ...l0st in hurt... ...mah s0ul will rise... ...but b0dy remain in dirt... g0ne The flashback comes to an abrupt end. The boy gently unfolds the paper in his hands, being careful that it doesn't tear. 'The words spoken...', utters a man. '...so sad...and depressing.' 'A man set through anger...and broken love.' Tommy reads through the entire emotional prayer. A sense of sadness compells him, and overruns his body. Tommy closes his eyes for a moment and pictures the pain...and feels the agony...the writer must have gone through. Tommy continues, 'The words spoken reflect on a man I once knew.' 'They relfect on a soul I once attempted to nurture and keep calm.' 'These words return ancient memories of searching for lost love...and conquesting the fear and obsticles that stood in our path.' He folds the paper carefully, and then proceeds to place it into his pocket. Time passes as the picture relocates to a small diner in the center of town. Tommy enters, just looking for a cup of coffee to quench his thirst. He takes a seat at the bar when a familiar voice calls his name. 'Tommy. How you doing, man?' Tommy turns, recognizing the voice, but not being able to put it to a face. 'Rob?' asks Tommy, 'Wow man, how've you been? I heard about the baby, congrats!' 'Yeah...thanks. Could be better though,' Rob replied. In concern, Tommy questions Rob of his dismay, 'Why? Whats wrong, man?' Seemingly surprised that Tommy hadn't already heard, Rob explains, 'Chris went on a rampage. He ran out of town and I don't know where the hell he's been since.' Tommy has no major reaction. He stares at Rob, questioningly, 'Hmm...really?' The new evidence of his disappearence struck Tommy's mind. He stalls for a moment, then reaches into his pocket for the paper. Feeling as though he might be on to something, Tommy brings up the paper, 'Would you wanna take a look at something for me?' 'Yeah, sure thing,' Rob agrees. Tommy pulls the paper from his pocket and places it on the counter top. 'I found this on the street,' Tommy clarifies, 'The chance of it being Chris' is slim to none. But hell, its worth a shot.' Rob takes the paper. Seeing the fragile state it appears to be in, he takes caution unfolding it, as did Tommy. Rob begins to read through the writing. He begins to remember a past saga over Rebecca. Softly, Rob speaks, 'I do think its Chris. I remember well...' '...Rebecca...' 'Chris was upset. Rebecca was gone. She went missing. But Chris wouldn't take the time to listen to the whole situation. When Georgia got the news to him...' '...he ran off.' 'Well what really happened?' asks Tommy, concerned. Expecting his question, Rob immediately replies, 'Well, Rebecca left to make it all up to Chris. To make things right again.' 'How is that?' 'I don't really know how,' Rob mumbles. 'She never really mentioned it to anyone. She only spoke it between her heart and her soul. Nothing else was informed. Nothing...' '...or nobody.' 'So do you think he's looking for her?' Tommy tries pinpointing where, exactly, Chris may be. 'Do you think he's trying to find her, again?' A slight pause in Rob's response. He lowers his head as if in shame, 'For once...' '...no' 'No?!?' Tommy exclaims, 'Why not?' Rob glances back at the note and tries to find a reasonable explaination behind it. Rob tries to justify his opinion, 'All the note tells me...is that he's broken hearted. Something must have occured between the two that caused his emotions to revert to sadness.' Tommy stands, before even recieving his coffee. 'Rob, I have to find him. He isn't stable. God only knows what he'll do to himself.' Rob looks up at Tommy and smiles. His smile, though, is as if he were uttering the word 'duh' to Tommy. 'You're wasting your time.' 'What do you mean?' Tommy asks. Rob replies sharply, 'Its useless.' To be continued. . . .
