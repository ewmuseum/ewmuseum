The Supreme Court is now in session!

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/2 They’ve been silent for quite some time. Outside of the FSW ring, little has been heard from Politically Incorrect since their shocking return.. But lots has been heard from their enemies. Why? Why go to FSW? Why then completely bad mouth FSW if you wanted in it in the first place? Why try and humiliate the entire company? Why try and destroy everything the letters FSW have ever meant? Why create their own demented Supreme Court to do all of this? Why? You’re about to find out.. *Sitting inside the large Council Chamber in William S. Powell’s Des Moines mansion are Politically Incorrect, the Five Star Wrestling Tag Team Champions. Powell wears the title backwards, the gold pressing into his Armani-clad posterior, as Lovecraft uses his as a coaster for his glass of cognac.* Powell: “Lovecraft, I must say, you certainly do know how treat a worthless piece of metal.” Lovecraft: “Indeed, William, but remember, they brought this upon themselves.” *Both men smile..a smile devoid of happiness, a wicked smile..* Powell: “Indeed they did! Chip O’Meara and Paul Orion are responsible for this! When you disrespect the greatest tag team to ever step in a ring, you tend to pay somewhere down the line. THAT, ultimately, is why we are still here. Don’t get me wrong, if we were to get an offer, we’d JUMP at the first train out of this hellhole, but as long as we are here, we’ve got us some business to take care of.” Lovecraft: “Tyler Nelson…the Greediest Player in the Game, and the Six Star Superstar..and quite possibly the biggest idiot I’ve ever met. You see, Tyler had everything in PCW…he had money, power, fame, and the greatest competitors ever to back him up. Hell, Tyler, Xavier Kannon, Tim Walden and ourselves OWNED PCW! Literally! But then…Tyler had to ally himself with The Phantom Booker. And he fired EVERYONE…INCLUDING THE ULTIMATE AUTHORITY!” Powell: “It’s amazing to me how you couldn’t see Kannon’s betrayal coming. You turned your backs on us, Tyler, and you paid for it with your PCW career. At least, unlike Kannon, we had the balls to stab you in the front!” Lovecraft: “But, enough about Tyler Nelson for now…we face some old friends on Glory this week. Bad Frotitude..the men we beat for these very titles, and men I’m sure who value them much more highly than we do.” Powell: “It seems they have quite a burden on them. For, fail to win them, and we could be off at any moment…to stand mid-ring in one of the world’s greatest arenas for another company and give these titles EXACTLY the tribute that they deserve!” Lovecraft: “Yes…gentlemen, FSW is counting on you. The fans are counting on you. But, none of that is enough. You see, the Supreme Court is now in session. And, we give the victory in this contest to Politically Incorrect…” Powell: “IN A LANDSLIDE! See you on Glory…and remember…everyone’s watching! Don’t screw up..or these belts may never be seen again..” Fade to black.
