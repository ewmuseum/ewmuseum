Nothing is Forever

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'What did I tell you? Jason O'Neil is one tough son of a bitch.' Steven Caldera's overbearing voice booms over Kimbusa's speakerphone, resonating throughout the entire hotel room. The room is in a state of disarray... The bed has been overturned and slung into the corner; the end table and lamp still remain undisturbed. Kimbusa himself has made the room into a makeshift gym, an oriental rug thrown out over the worn carpet for the sake of comfort during meditation. 'Are you there?' Caldera receives nothing but silence from Kimbusa for a few moments, before finally receiving his answer. 'I'm a bit busy.' Kimbusa is busy indeed. In fact, he is upside-down; using the wall for support, the five hundred pound mammoth is balancing on his hands, supporting his incredible weight with his arms alone. His concentration has been interrupted, and his arms begin to wobble. The giant closes his eyes and inhales deeply; his balance is regained. 'You're making your Five Star Wrestling in-ring debut next week. I think it's time we started to talk about our strategy. I'll be at ringside calling the show, which I guess proves that the people running this company aren't total morons... You with me so far?' Kimbusa sighs, his attempt at passive excersize becoming more and more difficult. 'Yes.' 'Good. Now, Jason O'Neil may have gone to the hospital, but he's checking himself out tomorrow night. He'll be there. Remember what I told you; don't underestimate anybody. You thought O'Neil was finished after Glory, didn't you? Well, it doesn't matter. This is what I wanted anyway. O'Neil is going to have to either join us, or get sent packing. Understand?' Kimbusa finally gives up on his inverted meditation, and flips back down to his feet. He lands with a thunderous thud, but springs back to his feet with uncanny agility. Caldera pauses at the sound of the thump, but continues under the assumption that he has Kimbusa's attention. 'Now like I said... I'll be at ringside, but I'll be at the announce table, not in your corner.' Kimbusa interrupts quickly. 'I do not need anyone in my corner.' Caldera scoffs a bit. 'Relax, big man. I know that. Point is, I don't want you to overdo it just yet. We're not murdering anyone on national television, right?' Kimbusa seems almost let down by this, as he turns and heads towards the empty center of the room. 'As you wish.' 'Right. O'Neil is one tough customer, I've worked with him before. But you can take this man down. I want you to show him why he should join us. But more importantly, I want you to show the world who you are. This is your chance, Kimbusa, to prove to the powers-that be that we have what it takes to run this company some day. There's no such thing as a skyrocket to success, Kimbusa. But if it's pain and suffering you wish to inflict, and if it's satisfaction you want, it will only be found by demolishing the best this company has to offer. You got me?' Kimbusa nods, taking this advice to heart. 'This makes sense. Hurting O'Neil was... enjoyable. But the people... they did not seem to care.' 'That's right. We need to hunt the big fish. O'Neil could be a big fish if he wanted to, but he's just not too bright. But you stick with me, my friend, and together we'll take a ride to the top.' Kimbusa's eyes begin to flash his trademark red, glowing with anxious fury. 'Some day...'
