Trip to the "Head" Doctor pt. 1

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/24/02 The Scene arises from the fiery pits of hell to 'ShowTime' Erik Blake lying on a leather chair staring up at the ceiling in a room. Suddenly a stunningly good-looking woman comes walking in with a clipboard and sits behind ShowTime. 'I wonder if Hotel Erotica comes on tonight? I haven't watched that in a while...' ShowTime says as the lady starts to giggle slightly as ShowTime notices the lady in the room and sits up. 'Well, Mr. Blake, after the X-Rays we took of your head there does seem to be some mild brain damage there.' the lady says as she holds up the X-rays into the light. 'Give it to me straight Doc...am I gonna live?' ShowTime says in a dramatic voice. 'Uh, yes. Yes you are. The X-rays just shows the constant flashbacks you've been having.' The doctor says. 'Flashbacks? But half the stuff has never happened. What is up with that?' ShowTime asks. 'Well, maybe it is just what you were thinking about doing when you were a child. ' the Doctor says. 'So what your saying is...when I was a child I wanted to jump off the Sears Tower and talk to myself as I fall?' ShowTime says. 'Well, yes in laymen's terms.' the doctor says. 'So you mean is that I'm a man in my 20s who had a childhood so bad that i tried to kill myself with a 9mm?' ShowTime says. 'Yes..' the doctor says in a frustrated kind of voice. 'So what your really trying to say is....' ShowTime says in a taunting way as the doctor's face turns red with anger. ShowTime gets up and sits in front of the desk and starts fiddlin' with the pencils. 'So Doc, what can I do to stop it? It's starting to get annoying...' ShowTime says. 'Well, Mr. Blake, it's actually non-fixable.' The doctor says. 'WHAAAAAAAAAAAT?!' ShowTime says as he knocks over the pencils in his puny rage. 'I'm sorry but humanity hasn't figured out a cure for this particular illness.' the doctor says. 'Oh, well humanity is nothing but a bunch of raped donkies!' ShowTime says as he starts to pick up pencils and bend them in two. 'Mr. Blake! That is totally unescessary!' the doctor says. Then ShowTime picks up the computer on the desk and carefully places each and every piece on the ground. Then he sticks his tongue out at the doctor as he moves towards the window. He pulls down his pants and sticks his but on the glass. 'OH SAY CAN YOU SEEEEEEEEEEEE MY BIG LARGE HUGE ASS THAT'S MOONING THE CITY OF CHICAGO!!!!' ShowTime starts to sing in the melody of the National Anthem. 'MR. BLAKE! THIS IS OUTRAGEOUS! For every second you do this more I'm charging extra...' The doctor says as ShowTime suddenly pulls up his pants and gets in the face of the doctor. Then he licks her lips and runs out as the Scene fades...
