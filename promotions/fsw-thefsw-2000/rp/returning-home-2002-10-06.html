Returning Home

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No... please... no. How had this happened? How could he be so weak? How could he be so stupid? Doubts had been flooding through Kimbusa's head all morning. He couldn't shake Epiphany's words of the previous morning... 'If you lose, Caldera will never let us be together...' And he had lost. He hadn't listened to that son of a bitch Caldera. He let love overcome hate, and it weakened him. He let visions of demons fade away into visions of beauty, and it gave his opponent the chance he needed to defeat the big man. Kimbusa simply hadn't cared about the match. He tried to motivate himself, he tried to take Epiphany's warnings to heart. But only now, after reality had time to sink in, Kimbusa has realized that Steven Caldera very well might make good on his promise to make his life a living hell. Caldera had truly been hell bent on leading Kimbusa to victory. Caldera had needed the glory to further his career; but now he finds himself quickly fading into the obscurity below the shallow memories of the fans of professional wrestling. Caldera would surely want to take his anger out on someone... And Kimbusa seemed to be a handy target. Kimbusa hadn't spoken to Caldera yet, but Caldera had sent his men to collect him immediately after hearing of his failure. At first, Kimbusa was hopeful... After all, he had come from the shadowy reaches of some isolated mountainside to the highest echelons of the biggest tournament of all time, and had come incredibly close to victory. Was this not a tremendous accomplishment? But the more he considered Caldera's personality, the more he realized that he was in a significant amount of trouble. He had been too frightened to phone Epiphany, the daughter of his late mentor who passes away only weeks ago. It had been an emotionally turnbulant tournament for the big man; he had lost his long-time mentor and the only father he ever knew. He had come to blows with the only man keeping him free of prison. And now he had seeminly lost the only source of brightness in his otherwise joyless life. Kimbusa had been experiencing some incredible new feelings lately. Joy. Happiness. Perhaps even love. They had been so powerful and so potent that all his anger and frustration had faded away within days of meeting the source of his metamorphosis. The girl Epiphany had changed his life. He had even come to enjoy the sport of wrestling itself, and was looking forward to competing in Five Star Wrestling more often. He had engaged in a chaotic battle with Azala Zameer recently, and had enjoyed the FSW competition. But despite these lights at the end of his tunnel, one thing kept holding him back... Steven Caldera. Caldera had gone out of his way to cause Kimbusa pain, to ensure that the demons weren't driven away fully. Kimbusa had finally snapped and nearly assaulted his benefactor in his own office, leading to what Kimbusa assumed to be potential legal trouble. And so it was with a feeling of great trepidation that Kimbusa met with Caldera in his New York high-rise apartment. It was dark by the time Kimbusa arrived. He hadn't slept since his fateful match that saw him removed from the tournament; but in reality, Kimbusa couldn't have slept had he tried. All he could think of was Epiphany, and when he might see her again. Caldera had the room lit dimly, with his golden chandeliers lowered to the point that his furnishings became mere sillhoetes hiding in the shadows. Upon entering at the behest of one of Caldera's aides, Kimbusa finds Caldera sitting with his arms and legs crossed, perched in his leather chair, quietly examining the shadows. Kimbusa had never been afriad of Caldera, despite his excellent shape and renowed skill; but at this moment, he had to admit to a certain level of anxiety. Caldera, for his part, remains silent, maintaining his focused stare upon the fireplace across the room. Kimbusa enters the room only to have the door slammed behind him, plunging the two men into a deep darkness. Kimbusa once found this darkness comforting, but now he wished desperately to raise the lights. 'Why did you fail me,' Caldera finally whispers slowly, his voice cracking beneath the power of his anger. Kimbusa considers this for a few tense moments, before Caldera's barely visible eyes flash upward and meet Kimbusa's. The two share a heated glance for a moment, before the tension brings Kimbusa's voice to the surface. 'I fought hard for you. I nearly won your tournament. What have I done wrong?' he inquires with a legitimate sense of confusion. 'We had an agreement. You agreed to win this tournament for me in exchange for your freedom. But you did not win. The semi-finals aren't going to cut it, Kimbusa. I needed you to win,' Caldera snaps back viciously, with a flair of hatred so bitter than it actually makes Kimbusa wince. 'I did my best,' Kimbusa says with a weak voice, actually unsure of how to respond. 'No, you didn't,' Caldera spits out, charging into Kimbusa with the most heinous voice he can find. 'You're a five hundred pound monster. Your best would have obliterated every man you met. You weren't trying, Kimbusa.' Kimbusa seems confused by this, and even a bit concerned about Caldera's direction. 'What are you implying?' he asks suspiciously. Caldera sits forward in his chair, allowing a single stream of light flowing in through the window to cross his face, illuminating his dark, piercing eyes. 'You broke our agreement, Kimbusa. You breached the contract. And that means that I am no longer able to maintain the litigation that has kept you free all these months.' Almost on cue, the door opens again. Four large men in deep blue uniforms enter the dark room, their shirts embroidered with large 'NYPD' letters. Kimbusa does not turn to face them; he already knows what to expect. All he can manage is a face of utter disdain and hatred for Caldera, who remains cold and uncaring despite the life he is about to ruin. 'These men will take you to your new home,' he says bitterly. One of the officers reaches out for Kimbusa's arm, but in a flash Kimbusa has the man under the shoulder, and launches him into the room with a high judo toss. One officer quickly draws his nightstick, but is met with a lightening quick foot to the jaw, sending him sprawling back into the hallway. The other two immediately respond by taking hold of Kimbusa's arms, while the first recovers and draws a nightstick of his own. The first officer moves back towards Kimbusa and swings with his stick, but Kimbusa motions one of the officers in the path of the blow, and the man is sent to the floor in a heap. The first takes yet another swing, but it is quickly dodged by Kimbusa, who buries his fist in the officer's trachea, sending him into a panicked search for breath. Kimbusa then easily dispaches of the remaining officer with a forceful, headfirst shove to the wall, ending the destruction as quickly as it began. Kimbusa, recovering his breath, turns to angle his agression at Caldera. But upon turning he is greeted by a hot stream of liquid that instantly ignites his face. With a bloodcurtling scream, Kimbusa drops to the ground, clawing frantically at his eyes to relieve the pain. Caldera drops the first officer's mace and kneels beneath the moaning sumo. With the same cold, evil sneer, Caldera reaches out with his collared hand and delivers a simple smack to Kimbusa's face, which Kimbusa meets with a blind fist to the air. Caldera chuckles at Kimbusa's helpless state, and begins bearing down on him with his terrible voice once more. 'You will never be free again, Kimbusa. You belong to me now.'
