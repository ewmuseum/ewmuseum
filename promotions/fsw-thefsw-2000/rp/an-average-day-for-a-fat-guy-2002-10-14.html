An average day for a fat guy

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/10/02 (The camera opens up. You are in a cinema watching a film. There is a big person in front of you and it’s hard to watch the film with the person there. You move your head to the side of the person, so you can see the film. You now start to hear a weird noise. It sounds like someone eating something very loudly. You realize it’s the person in front of you. You stand up and tap the person on the shoulder and the person turns around. It’s The Overweighter. He’s eating an apple. The Overweighter quickly hides the apple.) The Overweighter: What do you want? You: You’re The Overweighter right? The Overweighter: Yeah, what can I do you for? You: You were just eating an apple? The Overweighter: Shhh someone might hear you! I’m trying to lose some weight for FSW! You: You’re in FSW now? The Overweighter: Yeah, can I watch the film now? You: I Thought it was a rumor, but I guess it’s true. The Overweighter: Yeah, okay. Can I now watch the film? Person in front of The Overweighter: Can you two shut up? Hey it’s The Overweighter! The Overweighter: SHH! (The Overweighter slides down in his chair to hope nobody else will see him. The person in front of The Overweighter turns back around. You sit back down. A few minutes later you hear a weird noise again, you tap The Overweighter on the shoulder again and he turns around.) You: Can you eat a bit quieter? The Overweighter: Only if you go and buy me some sweets! You: Okay, deal. (You get up, walk out of the room and up to the counter, where they sell sweets. You get some sweets, walk back towards the door to the film you’re watching and walk in. You see everyone around walking out of the film. You walk up to one of them.) You: Pardon me, but has the film finished already? Person: No, but some guy farted and it’s really bad! (You look around. There is nobody else in the room but you and The Overweighter. You walk towards him. You’re starting to smell something really bad and you feel like you’re going to throw up. You can’t take anything else so you throw the sweets at The Overweighter, run out of the room and The Overweighter is left to watch the film on his own. The Overweighter sits up, looks around and sees the room empty. He sniffs his armpits and sits back down to watch the film. The film finishes. The Overweighter stands up and walks out of the room. There are a load of people in gas suits, with gas masks and loads of air fresheners in their hands.) Man in a gas suit: The film’s finished and the source of the smell is gone, so it’s safe to enter. Squad 1! Go! (A group of people in gas suits run into the room with the air fresheners spraying everywhere. The Overweighter is outside. There are lots of ambulances everywhere and they are carrying people that are unconscious.) Doctor: Wow! I don’t want to know what that guy ate last night! Quick lets get out of here! It’s the smell creator! (The Overweighter looks around. Everyone is running like a nuclear war is just about to start. The Overweighter gets on his motorcycle and rides down the road. There is an endless amount of sheep walking down the road. They are taking up the whole road. The Overweighter stops and the sheep get closer. They are now walking past The Overweighter. One of the sheep stops and stands up on his hind legs frowning at The Overweighter.) Sheep: Hey, you’re the guy that ate my mother! The Overweighter: … Sheep can’t talk! Sheep: What do you think I’m doing you fat son of a pregnant dog? The Overweighter: Ha, ha, ha, ha! Sheep: What’s so funny? The Overweighter: Nothing. Sheep: Well it won’t be very funny when you have 100 of us on your back! The Overweighter: Really… I’m in a mood for MINT SAUCE! Sheep: AH! (All the sheep start to run as fast as they can down the road. The Overweighter is confused and asks himself why they were walking down the road in the first place. The Overweighter rides past a Welsh farm. He looks over the fence whilst going past and in the distance there is a man. It looks like he’s urinating in a white bush, but then The Overweighter sees that the bush isn’t a bush, but a sheep. The man that’s taking a piss isn’t taking a piss his really <ROLE-PLAY EDIT> the sheep. The Overweighter is now scared. He is running at his top speed to get away from a Welsh Sheep <ROLE-PLAY EDIT>. The Overweighter stops off at BiGG Mouths Burger Bar. There is a special offer on. Buy 3 Superpounder Deluxe and get a free Heaven Is A BiGG Mouth’s by The Overweighter on CD. The music is playing in the background. The Overweighter goes up to the counter and orders 20 Superpounders.) The Overweighter: This song is rubbish, who is it? Spotty Teenager: It’s you. The Overweighter: Oh! I knew that! I was just testing you! (The Overweighter smiles, walks over to a seat and sits down with his 20 Superpounder deluxe. The Overweighter goes to take his first bite when his mobile phone rings. He answers it.) The Overweighter: Hello, who is this? Paul Orion: Hi, I want you to consider schooling again. The Overweighter: Schooling? Again? I never went to school! Paul Orion: You what? The Overweighter: Mr. Orion, why do you want me to go school? Paul Orion: Well, you’re not the brightest person around are you? And I don’t want you to feel left out as the only uneducated person in FSW. The Overweighter: Okay thanks. Paul Orion: I will send you the details in a letter, okay? The Overweighter: Yeah, thank you once again Mr. Orion. Bye. Paul Orion: Bye… What a stupid fat fool… The Overweighter: I’m still here you know. Paul Orion: (silence) (The phone goes dead. The Overweighter shrugs his shoulders and eats his Superpounders. He gets up, walks out of BiGG Mouth’s Burger Bar and gets on his motorcycle. He rides down the road. He was heading home for the rest of the day when he sees a pig flying past in the sky.) Flying Pig: Hi, nice day today, isn’t it? The Overweighter: First sheep and now pigs? Flying Pig: What? Haven’t you ever seen a flying, talking pig? The Overweighter: Yeah of course I see them all the time! (The Overweighter is getting very scared. The pig sees The Overweighter is scared, so he flies down low towards The Overweighter and pokes him in the eyes. The Overweighter stops his motorcycle straight away. A car stops behind him and a woman sticks her head out of the window.) Woman: Are you okay? The Overweighter: No, I was poked in the eyes by a flying pig! Woman: Oh, male drivers looking for any excuses. (The woman sticks her head back into the car, drives past The Overweighter and sticks her middle finger up at him. The Overweighter thinks she is waving. He waves back and smiles. The Overweighter’s eyes are blurry. He can’t see anything so he gets off his motorbike and walks straight across a main road. A car serves to miss The Overweighter and smashes into another car. Another car smashes into the back of those cars and then another and another.) The Overweighter: My God! I know I can’t see at the moment. But I could drive more competently than they can! (The Overweighter walks into a hospital and thinks he can see a doctor.) The Overweighter: Doctor, Doctor I think I'm blind! Doctor: Yes you’re right, because this is a restaurant! The Overweighter: Okay, in that case I will have a burger with fries and a large coke! Waiter: Coming right up. (The Overweighter stands there not being able to see. Without him knowing, someone puts a ‘Piss on me’ sign on his back. A dog walks past, sees the sign on him and walks into the restaurant. The dog starts to urinate all over The Overweighter’s leg.) The Overweighter: Hmm, it seems to be getting a bit warm in here. My leg is boiling hot and sweating like mad! (The waiter walks back with The Overweighter’s food.) Waiter: Why are you talking to yourself? The Overweighter: Can’t a man talk to himself. What’s that smell? Did you piss yourself, or something? Waiter: No. That smell would be the dog pissing on your leg. The Overweighter: What kind of restaurant is this? You let dogs in here and let them piss on peoples’ legs? Waiter: It isn't my fault. It’s the sign on your back. The Overweighter: Right, who would put a ‘Piss on me’ sign on someone’s back? Where’s my food? Waiter: You ate it already. The Overweighter: Did I? Waiter: Yes. Now can you please leave my restaurant! Oh, and that will be $5 please. (The Overweighter puts his hand into his pocket and pulls out a $20 note and gives it to the waiter and walks out of the restaurant. The waiter sits down at a table with The Overweighter’s food. He starts to eat it.) Waiter: Stupid idiot. (The Overweighter walks into the middle of the road. There are policemen trying to clear up a huge crash, which had nothing to do with The Overweighter (That’s a lie). The Overweighter is walking around with his hands out, trying to feel his motorcycle. He feels something like a motorcycle and picks it up. He sits on it, rides down the road without being able to see he goes through a red light and doesn’t turn a corner. He rides straight through a Starbucks coffeehouse and out of the back with a cup of coffee in his hand. He’s drinking it with a couple of biscuits as well. There is a brick wall in front of The Overweighter. He is zooming straight for it and is just about to hit the wall when the wall jumps out of the way.) Wall: Ha! You think I'm going to let that thing hit me? (The Overweighter rides straight into a river instead. He hits his head onto a big rock and floats down the river, unconscious. He slowly sinks to the bottom. A group of people jump into the water, carry him out and The Overweighter is taken away in an ambulance. The doctor in the ambulance looks him.) Doctor: Wait a minute! It’s the guy that made that deadly gas in the cinema! (The Ambulance stops at The Overweighter’s house and throws him out of the back of the ambulance (when I say throw, I really mean slowly slide. He is heavy you know). The Overweighter hits the ground and slowly sits up seeing the ambulance driving off into the distance. The Overweighter falls back and hits the ground. He is steering into the sun, but doesn’t notice because he’s practically blind. His eyes slowly shut and the scene fades to black.)
