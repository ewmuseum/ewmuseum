Kelly's Steakhouse Part I

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today I arrived 5 minutes later than I said I would. Not really a big deal, but I'm usually right on time. I looked at the doors outside the restaurant, and had to laugh. My former tag team partner, turned Agent to the stars, Jim Kelly had just painted a huge likeness of himself on the side of the building of his new restaurant. I entered, and standing right up from was Jim Kelly, my agent. 'Jason, you're late.' he said with a slight smile. He turned and looked at me and smiled his goofy smile. He wore his usual attire of an Armani ripoff suit, and a purple tie. I swear this guy and purple were something else. everything he owned was purple. His car, his office, his house. Purple. Everywhere. Makes you kind of wonder. Had I not roomed with the guy on the road, I would have wondered about him. Rumors had it for years that Jim loved cock more than anything else. And I'm not talking about roosters. But, strange souds coming from our shared room that forces me to sleep on the couch on the phone with my late wife put a stop to those rumors, and soon, he had the ladies lined up. 'Well, if it wasn't for the shitty directions, I would have been here on time.' I fired back. Jim threw a punch that hit me in the arm. Man, for a little guy (Jim stands just 5'6') he had some power in his punch. He hugged me, and smiled at me, beaming that I was here to see Kippo's Steakhouse. 'Nice place though ya got goin.' 'Thanks, man. Come on. Let's go in the back, talk contract.' I followed him past the bar where I grabbed a tall cold one, and went into his office. Inside were some animal heads, a few trophies, plaques, and other assorted nostalgia related items. 'Take a seat,' he said. Back in the day, Jim was a hell of a wrestler, even if it wasn't for his strange propensity for ripping off people's moves. He even tried to chokeslam alot of the bigger guys. To make up for his short stature, Jim was rude, arrogant, and a hell of a heel. A knee injury forced him out of the game, and as a result he took to managing everyone else's money. The guy was good at it, and made me an incredible hunk of change, in just a short time. I sat in a new age leather chair, and felt like I was going to fall backwards. 'Those things are the rage in Sweden. Pretty soon, all those Swedes are going to be knocking on our doors, invading this country. And hey, if they can make quality furniture like that, then, I say, let em.' 'I gotta admit, there are some fine stitches in here.' 'Oh yeah. You just don't get crap from Sweden like you do from like Yugoslavia. Now there's a crap factory.' 'Yugoslavia's a crap factory?' 'Oh yeah. I mean, Hello? The Yugo. First of all, they couldn't even name the damn thing anything intelligent, and second, it was a peice of shit!' 'I take it you owned one?' 'Damn right, I did. Damn thing blew up on me. Anyways. Let's talk contract.' 'Let's do it.' 'I just got off the phone with that guy over at FSW. They're offering the same money, better downside, tremendous upside. Nothing too wordy. Looks to be a good deal.' 'Sounds fine with me.' 'Good. I figured you'd say that, so I told em to send over the girl with the contract. Now, your endorsements. Slim Jim LOVES you as the new Slim Jim guy. Next commercial they have the greatest idea that involves you...' I stopped listening and started thinking about things. Michelle always handled this for me, and to be honest, good ol' Jimmy pretty much took her place. I never really worried much about my offers, downside, upsides, left sides, right sides, whatever. It really just didn't matter to me. 'Jason?' I heard just barely. I looked over at Jim. He was smiling a giant Jim Kelly smile that was impossible not to say yes to. No wonder he got this far in buisness. 'Sure. Whatever you come up with is fine.' I answered. I could have agreed to getting raped by nuns, and I wouldn't have been the wiser. I think he knew this and added in an extra 5% for himself everytime. Procurement fees he called them. Highway robbery I called it. A buzzer sounded. It startled me a bit, as Jim put his finger on a button on the phone. 'Yes?', he answered. 'Mr. Kelly, a Ms. Levine is here to see you and Jason.' 'Send her in.' Jim said. 'Must be the girl from the office with the contract.' Jim stood and straightened his tie. I adjusted my leather jacket, and white turtleneck under it. Jim sprayed a few shots of binaca in his mouth, smacked his lips and breathed a hard breath out into his hand, smelling his breath. 'Perfect.' He said. 'Disgusting.' I fired back. The door opened before he could answer me and in walked a very attractive woman. Dressed in a grey buisness skirt and jacket with a white shirt underneath, she walked into the room and extended a hand in my direction. 'Mister O'Neil, I'm Jamie Garcia, from the FSW offices.' 'Pleasure to meet you, I answered.' 'Likewise.' I think I held onto her hand just a bit too long. She looked into my eyes, and pulled her hand away, as if I just bathed in toxic waste. I watched as she walked over to Jimmy, shook his hand and he invited her to sit at the conference table as they went over the terms. He lead her over and turned around as she stood in front of him. He winked and made lewd gestures behind her back. She turned and caught him. 'Mister Kelly. I'm here for one thing and one thing only. That is to sign your client, Jason O'Neil to a long term contract to FSW, who happen to pay my paychecks. I am NOT here for you to ogle, nor am I here for your lewd gestures. We are going to sit down and sign this contract without you staring at my breasts, my ass or anything else. Are we clear?' Jim was flabbergasted. 'W...w-hy s-s-s...certainly.' 'Good. Now, if we could get started, I have a plane to catch.' Twenty minutes later, I was a permanent member of FSW. We stood, and offered Jamie a drink, which she respectfully declined. Jim shrugged, and I showed Miss Garcia to the door, and walked her to her car. I made a little bit of small talk, but she didn't seem to be interested in converstation with me. She shook my hand, and said good bye before getting in her rental car, and without so much as a second look, she drove away. Jim walked out just a few seconds later, and put a hand on my shoulder. 'Dude, I think i just found the future ex-Mrs. Kelly. Whatcha think?' 'She's not your type, Jimmy. She's got a pulse.' 'Shut it. I think...I'm in love. You've gotta hook me up, bro. From the moment I saw her, i knew there was chemistry...' Funny. I felt the same way.
