'Shaved'

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-29-02 “You know what the problem is with your average run of the mill professional wrestler Jake?” “No, what?” “They just have no….panache.” “Panache?” “It’s kindof like having your own particular idiom.” “Right…and that is?” “Style.” “Now THAT I understand Timo.” Two men who had become familiar with one another, yet seemingly known one another for quite a long time sit in the local Subway shop, partaking in the splendor of the world greatest subs. Timo looks down at the meaty triple cold cut stack and smiles at the delectable portions that lay before him. He looked over to his counterpart, who had his mouth full of a dripping Southwestern style chicken sub. He had southern sauce dribbling down his cheek, and needless to say, the young Latino man looked out of place a bit. Two women walked by, one in a mini skirt, and a low cut shirt. It was obvious to anyone in the room what she was trying to peddle. Jake looked up and nodded, Timo shaking his head. The kid needed work. In AND out of the ring, apparently. “See that’s what I’m talking about kid, you cant just smile at any old dame that walks in your path.” “I can’t?” “Naw, you have to pick one out, hunt it, track it, snag it, THEN bag it. There really are no steps you can miss here.” Jake looked over at him with a strange contortion of his face, and pointed at the two girls. The one next to the hot girl was slightly less attractive, and dressed far more conservatively in a pair of blue jeans and a turtleneck. “You mean I should compromise my standards?” Timo shook his head. “Compromise nothing kid, you just got to know how to play your cards.” Needless to say, Jake still looked confused. “Okay, say you go talk to the good looking one right?” “Right.” “Well you have automatically eliminated yourself from getting the other one then.” “But….that doesn’t sound right.” “Trust me. See, that girl is going to say to herself “That guy must be really shallow”. Well either that or she will think you don’t like her.” Jake stops to ponder this turn of events for a moment. He seems to have formed a thought but it escapes his mind quickly. Timo places a hand on his shoulder and nods. “Now look at her right calf.” The jeans are jacked up just enough to show some skin, and there lies a small tattoo of a barely decent girl. Timo smiles as Jake gets a look of serious desire on his face. The young man smiles to his new found mentor and points. “I know Jake, I know.” As the seemingly dull girl begins to talk to the man behind the counter, it is obvious she has a tongue stud of some sort. Timo chuckles and watches as his young compatriot seems to be enjoying the view. He taps Jake on the shoulder, as if to shove him forward. “Okay, now when they turn and come back, say something.” “No way man, I cant.” “What do you mean you cant? You were ready to a minute ago.” “DUDE…L-E-S-B-I-A-N-S.” Timo almost breaks out laughing, but notices the two women walking in their direction. Jake does the best he can to appear small and unnoticed, while Timo does the best he can to get the young man noticed. “Excuse me ladies, I couldn’t help but notice that you had a tattoo on your right leg there.” They both stop briefly to look at him, unsure what to make of it. He points at the leg, and holds up a hand, signaling for them to wait. He rolls his sleeve up and shows the tattoo of the leather band that has shark teeth dangling from it. “Wow, who did that for you?” “Well, back on the Island, they have many skilled tattoo artists.” “Island? Are you from Hawaii?” “He shakes his head and laughs, even though he is annoyed. “No, no, Samoa, but you are close. Where was yours done?” The nicely dressed young woman smiles a bit and blushes. “A friend did it for me, it is kindof supposed to be secret.” Timo scratches his head a bit and looks down at it. The tattoo bares a VERY close resemblance to the scantily clad lady friend of hers. He smiles, putting on the million dollar smile that the women love and proceeded to embarrass the hell out of his understudy. “You know Jake here has a tattoo too.” Both girls seem to be interested by this now, it is apparently something they like. Timo, being the master of wit, and jackass that he is, looks over to his friend, who is trying his damnedest to give Timo ‘The Mexican Look of Death’. Timo shirks it off and smiles. “Really? Where is it?” He smiles at them and kinda blushes, knowing full well he doesn’t have a tattoo. He is fishing for something to say when Timo puts the words in his mouth. “You can’t see it.” Jake begins to lunge for Timo, but a swift stomp on the foot stops that motion. Both women look let down, not realizing what Timo meant. “But…why not?” Jake looks over fishing for something to say. Timo rolls his hands in the air as the girls watch Jake, trying to get him to carry with the line. Finally he seems to get what Timo is doing and plays along. “Because….umm….You cant SEE it…you know, with all my clothes on.” The girls blush. “But, you know, I guess I COULD show you, if you really wanted to see it. But, it’s kinda hard to find.” Timo sits back and relaxes, eating his cold cut trio. “If ever there was a master, it was you.” He told himself. Finally one of the girls smiles devilishly, actually upon closer examination, they BOTH were. The younger, and more sensibly clothed one reached out and grabbed Jake lightly by the shoulder. She leaned down and whispered something in his ear, and he almost dropped his sub. She leans back up and reaches for a napkin, writing their names and a phone number on it. She hands it to Jake and waves that little cutesy girl wave as they both walked out. Jake looked over at Timo. “Panache?” “Panache.” “My Particular Idiom?” “Your Particular Idiom.” “Style?” “Yeah man, definitely Style.” Timo leaned back and began to eat his meal again, but not before Jake stopped him. “Hey Timo.” “Yeah kid?” “You know what she whispered to me?” The Samoan put the sandwich down for a moment and leaned forward, looking side to side to make sure that nobody else was going to hear this juicy detail. Jake leaned back and smiled a bit. “She said only one word, but it was just enough to entice me.” “Yeah…and?” “Suffice to say Timo, you were right, despite what she looked like, and how she was dressed, I bet she is wild.” “Yeah…so…what did she say?” “Who would have thought one word could cause me to…” “JAKE!” “What?” Timo laughed a bit and shook his head. Jake was just like him in so many ways. This was going to be a great teaching experience for The Samoan. Still the fact remained that he needed to know. “What did she say?” Jake looked around and smiled, nodding in the coy “I’m a stud” way and replied smoothly… “Shaved.”
