Someone will be buried alive on Saturday Night....

27 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
|Someone will be buried alive on Saturday Night....|
Mr. Platinum Carl Cash | 09/27/01
I was watching the TV the other day; well I believe it was today. You see I saw the thing that Human put out, and I have to say I was not impressed. You see Human there is one thing I have that you don’t, wait a minute there is a lot of things, Fame, Money, Looks, and many more things those are just to name a few. Human this Saturday at Hallowed Knights, we will be alone in a graveyard in New York City, fighting over dead bodies, and then a little bit after you will be joining them. You see Human I have been in a graveyard match before. I don’t know if you have been in any, but obviously I didn’t lose, because I wouldn’t be here today would I, well the other man walked away from it but only after people dug him out. I am not afraid of anyone or doing anything, I am on of those men that have no limits. I show no fear, I have no fear can come this Saturday anything that I need to do; I can and will do it.

There is an award show on Friday night that I will not be attending, you see this Friday night I will be at the New York City graveyard where we are fighting, putting your casket in the ground, you see right now I am getting a gravestone for you it will read Here lies Human, put to rest September, 29th. You see this is going to be my stepping stone in FSW, this is going to bring me to the top, you see I have been a one man wrecking crew here, I will admit you did beat me, but that was lucky. I did not know what to accept from you and well now I do, I know each and every move that you will do, and I have you scouted out. Just wait until this Saturday, if you are not ordering Hallowed Knights, I would request you do, at least Saturdays version because it will be a match for the ages. It will go down in the top match in FSW history; it could be the top match in FSW history. It will make one of us, and it will break one of us, one of will be a legend, and one of us will be well gone. It will make on of us a rising star, and that one person is going to be Carl Cash, well because Human, I am the man, I am the future of FSW, there are so many people that would love to be where I am, and I am doing this for all of them, all the people that will never get the chance to be in the primetime, well I am going to succeed. I am going to win, and as you see Human the Casket is already at the graveyard, so I hope you are ready to be buried alive.

The camera goes to a New York City cemetery, the one where the FSW match is being held, and it shows the casket that Carl Cash had bought a few days earlier, being set there. Then the Camera goes back to where ever cash is

You see tomorrow night, I will be there digging a whole for that Casket and on Saturday night at Hallowed Knights, you will be buried in it, because be all that you can be, but you’ll never be as good as me

With that the FSW camera goes off the air with Carl Cash laughing
