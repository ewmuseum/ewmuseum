Captain MARVEL vs. Vince McMahon!

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Wednesday, July 31st, 2001 Incredibly Deep Narrative Voice: There have been many brave and heroic men throughout history... [Camera shows 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous watching Monday Night RAW on television as he sits on his big lazy boy reclining chair. He has a bag of Lays Dill Pickle Potato Chips on his lap, munching away at them as Mini-Mike does the same on his small lazy boy reclining chair.] Incredibly Deep Narrative Voice: General Douglas McArthur....Winston Churchill....US Fire Departement.... [Mike watches the T.V. as Chris Benoit wrestles Kurt Angle in an ultimate submission match inside a hell in a cell. Mike seems like he is being very entertained by the technical skills of both men] Incredibly Deep Narrative Voice: But one man is above and beyond the rest... [On the screen, 'No Chance' blares over the speakers and Vince McMahon struts out in a grey suit with a big grin on his face. Mike shakes his head. This is not going to be good] Incredibly Deep Narrative Voice: One man's spaldings are JUST a little bigger.... [On the screen, Vince raises a mic to his lips as the fans boo in disapproval of having him interrupt a perfectly good match with his old, boring self.] Vince McMahon: Ladies and Gentlemen.....I apologize for interrupting this match......but I have.........a very special announcement to make. Mike Marvelous: [Yelling at the screen] Stop pausing! You're just making it more boring! Vince McMahon: I've decided...that the main event for Wrestlemania X-9.....will be a Gravy Bowl Bra and Panties Match feauturing none other.....than Mae Young.....and the Fabulous Moolah....for the Undisputed Title! Mike Marvelous: WHAT!?!? WHAT THE HECK!?! Vince McMahon: And to replace the main event for RAW...I'd like to bring out The Gobbledy Gooker and Kamala...who will compete in THIS VERY RING...right now! Mike Marvelous: YOU SUCK, VINCE! WHY WON'T YOU DIE!?! [A chorus of boos answer Vince's bright idea.] Vince McMahon: Thank you, thank you. And enjoy the show! [Kurt Angle and Benoit exit the ring confused and upset that their match was scrapped as The Gobbledy Gooker and Kamala make their way down the entrance way. The audience starts packing up to leave] Mike Marvelous: ARRRRRRRGH!!! Incredibly Deep Narrative Voice: This man is CAPTAIN MARVEL! By day he is mild mannered wrestler 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous, but when evil lurks...he transforms into Captain MARVEL to save the day! [Camera shows Mike turning off RAW] Mike Marvelous: I don't get it, Mini-Mike! What has happened to the WWF...oops, I mean...the WW*EEEEEEEEEE*.... Mini-Mike: I dunno, Michaew, but I have a feewing that it's aww Vince's fauwt... Mike Marvelous: You may be right, my midget clone...you may be right....c'mon, let's go get some Chocolate Teddy Grahams and Blue Koolaid! Mini-Mike: WOO HOO! [Mike and Mini-Mike get up off their recliners, brush the chip crums away, then begin to walk towards the kitchen...but they stop dead in their tracks as the red phone in the glass cover lights up and begins to ring.] Mike Marvelous: The phone is ringing, Mini-Mike! You know what that means! [Mike lifts the glass cover off and picks up the phone] Mike Marvelous: Hello, Annonomous Tipster? [pause] Yes...[pause]...yes....[pause]...uh huh...[pause]...oui...[pause]....da....[pause]...si...[pause]...uh huh, uh huh, uuuuh huh....[pause]EGADS!...YOU DON'T SAY! Don't worry annonomous tipster! I'm on it! [Mike lifts the phone high in the air then slams it down in dramatic fashion] Mike Marvelous: TO THE MARVEL CAVE! [Mike and Mini-Mike run into the bathroom. Mike throws the toilet lid open and they both stare down the bowl] Mike Marvelous:....You first, Mini-Mike... [Mini-Mike frowns in disapproval as Mike lifts the midget into the air and carefully places him in the bowl.] Mike Marvelous: Adious, compadre... [Mike pushes the flush lever and Mini-Mike spins around in the toilet] Mini-Mike: EEEEE! EEEEE! EEEEEEEE! [Mike-Mike disappears down the toilet followed by a sucking sound] Mike Marvelous: Ok...I guess it's my turn now... [Mike puts one foot in the toilet, then another, then braces himself before pushing the lever. He whirls around a few times, then disappears into the toilet just like Mini-Mike did. He travels down the pipes and into the Marvel Cave, where he falls to the cave floor into a pile of sewage. Mini-Mike is also there.] Mike Marvelous: Oops...I forgot to tell Alfred not to use this toilet... [Mike and Mini-Mike get up and walk slowly towards the makeshift phonebooths as sewage drips off their clothes. After a few seconds, the doors fling open...but it is no longer Mike Marvelous and Mini-Mike...it is CAPTAIN MARVEL AND MINI-MARVEL! Defenders of justice, freedom, and the Can-American way!] Captain MARVEL: To the MARVELmobile! [Our heroes run to a secret garage where Mike presses a button. The garage door slides open to reveal an old Chevette piss wagon with a rusty body and a cracked back window. Captain MARVEL and Mini-Marvel hop in and Captain MARVEL presses a button to open up the Marvel Cave. He then presses another button a the wheel and fire shoots out of the muffler and the car takes off at lightning speed...destination...wherever evil lurks!] Meanwhile.... [At the Titan Tower, Vince McMahon sits behind his desk in his office. He steeples his fingers underneath his chin, seemingly deep in thought. He is not alone. In the office is every mid card talent of the past and present. They all wait patiently for their boss to speak...which he doe] Vince McMahon: My father had a dream...his dream was to build the best damn wrestling federation in the world. So he built the WWWF...and he turned it into something big. But I also had a dream...I bought the WWWF off of my father, and I turned it into something HUGE! I turned it into...the W...W...F. In the mid to late 80's, business was booming. We got a television deal, signed some of the biggest stars in the world, and established ppv events. Wrestlemania III broke the in-door event attendance record held by the Rolling Stones! I created the immortal Hulk Hogan...then later, in the 90's...I created 'Stone Cold' Steve Austin. I broke the 34 month winning streak held by Nitro and never looked back! I bought WCW and pissed all over it! I turned ECW into a memory! [The mid carders begin rallying behind Vince, clapping and nodding their heads] Vince McMahon: But lately...things have changed. Ratings have plummeted....attendance has gone down....buyrates have dropped....people have stopped caring! But I will MAKE them care! Next week on RAW...I will switch RAW on to every channel in the US, and play a special hypnotic message that will make EVERYBODY watch from now on! They won't be able to leave the t.v.! They will go to every show, buy every pay per view, watch RAW and Smackdown every Monday and Thursday night! Hell, they'll even watch Heat and Velocity! I won't just rule the world of sports entertainment....I'll rule THE WORLD! Or my name isn't Vincent...K....McMahon! AAAHAHAHAHAHAHA! MWAAHAHAHAHAHA! [The mid carders start cheering but they are interrupted as the door bursts open. Standing in the doorway are Captain MARVEL and Mini-Marvel! And they overheard Vince's sick plot to rule the world of t.v.!] Captain MARVEL: Not so fast there, Vince! I heard every word of your sickening plot! If there's something I hate, it's evil company owners! Vince McMahon: Well, well, well...if it isn't Captain MARVEL...here to save the day. Let's talk seriously now...you can't stop me. NOBODY can stop me. I'm a billionaire, and I have some of the toughest customers in the world backing me up! Captain MARVEL: What you're doing is taking away people's freedom to choose the program that they want to watch! Instead of coming up with a fiendish plot to hypnotize the viewers, you should try to improve your product! Cut down on all the stupid over the top storylines! Stop hot shotting angles! Start giving the fans what they WANT! They want WRESTLING, Vince! Not cheesy backstage vignettes with corny acting! Vince McMahon: How dare you question my genius? I am one of the wealthiest men in the world! And soon...I'll be the most POWERFUL man in the world! I'll buy out every other wrestling federation in the world...no more NWA TNA...no more XWF...no more WWA...no more FSW! I'll buy out every wrestler's contracts and turn them into circus acts! Timo Bolamba will be renamed Butt Boy, and he'll be Rikishi's manager, in charge of washing his thongs! Johnny Raindance will become Squanto the homosexual indian who sniffs gasoline. 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous will be the new manager for Billy and Chuck! Captain MARVEL: Not if I can help it, fiend! Vince McMahon: I'm tired of chatting with you. I have a world to take over. Seize them! [Captain MARVEL and Mini-Mike watch as the mid carders circle around them preparing to attack. Christian and Lance Storm run up to our heroes with steel chairs and go to hit the con-chair-to, but they duck and the chairs collide, sending shockwaves through their bones. They drop the chairs and Captain MARVEL and Mini-Marvel pick them up, then give them chair shots of their own] *SPIFF!* [Rikishi walks up to Captain MARVEL and grins maniacally] Rikishi: Before I stick your face up my ass...I should warn you...I had Taco Bell for lunch! [Rikishi runs backwards at Captain MARVEL ass-first, but Captain MARVEL sidesteps out of the way and Rikishi backs that ass up...right into a statue of the Fertility God...who's stone penis sticks up Rikishi's ass. Rikishi's eyes bulge out of his head] *BOCA!* [Dean Malenko runs up to Mini-Marvel. They are both around the same height, so they start brawling with their fists. Everybody stops fighting at once to watch the two midgets fight] Test: MIDGET FIIIIGHT! Captain MARVEL: C'mon, Mini-Marvel! Kick his butt! [Mini-Marvel hits a stiff forearm shot on Dean, but Malenko recovers and takes down Mini-Marvel with a scissor takedown, then locks on the Texas Cloverleaf] Captain MARVEL: Fight your way out of it, Mini-Marvel! Don't give up! [Mini-Marvel squeezes his eyes shut, and with the strength of 20 midgets, he rolls over and fights his way out of the hold, then kicks Malenko in the chin. Malenko backs up from the impact of the kick and then falls out of the office window] Dean Malenko: EEEEEEEEEEEEEEeeeeeeee! *SPLAT!* [Test runs at Captain MARVEL looking for the big boot, but our cunning hero grabs his leg and gives him a shot to the spaldings.] *HONK!* [Tommy Dreamer charges Captain MARVEL with a clothesline but his arm snaps on impact because of all the past injuries he's sustained.] *BORT!* [Billy and Chuck approach our heroes from behind and go to hit double superkicks on both of them, but they duck the kicks, spin Billy and Chuck around] Captain MARVEL: Do you know what time it is, Mini-Marvel!?! Mini-Marvel: ATOMIC WEDGIE TIME! [Our heroes both reach into the back of Billy and Chuck's tights then pull their thongs up over their heads] *MINT!* [There is one mid carder left...Steven Richards. He looks at Captain MARVEL, then looks at Mini-Marvel, then pulls a kendo stick out of his tights and breaks it over his own head] *BLORPH!* [Captain MARVEL and Mini-Marvel walk up to the desk of Vince McMahon] Captain MARVEL: Give up, Vince! You've been defeated! Vince McMahon: Oh...have I? [Vince suddenly pulls a double barrelled shotgun out of his desk drawer and points it at our heroes] Vince McMahon: On the contrary...it looks like YOU'VE been defeated, Captain MARVEL! Say your prayers! [Vince cocks the gun and is about to fire when his cell phone rings] Vince McMahon: Hello...[pause]...yeah...[pause]What!? [pause]...no, it can't be! NO! [pause]...NOOOO! [Vince hangs up the phone and drops the gun] Vince McMahon: That was just my accountant... Captain MARVEL: And...? Vince McMahon: I'm bankrupt. I guess I shouldn't have created the XHL(Xtreme Hockey League) after all. I thought it was gonna be a hit! Sure, XFL failed, but I thought for sure that XHL would skyrocket! Especially with Jim Ross and Jerry Lawler as the commentators and Earl Hebner and Charles Robinson as the referees! But now...I'm ruined. I don't even have enough money to air another show of RAW! Captain MARVEL: Well, you should've learned from your XFL mistake... Vince McMahon: Oh, shut up! [Vince walks over to the window and jumps out] *BLAMMO!* Captain MARVEL: Wow! That's the first time the villain defeated HIMSELF! Well...if anyone can do that, it's Vincent K. McMahon. Mini-Marvel: You said it, Michaew. Captain MARVEL: Well...the world is safe from evil for another day...but for how long?! For now...our work here is done. So AWAAAAAAY WE GO!!!!! [Captain MARVEL and Mini-Marvel take flight and fly out the broken window into the sky] Incredibly Deep Narrative Voice: Well kiddies, it looks like Captain MARVEL has cleaned up the world of sport entertainment! What adventure will our heroes go on next week? There's only one way to find out! Tune in next time! Same MARVEL time! Same MARVEL channel! [A big 'M' spins onto the screen then spins off as the scene fades to black]
