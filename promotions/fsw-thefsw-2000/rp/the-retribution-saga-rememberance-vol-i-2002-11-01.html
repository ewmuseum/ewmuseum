The Retribution Saga - Rememberance Vol I

1 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/1/02 'Dude, you sure you wanna watch this?' 'Yes.' Jason O'Neil sits in a large room. Speakers line the walls, and the lights are dimmed. Three men sit in the large chairs in front of a giant television screen. Flanked to each side are friends from his former wrestling company owned by Steven Caldera. Jeff Wylde, a large black man standing over 6'8' tall sits to his left, wearing a black leather jacket, and white shirt. To his left, Twisted, his confidant, and one of his best friends from the GCW sits wearing a 'Lost Soul' T-shirt and jeans. The two look wearily at the dazed O'Neil. O'Neil looks left and nods at Wylde. Wylde looks at Twisted, who nods slightly. 'Fine. Don't say we didn't warn you though.' Wylde says as he stands up and produces a tape from his coat pocket. He walks over to the large TV and puts the tape in the VCR. The Television goes to snow and suddenly black as the tenth edition of Glory kicks off. 'Fast forward it, Jeff. Get to what I want to see.' 'Ok.' Wylde pushes the button. The action whizzes by as O'Neil stares at the screen, mesmerized by the action. 'Wait. Just tell me this, Jace. What do you remember about that night?' 'Last thing I remember is looking at Stevens and seeing a flash of gray overtake my eyes, which quickly faded to black.' 'Wait. So all you remember is the chair shot from Stevens?' Twisted asks in disbelief. 'That's it. The next thing I remember is finally opening my eyes about 3 weeks ago. Why?' 'You really haven't seen this?' Wylde asks, afraid of the answer. 'I really haven't seen it. I have no idea what happened after that chair shot. Play the tape.' Wylde takes his finger off the button just as Michael Stevens delivers the crushing chair to the side of O'Neil's head. O'Neil looks on in horror as Kimbusa and Stevens proceed to decimate him, finishing him off with a Bonzai drop to the head. O'Neil watches himself on the screen, surrounded in a pool of his own blood, and barely breathing, for the first time. Silently, a tear rolls down his cheek, as he feels nothing but pity for the poor man, being destroyed at the hands of these mad men. 'Turn it off.' A silent tear rolls down the cheek of Jason O'Neil, his life changed forever. Retribution must come...whatever the price.
