Interview Part 1

29 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/29/2002 “Tell me again why we are here?” Boda asks Chip O’Meara. “I thought we were going to sabotage Orion’s office?” “That’s still the plan. I need to get that bastard” Chip calmly responds. “Losing the tag match was not enough. But, the reason we are here is I got some obligation to do a magazine interview. Some type of wrestling rag wants the real scoop on Five Star Wrestling. And of course, why not do it with yours truly, and not that windbag Paul Orion.” “But why do I have to be here? I could be working out, or giving the Boda-Sausage to some young pretty blonde.” Boda quips as he holds his ‘package’ in his right hand, giving it the stereo typical manly shaking. “Well, I need you here.” Chip states “You know, just incase Orion tries to get back at me for Glory. I can’t be left alone, especially with some magazine writer I don’t know from jack squat. For all I know, this reporter could be a Orion goon. Hell, it could be Orion himself, and dammit..... I don’t need this shit.” “Fine.” Boda huffs. “Calm down, I’ll stay. But why this place?” “This is where the reporter asked to me me.” Chip tells Boda. “So, since it’s for our good, as in me and you, big man, then I agreed to meet him here. But, dammit, he’s running late.” And, exactly, what is this place? Well, upon looking around, we notice both Chip and Boda are sitting, at a picnic table, in the middle of a park. The sun beating down brightly on the near by pond. Birds chirping in the treetops as little children run rampant through the playground just about 200 feet from where Boda and Chip sit. All seems peaceful, so why does it bother Boda to be here? “Man, I hate parks.” Boda spews. “Always got little kids and families, trying to have fun. It’s sickening. Hell, it’s almost as bad as having to carry Cassius’ pathetic carcass through another one of the Dead on Arrival tournament matches this coming week. Especially since it’s Nelson and his little slave boy O.G, I’ll have to work twice as hard this week. Oh well, I am Boda after all.” “Yeah,” Chip interrupts, “But try not to piss Tyler off. He’s essential to my overall plan.” “Overall plan?” Boda stands up in anger, “OVERALL PLAN? You do know who Tyler Nelson is right? That son of a bitch would rather pat you on the back with a 12 inch dagger, than give you anything, and I mean anything.” “I brought him into FSW to help me.” Chip remarks. “He’s a power player, and he will help me defeat that son of a bitch Orion.” “I can’t believe I’m hearing this from you,” Boda sneers. “The only person Tyler Nelson has ever helped..... was himself. Hell, if there is one person I trust less than Cassius..... his name is Tyler Nelson.” Boda sits back down, causing the table to move a bit. Sure, Boda has no reason to trust Tyler Nelson. After all, look at the history. Tyler takes over PCW about a year and a half ago. To build a remarkable, and self preserving stable of hired help, Tyler implores Boda to join him. Of course, Boda was known as ‘Hardknocks’ Chris Allen back then, but all in all, it was still Boda. So, as many of you do, or do not know, Boda, aka Hardknocks Chris Allen, became first a tag partner with Homicidal Anthony Cay, forming The Media Assassins. Together, the two made sure no one, and I mean NO ONE, got at, or too, Tyler Nelson. Boda did his job well. Anthony Cay.... well, he sucked, and got CANCELLED. So, then, Boda went it alone, till Tyler, needing to make up for penis size, needed to increase his size of his entourage, hired Jon Kano. Teaming with Kano this time around, Boda formed the team Media Moguls. Well, as history dictates, this lasted about a month, maybe two, till Tyler, seemingly egotistically, CANCELLED BODA. But, Boda wasn’t bitter about it. No. Getting fired from PCW made Boda bigger, better and badder. Firing Boda was the catylist to Boda’s title winning spree, eventually, as we all know, winning the PCW World Title 2 times. Of course, as Boda made his way back to PCW, after getting CANCELLED, seemingly, Tyler Nelson never once tried to intercept the direction Boda was heading into. Could it been that Nelson knew Boda was going to be a leader in PCW? Quite possibly, but my money’s on the fact that Tyler was scared. Scared to approach Boda, scared because he fired the monster, when he didn’t need him anymore. Well, Boda came, Boda saw, and Boda conquered PCW. Now, Boda’s moved onto FSW, and low and behold..... that weasel Ty Nelson and his gorilla have followed suit. Well, if Tyler thinks he’s going to run FSW like he did PCW..... Boda has something to say about that. As far as Chip goes, FSW is HIS, not Orion’s, not Nelson’s. It’s Chip O’Meara, and Boda, is gonna make sure it stays that way. After all, Boda was hired to be be CHIP O’MEARA’S HIRED GUN. “Hey there Chip.” A voice speaks from behind the position the two FSWers sit. Chip and Boda turn, and they see a short, drivel of a man. His coke bottle glasses, and pocket protector stand out amid his acne ridden face. In his left hand, a recording machine. “Well,” Chip remarks to Boda. “Looks like my interviewer is here. What a dweeb.” Boda snorts in laughter, as Chip speaks out to the man. “Hey there, glad you could make it.” The reporter walks over, and plops his recorder on the picnic table. He extends a hand to Chip, which the Commissioner gladly shakes his hand. As the reporter reaches out to shake Boda’s hand, Chris nearly turns and ignores the little peanut of a man. Suddenly, a cell phone rings, and Chip takes his out of his pocket, and answers it. As Chip talks, he walks off and leaves us watching the nerd set up for the interview. Chip clicks off his phone conversation, then disgustidly walks back to the table. “I hate to do this,” Chip says as he shakes his head, “But I have to go. Something happened, and my presence is needed at FSW Offices. Chris, you do the interview. You need some publicity.” “But” “Sorry, but I have to run.” Chip says hurriedly as he takes off, leaving the nerdly reporter and Boda, all alone. “Shit” Boda says under his breathe.” “Well, Boda.” the gawky reporter retorts, “I’m a huge fan. I guess I could interview you, get your thoughts on FSW and what not. You mind?” “I mind, yes.” Boda snorts back at this little geek. “But, Chip wants me to do this, so I will. Just don’t cross any lines, and I won’t cross any boundaries. Got it?” “Yes.” The reporter responds as the scene fades to black.
