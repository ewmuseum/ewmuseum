Questioning the Managemnt

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-31-02 [Narration] [Last week we saw one of the most unsual things in the history of FSW. Even people outside FSW say that they have never seen anything like what happened last week during EOEs match. Incase you dont 'A Tag Team' came out during EOE's match, their explanation of being out was someone backstage told them their match was about ot take place at that time so they made their entrance along with their mics. This distracted EOE, and from out of no where Walden rolled EOE up 1-2-3 count. EOE had been undefeated until that point and he's breathing for fire because of the way he had to lose and he wants some answers from someone before the show starts tonight.] --------------------- We open to EOE and Eve just a few kilometers away from the North Charleston Arena in a rented Escalade that he is completely dogging out. EOE is driving like a bat out of hell trying to get to the FSW management to get some answers. Eve- Damn, slow down before you miss the exit. EOE- You ride, I'll drive, this aint my shit anyway I dont care what happens to it. Eve- Ok but do you not care if we flip tha f*ck over? The exit is right up there slow down dude! As EOE weaves in and out of traffic he swiftly comes up on his exit and aggressively forces cars to let him in the right lane, then he get's his way and makes the exit, and drives up to a stop light. EOE- Which way do I turn? Eve- You tell me... Jeff Gordon. [EOE gives her a threatening look] EOE- Look Eve I aint got time for your shi... [Eve interupts] Eve- OK OK OK, you turn the opposite of your left. EOE- You're trying today me, you really are. Eve- Turn RIGHT DAMN! EOE- I know that woman. Eve- Look don't get mad at me, I ain't done shit. I'm sure you can hold that anger for just a little bit longer, for those people when you get in the arena. I'm not trying to hear you anyway, you crazy today. I mean you are crazy everday but today, you are off the hook. Driving all crazy with me in the car. If had listened to me from day one, you wouldn't be in the positon anyway. You would be in one of those number one contenders match tonight, but you are in a worthless handicapped tag team match against to little boys who don't know how to act when they get high. It's all on you, don't be gettin at me. [EOE stops for the guards to give him his pass, unfortunately it's one of those old security man who doesn't exactly walk with alot of pace. The man slowly writes his name down the slowly walks in the little booth and slowly get his pass. He retreives it but then drops it. EOE sits back in his seat and taps on the stirring wheel trying to be patient. THe man then get's a string to put throught hoe oin the pass, btu then he drops that ass well. EOE sits up in his let's out a sull.] EOE- Look man can you hurry your old slow ass up. I'm in a hurry here. [The man is obviously trying his best to speed up but it's not good enough for EOE.] EOE- I tell you what. [EOE get's out the car and goes in the booth with the man. He pics up two passes and two strings and then ties them himself.] EOE- Is this what you are trying to do? What so hard about this? [EOE storms out and get's back in the Escalade. HE looks at the startled and then drives off.] Eve- Now that was just wrong. That wasn't even called for. Eve shakes her head and continues to look straight ahead.] EOE- And who are you suppose to be today? Mighty Morphin Muthafu***** Power Ranger? [She waits to respond for a minute.] Eve- The pink one. [She mutters.] [EOE stops the vehicle in the middle of the street.] EOE- I tell you what...get out, get out the damn car now. I'm not to hear your shit today. [Eve looks up in the air, as she has been through this event a thousand times.] Eve- You are acting real stupid today. I told you, you woudln't been in this situation had you listened to me. Besides I'm not getting out and walking anywhere it's hot, very hot, too hot to be walking infact. So stop trippin and go on down the street, cause you are acting crazy today. EOE- So you're not going to get out like I told you? Eve- Huuuuh! No Shawn I am not. [EOE nods his head.] EOE- Aight, f*ck it I'll walk. [EOE get's out the Escalade in the middle of the street and takes off walking, but not before slamming the door and making the vehicle talking to himself.] EOE- I ain't gotta listen to that shit. Eve- Shawn get back in the car and stop acting crazy. SHAWN! [EOE throws up his middle finger. She slides over from the passenger side.] EOE- And you better get my bags out too. Eve- F**k your bags and your parking spot. [EOE turns around.] EOE- Don't get em out and see what happens, and find a good parking spot! [Eve burns off delivering a middle finger of her own. As EOE walks towards the entrance way, he can't help to but think about those to little boys that interupted and cost him his match last week, the more and more he thinks about it the faster he walks. Before he knows it he's made his way back doors of the arena. EOE looks up and realizes where he is.] EOE- The shit is about to go down. [EOE pulls the doors back with fury and as usual alot of people running around everywhere trying to get things right for tonights show. After a few moments alot of people spot him and things to start to get quiet because they know he's pissed out the management team. Alot of whipsering amongst groups of people start. As EOE slowly walks by with a very pissed off look on his face, as he makes eye contact with people and walk by people they look and get out of his way. EOE stops until he reaches a point were he think everyone can here him.] EOE- Ex-fu**ingcuse me I need everyone who was in charge of last weeks time schedule... [EOE looks around.] EOE- In this room right here and don't make me ask again. [Everyone starts to talk amongst, and people start to walk towards the room.] EOE- No No no wait, forget it I want everyone to stand right here, all you management team people shutup and listen because I got a question to ask. [Everyone stops in their tracks and a hush falls over the crowd.] EOE- *Ahem* Which one of ya'll fu...people...Einstein's or whatever you consider yourselves, which one of ya'll told those two little boy's that it was their time to go out and wrestle. [Everyone starts looking around. But no one speaks up.] EOE- Oh ok, that must have been one quick ass cat to go around and snatch everyones tongue that quick. [EOE stands their for a response but no one says anything.] EOE- Why am I hearing crickets? Ok then act dumb if you want too. [EOE picks up a chair. The people then start talking.] A man- It was Adam. Everyone- Yea Adam. EOE- Who in the hell is Adam? That man again- Well he heard about the mistake he made and chose to take off. EOE- Oh? And where does this Adam live? Lynn - Well um we don't know. EOE- You don't know? Maybe on a street called LIAR! Lane perhaps? Do you people think I'm dumb or something? You think I fell of the back a marijuana truck like those two kids did? I'm not dumb and I know that one of you hoes made that mistake. I'm going to get that paper from last week and find out who was in charge of what. and when I do it's going to be some smoke in the city. Sam- Come on EOE, whoever it was last week know they messed up that's why you're in the handicapped match tonight. I requested it because I know you didn't deserve that. [Sam continues to smile as if he's done EOE a favor.] EOE- YOu think you've really done something? Let me tell you noting can take the place of that screw up. The what's worse you go from one f*** up to another. Lynn- What do you mean. EOE- You think I want to be involved with a couple bootleg cheech and chongs? I could have been in a BATTLE ROYAL to get in a title hsot at Genesis, but now thanks to you pussies I have to deal with something way below my standards. You know things haven't changed around here you always find a way to screw me over well one day my day will come again and when it does, you're going to wish you were never accepted here, I promise you that. I mean I don't understand out of all the matches you people screw up mine a week before I could be a number one contender? That's just too damn coincidental to me. Lynn- You mean to tell me EOE- Shutup I'm talkin. i want to some answers. Sam- Gunslinger makes the matches. EOE- Oh he does? Next time you see him tell him about to be a fist slinger, if something like this happens again and I don't some answers. [As EOE continues to lay into the entire backstage, Eve comes up behind him with not so pleasant look on his face. She taps him on the shoulder. EOE continues to talk ignoring the tap.] Eve- I need to talk to you and your stupid brain. EOE- Look, I'm handling something. Eve- So do I, now do make me act stupid out here, find out room and meet me there! She turns and walks away. [EOE blows it off]. EOE- Anyway, I want to know who had something to do with this. Eve- NOW SHAWN ELLIS! [Eve turns the corner]. EOE- Dammit, look none of you people move, I'll be right back. [EOE walks off as soon as he is out of the managements site everyone scatters everywhere.] 'Hurry up for he comes back he's crazy!' they all shout. [End].
