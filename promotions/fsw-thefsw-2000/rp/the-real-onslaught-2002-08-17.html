The REAL Onslaught

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/17/02 Sacrifice. Big Daveylicious sacrificed a shot at the World Heavyweight title for the good of the Five Star Fi...Four. Instead of thinking jealously like the rest of the new generation of the FSW. Big D prides himself with his stable mates to remake the FSW of old. Instead of destroying the FSW like the rest of the roster has been doing. Just look at Bad Frotitude. Two men that have no care or drive in the world but just to party on. It’s pathetic. Is this Paul Orion’s secret competition that's supposed to drive fear into the hearts of Big D and Leon Chambers? This is the competition that’s SUPPOSED to destroy Big D and Leon Chambers? If it is than Paul Orion is... A.) Secretly on the Five Star Fi..Four’s side. B.) Secretly to pay Britney Spears a million dollars for sex. C.) Old wrinkly incompetent man who fines Matlock, Prunes, and being raped by the orderlies is the best value of entertainment in the world. If you picked (A. WRONG! Even though the lack of tag team competition against the likes of Big D and Chambers are no challenge for them. That old senile goat Paul Orion doesn’t know talent if it kicked him in the ass. He’s too stuck up talking about his past fighting Gorgeous George or fighting Hercules in Rome. If you picked (B. YOU’RE A DUMBA$$. No offense or Britney wouldn’t give her virginity up for money or even that amount. Doesn’t she get more than that? Expect this to happen when her career is over. But than again she can always go for the record of the oldest virgin if her career doesn‘t do good If you picked (C. You’re right! Tell them what they won, Johnny! Johnny: They won new knowledge that Paul Orion finds Matlock, Prunes, viagra, and being raped by orderlies good ol’ fashion entertainment! That’s enough, Johnny. Johnny: But I wanted the viewers to feel the emotion and... Johnny, shut up. Johnny: Okay. Besides all that the point is. Whoever Paul Orion puts up against the Five Star Fi...Four will be sacrificed. Whether you’re Timo Bolamba,Canadian Criminal, Bad Frotitude or even Aunt Clara who makes the best damn muffins in the world! (The scene fades inside FSW Studios. Were facing a dark room with the spotlight on two director styled chairs. In the right chair with his hands politely in his lap sits, Big Daveylicious clad in a Armani suit, Gucci shoes, and of course his signature Oakleys. On the left chair sitting beside Big D is his former tag team partner and former stablemate, Epitome of Evil.) Big Daveylicious: Every time I speak with the other FSW old timers they always complain about this Onslaught. I often wondered why and whom is this Onslaught. But fear not my FSW friends! As I have found ! Thanks to the magic of the internet and my friend Michael Graves who still lives in the basement of his parents reading comic books. I found him! Epitome of Evil: Big D... Big Daveylicious: Evil, I know what you’re thinking. This is the guy who supposedly talks bad about me. But I want to clear things up and hopefully result in a nice handshake. Like gentlemen. I mean this Onslaught can’t be hardly the a$$hole you, Jacob, Mike, and the others think of. (Epitome of Evil laughs hysterically.) Big Daveylicious: What? I’m serious. Before I reveal the REAL Onslaught. I spoke with the First Generation of the FSW. Here’s what they had to say... (The scene fades to black than fades to FSW Extreme legend, Razor Blade sitting in the director chair along with Big D.) Big Daveylicious: Hello, people. I’m here with one of Onslaughts most hated rivalry, Razor Blade. The man with the worst history with Onslaught or so I’m told. Razor Blade, what are you’re thoughts on Onslaught? Razor Blade: Onslaught, a familiar name from my past. A guy who ducked me day in and day out. Onslut is best described like that Crick in your neck when you slept wrong the night before. Onslut was nothing, a nobody. He never had the balls to face me....although I guess that is what made him so smart. Big Daveylicious: Who’s Onslut? Razor Blade: Onslaught. Big Daveylicious: That’s what you call the cricks in you’re neck? Usually, when I get those I go to my masseuse, a 300 pound Chinese mammoth of a woman named Chi Shlong. Razor Blade:... Big Daveylicious: She’s usually great on my back. I usually can’t feel a thing...or breath a thing. But after I fall unconscious I wake up in a nice warm tub naked covered in honey. (Big D scratches his chin.) Big Daveylicious: You want her number? Razor Blade: You’re an idiot. I was talking about Onslaught. Big Daveylicious: Well, I’m not sure about his sexuality. It’s okay with me. But I’m sorry I don’t have his number. (Razor Blade grabs Big D by the throat.) Big Daveylicious: Sir, I might accept your sexuality but I’m not into it... (Blade starts to choke Big D as Big D gasps for air.) Big Daveylicious: Cut to commercial! (The scene fades to Big D’s lackey, Michael Graves face. A very close in shot.) Narrator: Tired of being pushed around? (Michael nods.) Narrator: Want to show you’re anger without hurting anyone? (Michael nods again in a sad face. The camera switches to a monkey jumping up and down angrily.) Narrator: Have no worries or fears! Doooooooo what Pete the Pissed Off Primate does... (Michael turns to see what the monkey does as he does this the monkey digs up his rectum and flings his poo at him.) Michael Graves: HEY! He flung his poo poo at me! Narrator: Yes, he flings poo at you! (Michael scratches his chin as a very sexy blonde girl passes him. He stops her as he moves in front of her. Besides his ugly gothic wannabe look the stinch of the monkeys poo on Graves can be smelled and shown all over his shoulders as the blonde holds her nose.) Michael Graves: Hey, baby wanna ...date? (She wips out a badge and a gun indicating she's a cop.) Blonde Girl: You have the right to remain silent... (Michael starts freakishly shaking as he puts his hand in the back of his pants, he pulls out some poo, and flings the poo on the girls badge. She drops it, smacks his face so bad that some of his white face paint comes off of his face onto her hand.) Cop Girl: EWWWWW... (She panics and kicks him in the balls. He falls over on the ground onto the floor as she handcuffs him with her handcuffs.) Michael Graves: I thought if you were mad you can fling poo at people! Narrator: Sorry, Pete the Pissed Off Primate flings poo at you. You don’t fling poo. (With that the scene fades to the FSW Studio with Big Daveylicious still sitting in the director styled chair rubbing his neck with Epitome of Evil snickering in the other directors.) Big Daveylicious: Hi, viewers. Sorry we had to cut off to commercial so early and thank you, Razor Blade for that unusual neck hug?...Ahhhhhh, who am I kidding he was CHOKING ME! Where were you? Epitome of Evil: I was...uh...I saw...a naked woman? (Big D jumps out of his seat looking around.) Big Daveylicious: WHERE?! (Big D takes his seat back looking dissapointed.) Big Daveylicious: You lied there are no naked woman! But besides that I’d like to ask you a few questions, Evil. Epitome Of Evil: I hate his skank hoe ass! Big Daveylicious: You hate his skank hoe ass? Well, to be honest I don’t hate any skank hoe’s ass unless they’re male skank hoes. Prostitution is for women and not for Onslaught or any male or transvestites. Epitome of Evil: Hehe. Big Daveylicious: But for our next guest is a man whom has some past with Onslaught. Another FSW legend, “The Idol” Jacob Jackson! (Idol walks into our view. He shakes hands with his comrades. EOE gets out of the director chair and stands up to let Idol take a seat in his chair.) “The Idol” Jacob Jackson: ‘Tsi you’re Idol! Big Daveylicious: Idol, I’d like to know what are you’re thoughts on Onslaught? “The Idol” Jacob Jackson: ummm... Onslaught...I'll just say he has a big head, and hmmmm...easily gets on my nerves. Big Daveylicious: What does he do? “The Idol” Jacob Jackson: He didn’t share the half gallon of Cookie Dough Ice Cream with me! (Idol walks off the screen running off to seem like he’s crying. Big D shakes his head.) Big Daveylicious: You know it’s one thing to have a big head Onslaught but it’s another thing when you don’t share a half gallon of Cookie Dough Ice Cream with somebody. Esspecially, with “The Idol” Jacob Jackson. Than again. I wouldn’t share a half gallon of ice cream with my own mother! But besides that lets see our next guest. A man with some hard feelings about Onslaught. Epitome of Evil: Hard? Why are you such a perv? Big Daveylicious: When I say “hard” I mean hateful feelings. Not “hard” as in horny. What do I look like a guy who would have made it with a Guatamala hooker? Epitome of Evil: You did that last week. Big Daveylicious: No, I didn’t. Lets bring in MIKE MARVELOUS! (Mike shakes hands with the other Five Star Five members and takes a seat.) Big Daveylicious: Mike, I’d like to know you’re thoughts on me sleeping with a Guatamala hooker? Epitome of Evil: But I thought you said... Big Daveylicious: I...I mean what are you’re thoughts on Onslaught? Mike Marvelous: Your Canadian Hero thinks that in a nutshell...Onslaught is a poophead! Big Daveylicious: Wow, I never seen a nutshell filled with poop heads. I didn’t know nutshells had poop heads in them. Especially, with shit in it. So that’s what Onslaught is? Mike Marvelous:...yeah, I guess. Big Daveylicious: Thank you for your time, Mike. (Mike walks off out of our view. We move very close to the face of Big D.) Big Daveylicious: After all these interviews. I can say one thing. I think you’re all sadly mistaken about Onslaught. I mean he might be a pisonic force of nature but that doesn‘t mean he‘s a nutshell with poop heads in it. Show the pisonic force some love! Epitome of Evil:Idiot. (Suddenly, Big D’s gothic lackey, Michael Graves runs to the stage with a comic book in hand. ) Michael Graves: Big Daveylicious is not an idiot! He’s a sexy b*tch! Big Daveylicious: First off, I’m not an idiot. Secondly, I look no way like my Aunt Clara‘s French Poodle. If you find them sexy you need help. (Michael hands Big D an X-Men Vol. 2 issue #53 which was made in 1996. Didn’t know that I knew that, huh? What‘d you expect a Playboy?...Okay, I was too.) Big Daveylicious: Thanks, to the staff of Marvel.com. I found the REAL Onslaught. Unfortunately, he isn’t some wrestler with a huge ego or even a crick in you’re neck or a nutshell with shit in it or even a skank ass hoe. As a matter of fact he is...a sentient psionic entity created from the consciousness of two mutants: Professor Charles Xavier, telepathic founder of the heroic X-Men, and the charismatic subversive known as Magneto, Master of Magnetism. Epitome of Evil:.... Big Daveylicious: Basically, you guys should be bitching to the makers of X-men about Onslaught. I mean crap guys it’s a comic book character! Not anything else. Especially not a crick in you’re neck or a nutshell with poop heads in it or even a wrestler with a huge ego or a skank ass hoe. Epitome of Evil: I... Big Daveylicious: It’s okay. I just want to know how does Onslaught deal with the FSW? You’re not telling me that some of the roster hates a comic book character too. I mean he’s a pisonic force damn it! How does Onslaught deal with the FSW? Was Onslaught just a name egomaniac FSW Wrestler used but people call him Onslut? The world may never know. (Suddenly, the scene fades into a 17 year old kids room stacked with various X-men comics stacked up on his bed as he just watched a Big D promo. Disgusted the teenager shakes his head.) Teenager: THAT’S IT! NOBODY SPEAKS TO ONSLAUGHT THAT WAY AND GETS AWAY WITH IT! Right, after I read X-Men Vol. 2 issue #53. (With that the scene fades to X-Men Vol. 2 issue #53 making us wish it was Playboy.) This RP is sponsored by FUN: The reason we play this game.
