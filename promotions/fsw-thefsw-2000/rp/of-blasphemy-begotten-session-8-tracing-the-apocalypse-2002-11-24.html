Of Blasphemy Begotten: Session 8 – Tracing the Apocalypse

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Black Pharaoh leered balefully at Shanahan, the Boston native intrepidly glaring back with a snarl curling his upper lip. In the background jaunted the ghoulish horde, wavering as if impatient atop the crown of the altar while the living chamber continued to pulsate and bellow fumes and toxic fluids from ducts in the walls. Shanahan became vaguely aware of a sinister piping in the distance, like the monotonous whistle of some remote flute played on demonic lips. The Black Pharaoh, the humanoid avatar of blasphemous Nyarlathotep, breathed in an exuberant sigh. “I take it that thing you serve at the center of infinity is pleased?” asked Bruce through clenched teeth, the drone piping of the flute threatening to shred away at his sanity. The Black Pharaoh scoffed indignantly, running his fingers absently over the serrated blade of the scimitar. “Hardly. Have you not read what your fool little band of mortal crusaders gave unto you? Lord Azathoth is a mindless entity, a primal urge made manifest to churn and seethe at the center of all creation. Nothing pleases the Creator Of All Things, you human wretch! He is blind and mindless, his purposes as archaic as the oldest of all he created in that trifle event you humans refer to as the ‘Big Bang’, the cataclysmic creation of all you know billions of years ago by the decree of our glorious sultan!” The ghouls continued to stir restlessly at the flanks of the Black Pharaoh, their presence now nothing but the irrelevant wandering of manipulated souls. Their task was done; they had lured the stalwart ex-champion of mankind into his lair and what became of their ragged bodies now was little concern to him. One of the forgotten cadavers inadvertently brushed up against the vile Messenger of the Outer Gods, prompting him to brusquely turn and behead the lifeless avatar in a swift stroke of the unholy blade in his hands. A spray of blood, congealed and reeking of the grave, splattered onto Bruce’s shirt. He barely noticed in light of the multitude of queries rambling through his havoc-usurped mind. “If you claim I’m your damned ‘Chosen One’, why did you try to force me into suicide in the caverns in Romania?!” The Black Pharaoh blinked, leering with celestial amusement at the confusion dotting the face of his mortal adversary. ”Because it was already known you wouldn’t take your own life. In essence….we forbid it. It was a game, youthful fool, a test of how you would react under adverse pressure. Reading your thought processes, the complexities of your brain and its rationalization as you sought resolution. It has already been scrawled in the stars, Bruce Shanahan, your destiny is wholly preordained. There was no real danger of us losing you on that day in the labyrinths of the European underground…..besides, even in the far-fetched case you chose to plunge that dagger into your heart, we would have inhibited your death. Thus your predetermined fate was never in any danger, as we alone hold the key to your final demise.” The disclosure was nothing shocking to the jaded native of Boston; he had long but dismissed all limits he had previously placed on the capabilities of mankind’s nethermost oppressors. To deny they had any command over the dominions of life and death would simply be asinine and it was an aspect of their power he would not veil with cynical ignorance. The Black Pharaoh, now mocking the hapless Shanahan with a diabolical chuckle, peered at a spot on the high arched ceiling that generated the same pulsing appearance as the rapidly beating walls. “Your pride is admirable, but in this dimension it is all but constricting. Even now, as you realize how futile that defiance is, still you contemplate it. Even in light of our overwhelming powers, you refuse to comply with what is simply inevitable!” The stress was simply too much for Shanahan at this point. He collapsed to one knee, his faculties jarred as a barrage of outlandish images flooded his thoughts, forced on him by Nyarlathotep’s savage and ruthless bidding. He was overcome by visions of apocalyptic immensities as they rampaged across the dense urban landscape of Earth, virtually swallowing entire neighborhoods and shrugging off the endeavor of military onslaught like one would swat down flies. He saw the crumbling, burnt-out husks of major metropolitan centers and scorched wreckage littering the outskirts as the panicked populace had vainly attempted to flee, only to take flight directly into the waiting jaws of hideous races shuttled in from the fourth dimension and beyond by the wicked Old Ones. He saw disaster wrought upon disaster as tankers capsized in the high seas, and entire outposts of civilization toppled to the ground in a shower of smoldering ash. Perhaps the worst of all were the horrendous prophecies of the dark lords as they brooded on profane thrones amidst the desecration of humanity, surveying the penultimate turmoil as fire rained on the terror-stricken survivors and yawning pits ruptured across the tainted planet, poisons infesting what little ration remained for those who escaped early demise. In time, those who survived would either replace the ancient shoggoths as servitors of the dark and elder races, or merely exterminated out of spite for their weakness and arrogant sense of uprising instilled as a whole in the fledgling species The scholarly among humankind would logically surmise that these prophecies were imprinted in Bruce’s mind as a form of catalyst to induce his own blossoming madness to finally overwhelm, the apparitions being pervaded with lies and falsehoods constructed of ideas dreamt in far-removed planes shunned by those sane and righteous. Bruce was one of an elite society who realized no such methods were needed; the Great Old Ones were not only as real as the dew in the air and the clouds in the sky, but dreadfully were indeed supremely powerful and justifiable of the demigod status of which they had been christened with in a multitude of forbidden tomes and blackened lore condemned by the pious and rational of human society. “Personally I have little concern for the frenetic whole of mankind, and I take little heed of their exploits,” articulated the Black Pharaoh, his back to the crouched sadist of professional wrestling as he eyed the looming altar and the array of symbols inscribed in a spiraling column up its stout base. “I am but a Courier, a vagabond given the eternal task of bearing commands and serving the priests and servants of the glorious Sultan At the Head of All Creation. But I must admit….on occasion I relish the opportunity to encourage madness among such a haughty and self-righteous people. I take no pleasure in directly causing chaos and devastation; I prefer to let them breed self-destruction at their own accord, serving as only a medium. And the truly ironic thing about it Bruce Shanahan?” Nyarlathotep’s tone in his last statement was one of question moreso than statement, and it instigated Bruce into raising his head to lock eyes with the macabre fiend. He had once heard the reprehensible Outer God described as a hell-spawned maestro of darkness, whose morbid symphony brought madness on nearly all who heard its tune and his sheet-music shunned by all the but the most courageous of lore-hunters. “The ironic thing is that your feelings are no different than my own.” To agree with the Crawling Chaos was a foul and bitter pill to swallow, yet he could offer no dispute. Disasters that brought worldwide heartache and mourning in the past had often served as sources for humor and brought a jovial leisure to his blackened heart. Few still walked the Earth whom he treasured as comrades or family, and even his desire of their security failed to override his yearning to watch the world burn in a hellish inferno. ”You are no different than I,” accused the dark-skinned avatar. ”The suffering of mankind serves no purpose to me, yet to you it is a blessing. Whereas I savor in triggering hatred and insanity, you choose to bring physical pain and bloodshed. So tell me…..which of us is the real monster, Bruce Shanahan? I read your mind, and even now you are indisposed with the torment of Humane. You sit at the feet of an avatar representing a deity more powerful than the Christian God! Yet still you think of petty mortal vices! You are preoccupied with some lesser fool of the Earthly plane, while I stand before you and deliver the predestined elegy of the human race! There truly is no separation between us, Shanahan. We both revel in malice and the sorrow of others. Yet unlike you, I admit to my nature.” The images still flooded Bruce’s mind, pervaded by spliced flashes of his esoteric journey back in the material reality of Earth. His coming battle with Humane of GW, the duel intended on catapulting the victor to even greater glory. As these ideas melded into the loathsome prophecy instilled in his head like some gruesome exhibition, he could only close his eyes and feel the tears push to escape. Empty prayers, empty promises.......
