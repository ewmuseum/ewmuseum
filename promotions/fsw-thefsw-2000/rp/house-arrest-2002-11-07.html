House Arrest

7 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-07-02 Wednesday had come and gone, and the doorbell never rang. The sun rose and set, but the phone never made a sound. Glory had boomed from Kimbusa’s 48 inch flat-screen television for two hours that night… And he was never called upon once. His door remained locked from the outside. What had happened to cause Caldera to overlook him? Caldera was at the announce table the entire night, calling the action with his colleagues. When Violence Jack and Mike McClure made their in-ring debuts, Caldera had nothing but the highest praise for his two combatants. But throughout the entire show, the name Kimbusa had not been mentioned once. Had they totally forgotten about him? He remains under FSW contract… His paycheck was slid under the door during a Glory commercial break. Although the payment was significantly lower than usual, at least it assured Kimbusa that he still had a job… for now. And perhaps he is beyond caring now. That FSW contract is nothing more than a prison sentence. That FSW contract, and his tie to Steven Caldera, was just as damning as the padlock sealing him inside his 30th floor apartment. Every hour, he had lifted the receiver on his telephone and dialed her number… “We’re sorry, this call cannot be completed as dialed. Please try again later,” came the machinated voice. Every hour he tried, and every time he was asked to try again later. Caldera had not left any room for freedom; Kimbusa would not be speaking with Epiphany. Epiphany is, in reality, everything that Kimbusa is not. While Kimbusa had become the very embodiment of resentment and anger, Epiphany radiates happiness and joy. She seems to be the earthly manifestation of heaven itself; spreading wonder and hope to all those around her. Only those with truly black hearts might resist her beauty… Kimbusa’s rage had melted beneath her power. But Steven Caldera had resisted, and resisted admirably. For a time, Caldera seemed as if he was legitimately concerned for Kimbusa, as if no matter what happened with the tournament, Kimbusa would be well taken care of. But Kimbusa had quickly learned that Caldera’s concern was entirely disingenuous… The only promise Caldera kept was to deny Kimbusa his freedom should he fail to win the tournament. Kimbusa did indeed fail… and he had, indeed, been imprisoned once more. His house arrest could be tolerated. At least he was well fed. But it had now been two months since he last spoke with Epiphany… He had no idea of what had become of his estranged soul-mate. Was she still in Japan? Had she sought him out in America? Or had she forgotten about him entirely? The questions were driving Kimbusa mad. It was at this point that Kimbusa realized that remaining in his prison was no longer an option. One way or another, Kimbusa would have to get to Glory next week, with Caldera’s approval or not. But there was only one open portal out of the apartment… Kimbusa’s 30th floor window was unlocked. But as a five hundred pound man standing three hundred feet above the pavement below, Kimbusa did not foresee a happy ending to that exit. The room is dark… Glory had ended hours earlier in the night, which had finally given away to morning. The four-o’clock moon was shrouded by the Houston haze and the arriving rain, allowing only a small bit of the city’s light to enter Kimbusa’s open window. Kimbusa hadn’t slept; ever since Glory went off the air, Kimbusa had remained awake, staring at the ceiling from the comfort of his silk sofa. The night had afforded him plenty of time to formulate an escape… And still, his best option seemed to be jumping out the window. There MUST be a way out of this room… Looking around, the room seemed to be perfect in its provided amenities. With all the modern luxuries and culinary options available, one would wonder what kind of man would want to escape. But the objects in this room were all fake; they never belonged to Kimbusa, they never served to fill the void. There was only one thing that could satisfy Kimbusa… And she was on the other side of the planet, hidden behind the ironclad barrier of Kimbusa’s own front door. After hours of lying still, Kimbusa finally rises to a seated position, stretching the stiffness from his back. With a sigh, he surveys the room once again. The entire wall opposite the sofa is still flashing with the electronic readouts and displays of his audio and video equipment. Amazing how, in fifty thousand dollars worth of equipment, there was still no way to totally dim the room. Kimbusa slowly stands and walks to the door. With a hesitant touch, he grips the knob and turns firmly… Locked. Kimbusa slowly turns and takes a step back, before returning with a stiff sidekick thrown with all his might at the center of the door. Nothing. Kimbusa slowly slinks down to the floor, leaning against his sealed doorway. With a pained arm, Kimbusa reaches upward and grabs hold of the nearby telephone receiver. With slow-moving fingers, he slowly dials Epiphany in Japan once again. ‘We’re sorry, this call cannot be completed as dialed. Please try again later.’
