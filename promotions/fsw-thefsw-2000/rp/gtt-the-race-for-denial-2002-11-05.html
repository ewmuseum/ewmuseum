GTT · The Race For Denial

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: whoo~! It seems like eternity since Deville was last confronted with such pathetic circumstances. Over the past couple of weeks he's gone from being surrounded by all of the best to finding himself besieged by the very bottom of the barrel. In a futile effort to keep his spirits up he tries to act like his regular self, but being in the presence of such wretched scum inevitably takes a hearty toll on even the most vibrant soul. We'll have to try and make the best of a bad situation, Deville moans in his head, feeling sorry for himself. He always addresses himself like a collective, saving the pathetic embarrassment of hearing phantom voices to those of a lower plane. His tone now is glum with resignation, and his outward appearance reflects that inner feeling as he trudges down King Street in the same white suit he slept in the night before. His hair is combed back, but sans gel; his typically glossy shoes are beset with smudges and scuffs, and his face has the look of a sorry, beaten man. He needs to blend in, after all... Deville is slumming. The tale as he heard it is tragic, inspiring sympathy in even the coldest of hearts. Apparently this area used to be extremely well-developed and prosperous, crammed full of ambitious spirit and golden potential. Workers would huddle around water coolers in offices the city over and whisper about how they would someday be there, eyes wide and brimming with excitement at the prospect. It was arguably the best of its era, attracting only the best and receiving praise by all... It was the talk of the town. Suppose it still is, Deville smirks as he casts a disgusted eye at the dilapidated buildings jutting out on all sides like weather-worn tombstones. Unartistic graffiti lines almost every square inch of wall below eye level, cramming up and jumbling over eachother in an eery representation of it's inhabitants. No cars line the streets as everyone is too poor to afford one, having wasted their money on drugs, and almost every other window is broken or boarded up. The smell of failure hangs on the air like a soiled blanket, and it's repugnance stings Deville's nose. Now all people can talk about is how pathetic it's become... How it's a shell of its former self. He's right, for the most part. However, there are still a great many who haven't visited the area in quite a while and may still be found wondering over its former glory, as if it's been preserved to this day. Being on the run forces people to undergo unfavourable conditions, and for Deville it means hiding out among the likes of whom he normally wouldn't take the time to extort. A warrant for his arrest has yet to be issued, but he is nonetheless certain he is being hunted. Strangely, even here amidst society's most underdeveloped and carnal, he doesn't feel like prey... Quite the opposite in fact. As he spies a group of reckless street youths hanging about the cracked cement steps of foreboding apartment building, he becomes the predator. At least it gives us an opportunity to experience the life of our next GTT victim, he thinks as he turns his gait towards the gang. The five street-hardened young men don't notice his approach until he is almost on top of him, as they are engrossed in a seemingly competitive contest of rapping at one another. Deville stifles a shudder as the abomination these men have made of poetry and music assail his ears, and he manages to force a wolfish smile when they finally see him and stop. The smell of marijuana is thick in the air due to a frail wind, and its effect can be seen in all five pairs of eyes - not just the short-lived redness, but the glassy dullness that can only set in with years of ignorant abuse. 'Whatchu peepin' at, cracka?' one of them pipes up in his city drawl, flashing a crooked set of yellow teeth in a grimace meant to strike fear. Two others stand up and wander around behind Deville, grinning at him as they do so. They're all dressed pretty much identically, with wide pants large enough to fit damn near every professional wrestler in the world hiding their toothpick frames. Every one of them has at least one fake gold ring, and numerous chains dangle from each stack of dimes they try and pass off for necks. The wolfish smile grows at their less than warm greeting, and Deville knows he's found what he was looking for. This would undoubtedly prove the first bit of fun he's had all week. 'Greetings gentlemen,' he begins in his cultured voice, addressing those still before him and plainly ignoring those who sought his back. 'I couldn't help but overhear your singing... I must confess it piqued my interest.' 'Step da fuck back, cracka, ?fore you get stomped,' one of the heroes calls from behind him, audibly sucking his teeth at the end as if to emphasise the threat. Deville starts turning to address the teen when the first one pipes up again. 'Yeah,' another interjects, flashing a full set of gold teeth, 'I don't bust rhymes fo' crackas.' 'Hold up, dawg... Whitey might have skills,' he drawls to his buddies and they all start laughing through their noses. 'How ?bout it, cracka? You got skills?' Deville cocks an eyebrow, 'Skills? Of course I have skills,' he responds with a confused look. 'What type of skill are you lookin' for?' He queries, looking from one blank face to the other. The group just stares at him for a moment, then they explode into another fit of nose-laughing, this time taking it one step further by whipping their hands before them so their fingers slap together and making a cracking noise. Deville just stands there smiling, marvelling at how the lessers communicate their glee. 'Damn honky,' the first one cries between fits of laughter. 'What da fuck you doin' here? Ya obviously ain't got no skills! And you ain't got no ?preciation of our skills, as ya ain't even know what dey are!' 'True dat!' A new one exclaims, stilling whipping his hand like a noisemaker. 'Bitch needs to get steppin', oth'wise bitch gonna die!' He continues, reaching inside his Fubu jacket to grab what Deville can only assume is a knife. 'Alright boys,' Deville sighs, letting the smile wash from his face. 'You caught me... You're a lot smarter than you look. To tell you the truth I'm obscenely disgusted with that verbal diarrhea you insist on desecrating the air with. But that's not why I came over here...' Deville trails off at the five confused faces, giving them a moment to decipher the English language of which they pretend to speak. 'What da fuck you sayin', honky?' the one who spoke second asks. 'Think honky's talkin' smack!' the first one chimes, eyes bugging out of his head. 'That was smack talk? Dun even know what da fuck he's sayin! Crackas is weak!' Another bawls, still cackling from before. 'Why da fuck are ya here, den, honky?' the third one asks, eyes narrowed in suspicion. 'You lookin' for dope?' 'Yes, and I already found five.' Deville cracks and is met by five blank stares. 'Alright then... The truth is, boys, that I've been hearing all sorts of shit about how these rat-infested streets,' Deville pauses and points around, 'make people dangerous, and I just flat-out don't believe it. You've already proven that they make you stupid, but how about tough? I know that my line of work makes you develop a shell, and you must be a killer to survive... I'm a little distraught at the comparisons drawn between me and you... So I want to know if it's true. You guys reckon you're pretty hard, right?' 'You doubtin' us, honky?' the first one asks, almost beside himself with rage now. The on beside him jumps to his feet and whips out a switchblade. 'You think we scared a some big dumb cracka, cracka? I'll slice yo' white ass!' He slashes the knife in front of him a few times, savagely cutting the air. Deville makes big eyes and holds his hands up in mock surrender. 'What da shizzle, crackizzle?' the last one finally manages to slur. 'You think you bizzle? Wanna get kizzled?' It's Deville's turn to be confused. He's heard of such aberrations in the language, but he'd never actually witnessed anyone uttering them. Before he gets a chance to inquire, a pair of hands ram into his back and shove him with all their might. He stumbles forward almost on top of one of the others, who quickly uppercuts him square in the jaw. As Deville collapses back onto his ass, two rush in and begin feeding him the boots. What the fuck?! Deville's mind explodes. This isn't how it's supposed to go! The two suddenly stop kicking him and he feels their arms grab him underneath the arm pits, then finds himself back on his feet. The one with the switchblade saunters forward, flashing the knife ominously. He places it underneath Deville's eye and grins, revealing a full set of full teeth. 'Whatchu know ?bout our pain, cracka? Wanna shed a tear o' blood for us?' The cold steel point presses against the corner of Deville's eye, slowly increasing the pressure so that a small drop of blood does appear. The wielder's shoulders start bobbing up and down as he laughs at his prey, thinking he sees fear in Deville's face. The two men holding Deville up suddenly find themselves on the ground, and before the one with the knife can react his hand is snatched away from Deville's face and squeezed so that he squeals and drops the weapon. The other jump up to help their pal, but then they see the gun in Deville's other hand, which is now pressed up against knifer's throat. Like the rats they are, his friends all run for their lives, deserting at the first sign of trouble. 'Now what, thug?' Deville asks as he wipes the blood from his eye, the wolfish grin reappearing. The thug's eyes go wide and his jaw drops, his lips beginning to tremble as he hears the chamber being loaded. 'Where's your boys now? Hell... Where's your skills?' 'Fuck you, cracka,' the thug manages to cry in a trembling voice, much to Deville's delight. He slowly traces the gun up the guy's face and presses it against his lips. 'Such a nasty mouth...' Deville says softly, moving the gun around in the slightest circle, as if trying to burrow it between the lips. He can literally smell the fear coming off the man, and he actually laughs as a tear drips down from one bloodshot eye. 'What's wrong, thug? I thought you guys were hard? I thought you guys were gangsters...' The thug opens his mouth to respond but the words are choked out as the gun is rammed up against the back of his throat. His eyes bulge even larger as his life undoubtedly flashes before his eyes, and the tears begin to flow freely. His mouth begins working around the barrel of the gun and some sobs ring through the silence. After a moment Deville realises the man is begging for his life, and the mixture of amusement and disgust overwhelms him. 'BANG!' Deville shouts at the top of his lungs, and the thug almost has a heart attack. He collapses to the ground, likely convinced of his own death with his eyes clamped shut, and Deville bends down to wipe the slobber off his gun on the man's jacket. 'Wake up, bitch... You're not dead,' Deville snarls, then spits in the man's face when his eyes flutter open. 'You may as well be, though.' The man seems to consider the thought as Deville turns and walks away, but it doesn't stick in his head long. It's already gone by the time Deville calls back over his shoulder. 'No... You're definitely not a gangster... Or even a thug. All you are is a sentimental bitch.' ~><>...<><~ The man known as Pain drives around in his Bentley, roaming the ?hood he lives in with a sort of condescending pride. Yeah mothafuckas, he thinks, now I'm workin' fo' da po-lice! He's been celebrating being hired to kill one Angelo Deville for the past few days, blowing his down payment on a new car and some hookers to christen it. He's got the radio station turned to classical music, for he's a cultured man now. Left behind are his days of gang-banging and crack smoking... He's hit the big time now... People are finally realising he's more than just a two bit thug. He begins to think back on what he's learned through his experiences, but nothing substantial comes through. All he can summon are the words happiness and pain; whenever he tries to delve deeper his brain wanders to some meaningless and pointless topic. 'That's because you didn't learn anything,' a mysterious voice shouts over the sounds of the instruments, and Pain jump in his seat, startled. 'Who dat?' He demands angrily, pursing his lips together and looking in the rearview mirror. 'Just one of the crazy voices you create so you have someone to talk to,' the phantom responds smugly. Pain presses his foot harder on the gas, growing even more pissed off as he looks all around for the source of the voice. He tries to ignore the voice and just keep driving, but it's persistent. 'I am your brain on drugs!' 'SHUT UP!' Pain screams, practically foaming through his gold teeth. His glance darts out the passenger side window as he looks for a way to escape his own madness, and instead he spots a homeless man lying on the ground. Just as their eyes meet, the bum grimaces. Knowing it must be the bum practicing ventriloquism, Pain slams on the brakes and screeches to a halt. Before the bum knows what's happening, he's thrown up against the wall by a large black man with a bald head and ridiculous purple clothes. The bum is half drunk and his long white hair is caked with dirt and grease. His clothes are tatters of patches from old sleeping bags and blankets, multicoloured and vibrant like a clown's outfit. Pain can't help but feel the need to steal them. 'What's dis ?bout, cracka? You tryin' to hold me down? Always tryin' to hold da black man down!' Pain yells, mere inches away from the bum's face. The bum's eyes go wide and he begins to futilely struggle in the man's grasp, certain this gold-toothed idiot must be trying to rob him of the nothing he has. 'Leave me alone... You crazy?' he manages to mutter as his eyes focus on the loony. 'Leave you alone?! Leave you alone?!' Pain parrots. 'How ?bout you leave me alone? You're the one that's crazy! You! You! No... Me! No! Wait! Argh!' The gibberish starts flowing freely and the bum manages to slip out of the grasp as Pain argues with himself some more. 'Damn drugged out thug... Leave me alone.' The bum asserts again, then breaks into a run as Pain wheels on him and roars at the heavens. 'THUG?! I'm not a thug, damnit!' He says, seemingly forgetting the fact he just assaulted a homeless man for no apparent cause. 'Dis is ?cause I'm black, ain't it?! All niggas must be thugs, right?! Fuckin' racist whitey! I'll show you thug!' Pain begins chasing the bum, and manages to catch him just at the end of the alley. But the bum proves to be too much for him this time, and takes him down with a well-aimed knee to the gonads. Pain doubles over in his namesake, still mumbling about not being a thug. What da fuck jus' happened? his mind demands through the anguish. Racist bum just dealt wit' me but good! But I'm a champion, damnit! I am! I'll show everyone! His mind continues to jump around like a two dollar whore, visiting anything and everything it can find. Especially that Deville! And so the champion lies in the gutter, cupping the swollen balls he received for trying to rough up a bum, asserting over and over that he's not a thug. His pike-like memory relapse kicks in again and he forgets what the hell he was thinking about, along with anything he may have learned in the past little while, and happily succumbs to the beautiful blackness and emptiness that is his only friend.
