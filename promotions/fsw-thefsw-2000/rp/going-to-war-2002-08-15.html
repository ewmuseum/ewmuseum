Going To War

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday Angelo Deville parks his newly acquired Cadillac Deville DTS in the nondescript carpool parking lot off the 401 and kills the engine. Seeing that Mickey is already waiting for him, Deville wastes no time getting out and locking up. Besides, he isn't really in the mood to idle; he's in the mood for action. As always, the man is making a smart Armani suit look good and has his hair groomed to perfection. His blue eyes squint in the sunlight as he makes his way over to Mickey's black Tiburon, with it's engine rumbling smoothly, and climbs in the passenger side. Easing himself into the bucket seat, he casts his cool gaze on Mickey, dressed just the same as him but looking half as good. 'Afternoon, Mickey,' Deville greets the man as he buckles up his seatbelt. Mickey was known to drive like a maniac - a blind maniac. 'Aye, same to you, Angelo,' Mickey returns in a casual tone, casting a glance at Deville's car and nodding. 'When did ya get the new car? And how'd you manage to mess ?er up so soon?' He smiles as he asks the question, but Deville doesn't find the situation at all humourous. 'That there,' Angelo begins in patient tones, 'was a rental car I got myself while I was down in Boston. While I was inside the courtroom attending to business, a bunch of punk kids took a baseball bat to it. So when I took it back to the rental agency, they wanted fifteen grand to repair the damages. Fifteen grand! You believe that?' Angelo asks, scandalised, and Mickey shakes his head. 'So what'd you do?' Mickey baits him to continue. 'I bought the damn thing. Like I'm gonna give them fifteen grand for a car I'm never gonna drive again.' Angelo smirks to show just what he thinks of that idea, then further justifies the purchase, 'Besides, I can get her fixed up for free.' 'Not bad at all,' Mickey says as he engages the car and begins pulling out of the parking lot. 'So, what happened at court? You had that under control, eh?' Mickey asks pleasantly, concentrating on the road and so missing the angry shake that wracks Deville when he thinks of it. 'Don't even fuckin' mention that, Mickey,' Deville snarls. 'That goddamned O'Neil appointed himself as the kid's lawyer and screwed up all of my plans. I could've killed him.' Angelo stares at the road for a moment as they get onto the highway and accelerate up to a conservative 130. 'I should've killed him,' he amends. 'O'Neil? Who's that?' Mickey asks, darting a glance at Deville. 'Some punk from my wrestling organisation. Thinks he's some kind of hero to the people.' This last bit is said mainly to himself, and he gets lost in thought for a moment. 'Don't you worry about him, Mickey. I've got a surprise for him this Sunday... That sonofabitch is costing my a lot of money, and it's gonna come out of his ass.' Mickey has no response to that, and the two drive on in silence for the better part of ten minutes before pulling off the 401 and getting onto a back road. They're forced to slow down as gravel spits up alongside them. Various coniferous trees line both sides of the road, and there isn't a house to be seen in any direction. 'It isn't too far from here,' Mickey breaks the silence. 'Should be a road coming up.' Good, Angelo thinks to himself as he nods. Time to straighten these bastards out. More people who've been fucking me around for no good reason. I'll show them... I'll show them all. As Mickey said, a road appears on their left and they turn onto it. It isn't really so much a road as an extended laneway, and it curves about every twenty feet so you can never see what's coming up ahead. Eventually the trees thin out on the right side and a ramshackle old house comes into view. Several trucks and motorcycles are parked haphazardly on the driveway and the front of the property. There is no lawn to speak of, just an assortment of weeds. 'You ever dealt with the red and white?' Mickey asks, stealing another glance at Angelo as they pull up into an open spot on the driveway. Angelo shakes his head so Mickey continues in a sober voice. 'They're tough bastards, no doubt about that. Just play it cool and shoot straight, and there shouldn't be any problems.' Angelo thinks about asking what Mickey says by 'shoot straight', but decides against it. His own interpretation will do. He sighs as he attunes to the comforting weight of his custom pistol pressing against his chest. Mickey parks the car behind a beat-up old Ford truck with Born to Ride scrawled across the back window in flaming letters. The two men get out and stare at the house for a few seconds, then begin making their way up to the front door. The house itself is a three-story get-up, but it probably hasn't been properly maintained since it was built roughly a century ago. Some bricks are missing, paint is peeling off the siding, and some shutters hang crookedly from their hinges while others litter the ground immediately around the house. Beer bottles are strewn everywhere and Angelo stops counting the firepits scattered about the property after he gets to five. Above the front door - which is more like a door leading into a barn than anything on a modern home - is a sign with the telltale helmeted skull with the golden wings... The calling card of the Hell's Angels. 'Let's use the back door,' Angelo suggests in a quiet voice as they reach the front door, but Mickey waves the suggestion away. 'They already know we're here,' Mickey elaborates, raising a hand to knock on the door. 'Just follow my lead and try to keep things calm.' Scowling, Angelo pushes past him and opens the door without knocking, barging in but managing to move quietly as he does. Turns out Mickey was wrong, as they're immediately greeted with raucous laughter, shouting, and the acrid smell of marijuana smoke. The sounds are fairly close to the main hall, which they find themselves in now. Up ahead, a beer bottle smashes into the holey plastered walls. Mickey and Angelo exchanges a look, then begin making their way towards the room it came from. 'Sounds like a lot of them,' Mickey says in a low voice, but one devoid of worry. He's simply stating facts. Angelo just nods and strides through the doorway, followed closely by Mickey. The laughter abruptly stops and half a dozen men jump to their feet in alarm, some reaching for pistols and any other weapons that happen to be nearby, such as baseball bats and pool cues. There are seven of them in all, mostly older men who've drank a little too much beer in their lifetime and have forgotten to shave for the last couple of years. They all cast a rather intimidating presence, but it's dulled by the glossy sheen in their eyes and their uncomprehending demeanour. The room itself is outfitted with three old couches, a couple of la-z-boys, and a pool table as the centrepiece. Not a very nice place to die, Angelo chides himself as he raises his empty hands in a peaceful gesture. Mickey follows suit once again as he moves up beside Deville and both men stop. Three guns are now pointing at them, and while the men are definitely inebriated Deville would bet his fortune they'd still hit their target nine out of ten times. Better to try and defuse the situation. 'Gentlemen, let's not get ahead of ourselves. I'm Ang-' Deville begins but is promptly interrupted by the largest man in the room, a monster of a man weighing at least three hundred and fifty pounds, and not much of it beer begotten. 'What the fuck do you think you're doin'?' The giant demands in a shout as he waves his pool cue around menacingly. 'You fuckin' suits wanna die today or sumthin'?' The slur in his voice is readily apparent, and he is wavering slightly, but at the same time he seems to be in full control. And he's pissed off. Really pissed off. 'No, not today,' Mickey begins in a pleasant yet hard voice, raising his hands in a soothing motion. 'We just want to-' Another interruption, again by the same man. 'Did I fuckin' ask you, twerp?' His tone and his eyes say that he expects a fight; and wants one. 'Yeah, you did,' Mickey retorts, anger creeping into his voice. 'Try to stay with us here, Goliath, we'll move kinda slow.' The hostility in his voice is now apparent as Goliath's, and the large man's eyes bulge at being addressed in such a manner. 'What'd you fuckin' say, boy?' He demands in hushed tones as he takes a couple of menacing steps forward. Deville promptly steps between the men and flashes his teeth in an ambiguous gesture. 'Woah now,' Deville begins, throwing his voice in a futile attempt at intimidation. 'We're here on business, so let's all just put the weapons away and have a seat, shall we? We're all gentlemen here.' Angelo motions for everyone to have a seat and even manages a heartfelt smile for everyone. Goliath seems to think it over for a minute, then he gets his own disturbing smile. 'The last time I did sumthin' of the gentlemanly sort,' Goliath says, advancing until he's pressed right up against Deville. Goliath stands a good six inches taller, and he has Deville by about a hundred pounds, but there's no concern in Deville's face before the next sentence. 'Was when I was fuckin' your mother.' This gets a huge round of laughter from his cohorts, but he manages to keep his face serious. Deville, admirably, keeps his temper in check and even gives a little smile of his own. 'Oh really,' he states in a hollow voice. 'And what was so gentlemanly about it, you fat piece of shit? You put a bag over that ugly mug of yours?' Angelo queries with a snarl and the laughter abruptly dies off. Goliath seems stunned for a moment, then he bursts out laughing and the other men join in once more. 'I know who you are,' Goliath chokes the laughter off suddenly, his voice deadly serious once more. 'You're that wrestling gangster from television, right? I've been watching your shit for a couple of months now.' 'Is that s-' 'Yeah... You fuckin' suck,' Goliath barks into Deville's face, and this time it warrants a reaction. 'I see you runnin' around extortin' hundred pound nobodies and gettin' yer ass whooped by that flake O'Neil. That guy's been fuckin' you around for weeks now and you ain't done shit about it. You don't seem like much of a gangster to me... Shit, cupcakes? Ya bake ?em yourself, big man?' Another thunderous round of laughter. 'No, no, no,' Angelo says with a chuckle. 'Your sister baked those up for me,' his voice takes on a serious tone as he returns the childish insult. 'Right after I pulled my-' The words are stolen from his mouth as he's forced to dodge a haymaker from the big - and now irate - man before him. He put a hell of a lot behind that punch, because the follow-through makes him stumble enough that Deville is able to hook him into a half-nelson and beat him to the ground via punches to the back of his neck. Goliath struggles, but with this kind of leverage Deville has too much of an advantage. Then a clicking sound is heard, and Deville recognises the sound of a gun being cocked. 'Alright,' he says in a voice wrought with resignation as he begins slowly turning around, still holding Goliath in the half nelson. He turns his head so that only Mickey can see it, then flashes him a quick wink before accelerating the turn and dropping to a knee behind the massive bulk of Goliath. His gun is out and cocked before his knee hits the floor, and Mickey quickly follows suit by drawing his own and diving out through the doorway. One of the bikers fires his gun and Angelo is shocked to hear Mickey scream in agony. Instantly, his gun moves around Goliath and begins firing into the room where Angelo suspects the pistol-holders are. Their drug-addled reactions are all that Angelo had hoped for, and they begin pumping Goliath full of lead before it dawns on them what they're doing. He hears one of the men scream and one of the guns falls silent. Mickey pokes his head and arm around the corner and fires a bullet into the head of the man holding the baseball bat, but before he can fire another shot he is tackled into the hallway by one of the pool players. Cursing, Deville continues firing and listens for the merciful sound of their bodies hitting the floor. After hearing only two, Goliath's body crashes into him and he finds himself smothered beneath it. It and another man's weight. Shots fire out in the hallway and a man screams. Mickey? Angelo wonders in his mind, but the thought is emotionless. All of his emotions have been eviscerated by the surge of adrenaline now pumping through his veins. The adrenaline that allows him to easily shove the five hundreds pounds off of him just as a fist comes crashing down into the wooden floorboards beside his head. Using the momentum from the push, Deville rolls his way over to the wall and whips the gun out so it's facing the centre of the room. The man who had tackled him to the floor has recovered and is currently lunging for his throat. Three quick bullets into the chest make him fall helplessly and uselessly just before Angelo, however, and he is once again thankful of a shield, even if this one doesn't serve as well. At least two more, and one with a gun. I think one has a gun, at l- the thought is confirmed as a bullet whizzes by his head. The pool table has been overturned and the man appears to be hiding behind it. There is no sign of the other man, so Deville assumes he's hiding behind the table as well. There is no sound from the hallway. Deville fires a cover shot into the pool table and relocates to the side of one of the couches, moving as quietly as possible in his rush. A bullet rips through the cushion beside his head, indicating he wasn't as quiet as he could have hoped. Deville ducks down behind the couch so he isn't visible from the pool table and wonders how he can get over there. 'Hey,' a shaky voice calls from behind the pool table. 'It doesn't have to end like this...' The man trails off and Angelo can almost feel the lie it's so thick. A few witty remarks enter his mind, but he chooses silence for the time being. What to do, what to do... His mind idles as it waits for the engine to kick in, then the sound of a real engine bursts through the house as one of the bikers takes off. Or maybe that's Mickey... Would he leave me here? Angelo dismisses the question as quickly as it popped into his mind. He'd known Mickey since he first got into the business, and the man had always been square. The engine roars and Angelo makes his move. Peaking to make sure it's clear first, he crawls over to the front side of the pool table and lays down facing up and towards the door. He can see Mickey laying in the entranceway to the room, with a dead biker beside him. Two small pools of blood are gathering, one for each man. From Mickey's chest? No, goddamnit, he argues, but can't convince himself of the lie. The motorcycle screams off away from the house and the sound of the engine gradually disappears, leaving only the heavy sound of the last biker's breathing. Angelo can hear him moving to the far side of the table, moving towards the door. He gets the gun into the position and holds his breath patiently. Nothing to it... Just wait him out. The very tip of the guy's head pokers around the corner. His black hair is thick and greasy, and Angelo is positive he can see little bugs crawling around in it in his ethereal state. A little more... The first piece of skin is seen as the brow crests the pool table and the man's movements suddenly accelerate... To the ground. Deville fires two shots straight into the man's temple and then continues firing blanks in his shock. Figuring he's the only one left in the house, he climbs to his feet and begins making his way to the hallway, shaking like a virgin about to be deflowered. 'Mickey?' Angelo begs for a response, but gets none. He reaches his fallen comrade and sees no movement in the chest; only a bullet hole and a large bloodstain. The biker beside him has the same thing. The whole house now has the rusty smell of blood and death. Angelo doesn't even give it a moment's thought as he stumbles through the hallway to the front door. He stops on the makeshift front step for a couple of minutes, staring out at nothing as the sun beats down on him. Thousands of thoughts swirl through his mind, but he can't focus or concentrate on a single one. Hundreds of voices speak all at once. Everything is a blur. A buzzing, dimensionless blur. He doesn't know how long he floats inside that storm in his mind, but when he comes out of it he knows time is of the essence. This was just business, the voice in his head is cool and collected now. And it's alone. He plucks a zippo from his pocket and lights in, then tosses it into a stack of empty cigarette packages just inside the door. It was all just business... But now it's personal. You want to nail me on a personal level? That's fine. It's all fine... Now it's war.
