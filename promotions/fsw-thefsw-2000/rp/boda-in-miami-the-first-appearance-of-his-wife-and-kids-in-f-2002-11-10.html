Boda in Miami, the first appearance of his wife and kids in FSW.

10 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/10/02 Watching the news, he pays particular attention to the sports scores. “All right, the Gators won.” Suddenly, a knock comes to the door. Boda gets up, walks across the room, and opens the front door. “DADDYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY” That is how we last left Boda. He just kicked out his latest female conquest, and just in time, because, to his surprise..... “Hello Chris.” Stacey Sloboda pops her head into the door way. Boda, a look of surprise on his face, looks at his two daughters, Kenzie and Carleigh, then up at his lovely wife Stacey. “I told Chip we were coming today to visit you.” Stacey plants a kiss on Boda’s lips, Chris barely able to return the kiss. Kenzie and Carleigh run around inside the hotel room. “So, you told Chip you were coming, huh.....” Chris asks his wife. “Let me call him, and tell him you made it here okay.” Boda picks up his cellphone, and starts to dial Chip’s number. “He’s downstairs. Talking to some sluts. Look like a couple of strippers.” Stacey smiles and laughs. “I guess he’s hard up to get laid or something.” Boda turns on Nickelodean for his two girls, as Stacey pours a cup of coffee. Boda steps out into the hallway, as Chip picks up the phone on the other end. “Goddamn you Chip.” Boda snarls into the phone. “Why didn’t you tell me the wife and kids were coming today?” “Hey.....” Chip tells Boda. “If you’d leave your cell on, I’d of told you last night. But no........ Mr. I’m a screw every girl in Miami has to turn off his cellphone. And now, I got those two girls from the club the other night, standing here, mad, because you can’t attend to them now.” “Tell you what..... Give them some money, and send them on their way. AND GET YOUR ASS UP HERE. This was not the company I planned on today.” Boda hangs up on Chip before the commisioner can say another word. Walking back towards the hotel room door, Chris thinks to himself....... ’God, I hope she doesn’t get on about me flirting all the time on t.v. I mean, she can be a bitch about those things.’ Chris opens the door, and walks into his room. “Man, it’s so good to see you all. I just figured you’d of waited till we arrived in Orlando after the show this week.” “Nah, I needed to get away a bit. Mom is being a nag, and well, I’ve missed you.” Stacey walks over, giving her much bigger husband a hug. “Besides, the way you’ve been hanging all over the woman on television lately, I figured you needed to be with me, a little more.” “Oh, come on.....” Boda rolls his eyes, trying to not look at his wife. “You know it’s all part of the new gimmick. I’d never cheat on you, not ever. You are my life.” Chris leans down, and kisses Stacey. “Besides..... there isn’t anyother woman for me.” We can see Stacey get all teary eyed, and Chris, well, Chris being Boda. The door to the hotel room opens, as Chip O’Meara finally makes his way to Boda’s hotel room. “Sorry I’m late.” Chip says to Chris. “Hello Stacey, girls.” Chip waves to the little Boda babies. “Chris.... we need to talk about this weeks plans. Got a minute? You know I put you in a match against Jason O’Neil” “I know. I could care less about getting to beat his ass this week. But, Chip” Boda smiles and nods. “I got a better idea. Seeing as it’s been a long time since I’ve seen Stacey, and since it was such a surprise that she came today.......... why don’t you take the girls to the zoo, and let me and Stacey spend some quality time together.” “THE ZOO?” shouts Kenzie. “I LOVE THE ZOO. PLEASE, CAN WE GO? PLEASE?” Both girls get real excited. We can see Chip’s eyes roll, as if he is not happy about having to do this for Chris. “Sure girls.” Chris remarks. “Uncle Chip here will take you to the zoo, and maybe, if you’re good..... a movie. Mommy and me need some time to talk about a few things. Have fun.” So off run the girls, followed by Chip. Boda shouts to Chip as he walks out the room. “Thanks Chip. We’re even now.” Chip shuts the front door, as we see Stacey kneel down infront of her husband, and a smile caresses Boda’s face. [to be continued]
