My tolerance level is being tested here...

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-26-02 -----Tolerance. The ability to accept others for who they are. Despite the things they do to make you angry, or just plain get on your nerves. Tolerance is something that is a necessity in this world. You have to accept others despite their shortcomings. But sometimes, tolerance is tested. Sometimes, you are forced to tolerate losing to the same no-talent hack TWO TIMES due to outside interferance. That's about the time that tolerance is launched right out the window.----- ***Nicole Roberts stands in the kitchen of the palatial Tucson home that she and her husband Aaron bought a few years ago. She's wearing a long t-shirt, probably Aaron's, and not much more. Her left leg is pulled up, and making a four shape against her right one. As Aaron walks into the room, he stops short at the door, and gazes into the kitchen at his wife. This is their fifth year of marriage, and Aaron still adores her. Still loves to just look at her. Her long hair is pulled into a little pony tail at the back of her head... so is his. He wanders over to her, and wraps his arms around her. Insticntively, she returns to embrace as though she knew he was coming the whole way. He still adored her. But there was one thing he absolutely hated about her, and he had a feeling he was going to get a taste of it right now...*** 'How's it going at work, Aaron?' asks Nicole with an inquiring tone. 'You know how it's going, I tell you how it's going, I'm sure all your friends inform you of how it's going... so why do you insist on asking me week in and week out how things are going?' Aaron answers questions with questions often. It diverts the attention from whatever it is he wants to divert attention from. 'I'm not trying to nag, or something, I just keep hearing that you're taking big falls, and doing stupid things,' Nicole calmly replies. 'I'm a professional. It's what I do.' 'Yeah, yeah, be a hard ass. I know that's what you want everyone to think you are. But Mary was telling me just yesterday that she saw this match you were in where you were strapped together with some other guy... and then a third guy came down and jumped you--' 'I was fine,' says Aaron, cutting off his wife... but she's persistent. 'AARON! You were bleeding! She told me! And I know you don't do that cutting your forehead crap, so that was REAL! Don't lie to me about it!' exclaims Nicole. 'You know, this is your one fault. The one thing that I cannot stand about you is this crap. When I tell you I'm alright, you just believe me. Just stand back, be supporting, and believe me. I won't do something that I know will get me hurt!' With that, Aaron turns and leaves the room. He grabs his keys from the table beside the door, and storms out, 'I'll be back soon.' ***Nicole watches her husband storm through the front hall, and grab his keys angrily. He snarls something at her, but she isn't listening. Later tonight, he'll show up back at the house and be genuinely sorry for his behavior tonight. Aaron has always been one to point out everyone else's flaws, but ignore his... ignore the fact that even without alchohol in his system, he flies off the handle a lot. No, he isn't an alchoholic. Not by a long shot... his temper is bad enough that you would think he is, however. Nicole knows that Aaron adores her... she knows that the sun rises and sets on her. She realizes that without her, his life wouldn't be complete. She has just always wondered if he knew all those things were true. A small tear falls from Nicole's right eye... she wipes it away slowly with her left index finger. Stares at the door for a few more seconds, and heads into the kitchen. The eggs she was cooking are burnt now...*** 'Ugh. And those were the last three I had.' -------------------------------------------- ***Aaron slams the door behind him, and heads down the front porch steps. He jingles the keys around in his hands for a few seconds until he locates the key for his Acura RSX. He unlocks the driver's side door, and slides into it. You can hear his aggrivation in the ignition as he ferociously turns the car on. The engine roars a little bit, then settles as he slides the clutch in and puts the little car into reverse. He pulls the car around, and puts it into first gear, driving down his long driveway towards the gate. As he nears the gate, Aaron pushes a button on a little plastic box above his head. The gate swings open with an 'I need oil' sound, and Roberts speeds out of the driveway as the gate begins to close once again.*** -------------------------------------------- 'Shot of Cuervo 1800. And give me a Corona with a lime twist to chase it,' says Aaron Roberts to the man standing across the mahogony bar from him. ***Aaron had come across town to a small bar that he frequented. Everyone there knew who he was, but treated him no differently because he was a wrestler, or famous, or rich. He loved it here. The bartender was a young man... he seemed younger than Aaron. Probably around 24. Dark hair, bright eyes, probably considered pretty attractive... but Roberts isn't interested in men. All he cared was that the boy knew how to pour a bottle of Cuervo. He had never really believed himself to have a problem. He never really got drunk... just took the edge off a little bit with a few shots, and a beer or two. Nothing really powerful. His only flaw was a wife who nagged too much. But, for the life of him, he could not stop loving her. She was exactly what Aaron had been looking for all his life. Stability. Love. Kindness. She didn't judge him... except when it came to his profession. She hated him wrestling. But, as they say, she'd have to get over that sooner or later. Aaron's eyes scan the bar, looking for people that he knows. But not too many people are at bars this early in the day. Maybe he did have a problem--*** 'Here you go,' the bartender says, cutting off Aaron's thoughts in mid-stream. 'Thanks,' replies Aaron as he slaps five dollars on the table, 'and keep the change.' ***Aaron's mind begins to wander to Wednesday night. As he swallows the shot of tequila, he smiles as the memories of being hit with a chair by Leon Chambers slowly fade. As he chases it with the Corona that he ordered, he feels fabulously as he forgets that he's jobbed the Jack Brooze two times now. Yea, everything's starting to go Magnum Wolf's way again...***
