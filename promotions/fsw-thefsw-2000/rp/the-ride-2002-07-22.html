The Ride

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/22/02 Everyone loves a roller coaster. From the race up to a dizzying height to the stomach churning drop, your sense of adventure calls you to it. The sensation is a siren song of stomach churning excitement. It's the loss of control that's so inviting, the fact that you're ripped from your routine and even your normal equilibrium. Up is down, down is up and you don't have the first guess about side to side. And if you're lucky, your fun won't be spoiled by getting sick. But sometimes things go wrong and that safe little adventure takes you somewhere you never wanted to be. ~ 'God damn that stupid little sheep pimp,' Lo-Key spat. In his hand he held a letter, one of dozens from Ming. He tossed it into the trash can as he had the others and shook his head. 'What the Hell did I do to deserve the rabid Chihuahua of wrestling trying to basically hump my leg?' He glanced around and said, 'Good thing there's no one to answer that.' He paced over to the answering machine and watched it blink once... twice... three times. Three messages. And he was betting none were anything he wanted to hear. [beep] 'Mr. Thorson, this is the manager of the Hyatt Regency. We were shocked to discover the level of damage you managed to perpetrate on our hotel and would like you to know that if the repair bill is not paid in full we will be pursuing legal action against you. Please contact us at once and advise us of your intentions.' Oh yeah, I'll settle up. Last thing I need is another tick on my rap sheet. You're lucky it was just property damage though. Everyone is. [beep] 'Lou, pick the phone up if you're there.... ok, play it that way. Your parole hearing is next week so I wanted to give you the heads up. I hope you've managed to keep out of trouble... for a change.' Ah Dmitri, my slimy Turkish lawyer, looking out for my interests to the tune of $300 an hour. As long as he gets me through these endless, boring, insulting parole hearings I don't care. It's demeaning to be checked up on like you need a baby-sitter just to get through life day to day. And it's too simple to nod and smile and walk free to do whatever harm is on your mind. I should know... [beep] 'Lo-Key! Hey man, it's Paul Orion. Listen, I know that tag match may not be your first pick of things to do right now, but just think of the ratings! And by the way, your parole officer called today. I gave him a good report and told him you were a great worker for the company... didn't stir up too many waves or pick fights with management. And I know you wouldn't wanna make a liar out of me, now would you? Heheh... see you at the arena pal.' Lo-Key stood staring at the machine through narrowed eyes as a low growl escaped him. His color rose a little, the low boil in his blood showing through. 'Son of a bitch!' He took a deep breath and muttered, 'Gotta keep it under control.' His shoulders rose and fell a few times and he finally seemed to calm down and said, 'Dammit. It used to be so simple. I could bash a few skulls, inspire fear and then move on, knowing that people were well aware that they couldn't fuck with me without dire consequences. Now it's all different.' He paced across the room and sat slowly on a black leather couch that creaked against his denim clad legs. 'Without a name that means anything to anyone and a reputation to fall back on, I've had to start form scratch,' he said and sighed. He picked up a small black dagger and sat back, balancing it on the end of one finger lost in thought. 'I wouldn't mind it if not for the fact that I have to try and play well with others,' he snorted. 'And I just have to wonder if Orion really knows what he's getting into. Maybe I should take the time to inform him. Maybe I should let him know that he's going to be the one responsible for the destruction of three people and not just two.' Slowly a smile crept over his face and said, 'This isn't going to be the ride that Ming bargained for, nor anyone else. With Ramey and Fro-boy out of the way and Ming on the shelf, there won't be any reason to waste my time with them anymore. Maybe... things are still simple, just different. Never let it be said that I've forgotten my way to the top.' He chuckled and tipped the dagger over onto a table where it fell, a tiny red stain on the end. ~ Step aboard if you dare, and see where the ride takes you. But be warned, you might lose more than your lunch.
