Toys-R-Keons

22 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
|Toys-R-Keons|
'X-Clusive' Keon Karolina | 07/22/01
[ The camera fades in on a busy Monday morning, on 34th street in Charlotte, North Carolina. A man in a suit is arguing with some security guard about a parking meter. The jailhouse is on the right surrounded with beautiful green grass. Flowers are cropped up in certain parts of the field before the entrance of the jailhouse. On the left is another building that has a parking lot full of nice business cars. Over the top of the building Erickson Stadium appears in the distance. Definitely Charlotte. A squalling tires sounds breaks the argument with the guard and the business man. A Hummer appears, zooming in from behind. The black H2 Hummer beholds the hometown kid who came straight off a hot victory and a steel chair shot from The Norwegian Superstar. Keon, wearing shades and his small dreadlocks, both ears pierced with diamonds in them, he parks in a reserved parking space. The camera listens in at the two men who begin arguing again. ]

Man: That’ Keon Karolina!

Guard: And he parked in a reserved parking lot of the jailhouse.

Man: He doesn’t work there.

Guard: Not unless he is a part time lawyer or bondsman.

Man: No, Keon is a full time wrestler. Did you watch it ?

Guard: Yeah, I did, it was a pretty good show. But I spilled all of my donuts when that Mexican guy attacked Keon with a chair.

Man: He is Norwegian. But he disgraced our town. Keon faces him next Friday in a Empty Arena match.

Guard: But FSW is going to be in Chicago.

Man: So...? Keon faces TNS next week.

[ Keon gets out of the car and walks toward a lumber store on 34th Street. The cameraman quickly moves to Keon to get some words. ]

Keon Karolina: Why am I going to this lumber store? Of course, going to get some toys to take to the arena. Already? Damn skippy. Keon Karolina is already preparing for the match-up right here in Charlotte Town, North Cackolacky. But, who do I go against? The Nor-Crapping Shit. Oh yes, the boy that ran in after I beat the shit out of Adrian Paige and attacked me with a chair. But its all in the raisin Friday, when I show TNS and every other superstar in the FSW that I’m not going to take any bullshit like that. Its not going to happen in my league. And everyone who tries to interfere with me...knows that there is hell to pay. Thats enough of words.

[ Keon walks into the lumber store as the camera fades out and the two men are left speechless. ]

( OOC: Sorry for such a short roleplay! Trying to get things started off! )
