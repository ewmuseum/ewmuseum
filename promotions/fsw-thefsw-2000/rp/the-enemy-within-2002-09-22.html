The Enemy Within

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-22-02 -Hmm, the night has gotten a bit colder now. Must be getting to be the fall…Sad, so the days of summer have once again passed.- Timo Bolamba sighed as he sat upon his plush recliner, sprawled out across it like a Roman Emperor. The falsist grapes being fed to him were mere thoughts in the wind that passed through his long loose hair, yet still carried a meaning to them that was true as it was the day he had began in this sport. -My shoulder hurts a lot lately, I must be getting older. The big time can really take it out of me, it would seem. Surely that same breeze that floated hither through my hair would know of such a pain? To and fro through its violent little life it scurries in and out, hot and cold. I guess humans are more like the breeze than we think.- Closing his eyes he could see images passed before him of the days when he was young, and the times he once had, but really, little of that mattered now. Timo had started a boy and became a man in this game, and he would probably die a man to this game. -Such a cruel fate we bestow upon ourselves, little breeze. You are so carefree, yet you have so many obstacles, how do you do it? The broken pane of glass you slips through nary scratches your hide, and you would frolic through it as if it were not sharp at all…Yet the very same pane would cut me deeply, perhaps even to my death.- Stars still hidden by the clouds shone in the sky, and the wisp of a breeze grew to a fully shrill wind. The cold nettle stung Timo’s face as he looked forth into the window that they entered from, yet he admired them all the same. -You display a lot of courage my friend, winding around me, taunting my warm breath to face you in a war you are doomed to lose. My eyes cannot see you, yet my face can feel you as you slap me, itching me on to fight you, yet…I cannot, for I am intrigued by your cruelty.- The plaid curtains that hung upon his wall fluttered with the entry of the foe that had baffled Timo, and even though he did shiver some, the cold was a welcome change to his lavish life that was so warm. It did remind him of being young again, in many ways, and that was quite a good feeling. He wanted to stand, but instead he lay still, enveloped in the cool air. He sat staring at this open window, deep within a thought that disturbed him deeply. Something he could not put past himself. Why was he worried so about his life? What was it that drove him on? -Timo old buddy, this is indeed your mind talking, and we would thank you kindly to quit poking at us with Q-Tips. You know why you cannot close that window. It is so clear to you, yet you stand here looking at it like a child looks at a candy store. You desire that sting, you desire that pain don’t you?- He cocked his head, feeling the melodramatic thought race in his mind, cackling, taunting him forward into the depths of his self induced madness. The circle he ran was now showing a groove in the sand, worn from years of struggling with it. -Just close the window Timo…it is that simple.- “I can’t you fool!” He shouted this brazenly into the desolate empty night. The house was silent, nary a soul would tread a path to his aid, nor did he want it. They were all gone for now, having left on their own endeavors, leaving him to his foolish madness, and this silly stupid wind that would torment him so. -Then you will continue to feel its stinging, the cold burning hitting your senses and painfully slapping you, mocking you as your so called “stardom” you have thought yourself into having is jeopardized. You know the answer, and it is not I who is the fool.- “Ye you still torment me brain, but do you have any idea of whom you speak to?” The wind settled and a smile settled upon his face. The voice had stopped protruding his conscious, and now he seemed content for the most part. Then with one sudden swoop, the howling wind had picked up once again and threw its icy grip around his torn judgment. It now did more than slap him, it was insulting him! “Damn you and your icy grip you foul fiend, what do you want with me? I am just a man trying to do my job! All I want is what I deserve god damnit!” -That is exactly what you will receive then Timo, for you desire more than you think.- He stood now to face the window with the wind that stung him, and it pushed at him, throwing him backwards, flinging the mighty warrior to the ground, belittling him, insulting him. He crawled on his knees, tears streaking his face as he cursed this demonic wind he struggled with, fighting it with his soul now. “Go back to hell where you belong!” -YOU KNOW THE ANSWER! LET IT INTO YOUR HEART AND BE FREE OF THE PAIN!- “I CANNOT! You stupid arrogant beast of a thought! What permits you to torment me so? What gives you the right to throw salt into my wounds when I am so much more than one perceives? I worked my entire life for this, you have NO right!” -WRONG!- The howling commenced and it now carried with it icy shards that cut him deeply, making the so called hero cringe and fear his own creation. Timo cowered in fear, the gaping maw of uncertainty hanging above him now, smiling as it made to swallow him whole. “What do you want?!!” -Understand you fool! Understand what this is about!- He looked at the great force that had drove his seemingly unstoppable pride and self image back to the point that it would be made to look insignificant as a small rodent, and he gritted his teeth. He let forth a guttural roar from within that not even he knew existed. “You want to fight me! Come on then, lets do it!” -Yes! That’s it!- “Talofa Mother Fucker! You now speak to a man who has nothing to gain! You now speak to a man who has nothing left to fear! You cannot scare me, and you cannot drive me into this madness anymore you son of a bitch!” He rushed forward, impaled by the icy torrent of frozen rods that were slung through the air by this demonic cold he faced. Timo bared his teeth and flung back, his own burning inside of himself pushing back the doubt and despair he had faced. He no longer cringed at his loss, but benefited from its experience. “Come forth and face me now demon, for you stand cowering now that you can no longer intimidate me!” The truth of his words flung a harbinger of fury towards this night air. He stood now at the sill, and he looked at the meager offering of a breeze that now tried to sting him…and he laughed. “You cannot hurt me anymore, for I do not desire to be something I am not, cruel wind. I will let my paths come to me, and I no longer fear the scorn of my pride being hurt. I am a man Damnit, not some sort of boy who thinks he needs to champion a cause to be the best warrior.” He reached for the top of the pane, and smiled just before he spoke a final time. “Go home, and never trouble me with your greedy ways again, you bastard.” The window slammed shut, and Timo awoke from his bed, groggy, but fully aware of what had transpired in his dream. He sat up slowly and blinked a few times, wondering what he just had come across, and then, it was all clear to him. -My friend, you have done well tonight, go now, and remember the lesson you have learned.” He reached his hand do his face, wondering if at all this had been real, or merely one of his delusional dreams. When he pulled his hand down though, the crimson blood from a small gash in his cheek made him smile. “Well, I guess that’s something you don’t see every day…”
