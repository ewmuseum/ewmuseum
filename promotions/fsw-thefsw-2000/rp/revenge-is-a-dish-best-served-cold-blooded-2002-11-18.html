Revenge is a dish best served cold....blooded.

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/18/02 **This RP is also used for PCW's Alliances as well, but serves to cover FSW business as well.** Fffffffthud As the camera fades into view we are focused on a picture of the current CEO of PCW, Xavier Kannon. There is a dart stuck smack dab in the middle of the picture, seemingly protruding straight out from Kannon’s nose. We quickly pan to the left to reveal the thrower of the dart as none other than Tyler Nelson. The former CEO of PCW looks across at the photo in complete disgust. “Xavier Kannon,” says Nelson disdainfully. “My brightest pupil, my trusted ally, my worst enemy….one man managed to become all three.” Our setting is the office of Nelson within the confines of the Greed, INC high rise building on the outskirts of Dallas, Texas. Having resolved, or more accurately beaten, the charges brought against him by the federal government this office is now home once again to the Greediest Player in the Game. The lavishly decorated office is nothing short of spectacular, with every amenity that a man of such power and wealth could want. From the priceless original artworks and the handmade sculptures all the way down to the handcrafted leather furniture and the marble floors, the office reeks of overindulgence and gluttony. “You may have learned a lot from me, Xavier,” Nelson seethes. “But you haven’t leaned everything. I taught you enough to survive, but I don’t stay the Greediest Player in the Game by making someone else greedier than me. There is one more thing to teach you, and I’m going to give you the lesson first hand this Sunday.” Behind Tyler, seated at one of the fine black leather couches, is Tyler Nelson’s Mob Rule associate Angelo Deville. His Universal Title rests on the couch to one side, and his FSW Primetime Championship lays on the other. He sits forward on the couch, busily fiddling with something. There is a briefcase laid open on the glass coffee table in front of him, and his silence is rather uneasy. Nelson reaches over to his mahogany desk and picks up another dart. He lines up and takes aim, tossing the sharp projectile toward the dartboard again. Fffffffthud The dart, ironically red in color, sticks right between the eyes in a photograph of OSW World Champion ‘The Russian Bear’ Ivan Stanislav. A dismissive snort from the former 2-time PCW World Champion. “And you, Ivan……you stupid Commie son of a bitch.” The hatred seeps from the very pores on Nelson’s body. “You dared to dessert PCW, you dared to dessert ME! I made you what you are today. I brought you over from Russia and turned you into the propaganda machine you have become. It was all because of me. The Red Army, the World Title, the reign of terror…..all because of Tyler Nelson. I gave you the money. I gave you the power. I gave you everything and how did you repay me? You stabbed me in the back and went to the competition. You got in bed with that whore Jane Reagan, not that you were the first or last.” A sick chuckle from Nelson before he continues. “You had everything on a silver platter,” says Tyler with a confused tone. “All you had to do was destroy anyone in my path. But you couldn’t do it, could you? As simple a task as it was you couldn’t get it through that thick Russian skull of yours to just to what you were told and shut the fuck up!” The heightened tone in Nelson’s voice causes a casual glance from Deville, who then goes back about his business on the couch. The focus is a bit blurry so we can’t exactly see what he’s doing. Tyler takes a deep breath and regains his composure. “Xavier Kannon. Ivan Stanislav. Two men who have chosen, and chosen wrongly I might add, to make an enemy of Tyler Nelson. There may have been small victories littered on the battlefield, but it is inevitable that I shall win the war…..and the war ends this Sunday with the eradication of all who would choose to meddle with Mob Rule.” Nelson picks up yet another dart, but instead of aiming it at the board he grits his teeth and curls his lip as he hurls the dart toward the board overhand, like a baseball pitcher. The dart speeds through the air almost like a bullet… Fffffffthud ….and hits on a photo of Timo Bolamba and Mike Marvelous, arm in arm and all smiles. “And you two, our supposed ’partners’.” Tyler pauses as he turns away from the dartboard and heads over toward the couches where Deville is seated. Nelson’s Mob Rule cohort leans back in the leather couch, sinking slowly in it’s comfort. He raises a black object in the air and we hear the distinctive ‘click’ of a gun cocking. “I’m saying that we take care of that little problem right now,” a coldhearted Deville says. Nelson grins as he sits on the couch opposite of Deville’s. “Certainly an attractive option, but I think we need something a little more….,” Nelson ponders for a few seconds. “….Universal.” Deville cocks his head to the side slightly. “I’m not sure that I catch your drift. You’re not suggesting…” Angelo glances down at his Universal Title belt. “No, no, no…,” says Tyler. “I meant something that can take care of all of our problems at once. We will have every top star from every top promotion in the business at our mercy on Sunday. We will have the opportunity to bring the wrestling world to it’s knees, bowing to us in servitude. We will have the chance to show the world exactly why the Mob Rules.” “I catch what your saying now,” says a deviously smiling Angelo Deville. “You don’t want a .9mm, you want a gatling gun. Not a problem.” Both men erupt into evil laughter as the camera pans back and fades out simultaneously.
