The Can-Am Connection Reunion Special!

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 19, 2002 [Scene opens in a large auditorium with stadium seating. The place is packed full of people, and they are all seemingly awaiting the start of some sort of show, because the large red curtains are still closed. Suddenly, the red curtains begin to slowly part to reveal an empty stage. After a couple seconds, one spotlight comes on to reveal a large M on the left of the stage, and another spotlight comes on to reveal a large J on the right side of the stage. Footsteps are heard on the stage until 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous steps into the spotlight in front of the M, and 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson steps into the spotlight in front of the J. The audience bursts into applause. They are both holding what looks to be many gold belts. Mike picks up a microphone and grins at the audience. An audible groan is heard as the whole crowd groans as they shield their eyes from the brightness of his teeth. He begins to speak] Mike Marvelous: Greetings ladies, gentlemen, and MARVELites alike! Tis' I! Your Canadian Hero! Jacob Jackson: And tis' I, your Idol! Mike Marvelous and Jacob Jackson: Tis' we! The Can-Am Connection! [Audience cheers] Mike Marvelous: Jacob and I are here to entertain you, the fans, as well as celebrate the reunion of our tag team, since our friendship never did die! Jacob Jackson: We are also here to celebrate the achievements of...well...us. As you see, Michael and I have taken the liberty to buy the replica belts of each title we have won in FSW over the years. Mike Marvelous: And as you can see, we're having a hard time holding them up! [Jacob wobbles and almost falls over, but Mike grabs him by the elbow to keep him from falling] Jacob Jackson: Whoa! Thanks for the hand, Michael! I almost got killed by my own accomplishments! Mike Marvelous: Anytime, Jacob, anytime. Anyway, ladies and gentlemen, what you see is 4 gold belts a piece! That is 8 championship belts in total! Each of us individually has held more gold than anyone in FSW history, except ourselves. Jacob Jackson: And each of us combined have held more gold than any single man on this roster can even dream of! [Each man, who has two belts around their mid section and two belts in their hands, look at each other and grin] Mike Marvelous: Oh boy, do we have a show for you today! Jacob Jackson: Yes, but first, I have to put a couple of these belts down before my arms rip out of their sockets! Mike Marvelous: Me too. Ha ha...we just have so much gold! GOLD! GLORIOUS GOLD! [Mike begins to take one of his world famous gold ceisures that he used to take whenever the Can-Am Connection won any new titles] Jacob Jackson: Michael! MICHAEL! It's not REAL gold! They're only replicas of the gold we won before! [Mike's eyes open and he stops shaking. He gets up and dusts himself off] Mike Marvelous: Party pooper. [Jacob and Mike throw the belts off stage] Mike Marvelous: Now let's bring out our first guests! Jacob Jackson: We like to start things off with a bang...so ladies and gentlemen...please welcome...George W. Bush and Jean Cretchien! The President of the United States and the Prime Minister of Canada! [Bush and Cretchien walk out onto the stage dressed in suits, waving to the audience. They walk up to Mike and Jacob and shakes hands with them] Mike Marvelous: It is such an honor and a priviledge to have you two here today! Jacob Jackson: One could say that you are the political form of the Can-Am Connection! Ha ha ha.....ha. Nobody's laughing... .......... Mike Marvelous: Oh, I get it! Ahahahaha! George W. Bush: Riiight. Jacob Jackson: Well, we are really glad, Mr. Bush, that you could take time off from defending our country against the terrorist threat, and taking it to the teliban over in Afganistan! And Mr. Chretchien...we're glad you could take time off from...whatever it is you do... [Chretchien continues waving at the audience] George W. Bush: The pleasure is all mine, Jeremy Jackson. Jacob Jackson: Umm...it's 'Jacob' Jackson. George W. Bush: Of course it is. Anyway, I have always been a fan of the WWF, and I'd like to say that I watch you two every monday night. You remind me of our US Marines...always kickin' butt. Keep up the good work, boys. Mike Marvelous: Umm...we're from FSW. And WWF is not longer WWF...it's now WWE. And our weekly show in on Wednesday...not Monday... George W. Bush: Ha Ha Ha...that's what I've always loved about you, Matt Marvelous. You've always been a jokester. Mike Marvelous: It's 'Mike'... George W. Bush: Of course it is. Mike Marvelous: Moving on...how are things with you, Prime Minister Chretchien? [Cretchien speaks in his french accent in very poor English in his very hoarse voice] Jean Chretchien: Things. Things are...you know...great. Merci. Common ce va? Mike Marvelous:....I'm good. So, are you a fan of FSW? Jean Chretchien: Omelet du Fromage. Jacob Jackson: Umm...how long have you been watching..? Jean Chretchien: Omelet du Fromage. Mike Marvelous:....Riiiight....so-- [George W. cuts Mike off] George W. Bush: Sorry to interrupt, but I've prepared a speech-in French, might I add- to present to Prime Minister Poutine to express America's friendship with our neighbours to the North. Mike Marvelous: Oh, of course. By all means, Mr. Bush. [Bush clears his throat and takes out a piece of paper] George W. Bush: Moi est...vou cest mon qui te le bon parle. Ce sous le pour von avec...mon casier. De prélevec son tu chausseur....Merci beaucoup. [George Bush bows and waves to the audience while doing his trademark smirk and his trademark squinty eye face. Jean Cretchien, on the other hand, looks angered at something...] Mike Marvelous: Umm...President Bush...you just called Prime Minister Cretchien a 'Hemaphroditic sex fiend with a foot fetish and a hairy nipple'... George W. Bush: ...huh? [Jean Cretchien begins to swear at George W. in French] George W. Bush: Hey...whoa there, Mr. Cretchien. It's an honest mistake. Jean Cretchien: Tetê le Merde! George W. Bush: Whoa, there, Mr. Poutine! Don't say anything you're gonna regret! Jacob Jackson: Gentlemen...please! Let's just calm down a bit here, huh? [Jean points at Bush and musters the best English that he can] Jean Cretchien: President Bush...ce le poo poo pants! POO POO PANTS! George W. Bush: Oh, it's on now, Frenchie! [Bush reaches over and snatches Cretchiens toupee off his head] George W. Bush: Ha ha ha ha. Nice rug, pissant! [George's smile disappears as Cretcien kicks him in the gut and nails him with a stunner. Jean sits on Bush's chest and begins showering him with lefts and rights, but Bush rolls over and hits some lefts and right of his own] Mike Marvelous: Mr. Bush! Mr. Cretchien! Please...break it up! [Both men fight to their feet. Cretchien chops Bush across the chest a few times] Audience: WOOOOOO! [Bush gains the advantage with a thumb to the eye] Jacob Jackson: My god! What a dirt underhanded tactic by George Bush. The president lifts the prime minister up into a fireman's carry and OH! F-5! F-5! MY GOD! AS GOD AS MY WITNESS, THIS MAN IS BROCKEN IN HALF! Mike Marvelous: Jacob! Don't provide commentary! [Bush stands at Cretchien's head, kicks him in the shoulder...waves his arms back and forth, runs to one side of the stage, then the other, then he runs over to Cretchien's prone body and nail him with the people's elbow!] Mike Marvelous: JUMPIN' PIE YIMINY! [Bush raises his arms in the air as the audience cheers, but as he is doing this, the 57 year old Cretchien spineroonies to his feet and when Bush turns around, Cretchen nails him in the groin and hits him with a scissor kick!] Jean Cretchien: Tu est dig that? SUCKAAAAA! [Bush gets up and both men brawl until they are off the stage. Mike and Jacob just stand there in silent awe as the family cheers in approval] Mike Marvelous: Ladies and Gentlemen, I apologize for that. Jacob Jackson: Don't apologize, Michael! It's only 15 minutes into the show and already these people have seen the greatest tag team in the world as well as America and Canada waging war on each other! Mike Marvelous: You're right! WE ROCK! Jacob Jackson: Now for our musical guest...making his return on OUR show...the latino sensation...Riiiiicky MAAAARTIN! [Ricky Martin comes onto the stage in a black suit but he looks horrible. It looks like he's gained 70 pounds over the past 2 years. The music begins and he begins girating his pelvis] Ricky Martin: LIVIN' LE VIDA LOCCA! LIVIN' LE VIDA LOCCA! SHE'S LIVIN' LE VIDA LOCCAAAA-OOOOOW! [As Martin shakes his hips, a loud *CRACK* is heard followed by a scream. He falls on the stage] Mike Marvelous: Oh no! This is horrible! Somebody get a stretcher! Jacob Jackson: Looks like his hip swivelling days are over... [The paramedics come in with a stretcher and carry Ricky Martin away] Jacob Jackson: Well, so much for our musical guest... Mike Marvelous: I have an idea! Why don't I just get my good friend Captain MARVEL to sing a song! He's backstage...all I have to do is go get him! Jacob Jackson: Ok... [Mike runs to the back. A loud swooshing sound is heard and out comes Captain MARVEL with a microphone.] Jacob Jackson: Hi Captain MARVEL, where's Mike? Captain MARVEL: Oh, he's gone to the little boy's room! OH-HAH-HAH-HA! [Captain MARVEL smiles] TING~! Jacob Jackson: I understand you are going to sing a song for us today. Captain MARVEL: Yes! I'm going to sing the Captain MARVEL theme song! Sung to the tune of 'Camptown Ladies'! Hit the music! [The small band beside the stage begins to play as Captain MARVEL raises the mic to his lips] Captain MARVEL: Captain MARVEL has arrived! Do daw....do daw! Captain MARVEL has arrived! Do dee do daw day! He's here to save the DAY! And make the bad guys PAY! Captian MARVEL has arrived! Do dee do daw day! He kicks evil in the sweets! Do daw....do daw! He kicks evil in the sweets! Do dee do daw day! He's here to end disMAY! He-also-fights-cavities-and-tooth-deCAY! He kicks evil in the sweets! Do dee do daw day! He is such a super guy! Do daw....do daw! He is such a super guy! Do dee do daw day! He scared the fiends aWAY! Even if you are GAY! He is such a super guy! Do dee do daw day! [Audience cheers as Captain MARVEL bows] Jacob Jackson: Bravo! Can you sing that again!? Captain MARVEL: Ha ha ha! You're a funny little lad, Jacob Jackson. Have a lollipop. [Captain MARVEL extends his hand to Jacob and gives him a little red lollipop] Jacob Jackson: WOW! Thank you Captain MARVEL! Captain MARVEL: You're very welcome! And AWAAAY I GO! [In a flash of speed, Captain MARVEL is gone from the stage. A few seconds later, Mike Marvelous walks out from behind the curtain and takes his place on the stage once again] Mike Marvelous: So, what'd I miss? Jacob Jackson: Captain MARVEL came out, sang his theme song, and..and...he gave me a sucker! Mike Marvelous: WOW! You are SO lucky! Jacob Jackson: I know! Mike Marvelous: For our next act, we have the comedy stylings of Byron 'Love Machine' Caaaalver!!! [A smallish, timid lad about 17 years of age with glasses and a neat haircut walks out onto the stage and takes the microphone. He clears his throat and the mic squeaks. He taps the microphone with his finger] Byron Calver: Umm...testing...testing...one, two, three. *SQUEEEEEAK* [Byron's hand begins to shake and his knees start knocking together] Byron Calver: Uh...w..what do you get when you c-cross a porcupine w-with a b-balloon? [Silence] Byron Calver: Umm...P-POP! [Silence. Byron adjusts his glasses and clears his throat once again] Byron Calver: *Ahem* Uh...knock knock. [Silence. Mike feels sorry for Byron so he answers] Mike Marvelous: Who's there? Byron Calver: Boo. Mike Marvelous: Boo who? Byron Calver: Heh..d-don't cry. [Silence] Byron Calver: Geez...t-tough crowd...ok. I g-got one for you. What's the difference between r-roast beef and p-p-pea soup? ........ Byron Calver: Anyone can r-roast b-beef, but it takes a t-talented p-person to p-p-pea soup. [The mic squeaks. Criquets can be heard outside the auditorium. A dog barks off in the distance] Mike Marvelous: Umm....give it up for BYRON CALLLLVEEEER!!! [Byron bows. A bottle misses his head by two inches and crashes against the wall. He walks off the stage] Mike Marvelous: Well...that was...interesting, don't you think, Jacob?....Jacob? Jacob Jackson: *ZZzzzzzz* [Mike nudges Jacob, who has fallen asleep standing] Jacob Jackson: For our next act, we have an eating competition between Hannibal 'The Cannibal' Lecter and OPRAH...WHINFREEEEEY!] [Oprah walks out onto the stage blowing kisses to the audience, and Hannibal is carted out onto the stage on a hand cart. Security remove his mask with care, cuff his hands, and remove his leg restraints.] Mike Marvelous: Before we get down to the eating match, can I get a few words from our competitors? [Oprah takes the microphone from Mike. She seems to be crying] Oprah: *sniff* privelidge to be on your show, Mike. [Blows nose] May the best person win. Mike Marvelous: Oprah...why are you crying? Oprah:...I don't know, honestly. It's a habit I got from blubbering like a moron on my show every day. Well, I'm ready for a eatin' contest! Let's kick this bitch into overdrive! I'M HUNGRY! Hannibal: Oprah...after I beat you in this contest...I'm going to eat your liver...with fa fa beans...and a nice ciante! FEH FEH FEH FEH FEH-- Oprah: Shut up with your 'FEH FEH FEH' bull shit, cracka! Sounds like you tryin' to suck on yo mommas tit or somethin' ya artsy fartsy British fag! Hannibal: You're very brave. The last person who talked to me like that, I ate his-- Oprah: Bring out the food, god dammit! Oprah's hungry! [Two men wheel out a tabel with many dishes on it. The first course is a bowl full of hotdogs. The second course is a bowl full of hamburgers, and the third course is a bowl full of steaks] Jacob Jackson: Ok, contestants. On your marks...get set...GO! [Hannibal digs his face into the bowl of hot dogs and begins eating them. He has one hotdog gone....two hotdogs....three hotdogs.] Mike Marvelous: Look at Hannibal go! Oprah hasn't even started yet! [Oprah looks at Hannibal, looks at the dishes, then she inhales every bit of food in 5 seconds, including Hannibal's] Jacob Jackson: HOLY HECK! OPRAH JUST INHALED THE FOOD! OPRAH WINS! OPRAH WI-- [Oprah looks at Hannibal, sneers at him evilly, then opens her mouth] Mike Marvelous: NO OPRAH! YOU'VE HAD ENOUGH! [Oprah inhales deeply once again. This time, she inhales Hannibal. She makes a few funny faces, then coughs up a skeleton] Jacob Jackson: GOOD LORD! OPRAH JUST ATE HANNIBAL THEN SPIT OUT HIS SKELETON! Mike Marvelous: This is the most revolting thing I've ever seen! [Oprah does the raise the roof sign until suddenly, a long, pink tongue wraps around her and seconds later, Oprah is gone and Rosie O'Donnell is standing on the stage with a large bulge in her stomach] Rosie O'Donnell: *Burp* [Rosie walks off the stage] Mike Marvelous: What a horrible turn of events... Jacob Jackson: I guess this goes to show you that there is always a bigger fish... Mike Marvelous: Well, that is all the festivities we have for you tonight, ladies and gentlemen, but if you want to see your Canadian Hero and your Idol, all you have to do is watch FSW Glory and go to the FSW site to view our promos! Jacob Jackson: Nexy Glory is going to be a good one, so be sure to tune in! Mike Marvelous: There are two matches to determine who will go at it the next Glory for #1 Contendership to the FSW World Title! In one match, it's Timo What's-his-name vs. Johnny Riverdance. In the other, it is your Canadian Hero vs. Big Daveylicious. Jacob Jackson: And in the main event, your Idol goes up against the Canadian Criminal for the spot as the special guest referee between the number one contendership match! Mike Marvelous: After Canadian Criminal ruined the return of the Can-Am Connection last Glory, I wish I was taking him on in the main event. Nobody hits your Canadian Hero in the head with a baseball bat and gets away with it! Plus, we had that match won. Jacob Jackson: But we have a little announcement to make...MIKE WASN'T THE LEGAL MAN WHEN HE WAS PINNED! That's right Chris Esso, Irving, Shell, whatever your last name is. Your little plan never worked. The Can-Am Connection did NOT get defeated. But there is going to be heck to pay for you, buster! What makes you think that you are even fit to step in the same RING as your Idol?! I've been winning gold since you were yay tall! You might as well lay down and let me pin you on Glory, because it will be a lot easier for you. Mike Marvelous: And Big Daveylicious...I want you to know that when I beat you, it'll be nothing personal. Just business, you know? It's alright though, I'm sure you can go win the IC title or the Primetime Title! You'll be a star one of these days, Big D. But next Glory, you're rise to the top will have to wait. And Timo Balooga...Johnny Riverdance...I just want YOU two to know that it doesn't matter which one of you wins next Glory! Either way, the outcome will be the same! I will be the number one contender, and I will once again be FSW World Champion! Jacob Jackson: And Michael, when I become special guest referee for you number one contendership match, I will be calling it right down the middle. Mike Marvelous: Of course, Jacob! I wouldn't expect anything different from a great sportsman like yourself! [Jacob winks at Mike and Mike winks at Jacob] Mike Marvelous: Well, ladies and gentlemen...that's all for now. Until next time...brush your teeth...pay your taxes...drink 6-8 glasses of milk per day...but most importantly... Jacob Jackson: WORSHIP US! [Scene fades to black as Mike exits to the left of the stage and Jacob exits to the right of the stage.]
