The End of Pop'n'Fresh

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Wednesday, July 31st, 2002 [Scene opens once again in the home of 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous. Three voices can be heard coming from the kitchen. The camera enters the kitchen to see Mike Marvelous and Jacob Jackson standing beside the counter. In front of them on the counter is...none other than...Pop'n'Fresh, the Pillsbury Dough Boy. But Pop'n'Fresh doesn't look his usual cheerful self...] Mike Marvelous: Finally, you're going to pay for all the crappy products you put out on the market! [Pop'n'Fresh replies in his squeaky, nasal voice] Pop'n'Fresh: What...what are you talking about? Jacob Jackson: The toaster strudels that toast on the outside but stay cold on the inside...the packets of icening that cover maybe a quarter of the strudel! Mike Marvelous: The apple turnover dough that you can't even get out of the freakin' can! Jacob Jackson: The holiday cookie dough rolls that have no destinct markings so you know where to cut the cookies! Mike Marvelous: You're evil, Pop'n'Fresh...and it's time to put an end to you! Jacob Jackson: We're going to make you pay for your crimes! Pop'n'Fresh: You're both crazy! [Jacob grabs the little dough boy and holds him while Mike continuously pokes him in his belly] Pop'n'Fresh: Woo hoo! Woo hoo! Woo hoo! Woo hoo! Woo hoo! Woo hoo! Woo hoo! Woo hoo! Woo hoo! OK! STOP! PLEASE STOP IT! I ADMIT IT! I intentionally made crummy products that never cook right and are hard to get out of the package! I'm a horrible person... [The dough boy breaks down crying and Mike pokes him again...but he accidentally pokes too hard and his finger goes through Pop'n'Fresh's soft dough mid-section] Pop'n'Fresh: OW! You dickweed! Look whatcha did! Mike Marvelous: Oops! Jacob Jackson: So...Pop'n'Fresh...you admit to your crimes... Pop'n'Fresh: Yes! I'm guilty! Mike Marvelous: The jury has reached it's verdict, Jacob. Jacob Jackson: Proceed. Mike Marvelous: We the jury have found Mr. Pop N. Fresh....GUILTY on all counts. Jacob Jackson: Then I have no choice but to sentence you to DEATH by oven! Pop'n'Fresh: NO! PLEEEEASE! Spare me! Have mercy, judge! Wait...I'd like to pleed insanity! Yeah! I'm crazy! Jacob Jackson: You have to do that in the beginning of the trial, dummy... Pop'n'Fresh: Oh....poop. [Mike opens the oven door and Jacob throws Pop'n'Fresh in. Mike then slams the door and cranks the temperature up to 375 degrees as two small hands thump against the glass of the oven door.] Mike Marvelous: So...do you wanna go watch some t.v., Jacob? Jacob Jackson: Sure. [Mike and Jacob walk out into Mike's den and turn on the t.v. Immediately, two lesbians flash up onto the screen, licking each others tits and moaning like wild animals. Mike quickly presses 'Stop' on the vcr, then 'Eject'. He grabs the tape and throws it out the window then looks innocently at Jacob] Jacob Jackson: Michael...was that a pornographic video I just saw? Mike Marvelous: No...it was...umm...Women's Syncronized swimming. I always watch reruns from the olympics... Jacob Jackson: Oh...I, uh...didn't know you were a fan of syncronized swimming.... Mike Marvelous: Oh yeah...a big fan. *DING!* Mike Marvelous: The timer just went off! [Mike whispers to himself] Whoo...saved by the bell... [Mike and Jacob walk back into the kitchen and open the over door. Mike takes out the cookie rack with his oven mits and puts it on the counter. On the tray is a Pop'n'Fresh cookie....since he was baked alive. Mike breaks the cookie in half] Mike Marvelous: Want some, Jacob? Jacob Jackson: Sure. [Jacob pops the legs into his mouth as Mike chomps the left arm off the upper torso.] Voice: Mmmmmph! Jacob Jackson: Wait...do you hear something? Mike Marvelous: Meh...probably nothing... [Scene fades to black as Mike closes his teeth over the head of the Pop'n'Fresh cookie, which seems to be wearing an expression of pure horror.] *CHOMP!* The End.
