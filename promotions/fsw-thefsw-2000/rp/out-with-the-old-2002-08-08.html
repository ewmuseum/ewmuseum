Out With The Old

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday 'Fuck Deville,' snarls a low-pitched voice with a thick Italian accent, 'where the fuck has he been for the last few weeks?' The voice asks in an exasperated tone. The voice belongs to a burly man dressed in a gray pin-striped suit. Two cold black eyes stare out from underneath his heavy brow and thick, slicked-back black hair, darting between the three other men in the room. 'I'll tell you where he's been, Paul; the mick son-of-a-bitch has been on national television, using all of that useless muscle to kick the tar out of old women,' a calm and slick voice responds. This man is much thinner than Paul and seems to be able to control his emotions as much better as he does his appetite. The only thing they have in common is the suit... And the eyes... And the hair... And the accent. 'Hold on, Adrian,' begins another voice, this one strong and forceful, with a touch of warning in the words. 'That's just wrestling. It's all fake; bullshit. Angelo would never attack a wom- well, let's just say that it's all staged and leave it at that.' The third man is dressed much like the other two, but he appears to be much older and has shorter hair that he doesn't gel. 'If that's so, Mickey, then why is he the subject of a police investigation and bringing heat down on the family?' Adrian challenges in that same calm tone and the room goes silent. The room they're in is large and sparsely furnished. A large oak desk cluttered with papers dominates the back half of the room, with it's plush leather la-z-boy behind and two arm chairs at angles in front. There are a few plants, a woven rug, and a grandfather clock, but besides that the room is essentially bare. There is but one entrance into the room, a large set of steel-enforced double doors. The only other thing of note is a large painted portrait hanging behind the desk. The man in that portrait is the fourth individual in the room, and he is presently reclining in the la-z-boy, idly regarding the ceiling as if it were playing a dull movie he had seen a dozen times over. Paul and Adrian are standing off to his left side about three paces away from the corner of the desk, and Mickey is sitting in the chair on the right. The silence continues for several long moments with the first two exchanging angry glances and the latter examining his suit. 'Deville is a good kid,' the man behind the desk declares, keeping his half-open eyes locked on the ceiling. His voice is almost in contradiction to his face and body, being soft and melodious. He heaves a little sigh and leans forward in his chair, finally gracing the others with his eyes. 'He's just a little rough around the edges.' 'Rough around the fuckin' edges?!' Paul demands incredulously, taking a few hasty steps forward. 'What about that shit in Quebec the other week? What about that time with the fuckin' Jap tourists? What the fuck do you mean, ?a little rough around the edges'?' The man is shouting by the end of his tirade, and before he realises what he's doing he brings one fist crashing down onto the desk. His eyes bulge as the thud registers in his mind, and he remembers himself enough to take two careful steps backward. Adrian is feigning indifference and Mickey finds something really interesting on his suit to mess around with. The man in the recliner purses his lips and arches an eyebrow at Paul, who stumbles over the lip of the rug and barely saves himself from falling. 'Forgive me, Mr. Salvatore,' Paul pleads in a much more composed voice with a bow of his head. 'It just angers me when I see some young mick jeopardising everything we've worked so hard to get over all these years. He doesn't follow the rules.' Amazingly, the man manages to voice an entire thought without cursing up a storm. Mr. Salvatore eyes him coolly for several seconds before responding, much to the man's discomfort. 'Deville might do things a little differently than we're used to, but he gets the job done. So he has a bit of a temper... Don't we all?' Mr. Salvatore pauses for effect and the other man blushes satisfactorily. Besides that, Salvatore thinks to himself, the mick is our top earner right now. You can't argue with results, but... 'He's a liability to the organisation,' Adrian pipes up again with his salesman's air. 'To you. To us. To everyone. We all know he brings in a lot of money, but he does it in a highly unorthodox fashion, and I don't think I need to remind anyone in this room that it only takes one hole to sink the whole bloody ship.' 'He's just a little wild, that's all,' Mickey interjects. 'He'll learn the ropes and settle down in no time at all. I see big things for this kid,' he pauses and stares hard at the two men standing together, 'so long as no one hamstrings him before he's ready.' 'Forgive me for speaking so bluntly,' Adrian steps forward with his calculating eyes locked on Mr. Salvatore's, ignoring Mickey. 'But perhaps it would be better if we never give Angelo the opportunity to become that hole.' The threat hangs heavy on the air, and after a moment Mr. Salvatore begins nodding slowly. 'You can't seriously be entertaining this idea, Mr. Salvatore!' Mickey exclaims. 'You see the potential there... We all had our reckless days, they're what developed us into the men we are today.' 'Enough,' Mr. Salvatore says quietly as Paul opens his mouth to object, and the room goes deathly silent once more. Mr. Salvatore leans back in his chair once more and again focusses his attention on the ceiling-movie, clasping his hands over his gut and taking slow, deep breaths. After only three or four seconds, he leans forward again with a smile on his face. 'Here's what I'm gonna do... Mickey, you are now the official shadow of Angelo Deville. You're going to take him under your wing and guide him to be more like, well, more like me. From now on you're responsible for the man's actions.' Mr. Salvatore leaves the threat half-unspoken, but everyone gets the gist, especially Mickey. 'Yes sir, Mr. Salvatore,' Mickey replies, seemingly unruffled. 'But Mr. Salvatore,' Paul begins to protest but is interrupted by the phone. Mr. Salvatore glares at Paul for an instant, then picks up the phone. 'Hello.' A brief pause. 'Send him in,' Mr. Salvatore says with a smile and hangs up the phone. 'You were saying, Paul?' Mr. Salvatore arches an eyebrow at the man. 'I know your decision is the right one, Mr. Salvatore, but,' Paul stammers, then turns his attention to Mickey. 'You just make sure you tell that mick bastard to-' 'You can tell him yourself,' Mr. Salvatore interrupts. 'He's on his way in right now,' he smiles at the men coldly, and it grows at the look of concern that falls over the faces of Adrian and Paul. Just then the double-doors burst open and Angelo Deville swaggers into the room, dressed in an immaculate white suit and with his blonde hair slicked straight back. He casts a quick glance at the two men standing off to the side and dismisses them just as quickly. Flashing a huge smile, he makes his way to the front of the desk and gives a friendly nod to Mickey. 'Angelo,' Mr. Salvatore begins, 'so nice of you to finally drop by.' The sarcasm in his voice is palpable, but there is a seriousness to it that doesn't elude Angelo. Wisely, he keeps quiet and stands until Mr. Salvatore motions for him to sit down. 'Thank you, sir,' Deville beams as he takes his seat. 'There's an explanation for my absence, and I'm sure you'll deem it fitting if only-' 'Don't worry about it. Besides,' Mr. Salvatore waves a hand at Adrian and Paul, 'I've already been brought up to date on your activities.' Deville's eyes narrow momentarily but he doesn't look back at the two men. Regardless, they shift their feet and exchange a glance. 'I hear you're having a bit of trouble with the law.' 'Hardly,' Deville snorts, turning his head to Mickey and flashing a wink. 'There was a bit of a situation with some bottom-feeder who feels I owe him some money, but I've taken care of all that. It's certainly nothing for you to concern yourself with, Mr. Salvatore.' Angelo smiles again as if to reassure the man. 'You've taken care of nothing!' Paul interrupts from behind him. 'You're presently being fucking sued by this bottom-feeder, and the police are investigating you with regard to his attempted fucking murder!' Deville's back straightens at this, but he still won't dignify the man with a look. 'You know as well as I do, Mr. Salvatore,' Angelo states in a level voice, still smiling, 'that the police have trouble finding their own wives' cooches. As far as this lawsuit is concerned, the plaintiff is in a coma and the lawyer he had just committed suicide. Our hearing is scheduled for later on in the week, and I'll likely be the only one there. Like I said: I've taken care of these things.' 'You'll have to forgive Paul, Angelo, he's just concerned that you're bringing to much attention to all of us, what with your appearing on national television once a week.' Mr. Salvatore says, casting a withering glance at Paul. 'Not to mention your apparent addiction to murdering people for no reason, like those young boys in Quebec,' Adrian amends. Angelo simply ignores the comment and keeps his attention focussed on Mr. Salvatore. Boy's got more brains than these two fools together... Let's hope I don't have to blow them out of his skull, Mr. Salvatore thinks as he admires Angelo's handling of the situation. 'I'm glad to hear you're not worried about any of this, Angelo, and I hope it stays that way. It would be most unfortunate if I were somehow to become involved.' Salvatore warns. 'Of course. I must continue to stress that you needn't worry about my, ah, exploits, regardless of what you might hear.' Angelo almost casts a glance back at the two monkeys standing behind him, but catches himself just in time and crosses his legs instead. 'Good, I'm glad, because I need you to go back into Quebec to sort out a little issue with the Red and White. You're familiar with our arrangement?' Mr. Salvatore ignores Angelo's cringe at the mention of Quebec. 'Yes sir,' Angelo answers honestly, recalling his last trip there. 'Good,' Mr. Salvatore booms. 'Mickey here will be along with you anyhow, so if there's anything you're fuzzy on he'll have the answer. I want this to be handled in a civil manner, Angelo.' Mr. Salvatore glares for a moment to impress the importance of this. 'We don't need a war.' 'You've absolutely nothing to worry about, Mr. Salvatore.' Angelo declares with a grin as he and Mickey exchange a nod. 'If that's all then I-' 'That means you're going to handle things the old way, Deville... You're not supposed to act like some mindless thug from some fucking ghetto somewhere.' Paul interrupts with a sneer. Angelo's eyes bulge at being compared to those tactless monkeys who fancy themselves as gangsters, and he rises from his chair. 'Excuse me a minute, Mr. Salvatore,' Angelo manages a smile as the boss nods. Mr. Salvatore leans back to observe as Angelo turns and stalks over to where Paul is standing. Paul is a few inches shy of Angelo, but the man still outweighs him by at least forty pounds. Neither man seems very concerned about the other. 'What was that, Paul?' Angelo asks, getting uncomfortably close to the other man. 'You fuckin' heard me ya little mick prick,' Paul snarls and seems pleased when Deville's mouth opens momentarily in shock. 'I said you might want to try doing things the old way, try to maintain the fucking prestige that we've enjoyed in this outfit for who knows how long. Remember the good ol' days? I want ?em back... With or without you.' 'Without me?' Angelo asks rhetorically, resisting the urge to strangle this man with every fibre of his being. 'You listen up, you tired, outdated piece of crap... This isn't the old days. Your time has come and gone, and as much as you want to resist it and cling to the top, it isn't going to happen. This is a whole new era, and I personally will not allow it to be handicapped by nostalgic old pricks like you who can't bear to adapt. We're moving forward... With or without you.' 'You- You better watch your mouth, mick,' Paul stammers and finally manages to shout, growing red in the face and beginning to shake with adrenaline. Angelo seems to feed off of it and leans in even closer so that their noses are almost touching. 'No, you'd better watch your mouth, old man. You ever call me a fucking mick again and I'll kill you.' Angelo spits through gritted teeth, fed up with taking shit about his ancestry. Paul scrutinises the man for a moment and then figures he either isn't serious or doesn't have the balls to do it. 'What was that... Mick?' Paul somehow manages to smile while he growls this, then spits on Angelo's shoe. Paul starts laughing and begins turning his head to Adrian for support, but Angelo's hands whip up from his sides and grab his double chin and the back of his head. There is a sickening snap as Angelo twists Paul's neck like a bottle-cap, and the fat man falls to the floor. Mickey and Mr. Salvatore both jump to their feet, and Adrian takes a couple of steps backward. Deville glares around at all of them. 'I've no time for this shit,' he says coldly, lost in the euphoria that always follows the deed. 'If these old bastards can't keep pace with the changes taking place... Let ?em die.' Angelo pauses for a moment as all three men stare at him, speechless. 'Everyone needs to start handling their own business... You all know I'll handle mine.' Deville casts one more disgusted glance down at the limp body of Paul, then turns on his heel and strides out of the room. The large double doors swing shut behind him with a bang, and the echo seems to hang about in the following silence like a gunshot. Mr. Salvatore is the first to recover, and he sits down heavily in his chair. 'Adrian... Adrian!' Mr. Salvatore shouts and the man snaps out of his daze. 'Get rid of that body... Now.' Mumbling, Adrian begins doing as he's told, slinging the huge body up onto one narrow shoulder and stumbling towards the doors. As they close behind him, Mickey sits down and shakes his head regretfully. He and Mr. Salvatore stare at eachother for what seems like hours, though in actuality only a minute passes. 'A new era, huh?' Mr. Salvatore asks, then mimics the head-shaking. 'We'll give him a shot... See how he handles the red and white situation. If he can pull this off... Good. If he can't...' Mr. Salvatore considers for a second, then shrugs his shoulders. 'If he can't then he's dead.'
