Frustration

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 22 Note: This is only to lead into a future roleplay I plan to write for my next match. Wednsday night. The door swings open. Chris stomps his way out, aggrivated and pissed. He is still in his wrestling attire as he slams the door shut behind him. Tommy Gunn walks up to him an tries to calm him. 'Chris, calm man. They blind sided us. It isn't anything major.' Tommy pleads. Bagwell shoves Gunn aside and throws his bags at him. Chris screams violently, 'YOUR RECORD WASN'T SACRIFICED! IT WAS MINE! BECAUSE OF THAT GOD DAMNED DAVIDSON.' He continues marching away from Gunn. Gunn picks up Bagwell's bags from the ground and tries to chase after him. Bagwell continues in the direction of the parking lot to his Rent-a-car. Tommy tries to reason with him, getting him to think straight again, 'So what, Chris. You lost one mother f*cking match. ONE MATCH. And it was a screwjob. Who in the hell cares.' Bagwell stops dead in his tracks. Tommy, for a second, thinks he caught his attention. He comes up on the side of Bagwell. Tommy repeats himself, trying to imprint the idea into Chris' head, 'Its just one loss. A cheap loss, at that. It hasn't tarnished anything. It doesn't CHANGE anything. You've still got your entire career ahead of you.' Chris takes Tommy by the shirt and holds tightly. He gleams down into his eyes. Tommy gleams back and can see the fire burning within his soul. He can feel the anger through his blood. He can smell the hate seeping from Chris' pores. 'It's not about what the books say. Its about the mind, ' mummers Chris softly. He releases Gunn and shoves him back a bit. Tommy sees it as a lost cause. He allows Bagwell to continue forth to his rent-a-car. The car is not shown, but instead the face of Tommy. The vehicle door can still be heard slamming shut. Tommy softly mumbles to himself, '...and still no Rebecca...' Bagwell speeds away, off into the distance. Tommy stands there, motionless...speechless. Shift.
