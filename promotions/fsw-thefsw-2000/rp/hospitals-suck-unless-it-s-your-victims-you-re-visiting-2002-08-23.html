Hospitals Suck... unless it's your victims you're visiting.

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/23/2002 **Making an impact..... that’s what it was all about. Now, after debuting on Glory, and laying the proverbial boots to ‘The Gunslinger’ Paul Orion, the dirt sheets and the internet will be ablaze with talks of ‘BODA IS BACK’. And dammit, why not. He deserves it. He deserves the attention. So long has he been out of any spotlight. So long has anyone, fans and wrestlers alike forgotten the once mighty PCW 2 Time World Champion. But, not anymore. Now, the man whose had monumental feuds with the likes of MeanStreak, Duke Williams, Vampir Noseferatu, Ivan Stanislav, Jason Snow, Miyagawa and Timo Bolamba is back. Back in the saddle, back to kick ass, back.... to make another run at becoming a legend in another federation. And who’s going to stop him in FSW? NOT A SINGLE PERSON!!** “Can I help you?” says a cute red head sitting behind a large information desk “You sure can baby..... we are here to see Paul Orion. He was brought here a little while ago.” responds a cocky, young , brash Chip O’Meara. Hours ago Glory went off the air. All laid witness to what will become a new, improved and better ERA for FSW. All eyes watched as this young Chip O’Meara trashed verbally, the reputation, the claim to fame so to speak of the CEO of FSW, Paul Orion. Then, something hit Orion like a tractor trailer hitting a Ford Pinto head on. Boda. Shocking everyone, the former owner of RUSH appeared, and laid to rest, any doubts that this new Commish was to be taken lightly. As quick as ever, as if he never lost a step, Boda jacked Orion up, and crashed him to the ground with the now infamous BOUNCED CZECH. Now, as if they actually cared, Chip and his hired gun, Chris ‘Boda’ Sloboda arrive at New Orleans General, where the CEO was taken after the attack. “Are you family?” asks the red head. “Yeah.... we’re his ......we’re his sons. And we are really worried about his health” lies Chip. Hospitals have never been a place Boda liked to visit. Sure, he’s sent his share of opponents to many top notch hospitals nationwide, hell even world-wide. But to visit one, always makes Boda a little edgy. Maybe it’s the whole death thing. Or perhaps it’s some sort of bad childhood memories. What ever it is, we can watch as Boda seems to be pacing in the lobby as Chip lies in an effort to have the two ‘visit’ the fallen CEO. “Well, it looks like he’s been taken to a private room.” the red head tells Chip, as she looks up from her computer, “It’s room 420. Take the elevators to the 4th floor, and head to the right. Last door on your left.” As Chip walks off, he motions for his protection to follow him. Boda walks past the red head, who just kinda stares at this monster of a man. Without as much as looking at her, Boda barks a resounding “BOO”, which makes for one jumpy receptionist, and one laughing monster of a man. “That wasn’t very nice” laughs Chip. “Who ever said I have to be the nice one?” retorts Boda, causing a little more laughter from his companion. “Let’s get this over with. I hate hospitals” “Sure thing pal.... I just need to talk to Orion.” Chip says with a smile as he pushes the elevator up button. “Yeah, and you know what Chip.... I never got a chance to properly introduce myself. That was a little rude of me.” A smile crosses Boda’s lips this time. As the two men chuckle among themselves, they wait for the silver doors of the elevator to open. Standing there, staring in the reflective doors, Boda reminisces about times past, especially surrounding the months in PCW that Boda and Timo went one on one so many times. Back and forth, the two traded victories. New Blood against Wild Dueces. The feud was spectacular. The matches, to steal a line from FSW... FIVE STAR. Neither man ever gave an inch, but both took a mile. Of course, how the feud played out in PCW, everyone knows. Timo faded like a blown lightbulb, and Boda went on to World Championship Gold. Perhaps, as he’s heard before, Boda is the reason Timo never won the big one in PCW. Did Boda outshine The Samoan Silencer? Most say yes, many say no, but Chris Sloboda knows the true answer, and to him, it’s the only one that matters. And to prove how much of a man he is, he’s asked for his first match in FSW to take place this coming GLORY. To settle some unfinished business. With a ding, the doors open, and the two men slide inside, pushing the little white button with the black #4 on it. The doors shut gently, and up goes the elevator. Moments later, it stops on the fourth floor. Stepping out, the two new guard of FSW walk down the right hallway, and stop just outside room 420. The buzzing of cardio monitors and oxygen can be heard coming from the room. The door opens, and out pops an orderly. “Oh, hey, can I help you guys?” “Yeah, we just came to visit that worthl....... I mean our dad.” covers up Chip, almost blowing his ‘cover’. “How’s he doing?” “Well, looks like he took a hell of a lot of trauma for a man his age.” The orderly responds. “But he will live.” “DAMN” Boda shouts out. “Err.... I mean, DAMN, this sucks. He’s such a good dad, and I hate to see him in this kinda shape.” “Well, if you two want to go see him, make it quick. Visiting hours are almost over” the orderly directs Boda and Chip before walking off down the hallway. “Let’s go visit ‘dad’. After you Chris.” Chip holds the door open for his ‘brother’. Inside the little room, two beds, one bare and naked. The other, occupied by Orion. Laying there, machines and gadgets hooked to him, he looks almost like a masterpiece of a fight that he lost. Boda and Chip step into the room, with Chip standing next to Orion’s bed, and Boda plopping down on the empty bed. “Hey Paul.....”smiling cockily, Chip talks to the non responsive Orion “how’d you like the rest of Glory? What? You missed it? What happen? You get run down by someone? So, how you feeling friend? A little beat up? Well, I’d say so, you look like shit. Wait, actually... you look like 60 year old man that got introduced to my enforcer, the hard way. But, since you never got properly introduced.... let me have you meet the future of FSW.” “Hey Gunslinger..... how’s it hanging?” Boda jumps to his feet, approaching the bed that Orion is laying upon.”I’m Chris Sloboda, but my friends call me Boda. Now, my enemies, they don’t call me anything. Rather they call an ambulance so there is one ready for them when I get done with whooping their asses. And speaking of asses.....how’d it feel to be the first FSW member to receive the BOUNCED CZECH? I can see you’re to broken up to talk about it, so I’ll take my leave. Just a word of advice though...... when you come too, and are ready to return..... find a suitable replacement for Timo Bolamba. I don’t see him making it much longer with me around now.” “That was Boda.” Chip whispers to Paul’s ear. “He’s short on words, but well, his actions speak volumes. Chris... I’ll meet you in a second outside. I want to make sure Paul here understands one more thing.” Boda nods his head, and walks out of the hospital room. Opening the door, Boda sees the orderly standing right outside the door, listening. The scene fades out as Boda asks a simple question of the orderly. “Can I reserve the empty bed next to him?” “Why?” responds the orderly “Because after I’m done with Timo Bolamba,.....” Boda smiles sadisticly “He’ll need a hospital bed to recover in.”
