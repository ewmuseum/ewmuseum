Stubborn

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/30 “How is he Doctor?” “Good. He’s still clinically brain-dead, but, that could very well change. He’s been responsive to touch, and other senses, so I guess It’s just a matter of time.” “Any word if it was the cupcakes from Deville?” “None. We’re not even sure what he ate that day. This boy’s either got an immense resistance to Cyanide, or whoever tried to kill him got some weak stuff.” “Interesting. Thanks, Doctor.” Jason O’Neil stands over James, looking at his lifeless body. The machines that keep the poor mans body functioning beep and whirr while James lies frozen in time. Thomas Brock steps forward, as if out of the darkness, and puts a hand on Jason’s shoulder. “Jason, you’ve been here every day for a week seeing him. I know you’re concerned, but…” “Thomas, have you received word yet?” Jason cuts off Mr. Brock, as he speaks. “Yes. Everything is fine. All the papers have been sent to the proper channels, and It should be a matter of time before it’s cleared.” “Good. I hate paperwork. Does Angelo or his lawyer expect anything’s coming?” “I doubt it. Unless they’re sniffing around like…Well, maybe they do. I have no idea. Have you heard from Mister Deville?” Jason laughs a little and looks at James, who stirs faintly. “Yeah. I’m sure I’ll hear from him Wednesday in the ring too.” “Why are you still getting in the ring with him? You know you’re going to be marked for death, man. Hell, he’s probably already drawing the skull over your name now.” Jason puts his hand to his chin and looks to the left. He looks into the eyes of Brock, with a cold, icy stare, and smiles faintly. “Thomas, were you a bully, or did you get bullied a lot?” Thomas looks taken a back by the question, clears his throat, and answers, “I guess I was a bully. What’s that hav-.” “Everything, Thomas. Everything. Angelo Deville is a bully. Plain and simple. He thinks he can bully me into giving into him, and he bullied James into this hospital bed. I’ll be damned if he gets away with anything he’s done in the short 5 weeks we’ve been in FSW together. If he can’t see the error of his ways, then I’ll beat it into him. It doesn’t matter if it’s in the ring…or in the courtroom. I’ll show him he’s messed with the wrong man, and the wrong family.” Thomas backs away, and shakes his head. “Jason, look. Angelo Deville is different from you and me. He’s…well…connected. He’s going to win. No matter what it takes. Even if you beat him in the ring, or we beat him in the courtroom, he’s going to keep coming for you. He’s going to keep coming for James. And if he can’t then someone in his ‘family’ will.” “ I don’t care, Thomas. I’m ready, I’m willing, and I’m able. And so what if he’s connected. Right now, James is sitting here, connected to machines. If it wasn’t for me barging in on him a month ago, he wouldn’t be here. Part of this is on my head too. James needs me, and I need him. That’s all that matters right now.” “I hope you’re right Jason. For your sake, and for James.” A cell phone rings. “Hello? Yes. He is? Good. SO he’s taking over? Good. I’ll let him know. Thank you.” Thomas looks at Jason and smiles. “You’re in, Jason.” “Excellent. I can’t wait to see the look on Angelo’s face.” Thomas puts the phone in his pocket, and walks over to James, sitting next to him. “James. I know you can hear me. I’m leaving. I’ll be back later. I have to sign some papers.” Thomas shakes hands with Jason, and walks out of the room. Thomas looks at Jason, who walks over to James, and shakes his head. “Stubborn son of a…” is all he can say as he walks out of the room.
