Of Blasphemy Begotten: Session 7 – Blood-soaked Memories: Re-emergence

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(NOTE: The last repost I didn't scan before its repost, and was rather fragmented. Included notes for ideas I was already planning and such. Wanted to clear that up for any who may have read them) _________________________ A light. A cloud. A prayer. Three things that bestow comfort on the erratic persona of mankind. The light vanquishes darkness, lifting the veil that triggers the natural xenophobia inherent in all members of this fledgling species. Clouds, especially to the adolescent mind, open the imagination and offer a ‘security blanket’ that offers solace from this often bleak reality, in the form of their billowing bulk and infinite forms. As for prayer……it completes the triad as a source for consolation of the fragile human mind. Prayer operates on the suggestion that there exists a greater power beyond the known heavens, a lone omnipotent deity who offers aid to his mortal children and accepts the weight of our greatest burdens. It’s always been a trait of mankind to fear what we don’t understand, and to both create and seek objects of relief against these dread enigmas. The light subdues the fearful shroud of that which we cannot see. Clouds are a virtual key to the imagination in our youth, unlocking the gate which harbors the endless vistas of creativity and vivaciousness in our consciousness. Prayer offers to cure our aversion to the hellish antagonism faced in our daily life by the agents of the Great Abyss by serving up the notion a glorious being exists with the intention of aiding our young species. They all help to maintain the candy-coated falsehoods of what we perceive as daily life, serving to blot out the veil between what we accept and what better remain unplumbed. Beyond that veil prowls a horde of loathsome things who fear the light as we do death, and there exists a range of cosmic principles accepted as myth everywhere except our own Earthly reality. Those who have glimpsed these profane images of the bizarre never escape unaffected; those who dare to probe deeper into their alien origins go unceasingly mad. A select few have resisted the inevitable, prolonging it long enough to do what they can to conceal the macabre certainties mankind should never wholly explore. Among these virtuous paladins are brilliant scholars and scientists, valiant adventurers and haughty militants. And among even these stands an oddity in their ranks: Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan; professional wrestler, bitter street thug and an unlikely participant of a cosmic jihad stretching eons before the first human was even conceived in the mind of God. The demons of the past have continued to beleaguer the ex-Extreme champion of Primetime Championship Wrestling. Memories of his disgrace six months ago at the hands of the White Mexican still afflict his focus on what is yet to come, and with every demolished carcass left in his wake the feeling has only increased. And now comes the pinnacle of his anxiety: the Round of Sixteen. It was at this point that he met his in-ring demise versus the then-prodigal son of PCW; when the two clashed for the honor of winning their bracket, Shanahan’s spiral thus began. The outward conditions may be radically altered, but to the one-track mind of professional wrestling’s ‘Violence Jack’ they are all too similar. His previous defeat was marred with ostensible omens and outside distractions that wracked his focus, health and resolve. The tables are now turned as he goes into this ‘Sweet Sixteen’ war versus GW’s venerable Humane as the odds-on favorite by decree of the Vegas gambling community, and his fate now rests sheerly in his own hands. Or does it?...... Revelations have come to expose Shanahan’s dismal bloodline and his relation to the infamous 12th century master of the black arts and prophet of the End Times, Aleister Crowley, who also served as an intermittent enemy/ally of the magically imprisoned cosmic deities awaiting their Earthly resurrection. This in turn has opened the door to Bruce’s own dark destiny: as Earthly agent of the Great Old Ones, his purpose to open the gateway separating their celestial prisons and the vulnerable populace of Earth and awaken the dormant fate of a genuinely powerless human race. (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan) I started awkwardly from a state of sleep, feeling the rush of forward movement as my eyes batted several times to shake the effects of prolonged slumber. Something felt oddly familiar about my stark surroundings, from the peculiarly-hued lights to the gaunt forms shuffling silently about the dimly-lit cabin where I was now situated. Glancing out the window I discerned the constant blur of extensive walls running parallel to either side of what appeared to be a train that I now rode within, faint glows dotting the twin surfaces and lining the winding path of the rail-directed vehicle as it veered precariously around several jagged corners. I was suddenly overwhelmed by a stark feeling of dismay, and at first could find no visible source for such a powerful wave of emotion as this was. Apprehensively I elevated my eyes to gawk at my darkness-blurred environment within the cabin, and already I knew what horrors would grace my vision. One of the shambling figures had paused long enough to swivel around and gaze on me with an eerie aura that initially chilled my bones to the core. Only then was that dark aura dispelled by the even superior horror of the eyes that now held me in their fixation. Again, just as memory incurred: deep red and blazing with the fury of hellfire, collaborated with an omnipotent and invisible power that held my fascination like a sinister hypnotism. Only through a surge of my own will could I rend my eyes from the blasphemous sight, turning my tortured sight away while out of the corner of my eye spying the same decrepit shape shuffling past my own motionless form and toward the back of the train. The putrid odor of death brushed my nostrils as it ambled sullenly by, crusts of dirt falling from the torn fabric of its shredded clothing. More of its kind also lumbered silently through the cabin. I had been in this situation before, and the morbid reality of passing through this unholy dimension again was nearly more than I could bear. I already knew what existed on the horizon even before the howling squeal of the train’s brakes penetrated my ears. As I suspected, we came to a screech at the foot of a raised platform, the perimeter lined with monoliths and columns of some black metal that shimmered with a wavering haze. The doors creaked open with what isn’t described properly as a creak as much as a shrill shriek. Unlike my last passage through this reprehensible and evil space, I shot to my feet and shoved past the line of ghouls which stood in a steady procession to depart the train. I dashed out the still-creaking doors and shifted my eyes to run along the gaunt outline of the transport that brought us here. I observed the obscene angles and acute geometry not found on any Earthly assembly line, ran my hand briefly over the blackened alloy composing the extensive vehicle. The contours and edges melded with such intricacy it was obvious this was no mass-production, or at the very least not by any human hands or man-made machine. At that instant the real inquiry finally popped into my head: Why the hell was I here again? It was a realm I had prayed I would never visit again, as the atmosphere rivaled the harshest of all I’ve experienced. Only months ago I passed through this same subterranean hell while watching those fallen in the then-Global Title Tournament skulk by and to their execution at the blood-stained hands of the one I had deemed the Great Druid….. Apprehension clutched at my heart at that moment as I bolted past the animated corpses of the lifeless conquered. Undoubtedly I was again locked in some nightmare, with the specters of the tournament vanquished acting as some vile symbolism as they marched intuitively by. I hurtled up the steps of the platform, racing without a second glance by the looming monoliths and the eerily-drab torchlight illuminating the vast, darkness- bordered chamber. It took only moments to penetrate the wall of opaque darkness of which the plodding ghouls passed through, seemingly sinking away to be swallowed by the black gulf beyond. I hurled myself through and landed, as I remembered, at the foot of the black bride that brought images of purgatory to mind. Composed of the same black metal which seemed so commonplace in this God-forsaken abyss, the overpass straddled an equally foreboding canal of fire and brimstone which extended out of sight to both the east and west, and exuded the acrid scent of sulfur. I slid into the narrow margin between the succession of ghouls and the side railing, resting my hand on the hell-forged alloy and finding it surprisingly cool to the touch seeing as the immense heat from the fiery river below should surely rise to singe the bridge and rail. I slid along the bridge and kept in relative stride with the ominous army of souls treading the same passage. At this point we came to the open gate of my memory, the crown of the gateway decorated with the statues of deformed gargoyle-like beasts and what appeared to be demonic, bloated cherubs. The procession filed silently in, my vision again blocked off by an unholy and impenetrable darkness as to what lay ahead. Fighting instinct and common sense I continued through into the void beyond. The darkness melted away instantaneously, revealing a scarlet-carpeted corridor adorned with jagged protrusions from the walls, which were painted dark and dreary shades streaked with lighter colors forming innumerous monstrous symbols of the occult. The next part of ornamentation was the most gruesome and stood out premier in my memory, but no preparation can prepare a (mostly) sane man from the profane satire lying in my path. I cringed at the all-too-familiar image of moaning figures stretched out across the adjoining walls, arms and feet nailed with grim black spikes and splattering the floor with their blood. They groaned and cried out for help as their life dripped onto the floor and blended seamlessly into the equal-shaded carpeting. Familiar faces christened the malicious gallery of death: Bruce Blade, Chris Chase, Matt Blitz, Dante Jackson…..they and the others squirmed against the blood-tainted walls while crying helplessly for a deliverance I painfully knew would never occur. At that point, a muscle-ripped form bumped into me with a jolt. At first I had trouble discerning the figure through the blood and mutilation, the lifeless eyes. It gave a meager nod as if in apology before pivoting to walk away. Only now could I define the bleak visage, splattered in congealed blood with a forlorn stare passing through me as if I held no physical manifest. One eye twitched involuntarily in its swollen socket, the ravaged husk of a body shuddering under some potent force indiscernible to my mortal eyes. A.J. Trinidad. The sight was shameful; Trinidad had given me a bitter fight before the smoke cleared, and to see an athlete of such auspicious skill in the condition before me was a spiritual blow. All consciousness and emotion was absent now from Trinidad; drained out by some void-spawned wraith lurking in the darkness somewhere in these unholy hallways. My God ….. déjà vu. It was just like the last time, the Global Title Tournament of March. The visions haunting my slumber and plaguing even my waking thoughts with apparitions of the dead and the obscene. Never can I forget the procession of lumbering specters posted as grisly avatars of those vanquished before me, those mindless and marching to their own post-mortem executions. Nor can I dismiss the memories of the cloaked Druid and the gaunt ceremonial blade he wielded as he ceremoniously beheaded each of the fallen. “AJ!” I cried out in panic. “What the hell…..?! No, it can’t be! NOOOO!” With a meshing of rage and desperation in my heart, I sprinted down the hallway past the groaning martyrs and kicked open the towering bronze door at the head of the passageway. I knew what lay beyond that yawning threshold, and I held no fear for its inhabitants. The massive entrance skidded to a violent shut as I gazed awkwardly around, my heart leaping into my throat. In this room, things had changed. Still present was the raised stage and the altar of blackened, shimmering alloy. It was encircled by the mob of ghouls whose lifeless eyes stared blankly ahead. This was not dissimilar from my last ‘visit’. But what was different, was the walls. They pulsated and throbbed like the pulpy flesh of some enormous fiend, noxious liquids and gases inexplicably spurting forth from ‘pores’ and tainting the atmosphere. It was like dwelling in the recesses of some demonic heart. For the first time I spotted the robed figure as it arose from within the dead mob obscuring its presence. In one hand it clutched the same scimitar that had beheaded Xavier Kannon and Tyler Davidson in the past. The Druid paced slowly toward me, the path before the evil being separating as the lifeless forms divided to give way to the black-shrouded devil. It stopped only several feet from from position, patting the razor-edged blade. Now the ghoul spoke, mist spewing from beneath the shadows of the hood into the cold air. He grimly ground the serrated edge of the object into his own hand, the curved blade sickeningly splitting the flesh of his palm and drawing a thick stream of blood to flow and drip onto the granite floor. Horrifyingly, the ground seemed to voraciously suck up the rapidly pooling fluid like a famished sponge, issuing a repugnant hiss as the last of the blood drained away. “Echoes of the past, dear Shanahan. Was it not between these hallowed walls only months ago that your feeble mind first drew comprehension of the bleak reality infecting what you once perceived as truth? Between these walls, under the observation of the Old Ones, your precious world crumbled and left but a burnt-out frame of what you once beheld as life. How did it feel to lift the veil of illusion and peer into what lies beyond the perimeter of meager human knowledge?” I only shuddered as I deliberated on the identity of he who was beneath the blackened robes, encrusted with their cuff-linings of gold and silver trim. The question swiftly struck as almost rhetorical, as the chilling echo of a hundred agonizing souls betrayed the identity of the shadow-masked abomination beneath the robes. Slowly a bony hand arose and clutched at the hood, sliding it back to reveal a diabolical grin beneath seething red eyes. Absent was the Egyptian headdress commonly worn in this particular avatar of the ancient fiend, but its presence wasn’t necessary to distinguish the identity of the monstrous Outer God who had haunted my sleep for months and had even held dominion over my body for a short time. “Does this not please you, Bruce?” inquired the grinning monstrosity. “You have lived a life reveling in violence, death and the demise of those you so eagerly scorn. We are practically handing you the prize you so yearn for. It is the least We can do for the one who will awaken the pantheon of slumbering servitors of Great Azathoth’s court at the center of All Creation.” I had defied Nyarlathotep in the past, because somehow I felt I could; some alien feeling told me that even with the infinite power of the abominable Outer God, there was a reason that that the Crawling Chaos had not already killed me. Surely he had the opportunity many times in the past, yet something had stayed his hand; and only days ago, before the beguiling Council of Elders, had that motive been revealed. And still no obligation to concur with the monster stirred my heart. “What the hell do ya mean am I ‘pleased’?!” I roared, rage searing. “You expect me to be happy because you’re marchin’ around a bunch of lifeless drones to represent all those sorry f*cks I wrestle with?! You’re outta your mind! And you’re also outta your mind if you think I’m going to help you destroy the human race!” Nyarlathotep cracked a grin beneath the guise of his brawny humanoid avatar, the dark-skinned Egyptian that had come to be known as the Black Pharaoh. His eyes flashed irregularly and he gestured toward the lifeless army surrounding the altar. “This does not please you, Bruce Shanahan? Before you is an ethereal representation of what awaits when you fulfill your duties. Face it…..you are human. You revel in glory and recognition, aspire to be indestructible in what you do in mortal life! We offer that opportunity to you with no strings attached, the ability to be a veritable God! You can rampage untouched through the remainder of this lowly competition you find yourself in, no one will be able to oppose you!” The Black Pharaoh extended a flat palm, where a tinny orb manifested from a swirling array of particles. He raised it to eye level where it arose to slowly spiral and rotate, twinkling like a shard of frozen starlight. “All you must do, is take it.” I must confess that the possibilities churned in my head as I retreated a step back. For too long now I had sat back and watched others, the likes of Ivan Stanislav, Tyler Nelson, Jason Snow and Sebastian Toomes, as they were praised and presented as the pinnacle of professional wrestling, while I still lurked in the common crowd. I was now presented with the prospect of rising above them all and taking my place among the premier names of all time. But to have such a distinction would mean betraying my species, sentencing mankind to eradication. As much as what the Outer God said was true, not even I, the vendetta-driven Boston’s Bastard Son, could find the tenacity and ruthlessness to do such a thing. Besides…..how long would such glory last in the face of an extinct race?.... The Black Pharaoh sneered, withdrawing the thick hand clutching the twirling orb as it dissipated into droplets of glittering water, scattering into the atmosphere. “Make no mistake, mortal. The rewards we offer you are merely tokens of our appreciation. We justly reward all who revere and dedicate their lives to us….but you shall do what we command. There is no option. We are not crusaders of the fool ‘democracy’ much of your hypocritical race promotes, we offer fortune only by our good graces. Accept our kindly blessings and do what we wish, or do so without reward and drown in sorrow when your days end!” The Black Pharaoh stiffened erect, glaring at the faltering mortal as the air chilled until clouds of mist spewed forth from the fiend's blackened lips. It was apparent I was at a crossroads. And I needed to make a decision....
