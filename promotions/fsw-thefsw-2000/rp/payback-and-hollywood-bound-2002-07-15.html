Payback and Hollywood Bound?

15 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/15/02 So many enemies and not enough of poor Tyler Davidson to go around it seems. For the past two weeks now Tyler has been enjoying life in the fast lane as he always does with only one small change, the addition of Five Star Wrestling to his ever growing resume. Obviously nothing will ever come very easy in the life of Tyler but now things seem to be on the right track at the very least. Competing in one of the top feds the world has ever seen while also coming into ownership of another through Davidson Enterprises. Despite all of this though, the past few weeks have been quiet ones for Tyler. He has done little interaction with the fans and has hardly cut any interviews for the new promotion. Many people have been left wondering quite a bit about just what the status of Tyler was in the business. Many people believing and pointing at rumors that he was on the verge of breaking out of the wrestling business and into another field. Many have pointed out towards Hollywood to try and capitalize on the success of other wrestling stars that have made it big in recent years. As if all of that wasn’t enough, Tyler has had little time on his hands inside the ring as well. In a matter of two weeks time in FSW, Tyler has managed to make enemies with half of the active roster in the company. At the very top of the list of enemies though sit the names of Chris Bagwell and Leon Chambers, two names that could oftentimes be linked to winners and champions. First on the list of enemies is Leon Chambers, the man who stole a victory away from Tyler two weeks ago. Now at the Davidson Estate, Tyler is resting up for the next road trip into Boston in just a few short days. Will some of the questions be answered finally? Let’s find out. “I can’t stand that Chambers bitch,” Tyler can be heard saying as he walked through the expansive backyard of the Davidson Estate in Connecticut. “Yeah I think you’ve said that a few times now Ty,” came the response from the man standing next to him, Daniel S., the long time friend and manager of Tyler. “I believe that may actually be the hundredth time today you have damned either Chambers or Bagwell.” “So?” Tyler demanded. “What’s your point? I don’t like either of them so I’m allowed to damn them to hell, or to any other unspeakable punishment including that hell hole called New Jersey.” “Hey now,” S. interrupted. “Lay off Jersey. I’m not to far away from there after all.” “I’m sorry,” Tyler said apologetically. “That’s okay, just try not to badmouth it again,” S. advised. “I meant I’m sorry that you’re not far from Jersey,” Tyler corrected. “That must suck.” “Shut up!” S. shouted. “Don’t you have anything better to do other than be a smart ass?” “No,” Tyler answered. “What am I going to do? Train for my match against Leon? We all know I’m going to whip the guy’s ass on Wednesday.” “I don’t know anything,” S. said. “You didn’t fare to well against him the last time. As a matter of fact I seem to remem…..” “It’s so important to whatever career you have left that you don’t finish that sentence,” Tyler warned drawing a loud laugh from S. “Not my fault you lost,” S. finished anyway. “And to Leon Chambers at that.” “Shut it,” Tyler fired back. “We both know I got screwed in that one. The little fruitcake had his feet on the ropes to pin me.” “Excuses, excuses,” S. replied sarcastically as the two of them walked by the pool on their way up to the massive house which Tyler’s family has called home for years.. “You always have one ready for every occasion.” “Ah shut up already,” Tyler said before reaching an arm out and shoving the unsuspecting S. right into the deep end of the pool. By the time he came back above water Tyler had already moved on leaving his friend behind and drenched shouting obscenities to the wind. The scene then changes to the interior of the Davidson Estate just as Tyler walks in through the back door and into a kitchen. Standing behind the table reading a magazine is Tyler’s father, Hunter. The older man looks up at his soon with a bit of a grin before speaking. “It’s about time you got in here,” he started out. “I’ve been waiting for you almost twenty minutes now.” “Why? What’s going on?” Tyler asked puzzled by the look his father gave him. “The phone has been ringing off the hook for you,” his father answered. “Any idea what it’s about?” Tyler questioned even more confused. “I believe it has something to do with this,” Hunter said tossing the magazine in his hands down onto the table in front of Tyler. Tyler looks down at the magazine slowly before reaching down and picking it up. On the cover of the latest issue of PWI was a picture of PHD, Howard Porter, and Alan Kriegman being revealed as The Vax in OSW. One of the subtitles for the magazine this month however, was “Tyler Davidson: Hollywood Bound?”. A slight smirk crossed the face of Tyler as he read over the cover a second time and then turned to the page indicated only to see himself in the pictures standing alongside some Hollywood celebrities. One picture had even gone so far to portray Tyler as a possible Jedi Knight in the final installment of the Star Wars prequel. Tyler tossed the magazine back down onto the table laughing as he looked up at his father. “So they’re calling about this?” Tyler asked. “I guess so,” his father answered. “Unless there is something else about you that’s newsworthy lately.” “Hey,” Tyler responded. “I’m always newsworthy. You people just don’t ever realize it.” “Oh my mistake,” his father replied full of sarcasm. “So which one are you going to start off with anyway?” “Who said I’d start with any of them?” Tyler asked. “I have my hands full wrestling right now. Might not have the time to start making movies. Then again I might have more time than expected. Guess we’ll have to wait and see.” “Fine keep your damn secrets,” his father responded grumpily. “You’ll have to tell me sooner or later anyway.” “No I won’t,” Tyler answered smartly. “Yes but since you’re such a good son you will tell me,” his father suggested. “Where’s Dan anyway?” “Oh he decided to go for a swim,” Tyler answered smugly. “He should be along in a minute or two.” “Right,” his father said confused by his sons answer. “Which reminds me,” Tyler continued. “I really should be going right now. Be back soon.” Tyler quickly turned and continued down the hallway just as the sound of the back door can be heard opening again and the sounds of wet footsteps can be heard stepping into the house. Just as Tyler disappears out the front door the shouts of S. can be heard calling for Tyler as his father simply sits back and laughs at the sight of a dripping wet S. stomping through his house.
