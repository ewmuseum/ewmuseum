Star Trek Part 1: The Opening of Pandora's Box

16 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-16-02 Patrick Stewart’s Voice: Space, The Final Frontier. These are the voyages of the Starship Enterprise, it’s continuing mission, to explore strange new worlds. To seek out new life and new civilizations…TO BOLDLY GO WHERE NO MAN HAS GONE BEFORE! The main theme of Star Trek, The Next Generation plays, and The U.S.S. Enterprise flies through deep space within a warp field. Sublime lights pass as stars shoot past the ship, all the while it seems to flow so elegantly through the continuum. Starring: From The Next Generation: Timo Bolamba as: Captain Jean Luc Bolamba of The U.S.S. Enterprise Kramer Wild Wing as: William “Wild Wing” Riker Alexander Miyagawa as: Miyagawa LaForge Chris Sloboda as: Commander Warfboda Tim Walden as: Ensign Timothy Crusher Hans Kruger and Big Daveyliscious as: HANS and Big Dataliscious Tyler Nelson as: Romulan Admiral Nel-son Ivan Stanislav as: Klingon General Ivan Gratta Alexie Ruslan as: Klingon General Katang Clyde as: The Enterprise Bartender Sam Collins, Lynn Lane, and Steven Caldera as: The Mandatory Expendable Red Shirted Ensigns The Violator as: Brother of Jimmy The Gorn Kathryn the Bi-Sexual Valet of Deamont as: Kathryn Troi Erica the Bi-Sexual Valet of Deamont as: Erica Crusher Ron Meischker as: Ron, the omnipotent force that bears a striking resemblance to Q. From The Original Series: Mike Marvelous as: Captain James T. Marvelous Paddy O’Shae as: Chief Engineer Patrick “Paddy” O’Shae Cassius Deamont as: Cassius “Bones” McCoy Azala Zameer as: Zameer with pointy ears (Spock) Violence Jack as: Ensign Jackov Charlie Loc as: Lieutenant Loco From Deep Space Nine: Six Finger Cisco as: Captain “Six Finger” Benjamin Cisco Asa Fountain as: Quark with a major Fro Jesse Ramey as: Romey, Quark’s brother Jason O’Neil as: Chief Engineer Miles O’Neil Afwa Kilmanjaro as: Constable Afwa Casey Deltura as: Chief Medical Officer Casey Bashir Special Guest Stars: Angelo Deville as: Cardassian Commander Deville Ming as: Ming (Khan) Orpheus Grant as: The Collective B.O.R.G. (Biggie’s Original Rappin Gangsta’s), a group of Gangster Cliché’s with no thought process other than dominating the universe through assimilation of otherwise good music. See also Commander Puff Daddy. Todays Episode: The Final Countdown Captains log, stardate 111102. We reached an agreement with the Romulan government to release Admiral Nel-son to them several months ago, and his trial came to pass. The peculiar thing is, a few days ago, he vanished from his cell without a trace. We move to investigate this occurrence, and enter into deadly ground. “Captain, We are now being hailed by the Romulan leader.” Jean Luc Bolamba was greeted by the voice of Timothy Crusher, the local cabin boy and ensign extrordinaire. He looked around the bridge to his loyal crew, and he gave a hearty smile. The Captain rose form his chair and stood in front of the viewscreen, nodding to Tim. “Onscreen.” The screen comes to life and as the Romulan leader Kromat takes form on the screen, Bolamba crosses his arms in a diplomatic fashion. He had tried to stay away from this area after the previous incident involving Admiral Krem-men and then Commander Nel-son. It would appear that he once again had a quandry on his hands though, and that would require much attention. “Captain Bolamba, I see we are forced together under dire circumstances.” “Agreed. What do you make of this disappearance Kromat?” The Romulan sighed and shook his head. He looked back to the screen and produced a holo-image of a warp vesicle. The Captain turned his head to the side in a curious fashion, and peered deep into the undulating green matter. “How…odd. Where did you come upon this entity?” “Captain Bolamba…it was in Nel-Son’s personal confinement area. Jean Luc turned and held his hand to his chin, stroking it in thought. What an unlikely occurrence this was indeed. “Kromat, may I send an away team down?” The Romulan leader nods and Bolamba turns to his crew. Commander Warfboda, William “Wild Wing” Riker, Timothy Crusher, HANS, and the mandatory red shirted ensigns. He nods and Warfboda, HANS, and Ensign Sam Collins head for the transporter room. “Kromat, thank you, we will keep in touch. I have a feeling that something bigger than all of us is behind this.” With that the screen fades to the passing starscape and Bolamba walks to his chair to sit down. As he kneels his head to rest on his knuckle, he thinks to himself. -I wonder what Captain Marvelous would do.- Somewhere in the remote past, a deep sea sounding beep comes from an onboard computer. A large black man with pointy ears stands rigid whilst a bald headed bearded man in a red shirt mans the controls. An oriental man oversees the operation. The shot pans to the Captain Chair, where sits Captain James T. Marvelous, the greatest Starfleet Captain ever. He conducts himself as we all know he would, by grabbing Uhura’s ass as she walks by. He stands up smiling, almost whimsical, and walks until he stands just to the left of Zameer. He looks up to his second in command and makes a few hand gestures. “Zameer….I….wonder if we will find him.” “Ming Captain? Illogical.” “I…know…it would seem as if I had destroyed him…but he…is out there…I can…feel it.” The Captain turns to his loyal crew and to one man in particular. “Ensign Jackov…” Everyone on the crew chuckles but the bald headed man who simply rolls his eyes. He turns in his chair and faces Captain Marvelous. “Aye sir.” “Enough men…Do you…read any life forms?” Jackov turns to the computer and begins a scan of a ship that is believed to hold Ming inside of it. He has a puzzled look on his face as he turns back to Jim and shrugs. “Nothing Captain. Nothing.” Marvelous turns dramatically and wonders aloud to himself. “Could this…be the hand of an unusual foe? Could this…plight…be cast upon from above? What…of…it…” Elsewhere, the shot pans to a large mass floating in deep space. The semi round space station orbits in a plane known to exist in hostile territory, and is known to all as Deep Space Nine. Onboard a Six Fingered Captain known as Benjamin Cisco paces in front of the monitor, watching as an eclipse passes over the home of the Cardassians. An ominous hush falls over the crew of the great station, Constable Afwa shakes his head and turns away. It is clear why they are all in dismay, as it appears the planet is breaking apart from within, and they have scant little time to change anything. Their only hope is to move the station, and even then, it would not have the power to save them from the ensuing chaos that the planets collapse will have caused. Benjamin holds up a six fingered hand to gain the attention of the crew, and stands upon a podium. “Ladies and gentlemen, soon Cardassia will break apart, and we will all be in grave peril. I know not what is causing this, or why The Cardassians have seemingly destroyed their entire planet but I can assure you that unless something drastic happens, we will all perish.” A murmur of unrest fillets itself through the crowd as Ben slams his six fingered fist on the podium. Afwa Steps into the Captains spotlight for a moment and holds his massive paws in the air. “Pee-puhl, Afwa no want scared for friends. Afwa have contacted Friend Bolamba, Bolamba will help!” Cisco turns to his chief mechanic, Miles O’Neil and confides in his friend. “How much power will we have Miles?” “Well, if we put the shields up…not very much. That planet is going to cause a catalyst that will rip us apart if we don’t get out of here…and frankly, our engines cant handle that kind of load Captain.” The Captain turned and sighed lightly. What on earth had the Cardassians done now? Why would the needlessly blow their entire planet up? This just had to be the work of something higher, something that could threaten very existence. “Miles, I want to go to red alert, get our shields up and get The Defiant loaded with civilians.” “And what of you Captain?” “I will be staying here. May god protect me.” He turns and walks down a corridor as the red alarms begin to flash all around him. One man alone walks against a crowd that has gathered and began to flock toward the only ship capable of saving them. Cisco reaches his quarters and the door opens with the little woosh noise that Star Trek fans are accustomed to. “Dear god, what has happened.” The voice was that of Jean Luc Bolamba as he watches the cracking surface of the Cardassian planet. He sat alone in his quarters drinking a cup of tea and watching as his good friend Constable Afwa looked downtrodden. Several books line his desk, as well as a few musical instruments. He nods and the view fades to black. The lifestyle of a Starship Captain was not an easy one indeed, but he was in a real jam now. Jean Luc motions to his chest and taps the Starfleet insignia, calling over his com link. “Number one, we have a dilemma.” The voice of Wild Wing chimes back at him, chipper, and unaware of the impending doom aboard Deep Space Nine. “Yes Captain, the Romulans are being cooperative though, and I think…” “Will, this is far beyond the Romulans now.” The returning voice was now very sincere, almost hurtful in its questioning. “Sir?” Bolamba sighed and looked at the viewer again. Somewhere out there some of his best friends were in dire peril, and on the planets surface, three of his best men were in enemy territory. “Cardassia, Will, its somehow being destroyed…and it is going to take Deep Space Nine with it. We have two options, leave the men behind and go now, which would mean almost certain death for them…or leave Deep Space Nine to crumble…” There is no response, as suddenly the ship seems to have stopped moving. Bolamba taps his communicator a few more times, but nothing. He looks around, then tries to exit the room, but the doors are sealed tight. Jean Luc turns once more and looks outside, everything is still, the stars are perfectly placed, the Romulan planet is below, and he is utterly confused. “What the…” “Hell?” Bolamba jumps at the sound of a voice, and sees a man sitting in his chair, drinking a MaiTai. The Captain steps forward and stands firmly in front of the desk, curious just to how this stranger got aboard his ship, let alone how he had snuck up on him. “PLEASE Captain, please don’t tell me you believe in such an archaic structure of religion.” “Just who do you think you are?” The young man smiles and puts his feet down. He extends his hand and begins to hum the chorus to “Sympathy for The Devil.” “You don’t mean…” The young man scoffs and smiles at the bewildered Captain. “Really, do you take me for a fool Bolamba? I am neither here nor there really. And you have a question…do you not?” Bolamba looks confused, and as he helps himself to a sip of tea, the young man takes a sip of his own drink. The Captain can’t help but wonder exactly who this man is, or why he is here, but that goes without saying. The honest question in his mind is… “How, oh how do I save the crew aboard that space station? That IS your question is it not Bolamba?” The Samoan nods, looking around to his surreal surroundings, wondering if life had just ceased altogether. The being snapped his fingers and the MaiTai disappeared. He leaned over the Captain’s desk and grinned like a fool. “That’s why I am here. You see Mr. Bolamba, they call me Ron, and in this...omniverse, I control all. I have been watching you and to be honest, you provide me with quite a lot of entertainment sir. The way you dealt with Admiral Krem-men was outstanding, but your brutish force..ugh.” “And what would you have done “Ron”? Condemned this crew?” The man scoffs. “Bolamba, you are a savage creature aren’t you. You really believe that what you did was so noble, saving a group that belongs to a savage race, barbaric and behind the times. I would have let them die, were it me. But you see, that’s why you are here. My entertainment, and ego pushed me to these circumstances.” The Captain turned at his words and glared deep within the soulless eyes that reside in Ron’s head. There was no compassion, there was no hunger, there was one fire though, one to smite all that opposed him. The Captain could not understand such wishes, and he shook his head and turned away. “Those ways have passed Ronn. Our life now is much better, we have no crime, only would be criminals like Krem-men. What I did was a justice to the people, not a barbaric act. What you claim to have done is wrong, and I believe even you know that.” A slight laughter protrudes from behind the desk, amidst a wash of sarcastic clapping. “Oh, you humans are delightful. Especially you Jean Luc…but I have made plans with others like you…I think you know them. Captain Benjamin Cisco and James T. Marvelous?” Bolamba turns sharply at the mention of Marvelous’ name. Many a story has been told about the hallowed halls he kept within the ship bearing the name Enterprise. This ship was too the name of Bolamba’s, so it peaked his interest. “What do you mean?” “Well my friend…can I call you that? Oh well, I will anyhow…I have procured myself three of the greatest challenges for you and your friends to overcome. I believe you know one of them already…his name is…” “Admiral Nel-Son.” Ron smiles and claps once more. “Very Sharp Bolamba! So now you see what happened to him…yes sir, it was me! And with the aid of my handy dandy uber-mod skills, I have brought forth an old friend to help you. So with that said…you can save your friends, or you can save yourself. You have twenty four hours Bolamba!” With a flash he is gone and The Samoan looks bewildered. Suddenly the stars are moving again, and the ship appears to have returned to normal. The one difference he notes is a flicker of movement outside the window, and as he stops to look outside, shock beguiles his face. “Good lord, that’s…” -To Be Continued-
