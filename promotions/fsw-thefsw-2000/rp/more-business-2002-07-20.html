More Business

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/20/02 Yet another show has been put in the books for the famous Five Star Wrestling organization. Unlike the past shows however this one came with a bit of a change. Avenging an earlier defeat at the hands of Leon Chambers, Tyler Davidson managed to somehow pull through inside of a fifteen foot high steel cage and pick up a highly impressive victory against a man that had just beaten him two weeks ago. Many of the critics however have not been silenced by Tyler’s victory. Many consider the recent victory over Leon Chambers to be nothing more than a fluke as all he had to do in order to win was to escape the confines of the steel cage. Not a real victory in the least many would say and in most cases Tyler himself would be inclined to agree. He has never been one to take a cheap way out of a match and the fact that he was forced to compete inside a steel cage where the only way to win was by running from your opponent was not something that sat well with the young star. It turns out to be just another bit of life that Tyler must learn to deal with as he prepares for the events of the next show. He finds himself now placed into a match against a relative unknown to him in Jason O’Neil. He has heard of the man by name only and his recent antics in FSW against Angelo Deville, a man that is a real star unlike O’Neil. O’Neil since day one has been trying to ride the coattails of Deville to stardom and now he tries latching himself onto Tyler as well. Not only does he have O’Neil trying to use him for a path to the top but he still must keep a wary eye over his shoulder for the constant presence of Chris Bagwell. Yet another individual who cannot stand the stardom which Tyler has achieved in such a short period of time. Bagwell for weeks now has done everything he could in an attempt to hold Tyler down but nothing has yet been successful. Tyler has persevered and continues to push his way through and to the top despite the best efforts of Bagwell and others like him. The scene opens at last in New York City, a definite change of pace from the Davidson Estate in Connecticut where Tyler has spent most of his off time the past two weeks. Now though, he finds himself seated in his expansive New York City office at the Davidson Enterprises Headquarters. The work had been allowed to pile up while Tyler was going about his own business in the wrestling world letting the real world slip further and further away as he always has in the past. Amongst the piles of papers several items that appear to be potential movie scripts can be seen shuffled off to the side by Tyler for later reading. As he looks over some of the papers marked as urgent a knock comes at the door to his office and sticking his head in from outside is a scrawny little man with a pair of glasses and a badly receding hairline. “Excuse me Mr. Davidson,” the man stuttered out nervously. “What do you want?” Tyler asked. “As you already know I’m quite busy.” “Yes I know and I’m sorry sir,” the man answered hesitantly. “But Mr. S. is outside and demanding to see you.” “Probably still mad about that pool incident,” Tyler grumbled while suppressing a laugh. “What are you interrupting me for about it?” “Well I wanted to make sure I should let him in sir,” the man responded confidently. “You told me you were busy and didn’t want to be interrupted so I thought that might include him as well.” “He’s the Director of Operations for one of my companies,” Tyler answered sarcastically. “What do you think you should do?” “Let him in?” the man answered doubtfully not even catching the slightest bit of sarcasm in Tyler’s question. “No make him wait outside,” Tyler responded sarcastically again. “Oh right sir,” the man answered about to turn and walk out of the room. “Wait a minute you idiot,” Tyler called out to him. “Do you not know what sarcasm is? Send him in!” “Right away sir,” the man said flustered and embarrassed as he ran out of the room. After a few minutes have passed the door again swings open and Daniel S. walks into the room not having a happy expression on his face at all. He barges into the room looking directly at Tyler who is paying little attention as he’s still looking over some of his paperwork. “We need to talk about that damn company of yours,” S. announced breaking Tyler’s concentration on his papers. “What about it?” Tyler asked. “Too much work?” “Not at all but some of those jackasses you hired keep screwing things up and it’s getting to be a real pain in the ass,” S. explained. “Three straight weeks there has been some funky crap going on with my entrance and crap.” “Yeah I noticed that,” Tyler responded. “Didn’t you fire them for it already though?” “Yeah all three times it happened,” S. answered. “If it keeps up there won’t be any more production people left in the business for me to hire.” “Heh, sucks for you then,” Tyler replied putting the papers down in front of him and leaning back in the chair. “You do what you got to do there. I have the other businesses to take care of.” “Yeah, yeah, always taking the easy way out,” S. responded. “You run a club while I get stuck running a promotion. I don’t think that’s very fair.” “You wanted the job so you got it,” Tyler answered. “Besides I’m also busy with other details. I still have to compete in Five Star Wrestling after having left $oldout last week. Plus I got all these possible Hollywood deals coming my way also. Some of these scripts haven’t been half bad that I read so far.” “Yeah right,” S. replied sarcastically. “Next year we’ll be seeing Mr. Nanny 2 starring Tyler Davidson.” “The hell with that,” Tyler shouted absolutely horrified by the notion as would everyone else be if it were to happen. “I’m not Hulk Hogan; I actually have a little something called talent that so few people possess nowadays. Just look no further than Chris Bagwell and half that FSW roster for example. I mean we got that jackass Bagwell following me around like a lovesick puppy week in and week out trying to catch just a little glimpse of what it’s like to be a star.” “The guy did actually win one of those big tournaments you know,” S. informed Tyler. “He’s not going to be much of a pushover. I think he won the first annual RPGod tournament. Wasn’t it RPGod 3 you lost in the first round during?” “Shut up,” Tyler replied hearing the smart ass remark. “He just got lucky when he won it. Everyone knows that in reality he couldn’t hang with me in the ring. He’s nothing more than an overgrown and out of shape ape. I could outlast him in the ring and out of it as everyone knows damn well already. I would run circles all around his punk ass and then to top it off I’d make him tap out like a little bitch to my Future Shock.” “A move you stole from me I might add,” S. interrupted with a smirk. “A move I improved upon you mean,” Tyler corrected him. “As if that Bagwell jackass isn’t enough for me to beat around the place they been feeding me this Leon Chambers dork the past few weeks. How many times can I kick the crap out of the same guy before he gets tired of it and gives up? Him and his whole little Old FSW Posse can go to hell as far as I’m concerned it’s out with the old and with the new and Tyler Davidson is the new.” “I guess that makes them all the old?” S. asked finishing the sentence for him. “Damn right,” Tyler agreed. “I’ll have myself a special surprise for Bagwell on Wednesday night if he tries getting involved in my match but its O’Neil I need to watch out for. He can be pretty damn tricky from what I seen so far.” “Boy you’re right on top of your game today aren’t you?” S. replied. “I figured you wouldn’t even know who you were facing this week.” “Well if you were any kind of manager you would make sure I knew this stuff,” Tyler responded. “So tell me about this O’Neil guy. What do you know about him?” “Not a damn thing,” S. answered. “What?” Tyler asked dumbfounded. “Aren’t you supposed to know this stuff and like manage and advise me on it all?” “Screw that,” S. replied. “That would mean a lot of work for me.” “…………” “Besides he’s facing you anyway,” S. continued. “It’s not like he would have much of a chance of actually winning.” “True,” Tyler agreed. “You should still find some stuff out about him for me though. That’s what managers are supposed to do.” “Nah that’s cool,” S. replied. “I’ll pass on that part of the job.” “Damn lazy ass,” Tyler mumbled under his breath. “What was that?” S. asked. “Nothing,” Tyler replied quickly. “Was just saying how I really need to get back to work here.” “Oh right,” S. responded. “You go and do that while I take care of some of my own business here. I’ve got some people to go fire again.” The scene fades to black.
