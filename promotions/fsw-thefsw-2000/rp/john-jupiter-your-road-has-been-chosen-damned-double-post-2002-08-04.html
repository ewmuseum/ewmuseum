John Jupiter. Your Road Has Been Chosen. (((Damned double post)))

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-04-02 -----Retribution. Time is the greatest healer, or so they say. The old quote means that with time, you begin to forget the hate and anger that something once caused deep down inside you. You begin to be more open to forgiveness. Magnum Wolf does not live by this quote. You piss him off one time, you better have a damn good reason for it, or a damn good arguement for why he shouldn't just drop you clean on your head. John Jupiter has neither of these things. Retribution is something that John Jupiter will soon receive.----- John Jupiter. John Coens. John whatever the hell his name wants to be whenever the hell he wants to it to be that. CCW superstar. Wow. PCW... well, PCW something or other. Not very much, as memory serves. So let's focus on his time in CCW. It was backstage that I met Mister Coens. I was a member of LCW-Mercury, and he was sticking his nose where it didn't belong. In my damned business. Sometimes, I have been called a hothead. Or even arrogant, of all things. I've been known to say things I probably shouldn't. But I've also been known to speak the truth. And the damn truth about the matter of CCW was that in the state it was in during my tenure in LCW-Mercury, it sucked. Their finest example of a match was a BURNING BUILDING MATCH, for God's sake. So, as you can plainly see by that match alone, I had to ridicule them. To no end. And as I did, the so-called CCW legends began coming out of the wood work trying to get a peice of Magnum Wolf. They saw some upstart nobody talking trash, and immediately thought that their name would be enough to scare me away from the path I was on. So I said up yours to Venneman Penndragon. I said up yours to John Coens. I gave the proverbial middle finger to Ace Hawk. I was untouchable. The number one guy in PTC. THE MAN. And John Coens just couldn't give up this quest to piss me off. His mouth ran, and my mouth ran. I attempted to have matches with him... and he continued to run away from me in fear. So I gave up on him. Figured that our paths would never cross. For his good, and the good of the rest of my career. But now. Now, it is my time. Now I have been given the chance to take care of some long awaited business. He doesn't realize how much of a mistake he's really made here. He went into that ring the other night with intent to only attack SDMJK. He acted as though he didn't know who I was. And maybe he doesn't actually remember. All the worse for him. This was my third match in FSW. A match that was once again lost because of outside interferance. You can only take this kind of crap for so long, you know? The last time, my patience was tested. This time, my patience has been snapped into two peices. John Coens walked into a mine field without even knowing it. He walked over me to get to his target in SDMJK. He paid for it physically that night. There is no doubt in my mind, or in the minds of anyone who was there that he felt retribution from me physically. But the retribution sees no end in mere physicality. My arguements with this man were a part of the events leading to my being fired from LCW. This man's words sent me into such fits of anger as I've never had. This man's complete IGNORANCE of who I was when he climbed into MY ring pissed me off so badly... that now? He has to pay. And continue to pay until I decide he's had enough. Blood is the beginning, Mr. Coens. But blood is not anywhere near the end. At least not for you. You see, it's not about titles. It's not about money. It's about pride, Mr. Coens. I've been awaiting this match for quite some time now, and I don't plan to waste my opportunity to get you into the ring. I plan to use this chance to my advantage. The last man in FSW who pissed me off ended up being wheeled out of the arena on a gurney. Compressed his punk ass spine with my finisher, John. I don't play around with people like you. I go into the ring. I do my job. I drop you on your head a few times. And if need be, I will go BEYOND my own restraints to get that job done. The problem here is that you don't know how badly I want this match. You don't realize the amount of anguish you are about to go through. You can't begin to comprehend what I have in store for you, Mr. Coens. Wednesday night on Glory is my night.
