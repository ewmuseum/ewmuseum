The first battle in a very long war

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today George Armstrong Custer, a few days before The Battle of Little Big horn went to a psychic. Widely regarded as quacks at that time, Custer was a man who believed in the occult. He embraced it. Before all battles, he would go and see how his losses could turn out. On this fateful day, he went to see his psychic. She told him that tremendous losses would occur in his next battle. There would be one, sole survivor. Being the ego driven man he was, he only could assume that that one man would be himself. As he rode into battle he took chances most generals wouldn’t even dream. He split his armies at the flanks, knowing that he would be the only man to walk away from this battle with his life. He lead the charge, sword held high, ready to take all them damn injuns out. Low and behold, there was one survivor. His horse, Commanche. Custer, just like myself, took risks, and came out on the short end of the straw. However, mine didn’t result in my death, like his. No, mine took me to a fate much worse. My fate, has been determined already. I know it’s coming, and I accept it. Destiny lead me to being the man I am. I’m certainly not the biggest of men, standing six foot two inches tall. I’m not the strongest or fastest. I’m merely an average man, with an above average talent. The talent for getting under people’s skin. Angelo Deville is just such a case. In the very beginning, I gave him a chance. It was I who was going to approach him about solidarity, about sticking together, rookie to rookie, friend to friend. He took those thoughts, and laughed at me. He put me down, like no other man has before, and probably never will. But he started something. Something golden. Something the FSW needs more than anything. We’re heroes and villains. Angelo, the tough on the outside, pained on the inside villain, hurting inside, bringing his pain to others to feel some sort of joy in his pathetic life. The Scarecrow, the hero, with his past, making him the man he is today, fighting for the little man. The overlooked man. In the end, there will be only one survivor. Unlike Custer though, I’m more cautious, more cunning. Genesis, will not be our little big horn, Angelo. It will be just the beginning of the war that will see more bloodshed, more tyranny, and more pain than any other in history of man to man combat. In the end, who will come out on top? Who will be the sole survivor? Only time will tell.
