Calling a meeting

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-17-02 Calling a meeting... EOE working with other people? Being team oriented? EOE coinciding with people that he'd rather see roll over a die? Yep it's true, EOE for once has decided to be a team player with people that he would drop off in his hood and leave them for dead. Why? That's the question he had to ask and come up with an answer for himself. For one thing he didn't think anyone would have the nerve to come up to him and ask him to join any kind of team, but hey Big D and Mike have two of the biggest egos on this planet and EOE wouldn't put any thing past them. He was shocked to see them swallow their pride and approach him and ask himfor help but who wouldn't want him as a partner? When EOE was first approached with this idea it wasn't pleasant at all, let's just say he told them were to take their idea and put it. But then he started to think to himself, 'you've proved you can kick ass in twos, fours, sixs, eights it doesnt matter, why not try something different and make it easy on yourself, you're not getting any younger.' He knows they're using him to make it easy for them so why not do the same for his benefit. EOE has alot of pride, but he's not dumb, all my life he's had to make sacrafices and when he ranks this with all the other sacrafices that he's made, it doesn't measure up to any of them. It's just a matter of keeping his feelings in, pretending he doesn't see or hear certain things, and putting his personal differences aside....for as long as he can stand it of course. EOE has have never liked Big D, Mike and that other person for the people they are he never really knew Leon but I've known for a long time that when it comes to being successfull in FSW and making it a historic plce, they've been some of the best along with me and if any outsiders would try to invade thier territory he would be the first to defend it along with them like he's done before. And what do you know? We have outsiders we have other people coming from other places trying to take their shit, but as long as EOE is in FSW, that's not gonna happen. He told you by any means. So far they've been holding our own, but there's some things that he thinks they should start focusing on more, it's time for them to let people know exactly what it takes to be a top player here and that's why I called this meeting at a private place in Boston, Massachusetts. EOE didn't ever think he would meet up with any of these.....people any other place but the ring but trust it's all gonna be worth it in the long run. EOE with a blunt in his mouth along with Eve in his are sitting there in the private conference room waiting patiently for them, while soft Godfather like music plays. EOE- Where in the hell are these guys they told me they were on their way. Eve- Well you know slow people do slow things, I still don't understand this. I mean why do we have to look as the guys so often now, I'm used to you tossing them around now ya'll are all buddy buddy. Eve apparently still cant deal with situation. EOE- Look, first of all they are not my buddies we are just business partners, and didn't I tell you just to chill it will all work out my way on down the line, trust me. Eve- Well how far on down the line are we going to have to go? {EOE inhales and begins to talk while he's choking.} EOE- Just chill ok, aaahhhhaa! damn this is some good shit. Wait I hear cars driving up it must be them, must be them. ~The secret knock is heard at the door that EOE gave.~ EOE- Yep that's them. (EOE opens the sliding peep door confirms it's them then opens the door, all four a meeted with a cloud of smoke.) (Big D comes in his hand over his nose followed by Mike, Leon, and ???) Big D- Man?!? Before the match? Put that out will you. EOE- Hey I kicked your ass high, won the title high, and I'm going to stay high, so deal with it. But if you're to bitch then... {EOE ashes it in the ash tray. As they all take their seats.} Mike- So what's on your mind. Leon- And this better be good, I hope you're not waisting time. EOE- Do you think I want to look at you for the hell of it? I don't... trust. Big D- Well that was rude. EOE- Look business de family has been good, but I think it can be better. We need to ehhhh... Mike- Man why are you talking like the Godfather? {EOE gives Mike a look of being disturbed}. EOE- Just play along...I'm high. (EOE continues with his Godfather like accent.). Now, business for the de family has been good, but we need to ehhh...start going for bigger and better things. Now the Five Star Five, we have to put it down we have to take things and run with it. You see these people don't know what it takes to be a FSW top player. {??? starts to play along} ???- Ehh what do you mean. EOE- I mean gold. Leon- He means titles. Mike- He means championships titles. EOE- Good good, you see where I'm going with this. ??- But there's a problem...Evilfatha, how do we decide which gold do we go for, ehhh it's obvious that we want the big one. Big D- Yes this big one. Mike- The grand prize. Leon- The one that means you're the shit. EOE- You see the Evilfatha has been thinking of ways to ehhh..to make this legit. The problem is how do we do that. Big D- Straws. Mike- No No. ???- Paper, rock, scissors. Big D- I don't like that game. {Eve cuts in} Eve- How about you fight for it? EOE- No competition my lady, no competition. {They all look at EOE}. Mike- The hard part is, finding a way to sort these titles out with out one of us not being pleased. Leon- Yea that's the hard part. (They all look around each other with suspicious as the music plays. EOE lights his blunt and continues to smoke.) EOE- We all have big things going tonight and I don't want this conference to interfer with what we have to do tonight. So I tell you what, how about we all continue to think and when you think of something you call of conference. Does that sounds good. Big D- Yes Evilfatha. Mike- Ehhh sounds ok to me. Leon- We shall meet again. ???- Yes, meet again. {EOE starts to laugh.} EOE- Damn we're all crazy, get out of here. I see you at the arena. {They all get up from their chairs, but you can tell they're all still thinking. Big D is the last to leave but he stops.} Big D- I hope you are able to atleast move tonight. EOE- I'll be ok, now get out. {EOE blows the smoke his face and slams the door. EOE turns and looks at Eve}. EOE- He about we all fight for it. (EOE says mocking Eve shaking his head.) Eve- It was just a suggestion. Now give me that you have a match tonight. {She takes the blunt out his hand.} EOE- You're just trying to start things.
