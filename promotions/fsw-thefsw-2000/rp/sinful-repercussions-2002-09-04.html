Sinful Repercussions

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 4, 2002 Well, it was a start.. About the Friday after Genesis, I finally began to return to my public life as usual...It took a little time to get over the general fear that I would get laughed at by everyone I met, for I'd never really taken such humiliation as the night brought, but after a couple of false starts with crude remarks and such by the typical fans in bars and such, I started my road back to a shameless mainstream life with the places where I knew I wouldn't be likely to endure the brutal mocking of the lower classes of fans...I stayed around the more posh establishments, or just bought my own food from the supermarket, I guess I was just still a little fan-shy, I wanted to forget it'd ever happened and I figured that though that wouldn't be possible, I could always take my societal re-entry gradually, start out with the nice people and then return to my roots when one of two inevitable things happen: Either I'll learn to live with my shortcomings' publicity, or they'll just do as most human beings tend to do, and forget as they would tend to let their own minds' memories drift into blurry snapshots of the night, gradually breaking down into a faded, distant unknown, yes they will undoubtably let themselves forget in due time...But will they let themselves let me? I grasped my keycard, slipping it into the door-mounted recognition device, and immediately back out after just a split-second, the time just enough..to frustrate me, as naturally the signal light remained red...I placed the card back in, the signal read green, and I turned the door handle and pushed, but I failed yet once more, delays of maneuvering getting to me..I tried once again, and the timing finally fell into place...I didn't delay much upon entering the room, just tossed the card to the table at my left, dropped the bags to the floor, and laid myself down upon the bed... And I started to think... Considering my track record lately in the ring when I did think, probably not the best idea... Perhaps I should just have let anger take over, as it naturally would have in the weeks after Genesis, for while getting the groceries in a nice, positive environment that the market naturally displays, indulging in a sweet bowl of Haagen-Dazs, or immersing myself in a warm bath pleasured me in a way to distract me from my rage, they were all only temporary, and I always afterward reiterated my pure hatred for the man who had felt it little more than a pleasing joke to strip me bare inside the ring in front of a nationwide audience...Unlike before, with Blair, there was little provocation of the time, you would have thought that his tainted victory previously over me would have been enough for him, but the bitch just couldn't help but come back for more, try to show who the big dog was perhaps, but this didn't honestly make a lick of sense..In all the time I knew him, this was the only time he'd ever pulled something so demeaningly personal on a foe of his, he'd never even done anything like this back when I'd originally broken away from him and gpne on to greater things, he didn't even do anything like this to the women he verbally demeaned in his time.. ...Had he ever done much with a woman besides try to act macho? All the time I knew 'Big Daveylicious' and his ridiculous attempts to bolster his masculinity, we'd become quite good friends..But maybe his entire female compulsion was nothing more than mere overcompensation publicly for private thoughts he knew all too well, a jiltedness that struck deep at his heart on that fateful November day, let linger until it stewed to a boil...Okay, now I was getting ridiculous. Big D, for all I know, probably was gay, God knows the overcompensation thing but due to low budget, I didn't get a private shower stall away from the rest of the locker room in the old day, he almost surely had to have known about my size in advance, and that made sense in two ways...It firstly would probably have turned him rather off, and secondly, he knew that I had a weakness just waiting to be exploited in this case, and his thirst for revenge on me could be satisfied fully by the mere fact that thanks to him, the world knew of my miniscule member... So he waited..And waited....And when I packed up and left my ownership position for greener pastures, he left soon after. I reached a deal with Five Star, and when he realized I was here, his motivation grew double what it had previously been, as not only could he get his ultimate revenge on his former friend Timothy, but he could come back to his original base of fans and regain the stardom of his past in the process, re-establishing him in the community after his mediocre PCW run, losing matches left and right without the interest of fans who just didn't see him as entertaining.. From the moment they heard the name, he was doomed, I guess. 'Big Daveylicious'? Two hundred and forty-some pounds barely cleared the cruiserweight limit, let alone being big, when they heard big the fans thought of the Russian Bear, the Monster they call Meanstreak, or even FSW's own Ameri-Czech Badass..Hell, the word could be associated with lots of also-rans in his time, Samoans, Satanists, 'Swamp Dawgs', hulking mental deficients, 'Edgecrusher', and even a big pointless black guy who just ran in and beat up people the boss didn't like..These men were hardly worth praising, but generally they lived up to the reputation of being literally big men, as they held great size advantages over their opposition, including while placed against 'Big' Daveylicious...It was ridiculous, how could they expect him to be successful while he was unable to take on other legitimately big men without at least 3 other men at his side? Eventually, they realized that he'd have to take on smaller opponents to be considered big, but by then it was rather too late, as after the women all left, the cruiserweights were all still busy fighting amongst themselves and the charade was just kidding itself now to put him against opponents of at least similar stature and still refer to him as big, and the crowds reacted as would be expected..This sort of a man may have been thought large in the old days back in F-S-Dub's prior incarnations, but the fans I grew to love in my old days knew that one couldn't just claim to be big without backing it up in his size, and his attempts to be a power wrestler typically failed miserably when put against people larger than him, leaving him hopelessly mired in the low-level matches out of stubbornness to change his style. The name Daveylicious just continued to betray the man's unfailing serious attitude of his early PCW run, as he generally was motivated by his own selfish desires, greed, lust, and when it came to me, envy..The guy was a walking deadly sin, and yet he remained on the roster without showing any real drive or determination to actually succeed for any truly proper motivation, even the name Big Daveylicious showed his true inner perverse fantasies, wishing little more in material value besides monetary gain than to have an entourage of attractive women of loose moral caliber...I stopped my blinking as I lay, now staring at the ceiling, asking myself why I had ever fallen so far as to accept him as an associate... The answer was simple. Money.... It had odd power to me...Both the power to bring together men who in normal situations would not acknowledge each other, and the power to fuel their sinful desires...But as in the case of Big Daveylicious and myself, when the two met... The collision was one.... Of ultimate repercussions.
