GTT · Facing Fate

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: whoo~! The sleek black Cadillac DeVille roars down the highway at double the posted speed limit of 80 km/h, smoothly merging in and out of the light traffic with the finesse of a NASCAR racer. The front of the car is speckled with dust, dirt, and entire cities of hapless insects, but the rest is in immaculate condition. The sunroof and windows are open to take advantage of Summer's last hurrah, facilitating a blanketing of the area with the soothing sounds of the Stones. A large arm draped in black silk hangs nonchalantly out the driver's side, the fingers keeping time with the music. Angelo Deville smiles as he switches lanes to surpass yet another cautious motorist who shouldn't be on the road. Deville doesn't even hear the scandalised honking of the horn as he whips past, though he wouldn't have paid it any mind if he had. Deville enjoys driving and views it as a competition, and like with any contest of skill, he expects his opposition to recognise their own inferiority and content themselves with eating his dust, whether literally or figuratively. Of course, he doesn't mind if they make a small protest as he fires past - that's to be expected of anyone with even a hint of pride. Likewise, he expects that the 'victim' not get angry when their wailing is ignored - that's to be expected of Deville. In spite of his reckless nature, he's never been in an accident. ...Well, he's never been in an accidental 'accident'. A car way up ahead pulls to the side of the road to avoid any possible collision with the madman, who simply continues tapping along to the music. What kind of inbred mook can't avoid smashing into someone while doing something as mindless as driving? Deville queries his mind rhetorically. The scared and the stupid, he responds anyhow, ruefully shaking his head. If you're scared to drive... Get off the fucking road. The rural landscape zooms by in a blur, the occasional farmhouse or line of trees providing the only lapse from the monotony of the fields. Angelo is currently on his way to the airport, coming out of a meeting I'd be wise not to detail here. A meeting regarding The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament of which Deville is a controversial contestant. Everything is falling into place now, his inner voice takes on a smug smoking-after-sex quality. As the kids would say, 'I own this bizatch!' Deville unconsciously raises a disparaging eyebrow as he glares at himself in the rearview. After a sound but silent scolding, he and his mind make a deal to never again sink so low. So do we have anything to worry about? Deville probes his mind, giving a wide berth to the section devoted to Tessa Windsor. No, the answer shouts, She's keen and persistent, but all the want in the world can't stop me from snapping her over my knee. Nothing to worry about at all... And if something should present itself, there are ways to- The thought dies abruptly and finds a string of curses rushing forth to take it's place. Deville's blue eyes turn to ice as he glares into the rearview mirror. They focus on the green shitbox rattling along the road behind him. Keeping up with him. Competing. The ancient Acclaim looks horrible with giant sections of panelling rusted completely off the sides and around the bumper. The engine has obviously been maintained, however, as it manouevres effortlessly around the curves at just over 150 km/h. 'What the hell do you think you're doing?' Angelo's incredulous voice is drowned out by the music. His grip on the steering wheel tightens as his foot tromps down on the gas pedal and the car slides smoothly up through the one-fifties and -sixties. His eyes are focussed on the mirror as much as they are the road, but still he manages to maintain perfect control... And still the car rides his ass. Are you stupid? Look at you... Deville flashes another look in the rearview and snorts. You can't test this. As if in response, a significantly less-than-dainty female arm flies out the driver-side window of the follower, happily waving a bottle of beer around in some sort of highway hellion salute. Deville purses his lips as he continues accelerating up towards 200 km/h, dodging and weaving around the occasional cars he and his adversary catch. Why must people push themselves beyond their limits? That old carriage has no dreams of keeping pace with me... Yet it fumbles along. Deville marvels at the piss-poor product trying to hum like a sports model. Can already smell the burnout... Someone put this bitch out of our misery. Deville's peripheral vision just manages to pick up a police cruiser moving towards the highway on a sideroad as he whizzes past. Goddamnit! Deville shuts the stereo down and listens for the tell-tale wailing. It hits just as the Acclaim passes the road, providing Deville with yet another reason to curse it's driver. He pushes his Caddy up to 210 and the Acclaim amazingly manages to keep up. The road settles into a straight stretch of pavement and within seconds the flashing cherries become visible in Deville's rearview mirror. The cop pulls up beside the Acclaim and hovers like an angry parent for several seconds before both cars begin slowing. Angelo grins as the shitbox throws on it's right blinker and concedes defeat. That'll teach you to leave your element... You're not ready for this. Arrogance cocoons Deville as he reaches forward and cranks the stereo up higher than it's original level. His thoughts begin to wander back to the GTT when he notices that the cop car is still on his ass. Apparently, it abandoned it's first catch in favour of bigger fish. 'No the fuck...' The words trail off as Deville crushes the gas pedal into the floormat and the car lurches up well over two-hundred. He marvels at the engine in the beast as it continues to gain on him, moving much faster than even his DeVille can go. 'Alright... Fuck it,' an angry shrug of the shoulders provides the physical translation. Smoke flies up from the back of the car as the tires unravel onto the road. They squeal like a barn full of starving pigs as the front end dips down and the ABS kicks in. The cop is forced to swerve into the lane beside Angelo to avoid a collision, but both cars eventually come to a stop with about twenty feet of space between them, the cruiser out in front. Deville closes the gap to about five feet before cutting the engine. Soiling the unspoken security blanket of the traffic cop, Deville opens his door and steps out onto the road. He feigns a casual stretch as he takes account of the situation, which has one very good and one very bad point. The bad part is that it's a female cop, whom Deville has always found to be far more aggressive than their male counterparts... Napoleon Syndrome, isn't it? The good part is that she isn't on the radio right now. Please God let this bitch be reasonable, Deville snarls as he strolls up alongside the cruiser. The officer makes no move to get out of the car so he proceeds right to her window and bends down to look in. 'Please go back to your vehicle, sir,' her voice is tense, slow, and mechanical, like a female Robocop on downers. Her blue uniform fits snugly on her taut body, outlining the type of body Deville would normally try to jump on, and her face isn't half-bad either, though her eyes are hidden behind large shades and her black hair is tucked into her hat. She doesn't bother looking at Deville to see the order carried out - she knows it will be. 'What's the problem, officer?' Deville shows no signs of cooperating, propping one elbow up onto the roof of the car and leaning so that his pelvis covers the open window. A shocked and agitated gasp sounds from her throat when she turns to repeat the order and finds herself face-to-unit with The Crimson Angel. Gritting her teeth, she flicks the latch and drives her body into the door, sending Deville sprawling to the ground amidst a cloud of curses. She reeks of satisfaction as Deville climbs to his feet and dusts himself off. He resists the temptation to simply snap her neck and be done with it as he closes the gap between them, towering over her. In an attempt to smite the intimidation, she presses her hand against Angelo's chest and pushes, but only succeeds in shoving herself against her own car. Oh god, the voice in his mind is notably disappointed now. There's no hope. Angelo smiles anyhow as he looks down at her and takes an accommodating step backwards, which secretly pleases her to no end. She glares at him from behind her shades as she plants her hands on her hips and takes a deep breath. 'What was going thr-' she begins but Angelo abruptly interrupts. 'Can you believe the nerve of that woman?' He nods his head back down the road to where the other car has presumably pulled a u-turn and hightailed it. The officer stares at him for a moment, uncomprehending. 'Excuse me?' Her brow furrows and her voice takes on an uncertain tone. 'Well, what is the likes of her trying to compete with me for? It's ludicrous,' Angelo affirms the statement with a slow nodding of his head. He sighs regrettably before continuing. 'It's people like her that ruin the system for everyone else. Where does she get off?' 'Excuse me?' The officer repeats her question, but this time the voice is fringed with outrage. She must struggle to keep it level. 'You were doing the exact same thing!' 'What?' Angelo seems genuinely shocked and hurt by this comment. 'She was racing with me, damnit. I have no desire to race... I just want to speed a little. Is that a crime?' Angelo cocks his head to the side and manages to put on an abashed face. '...Yes! Yes it is!' She shouts in response. 'You were doing well over two hundred! You're in an eighty! This is going to cost you your license! At least!' Despite her emotional attachment to the job, she manages to rattle all of this off matter-of-factly. It comes across the same to Deville though: unacceptable. He takes off the mask and glares down at the petite authority before him, burning hate into her soul. 'Let's not get carried away,' he pitches, 'I'm sure we can work something out.' The officer's eyes go wide at the implied bribe, and she raises an admonishing finger. 'Don't insult me, sir,' she warns flatly, then adds as an afterthought, 'or yourself.' 'Surely there's something you want...' Deville lets the last word trickle off his tongue, teasing the trepidation in the air. 'What?' Her face suddenly tightens and her words come out stilted and angry. 'Oh I see. You think you can push me around because I'm a woman!' Angelo raises his arms to protest and his eyes go wide with surprise, but she nods her head vigorously and continues. 'Everyone thinks that! I'll show you! RAR!' 'I never said or implied anything of the kind... Uh... You bitches are doing a bang-up job,' Angelo fumbles for something as she turns back to the car, trying to muddle his way through this unexpected territory. 'Nice work, toots.' Her back goes momentarily rigid, but that's the only sign of having heard him she'll concede. She reaches in through the window and pulls out a pen and pad. Continuing to ignore Deville, she flips the pad open and begins scribbling furiously. Deville takes a slow step forward, then another, leaving mere inches between the two of them. 'Back up... Now,' she barks. 'Or what?' Deville challenges, voice oozing with belligerence. 'Or I'll twist you up, that's what,' her demeanour is cool and calm, but the voice trembles slightly. 'You're a buck-ten! Soaking wet!' 'You'd be surprised!' The wan smile reinforces the statement, but Angelo isn't buying it. 'You'll hurt me, will ya?' She nods. 'Will I like it?' Angelo asks with a grin as he presses his body up against hers. She gasps and tries to withdraw, but the car is right there pinning her in. 'Move!' The below is low and guttural, not at all feminine. Angelo does move, but only his hand. His right hand jerks up and seizes her much smaller one, forcing her to drop the notebook and pen. Her other hand darts instinctively for her gun, but just as it closes around it is enveloped by Angelo's left. 'You... You're under arrest!' She stammers as she struggles futilely to wriggle free. Angelo chuckles under his breath as he leans forward so his lips are almost touching her ear. 'Let's get physical, baby,' he whispers, making her heart pound with fear and an involuntary shudder wrack her body. She can't tell in her state of mind, but there is no sexual connotation in his words. Perhaps it is best that way. 'Stop! Now!' She lifts a knee into Angelo's crotch, but he manages to block with his own knee before tying up her legs with his. 'What's wrong with you?' She cries in frustrated desperation, the first real signs of an emotional breakdown beginning to surface with a sob. 'Me?' Angelo demands in a loud voice, incredulous. 'Nothing's wrong with me! I was just driving along minding my own business. You're the one that came along and interrupted my ride... There wasn't a peep of trouble before you showed up, and now you want to waste my time and push me to the edge?' The officer continues trying to struggle, but Deville easily overpowers her and keeps her in place. 'Well, I can't allow that,' Angelo says, his voice a whisper once more. 'I'm going to make an example out of you.' The futile struggling intensifies many-fold as she feels her arm with the gun begin to rise up along her side, forced to hold the gun by Angelo's glove-like hand. She doesn't know whether he is simply trying to scare her or not, but the tears begin flowing freely and she begins pleading for mercy. 'No,' she moans, 'please! I'll do anything! Let me go! Help!' The gun continues tracing it's way up underneath her arm and onto her shoulder. A car fires by in the opposite direction but the driver pays no mind to the scene. 'We can just forget all of this! I'll leave! I promise! Please!' Each word is interjected with a sob, and the struggling is so intense now that the whole car is shaking and Angelo is actually having to strain to enforce his will. 'Too late now,' he blows into her ear, 'so just relax and accept fate. It's far better that way.' The gun runs up onto her neck, the cold steel raising goosebumps on her flesh. Tears drip onto the barrel as it scales her face and stops just beside her eye, pointing straight into the temple. 'It's your own fault,' Deville informs her solemnly as he massages her finger onto the trigger. 'I was content to just coast the whole way, but you had to come along and ruin that.' Deville shakes his head almost regretfully as she again begs through the tears, this time able to mutter only gibberish. 'You want to get in my way and call me out? Act like a big shot? Push me around? Well shit, here I am,' he states the obvious, then goes on in a voice to make a polar bear shiver, 'and what did it bring to your career? To your life?' A loud and continuous wail of pure fear escapes her lungs as the pressure on her trigger finger increases slowly. She can feel herself sealing her own coffin, but she's helpless to stop it. In spite of the sound booming from her own throat, her mind is eerily quiet and alive, perhaps more alert than ever before. 'The end,' the whispered answer cuts through her mind and echoes like a siren in a cave. Before it has time to resonate she hears a click from inside the chamber of the gun, then everything goes black. The explosion puts an end to the piercing wailing, basking the area in complete and utter silence save for Deville's heavy breathing. Face and upper torso splattered with blood, he still supports the officer's limp body in his hands. The ringing in his head is cool and refreshing, symbolising another fight fought and won. Angelo releases the dead body, making it fall to the ground like a pile of dirty clothes, and turns to the cruiser. Without hesitation he retrieves a zippo from his breast pocket and sparks a flame, then retrieves a cigar from a pocket on the other side and lights it. He puffs triumphantly for several seconds, then tosses the lit zippo underneath the driver's seat before turning his back on the mess. Tendrils of smoke begin rising from the car as Angelo skips over the growing puddle of blood and climbs back into his Cadillac. Time to get back on course, he scolds as he pulls away from the horror... Now where's the competition?
