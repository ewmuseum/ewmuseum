Every Got Fecked By a Squirrel?

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/11/02 SCENE OPEN The scene opens in the picturesque landscape of an apparently lifeless forest. Only an occasional gust of wind rippling each needle on the thousands of pine trees brings this fairytale scene into the world of sound. Even the grassy floor seems to have difficulty in exposing life it could possibly shelter. As the camera pans up slowly to the slightly bleak sky we hear a snap of a twig from something of obviously sufficient weight. The camera pans slowly back down and we see three figures emerging through the rough landscape of plant life. The figures are those of three close friends (although two of them would most certainly not agree to that). The man leading the pack is Patrick Smith, an Englishman who can only be described as sophisticated. Clad thickly in suit, tie, sunglasses and bowler hat, he amazingly doesn’t seem to be dirtying any part of his spotless suit – even his neatly parted hair isn’t moving in the wind! The next man is by no means a small man or even a timid man; he is in fact Patrick McClintock (born and proudly bred in the Highlands of Scotland), a man whose mouth has got him in many a tough situation with only his shy strength was there to get him out of it. Dirty and smelly as ever, he wears his traditional Kilt and a tartan beret that shields his face (which is clad in a thick ginger beard containing a meal of food) from the sun. The final man, struggling but proudly holding his own, is Paddy O’Shea, a resident of Ireland who saw his incredibly promising career in PCW fall when a vicious drunk-driving accident suspended his future. O’Shea who recently signed a contract with FSW is hoping he can take the once strong potential he had and put it into action in strengthening his wrestling career in FSW. Clad in an emerald green and black waistcoat, tan shirt and black trousers, O’Shea seems to be struggling for soberness even by the way he’s walking Paddy Englishman: Beautiful. Just Beautiful. Almost as if taken from a fairytale. The forest seems lifeless with only natural forms disturbing this pleasant area yet you can sense the place is just so alive. Something special wouldn’t you say so Paddy? Paddy Scotsman: (confused)…yeah…just beautiful…remind me again, why the hell are we in this hippies heaven? I bet those trees are drugged up at this very moment. Paddy Englishman: Don’t be ridiculous. We’re here because I hoped it would bring an insight for Patrick into what Casey Deltura is really about. I believe fighting fire with fire by perhaps relaxing Patrick will keep him in control and make his match successful. After all, debuts can end a career before it barely starts. Isn’t that right Patrick?…PATRICK! NO! The two are soon locked at the sight of Patrick O’Shea aiming and pushing piss as hard as he again at a nearby squirrel who seems to have stumbled from his slumber at all the commotion. O’Sheas aim stays true as he watches the bullet from the gun turning the chestnut coloured squirrel a brown-black. O’Shea falls to the ground in fits of laughter with his amusement obviously shared in McClintok. Paddy Englishman: That was totally unacceptable Patrick – your time urinating on a squirrel could easily be changed into perhaps trying to care about beating Deltura! You have soiled this natural environment; I hope you’re proud of yourself. Paddy Scotsman: You know what Smithy, you’re bang on the target. This forest needs to be natural and the only way to restore that is to get NAKED! Without a second thought, McClintok is ripping his clothes off and then very soon after, he’s scampering up a tree naked and causing all sorts of commotion with the obviously disgruntled animals. Paddy Irishman: HOLY FECK! Aye ne’er knew Scotsmen had such hairy arses. If anyone ever goes blind an’ wants to slot their chicken in his hen house then they can expect a good place t’ sleep. No’ where the fecks tha’ bottle o’ Scotland’s finest O’Shea quickly rummages through McClintoks deserted clothes and it isn’t long before he emerges with a quarter bottle of vodka. It takes even a shorter time to take the lid off and sink it. Paddy Englishman: Please Patrick, can we just get a benefit from this visit and perhaps make a decision right here over whose going to win between you and Deltura. By properly preparing you can’t lose Paddy Irishman: (childishly giggling) Ten bob on Deltura Paddy Englishman: Well fine Patrick, if you can’t take my kind gesture to help you become a better wrestler then fine. I’ll enjoy myself then and go bird watching. At least then the visit won’t be a complete failure although I’m sure you can muster a pint of Guinness to not let that happen as well With that in mind, Smith isn’t long storming off obviously quite disgruntled even after living with O’Shea all this time. O’Shea seems to taunt him as he walks although it could be mistaken for a natural body instinct to wipe off his drunkenness. As soon as Smith seems to be out of sight, O’Shea stumbles over to a nearby tree before sliding down it clumsily. As he strains to maintain even a sitting position he begins to feel sleepy. He begins to talk to himself but his drowsiness can be detected in his speech. Paddy Irishman: Arse feckin’ hole. Who the feck does tha’ knob think he be. In case he doesn’ notice, aye pledge no allegiance t’ no queen…Now…where the fecks tha’ Vodka…? Aye…think…tha’…ay- With that, Paddy nods off to sleep with an empty bottle of vodka still clutched in hand. Patrick is soon in dreamland as he dreams himself waking up in the exact spot he’s sleeping in. Paddy Irishman: Feck, tha’ was quite a sleep. No’ where did those two get to? Hey, wha’ tha’ feck be tha’ right there? O’Shea seems to be pointing to a bundle of fur. As it turns, we see it is the same squirrel O’Shea previously urinated on. Only his tail is left dry and O’Shea tank has been refilled with the addition of the vodka he early drank. Paddy Irishman: (sniggering) Well…if ye wash a T-Shirt, ye might as well wash tha’ matchin’ shorts With that, O’Shea quickly pulls down his trousers. The camera sings round violently as he extracts his prized sheepdog. As O’Shea childishly aims in the air, the squirrel jumps in the air and as if his body froze in the void, he begins to guzzle it, scaring the piss round back up the drainpipe. Paddy Irishman: Wha’ the feck? Aye thought they put feckers like ye in area 51. Now wha’ tha’ feck would ye be? Much to Paddies dismay, the squirrel respond to his question Squirrel: I’m a squirrel dickweed, what do you think I am? A leprechaun? Paddy Irishman: Hey ye wee feck. Aye think aye would kno’ a leprechaun if I saw one Squirrel: I wouldn’t get too cheeky with me, after all, you do seem to have a problem with your gentiles. And it’s not just the size of them… O’Shea shyly looks down at his fully exposed cheesy war knob. He quickly pulls everything up and tucks what can be tucked back in, going red with shame. Paddy Irishman: …well…at least aye don’ feel so insecure tha’ aye have t’ spend all Autumn collectin’ nuts. Squirrel: (Obviously a bad-tempered squirrel) I wouldn’t annoy me, you will regret it. That is unless you generally drive on the wrong side of the road and by the smell of it, I think I would be right. That is if you have the brains to know what I mean O’Shea as arrogant as ever seems to underestimate what the squirrel is planning to do and continues his verbal abuse of the critter Paddy Irishman: Yeah, whatever ye say. In me opinion, if yer tail is bigger than yer dick then there be a problem right there. Would aye be right? Unless o’ course ye got a feckin’ puss! Swelling with rage, the bad-tempered little squirrel seems to erupt and he lunges at O’Shea. As O’Shea turns to run, the squirrel grabs him by the backside and brings him to the ground. The squirrel goes straight to work in putting his little…er…squirrel in O’Sheas backyard. O’Shea screams for mercy but the squirrel only digs deeper Paddy Irishman: MERCY! MERCY! AYE’LL GIVE YE GOLD! PLEASE! AHHHHHHH! IT’S ENLARGING! As O’Shea screams, he faints from the shock. When he wakes up he is back out from his dream and apparently sobered up. O’Shea begins to frantically rub his backside in the hope it’s the right size Paddy Irishman: (To apparently no one) Bastarding squirrels. Jest ye try an’ bumfeck me again an’ aye’ll rip off yer little maggot. McClintok seems to pick this up and he swings down on a branch and lands on the ground naked beside O’Shea clutching a small furry bundle. Paddy Scotsman: Everything alright big guy? I heard you screaming in my time of becoming one with the animals A cheesy grin comes over him face. O’Shea rolls his eyes but when he looks down he sees the small bundling moving. His eyes increasing enlarge until the point when a squirrel’s head emerges. This makes O’Shea release a loud bloodcurdling screaming. O’Shea quickly talks flight and as McClintok looks on in confusion, O’Shea goes out of site in the direction of Smith SCENE CLOSE
