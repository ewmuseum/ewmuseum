Finding An Alignment (Part 1): The Setup

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-3-02 The first loss. To most wrestlers, it is the toughest to take. In this certain wrestler’s bloodline... Any loss is hard to swallow... Most especially, the first. To go back into the past, and give a little history on first losses in the Irving family name... I will bring you to the Dream Wrestling Federation and the most recent, excluding Christopher Irving himself, of the Irving Family Wrestlers... You already guessed his name: Doozer. Doozer fought seven matches before losing one in the Dream Wrestling Federation, or as he now calls it... After dominating it, the Doozer Wrestling Federation... And after that first loss, Doozer quit. He quit, only to be let back into the federation in a day or so. He fought about four more... Lost again and walked again. To move even further back on the timeline... I can bring you back to another well-known wrestler. He went by the alias Chris Cross, a Grandfather to Christopher Irving, and that was his real name as well. After Chris 'Cross' Irving’s first loss, he seemed to lose a little swagger in his step. Maybe the same swagger that intimidated his opponents and lead him to a twenty-three match winning streak... Yet, when he lost that first match and that swagger... He kept losing like it was just another bad habit. Chris Cross lost eight matches in a row, falling to twenty-three and eight... He left wrestling shortly after his eighth loss in a row... Winning his final match against a jobber... More of a gift from the owner of his fed than a match really; since the two were very good friends and the owner didn’t want Chris to leave on a bad note, a losing note. And we could travel further back in time and I could tell you about every last Irving in wrestling, but I just don’t have the time. I will say one thing about all of them though... It seems to be a common trait... They don’t seem to lose often. Yet, when they do, they are not happy about it. To make the level of hatred towards losing more clear... Think of it this way... Say, you don’t like to lose... Put yourself on a scale from one to ten... Times that by four, add three more, and then double it... That’s how much an Irving hates losing. But will Christopher Irving ‘The Canadian Criminal’ leave Five Star Wrestling? Maybe... If Mike Marvelous or Jacob Jackson have anything to say about it. Well, they don’t... So he won’t... Will he go on a losing streak? Not if Doozer has anything to say about it... And he does, so he won’t. And, if there is anyone who knows how to stop a losing streak from occurring, before it starts... It is the boy’s uncle, Doozer... The most losses Doozer has ever taken in a row... Two... And it’s happened once before. --- But today I am not here to tell you about Chris and how he is handling his controversial loss to Jacob Jackson after defeating three or four straight in the gauntlet match. I’m here to give you an update on Christopher’s day. Being as this day is almost over... I don’t believe anything else will be happening anytime soon, so I feel it is safe to give you the summary of his day. Why am I picking today out amongst the others? Today is the start to a long journey, that Chris’s uncle hopes will end up with Chris obtaining an alignment. As of this very moment, Christopher Irving is a neutral to the wrestling world... And that fact doesn’t please Doozer in the least bit. 'Sure, you can make it big as a neutral...' Doozer would tell his nephew, 'But you have to rely on talent, one-hundred and ten percent...' And Doozer, or as he now likes to be referred to as Scott Lavigne or Xavier Cross (his rapping name), liked to do anything but give one-hundred and ten percent... If it was up to him, he wouldn’t even give ten percent! So, Scott decided to set up a little get-together between the two, himself and his nephew, promising to be fully sober. With that one requirement met, The Canadian Criminal grudgingly agreed. Even though he would tell his uncle over and over about how he did not have to have an alignment... And before long, his true alignment would come to him anyway... --- At the time of their meeting, Doozer was sitting down on a park bench, in a small park atop a hill, over-looking the city. Within moments, Christopher Irving arrived on the scene. He sat down on the opposite end of the medium-sized bench, and twisted his body around partially so he was more or less facing his uncle. 'So what did ya wanna talk to me about, Unc?' Chris asked while avoiding eye contact from his Uncle. 'Well... like it or not... You need an alignment!' Doozer replied in the matter-of-fact manner. 'And I think I’ve figured out a little process that’ll help us get you one.' Christopher took in a deep sigh, hesitated for a moment, and replied... 'Seriously, I don’t think I need to be face or heel just yet... I’m getting by just fine as a neutral.' 'Did you lose that gauntlet match?' Doozer asked his nephew while already knowing the answer. 'Come on... You know I lost it... You said you would be watching...' Chris answered with a lowered head. After making his nephew relive the loss in his own head, Doozer threw out another question... 'Do you know why?' 'Well...' Before Irving could even give an answer, Scott cut him off and answered for him. 'It’s because you are a neutral, kicking everyone’s ass... And that pisses a heel like Marvelous off... So he went down there and made sure you didn’t win... If you were a heel, you’d have guys like Mike looking out for ya...' 'Maybe I don’t want guys like Mike looking out for me!' 'Hey, if you are the one losing... I don’t care what you want. I’m not letting you fall into a losing streak like the last Chris Irving!' Scott barked back at his nephew, going for the tougher approach. To more or less, lay down the law. That’s all it took, and the also short-tempered Christopher Irving stormed off, red-faced and really pissed off. As he walked... Or rather, fast-walked... Away from his uncle, Scott yelled out to him the last words they would speak to each other that night... Or well into the next day... 'IF YOU KNOW WHAT’S GOOD FOR YOU... YOU’LL SEE ME AT THE GYM! YOU KNOW WHEN, AND YOU KNOW WHERE!' Right as Scott Lavigne yelled that out to his nephew, Christopher Irving... A rather large man walked between the two relatives’ path... To say the least, the man was slightly obese... And he took offense to the comment, thinking it was aimed towards himself... 'Hey, you can jus go to hell, you muscular... well-defined...' The man’s face turned beet red and he sniffled. 'In-shape...' The man sniffled once more. 'Good-looking...' Another sniffle. 'Rich... pathetic...' The sniffles started to turn into tears... 'BASTARD~!' With the out-burst, the fat man ran... Er... Waddled away balling his eyes out while Doozer couldn’t help himself but fall to the ground, off his park bench... Laughing his ass off.
