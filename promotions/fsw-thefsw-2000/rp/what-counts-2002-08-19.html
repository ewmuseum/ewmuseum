What Counts

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday Wildly hoarse screams fill the cool night air. No, it's not what you think... These screams are the very definition of joy in it's pure and simplest form. Underneath the noise of the wailing children can be heard the soft rumble of machinery and the discordant jumble of unsynchronised instruments, as if thousands of radios were all turned on at the same time yet tuned to different stations. The smell of popcorn, cotton candy, and myriad other sugared goods tickle the nose, while spilled pop, spit-out gum, and other unmentionables stick the ground to your feet. Hundreds of people mill about in all directions, shouting to each other so as to be heard over the rest of the din. All of them without a care in the world... All of them here for fun. Fucking Exhibition, Angelo Deville thinks to himself as he glares out at all the happy faces around him. Someone always needs to be different, it seems. Goddamn sideshow for all the blissfully ignorant and incompetent to wallow in this pathetic excuse for fun and success. He is referring, of course, to the rides and the games, though truth be told he isn't really angry at any of these people. As a matter of fact, he doesn't even hate the annual spectacle that is the Exhibition... he merely detests it. 'Angelo, it's me! Meet me at the Ex tonight at ten... At that thing with the hammer and the bell. It's urgent! Somethin's terribly wrong!' Anthony's voice, almost on the verge of panic. Angelo had received the message on his answering machine almost four hours ago, at seven o'clock. It wasn't like Anthony to be this late, especially if he were the one calling the meeting. And Anthony scared? It's a contradiction of terms. Goddamn punk is too stupid to be scared. I'll show him... Wasting my fuckin' night like this. Why'd he want to meet here? Angelo has been standing beside the appointed game, this year sporting the name of 'Tower of Power', since quarter of ten. The carnie running the game has yet to give him hassle about it, but then if he was a dirty little man he doubted if he would bother a tall, angry man with bodybuilder proportions, wearing a black Armani suit and a matching fedora. Kids were another matter. They pointed and some even laughed for some ungodly rap-inspired reason, but at least no one had recognised him. The last thing he wanted was some shrill voice screaming his name and drawing attention to Five Star Wrestling's Primetime Champion. They'd all be pawing and begging for autographs, pleading with me to regale them with tales of how I beat, outsmarted, and then again beat that freakish tweener, Jason O'Neil. Angelo scrutinises the busy crowd with renewed suspicion. No, this crowd would probably just gape with awe... They don't have the mental acuity to form coherent sentences in front of the likes of me. Angelo smiles at that thought, then chuckles as he marvels at how true the statement rings in his mind. Like O'Neil, these people are the beaten and the downtrodden, destined to forever be the second - or even the third - string. For a brief moment, Angelo almost feels sympathy for them. Then he remembers how they got there and begins chuckling once more. Living under someone else's rules is the mating call of the coward. They're all like pets in the zoo, living in someone else's cage and living their lives according to how the lawmen see fit. They never get what they want because they don't know... Any time they get an independent thought their fears squash it and convince them it's wrong. Angelo shakes his head in disgust at the people around him, though none of them pay any attention. Animals. Pets. Real men get what they want... Real men make their own rules. And real men live by their own goddamned schedule, he thinks with an agitated glance at his timepiece. Fuck Anthony. I'll show that bastard fear... I'll make him my fucking pet. The grin returns as Angelo turns and stalks away from the meeting place without a look back. He plows through those in the crowd too absorbed in their own thoughts to notice the giant man striding down the street, even knocking a few of them over. Angry shouts follow him, but he ignores them. He ignores it all. The barkers. The kids. The drunks. All of them. 'Hey, wannabe Godfather!' A gruff voice bludgeons all the other sounds down. 'Big guy with the fedora!' The voice elaborates, and Angelo slows down and turns his head slightly. 'Yeah, you! Where'd ya get that suit - the Salvation Army?' A small crowd around the man bursts out laughing as Angelo stops completely and turns to face the loud-mouth. The man is standing on top of the counter of his booth, pointing right at Angelo and almost bursting his sides at his expense. Angelo gives a confused look at his suit, then turns an angry gaze on the carnie. 'No the fuck...!' Angelo barks, scandalised and shocked, as he begins marching over to the booth. The humourous face quickly turns to one of alarm as Angelo looms forward, and the man jumps behind the counter, holding up his arms in a submissive gesture. 'Woah,' he says in his grand carnie voice, 'calm down there, big guy. I'm just funnin' ya,' he soothes with a grin copyrighted by his trade. 'My assistant here figures that you're a marksman,' he continues, motioning to a beautiful but seedy-looking blonde off to his right. 'I, however, don't think you've ever shot a gun in your life. ...Care to settle the bet?' 'Sure,' Angelo responds with a humourless smile. 'How ?bout I shoot you in the fuckin' mouth?' Angelo growls as he reaches into his breast pocket. The crowd surrounding the booth gasps and even the carnie is stunned into silence for a moment. He recovers himself quickly however, and snatches one of his rigged rifles from the wall. 'Here,' he insists, handing the rifle to Deville. 'All you gotta do,' the carnie begins explaining the game as he gestures to the board behind him, which is covered with dozens of postcard-sized pieces of paper, 'is shoot out any one of these planets with one round of ammo.' Deville hesitates for a moment, hand still inside his jacket, debating whether or not a pistol-whipping is in order. After a moment's deliberation he decides against it and withdraws the hand, then accepts the rifle with it. 'Any one I want?' He asks as he flashes a wink at the assistant. 'Well, the bigger the planet the bigger the prize, of course. You can go for Pluto or Earth if you want,' the carnie sounds very disappointed with that option, as if only the sorriest excuses would ever opt for that. 'Or,' he continues, perking up like a bubbly cheerleader, 'you could go for one of the big mamas, like Saturn or Jupiter!' 'Jupiter, eh?' Angelo asks rhetorically, stroking his chin with his free hand. 'How much?' 'Ten bucks,' the carnie beams, having successfully hooked another sucker. 'And if you can do it you can take any prize hanging from the top. If you can't do it the first ti-' The carnie cuts off with a yelp as Deville begins firing the automatic B.B. gun into one of the postcards. Slowly, Jupiter is vanquishing from the paper and Deville is grinning. A small crowd is gathered around, watching with half-assed interest, wondering whether or not the game is rigged. The gun suddenly stops firing and the carnie leaps into action, jumping over to inspect the large hole in the centre of the card. Deville, arrogant in his victory, places the gun on the counter and begins nodding knowingly to all those around him. A couple of teenage girls smirk, a couple of young boys smile. The carnie turns around, shaking his head regretfully. 'I'm sorry, cap'n,' the carnie says in his practised sympathy voice as he holds the card out to Angelo. 'You missed a tiny little spot right there.' The carnie points to help Deville find the speck of colour defiantly staining the white background, then recoils as Deville draws his arm back to deliver a backhand. He studies the card for a minute, eyes narrowing the entire time. His face begins to flush and a couple of people in the crowd begin chuckling at his expense... Again. 'Bullshit,' Angelo says quietly, then when the carnie begins to protest he shouts, 'Bull-fucking-shit!' A couple of people in the crowd scream and the carnie hits the deck as Deville's Special is yanked from his breast pocket. With a glower, Deville aims the gun and fires, obliterating the entire left portion of the card and destroying the last remaining trace of Jupiter. Some people in the crowd have started running, while others simply stand by and watch, fascinated. Angelo leans over the counter to look at the cowering carnie, then lets the destroyed paper flutter down onto his back. 'What spot?' Angelo snarls as he reaches up and plucks one of the giant stuffed rattlesnakes hanging from the top. With a satisfied laugh, he turns from the booth and begins walking away. 'Hey mister,' a young voice calls up to him from around waist-level. 'That wasn't fair.' Angelo turns and sees a small boy of about five, eyes wide and disillusioned and the corners of his mouth drawn down. His mother rushes forward to pull him away from the madman carrying the gun, almost crying herself. 'Fair?' Angelo smirks as if the word is foreign in his mouth. 'Fair is for losers, kid. Winning is what counts.' The picture of pride, he turns and saunters victoriously down the road, his equalizer in one hand and his prize in the other.
