The Siamese Pussy (THE END of a series that may have gone on forever. Heh.)

1 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-01-02 ***The scene opens in a small office. The film being used is black and white... the air is filled with smoke as usual... but the smoke is darker this time. Without warning, a fire spreads from behidn the camera, and over the entire office. Within seconds, the entire place is an inferno of chairs, tables, and an especially bright flame has kicked up right around the globe in the center of the room. As the blaze continues to burn everything that used to be Magnum Wolf, the camera begins to pan back to see a sound stage, disguised as a small office. As the orange flames dance, the color begins to return to the camera, and now everything is in vivid, living color. You can almost feel the heat as the former gimmicky world of Magnum Wolf burns down into ashes. Peices of paper from the books on every shelf float in the air as the heat rises. As the camera gets further and further from the flames, you begin to see the outline of a form about 10 feet away from the 'office'. He's holding a can of gas in his right hand, and his long hair is falling over his face. With a swift snap of his wrist, the figure tosses the gas can aside, and flips his hair out of his face.*** ***Magnum Wolf stares long and hard at the camera as the fire blazes behind him. It's an eerie picture, seeing a man who was once so jovial with a sinister look in his eyes, and a fire putting an end to all that used to be Magnum 'P.I.' Wolf. The look in his eyes can only be described as contempt. Amazingly, he is looking only at a camera, but the hole he burns in it is amazing.*** 'For 5 months now, I have been on the Five Star Wrestling roster. For five months, I have been shit on by everyone from Paul Orion, to all of the staff, to the roster, right down to Chip O'Meara. I've suffered loss after loss to rediculously untalented individuals because I just didn't have the star power to deserve a push. 'So, one day, I walk into Orion's office, and I say 'What can I do to prove to you that I deserve a little something extra here in FSW?' You know what the little prick told me? He told me that I just wasn't gimmicky enough for Five Star Wrestling. I was being lost in the fold because there was nothing 'special' about me. I was just another angry heel, and no one would ever buy me pinning Tyler Nelson, or Timo Bolamba... hell, they apparently wouldn't even buy me pinning Jack Brooze. I know, not even you know who the hell Jack Brooze is... but Orion apparently thought that he was just GRAND... forcing me to job to him not ONCE... not TWICE... but THREE TIMES. 'So, I asked him how I could get more gimmicky. I liked my job here, and I wanted to keep it at the time. We had a few more meetings after that, outlining what he thought was a GOLD GIMMICK... telling me that all I had to do was cut a few promos in Film Noir style, and then act like a crazy Private Eye on TV, and WHAM BAM THANK YA MA'AM, I'd become a top draw in no time. You think that happened? No. Magnum Wolf became nothing more than comic relief. I was forced to use a plastic penis in one of my early segments with this gimmick because Orion just thought it was HILARIOUS. Yeah, what a great gimmick I had.' ***Magnum now walks across the set, which has begun to die down in heat now. He picks up a little peice of paper off of the desk. One that hasn't completely burned up, and looks at it. He points casually to a name on the list.*** 'This is where it all began. 'The Curse'. Another reason Magnum Wolf is just laughed at by most, if not all, of Wrestling Tattler. When Jack Brooze left after our feud, no one thought anything of it. It was a fluke. The guy was lazy, and he didn't want to work anymore. Then came Kyle Velocity. Then John Jupiter. Then Davidson... Bagwell... I almost feuded with Walden, but he left before the prospect even came to the forefront. Then Big Daveylicious... sort of. The gimmick was a curse, and the curse was on more than just my opponents. How do you think I felt when everyone I've ever worked with in FSW has just left? I was done with it. If I couldn't get ahead, even when doing this stupid gimmick... then I was done. 'But O'Meara didn't see it that way. Not by a long shot. I walked into his office, and asked to have my contract torn up so that I could go about my business in the wrestling world. Instead of giving me exactly what I wanted, Orion simply told me that my contract was binding, and that like it or not, I was in FSW for the long haul. Stuck in the halls that had screwed me for so long. Trapped where I didn't want to be. When I asked him for an explanation, all he told me was... 'While FSW doesn't have a place for you right now, I know a few other federations that do. And we'd rather not have you in the hands of those federations.' It was all about money, and ratings. That was all it was about. They didn't care about me, or my career. In Chip's eyes, I was being paid to do nothing, and I should just sit back and enjoy it. Unfortunately, I wasn't being paid to do nothing. He still stuck me on shows, doing stupid ass segments involving stupid ass themes. I was thrown into matches with people I had no chance of beating. I was forced to be pinned by nearly every member of the roster, and still given not a drop of respect by anyone involved.' ***Magnum walks across the set again, towards a set of legal books. He pulls one off of the shelf, and thumbs through it. It's called 'Politically Incorrect'. Symbolism. Weak, but still symbolism. He raises an eyebrow as he closes the book once again.*** 'Enter: Politically Incorrect. No, I didn't even know they were The Dead Presidents at first. But after Bad Moon Rising, they showed up at my locker room with a little proposition. They informed me that they had been trying to get out of their contracts, as well. Everything seemed to work in our favor. If they won't fire us... why not fuck with them? Seems easy enough to me. I'm not genius, but I know a good thing when I see it. So we had a plan, and it would take effect at the next Glory. We knew Mob Rule would get involved... but we have one thing that Mob Rule doesn't. Nothing to lose. Nothing to hang around here for. Whether they will admit it, or not, there is always a time where they will stop short of doing something for fear of being fired. That fear is not present with us. It is, in fact, what we want.' ***Magnum walks away from the bookshelf, and towards the camera now. He gets to a point where you can see only an upper torso shot of him, and stops, pointing at the camera...*** 'You created us, O'Meara and Orion. You made this group what it is today. It will be us who ruins this federation for everyone involved. If you thought ratings were taking a slight dip before... just get ready. We're about to make Glory as bad as it can possibly be.' ***With that, Magnum Wolf turns to his right, and grabs the gas can he tossed aside earlier. He opens the top, and throws the entire can onto the reminaing flames in the office. As the red can hits the flames, it explodes, and the inferno is once again ablaze. Magnum walks off of the set, and leaves it burning...***
