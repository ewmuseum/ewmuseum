When The World Was Young

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-08-02 “So basically, what you are trying to tell me is that I have to team up with that ass grabber?” “Umm…Timo, you got to do what they want…it’s in the contract.” “Well let’s get one thing straight bucko, I don’t like him, I don’t want to help him, but I cant just sit back and let Mike and his lover take control of this place. By gummit, FSW is going to be the place to be, and I’m gonna make sure it happens.” “Timo, you need to lay off the prospector slang.” “Con Sarnit!” Timo Bolamba, the Samoan man of the hour sits on his deck with a tall frosty glass of Shasta’s Tiki Punch. He takes a sip and curses a bit in Samoan for his lack of foresight. Now he will be stuck fighting with one of his oldest and most despised nemesis, Johnny Raindance. To his right sits his beloved wife, Gwenneth Cross, and in her arms she holds the apple”s” of his eye, his twin children, Timo Jr. and Little Lou, whom he named after his fathers best friend, Captain Lou Albano. The Samoan’s brow is furrowed a bit and he stands, clasping his hands in some sort of mock victory. “I got it!” “What?” Timo turns to face her and smiles. “I can tell him I don’t want to be on his team, and I know he doesn’t want to be on mine, so then we will just go our separate ways, and I can beat those two jokers by myself!” Gwenneth rolls her eyes and goes back to paying attention to the kids. Timo’s wild notions often earned him a lot of trouble, and this could shape up to be the same situation. He sighed a bit as he knew what needed to be done. “You think Johnny even remembers me?” “I don’t know Timo, it’s been a long time. I’m sure he was only acting on part of that sleaze Freddy Ryder anyhow. Look, you are a world apart now, and you both have went along in your careers. You have come along way Timo.” He chuckles. “What’s so funny?” “That sounds like a Charlie Brown book. “You’ve come a long way Charlie Brown”. Hehe, the Peanuts are funny because they have a dog that thinks he is flying ace.” “Snoopy?” He whips around in dramatic fashion and looks quizzical as a Samoan might. “No, I was just trying to talk about the comic strip, I didn’t mean to pry.” She rolls her eyes yet another time. He turns to walk into the house and Gwenneth looks up to the door. She holds her hand up and one of the babies lets out a bit of a sigh, making her smile and look back to them. He turns and sees his family sitting on his patio and grins. It is a fine day in Detroit Motor City, and there is only one thing to do to keep his Samoan mind fresh. Timo opened the door to his garage and flipped a light on, revealing several cars under covers. He looks near the light switch and runs his hand along the signs, stating which keys belong to which cars. “Cuda, Cobra, Corvette, Charger, Challenger, Chevelle, Camaro…Damn, I have a lot of “C” cars.” The finger runs past another ten or so names until he strikes the car that suits his taste. He smiles and grabs the keys off the wall. As he walks away, the shot catches the name on the wall: Batmobile. The sound of a well tuned Porche engine screams out of the door ad the modified F-1 Super Car flies out of the Samoans garage, a small flame behind it for dramatic effect. Timo stops at the end of the driveway and looks both ways, as any good driver should, then proceeds to tear a hole (metaphorically) in the ground with his Pirelli P-Zero Fioria tires. “Ah what a day for a good drive, the air is crisp, yet clean, the tar is warm, yet not too hot as to leave marks on my ride, and the cop that saw me peel out back there only waved. It IS good to be the king.” Timo pulls up next to an old Jeep that looks like it has seen a lot of better days. Timo slides the cockpit open as he looks at the driver, realizing who it is. The man sitting in the seat smiles and waves as Timo slips a sly grin in. “Hello loyal citizen, would you like to ride with Batman?” “Afwa want ride in Fancy car Timo-Man!” “Well my friend, you are in luck, because I just happen to have another can of cold refreshing Shasta Tiki Punch on hand, and a spare seat, care to partake?” Afwa Kilmanjaro, Timo’s Samoan friend, and old brother from the days of PCW smiles at him. He knew Timo had something up his sleeve and he had a good idea what it was. “Timo, you have new endorsement?” “Well buddy, looks like I can’t put anything past you! Come on man, we will go pull in over at that restaurant and catch up, whaddayasay?” Afwa thinks it over for a moment and smiles. He nods and gives a huge thumbs up. For a bit of brief background, Afwa stands well over seven feet in height and tips the scales at nearly four hundred pounds, so anything he does, including giving a thumbs up, is huge. The large Samoan man looks at Timo and grins, remembering how they became friends, and the one thing he always asked Timo for. “Timo, you think they have Tiger Prawns?” Timo grins ear to ear. “My man, if they don’t, I will have them flown in from Tibet!” That was the Timo Bolamba that the world had come to know, a generous man with a kind heart, even befriending one of his greatest enemies and mentoring the larger man. The two cars pull into the restaurant and pull to a stop. Timo looks up at the sign and realizes they have made it to a Kentucky Fried Chicken. Another smile graces his face. “What so funny Timo?” “Afwa, have I ever told you about the time Miyagawa, Ed and I were attacked by the Cult of Colonel Sanders?” “Me no remember Timo.” “Well, it was a cold and stormy night, we were a hankering for a meal at a good price when we came upon Kentucky Fried Chicken. Now there was a lot of opposition, but needless to say I was famished and the chicken sounded finger lickin good…” The scene fades as the two old friends enter the building. What will the future hold for Timo and Johnny Raindance? Who knows, only one thing is for certain in the Samoan World, and that is nothing is for certain. Tune in next time for more exciting tales on “Timo Bolamba…This Is Your Life!” “Wait, Wait!” came Timo’s voice. “Umm….yes?” It was the narrator. “This is your life? That is so weak!” “Umm…..okay how about…Timo Bolamba’s Half Hour of Samoan Ass Kicking!” “Sa-Weet!” fin.
