Believe...

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov 19th Domination... The word is painted in bold yellow letters on the black wall. The sign, much like the premise behind it's placement, has withstood the test of time. Etched both on the wall and in the minds of those who've labored and learned and lived it's meaning. Those who bleed black and gold; those who never say die. Dan Gable, the man whom painted the word on the wall, has a saying. 'Good things come to those who wait, but only those things that are left behind by those who hustle.' In this space, from the top rafters to down in the dungeons of Carver-Hawkeye Arena, work is not a four letter word. It's a way of life. To be able to even enter this mecca of intensity requires a man of integrity and fortitude. To be allowed back, requires a man to be a champion. Thus the invitation is always open for those who earned the right to return 'home'. The Hawkeye Wrestling Club or HWC, is comprised of former champions that are allowed to return to train in the eye of excellence after the college years have ended. Some train for the upcoming World Championships, some the Olympics, some for the sake of staying connected to their past. But for any to be able to run the loop, climb the stairs, work the weights, or hit the mats at Carver Hawkeye; they best be able to point to their name on one of the 18 NCAA National Title trophies that are encased in the glass at the entrance. Jason McAllister can. He can also tell you the exact spot on the mat where his pinned a wrestler from Penn State to win the NCAA Title at 175. The same can be said for any of the other champions in the building today. While Jim Zalesky runs the next generation though it's paces, Terry Brands and Lincoln McIlravy battle each other with an intensity just a hair shy of armed combat. John Oostendorp moves weight on the sled with a kind of power usually reserved for heavy farm or construction equipment. McAllister fights though the burning in his legs during a 'buddy carry'. A particularly draining exercise where you physically carry another person up the 450 stairs from the floor to the top of the arena, then around the mile concorse, and then back down the 450 stairs. Stopping never crosses his mind, the assault on his pride would be too much, especially knowing that the man himself was in the building. Ever-present, with a will that would make Patton look like a shrinking violet, Dan Gable's shadow loomed large over this building, this program, and all the men who gave their alleigence to to him and his philosophy... Domination... His voice inspired awe, respect, and a little bit of fear, even now as it boomed out across the building.. 'MMAAAAACC!!!!!!' Without hesitation, McAllister turned in the direction of the voice, continuing unabated until there was a sudden tapping on his shoulder. 'Umm, if you're gonna go talk to coach, at least lemme down.' McAllister nodded an apology as he released Mark Ironside, who was training for the World Championships. As McAllister ran on, he heard Mark's voice from behind him again. Ironside: Good Luck. Impossible to tell if that was meant for the match or for this encounter with Gable. To which he'd need it would be played out soon as Gable emerged from his office, limping a bit from the hip surgeries. In the coach's hand was a large brown envelope. Gable: Mail for you. You'd think they'd have stopped delivering here for you ten years ago. Gable handed over the envelope and winced at the look on McAllister's face when Jason he saw the return address. Anniston, Alabama. A plain brown envelope of that size, from that address could only mean one thing. Or could it? Without a pause, Jason ripped open the package, fully expecting 'legal documents' that needed signed. What he found took his breath away, and that of his mentor as well. An 8x10 glossy, taken years ago. Jason and John Jupiter, CCW Tag Title belts around their waists. Neurosis with his CCW Hardcore Title draped over his shoulder. Billy Conners flashing his famous 'devil may care' grin. Savanda Carlson, aka Lady Cat with an arm around John. And Kelly Bowe, CCW Women's title in her hands, her lips on Jason's cheek. The picture, an echo of a happier time, before everything had fallen apart. Written on the back in black marker, in the same handwriting she had used on their wedding invitations, were the words... ~ I still believe... ~ Slowly, McAllister's gaze rose from the picture. Rising until his eyes locked with the those of his mentor, coach, and friend. Gable: Give 'em hell son, give 'em hell. Gable put a hand on McAllister's shoulder briefly, nodding to reinforce his thoughts, before turning to return to his small, cluttered office from which he built a dynasty. Leaving behind him a former pupil, whose eyes now burned bright as ever with renewed determination. Domination. Believe it...
