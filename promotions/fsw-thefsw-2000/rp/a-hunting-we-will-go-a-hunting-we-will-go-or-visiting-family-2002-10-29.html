A Hunting WE will go, A hunting WE will go.... or, Visiting Family while taking time off.

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/29/2002 BANG A shot rumbles through the early morning lite. A domino effect takes place with the echo of the shot through the dense woods, and a flock of birds taking off from the tree tops, and flying through the air, almost blackening out the little sunlight we see so far. Suddenly, the forest goes quiet for a minute. “Got him.” A familiar voice booms out from a tree stand about 20 feet above the ground. Looking up, we see none other than the Big Bad Boda Daddy himself sitting in the tree stand. “Did you see that Jason?” “Yeah man, nice shot. I think he’s a 6 pointer” speaks out Jason. To bring everyone up to date who is unfamiliar with the family background of Chris Sloboda, Jason is Boda’s cousin, and lives in LeLand North Carolina, which is a small city, about 10 minutes west of Wilmington. LeLand is also the home of Micheal Jordan’s mother. “Man, it’s good to have you visit.” “Man, the past two weeks have been fun.” Boda tells Jason, as the both of them climb down from their tree stands. “I miss the thrill of the hunt. I mean, sure, beating the living shit out of my opponents in the ring is fun, but shooting a helpless deer, or the occasional bear is where the real adrenaline is.” “I know what you mean. I know.” Jason slings his rifle over his left shoulder, as does Boda. The two cousins walk about 100 yards to where the buck Boda just shot fell. “So, how are things going in FSW? I watched the PPV two weeks ago, and I tell you man..... I was worried after the match about you. You sure put alot into that match.” “Yeah, and I made Cassius pay for all the bullshit he’s done to me over the past 6 months.” Boda says with a smile. We see a few stitches still in his forehead from the Last Man Standing Match at the Bad Moon Rising PPV. Chris plops the rifle he is using over his right shoulder, as he bends down, and picks up the head of his ‘victim’. We see the rack of the six pointer, which is drawfed in comparison to Boda’s massive size. “Well, looks like we will eat well tonight.” Boda says with a laugh. “Indeed.” Jason helps Boda pick the deer up. “This thing must be about 250 pounds or so. He’s a biggin. So..... you get me tickets to the show this week?” “No......” Chris tells his cousin, a look of dispair appearing on Jason’s lips. “Man, I was only fuckin with you. Of course you can come. I mean, have I ever left you behind when we were in North Carolina?” Jason shakes his head no. The two continue to carry the buck out of the woods. Coming out of the dense forest, we see a house, with a plume of white smoke coming from the chimney. Standing outside, on the backporch, is a middle aged woman. As Boda and his cousing Jason get closer to the house, they set the deer on a table set up for skinning and cleaning. Jason stays as he decides to clean the catch of the day. Chris, leaving his gun, heads for the house. As he gets closer, we see the woman on the porch throw a smile at the former PCW World Champion. Chris steps his massive frame on the wooden porch, and gives the woman a huge hug. “Hi mom.” Chris returns the smile. “Did we wake you?” “No.....” Boda’s mom says. She grabs her son by the hand, and leads him to sit down at a picnic table on the porch. There is some pancakes, sausage and chocolate milk on the table. “Sit, eat. You need some energy. Especially after the way you battered you body last week. I had to stop watching. Why oh why couldn’t you stay in the NBA? But no.... you got your father’s attitude, and got yourself kicked out. You don’t see your brother, the doctor, losing his temper and getting kicked out of hospitals.” “MOM....” Boda shouts out. “How many times do we have to go over this? It’s ancient history. I LOVE wrestling. Always have. The best part.... I can’t get kicked out of wrestling for hurting someone. I mean, come on, if that could happen.... after the way I destroyed Cassius Deamont, I’d be fedless now. I can do what I want in FSW, just like I did in PCW because of one thing..... because I R BODA~!” “Do me a favor son.....” Bodamom says. “Shut up, and eat your breakfast. Save that I R BODA~! shit for the fans. It don’t jive with me.” Boda gives a strange look at his mom, then finally starts to pile the food into his mouth. “Yes mom.” The scene fades as Chris leans over, and kisses his mom on the forehead.
