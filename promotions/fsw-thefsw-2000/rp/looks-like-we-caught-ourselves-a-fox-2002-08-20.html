Looks Like We Caught Ourselves A Fox

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-20-02 The scene opens up on the inside of a huge hotel right outside of the Orlando area, we find ourselves wondering around the lobby area, as Asa Fountain makes his way in through the front door a clerk makes his way over to Asa very quickly. Clerk: Um, Mr. Fountain, I’m sorry to bother you, but we believe that there might be a problem with your friend. Asa: (Flipping down his shades and looking at the man) You mean Ramey? Clerk: Well, yes, sir. He kept calling us earlier asking for Moonshine. Asa: (Taking his sunglasses off and placing them on the rim of his shirt) You ain’t got nothing to worry about, cat daddy. Ramey always drinks his shine and is probably unaware that this place doesn’t house such beverages. Clerk: (Grabbing Asa’s arm as he tries to walk off) Well, it’s not only that Mr. Fountain, but he has been trying to do some things that we wouldn’t mind having less of. Asa: (With a look of wonderment) Like what? Clerk: Well, earlier today we found him passed out in the middle of the hallway and we had to take him back to his room, but his stench was almost unbearable for me and my coworkers to handle. Asa: So, you’re trying to say that my hillbilly friend needs to take himself a shower? Clerk: Well, among other things, yes. Asa: And what would these other things be, jive turkey? Clerk: Well, last night we were served with a complaint from a woman about a very hairy man that didn’t speak to well, coming out of the shadows and basically trying to rape her. Asa: (Laughing) Oh, Ramey wasn’t trying to rape that woman, he was probably trying to hit on her or throw up on her. Ramey is usually in a constant state of a hangover, so I don’t think he meant anything by it. Clerk: Well, the last time we checked he was asleep up stairs in his room. Would you mind trying to get him to at least take a shower tonight and maybe take him out of the hotel with you the next time you leave the primacies? Asa: Sure thing, I would have asked him to go with me today, but I couldn’t find the guy anywhere. Clerk: Well, that is because he was passed out by the pool all night last night. Asa: So, you’re basically trying to tell me that the guy stayed in the water most of the day yesterday, yet he still don’t smell any better? Clerk: Well, he never really got in the pool from what we were told. Asa: Well, what were you told, groove daddy? Clerk: We were told that Mr. Ramey had been roaming around the pool looking for his pet, Elmer. Asa: Jesse must be really out of it because Elmer is back home in West Virginia. Clerk: Maybe your friend is just a little homesick then. Asa: Perhaps. Clerk: Either that or he really needs to cut down on that drink of his. Asa: (Sighing) I’ve been trying to get him to stop that stuff, he knows it’s not good for him or at least I believe he has some kind of idea about it. Clerk: Oh, and another thing, we were told that he was carrying around a gun with him and we really don’t approve of this. Asa: Don’t worry he’s harmless. That gun was only given to him at an NRA meeting that I thought was an AA meeting. It’s not loaded and Jesse thinks he’s supposed to hit jive turkeys over the head with it in self-defense. Clerk: Well, doesn’t it bother you knowing that he could use it on anyone? Seeing as how he is in a constant state of drunkenness, he could assume that anyone is trying to cause him bodily harm and could get himself into some really bad trouble if he was to hit someone with it. Asa: Well, I guess you do have a point there, cat daddy. I’ll have a little talk with Jesse when I get back up to his room and I’ll take care of everything you’ve talked about. Clerk: Thank you, Mr. Fountain. Asa begins making his way off and into the only open elevator, whose doors almost shut up on him. There is one single female in the elevator with him, one that is reasonable attractive. Long red hair, somewhat pale looking skin, perfect figure, and wearing a lovely looking red dress. Woman: What floor are you going to? With just the sound of her heavenly voice, Asa nearly melts in his spot. Asa: You’ve already got my floor there. The two continue on their way up to the seventh floor, as Asa begins to almost sweat wanting to say something, but he doesn’t. He holds it in and we can see the obvious amount of pressure building up in his face as the elevator comes to a hault and the two begin to make their way down the hallway, Asa wondering where she could be going to, because on this level are mainly Five Star Wrestling workers. Finally coming to a complete stop in front of the same door. Asa: I’m sorry, miss, but do you have the wrong room? Woman: No, I’m afraid I don’t. I’ve come to see a gentleman by the name of Ramey. Asa: (Eyes bugging out of his head) You’ve got to be kidding me right? Woman: No, Jesse and I meet last night. It was kind of a sudden thing and he scared me at first, but I thought his drunken rambling on was a bit cute after a while. I told him that I’d come and check on him today. By the way, my name is Sharon Wallace. (She extends her hand to Asa) Asa: (Reaching over and shaking her hand) Pleased to meet you. I’m Jesse’s friend and I was on my way to check on him, but seeing as how you’re already on your way in, I believe you could handle it. Sharon: Well, it was very nice meeting you Asa and I’ll tell Jesse you were somewhat worried about him, but yes, I believe I can handle things. Sharon enters the room and closes the door behind her. Asa begins to make his way back down the hallway toward his room as he turns and looks back at Jesse’s door in disbelief. Asa: How in the hell did Ramey manage to catch himself a fox like that? Asa turns and continues making his way down the hallway, back to his room as the scene fades to black.
