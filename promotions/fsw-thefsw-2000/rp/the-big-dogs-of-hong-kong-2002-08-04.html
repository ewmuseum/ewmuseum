The Big Dogs Of Hong Kong

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-4-02 *The scene opens up in Hong Kong, China. Pablo and Fran-Man just got off the plane and are heading out the airports doors to catch a cab....* PD: say man you got any money? FF: Uhhh...I dont got any money for a cab....just weed. PD: Dont they like cut your hand off here for drugs? FF: I dont know, But I think that is Japan. PD: Well then lets just walk. FF: Sounds good to me *Pablo Diablo and Francisco Fumar start walking when a Chinese guy approaches them* Chinese guy: Aww Amerikonss FF: What the hell did he just say? PD: I think he said Americans. FF: No, Im not Amerikon....im...MEXIKON... Chinese Guy: Aww Mexikon!!! PD: Im Amerikon Chinese Guy: Aww Amerikon!!!......Amerikon...Mexikon... PD: You ok man? Chinese Guy: AMERIKON!!!!MEXIKON!!!!!!! FF: Are you high? Chinese Guy: AMERIKON !!!!! MEXIKON!!!!!!!! PD: I think he has been smoking on that ewwwweeeee! Chinese Guy: EWEEEEEEEEEE AMERIKON MEXIKON!!!!! FF: Maybe he has some more.....lets jump him. *At that time PD and FF jump the chinese guy and search his pockets.....but he has nothing....alot of other Chinese people are looking on at the incident....but ride off on their bikes* PD: I thought he said he had some EWEEEEEEE!!! FF: I guess not but he smells like fish. PD: Probably works at a fish market......come on lets go. *Pablo and Fran-Man continue walking.* PD: Hey check out that get up.... FF: Yeah that guy prolly has some herb. PD: Say man whatchu workin wit? *The man dressed loud looks over....* Man: Who me? PD: Yeah. Man: Well allow me to introduce myself.....The name is Ron..... PD: Alright, Hey Ron....You got any herb? Ron: I dont happen to have any herb man but I do got some the finest ass you ever seen. FF: Say homes, we dont want to see your ass. Ron: Im talkin about the hoes man....the hoes! *Ron runs over and grabs one of his girls off the street corner* Ron: Look here....Shes mysterious, shes sexy.....she's PHANTOMHOOKER PD: Phantom Hooker eh? FF: Hmmm......nah Pablo.....we got money for herb ....not hoes... PD: Yeah.....she kinda looks like a crack whore.... PhantomHooker: YOU got some CRACK!!! PD: No.... PhantomHooker: DAMN! FF: Yall are starting to freak me out.....Bye Ron....Bye PhantomHooker....c'mon Pablo.....lets go..... PD: yeah....I want some herb.....hey....isnt that Jesse Ramey over there? FF: YEAH IT IS....didnt he beat our ass that one week? PD: Yeah, but maybe he has some herb.....lets find out *Pablo and Fran-Man walk over to where Ramey is at* PD: Say man you got some herb? Jesse Ramey: No but you see that guy on the corner? FF: Yeah? Jesse Ramey: His name is Ron, and thats one of his ladies PhatomHooker....she's some good ass I tell ya. PD: Nah man she is a crack whore... Jesse Ramey: Really? FF: Yeah..... Jesse Ramey: HEY YOU CRACK WHORE!!!! *yelling* Jesse Ramey: SELLING ME SOME MESSED UP SHIT!!!! *At that time Ron took notice......and whistled.....out of nowhere a bunch of Big dogs ran after PD and FF and Ramey* PD: OH SHIT!!!!! BIG DOGS!!!!!! FF: RON HAS BIG DOGS!!!!!!! RAMEY: RUN!!!!!!!!!!! *The three run off in the streets of Hong Kong* To Be Continued
