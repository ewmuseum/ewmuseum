What's the Point?

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Twenty Hundred and Two 'It's a game mommy, it's a game!' A small boy, about the age of 9 or 10, declares to his mother who is watching him fail in his attempt to run down an escalator which is designed for customers of the large shopping mall to travel upwards. 'Careful Rubin!' The mother joyfully but sternly whilst wearing a huge smile on her face – she is glad to see her son having fun, but is aware of the danger... she doesn't want her little boy to injure himself now. 'Siderman, the name's Spiderman!' The success of the new movie has obviously made the Marvel Comic character popular with young children once again, as this is the reply the young by gives his mother. 'Very well, Spiderman.' The mother corrects herself for her son in an apologetic manner before rolling up the sleeve of her white blouse and looking at her watch. 'C'mon, Rub... I mean Spiderman, we have to go now.' She adds in an authoritative tone, knowing that her son would simply ignore her if she used her usual soft-natured voice. 'Come and get me!' He chose to ignore her anyway as he excitedly replied with a demand of his own. The mother, an unattractive yet slim woman in her mid-thirties, decides against this. She folds her arms to signify her objection in playing such childish games and retorts 'Come on Rubin, this is silly... Don't make me come and get you.' The Lady, who by now is visibly annoyed with her son, takes to the escalator. As it carries her to the next floor, her son (or Spiderman) let's out a scream of excitement and decides to jump into her arms causing them both to fall backwards down the escalator. Luckily, the women fell into the arms of a group of business men dressed in black suits enabling her to regain her balance and give her son the punishment he deserves. Observing all of the commotion from a small park-like bench, within the mall, is a well turned-out Leon Chambers who is eating the first half of his tuna and cucumber sandwich. However, the man he is sharing the bench with is an odorous homeless man who appears not to have eaten in about a week. The mans' clothing is extremely shabby – a long swamp green wax coat covers most of his aging body. His long white hair and beard needs to be cut and is probably flee infested... and God knows what else. On the other hand, Leons' attire consists of a smart, possibly designer, charcoal black pinstriped suit with a milk white shirt, fastened at the collar is a black tie with a silver dragon design embroided onto it. Although Leon is not too pleased with the state his present company is in, his posture does not show his disapproval as he has his arm on the back of the bench, near the man – almost as if he is willing to converse with him... as, in fact, he does. 'Two examples of the stupidity of the youth right there.' Leon expresses his views in an attempt to catch his attention. The man replies with a single grunt. Ughh, is this guy for real? Leons' sanity asks before responding to the man with a gentle nudge on the shoulder... then a more firmer one as the man makes no acknowledgment of Leon... still nothing. Leon now stands from his seat, brushes the crumbs off from his sandwich and picks up the old mans' stick, which he apparently uses as a walking aid. At first, Leon attempts to poke the mans upper arm but still, the man does not reply. The unclean red cowboy hat on his head is tilted over his eyes so Leon does not rule out the possibility that the man is sleeping... so in one sudden sharp, powerful stroke – Leon slams the staff down on the poor guys' belly - to which he responds with an unbelievable roar of distress and agony as he leaps up from his seat. The man appears to be heavily intoxicated as he warily looks around him, not even noticing Leon. After a few seconds, he seemingly forgets that he had just been 'attacked' and, once again, falls back into a heap on the bench. A relieved Leon wipes the sweat from his brow as he realized that the man is almost twice his size. He then sits on the bench once more – this time however, he is a little further from the man. 'Hey maaaan, do you got a cigarette I can have?' After a few moments silence, the homeless man says aloud, this time, he is hoping to catch Leons' attentions. 'Huh?' Leon asks himself as he considers the question. 'Do you honestly think that someone with my perfect complexion can afford to smoke? Of course not, simpleton.' Leon now sits on the edge of his seat as he points to his face. 'Look at me, I am fucking beautiful... I don't do any of that crap... who do you think I am?' The old man shrugs, not knowing what to make of Leons' unnecessary, over-the-top reply to his simple question as the generic reply is usually one of the following: 'yes', 'no' or 'fuck you!'. 'Don't you just hate kids?' Leon throws out another question as he believes the man will reply this time around. 'Yoergrmphnut.' The man inelegantly rubs the palm of his hand over his nose and face as he makes nose sense at all. 'I mean, take what just happened for an example.' Leon undeterred, makes it his goal to talk to the 'idiot' so he decides to continue anyway. 'You know, the kid on the escalator! First of all, he likes the 'good guys'. Spiderman appears to be the flavor of the month. Most kids love the 'heroes' and I can't really blame them, I mean, Hollywood make them out to be immortal but in real life that is not the case. You have to be a ruthless bastard to survive in this world... the good guy never wins! It's a sad fact, but nonetheless it is 100% true. Just like me, you see, I don't hide behind flashy gimmicks nor do I perform heroic feats... With me, what you see is what you get. I am The Ideal, a real person... all I care for is number one.' 'Grmph... If you're not going to eat that, can I finish it... I am starving dude.' The man is referring to the rest of Leons' sandwich at which he has seemed to sober up at the sight of. He picks it up with his grubby hands before Leon gives him permission so all Leon can do is nod his approval as the guy bites into it. With his mouthful he asks. 'What were you saying again? Sorry, I wasn't listening dude.' 'Ah, forget about it. I don't suppose you have a television do you...' Leon pauses as he realizes that he hasn't been courteous enough to ask his name. 'What's your name punk?' 'That's a darn good question...' That is all the man says. 'And the answer is...' Leon motions with his hands for the old man to finish the sentence although he seems to occupied with the sandwich. 'Ah, forget it... Later Loser!' Leon, too annoyed with the inadequate company... he decides to up and leave. pointless
