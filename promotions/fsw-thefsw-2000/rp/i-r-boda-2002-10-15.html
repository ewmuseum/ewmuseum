I R BODA~!!

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/15/2002 Like I’ve said before, I am here for a reason, I am here because I chose to be here. Noone told me ‘Hey Chris, go to FSW, and do what you do best’. NO!! I was asked to come to FSW, I was offered a position in FSW, and dammit, I chose to take it, because.................. I R BODA~!! The scene opens at a local gym. We see your typical bodybuilders, pumping iron, flexing on the young pretty thangs. We watch as the female lifters push the barbells up, and down, sweat pooling around and dripping down through the cleavage. “Come on, one more, push it Chip, One more.” We cut from the big breasted blonde woman with muscles bigger than the average man, to a corner of the gym. There, we see Boda, and if our eyes don’t decieve us, Chip O’Meara, laying on a weight bench. “Chip, you’re the man, just one more.” “Two” Chip exudes as he pushes the barbell up, and back onto it’s rest spots. “This sucks. I’m not a wrestler. And here you are, trying to kill me. Why? All we need to do is have you in the ring on Glory, you can tag me in when you have Orion beat down. Why do you have me pumping this iron?” “Why? Boda stands, and places about 300 pounds of free weights on the barbell. This makes the total 350 pounds. Boda lays on the bench, and jerks the barbell up, and down, about 5 real quick times. “Because I R BODA~!” Chris presses the 350 pounds another 5 times, before stopping. “Yes, you R BODA~!” Chip smirks. “But that doesn’t mean I have to kill myself working out. That is why I hired you.” “Well, I see your point” Boda points to another piece of excersise equipment. “But, what is going to happen, say, if me and Cassius aren’t brawling in the ring, and it’s up to you, to go one on one, with the Gunslinger?” “It won’t happen” Chip remarks skiddishly. “It can’t happen. But.........” “But nothing.” Boda barks. “I R BODA~! And I didn’t become BODA~! by turning down workouts, or challenges. And after this weeks Glory, you’ll be saying, with pride........ I R CHIP!! Say it with me.” Chip takes a seat, and Boda hands him a 25 pound dumbell. Chip’s arm drops, but as he starts to mutter ‘I R CHIP’ he feels the strength the bring his arm up, and curl the 25 pound dumbell. Chip smiles, enthusiasticly, and shouts ‘I R CHIP!!” “That’s my boy.” Boda smiles as the scene fades out.
