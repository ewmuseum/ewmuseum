Commercial: The Joy of Pepsi

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/02/02 COMMERCIAL: The Joy of Pepsi *The scene is of your standard red-carpet event, as evidenced by the long, red satin carpet stretching from the curb and off camera toward a building. The carpet is bordered on each side by velvet ropes which, even aided by burly security guards, barely manage to hold back the surging throng of people. These crowds reach out to try and get a handful of the glamorous personages strolling down the carpet. Some of the celebrities smile and wave cordially, but most simply march along, heedless of their admirers. * *The camera pans right, following Brad Pitt and Jennifer Aniston down the carpet, until they reach the door-way. Standing in front of the door is the striking figure of a tall, young Native American man wearing faded black jeans, a pair of snakeskin boots and a matching vest that plays about his lithe, flawless physique. Around his neck is a beadwork choker, and his face is handsome, with the bearing of an eagle. He?s got a cascade of black hair hat falls cleanly behind his shoulders. A relaxed smile on his lips, the man greets the glitzy couple, and anyone else who passes through the door.* MAN: Jennifer. Brad. Go on in... Jack Nicholson! Where?re those Laker tix you promised me? Jennifer Hewitt. Be my guest. Bob Dole! How?s it hangin?? DOLE: Long and loose, Johnny. The chicks can?t stay away! *The former Senator gives Johnny a high-five and goes inside* JOHNNY: Carson Daly... okay, okay, you can go in, but you owe me. Vin Diesel... Missy Elliot, Samuel Jackson, go on in. Wait a minute! Hold it right there! *No sooner does Sam Jackson head inside than Johnny steps to block the path of a young couple on their way inside. The young woman, a curvy bottle-blonde, is none other than Britney Spears. In her left hand, she holds a can of Pepsi. In her right, she holds the hand of boyfriend Justin Tiberlake, who is so caught by surprise at Johnny?s sudden motion that he walks straight into the big Indian?s chest.* JOHNNY: Hold up, son. This party?s 21 and over only. JUSTIN *laughing it off*: Yeah, real funny, man. I got ID. *He hands Johnny his ID card.* JOHNNY *breaking the ID card in half between his two fingers* Not anymore. JUSTIN: Come on, man! I?ll buy you a Coke! JOHNNY: A Coke? You?ll buy me a Coke? Son, I?m Johnny Raindance, and I can?t be bought so easily. BRITNEY: How about a Pepsi? JOHNNY: Now you?re talking. *He takes the offered Pepsi* You can go in... Junior here goes home to Mommy. JUSTIN: But... but... that makes no sense. JOHNNY: Sure it makes sense. Show him, honey! *Out of nowhere, the lights dim, lasers flash about, and Britney, aided by two teenie-boppers from the crowd, two overweight security guards and Bob Dole, goes into her whole song-and-dance routine, with the ?pum-pa-pum-pum-pums? and the shaking of the groove thang.* *After the routine winds down, Justin?s face lights up and he shoves Britney in front of Johnny.* JUSTIN: C?mon, dude! I?ll give you my girlfriend! JOHNNY: Hey, right on! *He?s cut off as Bob Dole rushes in, takes Britney by the arm and leads her inside* DOLE: Come on, baby, let?s bust a move! JUSTIN: But... that?s not fair! JOHNNY: No... *clapping a hand on Timberlake?s shoulder, he hands the pop star a can of Pepsi* That?s the Joy of Pepsi. *As the Indian and the Boy-bander toss back a can, we fade to a black background with the Pepsi logo over the words ?Taste the Joy of Pepsi.? (Fade out.)
