How it all came about (An Evil narration

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 070-06-02 How it all came about... When you go to a place and establish yourself as a great, you begin to call it your home, your grounds, your territory so to speak. While you're there you do absolutely everything in your power to make great and to make a stay on top even it means going over or through someone. But when it deceases and closes its door then it pisses you off and in your eyes you've failed yourself, your family and your business. When you're at place a businness and you are considered as a top person it's your job to keep it up and running simply because all eyes are on you and you don't have to worry about going somewhere and starting all over again at the bottom; sort of like your retirement plan. Well that's the situation that came my way about seven, eight, nine months ago -however the hell long it was- . I was chillen at my crib in Texas when I got a phone call from one of the FSW reps. I can't exactly remember who it was but I knew it wasn't great news. He didn't stall or beat around the bush he just came out and said 'Sorry we tried, but FSW cannot and willnot continue.' Yea, that plain. I didnt reply I just hung up the phone and told Eve that we had to find another place to go. I wasn't in a rush because financially I'm set, I had nothing to worry about there, but I wasn't ready to never enter another ring again. I searched and I searched for a long time with caution, patience, and pickyness. I talked to a few people but couldn't seem to find a place to call home. I long time went by until I finally found a place. Thanks to my previous hard work I was taken in with open arms. You see, hard work does pay off...even for a evil man like me. When I got there opened the doors and started to explore my little rare smile soon erased from my face. Reality hit me, I'm use to people running up to me discussing this and that to me, but here of the wrestlers and staff new me hell probably didn't even care about me; the feeling was mutual, but when the reality that I had to start all over hit me between the eyes I was bitter. I understood though people, had already established themselves at this place I had to fight as if I was a rookie all over again and beleive I was ready, willing, and able to do it. A little challenge never hurt any one. I went in and done my duty night in and night out no matter how boring and old it seemed. A few months went by and I was finally beginning to get the bitter taste out of my mouth when my past came and bit me on my ass...sort of. You know when you become famous people will use your name to get the green and to reach the same fame as you. When someone becomes famous do you know where the nosey ass media people go to get stories out of you? They go back home, and every once in a while they find someone who uses your name to get fame sometimes it works sometimes it doesn't. And what's so bad about it is that when shit get's put out there you may never find out who it is..luckily I did I guess you can say luckily. I was in match were I was looking to go on to bigger and better things afterwards because I was bored and I was damned if I stayed in that position, when a man that claimed to have rivaled with me in the FSW and sucka hit on the side of my right eye with a chair cracked my eye socket bone, you would think a man with a chair would be man enough to hit me in the face but to he did his damage. The pain that went through my body was a pain that I have never felt before I thought someone had shot me in my eye. When I came to I asked who in the hell hit me they told it was a man who was my rival in FSW. Now EOE knows his rivals because you have to damn good for me to consider you a rival and he was no rival. He was just like I stated before a man who was looking for fame and he used my name to get in. I was determined to get back in the ring the following event but the doctor who always spoils the party to me just how serious this injury was and as stuborn as I was, I agreed not to go back into action. I did it for a reason though, any other time I would have told the doctor to suck my..well you know. It was my time to finally sit and think did I really want to continue because I knew when I came back I would have to start all over again and at that time I just wasn't feelin it. I needed a motive, as sat there and waited but no motive came I then realized the only motive I had was to go back and get revenge. So I decided to do and I'll be damn if the individual that used my name wasn't even there anymore, now ain't that about a bitch. So I sat at home with my girl and watched the NBA playoffs, I was high and the only thing that was going through my mind is 'fuck it, I don't need to wrestle anymore anyway'. I had made my mind up I was done with kicking peoples ass...in the ring. I was just about to break the news to Eve, when my cell phone started ringing. I didn't feel like talking so I told Eve to answer it. When she answer I saw the startled look on her face and she just handed me the phone. 'Hello' I said. 'Hello Mr. Ellis or shall I say EOE, this is Lynn Lane' 'Lynn?' 'You long time no hear from and I bet you're surprised to hear from me right?' he asked 'You know I am, what are you doing calling me?' 'Well, it's like this I know that you havent been particularly happy about having to go to another place and establish yourself all over again.' he explained 'I know that you are not under any contract restrictions and I thought I would call you and ask if you would like to come back home'. 'Where are you getting at?' I asked. 'Oh I you didn't hear? FSW is reopening under new management.' Lynn said. 'Oh it is?' I asked trying not to sound happy as hell but I was frontin I was happy for the first time in a long time. 'Look EOE, you know that it won't be the same without so will...' 'Yea, I'm back in'. I answered without him even getting the words out. 'GOOD! Mr. EVIL is back in! I LOVE YOU!' he got little bit too excited. 'You you wha?' I dont even play that shit. 'Oh haha, sorry I just got happy, look you alreadt know the routine I book a flight for you next week and we'll take it from there.' said Lynn. 'Aight then, peace.' I put the phone down inhaled from my blunt and let out alot stress. Eve could see look of happiness on my face. 'What are you so happy about and what did Lynn want?' asked Eve. 'He told me that FSW is reopening and you know I couldn't pass that up. 'So you mean we're going back to FSW? YES! I the first lady there and plus those other girls at that other place had major issues.' she said. 'Well so much for having to climb that mountain again, you and me both are set in stone there.' 'Yea you're right, we're going back home.' And that's how it all came about, I was back in FSW not even questioning whether it was the right move or not. I mean who wouldn't go back to a place were they are already at top. I secure in FSW and will do anything to stay that way. New faces, doesn't matter, old faces, they already know. I am the man there and I'll be damn if anyone is gonna to take mine.
