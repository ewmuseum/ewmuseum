Lurking Death Part 3

23 August 2002

Date: 8/23/02 ~The scene again reopens with a view of the Davidson Estate in Connecticut. The leafless trees surrounding the house are seen blowing violently in the wind as a major thunderstorm seems to be coming through the area. Bolts of lightning and the loud blasts of thunder come more and more frequently as you are taken back into the familiar Davidson Estate Library. The old man is already seated at the desk and at first glance he seems to be sound asleep at the desk. His head is hanging forward looking over the desk and there is little movement coming from him aside from the rise and falling of his chest as he breathes. Suddenly a loud blast of thunder from outside wakens him and he lifts his head to look around the room. He seems a bit puzzled as to where he is but his senses come back to him as he finally realizes where he is seated. He picks up the pack of cigarettes at his side on the desk and pulls one out and lights it up as he puts it to his mouth. He reaches down under the desk into one of the drawers and pulls out the book as he places it back on top of the desk in front of him. He places the cigarette back down on the ashtray and rubs his eyes trying to rid himself of the last bits of sleep. He opens the book back to his previous entry and reads it all over quickly to get his bearings before he reaches out for the cigarette again and takes in another puff of smoke. He blows the smoke slowly out and grabs hold of the pen. He looks down at the previous entry again and flips over to the next page and begins the writing picking up right where he had left off the night before.~ ~The Davidson Chronicles Chapter Ten: Lurking Death Part 2 8/23/2052 by Tyler Davidson~ Genesis has come and gone as so many shows before it has. Leaving me sitting vulnerable to despair and depression as things seem to happen with me often. I lost perhaps the biggest match in my FSW career to a complete joker like Chris Bagwell. The fact that he got a lucky pin in both the first and third falls of our match did little to comfort me as I know I should have beat him. I had my small measure of vengeance later that night at least. The mind games had continued despite my defeat and it was Chris Bagwell who finished the night on his back following a well placed chair to the head by the person he thought was the “love of his life” Rebecca. In reality it had been my manager Daniel S. who I coerced into dressing in drag in order to fool Bagwell. The plan worked better than I could have ever expected when Bagwell fell right into the trap. What was his reaction I wondered when he regained consciousness and realized the truth? The truth that I had never had Rebecca at Genesis. He certainly must have felt the fool later that night and probably drowned his sorrows away in a bar somewhere picking a fight with a complete and total stranger. That was the way of Bagwell’s life it seems. No one ever knows it seems what the truth can hold. Bagwell was not as far from the truth as most would have thought and yet he was still far away from his own goals. He wanted Rebecca back in his life and he thought going into Genesis that I would be the key to that happening. Perhaps it would be in my power to one day, make sure it did indeed become a reality for Chris. It was something that I would have to think carefully about in the future. Genesis was still a tough time for me personally as well. After it was all over my thoughts and memories persisted to eat away at my insides. I knew something was wrong but constantly my mind had taken me back to a time that I would love to forget. It would not cooperate with me on this night or any other it seemed. I suffered from sleepless night after sleepless night for days after Genesis which brought us to the day before Glory. I was determined to make sure it was not something which would affect my performance in the ring this week and did my hardest training regimen to date as I pushed and stressed myself in the ring trying to wear myself down enough for sleep to creep in. It had worked better than I could have imagined but by the end of the night I had wished it hadn’t. I saw it all again being replayed inside my mind and all of my dreams played it out. The obvious tragic times of my childhood and adult life began as if in a constant loop mode. The visions flooded my mine. The rape, the murders, the lives being cut tragically short. It seemed in the span of ten minutes I had relived my entire life tragedies over again as though they had just happened. I knew what I had to do with them as it happened so many other times in my life but could I really do it? Or were these visions something I must sooner or late come to grips with? No the visions that came to me that night I had thought were better left a distant and locked away memory. That’s what I had told myself over and over again as I leaned over the bathroom sink with water dripping down my face and staring at myself in the mirror. My blood shot eyes peering into my very own soul as I looked forward into the deep recesses of my mind. At least that was the way things had seemed to me though as I stood there trying to shake the images from my mind. What I was really doing of course was trying to convince myself that what I saw and the thoughts in my head were nothing more than a series of hallucinations brought on by the lack of sleep I had been suffering from and the over exertion my body has gone through in the past seventy two hours. So what if I knew I was lying to myself. So long as it helped me get to sleep that night I was all to willing to do it. The sounds of my mother’s voice as she try to sooth me back to bed after a bad nightmare went through my mind as though she was there in the room with me. She wasn’t of course as she had died several years earlier. There was no one there. No one but me and my reflection in the mirror and neither of the two would be of much comfort this night. Neither of the two would be able to sooth the nightmares away with the magic words of calm and comfort that had seemed to work so well all those years ago. I ran some more water from the faucet over my face again and wiped my face off in the towels. I was tempted to remain seated in the living room all night and watch nothing more than nick-at-nite reruns but the idea of it was enough to put me back to sleep again. Eventually I had no choice but to give into the demands of my body and return to the bed. It had taken most of the morning hours to pass before I was really able to fall back into a deep and comfortable sleep again. Once again the thoughts and images flooded my mind which I still am not certain I am in full possession of anymore. ~Another loud blast of thunder can be heard followed quickly by a flash of lightning. The old man looks out the window to his right casting a weary gaze on the window being pelted with rain drops. He takes his pen and places it gently back into the ink jar before reading over the pages he had just written. He takes another cigarette out of the pack and lights it up while looking over the page before closing the book as the scene fades slowly to black.~
