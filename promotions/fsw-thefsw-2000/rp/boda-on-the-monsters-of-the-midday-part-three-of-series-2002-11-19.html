Boda on The Monsters Of The Midday...... part three of series

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/19/2002 *Continued from Rp entitled..... Boda Goes To REAL Radio part two. This is the actual part where I, Boda, your favourite FSW heel, plays a guest on his favourite talk show. This one is of course entitled..... Boda on The Monsters Of The Midday.* The elevator doors open on the fourth floor. Out into the hallway steps Boda, and he is instantly greeted by a tall, skinny dorkish man. “Tuttle.” Boda smiles as he shakes the intern’s hand. “What’s up man? Getting any pussy lately?” “Nah.... I can’t be like you Boda.” Tuttle remarks. Tuttle is a geek. Not a ladies man like our anti-hero Boda. His sole purpose on the show is to take all the brunts of jokes. He does all the gopher work, as his pay is ZILCH. To compare Tuttle to a wrestler is to say Tuttle is an indy wrestler, trying to break into the business. None the less, Tuttle escorts Boda into the studio, where sitting from left to right, around the table is Russ Rollins, Savanna, Bubba ‘Whoop Ass’ Wilson, Dirty Jim, Jeff Howell. Tuttle leaves as BlackBean, one of the producers gives Boda a microphone, and a seat. All the Monsters wave to Chris, who in turn waves back. “So, if you’re looking for a great car, at a great price..... Come to Thurston’s Used Cars, located on 17-92, right next to the Little 500 Go-Kart Track.” The commercial ends, and we are suddenly pleasured with the soothing sound of ‘HERE TO STAY’ by KoRn, as the Monsters of The Midday comes back from the break. “Welcome back to The Monsters Of The Midday, on The Real Radio Network, I am Russ Rollins, the leader of this group merry men and women. And, it is, as always, my pleasure to introduce to you at home..... not only a good friend of the show, but a local sport star.” Russ Rollins, the robust host of the show introduces Boda to the fans. Russ is a lover, pimp, booty daddy, hence why him and Chris get along so well. [detail withheld] Russ loves a good sandwhich, even going as far as being a sponsor and local spokesman for Sobiks Subs. “So, please welcome to the show........... Chris ‘Boda’ Sloboda.” Canned applause is heard over the air waves, as the members of the show actually applaud. “Thanks Russ, and as always, it’s a pleasure to give you a ratings boost.” Boda states, in Boda-esque fashion. “Hey Savanna, when you gonna get you some of this Boda-sausage?” “Oh come on, I know you’re married Chris.” Savanna pipes in. [detail withheld] “But, if you ever get single..... I might take you up on that offer.” “Well, guess I need to call my lawyers.” Boda laughs, as does Dirty Jim. “So, B...... “ Dirty Jim is Russ’s brother in law. Dirty Jim THINKS he is a player, but he couldn’t win at a game of solataire. All in all, Dirty Jim is good people, who is the subject of the most flubs on air. His most annoying attribute is he constantly calls stars and athletes nicknames, usually just their first initial. “How you been? Life on the road treating you well? Glad to be home? Can I get one of your lady friends?” [detail withheld] “Hey Jim. It’s been a long time on the road. But since joining FSW, it’s been a great time. I’ve whooped the ass of many that think they can take it to the Big Bad Boda Daddy. But you know what.... They found out they can’t hold a match to my flame. Being home... itis always good. Seeing my family, makes being on the road a little easier. And no, I keep all the ladies to myself. There’s plenty of Boda-Sausage for them all.” Boda responds to D.J’s questions. The last part garnering a laugh from the rest of the group. “So, tell us about the show tomorrow night at The TD Waterhouse. I hear you are in the main event.” Russ keeps the show rolling smoothly. “Well, tomorrow night, Orlando is going to witness a wrestling venue unlike anything ever to blow through this town. FSW is a different type of organisation, and tomorrow night, will be no different. And of course I’m in the main event. I mean, after all..... I R BODA~!! and this is Orlando. I have a feeling I’ll be all over the show tomorrow night.” Boda answers Russ’s questions. “Oh, a little bit of backstage info for the fans? So, tell us about this main event. Is it important?” Dirty Jim, the ever knowledgeable asks. “No Jimmy, not backstage info. More like Boda info. I plan on making my visit to my home town, memoriable for EVERYONE at the Waterhouse, and all the fans watching. As for the main event, of course it’s important. I mean, you got 4 undeserving wrestler and the main bad guy, the man that deserves to be World Champion.... ME, in a ladder match. The winner gets a shot at the World Champion come the next PPV in December. I mean, come on, I’m a shoe in to take this match tomorrow night, hell, I probably won’t even break a sweat. Six Fingered Freak..... he’s not even worthy to tie my shoes, Jason MacAllister, some overratted ex-amateur champion, Azala Zameer, who fell of the boat from Africa and of course, Mike Marvelous, who is a shell of his former self. I’ve got this match locked, and loaded.” Boda egotistically tells the show members. “Hello Boda.... I’m Bubba Whoop Ass Wilson, and I’ve been known to whoop a man’s ass.” Bubba chimes in. Now Boda has met Bubba many times, but the poor redneck messiah's memory ain't all that great[detail withheld]. Bubba is though, a MUSICAL DAMN GENIUS, and once was in a 50’s cover band named Clutch. He always has to mention that. “But, I must say.... you might give the old Whoop Ass a run for his money. What you gonna do once you win that title shot?” “Well Bubba, first off..... It’d be a cold day in hell before you beat the likes of me. Secondly, I’m going to walk into the PPV in Hawaii in December, and smack down, beat down, destroy, maim whoever the hell f[CENSORED] the champion is.” Boda lets fly with the f-bomb, a radio no-no. “HEY, watch the f word, the FCA will be all over us. Well.... it does look like it is time for a commercial break. Boda.... can you stay for another segment?” Russ asks Chris. “Sure thing. I mean, it’s not like I need to warm up and work out for my match tomorrow night.” Boda laughs. The show cuts to a local commercial. And this promo is over.
