Cast Aways #4

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday, July 30th, 2002 [Scene opens in the deep jungle of the deserted island. Mike and Jacob are nowhere to be found. The camera pans around the jungle. There are many trees...vines...shrubs...exotic plants. Monkies and birds can be heard calling in the trees. The camera spots a small meer cat and his plump wart hog friend as they walk around the jungle, collecting bugs for their lunch] Timon: Hey Pumbaa, lift up that rock. Pumbaa: Ok, Timon. [Pumbaa shoves his hoof under a large rock and lifts it. There are many bugs underneath...big, fat grub worms...colorful beatles...ants. Timon picks up a grub worm and holds it up] Timon: Lunch is served! [He drops the worm into his mouth and swallows] Timon: Mmmm...that was a juicy one! [Pumbaa picks up a beetle and pops it in his mouth] Pumbaa: Crunchy! Oh wait! Timon! We forgot to say grace! Timon: You're right...*ahem* Rub a dub dub, thanks for the grub. Pumbaa: Amen. [The two animal friends continue their meal while relaxing] Pumbaa: You know, Timon...this is the life. Timon: I know, my big dumb wart hog friend. It can't get any better than this. Pumbaa: I hope it never ends. Timon: Of course it won't. Remember our old saying...Hakuna Matata! No worries! Pumbaa: Hakuna Matat-- [Before the wart hog can finish, a spear plunges through his side with a sickening sound. The camera pans up to where Mike Marvelous holds a spear and is grinning because of his kill.] Timon: P-P-P-PUMBAA! [Timon runs away as a spear JUST misses his head. A spear wielding Jacob Jackson tries to run after him, but he gets away] Jacob Jackson: Darn it! I missed! Mike Marvelous: That's alright, Jacob. We're havin' pork for supper! Jacob Jackson: Thank goodness...I'm so hungry too! [Mike wraps a rope around the hooves of the dead hump that used to be one of Disney's proudest creations and begins to drag it through the jungle as Jacob follows] Mike Marvelous: Boy, is this thing heavy. Jacob Jackson: We're almost at the beach. [Suddenly, some sort of wildcat jumps out of the bushes and grabs the corpse of Pumbaa. Mike lets go of the rope in shock and the cat carries the hog into the bushes] Mike Marvelous: SON OF A-- Jacob Jackson: Michael, I know, this sucks, but it's no reason to resort to profanity. Mike Marvelous: Right. [Mike and Jacob walk out of the jungle empty handed, with their heads hanging low. They walk sadly back to the cave and have a seat inside] Jacob Jackson: Well...so much for eating. Mike Marvelous: Yeah. Looks like we're going to starve. Jacob Jackson: Yup...no food for us... [After a few hours of waiting in that cave, both men become delirious from hunger and exhaustion. Mike sits there with his eyes half open as Jacob sleeps with his head slumped against the cave wall.] Mike Marvelous: So....hungry.... [Mike begins to stare at Jacob...except he doesn't look like his best friend any more...he looks like a Big Bacon Classic Burger from Wendy's. Mike continues staring in wide-eyed awe as his mouth begins to water. The giant burger speaks to him] Big Bacon Classic: Eat me, Michael. Mike Marvelous: Eat...you... Big Bacon Classic: Look at my toasted buns...my fresh lettuce and fresh sliced tomato.... Mike Marvelous: Fresh...sliced...tomato... Big Bacon Classic: Look at how the cheese is melted over my juicy all beef patty... Mike Marvelous: Cheeeese.... Big Bacon Classic: Look at my brown, crispy bacon... Mike Marvelous: Mmmm...Bacon. Big Bacon Classic: Eaaat meeeee....eaaaat meeee.... Mike Marvelous: Eat...you....eat....you.... [Mike begins to slowly walk over to the giant burger with his arms stretched out. Meanwhile, Jacob wakes up and sees Mike walking towards him in some sort of a trance] Jacob Jackson:....Michael? Mike Marvelous: Yes...burger? Jacob Jackson: Uuuh....what are you doing? Mike Marvelous: I'm...eating...you....just...like...you...told...me...to... Jacob Jackson: Huh!? Mike Marvelous: Let me sink my teeth into your buns! Jacob Jackson: My buns!? Michael, what are you talking about! Mike Marvelous: Just one bite... [Mike leans over and bites Jacob's shoulder] Jacob Jackson: OW! You lunatic! [Jacob slaps Mike to try to snap him out of it, but he keeps advancing] Mike Marvelous: Mmmm...spicy. Jacob Jackson: EEEEEEK! [Jacob dodges Mike's outstretched arms and runs out of the cave. Mike sees him take off and begins to run after him] Mike Marvelous: NO! COME BACK, BURGER! Jacob Jackson: Leave me alone, Michael! [Jacob runs into the jungle. He runs for about a minute, then stops to listen...he can hear Mike following behind him...followed by Mike's voice] Mike's voice: I'm coming for you, burger! You may be fast food, but that's the way I like it! Jacob Jackson: EEP! [Jacob begins running again as Mike bursts out of the bushes, hot on his trail] Jacob Jackson: Why are you doing this!!! Mike Marvelous: You told me to! Jacob Jackson: No I didn't! Mike Marvelous: Well, come here and we'll talk about it! Jacob Jackson: No way! [Jacob stops running, grabs a tree branch and bends it back. When Mike catches up, he releases the branch and it swings back, smacking Mike in the face. He falls down on the ground, unconscious] Jacob Jackson: That's what you get, Michael! You're craz-- [Jacob's talking stops at once as a dart whistles through the air and sticks into his neck. His eyes roll back in his head and the camera fades to darkness.] ----------------------------------------------------------- [When Mike and Jacob awaken, they see 3 men standing around them. They are all dark skinned and have their faces paints. One man has a bone going through the bridge of his nose, and the other two have bones stuck in their puffy black hair as ornaments. They are shirtless and are dressed only in loinclothes made of leaves] Jacob Jackson: Wh-who are you people? Mike Marvelous: Where am I? Last thing I remembered was me chasing a Wendy's Big Bacon Classic through the woods... Native #1: Hooga chi gon jow! Jacob Jackson: Oh no! We've been captured by savages, Michael! Mike Marvelous: I've seen this type of thing before, Jacob. If they're cannibals, they'll put us in a giant pot and boil us alive then eat us...then they'll sell our shrunken heads on the black market. Jacob Jackson: Shrunken heads? EEP! Mike Marvelous: There's also a chance that if we stand up, they might revere us as their Gods... Jacob Jackson: Ok...it's worth a try... [Mike and Jackson stand up with their chests puffed out and their heads held high] Mike Marvelous: We are your Gods! I order you to let us go or we'll burn your village and destroy your crops! Jacob Jackson: Yeah! What he said! [The natives look at the Can-Am Connection, then at themselves, then they break out laughing. Native #1: Man, these are some messed up honkies! Native #2: 'We are your Gods!' Hahaha...that's a good one! Native #3: Hey white boys, you look like you're lost. Mike Marvelous: Jacob, they can talk! They must be a civilized tribe! Jacob Jackson: This is a miracle! Let's try to speak to them, Michael! Mike Marvelous: Can you tell me where we are? Do you understand? Native #2: Of course we understand... Native #1: You're in Samoa, dork. [Mike's jaw suddenly drops open] Mike Marvelous: You mean we've been stranded in Samoa this whole time...when that is where we were going in the first place? Native #1: Yeah, you must be stupid or somethin. [The third native pulls a cell phone out of his loin cloth] Native #3: Hey, what up, brutha? Yeah...we found these two white dudes in the middle of the jungle. They say they're lost.....you want us to bring them to the village?...ok...alright. Peace out. [Native #3 closes the phone up] Native #3: You guys wanna come to our hood with us? Mike Marvelous: Your village? Sure...I'm sure we could learn a lot from each others cultures. We could teach your children how to read and write... [The natives again look at each other and shake their heads. Native #2 grabs a tree and bends it back. Behind the tree is a huge city with sky scrapers, cars, buses, cabs, businesses and a subway. He motions for Mike and Jacob to step through] Native #2: Welcome to our village. Mike Marvelous and Jacob Jackson:........ [They both step through the secret passage way and the natives follow.] Mike Marvelous: It seems we were wrong about you.... Native #1: I'm gonna call a cab and then you just tell him you wanna go to the air port. Ok? You do know what an airport is, don't ya, whitey? Mike Marvelous:...Yes. [The native calls a cab and the Can-Am Connection get in it.] Mike Marvelous: Take us to the airport please. Driver: You got it. Jacob Jackson: When we get home...let us never speak of this again...ok, Michael? Mike Marvelous: Sounds like a good idea, Jacob... [Scene fades to black and the cab drives towards the Samoa international airport, carrying the very embarrassed Can-Am Connection. It looks like they're going to make it to Glory after all.]
