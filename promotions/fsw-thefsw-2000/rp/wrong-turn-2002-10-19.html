Wrong Turn

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday, October 19th, 2002 [Scene opens in to a stretch of highway, lined on each side by thick trees with leaves of red, yellow, and green in the mid autumn weather. An old, small pale-green, busted up Pinto. Inside the car is none other than the Dastardly Duo, 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous and 'Dastardly Bastard' Timo Bolamba. Mike is driving as his eyes stay barely half open with exhaustion, while in the passenger seat, the Dastardly Bastard sits low in his chair with his head hanging down onto his chest, sleeping. A thin trickle of drool trails from Timo's bottom lip onto the front of his shirt. Mike watches as the dotted yellow lines in the middle of the road disappear under the front of the car. As each line zips under the car, Mike's eyes grow heavier and heavier, until finally, his eyes shut and his head falls limp onto his chest in a deep sleep. The car begins zig zagging on the road as Mike's hands slip off the wheel, and the sudden jerking movement of the car wakes Timo. The Samoan blinks his eyes, yawns and looks sleepily over at his tag team partner.] Timo: *Yawn* Hey Mikey...are we in Massachussetts yet? [Mike snorts then continues on sleeping. Timo notices this and shrugs his shoulders.] Timo: Alright...tell me when you wake up. I'm goin back to sleep... [The Dastardly Bastard slumps back down in the chair and closes his eyes to go back to sleep, but his eyes suddenly open and he pops straight up in his seat] Timo: Wait! IF YOU'RE ASLEEP...WHO'S DRIVING THE-- [The car swerves hard to the left, breaking the boundary and going into the wrong lane. The Pinto zig zags into oncoming traffic as Mike's foot stays on the gas in his unconscious state.] Timo: OH GOD! MIKE! WAKE UP! [Timo shakes Mike, but it has no effect. Timo grabs the wheel and frantically steers the car, dodging cars right and left as they miss the Pinto by mere inches. Mike finally opens his eye slowly and looks up to see Timo leaning over him and steering the car] Mike: Hey Timo...whatcha doin'..? Timo: HOLY MARY MOTHER OF GOD! WE'RE BOTH GONNA DIE! AAAAAH! Mike: Huh? [Mike sits up in his chair to see what all the noise is about, and notices the car speeding right towards the Pinto] Mike: EEEEEEEEEK! YOU'RE IN THE WRONG LANE! Timo: I KNOW, DUMBASS! YOU FELL ASLEEP! Mike: IT'S NOT MY FAULT! THE LINES ON THE ROAD HYPNOTIZED ME! [Suddenly, a car clips the back end of the Pinto, sending them crashing through the barrier once again, into their own lane of traffic. Timo lets go of the wheel and flings himself back in his seat, breathing very rabidly as his eyes buldge and his fingers crasp the arm rests, making his knuckles white. Mike clears his throat, takes the wheel again and begins driving like he was before he fell asleep.] Mike: Heh...that sure was a close one...yup. Timo: *Huff* Do you realize... *puff*...how close....*huff*...you just got us...*puff*...to being...*huff*...killed!? Mike: Close is only good in horse shoes and hand grenades my Samoan friend! So, where should we stop and eat? [The Samoan Silencer looks up in time to see a green road sign with 4 words printed on it in white:] Canadian Border: Straight Ahead [Timo slowly turns his head to Mike with a look of disdain on his face. He speaks through clenched teeth] Timo: Mike...why are we at the CANADIAN BORDER!?!? Mike: Umm...erm...isn't Musquodobit in Canada? Timo: Musquodobit!? The Pay-Per-View is in Massachussetts!!! Mike: Oh...well...isn't that in Canada too? [Timo smacks his forehead] Timo: NO, YOU IDIOT! IT'S THREE STATES BEHIND US! Mike: ....Oh. [Timo slams his head repeatitly into the dash as Mike drives up to the Canadian border. A member of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police walks up to the vehicle in his mountie uniform and peers inside] Mountie: Where you guys from, eh? Mike: Umm...could we just cross and then turn around? We sort of made a wrong turn. Timo: No, Mike...it's getting dark, we might as well go find a hotel in Canada... Mike: YAAAAY! Mountie: Ahem...where you guys from, EH? Timo: I'm from American Samoa and my dimwitted friend here is from Canada just like you... Mountie: Ah! A hometown boy, eh? How's she goin', boiy? Mike: Not bad, budday! Ahem...turn your head for a second, Timo. Timo: Why!? Mike: Duh! Cause we're about to do the secret Canadian handshake, THAT'S why! It wouldn't be secret if you saw it! Timo: O...kay. [Timo turns his head as Mike sticks his hand out the window as he and the mountie exhange with the-- ***SORRY...THIS ROLEPLAY HAS RUN INTO TECHNICAL DIFFICULTIES...PLEASE STAND BY*** [The scene cuts in as Mike pulls his hand back into the car and nudges Timo] Mike: You can look now. [Timo turns his head back around] Mountie: So, what's yer business in Canada, eh? Timo: We're just gonna stay the night at the nearest hotel and then leave in the morning... [The mountie suddenly looks at Mike and Timo, noticing they are both of the male sex, then cocks his eye suspiciously at them] Mountie: Hey...you too aren't...you know... Mike: Bird Watchers? Timo: Jahova's Witnesses? Mike: Girl Guides? Timo: Vaccuum Salesmen? Mike: Swedish Chefs? Timo: Bull Fighters? Mountie: NO! QUEERS! ARE YOU QUEERS! [Mike looks at Timo, Timo looks at Mike, and they both burst out laughing] Mike: Haha...you thought we were...you know...hahaha...that's funny. Timo: Hehehehe...us! Queers! What maple leaves have YOU been smokin'! Heh heh heh. Why would you think something like that? Mountie: Well...I dunno...I was just wondering. cause *I* am. Timo: STEP ON IT MIKE! [Mike slams on the gas and the car squeals its tires before flooring it across the border. The Mountie bows his head in sadness] Mountie: Dammit...so much for lovin'. [Scene cuts about a mile ahead, where Mike finally slows the car down, happy with the distance he's put between himself and the gay mountie.] Mike: Whew...what a weirdo... Timo: Mike...you Canadians aren't ALL like that...are you? Mike: Are..are you insinuating that I'm...I'm... Timo: A Flaming Canadian Queen? Mike: YES! I mean..NO! NO, I'M NOT GAY! I'M NOT EVEN SLIGHTLY HAPPY! Timo: I dunno Mike...how many women have you been with in your life...? Mike: I'm with women all the time. I was with two women in a department store the other day, and last weekend I was with my mother. Timo: YOU SICK LITTLE BASTARD! Mike: What? What's wrong with that? I'm with guys a lot too. Guys and girls. Timo: I NEW IT! QUEER AS A THREE DOLLAR BILL! Mike: What's wrong with being with other guys? I'm with *you* right now! Timo:...OOOOH, I thought you meant...hehe...nevermind. Mike:......GET YOUR MIND OUT OF THE GUTTER! [Mike puts his attention back on the road with a frown on his face while Timo turns his head away from Mike to hide the big smirk on his face. They continue driving for a bit, then Timo begins tapping on the dash with his finger.] *TAP!* *TAP!* *TAP!* *TAP!* [Mike slowly moves his eyes towards the source of the sound and his frown grows even larger. Timo begins tapping even more.] *TAP! TAP!* *TAP! TAP!* *TAP! TAP!* *TAPPITY! TAPPITY TAPPITY TAPPITY!* [The small red veins in Mike's eyes begin to bulge out and his teeth begin to clench in anger. Timo picks up the pace even more, making more noise with his one finger than the average percussionist] *TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY! TAPPITY!* [Mike finally snaps and slams his fist on the dash] Mike: STOP THAT GOD FORSAKEN TAPPING! [Timo stops in mid tap and looks at Mike with an innocent look on his face] Timo: Tapping? What tapping? Mike: Just STOP it...ok? Timo: Ok. [Mike puts his hand back on the wheel and begins driving now, his face twisted in a grumpy scowl. Timo suddenly gets a mischievous grin on his face, raises his finger in the air, then with the strenth of 10 Samoans, brings it down one more time on the dash] *TAP!* [Mike slams his foot on the gas and snarls with rage] Mike: ARRRRRRRRGH!!! I'LL SWERVE THIS CAR INTO THE NEAREST LAKE AND KILL US BOTH! I SWEAR TO GOD! Timo: Ok...ok...I'll stop. Mike: THANK YOU! [Timo slumps back in his chair with a bored look on his face] Timo: *sigh* [The Samoan glances out the window and looks at all the trees moving in the breeze, then turns his attention to the radio and Cassette Player. He reaches over and presses play on the Cassette Player and the old theme from The Incredible Hulk comes on. Timo's eyes light up and he begins to sing, while moving his hands side to side with glee] Timo: DOC BRUCE BANNER! DOUSED WITH GAAAMMA RAYS! TURNED INTOOO THE HULK! *Squeaky voice* And he's so geeenerous! WITH THE STRENGTH OF A-- Mike: What..what is that? Stop it! Timo: Ok, I'll fast forward to the next song... [Timo presses the FF button, which produces a 'whirring' sound followed by the beginning of the SpongeBob SquarePants theme. Timo sings along immediately] Timo: WHO LIVES IN A PINEAPPLE UNDER THE SEA!? SPONGE! BOB! SQUARE! PANTS! Obsorbent and yellow and porous is he! SPONGE! BOB! SQUARE! PANTS! If nauticle nonesense be all that you wish! SPONGE! BOB! SQUARE! PANTS! Then jump on the deck and flop like a fish! SPONGE! BOB! SQUARE! PANTS! SPONGE! BOB! SQUARE! PANTS! SPONGE! BOB! SQUARE! PANTS! Sing it Mike! Mike: Sponge Bob...Squaaare paa--*ahem*, I mean...SHUT UP! Timo: Now for the next song on my cartoon theme song tape! [Timo presses the button again. This time, the Spiderman Theme comes on] Timo: SPIDER MAN! SPIDER MAN! DOES WHATEVER A SPIDER CAN! SPINS A WEB...ANY SIZE! CATCHES THEIVES...JUST LIKE FLIES! LOOK OUT....HERE COMES THE SPIDER MAAAAN! Mike: TIMO! SHUT UP! Timo: IN THE CHIIILL OF NIGHT, AT THE SCEEENE OF A CRIME! LIKE A STREEEAK OF LIGHT, HE ARRIIIIVES JUST IN TIII- [Mike's hand suddenly darts forward, jams the eject button, then grabs the tape and tosses it out the window. Timo whips his head around in disbelief then snarls at the Canadian Hero] Timo: YOU JUST TOSSED MY TAPE! IT TOOK ME FOREVER TO MAKE THAT, DUDE! Mike: Can't we listen to something more...relaxing...like, I dunno..Frank Sanatra or something? Timo: Hell no! Mike: Barry Manilow? Timo: Nope. Mike: Anne Murray? Timo: Anthing but THAT whore. Yeesh, you Canadians are all the same... Mike: Damn...isn't there a hotel somewhere around? The gauge is on the empty. Timo: Did you pack an extra container of gas? Mike: Umm...nope. Timo: Great...that's just GREAT! [Timo folds his arms and sighs. It's already starting to get dark.] Mike: Well...we can just sleep in the car... Timo: Fine. But Mike...you do know that you're an idiot, don't you? Mike: Hey now, what's that all aboot! Say something nice or don't say anything at all Mr. Man! Timo: Mike...one of these days, I'm gonna kick your ass. [Scene fades to black as the Canadian Hero and the Samoan Silencer frown at each other, fold their arms, then turn their heads away from each other and close their eyes. Tomorrow, the bickering will stop...because tomorrow...they have to act as a team one more time if they want the FSW World Heavyweight Title. Can they do it? Tune in to Bad Moon Rising...]
