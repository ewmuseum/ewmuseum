GTT · Pressure

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: whoo~! As consciousness seeps back into Deville's battered head, he has trouble remembering where he is. The restraints gluing him to the feeble wooden chair bring the realisation tearing into his wrists and ankles when he tries to stand up. An involuntary moan breaches his lips as he stares around the dirty room and tries to determine where the infernal ringing is coming from. Spying the big Russian pair curled up in fetal positions by the fire, memories of the vicious beating come rushing back and it dawns on him that the ringing is in his head. Lousy motherf- Deville's mind snarls, and he's shocked to find that even thinking brings pain. His right eye is swollen shut and most of his face is caked with dried blood, as is the majority of the front of his white suit jacket. A lick of the lips brings a sharp metallic sting to his senses, and he blinks his one good eye in surprise at the unmitigated pressure he now feels to bring this sorry situation to it's rightful conclusion. It has been breeding and blossoming in his mind without his knowing, but now that he recognises it, it threatens to erupt like Mount St. Helens. Enough screwing around, his voice echoes through the fog. Time to kill this Russian scum. A loud snore sounds from Nikolai and Deville is most pleased to see Boris jerk. 'Boris... You awake?' Deville whispers, careful not to wake Nikolai, and Boris is up like a shot. He stumbles over to stand over Deville, still groggy with sleep. Drool glistens on his chin as he pulls his lips back to flash what Deville can only guess is supposed to be a smile. 'What, dog?' Boris barks in his guttural voice, paying no mind to keeping the noise down. 'You have more to say about Comrade Mortality, maybe?' Boris waves a clenched fist under Deville's nose to show exactly where that will get him, and broadens his smile with smug superiority. 'Yes, actually,' Deville responds in a whisper, then hastens to continue as Boris winds up to belt him. 'But not how you think... I had a dream last night, Boris, and I awaken now with a clear and sober mind.' Boris lowers his hands but continues to eye Deville with considerable suspicion. 'What do you mean, dog?' Boris growls and leans forward so that his face is mere inches from Deville's. 'I mean I am tired of being a dog,' Deville begins slowly, casting his eyes down in shame. 'I have seen the light... Communism is the path for me.' All of the suspicion drains out of Boris' eyes in an instant, and his ugly face lights up with the purest glee. Deville winces as his shoulder is unceremoniously slapped with approval and looks up to find Boris grinning down at him like a proud father. 'This is wonderful!' Boris exclaims. 'We must wake Nikolai and tell him! Comrade Mortality will be so pleased!' Boris begins prancing over to where his partner lays peacefully, bursting with impatience to spread the good news. 'Wait,' Deville whispers hastily. Boris stops and turns around, confused. 'Nikolai must be tired after knocking so much sense into me... We should let him sleep. You and I can talk.' Boris narrows his eyes as he considers the proposition, then shrugs his heavy shoulders and returns to Deville like a happy dog. 'You must tell me what the dream said to change your mind, Comrade Deville... Only hours ago you were the most heathenous dog on the planet!' Boris clamours, nearly vibrating with excitement. 'Of course,' Deville says with a smile that brings pain to his soul as well as his body. 'Grab a seat and I'll tell you every detail.' Boris looks around the room for another chair and finds none, then turns back to Deville with a look of utter helplessness. Deville scans the room himself, his frown deepening with every degree he turns. 'I will stand,' Boris states firmly. 'Now let me hear of miracle, yes?' 'Oh I can't, Boris,' Deville says glumly, shaking his head with regret. 'You really must be sitting down to hear this.' Boris purses his lips like a little kid who has just been refused his favourite toy, and Deville just continues shaking his head. Boris seems about to sit on the floor when Deville pipes up again. 'Hey!' Deville's eyes go wide and the smile returns as he pretends an idea just struck him. Boris sees the look on his face and almost creams his pants. 'What?' He cries, the poster child for anticipation. 'We could switch spots,' Deville suggests innocently, willing the friendly smile to melt away any doubt. Boris seems dumbstruck. 'I don't think Nikolai would-' 'Nikolai wouldn't mind,' Deville interrupts. 'We're all brothers now, right? Besides... Nikolai doesn't believe he's the boss, does he? You and he are equal, are you not?' Deville plants the seed and it takes immediate root. Gritting his teeth and mumbling to himself, Boris kneels down and begins making short work of the knots holding Deville to the chair, starting first with his wrists. C'mon, you dumb piece of shit, Deville's mind pleads as he darts a glance over at the giant form of Nikolai, barely six feet away. Hurry it up! Deville has to forceably restrain himself from attacking Boris when his left hand is released from it's bond. Instead he quickly clenches and unclenches it, working the numbness out. Boris is still mumbling to himself, so he doesn't notice Nikolai stirring in the background. Deville does, however, and his eyes go wide with panic. 'Boris!' Nikolai roars with anger. Boris lets out a little shriek as he turns around to face him, having just released Deville's second hand. 'What in the name of Moth-' Nikolai begins to demand, but his voice falters when he sees Deville's hands jolt up on either side of Boris' head. The room is filled with a sickening snap as Deville breaks the man's neck and Boris crumples to the ground, dead. Nikolai throws his head back and bellows at the heavens in anguish. Deville, his mind frantic yet amazingly clear, drives an elbow back into the backrest of the chair, splintering and shattering the frail wood into fragments that shower the back wall. The sharp spikes that remain dig into his back as he rises up onto shaky legs and tries to take a step forward. Nikolai's cry cuts off as he sees Deville coming forward, changing to mirthless laughter as Deville's numb feet refuse to support his weight. He collapses helplessly to the ground atop of Boris, seeing his grandiose plans falling with him. Nikolai swaggers forward, seething with anger at Deville's actions and relief at his ultimate failure. He looms over Deville for a moment, revelling in the moment, and then his eyes go wide. Time slows down as he sees Angelo's left hand emerge from Boris' waistline, and his pathetic communist life flashes before his eyes as the arm whips around and points at him. The staredown with the revolver lasts only a fraction of a second, then Nikolai's world goes permanently black. Deville pushes the dead body off of him and crawls off of Boris, finally slumping down in the earth and letting his inanimate saviour drop from his hands. After what seems a very long time, he finally finds the will to sit up and unfasten the straps from his ankles. His feet tingle excitedly as fresh blood assails them, and Deville smiles in the comforting knowledge that the battle is won. Ice encapsulates his heart as five sharp raps sound on the shoddy wooden door. Deville leaps over to Nikolai and searches through his pockets, relaxing only when his hand closes over the familiar handle of his own custom pistol. As he gets unsteadily to his feet, the knock comes again, louder. 'Comrade Mortality?' Deville inquires, doing his best to thicken his voice and sound Russian. He botches the job but doubts a mind feeble enough to embrace communism would note the falsehood. There is a long moment of silence, then a gruff voice calls through the door. 'Aye,' it answers simply. The single word is enough for Deville. He marches over to the door and places the nozzle of the gun against it at eye level... Welcome to America, Comrade Mortality. ~><>...<><~ McLean and Boutilier stand outside the small shack, still marvelling over the sheer number of vodka bottles littered about the place. Boutilier is grinning and nodding at a less than impressed McLean, proud of his false Russian accent. 'What the hell are you doing?' McLean demands in a harsh whisper. 'You're not Conrad whatever!' He scolds, and Boutilier shushes him with a wave of his arm. 'Sssh... Deville must be in there.' Boutilier whispers back just as harshly, keeping his eyes trained on the door. 'I'm gonna kill that sonofabitch if it's the last th-' The word is ripped from his mouth as a bullet explodes through the door and takes off the left side of his head. His mouth continues to work like a fish out of water as his eyes glaze over and he does a 180, then falls flat on his half-face. More bullets tear through the wood and McLean just barely manages to dive out of their path. The gunshots finally die off but are immediately replaced by the sound of the holey door being kicked off it's rusted hinges. McLean turns just in time to see Deville charge through the gaping hole into the moonlight, a sadistic and blood-thirsty look in his eyes. He screams and begins fumbling for his gun, but Deville turns on him like a rabid dog and fires one clean shot into his hand. The confusion that washes over Deville's face does nothing to reassure McLean as the large and bloody form stalks over to him. McLean knows his hand is gone, but he also knows that is the least of his concerns at the moment. Deville looks to be out of this world, some spectral being here to collect a few token lives and be on it's way. He glares down at McLean with cold, lifeless eyes. 'You're not Comrade Mortality,' Deville says in a monotonous voice, making it a statement rather than a question. McLean shakes his head frantically, hoping the denial will save him. 'So who are you?' The question cuts through McLean like reality through Bobby Stormbringer's world: brief and devastating, but not making anywhere near the impact it should. 'Officer Shaun McLean,' he stammers before he can think better of it, and his eyes are just beginning to widen with the realisation of his blunder when the first bullet tears through his chest. Another follows almost immediately, then one blasts through his skull and the end is upon him. Deville stares down at the corpse for a minute, lost in a void of nothingness. Thoughts try to break through, but the ringing is back and louder than ever. The pressure grows and grows until the barriers suddenly shatter like a drunk girl's morals on prom night. The force behind the pressure sits naked and exposed in the centre of Deville's being, neon bright and impossible to miss. He smiles in self-satisfaction as he makes his way over to the officers' unmarked car, focussed on the task at hand. He actually begins whistling the Stones' Sympathy for the Devil as he climbs behind the wheel and turns the key. The wheels screech as he immediately kicks the old shitbox into gear and screams away from the shack and its carnage, in a mortal hurry. Enough time has already been wasted... He's off to do what he should have done long ago... Bury Digital Mortality.
