In case you didn't know

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-02 A shrieking scream in the middle of the night Mysterious knocking at the window, making pale skin turn white. Foot steps from behind, on a walk home alone A wolf howling at the moon, in a deafening tone. Realizing you’re safe is a feeling quite exquisite, Glancing around, awaken, all is right. Or is it? FEAR~ Azala Zameer A huge, colorful African native country flag supplies an ample backdrop while looking up to a podium and rostrum set up in a most militaristic fashion. One could assume as much as the President of the United states was set to meet with a leading official of the African government, but not our trusty field reporter Micah Dikey who has made his way through San Diego’s Sunday morning traffic lull to the Hyatt Resort convention center for the first official press conference regarding the newest, and biggest of FSW’s superstars; Azala Zameer. Reporters still filter in packing the small room inside the hotel and seeing as how the press conference was supposed to begin some thirty minutes ago, most of them are counting their blessings, as jobs may be on the line in situations like this. Rubbing elbows with the other lowly reporters in the field was not Micah’s idea of a great way to spend his Sunday morning, usually his day off, but this was one report he was not going to miss. Looking around curiously Micah taps a gentlemen on his should and says, “ Hey, um, excuse me?” The man wields around looking down upon the shorter Micah, “ What do you want?” he responded begrudgingly. Looking to the other reporters name tag and reading it, “OK, yeah, Jon, anyway. I was just wondering if you had any idea when the show was gonna get going?” Micah asked. Jon looked at his watch and responded, “ Shit, already 12:30 pm, this thing was supposed to get started over 45 minutes ago. I am supposed to pick my kids up from my bitch of an ex-wife’s house in a half an hour. I’m never gonna make it now.” Feeling it be in his best interest to just wait patiently, interfering in nobody else’s business Micah takes a seat in the gallery waiting. Another forty minutes or so rolls by before the eagerly anticipated drums begin to play bringing the gallery to their collective feet. Standing up on his tip toes to try to get a better view from his third row seat Micah sees the door to the back of the platform open and for the first time in person, bears witness to the African Nightmare himself, led out to the stage by the Lt. Fredric Mandela. Taking a brief moment to get situated, Mandela leaves Zameer in the background as he steps forward to the microphones at the podium. Looking down upon the audience made up entirely of white devils, Lt. Mandela grunts as he begins to speak, “ This makes me sick! I look out amongst you and fail to see a worthy human being in the whole lot of you. This is the reason, the reason we have come to this country, this hall on earth. To take back what is rightfully ours and make the advancement of Americanism virtually impossible.” Pausing on his last remark, Mandela looks back to Zameer who stands strong in the background, a position that we’ve all come to recognize when the Lieutenant is speaking. Micah waits hidden with in the crowd listening attentively as the feeling of hostility begins to grow with in his American counterparts. Mandela points back to the African Nightmare and begins again. “I bring to you all that is good and pure,” Mandela starts in, “ a man untouched by the Zionistic impressions you filthy Americans hold so dear. Your freedoms make me sick, and ultimately will be your undoing. I could sit up in front of you for days on end preaching the raison d'être that your existence will eventually peril, but that would be much like speaking to a room full of deaf ears, so instead, I will take you trivial questions and answer them as I see fit. A sea of hands shoot up and each reporter is now trying to shout louder than the man or woman next to them. Mandela scowers the crowd and points to a woman dressing in a navy blue business suit in the front row. The remainder of the hands go down and the reporters almost in unison pull out their voice recorders and writing pads as Mandela turns an attentive ear to her question. “Why is it that you hold this rage, this obvious fury for the American nation and it’s people?” she asked rather confidently. Starting down his nose in her general direction Mandela scoffs slightly then replies, “ If you don’t know the answer to that question you have no reason being in this room. Now be gone!” Watching as the lady reporter packed up her bag and retreated out of the room Micah Dikey’s hand again shoots up in competition with the other journalists. Oddly enough, this time, Mandela points over in his general direction. “You,” the Lieutenant declared, “ the man with the black hat.” Grabbing his hat off his head Micah notices that it is indeed him that Mandela had pointed to, and after taking a second to compose himself, looks down to his notebook where he has jotted down a few question to ask in the case that he was called upon. “Yes, Mr. Mandela,” Micah fumbled nervously “ You preach against the white devils of the United States, yet, your next opponent in the FSW is Asa Fountain, a black man. Does your approach differ here now that you are going to fighting against a fellow native son of Africa?” “A black man, yes” Mandela again responded, “ a son of Africa, no. We can see through the afroman’s pigmented skin color and underneath, he’s exploited the American lifestyle just as much, if not more so than the rest of you. He’s what I’ve discovered as the MTV generation here in your United States, and his flamboyancy and showmanship represent everything that is wrong with you people. Everything is a celebration, it is always a party with Mr. Fountain, but his party will soon be cut short. He is nothing more than a white devil incognito, and shall be annihilated just the same!” Spitting a cud onto the floor beside the standard from which he speaks, Lt. Mandela looks back to Micah and says, “ You’re first question was honorable, do you have any others?” Looking a little surprised, Micah looks back to his notepad and asks, “ With this tag team tournament on the horizon, the Dead of Alive Tournament for the FSW World Title, what is your reaction to possibly having to team up with another superstar and fight side by side?” “We team with no one!” Mandela shouts out, “ The mere thought of us needing aid from an American is sickening to me and if put in that circumstance, we shall handle it accordingly.” “Accordingly?” Micah inquires. “Yes, accordingly” Mandela states, “ Azala and myself will fight handicap matches the entire course of the tournament if need be, for we shall allow ourselves no hand of the white man!” Signaling the end of his comment against prompts a frenzy of hands from the reporters, but their enthusiasm is soon cut short by Mandela’s voice booming over the top of their own. “My time is no longer yours” He screams, “This interview is over!” Causing the audience to hush in his response a murmur of voices can be heard faulting Mandela and Zameer for what the populous obviously feels as a short, uninformative waste of their journalistic time. But paying no mind to the chatter behind them now, the Lieutenant leads Zameer back out the door from which they entered. The reporters begin to filter back out into the hotel resort lobby and Micah, amongst them all, may be the only one with a smile across his face. His strides carry him confidently towards the rotating front doors until he’s cut off by hotel security. “Mr. Reporter sir” says the security guard. “Yes” replies Micah Dikey now turning on his heel. “Can I ask for a moment of your time?” the security guard continues “ Lt. Mandela would like a word with you.” Feeling a lump in his throat, half out of excitement, but perhaps more out of consternation, Micah sheepishly replies, “ All………Alright.” The security guard whisks Micah off through a door and to what he can’t possibly guess, but this very well may be the turning point of his career, or life. ~ The fear is there, don’t deny it, Embrace it and scurry to safety, or you too will be run over by the African Nightmare! ~
