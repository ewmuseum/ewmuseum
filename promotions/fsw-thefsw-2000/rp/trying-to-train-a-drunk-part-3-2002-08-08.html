Trying to Train a Drunk (Part 3)

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-8-02 (Opening up in the mountainous region of Harts, West Virginia. We find ourselves nestled back in the sticks and at a little wooden shack, or what these people would call a home. No power lines are anywhere in sight, a couple of little lanterns sit on branches of trees and one small pick-up truck sits in front of the house and wrote across the side of it is Farm Use. The sound of more rolling wheels are heard as leaves continue to rustle in the background and the silent noises of a motor are heard when a small black car pulls up next to the pick-up. The camera pans around to onto the front of the small little shack and we see Jesse Ramey emerge from the door way and just looking on at the car with a smile on his face and some little dribble from his chew running down his mouth. Back to the car, we see the door open and out steps Asa Fountain. Asa begins to look around at the surroundings and just shakes his head.) Asa: Come on, cat daddy. I know you’ve gotta have a much better pad than this with all that money you’re getting from FSW. Jesse: I done spend that green stuff, don’t need be spending it. Asa: Why not my hillbilly friend? Jesse: That there stuff done make a good bed, so I’s just sleeps on it. Asa: (As his mouth begins to almost hit the ground) You’ve got to be kidding me man. You just sleep on all the money they give you? Jesse: (Nodding his head and spitting out a chew) It done make a good fluffy bed. Asa: Cat daddy, we gots to go spend some of that money soon. Jesse: You’s want me to go get some of it and brings it out? Asa: Sure thing man. (Jesse walks back into the small little shack and Asa just begins to walk around his car as a little animal pops out from under his car and latches onto his leg and begins humping it) Asa: What the hell? (Asa yells out and Jesse comes running with a bag full of money) Jesse: That just be my little friend, you done have to worry about nothing. He like you. Asa: What the hell is it, cat daddy? Jesse: Round these parts we call it a Three-Toed Ringed Sloth Monkey. Asa: (With a look of surprise on his face) I’ve got an idea, cat daddy. Could we take this jive thing to Glory with us? Jesse: Sure thing I guess. Let’s me go get Elmer’s cage and we’s can be on our way. (Jesse sets the bag of money back down on the porch as he walks into the shack once more. Asa looks down at the little demon like creature still humping on his leg as it looks up and just smiles at him.) Asa: Jive monkey, you’re going to go with us to this weeks Glory and we’re going to cause some problems. (Jesse walks back out onto the porch with the cage and picks up his bag of money) Jesse: Elmer, (Jesse whistles) here boy. (The little creature looks up at Asa and winks as it hops off his leg and makes it way over to the cage and crawls in. Asa looks down at his leg and a look of disgust comes across his face as he notices the little trail of white stains making their way down his leg.) Asa: Ah, man, this just isn’t jive, turkey. Jesse: (Smiling) He just like you. (The little creature brings its hands to his mouth and blows a kiss to Asa and then whistles at him) Asa: Can we throw the creature in the trunk? Jesse: Nah, he’s done gotta sit in the backseat. Asa: This is going to be one hell of a jivacious trip, my friend. (Jesse walks over to the car and places all of his items on the inside of the vehicle as Asa makes his way around to the other side pulling out some napkins from his pocket and beginning to whip the Three-Toed Ringed Sloth Monkey seamen from his pants.) Asa: Glory is going to be one hell of a ride of Ming daddy. That’s for sure. (Asa finishes and steps into the car as the scene fades to black)
