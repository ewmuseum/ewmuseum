The Honorable Mike-san

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, July 14th [Scene opens in the snowy hills of Canada...Nova Scotia to be exact. The camera pans over the hills and the green snow topped trees until it stops at the foot of a mountain. The camera then pans up the huge, rocky mountain...up.....up.....up....up to the top of the mountain where there is a huge set of stone steps. The camera pans up....up....up..(whew...this is a long walk)....up...to the top of the stairs where there is a small stone building, resting on the peak of the mountain. The building looks like it has been sitting there untouched for centuries. The camera man, who is hyper ventillating from all the climbing he just did] Roy the Cameraman- [Gasp...gasp]Holy...shit...[gasp]I hope that I wasn't given the wrong address to shoot this [gasp] promo for Mr. Marvelous[gasp]. What is he [gasp] DOING up here anyway!? [The cameraman slowly makes his way up a stone pathway to the entrance. At the entrance there is a small Mongolian monk dressed in a red robe with a yellow rope tied around his waist. He puts his hands together, smiles, and bows at Roy the Cameraman] Roy the Cameraman- Hi...umm...I'm here to shoot a promo...understand? A pro-mo...promo...with a Mr. 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous....Can I speak to Mike...Marvelous. Monk- Ah...yes. Honorable Mike-san is inside meditating. He is deep in thought right now. You come back later? Roy the Cameraman- N-no...sorry, you don't understand. I am from FSW...F...S..W. Or as your people might call it...Big Comp-an-y. I REALLY need to see Mr. Marvelous, umm...now. Yeah, now would be a good time. Monk- [sighs]As you wish. But please, no stay long. He is preparing himself for his uh...match next week. Big match. Roy the Cameraman- Oh, don't worry about it. I'll be in and out faster than a porn star with premature ejaculating problems...ha ha ha...ha ha ha... [The monk doesn't crack a smile. He just look at Roy the Cameraman with the same stone expression and Roy's smile quickly disappears.] Roy the Cameraman- Ooookaay...I see. They must not have invented jokes where you're from yet. Ok...I'll let myself in. [Roy slips between the monk and the wall, opens the door himself, and steps inside. After he is in and out of ear shot, the monk shakes his head] Monk- What a jack ass. [Once inside, there is a long narrow hallway. Roy walks down the hall quietly and slowly with the camera, looking around at the ancient pictures hanging on the walls. As he reaches the end of the hall, he enters a large room with a bunch of little bald kids, dressed in the same manor as the monk outside. Two of the kids are playing with building blocks...except...the blocks are floating in the air. The kids make gestures as if they are the ones controlling the blocks with their minds. There is also another small child sitting on the floor beside a bunch of bent metal objects. He has a spoon in his hand and he is staring at it as if he is concentrating on the spoon and the spoon itself. He slowly turns his head and the spoon bends as if it were a rubber band. The kid then straightens his head and the spoon goes back to it's regular state. Roy approaches the kid with a look of awe] Roy the Cameraman- Wow! That was amazing! How..how did you DO that!?! [The kid looks up, hands Roy a spoon, and speaks in an English accent] Child- When bending spoons, you first must realize that it is not the spoon bending, but rather it is you that is bending. But in order to do this...you must recognize one thing... Roy the Cameraman- And what is that? Child- ....There is no spoon. Roy the Cameraman- Umm...what the hell are ya talkin' about, kid. The spoon is right here! Child- [sigh] I guess some people have it and some people don't. Roy the Cameraman- Hey kid...what's that supposed to mean!? Hey...you tryin' to get smart with me? Child- No, I'm only trying to he-- [The kid is cut off as Roy grabs him, pulls his robe over his head and starts giving him some shots to the kidneys] Roy the Cameraman- I'll teach you to get smart with me, ya little smart ass! Child- OW! Please, mister! Stop! You're hurting me! [Roy suplexes the kid onto the marble floor, gets up, and drops a couple elbows on him. After the beating is done, he stands up and sneers at the kid, who is writhing around on the floor holding his sides] Roy the Cameraman- YOU THINK YOU'RE BETTER THAN ME, PUNK!?! Well, you can take your spoon and shove it up your ass! You're nothin' but a bald little freak! And that goes for the rest of ya! [One of the other children steps forward cautiously and attempts to talk to Roy] Child #2- Umm...mister...it seems as though you are harnessing too much negative energy. You must conquer your anger or your anger will conquer you. Roy the Cameraman- Yeah...I guess you're right. [Roy walks over to the spoon boy and helps him up] Roy the Cameraman- Hey kid...I'm sorry. Child- Quite alright, sir. Roy the Cameraman- Hey...I'm gonna go shoot that promo now. See you kids later. Oh yeah...and don't forget...stay in school! Ha ha ha...ha ha...ha...joke. [The children don't laugh] Roy the Cameraman- Well then...I'll just be on my way. [Roy walks down another corridor and out of sight. As he does, one of the kids pulls something out of his robe and smiles] Child #3- Look guys! I snatched his wallet when he wasn't lookin'! All the Children- YAAAAAAAAY!!! [The kid opens up the wallet and pulls out a small baggy filled with a green substance] Child #3- Hmm...I wonder what this is. [Meanwhile, Roy carefully steps down the corridor, carefully avoiding the stone pedestals that hold up ancient vases. When he makes his way to the end of the hall, he steps into a small, round room. In the center of the room is 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous, dressed in a red robe like the other monks. He is sitting cross legged in the center of the room suspended in mid air. He looks like he is deep in thought. Roy slowly steps a couple feet forward and speaks] Roy the Cameraman- Ahem. [Mike's eyes suddenly open and he falls flat on his ass] Mike Marvelous- OUCH! Hey! Why did you sneak up on me like that!? Can't you see that I am harnessing the energy of the great spirit in hopes of cleansing my soul and my body!? Roy the Cameraman- Umm...sorry...I'm here for your promo. Mike Marvelous- Ooooh...I forgot. [Looks at watch] Well...you're late anyway. Come on in. [Roy walks further into the room as Mike gets up and brushes his robe off] Roy the Cameraman- So...what are you doing way up here with all these monks? Mike Marvelous- They're helping me prepare for my in ring return to FSW. Actually, I have been living here ever since my career in OSW. Roy the Cameraman- Yeah..I was meaning to ask you something...didn't you sorta...die...in OSW? You know...that whole 'Vax' thing with the little Japanese mechanical dog goin' around...heh heh heh...Didn't he, umm...blow you up in your locker room? Mike Marvelous- Ah, my friend...he DID blow up in my locker room. And I WAS almost killed. But something happened that faithful day that nobody could have forseen. Before that evil mechanical pooch approached to do his evil master's bidding, I had consumed a can of MARVELicious Soda. Now, when the dog exploded, something amazing happened. The soda running through my veins protected me from the flames and the gamma isotopes from the blast entered my body giving me SUPER POWERS! I was knocked unconscious from the blast, though...and when I awoke, I was here...in the confines of the Sacred Monastary of Mount Maple leaf. Here they taught me to control my powers and become one with my special abilities. They discovered that the MARVELicious Soda still courses through my veins, and it gives me super human strength and amazing abilities. One day, a pigeon flew through my window with a message tied to it's leg. I read the piece of paper and it was from an annonomous source. It said that FSW was re-opening. When I heard this, I began training at once and as the world saw on July 3rd, I returned to defend the honor of FSW. Roy the Cameraman-.................Oh. Mike Marvelous- Umm...you DID get that all on tape...didn't you? Roy the Cameraman- Uh....was I supposed to? Mike Marvelous- [sigh]It's ok...I have a feeling someone else is watching us anyway...recording our every move... Roy the Cameraman- Indeed... [Roy suspiciously looks at the writer of this roleplay, but then quickly looks away as Mike grabs him by the shoulder] Mike Marvelous- No! Don't LOOK! Unless you want to suffer the wrath of the allknowing, allseeing handler! Roy the Cameraman- Handler...[laughs nervously]Whatever are you talking about? Mike Marvelous- You catch on quick. Ok...well, we better be on our way. Roy the Cameraman- No promo!? Mike Marvelous- Of course not, silly. Now...let's get going. Roy the Cameraman- You mean I walked all the way up this god damn mountain with this heavy ass camera for NOTHING!?! Mike Marvelous- Well...I wouldn't say for nothing. You got to meet your Canadian Hero! Isn't that worth the walk? [Roy looks at Mike with eyes glowing red as the veins in his neck stand out. He begins to walk slowly towards Mike with his arms outstretched to strangle him. Mike, realizing the predicament he is in, slips Roy a hundred dollar bill and Roy goes back to normal.] Roy the Cameraman- You're a mind reader. Mike Marvelous- He he...yeah. [Mike and his new cameraman walk back down the hall and into the room with the monk children in it. There seems to be a lot of smoke in the room. Mike waves his hand to fan some of the smoke away so he can breath.] Mike Marvelous- [cough] What..[cough] is going...[cough]..on in here!? [Suddenly, the kids appear from behind the smoke. Their eyes look glazed over and they won't stop giggling. One of them is holding a big lit joint. He takes a puff then passes it to the next kid.] Child #1- WHAAAAASSSUUUUUUP!!!! Child #2- WHAAAAAAAAAAASSSSSSUUUUUUUUP!!!!!! All the Children- WHAAAAAAAASSSSSSSUUUUUUPP!!!!!! Roy the Cameraman- WHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAASSSSSSSSUUUUUUUUUUP!!!!!!! Mike Marvelous- WHAA--I mean...Roy! What are you doing! Don't ENCOURAGE them! Roy the Cameraman- *Ahem* Yeah, sorry. [Roy suddenly notices something on the ground. An empty baggy with a small amount of green residue at the bottom.] Roy the Cameraman- You little bastards got into my stash! Wait... [Roy pats his hands over the sides of his legs feeling for the small lump in his pocket that used to be there. When he feels nothing, he realizes that his wallet is gone] Roy the Cameraman- OK! WHICH ONE OF YOU LITTLE FREAKS TOOK MY WALLET!?! [One of the kids looks at Roy, coughs, and out comes his wallet. Roy picks up the mucus covered wallet and frowns in disapproval] Roy the Cameraman- You kids are sick! Mike Marvelous- Children! How could you!? You gave up a lifetime of peace for...for THIS!? Child #4- Fuck peace, man! We got WEED! AAAhHAHAHAHAhahahAHA! [Mike and Roy quickly exit the smoke filled room, run down the corridor, but stop at the end of the corridor. The monk that was guarding the entrance way is stripped down to his underwear and dancing around as his flabby monk tits bounce in the air. He appears to have suffered from the second hand smoke and as a result, is pretty baked himself. He starts doing the monkey as he shakes his monk fanny in the air wailing like a cat in a blender in his destinct Mongolian accent] Monk- You wooork haaard for you money! Eh eh..eh eh. SoooOooo haaard for you mooon-aay! Eh eh..eh eh. You work haaard for you money and we gooonna treat you RIGHT! WHOO! [Mike and Roy slowly back out of the monastery then run down the steps...down....down....down...down to the bottom] Mike Marvelous- Whoo! Thank god we got away from that horrific scene! What did you do to them anyway!?! Roy the Cameraman- Umm...they found my secret stash. Mike Marvelous- Secret stash? Of what...? Starburst Jellybeans? I sometimes keep a secret stash of those... Roy the Cameraman- No...actually, it was my secret stash of Mary Jane... Mike Marvelous- Mary Jane? You know her too? But what was she doing in your wallet? How did she fit? She's a big girl you know... Roy the Cameraman- No..not Mary Jane as in a person...I mean weed, man. Pot. Marijuana. [Mike grabs Roy by the collar of his shirt] Mike Marvelous- I am going to say this once, so listen carefully, buster! Roy the Cameraman- *Gulp* Ok... Mike Marvelous- JUST...SAY...NO! [Mike lets go] Roy the Cameraman-.....Ok. Mike Marvelous- Well, we've got a long trip ahead of us. Let's get moving. Roy the Cameraman- Didn't you say you had secret powers or somethin'? Why don't you just fly us? Mike Marvelous- I need a cape to fly, dummy! Man...some people are just so stupid... Roy the Cameraman- [Rolls his eyes] I guess so...well...let's go then. [Mike walks up to a certain spot on the mountain, moves a tree branch out of the way, and presses a small red button that is imbedded in the stone. Almost immediately, a door opens up in the mountain. Mike steps inside] Roy the Cameraman- You mean there was an elevator in this mountain the whole time and I climbed for nothing!?! Mike Marvelous- Yup. Now get in. Roy the Cameraman- [Sigh] I hate my life... [Roy gets in and the camera fades to black as the doors close and the elevator goes all the way to the bottom and lands on a mountain goat]
