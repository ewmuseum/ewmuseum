Alternate Training Methods of The Wild Samoan

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-16-02 The clicking sound of a ratchet is heard in the background. The scene opens in a well lit garage, and is accompanied by several classic cars. Two Samoan feet stick out from underneath a 1968 Corvette, and as the camera looks at the car, a hand reaches out to grab for a tool. An oily mitt reaches and grabs a 7/16 wrench then disappears again. A door opens and inside the frame stands a young Latino man. Jake Estrada Jr. walks down the steps and into the huge garagemahal that Timo has built. “Yo, you need a hand down there?” The Samoan grunts a little as he strains to loosen a bolt. “Not really. I’m just having a hell of a time with this nut on here.” Jake walks down to the side of the car and looks down into the engine bay. It is chromed to the hilt, with a K&N Air open element air filter sitting inside of an Edelbrock Elite Series shroud. Below that sits an Edelbrock Victor Single Plane manifold, topped with a Holley 850 double pumper. “Damn Timo, you must really have a lot of money into these cars.” “I have a lot of money to spend Jake, I can think of worse things to spend it on.” Jake thinks about it for a minute and shrugs. The fact was, Timo probably had tens of thousands into these cars, and to be honest, Jake wondered how he could do it. “Hey I was thinking, shouldn’t you be training or something? Maybe we can go spar for a while.” “Don’t need to.” Jake steps over Timo’s legs and grabs a torque wrench from the huge box next to the car. He looks out over before putting it back in and turning back to look down the gap in the engine back at Timo. “You don’t need to? Are you sure?” “Yep.” “But…” “But what Jake? How much better am I going to get before tomorrow?” Timo reaches his hand out a bit to feel for a tool, and can’t find it. Jake looks down and kicks a screwdriver towards the hand. Timo grabs it and reaches back in to go to work again. Jake sighs a bit and stands up straight. “Well, what about going out to the arena? Don’t you think we should get ready for the show?” “Bad luck.” “Bad luck what?” “It’s bad luck to go to the arena early. Why do you have to ask so many questions?” Jake opens the door of an old Dodge Charger and sits in the seat. This car hadn’t been restored yet, in fact, it was really a mystery why Timo hadn’t started working on it. It still had the old car smell, and as Jake swung his legs inside, he adjusted the mirror. “I don’t know, I guess I just wanted you to get ready for this show. It is pretty important and all.” “I’ve been there before with better people Jake.” “Yeah but you have also never won it before Timo…” This elicits a grunt from the Samoan. “So?” “So maybe you were out of your league.” Timo rolls out from under the car he is working on with a nut in his hand, the other hand holding a wrench. He stands up and shakes his head while he looks at Jake. “Out of my league? I was cheated every time.” “Sure, I know that, but you didn’t have the backup, and even the best cant take on the entire New Breed.” “Ivan did it.” “You aren’t Ivan Timo. You are almost twice as small, and you feel pain, despite what you try to tell other people.” Timo turns around and goes back to working on the car again. He tries to ignore Jake, but the young man is persistent. Jake walks up to the side of him and grabs his shoulder, half spinning the irritated man around. “Come on, you cant be that dense, you are walking into a trap!” Timo swats his hand away and grabs him by the collar of his shirt, making Jake jump at the sheer speed and power that it was done with. Timo had never been so forceful with the kid. “You think I don’t know that? I have to fight some chair wielding maniac, a guy who thinks he IS FSW, and a legend if the past, you think for a second I don’t know what’s going to happen? You are right, maybe nobody can beat three people like this, but do you know who I am?” Jake nods a bit. “I am Timo Fucking Bolamba. I Overcome Adversity, Its on my damned resume!” He lets go of Jake and shakes his head. The Samoan turns back to the car and begins to unbolt a header manifold from the engine. Jake steps back and turns, looking at the old Charger again. He tries to open the hood, and cant figure it out. Timo turns around at the commotion and shakes his head. “You can’t do it like that.” “Well where is the latch? It has to be in here somewhere.” Timo walks up to the old car and slams his fist down on the hood, and it pops up. He reaches under it and flips the latch, opening the hood. Jake looks over at him sheepishly. “See Jake, there’s more than the obvious way to do things. Why are you even playing with this thing anyhow? I bought it from an old guy because I needed a parts car.” Jake opens the hood and looks at the engine. The whole car was in bad shape, rust spots and minor body work needing to be done. Timo turns back to what he was doing as Jake wipes off a valve cover. “What does Hemi mean?” Timo’s head snaps back and he walks briskly over to the car, wiping the rest of the valve cover off, and smiling heavily. He begins to laugh a bit and looks to the befuddled Jake. “Jake, this is a Hemi, one of the finest engines on gods green earth.” Jake looks over as The Samoans eyes light up. He grabs a rag from his pocket and begins to wipe the engine off. Timo leans back and smiles. “You see kid, all the matches, all the pressure, you can’t let it get to you. I have trained my body for my whole life. I guess the only thing left is to train the mind.” “Man, I can dig that.” “Well, in that light, it looks like we have a lot to do, we should get started, shouldn’t we?” “We?” Timo smiles. ‘You are right, I cant win by myself, but I’m not by myself am I?” Jake nods and grabs a rag. “You’re all heart Timo.”
