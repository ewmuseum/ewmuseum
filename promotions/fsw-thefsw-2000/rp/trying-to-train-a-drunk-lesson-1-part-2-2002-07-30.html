Trying To Train A Drunk (Lesson 1, Part 2)

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-30-02 Jesse: I dun know about this here, Asa. **Jesse says as the picture comes into play and we see him sitting in the seat of a roller coaster, and next to him is Asa Fountain. The two wearing normal attire, or what they would consider to be their normal attire. Asa in his usual Seventies disco outfit and Jesse wearing the normal suspenders with no shirt on.** Asa: Just hang on their cat daddy. This is only a mere part of your training. Jesse: I dun see why we’s gots to do this though. These thingies here kind make my stomach wheeze. Asa: You just sit back and relax there my jivacious hillbilly friend. **The scene begins to pan out and we can now see that the two are seated on the Son of Beast ride located at the Kings’ Island theme park in Cincinnati, Ohio. Jesse looks to be very squeamish as the coaster begins to make it’s way up, up, up.** Jesse: Asa, I dun like heights all that much. Asa: Don’t worry over there, Jesse man. This thing is nothing more than a roller coaster; you’ve got nothing to worry about. Jesse: Why’s I gots to do this though? Asa: It’s all part of the training process that I’ve begun with you. If I can’t keep you from drinking them I’m going to have to train you to withstand some of natures forces, gravity being the first. Jesse: What bout the other day in the diner? I thought that part of my training thing too? Asa: No, jive turkey. That was only to try and keep you awake for that lecture I gave you in the car. Jesse: I dun need no lecturing. **Jesse’s eyes begin to bug out of his head as the coaster begins to pause at the top of the enormous hump and all we can see now is down. Jesse’s eyes get even bigger as. ** WHOOSH! **The coaster makes it’s way down getting faster and faster by the second and the only thing that can be heard is Jesse’s freighted and panicky yelling. Asa just holds on and a smile comes across his face. ** Jesse: Stop this dang here ride! Asa: Whoo! Jesse: I’s not going to live! **Cutting all the turns sharply and making all of the little humps, which cause the ride to go even faster until they come in sight of the loop. ** Asa: Hold on over there, Jesse man. Jesse: I’s not going to be able to stand this. **The coaster hits the loop and all of a sudden, in mid air, Jesse releases with a huge stomach turning flow of vomit. It seems to be suspended in mid air until they come rushing back down and Asa looks up in a frightful manner as he sees it coming down, only to miss them by a split second and come crashing down on the people seated behind them. ** Asa: That was a close call. Jesse: I done went and puked and didn’t even get my spenders dirty. **The sound of moaning coming from the back rows of the coaster are heard and some vomiting from the other passengers is heard as Asa turns around and looks to see the horrendous sight, almost making him throw up in the process. ** Asa: That’s one messy site back there. Jesse: Where? (Jesse tries to turn around only to have Asa catch him and make him look straight ahead) Asa: Don’t look back there, cat daddy. It’s not a pretty sight. **Asa whips a bead of sweat from his head as the coaster comes into a stopped position back at the starting station. The people on the walkway, in line for the ride, just look on at the sight with wide-open mouths. ** Asa: (With a look of shock on his face) Hurry up, cat daddy. They’re going to blow! **The roller coaster seats are released and things go into an almost slow motion scene, as Asa lunges out of the coaster seat and drags Jesse along behind him as he dodges the people in the crowd. One by one, they begin to vomit right after Asa and Jesse make their way past them. It all causes a chain reaction of vomiting as Asa continues on down the heavy line of people.** Asa: (In a slow motion voice) Nnnnoooo! **Just at the last second Asa and Ramey come diving out of the line and onto the hard pavement outside. With a loud thud everything comes back into a normal speed and then the loud sound of people vomiting from within the rides lines are heard.** Jesse: What done went on back there? Asa: (Shaking his head in a saddened look) They’ve all been taken by the vomit. Jesse: (Looking rather confused) I done see no one vomit back there. Asa: (Lowering his head) It’s okay, cat daddy. They’ve all went to a better place. **Asa and Ramey continue walking off down the little path to the next ride as the sound of people vomiting in the background is still heard. The scene slowly begins to fade out and into nothingness.**
