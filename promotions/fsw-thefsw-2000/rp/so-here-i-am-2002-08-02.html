So Here I Am...

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.05.02 FSW. OSW. Just a letter, yeah? Maybe so... Or maybe not... A difference of letters can mean a lot of things... A new company... A new roster... A new start? Most probably.. The scene fades in from the darkness previously filing the screen to focus on a dimly lit wall, presumably of someone’s bedroom or room, the bed-side cabinet, assorted pieces of jewellery scattered on top of it giving this impression. The camera’s angle doesn’t really show much besides the previously listed details, but as it swivels round, a more complicated sight comes into view. One of tremendous complications, of extraordinary complexness... Michel Krieg. The looming German giant sits on the edge of his bed, a pair of dark boxer shorts the only garment of clothing restricting the viewers from his complete nakedness. With his head gazing up to the right, looking towards the half-curtained window of his room, the one they call the “German War Machine” looks more like a philosopher than a physical brute. Of all the mentally challenged wrestlers to grace television, from the Graphic Violence’s to the Meanstreak’s, Michel Krieg’s mental problems don’t seem as apparent as the two previous mentioned men’s. Just because he doesn’t look like a fruitcake doesn’t mean he isn’t one, right? Fruitcake not being the best word to use. Maybe loony and crackass are a bit harsh too. Describing him as having a few screws loose or a couple of sandwiches short of a picnic may be more appropriate terms. It’s not that he’s like, ye know, crackers or anything. He’s just got a few issues, mainly his personality complex. Who’s to know if he’s right in the head. It’s not like Germany’s really had many problems with it’s national athletes, mental health-wise. A few drug scandal’s and cheating are close, but not quite. Anyway, back to the scene... Gazing up at the window like he’s studying it’s specifics, a few words mutter from his mouth... “...Capitalism...always pays...” What’s this we hear? The man who so personified the art of Communism supporting the opposition? Ah, maybe you don’t know...Let’s take a brief flashback...to...OSW... Images flick across the screen, mainly of Krieg talking with Kory Storm, some of him speaking to Ivan, both of them looking defensive. More images appear of him running to the ring during a Ivan Stanislav match and of him walking alongside Frankie Bones and Storm join the facade...Sound clips play over the brief flashback footage... “Do not worry Comrade. I will prove my alliance to you and the Army very, very soon...” sounds Krieg, “Krieg has turned on Ivan and cost him this match against El Drago” calls Nate Dumas from his ringside position at OSW’s 29th of July edition of “Slam!”, “Glad you see it my way. Please doing business with you” says Krieg after taking payment for taking out Jim Axtell from Kory Storm, The brief flashbacks seem to fade away, the Berlin native still looking out of the window, the look in his eyes distant, “He will pay...” Who? When? How? Why? Who is “he”? When will “he” pay? How will Krieg make “him” pay? Why does he want “him” to pay? All answers on a postcard...
