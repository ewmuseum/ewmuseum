I Think You Missed One

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday 'Look at that fat son of a bitch,' Angelo Deville declares with disgust as he glares across the vast expanse of his restaurant, A Little Taste of Hell. He's dressed in an immaculate white suit and his usually unruly blonde hair is slicked back so that not a single strand stands out of place. His brown dress shoes have such a high polish that one might think the subsequent glare is the reason for the scowl on his face. Listening to him would tell you otherwise. 'How much goddamned food do you have to eat before you know it's good, anyhow?' 'Relax, Angelo,' soothes the only man who is listening to him, Anthony Carpelli. The lanky man is looking in the same direction as Angelo, though he manages to hide his apprehension behind a score of white teeth. He's dressed pretty well identically to Deville, only his suit and shoes are both black. 'He's just doing his job.' 'Well he's certainly having a good time at it. Fat prick.' Angelo retorts, crossing his arms over his chest and clenching his jaw. The object of this barrage is a severely overweight individual who has made - and apparently enjoyed - his living as a food and restaurant critic. He is wearing a suit just like the other two, but unlike theirs, his is of a significantly poor material and quality. The way he's been gorging for the past two hours, it had better be made of spandex. Despite the frigidity of the room brought about by the modern wonder that is central air conditioning, the critic has sweated two round circles through the armpits of his suit and beads of perspiration cover his balding pink scalp. He is currently on his sixth course of the evening, which just happens to be a pair of lobster tails. His eyes and mouth are a frenzy of floundering flesh as he fumbles the fish with his fat fingers up to his face. The other patrons have long since stopped giving him strange, questioning glances. One would never suspect that this man, John Duchesne, is a highly respected member of the media. 'He's gonna scare away all my other business! Shit! What if he mistakes one of them for a chicken leg or something? Angelo asks incredulously, turning a mortified face to Anthony, and the latter starts to laugh. He chokes it off abruptly when he realises Angelo is at least half-serious. 'Woah, hey, calm down, man. Just keep him happy and tomorrow we've got a glowin' review in The Citizen,' Anthony offers in an attempt to mollify the bigger man. When he sees that it isn't helping he tries to change the subject, 'So, uh... How's the rasslin' been treatin' ya?' He smiles as Angelo turns a disapproving glower on him. 'Shit... I don't even want to talk about that,' he mutters with palpable loathing, then goes on to contradict himself. 'First I'm getting hassle and fines from the boys upstairs for roughing up one of their flunkies, and now they've booked me in a match against some hapless misfit who wants to pretend pain is his friend.' Angelo shakes his head before continuing in an increasingly irritated voice. 'The guy's an absolute whacko. He acts like he's some kind of hero because everyone hates and makes fun of him... You know the type.' Angelo pauses long enough for Anthony to nod, indicating that he does, then goes on in a more thoughtful tone. 'You remember those Batman movies - all twenty of them? Who was the guy with the disfigured face?' Angelo cocks his head and raises an eyebrow, trying to remember. 'You mean Two-Face?' 'No, no, no. The guy that had half of his face burned off by acid. He's always flipping a coin or some such nonsense.' Angelo says rapidly, drawing his eyebrows down. 'Yeah,' Anthony replies slowly, darting his eyes left and right before elaborating. 'Bugger's name is Two-Face.' Angelo narrows his eyes at the other man, scrutinising him, but says nothing. Across the room, the obese critic has now moved on to dessert: a piece of apple pie and two slices of double chocolate cake. 'Crazy son-of-a-bitch from what I remember,' Anthony fumbles nervously, fidgeting under Angelo's stare. 'This O'Neil guy acts like him?' 'No,' Angelo replies as he spares a glare for the critic. 'The ugly bastard looks like him.' Anthony just stares at Angelo, clearly disbelieving. 'Well... Alright. He doesn't look exactly like Two-Face. More like the love-child of Two-Face and Goldust.' Angelo amends. 'Oh... Ouch,' is all Anthony can say. The two men share a long moment of silence as they survey the hustle and bustle of the restaurant. Waiters and waitresses dressed in tailed tuxedos whip by every which way, weaving effortlessly between the tables. The tables themselves are covered in royal blue silk, with silver candle-holders and a crystal bucket filled with ice and a bottle of champagne on each one. Every chair is high-backed with purple velvet upholstery draping every inch of the business side. The walls are covered in wood-panelling and landscape pictures to relax the eye and the mind, and four crystal chandeliers hang from the gymnasium-high ceiling. Off to one side of the room stands a stage where musicians will soon be playing mainly jazz music. Mercifully, Duchesne inhales the last of his dessert and leans back with a contented sigh, triumphant in the knowledge that he has single-handedly starved another third world country. Straining the sturdy oak legs of his chair, he pushes it back on to two legs and snaps his fingers at Deville, signalling him over. For one fatal instant Deville's eyes glaze over and Anthony thinks the restaurant dream is dead, but then he hides the anger behind a patronising smile and offers a wave as he begins walking over to the arrogant glutton. 'Do I look like a fucking dog?' Angelo asks Anthony through clenched teeth as they make their way across the plush red carpeting. 'Or a waiter? I should drag this fat piece of shit outside and teach him a little something about respect. No-good, child-molesting, piece of ass-waste. Who does he think he is? Hell... Who does he think I am?' This last trails off just as they reach the table. 'Mr. Duchesne! Don't tell me you're done already!' Deville says with feigned pleasant astonishment, as if the man hadn't already consumed his weight in food. 'Are you sure I can't get you something else? More champagne, perhaps?' Angelo asks as he flourishes his hands towards the dozen empty plates and bottles on the table. 'No,' the fat man begins in a strangely high voice, then makes no attempt to stifle a horrendous belch. 'I think I've had my fill.' Yeah, for about six years, Angelo's mind shouts, but his tongue is much more civil. 'We're very pleased to hear we've left you satisfied, Mr. Duchesne.' Angelo says, more and more disgusted with himself with each passing second. Anthony simply stands at Angelo's side, mimicking his patronising smile and mannerisms. Duchesne looks from one to the other from beneath his heavy unibrow, then barks a guttural laugh. 'Cut the shit, Deville!' He bellows, his tremendous gut smacking into the table as he jiggles with amusement. 'What are ya - a woman?' This inspires even greater laughter from pilsbury, and Anthony instinctively throws a restraining arm against Angelo's chest. The latter remains cool, however, allowing his eyes to bulge and his smile to fade only slightly. Keep with the program... Don't screw it up now. 'I don't know wha?' Angelo begins, but Duchesne cuts him off between bursts of raucous laughter. 'Bullshit. Talk to me like a man, Deville! I'm not gonna castrate yer restaurant if yer not all smiles and sunshine!' Duchesne informs him, but Angelo doesn't let this assurance wipe the smile from his face. 'While we're on the subject...' Deville trails off, hoping Duchesne will give him a preview of what the article will say. The big man stops his laughter and regards Deville soberly, realising he can't get Deville to just relax and be genuine. Some people just have no respect for charm. 'Alright, Deville. Have it your way.' The man replies resignedly. 'You're not a very inspiring host, but your restaurant is superb. The place is really classy, what with yer plans of having a live band; the service is excellent; and the food - you've gotta introduce me to your chef!' Duchesne rubs his belly with both hands as if to emphasise that last, then shakes his head at the eagerly pleased look on Deville's face. 'Overall, Deville... I'm gonna give ya four stars!' The critic yells happily, standing up to shake Deville's hand. Deville's eyes go wide and he regards the fat man standing in front of him as if he were the Stay-Puft Marshmallow Man. He ignores the hand. 'Four stars?' Angelo asks quietly in a voice Duchesne mistakes for pleased wonder. 'Four stars?' He repeats, louder. 'You got 'er, pal!' Duchesne confirms the perfect rating, beaming pleasantly at the two men standing across from him. 'Only four?' Deville demands, a hint of anger creeping onto his face and into his voice. 'Why not five?' 'What? Five stars? You're crazy! That rating doesn't even ex-' Duchesne tries to answer in a confused voice, but Deville rolls right over him, his voice rising in intensity with every word. 'Are you trying to tell me I'm not Five Star material, you fat son of a bitch? What - I don't present a pleasing enough package? My style is wrong? What's the fucking problem?' Deville is irate now, and he has begun moving around the table with his hands clenched into fists. Many patrons of the restaurant have stopped eating to see what all of the hassle is about. All three men are oblivious. 'No, wait! You don't understand!' Duchesne protests, his eyes wide with fear as he begins backing away from Deville with his hands held up pleadingly before him. All of his former swagger and arrogance is gone, frightened away by this man who is half his size but obviously twice as strong... And apparently unstable. 'You can't have five stars! You can't!' He squeals, and the last word is ripped from his mouth as Deville lunges forward and cracks him across the jaw. There is a collective gasp as all of the restaurant's customers look on with surprise. A few couples get up to leave, but most are rivetted to their seats. Duchesne stumbles backward under the blow and collapses through an unoccupied table. The candlesticks, bucket, and champagne crash around him and blood spurts from his mouth, which is opening and closing like that of a fish. He goes to yell for Deville to stop, but the word turns into a squeal and a groan as the man stomps viciously on his over-saturated stomach. Duchesne curls up into a quivering ball As Deville continues laying the boots to him. 'I can't, huh?' Whack! 'Not good enough?' Whack! Four stars, eh?' Angelo screams all of this with maniacal intensity, and his hair is no longer in perfect order. It sticks up at odd ends and bounces around wildly with each movement, a perfect analogy for his state of mind. His eyes burn down into the simple food critic sprawled on the ground, who is now sobbing like a baby and retching as if he's going to puke. Deville grabs him by the collar of his suit with his right hand and pulls him to a sitting position, then throws his whole body behind a punch to the face. The man falls back slowly, half unconscious but still sobbing uncontrollably. 'I'm Five Star material, asshole! Five Star!' Deville shouts as he circles around the fallen fudge-fan, assailing him with brutal kicks as he tries vainly to cover his hippopotamus-like body. 'Alright!' Comes a shattered and broken sob from somewhere beneath the quivering mass of flesh. The words are almost unintelligible for the crying, yet crystal clear in their intensity as they wail throughout the room. 'You're Five Star material! Five Star!'
