Date: 28/10/02 (The camera opens up and you can see The Overweighter. He’s in the local mall and he’s looking around the shops. At the moment, he is in a toyshop. He’s walking down an isle with Action Man figures and Barbie dolls.) (The Overweighter picks up a Barbie doll. He starts to read the back of the box and the Barbie doll falls out and hits the ground. The Action Man figures are slowly coming to life and climbing out of their boxes. The Overweighter has his back to them. They all stand ready and all of a sudden loads of Action Man figures jump on The Overweighter from the shelf.) (The Overweighter turns around, sees there is no one there and carries on reading the back of the Barbie doll box. What he doesn’t know, is that there are loads of rock-climbing Action Man figures hanging off his back and loads of street racer Action Man figures on the floor in the cars.) (The Overweighter steps back and steps on one of them. The others quickly hide. The Overweighter turns around, looks down and sees an Action Man Street Racer in a car. He picks it up, puts it on the shelf and carries on reading the back of the Barbie box. A little kid walks up behind The Overweighter.) (The kid sees all the rock climbing Action Man figures hanging off The Overweighter. The kid runs to tell his mummy. A Stealth Action Man turns on the rock climbing Action Man’s Stealth suit. The little kid comes back to show his mother the Action Man figures hanging onto the back of The Overweighter. The mother sees no Action Man figures, grabs the little kid’s ear and drags him out of the toyshop by it.) (The Overweighter finishes reading the back of the Barbie box. He turns it around, sees there isn’t a Barbie in it and looks down. He doesn’t see it because his belly is in the way. He goes to take a step forward but the Barbie toy is where his foot will step. So the Action Man figures climb to the top of The Overweighter. They start to hit the back of his head. He turns around sees nobody there again and walks forward. He steps on the Barbie toy, it breaks into loads of little pieces. The Action Man figures jump off of The Overweighter and go to the aid of the broken Barbie toy.) (The Overweighter walks down a different isle. There are crayons and coloring-in stuff. The crayons see The Overweighter and hide behind the coloring book.) Crayon: He ate my brother the other day! Colouring book: The other day? He’s an adult! Only kids do that sort of thing! Crayon: I know! This guy is a kid trapped in a man’s body! (The Overweighter walks past the crayons and coloring books. He walks up a different isle with puzzles in it. He sees a puzzle that’s out for demonstration. The Overweighter starts to play with the puzzle. He puts most of the puzzle together. He takes a deep breath and a piece of the puzzle goes down his throat. He lets it go down instead of bringing it back up.) The Overweighter: Yummy! That had a nice taste to it! What was it? (He picks up another piece of the puzzle, puts it in his mouth and swallows it. The Overweighter looks up and sees the manager of the toyshop. He is shaking his head at The Overweighter.) Manager: That’s it! You have to pay for that puzzle now! (The Overweighter looks up, sees the manager and smiles.) The Overweighter: Okay. (The manager picks up the puzzle The Overweighter was eating and walks with The Overweighter to the checkout.) The Overweighter: Hey! This puzzle has two pieces of puzzle missing! Manager: That’s because you ate them! (The Overweighter pays for the puzzle and walks out of the toyshop with the puzzle in a bag. He starts to walk past lots of clothes shops. He stops and walks into one. There is a row of football shirts. He comes to the end of the shop. There are caps, ‘hoodies’ and some very baggy jogging bottoms. The Overweighter looks at it all, there are a few kids next to him and they are looking at The Overweighter.) Kid: Hey man! Ya don’t want that stuff, innit! Kid2: ‘ere get this, mate! This is wicked mate, wicked! Kid: And some of this stuff. Otherwise ya get bashed in the street, innit! The Overweighter: Ha, ha, ha! I don’t think I will get bashed in the street. If anyone tries to bash me in the street, I think they must be suicidal. Kid: Ideal! I think we ave an ard man ‘ere. Kid2: Man why do ya speak weird? Ya want to speak like this, mate. It’s wicked. The Overweighter: Nah, man! I think da way I talk is ideal! Kid: Ya got it, you’re truly wicked! (The Overweighter picks up the clothes the kids pointed at, which was a Nike ‘Hoodie’, Adidas jogging bottoms and a Nike cap. He goes to try them on. He walks into the changing room, but suddenly you hear a huge rip.) (The Overweighter walks back out of the changing rooms with a huge rip on the Adidas jogging bottoms and the Nike ‘Hoodie’. He gets a member of staff to get him the same clothes their biggest size, a member of staff comes back with them and hands them to The Overweighter.) (He goes back into the changing room, takes off the ripped clothes and puts on the bigger ones. He comes out of the changing rooms with his cap on normally. A bunch of kids walk into the shop and the other kids quickly run up to The Overweighter. They put his cap on the back of his head, so it’s sticking right up into the air, nearly falling off. The bunch of kids walk past The Overweighter like they didn’t notice him.) Kid: That woz close, man! Kid2: Those kids over there are ard and they would ‘ave bashed you up if they saw your cap like that, mate. The Overweighter: So what, man? I’m ard! I can take on anyone, coz I’m wicked! Kid: I’m DJ Dave. Kid2: I’m DJ Daz The Overweighter: I’m DJ Overweighter Dave: Nah, man. I’ll think of a better name for ya. Daz: What ‘bout Fatboy Fat, man? Dave: Nah, man. That’s copying FatBoy Slim, but without da slim, innit. The Overweighter: What ‘bout Da American Lardass? Is that wicked? Dave: Totally, man! Daz: That’s mint! (The Overweighter goes the checkout and pays for the clothes he’s wearing. He walks out of the shop. Dave and Daz are following him. They walk up to him.) Daz: Hey, man. Come with us. We will set you up with some good stuff. (The Overweighter follows them, they take him to a Jewelry shop and they walk in. They go up to a huge gold chain with a medallion on it. The Overweighter grabs it and puts it on.) The Overweighter: This is wicked, mate! I’ll take it. (They all walk over to the rings. There is one, huge, fat ring. The Overweighter picks it up and puts it on.) The Overweighter: I’ll ‘ave this as well. Dave: Ideal! (Daz and Dave take The Overweighter all around the shop. The Overweighter buys everything they point at. They come out of the shop. The Overweighter has 7 chains around his neck and 6 rings on each hand. He crosses his hands in a x shape.) The Overweighter: Ideal! (Daz and dave stand next to him. They all walk out of the shopping mall and they look down the road. They see a black car driving down the road. They slow down as they come close to The Overweighter, Daz and Dave. Someone in the car throws a brick out of the window. It hits The Overweighter’s foot. He bends down and picks it up. There is a piece of paper stuck to it. He pulls it off. Daz takes it and reads it out.) Daz: We ‘ave all your bitches. We iz going to kill them. Well I think that is what they tried to say? The Overweighter: We got to get our bitches back. Dave: Quick! To da Wicked Mobile! (All 3 of them run down an alley. All of a sudden a Mini Cooper zooms out of the same alley that they went down. Daz and Dave are in the front seats and The Overweighter is taking up the whole back seat. He is also curled up into a ball so he can fit into the car. They zoom down the road the black car went down. They see it in the distance, so they speed up to catch up with it. The black car pulls into an old warehouse. The Overweighter, Daz and Dave stop behind the black car. Dave runs out of the car, goes up to the black car and sees there is nobody in the car. He walks back to the Wicked Mobile.) Dave: They must ‘ave gone inside. Daz: Okay, let go inside. (The Overweighter and Daz get out of the Wicked Mobile and walk over to Dave. They are standing by the door to the warehouse. They open the door, walk in and no-one’s there.) Daz: DJ Dave? I ‘avent got a bitch, have you? Dave: No, ave you got a bitch American Lardass? The Overweighter: Nah, I though we were ‘ere for your bitches, man. (Suddenly the door shuts behind them. Daz, Dave and The Overweighter turn around and there are 20 odd people with crowbars and iron polls.) Man with crowbar: Now we are going to teach you three a lesson because you ain’t mint. The Overweighter: THAT’S IT! I’M FED UP WITH YOU PEOPLE! (They all look at The Overweighter.) The Overweighter: You people need to learn how to talk properly! And ‘Teach’ and ‘lesson’ I don’t like in the same sentence! Man with crowbar: SHUT UP! The Overweighter: Look! When you learn how to talk properly, I will shut up. Out of my way, I’m going. (The Overweighter goes to walk out of the warehouse, but a man with a crowbar slams it shut so The Overweighter can’t get out.) The Overweighter: Okay, now let me tell you something. I’m 7’2” and 535 pounds and all of you in this room wouldn’t be able to weigh as much as I do, all together. Imagine the power in one punch. (The man opens the door for The Overweighter. The Overweighter walks out and opens the door to the black car. He gets in, there is a tape in the tape player and The Overweighter puts it on. It’s So Solid Crew.) (The Overweighter sees the car keys on the other seat and he takes them. He puts the keys in, turns them and drives off down the road. The people in the warehouse hear the car drive off. They all run out and try to run after The Overweighter, but he was going too fast for them. He stops the car, puts it in reverse and goes back to the warehouse.) (All the men are standing around the car. The Overweighter winds down the window and throws the So Solid Crew tape at them and zooms off down the road.) The Overweighter: Stupid ‘Townies’! (The Overweighter gets home. He stops the car and gets out and gets into the house. There is a letter on the floor. He picks it up and sits down in a big armchair. The letter is from Paul Orion. It’s the details about going to school. He reads it. The Overweighter’s brother, Candy Cane, walks into the room.) The Overweighter: Hey, look at this. I have to go to school, so I can fit in at FSW. Candy Cane: Okay, didn’t you go to school? The Overweighter: No… I have my ring debut soon I’m against some Zameer guy. Candy Cane: I have heard of him, he’s going to catch up with you. The Overweighter: Huh? I don’t understand? Candy Cane: Well, he’s nearly as heavy as you are. The Overweighter: Cool! A fellow heavy man. Candy Cane: What about me? The Overweighter: Another heavy man. (The Overweighter smiles and it fades to black.)
