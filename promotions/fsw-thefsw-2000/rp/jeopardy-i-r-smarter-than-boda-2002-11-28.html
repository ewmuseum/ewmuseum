JEOPARDY! I R Smarter than Boda

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/02 :: The scene opens with a long shot and we see that we are on a game-show set and stage, getting ready for the game to start. The blue background, the three podiums and then the one podium off to the left, followed by the massive collection of TV screens….and the gigantic letters that read “ JEOPARDY” are seen from this shot. Interrupting this scene a voice comes over the picture we see.:: Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen! This is JEOPARDY! Now, the contestants…. :: A woman wearing a business suit makes her way to the first podium on the right and stands in front where the blue screen on the podium reads in perfect cursive “ Rebecca”.:: Announcer: First, a corporate lawyer, from Atlanta, Georgia…Rebecca Stern! :: The crowd claps in correspondence to the massive blinking “ Applaud” sign that hangs over them.:: Announcer: Second…a Five Star Wrestler in Five Star Wrestling, from Austin, Texas…Cisco Milloy! :: We are shocked to see Six Finger Cisco make his way out to the middle podium dressed in a fancy black Gucci Suit and stand behind the podium where Cisco’s messy signature can be seen. Cisco smiles on.:: Announcer: And finally, our 2 day champion, who has a two day total of $30,600.00, a rocket scientist, from Los Angeles, California, Alan Learnwell! :: Alan Learnwell comes out dressed in a gray suit that is very bland and ugly, with a dorky red bow-tie, and thick black glasses to add to the getup.:: Announcer: And the host of JEOPARDY! Alex TREBECK!!!! :: Out comes Alex Trebeck from the sliding glass doors behind his podium. Alex makes his way to the podium and begins his spiel.:: Alex: Thank you Johnny. We should have an interesting game tonight, we’ll see if Cisco or Rebecca can stop the intellectual juggernaut that is Alan Learnwell. Alan: Not a chance Alex. Alex: Don’t talk Alan! I’m the star! Cisco: Yeah Alan! Don’t be a dick! :: As Cisco says dick a bleep comes over the screen that we are watching and the crowd starts to cheer.:: Rebecca: Well I never! :: Cisco looks at Rebecca up and down and grins with a sick smile while raising his eyebrows up and down in interest.:: Cisco: No? Well I’d love to be your first sweetie. Alex: Cameras on me! I’m the star! Not these 15-minutes-of-fame-losers! :: The cameras quickly zoom back in on Alex as he settles down from his rant. Alex loosens up his silver tie, cracks his neck and begins to read off of the cards in front of him.:: Alex: The categories for tonight are… The State of Texas The History of Wrestling Famous Freaks …and last but not least Hors and beer :: As Alex says this category Cisco raises his arms and yells.:: Cisco: Yeah! Hors and Beer! My two favorite food groups! Rebecca: Well I never! Alan: Um…Alex….Hors is spelled wrong… :: Alex smacks his forehead and wipes his eyes clear of the tears of anger building up.:: Alex: Just pick a category Alan. Alan: Okay…I’ll take “ The History of Texas” for $500. Alex: Answer: “ This current President once governed the state of Texas” :: Everyone tries to buzz in but Alan does so first.:: Alex: Alan! Alan: Who is George W. Bush? Alex: Yes. Pick again. Alan: I’ll take “ Famous Freaks” for $500. Alex: Answer: “ This man had such hits as “ Thriller” and “ Thriller”. :: Everyone again tries to buzz in but Alan does so first.:: Alex: Alan! Alan: Who is Michael Jackson? Alex: Yes! Pick again! Alan: I’ll take “ Famous Freaks” for $200. Alex: Answer: This man was caught pleasuring himself in a movie theatre some time ago. :: Cisco buzzes in.:: Alex: Cisco! Cisco: Who is Pee-Wee Herman? Alex: Yes! You’ve got control of the board! Cisco: I’ll take “ Hors and Beer” for a hundred bucks. Alex: Answer:…. Cisco: No, no, I mean I’ll buy some Hors and Beer for a hundred bucks. :: Cisco laughs at his own joke and tries to pat Rebecca on the back. She jumps away.:: Alex: Answer:…” Married Tommy Lee, The King of Beers”. :: Rebecca buzzes in.:: Alex: Rebecca! Rebecca: Who is Pamela Anderson, and What is Budweiser. Alex: Yes! You take control now. Cisco: I’d like to take control of her…. Rebecca: Fresh! Rebecca: I’ll take “ The State of Texas” for $100. Alex: Answer: This is the capital of Texas. :: Alan buzzes in before Cisco and Rebecca.:: Alan: What is Austin? Alex: Yes! That was an easy one! Cisco: Yeah, especially when you have the answers right in front of you… Alex: Yeah, especially when…Shut up! Alan, you have control of the boards. Cisco: Hey Alan, pick something that’ll make Alex show off his French accent. I love it when he reads answers that have French words in them. :: Cisco mocks Alex and talks with an exaggerated French accent.:: Cisco: Blue Bla, Bluh Bla, Blue Bla Bla Bla. Alex: Cisco stifle! Alan pick! Alan: I’ll take “ The History of Wrestling” for $500. Alex: Answer: “ This man is known as the greediest player in the game”. :: Secretly under the podium Cisco reaches over to both Rebecca and Alan’s wires that connect to the buzzers and pulls them out of the podium, rendering them useless. The two click like crazy to answer in but Cisco coolly and calmly buzzes in.:: Alex: Cisco! Cisco: Who is Tyler Nelson? Alex: Yes! Pick again! Cisco: One question? Alex: Make it quick Cisco: Would you have excepted “ Who is the Fucking Asshole that cost me my #1 contender’s match”? Alex: … Cisco: No? How about “ The little pussy that is going to be choked out by me, Six Finger Cisco, next time I see him”? Alex: Please…pick again! Cisco: I’ll take “ Famous Freaks” for $400. Alex: Answer: “ This man likes to hunt a lot.” :: Cisco buzzes in as the other two contestants try to buzz in, unaware that Cisco has yanked the cord on them. Cisco buzzes in.:: Alex: Cisco! Cisco: Who is Ted Nugent? Alex: Yes! Pick again! Cisco: I’ll take “ Wrestling History” for $100 Alex: Answer: ….. :: Alex begins to read slowly and wonders about what it says on his card.:: Alex: ….This man will beat the living hell out of Boda and Mob Rule when given the chance, he is also way smarter than Boda, the stinky retarded ape, who…. :: Before Alex can finish Cisco buzzes in.:: Alex: Cisco? Cisco: Who is me! Six mother fucking finger Cisco! Alex: ….that’s right….something’s wrong! Someone has fixed the questions! :: Rebecca and Alan lift up their buzzers and learn that someone has ripped them from the podiums. They both look at Cisco in anger. Alex is now furious.:: Alex: Security! :: Cisco kicks over his podium and runs right at the camera. Cisco gets right up to the camera and all that we see is Cisco’s face.::: Cisco: You see me Boda?! I’m smarter than you! I just proved it here tonight by winning JEOPARDY! You may be bigger than me, but I am going to outsmart you and kick your stinky retarded ape ass! In the background we see security rushing towards Cisco. Just as security grabs Cisco the scene goes to static/snow. From here the static/snow closes in on a tiny circle, until the circle disappears and the scene goes to black.::
