Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13.08.02 Marvelous? How can he be marvelous? He's in FSW, right? Mr. FSW? Is that so? Very smug, huh? What's the big deal with him anyway? I mean, he's some scrawny American, just like every other scumbag around here, yet he gets the 'Mr. FSW' moniker? If anyone's 'Mr FSW', it's me. Just look at me. 7'0' Near 300 lbs. Who in hell is going to stop me? Typical Americans, isn't it? Make the weaker, less skilled of their workers one of their most presegious employees... Wait... Didn't he used to be in OSW? The home of the Red Army, hyper-extended fueds between former enemies, broken relationships between the bosses, workers and fans? Sounds like it to me... Did he leave? Or was he fired? With a gimmick like his, I suspect it's the latter... Or maybe he took the same move as me and jumped ship to somewhere where stars are recognised...? Doubt it... Damn this mind of mine! So many conflicts...! So many agruments, swirling around in here...! Why can't I just get things straight? Work them out, apparently like everyone else? Maybe I'm a freak... ...Or maybe I'm unique. Fin.
