Date: 8/25/02 Our scene opens outside of a pharmacy. But this isn’t just any old pharmacy, oh no. This isn’t one of your regular run of the mill pharmacies. Indeed it is not, for it is a very sexy pharmacy. I know what you’re thinking, “How could such an average looking pharmacy be as sexy as you claim?” Well, I’ll tell you. This is the pharmacy frequented by the man, the myth, the legend, the immortal, the best there ever was, the best there ever will be, the nature boy, the next big thing, the big show, the rabid wolverine, the machine: MING. Yes folks, everyone’s favorite FSW SUPERSTAR graces this pharmacy with some regularity. This is by no means to suggest that Ming has any physical ailments that require treatment, for Ming is infallible. No, Ming just loves that crisp, clean, medical atmosphere! Ming: I do? Yes you do! For the smell that emanates from this hospital pharmacy is the smell of your youth! Ming: No, I don’t think so… Yes! For your father was a brilliant but cold hearted brain surgeon, while your mother was the tender soul that pulled him out of his dark shell! Ming: Really? That’s not how I remember it. Too bad! That’s how it’s going to be from now on here in the land of Ming brand promos! As I was saying, our ever-heroic protagonist is on his way to his pharmacy as we speak! For indeed, Ming needs sexual enhancement! Penile enlargement! Phallic fulfillment! Yes, Ming is here to pick up his prescription… of VIGRA! Ming: Dude, could you quiet down? This is kind of embarrassing. Sorry, I’ll keep quiet. We wouldn’t want all these people to know THAT YOU CANT GET IT UP! Ming: GOD DAMNIT! Gee, look how nosey all these people are! Yes, every single person in this room is staring at Ming! How very mortifying this must be for him! Ming: I hate you. So? It doesn’t matter whether or not you like me; I still get paid! Oh just look for union label… Ming: Why does my agent never hire a decent narrator? Maybe because you’re a total asshole. Ming: Yeah, that’s probably it. So anyway, everyone is staring at Ming thanks to his obnoxiously loud and extremely embarrassing comment. Ming: WHAT? YOU SAID THAT! NOT ME! Reality is only what the people perceive Ming. I said you said It, therefore you said it. Ming: Damn you! Damn you to hell! Anyway, your FSW TAG TEAM CHAMPION makes his way into the pharmacy in search of the cure for what ails him. Impotence is a condition suffered by many men, men such as Ming. Ming: Not any more damnit! Didn’t you see me bang Alyson and Sarah on Genesis?! My penis works just fine! I don’t know about that Ming, I saw them afterwards and they were looking rather unsatisfied. As I was saying, Ming strolls to the counter with only one thing on his mind: Rock Hard Cocks. Ming: No I don’t! Heh, that’s not what I heard from that old man… Ming: I was tricked! Tricked I say! Fine, you believe what you want to believe. But I digress, Ming, in his quest for pulsating members, has made his way to the counter. Ming: Excuse me miss, I’m here to collect my prescription. Yeah, your prescription… for an enormous penis! Nurse: Hold on sir, I’ll have it for you in a moment. She’ll have it for you in a moment all right. OH YEAH! Ming: Have it? Have what? What in blazes are you talking about?! You are so naïve Ming. It’s obvious from the way she was looking at you that she wants some of that Ming loving. Go for it man, she’s all yours! Ming: But… but…. She’s got to be at least 60 years old! And she’s all wrinkly! Love is timeless Ming! Love knows no age limits! Ming: Oh man, that’s just gross. Hey, you’re the one who had sex with a Sloth. Back to the situation at hand, the oh so lovely nurse has returned to the counter bearing a small bottle. Nurse: Well Mister… uhh… Mr. Ming, here’s your prescription. Your co payment comes to $20. Ming: Hold on for a second… Ming thrusts his hands into his ever so tight pants in a search for his wallet! He reaches around for a few seconds when his eyes suddenly go wide. Ming: Umm, miss? I seem to have… err… forgotten my wallet. Is there anyway you can just… umm… let me have them? Nurse: Well, there is one way… Suddenly, with a burst of energy one would never think possible from such a hideous woman, she reaches across the counter, grabs Ming’s face, and thrusts it into her sagging bosoms. Ming: Cant…. Breathe! The woman continues to smother Ming for an excruciating period of time, then finally releases him back into the land of the living. Nurse: Well sonny, I’ll let you have this bottle here if you let me be the first person you test it out on. Ming: Uh… huh? Wha….? You… and… sex… err…. me? Oh… god… Ming stares at her in rapt terror as she not so alluringly licks her lips. Suddenly, in a dash of heelish brilliance, Ming forms an elegant yet simple plan. Nurse: I’m waiting little man… Ming: You want it baby? You got it. Ming hops the counter as the old woman smiles in anticipation. However, rather than nailing her in the way she had planned, Ming nails her, not with his penis, but with his punch! Ming: Take that vile woman! Ming grabs the bottle from the clutches of her frail old hands. Ming: Yoink! The Mingster leaps over the counter with a burst of energy and makes it out the doors just as the horny old lady hits the alarm. Red lights flash and an ear splitting alarm sound, calling to arms a team of doctors. But these aren’t just any doctors, no sir. These are Ninja Doctors, ready to take care of any harm that may come the way of the fair and true hospital! Ninja Doctors: You dare steal from our pharmacy! Prepare to meet your maker… Medical Style! The Ninja Doctors charge at Ming brandishing various medical Ninja weapons. Ming: EEP! Ming makes a run for his car, but not before a Ninja rope ensnares his feet. Ninja Doctors: Fool! You thought you could escape our Ninja wrath?! The Ninjas drag Ming back inside as he squirms in a futile attempt to break the bonds that tie him. He is unceremoniously placed in front of the nurse who looks down at him slyly. Ming: Oh please no…. no… NOOOOOOOOO! Suddenly Ming wakes up with a flash. He seems to be in his bedroom, but it is far to dark to tell. Ming: Oh thank god, it was just a dream… Unknown Voice: What’s that sexy? Ming: Oh nothing honey, go back to… wait a second… Ming turns on the light. Lying in bed beside him is the old wrinkled woman. Nurse: Thanks for the best night I ever had! Ming’s jaw drops as he sits up, clawing at his eyes while writhing in abject pain. Ming: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! ~Fin~
