ShowTime Journal #0005

14 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14/02 I find myself atop of the tallest structure in the U.S., the Sears Tower. The wind blows against my shirt as the air is thin. I can feel small amounts of air attempting to fill my lungs but it isn't going fast enough. 'Why...even....bother...' I empty my lungs as I launch myself off the edge into the clouds below. I can feel the sharp air piercing through my tender skin. As the blood rushes to my brain...my eyes fill with tears and blood as I scream to the top of my half-empty lungs. Through the bloody eyes I see the bottom is bottom concrete is coming...I can't scream anymore...my mouth is filled with blood...I can see the ground clearly now. I notice everyone sees my falling and rushes out of the way. I hear women screaming at the sight of my half-dead bloody body falling. ...'I'm...done...' My body finally smacks against the ground. My head is half-cracked open...blood pouring from my eyes, mouth, nose, and ears. I can't realize how I am still alive...after falling from 100+ stories high... 'Why...' I hear the people crowd around me with their chatter. 'I can't believe he is still breathing...' I look up at the sky as rain begins to fall. Every raindrop is like a rusted dagger stabbing me. I listen as the ambulance rushes over and rises me off the ground and into the car. 'It looks like he fell from 110 stories high...how is he still breathing?' 'This guy is truly lucky.' I squint at the bright lights shine down into my eyes...I try to muscle a word to the paramedics but every breathe is like a direct stab in the heart... 'CAN YOU HEAR ME? ARE YOU ABLE TO SPEAK?!' 'You idiot. This guy's head is half-cracked open...blood is everywhere...he is barely alive and your asking him to talk.' '...H-H-....H-Hey' I managed to muster a few words for the doubtful paramedic. 'Oh my God, I can't believe...he is trying to talk.' 'Pl-Pl-please...finish....finish...the job.' I looked up and the Paramedics were looking at each other in confusion. 'Please, I...I have nothing else...to....live for.' 'Well, if he wants it...' 'NO! We can't do that! We have no right!' 'The guy said he wants to die! Let him die!' 'How do you know he isn't drunk?' 'DRUNK?! Half of his brains were left on the concrete...I'm doing it.' I watch as he pulls out a pocket knife as the other paramedic turns away...He jabs it into my stomach and that is the end... 'ShowTime' Erik Blake is seen tossing and turning in his bed once again. He wakes up sweating and panting for air... 'Another...dream....' ShowTime says as the scene fades.
