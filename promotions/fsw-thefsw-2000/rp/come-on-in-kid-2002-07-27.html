Come on in...Kid

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-27-02 “Timo, come on son, let’s go.” Timo nodded and turned around never looking back as he left the hospital room, something having changed inside of him. He looked determined again, he looked ready to put it all on the line. He looked like he wanted to win again. A hand gripped his shoulder heavily and he turned seeing his cousin, and friend of Domino, “The Big Kahuna” Manatiki Lupo. Tiki was a really large guy, and had trained often with Timo. He scruffed the smaller Samoan’s hair and smiled, but got nothing in return but a look of determination. “Timo man, you got to chill. You know what I’m saying? Domino was a cool dude, he wanted you to excel.” Timo looked up and smiled a bit at his bigger cousin. “Yeah I know, and that’s what I’m going to do. I have been wrestling again Tiki, and this time around, I’m not holding back.” They reached the front door , and Timo let off one of his trademark smiles, which despite the streaks from the tears that lined his face, looked natural on him. He gripped the pants he had been given in his hand and reached up, receiving a large Samoan hug from his counterpart. “Good luck Timo.” He turned to his father and again the two Samoan’s hugged one another. Rufan clapped his boy on the shoulder and chuckled deeply. “You have a hard head son. Go get them bastards for me, show em not to mess with a Bolamba!” “Yeah, I think I will father. I gotta go now, you guys all take care. I got some stuff to do while I’m here.” He exited the building by himself and hopped into his rental car. He knew that Gwenneth would get a ride back to the hotel with his parents, or Tiki. The big Samoan was as close to his brother as one could be, his father had died when Manatiki was just a small boy, and he had come to live with Timo and his father Rufan Bolamba. The Focus came to life quickly after the taught sounding whine of the Ford starter engaged. He threw the car into first gear and used all one hundred-thirty horsepower as he left in a hurry. The city streets were littered and crowded, but he had no trouble maneuvering through them in the small car. He actually pondered for a moment buying a foreign car just to work on, but he wasn’t too sure about that prospect. Timo’s mind wasn’t really all there right now, and it did show. Sometimes he would forget to put a blinker on, sometimes he would speed just a little more than usual. He hummed a sad song as he sped through the town, passing the Philadelphia Court building. He pondered stopping just to run up the steps and jump around like an idiot, but it had been done. He proceeded past the building and finally pulled into an old run down district. There were kids in the street playing stickball, some guys standing around a barrel with a fire singing songs, an Italian guy in a sweatshirt running while heroic music played. “Wait a second, this isn’t where Domino’s gym is at…” He pulls out of the neighborhood and down the highway some more. Timo finally found the district he wanted to be in, and pulled into the parking lot of a gym. Timo looked u at the sign and sighed. He reached into the car for the pants and walked into the building, undoing the padlock with a key from his pocket. He fumbled around with the chains a bit before stringing them through the door handle, and opening the door. The lights inside were all off, but he knew his way around the gym like the back of his hand. As he tripped over a barbell on the ground, and almost took a dive, he thought to himself. -Come to think of it, I have never looked at the back of my hand- He reassures his footing and stands still for a moment. His movements had stirred up the dust from the ground, and it mixed with the pungent aroma of decades upon decades of sweat that permeated the room. Timo sneezed in the dark, and looked around as things began to take shape. He walked over to a power breaker and threw it, suddenly flooding the large building with light. He looks around sullenly and sees the pictures that are on the wall. Bruno Sammartino, Jimmy “Superfly” Snooka, Randy Savage, Verne Gagne, Ricky Steamboat, and then he comes across the last picture. “This wasn’t here before…” It was a picture of Timo with Team Asylum. He looked so happy as he held his Television title, Jeremy Howard held his hand over Timo's shoulder. Ed, whom Timo had recently learned passed, was standing with Bob~!, and Miyagawa was in his traditional Jap/American garb. He smiled a bit and reached up, placing a hand on the picture. “Hey Kid, you train with me, and there aint nothing you can’t handle.” Domino’s words echoed through the room, although it was likely just Timo’s imagination. He smiled anyhow. Timo loved this place, he loved being away from it all, and getting strong, just like Dom said. The Samoan turned and looked around the room, his eyes stopping at the ring. He slowly walked over to it, and climbed up the steps, then through the ropes. The ring had dust on it, and obviously hadn’t been used in a while. Timo ran against the ropes, and the whole ring creaked as he hit them. God was it a lovely sound. The plywood sheets needed to be replaced in the ring, and the mat was torn in a few places, but with a little time, and a lot of money, it could all be done. Timo began to look around, and he saw the faces of the past all around him, working out, getting strong. “Jumping” Jim Stevens, Timo’s best friend and tag partner was walking around naturally, he even picked up a rope and began to jump it. Over to his left, Lars Ericson was trying to break some sort of power lifting record. Behind him on the free weights, Gary Daniels was training with the gym hand. He could hear it all around him, and it was all good. “Timo, patience is the key to happiness in this sport. You may not win today, or tomorrow, but someday, if you are patient, you will be at the top, and you will look down, seeing the faces that want to learn from you. You got to embrace them Timo, like I embraced you.” Timo took a deep breath, and stepped out of the ring. He walked to the door and opened it, turning to look back inside as he did. He heard the sound of footsteps falling behind him and turned around, seeing a young man with an old and worn duffel bag standing behind him. “Can I help you?” “You…You’re Timo Bolamba…from PCW right?” “I used to be…” “No way, I saw you the other day on the wrestling show! Is this…your gym?” Timo looked down at the kid’s eager eyes, and he saw it within him. That same fire was burning, it shone like a beacon in the night, and what could he do? Timo smiled and flipped the lights on in the gym. “What’s your name?” “Jake Estrada Jr.” Timo looked down to the bundle in his hands, then at the young man. Gosh, he couldn’t be any more than eighteen, but he was about the same size as Timo, maybe a little smaller. He nodded a bit, and tossed the pants at the young man. As Jake unfolded them and saw what they were, a smile embraced his face. He looked back up to Timo with eager eyes, “Come on in…Kid.”
