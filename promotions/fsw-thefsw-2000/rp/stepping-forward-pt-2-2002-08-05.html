Stepping Forward Pt. 2

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 5, 2002 'Rob, I understand you guys got in some arguement...but don't just close your eyes to what is going to happen.' Tommy persists, not understanding the reasoning behind Rob's sudden change of heart towards his former friend. Rob stands from the stool and gets close into Tommy's face. 'Look...I understand you and Chris were good friends. But nobody...NOBODY...was ever as close to that boy as I was. I knew everything that ran through his mind. I was always willing to be there for him..to save him.' '...but the sonuvabitch didn't wanna listen.' 'The sonuvabitch just kept walking into things far beyond which he could handle.' 'And he always got F*CKED!' Tommy takes a step back, feeling slightly intimidated. Rob, showing no remorse, continues, 'I am telling you right now. Whatever you may do...if you go out and look for Chris, you are going to end up in a hell hole somewhere. And IF, by chance, you DO come across him...you won't get any answer you were looking for. He'll walk the hell away...just like he always does.' Tommy snaps back, in a fit of anger. 'And what the hell are we supposed to do, Rob? Sit here on our asses and wait for him to POSSIBLY come back?' Rob turns and sits back in the stool. He sips from his cup before answering. 'Tommy...' He pauses for a few seconds, lowering his head in shame and sorrow. '...thats just about all we can do.' Tommy takes a seat again. The waitress comes to finally take his order. 'The usual,' he says in a practical whisper. The waitress clarifies his order, 'Coffee and a Taylor Ham, Egg, and Cheese sandwich?' Tommy interrupts, 'Minus the sandwich.' She smiles at Tommy and gives him a little wink, 'You got it sweetheart.' The waitress goes off to make his coffee. Tommy decides to continue conversation with Rob, 'So how long before he comes back?' Rob replies almost immediately, 'He'll be back tonight.' Shocked, Tommy questions, 'Oh? What makes you say that?' Rob smirks and shakes his head, 'He's got an opportunity arising.' 'An opportunity,' questions Tommy, 'is that so?' 'Very so,' answers Rob. Tommy sits back in his chair as a look of slight concern dawns in his eyes. 'But...' he pauses. '...do you think he'll actually show?' Rob looks into Tommy's eyes. At first, he figured Tommy was kidding. But looking into his eyes, he sees true doubt. 'Bagwell wouldn't miss this chance..for the world!' 'But do you think there is a possibility?' Tommy persists, 'Just the slightest bit?' 'The only way Chris will lose this match is if they brutally murder him or decapitate his body,' Rob answers, 'His life...his career...his dignity...' '...his honor...' '...all lies on this one match.' Tommy then brings in another prime factor, 'And what about Rebecca?' 'What about her?' Rob says, showing no real concern as to her whereabouts. But Tommy, however, keeps her tight in mind, 'What do you think happened? You think she'll be back?' Rob sits silent for a moment. After a few seconds of silence, he says, 'For all I know...she's probably dead.' With those words, the picture fades. The scene shifts. It reopens inside the rent-a-car. The camera shows a shot of the road and zooms out from first person driving view to a view of Chris, maintaining control of the vehicle, on the road. 'Davidson...' '...Orion...' '...FSW...' '...they think I'm out of it....' '...they think victory for any of them is imminent...' Bagwell laughs hysterically, yet maniacally at the same time. 'But they...' He pauses. '...they ain't seen nothing yet!' The camera fades. ...But then reopens briefly. 'AND THEY WILL BE TORN!' The transmission ends.
