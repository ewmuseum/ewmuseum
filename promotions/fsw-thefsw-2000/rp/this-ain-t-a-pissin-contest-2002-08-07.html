This ain't a pissin' contest.

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-07-02 -----Dispute. Disputes arrise many times in this world of ours. Not just the wrestling world, mind you. Disputes are commonplace no matter where you look. Industry, teaching, etc. But the place to find a dearth of these disputes? Wrestling. Magnum Wolf and John Jupiter are in two distinct disputes. One being physical. Magnum hit John with a ring bell... and John wants revenge for it. The second is mental. Magnum Wolf believes deep down inside that John Jupiter was, and always will be, scum. Plain and simple. And Jupiter disputes that claim and says that he only wants this match because they never had a chance to go toe to toe. These disputes are going to quickly boil over into one hell of a match. Ending both disputes in one night? That seems like wishful thinking.----- **The scene crackles to life in a small gray room. The walls are bare, save for a 'Macon Coliseum' symbol on the far wall. The rest of the room looks pretty basic; big comfy chair in one corner, TV in the other. The one very distinguishing feature of this room has to be Magnum Wolf. Five Star Wrestling will be using these facilities tonight, and Magnum likes to be there nice and early so he can relax before Glory. In his hand is a remote control, which he is attacking with vim and vigor, trying to find something decent to watch. His mindless channel changing finally comes across Sports Center, which he watches for a few seconds with some interest. He sees that the Reds have lost again, and changes the channel with disgust.** 'Can't those lousy bums win a few games in a ROW? They've been 3 1/2 games behind the Cardinals for nearly two months now! Jesus! Some people just can't win when they need to,' says Magnum as he shakes his head at the TV screen. **Magnum turns halfway in his chair, and looks at the camera with a bit of curiosity. His head cocks just slightly to the left, and he asks an interesting question.** 'What the hell are you doing here?' Before the cameraman can answer the question, Magnum cuts him off, 'Wait-- let me guess. You came here because John Jupiter had something to say about me, right? And you want to know how I feel about talking so badly about a man who is SOOOO righteous. Right? You wonder how I could speak so horribly about a man who respects my ability in the ring, and only questions my motives, right?' **The cameraman does the 'more or less' symbol by shruggng his shoulders, and cocking his head to one side. Magnum snorts loudly as he does.** 'Because John Jupiter is full of sh[bleeep!]' **Magnum lets the shock of his last sentence set in for a few seconds. He revells in the shot of anger it must have sent down John's spine. He smiles as he thinks of it. Slowly, he returns to the promo.** 'He's such a tool. A tool for the man. He always sucks up in public... makes himself look like this great guy who respects all of his co-workers, and loves every federation he's in, and the guys running it. You never hear him complain publicly about booking, or any of that. You know why? Because he's afraid. He's afraid to speak his mind. Think I'm wrong? What's going to prove me wrong? The fact that he spoke out against me when I made fun of CCW back in PTC? That is only going to prove me more right. Check it out,' Magnum starts counting on his fingers by pulling the index finger back on his right hand with his left hand, 'First, I came out and starting saying what I thought and how I felt about CCW and it's stars. I didn't pull a punch because that's the way I am. If I want to say something, you damn well better believe I will say it,' He pulls back his middle finger along with the index finger now. 'Then Andrew Hawk or Ace Hawk or whatever his name is shows up, and replies to my attacks with attacks of his own, followed closely by our very own John Jupiter,' He pulls back his ring finger now. 'Shortly after, Dave Beaumont, my boss at the time and owner of LCW-Mercury, decides he doesn't like what's being said. Now the heat gets on because CCW's owners don't like what's going on, LCW's owners didn't like what was going on, and lastly, PTC officials didn't like what was going on. Now... with all of this bad press flying around, do you really think that John Jupiter just happened to DECIDE to take a little vacation from wrestling at that time? Or is it possible that he realized the mistake he made by opening his mouth for the first time ever, and he went running scared to save his school boy reputation?' **Magnum relaxes all of his fingers now, and stares intently at the camera. This is all very logical in his mind. Although it isn't clear how logical it is to the rest of the world. But, Wolf's eyes are intense, and he's ready to continue.** 'I know who you are, John Coens. I know that no matter how hard you try, you can't be as great of a person as you want to be. I know that slowly, your life will degenerate into something you never imagined it would be. I know that you're a ticking time bomb. Ready to blow at any moment. And believe me when I say that Magnum Wolf wants to be in the ring with you when you finally snap. Once you shake this good guy image, you could become something big. Something bigger than anything the wrestling world has ever seen. You've got the glimmer in your eye... you just have to let go of preconcieved notions about yourself, and about the rest of the world. Start looking at things from your OWN perspective rather than the perspective of those around you. Be a lead dog... stop following the pack. Trust me. I've been doing it my whole career, and I'm a hall of famer in 5 different federations. A 13 time champion in one of them. The man who pinned each and every LCW-Mercury champion there has ever been... and most of the Evo champions. The man who was a relative no one to the men who put together RPG3, and was an entrant. The man who will shock everyone in the GTT... John Jupiter... I'm so far above you that it's a joke to hear you talk about your accomplishments. FSW... this is my baby. Just a little thing I wanted to do. A favor I did for a friend in a high place. I'm making FSW something big... a show that everyone watches. A federation everyone wants to be in. Maybe if you can let go of your humbler than thou art attitude, you can help me. But until that day, Magnum Wolf is hunting you, son. And he is pissed off.' **The scene fades to black as Magnum turns around in the big comfy chair. He stares at the TV screen once again, and begins flipping through the channels again.**
