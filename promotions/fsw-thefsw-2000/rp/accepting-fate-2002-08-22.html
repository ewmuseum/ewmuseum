Accepting Fate

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday 'You sincerely fuckin' believe you can just waltz in here and take what's mine?' Angelo's voice is sharp and crisp, but the anger is nothing compared with his incredulity at the situation. He's currently leaning back against the plush velvet pad of one of the many booths in his restaurant, A Little Taste of Hell, puffing on a cigar. The band is providing a nice background of blues that helps the conversation from being overheard. As usual, his black suit is impeccably pressed and his slicked back blonde hair is unmarred by any renegade strands. His scrunched brow is a window into the disbelief running through his mind. A frosted window, however, as only a fool lets an adversary know exactly where his mind is. This minute's adversary is Johnny 'Lips' Restelli, a man notoriously known within the family for being ruthless and unusually cruel. He earned his moniker several years ago by cutting the lips off of a woman he was dating after finding her in the arms of another man. Angelo couldn't help thinking they'd get along great if the man wasn't so goddamned stupid. And such a slob. Restelli is slouched across the table from Angelo, his stubby body hanging over the table as he slurps long strands of spaghetti into his mouth. His suit is creased and crumpled as if the man had just slept in it... In a dumpster. Fringed by greasy black hair, his pink scalp bobs up and down as he answers Angelo without sparing even a glance. Look at me, motherfucker, Angelo snapped in his mind. If you're gonna rob me at least look me in the fuckin' eye while you're doin' it, you prideless sack of shit. Angelo knew it would be a mistake to take the man at face value, since that would put him on level with a recently put-out bum. Restelli was dangerous alright, and his temper rivalled Angelo's own. The wrong statement could easily turn this into a bloodbath. Yeah, better employ some tact. 'What makes you think you deserve a piece of the action?' Angelo asks in a level tone, calmly folding his hands on the table. Restelli spends another ten seconds noisily sucking up the noodles, then wipes his mouth on the back of his pudgy hand and straightens up to his normal slouch. Barely thirty years old and he had already acquired a hunchback. He regards Deville for a moment like the man had asked him why wood comes from trees. 'I've been in this business much longer,' he states simply, his oddly soft and melodious voice belying his physical appearance. Angelo can only stare, his teeth unconsciously clamping together and grinding. To say he's at the end of his wits with people thinking seniority corresponds directly with power would be like saying the Pacific is a big drink of water. 'Besides,' Restelli continues, flashing a greedy little smile that reveals teeth unaccustomed to the business end of a toothbrush. 'You're on shaky ground with Salvatore right now. It's open season, angel, and I'm baggin' my limit.' 'What does Salvatore have to be pissed about? I've paid the mortgage on his house five times over.' The implication that Restelli has done nothing of the sort during his longer tenure hangs heavily on the air, but the man is undaunted. He simply continues smiling that hyena's smile. 'Mickey.' The name of the recently deceased causes another long silence that 'Lips' seems to revel in. He goes back to slurping his spaghetti while Angelo considers the possibility that Mr. Salvatore blames him for Mickey's death. I wouldn't put it past that old gremlin, but I'll be damned if this parasite is going to make a profit off of it. The anger he's been holding in this whole time starts to bubble up as he considers the state of things. Deciding enough is enough, he reaches over and grabs the fork out of the stubby hand, ignoring the grunt of protest as he casually places it on the plate. He reaches his other hand across the table and plants his cigar right into the middle of the still impressive mound, so it sizzles and sticks out like some kind of french fry tree. 'What the-?' Restelli begins to question Angelo's audacity, but he's immediately cut off. 'Shut the fuck up,' Angelo snarls in a low tone, and the other man's eyebrows rise as if trying to replace the lost hair. 'You think I'm going to give you half of A Little Taste of Hell just because you've been here longer than me? Or because Salvatore's feathers are a bit ruffled? Your stench must have fried your fuckin' brain... Now get the fuck out of my restaurant.' The whole thing is delivered in the same tone and pitch, but Angelo's face grows harder and more dangerous with each passing second. Restelli's response is to pull a 9mm from his lap and place it on the table facing Angelo, his finger resting snugly on the trigger. The smile turns to a smirk as Angelo looks from Restelli to the gun, then back again. 'Talk like that again and I'll shoot ya,' Restelli laughs humourlessly. 'You owe me some respect, Deville, and for your little indiscretion there you owe me a little bit of my share in advance. Like right now. I was gonna give you a grace period, but you just lost that.' Could I grab the gun? Angelo queries himself, wanting desperately to kill the man sitting in front of him. No, he'd fuckin' shoot me the second my hand twitched. Sonofabitch... 'Fine,' Angelo concedes with a resigned sigh. Incensing Angelo further, Restelli's smile reflects his knowledge that Angelo would surrender. 'That's a good boy,' Restelli beams. 'I want fifty. Right now.' 'It's in the office,' Angelo says numbly, disbelieving how the day has degenerated so quickly. 'Well...?' Restelli motions with the gun for Deville to get up. 'Let's go.' Both men stand up as Restelli places his gun-hand inside his coat and waits for Deville to lead the way. Grudgingly, Deville starts marching across the cotton-like carpeting, nodding and smiling to people he knows as he weaves through several rows of occupied tables. Cigar and cigarette smoke hang heavy on the air, but the haze they create is only partially responsible for the pounding inside Deville's head. They finally come to a large, plain oak door, which Angelo opens and beckons Restelli to lead the way through. 'Come on, Deville. What the fuck do I look like?' Restelli spouts the rhetoric and for a moment Deville is going to respond. Deciding the man's ego is probably a little too fragile to be confronted with the truth, Deville just shrugs innocently and strides through the doorway. Restelli follows him in and the door closes softly behind them. The hallway extends to their left, and is unremarkable except for the camera in the furthest upper right corner. The hallway is only about fifteen feet long, and Deville is halfway down when he shoots a glance up at the camera and adjusts his tie. 'Keep your hands at your sides, Deville,' Restelli warns as he plucks the gun out of his jacket and presses it against Angelo's back. 'You never itch, is that it?' Deville asks with a suspicious glance over his shoulder. Restelli ignores him and the two reach the door. Deville begins fumbling around in his pocket for his keys, going through five before finally plucking out the small silver ticket and placing it into the slot. 'Right through here,' he spits, certain to keep the grudge in his voice. The door opens and Deville leads them into a rather large room with a dozen television monitors lining the left wall. Along the far wall is Deville's desk with a waist-high safe tucked up against the right side. A few plants dot the room, but otherwise it is empty. Except for the two men laying in wait, one on either side of the door. As Restelli's outstretched arm enters the room behind Deville, four hands spring into action, disarming the slob before he thinks to flex his trigger finger. His anguished cry is quickly cut off as he's beat to the ground and pistol-whipped with his own gun. Angelo continues walking calmly into the room as his goons knock Restelli to the ground and begin mercilessly kicking and stomping him. Restelli groans and grunts, but at least he has enough pride not to cry out. Deville will give him that. 'Thanks boys,' Angelo says as he turns around. 'Now leave him be,' he continues, and the two thugs pause long enough to cast a questioning glance at Deville. 'I'll take over from here.' Deville snarls and the two men understand, smiling and taking a couple of steps back to enjoy the show. 'You want what's mine, John?' Angelo asks as he steps forward, nonchalantly producing a large knife from his left breast pocket. 'Well so do I... And you don't have the power, smarts, or skill to overcome my will.' Angelo kneels down beside him and taps the back of his head with the knife. 'Now roll onto your back and accept your fate.'
