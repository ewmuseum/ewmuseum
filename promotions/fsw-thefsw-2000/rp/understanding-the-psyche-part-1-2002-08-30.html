Understanding The Psyche (Part 1)

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-30-02 When fighting one person that you know nothing at all about, you’ve got to try and get into the mind of that person. Not only for a better understanding of what they may bring to the match at hand, but so you can gain the upper hand on your competition. Now, depending on who your opponent is will also come into play. Seeing as how it wouldn’t really take much to figure out the psyche of one of the less men in your respected organization, going up against someone who is actually holding a title is going to be a challenge in itself. This opportunity could very easily break a single person’s career or it could help elevate him into something that noone has ever seen before. As things are going now, the person that we must figure out is one who goes by the name of Angelo Deville. A regular mobster of sorts, seen committing gangster related activities in the business, or has he? Something that you’ve got to think about fairly well, is using this against him going to be able to help you get under his skin, or is it only going to be something that he would smirk at and chuckle, like any made man would do? Today, we find ourselves in the back alleys of a little town. Jesse Ramey is seen wandering around, stumbling a bit, obviously drunk. When out of nowhere walks a dark figure. Dark Figure: Mr. Ramey, I have asked you to come here today so that I can give you some info on the man that you’ll be facing this week. Jesse: (Looking really hard) I’s done can’ts see yous over there. I’s needing yous to come on over heres in light so I’s can see your face. DF: No, Mr. Ramey, I can’t do that, because with showing you who I am could mean really bad things for me. Jesse: I’s done care, you’lls be alright. Jesse begins to make his way over to the man standing in the alley, in the darkness, and he begins to run, so Jesse in a drunken rage gives chase to the man. Finally, downing him in the light of outside the alley, revealing him to be the Vendor that Angelo Deville had been having problems with in the past. Jesse: Hey, you done be that one guy who done give out them there drinks that people be wanting. I’s know you from them there shows we be putting on, yous do a good job each place giving all them there people whatin they want. The Vendor tries to make his way back to his feet, pushing Jesse off of him and whipping the dirt off of his clothes. Vendor: Dog gone you stupid hillbilly. I didn’t want anyone knowing who I was, because now Angelo is going to try and kill me. Jesse: I done want me one of them there drinks man, yous gots any on you? Vendor: You obviously know nothing, do you? (The man shakes his head) I hope Angelo destroys you. Jesse begins to look a little bleakly at the man until finally he begins shaking his head and walks over to him and taps him on the shoulder. The man doesn’t turn around, so Jesse grabs him by his shoulders, twists him around, and slams him up against the brick wall. Jesse: (Talking without the hillbilly accent and kind of whispering) Okay, buddy, you better be telling me what I want to know. All of that being stupid hillbilly crap isn’t as easy as it seems and I didn’t come all the way out here just to get fucking lip from you. I’m going to go back into my gimmick here and you’re going to like it, you’ll answer the questions that I ask and you’ll like doing it. Don’t make me break Kay-Fabe one more time because if you do, (Jesse draws back his fist making the man quiver in fear) I’m going to knock the shit out of you, leaving you with no teeth and I’ll kick your guts in so much that you’ll be shitting in a bag for the rest of your life. Now, do you understand me? The man nods his head in approval of what Jesse just said. Jesse lowers him down to his feet and fixes his clothing. Jesse: (Back in his hillbilly accent) Aight, nows whats do you know? Vendor: (Looking around) Let’s do this somewhere else, I can’t risk being seen with you out here on the streets. He’s probably already on his way here now. Meet me tomorrow at this address. The man hands Jesse a piece of paper as he begins to walk off and Jesse is kind of left standing in awe, wondering if what he did earlier even came close to sinking into the man’s head and the scene fades to black.
