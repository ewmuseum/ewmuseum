Date: 7/23/2002 Guest Staring White Mexican, Scott Taylor and Teresa Tomas 'Our scene opens in a secluded remote area somewhere high the Denver Mountains. Up on top of this mountain is one of the largest hotels built from the 1930's to present time, the name of this situate is the Over Flow Hotel. A place where travelers during the summer can get away from the big city lights and the hustle and bustle of progression. Now, during the summers this is a lively active enjoyable place to call home for a few weeks. But during the winters; there are ruthless unsympathetic callous snowstorms. People must leave before the blizzards begin and the roads are no longer able to be driven upon with out a snow cat. During these long winter months the owner of the hotel hires a man and his immediate family to be the caretakers. Funny thing about this place is that the male caretakers seem to go a little mad from time to time. Back in the early days a man by the name of Mark Makonie slaughtered his entire family and their cats. He then took a shotgun and blew the back of his head clean off.' 'This year like others is no different, the people are leaving the Over Flow Hotel and the staff is packing their bags and leaving as well. The owner of the Over Flow who was swamped for time had no choice but to except his only applicant who applied, a man by the name of Lance Perfection.' 'The scene takes us inside the Over Flow hotel in which we see a younger man around the age of 30 years or so standing in the lobby awaiting the hotel manager for his interview. This man that you see is about 6 foot 2 inches tall, not a well built man but around average. He carries a bit of a beer belly having been former alcoholic now restored to health, cured of the dieses. He is not an attractive man, rather homely really with a rather large cranium and balding to boot. He has a slight comb over covering most of the middle of his baldhead. From head to toe he wears a gray 25 dollar suit given down to him from father to father and so fourth. His shoes are brown with rusted dirt stains, but he is after all the only applicant for job. Having the reputation that this Hotel has it isn't UN heard of not to have many locals sign up for the duties.' 'A tall well-dressed gentleman with his flirted mustache waxed into a curly q, hair slicked back with so much grease he could fry an egg on his head and feed an army. This man is the owner of the Over Flow hotel, he struts towards the poorly dressed applicant with a snubbing snob like look upon his face. The tall gentleman glares at the poorly dressed man giving him a glance over sizing him up. The hotel owner just sighs and shakes his head giving the poorly dressed man a feeling of insecurity towards the job.' Hotel Owner: So you are he, the job applicant who would like to have the honor of looking after the Over Flow Hotel. Well I am sorry but I am afraid I have forgotten your name. Poorly Dressed Man: Well my names Lance Perfection sir. Hotel Owner: Ah that's right Lance Perfection, How do you do my name is Scott Taylor. I am the resident owner of this establishment. 'The two men embrace in a shaking of hands after introducing themselves to one another. As Lance begins to pull his hand away he finds that after shaking Scott Taylors hand, a strange white substance lingered with in his palm.' Scott Taylor: Follow me into my office where we may speak of business. 'Scott Taylor begins to walk towards his office when he stops by a near table with a small Mediterranean poodle tamely waiting for him. Scott pets the creatures head and then lifts it into his arms, slowly he slides his thumb up the miniature dog’s rear end. He holds the creature up like a puppet wiggling his thumb around in this pouches anus as if getting some sort of sick pleasure from the whole ordeal.' Lance Perfection: 'says to himself' So that's what this white stuff on my hands is... 'Lance whips his hands on his brown suit pants leaving the white stain on the thigh leg. He then follows Scott Taylor into his office where they can discuss more personal matters. When in the office Scott bluntly sits down behind his desk looking over the single application as if needing to make a decision about who the caretaker will be. Lance on the other hand plops down in a hard wooden leather seat torn on the corners causing sharp knife like razor sharp edges. The young homely man's hands begin to clam up so bad that when he touches the leather of the chair he stains it with the greases from his earlier lunch.' Scott Taylor: I see here it says you are a family man, is that true? Lance Perfection: Yes sir it is, my wife Teresa Tomas. And of course MEXI, But don't worry sir MEXI is house broken. Scott Taylor: Is MEXI your pet? Lance Perfection: Well no sir; MEXI is my son! Scott Taylor: And you say he is house broken? Lance Perfection: Yes sir broke him myself. Scott Taylor: Riiiighht.... I myself have never been much of a family man, for as long as I can remember it has just been me and my Mediterranean poodle Peanut. Him and I we have laughed together, hurt together, loved together 'Thumb goes further up the dogs butt' Made sweet sweet love together. 'A strange blissfully erotic grin comes over Scott Taylor’s face speaking of his puppy Peanut. He holds Peanut up in the air while singing and making the poor little dog dance around while still amazing holding his thumb up the dog’s rear end.' Scott Taylor: Sometimes I feel like a Peanut, sometimes I don't, I've got Peanut you don't! Lance Perfection ok...... 'looking at Scott Taylor strangely' Scott Taylor: Oh right, any way congratulations you are hired! Bring your family and feel free to stay in any room you choose. Now, let’s just shake on it and its a deal. 'Scott Taylor reaches his hand out still with the dog securely perched on his thumb. Lance wonders if she should shake or not but he knew if he hadn't and declined it would be an insult to the owner. So Lance sucks it up and shakes the hotel owner’s hand, a clear juice drips from the dog’s rear end down Scott Taylor’s thumb and into Lance's hand. He quickly pulls away and leaves the office as hastfuly as possible.' 'Meanwhile back at the trailer park, Teresa Tomes is packing up their best clothes, for the trip to the Over Flow Hotel. Mexi is in his room listening to some kind of loud head aching rock music. Teresa walks over to Mexi's room knocking on the door a few times, but because the music is so loud Mexi cant hear her to answer. So like any mother she opens his bedroom door. When she walks in her mouth drops to the ground for she is absolutely horrified at what she has found. Mexi her son is bent over with his legs over his head and his face in his crotch.' Teresa Tomas: What did your father say about trying to do your own business to yourself? 'Mexi unfolds himself, from himself. He turns the loud rock music down and then belches in his mother’s direction.' Teresa Tomas: Its that damn rock music, all their groovy ways and sucking of them own selves is polluting your mind. If your father were here he would probably fall over in a heart attack. Mexi: Mom calm down! I wasn't trying to do as you think I was trying to do. I was having a conversation with the little boy who lives in my ass. And if you or dad know a better way I sure would like to hear it. Because surgically removing my ribs were rather painful. The young White Mexican boy pulls out a zip lock bag with his ribs with his lower rids tightly sealed in them. Teresa Tomas: You pack those away MR. This is a respectable trailer, what happened to mine and 'slowly says' your dad’s son. 'Even though he is the child of a Mexican man after a wild night of tequila filled burritos and hat dancing that Teresa had while her and Lance were broken up for 15 minutes.' Mexi: 'FART' That's where he went mom try and catch him, he is riding on that fart! Teresa Tomas: Just pack your bags your dad will be back tomorrow mourning and he will want to leave early. 'With that said she slams Mexi's room door behind and goes back to packing the paper plants and tank top t shirts with plad pants.'
