Always have a spare battery. Pt. One

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/01/02 Jupiter: No … a little more to the left … ok … higher now, and zoom in. There. You’ve got it! (The screen bursts to life, as a portable digital video camera flickers on. We are at, what looks to be John Jupiter’s condo in his hometown of Toronto. John is sitting down on the leather couch across from the television his brother, Gavin, propped the camera on. He’s got on the usual khaki shorts and sandals, but today he sports a top that has never before been seen on him, a Five Star Wrestling shirt. He’s cut the sleeves off of the shirt, probably because they were a little tight, probably because he likes to show off his arms. He gets that part of him from his step-brother. He pulls his jaw-length hair back behind his ears and motions for his brother to get on the couch.) Jupiter: Get over here … come on … sit down. I’ve only got enough battery power in this to last for a little while. (His brother trods over to the couch and plops down beside him, stretching out regally, as if he were king shit. But then again, in his eyes, he is.) Jupiter: Alright, let’s see what’s on the agenda today. I’ve got a few things in particular to address … namely, Kid Wonder, or Super Duper Masked Jobber King if you will, and Magnum Wolf. More might come to mind later, but those are the two most important ones. Gavin: Yeah bro. Kid Wonder got the ass-kicking he deserved! (John heaves a huge sigh at the interruption.) Jupiter: Shut up Gav. (Gavin looks over at his brother and raises his eyebrow, questioning his authority.) Gavin: And who are you to make me? Jupiter: I’m your younger brother, who was always the one who was better than you at wrestling. Not to mention you’ve been out of the business for about 3 years now. Wrestling isn’t like riding a bike. You don’t just hop back in. Gavin: What, you think I lost it? You want to arm-wrestle? Jupiter: Don’t even consider it. You weigh no more that 220 and you’ve lost half the muscle you used to have. Gavin: Ok fine … I may not have the muscle you’ve got … but I do have something you never could have. Jupiter: And that is? Gavin: A different woman every night of the week. Jupiter: That’s something to pride yourself on. Gavin: Oh c’mon bro. Aren’t you tired of trying to find Mrs. Right? Get with it! There is no Mrs. Right. There’s only Mrs. Right Now. And right now I’ve got a date with two girls tonight. One of which I know is going to be sooo easy to take it to. Jupiter: I had Mrs. Right Gav. Savanda, you remember Savvy, don’t you? Gavin: Meeee-ow. That girl was mint! And you let her go to the grasp of Tyler Nelson! Pussycats are loyal if you treat them well bro … what did you do to send her there? Jupiter: I lied to her ….. Gavin: Adda boy! Jupiter: I had no choice though! I had to keep myself secret from everyone. They all thought I was dead! They all mourned my death and had a funeral for me. Gavin: Oh yeah … I remember that. Miyagawa stole your ashes. Jupiter: He stole the ashes from your stogies. But that’s not the point … what I was getting at was that I’ve been working my ass off these past few weeks and months since I found out that CCW was folding, and then a situation arose that I just couldn’t pass up. Gavin: Oh god. Don’t start with your “I want to work with Paul Orion” bullshit again. I’m sick and tired of hearing about that. I’m starting to think you can’t find Mrs. Right because you’re more into the dick. Jupiter: Dammit Gav! Stop talking! I’m trying to cut a promo here for FSW! Gavin: Fine … do your damn promo. I’m going to go grab a beer. Jupiter: Good. Leave. (Gavin pulls himself off the couch and then hops over the back of it, giving his brother a playful slap on the back of the head as he walks by. He disappears into a room in the background, and you can hear him crack open a beer. John, removed of the distraction, takes a breath to settle down, and regain his bearing.) Jupiter: Just after I received a phone call 4 weeks ago to tell me that CCW has closed it’s doors for good, that Andre failed to do his job in keeping it alive, I got a phone call from the last person I expected. From a man I thought I failed so long ago. The Phantom Booker. He calls me up and starts with some small talk, asked me if I heard about CCW closing, and I was like … yeah, I just found out now. He asked me what my plans were for the future now. I really didn’t know. This wasn’t really something I was planning on happening. But now that it has … I’m stuck in the middle of a maze, not knowing which way to go. He tells me he’s got an offer. He knows that I’ve been a long-time fan of Paul Orion, and asks me if I’d like to work with him. Work for him technically, but at the same time … to be a part of his career. I ask him how. He says three words, Five Star Wrestling. I told him that I’d have to think about it, and that I’d get back to him when I made up my mind. (John pauses to take a sip of water.) Jupiter: A few days later, I’m browsing through some news websites for ew24-7, and I see something interesting. The Super Duper Masked Jobber King has made his way into FSW, and he was ready to start a career. Seeing that name again. Knowing that Kid Wonder was now in the fed that I had just been offered a job in, I called The Phantom Booker right away. I said yes. I want to join FSW. I’ve got some extra, unfinished business to take care of there. He pulled out his magic wand and waved it around, and I don’t know how he did it … but within a few days, I received another phone call, from Howard Porter. I was anxious to get started, but they told me it would have to wait just a little bit, because there were some big things going on. I said it was ok … so long as I got to get to SDMJK. He was a little hesitant, but said he’d work something out. (At this point, Gavin sticks his head out the doorway of the kitchen with a curious look on his face.) Gavin: Are you still rambling on out there? Jupiter: Yes Gavin. It’s part of my job. Gavin: Well hurry up. You need to go get more beer. Jupiter: Get off your lazy ass and get it yourself! Gavin: Screw that! This is your place! Jupiter: Will you please let me finish?!?! Gavin: Fine … fine … take as long as you want … I don’t need beer … you’ve only got one more …. (John turns back to the camera, and we can still hear Gavin muttering angrily in the kitchen, complaining about the lack of beer and something about porn.) Jupiter: Frickin kid. Now here I am. I got to take a little bit out of Kid Wonder on Glory 5, and I can’t wait until the next time I see him. It’s a shame I couldn’t get the mask off of him though. I’m starting to think he wears it out of shame. It’s a pity. He could be such a good wrestler, but he wastes his talent and insists on making my life a living hell. One day he will get his due though. And I will be right there waiting. I don’t think most of you know just how bad it was for me in PCW with the way he was to me backstage. I mean, a lot of the times, you can have some fun with some guys … he was just one of them who for some reason, had it out for me. I don’t know if he felt threatened or what, but anytime I’d try to play a joke or something like that … it was like an entirely different side of him. Everyone out there seems to love Kid Wonder, the loveable wrestler who is so good and everything else. No one knows what he was really like though. All I could do at that time, was go with it … ride it out … I was knew to PCW, and I didn’t want to screw things over for myself because of this. I ---- What’s that blinking? (John gets off the couch and walks over to the camera. It’s like an extreme close up of his face. He realizes what the blinking is …) Jupiter: Gavin! (From the kitchen) Gavin: What?! Jupiter: Do we have another battery for this thing kicking around? Gavin: No … you said we wouldn’t need one. Jupiter: Dammit. The battery is about to go. Gavin: Well let’s go get some beer then. You can charge the battery while we’re gone. Jupiter: I guess I’ve got no other choice, eh? Gavin: Nope. Beer. Jupiter: Alri – (The camera cuts out midsentence and leaves us in darkness. What else did John have to say about Kid Wonder? Will he actually reveal the stuff that went on behind closed doors with him and K-Dub? For the answers, we’ll have to wait until the battery is recharged.) To be continued when the battery recharges …
