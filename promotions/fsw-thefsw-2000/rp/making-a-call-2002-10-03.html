Making A Call

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-03-02 ***Things sure have changed in the last month... Francisco and I got in an arguement about what was the best movie of all time... so I said 'Scarface' with Al Pacino and he goes nuts and starts ranting and raving how 'The Godfather' is ten times better .. so I say to him 'Fran Man, you need to stop trippin off all that cid and calm down' so he goes even more nuts and leaves me high and dry at the crib.... Well I didnt think it was nuttin, figured he was just gonna go down to Johnny's and see some naked women dance and squash everything... so a few hours go by and I'm not worried..Hell, I was having too much fun playing Grand Theft Auto 3.. then a few more hours go by and I'm getting a little worried... then nightfall came and I started to wonder if he was really mad enough to just up and leave.....well today I'm gonna go try and find him but first I'm gonna give Five Star a call and see wassup with my contract.. My name is Pablo Diablo. I have not smoked in 3 weeks...and I feel so good that I want to get high.*** (((The scene opens up in a darkly lit room but glows from the flourescent blacklights that are strategically placed throughout the apartment to give it that perfect glow. On the wall hangs a poster of Scarface which reads 'I always tell the truth...even when I lie' in white text which is beaming out at you due to the blacklights effect on the poster... there are two couches in the room, one is big and the other is little.. Pablo is seen sitting on the big one smoking a newport cigarette wearing a Five Star Mike Marvelous T-Shirt.. which on the back reads 'Grand Slam Champion, Now Ain't That Marvelous?' Pablo takes a drag of his cigarette and then picks up the remote control to the T.V. and turns it on...))) (((Televison Blaring))) 'And in other news, more lawsuits have been filed to R & B artist R. Kelly.. Kelly had nothing to say but his lawyer had these words to say 'My Client........ (((Mutes the T.V.))) Pablo Diablo: Dude, we all know Kelly peed on those girls... he might as well admit it and say he was high or something... (((At this time the phone rings that is in front of him on top of an old wooden coffee table .... *Ring...Ring....Ring....Ring....* ... Pablo reahes down and grabs the phone and in one hand answers it while in the other taking another drag of his cigarette...))) Pablo Diablo: Yo! (((Other end of the phone))) 'Yeah Hi, this is Jeff down at Five Star Wrestling. I'm returning your call from earlier today Mr. Orion is too busy to see you this afternoon.' Pablo Diablo: Damn. Well uhhh how about tommorrow? (((Other end of the phone))) ' Nope, tommorrow isn't looking good either....infact the whole week isn't.' Pablo Diablo: You sure were quick to tell me 'No'. Don't you need to check on your computers or something? (((Other end of the phone))) 'I know my job sir and that's what I'm doing.' Pablo Diablo: Okay, okay... Well how about Chip O'Meara? (((Other end of the phone))) 'Nope he is busy too.' Pablo Diablo: Well shit man! (((Other end of the phone))) ' Would you like his voicemail?' Pablo Diablo: Yeah, Sure. (((Voicemail Recording))) 'Hi this is Chip O'Meara with Five Star Wrestling, I'm not in right now but if you leave a voicemail I will get back to you as soon as possible.' (((Voicemail Voiceover))) 'I'll record your message at the tone.' ***BEEEEEEEEEPPPPP*** Pablo Diablo: Yeah hey man this is Pablo Diablo, one half of ATT, I was just wondering when we were gonna get another match coming our way... I'm getting pretty low on cash... so uhmm yeah, hit me back up when you get a chance, the number is [detail withheld]. Thanks. (((Pablo hangs up the phone and turns the T.V. off... he disposes of his cigarette in the ash tray and then looks over at the camera man who has been filming all of this.... the man is some scroungy looking teenager around the age of 19 who is recording everything with a home video camera))) Pablo Diablo: Well as you heard Jimmy, I dont have much cash left.... So I'm gonna have to quit paying you to be my personal camera man. Jimmy: Awww hey man its alright... I like doing it...by the way i got a story to tell ya. Pablo Diablo: Oh yeah? Jimmy: Yeah, see I used to work at this grocery store, you know working cashier and bagging groceries and that kinda stuff.... well one day I went in to check the schedule and I wasnt booked for the week... Well at first I was kinda happy because I had been working overtime the whole past week.. so the week of no work is up and I go in the next week to see if I work and then again I hd a week off...this same thing happend for 2 more weeks until I got the hint....get it??? Pablo Diablo: So your saying that Five Star Wrestling just wants me to wait it out and bag groceries in the meantime? Jimmy: No I'm saying you might want to take the hint that you may have been layed off. Pablo Diablo: You mean fired? Jimmy: For someone who is supposed to be able to remember alot of wrestling moves you sure dont have much brains up there... yes fired. Pablo Diablo: Dude, If I'm fired... I'm so gonna go apply for unemployment (((At that time a knock on the door is heard *Knock, Knock, Knock* Pablo gets up and answers the door to find a Francisco Fumar with a torn shirt stumble in the door))) Jimmy: My camera batteries are going out.... I'm gonn have to recharage.. Francisco Fumar: DUDE YOU AREN'T GONNA BELIEVE IT!!! (((The Camera fades out to static)))
