What's B.D.S.A.A?

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/2/02 History. It can be the thing that makes you snooze behind some fat kid named Ernie while Mr. Pooperusky tells you about the American Indian War or it can be the past of your life. How people remember you. It can eat away at a person sometimes. It did for the man named Big Daveylicious. His FSW history of being a World Heavyweight champion and being the longest reigning the now defunct FSW Intercontinental title made him into a known wrestling star. It got him his PCW contract. But joining came penalties. Through his whole PCW career he was tormented and looked down upon by the roster due to the other FSW wrestlers short term PCW careers. Joining the PCW was the make or break of his career. By joining he figured with his history that he could be the new PCW World Heavyweight champion very soon. Unfortunately, for his sake his time with the PCW was not being the World Heavyweight champion. But being a chauvinistic pig fighting the likes of some PCW Divas that didn’t deserve to be in the ring, joining a money hungry stable which turned on him, a failed relationship with Jaden, a ruined friendship and winning the Extreme title after several months with the company barely put him anywhere. Some wondered why he stayed with them. Perhaps it was to live the type of lifestyle he wanted to live as a superstar or maybe it was his hunger for gold. His Extreme title reign lasted 36 days and nearly beat the PCW’s Extreme title reign of 41 days but was cut off short and had to settle with PCW’s Second Longest Extreme title reign due to the company firing everyone. They rehired him due to his skill being the Extreme Champion. But vacated his title he worked hard for. Not happy in the direction he was going he joined the company that made him who he is the FSW. Now, he fights back in the main event fighting Johnny Raindance not only to win but to set his career back on track. (The scene fades into a control room of a studio. In the control room sits Michael Graves and the producer is looking through the sound proof glass at a man on a stool looking towards the ground in a wrinkly Armani suit, a black goatee, and bleached blonde hair. He holds a paper in one hand and a beer bottle in the other a microphone towards him. Precisely drunk is this FSW legend , Big Daveylicious. Knowing him he probably celebrated on a night on the town when he got his FSW contract.) Producer: Okay, Big D. Were gonna try it once more. Just remember introduce yourself and read what's on the paper in front of you. Still got it? Big Daveylicious: Yeah, yeah, yeah, I got it. Producer:Okay, Big Daveylicious Substance Abuse Awareness Campaign. This is take nine. Big Daveylicious: Hi, this is Big Daveylicious of Miami, Florida. Kids, when you drink and drive... Producer: No, Dave. Cut, cut. It's not when you drink and drive. Big Daveylicious: Well, let me do it again. Producer: Yeah, good idea. Big Daveylicious Substance Abuse Awareness Campaign. This is take ten. Big Daveylicious: Hi, this is Big Daveylicious. When I drink and drive I.... Producer: Cut, Dave, cut! This spot is supposed to be against driving intoxicated. Big Daveylicious: ...What? Producer: Nevermind. Uh...we'll do it one more time. Now, remember say 'Don't do drugs and if you must drink this holiday weekend don't drive. Got it? Big Daveylicious: I told you I got it. Producer: Big Daveylicious Substance Abuse Awareness. This is what?...Take eleven? Big Daveylicious: Hi, I'm Big Daveylicious telling you if you do drugs this weekend don't drive. Producer: Wrong again, Dave! It's don't do drugs! DON'T DO DRUGS! Big Daveylicious: That's what I said. Producer:No, it's not. That's not even close to what you said. Big Daveylicious: You come in this booth and I'll show you how close it was, Sherlock! Producer: Were gonna do it again, Dave. Say 'Don't do drugs. If you must drink this holiday weekend don't drive.' Big Daveylicious: This is Big Daveylicious. Holidays are for drunks. Do drugs. Producer:Uh...thanks, Dave. I think with a little editing it should be just perfect. Big Daveylicious: Editing?! It’s perfect if I do say Mister Wizard of Oz. Can you take me home back to Auntie Em and give my friend a brain. (The producer looks at a snoring Graves lips on the window of the control room. We than zoom into Graves as into his white skin fading to white and than black and blue and maybe even poop.)
