Funk Soul Brothas~!

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, August 19th, 2002 [Scene opens in the appartment of Jacob Jackson. Both Mike and Jacob are sitting on chairs, holding bags of ice on their sore joints after the grueling Fatal Fourway at Genesis. Jacob painfully raises his hand to grab the remote, then slowly brings his thumb down to press the 'Power' button on the t.v. when a *snap* is heard] Jacob Jackson:...Ow....there goes my thumb. [Mike turns his head slowly to face Jacob when his neck also cracks] Mike Marvelous: And there goes my neck... Jacob Jackson: So...sore... Mike Marvelous: We probably wouldn't be so sore if you hadn't of screwed up our game plan at Genesis! [Jacob slowly raises his eyebrows in protest] Jacob Jackson: *I* ruined the gameplan, did I? You're the one who tried to hog the win for yourself! Mike Marvelous: Psssht! WHATEVER! You broke up the pin! Hey, I would have won the title...but at least the title would've been in the hands of the Five Star Five...err...Four. Jacob Jackson: Yes...if it wasn't for Frampton... Mike Marvelous: Yes...Frampton. Damn you Frampton`! He will pay... Jacob Jackson: I know...why don't we just supress our rage for awhile...forget about Genesis...and go out and have some fun? Mike Marvelous: ....Go out? As in...the outside world. Jacob Jackson: Yes, Michael. Let's go...clubbin'! [Scene fades out as both Mike and Jacob get up out of their chairs, crack their backs, and limp out of the scene] --------------------------------------- [When the scene fades back in, we are at Studio 54! The most famous and exclusive night club in the world! Many people are lined up at the front of the club, but only a few get past the hefty doorman holding the velvet rope. Many limos are pulling up with famous people, like Julia Roberts, Tom Cruise, Ice Cub and Justin Timberlake! But among these limos is a small rental car that puts up the the curb, stops, coughs out some exhaust fumes and opens its doors. Out steps GROOVIN' MIKEY MARVELOUS and STYLIN' JAKEY JACKSON, the FUNK SOUL BROTHAS~! Decked out in white disco suits studded with diamonds. Mike has a blue undershirt underneath his white suit, and Jacob has a red suit under his. They both strut up to the line and people look on in silent awe and shock.] Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: Let's go get our groove on, Jakey! [They wade through the line up of slack jawed yokels and make their way up to the front, Jacob elbowing Denzel Washington for his spot at the front] Stylin' Jakey Jackson: Take a walk, Denzel! [The large doorman looks at Mike, then at Jacob, and furrows his brow] Doorman: Uuuh...who the hell are you two? [Mike and Jacob immediately turn around to reveal their names spelled out in the back of their suits in diamond studs. Mike has 'MIKEY' spelled out on the back of his suit, and Jacob has 'JAKEY' spelled out in the back of his. The doorman just looks at them and shakes his head] Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: Does that help at all, crome dome? Stylin' Jakey Jackson: Don'tcha know a...CEL-EB-RET-AY~! when ya see one, walrus face? [The doorman looks down the list on the clipboard in his hand] Doorman: I don't see your names on the list.... Groovin' Mike Marvelous: That's cause you're blind, Daddio. Lemme see that list for a second. [The doorman reluctantly hands over the list, and in a move of sheer genius, Jacob throws up on the doorman's shirt, distracting him long enough for Mike to take out a ball point pen and write their names in the vip section of the list] Doorman: UGH! SICK! Stylin' Jakey Jackson: Oops...sorry...I've got the flu from having too much SEX! Doorman: Why don't I believe you... [Mikey thrusts the list into the doorman's face] Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: Hey, daddio...you missed our names. They're in the V-I-P section! As in, Very Important PartAY~! animals! [The doorman let's out a long sigh and unhooks the velvet rope. The funk soul brothers slap five and walk inside. When they get inside the club, they look around at all the people, standing around, moving to the music. The room is completely dark except for the strobe lights and lasers flashing around while the rave-like music plays. Mikey and Jakey strut inside, scoping out the scene. People begin to stop dancing and cast glares at the two losers] Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: This is my kinda place, BRA! Stylin' Jakey Jackson: You got that right, BRA! Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: BRA! Stylin' Jakey Jackson: BRAAA! [They both slap five, give props and wiggle their fingers in some sort of secret handshake, then walk up to a pretty lady dressed in a tight red dress. Mike walks in front of her while Jacob goes behind her] Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: Hey baby...did you know that I'm alot like Fred Flintstone? Lady: Uuuh...is it because you both have no necks? Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: Naaaw! It's cause I can make yo' BED-ROCK! Get it? [Jacob breaks out in laughter behind her and the two friends slap five as the lady rolls her eyes in disgust] Lady: How the hell did you two losers get in here? Stylin' Jacob Jackson: We own this joint, baby! Lady: Yeah right! Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: How's aboot we cut some rug, bab-ay? Lady: How bout you go fuck yourselves. [Mikey and Jakey begin dancing with her anyway, and catch her between a duel pelvin thrust move that knocks her back and forth between them until she slaps Mike in the face and swings her foot backwards, nailing Jacob in the groin.] Lady: Weirdos [Jacob falls to his knees and Mike holds his face as she walks away] Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: What'd we do wrong, BRA~? Stylin' Jakey Jackson: [Through clenched teeth] Whatdoya mean, dood? She wants us! Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: Let's go find some action! [The Funk Soul Brothas groove their way through the crowd, picking out various ladies to dance with, but they all reject them. Suddenly, Mikey gets an idea] Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: Jigga! I got a idea! Follow me! [Mikey hops up onto the stage and Jakey follows. The DJ stops playing his rave music and looks at the two men oddly until he is thrown off the stage. Mike grabs a microphone and hands one to Jacob. Mike walks over and whispers something in Jacob's ear. The crowd murmers in mass protest] Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: Ladies and Gents! Me and my friend Jakey here are gonna treat YOU, to a hip tune for the hippest club in the world! Let's drop some beats on these suckas, Jakey! [Jacob starts speaking in a very deep and scratchy voice] Stylin' Jakey Jackson: OOGA CHACKA OOGA OOGA! OOGA CHACKA OOGA OOGA! OOGA CHACKA OOGA OOGA! OOGA CHACKA OOGA OOGA! [Mike suddenly breaks into song as he holds the microphone up to his lips. The crowd boos. Jacob continues the OOGA CHACKA backround singing as Mike sings] Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: I can't stop this feee-lin'! Deep insii-iide of me! Giiirl you just don't reeeal-iize....whatcha do to me....When ya hold me....in your arms so tight...you let me know....everything's alriiight....IIIII-IIIII-IIII'M....HOOKED ON A FEELIN'! I'M HOT ON BEL-IEEEE-- [Suddenly, Mike is grabbed by the scruff of the neck by a bouncer and pulled off the stage, followed by Jacob. Both of them kick and scream and they're hauled out of the club. People watch in shock as this is all going on] Stylin' Jakey Jackson: YOU CAN'T DO THIS TO US! WE'RE WRESTLERS! Groovin' Mikey Marvelous: WE'LL BE BACK, LOSERS! YOU CAN COUNT ON IT! MWAHAHAHAHA...BWAHAHAHAHA...AAHA--uuugh! ggggh! [Mike stops laughing as one of the bouncers hits him with a forearm to the throat, crushing his larynx. Both men are thrown out of the club on their asses and the people in the crowd look at each other...then] Crowd: WHOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! [Everyone applaudes, the music starts up, and everything returns to normal. The night of the Funk Soul Brothas has come to an abrupt end. Fade to black]
