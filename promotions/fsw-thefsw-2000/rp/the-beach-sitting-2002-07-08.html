The Beach Sitting

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 8 It had been a windy day. Just a bit cool to satisfy as a good beach day. Yet I made my way to the ocean side, regardless of the weather condition. I had some thinking to do. I had to gather some thoughts again. It had been months since I'd thought to be extremely competitive. I'd had to again build myself emotionally as well as physically for a killer adventure, to say the least. 'You don't plan to go swimming, I hope.' She was always so concerned about me and my well being. My dear Rebecca, always wondering what went through my mind. Yet she was still so young, and niave. Telling her of my inner thoughts and troubles would have brought more gloom to her life. I most certainly did not want that. 'Oh, but I do, Rebecca!' I absolutely loved to play games with her. I found it adorable to see her care and concern when I told her of some crazy escapade that I had planned. 'Why else would I come to the beach, sweetheart?' 'Chris, you're absolutely absurd! Its gotta be like 65 degress out here!' 'Sixty-two, actually.' 'Yeah! And thats the air alone. The breeze drops that a bit. Then the ocean temperature on top of that.' I could never get over how hard she always tried to keep me doing well. Even during the gloomy days. The hard times. My Rebecca was always right there by my side. 'Aww, darling.' I stepped closer to her and grabbed her head with my right hand, running my fingers through her soft hair. I admired every little strand that my fingers touched. 'I'm just fooling around. It is far too cold for my tastes!' 'Good!' 'Oh good, is it?' 'Well, you are always looking out for me and I always end up complying. So now the tables are turned and I am doing the exact same for you!' In her own little ways, she always found how to be right. The tables were in fact turned in this particular situation. With no idea how to reply in words, I let my actions speak. I pulled her close and placed a gentle kiss on her fore head. A smile came to her face and she laid into my arms. After what felt like lifetimes, but were mere minutes of her in my arms, she decided to break the barrier of silence in which had been created with my initial kiss. 'The house on the distant island,' she murmered. I titled my head and looked across the bay, taking into sight the home in which she spoke of. Practically in the middle of nowhere stood a house on a little island in the midsts of just water. How did it get there? Who owns it? Why would anyone actually wish to leave so secluded from everything? Those questions not only lurked in my mind, but the minds of the surrounding townspeople, as well. 'What about it?' I questioned. Rebecca sort of pushed me away, but unintentionally. She wanted to take a better look at the island. And as her eyes locked into the house, she seemed to have lost herself in it. It was as if a whole new spirit had entered her body and spoke rhythmic words for her. She began, 'Alone lives that elderly lady.' 'Through the height of day...' 'And the depth of night...' A sudden scare had posessed me. But with a few blinks of the eye, it vanished and my mind practically went blank. Rebecca's mind, however, still dwelled on that secluded home. She continued, 'Alone lives that elderly lady.' 'Through the best of her content...' 'And the worst of her fright.' Then, it was as if the spirit in which posessed her had left. Rebecca fell back into my arms, smothering her face from being seen. I didn't say anything at first, for the simple reason fact that i had really nothing to say. But I couldn't stand there in silence. I had to find out what that whole episode was all about. So I came right out and asked her, 'Are you alright, Rebecca.' 'Yes. Fine,' she replied sharply. 'What is this all about?' 'What is what about?' She acted as if she had no clue what I was talking about. But she seems to forget that I know her very well. I can always tell when something sits on her mind. 'Don't play stupid. I know there is something on your mind.' 'You don't tell me everything thats on your mind!' She got me. I was speechless for a moment, because she was very right. I don't tell her whats always on my mind. I had to think of some way out. 'Don't play games Rebecca. Sometimes, even I don't know whats on my mind. You know as well as I that I'm mentally crazy.' Rebecca thought for a little bit, nodding her head to what I had just said to her. 'I'm just worried, Chris. Thats all.' Before even giving me the opportunity to speak, she answered my question. 'I'm worried about this whole Five Star ordeal. I just don't know about it.' I knew this was coming. I knew she'd give me the pity story about getting back into the business again. 'Rebecca, you saw me the other day. I was on fire.' 'And you were also assaulted afterwards. Chris, things like that will take you down.' 'But I'll get my revenge sooner or later. Its just a matter of time before Tyler gets whats coming to him.' She still persisted, 'But what about Leon? You have to climb past Leon first. And while you're trying to fend him off, Tyler could come and ruin things.' A sudden crash of thunder rolls through the skies and is followed by a streak of lightning. I quickly said, 'Leon will not be a problem at all.' I took her hand and we scurried to the car as the rain began to pour down. It struck to the ground like Leon Chamber's shoulders will strike to the mat.
