Beginning of a New Legend

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/30/02 ~Prologue~ Time can often have a funny effect on people. Throughout the course of time all that is around you is in a constant state of change and usually everything within a person is changing through time. In the life and times of Tyler Davidson that has become quite evident. To put things into perspective perhaps it is best for us to take a step back and look to the past to enlighten those who are unsure of the past events which have led us to the present reality. ~Begin Flashback~ You again see Tyler as he rolls over in the bed. The sun now shining in the sky comes through the curtains of the hotel room and shine into his eyes but the thing that wakes him is the ringing of his cell phone at the side of the bed on an end table. Tyler slowly wakes from his slumber and tosses an arm over to the table as he fumbles around for the phone before finally getting a hand on it and bringing it to his ear and speaking. ”Hello?” he muttered into the speaker barely able to open his eyes yet. He attempts to push himself up out of the bed but with little strength behind it as a voice comes back over the phone. ”T-ty?” a female voice said. At first sound it could have been mistaken for his girlfriend Heather though the sounds of sobs and tears from her makes it quite difficult to tell exactly who it was. “Yeah, this is him. Is that you Jess?” he asked suddenly coming to his senses with a bit of alarm. “Y-y-yes it is,” she slowly answered him. She was obviously quite distressed but what it was about he had no idea. Fearing the worse though, he finally managed to answer back too her. ”What’s wrong? Did you here something about Dan?” he asked with his alarm increasing further. At first he heard nothing but silence and the sound of Jess crying before she would answer again. “N-no I haven’t,” she eventually answered. “It’s about Heather, Tyler.” “What about her?” he asked unsure of what the problem could be. ”I’m so sorry,” was the only response she would give at first. “Jess, what happened?” he asked again feeling an even greater sense of urgency. “I’m sorry Tyler but….” Jess began before breaking off again in tears before being able to finish the sentence. “She’s dead.” The phone falls from Tyler’s hand and comes crashing down onto the ground below him. Tyler in a state of complete shock sitting at the edge of the bed slowly slides off onto the floor his head in his hands as the tears slowly become to come forth and the only sounds left is the voice of Jess on the phone screaming for Tyler to answer her and the sounds of his own crying as the scene fades to black. ~End Flashback~ What would you do if the most important thing in your life was suddenly and violently taken from you? That is the question which for two months now Tyler Davidson has had to live with. That is the question which Tyler Davidson must somehow find an answer to. It is evident that thus far the answer has not been to the liking of the fans who once praised Tyler for his abilities in the ring and his character as well. As things always do over the course of time however Tyler had become a changed man. The question then becomes was it the events of his life which had changed him or has Tyler always had this darkness building inside of him? The truth of the answer can oftentimes be one of the most frightening realities a man is forced to face. The future can indeed look quite dark from a certain point of view. Whether that is Tyler’s or not remains to be seen. ~The Beginning~ All you can see around you is the darkness of a stormy night in the Connecticut area. Bolts and flashes of lightning light the nighttime sky as the sounds of thunder rock the nearby houses. As the winds blows through the trees lining the area, another flash of lightning lights the area well enough for you to see the figure of a man standing out in the downpour of rain. Through the flashes of lightning you can see the figure of the man as he rushes through the area before coming up to a large mansion door. He grabs hold of the wooden door and turning the knob makes his way into the building and out of the downpour of rain. Immediately upon his entering the building the man, a young one with black hair drenched from the rain falling down in front of his face and a black goatee, is approached by an older man with short graying hair and clean shaven. “It’s about time you came in from the rain Tyler,” the old man began as he looked Tyler up and down noticing the puddle of water forming at his feet. “You’re dripping water all over the place. Get your ass into the bathroom and dry yourself off.” “Yeah, yeah I know pops,” Tyler replied as he walks by his father, Hunter, and into a side room of the hallway. After a few minutes he eventually reemerges and joins his father in the living room. Tyler crashes down onto the nearby couch across from his father and looks at the television to see the latest highlights from the New York Mets-New York Yankees baseball action. As Tyler becomes seemingly entranced with the highlights his father looks over at him. “How was your short little trip over to Canada?” his father asked trying to start some conversation. “It was Canada,” Tyler answered him. “How do you expect it to be?” “I thought it might be a good experience for you,” his father replied. “It’s always good to get some international flavor once in a while.” “Oh yeah,” Tyler agreed sarcastically. “It was tons of fun. I went in and did what I had to do once again.” “Nothing new there obviously,” Hunter said looking away from his son and to the television which was now at commercial. “What’s next for you then?” “I have to head back to New York in a few days,” Tyler answered. “I have some more business to take care of there at the office.” “Any more big business deals going down?” his father asked curiously. “Yeah there is actually one Dan and I must finish up,” Tyler told him. “It’s not something that can be talked about yet though.” “Of course,” his father replied. “What’s next in the wrestling world?” “It’s just about that time for Five Star Wrestling to begin finally,” Tyler answered. “I’ve been waiting weeks for this one to start and it’s almost here now. It’s time for me to once again make myself known throughout the world. This one is without a doubt going to be the biggest challenge of my career but I have no intentions of failing here.” Tyler turns his attention away from his father again and back towards the television. He sits there staring intently for a few moments as more sports highlights are being played over on ESPN Sportscenter. His concentration is broken again however by the sounds of his father’s voice breaking through the silence. “Things aren’t going to be quite that easy Tyler,” his father began admonishing him. “You probably don’t even know who your opponent is.” “Au contraire,” Tyler responded full of sarcasm. “I actually do know who my opponent is. I just don’t know who the hell he is. That’s what Dan is around for anyway. He’s my manager and is off doing manager like work now.” “Of course,” his father replied quietly. “I should have known. You’ll have to learn things the hard way again.” “That’s always been the way of my life,” Tyler said softly turning to look out a nearby window. “We already know how badly things can go which is why I’m trying now to just move on. Five Star will just be the first step in that process. Whoever this Leon Chambers guy just found himself a very unlucky break. He has to face a very uncaring and pissed off Tyler Davidson and that equals one very long night for him. Speaking of long nights though it’s going to be a long one for me if I don’t get some sleep now.” “Have a good night Ty,” his father called after him as Tyler quickly got to his feet and dashed from the room. The scene slowly fades to black one last time as Tyler disappears up the staircase.
