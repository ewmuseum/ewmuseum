The Fracturing Reality – Deceit in the Stars

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Excerpted from the future memoirs of Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan) Every force present in the universe has an opposition. These are personified in such concepts as man and nature, black and white, reality and illusion, love and hate. For one to exist, so must the other; it is the ever-shifting balance we call yin and yang, the eternal equilibrium maintaining proper order in all things. Without good, there is no evil. And without the existence of darkness, light cannot subsist. But sometimes, even between the most polar of concepts, there lingers an indefinable boundary separating the defined and the undefined. Mankind has developed theories around many of these inscrutable models, using scientific laws and mathematical formulas designed to delve these cosmic notions and solve the jumbled chaos that is the puzzle of creation. Even life and death are undefined, yet clearly contradictory states. Where does life begin? Where does it end? Abortion is a primary forum for this dispute, as is the case of acquiring the right of ‘pulling the plug’ on loved ones trapped in hopeless, comatose existence. Mankind has yet to fashion a formula outlining the moment that life officially blooms; likewise for the concept of when existence evolves into casualty. There are vistas of knowledge that humanity is simply not meant to scrutinize or fathom, and such is the method of the greater cosmos. To truly probe the great chasms of nethermost space is something God never intended. Insanity is the safety valve that He installed as a fail-safe for such insubordination. How do I know this? God told me. Or, more specifically the Gods told me. I can already decipher the expression on your face as you read these pages. Bah! You think me for mad, just as the fools who sent me to that rat-infested asylum do! As I have already stated, the human mind simply can’t comprehend such things as I have seen. You think me mad, however I am nothing but sane. There are a select few who glance into those darkness-tinged landscapes and the charnel pits where the sane and rational rightfully cease their exploration, and to emerge psychologically intact from those profound truths is by no means a feat worth scoffing at. I am one of the few to grasp what lies beyond the margins of conventional human lore, and those painfully expurgated tomes that mankind hides behind lock-and-key cannot even begin to trace what lies beyond traditional understanding. But still I fear I am no longer among the sane whom have done so. I held out for many months through being constantly bombarded with revelations of the End Times and a bird’s-eye view of the desolate wasteland that will one day be the Earth, purged of the plague called man. I was forced to read the pages of cumbersome tomes detailing what little is known of the universe’s greatest anomalies. But in the end, no man can withstand their bidding for long. A man cannot overcome a deity. By decree of the Old Ones I have peered into the gulfs of breath-taking antiquity and had bestowed on my limited mind the vastness of celestial wisdom. This too happened to the one known as the ‘Mad Arab’ Abdul Al-Hazred, philosopher, magician and author of the coveted and arcane Necronomicon. Though while Al-Hazred is purported to have been devoured in broad daylight by an invisible demon for his revelations, I have attained the favor of those antediluvian lords. And in turn, they have blessed me with exquisite scenes of what lies beyond what we know and willingly accept for truth. Breath-taking antiquity versus maddening blasphemy…..two facets as parallel as the association of life versus death. And like many of the aforementioned concepts, humankind has no law nor formula to properly dissipate the mysterious line separating these concepts. Nor should they take the time to attempt to distinguish the elements; to do so will all but guarantee an epidemic of mania and insanity as mankind plunges to its own doom. I have persevered through a lifetime of oppression and persecution where I was looked down upon as both a beggar and a menial vagabond, where strife and discord counted among the daily routine and to trust was not virtue but vice. To make ends meet and persist for another day, I turned to a life where trophies were scars and a good night was a flimsy roof over my head with less than a dozen leaks. My friends were mercilessly gunned down in cold blood over petty disputes while I prayed on my knees endlessly beneath darkened, rain-filled skies lit by nothing but pale street lamps. Only by destroying my own body in combat was there the token chance for survival; to be surrounded by a perimeter of barbedwire, shards of razor-edged glass and the lapping tongues of orange flame was both my purgatory and my source of salvation. Then, condemned for the sins of the most notorious of my bloodline, my mind is assaulted by the onslaught of a hundred infuriated gods spawned in the distant nebulas. They played off my fears and my frailties, manipulating my will as one would direct a pawn in a chess game. Now give me a suitable reason to fear the wrath of Kimbusa. I am loathe to entertain that foreigner’s embellished ideas of sorrow and anger. What does an unenlightened mortal truly know of internal wars within the self, of torturous demons residing within the uncharted crevices of the human condition? They are even unable to grasp the idea of parallel planes and the nature of the shadow-clouded wyrm-holes resting in the void of space, how can such concepts not evade their scrutiny? I shall honor the sentiment of one Kimbusa, the misbegotten oddity of Five-Star Wrestling, and concur with his claims of frightfully capacious anger inhabiting his psyche. I suppose the unfortunate sap deserves some restitution. Any apprehension I had about this impending confrontation has been alleviated by the Masters. Even his Eminence, Nyarlathotep, has seen suitable to take personal interest in the exploits of I, his most recent and treasured Earthly agent. So many yet-undenounced curiosities that had swirled about in my mind were put to rest by the Masters upon the Conversion: my inquiries about ‘the mark’, the Crawling Chaos so easily relinquishing his embrace on my soul only weeks ago following the now infamous Tropical Massacre, an obscure interpretation of the cryptic prophecy uttered by the winged Byakhee-Lord in my dreams and the putrid fetus it guarded from atop its shadow-veiled perch. All has been revealed to me. And armed with this new knowledge, I go forth back into the society I despise as an unstoppable force of both mind and body. PCW shall feel my wrath, Kimbusa shall suffer the pain, and eventually society shall grovel before their rightful rulers from beyond the stars with yours truly at the pulpit of their reverence! Glory Be Thy Name! Ia Shub-Nuggurath!! Now you probably ask yourself how I can claim to be sane, and yet moments later claim the contrary. If you, reader, have absorbed any of the information scrawled on these pages, you would have no need to make such analysis. Just as life and death are separated by a fine line still unplumbed by the understanding of this infantile species, so is the border between the sane and the insane. Our universe is a most deceptive master, and it is all a matter of interpretation. I know the punch-line to the eternal riddle. I merely put it in terms your simple mind can understand. * * * Captain Marcus Stryker, commander of the elite Delta Fox unit and operative in the enigmatic underground brotherhood known as the Blackhelm Coalition, nodded with elation at the fine Arial type on the newsletter he clutched in his hands. Jamming the crinkled pamphlet into the hands of Ryder Slade, Stryker let out an ecstatic cheer and slid energetically into the den to grab his coat. Primetime Central/Wrestling Tattler Organization Continues Golden Turnbuckle Tournament; Inphino Blitz, The End, Violence Jack, Angelo Deville Rank Among Winners In Sweet 16 Round The brawny, round-faced Slade hoisted himself to a standing position, wincing minutely as a surge of nerve-wracking pain shot through his still injured left arm. He grinned broadly, looking down on the comparatively-dwarven Stryker from the peak of his near 7-foot frame. “Ole’ Bruce has done it, my man!” exclaimed Marcus with a fervent nod as he snatched up the creased bulletin, skimming it yet again with his vigorous green eyes. “I just hope those bastards from PCW are right....I hope he relieves some stress in that ring. I don’t wanna see the guy recommitted and spend the rest of his life in a padded room in Greensburgh Sanitarium.” Slade lowered his eyebrows as he observed the discontent dotting the expression of Stryker, could almost see the pain contorted in his commander’s face and muscles. The youthful officer blamed himself for the madness afflicting their ally, and refused to do anything other than accept responsibility for his psychosis. Bruce had forged alone into the heart of Miskatonic University to liberate the captain from the clutches of the corrupt High Council of Elders, fleeing from an imperceptible horde of spectral hunters lurking across the central grounds as darkling sentinels and subsequently from the vile, brooding mass of an archaic shoggoth guardian. All of this Stryker had confided in his team along with the warning that disclosure of the information could initiate the apocalypse; regardless of the knowledge they held, to reveal such a tale to their superiors would result in worldwide Gehenna and the annihilation of all they knew. Officially to their cohorts came the falsified admission that the Elders were simply victims of misfortune and coincidence, though it pained them to tell such a lie. Only days after the daring rescue, Bruce had gone insane. He was committed to Greensburgh County Sanitarium after being found gibbering madly in monstrous tongues unknown to all but the most studied of occultists, his breakdown reportedly caused by recent defeat with his own veritable phobia of failure triggering the neurotic reaction. Since then, Bruce had been locked away in that loathsome asylum, shrieking maniacally at intervals in the night and claiming repeated visitations by “that damned Black Pharaoh from the abyss”. Delta Fox was saddened by their new comrade’s mental collapse and had visited him on a pair of occasions, each time met with only glazed eyes and a perpetual catatonic appearance. Bruce appeared lost, another casualty of the dark forces outside most of mankind’s perception. In a drastic turn of events and a nearly unprecedented change of heart, those charged with Shanahan’s care had inexplicably proclaimed an end to his psychological regression and issued papers for his release. Such was declared only hours after his defeat of Humane, and the native of Boston, Massachusetts now waited patiently for pickup from the sanitarium. PCW’s collective management itself even seemed slightly taken aback at the abrupt decision but agreed with their experts and endorsed Bruce’s release. Captain Stryker and his squad, given news of the action immediately by inside operatives of the Coalition, took on the task of picking up Shanahan from the asylum and they now prepared to take their rental car from the Roadside Inn to the sanitarium only miles down the road. “C’mon!” shouted Stryker, beckoning his burly counterpart. “Let’s get our boy out of that hole!” Slade, the resident giant of the Delta Fox force, adjusted the sling still supporting his healing limb and threw on a sea-green Miami Dolphins cap. The door to their stodgy room whipped shut, prompting the towering soldier to draw it back again with a mellow creak before shambling down the stretching corridor after his anxious commander. * * * The silver Optima rumbled over the crunching gravel into the vacant parking lot of the sanitarium, coming to a halt only inches before a chain-link fence covered beneath a thin film of rust. The foreboding structure had the look of an abnormally colossal Victorian mansion converted from the late 1700’s, braced on the front stoop by circular pillars and walls overgrown with vines. It was like a shadow taken from the vaults of time, altered from its original purpose on the old-time plantations and placed in the modern world to serve as a sanctuary for the mad. Stryker slipped out of the door and into the open sunlight, squinting as he raised a flattened palm to shield his gaze from the blinding rays of the glaring sun. He discerned two shapes silhouetted against the overwhelming radiance of the background, one standing upright and the other in a prone, sitting posture. Both figures wavered in the haze of the rising heat ascending from the walkway in a blanket of swaying, vertical waves. Slade climbed from the vehicle and up the walk, a grin widening across his face as the figure of Bruce Shanahan, PCW’s ‘Violence Jack’, arose from the rocking chair on the outside terrace. Clad in the same sweat-drenched attire he had been wearing while being dragged screaming over the threshold of the sanitarium, Bruce proffered a weak smile to his comrades. At his side, Dr. Okoye remained stoic and didn’t move. Slade shook the hand of Shanahan before glancing awkwardly at the doctor. “Bruce! Thank the Lord, I was afraid it was all over for you!” Stryker pounced up the stairs and likewise took the hand of his companion, offering a firm grip and shaking ardently. Bruce’s smile curled up wider as Marcus released his grip. “I was gettin’ scared I was goin’ to end up starting at white rubber walls the rest of my days,” he cracked. Stryker looked at the doctor, who retained a peculiar somberness and was yet to move since they had pulled into the parking lot. There was a curious nature surrounding the taciturn physician, an aura almost sinister. “So…..where are the papers?” inquired Stryker, running a hand through his shortly-cropped blond hair. Okoye elevated an eyebrow as if puzzled. “Papers, sir?” “The papers we need to sign to get Bruce outta here, doctor. Don’t we need to sign some documents for his release? I was informed by his and your employer that I had to put my John Hancock to a few things before he could go…..” “No, not necessary,” replied Okoye, for the first time changing his expression to that of a mild beam. “He has already been cleared. He may leave right now.” Stryker picked up on the same suspicion as his mammoth subordinate, the flat and almost lifeless ambiance of the doctor’s voice catching his attention. He backed up a step, resting a hand on the shoulder of his freed ally. “Ah good,” he said with a meager grin. “Then I guess we’ll be going. Bruce you didn’t have any other….” “No, he brought nothing with him,” noted the physician quietly. “He came without any supplies or foreign items.” Stryker abruptly nodded as Bruce stalked off the terrace and toward the waiting car. Slade cast a final dubious glimpse at Okoye before treading after the captain, his instincts alerted just as his superior’s were by the weird stench wafting from the body of the doctor. Bruce shot one final, odd look back before lifting his bulk into the backseat and settling in. * * * None noticed the sickly yellow pus dripping sporadically from the outstretched fingers of Doctor Samsanov Okoye, nor the curious menacing smirk tainting his lips as the trio strolled down the weed-infested sidewalk to the parking lot. And they surely took no notice of the inhuman rumble that emanated from his throat in the form of a parodied snicker. In the storage room of Greensburgh Sanitarium, mangled in a ghastly bulk of putrid decay, lay a vaguely human form. A tarnished nametag adorned the left breast of the bloodied white coat. Mottled and stained, the lettering was hard to decipher and only certain letters could be distinguished. D—CT-R S-MS—OV -K-Y-
