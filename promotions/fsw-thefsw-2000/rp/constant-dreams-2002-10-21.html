Constant Dreams...

21 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/21/02 I cried as I heard his hand strike her. I buried my face into my pillow. 'No, please stop! I’m sorry.' I can’t take it anymore. I run out into the living room to see her on the floor. 'Go back to bed' She cries as he glares at me. My eyes widen and I run crying into my room. I hear her screams and the sound of his fists hitting her. Again, he’s angry. I lean my back against the white door, and freeze as the sound of his beating stops. Silence. I panic as his footsteps near my door. I scream as he forces it open. I begin to fall, but he catches me. He then slaps me. 'Why?' I ask, and he doesn’t respond. The smell of alcohol and sweat makes me sick. His brown eyes were so intense. I could do nothing but cry as he beats me. As the time passes, he kicks me and shoves me in the closet. I sit there and cry. 'Why? Why… ' I manage to stumble into the living room. Her body lay lifeless; blood pooling beneath her. The hard wood floor looks almost beautiful…I cry harder. 'Mother? Mother?!' I hug her cold body as he comes in. Beer bottle in hand, he looks at me. Tears steamed down my face. 'Leave me alone!' I scream as he comes near. I turn to run, but feel the glass hit the back of my neck. He stands over me as things go dark. 'No….' I awake in the small hospital room. My mind is clouded. 'We caught him.' It doesn’t matter. That doesn’t bring my Mother back. Before I realized what’d happened, I was being taken away. 'Welcome to your new family.' They aren’t my family. 'Please, we love you. Doesn’t mean I love you. 'Tell me what you’re feeling.' You’ll never understand. I scream and slam the door. School, friends, life. 'It’s too hard. I can’t take it anymore.' I bite my lip as a sickening thought comes to mind. The way of a coward. I tried to stop my mind of thinking about it. I was going to break. I made a hard decision. Knife, gun or pill. I took the man’s gun and sat in my room. I cried once again. 'I’m sorry…' I put the gun to my head. I closed my eyes. Click...Click...Click... No Bullets...No meaning....no point. ShowTime is tossing and turning in his bed when he sits up sweating frantically and feeling his face. '...Just a dream...' ShowTime says.... Scene Fades...
