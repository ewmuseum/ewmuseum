GTT · Knocking on Heaven's Door

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: whoo~! 'So can you help me or not?' Angelo's voice is quivering on the edge of impatience, reflecting the stress which overcomes him when he must be diplomatic for too long. He leans back into the plush leather La-z-boy, stretching to release the tension swelling his muscles. For the better part of half an hour he's been sitting here trying to explain the same thing over and over again in different ways. If he doesn't understand yet, I swear to fucking God... Angelo lets the unspoken threat live through his eyes, which flash dangerously as the man sitting across the desk smiles and begins nodding slowly. The man is Father O'Brien, a priest in a small town outside of Vancouver. In his early forties, O'Brien has paid attention to staying healthy all of his adult life, as is evidenced by his sleek and vibrant face. Grey eyes sip longingly at the world from beneath a close-cropped mop of red hair, and the corners of a thin mouth twitch subtly but uncontrollably, as if every second he is reliving a fond memory. The eyes are closed now, as they always are when he is deep in thought. As the moment drags on Angelo heaves an exasperated sigh an studies the room for the twelfth time. The room is small but very quaint, with tasteful furniture and an underlying scent of lavender. Aside from the two leather chairs holding the pair, the room is empty save for a desk, a filing cabinet, and a bookcase. Pictures of Jesus adorn every spare inch of wall, as if the room is to act as a shrine. 'The whole world is,' is all O'Brien would say when Deville asked him about it. 'Look pal,' Angelo huffs. 'There's nothing to ponder here. Let's just cut to the chase... Where can I find The Disciples?' The old man's eyebrows raise at the direct question, but that is the only signal he gives at having heard anything for a very long moment. Then his eyes suddenly snap open and regard Deville with a newfound suspicion. 'I can't say that I know what you're talking about, Mr. Deville,' O'Brien responds evenly with a dismissing wave of his hand. 'Stop wasting my time, old man... I need to know about these Disciples right away,' Angelo pauses and the old man shrugs apologetically. 'I haven't been able to find a single clue all week, except that the object of my search lives nearby.' 'Pardon me, Mr. Deville, but how can I say this?' O'Brien humours his rhetoric for a moment, then answers. 'You're not, ah, speaking my language,' he drones on as his eyes take on the anticipatory glow of someone who plays these games often. Deville stares at O'Brien for a moment, then rolls his eyes as the simplicity of the situation unfolds itself to him. He raises his left index finger as he reaches inside his jacket with his right hand, pulling it out moments later with a clipped wad of cash. O'Brien's eyes go wider and almost pulse with greed. Peeling a few bills from the top, Deville lets them flutter to the desktop and rises to his feet. He leans over the desk and locks eyes with the priest. 'Now how can I find Matt Blitz and the Disciples?' Angelo demands resignedly, wishing he had known the path to the answers from the get-go. He supposes he should have thought of money and bribery, this being the House of the Lord and all, but it didn't cross his mind. 'You can't,' O'Brien answers curtly, then snatches the money from the desk. Angelo immediately snaps his eyes to the man's hand, but the money has already disappeared somewhere. 'Why not?' Angelo asks with a furrow in his brow and a pursing of his lips. The priest's eyes shift from Deville's to the money, then back to Deville's. Sighing, Deville plops another couple of hundreds on the table and O'Brien's mouth magically begins moving again. 'No one can,' he says mysteriously, and stares across the table. Angelo is about to snap when he finally continues, 'No one knows where they are... One in the know must arrange an appointment.' The words trail off slowly as the priest likely has second thoughts about selling his information so cheaply. 'Would you be such a one?' Angelo asks, exasperated. 'Maybe,' the man replies with another meaningful glance towards the money. O'Brien jumps back as Deville's fist slams the table just a few inches before his face. 'That's it!' Angelo shouts, tired of pussy-footing around. 'Obviously, you can contact these guys. I'll give you everything I have right here if you set an appointment with Matt Blitz for sometime tomorrow, and double it again after the meeting is through.' O'Brien licks his lips fiendishly, as if he can taste the money on them. 'I think that might be arranged, though a more sizable donation would be appreciated...' 'This is more than enough,' Angelo growls through gritted teeth, though he is pleasantly surprised by the degree of the man's greed. Just make sure Blitz is at this address tomorrow afternoon,' Angelo admonishes as he retrieves a paper from the desk and scrawls an address on it. The priest looks down and nods solemnly. 'I cannot guarantee Matt Blitz, but one of the Children will be there,' O'Brien rises slowly to his feet, indicating the meeting is at an end, and audibly clears his throat as he shoots another hungry gaze at the cash. Angelo tosses the money down on the desk and turns on his heel. As he stalks through the doorway he mumbles only half to himself, 'Tomorrow's the last chance of meeting before the match. That bible thumper better be there... All this waiting is pissing me off.'
