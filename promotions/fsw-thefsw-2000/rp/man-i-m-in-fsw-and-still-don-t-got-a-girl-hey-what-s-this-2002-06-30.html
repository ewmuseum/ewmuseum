Man, I'm in FSW and still don't got a girl...HEY! What's this?

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 30th, 2002 ~Can you say “boo yah”? Asa sure can. The last ten months of his life have been nothing short of insane. Graduating PWA, spending a few months in the great fed known as NTICW, and moving onto probably the greatest fed on this earth, PCW. Retiring, returning, retiring, returning. A repetitive process that has gained Asa a reputation of being unreliable, which came back to bite him in the ass when he wasn’t re-hired by PCW. However, things are finally looking up, because Asa has just been hired by a new great federation in the WT family. That federation, is Five Star Wrestling…~ ~The scene opens in the city of Boston Massachusetts, around 9:35 PM. The city bustles around, just like any other day. People heading home after a long day of work, and taxis picking up people to bring them to their destinations. Today seems to be a particular gloomy night for the people of Boston, even on a nice night like this, but it is not so gloomy, for Asa “Fro” Fountain.~ ~We see Asa dancing down a sidewalk of this great city. No really, he’s actually dancing. Asa is discoing in happiness, shuffling his feet, pointing the finger, and spinning all the way, singing to himself a little tune.~ Asa: *In musical voice* “Oh yeah, still got it. Joinin’ the FSW, still got the jive. Doop doop be doop… ~Asa continues his…singing as he dances down the street. Naturally, he gets some interesting looks from people, but Asa doesn’t care. He eventually comes the street, and stops at the crosswalk. He jumps to the curb, and takes a deep breath, making his five inch afro shake a bit. He smiles all around as people look at him funny. He just gives them a wave.~ Asa: “This calls for a celebration!” ~Of course, he is talking about celebrating over the fact that he is in FSW. Across the street, sits a tiny building. The building is purple in color, with “Gavin’s Jive Hall” written in bright yellow letters. The crosswalk light turns to “walk”, and Asa dances across the street. He knocks on the purple door of the Jive Hall, and a little slit opens in the door, and two white eyes peer out, and speaks in a scratchy voice.~ Person behind door: “Who’s this?” ~The eyes look up and down Asa’s yellow wind pants and orange t-shirt. The eyes make it to Asa’s face. The slide closes, and the door opens up to reveal a short little man, wearing baggy jeans, round sunglasses, much like Asa’s, a tie-dyed shirt with a peace sign on it, and a bandana. His long, gray hair shows he is pretty old. The man pats Asa on the back.~ Person: “Ace Cat! What’s shakin’ friend! I haven’t seen you in ages!” Asa: “Pfft, same old shit, Gav my man. How are things at the jive hall? Hope the same old jive turkeys are hangin’ here.” Gavin: “Oh yeah, oh yeah. They’re still here, and a couple new ones too! Come on in man, and I’ll buy you a drink…” ~Gavin leads Asa into the Jive Hall. They move through a small hallway, that opens into a large room. Asa smiles and nods his head as he watches all the 70’s fanatics disco the night away on a lit up dance floor. A DJ plays “Le Freak”, and disco lights flood the floor. Asa walks in onto the floor. After saying hi to some old friends, he walks over to the bar. A particularly good looking bartender, with brown hair, and fair skin walks to him from behind the bar. Her name tag says Lisa.~ Lisa: “What can I do for yah Ace?” Asa: “Oh, you know what I like, suga mama! Stop playin’!” Lisa: “Mikes hard lemonade?” Asa: “You got it foxy gal.” Lisa: “Comin’ right up.” ~Lisa leaves for a minute, and returns with Asa’s drink. Asa winks at her as she goes onto another customer. Asa takes a swig of his drink, and turns toward the dance floor. He bobs his head to the beat as he looks at the people dancing. Suddenly, he notices someone. It’s a black woman dancing on the floor. But this is no ORDINARY woman, this, is an afro chick! Asa smiles and sets his drink down. He gets up, and discos his way over to the foxy chick. She’s wearing an orange tube top with matching bell bottoms. Her hair is a reddish-purple afro! It bobs back and forth as she dances. Asa moon walks up to her, very smoothly.~ Asa: “Hey there, foxy mama, would you be so kind as to tellin’ me your name, suga mama? ~The woman turns to Asa, and smirks. She’s wearing purple lipstick and has very smooth looking skin. She smirks, tilts to the side and puts her hands on her hips.~ Woman: “And who wants to know, oysta pops?” Asa: “Asa “Fro” Fountain, suga mama. Now could I get your’s?” Woman: “Melanie Royston. So, you from around he…” ~Their conversation is interrupted as the door slams open, and a man in a mask, holding a gun barges in.~ Man: “ALRIGHT! DROP YOUR VALUABLES AND PUT YOUR HANDS IN THE AIR!” ~Everyone does so, except Melanie.~ Man: “YOU! DROP YOUR STUFF WOMAN!” ~Melanie rolls her eyes and walks over to the man.~ Man: “I SAID DROP YOUR BELONGINGS AND PUT YOUR HANDS IN THE AIR!!” Melanie: “Man, cat daddy, what’s your problem? You can’t just come on in here and take everybody’s stuff! What’s wrong witchoo? Man: “LAST CHANCE BITCH!” Melanie: “Oh, so now I’m a bitch, huh? Well bitch this!” ~Melanie plants her high heeled foot into the crotch of the man. The man immediately drops his weapon, and falls to the ground, clutching his crotch area and moaning in pain. Melanie kicks him in the stomach a few times, and stops.~ Melanie: “Who’s the bitch now?!” ~Melanie walks back to the dance floor. The dancers cheer as the bartender calls the cops. She walks back to Asa with a pen in her hand, and writes some numbers on his palm.~ Melanie: “There’s my phone number. Give me a call sometime, oysta pops.” ~Melanie winks at Asa, and walks away. Asa watches her with a smile on his face as the scene ends.~
