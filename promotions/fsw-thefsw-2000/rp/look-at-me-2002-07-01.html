Look at me.

1 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-1-02 Look at me. I’m a freak. Go ahead. Say it. You know it’s true. I know it’s true. Day in and Day out, I live with the fact that I may never regain the sight in my left eye. I may never regain the use of the left side of my face. Go ahead. Tell me I’m a freak if it will make you feel better. Go ahead. I dare you. No, wait. I want you to tell me I am. Look into this gray, cloudy, useless eye. And look hard.. I wake up daily, and stare at this face. It stares back at me and I realize the one man I hate more than anyone in this world, is the one that stares back at me. The one with the scarred face. With the gray dead eye. Jason O’Neil. Study my face. Remember it. Remember it well You’re going to be seeing a lot of it. Soon. Very soon. I am a freak. We’re all freaks. Everyone has something to hide. Something they don’t want the world to see. I can’t hide mine. Sure, I tattooed my face to take a bit of attention away from it. But the fact is, that just drew people closer to it. Children stop and stare. Adults point and laugh. Does it bother me? Not really, no. Should it bother me? Probably. I should wanna grab every child and make them stare at my horribly disfigured face. I should admonish every adult that points and laugh. But I don’t. It’s hard being a freak. It’s even harder to crawl out of the hole I’m in. Guys like me, coming from the small time suddenly thrust into the big time usually can’t adjust. FSW is a whole new world for me. Guys like myself, Carl Blair, Lo-Key, and Angelo Deville, have a hell of a challenge ahead of ourselves. We not only need to prove we can handle it in the big time, but we also need to prove to each other that we’re better than anything the FSW can throw at us. Better than Jesse Ramey. Better than Big D. Better than the Fearsome Fivesome that are yet to be announced. It’s a hell of a mountain to climb. If we can’t do it, then we’ll never have the opportunity again to shine like this. The rest of our lives we’ll be the men who couldn’t make it. Just like when you’re a freak. At least with being a freak, I’m guaranteed a reaction. I know that people are going to point and stare at me. I accept that. But come Glory, when my name is announced as an FSW member, what’s going to happen when no one makes a sound? Instead of being the freak with the funny eye, I’ll be the Freak without a Fan. Then again, being like a scarecrow…that doesn’t bother me.Nothing does…Not even calling me a freak. At least then I can laugh and shake it off. Just like the scarecrow...
