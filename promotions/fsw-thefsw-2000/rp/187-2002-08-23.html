Date: 8-23-02 'You murdered the poor kid.' Steven Caldera states this ominous line with a tremendous amount of glee. His voice booms on the other end of Kimbusa's telephone, giving Kimbusa cause to hold the phone a few inches from his ear. 'Poor Jason. I was really hoping to recruit his help. Guess we'll just have to move on.' Kimbusa grimaces. 'You overexaggerate. He is not dead.' Caldera laughs a bit, chuckling at Kimbusa's misinterpereting his hyperbole. 'Of course... but by God, you sent him packing pretty quick. I thought we were going to make an example of Jason over the next four weeks... It only took you four seconds. I'm impressed.' Kimbusa's eyes light up with a flair of anger, and he takes pleasure in it. 'What now?' 'Now, I sit at the announce table. And you... you just go hurt people or whatever it is you want to do. I'll be in touch.' Kimbusa nods as the other end of the phone clicks. Without turning to look at the reciever, Kimbusa reaches back and hangs up the phone. Quiet. The hotel room is modest. While Caldera insisted that he buy himself only the best with his incoming fortune, Kimbusa was content with much more modest quarters. A single 40-watt bulb lights the lamp on the nightstand, which sits in the corner next to the queen sized bed. Curtains drape over the single floor-to-ceiling window, drowning out the light from the setting sun. The carpet is thin and worn, the ceiling cracking and tinted yellow. The television rests on the dresser across from the bed, only four feet away. But Kimbusa had no intention to use it. He methodically turns and stretches out on the bed, resting on top of the surprisingly comfortable mattress. It had been a long week. Prison, seven plane flights, hospitalizing Jason O'Neil... He truly hadn't considered the possibility that O'Neil might have been ruined so easily. When they took him away on that stretcher, it looked as if O'Neil's strength might return to him at any moment. But the Scarecrow appeared to be mortal like any other, despite what Steven Caldera might have boasted. It had felt good to feel the air rushing from O'Neil's lungs. But it was the type of satisfaction that eased his craving only temporarily. It was only a quick fix. He needed more. This was what he had been after all along. He didn't find it in OSW... Perhaps it was the simple fact that he didn't have a man like Steven Caldera to guide him. Or perhaps he was simply a different person entirely. Either way, it didn't matter. He felt the same way on the FSW television show that he did that night in Tokyo eight years ago... But then it had frightened him, now it had comforted him. The metamorphosis was nearing its completion. Sumo to villian, villian to killer, killer to fearfulness, fearfulness to demon.
