Carrying the Torch for the FSW

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/9/02 Coterie. An intimate and often exclusive group of persons with a unifying common interest or purpose. Perhaps the best example of the word Coterie is Big Daveylicious, Mike Marvelous, Epitome of Evil, and Leon Chambers. Since the beginning of the FSW these four men came out with something different, new, cutting edge. Each one of these wrestlers built the FSW. One brick by one. Standing by the company that made them stars and continued to build it up. Each man in this group has a history in the FSW. Mike Marvelous. An FSW Grand Slam Champion and Hall of Famer. Mike’s fought the best of them in the old school FSW Main Eventers. Epitome of Evil. Quite possibly the most violent man in the FSW today. This 7’1 monster had many great battles as the FSW World Heavyweight Champion. Also famous battles with Big Daveylicious and beat dethroned FSW Hall of Famer, Jacob Jackson. Leon Chambers. Came in the company under the name Falken and became the greatest Lightweight champion. Big Daveylicious. FSW World Heavyweight Champion and Longest reigning Intercontinental Champion. Very quick success and a mouth with it. Cocky? Gots a right to be as his stalemates also should. Beating guys like Creature, Epitome of Evil, Johnny Mayhem, Jacob Jackson, Marc North, Mike Marvelous etc. is no easy task. These men put their differences aside. Forgetting their rivalries against one another realizing that the FSW could go out of business again due to ignorance of the newbie FSW stars and being owned by an old fart, Paul Orion whose favorite form of entertainment is Matlock. They’re company that they helped build be broken down by careless acts of ownership. Have no fear. Have no tears. FSW isn’t dead yet. It’s gonna be saved by the men that built it. It’s gonna be saved by The Stars. (The scene fades to the mean streets of Miami, Florida. Well, okay not really mean but have an imagination! Sunny skies, mild traffic, and lovely scenery. Now, this is getting boring. But what's all that dang gummit commotion about down below with that muscular figured man doing over in the middle of the road speaking with that 7’1 monster in the black trench coat. As we move in closer we see the monster in the trench coat is after all not the vintage Undertaker but, Epitome of Evil. We can't see who the guy clad in black Nike pants, black jacket with a hood, and a pair of Nike running shoes is but he falls to his knees into a puddle splashing EOE in the process and lifts his arms up in glory. He tilts his head up, dropping the hood of the jacket, reveling the face of Big Daveylicious smiling. EOE simply wipes himself off ignoring Dave.) Big Daveylicious: Whoooooooooooooooooooo! I didn’t job to the Riverdance! (EOE taps Big D on the shoulder.) Big Daveylicious: What? Can't you see I am taking this moment of true glory and letting myself shine? I mean I am the only guy to never job to the Riverdance! I didn’t job to an award winning dance show! (Big D smiles proudly as EOE sighs.) Epitome of Evil: Big D, its Raindance, Johnny Raindance. Not Riverdance. (Big D's eyes open and look at EOE.) Big Daveylicious: You mean I did job to the Riverdance? Crap! Epitome of Evil: No, you didn’t job. You didn’t job to Johnny Raindance. Big Daveylicious: Who? Epitome of Evil:... (EOE just shakes his head and walks to the sidewalk were Big D’s loyal lackey goth, Micheal Graves stands by with Big D’s agent, Tommy Stillinger. Big D just shrugs and follows EOE. Oddly enough Stillinger seems to be holding a unlit torch with the FSW logo on it. Big D takes notice.) Big Daveylicious: What’s with the torch? Tommy Stillinger: What’s the “Man Carrying the Torch for the FSW“ running around without his FSW torch? Big Daveylicious: A torch? Epitome of Evil: I don’t like this. Tommy Stillinger: C’mon think of it? Think of it as Jake “The Snake” Roberts with his snakes and “Hacksaw” Jim Duggan with his 2x4! Look what it did for their careers! Big Daveylicious: Uh? Isn’t Jake Roberts some crack addict fighting in High schools? Tommy Stillinger: He’s called “Mr. DDT”. Think they might call you “Mr. Power Bomb”? Big Daveylicious: My finisher is not a power bomb. It’s the Daveylicious Bomb! Tommy Stillinger: Riiiiight. Listen, Dave think of it this way. You do this you get fame, fortunate, girls, your name in the lights, and start raising my salary. Big Daveylicious: Ok, I’ll run with the torch. (He reluctantly grabs the FSW Torch as his agent sets it on fire with his lighter creating a flame. His agent gladly waves his hands in the air in a motion.) Tommy Stillinger: Look what I have created! FIIIIIIRRRRRRRE! Big Daveylicious: Damn, its hot. Micheal Graves: Thanks, Dave. I’ve been working out on the Butt Master. (Michael does some thrust in and out.) Michael Graves: I squeeze my little tushy in and than out. In and out. (Disgusted looks come across the trio on looking Graves antics.) Big Daveylicious: I was talking about the flaming... Micheal Graves: Yes, I confess. My heart is flaming for you... Big Daveylicious: Your sexuality won’t be the only thing flaming as I take this torch and stick it up your ass! Michael Graves: Is that a promise? Big Daveylicious: Ugh! Not literally. (They all shake their head at Graves in disappointment.) Johnny Stillinger: Just remember Dave. Run around the city carrying the torch like your gonna win the FSW World Heavyweight title. Big Daveylicious: WHOOOOOOOOO! I run with a torch on fire! (Big D starts jogging carrying the torch in one hand lifting it up in proudly with pride. Not noticing the fat man ahead of him bending over to tie his shoe. But suddenly a little kid hugs his waist for some reason. Dave jogs in place to see the kids problem.) Little Kid: Are you my Daddy? Big Daveylicious: Uhhhhhhhhhhh.....There was that one time with that Tijuana hooker....or that other time with that circus midget...BYE! (Dave throws the kid off of his waist and runs toward the fatman tying his show. Big D is unable to notice him as he looks back at the kid. Suddenly, he trips over the fat man, flipping over the mans body with the torch slipping out of his hand, he falls to the pavement as the torch is about to land on the pavement just as well but what luck it lands on his back. We start seeing some flames appear on the back of Big D from the torch but he doesn’t notice it when he grabs the torch off of his back. He smiles and “raises the roof“ with his hands.) Big Daveylicious: Whoooooooooo! The Roof is on Fire! (The fat man still in place were he was looks up and starts waving his hands.) Big Daveylicious: Yeah, the roof is one FIRE! Whoooooooooooooo! Fat man: You’re on fire! Big Daveylicious: I know my careers on fire with my desires! Fat man: Litterally you bubbling idiot! YOUR ON FIRE! (Dave takes a little look to his back and screams.) Big Daveylicious: I’M ON FIRE! Fat man: Stop, drop, and roll! (He takes out a baseball from his pocket, drops it, and it rolls on the pavement. Dave is frantically screaming realizing this didn‘t work.) Big Daveylicious: I didn’t work! Fat man: LITERALLY, STOP, DROP, AND ROLL! Whatever you do don’t run.... (Dave runs frantically with the flames growing on his back carrying the torch as he doesn’t notice that he is running in the road. He just runs crazily towards a luxurious wishing fountain.) Big Daveylicious: I’m gonna make it! (Suddenly, as he jumps for the fountain a speeding car hits him in mid air onto the hood of the car, sending the torch into the fountain and sending Big D on the ledge of the fountain.) Big Daveylicious: Why can’t I just go in damn it! (He pushes himself into the fountain. The fire dissolves off of his back. He brushes himself off wet, and barely stands himself up.) Big Daveylicious: Whoooooooooo! I’m the man who carries the FSW Torch! (Big D looks at his left hand which he had carried it with and looks down in the fountain seeing the torch float unlit again. He falls to his knees angry and upset.Not noticing the little kid standing near the fountain. He just ignores him.) Voice: Are you my daddy? Big Daveylicious: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Damn it! The torch is not lit! (. He lifts the wet torch and throws it out of the fountain in mid air.) Little Kid: Are you my Daddy? AHHHHHHHHHH! (It lands onto unsuspectingly onto the smack down the boys mouth.) Big Daveylicious: Oh crap. Well, c’ya later kid! (He runs out of our view as we fade into the torch lying onto the kids bloody mouth into the FSW logo.)
