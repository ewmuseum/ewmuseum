Right about now ...

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/19/02 (Welcome back friends and enemies, after a long trip to Ohio, which resulted in me being away longer than I hoped, I have returned ... and I feel like doing an unconventional rp. Since I like tormenting Bryan so much, I think I will do a KVL style rp. You know the drill. Song lyrics. Rp. Pretty simple eh? Wait till you see my next promo. I just got the idea for it, whilst writing this little intro. I almost don't want to write this one now, because I want to write the other one, but I guess I'll just do two ... that sounds fair enough. Anyway, so here we go ... this one won't be a long one. I've come to the conclusion that rather than writing one long rp per week ... I'll just do several shorter ones. I just finished my coffee ... and I'm lighting a smoke up right now, so I guess it's time to start this rp. The song? Well ... that is something I'll leave up to the new SDMJK to reveal to you all. The song will be in regular type, and John's speech will in thought, marked off with the brackets.) I gotta get me a grip and be strong. Gotta show I can rip, moving on. (Things are finally starting to come around here. I'm 2 and 1 in FSW. I just found out one of my reasons for fighting here is wrong. Kid Wonder eludes me. How could I have mistaken Kyle for Kdub? I've got more reasons to stay and fight now though. One reason in particular. Primetime Title.) As I trembling, It's not ending. Gotta avoid to make slips and meet nongs. (No matter how nervous I am about what is to come. Of what has happened, and what may never happen ... it'll never be over. I just can't make mistakes, or at least limit the ones I do make so that they can't affect me. Especially with the path I'm on now. Angelo Deville for the gold. I can't screw up on that.) 'Cause I want it, yeah I wanna live my life. I wanna live my life in this world. And I need it, yes I need it, yeah, I need to spend my days on this earth. (As long as I have fun doing it though. I don't really care. I've already died once here and lived my life too conservatively since then. I've got to go all out. One hundred and ten percent.) I gotta learn to say NO, disallow. Well, it's my turn to go pro, right about now. (I haven't done too much lately. I was in CCW. I was a legend there. A hall of famer. But this is the big leagues. CCW was great, and a lot of fun. But it's just nowhere near the professional level that these feds are. Here's my chance to do it. To prove that I can make it anywhere. Not just CCW. Winning this belt will be enough to show that I'm no joke. Holding onto it will be the next step. Moving past that will be the ultimate one.) I've been fading for too long, degrading. I gotta earn me some dough, this is how. (This is what will put me back in the spotlight. Back in the big bucks. Back in the game. People considered my prime to be in CCW when I was fighting beside J-Mac. Those were great days ... but my prime has yet to come. It's time to step out of the black and white, and into full colour. No more fading in with the rest. It's time to be at the forefront. It's time to shine.) At 23, I was far from feeling free. At 24, my life was a big bore. Now I'm 25 and I'm still not high, yeah, I'm 25 but still alive. (Ok ... so I'm 26. I'm still young. I'm not at my best yet. But I've still got fight in me. As long as I'm still alive, I'll still be fighting.) I'll never give up until I reach my goal, until I reach my goal... Controlling body and soul. (Angelo ... you are the goal right now. What you throw over your shoulder, or wrap around your waist ... that gold ... that is my goal. My dream ... however ... is far bigger. You are just the first step. But don't worry. It's a step in the right direction. And when the dream is realized ... that is when FSW will step into the forefront, with me at the head.) (OOC Note: Bryan ... you should know this song. Not sure if you listen to the band ... but it's up in your alley. If you can't get it ... I'll let everyone know in my next promo.)
