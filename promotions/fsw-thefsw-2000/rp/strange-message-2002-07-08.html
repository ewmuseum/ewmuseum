Strange Message

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-8-02 “Hey cousin Harold brings me another bit of that there moonshine.” One of the relatives yells out from around the fire, where we see several scantily dressed men and a numeral amount of woman. “You sure we should be drinking this stuff round a far?” Another one of the relations let out as some noises can be heard coming from behind one of the bushes in the woods. “Jesse get out of them their bushes with my daughter.” One of the men say, standing up, but falling back down and taking another drink from his moonshine jug. “Hang on a second, Uncle Leroy. I’m almost finished.” Jesse yells out from behind the bush. “That moonshine sure does some funny things to that there boy.” One of the older leans over and whispers to Uncle Leroy, who is still guzzling away at his moonshine jug. Jesse walks out from behind the bushes holding, what is obviously, his cousin’s hand. She begins to whip at her mouth as Jesse walks over to the fire and takes one of the moonshine jugs and begins tipping it up in front of all the men. Downing it like it were nothing. “Uncle Leroy, I need to ask you a question.” “What you need there, boy?” Uncle Leroy says, trying to stand up. “I wanna ask you if I can marry, Cindy May.” Jesse takes another big drink of the moonshine and just looks on at his uncle. Leroy begins to eye Jesse, but then begins to rub his eyes and smile comes across his face, “Boy, if’n I weren’t yur uncle, I’d say no, but by golly boy. The girls done sweet on ya and I can’t say no.” A huge smile comes across Jesse’s face as he looks at Cindy May and plants a huge kiss on her. “Wez going to get married Cindy May. Wez ain’t going to be cousins anymore, wez going to be umm…Let me drink some and I’ll think of it.” Jesse begins to stagger over to the old bathtub where the moonshine is being made, as he makes his way he drops his jug into the tub and pulls out a full jug. He tips it up and begins to drink as another girl walks over to him. “Jesse Ramey! I thought you were going to marry me!” She says with anger in her voice. “I’m sorry Peggy Ann, I only said that stuff to get in yur pants.” Jesse says as he begins to laugh. “I’mma telling you now, Jesse. You best make up yur mind, cause if you don’t marry me, then I’m tellin’ pa and he ain’t gonna like it.” She says crossing her arms. Jesse begins to laugh, “Peggy Ann, your pa is my pa too. He ain’t gonna be mad at me. He’ll just laugh and pat me on the back.” Peggy Ann pouts up and begins to make her way back to the fire as Jesse continues to down his moonshine and finally makes his way back as an older looking man approaches him. “Beware my son, of the great misfortune that this drink will bring unto your life. The bearing it will bring will be a hard one and the road you must travel will be a long one. Delusion and bewilderment are surely in your future and soon you will find yourself talking to the most unthinkable things. Beware!” The old looking man begins to make his way off as Jesse walks back over to the fire. “Wonder who that old man was.” Jesse says as all of the other men at the fire look at him strangely. “What?” “What older man?” Uncle Leroy asks. “That one there I was just talking to, Uncle Leroy. He look like he was going to get him a little sip of our moonshine. Thought maybe he were with one of you fellers.” Jesse says as he turns back around and finds no one behind him or off to the sides. “Boy yous done been visited by a spirit and yous best listen to what he be preaching. It surely be the truth.” Uncle Leroy says as Jesse just continues looking back into the woods. Finally tipping up his moonshine once more and making his way back to the fire. ~End~
