The Elmer Files (Part 1)

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-18-02 The scene opens up to a very familiar theme song as we see Steve Irwin pop into the corner of the screen with a massively huge smile upon his face, much like you always see him. A little more gay looking today than usual, he is making his way through the forests of an unknown area. Steve: Hello, ladies and gentlemen, today I’m coming to you live from the backwoods of Harts, West Virginia. Steve continues to make his way through the woods, walking very slowly, as to not make too much noise on the ground. Steve: On today’s episode we’re going to be looking for the most elusive creature in all of the animal kingdom. One that you’ve only heard stories of, but have never seen. Well, today I’m doing you all a tremendous favor in looking for this creature and putting my life on the line to boot. Steve begins looking around a bit and then squats down to the ground rustling in a pile of leaves. Steve: You see here, (Picking up the leaves) this here is a white substance, and if I’m not mistaken, it’s the sperm of the creature we’re looking for, but just to be for certain I’m going to taste it. Steve lifts up the leaf and begins licking it and a huge smile comes across his face a little bit of the seamen is still along the side of his mouth. Steve: Now, that is one hundred percent seamen of the creature we’re looking for. I can tell you that from experience and just plain know how. Steve stands up and begins to walk around a little bit more, finally wiping the excess of the seamen off of his mouth. Steve: Now, that sperm sample that I just tasted seemed to be freshly made, so the creature shouldn’t be too far from these parts. I’m going to look around for some more clues on where I might be able to find the beast. Steve continues walking around and finally stops in his tracks and looks into the camera. Steve: Now, I don’t know why I just called our creature a beast, because in all reality, this is a very cuddly little creature, which is somewhat similar to a Koala. So, you could basically call it a backwoods marsupial. It’s a sort of dark blueish color and it grows when water is added to it, and from looking around today is rather damp and we might want to watch ourselves. Because when the creature becomes wet he grows in size by at least seven times and it goes into a humping frenzy. I’ve never actually seen an animal like this in captivity before, but I have seen photos and it’s not a pretty sight when this thing gets loose. Steve turns back and continues making his way through the woods, passing through trees, branches, and briar bushes. Stopping suddenly again and pointing into the woods. Steve: Look there, we’ve finally found our creature! It’s so beautiful, come on; get a close in shot on the creature. The camera pans in really close on the creature, now presumably Jesse Ramey’s pet Three-Toed Ringed Sloth Monkey, Elmer. His size is massive, somewhat similar to the night we saw him getting Ming. Steve: The creature is still obviously overcoming the recent rainy weather, because as you can see, he is still rather large in size and is seemingly humping that nearby tree. Now, we don’t want to cause too much commotion because the creature could turn on us and come after us. Now, I am aware of what I am doing here and feel that I can make my way closers to this creature and in the process, I’m going to jam my thumb up his bum and find out where or not it’s a male or female. Steve begins to make his way closer to the creature, but to his amazement, not looking down at the ground while your walking could be a bad thing, as he trips over an up rooted tree root and goes tumbling down to the ground hard. Steve: (Hurting obviously) Hopefully the creature won’t notice what just happened and will continue it’s mating ritual with the tree. Elmer brings himself down to the ground and begins making his way around, finally hoping up on his hind legs and sticking his nose in the air. Steve: The creature seems to be sniffing for something, what? I have no clue. Elmer flops back down on all fours and begins looking in Steve’s direction. Steve: (In total shock) Oh, shit. This doesn’t look good. The camera man runs away and hides behind a tree as quickly as he possible can as the giant Elmer climbs on top of Steve and begins humping him up the butt. Steve: (Yelling into the camera) You see, I didn’t really have to stick my thumb up his bum to find out whether or not this creature was a male or female, because now, he’s getting me up the bum and I know for a fact that he’s a male. Elmer continues pumping away at Steve’s butt. Steve: Now, one thing I can surely tell you are that these creatures have tremendous reproduction organs, seeing as how this little guy has probably been going at trees constantly today. He’ll more than likely do his business in me bum one more time and be on his way, I’ll probably need to go and get myself a coffee enema after today to get all of this gook washed out of me bum, seeing as how this little fella has done did his business up in me, several times. Elmer lets out a massive roar and makes his way off of Steve, revealing a very large hole in the back of his shorts. Steve: And ladies and gentlemen, that concludes today’s special on the elusive, Three-Toed Ringed Sloth Monkey. (Looking at the camera man) Now, go get me some bloody help, I can’t move. The scene fades out on Elmer making his way through the woods, whistling a little tune.
