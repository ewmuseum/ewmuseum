Moving On

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August25 Danbury, Connecticut The grass so green, as green can be. So cleanly cut and managed to give off the greatest of impressions to each of those whom glance upon it. A cool, lust breeze sweeps across the land, sending a minor vibration through every strand of grass along the estate. A low pitched rustle can be heard as you glance up and across the line of trees in the near background. You glance further, taking notice to an iron fencing stretching around the estate, but staying hidden beneath the shadows of the trees. Welded on the iron gate is a wealthy looking name plate. You squint your eyes to read just exactly what it reads, for distance plays your enemy at the moment. The sudden sound of a speeding car startles you, causing a slight fright. You peer behind you, taking a glimpse at the interstate highway. Looking back towards the home, you decide to take a few steps forward to obtain a better look at the name plate mounted on the iron gate. The name plate reads...'DAVIDSON ESTATE.' 'I've arrived.' Chris was dressed in a windbreaker jacket as there was a slight chill on the near-end summer night. The knee-caps of his dark blue jeans torn up and raggedy. Chris stops in his tracks with a smile upon his face, as he peers up at the estate of Tyler Davidson. He glances down at his white Nike shoes and grins at the dirt and grime covering them. 'Filth,' he says with utter disgust. 'That is what i gather from just a few moments on Tyler's grass. A slop of filth all over my new sneakers.' Chris looks back towards the estate and decides to progress forward. He slowly paces towards the iron gate. 'Tyler interrupted my path. He ruined my life.' Chris glances around him as he walks, making sure there is nobody else in site. 'Now I prepare to finish his.' He reaches the iron gate and peers up to the top, where the metal is cut in a sharp, triangular fashion. 'Those points...they'd cause a punction in skin that could not be stitched back together. One would bleed to death before the punction would heal.' Chris rethinks his way of entry. He peers to the left and then to the right, taking notice of the seemingly never-ending iron fence. He raises his hands, contemplating pulling at the fence. However, before touching his skin to the iron fence, a very minute sign catches the corner of his eye. 'A little feisty, are we?' Chris smiles at the small sign reading '10,000 volts'. 'He must've been expecting some sort of attacker.' Chris takes a few paces back, surveying the scene. No apparent SAFE entrance into the estate. 'Guess this may be a bit harder than I had presumed.' Chris smiles as he continues backstepping. 'But I'm always up for a slight challenge.' Shift Bagwell is out of the picture. But in a sense, his spirit remains. He remains in the form of another man with the same plan for operation! Tommy Gunn, a dear friend of Chris, is brought into picture on a small speedboat. He's wearing a black zippered down vest and a dark blue and white bandana. He also has on a pair of baggy black cargo pants, armed with various tools for breaking and entering. The speedboat approaches a port where a few other large yachts are harbored. Tommy stands at the nose of the boat, seemingly directing traffic. Another man, unknown at the moment, appears to be driving the vehicle itself. Tommy points up into the trees at what looks like a large estate. Perhaps one we've seen before? 'There it is,' says Tommy with plenty of confidence in his navigation skills. 'Davidson Estate!' The large mansion can only be seen in an outline, without much detail because of the night fall and gentle fog mist in the air. Suddenly, Tommy feels a slight vibration. He reaches into his right pocket with his right hand and pulls out a small motorola cellular phone. Tommy glances at the caller id and a smile graces his face. He presses the SND button, answering the call and raising the phone to his ear. 'We're right out back,' he says, not even greeting the caller. 'Perfect,' replies the caller. 'The front is as sealed as can be. The only entry is probably going to be from the back.' With that comment, the caller can easily be identified as Chris Bagwell, who was last seen at the front of the estate. 'We're going to dock,' Tommy says to Chris. 'ROLF,' he then yells to the driver. The driver of the boat peers out from within his cabinet space, showing to be a rather tall and husky man. Tommy commands, 'pull us close to the dock and we're gonna bust in.' Chris laughs in concurrence on the other end of the line. 'I'll let you know when we're in,' Tommy assures Chris. He concludes the call by pressing the END button, and proceeds to place the phone back into his right pocket. Rolf pulls their speedboat close to the Davidson Estate docks. Tommy lifts a rope from the deck of the boat and tosses it onto the dock. He takes a few steps back and sprints forward, leaping off the boat. Tommy lands on the dock, trying to make as little noise as possible. He grabs the rope he threw moments ago and pulls the boat closer to the dock. Rolf tosses him a spike and Tommy slams it into the wooden dock. He ties the rope around the spike, securing the boat. Rolf then turns off the ignition and climbs out of the boat, joining Tommy on the dock. 'Lets show this bastard how to play tough,' Tommy whispers to Rolf. Rolf nods his head, giving a slight smile. His rotted teeth are exposed, giving a disgusting quiver to any who take a glimpse. The two men begin walking up the dock when suddenly, Rolf stops in his tracks. 'What the hell,' Tommy questions. 'I smell...' Rolf speaks softly and cautiously. He takes a few steps forward, VERY slowly. He suddenly thrusts his foot out to the right and a dog cry is heard followed by a thump. Tommy jogs up and looks, 'Hot dayum!' Rolf smelt a dobermin pincher hiding behind a tree. He kicked the dog in the throat as it was about to lounge at him, killing it by crushing it's throat. 'You're amazing, Rolf. Just simply amazing.' Tommy laughs aloud. The two continue forward to the mansion that is Davidson Estate! Shift The picture cuts back to the front, where Chris has conjured up some aluminum from an earlier meal. He folds and twists the aluminum into a curve like semi-circle. He approaches the gate of the fence and rolls his eyes back and forth between the coded gate opener and the high voltage gate itself. Bagwell smirks. 'Looks like the only way in for me...is to FRY THIS SHIT!' He touches the aluminum to the coded padlock and points the opposite end towards the fence. He thrusts the aluminum and releases it, trying to make it touch the fence. It hits the fence, however doesn't remain on the padlock. Thus it falls straight to the ground, between two iron spikes. 'God dammit!' Chris yells, slightly pissed. He kneels besides the fencing spikes and carefully reaches his hand between them, grasping the strip of aluminum and pulling it back into his possession. 'I've gotta think of a way to get this shit to blow.' Chris looks around for something to improve his idea. He takes notice of a dangling tree brance. A light suddenly goes off in his head. Chris sprints to the tree, jumps up, and rips off the tree branch. He wraps the middle of the foil around the branch, still retaining the semi-circular shape. 'This should make due.' Chris then approaches the padlock and iron gate. He touches one end of the foil to the padlock and the other to the fence. The iron fence begins to glow as the foil grows red hot. The branch begins smoking, as does the padlock. Suddenly, the branch catches fire from the excessive heat. Bagwell's eyes light up as the fire starts to climb towards his hand. CRACK, CRACK, POP!! The padlock BURSTS, opening the iron gates. Bagwell drops the flaming branch immediately, breathing a sigh of relief. He smiles sadistically as the iron fencing sparks and the voltage blows. Bagwell steps over what was the gate, and enters himself into the Davidson facility. 'Tyler, I hope you're ready. Because Bagwell is just about to interrupt yours, Magnum's, and Angelo's little slumber party. So let it be known and let it be sworn.' Shift The picture cuts to Tommy and Rolf in the yard. 'THEY'RE ABOUT TO BE TORN!' Fade.
