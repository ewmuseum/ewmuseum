The Siamese Pussy (Part 2 of a series that may go on forever. Heh.)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-5-02 ***The scene comes to life once again in the office of Magnum Wolf. The same black and white motif, the same scratches on the film. Fake scratches... but scratches nonetheless. Smoke swirls around in the room... all eminating from Magnum's cigarette, and snaking upwards until it reaches the ceiling fan. It then disperses into the rest of the room like an explosion of water on a duck's back. Seated in the chair behind his desk is Magnum Wolf. A faint snoring noise can be heard every couple of seconds. Without warning, the door bangs open, and smacks against the wall. Magnum wakes up, and crashes to the floor in a heap... again.*** 'WHAT! NO MOMMY, I CAN CLEAN MY OWN SHEETS!' Magnum screams as he wakes up, and finds himself sprawled out on the floor. ***The voice can be heard... seemingly coming from nowhere...*** 'It was at that moment that I realized why Burt Reynolds never hired a washed up wrestler to be his right hand man. They simply had no class. No grasp of how to be cool, and calm, and confident. That, and he was ugly. This was one of those moments that made me wish I had a female right hand WOMAN... And I do mean right hand...' 'MAGNUM!' Virgil shouts, interrupting the voice that seems to come from nowhere. 'WOLF! Is this like Marco Polo? I rule at that game,' comes the reply from Magnum, still on the floor behind his desk. 'Stop foolin' around, and get off the ground! That hot bitch is here again! And she looks mad!' ***Magnum jumps up from behind the desk, straightens a few papers, and ruffles his hair a bit to look like he's working hard. Without much thought, he also grabs one of the bottles from within the globe near him, and spins off the top. With one fluid motion, a large amount of liquid pours down his throat. As the burn from the alcohol attacks his stomach, he smiles slightly, then looks back at Virgil as he places his ass in the chair behind him.*** 'Send her in.' ***Virgil nods vehemently, then disappears from the doorway of Magnum's office. Soon enough, the space previously filled by the horrendously unattractive Virgil is now filled by the INCREDIBLY attractive Ima Shaven. Magnum smiles as he sees her. She's clad in a tight black skirt that is barely covering any of her thigh, and a silky black top whose neckline dives straight to hell. She saunters into the room, and looks coyly at the private eye behind his desk. She carefully sits down, so as not to reveal herself to Mr. Wolf, then crosses her legs, and lets the one on top dangle just slightly so that it bobs up and down. Magnum's eyes are glued. To... her... eyes. Yeah. Eyes.*** 'What can I do ya for, Ms. Shaven?' 'Excuse me for trying to tell you how to do your job, Mr. Wolf, but shouldn't you be out and about searching for clues, and what-not? Instead of hanging around your office like some sort of bum?' 'What? You never came back to talk to me! I don't even know what the Shaven Pussy looks like...' 'If you haven't seen one at this point in your life, you're doing something wrong.' 'WAIT, WAIT, WAIT!' Magnum exclaims ***Magnum stands up in his chair, quite angry. The scene fades into color, and basically, the scene wipes away until all you see is Magnum behind a desk, and Ms. Shaven sitting in her chair...*** 'No way! This is my promo... I'm the humorous one here. Everyone has a part in a promo like this. Virgil is the bumbling idiot assistant... I'm the suave comedic genius star... and you're the T & A. Period. Don't start trying to be a comedian or I'll start calling you Chris Bagwell.' 'Buh dum ching...' Ima Shaven says sarcastically. 'Now then... take it from your line right after I say 'I don't even know what the Shaven Pussy looks like', and this time, no adlibbing, capiche?' 'Whatever...' ***With that, Magnum sits back down, and his scene blurs back into focus. The screen returns to black and white, and Ima continues with her line...*** 'Well, you didn't really need a description of her, did you? How many cases of stolen pussy do you have in your unit?' 'I never steal pussy, ma'am. They always consent to my unit. As far as this case is concerned, all I really need is a description of the cat to get on my merry way.' 'I told you it was Siamese. They all look basically the same. It has a collar on that is adorned with diamonds. Not a very common cat, to say the least.' 'Yeah, ok... I'm on my way right now to start looking... have you seena magnifying glass laying around?' 'I just got here.' 'Nevermind... I must have left it somewhere.' ***Magnum stands up, and leaves the room. Ahead of Ima Shaven. Ima looks confused for a second, then notices the bottles of alcohol in the globe near Magnum's desk. With the quickness, she snatches up a bottle, and begins to drink it as the scene fades to black...***
