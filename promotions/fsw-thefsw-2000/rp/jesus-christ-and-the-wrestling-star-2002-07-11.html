Jesus Christ and the Wrestling Star

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-11-02 “I’m sorry sir, I cant help you, I really wish I could Mr. Guadeloupe.” “BOLAMBA” “Lasagna” “BOLAMBA” “Labamba” “BOLOGNA…..Aww crap, now he has me doing it!” Timo Bolamba, the perpetual master of all things Samoan is standing at the counter of the local hardware store, trying to convince a very nice young man by the name of Billy to give him a deal on a rotary tool, yet it appears to be a no go. One would think with the type of money that Timo rolls in, he wouldn’t care, but hell, lets face it, principle is principle. “Look kid, I’m sure you are very good at what you do, but why cant you just…well you know…drop the price a bit for a star?” “Well sir, not to be an ass, but you aren’t exactly Tom Cruise.” The squeaky voiced teenager was testing the Samoans patience. It wasn’t that he couldn’t get the deal, he just wanted to prove he was right at this point. Stupid stock kids, always trying to make crap up to get their asses out of a sling… “Excuse me sir?” “What?” “You were just talking about how stupid stock kids make crap up to save their asses…if I’m not mistaken.” “You heard that?” “Yes I did, and I don’t appreciate it.” Timo could tell at this point his inner narration was a little left to be desired. Either that or this kid was damned good, not to take chances Timo decided to test his luck a bit. -Okay kid, if you can hear me, try this on for size. I want chicken I want liver, Meow Mix Meow Mix please deliver-. The poor kid stood there like a stone watching Timo grin foolishly as he entertained himself. Timo seemed satisfied with his work and even took the time to point at the kid and laugh. “What is your problem?” “In your face you pimply twit!” “Okay now that’s just uncalled for Mr. Costanza.” Timo looked irritated, and threw his hands in the air. He had been trying to hold back but apparently it wasn’t going to work. Like a fat man in a buffet line, he knew he was going to have to make his mark. The Islander pulled out a piece of paper, the card for Glory as it were and held it up for the kid. “Look familiar?” The teenage boy squinted at the picture then seemed to grin a bit. He looked at Timo, then back at the picture, then back at the Samoan again. Sure enough, take the shirt off, put him in some wrestling pants and it was sure fire that you had… “Big Daveyliscious! I knew it was you!” “WHAT?!” Timo held the picture aloft in scorn, then tried to restrain himself from kicking the kid in the sack. Jeez, this had to be a joke, but just to make sure, he knew he had to take matters into over innings. He folded his paper back up and put it in his pocket then looked down to the 5 foot tall kid that went maybe a buck ten, and was riddled with zits. “Now kid, I know you are bright in your own special way, but do I look like I have “Cum Dumpster” tattooed on my forehead?” “No, from here is looks more like Dumb Ass, but I could be mistaken…it could be Ass Pirate, or even Rump Ranger.” Timo’s eyes light up and he starts to clench his fists in a violent manner. His face turns bright red as he tries to restrain himself. He closes his eyes and tries to imagine things to make him happy, and luckily for him, his ten second attention span quickly takes over. “Big Blocks, Hemi, 428 Boss…Corvette…ooh 69 Charger with a 440 six pack!” Timo goes on for several minutes rambling to himself, all the while the stock boy is patiently waiting. He whistles at the Samoan, but it has no effect. He tries again, this time snapping his fingers in front of his face, yet again there is no response. Finally the kid resorts to the one thing that can snap any man out of a day dream. “Whoa, I think I just saw Shakira run by naked!” Timo’s head whips around so fast that he is hit in the head by a man walking by with a 2x4 over his shoulder. The Samoan stumbles around for a bit, but the words of the boy cause the other man to swing around, clocking Timo again in classic Three Stooges fashion. Timo shakes his head and looks at all the pretty birds, then appears semi coherent… “Anyone get the number on that bus?” Then he falls over. “Hey buddy, you okay?” There lay the man who had been hit by every number of steel chair and flying 400 lb. men, knocked senseless by a two shot from a board. He looked up at the form standing above him and it was clear who he was talking to. “Jesus?” “Umm yeah…that’s me.” “Whoa…did I die?” “No…umm…my son…but I need you to do something for me…yeah” Timo got to thinking about it, and he couldn’t quite figure out why Jesus would talk to him in a hardware store, but hell, does god play favorites when it comes to his children does he? Why would Jesus discriminate against a Home Depot over Hardware Hanks? It didn’t make sense, but Timo wanted to figure it out… “Hey, you still with us?” “Oh yeah, I’m here Jesus, just my stupid inner dialogue taking control again. So what did you need?” The form that was still unclear due to the lighting in the warehouse like store reached out his not quite samite clad hand and Timo took it. The Samoan stood up to look in the face of a man in his late forties, wearing a nametag that said “Carl”. “Dude, I never knew your name was Carl.” The man looks down and then back up, shaking his head in disbelief. He hands Timo a box with a Rotary tool inside and looks the Samoan Warrior over to make sure he is okay. “Tell you what, take this, compliments of us to say we are sorry for the incident, and please have a nice day?” Timo looked down upon the box and smiled. He got his rotary tool, what was better, he had received it from the hand of the Lord himself. “Wow…Holy Dremel…” Timo thanked his Savior…whom was named Carl, and headed for the door. He had a lot to prepare for, mostly finding a way to rearrange the faces of Mike Marvelous, Big Daveyliscious, and maybe even Johnny Raindance if he gets in the way. Soon enough he had got back home, but when he walked in the door, there stood the creature from the cole cream lagoon…had her hair curlers looking like horns. He smiled stupidly and tried to make nicey nicey but she would have none of it. Gwenneth stood holding the twin children of Timo’s and also holding a very pissed off face. “Where have you been for the last four hours? You said you were going to get a tool!” “And did I ever! Look, some dude who told me he was Jesus, but his real name was Carl gave this to me because I got killed, but I came back to life, and this kid named Billy called me an Ass Pirate, but I didn’t kill him, even though I wanted to! You can tell those self aggression classes have been paying off, anywho, I got in there and this kid was all like “Hey Cock Knocker, you gotta pay full price! Then I’m all like, but hey, Its not like I’m Tim Walden or something, then he was all trying to get on my nerves and I gave him a mean look and the kid tried to ramble something under his breath but I caught him, and he told me I was the golden king of McDonalds…” “TIMO!” “And then………What?” “Did all that happen or are you just making it up?” . . . . . . . . “Okay I’m PRETTY sure most of it happened…especially the part about Jesus…but I might have made up the last part…” “Oh Timo…why don’t you go lay down…you want some Tiki Punch?” What else was there left to say for the Samoan Asskicking Machine? In his very best Eric Cartman voice he looked up and smiled: “Yeah I want some Tiki Punch!”
