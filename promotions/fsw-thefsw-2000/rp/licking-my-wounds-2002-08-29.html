Licking my wounds.

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/29/2002 I am Boda. There is NO guessing you'd call me a bad guy. I'm what the wrestling industry, the wrestling marks and smarks call a HEEL, the one the fans HATE. I don't want to be some hero..... I AIN'T. I’m not even an anti-hero. I’m the type of guy your parents told you to be afraid of. I do not want people to hate me because they are told to. I want people to hate me because I MAKE THEM HATE ME. I want to HURT PEOPLE. I want to KILL any opposition to me or the commissioner of FSW. I wasn’t given a choice in this........it’s the position I CHOSE to take. Thursday evening. 20 hours gone by since the tragedy that happened on Glory. The scene opens......... Excuse me? What tragedy? You all can honestly stand there, and ask me ‘What Tragedy?’ Well, before I continue, I’ll educate you all. The tragedy I am talking about is the one where not only Mike Marvelous showed the reason why he’s no better than the scum on the bottom of a bacteria filled test tube, but the unexpecting appearance in the shape of ‘revenge’ from Paul Orion, directed at Boda himself. Oh yeah.... now all of you remember what I am talking about, don’t you? Can I get on with this story now? Good. Where was I....... The scene opens the day following Glory. It’s a drizzling, wet day in San Antonio. The streets are buzzing with vehicles heading from places of work, to the comfort of home and family. Of course, that is all happening some 15 stories below, as we are inside the hotel room of Chris Sloboda. Staying at the Five Star Wrestling Official Hotel of Choice, The Marriott, Boda stands in front of his window, overlooking the busy streets of San Antonio. As Chris looks at the traffic, and the rain beads on the window, we can see the reflection of his face in the glass. The look on his face is one not of pain, not one of anger..... but one more of humiliation. Damn them. First Mikey Marvelous. He’s not changed one god damn bit since PCW. Why, why did I for one minute think I could trust that backstabbing son of a bitch? I had Timo right where I always had him.... on his back, begging for me to finish him quickly, then BAM.... Marvelous pulled one of his ‘I CAN’T BE OUTSHINED BY ANYONE’ moments, and laid me out, after I took Timo apart. MARVELock..... yeah, it didn’t feel like a slap on the wrist, but I swear.... next time.... He won’t get to pull this shit. Not on me. Not again. Boda breaks his glance from the streets below. The monster of a man opens the window, and let’s in a breeze of rain fresh air, and some rain. As the water pools on the window sill, Chris shuts the window and runs his massive right hand through the water. Placing his eyes back on the activity outside, we see Boda’s reflection again in the glass as he combs his wet hand through his hair, from the back, to the front. Damn..... that’s tender. Been a long time since I took a chair shot to the head. Probably one of the reasons I decided to retire in the first place. But, I never expected that geezer Orion to actually pull something like this. Hell, if anything... I’d of expected him to talk The HIT into joining FSW. I mean, after all.... The HIT never beat me, and he’s all but bragged he’s beaten all the original Red Army members... but Boda. Damn Orion..... he’s got to pay for this little stunt. Bringing in a low life, piece of shit, to counter measure Chip’s hiring of me. I knew I should of killed that bastard when I visited him in the hospital. And as far as Orion’s ‘saviour’ of FSW..... Cassius Deamont, I should of killed him at CrossRoads, instead of letting him lay there, in a pool of his own crimson blood. Now.... now I have a welt on my fuckin head, and a tag partner I can’t turn my back on. “How’s the head feel?” Chip says with a sense of humor about it. O’Meara closes the door behind him as he enters into the hotel room of Chris Sloboda. Chris never breaks his glance from the window. “Oh, you think it’s funny?” Boda snarls at his boss. “You think that Orion pulling one over on us, and having that jackass Deamont knock me the fuck out with a steel chair......is funny?” Chris finally turns to look at Chip, who is standing there, reading some sort of paperwork. “No, that’s not it at all. It’s not that I find anything that Orion tries to do as funny.” Chip rebounds, almost to protect himself from a very angry Boda. “I was just reading this paperwork, and looking over the Dead on Arrival Tournament teamings. Look at it..... Timo and Marvelous.... hell, those two couldn’t work together if they were siamese twins. Myself and Orion.... come on.... like I’m a waste my time being partners with that Depends wearing old fart.” “And what about me and Cassius Deamont?” Boda questions. “You think, for one god damn minute...... that me and him are going to click? You think, after his attack on me last night, and what I did to him at CrossRoads.... that we can survive, to face each other for the World Title?” “Well.... I mean.....” Chip, for once, is speechless. “I think not.” Boda turns back to his position at the window. “Actually......” Chip smiles, dropping his paperwork on a table, and walking towards the Big Bad Boda Daddy. “Actually, I think you two can pull this off. Hear me out on this. Orion thinks he’s pretty smart.... hiring a man, with full blown hatred for you. Then, to team you two up, knowing that at a drop of a hat.... you two will destroy each other. BUT..... what if..... you and Cassius make it all the way to the PPV. Then blow up at each other in the main event. You, of course, will beat him. And FSW.... will crown their first World Champion..... much to the dismay, and back firing plans, of Paul Orion.” “I don’t know man......” Boda says doubtfully. “I owe Cassius alot of Boda style ass whoopings. And I don’t have the longest wick in the stack of dynamite.” “Okay, I see your point. But, do me a favor..... we need to make Orion pay for this shit he pulled last night.” Chip is almost childlike with glee. The mere thoughts of making others look bad, ruining the hopes of others, or just plain crashing someone's party, makes Chip ever more cocky, and happy. “Try to make it work. I mean, you’re Boda.....2 Time PCW World Champion. He’s Cassius Deamont...... Former RUSH Wrestling World Champion. The prospect of you two as a team..... blows the roof off anything PCW or OSW can throw at us. Boda vs Cassius, at the PPV... for the World Title....equals RATINGS. Do this for me. Do this for FSW, and dammit... do it to make yourself World Champion!” “Yeah.... what ever. I need to think about this for a bit.” Boda blows off the prospect of ratings, and championships. After all.....Cassius did far more to him than attack him with a Steel chair. Cassius Deamont cost Boda RUSH. Cassius turned on Boda, and threw away what would of made both of them the top game in RUSH. But, fuck it.... that’s now in the past, and Boda is all about making the future his. And, as far as Boda is concerned.... Cassius isn’t going to be in his future for long. “All right, well.... let me know what you think about this plan of mine” says Chip as he heads for the door to leave. “And, hey..... don’t worry big man..... Orion WILL get his. Let’s go eat” “I know.” Boda nods his head, as we see his reflection again in the window. Lighting crashes outside the window, but Boda seems to ignore it. “I’ll meet you downstairs in a bit.” And out the door goes Chip O’Meara. Boda doesn’t budge from his spot in front of the hotel window. Live with Cassius as my partner? Is he fucking crazy? Never before, has someone played me the way Cassius did. Ivan Stanislav didn’t use me and turn on me. Homicidal Anthony Cay never did. Jon Kano didn’t even do this to me. People don’t use me. I use people. That’s the way I’ve always done business. But little ole Cassius, in an attempt to make himself over, to make himself better, does what he wants. He uses who he wants. But I got news for Cass. He chose the wrong mind to mess with. I am a warrior. And a true warrior knows the battle is won before it even starts. I’ll let Cassius get away with this little victory..... because, I shall win the WAR. The scene draws to a close as Boda slowly pulls the drapes together, covering the window and blanketing the room in darkness.
