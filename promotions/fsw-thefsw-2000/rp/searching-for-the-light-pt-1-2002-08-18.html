Searching for the Light Pt. 1

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Day after Genesis The body of a large Asian man envelops that of Jason O’Neil. The portly, tall man stares at O’Neil lovingly, as he holds him close to him. He smiles and hugs him, finally letting O’Neil go to catch his breath. “Oh, Jason-san. Good to see you. How go wrestling career?” the man says, putting a large arm onto the shoulder of O’Neil. Standing at least 6’10, and weighing in the neighborhood of 600 lbs plus, this large, Asian man could snap O’Neil like a twig. But his gentle smile, and calm demeanor seems to fly in the face of what he once was. Takahiro Yamamoto was a former Yokozuna in Japanese Sumo wrestling. He retired at the age of 31, reaching a level of honor, and national pride, never before seen in sumo. Respected by all for his knowledge of not only sumo and Japanese culture, but he was a master gardener, a fantastic chef, and a wonderful lover. Or so it was told. “Good, Takahiro-san. I’ve come with questions for you.” O’Neil replies. “I was expecting. I saw TV the other night. You have questions about Sumo that was on TV with you?” Takahiro questions. “Come, step into dojo. We talk over tea.” The Scarecrow followed the large man through a simple glass sliding door. They walk through a small garden area. O’Neil notices a few vegetables growing on the ground, as well as quite a few bonsai trees. Takahiro opens a small door, and barely fits through as O’Neil follows. Inside the room is very plain. A few scrolls line the walls. Both men take off their shoes, and walk over to three scrolls on the right hand wall. They both bow, and pay homage to Takahiro’s former sensei, Fukui Kobeyuki. Takahiro stands, and leads O’Neil to a small table, with 2 small glasses, a teapot, and a candle. They both sit, cross legged, facing each other. Takahiro pours the first cup of tea, and holds it up to O’Neil, offering the guest in his home the first taste of tea. O’Neil accepts the glass, and drinks it. O’Neil does the same, offering the glass as a guest, to show his appreciation for Taka. Taka accepts. “Now, Jason-san. You have question about sumo. I do know that man is Kimbusa. Member of Yakuza.” Takahiro replies. “Yakuza, Taka-san?” “Hai. Similar to mafia in this country. Very well organized. Yet, these men have no honor.” “SO then, why is he with Steven Caldera of all people?” “Dunno. Perhaps he owe Caldera favor. Or maybe this Caldera way of trying to rid himself of you once and for all.” “I don’t follow, Taka-san.” “Back when you came to me first, you were under Caldera. You had potential, my friend. But Caldera could not stand to keep you happy. He try to do you in. He no like Jason. He know that Jason O’Neil pose big threat tho himself, so he no want to meet him face to face. He scared. He know that O’Neil be victorious. He have no honor.” “So what does that have to do with this Yakuza?” “You never been in ring with man so large, Caldera exploiting perceived weakness.” ”I see. So, essentially, Caldera’s sending this man to do his dirty work for him?” “Hai. That is way I see.” “Interesting. SO what should my plan of attack be, Taka-san?” “Caldera need to be center of attention. And just like dog who bark at fleas, once fleas are gone, dog stop barking.” O’Neil sits and ponders this statement for a moment. He pours two glasses of tea, and drinks from his cup. “So, you’re saying if I give Caldera ammunition, he’ll continue to come after me?” “Hai.” “But, if I basically ignore him, then like a fungus, he’ll go away?” “Hai, Jason-san. You got it. Caldera need attention. That’s why GCW was always Caldera TV. Without spotlight, he nothing.” “And since I’ve made a name for myself here, Caldera needs to get that spotlight back. So, he picks me, out of everyone.” “Hai. He always did have way to manipulate you, Jason-san.” “Yes, that he did.” “But, you will not be able to ignore Yakuza. He will be big trial for you. Yakuza spend many, many year learning many, many form of combat. He may be big man, but he move with grace. No matter how heavy, a butterfly is still butterfly.” “I understand, Taka-san.” “Tell me, Jason-san. Mentally, are you up to challenge? I saw how you got angry with young man at show. Can you handle all of this?” “I don’t honestly know, Taka-san. It’s just difficult to know who to trust these days. I’m in this new company, with these new people, and I’m just bombarded from all sides. I’m not used to being the guy that the fans cheer for. I’m used to being the guy they hate with a passion. It’s very new to me.” “Hai. But you handle it well. People need someone like them to look up to. You are champion for common man, and common cause. There is no greater honor, Jason-san.” “Maybe you’re right. I think I might have overreacted a little bit when it came to James. I was just so angry.” “What did you do, Jason-san?” “Here, let me show you.” Jason stands up, and offers a hand to his large counterpart. They stand and walk out the dojo, and through the garden, to where O’Neil has parked his rented Chevy Lumina. He pulls his keys from the front pocket of his jeans, and presses the trunk release button on the remote. Jason and Taka walk to the rear of the car. Taka peers into the trunk, with his small eyes, growing larger as he peers into the trunk and sees the body of James inside. “Oh, Jason-san. Is he dead?” Takahiro asks, in a tone showing no remorse. “Oh, I don’t know.” Jason slaps James on the head. James wakes with a start, and starts screaming through his gag. Jason looks at Taka who looks at Jason who looks back at James. James looks at the two large men, and faints. “Oh, Jason-san…”
