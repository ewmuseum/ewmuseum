Im Not Dead Yet!

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 1, 2002 Psych Ward. We open outside a very confined area. The building isn't so tall, but instead long and wide. The bricks are all painted with a shiny white coat, following a similar scheme all the way through. The camera takes a wide shot, showing off the oh so desirable barred windows. Little ways down the street, a small Volkswagon Beetle comes driving up. It is a shiny pinkish color with baby blue stripes going up the middle. The Beetle pulls up the entrance doors of the WARD. The driver's side door opens, with the engine still running. The door shuts, but it appears as though nobody had stepped out. Well, until the camera focused a bit lower, towards the ground. Standing outside the driver's door of the Beetle is a man roughly 2'11', IF that. He has dark skin and thick, dirty, and wavy hair. He's dressed in a pair of blue jean overalls with no shirt. Around his neck is a thick, solid gold chain which extends down to his waist area. At the end of the chain is a gold pendent of the letter 'J'. The little man walks around to the passenger side door and pulls open the handle. The passenger side door opens and out steps a seemingly troubled man. He's wearing a bag over his head, to cover his face. The paper bag has holes cut for his eyes and mouth. The short man takes the taller man's hand, 'Come come me freend.' The man with the paper bag on his head replies, 'Now what was your name again?' 'Jose,' the little man responds. 'I de brodder of Joseca.' 'Joseca? Who the hell is Joseca?' The man with the paper bag on his head seems stumped at the mention of Joseca's name. Joseca? Who can that be? 'Joseca is me brodder. We gone to look fo em. He missin.' Jose seems saddened at the missing location of his brother, Joseca. 'He and Chris rentta home es burn down.' At mention of the name Chris, the man with the paper bag on his head gains interest. 'Chris? Chris who?' Jose frowns, rather disgruntled that the man with the paper bag on his head isn't all caught up with the times. Jose sharply responds, 'Chris Bagwell...uh duh!' The man with the paper bag on his head nods his head, better understanding now. 'Well why are we here then,' he asks. Jose smiles and starts pulling him towards the doors, 'Let's worry about that later!' SHIFT. We're brought back to Chris Bagwell and his friend Joseca, whom he met during the third round of the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Bagwell is still carrying the nearly deceased Joseca over his shoulder. You can clearly see where he was being strangled by the lines across his neck. His neck is rashed up beyond belief and he still remains passed out. Bagwell nears a McDonalds restraunt. His mind goes into a sudden lapse. A flashback. FLASHBACK 'Joseca,' Chris says with fierce tenacidy. 'You've got to help me get a hold of him. Okay?' Joseca is silent for a moment. He stares back at Chris, completely motionless. Finally, he nods his head and replies, 'Otay.' Chris smiles, in attempt to raise Joseca's morale a little bit. 'Cheer up, buddy. I'll tell you what. I'm going to hop into the shower and when I'm finished, we'll go out for a bite to eat. Then we'll get cracking on finding Rob Jenks!' Joseca jumps up to his feet with a smile on his face, 'We go Mac Done-elds?' Chris laughs and agrees, 'Sure, if thats what you want.' He proceeds out of the room and towards the bathroom. Joseca looks around the room, realizing he's alone with nothing to do. He eyes out the television set, and waddles over to it. Joseca pushes in the POWER button rather hard, and the television set goes on. He smiles and waddles back over to a chair. FLASH FORWARD. Chris gives a little smile and walks up through the parking lot of the McDonalds. He contemplates going through the drive through window, however he doesn't quite have a car and isn't in the mood to argue with the workers. Realizing he can't just carry Joseca into the restraunt over his shoulder, he walks to a nearby bush. He drops Joseca into the bush, making him completely unnoticable. 'Perfect! Can't even see his little ass,' Chris says, proud of his idea. He turns and proceeds to make his way into the McDonalds. Inside, the serving line extends to the entrance door. Chris grunts and mumbles to himself, 'there ain't a chance in hell I'm waiting on this line.' 'Can I help who's next?' A very pretty young girl announces out loud for the next customer to be served. An elderly man slowly walks towards the counter, using his walking stick for support. Chris smirks and cuts the line. He shoves the little old man to the side. He loses his balance and falls to the ground, beginning to have compulsions. The register girl looks at the old man, and then back up at Chris. She smiles and says, 'Welcome to McDonalds. Can I take your order?' Chris smiles back, blatantly checking the girl out. He stares at her chest for a moment, admiring her voluptuous, nicely curved breasts. 'I'd like some breasts,' Chris realizes what he said and quickly tries to rephrase it, 'Chicken breasts, that is.' The register girl smiles and runs her eyes down Chris' body and back up. Suddenly, Chris feels a nudging against his leg. He peers down to the right, seeing the old man on the floor starting to get back up. Thinking it was the old man, Chris right sidekicks the little old man in the head, sending him back to the floor. More than likely unconcious! But I'm over it! Chris again feels the nudging, finally realizing it was at his left leg the entire time! Not the right! He glances to the left and low and behold who does he see? A tiny Mexican man, who has to pee! 'I gots to PEEEEE,' Joseca yells. The register lady points in the direction of the bathroom, 'Right over there, little guy.' Joseca waddles quickly in the direction of the bathroom. He walks right over the little old man on the floor and the old man lets out a dasterdly groan of pain. Chris quickly glances over to him, dropping a dirty look in his direction. He glances back at the register girl and says, 'So how about them breasts?' 'We don't have chicken breasts at McDonalds,' she replies, delivering a huge upset to Chris. 'YYYAAAAAWEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!' Joseca comes racing out of the bathroom, screaming in pain and agony. 'What the hell is up your ass,' Chris screams at him. Joseca turns around and bends over. Out pops a little plunger! Chris' eyes widen and his jaw drops 'Ouch!?!', he exlaims in question. Joseca continues to hop up and down, screaming in pain, 'Help-ah me!!' Puh-lease!' Chris smiles and responds, 'Now if only we had some expertise from the THINK TANK!' SHIFT. The camera cuts to 'the crew'. In other words, Tommy and Rolf. They're walking along the streets, looking for some extraordinary adventure to embark upon. After the invasion of Davidson's Estates, they're now on a constant mission for a fun challenge. Well, Tommy is. Rolf just enjoys killing animals. Anyway, Tommy and Rolf come across the McDonalds fast food restraunt where Chris Bagwell and Joseca were last seen entering. Tommy turns to Rolf and asks, 'You hungry?' Rolf looks at Tommy and shrugs, not saying a word. Tommy, hungry himself, asks again showing persistance, 'Well we can get something to eat if you want. It doesn't bother me.' Rolf stares at Tommy for a moment, realizing that Tommy just wants to eat. Rolf shakes his head no. Tommy grows slightly upset but tries to hide it. 'Well, Rolf, I know you will be hungry later. So lets just go in now and get a bite to eat.' Tommy walks to the door. He looks back at Rolf, who just shrugs. Rolf then goes to follow. Tommy turns back towards the entrance door, and gets smacked in the face with it. 'BASTARD!' he yells. Out waddles little Joseca! Right behind him is Chris, with a big red hand mark across his face. 'CHRIS!' Tommy shows great excitement to see Chris, after hearing of a possible death in his burning rent-a-home. Chris holds his face and doesn't say much back to Tommy. 'Yeah.' Tommy then looks down at Joseca. Joseca quickly hides behind Chris, tightly grabbing his leg. 'Whats up with the midget, Chris,' Tommy asks. 'He wan eat me,' Joseca says referring to Rolf. Tommy and Chris glance up at Rolf, who is licking his lips while staring steadily at Joseca. Together, they laugh. Tommy decides to bring up mention of the red mark across Chris' face. 'Get hit or something?' Chris grins, 'Yeah, by the bitch inside. I feel over laughing at Joseca and accidently landed on her tit.' 'Accidently,' Tommy asks suspiciously. Chris just gives a huge smile. 'Ahh well,' he says. 'I've got bigger things to worry about. A THINK TANK, to be more specific.' 'I've heard,' Tommy replies, 'Porter and Kriegman in Five Star, huh?' Chris just nods his head in compliance. Tommy continues, 'You've been through a helluva lot lately.' Chris nods again in compliance, 'Well I'm not dead YET!' Fade.
