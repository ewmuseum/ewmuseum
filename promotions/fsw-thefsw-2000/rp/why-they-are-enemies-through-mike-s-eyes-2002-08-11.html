Why They Are Enemies...Through Mike's Eyes.

11 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-11-02 [Scene opens in a small bar and lounge called Rod's Bar and Grille. Mike Marvelous walks through the door and is stopped by the door attendant] Door Attendant: Umm...sir, you have to wait to be seated. Mike Marvelous: That’s ok...I’m meeting someone... Door Attendant: Oh...sorry. [Mike walks past the attendant and into the maze of tables and stools. He stops at one particular table and sits down. The person sitting across from him is non other than his arch nemesis, Timo Bolamba! They both eye each other with deep, unsettling hate] Timo Bolamba: So, I see you did decide to show up. So why did you choose this place anyhow? The atmosphere is rather...odd. Mike Marvelous: Big D recommended it to me...he said it had free bread with the meal [Timo shrugs his shoulders and looks at the menu. Mike eyes him from the corner of his eye as he reads his own menu. He doesn’t trust him. Not one bit.] Timo Bolamba: Free bread is always a good thing. Mike Marvelous: Yes...yes it is. [Timo clears his throat and Mike jumps, a bit startled. When he realizes Timo isn’t up to anything, he begins to speak.] Mike Marvelous: They should give us free drinks too for the price of this food...sheesh, 16 bucks for a steak! They don't have ridiculous prices like this up in Canada... Timo Bolamba: Out in Samoa, we just carve what we want off the steak and ride the rest home. [Mike looks strangely at Timo] Timo Bolamba: What? Okay, well maybe not, but I have always wanted to try. Mike Marvelous: Hey...I thought you Samoans only ate...you know...raw meat. Timo Bolamba: Raw? No man, they only do that on Somata, which is the island like an uncle nobody talks about. Regardless, I think we have something else to talk about here, so why don’t we get on with it? [Mike smirks and folds his napkin, glad that Timo is finally cutting to the chase] Mikey Marvelous: Yes, it seems we do have something to talk about...my title shot at Genesis for one' [Mike gives Timo the eye and Timo replies with an obnoxious snort] Timo Bolamba: YOUR title shot? I think you are mistaken my friend. You see, when it was all said and done I was the one with the arm raised, not you. Mike Marvelous: What are you talking about Bojumba!? I had my arm raised by the official referee then Canadian Criminal, a man who had no right to be there, ran in and raised your arm! So therefore, I WON! Tomo Bolamba: Bojumba? I'll Bojumba you MarveLOSER! [Timo stands up and grabs a loaf of Italian bread, swinging it in the air in a menacing manner. Just then, the waiter walks up and nervously steps in between Timo and Mike. He glances around then pipes up.] Waiter: Hey, can I get you fellas anything? [Timo drops the bread and puts his hands innocently behind his back then sits down. Mike drops the butter knife that he had held behind his back and folds his hands, smiling politely at the waiter] Mike Marvelous: Yes, I’ll have the moose steak with a baked potato, please. Waiter: And for you sir? [Timo looks down and hastily orders.] Timo Bolamba: Umm, I'll have the Buffalo Wings, a Smirnoff Ice, and fries instead of a baked potato. Suck that you Canadian Ponce! Mike Marvelous: And waiter...can you get my friend here a bib? I'm sure he eats like a pig, being from Samoa and all! Timo Bolamba: Hey, I told you Marvelous, I only ate out of the trough on a dare! Mike Marvelous: Haw haw...anway, can you bring me a glass of sprite too, waiter, thank you.' [The waiter turns around and leaves after writing down the order. Mike glares at Timo and frowns] Mike Marvelous: Did you have to be so rude in front of the waiter!? [Timo reaches for the French Bread with his right hand, with a menacing look in his eyes. Mike notices the glare Timo is giving him and becomes uneasy] Mike Marvelous: Timo...why are you looking at me like that...? [Without a word, the bread swings forth, toward Mike's unsuspecting head. The bread catches Mike's left temple and breaks in half with a loud crunch. Crumbs and spit go flying as Mike's head snaps to the right. He grabs his head in pain] Mike Marvelous: OUCH! Did you just hit me with a loaf of bread!?! Timo Bolamba: Who's the monkey now?! Mike Marvelous: GAAAAAH! [Mike lunges across the table and begins choking Timo with both hands as the glasses and utensils rattle over the table and the cover almost falls off Timo reaches up and pokes for Mikes eyes with the classic Rowdy Roddy Piper eye gouge. Mike's head arches back and he let's out a howl of pain. He immediately reaches for a glass of water and throws the contents into Timo's face] Mike Marvelous: Take that, you louse! [Timo runs around in circles rubbing his face, making a scene and knocking over tables, chairs and whatever else gets in his way.] Timo Bolamba: I'M MELTING…MELTING!!!! [Mike gets up out of his seat with a concerned look on his face. He walks slowly but cautiously over to Timo, who is writhing on the ground, and taps him on the shoulder] Mike Marvelous: Are...are you ok? I didn't mean to— [Timo stops making a scene and swings for the Royal Crown Jewels of Indonesia, striking Mike dead on and watching him drop like a bad pick up line from Big Daveyliscious' mouth. Timo stands up and watches Mike writhe in pain.] Timo Bolamba: Man, you sure don't give a guy much of a target. [Timo begins to laugh as Mike twitches on the ground, but in his laughter, he doesn't notice Marvelous recover...and he doesn't notice the boot rocketing directly for his genital area. Timo suddenly feels the force of his unit being crushed, and he falls on the ground faster than Tim Walden. The Samoan rolls around, clutching his main vein, cursing in Samoan.] Mike Marvelous: I learned that in Nam! [Mike springs to his feet, grabs a chair, holds it over his head and is about to bring it down on the Samoan Silencer when...the waiter walks up with the food. He looks awkwardly at the two men trying to kill each other in the middle of a lounge. He places the food on the table and walks backwards with a frightened look on his face] Waiter: Uuuh...here you go, enjoy your meals... [Timo and Mike stop brawling as they get a whiff of the food on the table] Timo Bolamba: Hey…….those buffalo wings look tasty… [Mike and Timo both scramble towards the table and jump on their seats. They begin eating their food, but as Timo chews the meat off the bones and Mike cuts off chunks of his steak, they never take their eyes off each other. They both stare at each other suspiciously.] Timo Bolamba: These are some good wings.... Mike Marvelous: Yes...and this is a fine steak...could you pass the salt? [Mike grins mischeivously at Timo. Timo reaches for the salt and has a moment of inspiration.] Timo Bolamba: Look Mike, an eagle! [Mike quickly whips his head back to see America's official bird as Timo unscrews the top of the salt shaker just far enough, then waits for Mike to turn back.] Mike Marvelous: I don't see any eagle. Timo Bolamba: My mistake…friend… [Timo glares mischievously as he hands mike the tampered salt.] Timo Bolamba: Here is that salt you wanted. [Mike looks at the salt, then looks at Timo, then reluctantly accepts.] Mike Marvelous: Thank you. It appears you actually have manners. I must say, I'm impressed. [Mike goes to sprinkle a couple of dashes of salt on his steak and potato when the top falls off and a shower of salt falls on top of the meal, forming a white pile. Mike looks at his now-ruined meal, then at Timo, and his face begins to turn red.] Timo Bolamba: Enjoy your meal, Mike. [Marvelous lets out a long sigh, picks up the steak, and brushes some of the salt off. Timo shakes his head, smiling, then begins eating his wings again. As he eats, he sees the reflection of Mike in his glass raising his fork in the air to throw it. Timo looks up just in time to see the fork flying straight at his head, but he bends backwards Matrix style and the fork whizzes by and sticks in some lady's purse.] Mike Marvelous: Damn! I missed! Timo Bolamba: Are you crazy you idiot? You could poke someone's eye out with that thing! [Mike grits his teeth together] Mike Marvelous: Well, you shouldn't have dodged it, then! [Timo tries to compose himself a bit, and calms down, he looks over and holds his hands up in truce. Suddenly, he seems to notice something he likes and he speaks up.] Timo Bolamba: Say Mike, that's a nice pair of pants. [A smile replaces Mike's frown and he looks down at his pants in self-admiration] Mike Marvelous: Really? Yeah, I sort of like them too. I got em' from JC Penny's. Timo Bolamba: Yeah? What are those made of? They look comfortable. Mike Marvelous: They are comfortable! They're 100% Polyester! [Timo continues his analysis, undaunted in his quiz of the pants.] Timo Bolamba: Polyester? You must have paid a pretty penny for them. Mike Marvelous: Yup...for these particular pants it was 60 dollars Canadian. [Timo's eyes light up, smiling a bit. He nods and eats a bit of bread. The Samoan points and seems he has a question.] Timo Bolamba: I have one more question for you. Mike Marvelous: Uh...ask away. [Timo gets a shit-faced grin.] Timo Bolamba: Do they make them for men? [The smile suddenly drops off Mike's face. The big vein in his forehead begins to bulge out and his left eye begins to twitch. Timo can't help but chuckle to himself as Mike secretly pictures Timo being eaten alive by wild muskrats in his head.] Mike Marvelous: You think you're soooo funny. Well...I got news for you.....you're....NOT! Timo Bolamba: I thought it was pretty funny. [Timo laughs to himself, then seems like he has something else to say.] Timo Bolamba: You know, i saw you and Jake Jackson walking in the hall the other day and it reminded me of a Raisin Bran commercial. [A puzzled look comes over Mike's face.] Mike Marvelous: What the heck are you talking about? [Timo chuckles to himself again as he thinks about it.] Timo Bolamba: Two Scoops of Fruit!! [Mike suddenly snaps and pounds his fist on the table.] Mike Marvelous: I don't know why I ever agreed to meet a savage like you in a public place! I don't know...maybe it was wishful thinking that you'd recognize that I pinned you and I deserve the title shot at Genesis. Well, it looks like I was wrong! [Timo stops laughing and sighs a bit. He looks over at Mike and nods, again holding up the truce hands.] Timo Bolamba: Okay, you are right Mike, here I am making a fool out of you, and you came out here on your own will. Tell you what, I'm sorry, have a roll or something so you don't get hungry. They really are quite exquisite. [He reaches for a Tabasco laden roll and offers it to Mike, straight faced. Mike accepts the roll and lets out a long sigh.] Mike Marvelous: I'm glad that you've decided to quit your childish ways so we can get down to business. [Mike brings the roll up to his lips and takes a bite. He chews it a couple of times, but instead of the normal expression of bliss worn by regular roll eaters, he has a look of wide eyed horror. His eyes begin to water and his cheeks begin to suck in as the Tobasco sauce works it's magic. Sweat begins to trickle down his brow. Timo breaks out laughing.] Timo Bolamba: Hey MARVELoser...hot enough for ya? [Mike suddenly spits out the roll and dumps his glass of sprite down his throat, but it doesn't do much to help.] Mike Marvelous: WA-HER! WA-HER! I EED WA-HER! [Timo jumps up and cups his hands to his mouth.] Timo Bolamba: Quick, someone, this man needs help, can someone get him a drink?' [A tall man dressed in a flamboyant shirt turns around, and its apparent Timo has figured out where they are. The man walks over and wraps his arm around Mike, then speaks with a high girly voice, complete with a lisp.] Gay Man: I can buy you a drink sexy. [Mike looks at the strange man with a look of cock-eyed confusement and pain, then it suddenly dons on him. There is not a woman in this place. The woman who's purse the fork got stuck in is actually a man dressed up in women's clothing wearing a wig. The male bartender is dressed in a white tank top. Mike quickly backs away from the gay man trying to buy him a drink, and suddenly, he forgets about his burning mouth] Mike Marvelous: EEEEEEEK! THIS IS A.....A....A.... Gay Man: Really happenin' place! [As Timo laughs at the plight, he feels a hand grab his ass, he turns around to see a 6'8' 290 lb. black man standing behind him and his smile instantly drops.] Gay Man #2: Hey there, you feel like some Alabama Blacksnake? Timo Bolamba: Oh Dear God. [Timo looks at Mike, Mike looks at Timo and they both come to one mutual agreement with their eyes: They have GOT to get out of this place!] Mike and Timo: EEEEEEEEE!!!! [Both men run out of the lounge without even paying the bill. Two very gay bouncers stand in their way, but they are easily dispatched by a Canadian Quick Kick and a Cave-In. They both hit the doors and book down the street with a mob of horny flaming gays after them in hot pursuit.] Timo Bolamba: Run Mike, Run for your life….and your Sweet Virgin Ass! Mike Marvelous: Oh God! Oh God! I want my mommy! [Timo looks over his shoulder and notices they have left the pack of homosexual men far behind them. He stops with a huff and puff, as does Mike and they look at one another. Timo is the first to speak.] Timo Bolamba: I think its safe to say we should never speak of this incident again. [Mike doesn't bother answering, but rather nods in agreement and wipes a drip of sweat off his brow. Timo looks over at Mike and makes sure nobody has followed them. He makes a sour face and Mike looks at him trying to figure out what he is doing.] Mike Marvelous: What's the problem? [Timo hesitates to answer.] Mike Marvelous: What the hell did you do now? Timo Bolamba: You remember how we parked at the bar? [Mike's face droops.] Mike Marvelous: Yeah… Timo Bolamba: When you weren't looking I let all the air out of your tires. [Mike, too tired to get angry, simply bows his head and lets out a moan.] Timo Bolamba: Tell you what, lets go back, I will give you a ride, it's the least I can do. Mike Marvelous: ...Ok. Bloody decent of you. [They get back to the car and the gay men come storming out again, horny for butt loving. Timo quickly starts the car and as Mike opens the door Timo shouts to him.] Timo Bolamba: Remember calling me Rotunda? [Mike looks at him like 'Now is not the time', but he persists] Mike Marvelous: It was Bojumba, but why does that matter? [Timo looks back nervously and then back to Mike.] Timo Bolamba: Well Mikey, in this business, sometimes a wrestler needs to pay the piper…and that wrestler is you! [He hammers the gas, and the door rips from Mikes hand. Timo speeds away, leaving Mike trying to get into his car, which has gum jammed into the keyholes. Mike looks over his shoulder before running from the gay mob and screaming into the night… Mike Marvelous: TIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIMMMMMMMMMMMMOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!! [Scene fades to black as Timo watches Mike trip over the curb in his rear view mirror, and bursts out laughing as the image of Mike let’s out a shriek of horror as the ass posse descends upon him. Shards of clothing go flying through the air as 50 gay hands claw away at Marvelous. Mike’s arm sticks out of the mob, reaching for something to grab to escape, but the arm is quickly pulled down, never to be seen again]
