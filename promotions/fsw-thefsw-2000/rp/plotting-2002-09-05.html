Plotting.

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sometime between tomorrow and yesterday “So, how did it go?” A simple question, and one that would normally lead to a simple and straightforward answer. However this situation has turned out to be far more than simple, and things are turning out differently than expected. “Not really sure Baby. I just got done having my first real conversation with Kimbusa. He wasn’t what I expected by any means. More than anything, he seems nothing more than a big dumb animal. That could be a good thing, or a bad thing. I’ve got to figure out how I’m going to proceed with him, carefully. If I make the wrong move, the bastards liable to turn around and savage me worse than he did O’Neil. Caldy’s got a big job on his hands trying to keep that monster on a short leash.” The speaker is none other than The Evolution of Pro Wrestling himself, Michael “Spitfire” Stevens. He reclines lazily on a white overstuffed couch, in what is obviously a luxury hotel room. The rooms décor is modest and elegant, done all in muted shades of white, and light blue. Everything in the room is carefully designed, carefully placed, to express the central theme of the entire room. To even the most untrained eye, this room speaks, and its words are clear, and concise “only the most wealthy are welcome,”. While it would seem impossible, the room almost seems to be alive, holding itself in the highest regard. This room, bares silent testament to the class system in America, for without rooms like these there would be no way to tell who is best, who owns the most. Looking into the room, one can easily imagine it holding the most noble elite of our society dining and laughing it its hallowed stillness. The very air seems to conjure imagines of expensive tuxedos, and elegant dresses of the fashionable upper crust. And yet there’s something out of place in the room. One small but important piece of this puzzle is completely wrong, and destroys everything about the room, destroys all its dignity, all its haughtiness. That one piece, is the inhabitant, Stevens himself, dressed in his transformers tee-shirt that must be four sizes too small, and his ripped and duct taped blue jeans. Even his hair rages against this system, a bright shock of vibrant red against the achingly white background, gel-spiked into a chaotic mess that obliterates the prefect order the room would have otherwise achieved, into sheer chaos. And yet while he does not fit, what makes this man seem so at home in the world of riches he currently inhabits? Perhaps it’s the way he holds himself, for even in his lounging position, his baring is confident, dignified. He holds himself in such away that makes him seem secure anywhere he goes, where it be confidence or arrogance is uncertain. The only certainty is the obvious, Stevens is here, in this place, and he doesn’t appear to be leaving anytime soon. Seemingly from out of the air, comes the questioning voice. It seems sweet, innocent, almost to a fault. The words seem to ooze with a sweetness bordering on sickening. This is the voice of a young woman, almost a girl, but one who does everything in such a way as to deceive the world. The voice of a woman that uses a disguise of innocence to get whatever she wants. “Can you use him?” “Don’t be stupid Sheena, of course I can. I’ve just got to figure out the best way to go about doing it. I’ve got to work a little harder, get into the beasts head, figure out exactly what makes him tick. I’m not worried about it though, even animals can be taught simple tricks, and that’s exactly what I’m going to have to do. Still don’t think for a second I’m not going to make arrangements of my own, to insure our victory. I’m not going to let that fat bastard’s lack of a workable brain stop me form advancing towards MY world title.” “What about your competition? Aren’t Boda and Deamont pretty good?” “Separately, perhaps. I’d even go so far as to say that Boda himself could be in the same league as myself, if you can believe it. He’s got all the tools to survive, he’s one of the fit. Cassius, well he’s got some talent too. But the fact of the matter is they’ll never be able to co-exist. They hate each other, and that’s the key. As long as I make sure that all their hatred for each other boils to the surface, I can easily capitalize on that weakness. I might not like Kimbusa too much, but at least I can be assured he’s going to go out with the sole purpose of destroying someone, and that’s easy enough to use to my advantage. The shaved gorilla even went so far as to say he doesn’t care about titles and he’ll leave all that stuff to me just so long as he can get the opportunity to rip some people apart. That works fine for me as long as I can keep him pointed at my enemies, the weak, I’ll be fine. I don’t see any reason I should dirty my own hands on the knuckle dragging cretins that populate FSW, when I can have Kimbusa whip them out for me and reap the morsels of victory from his reign of carnage.” “That’s great, but you better know what you’re getting into with him Mike. You said yourself he’s nearly uncontrollable. Listen, I should go. I need to finish up a few things out here in California before I can get down to St. Louis. Bye bye, and I love you.” “Yeah, sure bye Sheena, ditto.” His hand glides across the couch, to push a bottom on the phone located on an end table. After a moment he stands, with an easy confidence, he glides across the room, to a small wet bar located near a set of glass double doors leading out onto the balcony. As he pours himself a drink, he begins to let his mind wander, onto the task at hand and, exactly how he must achieve his goals. “I’ve got to figure out how to turn all this to my advantage. I don’t trust Kimbusa to respect how important all this is. With the World title I’ll have the power I need to make some changes, make the world safe for people like me. I think its obvious I’ve got to get Boda’s ear, I need to try and convince him that it’s going to be more to his advantage to aid Kimbusa and myself in destroying his partner than doing something crazy like trying to win this one for himself. Might be difficult, he’s brighter than he looks, although it’d be next to impossible to NOT be brighter than he looks. But fuck it, I’ve got o make sure he doesn’t go out there to win.” With a glass in front of him now full almost to brim with expensive Scotch, Stevens, our Evolutionary role model turns on his heel. Stepping outside on to the balcony, he lets the slightly chill air sink into his bones. The night is dark, and the stars are nearly invisible thanks to the lights of the St. Louis night. A light breeze blows, adding to the chill of the night. To steel himself against the cold, he gulps the fiery liquor. A sigh of pleasure escapes his lips, as he finishes the glass in another gulp. From his high vantage point Stevens can see half of downtown St. Louis. Only one word can describe the look in the eyes of our young role model as he looks over the city, hunger. Hunger to go and enjoy the world at large, hunger to exploit the masses that are so far below him. “Oh what the hell, I’ll figure something out. I always do, that’s what makes me so goddamn great. Those people down there, so small and insignificant, blissfully unaware that their future stands above them. I think its time I paid them all a little visit, used them as only I can. So what if a few people get hurt, broken, I couldn’t care less. They’re scum, their only purpose is to lift men like myself up. Yes, tonight I’ll walk among the troglodytes, I might even force a few of them to stare evolution in the face. It’s gonna to be a good night.”
