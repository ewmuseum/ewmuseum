-GTT- Warming Up (v Foamy)

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Listen to me, you son of a bitch! If you call me one more time, I’ll see to it that you and everyone you know spend the rest of your lives in a cardboard box! Never call me again!” Kimbusa’s drooping eyes open slightly, reacting to the noise. The shouting was coming from the front room of his apartment, which made little sense. There was nobody else in the apartment with him… who could have made their way inside? “Jesus Christ. I’m getting sick of this.” Someone is definitely here. And Kimbusa knows exactly who it is. Slightly annoyed by the early morning intrusion, Kimbusa rises to a sitting position, rubbing the sting of the rising sun’s light from his eyes. He sighs as the distinctive ringing of a cellular phone sounds in the main room beyond, followed quickly by an annoyed voice. “Damnit! Hello? I’m sorry, he’s not interested. No… NO! Goodbye! For the love of…” Kimbusa slowly rolls his way out of bed, and stops to allow his mind time to begin functioning. He feels surprisingly well rested, and as a result has actually accepted the apartment as home for the time being. He had not yet ventured out into the city, despite Caldera’s insistence. But the apartment was acceptable, perhaps even comfortable. The bed had been set up with silk sheets and down pillows, on a mattress that Caldera had been quick to point out had set him back a few dollars. Kimbusa would have been content to sleep on the plush carpeted floor of the bedroom, but after a few nights in the bed, he began to realize that this investment in his comfort had been doing wonders. He hadn’t felt so rested in months, years… His sleep had been peaceful and deep, and as a result he felt completely refreshed, and perhaps a bit anxious to get on with the tournament. Another ring. “Shit!” Clad in his red silk pajama pants, Kimbusa slowly shuffles his way out of the room, grooming his jet black hair with his hands on the way. The hallway leading out of his room and towards the front of the apartment is short but decorative nonetheless; Caldera had done him the favor of having some rather peaceful nature photography hung on the walls, which Kimbusa had taken a liking to immediately. He finally makes his way out into the main living area, where Steven Caldera is pacing rapidly. His tie is loosened, his jacket thrown carelessly over the recliner. His cell phone is pressed to his ear again, as he speaks quickly and angrily. “Didn’t I just tell you never to call me again? Ha! Yes, a million is a lot of money. No, he is not going to be bought. Go to hell. You heard me.” Caldera flips his phone closed again, and sighs heavily. “Well, welcome to the real world, my friend. These jackals have been calling me all morning.” Kimbusa shuffles towards the back of the room, where a small bar has been set up. Expensive bottles of champagne and hard liquor occupy the racks, but instead Kimbusa selects a shot glass from the counter, and fills it with water from the nearby tap. “The media?” he inquires before sipping his warm water. “Yes, the goddamned media. Your little confessional has leaked out somehow. Everyone wants the story on Kimbusa now. ABC just offered us a million bucks for the exclusive. I told him to go to hell,” Caldera states with an exasperated voice, before moving over to lean himself against the wall. “I heard that,” Kimbusa mutters, while refilling his shot glass. “Why don’t you simply turn your phone off?” Caldera, his face buried in his hands, looks up at Kimbusa in a state of desperation. He obviously has not slept in a while, and seems to have been on the road all night. His hair is messy and matted, his clothes wrinkled and most likely two days old. “I can’t turn it off. I have to wait for the airport to call to let us know when the jet gets here.” Kimbusa swallows another shot of warm water, and returns the shot glass to its proper place. “We leave for the matches this morning?” Caldera chuckles slightly, before heading over towards the recliner. He slumps himself down in the chair and kicks it back, sighing with contentment. “We need to get you into the loop, my friend. You couldn’t tell me what your opponent’s name is, much less what day the match is. You’re going to have to know these things.” Kimbusa grins slightly, and begins walking back towards his bedroom. “His name is the Ninth Wonder of Foam. And the match is tonight.” Caldera, shocked, slowly sits up and turns to face Kimbusa. But the big man has already disappeared down the hall to change. Caldera smiles with glee, as he slumps back down in the chair to relax for a few moments. Kimbusa’s duffel bag rests in the corner next to the door, where he had prepared for the trip the night before. His confession to Caldera had done something to him, as if letting it out in the open had helped ease the struggle. Further, Caldera’s sensitivity during the ordeal had opened Kimbusa’s mind to his world, the world of sports entertainment. Kimbusa had done a bit of research, and found that Caldera had left most of his information behind. Kimbusa had flipped through Caldera’s scout reports of this Foam gentleman, and was fascinated. He hadn’t cared much for the detailed findings, as he considered Caldera’s in-depth preparations a waste of time. Nonetheless, it had helped to see the Ninth Wonder of Foam that night in a photograph rather than in the ring for the first time. Kimbusa slides into a pair of wool pants and a nice white shirt, before heading back out to the room. Caldera appears to be sleeping, a rather vulnerable state that Kimbusa seems to find amusing. However, his cell phone rings again, and Caldera jumps up, flipping it open. “What?!” Kimbusa braces for another verbal tirade from Caldera, but it doesn’t come. Caldera simply flips the phone shut, and turns back to Kimbusa. The big man is standing in the doorway with his duffel bag, ready to go. Caldera smiles. “It’s time.”
