Caarazeee Taxi

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: meh :: The scene opens and we see an overhead shot of a city. The sky is a light blue. The sun is beating down on the tiny scene below. It is a hot and humid day. The type of day that puts people into bad moods. From this shot we notice the various buildings and landmarks that make this city what it is. We are not sure what city it is but it is clearly well populated. The tiny cars moving up and down the streets are unintentionally hypnotic. This scene is just another day in the city, quite, calm… Voice: TAAAAAXIIII!!!!!!!!! ::The scene quickly cuts to a sidewalk in this busy city. Along this sidewalk are many shops. There is a coffee shop, a comic book store, a hair salon, a bar, and some high class clothing stores. The sidewalk is littered with paper, cans, and the occasional soiled piece of cloth. This very scene is almost repeated across the street on the other side of road, with the difference being that the stores are different… Voice: TAAAAAXIIII!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! :: The camera cuts to an irate man. The man is wearing a pair of black Levi shorts, with a plain black T-shirt. We recognize the man as none other than Six Finger Cisco.:: Cisco: TAAAAAXIII!!!!!!!!!!!!!! :: Cisco is flaying his arms widely at anything that looks remotely like a taxi that drives by.:: Cisco: This sucks. :: Cisco looks at his watch. It reads 10:15am.:: Cisco: Oh shit! Only 15 minutes left! Cisco: TAAAAAXIIII!!!!!!!!!!! :: Suddenly a cab pulls over to the curb.:: Cisco: (Looking up to the sky) Thank you! :: Cisco opens the door and hops in the cab. Inside it can be described as…well…a cab…with a blue interior. The driver is a man about 5’5, 300lbs. He is Italian, black hair, heavy accent, and an attitude to boot. He is wearing a plain white shirt with pit stains, and a pair of khaki pants.:: Driver: Where to bucko? Cisco: McDonald’s. Step on it. I have less than 15 minutes before they stop serving those kick-ass Sausage McMuffins Driver: Yeah. Isn’t that weird? Why stop serving breakfast at 10:30am? I mean, who wants a burger at 10:31am? Cisco: (annoyed) No one. Now can we go please. Driver: Yeah, yeah. Cool your jets, I’m going. :: The cab pulls into traffic and begins heading to the nearest McDonald’s.:: Driver: (Looking at Cisco through the rear-view mirror) So you like those McMuffins eh? Cisco: (Trying to ignore the Driver) Yeah. Driver: You know, those things aren’t too good for ya. Cisco: Yeah, I know. Driver: So why do you eat them? Cisco: Well, because I am a pro-wrestler and I work out all the time. A little fat intake here and there doesn’t hurt. Driver: You’re a wrestler?! Damn! You’re awfully scrawny for a wrestler! Cisco: Dude, I weigh 250. I’m 5’11. What the hell are you talking about? Driver: See. You’re overweight. Cisco: You just said that I was scrawny. Driver: You know what you need to do to take off that weight? Cisco: (Under his breath) Yeah, not listen to your fat ass. Driver: Excuse me? Cisco: I said, here we are, stop the cab. Driver: Oh geez. Time flies eh? Cisco: (Under breathe) About as much as you do tubby… Driver: It’s gonna be $20. Cisco: $20! For what? You drove me like for 5 seconds! Driver: Yup. 4 bucks a second pal. Cisco: Fine. :: Cisco reaches into his pocket and finds that all he has on him is a ten. Cisco now gets out of the cab and is leaning over to talk to the driver through his window.:: Cisco: Shit! …can you help me out? I only have 10, can I pay you back tomorrow or something like that? Driver: Come on. Whattya, think I’m stupid Cisco: What? Driver: You brag about being a rich pro-wrestler, you’re on your way to buy breakfast, and you mean to tell me all you have is 10 bucks? Cisco: Yeah, I must have grabbed the wrong bill off of my dresser or something. Driver: Okay. How bout this? You pay me, or I take the rest of the bill outta your ass. Cisco: What? Are you fucking crazy? Driver: You’re damn right boy. Cisco: Bring it on fat boy. It would be a pleasure to kick your ass. Driver: Yeah?! :: The driver reaches down in his seat and pulls out a metal baseball bat. He gets out of the car and is ready to attack Cisco. Cisco jumps back.:: Cisco: Whoa, whoa, whoa. Hold it now buddy. Don’t do anything crazy. :: Cisco puts up his right hand as if to hold back the driver. The driver drops the bat.:: Driver: What the hell is that?! Cisco: What? Driver: Is that a sixth finger? :: Cisco looks at his right hand. Looks back at the driver and smiles.:: Cisco: Yeah. Driver: That’s weird. Cisco: Scared! :: Cisco moves his hand towards the driver but the driver doesn’t budge.:: Driver: No, but how the hell did that happen? Cisco: Inbreeding parents spawned this thing. Driver: Inbreeding parents? Where the hell you from? Texas? Cisco: (sigh) Yeah. :: The driver looks real sad all of a sudden.:: Cisco: What the hell’s the problem? Driver: Promise you won’t laugh? Cisco: … :: The driver takes off his shoe followed by his black sock to reveal a 6 toed foot!:: Cisco: OH. MY. GOD. Driver: I know! My parents were distant cousins too! :: The driver sounds like he is going to cry.:: Cisco: Sorry man. I know how you feel. :: Cisco wiggles his sixth finger as he says this sentence.:: Driver: All my life I’ve been ridiculed by people for this extra toe. Cisco: Me and you are a lot alike. Driver: Yeah. Cisco: So I guess you can call the bill off eh? :: The driver clears his throat and gets serious.:: Driver: I’m afraid I can’t do that son. Cisco: What? Come on. Can’t I do something? Can I do an hour of your shift or something like that? :: The driver smiles.:: Driver: You! A cab driver! I don’t think so. Cisco: Just because I speak English doesn’t mean I can’t drive a cab. Driver: If you really want to, I’m willing to let you work off your fare. Cisco: Lets do it. Driver: Okay. You come back with my cab here in one hour. If you can make 40 dollars or more in one hour then we’ll call it even. Cisco: I only owe you 10 dollars, and you want me to make you 40? Driver: That’s the deal. Cisco: Fine. :: Cisco takes the keys from the driver and gets into the driver’s side of the cab. The driver comes over and leans in the window.:: Driver: Hey…don’t tell anyone about that extra toe, will ya? Cisco: It’s nothing to be ashamed of. Besides, I’m in the same boat as you. Driver: Thanks, I’m glad you understand. See you in one hour. :: The driver heads into McDonalds and Cisco rolls up the window on the cab.:: Cisco: Fucking Freak! :: Cisco makes the proper adjustments to the rear-view mirror and seat.:: Cisco: Fucking Italians! Cisco: Fucking Everybody! Cisco: Who the hell does mob rule think they are? :: Cisco starts the engine and puts the car in drive. Cisco then pulls the car out onto the congested street and begins his journey to find some customers.:: Cisco: Cost me my contender’s match will ya? Fuck you Nelson. Fuck you Deville. Fuck you Ming. Fuck you Chase. And Fuck you Boda! :: Cisco honks the horn at a Dodge neon that cuts quickly in front of him. Cisco then screams out the driver’s side window.:: Cisco: Hey and Fuck you too asshole! :: Cisco’s road rage continues to build until he spots a young man dressed in business attire flagging him down. Cisco slowly pulls the cab up to the curb and the young man opens the back door and gets in the backseat of the cab.:: :: Cisco tries to act professional, looking over his right shoulder and calmly asking…::: Cisco: Where to boss? Young man: 5th avenue. :: Cisco giggles. The young man is confused and somewhat angered by this.:: Young man: Is something wrong? :: Cisco is still giggling.:: Cisco: No, no…but I’m not sure where that it is….is 5th avenue on the corner of Hershey way? Or no, no…it’s off of Butterfinger drive…nope…that’s not it…it’s the first left off of Reese’s St…right? :: Cisco giggles.:: Young Man: What are you fucking retarded? :: Cisco stops laughing and tries to explain to the young man what was so funny to him.:: Cisco: 5th avenue?…. Young Man: Yeah? So? Cisco: It’s a candy bar! :: Cisco stares with a blank smile at the young man.:: Young Man: Whatever. Just get me there. It’s right off of Sumner Ave. :: Cisco gets serious and pulls out onto the congested street. Cisco looks in the rear-view mirror and begins questioning the man about his life.:: Cisco: So, what do you do for a living? :: The young man sits in the backseat with a relaxed legs-crossed position and answers.:: Young Man: I’m the Chief Financial Advisor for a VERY powerful computer company, located right here in the city. :: Cisco raises his eyebrows and nods his head in approval.:: Young Man: You? :: Cisco looks in the rear-view mirror and answers:: Cisco: Well, I’m a wrestler in FSW. Young Man: Really? That pay good? Cisco: Yeah, it’s not bad… :: Suddenly the man tries to open the back cab door and get out.:: Cisco: Hey! What the fuck are you doing?! Young Man: You’re not supposed to be driving this cab! :: Cisco is now talking very fast now that the man has found him out.:: Cisco: How the fuck did you find that out?! :: The young man frantically tries to jiggle the door open:: Young Man: You idiot! I asked you what you do for a living and you said you were a wrestler…NOT A CAB DRIVER! Cisco: Well what the fuck would make you ask me that?! :: Cisco slows down the car and pulls it over to the near curb. The young man calms down some but is breathing heavily.:: Young Man: I noticed that the picture on your license didn’t match your face… :: Cisco checks the displayed license and notices this also.:: Cisco: Alright listen. I owe the guy that drives this cab 40 bucks, so I told him that I would work an hour of his shift to pay him off. That’s why I’m driving this cab. :: The Young man breathes easier now after hearing this and wipes away the sweat that has gathered on his forehead.:: Young Man: Oh. I see. :: The Young Man laughs and Cisco is confused.:: Young Man: You’ve got yourself into quite a problem. Cisco: Tell me about it. Young Man: That’s nothing compared to the problem that I have. :: Cisco looks back at the now sulking young man to find out his problem.:: Cisco: Do tell. :: The young man perks up and begins his story.:: Young Man: Well, my girlfriend….just fucking sucks! :: Cisco gets an empathetic look on his face as the man continues on.:: Young Man: She’s always flirting with other guys…and she doesn’t appreciate all the time and work I have put into making our relationship work….or try to work. :: Cisco nods as the young man talks and gives his perspective on the situation.:: Cisco: Well, let me give you some advice. Treat a women like shit, and she’ll strive for your attention. :: The man looks at Cisco with a confused look.:: Young Man: Really? That works? Cisco: Think about it. Women need approval. If you act like you don’t like them, or don’t approve of them…they will do all that they can to make you like them and approve of them. :: The man nods.:: Young Man: Thank you. I’m going to try your advice. Cisco: No problem. I’ve been there before. Young Man: Okay. No you go. Cisco: Go….where? Young Man: You tell me a problem. Cisco: Okay. I’m a wrestler. I have this problem with someone much bigger than me who has fucked with me more times than Joan Rivers has had plastic surgery. I know that if I try to go toe-to-toe with him that he will beat me. It’s not that I don’t trust my ability, it’s just that this guy is fucking huge. What do I do to beat him? Young Man: Simple. Out smart him. :: Cisco nods.:: Cisco: Outsmart him eh? That’s just crazy enough to work. Thank you. :: The young man sits up a little and grabs a brown leather wallet from his black pants. From the wallet the man takes out two twenty dollar bills, and hands them to Cisco.:: Young Man: Here, pay off the cabby. And thanks for the advice. :: Cisco takes the cash with excitement.:: Cisco: Thank you. :: The man unlocks the door and gets out of the cab. Cisco looks over his shoulder to thank the man before he closes the door.:: Cisco:….and thanks for the advice. Young Man: No problem…. :: The young man shuts the door and walks off to flag down another cab. Cisco puts the car into drive and pulls out onto the busy street. Cisco begins thinking on how he can outsmart Boda, as he drives back to where the he left the cab driver.:: Cisco: Outsmart Boda….hmmm….I’ve got it! :: Cisco beams with excitement at his revelation.:: Cisco: First, I’m going to prove to Boda that I am smarter than he is. :: The shot is now an overhead shot of the cab driving down the congested street. From this shot we go back to a long shot of the city and then up into the blue sky once again. The screen is now just the sky as the background as the words “ The End” come across the screen. Just when we think the camera will fade to black, a “?” pops up at the end of “ The End” and the trumpets play a shock beat: DUM Dum Daaaaaaaaa!!! The scene fades to black.::
