All Gimmick, No Science!

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/01/02 “Don’t worry, Michael, atleast the belt will stay in the Five Star Five.” Jacob stated to his best friend who sat with a head down, in what seemed to be an FSW locker room. “You don’t understand, Jacob. It was supposed to be MY turn, then here you come once again, and I’m stuck in your shadow.” Mike explains to his partner while flashing a few looks of disgust at him as well. Jacob shakes his head while getting up. Walking to his bag, Jacob begins to explain. “I can’t believe you, Michael. You have changed so much. It was never about the gold in the beginning. Sure, we were proud of our accomplishments, but don’t you remember when why we first started our team?” Jacob asks the depressed Marvelous. A smile suddenly replaces the glum features of Mike’s face as yes..he remembers. *$ CHA-CHING $* Jacob shakes his head in disgust. “No you idiot! Money was never a problem, don’t you remember, our rich parents already had our futures all set for us anyway!” “..oh…yeah…” “Well, maybe this will help you remember..” Jacob says as reaching into his duffel bag. He then pulls out a small round container. Mike’s eyes light up with glee, “IDOL CREAM~!” “That’s right, Michael. Idol Cream: All Gimmick, No Science. Now do you remember the sole purpose that Idol Cream was created for?” Mike nods his head quickly. *$ CHA-CHING $* “Heh..” Jacob nods his head slightly, “Well..yes, but why do you think I had this product created?” “I remember, Jacob. It was created simply, so millions of people would worship us, and try to be like MIKE, and Jacob!” “That’s right Michael. Soon after this product was created, sales skyrocketed, and everyone bought the product, simply because they wanted to be like us. Them ingrates, who call themselves wrestling fans, spent most of their lives, trying to mock their idols. It’s funny how some people will buy a product, because it simply has a famous person’s name on the label.” Mike nods in agreement. “Do you remember the chain reaction this product set off. I simply started the trend. Soon after there was California Cream, and not to mention, MARVELotion. Mike begins to blush a bit as Jacob says this. “But none of those products, can reach the standard of Idol Cream, Michael. Not even MARVELotion.” Mike actually nods in agreement. “To be honest with you, Michael. I don’t know how this product reached the success it had. It simply is no more then a cheap substitute of KY-Jelly. But once again, people bought it, because I, “The Idol” Jacob Jackson, told them to. I told them if they bought it, their lives would be greatly improved. It would clear up acne, which..it doesn’t, and it would make them feel better about themselves, which is doesn’t unless..heh..it’s applied to the genital area.” “Ahh..yes…memories.” Mike says with a far off look on his face, “But what does this have to do with me, possibly being cheated out of my World Title shot at Genises, Jacob?” “Actually..nothing. I was just waiting for the right time to tell you, that Idol Cream, is once again hitting the shelves in a store near..YOU!” “Me?” “Sure, Michael. Try and understand this, when Idol Cream is put back on the market, it will once again bring attention back to me. And when there is attention being put back on me, you also get a piece of the spotlight. Not only will we become wrestling idols once again, but everywhere we go people will recognize us, wrestling fan or not.” The Can-Am Connection get far off looks on their faces, imagining the possibilities. --------- Cars fill the streets, and people walk the sidewalks of a large city. Within the crowd are, the Can-Am Connection. They walk, carrying themselves high. Eyes follow everywhere they travel to, and people turn their heads to just keep a gaze on the duo. “You were right Jacob, look at all the attention we’re receiving from these lowlifes.” Mike states to his partner. “I told you Jacob, now we’re not far from accomplishing our goal. Remember when I first started out in the wrestling business. I joined the sport for nothing more then to, straighten out the lives of these scum. And now with my product back on the market, and this attention we’re receiving, we’re not far from our goal.” Jacob says with a smirk on his face. “There’s the con-artist that stole our damn money!” screams a voice from the crowd. Jacob and Mike look at each other with concerned looks. They quickly begin to run, but the crowd is right on their tales, and soon our wrestling heroes are cornered. “Wh-what seems to be the problem folks?” Jacob asks trying to reason with the crowd. “Your product said it would cure my acne, all it did was made it worse!” says a small skinny boy while pushing his glasses up the bridge of his nose. Jacob looks on with disgust at the kids face, boy if the Man On The Moon could see this man, he would drop to his knees in envy, fore this kid had more craters then Angello DeVille has mugshots. “W-well..did you read the warning label..?” “Your product promised to give me the courage to talk to girls. All I got was a slap in the face!” Says a small balding man in about his mid-forties. “He’s right!” Says a rather large lady with rubbing the palm of her hand while glaring at the balding man. “W-Well what did you say to her?” Jacob asks the man. “I simply asked her if I could take her to the All You Can Eat Buffet, at KFC” “Your product said it would increase my pleasure if applied to my genitalia while masterbating..I just gotta say man..I love IDOL CREAM~!” pipes up a young man of about 15 years of age, he follows his statement with a high five to both members of the Can-Am Connection. “Your product made my wife leave me for a better looking man!” Says a rather ugly man in about his thirties. “Are you sure it wasn’t your looks?” Asks Mike. “KILL THEM!” yells the ugly man. --------- “Oh..my..” Mike says while looking at Jacob still holding the can of Idol Cream~!, in his hand. “Just forget about all that, Michael. The important thing is…well…I’m not sure…but..well I guess you were right.” Jacob says as his eyes light up, and he smiles. *$ CHA-CHING! $* “I gotta tell you Jacob, you’re little speech hasn’t made me feel any better at all. I still can’t believe that I will possibly be cheated out of my World Title shot.” Mike says hanging his head down, staring at the ground. “Well, I know what’ll make you feel better. Here..take this, go to the bathroom and don’t come out until you feel good about yourself again.” Jacob tells Mike while handing him the can of Idol Cream. Mike reluctantly takes it then walks to the bathroom. --------- In an undetermined amount of time later, Mike walks out of the bathroom with a smile on his face, he then gives Jacob the thumbs up. IDOL CREAM~! All Gimmick, No Science.
