The Elmer Files (Part 2: Crashing the VMAs)

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-29-02 The scene opens up panning around a bunch of largely set up materials, making our way around a bunch of crap and finally resting on the front stage of the MTV Video Music Awards show. The Hives and The Vines, just finishing their tremendous on-stage performances, we now see Jimmy Fallon appear in the corner of the screen wearing his new bit of clothing and his obviously gay smirk across his face. Jimmy: Our next presenters are definitely a pair of people I’m sure none of you expected to ever see appear on an MTV stage ever. Let alone ever see this man on a stage. The combination is very weird as one is a hillbilly from Hicksville, USA and the other is the soon to be new Queen of the vocal world. They are Jesse Ramey and Norah Jones! “Don’t Know Why” by Norah Jones hits the house PA as Jesse Ramey and Norah Jones begin to make their way out onto the stage. Norah wearing a very lovely looking blue sparkly dress, while Jesse is wearing his usual wrestling attire in his blue jeans and boots. The two approach the podium as “Don’t Know Why” comes to a close. Norah: (The fans still in an obvious uproar, as she giggles a little bit) Tonight we’re out here to present for best music video by a wrestling superstar. Jesse: (Looking a little confused) I done know this be a category to begins with. Norah: Well, it was last minutes add in. Jesse: I’s understands that, we going to be going to those picks? The scene begins to swivel around as we see a large Moon Man award inside of a wrestling ring, coming off of the top rope as the voice begins to talk. Voice: “I Am Evil” by Mike Marvelous. A short clip is shown of Mike Marvelous doing his thing in the ring. Voice: “The Goodness Within Us All” by Timo Bolamba. A couple of short clicks are shown of Timo hitting a couple of high spots. Voice: “Soviet Nations” by Ivan Stanislav. A couple of clips are shown of Ivan’s past PCW work and his current OSW work. Voice: And “I Wanna Hump” by Elmer The Three-Toed Ringed Sloth Monkey. The scene shows Elmer humping a lot of things and then cuts back to Jesse and Norah. Jesse & Norah: And the winner be. A short pause. Jesse & Norah: ELMER! The fans go into another frenzy as both Jesse and Norah clear the ring and quickly make their way to the back when Elmer comes out to the mic and hopes up onto the podium. Elmer: (In his little squeaky voice) I’s be love you all! AHAHAHAHA! Come over here Jimmy Fallon! Elmer hops down off of the podium and runs over to Jimmy Fallon doing him and beginning to hump on him when the scene begins to get a little fuzzy and we hear Jesse in the background. Jesse: You’s be alright there Elmer? Jesse begins shaking on Elmer as he slowly wakes up, fading in from humping Jimmy Fallon to his eyes opening and seeing Jesse. Elmer: Stupid, hillbilly. I was humping Fallon! Jesse: This be the last time I’s let you stay up and watch that there show. The scene fades out on Elmer rolling back over and going back to sleep when a little wavy cloud appears and we see him continuing to hump Jimmy Fallon and smile comes across Elmer’s face.
