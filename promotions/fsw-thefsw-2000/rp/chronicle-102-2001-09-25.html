Chronicle 102

25 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
|A coffin and a shovel??? What the hell am I doing|
Carl Cash | 09/25/01
The seen is at a funeral shop, it is outside and you see the sign saying Bobs Casket Shop. The camera walks in and sees “Tha 1 Man Mafia” Carl Cash sitting they’re looking at Caskets he notices the camera and signals him over.

Carl- You see I am in a graveyard this Saturday against Human, so I thought I would buy him a little present, you see I know I am going to be burying him in a grave yard, So I though I would buy him a little present, and if you look right here, you can see it.

The camera zooms in on the casket

Carl-You see as long as I am burying him alive, I better get him a good casket so he can rest pretty easy. You see I also have to go to the store and buy a few more things for the match. But now I need to go talk to Bob about this

Cash walks up to the counter

Carl- Bob, I want that one

Bob- Ok

Carl- I want some engraving and a note

Bob- OK, tell me what you want

Carl- I want to human from Carl

Bob- All right and

Carl- and on the note Human, I hope you enjoy your present you’ll be seeing a lot of it

Bob- Ok it comes to

Before he can finish Cash throws down a big wad of bills

Carl- that should cover it right

Bob- Yes sir

Carl then signs the papers on where he wants the casket to go, and then goes outside and gets in his 2002 Lexus and starts to drive

Carl- you see I need to find an ACE hardware or something around here, because I need to buy something there also, you see I am going to be digging some dirt and stuff, so I figured I should get my own shovel, and you see not just a regular, shovel. First I am going to buy the shovel, then I am going to take it to Jake the jeweler, and you see I am going to get the wood wrapped in platinum, then I am going to get the shovel head made of platinum, so it will be a platinum shovel. Now I can just throw money around like that, I am rich I was a smart person when I first got wealthy. Oh damn there’s an ace

Carl Cash then pulls over to the ace, and walks inside and asks the person where he can find a shovel; he goes over and buys the shovel

Carl- how much is it

Girl- 15.99

Carl- When I get done with it, it will be about 5 million

Girl- What?

Carl- do you watch wrestling

Girl- No but my boyfriend does

Carl- FSW?

Girl- that’s his favorite promotion

Carl- Here is 50 bucks have him order the pay per view and then you can see what I am doing to this shovel

Girl- Hey do you think I can get your autograph for him

Carl- Sure

Carl signs the piece of paper and then leaves and the FSW camera fades to black
