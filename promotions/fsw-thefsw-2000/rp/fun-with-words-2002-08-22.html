Fun with words

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-22-02 (Welcome back ladies and gents ... to the greatest, no ... the bestest roleplay ever. I say this, however, I can't be to positive on that. I think it's mostly just confidence ... or stupidity. I dunno. I just wanted to write this ever since I wrote the last one, and now that I'm sitting down to write it, I'm not sure how I'll do it. I guess I'll cheat a little bit. Write a roleplay, and include the original idea WITHIN! Imagine that ... a roleplay within a roleplay. It'd be like Mulholland Drive with a movie within a movie, only less confusing. And without the lesbians I'm afraid. Although Lesbian Pron does kick ass. Anyway, that's not the point of this pre-promo message as usual. This is where I'm supposed to say what's going on ... because I know all of you can't possibly comprehend John's daunting life and his unwillingness to just let things happen. He's the one in control. He's got the ball. Your the net. Swoosh. That made as much sense as a TMM rp, but still. Enough of this ... even I'm starting to think it's filler. But it's not. It's just me, rambling.) (Our scene opens up to a lovely little coffee shop in Toronto. Queen St. East is the area, the corner of 29th is the coffee shop. It's a nice little place. Something you'd expect to find in a movie. The good looking waitress walking around in her white outfit, perfect down to the short miniskirt. She's refilling everyone's coffees and handing out sandwiches and fries. A true professional. Swingin her bottom towards the back of the shop, she stops at a booth housing only one man, with a cupajoe, black, decaf. He's slowly munching away at his now cold fries, but staring intently at the book in front of him.) Waitress: Whatcha readin? (John doesn't speak ... he just shows her the cover of the book. For those of your expecting something sophisticated ... brace yourselves. He's got a Mad Libs book. The pages are filled with words, which are then scribbled out as he finds they make no sense.) Waitress: Can I read one of them? Jupiter: Nah, They're all stupid. Waitress: Well, I'm sure you'll get a good one soon. Jupiter: When I do ... you can read that one. Waitress: Deal. So you want a refill? (John holds his coffee mug out and nods his head.) Waitress: I'll be around if you need anything. Jupiter: Ok. Thanks. (He turns his attention back to the book, and pulls his pen out as he starts a new page. This one appears to be rather short. Let's see what John can do with it.) Jupiter: Name ... John Jupiter. (John jots it down) Jupiter: Place ... Wrestling Ring (Fills in the space.) Jupiter: Now an adjective? Enormous (Goin pretty good so far.) Jupiter: Verb? Slammed. (Alright ... we're kickin) Jupiter: Phrase ... hehe ... Kick of Fear! (This is gonna be sweet ...) Jupiter: Object? Tylenol. Container? Can. (How long is this thing dammit?) Jupiter: Adjective? Sleepy. Animal? Possum. (Teehee ... Mingster.) Jupiter: Body Part? Keep it clean John ... leg. (Good Save. Possum and Ming don't make for clean talk.) Jupiter: Animal Noise? Snort. (What kind of possum snorts?) Jupiter: Object? Stick. (Beat the possum with a stick. It's got to be diseased after ming) Jupiter: Rename the container? Can. Jupiter: Another adjective? Great! (Where is this story going?) Jupiter: Rename place? Wrestling Ring! (This is frickin easy!) Jupiter: Past-Tense Verb? Ran. Place again?! Wrestling Ring. (Repetive much?) Jupiter: Angry Phrase huh? How about ... I Jobbed to Hank~! (No one ever said that with joy.) Jupiter: Adverb? What the hell is an adverb? S'cuse me miss? (The waitress comes back over.) Waitress: How can I help you? Jupiter: I feel like a bit of an idiot ... but ... what's an adverb? Waitress: A word to describe a verb. Like ... swiftly. Jupiter: Perfect! Thanks! (John writes Swiftly down in the spot.) Jupiter: Another place? Condo. Jupiter: And the last thing ... mood. Hmmm ... unsatisfied. (That's it ... let's read the whole thing now ... wait though! The waitress has to read this one. It's a good one!) Jupiter: S'cuse me again ma'am. But I've got a good one now I think. Waitress: Ok, let me just get the other girl to do my rounds, and I'll sit down to read it. Jupiter: Ok. (The waitress goes and gets one of the other girls to refill everyone's coffee for a few minutes whilst she sits down and reads the mad lib.) Waitress: Ok. Let's see how you did. Do you mind if I read it out loud? Jupiter: Just not too loud. Waitress: Ok. Here goes. My Summer Vacation by John Jupiter One day I was walking to the wrestling ring. I saw a enormous lady who needed help. I slammed her. She said Kick of Fear , and gave me a tylenol. I put it in my can. I then continued on my way. Soon, I saw a sleepy possum. It had hurt its leg. I wrapped it with a bandage, and it said snort. I got up and kept on walking towards my destination. Soon, I stumbled across a stick. I picked it up and put it in my can. It was getting great and I still had to go to the wrestling ring. I ran as fast as I could. I finally reached the wrestling ring. I jobbed to Hank~!, I said, it was closed. I started walking swiftly back towards my condo. I felt unsatisfied about what I had accomplished. (The waitress is trying to absorb it all. It is quite deep after all.) Waitress: It's ... interesting. So you're ... a wrestler? Jupiter: Yeah. Waitress: I'd stick with wrestling then. I hope it's better than your mad libbing. Jupiter: This stuff isn't so easy! You try and do better! Waitress: Tell you what ... next time you come in ... bring in that book, and I'll write one better than you. Jupiter: It's a deal. Can I have my bill? Waitress: Sure thing. (She goes to fetch his bill and returns with a candy as well. On the bill is written, $3.75 and her phone number. He leaves a $5 bill, and walks out. The whole walk home he's thinking to himself ...) Jupiter: I better be damn well better than my madlibbing against Angelo. I gotta show this girl that my wrestling is better than my madlibbing next time I pop by. What better way to do it than with some gold around my waist.
