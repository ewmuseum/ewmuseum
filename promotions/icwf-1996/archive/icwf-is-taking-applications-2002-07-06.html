The ICWF Is Taking Applications

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey there, The Intense Championship Wrestling Federation has opened its doors once more towards finding the best and the brightest singles and tag team wrestlers on the net, which is why I come to you today. A bit of history on the ICWF- This is a federation that's last for nearly 6 years, which is almost unheard of in the e-fed world. We take great pride in being members of this federation, and hope that it never dies. This fed has succeeded based on participation, creativity and effort. E-fed wrestling is great fun, but you only have as much fun as work you put into it. To make it simple, we want a commitment. We will not bring you aboard if you only are interested the first week that you're involved. I ask that before you respond to this, you take it into thought if you truly wish to spend some time in one of the best federations on the internet. Aside from a commitment, participation in the flash-floods (flashes are character role-plays) and developing angles with other handlers. These are the things that will get you noticed and get you success in the ICWF. In closing, please review the topics of membership I've mentioned, and if interested review the 'Rules' section of icwf.org to see how things operate and if you still wish to join our ranks, you can email The President personally at [detail withheld] or the Director of Applications at [detail withheld] and we can get you situated, participating and wrestling as soon as possible. We hope to see you in the ICWF, so please visit http://www.icwf.org and check it out for yourselves. Thanks for your time and effort in this matter.
