We are not the finest federation around nor do we claim to be. We are however more than dedicated to try and make this the finest and
*funnest* federation around. Let's start off with behind the scenes action. The TBWO is headed by a 3 man staff although we make it look like we're bigger. Las
Post is the President of the Tampa Bay Wrestling Orginazation. Doug Coffin is the Vice-President of the TBWO. Rob Loney heads up the pack as Commissioner.
It's very important that you follow the rules we outlined in this handbook. This is what makes or breaks your stay. TBWO is meant strictly for fun, and is not to be taken too seriously. It's entertainment at its best ... we reward those who work hard. It's all about how much time and effort you're willing to put into the TBWO. Read below for more information on how to make your stay here better than a trip around Florida! Thank you.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [2] Rules Of The TBWO Like any place, there are rules that must be followed. To assure the rules are followed the best way possible any troubles that arise will be dealt with in the following three ways.
FIRST TIME: You get a warning. SECOND TIME: You're suspended. THIRD TIME: You're banned from the TBWO ... this is as lenient as we can be about things. Please follow these rules and all will be well! * ROLE-PLAYING RULES: These are simple and so important! Please abide by them and everything will be okay. - Maximum of _TWO_ flashes/roleplays a week. (I don't want to see more than two, so do not send them in.) - Maximum ONE flash per packet. (Do not send the flash masters more than ONE flash for each packet unless you have an e-mail system that won't allow you to finish a role-play and you have to finish it off in a separate email. Although
I see no reason as to why that should happen more then once or twice.)
- You MUST role-play twice every two weeks. If you cannot do so, then please contact the president and we'll be happy to work with you. If you have a match, please role-play -- YOU WON'T WIN IF YOU DON'T ROLE-PLAY.
- Any questions? Contact me and we'll work them out. - Role-plays come out two times a week and go to [detail withheld] , [detail withheld] , and [detail withheld] . * NO CURSING: Sure, you can say the words that Steve Austin says ... other than that, mask them. Cursing isn't something you have to do every breath your wrestler takes! * NO REAL WRESTLERS OR REFERENCE TO REAL WRESTLING FEDERATION: We like 'em as original as they come. Sure, I like Shawn Michael and Randy Savage ... but please, no use of their nicknames, ripping off of their slogans, actions, etc.
Do not refer to comparing your wrestler to WWF or WCW. This is a must! If I find this, you _will_ be warned. Then after a warning, you know the rest.
* NO PHONY WRESTLERS: This means no 8 foot wrestlers or 700 lbs. Keep the limit good! The tallest will be 7'3 and the heaviest will be 550 lbs. * LIMIT TO WRESTLERS: You can handle 1 character(or tag-team) in the TBWO. Whether it be a male, female, or tag-team... you may only handle one. * NO COLORS OR FONTS: This is if you're on AOL, don't send flashes or anything in with fonts or colors! If you do, I won't bother to change them to regular black text on white background. I'll just delete the role-play.
* YOU MUST ROLE PLAY: Roleplaying/flashing is something you have to do. If you're going to join the TBWO just to sit around, you'll be terminated. So please, role-play They're sent out two times a week in role-play packets called "Hot Flashes." Send all those to
[detail withheld] , [detail withheld] , and [detail withheld] . . (see first rule on roleplaying) * NO USING OTHER CHARACTERS IN FLASHES: It is extremely against TBWO rules to use someone else's wrestler in your role-play without their permission AND mine. And even if I am told that you got the other handler's permission, I will double check by asking that handler. If it is a lie, you will be suspended immediately.
* HAVE FUN! The TBWO is for fun and games. We can only hope that no rules are broken, because if they are it can lead to suspending, or even being thrown out from the league!

TBWO is a
BOOKED league and role-playing is a must. To win a match, or even get a match you must ROLE-PLAY! Role-playing is a must for champions, challengers, etc.
If you expect to win ... then you know what to do. Role-plays are sent out in weekly packets two times a week called "Hot Flashes." All RPs go to [detail withheld] , [detail withheld] , and [detail withheld] . ~~~ Role-playing Tips ~~~ SCENE AND APPEARANCE: Make sure that you describe what they're wearing, or what they look like. Who wants to read something that they can't imagine?
ORIGINALITY: "And, that's the bottom line because stone cold said so!" I know that EVERYONE has heard that line. We here at the TBWO don't want all that
STONE COLD stuff. We want you guys to be ORIGINAL, and we want the best that you can throw at us. Give us your best shot!
CURSING: Yeah, yeah ... we all like to curse, I know that ... but, sometimes we get out of hand. So, we don't promote cursing ... therefore if your character is that type of bad-ass guy, then be sure to mask ALL curses!
SPELLING & GRAMMAR: No one likes an idiot who can't spell worth his life when role-playing. Make it to where you can understand what they're saying!
LENGTH: Sometimes role-plays can be the best with only a short paragraph ... but, we're not asking you guys to go out and make a two sentence role-play. We don't want a novel either ... make them long, or short, but fun to read.
Whatever you like, and whatever catches the eye! When you get bored with the role-play that you are writing, then it most likely means that its over, so finish it up with some last minute touches, do a spellcheck and send it in.
CHOOSE A GOOD CHARACTER: The main key in role-playing is choosing a character that you can adapt to. If you like being crude, then make a crude character.
Just make sure that you can adapt to that character, and show that you know what you're going at.
HAVE FUN: Please keep in mind, that the MAIN MORAL in E-wrestling and role-playing is to have fun! All the way...you're in this to win ... yes, but please do remember that what makes E-WRESTLING is winning and losing. Win some, lose some, that's the way it goes! If you take e-wrestling far too seriously, then you should take a time out from e-wrestling.
~~~ Role-playing Rules ~~~ DO NOT USE REAL WRESTLERS: Here's the deal ... TBWO is its own world, am I correct? Great! A lot of people like using the reference of real wrestlers.
DO NOT DO THAT! NO REAL WRESTLERS! No calling people "Stone-Cold Wannabes" or any of that garbage. Please do not use REAL WRESTLERS, COMMENTATORS, REFEREES,
ETC. You can use other e-wrestlers of course. Don't use WWF, WCW, ECW, etc., or any other promotions but the TBWO alone itself. No other real leagues.
Other fantasy leagues, fine, we have no problem whatsoever with that. NO SHOOTING: Shooting is when you're going out of character and taking personal shots against the TBWO or anything that has to do with the TBWO. If you do this you'll be suspended. Or maybe even fired. For those of you who know about "shooting", make it a limit.
NO SPAMMING: Spamming, eh? Spamming is when people send in more role-plays then who knows what. ONE ROLE-PLAY PER DAY ONLY COUNTS! Say Bob sent me two role-plays, well if George (his upcoming opponent) sent me one, then only one of those two of Bob's counts! Remember that one. Spamming would also be considered once someone writes constant role-plays of 170+ lines. Keep in mind that this is _NOT_ an AOL fed.
QUALITY V/S QUANTITY: Well now, people here in the TBWO, that's another version of spamming. If Bob sends me those two role-plays and they both aren't as good as his opponents, and George sends me that one great role-play then George wins. Quality is a lot better than quantity. Ask around.
MASK PROFANITY: Another one of my favorites. I do not care if you curse. But, please, MASK THEM! Use (#, %, *, @) .. whatever! Just do not use the bad curse words without masking them. I do NOT have time to go through every role-play and mask them all. Do you realize how long that would take me?
That's exactly why I will delete role-plays with lots of unmasked cursing in it. ~~~ Strats ~~~ What is a strat? EASY! If you want your wrestler to do something special then E-mail [detail withheld] with a special "strat" that you want your wrestler to perform. Like interfering in a match, or using some kind of object. This is a detailed summary for your match. For those of you who don't know, strat is a shorter word for strategy.
A few rules for strats, challenging and requesting matches... SEND IN STRATS EARLY: I send out the preview for Revolution on Saturdays for the next card(s) ... and never even receive strats until Monday night for
Tuesday's show. THAT IS A BIG NO NO! Why? The matches have already been written! That would mean your strat will be no good. Try to get strats to me by Friday night or Saturday afternoon.
MATCH REQUESTS/CHALLENGES: You want to see your wrestler do something? Do not be afraid to e-mail me and request it. I'm usually nice about these things. I like it when people get involved on their own. :-)
USE THIS FORM FOR A STRAT: Wrestler Name: [who is this strat for?] Match-up: [who v/s who?] Match Date: [date it takes place] Main Gameplan: [this is what will be the main thing to happen during the match...your main focus]
Special Moves: [moves they'll use, counter, execute, etc.] If They Win/If They Lose: [what will happen? how will they react?] Extras: [anything else you'd like to include that we didn't mention] You can do anything like this ... and be even more informational with it if you'd like. - Send all strats and angle ideas to [detail withheld] -Send all In-Rings/Backstage Interviews to [detail withheld] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 5) Titles If you follow the rules and do all the stuff we ask if you, you're going to grab one of these babies sooner or later!
Florida State Championship - This will be the TBWO's World Title. The big gold everyone will want to get there hands on, so if you have it you're either very lucky or very good but you better watch your back, because everyone will be gunning for you.
Tampa Bay Champion - This will be a big title, not quite as high as the State title but if you have it you're a big name around here. Just one step away from the big title ... but you'll be so busy defending this one you may not be able to go for the big gold for a while.
We will get a Lightweight, Women's and Tag-Team titles when we see fit. Right now we're keepin' it down to two titles because we want more participation.
