UPW, pick any man you want, I don't care. My man will destroy him. Next Week, UPW!! FORBES: What was that? Kevin Angeny has just interrupted our show, and apparently, tonight, we will find out just who will be facing Angeny's new man. JOHNNO: Yes, we will. If Kevin Angeny has this guy, he's gotta be good. ELVIS: We haven't started our telecast, and already he's interrupting us. Before we go any further, let's update you one what's happened so far. Every entrance, the crowd meter will show up, and a '>' will indicate a cheer, while a '<', will indicate a boo. The more of them, the louder. Obviously the crowd is against this guy. The highest you can get up to is 10. Marcus Mantini will definitely have a shot at that rating. Here he comes!! BELLOW: And his opponent, weighing 250 pounds, Marcus Mantini!! >>>>>>> ELVIS: A great response for Mantini. He is one of the crowd favorites for sure. JOHNNO: He's a great athlete, yada, yada, yada, but can he defeat Kadugan, who's KaduganBomb is devastating. ELVIS: The KaduganBomb is a modified version of the Death Drop, where he lifts him up and drop him into a pin. FORBES: These two guys ready, the crowd is really pumped. Marcus Mantini has been out the last few weeks for autographs and everything. He is focused on wrestling now. The music has stopped and they are ready to go at it. ELVIS: Kadugan is used to using cheap shots very often. He is a great wrestler, but if he gets the chance, he'll cheat you out of the match. FORBES: Both men are holding each other...Mantini headlock on Kadugan...Kadugan sends him to the ropes...Mantini comes off with a frankensteiner!! What a move to start the show!! A cover...1...2...Kadugan won't stay down...Mantini hits the ropes again...drop-kick finds the mark...Mantini starts wailing away on Kadugan...oooooh! He missed that punch and Kadugan back dropped him to the floor. Kadugan using the ropes to slide himself under...he connects his feet with Mantini's chin and Mantini hits the railing!...the ref starts the count, Mantini slow to get up. Kadugan breaks the count and goes outside the ring. He picks up Mantini and bodyslams him!!! ... Mantini thrown back into the ring...Kadugan follows...he goes to the top ropes...a big man like him taking a gamble...flying legdrop take down Mantini...he grabs the legs...1...2...Mantini just kicks out...what action we have here so far. What a match-up!! Kadugan whips Mantini into the corner and he comes out with a crossbody and their momentum takes them all the way to the floor!!...Kadugan is up and he's letting Mantini know who his fists are. Mantini is down, and Kadugan smartly gets back in the ring. ELVIS: Kadugan's a big guy. He's also a smart guy. He knows what he's doing. FORBES: Mantini back in, Kadugan to greet him...Mantini slides under the rope and trips him with a fantastic move...he returns to the ring apron...he's going to the top rope!!...flying cross-body...1...2..Kadugan gets out of that one. Mantini hits the ropes, Kadugan ducks his head and Mantini piledrives him!! 1...2...Kadugan foot fell outside the ring...he got lucky there...Mantini is winding up!! He's going for the flying forearm!!! He hits the ropes...OHHH...he missed and hit the floor...Kadugan moves quickly...he goes to the top rope!! This big guy is up about 10-15 feet...clothesline hits Mantini into the barrier...what a match-up we have here...Mantini is back up and into the ring...he hits the ropes as Kadugan climbs back in and a kick to the stomach by Killer...he sets him up for the Kadugan bomb!! He...got it!! NO!!! Mantini reverses it...1...2...3!!!!! BELLOW: The winner of this match, Marcus Mantini!!! FORBES: Death drop didn't work and he used a victory roll to get the win. JOHNNO: Mantini is one of the top athlete's here in UPW, as much as I hate to admit it, but Kadugan had one hell of a match. The competition is stiff here in 'The U'. ELVIS: No question Johnno, Kadugan's skill is right up with the rest of them. JOHNNO: Mantini's skill is great, but I'll tell you this, he has either Carr or Kevin Sanders next round, and both are tough. FORBES: Yes, both are very tough, and I think Carr may have the advantage here. ELVIS: Carr weighs over 300 pounds. I definitely call that an advantage. FORBES: Let's go to Bellow. BELLOW: Coming down to the ring, weighing 356 pounds, Carr!! <<<<<< FORBES: As the car screeches blast over the sound here at SkyHigh Arena, the jeers almost deafen the sound. Many people have seen what Carr is all about. He beat Brin Taylor after many, many "bending" of the rules, and the crowd just doesn't like that. BELLOW: And his opponent, weighing 237 pounds, Kevin Sanders!! >>>> FORBES: The crowd is really behind Kevin Sanders, they saw him beat Rex Harley earlier tonight, and that was a great match-up. ELVIS: "Rebel", in my opinion, was ripped off by a lucky Sanders. I don't think that will happen with Carr though. JOHNNO: That's a point well made. Carr and Sanders are mismatched quite a bit. Sanders is a high flyer, while Carr will pummel you into the ground. FORBES: There's the bell. What a take down by Carr. ELVIS: Carr has a different style. Sanders moves out of the way!...A cover...1...2...Carr gets out of that with a fierce! He threw Sanders all the way to the floor! WOW!! Did you see that move?? I guess not, for obvious reasons, but, boy, what a maneuver by Carr. He laid across the top rope and just flipped outside landing his back right onto Sanders. Carr gets back in the ring, goes back out and breaks the count...something is happening as the bell rang!! Apparently the timekeeper thought the ref counted to ten...the ref is checking it out and OHHH!!!!!! Carr just nailed Sanders with that chair...Sanders is not moving...Carr is in the ring...the ref has everything straightened out, he starts the count on Sanders...What a cheater...Carr uses every edge he possibly can...the ref is at 6 and Sanders is not moving at all...8...9...10. This match is over! BELLOW: Here is your winner, as a result of a count-out, Carr!! FORBES: It will be Carr and Marcus Mantini in the first semi-final. King Richard received a bye to the semi-finals, so the winner between Farlane and Buagar will get the last semi-spot. ELVIS: Yes, that match is coming next.
