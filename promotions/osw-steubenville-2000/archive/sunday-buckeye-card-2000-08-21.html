Ohio State Wrestling Proudly Presents... _S U N D A Y B U C K E Y E_ Live on WTOV 9 Sunday, August 27th, 2000 3:00 A.M. Live from the Bellaire High School Football Stadium Bellaire, Ohio MATCH ONE: Jerry Anarchy vs. "Kodiak" Nolan Cayne - Jerry Anacrchy, a newcomer to this week's card, is going up against a person that has one last chance to proove himself here in the OSW. Nolan Cayne either had to put up or hang them up in this match. On the other end, Jerry
Anarchy has the chance to proove himself worthy against a very larger, Nolan
Cayne.
Match Two: "The Real Deal" Dean Collins vs. "The Extreme Goddess" Kieran Erotikka - Erotikka, coming off a disappointing loss last week due to a gorilla, has another chance to get her best foot foward against Dean Collins. Dean Collins, coming out of draw in the last Sunday Buckeye, has one more chance to proove himself to the OSW.
Match Three: Ernesto Zapata vs. Sto Rox - Ernesto Zapata, coming off yet another draw in last weeks Buckeye, can show his stuff against newcomer, Sto Rox. Both men are pretty equal in size and both excel in highflying and high risk manuvers. This should be a very interesting match to watch.
*~* Main Event *~* "The Human Aphrodisiac" Bradley Rose vs. El Macho Sasquatcho - Rose is going up against the "gorilla" that helped him win his match against Kieran Erotikka in last week's Buckeye. Nobody is too sure if Rose is happy or upset with what El Macho Sasquatcho did. All we know is that Erotikka isn't happy and the OSW has a gorilla wrestler.
