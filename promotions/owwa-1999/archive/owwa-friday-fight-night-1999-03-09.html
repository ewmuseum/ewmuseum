Friday Fight Night

9 March 1999

FRIDAY FIGHT NIGHT (The OWWA screen opens to a packed house with tons of people yelling, then the camera turns to the commentators)
Blaze- Hello ladies and gentlemen, we have a whole bunch of tag teams matches for you tonight and we are ready to kick it off with the new
team of Grim Reaper and Decapitator VS Reaper and Minion Malcom- Already in the ring we have the team of minion and reaper and now coming to the ring it's DIMENSIONS OF FEAR
(Aqua Lung blares over the P.A. as Decapitator and Grim Reaper walk out to the ring. The fans seem to like them and they seem to be pretty sure of themselves)
Grim Reaper and Minion are starting it off. Grim Reaper is pounding on him with a series of rights, Minion fights back with a left, but Grim
Reaper Gives him a kick to the stomach. Grim Reaper is setting up Minion for a piledriver and he nails it. Grim Reaper is throwing Minion into the corner and he follows him with a knee to the mid-section of Minion.
Grim Reaper hits Minion with a DDT. Grim Reaper is picking up Minion, but Minion goes for a legsweep and he gets Grim Reaper down. Grim Reaper jumps up pretty fast and he looks pissed. He is picking up Minion and has him on his shoulder. Grim Reaper slams him down hard and he hits him with a leg drop. He is picking up Minion and throws him into the turnbuckle. Grim Reaper tags in Decapitator and they are setting Minion up for a double suplex from the top turnbuckle and they hit it perfectly. What is this? Decapitator actually takes Minion over to his corner to tag in Reaper. Reaper is in and he is giving a bunch of left hands to the face of Decap. Decap puts up his hand to block it and knees
Reaper. Reaper goes down. Decap is puttin Reaper in a Boston Crap.
Reaper has the ropes but Decap is not letting go. Minion is in and goes to kick Decap but he hits Reaper. Minion jumps out of the ring and
Reaper follows. They are arguing back and forth. Reaper is climbing back up but Decapitator is waiting. He kicks Reaper in the stomach and he is setting him up for a Piledriver off of the mat onto the cement. He calls that The Decapitation. Decap is picking Reaper up into a double choke.
He is spinning around and he slams him down. He calls that the Death
Rattle. He is going for the pin, 1.... 2.... 3
Malcom- And your winners are Dimensions of Fear. Blaze- Wow, Dimensions of Fear dominated that match. I wouldn't be surprised if they won the tag titles. And now for our next macth, we
have Dogg Pound Vs Da Playas Malcom- Now coming to the ring Offspring and Goliath, Da Playas (Da Playas come to the ring with a mixed reaction, Offspring looks pretty sure of himself, the new guy Goliath is huge.)
Malcom- Now coming to the ring Minions Of Hell, Bulk and Collossus
(Minions of Hell run down to the ring with many fans cheering them on)
Minions Of Hell are taking it to Da Playas. Bulk and Goliath are being told to get out of the ring. Offspring is hitting Collossus with rights.
He's goes for a Belly To Belly Suplex and he hits it. Collossus is right back up. Offspring gets hit with a Vertical Suplex. Collossus is putting
Offspring in a Sleeper Hold. Offspring is trying to get out and Goliath jumps in and and kicks Collossus and saves Offspring. Collossus is up and he is telling the ref to get Goliath out. The ref gets distraced and
Bulk gets in and hits Offspring with a DDT. Offspring is gettting pulled up by Collossus. Offspring hit Collossus with a Low Blow and the ref didn't see it. Offspring with the tag to Goliath. Goliath hits Collossus with a DDT. He picks up Collossus and is going for another DDT.
Collossus counters with a Northern Lights Suplex. Collossus is going for a tag to Bulk. Goliath gets up and tags in Offspring. The four of them are pounding on eachother. Bulk got thown out. Here comes Unknown with a chair! The ref is looking at Bulk. Unknown is going around Bulk. Bulk goes to hit him but Unknown ducks the punch and hits him with the chair.
The ref calls for the bell. Unknown gets in and hits the other three with a chair, laughs at them, then runs out.
Blaze- Your winners by dq MINIONS OF HELL. So the Minions of Hell move up in the brackets. But I know that Offspring is not worried he is only
looking at the hardcore title. Now we have Gary and White Trash Vs Masked Dawgs Malcom- already in the ring white trash and gary. Now coming to the ring THE MASKED DAWGS (THE masked dawgs come down to the ring with alot of boos, it doesn't bother them though.) Fake Masked Dawg is taking it to White Trash, he is hitting him with kicks to the stomach, F.M.D. goes for an atomic drop, he is picking him up he goes for a reverse atomic drop, F.M.D. is tagging in Masked Dawg,
Masked Dawg is putting White Trash on the turnbuckle and he goes for the move he calls the finisher and he hits it, F.M.D. is up on the turnbuckle and hits the shooting star press. and Masked dawg goes for the pin, 1.... 2.... 3 Blaze- Masked Dawgs are undefeatable, I don't see how anyone can beat these two. Hey who is coming to the ring. It's the dogg pound and unknown, Masked dawgs don't see them, Masked dawgs just got hit in their backs with those chairs hard, wow, Dogg Pound and
Unknown are hitting the Masked Dawgs with chairs and just beating them, get some security.
(William Wallace steps out from behind the curtain) Wallace- Guys, guys thats enough, I think our point was made. So I quess by now you figured out who the corprate tag team is, THE DOGG POUND. Now
Amen I think you know that your going to be getting something very similar to this. Except we won't be as nice to you. Come on lets go.
(The Corporate teams leaves as the camera turns toward Blaze) Blaze- That was dispicable. I can't believe the actions of our prez. But he can't get into the next match as all the trash cans are being brought into the ring and the cage is lowering. That is a very oversized cage, it leaves alot of extra space around the ring, there are about 15 trash cans filled with weapons in the ring and around the ring. So lets get it started.
Malcom- Now coming to the ring, the master of the coils, ANACONDA. (Anaconda comes out to a stand up ovation, the fans are so loud you can't hear his music) Malcom- Now coming to the ring AMEN THE IRISH ICON O'MIGHTY (THE FANS KEEP CHEERING AS AMEN WALKS OUT) Amen and Anaconda are hitting eachother. Anaconda ducks a punch and rolls under Amen's arms and hits him with a Belly To Back Suplex. He is stomping on Amen. Anaconda opens a trash can and he pulls out a bat. He goes to hit Amen but he misses and hits the ropes and the bat bounces back and hits him in the head. It didn't hit very hard because he is still up. Amen gets a chair. They go for eachother and they hit the weapons together. Amen got the worst of that one.Anaconda is trying to tear off Amen's kilt. Amen kicks Anaconda in the head. Amen grabs a trash can and throws it at Anaconda. Amen has a chair and he hits
Anaconda hard. There is a woman coming down to the ring. Amen is a little distracted. He is near the edge of the cage talking to her.
Anaconda has a board and he hits Amen in the back of the head. He is picking up Amen and he is going for a DDT. Amen blocks and hits Anaconda with a kidney shot. He whips Anaconda into the ring post and follows with a lariat. Amen is climbing back into the ring. He is up on the turnbuckle flexing his muscles. Anaconda is in and Amen sees. They both pick up a trash can lid and they try to hit eachother. Anaconda ducks and Amen misses. Anaconda hits Amen in the back of the head. Hey what is that Gnome was hiding in one of the trash cans? Anaconda sees him and he hits him with a trash can lid. Unknown is running down to the ring. He unlocks the cage and comes in. He looks at Anaconda who is beating up the Gnome and says he is going after Amen, but when Anaconda turns his back Unknown Lariats Anaconda to the back of the head. Unknown throws him into the corner and he sets Anaconda up for a Tornado DDT. Oh, he hits it! And right into a trash can! Unknown is leaving the cage. Amen is going up top and he hits the Kilt Killer! Now he is wrapping up
Anaconda into the Four Leaf Clover. Anaconda is tapping out. It's over.
Blaze- The winner is Amen, the winner is Amen! But the Corporation helped him out. What happened? (Wallace walks out with The Dogg Pound and Unknown goes up to join him along with Amen) Wallace- Ladies and gentlemen your new and first OWWA HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WORLD,
AMEN THE IRISH ICON O'MIGHTY. Anaconda, nothing personal you just got into the wrong place at the wrong time. Now whenever you are ready just tell us what belt you want. But back to the subject at hand, all you pathetic worthless slobs believed that I hated Amen. No, I could never hate this man. And now the Coporation is complete. The President of the
OWWA William Wallace, The Commisioner of the OWWA Carl Crack, The Vice
President of the OWWA Alec Empire, The Corporate Bodygaurd Unknown, The
Corporate Tag Team The Dogg Pound, and of course The One, The Only, The
Best Wrestler In The OWWA Amen The Irish Icon O'Mighty.
Thank you ladies and gentlemen but we are out of time. (Camera fades to black as the Coporation walks into the backstage area)
