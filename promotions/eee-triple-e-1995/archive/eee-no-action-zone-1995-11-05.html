"EEE No-Action Zone Episode 1"

5 November 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No-Action Zone-Episode 1-November 5, 1995 Hosts: Toad Pettingzoo Dok Hayes -------------------- (An annoying guitar feedback sound is repeated as clips of wrestling moves are played) (Camera opens in a studio with hosts behind desk with EEE merchandise all over the place) P: Hey everyone, and welcome, to the first show here in EEE, the No-Action
Zone! Yep, we will be here every week recapping all the matches of the week and occasionally we will have a match right here in the studio! Right Dok?
H: Weeeeell! Before I get to that I want everyone to call the EEE Hotline at 1-500-EEE-SHAFT! You can hear generic taped interviews from the stars of
EEE. You can get a merchandise catalogue full of overpriced goodies, just in time for Christmas! But now back to the non-action. Commissioner Mike
Crunch went out and found the most extreme wrestlers in the e-universe and paired them up for these tournaments. We will first look at the pairings for the Continental championship.
Psycho vs "Hurricane" Bobby Russo Jonney Angel vs Barry Hardly Bobby Hall vs Duane Spill The Endless Enigma vs Iron Mike Dulle P: Wow! This should truly be a hot tournament, I mean we got eight of Triple E's hottest stars competing in one tournament to decide the Continental champion. Just look at that first bout (points to bulletin board) A man known only as Psycho taking on a very charismatic Bob Russo.
I was in the back there a little while ago and Bob Russo is determined to win the Continental title and he will get his first chance this Thursday on
Worldwide Superstars!
H: I want to inform all our viewers out there that the matches scheduled for Monday Nite War have been moved to Worldwide Superstars! Nooooow, let's move on to the Tag Team division:
P: We got four extreme tag teams ready to lock it up for the straps! Majestics vs Midnight Rockers X vs Detroit Bad Boys P: Now little is known about these four teams. All I know is we have got teams from all over the USA, from Detroit to LA. Let's go to some prerecorded comments from the Majestics and their manager the Gypsy
(Camera shot outside some palace like structure. The Gypsy is out there by himself) G: You know what you little no nothings out there. When I decided that being a manager of champions was my ultimate goal in life, I did a few things. I went out and found two of the best in the business. Sure, they have never wrestled a match in their lives, but I took them and trained them for hours and hours until I knew the were ready. Then I decided that it was time to reek havoc on some poor federation out there and I couldn't find one good enough. Until now. The Triple E has offered to put my men, in the tag team tournament as the number one seed. Do you think my men are deserving?
I don't care what you think. I care what they think... (Camera opens up and the Majestics, Scotty Kage and Chris Pillon walk in the shot) G: Tell em Chris! P: Well, I am not one to do much talking. All my talking is done in the ring, but not with words, with pain...
K: That's right, all you nickel and dime hasbeen wash up teams! We have come to dominate and dominate we will!
(Camera back in studio. Pettingzoo and Hayes roll their eyes) P: OK? Well lets get to the bracketing of the International title tournament: Super D vs Masked Marauder Scarlet Dagger vs Bates Motel Randy Ramos vs Kendler Bloodshot vs The Rebel H: Wow, what a contrast of styles we have in this one, it is truly International. We have got the excellent Mexican flying abilities of the
Masked Marauder and Scarlet Dagger. Japanese skill of Super D and Bates
Motel and the rough and tumble Rebel and Randy Ramos representing America among others. P: Should be a great tournament. But before we get to the bracketing for the World title, let's go back a few weeks and see what happened when our own Jeffrey Schiavone ran into Stan "The Lariat" Manson:
(Camera shows Schiavone running up to a old beaten up Cadillac with a burly, redneck in the drivers seat)
S: Fans, I am trying to catch up with Stan Manson, who has just signed with
Triple E. Mr. Manson, Mr. Manson...You have just signed, what are...
M: "Shaddup you pipsqueak! I'm gonna make this little visit short and sweet. All you little boys out there are soon gonna feel the brand of a good federation and with that comes a wrassler uglier than your Momma. That's right, it's me...Stan "The Lariat" Manson. Dirtier than your Grandpa's diaper's and meaner than a striped snake. There's a World title at stake in this here organization and I'm gonna do anything I have to do win that damn thing. I'm been run outta more federations than toads have warts...and nuthin, I SAY NUTHIN' will stop me here. I'm puttin my name in the hat so you sissies might wanna do the same... Ahhh hell,I've had darn nuff of this interview!...What are you looking at mama's boy?"
(Camera shot has Manson spit his chew onto Schiavone's foot as Manson peels off and screams: "HEEEWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!! Schiavone looks down and gulps as he sees his shoes have been ruined)
(Camera back in studio) P: Wow! I mean have you ever seen a man that angry, yet that focused? H: I'm just glad Schiavone did the interview and not me (Hayes polishes his alligator boots in front of the camera) P: Let's see the pairings for the World title tournament: The Professional vs Canadian Canuck The Specialist vs Stan Manson Apocalypse vs Cactus Jackson Lonestar vs Prince of Pain P: Now just let me remind you folks that all of these matches will take place over the next few weeks right here on Triple E TV! The finals for all the tournaments will be held the same night as Survivor Sequence! Teams of five, strive to survive! More information in the weeks to come!
H: Don't forget, the Triple E hotline is open and don't forget to have your parent's credit card ready! Just call 1-500-EEE-SHAFT. It is only two dollars for the first minute of slow talk and four dollars each additional minute.
P: So tune in this week for Worldwide Superstars, this Thursday, when all the action begins. Thanks for tuning in, I am Toad Pettingzoo along with the Dok, sayin so long till next week!
