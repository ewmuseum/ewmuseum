Wednesday Night Warfare, Part Two

24 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[PG] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Mid-West Championship Wrestling Update Wednesday Night Warfare Live from Cobo Hall Detroit, Michigan Part Two ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ LR: This match is for one fall, with TV time remaining. It is our Main Event of the Evening!!!!!!!! [Crowd Roars] LR: First, making his way to the ring area, standing 6'3, and 315 lbs, from Floral Park, New York, here is Tommy "Hardcore" Nebgen!!!!!!!!!!!!! ["Terrible Lie" By the Nine Inch Nails roars over the PA, as Tommy Nebgen comes out. He is wearing black tights with the words "Hardcore" in script in the back, black O'neal wrestling boots, white shirt cut at the sleeves with the words "HaRdCoRe" in the front and the MWCW logo in the back] JS: The crowd seems to love this guy! It looks like Tommy is going to be very fan friendly here in the MWCW! PS: Yeah but what good is a friendly crowd going to do for ya? Get ya a good deal on a used car? JS: Yeah, it well, it helps you win matches. PS: You've never wrestled, I have. The crowd means just about as much as the color of the ring ropes, which is absolutley nothing. LR: And his opponent, coming to the ring area standing an amazing 7'8, weighing 632 lbs, from Black Ridge, South Dakota, here is "Total Eclipse" Eric Black!!!!! [The Intsrumental Version of "Firestarter" by Prodigy can be heard as Eric Black makes his way to the ring area. He comes to the ring covered in a black hood and shroud. He removes them in the ring to reveal a black singlet, similar to the Giant's. He wears a black mask, kinda like Aldo Montoya's, but black. He has a large tattoo on his back of an eclipsed moon.] PS: Looks like someone forgot to schedule him for conseling when he was a kid. JS: Yes, Eric Black is a bit demented but, he can wrestle. I don't remember, did you ever face Black in the ring? PS: I don't think I did, and I don't really plan on it. I like this chair, its nice and soft and comfortable. JS: I do agree with that. Black is now in the ring and is taking off is robe and Tommy Nebgen charges in and starts in on him. Black is still in his robe and he can't see. This is like a hockey fight. PS: It sure is, but it isn't as sissy as a hockey fight. Nebgen now, backs up and clothesline Black over the top rope and Black flips over and he lands on his feet. Nebgen climbs to the top rope and he jumps and goes for a flying bodypress, but Black, somehow caught him! JS: How the hell did Black catch him? Back now takes him and rams Nebgen's back into the ringpost. Not once, not twice, not three times, but four times! Black drops him down on the floor, and now finally takes off his robe. PS: Black now picks up Nebgen and whips into the guardrail. Black now charges in with a shoulder, but Nebgen moves and Black rams shoulder and head first into the guardrail!!! JS: Black is hurtin now! Nebgen finally getting up and he is going for a chair! My god, this could get him DQed! Nebgen whinds up and............ *SMACK* JS: To the back of Eric Black's head!!!! Head refferre Skip Orlando is not calling for the bell, nor is he counting either of the men out. I guess he is going to allow them to go out at. PS: This is good, I like this!!! Nebgen has to find away for Black to stay down, and Nebgen is doing the job. Nebgen now grabs Black from off the guardrail and he has him set for the "Hardcore Driver" right on the outside of the ring area, but Black somehow picks him up and spinebuster on the outside!!! JS: Did you hear Nebgen's head and back hit the floor! Wow, what a not so nice sounding sound. PS: No, thats a good sounding sound. If you hear your grandma in the bathroom, and she is standing up, and unzipping her pants to go to the bathroom well thats a not so nice sounding sound. JS: Didn't need to hear that! Black now still recovering from that chair shot to the back of the head is grabbing something from under the ring...its....its....its a toolbox!? PS: That thing has to weigh around thirty to forty pounds and he is going to use it to smack it over the head of Nebgen!? JS: He could kill him! Black takes the toolbox and smashes it against the lower back of Tommy Nebgen!!! Look at all the stuff that fell out of the box! Hammers, wrenches, screwdrivers, etc, etc, etc. PS: I wouldn't be surprised if Black grabbed a screwdriver and well, "screwed" Nebgen with it. JS: Nebgen is starting to sort of get up, with the help of Eric Black. Both men take a licking and keep on ticking. PS: Kind of like your wife, huh JR? JS: Whatever you say Paul. Black is picking up Nebgen and now throws him into the ring. Black follows in by stepping over the ropes and now he grabs Nebgen by the neck and brings up for a chokeslam, but Nebgen is kicking and he kicks Black right in the face!!! PS: He got him right on the nose!! Black lets go and Nebgen falls down and Black has got himself a broken nose!!! JS: Nebgen now pops Black in the old ah...well the ah... PS: Scrotum! JS: If thats what you would like to call it, yes. Black now down on his knees and Nebgen with a ghettoblaster right to the back of Black's head!!! PS: I don't know how many more shots to the back of the head Black can take! JS: It sure can't be too healthy! Nebgen now going for the cover and Skip goes for the count...... REF:--1-------------------------2---and Black gets his foot on the ropes! JS: Black with those long legs gets his foot on the ropes. Nebgen pulls Black's leg and drags Black to the middle of the ring. Nebgen now going to the outside of the ring and grabs another chair! PS: He is climbing to the top rope! What the hell is he going to do....he jumps and Black rolls out of the way and Nebgen hits the chair off the mat!!! JS: Nebgen lets go of the chair and I guess there was a helluva vibration there as he is grasping his hands!!! PS: Black is still down though. He is sure lucky that he rolled out of the way cause he would be headed out on a stretcher right now. Nebgen now picks up the chair and again, he is going to try and hit Black with it. JS: He is about to use it and Black kicks the chair back at Nebgen and it hits Nebgen straight in the face!!! Black is now trying to get up and Nebgen is holding his face. PS: He can't get any uglier then he allready is! JS: Well I don't know if thats true. I guess Tommy Nebgen is a good lookin' kid. PS: So when's the wedding? JS: Oh would you stop that! Black just gave Nebgen a big boot right to the face and Nebgen goes flying into the ropes and he has his arms stuck in the ropes. PS: Not a good place to be!! Black now has the chair!!! This is like hitting the baseball of the tee....its a free shot! JS: Black has it and......... *CRACK* *CRACK* *CRACK* *CRACK* *CRACK* JS: My god!!! He just hit him five times with that chair and I am sure he would have hit him more if the chair wouldn't have broke!!! PS: That was the most unbelievable consecutive chair shots I have ever seen!! Nebgen is out and he is bleeding. You can't even see his face! Its covered in red!!! JS: Skip Orlando, the ref is trying to get Nebgen out of the ropes, but I don't know, I think its a done deal, no matter what Skip does! PS: Nebgen is hurting! He is out cold and I think Black knows this!! Black now dragging Nebgen out to the middle of the ring, much like Nebgen did to Black earlier in this match. Black goes for a nice, non-chalant cover and..... REF:--1----------------------2-----and Nebgen has the pin on Black now.......1-----------------2---------and Black kicks out! PS: How the hell can Nebgen still be conscious after all those chair shots!?! This kid has my respect. JS: Weren't you and Nebgen once scheduled to face in the FWA in a bigtime PPV? PS: Yeah, but he sort of chickened out. Anywayz, Black and Nebgen are both slowly getting up and Black charges in and nails Nebgen with a lariat. He almost took of Nebgen's head! JS: It sure seems that what he wants to do! Take off Nebgen's head! PS: This is so mother<beep>ing great!! I love these types of matches!! Black now getting up and he grabs Nebgen off the mat, and now he is putting Nebgen in a bearhug! JS: Eric Black has some extreme power and he is going to pop Nebgen like a pimple! PS: Nebgen is screaming in pain, but he lifts his knee again to the lower scrotum area, and now he belly to belly suplexe's Eric Black!!! JS: How the hell did he do that? Where is he getting this power from? Nebgen is rolling out of the ring. What is he looking for? He grabs a cane from one of the fans in the front row! PS: Nebgen slowly slides back in the ring. Nebgen is now choking Black with the cane!!! JS: Nebgen is now putting in a Full Nelson on Black, but with the cane still on his throat area!! PS: I have never seen that done before in my life! He is choking the life out of Black!! JS: Black is going down and down.....but wait!!! He just used his power snaped the cane in half, and Nebgen goes flying back!!! PS: Black getting up and Nebgen, has both of the halfs of the cane in his hands, comes charging at Black with them, but Black kicks him in the chest! JS: Black now lifts him up for the spinning powerbomb, which is Black's finisher, but Nebgen hits Black right in the eye with the broken half of the cane!!! PS: Black still has him in that powerbomb position, but he drops him right over the top rope and Nebgen lands right on his back and right on the concrete floor!! JS: My god, Black is almost eight feet tall, plus the ring is about five feet from off the ground! That is almost a fifteen foot drop!!! PS: Skip Orlando is now starting the count and Black is checking out his eye and he is bleeding from the eye and his nose. 1........... 2.......... 3.......... JS: Wait a minute!!! The team of Manslaughter just attacked Eric Black from behind!!! Torture hit him in the back of the head with one of the tag titles!!! PS: The ref has no idea whats going on, he is continuing counting! JS: Torture is setting up the both belts on the mat. Misery has Black in a piledriving position and Torture is going to the middle rope. They just spiked piledrived Eric Black on both of the MWCW Tag Team titles and Black is out. REF:.........7......................8................9 JS: Misery and Torture are now leaving the ring, like nothing happend and the ref just counted out Tommy Nebgen! PS: Man, I wonder what they hell they were doing out there and what involvement they have in this match. LR: The winner of this match, via a countout, "Total Eclipse" Eric Black!!!!!! PS: What a match!! MWCW Medics are coming out and both these men will be taken out on a stretcher! JS: I hope there is a local hospital close to here because both these men need instant medical attention ASAP! PS: Man, what a great match! Thats all you will see in MWCW, are great matches!!! JS: I agree with you there Paul! Folks,we are out of time, hope you enjoyed the show! So long everybody! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ MWCW™ ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ -RMM- Handler of: Primal Fear, Graphic Violence [Booze Murphy & Leech Rogers], "Stylin" Tommy Blayze, & "Hardcore" Joey Haven
