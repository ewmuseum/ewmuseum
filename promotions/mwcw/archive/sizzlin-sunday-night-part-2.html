Sizzlin' Sunday Night, Part Two

20 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Lance Russel]: This 4 corners match up is set for the MWCW Tag-Team Title. This match will be Battle Royal Style where you must eliminate both wrestlers. Fist coming down the Ring coming down the ring to the Stars and Stripes and weighing a total combined weight of 600 pounds here is Eagle Eye, and Liberty the All-Americans! [Paul Smith]: Not a super pop for the All-Americans but they are getting there share this should be a tough match up for all 4 teams! [LR]: Next coming down the ring to the music of Quad City DJ and a total combined weight of 560 pounds here is the Team of Coal-Train and Bullet-Train Watley the Wrong Side of the Tracks! JR Shaw: They aren't taking any time to the ring and they bust to the ring to a good size pop! The WST is not a well known tag-team and is looking to brake into the big time! LR: Coming to the Ring next to More Human than Human and a total combined weight of 500 pounds here is Mark Talbain and John Fenris The Exterminators! PS: Look from the crowd here comes Manslaughter for a sneak attack and this 8 man battle royal is on! Talbain just hit a front spring kick to the side of the head of Bullet and and Misery and Eagle are going at it in another corner! JS: Coal Train hit a big double axe handle to the back of Liberty's heads and is pounding on his back and Torture and Fenris are having a test of stregth in the center of the ring! PS: Talbain slide under the bottom rope and grabbed a steel chair and Bullet-Train rolls out also and Talbain rams the steel chair cross over his back and then takes it back into left field and swings wildly but Bullet rolls away from it and kicks Talbain in the knee and Talbain drops the chair! JS: Inside the ring Torture kicks Fenris in the midsection and hits a big swinging neck breaker! I will take a little break to say that you can elimate anybody by over the toprope, submission or pinfall. Two Refs are in the ring and the action is hot and heavy as all of these 8 men are giving it their all! PS: 6 inside the ring and 2 outside as Bullet hits a big drop-kick on the floor and Talbain hits hard on the guard rail! Inside the ring Coal-Train Irish Whips Liberty off the ropes but from behind Eagle-Eye hits a head-lock bulldog on Coal Train and Liberty drops an elbow on the back of head of Coal-Train! Misery goes to the second rope and hits a flying clothesline on Eagle-Eye and Eagle-Eye flies outside of the ring but though the second rope! JS: On the outside of the ring Talbain has taken control with repeated blows to the head of Bullet with the steel Guard Rail! Talbain Irish Whips Bullet into the steel Guard Rail and Bullet rams chest first and almost falls over. Talbain grabs the chair and slams it across the back of Bullet. PS: Coal-Train and Misery now are pounding on each other while the All-Americans watch Idlely by and begin to giggle! Coal-Train and Misery turn around are look stunned at the All-Americans and charge the All-Americans! JS: The All-Americans duck and Misery and Coal-Train titter on the toprope and the All-Americans push both over the toprope and Coal Train and Misery....NO! Misery slides under the bottom rope but Coal-Train wasn't so lucky as he lands hard on the floor! The WST is know a man down as The All-Americans begin to put the boots to Misery. PS: Torture and Fernis are chopping each other in the center of the ring and Torture hits a nasty headbutt and then a drop-toe hold on Fernis and quickly moves into a side headlock! JS: On the outside Tabian has Bullet in a sleeperhold but Bullet delivers a mule kick that brings Tabian to his knees. Bullet shakes his head and wipes some blood from his busted open head and looks for something to grab. PS: Bullet sees that dented and bloodied chair and goes to hit Tabian with it but Tabian pays back Bullet with a 4arm straight to the groin and Bullet gets a look on his face that every man fears and I think all men in the audince winced a little. JS: Torture Military presses Fernis and slams him down to the canvas and Torture picks Liberty and Eagle-Eye who have been putting the boots to Misery for about 2 minutes hits a double noggen knocker on the All-Americans and Liberty gets Suplexed out of his boots. PS: Misery climbs to the toprope and Torture sets up for a piledriver and they hit a big time spiked piledriver on Eagle-Eye and, 1, 2, 3, Eagle Eye has been Elimated. So WST and the All-Americans are down 1 man! JS: On the outside of the ring Tabian has Bullet in a boston Crab and a ref is outside to see if he submits. Out from the back charges Coal-Train and grabs the Steel-Chair and slams it across the back of Tabian's back and Tabian brakes the hold. No DQ in this one! Coal-Train press slams Tabian over his head and drops him head first on the ring apron with a version of the Train Wreck! PS: Coal-Train is walking back to the locker room as both Tabian and Bullet are on the floor on the outside. Inside of the ring. Misery has Fernis in a headlock and Liberty Irish Whips Torture and hits a big dropkick that takes Torture back into the turn buckle. Liberty backs up into the other corner and charges for an Avalanche but Torture using his power just catches Liberty and dumps him over the toprope and both of the All-Americans have been elimated! JS: Nobody has moved on the outside but on the inside of the ring both of Manslaughter is pounding on Fernis of the Exterminators. They Irish Whip him off the rope and Fernis ducks a double clothesline and bounces off the rope and hits a spinning heel kick on Misery but Torture is all over Fernis and pounding on his back! PS: On the outside a bloodied Bullet-Train gets up off the floor and climbs up onto the apron and onto the toprope! Bullet lets fly and nails Torture with a flying shouldblock called the Bullet Missle. Torture rams into the turnbuckle and Bullet hits a German Suplex. 1, 2, 3, and Torture has been elimated. JS: Only 4 men left. Torture on the way out pushes a camera man down and Now Bullet-Train, Tabian, Misery, and Fernis are still alive in this match. Bullet Train rolls outside and picks up a lifeless Tabian tosses him back into the ring. Misery rolls up Tabian but Fernis kicks Misery in the back of the head. Bullet grabs a new steel chair and climbs to the toprope and is perched on the toprope. Fernis though stops Bullet on the toprope and Fernis hits a dropkick on Bullet and Bullet lands hard on the top turnbuckle! PS: Fernis spring boards off the toprope and hits a spinning roundhouse kick but bullet shields himself with the chair but the chair flies into Bullet's face and Bullet lands hard on the floor a bloody physical mess. JS: In the process Fernis landed hard on the mat and Misery hits a big spring board elbow drop for the 1, 2, and 3 and Fernis is elimated! Only 2 men remain as a semi lifeless Scott Tabian and a some what still fresh Misery. Misery gets to his feet and grabs Tabian and hits a vicious powerbomb on Tabian. 1, 2, 3, and Manslaughter is the new and First MWCW Tag-Team Champions! LR: The winners of this match, and the FIRST MWCW Tag Team Champions, Misery & Torture, the team of Manslaughter!!!!!!!!!! PS: Wait a second Coal Train grabs one of the titles as he rushes to the ring and levels Misery with the belt and Misery falls like a sack of potatos and Turture races from the back but Coal-Train exits from the ring and grabs Bullet and head to the back! Turture grabs one of the titles and helps his partner up! What a hard hitting match! Manslaughter looks solid! Both challanges will come from everybody! Lets get back to the ring for the next match! PS: Well....now you see things my way. JS: No. Nebgen working over the face of Bradley with repeated kicks. PS: That's the greco-roman kick to the face. JS: Whatever. Nebgen now drops the leg across the head of Bradley. Bradley in serious pain and Vanessa is trying to get the crowd to cheer her man on.....she's working them around... PS: Nebgen rolls out of the ring... JS: And Nebgen walks right up behind Vanessa...spins her around...and KISSES HER!!!! WHOA!!!!! PS: She's not getting up from THAT one. JS: What about him? PS: Ah...all in a hard day's work. JS: Well, he gets in the ring and Bradley gets back to his feet.....Bradley sees his manager down!!! PS: Ooooh...Tarzan get mad. JS: Bradley is incensed! IT'S OVER!!! PS: Nebgen covers... JS: ONE!!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!!!!!! [music: "Terrible Lie" (Nine Inch Nails)] PS: I told ya, Shaw, Nebgen would win...
