[MWCW-PPV] Blast Off '97, Part Three

1 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---Continued From Part Two--- JS: Folks, I think its about time we will go to our main event. Two men, two best friends, will go at it, for the MWCW World Title. One man will be the winner and the other, will be in the books as the that was beaten for the first MWCW World Title. PS: Yeah, but will Gruesome be competing. JS: Right now, we have "Mean" Jean Murphy in the back, Jean? ["Mean" Jean Murphy is standing next to a man who is in a suit. Behind them is the MWCW Logo] JM: Folks, I am here with a local doctor that is he to make sure all wrestlers are okay before and after their matches. His name is Dr. David Lang. He has been looking over Ozzy Gruesome since Ozzy's attack in the first round match up. Dr. Lang, what comments do you have to make? Dr. DL: I feel that Ozzy Gruesome WILL indeed be okay, BUT thats if he does not wrestle in this match up. I have looked over Ozzy for the past hour or so, and have noticed that he does not have a full turn with his neck, so he is not able to move it. This may due to strain in the neck, or it could be bone movement in there. We have NOT yet taken an x-ray, so I do not know the exact diagnosis. I think it would favor Ozzy, if he did not wrestle in this match, till we had the exact diagnosis. JM: So, if he does not wrestle, he will be okay, but if he does, he will be risking serious injury? Dr. DL: Like I said, it may just be strain. He had all movement in his arms and legs, so it was not a stinger, but I just can't differantiate the strain to the bone movement, so I just don't like the idea of him wrestling tonight. [Ozzy Gruesome comes in to the scene. He has his neck wrapped in ace bandage with ice in the bandage] OZZY: Dr. Lang, I respect you, but I did not come all this way to NOT wrestle in the finals. So no offense to you, but I am seeking a second opinion, and thats from the fans, and I know they think I am allright to wrestle! [Cuts back to JR Shaw and Paul Smith] JS: Ozzy Gruesome will be wrestling tonight and he will be facing Lobo. These two men are best friends outside the ring, but whats it going to be like, inside the ring. PS: I will give Ozzy this, he is one tough SOB. JS: One tough SOB he is. You are right on the button there. I guess we are ready, lets go to Lance Russell! LR: Ladies and Gentlemen, it is now time for the main event of the evening. It is NOW Time for the Finals in the MWCW World Title Tourney!!!!!! [The fans in the Pontiac Silverdome just explode with enthusiasm] LR: First making his way to the ring area, He stands 6'10, weighs 385 pounds, from Charlotte, North Carolina, here is Lobo!!!!!!!!!! ["One Last Mouse" By the Mighty Mighty Bosstones roars over the loudspeakers as Lobo makes his way down the aisle. Comes to the ring w/a black leather trenchcoat and Louisville Slugger] JS: Lobo seems to be keeping his mind on the match at hand. Though it is against his best friend, who I might add, is risking a severe injury, and Lobo is just trying to black that out. PS: But how long can he do that. I mean, how long can he pretend that he is not wrestling his best friend, who is more like a brother to him. JS: Thats a good question and we are about to find out. LR: Next, making his way to the ring area, standing 6'10, weighing 328 lbs, from Berlin, Germany, here is Ozzy Gruesome!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ["Antichrist Superstar" By Marilyn Manson roars over the loudspeaker as Ozzy Gruesome makes his way to the ring area. Ozzy Gruesome has Long, brown leather Trench coat over his tights. Black, leather, top hat. Black, padded gloves, like a Biker would wear when riding. He also has some ace bandage around his neck. He is walking very gingerly to the ring] JS: I don't know if this is a good idea. Ozzy just doesn't look right. PS: Ozzy NEVER looks right. JS: Thats true, but...... PS: The doctor said it may just be a sprain and it could be no big deal. JS: Yeah, and he also said that it could be bone movement and could be VERY severe. PS: I guess we will have to find out how serious it is during this match. Anyways, Ozzy is finally in the ring and we may just be ready to do this thing. The refferee of this match is.......FAT. JS: Would you stop that. Thats Senior official Ray Taylor and yes, he is a bit big boned. PS: Yeah, like dinosaur boned. JS: Allright, Lobo and Gruesome now meet in the middle of the ring and they shake each other's hands. They are saying something to each other, and I can't quite read their lips. PS: I sure wish I knew what they were saying to each other, but I guess it doesn't matter, since I don't speak ebonics anyways. JS: The bell sounds and here we go!!! Lobo and Gruesome now lock up in the middle of the ring. Lobo is the stronger of the two and he pushes Gruesome into the corner. PS: He begins punching away at the gut and chest area, trying to knock the wind out of Gruesome, but now Gruesome reverses it and now Lobo is in the corner. Ozzy is now pounding away at Lobo. JS: Gruesome now takes Lobo and sends him into the opposing corner and Ozzy comes charging in at him, but Lobo moves and Ozzy goes shoulder first into the post and Lobo rolls him up from behind........ REF:--1-----------------------------------2-------and Gruesome kicks out! JS: That was pretty close. We have had many close calls tonight in all of our matches. Lobo now takes Ozzy off the mat and lifts him up and now sets him on the top turnbuckle. PS: Lobo now going to follow him up and now both men are standing up on the top turnbuckle and what the hell is going to happen up there? It looks like Lobo is trying to go for a super superplex, but Ozzy isn't going to let that happen. JS: Ozzy now has Lobo in the belly to belly suplex position, but if Ozzy goes for that, he will land on the spanish speaking broadcast table. PS: There they go and........... *CRAAAAASSSSSHHHHHHHHHHHH* PS: Oh my god!!! I can't believe that!!! JS: My god, that had to be one of the hardest impacts and crashes I have EVER seen in my whole life. PS: A word of advice, if you are a foriegn broadcaster and you decide to broadcast at the MWCW Events, put your tables away from ringside. First the Germany table and now the Spanish one. JS: Both of these men are still down. When they went through that table the monitor slid down and hit Ozzy right in the side of the head. PS: Refferee Ray Taylor isn't even counting, which makes sense, cause we can't have a double countout in this match. JS: We just might have to take it, cause neither of these men are moving. Jose Rodriguez & Pepe Gardino, the spanish broadcasters are trying to help both men, but that isn't doing anything, cause neither of them is really moving yet. PS: That Jose is a big boy! He is picking up Ozzy and now he carries him over and slides him back into the ring. Now he is picking up Lobo and now carrying him over and slides him back into the ring. JS: Lobo rolls right on top of Ozzy!!!!!!!!!! And now the cover................. REF:--1------------------------------2------------------------------------ 3....no Ozzy just kicked out!!! JS: How the HELL did he kick out! I think that had to be instinct cause there is no way Ozzy knows where the hell he is or what the hell he is doing, nor does Lobo. PS: I think Ozzy is starting to gather up some energy and is finally sort of moving around, but Lobo on the other hand, I don't think he knows what the hell is up. JS: Ozzy rolls over to the ring ropes and he is trying to get up, but he sure is moving slowly. On the side of his by his temple, you can see a big bump, which is from when the TV monitor hit him in the head. PS: Yeah, you can also notice on Lobo's back, he has some pretty ugly scratches from the table. JS: Gruesome now finally up and appears coherent. He begins walking over towards Lobo and gives him a nice swift kick to the chest. PS: Hey, Ozzy should go be a field goal kicker for The Jets, they need all the help they can get. JS: He now lifts Lobo up to his feet. He can barley stand. Ozzy now grabs the left arm of Lobo and Ozzy gives Lobo a nice sabuk kick to the chin and by holding that arm, Lobo can't even fall down. PS: Thats good point there JR. He is holding him up for more punishment. Ozzy now going for the Russian Leg Sweep, but somehow Lobo blocks it, and now Lobo makes it so Ozzy hits face first on the mat!!! JS: That was quite a nice reversal by Lobo. The guy didn't get much respect from the wrestlers in this tourney, cept from Ozzy, and now Lobo shows them all that he can hang with the best of them. PS: Lobo now finally getting some energy back in this match, which is good to see. Lobo getting up and begins to walk around and is trying to get some energy and wake himself up. We have seen both men do this, so in this short time, both men appear winded. JS: After going through that table, you can understand why. PS: Oh yeah. I have been through a couple tables in my day, and I know, that with the right impact, you can be sore for days after going through them. JS: I don't think I want to know. In FWA, you may remember, Malice through me through a glass window. PS: So, when you saw Malice tonight, I am sure that scared the hell out of you, huh? JS: Moving right along.....Gruesome is getting up, but Lobo grabs him by the head and a swinging neckbreaker!!! PS: He snapped it quite nicely and Ozzy is holding his neck!! Lobo looks like he is concerned but he picks up Ozzy again and now he whips him to the ropes, Ozzy bounces back and Lobo back bodydrops him. Now Lobo bounces off and jumps up and drives his first right into the forehead of Ozzy and now the cover............. REF:--1-----------------------------2---------and Ozzy kicks out! PS: Lobo quickly gets up and jumps up again and drops a legdrop right to the facial area of Gruesome and another cover............ REF:--1---------------------------2-------and Ozzy kicks out! PS: Now Lobo is taking out his aggretion on the refferee ad he is telling him to count faster. Lobo, man, you and I both know, concentrate on your opponent, specially when your opponent is Ozzy Gruesome! JS: Finally Lobo realizes that arguing with the ref won't help him win this match, since its wrestling and not debate. PS: Well I am sure Lobo is the Master Debater. JS: Would you stop that!? PS: What? What did I do? JS: You know what you said. Ozzy Gruesome now is up and Lobo grabs him by the neck, and looks like he is going to go for a chokeslam, but Ozzy grabs the arm that he is trying chokeslam him with, and trips Lobo and holds onto that arm. He has Lobo in some kind of arm lock, and Lobo is in pain. PS: Gruesome may be able to break Lobo's arm! He has that thing locked pretty well, and he has him in a pinning predictament and the cover........... REF:--1-----------------------------2-----------------------and Lobo gets his other arm up. PS: Now Ozzy begins to stand up, pulling Lobo up with him. Ozzy lets go of the hold and drives his forearm right in to the face of Lobo. JS: Ozzy steps back and now comes at Lobo, who steps to the left and pushes Ozzy to go over the ropes, but Ozzy's head gets stuck between the ropes and he is all tangled in the ropes!!! PS: This is not good for Ozzy's neck!!! Those ropes are going to pop his head right off. JS: Lobo now looks very concerned. He and Ray Taylor are now trying to get Ozzy loose. They are both pulling the ropes and finally they have him out of there and now Ozzy is out cold and laying on the mat. PS: Go for the pin LOBO!!!!! Lobo go for the damn pin you dingledorf! JS: Lobo is checking Ozzy's pulse and seeing if he is breathing! It appears that Ozzy is breathing, cause his belly is moving up and down. PS: Lobo has the chance to pin him, but he is acting like this is an episode of "ER". JS: He now has a sort of evil grin on his face. He is picking up Gruesome from the mat and now it looks like he is going for the Lo-Bomb!!!! PS: You go boy!!! Damage is good! He is wasting sometime, but maybe he wants this to be perfect! JS: He brings him up, but Ozzy does a frankenstiener!!! Ozzy stays on him and has the legs hooked and now the ref goes for the count.............. REF:--1-----------------------------------2------------------------------- ------3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! PS: HOW THE HELL DID HE DO THAT!!!???? HE PULLED THAT OUT OF NOWHERE!!!!! LR: Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this match, and the FIRST EVER MWCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION................. OZZYYYYYYYYYY GRUEEEEESSSSSOMMMMMMEEEEEEEEEEEEE JS: Ozzy has done it! Ozzy Gruesome has won the title and he looks shocked, as well does Lobo! PS: Lobo wasted too much time and showed too much compassion for his friend. I guess thats a good thing, if this were an episode of "Full House" but this is MWCW. JS: They now hand Ozzy the belt and he has it grasped in his arms!!! Finally he stands up and shows his German fans the belt that he worked his butt off to get!! PS: If I were Lobo, I would attack him from behind and take that belt. C'mon Lobo, be man and beat the crap out of him. JS: Lobo walks over and he turns Ozzy around and embraces him with a big hug!!! PS: Awwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwww......if I would have known this was going to love connection, I wouldn't even showed up! JS: Folks we are out of time, but for all the MWCW Staff. Good night, and we would like to Congratulate Desperado and Ozzy Gruesome for being the first ever MWCW Singles champions!!!! [Credits Roll as a Stil of Ozzy Gruesome hold the MWCW Belt in his hands] In Memory of Princess Diana. You will be missed! ~~~~~~~~~~~ MWCW™ ~~~~~~~~~~~ -RMM- Handler of: Primal Fear, Graphic Violence [Booze Murphy & Leech Rogers], "Stylin" Tommy Blayze, & "Hardcore" Joey Haven
