There are people just getting to their seats and there are others that have been in their seats allready. Then we take a look at the massive crowd just coming into the Silverdome. Then finally we see Chucky Lane standing in front of the entrance to the ring area and finally he speaks] LANE: Hello MWCW Fans here at the Pontiac Silverdome!!!!!! We are only thirty-five minutes away from the biggest PPV in wrestling's history........ARE YOU READY!!!!!!! [Crowd can be heard screaming "YAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH"] CL: Folks the clock is ticking. We are going to find out, who just will be the MWCW World Champion. We will find out who will be the Mid-West Champion. Will Manslaughter retain their titles? Will Tommy Van Dam live up to his hype? It will all happen tonight, here at the MWCW Blast Off PPV!!! Right now, lets go to "Mean" Jean Murphy who is in the back locker room, Jean? ["Mean" Jean Murphy is standing in the back locker where you can see MWCW Officials walking back and forth. She is wearing a nice little outfit, but its a conservative one at that] JM: Hey Chucky! I am back here, looking for people to talk to, but everyone is so pumped up about the PPV, that they are locking their locker room doors, so I can't get a word with anybody! [Suddenly a HUGE 7 Footer steps out and scares Jean] JM: Who are you? MAN: EVERYBODY SHUT UP BECAUSE DEATH ADDER HAS SOMETHING TO SAY! MWCW, wrestling's scariest wrestler is here! I have dominated all over the wrestling world, and now I come here to MWCW to continue my dominance! I have seen some of these losers here in MWCW and I know I can take any of them. Sign me a match, and you will see the result of my domination! JM: Where did you come from and who brought you here? DEATH ADDER: I wrestled in the MWF, and in other wrestling orginazations. If you are questioning my ability, I can prove you that I am what I say I am. Who brought me here is no one's business. JM: Are you going to involve yourself in tonight's card? DEATH ADDER: I am here to scout out someone in particular. I ain't here to interject just quite yet, but my time is coming and there for you all better be scared! [Death Adder walks away and "Mean" Jean looks very surprised] JM: Well Chucky, I guess anything can happen on LIVE TV. Back to you! [Chucky Lane is now standing in the middle of the ring] CL: WOW! Unbelievable! Former MWF World Champion is here! This has to be one of the superstars signed by the MWCW Offfices! Folks, we are now ready for the Free-For-All Match, but now, lets go to the commentators, Paul Smith & JR Shaw!!! [Paul Smith & JR Shaw are both in nice suits. They are both looking excited for the start of this whole PPV] JS: Hello fans of MWCW Wrestling! The time has come and we are here at the first MWCW PPV. We are here at MWCW Blast Off, but this is only the Free For All, but still, its the start of the PPV! PS: This is the start of something that I am sure will last along long time! JS: I certainly hope so! We are almost ready for our Free-For-All Match Up and the winner goes into the first round of the Mid-West Title Tourney. . . . . . . . [All of the sudden "Crash into Me" by Dave Mathews Band roars over the loudspeaker] PS: What the hell....it can't be who I think it is? [A man standing around 6'2 and weighing around 250 lbs comes walking out. He begins to walk over to the broadcast booth] PS: Damn it, its CRASH!! I hate that <beep>hole! JS: CRASH is a superstar form NWWF and LWF. He has been getting paid the BIG BUCKS all over and now he is here! [Crash puts a headset on] CRASH: Well Paul Smith, we meet again. PS: Yeah, unfortunatley for me. Damn Crash...two words....Tic....Tac! CRASH: Smith, you always trying to be funny, but whats funny is your win loss record. Hell Smith, everyone knows you're a washed up hasbeen who can't make it anymore, so he comes over here and becomes a "commentator". PS: Listen up assbreath, I still wrestle in FWA, WWFL, and other leagues, so don't start with me. CRASH: Oh yeah Smith, those are the BIG leagues. Come on Smith, just admit, you don't have it anymore. You are like the Hulk Hogan of wrestling now. Just can't let the young guys take over. JS: Come on guys, lets stop arguing and lets go to the Free-For-All match up. While you two boys were arguing, both men were allready introduced. [We see "Super Sexy" Steve Wells & Pancho in the middle ring getting ready to lock up] CRASH: This Steve Wells is about as Sexy as Paul's Ugly <beep> mama! PS: Well you know Crash, I could have been your daddy, but the monkey beat me up the stairs! JS: Can you two stop beraiding each other and keep your head on this match. We have two very young talented wrestlers in the ring. PS: Yeah, and I am sure either one of them could whipe the mat with Crash. Crash, I don't know why you think your so high and mighty when you haven't achieved anything here in MWCW. CRASH: I will prove myself, don't worry Paul. Aslong as I don't have to wrestle hasbeens like you, it should be a tough climb, but a worthy climb. JS: While these two argue, I going to commentate. These two men are getting ready to go at it, but wait a minute. Some man in a a cowboy hat, tight jeans and cowboy boots is making his way to the ring area. On the back of his vest it says "Hawk" PS: Hey, thats Big Christian Hawk's nephew or son or something! He said he was going to send him to MWCW. JS: He is climbing in the ring now and he clotheslines both Pancho and Wells. He grabs Pancho, and brings him up and powerbombs him, but making his head snap off the top turnbuckle!!!! CRASH: I like this stuff. Reminds me of Smith in the early eighties! JS: He now grabs Steve Wells and he spits in his face!!! Now he slaps him!!! He picks him up and does the same powerbomb on Wells, but Wells falls on top of Pancho and the ref counts....... REF:--1-------------------------------2--------------------------3!!!!!!!!! LR: The winner of this match, and advancing to the First Round of the Mid-West Title Tourney, "Super Sexy" Steve Wells!!!!!!!!! JS: What was the point of that? Why did he do that? CRASH: Maybe he saw Paul Smith's mom and had to take out his aggretion on everyone else. [Paul Smith rises as does Crash] PS: You know Crash, I have had about all out of you that I want to hear. CRASH: Oh really? PS: But, out of respect for the MWCW Staff who has hired me, I am going to give you a chance to walk out, cause I am not going to do anything. My job here in MWCW is to commentate, nothing more. [Crash rubs Paul's head like dad's do to their sons] CRASH: I always knew you were a coward Smith. This just proves it. I am outta here. MWCW Crash is here, and be prepared for a Crash Landing coming near you! [Crash leaves. As he does, we see "Mean" Jean Murphy with the man that attacked Wells and Pancho] JM: Mr. Hawk, what are you doing? SCOTT HAWK: Folks, if you don't know who I am. I am Scott "The Impaler" Hawk, and I am here with a relative of mine, Big Christian Hawk. The reason for my doings is I am sick of Steve Wells. The guys calls himself this "Super Sexy" name and he ain't nothing but a pretty boy with no talent. I hate guys like that, and thats why I did what I did. JM: Yeah, but why help him win his match? SH: I didn't help him win. It was purley coincidental that he won. Now if you don't mind. I've got things to see, and people to do. JM: So many surprises allready folks and this was just the Free-For-All. The number is on the screen and make your orders now! For those of you who ordered the PPV, we will continue, for those who didn't, later!!! ~MWCW Blast Off~ -Only on PPV- $17.95 1-800-4-MWCW -RMM- Handler of: Primal Fear, Graphic Violence [Booze Murphy & Leech Rogers], "Stylin" Tommy Blayze, & "Hardcore" Joey Haven
