SFW Sunday Night Explosion

5 April 1999

(A camera pans around the screaming fans inside the arena,as some heavy metal music plays in the background.A big SFW Explosion logo comes up on screen,and is then cleared by flames coming out the bottom of the screen.The camera then switches to the announce position.)
MF:Welcome everyone,to the next wave in the wrestling world.This is Super
Fire Wrestling,and these 15,000 fans have packed into the sold-out Copps
Coliseum to see Sunday Night Explosion!!!Mikey Freedom along with Bobby
Ventura here to bring you the best in wrestling action.What a card to start off with Bobby.
BV:You got that right,the first round of the TV title tourney,an HOW grudge match between Taryn Archer and the Twister,plus the war of words between
Cremator and Casey Stanfield will be settled in our main event.
(The lights go out in the arena) MF:Hey,wait a minute,folks,I think we've had a power cut.... (The big screen over the entranceway flashes up GET READY in big red letters.Then it is followed by SUPER FIRE WRESTLING IS ABOUT in even bigger writing,then TO EXPLODE fills the screen.Just then the four corners of the ring explode with fireworks and a huge explosion goes off at the entranceway.After it dies down,Fire walks out and makes his way down to the ring.He gets in and takes the mic off of the the ring announcer.)
Fire:Now you all know why Fire is here.Because I am the most explosive superstar in the sport today.It don't matter who they put in front of me.The
Fireball will engulf them all.Just like this punk Marshall.Bring him out here,let me teach him a lesson.
(Fire throws the mic back to the announcer as the New Generation theme plays) Announcer:Ladies and Gentleman,this is a Television title first round match,scheduled for one fall,with a 15 minute time limit.Coming down the aisle,representing the New Generation,weighing in at 231 pounds,'The
Revolution'Joey Marshalllllll!!!! And in the ring at this time,weighing 281 pounds,Fire!!!!! MF:This should be a real good match,Fire's power vs Marshall's speed.Who are you for,Bobby?
BV:I'm rooting for the HOW guy,Fire.I mean,they did employ us at one time,remember? MF:Yeah,but I think Joey could have a big enough speed advantage to snuff out the Fireball.And there's the bell and the match is underway.
(Joey Marshall catches Fire and tries to lift him up,but Fire reverses and back body drops Joey to the mat.Fire picks him up,throws him off the ropes and connects with a powerslam.)
BV:Joey should have kknown not to try a power move this early,and he paid for it big time. (The two men exchange forearm blows,then get into a test of strength.Joey suddenly lets go and executes a Dragon Screw,sending Fire out of the ring.Fire quickly reenters and starts chasing Joey.Joey tries to evade the bigger man,but gets caught and is subjected to a number of knee lifts to his face.)
MF:Fire's quickly trying to exert his authority on the smaller man,to good effect.Joey has got to try and stay mobile and not get into a grappling match.
BV:Or he's dead meat. MF:Yeah,there's no hope for him. (Fire body slams Marshall,then stes him up for the Fireball.) MF:Look out,Joey's going for a ride!! (Fire sets it up,but Joey escapes and executes a bulldog) BV:What an escape,the kid's got an opening now!! (Joey starts stomping on Fire,then puts him in a Torture Abdominal Stretch.after a few seconds,Fire escapes.Both men rise to their feet and Joey starts forcing Fire into the corner with a few kicks.From nowhere,Fire grabs
Joey and Belly-To-Belly whips him over the top rope to the floor!!!!)
MF:Oh my god!!!Joey Marshall must have been at least 12 feet off the ground!!How can he still be concious after a bump like that?????
(Marshall gets groggily to his feet,and reenters the ring.Fire slams him and then throws him out of the ring again.Fire gets up on the rope and shouts at the crowd.)
BV:Come on Fire,it's a wrestling match,not a popularity contest!! (Marshall gets back in,and Fire continues to ponud on him.He takes him down with a punch to the back of the head,then applies a Bow and Arrow.Joey screams in agony,but doesn't submit.he escapes,but Fire quickly applies another one,Joey struggles free and in desparation kicks Fire in the knee.)
MF:Joey's got a brief respite.Can he take advantage of it? (The Revolution slams Fire.He picks him up,then does a Frankensteiner out of the ring.The fight moves outside as the ref starts his count.Fire gets up and walks into THE FULL CIRCLE!!!)
MF:Full Circle!Full Circle!!Joey's got the advantage now.Fire is in real trouble. (Joey throws Fire back in.He picks him up,then executes a Frankensteiner.the ref counts,but Fire kicks out at 2.Joey goes for a second Frankensteiner,but
Fire powerbombs him in desparation.)
BV:The kid was so close,but made a rookie mistake.Never go for the same move twice. (Fire takes a second for a breather,then goes towards Joey.Marshall jumps up and starts hammering on Fire,then throws him outside to a big cheer from the crowd.When Fire gets back in,Joey goes for a suplex,btu Fire reverses it and hits one of his own.Joey gets up very slowly,and looks in trouble.Fire closes in and hits a Screwdriver.)
MF:This could be it.I think Joey's got no more to give. (Fire picks up Joey's legs and turns him over into a Scorpion Deathlock in the centre of the ring.Marshall tries to hang on,screaming all the time,but then starts tapping furiously.)
BV:He tapped out!!Fire takes the first ever win in SFW!!! Announcer:Here is your winner by submission,Fire!!!!!! (Fire kicks Marshall out of the ring and starts showing off as the camera goes back to the announce table.)
MF:Fire taking the win after Marshall tapped out to the Scorpion Deathlock.I think Fire could be a force to be reckoned with in the final.
BV:Don't think you've seen the last of Joey Marshall though,the kid has got some heart,and he will improve.
MF:The next match on the card sees Tyler Jones,known as 'The Titan',taking on
Flame,in a battle of the cruiserweights.
BV:Both guys looked good in training,but I think Flame may have the edge in this one. MF:Let's get to the ring for the introductions. Announcer:This match is scheduled for one fall,with a 15 minute time limit.Coming down the aisle,weighing in at 200 pounds,'The Titan'Tyler
Jones!!! And his opponent,weighing in at 222 pounds,Flame!!!!!! MF:As Flame makes his way to the ring,just got time to tell you that Explosion next week will be from Albany,New York.The main event will be the final of the
TV title tournament,a 4 man elimination brawl.We know Fire will be in that final,and the other entrants will be found tonight.Now let's get to the ring
for Tyler vs Flame. (Flame gets a couple of knee lifts in as the bell rings.He throws down Tyler and puts on a chickenwing,which Tyler gets out of easily.Tyler then executes a couple of takedowns and elbowdrop combinations)
BV:Early on,Jones is trying to wear Flame down,while Flame is looking for working Tyler's head.Trying to knock his brain in?
MF:I don't think that will work. (Flame slams Tyler down a couple of times,then throws him out the ring.Tyler gets in,and walks onto a swinging neckbreaker.Both men get up,and Tyler takes down Flame.Tyler presses the advantage with a body slam.He goes for a DDT,but
Flame gets a couple of punches into Tyler's ribs.)
BV:Tyler's upping his game.He going for a bit more power now.Flame better keep up. (Flame slams Tyler down and goes up top.He executes a Corkscrew Plancha,but
Tyler rolls out of the way,and Flame hits mat.Tyler picks up Flame and delivers a bone-crunching Piledriver.Tyler picks him up and delivers a second
Piledriver,followed by a Belly-to-Belly suplex.he then goes to the top and goes for the Moonsault Press,but Flame rolls away.)
MF:That could have finished it. (Tyler executes another Belly-to-Belly,and then bulldogs Flame out of the ring.When Flame goes back in,Tyler slams him down.He goes up top for another
Moonsault press,but misses it.Flame gets up and suplexes Tyler Jones.He follows it with a Snap Suplex,then sets The Titan up for a powerbomb.He throws him up,but Tyler reverses it into a Huracan
Rana...1.....2....KICKOUT.Flame gets up and plants a kick on Tyler.Tyler then blocks a second kick,and locks on the KING OF KINGS!!)
BV:Ooh,baby.Call it,ref,this one is over. (Flame desparately reaches for a rope and just snags it,and Tyler is forced to break the hold.Flame backs off,but then form nowhere executes the awesome
Eiffel Parachute!He covers...1....2......KICKOUT!Flame picks him up and places him on the turnbuckle.He climbs up to execute a Superplex,but Tyler pushes him off,and lands the Shooting Star Press...1...2....
3.NO !Flame kicks out.Tyler goes for a suplex,but Flame reverses into the JUMPING DDT!He picks
Tyler up and delivers a powerbomb,but Tyler kicks out at 2.)
BV:Tyler's taking some punishment.I can't believe he's still in there. (Tyler suddenly catches Flame in a piledriver.He picks him up and locks in the
King of Kings,but Flame again reaches a rope.Tyler in utter frustation,Tyler starts biting Flame.)
MF:Hey,Tyler,what are you doing?Ref,stop him!! (Tyler breaks at 3.Flame is spurting blood.Flame backs off and hits another
Eiffel Parachute,then another...1...2...KICKOUT.Flame goes for yet another but
Tyler pulls off a Belly-to-Belly then locks on the King of Kings!The ref asks
Flame if he should stop the fight.Flame doesn't respond for a moment then starts nodding frantically.The ref calls for the bell.)
Announcer:Your winner,'The Titan' Tyler Jones!!!!!!!! BV:Flame couldn't take it!Tyler Jones wins with his patented Hammerlock,the
King of Kings!!Hey Mikey,where you going?
(Mikey gets up from his chair and goes into the ring) MF:Ladies and gentleman,welcome the latest addition to Super Fire Wrestling,'Wild Card' Ricky Watts!!!!! (Ricky Watts comes out to a moderate pop.He gets in the ring and walks up to
Mikey.) MF:Welcome to SFW,Ricky.Why don't you tell us a little about your background? RW:I was born in Oakland, California, in 1979, of an immigrant family, I then quit school after the sixth grade and associated with a notorious street gang,becoming accepted as a member.'Big John' Riggs was the street gang leader and among the other members was my brother Damon, who would later attain his own notoriety as a thug. My brother Damon was an awesome athlete
Mr. Freedom . . . we both pushed the cards to the limit . . .
MF:What do you want to try and accomplish here in SFW? RW:Well . . . being the greatest isn't easy Mr. Freedom . . . (Ricky grabs the microphone) MF:Hey! RW:Shut up a minute.Everytime I walk down that aisle...I know its just me and the other guy in the ring... as I'm walking through the ropes, I look into his eyes...I feel the friction between us...feels like the ring is on fire...I'm sweating...he's sweating...then the lock up...he looks at me...and I laugh...cause I know I got the win...and as he ponders...cause he knows...I just got a Full House...
(Ricky Laughs,and slams the microphone into Mr. Freedom's chest and throws his hands up in the air as the crowd boos)
MF:What is your impression of the competition in the federation? RW:Well, to tell you the truth, I don't know any of these guys, but either way...there all jobbers compared to the greatest of all time... MF:Finally, when do you think you will step into the ring and make your debut?
RW:Until someone challenges the greatest of all time, I'm not stepping into the ring, because to them, its a once in a life time moment, its like getting an autographed baseball of Babe Ruth . . . they'll cherish it forever . . .
(Ricky leaves the ring and exits the arena to a chorus of boos.Mikey is shaking his head as he takes his announce position again.)
MF:That guy could learn some manners.Hopefully our next matchup will take my mind off of him.Cerberus,a multi World Champion,takes on Cyclone.Lets go to the ring.
Announcer:This is a Television title first round match,scheduled for one fall,with a 15 minute time limit.Coming down the aisle,representing the
Minions,weighing in at 320 pounds,Cerberus!!!! And his opponent,representing the Natural Disasters,weighing 298 pounds,Cyclone!!!!! (Cyclone's music starts up,but no-one appears.The music stops momentarily,then starts up again,but still no-one comes in.The referee has no choice but to start counting)
MF:Where is he?The ref's counting..18...19...20..The referee has called for the bell,and Cerberus is victorious!!!
Announcer:The winner by Countout,Cerberus!! BV:Cerberus becomes the second man in next week's final. MF:Eh..well,since we seem to have a bit of time to kill..we will take a break.Come back for the third of our TV title matches when Hollywood takes on the Superstar,Matt Williams.
COMMERCIAL BREAK MF:Well,fans we are back with the next first round match in the title tourney.Remember the winner of this match will go forward to take on Fire and
Cerberus in that Brawl for it all next week for the TV title.Let's go for the introductions.
Announcer:Ladies and Gentleman,this is a Television title first round match,scheduled for one fall,with a 15 minute time limit.Coming down the aisle,representing the New Generation,weighing in at 247 pounds,'The
Superstar' Matt Williams!!!! And his opponent,weighing 253 pounds,Hollywood!!!! BV:Hollywood looks good.I think he's gonna put on a star-studded performance here tonight. BV:Williams starting to show some offense. (Hollywood goes for a Suplex,but can't get Williams up.Matt responds with a body slam.The Superstar then throws Hollywood off of the ropes and catches him in a Tilt-a Whirl slam.Hollywood gets up and throws Matt Williams into the corner and follows up with a lariat,taking Williams down.)
MF:Hollywood with a slight advantage so far. (Hollywood picks up Williams from behind,but the Superstar responds with a bulldog.Matt then picks up Hollywood and executes the Corner
Powerslam...1...KICKOUT.Matt then executes a Shoulder Neckbreaker. Then,when he attempts a backdrop,Hollywood half blocks it and both men fall out of the ring.)
BV:Now we are gonna see some brutality.These guys are going at it tooth and nail. (The ref counts up to 5.Hollywood hits a piledriver on the floor.He considers heading back to the ring,then goes back after Matt.He slaps on a figure-four as the ref counts to 10.Matt Williams escapes at 14.Hollywood tries to go back to the ring,but Matt Williams grabs him and hits a DDT on the floor.Williams quickly jumps back into the ring.)
MF:What a DDT!!I think Hollywood is out cold...and there's the bell....Hollywood has been counted out,Matt Williams advances to the final!!!
Announcer:The winner by countout,'The Superstar' Matt Williams!!!! BV:Well,Matt Williams will take on Fire and Cerberus next week.They will be joined by either Fantom or Outlaw X,the last first round match tonight.
MF:But that is later on tonight.Now we have a bit of a grudge match from High
Octane Wrestling.Taryn St. Archer takes on Twister.Both men are up for this match,so let's not keep them waiting,let's get it on.
Announcer:This match is scheduled for one fall,with a 15 minute time limit.Coming down the aisle,representing the Natural Disasters,weighing in at
280 pounds,Twister!!!!
And his opponent,weighing 245 pounds,Taryn St. Archer!!!! (Both men circle each other for several seconds,neither man wanting to show his hand first.Suddenly Taryn grabs Twister,throws him off the ropes,and executes a Tilt-a-Whirl backbreaker.He tries to follow it up with a piledriver,but Twister blocks it and throws Taryn down.)
MF:A tentative start to the match.Neither man wants to go all out to start with.I wnder if Twister is concerned about Cyclone not showing up?
(Twister starst working on Taryn St. Archer's upper body with knee lifts,slaps and elbow drops.Suddenly Taryn gets some offense going with a

BV:Man,that's gotta hurt.Taryn has the advantage now.Can he take advantage? (Archer picks up Twister,but Twister manages to get his knee in.Twister takes a short breather,then grabs Taryn.He sets him up and delivers a Tombstone piledriver.Taryn gets up groggily.Twister runs at him,but Taryn sidesteps,and hits the Jumping DDT again!!He quickly climbs up top and hits a 450
Splash....1....2....3!!!!!!) Announcer:Here is your winner,Taryn St. Archer!!!!! MF:Wow!!Taryn St. Archer with the win from absolutely nowhere. BV:This guy's gonna go far,if he can pull out wins from the air like that. MF:I think Twister was just caught cold.Next time will be different for him.Anyway,it's time for our last first round match in the Television Title tournament.Fire,Cerberus,and Matt Williams are there.Who will join them?
Announcer:Ladies and Gentleman,this is a Television title first round match,scheduled for one fall,with a 15 minute time limit.Coming down the aisle,representing the Minions,weighing in at 270 pounds,Fantom!!!!
And his opponent,weighing 215 pounds,'The Top Dogg' Outlaw X!!!! BV:This should be a good match.Outlaw X is a lucha type who can brawl,while
