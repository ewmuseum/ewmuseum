FeWS Thunder

16 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(An overhead shot of the venerable Greensboro Coliseum.) [John Cruise, in a voice-over] You are looking at the Greensboro Coliseum, the site for some of the greatest moments in wrestling history. . . . (Shot of Lex Luger and Sting holding up the Crockett Cup from 1988.) And some of the greatest matches the sport has ever seen. . . . (Shot of Ric Flair chopping Dusty Rhodes. . .fade to Magnum TA giving Tully Blanchard a belly-to-belly suplex. . . .fade to The Cleveland Scorpion clotheslining "Dirty" Doug Simpkins. . .fade to JD Power incredibly suplexing Khan the Destroyer. . . .) Tonight. . .the FeWS returns to town. . . (Akira Hashimoto holding the FeWS World Title Belt high over head.) for FeWS THUNDER!!!!!! (A computer-generated stormcloud rolls over the in-ring scenes, and a single lightning bolt shoots from the cloud. It strikes a wrestling ring down below with a big explosion, spelling out in its wake--FeWS Thunder!! The scene fades, replaced by a raucous crowd inside the Coliseum. The camera pans the arena a few times, before coming to rest on the Broadcast Team for Thunder. Veteran play-by-play man John Cruise stands in the center of the trio, wearing a dark suit, with the FeWS emblazoned on the left chest of his jacket. On his left stands legendary Independent Supercard star cum color commentator "Hollywood" JP Palmer, dressed in his traditional loud Hawaiian shirt and white slacks. And on Cruise's right stands future Hall of Famer "Dirty" Doug Simpkins, wearing black jeans and a black t-shirt, bearing a picture of Jason Midnight, with a caption reading DIRTY DOUG KICKED MY ASS.) [Cruise] Welcome, everyone, to another edition of FeWS Thunder!!! With me, as always, my broadcast partners, the legendary "Hollywood" JP Palmer and the equally-legendary "Dirty" Doug Simpkins. Gentlemen, a big show on tap tonight. [Palmer] That's right, Cruiser, we've got a big main event lined up: My man, "Sinful" Steve Mercer, gets a chance to end Akira Hashimoto's World Title reign before it even begins!!!!!! [Cruise] That's right, fans, _new_ World Champion Akira Hashimoto makes his first title defense tonight, on the heels of what many are calling the greatest match in FeWS history, the main event at Heat Storm!!!! [Simpkins] It was a great match, I'll give you that, but my boy Chris Fantasy was robbed!!! I'm sure he'll have something to say about that tonight on Weaver's World!!!! ("Zombie" by the Cranberries begins, accompanied by a nice ##POP## from the Greensboro crowd.) [Cruise] Well, let's get right to the action this week, as we've got a Television Title defense!! Let's get it up to ring announcer Mickey James!!! [James] Our opening contest tonight is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. . .and it is for the World Television Title!!!!! (Mild ##POP##.) [James] Introducing first. . .the challenger. . .weighing in at 222 pounds. .
