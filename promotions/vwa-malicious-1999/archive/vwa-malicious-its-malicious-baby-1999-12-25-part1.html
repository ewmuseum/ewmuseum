He turns around noticing the camera and presents himself in a much more forman manner.]
Parkman: "I saved you _AND_ this company, and you want me to defend my title? Is that what I am hearing?"
Williams: "That's about it. Nobody is above 'COROPARAL PUNISHMENT'! Not even the almighty Rex Parkman. You see, your actions on the past Malicious Friday Night, are what got you into this mess, and I just hope
Tommy Stylz, shows you what true punishment is all about. Have a nice day."
[Scene fades out to a commercial break.] ------------------------------------ A Look at VwA's Hall of Fame ------------------------------------ [Scene fades in, a dark room, nothing is visible, except darkness. A voice is heard, deep and somewhat eerie.]
V/O: "Many men shall grace the VWA, past as well as present. Though not all will leave such a mark, that we will, never-ever forget their names, and in remebrance to them, the VwA has incorporated the Hall-of-Fame, only those, who are the most elite, dedicated, and all around good guys have what it takes, so do you have what it takes?"

Spence: "What the h[BEEP] is wrong with Blake Feldman, what turn of events could make him think he's Homer Simpson, a cartoon charactor?"
Palmer: "I don't know, but to tell you the truth is pretty damned funny." [Prophet comes out, fans do not seem to notice, watching Simpson/Feldman ramble on and on like a drunk at Moes.]
*DING*DING*DING* [The two grapple, both basicly the same size and shape, continue to win in strength, though Feldman oddly screams, DOE into the face of Prophet, who shocked breaks the hold.

[FACE POP.] Palmer: "For once, I'm interested in the match." Spence: "Yes, it is pretty interesting." [Blake whips Prophet to the ropes, and makes a dog like face, raises his hands resembling a bear and guns down Prophet tackling him, he begins biting at the ankles of Prophet.]
Palmer: "ANKLE BITER!" Spence: "Un-orthodox style" Palmer: "That little mut." [Prophet kicks Blake off rolls out the ring, Blake follows, and Prophet begins yelling at Blake.]
Prophet: "Bad dog, stay. NO! Bad dog!" [Blake begins barking, on his hands and knees chases Prophet, chasing him down to a row of concession stands, Prophet takes a pack of pork rines from the stands throws them at Blake rolls him over for the pin.]

Lights begins to flash, flicker throughout the arena. Michael Long comes out from behind the curtains wearing a long, black, tampered Trench coat, with his long hair covering his face. He begins to walk leisurely.
#Cause you can feel my ANGER!# #You can feel my pain...# #You can feel my TORMENT...# #Driving me insane!# [The crowd really give no reaction but silence. Long reaches ringside, and calmly jumps in. We walks to the corner, and stands there motionless, awaiting for the bell to ring.]
Palmer: "Man, I hope "Incredibly" DeDD LeE kicks Longs arse. Long is so damn poetic, it's so boring, its like everytime he's about to do an interview the fans go: "C'mon, Senyorito, time to drain the lizard!" haha!
Spence: "Not funny, just not funny." [The arena falls into a chilling darkness. The crowds talking comes to silence. On the titan tron a light comes up from blackness. "Incredibly" Dedd Lee wrestling. Hardcore sceens are shown and various opponents on the mat bleeding. The music comes very lound now, and smoke fills the enterance ramp. When it clears you see and enormous man standing there in black leather pants and a trech coat. He has on black sunglasses and his arms are crossed. The fans began to cheer at this amazing person. Spence: "Spooky." *DING*DING*DING* Spence: "I've gotten word, this is now a anything goes match, falls count anywhere, ought to be interesting."
[The two grapple, LeE the larger of the two easily wins the hold, lifting Long off his feet, holding with one hand a large portion of both feet, and, with his other, clinging to his face, his begins spinning Long around, sort like, a
upside-down windmill. [After a moment or so, LeE becoming dazed from spinning easily tosses Long outside the ring.]
Spence: "Wow, brute strength shown by DeDD LeE" Palmer: "That's "Incredibly" DeDD LeE nimrod." [LeE follows Long to the outside, grabs Long from the mat, only to receive forearms to the mid-section, Long quickly moves out of reach of LeE, and franticly searches for a weapon.]
