Monday Night Mayhem — Match 1

28 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday Night Mayhem From Madison Square garden at 7.30 pm [ The screen fades, then suddenely the TIRW logo starts flashing on the
Titian Tron. There are fire works going off and the crowd are on their feet.
Then suddenly the ring announcer shouts: LETS GET READY TO RUUUUMBBLLE. The cameras are now centred on the commentary team of Doug Goosens and Ally
Smith. ]
DG: Hello everyone and welcome to the very first Monday Night Mayhem at
Totally Insane Rage Wrestling. What a line up we have here.
AS: Yeah your certainly right there Doug. Well let's get on with the show. First up is an 8 man tag match between The Family and The Legion of Destruction. Let's go over to the ring announcer. Match 1 The Family Vs The Legion of Destruction S P: Ladies and Gentle the first match is scheduled for one fall. Making there way down the aisle weighing in at a total combined weight of 990 pounds from Italy - The Family, consisting of Alessandro Leoni, Riccardo
Leoni and the Leoni Brothers.
[ The Godfathers Waltz comes on and a gang of obnoxious wrestlers appear from behind the curtains, they are carrying the Italian flag with the massive 7 ft monster Antonio Rocca following them. The crowd are on there feet booing]
AS: Well you can't help but feel this match will throw the rules book out of the window. DG: I agree with you Ally there is one thing the Enforcer can't do in a match and that is not interfere.
[ The Family reach ringside ] SP: And there opponent weighing in at a total weight of 1,450 pounds led to the ring by Blayne Johnston and three Minions - The Legion of Destruction, consisting of The Exorcist, The Grim Reeper, Hell Raiser and The Master of
Darkness.
[ The lights go out and they start to flicker then suddenly an explosion is heard the light come back on and there are The LOD standing in the aisle way, they look mean and start walking towards the ring. ]
AS: The LOD has promised to be one of the most dominating forces in TIRW today. [ They reach the ring. ] DG: Well it looks like The Exorcist and Riccardo Leoni are going to start this one out. [ Just as the bell rings the Presidents voice is heard, he is standing on the platform with his CEO Steve Long.]
CA: Well first I'd like to say thanks to the fans for coming to the grand opening of TIRW. Now this match seems a little boring to me for our first ever match so I have an idea, let's make it a No Holds Barred Match.
[ The crowd go wild. The two teams in the inside of the ring are nodding there head looking happy with the decision]
DG: What an impact that will make in this match [ The Prez and the CEO walk out of the arena. ] AS: Well the bell has rung. Riccardo Leoni runs off of the ropes towards the
Exorcist but the Exorcist lifts his leg and hits the Leoni with a big boot.
The Exorcist picks him up and does a gorilla press slam. DG: That'll knock the wind out of you. The Exorcist sets him up on the top rope for the Grave Yard but here comes Francesco Leoni. He hits the Exorcist with a chair shot. Now the Master of Darkness enters the ring and attacks
Francesco Leoni.
AS: My God here comes Antonio Rocca, he picks up The Master of Darkness like he was a feather. He throws him out of the ring. The Grim Reeper and Hell
Raiser are on the outside battling with the rest of the Family, while in the inside Riccardo and Antonio are taking it to The Exorcist. Blayne Johnston runs into the ring and distracts Antonio Rocca.
DG: One of the Minions is handing The Exorcist a metal object, he plants
Riccardo Leoni with it and confronts The Enforcer. My God look at the size of them.
AS: The Enforcer grabs The Exorcist by the neck, what's he trying to do? DG: It looks like he's going for a chokeslam. He's got no chance. The Exorcist gets out of it and runs off the rope he executes a flying clothesline and takes the enforcer down. He's stomping on him. In comes
Blayne Johnston with a metal chair. He lays it onto the Enforcer's head.
AS: The Exorcist is running off the ropes, OH MY GOD LEG DROP, LEG DROP ONTO A STEEL CHAIR. DG: The Enforcer is busted wide open. The Family have knocked out both members of the LOD, they run into the ring and Francesco and Roberto go after the Exorcist. Alessandro Leoni gets Blayne Johnston.
AS: He gives Blayne Johnston an Offer he can't Refuse. Blayne's out. He ain't getting up after that.
DG: Riccardo makes the tag to Alessandro, the Exorcist makes the tag to Hell
Raiser. Oh no here we go again
AS: They are all in the ring after the family showed The LOD the fingers. The Ref has been knocked out. He squashed by Grim Reeper. DG: What's this, here comes Junk Yard Dogg! AS: What's he doing here. He's standing at ringside. DG: The ring starts to clear. There are only two men in the ring - Alessandro Leoni and Hell Raiser. He executes a drop kick. That was an excellent dropkick Ally.
AS: It certainly was. The ref starts to get up. He goes over towards the ropes, What the Hell JYD just nailed the ref. JYD grabs a chair. Alessandro
Leoni tags Roberto Leoni. Hell Raiser tags in The Master of Darkness. The two of them are going at it. JYD whispers something to Blayne Johnston.
Blayne Johnston tells the rest of the LOD something. They go round to the
Family DG: The Family and the LOD have started again. The ref is on the outside trying to create some order. The Master of Darkness throws Roberto Leoni off of the ropes.
AS: JYD has just nailed Roberto Leoni with a chair. The Master of Darkness covers him. JYD tells the ref to get back inside the ring. JYD leaves ringside. The ref sees the pin
DG: He counts 1.......2........3. It's over, the family don't believe it. [ The LOD enter the ring and show off to the crowd. The Family get 2x4's and enter the ring. They attack the LOD. The LOD are all out cold. The family spit on their opposition and leave the arena. The Enforcer runs back to the ring and executes the Jack knife Powerbomb on Hell Raiser and the ref. ]
[ The Enforcer has to be taken back to the locker room by the rest of the
Family. ] DG: What a match that was. AS: What a way to kick off Monday Night Mayhem
