NBW Presents: SLAM 40

30 November 2013
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spike Saunders — 30 November 2013 Goooooood evening KnightWatchers! We are just a few weeks removed from Pinnacle II which saw not only a new Blitzkrieg champion and Dynasty Tag Team Champions crowned, but we also saw a new World Champion! SLAM40 comes to you once more from the Epic II Arena in St. Louis.
Tonight the Blitzkrieg Championship will be put on the line, and the new World Champion will also be defending. We will also see the in-ring return on SLAM for 'The Villain' Jack Owyns as he faces the Planet-sized Superstar Jupiter in a final match with NBW, after the events of Pinnacle II. I have also been informed that everybodies favorite duo For The Win will be in action against the Family Keeling. And as a special treat for the holidays and kickstarter for our upcoming tour of Japan, we have two new athletes looking to secure a contract with the NBW in an exhibition match!
All this and more on this week's edition of SLAM! Be sure to stream it today! SLAM: Episode 40 And of course from all of us at NBW we wish you all a Happy Thanksgiving! Get stuffed, and pop open a beer -- then click on over to SLAM!
