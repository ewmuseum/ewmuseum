SLAM 123 - A flight for a Sparrow, and Sudden drop for a Warrior.

Spike Saunders — 2 September 2019 Two weeks ago NBW saw Clan Strongbern obtain the Dynasty Tag Team Championships, and in doing so injuring Adam Roebuck of The House. Tonight by order of interim GM Richie Keal the Clan are all in action. Including our Main Event which will see the Blitzkrieg title on the line when the current reigning double champion Jonny Bedlam defends against the former Little Ricky Strongbern ? now simply known as Ricky Strongbern.
Also in action is Dude in Mask II as he takes on one of the brothers Creede, Arthur Pendergast sizes up against Erick Davies in one on one action, while Rune will take on Mark Mercury as announced on social media earlier this week.
Of course, there is more than that to be seen and heard! Not to spoil the fun but a Sparrow will soar! So what are you waiting for? Click on over to watch the stream now!
