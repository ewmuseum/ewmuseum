NBW Presents: SLAM 48 - An Explosive Night for Hiroshima

9 May 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spike Saunders — 9 May 2014 Folks we're just days away from the gargantuan pay per view spectacular known world over as LEGACY. The Champions will be in action defending their respective championships, but before we get to that fateful night we roll through Hiroshima for SLAM's 48th episode.
General Manager Jack Harmen has signed a massive Main Event featuring the Blitzkrieg Champion Judasbleek, the former Double Champion 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs, and number one contender for the World Championship Vic Gravender Versus the World Heavyweight Champion Chris Moliano, the first ever World Champion Uncensored and Benjamin 'KO' Jones! A massive six man match that will surely live up to the hype.
Also in action tonight is our Infamy Champion Son of Malta as he defends his championship against Japan's own Ai Tso. We will also have a Fatal Fourway match to determine who enters the final slot in the Dynasty Gauntlet Tag Match at Legacy. We've also got some Tag Team action for you prepared!
Of course that is not all however you will need to head on over to Hulu plus or catch the stream here on the official website.
