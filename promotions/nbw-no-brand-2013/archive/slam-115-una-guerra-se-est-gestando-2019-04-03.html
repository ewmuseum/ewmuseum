SLAM 115 - Una guerra se est? gestando

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spike Saunders — 3 April 2019 The NBW once again comes at you from the land of Mexico with a stacked show full of action, intensity, and dare we say INTRIGUE?
All singles titles are on the line on this night as each face a challenger that attempts to trip them up on their way to Lucha De Mayo! Alex Reyn takes on Billy Law! El Dragon Loco gets a shocking rematch for the Blitzkrieg against Jonny Bedlam!
Gamberro is in action, as is Son of Malta and ?Big? Rick Strongbern! While the tag scene heats up as Turner & Hooch debut against The Law, Clan Strongbern takes on The Cartel and The House must hold onto their title shot in a Dynasty Tag Team Gauntlet!
And in the main event of the evening, Pat Gordon Jr must face a ghost from the past and defend the World Title against La Calabaza Loco!!!
Who won, who survived, and who are those suits stalking the premises? All will be seen tonight on SLAM 115!
