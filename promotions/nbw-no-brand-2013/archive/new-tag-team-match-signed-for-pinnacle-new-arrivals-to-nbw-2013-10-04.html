New Tag Team Match Signed for Pinnacle! New Arrivals to nbW!

4 October 2013
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spike Saunders — 4 October 2013 More big news has just reached nbW headquarters. A new match has been added to No Brand Wrestling's Pinnacle PPV. The former Dynasty Tag Team Champions Aleczander and JAC of The Family Keeling were knocked out of the Dynasty Cup tournament, but that doesn't mean that they'll take the loss lying down. Junior Keeling had laid out an open challenge several days ago, demanding any two people to come and face the British strongman Aleczander and the former shootfighter Johannes Antonious De Castanovo.
Today, their challenge has been answered. From the Japanese promotion, Kyoto PRO, one of the most established teams in that organization's history will make the jump to American soil to take on the former Dynasty Champions. The team of Gold Diamond has stepped up and put a word to nbW headquarters.
Gabriel Gold - an American-born wrestling prodigy - has cut his teeth the last three years on frequent tours with Kyoto PRO, an organization that was also once home to nbW World Heavyweight Champ Vic Gravender. Gold boasts an impressive record, including a three-time Kyoto PRO Cruiserweight Champion and a three-time Kyoto PRO Tag Team Champion. He boasts an incredible technical skillset and isn't even thirty years of age.
His partner is Tamon Ohara, a six-five, two-hundred seventy-pound force of nature that plays a beastly contrast to Gabriel Gold's cruiserweight style. Nicknamed 'The Diamond Beast' for a world-renowned tough skin and resistance to pain, he too is a decorated warrior sharing three tag title reigns with Gold, one with his older brother, Mark Weiler as well as a reign at the top of the mountain as Kyoto PRO's Openweight Champion.
This big tag match has been signed for Pinnacle and both teams seem evenly matched. Aleczander and Tamon Ohara will serve as their respective teams' powerhouses while Gold and JAC both boast striking backgrounds.
Who will win in what is sure to be a hard-fought battle? Order Pinnacle and find out! Source: TheNBW.com
