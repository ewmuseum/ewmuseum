NBW Presents: GODS Versus Titans

6 February 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spike Saunders — 6 February 2014 Good evening all! NBW's Pay Per View Event: Gods Versus Titans is now available for your streaming pleasure. Prepare to start off with a heavy dose of impact when the Blitzkrieg Championship is defended by Judasbleek against Max Hopper, and our double Main Event features both the Dynasty Tag Team Championships being defended by Derecho and Son of Malta against For The Win and The Hype, as well as our World Champion Saphire defends against both Zed and Chris Moliano.
It has also been announced that Son of Malta will be pulling double duty and will be defending the Infamy Championship. He joins a growing list of exciting matchup's for the PPV as shown in the full scheduled lineup below.
Blitzkrieg Championship: Judasbleek (c) Versus Max Hopper Team Hoss Versus Erick Davies and Alyx Norwood Benjamin Jones Versus Simon Starks Eric Yulan, Zatch Rollins and Nemo Versus The Handsome Man Modeling School and Xiang Johannes Antonious de Castonovo and Aleczander the Great Versus Ravage and Spike Saunders Infamy Championship: Son of Malta (c) Versus Matt Haddon Dynasty Tag Team Championships: Derecho/Malta (c) Versus The Hype Versus For The Win World Heavyweight Championship: Saphire (c) Versus Zed Versus Chris Moliano All this and much more can be found on Gods Vs Titans. Call your local cable provider today to order the event. Enjoy and as always we'll see you at SLAM when we come to you LIVE from Taipei, Taiwan!
