NBW Presents: SLAM 89 - Cooperation is Key

15 March 2017
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spike Saunders — 15 March 2017 [h=5]PUBLISHED BY: ADRIA HOYT[/h]St. Louis, MO - In April NBW returns to Singapore's floating arena for 25 To Life, but that's several weeks away. We still have TWO episodes of SLAM to get through. Speaking of, tonight... It's all about cooperation when in our Main Event the clients of Fenton Woods, Warren Spade and E.Z. Blaze, team up to face NBW World Champion Derecho and former multi-time champion Ravage in tag team action! We'll also see The Ke'ela brothers as they face off against two members of the Clan Strongbern. And the Blitzkrieg championship will be on the line whe Jake Tockwell defends it against one half of the Unstoppables out for revenge, Tyson XL!
We will also see the continuing path of rebuilding by the Rich Family's Freddie Rich as he faces a familiar face from the past looking for a bit of retribution after what Rich did with one of the twins. In addition as announced on our last episode, Zed and Michelle Couli will face the team of Zhalia Fears and the one person that can't touch Zed, Mitchell Quinlan. And to kick everything off will be Davey LaRue facing Alan Envy in a No Holds Barred match!
All this and of course much more on tonight's episode of SLAM! CLICK HERE TO STREAM!
