BCW — 4 April 1999

4 Apr 1999

NeWA British Championship Wrestling Something new just washed ashore! The Phantom vs Scott Simpson The Phantom looked to get a measure of revenge on Simpson for his actions the night before. The Phantom hit Scott with high impact moves a super DDT. Scott battled back and nailed the Phantom in a "Neck Buster" for the three count at the 4:03 mark. Hypnosis vs Myst Hypnosis and Myst took to eachother like oil and water. Hypnosis pounded on Myst and Myst doudged the beating a much as he could. Myst got his big break when Hypnosis missed an attempted moonauslt. Myst took his time and droped a leg on the thraot of Hypnosis. He then truied to pick up Hypnosis, but got rolled up for the three count instead! Myst was so mad at getting pinned he attacked Hypnosis with a steel chair, then the lights went out, and when they came up, Myst was being atttacted by the men who had beaten on Jim Extreme the night before! They pointed to Hypnosis, who didn't back down, and then left Myst laying in a pool of blood. Steven Pain vs The Creature Steven tried to out smart the Creature, yet he got cought in an belly-to-back overhead release suplex! pain was in severe PAIN! The Creature stoped on the back and neck area's of Steven. Finally he made Steven black out from the Mandable Claw in 8:23! John Cole vs Agnus the Destroyer Cole and Aguns traded blows from the opening bell. Both men used wrestling holds, as well as high-rick moves, but Cole was just "To big" for the Destroyer! Cole used a flap-jack, a piledriver and the "Gator Bite" to finish of Agnus in 15:12. Brent Morris vs Jimmy Extreme Morris took Extreme off his feet early and rode the tide of the fan support! Morris put on a wrestling school and Jim was the student. Brent missed one crutal move, The Blazing Bomb, and Extreme took Morris out with an X-Fator for the win in 8:36. Four Man Battle Royale for the BCW National Title "Big Country" Chris Mann, Titan, HICAL, The Wraith "Last man Standing" Pinfall, Submission or over the Top Rope to the floor will eliminate a competitor. All four men are in the ring. DING, DING, DING! All four men start to trade blows. Mann and Titan start to pound on each ohter, while HICAL and the Wraith start sluging it out. HICAL scoop slams the Wraith. HICAL stomps on the head of Wraith. Titan drops Mann with a low blow and blindsides HICAL. Titan whips HICAL into the ropes and nails him with a side walk slam! Titan goes for a pin as the ref counts: ONE..TWO...THRE... HICAL kicks out! Titan can't belive it! Mann gets to his feet, as does Wraith. Both men attack Titan, trying to throw him over the top rope. Mann slides out under the bottom rope and grabs a chair. He throws the chiar to Wraith, but HICAL intercepts it and slams Titan, Wraith and Mann, once he enters the ring with it! HICAL is standing alone in the ring with a chair in his hand. He motions for the mic. HICAL: Last man standing! <He looks around. The other three men are still on the mat.> Looks like that is me! Give me the belt! <The time keeper hands HICAL the BCW National Title> NOW I AM the BCW Champion! Chris Mann gets up and spins HICAL around. He plants a fit to the gut and a DDT to the mat. HICAL hits hard! The BCW Title lands beside Titan! Titan plays dead as Mann comes to him to get the belt, but get the belt in the face! Titan smashes Mann with the belt and Mann falls on the Wraith. The ref counts: ONE..TWO...THR.. Wraith with a shoulder up. Titan picks up HICAL and powerbombs him onto the BCW Title! The Wraith gets to his feet and catches Titan with a chop block to the knee! titan limps around the ring in pain. Mann gets to his feet. He and the Wraith work on the knee of Titan. They get Titan to the ropes and try to throw him over, yet he still won't go! Mann continues to work on Titan while the Wraith goes over to HICAL. The Wraith picks up HICAL by the hair and sets up for a piledriver. The Wraith is to close to the ropes and HICAL is able to reverse the move and send the Wraith over the tope rope and to the floor! <The Wraith hs been eliminated> Mann pounds on the skull on Titan untill HICAL comes up from behimd and gives Mann and inverted Powerbomb! HICAL them places Titan on the ropes and superplexes him ONTO Chris Mann!! Titna rolls over and covers Mann. The ref counts a slow count: ONE.......TWO.......THREE! <Chris Mann has ben eliminated> HICAL stomps on Titan then picks him up and whips him into the corner. HICAL followes him in and clotheslines him! HICAL shoots him into the cross corner and tries to follow him inbut Titna duck out of the way and HICAL hit the top turnbuckle and sales over the ropes and hits the floor! <HICAL has been eliminated> Titan is the NEW NeWA European / BCW National Champion in 22:13!!!
