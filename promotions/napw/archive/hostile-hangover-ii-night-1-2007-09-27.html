He goes to the second rope and dives off, trying to drive the edge of the chair into Kurtis' throat! Matt moves just in time, and Deathrow catches the chair right under the chin! Both men are down. Meanwhile, Phoenix is wading through chairs and fans to get at Chad Kurtis, who hasn't moved since being tossed to the outside. He finally finds his opponent, and tries to drag him up... Chad Kurtis with a jaw jammer! Phoenix is stunned! Kurtis to his feet. He's bleeding now, from his face and back, but he's still got fight left. With a boost from a nearby chair, he hits an enzuigiri on Phoenix! Phoenix drops to one knee, and Kurtis nails him with a Shining Wizard! Phoenix is down, but there are no pin falls outside the ring! Inside the ring, Deathrow and Matt Kurtis are both back up. Deathrow has a huge gash under his chin now from the chair, and is also bleeding from the mouth, but that doesn't stop him from attacking. Matt ducks a clothesline from Deathrow, tries to hit one of his own, only for Deathrow to cling to the ropes. Matt charges, and gets low bridged. Deathrow quickly follows him outside. The two men brawl around to where their partners are grappling. Chad Kurtis calls for his brother to help get Phoenix into the ring. Matt helps drag Jake up... and Jake has a look of fire in his eyes as he boots Chad away, knees Matt in the gut and chokeslams the big man to the floor! Deathrow grabs Chad Kurtis and rolls him into the ring, grabbing two chairs and tossing them in as well! Outside the ring, Jake Phoenix takes a moment to savor the cheers of the fans (and regain his balance). He turns to where he planted Matt Kurtis, and drags him to his knees. He lays in with repeated forearms, drawing more blood from the American Nightmare's head. He pulls Matt to his feet, and sets up for a powerbomb, but Matt fights out of it. Jake lays in with stiff shots to the jaw, then tries to lift Matt up for a powerslam.. Matt slides out of it, and pushes Jake into the guard rail! Jake goes sternum first into the rail and turns around into a Yakuza kick! Jake Phoenix is knocked over the railing to ringside! In the ring, Chad Kurtis has avoided the sweaty ball claw, but is hit with a dominator! Deathrow covers, but only gets a two count. Knowing he needs to do more, he starts to set up the chairs he brought in.. Chad Kurtis with a roll up! trying to end this with wrestling, he gets a two count! Deathrow is up and PISSED, trying to drive his boot into Kurtis' skull... Kurtis avoids, and goes behind for a crucifix pin! One... two... Deathrow kicks out! Kurtis with a head scissors, takes Deathrow into the turnbuckles! Kurtis with a school boy! One... two... Deathrow kicks out again! Kurtis goes for a Frankensteiner! ... And gets powerbombed into the turnbuckles. Deathrow stops Chad's momentum with one huge move. Outside the ring, Phoenix is on his feet. his eyes are glassy from the head shots, but he won't quit. He looks around for Matt Kurtis, who has found a chair for himself. He swings, Phoenix ducks, goes behind and lifts up Matt for a back drop... Matt with a thumb to the eye, Phoenix drops his man, and Kurtis picks up his chair. SMACK A shot to the head and Phoenix is spinning! SMACK The chair starts to dent a bit. We can see Jake's blood on the seat of the chair. SMACK The chair is destroyed. Blood is now pouring out of Phoenix's forehead. He drops to his knees, giving Matt an open invitation to bring the chair down across the Murder City Devil's back. Repeatedly. In the ring, Deathrow has set up his chairs. Chad Kurtis is holding his back in pain, but it looks like he's about to get hurt even more. Deathrow roars into the corner, driving his elbow into Chad's face. Kurtis slumps forward, and Deathrow sets up for a powerbomb! He's looking to drop Kurtis across the chairs! He powers Chad up, he's ready to plunge him into the metal... SMACK Matt Kurtis is in the ring. He's found a non dented chair, which he used to clobber Tommy with. Tommy drops Chad, who avoids hitting the chairs. Matt lifts up the chair to nail Deathrow again, but Deathrow reaches out and grabs Matt by the nuts. Matt drops the chair and yells in pain, and Tommy squeezes with all his might. And grins a blood soaked grin. Matt frantically punches Tommy in the face to get the Testicular claw released, while Chad has turned the chairs around BACK TO BACK. Matt, finally out of the most dreaded of all wrestling holds, angrily brings the chair down across Tommy's skull, then sets him up for the Bluegrass Bomb. ONTO THE CHAIRS! The fans wince in unison as Deathrow is nearly broken in half! Jake Phoenix has somehow managed to pull himself up using the ring apron, but Chad Kurtis baseball slides a chair into Jake's face! Phoenix is down! Matt Kurtis covers! ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! And just like that (though with a Herculean effort) the remarkable reign of Phoenix and Deathrow is over. WINNERS AND NEW CHAMPIONS: Bluegrass Mafia by pinfall! Some cheers, some boos. A lot of people are stunned. But what a way to end night one of Hostile Hangover II!
