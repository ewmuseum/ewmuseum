[end quote] Ro-Bot — 11 December 2010 It was a difficult task narrowing the nominations down to only thirty matches from NAPW's tenure. The majority of the nominees were taken from the End of Year Awards in years prior. So only matches that were originally recognized as awesome were entered. I attempted to narrow it down to 5-7 matches from each year to be represented, keeping in mind matches that truly had value and should continue to be recognized. I did not STRICTLY adhere to the voting we just did. I used that as an overall gauge but I cannot in conscience put fun matches like D-Rex vs D-Men and Tagstrav V, which otherwise lack historical value and lasting impact, as high as they were voted. Those matches more recent & fresh in our memories, especially for people who were not around for the 'classic' days, tend to get more votes than they would when removed by time & distance. Nonetheless they made the list because YOU DEMANDED IT. It's also worth noting that in cases of multiple match-ups between various wrestlers (Phoenix vs Ravager, Parker vs Beast), I selected the most memorable or best match of the series to represent it.
I think it is a good list, if flawed. It's tremendous that so many tag team matches are on the top 20; a testament to how important & prestigious Tag Team wrestling has been to NAPW. It's also fitting that the list is not so much a best of NAPW as it is a "best of Ravager" list. I kid! Ravager was involved in MOTY candidate after MOTY candidate. You can't talk about NAPW's great matches without talking about Ravager over and over again. Casino, D!. Lloyd Rees, D-X all get a lot of due, as they should. I love that Krusty Kid Paul vs The Show is in the top 6. House of Horrors has proved a popular gimmick.
The single most under-represented wrestler on the list is Donovan Astros. His matches against Ravager, Bruce The Beast, Dan Ryan all somehow missed the cut. It's not an outright crime, it just shows how difficult it can be to filter hundreds and hundreds of great matches down to just twenty. I also chose not to wait for the final show to come out; it may be that matches like Lloyd Rees vs Chris Casino IRON MAN (but was it better than the 30 minute draw?) and the 4-Way Ladder Match could make this list. It was already hard enough to narrow things down.
