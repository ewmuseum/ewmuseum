PURE HONOR: NORTH T. GUNDERSON vs MARK CAGE Cage and North shake hands and the bell sounds. Cage grabs North for a headlock. Instinctively, North quickly goes to the ropes, using his first rope break. North's smile flickers momentarily, a hint of irritation on his face - he instinctively went to the ropes as though it were a normal match. But only for a moment. With a grin, North once more locks up with Mark Cage. Cage overpowers him and throws him down. Cage lets North get up. The crowd cheers the sportsmanship. Gunderson shoots for a leg and gets it on Cage. He applies a quick leg lock and out of desperation, Cage hits him with a right hand. John Sharplin gives Cage a warning. Gunderson broke the hold to attend to his nose. Cage takes advantage and gets behind the rising Gunderson. GERMAN SUPLEX. Cage goes for the pin. One... Two... Kick out by Gunderson. North rolls closer to the ropes - Cage does a baseball slide knocking him to the floor. John begins the count One... Two... Three... Four... Cage waits inside for North to return. The count is up to eight when Gunderson enters the ring. Cage wastes no time in applying a front face lock, looking for a DDT. Gunderson reverses it! Northern Lights with the bridge! ONE! TWO! Mark Cage gets his foot on the ropes - that's a rope break right there. Gunderson tries another pin but is countered into a small package. One...Two... North is in the ropes as his legs are flailing, which eats up his second rope break. Cage and Gunderson rise to the cheers of the crowd. Gunderson charges into Cage and BAM. Closed fist floors North. The referee issues a warning to Mark Cage for the closed fist. Cage's new manager, Chris Mason, chimes in "No problems, Mark, you've still got him!" Cage went for that closed fist on instinct. He picks North up, this time chopping his man with the open hand. Cage attempts a Brain buster... He nails it. One! Two! Kick out by North. Mark looks for the kill - triangle choke applied! He gets it almost cinched in... but desperately Gunderson gets to the ropes. And that's North's final rope break. Both men to their feet - Gunderson kicks Cage right in the leg. While Cage is staggering, Gunderson comes from behind with the Million Dollar Dream. Cage is trying to get to the ropes! He is fading, but is slower in movement. Sharplin tries to raise the hand of Cage, but Cage doesn't let it drop. The crowd are cheering both men on. Cage wills himself near the ropes. He kicks off of them and falls backwards with North under him a la Bret Hart/Steve Austin! John Sharplin makes the count! One... North can't get free - Two... Three! Mark Cage with a big Pure Honor win, but North Gunderson looks disappointed. The two men take a second to catch their breath. North's big smile returns. Cage extends his hand, still favoring his neck... North shakes it. The crowd cheers the respect and sportsmanship!
