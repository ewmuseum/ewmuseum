NIGHTMARE VS THE YELLOW CHICKEN Nightmare just STARES at The Wrestler Formerly Known As Carter Owens strut around the ring, flapping his arms and scratching the wrestling mat with his feet. As the Yellow Chicken moves towards him, Nightmare starts backing away. He may have seen visions of great horrors in his dreams every night, but he's never seen anything like THIS before. Closer and closer he comes, and Nightmare just stares in shock and horror...until the Yellow Chicken gives him a hard peck on the forehead! Nightmare shakes off the head-butt, and then roars as he floors the Chicken with a clothesline. Nightmare runs, rebounds off the ropes and then comes down with a leg drop on the prone Chicken's chest. Nightmare goes in for the pin, and grabs the tights, but the referee sees it and stops the count at two. Nightmare pulls the Yellow Chicken to his feet and then sends him right back DOWN again with a double-arm DDT. A few boots to the back, and the Yellow Chicken is in some serious pain. Nightmare applies a blatant choke but lets go after three, and spits in his opponent's face. "You think you scare me, Chicken?" he screams, as the Chicken slowly gets to his feet. Nightmare with a hard right hand, but the Chicken comes back with one of his own. Nightmare retaliates, but the Chicken fires back again. Nightmare with the right hand, and again, and again, until the Chicken is staggering in the ring. Nightmare lifts him up for the shoulder-breaker, and stalls, screaming at him again. "NOTHING scares Nightmare!" -- but the Yellow Chicken scrambles out of the Dream Demon's arms and lands on his feet, then rebounds off the ropes to nail Nightmare's right shoulder with a Running Knee. The Chicken locks Nightmare up for a shoulder-breaker of his own, and connects! Nightmare's on his knees, clutching his right arm, but so is the Chicken, who gets up to the second rope for a Cock of the Walk, and Nightmare's rolling around on the mat, wincing in pain and holding his arm. The Yellow Chicken struts all around his opponent, then lets out a furious "Cluck Cluck CLUCK!!!" and applies the Cross-face Chicken-wing! Nightmare's got NOTHING left, and taps out!
