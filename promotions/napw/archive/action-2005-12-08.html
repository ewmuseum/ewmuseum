NAPW Action!

8 Dec 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STATIC vs THUNDER vs DIAMOND vs KHAOS Khaos, Diamond, Thunder & Static are in the ring (each man's second on the outside) and the referee calls for the bell. They quickly pair off with Khaos and Static trading rights and lefts as Thunder and Diamond are hitting each other with open-handed slaps to the chest. Khaos rakes the eyes of Static and sends him into the ropes catching him with a back elbow smash! Thunder meanwhile has taken the upper hand on Diamond and sends him to the outside with a stiff right hand. Thunder follows Diamond out to the floor as Khaos is choking Static against the ring ropes. On the outside, Thunder tries to whip Diamond into the steel ring steps --- reversed! And it's Thunder who finds himself smashing himself against unforgiving STEEL. Diamond rolls back into the ring and clubs Khaos from behind with a double axe-handle shot to the head. Diamond whips Khaos into the far ropes but gets caught with a spear from his opponent! Khaos quickly stands up and follows up his spear with a few kicks to the head of Diamond! Static comes from nowhere and takes Khaos off his feet with a running clothesline. Diamond is getting to his feet - popped with an enziguri from Static that sends him to the outside! Static pulls Khaos off the mat, whips him into the ropes and nails a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker on his man. Thunder slides back into the ring only to take a picture perfect dropkick to the face that sends him flying back to the outside. Khaos is pulling himself off the mat when Static comes up from behind him and drops him with a belly to back suplex. Static goes for the cover but it's broken up at two by Diamond! Diamond rakes the eyes of Static and follows up with a jawbreaker. He goes in for the cover but gets shoved off by Khaos before he can even get a one count. Thunder again makes his way back into the ring and now all four men are inside. Thunder nails a rising shinning wizard onto Khaos that sends him rolling to the outside. Thunder takes a clothesline from behind by Static that sends him up and over the top rope. Thunder lands next to Khaos in a heap. Diamond tries to rush Static but is backdropped over the top rope and down onto the pair of Thunder and Khaos! Static climbs onto the ring apron, springs up onto the second ring rope and hits an Asai moonsault onto the three men on the floor! The crowd is on their feet chanting "NAPW!" As Static pulls Khaos up from the floor and rolls him back into the ring. Diamond and Thunder are getting back to their feet --- Static grabs both men by their heads and knocks them together with a loud thud. Thunder and Diamond collapse to the arena floor and Static rolls back into the ring. Khaos is laying in wait for Static and nails him with a double arm ddt as he's getting to his feet. Khaos goes for the cover, 1...2... Static kicks out! Khaos pulls Static to his feet and whips him across the ring into the far turnbuckle. Khaos sets himself and hurtles himself across the ring for an attempted spear. At the last possible second Static dodges him and Khaos slams sternum first into the turnbuckles! Khaos staggers back is gets hit with a hangmans neckbreaker that leaves him laying prone on the mat. Static quickly scales to the top rope and takes flight with his "Moment Of Clarity" (shooting star press) onto Khaos! He hooks the leg for the pin attempt, ONE, TWO, TH --- NOOOO! DIAMONDSAULT! The Decapitator just hit his patented Diamondsault from the top on top of Static and Khaos! He hooks Static's leg, one, two, three, it's over! Wait a minute, Thunder's tag team partner Tempest just nailed Decapitator Axe with a chair on the outside! And now he's in the ring, oh my! Storm is assaulting the lone Diamond... Tempest hauls him up! GORILLA PRESS by Tempest! Thunder is on the top rope... OH MY! Follow-up legdrop! Thunder locking on the milliondollardream sleeper on Diamond --- here comes Axe! Storm get out of the ring in a hurry! This one isn't over, folks! CHRIS CASINO vs THE MOOSE The Moose is impatiently waiting in the ring while Chris Casino and his manager Terry Brandon take their sweet time getting there. Are they signing autographs or what? Moose can't wait any longer, and the second Casino climbs into the ring, he lashes out with a HUGE running clothesline that takes Casino right off his feet. The bell sounds. The crowd starts chanting "MOOSE, MOOSE!" while the hometown boy stomps away at Casino in the corner. Wait! Casino comes up, sticks his thumb in Moose's eye and DROPKICK, sending Moose tumbling. Casino proceeds to take the boots to Moose's head, then turns and taunts the crowd to a chorus of boos. The Moose, back on his feet, goes to grab Casino from behind - POW! Superkick out of nowhere! Down goes Moose, and Casino is now in firm control. Casino picks up The Moose and plants him in the ring with a devastating brainbuster suplex, then heads up the turnbuckle and comes off the top rope with the Cash Out! One, two, three - that's all she wrote! Terry Brandon slides into the ring to celebrate with Chris Casino, they may as well have just won the Championship. THE DUDES vs MIRAGE/NAMELESS ONE Mirage is busy making it clear to his minion that only MIRAGE may start the match. Meanwhile, The Dudes are playing Rock/Paper/Anything to see who starts! Johnston picks Paper, Scott picks Flamingo, Flamingo beats Paper, Scott gets to start! Johnston and the Nameless One hit their corners, so GET YOUR TICKETS READY! as Scott and Mirage LOCK-UP! Mirage, grunting, is steadily forcing Scott into HIS corner, slowly, step by step... and Scott EXPLODES, pushing Mirage off of his feet and onto the mat. Mirage recovers with a quizzical look on his face, then rushes Scott with a lariat--ducked! And Scott's unloading with his right hand, ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Scott whips Mirage into the ropes, he's on the balls of his feet--LOU THESZ PRESS! Scott's on top of Mirage, roars, and now a BIG HEAD BUTT with Mirage having nowhere to run to! CLONK! Scott rolls off of Mirage and tags in his partner, the lighter Michael Johnston, who wastes no time getting to the top turnbuckle--Mirage is up and groggy--CROSSBODY! Off the top rope! Johnston hooks the leg, ONE! TWO! Just TWO! The Nameless One has his fill at this point, and runs in to deliver a fieldgoal kick to Johnston's head just as he's picking himself up! Dick Kiebiech sends Nameless back into his corner and reminds him to get a clean tag, but the damage has been done--Mirage has the opening he needs to openly choke Johnston while the ref's distracted! He lets go as soon as the ref turns back around, but with Johnston hurt and grounded, Mirage FINALLY has control of the match! Sneering at Cameron Scott, Mirage drags Michael Johnston back to HIS corner and tags in the Nameless One in! Five seconds of legal double-teaming! BOOT, BOOT, BOOT, BOOT, BOOT, thank you for your time. With Mirage back behind the ropes, Nameless picks up a groggy Johnston, looks for Mirage's approval, then PLANTS him on his knee! BACKBREAKER! Johnston's too busy nursing his back to resist Nameless picking him up again, this time draping him across his shoulders--SAMOAN DROP! Nameless is deliberately working on Johnston's back, trying to break him in half! Nameless drags him to the centre of the mat, and hooks the leg-ONE! TWO! Knocked off--BY MIRAGE? Mirage is HOPPING MAD, yelling at his protege, physically demonstrating a tag--he's insisting HE gets the pin! Nameless ashamedly bows to his master's will, as both men walk back to the tag rope, Mirage grabs it, Nameless dutifully provides the tag, and Mirage returns to... now where IS Michael Johnston? Oh, in his corner, making a tag. Cameron Scott is a HOUSE! ON! FIRE! Shoulder-blocking Mirage DOWN! Mirage gets up as Scott hits the ropes, SHOULDER-BLOCK! Mirage picks himself up again, and Scott just THROWS himself off of them ropes--LINEBACKER TACKLE! takes the Lord of Pain down! Nameless jumps the ropes to help out Mirage--and eats DROPKICK OFF THE TOP ROPE, courtesy Johnston! All four men are going at it in the ring, Kiebiech has lost control of the match! Nameless is up, Scott and Mirage are trading blows, Johnston is CHOKESLAMMED out of his boots by Nameless! And now The Dudes' valet Tiffany screams at what she sees--Bio and Kar are strutting out from behind the entrance, and now Mirage gets 4-2 odds! WAIT A MINUTE! FROM BEHIND! IT'S THE CRUSHER!!! He's just knocked Bio and Kar like BOWLING PINS and he's not stopping! He's got a bandage around his knee and he's after the man that caused the injury--THE NAMELESS ONE! Nameless instinctively backs up against the ropes, Crusher slides in--SPEAR!!! SPEAR drives Crusher and Nameless BOTH to the outside! And they're GOING AT IT as Scott, Mirage and Johnston all walk to the ring's edge and watch, perplexed. The gorgeous Tiffany loudly clears her throat, which seems to jolt the same thought loose in the Dudes' brains. They clutch the distracted Mirage, sweep the legs--BLACK RUSSIAN! Mirage hits the mat with IMPACT! Johnston dutifully slides out of the ring, and Scott gets the cover! ONE! TWO! THREE! Carve a notch in the Dudes' Win-stick. And now Bio and Kar have gotten around to Crusher, joining Nameless in a three-on-one beating! In the ring, the Dudes size up the odds, then Scott whispers into Johnston's ear. Johnston nods OK! Scott picks his partner up in a military press, walks to the edge, and TOSSES his partner into the four Superstars! Johnston's crossbody knocks 'em ALL down! Tiffany, smiling a nervous smile, grabs Johnston by the arm and escorts him back to the ramp with the pumped-up Scott. NON-TITLE: D! vs LOBO Lobo no stranger to fighting men smaller and faster than him after his bouts with Ravager & Static over November, and of course D! spent a great deal of time contending with the 260-plus Plague leading into Black Thursday. D! hits the ropes and tries to knock Lobo down, but that doesn't really budge the man. D! hits the ropes, and tries it again. Same result. Lobo chuckles at D!'s inability to have an impact on his mass. D! looks at the crowd and winks, and then...tries it one more time? STOP SHORT. KICK IN THE GUT! Can YOU do the Can-Can? Rockettes Kick of Doom is what he calls it, and it seems that D! is having a little fun with Lobo. The strongman backs off. Abs of steel or no, getting kicked at full-bore repeatedly isn't exactly pleasant. D! getting a little serious now, lashes out with a kick to Lobo's right side, then a left kick, and thennnn...BIG roundhouse from the champion! Lobo staggers against the ropes. D! gets a head of steam and rushes Lobo, but Lobo tosses D! up and over the ropes to the outside! The champion lands hard on the concrete, and Lobo follows up outside. Oh my gosh, he just picked D! up and LAUNCHED him at the ring post! That could be the turning point right there. Lobo sends D! inside the ring and climbs in after him. Scoop and a slam puts D! on the canvas, and Lobo follows it up with a big leg drop. Lobo likes that Dragon Sleeper, and he'll work the neck and head to set-up for it. Head scissors by Lobo! Those massive, tree-trunk like legs of Lobo's wrapped around D!'s head, and he's squeezing. D! trying to find some way out, he's certainly not going to muscle his way out of this one. The crowd getting behind the champion, chanting "D!, D!, D!". Lobo responds by squeezing his legs together even further, squashing the champ's head like a grape. D! slapping the mat in pain and frustration. D! flips over so that he's facing the canvas on his hands and knees. Lobo still has that headscissors locked. D! pushes up, doing a headstand, and it's enough to wiggle out of the hold. Gasping for breath, the champ gets to his feet. Lobo hits a powerslam! Cover...gets two. Lobo drives the elbow and covers again, and that gets two. He picks the champ up and sends him into the turnbuckle. Lobo charges, D! flips up and hits a sunset flip! Lobo gets out at two! Axe-handle by Lobo, but D! hits a jab to the solar plexus, cutting off the big man's wind. With Lobo stunned, D! hits the second turnbuckle...comes off with a bulldog! One, two, Lobo powers off. Irish whip sends Lobo to the ropes, the big man puts on the brakes! D! sent to the ropes, Lobo with a back body drop! Oh man, the champ is HIGH in the air and hits the canvas, skittering outside the ring from momentum! Lobo looks pleased... and hold on here. CHRIS CASINO & TERRY BRANDON are coming down the aisle. Lobo looks a bit surprised, and D!, on the outside, is looking down the aisle as well, caution and curiousity warring on his face. Casino and Brandon stop halfway down the aisle, gesturing for the men to continue their match. D! back on the ring apron, Lobo comes after him, D! with a shoulder block to the midsection! And then a hotshot on the top rope! The champion has Lobo stunned. D! back in the ring, and he throws some hard-hitting chops and kicks, beating Lobo back into the corner. Reverse knife-edge chop draws "WHOOOO" from the crowd, and D! takes a run out, and back in...STINGER SPLASH! Lobo stumbles forward, D! quick to irish whip Lobo to the opposite turnbuckle...and...STINGER SPLASH! Lobo dazed, D! turns to the right, to the left, and hits the STANCE. BEAT-O-BARRAGE in five, four, three, two, ONE REFEREE! IN WAY! GUH! Wait a minute! Lobo just pulled the referee directly in the way of the initial flurry of the Barrage! D! momentarily thrown off balance, and Lobo seizes the moment for a CHOKESLAM. D! kicks his way out of that at the height! Clothesline is ducked, D! grabs ahold of a sleeper...NYQUIL DRIVER! DING DING DING! The bell rings?! The referee is calling for a disqualification of Lobo! And Lobo doesn't like that at all...he's attacking D! after the bell! But D! fights back! The Champion...BEAT-O-BARRAGE! He's peppering Lobo --- Lobo falls out of the ring over the top! Accidental or a way to escape the Barrage? Lobo snarls and storms down the aisle... moment of tension as he passes between Casino and Terry Brandon. In the ring, the referee hands D! the NAPW title belt. D! raises it high, ascending the turnbuckle to the roars of the fans... ...as Chris Casino and Terry Brandon look on.
