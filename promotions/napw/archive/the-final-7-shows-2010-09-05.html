The Final 7 Shows

5 Sept 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Many of you know that NAPW will be closing its doors after Black Thursday VI on December 9th. It has been a fantastic five years but due to various reasons, I have less and less time to dedicate to e-wrestling. E-fed veterans, I’m sure most of you have had similar experiences regarding fed closings; usually they just happen out of nowhere leaving everybody hanging. I want to end NAPW on the right note, leaving people with nothing but positive memories. Starting September 22nd we will run through our last seven shows on a bi-weekly basis, with RP periods of at least 10 days for each show. We have a very strong current roster, but this is your opportunity to get in one last NAPW match. If you want to wrestle on any of the last seven shows, or even do one final run, then please visit www.napw-online.com and email me at [a real contact e-mail address, redacted per the Museum's standing privacy rule — see Curation, the privacy rule]. One show in particular that should be a lot of fun is our “Trios” tournament, which we are calling Le Roi de Trois (The king of Trios). It will feature three-man teams in a two-night tournament, where I steal the idea from CHIKARA PRO because it’s fun. Here is the schedule. Again, if you want to be involved in any of these shows get in touch with me (the info is at napw-online.com) and let me know. This is completely up to you. When NAPW is gone, it is gone! 9/22 LETHAL LOTTERY 2010: LETHAL FOR THE LAST TIME Featuring LETHAL LOTTERY and BATTLEBOWL! 10/6 NAPW: IN ENGLAND! NAPW invades England! 10/20 NAPW: IN JAPAN! The Land of the Rising Sun hosts NAPW! 11/3 ANNIVERSARY ASSAULT V Featuring the CHAMBER OF HORRORS match! 11/17 LA TOURNEÉ ROI DE TROIS – La Première Nuit 11/18 LA TOURNEÉ ROI DE TROIS – La Deuxième Nuit Two-night trios team tournament! The second night will feature TAGSTRAVANZA VI Gauntlet for all eliminated teams! 12/1 GOODBYE TO COWTOWN The final show in Calgary at the Ogden Legion! 12/9 BLACK THURSDAY VI The final NAPW show, ever, as we say goodbye to the Polish Hall and Edmonton! If you have contributed over the years, thank you for being a part of making NAPW a successful, fun and memorable e-fed. Sincerely, Ryan Ro
