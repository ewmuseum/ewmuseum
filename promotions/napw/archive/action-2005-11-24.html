TECHNIQUE vs DJ "WOOPASS" WIGGER The referee calls for the bell as Technique and DJ Wigger tie up in the center of the ring. Technique takes Wigger to the mat with an armdrag and "Whoopass" quickly gets back to his feet only to walk into another deep armdrag. DJ Wigger uses the ring ropes to pull himself up, turns around and takes a beautiful standing dropkick to the face courtesy of Technique! Wigger rolls to the outside to buy himself some time as Technique plays to the crowd. DJ Wigger starts to climb back into the ring when he takes a baseball slide into the sternum from Technique sending him sprawling back to the floor. As Wigger tries to get back to his feet on the outside Technique scales to the top of the nearest turnbuckle and waits for his chance to fly. DJ Wigger gets up, looks around for Technique and finds him just as he's hit with a flying cross body block that sends both men crashing to the floor! The fans give Technique a nice ovation for the high risk move and he smiles back at them as he pulls himself to his feet. Technique pulls Wigger up and rolls him back into the ring sliding in behind him. Technique climbs up onto the ring apron and spring broads over the top rope landing with a legdrop across the chest of DJ Wigger. Technique goes for the pin, One...Two...Wigger manages to get the left shoulder up and breaks the count! Technique goes to the nearest ringpost and climbs to the top as Wigger lays prone on the mat. Technique again takes flight, this time with a 450 splash! As Wigger lays writhing on the mat Technique quickly climbs to the 2nd turnbuckle and motions to the crowd for his "Crossbones" finisher. DJ Wigger gets slowly to his feet and Technique leaps from his perch catching Wigger with a stunner off the ropes! Techniques goes for the cover as the referee counts. One, two, three! Technique with the victory! MIRAGE vs J.C. COOK Mirage has brought his minions Kar, Bio and Nameless to ringside. J.C. Cook points at the flock of followers and yells at them that he'll kick their ass if they decide to step into the ring. Mirage and Cook lock up...Mirage with the reverse atomic drop. Mirage whips Cook into the corner turnbuckle, drops to a three point stance and runs at Cook for all he's worth. Spea-NO! Cook lifted his foot and got the boot into Mirage's face! Cook picks up Mirage and delivers a full nelson slam! Kar jumps onto the apron and starts to get into the ring, but the ref is there to cut him off. Bio slides into the ring and clotheslines Cook! He gets out of the ring just in time for the ref to turn around. Mirage gets up slowly, and picks Cook up for an Alabama Slam! Mirage covers Cook quickly! One! Cook kicks out before the ref counts two. Mirage looks disgusted, and his followers are all yelling at the ref. Mirage climbs on top of Cook and starts punching him in the face. Cook starts punching back with momentum. And the crowd is clapping in time with Cooks punches! Both men and laying into each other, but Cook seems to be getting energy from both Mirage's barrage of blows and the crowd's support. He's standing up! He grabs Mirage and whips him back into the turnbuckle. He starts running in place and the arena cheers as he runs into Mirage with a FUZZY SPLASH! Nameless pulls Mirage out of ring as the crowd boos. Mirage rests on the outside as his three students yell at Cook to come out if he wants a piece of them. Cook looks around at the audience, shrugs, and runs to the ropes and jumps THROUGH THE ROPES ONTO ALL FOUR MEN! The audience goes nuts! Cook throws Mirage back into the squared circle and kicks him in the gut in the middle of the ring. He's setting up for the Pressure Cooker! Bio jumps onto the apron and distracts the ref as both other men slide in to help their master. Nameless suplexes Cook, and Kar climbs to the top! Kar goes for the elbow, but is denied by Cook's boot to the face! The ref turns around and sees the two minions in the ring, and points to the back! He's kicking out Mirage's army! Mirage is livid and starts yelling at the ref! Mirage is too busy bickering with the ref so notice Cook behind him, lying in wait. Mirage turns around, and another boot to the gut! Cook with the PRESSURE COOKER and the pin! One, two, three! Cook keeps on rolling with another victory on his resume! He jumps into the crowd to celebrate with his girlfriend, Monica. RAVAGER vs LOBO There's one hundred pounds of weight difference between these two men. Ravager and Lobo gaze at each other, barely two feet apart, black intentions in both eyes. There's the bell, and still Ravager & Lobo are locked in a grim tableau. Ravager makes the first move, raising his right hand up high. Wait a second...Ravager wants a test of strength with LOBO? Lobo almost laughing, but Ravager is dead serious. Lobo and Ravager lock the one hand. Immediately Ravager is in pain as Lobo crushes his! And yet, Ravager raises his other hand! Lobo shrugs, grinning, and crushes Ravager's other hand! Lobo clearly has the leverage here, forcing Ravager down to his knees. Ravager shaking his head, wait! The crowd behind him! Ravager is HULKING UP! No, sorry, he just headbutted Lobo in the crotch, neatly ensuring Lobo's body was just enough in the way that the ref wouldn't catch that. Lobo releases Ravager's hands and drops to his knees, grimacing in pain. Ravager smirks, and the crowd, well, they're behind him! Ravager looks at the crowd, and...GOUGES THE EYE. They cheer! Ravager shrugs, and drives a KNEE right into Lobo's cheekbone. Oh my, that was vicious! The shooter going right away to Lobo's arm now, driving the knee into the strongman's left bicep area! Ravager clearly knows how to pick a body part and exploit it! Ravager trying for a cross-arm breaker, he could break Lobo's arm! We saw this at Monday Night Fights, and Lobo had the strength to just STAND UP with Ravager on his arm! Lobo trying to shake off the initial assault, and yes, he seems to be getting to his feet again! Ravager knows what he's on about this time and releases the hold, swings behind Lobo...and kicks him in the back of the knee. Nothing pretty about that, but Lobo staggers! Ravager kicks him in the back of the knee again, and Lobo isn't going down, but he's sure limping. One more should do it, Ravager hits the ropes for added momentum...BAM! Lobo with a snap powerslam, using Ravager's own momentum against him! Ravager reeling in pain on the canvas from that HUGE, HUGE powerslam! Lobo trying to walk off the pain in his leg, but he's got to go after Ravager. Ravager picking himself up and Lobo...Lobo is straddling Ravager's back, keeping him down OH MY! THE FOREARMS! Lobo just CROSS-FACING Ravager across the face and the back of the neck, raining down savage forearms! We know that Lobo likes to use that Dragon Sleeper, this'll soften Ravager up! Sheer muscle smashing against bone and sinew. Ravager collapses back to the canvas, but Lobo doesn't feel like leaving him alone. "You like to play games, huh?" Lobo yells as he casually lifts Ravager to his feet and slings him across his shoulders. And what's this...LOBO WITH AN AIRPLANE SPIN! He's trying to humiliate Ravager! Lobo stops spinning, looking somewhat dazed himself, but Ravager is still on his shoulders and in a bad way! What's Lobo going to do here...OH GOOD LORD! F5! F5! F5! There's no other name for it! Lobo just DROPPED Ravager! He covers! ONE! TWO! THREE! ... And RAVAGER barely, BARELY got his FOOT on the rope! Lobo is raising his arms, but the referee trying to tell him that his opponent's foot was on the ropes! Lobo with a look of pure anger on his face, and OH MAN. Lobo gathers Ravager up in his arms... He lifts Ravager up high and drops him face-first on the turnbuckle! Ravager writhing in pain on the canvas, holding his face, as Lobo steps on the second turnbuckle and puts his arms out. HUGE heel heat for Lobo! The crowd absolutely not a fan of his! Lobo turns around and pulls Ravager up one more time, the strongman is in complete control. Hold on! Ravager firing with shots to the breadbasket, trying to turn the tide! Look at the abs of Lobo, he probably barely feels those - but Ravager isn't some brawler, he's a shooter! Ravager ducks a punch of Lobo's and quickly hooks a Russian Leg Sweep! Fantastic counter by Ravager, who pulls himself up by the ropes, trying to recover himself. Lobo back to his feet, Ravager boots him in the gut...DDT! NO! Lobo POWERlifted Ravager up high, spins, SPINEBUSTER! Cover! One! Two! Ravager BARELY, barely gets the shoulder up! Lobo smashes Ravager in the face hard and covers, forearm in Ravager's jaw. One! Two! Shoulder up! Lobo covers AGAIN, Ravager expending more energy with every kickout! One! Two! Th---No! Lobo argues that count with the referee... RAVAGER ROLLS LOBO UP! ONE! TWO! TH---Lobo powers out! Unbelievaly close pinfall there! They're on their feet and...LOBO catches Ravager across the throat! Here comes a Chokeslam, that'll be all - No, Ravager with a savage kick to the abdomen to break it, he spins around Lobo! Oh my gosh, Ravager trying to DRAGON SUPLEX Lobo! He can't...quite...get the full nelson applied, Lobo's shoulders are just TOO massive...So Ravager changes his grip to the waist! GERMAN SUPLEX! GERMAN SUPLEX! OH MY GOD! And BOTH men are on the canvas, Ravager spent, Lobo dropped HARD by that German Suplex! The referee with no choice, he's got to make the ten-count! One! Two! Three! They're stirring, but not much...four! Five! and Lobo is holding the second rope!...six...seven...Ravager's stirring...eight...Lobo on his feet at eight! And he's not wasting any time, he's got Ravager by the neck, DRAGON SLEEPER TIME! Ravager has this scouted, and he arm drags Lobo down! Lobo enraged, charges at Ravager, who moves out of the way! Lobo smashes into the turnbuckle, and that's the opening Ravager needed! He hoists Lobo up to the top turnbuckle! Super Brainbuster coming up, but Lobo is fighting that! Wait a minute! We've got Lobo and Ravager both fighting on the top turnbuckle for their finishers! Lobo Driver or Super Brainbuster, what's it going to be? Lobo seems to have the advantage, starting the superplex...Ravager holds onto the top rope! Lobo tumbles to the canvas, but lands on his feet! He's coming back to the turnbuckle, Ravager dives off onto the man's shoulders, flips over, VICTORY ROLL --- RAVAGER IS HOLDING THE BOTTOM ROPE! ONE! TWO! THREE! Ravager rolls out of the ring, and Lobo is in absolute shock! Ravager has won the match using the ropes! It's going to be Lobo! Ravager! Static! for the Provincial Title at Black Thursday, and tonight, Ravager has a HUGE win leading into the match! Good night!
