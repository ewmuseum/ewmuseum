NAPW Charity Show

12 Oct 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kevin Kodiak vs Al B. Damned (w/Sebastien Martyr) The Damned make their way to the ring to the disapproval of several hundred NAPW fans in Red Deer. Edgar Winter kicks up and out comes the fan-favorite Kevin Kodiak. The lumberjack is wearing a Santa cap and hauling a large sack. He walks over to the donation area and begins pulling out new toys and gifts from the sack! What a donation from Kevin Kodiak to the Firefighters Children's Charity! Kodiak gets into the ring to a solid pop. Martyr is telling Damned to jump Kodiak from behind... Al has misgivings? Well, the chance is gone. Here's the bell and a big power match starts off. Two big bulls run into each other, neither man moves. Off the ropes, they collide again. Same result. Kodiak and Damned pump up, Kodiak getting the crowd behind him, and they hit the ropes... crossover... again... Martyr grabs Kodiak's foot! Kevin stumbles out and is caught with a mega body slam! Kodiak gets up... caught with a double choke! Damned holding the big Kodiak up high, then slams him down. One, two, Kodiak kicks out. Martyr telling Damned to go for the kill. Al sets Kodiak on the top rope for the Baptism. Gonna hit it --- Kodiak says nay! Holds the top rope and starts pounding on Damned. Damned stumbles out, but then charges in. He eats Kodiak's size 14 in the face and drops to the canvas. Kodiak flies with a top rope splash! Cover one, two, Damned able to kick-out, but what an impressive move from Kodiak. The crowd coming alive for the lumberjack as he hits a spinning leg lariat. Damned pops up, tries a right hand but Kodiak blocks and fires some of his own. Another, another, and Kodiak's going to try for TIMBERRRR. But he can't muscle Damned over. Kodiak's a big man, but Al B. Damned is one pound short of the 300 mark. Kodiak tries again... Damned fights out and sends to the ropes, huge atomic drop. Damned covers... another big kick-out by Kodiak! Damned sends Kodiak to the ropes, Kodiak reverses! Big double ax-handle stuns Damned, Kodiak off the ropes --- Martyr grabs his ankle again! Kodiak however turns around and pulls Martyr up to the apron! Damned charges to save his mentor, but Kodiak dodges and Damned blasts Martyr off the top rope! Al is stunned... KODIAK OFF THE ROPES! NORTHERN BC LARIATOOOOOOOO! COVER ONE! TWO! THREE! Kodiak wins! Kodiak has his arm raised to the cheers of the crowd... and then he puts his hand out to the disappointed Al. "Come on Al... shake my hand... in the spirit of charity..." Al looks uncertain, but then shakes Kodiak's hand to a good ovation. Sebastien Martyr comes around to witness this display of sportsmanship. Whoops. He doesn't look too happy with his protege... "HUH" blazes through the arena, the crowd knows who it is, they start to boo. Parents hold up their kids in the air, they boo as well. Prince W. Darko sticks his hand out from the curtains, someone immediately tosses a soda bottle. He now walks out, another bottle is thrown, he's able to evade it completely. He rolls into the ring, not taking the risks of standing of the apron and getting caught by a plastic projectile. The Prince starts talking."What I wanna know, if you people are so poor, why the hell are you throwing bottles at me? I mean..." The crowd showers Darko with boos. "SHUT UP YOU POOR PIECES OF SHIT!" The crowd is booing louder now. "See, I show up here, taking time out of my life, MY LIFE! You people should be happy to see me. I didn't have to be here, I (BLEEP) donated my (BLEEP)' time to be here, HERE! What the (BLEEP) have you people done? You assholes weren't even decent enough to pay for a show, we gave it to you for free, and you still have the audacity to treat me like this? And just to think, I was about to place my Heritage tittle out on the line to anyone in the backstage area. Oh well, since you guys want to be like that, you get no match, none---" "Bad Boys, Bad Boys!" It's the Extreme Jobber champion "Bad Boy" Joey Malone! And the crowd pops? The crowd POPS! Joey Malone storms into the ring and LAYS INTO PRINCE W. DARKO! HERE'S A REFEREE! WE HAVE A TITLE MATCH! -Heritage Title Match- Prince W. Darko (c) vs "Bad Boy" Joey Malone The crowd is cheering every move the "Bad Boy" makes, Joey Malone throwing wild rights and lefts. He sends Darko to the corner and climbs up for a ten-punch count-a-long! Crowd counts along loudly and enthusiastically. Malone gets down and Darko takes two steps out and then does a Darko Dive, the Zamundan version of a Flair Flop to the canvas. Joey covers, Darko kicks out at two! Darko slowly getting up... put right back down with a ROCKER DROPPER! Joey goes to the rope and hits his flying fist drop! HE COVERS! ONE! TWO! Kick-out by the champion! Crowd wanted the finish so bad, but Joey picks Darko up, DDT, no, low blow by Prince W. Darko. Joey collapses to the canvas and Darko stands, an expression of utter contempt and anger obvious even beneath his facemask. The champion grabs both of Joey's arms and then sickly boots the man in the face to a torrent of boos... then Darko slaps on the Zamunda Yoke! Joey Malone is done for folks. The arm drops once. Drops twice. Drops three times nnnnnno! The crowd trying to rally Joey Malone, who is reaching for the ropes. Crawling! He makes it! Darko holds the move til the four count and lets go, arguing with the referee. Joey tries a charge, but no --- Darko destroys him with a vicious Laid Out Number #2, planting Joey into the canvas with the inverted STO. Darko makes a lazy cover, one, two, he stops the count and pulls Malone up. Oh come on! Scoop and a slam, followed up by Just Perfection. Springboard swanton connects, but Darko doesn't go for a cover? What arrogance! Darko just taunting the crowd, he wants the Zamunda Driver. Joey slowly gets up, out on his feet, and Darko comes in and hooks the arms REVERSED! HOLY HELL BACKSLIDE PIN ONE! TWO! TH--- Darko JUST GETS OUT! And Darko is LIVID! Takes down Joey and pounds him in the face. He stands up, screaming that nobody insults Zamunda like that! Only with more cursing. Darko roughly hauls Joey up and there's no more miracles, it's a sick Zamunda Driver dropping Joey right on the back of his head. There's no getting up from that. Your winner, and still Heritage champion, Prince W. Darko. Darko gets up and holds the belt high, talking more trash to the crowd. "This is what a (BLEEP) winner looks like!" Darko heads out as Joey comes to in the ring. Frank gets on the microphone again then... "Your loser, and STILL Extreme Jobber champion... "BAD BOY" JOEY MALONE!" The ref hands Joey his the tin and pleather Extreme Jobber title and his face lights up! Joey heads out to a tremendous ovation from the Red Deer fans with his title held high. "The Falcon" Jacob Venar vs "Simply Wrestling" Andrew Rossi Referee John Sharplin calls for the bell as Andrew Rossi and Jacob Venar lock up. Rossi locks on a headlock. Actually it's suspiciously close to a choke, but by the time Sharplin gets over to check, Rossi has adjusted so everything is nice and legal. "The Falcon" pushes out of the move, sending Rossi to the ropes. Rossi bounces back into a knife edge chop from Venar, followed by three quick kicks to the thigh, neck, then head. Rossi goes down, Venar covers, but only gets two. Rossi is up on his feet quickly, and cuts off a a hurricarana attempt with a powerbomb. Venar is in pain on the mat, and he gets even more hurt from some kicks to the spine from "Simply Wrestling". Rossi grabs the legs of Venar and sets up for the Manhattan Crab, but Venar fights with all he's got. Rossi catches a boot under the chin that sends him back a few steps, allowing Venar to get to his feet and go for a... Rossi cuts Venar down with a lariat! He covers! One... two... Venar gets a shoulder up! Rossi wastes no time in getting the Manhattan crab on this time. He locks it in and puts all his weight into bringing the pain. Venar tries to push out of it, but he's not quite strong enough to push Rossi off. He ignores all the calls to submit, and reaches for the bottom rope. Rossi reluctantly lets go, but hits a rising Venar with Lights Out! Rossi covers! One... Two... Venar gets his foot on the ropes! Rossi is showing a bit of frustration, thinking he should have had the rookie dead to rights. Rossi drags The Falcon up, and slaps him sharply across the face. ... Venar fires back with a slap of his own! Rossi hits back. Then Venar. Then Rossi. The crowd is getting behind the youngster, who lays in with four slaps to Rossi's face, then an enziguiri! Rossi goes down, Venar covers! One... Two... Rossi with a kickout! Venar to the second rope, he nails a rising Rossi with a double axe handle! Rossi is down again, and Venar covers! One.. two... Rossi gets his foot on the bottom rope! Venar goes to the top rope, and dives off with a flying elbow... Rossi moves just in time! He covers his prone opponent. One... two... VENAR KICKS OUT! Rossi is looking too tired to argue. Frankly, he may have underestimated his opponent. He plants a stiff kick to the head of Venar, then works over the neck and shoulders as well. Sharplin tries to break it up, but Rossi will have none of it. He drags Venar to the centre of the ring and locks on the Painkiller.. Venar slides out of it, and rolls up Rossi! One... two... Kick out by Rossi, who lunges with a lariat, Venar ducks, then school boys Rossi! One.. Two... Rossi kicks out again! He tries to get back up, but is nailed with a Shining Wizard! Rossi is down, Venar hooks the leg! One.. two... Rossi with a foot on the ropes! Venar was so close to another upset. He looks to Sharplin, as if to say "Are you sure?", then he climbs to the top rope, looking to "Spread His Wings"! Rossi is trying to pull himself up using Sharplin to steady himself... Sharplin says to let go... Rossi shoves Sharplin into the ropes! Venar crotches himself on the top rope! Rossi pleads that is was an accident, even if the fans think differently. He doesn't plead for long, as he drags Venar off the turnbuckle and hits the New York Nightmare II! But he doesn't go for a pin, instead locking in a Heel Hook! Venar yells in pain and tries to struggle out of it. Sharplin looks on in sympathy, remembering what the same move did to his colleague Morgan Smythe, but here he can only ask if Venar wants to quit. The Falcon says no, and he tries valiantly to reach the ropes. But the New York Nightmare may have been too much for him, and Rossi pulls Venar farther away from freedom, leaving the rookie little choice but to tap out. Rossi smirks at the angry fans as his hand is raised after the match. Venar takes his time, but makes it to his feet under his own power. The fans give him a nice ovation. There will be other matches... Stone Zellor vs Dez Carter Two fan favorites lock up at the bell, crowd chanting for both men. "Let's go Dez! Let's go Stone!" They tie-up, Stone with a headlock, Dez with a back suplex counter, Stone flips out and hits a big PIMP slap! Dez goes down, midly stunned and holding his cheek. Lots of respect here, but lots of competitive spirit as well. Another tie-up, Stone takes over with an arm twist. Dez counters and ZAM WOW! Dez Carter unloads a STIFF back hook kick right into Stone's face! Hot damn. Back into it, now these two start unloading chops on one another! Back and forth, but Dez Carter delivering the stiffest shots stun Stone, Carter bounces off the ropes ROARING ELBOW! Two count off that, Dez picks his man up, GO 2 SLEEP --- Stone counters! Toe-kick! SLAMMY TIME! Dez reverses with a back drop into a bridged pin one, two, Stone bridges up, great strength! Off the ropes and a discus clothesline takes Dez down! There's a cover for two. Stone picks the man up and does a dig, then drives a knee into Dez's ribs... clothesline! Dez ducks behind with a DRAGON SUPLEX --- gets folded over, and here's the inverted cravate crossface! Could we have a submission? No! Stone Zellor gets to the ropes. Carter on the offensive with another flurry of chops, then he puts Stone on the top rope. HIGH kick knocks Stone silly and Dez climbs up, what's he going for? Superplex, no, Stone showing some fight! He knocks Dez Carter off. Carter prone on the canvas, Stone takes flight... DIVING HEADBUTT! ONE! TWO! KICK-OUT! Stone Zellor gets up and pulls Dez up for a Tombstone piledriver! Dez kicks and reverses it, he wants the Tombstone... Stone kicks and reverses it again, HITS IT! ONE! TWO! THKICKOUT! Stone thought it was three, so close. He gets Dez and throws some chops, off to the ropes, Dez holds the ropes! Stone charges and gets body dropped over, but lands on the ring apron. Dez hooks his man for a suplex bringing back Stone into the ring --- Stone reverses and lands on his feet! Slammy Time attempt --- reversed by Dez, GO 2 SLEEP --- Stone grabs the top rope wildly! Dez can't drop his man, Stone gets down and rolls Dez up! One! Two! Dez counters into a pinning predicament! One! Two! Stone reverses ONE! TWO! REVERSED! ONE! TWO! ROLL THROUGH! ONE! TWO! THREE! Wow! Stone Zellor gets the fall and WAIT A MINUTE! Prince W. Darko hits the ring, he's attacking Stone Zellor! Dez Carter pulls him off... ANDREW ROSSI! "Simply Wrestling" is attacking Dez Carter! Rossi and Darko trying to injure their rivals... Dez! Stone! Turning things around! Wait a minute, here's a referee! We have an impromptu tag match! Stone Zellor & Dez Carter vs Andrew Rossi & Prince Darko All these men have wrestled already tonight, but now it's on! Prince Darko and Stone Zellor going at it on the outside while Carter and Rossi start tearing into one another. Carter nails Rossi with a big kick to the side of the head! Rossi staggering, Carter with a dragon suplex LOW BLOW. "Simply Wrestling" using another short-cut, he grabs Carter and nails a sick Brainbuster. One! Two! Kick-out by Dez Carter. Rossi looking for the Heel Hook... hold on! Stone Zellor flies over the top rope with a springboard diving headbutt onto Rossi! Who's the legal man? WHO CARES! Stone Zellor going for Slammy Time on Rossi --- Darko from behind with a low blow! "YEOWWWWW!" That's the sound of Darko in pain, the kind of pain you get when you try to punch steel. BALLS OF STEEL! Stone Zellor turns around and PIMP SLAPS Prince W. Darko into next week! Zellor turns around LARIATOOOOO! Rossi just devastated the man! One! Two! Th---Dez Carter makes the save! Carter throws forearm, forearm, forearm, off the ropes ROARING ELBOW blocked! Rossi wants the New York Nightmare II... Carter rolls him up! ONE! TWO! Rossi kicks out! Back up and Rossi tries another lariat but Carter blocks KICKS ROSSI in the face. EAT IT. AND THEN! GO 2 SLEEEEEEEEP! Carter covers Rossi ONE, TWO, PRINCE DARKO WITH THE HERITAGE TITLE BELT! To the back of Carter's head and that's a disqualification win for Carter and Stone. Speaking of Stone, he's getting up... Darko nails him in the head with the title belt as well! Rossi slaps the Heel Hook on Dez Carter, he's trying to break Carter's ankle like he did Jeff Garvin's! Darko stomping away on the man he took the Heritage title from... Here comes Kevin Kodiak! Kodiak hits the ring and takes a giant swing at Darko, but the champion cuts and runs. Rossi does likewise. Kodiak makes the save but Prince W. Darko and Andrew Rossi have made their point. These rivalries aren't done, not by a longshot. At "Mark of the Beast" it will be Rossi vs Carter for the #1 Contendership, Kodiak challenging Darko for the Heritage title. And you know Stone Zellor wants a rematch for the belt soon as well! -Six-Man Tag- The Beast & Northwest Express vs Jason Cruise & Coast 2 Coast The heels attack before the bell and we have a pier-six brawl! Cruise on Beast, Yukon on Haggard, London on Odyssey. Referee Dick Kiebiech will have his hands full here. Link whips Yukon, but the biggest man in the match simply puts on the breaks. Haggard tries again, Yukon whips Link instead and hip tosses the man to the outside. Odyssey Baldwin nails Yukon from behind and sends him out to the floor! Randy London with a cactus clothesline takes Odyssey AND himself to the floor! That leaves Jason Cruise and The Beast on the inside, and Cruise is trying to put The BEast away. He's going for Cruise Control ... The Beast is too strong and drives thudding forearms right into Cruise's back... SUPLEX TO THE OUTSIDE! The crowd on the concrete half-catches, half gets wiped out by Cruise. And then The Beast roars, the crowd getting to their feet as they figure it out! THE WORLD CHAMPION WITH A HEAD O'STEAM --- BEAST OUTTA CONTROL PLANCHA OVER THE TOP ROPE! EVVVVVVERYBODY goes splat! Beast picks up Jason Cruise gorilla press style and sends him back into the ring. The Beast rolls in, Cruise begging off, Beast isn't going to have any of it. He yanks the man up unceremoniously and using his great power starts breaking Cruise in half with a Human Torture Rack! This could be it right here, watch out for HAGGARD! KENTA KNEE catches Beast right in the face! Jason Cruise lands on top with one, two, the world champion powers out. Haggard and Odyssey double-teaming The Beast now, Coast 2 Coast looking to do some damage. They whip Beast to the ropes, double clothesline, ducked by The Beast... RANDY LONDON!
