The Phenomenons (a tag team application)

21 April 2014

Name: [name withheld] Email: [detail withheld] Wrestler Name: Eric Cameron & Shane Stone Nickname: Alignment: Bad guy Entrance Music: "Original Prankster" by the Offspring Height & Weight: Eric = 6'1" & 211 lbs. || Shane = 6'3" 248 lbs. Hometown: Eric = Philadelphia, PA, USA || Shane = Chicago, IL, USA Wrestling Style: Eric = High Flyer, improvised martial arts he mostly learned from movies || Shane = Primarily suplexes and submission holds Signature Moves (limit 10): Shane Stone = Shane is a well-trained wrestler and athlete, unlike his partner. His strategy is usually to use explosive suplexes (brain busters, northern lights suplex, belly to belly, belly to back) and Judo throws to get his opponent on the ground, at which point he'll switch to submission holds (rear naked chokes, hammerlocks, omoplata, knee and arm bars, gogoplata, the Peruvian necktie, etc.). Eric Cameron = Eric has little to no formal training as a wrestler. On the other hand, he has absolutely no interest in his own physical safety, so he just generally throws his body into the action without hesitation. He watches a lot of Kung Fu movies, and will generally stick to wild kicks whenever he has to go one on one with anyone. He is tremendously athletic, though, and fueled by caffeine, so he'll go kick crazy. Both cheat at every opportunity. Eric uses a kendo stick whenever he can get away with it, plus Francis the Skunk isn't the only thing they have in that burlap sack they carry around. And Eric's started using a blue mist attack recently, but he's not very good at it, and will sometimes break it on accident and run around the ring coughing blue mist out. Finisher (limit two): Eric Cameron = The Main Event ... Cameron leaps off the top rope, somersaults over the standing opponent as he faces away from him, catches him around the neck, and delivers an Ace Crusher. Shane Stone = Philotherianism-Plex ... a wrist-clutch exploder suplex. Biography: The Phenomenons are something of a cross between Peter Pan's Lost Boys, cult leaders, and idiots. Traditionally their alignment is neutral, and their sway into either face or heel position is really irrelevant to any change in who they are: they will cheat at every opportunity, kidnap people, and so on whether face or heel. They're simply giant children who do whatever they want. Typically, though, everything is done with a mischievous attitude that drives them into face territory. Regardless of whether people cheer for them or not, though, they are convinced they are the most popular tag team in wrestling history. Except in one place: Canada. The Phenomenons hate Canada with every fiber of their being. They have reached out to New Alberta Pro Wrestling as a last ditch effort by Eric Cameron to snap Shane Stone out of the craziness of retirement in the suburbs, by throwing him into the lion's den of his mortal enemies. That said, this is their first time ever going into Canada, and they've never knowingly met a Canadian. Shane is the more grounded member of the Phenomenons, but also easily manipulated. Eric is clearly the driving force behind their insanity. How the two of them became a tag team is really irrelevant to who they are. They exist, they do what they want, and their reality is entirely whatever they decide it is. They have been known to decide that the same team that just brutally attacked them are now their best friends and treat them as such for months on end, regardless of how many times its proven differently. They will simply decide who their friends and enemies are and nothing will change their mind. Generally, they prefer childish pranks as a way of furthering rivalries (which are often still friendships in their minds). They've gone places, they've won titles, they've lost titles... it's all a jumble and really unimportant at this point, isn't it? Still, all of that was fine and dandy when they were in their early 20s. Years ago, Shane Stone finally broke away from the Phenomenons, returning to his estranged wife Jessica and settling down in the suburbs. Eric, broken hearted, attempted to go on tour with the Phenomenal Army as a musical act, but a public clash with a drunken Pete the PG Tips Chimp landed him in the hospital and their musical aspirations dashed. He went to work at a zoo, but kept trying to free the animals. Eventually he made up with Pete and had a reunion concert, but no one showed up, because the band was mostly just Pete screeching and a couple of camels standing awkwardly by a drum set. Finally, he decided it was time to get the Phenomenons back together. So he lived in Shane's basement unknowingly for six months, kidnapped his former partner, and fled the country, hoping to use the threat of Canada to return Shane to "normal." Now in their 30s, they're really too old for this crap. Oh, and the Phenomenons love animals. It gets them into trouble. Appearance: Shane Stone = Generally wears a pair of cargo pants with random T-Shirts. He's actually a fairly talented airbrush artist and has an arsenal of hand-made shirts, generally with their logo, faces, or any random idea he has at any given time. Usually wears a colorful ski cap or a baseball cap. Short blonde hair, and well built. White boots with airbrushed logos or faces. Eric Cameron = Completely got the idea of wrestling tights wrong, and his black pair of tights look more like a wetsuit. Usually wears a blue cape, and a Burger King crown. Bright red hair, now flecked with gray throughout. White boots with airbrushed logos or faces. Manager: Yes Manager Name & Appearance: The Phenomenons don't have a manager per se, they have an army. The army shifts, shrinks, and grows over time, but primarily it consists of: Toby Jenkins: His first time being drafted into the Phenomenal Army, Jenkins is a former wrestling announcer that the Phenomenons declared as their best friend many years ago. They've utterly ruined his life over the years, including two of his homes, and after his latest abduction, he's accepted that willingly following them might be less destructive to his life than being surprised again. Currently operating as a private videographer, because the Phenomenons don't believe Canadians have video cameras. The Phenomenal Camels: Montezuma and Alibaba, two large camels (one dromedary, one bactrian). The Phenomenons saw them at the zoo once and staged an elaborate rescue attempt. They spit on command. Pete the PG Tips Chimp: Former spokes-chimp for the British PG Tips Tea Company, Pete ran away to join the wrestling world. Becomes noticeably drunk when drinking tea, and bites opponents in the testicles. Francis the Polar Bear: Large polar bear that the Phenomenons have trained to despise fans of Star Trek. Francis the Skunk: Francis was a member of the Army in the past, until the Phenoms dropped him into Toby Jenkins' ventilation system. The house was ruined and Francis starved to death before the Phenoms remembered to rescue him, but afterwards they put his remains in a burlap sack, which they often talk to. Trailer Park Luchador: Young wrestler that wears a luchador mask and a John Deere cap that just sort of fell into the group and never left. The Rabbi of Pain~!: Former referee that acts as the Phenomenons' spiritual adviser and confidant. Regularly tries to talk them out of whatever they're about to do, and fails. And so on. The Phenomenons drive around in a large van styled after the Scooby Doo Mystery Machine, with Shane's own airbrushed graphics on it, and tow the camels behind them. Tag Team?: Yes Tag Team Name: The Phenomenons Tag Team Partner: Eric Cameron & Shane Stone Tag Team Signature Moves: Generally any combination where Shane will perform a suplex and Eric will meet it half way or at the conclusion with a kick, splash, or leg drop. The most common will be Shane performing a suplex in the ring, where Eric will flip over the top rope to perform a leg drop. Eric will also try to take cheap shots while Shane has a submission hold on. Tag Team Finisher: The Phenomenal Ending: Shane Stone will lift an opponent up, where Eric Cameron will kick the opponent in the face any way he can ... usually from a superkick off the top rope, but he'll take what he can get. Shane turns it into a slam or a suplex with the opponent's momentum after the kick.
