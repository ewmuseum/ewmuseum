NAPW Roleplay

Phenoms Travolta

c. 20 Oct 2014

Eric Cameron & Shane Stone — the Phenomenons

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open in a scene of eternal darkness. It goes on and on forever. Absolute darkness. Who knows what lies beneath? What could this mean? Why, after all this time, have the Phenomenons, masters of all that is light and happy, embraced the darkness? Why … why has the … Shane: “Why are you standing in the dark?!?” Let there be light! A single lightbulb illuminates the scene … but oh, the darkness still creeps in on the edges. The single bulb sways on a cord with a light creak, from side to side. The light in the room is small, and darkness approaches on all sides … but in the center, Eric Cameron stands, ready to defend that light! From the darkness, and boogeyman, and stuff. Eric: “You think we’re scared of you, Lambert?” Shane Stone starts to walk into the scene, then shrugs and walks back out of the room. Shane: “Sorry, didn’t realize you were trying to do a creepy promo.” Eric: “Rod damnit! Can we start over again?” Trailer Park Luchador: “I don’t really know how to rewind a GoPro.” Eric: “You are the WORST VIDEOGRAPHER IN THE WORLD!!!!” Eric Cameron kicks something in the dark, which illicits a muffled groan of pain. Eric: “Hush, I’ll get to you. Anyway, where was I?” An awkward moment passes. The darkness continues to creep in the light … but Eric will not be swayed! Eric: “Oh, right. Lambert … I’ll give you this, your laugh is really creepy. Like, really, really creepy. That’s good. You kind of have to have that cool demon laugh if you’re going to become the object of people’s nightmares and all that good stuff. But that’s all you have going for you!” Eric stalks from one end of the room to the other. Into the darkness … back into the light … back into the darkness … Eric: “Lambert … dear, sweet Lambert. I have something to tell you. Something important. I am an artist as well, you see.” He grins maniacally at the camera. Eric: “For you see, you may create masterpieces of sight and sound, I do not doubt that. But, my friend, when it comes to smell, you are but a four-year-old fingerpainting your blob-family being eaten by your irradiated pooch while a UFO shoots your house in comparison to the Rembrandt that is—“ Cameron suddenly jumps around—and in rushes Shane Stone. Shane: “NO! No, Eric, ENOUGH!” Cameron looks impossibly hurt. Eric: “But I was just going to—“ Shane: “NO! We are the NAPW Tag Team Champions! We are not going to start our first promo against our mortal nemesis with a fart joke.” Eric: “But—but--!!!” Shane: “NO! I am half of this team, and I am putting my foot down!” Eric: “Fine, you great big stick in the mud. Any other arbitrary boring rules you want to put in place? No fart jokes … why I never.” Shane: “Come on, man … this is LAMBERT HANRIEL. And that demon monster woman of his! If we can’t take THIS fight seriously, Nobody might literally kill us.” Eric: “Well if no one is going to kill us, what’s stopping me from doing fart jokes?” Shane: “I swear to Rod if you start doing the who’s-on-first gag on me right now …” Eric: “Nobody would do that in this serious moment!” Shane: “Wait, I don’t …” Eric: “Yeah, that was a who’s-on-first joke.” Stone punches Eric directly in the chest. Eric: “JEEZ! That hurt. You’ve never hit me before. Man, you’re strong. Is that why we win? Should I be working out, too?” Shane: “I don’t see how that could possibly help us at this point.” Eric: “Lambert’s doing it.” Shane: “Yeah, well, maybe we should have started trying to win this match weeks ago instead of the last damn minute. You ever think about that?” Eric: “I meant to start prepping for it. But, you know … Jerry and I started playing hide-and-go-seek, and the next thing I know the month was almost over!” Shane: “I’m going to hit you again.” Eric: “He’s a REALLY good hider.” Shane: “HE’S A NARCOLEPTIC GERIATRIC!” Eric: “That’s probably why he wouldn’t come out even when I was yelling that I gave up.” Shane: “Seriously, you’re getting hit again.” Eric: “C’mon, what’s it matter if we take this match seriously or not? We haven’t taken anything seriously to this point and it hasn’t burned us yet!” Shane: “Come on, beating the A-Team? Rabble? If those guys weren’t former tag champs we wouldn’t get any credit for beating them at all. Everyone knows this entire tag team division just really boils down to when us and Lambert & Co. are going to finally butt heads.” Eric: “We beat them already! Twice! Kind of.” Shane: “Dude, I still don’t know how we beat them. That flash pin was the greatest thing in the world … but instead of thanking our lucky stars and doubling down on getting better, you spent three straight weeks playing in the snow with Pops!” Eric: “Come on, didn’t you see his promo? There’s like fifteen more things that I can tease him about! What about –“ Shane: “ERIC! Once, just this once, let’s take this seriously. We’ve got a real chance here to take that next step into immortality! We could legitimately start pushing ourselves into being the greatest tag team in Canadian history. We could legitimately be pouring ourselves into a fundamental conflict with Lambert that could shape human destiny. We have an essential conflict of the human condition that hangs precipitously in the balance between us and Lambert … good versus evil! The present versus the future and past! Heroes and villains! Embracing the enjoyment of the here and now versus delayed gratification that prevents oneself from ever enjoying where they truly are at that given moment! Isn’t that the core conflict between us and Lambert? A true, iconic clash of values and perspectives that reflect the inherent challenges that so many people around the world face … all played out in one critical battle between three people that, despite their love and respect for each other, are driven into a deadly game of destruction based on a deep-rooted difference of opinion on how life as a whole should be lived!” Eric: “Is this a bad time to tell you that I kidnapped John Travolta?” There’s really nothing to say to that, is there? Shane: “I … I …” Shane Stone has known Eric Cameron since they first formed the Phenomenons in 1999 – a pairing meant to carry Shane’s mentor to glory on their backs, but which descended, moment by moment and animal by animal, into absolute madness. Eric has never seen Shane be this determined NOT to have fun. And Shane has never been this completely dumb-founded at his partner. Shane: “You didn’t actually kidnap John Travolta, did you?” There’s a fire in Shane’s eye that isn’t sitting well with Eric. Eric: “Umm … no?” Shane: “Eric … seriously. Please tell me you didn’t just kidnap John Travolta.” Eric: “But … you heard Lambert! It’s just us! The two of us, and Pops, and that dirty homeless guy, and our camels, and Trailer Park Luchador! And those guys can’t help us! They’re good for nothing!” Trailer Park Luchador: “Hey. That hurts.” Eric: “Dude, you never even won a match. Ever.” Trailer Park Luchador: “I have a magical luchador mask!” Eric: “There’s no such thing as a magical luchador mask!!!!!” Trailer Park Luchador: “THERE IS SO AND I’M WEARING IT RIGHT NOW AND IT MAKES ME INVINCIBLE!” Eric: “I’M GOING TO KICK YOU IN THE—“ Shane: “ERIC! This is NOT the time to turn on each other! Now please, tell me you didn’t do what I think you did.” Cameron looks back at his partner sheepishly. Nervously. Eric: “But … but … Lambert said we didn’t have our belt armor OR John Travolta to bolster us. And we can’t do the belt armor again … that goes 100% against our vow to ever use the same strategy twice! Even though we totally DID win all of the Rabble championship belts, and so we could do it again if we wanted to! So really the only option was …” Shane: “Eric, in the name of all that is holy, for the future of the Phenomenons … please tell me you did not kidnap John Travolta.” Eric: “I did not kidnap John Travolta.” Muffled screaming from behind Eric proves the lie in that statement. Shane: “ERIC!!!!!!” Eric: “I might have kidnapped John Travolta.” Shane: “I can’t believe you just lied to me! Where in the heck is the real light switch in here anyway!? You and your stupid light bulb hanging by an extension cord mood setter!!!!” Eric stands uncomfortably in the center of the room for a few long, awkward moments, before Shane finally finds the real light switch. In the corner of the room, it sure looks like John Travolta is very awkwardly struggling against far more ropes that it logically could have required to tie him down to a chair, and in his mouth … Shane: “Oh mother of Rod … did you put Francis in his mouth!?” Eric: “I couldn’t find anything else to gag him with!” Shane Stone begins dry heaving in the corner. Eric: “Oh you big baby. I’ll take it out.” Eric Cameron snatches Francis the Skunk’s burlap sack out of John Travolta’s mouth. John Travolta: “HELP!!!! HELP!!!! HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEELLLLLLLLLPPPPPPPPPPPPPP!” Eric: “Dude, we’re in Canada. Where no one can hear you scream. It’s virtually identical to space.” John Travolta: “HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEELLLLLLLLLLPPPPPPPPPPPPPPP!” Shane: “Mr. Travolta … I am so, so sorry. Is there any chance you’ll autograph my championship belt when I untie you?” John Travolta: “I’m not even John Travolta you idiots!!!!!” Eric: “Wait, what?” John Travolta: “I don’t even look that much like him! My name is Phil Francavilla! I’m a TERRIBLE JOHN TRAVOLTA LOOKALIKE!!! Who the hell would kidnap me anyway!? I work weddings!!!!! YOU KIDNAPPED ME FROM A WEDDING!” Eric: “John Travolta doesn’t work weddings? I thought your career just really went downhill.” Phil Francavilla: “… is he seriously this stupid?” Shane: “Yes. And I take back my request for an autograph. Maybe a photo, though, if you turn your head awkwardly to the side?” Eric: “Well, okay then. I did not kidnap John Travolta.” Shane: “You kidnapped a John Travolta look-a-like!” Eric: “I know, and that’s probably a good thing. I bet NAPW would have been really mad if John Travolta had sued them.” Phil Francavilla: “I’M going to sue them!!!” Eric: “Dude, if we just tell our fans to go to JohnTravoltaLookALike.com for you, will that shut you up?” Phil Francavilla: “I … actually, yeah, that’s probably okay.” Eric: “See! All’s well that end’s well. Except now Lambert really was right … we don’t have John Travolta to bolster us. Oh … crap.” Shane: “Seriously, this is why you haven’t been training for this match!? Because you thought John Travolta would save us!? How was that even going to work!?!?” Eric: “I had several plans.” Shane: “I swear to Rod, if one of them included us removing our faces and switching them with LEGION …” Eric: “Don’t be ridiculous!” Shane: “Eric …” Eric: “I HAD OTHER PLANS TOO OKAY?!” Shane: “NAME ONE!” Eric: “Something to do with him being an archangel?” Shane: “I hate you so much right now.” Eric: “Look, is it too late to start taking this match seriously?” Shane: “Almost certainly.” Phil Francavilla: “This is getting kind of awkward. Is anyone going to untie me?” Eric: “Come on, Shane. There’s got to be something we can do. Isn’t there?” Shane Stone rubs his forehead, shaking his head from side to side. Shane: “There might be. I’ll see what I can do.” Shane Stone stalks out of the room. Eric quickly follows after. Phil Francavilla: “You forgot to untie me!” Eric Cameron runs back into the room. Phil Francavilla: “Oh thank God. Eric: “And we will, boy and kinda-girl. We will be Phenomenal indeed!” Eric runs back out. Phil Francavilla: “Seriously, you guys forgot to untie me!!! Anyone?!” Done!
