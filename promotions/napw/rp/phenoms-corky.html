Phenoms Corky

c. 20 Oct 2014

Eric Cameron & Shane Stone — the Phenomenons

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Trust me. He's our only hope." “Are you sure? He's always kind of … creeped me out.” Shane Stone and Eric Cameron sit high atop their magestic camels Montezuma and Alibaba, marching through a working class but picturesque Chicago neighborhood, having made their first visit back to the United States since their unceremonious flight/kidnapping to Canada-Land. They would make a rather ridiculous sight even if it wasn't for the pair of championship belts each man wears, one atop the other, and even if they weren't trailed by a scrawny young man in a luchador mask and a John Deer cap, a diseased homeless man, a John Travolta impersonator, a chimpanzee, and an elderly man asleep in a sled being dragged behind the camels. Phil Francavilla stubs his toe as they march onwards. Phil: “Oww! Have mercy!” Eric: “You're the worst John Travolta impersonator in the world. Literally the worst.” Coming to a quaint townhouse, the Phenomenons dismount from their trusty steeds, climb the steps, and rings the doorbell. As they wait, Shane checks the sack he carries with him, ensuring their offerings to their potential savior are still ready. Eric: "You think he's still alive?" Shane: “I'm just surprised he still lives here. I figured he'd be a billionaire by now.” Trailer Park Luchador: “I figured he'd be dead.” Phil: “Wait a second … who are we visiting?” Eric: “First off … I don't know why you're still here, Phil. We clearly released you.” Phil: “I don't really have anywhere else to go.” Eric: “Second … we're visiting our old friend Corky Spectre to see if he can figure out a way for us to beat Lambert and his she-devil. He's not the only one with old friends he can call on!” Shane: “Corky's a boy genius, and we practically helped raise him. He is a little … off, though.” After a few awkward moments go by with no one coming to the door, Shane rings the bell again. He waits longer, but still nothing. A third try gets the same result. "Hmmm... maybe he really did move... " Acting on a whim, Eric tries the knob and finds it unlocked. Grinning at his partner, Eric pokes his head in and can just make out a faint clicking sound. "Corky?" There's no reply, and yet Eric could swear he heard what sounded like heavy breathing. Eric: “Oh, Rod. He might be hurt!” Concerned, the Phenomenons enters the townhouse. The living area is empty except for mismatched furniture, multiple video systems, and a giant TV. A look in the kitchen reveals that Corky Spectre, boy wonder, has been subsisting off of pizza and Chinese takeout. Shane and Eric tip-toe through the hallway, and quickly notice the clicking sound gets louder by the stairs. Cautiously, they climb up until they reach a door that's ajar. The clicking sound definitely seems to be coming from inside that room and the heavy breathing sound can be heard as well. As the Phenomenons get closer, they can make out words. "Yeah...yeah... yeah... " Shane pushes the door open and his jaw drops in horror at what he sees. "Jumping Jehovah!" Inside the messy bedroom, Corky Spectre sits at a computer only wearing a set of Tsunami Oni underoos. His bespectacled eyes stare at the monitor with the intensity of a religious zealot. Corky's attention is so focused, he doesn't even notice his army of visitors. Eric quietly walks over to see what has their young friend so mesmerized. Porn. Lots of porn. Porn of every kind and orifice. Spanking, watersports, gangbang, girl on girl, and Eric is pretty sure he saw a llama pass by on one of the many windows Corky is furtively opening. Eric: "Good Rod, Corky! What are you doing? No one should ever do that to a poor, innocent llama!" The youngster doesn't even blink, let alone look at the Phenomenon. "So much... there's so much... all for me... " Eric has absolutely no idea how to snap the addicted youngster out of his trance … but luckily, Shane does. So he reaches over and turns off the power to the monitor. As the screen turns black, Corky's expression turns from worship to horror. Corky: "Noooooo! My pretties!" Corky's head whips around and he stares at the Rabbi with bestial rage. Corky: "You! You took my pretties from me!" The Phenomenons are immediately afraid... not of Corky, of course, who likely couldn't do physical damage to a stuffed animal, but of the fact this whole thing is teetering close to being a LOTR parody, one already done by South Park. But then their attention is grabbed by Corky jumping up onto his chair like he was a pale ape with a whole lot of hair replaced by acne. The sight of Corky's yellow stained undies with a bulge poking out underneath them makes the Phenomenons praise Rod that they hadn't eaten yet. Corky snarls and lunges at Shane. Corky: "Rrrrraaaarrrrr!" SMAK! A backhand catches Corky across the face! SMAK! SMAK! SMAK! Corky: "Ow! Owie owie ow ow! Please stop hitting me!" After several long moments, Shane Stone, the least friendly member of the Phenomenal Army at the moment – and Pete the PG Tips Chimp isn't happy about his place being stolen – stops his bitchslappng. Corky settles down in his chair, gently rubbing his pinkened cheeks. Awkwardly, Shane reaches out, ignoring his old friend's immediate anticipatory wince, and gently pats the top of Corky's greasy head. Shane: "Sorry, buddy. But you were completely out of your mind there! You shouldn't be so obsessed with those.... nasty girls on the computer!" Corky looks up at Shane sadly. Corky: "But... they're the only friends I got... " Shane: "Dude! That's totally not true! We're your friends, Corky!" Looking upwards, Corky Spectre finally seems to be aware of where he is and what's going on. He quickly plucks the eyeglasses off of his pimpy face, somehow manages to clean them off – somewhat – with his underoos, and puts them back on. Finally, a giant grin jumps across his face. Corky: “Shane?! Eric!? Luchador! Oh my Rod, I haven't seen you guys in YEARS!!!!” Shane grimmaces as Corky launches himself onto the Phenomenon in a giant nerd-hug. Thankfully the pup-tent has begun to fade away. Corky peals himself off of Shane long enough to hug Eric, then the Trailer Park Luchador, and then luckily stops himself before hugging the homless bum and John Travolta impersonator that are simply randomly following the Phenomenons around at this point. Corky: “Umm … who are those guys?” Eric: “He's not John Travolta, and really that's all that matters at this point.” Corky: “Oh, okay. But … holy crap! I can't believe you guys are here again! You're back together!!!! THE PHENOMENONS ARE BACK!!!” Shane: “Yeah, it's been like nine months. Come on, we went to Canada, not the moon. You could have read about us on the internet or something.” Corky just blinks. Shane: “Whatever, fine, we're back.” Corky: “This … this might be the happiest day of my life ...” Shane: “Yeah, yeah, I know. We're awesome.” Eric: “Shane's in a poopy mood lately, Corky. Sorry.” Shane: “Umm … so what's been going on with you, Corky? Hanging out with any of the old gang?” Corky: "Not since UWF closed. That was the only time when people talked to me. Some even treated me with... with... what's it called when someone doesn't give you swirlies or beat you up for your lunch money?" Shane: "Respect?" Corky: "Yeah, respect." Eric: "Corky, buddy, high school was rough, but you get through it! We've been through this." Corky: "High school? That's what happened to me last week when a couple Girl Scouts dropped by to sell cookies!" Shane sighed and shook his head. Shane: "So are you not a referee anymore? How can you afford these sweet digs, anyway? Not like being an ex-ref pays that much. I know we're not making squat from royalties." Corky: "My dad." Shane: "Lew? HE helps you?" Corky: "Shhh... he really doesn't like that brought up very often. He sends me a check very month or so. More than I can spend, even on premium sites." Eric: "Oh, like a trust fund?" Corky: "More like hush money, I think. Too bad I can't buy friends like I had in UWF... " Shane pats the diminutive man-child on the shoulder, grinning happily. Shane: “Corky … boy am I glad we found you at this perfect moment!” Eric: “Rich in hush money and with no friends?” Shane: “Exactly! It just so happens that WE are your friends, and WE are in need of your hush money and big giant demented brain!” Corky: “Really?” Eric: “Oh yeah.” Corky: “Why, what’s been going on in Canada?” Shane: “Dude, it would take so much time to explain.” Corky: “I don’t really have anything but time these days. And porn. And pizza. Time and porn and pizza.” Eric: “Okay, so you see, I kidnapped Shane and we ran away to Canada and I convinced him to reform the Phenomenons with me (kinda brainwashing but don’t tell him) and then we were running around and we hated Canada and then we met the King of Canada and we beat him up and I took his crown and then I was the King of Canada but then he found us later and he was all like “you can’t be the King of Canada if you’re not Canadian!” and we were all like “Noooooooooo!” and he was all like “Yep!” so then we were Canadian and it was really depressing but then we decided to start trying to really be Canadian and be good Kings to make the Canadians like us so we won TAGSTRAVAGANZA~! and we became the NAPW Tag Team Champions and they gave us $10,000 and we were all like “let’s give this money to the Canadians like good leaders!” but then we realized ROBOSAURUS~! was nearby so we spent almost all of our money renting that and we wrecked the arena with it on accident, cuz nobody told us how to drive it, but we gave the rest of our money to the Canadians but it was only like $1 each and so it didn’t really win them over, but then we won and we just kept winning, and we beat the A-Team and some scrubs interfered and I don’t know why, and we beat Future Shock somewhere in there, and then we stole all of the Rabble’s fake titles but they weren’t fake anymore cuz they spent all of their money making real ones but then they stole them back and we had an epic battle for ALL THE TITLES~! and we won and we didn’t have to put up missing posters anymore but then we went to a bar in Canada because it was the only one that had the Honey I Shrunk the Kids pinball game and we ran into Lambert Hanriel, who used to be the coolest guy in all of time and space cuz see he’s a time traveler and kind of a wizard but I’m not really sure and he gave us ECTO HI-C SLIMER COOLER AND IT WAS THE GREATEST THING EVER but then we had a really awkward standoff and I’m pretty sure he went evil and somehow we went like really good in comparison to him and then I stole the pinball game, so you can totally come over and play it whenever you want, but now we have to go two-on-two with not-cool-anymore Lambert and his like monster woman and we already used the trick where we wrap ourselves from head-to-toe in championship belts so she can’t hurt us so obviously we can’t do that again and we thought maybe if we had John Travolta we’d be able to beat them but then I kidnapped the wrong John Travolta so that’s not going to work because this John Travolta sucks and he isn’t an Archangel or a Phenomenon or anything but really that about gets us back to today I think.” Shane: “…” Corky: “…” Eric: “Shane said it was a long story.” Corky: “Hmm, so you already tried wrapping yourselves in belts?” Eric: “First thing we tried.” Corky: “And that didn’t work?” Eric: “Well, it did for a little while. She’s really strong. But we DID win, so I guess it did technically work.” Corky: “And you’re sure this John Travolta won’t do what you need?” Eric: “He barely even looks like John Travolta.” Phil: “I’m standing RIGHT HERE!” Corky: “So basically what you’re saying is that you have a battle upcoming with your mortal nemeses and you’re looking for an advantage that will allow you to survive this epic showdown and emerge victorious with your championships and glory and all that?” Shane: “Dude, you seriously followed along with all of that?” Corky: “Of course I did. And we’re going to play that pinball game as soon as we’re done here. I’ve never been to Canada, you know. But I do see your point here … how do you defeat a time traveler? Surely he would know your tricks before you pulled them.” Eric: “Exactly!” Corky: “Have you tried working out and getting into peak physical perfection?” Eric: “Already ruled that out. Spent all of our preparation time kidnapping fake John Travolta there. Kinda thought that was going to work out in our favor.” Shane: “Good Rod you’re dumb.” Corky: “Oh, I’ve got it. What about if you lured them in and then … EVIL BLUE MIST~! Right into the time traveler’s face, and then pinned him really fast when he’s blinded?” Eric: “Dude, if you only knew how many times I’ve tried that already! The bad guys make cheating look SO easy. I really don’t know how I’m so bad at it.” Shane: “We're new to it, though. If we had more experience –“ Corky: “You guys already rule out copious amounts of steroids?” Shane: “I think we’ll skip that one.” Corky: “Hmm … well, luckily, I think I have just the thing for you.” Eric: “Is it a Face/Off machine? Cuz Shane already POOPED ALL OVER THAT IDEA, DIDN’T YOU, PARTNER?!?” Shane: “YOU CAN’T REALLY REMOVE SOMEONE’S FACE AND TAKE THEIR IDENTITY!” Eric: “CAN SO!” Corky: “Umm, I was going to say my idea …” Eric: “Oh, right. Carry on.” Corky: “Anywho … you guys remember Luca the Imp, right?” Shane: “Two foot tall demonic imp that always cursed a lot and had really nasty stuff falling out of his penis all the time? Yeah, rings a bell.” Corky: “Yeah, well, for a long time he was just Luca the little manager-thing for the Dark Lords, the second best-tag team in Chicago history, after you guys of course. But then when his team retired, Luca found a brilliant inventor and found a way to get himself into the wrestling ring … using LUCABOT, the 8 foot mechanized terror machine that destroyed all in his path! And so he went from just another flying green imp into being one of the most feared opponents in Chicago wrestling history, winning the World Championship and being invulnerable except for battery power and cords and things. Little stuff.” Eric: “Go on …” Corky: “So, anyway, I’ve been thinking about becoming a wrestler myself, to get my dad’s attention … but I don’t really know how to wrestle, and I don’t like working out, and I don’t want my penis to go weird through steroids, so I came to the conclusion … what if I used LUCABOT? So anyway, it was still sitting outside the arena in Chicago seven years later. That was a cool coindence. So… voila!!” Corky Spectre rips away a sheet, revealing … the massive eight foot tall robot LUCABOT! Corky: “So I’ve been reverse-engineering this for years now getting ready to become a wrestling terror of my own, but you know … I think it would fit you, Eric! And if you were to use this, PLUS my super-secret “no time traveler can know what you’re planning before you do it in the present time and no givesies backsies time traveler” helmet, I don’t think there’s any way in heck this Lambert guy could ever stop you!” Eric: “Corky … you’re a life saver. And true proof that you don’t need to work hard or do preparation work to be able to be a champion. Sometimes you can play hide and go seek for three weeks then steal a fake John Travolta from a wedding and luck into a giant robotic suit of armor!” Corky: “Umm, yeah. But you guys are going to be my friends now, right?” Shane: “Absolutely!” Corky: “Yay!” Eric: “Now just help me into this thing …” Fade away. ----- Several hours later. Stomp. Stomp. Stomp. Stomp stomp stomp. L.U.C.A.B.O.T. stomps through the empty, moonlit fields north of Chicago on its impervious march to Alberta, where only doom awaits Lambert Hanriel and his wench. Stomp. Stomp. Stomp. Eric Cameron can see glory in his future. Stomp. Stomp. Stomp. Nothing can possibly stop Eric Cameron and his giant, invincible robot now! Stomp. Stomp. St— Eric: “Hey, what happened?” Eric hops out of the giant suit of armor, running around it angrily. Finally he sees the blinking red battery light and screams into the night. Eric: “WHY ROD WHY?!?!?!!?!” Eric kicks the suit of armor, nearly breaking his toe. He leaps away with a scream. Eric: “OUCH!!! Oh Rod damnit … WHY DID I TRY TO WALK IT ALL THE WAY BACK TO CANADA?!?!?!” Eric stalks off into the night, walking on his own, shivering, all the way back to Canada-land. --------- On the other side of Chicago, the Phenomenal Army has piled into their Phenomenal Van, now complete with one creepy nerd still dressed in only his underoos. With a trailer behind them bringing their camels home with them, the Phenomenal grouping has finally, truly accepted Canada as their home, leaving Chicago into their rear-view mirrors, without even a thought of returning. But on the other side of the road, a Ford Explorer screeches on its breaks just moments after the Phenomenal Van drives past, heading north. Nearly flipping the SUV over, it finally settles down on the shoulder of the road. A woman leaps out with fury in her eyes, slamming the door shut behind her. As she glares north-bound, there’s no mistaking that van, or the giant air-brushed caricatures on it, or the camels being dragged behind it. There’s no denying her husband was back … and is now gone again. Jessica: “SHANE!!!!!!!!!!!” Leaping back into the van, Jessica Stone barely avoids several car accidents, nearly gets stuck in the median, but finally, angrily, hits the road heading north. Heading to Canada.
