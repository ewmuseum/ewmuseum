[end quote] Jake Phoenix — 20 May 2009 Subject: Re: WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Jake Phoenix © vs The Outlaw Cameras are backstage at the Ogden Legion Hall in Calgary after The Month From Hell has concluded. A number of stagehands, rookies and NAPW talent are milling around the back, but all of them are staying away from one corner of the backstage area.. the corner where "The Murder City Devil" Jake Phoenix is sitting. His attention is not on anybody in the backstage area, but on one single thing - the NAPW World heavyweight championship belt, grasped firmly in his gloved hands. Phoenix's expression is simultaneously one of accomplishment, exhaustion.. and almost disbelief that the goal of his career is finally in his hands.
The only person that dares intrude on Phoenix is everybody's favorite intern, Pete. Pete walks out of the crowd and near Phoenix, smiling all the way.
Pete - Jake, congratulations! You finally did it! It's over! You've got the gold! Phoenix - Over? Phoenix slowly looks up at Pete, his expression replaced by one of sheer determination. Phoenix - It ain't over. It's just started. - ~ - 24 hours later, Jake Phoenix is at the NAPW offices, this time wearing his "Murder City Tour" t-shirt, black jeans, Oakleys, and after long last, the NAPW World heavyweight championship belt over his shoulder. Phoenix has one hand around the bottom of the belt, not letting it go. Pete is standing by in his cheap suit, microphone in hand. The NAPW-Online.com logo is edited in behind them.
Pete - Ladies and gentlemen, Pete right here for NAPW-Online.com, with an exclusive interview with the newly crowned NAPW World heavyweight champion, "The Murder City Devil" Jake Phoenix. Jake, congratulations on winning the gold this past Tuesday night from a very tough Chris Casino!
Phoenix - And? Pete looks up at Phoenix in confusion. Pete - And.. and what? Phoenix - Go on, say it, I'm waiting. Talk about what happened. I know you didn't just come here to talk how awesome I am and how I finally got that monkey off my back. Go on.
Pete - Ummmm...well, Evan Cartwright came in during the match and hit Casino with the title belt, which gave you the chance to give Casino a Tombstone for the win and the belt..
Phoenix - See, there you go. I knew you could get it out. Yeah, Cartwright came in there, yeah, I'm sure Casino's pissed off about it. Hell, I'd be pissed off too, but hell, how many times have *I* gotten *BLEEP*ed over by crap like that? You take what you can get, and what I took was more important than anything else. What I took was the World title, and whether Cartwright was involved or not, nobody can take that away from me!
Pete - Well, you don't have a lot of time to rest, because in just six days' time, you're defending the NAPW title for the first time against The Outlaw.. the man that laid you out at the end of the title match last night!
Phoenix snorts lightly and smirks. Phoenix - Yeah, guess he did, huh? Gotta say, that move of his didn't exactly tickle, especially after I went 20 minutes with Casino. Pretty easy to do it to me when I'm not expecting it after that fight, huh, Outlaw? Don't blame ya, I'd have done the same thing. But there's a big difference between you doin' it when I'm out of it, and you doin' it when I'm goin' one on one with you and throwing big soupbones at your fat head, huh? Me? I can pick you up and drop you on your *BLEEP*in' head at any time, in a match, out of a match, in the damn parking lot. And boy, I'm gonna really enjoy hearin' your head split open like a hollowed-out bowling ball next Tuesday night.
Pete - You're going to have to worry about Jack Stantz as well.. and they're saying it's going to be easy to beat you after your match with Casino, and he'll be well rested! He says it's his destiny to win the World title.
Phoenix - Easy, huh. Phoenix looks down at the ground. Phoenix - You know, I think he's had it pretty easy so far 'round here. Yeah, Hatework, Daniels, tough guys. Real tough. And he took KRENSHOV to the limit, but he ain't around here anymore - I am. I'm the one still standing here after everything, and I'M the one standin' here with the World championship belt. Two years here in NAPW, twelve years in the business, and I'm finally where I want to be. I'm finally where I NEED to be - at the top of the heap. Your DESTINY? Winning the World title is your DESTINY?
Phoenix whips the World title belt off his shoulder and holds it up to the camera. Phoenix - Your DESTINY, Outlaw.. is my *BLEEP*in' REALITY. The REALITY is that I'm now the heavyweight champion of the world. YOUR reality, Outlaw, is that you have one chance to change that fact. You have one chance to take me out of that reality.. and I will be DAMNED if you're going to do it.
Fade out. Signature: "The Murder City Devil" Jake Phoenix NAPW World Heavyweight Champion NAPW Tag Team Champion (06/19/2007-09/27/2007) REBEL World Heavyweight Champion (03/11/2008-05/13/2008) NAPW Canadian Heritage Champion (04/22/2008-02/17/2009) NAPW World Heavyweight Champion (05/19/2009-)
