NAPW Roleplay

MAYDAY

in-universe 28 May 2014

Eric Cameron & Shane Stone — the Phenomenons

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At the back entrance to the NAPW arena, a bizarre confrontation is going on. The Phenomenons – Shane Stone and Eric Cameron – sit atop two giant camels, arguing vehemently with a group of security guards. Eric Cameron: Get out of our way, Canadians! Grrr!!! Cameron swings his stick wildly, trying to drive away the security guards that are preventing his entrance. Security Guard: We have strict orders! No live mammals allowed on live NAPW events! Shane Stone: These are no live mammals! This is Montezuma! And this is Alibaba! They’ve never missed one of our matches! Except for that time they got kidnapped, and we had to wrestle a ladder match with all of our kidnapped animals suspended above the ring … hoo boy, that was intense. The security guards look completely baffled by this entire situation. Eric Cameron manages to connect with the head of one of the guards and shouts in joy. Security Guard: Don’t make us taser these camels! No live mammals allo— A loud [b]GRONK[/b] interrupts the security guard, who has been looking awfully exasperated. And that is when the camera turns on its axis to show the newcomers… Sitting on a pair of llamas. Shaggy coats, long necks, derpy eyes, the works. Behind them, a purple DeLorean DMC-12 is slowly rolling up, NAPW employee Bobby Korderas at the wheel. And as for the two newcomers sitting on the llamas, it was fairly obvious who they were. Especially since one was standing on llamaback, arms out to both sides. He wore a white linen jacket, matching pants, and a black “NAPW - 100% CANADA APPROVED” tee-shirt(Free from the merchandising department!). Looking awfully… dashing, Lambert Haniel leapt off of his llama, landing in a forward tumble, and came to his feet just alongside the Phenomenons, atop their camels. Lambert never lost that dashing grin, and now, turns it onto the Phenomenons. [b]Lambert Haniel:[/b] Gentlemen. Shane waves happily at Lambert, but then goes back to glaring with intense hatred at his tag team partner, Nobody. Meanwhile, Lambert looks to the security guard, taking a look over the chunky man and his vastly important clipboard. The man blinks, taken aback by not one tag team, but two showing up with weird animals in tow. [b]Security Guard:[/b] You-... Uh… You can’t bring those animals in here. The fines would be huge, an- Lambert digs into his pocket, and comes out with a little leather sack. The sack clinks and jingles, in the way that only coinage could muster. A flick of the wrist, and the sack lands with a loud noise on the security guard’s clipboard. The security guard looks down into the sack, fingers spreading the mouth of the bag, and his eyes go hugely wide. [b]Lambert Haniel:[/b] Everything copacetic, bru? The security guard nods, toddling off to the side to count his new moneys, as Lambert wheels on the Phenomenons. The two tag team members of the crew, as well as all of their assorted hangers-on and friends, watch Lambert closely. A few feet back, the mountain who rides a llama, Nobody, climbs off her trusty steed. The llama gives a groan of relief, before stepping up to its brother. The two llamas appear to be conspiring. Lambert extends a hand to the Phenomenons, grinning charmingly. [b]Lambert Haniel:[/b] It’s a treat to get ta meet you fellas before our match… I love everything you do. [b]Shane:[/b] Lambert!!! The only cool person in all of Canada! YES! Can I hav— [b]Eric:[/b] What’s SHE doing here?! The Phenomenons glare past Lambert and the masked Nobody, who still sits atop her llama. Eric snarls – Shane actually barks. Nobody simply tilts her head to the side, glaring at the two men on smaller steeds. An incredible amount of intimidation and menace is reflected in that simple gesture, and Shane Stone visibly shivers. [b]Eric:[/b] Lambert, you know we really like you. Granted, we have no clue what you were thinking when you called your team “Those Who Would Inflict Three” … that’s just silly. But still, we like you! [b]Shane:[/b] But HER!!! [b]Eric:[/b] She attacked us with biological warfare, Lambert! Vengeance must be paid! [LAMBERT EVEN CHARMS THE LICE RIGHT OFF THE PHENOMENONS, SENDS THEM OFF TO PESTER THE RABBLE] [LAMBERT IS FRIEND TO ALL ANIMALS AND MAKES NICEY-NICE WITH THE P’S CAMELS, THE PHENOMENONS GET HUFFY BECAUSE THOSE ARE PHENOMENAL ARMY PROPERTY] As Montezuma and Alibaba, the Phenomenal Camels, bow to Lambert, the Phenomenons topple off their trusty steeds. Eric Cameron rolls back to his feet and stalks back towards Lambert, while Shane Stone falls into a heap by a soda machine. [b]Eric:[/b] How … how dare you!! No one tells the Phenomenal Camels what to do! Montezuma, spit on him!!! Rising back to their feet, the Phenomenal Camels … wander off to socialize with the llamas. Eric Cameron is incensed at the treachery, duplicity, sedition … [b]Eric:[/b] You … you … JERK! [b]Shane:[/b] Eric!!! This is no place for that kind of language! [b]Eric:[/b] You’re right. I’m sorry. Lambert smiles a disarming grin. [b]Lambert:[/b] One thing before you go, gentlemen… I realize that we’re in a business of violence and brutality, so I wanted to make sure I said this. Whatever happens in the ring… Let’s leave it in the ring. No need to get upset over who kicked who in the head, who tried to choke a Scar to death… And with that, Lambert Haniel spins away and disappears with a flourish, leaving the Phenomenons staring after him agape. [b]Eric:[/b] Rod damnit, he’s just too cool to hate!!! [b]Shane:[/b] In retrospect, I kind of miss the lice. Suddenly I feel very lonely. Sadly, the Phenomenons stalk off, unsure of whether they hate their upcoming opponents or not. In the background, Scars enter the arena without saying a word, and no one cares.
