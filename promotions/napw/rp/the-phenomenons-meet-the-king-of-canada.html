The Phenomenons Meet the King of Canada

c. May 2014

Eric Cameron & Shane Stone — the Phenomenons

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Phenomenons Meet the King of Canada-Land [Eric Cameron is driving the Phenomenal-Van through the deserted wastelands of Canada-Land, his chest nearly pressed against the steering wheel, eyes darting from side to side in a very evidently perturbed state.] Eric: “Look at them, Toby. Everywhere we go in Canada-Land they stare at us, with their beady little eyes. This is almost certainly the first vehicle any of them have ever seen. We must be like aliens to them. Rod damnit, I just hate Canada so, so, so much!” Toby: “Yeah, that’s why they’re all staring at you.” [From outside the van, the scene is a little more clear: Eric Cameron is driving, incredibly slowly, a ridiculously overly painted van, which includes several awful insults at the Canadian “race,” dragging a horse trailer holding two camels, through Edmonton’s Muttart Conservatory. A group of schoolchildren who look to be on a field trip are staring at the insane scene with horror as the Phenomenal-Van tears up the beautiful botanical garden.] Eric: “I can’t handle this anymore. Pete, you drive.” [Eric hops up and climbs into the back of the van. With no one driving it, the van rolls toward the group of children, but luckily Pete the PG Tips Chimp hops into the driver’s seat, and, utilizing sticks to reach the pedals, immediately improves the quality of driving, leading this contingent of the Phenomenal Army back to actual roads.] [In the back of the van, Eric Cameron rips the sack off the head of his estranged tag team partner, Shane Stone. Shane immediately begins screaming in anger at his kidnapping.] Eric: “Toby, it just really fires me up inside. I’ve heard of this: Stockhome Syndrome. Whatever that she-beat did to my best friend in the whole wide universe must have been so horrific that Shane can’t even see my face without screaming.” [Shane tries feebly to kick Eric in the testicles, but his legs are tied to the chair as well. Eric drops the sack back over his head and sits down.] Eric: “Luckily, I know just what to do!” [Toby Jenkins, shanghai’d videographer for this crew, finally perks up.] Toby: “So what exactly are we going to do?” Eric: “I’ve developed an incredibly intricate plan. Our brilliant rescue operation the other day was only Step 1. Step 3, of course, will be to arrive at New Albertha Pro-Wrestling just in time to compete in the TAGSTRAVAGANZA event, reunited as the Phenomenons, where the daily threat of dastardly Canadians will be enough to fully remind my buddy Shane of everything we stand for, returning us to the good old days, where we can finally accomplish our TRUE goal!!” Toby: “Umm … winning the NAPW Tag Team Titles?” Eric: “The what? No! CONQUERING THE EDMONTON VALLEY ZOO!!!!!!!!” Toby: “…” Eric: “Liberation might even be a better word! For we will not arrive as conquerors, but liberators, freeing the oppressed of the zoo, and building a self-contained world for ourselves! The only thing worse than our persecution by the Canadians is the idea that these dastardly Canadians keep poor, innocent animals locked away in tiny little prisons, like, like, animals!” Toby: “I’m pretty sure they have zoos in the US …” Eric: “Rod damnit, Toby, this isn’t the 1800s anymore. The rest of the world has achieved some level of decency at this point. But no Canada-land. Never them.” Toby: “Right … so wait, what was step two?” Eric: “Oh. Step two is cake. Un-brainwash Shane.” Toby: “You mean the incredibly angry Shane you just kidnapped from his home and wife that looks like he wants to murder you?” Eric: “Of course that Shane! But that’s only because he’s been tortured into insanity! But luckily, I know exactly how to break him free of this spell.” Toby: “I’m afraid to even ask.” Eric: “All I have to do is find a TV, and play this little tape.” [Eric lovingly pats a videocassette sitting in his lap.] Eric: “Shane believes everything he sees on television. This will work without a doubt!” Toby: “If I hadn’t known you for 15 years, I might actually start wondering if you were an evil genius that’s been torturing Shane for as long as you’ve been torturing me.” [Eric stopped paying attention while picking his nose.] Toby: “So I guess all you need is a TV. And a … VCR? Seriously? You really did go into hibernation when you stopped wrestling.” Eric: “And there’s the one and only problem with my brilliant plan, Toby: where, in the name of all that is good, fun, and democratic in this topsy turvy world of ours, am I going to find a television in Canada-Land?!?!?” Toby: “…” Eric: “And that’s when it struck me: New Albertha Pro Wrestling was created by the sudden discovery by Canadians that wrestling existed because of the one television set in all of Canada-Land. All we have to do, Toby, is find the owner of that one television set ………….. the King of Canada-Land!!!!!” Toby: “Oh my God. Please don’t tell me we’re about to go on some ridiculously long, drawn out adventure to find some mythical King that takes us weeks on end and ends up in some bizarre situation where we’re conquering a fishing village with you riding a polar bear like a knight. I had enough of that 15 years ago.” Eric: “That was my original plan. But then I learned that New Albertha Pro Wrestling could only give us two spots on TV a month – a month!!! – because there’s only one network for that one TV in Canada-Land, so we just Googled him instead.” Pete: “Ooooo! OOOO!!! OOOOO AHHH OOO!!!!!” [Pete the PG Tips Chimp points excitedly in front of the van, which he has expertly driven to the home of the King of Canada without incident.] Eric: “We’re here!” [The Phenomenal Army quickly scrambles out of the Phenomenal Van and approach the shack they’ve arrived at. The dilapidated building, hidden under a highway overpass, seems ready to fall over at any moment … which isn’t helped with a horrible looking drunk stumbles out with a bottle of Molson beer in his hand, slamming the almost-door shut behind him. The man has a giant dirty blonde mane of hair with an old, metal crown within it, and a huge, tangled beard covering his dirty face. His upper half is bare and absurdly tanned, and his old military cargo pants hang far, far too low on his waist, displaying far too much dirty old man pubic hair.] King of Canada: “What! WHAT DO YOU WANT?!?” [Eric Cameron nods at the Trailer Park Luchador, who immediately drops to his knees in front of the hobo. Eric refuses to participate in asking a Canadian for help.] Trailer Park Luchador: “Oh great King of Canada-Land … we are the Phenomenal Army. We have traveled low these many days across your great land, bringing light to areas where there was none before, only to arrive on your doorstep, entreating your aid for our noble quest!” [The King of Canada scratches at his tangled pubic hair. A bird seems to be living in his beard.] King of Canada: “Huh?” [Before the Luchador can dive into the next eloquent request for assistance, Toby Jenkins, kidnapped videographer, intervenes.] Toby: “These idiots think you have the only television and VCR in Canada. Actually, you might have the only VCR in Canada. And they want to use it. And we brought beer.” King of Canada: “Oh. Then hell yeah you can.” [And with the King granting their formal request for diplomatic assistance, the Phenomenal Army now moves into the King’s home.] Toby: “Why do you call yourself the King of Canada, anyway?” King of Canada: “Cuz I’m the King of Canada, numbnuts.” Toby: “Right.” [Inside they go, then. Pete the PG Tips Chimp pushes Shane Stone’s wheelchair into the room, as he screams crazily.] [Several minutes later …] [In the back room of the King of Canada’s Hobo Shack, Shane Stone is strapped helplessly to a wooden chair. A sock has been shoved in his mouth. In front of him is a small, black and white television, with an ancient dual VCR/Betamax hybrid player connected to it. An extension cord is providing power to the set, which snakes out of the room off to wherever its owner has Gerry-rigged it to receive electricity from.] [The ancient hobo who claims to be the King of Canada holds the VCR tape in his dirty, feces-stained hands. Shane can only plead with his horrified eyes. To no avail – the King grins sadistically and slips the videocassette into the player, hits play, and glares back at Shane, reveling in his role as torturer.] Shane: “Pephe? Pephe?! PEPHE!!!” [He struggles uselessly against his bindings as he completely fails to call for help from Pete the PG Tips Chimp. Suddenly, the screen comes to life, with … ] [The Trailer Park Luchador is on the television set, yet wearing a long blonde wig over his luchador mask. He’s wearing a dress, with a name tag stuck directly over the empty left breast, where it’s written: “Hi, my name is: JESSICA STONE.” Shane looks confused.] Shane: “Jessmphca?” [On the screen, the Trailer Park Luchador poses demurely as he attempts to play the role of Shane’s wife, hands crossed behind his back. He occasionally pulls one hand out, holding his script, when he forgets his place.] ‘Jessica’: “Shane. It’s me, your wife –“ *quick glance at the script* “—Jessica. I always knew this day would come. I always knew that our marriage was a lie.” [Shane sobs into the sock.] ‘Jessica’: “I mean, I know you were totally the best husband a woman like me could ever have. But I knew, deep down, that you were really married to wrestling. To the Phenomenons!” [Shane shakes his head violently in disagreement.] ‘Jessica’: “I know you’ll deny it, but that’s only because of what I’ve done to you. I cruelly stole you and brainwashed you so that I could keep you for myself … but I always knew your real family would rescue you from my evil clutches one day! And so my greatest fear has finally been realized! You’ve finally awoken from the spell I put on you! You’ve returned to the Phenomenons, to make best friends and have super awesome matches and fight Vietnamese people and all of the great things you did for so many years before I came into the picture!” [Shane thrashes about violently.] ‘Jessica’: “I just wanted you to know that it was all my fault, and I take full responsibility for everything I did to you! You truly are Shane Stone, the Phenomenon, one half of the greatest tag team in the universe, hero of billions …” Ghostly voice off camera: “… and trillions …” ‘Jessica’: “… of fans! And you’ve returned to them, and they’ll be so happy! Finally people can live again! But now I’ve realized the horrible things I’ve done to you … and I can’t live with myself! I have to break you from the evil spell I put on you … and the only way I can do that, is with my own death!” Shane: “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMPHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” ‘Jessica’: “I love you Shane … but I love the Phenomenons more! Promise me you’ll never stop being the Phenomenons! Promise me you’ll go to …” [Quick check of the script.] ‘Jessica’: “… New Albertha Pro Wrestling’s Mayday event, where you’ll face Jason Richards and Johnny Ajax, as well as Those That Would Inflict Ill, in the most eagerly awaited return match in wrestling history!” [Shane just sobs.] ‘Jessica’: “Promise a dying woman that! And now, I must commit seppuku for my crimes. Good bye, sweet Shane! Good bye, Phenomenons!” [‘Jessica’ lifts up a can of Coke with a crudely made ‘Rat Poison’ label taped to the side and drinks deeply. ‘She’ falls to the ground in a dramatic death scene. The King of Canada wakes up from his drunken stupor and starts laughing at the incapacitated Phenomenon as he cries.] Shane: “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMPHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!” [But as all of this goes on in the other room, far more important matters take place in the “dining room” of the Hobo Shack. A three legged poker table, with a fourth leg made out of delicately balanced empty beer cans, sits in the center, with Eric Cameron and Toby Jenkins sitting across from each other. Pete the PG Tips Chimp is running around looking for things to steal, while the Trailer Park Luchador, now in real time, is standing awkwardly off to one side, filming the scene with the GoPro camera that’s attached to his John Deer cap.] Eric: “Alright. That’s settled. Now, let’s get to work! We’ve got wrasslin’ to prepare for!” Toby: “Wait, are you sure that’s going to work? Shane’s just going to watch that videotape and poof, he’s back to your idea of normal?” Eric: “Toby, Toby, Toby. What did I just tell you about these Canadian TV limitations? We’ve got a match in 20 days and they’ll only let us get on TV one more time until then. Of course this is going to work. There literally just isn’t time for us to try a bunch of crazy scenarios, each more ridiculous than the last, until we finally discovered the one that will wake him up. Gotta fast forward for the Canadians.” Toby: “I honestly don’t even care. Anyway, I guess you already know about your first match in your new home – NAPW, which just rolls off the tongue. Actually, one of your opponents somehow sent you guys a present. It’s pretty creepy. I don’t know how the guy even found us … we stopped at a gas station because you wanted a 94 ounce Coca-Cola, and it was just sitting on a toilet when I went in.” [Toby Jenkins reaches under the poker table they’re sitting at and pulls up a rather elaborate fruit basket. Every sort of fruit imaginable seems to be sitting in the basket, and a large card labeled with “The Temple of Truth” on top, simply says “For the Phenomenons.”] Eric: “Oh! Oh! I like presents!!” [Eric immediately starts hovering over the basket, looking to pick the right fruit. Toby opens up the card and starts skimming.] Toby: “I’m not sure what shaking trees means, but apparently you’re invited to do it.” Eric: “This one!” [Eric Cameron snags a giant apple and takes a huge bite. He almost instantly gags, coughs, and spits out a huge chunk of the apple.] Eric: “What in the name of Rod … WHAT IS THAT?!?” [Eric doubles over, spitting every last piece of fruit out of his mouth.] Eric: “That wasn’t candy! There was no caramel AT ALL on that apple!!!!” [Eric snatches the card away from Toby in disgust.] Eric: “Wait, Those Who Would Do 3? What in the heck does that mean?!” Toby: “I think that’s –“ [Eric Cameron tries to spike the nice fruit basket card, but, being a simple card, it just kind of flutters away harmlessly. Eric glares into the GoPro strapped to the Trailer Park Luchador’s head with murderous intent.] Eric: “Well look here, Those Who Would Do 3, I bet you think you’re just sooooo hilarious, huh! How DARE you try to poison us! And your name doesn’t even make sense! We would do stuff too! I would do things!! And I’ve never even heard of Those Who Would Do 1 OR 2! Toby, who are these practical jokers!?” Toby: “A, I’m pretty sure their name is Those Who Would Inflict Ill, like an illness? B, they’re one of your opponents in your first match, but I don’t know anything about them. I don’t think anyone does. All I’ve heard is that it’s mostly this guy named Lambert Haniel, who is known as ‘The Time Traveling, Intergalactic Lord of All Funk and Love.’” Eric: “Huh. That’s kind of a cool nickname. How come I don’t ever get cool nicknames? Well darn, if you’d told me that instead of just poisoning me with stuff that looks like candy, maybe we could have been friends. But now WE’RE MORTAL ENEMIES LAMBERT!!!!!!!!!!!!” Toby: “Oh God.” Eric: “At least for three more weeks! Then I’ll probably forget, because you sound kind of cool. But …” *shakes fist* “Mortal enemies first! You’re going to pay for this poisoning attempt.” Toby: “Anyway … your other opponents are Jason Richards and Johnny Ajax, two members of Scars. They … ahem … live by a code of honor and respect …” Eric: “Yawn.” Toby: “… they like Japanese wrestling …” Eric: “Boring.” Toby: “They work really hard and fight with pride …” Eric: “BORING.” Toby: “And they train and eat their vitamins and know lots of wrestling moves …” Eric: “BORING BORING BORING! At least Lambert’s cool. I mean, he’s a murderous, poisoning, hippy time-traveling monster, but cool. Good Rod, what are these guys going to do, wrestle us to death or something?” Toby: “You realize you are a wrestler, right?” Eric: “BORING! You know what’s cool about Japan? Sumo wrestlers. Big fat guys in diapers slapping each other is cool. And I like those big bright pictures with the giant eyes. Oh, and they have those little pictures of cute animals everywhere … I was on the subway once, and they had these cute little raccoons that were laughing at people that get their hands caught in the door and their fingers chopped off. So awesome. Where was I? Oh, right. You know what’s not cool, Toby? Those guys. Scars = not cool.” [Toby simply shakes his head and flips through a few more pages of his little notebook. Since being kidnapped, doing background research on the Phenomenons’ new Canadian wrestling home has been an excellent excuse to not engage in Eric’s repeated TP’ing of various Canadian landmarks as they’ve made their way across the country.] Toby: “Oh, so the tag team champions are a group called the Rabble. They’re from Germany, and they have dad-issues.” Eric: “Germany … Germans … Nazis! Trailer Park Luchador, I don’t remember, do we hate Nazis?” [The Trailer Park Luchador unrolls a long parchment. At the top, plainly written, is the title “Groups We Hate.”] TPL: “Hmm … Star Trek fans … Canadians … communists … Bon Jovi fans … people that read … house cats … everyone who has ever worked for Pepsi or RC Cola … here it is, Nazis! We definitely hate Nazis!” Eric: “Score! So many people in this place we can hate! I love hating New Albertha Pro Wrestling already! Screw you, Nazis.” [The people in the room finally realize the muffled sobbing of Shane Stone had stopped, and an eerie quiet had descended on the Kind of Canada’s Hobo Shack. Suddenly, a crash … a scream … and the King of Canada comes flying through a patched cardboard wall that had separated the two rooms. His unconscious form completely hurdles the table and he crashes into the dead fridge head-first. Toby looks horrified at the possible murder of a homeless man, but no one else seems to notice him.] Eric: “Sh … Shane?” [Shane Stone stalks into the room, visibly shaking with rage. Pieces of the broken chair are still strapped to him. His shirt is in tatters, his hair disheveled, a trail of destruction flowing behind in his wake.] Shane: “Need … soda …” [Clearly already prepared for this, Pete the PG Tips Chimp dances over to his long-time friend with a giant convenience store Coke between his tiny, furry hands. Shane snatches it away, and we watch for a long moment as he chugs the 72-ounce beverage down. He sucks up every last drop, leaving us listening to the sound of air being sucked into the straw at the end. The large cup falls from his hands, and then … and then …] Shane: “Whew. Where the heck are we, Eric?” [Eric Cameron simply sprints over and gives Shane a giant hug.] Shane: “Dude, calm it down. You act like … TOBY!!!!!!!!” [Shane Stone, returned as a Phenomenon, drags the clinging Eric over to Toby Jenkins, hugging him.] Shane: “Where the heck have I been? You joined the Phenomenal Army!!! This is … this is the happiest day of my life!!!” Toby: “Mphdshaht.” [Toby gets out nothing from the crushing man-hug. Pete the PG Tips Chimp rushes over and throws a death hug onto Shane’s leg. The Trailer Park Luchador tries to hold back, but finally he gives in, charging into the bear hug as well as he flips off the camera.] [A long, boring, tearful reunion and explanation of events later …] Shane: “I just can’t believe we’re in Canada. I feel disgusting all over.” Eric: “I know. It’s horrible, but it’s the only place I could think to hide from your captor. But now that we’re here, we might as well make the most of it, and finally … DESTROY CANADA ONCE AND FOR ALL!!” Shane: “How are we going to do that, though?” Eric: “I don’t know. It’ll probably involve winning tag team titles or kicking people in the head or something. It always seems to somehow.” Shane: “You know, I like the sound of that. I just really wish there was some way that we could make our place felt in this new, scary land of Canada. Like if there was some crazy tag team only extravaganza … maybe the winning team could get, like, tailored pants or something as a prize! But no, that’s probably too crazy to even ask for. But man, some tailored pants would be pretty sweet.” Eric: “You’re talking madness.” Shane: “Let’s hit the road, Eric. If we’re going to live in Canada now, we’re going to have to blend in with the locals … which means we better start building our igloo.” [Eric Cameron starts to step over the obliterated hobo, but abruptly stops. Inspiration striking, he quickly tosses his Burger King crown aside, and steals the crown off the King of Canada. He flicks a louse off the edge of the crown and then settles it onto his own head, tilting it strangely to one side. He grins happily over his new trophy.] Eric: “Huh. Well I guess I’m the King of Canada-Land now!!! Guess what, Lambert, now I’m the Universal … Cool … umm … Capering Cacophonous Kind of Canada-Land. So now we both have cool nicknames!” [Shane Stone goes to follow his partner out the door of the hovel, but suddenly stops in his tracks as he sees the fruit basket still sitting on the poker table.] Shane: “Oh, candy!” [Shane reaches out to grab an orange out of the fruit basket. Eric turns, eyes going wide in horror as his partner makes a horrible mistake.] Eric: “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” [His warning is too late, though. Shane chomps into the orange with childish delight. A moment passes. Seconds. A full minute. Shane’s face has turned a horrifying shade of green, and with great effort he finally manages to swallow the bite of fruit. He sways on his feet, but Eric rushes over, steadying his partner. He glares into the camera with unbridled rage.] Eric: “You monster, Lambert. You … MONSTER!” [And with that, the new King of Canada-land, Eric Cameron, helps his partner Shane Stone stumble back to the Phenomenal-Van. Their next stop is anyone’s guess … but the Phenomenons have returned. And they … Don’t. Like. Fruit. Oh, and they want to wrestle people and win and stuff.] [Fin.]
