Blueish grey smoke hangs above their heads and the music blares so that one must shout to be heard in the room. Sean sits there, a shot glass in one hand and a Marlboro Menthol in the other; a look of anger on his face. Seamus sits there, much the same, but his shot glass is empty.
Seamus: "Two more Bushmills and just bring the damn bottle why don't ya." The bartender nods, bringing the twins the bottle of Bushmills and sitting it in front of their persons.
Sean: "We've been here since the fall of 2008." Seamus: "Sometime around there, yeah." Sean: "Given one title shot during that time." Seamus: "Yeah, what is your point." Sean: "We bust our ass, bleed for this company, and put our bodies on the line each and every week."
Seamus nods as he pours himself a shot and refills the just emptied glass of Sean. Sean: "The fans love our high flying, gravity defying style of wrestling." Seamus again nods, a look of curiosity on his face. Sean: "And all we've gotten is one lousy f*bleep*ing NAPW Tag Team title shot to show for it."

Another nod and exhale of smoke. Sean: "But all we get is one moth*bleep*ing tag team title shot." Exhale, but no nod. Sean: "And all you can do is sit there and say nothing about it?!"
