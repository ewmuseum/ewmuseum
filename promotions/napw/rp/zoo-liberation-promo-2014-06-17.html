Zoo Liberation Promo

17 Jun 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The world is all blackness, and Jerry Kohler, “Pops” to his friends (and, well, strangers, too – he's quite old, and such a diminutive tends to stick with a man at some point, regardless of familiarity), has been drifting in and out of consciousness for quite a while. Typically, such a thing wouldn't even be out of the norm for him: he'd long since made peace with his own unique blend of narcolepsy and dementia, and significant periods of time being utterly lost to him were as familiar as his own well-lined face, and no more cause for concern than a pimple for most.] [The darkness is out of the norm, though. Usually when he regains consciousness, everything immediately comes back to normal.] [And the stench … even for a man well-accustomed to his own flatulence and incontinence, there's not quite a way to describe the incredible smell emitting from a burlap sack that held the decomposing remains of a skunk for oh so very long.] [And the sounds around him, when consciousness does return … it's like there's a war going on around him. Screams of pain, of triumph, of bestial delight and vengeance … those are a bit out of the norm as well.] [Yet you don't survive for as long as Jerry Kohler has in the world of professional wrestling by being especially timid. Unusually alert for the time-being, Jerry tests his restraints.] Kohler: “Eww, what the heck are these?” [Struggling feebly against the restraints, he's suddenly shocked to discover that he isn't alone in … in … wherever the hell he is.] “They tied you up with Twizzlers. It's actually kind of impressive. I'm not even sure how they did it. They had rope, bungee cords, sheets … I suggested all of them, just to get them to hurry up. And they sat there for three hours tying Twizzlers together into something to hold you down. I'm guessing they plan on eating them to celebrate a victory in this ridiculous war they started. Lord knows they need more sugar fueling them.” [And he's more shocked to discover that he recognizes the voice, from … somewhere?] Kohler: “Eh? Who is that? I know that voice.” “Oh come on, Pops. We worked together for like eight years.” [Suddenly, the burlap sack is ripped from Jerry's face, exposing him to the … furry, angry face of Pete the PG Tips Chimp, former spokes-chimp for the British PG Tips Tea Company. Pete picks his nose while gazing curiously at the elderly man, who stares back, utterly convinced the chimpanzee has been speaking to him.] Kohler: “Oh God, am I having that Planet of the Apes dream again?” [Jerry quickly glances down at his own frail form, clearly disappointed.] Kohler: “I usually look like naked 1960s Charlton Heston in that dream. I just look like naked Charlton Heston's dad, now, like normal. This stinks. Wait, why am I naked if this isn't a dream?” “I think the chimpanzee did that. I didn't catch what all the grunts and shouting meant while he was stripping you, but he definitely seems to have it out for you.” [Toby Jenkins, kidnapped videographer of the Phenomenons, stumbles into view, drinking from a bottle of Canadian Club rye whisky.] Toby: “Mr. Kohler, welcome to hell.” [Jerry Kohler blinks blankly at the drunk man in front of him. He turns his gaze back to the chimpanzee … he remembered, with absolute certainty, seeing that chimpanzee as the World Heavyweight Champion of a major wrestling organization once. A beast feared by all competitors who set eyes upon him … ferocious teeth that mutilated the testicles of any opponent who dared threaten his dominance. There was no doubt in Jerry “Pops” Kohler's mind that he once stood just feet away while that diminutive chimpanzee strode into the wrestling ring, drunk on PG Tips Tea, stumbling around with a massive championship belt that covered nearly his entire body, headlining a Pay Per View extravaganza …] Kohler: “I'm finally going senile. There's no way any of that happened. Is there?” [Pete the PG Tips Chimp eats a very large and very wet ball of snot directly in front of Jerry's face, scratches his own testicles, and hops off the barstool he was standing on, shuffling off to find something more interesting.] Toby: “Mr. Kohler, you're my only hope. We've both been kidnapped by quite possibly the two most dangerous men-children in the world. We've disappeared from all that is normal and sane in this life, with no hope of ever being released or returning to the lives we once knew. Please, please, tell me what has been happening on Game of Thrones.” Kohler: “I have no idea what the heck you're talking about, son.” [Drunk, dejected, and disappointed, Toby Jenkins collapses against the wall and slides down into a heap, utterly defeated.] Toby: “Then there truly is no hope.” Kohler: “I was a prisoner of war in Vietnam, the Korean War, and World War II, boy. If you think you're going to get anything out of me more than … holy crap, I just realized I was a REALLY crappy soldier. I'll tell you anything!” Toby: “Don't be ridiculous. I'm just another captive here. The chimpanzee is actually our jailer … which might be the smartest thing those freaks have done yet. He's the only one of their group I'm actually afraid of.” Kohler: “Who are we talking about? Who kidnapped us?” Toby: “The Phenomenons! Eric and Shane … back together again. Well, technically I guess Shane was kidnapped, too. The more I think about this whole thing, the more convinced I am that Eric's a cult-leader, and we're all going to end up wearing white Nike's and sipping refreshing arsenic-laced Kool-Aid at the end of this.” [Jerry drifted off about half way through that, but wakes back up slowly without missing much of a beat.] Kohler: “Those names sound familiar … those sweet kids that always rode the camels and had BBQs with clowns?” [From his spot on the floor, Toby Jenkins glares at the elderly referee with murderous rage.] Toby: “Those SWEET KIDS have ruined my life! They've taken everything from me! Do you know how I've spent every December for the last THIRTEEN YEARS? I'm nearly forty-years old and every Christmas I become an overgrown Macaulay Culkin, setting up booby traps around my home and rocking back and forth with a shotgun.” Kohler: “Why?” Toby: “Because those sweet kids of yours think it's oh-so-damn hilarious to break into people's homes at Christmas and stage wrestling matches! I spent half of those thirteen years being kidnapped out of my house to commentate at other people's houses, and three times it was my house they destroyed! My family has been ruined … I'm a wreck!” [Swig of rye whisky.] Kohler: “Look, I … hiyah!!!!” [Jerry Kohler breaks through his Twizzler handcuffs and launches himself at Toby Jenkins, but trips on his Twizzler-foot-cuffs and falls flat on his face.] Toby: “And now they're out there 'liberating' the Calgary Zoo. The last I saw them, Eric was riding a hippopotamus while calling himself Howling Mad Murdock, acting out quite the elaborate A-Team parody during this whole extravaganza. Come to think of it, they might have kidnapped you to act the part of Colonel Hanibal Smith during their shenanigans.” Kohler: “What about Mr. T?” Toby: “They wanted to be more 'high-brow' than just going with the inevitable Mr. T jokes. Which I'd imagine is why they've pantsed every member of the zoo staff and staged a ring of primates to defend the premises from the police with Super Soakers filled with urine. High-brow. That's what we do here.” [Pops is feebly attempting to eat the Twizzlers binding his feet together, and failing miserably.] Kohler: “This all sounds kind of exciting and elaborate. So are we being held out here while they rant about wrestling stuff?” Toby: “I think that's the plan. However, I stole their GoPro camera, so they're out there ranting about wrestling and making ridiculous parody-insults against all of their opponents without anyone seeing it.” [Toby Jenkins reaches out and pets the GoPro camera sitting on a table and drunkenly chuckles.] Toby: “I might not be able to ever escape the insanity they've brought on my life, but at least I can ruin this promo!” [Suddenly, the door to the small zookeeper's office bursts open. Eric Cameron stomps into the room, his blue cape billowing out behind him, his face painted with a terrible attempt at camouflage, and, atop his head, beyond the stolen crown that he believes makes him the King of Canada-Land, is the mounting equipment that last time we saw him was holding his GoPro camera.] [This time, it's holding a Hostess Ding Dong, with a piece of tape on the front side with “GoPro” scribbled on it. Eric continues ranting at what he believes is a camera.] Eric: “Point nine hundred and sixty four on why we're better than Future Shock: We didn't steal our name from other wrestlers, we stole our name from an awesome John Travolta movie!” [Suddenly, Eric sees the actual GoPro on the table. He reaches out, snatching away the Ding Dong, furious.] Eric: “But … but … WE CONQUERED A ZOO! THERE WAS A GIGANTIC ACTION SEQUENCE WITH GIRAFFES AND LIONS AND CHIMPANZEES AND PENGUINS AND EVERYTHING!! AND I RANTED ABOUT FUTURE SHOCK FOR THE ENTIRE WAR!” [Shane Stone jogs into the room happily. Seeing the Ding Dong, he lights up even more, and quickly snatches it out of Eric's hands.] Shane: “Oooo, chocolate!” [Eric falls into a heap on the floor of the office.] Eric: “We can't do it, Shane. I give up. Our zoo liberation promo was going to be our crowning jewel! It had everything … violence, yet with depth, like a Lambert promo! Animals, and … more animals! We skewered the A-Team with our biting parody, out-ranted the ranting Future Shock for three hours, stared intimidatingly into the camera without saying anything for fifteen minutes and then broke a bunch of stuff like LEGION, spent thirty seconds calling my dad and begging for money and then randomly insulted one opponent like we were Rabble, and for a good five minutes I didn't pause for one single breath while I spoke in a long monotonous line while refusing to acknowledge the match we just lost like Jason Richards! AND NO ONE WILL EVER SEE IT!!!!” [Eric sobs quietly to himself.] Eric: “We'll never be able to out-promo Lambert. He's just too gosh-darn cool!” [Shane looks around optimistically for more Ding Dongs.] Shane: “Maybe we could beat him in the ring?” Eric: “Oh what's the use. We already beat him in the Thumb War. We'll never beat him where in counts … in cool, ridiculous television ti--” Toby: “HIYAH!” [Toby Jenkins dives into the scene, hitting Eric Cameron over the head with his bottle of Canadian rye whisky. It doesn't break.] Eric: “OWW!!” [Shane just stares quizzically as Toby throws open the doors of the zookeeper's office. Outside, a lion is slowly picking at the carcass of a penguin, but he doesn't seem too interested. Toby still screams in horror, and runs off in another direction, towards freedom.] Eric: “Huh. We didn't lose video guys that fast back in the old days. POPS! You're our new video guy!” [Jerry Kohler lies on the ground, naked and snoring, tangled in the Twizzlers that he could never get free of.] Eric: “Hmm. This is going to be trouble. And Pete, why is he nekkid?” [Pete the PG Tips Chimp responds by throwing feces at the back of Kohler's bald head.] Shane: “Pete's still mad that Jerry was the referee in the match where they stole his championship. I don't blame him. That was some darn horseplay … and not the horseplay I like, where you get to actually ride horses and joust unsuspecting pedestrians.” Eric: “Oh well. Is there anything even left to really say about that one show now?” Shane: “You mean … TAGSTRAVAGANZA~! ?” Eric: “Yep.” Shane: “Only that I'm totally cutting off the sleeves of my blue jacket if we win.” Eric: “I like it! Anyway, screw this promo.” [Eric just shuts the GoPro off, because the Phenomenons are bored.]
