NAPW — Roleplay

WAR GAMES

25 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kkp — 25 February 2009 - Day Of The Show, Dirty Money Locker Room - The three members of Dirty Money (four if you count Suzy Scum) are getting in the right frame of mind for what will be a night of heartache and bloodshed. Chris Casino, dressed in his ring gear and sitting on a bench, is quietly tapping up his hands. Evan Cartwright is chill, still dressed in his suit and chatting on the cell phone; and KKP? He's looking down at the floor, lost in his own violent thought process.
Krusty: We end this tonight, guys! NAPW doesn't have room for cheap Dirty money knock offs. Tonight we (BLEEP) up The Faces and we dismember III.
Casino: As long as we're spared anymore of those annoying media blitz promos from The Faces after tonight. I've seen more entertaining shit on PBS.
Krusty looks over to Casino. Krusty: You're cool going in first? Casino: Yeah, I need to work out some demons tonight. I need to spill the blood of The Faces in order to help me forget about Monique. I have to tear the flesh off III so I can bury the shame of losing the Tag Team Championship to those clowns last week. But most of all... I just want to kick someone's ass.
Krusty: (laughs) You have issues, Chris. I dig that about you. Casino shrugs his shoulders. Krusty: (looks over at Cartwright) Hey, Chatty Cathy! Get the (BLEEP) off the phone! Cartwright holds up a finger and smiles at Krusty. Krusty: Damn, he loves to talk on the phone. Casino: Tell me about it. Try getting him off the line when he calls your ass drunk at two in the morning.
They share a laugh and all of the hard feelings of the past week seem to evaporate. Krusty: Look, Chris, I just wanted to tell you that I'm.... Casino holds up a hand, cutting him off. Casino: Wait until we win this match, then you can get all mushy on me. Tensions have been running high this week and we've all said things we didn't mean. Well, except when I said I wanted The Faces Of Death to die a slow, horrid, painful demise. That I did mean.
Again they share a laugh. Click! As Evan hangs up the phone he turns his attention to his team mates. Evan: And suffer they will. Paul walks over to Suzy and talks quietly to her in the corner, giving her a pep talk most likely. He turns to his teammates and excuses him self from the room for a moment.
Krusty: I’m going to walk Suzy to Demo’s room, I’ll be back… Evan you better get ready. Paul and Suzy head out the door leaving Chris and Evan to prepare and talk amongst themselves.
Chris: This feels like old times, cuz. Remember when we did stuff like this back in the day with the likes of Dev, Chopper, Longshot, and Pit Boss?
Evan: Don't forget Mad Dog, the world's strongest man at 6'10" 256lbs. Chris (laughing): How can I forget that? Did he ever shack up with what's her face...? Evan (grinning): Stephanie. McMahon. DOG! Both of their sides figuratively split and they can't help but clutch their sides from all the mirthful laughter.
Chris (wiping away a tear): The good old days. Evan: Which are behind us now... time to start some good new days for our clique. We took over every fed we'd been in before and NAPW has been no different until Bob and Kenny decided that they couldn't let us have fun without being challenged for sandbox supremacy. Then Ryan, Rees, and Brucey decided they want to rip the spotlight from us and shine it on their irrelevant asses. Don't even get me started on that psychopath, D!
Chris: I wasn't planning to. But you're right, we've got to keep on doing what we do like the viral locusts that we are.
Evan: Well put... lovely even. Chris: Why thank you, sir... so, tell me, what's the deal with you always trying to stick it to Paulie's girl? I know you got a thing for Suze and all but c'mon.
Evan slaps his stablemate upside the head as Casino is back in hyena mode. Evan: Think whatever you want, dirty-minded pig (BLEEP). Nothing going on there, I just treat her well cause she's my buddies girl and she's on our team. Besides, she's a good worker, I admire that.
Chris: You'd better subscribe to the bros before hoes concept. Evan: You know I always do... I'm insulted you even brought it up. But I think Paulie is more worried about her teaming with REPO-MAN than if she were teamed with me. I kiss her hand on occasion, but REPO is the kind of guy that will lick the soles of her feet.
Chris: Yuck! Evan: I'm not worried though, we've got our own thing to do. I'm going to get changed in my gear, meet you guys back here in about ten or fifteen?
Chris: What, no group shower this time? Evan (laughing): You're funny on show nights, Chris. Catch ya later. Evan departs as Chris goes back to taping himself up while watching the door that KKP and Suzy Scum disappeared behind.
Casino: Ma.. Doesn’t have enough time to get three letters out his mouth before the door comes crashing open and in walk's Paul. He seems rather calm, collect at an eerie ease.
Casino: how’s Demo and Suz looking? Krusty: They’ll be fine. Casino goes back to the wrapping of his wrist. Paul grabs him self a cold one and then lays down on the bench, one arm drapes over his eyes as he let’s out a breath of relief. The two remain calm, trying to remain focus as they get them selves in the mental state any superstar needs to do before going out to that ring and tearing another man apart, before they go out to the hundreds of booing fans and kill one another for the right to call your self the best.
Krusty: How ya doing man? As he takes a sip from his beer. Casino: I’m fine man, I’ll worry about my problems after the war. Back to the silence as casino is gripping the air in his hand testing the tape job. He begins to stretch as he turns to Paul.
Casino: Don’t you ever warm up? I mean even stretch, it’s a miracle you still have ligaments.
Krusty: All I need is this.--Holds up the Beer, followed by a sip.-- And this. As Paul points to his brain Evan comes walking in, another one ready to do battle, one more to go.
Evan: So ya boys ready to do this? Paul stands up, he cracks his neck like the tough guy he is and walks over towards Evan and Chris, a level of dedication can be seen in his eyes, but a smile of horror runs across his face.
Krusty: Boys, for months people have shat on the name of “Dirty Money” Called us anything but what we deserve. --Glares to Evan, then slides his head to Casino.-- The Best. We dominated NAPW last year, it was “Dirty Money” this “Dirty Money” that. It was all over the awards, it was etched in the tag team and world title.
Paul puts one arm around Casino and the other around Evan as the draw there heads in for a traditional huddle, although they do remind high enough to see the backs of Casino and Evans head and Kkp’s evil eyes darting about as he talks.
Krusty: We have beaten everyone there is to beat, and still those parasite cock worms in the F.O.D or the lost souls of “LDK”, Dan Ryan and the Beast. Three morons who managed to get together and take a head count. Walk around like there the king of this dog pound, well they do nothing more then talk, cry and scream about the things we have done. Why III is nothing more then a bunch of envious boys who are desperate to steal the spot light back. But I need to know.?
His eyes dart to Evan. Krusty: Are you with me in this? there is no I quite tonight. There is no giving up until we die?
They both nod as Paul turns his eyes to Casino. Krusty: We are going in to that ring one hundred percent focused, nothing to distract us. If they break our arms we scream when it’s over. If they slit are throats we die when we win. No giving up. There is no question about it, we are the better team and tonight, we prove it. Do you feel me.
They both nod there heads again, as the crazy in-depth Paul continues his out of the know where prep talk.
Krusty: Now lets break their bones, crush their skulls, rob them of there mother (BLEEP) dreams. Send three of the soon to be six victims to the bottom of the ladder, on fire.
Evan: This is War Gentlemen! Casino: Now lets go make some causalities! The Three turn around and head for the door, their faces full of confidence, their eyes full of rage. They are ready for the battle there is no question about that, but is the battle ready for them?
SLAM! As the door slams shut behind them --Camera fades to black.-- Signature: World Title X1 Tag Team Champion X1 (The Awesome power that was Sexy Adorable Drunks, S.A.D) DIRTY MONEY BITCHES!!!!!! Wins=13 Looses=7 Ties/Draws=2 Bruce Richards — 25 February 2009 (The NAPW studios. A disturbingly shirtless Lloyd Rees is in the corner, fuming, while Dan Ryan is talking to him angrily in the corner. Bruce Richards is talking on his cell phone, blowing off some steam.)
BRUCE RICHARDS: I mean, it's just pathetic. For one second I think that Rees might be able to man up and put his damn ego aside for the team, but the guy's just so unprofessional I don't know what to do. (Pause.) Yes, I'm aware that he's a Maritimer. (Pause.) No, I was not aware that all Maritimers were irrationally emotional. (Pause.) No, I was not aware that you were joking.
(Dan Ryan and Lloyd Rees emerge from the corner, Rees grabbing his shirt from the ground and pulling it over his shoulders. Ryan taps Bruce on the shoulder, and he goes back to the phone.)
BRUCE RICHARDS: Looks like we're going to give this another shot. I'll come over later. (Pause, and Bruce laughs.) Sounds good.
(Bruce hangs up the phone and stares hard at Lloyd Rees, who stares hard right back.) DAN RYAN: Okay. We've had a talk. BRUCE RICHARDS: Good. DAN RYAN: And we've decided that you need to apologize. BRUCE RICHARDS: Oh my (BLEEP) GOD. Apologize for what? For making the best out of a seemingly bad situation with Mister Bunny Lover? For actually pulling out a win against Dirty Money? For ending Casino and Cartwright's undeserved title run before it even began? What the hell do you want me to apologize for?
LLOYD REES: Fer walkin' away. BRUCE RICHARDS: This is ridiculous. LLOYD REES: Nah, it's not. Dis is what it's been about right from da beginnin', Bruce. Ya walked away from me an' Dan when we needed ya, and now I don't know if I can trust ya.
BRUCE RICHARDS: You were in trouble because you ran down and got yourselves in a situation that I kept asking you to steer clear of.
LLOYD REES: Yeah, an' you know what? We're sorry about dat. We weren't listenin' to our partner. But part of bein' a partner is helpin' yer pal out even when he's got himself in a stupid situation. An' you didn't do dat.
DAN RYAN: So yeah. We apologize to you for what we did. But what you did? Leaving us to the mercy of the Faces and Dirty Money? That's not cool either, Bruce.
(Bruce looks back and forth at his partners. He frowns. But he nods his head.) BRUCE RICHARDS: I'm sorry, Lloyd. I know it's hard to trust me now. But I promise you that you will be able to trust me when it comes down to the bone on Thursday.
LLOYD REES: All right, boys. Let's do dis. (Once again, the members of III stand in front of their the banner, Rees in the middle, flanked by Dan Ryan and Bruce Richards. Once again, the Camera man gives them the sign to start. Lloyd Rees looks to Dan Ryan on his left, then to Bruce Richards on his right. He takes a deep breath, and begins.)
LLOYD REES: It's no secret dat da t'ree of us have had our share o' problems. Some might even say dat we had more d'han our share. But dat's what happens when ya put together a group like III. Ya got people dat don't always see eye ta eye, and guys wit' different points a' view. Sometimes, it's hard puttin' yer diff'rences aside and doin' da right t'ing.
DAN RYAN: But even though it's hard, it's something you have to do when you're surrounded by people who are working hard to destroy the thing you love. And no-one can say that Dan Ryan, Bruce Richards, and Lloyd Rees don't love the NAPW.
BRUCE RICHARDS: Well, people could say it. But they would be dead wrong. This company helped each and every one of us out in different ways. This is the company that gave me my big chance, straight out of Gastown, when I was a nobody thinking I was a somebody. And it gave me a place to grow and be a better wrestler than I could have ever imagined.
LLOYD REES: An' da NAPW wuz da place d'hat made da Lemondrop Kid a genuine sensation. Yers truly is a Grand Slam Champion here, but not only dat, I got nine different title runs. Lloyd Rees helped ta lay da bricks in dis house we call da NAPW, an' I don't much like it when people don't respect me in my own house.
DAN RYAN: And for me, the NAPW was the place that I learned to reinvent myself. To show that I wasn't the same Dan Ryan that had dominated the scene for over ten years: that I could have a different outlook on things but still be just as damn good as I ever was. So when I see people making a mockery of this place, with their delusions of grandeur and complete lack of respect for anyone including themselves, it gets my dander up.
BRUCE RICHARDS: We've said a lot about our opponents over this past week. And it's all been true. It's true that FoD! are two-faced liars that would use you as a stepping stone to get where they want to be, which is further down into the sewer, but also wouldn't hesitate to stuff your head in a bag the minute you became even slightly inconvenient. And it's true that Dirty Money may have shone real brightly at the end of 2008, but in 2009, after losing all their titles and all their standing, their shine was revealed to be nothing more than that of fool's gold.
LLOYD REES: So at War Games, dere will be a meetin' of powers like ya never seen before. DAN RYAN: And blood will be shed on all sides. BRUCE RICHARDS: But even though nine men will go down into the mud to fight...only III will rise again.
LLOYD REES: And ya can bank on dat! (Fade out.) Signature: Former NAPW World Champion Five-Time NAPW Tag Team Champion "Curse You Richards!"
