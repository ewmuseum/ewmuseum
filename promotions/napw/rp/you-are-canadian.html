NAPW Roleplay

You Are Canadian

20 Jul 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Eric Cameron slumps down, landing with a thud on his bottom. His face is a mask of confusion, horror, and revolt. Whatever has just happened to one half of the NAPW World Tag Team Champions is unbearable ... Unbelievable... Un phenomenal!] Eric: "It can't be ... It just ... Can't ..." [With barely the strength to even process the information, Eric just collapses, a single tear rolling down his cheek. In front of him, and old homeless man once known as the King of Canada scratches at his testicles absently, bored.] Ex-King: "I'm afraid it is, son. You ... Are Canadian." [Suddenly, Shane Stone races between the two men.] Shane: "Nooooooo!" [Shane throws himself through the glass window, where he crashes four feet to the ground outside in a misguided suicide attempt.] Eric: "But I don't ... How ..." Ex-King: "But I just told ya ..." [Flashback!] ------------------------------------ [One hour ago.] [Shane Stone pounds on the mat of the wrestling ring, his arms rippling with muscles he does nothing to maintain, shown off by the sleeveless blue jacket he wears.] Shane: "Hip toss! Hip toss I said!!!" [In the ring, ancient member of the Phenomenal Army, Jerry Kohler looks completely befuddled. And that is only partially due to the tiny black and white striped tights that account for the only hidden part of his wrinkled body. He finally gives up at the ghost wrestling he was doing in the ring, and sighs at Shane.] Jerry: "I just don't get it! How am I supposed to learn how to wrestle by just pantomiming moves? It doesn't even make sense!" Shane: "Aww, young Padawan. There is much you are yet to understand!" Jerry: "Grrrr!" [Off in the corner, Eric Cameron finally looks up from the large stack of Canadian money he's been counting ... Namely, the $10,000 awarded to the Phenomenons as winners of Tagstravaganza~!] Eric: "Ya know, Shane, maybe he's right. Pops has been making awesome progress at ghost wrestling. Maybe we should spend some of our prize money here on some sparring partners?" [Shane glares at his partner... Who continues to look ridiculous in a large gold crown and a blue jacket tied around his neck as a cape.] Shane: "You know what we agreed to spend that money on!!!!" Eric: "Relax, Bud! We have some spending money left over after we get the big surprise for the show paid for. Let's loosen up these purse strings and give this kid with the giant dreams a real shot at superstardom!" [Jerry grins happily. He forgot his dentures today, so it's a big, sloppy, gummy grin.] Eric: "What about those guys?" [Eric gestures at two monstrous men, clearly abusing absurd amounts of chemicals. Jerry's face immediately goes from happiness to pure horror.] Shane: "Hey! Roid guys! Could you help us out for a minute?" [The two monsters look confused, but eventually waddle walk their musculatures over to the ring.] Shane: "What's your names?" [The bigger of the two, wearing full face makeup, speaks up first.] Guy: "They call me Ultimate Hogan." Other Guy: "And I'm Hulk Warrior, brother." [Shane, Eric, and Jerry just stare dumbfounded at the pair.] Ultimate Hogan: "Get it?" Shane: "Oh, I think everyone gets it, brother." Hulk Warrior: "They call us the B-Team." Shane: "Umm, how do you guys get by without getting sued? You seem to be breaking five different copyrights at once!" Hulk Warrior: "We're in Canada Land, brother! Laws don't count up here!" Ultimate Warrior: "Oh, we also like to sing along to various copy written songs whenever a camera is nearby!" [Suddenly, the B Team breaks into song.] "Did you ever know that you're my heeeeeeero! "You're everything I wish I could be!" Shane: "Dudes, cool it. If you get us sued, they'll take all of our Canadian funny money! And we have awesome plans for that stuff!" Eric: "Could you guys help out our buddy here? He really wants to learn how to wrestle." [Jerry's tights have gotten very wet as the massive idiots climb into the ring. What they lack in ability to bend over or really move their limbs at all, they more than make up for with strength, unoriginal names, and delicate voices of angels.] Hulk Warrior: "You want us to fight him, brother?" Ultimate Hulk: "Doesn't seem very fair..." [Suddenly, Eric Cameron blasts both men over the head with the Trailer Park Luchador's cane. They fall completely unconscious on the mat.] Shane: "And then you pin them! Lesson complete." Jerry: "What? I still don't know a single move!" Eric: "That's lesson two! The springboard 450 splash! Now start off outside the ring..." [Jerry is horribly confused, but luckily at that very moment, the doors to the gym swing open. A tumbleweed blows through. Eery piano music plays. And in steps the old homeless man who once ruled all of Canada Land. The ...ex-King of Canada.] Eric: "You again? We totally got bored of you an hour ago when we shot the prelude to this!" Ex-King: "I have to be here so the beginning makes sense. And I have to explain to you that what you've refused to hear all along. That because you are the king of Canada... You are Canadian!" [A scream. A crash.] "Noooooooooooooo!" [To be continued when it won't count anyway.]
