The Phenomenons' Entrance at Beach Blanket Bodyslams

in-universe 29 Jul 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FRANK WARBURTON: And their opponents, accompanied to the ring by the Phenomenal Camels Montezuma and Alibaba, Pete the PG Tips Chimp, and … uh… FRANCIS THE SKUNK. They are the REIGNING NAPW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!!! At a – Frank's brilliant introduction is interrupted strangely by a disheveled homeless man who crawls into the ring, dragging his pants back up behind him. Frank is clearly disgusted by the smell that's following the man, and only with extreme care is he willing to take the notecards being handed to him. BILL HEWSON: What in the world is happening here? Nobody interrupts Frank Warburton!! JACK JONES: Obviously the champions are changing the script! Good for them! That's the prerogative of main eventers! FRANK WARBURTON: Excuse me ladies and gentlemen, I've just been notified that the Phenomenons would like to amend their introduction. Due to their triumphant victory over the previously undefeated Rabble, they are now ALSO the reigning @nerdist Podcast Tag Team CHAMPIONS! BILL HEWSON: They can't possibly mean to ... FRANK WARBURTON: Additionally, they are ALSO the reigning Edmonton Rush 2014 NLL Champion Tag Team CHAMPIONS! JACK JONES: Oh, it's happening! FRANK WARBURTON: AND ALSO the reigning “HMV Pure Rewards Card” Tag Team CHAMPIONS! BILL HEWSON: And I thought the Rabble were annoying... FRANK WARBURTON: AND ALSO the reigning Safeway Refresh-Brand Natural Spring Water Tag Team CHAMPIONS! JACK JONES: Champions deserve to be recognized for their achievements! FRANK WARBURTON: AND ALSO the reigning “Andrea, Dave loves you and wants to spend the rest of his life with you, will you marry him?” Tag Team CHAMPIONS! ALSO the reigning TacoTime Tag Team CHAMPIONS! ALSO the reigning Snickers Peanut Butter Bar Tag Team CHAMPIONS! ALSO the reigning Mickey Mouse Tag Team CHAMPIONS! ALSO the reigning Mickey Mouse Tag Team CHAMPIONS! ALSO the reigning The 300: Rise of an Empire Tag Team CHAMPIONS! AND thanks to the generosity of Mr. Lambert Hanriel, they are ALSO THE REIGNING UNIVERSAL TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS OF THE WORLD!!!!!!!!!!! BILL HEWSON: That doesn't even make sense! FRANK WARBURTON: At a total combined weight of SIX-hundred and THIRTY-nine pounds, they are ERIC CAMERON! SHANE STONE! AND tonight only, THE TRAILER PARK LUCHADOR! THHEEEEEEE PHHHENNNNOMENONS!! Nothing happens for one long, awkward moment. Finally, the old, homeless man in the ring realizes that he forgot something, coughs, and hands another notecard to Frank Warburton. BILL HEWSON: This is never going to end, is it? FRANK WARBURTON: Excuse me, it's just come to my attention that the Phenomenons will be accompanied to the ring tonight by none other than … the 30-ton, fire-breathing, transforming T-Rex that eats cars and planes for an afternoon snack!!!! The 40-foot tall metal dinosaur with power that dwarfs even his pre-historic ancestors!!! Sporting 20-foot-flames that shoot from his nose and a jaw that crushes with a STAGGERING 28,000 pounds of pressure per square inch … here tonight to promote his presence all week at Castrol Raceway near the Edmonton International Airport – show your ticket stubs from tonight's event for a 10% discount!! – HE IS ROBOSAURUS!!!!!!!!!! BILL HEWSON: … JACK JONES: Oh … no …. BILL HEWSON: They can't … possibly … mean to … And just like that, a monstrous roar erupts, and a massive explosion of flames erupts in the night air. JACK JONES: We're OUTSIDE!!! There's nothing to stop them!!! In the parking lot for tonight's event, it rises – transforming from its shape as a truck, the monstrous ROBOSAURUS rises to its full height … and in the cockpit, the Phenomenons, Shane Stone and Eric Cameron, look to be having the absolute time of their lives. BILL HEWSON: We … gave them … $10,000 … for this?!?! Having little to no idea on how to actually operate the giant ROBOSAURUS, the monstrous machine staggers drunkenly through the parking lot. Just prior to heading towards the ring in the distance, ROBOSAURUS stops over a lone Lexus in the parking lot. JACK JONES: Wait, that looks like … And just like that, ROBOSAURUS pulls the Lexus up, and, with a swift, destructive crunch of its jaws, crushes the car, letting it fall to the concrete in two separate, obliterated pieces. JACK JONES: THAT'S MY CAR!!!! ROBOSAURUS roars in victory, followed by another monstrous eruption of flame through its metal nostrils. Within the cab, the Phenomenons throw a mocking PHENOMENAL HIGH FIVE~! at each other. BILL HEWSON: And you like them!!! With that accomplished, ROBOSAURUS drunkenly stumbles forward, rampaging through the backstage staging ground of the BEACH BLANKET BODYSLAMS event here at Sylvan Lake. NAPW employees scurry in every direction, screaming in fear at the metal monstrosity crushing catering tables and collapsing dressing rooms. Various security guards try to impede its progress, but there's nothing they can do but run in terror just before the beast can step on them. With an absurd path of destruction trailing out behind them, finally ROBOSAURUS erupts from the backstage area, into the makeshift outdoors arena. The entire entrance area is absolutely annihilated, and the beast shakes off tattered entrance curtains and the steel poles that had held everything together. With another roar and a burst of flame, the opening notes of Offspring's “Original Prankster” sound out through what's left of the NAPW sound system. Until the break of dawn!!! The cab compartment of ROBOSAURUS opens up and the Phenomenons stand up, bowing to their audience. There are no cheers or boos greeting their arrival … the audience here tonight is absolutely, across the board, speechless, stunned into silence at everything that just happened. Life life Cannot go by the letter Time time Prozac can make it better Noise noise Any kind will do Can you feel it slip away When it's all on you Behind ROBOSAURUS, the rest of the entrance area collapses. Several members of the NAPW ring crew are simply sobbing on the floor as the amount of work this will be to clean up dawns on them. The two men working security at the entrance rip their security badges off their uniforms, spike them onto the ground, and walk out. BILL HEWSON: They've … destroyed everything … JACK JONES: MY CAR!!!! Crime crime Rockin' like Janet Reno Time time Eighteen and life in Chino Freud Freud All along it's true Well you'll see there comes a day Catches up to you Hopping down to the ground near their destructive pet, Shane Stone and Eric Cameron flex wildly with the Universal Tag Team Championship of the World belts around their waists. Their movement is clearly limited by the absolutely massive sold gold belts. BILL HEWSON: They're not even wearing the NAPW belts! Knock down the walls, it's alive in you Knock down the place, you're alone it's true Knock down the world, it's alive in you Carefully emerging from the audience, now that there are no security guards left to stop them, are more members of the Phenomenal Army. Montezuma and Alibaba, the Phenomenal Camels, walk out beside their masters, their necks draped with every single title belt the Phenomenons had stolen from the Rabble the day before. Pete the PG Tips Chimp rides on the back of Montezuma, barely managing to hold the NAPW Tag Team Title belts in front of him, with the burlap sack that contains the deceased remains of Francis the Skunk thrown over one tiny, furry shoulder. BILL HEWSON: Oh dear. We can't even keep their animals out of the ringside area … who decided to let these guys headline the OUTDOOR event?!?! You gotta keep your head up through it all You're gonna Bust out on it - original prankster Break out yeah - original yeah Bust out on it - original prankster You never stop now, stop now That's what the main man say Pete the PG Tips Chimp hops off the Phenomenal Camel and, delicately, rips off what he had been using as a saddle. The Phenomenons hold up the NAPW Tag Team titles triumphantly before gingerly placing them in the “saddle,” which turns out to be a massive sack. They begin, one after another, holding up each of their liberated championship belts, placing them one by one into the sack. JACK JONES: This will never end … Finally running out of championship belts, the Phenomenons remove the Universal titles from around their waists, Eric clearly straining to carry the weight of his belt, and lightly set them into the bag. Pete the PG Tips Chimp cinches the bag tight and begins dragging it down the ramp … but the bag is now so heavy he nearly falls over. He turns his back to the ring, dragging with all of his power, making slow but steady progress. Meanwhile, Shane and Eric take a racing stance, count down 3, 2, 1 … and burst toward the ring as fast as they can run. BILL HEWSON: I'll give them this – this is the most ridiculous entrance I've ever seen. Frank Warburton is leaving the ring, shaking his head sadly, and doesn't even see Eric fly past him, ripping the microphone out of his hand. He slides deftly into the ring, leaps to his feet, and twirls proudly as Shane just barely finishes after him. Eric Cameron calmly adjusts the crown atop his head. ERIC CAMERON: MY FELLOW CANADIANS!!! BILL HEWSON: They're not seriously going to keep this going … ERIC CAMERON: Tonight, we wanted to sincerely apologize to ALL of Canada for our previous actions! The Phenomenons have been unfairly judging your beautiful country all of this time, and unfortunately, no one told us that being your King accidentally forced US to be Canadians too – Shane clearly gags at hearing this out loud. ERIC CAMERON: And so we wanted to assure all of you that, as your rightful rulers and fellow Canadians, we will rule over your country with grace, dignity, and fairness for all! Not just those of you here at this kickass lake tonight, but all Canadians everywhere – mounties, hockey players, and even French people alike!!! The audience seems to finally be coming out of the shock of seeing the NAPW stage obliterated by a robot dinosaur and greet this announcement with a fair round of jeers. Bizarrely, some just start cheering wildly, though, which would have to be credited to good alcohol sales. ERIC CAMERON: And just to prove to you that we are genuine in our promise, tonight we are announcing that we will be DONATING our $10,000 prize money from winning TAGSTRAVAGANZA to all here in attendance!!!! A huge roar greets this promise of free money. Shane whispers in Eric's ear. ERIC CAMERON: Oh, right. I mean we're donating what's LEFT of our prize money after renting out ROBOSAURUS!!!! You can't really put a price on the GREATEST ENTRANCE IN WRESTLING HISTORY … but Castrol Raceway DID, and it was $9,211 for the night. We also bought a really big bag to carry all of our title belts. And a big box of cookies. And remote control helicopters. But the other $578 is ALL YOURS!!!! Lighter cheers this time … followed by a sudden realization of how much that is per person, and a disappointed groan. BILL HEWSON: They spent nearly their entire winnings on renting that giant monstrosity … JACK JONES: They can't give that money to THEM! They won't have anything left for me to sue them for!!! Eric and Shane now face the A-Team and Gustav, who stand in opposite corners, clearly not happy about teaming together or the incredible amount of time they've been left standing in the ring. ERIC CAMERON: Alas, we also have sad news to report today. Unfortunately, the Trailer Park Luchador suffered a severe injury this afternoon while fencing our polar bear, and will be unable to participate in tonight's match. Gustav grins wickedly, smelling an advantage for his revenge, but the A-Team are clearly prepared for more shenanigans. ERIC CAMERON: But luckily we have a substitute already prepared to take his place in tonight's match!!! Kerry and Randy nod angrily, clearly already prepared for this little twist. The only question left is what kind of monster the Phenomenons will have hired to tip the match to their advantage. BILL HEWSON: If ROBOSAURUS is their partner, so help me … ERIC CAMERON: Ladies and gentlemen, we give to you our partner in tonight's extravaganza! Making his professional wrestling DEBUT, weighing in at ONE hundred FORTY-seven pounds, he is the SCOURGE of shoplifters, the REIGNING WORLD WAR II CHAMPION OF THE WORLD … JERRY … “POPS” … KOHLER!!!!!!!!!!!!! JACK JONES: That's it, I'm quitting. Gustav actually falls over laughing as Jerry Kohler, the ancient former referee and current social media spokesperson for the Phenomenons, emerges carefully from the backstage area, sidestepping the destruction that can be seen everywhere. He wears only a pair of Chuck Taylor's and old school wrestling tights with a referee's black and white pattern on them. The tiny, elderly man flexes feebly from between the legs of ROBOSAURUS and begins stalking towards the ring. Even at the elderly shuffling pace he's traveling at, he easily catches and passes Pete the PG Tips Chimp, who is still slowly making his way to ringside with the bag of championship belts. Eric tosses the microphone out of the ring and he and Shane both stick their tongues out at the A-Team, but Kerry and Randy waste no time launching themselves at the Phenomenons, beginning the brawl. DING DING DING!
