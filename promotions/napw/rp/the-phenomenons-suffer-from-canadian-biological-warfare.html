The Phenomenons Suffer From Canadian Biological Warfare

c. May 2014

Eric Cameron & Shane Stone — the Phenomenons

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Phenomenons Suffer From Canadian Biological Warfare [Scratch.] [Scratch.] [Scratch scratch scratch scratch scratch.] Eric: “I can’t believe they did this to us …” Shane: “They’re Canadians, Eric. I can’t believe it took them this long!” [Shane Stone and Eric Cameron, your Phenomenal Heroes, are absolutely miserable, ladies and gentlemen. And why are they so miserable?] Toby: “I’m pretty sure you got lice from that homeless idiot.” [Shane and Eric are dressed absolutely ridiculously in giant, Eskimo-style parkas, lying in the grass in front of what looks to be a lop-sided igloo made out of bricks. Behind their “igloo,” it’s pretty obvious where the bricks came from, as the skeleton of the former house can still be seen. Lying in the grass, sweating profusely, they scratch, itch, wipe sweat, and repeat, all while groaning in abject misery. Eric continues to wear the crown he stole from a homeless person a week ago.] [Toby Jenkins, being the only competent one of the group, is dressed in a Hazmat suit and is slapping a bottle of lice shampoo into his palm.] Eric: “Toby, I appreciate your attempts to diffuse the situation, but it’s not needed. We ALL know what has transpired here. Canada-Land has declared war on us for what we did to the former King of Canada-Land! Unable to defeat us physically, and knowing the terror we’re about to unleash upon all of New Albertha Pro-Wrestling, they’ve stooped to such a low-down, dirty place as biological weaponry. And oh, it hurts!!!” [Eric wallows in the grass miserably, scratching at his head around the crown.] Toby: “If you’d just use this shampoo …” Shane: “NEVER! You bought that here … in Canada-Land! There’s no telling what kind of dastardly attack could be within it! Get it away from our new temporary headquarters, Toby! Begone!” Toby: “Could you guys at least take off the damn parkas? It’s MAY. The average temperature in Alberta is 70!” Eric: “Don’t play temperature games with us, Toby! That could be anything in Celsius!” Toby: “No, that would be 21 in Celsius.” Eric: “Exactly! 21 is WELL below freezing! These Canadians sure would like to trick us with this unseasonably warm spell into foregoing our defenses … they’d just love to have us freeze to death up here, so they can giggle and go “oh look, the Americans can’t handle a Canadian summer, eh?” Well it’s not going to happen, Toby! Not to us! NOT TO THE PHENOMENONS!!!!” Shane: “Eric! I just thought of something.” Eric: “Really!? What?” Shane: “Aren’t you the King of Canada-Land now?” Eric: “I have the crown, don’t I?” Shane: “So can’t you just order Canada-Land to stop launching biological warfare against us?” Eric: “Good point! Trailer Park Luchador, I have a decree!” [The Trailer Park Luchador scampers out of the brick igloo, in a lighter winter jacket, but with both his luchador mask and John Deer cap absolutely drenched in sweat. He quickly produces a notebook and nods to Eric.] Eric: “Dear minions of Canada-Land. Immediately cease and desist your horrendous biological warfare against us, the Phenomenons. Should you not comply immediately, I will dissolve Parliament or something else like that that kings do. In return, I will not order a nuclear attack against Greenland. Now go, Luchador … twitter that to all of Canada!” [Shane and Eric sit in the grass of their lawn, scratching uncomfortably at their heads and groins. Occassionally they check their watch.] Shane: “Shouldn’t they have stopped by now?” Eric: “I really thought that would work.” Shane: “OH ROD. What if this never stops!?!?!” [Oblivious to the Phenomenons, Pete the PG Tips Chimp waddles out of the igloo, picking lice out of his own fur and eating them.] Eric: “Wait, Shane. If my decree as King of Canada-Land didn’t make this stop, don’t you see … there’s only one possible explanation.” Shane: “What’s that, Eric?” Eric: “Canada didn’t do this to us.” [Eric glares dramatically into the GoPro camera.] Shane: “But … if not the dastardly Canadians, who could have done this to us?” Eric: “Don’t you see, Shane, it all makes sense now. Who would benefit from attempted mutiliation, destruction, and possible murder of the Phenomenons today, May 20th, just eight days prior to New Albertha Pro Wrestling’s MAYDAY event? I ask you, WHO!?” Shane: “Surely not the Crimson Mask!” Eric: “No, no, I considered him, but immediately discarded the idea. No, only two people could possibly benefit from such chicanery.” Shane: “Gasp, you can’t mean …” Eric: “THOSE THAT WOULD INFLICT THREE, SHANE!!!” Shane: “NO! He gave us candy! Really bad tasting candy, but candy!” Eric: “I’ve told you a million times, Shane, we can’t trust people just because they give us candy.” Shane: “And we will always disagree on that subject.” Eric: “But don’t you see, Shane: just hours prior to this debilitating attack perpetrated against the Phenomenons, what happened?” Shane: “You took a crown from a homeless man with a bird living in his beard.” Eric: “No. We received a friendly basket of not-candy from the Time Travelling, Intergalactic Lord of all Funk and Love.” Shane: “Hehehe.” Eric: “But don’t you see! That was not just some innocent basket of not-candy, Shane. That was a ticking time bomb of biological warfare. That’s why the not-candy tasted so horrible!” Shane: “But why? How?! Why would someone with such a cool nickname be so completely and utterly horrible to us?” Eric: “It’s finally all clicked, Shane. It wasn’t Lambert at all. Lambert Haniel has far too cool of a nickname to ever be such a dick. And while I barely understood half of what he said when we watched his interview, the parts that I did understand sounded cool. No, Lambert started this innocently, as a gesture of good will and obsequiousness to the greatest tag team in the universe … the Phenomenons.” Shane: “But I thought you said …” Eric: “Shane, don’t you know who his tag team partner is?” Shane: “Not a clue, actually.” Eric: “There! Don’t you see?! Remember when we were binge-watching Netflix the other day after we stole the internet from the Canadians? The greatest trick the devil ever pulled was convincing the world he didn’t exist! Or rather … SHE doesn’t exist!” Shane: “GASP!!!” Eric: “Oh that’s right, Shane! The Devil has infiltrated Lambert’s home and his very own tag team! The Devil is a member of Those That Would Inflict Three! Shane … Lambert’s very tag team partner … it’s a WOMAN!!!!!” Shane: “NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” Eric: “It’s just like when Keyser Soze stopped walking with the limp and everything became clear in an instant! The devil in Those That Would Inflict Three … our true enemy … is the one hiding in the shadows, playing mind games with us! She takes the name Nobody so that she can barely even be noticed … but we’ve noticed you, Nobody!” Shane: “I hate women!!!” Eric: “Oh yes, Nobody. You didn’t think we’d figure out your plan, but we know everything now. Lambert Haniel tried to be our friend. He saw who we were and he liked us, and you couldn’t handle it! He reached out to us with a basket of candy and a kind letter inviting us to shake trees together!” Shane: “I LOVE shaking trees!” Eric: “But then Nobody saw her chance. She replaced all of the delicious candy that Lambert purchased for us, replacing it with foul-tasting not-candy. And she poisoned it all with this horrific biological attack. Shane, Pete, Luchador, Toby … I give to you the true mastermind of everything that has befallen us this week. I give to you NOBODY!” Shane: “I can’t believe Nobody attacked us with biological weapons!” [Toby Jenkins has been sitting leaned against the Phenomenal Van, beating his head against the side, for quite a while now. Finally, he looks over at them.] Toby: “Couldn’t you guys at least blame this on Jason Richards and Johnny Ajax? You haven’t even acknowledged them once yet.” [Shane looks to be in heaven as Pete the PG Tips Chimp has begun picking the lice out of his hair and munching on them. Eric simply waves a dismissive hand at Toby while he continues scratching furiously at his own head.] Eric: “Don’t be ridiculous. Boring people don’t hatch ingenious plans like this. And those guys are boar-RING.” Toby: “I tried …” Eric: “So listen here, Nobody! We know what you’ve done to us. We know what you’ve done to Lambert, playing the innocent little quiet partner while manipulating him and ruining his gifts behind the scenes! And it’s quite possible you’ve even been intercepting the video interviews of Jason Richards and Johnny Ajax before they could reach the airwaves, destroying them! Maybe they’re not boring! Maybe they’re really cool and funny and interesting and not just some little dorky nerds touching themselves to chain wrestling! But we’ll never know because of you! You, the woman who has caused such pain!!!” [Shane Stone is shaking uncontrollably with rage, his face bright read, having now decided that Nobody is the true enemy.] Eric: “So here’s what we’re going to do! May 28th! MAYDAY! I’m going to go kick crazy on you, and flip around, and stuff! And Shane’s probably going to stay away from you, because he’s in a really bad place right now when it comes to ladies! And we’re probably going to win, but that’s not what’s important! What’s important is that your reign of terror must end! We’re going to save New Albertha Pro Wrestling from you! YOU MUST BE STOPPED!!!!” Toby: “It’s their first match!!!” Eric: “Oh. Well, then we’re going to save Lambert from your evil clutches! And he’ll join the Phenomenal Army and live in our igloo with us and help us fight Canadians and free animals and stuff!” Shane: “I HATE NOBODY SO MUCH!!!!!!!!!!!” [Eric starts dragging his head across the grass, using the ground to itch it, while still going on a diatribe.] Eric: “And that’s just the beginning! Because after we’re done with Nobody, you’re next, New Albertha Pro Wrestling! We’ll fight poor Nazis! We’ll fight crazy guys in masks! We’ll fight Mr. T and that punny doctor Frank! We’re here to go to war with you, Canada-Land! This is an invasion!” [Eric stops dragging his head and looks into the camera intensely. It lacks the intended effect, though, as he’s still bent over with his head on the ground, as Shane shakes with rage while a chimpanzee picks lice out of his hair.] Eric: “It’s time you meet the Phenomenons, Canada! The war has just begun … and it will be waged … on the backs of camels!!!!!” [Scratch, scratch, scratch. The Phenomenons are coming, NAPW. And there’s one thing that’s guaranteed: Everyone in NAPW will end up with lice before MAYDAY is over with. Cuz once that stuff gets in the building, you’re pretty much done. Get some lice shampoo. You’ve been warned!]
