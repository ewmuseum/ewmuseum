Phenoms Return

c. Apr 2014

Eric Cameron & Shane Stone — the Phenomenons

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A long time ago, in a strange land far, far away (Chicago) …] [The match is over, and two men – nay, two heroes – lounge about in their private dressing room, gloating over a battle well fought. The largest of the two men, dressed in a gigantic pair of cargo pants and a t-shirt with his and his partner’s faces airbrushed onto it, reclines across the back of two giant camels, both of which seem to be eating out of a giant bushel of Skittles being held up by a redneck in a luchador mask with a green John Deere cap over it, his mullet clearly flowing out of the back. The other man, wearing wrestling tights that look more like a wetsuit, a cape, and a Burger King crown, is fencing a polar bear with a giant metal stick.] [The entire scene is pure, ridiculous, anarchy. They are Shane Stone and Eric Cameron, the Phenomenons, in the height of their triumph and revelry. It was a long time ago.] Shane: “Eric … this is the life. We do what we want, we’re the best tag team in the whole darn universe, the Bum Boozler hasn’t tried to violate me in at least six months … I don’t think I’ve ever been this happy before!” Eric: “I know what you mean! I was just telling Francis the Skunk the same thing.” [Eric Cameron shakes the burlap sack that at least once contained the deceased remnants of a pet skunk. Suddenly, Shane sits up, now sitting between the two humps of the closet camel.] Shane: “Eric, I need you to promise me something.” Eric: “Anything!” Shane: “I mean it!” Eric: “I do too!” Shane: “Don’t ever let anything change this! We have to stay this way forever … the Phenomenons! Forever!! Promise me!!!!” Eric: “Nothing will ever make us stop being the Phenomenons!” [Eric runs over and pinky swears with Shane Stone.] [Bubble-y sounds take over as the dream scene fades away. Years go zooming past us, as we now find a man that looks curiously like Eric Cameron waking up from a deep sleep, sitting up with his eyes wide. His ring attire is long gone, his hair unkempt, and a massive, quite frankly ridiculous red beard over his face with the first specks of grey showing up.] Eric: “Shane … NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” [Eric clenches his fists and shakes them for dramatic effect while screaming into the heavens in misery as he finally recalls this long ago scene.] Eric: “I failed you, Shane! But I will save you! We will be the Phenomenons again!!” [Six months later.] [Eric Cameron paces across a dim, nearly empty room, with bare, concrete walls. On several shelves there are old spaghetti sauce bottles filled with a gold liquid. He’s dressed all in black, with a black skip cap sitting just over his eyebrows.] [In front of him, tied to a chair, is the figure of a man with a burlap sack over his head.] Eric: “And so you see, that is what has brought us to today. I failed, Toby. Shane made me promise … he made me swear that we’d be the Phenomenons forever! Yet I let him be hoodwinked and brainwashed by that … that … jezzebell! I let him quit wrestling, when I knew that’s not what he wanted! I let the Phenomenons … DIE!!!!” “MPHHGHGGHHH!!!!!!” Eric: “Oh, right. Sorry, Toby.” [He quickly jogs over to his kidnapped victim, rips the bag off his head, and un-gags him.] Toby: “What in the … why … you have to stop kidnapping me!!! When will this madness end?!?” Eric: “Toby! This is no way for us to reunite. We should be tearfully embracing!” [Eric immediately envelops the nervous, smaller man in a giant hug.] Toby: “Why? WHY!?!” Eric: “I need a videographer. D’uh. Trust me, where this journey is going to end up, there won’t be cameras, so I need someone to document our travels for our adoring public. Besides, did you really have anything better to do?” Toby: “I … well, no, actually. You’ve pretty well ruined my entire life over the time I’ve known you. My wife’s gone. My kids won’t talk to me anymore. Shock and surprise, the insurance company refused to pay out when you blew up my damn house after staging a six team Venetian Home Rumble brawl in it without telling me. It pretty much emptied me out the FIRST TIME you destroyed my house with that damn skunk.” Eric: “So you’re in?” Toby: “Fuck it, I’ve literally got nothing else to do with my life. And at this point it’s probably safer to be willingly with you than dragged in to whatever insanity you have planned without warning.” Eric: “YES!” [Eric Cameron unties Toby Jenkins, former wrestling commentator who the Phenomenons adopted as their best friend many a year ago, whether he liked it or not. He didn’t.] Toby: “So, where in the hell are we?” Eric: “Oh, in Shane’s basement. I’ve been down here for six months.” Toby: “How in the hell haven’t you been caught yet?” Eric: “Shane’s afraid of the dark. D’uh. He’d never go into a basement.” Toby: “But … you’re afraid of the dark, too!” Eric: “And that’s why the lights are on.” Toby: “But … I don’t …” Eric: “Look, its best that you don’t ask a lot of questions about this. It’s been a long time down here, I’m living on cold hot pockets – cold pockets? – and I might be going crazy. So let’s just move this along.” Toby: “Going?” Eric: “So anyway, right this very moment, Shane Stone, my lifelong best buddy, is being bludgeoned mercilessly directly above us with … blech … unholy matrimony and boring, mind-numbing irrelevance in the suburbs.” Toby: “That’s something normal people tend to like.” Eric: “We’re hardly normal. We’re phenomenal. And it goes against everything that makes us Phenomenons! We don’t get married and settle down in the suburbs … the only time we go to the suburbs is to wrestle in random people’s living rooms!” Toby: “Whatever you say.” Eric: “So this is my gameplan. I’ve devised an incredibly complicated scheme. I intend to kidnap my bestie Shane from the evil clutches of this she-devil, without her ever knowing. I’ve developed an intricate stand-in to take his place. If all goes according to plan, we’ll be off destroying tag teams before she ever even realizes he’s gone.” [Eric nods over at an inflatable woman wearing a black Phenomenons T-shirt with a “Hi, My Name is Shane Stone” sticker on it.] Toby: “Phenomenal plan.” Eric: “They don’t call us the Phenomenons for nothin’, Toby!” Toby: “Because you’re kind of egotistical?” Eric: “No, because Shane looks kind of like John Travolta, and I have telekinesis.” Toby: “Wait, this whole time you’ve been calling yourselves the Phenomenons because you watched some crappy 90s movie and now you think you have mind powers!?” Eric: “Know that I have telekinesis. Know it.” Toby: “15 years I’ve been putting up with you. I didn’t think you could keep surprising me. But you do. Every time.” Eric: “Now listen, Toby, you have a vital part to play in this rescue operation. Every aspect of this has to go off with precision, or it has no chance at success. Now listen …” [Eric Cameron leans in to Toby Jenkins, whispering his malevolent plans.] [Several hours later …] [Shane Stone opens the front door to his quiet, suburban home, stepping out into the cool morning day with a miserable look on his face. He’s dressed in a very un-phenomenal plain gray suit with a black tie. In the background, his wife is yelling at him.] Jessica Stone: “Do NOT forget the groceries again! I swear, it’s like living with a child!” Shane: “Yes dear.” Jessica: “And you’d better get my Tootsie-pie her dog food!!!” Shane: “Yes dear.” Jessica: “And pick up dinner!” Shane: “Yes d—“ [Eric Cameron levels Shane Stone over the head with a giant cane. Shane collapses in a heap, holding his bloody head.] Shane: “What the – Eric? Oh, oh no, I can’t --!!!” Eric: “Hi, buddy!” [Eric hits him over the head another four or five times until Shane stops screaming.] Eric: “Quick, Toby, the door!” [Toby Jenkins runs over and eases the door shut.] Toby: “This was your brilliant, intricate plan? Bludgeon him over the head repeatedly and close the door?!?” Eric: “Well, it worked, didn’t it?! Help me drag him!” [A large van suddenly screeches to a halt outside of Shane Stone’s house. The driver of the van is wearing a black skip cap with eye and mouth holes pulled down over his face, with a John Deere cap sitting over it. In the passenger seat, a chimpanzee is wearing a similar child’s version of the ski cap. Being horrible at stealth, they honk the horn to let Eric know they’ve arrived.] [Switching into stealth mode, Eric Cameron pulls down his own ski mask, and Toby clumsily follows suit. Slowly, the two men drag Shane Stone to the van – which, of course, is brightly colored with “THE PHENOMENONS!!!!!” written across it, and their huge faces grinning idiotically underneath. A small horse trailer behind it has two camels munching on something. Not exactly covert.] [In the van, Shane Stone is quickly tied to a chair in the center of the van, a dirty sock shoved in his mouth, and the burlap sack from Toby’s own kidnapping thrown over his head. Just in time, as he starts groaning and rocking.] Eric: “Trailer Park Luchador, drive!” TPL: “Shhh! I thought we were undercover!” [Anywho, they drive off.] Toby: “Wait, Eric, where exactly are we going? You said it was somewhere far away without cameras … do you have a cabin in the woods or something?” Eric: “Oh, better than that, Toby. We’re going completely off the grid, where his wife will never be able to find us. We’re going to the one place in the world where Shane Stone will have no choice but to confront who he really is, and return to being my best buddy, the hero of trillions of fans around the globe.” [Eric pauses for effect.] Eric: “We’re going to Canada.” [In the back of the van, Shane Stone lets loose a blood curdling scream at the possibility of being smuggled into Canada.] Toby: “I’m pretty sure they have cameras in Canada …” Eric: “Well, they don’t, so I bet you feel stupid now.” [Eric turns around to his old partner, grinning happily.] Eric: “Don’t worry, buddy. I know this is breaking out the big guns, but it has to be done. I have to get you back to normal. And this is the only way. We’re going to a place called Albertha. I heard Canada just got their very own wrestling promotion, after their President finally got a television, the first in all of Canada. And we’re going there. Shane, we’re joining New Alberta Professional Wrestling …… in CANADA.” Shane: ““MPHHGHGGHHHOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!” [Back at the house …] [Jessica Stone eases open the front door, finally bothering to check out the sound of the commotion outside. She cinches her fluffy pink bathrobe around her to keep out the morning air. Lying on the ground outside of the house is the blow up doll in the Phenomenons T-shirt. It seems that Eric poured a bit of ketchup on its head, and there’s a tape recorder a foot away repeating “I think I need to go to the hospital” every few seconds, very clearly a poor attempt by Eric to mimic Shane.] Jessica: “Oh God damnit, it’s started again ...” [End.]
