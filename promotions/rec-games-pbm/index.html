The Usenet newsgroup for the wider play-by-mail hobby, hosting fantasy wrestling as one genre alongside war games, space operas, and gladiator sims — and the earliest attested home for the hobby's own invented-roster wrestling leagues, from November 1990 onward. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

rec.games.pbm was the wider play-by-mail hobby's own newsgroup — a shared room for war games, space operas, gladiator sims, and every other engine the hobby ran under mail or e-mail, with fantasy wrestling as one genre among the rest rather than a subject of its own. Its earliest attested activity on record runs from 1988; no creation record for the group itself survives in this project's holdings.

The earliest invented-roster wrestling league this record attests announced itself here: the World Electronic Wrestling Federation's founding post of 29 November 1990. The Nippon Electronic Wrestling Association followed in September 1991, naming the WeWF directly as its own inspiration. By April 1992 a separate design was already circulating: one poster sought alpha testers for a wrestling PBM of his own, its rules — in his own words — “in development since, oh about 1989-ish,” predating even the WeWF's own founding post; no title, ruleset or later trace of the game survives in this record. The WeWF's own ruleset kept spreading regardless, past its own founder's hands — by August 1992 a second operator was running his own e-mail wrestling game “under the old WeWF rules,” recruiting players straight from this same newsgroup.

The hobby's own directory, the PBM List, was posted here from March 1991 by its original compiler and taken over in 1993 by the editor of a separate PBM fanzine, whose own November 1992 issue — a register of a dozen other PBEM games and leagues, none of them wrestling — carried an editor's line repeated across five consecutive issues: “I refuse to publish contact information for any form of PBEM Wrestling.” Wrestling was recognized here as a real, standing genre of the hobby — just not always a welcome one in its own steward's own hands.

The traffic ran both ways with the newsgroup next door. In November 1992 one poster asked here whether a fantasy pro-wrestling PBEM game was even possible, naming rec.sport.pro-wrestling outright as the place that had turned the idea away: “this is unwelcome in the rec.sports.pro-wrestling group... hope to hear from fans unwelcome in rspw!” — the same exclusion rec.sport.pro-wrestling's own record documents from its side.

A newsgroup has no closing act, and none is recorded for this one. The PBM List was still posting here as late as this project's own walk of the group reaches, in 2003; sixty-two editions of the List across 1991–2002, and a steady scatter of further wrestling recruitment posts, remain uncatalogued in the collection's own desk paperwork, waiting on a future pass.
