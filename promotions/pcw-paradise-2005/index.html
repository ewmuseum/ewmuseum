Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Paradise City/Championship Wrestling's own founding F.A.Q., “Return to Paradise!”, posted 18 May 2005, narrates its own promotion as the second act of a backyard operation: “Tombstone” Timothy Jeter, an independent wrestler turned Powerball millionaire, built Back Yard Championship Wrestling on his own hillside estate in Paradise, California, drawing a couple of local names and one national star to an open-air arena that topped out at 954 tickets sold before talent trouble brought it down.

Two and a half years and a stint running a bottling plant later, Jeter returned under the Paradise City/Championship Wrestling banner, promising a back-to-basics promotion built on wrestling rather than reputation — only for legal trouble over hired talent to shut it down again for two years. The 2005 F.A.Q. announces its own third opening under the same PCW name and staff: Jeter as owner, Riley Brooks as head referee, Christina Araya and Corey Levinn on commentary, Trent Munson on backup announcing, and Twist and Mojo behind the scenes on backstage segments. Its own championship, the J-Paradise Championship, is named for the promotion's own home turf.

No further document under this name survives; whether this third opening ever ran a card isn't in the surviving record.
