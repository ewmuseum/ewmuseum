PCW will be running on a bi-weekly schedule with a smaller sized roster and hosted in-character flash packets.Occasional matches/match-highlights will also be included in flash packets. Focus will be on character and ECW-style action. (High work rate and hardcore.) Good place to try a new character! ------ IN CHARACTER ------ On November 15th of 1997 a young independent wrestler, "Tombstone" Timothy Jeter, bought himself a birthday gift. A Powerball ticket. Three days later he was the world's newest millionaire. With the largest Powerball jackpot ever to this day, Timothy moved south from Oregon to Paradise, California. There he bought a large chunk of land in the hills. Now living in a mansion he decided to do something he'd always dreamed of. Start his own wrestling federation. Building it in his back yard the stage was set for BYCW. Back Yard Championship Wrestling had the capacity to handle 2,000 people at any one time, though their largest gate was 954 tickets sold. With a column held roof to protect from ill weather the arena was an open air event. The addition of a professional entrance ramp and small video wall gave BYCW just the right amount of glitz to play with the big guys of the California independent circle. This lead to a couple local big names and one national star signing on the dotted line despite the small scale of the operation. Things weren't destined to last though. Management's inability to keep those "name" employees in line and on camera, led to the downfall of the world's biggest back yard wrestling promotion. Two and a half years later and one successful run as owner of a bottling plant for Seven Up Inc. Timothy Jeter is back. Taking a more reasonable path the newly dubbed Paradise City Championship Wrestling, PCW for short, will be all about the basics. Great wrestling. Great talent. And no tolerance for the "Name Game"! If you're looking for a place to make a name for yourself go somewhere else. If you're looking for wrestling's Paradise Island then come on down to the green grass where the girl's are pretty. Paradise City is the home for you. Come quick though because space is limited.
