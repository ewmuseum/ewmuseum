Application ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- KAYFABE STORY ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- On November 15th of 1997 a young independent wrestler, "Tombstone" Timothy Jeter, bought himself a birthday gift. A Powerball ticket. Three days later he was the world's newest millionaire. With the largest Powerball jackpot ever to this day, Timothy moved south from Oregon to Paradise, California. There he bought a large chunk of land in the hills. Now living in a mansion he decided to do something he'd always dreamed of. Start his own wrestling federation. Building it in his back yard the stage was set for BYCW. Back Yard Championship Wrestling had the capacity to handle 2,000 people at any one time, though their largest gate was 954 tickets sold. With a column held roof to protect from ill weather the arena was an open air event. The addition of a professional entrance ramp and small video wall gave BYCW just the right amount of glitz to play with the big guys of the California independent circle. This lead to a couple local big names and one national star signing on the dotted line despite the small scale of the operation. Things weren't destined to last though. Management's inability to keep those "name" employees in line and on camera, led to the downfall of the world's biggest back yard wrestling promotion. Two and a half years later and one successful run as owner of a bottling plant for Seven Up Inc. Timothy Jeter was back, armed with the knowledge of how to run a proper business. Taking a more reasonable path the newly dubbed Paradise City Championship Wrestling, PCW for short, was to be all about the basics. Great wrestling. Great talent. And no tolerance for the "Name Game"! However, running into legal trouble over some of the talent hired, PCW was shut down for two years! Now Timothy has cleared the way to return and is look for talented wrestlers to fill his roster! If you're looking for a place to make a name for yourself go somewhere else. If you're looking for wrestling's own Paradise Island, a place that is purely about the sport of prodessional wrestling, then come on down to the green grass where the girl's are pretty. Paradise City is the home for you! ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- OOC STORY ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- I RP a lot. I do fantasy booking a lot. Neal and Mitch said I should start a fed. I wanted to start a fed. I did. Yay. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- PARADISE CITY STAFF ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- -BRASS Owner and CEO - Timothy Jeter. The one time wrestler hung up his boots once he got rich in order to further his dreams of owning his own successful promotion. Failing originally due to his lack of knowledge in the area of a being an employer Timothy has spent two years learning the ropes in a more typical business world situation. Coming back to wrestling he felt more ready than ever. He knew when to be tow the line and when to be leniant. After the last two years of tough court battles he also knows when something is too good to be true! Cheif of Operation - Open Head of Security - Open Head Referee - Riley Brooks One of three child hood friends, Riley has been by Timothy's side through thick and thin. In the BYCW Riley announced matches with Christina. Chaffing from lack of action he's been actively wrestling since the demise of Timothy's second federation. Loyal until the end though, it wasn't hard to persuade him to come back again! -ON AIR TALENT Lead Announcer - Christina Araya Christina, life long friend of Timothy and Riley, was the first of the three to discover wrestling. Never interested in doing it herself she always found herself giving Riley and Timothy tapes and tapes of new moves she'd seen while watching everything she could get her hands on. Her knowledge of wrestling is nearly peerless and serves her well as lead announcer and main play-by-play person. Quite the tomboy, Christina is 'one of the guys' and often shocks people when she does anything to overtly show her femininety. Secondary Announcer - Corey Levinn Corey is an outsider. The only member of PCW's staff not a former acquaintance of Timothy or an experienced wrestling personality. Corey is still learning the ropes, but taking to it quickly. A bit of a refined gentlemen, his taste in wrestler's in drawn down a strict line. Tasteless 'boors' are frowned upon and people cut of a more expensive and usually 'stiffer' cloth are what he likes, regardless of tactics and fan approval. Disproves of Christina's un-lady like manner. Back Up Announcer - Trent Munson Trent is the cousin of a well known announcer from back South. A pure violence junkie Trent is into anyone who bleeds a lot. A self styled goth he's always quick to jump to the defense of anyone with a darker persona than the norm. Gets along very well with Christina. Backstage Analyst, Host of "Bare Necessities" - Twist Twist got his job mostly by hanging around so God damned much and having such a loud God damned mouth. He's a slacker with more mouth than muscle but enough wit to talk himself out of any situation that doesn't involve him trying to get laid. At that point his pants take over and he usually ends up getting smacked. Or laid. Oh and he doesn't shy away from letting you know he hates you. A LOT. Backstage Interviewer - Mojo When found at the interview area during the very first Back 2 Basics, this mute slacker friend of Twist ended up conducting an interview Twist had been assigned. Microphone dropped in his lap and no idea what he was doing led to an interesting segment to say the least! Given the job full time, he's still silent but at least now ready to meet the challenge! Ring Announcer - Open -REFEREE STAFF Senior Ref(s) - Mack Darrin, Jonesy Sawyer, Henry Logan Mack is a more typical referee, trying to walk a strict line of letting happen what need's to but keeping every "i" dotted and every "t" crossed. Jonesy specilizes in officiating cruiserweight style matchs. He speaks 7 languages and is quick on his feet! Henry Logan is a hard nosed bruiser, who takes no guff from the boys. Gets some heel heat for strictness regarding the rules. Junior Ref(s) - Jack Ching, Felicia Dee Jack Ching is easily he most lenient referee around. He loves to just sit back and watch everyone go at it! Felicia is very sexy woman who likes to show it off. Still learning the ropes, but eager to learn, she takes her job seriously! ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- PCW ARENA SET-UP ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- The PCW Arena where all our Back To Basics currently are held is actually the backyard of Timothy Jeter's sizable property in the hills of Paradise, California. There is a professional grade ring with all black turnbuckles, ring posts and canvas with white ropes and ring apron. The PCW logo is emblazoned in the center of the ring in Paradise blue. Paradise blue ring mats. Old school guard rails surround the ring and end half way up the entrance ramp. Bleachers surround the ring on all four sides, with a capacity to hold 4,000 fans. The entrance ramp is level with the ring apron all the way down the aisle. A high grade entrance is a left over picked up on the cheap by Jeter when he first opened BYCW. It's attached to a large guest house on the side of Jeter's estate. The entrance itself is your typical steel platform. It has a large oval shaped doorway in it's center with huge steel hands wrapped around it and slightly holding it up. There is a sliding door that can be used for a more elaborate entrance. 3 steps lead down from the opening to the actual stage itself. To the left is a small video screen (3x5 feet). ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- RULES ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- I'm not gonna waste a lot of time here. Don't use PCW characters that aren't your own without getting permission and discussing it with me. Don't do anything that couldn't reasonably be done in real life. Dream sequences, sure, just describe it like you'd see in a movie. A whole RP done in your character's fantasies? Sure just act like it's a movie. Which still means you have limits. This isn't a big production. There will not be insane special effects in the promo for a wrestler in a small indy fed. Basically, just be polite and keep it mostly realistic. If you ever have a problem with a match ending don't come to me angry. Calm down, THEN come talk to me. I'll always be willing to discuss things in a manner that's fair to ALL the parties involved in a dispute. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- ROLEPLAYS & STRATS ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- Roleplays will be sent to me. I will then compile them with commentary by Twist and send them out as the bi-weekly show, "Bare Necessities." There are to be NO in-ring segments sent in as material for Bare Necessities. However, there will usually be highlights from one or possibley more matches there. These will be booked based on roleplay turnout. Strats are highly encouraged! If you do not send in strats your character will behave as close to their characterization during application and RPs have shown them to act, however this might occasionally go against something you have planned. So, again, use strats! They can be extremely effective in furthering your development. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- PCW EVENTS ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- BACK TO BASICS B2B is the PCW's main show. Broadcast over the web and over multiple public access stations around the US, and now seen on many small affiliate networks. Including syndication in Japan. This is where angle, inring and backstage segements and most matches take place. Scheduled bi-weekly, the show is usually about 2 hours long give or take. Most matchs will be presented in a highlight reel format, with the more well contested matchs gaining more coverage. BARE NECESSITIES Bare Necessities is where you get to see everyone talking and doing what they do. It's basically our flash packet, only with a twist. In fact THE Twist. Twist hosts Bare Necessities, generally talking smack about everyone. There is also all most always a single match on this show. On rare occasions there will be multiple matches. Either way presented in the form of highlights typically. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- PCW SPECIALS ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- None scheduled yet. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- APPLICATION ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- Remember the scale of this federation. You CAN use a "Big Name" character but don't expect any special treatment for him. We're still a medium production. We still don't/won't have pryo, it is a no go for zoning reasons. PPV vs Super Card is up in the air. We have a cruiser/junior division, the J-Paradise title division. This is open to male AND female characters who weigh 200 or less pounds. There is also a planned tag team division!
