2/1/05 - Evolution

Evolution has aired!

Evolution started with a bang from the Global Arena in Dallas, texas. With Octane just a few weeks away, the Global Roster was out in stride tonight to show just what they're made of. Don't miss one exciting moment of Global's Evolution!
Evolution

Global Wrestling Evolution was a hot show as three new faces made their in ring debuts, and another returned to the octagon after a long absence.

Ken Partridge Jr. made a showing on marvelous matters, but where else would his handsome face appear on the show? Rest assured he had a hand in things elsewhere.

The tag titles were on the line as three hall of famers and another man who has the potential to be such, took to the ring in a great match up.

And of course, who can forget the main event as an unmasked Jesse Jamester battled Christian Zenith, the # 1 contender to KPJ's World title?

Think your ready to Evolve? Then click the button. I dare you, c'mon...I double dare you.



With a few delays Reload has come in gone. Who would show up? Who would walk away champion? Many unexplained Mysteries and new stars are born, Reload was truly that a Reload of Global. To check out the action click the results link in the PPV banner. Global now looks to High Octane in February… What will happen along the way?


Tokyo was set ablaze as Global had its last PPV of the year, SIT was a spectacular event, Fighters trying to last till the end to become the third man to go to Reload to fight for the World title. Who was that man? Could Jesse Jamster repeat last year’s performance and win the whole show? Which Teams came out on top? And what was up with Dinkler? Check out the latest PPV Results to find out.
