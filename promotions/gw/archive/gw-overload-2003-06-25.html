Overload, 25 June 2003

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The electrical currents run heavy through the machinery, blowing away the metal that surrounds the big Overload logo plastered into the screen, as it rushes at us the O lights up and we go through to see Trista Romano dressed in a smart business skirt smiling enthusiastically, the screens behind her on and ready displaying classic and not so classic matches in the background. Trista: Hello everyone and welcome to a very special Overload! This weeks Overload will be a pre-show for the Global Wrestling Pay-Per-View WHIPLASH! We have an action packed night for you, all of it getting you the viewer ready for the non-stop wrestling thrills of Whiplash! To get us started tonight we have Angus the Bull, one of the up and coming superstars in the federation who will be participating in the tag-title match, facing off against Boltz! The screen from a T.V. behind her surges forward and fills up our view with Angus the Bull lifting Boltz into the air and hurling him into one of the eight turnbuckles. Brandi: I really don't think Boltz can take much more of this. Mark: I think he stopped taken much more when he fell unconscious... Angus whips Boltz hard into the ropes and levels him with The Bulldozer! Mark: BULLDOZER! Angus makes an easy pin and stands up for the crowd with his hands above his head, he leaves the arena, after a good warm up for Whiplash. Trista: Not an impressive match... Trista Romano replaces the view of the arena. Trista: But what was impressive were the events that took place between Angus and Salomane only a week ago at Evolution! Lets see that now! Angus jumps up onto the ring apron and leans under the second rope stepping into the ring. Salomane puts out his hand as a technical guy throws him a mic. Salomane leans over and picks it up, holding it in his hand. Angus gets in the face of Salomane, as they start saying things to each other that the camera can’t pick up. Suddenly Angus pushes Salomane as he takes a few steps backwards, Angus raises his right fist to hit him….but stops. Instead he puts out his hand to shake. Salomane smiles and shakes Angus’ hand as they nod at each other. Salomane slowly raises the mic to his mouth. Vation: Angus The Bull has become the first follower of my cause… He is the first of many, alone I may have been fragile and a threat to no one, but I get stronger by the day. Ladies and Gentlemen… You’ve witnessed the birth of Genesis! With that Salomane throws his mic to the floor as “Little Green Men” by Project 86 hits the arena as Angus and Salomane shake hands once again. They leave the ring and walk down the ramp, and go behind the curtain. Trista: With the formation of Genesis you can expect for things to really heat up at the Tag-Title match. Another up a comer well have to watch for is Ice, Ice will be participating in the Cruiser Weight ranking match and to get ready for the match he fought against Venom Alexander... Venom goes up top with a downed Ice, Venom leaps for a big splash but Ice was only playing possum as he rolls out of the way and Venom hits hard bouncing into the air and onto his back, it's just this moment that Ice needs to capitalize and he quickly gets the pin after THE HANDS DOWN. Brandi: Ice is practically possessed, he seems to be in just the right state of mind to take that cruiser weight match at Whiplash to a whole new level. Trista: Recently we received word from former Cruiser Weight champion Roz Blayze from a Mexican Hospital bed. Steven Dinkler: Hello every one. I am Steven Dinkler here with Roz Blayze in Some Mexican Hospital in Mexico City. Hello Mr. Blayze. It hasn’t been very long since you were out of a hospital. Then you fought Tyler Pierce and Lost your coveted title. Roz: First off I would like to thank god that my neck is not broken. My throat was stretched. So I’ll be back in a month. Second. Tyler. Congrats. When I get back though. I'd like my Re-match. SD: Now. Dynamite X and Venom. They Lost to Sean price. Two loses for the Xodus. By the way. What does Xodus mean? Roz: Well. Xodus is a street Drug. It's native to Mexico. It's like a better weed. It makes you feel a real good feeling. SD: Well. So far the TUE has two up on the Xodus. What do you have to say about that? Roz: They are some of the best talent in the GW. Deacon. Sean and Tyler. Great Guys. But now the Xodus has a Plan. It's not something I can tell you. SD: What’s it like not wrestling. Roz: I hate it. I really do hate it. I know that I’m going to be out for a month. I wont be able to see my kid for a little while. I can’t really move my neck and wont be able to for the next two weeks. So it sucks. SD: Well. This is about all the time these people will allow me. Thank you Mr. Blayze for your time. Roz: Your welcome. SD: This is Steven Dinkler for GW signing off. Trista: You can expect that match down the road I'm sure, speaking of T.U.E. they have been jaw dropping they moment they entered with Deacon Holmes completely obliterating the hearse and knocking Leviathan out in the process and Shawn and Tyler set to capture tag-team gold at the Pay-Per-View. Another match at the Pay-Per-View to see is Derek Frost vs Salaok, this match should be full of hate due to the old feuds between these two and Salaok's responsibility for kidnapping Cindy. Derek has a lot he wants to prove and a lot he wants to do, in the end though can he take down one of GW's biggest legends? It'll be a night to remember, and don’t forget the rematch that had been in the making for a month, Sage Sabbot versus Brick! In The Pit for the World Heavyweight Title! We leave you tonight with a match between Trey Reed and a member of T.U.E. Tyler Pierce! Tyler ducks under a diving clothesline and bounces of the ropes gaining momentum in an attempt to connect with a flipping front drop kick but midway through the flip Trey drops him in an impressive powerbomb that he hooks into and rolls Tyler into a Boston Crab. The ref checks for the tap out, but Tyler manages to drag himself to the ropes despite it costing him. Trey doesn't waist time and leaps onto the top rope above Tyler. Brandi: The Last Action hero just spring boarded off into a leg drop across the back of Tyler's neck! Mark: Even I have to admit that was brutal! Tyler is clutching the back of his neck and Trey seems satisfied, he starts dragging him to the center of the ring, he pulls him to his feet and delivers a quick release snap suplex tossing him towards a turnbuckle, he quickly runs along the ropes onto the turnbuckle and comes off with a 450 splash, he goes for the pin. Mark: 1.. 2.. Kick out by Pierce! Brandi: Trey is impressed at this and takes a second, to stand above Pierce a little awestruck at how he kicked out of that, but business soon takes over and he brings him to the center of the ring where he pulls him up into a float over belly to belly suplex he holds on though and pulls him to his feet with a huge double underhook suplex! Trey in true Action Hero fashion goes to the farthest turnbuckle and perches there as Tyler slowly comes to his feet and turns around, Trey leaps and the fans rise to there feet! Brandi: MUTUAL SLUMP! And there’s the pin! 1.. 2.. 3!!!! Mark: NOOO!!! Brian West: Ladies and Gentlemen! Your winner via Pin Fall! Trey Reed!!! The Overload them blast out as Trey Reed is celebrating in the ring and Tyler Pierce walks to the back... The screen split and We see Judgment standing in a part of the arena waiting a small TV. He has his hand on his chin and a sick grin on his face. The scene fades to the Overload logo… Then to static.
