"Canadian Icon" Jean Claude Dafoe

The Canadian Icon... A person who proved every one WRONG! Dafoe would accomplish things in GW that the critics said he could never do...

Dafoe would enter the GW as the Canadian Gothic... A hated man.. his feud with Hans Kruger, one that has never really ended and one that made both men superstars in Global...

Dafoe would return from a injury to side with long time rival Hans Kruger right before Kruger retired

Later even, Dafoe would also  team in a unsteady alliance with fellow Canadian. Chris Camero to form the Canadian Icons, gaining the World tag team titles.  Also feuding with his own partner at times.
Dafoe left for the love of his soon to be wife with the love and respect from the fans... 

Gone forever... Who knows.. But Dafoe will always be remembered in Global.

Memorable Feuds:  Vs Hans Kruger Vs Chris Camero Vs Terminator

Accomplishments: First ever Grand Slam Champion!!!

Words from his peers: 

When Dafoe showed up at a PPV [I forget the one at the time] He had a altercation with Hans Kruger. I then saw something, It took a bit But eventually i signed Dafoe to a contract and when he and Kruger clashed it was Magic. Many said Dafoe would never be world champion, but he proved them all wrong. the man had the talent and just needed the place and chance to shine. when he did  he damn near blinded everyone.   - Bubba McCoy -
