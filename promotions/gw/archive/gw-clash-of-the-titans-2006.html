They had to act normal at the moment, not give into the panic. Jake Hix: “Hey, that’s enough!” Xavier Black: “That’s not enough! Jake grabbed his brother around the throat and lifted him up. Xavier slammed an elbow into the side of Jake’s head, but the slam into the solid wall forced all the air out of his lungs. Jake Hix: “Then when I went and saw her…the doctor told me that she was pregnant. I lost more than you’ve ever had Xavier. I’ve lost every woman I’ve ever loved. So suck it up, and go out there and do what has to be done.” Jake turned and head to the door. He pulled it open, then felt the feelings he had under control try to escape the cage he had locked them inside of ever since he heard the news. He didn’t turn around, but his words were filled with all his own broken dreams and fractured fantasies of any happiness. Jake Hix: “Don’t ever think you have an exclusive hold on pain Xavier.
