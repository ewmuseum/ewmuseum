The GW Rundown vol.1The GW Rundown

The time has finally come. With everything we have going on in front of and behind the cameras in Global Wrestling it's high time we had a voice to step back from it all... take a good look... forget all the politically correct BS and cut to the damn chase to let everyone know what's what in GW. Well... Here it is! The Global Wrestling Rundown has finally arrived!

And as for me? I'm the one uncorrupted, unbeholden voice in this place that has no other agenda, but to tell the truth and tell it like it is. You can just call me the GW Shadow. "Who knows what evil lurks in the heart of men? Well, just like the original Shadow... "The GW Shadow know." .So.... Sit back... relax... but most of all? Strap yourselves in cuz it's gonna be a bumpy ride!!!

History Lesson

Well a Global Wrestling tradition, Master of the Mat, has come and gone. It was build as the premiere event of the year for Global Wrestling and, despite my love of the critique, it lived up to the hype! Without a doubt the show was chocked full of action and surprises at just about every turn. Not the least of which is the return of a former Global Wrestling Word Champion!!! Alright, alright...enough of that... let's get down to brass tacks! Here's all the big points you should take note of:

First Point: From Head Hunters to Dead Meat

When it comes to the tag team to division, to say it needs a little perking up is a serious understatement. So when Arefiev and Zergolov Vergomovz hit the seen needless to say many of the fans who wanted to see a revitalized division were pretty excited to see them come in. Too bad the only way they'll likely get gold now is if they get the gold rails instead of the brass on their coffins. I don't know what the hell these guys were thinking getting mixed up with with the likes of Maverick Reno and TUE, but whatever it was we'll likely get to see it for ourselves when they wind up with their brains beat out!

I understand the idea of wanting to make a big splash on the scene in GW. I understand how tempting it might be to make some quick cash by taking up Reno on his offer of a quick buck for Josh's head on a platter. I can kinda understand the two newbies begging for forgiveness and second chance after failing miserably the first time, only delivering the prone body of a janitor rather than the World Champion. What I can't get my head around... is getting the second chance... having Reno give you back up to get the job done right the next go round, in the form of his own personal TUE security detail... then tricking those men into destroying their bosses own car before driving off flipping them the bird. Ok......... I've heard of the suicidal style of wrestling before, but that goes waaaaaaaaaaay beyond wrestling. These two guys aren't suicidal, they just have a f*@!ing death wish!!! Well folks, as much as you want to see the tag division infused with new talent... Don't bother getting too attached to the Vergomovz. I have a feeling Reno and TUE are going to do a genie from the lamp impression and make their wish come true.

Oh, and speaking of Reno and TUE, that brings me to my next point...

Next Point: Bubba McCoy Behind the 8 Ball

What in the hell is Bubba going to do? The GW owner, through no fault of his own, has walked back into a company that barely even resembles itself when he was put in a coma by "The Nevermore". Something tells me Bubba was probably actually glad about since it meant he walked back into a company where the reign of terror of Saloak and his buddies had finally been broken. That is until he noticed that Maverick Reno, the GW "savior", had had jumped right past reigns of terror and proceeded to lock this company in a strangle hold. Making matters worst, in her haste to get Maverick into the company gave the man a contract that has allowed him to out manuever the owner of the company himself!

When Bubba tried to toss John Zero and Deacon Holmes out of the arena Reno called in his high priced mouth piece lawyer and got them right back inside. Bubba tried to isolate Reno in a seperate part of the arena where he couldn't cause any harm by giving orders through his newest, cheapest and, dare I say, deepest throat... I mean, mouth piece Christina McCoy...or any of his other assorted atomitons, for that matter. So, what does he do? Reno gets his lawyer to, once again, dig through his overly indulgent contract that basicly let's him call all the shots and puts the cabosh on the isolation idea. It's clear that Maverick Reno, Christina McCoy and TUE have themselves a royal flush. Now the question is can Bubba come up with all the aces it'll take to knock them down a peg?

Big Point: It's a long way from over
Like most GW observers I could have sworn I'd seen the last embers of the Deacon Holmes, Brick war burn out months ago. Well, apparently, all that means is me and most GW observers need some new contacts! These two men have taken great pleasure in pounding eachother's skulls in, in the past and it looks like we're about to take a trip back in time as the war between these two superstars, that has been so damn entertaining in the past, is about to erupt in another volley of violence. It all started a couple of weeks ago when Deac and Brick had to team up to try to make it into the next round of the Master of the Mat.

Needless to say that turned into an unmidigated disaster with Ely Braxton putting his hands on Deacon even after Brick said he wouldn't let it go down. Deac took that hard and the two words. Brick responded, not just by throwing Deacon around a bit...but by producing a pair of lacy panties he claimed belonged to Deacon's chick Rhazia!!! Deacon, as you can imagine go pretty heated about that even though Rhazia said they weren't hers. Oh, before I go on, might I offer up a solution to the mystery of are they hers or not... I say we pull a Johnny Cochran... bring her down to the ring where we can all judge for ourselves and let her try them on... If the thong don't fit, you must acquit! I mean geez...if a little latin booty doesn't make the ratings spike, at least most of us will get a spike of our own.

Anywho... Deacon came to Master of the Mat and got in Brick's face about how he didn't believe him and was just playing games. But you got to wonder who Deacon was trying to convince with his apparent faith in his relationship, because when Brick confirmed Deacon's suspicions that they hadn't slept together....cuz they were a bit too busy to sleep Deac proceeded to try to tear Brick's head off. So... Is Brick tappin' that ass... or is he just tappin' some insecurities? Don't know...but there is one thing I do know. You'd be well advised to stand back cuz this thing is about to blow!!!

Bigger Point: Brrrrrrrrrrrrrr...

It's getting cold in GW and it ain't just you or me. It's not often you honestly get to see the last thing you EVER expected go down right in front of your eyesl, so you know Master of the Mat delivered a special moment...

IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIICE!!!!!!

What in the crap?! Where the hell did he come from after months of laying below the radar?! Why the hell did he come back after being dead silent for so long?! I can't say for sure, but I have some ideas and they're likely the last thing you may have thought about...but we'll talk about those later....

Biggest Point: Josh is The Real Deal

For scores of GW fans "The American Youth" Josh has been only one thing in their eyes for ages... The Quintessential Underdog. Even after winning the World Title he remained doubted and looked upon as a fluke. I mean what else could it mean when a cruiserweight wins the big one except that he's lucky as hell? Well... Josh has answered that about a million times over after his display of will and drive at the pay per view event of the year! Not only did he retain HIS World Championship, but he defeated "The Most Evil (and damn sure one of the toughest bastards walking) Man in Wrestling" Maverick Reno to become Global Wrestling's 2004 Master of the Mat!!! He answered doubts about the good versus lucky argument, but showing he may well be the biggest thing in the business, bar none.

Now what he has to do is clear. Take about 30 seconds to enjoy it and savor it... Then go back to watching his back. And speaking of watching his back might I inquire of the champ... How long are you going to be carrying around dead weight? Remember the "Ides of March" Josh... Look to thy friends.................

The GW Forecast

Evolution 08-09-04
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Standard Match
Trey Reed vs Dagoth Kinslayer

The returning Reed takes on up and comer Dagoth Kinslayer. Will Reed the Professional Athlete be able to fend off and put away the maniacal Sword wielding monster known as Dagoth. Or Will Dagoth make a statement out of Reed?

The Breakdown:

Trey Reed- Returning superstar who had a decent showing in MOTM, but had a little too much ring rust to make the impact he wanted.

Dagoth Kinslayer- He's big. He's bad. A nasty combination for someone who can use them together which, to this point he hasn't been able to.

Winner: Trey Reed. I think this guy has the motivation to come back in and make a splash while Dagoth is way off his game. But who knows. The one advantage any monster has is the ability to wake up and breathe fire at a moments notice....

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tag team challenge match Winners to advance to the PPV for the Vacant GW Tag team titles.
The Flying Vergomovz vs Gonzales / Train vs Jamester/ Roswell

The Vergomovz coming off high praising for there recent efforts now look to face a new challenge in the forms of the teams of Juan Gonzales and Train and Jesse Jamster and Max Roswell. Can the Vergomovz keep their hot streak or will one of these other new teams knock them off their path to glory. Both teams have been paired up before and now are aiming for the Vacant Tag team titles.

The Breakdown:

The Flying Vergomovz- Hella motivated (if not very bright).

J-Gonz / Train- Hmmm... These two seemed to be at odds a while back. Don't know exactly what the deal was, but it don't vote well for them.

Jamester / Roswell- Jesse and his little Acolyte Max are getting their first test. Some times great friends don't always make great partners.... They may need more more seasoning as a team than this allows.

The Winners: The Flying Vergomovz. Assuming they live long enough....

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Platinum Title Contendership Match Weapons Match Juri Hyobanshi vs Vennemen Pendragon

Once again The Psycho Freak Juri Hyobanshi takes on our lord Vennemen Pendragon. This time with No rules and Weapons.

The Breakdown:

Juri Hyobanshi- Needs Ritalin and Prozac.

Pendragon- Needs Lithium and Prozac.

The Winner: Juri. I could almost say flip a coin and pick a nut this thing is so close. However, I think Juri is more than nuts....she's a psycho with a sadistic, mean streak that's going to win her this match.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Standard Match Sage Sabbot vs Brick

In a Long awaited return match. Two former GW World champions face each other. Will history repeat its self or will a new page be written.

The Breakdown:

Sage Sabbot- The Harvester of Tears has been back in GW awhile now and has every motivation to squeeze as many drops (be they tears, sweat or blood) as he can.

Brick: Tough S.O.B. Nuff said.

The Winner: Sage Sabbot. Look for this to be by the skin of his teeth. And not only that, don't be surprised if Brick goes down due to a nasty spider bite by a creature that wants to prove one of the things Brick shouldn't be doing at home is banging someone elses chick.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Main Event Triangle Match International Title Match
Leinad Soma vs Deacon Holmes vs Ken Partridge Jr.

With the recent maneuverings of the Front office and the Mysterious VP of GW. The International title now lays vacant, this will decide who will be the new champion... The Vice Presidents pick... Deacon Holmes. Bubba’s pick... Leinad Soma, and Ken Partridge Jr. smack down in the center. Who will walk away as International Champion at the end of the night?

Leinad Soma- This man is seeing red after being stripped of the title he won just a couple of weeks ago. I'd hate to be anyone standing in his way.

Deacon Holmes: Normally I'd count him as the front runner to take this thing, but somehow, knowing his temper and his mouth... I have a feeling him may wind up looking up at the lights courtesy of a Brick up side the head.

Ken Partridge, Jr.- The former champion that had this title taken from him by Leinad Soma. Yeah, he has a Television Title around his waist, but after Josh retained his World Title AND became Master of the Mat? He needs to take this lest some begin to question whether he's quite as "Elite" as he claims to be.....

The Winner: Leinad Soma. This is going to be a tough match with probably a million near falls, but you can look for the last champion to draw first blood. Figuratively and literally...

Wild, Unsubstantiated Rumors
(so they're probably true...)

Mystery VP
Who the heck is this guy? In a short time he's weilded an awful lot of power and flexed his muscle a time or two. There are a lot of names floating around, but there are only a few likely suspects....

Donovan McCoy... Though the bratty attitude might believe you to think so Christina is not an only child. Christina got her back up by marriage. Bubba might have got his by blood.

Dane Rinnier... I don't claim to know much about this character, but, judging by the way our new "Head of Security" seemed to creep out his daughter Bubba may have decided he'd be a natual choice for the position.

Ice.... That's right, you heard me. After dropping off the face of the Earth for 2 months it looks like Ice has finally reemerged from the shadows bring MOTM to a startling end. Could this former superstar have returned to take a spot fighting his battles with the power of the pen rather than the sword?

Elvis... The word is backstage at the last several events the catering staff has recieved several requests for fried peanut butter and banana samiches... Coincidence? Maybe. Maybe not.


Sex, Lies and Videotape
Ever since the Deacon Holmes, Brick thing resparked itself a little while back, I've heard talk of a very special video clip circulating on wrestling websites featuring (or so it's been said) Rhazia, Deacon's girlfriend, doing one hell of a Paris Hilton impression with... Well, from what I heard who with is unknown. Apparently the clips is quite short and doesn't reveal the the recipient of the....ummmm.... "Fine Job" she's performing in the tape. The only thing that it proves is that the man is black and lends some creedence to the theory that a girl might not want to go back.

On a side note, anyone with this clip I would be greatly obliged if you'd send it my way as I need it for... ahhhhhh... Research! Yeah...that's it... Research.


Bun in the Oven
I have heard reports that Christina McCoy was spotted, doing her best to be incognito, sitting in the waiting room of the Ob/Gyn clinic at the Dallas Medical Center a couple of weeks ago. Maverick Reno has put Bubba McCoy through a hell of a lot of grief over the last few weeks. Could the icing on the cake be fathering his Grandchild?!


Crack in Elite Armor
So it's pretty much been established Josh and Ken Partridge, Jr. are about as close as brothers. But everyone knows there are two things you should never participate in with family... Business and Money. Several reliable sources have told me that Ken Partridge, Jr. sent some very inflamatory remarks about how Josh "wasn't watching his back" in the match KPJ had with Leinad Soma where he dropped the International Title.

Now according to my sources it all blew over rather quickly, but let's get real folks. People say they don't mean the shit they say when they're mad, but actually the always do. It's just they wouldn't have said it under other circumstances. I think KPJ is hiding a little jealousy under those long blonde locks. When I told Josh to look to his "friends" earlier I actually meant Friend in the singular... I also wondered how long The World Champion and 2004 Master of the Mat... arguably the biggest thing in the business now, would continue to carry the remaining elite (i.e. KPJ) around on his shoulders. There's no doubt KPJ has the TV Title to be proud of, but compared to the spotlight shining on his "buddy" Josh right now, his spotlight is just a twinkling star.

How long can the Elite go on like that? I know his name was circulated in the discussion about our mysterious new VP, but a more likely reason for Ice to be back is to mend fences. If there is a rift forming a mutal friend like Ice dropping in might be just the way to put it behind you. But in the end? This is wrestling folks. Power and Money is what it's all about. Friendships? They're a luxury you don't tend to have when you're on top....
