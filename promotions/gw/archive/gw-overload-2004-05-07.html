Jushin is still well rested and is quickly onto the attack of Takahashi. He pounds away at Takahashi with a variety of kicks. Takahashi is overwhelmed and ends up being forced back into the corner. Jushin punches him in the face and then sits him atop the top rope. Jushin climbs up behind him, and delivers a huge Frankensteiner onto Takahashi. Sights: Great move there by Jushin. Jushin quickly moves over and makes the cover on Jetsui. 1… 2… Bishop: Kick out by Jetsui!! Yahooo! Sights: What happened to being non-biased! Bishop: Hell, I threw that rule out the window a long time ago. Jushin shakes his head and grabs Jetsui. He bends over to pick Jetsui up, only to be met with a spray of mist. Sights: Twice in one match! I’ve never seen anyone use the mist twice! Bishop: This guy is a genius!!! Jetsui quickly takes advantage of this situation and begins to go on the offensive. He grabs Jushin and pulls him up to his feet, before delivering a quick palm thrust to Jushin’s throat. Jushin grabs hold of his throat and falls down to the ground struggling to breathe. Takahashi does not slow down the momentum as he runs off the rope and comes back and delivers a modified version of a Rolling Thunder, instead finishing with a leg drop. Jetsui follows up quickly with a pin. 1… 2… Sights: Jushin kicks out at the last second! Bishop: Damn it! You can do it Takahashi! Sights: Why do you want him to win so badly? Bishop: With a nickname like “The Great Nanban” why would you want him to lose? Jetsui does not lose his focus. With Jushin still down he makes his way towards the turnbuckle and begins to ascend. He leaps off at Jushin attempting The Death From Above. But, Jushin moves out of the way at the last second, barely avoiding contact. Sights: Jushin just got out of the way of that one. Jushin quickly begins to crawl his way towards the corner where his brother is. He makes the tag to his big brother at the last second just before Jetsui can stop him from making the tag. Ichiro quickly begins his assault. He targets Takahashi’s midsection as they are sore from his missed 450 Splash. Ichiro delivers several stiff kicks to his stomach before finally locking him into an abdominal stretch. Sights: Good plan of attack here by Ichiro. Bishop: I hate the Souls! *clap, clap, clap clap clap* Ichiro finally is forced to break the hold, but the damage is done as Takahashi falls to the ground gripping his ribs in pain. Ichiro walks over and tags his brother, who immediately begins climbing to the top rope as Ichiro makes his way back to Takahashi. Sights: Uh oh, I think I smell justice coming on. Bishop: I hate when you talk in metaphors. Ichiro lifts Takahashi in a reverse powerbomb position. He lifts him into the air and Jushin leaps off of the top rope, executing a flipping leg drop. Ichiro forces Jetsui’s face into the mat as Jushin connects with the back of his head. Sights: Swift Justice!! Jushin rolls Takahashi over and goes for the pin. 1… 2… 3!!! West: Ladies and gentlemen, Takahashi Jetsui has been eliminated and his team has been removed from the match. We are down to our final two teams. Sights: This is it, the final two! Bishop: And right now I can’t figure out which ones I hate more. Kali slides into the ring to square off against Jushin. However, Ichiro quickly slides back into the ring and it is now two on one in favor of the Souls of the Black Lotus. The referee tries to maintain order, but to no avail. Kali does not back down however, and is quickly reassured as Shade slides into the ring from the outside and stands side by side. The two sisters stand next to one another and the two brothers stand next to one another. Sights: Here we go!! With her words seemingly sparking the action the two groups pair off and begin to exchange blows. Ichiro partners off with Shade while Kali and Jushin stay together. Ichiro quickly begins to gain the advantage and delivers a stiff kick. This sends Shade reeling backwards and she stays lying on the mat. Ichiro goes to advance but in an attempt to maintain order the referee holds onto Ichiro and forces him out of the ring. Ichiro tries to resist as Shade gets up and smiles arrogantly at Ichiro. Meanwhile on the other side of the ring, Jushin has gained the quick advantage and with Kali down on the ground is about to go for The Dark Heart. He stands backwards and executes the first of three moonsaults, nailing Kali. He then quickly gets to his feet and runs, springboarding off the second turnbuckle he nails the second of three moonsaults. He gets back to his feet and begins to climb to the top turnbuckle for the final moonsault of the tri-fecta. Sights: This is it, if he hits this it might be game over Black and White. However, he does not hit it, as Shade quickly grabs him by the pants and pulls him back into the ring. Jushin lands hard on his back and Shade quickly runs over to Kali and places her arm across Jushin. Ichiro tries to get by the referee but the ref does not let him through. Shade quickly rushes over and grabs Ichiro from behind and throws him out of the ring. She then turns the ref’s attention to the pin fall. Sights: No, they might steal one here. 1… 2… 3!! West: Ladies and gentlemen, Jushin Nakazura has been eliminated. Sights: Well, it’s two on one now, and that is a huge advantage for Black and White. Kali and Jushin both lie motionless now as Shade comes to the outside of the ring to find Ichiro. Ichiro however, has already slid back into the ring and is stalking closer to Kali. He rolls Kali off of his brother and the crowd begins to cheer as he makes the cover. Shade has no idea what is going on inside the ring as she is still looking for Ichiro outside the ring. The referee drops to make the count as Shade turns around. 1… 2… 3!!! West: Kali has just been eliminated from the match and we are down to the final two. The next fall will decide who the Number One Contenders are for the Tag Team Titles. Sights: What a brilliant move by Ichiro Nakazura, Shade had no idea what was going on until it was too late. Shade slides into the ring and rushes right after Ichiro as the referee clears both Jushin and Kali from the ring. Shade throws punches, kicks, whatever she can at Ichiro, but still Ichiro manages to side step it all. Finally, Shade throws out a desperation kick, only to be caught by Ichiro. Shade goes for the enziguiri, but Ichiro quickly ducks out of the way sending Shade to her back in pain. Ichiro quickly follows up his advantage and applies a spinning toe hold submission. Shade eventually reaches the ropes and Ichiro is forced to break the hold. Ichiro does not let up however, not wanting to let his rematch against the Consiliu slip away. He grabs Shade by the hair and forces her backwards into the corner and executes an Irish whip across the ring. Shade lands in the far corner and Ichiro comes charging in after attempting a flying elbow. However, Shade pulls the referee in the way, and the referee is knocked out cold taking all the damage from Ichiro’s blow. Bishop: I still don’t know who I hate more!! Sights: Just shut up and call the match then! Ichiro bends down to check on the referee, only to be met with a stiff boot to the face from Shade. Shade grabs Ichiro and forces him back into the ropes to attempt her sister’s maneuver which earlier on eliminated Mike Page. Ichiro manages to land a kick that forces her away. However, he is then grabbed from behind by Kali who is back up on the ring apron. Sights: Kali with the interference here. Bishop: And the ref isn’t even there to see it happening. How beautiful. Sights: I thought you didn’t like Black and White. Bishop: I don’t, but I love the art of cheating. Shade then gets back to her feet and runs and delivers a stiff boot to the groin area of Ichiro. As Kali lets go of Ichiro, this sends him straight to the ground in pain. Shade lifts him back to his feet however, and leave him standing dazed. The two women bounce off the ropes and again with perfect timing and precision execute their finisher, A Lighter Shade. Sights: Illegal move right there, there is no way Kali should have been allowed to help out her sister. Bishop: Ah, but she did, and now she is going to wake the ref up for the pin. Sights: The Souls are about to be robbed here, but there is nothing that can be done. Kali has revived the referee and after A Lighter Shade, here comes the pin. 1… 2… 3!!! Bishop: I love it when the cheaters win!!! West: Ladies and gentlemen Ichiro Nakazura has been eliminated. The winners of this match after a grueling time of 47 minutes and 33 seconds, and Number One Contenders to the GW Tag Team Titles!! Ladies and gentlemen your winners, Black and White!!! I’m really thirsty! Bishop and Sights: Shut up!! ---- The cameras swivel back to where Gunner Addison and Anthony Spivey are located as they each have a big smile spread across their face.
