Hotlist (October 2007)

1 Eryc Morgan: The Dark Flame is now on top of the Global Mountain. Defeating His former ally and stable mate John Adams at Reload for the title. Not to mention his year ending win at SIT. Right now after battling for a year, Eryc Morgan is indeed King of the Mountain.  -
2 John Adams: The Morbid Saint Comes in at Number two. Having lost his world title to Eryc Morgan keeps him from the Number one spot. But Adams had a stellar year and shocked everyone by taking the title from the longest reigning champion of Global, Jake Hix. Can Adams take his place back? We shall see at High Octane.  -
3 Jake Hix: The Big Dawg spent most of 06' at Number one. But now he finds his self at Number three. The Big Dawg is not happy after loosing the title to the likes of John Adams. And now His rival Eryc Morgan has the title he wants. Can Jake Hix knock Adams and Morgan down and take his title back and let everyone know just who's yard it is? We shall see. -
4 Digital Morality. For years everyone knew his skill and now so does Global. Digital is now the Global International Champion. The Puerto Rican native is looking to show he can still go and dominate in the ring. But will he be able to hold on to his newly acquired prize? With a hungry pack of wolves coming after him... Who knows.  -
5 Scott Taylor. Taylor was and apparently still is the last ever AWC Livewire Champion, and he has let everyone know it. Since his arrival in Global he has raised the eyes of the fans. With his victory at Reload against The Paladin Joshua Kosidilo, and having a impressive Undefeated record can anyone take his Livewire belt away?.  -
6 Claudius Augustus: The Emperor of the New Roman Empire has surly made heads turn after beating Psymon and joining the True Saints...but do both of these guys have what it takes to defeat Global's International Champ Digital? -
6 Psymon: The Monster of LOW has defiantly made heads turn with dominating the other LOW alumni's in the Barbwire and flaming tables match...but with a lost to Augustus can he realize his dream of a Global Title? -
7 BloodAngel: has been making waves ever since he lost his Platinum Belt…he has joined SIN…and is also going one on one for the World Title against one Eryc Morgan…but does he have enough to get by the Dark Flame? -
8 Xavier Black: Black now holds the Platinum title, after besting BloodAngel for the title. Much like his Brother Jake Hix, Xavier has started here. Will Black dominate the Platinum Division like he and Pitt have done with the tag division? Or like his Brother did the World title Division? Who can stop Xavier? We wait to find out.  -
9 T.W.C: They are the current Tag Team champions, The Most dominate champions in 2006. But it seams there is cracks in the foundation. Could this be the right time for any team to send them crashing to the ground and take there gold away? -
9 Jocque Noreault: Noreault rose to International Champion by  defeating Dusk and Digital Morality...  Then He lost it to Digital at Reload.  2006 was a bright year for Noreault. One has to wonder if The loss to Digital has had more of a toll on the Canadian Dream then people realize.  -
10 Josh Kosidilo: The Ironman was poised and primed for his chance at the World title, What happened then? Scott Taylor is what happened. The Iron Paladin has had a string of bad luck as of late. He recently lost to Scott Taylor at Reload...but besides that he still is a solid member of True Saints, Now focused on getting livewire off Taylor when he goes for a rematch against Scott again... is it his time to Shine? Or will the Livewire Champion Scott Taylor still reign supreme?  -
11 Brick: Brick is now in a position he has never been in before. behind the desk. He is the New Commish of Global having taken the job from Train. Even here though he has still managed to draw the wrath of people who wish to try and out number him. SIN!  But will they succeed where others have failed. and add Bricks new found power? Life may become a bit unlivable for SIN soon.  -
12 D.U.I.:Brandi has placed out a Tag team challenge. many have joined to step up. But one team has really caught her eye. D.U.I. Have most defiantly answered the call of Arms.. Will T.W.C be able to hold the titles for long? Or will DUI take them for there self's.  -
13 Devlin Rubis: Number 13, to many this would be a bad omen, a sign of Bad luck but Not for Devlin Rubis. Reunited with his Brother John Adams in SIN.  Rubis looks to once again start his rise to the top.  -
14 Jason Ortiz: He was LOW hall of fame... and the fans of this Crackhead never thought they would see him again... boy were they wrong. Since he has been back he has certainly made waves by doing quite well in the LOW match at High Octane... and with his recent win over Tommy Osbourn he is certainly a force to be reckoned with. -
15 Tommy Osbourn: Osbourn has been on quite a streak in Global with a win over Aka... But, after a tough loss at the hands of Jason Ortiz on Fusion, Tommy will have to try and turn his luck around with his chance at the Platinum Title against newcomer Teresa Tomas and champion Xavier Black. Will "The Trip" be able to hold his footing at Night of Champions. - 
16 Teresa Tomas: The Redneck Princess. Teresa looks to be back in top shape and form. Focused on climbing the ladder in Global.  And she just may reach the top. If she can avoid distractions like; Scott Taylor or alcohol. But Jack Slade seams to be guiding her down the right path. It is just a matter of time before she makes it.   -
17 Karma: Not much is known about the man named Karma. Though we know that he is not to fond of Bullies like Atkins. Karma is the Nobel hero it seams. One with the skills to back him up.  How far will Karma go? We shall find out. One thing is for sure. You do not want to be on the Bad side of Karma. -
18 Reginald Atkins: The Brit Twit. Atkins has recently gotten under the skin of Not only Brandi Sights. But Karma as well. Attacking staff members for no reason, other then "Just cause I can." Will this backfire on him, Or will he knock Karma aside? -
19 Jenny Callihan: Hailing from BACW. Jenny is on a Mission to show she is more then a piece of eye candy for the fans to look at and lust over.  So far she seems to be accomplishing that mission. 
 -
20 Damien Diamond: Second generation talent. Seams to have issues with The Adams Family. Will this distract him from a golden chance? He faces Scott Taylor for the AWC LiveWire championship.  -
20 Aka Osualdo: Not too much is known about the Big Man from Africa. His path of Violence is though. Already putting one person out of action with a Broken Jaw.  Tommy Osbourn Managed to get a win but at what cost. His Violence has even seen Brick Ban him from the Arena. What's Next? Who Knows? -

Thoughts: This is the first list of 2007. This is a compilation, Starting from the first Fusion after the December break to current. Who knows who will rise and fall after N-O-C and High Octane.  Allot of new talent has walked though the doors.
