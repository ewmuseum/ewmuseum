The World Championship.

 The title every one wants to hold, but few ever gain.  Justin Marked lays claim as the First ever GWF Champion of the World. Unifying two worlds to forage one. As FWL and UCWF merged into one there were Two champions...  Insane Cain of UCWF and Justin Marks of FWL, and Two top contenders Griven of FWL and Bobby brawler of UCWF..  In a four way match to decide the new champion.. Justin Marks at the end of the night was the one to become the First ever Global  World champion.

 Some of the Memorable champions to have held this title are: Heru Tenshi, The Forever Knight,  Jean Claude Dafoe, Hans Kruger, Ben J Pailthorpe, Jay Robson, Jonathan Davis, Tragic Hero, Salaok, Mani Shigeru, Silent Wind, Sage Sabbot, Maverick Reno, Brick, Deacon Holmes, Akodo Rokku and Ice. 

 Ben J Pailthorpe, Justin Marks, Jean Claude Dafoe, Jonathan Davis, Salaok, and Silent Wind being the only multiple champions to date.   Davis, Marks, Dafoe and BJP with two, Silent Wind with three and Salaok with a record setting FIVE title reigns. 

 Currently Josh is the reigning champion and the Longest Reigning champion in Global History, breaking Hans Kruger's longest rein record of 75 days. Josh 

 With New stars entering Global and Global  going into what some say is a new era. Who the next world champions will be has yet to be decided.  But rest assured the Prestige of the title will always remain in tact, and only the Best the Global  has to offer will claim this title.
