Hall of Fame — Insane Cain (April 2005)

29 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Insane Cain Some call him crazy, Some call him the best at what he does. What ever he is, he left a lasting impression on the face of Global forever. 

Cain always had his own style, High Impact and brutal. This kept him as a Fan favorite all though out his stay in Global, Although he never officially claimed the Global Championship, he held one of its predecessors in the UCWF Championship. Cain was It's last and only World Champion. 
Cain will forever be remembered for his contributions to this federation in the early days both in and out of the ring. Would Global be the same today if not for his presence? Very Unlikely. 

Key Wins:  Defeated Salaok

Words from his peers: 

Insane Cain was just that, Insane. The man was off the wall. But he knew what he was doing. IN the early stages of Global Cain was the man. Very hard to beat. I have no doubt in my mind if he would have stayed long after the Merger, Cain would have held that World title in due time. One of the best in the business with out a doubt . - Bubba McCoy -
