Time: Four minutes and forty three seconds. Advancing: Arefiev Vergomovz. Arefiev Vergomovz vs. El Serpenti. Stunning the world by knocking off the red hot Arefiev Vergomovz, a confident El Serpenti took on his next opponent Ken Partridge Jr. Ken, a master technical Wrestler had been watching the match backstage and quickly went back to work on Serpenti's knees. Utilizing maneuvers such as low dropkicks, Sharpshooter's and single leg Boston crabs Ken had almost pounded Serpenti into position only to see Serpenti roll out of a sharpshooter, as Ken chased after him Serpenti kicked Ken in the face and stunned him. El Serpenti would next face a man that an authority no less than the World Champion, Josh, himself has referred to as the future of GW, and that was Adam Myers. Myers was evenly matched with Serpenti, showing that he was an equally talented Luchador as the two men flew through a series of fast paced offense, opening eyes and drawing the crowd into it. The impartial crowd stood in awe of the spectacle before them, but as this Lucha contest proved to be a stalemate Adam Myers turned to his Japanese training and used his great technical abilities to ground Serpenti. Working on the already wounded knees of Serpenti Myers focused on these until he was able to limit Serpenti to the mat, where he simply out Wrestled him. Hitting El Serpenti with the Cure, Myers made the cover knocking Serpenti out of the match. Time: Seven minutes thirty four seconds. Advancing: Adam Myers. Adam Myers vs. Rich Sherwin. Ladder match. Sherwin started the match off by taking advantage of Myers already worn out condition as he pummeled him with knees, elbows, fists and forearms before whipping him into the corner. Here Myers countered by back body dropping a charging Rich Sherwin over the top rope and out of the ring onto one of the ladders on the outside! Myers leapt at Sherwin with a big Asia Moonsault only to miss as Sherwin rolled away and Myers landed ribcage first on the steel ladder. Sherwin quickly recovered as he retrieved another ladder and went into the ring in search of the Cruiserweight title, those dreams were short lived however, as Adam Myers reentered the ring with a big springboard dropkick that send both Sherwin and Myers down hard. Both men recovered at seemingly equal speed as they began to climb the ladder, at the top they met and battled viscously over possession of the Cruiserweight title lashing out at one another with hard punches. The ladder teetered and with a crash the ladder slipped and sent both men down as they each hit the top rope! The top rope did them no favors and it took each participant a good time to recover, prompting the crowd to break into a seemingly endless chorus of "GW" chants. Finally the two men made their way to their feet, Sherwin attempted a superkick but Adam Myers ducked, stepped behind his fellow GU graduate and drilled Sherwin with the Cure! Turning to the ladder, Myers ascended the steel steps and procured the gleaming Cruiserweight title, holding it high for the entire world to see their new Cruiserweight Champion, "The Prototype" Adam Myers!
