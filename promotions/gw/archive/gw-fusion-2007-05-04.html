---- ---- Opening Doors ---- [The back door of the arena opening is nothing that is surprising- people come and go, go and come, a million times during the event of a wrestling show. It's hard to continue paying attention to the door every time it opens. But there are times when, as an ace reporter like Noi Tsunami, it's good to pay attention. This is one of those times. The door swings open, pulled by a sun-browned hand, which is the first thing to appear through the door. It is shortly followed by the rest of the man, dressed rather simply in jeans, black work-boots, and a short-sleeved, button up Dickies workshirt. His dirty-blonde hair is pulled back into a somewhat messy ponytail, and he carries no official identifying material, and no gym bag, but, to trained eyes, it's point-blank obvious who he is. If Noi Tsunami's eyes were any wider, they would probably roll right out of her head. Suddenly, Noi is on her feet, microphone in hand, trotting across the concrete floor of the arena, in pursuit of The Interview.] Noi: KARMA! Karma wait up! [Karma almost winces as he turns. As one might guess from his very casual and unassuming dress, he was trying to get backstage in a manner that was very inconspicuous, but you can only be so inconspicuous when you haven't been around for more than a month. He turns and offers her a conciliatory smile, as always putting on his kind face.] Karma: Good evening, Noi. How are you tonight? [Noi looks at him, surprised that he can be acting so normal in a moment like this. Then again, she had noticed that a lot of things were momentous to interviewers that really didn't seem too special to other people in the world. It was a gift.] Noi: I'm fine. I hope you've been doing well, this is the first time we've seen you in quite some time. We thought you might have left Global along with Jenny- were you considering it? [Karma looks taken aback by the question. He closes his eyes for a moment before answering, in that same soft voice that he always uses, one that does not beg for attention, but gets it all the same because it is simply so different from the norm of people screaming for attention.] Karma: I'd be lying to say that I hadn't at least considered the idea. Jenny Callihan is someone I consider a very dear friend, and I owe her a lot for the help that she has given me since I first came here. But, there's also the fact that Jenny has other obligations that she needs to take care of. She is one of the few lucky ones of us who has managed to find a promotion to call home, and its situation right now has pulled on her heartstrings... she went back where she was needed. I can understand her decision, even if it saddens me to be here without a tag team partner, and a woman that I greatly respected. [Noi nods. There doesn't seem to be a particularly better response.] Noi: So, I don't see your name on the card tonight. In fact, you're not even coming in with gear or anything... do you have a meeting or something? [Karma nods in assent.] Karma: I have a meeting this evening with some of the Global officials to determine my future in the company. [Noi's eyes widen- things like this don't sound good, NEVER sound good.] Noi: Are they thinking of releasing you? Karma: Not that I know of. I've been on leave. [Noi almost appears to breathe a sigh of relief- it's unprofessional, but a man without an ego is a fresh breath of air in this business.] Noi: On leave. Have you been injured then? Where have you been? What have you been up to? [Karma smiles slightly at the rapid-fire barrage of questions.] Karma: I had a slight injury after my last match, nothing to keep me out as long as I've been out, though. To be honest, I've been taking some time to deal with tying up some issues back home... in Norway. I've also been taking the time to reconsider my position here. Noi: Reconsider? So you're thinking of leaving? [Karma chuckles softly.] Karma: Not all of us have a position in Global, and I realized that as I watched a good deal of people filter away from the company in the past few weeks. I don't believe it's been easy on the management of the company to watch such a thing, nor has it been for me. It's made me stop and wonder exactly what I'm doing here. I've had to evaluate my motives carefully. Noi: And the conclusion? [Noi presses the microphone eagerly towards Karma. He seems as unflappable as ever.] Karma: The conclusion is simply that I will have to redouble my efforts. I was given a momentous opportunity when I signed a contract with Global Wrestling, and I believe that it would be disrespectful to the management of this company if I were to pretend like that opportunity were not even a part of my personal considerations. [Noi almost seems relieved.] Noi: Now, when we saw you here last, you were passing some words with Reginald Atkins. Do you intend to continue pursuing your grudge with him? [Karma's face suddenly becomes rather guarded, and he shrugs slightly.] Karma: I have no respect for Reginald Atkins, nor does he for me. The simple fact of the matter is that we will probably NEVER have respect for one another, and that is acceptable. I will take on what is set before me. If Reginald Atkins is one of those things, then I will deal with that when it comes down the pipe. I don't make it a habit to go looking for trouble, but mark my words that if he does, he will find it if he comes knocking on my door. I don't have any respect for people who victimize those weaker than them. [Noi pauses, almost taken aback by the hardness of his words. But, dead air is dead air, something she was studiously taught to avoid when she was learning the ropes of the business.] Noi: Will we see you back in a Global ring any time soon? [At that, Karma's face finally breaks into a relaxed smile again.] Karma: I have high hopes of that, Noi, high hopes. I'm not done here, in fact, I don't even think I've really begun. I've had some time to readjust my own personal expectations, but now I feel that it's time to get back on track. With any luck, Noi, yes, you will see me in a Global ring VERY soon. [Noi can't help but smile at him.] Noi: That's great news. It's good to have you back, Karma. [He bows his head slightly.] Karma: Thank you, I appreciate the sentiment. Have a good evening. [With that, he turns and leaves the scene of the interview. Noi is almost taken aback by the good manners with which he conducts himself- it's a rarity in the wrestling world, after all.
