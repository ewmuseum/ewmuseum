---- ---- Wethercade Pt. I ---- Part one of Scenes recorded earlier today] [It’s the break of dawn and the sun can be seen rising just above the horizon. Still there are many dark shadows that cast down on the ground. It’s a very quiet morning, not even the morning bird to sing or the sound of a postman delivering letters. Instead the whole area is filled with peace and quiet. In the neighbourhood stands an expensive red brick house with two floors and a fair sized garden. Inside the house are two people sat at a table in a kitchen, a man and his wife talking over a morning coffee. The man is dressed in a navy blue suit, a light blue shirt and a long green striped silk tie. The wife is wearing only a peach cotton dressing gown and red slippers. [Sean shakes his head and looks down deep into his coffee, as if he is trying to find an answer in the bottom of the coffee mug] Sean: If there were then I’d be looking into it by now!
