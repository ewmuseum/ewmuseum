Wrestler Spotlight: Tragic Hero
During the weekend following Wrestlecade 3 I had the opportunity to sit down with none other than current GW International Champion, Vincent Welch, The Tragic Hero.

Soon after successfully defending his championship gold against Sage Sabbot, Jason Dynasty, and Derek Frost, the retirement of "The Iceman" was announced. When asked if he felt he had anything to do with Dynasty's decision he had this to say:

"I think I had something to do with it. I beat him for the International Title, and he knew he would probally have to face me for the World title. I think knowing that he got scared and didn't want the embarrassment of losing to me. It disappoints me though, I was looking forward to facing him for that World Title so I could beat him again."

With that said I questioned his immediate goals in GW, In response he said:

"My immediate plans... Well first off I'm going to focus on trying to get that World title, I think Chris Camero has great talent, but he's too arrogant for his own good. He has the ability to beat anyone in this federation, and he's shown that ability, but that's only if he shows up for the match. He needs to get over his arrogance before he can be truly great. He fights himself more than he fights anyone else.

"Well, you take your firsts steps towards your goal of World Championship gold Monday night on Evolution against "The Ladies Man" Jay Robson." I interjected.

"I think I can defeat him, he's washed up and doesn't have it anymore. If you would have asked me this a year ago, then I would've been intimidated, but not now. I know my ability, I'm going in with a win against some good talent.. I'm not worried about Robson.. in fact if he wasn't facing me I wouldn't even have known he was still a part of this company. After winning the World Title I'm going to get The Nevermore out of power... Salaok has no right to hold the power he does, and I'm going to make sure that the GW won't become just like the Nevermore, since the only other person who seems to care the least bit is Brick, and I don't even know how much."

After inquiring about Ely Braxton and Gunner Addison who also seem to be fighting against Salaok and company. In his words they are...

"Fighting it for the wrong reasons... It's just a power struggle, there needs to be a third party to come in and gain nothing out of it. Braxton, Addison and that whole sect is going to gain power with the removal of the Nevermore. I don't want power, I just want to see them not have it."

Which prompted me to ask who should...

"Anyone, as long as it's not The Nevermore, or me."

Feeling I had waited long enough, I finally asked what the deal was between him and Quartz. GW fans from all over are dying to know the answer to this question.

"As far as I'm concerned we share a mutual respect in the ring. As far as outside of the ring I don't condone his actions. He's still involved with the Nevermore, which I have always vowed to destroy... As far as the Rose that's no one's business except for mine, and we'll see what comes of it... But the fans can be certain that I shall never walk with the Nevermore. We've all seen what happens once you get involved with them. Take Sage for instance... I think he's a great young talent, unfortunately he's wrapped up with the Nevermore. At Wrestlecade I was surprised by him, I didn't think he would come out like he did. I would like to see this kid in the ring more often, and I'd like to see him away from the Nevermore and the Harvesters, but I won't try to brainwash him like they've been doing.

Changing gears I decided to ask him what ever became of his brother Brian.

"Brian and I were once extremely close.. We toured through underground circuits together, we fought together, and like all brothers we fought against each other. The GW changed all of that... We came together, but I left due to personal reasons and we never kept contact since... I haven't spoken to him since he moved a few months ago... But oh well, that's the way things go. The past is lost and shouldn't be dwelled on much...."

With that said he bid me adieu and exited my office.
