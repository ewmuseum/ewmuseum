Death of a Lion The cameras cut to the Global parking deck where a black limousine has just arrived. The back door opens quickly and out steps Global Wrestling's Rokku Akodo. Rokku turns and reaches into the limousine to grab his duffle bag. He steps back and slams the door before turning and starting towards the arena entrance. The black limousine pulls off and moments pass before a black van pulls up behind Rokku. Rokku glances back at the van and watches as the drivers side door opens and a man in a black suit gets out. He slowly walks away from the van and out of sight, leaving the van in the middle of the passage way. Rokku turns back to the arena and starts walking, his eyes focusing on that alone. The sound of screeching tires is heard and Rokku jumps back, nearly being hit as a black van pulls directly to him, the grill inches from his chest. Rokku stands and slams his fists on the van; the engine revs and it starts to smoke, the wheels burning against the concrete. Suddenly, it takes off and Rokku begins to run. Within ten steps, he's stopped by the first van. He turns and jumps up... a second too late. The second van slams against him, knocking him back against the first. It then slowly edges closer, pinning him between the two vans. A thin trickle of blood comes from the corner of Rokku's mouth, his eyes closing tightly in pain. The van doors open and out steps Maverick Reno. Slowly, he paces forward adorned in a black suit and trench coat, his hair tied back tightly, a pair of blue circular and frameless sunglasses upon his face. He smiles and lifts a gloved hand and traces the contour of Rokku's face, his smile turning to a smirk and then a grimace; he draws his hand to the side quickly and strikes him, another trickle of blood pouring from his nose. The second door opens and out steps John Zero, a pair of bats in hand. He tosses one over the hood of the van to Maverick who catches it and slams it across Rokku's upper chest. Zero is quick to follow suit. John grabs Rokku's hair and jerks his head back against the first van, opening up his neck. Reno holds the bat up and grins before smashing it directly into Rokku's Adam’s apple. A coughing fit overtakes him, his chest still constrained by the two vans. A second later, Reno sends the larger end to the temple of Rokku's head, sending his head whipping to the right where John meets his head with the tapered end, sending it ricocheting in the opposite direction. John walks back to the van and climbs in to slowly back it up. Rokku drops to the ground. Writhing in pain, Rokku wraps his arms around his chest and assumes a fetal position. Reno positions his bat as a golfing club and swings, striking the back of Rokku's neck. He rolls to his other side, trying to escape the beating. Now, coming down in a cave-man like style, Reno smashes the bat into Rokku's ribs coughing another gasp for air and a coughing fit, complete with a cloud of blood emitting from between Rokku's lips. Reno leans down and grabs the mask still hanging on Rokku's face and speaks, his voice being completely hidden as their are no microphones near by. A moment passes and the first man returns, climbs into the first van, and pulls away. Reno slams the bat into the mask, using it to add even more of an impact to the jaw region of Rokku who has finally passed out. Another strike to the same spot at a sickening crack resounds. Reno positions Rokku on his back and exposes his neck quickly. Reno positions the bat upside down and lifts it high into the air. Now with an amazing strength and speed, Reno brings the bat down over and over again, repetitively slamming the bulk of the bat's tip into Rokku's throat, blood trickling more and more from the corners of his mouth. Reno drops the bat finally and turns, slowly walking off... John and the two vans having left much earlier. ---- Opening The screen is black before a line of static shoots through it.
