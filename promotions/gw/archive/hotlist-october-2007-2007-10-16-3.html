Hotlist (October 2007)

16 October 2007

1 Jake Hix: The Big Dawg proved just whos yard it was and took back his title, will 2007 be a repeat of 2006 for Jake Hix? One has to wonder. But right now Jake sits on top of Global once more. 
 4
2 Eryc Morgan: Morgan stays at the 2nd position again. He came close to gaining the World title back at High Octane. But with Brandi's new ruling,  What's in store for The Dark Flame? 1
3 BloodAngel: BloodAngel just a mere week after taking the title  from Eryc Morgan, looses the title to Jake Hix. But it was not Hix that beat him Infect it was his own stable mate John Adams that cost him his title. What will happen now? 1
3 John Adams:  Adams failed to recapture the world title, not only that but his involvement some how after Brick said NO. Cost BloodAngel the world title. And now Adams find his self in Morgan's Boat with Brandi's ruling. 3
4 Digital Morality: No title defense for Digital at the PPV, But the Porto Rican superstar still shines in the top five. He now looks to defend against Psymon on on one. Can Digi remain champ? 5
5 Josh Kosidilo: A big win for The Iron Paladin. Josh did what he said he was going to do. Defeat Scott Taylor.  Kosidilo Also proves he deserves to be in the top five with that win.  What is next for Josh? We shall see. -
6 Tommy Osborne: After a very unpredictable match where Aka was fired from Global..  Osbourn Retains his title.  7
7 Scott Taylor: Taylor drops down a bit after his loss to Kosidilo. But he still stays in the top ten, Now teaming with The Redneck Prinicess Taylor finds his self in a strange place. Can he Trust Tomas, and can they defeat Karma and Callihan 6
8 Rebel Express: No one expected what happened to happen. The rebel Express makes a big leap, not only winning there match against Best In Show but also destroying the Tag Team Title match. Will there be hell to pay? 20
9 T.W.C.: TWC keeps the Tag Team titles. But how long can they stay together and keep going. With the Rebel Express busting up the Title defense at High Octane. Look for the Tag Champs to be looking for some revenge. 9
10 Brick: The Commissioner returns to the list. He really put his foot down and did as he said. He warned Aka and now Aka is fired. But many call it fowl on this decision. Will Brandi be happy.  -
11 Reginald Atkins: The Brit Twit rises up again, now with Aka gone he becomes the new Number one Contender for the Platinum title. This week he faces off against Augustus. Can Atkins keep his ranking? 14
12 Ely Braxton.: Braxton showed the world he is back, Not only at N-O-C.. but at High Octane as well.  He also showed he does not play nice. One things remains to be seen. Can Ely get it done in the ring when he knows he has to fight.  -
13 S.o.t.B.L.: Ichiro and Jushin returned at N-O-C And now seem to have a target painted on them by True Evil.  In a battle of the Hall Of Fame. who will survive? 15
13 True Evil: Salaok and Quartz returned at N-O-C And now seem to have painted a target on the Souls of the Black Lotus.  In a battle of the Hall Of Fame. who will survive? -
14 Teresa Tomas: The Redneck Princess drops down a bit this week do to not having any matches. but still had a presence at High Octane.  With a tag match coming up, will she rise back up the list?   12
15 Psymon: After his loss to Digital Psymon has changed and dug out a blast from the past in the LOW Extreme title. Some say he has snapped and lost it. But if that is true what does that spell for Digital Mortality? 11
16 H.N.U.: The LOW Monster failed to gain the International championship.  Came close but Digital was able to put him down. What is next for the Monster known as Psymon? -
17 The Former Champions, had a impressive return, almost winning the  Tag Team Rumble at N-O-C. With Coach Driller by there side, this team looks to make it to the top and claim the championship they feel is theirs. 16
18 Jenny Callihan. Jenny stays at 18 with no match at the PPV, and a slight altercation with Teresa Tomas. But this week that will be more then that, as she teams with Karma. Will Karma and Jenny make good in there match and beat Taylor and Tomas? 18
19 Karma: The Anti Bully it seams. The Defender of the week. Karma has shown he is a knightly Person with honor and Chivalry. But will he be able to be that against Taylor and Tomas. -
19 Claudius Augustus: The Emperor took a hard defeat at N-O-C- and has fallen down a lot of spaces in the Hotlist.  But now he is back and  has a match against Atkins. Will it be thumbs up or thumbs down for the Brit Twit? -
20 Joaquin Pierre.: The French man made his debut at High Octane. Against Karma. It did not go as he wished, but Pierre looks to over look that defeat and take a win against a Fiasco this week.  -
20 D.U.I.: The Frat Boys fall down the list hard. After a bad night at the hands of The Rebel Express. One has to wonder if the opportunity to take the Tag team titles slipped away from them. But Don't count them out just yet.  13

Biggest Rise: - Josh Kosidilo 16pts. Biggest Drop: - D.U.I. - 8pts. Off the list: Prima Donnas, Best in Show To watch for: Julian Fiasco, Sam Potright, Shane Weathers, Amanda DeAngleo

Thoughts: Well High Octane took a lot of people by surprise. Jake Hix claimed the title for his 3rd time I believe. High Octane also set the stage for some new feuds. With John Adams and Eryc Morgan not able to go for the World title till after WrestleCade. [Unless they win COTT] Who will step up. Aka getting fired that created even more controversy.  What will happen next week?
