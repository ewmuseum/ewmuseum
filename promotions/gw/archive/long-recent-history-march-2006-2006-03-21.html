Long — recent history (March 2006)

21 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recent History The story of Ronnie Long's GWF career has always been this. Make an impact, get a title shot, get injured, make a comeback, rinse, lather, repeat.

Long's GWF debut was against James Primus, whom he beat.

Long was one of the founding members of the first incarnation of The Foundation, lead by Jason Ayers. While part of this stable, he won the GWF Television Championship, defeating Ben J. Pailthorpe (Firestarter at the time) to win, and losing it to Cage two and a half months later.

After losing the Television title, Long turned to the tag division. With stablemate Daeriq Damien, he won an 8 team Electrified Dome match to win the Xtreme Tag Championships. Long and Damien would lose the titles a month later to D-Ranged.

This was when Long started his pattern of being unable to put forth a sustained effort. When the Foundation added new talent and Long found himself lost in the shuffle, he quit the stable. He would hook up with a girl named Deanna who would become his valet, bounce around the undercard and midcard, and briefly rejoin the Foundation. He even briefly joined the Nevermore. But in the end, aside from a 10 minute Hardcore Title reign, Long never accomplished anything further in GWF.

So what to do? A change of scene. Long left GWF and went down to the Innovative Wrestling Alliance, a large indy fed in Florida that Blade Entertainment owned 40% of. Within a month of showing up in IWA, Long joined The Foundation, another stable, this one the Blade E. force in IWA. Long would win the Tag Titles with stablemate Chris Wright. They would later become a 3 man tag team with fellow GWF alumni Brian Simon. Wright and Simon called the 3 person unit 'Sexcellence'... well, OK, Ronnie called it that too, but he likes to forget that Sexcellence ever existed... anyway, Sexcellence eventually split from The Foundation, thanks to Ronnie's fight with the leader of the stable, Neo.

This was the turning point in Long's career. Long fought Neo in a match that rivaled anything done in the USA. In the end, Neo, weilding a razor wire wrapped chair, hit Long three times, but Long shook this off and Roaring Elbowed the chair into Neo's face. The match ended by no contest when neither man could get back up.

Still, Long claimed he had reached a realization. Forsaking his prior way of calling himself 'the dark side of humanity', it became his belief that he could, to put it in his words, 'save the world from the darkness it lives in'. Long quickly became not just a fan favorite, but a hero, to the IWA fans. He perfected his philosophy, and watched his career take off like never before.

When it came to the IWA Title, Ronnie decided to leave that prize for his friend in that federation, Jeffy Andrews, and to seek something else. It was here that he decided to return to the GWF... now called the GW.

So Ronnie's back. He knows the GW made him. Due to the amount of punishment his body has taken over the years, he knows his career won't be one of the ones that lasts into his 40s. He gets injured frequently - he still has a rib injury that didn't heal correctly that pains him - and he knows each one might be his last. Although he's only 26, he feels that his time is running out... and he's determined to give GW one last good run before that happens.
