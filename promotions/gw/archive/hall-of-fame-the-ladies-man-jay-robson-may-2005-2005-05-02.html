"The Ladies Man" Jay Robson

Jay Robson is known for his exploits with the ladies and because of this man, when fans hear a women moan at a Global event, they automatically assume that he's the one behind it. Jay formed with Derek frost to form a memorable Tag team known as Cold Blood. Arguably one of the greatest tag teams to never hold the World tag team titles.  
Jay was also one of the original members of the Global chapter of Youngbloods.  

Later Jay would leave the Youngbloods and form his own stable.. the Mile high Club.  The 'MHC' would dominate for a while before splitting up...

Later even the Youngbloods would reform for a while to stand up against the TUE in its beginning stages in Global. only to break up once more. 

Jay has both been loved and hated by the fans, but will always be remembered, and respected for his accomplishments.  

Key Wins: 

Memorable Feuds:  Vs True Evil [As cold Blood with Derek Frost Vs Salaok Vs Rob Vertiruoso  Vs Kamikazi Vs Chris Camero Vs Derek Frost

Titles Held:   Global World Championship International Championship World Tag Team Championship Cruiserweight Championship Primestar/TV champion Street fight/Hardcore champion. Pacific Champion United States Champion Awards: 2000 Rookie of the year

Accomplishments: Grand Slammer 2001 Master of the Mat First Triple Champion (World, International, and Cruiser Weight)

Words from his peers: 

TLM was one of the guys i picked up prior to the merger. He was doing some collage wrestling and  when I saw his raw talent I knew he would be great. Johnny Cruse was also Impressed [Cruse then part owner of the company while I was Talent Director] Cruise took Jay under his wing showed him the roped and  after that TLM flew like a jet. Often controversial but none could mistake the drive and talent the guy had. one of the greats for a long long time.   - Bubba McCoy -

A man that I've had the pleasure to fight in a ladder match for the Cruiserweight title. And it was literally, one of the greatest matches I've ever had. Jay gave me bruises that will never fade. He made his mark in Global. - Sage Sabbot -
