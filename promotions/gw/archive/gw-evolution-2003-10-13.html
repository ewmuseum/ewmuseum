Evolution, 13 October 2003

13 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier Tonight ---- The McCoys Christina Mccoy sits in her office, a remote control in her hand. Her eyes are focused straight ahead, bags beneath her eyes and a look of sadness overcoming her. She slowly lifts her hand up and presses pause and then rewind. A moment passes, her eyes closing as she stares blankly ahead. Suddenly she presses play again; this is not the first time she's done this, it has become a natural function to her. The scene slowly pans over to show the video tape received just a week before. Donovan hits the floor in a heap and the static once again overcomes the screen. A knock comes at the door and Christina quickly turns the tv off and attempts to collect herself. Christina McCoy: Uh com... co.. wait. Who is it? Person at the door: It's me, Darlin', Ely. Christina McCoy: Oh...come in, Ely. Ely Braxton pushes the door open and steps inside, he closes the door behind him and glances at Christina. Ely: You're thinking about him aren't you? Christina McCoy: How can I not be, Ely? How can I not be... Ely: Well, I understand, Darlin'. I really do, but there's only so much we can do. Christina looks down at the table, tears now streaming down her face Christina Mccoy: I came here when Daddy got sick and I said "I'm gonna make Dad proud of me." I started working here and ... and I started trying so hard. Salaok was in control and all I could think to do was to get help, I kept asking "what would Dad do?" and ... I made a mistake, Ely. I made a big mistake. I ... I... Ely: Now calm down... Christina McCoy: No no! Damnit. Let me finish. I called those... those pieces of shit and I asked them to take care of Nevermore. They did, I have to admit. She laughs slightly as she says this They got rid of them without too much of a hassle. Then then they... they started running amuk! So what do I do? I let it go... I let it go! Now look, Ely! My brother's missing and I know they're behind it! I know they are! Ely: Christina, you can't blame yourself. Christina McCoy: But I'm the one who... Christina is cut off as the phone in her office rings. Both Ely and she glance at it before she, in a huff, picks it up and answers. Christina McCoy: Samantha, I asked that no one disturb me... except for Ely. There's a slight pause and a look of surprise comes over her face. Christina: Dad? the scene fades to black ---- Static fills the screen and slowly the GW logo fades though the static and fades away… ---- The screen suddenly flickers to life with static as words appear in black. It Sustains us... It Changes us... The beginning of Miseria Cantare by AFI begins to play, and the Evolution opening credits play out , featuring the latest stars of GW.. As the opening credits fade the words WELCOME TO GLOBAL WRESTLING Flashes on the screen… WELCOME TO………. YOUR EVOLUTION The music stops and a gun shot blast is heard, starting Evolution. The GW logo pops up on the screen and the smoke surrounds it. Then static fills the screen… and fades away to a view of the GW Commentators box.. Where Brandi Sights: And Mark Bishop sit behind the announce desk. ---- Brandi Sights: Hello fight fans.. Welcome to another exciting edition of GW Evolution I'm your host. Brandi Sights along with my broadcast partner, Mark Bishop. Bishop: Who said you’re the host.. THIS IS MY SHOW DAMN YOU!!! Sights: Ohh Please… Christina said it okay. After that little stunt with Fox and My Little Pony, your lucky your still on this show.. Bishop: Damn Uncle Bob. Remind me to fire him from my family. Sights: Sure thing.. Well tonight we have a awesome night lined up.. Get this Young Bloods vs TUE its all out war here tonight! Bishop: Is Cash even in the Youngbloods? We know Jason Hughes is. Sights: not sure I am sure we shall know soon enough. Well lets get this one started… Wait.. I here we are going to a live feed… ---- Three Way Elimination Tony Stecchino v. Ken Patridge v. Ben J. Pailthorpe SUMMARY: To start off, all three competitors gave one another a run for their money until Stecchino grabbed a stunned Partridge from behind, spun him and delivered the power between his legs. With a quick pin, it was now down to Stecchino and Pailthorpe. With this pair off, a powerful display of technical wrestling was the order of the match. Both Stecchino and Pailthorpe matched one another move for move in mat wrestling and then continue up the tiers. When Pailthorpe attempted to clothesline a gloating Stecchino, Stecchino ducked underneath his forearm, hooked him and delivered a few well placed blows to his midsection. With one powerful display, Stecchino lifted him into the air and planted him with the Break Up for the win! Time: 6 minutes, 24 seconds. Winner: Tony Stecchino ---- More Mysteries The scene fades to nothingness. A thin white line appears on the screen and bisects it horizontally. A heart beat bellows from the silence and a normal heartrate begins with the line hitting peaks and valleys like an EKG. The sound of a person breathing heavily from being out of breath can be heard below the heart beat. The pulse quickens and and the breaths become faster and shorter. The pulse grows to be on the verge of the person's heart exploding due to it pounding as fast as it can take it. Suddenly, the roar of a tiger explodes over all of the sounds. In the blink of an eye, the scene changes to footage of a masked man standing alone in the center of a ring of an empty stadium. The camera circles around his tattooed body at a high rate of speed, as the man stands with every muscle tensed and his taped fists clenched. The camera makes its way to his front and quickly zooms in so only his cold brown eyes can be seen, as they shine through his bandana-like, black mask. "Isei" The screen flashes and the scene quickly changes to show the masked man in action, as "Of Wolf and Man," by Metallica, begins to blare. The mystery man catches his unknown opponent in midair to counter a flying cross body. From there, he tosses his opponent up into a fireman's carry and as soon as their body lands in position, he whips them down whip a Death Valley Driver, in which the masked man over rotates, so his opponent lands on their back. The footage cuts to the man with another opponent and he counters a clothesline attempt into a full Nelson. The unknown superstar lifts his foe from the ground and slams them down face first with a modified, reverse, sitout powerbomb. "Hishou" The screen flashes again and the footage returns to show the masked man standing on the top of a 20foot high ladder. His un-named opponent lays lifeless on a table, as he leaps from the ladder and executes a 540 corkscrew splash that drives both men through the wooden table. The scenes change rapidly and we see the mysterious superstar standing on the apron of the ring. He shoots himself up to the top rope near the corner, springboards himself over to the adjacent ropes, bounces off of them with his back to his fallen foe in the ring, and twists into a frog splash. "Kakutou" The quick explosion of white light engulfs the screen once more. The man in the mask kicks his opponent in the back of the left thigh firmly, delivers a stiff kick to their midsection with his left, and then catches the foe in the back of the head with an enziguiri with his right foot. The foe plummets to the ground and clutches the back of his head in agony. The next selection appears without warning. The mystery man tries a spinning, heel kick and the foe ducks it. Using the momentum from the heel kick, the masked man spins down for a sweep attempt to only have it jumped over. Still using his momentum, he corkscrews back up to his feet and launches a leaping roundhouse that sends the unknown opponent spiralling to the ground in a heap. "Bakuhatsuteki" The flash of white light returns to cleans the screen of the out footage and usher in the new. The mysterious wrestler races towards his opponent executes a forward roll. Just has his feet hit the mat to finish the roll, the masked man explodes into vicious clothesline that nearly turns his foe inside out. Just as the last shot ends, a new one begins. The mysterious man anticipates his opponent's next move, easily casts their punch attempt to the side, takes them down to the mat roughly with a well-executed STO. "Okibi Doragon" The screen flashes a final time and displays the man in the black mask rushing towards his dazed foe. The unknown superstar places his left foot on the right knee of the un-named opponent and uses it to push off into a backflip. The masked man's right foot trails behind during the beginning of the flip and catches the opponent square in the jaw, as he pulls it up into a tuck position with his left. Just as the foot makes contact, the footage stops and executes a Matrix-style wrap around view of the shot with the black mask clad man hanging upside down. The footage returns to normal speed and the mysterious superstar lands safely on one knee while the opponent is sent flying backwards and to the mat. The scene fades to a plain black screen once more and the roar of a tiger can be heard, as a "T" appears on the screen with the white horizontal line of the letter being held up by a quartet of animal like slashes. ---- Where is this leading? Jack Rocker with a look of utter boredom continues to wait at the reception desk, he has waited there since his arrival to find out where his locker room is and despite calling the receptionist several times there is still no one to help him. Jack rocker shakes his head thinking about how he had dressed specially for his first night, he was wearing a black Nike t-shirt and a pair of black Nike jogging bottoms and of course his £20 black Nike trainers from England. Jack looked at the clock on the wall and finally decided he had had enough of waiting and would go of by himself in search of his locker room. Jack walked down the corridor then took a left turn; he walked down another corridor until he walked straight into none other then Tony Stecchino. Tony: What the hell. Apologize you measly little jobber for you just walked straight into the GW main eventer and legend Tony Stecchino. Jack just shakes his head. Jack: I aint gonna fucking apologize to you. I don’t care who you are, I’m not going to shit my pants just cause some overpaid and over rated guy walks straight into me. It is Tony’s turn to shake his head. He steps closer to the rookie and the rookie attempts to step closer to Tony as well but Tony’s bodyguards push Jack back. Tony points to himself. Tony: This is the best wrestler alive today. This is a legend and a main eventer, this is a body that is championship material. Not someone like you who doesn’t even deserve to be a wrestler, you have no class, no honor and no respect. You haven’t even wrestled you first match and you think you’re the greatest. Jack shakes his head and his face goes red, he once again tries to step closer to Tony but the bodyguards hold him back. Jack: You ought to become a fucking politician because all you’re talking is bull shit. You’re not no legend, instead you’re a young cocky bitch that doesn’t have the guts to walk around by himself in case a big bad man beats him up. Well the small bad man is here Tony and he can still get you even if a group of your bodyguards stand between us. I tell ya what, would little son of a bitch like the first shot? Tony waves for the bodyguards to move out of the way and he steps towards Jack, he stops when he is just one footstep away from Jack Rocker, Jack smiles an insane smile where as Tony keeps his face straight and emotionless. Tony clenches both fists in a sign to show he is prepared to fight and Jack Rocker does the same. Tony: You want a fight kid? I hope so because you’ve got one, and I won’t need a free first punch so I’m letting you take the first shot. Then again, why should I fight you after all, you’re just a rookie grunger who wants to be a wrestler. You’ve never lived a hard day in your life and I wouldn’t want you to start now. Guards, get back where you were I’m not going to fight a little twelve year old wannabe. The bodyguards walk to the front and side of Tony, while Jack shakes his head. Tony smiles at Jack but Jack just ignores it and turn around, he walks of towards the end of the corridor but suddenly without warning he gets clotheslined from behind. As Jack lies on the cold concrete floor Tony stomps away at him smiling a sick smile. After several stomps Tony allows Jack to get to his feet, it takes Jack a couple of minutes to clamber to his feet but he does and he turn around to face Tony again. Tony holds both hands in the air but then low blows Jack that causes Jack to sink to his knees. Tony pounds away at Jack’s forehead with closed fists, opening up Jack more or less instantly. Eventually Tony tires of this and he throws Jack to the ground face first. Tony Stecchino, turn away from Jack Rocker and walks up the corridor, followed by his group of bodyguards who as always show no emotion despite walking past the bloodied body of a nineteen year old. As Tony walks away Jack Rocker lifts his head of the ground a laughs a crazed laugh, a laugh that any maniac would be proud of. Jack: Soon Tony…soon it’ll be you tasting your own blood.”Jack then dips his right hand’s index and middle finger in the blood, Jack stares at the blood on his fingers and then places the fingers in his mouth and tastes the blood, he removes his fingers from his mouth and laughs once again. ---- Standard Match Jushin Nakazura vs Jin Akiyara SUMMARY: After a hell of a start and an insane display of high flying and technical maneuvers, the match slowed down quite a bit. The Upper Echelon was quick to surround the ring implementing the intimidation factor on Jin Akiyara. With this new factor, Akiyara begun to watch his back and act very cautiously when nearing the ropes. Ichiro Nakazura quickly grabs the ankle of Jin Akiyara, causing him to stagger forward. While this was going on, Deacon Holmes quickly slipped a pair of brass knuckles to Jushin who, without a second thought, slammed the knucks into the face of Akiyara knocking him to the mat. The bell was called and the match was awarded to Jin Akiyara by disqualification. TUE then swarmed the ring and proceeded to deal Akiyara a beating he would well remember. Jushin Nakazura then took up a mic and spoke stating that "GW is The Echelon's territory; this ring is the only place where any of you get a shot and you see what has happened to one of your fellow roster members. No Man's Land is Hell... Welcome to Hell, Global Wrestling." Time: 4 minutes, 12 seconds. Winner: Jin Akiyara by Disqualification. ---- ---- Echelon Gauntlet Brick v. Sean Price SUMMARY: AFter a hellatious battle with both men growing extremely weak and a hardcore lover's heaven, the match quickly dropped to the mat. Meaning, both men hit the mat after Brick met the forehead of Sean Price with a two by four and Sean Price brought a club straight up, nailing Brick directly between the legs. Both men hit the ground with a thud and after an insane set of chanting and ranting, Brick was the first to make a cover to the bloodied Sean Price. With the first three count, the match started again! 1st Win: Brick Sean Price was quick to grab one of the new weapons thrown into the ring, a rubber hammer, and slam the head against the forehead of Brick. As Brick hit the mat, Price quickly applied both the hammer and the nightstick to the face of Brick, making sure that he was out of the count. Brick was quick to tap, giving Sean Price the second win. 2nd win: Sean Price With one more win to go and the match at a complete standstill, Sean Price and Brick were pulled to the sides of the Octagon. Knowing they would, the TUE lumberjacks attempted to get involved. Sean Price slipped his hands to the outside and John Zero slipped a pair of brass knuckles onto each hand - diamond encrusted and made of platinum. With a quick thrust punch, Sean Price struck Brick once, twice, and then a third time, taking him to the mat with a loud thud. Sean Price made a quick pin however, Brick miraculously kicked out. The crowd continued to stomp and chant for Brick while booing Sean Price. Price grew angry with Brick's return and began to attack him mercilessly with his bare fists, having dropped the knuckles back to Zero. Brick reached to the side and grabbed the night stick; at this point, he spun it once and brought it up and to the side of Sean Price's head. With a quick and desperate cover, Brick gets the 1... 2...3 just before the shoulder of Price went into the air! Speculation on his win was made although the official's call was upheld! Time: 15 minutes, 54 seconds. Winner: Brick ---- Johnny Cruse speaks Summary: Johnny Cruse sat down with Alexis Steele and spoke on his deals with The Echelon and about his wife. He admitted to giving TUE what they wanted in exchange for his wife and her safety. Although TUE denies any involvement in his wife's abduction, they were able to support her and keep her alive until an agreement could be reached between Echelon Inc. and Johnny Cruse. An agreement was reached although, Cruse declined to speculate on the arrangements only by stating "I apologize to Global Wrestling, to Bubba McCoy and his daughter Christina, I apologize to The GW roster, the fans, and also to my friends... Jay Robson and Ben J. Pailthorpe. I, I'm simply sorry. That's all I have to say." ---- Thumping The Help Cut to the back area as we focus in on two metal doors labeled simply "Parking Lot". The left door cracks open showing the shiny back of a superstar's jacket. The superstar backsteps a little as he talks on a cell phone. His hair dangles over his face as he leans to that side. He spins around to show that its Ace Cash. A grin creases his face as he strolls down the hallway. Ace: Yeah yeah, I don't care if you have to slip him a benji, get me a table at that resturaunt! As the hallway reaches an intersection corridor, a figure is seen leaning against the wall. Upon seeing Ace, he gets off the wall and runs down to talk to Ace. The figure rushes up to Ace and stops him for a moment. Before Ace stands a short, young man with bushy black hair staring back at him with a black t-shirt and jeans. Man: Excuse me, are you Ace Cash? Ace: *to the phone* Hold on a minute... Yes, I am. What of it? Man: Oh, I'm sorry. I didn't realize you were on the phone. I'd hate to bother you, but could I talk to you for a minute or two? Ace: I'm a pretty busy here. Uh, I guess. *to the phone* Sorry man, I got a fan that wants an autograph. Yes, of course I have fans! Jesus, who do you take me for? Ah, whatever. I'll call ya back. Ace hangs up the phone and turns towards the man. In doing so, he pulls a sharpie out of his back pocket with a notepad. Ace: Right, who should I make this out to? Man: Uhhh...I think you're a bit messed up. See, my name's Johnie Ringo. Ace: Ah, I see. Johnie.....Ringo..... Ace leans down and starts autographing the paper. He finishes and hands it to him as he starts to walk off. Ace: There you are, bud! Keep the peace, stay a fan. Johnie Ringo stops him right quick and pulls him aside. Ringo: You still didn't understand. I didn't want an autograph, I wanted to ask you for some advice. Ace: Advice? Son, I'm a wrestler. Not a shrink! Ringo: Just a minute. You see, I'm new here and I wanted to ask you what it takes to get noticed right away here in the GW? Ace: Well kid...you gotta tough it out and fight big. Aim high and try to take someone down in the process. Ringo: That's JUST what I plan to do. Ringo pulls a nightstick from his back pocket and cracks Ace across the head with it. Ace staggers forward a bit while Ringo grabs Ace by his shirt and launches him into a set of folding chairs sending them scattered about. Johnie picks up one and waits for Ace to fight to stand. Ringo: Stecchino has a message for you, Ace! Stay the hell out of the Echelon's business! *CRACK!* The walls vibrate with that chairshot to skull of Ace Cash. Ace drops to the top of the chairstack. Johnie Ringo goes to stand over him. Ringo: I'm terribly sorry, bud. But work's work. And I gotta get paid somewhere. You just happened to be the job. Ringo looks at the crumpled piece of paper in his hand and scoffs. Ringo: 'Keep cheering, Ace Cash.' I think I'm gonna keep this one. Might be worth something someday? Ringo turns to leave and whistles "Whistle While You Work" as he struts away. ---- Main Event World Championship Fatal Four Way TLM v. Deacon Holmes v. Ice v. Akodo Rokku SUMMARY: The match started off fast paced and stayed there. Ice was on a rampage trying to keep his title, attacking everyone but Deacon Holmes. Holmes fought with Akodo while Ice dealt powerful blow after powerful blow to TLM. After minutes of fighting, Deacon was able to push Akodo out of the ring and rush TLM, nailing him with a jumping dropkick. TLM staggered into the arms of Ice who quickly applied and implemented a Hands Down! Ice rolls away and Deacon makes a quick pin, taking TLM out of the works! Eliminated: Jay Robson Time: 3 minutes, 15 seconds by: Deacon Holmes Rokku quickly jumped back into the ring but was quickly double teamed by Ice and Deacon Holmes. After a few minutes of TUE powerplaying, Deacon got caught by a desperation powerful super kick that sends him staggering back into the turnbuckle. Ice then rushes Rokku but Akodo ducks and Ice flips up and over the top rope, landing on the outside. With a very fast set of moves, Rokku jumps and nails a tornado DDT on Deacon Holmes, slamming him head first into the mat and making a quick pin. Eliminated: Deacon Holmes Time: 8 minutes, 29 seconds by: Rokku Akodo Now with the last two, Akodo and Ice end up in the ring in a stare off. Akodo is the first to swing but Ice ducks under and pushes him towards the ropes where Deacon is quick to jump up. Ice bumps into the referee, knocking him to the mat. He quickly helps him up while Deacon Holmes tosses powder into the face of Rokku. Now with a quick maneuver, Ice grabs Rokku and nails another Hands Down before making a quick pin to get the win and retain his title. Winner: Ice Time: 10 minutes, 2 seconds ---- Surprise Endings ---- Just as members of GW rush out to the aide of Akodo, TUE members surround Ice and Deacon Holmes. The two groups begin a brawl before the lights all flicker on and off and then all goes silent. GW Security rushes the bedlam and dives in, seperated the feuding groups. Christina McCoy walks out, Ely Braxton at her side and a great distance between them. Samantha Vale stands right next to Christina, her arms crossed and ready for anything. Suddenly, Jesse Briggs pushes Bubba McCoy, in a wheel chair, out on the ramp. He has a microphone in hand. Bubba McCoy: The games are over! Sights: OH MY GOD NO! HBO has a movie coming on now! We're going to have to cut away! Bishop: YAHOO! TIME FOR THE PORNO! Sights: We'll get the information to you as soon as possible! Good night everyone! Bubba McCoy: I've come to... the scene fades to black ---- ---- ---- Global Wrestling EVOLUTION Producer J. P. Manager Ryan L. Writers Ryan L. Brock G. Kevin N. Special Thanks to Brock G.
