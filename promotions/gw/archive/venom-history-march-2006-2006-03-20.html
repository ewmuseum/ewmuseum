Venom — history (March 2006)

20 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Venom Alexander, real name unknow, but the story of Venom is a story won againts all odd's, he grew up in a small run down neighbor hood in Cleveland Ohio, where he live with his mother, they had no money for food anything like that nature. So Venom, dropped out of school, so he could bring home some money to help his mother.

He would jump from job to job, whichever would pay the most for him since not having a high school degree. When he turned 17, his mother died of cancer he had no one left in his life.

Years went by, and Venom found himself wrestling in a small fed, under the name Kray-Z alone with his long time friend Cobra who was under the name B-Z, the went held tag titles once in the career.

Now on to the present, Venom finds himself in the GW looking to continue success in honor of his mother.

He still keeps in touch with friend Cobra, who he battled in a streetfight, reason for Cobra leaving. Cobra discovered he had HIV and decided to leave the wrestling world.
