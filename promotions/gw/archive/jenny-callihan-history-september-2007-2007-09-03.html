Jenny Callihan — history (September 2007)

3 September 2007

History

There is no short answer to describe Jennifer Callihan's impact on wrestling. Her career had been pretty much the typical womanly role until she stepped foot into the BACW the week of July 7, 2003. She became a household name to anybody who been exposed to BACW.

The little girl (term used very loosely) from Apache Jct, AZ started her life as a gymnast under her father and mother's dictatorship coaching. Finally, she had enough, and ran away from home. Moving in with her Aunt, she finished High School, but she went through a serious depression resulting in her going from a very slender 100 lbs to a buldging 230 beast.

Overally depressed with her weight, she decided to enroll in The Ranch, Davey Scott's wrestling school, and through there she got deeply involved in the world of wrestling, and slimmed down to a firm 130 lbs.

She did minor work in several different promotions, which one she was notorious as the character of 'Starlet'. It wouldn't be wise to call her that as it would result in the same thing as calling her a slut. Never a good idea to get into that situation.

However, through her teacher, Davey Scott, she got a contract with AWA, and started wrestling with mild success, but again a typical womanly role that severely underused her talents. However, this would put her in connection with her new boss, Shadow.

Due to the lack of interest of Jenny in AWA, her disappearence was not really noted, but when she showed up on Bloodbath
July 7th, 2003, she made history by doing something that shocked even Shadow at the time. At the time, BACW was still subjected to womanly roles, and the epitome of it was Kaye Heart and her 'beautiful ways'. To make a long story short, Kaye suffered life threatening injuries from the hands of Jenny Callihan as she COMPLETELY dismantled the woman, and set a mark for what the Queen of Extreme was to become, and solidified it with her battles with former classmate at the Ranch, Vixyn.

When she beaten Pandora in a vicious Condemned Building match in a condemned building match, she helped change the standards for women. Although Shadow and Vixyn were doing it before, Jenny made it acceptable among the fans. Her no-bullshit attitude won her a lot of accolades, and she broke more barriers during an interview when confronted about her sexuality, in which she confessed she was bisexual. She has been consistently asked about her past relationships, but she kept those a secret.

However, people became surprised when she announced her engagement to Derrick Steinbreiner, then when she wed, the whole world weep. Ok, that was a little bit of an exaggeration, but one of BACW most eligable bacholarettes was off the market. However, the marriage didn't last as two laters, Derrick told her they were getting divorced while she was in Austria...and he was in Seattle.

However, this didn't stop Jenny from being..Jenny, and kicking tail for another Queen of Extreme championship, but she went into a retirement as things weren't going so well healthwise. She hung her boots up after the Temple of Doom match, which by far was the most brutal match she ever participated.
