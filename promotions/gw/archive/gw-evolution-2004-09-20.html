---- Afraid of the Dark? Kitty Mac walked down the hallway, her steps light, and her feet barely touching the ground. She was completely unaware of her surroundings, and the goofy smile that adorned her lips was testament to that fact. Her mind was elsewhere, contemplating El Serpenti’s rock hard body, and his amazing green eyes. She shouldered open her locker room door and let her backpack fall from her shoulder as she flicked on the lights. Strange, nothing happened. Frustrated, she hit the switch again. Kitty: “What the hell?” She didn’t notice the dim speck of light in the corner, the telltale glowing orange tip of a cigarette, nor did she smell the smoke, which was perhaps a blessing in disguise. The room was utterly silent, still as a tomb. A prickle of fear turned her skin cold, making goosebumps appear on her arms. She rubbed them absently, and then shouted, her voice reverberating in her ears. Kitty: “Hello? Is there someone there?” Instead of waiting for an answer, she turned on her heel and stormed back out into the hallway. There was no way in hell she was getting ready for her match with Adam Myers in the dark.
