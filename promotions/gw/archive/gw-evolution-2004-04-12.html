---- Tall Problem, Short People. (Taped Earlier Today) There is a large crowd of vertically challenged individuals outside the rear entrance to the Arena where Global Wrestling's superstars enter. They are outside protesting the Nomad Donny J McNasty's use of vertically challenged people on last weeks Evolution on HBO when he won the drinking contest, there was a large group of these people dressed up as Leprechauns who carried McNasty back up to the stage as if he was a king. It just so happened to offend a group known as, Vertically Challenged People are People too. And that is who is here protesting this afternoon. There are signs that read, "We are people too" "Good things come in small packages" and "Fire McNasty Now" As the people are protesting, Mr. McNasty himself is about to walk into the rear entrance of the arena and the angry crowd sees him and chases after him. McNasty sees them and tells them to stop right there, and they all halt. He begins to address the crowd. "Hey look people, I know you are people to. Midgets are cool with me." The crowd becomes infuriated with the use of the term Midget as they like to be called Vertically Challenged. Some jeers can be heard such as, "Go back to Ireland you drunk" "Your mother should have spanked you as a child" McNasty sensing the danger decides it's time to run into the arena and stay out of harms way. As he runs in the angry crowd try to follow him but decide not to break the law and don't enter the building, the man who seems to be the ring leader of them all turns around and tells them to quiet down. "Ladies and Gentleman, lets not get too angry now, lets calm down and lets think how we can get his attention, next week we will continue our march for equality." ---- Ambushed Outside of the Global Arena, reporters and fans line a guardrail before the main entrance doors. A black Escalade on 22" chrome rims pulls forward, behind that a string of limousines separated by another tricked out Escalade in the middle. Behind the second set of limousines, complete with tinted windows and 20" inch chrome rims, is a third Escalade. From the first, John Zero steps forth, right behind him comes Victoria, from the second limo, comes Deacon Holmes, from the third Naria Xiou and Tyler Pierce. From the fourth limousine, The Consilliu Maverick Reno and Tobias de Sarret step out. The group is instantly surrounded by TUE security and they begin to make their way towards the door. The group stops slightly, to glance back at their grand entrance caravan. The large amounts of security scramble around to make sure everything is set. As The Upper Echelon stands the fans scream, many with obscenities towards the group. Maverick Reno, stands tall looking around, smiling at what he has created. There is a loud humming that begins to drown out the cheer of the fans. The Echelon members look around confused suddenly there is a small click. Underneath one of the Cadillac Escalades a large portion of Plastic Explosives is ignited and there is a loud screeching and a burst of flames as the fire erupts around the SUV. The SUV is shot ten feet in the air from the explosion and tires and debris fill the air. The Escalade lands crushing one of the limos and sending a huge ball of flames that covers a few of the limousines. The TUE members rally around and begin to stand up, the fans still standing in shock at the fire and chaos which they see before them. Reno begins to curse under his breath and checks off the members of his Echelon. Zero and Victoria rise, unharmed. Pierce, Xiou and Deacon Holmes all fine. Where was his partner? He scans the crowd and a look of shock comes over his face when he finally finds Tobias de Sarret. Tobias is lying unconscious on the ground, a flaming tire sits only feet from where he lies, the flames almost tickling his face. Reno runs over and picks up de Sarret who was hit by the tire sometime during the explosion. The members of TUE quickly file their way into the building Reno carrying de Sarret and Tyler Pierce screaming for medical assistance. GW Staff rushes out to check on the fans, all of whom are silent. They look on and scan the scene. You can brag about being on top...sign that contract and I'll knock you off your perch. American Youth bangs his fist down hard on the table. American Youth: Just write your name on the dotted line, roll the dice and take that leap of faith! Because if your lucky...maybe the dice will roll just right, If your lucky maybe when you take your leap of faith you'll land on your feet...but the alternative is reality. The alternative is having me kick your ass...again. Ice holds his hand out in anticipation of the microphone again. Josh does just that, handing the microphone to Ice. Ice: Josh, buddy, pal, amigo, BROTHER. You want this so bad. You need this so bad. Well then let's not wait anymore. Let's make it official. Ice snatches the pen from the clipboard, and places the pen down, as he beings to scribble his signature. Before actually signing, Ice stops, and looks up across the table at "American Youth" Josh. A sick, sadistic smile crawls across Ice's face. Ice: Josh, I don't feel it right, to sign this paper, this way. If we are going to do this, let's do it the way we always have, and do it ALL THE WAY. Ice twists around and bends over, reaching into his boot. From out of his left boot, Ice pulls out a small black pocketknife. Quickly he flips it open, and slices the underneath side of his right index finger. Blood begins to ooze from the finger, as Ice places it on the paper and signs the contract with that very blood. Ice looks up from the paper, and sets the pocketknife on top of the contract. A demonic expression engulfs Ice's face, as he slides the clipboard across the table towards "American Youth" Josh. Josh stops as he picks up the knife, holding it tightly in his left hand this causes a stir amongst the GW faithful as the suicidal American Youth places the blade against his wrist. He pauses a moment letting the bloody stained blade rest on the veins shooting up his wrist before slowly turning the knife to his hand. In one quick motion he nonchalantly slices his index finger open as the blood begins to flow. Dropping the knife back onto the table, Josh lifts his index finger up and smiles a bit amused by the sight of his own blood. Looking back down at the contract before him Josh flips through the pages of the contract before stopping. Slowly he lowers his bloody finger and with a faraway look in his eyes he scribbles his name across the page in blood as Ice smirks in satisfaction. Ice: Get used to that. It won't be the last time I am the reason for your blood flowing. Good luck, I'll see you at WRESTLECADE. Ice winks, and grabs his belt off the table. He stands, and walks past Josh, without looking at him, as Josh's eyes follow Ice. Ice steps through the ropes, and heads back up the ramp, without looking back at the ring, or "American Youth" Josh once. Christina McCoy picks the microphone up from the table and goes to speak, but American Youth covers it as his flowing blood drips onto her. He tosses the microphone as he turns away.
