R.I.P. Tony Stecchino: 1979-2005

Today GW has learned that former 2 time GW World Heavyweight Champion and 2003 Showdown in Tokyo winner, Anthony Cicero Stecchino Jr., better known as Tony Stecchino, was killed in a fire in his home outside of Rome, Italy, late last night. Stecchino sent a letter to GW proclaiming his apologies for every wrong doing that he has done in and around Global and hopes that all those he has hurt can move on with their lives and forgive him.

Tony leaves no behind no family. He was the last of his tortured family to perish. He was 26.

Tony Stecchino
August 15, 1979 to December 22, 2005

Global's Foundation Rocks

Global Wrestling regrets to announce that Dawn McFarlane, Shadow, Virgil Ambrosi, Emoticon, Christian Zenith, and more have decided to part ways with GW. There is an open door policy for them and we pray that one day they decide to return. For a full story, please read the NewsWire.

In the wake of this, the Board of Directors would like to offer this Irish Proverb in concerns to the foundations of GW being rattled.

There's no need to fear the wind if your haystacks are tied down.

We believe that our haystacks are definitely tied down.
