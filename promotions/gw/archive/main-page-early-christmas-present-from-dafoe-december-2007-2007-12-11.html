Early Christmas present from Dafoe


-11-12-2007-

In a shocking night where the two owners tried to out do each other, the entire federation was almost put into shock when Dafoe revealed his secret opponent to face Salaok.

Not only did Dafoe get the upper hand on Salaok with that match, but the entire night, Dafoe had Salaok's number and revealed his team for Showdown in Tokyo.

To find out who will represent Global at Showdown, tune in to Fusion!

Showdown World title match named!

On last weeks Fusion, Jean Claude dafoe did something that no one was expecting when he revealed that the Main Event at Fusion was actually to name the Number One contender to Darke's World title.

Thus Digital Morality will be facing Christian Darke again for the World title at Showdown in Tokyo which if only a few short weeks away.

Rumors have it that there is going to be a change in the way the PPV is run this year, but no one has released full details as to what these changes may be.

All that is known is Darke Vs Digi round two is guaranteed!


-11-12-2007-
Global to crown a new champion?

That’s right soon the Rising Sun championship will have an owner. This title will indeed gain its birth at Showdown In Tokyo. But who will fight whom to gain this brand new title to Global?


-11-12-2007-
Fusion heading your way!

What will be the fall out of Dark Rituals? With many of Global Stars amongst the walking wounded who is left to fight. And whom will they fight? Check out the card line-up for all the details of this coming Fusion.


-11-12-2007-
Inter-promotional match up?

Global International Champion DDO, to take on WfWA’s World champion “Irish Fire” David Paige soon. Word is this happened though the online wrestling magazine called EWzine. A challenge was issued by DDO and picked up By Paige. Rumor has it that the two will fight soon. And maybe even on a coming edition of Fusion. Other reports say it will be held in a undisclosed location. Currently David page is indeed a free agent. And is the current WfWA World champion. WfWA is a confederation of independent federations representing regions. Paige is that combined body’s champion but has no home fed. Rumors are that Global officials have tried to get Paige to sign to Global. No word from Global or Paige is this is true. We will update you more as the information comes to light.
