Heru Tenshi  aka  "The Fallen Angel"  Brain Welch The dark hero. Dark and Sinister but always found his way fighting for the light. His life intertwined with that of Johnathan Davis, and Salaok, Often at odds with his own Brother. After the death of Telena, His beloved Bride to be, Brian faded into the sunset of Japan.
