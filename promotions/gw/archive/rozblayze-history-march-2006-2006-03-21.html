Rozblayze — history (March 2006)

21 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Roz grew up in a broken home. Alaways being told he was neve going to amount to any thing. Alaways being beaten and thrown out. At the age of seventeen he bagen to Wrestle for the UWF. After a Hardcore title match at a PPV his back was broken in a car accident. Roz fought back from his near crippling injury and came to the OCW after a short stint of winning only one match he got burned out and took a vacation. On his return he went back to his loseing ways and decided to quit the OCW all together. Going to many other odd places he found quite a home with the SMW. His first few match in were a bit hard but he soon fell into his place. He began to work his ass off and won a Hardcore title shot. After munchign the Hardcore champion. He went after the only undefeated man in SMW Histtory. Apoun beating him he became a superstar. Then Roz went on to become double champion by holding the streetfight and United states chmapionships at the same time. Then a great loss hit him. Breaking his right knee. He was told that he should never wrestel again. After healing and relearning to walk Roz took back to the ring and found the OCW once again. after going 4-1-0 His knee got busted bad and the OCW let him go. After healing he found a home with the ICWF. Walking stright into a match with Lurr his first outing wasent so good. But he worked on it and after his next match he began to win. Winning his next four matches. Then the Insain Rumble came along and Roz lasted about 31 minunites of the hour. and keep on in the ICWF untill it's last show. The October PPV. Were he lost to Salvio Syn in an Europen title match. He went on to the CWa and won the hardcore championship after this. Now he is Back in the GW hopeing to make it Big.
