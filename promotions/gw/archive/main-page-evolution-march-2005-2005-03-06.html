Evolution.


"The time to evolve has came once again, as the flagship show of Gloal hits the airwaves with an impact. On tonights show we would see Christian Zenith forking out cash for a new door, Train and Brick come face to face, again! Not to mention that it seemed as if everyone who had a legitimate claim to World title contention met with Bubba McCoy, but that wasn't even half of the action. The Platinum title tournament rages on, the Televison and Cruiserweight title were up for grabs and as for the the mystery main event? Well, it's not like we're going to give that away here, tune in to Evolution, airing only on HBO!"

2/15/05 EVOLUTION

Happy Valentine's Day... GLOBAL style. The scent of love was in the air when Train took on two lovely ladies, are they violent vixens or did they just want some love? Train made it his mission to find out, and you will too.

Christian Zenith and Ken Partridge Jr. & Co. lock horns... but with Salaok as referee? The Harvester of Minds don't wear stripes, but he will wear the power... seems like he has another accessory in mind, one that belongs to a certain "Superstar."

Last but not least don't forget about the PLATINUM series kicking off from The Outback! With Corvus' Television Championship on the line against TWO opponets, Brick had his chance to taste gold once again, but could he get past Myers' looking for a double championship? All the questions are answered inside... EVOLUTION!

2-7-05 EVOLUTION

Evolution has once again hit the airwaves and the atmosphere could be described by two words... GLASS DUNGEON.

A new Television Champion was crowned, but who could it be? The debut of Hessian, Leinad Soma and Train's return to Evolution, A night full of tension, fight, blood, and gore... how can you resist? You know you want to... just take a look, and behold EVOLUTION in all its glory.
