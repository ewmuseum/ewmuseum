Overload, 20 June 2003

20 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Overload Logo smashes onto the screen engraved in iron, the sounds of drills and saw burst into the background and sparks fly as the logo falls away leaving Trista Romano standing at the Overload studio all smiles. Trista: Welcome to another action packed night that will simply OVERLOAD your senses! We have an awesome night of Global Wrestling action for you and to get things started off we have the explosive Dynamite X taking on the quick man known as Blitz! Let's get it started! The camera backs up the slams hard into a T.V. screen already into the match, Dynamite has Blitz about the throat with a chokehold raising him up off his feet and suspending him over his head while the ref begins to count for the release. 1..2..3.. X drops him to the mat and follows up with a huge splash, but Blitz rolls aside just in time and pops up to his feet, he bounces off the ropes and meets an already standing Dynamite X with a jump flying kick that staggers the bigger man, dropping low Blitz sweeps Dyanmites feet out from under him and continues the assault. Brandi: This is an amazing clash of styles here, Blitz, the fast and more technical men of the two now taking the advantange using style over brute force and violence. Mark: But where is the fun in that? I WANT TO SEE VIOLENCE! Blitz goes for the pin by Dyanmite powers out tossing Blitz to the center and coming up and at him like a freight train stomping over Blitz's fallen form. He grabs him by the head and wrenches him to his feet locking him in a headlock and clenching it in hard. Brandi: Blitz really struggling at that move, it looks like Dynamite X is doing his best to control the pace of this match and it appears strangling his opponent seems to be the best way to do this. Mark: This is more like it, I wanna see someone die! Brandi: MARK! X drops Blitz with a DDT that shakes the ring, both men are on there feet quickly though and a quick side kick towards the face of Dyanmit, but X catches the foot and drops to one knee driving his fist hard into Blitz's stomach. Blitz keels over leaving perfect oppertunity for Dynamite to move in and hook him up into and slam him down with. Mark and Brandi: THE XTERMINATOR! Brandi: And theres the cover, 1..2..3! The scene fades away to Trista standing and watching the closing moments of the match, she turns back to the camera but before she can say anything the scene shifts, Ronnie Long and Vortex are brawling in the parking lot and Ronnie Long seems to have the upper hand before a blind sided swing by Vortex with a two-by-four tears Ronnie out of his boots, but the excursion is broken up when security arrives short after. Trista: What a match huh? And followed by what appeared to be quiet a violent brawl... Who would have seen that kind of outcome? But if you thought that match was great, get ready because we've got something that'll knock your socks off. Two men who have there history at opposite corners and one man caught in between, whats going to happen when Ice, Christopher Canada and Angus the Bull all meet in one ring? Let's find out! The scene jumps to a T.V. on the opposite side and the first sight is both Ice and Angus throwing punches that collide with either side of Chris Canada's head sending the Canadian out of the picture for now as the other men step aside and back up. They begin to circle eachother, there eyes locked and intent on the big picture. They come together locking up, Angus using power to push Ice down and tuck him under for a powerbomb, but Ice counters with a backdrop. He turns standing face to face with the already risen Angus, Ice goes for a clothsline, Angus ducks and counters with a clothsline of his own but Ice doesn't go down and instead boots Angus in the stomach hooking his waist for a huge suplex in the center of the ring. Brandi: It looks like Christopher has slid out of the ring and is biding his time to nurse that headache he must have now while Ice uses all his knowledge to keep ahead of Angus in an attempt to even the score between the two. Mark: As long as one or the other bleeds I'm ok. Ice brings Angus to his feet but The Bull doesn't come nicely and grabs Ice up by the legs and pulls him over his shoulders running across the ring and tossing him into the ropes causing him to bounce off and meet the roaring fist of Angus, knocking Ice clean off his feet and causing a trickle of blood to run from his lips. Mark: YES! Angus looking satisfied with himself walks slowly over to the side of the ring, placing his hands on the ropes and raising his boot slowly inch by inch, aiming it right over the face of Ice before... Brandi: Angus is now dropping any manner of style and has begun to lay into Ice's face with boot after boot after boot and the ref is trying to drag Angus back, he's beginning the count. 1..2... Angus stops at the two and grips Ice by the legs dragging him out, he spreads Ice's legs and drives a knee into his stomach, Ice writhes in pain in the center of the eight sided ring while Angus takes his time circling to the head of his only real opponent. He grabs Ice by the hair and yanks him to his feet, he wrenches him over but Ice blocks and using amazing power hooks Angus into a gutwrench and drops him into a powerbomb! Brandi: That must have taken all the wind out of Angus, but then again from the looks of Ice it must have done the same to him as well. Mark: Look at Ice though! He's bleeding! Man this is great, It's awesome! Brandi: Your sick. Mark: You have syphallis. SMACK! Angus pulls himself to his feet by dragging at the just in reach ropes but Ice has recovered much faster and rushes in for a front drop kick right to the spine of The Bull, he rolls back up onto his feet and grabs the back of The Bull's head pulling him up into the air with a german suplex but spinning halfway into it and dropping him in a modified shoulder breaker that causes Angus to immediately grab for his shoulder. Brandi: Amazing move by Ice! Ice raises his arms before turning back to the assault, he pulls up Angus legs and turns him over locking in a lackluster boston crab that Angus is easily able to get out of by pulling himself to the ropes. Ice backs off letting Angus come to his feet and both men step to the center of the ring each glaring into the others eyes. Mark: All's quiet on the front... Until they explode, rights, lefts, lefts and rights punch after punch, they don't even both blocking the other, they go with the theory that if you hit the other person harder and more then they'll cave in first and they attack the theory with enthusiasm, blow after blow is landed on the other until both men's faces are black and blue, Angus catches one of Ice's fist before driving his fore head right into his nose causing blood to explode from Ice as he staggers backwards giving The Bull all the room he needs for.. Brandi: BULLDOZER! BULLDOZER ON ICE! Angus covers for the pin, 1..2... Brandi: NO! Chris Canada has broken up the pin and is trying to drag Angus off, Angus is angry and explodes, BULLDOZER on Chris Canada! Angus covers again on Chris, 1..2.. Ice though has recover and slams en elbow across Angus's back, he pulls him to his feet for THE HANDS DOWN! Mark: And theres the cover 1..2 NO! Angus has the ropes! Ice is furious and has just leaped across and pulled Chris up int THE HANDS DOWN! Another cover 1..2..3! He gets the pin just before Angus could get over there, Angus though still angry picks Ice up and drives him down with another Bulldozer! This is amazing! This is awesome IT RULES ASS! Fans: GW! GW! GW! GW! The lights suddenly go out as “Little Green men” by Project 86 is heard inside the arena. A spotlight falls on the entrance as Salomane Vation walks out from behind the curtain, his white robe floating out behind him revealing the primestar title around his waist. He stands on top of the ramp, staring down at the three men inside the ring. The lights stay off, apart from the spotlight he stands in. A technical guy hands him a mic, as Salomane holds it up to his mouth, his music stops. Salomane Vation: The time has come for me to strike up an army…An army that is strong enough to battle the force of evil that I have been warned about. I have been told who to select from a power higher than any of you shall ever know, and the first man is currently standing in that ring right now. Once I have finished I shall make it clear as to whom that one person is…and once I do they have until Monday night on Evolution to either accept or reject their calling. He throws down the mic, and begins to walk down to the ring, avoiding all the fans that want their hands tagged by him. He constantly stares at the three men inside the ring as he climbs up the ring steps, and leans under the second rope into the ring. Once he gets into the ring he looks at the three faces of the men standing there in front of him. First at Christopher Canada, then at Ice, and then at Angus the Bull. He walks up to Angus and grabs him around the throat with his hand as the crowd start shouting and cheering. He stares at Angus’ face, but suddenly lets go and big boots Christopher Canada from the ring, he then grabs Ice around the neck and lifts him high in the air brining him down in a hard chokeslam. He looks back at Angus again, before picking up a mic that has been rolled into the ring. Salomane Vation: Monday Night… With that “Little Green Men” by Project 86 begins to play as Salomane exits the ring, and walks back at the ramp. Not looking back at Angus. The scene returns to Trista Romano. Trista: An amazing match, with an amazing ending, I doubt we'll be seeing the last of those men and one man I doubt we'll ever see the last of is Humane as he took on Kid Flash in a match that well... you'll just have to see for yourself. Humane had just ducked under a calf kick and with a bizzare display of wrestling a sick look of glee he flings the man up and over in some form of a german suplex sneding him across the ring and crashing into the canvas. Humane follows up with a move that could be characterized as running and well falling. Brandi: Of course this is another display of Humanes bizzarre wrestling style... Humane, gets to his feet and against falls over crashing across Kid Flash's ribs all the while having a smile on his face. Flash rolls out of the ring but isnt safe as Humane steps over the ropes and is about to leap when he loses his balance ever so slightly and falls with all his weight on the Kid's head both men falling into the ground outside the ring. Mark: All I know is that retard better not come closer to me, I might just catch its stupid. Brandi: Shouldn't you be saying that the other way around? Mark: What's that supposed to mean? Brandi: What you think it means! Humane has recovered and tosses Kid Flash into the ring, he rolls in after him but Kid has recovered and bounces off the ropes driving his feet in a drop kick straight to the knees of Humane and Humane falls like a tree shaking the ring as he slams down face first and just lays there. Kid Flash is hesitant to continue and moves slowly over to the fallen nut ball, he taps him once, taps him twice and then decides to roll him over only to have his head grabed and pulled down, Humane rolls him over and uses his sheer bulk to go for a pin. 1...2 Brandi: Almost a three count but somehow Kid Flash is able to get a shoulder up. Humane though pulls him up and presses him into the air, a look of compete detachment from reality he has begun to run around the ring in a very familiar ritual... Humane: WHEEEEEEEEEE!!!! Mark: And theres the drop... ouch, Kid Flash not moving as Humane pins him... 1...2..3 Humane is the winner... errrr yay? As Humane celebrates the crowd goes wild and Trista Romano watches from the Overload nerve center. Trista: This next match was something completely different from the one Humane would enjoy... Ace Cash and Trey Reed collide in the air with a double flying clothesline, both hit the ring with a thud and both get back to there feet and sprint to opposite sides leaping up and springboarding off the top spinning in mid air, Trey hits hard through with a flying kick coming over Ace Cash's move. Both hit the canvas though and it takes the wind out of each man, Trey is at his feet though and the Last Action Hero is in prime fighting condition as he brings Ace to his feet and takes him over with a belly to belly followed by a standing moonsault. He goes for the pin but only receives a two count, he goes for another moonsault by Ace is out of there by the time it lands and has already made it to one of the many turnbuckles jumping to the top with all his flair and wearing a cocky grin as he comes off clenching his body together in a ball before releasing all his power in a flipping torpedo drop kick just as Trey is standing. Mark: That move sure connected! I think I see Trey's head over in England! Brandi: And Ace sure seems happy with his usual flair, he stands in the center of the ring for a moment and poses, flexing his muscles and sporting his pearl white teeth... man he is dreamy... He doesn't show boat long as he is back to buisness rolling across Trey's mid section with a knee, an elbow and summersault, he goes to the top and comes off with a huge leg drop but The Last Action Hero pops up just in time and Ace lands hard, his face going wide as his hands shoot to his rear and the crowd goes wild! Brandi: Amazing match between these two! Trey pulls Ace to his feet and then drops him again with a toe-hold, gripping Ace by his braids he delivers a reverse suplex and follows it up by sprining off the second rope and dropping an elbow straight to the heart of cash. He goes for the pin but only gets another two count as Cash kicks up and comes to his feet, Trey goes for a clothsline and Cash ducks, he places his hands on the hips and smiles to the audience before driving a quick side kick to the jaw of Trey. Mark: Trey catches the foot though and a tackle to the legs takes this otherwise air based match to the ground, Trey tries for a leg lock but Ace's expierence allows for him to be at the ropes and out of the hold in seconds and both men are up again and ready. Ace goes for a high spin kick, Trey goes for a low sweep, they both miss there mark and each one tries the opposite move, they back off and Ace leaps forward hooking Treys waist and barreling him over coming up to his feet with Trey on his back. Ace is at his feet and hooks his leg a quick catapult sends Trey flying and drops him into the center of the ring. Ace sees it as a perfect oppertunity and leaps to the top, he points to Trey and points to the crowd. Brandi: CASHFLOW ITS THE CASHFLOW! HE'S GOING TO HIT THE CASHFLOW! Mark: Jesus woman, he hasn't even left the turnbuckle, he's still playing to the crowd. Brandi: Ohhhh... he's so awesome.... And Ace leaps, going for the Cashflow but having his face met by an upraised boot from Trey Reed shocking him with complete surprise and causing Ace to fall flat on his back and hard. Trey is at the turnbuckle and doesn't even bother going to the top, he leaps from the second with the... Brandi: NO NO NO MUTUAL PLUNGE! NO NO NO NO! Mark: And theres the pin! 1...2...3! YES!! Looks like your little pretty boy went down! Brandi: NO! NO! NO! NO! NO! Trista is back in the Overload center shaking her head. Trista: Poor Brandi, looks like things just didn't go her way, and it looks like this last match didn't go anyones way. The scene opens instantly with two chairs smashing against eachother and Jay Robson pressing in with Mark Kullypso, each one looking for an edge over the other, Jay presses of and swings low smashing the chair against Mark's knees causing the ref to signal for the DQ win, but Jay isn't done and he lays into Mark again and again, no real target just Mark himself, hitting him with shot after shot from the chair as the scene fades away to Trista Romano, it still plays in the background the chair smashing down again and again as security piles into the ring.
