Insane Cain, Bobby Brawler, Justin Marks, Johnathon Davis and then Salaok. The numbers appear on the screen again and the final digit ticks over. 2001 More black and white pictures show up on the screen. Quartz, Derek Frost, Jay Robson, The Forever Knight, Tragic Hero, BJP, Mark Kullypso and The Fallen Angel. Their faces are soon replaced by the numbers again and like before, the last digit ticks over to mark another year. 2002 This time the faces, although still in black and white have a more modern look to the background as Silent Wind, Deathsun, Draven, Chris Camero, JJ Dynasty, Sean Price and Maverick Reno replace each other on the screen. 2003 This time instead of faces showing up on the screen, letters take their place as E…V…O…L…U…T…I…O…N rolls onto the screen. A small screen appears in the bottom left hand corner of the screen and begins to count down towards Dead Zone, live on PPV. Voice Over: Global Wrestling’s PPV event Dead Zone is due to start in just under fifteen minutes. There is still time to order the event by calling your suppliers now. However, in a lead up to this event, we have this footage to show you all. The scene opens up to a small warehouse located in Houston, Texas. Everything is quiet at this time with little to no life seen around the building or compound. Rain has just recently begun to pour from the heavens above, cleaning the grit of the metropolis from the streets and the pollution from the air. Beams of light hit the rain, reflecting small glimmers of light from the air. The headlights illuminate the asphalt as well until they're suddenly shut off. The car rolls to a stop in front of the decrepit structure, the black paint shimmering in the nights pale light. The BMW's windows are tinted to an illegal extreme, and the chrome wheels glimmer with the reflections of a nearby light post. Both the drivers and passenger side door open, a reflection moving over the door revealing shaved off handles with nothing remaining aside from a small square screen with a built in numeric keyboard underneath it. A man steps from the driver's side, dressed in all black including a black ski mask and black gloves. He closes the door and glances over the top of the vehicle towards the second man, dressed the exact same way but of a smaller stature. The back door opens and a third steps out, also dressed in all black but without the ski mask as the other two men wear, this man is no other than Deacon Holmes. "Ready?" the word escapes the lips of the man on the passenger side and the driver simply nods, pulling a steel bat from inside the black Beamer. With this, the second man nods and turns, revealing a pair of billy clubs tucked into the back of his belt. The third, Holmes, raises his fists and bangs together a pair of back knuckles, a certain look of mischief glowing in his eyes. Upon nearing the gate, we can see a chain wrapped securely around the sliding fence and the post mounted securely in the ground. A deadbolt hangs on that, keeping it secure. The first man brings the bat up, slamming the metal column against the lock, creating a loud metal clinking noise. The second shakes his head and points up while the first one nods and tosses the bat over and to the ground on the opposite side. He quickly scales the fence and drops the opposite side, landing on the ground there. The second man jumps quickly and brings his legs up, springing over the fence and landing on the opposite side, rolling to a crouch. Holmes jumps up, grabbing the top and pulls himself over and lands on his feet on the opposite side. All three men now stick to the fence, walking away from the lights as they approach the building from the eastern side. A foot step is heard from around the corner of the building and the men stop, the first slipping behind a tree and readying his bat, the second pressing his back to the wall of the warehouse, and Deacon quickly stepping back around the corner and pressing his back to the wall. A man dressed in jeans and a black t-shirt walks around, the tip of a cigarette glowing in the dark. As he approaches, the first man looks at the second, making some type of motion. With this, the second man tosses a small rock a few feet beyond the tree, causing the guard to glance and then start in that direction, drawing a gun from inside his waist. As he walks slowly towards the rocks point of impact, the second man ducks down, sticking to the shadows so that he can't be seen while the first readies himself. When he's in range, the first man uses the glowing tip of the cigarette to aim and spins from behind the tree, swinging the bat as hard as he can and slamming it into the face of the guard, sending him to the ground in a heap and completely unconscious. He glances towards the passenger and nods and starts walking back towards his destination whilst the second man presses the toe of his boot against the cigarette, putting it out. Deacon Holmes steps from the other corner and glances at the handy work of his cohorts with a hint of a snicker before stepping over and towards the first man. They quickly follow the driver. They stop once they reach the back of the warehouse, finding a window with a few loose boards. With a quick swing, the driver smashes the wood and shatters the remainder of the window, creating an entrance for himself and his accomplices. They quickly slip into the building, the second man drawing a club from his belt and preparing himself for a fight, keeping his other hand free. Footsteps are heard scattering about on the cement floor of the warehouse, the clatter heard from the trios entrance has apparently caused a bit of excitement. Men, casually dressed, begin to approach from all sides now armed with two by fours and chains. One swings a chain at the passenger and he raises his arm, letting the club make contact. He then grabs it and wraps it around his free arm, jerking him in before spinning and slamming the club to his forehead, sending him back first to the cement ground. Blood begins to seep from the nostril and lip of the man, now unmoving on the floor as others now advance. One, armed with a two by far, swings ferociously at Holmes who ducks and sends a set of hard brass punches to his midsection, raising to send one to his jaw and then finishing with an uppercut that breaks the jaw of the man opposing the trio. Quickly, the driver slams the bottom of the bat into one mans face and quickly turns, swinging it and nailing two more upside their heads. The Passenger then jumps up, grabbing a chain suspended from the ceiling and nailing two men, one in front and one in back with a set of powerful kicks, sending one man to the floor and the other through a boarded up window. He then drops from his elevated position, back handing a third man with the club as he comes down, sending him spinning back into another guard. Holmes begins using the power of his brass knuckles, nailing two men with an open back hand and a third with a spinning right, sending them back and staggering them long enough to follow up with final blows for each. Two more guards arrive on the scene and quickly advance on the driver. He tosses the bat to one of the guards and slams his fist into his face and spins, kicking the bat directly against his forehead. The second grabs at him but only to be flipped to the ground. The Driver grabs the bat from the floor and slams it across the face of the downed man. The guard who caught the man before now rushes the Passenger who quickly sidesteps and slams the club against the back of his head whilst drawing the second from his belt and brings the brunt of that one against the opposite side of his head, spinning around him to bring the first to his head again, quickly silencing him. After a quick assessment of the mess, nine guards total with seven remaining inside the warehouse. A tenth makes his appearance and rushes the three men, a fire axe in hand. Thinking quickly, both men step out of his way, the passenger dropping and spinning to sweep him while the driver slams the bat against his throat, both men hitting him at the same time. As he falls forward, Holmes finishes the vicious cycle with a hard right from his brass knuckle covered hand. He hits the floor, the axe sliding to the opposite wall. With a quick nod, they move up the narrow grate steps towards the second floor of the warehouse. Upon reaching the second floor, two paths lay before the three men, a catwalk to the opposite side of the warehouse and an office door directly ahead. "Split up" is all that was said from the Driver who quickly approached the office door. The second shrugs and quickly begins his move along the cat walk, Holmes walking just feet behind him, able to keeping an eye out both ways. We follow the passenger for a moment and see the door open at the far end, two guards rushing him. He jumps, grabbing one of the metallic cords from which the catwalk is suspended and spins around it bringing the heel of his boot across the face of one man, causing him to lose his balance and stumble to the floor of the walkway. The passenger then drops and brings one club up between the legs of the second guard, nailing him as hard as possible before slamming the second against his knee, knocking him to the ground. The first has stood again and he rolls over the man, kicking the second's knees out from under him to bring the ends of both clubs down against the back of his head, knocking him unconscious. During this interval, Holmes has had his own bits of fun. A set of two guards rushed passed the Driver and down the pathway only to catch hard rights all around. With a quick, catlike reflex, Deacon drops to one knee with the neck of one opponent in his hand, snapmar'ing him over and off the edge of the catwalk. Holmes then spins quickly and slams the knucks into the midsection of another guard only to follow up by slamming both into the man's knees, dropping him to an equal level at which point he sends him a right cross, knocking him to the ground. The third man back up slowly, having watched with fear the efficiency in which both men took down four guards. The Passenger steps up beside Deacon, their eyes meet then they both glance at the guard. He takes a few steps back and turns, running towards the exist. The passenger drops to one knee and sends a club flying, it makes contact with the back of the guards knee, knocking him to the floor with a hard thud. Deacon rushes and jumps and comes back down, slamming his fist into the forehead of the guard. He picks up the club and tosses it back to the Passenger. We now change back, following the Driver as he attempts to open the office door but finds it locked. He bashes the window and reaches inside, unlocking the door. Foot steps are heard from inside so he quickly steps to the side of the door. It swings open a man, armed with an Uzi steps out dressed in a black suit. He steps outside the door and begins looking around, spotting the Passenger in the distance. Quickly, the Driver nails him in the back of the head with the end of the bat, knocking him to the ground. He quickly steps onto his back and swings down, nailing him on the back of his head. With this, he quickly turns and makes his way into the room, only to duck instantly as a crowbar nails the door frame next to where his head once was. He quickly grabs the man about the waist and lifts him up, slamming him against the wall, causing him to drop the crowbar. The bat had been dropped before and forgotten about, as the Driver leans up he slams first one and then two more punches into the face of the guard, rendering him unconscious. The man slides down the wall, his face bruised and his lip bleeding. The Driver looks around the room, and grumbles, finding nothing of interest. He quickly rushes out and starts down the catwalk. The passenger has opened the door on the other side of the building and stepped inside along with Holmes, finding a small black safe against the far wall. Holmes crouches beside it and presses the side of his head to the front, keeping very quiet as he attempts to crack the safe. The driver walks in and quickly moves to a file cabinet and begins rummaging through the files inside. He continues, speaking aloud, "I want a peanut butter sandwich." The Passenger glances at him, giving him a strange look. With this, he pulls a photo from a file marked "Cruse," and begins studying it closely. "Will you please keep your mind on the job at hand?" are the only words the passenger says before returning to his work with the safe. "Would you both please keep it down so I can do this?" Deacon adds before going back to the safe. Within moments, Deacon has broken the combination and opens the safe door. He draws a stick from inside a pocket and breaks it, causing it to glow green and give off light. The kneels down passenger pulls a vile from inside and glances up at the driver, smiling. Both men nod and the passenger speaks again, "Lets get out of here before backup arrives, I don't want to be here when they get angry in numbers." and a nod is the response.
