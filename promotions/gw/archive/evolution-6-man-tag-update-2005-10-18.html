Evolution 6-Man Tag Update

With the recently announced 6 Man Tag Team Match between Hunting Grounds members Christian Zenith, Vivica Valentine and Dawn McFarlane facing off against Global Wrestling World Champion Mindy Mercer, Michael Mercer and Jake Hix many fans are looking at the match as a dangerous encounter.

Rumors have swirled around the backstage area about planned attacks and counterattacks before the match before what is sure to be a brutal match.

Noi Tsunami spoke with Acting President Christina Reno about the rumors.

“After reviewing what happened on our last Evolution, I am ordering all of the participants in the 6 Man Tag Team Match to stay away from persons on the other team. That is extended to include all of the members of Hunting Grounds, as well. I handed down fines and suspensions the week before, and I will not stand for any of more incidents; punishments will be severe. The fans want to see this settled in one place, the octagon, and that's where it will be settled.”

Noi Tsunami caught up with Jake Hix after the statement to get his views on the match. Jake Hix seemed more than willing to speak.

“I’m glad Christina is laying the law down. I’m waiting until we’re inside the octagon, and then I can do what needs to be done without fear of management interference. The Hunting Grounds have been running their mouth, laying people out and trying to run the whole damn show! Enough is enough!”

When asked about match strategy in a rare 6 Man Tag Team Match, Jake had this to say.

“It’s been a long time since I’ve done any tag team matches, yet alone a 6 Man Tag team Match. I’ll be working to train with Mindy and Michael this week, but in the end the result is still the same. I’m gonna slap the taste out of Zenith’s mouth, backhand Vivica out of her pumps, and then for Dawn I have something special planned. I’m going to take her four million dollar ass down, break her four million dollar ass and of course, she’s going to be my four million dollar bitch!”

Not one to get a single side, Noi also caught up to Christian Zenith. When asked about the new stipulation he had this to say.

“Why would I waste my time attacking Team Mercer when I can embarrass them in front of the entire world in the middle of the ring? That rumor is just ignorant; I have better things to do with my time. If they want to try and take another cheap shot at me that's fine... I'm sure they won't find it so easy when I'm ready for them.”

Noi also spoke with Zenith about the presence of Jake Hix in the match. Christian Zenith had some very inflammatory words for The Big Dawg.

“Jake Hix can talk about breaking bitches as much as his heart desires. He breaks down bitches and I end people's careers, you tell me who you would rather face? If you're going to bother spending the time coming up with a nifty catch phrase, the least you could do is try to make it true in the ring. He doesn't even have that going for him... take a look at the Master of the Mat Finals if you don't believe me. I think we all saw who the bitch was in that situation."

The stage is set for a brutal showdown between these six competitors on Evolution on August 29th.
