Rules (October 2004)

12 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Hello and Welcome to the Home of Global Wrestling...   Before we get to the rules of GW. I want to give you some information on GW / GU.

Global Underground.

This is where you will start your GW Rping at. We the staff grew tires of the constant apps in but nothing after the person was hired.

So Now all are required to go though the Global Underground.  Now before you scoff at this idea think of the following.

Going to GU allows Us the staff to learn about your characters Pre debut into GW Proper.  ON the Same note it allows you as a new player to get to know the members of this fed. The current storylines of the fed and See how things are done.  

Also Upon Graduating, You will heave a more impactfull debut angle then ever able to give before.  Reason is you have proved your self already by passing though GW and this is a reward for doing so. 

GU has its own rules as well so check those out on the GU website. 

Global Wrestling 

GW is a Mixed hybrid fed of Fighting Martial Arts and Wrestling.  Your character can have up to three styles of fighting...   GW is located and Based in Dallas Texas..  and it only travels on PPV's most all cards take place at the Global Arena. 

GW Is not your Average Federation, Here there is no  Guy playing the role of a gimmick.  Here every thing is real... and GW is known to shatter the reality barrier.. S0 it is plausible for a Cybernetic Ninja to fight in GW, same as a guy who is the head of the Mafia, same as a guy who is a pro wrestler  looking to make a name for his self in the business. So If some one is playing a Vampire in GW then they really are one..   Just In character, many discount that believing it to be like other federations. So Your character could not walk into the police station and have some one shot for being a Demon....  cause then he would be committed into the Psyche ward of a nearby hospital :)

Also there is such a thing I will enforce here is In Character and OOC knowledge...  IE say  I know  Guy 1's Character is a Vampire...  But unless he has told My character , My character don't know, just like the rest of the world.  

GW is highly focused on Shows, In character news. So, Send in segments...  Send in News and Rumors to promote your character [s] Also the Rping, while not very demanding at 1 - 3 rps a week, It is still very important. It helps in wining your matches, Also keeps you in the minds of the staff.  Do that and you' will get pushed better, and higher up the card.

Style of Rping.  

We ask for Novel and Prefer Novel [Story / Book style] But we will not frown on script.  If done right Script can be very good.  But in a whole We the staff feel that Novel style is the better style.  So we ask you at least give it a try.  Also there are Hybrids of the two styles that are nice as well.

All things for the cards:

Every thing in the cards are in a present tense and in script format.  

News and Rumors postings...  

These should be done From the view point of a on camera GW reporter. A Page is coming to help in that area. 
Now that you know all of this... You still wish to join  We will do our best to make GW a fun and enjoyable place for you...  But there are  some things and rules that are set down.  By Filling out the application to GW It is assumed that you have read and agreed to these rules.

Thanks,

Sincerely,

[name withheld] GW Owner.

Stating right here, GW has a Zero Tolerance policy. Meaning WE WONT TAKE ANY SHIT!. 1 mess up, the person is gone. If WE see fitting to fire this person. OUR WORD is LAW. We  try and be as Nice as We can, BUT WE have ZERO time for problems. This is meant to be fun. Lets keep it that way. Please!

Have fun. This is a game. Check your attitudes at the door. Attitudes like "I am the best and you all suck" and stuff like that will not help you here. Keep that in your role-plays. We pride my ourselves on GW being a Family type OOC feeling.. Don't mess with that. If you cant play nice then don't play here at all. 

No whining! If you have a problem with something, email The Front Office. 

Rping Rules.... There is a Role-play Cap in GW...  1 min -- 3 max for each week.

You must do AT LEAST 1 Role-plays per week for all wrestlers, whether you have a match or not. 
No In arena or In ring Rps..  No Trash Talk Rps, Save those for cards.  Role-playing is important. It helps decide contender status and pushes.  No Role-playing, No push. No matter who you are, or how good you are.  The No Show Ruling...

This gets irritating and old fast, not just for the staff but your fellow fed member who is placed against you. So with GU in operation. this is what will happen.

[1st no Show.] You will not be further booked for anything until you clear why and what happened with the Staff [ See staff page of this website.] This is a warning action.  

[2nd No Show.] You been warned by the first offence, now you shall be demoted to GU. Sorry but We cant allow this stuff to continue.  You ask us for on time results. We ask you for on time Rps. 

Now we know life can toss many a curve ball. So we will judge each situation under its own merits.  So please inform us why this had to happen.  [Consecutive  No Shows will result in this action no matter what though.]

If this happens  and you wish to remain in GW then go though GU and you'll be back. [More on this ruling to come if anything changes]

If for any reason you can't role-play any given week (illness, vacation, etc), email The Front Office or post on the OOC either prior to or immediately after the situation occurs.   [We grant time off request.. And angles can be worked out to get your character off the cards till you return]

Do not put OOC comments on the RP board. If you think someone RP sucks, help them get better, or don't say any thing. THIS RULE WILL BE ENFORCED! We will not tolerate this. It will result in you, no matter how good you are, being fired and banned from the site. 

If a card isn't posted, do not bug the writers about it. If it is late, there is a good reason for it to be late. We will try not to do that, but if something does happen, please understand. We are human and have lives to take care of, just like you. Remember this. 

Deadline Information and rules:

Role-play Deadline is always [Unless stated other wise] Sunday Midnight CST Card Segments Deadline: Sunday 6 Pm CST.  Segment Rules Please send them in a Microsoft word compatible file attached to your email.  Send to all Email addresses the Front office uses. Also Please Italic all action with [I] and [/I]  Replacing the [and ] for < and >. Also Please  Title the segment and use center and /center before and after that. 

We ask for you to state where you would like the segments to go. But reserve the right to place them where we feel best. [ DO NOT ask for opening and closing of the card. We will chose who gets to use those spots. ]

Please work out Segments involving other players with that player. So they can make sure there character is used correctly. 

Match Writers Deadline and rules. All matches due with alternate endings by Saturday midnight.  Correctly coded and htmled. More rules to be posted on the staff board.

If you have any concerns, angles, or anything in general to say about the GW or events, feel free to email The Front Office. 

We reserve the right to terminate anyone at any time. There are various offenses leading up to this, such as not RPing, racial slurs, harassing other Players, etc. You will be Suspended first, then terminated if the situation continues.
*note* We the staff will decide each case in it's own light. Don't ask or talk about the issue.

You must use ORIGINAL wrestlers. Using Real Wrestlers or Real Celebrities for your Wrestler is not permitted. (Unless We the staff choose other wise)

We're going for creativity here. Straight up racial slurs or any other seriously nasty comments will not be tolerated in role-plays. You will get one warning. If you do not learn from this, you will be terminated. Also, see the above No OOC post on the RP boards rule.  You're not here to upset people. You're here to have fun. 

Do not use another Player's wrestler, manager or characters if you don't have permission.  Same goes for the on-air staff as well. Get our permission first. Some are cool to use others may be used by some one. 

IE Bubba McCoy. He is Handled by [name withheld] time to time in other feds and even here in GW. 

Use you common sense and judgment. You know what is in bad taste and what is not.

There is a CHARACTER Exclusivity rule in GW...  This means YOU can have your character in GW and no where else... Make a new character to join other feds... But the GW character is in GW till he/she leaves..

Titles must be defended at least once every two weeks.. We will pick the contenders and give them a shot. If the Champ refuses to defend he will be stripped of his title and suspended. 

Remember We do this for fun, BUT we want to still be one of the Best federations on the NET. 

And once again HAVE FUN DAMNIT!!!   You may now go to the application...  Remember we deem you know these rules and there is no ability to say I DID NOT KNOW!!! It's here in plain text.
