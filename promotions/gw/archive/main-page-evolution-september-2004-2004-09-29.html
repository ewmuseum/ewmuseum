Evolution


Evolution has hit the fiber-optics once again.

HBO proudly presents Evolution on Tuesday, September 21st. The night started with a blood curdling scream as the lights went out in Texas.

What the hell am I talking about? A fearsome fourway for final ... drats, what's an 'f' word for victory... darnit... oh well. A fourway dance started off the evening with a frantic fury!

The night barreled forward with a ferocious tag team match featuring the Flying Vergomovz and Ruckus Inc. Who would come out on top and who would fly sadistically through the air with the greatest of ease? (... maybe that's self explanitory).

The Television dance began and it ended with ... well we have no clue. Damned Emergency Broadcast System. But ... YEAH!

Mac and Myers go head to head again but uh oh, more monkeys and their trapeze! Next we'd find GSA and The Elite battling head to head for dominance in the Tag Team Brackets. Who wins and who loses? Who cries and who ... um... yells in triumphant victory!? Also, four people leave the roster and two are remembered forever? What does this mean?


Evolution hit the fiber-optics tonight with a bang. Donny McNasty and Train would headline the night with their pay per view blood-fest. What happened in the circle of vehicles?

Superstars would meet head to head in the Global Octagon. Who would come out on top in several situations? Who reigns as Global's Television Champion?

1,2 buckle my shoe? And what the hell is momento mori all about? Check it all out on HBO!

Whiplash Airs!


Global Whiplash has hit the airwaves only on PPV!

What will happen? What will go down? Who will walk out of the Cruiser Battle Royal as the new Cruiser Weight Champion? Will be it be a Global vet or a Global Rookie? After this, who will be the vets and who will be the rookies?

Surpises everywhere? Messages were sent but who received them!? Only one way to find out...

A new International Champion! But who? The epic battle continues between Brick and Sage Sabbot on Whiplash!

Juan Gonzalez defends his title against Venneman Pendragon, but how well? Can he hold his own against the epitome of dragons and castles?

And to top it all off, Josh and the Monster battle head to head for the coveted World Championship. Will Josh continue his winning streak or will Leinad Soma prove his prowess by stripping the champion?

Also note, the Platinum Title Match will air on Evolution, September 13th instead of on the pay per view due to technical difficulties.

Evolution has hit the airwaves.


Everyone's favorite television show is back and looked just as good as ever, despite the delay caused by construction on the Global Arena. The card was fully loaded as we kicked things off with a red hot three way dance to crown the valedictorian of GU and springboard their career into greater heights. Would Rich Sherwin prove to be better than ever as he made his return or would the up and coming Adam Myers take the victory? And last but not least, how would the underdog Kitty Mac fare in this athletic contest?

Also, would Train and Brick be able to co-exist or would they implode? And on the same lines would Josh and Pierce be able to hold it together or would they also be at one anothers throats?

These are just a very few of the highlights as we also saw Donny J. McNasty show his truck driving skills, two roster members were stripped of Gold and the most important question of the night...who would walk away from Evolution as our newly crowned Tag Team Champions?
