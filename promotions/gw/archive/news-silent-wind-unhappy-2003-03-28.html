Silent Wind Unhappy?

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Silent Wind Unhappy? It would seem that the new World Champion of GW is not happy about the way in which he has found himself holding the title belt. After being handed the belt on Evolution after co-owner Salaok fired Maverick Reno, Silent Wind says he feels cheated out of earning the belt. And has stated he will let Salaok know this in his own way very soon. As all know Salaok is a long time fired of Silent Winds father the monster known as Quartz. One has to wonder if Quartz had anything to do with Salaok's decision. Nonetheless fact remains is that GW has a very unhappy and angry champion. Silent Wind is a big time player in Global Wrestling and the management is trying there hardest to make sure everything turns out right in the end. More on this to come, stay tuned.
