Reginald Atkins — history (September 2007)

3 September 2007

History

Not much is known on Atkins' past before his fifteenth birthday. There were no records to his schooling, health report, and even his birth certificate is missing. However, there is no denying he exists. Just ask Lyell Freivogel, his childhood friend. There's no mistaking the damage the two of them caused together.

It was around his aforementioned fifteenth birthday that Reginald Atkins met Jett. Both Jett, a tall and strong German lad, and Atkins were sent to live in a mansion in Connecticut because their families were having trouble dealing with them. The attendant of the mansion was Yancey Jetters, a New England millionaire who took on charity cases for media's sake. The two were mostly left alone to do whatever they wished, as Jetters had no intent of actually parenting the two.

Whatever they wished was to cause mischief. Together, Atkins and Freivogel found joy in damaging animals, property, and other children. As the years went by, the degree of damage grew. By the time they were nearing twenties, Freivogel and Atkins had changed from stealing milk money to robbing and abusing pedestrians.

When these incidents became too frequent, they were traced back to Jetters, who was not concerned about his boys' behavior. However, one of Jetters' close friends, JD Carter, saw something in the two's wanton destruction. As the owner of a small independent wrestling company, Carter asked Jetters if he could get the two to be part of the roster. Once everything was finalized, Freivogel (who at the request of Carter changed his last name to Jett) and Atkins joined the IWE under the name Enemies of the State.

In their five months there they never lost a match, winning the IWE Tag Championships and defending them successfully before the IWE closed down due to Carter's bankruptcy. In the years since the folding of IWE, Atkins has been training with Jett's help, looking to find another place to feed the hunger he found at IWE. Many places have folded upon his entrance, and now he is looking for a steady place to fight with Jett as his manager.
