The Hixes executed a double-team powerbomb on Vivica, but before a cover could be executed, Virgil and Shadow intercepted, splitting the pair and continuing in the battle. Emoticon and Zenith battled briefly, only for Emoticon to be taken down with a reverse DDT from Valentine, and Black to snap Zenith over with a German Suplex for two. Shadow soon attempted the Obtenebrate on the recently downed Pitt, only to be caught around the throat by Hix and dropped with a thunderous choke slam for a very very close two count. Finally, the ranks began to thin. Virgil took Emoticon down with a hard roundhouse kick to the side of the head, only to be speared from the ring by Pitt Hix. Zenith and Jake tackled one another to the floor, and Vivica did her level best to hold off Xavier Black as the fight spilled outside. This allowed Shadow to take advantage of the situation, springing spryly to the top rope and flying off with her Levin Bolt Phoenix Splash, crash-landing across the prone Emoticon, and hooking the leg, securing the cover for the one, two, three. Result: Zenith/Valentine/Shadow/Ambrosi def.
