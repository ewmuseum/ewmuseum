Main page: Tokyo was set ablaze as Global had its last PPV of the (January 2005)

12 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.


Tokyo was set ablaze as Global had its last PPV of the year, SIT was a spectacular event, Fighters trying to last till the end to become the third man to go to Reload to fight for the World title. Who was that man? Could Jesse Jamster repeat last year’s performance and win the whole show? Which Teams came out on top? And what was up with Dinkler? Check out the latest PPV Results to find out.

Evolution, November 15, 2004

And once again, Evo hit the airwaves with as much pomp and circumstance as a high school graduation, or maybe not. There was however, more than one declaration of war. Who got beat down? And who had their hands raised in victory? Tune in to Evolution to find out, only on HBO... and only from Global!
Evolution November 8, 2004

Evo hit the airwaves with smoke, and sparks tonight. Technical difficulties aside, it was one hell of a ride. Who would be deemed worthy in the first round of the tournament? Who would be named for Showdown in Tokyo teams? And finally, would Zenith be able to hang on to the gold? Tune in to Evolution to find out, only on HBO, and only from Global!
Evolution, November 1, 2004

Evolution hit the airwaves with nothing short of a big bang suitable for a nuclear blast! The fallout was everywhere! Just who got burned?! And who was on the receiving end of some of the nights biggest upsets?! Tune in to Evo to find out! Only on HBO! And only from Global!
