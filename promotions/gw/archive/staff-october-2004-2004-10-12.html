Global Wrestling Staff

Global Owner: Bubba McCoy Bubba a veteran fighter  bought GW as it was being sold off by Blade Entertainment.  Bubba then a GW Color commentator and GW talent relations Coordinator saw a chance to make a difference and a change in this business...  SO HE took control and crafted GW in his vision. Combining the hard nosed  in your face style of fighting promotions with the  Glory and Superstardom of Wrestling. The combination has been interesting to say the least. 

No longer just another wrestling permutation  GW started growing in credibility in more then just the Pro Wrestling world but legitimate Sports programs as well... 

Bubba is currently in control of GW and things seam to be going good.   

Global Vice President: Unknown Recently a new voice has rose up. Hire before Christina McCoy's untimely release from the front off ice by her father.  This New VP has been causing all kinds of problems. None know who this person is, not even Bubba. Rumors are rampant though, from Jean Claude Dafoe to Mark Bishop have been named to this position on internet fan sites.  Who is this VP and what is this VP's agenda...

Evolution Commentary Team: Brandi Sights - Logan Creed - Mark Bishop:

Mark Bishop:  Mark is a slime ball of a person.  Very Greedy, but at the same time he lack the power and strength to back of his mouth... this causes him a lot of trouble... It is then Bishop gets stronger people to do his dirty work.  Mark has a love of Women [Even if they don't have one of him] Gambling and Violence and blood shed.

Brandi Sights: Brandi is a virtual Wrestling and Fight databank.  Her knowledge of the sport lets her call matches right down the middle.  Brandi at times has let her emotions get the better of her.. Hot tempered and hot blooded.   Alexis known for a long time as the Queen of gossip  is not afraid to let her mouth be heard...  

Logan Creed: With The job as third commentator open Bubba McCoy has decided to place his long time friend and former Manager Logan Creed into the announce chair. Logan is a former pro wrestler in Japan. Don't let his small size cause you to under estimate him. Logan was not called the hardcore dwarf for nothing. He is tough as nails and has a foul temper.  Logan once upon a time served as Commissioner for GW.

Other Staff: Reporters | Referees | Production Workers | Other Staff

Steven Dinkler: [Head GW Reporter] 

Dinkler is the constant Professional to a fault. He been the main reporter for GW since say one.  Strait to the point and  a perfectionist, Dinkler is the man for the job. Dinkler expects and only excepts the best. 

Alexis Steel:   Alexis has been in Global for a long time. Briefly was co commentator of Evolution. She is known to be  a big time rumor starter but often times those rumors have a base of truth to them.  Many label her a Air head and not cut out for this business, but she always has the big stories and inside dirt on many a Star in Global. 

Gunner Addison: 

The only female to grace the GW Hall of Fame. Gunner is a former Multiple GW champion but now is Part time valet to Ely Braxton and a backstage reporter.  Very sharp and very beautiful. Gunner knows the ins and outs and is not intimidated by many she interviews. 

Noi Tsunami: 

Asian Female Reporter, Knows the ins and outs of the sport. Very Smart. has interviewed many a start in Japan before being hired on at Global. Some say Noi is a bit to aggressive when it comes to getting the story, but she normally lands some good ones. 

Jack Wolfe: [Head Senior Referee] 

Wolfe is a old dog of the sport. He is a grizzled former fighter and wrestler, he has been in many a fight  and knows how heated fights can get.  Wolfe is one to just let the fight go but will call it right down the middle. Not much for the rule book and so the fights he refs normally tend to get crazy. 

Brad Jones: [Referee] 

Jones is allot like Wolfe in the fact he don't enforce the rules. In fact all he considers his self there is to decide a winner.  Often times though he is distracted and talking to the ladies around ringside.  It has been rumored that Jones is susceptible to bribery but no proof has ever been found to  make this true.

Dave Simmons: [Referee] 

Simmons is a young and rising Ref. Originally his planned field of employment was being a personal trainer, but he landed a job in global. He is very professional and  the rules are strictly enforced in matches he referees, a stickler for rules and regulations. 

Jimmy Smash: [Referee] 

Smash is well not that bright.  Almost dimwitted. It is said Jimmy is often under the influence of something [We wont say what] Most times he misses the  pin falls and is lucky he can count to three. Many say he only got the job cause he is related to Brandi Sights.
