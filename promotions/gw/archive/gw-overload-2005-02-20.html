Overload Global Arena: Las Vegas Las Vegas, Nevada ---- Deathsun Starts The Show GW Reporter Noi Tsunami stands in the lobby awaiting the start of Overload, the new weekly GW show. A few of the stars pass by but she shows them no interest, she’s waiting for one person….DeathSun. Her sultry looks and long legs attract a few of the crew and others but they are ignored. They must have hit it off as she waits for him even after an unspectacular loss last week to Corvus of all people. She begins to look a bit discouraged as time passes but after a few more moments a locker room door opens and her face lights up a bit as DeathSun comes in to view. He’s dressed in an Armani suit and wearing some ruby lensed Gargoyles. He walks with a purpose, a bit discouraged from the past few weeks, but too confident to let it show. He sees her a smirks, not the bragging smirk he usually has, but a smirk reserved for someone he actually is glad to see DeathSun – Hello Noi, what can I do for you? Surely I’m not worthy of a story or anything after losing to Count Chocula. Noi - laughs You are always worthy of a story Michael-san, one loss does not tarnish all you have accomplished. DeathSun – If you say so, beautiful…I don’t find myself very interesting right now. My opponents are much more interesting, first a vampire and now a devil worshipping murderer. Maybe I am in the wrong business… Noi - laughs again No, they are…this is supposed to be fighting, wrestling, not carnival. DeathSun – Very well put my lovely…but perhaps I must adapt to my surroundings, become the person I once left behind. The person Salaok and Quartz knew very well…the person I hate to see in the mirror for I fear the tales the reflection wishes to tell me. Tales of a life long gone, but waiting below the surface, scratching and clawing to escape. You go and tell the rest of GW that the games are about to begin…and everyone knows you don’t play games with the man who makes the rules…. Get Ready GW, the real DeathSun is here. Noi begins to speak but DeathSun simply walks away. She watches as he goes, a look of desire in her eyes. A similar look is returned by Michael maybe with hunger added to it…but he doesn’t act on it, simply walking away for now. ---- OVERLOAD BEGINS.............AGAIN! Slowly the picture fades in to a studio setting, which consists of a small wooden desk with a light grey cloth as the backdrop. Across the grey backdrop in a metallic blue is the word OVERLOAD.
