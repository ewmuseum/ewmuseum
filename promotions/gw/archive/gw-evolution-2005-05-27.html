GLOBAL WRESTLING PRESENTS E V O L U T I O N May 27, 2005 From Las Vegas, Nevada ---- ---- Arriving With Gold In Hand The crowd following the huge Pay-Per-View was packed into the arena. The energy from the fans for such a huge Evolution Card was amazing. The electricity seemed to fill the entire arena even down to the back to the parking lot. The limousine pulled into the closed space and parked at the curb. John stepped out of the driver’s side of the long, black limousine. He moved to the back and opened the door for the man inside. The behemoth stepped out wrapped in a wool, pinstriped suit of light gray coloring paired with a blue striped button down shirt. Of course, for a man of his unusual dimensions and size the shirt had to be custom made, but he didn’t mind the price for perfectly fitted suits. A duffel bag in his left hand, he held his newly won prize in his right. The Global Wrestling Television Title was now his; lock stock and cash on the barrelhead. He planned to never let the belt go, unless he was replacing it was something even better in the long run. Jake Hix nodded at John, thankful to him as always for the faithful service as his driver. In the coming weeks his home would be completed, and he would be driving himself unless there was some special occasion. Mike the Security Guard was stationed at his post as always. He smiled at the Big Dawg as he walked up to the door. Mike: “Jake! Congratulations on the title win!” Jake took the congratulations with a smile. Jake Hix: “Thanks Mike! Looking forward to the first night of defending the title. How’s the crowd tonight?” Mike: “Packed and ready.” Jake Hix: “Train’s party?” Mike: “The talk of the backstage gossips. Some huge bash.” Jake Hix: “I may have to check that out.” Mike: “Have a good night.” Jake pulled the door open and headed inside the arena. He could hear the crowd inside the arena, cheering and making noise for the show. He walked down the hallway, nodding and smiling as he made his way to the assigned dressing room. He slipped inside, where he was all by himself. Jake set the duffel bag inside his locker. The television title was coming along with him for the evening. Jake walked over to the mirror, and gave himself a quick once over. He pulled his hair back into a ponytail, and tied it with a black rubber band. He headed back out of the locker room to find the party. He liked the idea of a nice party before the night began. A little drink, a little dancing and maybe even some female companionship would set a great tone for the evening. Not knowing where the party was being held, Jake wondered the halls and made some subtle inquiries. Finding out what he needed, Jake was making his way when a voice from the past beckoned him out of his march to fun. Unknown Voice: “They told me I could find you roaming around her somewhere. Who would have thought an antique like you would be let back into the ring with the young lions. Long time no talk Hix.” He stopped, realizing who the voice speaking was without even turning around. He spoke with unmasked contempt. Jake Hix: “Better than being some slimy bastard crawling around on the ground looking for sludge. Who let you back out of the swamp Reno?” Jake Hix turned around and saw exactly what he expected. Tacky white polyester suit with a bright, baby blue shirt with huge collars. The balding head and ugly features ignored, the mans till had dangerous black eyes that were currently locked onto Jake. Chuck Reno was a reporter, but not a good one. he had the knack of pushing people’s buttons, getting on their nerves and making sure that he got scoops in the most underhanded ways possible. If Reno was coming around, things weren’t changing for the better. Last Jake heard the man was retired and doing voice work for local sponsors in Phoenix. Maybe doing cheap commercials wasn’t enough to pay his bills. Chuck Reno: “I never left. I’m always on the scene keeping tabs on everything. I heard about you doping this, and thought what is a forty year old doing in a league like this?” Jake Hix: “Same old Reno. Never could keep your facts straight. I am a little old, but I’m still only thirty-five. How did you get back here?” Reno showed his media pass to the Television Champion. Chuck Reno: “I’m a reporter, remember?” Jake Hix: “Opinions vary on that.” Chuck Reno: “Well, I’m glad I ran into you. You remember Tony don’t you?” Jake nodded at the approaching cameraman. Tony had been through all sorts of Hell just working near Reno. He stayed because Reno paid him better than anyone else would have for camerawork. Tony: “Hey Jake.” Jake Hix: “You haven’t left this sorry son of a bitch yet?” Tony: “He still pays the best, and life is never boring.” Jake had to give him a smile at that one. Reno created drama with his mere presence. Jake turned around, and continued on down the hallway. Chuck motioned at Tony, and they began following him down the hallway as he tried to walk away. Chuck Reno: “Jake, I wanted to ask you a few questions now that I have you here.” Jake Hix: “On my way somewhere.” Chuck Reno: “I know, the big celebration party. There are some rumors I think you should be addressing though.” Jake Hix: “I haven’t been here long enough for there to be rumors Reno. You’re just stirring up crap like you always do.” Chuck Reno: “I’m reporting on current events. The rumors aren’t about you so to speak though.” Jake Hix: “I don’t speak about other people. That’s how rumors spread, when gossips get together and everything.” Chuck Reno: “Well, I have been hearing some interesting rumors regarding the Hix family. There’s been talk about all sorts of things with your family.” Jake Hix: “I don’t talk about my family. That is private information, and none of your business. So next topic Reno.” Chuck smiled. He knew he had found the button to push. Chuck Reno: “You used to talk about Pitt all the time Jake.” Jake Hix: “When I was competing against him, but he isn’t in GW. So there’s nothing relevant to us talking about it now.” Chuck Reno: “Jake, I heard rumors of a fire at the Hix Family Ranch. Lots of damage and destruction thanks to those fires. That’s the reason you returned to active competition. To pay for those damages you caused. How do you respond to that?” Jake Hix: “That is the dumbest thing I ever heard. I came back because I want to kick some ass again.” Chuck Reno: “Well, how do you respond to reports that your brother Pitt was injured in that fire, along with serious injuries to your brother John? There are even some people who say that one of them died in this encounter?” Jake stopped. He had an incredulous look on his face, but inside his entire core was quivering in panic. Someone was feeding Reno all sorts of information. Jake Hix: “Chuck, how stupid are you? If that was the case then there would be police reports, fire reports, insurance reports and all manner of other information you could reference. We had the ranch redone after forty years, and all of the sudden you come up with these hair brained conspiracies. Check your facts, check your sources and then get a clue. Now, my party is right up there, and I’ll not be seeing you anymore.” Chuck waited until Jake was about ten yards away to yell out his next question. Chuck Reno: “Well, what about reports that your little sister got knocked up while whoring around some cult?” The questions topped Jake Hix cold. He turned around, rage covering his face. Jake Hix: “What?” Chuck Reno: “I could have it wrong. Some reports just speak about her being hospitalized for some sexually transmitted disease. You know, gonorrhea, herpes, AIDS or something like that. How do you respond?” Jake Hix dropped the Global Wrestling Television Title. His arm lashed out, the fist crashing into Reno’s face with a loud crack and smash. Blood came out of Reno’s broken nose and busted lip as he fell down onto the concrete knocked out. Jake Hix stood over Reno as he spoke. Jake Hix: “You speak about my family again, and you won’t ever be able to ask your questions again. I’ll rip your tongue out and break your jaw into a thousand pieces!” The Big Dawg turned around. He walked down the hallway, grabbing his Television Title from the floor and heading into the celebration party. He wasn’t going to let Reno ruin his night. Investigations would follow soon enough, and he would find out what he needed to then.
