Recent History Just when they thought it was safe
to go back into the ring...

Train has returned.

In a developement nobody ever saw coming the former GW Platinum Champion made an unexpected, but as always, thunderous return to GW. After taking out two GW young guns Train grabbed the mic and made it very clear why he'd returned. He has set his aim of the on the top prize GW has to offer...

The GW World Heavyweight Championship.

And it's become obvious no one maybe able to detur him from his dreams of GW gold. He's gottent just a taste of it, grabbing the GW Tag Team Titles with his unlikely and very reluctant tag team partner Brick in the newest incarnation of Ruckus Inc.. That relationship remains tenuous at best, but they also remain the most feared team on the roster.

However tag team gold is obviously not enough to curb his hunger. A point he proved as put on a gutsy, smash mouth performance in the 2005 'Clash of the Titans' Global Brawl. Train fought his way through 19 other superstars in the huge battle royal, securing a spot in the main event at the biggest PPV of the year, Wrestlecade. Coming out the victor in that chaotic melee assures Train a shot at the richest prize in the game... The GW World Heavyweight Championship.

Can a one time crook from the south side of Chicago transform himself into a world champion? Can GW's O.G. finally secure a legacy as one of the elite in GW history?

Only time will tell....
