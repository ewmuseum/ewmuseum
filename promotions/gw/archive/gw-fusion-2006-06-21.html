The scene opens in the parking lot as “The Deathscythe Outlaw” Kyo Kushinaga is arriving to the arena with a suspicious looking knapsack drape over his shoulder. Loud cheers from the million of the fans at attendance can be heard in the background. He soon stops in the middle of the parking lot where he takes a pack of cigarette out from his pants’ pocket as he pulls one out from the pack. Kyo places the cigarette in his mouth and lights it at the end with his favorite silver lighter. He takes a long drag from it before he breathes the smoke past his lips in a soft sigh. Kyo just stands there and savors the warm scent of his mistress that is nicotine. All of the sudden, he pulls the cigarette out of his mouth as he spins around and flicks the lit cigarette in someone’s face that reveals to be one half of the World Tag Team Champions, Xavier Black. Xavier tries frantically to get the burning ashes out of his eyes. Kyo uses this chance to unload fierce punches on him. The fans are heard cheering even louder, but the loud cheers quickly turns to boos when Pitt Hix soon comes out of nowhere as he stops Kyo’s assault on his tag partner with a hard forearm shot to the back of the head and begins pounding away on him with fierce punches of his own. Kyo doesn’t back down as he fight back against the brawling Pitt. Xavier runs up to Kyo from behind and repeatedly drills his elbow on the back of the head. The Tag Champions are overpowering them with the double team. The fans begin to chant something else as they’re chanting “Grave Revolution”. With a loud battle cry, Kyo gives Pitt a hard shove and then follows up with a hard elbow in Xavier’s face. Once Kyo has been given some space, he slips his hand in his knapsack as he smirks at them in a slight disturbed manner. Kyo: “I knew both of you would try this shit and that’s why I came here tonight prepared.” He pulls out a four foot steel pipe that he been modified into a slight baseball bat, but it’s larger and a bit wider. The fans are cheering and chanting “Outlaw” much louder than before. Xavier tries to step up to Kyo, but he quickly back away and avoids a swing that almost connected with his head. Pitt tries to run up behind him, but he nearly falls on the floor when trying to avoid a hard swing from Kyo’s blunt object. Kyo: “What’s wrong, fellas? Lost ya’ll nerve to kick my ass? Perhaps, this will help motivate you.” He keeps his serious eyes on the Tag Team Champions, but he turns his attention to a nearby car where he destroys it’s gas tank completely. The gas pours out of the large hole in the car. Kyo soaks the top of his weapon in the flowing gas. Pitt and Xavier uses this chance to sneak up on him, but they stop dead in their tracks when they see him light the club on fire. The fans reacts howl in sheer excitement as others are still oddly enough chanting “Grave Revolution”. Kyo holds the flaming club up in the air, but he soon points it straight at the Tag Champions with certain death in his eyes. Kyo: “So, who wants to take a trip to the local morgue first?” He glares at them seriously while the hot flames are dancing around the weapon. Pitt and Xavier are staring at him cautiously. Suddenly, the flames on Kyo’s weapon gets extinguished by somebody. Kyo: “WHAT THE HELL!?” He has a furious look on his face. Kyo: “Whoever did this shit is about to get their ass kick!” He turns and looks with surprise when he sees the person who’s responsibly is none other Train himself who has a fire extinguisher in hand. The arena erupts with loud cheers. Kyo: “What the hell are you doing here? I had things under control here. I didn’t need your help.” The Tag Champions are about to not only sneak up on Kyo, but there are also going to sneak up to Train too. Security soon comes rushing out of nowhere and preventing the Tag Champions from doing so. Brandi: “That’s enough!” The Global President, Brandi Sights enters the scene with a dead serious look on her face. Kyo rests the club over his shoulder as he ignoring the scorching feeling of the club. Train winces a bit as he stares at Kyo with disbelief. Brandi looks at Kyo with dead seriousness. Brandi: “As for you, I’m going to assign you security to watch over you.” Kyo: “What!?
