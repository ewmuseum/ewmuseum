State of the Tag Division by Steven Dinkler

Hello all Steven Dinkler here.

And I am writing a Column on the GW tag division.

Over the years We have seen some great tag teams.

Teams like Canadian Icons,. Southern Comfort, Euro Stars, Silent Assassins, Cold Blood, Hell raisers, The Welch Brothers, Sean Price and Jay Robson, Corrick and Dynasty, Dog Pound, The United Kings. And of course current 8 time Tag champions True Evil.

There have been some bitter rivalries over the tag titles such as the Rivalry of Cold Blood against True Evil or the feuds seen with The Welch Brothers against True Evil.

Other teams have come in and left GW. Making small impressions. But the GW tag Division has never really had a talent full roster. At times some hopes but soon faded away.

I my self am a big fan of Tag team competition. I find it sad that currently there are no strong challengers to the GW Tag team titles…

But I do see a slight glint of hope. Sean Price and Tyler Pierce have a great chemistry. And could be future tag team champions.

Angus The Bull rumor has it is bringing in a tag partner.

Word has it Christina is in talks with a Popular tag from China.

This could be the beginning of a rebirth of the Tag division.

The recently formed team of Dynamite X and Venom Alexander.

Rumors of a ColdBlood reunion, sparks my interest a lot.

There is even rumors of Salomane Vation aligning his self with Tragic Hero.

There are several stars on the GW roster who could benefit from a being partners and being in the Tag division. Guys who are struggling to gain any momentum, struggling to gain any popularity in the eyes of GW fans. Many Big names of GW’s past have been spawned in the tag team division.

I for one am hoping to see a pick up in this division. So this is my plea to the GW roster. Save the division for extension…

I will return later with another look at the GW Tag team division in the weeks to come.

Steven Dinkler Signing off.
