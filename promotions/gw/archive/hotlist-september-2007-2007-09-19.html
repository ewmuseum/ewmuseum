1 SiN Twins:  Adams and Rubis gain a BIG BIG win over Jake Hix and BloodAngel, as Hix walked out on BloodAngel last week. Thus causing the Twins to get a big rise in the Tag ranks. This also pushes them to the top of the Hotlist this week. 3
2 Jake Hix: The Big Dawg drops down a spot after garnering a loss against John Adams and Devilin Rubis, As Jake walked out on the top contender for his World title; BloodAngel. But still being World champ keeps him int the top 2.
 1
3 BloodAngel: Picks his self back up after being walked out on by his "partner" Left to suffer the frustration and anger of the SiN Twins.  BloodAngel now looks to get some payback. Can he this week. 3
4 Psymon: Psymon change since picking the LOW Extreme title of its dusty shelf has shown. Since then he has been a REAL Monster in the ring and picked up the International championship. 15
5 Digital Morality: The Porto Rican superstar looses his strap but wins a big match against another champion last week, Digital still wants his title rematch against Psymon but he is not the only one that wants part of Psymon. 4
6 Scott Taylor: The ACW Livewire Champion is still undefeated in singles competition and that despite his two tag team losses keeps him in the top ten this week. Taylor also looks to challenge Psymon now for the International title.  7
7 Tommy Osborne: Still holding onto the Platinum title but for how long./ He recently loss to Digital.  One has to wonder what help SiN is really giving to Osborne.  6
8 Projekt Mayhem: The Morgan's have captured the Tag Team titles but can they keep the titles as they now face the Nakazura's?

 

 2
9 Brick: The Commissioner continues to put pressure on SiN, But with the Global Constitution cutting off any path Brick may take, one has to wonder when he will drop the role and return to the ring to deal out his own law and order. 10 -
10 Claudius Augustus: The Emperor gains a big win over Atkins and now finds his self at the number ten spot of the Hotlist. lets see if the Emperor can maintain this as he becomes one of the top challengers to Tommy Osborne's title. 19
11 Julian Fiasco: The fiery Italian has made a defiant impression on not only his former partner but Joaquin Pierre and anyone else he may come across.  With a big win over Pierre, Fiasco Sits at number 11 and looks to now be a challenger for the Platinum championship.  -
12 Reginald Atkins: The Brit Twit has not had a few good weeks. And now with his self being distracted by Nate Boswell and VYC, one has to wonder if he can recover and gaiun a Platinum title shot?  11
13 S.o.t.B.L.: Ichiro and Jushin have gone after each other non stop the past few weeks. But with a Big win last week and the right to challenge for the Tag Titles, sits them at number 13 this week. 13
14 True Evil: Salaok and Quartz have taken the war to SOTBL. After being knocked out of last weeks match one has to worry if Salaok will be able to make it this week. 13
15 Jenny Callihan. Jenny climbs up with a win over Teresa Tomas and Scott Taylor.  But a loss to Amanda DeAngleo last week one has to wonder if Jenny can bounce back. I say she can. 18
15 Amanda DeAngleo: The Apocalypse has set her sights on Global and  has gone out to make a impression, to the head of Teresa Tomas, Jenny Callihan and anyone else that gets in her way. -
15 Teresa Tomas: The Redneck Princess drops down a bit this week after taking a tag loss and then getting taken out of the match  last week. But you can be sure she will be fist of fire this week. 14
16 Rebel Express: Have encountered a roadblock on there way to the Tag Team Titles. DUI. Both teams have taken it to each other and none know who is going to come out on top of this war. 8
17 Karma: has drop down a bit after two losses in tag team action, Can Karma recover or is he to distracted by the actions of Atkins 19
18 D.U.I.: The Frat Boys rise up as they seam to put the breaks to the The Rebel Express. Both teams have taken it to each other and none know who is going to come out on top of this war. 20
19 Shane Wethers: Wethermania has arrived in Global and the one to feel it the most is Durian David Orthane.  This is a battle that is heating up and no one knows where it will go. -
20 Durian David Orthane: DDO took exception to the Rock star Shane Wethers showing up in Global and now has suffered for ruining his party. But in the end will it be Wethers paying the price? -

Biggest Rise: - Tie - Psymon & Claudius Augustus. 12pts. Biggest Drop: - Ely Braxton. - Off the list Off the list: Ely Braxton, Josh Kosidilo, TWC, To watch for: Nathan Boswell, Dr. Kasidy Drake, Mike Storm, Angus Kyndall, VYC.

Thoughts: Allot of changes this list. Kosidilo drops off after a big win over Scott Taylor, Josh has taken time off to deal with life issues outside of Global, we wish him and his family the best. TWC Retired after a very long run as tag team champions, they will defiantly be missed.  with a lot of people finally taking there spots in global and things settling in heading to WrestleCade, Global's Roster looks pretty solid.
