Dark Match Elia Smith vs. The Back Alley Brawler vs. Danielle Northsky Winner: Danielle Northsky ---- Damsel in Distress Lines of sadness snaked their way across Shadow's face. She wasn't very pleased at the moment, and contrary to what most people might have thought... it didn't have anything to do with losing the TV Title, or losing Master of the Mat. In fact, that was probably about the last thing on her mind at the moment; life this week had suddenly become much more complicated than just worrying about Global. Her eyes had become a pool of liquid jade sorrow, and honestly, focused on not much other than the floor, as she paced outside the locker room. She didn't feel much like company at the moment. So, of course, company arrived. It was in the form of her fellow semi-final washout at MOTM, the man known to Global fans as Emoticon. Tilting his head to one side, he quirked an eyebrow at the seemingly-sad former champion and said hello. Emoticon: Nahz-dahr... err, sorry if I pronounced it wrong. Shadow looked up rather suddenly, with a hurried attempt to conceal her mood at the moment. She smiled half-heartedly. Shadow: No, that's right... Nazdar. He smiled under the mask for a few moments, before his eyes showed that he was deadly serious. Emoticon: What's wrong? She paused, for a moment considering lying to him, but... why bother? She sighed. Shadow: I'm a little frustrated at myself. I got in way too deep last week, and screwed up my chances at getting anywhere... Of course, that was only half the story. Shadow wasn't entirely sure why she felt like she could just pour out the rest of it to her companion, but out it came, rushing like an undammed river. Shadow: And my personal life is absolutely falling apart. Everyone has a different set of expectations for me... no matter how I act, I'm disappointing someone else, and on top of all that, this week, I get the biggest opportunity that I've ever HAD in Global, and I'm going to blow it because of... Well, that certainly explained it well enough. Emoticon looked into Shadow's seemingly sad eyes. Sure, he had already competed tonight, but he wasn't too injured from the contest. Calmly, he crossed the distance between them, placing a hand on her shoulder. Emoticon: They DO tend to hurt, though. I would hate to cause you pain... A slightly unsettling smirk came over Shadow's face. Quietly, without the usual force, she pushed out one bone spike from in between her knuckle, and held it up for him to see. Shadow: I'm no stranger to pain. It doesn't affect me much anymore. Emoticon: What in the name of...? Taking a step back, Emoticon took a deep breath. That was bone, undoubtedly. And it was a spike. She jerked her head towards the door of the Hunting Grounds locker room. Shadow: The HG locker room is fine... No one's in there at the moment. His nod was far more casual than he felt. There is none, as Ken uses his power advantage, powering Shadow up in the air. POWERBOMB COUNTER! ON THE LADDER! Bishop: Big counter from Partridge, when he needed it the most! That’s the only submission that we know Shadow’s capable of, and KPJ survived it. Sights: We apologize for the behavior of our commentary partner, by the way. Laughing during a main event is NOT appropriate behavior. Creed: I’m sorry! Gods, I’m sorry! I can’t help it! Ken now clutches at the back of his neck. Clearly, the contest has taken a great deal out of the International Champion. Still, he manages to get back to his feet, charging the very slowly rising Shadow, spiking her to the mat with DELIRIUM! Sights: The running signature DDT of KPJ! It’s on the button, and it might win him this match! All he’s got to do is get up the ladder, and retrieve the International Title. Slowly but steadily, Partridge sets up the ladder, beginning the long climb towards retaining his championship. Step… By step… By step he goes, until he feels something grabbing at his ankle. He tries to kick back, but to no avail. The blows miss, and Shadow begins to climb the ladder behind him. She manages to catch KPJ with some forearms to the back, before grabbing one of his arms, and pulling it straight out. Creed: Oh no… Bishop: What in the name of God is she going for? Bracing the larger man’s arm, she falls back with it, pulling Partridge off the ladder with a hybrid armbar/single arm DDT! Partridge’s shoulder is driven into the mat, and he howls with agony upon landing, to the concern of his many fans. Bishop: That’s the biggest move she’s hit in the match! It’s a move that damages a joint, in this case, the shoulder of KPJ. It may as well be a bullseye! Upon recovering from her own hard landing, the vampire allows herself a small smirk. The ladder is mere feet away, and she quickly slides it towards Ken, putting the left arm she had already damaged inside of it, and slamming it shut over the arm. Sights: As my colleague was saying, Shadow’s now got a target, and the target appears to be KPJ’s left arm! With Ken’s arm secured, Shadow begins walking towards the nearby corner. The crowd rises to its feet as they begin to realize what the superstar has in mind. She mounts the turnbuckle slowly, showing all of the effects of the war that she has been in. Bishop: She’s going to risk herself here to finish KPJ off! If she hits whatever she has planned with Partridge’s arm in that ladder, the International Champion may not HAVE an arm to speak of! Flashbulbs go off as Shadow leaps, body twisting and flipping over itself in mid air with breathtaking grace. LEVIN BOLT! *CRASH!* Creed: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Sights: Oh good God… Bishop: PHOENIX SPLASH, OFF THE TOP ROPE, ONTO HIS ARM! Shadow just went chest-first into the ladder, but in doing so, she’s delivered an IMMENSE blow to KPJ’s title reign! A screaming KPJ managed to get the ladder off of his arm, cradling the injured limb to his chest as his feet kicked in agony. Shadow, for her part, was busy clutching her midsection from its collision with the ladder. Both competitors were down, as the referee looked at them both nervously. Creed: Both of them are in deep trouble! This contest has taken damn near everything out of both of them, but they’ve got to keep going! Bishop: Right now, the contest is clearly advantage Shadow. If KPJ wants to win, he’s going to need to put Shadow AWAY. I don’t think Ken can take another fall like that. The Black Madonna finally manages to get back up, attempting to capitalize, but Ken Partridge, Jr. manages a right-handed knifeedge chop. He follows with a second, and a third, as the crowd’s ovation grows higher and higher with every blow. Bishop: I’ll be damned, the International Champion’s fighting back! Three more chops follow, as the crowd’s cheering seems to feed the champion. KPJ connects with a hard boot to the midsection, managing to get Shadow up in the air with mostly one arm, scoring with the cradle piledriver. Sights: Partridge launching a comeback here! He’s on at least his second or third wind right now, so he needs to end this thing while he’s still got something left! Practically working with one arm, KPJ still managed to set Shadow up, lifting her up to a seat on the top turnbuckle facing the crowd. Partridge follows her up, climbing to the very top, before snapping her off with his reverse Frankensteiner known only as… Sights, Bishop, and Creed: THE CURE! Bishop: Both competitors down! Partridge got his move off, but they’re both absolutely out of it! Whoever gets up first is going to have one heck of an advantage. Shadow and Ken Partridge, Jr. lie motionless at first, as the chant comes down from the rafters of the arena. “K-P-J! K-P-J! K-P-J!” Sights: The crowd getting behind the International Champion! Trying to will him to his feet! Trying to get him back up one more time to cheer him to victory! Slowly, but surely, Partridge rises, staggering to his feet, trying to clear the cobwebs. He is utterly exhausted, but nonetheless manages to set the ladder back up. The champion climbs, scratching and clawing his way up the ladder, until finally he stands mere inches from victory. Sights: KPJ’s standing up top! All he needs is to pull the title down and this contest is over! KPJ stands oddly, with the back of his legs braced against the very top step as he tries to pull the title down. He seems to be trying to make sure that the belt will not fly away from him. Meanwhile, on the ground, Shadow begins to recover, crawling towards the ladder, and slowly ascending it. Bishop: Time’s running out for KPJ on this exchange. If he can’t get the title, he needs to turn around, stat. Creed: He doesn’t even see her! He’s too concerned with getting the title! Reaching the top of the ladder, Shadow wraps both arms around Ken’s throat. The off-guard KPJ is pulled down, as she uses the top of the ladder to bend his body around. Her legs wrap around the ladder to apply a body scissors just below his waist, and one of the choking arms slides down to grab his injured arm with a crude chickenwing. Sights: Crossface chickenwing! She has Partridge stretched out over the top of that ladder….I’ve never seen anything like that in my life! Bishop: KPJ’s in trouble! He’s in big trouble! He’s got nowhere to go on top of that ladder, so close, and yet so far! Creed: He’s got to unbalance the ladder! Come on KPJ! STRUGGLE! KNOCK THE LADDER OVER! Indeed, he seems to be trying such a thing, but the odd position he is in makes motion nearly impossible. Bravely, the champion fights the pain, anguished screams being ripped from his body as the hold tightens. Bishop: Partridge trying to hang on! Trying to somehow, somew-- Finally, there is no choice. *tap tap tap* The bell sounds, and Shadow releases her opponent, allowing the now former International Champion to flop to the canvas. Despite her exhaustion, she manages to stand and claim the International Championship, holding it up for a shocked crowd to see. Creed: I don’t believe this! Sights: Believe it!
