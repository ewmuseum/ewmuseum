Bobby Brawler

With a bag of glass and a chair wrapped in barbwire and loaded with C-4 explosives. Bobby Brawler set the stage for the Violence that has been known to plague Global ever since. Many say Brawler was ahead of the times.  Bobby Brawler was known as The Bringer of Hardcore.  This was a understatement  now looking back in time. Brawler was what Global now is before Global became what it was destined to become.  

While many back then merely fought matches, Bobby Brawler fought wars. 

Loyal to a fault. If you befriended Brawler then you had a Friend for life. But be on his bad side and you come to learn just why he is the In-Human Monster.

Where is Brawler today? Who knows. But his memory lives on in a legacy of pain and bloodshed

Words from his peers: 

Brawler was tough as nails and as sharp as one. Many would never suspect Brawler to be as smart as he was, and that was the down fall of many. I truly believe he was ahead of his time. For now Global is what he did out there back then. Back when guys were selling moves and playing it safe Brawler was kicking someone's teeth in... When you stepped into the ring with Brawler you know it was real and you were about to fight for your life. - Bubba McCoy -
