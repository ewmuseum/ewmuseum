---- E V O L U T I O N June 13, 2005 From Las Vegas, Nevada ---- Signals Back in the locker room to the arena in Las Vegas, Nevada. We find the blond tressed wrestler known as Elia Smith as she's walking down the hall, working on getting psyched up for her match later on tonight. She turns a corner and spies the big man in the federation himself, Bubba McCoy, as he's leaned against a table near the coffee station for the workers. It looks like he's filling up a cup and getting it prepared for him to drink it. As he's doing this, he's also talking on his cell phone. Bubba: What? You're here? But you're not supposed to be here until... Elia gets a curious look on her face as he starts to walk away, almost absent-mindedly leaving behind a folder of things on the table. Bubba: You've bought tickets? Well, I'll let my men know to ensure that you have no one bother you then. Are you here for negotiations or just checking things out? Oh, it's a we'll see type thing. Well in that case, I'll make sure things are setup for whatever may happen. I'll be seeing you in a bit. Bubba starts to walk away, leaving the folder behind on the table. He pulls the phone away from his ear and then speaks into it. Bubba: Call Security... The phone responds to him, signaling that it's dialing the head of security for Global Wrestling, so that Bubba's orders can be send and carried out to him. Elia steps up to where his things were left on the table. She picks up the folder, looking like she's intending to call out his name to remind him that he left a few things. As she does though, a couple of what appear to be magazines fall out of it. One is a wrestling magazine by it's cover, showing a collage of some of PTC's wrestling greats on the cover, appears to be covering the beginning on the CW2 tourney and a few other things. Inside of it though, is sticking out a book mark. Elia opens the magazine to the bookmark and finds an article about the event last month in Atlantic City with CORE that she took part in. A reviewer has written a recap of the show and given his thoughts on the whole thing. The comments about her opponent that night are actually highlighted in yellow, by hand, more then likely by Bubba himself. As Elia tries to put that magazine back in, another one falls out... this one has a definite purpose. It's a copy of an For Him Magazine (FHM) that's not been released yet, in fact there is a notice on the front that's it's a promotional copy. On the cover is a woman of dark raven hair, angular face, and darker skin kneeling on a beach with the smallest bikini that could be found to cover her curves. The woman, Elia recognizes all too well. A look on her face shows that she's decided to not remind Bubba what he left behind as she proceeds to deposit the folder, magazines and all, into the trash can next to the coffee station. ---- Of Blood. The door to the arena swings open as the flood of Global combatants entering the building continues. A pair of figures, similarly sized save that one is obviously a woman, walks in together, gym bags similarly slung over shoulders. They are not the first to enter this evening, nor will they be the last- but they are perhaps the oddest, at least in terms of looks alone. On the left side is Shadow, dressed in her typically elaborate and pristine attire- a pair of slim-fitting black velvet pants that hugged her sleek curves until the knee, where they flared out over a pair of boots, and a matching shirt tailored in similar lines, save the sleeves, which were a gauzy black material, and belled out gracefully at the bottom, not quite covering her hands. She glances at her companion for a moment- to his masked face and slimly outfitted form- and then a smirk dances across her features as graceful as a prima ballerina. Shadow: "So tell me... do you show your true face to no one?" She asks her question with a slight smile of amusement, but her voice holds no hint of mockery. “Very few people,” he replied, somewhat sadly. The man known as Emoticon winced slightly, rubbing at a bandage along the line between his neck and collarbone. “Things would change. Identity has a habit of doing that.” A small moan of pain escaped him as he continued to rub over the white bandage, as if trying to soothe the injury beneath. Emoticon: “But enough of that. I do hope you enjoyed last week.” Shadow had been listening to him intently as he talked, but soon enough, she simply smiled at his changing of the subject. Shadow: "Of course... it was quite nice to be able to keep company with a man who behaved himself." She arched an eyebrow, parrying that thrust with another change of subject. She could play this little redirection game of his as well as he could, but admittedly, she also just enjoyed making him squirm a little bit. Shadow: "You know, I have a good bit of... medical experience. I could work a few of those kinks out of your neck if you like... wouldn't want you going into your match unprepared." Despite the fact that she was trying to ignore who he was facing, it wasn't quite working. The smirk slipped from her face. His eyes narrowed, as he looked at her darkly. Emoticon: “Medical experience? YOU’RE the one who made me need these bandages in the first place!” Emoticon’s tone was level and accusing, although he smiled at Shadow, a broad grin. Emoticon: “Besides, after what you did to me…behaving well was really my only option.” He scratched again at his neck. Clearly, something wasn’t right here… Though there was a little bit of trouble behind her eyes, she smiled again, a bit of a playful, dangerous tone slipping into her voice. Shadow: "So, you're telling me that the knight on brilliant white horse thing is just an act for when you're terrified of someone?" The tone of her voice was teasing- something not often heard from Shadow. In fact, despite Emoticon's seeming irritation at the moment, it was easy to tell that Shadow liked him. Or, at the very least, liked messing with him. Emoticon: “It’s not an act,” he replied, searching her face, trying to read her but failing miserably. “but there are some parts of the act that I can’t always do. For example, if I tried to sweep you off your feet and into my arms, I would probably need a bandage on the OTHER side of my neck too.” Emoticon grinned. She was toying with him…not something that he was especially used to. Despite being beautiful, Shadow was clearly more than just a pretty face. Intriguing, especially to one like Emoticon for whom a safe existence was something of a boring one. She hmphed under her breath slightly, a noise of amusement. Shadow: "I'm afraid you quite overestimate my violence. Romance is a dying art, and those who do it well rarely receive my ire." At that, he couldn’t help his deepening smile. Emoticon: “Really now? Perhaps I should have been more elaborate with my technique…” She laughed softly under her breath. Shadow: "Perhaps so... although I must admit, at the word technique, I'm a bit disappointed... you had me fooled into thinking you worked on instinct alone." Her smile widened slightly, though this time, there was no trace of the fangs which had so alarmed him last week. Emoticon: “The Book of the Courtier, Shadow, was a great Renaissance work. The fact that it had to be written should be clear enough that courtly love is not instinctual, sadly enough. You should be thankful that I’m not instinctive…otherwise my concept of ‘love’ would be like the 6’9” 300 pound Neanderthals around here..pound pound pound…where’s the fun in that?” At that, she did laugh aloud, another rare occurrence. Shadow: "I suppose you're right about that... I'm familiar enough with the work. I was just wondering when men were going to put it into practice, instead of displaying their great homophobia at the thought of buying a rose instead of flexing a muscle." She rolled her eyes slightly, her disdain unmistakable. Emoticon smirked, pausing and opening his bag for a brief moment. Emoticon: “The rose is such a versatile flower, wouldn’t you say,” he asked, bemused. “So many different colors…” His hand dipped into the bag, before emerging clutching a plastic-wrapped red rose. Seemingly perfect in every way, the flower was offered to Shadow calmly, Emoticon’s eyes remaining level, betraying nothing. Emoticon: “Take it,” he encouraged, nodding. Shadow paused, and then took the rose that was offered her gently, taking care that her sharp fingernails didn't scratch against his hand when she did so. She looked over it, and though she didn't blush, a smile came over her face that could almost be described as sweet, save for whose face it was on. Shadow: "Aren't you prepared for every occasion?" She shook her head slightly, attempting to keep up her unaffected veneer. But it was plain that she'd been caught quite off guard, and was even pleased by that. Emoticon: “Almost,” he replied. “Almost every occasion.” He winced momentarily, as if recalling something. Emoticon: “I was woefully unprepared for last week,” he sighed, tapping the bandage again, calling attention to it. Shadow: "I do apologize for that," she said, her voice sounding quite sincere. The expression in her eyes quickly changed back to its default one, and she continued. Shadow: "But you must admit, it really wasn't as bad as you're making it out to be." Emoticon blushed bright red, instantly. Unable to help himself, he squirmed noticeably under Shadow’s unrelenting gaze, clearly not wanting to admit anything of the sort. Emoticon: “I…okay, okay, I admit it wasn’t THAT bad,” he said. “I had no idea it could be so painful, though…” She looked down into the flower she held, inhaling its scent for a moment before answering. Shadow: "That's surprising. I could think of far worse things that could happen to a person, though..." That was a thread she chose to leave hanging at the moment, and she seemed completely unaffected by Emoticon's discomfort, as she often seemed unaffected by most displays of emotion, save in rare cases. Also true. She had a habit of being right, often. Emoticon: “But seriously,” he continued, “I really should have been more careful when dancing with you. Those nails of yours truly are razor-sharp…” She smiled, flicking a hand in front of her to glance down at them for a few seconds. She smiled over at him. Shadow: "You're absolutely right... perhaps I should consider finding a new manicurist." Emoticon: “Not that I’m complaining too loudly,” he retorted, unable to resist pushing things, however slightly. “I can think of several situations where I wouldn’t especially be minding those nails thrust into my poor, tender skin.” At that, a broad grin crossed her face. Shadow: "I'm afraid it will take more than one rose to get to that point, mon chevalier." Emoticon: “Far more,” he said, knowingly. “Besides, I’m sure I’m not alone. One of your beauty must be cursed with multiple options.” She cast a sidelong glance at him, amused. Shadow: "Yes, what a terrible curse," she said, laughing. "But I assure you, however many or few options there be, there is only one who seems to know how to approach the matter without making a veritable walking embarrassment out of himself." He took a deep breath at that. Should he even bother breaching the subject he had in mind? To do so would possibly anger her, but at the same time, it would let him know if the various techs and other backstage Global employees he communicated with were pulling his leg or not. Emoticon: “Would that include a certain strawberry-blonde stablemate of yours,” he asked, nonchalantly. Shadow: "Would it break your heart if I told you no?" she returned. Truth be told, she was a little bit irritated at the moment that he'd asked, but hey, the rumors had been going around long enough that it was only a matter of time until somebody outright asked. She was relieved that it had been Emoticon, and not someone that she would have eviscerated with her teeth instead of answering. Emoticon: “Not really… but if it were true, it would certainly explain why the woman is tense enough to cut steel with her bare hand.” He shook his head, especially considering the bizarre experience he had during the week. Dawn’s voice continued to taunt him on occasion, echoing through his mind. Emoticon: “Damn her,” he mumbled, soft enough that she hopefully couldn’t hear. Of course, if he would have known she was a vampire…he would probably have realized she could. Of course, he didn’t think vampires existed, so I’d say he’s screwed in the “not fun” way, wouldn’t you? She arched an eyebrow at his aside, but chose tactfully not to mention it. Shadow: "Perhaps that... perhaps simply that she is not comfortable having someone look after her, and would rather care for herself. Who's to say?" Her body language was enough to convince him to change subjects. Besides, observing her body language gave him a reason to look at her more closely, so it was a win-win situation for the most part. Emoticon: “Well, Miss Shadow, it’s been a pleasure as always, but I’m opening the show tonight. I’m afraid I must go prepare for that contest.” Shadow inclined her head towards him slightly. Shadow: "And good luck in that contest, monsieur.
