Not letting go of SW’s arm, Soma wrenches back at it, trying to pop it out of it’s socket and then drops a leg straight down on the shoulder joint. Soma gets to his feet, still holding onto the arm and pulls Silent Wind up with him, twisting the arm to make sure he has full control before whipping him into the rope. Silent Wind however has the ring knowledge enough to grab a hold of the ropes which annoys Soma who runs at Wind. Silent Wind has just enough time to duck his head and back body drops Soma straight over the top rope and crashing down onto the announcers’ table. Everyone scatters as Soma’s body destroys the table and he lays there motionless as both Silent Wind and Zero drop down out of the ring and move towards him. Silent Wind doesn’t seem happy with the amount of damage down by the table and so reaches down and grabs a hold of Soma, pulling his limp body up into the air. Silent Wind uses Soma more like a spear as he throws him straight at the crowd barrier and his body crumples on impact. Zero walks over and pulls Soma back up, onto to roll him back into the ring where Zero and Silent Wind follows him. The two men pull Soma to his feet and Silent Wind begins to set up for The Eighth Sin. Pulls Soma into a Jackknife powerbomb Silent Wind then turns and positions himself as Zero climbs to the top turnbuckle. But they take too long as Jackson who is back on his feet runs and hooks Silent Wind’s leg from behind, knocking his feet out from under him and leaving Zero perched on top of the top rope. Jackson rolls to his feet and stares straight at Zero who just wants to dive at him but knows it wouldn’t work out the way he wants it to. Silent Wind suddenly sits up, much to the shock of Jackson who was hoping to have a little more free time with Zero alone. The referee comes over and instructs Jackson to get out of the ring because he’s not the legal man, which brings a grin to the face of Zero who then looks straight at the prone body of Soma laying motionless in the ring. But before Zero has a chance to do anything, Jackson grabs a hold of Soma’s leg and drags him over to their corner. Jackson quickly gets out of the ring and then reaches through and tags the out cold Soma to get himself into the match. The referee has no choice other than to accept the tag and then turns his attention to Zero who also isn’t meant to be in the ring. Jackson walks over to Silent Wind who is beginning to get to his feet when suddenly Zero jumps over the referee with what looks to be a top rope spear only to be cut short. Jackson sees it coming and grabs a hold of Zero’s head as he flies forward, pulling him into a DDT. Zero bounces off the mat in a bad way and Jackson quickly rolls to his feet only to find Silent Wind is already there. The two men look at each other, eye to eye with only an inch the difference in favor of Silent Wind. Jackson, not wanting to get into enough fist fight chooses the quick option of a toe kick to the gut and then a knee lift to the face which causes Silent Wind to bounce off Jackson’s knee, but stands straight back up again much to JND’s shock. Silent Wind swings a massive arm and clotheslines Jackson to the mat, only to have Jackson get straight back up to his feet which Silent Wind seems slightly surprised by as the two once again get in each other’s faces. This time Jackson throws a punch but Silent Wind sidesteps and grabs a hold of the loose arm, dragging him down into an arm bar and seemingly enjoying it. Using the arm in his control, Silent Wind gets to his feet and then drags Jackson up as well before throwing him into the ropes and greeting him upon return with a boot to the face. Zero get to his feet again slowly and the referee sees this and has to get him out of the ring, causing him to turn his back on SW and Jackson. Silent Wind grabs a hold of Jackson’s head and drags him over to the turnbuckles and placing him sitting in the corner. Silent Wind moves across the ring and then runs full speed at Jackson extending a knee to smash into Jackson’s face but Jackson manages to get out of the way at the last second. Silent Wind crashes into the turnbuckles and bounces out straight into a roll up by Jackson. 1… Kickout! Jackson rolls to his feet while Silent Wind rolls out of the ring before grabbing Jackson’s foot and dragging him out of the ring as well. The two men begin to brawl on the outside of the ring until Silent Wind once again gets the upper hand and then doubles Jackson over. Silent Wind pulls Jackson up for a powerbomb outside of the ring when suddenly Zero shouts out to Silent Wind before running and spring boarding himself off the top rope and in combination with Silent Wind, hits The Eighth Sin on the outside of the ring. Jackson’s face smashes into the mat and blood begins to pour from his nose but he isn’t the only one who’s hurting as Zero is certainly feeling the impact of his own massive move. Silent Wind pulls Jackson to his feet and then rolls him into the ring and follows. Silent Wind carelessly hooks Jackson’s leg and goes for the pin. 1… 2… Soma is able to make it just in time to drop an elbow on Silent Wind to break the count. Silent Wind quickly gets up as does Soma who is still feeling the effects of being driven through the table earlier soon finds himself with Silent Wind’s hand around his face pushes him backwards into the turnbuckles. Soma throws a wild punch which connect, but only seems to make things worse of him as Silent Wind suddenly unloads on him with fists hitting every part of his body imaginable. Silent Wind pulls Soma out of the corner and grabs the back of his neck, only to lift him high into the air. Soma soon finds himself back down on the mat, face first as Silent Wind slams him down with a reverse choke slam, which shakes the ring. Knowing that Soma isn’t the legal man, Silent Wind walks over to Jackson and kicks him to see if there is any response which there is none. Silent Wind pulls Jackson’s body up and sets him up for what looks to be The Mark. Pulling Jackson up but Jackson suddenly blocks it and turns things around with a back body drop, but it doesn’t work as well as planned. Silent Wind manages to hold on and pulls Jackson down into a sunset flip, straight into a pin. Silent Wind puts all his weight on Jackson’s legs holding him down as the referee makes the count. 1… 2… 3. Bishop: ITS OVER! ITS OVER! GSA ARE THE NEW TAG CHAMPS! OH MY GOD! OH MY FUCKING GOD!
