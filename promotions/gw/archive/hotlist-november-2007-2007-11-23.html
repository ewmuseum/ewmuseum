Hotlist (November 2007)

23 November 2007

Status

1 Jean Claude Dafoe: returns to Global after a long absence, to stand on the other side of the battle line against Salaok, as Co Owner of Global. What does the HOF'er have in store for The Nevermore and Global. We will have to find out. But Salaok most likely wont like any of it.  - -
2 Salaok: drops down a bit at the return of the Canadian Icon. Salaok finds his self as not the sole owner as he thought, but half owner. The battle lines are sure to be drawn and the big question is who will suffer in the crossfire.  1 -
3 Adam Stryker: drops to the 3rd spot this week as the result of the return of Dafoe. But none the less he keeps winning , Defeating and retaining his International Title against John Adams in a hell of a Cage Match 2
4 Christian Darke:  despite being World champion Dark drops a few spots in the Hotlist. Staying a constant presence in the Nevermore stable.  Recently Battled Brick Ely Braxton and Digital Mortality to a No contest brawl 3
5 Digital Morality: takes a leap up the Hotlist this week even with being fired by Salaok. The fans and Global said good buy to a hero. Hopefully one day Digi can return and entertain the fans once more for Global. 8
6 John Adams:  drops down a few spaces with a loss against the International champion and the rise of Digi and the return of Dafoe. But don't count out the Profit yet. He is sure to rebound from this loss. 4
7 Killer Pirates drop down a few more spots do to the rise Digi and the return of Dafoe. The Tag Champions stay strong waiting to see who will challenge them for there titles at Dark Rituals. 5
8 Brad Jackson: gains another win and another spot in the Hotlist as he put a member of the Nevermore, DDO down for the count. Who will be able to stop Jackson as he rises fast in the ranks knocking down all in his path 10
9 "Captain" Jack Morgan: rises up the ranks with a big win over the Platinum Champion, Lantlas Anduril. And now he gains another chance to face off for a International title match. But in his path this week ar Jackson and DDO, and the Pirate sail past these big sea monsters for the win? 11
10 Lantlas Anduril: drops down this week with a loss against Jack Morgan? This week He defends his title against a new challenger in Brian Simon can Lantlas fight off the challenger or will we have a new champion? 7
11 Durian David Orthane: drops down the Hotlist as he suffers defeat at the hand of Brad Jackson. As well as the rises of Digital, and the return of Dafoe, Can DDO dust his self off and defeat not only Jack Morgan but The man who defeated him, Brad Jackson?  9
12 Juvian Ramorez. entranced in a heated feud with Joaquin Pierre. Will his mind games work to continue to keep the French man down? 15
13 Brick: tried to counter Salaok's evil ways.  And paid the price as he too joined Digital in the unemployment line? Or did he, Has brick been taken out of the role of Commish or is he still there?  13 -
14 Glory Bound. rises up again with a solid win over Rough and Tumble,  But now they face there biggest opposition yet as they take on the legendary tag team of True Evil. Can the combustible duo stay together long enough to survive?  16
15 Brian Simon: leaps up after  a win over Braxton and Pierre to earn his self a International title shot.  Can Simon defeat the Elvish Warrior, Lantlas?  20
16 Ely Braxton:  barley lost his last match can He break this streak of bad luck against Juvian this week?  14
17 Bubba McCoy:  drops down the Hotlist but plays a big part in the return of the Canadian Icon Jean Claude Dafoe.  What will Bubba's role be now? And is there any way he can regain control of a fed he built?   6 -
18 Rough and Tumble: Suffered another loss against the team of Glory Bound. Word is that they were suffering from food poisoning, And still fought a hell of a match. No word is proven yet if they were are were not poisoned. 17
19 Quartz:  drops down again with little activity from the Global Monster, people say though that when Quartz is quiet some one is about to get hurt. Just a matter of time till we see his target.   15
20 Paul Samson: returns as he answers Caleb's open challenge for this coming Fusion.  Will the Mastodon known as Samson be able to take down Caleb -
20 Markus Caleb: issued a open challenge and got his answer in the form of Paul Samson. Can Caleb survive the hype and the running train coming at him in Paul Samson? We shall see in his debut match this week on Fusion. 19

Biggest Rise: Dafoe 19pts Biggest Drop: Quartz -  4pts Off the list: Kaden Calix To watch for: Paul Samson, Donny Diamond, Julian Fiasco, Jack Gaither, Jack Williams, Cady Coleman.

Thoughts: Interesting development with Dafoe's return. Digital fired? Brick fired? Salaok's out to destroy the federation? All that and new debuts. Glory Bounds made a big impact in a few short weeks and now Samson and Caleb going at it. Things are starting to look really solid.
