Ichiro grabs his head with one hand and slams a fist into the side of Reno’s face with the other, knocking him to the side. Reno rolls and gets to his knees, trying to catch his breathe. Ichiro slowly sits up, still holding the back of his head. Reno looks up at Ichiro slowly, the two men making eye contact. Sights: It looks like Ichiro is saying something. Bishop: But what! I SWEAR! We need microphones down there. Reno smiles and responds, again unheard. This seems to infuriate Ichiro who swings down at Reno only to have his punch caught; Reno jerks Ichiro to the mat and rolls over, slamming a elbow down into the center of his back, directly between his shoulder blades. Another elbow slam and Ichiro yells out. Reno grabs Ichiro about the head and jerks back, applying a modified version of the Canadian Cross-Face. Reno quickly releases it however and stands before kicking Ichiro over. Sights: Ouch. Oh my god. This is getting violent. Steele: Yeah. Me no likey. Reno grabs Ichiro by the hair and jerks him up only to receive a hard right followed by a stiff back hand and a short kick. Reno drops to one knee, not prepared for the attack. Ichiro prepares to finish the combo when he’s nailed from behind, John Zero slamming an axe-handle into his upper back. Reno jumps up, nailing a sambo-suplex. Reno spins around and slams Ichiro down, again The referee is yelling at John Zero, telling him that he’s not the legal man. Zero grabs him and shoves him back and through the ropes. Bishop: WOOO! Now it’s gonna get fun! Ichiro drops and spins, sweep kicking John Zero’s legs out from under him. Ichiro spins up and delivers a devastating axe-kick to John’s stomach. John sits up, grabbing his gut before Ichiro staggers back, one step and then another. He turns and Reno grabs him from nowhere and delivers Strife’s Decimation! Ichiro rolls over, coughing hysterically. John slowly gets to his feet, the Consiliu circling Ichiro like a piece of raw meat. Sights: uh oh… The crowd is booing loudly, standing on their feet. Zero pulls Ichiro up and Reno slams hard right after hard right into his face, his nose breaking under the pressure of the horrendous blows. Finally Reno slams an elbow into his face, blood splattering to the side and onto the mat. John shoves him forward and Reno picks him up to deliver a massive spine-buster. Sights: Someone end this! Maverick Reno straddles Ichiro from behind and lifts him up, forcing him into a half-lotus position (ironic, eh?) and John rushes in and lets himself go, slamming all of his weight, via his foot, directly into Ichiro’s face. Bishop: WOO HOO! Reno slams him face first against the mat now and then again before Zero grabs him and pulls him up. Reno slams a hard kidney-punch into his side before Zero whips him to the corner. So weak, Ichiro begins to slump down towards the mat but not before Reno slams a knee into his mid-section. A hard right and Reno lifts him up and places him on the top. Steele: Oh goody, we go up top now! Reno grabs Ichiro and sets him up for a super-plex… and lifts him high into the air, spinning and delivering a SUPER-GORD BUSTER FROM THE TOP ROPE! Bishop: OH MY GOD! I haven’t seen Reno do that in years! Sights: Right, he used to call that The Decimation! But not anymore, he’s just pulling out all the stops to cause as much pain to Ichiro Nakazura as humanly possible! Reno slowly gets to his feet as Zero pulls Ichiro up. Sights: It doesn’t look like anyone is coming out to help Ichiro! The Legion is dead. Akodo Rokku dead… Jushin Nakazura dead… Kakita Yoshiko, damned near paraplegic. What’s next!? Quickly, John Zero rushes to the turnbuckle and runs up. He turns, aims and leaps off… ZERO DELIVERS AND ELBOW TO THE THROAT OF NAKAZURA AS RENO DROPS DOWN! Sights: Oh My God! Ichiro Nakazura may not have made it through that! Bishop: THAT WAS GREAT! Steele: NOOOO! BAD BAD BAD! John Zero and Maverick Reno shake hands as John drops down and rolls from the ring. Reno leans down and Naria Xiou hands him the mahogany box from earlier; he turns and places it upon the chest of Ichiro. Reno takes a microphone and kneels next to Ichiro as John tosses the referee back into the ring. He crawls over as Reno places a hand atop the box. Maverick Reno: The hand of your brother beneath mine… with this, meet your fate. The referee counts. Sights: 1…2…3! Reno has pinned Ichiro Nakazura in the most inhumane way possible! Bishop: What did he mean by the hand of your brother beneath mine? Sights: No clue. Reno leaves the box and rolls out of the ring, walking away with John Zero and Naria Xiou at his sides. Bishop: That’s all for Evolution, ladies and gentlemen! Sights: Be sure to catch us next week! The camera zooms in on the bloodied and beaten face of Ichiro Nakazura before fading to static.
