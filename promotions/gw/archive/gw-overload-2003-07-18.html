GW — Card

Overload, 18 July 2003

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The electrical currents run heavy through the machinery, blowing away the metal that surrounds the big Overload logo plastered into the screen, as it rushes at us the O lights up and we go through to see Trista Romano dressed in a red skirt and a black top smiling enthusiastically, the screens behind her on and ready displaying classic and not so classic matches in the background. Trista: Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome to another edition of GW Overload. In this week’s edition of Overload we will be discussing the reformation of one of the greatest stables ever to grace the halls of the GW, the Young Bloods. Not to mention we will be have a sit down interview with Sean Price in which we will discuss Tony Stecchino joining the TUE. We will also be brining you the very end to the incredible main event that took place this past Evolution between Brick and Deacon Holmes. But before we do that lets head down to the ring to see the ending to the Ace Cash and Ely Braxton match. The camera zooms into the tv screen directly behind Trista and as it does we see the Ely Braxton and Ace Cash match taking place. Sights: Braxton staggers to the side, shaking his head. Ace Cash starts to pull himself up from the back, a small line of blood trickling from his nose. Braxton reels back, staggering and shaking his head. Suddenly he grabs his chest and starts to cough, foam coming from the corners of his mouth. Bishop: What the hell is that about? Sights: I don’t know but I am sure it can’t possibly be good. Bishop: Way to go, did you learn that in nursing school? Sights: He hits the mat, holding himself up. Cash turns around and, without noticing the situation that is developing, pulls Braxton up and nails a very hard double arm DDT. Cash rolls off and rushes the top rope… he jumps to the second and steps to the third. He leaps from the top and … CASHFLOW! 1.2.3! This one is over Bishop: That was a waste of a match. Sights: Medics have rushed the ring and are checking on a now fully unconscious, and foaming, Ely Braxton! The camera fades out of the match and back to an image of Trista, except this time she is sitting next to TUE member Sean Price. Sean is ofcourse decked out in a suit as he always is when he is not in the ring. Trista: Well that sure was an unexpected ending to a very good match. Ace Cash was the winner but there has been no word exactly on the condition of Ely Braxton. We will give you an update as soon as we find one out. Now, the man sitting next to me has made a name for himself in the short time he has been in the fed. He is a former tag team, platinum and International champion as well as a former member of the second installment of the MHC and a founding member of The Upper Echelon. Ladies and gentlemen…Sean Price. Sean: Thanks for the introduction Trista. It was nice of you to point out exactly how great I am. Trista: Well it was nice of you to come on the show. But I don’t know if I would use the term great to describe your so far successful career here in the GW. Sean: I would. Trista: Ok then. Now this past Evolution some unexpected happenings took place. The Youngbloods headed up by Jay Robson, your former tag team partner reformed, but not to be over shadowed the TUE also added another man to their arsenal. What are your thoughts on the reformation of YB and the addition you guys made. Sean: First of all, any stable headed up by Jay Robson is sure to fail. Take the second MHC for example. That stable started off good, but then it became nothing more than a complete failure. If it weren’t for myself and Jason Dynasty the MCH would have been a laughing stock. More so than its esteemed leader. Which is exactly why we kicked him out of the very stable that he created. As for BJP and Trey Reed. Although Reed has not officially said whether he is in the YB or not, as far as the TUE is concerned, if he runs with them period he is the enemy and we will greet him the same way we greet any enemy. With a harsh reality check. Trista: You bring up an interesting point. People have been calling up the show and emailing us here at Overload asking us if Reed is part of the YB or not, and as far as we know he is not. But I am sure we will find out in due time. Sean: It makes no difference to me if he is or if he isn’t. Your either with us or against us. It’s that simple. Tony Stecchino knew that and that is exactly why he is the newest member of the TUE. Because he embodies everything that the TUE is. Money, power, attitude and direction. That is the TUE, and that is Tony Stecchino, so it was only natural that the two joined forces. TUE was always the most powerful stable in GW history but now with Tony on our roster we are that much better. Trista: Well with a group consisting of yourself, Tyler Pierce, Deacon Holmes, Tony Stecchino and John Zero, few would be hard pressed to disagree with you. But before we go any further lets take a look at the ending of the Jay Robson and Kid Flash match. The camera again pans to the video screen where the Robson and Flash match is being played. Sights: Kid Flash and Jay Robson lock up in the center of the ring. Robson quickly brings a knee up to his gut and hooks him about the neck and shoulders. Bishop: Good match by the leader of the YB. Sights: Robson snaps back and nails a fast snap suplex. Flash bounces on the mat, grabbing at the back of his head, which happened to be the point of impact. Robson gets to his feet and makes a pose for the Overload audience! Flash slowly starts to get up … he and Robson with a second lockup. Bishop: Could this be the end or does Kid Flash have some more fight in him? Sights: Robson with another swift knee to the gut… Modified Frost Bite! Jay Robson makes the pin. 1…2…and 3! Jay Robson takes the victory! Trista: That truly was an awesome match fought by a new comer to the fed and a Hall of Famer in Jay Robson. Sean: Hall of Famer? Robson is a joke plain and simple. Same with his former tag team partner Derek Frost…Cold Blood, give me a break Tyler and I would of demolished them had we ever faced them. Trista: Now Sean, try to remain attentive here. This is an interview not some promo you cut. I am asking the questions. Sean: Keep up that attitude and your gonna be asking your questions to the wall I can promise you that. Trista: Well Jay Robson is in the Hall of Fame, as is Derek Frost, so that has to say something for the legacy they left behind. Sean: No, that says something for the other garbage wrestlers they faced in their prime, if you even want to call it that. I can sit here with absolute certainty and say that when all is said and done and the GW officially closes its doors, the TUE will be the first and only stable in the Hall of Fame because we are that great. Trista: That’s one heck of a goal to strive for. Good luck with that. And thanks for coming on the show. Sean: Anytime cutie. Sean takes off his head set and then heads off to the back but not before turning around to give Trista one last look before disappearing into the shadows. Trista’s face is bet red but she goes on with the show nonetheless. Sights: Mike McKendrick staggers back from the corner after an irish whip from hell. Asylum's menacing form stands in the center of the ring. Mike ducks what would be a hard right and comes up behind Asylum… Asylum turns and slams a back elbow directly between his eyes, sending Mike back first to the mat. Bishop: I have been impressed with both of these men so far. And that takes a lot to impress me. Sights: McKendrick rolls to his stomach and attempts to stand; Asylum grips him by the shoulders and pulls him up. He hooks him about the waist and… release German suplex! McKendrick is hurled right over Asylum's head and to the harsh and unforgiving mat! McKenrick pulls himself up on the second turnbuckle. Bishop: Another good move by McKendrick, he has been full of them this match. Sights: Asylum turns and rushes his opponent, slamming his boot directly into the side of his head, his head against the steel post. McKendrick falls to the side, completely unconscious. Obenockeresque checks on McKendrick… he's yelling for the bell. This one is over! Mike McKendrick can't continue. Trista: Well folks that is all we have for you on this weeks edition of Overload, tune in next week as we will be giving you a special edition of Overload in which qualifying matches for Master of the Mat will be taking place. Have a good night. The scene fades to the Overload logo and then to commercials as the Overload credits roll in the upper left hand side of the screen.
