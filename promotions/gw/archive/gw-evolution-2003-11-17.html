The explosion will last only a brief matter of seconds but will be intense enough to give second degree burns to either competitor within ten feet of the explosion. Jushin looks down at the small piece of C4 strapped to the middle of the cell as Ichiro begins to ascend the ladder and is now almost inside the fourth chamber. Jushin walks to the stairs rushing to get out of the chamber, he pushes up on the small trap door. But realizes the door won’t open. Sights: The door won’t open until the C4 has gone off. Both men are trapped. Bishop: This is not good. Ichiro steps into the fourth chamber, and the way down is sealed behind him also. The timer now reads 1:30. Ichiro runs towards Jushin and the two brothers begin to brawl. The time ticks away closer and closer to the imminent explosion, which both brothers are forced to endure. Each man tries to battle his opponent closer to the C4 in the middle of the ring, instead of himself. 30 seconds. Ichiro gains an advantage after he nails a Japanese Arm drag take down on Jushin, he then continues to work Jushin over, as the timer ticks the seconds away. 15 seconds. Ichiro and Jushin now stand toe to toe, each man on either side of the C4. They exchange punches and kicks slowly, as each man is drained of energy. The blood pours off of their bodies and both men are battered, beaten and bloody messes. Ichiro’s blood is flowing from a cut in his head, a cut on his upper arm, and a series of cuts on his back. Jushin is leaking blood heavily from his face, and slightly from his back and chest. 5,4,3,2…… The brothers still brawl almost unaware of the nearing explosion. 1…. The brothers realize the danger, and each man begins to back away, they dive away as the clock reaches zero and the explosion goes off. Each man yells in pain as the heat sears the already bloody skin. Ichiro is in pain gripping his face, while the quicker Jushin is grabbing his legs in pain. The two finally begin to stir after a few seconds and the hydraulics in the cell kick in. With a slight hymn the door to the scaffolding opens up. Ichiro is the first to his feet and begins to walk towards the small set of stairs that will lead him onto the scaffolding. Jushin is quick to follow and the two men throw punches softly, but strong enough to knock each other around in their weakened conditions. They move slowly and sluggishly and their faces grimace in pain as they make their way slowly up the stairs. Sights: This is it, the final stretch. Bishop: Finally the torment will end!! Ichiro is the first onto the scaffolding but it is not an advantage as Jushin is right behind him, trying to trip him. The brothers move slowly brawling onto the scaffolding above the pyramid of tables. The two now stand toe to toe, swinging punches, any advantage could lead to a win or a loss. Sights: Any slip up will cost the man the match here Mark. Bishop: I just hope it ends soon. Ichiro begins to overpower his brother, and the blood flow worsens. Each man can see a pool of blood forming at his feet. Ichiro swings several punches, as he regains some sort of second wind. Jushin sees the match slipping away and decides to take a gamble. He has one shot to knock his brother off and end the match. Jushin rears back and ducks, Ichiro misses a heavy punch and throws himself off balance. Jushin takes this moment to strike, but Ichiro reacts quickly and catches the punch. The two engage in a test of strength atop the scaffolding. The loser surely will fall from the scaffolding. Sights: What a dramatic finish!! Bishop: Are you kidding me, a test of strength? Not very exciting if you ask me! Sights: A test of strength between brothers who have been through twenty five minutes of hell with each other, A test of strength atop a set of scaffolding roughly about sixty to seventy feet high. A test of strength where the loser will lose the match and take the Nestea plunge! Ichiro begins to arch backwards as Jushin begins to overpower him, he struggles back, as a look of determination consumes his face. He pushes Jushin back into an arch, however, Jushin cannot hold onto it any longer and slips. He falls off of the scaffolding, but is caught just before the fall by Ichiro. Bishop: What the hell?! Sights: Throughout all that their brotherhood still shines through!! Ichiro struggles to pull Jushin up, as Jushin looks at him shocked. Jushin: Drop me!! Ichiro: No!! Jushin: Do it!! Ichiro: I can’t let you fall! Jushin: You idiot!!! Let me fall!! Ichiro begins to pull his brother up, but Jushin will have no part of it. He reaches up and punches Ichiro’s forearm with everything he has left. The pain causes Ichiro’s hand to open up and release his grip, he yelps in pain as his brother falls. Crashing through the entire pyramid of tables. Ichiro looks down in shock. Sights: Jushin just made his brother drop him! This match is as good as over. Ichiro looks down but does not understand what just happened. He walks over across the scaffolding slowly, and stumbly as the blood continues to pour from his body. He reaches up, and straps himself into the zip line harness. He hooks the last strap, and then sends himself down the line. Seconds later his feet touch the entrance ramp. He raises his arms in victory as his music of Toxicity by System of a Down begins to play. He unhooks himself and then raises his arms in victory one more time before he collapses to the ground. Sights: Ichiro has done it! He has proved the TUE is not indestructible! Bishop: Oh my god! We need to get some EMT’s out here for these men. The Souls of the Black Lotus are in trouble. Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen your winner after a time of 27:53, “The Eternal Soul” Ichiro Nakazzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuurrrrrrrrraaaaaaaa!!!! The EMTs rush out on stage to help the Nakazura’s as the screen begins to fade. ---- ---- ---- ~Evolution~ Producer J. P. In Association with Big Dawg, INC Es Dub Enterprises Manager Seth B.
