Staff (September 2007)

4 September 2007

Recent History

Following what has been termed as the Exodus at the end of 2005, Global went to rebuild itself. Beginning the year at Global Reload, everything became about new directions as the company moved forward following the loss of a large block of talented competitors.
Through various changes in leadership, Global entered what was thought to be a stable period when Brandi Sights moved from the Announcer’s Table to the Boardroom. 
Unfortunately, a car crash due to brake tampering has led her to the current medical leave while she heals. Train has assumed the duties of Commissioner while waiting for the return of his friend.

That was last year.... Global has now reloaded and stared 2007 off with a roster of rising stars. The recovery is over and Global now shines once more.  Now a more stable and Revitalized Global looks onward to the Future. LONG LIVE GLOBAL!

On Camera Staff

Management

Owner: Bubba McCoy- The boss. Bubba is a former Tough man Champion from Alabama. Bubba entered Pro Wrestling in WCWF, There after a injury he turned into a commentator. Not happy with the direction of WCWF he formed his own company, UCWF. Bubba then bought FWL and merged the two into GWF. During this time he stayed in his role of Commentator, Hiring different people to run his company, on camera.  Not happy with the direction of Global or Pro Wrestling anymore, He fired his staff, dropped the F and eventually W from the name and turned the company into Global. Looking for a more innovative and hard hitting show, Bubba got rid of the Squared Ring of Pro Wrestling fame and brought in the Octagon. He also changed the rules of how the matches went and got rid of the backstage politics of Pre Decided matches. Today Bubba has made a company that meshes the entertainment of Pro Wrestling and the hard hitting action of MMA. 
Currently Bubba is enjoying time away with his wife after years on the road. His current location is unknown.
President: Brandi Sights - Brandi Sights started her career in WCWF. When Bubba McCoy left the company to form what is known today as Global, She followed, continuing her commentating duties at his side. For years she was the Voice of Global.  When Bubba's Daughter Christina McCoy left she formed a championship committee, Brandi was their spokesperson. Bubba tired of there politics and greed and fired them and placed Brandi in charge of Global. 
Commissioner: Brick - The fist ever Global member is now the Commissioner. Having had his share of action over the last seven years.  Brick bested Train for the position as Commissiner and has found a whole new battlefield.

Show Production

Due to the recent fire in Las Vega, the Global HQ offices and all shows are now being held at the Global Arena in Dallas, Texas.
Ring Announcer: Brian West - The quintessential hype man. brain loves his job and loves to hype the crowd and get them into the match. His style is a combination of Boxing Announcers and Pro Wrestling Announcers. Some time he has been known to give out new nick names to the fighters in global, that have a tendency to stick. 

Commentary Team

Katrina Starr - Katrina has a detailed knowledge of the business, wrestling / fighting history, and a keen sense of how to best handle herself on air. She is without question behind the crowd favorites.
Mark 'OUT' Bishop - Mark is a sniveling, sexist asshole. A man that would sell his grandmother for a quarter (if he could get away with it) He degrades women more than anyone else, but at the same time craves their attention and to make them sexual objects. Mark is firmly behind the heels. He has a obsession with violence and seeing blood spilled, Unless it is his own then he passes out.

Interviewers

Noi Tsunami - The lead interviewer on staff with no fear of anyone. She is always looking for the story to show to the fans, and is often called on for interviews.
Alexis Steel - Eye candy for the male fan base, Not completely, Alexis knows wrestling and has been with Global for an extended time. Unfortunately, she conducts herself like a beach bunny bimbo bouncing about the backstage. She has fallen out of favor with many of the wrestlers as Noi has asserted herself.
Chuck Reno - A man best known for being a coward and a weasel, he has cleaned up a little although he’s still a weasel. A small man with a balding hairline and and no morals or ethics. Sometimes contributes columns to the news.
Steven Dinkler aka The Dink - At one time he was the head reporter. After garnering a bit of fame however he got lazy and Noi quickly moved up and ousted his spot. Since then he's turned into a cynical and sarcastic bitter reporter. Mainly demoted to house shows as of late.
Trista Romano - After years away from Global, Trista a former fighter and manager in Global, has returned. Mainly put in charge of the Global website Global-Online. She brings the inside scoop to the fans.

Referees

Jack Wolf - The head ref, he calls it right down the middle and has the respect of everyone.
Dave Simmions - A strait shooter, down the middle dork.
Brad Jones - A very average referee. Does his job, never makes waves.
Thomas Striker - Tom is a very controversial referee, accused many times of taking bribes, looking the other way, and making fast counts. Nothing has been proved, but many of the roster members do not like him. He is also quick to call a DQ in a match as well.
Jimmy Smash - Jimmy is a poor referee at best. Slow at counting and never enforces the rules. He is though quick to DQ for being hit. (Rumors are that he is normally drunk or high or both!)
Brian Clark - A referee more interested in the ladies who come to Global shows. Often distracted by the women, he misses counts and some wonder how he hasn’t been fired. He is never given top matches, but usually makes the right call when he isn’t watching the women.
