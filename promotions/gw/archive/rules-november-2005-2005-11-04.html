Rules (November 2005)

GLOBAL Rules and Guidelines

*NOTE* If your question is not answered here, please feel free to contact the staff. General Information GLOBAL is a Mixed hybrid fed of Fighting Martial Arts and Wrestling. Your character can have up to three styles of fighting listed in there bio. You must use ORIGINAL wrestlers. Using Real Wrestlers or Real Celebrities for your Wrestler is not permitted. (Unless we the staff choose other wise)
GLOBAL is located and based in Los Vegas, Nevada. GLOBAL only travels for PPV’s and special shows. Most cards take place at the Global Arena in Las Vegas.
I will state this now… GLOBAL is not your Average Federation, Here there is no Guy playing the role of a gimmick. Here every thing is real... and GLOBAL is known to shatter the reality barrier. So it is plausible for a Cybernetic Ninja to fight in GLOBAL, same as a guy who is the head of the Mafia, same as a guy who is a pro wrestler looking to make a name for his self in the business.
So if some one is playing a Vampire in GLOBAL then they really are one. Just In character, many discount that believing it to be like other federations. So Your character could not walk into the police station and have some one shot for being a Demon.... cause then he would be committed into the Psyche ward of a nearby hospital
But I do have my limitations…IE I would not let a guy who was an alien with four arms, super strength, and can shoot laser beams out of his head. That’s a bit over the top…. Even for GLOBAL!. Just remember one thing when creating a character and before submitting an application. Is it remotely possible that this could happen today? It’s all about fun, for everyone. Also there is such a thing I will enforce here is In Character and OOC (out of character) knowledge... IE say I know Guy 1's Character is a Vampire... But unless he has told my character, my character don't know, just like the rest of the world. Unless of course he is showing fangs and stuff on the shows, then all know it.
There is a CHARACTER Exclusivity rule in GLOBAL... This means YOU can have your character in GLOBAL and nowhere else... Make a new character to join other feds... But the GLOBAL character is in GLOBAL till he/she leaves. [This is the rule unless the staff allows other wise.] GLOBAL is highly focused on Shows, in character news. So, Send in segments... Send in News and Rumors to promote your character(s). Also card segments. Even if you’re not booked for a match segments are very important, and a way to keep your character in the booking staffs eyes. Just cause your character does not have a match does not mean he/she is not at the show. We reserve the right to terminate anyone at any time. There are various offenses leading up to this documented in the rules. You will be suspended first, and then terminated if the situation continues.
 

Role play Information and Rules: Role-Play Deadline: 12:00 AM CST with a one hour grace period on the day before the show.  Any roleplay posted at 1:01 AM CST will not be counted.  Special situations will be weighed individually.  These deadlines will be posted with the card or on the GLOBAL Forums.
Card Segments Deadline: Two days before RP deadline. These deadlines will also be posted on the GLOBAL Forums. Role-Playing/Promo Information: We ask for Novel and Prefer Novel [Story / Book style] but we will not frown on script. If done right Script can be very good. But in a whole I feel that Novel style is the better style, so I prefer it for this federation. We ask you at least give it a try. Also, there are Hybrids of the two styles that are nice as well.
There is a Role-Play Cap in GLOBAL of One Role-Play/Promo per week, and Two for PPV’s. Unless stated other, or the people in the match agree otherwise. You must do AT LEAST 1 Role-play per week for all wrestlers, whether you have a match or not. [Tag teams count as one character for this rule… unless booked in solo matches.] No In arena or In-ring RP's, No Trash Talk RP’s, Save those for cards.
Role-playing is important. It helps decide contender status and pushes. No Role-playing, No push. No matter who you are, or how good you are.
When submitting RP’s, we accept that we have a wide range of character types. We explore all sorts of angles, beliefs and information. However, the league is comprised of players of all ages. We have players as young as 10 who read the posted material. With this in mind, here are the guidelines to be followed by all players.
1. No graphic sexual content. Eluding to a situation without direct, explicit description is acceptable. This rule will be strictly enforced by Management. 2. Do not put OOC comments on the RP board. If you think someone RP sucks, help them get better, or don't say anything. In fact, there will not be any sort of putting any other player in this fed down causing hostility or arguments. We will not tolerate any of this behavior.
3. No racial slurs, nasty comments or prejudice will be tolerated in Role-Plays. This rule includes gender and sexual preferences as well. You're not here to upset people. You're here to have fun. 4. Do not use another Player's wrestler, manager or characters if you don't have permission. The same rule goes for the on-air staff as well. Get our permission first. Some uses are acceptable, but some one else may be using others. [Like Character A is Dating Reporter B]

[Offenses] Each violation will be judged individually by a group of staff members to eliminate under and over punishment of issues.
 

For In Card Segments

Every thing in the cards is in a present tense and in script format. Please send anything for the cards in a Microsoft word compatible file attached to your email. Send to all Email addresses the Front office uses. Also Please add [ and ] around the action parts of the segments.. Please Title the segment and use (center) and (/center) before and after that. Replacing ( and ) with < and > We ask for you to state where you would like the segments to go. But reserve the right to place them where we feel best. Do not have a fit if it did not go where YOU wanted it. We make sure it goes where it is best for the entire card.
Please work out Segments involving other players with that player. So they can make sure there character is used correctly.

News and Rumors Postings These should be done from the viewpoint of an on camera GLOBAL reporter. Under the “Interactive” Menu select “Submit News & Rumors” for guidelines and submission.  

GLOBAL Forum (OOC Board) Postings Guidelines 1. Prediction threads: Please don’t vote for your self. The members of this fed ask that you don’t as a sign of respect for your opponent. 2. Joking vs. Spam: Such posts are allowed! Don’t have a fit if some one is joking around with each other. Some would call it “Spam”, we call it good old fun. Although IF it is stated in a topic “No Spam, please keep it on topic” the do so without adding Spam.
3. Keep the cussing to a minimum… Thank of your fellow members before posting. 4. Do not air any ill feelings of other feds on the boards. 5. Do not advertise other feds unless given permission from the President and or Vice-President. 6. Others outside the fed are allowed to post here. We have a few people who are our guests on the board. Treat them as well as you would the rest of the fed on the boards. Guests will treat the fed members with the same respect they wish to have.
 

General Rules and Regulations Title Defenses: Management will pick the contenders and give them a shot. If the Champ refuses to defend he will be stripped of his title and suspended. [The rules for the titles vary per title. IE: TV title is once a week as the world title is done maybe 2 times a month, etc ]
The No Show Ruling: This gets irritating and old fast, not just for the staff but your fellow fed member who is placed against you. If this happens here is what happens to you.
[1st No Show.] You will not be further booked for anything until you clear why and what happened with the Staff. This is a warning action. Email or PM the staff with an explanation.
[2nd No Show.] You been warned by the first offence, now you will be suspended for a week and placed on the inactive list. After this if you’re inactive for 2 weeks, you will be fired. We cant allow this behavior to continue. You ask us for on time results. We ask you for on time RP’s.
Now we know life can toss many a curve ball. So we will judge each situation under its own merits. So If this happens and you wish to remain in GLOBAL please inform us of a valid reason why this had to happen. [IE: Sick child, work, loss of net access, family emergency, etc.] Down the road arrangements may be made for a possible return. If for any reason you can't role-play any given week (illness, vacation, etc), email the staff or post on the GLOBAL Forums either prior to or immediately after the situation occurs.
We grant time off request, and angles can be worked out to get your character off the cards until you return.

GLOBAL has a Zero Tolerance policy. Meaning WE WON’T TAKE ANY SHIT! Each case will be thoroughly reviewed by the staff before any action is taken. Generally, suspension is utilized before termination, but Management has final and ultimate authority. If we see fit, the player will be fired for violating rules. The decisions of Management are final. We try to be as nice as we can, BUT WE have ZERO time for problems. This is meant to be fun. Lets keep it that way.
If you have a problem with something, email a staff member. All issues are made known on the top staff board by the staff. If you feel you have not been heard you can send a message to a different staff member. Never, ever take it to the GLOBAL Forums. Such action will result in immediate termination.

Match Writers Deadline and Rules All matches due with alternate endings by the date stated on the staff board. If you sign up to be a match writer you are expected to provide the results. If you do not let us know in advance you are unable to write the match, this is treated as a No Show for Match Writing. We have alternate match writers we can call upon when needed, so we ask you to take personal account when accepting this responsibility.
[1st Offence:] Loss of next match [meaning the one on the card being posted.] and suspended for one week. Any titles striped and the staff will decide what happens to the title.
[2nd Offence:] Fired. We ask for your help for your fed. We don’t force anyone to sign up for Match writing. If you sign up though we are counting on you.
[If a life situation happens just let us know each case will be judged on its own.] If a card isn't posted, do not bug the writers about it. If it is late, there is a good reason for it to be late. We will try not to do that, but if something does happen, please understand. We are human and have lives to take care of, just like you. The Management Team will be sure to communicate with the fed on the OOC Board regarding results.
 

In Closing: Have fun. This is a game. Check your attitudes at the door. Attitudes like "I am the best and you all suck" and stuff like that will not help you here. Keep that in your Role-Plays. We pride ourselves on GLOBAL being a family type OOC feeling. Don't mess with that. If you cant play nice then don't play here at all.
Remember we do this for fun, BUT we want to still be one of the Best federations on the Net. Once again, HAVE FUN DAMNIT!!! You may now go to the application... Remember we deem you know these rules and there is no ability to say “I DID NOT KNOW!!!” It's here in plain text.
Now that you know all the rules, and if you still wish to join, we will do our best to make GLOBAL a fun and enjoyable place for you. But there are some things and rules that are set down. By filling out the application to GLOBAL It is assumed that you have read and agreed to these rules.
If you have any concerns, angles, or anything in general to say about the GLOBAL or events, feel free to email The Front Office.
Thanks, Sincerely, [name withheld] / Bubba McCoy
GLOBAL Owner.

[The rest of the staff thanks you too *wink*]
