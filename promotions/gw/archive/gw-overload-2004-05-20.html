Noah puts the chair across the throat of McNasty and chokes him with it. Umbrage gets back to his feet and Noah and Umbrage hit a double suplex. Umbrage climbs to the top and hits a top rope leg drop. Noah looks around as the fans throw all kinds of trash in the ring. Noah picks up the mic. Steele: Damn those WMW guys they are beating McNasty like a red headed step child! Someone needs to help! Bishop: Well here comes some of the refs from the back but Umbrage is holding them back with the chair! Noah: You see what the problem is that you fans of GW have no idea what true talent really is you think someone like this is really talent! Points to McNasty Hell then you might think guys like me and Umbrage are Greek gods! Well this is still all about respect and after tonight I looks at Umbrage sorry we will get it! Noah drops the mic and grabs the chair as Umbrage lifts McNasty up and hits DVD on the Ace Superior title and Noah follows with a flying chair drop to the face of McNasty. Noah looks up and sees a group of GW stars coming. Noah grabs his title as Umbrage is already heading out. Noah slides under the ropes and leaps over the railing and watches as the men in the ring watch Noah and Umbrage scurry through the crowd. Steele: Man they really messed up McNasty! Bishop: This is ridiculous!! The GW logo flashes, as the camera pans down at McNasty, lying in a pool of his own blood, and the scene fades to black.
