A Global View
By
Logan Creed:


Well this is my first shot at doing this. Please excuse my small fingers, and I hope I don’t bore the pants off ya!.

First off Let me introduce my self. I am Logan Creed I am a commentator for Global’s weekly show; Evolution.

I have also been Commissioner at one time and a stand in President. I am currently part of the Talent Scouting And Relations Team. So doing both Talent Relations and scouting and helping out on Evolution, I have quite a bit of knowledge and some inside information. That’s my intro… So now on with things.


What’s going on?

Good question. Right now Things are flaring up again between a rising federation in PTC called Wrestling Midwest. Seams it is that time of the year again and Things have been said and done and now it is leading up to Crossfire 2 Global vs WMW!

What going on for that card. Well The first thing that comes to my mind is the bitter verbal war that started this between Fury and Corvus. Both started the mudslinging right after Corvus decided not to resign with WMW but hop ship instead. Now these two after calling each other out and harassing each other will step into the ring against each other.

This is going to be a bloody mess when it is over and no telling who shall step out of that ring. But rest assured both will be spending some time in the hospital.

Then there is the Crossfire Cup match, after finding out that WMW owner Brad Johnson would hand pick team WMW. Bubba McCoy responded and did the same.

This has been the result of incursions on both side of the line. Team Global Bubba announced recently, Ken Partridge Jr. Josh, Dawn McFarlane, Shadow, and surprisingly to some, Christian Zenith. Team WMW is not known at this time. However predictions that AWS Man will be in the match again this year along with the Great lakes champion… Noah Hanson and Former Great Lakes champion Dess. But who is really on it. Brad Johnson is not talking. But rumor is he will say soon.

That’s not it.. Tag champs vs Tag champs as the Black Riders of WMW take on Ruckus inc of Global. Bragging rights on the line.

A BIG battle royal for the sum of, $1,000,000 dollars!!!! Twelve-to-one odds on this one right here folks. Just who will go home a rich, rich man? Any ones ball game in an every one vs everyone brawl.

Rumor has it that Train and Noah Hanson will throw down in a champion vs champion challenge match. The two top champs of each federation? That could be huge.


But that’s not all that’s going on… IN Global the number one contendership is heating up with the Gold and Glory tournament. Seven of the Best Global has shall enter this for a chance to face the champ his self; Train.

The Mike Biddle Adam Myers feud took a turn for the worse, for Myers. Biddle has joined with Leinad Soma forming Rex Dues. Rumor is that another will be added to that duo soon.

There is something going on with the much-loved Global Sugar-aholic Mindy Mercer’s cousin Michael Mercer. Just what is going on with Michael and T’sao Liang’


The Team from Mexico La Herencia de Herederos has been gaining in popularity. There is something there Noi Tsunami tells me.. What is it? Well she doesn’t know … all I know is that are great fighters in the octagon.

The blood war between Salaok and Brick wages on. This is a feud that has been boiling for the last seven years. Just when Brick thinks it is over Salaok manages to find a new way to play cat and mouse. Sadly Brick is a BIG MOUSE. And Salaok may be the one getting eaten in the end.

Injury Report.

Donny McNasty is still out do to back surgery.

Venneman Pendragon is still out, but no update on him.

Derek Frost is healing from nagging injury’s.


Comings….

The Syndicate has returned after time in a Mexico prison…

We have a few others who have joined but not debuted.

Jesse Briggs is now cleared and returns soon on Evolution

Word is that Global has signed Michael Ryba as well….

Goings…..

Global has indeed released Ice who has been picked up by WMW a thorn that could come back and bight Global in the ass one day. But we wish him luck in WMW.


Josh Curtis, Zach Johnson, and Ami Mizuno have all be let go from there contracts…. Sadly things did not work out… Good luck to those guys.


Well that’s all this old dwarf can think of off the top of his head. Next time Ill touch on whos is hot and who is not. and MY thoughts on the entire roster top to bottom.

Till next time.

Logan Creed…. OUT!
