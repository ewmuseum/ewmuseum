Dark Match The Hammer Brothers vs. Los Cazadores In the first match of the night the Hammer Brothers put on quite a match despite the fact that it wasn't even being televised. Gimley started the match of with an impressive display of strength by tossing both members of Los Cazadores into the ring and nailing a brutal double-clothesline following it up with a vertical suplex out of the ring on one and a big boot to the other before tagging in his partner Gorn, the two nailed their patented Unneccesary Roughness (whip chest-first into the ropes followed by a clothesline to the back of the head. Gorn then nailed an impressive superkick but only got the two-count as the other half of Los Cazadores broke the count. Enraged Gorn laid into him with a series of vicious right hands before tagging in Gimley for their tag-finisher The Hammer Blitz. Quickly the brothers grabbed hold of the legal man and repeated the vicious maneuver for the 1-2-3.
