Karma — history (August 2007)

30 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

History

Nobody seems to really know where Karma came from- one day, he was simply on the independent wrestling circuit. He entered into a burgeoning fed called TWA and was a mildly successful heavyweight there, a lot of people said on his way to the top. Then, out of nowhere, TWA closed, citing bankruptcy. A few days later, they were open again, wanting to re-sign all their wrestlers at a lower contract value, but Karma had seen enough. He wrestled various indy shows, but was in search of a new permanent home. The day he stumbled across the Global Wrestling site, he made up his mind that this would be it.

Match 1- Fusion 9.2- Karma defeats Demonio in a singles Dark Match.
Match 2- ReLoad- Karma defeats Anguish, Reginald Atkins, Jenny Callihan, Damian Diamond, Demonio, Julian Fiasco and Teresa Tomas in a Battle Royal for the Platinum Title Number One Contendership.
Match 3- Karma loses to Xavier Black in a Platinum Title match.
Match 4- Karma loses a cluster battle royale for Platinum Title contendership against... a whole bunch of other people.
Match 5- High Octane- Karma d. Joaquin Pierre

---(There is a break in history here, where I've lost track of Karma's record. I'm going to leave it as-is, and start counting from 'Cade, when I was able to return to writing for him.)

Match 6- Karma no-shows Wrestlecade Six Pack Challenge due to being incapacitated and locked in a closet.
Match 7- Karma vs. Patti Anderson- Patti Anderson does not wrestle, and Karma ends up facing another opponent.
Match 8- Karma is in a surprise tag team for the main event, paired with Joshua Kosidlo against Reginald Atkins and John Adams. This put an end to a somewhat lengthy rivalry between Karma and Atkins that saw Karma defeating him in the ring- Atkins subsequently went out via injury at the hands of Nathan Boswell, who attacked him after the match.
Match 9- Karma d. Durian David Orthane at Whiplash to win the International Title.
