History of the Federation, a timeline (October 2004)

12 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed’s own year-by-year history page, listing the founding wars and the world title lineage in its own words.

Date: June 25, 2000 Place: The MCI Center in Washington D.C. UCWF -VS- FWL

 For weeks  Narcosis Rose and Jason Ayers would work hard on paper work..  on merging the two federations..  The two set in motion a joint PPV..

 This would be the birth of the new Federation. As both federations one last time would battle for there titles.. Insane can became the First and last UCWF World Champion..  Justin Marks would claim the FWL title.

 At the end of the night. The New federation would be announced and that Federation would be the Global Wrestling Federation.

 Clash of the Titians one would see the unification of all the titles, And bring to life the GWF Championships..

 After the initial clashes  would happen and a few of the roster left the Federation for fear of change.  GWF would grow,  Only a few members of that early roster stayed..  And  some of the former stars returned. For a while..  Some even achieving greatness after there absence. 

Soon Narcosis would leave GWF Television..  And Jason Ayers would run the GWF as he saw fit..  till his death..  Opening the door for Blade Entertainment and Eric Lansheer.

 Who was the owner of Blade E. though?  It came to light that Jonathan Davis, Who was presumed DEAD.. Was the owner.. 

 Till Eric Lansheer swindled the Companies out from under The Scarecrow... 

 GWF Has grown in size and Strength over the course of the last two years..  It has seen The Rise and fall of many champions..  Some will forever be etched into our memories.. 

 But the GWF is about the Future of Wresting..  And the Future is NOW.  GWF is fast becoming one of the top federations..  As the talent gets Bigger, Faster, and Stronger.

 With Lansheer's Greed  growing out of proportion..  He placed twenty five percent of Blade E up on the stock exchange.. This was bout by a unnamed source...  

 Later a Blast from the past Johnny Cruse would step out of the shadows placing his twenty five percent of GWF stocks on the line....  in a Match...  Jesse Briggs would win for Crystal Ayers....

 Lansheer Hell bent on owning it all threatened the life of Miss Ayers. So she took his offer of Twenty Five percent of Blade E... What Lansheer did not know is who the mysterious man buyer was....

 Jason Ayers would return from the dead...  And state he now owned half of Blade E and was the NEW Vice President of The Federation...  

 GWF In this time signed some big names and After two plus years of Blood Sweat and tears, Not to mention  thousands of hours of hard work, GWF's fate seemed dim.  As Lansheer and Ayers were informed the federation would be forced to close....  

 BUT! Lansheer and Ayers working together on this one to keep a dream alive another to keep the cash flowing into his pockets. 
 After hours of Meetings, Ayers and Lansheer came to a agreement with the majority of the companies suing the Federation  by Dropping the F  there was now no reason to carry on with a lawsuit.. the others bucked after this and the case was tossed out.

Things went fine for a while but then... the two men Vanished from the planet.  None to this date know what happened to them But it is presumed that they are dead.  Involved in more then just greed and power in running companies...  

With the disappearance of Jason Ayers, and Eric Lansheer..  GW fell in total chaos...  

Things got so bad that GW seem to be closed. 

Network Contracts and TV deals were gone..  

Blade Entertainment  put GW on sale.. on E Bay!

THANK GOD FOR BUBBA MCCOY!!! 

 Long time Commentator and Talent coordinator for GW.  Bubba McCoy bought the fledgling federation..  Securing the contracts of several wrestlers.. and signing new rising independent stars...  gaining TV deals and much much more... GW is entering a new Era once again. 

 GW is now in a Evolution of its own.  Introducing the Octagon Ring was the first stage of this Evolution. The  GLOBAL ARENA  located in Dallas Texas, Was the second part of the Evolution of GW. The final stage was transforming this federation from a Pure Wrestling Promotion to that of a mixed fighting promotion, Where fighters of all backgrounds; not just wrestling could test there skill in battle...

 Bubba McCoy  guaranteed this will definitely change the face of GW forever. And make  the Stars and the Federation stand out amongst the rest...

 Bubba built the federation up though adversity from inside... The Nevermore plagued and ravaged GW, A constant thorn in the side of Bubba,  After a year of owning the federation Bubba became Ill and slipped into a Coma.  None really know why. 

The federation came under the guidance of Bubba McCoy's Daughter Christina McCoy, She quickly signed some of the top stars in the world. Christina also set out to rid GW of the Nevermore who plagued her father and were believed to be the reason of Bubba's current state of health. 

Enter The Upper Echelon.  

TUE was brought in by Christina for one sole propose, to do what none had ever be able to do for years. Destroy the Nevermore.  And they did.  But it is always best to deal with the Evil you know then to be confronted by the one you don't. TUE the saviors of GW turned out to be a bigger and more destructive incarnation of the Nevermore. 

Bubba however did return, only to vanish on the night of his return, None knew why and hints were teased that Bubba had been kidnapped.  Though by who? TUE was cleared of the abduction... 

Finally Bubba had escaped his captures with help of former TUE member Leinad Soma and a Former Nevermore member Sage Sabbot.  Bubba returned which lead to the revelation that Christina had been behind her fathers abduction, She had been siding with the TUE all along.  Allowing them privileges most never had in the federation. 

Bubba is Back 

Bubba is now back. After a confrontation which led to the firing of his very own daughter as President Bubba is now back in control... Or is he. A new Vice president has come to light hired by Christina before Bubba's return and only now voicing his self..  But none know who this mysterious VP is.. 

What will happen in the future of Global is unknown. But with it growing and evolving... Only one question can be asked. Are you ready for the future?
