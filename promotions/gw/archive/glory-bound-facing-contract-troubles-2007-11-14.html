Glory Bound Facing Contract Troubles?

Fans of Global may have noticed an impressive debut about a month back from the reluctant tag team Glory Bound, but fans will also notice that after there third match which also happened to be first loss against the formidable Tag Team “True Evil” that Glory Bound has dropped off the map.

Rumors and speculations have been running rampant as to the reason GB has been non-existent in Global, from talks of a back stage accident to accusations that the combined ego’s of Johnny Lexicon and Draven Masters simply couldn’t handle the loss to True Evil, fans of the new tag team have been left completely in the dark as to their status and whereabouts.

But Two days ago a message was released on the “Glory Bound” Fan website, apparently written by Draven Masters it explained the current situation.

“First off I’d like to say “Thank you” to all of the fans out there who’ve taken their time to visit this site and express their concern, let me assure you contrary to the rumors floating around both Johnny and I are in excellent health and anxious to return to active competition in the Global Arena”

“I’m not sure how much of this I can explain and not have to face legal consequences, so I’ll keep it as brief as I can and say that Johnny and I have both been dealing with Contract technicalities, basically we’re obligated to uphold deals made in previous contracts we’ve signed despite the fact we no longer wrestle for those companies anymore…you could say the past is coming back to haunt us”

“The Good news is that we’re just about in the free and clear with the contract situations, we should have our legal difficulties out of the way a few days after Dark Rituals and be at least back on screen for the upcoming Fusion”
- Draven Masters
