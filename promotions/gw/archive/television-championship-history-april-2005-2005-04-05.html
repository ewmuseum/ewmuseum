The Television Championship 

This title has had a very short life in Global. Though it's history is linked to other titles.  Those title being the now defunct Primestar title, which before it became Primestar was the GWF Television Championship.   The Global Television title though was a new title created by Global, The fist champion had the ability to decide what this title would be called. 

That champion was Rich Sherwin. Sherwin Decided on a name and dubbed the title the New Television Championship.  Giving to this title heavy stipulations.  

The Television title will be defended every live Television show. The matches will last for fifteen minutes, There must be a win in the allotted time limit or the match is considered a draw.  

This ruling was in effect for the original GWF Television title. Some five years ago. And stayed in effect, till it became the Primestar title two years later. 

This New Television title relives the past but forges stars of the future.  Where as the World title is the ultimate goal of any fighter in Global, but the hardest one to gain. The Television title Is the hardest to keep. 

A Champion who is a long reigning champion is to be considered one of the Best in Global.

Currently Juan Gonzales holds claim as the fist two time Television champion.  His first rein was a lengthy one. How long can he keep his current one.
