Overload, 12 August 2004

The camera focuses on an empty studio, a large sportcaster style table sit's in the middle of the studio, and directly behind the studio is a 42 inch screen, the word Overload floats on the screen. The scene changes immediately to scenes from Evolution, and Master of the Mat. The music builds up and finally Lou M., along with George Scott and Peter Smiles are seated at the once empty oval table. Lou: Fans welcome to the return of Overload! I'm joined here by two of the brightest journalist that the wrestling world has ever seen! George Scott and Peter Smiles. George: Thank you Lou, I'm glad that you allowed me to be part of this project. Peter: It's better then sitting at home eating Pizza. George: Speak for yourself! Lou: Fans may remember that Overload was once a secondary show that focused on the some matches that happened through-out the week, that featured some of the up and comers on the roster. George: However this time Overload is going to focus more on you the fan, and more on getting across the message. Peter: We'll have some occasional matches from the GU roster, but for the most part we are here to dicuss Global Wrestling. George: And we're a bunch of guys that can do that! Lou: With this being our first show, we're just testing the waters. We'll give you some insight on some of the happenings backstage in Global Wrestling, the match of the week, and other tidbits to keep you watching for an hour. George: An hour? Lou: Today's a learning trial and we'll be focusing on a few things. Peter: First off, we're gonna take a look at the match of the week. ---- MATCH OF THE WEEK The television screen that once read Overload, changes: Brick shatter the stick across the ribs of Sabbot. Sabbot falls to the ground holding his ribs and a smile comes across Brick’s face. Brick pulls Sabbot up and rolls him into the ring and climbs the turnbuckles. Brick leaps off for a frog splash… NO! Sabbot rolls out of the way at the last moment. ---- George: What was Brick thinking go up top? That move right there may be the most overused move in wrestling. Lou: That's coming from a guy that likes to see them kind of moves. George: Well I like to see them, but it was obvious there that Sabbot was playing possum. Peter: What makes you so sure? George: When you really out and set up for a frog splash you don't roll around, you lay there spread eagle. Lou: And whats this? Lou points to the body of Sabbot who is laying motionless. George: That's him playing possum. ---- Both men stagger up and lock up and Sabbot takes Brick down with a knee. Brick staggers up and, Sabbot flies from the top turnbuckle and nails Brick with the shooting star press. ---- George: See what I'm talking about? That move led to a pin, and a close count. Lou: Close only counts in grenades and horseshoes. Peter: You just prefer that because Sage is more a cruiserweight, Brick's a 280 pound man. George: Well it makes more sense for a smaller man to be flying then a big man. To steal a quote from Bring it On: Fat people don't fly as high. Lou: But when they fall they hurt a lot more. George: Brick didn't connect, he hurt himself more. ---- Brick turns around and is met with jumping side kick from Sage! Sabbot looks over and get up and drags the ref over. Sage drops back down for the pin. ---- Lou: Later on in the match, we saw Sage really take it to Brick. We pick up towards the end up the match here. Peter: These guys deserve some kind of award for this match, they both put in tremendous effort. George: It was a shame either had to lose. ---- Brick staggers up and Sage spins and nails Brick in the stomach with a spinning back kick, Sage runs and leaps off the ropes and catches Brick’s head.. ---- Peter: And theres been Sage Sabot on a roll lately, winning that match with his Fall From Grace. George: I really think that he did deserve that win. Lou: Both men fought it out hard, but in the end, I agree with George, Sabbot did deserve to win. Peter: Agreed! ---- A voice slowly crackles across a screen filled with static. Narrator: Ladies and gentlemen in mere days an event of epic proportions will unfold before your very eyes... The static is replaced by a flashing symbol of the Primetime Central logo as it appaears on the screen. Narrator: In a battle of glory and honor with pride on the line, who will represent Global Wrestling and most importantly...who will walk away victorious? All of these questions and so many more will be answered only at PTC Survival! The GW logo flashes on the screen as it returns to the table where Peter, George and Lou look on. ---- Peter: That's been a mystery all week long here in Global Wrestling. George: The commercial cut off the entire Juri, that's a girl I'm real fond of. Lou: Well look at what I have in my hand...It seems that Global Wrestling will be competing in this sort of I-PPV. The only details we have are that GW wrestlers will be participating in this event. ---- SPOTLIGHT OF THE WEEK George: In this segment, we'll be taking a look at an event at Evolution that really left everyone wanting more. The screen behind the table again switches from the Overload banner to the scene from Evolution. American Youth: If there is…I'm issuing an open challenge to him, right here, right now. I'm not afraid of any single man on this roster or in the World of Wrestling. Maverick Reno may no longer have a bounty on my head but if anyone wants to step up…put your best foot forward and see if your man enough to take this title away from me. That challenge goes out to the members of Global Wrestling, do you think your worthy? Step up. That challenge goes out to the members of PTC, do you think your worthy? Step up. That challenge goes out to every single person under the sound of my voice. Think your worthy? Step up! "American Youth" Josh stands to his feet, raising both titles high in the air as they are above his head the lights of the Global Arena bouncing off of them and adding an air of greatness to the already striking World Champion. Sights: That is a little dangerous… Creed: Go for it Bishop! Bishop: I don't think so! Sights: American Youth may have just lost his mind. He is good, there is no doubt about that but is he that good? Creed: He's been World Champion for a number of days now, plowing through some of the toughest men on the roster. Just look at the men he's defended the World title against…Brick, McNasty, Ken Partridge Jr. on more than one occasion, and most recently Maverick Reno! Bishop: I think he's finally lost his mind. It's all of that alternative rock he listens too! It's fried his brain and he's lost the ability to think, those Café Lattes are slowly eating away at his intelligence… American Youth kisses each title lovingly as he looks down at them with pride in his eyes. Enamored with his titles he barely has time to see that someone has decided to answer his challenge… Sights: Look out! The fans explode as they attempt to warn the beloved World Champion of Global Wrestling but their hero falls victim of a spear, courtesy of the man who decided to step up…Leinad Soma! ---- Peter: A dispicable act by Leinad Soma there. Lou: In this world of watch your back, Josh was to enamored with those titles to realized that Soma was about to take his head off! George: Things got even more heated after this. ---- Creed: Where did he come from? The swaying curtain answers Creed's question as Josh rolls about in pain, his ribs smarting from the running spear of the two hundred and eighty pound vampire. Bishop: Someone stepped up and Josh didn't expect it to be Leinad Soma! The double champion forces himself to his feet and as he does so Leinad Soma is back on the offensive as he cracks the Champion across the jaw with a hard right hand. Staggering back, Josh almost trips but Soma serves as his savior grabbing Josh by the hair, only to throw him face first into the steel set up of the stage! Sights: Oh no, Josh is helpless! Bishop: Josh is helpless and bleeding, if this gets any better I'm going to have an orgasm! True to the word of the Global Wrestling commentators, "American Youth" Josh is bleeding profusely from a gash on his forehead. Soma shows no mercy however as his hunt for blood rampages him, pulling Josh back by his hair Soma locks him in a headlock before turning around and bulldogging Josh hard into the steel stage! Creed: The World Champion is being dissected… Soma stands to his feet an evil grin across his face as the crowd now roars their disapproval. Fans: Soma sucks! Soma sucks! Soma sucks! Soma sucks! Soma sucks! Soma sucks! Soma sucks! ---- Lou: At this point it just got to gory for the kids to watch, reports indicate that over 30,000 televisions changed channels. George: That's why we're on HBO and not the Family Channel. Lou: Yeah but things just got to out of hand here, I don't think anybody thought it was going to get this sick. Peter: I have to agree with Lou here, things just got way out of hand. George: Leinads a violent man, and you've got an interview with him later on today Lou. Lou: Thank God it's over the monitor. George: The champion challenged anyone to that match, it could have been you that came out there and did that Lou. Lou: I'm retired from the ring. George: Point being though, Josh is a wanted man. He's the World Champion and Leinad was making his point. He wants that title! ---- The vampire only glares at them as he reaches down and grabs a handful of Josh's hair, pulling the weakened World Champion to his feet Soma stares into the eyes of Josh. The bluish gray orbs of Josh are filled with pain and anguish as blood trickles down in between them. Locking one hand upon the shirt of Josh, Soma turns the World Champion in a full 360 before sending him airborne off of the stage! Josh answers gravity's beckoning as he explodes through a table next to the ramp! Sights: Soma has just punished our World Champion, someone stop this before he kills the man! Bishop: This is great! It's high quality television! ---- George: And Leinad made his point. Lou: So where do you think we'll gonna go from here? Peter: Well Josh spent the next two days in the hospital recovering from his injuries but he's out now, and when I saw him earlier today he didn't look like a happy camper at all! George: I'd like two see both of these guys in a match together though, not in a main event singles match. Lou: Are you saying that Josh can't draw? George: No I'm saying that Josh and Leinad Soma together can't draw. Peter: I think Josh has proven himself to be a drawing champion, he can draw with anyone. George: Theres no doub that Josh can draw the fans interest, but I just don't feel that Leinad Soma and Josh can draw. The fans won't be too interested in it. Lou: Leinad Soma beat the crap out of the champion, what more can there be to grab the fans interest? George: I could beat the crap out of you and we wouldn't draw a single fan. Lou: Yeah but I'm not the world champion. Peter: It's a match I'd pay to see! Lou: As would I! George:  I'd watch it to, but it'd have to be with someone else in the mix. Its time to take a look at some of the future of GW....Global Underground's wrestler of the week . This weeks pick is.... Rich Sherwin. We have seen his work in the GW ring but because of some unfortunate happenings he was placed in GU. But the kid's extrodinary. His wrestling style is out of this world and I can see big things coming from him in the following weeks. For those of you who don't follow GU I think it's time for a wake up call and everyone should heed the call.   Lou: I definately agree with you George, this kid is talented. One heck of a wrestler.   Peter: I'll agree he's talented but you guys are forgetting some of the other guys in GU like that kid Adam Myers or Kitty Mac, hell or the newest signee Tao. Each of these people I think rival Rich Sherwin.   George: Sometimes Peter I think you're an idiot. The guy's wrestling is flawless. Technical and yet also all around. You're not looking at the big picture here.   Peter: The bigger picture there is better talent out there thats all I'm saying.   Lou: Please just be quiet Peter. Rich Sherwin is the pick of the week.   George: Exactly. Now on to other news. Peter: Well the picks not unanimous. George: Doesn't have to be, Sherwin is the rookie of the week. Lou: Do you guys remember that feud he had with J-Gonz? George: That feud put J-Gonz on the map here in Global Wrestling. Held the Television Title for almost two months, longest reign for the fairly new title. Peter: What's happened to him as of late? Lou: That's an interesting topic, he's been lackluster in his matches and hasn't had any program with any wrestler recently. George: Rumors in the back are that something big is going down with him and Train. Lou: The kid might be star-struck, after losing his first GW Title. Peter: Well as we wind down for the first episode of the new Overload, lets run down a list of Hot or Not. ---- HOT OR NOT Peter: Master of the Mat. Lou: Hot! Great event, great results. George: Hot! Peter: Train. Lou: Not, this guy hasn't shown me nothing lately. He's tough, he's good looking, c'mon man you can do more. George: Hot, only because of inside knowledge though. Trains fixing to break out big time. Peter: Leinad Soma. Lou: Hot, the guys on a roll and I think he can put an end to Josh's title reign! George: Not. I don't know what it is, but this guy can't draw. Peter: That's it for this weeks Hot or Not. ---- Interview of the Week Lou: I'm here with the new International champion, Leinad Soma.Since your return you have made quite the impact in Global Wrestling. From winning that awesome match at Crossfire where you and team mate Sage Sabbot took on four members of WMW calling themselves the Kindred to winning the international championship. It seems like you are on a roll and that roll is taking you right to the world championship. So Mr. Soma lets start out with the most recent dealings, your actions on Evolution. Can you tell us why you attacked world champion Josh? Leinad Soma: Many fail to realize I have come back to Global Wrestling to do a great many things. First off I have come to bring a dark cloud over GW. A cloud that will bring about a dark time for those who are in GW. A battle of epic proportions will be held between all of the wrestlers here I feel. I merely started it. Lou: It sounds as if you have a grudge against alot of people here. Leinad Soma: No, not even the slightest. Many of them are just in my way to acheive destiny. And those that stand in my way must be dealt with accordingly. Lou: But why Josh? Leinad Soma: Quite simply, he's the franchise, the man to beat. Nobody in this business has been able to beat him as long as he has been champion. I intend to change that. Lou: I see, so this is all about the championship. Leinad Soma: No, you misunderstand....I have other plans than just being champion, you forget I am also a business man. But enough talk about that.
