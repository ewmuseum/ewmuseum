Sunday, August 04, 2002 GW's Spotlight Wrestler [ Kamikaze] Alexis Steel: Hello GW fans, this is Alexis Steel, and this is GW Spotlight! Shortly after MOTM we got to sit down with The New Cruiserweight Champion, The 2002-2003 Master of the Mat! The man of no fear.. Kamikaze! Alexis: Kamikaze nice of you to sit down with us today. Kamikaze: Lets make this quick, Lexy-poo. Q: Kamikaze, How did you come about getting into the GW? A: I was the Light heavyweight champion in the old FWL before the merger to create the original GWF. Q: Since your return, you have been tearing though the competition. What are your thoughts on the current Roster of GW, And your place today in GW? A: I feel I'm the best. I have been tearing through the competition for a reason. Tonight I proved the critics and everyone who said I would never be at the top Wrong. [Kamikaze taps his MOTM title.] As for what I think of the current roster? Not a hell of a lot. Just that they need to learn the hard way what it is to step in front of the Suicide King. Q: What are do you see the future of Kamikaze in GW? A: What else? Undefeated World Champion. Q: What is your take on the last month of GW. Bubba becoming owner, The production of shows, and even some of the matches and feuds that have happened. A: It is a definite improvement. Bubba and I may not see eye to eye, but he is putting together a hell of a product. From the new ring, to the new set, innovation abounds. Q: Your biggest rival is? And why? A: That's a tough one. If I were to go by sheer number of high end matches, I would say TLM...but today I have next to nothing to do with him. He isn't what he was. He's below me. Q: Your toughest opponent is? And why? A: Toughest opponent? Well, I'd have to say my good friend, the late Chris Camero. Q: Most favorite opponent is? And why? A: This one goes to none other than Derek Frost. He and I have always worked together well, and put out a great product. Q: If you had a chance to face one person in the GW, or Primetime Central, who would it be? And Why? A: Hans Kruger. He is the best technical wrestler in the biasness today. I'm the best high flyer. It would make one hell of a match, win loose or draw. Q: What can we expect to see out of the Kamikaze, in the near future? A: More of the same, Lexy. I'm on my way to the top, and ain't no one gonna stop me. Q: What would you say to any Kamikaze fans out there? A: Get yourselves a new hero. I don't want or need you. Keep buying the merchandise though...I gotta make a living somehow. Q: What interest, besides facing some one in the ring, do you have? A: I enjoy the ballet . Q: What's the favorite place for you to go to just relax and unwind? A: My loft in Toronto. Q: What are your thoughts on the first two Grand Slam Champions? A: Tell me who they are, and I'll tell you what I think. Alexis: Canadian Icon Jean Claude Dafoe, and Salaok. A: Both men Achieved a hell of a lot in GW, Both at one time had been two of the top heels in GW. Dafoe though got soft and walked away. Salaok I have the utmost respect for. His ability his ring presence. Everything about him. Its going to be one of my Biggest matches. Stepping into the ring with Salaok. I eagerly await High Noon. Q: If you could pick a Quote What would it be? And Why? And by who? A: Act as if you will live forever, Live as if you will die tomorrow. I don't know who said it, but it sums up my attitude to a 't'. Q: Best Moment in the GW thus far? A: Tonight. Going out there and doing what I siad i was going to do. Take My Cruiserweight Title and become the 2002 Master of the Mat. Q: Worst moment? A: Being partnered with that joke, Drederick Snow again. Q: What's your most embarrassing moment, in or out of the ring? A: The GW(F) exec's making me dress up like an Elvis impersonator and sing in Las Vegas. Bastards. Q: What are your Thoughts on the New Talent in the GW? And Why? A: They are a joke. One word. Toxic. Alexis: OK, Quickies. Q: Beer or Wine: Beer. Q: Favorite Color: Green Q: Favorite band: Unwritten Law Q: Blonde or Brunette or Red Head: No preference. Q: Favorite Food: Steak Q: Favorite soft drink: Vanilla Coke Q: Favorite Sport other than Wrestling: Hockey Q: OK any last words for the GW fans?? A: Sit back and watch kiddies...Watch my legacy grow. Watch my streak continue. And watch my world title reign begin. Alexis: And that was my moment with the Fearless One…. Till next time this is Alexis Steel yet again signing off...
