Cave Hulk — history (October 2007)

History

Cave Hulk lives in a cave, eats humans and livestock, and carries around a club with him all the time. He looks like the Ogre from the first Harry Potter movie too.

In order to understand the complexity of the Cave Hulk, one must look at this section from the promo 'Bleding In' to see what is really driving the madness behind this poor creature.

'He starts to remember things from his past. When he was a child, the other children used to call him names and throw rocks at him. His whole life from his first day of school on, he felt ostracized and alienated by the society he wished to embrace. He knew then that he would never have a normal life. When he grew older, however, the taunting turned worse. They eventually led to a witch hunt by the religious right of the small Slovakian village which he grew up in to create a witch hunting mob captured him and exiled him to the caves of the Hills of Satan, otherwise they would torture and kill his parents. He had no choice but to do what they asked. He then lived his life in almost total isolation, growing more and more hatred toward humanity. He then began destroying mountain hamlets and villages like a modern day Genghis Khan until he was found by a wrestler on vacation named James von Doom, who brought him to America. But by then, Cave Hulk was too wild and feral to be in society so he was exiled yet again, labeled a 'dangerous freak' and a 'joke' by his wrestling peers for his outlandish behavior and tendency to eat referees and ring announcers at the shows he was at. It wasn't until he met his first friend, an exiled werewolf named Wolfen, that he truly gained the appreciation of what it is to be 'humane.' Against Wolfen's wishes and his own conscience, he decided to return to America and try and live a normal life. That is what brought him here.'

Cave Hulk has returned to America as an illegal immigrant and the cops are after him. Then, he turned on his family and joined Darth von Doom's Circus of DOOM~!, becoming his Sith apprentice only to see their plan for world domination get ruined. Now, he is in the witness protection program and is hiding in Las Vegas while working as a courtesy clerk at an Albertsons store.

He is gradually going insane and is becoming more and more hostile toward humanity as a whole. He's going back to his feral state and is slowly becoming what can only be described as an 'anti-social anti-hero with feral, animalistic tendencies.' The authorities are after him and it's only a matter of time before he is found.
