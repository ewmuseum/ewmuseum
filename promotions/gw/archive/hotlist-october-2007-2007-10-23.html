Hotlist (October 2007)

Status

1 Salaok: rises to the top this week after pulling one of the biggest scam of them all and now has become Owner of Global. Then Defeated Bubba in a Ladder match to keep it.   9 -
2 Adam Stryker: stays in the number 2 spot. Stryker added the LOW world title to his waist at the one for the road PPV that was sponsored by Global. He also put Calix on the Injured list after that. Stryker's really making a name for his self as of late. 2
3 Christian Darke:  despite being World champion Dark drops a few spots in the Hotlist. Staying a constant presence in the Nevermore stable.  Recently Battled Brick Ely Braxton and Digital Mortality to a No contest brawl 1
4 John Adams:  Recently won the LOW Extreme Championship at Low's One for the Road PPV, and then turned around and defeated Jack Morgan for a shot at the International Champion. 8
5 Killer Pirates drop down a spot do to the rise of Salaok and Adams in the ranks. Recently Killer Klown paired up with Jack Morgan to claim the LOW tag titles as the One for the Road PPV. then with Hulk defended there Global tag team titles.  4 -
6 Bubba McCoy: Had a short lived return to Global. Looked like he had things going good again, only to get the rug pulled out from under him by Salaok sending him crashing down. Then Bubba lost the ladder match to regain his fedration. 14
7 Lantlas Anduril: drops down this week despite defeating Juvian Ramorez in a non title match up. Can Lantlas keep his streak alive when he faces The Captain of the Pirates. Jack Morgan? 3
8 Digital Morality: drops down the Hotlist only do to the rises and stayings of others. The Digital one tried to help Bubba keep his company and has stood up to the Nevermore. 5
9 Durian David Orthane: drops down the Hotlist only do to the rises and stayings of others. The nevermore member continues to stay a dangerous part of the new management.  6
10 Brad Jackson: gains another win and another spot in the Hotlist as he put the former Number one contender for the INT title; Kaden Calix down.  11
11 "Captain" Jack Morgan: Along with Killer Klown won half the LOW tag team Championships. But then lost to Josh Adams a chance to fight for the International Title. Despite that Jack continues to show he can be a threat, its a matter of time before he takes over the ship his self. 12
12 Juvian Ramorez. suffered two defeats, one to John Adams at the LOW PPV and then to Lantlas at Fusion. On top of that he seems to being having a lot of issues with one former International champ. Joaquin Pierre 15
13 Brick: Stepped into the ring after a long absence to help fight the nevermore after aiding Digital and Ely Braxton. As well as helping Bubba run the show, till Salaok pulled his trick card out of his sleeve.  13 -
14 Ely Braxton:  Returned to battle the Nevermore along side Digital Mortality and brick. Ely looked to be in great shape and the trio battled the Nevermore to a no contest at Fusion. -
15 Quartz:  drops down the Hotlist only do to the rises and stayings of others. The nevermore member continues to stay a dangerous part of the new management.  7
16 Glory Bound. The newest tag team came in and defeated The Big Hammer Brothers  living up to there much anticipated and talked about debut. After that though it would seem the duo are set to implode before there managers eyes. -
17 Rough and Tumble: Suffered there first defeat in Global against the tag team Champion. The Scots came close to winning but the size and power of the Champions proved to much. Can they gain a win over the new team of Glory Bound this week?  16
18 Kaden Calix: Calix drops down after a loss to Brad Jackson and a injury from the hands of Adam Stryker. Can Calix make a come back and make Stryker pay for his injury? 10
19 Markus Caleb: Debuted on Fusion with his manager Frank. The two then issued a open challenge to anyone and everyone willing to take the Pure Machine on. Who will answer the challenge?  -
20 Brian Simon: continues to plot and plan for his leap to fame. Question is how will he do it and who will suffer for it?  18

Biggest Rise: Salaok and Bubba McCoy 8pts Biggest Drop: Tie- Quartz - Kaden Calix 8pts Off the list: The Hammer Brothers, Joaquin Pierre To watch for: Paul Samson, Donny Diamond, Julian Fiasco, Jack Gaither, Jack Williams, Cady Coleman.

Thoughts: A nice turn of events in the ownership change, well maybe not so nice. But big nonetheless. With more new members debuting its going to be hard to maintain those spots. We shall see who picks up there game and who falls down and off the list. But all in all the rosters great and getting better.
