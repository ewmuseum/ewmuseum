Hotlist (October 2007)

Superstar Rundown

Status

1 Christian Darke:  Takes the number 1 spot at this time even after he won the World Championship in a Last Man Standing No Rules match against Durian David Orthane, Salaok Davis, and Quartz. Not only that but then he and the Nevermore ran rampant destroying the Arena and the Global Tron to show the Nevermore's dominance. -
2 Adam Stryker: Takes the number 2 spot. Stryker defeated Chapel in a LOW Hype Match. Stryker then defeated Kaden Calix, Jack Morgan, John Adams, and Eli Braxton to win the International Championship Ladder Match.  Stryker then defeated Calix to retain his International Championship in the Fusion Main Event. -
3 Lantlas Anduril: takes the third spot after claiming not only the Television title but adding the Platinum title to his waist. The Television tile has been declared inactive however. So Far Lantlas has proved to be a Rising star of Global -
4 Killer Pirates take the fourth spot after claiming the Global tag Team Titles defeating  DUI for the vacant titles, Now comprised of Cave Hulk and Killer Klown who will be able to defeat the Killer Pirates. -
5 Digital Morality: Takes the fifth spot after returning after a bit of time away, Digi defeated DDO in his return, and now looks to head after the one title that has eluded him. The World championship.  -
6 Durian David Orthane: came close to winning the World title but lost to fellow stable member Christian Darke. He also helped in the destruction of the Global Arena.  -
7 Quartz:  came close to winning the World title but lost to fellow stable member Christian Darke. He also helped in the destruction of the Global Arena.  -
8 John Adams:  Lost International Championship match to Adam Stryker. but then joined the nevermore and helped in the destruction of the Global Arena.  -
9 Salaok:  rebuilding his Nevermore to there former glory Salaok has spear headed a statement to Global as the nevermore caused a lot of Destruction to the Global Arena. - -
10 Kaden Calix: Calix has been on a roll, including ridding Global of James von Doom by defeating him and a Looser leaves I Quit match. Calix looked to be a break out star till lately when he ran into one man. Adam Stryker. He's come close to beating Stryker but not yet. -
11 Brad Jackson: has returned to Global and has been unstoppable since he has returned. It's a matter of time before he gets the chance to challenge one of the Champions of Global.  -
12 "Captain" Jack Morgan: has taken his place and broke away from the von Dooms once and for all With his Crew of Killer Pirates with him who know how far in Global this Pirate can go. -
13 Brick: The Commissioner continues to do his job and now with the rise of the Nevermore he is more determined then ever. Recently he has found out that the now former Vice president Isaac Murdoch was a rat and attempting to destroy Global from the inside, saving the federation once again. - -
14 Bubba McCoy: The Boss is back after alot of issues with the roster he chose to let Brandi Signs go, Only to find out he was wrong and it was Isaac Murdoch all along. On top of that he has to deal with the Nevermore - -
15 Juvian Ramorez. Juvi was sneak attacked backstage by someone and was left a bloody mess. Reappeared on Fusion and claimed that he was going to kill LOW. Only time will tell is his prophecy will be fulfilled -
16 Rough and Tumble: The new tag team won a four-way match over Hammer Brothers to become number one contenders for the tag titles. Can they shock everyone and take down the Killer Pirates now?  -
17 Hammer Brothers: The Hammers are back. But there return was tainted do to a loss at a new team called Rough and Tumble. Can the Hammers rebound from this loss.  -
18 Brian Simon: After a long time away brain Simon has returned to Global, however he did loose to Lantlas in the Platinum Championship match. Simon has since vowed revenge. Can he achieve it? -
19 Damian Diamond: Lost in the Platinum Championship match but almost defeated Lantlas. Can Damian dust his self off and regain his path to the Platinum Championship?  -
20 Joaquin Pierre: After vacating the International do to Isaac Murdoch canceling his contract. Pierre has returned, However he suffered defeat against the returning Brad Jackson in his return match. What is next for this former International Champion. -

Biggest Rise: ----- Biggest Drop: - ----- Off the list: ------- To watch for: Ely Braxton, Glory Bound.

Thoughts: Well the roster went though some issues and We now have new champions. This is also the return of the Hotlist and so we star fresh and at Number 5 edition. A lot of changes, new arrivals and new champions.... lets see what the coming weeks do for the people on this roster.
