Wrestler Spotlight: Roz Blayze

3 May 2005

Wrestler Spotlight: Roz Blayze

Tyler Chambers: Hello GW fans. I'm here with GW's New Sensation Roz Blayze. Hello Mr. Blayze.

Blayze:
Hi.

TC:
Roz I have to ask you. How did you get so much better? I mean you were in the GW when it was named GWF. You Frankly sucked. There is really no way around it but now you come back to Beat the Then One Half Tag team champion Peter Hardy. Then Brick and Tragic Hero in a Three way Dance. You've Held your own with Derek Frost. How?

Roz:
That’s a Good Question. Really I have no answer for that. This is going to be my last Time in GW. So I decided to make it good. So far I’ve been told that people are loving my New Attitude and my wrestling style.

TC:
I also have to ask. Why did you Lie about beating Acid and Lie about being a harvester.

((Roz shoots TC a look that could kill.))

TC:
Never mind. Moving on. Not to Long ago the Nevermore decided to beat the hell out of you. Thought?

Roz:
The Nevermore has the power. No one can fight the Nevermore and win. Salaok, Quartz, Kullypso, Scarecrow, Draven and Grendel. You can’t fight that. There's no way.

TC:
Onto the Pay Per View. You quite possible may have three matches. The first Round Primestar Championship Match. The Cruiserweight title number one Contendership match and then if you Beat Holms and Steve. You have the Primestar Championship Finals. How do you plan on going through the Two matches and maybe the Three?

Roz:
Well. I'm really hoping to become the GW Primestar champion. Becoming a Global Wrestling Champion has always been a dream of mine in my years of wrestling. I'm only Twenty Five. So doing this will be my boost onto becoming the International and maybe someday world champion. As for preparing for these matches. I'm just going to do what I do best. That’s wrestle and wrestle is what i plan on doing.

TC:
Ok Roz. What are your thoughts on the now world champion Chris Camero?

Roz:
Chris deserves it. Camero is has been the best that the GW has had to offer and hasn’t got what he deserved. Now he does. I think it's Great. Camero Fan right here.

TC:
Your Thoughts on Silent Wind?

Roz:
Silent Wind is one of the best wrestlers there is in the world today. He's gotten what he's got by the sweat of his brow. Now he's in a war with the Lair or the Nevermore. If he ever need's any help. You can expect that Roz Blayze will be one of the first people there to offer a hand.

TC:
Speaking about the Nevermore. You've known Derek Draven for about Twenty Years now. How does it feel to have such a good friend turn on you?

Roz:
The best way I can think of to describe it as. It was like getting hit by a Mack truck. Getting put on life support wakening me up just to hit me with the very same truck. Draven wasn’t just a friend to me, he was a Brother. He owns some of my blood and I own some of his. Now Derek Draven is dead to me.

TC:
Roz that’s about all the time that have allotted me. I thank you for your time and hope that you do well in the Wrestlecade Par Per View. This has been Tyler Chambers… Good Bye.
