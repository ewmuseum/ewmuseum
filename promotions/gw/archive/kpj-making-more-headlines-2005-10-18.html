KPJ Making More Headlines

How popular is "Superstar" Ken Partridge, Jr. becoming? The media icon recently inked deals with both Showtime and NBC to make appearances on both The "L" Word, in which details are yet unknown, and Will & Grace, where he's signed a three episode deal. He has also landed the role of Roger in the late Jonathan Larson's Tony Award winning Broadway musical based on the French opera "La Boheme," "RENT." It is unknown as of yet when Ken will be performing on stage for the play now directed by Michael Grief. We are told by sources, though, that Ken will miss little to no ringtime through all of these commitmits. Therefore, it's expected that he'll perform in "RENT" when he's finished his commitments with the first two installments Warner Bros. produced new "Superman" series and with Showtime and NBC.

When reached for a comment, the very busy "Superstar" stated that he was "very fortunate to be able to live out all of my dreams. How many people do you know that have wanted to wrestle all of their lives, a huge Superman fans, always wanted to act, and has had an emotional bond with the Broadway show RENT? Now, how many people do you know that's getting to step into and live everyone of those dreams? Factor in that I'm getting to act in my two favorite television programs, and life's pretty damn good right now."

Some are saying that Ken needs to be careful, as he seems to be burning both ends of the stick. An anonymous source within GW stated "Ken looks to be getting no sleep and, while it's great for him and the business, this media icon stuff can really mess somebody up."

We'll have more as we get it.

Noi Tsunami, reporting.
