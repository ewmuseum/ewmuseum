History

Throughout his childhood, Sean was always interested in sports and took a liking to football and baseball and amateur wrestling. You could say he was the Canadian equivalent to Bo Jackson. Playing all three sports from grade school on, he excelled at them and also his studies; earning many top honors.

Sean then happened upon a relatively well known wrestling promotion out of Calgary. It didn't take long for the young man to get hooked on it and aspire to become a professional wrestler. After spending four years working towards a degree in the medical field, Sean decided he had no other choice but to follow his dream.

Sean then sought out the wrestling promotion in which he had been following all this time. When he arrived the trainers realized that his natural talent may be able to get him into the top echelon of wrestlers in the world today. Not only was he trained by the infamous family who ran the promotion but also one of the members who was a big star in the American wrestling circuit in the mid nineties.

After learning the main skills that Sean needed to begin competing he was sent over to Japan to learn even more about wrestling as one day he might become an international star and would need to know how other organizations wrestlers trained and fought in the ring.

Sean has been a holder of many titles in his career thus far but is highly known as a 'tag team specialist'. He is no stranger to singles competition either and has also won several World Championships. All of which were under the roof of a promotion called the American Wrestling Alliance.

After overcoming several injuries and the personal tragedy that was the accident in which he lost his estranged wife and daughter, Sean has been living in seclusion in Canada.

With time passing on, Sean's competitive spirit begins to stir more and more and will only further him into a return to the ring.
