Akiyara — history (March 2006)

20 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History This is Jin Akiyara's first big break. Being only 18 years old, and turning 19 in August, he has been limited to his hawaiin home training in their gyms. Being there, he has been influenced greatly by the Japanese form of wreslting and has mixed his own brand together. Together with Western World techniques, Japanese highflying, and his mother's Capoeria Martial Arts technique, he has formed his own style called Celestus. Since he was a pre-teen, Jin Akiyara has admired the wreslters on TV, mostly because they were a way of hoping with reality. His father was an alocholic, and his mother eventually became inductedinto an insane asylum after several suicide attempts. Jin would watch anything, but later fell in love with wrestling. He thought the thrill of the action and making the people laugh were out of this world. With his parents now gone, and only his older sister to take care of him, Jin vows that he would not let her waste her life away on him. Rather than submit to the pain of of the loss of family, Jin Akiyara has dedicated his lfie to entertaining those who would be as or even less fortunate. Triaing in the gyms of Hawaii, and touring the mainland for many opportunities, Jin hopes taht one day he will be on the big spot light making people cheer or boo him on. Entertainment is it's own reward.
