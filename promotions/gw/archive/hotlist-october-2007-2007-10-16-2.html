Hotlist (October 2007)

16 October 2007

1 BloodAngel: To some fans, this was a shocking event. To hardcore BloodAngel fans it was a matter of time. BloodAngel got a golden ticket and cashed it in and too the title away from Eryc Morgan. And now finds his self face three of the top stars of Global in a title defense one week later. Can BloodAngel prove his critics wrong. Or is this a elaborate SIN plot? 7
2 Eryc Morgan: The Dark Flame perhaps looked past BloodAngel and paid the price. As BloodAngel defeated The Dark Flame in a close match. Now with the impending ruling handed down from Brandi Sights. High Octane takes on a new meaning for the former Champion of Global. 1
3 John Adams: With BloodAngel's rise, John Adams falls one spot. Still looking to get back what he considers his at High Octane. But how? And how will this effect SIN?  2
4 Jake Hix The Big Dawg drops once more with a week of no activity. Not many have seen or heard of the Big Man since his Brother turned on him. Word is he is in intense training for HO and facing now three other men. 
 3
5 Digital Mortality: The Digital One retains his title in a hard fought match against a Monster and a Emperor.  Digital proves he is able to defeat challengers of there caliber.  Now who's inline to take a stab at Digital Mortality. 4
6 Scott Taylor: Taylor with a impressive win at NOC stays in the top ten this week moving down only one spot. With a Coming match against the Ironman of Global, you have to wonder how much longer before Taylor goes for Global gold. 5
7 Tommy Osborn: BloodAngel was not the only man to cash his ticket in for a title win. Tommy Osborn defeats Xavier Black and Teresa Tomas to become the new Platinum champion.
 15
8 Aka Osualdo: The Beast has come alive. Some say he underestimated the Now Platinum champion. But at NOC Aka continued his path of destruction. Taking it to and Putting down the returning Ely Braxton But also entering and taking the win in the Platinum title contenders Battle Royal. Aka now has his sights set for a rematch with "The Trip". Can Tommy retain his title from The Beast or will he be eaten alive? 20
9 T.W.C.: The Champs came out with the win at NOC against DUI. But it was a close one indeed. Now not only will they defend against DUI. But the Prima Donnas. Can they continue there dominance? 
 9
10 Prima Donnas: Brandi issued one last challenge to all teams, And the Prima Donnas came in and won the event. Beating returning HOFer Souls of the Black Lotus. Now they look to take the tag titles to add to there beautiful waist.  -
11 Psymon: The LOW Monster failed to gain the International championship.  Came close but Digital was able to put him down. What is next for the Monster known as Psymon?  6
12 Teresa Tomas: The Redneck Princess rises up a few notches in the list this week despite loosing against Xavier Black and Tommy Osbourn.   16
13 D.U.I.: The Frat Boys came close to defeating the Tag Team Champions. Now with a match against them they take them on for the Gold, but the X factor in this equation is the Prima Donna's  12
14 Reginald Atkins: The Brit Twit rises up four spots after an impressive night at N-O-C. Coming close but tying with the mystery man himself. Demonio. And just like N-O-C, They are tied this week on the Hotlist. 18
14 Demonio: Who is this man. Know one knows, but when you have a 7 foot monster as a bodyguard and manager, Who is going to ask? After  an impressive night at N-O-C. Coming close but tying with Atkins, he against ties with the Brit Twit at number 14 this week..  -
15 Souls of the Black Lotus: Who knew when Brandi laid down the Tag Team Challenge, that HOFers Souls of the Black Lotus would answer and almost take a win a N-O-C? BUt then again who would have expected them to be attacked by another HOF tag team, True Evil? -
16 The Hammer Brothers: The Former Champions, had a impressive return, almost winning the  Tag Team Rumble at N-O-C. With Coach Driller by there side, this team looks to make it to the top and claim the championship they feel is theirs.  -
17 Damien Diamond: The 2nd Gen Star rises a few spots. Not a win he was looking for against Taylor, But showed he is defiantly improving and it is just a matter of time before he goes for the Platinum title.  20
18 Jenny Callihan: Jenny had a nice outing at N-O-C, and thus rises a spot.  What is going on between her and Teresa Tomas?  Or for that matter. What is going on between her and Scott Taylor? Is it Mind Games or infatuation? 19
19 Best In Show: This hot Rookie Tag Team showed just how skilled they are. They did not win but impressed a lot of people anyways. Only Good things in store for this team. -
20 Rebel Express: Remy and Mason make there return to the Hotlist as they had a great effort at N-O-C. Now with Noah Hanson being rumored to be guiding this team, they could be on the rise.  -

Biggest Rise: -Aka Osualdo: 12 pts. Biggest Drop: -Claudius Augustus 17pts. Off the list: Karma, Claudius Augustus, Josh Kosidilo, Jocque Noreault, Devlin Rubis, Brick, Jason Ortiz To watch for: Ely Braxton, True Evil, Joaquin Pierre, Layla Roberts.

Thoughts: N-O-C really changed allot of things we saw BloodAngel Tommy Osbourn and Aka Osualdo rise pretty high, as we saw several guys fall off the list, proving how just a few off weeks can hurt you. But don't count those who fell off the list out. they are banging at the door and may just as likely toss some others off the list as they climb back on.
