The International Championship 

This title has seen it's share of wars in Global Wrestling.

Many performers who have captured the International Championship have  gone on to become Heavyweight Champion, including the Jonathan Davis, Jay Robson, Rob Virtouso, Brian Welch, Jean-Claude Dafoe, Hans Kruger, Brick, Silent Wind, and Salaok.

Salaok has the honor of being the first ever GW International Champion. Brick holding the record of Most title wins with a record setting 4 title reigns to his name.

Currently Gunner Addison has the distinction of becoming the First and only Female to hold this top title.

Currently Leinad Soma holds the title.. 

What is the Future of the International Championship, What wars will be raged to claim this coveted prize..  Only time will tell..
