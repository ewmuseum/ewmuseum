Unseen Dark Rituals footage?
During a section of Global's Dark Rituals after the electricity went haywire as John Adams sent Joaquin Pierre into the electrical equipment on the side of Global’s stage.

This footage was not aired. But the cameras were recording and captured this footage. We at Global Online bring it to you now...

--------------------

The cameras open backstage where Jeff Wylde and David "RaZiel" Guerrero are seen arriving at the arena and heading towards their locker room. Both men have intese looks on their faces, knowing that they're going into a battle royal that is not typical of the kind either man has been in in the past. A Graveyard Invitational Battle Royal. The two seem to be in the middle of a conversation when the camera catches up with them.

Wylde: ...what I'm saying is, we probably won't win this thing, so we take out as many people as we can before we get taken out. After all we need to make an impact and let Global know they made the right decision by hiring us. Plus, wouldn't you just love to see the look on Roberto's face when he sees us? Teamed up again no less.

RaZiel: That asshole can go to hell.

RaZiel's words come out more as weapons than words. Obviously that name isn't one that brings up fond memories. His look of disgust only lasts a short time however as a smirk comes across his face once again.

RaZiel: I agree though. Tonight isn't about winning some match that's sole reason is to create a ruckus for fans that are in love with blood shed. Tonight is about coming in and quenching OUR thirst for blood shed.

Wylde nods as the two men reach their locker room, before they can open the door and step inside Noi Tsunami rushes up to them.

Noi: Gentlemen, can I have a moment of your time?

Wylde: No one said we were ever gentlemen, but yeah, I think we got a moment to spare.

Noi: Okay then, what brings you men here to Global.

Wylde: That's an easy one, to kick as much ass as humanly possible, and enjoy doing it. We're here because we heard that this is where the best are supposed to be, so seeing as how RaZ and I have faced others who called themselves "the best" before, we wanted to make sure these guys aren't just blowing smoke up our asses.

RaZiel: To be honest I'm here because I've been on the sidelines for far too long. I've watched as people I know walk out in front of fans like these night in and night out ... but cannot do half of what I can. Some have walked out into a Global ring. Beginning tonight Jeff Wylde and I begin to prove that no tag team can measure up.

Noi nods in approval as she turns back to Jeff Wylde.

Noi: It's obvious that you both know someone here in Global, who would it be that you're after?

Wylde: Well, anybody who knows us, and more specifically me, knows that I have issues with the team known as Glory Bound. Draven Masters, Johnny Lexicon and Zodiac. A long and bloody history, especially with Draven. But Johnny Boy, don't think I've forgotten how you bailed on 3WA when the three of us were supposed to be keeping it together. RaZ and I are here to get our pound of flesh from you two, and sooner or later we're going to get it.

His eyes are burning as he stares into the camera, almost as if he could see through time and space to wherever Glory Bound are. Noi turns to RaZiel.

RaZiel: Glory Bound is first on the list Global but if you want to know the truth? Nobody is safe. Johnny Lexicon and Draven Masters will be the sacrificial lambs. Once we are finished ripping them to shreds then we will step up further and further until the tag team gold is in our hands. And it only gets bigger from there.

Wylde: Big man's right. Glory Bound is just the first step to bigger and better things. Once we get done with them, the sky's the limit here in Global for The Reckoning. We've been gone far too long, and it's about time we stepped back into the ring, and do what we do best... hurt people. See, the thing I love about this business is, I like to hurt people, and I like to get paid. Wrestling gives me the oppourtunity to get paid to hurt people. When you're a kid you're always told that "violence never solves anything". In wrestling, violence solves EVERYTHING!

RaZiel smiles now but the smile isn't very pleasant. Sinsiter may be the word.

RaZiel: Welcoming to Reckoning Day Global.

The two men now nod to Noi before turning and disappearing into their locker room. Tsunami turns back towards the camera.

Noi: This is Noi Tsunami from backstage at Dark Rituals.
