The Cruiserweight Championship 

The title of the flyers, the insane, the crazy suicidal ones who fly high above the ring.  But not all champions take to the sky. Some have been ground attackers. Some martial Artist.  The title of the smaller guys. 

Mani Cruiserweight have gone on to become World champion. The list include TLM, Salaok, BJP, Kid Lightning, and Mani Shigeru. 

Some of the Memorable champions to have held this title are: Salaok, TLM, Kamikaze, Ben J Pailthorpe, Deacon Holmes, Jin Akiyara, And Derek Frost.

With TLM being the first ever GW Cruiser champion, And Derek Frost and Kamikaze, and Deacon Holmes as the only multiple champions. 

Currently Deacon Holmes is the Champion of this coveted title.

With New stars entering the Global and Global going into what some say is a new era. Who the next cruiser-weight champions will be has yet to be decided.  But rest assured the Prestige of the title will always remain in tact, and only the Best Cruisers the Global has to offer will claim this title.
