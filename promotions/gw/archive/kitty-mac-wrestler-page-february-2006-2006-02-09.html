Kitty Mac

Real Name: Kaitlynn McIntyre
Birthdate: April 22, 1979
Hometown: Hamilton, Ontario, Canada
Height: 5'6'
Weight: 125 lbs.
Nicknames: Kitty, Jade, Twisted Angel
Status: Heel
Entrance Music: 'Living Dead Girl' by Rob Zombie

Information

Wrestling Style: highflyer with a dash of martial arts... and a ton of guts!
Set Up Move: Cat Scratch Fever (eye rake followed by a hard bitch slap)
Finisher: Kiss This! (a handspring into a Frankensteiner)
Title History:
Award History: GU's First Valedictorian
Other Federations: WCWF, Freedom, SAWF
Other Federation Awards: WCWF Grandslam champ, SAWF Hall of Famer

Ring Entrance: 'Who is this IRRESISTABLE creature who has an insatiable love for the dead?!' “Living Dead Girl” by Rob Zombie erupts over the speakers as hot pink pyros go off. The JumboTron lights up with the darling image of a cartoon kitty, complete with askew halo… and then it shatters into a thousand points of light, the driving bass of Rob Zombie accompanying the explosion of pyros. Kitty Mac steps out onto the ramp, dressed to kill in black leather pants and a bubblegum pink baby doll tee, her midriff exposed, dancing and headbanging her way to the ring.
