Hall of Fame (September 2006)

1 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Welcome to the Hall of Fame He is where honored warriors of GWF's history are kept. Want to be a Legend, Make it to the Hall of Fame, Respect those who are here, They earned it. though Blood Sweat and Tears, all spilt for the  fans of Global Wrestling. 

Bobby Brawler With a Bag of glass and a Chair wrapped in Barbwire and loaded with C-4 Explosives. Bobby Brawler set the stage for the Violence that has been known to Plague the GWF. The Bringer of Hardcore. Bobby Brawler fought wars in the name of GWF, 

Loyal to a fault. If you befriended Brawler  then you had a Friend for life. But be on his bad side and you come to learn just why he is the In-Human Monster. 

Insane Cain   Some call him crazy, Some call him the best at what he does. What ever he is he left a lasting impression on the face of Global Wrestling. Cain always had his own style, High Impact and brutal. This kept him as a Fan favorite all though out his stays in GWF, Although he never official claimed the Global Championship he Held one of its predecessors in the UCWF Championship. It's last and only World Champion. 

Cain Will forever be remembered for his contributions to the GWF in and out of the ring.  Would GWF be the same today if not for his presence? Very Unlikely.

'The Forever Knight" Gabriel Sabor The Master of the Void. TFK came in saw and conquered.  Gaining the World title in Record time. His is a legacy of sorrow and tragedy. TFK has returned from his Void.  And now walks the halls of GW once again. recently letting Sylo and Kamikaze fill his rath!  

Justin Marks   The Phantom of the GWF. Holding claim as its first World Champion. Justin Marks and his High Powered offence Led him to many Victory's. His end would come at the Hands of his Brother in law. Salaok would finally put to rest this tortured soul. Or did he.. Marks would return to Face his brother, Only to give his self over to Salaok to save his only Son... 
The Marks Saga still continues..    

Tragic Hero The Hero of Tragedy. Rife with past demons, Tragik would refuse to be conformed, Gaining him a cult following. Often leading him to be arrested for insisting Riots with his presence. His Feuds with Johnathan Davis and his own Brother 'Heru Tenshi' Brain Welch are legendary. 

'Scarecrow' Jonathan Davis   Whoever said Scarecrows were not that scary, Never met the man known as Johnathan Davis. The Master of Mind games. Deadly to all. Johnathan Davis would set a trail of destruction that few can compare to. From his Feuds with the Welch Brothers. Also known as Tragik Hero and Heru Tenshi. 
Davis is the living breathing incantation of the fabled boogie Man, He has now since moved to PCW.  What victims wait for him there, is unknown. 
But never get to comfortable for you never when the Scarecrow will return. 

Heru Tenshi aka  'The Fallen Angel' Brain Welch

The other Welch Brother. Dark and sinister But always found his way fighting for the light. His life inter twined with that of Johnathan Davis, Often at odds with his own Brother. After the death of Telena, His beloved Bride to be, Brian fade into the sunset of Japan. 

Hans Kruger    The German Sensation. The German Tank, One of the most gifted and technically sound, Popular men in the history of the Federation. Hans Kruger rose to the top like none other. Claiming every title in the Federation. One week Shy of earning the Grand Slam, He was put down in the ring. A blown Knee would see the ending of The German Sensations GWF Career. Beloved and Missed by all. 
Even his biggest Rival, has Unparalleled Respect for the German. 

Will Kruger make a return to Global Wrestling?  One can only hope so.    

True Evil Two Years of Destruction, True Evil the only Tag team to Grace the Hallowed halls of Fame. Salaok and Quartz, Arguably the Best Tag team Ever in the Federations history. Its First Tag team Champions Unifying two worlds to forge one playground for there dark and sinister ways. 

Salaok Brother to The Scarecrow, Harvesters all of them.  Salaok of Pain, Quartz of Terror. Davis of Fear. Will the world of Global Wrestling ever be truly free of True Evil?  One may never know.

"Canadian Icon" Jean Claude Dafoe    The Canadian Icon...  A person who proved every one WRONG!.. Dafoe would enter the GW as  the Canadian Gothic...  A hated man..   his feud with Hans Kruger, one that has never really ended. made both men stars in GW...  Dafoe would accomplish things in GW that the critics said he could never do.. World Champion... And First ever Grand Slam Champion!!! Dafoe  Worked his ass off to become one of the Premier Wrestlers in GW.  Left for the love of his soon to be wife..  Gone forever... Who knows..  But Dafoe will always be remembered in GW...  

"The Ladies Man" Jay Robson Jay Robson, one of Global Wrestling's veterans from the original roster.  "The Ladies Man" busted his ass for years in Global Wrestling to become what he is today.  He's a Grand Slammer, 2000 Rookie of the year, 2001 Master of the Mat, and the First Triple Champion (World, International, and Cruiser Weight).  Jay Robson is known for his exploits and because of this man, when GW fans hear a women moan at a GW event, they automatically assume that he's the one behind it.
