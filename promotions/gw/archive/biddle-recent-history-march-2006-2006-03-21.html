Biddle — recent history (March 2006)

21 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recent History On January 10, 2005 Mike Biddle made his GLOBAL debut on Reload. Mike introduced himself by hosting an impromtu edition of his critically acclaimed 'Marvelous Matters'. The first guest? None other than future GLOBAL World Champion, Mindy Mercer. The interview was cut short when Mike started to hit on the young lady, who responded to his advances with an almighty kick to the manhood. And so started Mike's first rivalry with a GLOBAL superstar. Mike tried desperatly to get a date with Mindy but to no avail. Finally, the two faced off at High Octane for the Television Title and with some extra stipulations. The stipulations? If Mike won, Mindy had to go out on a date with him. If Mindy won, there would be a restraining order placed on Mike. Naturally Mindy won and Mike not only lost his Television Title but he lost his chance to be with the future World Champion.

Weeks later it was revealed that Biddle was involved in a plot against Adam Myers and his family. Myers's family had been kidnapped by the evil Richard Wood and Myers had been forced to undergo countless experiments. Biddle's role in all this? To keep an eye on Myers and send weekly reports back to Wood. The rivarly came to it's highest point when Biddle and Myers faced off against each other and Corvus for the vacant Platinum Title at Wrestlecade. Myers won the match and in turn his family was returned to him. However, a month down the road the two faced off again for the Platinum Title and Biddle won...injuring Myers in the process. No one knows when the Prototype will be able to return from injury Biddle caused him.
