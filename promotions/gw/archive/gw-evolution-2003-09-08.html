GW — Card

Evolution, 8 September 2003

8 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Redemption Jack, one of Christina’s elite security guards walks into her office. Jack: Madam, he’s here. Christina hung up the phone and looked straight at him. Christina: already? I didn’t expect him to be here this early. Jack smiled. Jack: I think it is because he has other people on his mind believe you me. Christina sighed. Christina: I know. Send him in, and then leave us alone. Jack nodded and walked out of the office. Moments later, Jin Akiyara stepped through the door, already stripped down to his wrestling attire. He was the same as he was last week, except for the rows of bandages around his abdomen. Christina: Take a seat. Akiyara shook his head. Christina: Please? Akiyara: I prefer to stand. I don’t plan on being here too long. Christina: I guess I should get straight to the point. How do I look tonight? Akiyara furrowed his eyebrows in shock. Akiyara: You look fine I guess. I guess tying your hair back in a ponytail brings out your eyes. But is this seriously what you want from me? An opinion on your cosmetic looks? Christina sighed and looked down at her white spaghetti top, and black jeans. Christina: Um, no not really. First off, I’m terribly sorry about what happened… Akiyara: Save it. It’s the last thing I need. Christina: Right, so let me say that I plan on doing everything I can to rectify it. You name your challenger and I will sign the match. Akiyara: Leave it as it is. Roz and Ice both have to fight tonight for the contender’s spot. The best bet will be to take it from there. Who ever loses will be hellbent on destroying the winner thus making it easier for me. I’ll fight the winner as soon as possible. Christina: Agreed. And I’ve hired some extra security to keep tabs on you from now on. I don’t need any thoughtless, sick, disgusting people like Ice ruining my show or injuring my workers. Akiyara: No. Do not absolutely do that. I don’t want anything from you. The only think I want from you is the matches made. I do not need any added security… Christina: But Ice… Akiyara turned around and headed for the door. As he opens the door he stops and looks over his shoulder. Akiyara: But nothing! I’ve never depended on anyone else before to fight my battles. And I am not about to start right now. With that said, Akiyara walks out the door, slamming it shut behind him. Christina: But.. OHH!!! The cameras fade as Christina stands there her face saddened. Static fills the screen and slowly the GW logo fades though the static and fades away… ---- The screen suddenly flickers to life with static as words appear in black. It Sustains us... It Changes us... The beginning of Miseria Cantare by AFI begins to play. Black and white pictures of GW hall of famers flash onto the screen as the music blasts. Pictures of Insane Cain, Bobby Brawler, Jean Claude Dafoe, True Evil, Jonathan Davis, Hans Kruger, Brain Welch, Tragic Hero, TLM Jay Robson, and Silent Wind flash across the screen. The music begins to play even harder… -E -Voice Over- =LOVE= Bobby Brawler stands alone in the ring. Blood covers his face as his trusty bag of Glass lays on the ground next to him. Brick is seen standing right in front of the GW Hell in a Cell. -V- =TO HATE= Pictures of Hans Kruger fighting Jean Claude Dafoe, flash across the screen. Then a picture of Brick fighting Salaok pop up onto the screen. -O- =YOU’RE= Insane Cain stands in the ring the fans around him cheering outrageously loud as blood drips off his forehead. Then a picture of Derek Frost climbing the turnbuckle with his arm raised in the air in victory. -L- =FAITH LOST= "The Forever Knight" Gabriel Sabor slowly rises from a crouch to a standing position towering over the turnbuckle he stands near. Vincent and Brian Welch slowly materialize in the ring both of them looking up slowly to glare into the lens before lowering their heads and disappearing again. U- =YOU= TLM turns around in the ring soaking up applause as he flashes a million dollar smile at the camera. The Canadian Icon Jean Claude Dafoe materializes in the ring with a 2 million dollar smiles always one to out do TLM -T- =ARE NOW= -I- =ONE= A German flag materializes in the background as Hans Krueger steps into the ring saluting the flag. Gambit is shown holding the International Title belt in his hands, he looks down on it like he has never seen anything like it before. Then Sage Sabbot is shown with the GW Cruiserweight Title in his hands, he looks down at it and smiles. -O- =OF= The Silent Assassins, Mani Shigeru and Silent Wind appear back to back Mani slowly grasps the blue Razor Wire as Wind removes the hidden dagger from his glove. Quartz and Salaok stand beside each other and smirk, the evil smirk that they are known for falls upon their faces. -N- =US!= The camera pans back slowly. To a empty Global Arena, Christina McCoy stands in the middle of GW Octagon…. Christina: It is how we have stayed alive and it is how we will Dominate.. A picture of Justin Marks holding the Heavyweight Title flashes onto the screen. A picture of Hans Kruger pops up on the screen holding the Heavyweight Title in his hand. A picture of Silent Wind holding the GW Heavyweight Title pops up onto the screen. Then a picture of Tragic Hero with the GW Heavyweight Title around his waist pops up. Followed By a Picture of Salaok, of Mani Shigeru, of Brian Welch, of Insane Cain, Of Bobby Brawler, of John Davis, of Tragic Hero, of The Forever Knight Gabriel Sabor… of Jean Claude Dafoe… of TLM Jay Robson… of Silent Wind… of Sage Sabbot… of Derek Frost… The pictures fade as the lights in the warehouse arena slowly come on as the likes of Jin Akiyara, Angus Bingham, Ice, Kid Flash, Deacon Holmes, John Zero, TLM Jay Robson, Ben J Pailthorpe, Lined Soma, Sean Price, Trey Reed, And Akodo Rokku, all appear behind Christina McCoy. The whole entire GW roster is seen standing beside each other. Smoke begins to surround the group as the music blasts even harder. WELCOME TO GLOBAL WRESTLING Brick steps forward through the people and looks directly into the screen WELCOME TO………. Brick: Evolution! The music stops and a gun shot blast is heard, starting Evolution. The GW logo pops up on the screen and the smoke surrounds it. Then static fills the screen… and fades away to a view of the GW Commentators box.. Where Brandi Sights: And Mark Bishop sit behind the announce desk. ---- Brandi Sights: Hello there ladies and gentlemen Welcome to Evolution I'm Brandi Sights along with my broadcast partner Mark Bishop. And what a night we have for you! It’s finally come! The rematch fans have been waiting for! The World champion Brick takes on Deacon Holmes in a New York Street Fight with the World title on the line. Bishop: New World champion tonight! I just know it! Sights: Also, the International and Primestar titles are on the line as Trey Reed defends his International title against Sean Price and the new Primestar champion Max Roswell defends his title against Kid Flash! Bishop: Title changes all around tonight! The lights fade and T… U…. E. Is heard in a digitized voice. 'In The End' by Linkin Park hits the PA and out walk TUE. John Zero, Leinad Soma, Deacon Holmes and Sean Price power walk to the ring, ignoring the loud boos from the crowd. Bishop: TUE! Sights: Well, you’re probably the only person glad to see them. All four of these men have matches tonight. Aside from Price and Holmes, Soma takes on the Steel Lion Akkodo Rokku and Zero gets in the ring with the Ghetto Blaster Master himself... Duzza! Bishop: And another of Brick’s buddies is gonna wind up in intensive care. Sights: I dare you to say that to Brick’s face. Bishop: Shh! Sean’s talking! Price: Tonight is a night of new beginnings. Tonight is the night TUE proves its dominance over Global Wrestling. Because tonight, I’ll not be Master of the Mat and a contender for the World title, I’ll also be International champion! Last week’s screw job on Deacon was just a minor setback. Tonight, he’s gonna bounce back in the biggest way possible, by taking the World title! Price then hands the mic over to Holmes. Bishop: Just like I said! Sights: Oh hush! Holmes: All throughout history, many old conventions have been replaced by newer innovations. The automobile replaced the horse and carriage. The CD replaced the LP. The DVD replaced the videotape. Tonight, I replace Brick! Sights: That remains to be seen. Holmes: See, a lot of people seem not to think I can do it. But that’s okay. A lot of people, including Brick, didn’t think I could take him in the Hard 10 match and I proved them wrong. Tonight ain’t gonna end in a draw. Tonight, I leave this arena with the title and I leave Brick in a position familiar to his sister: flat on his back! Bishop: Ha ha! “Party Up” hits the PA and the crowd erupts in cheers as Brick walks out onto the stage, holding the World title belt over his shoulder and a mic in his hand. Sights: And that did it! You’d think Deacon would have learned last week not to dog the champ’s family! Bishop: Hey, it’s not his fault that thug can’t handle the truth! Sights: Brick’s sister is a married woman! Bishop: Maybe someone should tell her that. Brick: Deacon, Deacon, Deacon. Every time I look at you I think to myself, “So much youth, so much talent, so much potential, so much motherfuckin stupidity!!” Holmes starts to throw a tantrum. Sights: WHOA! Brick: And I’ve been trying to figure something out about you and I think I’m close. Ever since you came to this company, you’ve mentioned my hips on a number of occasions. Then a couple of weeks ago, you pull a stunt that involves getting me naked. By the way, Deacon, what were *you* doing with a business card from Club Fantasia? Holmes is visibly uncomfortable but then starts to put on a front. Sights: Is Brick suggesting what I think he is? Bishop: Oh, now wait a minute… Brick: So that gets me to wondering and now here you are talking about getting me on my back… Holmes: Don’t get it twisted, Grandpa! It ain’t like that! Deacon Holmes is all man! Brick: You know, maybe you’re right. Maybe I do have it wrong. But just in case I don’t, a lot of cats there offered me their phone numbers! I’m pretty sure I can hook you up! Holmes starts to stomp around the ring. Price takes the mic as Soma and Zero try to calm Holmes down. Sights: The World champion really getting under Holmes’ skin! Price: That’s enough! Brick, you better pray that Deacon gets to you before I do! Because either way, someone in TUE is taking that title away from you! Bishop: Tell him, Sean! Brick: Well, let me tell you something, Price. Either one of y’all can get his ass kicked or y’all can interfere in my match tonight and you all get your asses kicked! Sights: Brick issuing a warning to TUE! Brick: Because I am not in the mood to deal with any TUE bullshit tonight! So if all of you feel like getting your asses kicked twice in one night, just try me! It can be you, Price! It can be Soma! It can be Zero! It can be you, too, Bishop! Bishop: What did I do? Sights: Plenty! Price: Well, Brick, since you’re in such a mood to kick some ass tonight, why don’t you come down to this ring and try to kick ours? Bishop: Yeah, Bricky, why don’t you? Brick: Well, you know what, Price? Let me think about that for just one second! Brick then rushes the ring and slides in. Brick hits Price, then Holmes, then Zero and then Soma. Eventually they start to overpower him. Sights: Here we go! Brick taking on TUE! Bishop: It won’t last. See? Sights: Numbers game overwhelming the champ! All of a sudden the crowd explodes as from the back run Duzza and Akkodo Rokku!! Both men slide into the ring and attack TUE. Sights: And the tide’s about to turn! The crowd roars again as the Youngbloods rush down to the ring. Bishop: Aw no! Sights: Well, this is no surprise! Seeing they’re outnumbered, TUE slide out of the ring as the Bloods slide in. TUE backpedals up the ramp, trading glares and threats with Brick, Duzza, Rokku and the Youngbloods. Sights: And is this just a taste of what’s to come tonight when these men face one another in the Octagon? This night is just getting started! And Fight fans I am being told that It’s time for our opening match.
