Sights: Good God, no! Bishop: He just hit a homerun with the brain of KPJ! Steel: That beautiful face…be careful with it! Dropping the Singapore Cane, Josh slowly steps onto the ring apron yet as he stands there he drops to a knee gasping for breath. Sights: Josh can win this match right here and right now if he could only get to the top rope… Using all of his strength, "American Youth" Josh stands and grabs the steel frame using it's support to pull him to the top rope. On the top rope he teeters off balance and stumbles…grabbing the frame to keep him on the top rope! Bishop: He's a little dizzy it appears. Steel: Hey, so am I! Bishop: I said dizzy…not ditzy. Releasing his grip on the frame Josh reaches up and grabs the wire, leaning out he doesn't remove himself from the turnbuckle as he stretches his body hand over hand keeping his feet atop the turnbuckle. Yet finally he has no choice but to remove his feet from the turnbuckle as he inches closer to the World title! Sights: Our World Champion is going to retain his title after a grueling hard fought battle. Holding onto the wire he makes his way hand over hand and finally reaching out he bats at the World title…yet suddenly Ken Partridge Jr. is standing below him! Bishop: Where did he come from? Stepping beneath Josh, KPJ grabs his legs and forces Josh into an involuntary sitting position on his shoulders. Holding onto the legs of Josh, KPJ steps away dragging Josh from the World title as the Champion desperately grasps for it. Sights: Just when I thought Josh had retained Ken Partridge Jr. came out of nowhere to take the victory away from him. Now completely away from the wire Josh rains down hard right hands on the back of Ken's head. KPJ however, has other ideas as he uses an Electric chair drop to bring Josh down stomach first on the top rope…and then he turns it into a slingshot suplex morphed with a wheelbarrow suplex! Bishop: Slingshot…wheelbarrow…suplex, I think… Sights: Call it whatever you so desire but I call it absolutely amazing! Ken turns away from Josh to ascend the top rope but before he can get away from his Elite cohort Josh snags the ankle of Ken Partridge Jr. and clings tightly too it. Bishop: Josh is now resorting to whatever he can pull out of his magic back of tricks… Sights: It's a desperate effort but it may be the only thing he is physically capable of…because if you look closely at the corner of Josh's mouth… Steel: He's bleeding! Sights: More of a pink froth at the corners of his mouth… Bishop: Pink…that's internal blood. Josh is on his hands and knees in front of Ken Partridge Jr. with his head still lying on the canvas. Reaching down KPJ grabs the head of Josh and inserts it between his thighs…in one quick motion Ken spikes the cranium of "American Youth" Josh into the canvas with a cradle piledriver! Sights: Oh, that was just plain sick… Bishop: I guess you don't have to worry about him suffering only from internal bleeding now. As KPJ turns away from his partner in crime we see a trickle of blood now running from Josh's forehead right by his hairline where he was impaled into the canvas. Seemingly unconcerned with the condition of Josh, Ken Partridge Jr. has World title aspirations as he steps up to the top turnbuckle and clings to the post. Sights: He's in pursuit of the World title now and after that piledriver I don't see how Josh is going to be able to stop him and continue his World title reign. Bishop: He won't be able to stop him and we're going to crown a new Champion. Turning, Ken grabs the wire and swings out onto the steel cable his feet dangling above the ring. Going hand over hand he inches his way across the wire until he reaches the World title…sparing a second to look down at Josh the eyes of KPJ bulge as the bleeding Josh kip's up to his feet! Ken makes a lunge for the World title but Josh simply grabs his foot and jerks him down from the wire to the canvas below where Ken lands on his feet stopping him from snatching the World title! Sights: Where did Josh find the energy? Bishop: Desperate men do desperate things and that was an act of desperation if I've ever seen one. Partridge lashes out with a hard right hand and cracks Josh in the jaw with a shot that is heard throughout the Global Arena and sends Josh back down to the canvas. Sights: I believe it was an adrenaline rush, because Josh went back down so fast it was a little embarrassing to be frank. Steel: Who is Frank? Sights: Never mind… With his opponent on the canvas KPJ turns to once more ascend the turnbuckles. With his back turned he doesn't see Josh kip up yet again! Bishop: He's playing possum! What a tricky little bastard! With Ken standing on the top rope and yet to turn around he never sees it coming as Josh takes off on a dead run and with a surge of adrenaline leaps to the top rope next to Ken. The eyes of KPJ widen as Josh drives his face into the steel frame! As Ken staggers and almost falls Josh steadies him by hooking in a half nelson and throwing the free arm of Ken around his waist as he hooks his wrist… Sights: Don't tell me he's going to do what I think he is… Bishop: No way! No fucking way! In one quick motion Josh leaps from the top rope to the outside utilizing the "Youthful descent" to drive Ken Partridge Jr. threw the announce table on the outside as it explodes into splinters! Sights: Fuck! Bishop: You crazy motherfucker! You almost killed us you son of a bitch! Fans: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! The cameras circle the pandemonium and we see the body of Josh lying on his back unmoving aside from his desperate gasps for oxygen. Next to him KPJ lies face down his blood running across the remnants of what used to be the announce table as Brandi Sights, Mark Bishop and Alexis Steel have all ran for cover. Sights: I can't believe that Josh would put us in that kind of danger! Bishop: Did you see that? That was freakin' insane, they were coming at us like two human torpedoes that almost landed in our lap! Steel: I think American Youth just destroyed both of them… Fans: You killed Kenny! Clap, clap. You killed Kenny! Clap, clap. You killed Kenny! Clap, clap. You killed Kenny! Clap, clap. You killed Kenny! Clap, clap. Sights: That chant may be a bit on the disturbing side but I don't think it's far from the truth. The eyes of Josh stare blankly towards the ceiling as KPJ painfully rolls over onto his back the arena looking on in a state of awe. Bishop: This is insane…it's inhuman, and to be honest…I love it! Sights: These two men have pushed one another to and beyond their limits. Bishop: Limits? If you've been paying attention it's pretty obvious that these men have no limits! Josh painfully rolls over onto his stomach pink blood dripping down his chin as it pours from his mouth. Ken Partridge Jr. is another story entirely as his blood has now evolved into a crimson mask. Sights: Bloody and broken would be the most fitting way to describe these two individuals. Bishop: They are putting on one hell of a performance tonight and I can't believe the things that they've done to one another. Crawling forward on his elbows Josh claws at the arena floor inching his way across and back toward the Octagon. Clawing his way across the floor Josh pulls himself to his feet with the aid of the ring apron before rolling into the Octagon and underneath the bottom rope. Sights: Love our World Champion or hate our World Champion you have to admire his courage…his heart…his drive! Bishop: Josh is just so full of pride that even though he is drained he simply can't allow himself to lose. Using the ropes to pull himself to his feet, Josh stumbles back into the corner. Turning slowly he steps onto the bottom turnbuckle and ascends them, all the while holding onto the post so the dizzy World Champion doesn't lose his balance. Sights: He's going for the title now and thankfully we'll see an end to this sickening battle. Bishop: He's the World Champion for a reason, and he's showing us that reason right now. Going hand over hand the wounded World Champion nears the World title hanging in the center of the X. Without hesitation he reaches forth and places his hand on the belt to pull it from the wire. But his back is turned and he never sees KPJ stand, leap to the apron, and suddenly he realizes his presence as Ken partridge Jr. drills the World Champion in the shoulder blades with a springboard dropkick! The dropkick sends Josh into a flip where he lands on his stomach, as KPJ crashes down next to him! Sights: That bullet had the Champions name on it! Bishop: Josh has shown why he is the World Champion, but Ken Partridge Jr. has just shown us why he is the International Champion! Both bleeding men lie on the canvas in pain, as Ken Partridge Jr. is the first to climb to his feet. Ignoring Josh he staggers about, blinded by his own thick blood as it runs across his face in a coagulated mask. Stumbling, Ken finally reaches the corner where he grabs the post and steps onto the second rope. Holding his ribs he pauses showing the effects of the Youthful descent through the announce table. Steel: New World Champion, New World Champion! Sights: Not yet Alexis… Bishop: …but soon. Showing his resiliency, Josh shoves himself to his feet and crawls to the corner. On the opposite corner KPJ has now reached the top of the turnbuckles yet still clings to the post clutching his ribs. Sights: It's now going to become a race to see who can go across the wire faster. Josh who is just now on the first turnbuckle or KPJ who is on the top rope yet can hardly raise his arms due to his ribs killing him. Climbing as quickly as possible Josh grabs the post and pulls himself to the top rope. Parallel to him, Ken Partridge Jr. has turned and already began inching his way across the wire in excruciating pain. Bishop: KPJ has the lead as they come out of the gate… Grasping frantically at the wire Josh swings out onto the X and begins inching his way closer to the World title…and to Ken Partridge Jr. Sights: Each man is soaked in their own blood and the blood of their adversary. Bishop: It looks almost as if the Red Cross truck had a wreck or something of that nature. Both men reach the center of the X and they stare at one another with weary eyes. KPJ is the first to move as he uses his right leg to boot Josh in the gut. The World Champ sways backwards…yet doesn't fall. Sights: Brilliant strategy by KPJ, this is the showdown! Swinging both legs up, KPJ blasts Josh in the gut with a double boot shot that nearly sends the World Champion from the wire. Josh however grits his teeth and…spits his own blood in the eyes of Ken Partridge Jr. sending the International Champion blindly collapsing into the center of the Octagon! Reaching out, American Youth jerks his World title from the center of the X and drops from the wire to the canvas below retaining his World Championship! The GW logo flashes, as the picture fades.
