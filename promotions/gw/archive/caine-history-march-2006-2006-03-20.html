Caine — history (March 2006)

20 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Jesse Caine was just another Georgia yank as they called him in the papers. Though one think had changed that, a robbery. When he had become mixed with a bunch of guys he didn’t need to be with, he had been pressured into robbing a store. The robbery went wrong and Jesse had been stabbed in the back ally from another one of the robbers, leaving him to be found guilty and frame Jesse of the robbery. After being sent to jail for 90 days he came out with a big hole in him. So he joined professional wrestling for no one would hire a criminal. Now he uses his Harley Davidson act bad boy to entertain women and get kicks out of what he calls wanks, or snot nosed pigs of the wrestling business.

From Indy fed to Indy fed he liked to wrestle, and he realized it be a great idea to get into something big. In 2001 he was in a big tournament that ended with him in the semi-finals. An injury had kept him from moving on and that is where he had loss to a punk named Louis Carrion with his own finisher, which at the time he did not know, the C9! The move was near insane at first for him to even witness, however after seeing it he dedicated himself to learning it. A 275-pound man doing this move is near insane, however he musters the ability to do it perfectly after trying it out in every match he had. It instantly crushed his opponents, and therefore he adapted it and called it the C9, for he lost to the 9th man in the tournament, and his last name started with a C, there C9 was born.

The tournament had changed Caine a lot. He knew he was good, not as good as he found out though. He prided himself on working hard, and in XDA, Xtreme Downtime Alliance, a company that had been recognized for it’s extreme nature Jesse took that opportunity. Never winning the world title, however winning the Xtreme title and defending it for months on end. He was known as the true Caine of wrestling, for his favorite move was using a Singapore Caine and busting it over his opponent’s upper body a lot. Though he had been fired from XDA for nearly killing a man when he swung the Singapore Caine and nailed his opponent in the neck, disconnecting his breathing.
