Fusion Card for 3/27/06

22 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Main Event 8 ManTag The Elite Ken Partridge Jr, Josh, and The Enforcers vs The Syndicate Eryc Morgan, Mike Biddle, and The Texas Wrecking Crew Word came down from Brandi Sights that the Board of Directors has ordered this match to take place as KPJ, the new #1 contender to the Platinum Championship, stands across the ring from the man that holds the strap, Eryc Morgan. It will also pit the new International Champion, Josh, and the new Tag Team Champions in The Enforcers against the men that they beat out for those titles at High Octane, Mike Biddle and The TWC. However, the lingering question is, how will Eryc Morgan coexist on the team of The Syndicate? DawgHouse SpecialGuest Brick The returning Top Dawg brings his new program to life in a grand scale... by interviewing the new World Heavyweight Champion, Brick! ThreeFalls NoDiscqualification Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis vs "DeathScythe Outlaw" Kyo Kushinaga Bad blood's brewed over the ending of the High Octane Four Corners match. Hopefully, this No DQ match can help settle some issues before Clash of the Titans. It's said that Kushinaga was the one who requested this match to place. "Big Dawg" Jake Hix vs Kris Kanton Kris Kanton returns from injury, but will he be able to escape death again when he takes in the angry former World Champion, Jake Hix? ScaffoldMatch Los Cazadorez vs The Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean It was scheduled to take place at High Octane, but thanks to a sneak attack, we never saw it. The Board of Directors has made the match for Fusion and stated that if anybody from either team interferes before hand, they're contract will be breached and they will be fired. Maybe, now, we can finally find out who will walk the plank. Shaun Styles vs Fazz McLovely Dylan "The Killer" Knight vs William Burr Predict away!
