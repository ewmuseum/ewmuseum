History of the Federation, a timeline (March 2006)

20 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed’s own year-by-year history page, listing the founding wars and the world title lineage in its own words.

Date: June 25, 2000 Place: The MCI Center in Washington D.C. UCWF -VS- FWL

For weeks  Narcosis Rose and Jason Ayers would work hard on paper work..  on merging the two federations..  The two set in motion a joint PPV..

This would be the birth of the new Federation. As both federations one last time would battle for there titles.. Insane can became the First and last UCWF World Champion..  Justin Marks would claim the FWL title.

At the end of the night. The New federation would be announced and that Federation would be the Global Wrestling Federation.

Clash of the Titians one would see the unification of all the titles, And bring to life the GWF Championships..

After the initial clashes  would happen and a few of the roster left the Federation for fear of change.  GWF would grow,  Only a few members of that early roster are still here,  Salaok Quartz, Michael Gilson aka Kamikaze back then, TLM Derek Frost.  Some of the Former stars returned. For a while..  Some even archiving greatness after there absence.

Soon Narcosis would leave GWF Television..  And Jason Ayers would run the GWF as he saw fit..  till His death..  Opening the door for Blade Entertainment and Eric Lansheer.

Who was the owner of Blade E. though?  It came to light that Johnathan Davis, Who was presumed DEAD.. Was the owner.. 

Till Eric Lansheer swindled the Companies out from under The Scarecrow... 

GWF Has grown in size and Strength over the course of the last two years..  It has seen The Rise and fall of many champions..  Some will forever be etched into our memories.. 

But the GWF is about the Future of Wresting..  And the Future is NOW.  GWF is fast becoming one of the top federations..  As the talent gets Bigger Faster and Stronger.

With Lansheer's Greed  growing out of proportion..  He placed twenty five percent of Blade E up on the stock exchange.. This was bout by a unnamed source...  

Later a Blast from the past Johnny Cruse would step out of the shadows placing his twenty five percent of GWF stocks on the line....  in a Match...  Jesse Briggs would win for Crystal Ayers....

Lansheer Hell bent on owning it all threatened the life of Miss Ayers. So she took his offer of Twenty Five percent of Blade E... What Lansheer did not know is who the mysterious man buyer was....

Jason Ayers would return from the dead...  And state he now owned half of Blade E and was the NEW Vice President of The Federation...  

GWF In this time signed some big names and After two plus years of Blood Sweat and teas, Not to mention  thousands of hours of hard work, GWF's fate seemed dim.  As Lansheer and Ayers were informed the federation would be forced to close....  

BUT! Lansheer and Ayers working together on this One to keep a dream alive another to keep the cash flowing into his pockets. 
After hours of Meetings, Ayers and Lansheer came to a agreement with the majority of the companies suing the Federation  by Dropping the F  there was now no reason to carry on with a lawsuit.. the others bucked after this and the case was tossed out.

With the disappearance of Jason Ayers, and the Kidnapping of Eric Lansheer..   GW fell in total chaos...   Things got bad that GW seem to be closed.  Network Contracts and TV deals  gone..   Blade Entertainment  put GW on sale.. on E Bay...   

THANK GOD FOR BUBBA MCCOY!!! 

Long time Commentator and Talent coordinator for GW.  Bubba McCoy bought the fledgling federation..  Securing the contracts of several wrestlers.. and signing new rising independent stars...  gaining TV deals and much much more... GW is entering a new Era once again. 

GW is now in a Evolution of its own.  Introducing the Octagon Ring was the first stage of this Evolution.  Currently construction is underway  for a Brand New  GLOBAL ARENA  located in Dallas Texas.  New Owner Bubba McCoy  has  guaranteed this will definitely change the face of GW forever. And make  the Stars and the Federation stand out amongst the rest.

With all this only one question can be asked. Are you ready for the future?
