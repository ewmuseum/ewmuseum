The GW Rundown, vol. 2Just when you thought it was safe to read the news again....

I’m baaaaaaaaaaaaaaaack.............

That’s right boys and girls. I’m sure many a member of the roster and staff were praying for my demise. Down on the little knees at night praying that the greatest threat to the secrets they dare try to keep would just shrivel up and die. Beseeching enough favor to destroy he whom would reveal your not so savory secret motivations and plots. Well I got a message for you from the man upstairs... He likes me better than you!

The GW Shadow is back!!!

Bitches.....

With that understood... No need for long preambles. Let’s get down to business...

~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~

The Top 5 Situations to Watch

5. Blood Fued
Ok, normally I wouldn’t even bother to mention Brick and Salaok being at eachother’s throats. I mean... gee, that’s new, right? But seriously folks. These to men have taken eachother to task so many times and as violent as it’s been in the past, surely this ain’t gonna get no better... hell it could only get worse. I mean Salaok put his hands on Brick’s family...and quite frankly, if no one else suspects, should be considered a prime suspect in the McNasty incident (although I know who actually did it... I’ll tell you later...) I mean as bloody and damn near deadly as this thing has been in the past, how much worse could it get?! Oh... I’m sorry..... Is that tempting fate? *tempt, tempt, tempt, tempt.....*

4. Blood Brothers/Enemies I doubt I’m alone in saying I’ve never seen two men who hate eachother as much as Train and Brick do be in as close a proximity as these two have been without killing each other. So far they’ve got a record one match together without trying to dismantle one another. Not only that, but it was the match the counted...the one where they put the GW Tag Team titles over their shoulders. But let’s be real folks. This thing ain’t gonna last forever... the countdown is on... Stay tuned.

3. The Ice Man Cometh....
Do I really need to elaborate?

2. Chicks in Charge
Gloria Steinem eat your heart out! Nobody does women’s lib like GW. Mindy Mercer, brand spankin’ new Television Champion. Aradia Xiomara, brand spankin’ new Cruiserweight Champion. The only thing that could be any more intriguing than this is some real spankings involving them in a couple of catholic school girl outfits with pig tails and they get to the principle’s office (my office) for a little... discipline. Wait..... did I say that out loud? Damn... My bad.... Anyway...

My real point is this. Life is good for the ladies now. No doubt the fans will be gracious and, to save face the rest of the roster will at least try to appear to be supportive. Don’t you just love political correctness? But what happens when the pc support of everybody who’s asked in public what they think. However, let’s see if, when the novelty of women champs wear off... and “The Boys” and their not so PC feelings spur them to start conspiring against these girls (which they will), if they have the chops to hold on to what they got. Watch your asses ladies. I know I am...

1. Reaching the Zenith
Alright, I pride myself on doing my research. So, after careful examination of the new World’s Heavyweight Champion Christian Zenith, I’ve made some interesting observations. He’s mean. He’s strong. He’s gonna be tough to beat. Oh, and one more thing.... This guy is a #%$@ing fruitcake!!! First reason you know the guy’s nuts is he’s making his living in constant danger of getting his ass kicked when he’s actually a friggin’ billionaire. Not only that, but the most disturbing thing is... the man has been linked to cult... yes I said, CULT activity back in his hometown of Chicago and across the country.

It looks to me like GW is set to have this man as their champion for a while they’d better be VERY careful the messages they allow this head case to put out on the airwaves. World Champions that are a danger to the roster are nothing new. World Champions that are a danger to the fans and the public at large?

That’s a whole other animal.

The Shadow Realm

Alright, here’s the part I really like. The reality check for Global... straight from the mind of The Shadow. My subject this week? Donny McNasty. Or, rather the possible demise of his career. Or, even more accurately, just how that came about. Don’t get me wrong, I don’t so much object to what happened to him, getting his brains beat in, in the dark. Hell, it’s a rough sport folks and life’s a bitch.

What I do object to is the rampant dumbassosity that seems to be catching around GW that keeps everyone from being able to figure out who the perpetrator of that attack was. I mean it’s unbelievable. I mean the guy that should be the prime suspect gets a clean pass with no suspicions while guys who pretty much had no reason to commit the crime (if you wanna call it that) have to bear the brunt.

First there’s Train. a man who went so far as to call a man on his cell phone to avoid a face to face confrontation when he and Donny were both in the same building at the same time. Why? Train has made it abundantly clear that his only goal in GW at this point os holding the title. Why get himself bogged down in another brutal war with McNasty (or his army of midgets) when he’s got his eye on the top prize?

Then there’s Salaok. Ok, I’ll admit it. Salaok Davis is a sick bastard. I don’t believe for a second this man would have the slightest compunction about hurting Donny McNasty the way he was. Morally speaking, there’s no doubt he’s capable.. And it’s obvious what happened to Donny gut under Brick’s skin so that could be a possible motivation. But here’s the thing. The same reason Salaok is a suspect is the same reason he wouldn’t do it... Salaok Davis is a sick bastard. In this ugly fued with Brick, Salaok has committed many an atrocity, including to threatening Brick’s infant niece and nephew. And that proves one thing. Salaok isn’t interested in the mundane. Beating up Brick’s tag partner isnt’ depraved enough for Salaok’s sick tastes. When he does something, I assure you it’ll be a lot worse than what happened to Donny.

So that brings us back to the begining. Who done it?

Actually the answer is obvious for anyone willing to pull their head out of the ass long enough to look.... Bubba McCoy

Yeah... that’s right... you heard me.

Think about it for a second. The second Donny got “hurt” Bubba’s only real objection was that one of his major draws had been snatched in Ruckus Inc. Oh, but he didn’t waste much time finding a replacement for Donny in his brand name tag team, now did he? And let’s be clear folks, for you folks not int he know of the goings ons behind the closed doors of the board rooms of GW. It’s no secret amongst GW officials that Bubba has had a hard on to see the two big, black dudes from the streets of Harlem and Chicago running ruckshot through the ranks of GW. I mean damn... talk about marketable. Especially as GW Tag Team Champions.

So, the only issues keeping that from happening basicly get nixed, one after the other, in the grand span of 2 whole weeks. First, of course, there’s McNasty himself. He comes to the ring to celebrate valentines day, to talk about who his heart beats for and somebody winds up nearly tearing it out. Talk about irony. Oh, and then there’s the little fact Brick and Train HATE EACH OTHER’S GUTS!!! Oh, but since nobody can prove anybody else’s guilt or innocence they’re forced to work together lest they be suspended in definitely. All because Brick assumed Train was the one that hit Donny and proceeded to try and take his head off. I mean in that context Bubba McCoy was simply trying to protect his investments.

Perfectly reasonable.

Not to mention convenient.

Rest assured folks, none of this is a coincidence. This was a well crafted, well orchestrated plan. The only real question is not who’s behind it, but who McCoy got to do his dirty work for him. I mean come on folks. I know he maintains that “I’m a real live cowboy” image... GW’s version of President Reagan... but the fact of the matter is cowboy’s don’t wear $2,000 suits, and the ones who do damn sure ain’t gonna get their hands dirty. You know... GW’s version of President Bush. The point is this the mastermind isn’t nearly as interesting as the hitman contracted to take Donny out.

But that’s a story for another time...

Wild, Unsubtantiated Rumors
(So they’re probably true)

Superstar or Big Poppa?
KPJ’s gun may have jammed at High Octane, losing that World Title, but the info I’m getting from reliable sources about town are telling me when the pretty boy Superstar does manage to bust a shot he may be nailing it straight through the uprights into the bull’s eye. I’ve had reports of Gunnar Addison being spotted at a local drugstore not too far from GW Arena and she wasn’t picking up a prescription. Unless you need a doctor’s note for a home pregnancy test.....

How ‘bout Holy Water with a twist?
Does anybody actually believe in vampires? People, for those suckers out there that are buying this Undead thing from one of the newest names to make a big splash in Corvus... I also have reliable sources tell me they spotted this man at a hoity toity Italian restaurant downtown enjoying a nice past dinner... while adding extra garlic to his meal... I thought vamps hated that stuff?

Woof, Woof or Meow, Meow?
We’ve all watched this little soap opera as week after week Mike Biddle pursues Mindy Mercer only to be blown out of the sky like the Hindenburg. Is it because Biddle is a smug, arrogant, jackass? Or is that he’s just not her type... as in his gaydar ain’t exactly tuned right... I’ve had a few reports of a couple of late night romps for the newly crowned GW Television Champion through a couple of well known “Female Favored Establishments”. Ok... sure... it could just be good clean fun. And hey, most women likes dogs. But some loooooove kitty cats....
Sidenote: Does anyone know what Aradia’s favorite animal is?

Road Buddies?
I don’t know why I’m bringing this one up. I mean it’s just too ridiculous.... I had some... jerk, tells me that he saw Gunnar Addison and KPJ’s new nemesis, Ice, getting chummy/touchy feely a few times way back when Ice was still around and KPJ and Gunnar were barely on speaking terms (despite all the public claims through publicists that they were “still good friends”). But I mean, like I said, that’s just stupid. cuz I mean... It’s not like one guy would get lonely on the road and betray his best friend by sleeping with his chick. I mean.. when has that ever happened?

Signing Off

Alright, I’ve had enough shining of light in the dark, crusty crevices of this company for one edition. So... until next time... Don’t worry boys and girls. Your secrets are safe.

Just not from me.........
