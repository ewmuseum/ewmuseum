1 Christian Darke: stays at number one this week as he retained his title in a grueling Ultimate Ironman match at Dark Rituals  4
2 Digital Morality: Takes the number two spot. Despite loosing to the World champion  in a grueling Ultimate Ironman match at Dark Rituals   3
3 John Adams:  moves up one spot as he became Lord of the Graveyard at Dark Rituals earning his self a title shot at any champion he desires. Who will Adams choose to face for there championship? 4
4 Durian David Orthane: Jumps up big time this week as he captures the Global International Championship (in some rather shady circumstances) from Adam Stryker. What is next for DDO now? 5
5 "Captain" Jack Morgan: Becomes Number One Contender for the International championship, not only that but almost become Lord of the Graveyard barely loosing to John Adams.  Can The Pirate keep shocking the world as he rising up the ranks of Global? 7
6 Brian Simon: Took on not one but two fighters at Dark Rituals and walked out retaining his championship. Although the rivalry with one Ely Braxton looks to have only just begun.  10
7 Killer Pirates Rise up with a nice showing in the graveyard battle at DR, and a BIG win, some would say a upset win over HOF Tag Team, True Evil. 14
8 Paul Samson: took on Markus Caleb in one of the most violent and bloody matches in a already violent and bloody event. Samson walked out the victor in a close fight. 16
9 The Reckoning: Make a Huge Impact on the face of Global, taking almost all of the tag team Division out, including the Tag Team champions in the Graveyard. Also the two were indeed the two of the last four in that match.

 -
10 Adam Stryker: Drops down the list as he looses to DDO. (in some rather shady circumstances) What is next for the former International champion? 2
10 Brad Jackson: battle against Quartz went to a whole new level of personal as the mind games continued from Quartz. The two fought to a draw in there match but much much more was on the line then just a win. This one is far from over that is sure.  6
11 Juvian Ramorez. moves up one spot despite not having a great showing in the graveyard.  His win over Pierre helps advance the Black Rose this week. 12
12 Lantlas Anduril: Took on Jack Morgan at Dark Rituals, Barely loosing to the Pirate. What will the Elvin Warrior  have to do to finally make Morgan walk the plank? 11
13 Markus Caleb: took on Paul Samson in one of the most violent and bloody matches in a already violent and bloody event. Though, Samson walked out the victor in a close fight. 16
14 True Evil:  Quartz battled not only alongside Salaok against the Killer Pirates, but he also battled Brad Jackson in a close contest, That match indeed in a draw. Not such a great night for the two Hall of Famers. 8
15 Ely Braxton: Ely battles brain Simon and Burn at Dark Rituals for the Platinum Championship. Not only did Simon retain, but Ely offers his hand in respect, only to git it bitten off by Simon as Simon viciously attacked Ely.  18
16 Kaden Calix: After a not so good showing in the Graveyard, Calix played a major roll in the outcome of the International title match. What roll was that? Well check out the Dark Rituals results to find out. 10
16 Mike Storm: Returned to action and did quite well despite loosing in the graveyard battle.  Storm took time away to heal and returned to Mexico and a tour of Japan representing Global. Now with those commitments over, Storm returns to action in the Octagon. -
17 Julian Fiasco: Returned to action and did quite well despite loosing in the graveyard battle.  Fiasco had a impressive run last time. But some actions out side of Global landed the fiery Italian in trouble and took him away from the Octagon. -
18 Scottywood: Debuted to action and did quite well despite loosing in the graveyard battle.  Scottywood came in though the open challenge and after words decided to sign to global. With a history with Juvian, what will become of that? -
19 Jean Claude Dafoe: Made a few appearances at Dark Rituals,  even changing the match that Salaok had set up for DDO. 13 -
20 Glory Bound. being held to other commitments with other places, Glory Bound has fallen in the Hotlist, But are set to return this week, So they have the ability to leap back up with a good showing.  15

Biggest Rise: The Reckoning 11pts Biggest Drop: Adam Stryker 8pts Off the list: a few people acutely To watch for: BloodAngel, The Aristocrats, Jack Gaither, Grimm, Cady Coleman, Ryukira Takashi, Tyler Sparks, Brock Michaels, Uncensored, Johnny Todaro, Remy Titisu, Austin Advent

Thoughts: Well we just came off what a lot of fans say is indeed the best PPV Global has had in a while. I agree it was a great show. We now see BloodAngel has indeed returned after some time off to heal from nagging injuries and personal issues, that should add a new element to Fusion. Also a lot of new faces have joined the roster. Who will rise up and take the Rising Sun championship?. All in all things are looking real good.
