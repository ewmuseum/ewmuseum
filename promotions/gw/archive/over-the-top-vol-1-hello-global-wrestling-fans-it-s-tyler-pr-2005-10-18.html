Over the Top Vol. 1 Hello Global Wrestling fans! It’s Tyler Preston he

18 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Over the Top Vol. 1Hello Global Wrestling fans! It’s Tyler Preston here again with my newest column, Over The Top! After last nights show of Global Wrestling fans witnessing the Global-Reload PPV, it has come to my attention that we, GW, are re-introducing Overload back to the weekly schedule. This is an awesome addition to our status in GW, for we’ve been quite busy preparing for the PPV, and with so many new signings we’ve found time to bring back a memorable show that will give you highlights of each and every match of the event prior to Overload. For instances if you missed Global-Reload, you’ll have a recap on what went down on Overload, now who can say that’s not awesome!

Onto other issues, first being this upcoming Evolution. We’ve got eight highly anticipated matches lined up for our audience, and hopefully you’ll stay with me as I give you the ups and downs of what I think each match will be like.

Standard Match
Donovan Savage vs. Kid Wikkid

Being the first predictions I’m going to explain how I do this first. First I’ll give a little bit of stats on the wrestlers if needed, and then some background information if I think it’s interesting. After that I’ll compare the wrestlers abilities and such and tell who I think will win. So first off Donovan Savage, a 6’4” 245 pound powerhouse and technical wrestler will be taking on Kid Wikkid, a 5’10” 220 pound youngster. Savage recently spent the last six years locked away in the Mississippi State Correctional Facility. For what reason we’re unsure but we do know his opponent won’t have a problem taking him on no matter what the odds. Wikkid, a former brawler at his last residence was a contender in a series of tournaments capturing much pride and glory as he walked out a quadruple champion 3 years straight. Lying about his age cost him though for the owner of the tournament kicked him out and stripped the titles he once valued as life, only to let him go on and live a different life. Wikkid is a shoot wrestler with submission and Lucha wrestling sills. A rumor is heard that UFC turned him down because he was too small, only after he broke their trainers neck during a pre-fight, yet that’s still just a rumor.

My predictions are that Donovan will most likely be as brutal as he can, being a new comer and being in jail for so long, he’s defiantly not the fighter you’d expect of in a ring. So in the end Kid Wikkid will most likely end up knocking out or pinning the big man after his famous K-Dub KO combo. It will be a good one nonetheless.

Winner: Kid Wikkid


Tag Team Match
Southern Discomfort
[Ely Braxton & Jesse Briggs]
vs.
Destruction Inc.
[KC Jacobs & Tommy Michaels]

These two teams were both eliminated early in the four-way gauntlet match at Global Reload. As disappointing as it was, now one of the two teams will get the pleasure of knocking the other one out of the Tag Title circuit completely, or for the time being at least. Anyone who’s anyone knows who Ely Braxton and Jesse Briggs are, two of the best wrestlers in GW, and have been around for a long time. Both are of the south decent that you can defiantly notice in their accents, but are great wrestlers none-the-less. However, KC Jacobs and Tommy Michaels are new and unknown to the GW and myself.

All I know is that it’s going to take Tommy Michaels and KC Jacobs a lot to keep these two southern boys down, and defiantly a lot of luck to miss the unforgiving Aftershock that Ely and Briggs deliver. Best of luck to the newcomers, I wouldn’t doubt on it being too big of a match, maybe more of a bathroom break for you big fans.

Winner: Southern Discomfort


Standard Match
Special Guest ref: Brick
Terminator Mike Page vs. Mark Makonie

Mike Page is a beast! The man towers over all in ring and probably is over seven foot. His massive structure and ability to wrestle will be tested though when Mark Makonie who is a ruthless Nazi who wants to punish people in GW. He doesn’t play fair, and will take cheap shots, so to anyone who thinks this will be a technical match, think twice, and then forget it cause this will be down and rough. With Brick being the referee we’ll have to see which way this flies. We know that Mark is racist, and if he gets out of hands, Brick will lay him out.

So whom do I choose? Well I’d have to say, Mike Page. Page may not be the smartest but he sure does have better odds against Makonie with Brick being there. Especially since I want to see Brick knock Makonie’s teeth in.

Winner: Mark Makonie


Standard Match
Terrelle Green vs. Gabery Breed

Terrelle Green lasted quite long at Global Reload with his partner under the tag team name ‘The Syndicate’ but now it’s one on one with Gabery Breed and the only word that comes to mine is, who booked this? Terrelle is a tag team contender, and Gabery is a singles… If you think Green is going to win, then you’ve got issues. But I will give him this credit; he does do a good job losing to The Souls of the Dark Lotus.

Winner: Gabery Breed


Standard Match
Brick vs. Kenji Lee

Brick, one of the best GW World Heavy Weight Champions of all time going head to head with Kenji Lee? Is it just me or does Kenji Lee have a 0-3 record since he started? So why is he facing Brick, no clue… But at least we get to see another feather be beaten down by the brick! WOO! I crack myself up… (Clears Throat) So anyway, prediction… Brick.

Winner: Brick


Standard Match
Kid Flash vs. Max Roswell

Kid Flash, one of the best cruiser weights in GW going head to head with Max Roswell, one of the best PrimeStar champions in GW. Should be interesting. Let’s see, currently Roswell is just returning to the ring after a long absences, and this will be a good match he has as him and Kid Flash show off there high flying abilities. It will be a great contest and the winner will defiantly be the next cruiserweight #1 contender, or up in that area.

Winner: Kid Flash


Tag Team Match
La Ultimo Fantasia
[Seymour Almasy & El Lunes Mascara]
vs.
Souls of the Black Lotus
[Ichiro & Jushin Nakazura]

The two best tag teams in GW here live as the pre-main event! It’s going to be great. Seymour Almasy, the PTC Global Champion and El Lunes Mascara a new comer who formed an alliance with Seymour are together to take the tag division by storm. Ichiro and Jushin put on a great show at Global-Reload as they won the 4-way gauntlet tag match, showing their talent is still strong within the GW ring.

My prediction is, this will be a close match. The team who gets their strategy down will most likely come out on top, and since Ichiro and Jushin are brothers, I’ll assume they’ll pull a win out in the end.

Winner: Souls of the Black Lotus


Main Event
International Title Defense
Jesse Jamester © vs. Ice

Ah the main event… Jesse Jamester the champion squaring off against Ice, the challenger. Ice won his match against Seymour last night and afterwards took the PTC Global Title and raised it in the air showing he wanted it more than ever before, and most likely thought he deserved it. Jesse Jamester on the other hand lost to Akodo Rokku, and from what GW Rumors say; he’s not been seen since the ending of the match. With Jamester’s loss and Ice’s win, what can one think than that of Ice continuing his winning streak?

My prediction is that Ice will most likely win but not at a small price, for Jamester may have lost his World Title shot, but he’s a true competitor and the loss was just a set back. So Ice will win by a hair in the end.

Winner: Ice


This has been Tyler Preston with your weekly predictions… Thank you all!
