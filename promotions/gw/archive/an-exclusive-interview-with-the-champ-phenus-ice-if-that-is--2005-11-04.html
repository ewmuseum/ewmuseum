An exclusive interview with the champ Phenus: Ice, if that is your rea

4 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An exclusive interview with the champPhenus: Ice, if that is your real name, how long have you been involved in professional wrestling?
 
Ice: I started in the business when I was around the age of 14 so what, that would make it 6 years now.
 
Phenus: 20 years old a world champion. Amazing, absolutely amazing. What made you get into the business at first?
 
Ice: Became an orphan at the age of 13, was wondering around the streets, looking for a place to eat, a place to sleep... I came to Gary's Gym, to warm myself up from the Canadian Cold outside, and Gary walked over, and said, you train or you leave.  So I trained, I stayed there, trained day and night... Gary one day told me about a small show coming into town, that was looking for young talent, and said i should try it out....I entered a 20 man battle royal, and was the very first person thrown out, but it got the juices flowing.

Phenus: Nice... Where did you grow up in Canada?
 
Ice: I claim Toronto as my home... but basically from the age 13-18 I was a nomad. I would go where I could to find work.  There I would eat and sleep, sometimes if I was lucky, it would be warm food, and some shelter, mostly not though.  But yeah, Gary's is where I call home, and it's in Toronto.
 
Phenus: Would you consider Gary a mentor?
 
Ice: Yeah, I suppose, He was only around when I was 13 till I was 15, before he died of cancer.  After that is when my career really started, as I traveled the country looking for work.  But when I was first starting, he was there, pushing me, helping me.  But a lot of the older guys I worked with when I first started really helped out as well.  So Gary would be a mentor, along with many others.
 
Phenus: After Gary's death, what did you do? Who did you train under? Where did you go? How did you deal with his death?
 
Ice: I didn't go to the funeral.  I was on tour with a small promotion, and we were doing a show here in the states.  So i got word of his death via telephone.  After the tour was over, I went back to Toronto, and the gym, and started training harder than ever before.  But that lasted for a couple months, as I was back out on the road again, looking for work, and touring North America.  Again, I was basically a nomad throughout that time.  I can't really say I dealt with his death, other than training, and exhausting myself in front of hundreds of people night after night.
 
Phenus: Did it hurt you emotionally not to be able to attend his funeral?
 
Ice: No, not really.  But I felt that I should have been there, out of respect for the man that took this orphan in.  Not so much for myself, but out of gratitude for what he did for me.
 
Phenus: OK fair enough. Let’s delve a little deeper into your past before we come full head. What was your birth name and what happened to your parents?
 
Ice: My first name is Brock; as I'm sure you have heard Star say many times.... however so I am not stalked ... i would prefer to leave the last name a secret.   As for my parents. It’s a long story.  My mother died of alcohol poisoning.  Yes she was an alcoholic.  That left me and my father.  My father, he owned a small trade store.  With crafts, and food, and things like that.  Good business.  Driving home, he got into an accident, and was killed instantly.
 
Phenus: Tragic. So there were no grandparents or aunts and uncles to take you in?
 
Ice: No, none that I know of, none that have tried to get in contact with me now.  So i just assume none to begin with.
 
Phenus: That’s amazing. Once you become rich and famous, relatives come out of the wood work. Look at me!
 
Ice: Ha-ha, yeah, I expected something like that.  But, haven't had that problem.  I think people might be a little scared of me, or ashamed of me, by my actions.  That could be reason enough.
 
Phenus: Why would they be ashamed of you? You are GW World Heavyweight Champion and you haven’t even reached the top of your potential yet.
 
Ice: Carl, have you not been watching?  Have you not seen some of the things I've done to people, that aren't in the ring?  Me personally, it doesn't bother me to do those things to people, but some people, don't like to be associated with a "menace to society", like myself.   As for being GW Champion.  You would think that would bring them all out of the woodwork, but again, when you willingly spill the blood of innocent people, for your own enjoyment, people tend to resent you, and your actions.
 
Phenus: I can understand that, but I am desensitized to most of that. I have worked with Tony Stecchino for God knows how many years and he has done a lot worse than you have, believe you me. Speaking of which. How does it feel to be in a group such as TUE?
 
Ice: Whatever.  The group thing doesn't matter to me.  I don't really like to associate myself with it.  I was contacted by Leinad Soma, and was hired on as apart of Soma Corp, to take care of things while he was away.  So TUE, no TUE, doesn't really matter to me.  Star finds it to be worthwhile.  With all the money, and benefits.  That I guess is the only good thing about it.  Is seeing her happy.  Especially with the new addition on the way.
 
Phenus: So not being able to work with some of the industries greatest legends is not a perk?
 
Ice: Carl, I've been on my own since I was 13.  I'm not very keen on trusting or relying on others.  This is a business.  The guys in TUE and I aren't friends.  We don't hang out.  We rarely talk.  It is a business deal.  And I am smart enough to realize, when I am no longer a service to them, I will become a target.  Also, there are guys in the TUE just like in GW that see this World Title around my waist, and desire to have it.  TUE alliance or not.  So, I don't look at the things like working with "legends", or anything like that.  The only thing to me that is a perk about this is the extra cash, that I get to spend, to make Star happy.  Other than that, it's just another business deal.
 
Phenus: You said you two were expecting. When? Boy or girl?
 
Ice: Late may, early June is when we are expected.  It's too early to tell if the baby will be a boy or a girl.  Either way doesn't really matter to me.  I just want it to be healthy.
 
Phenus: Outside the character of Ice, Brock how do you see yourself as being a father?
 
Ice: You're getting an exclusive, because this will be the FIRST and ONLY time you ever hear me say this, but I… I'm a little scared
 
Phenus: Cuz it’s your first and you have never been a father before correct?
 
Ice: Yeah, and I didn't have much of the family life growing up... I don't know how that quite works.  I've never been into showing emotions, or caring about people.  But, I love Star, more than she or anyone else will ever know.  And I know with her by my side, I can do anything.  And this family thing, will work itself out.
 
Phenus: That’s great; I truly wish you the best of luck with the up coming family.
 
Ice: Thank you, I'm sure Star would appreciate it as well.
 
Phenus: Send my best out to her. But do you see yourselves getting married in the future?
 
Ice: Yes, I thought about it, before the pregnancy.  I've just been waiting for that "right time" to pop the question.  Ya know?
 
Phenus: Yeah I know. I have proposed to over fifty women. Not a one, have said yes.
 
Ice: Ha-ha... sorry about your luck.  Maybe the next one will be the charm.
 
Phenus: Yeah... Cameron Diaz should say yes. She has no idea its coming.
 
*Long uneasy pause.*
 
Phenus: Moving on. How did if feel to win the Global Wrestling Heavyweight Championship of the World?
 
Ice: I’ve been in a lot of companies, and have been screwed out of my fair share of World Titles.  So for it to finally happen, against 7 of the best this business has to offer.  On such a big stage, and in such a crazy match, it meant a lot.  Being able to call myself World Champ, is me sticking it to all those promoters who screwed me over in the past.  Or those who said I couldn't do it.  I pride myself on being the best.  Holding a title doesn't always mean it.  But everyone who enters this business aspires to hold the World Championship once.  I now have done that.  I always felt I was the best before, this is right here, is proof.  I can honestly now say, I have nothing left to prove to ANYONE. 
 
Phenus: Who do you see as being the biggest threat to your title reign?
 
Ice: My unborn child and its mother.  Ha-ha.  Seriously though, I believe, that there isn't a single person in this company, ONE on ONE, that can beat me.  Rokku, Jin, Holmes are all tough competitors, but I just don't think they have what it takes.  The only thing, in my mind, that could end this title reign, is something happening at home, causing me to leave, or lose focus on the task at hand.
 
Phenus Yeah we all know that a woman can destroy any hopes of happiness for a man. Look at Peg and Al Bundy. Prime example right there
 
Ice: Ha-ha.....
 
Phenus: With Showdown in Tokyo 4 coming up in just under a month. Who do you see yourself facing to retain your coveted championship?
 
Ice: Any of the three I mentioned before, are the Top Contenders in my mind.  I know Rokku feels we have some unfinished business.  Jin and I haven't closed the book on our feud.  And I know Deacon wants his title back.  But honestly, I don't really care.  I won't duck anyone. 
 
Phenus: Wow... Strong words from a true champion. Would you like to say anything in closing before we wrap this up?
 
Ice: To the men in GW who wish to contend for this belt.  I'd like you all to remember one simple thing.  This is a COLD COLD world, and there is nothing colder than ICE.
 
Phenus: Well Ice it’s been an extreme pleasure to interview you today.
 
Ice: *nods*
 
Phenus: Well that is all for today. This has been Carl Phenus with the GW World Champion, Ice.
