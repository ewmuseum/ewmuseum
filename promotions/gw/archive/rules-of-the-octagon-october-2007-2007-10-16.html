Rules of the Octagon (October 2007)

16 October 2007

RULES of the OCTAGON According to Bubba McCoy,  Written by Bubba McCoy
The following rules have been laid down by my self to reflect the new out look of this company, The following six rules are approved and created by myself, Bubba McCoy, Owner of Global Entertainment Inc. 
Sincerely,  Bubba James McCoy II
-Rule 1: There are no count outs in Global:  If you leave the octagon side area, you have chosen to forfeit the match and the ref will declare the match a forfeit: Title's can change on  forfeit.
-Rule 2: There are no rope breaks in Global:  Rope breaks are for sissy boys, here in global they will not save you, fight your way out or tap out of any submission...
-Rule 3: There are no rules outside of the octagon:  In the octagon the referee is the law. Out side of the octagon however he has no authority. Outside of the octagon, it is anything goes. Be careful some guys love this and use it to its fullest advantage.
       -Rule 3a: Interference:  Rule 3, only applies to those booked in the match, any help from friends is considered cowardly and will not be tolerated. If you need a friend to help you. then enter the tag division. 
-Rule 4: There are rules inside of the octagon:  In the octagon the rules are enforced. Any violation will warrant a disqualification loss. Wins can only happen in side of the ring.
        -Rule 4a: Added stipulations:  Rule 4 stands unless stated otherwise in a added stipulation to the match.
-Rule 5: In the octagon the referee's word is law:  In the octagon the referee is the law, he can call the match at anytime for any reason as long as it is not outlawed to him in the above rules. Do not touch a referee. this will cost you the match and a hefty fine. 
-Rule 6: The ways to win:  There are four ways to win, any match. Pinfall, submission, knockout, rendering you're opponent unconscious. While the ref is giving a ten count do not get in his way, or it will cost you the match.  The ref can also call the match at any time he deems necessary and award a TKO. 

      -Rule 6a: Added stipulations: As with rule 4, Rule 6 stands unless stated otherwise in a added stipulation to the match.
