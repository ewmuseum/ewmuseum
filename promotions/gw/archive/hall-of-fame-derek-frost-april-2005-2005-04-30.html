Derek Frost

During his time in Global, Frost became known as one of the hardest workers on the roster.  Picked up in the beginning days of Global  Derek Frost un orthodox  and daredevil style has earned him  many championships.  Teamed with Jay Robson as Cold blood he formed  one of the most memorable tag teams of all time as Cold Blood.  
His feud with Kullypso is one of legend and Many believe that his best is yet to be seen. If this is the case then we are in for a hell of a ride whenever he decides to return. 

And that time could be now as Frost has returned  from retirement...

Words from his peers: 

Derek Frost has such a drive and determination in a man that I have ever seen. He has a style all of his own and watching him fight is a joy. Derek's Charisma  adds to his in ring skill to make him a true superstar. I think he felt very discouraged before his retirement, the world title slipping past him every time. Now that he has returned though with even more determination then before. It is only a matter of time though before he captures the title that has always eluded him..   - Bubba McCoy -

The motivation in Derek is just too extreme for my blood. He has so much heart and determination it is unrealistic. I still can't believe he hasn't held the World Title.. yet.  - Sage Sabbot -
