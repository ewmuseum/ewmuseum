Master of the Mat 2007: Three Main Events Promised
With this year's Master of the Mat less than four weeks away, Global is in the process of materializing one of the biggest cards in Global history. If you thought last year's Master of the Mat was extraordinary, you won't be disappointed in this year's Master of the Mat, Global's biggest sporting event of the summer.

So far, Fusion and Underground have been working out the First and Second Round tournament matches to see who the six wrestlers will make it into the semi-finals for the Pay-Per-View. Superstars like Joshua Kosidlo, Joaquin Pierre, James von Doom, Delylah, Salaok Davis, Quartz, Hank Cobb, Digital Mortality, Kaden Calix, and a mystery superstar from the Global Lottery.

Speaking of the Global Lottery, this will be everyone's third and final chance to participate in this event, and whoever has the strongest showing via votes from the Global fans, will get the coveted Lottery win, and will be the sixth participant of the Master of the Mat Semi-finals.

Now, onto the three Main Events - if you tuned into Fusion a few weeks back, Psymon and Jocque Noreault have been named number one contenders to Eryc Morgan's World Championship. What was rumored to be a Three-Way Match for the title has been changed into two Championship matches: Jocque Noreault gets his crack at the World Champion in the first Main Event. Whomever wins that match will face off with Psymon in the second Main Event. Will the championship change hands more than once, or will "The Dark Flame" extinquish the flames of both contenders' hopes?

Finally, the moment we've all been waiting for: the Finals of the Master of the Mat tournament. My predictions for the Finals is Joshua Kosidlo vs Salaok. Kosidlo has been working extremely hard in Global as of late, having a grueling feud against Psymon, and knocked his adversary out of the qualifying matches back a Whiplash. He has gone through Sean Molson and, in my opinion, can beat Joaquin Pierre.

The Salaok of old has resurfaced in Global, resurrecting The Nevermore, and winning the Whiplash Battle Royal to get the First Round bye. Along with Quartz, their vicious intent has put the Global locker on notice, telling anyone and everyone if that they run into the Nevermore, they will get crushed.

If you watched last year's Master of the Mat, Joshua Kosidlo was knocked out of the semi by Pitt Hix, one of the lesser Hix Brothers. However, Kosidlo defeats Jake Hix earlier in the tournament, and gets knocked out by Pitt Hix? That smelled like a screwjob to me, ladies and gentlemen. This year will be different for Joshua Kosidlo, because I believe he can win this year's Master of the Mat tournament, and then win the Global World Championship off of whomever the titleholder is after the Pay-Per-View event.

As for Salaok, he, too, can win this tournament, and become the World Champion for the umpteenth time. The problem is that he's been there before; Joshua Kosidlo, and Kosidlo's determination will be enough to win the tournament and win the title.

Also announced for Master of the Mat - a Six-Man Tag Team Match. Last week on Underground, Commissioner Brick announced a big Six-Man Tag Match: Three members from The Nevermore will take on the team of John "The Prophet" Adams, Joaquin Pierre, and the commissioner himself! That's right, Brick is stepping away from his desk and stepping back into the octagon one more time for this monstrous summer event!

That's all the news for now. When more news surfaces, I'll make it known, but stay tuned to Global-Online for all the news, shows, and Global action.

I'm Noi Tsunami, signing off.
