1 Christian Darke: Leaps back to the top spot after one hell of a match against the International Champion. Darke came out victorious showing Stryker why he is indeed the World Champion. But will he be as lucky against a Motivated and determined Digital Mortality?  4
2 Adam Stryker: Despite loosing to Darke he retakes his place as 2nd in the Hotlist. Stryker however now faces a third member of the Nevermore. Can he bounce back and retain his title against DDO? 3
3 Digital Morality: He is back. The Nevermore tired to get rid of him but thanks to Dafoe Digital has returned and now is set to challenge Darke for his World Title. Can the Digital one take the title away and knock Darke down the ranks?  5
4 John Adams:  fought against the unknown and the unknown was Digital. The two fought to a no contest as Darke interfered. One has to wonder would Adams been able to beat the Challenger to the world title?  6
5 Durian David Orthane: leaps up this week as he defeated the Dark Horse and the Pirate Captain to gain the chance to challenge for the International Championship. Can DDO defeat Adam Stryker?  11
6 Brad Jackson: looses his first match since coming back. But now has his eyes set on Quartz in a very personal battle. Quartz made things personal when he put his hands on Shane. Will the Dark Horse make the Monster suffer for his actions? 8
7 "Captain" Jack Morgan: suffered defeat, but you can't keep a good pirate down. But this Dark Rituals He faces off against the Elf Warrior once more. Will Morgan be able to defeat Lantlas once more and win a chance to challenge for the INT championship? 9
8 True Evil:  Salaok and Quartz once again stepped into the ring. This time Salaok made the match for a chance to challenge for the Tag team Championship. There opponents fast rising stars Glory Bound.  Barley winning the Match they now look to destroy the Killer Pirates and add more gold to the Nevermore.  But will Brad Jackson have anything to say about that?

9 Kaden Calix: Returns from a injury at the hands of Adam Stryker and returned with a big statement against the current International Champion with a Omega Driver.  It is just a matter of time before Calix gets Stryker in the ring. -
10 Brian Simon: leaps up again after what many consider a upset victory against the Elvin Warrior. Simon captures the title, but now faces off against not only a pissed off cowboy but a sinister member of the Nevermore.  15
11 Lantlas Anduril: defeated for his championship once more. Could the actions of a certain Pirate have distracted Lantlas causing him to loose focus? Well if so he has a chance to avenge that and maybe win the ring tot challenge the intentional champ at the same time. 10
12 Juvian Ramorez. defeats Ely Braxton and continues to torment Joaquin Pierre. Even attacking him backstage. Will Juvian defeat the Frenchman or will he go down in flames?   12
13 Jean Claude Dafoe: drops down a lot this week. Despite rehiring those fired by Salaok. Dafoe however opted to remain behind the scenes doing his job to keep Global going in the wake of a power hungry Salaok.  1 -
14 Killer Pirates drop down a few more spots do to little activity as none have dared step into the ring with the two monsters. But now they face there biggest challenge ever. True Evil. Can the Killer Pirates find that inner monster and take down the original monsters of global? 7
15 Glory Bound. falls a bit do to the set back loss at the hands of True Evil. But down count them down and out yet. Its just a matter of time before this team gets its chance to challenge for the tag team championship.   14
16 Paul Samson: took on Markus Caleb and the two fought a brutal match that ended in a no contest draw. Now they look to do it once more but this time there must be a winner. Who will walk away from Dark Rituals the victor? and who will be the victim. 20

17 Joaquin Pierre: returns to the list after weeks of being tormented by Juvian. Can Pierre recover from this and reclaim his place in the Global roster by defeating Juvian once and for all?  -
18 Ely Braxton: No dice against Juvian this past week. But that does not matter now as he has a chance to get some revenge against the Nevermore as he faces Burn. And a chance to gain a tile in global as he faces Simon in the same match.  16
19 Brick: falls down a lot as he sits at home waiting for a chance to return and get his revenge against Salaok and the Nevermore. Dafoe rehired him but Salaok's is making Brick start from the bottom once again. 13
20 Rough and Tumble: fall down to the bottom spot this week as they had the week off. But you can bet they will be rising back up as they head to dark rituals to play in the graveyard. 18

Biggest Rise: Kaden Calix 12pts Biggest Drop: Dafoe 11pts Off the list: Bubba McCoy To watch for: Donny Diamond, Julian Fiasco, Jack Gaither, Grimm, Cady Coleman, Ryukira Takashi, The Reckoning

Thoughts: Well Digi is back as Dafoe has returned him to his place and not only that has signed him into a world title match against Christian Darke.  Brian Simon rises up as he gains the Platinum title.  Still the roster is slowly getting settled and at this time the new year looks real good for the federation in a whole.  This week we head to one of the more out there PPV's Dark Rituals This year with a twists to it. here is to a great turn out for a great card for the PPV.
