---- The love of a legend. Josh hadn't even stepped foot into the Eggdome and he was already drawing a crowd. The crowd that had surrounded him was a flock of Japanese fans all wearing "American Youth", "184" t-shirts and even a few, rare "Kid Flash" t-shirts. A smiling Josh was happy to sign a few autographs for the fans, hi bluish gray eyes sparkling with a joy previously unseen in his most recent Global appearances. The hall of famer was a true legend in Japan, having made his mark there before ever achieving big league status in the United States. In addition to a smile Josh wore a black track jacket, the sleeves pulled up to the elbows. Beneath the track jacket Josh wore a white t-shirt with "Rex kwon do" scrawled across the front in red, a graphic of a kicking character appearing on the left side of the shirt, a Yin-Yang symbol in the background. The jeans of American Youth were slung low on his hips as he sported a pair of worn and torn $189.00 Paper, denim & cloth jeans that led down to a pair of black, size 8, unlaced Chuck Taylor's. Last but not least was a bag, heavy and black, thrown over his left shoulder. "Physically Josh appeared to be in better shape than he had ever been, but emotionally? One could see the smile on his face, the twinkle in his eyes, and tell that he was happy...or the best faker alive, and with "American Youth" Josh it was too hard to tell. As Josh signed autographs the cameras circled around catching his every move, even noticing the major change in his appearance...his hair. His hair was longer than in previous appearances, as it slipped a few inches past his color. Most noticeably was the color, no longer was his hair lined with streaks of silver and blue, instead the entire left side had been dyed white, the right side remaining its natural black. Josh's progress to the arena was slow as the fans swarmed him, he signed their autographs and shook their hands smiling broadly as he did so. The fans began to thin out as he reached the door of the arena and two looming security guards stood in their way. Josh stopped, turned and smiled at the fans, only a few lingering. He playfully mussed the hair of a young Japanese boy and leaned down so that a beautiful young Japanese lady, appearing to be in her mid 20's could kiss him on his cleanly shaven left cheek. Winking playfully at her Josh turned away and stepped towards the security guards, he reached into the right pocket of his track jacket to pull out his security pass but saw there was no need as the guards opened the door broadly for him. He nodded his head thankfully as he stepped through the door. As soon as the door of the Eggdome had closed behind him, the frenzy began again. A collaboration of Global Wrestling staff members stood before him all with an assigned task. Production assistant Rob Emmanuel, a nerdy kid in his early twenties was the first to speak.
