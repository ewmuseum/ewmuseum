Recent History 'He is the Global Icon! He is the Franchise! He is the Innovator of Fucking Greatness! OH MY GOD... it's 'SUPERSTAR' KEN PARTRIDGE, JR!'

After winning the old GW Primestar Championship and being stripped of it due to personal reasons... Ken Partridge Jr, the man heralded as 'Superstar', and the self proclaimed Innovator of Fucking Greatness has returned to Global Wrestling once again after a short leave of absence. This time, surprising all in forming what looks to possibly be the most dominant force since the original Four Horsemen in The Elite, originally consisting of himself, Josh, Ice, and Ace Harding. Later, alongside Bill Moresi and Gunner Addison, the former Primestar and International Champion's reign of superiority looked unstoppable as he has added a third GW Television Championship to his title history along with a stint with his first ever Global Wrestling World Title. However, after losing the Championship to Christian Zenith, Ken's eyes are set to a higher level. After welcoming back Ace Harding and a returning Josh to the Elite ranks, he captured his second International Championship. Now he's trimmed back nearly ten pounds to streamline his physique and improve on his already uncanny quickness, alowing all that stands between him and the Grand-Slam Title are the Platinum and Tag Team Championships, and Ken looks to snag the 'elite' honor before hanging up the boots.
