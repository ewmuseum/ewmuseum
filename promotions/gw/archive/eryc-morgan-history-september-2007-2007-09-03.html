Eryc Morgan — history (September 2007)

3 September 2007

History

The re-emergence of Eryc Morgan as a star in Global was not foreseen when he left the company in August of 2005.

A broken man, Eryc's passion for the octagon was gone and at the time would have said that he would never make an appearance for any fight promotion as long as he lived.

Morgan went back to his career as a musician and placing a backseat on aggressive competition of any kind. He felt that his life was better off not wasting it by being battered and bruised week in and week out.

That was until a life changing accident and made him realize that he did need the feeling that came with being inside the octagon of Global.

Striking a deal with Brandi Sights and Bubba McCoy, he was originally given a tag team contract only to see how things would work out because of his previous turbulent behavior before leaving.

His partner would be his brother Ash Morgan and they would form Projekt Mayhem.

Unfortunately for the two of them their family ties were not enough to keep them from having problems with one another and caused Ash to walk out leaving Eryc in a bind.

Global was happy with his performance and behavior and chose to give him a new deal as a singles competitor with some reluctance but thus far have been proven to have made a good decision.

Eryc has become a success story this second time around but he does not credit this to just himself. He attributes his stardom to the fans who have taken him into their hearts and helped make him what he is today.

Morgan is not alone though, he is backed up by John Adams and Joshua Kosidlo who make up the team of The True Saints. Their mission is simple: to remove corruption and negative entities from the Global locker room.

Their sights are set on Jake Hix and the Syndicate Rules: Extreme, who have used their clout to give them free reign of Global as their playground.
