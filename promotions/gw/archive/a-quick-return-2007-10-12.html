A Quick Return
After a meeting between Global Staff and his representatives, both Global and Joaquin Pierre are pleased to announce that the now former Platinum and International Champion has agreed to a new contract and will be returning to Global in the next few weeks. When reached for comment, a contrite Pierre had the following to say:

Joaquin Pierre: "I made a mistake, and I regret it from 'ze bottom of my 'eart. My contract with Global was up, and I decided to try and hold out for some extra money and some guarantees in my new contrat. Unfortunately, I let my ego get 'ze better of me, and I ended up walking out on 'ze company. It cost me 'ze International Championship, and it cost me 'ze respect of many fans and my fellow fighters in Global.

Joaquin Pierre: I am a man who admits when 'e 'as made a mistake, and I am willing to admit 'zat I made a 'uge one 'ere. I only 'ope 'zat 'ze fans and 'ze fellow Global members can forgive me for making a foolish mistake. I look forward to stepping back into 'ze Octagon and competing once again in 'ze new Global landscape."

We asked him whether or not the high profile departures of World Champion Corvus, Jocque Noreault, Josh Kosidlo, and Scott Taylor had anything to do with his departure, and Pierre held nothing back.

Joaquin Pierre: "I would be lying if I 'zaid 'zey did not. 'Eet was another part of my decision to walk."

To conclude the brief interview, we asked the last man to hold both the Platinum and International Championships (both the titles are currently vacant) what his plans for a return to Global were. Again, he was blunt.

Joaquin Pierre: "I plan on competing against 'ooever 'zey send up against me, and winning. 'Zis is my short term goal, long term, I plan on taking back what was mine, 'ze International Championship. But I realize 'zat at 'zis point I do not deserve anuzzer chance at 'eet. And I am content to start at 'ze bottom of 'ze ladder and work my way back up."

Pierre's stock was rising after a slow start to his Global career, and this reporter has to wonder what the future holds for the popular Frenchman. Will he continue his rise in Global, or will this recent controversy surrounding him cause a set back?
