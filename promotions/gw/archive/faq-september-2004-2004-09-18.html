FAQ (September 2004)

Global Wrestling FAQ

Welcome to the FAQ page... Hopefully this will answer some Questions of yours. If you have anything to add to this mail the staff... thanks!


I have not gotten an e-mail saying I am in the fed or not. Why? What's going on??

Well Likely We have not reviewed your application yet. Do not worry. We will get to your application as soon as we can. If it has gone more than a week with out a answer then mail me at [detail withheld]


I just joined, but I want a World Heavyweight Title shot. When can I have it??

Hold up there a bit, OK yeah... Everyone wants that title, and you may have been a many time world champ elsewhere, but there are guys in front of you. Don't worry about the World Title right now. Start chasing after the TV Title first, Build your guy up here, World Title will come in time.


How can I get a belt then?

Well when you're doing good, the staff will decide if you get a shot. Your time will come, so just build your character 'til then. Then start a feud with the champion.


I joined and was sent to GU. DO I have to go to GU?

Well yes.


Why do I have to go to GU?

Because, we use GU as a testing ground for you as a player not just a rper. We see how you act and how you play and how much you want to be in this federation. We look not just at Rping but OOC activity, consistency, and many other things.


How long do I have to be in GU and what is the Rping rule in GU?

Minimum of 1 rp per week. To graduate, 5 at an avg of 85. If all above 90, you grad with special honors. So that would make the minimum of 5 rps. It is really up to you how long your here.


I am new and no-one wants to start a angle with me. Why are you guys being such assholes??

Well they're probably not being a ass. Often they wont pay attention to you till after you make some waves. The good kind. Most are caught up in angles already. Although I have found that most are willing to do something to help there fellow member. Talk with them over Messengers and Emails. Run Ideas buy the one[s] you wish to angle with. If you need pointed in a certain direction then talk to a member of the staff.


How do I get angles?

Basically by working them out on your own , We allow you the freedom to run what angles you wish, as long as you work with the other people included into that angle, Once done and every th8ing is approved by your fellow fed mates, then Mail them in or IM me or the Staff. to get them. If you can I suggest you get some sort of IM service - preferably AIM [AOL Instant Messenger]. We do a lot of angles that way, and Most of the GW roster has that.


How can I start a good feud with someone?

Well as I stated above talk to your fellow fed members though messenger and email. Ask who is available to feud with and but don't try feud with someone who has other plans already, and don't harass people about working with you.


What about a push? How do I get a push here?

This is a often asked question. Firstly, you have to make your presence known. IN GW you get what you put effort into. Do card segments, Be consistent with your role-playing. Come up with storylines and feuds, Also let yourself be known on the OOC, in the good way. Don't be a asshole. That will not get you anywhere except fired. Become a member of this fed, not just here. Acutely put effort into the fed and you will do good.


Why don't my angles work out as planned?

Sometimes when doing results, I have to judge how it will flow sometimes that strat will not get used because time constraints, and clashes with other angles. Sometimes I may forget, After all I am human, But I will try not to do that. If This happens Don't get mad. Anything and everything can be fixed and worked for the next show. We try and not have this happen... sometimes it does though.


Who can I e-mail my ideas to?

Check the Front Office page on the site. There is a list of Emails to who you can send Ideas to, Also you may do this though instant messengers and private messaging on the boards. '


My RP's aren't going too well. What can I do to make them better?

Watch the top stars at work. Mail and ask some one. If all fails IM me and we will talk it over. Sometimes there is a need for a gimmick change or even a character change. Sometimes the guy you have wont get over. and you will have to make a new one. All of the greats have done this. Just ask any of the upper card guys,


How many wrestlers can I have?

This is tricky one. if you're new, 1, till you get the hang of the game. Technically you could get away with 3, 1 singles and a tag. But think about it! If you don't think you can handle doing more than 1, don't do it. If you try and fail it will make you look bad, Plus I rather have you do One or a solid Tag team.


Can I bring in another wrestler of mine?

Same as above. Yes but if you cannot handle more than one, then it is not a good idea.


I think I should be a top contender. Why aren't I?

Ranking is based on Role-plays, angles, commitment... a lot of things... If you are not where you think you should be then I have a reason for it. Prove me wrong, and you will get what you deserve.


Do you Sim or hand type the results??

We hand write them. Sims are too boring. I sometimes use a sim as a tool if I need a quick match, but even that gets edited to death.


Why aren't the cards up on time?

We, just like you, have lives outside of the e-fed world. Things happens and the card gets a little late sometimes. We will try our best not to let it happen.


I see you have places in your cards for commercials. Can I put one of my own in the results?

Yes, but it must be approved by me first. Do whatever you want to, as long as it is not too vulgar.


How can I cut an interview during a card?

Type it up and mail it in. Remember to add what matches you want it after. It can take place in the ring, back stage or elsewhere, via on the Globaltron.


I think I got screwed out of a match! What the f@#$?!

Hold on. Calm down. Likely you were out-RPed. You might not think so, but we the staff did. We try our best to make it fair and judge them fairly. However there are instances that I will add strats, angles etc into a match to make it interesting, instead of the "Winner by blah blah blah". Often times setting up a Pay Per View match or a big Feud, Remember, as in any Role-playing game We are the Games-Masters and we will toss in curve balls to catch you off guard. Don't bitch, whine or anything Just roll with it and in the end every thing will be cool. Remember this is a game, FOR FUN!! Also trust us. We know what we are doing.


How's GW different from other feds?

GW is different mostly in a in character since. IN GW everything is real. Nothing is fake. GW is based in Dallas and mostly only travels for PPV's The GW Ring is a Roped Octagon. OOC ways are. GW Is a hybrid of Fighting and Wrestling. GW is a Hybrid of Angle fed and RP fed. GW is largely user driven. You get out what you put in.


Why can I not access the Staff Board on the OOC Boards?

Well because you have not been given permission to. Or your permissions or the password has been changed. Staff and Match writers only have access to that board.


What's the Dark Hoarse Saloon?

It is a Bar / club owned by Bubba McCoy- Time to time GW has matches there. [Used to host a show there] ON the boards it is a IN character way to interact for those who like to Rp more then 3 times a week. Same goes for the Global Arena board. But that board is a challenge board and is all in character.


How do I get inducted into the Hall of Fame?

By being nominated and voted on by fellow members. This happens after retirement from GW and after a long lengthy and memorable stay in GW


What are some ways to get peoples attention when I am booked for a card?

Send in News, Rumors, and other info for the site. Role-play. Do card segments.


What's a card segment?

Its anything you see in the results that is not a match. Interviews, backstage attacks and goings on.


What's the deadline for Evolution, Overload, and the PPV?

Overload: Wednesday at midnight CST.

Evo and PPV's Sunday Midnight CST.

Where's the backstage area, I can't find it?

Dropdown menu. Backstage Link. Click login on the backstage banner.


What do the GW staffers look for in a roleplay?

Mention of the Match and the world of GW. Plus your own characters development.

This of GW as a action movie.


What can I do to make my time in GW better than it would normally be? (Commonly known as "How can I kiss your ass?" )

Well You can pitch in and help things Match write do news reports, hype the fed in various places on the net that is for doing that. A lot of things.


If I feel as if I am being held down or have been wronged, who can I go to? Is it better for me to bring it up on these boards, or take it up privately?

Any of the staff., and privately is best.


What is the history of GW on an IC stand point? Who was the first champion? Who did he defeat

That will come out with the full History pages.. Coming soon


Can I make my own column and share my views on the fed??

But of course! One rule I have on this though, KEEP IT IN CHARACTER, Whether its from your wrestlers P.O.V., Or a made up writer. But do this only if you can do a good job on it. If not you will be replaced. One thing. Don't break the feds rules on it, keep language and "other stuff" to a minimum, and all should be good. As Editor I will review them and may have then changed or even mailed back to you saying "This is not the level we wish it to be... Please redo and submit it.." Sorry, but I want the best, just as any real life fed or mag would want.


I See that you have Pictures for your Wrestlers. Can I use one that looks like my wrestler? Can I use it in my fed?

Sorry, but we spend a lot of time to make them. We use them from Magazine covers to bios to videos and other stuff for the GW, and don't want them being ripped off. If you are already in the GW then there may be exceptions, But you have to give credit to the people responsible for them, The artist work hard on these... REMEMBER THAT!


I am joining and I want a pic. When will I get it?

As I said before, we have lives outside of E-fedding. We will get them ASAP, but there is a rule that we have to make sure you are going to stay in the GW. We don't want to spend time on making you a pic to have you quit the day you get it. We also can make you a logo etc as well, but will do it only if we know you're sticking with the GW.


How do you make them anyways?

We use a Program called Poser 4, Supplemented by Photoshop, Bryce 3d, And other programs.


Can you make me, or my Fed some?

It is up to the artist there self's.. BUT GW comes first... This is highly unlikely though.


How Can I make the fed a better fed?

By a number of things. Try and help out on results. Offer ideas. Don't Bitch. Follow the rules. Pitch in on projects. Spread the word to your buddies from other feds. Help out a rookie. There are tons of ways.
