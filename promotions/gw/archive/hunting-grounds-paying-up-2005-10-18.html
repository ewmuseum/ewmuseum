Hunting Grounds Paying Up


9:29 PM CST - 08.17.05
After their horrendous assault on Mindy Mercer on the August 15th edition of Evolution, Christina McCoy had this to say to Hunting Grounds once the cameras went off.

"After reviewing with happened on Evolution with my Father and my brain-trust, I have decided that a statement on this matter is necessary. Tessa Windsor will be suspended from the arena next week and be fined $15,000. Furthermore, all members of Hunting Grounds will be fined a sum of $10,000. While there is not enough here to warrant suspending all of the members, it should be known that I will have a close eye on each and every one of them from here on out."

Christina Reno was not available for further comment. This incident comes at a bad time for Dawn McFarlane especially as she is scheduled to be in-ring with Christina Reno on the August 22nd edition of Evolution to sign her new contract. If anymore on this breaks, we'll release the information here.
