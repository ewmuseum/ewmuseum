For those of you with the benefit of flash photography...

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---- GW's own Ben J. Pailthorpe is rumored to be making an appearance with Ska-Punks Reel Big Fish when they play a free gig near New York's Time Square. When asked Ben had this to say, "Yeah im probably gonna hang out there with RBF, I met there bassist at a bar in Soho and we ended up becoming good friends, i've met the band before when they played at the Astoria in London." When asked whether he would play guitar he replied, "I'd like to, i've been playing for a few years now, i've never had the oppertunity to get onstage with it, I dunno, I can play a few Reel Big Fish songs, maybe they'll let me join in!" We then asked if he considered branching his career out into music he said, "I'd love to but my first interest in GW, anything else comes ins econd place to me, im focused on my job innit, im concentration on getting myself back up the ranks in GW and challenging for titles." Check out www.reel-big-fish.com for more information!
