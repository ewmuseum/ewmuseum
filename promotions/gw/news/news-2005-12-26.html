News

26 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Platinum Championship match for (re)Load has finally been announced. The vacated title will be contested in a Four Corners match, but only two of the participants have been named. We're told that numbers three and four will be named in the coming week and that the card will be announced completely by January 5th. We also now know that each and every Global roster member will be involved in matches on the (re)Load card, but it is yet unknown what the matches will be other than the three (or two and a half) named ones. Keep checking the (re)Load site for constant updates. UPDATE: Wednesday, December 28, 2005: A new name has been announced to the Platinum Championship Match. Check out the (re)Load card to find out who the Hall of Famer is. Plus, the Tag Team Championship match has been named and there will be a whopping eight teams participating for the vacated titles in an elimination match. Four of them have been named and you can find out who they are by checking out (re)Load's subsite. UPDATE: Friday, December 30, 2005: The rest of the participants in the Tag Team Championship Match have been named and looks as if one superstar will be pulling double duty at the show. We're still awaiting word on the rest of the matches and the final participant in the Platinum Championship match. UPDATE: Friday, December 30, 2005: The entire RELOAD card has finally been released and the fourth and final name in the Platinum Championship match has been named. Also, in a shocking move, due to her arrest, Elia Smith has been removed from the same match for the vacated strap. Who has taken her place and what is the hug final match that was added? Check our RELOAD subsite to find out.
