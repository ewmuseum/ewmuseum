More new signings.

2 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---- More additions have been added to the global roster. These last additions bring the Global roster to a sizable entity? Bubba McCoy said the talent scouts are being called back? “We have a few more potential signings” But other then those we are talking to right now.. We are not going to peruse any singles talent at this moment. I have a few spots for Tag team fighters, other then that?. I want to focus on those ones we have now and get things kicked off for 2006. But you never know when a spot will pop open? So just send in a tape and we will see.” With that said here is a run down on the recently signed stars of Global Corey Blythe: A former WCWF fast rising star? Blythe capture the INT TV title there? Although Not much else is known about Blythe, He is very talent in the ring however, there is no question to that. Blythe uses a striking / technical pro wrestling style of fighting. Carl Cash: A former member of PCW PRIME JEW Sold out Wrestling, Just to name a few. Carl Cash is a ring vet with many federations under his belt, and a few of their titles as well. Carl brings to the octagon Experience. Don’t let the name confuse you, He is not related to Ace Cash? But the two have worked together in the past.. David Crisp: A former fan just like anyone else. A fan of his hero's that were in the business that he so wanted to be in. After capturing the IGA Fight Club Title in his first year of wrestling, he lost it in a Haunted House Match that almost ended his short career. Taking time off he went to a short indy fed, almost gaining the Heavyweight Title but falling short due to his hero of ages turning on him. Now, ego and all, and not trusting anyone, he comes to GW to show them what true young and up and coming talent is. Some may say he is worthless but to him....he's got a Crisp Future! Markus Riley: Some may remember Markus as the Boyfriend of Former World champion Mindy Mercer. Well Now Markus decide to step into the Octagon. Is this about showing his Girlfriend up? Or is there something more here? I don’t know But Markus is a talented fighter and a former world champion of the UCW federation? Eddie Ross: He is a 3rd generation wrestler, his dad wrestled all over the world and that’s where he learned his craft, In his travels watched and learned and is well versed in a multitude of styles. He’s seams like a loner type with a ego the size of Mike Biddle’s? But he is very talented and is a former WCWF Tag team champion, now looking to strike out on his own. Prodigy Inc. Former Indy stars Bryan Prodigy and Karl Pace join Global after some long talks with Bubba McCoy. Signed just in time to make the Reload card. Former Tag team champions, Prodigy is a power high flyer with a bit of martial arts knowledge? Pace is a smashed mouth power striker. Both also have the technical wrestling background of Pro Wrestling. Definitely a tandem to watch out and a nice addition to the ever-growing Tag roster. Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean: Who hired these two lunatics? Global’s going to hell now. The two Psychos should be locked up and the key tossed in the fire. But there fighting skills cannot be denied sadly. They are former tag team champions of LOW and I believe the current now dormant PTC Tag champions? So they are a serious threat to the titles. But I still wonder who in the hell hired these two? I bet it was Logan? that damn drunken dwarf!
