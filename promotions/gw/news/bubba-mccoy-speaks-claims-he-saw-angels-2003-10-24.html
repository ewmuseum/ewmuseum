Bubba McCoy Speaks - claims he saw angels.

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bubba McCoy made a miraculous appearance on Evolution last week only to speak of his amazing grace with God. After months in a hospital and extreme illness due to poisoning, Bubba McCoy returns to Global Wrestling bringing in his footsteps hope, power, and a cross? Many rumors have spread all over the internet stating what was believed to have been said on Evolution after the program went off the air. Many have stated that Bubba McCoy began to lecture both the audience and The Upper Echelon about the grace of God. After his brush with death, James McCoy has found a new hope in life and decided to leave the world of wrestling, a world of trechary and hate, forever. No - that's a bunch of bullshit, but it's just an example of the many rumors spreading since Tuesday Night's Evolution. Hopefully TRUTHFUL light will be shed on the happenings very soon.
