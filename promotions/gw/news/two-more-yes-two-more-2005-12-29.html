Two more? Yes two more!

29 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Two more stars have been added to the ever-growing roster of Global... Here is a quick run down on these two newly signed fighters... Kaden Calik: He's not much of a fighter really, a through and through wrestler. He grew up doing amateur wrestling, learning the Greco-Roman Freestyle side of things. But after he grew out of that (without letting his father know) he joined a "sports entertainment" style wrestling promotion, where his amateur wrestling knowledge was good, but not good enough. In Athens Underground, he learned to compliment his amateur wrestling background with a new and improved that put his technical skills and his sheer power into one style that made him almost one of a kind. The Athens Underground (indy federations in Athens, Greece) is the one and only federation he's been in. There he won the tag team titles twice, and the AU championship five times, his fifth run was actually still in progress before his departure to the US to compete in GTT5, and to find himself a place to start his career. He once competed in the Greek Junior Olympics, where he illegally won the gold medal in the freestyle wrestling. His father lied about his age so he could compete. I caught up with Kaden shortly after leaving Bubba McCoy's office... His response to being asked about global and the competition: "Well, it's hard to say anything about a place that I haven't seen too much of, yet. It can't be half bad if they are taking a chance by hiring me, though. I'm sure the competition will be great, and I'm looking forward to every minute of it. It's my first shot at the big time, so I'm just excited to be here." Rich Sherwin: Sherwin is a former Pro Wrestler who retired a year ago, sighting his disinterest in "Wrestling" Said there was no more competition since it was all decided before you went to the ring... Now with Global’s Direction of a fighting based federation... Rich Sherwin has decided to come out of retirement... And has chosen a familiar federation with a new twist... and a Boss he knows. Sherwin style is of a Submissions Technical mat based style, with Power move ability.
