Global Star Named New Spokesperson for Mountain Dew

4 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Announced at a press junket earlier this afternoon, PepsiCo revealed that actor/novelist/profesional wrestler Kris Kanton will be the new face of Mountain Dew's ad campaign. This news comes in only weeks after Kanton announced he would not stay in retirement, but that he would return to the ring as a new signee to Global Wrestling. Kanton was originally the spokesman for Mountain Dew from 2001 through 2004 but was forced to step down from his duties upon signing a contract with the rival wrestling company of Four Winds Wrestling. 4WW, a subsiderary of Taleswind Productions, required that all athletes and staff employed by the company could not support competative brands. Kanton was known for introducing the "extreme" face of Mountain Dew bringing it into the prominance it is in today from extreme sports competitions to video games. Although the details have not been released nor confirmed it is widely known that Kanton took a pay cut to return to GW. Even with this though Kanton should apparently earn more money, as much as double, than he did during his tenure in 4WW through the soft drink contract, endorsements, and independant appearances. We were able to get a quote from the new GW superstar himself. When asked how he felt about his current situation he replied as follows. "I'm happy to be back with two of my old families, GW and Mountain Dew. I was ecstatic to learn that both companies wanted me to step back on board and I hope this will lead to a great future for the three of us. I can honestly say that this is the best it gets. I couldn't ask for anything more."
