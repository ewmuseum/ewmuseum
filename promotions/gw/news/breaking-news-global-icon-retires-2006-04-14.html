BREAKING NEWS: Global Icon Retires

14 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Global Icon, "Superstar" Ken Partridge Jr, has officially retired from professional wrestling. As it stands, his final match was an eight man tag in which Platinum Champion, Eryc Morgan, joined the ranks of The Elite. When reached for comment, the former World Champion had the following statement: "I may come in and work a farewell match at WrestleCade, but that would be it. I'm done. My body can't take it anymore. Gunner and I are going to get married and start a family. I'm going to keep doing my acting and enjoy an early retirement." He went on to thank all of his fans for their amazing support throughout his career. Ken had a huge list of accomplishments. In GLOBAL alone, he achieved the following: One Time PrimeStar Champion Three Time Television Champion Two Time International Champion One Time Heavyweight Champion of the World He also held the record for most Television Championship reigns and longest International Championship reign. He forged on to earn Most Improved Wrestler, Most Popular Wrestler, and Wrestler of the Year from the GLOBAL fans. GLOBAL wishes Ken all the best in his future endeavors. In a related story, Bill Moresi and Ace Harding have decided to retire as well. They stated, "if Ken's not here, our job is finished." They will defend their Tag Team Championship as planned at Clash of the Titans.
