Salomane Vation To Miss Evolution?

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reports coming in are saying that Salomane Vation, GW’s Primestar Champion, has gone to Israel. Rumors are that he has gone on some kind of a pilgrimage and will be heading through the desert towards the holy city of Jerusalem, but his actions have definitely not pleased GW’s staff, who are expecting him to show up on Evolution for a match that starts the show. “Well, we’ve tried to contact Jerusalem to see if they can get word from him, but we haven’t heard anything back yet. Let’s just say that if he doesn’t show up for Evolution maybe we’ll have to rethink his holding of a GW title,” said one GW writer. Vation is booked to have a match with The Poet tonight on Evolution, but he is also supposed to be getting an answer from Angus The Bull about a possible alignment. Vation offered the chance to Angus on Overload after his match, and gave him until Evolution to give him a response...But if he did not expect to be there you must wonder why he has set it up for this date?
