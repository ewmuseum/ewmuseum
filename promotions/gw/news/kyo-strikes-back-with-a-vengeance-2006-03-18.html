Kyo Strikes Back With A Vengeance

18 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the Platinum Championship Match, “The Deathscythe Outlaw” Kyo Kushinaga was rushed to the back to seek medical attention after he suffered Something Wicked from “The Darkness” Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis near the conclusion of the match. The medical team feared that Kyo Kushinaga suffered a concussion after he hit his head on the mat the way he did, so they decided to take him to the nearest hospital to do more testing but that was until Kushinaga sat straight up on the stretcher where he nearly scared the medical team half to death with this miraculous feat. It said that his first words were: “Can I have a cigarette?” I soon caught up with “The Deathscythe Outlaw” and decided to question him on the current situation. He didn’t say anything for ten minutes as he just sat on the stretcher and smoke a cigarette while he kept his serious eyes locked on the hard floor. Kyo finally responds and he had this to say: “I would like to congratulate Eryc Morgan for winning the Platinum Championship. Sure, he took the easy route to win the title, but in a fight like that it was every man for them self. I know he won’t lose a wink of sleep about how won the title. But sooner or later, the deception is going to start eating away at him. He will begin to realize that it wasn’t him that defeated The Deathscythe Outlaw instead it was another. Eryc won the title and that’s good, but you know it would’ve been great if he could’ve done it on his own. Again, congratulations to Eryc Morgan on winning.” I soon asked if he had any comments about what happened to him. He responded with this: “You mean that love tap” I’ll admit that it stun me. However, I’ve taken a hell of lot worse than that bastard’s Something Wicked. It’s not my fault that he fell for the possum trick. If he don’t like to lose then maybe he should took up another profession. How many times have actually defeated him? I kicked him in his jaw and out of the octagon at Reload. Then there was the time I planted him face first on the mat a couple of Fusions ago. And just tonight I got him with the oldest tricks in the book. There’s nothing I really need to say to him. Sure, I can call him all sorts of names. But I’m not going to do that. He wants to crush every bone in my body. He wants to prove that I’m nothing. Then he’ll get that chance to do on the next Fusion. That’s right I’m challenging Jackson to fight, but just not any fight. Oh no, it’s going to be a 2 out of 3 Falls Fight with NO DQ rules, so that way we can beat the shit out each it other without the fight needing to be stopped. Also, I think I should mention that he was unsuccessful on giving me a career-ending concussion. So, I’m ready to go.” With that he hopped off the stretcher and headed to Brandi Sights office to make the fight official.
