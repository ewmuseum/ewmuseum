Dawg Ready to Bite?

26 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been recently speculated that former superstar Top Dawg was in negotiations to return to the promotion and now it looks like that rumor has some real basis. Unnamed sources say they spotted a rather large and conspicuous GMC Yukon Denali XLT sititng outside GW headquaters late last week. The SUV, looking as if it came straight out of an episode of "Pimp My Ride", complete with 22" rims and what's been reported as one hell of a sound system attracted a whole lot of attention on a day where it just so happened every member of the GW recruitment and talent relations teams were on hand, a very rare occurence indeed. At the same time it's been reported that just about every member of the GW legal team was present at a meeting along side recruitment and talent relations teams. No source was willing to identify the GW prospect that was present in that high powered meeting, but it's obvious that the owner of the never before seen truck is someone who has no qualms about showing his urban root and Top Dawg definitely fits the bill. Since the superstar's identity can not be determined we obviously can't speculate as to when the Dawg will be making his in ring return, but you can likely expect it sooner rather than later. More details as they become available.
