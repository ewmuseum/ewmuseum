KPJ Getting Rave Reviews

22 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ken Partridge, Jr. has been getting rave reviews in his role portraying Roger in RENT during Global's break. As reported in August, Ken agreed to do what he considers his "dream role" during Global's off time for the Christmas Holidays. His final performance will be on January 5th. He an dthe rest of the Global superstars will return to the octagon one week later for the mammoth pay-per-view, (re)Load. In other news, "The Death of Superman," starring KPJ as the Man of Steel, will be premiering in March of 2006 and "Superman Lives" will be hitting theaters in September of the same year. It's been reported that the next Superman project he does will be entitled "Superman: Birthright," based off of the graphic novel of the same name by mark Waid, and will be a modern look at the origin of Krypton's last son. Warner Bros. wants Ken to sign on to do two more Superman films thereafter, to end the series. Rumors are flying regarding the last two films, but there is strong word that one could be based around the "Superman: Red Son" graphic novel by Mark Millar in which is depicted the happenings if the Man of Tomorrow had landed in Communist Soviet Union instead of the United States. We'll send out notice as soon as we receive word on whether or not Ken signs on for the last two features. Ken is next set to begin filming on the remake of "The Towering Inferno." We understand that he'll begin filming in April.
