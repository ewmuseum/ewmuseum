Who are the Mystery Men!?

18 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---- Over the last few weeks Two men have been on a killing spree so to speak in the GW. Assaulting World Champion Rokku Akodo, International Champion Jesse Jamester, Jin Akiyara, Ice, Leinad Soma, Max Roswell, and The Souls of the Black Lotus, as well as plenty others. So the questions have been asked Just who in the Hell are they!? I have asked this and have gone about looking at who these men might in-fact be. So lets take a look at the likely suspects. Could be a blast from GW past? There devastating assaults are reminiscent of long time Dominant GW tag team True Evil. Could these two men be Salaok and Quartz returned to rage war on GW? Could be But there are other suspects. Could it be the Tag champions there self? John Zero and Deacon Holmes have been known to play tricks on people countless times. But if it is them, the question has to be: Why assault there own TUE members? There are even more possibilities. The Canadian Icons. It is a known fact that Both Chris Camero and Jean Claude Dafoe have been seen in and around the Global Arena. Could this top tag team be making resurgence into GW its self? Then another possibility is that this could be two rising stars in GW, Maybe the likes of Terrell Green and Joe Giovanni? But Me I truly being that this is in-fact none other then two men returning to GW. Both Men before being sidelined with injuries made a impact into GW. I am talking about Kid Flash and Ken Partridge Jr. Take a look at who is attacked. High Profile names in GW. I am betting it is indeed Flash and Partridge that are returning to GW and making there presence felt as a dominate tag team.. And if so it is a tag team that can and will give the Midnight Sons a run for their money! Best be sure, I will be watching the tag ranks closely. So should you. Alexis Steel signing off!
