KPJ's Retirement, Autobiography, Film Projects, and more

22 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
During a recent Q&A with Ken Partridge, Jr. he was asked what he plans on doing now that he's officially retired from wrestling. The following is Ken's response to that subject. "I've already stated that I'll have my official retirement match at GLOBAL's WrestleCade VII. As far as who the opponent will be, while I have some ideas, it's still a mystery. As far as what I'm going to do now with my life, there a quite a few things I'm going to enjoy. Gunner and I are set to be married and have engraved the date in stone for Saturday, June 10th. I'm looking forward to enjoying being the husband that my beuatiful future-wife deserves. I'm also going to continue acting. I've already been contacting by Michael Grief about reprising my role as Roger in the Broadway musical RENT for an extended period of time beginning in August and I'm currently working on a script with Jack Black. I've still got to finish my third Superman installment and I've just finished the re-make of The Towering Inferno. I've been contacted by a few other Hollywood execs, but chances are, after the "Wonderboy" project with Jack Black, I'll lay low for a while and stay out of the public eye for at least a year. Gunner and I are also really looking forward to starting a family. To squash any rumors before the start, no, she's not expecting. The closest we are right now are or two dogs, Sadie and Blade. I'm also planning on finsihing two books that I've been writing now for the past three years, especially with my autobiography, "Life in the Spotlight," hitting stores in June. As far as any other wrestling commitments, I truly believe I'm finished with all in ring competition after WrestleCade. I've considered roles as Road Agent, Talent Advisor, and even Chairman of the Board of Directors. We'll see what pans out with those in the future. Thank you, and every other fan out there... not just KPJ fans, but all wrestling fans, for allowing me to live my dream. God Bless"
