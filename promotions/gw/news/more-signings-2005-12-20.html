More Signings....

20 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the theme that's been going on in the past few days... Bubba McCoy has signed a few more people to the roster... Eryc Morgan [ Formally known as Corvas] and his Brother Ash Morgan: have both resigned to Global for 2006.. A real solid and powerful addition to the roster. Shane Weathers: Shane is a Vet of Many, Most notably PCW and Eternal Wrestling. Shane has been almost signed for the past few years... But things fell though... Now Shane has signed to the federation for 2006. Michael Cervantes: This former Freedom fighter has now Joined the ranks of Global... Not much is known about his past but he does have a connection to the past of Global.. what could it be? Justice Inc. Blind Rage and Warrior: This team has been in few federations and is a Nice addition to the growing Tag Team Roster.... 2006 is defiantly going to be on fire for this division... Jackson Namenlos-Duisturnis: Jackson has resigned to the federation and will be returning to the Octagon in January... No word if He will be part of the ReLoad Card as of yet. Still the two other black masked men who have been talking to Bubba McCoy are not revealed... Bubba McCoy still wont answer my questions as to who they are... He just Smiles. But did have this to say. "You tell the people who are preaching Gloom and Doom for this Federation. They better check there heads and open there eyes Cause Globals about to set the world on fire!" With that..... I am Alexis Steel Signing off.
