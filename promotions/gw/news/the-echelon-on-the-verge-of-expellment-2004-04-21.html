The Echelon - on the verge of expellment?

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is Global Wrestling's Tyler Chambers. After tonight's airing of Evolution, I was witness to a scene backstage. I was standing outside of the VIP room entrance, awaiting a chance to speak to the new World Tag Team Champions when what should I see but the TUE flank guards coming around the bin. I readied myself with questions when I heard Maverick Reno's voice. He was saying something about, "begging for mercy" and that the Echelon never "begs" for anything. He came around the corner finally and I noticed that Zero was at his side. He had gotten his strength back and was walking on his own. Deacon Holmes stood in the center andwaria Xiou was a step or so behind them. He was talking about the tag match though and the way Draven had ran from Jushin Nakazura. I managed to get a few words on tape. "I'm going to talk with him, John. Something needs to be straightened out! We can't have members of our inner sanctum running out there and acting like that... like a fucking embocile infront of millions! It discredits us... what the three of us have built! Draven needs to learn that The Echelon is not a institution that accepts weakness." The words came straight from Maverick's mouth. Before they saw me and stopped, one last thing was said by John Zero. "A chain is only as strong as it's weakest link, Maverick. But what about the..." that's when they saw me. I didn't have a chance to ask any questions, the guards ran me off because of the ease dropping. I'm trying to get further details.
