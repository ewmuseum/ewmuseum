GW Champion to be a Movie Star?

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Word has it that Brick is looking to audition for the role of Marvel Comics superhero Luke Cage, Hero for Hire, in a film currently in development at Columbia Pictures. Luke Cage was once was a gang member, growing up surrounded by crime. At one point, he found himself framed for a crime he did not commit. While in prison, some scientists offered Luke his freedom if he would agree to go through with medical experiments. These experiments not only gave him his freedom, but they also gave him superhuman strength. From there, Luke went into the world to become a mercenary. He became a man who would fight crime, but only for a price. Cage was previously in development at New Line with John Singleton attached to direct, and then later director Hype Williams and star LL Cool J. Cage will be produced by Marvel Studios' honcho Avi Arad and Original Films' Neal Moritz (2 Fast,2 Furious). Curently no word has come from Columbia Pictures about Brick being any part of this film and Brick has been unreachable for comments. More on this as we get information...
