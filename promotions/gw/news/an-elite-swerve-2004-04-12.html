An Elite swerve?

12 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumors have been circulating that World Champion Ice and challenger "American Youth" Josh have something up their respective sleeves. Ice and Josh have a storied friendship and rivalry and are slated to face off at Wrestlecade IV. However inside sources have told us that the match may never take place. The popular rumor backstage is that American Youth and Ice will rip the contract up in attempt to "screw" Christina McCoy for making them play by her set of rules. There is no proof to back up this unsubstaniated rumor but it allegedly came from an unknown source in the Global Wrestling locker room. A lower card Wrestler who will remain anonymous for his own safety, sold this rumor to GW color commentator Mark Bishop for 4 dollars, 32 cents and a pog slammer, which totalled Bishop's entire assets after losing a hefty bet to GW's newest tag team. This breaking story has yet to be confirmed and the contract signing is still scheduled to take place on Evolution, we will update you with more news on this possible disaster if it becomes available. -Noi Tsunami, reporting.
