Jamester Missing?

3 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello this is Tyler Preston, new Global Wrestling Reporter. We've got a juicy rumor here on our hands and need the fans to help us in our search for fact and fiction. First off, after the Global Wrestling Pay-Per-View 'Global Reload' Jesse Jamester was seen entering his dressing room. As the media waited they had been suspicious of what he was doing. So about an hour after the show, the door was opened by an ally of Jamester, Jesse Briggs, and to no avail, Jesse was no where to be found. Cameras took pictures of the place but in the end, only one thing was left in the room as a reminder and absolute postive knowing that Jamester was there; his wrestling gear. Blood was found near the sink, but that would be apparent since Jamester did begin to bleed after Akodo Rokku's finishing move The Emperor's Right Hand'. Global Wrestling is now issuing a reward to anyone who can give news on Jesse Jamester's where-abouts at this time or if Jamester is okay. Thank you, Tyler Preston (Newest GW Reporter)
