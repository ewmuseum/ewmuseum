More New stars set to Debut on Evolution?

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---- The McCoy kids have been fast at work.. Christina and Donovan McCoy have had a flood of talent though there offices the last for weeks.. I been told if I leak out names I will be fired. But what I can tell you is this, A former GW World champion, A former GW Tag team Champion, A former PCW Star, A former Vintage Wrestling Star, Some hot new talents, A few Clowns and a Pair of Kangaroos have all entered the offices the last few days... When I talked to Christina McCoy about some of these Talents. She said the kangaroos were not hired... But wished them well in there efforts in other federations. But did say that several new stars would indeed be debuting this Evolution. Who will show up? Who will make an impact on GW? Watch and see.... Brandi Sights Reporting.
