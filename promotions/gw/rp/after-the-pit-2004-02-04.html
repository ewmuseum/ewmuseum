After the Pit

4 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/4/04 The moment was still fresh in Brick’s mind. His match in the Pit with Terminator had just ended with Brick gaining the victory. Almost right away, Terminator was back on his feet and in Brick’s face. Then, suddenly he stepped back and held out his hand to Brick. Brick looked Terminator up and readied himself, putting his hand out as well. Terminator then shook Brick’s hand and raised his arm high up. As Terminator then walked out of the Pit, he left Brick standing in the center of the Pit, confused. Brick had barely begun to process this new development in his mind when the lights in the arena start to dim. The Globaltron starts to flash different images. Images of Hitler and other people of that caliber appeared. Then came a line of people dressed in identical suits. And then, “I Stand Alone” started to play over the arena’s sound system as people in the crowd started to boo the music. It was Mark Makonie’s entrance theme. The Evolution before Reload, Brick and Makonie had gotten into a confrontation concerning Makonie’s definition of perfection. It was spurred by an offhand comment Brick had heard from Makonie. The fight ended with Brick throwing Makonie out a second floor window onto a car. Brick stands in the middle of the ring looking around waiting for the perfectionist himself to arrive. But the jumbo tron flickers off real quick as it doesn’t show any more of the images. The arena is dark as the ramp way has fire going down the sides, one of the trademarks of Mark Makonie’s entrance. Then Mark Makonie’s face shows up on the Globaltron. “So, Brick, you probably thought that I was going to come down to the ring, didn’t you” But I just thought that I would wait until you rest up because I know that you just had a tiresome match. I want to be fair. I want to make sure I get you when you’re fresh. I want it to be just perfect.” Ever so often, Makonie paused to take a drink. “That’s right, Brick. I am perfect, unlike you. Just look at your skin color, you coon. That sets you off to be imperfect in the start. You should just hop on your little boat and head back over to Africa where you belong. Next thing is that why do you have so much hatred towards me, Brick” I’m the new guy that comes into GW and you throw me out of a window the first Evolution that I’m at. You gave me something that I will never forget and that is this.” Makonie then removed the bandage on his head to reveal the deep cut from the window Brick had thrown him out. “This is what I get from you, Brick, for no reason. Well, there has to be an explanation out there doesn’t there” So, Brick, I’m waiting. Well, I have one for you, Brick. I have been thinking all day and drinking, that is. After all of this, I have devised a simple explanation. Say that I’m the new guy coming into this fed and you’re the veteran looking for the old spotlight that you used to have when you were at the pinnacle of your career. Now, you’re afraid that someone as fit and as perfect as me will take over your spotlight so you might as well destroy the chance that they have at any stardom in GW. Brick, you don’t rule this fed anymore. It has gone on to bigger and better things and one of those was recruiting me here.” Again, Makonie paused to take a drink. “So, Brick, I remember what you did to be on Evolution. Just wait, Brick we will tango sooner or later and for now I just hope that you don’t do anything that I don’t approve of. So, in other words, you have to be PERFECT!” As Brick walked out of the Pit, he pondered Makonie’s words. This wasn’t the first time he’d had to deal with someone making bigoted comments about him. In 2002, he’d had to deal with Brian Simon. On numerous occasions, Simon insinuated that the only reason Brick had made it as far as he did was because of his race and politics. It wasn’t quite as virulent as Makonie’s comments, but close enough. Now, two years later, Brick had to deal with the same garbage. “Brick! Brick!” Brick turned his head to see Christina McCoy coming towards him. “Brick, I just saw what happened. How are you doing?” “You think this is the first time I’ve heard that kind of crap” I ain’t na’ve.” “I just want to make sure you don’t go looking for trouble. I mean you just got out of the Pit.” “He wants to talk about how I singled him out because he’s the new guy” That’s bullshit! I singled him out because he’s a racist piece of shit!” “Look, Brick, calm down.” “Why did you even hire that motherfucker?” “I don’t know what a bigoted asshole he was when I hired him. I hired him for his talent, same as everybody else here. Of course, I will deal with him if I have to.” “Good. You do that.” Brick then walked away to ponder his next move. His feud with Terminator may have been over, but his stress was just beginning.
