The Life: The story of Ami Mizuno (Volume 5)

4 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-04-05 Evening Come one and come all to the show that never ends. Come see the amazing female sensation in the GW known as Mizuno Ami! Ok so that was a little far out but funny as hell wasn’t it? Its about 8:45 in the evening in New York City on a Friday Night and New York City is rocking with delight as another weekend has come and this weekend there is no such thing as snow in the forecast! YAY! Tonight is a special night for one person in particular that lives in the house on the hill just barely in the the city limits of New York City named Ami Mizuno and that is because tonight is the first real date that she is going on with Michiru Kaiou! Now a lot of you may be wondering *Haven’t these two gone out before?* and the answer would be yes ,but not by themselves! This is the first time that Ami is going out with Michiruy on a date without anyone watching over them and making sure that they stay out of trouble but I guess the better question would be why would Ami or Michiru need one? Both girls are over 18 and know about safety and the wonders of Mace so they should be ok. Asd the two get set to leave the house in Joshua’s 1996 Dodge Viper Ami walks over to the passenger side door to let her girlfriend in before walking to the other side to get in and get ready to pull out. As the two pull out of the residence a shilouetted figure upstairs watches them pull out before he turns toward the bed in room to address another individual. “Well Zack” Joshua began “my little sister has officially grown up and is going on her first date. I hope that she will be ok and knows what to do if she gets into trouble.” Joshua said somewhat worried for his younger sister. “Don’t worry hun” Zack said lying under the covers on his side of the bed “Ami will be ok...after all you taught her well in self-defense so everything should be ok right?” Zack asked and Joshua nodded before moving to his side of the bed and taking his spot next to his lover. As Ami skillfully drove the car through the streets to Morimoto’s dressed in her crisp blue suit with a white shirt and tie she turned and admired her girlfriend whom was in awe of her driving! Ami and Michiru had been a couple for about a little under 6 months and Ami knew that she made the right decision by asking Michiru to go steady with her. It wasn’t too long before they started dating that Haruka had decided that Michiru wasn’t the one for her and they split amicably but Ami soon made Michiru forget about the breakup a week or so later and the two have been an item in the world of wrestling arguably more so than Curtis and Zack have. Why? Well it would seem that Ami attracts somewhat of a younger audience with wrestling fans and is trying her hardest to get the right blend of fans to come to GW shows despite how some of the matches get extremely violent and maybe suggestive at times but the Connection believed that they needed to help both young and older generations appreciate wrestling and that is what the purposed of them going out and doing things such as this were. Yeah Ami was on a date with Michiru but even Michiru could appreciate Ami taking the time to stop and talk to the fans and sign autographs and that is something that is truly a lost art in this industry nowadays and you can make the ulitmate argument that in GW its really lost! As Ami and Michiru pulled into the parking lot Michiru checked her makeup in the mirror and as they got out of the car she made sure that her dress was straightened out properly before they made their way into the restaurant and Ami made her way to the hostess in charge. “2 for Mizuno.” Ami said to the hostes and she directed them to the table that they were to be sitting at. Now for anyone that has never been to a Japanese restaurant its not like you sit at a table by yourself...oh no you sit at a table with like 10, 20 or 30 people and you have to be careful cos the chef gets tricky and tries to flip food into your mouth that you ordered! When you get your food you are typically with a lot of people so conversation happens aplenty and that was so the case on this night with Ami and Michiru-san. “Excuse me miss” A young man started that was sittling next to Ami dressed in a “Connection” T-shirt, a pair of Wrangler Blue Jeans and black Timberlands “are you the famous wrestler Ami Mizuno by any chance?” the young man asked with hearts in his eyes. “Why yes I am yong one. I am the GW wrestler known as the “Ice Princess” Mizuno Ami but you are right to just call me Ami because all of the fans do.” Ami explained to the kid before continuing “What may I help you with tonight?” The youngest member of the Connection asked the young man as she saw the smile on his face. “Would you mind signing something for me? I want to get an autograph for me and my father.” The boy asked and Ami complied as the father watched how she signed it and smiled when he saw her draw the heart around the signature. Was it uncommon for fathers or mothers to ask for an autograph from Ami or any member of the Connection? No but that was because of the accessibility of the group. There are very few groups that purposely make themselves accessible to the fans before, during and after shows but that is only because of the fact that they are true “fan favorites”! PWI(Pro Wrestling Illustrated) once did a story on Curtis and his faction when it was just a small faction in the IWF and every time that they group came to the States or anywhere worldwide fans lined the streets to watch for the famed Viper pull in and the trio of Curtis, Johnson and Ami would draw “OSC” chants from fans so rabid that there were occasions that fans were being restrained from geting anywhere near them but that goes to show the popularity of “alternate lifestyles” outside of the US until now. Now people in America are becoming accepting of people like Ami, Michiru, Joshua and Zack and the reason is because people are starting to see that not all gays or lesbians are bad people but when you get brainwashed by those whom really make you believe that then your judgment getst clouded. As the night wore on Ami ended up signing about 20 or 30 different autographs and believe it or not so did Michiru because people saw her debut in Japan about a month ago ,which she won by the way, and people fell in love with her. Is it a surprise? Not really but mainly because of the fact that here was a faction so popular amongst a worldwide audience that them leaving or disappearing was very much unlikely. A few hours later after they had their dinner Ami was asked by a fan about the prospect of pulling double duty at the next GW PPV and she played it off aas she didn’t know what was going on but the fact remained that she knew and wasn’t going to tell but that everyone had to watch the PPV on Sunday to see what was going to happen and thus that is where we will leave it for tonight! Au Revoir Darlings ;)
