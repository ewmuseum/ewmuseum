Worst nightmare

28 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/28/05 [It's Saturday night, around 11:45 pm. Inside the club, the dance floor is littered with people shaking and gyrating to 'One Thing' by Amerie. The beats are thumping so loud to shake the entire building.] Oh, been trying to let it go Trying to keep my eyes closed Trying to keep it just like before The times we never even thought to speak Don't wanna tell you what it is Oh wee it felt so serious Got me thinking just too much I wanna set it off, but [Train walks into the club, decked out in a set of black slacks, baby blue button up and a black leather jacket.] It's this 1 thing that's got me trippin It's this 1 thing that's got me trippin (you did) This 1 thing my soul may be feeling It's this 1 thing you did oh oh It's this 1 thing that caught me slippin It's this 1 thing I want to admit it This 1 thing and I was so with it It's this 1 thing you did oh oh [As Train makes his way through the throng, he notices a woman standing at the bar. He can only see her from behind, but he likes what he sees.] Hey, we don't know each other well So what? I keep thinking to myself Memories just keep ringing bells Oh oh, Oh oh, Oh Train: DAMN!!! [Train walks right up to her and leans closer.] Hear voices I don't want to understand My car keys are jingling in my hand My heart feels a clicking towards your door Oh oh, Oh oh, Oh Train: You know, girl, I noticed from all the way on the other side o' the room an' I knew I just had to come over to talk to you. So what's up? It's this 1 thing that's got me trippin It's this 1 thing that's got me trippin (you did) This 1 thing my soul may be feeling [The woman turns around and, to Train's chagrin...she bears a strong resmeblance to Brick.] 'Brickina': Ooh, you cute! Let's dance! Train: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!! ---- [Train awakes in his bed with a sudden start.] Train: AAAH!! What the fuck was that?!!! [Sitting up in his bed, heavily panting and in a cold sweat, Train turns to his left to notice someone in bed with him.] Train: Huh? [The person turns over to reveal...] 'Brickina': Oh, you want some more o' this, don't you, daddy? Train: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!! ---- [Brick awakes in his bed with a sudden start.] Brick: AAAAAHHH!!! AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!! [Brick sits up in his bed, in a cold sweat, heavily panting, his eyes wide. He quickly turns around to see if anyone is in the bed with him. He's alone. He then breathes a sigh of relief.] Neighbor's Voice: Hey, Randall, shut the hell up!! Some of us have to get up in the morning! Brick: Why am I dreamin' about Train and bein' Wanda from 'In Living Color'? This is all cause o' that little bastard Deacon Holmes! Him and that shit he pulled with Club Fantasia! I'ma fuck his ass if I ever see him again! [Brick lies back down and, amazingly, manages to go back to sleep.]
