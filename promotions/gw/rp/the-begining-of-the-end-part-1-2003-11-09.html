The Begining of The End ( part 1 )

9 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/9/03 ' Don't you fucking walk away from me!!' I screamed, at her as she walked out of the room and slammed the door shut in my face. My head dropped in defeat as my desperate pleas for her not to go fell upon deaf ears. A cold chill consumed me as I turned, and slowly drug myself back to our room. As I made my way back to our room, many thoughts ran wild throughtout my head. Many thoughts, which were not good to be thinking of at a time like this. I finally drug myself into our room, and seated myself on the end of our bed. As I sat there my head fell into my heads. My palms covered my eyes, hoping for the darkness to clear my head. For a moment there was darkness, but that darkness was quickly replaced with colorful, vivid images, of the horrible thoughts that were racing throught my mind. As the pictures played out in my head, I softly whispered my concurring thoughts aloud. ' Where are you going my love? Why did you leave? Please don't be where I believe you to be. Please don't be doing what I believe you to be doing.' My hands slid up my face and throught my hair, before they stopped, cupping the back of my neck. My head lifted, and the force broke the claps of my hands. Slowly I leaned my head all the way back and stared into the endless abyss of white that consumed our ceiling. It was then my mind went blank, and my tension subsided. As my mind cleared, I involuntarily stood up, and headed for the door. I grabbed my coat and walked out the door. I walked and I walked, almost as if I couldn't stop myself, even if I wanted to. My trance-like state was abruptly ended as a man flying through a large glass window caught my attention. I looked down at the man, covered in blood and blood-stained glass, but the my ear caught something. 'Lady, we don't want any trouble. Just gather your things, and leave, and we won't call the police.' With those words, my head snapped towards the open space, that was a window moments before. I turned and made my way into the small tavern. I pushed the door open, and walked into the musky room, to see the bartender on his knees tending to a lady, who was bleeding. I took another step forward, to get a better view of the situation. I went to take another step, but stopped as the door to the women's restroom flew open. I smirked as I saw who walked out. I had a gut feeling this would be the person causing such a ruckus, but to actually see it, it made me snicker softly to myself. But that snickering was short as she saw me. 'WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING HERE! JESUS CHRIST, YOU'RE LIKE A FUCKING PUPPY DOG FOLLOWING ME AROUND LIKE A LITTLE BITCH! JUST LEAVE ME THE FUCK ALONE! LEAVE ME THE FUCK ALONE!' I was taken back by her rage towards me, and didn't know how to respond. She had never acted this way, especially not towards me. I felt hurt and confused and I'm sure it showed. Again my mind was racing, racing with emotion, and thoughts for a possible response to her. But, before I could get a response out, she angrily walked up to me, and violently slapped me across my left cheek. 'IT'S YOUR FAULT! YOUR FAULT! IF YOU DIDN'T HAVE TO BE SUCH A MUSCLE BOUND BARBARIAN! IF YOU HAD A NORMAL JOB! WE COULD BE A HAPPY FAMILY WITH A BEAUTIFUL BABY RIGHT NOW! BUT NO! BECAUSE OF YOU, AND YOUR STUPID FUCKING JOB THAT CAN'T HAPPEN NOW! I HATE YOUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUU!' Again she slapped me across the face, only this time with much more conviction behind it, before staggering out of the tavern. My eyes grew wide as I watched her walk out of the tavern. My body was frozen, unable to move, and my mind was in complete shock. I was in shock from seeing the carnage this petite woman, that didn't look like she could, or would hurt a fly had committed. I was in shock from being slapped in the face not once, but TWICE! But most importantly, I was in shock, because the woman I loved, with all of my heart, all of my mind, all of my soul, and all that I am, blamed me for the death of our child, and hated me for it. That thought, her words, they echoed inside my head over and over and over, as I stumbled back to the nearest barstool and took a seat. Just then my cell phone began to ring in my pocket. 'Hello? Lara? What's the matter?' 'Oh my GOD! I just got a call! I'll be there right away!' 'Lara! Slow down, what the hell are you talking about?' 'WHAT DO YOU MEAN WHAT THE HELL AM I TALKING ABOUT! Star was just in an accident!' 'WHAT! When?! What hospital did they take her to? Why wasn't I called?!' 'You weren't called?......' 'NO! I WASN'T!' 'She's at Memorial, I'll see you there later tonight.' I hung up my cell phone, and everything around me was a blur. Not again. It was too soon after the last time, I couldn't handle another being taken away. I couldn't handle HER being taken away. I opened my cell, and began to dial. 'Hello?' 'Hey, I know it's been awhile, since we have talked, but I could really use a friend and was wondering if you wouldn't mind being that friend.' 'OF COURSE!!! What's the matter, you sound upset.' I paused, trying to hold back my emotions, before answering. 'I'll explain later, I was just wondering if you could pick me up at the hotel.' She agreed, and I headed back for the hotel. It was a short walk in actual time, but to me, the way I was thinking, the way I was feeling, the walk felt like it took weeks. I got to the hotel just as the car pulled out. She rushed out of the car, and could immediately see that something was not right. 'What happened?' 'S...Star......was in a......an acc....accci....accident.' She gasped, and tightly wrapped her arms around me. I choked back my emotions, and drifted away into her embrace. The warmth of her body, the smell of her perfume, and the care behind her embrace eased me as we relinquished our hug. She shot me a smile of reassurance, that everything would be ok, and then reached for my hand. She took my hand into hers and led me to the car, before entering the driver's side herself. She started the vehicle up, but paused before taking off. She turned and looked at me, and then took my hand once again. 'Everything is going to be just fine.' My eyes swelled up with tears, but I fought them back, and meekly smiled at her. Keeping her grip of my hand, she put the car into drive, and we sped off to the hospital.
