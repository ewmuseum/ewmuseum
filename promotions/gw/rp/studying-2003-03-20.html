Studying

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20/03 Brick rushed as fast as he could to get to his VCR. Someone in GW’s Media department had given him a copy of the recent edition Of GW Overload. It was especially interesting to Brick, because of one match in particular. Global Wrestling’s current World Champion Ice took on Fnord of 4WW in an interpromotional PTC Extreme Challenge. Brick needed this bit of video to study possible weaknesses that he might use on the next Evolution when he faces Ice for the World title. 4WWs’ West Wind Champ Fnord appears as “Step Up” by Drowning Pool blares. The champ struts down to the ring as the fans are a little unsure of what to do but still gets a mixed reaction from some of the ringside fans. Fnord makes his way to the ring and gets into the ring and holds his title proudly over his head. Steele: Well Fnord looks like he primed and ready for this match. He is proud of his 4WW roots! Bishop: As well he should but he is facing an uphill battle against the best Global Wrestling has to offer as he is facing the World Champion in Ice. Steele: This should be one hell of a match! And this is a No DQ/Weapons match this will be brutal as there are weapons scattered about the ring. I can see a ladder, police nightsticks, and a whole trash bin filled with stuff! This is gonna be madness! Bishop: I agree! Fnord hands his title to the ref and looks around the ring at all the weapons as the lights dim slightly and “Haunted” by Evanescence blares and the GW World champ appears at the top of the ramp. He looks around at the crowd. The camera pans to the crowd and a sign that read “Ice is God” and another that reads “PTC fears the Ice-Age!” Ice makes is way down to the ring and climbs in and eyes Fnord and then holds his title high above his head. But before the match starts Eat You Alive by Limp Bizkit blares and Noah Hanson from WMW appears at the top of the ramp with his Ace Superior title over his shoulder. He makes his way to the ringside area and takes a seat next to the announcers. Ice and Fnord eye Noah who is now putting his headset on. Suddenly, the phone began to ring. “Hello? Hey, Aaron. Yeah, I’m just watching a video. Yeah, it’s of Ice’s last match. Yeah, I’m gonna study it. You think I can afford not to? OK, I gotta go. I’ll call you later.” Brick then hung up the phone and resumed watching the video. Ice stands up and pumps his fist in the air and turns and is met with a vicious clothesline from Fnord that drops Ice to the mat. Fnord then follows it up with a short running leg drop. Fnord rolls outside and looks through the trash bin and pulls out a hockey stick. He rolls back in and smashes the stick over the GW World champs back. Ice grimaces in pain and as he turns around Fnord breaks the hockey stick over Ices’ head! Ice looks like he could be busted open already. The ref checks on the wound and Fnord pushes him out of the way as he goes to pick up Ice the GW champ with a thumb to the throat that has Fnord gasping for air. Fnord staggers and Ice grabs him and hits a beautiful textbook belly-to belly suplex. Ice walks over to the ropes and wipes the blood from his face and rolls outside and grabs a chair and slides it inside and sets it up in the middle of the ring. Ice grabs Fnord and whips him into the ropes and on the rebound he catches him in a spine buster on the chair. Fnord is rolling on the mat in pain! Noah: Wow what a match already this is really a great match so far! Steele: Well Ice is the best we have to offer! And from what I understand you love hardcore type matches? Noah: Very much so this is my kinda match! Bishop: Well this is a primetime match one that could headline any ppv! And we have it here on Overload! Ice picks up the chair and folds it up and places it in the middle of the ring. Ice picks up Fnord and whips him into the ropes and catches him with a powerslam on the chair. Ice covers Fnord and the ref with the count 1…2...no Fnord kicks out! Fnord crawls to the ropes and pulls himself up as Ice stalks him. Fnord grabs his back in pain and floors Ice with a series if lefts and then rights but is slow to follow up as his back is still bothering him. Fnord picks up the chair and nails Ice over the head with it. Fnord wails on Ices’ back and Ice falls through the ropes and into the bin full of weapons. Fnord goes to the outside and looks in the bin and is met with a jar on his head the glass shatters and whatever was inside is all over the face and in the hair of Fnord. Fnord is bleeding from the head from the glass jar. Ice tosses out a bunch of things: a Sega Dreamcast, a boom box, a karaoke machine, a plastic baseball bat, an old wooden guitar. Ice climbs out and Fnord is waiting and he grabs someone’s drink from ringside and throws in the face of Ice. Fnord grabs the guitar and acts like he is playing it then bashes Ice over the head with it. The guitar is still around the head of Ice as the crowd cheers wildly on both men! Noah: Damn this is great I love it! Hell maybe they will take each other out so I can move up more! Steele: Well Ice is the best GW has to offer and he is a two time World champ. I dunno a whole lot about Fnord except for the fact that he is a champ in 4WW. Noah: He is the West Wind Champ, which is the equivalent to a hardcore champ. So this type of match should be nothing unusual to him! Bishop: Well at least we know Noah has done his homework and is well versed on the combatants in the ring! [Ice is still stumbling around the ringside area. He pulls at the guitar to try and get it off his head. He finally does and just in time to duck being hit with a Singapore cane. Ice kicks Fnord in the gut and nails a DDT on the floor that drives the 4WW star into the floor and his body snaps over hitting the trash bin! Ice again wipes the blood from his face and looks around grabbing the Dreamcast and as Fnord gets up Ice hits Fnord in the back and then over the head with it. Both men are bloody messes now and the blood is getting to a messy thing now. Fnord is stumbling to get to his feet as the Global World Champ is on the attack. Ice grabs a cord from the Dreamcast and uses it to choke Fnord. Fnord is down to one knee and Ice plants an elbow across the bridge of Fnords’ nose causing it to gush with blood. Ice pulls Fnord up and lifts him up for a tilt a whirl backbreaker. Fnord writhes in pain on floor as Ice looks around for something to use and sees a plastic baseball bat. Ice picks it up and starts hammering Fnord with it. It leaves many red marks and little else as Ice tosses it down. Ice goes to grab Fnord but Fnord hits Ice with a low blow. Noah: Ha it’s all good no DQ! Steele: Well I guess there is gonna be no Ice cubes running around now! Bishop: Well that could be a good thing! Ice doubles over in pain and is grabbing his ice cubes as Fnord stumbles and picks up a trash can and hits Ice with it then places it on top of Ices’ head and does a corkscrew leg drop! Ice kicks his feet as he is in pain from the move. Fnord tosses the can aside and looks around the ring as the pro GW crowd starts booing Fnord! Fnord gives the fans a dirty look as he pulls Ice up by the hair and pulls Ice towards the ropes and rubs Ices eyes across the ring ropes! Ice struggles in pain. Ice swings’ wildly hitting nothing but Fnord grabs Ice and chokes him against the ring ropes. The ref warns Fnord but in reality can do nothing about it as Fnord laughs at the ref and pushes him away! Noah: This match is starting to take its toll on both guys as they are starting to show a little fatigue! Bishop: Well this match is not an easy match for anyone! Steele: This match is just plain brutal! Ice finally gets to his feet but is met with a hard savant kick right to the face of Ice. Ice is slow to move as Fnord looks around and goes outside and pushes the ladder in the ring. Fnord sets the ladder up in one corner and then grabs the trashcan from earlier and climbs to the top rope with the can then to the top of the ladder. He launches off the ladder with the can under him but Ice moves at the last minute and Fnord crashes to the mat with a sickening thud. Bishop: My god these two men going to whatever links to gain the upper hand in this match up! Putting their very careers on the line here tonight! Noah: They are simply doing whatever they have to do get an edge any edge in this match and in the PTC! You gotta do whatever it takes to survive! Steele: Nicely put Noah! I had heard many things most mixed but I must say I am pleasantly surprised by your professionalism tonight! The match continues with both men struggling to get to their feet both bloodied from head to toe! Ice is the first to his feet and grabs Fnord and kicks him in the knee and then the gut then picks him up on his shoulders and hits a DVD on the trashcan. Ice gets up and looks around and sees the ladder and looks at the motionless Fnord and climbs to the top rope and rides the ladder onto the prone body of Fnord. Ice rolls across the ring and into the trash bin and the lid slams down on Ices’ head because of the force of impact. The ref looks around and checks on Fnord who is barely moving and then the lid of the trash bin snaps open and Ice stands up with a banana peel on his head. Ice looks up and removes the peel in disgust and rolls back into the ring! Ice sees that Fnord is still under the ladder and splashes the ladder and bounces off the ladder and then grabs his stomach in pain. Fnord slowly pushes the ladder off and rolls out from it and places an arm over the chest of Ice! The ref looks and makes the count 1…2... And Ice kicks out. Fnord is to week to get any offense going but crawls to the ropes and pulls himself and Ice is doing the same. They both make it to their feet and look exhausted the crowd gives both men a standing ovation. Noah: They deserve it this a hell of a match! Bishop: I agree these two have spilled a lot of blood in this match! Ice I expect that from but Fnord I had no idea he was such a tough son of a bitch! Steele: Yeah this has been a hell of a match! Fnord and Ice meet in the middle of the ring and start slugging it out with Ice getting the upper hand and flooring Fnord and then stomping away at Fnord until he is one the canvas slumped against the bottom turnbuckle. Ice pulls him up by the hair and gives Fnord a series of European uppercuts that stagger the 4WW superstar. Fnord stumbles into Ice who spins him around into a spine buster onto the ladder. Ice looks to the crowd and decides to go to the top and he nails The Blizzard and makes the pin! The ref makes the cover 1…2…3 Noah: Wow what a move to end a great and hard fought match! Bishop: Fnord took Ice to the limit and nearly pulled it out what a match! Steele: I see great things from Fnord! Bishop: What a match! Ice proved he was the better man and came out on top for GW! Steele: Yea, but Fnord put up one hell of a fight himself. But Mark, how will that grueling match effect Ice's title defense against former GW World champion Brick. Bishop: I don't think it will, but we'll have to wait and see this Monday on Evolution.... Steele: Well we're out of time! Thanks for joining us, and we'll see you on Monday for Evolution...GOOD NIGHT! The GW logo flashes. Just then Brick pauses the machine. “Competing in a hardcore match days before you have to defend the World title? I know Ice is crazy, but he can’t be stupid.” Brick could not afford to underestimate Ice. He was, after all, the World champion and quite capable of anything. But then again, so is Brick.
