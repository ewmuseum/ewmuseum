Like i was going numb..One seconed at a time. I felt the icy black hand of death touch me..Well, Grabe me. No, I was in no life threatning accident..My contract was up for re-assingment and i diddent know if a deal could be reached for a 27 year old,Bang up,Latino guy like me.. So they thought about it and i guess this subject at hand was more of a test then anything. They saw an opening were appretly my talents could be used and i was again signed. Then i took a look at my match, and i began seconed-guessing my own talents. Yes the ones that you've seen before, The talents that For a Very short period of time. Made me a Cruiserweight Champion but as i said. I said to myself..Could i do it..Agnist the elite. So let it be written, so let it be done. It's more like a Blood bet now. Right now, I would bet my right hand that Myself and Destruction Inc, Will win this match. Mr. K.C. and Mr. Michaels. Both seem to be Stout men. I hope that they can keep up with me in the ring, but that is another story for another time.)) 'And the Story at hand begins with Ice,Ace harding,KJP,and American Youth Josh, Apprently they think them selves to be some sort of super power. More or less, Two heads Tall. You know. But as i see it Three people Vs. Three people that isnt much more of a good old fasion beat down, At a slow pace. Something Latinos love. A good ol' slug fest. With some Tequila. And boys...I'm hopeing that you'll be able to stand up with me in those catarories, I'm a lightweight, Just as KJP. To my immediant knowing, we are the only two, So after the Bigger guys have slowen down. Myself and KJP will still be freasher then the rest of you. so in the wrestling we are very fairly matched..and aduie, in this i have a good chance to lose, which is not what i want to do' So what do i do aye? I cant lose this match. So how to i go about it. with reckless abandoment and try to end it quickly. Or do i take my sweet, sweet time and think the match through and play chess with my opponet's..So the Elite is alaready begin called a cancer. The groupe has not been around for two weeks and alaready we are judging it as a Never more or TUE. Tis true though. Almost any groupe that gains a small bit of power become narrsistic and trys to take over the company. Almost killing it in the process. Each time, slowly but surley GW begins to rise again, some times it's attacked while it's down, and went down fighting we did.only to rise again, and we will continue to rise, Everything has it's end, But GW will be around for a very long time. It guys like me carry the GW banner proudly into the next day people will see. 'No, GW will not die. Not by my watch. And the Mind that is leading the group that i presume is ICE and we all know he threw in his lot with TUE but apprently the TUE did pretty good becuse i see that Mr. Deacon Holmes has mananged to get three belts around his waist and a Congradulations to Mr. John Zero who hold Two belts, Platnuim and Tag. But the even bigger Who-Raas...The International title belongs to a Mr. Ice and the World to Akodo Rokku. What an impressive groupe of champions..Even better is the list of former champions. Derek Draven,Salaok,Hans Kruger,Chris Camreo. All of these men have held Global wrestling title belts and there will be many more who will hold GW titles well into the years of my death.' And so the world turns, Live it. Love it. Hate it. God knows what you do but i know that as the world turns we all have desiscions that we have to make and well all make bad desiscions sometimes. Thats just a part of life. I'm makeing a desision right now. I will accept nothing but a win at High Ocatne. My first PPV match in a few months. and boy every one knows how much better i do it at the Pay per views. I've won two cruiserweight championship Number one contender spots and a Cruiserweight title run at PPVS. So as i look at it, This is just a time to shine Super bright. The one night a month i shake all the bad shit off me and i go out and give it every bit that i have. All that my body can suspend. I'll bleed if i've got to. But on that one night. I give it my all and theres no one here that doesnt. You notice alot more people walking around with their heads up. Their Egos down. A respect for every one except your opponet you feel a personal respect for. 'So, American Youth Josh, KJP, and Ace Harding. You are going to be faceing Me..K.C. and Tommy..And that matters Becuse i've head good things about these two boys. I know about KJP, Ace is a New comer and Josh i've heard a couple of good things about him. And it comes down to being the best ion the ring. When all the mouth running is over. Then you see who the real men are. And they dont alaways have to be the winners. I watch wrestling all the time and i see the bad guy win. It doesnt make me sick all the time but there are times i would rather turn it off. So i take it that some other people feel the same way. and i'm gonna promise you. That the right person wins this match. Weather it be me or any other competitior on either team.' I know it will be right becuse damn it some...I'm gonna make it right...And that to me means more then anything else in the world right now. I just want to go out and have a good time wrestling. With my intentions on winning. I know that there are alot of ways to wlose this match. A Communication problem between me and one of the other uys. They are used to tagging together. They arnt going to be used to the three man tag situation. Thats understandable but then again i dont know if the three men have the same problem. I'm going to have to talk with my boys, Maybe some guidence from an elder wrestler will help them. Eye contact. You lock eyes with me and i'll know eveything you need to say. I'm a season vet. There is nothing that i havent seen in that right. I know whats going to happen. And sometimes these problems with that but most times you can almost see it commin. Now that oi look back at all my years of wreatling i should have wrote a book, Becuse listen to me. I make mistakes. But i make way less then you do my friend... 'Now, I've been a trainer and seeing what talents that people have had after my training has been a little less the phenmonal. So now i have The world by the Nads. I'm ready to take what is mine and that right now is a win at High Ocatne. I'm ready to show you all that Now. It's My time. And Mr KC and Tommy. You welcome to come along with me for as much of the duration that you are willing and or able to.' I welcome the fact that i would have some one to watch my back for a little while. It'd be kind of nice to not be looking over my shouldent again and again. But now i look on to High Octane. To a Victory. To me being one of the one's left standing.. ~~Fade to Black~~
