The Max-Attack: Chapter 9

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today BOOM Roswell flies back and hits a window smashing it. Pieces of signs and bench’s fly everywhere, one even hitting a lady. Roswell gets up inside the window and says sorry for breaking the window. Roswell runs out the window and stares into the smoke. Roswell sees a figure flying in the air and remembers it is The Blue Bat. Roswell starts running towards the figure but it is to fast. Roswell jumps into the air and starts flying towards The Blue Bat. Max catches up to The Bat and grabs onto his legs in mid air. Max quickly hits a mid air kick taking The Blue Bat out of the air. Max lands on the ground and waits for The Blue Bat to get up. Max turns The Blue Bat around but sees a blue ragged mask, and you can see half of his face. Max looks at the face before getting punched in the nose by The Blue Bat. The Blue Bat quickly jumps back into the air and flies away. Max jumps into the air again and starts chasing him again. Max dives onto The Blue Bat taking him out of the air again. The Bat quickly runs into a building out of sight. Max runs into the building to and starts looking around. Max sees someone run up a flight of stairs so Max runs up the stairs to. Max starts looking around the room that is up the stairs. Max sees a man put on a suit that is covering a blue kind of armour. Max runs at the man and knocks the man off his feet. Max starts stomping the man that is laying on the ground. Max looks at the guys face and sees that is not The Blue Bat. Max bends over and says sorry to the man. Max walks away still searching. Max walks up some more stairs and sees another man in a suit walk into a weird looking elevator. Max runs at the elevator but it closes before Roswell can get in it. Max waits for elevator to come back up. Max walks into the elevator pushing the button really hard. Max gets out of the elevator and walks into a room. It is a weird room that only has one person in it other than Max. A guy is sitting at a table smoking and looking out the window onto the streets. Max sees the man is wearing a trench coat and Max also sees a blue kind of armour. Max runs at the guy and knocks him over. Max grabs the man but throws it back down. Max sees that the guy is just made of plastic and it isn’t real. Max walks to a brown wooden and opens it. Max walks int the other side of the door and sees a very long hallway. Max sees a man with a trying to get through a different door at the end of the hall. Max starts running down the hall trying to see who the guy is. Max gets to the guy and runs into him. The guy falls to the ground, trying to crawl away. Max stops him with his leg and brings him to his feet. “Who are you?” asks Roswell. The man doesn’t answer and punches Roswell in the face. The man also kicks Roswell making him stay on the hard ground. Roswell gets up and kicks the man straight in the balls taking him down. Roswell rips the mans trench coat off and sees blue armour. Roswell reaches into the mans trench coat pocket and pulls out a bat like mask. Roswell throws the mask onto the man and kicks him again. “I know you are The Blue Bat. But what did you ever want with me?” asks Roswell The man doesn’t answer again and just punches Roswell again. The man flips onto his feet and puts on his bat mask. The Bat takes Roswell up and throws him straight into a cement wall. Roswell quickly gets up and hits a spinning kick taking down the Bat. Roswell takes his Max-Attack mask out and puts it on. Roswell takes off his normal clothes revealing his black and red Max-Attack outfit. Max flips to his feet punching The Bat yet another time. The Bat gets up and throws Roswell straight through a glass wall. Max gets up on the other side and is seen putting a little package in his mouth. The Bat tries to the punch Max again but Max ducks and sprays red mist into the eyes of The Bat. The Bat goes backwards holding his eyes. Max jumps through the already broken glass and starts walking down the long hallway. The Bat gets up but is a little blinded and runs after Max. Max ducks again and hits his wrestling move The Gravity-Plex. SCENE FADES
