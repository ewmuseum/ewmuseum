At the Dark Horse with Ely

27 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/27/04 The mood in the Dark Horse saloon was at an all-time high. People were dancing, drinking, riding the mechanical bull, etc. Then the door swung open and in walked Brick. The crowd cheered “HEEEY!!!” after he walked in. He looked around and saw his buddy Ely Braxton at the bar. “Hey, Ely!” Brick called, before heading over to the bar. He saw Braxton having his usual Texas mudslide. In this case, the glass was half-empty. “Bartender, lemme have an Angus Bingham.” “That’s one cranberry juice coming up.” Brick then sat on the stool right next to Braxton. “So, heard you put out an open challenge for a Bar fight. Any takers?” “So far, couple o” the new guys, Jarone and Terrelle Green.” “You know if Bubba were here, he’d probably throw a fit. This here bar is his baby.” Then an awkward silence fell between the two men. It had been months since Bubba McCoy was last seen. He disappeared mysteriously during an edition of Evolution. No one knew who was responsible, but the members of the Upper Echelon were the top suspects. “Damn. What the hell was I thinking?” “Forget about it. You’re right, though. He would throw” “So, what prompted you to issue this particular challenge?” “Been sitting in the back far too long. Had to stay back there just in case any fools get the idea to put their hands on my Christina.” “You mean since that one incident with Roswell and that cat Trey?” “Exactly.” “I don’t know, man. I don’t think anybody’s that interested in Christina, aside from you.” “What?” It was then that the bartender returned with Brick’s drink. “That’s one Angus Bingham special.” “Thanks.” As Brick took a sip from his cranberry juice, Braxton asked, “You were saying?” “Look, don’t get me wrong, man. Christina’s fine and all, but a lot of cats backstage know what might happen if they try to push up on her.” “Maybe a lot o” guys, but not all of “em.” “C’mon, let’s talk about something else.” “OK. So, how’s the Terminator thing going?” “Something else!” Brick growled. “I just wanna know how it’s bein” dealt with.” “Well, he and I are going toe-to-toe at Global Reload.” “Good to hear. I think it’s about time that sumbitch got his ass handed to him.” “And I just might. So when’s the Bar fight?” “Don’t know. Might be at Global Reload. Might be on the Evolution after that.” Brick then raised his glasses. “Here’s to those who deserve the ass-kickings they get.” “Here, here.”
