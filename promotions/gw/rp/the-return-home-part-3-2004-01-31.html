The Return Home (Part 3)

31 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31-01-04 With their calls made and a new goal in mind, the two Nakazura brothers made their way towards the door of their apartment. Jushin smiled and walked over to his closet. Ichiro figured he was getting his coat, but when he observed his brother he realized that he was completely wrong. The door swung open and Jushin smiled, as he pulled the small chain leading from the light overhanging the closet. Instantly with a click the entire closet was illuminated to what looked like a normal closet. There were shoes in racks kept neatly, a few leather jackets and spring jackets hung from hangers as well as a couple of neck ties and business jackets. But when Jushin pressed the small switch underneath of the shoe rack, the entire closet began to change. The sounds of gears in motion began to hum under the closet, and the rack slowly began to roll backwards. Underneath was the largest stash of illegal closet firearms that Ichiro had ever seen. Jushin smiled and reached his hand into the small containment of guns. He pulled out a couple of extra pistols and tossed them along with a specialized leather gun holster to his older brother. The two men quickly took their business suit jackets off and began to put on the new pieces of equipment. Minutes later they were downstairs, and the black Mitsubishi was pulling out into the Tokyo sunset. Minutes later the car pulled to a stop outside the small guns and ammunition shop. The man working was a personal friend of the brothers and had close links to their fathers empire. When they walked in they could see the nervousness and uneasiness in the man’s eyes. He looked as if he hadn’t slept in days, haunted by some constant nightmare. The two brothers approached him slowly, and noticed the slight smile and spread of relief across the man’s face. He smiled in what appeared to be the first time in weeks. The wrinkles in his aged skin seemed to crack as the smile spread across his face. The two brothers walked to the desk, as the man slowly emerged from behind the desk. The three men embraced in a hug for several seconds before breaking. The silence hung heavily in the air, and although not a word had yet been spoken, an entire eternity worth of conversation had been made. The brothers looked at the man, the stare was caught and the man returned to his place behind the desk. He smiled at them, obviously he was happy to see them. He was not able to help them greatly, in the mission that he was nearly certain they would soon engage in. He walked to the register and pulled out the small set of keys plugged into the top. He put the meal ring around his finger, he spun it around, keeping his attention slightly presumed while the two brothers looked around. “Nothing much here Yoshahiro, what’s wrong?” asked Jushin. “Since the incident with your father, my business has become much less active. I make less sales, I am often looted and robbed without your father’s protection. There is something that you must do, you boys are like my nephews. I am sorry that I cannot physically aid you in your coming task, but there is something that I can do for you.” “Excellent news Yoshahiro. Whatever help you can lend is much appreciated,” answered Ichiro. Yoshahiro let a broad smile come across his face. He went into the back room for a few minutes. When he emerged he was carrying several wooden crates. He smiled and placed them on top of the desk and reached underneath of the desk for something to open the crates with. He pulled out a small crowbar and began to go to work on one of the crates. The crates were small, large enough to hold about two pistols at most. They were very thin however, which was a good thing. The crates were made out of a strong wood and judging by Yoshahiro’s struggle with the first crate, the locking mechanism was quite good. “These are brand new on the market. I haven’t even brought them out until now, I didn’t plan on it either. I didn’t want to give Ishuzu’s men a reason to come in here more then they already have. God damned, prick motherfuckers. Anyways, the guns appear to be pistols, but they fire a completely different form of bullet. The bullet is made out of a carbon composite metal, it shatters upon impact. Allowing your blast radius to increase.” “Sounds like fun to me, I always like to play with new toys,” answered Ichiro. “If you like heavy power and lightweight and versatile, you are going to love these little goodies.” In a few more minutes Ichiro and Jushin both had a matching set of the pistols in their capable hands. They were smiling and waving them around the store. It was almost as if they were kids again, pretending to play Cop vs Robber games with their fingers. As Yoshahiro continued to pry open the remaining crates he remembered the first time he had seen Ichiro and Jushin. They were young then, not only in age, but also in maturity. He had seen them so many times since then, and always when they bought new merchandise they always played in the same manner. It was strange how the two boys could become men and change so much, yet still be so similar to their childhood. It was a remarkable thing. Yoshahiro finished opening the crates, there were six sets of pistols. He handed them specially fitted holster to handle the guns, however, the holsters only had room for three sets of pistols. While that seemed like perfect logic, there was one thing that didn’t occur to the brothers. They always carried the custom pistols that their father had given them, it was a personal preference. Everyone had their own standard gun that they liked, the sentimental value attached to those custom pistols made them the greatest killing devices in Tokyo. The look of confusion was obvious to Yoshahiro as he smiled at them. “There is someone who wishes to speak with you. He is happy to aid you in your quest,” said Yoshahiro. He then tossed another one of the custom holsters to the two brothers. Jushin caught it and smiled, he walked out and put the new guns and holsters into the trunk of the black Mitsubishi. Ichiro stayed inside and carried a school bag’s worth of specialized, carbon ammunition out to the car. With six new pistols and three holsters, they set off to find the final piece of the puzzle. Their uncle, Gohei.
