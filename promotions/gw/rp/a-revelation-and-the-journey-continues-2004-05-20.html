A Revelation...And The Journey Continues....

20 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-20-04 “I don’t know exactly what these poor unfortunate peasants expect of me, and I have been traveling towards yonder forest for a period of three days now. The green just never seems to get any closer. It is almost as if I have been sent this way in order to view the desolation of the place first hand. I have passed many villages and in each I have been vilified as a returning saviour?” The warm sun beats down on the broad back of Venneman Pendragon, Lord King and supposed saviour of this land. Ahead lies a small village and Pendragon stops and takes a deep breath before going in? “Each village brings me something unexpected. Never has a visit been ill, but always I leave uncomfortable. I am used to being honored as royalty, but many times these peasants are too forward with their gesticulations?” He shrugs and walks into the village proper. Here and there a dog lies sleeping but there are no people. It is a decent sized village, but still, nothing. He walks along and he hears singing coming from a hut. It appears to be a church. At least he assumes it is a church. A large hut with a wooden cross before the door stands off to the side of the road?” SHALL WE GATHER BY THE RI-VER?..THE BEAUTIFUL THE BEAUTIFUL THE RIIIIIIIVER! SHALL WE GATHER BY THE RIVER, THAT FLOWS THROUGH THE KINGDOM OF GOD!! Pendragon stood perplexed looking at the meager structure? “At least these poor souls can find some solace in divinity. And yet, in a land such as this, I am surprised that worship is allowed. I must talk to the preacher’Religious men know much, and I would know some of what he knows. The least of which is how far yonder wood lies”.” Pendragon walks into the building and the shot switches to the inside of the building. A few benches line the room, and a tall thin man stands at a small wooden pulpit. His gray hair is brushed back from his forehead revealing a face that at one time may have passed for handsome. He notices Pendragon but pretends not to. His eyes are black, and full of life, and his words carry the same feeling? Preacher: Aye’and in the lord we may draw strength in these dark days. But my children, we must fear not, for better days are surely on the way. We still have our faith, and Akumancha has yet to forbid out belief in a deity other than himself. But time flies, and we must return to our homes and our living. Let us pray?” “Lord’We thank you for all the blessings you have decided to bestow upon us. Each day that we draw breath is truly a blessing from you in these times of darkness. We thank you for our families and our crops, and we also pray for deliverance from the evil that has infested this land. IN Jesus name we pray”.Amen?” AMEN “Even now these people seem happy, if not content. The human soul has an amazing ability to persevere?” The people file out and Pendragon ducks his head, not wanting to go through the kneeling and worshipping that seems to happen when the people of this strange place recognize him. At last the people are gone, and Pendragon is left alone with the Preacher. The Preacher walks over to the golden armored King and smiles down at him? Preacher: Come, my friend. You and I have much to discuss. Long have I waited this day, and my role will be played as was meant to be. I am sure that you have many questions, and I will make clear to you as much as I can? The preacher motions for Pendragon to follow, and leads him from the church. Pendragon follows as the man leads him from the village by way of a small path that runs behind the church. At last they come to an ancient cemetery. Stones lie here and there, but there are some which are still erect. A huge stone dragon sits in the middle of the graveyard? Pendragon: Where is this that thee have brought me, Preacher? Preacher: Just call me John, my Lord. This is an ancient graveyard where many of your ancestors lie entombed. The dragon is the entrance to a huge underground tomb. Come’You have much to see? John walks to the stone dragon and presses one of the great toes of the beast. The dragon slides back on unseen hinges, revealing a stairway that leads down into gloom. John begins to descend and Pendragon follows, a but wary? Pendragon: Is there no light? How deep do these stairs run? John: No light until we shut the door’No eyes should witness this secret, as has been kept for these many long years? With that he stops and presses a place in the wall of the stairwell. The door above slides shut once more, and immediately the lights of gas lamps spring into view all the way down the corridor. Pendragon sighs in relief and the two men continue down into the depths of the tomb. As they walk, the walls are done in intricate frescoes. Some depict kingly fellows leading battles, and others depict wizards and dragons. Pendragon slows as he looks at each scene, his eyes rapt? Pendragon: Indeed this is the world that I was meant for’These men sought glory and honor, and achieved it as well? John: You shall see much. On the wall is a history of your house’The house of Pendragon. This is the world as it should have been. Not the dying husk that you call home. Pendragon stares incredulously at the man? Pendragon: Thou knowest of mine world? John doesn’t answer, but leads Venneman ever deeper into the tomb. Finally the stairs stop, and a huge corridor opens before them. Grand doors line the path, and a bold face is molded in gold to each one? John: These are the halls of the dead of your fathers, my Lord? Pendragon stares in awe at each face as they pass. All resemble him in a way. The strong jaw, the proud nose. Some have warm eyes, others fierce, but their relation is unmistakable. He runs his hands upon each face as he passes, the wonder never leaving his eyes. At last, they reach a huge open gateway. They walk through and come to a huge underground cathedral. High arches and colored windows fill the place, illumined by an unseen light? Pendragon: Many wonders have I witnessed in mine time, and yet this is the most beautiful place I have ever found mineself to be in? John: Aye’This is the ancient Cathedral, built by your first sire’Arthur. The windows show different scenes: The sword in the stone’Lady of the Lake’Different battles with crowned Arthur at the front of his armies, leading with the glowing sword. John: In this world, Camelot persevered long after the death of the mighty Arthur. In this world, the lineage was unbroken for a thousand years. Until the throne was usurped by the foul Akumancha, and you were rescued by Merlin the Wizard? Pendragon: Aye’Merlin. Many hats has that old fox worn. Why does he not help now? John: The magic of Akumancha is strong. Were he and Merlin to get into a battle of sorcery, there would be nothing left of this land. No throne for you to reclaim. Nothing. It is the strong will that will defeat Akumancha and his heathen hordes? John walks to the majestic pulpit and pulls a book from an alcove below it. He brings the book back to Pendragon and hands it to him. Pendragon gasps as he turns the pages and sees himself. His first moment in the Land, pants-less’His journey as a saviour through villages. He stops when he reaches the tomb and hands the book back? Pendragon: So mine story is already written? I shall see no more? John: But?.But this could tell you much! Pendragon shakes his head slowly? Pendragon: I do the things I do because they are the right choices at the time. Not because I am forced to choose so’I only wish to know where I must go. John sighs and motions for Pendragon to sit. The two sit Indian style on the floor, facing one another? John: Your task does not lie first in the forest. For that reason, the forest has evaded your coming thus far? Pendragon: Evaded? But how? John: The story must unravel as it is supposed to. You are fated to do a thing, and there is a certain ordained order that must be followed. The world is simply pushing you in the right direction. There is a object that you must retrieve to go against the magic. Without it, you will be vulnerable to the attack of the black arts of Akumancha. It is a Talisman that will keep evil magic from you. Pendragon sits there with a “What the hell art thou speaking about” look etched upon his noble face? Pendragon: Where? John: Far to the south it lies, across a mighty desert. There are those along the way who have been left to help you, as I have been left to help you. Fear not, for things are very much in order? Pendragon: I have no fear. I do the things that need doing. Nothing will alter me from mine course. South thee says? John nods, and light returns to the eyes of the tale teller? John: You must restore this land. The people are good and decent, and they need a hero, for they are weak? Pendragon smiles and claps John on the shoulder as the scene begins to fade? Pendragon: And I am the sworn protector of the weak and innocent?” “But what was this” Preordained? No higher power uses me as a chess piece in a game of strategy. This will go down how I decree it, or I will go down’A magical Talisman’I have got to be dreaming?” TO BE CONTINUED
