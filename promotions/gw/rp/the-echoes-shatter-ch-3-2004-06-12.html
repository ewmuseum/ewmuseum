The Echoes Shatter (Ch. 3)

12 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 12th, 2004 Prologue. … Has anyone every realized the way life is? How the world spins? Where the universe ends? How do the stars appear one night and not the next? How the moon fades away and the sun appears from dusk? Where the ocean ends and the core of earth begin? Sure there is a lot of scientific knowledge that tells us ‘the truth’ but that knowledge can and is proven wrong or altered sometime down the line. So what makes this world spin I ask? What makes me live? Sure my heart beats but what about the soul inside of me? Does it live on or do I fade away from existence whenever I’m done? How about this one… Who made god? If god is the superior being and everything was created by him/her, who created him/her? He/she didn’t just slip through the cracks of a molecule structure and appear in thin air did he/she? I don’t know… I wish to find out but that truth lies in a place I find frightening. So this comes to the point of this role-play. The time of life has been well spent by people and animals, but the question is how long is too long? Has the time for Jesse Jamester began to fade away? Has his life of glory and happenings become only a hope he wishes to accomplish, or is it possible and this is only a mere question of doubt? Story Continues. Hands covering his face as the tears escape through the cracks of his fingers. Jesse couldn’t control his feelings, his eyes were a misty red as he pulled his hands from them and wiped his eyes with the sleeve of his t-shirt. It wasn’t common of him to cry, he never did, it just wasn’t his way. Life had crept up on Jesse, the undesired and unwanted beliefs had become true, and the truth of solitude was now his future. Yet he couldn’t bare it, not in his last twelve years as a wrestler had he felt this way, where the meaning of life meant nothing to him. “Max… I can’t do this. I can’t keep living like this,” stuttered Jesse as he lay back on the couch and looked up at the light upon the ceiling fan, “it’s just too much for me to handle emotionally.” “Jess, you can do this! You’re Jesse f’n Jamester! You’re like the big brother I never had man. You have to fight it like you’ve fought problems in the past… Come on Jess, you know this isn’t the worst you’ve faced, remember your daughter being taken from you?” stated Max trying to motivate Jesse. “I know Max, it’s just too much to deal with while trying to beat this Takahashi Jetsui and knowing I’m so distracted by Nikki’s death still. It rests in the back of my mind, just growing until it reaches the surface,” Jesse answers now calming down and coming to reality, “It’s just hard, and I need to know I have someone their who will catch me if I fall.” “Well… I’ll catch ya, but I doubt I’ll be able to stop myself from falling too you big goon,” cracks Max, “Just consider me your pillow Jess, got it?” They share a laugh as it seems silly for Jesse to consider Max every literally catching him because Max is so small compared to Jesse, but they do know in thought it can happen and will. Grabbing Jesse’s hand Max pulls him off the couch to a vertical base and stares at him. “Come on man, let’s go out! It’s on me!” says Max as he pats Jesse on the back and the two leave the apartment. The Club. Max decided to take Jesse out to a club where he could relax, however the idea was more for Max getting some action with the ladies and Jesse to watch him pick up the women with his young body. Sitting firmly on the bar stool Jesse drank away the night not caring that he had already drank six shots and two margaritas with a rocket to top it off. Being an alcohol only two years ago this was the first time he’d drank since then. ‘This is what my life’s like now… Even though I try to forget the worries don’t escape my head. It’s like the blood in a bath tub, you can still see it even though theirs more water than blood. So when I turn my head to this drink, it lets the worries be drained from me in hopes of getting on with life.’ It was sad to see him waist away his life to the drinks but at the same time it’s all he could do to stop thinking of Nikki. And so it was. “Hey man, I’m going to have to cut you off, this is your last one,” said the bartender knowing very well that Jesse was already drunk and unable to drive as he was suppose to. “Okay,” slurred Jesse with a reply as he was having a bliss time just sitting their staring at the bar tabletop. A woman caught his mind from the corner of his eye. A hot looking blonde who reminded him a lot of his girlfriend Nikki. Getting up he made his way across the room stumbling a bit through some of the people and finally came to her. “Hello…” he slurred again but this time clearer than before, “Whats your name pretty thing?” She snickered at his drunk state and replied, “Amanda, and yours?” Jesse didn’t answer right away and then as he opened his mouth he vomited all over her dress causing her to run to the bathroom in grief. Hearing the screams from the other side of the room Max came running over. He grabbed Jesse as he slouched back and fell to a near by chair. Max shook his head but knew he had himself to blame for bringing Jesse to the club. Helping Jesse up the two walked out the back door and left to the parking lot where they got in the black dodge truck and left. Soiled memory. Burning like a coal Jesse’s brain had taken much punishment due to his lack of confidence. The sorrow that had built up for Nikki had surfaced as poison and he was now weakened by it. Though, that wouldn’t stop Jesse from living, it would stop him from believing. He’d never be the same as the memories of Nikki ran through his head. Her cute face, witty sense of humor, a smile that could melt the sun, and her way of relating to Jesse, those all made her the person Jesse loved and wanted to live with for the rest of his life. It could have been any person that he fell in love with, but it was she, and now that she was gone, he had nothing… A hollow brick that would crack under the pressure of the foundation it was built. This Jesse had to fix, without fixing it the world would be the roller coaster he’s riding and the tracks would guide him to insanity. ’You know that feeling when you wake up and you’re so tired you just want to keep on sleeping? So you roll over and try to sleep but instead you lie their awake unable to move. You’re body feels so controlled by the sleep that the only thing able to awaken you and get you moving is to think of what the day will be like? Well… to me I can’t do that, because the day ahead of me is a blank. This world, it doesn’t like me… not one bit.’ Laying on the couch Jesse puked again before finally rolling over and seeing Max about to fall asleep. 'Tomorrow... I clean up my act and start fresh. No more of this bull shit, I have to pull myself together for my team... and for my best friend, Max Roswell.' Their Jesse rolled over and closed his eyes before falling asleep.
