Intrusion Sergei Strakhlzhenski Global Wrestling Promo #403 Date: 7/8/03 Location: Surge's Apartment in Lublin, Poland Surge arrives at his apartment in Lublin after a flight from Las Vegas, where he suffered a loss at the hands of Max Queue in the PTC Tournament. He had felt down a little big at first, but then he realized that this freed up his schedule. Having requested some vacation time from Karl Acorah, he was going to live it up... well... he was going to try. There still hadn't been any word from Argentina on his brother. He wasn't getting his hopes up, but he wanted to be sure Igor was dead before he started mourning him. Igor had gone down in a Learjet that had been fired upon by an Israeli F-16. What an Israeli fighter jet was doing that far from Israel was anyone's guess, but for Surge, it wasn't a guess. He had fought off the Massod in Derby, England right after the card, and had learned what they knew about him and his brother, and, most importantly, the man that created them. Dr. Greiber had been an SS Colonel during WWII, and had been working on genetic experiments. Igor and Surge were a direct result of that experimentation... they were the prototypes to the doctor's attempt to make Hitler's perfect Aryan Warrior. It was ironic, though, that Surge was Jewish, and flew right in the face of what Hitler and Dr. Greiber were trying to do. If he had to sacrifice his brother to see justice done to Dr. Greiber, otherwise known as the Physician of Lublin, then so be it. He would deal with the loss, and go on with his life. He still had Frankie and his mother. Surge opened the door to his apartment and stopped, dropping his bag with an angry look. His apartment had been ransacked. Apparently, someone had caught wind of the letter and thought it was here. Surge walked over to the phone and picked it up. He started to dial, then stopped. He unscrewed the reciever and shook his head. Sloppy work, he thought. He yanked the bug out and smashes it on the floor, crushing it with his penny loafer. He then reset the phone and dialed the number he knew by heart. The answer came in Polish. 'Polska Bureau of Intelligence, how may I assist you,' came the voice on the other line. 'This is Sergei Strakhlzhenski, I need a team at my apartment,' said Surge. 'Someone has ransacked it, and I would like to know who.' There was silence on the other end, then another voice. 'Mr. Strakhlzhenski,' said the voice. 'This is Vladimir Wiklzowski, Deputy Bureau Chief of the Lublin area. How may I be of assistance?' 'Vladamir, send me a team,' said Surge, using his brother's old friend's first name. They had met on his last trip and hit it off pretty well. 'Someone was here looking for something. I would like to know who, and why.' After a short silence, Wiklzowski replied. 'Very well, we will have a team there promptly. We've actually been waiting for this call. We were going to have your place cleaned so you didn't know, but then the Massod took you. We figured you would want to know they ransacked your home.' 'The Massod,' said Surge, angrily. 'They better quit f***ing with me before they make an enemy they don't want to make!' 'We'll take care of the Massod,' said Wiklzowski. 'We just needed your permission to come to your apartment and get some evidence.' 'Take whatever you want, just make sure it isn't personal,' said Surge. 'I understand,' said Wiklzowski. 'They'll have strict orders. Just wait for us there.' Surge waits for the intelligence people to arrive, and shows them around. After they finish rumaging through his mess, he is left there to clean what is left. He still didn't have closure on this whole recent episode, and on top of that, he had found somewhere he wanted to compete, and didn't know how to break it to his good friend, Karl Acorah, the owner of his current fed. He had been reminded of that by a message that was left on his answer machine by one Bubba McCoy. It had been an invitation to compete that Surge just couldn't turn down. Global Wrestling... more than a wrestling organization... but not exactly a fight club. Surge felt he could excel there. This was a bad situation. Surge was torn, and on top of all of this, he had the wedding with Frankie in planning, and he had his brother missing and presumed dead in the South Atlantic off the coast of Argentina. Surge couldn't take it anymore. He threw down the picture he was holding and walked into his bedroom and collapsed into the bed, leaving all the cares of the world behind as sleep took over. 'Sergei,' came a questioning tone. It snapped Surge out of his deep sleep, and he sat up, looking around. In the doorway, he saw Frankie standing with a worried look. 'What happened to the apartment?' 'You don't vant to know,' said Surge. 'It has to do vis zi business vis Igor.' 'Oh,' said Frankie, looking down with a frown. 'Have you heard anything back yet?' 'No,' said Surge. 'Zay are still looking for zi wreckage. Zay have given up on finding survivors I tink.' 'I'm sorry Sergei,' said Frankie. 'I know you had just re-established your relationship with your family, and now all of this.' She walked over and sat down on the bed beside him. 'I'm here for you if you need to talk.' Surge smiled at Frankie. 'I appreciate that,' he said. 'I do need to talk, but not just yet. I vill need you more ven zay return Igor's body to my mozer. Zat vill be a hard sing for her to take.' 'Yeah,' said Frankie. 'We'll have to support one another in this.' Surge smiled and nodded. 'Oh,' he said, remembering what he had been thinking about before he went to sleep. 'I've been offered an invitation to Global Wrestling.' 'Global Wrestling,' asked Frankie. 'But you're under contract with Karl.' 'I know,' he said. 'I'm in a fix, and I don't know how to get myself out of it.' 'You'll figure it out,' said Frankie. 'You always do.' 'Yes, zis is true,' he said. 'For now, I vould like to put my apartment back togezer.' 'I thought you would never ask,' said Frankie. She got up and went into the other room, starting ahead of Surge. He watched her go, and as he heard her begin picking things up, he smiled again. At least he had Frankie. She would have to be his family from now on.
