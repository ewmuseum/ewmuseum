The Greatest Night Of My Life (Part 1)

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/03 He drives his green 1965 Buick Special with her in the car. He is in the best shape of his life. And it has paid off. He looks to his right, there she sits. With a smile on her face and as beautiful as can be. How can this be? He is not worthy of her. He blushes and smiles, the biggest smile ever graced upon his face. Her dark hair and her beautiful blue eyes, nothing can compare. She bares the most gorgeous prom dress, white with the sparkle on the front, that can only be found in her eyes. The sun shines down on her face, glistening’reminding him of an angel sent from up above. His black leather dress shoe slowly presses the gas pedal further. He can’t wait’anxious to hold her hand and to look into her eyes while dancing to the most romantic songs in the world. Thoughts running through his mind? What about if I don’t look good enough for her. You look good, don’t worry about it. But she is so out of my league. You are going to the prom with her, does that matter? Inside he shrugs his shoulders, it doesn’t matter. She is with him. He smiles once again. She looks over at him? “Why are you smiling so much tonight?” She chuckles, a drip of sweat falls from his face. His mind running around in circles, trying to think of something to say to not make her think anything less of him. “Because I love this song?” The drip of sweat evaporates into the air, relieving the tension of those few awkward seconds. He reaches over and turns the radio up. The radio begins to play the middle of 16 Candles. He sits dumbfounded, but smiles and looks at her face. “Never heard it.” “It is remarkable. Amazing, a masterpiece at its finest. The 2nd greatest thing in the whole entire world.” She nods her head, then a look of confusion falls across her face? “Then what’s the first?” He looks into her eyes, his heart warms up in a matter of milliseconds. “You?” He mutters as another smile falls across his face. She smiles back? “You are such a charmer Sage. One day you’ll find a perfect girl to appreciate it.” The thoughts run through his head, thinking? A perfect girl? I would prefer nobody except you. This crack in my heart can be filled with our love. He hesitates for a few seconds? “Sometimes the perfect girl isn’t what I want.” “What do you mean?” “Sometimes I feel that the perfect girl is sitting right beside me and she just doesn’t know it yet.” The awkward silence, just the thing he needed, a stake penetrating through his heart’waiting, wondering what runs through her mind right now. In his head, he thinks thoughts he has never thought before? I love her. I hope she loves me back. I told her. I hope she tells me back. She turns around again and smiles’a good sign. “Just stop it already, you are going to make me cry.” That is my goal. To make your heart melt. With me, you’ll never cry. With me, you’ll never be upset. With me, you will have everything. Because you are my everything. “It’s the truth, its how I feel.” “You are so sweet, Sage.” Those words. I have never heard before from anybody. But the words I long to hear. Three words that could fill that crack in my heart. They are what need. I love you. He hides his crossed fingers with false happiness, wondering if she cares about him as well. Instead of responding, he just smiles? She blushes, her face as red as an apple from his apple tree. To be with you. Oh love, tis a dream. Dream come true? Fill me in on this secret, love. I promise to speak no words. I love you. Make it reality. He pauses, thinking of something to say as the?
