The Max-Attack: Chapter 4

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The scene opens up to the outside of the stripper club. It is night time now and Roswell is seen walking down the streets holding his rib cage from the explosion. Roswell reaches into his pocket again making sure that the little ball is still in it. Roswell says to him self that he should get a hotel room for the night to get some rest for tomorrow as he is still trying to find his new enemy. Roswell walks down a street and kind of trips over something that is on the ground. Roswell picks up the item he tripped and inspects it. The item is a circular ball with little mysterious wings coming out of the side. Roswell takes out the ball that was in his pocket before and inspects them both. Roswell says that they are the same thing and the enemy must have threw another one at something. Roswell puts both of the balls into his pocket and keeps walking down the street looking for a cheap hotel to stay in for the night. Roswell then finds a hotel that reads “The Motel 6,” and walks into it wondering if he can stay in a room. Roswell walks over to a black counter with only one lady behind it. ?Is there a room that I can stay in for the night?? asks Roswell to the lady. “Yes, we always have opens rooms, because we ain’t to popular.” says the lady back to Roswell. ?How much will that be?? asks Roswell coughing after he does. “Since no one ever comes here, we lowered the price to 20 bucks a night.” tells the lady. Roswell reaches out of his pocket taking out his wallet. Roswell pulls 20 dollar bill out of the wallet and hands it to the lady. The lady hands a little book with two room keys in it so he can get into his room. Roswell takes the room keys and walks away to the elevator. Roswell pushes the button UP and the door opens. He walks in and pushes the number 5 on the elevator panel, and it starts moving. The door opens at the 5th floor and Roswell walks out of the elevator walking to his door. Roswell stops at the door number ?209' and puts the key into the slot. Roswell waits for the green light and walks into the room. There is one bed with a dresser and a bathroom. The room has a pretty bad stench but Roswell ignores it. Roswell flops down onto the bed and holds his chest again signalling that there is more pain from the destruction. Roswell takes out the two mysterious balls and studies them for a few seconds. He finds that there are little Japanese symbols on the tips of the balls. Roswell also discovers that there is one big symbol covering each of the wings. ?I wonder what these symbols could represent?? Roswell says to his self. “I mean they are funny looking for symbols” says Roswell again. Then from the window, Roswell hears a big boom and rushes to the window. Roswell sees a big building on fire with people screaming all over the place. Roswell turns off the light quickly and runs out of the room and also running down the stairs. Roswell opens the door of the hotel and runs outside to see what had happened. Roswell runs up to the building and finds another one of the little mysterious balls laying underneath a trash can. Roswell picks it up and puts it into his pocket with the 2 other ones. Roswell walks up to a fireman and asks what had happened. “The building just blew up like that. No one seen what happened except for the people inside the building.” yells the fireman. ?How could it just blow up by itself?? asks Roswell to his self. Roswell runs up to a police man and asks if he can go into the building and see what happened. ?Can I go in there and see what happened?? asks Roswell to the police officer. ?No, it is to dangerous!? yells the police officer. Roswell sneaks around all of the workers and sneaks under the police tape. Roswell runs into the flaming building with smoke everywhere. Roswell rushes to the stairs and runs up them trying to find some people. Roswell goes into the 3rd floor and sees a worker laying on the ground. Roswell goes and picks up the man and runs back down the stairs rushing to get him out of the building. Roswell runs out the front door and gives the man to the paramedics. Roswell rushes back into the building and runs back up the stairs trying to find some more people. Roswell goes into the 4th floor and sees another man laying on the floor at the end of the hallway. Roswell runs to get the man but stops halfway at the man. Roswell looks under on of the workers desks in the building and sees another little ball but sees a little timer on it. Roswell sees the timer and starts panicking. 0:05 0:04 0:03 0:02 0:01
