The Final Beginning. Part 11 - The Fall of the monster

The Final Beginning. Part 11 - The Fall of the monster Quartz Global Wrestling Promo #585 Date: 9/12/03 Location: The Johnson's house Almost an hour had passed since he had received the news that Lisha had died and yet still he scans the land looking for the two men who are to blame, not only for shooting Lisha, but killing her and her future child. But the more he searched, the more it seemed useless and Lisha’s death was beginning to hit home. So far all he had been running off was hatred and revenge, but even that can’t keep a man going forever. His red eyes scan the land on more time as the frost from his breath rises up into the air, bursting forth from his nose like a dragon. Nothing. He places his hands into his pockets, trying to keep them out of the cold and it is then that he remembers. Remembers what it is that Rose had given to him over dinner. Would she even know how much that information meant to Quartz? More than likely, knowing their close friendship from so long ago, she knew exactly how much it meant. Their closeness was something that Quartz had never felt before in his life, and has never felt since. She was the friend that helped him with his problems with his wife and ideas that The lair had put on him. Rose was his conscience. With his hand in his pocket, he pulls out the piece of paper which is folded over a number of times and with his cold hands he begins to undo those folds. His giant sized fingers made life with small fidgety objects difficult but he managed and looks at the address again. An address which he would make himself familiar with very, very soon. Turning his back on the grounds of the Lair’s castle, he looks at the giant building standing off nearly a mile away. His search of the grounds had been thorough to say the least, but both Mani and Jude had had a head start on the monster. With heavy steps of the knowledge of Lisha’s death, he begins to head back to the castle with one thing in mind. One thought that was a constant and that would rive him to face that which had taunted him. His son. But what would it bring him? What would happen when he saw his son face to face again? They had last seen each other with hate in their eyes and know Quartz was going to the one place he knew that his son would be. The trek back to the house was a lonely one and one full of thoughts which he didn’t want. This wasn’t like him to be caught up in the death of someone he knew. Even those who had passed away in the passed who he was close to, he had never felt this way. Lisha had grown on him over the years and now right in front of his eyes, she had been shot. But what hurt even more was the fact that it wasn’t in front of his eyes that she had finally passed away. No good-byes, no last words, no thank-you for the times they had shared. Nothing. Dispite the thoughts running loose in his head, it wasn’t long until he finds himself in the driver seat of his black pick up and tearing down the driveway. In a short amount of time he would be there and when the front door opened, he would swing his fist like never before. Jude would be the first one to pay, the one who pulled the trigger would be the last. The sun would begin to show soon and what he had to do would happen where it was pitch black, or full day light. No one would stop this. His hands gripped the wheel without knowledge of it and his knuckles were ghostly white with the pressure. The sound system was off and all that could be heard was the road rolling under the wheels. Every little imperfection in the road could be heard. Every bump that the road tried to put in the car’s way would be heard but never taken note of. Just like life for Quartz. no matter what he had placed in his way, he had found a way to over come it. Many thought of him as nothing more than the muscle behind Salaok’s mind, but for those who had had a chance to see the monster for what he really was, would know that was far from the case. those who know the big man would know that it was he who pulled a lot of the strings and it was he who had put a lot of the orders in place as it would have taken far too long to wait for Salaok to do it during the early years. Salaok had been a pupil to Quartz without Salaok even knowing it. But now, for this night, it would be Quartz who actually did do the work for Salaok, or at least his wife. Without moving his eyes off the target in front of him, his hand moves off the wheel and hits the lights, shutting them off and then turning off the engine, allowing the car to roll towards it’s final position. With the engine off, the car becomes stiffer to handle but that doesn’t matter as it only rolls at a few miles an hour before the brake peddle is squeezed down completely. Breathe in. Breathe out. His chest rising and falling with every heavy breath he takes as his eyes lock on the front door ahead of him. It was that which was inside that had tormented Quartz since he and his son and stood face to face on top of the sky scraper that fateful night. The night where Jude would destroy the building while they both stood on top of it. The night where Jude would fake his own death. Without meaning to let it out, a grumble comes from deep inside of his throat as his head lowers but his eyes never level the target. His massive hand finally lets go of the steering wheel, only to rise up to his head and push his head backward but stopping half way. With his fingers full of his own hair, he slowly brings it forward, dragging the hair with it and pulling it over his face. His face was now hidden behind the long black hair that Jude’s mother first feel in love with so many years ago now. Closing his eyes and taking one final breath, he reaches over to the glove box and pulls out a small hand gun. The metal object seems tiny in his large hands, but still enough to cause a fatal blow. But would he use the gun? Too impersonal for him normally, but in this situation, he didn’t want to leave anything to chance. You can’t really be revived after you’ve been shot a number of times in the head can you? Gripping the door handle, he gives it a sharp pull and thumps the door with his shoulder, sending it flying open. One foot after the other thuds down onto the road as Quartz pulls himself out of the car and stands there next to the car. In the dull blue moon light, his shadow is still a massive one as the moon shines from over his right shoulder. Something that would play to his advantage should this be another trick that Justin had set up and he be ambushed from behind, the shadows would tell him early. The lights in the house were on but the curtains were drawn. He passes the letter box and sees the address which matches that of what Rose had given to him in the note. If this is what he thought it was, the door would open and he would see his son face to face again. Not only would he see him face to face, but he would raise the gun to his son’s forehead and pull the trigger. The family of blood was one thing, but his family in The Lair was something that he cared so much more about. As he undoes and then does up the strap on his leather glove, he speaks to himself. ”I have no son. The Lair is and has always been my family. They killed a family member and the price on their heads is high.” Quartz seemed to almost be trying to convince himself that what he was about to do was right. But there was no question about that, it was right. Killing his son, Jude and his friend Mani is what had to be done. No longer would there be a Silent Wind and no longer would there be a Forgotten Angel. No, now there was going to be two less Harvesters in the world, and they would be killed the only way they should be. Killed at the hands of another Harvester. That was at least somewhat of an honorable death, even though they didn’t deserve it. The car had been left running and the drivers side door open, as long as this was done quickly there would be no problem, but should he try and take pleasure in this by dragging it out, he would risk having the car stolen. It didn’t matter much to him, as a car was nothing. He could afford many more like it and better. But these deaths would need to be fast. Need to be controlled and fast. There was no time to emotion. Each step thudding on the pavement as he came closer to the house. Each breath he takes becomes more and more controlled until he finally reaches the front door and with his left hand over the looking hole in the door, he uses his right to knock on the flimsy wooden door. Thud thud thud. Anyone who knew Quartz would know just how loud and dead the sound is when he knocks on a door. It’s almost double the sound of a normal man but as everyone knew, Quartz was no normal man. The shadow that comes from under the door walks right up to the door and stops as mumbling begins. The peep hole being blacked out clearly caused concern for whoever it was on the other side but the mumbles soon stop as two clicks can be heard from behind the door. This was it. This was the time that Quartz had been waiting for. Two clicks that sounded very much like guns and Quartz knew that both Mani and Jude were standing on the other side of the door now. The door handle turns and quickly Quartz regains control of his breathing and slows his heart beat knowing of what is about to happen. He pulls the gun up and then finally the door quickly swings open to reveal Jude standing there, exactly mirror image of his father with the gun drawn. The two men stand there with guns pointed at each others head, neither knowing why it is that they are not shooting the other’s brains out yet. ”I’ve been waiting for you father.” Jude says with almost pride in his voice that he was right. Quartz doesn’t move nor say a word except to raise an eyebrow questioningly. Finally after a few silent seconds, Quartz speaks. His voice likened to that of the dogs of hell, with his harsh and almost barking tone. ”Lisha has died, so has her child.” Whatever reaction Quartz was expecting his son to give, it certainly was not one of joy and happiness. ”Good.” ”How can you say that about a woman who helped you and cooked you countless meals. Took care of you when you needed it? How can you say that about a woman who never did anything wrong by you?” Jude begins to grin and shake his head as he continues to speak in a happy tone. “My, my father. You have gotten weak with age. There was once a time I am told that you killed your own Uncle for a contract and never thought twice about it. You’ve gotten weak father.” Suddenly there is another voice, much like Jude’s call out from around the hallway corner. “Yes father, you have become weak.” From the shadows walks another man but he stops before entering full light. “I have someone to show you father.” Quartz can’t understand what is going on, He holds a gun to Jude’s head and he is about and now there was some stranger calling him father and wanting to show him something. But Jude quickly begins to talk before Quartz can have any time to think about what is going on. ”Don’t worry father, you’ll like this next surprise. In fact, I dare say you’ve loved this surprise in the past.” Just as Jude finishes saying that, a woman walks out from around the corner and down the hallway towards them. Her face older than what it was when Quartz last saw her, but still clear as day to see whom this woman was. Quartz’s voice lowers as he speaks. ”Claire?” She nods as she walks towards him and speaks. “Yes Quartz, it’s me. You’re beloved wife.” Before anything can be said further, two gunshots ring out from behind Quartz and he kneecaps explode with the impact of the bullets driving straight through them. Falling to the ground as pieces of bone fly through the air and blood splatters to the floor, the echo of the gunshots can still be heard ringing through Quartz’s ears. Not letting out a scream of pain, nor a cry for help, Quartz lays there, his knees destroyed but looking up at his wife who he hasn’t seen since her supposed death a few years back. Suddenly the stranger’s voice at the end of the hall, still in the darkness sounds out again. “You know father, it would be fairly easy for us to kill you right now. But after the years of pain you’ve put us through, you deserve far, far more.” The stranger begins to walk forward and finally comes into the light. The shock on Quartz’s face is matched by no words as a man identical to Jude walks towards him with a needle in his hand. ”We’re going to have you looked after very well for the rest of your life.” Says Mani Shigeru as he walks in the door and actually stepping on Quartz’s fallen body. With that said, the stranger who looks like Jude walks up and places his foot on Quartz’s neck, holding his head down before reaching down to his back and jabbing the needle into his skin with little care. Still shocked by the whole situation, Quartz doesn’t struggle but just lays there in disbelief. In one night his entire life had just been turned inside out. Pressing down on the top of the needle with his thumb, the stranger lets all of the fluid drain into Quartz’s body and within seconds the room begins to spin for Quartz and then go black. His plan hadn’t gone as he had wanted it to. All because he didn’t shoot as soon as he saw his son. It was his own fault that he now lay out cold on the ground being dragged by the arms along the floor. He had lost.
