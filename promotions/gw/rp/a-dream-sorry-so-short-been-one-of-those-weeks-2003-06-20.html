a dream?(sorry so short been one of those weeks) Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #328 Date: 6/20/03 Location: Venom's housre The aftermath of Evolution had pass, Xodus face defeat in a hard fought match, against Price, and his collaborate John Zero. I must admit, Price was probably one of my toughest opponents, gave Dynamite X and I a ran for our money. Now I look to the future, June 27th 2003 against Ice, well since being summer; he is ready for a melt down. I will give the kid credit, I seen him in action, he will be a challenge. However, I like challenge’s it helps me out mentally and psychically. So far, its been one of those weeks, Jennifer spent most of the time in the bathroom sick. She went to the doctor’s yesterday, and is one week pregnant. “I, hope we have a daughter.” she said. “Would it be cool, Venom you already have a son, maybe this you will have a daughter.”She just looked at me, she had your hand on her stomach, it would be cool if it’s a girl, so I can keep away all the boys when she is 16. But I had more things on my mind, Roz is injured so it’s up to me and X now to carry the name Xodus till he returns. Maybe win a few titles while he is gone, to show the T.U.E and the Nevermore’s that Xodus, is a force to be wrecked with. “Hey, Jennifer I’m going to the gym to work out, you going to be ok?” she turned and nodded her head yes. So I went got my gear to work out in, and headed off to the gym. Thirty minutes later, I arrived at Total Workout just a few miles from my home. I entered and gave the woman, my id number. As usually the place was busy, but it did happen to show some life on the walls having poster’s up me up on it. One of me winning the platinum title, what a match that was. Well, I went to do some curls, when a old friend of mine walked in, but I’m no going to mention his name. “Venom, it’s been awhile, how have you been?” I told him I was doing well, that I was preparing myself for another hard match with this Ice person. He, was looking better than the last time I seen him which was a few years ago. I was kind of shocked to see him in this part of town. My old friend did not stay long, and neither did I, it was getting somewhat boring here. Therefore, I made my way back home. Just to see Jennifer past out in bed, she had a rough week. Not being able to be in mine and X’s corner at evolution. So I went back to the living room, to watch some T.V. but all of a sudden I got a strange chill down my back, there wasn’t any windows open. Then the lights started to flicker, which seem odd, I just put new light bulbs in. “What the hell, is happening here?” As I stood up, a image appeared out of my wall, I could look threw this image, it was the ghost of Cobra. I was thinking to myself am I going freaking nuts here. Cobra, well the ghost of Cobra made his way towards me, I moved away from, rubbing my eyes, I was I dreaming. “Venom, I come to you from the dead. I am here to tell that, not everything you see is real. Look harder, look in your heart. You will realize that you are stronger than ever, and success will soon come your way. You finally found happiness in Jennifer, and good friends in Dynamite and Roz. Don’t let it slip away.” Then Cobra disappeared just like that. What did he mean success will soon come? T.B.C
