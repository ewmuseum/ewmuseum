Burning Bridges

14 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/14/05 Outside New Orleans, 19.47, Virgil's eyes slowly adjusted to the darkness from that of sleep, his preternatural vision drawing a thin red contour around the various distractions that filled the room. He sat up in his bed, and pushed some hair out of his face, glowering. He loathed going to bed angry, especially at Shadow... although she'd convinced him to cool off and do it anyway. He reached over and pulled on the lamp string, causing a yellow light to spill into one of the several rooms of one of his several residences. Some might have considered Virgil slightly materialistic, with a house in most parts of the country, and to some extent, it was true. However, especially times like now. Though he was only frustrated at Shadow slightly for being with his stable leader, Leinad Soma, nearly all the time, he found himself disliking Soma more every night. Of course, the two of them had never actually gotten along in any sense. The day they'd met, there was nearly a cataclysmic fight in the desert, although somehow, Soma had convinced him that it would be more beneficial to solve their differences diplomatically. Exactly how this would have happened had he not brought up Shadow was beyond Virgil, though he supposed it wasn't entirely a horrible thing. Rex Deus, small as it was, still carried some sort of clout and favor among Global, something that he would need to have any sort of influence. Somehow, it hadn't occurred to him that he might be putting himself in danger by appearing on national television, but centuries of ennui had gifted him with a sense of danger. Of course, he'd been the one to seek Soma out. He should have known what he was getting into by associating with him. More than likely, Soma considered him a simple pawn to use in whatever 'greater purpose' it was that he was pursuing. Of course, Virgil hadn't had much interaction with Soma, or anyone in Global save Shadow, so there was little way for him to know. His curiosity did pull at his thoughts sometimes. Soma was a strange being, after all. Virgil wanted to understand exactly what he was, what he was capable of... but more importantly, he wanted to understand how he could become just as powerful. After all, Shadow did mention staying up to see the sunrise... obviously, it was possible. And thus, his intent to remain in Rex Deus was again He still found it rather difficult to coexist with Soma, for the simple fact that he apparently could not keep his hands from Shadow's lithe form. While Virgil could understand such an inclination, it couldn't keep his rather severe jealousy in check. Being alone for some two hundred years hadn't left him in much mood for sharing love, nor even lust, which seemed to be Soma's only interest. Virgil muttered to himself as he stepped out of his bedroom, slipping into a pair of leather pants, part of his usual attire. The last rays of pink and orange were fading over the horizon, and darkness was beginning to overtake the bayou behind his New Orleans estate. Something out of place caught his eye, as he quickly realized he was once again alone. A small note, stuck to the bottom of a mirror. Virgil-- My apologies- Soma called at the last minute about business, and I fear that the punishment for offending him is greater than that of making you jealous. Listen well, though- I will see you in Las Vegas, and I hope you will be as assured of my feelings then as you are now. I will remember how I felt last night. Let us hope, then, that you will remember what you felt as well. Amiti’s, je reste la tienne, Lia He smiled, a truly rare occurrence, especially one that wasn't laced with some sort of malice. No, at the moment, he was rather pleased. Suddenly, time seemed to stop, as his rather powerful ears picked up a rather familiar click sound in the bayou. He only had an instant to fall backward in an evasive maneuver before the plate glass window to the balcony suddenly splintered into a million pieces, and a small fireball erupted from the wall standing in the bullet's path. Virgil rolled to his knees, and dove into the bathroom. He could hear nothing else, and could only assume that his would-be assassin was waiting for another shot. The report was loud enough to prove that it could only be a rifle, and a large one at that... and the trademark burst of fire upon impact could only be a dragonsbreath round, probably the only bullet Virgil would bother dodging. This made things rather difficult, considering he now had three stories to negotiate, in a house full of windows that faced the west, where the shot originated. A plan quickly formed in his mind, and he felt the familiar burning sensation as his muscles tensed with abnormal power, before dashing across the living room, and leaping from the balcony, as another burst of fire shot up from the wall to the left of him. He grabbed hold of a cypress branch, and quickly let go, bracing his arms in front of him, and crashing through the rest of the branches, more or less breaking his fall. Upon landing, he hit the ground quickly, diving to the left, landing near the water. He could smell his assailant, about two hundred yards away... the direction wasn't quite clear. He gritted his teeth, and suddenly, conjured an illusionary form of himself. The doppelganger stepped out from behind the thick collection of trees, and immediately, a bullet went straight through him, and Virgil forced him to react accordingly. However, it wouldn't work... the scent in Virgil's nostrils, aside from swamp gas, was laced with a small amount of Kindred blood, thus likely a ghoul. He concentrated for a few seconds, before seven more copies appeared, much more detailed than the last. Now that Virgil had a reasonably good idea where the attacker was positioned, it would be simple. He gave his copies his scent, his mannerisms, and to the sniping ghoul, his powers. A wry grin crept across his face, as his copies darted up the hill toward the ghoul. He, of course, took an alternate route, and heard another shot fired, a miss this time. Shortly, he was positioned behind another cyprus tree, watching gleefully as the ghoul hurriedly reloaded his rifle. Virgil crouched low, sneaking carefully up behind him, as all seven of his clones reached the top of the hill, and promptly vanished, as the ghoul fell backward, holding his arms up in defense. Virgil hovered over him, waiting for him to relax, which he seemed to refuse to do. With adrenaline coursing through him, Virgil was in no mood to be patient. He took a step forward, bringing his sharpened claws down to the ghoul's stomach, pressing with all of his strength to force his hand straight through until he felt the cool soil on his fingertips. The ghoul let out a gurgling screech of pain, but was quickly silenced as Virgil clenched his hand, and pulled his arm out, taking a few organs he couldn't quite identify with him. Virgil reached down, and grabbed him by the hair, pulling the limp body up to stare into the rolled back eyes with a sneer, before tossing it over the hill, watching the visceral spectacle tumbled down the incline, into the foul-smelling water, disturbing the gases underneath the surface. Virgil reached down, and grabbed the large rifle, his strength making it seem like a feather in his arms, and he aimed carefully at the ripples in the water's surface, and fired. An orange blast of flame shot into the night sky, charring the spindly leaves of the cypress that hung over the swampy waters. As the smell of burnt wood and flesh met his nose, he smiled. ------------ Shanghai, 22.07, a warehouse at the shipyards, July 12th 'You understand, mister Ambrosi, that we will not tolerate this. We had a deal.' Wong's syllables were clipped and severe, but this time, Virgil was hardly in a mood to care. After receiving word that his ghoul ship crew had all been captured and executed by the Chinese government, he was out of patience for Cathayans and their threats. 'We did, Wong. You agreed to protect my crew at whatever cost, in exchange for the warheads, firearms, and conventional explosives that you required. I cannot easily replace them, despite what you seem to think. Your negligence has inconvenienced me severely.' Virgil narrowed his eyes threateningly, prepared to settle this however possible. In all honesty, it wasn't so much the fact that his crew was dead. International arms smuggling may have been his vice of choice, but the real reason was, of course, Shadow. Virgil had been getting increasingly irritated with the Cathayans refusing to change any facet of their schedule, which often forced him to deal with overseeing shipments when he should have been with her... instead, she was being lusted after by Soma, and he was annoyed in Seattle. 'We are not negligent. Your crew obviously made some mistake in their delivery procedure.' Virgil had a return fire ready for that one. 'And you let them rot in prison for a week.' Wong's countenance remained calm and stoic, masking the irritation that was seething underneath. He would likely be on the receiving end of punishment from this deal going sour, but would deal with that when it came. He had only found it personally acceptable to inform Virgil of his similar fate. 'We are not the western Anarchs, mister Ambrosi. We cannot simply level a prison for your convenience.' Virgil pointed an accusing finger at Wong, gritting his teeth. 'And thus you violated the terms of our agreement. Our association is terminated, and this is not negotiable. I appreciate your excuses, but I will not change my mind about this. Good evening, Wong.' Virgil turned to walk out of the rather bleak office on the waterfront, and upon his hand touching the doorknob, Wong shook his head. 'You know, Virgil, that my superiors will not be pleased with you.' Virgil threw open the door, and turned back slightly. 'Then let them come find me.' Virgil hastily made his way out of the warehouse after that, dodging everyone that he saw, in a quick attempt to get to the road as soon as possible, and a taxi to the airport shortly afterward. There would probably be a furor following him all the way out of Asia, and to the west coast of the United States as well... but he was planning on Cathayan bureaucracy giving him at least a few hours... as for what would be done when he got settled... that would be slightly more uncertain.
