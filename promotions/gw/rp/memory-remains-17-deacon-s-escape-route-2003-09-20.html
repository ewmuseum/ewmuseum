Memory Remains #17(Deacon's escape route)

20 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/20 Deacon Holmes stands in the center of a room filled with computers and other anonymous devices from which he’s just stolen a disc. A triumphant smile crosses his lips as he informs his partner, John Zero, “I got it.” over his earpiece. Holmes tucks the disc safely away inside his hoodie just as Zero replies “Good, now grab it and get back here.” At that very instant all the lights in the building go down and are replaced by intermittently flashing red ones. Accompanying the flashing lights are high pitched ear splitting sirens, along with a computerized voice repeating the words “Intruder alert.” over and over. “Holy shit!” echoes through Holmes? earpiece just as the words come out of his mouth as well. Slowly, the doorknob to the room begins to rotate and as it comes slightly ajar Holmes can make out about four bodies on the other side, most likely carrying guns. With no hesitation Deacon charges toward the door at full gallop before leaping into the air and thrusting both of his feet forward into it, causing the men on the other side to make no further progress as they were preparing to throw it open. Due to the door being pushed from both sides with such force, it remains relatively still, causing the bodies on each side to bounce backward. As Holmes kicks up from his back he notices the men once again preparing to rush the door from the crack and charges forward once again, this time hitting the door shoulder first before they get a chance to make a move then turning to where his back is flat against the door and pushing it the rest of the way shut, locking it immediately. Realizing his two hundred and four pound frame won’t do much in the way of keeping them out, Deacon quickly grabs a chair and props it under the doorknob in order to further impede his pursuers? progress. He then hurriedly looks around the room for a possible means of escape. After a matter of moments, the men on the other side of the door realize they’ve been locked out and just then a gunshot rocks the knob, making the lock useless. Much to their dismay they encounter Holmes? secondary means of stalling them, the chair propped under the now loosely hanging doorknob. Loud banging can be heard outside the door, along with a short, sharp, screeching sound as the chair’s legs scrape the floor as it slides forward from the blows to the door, which is slowly coming unfastened. In the middle of all this, Deacon attempts unsuccessfully to climb back up into the vent. “Deac, man, get the hell outta there.” creeps through Holmes? earpiece in a low tone from Zero. After a few more less than satisfactory attempts Holmes screams back “I’m tryin”, I’m tryin?! I can’t reach the goddamn vent!” Realizing the door is no longer an option, and that there is no time to climb back up into the vents, Deacon looks toward the other end of the room. “Don’t tell me, of all the times, this is when you need the fucking boost!” blares back in through Holmes? earpiece. Looking back at the door Holmes replies “Yes, yes it is. Shit!” A section of the wall across the room is a long, narrow piece of glass that looks out into a hallway lined with several closed doors. Surveying his options once more, Deacon finally makes a decision. He races away from the door to the opposite side of the room gaining more speed with each oncoming step, the sound of the door opening to his back. The door to the room flies open and four gunshots immediately ring out, one from each man behind him. Fortunately all four are off target and wind up hitting the pane of glass which Holmes is fast approaching, causing it to spider. Once within range Deacon, crossing his arms in front of him, leaps into the air and dives through the partially shattered glass. After landing on the other side he sits with his back to the wall as a means of taking cover from all the gunfire coming from inside the room. After a moment the firing stops as the men either notice that Holmes never got up or they just plain ran out of bullets. Nervously looking around, Deacon finds a fire extinguisher sitting inside of a small shelf just big enough for it with a glass door right next to him. He quickly leans over to his left and opens the small door, removing the fire extinguisher. While holding the heavy metal canister in his lap he rotates it to it’s back a reads a safety warning. “Warning, contents under pressure... this could be interesting.” Drawing one of his twin custom made desert eagles from the holster at the back of his waist, Deacon Holmes scampers to his feet to find the four lab coat clad men slowly approaching the opening. Both sides freeze momentarily, taking note of their relatively close proximity to each other. Just as they begin to raise their guns Deacon flings the fire extinguisher into the room causing it to hit one of the men in the chest, sending him to the floor. The other three take note of this and turn their attention to their fallen compatriot. Realizing he’s caught them off guard, a smile forms on Holmes? face as he twirls his pistol around his finger before whistling to regain their attention. Flashing a toothy grin Deacon watches as the eyes of all four men grow wide before squeezing the trigger of his desert eagle, expelling a fifty caliber shell punctuated by an exorbitantly loud, resounding boom. Upon collision with the extinguisher the bullet explodes, causing the pressurized contents of the canister to erupt. Holmes quickly collapses to the floor in order to avoid the flying chunks of brightly red painted metal. After a decidedly long enough interval Deacon rises to his feet and looks inside the room to find all four men laid unconscious on the floor under a white haze, three of which look badly wounded. “Talk to me Deac!” Exclaims Zero, probably hearing much of what just went on. After making sure the coast is clear Holmes moves down the hallway, responding “It’s cool man, those labcoats came back with guns. They ain’t goin nowhere for a while.” as he checks each door he passes; all of them locked. “You still stuck?” Inquires Zero, probably realizing Deacon hasn’t moved far from where he began. “Yep.” responds Holmes, attempting to twist the knob of a door at the center of the hall. Several feet behind him a door can be heard creaking open. Deacon whirls around, gun drawn, stopping with it’s barrel resting pointed at the slowly opening door. A man looking to be in his mid twenties wearing a pair of gray coveralls steps halfway out of the door before stopping dead in his tracks, eyes wide open after noticing Holmes standing in the middle of the hall with a hand cannon trained on his forehead. He slowly raises his hands into the air to show that he doesn’t have anything then quickly takes off back where he came from, slamming the door behind him. Holmes sets free a sigh of relief as his arm falls to his side and his shoulders drop. Seconds later the very same door bursts open just as Holmes begins to walk away. The same man he was certain had run away in fear instead opted to alert the nearby guards and was now ushering them through the door. One by one each of the eight men toting automatic weapons march out into the hall eyeing Holmes, who is already running full speed as hard as he possibly can in the opposite direction. He looks over his shoulder only long enough to notice the men beginning to take aim and realizes that he must do something fast. Thinking quickly Deacon redraws his pistol and empties three rounds into the oncoming door at the end of the hall, each bullet striking the door in one of it’s three hinges and blowing them off the wall. He then dives forward and rotates his body in mid air while drawing the second of his two pistols so that his back is back is the first thing to strike the door, causing it to fall backwards amidst a hail of gunfire from both directions. Holmes lands back first on a now collapsed door with the lab’s security squad at the other end of the hall still firing at him. In a panic Deacon aims above the men, and taking aim at the ceiling, shoots out several light fixtures to collapse and for jagged shards of glass to rain down on their heads. Examining the situation, Deacon notices that the shower of debris he created did little in the way of deterring his pursuers and hastily springs back up to his feet before ducking into the room. Once inside he grabs the top end of the door and lifts it back into the doorway, giving it a lunging kick to help hold it in place. Nervously, Holmes looks around and finds a chair to prop under the doorknob to further hinder progress from the outside. He then races across the room and locks the other door. With a temporary means of stalling any attackers now in place, Deacon decides to take another stab at climbing up into the ventilation shafts after shooting the cover off. Growing more frustrated with each unsuccessful attempt, missing the opening by mere inches each time, and to make a bad situation worse, both doors are beginning to move. Light from the hall can been seen through a small opening as the door pushes against the chair and the knob of the other turns back and forth repeatedly but to no avail as it is locked. In the center of the room stands Holmes, wearing a facial expression closely resembling that of a woodland creature caught in the headlights of an oncoming semi or a small child who knows he’s just done something wrong and is simply waiting for the belt to snap across his body. Nervously looking back and forth between the two doors, each of which holds his impending demise, Deacon manages to do nothing other than grunt three words to himself; “Fuck, fuck, fuck!” while wiping the sweat from his brow. Seemingly giving up, his shoulders drop as he continues “How tha hell am I supposed to get back in there?” Then through his earpiece comes just the bit of advice he needed; “Is there a chair you could use?” somewhat distorted by all of the commotion going on. Looking toward the slowly budging chair holding security at bay a mischievous smile forms on Deacon’s face as he wonders “Why didn’t I think of that?” out loud. Hearing Holmes? reaction to this development, John chirps back, “That’s why I’m the supervisor and you’re the grunt, now get your ass into gear.” Deacon charges toward the slowly opening unhinged door which now has several fingers clinging around the edges of it. Just before reaching it Holmes throws his right leg out in front of him and thrusts it into the door, causing it to shut completely and crush several fingers in the process. “I’m movin”, I’m movin?!” he yells into his microphone as he snatches the chair from under the knob and races back across the room with it in his hand. Deacon tosses it out ahead of himself, causing it to slide across the floor rotating slowly. Just then both doors burst open with a squadron of armed guards funneling in from each of the two openings. Without a second to spare Holmes bounds onto the seat of the sliding chair, then, almost at the exact instant his feet hit it, Deacon springs back into the air and grabs the edge of the opening and quickly hoists himself up inside just in the nick of time. Zero’s voice crackles through Holmes? earpiece once, this time saying “So am I.” The gunshots ringing out below Holmes that fill the vent shaft with holes temporarily distract him as he is far to busy scurrying through the vents on all fours while trying not to get shot to pay much attention to the voice in his ear. Suddenly what John just told him registers. “What!”” Holmes exclaims, posting up in a seemingly safe area over a room across the hall. “I’m gonna go get the car.” replies Zero. Holmes looks to his left and right in the darkness of the vent before asking “What about me?” worriedly. “You’re a big boy now, Deac. I still have you on mic.” is Zero’s reply. “Alright, alright.” groans Holmes as he continues crawling through the vents. Deacon works his way down a long straight path treading softly so as not to make a lot of noise and trying his best to avoid any rats that may be nesting in the vents. Just when things seems to be settling down, one of the metal panels that make up the floor of the ventilation shaft falls to the floor just and Holmes puts his hands on it, causing him to fall to the floor below in a cloud of dust and debris. Deacon lands hard on the fallen metal panel which knocks the wind out of him, leaving him in a heap on the floor gasping for breath and chocking on the dust that now covers him. After a few minutes Holmes finally pulls himself together and rolls over to find himself staring down the barrels of two guns, each just inches away from his face. He crawls to his knees but is then yanked the rest of the way up by his dreadlocks. One of the men who was standing over him remarks “All this trouble for some punk kid?” to his partner, causing him to chuckle. “How old are you kid, you can’t be much older than eighteen.” continues the man, this time to Holmes. Deacon’s eyebrows arch inward and a scowl forms on his face as he bellows “I’m nineteen years old, but I’m a nineteen year old son of a bitch!” before kicking his leg out behind him, hitting the man back there square in the crotch and dropping him to the ground. Deacon then grabs the guard in front of him by the arm he’s holding the gun with and spins behind him, twisting the arm in the process. Once there Holmes kicks the man in the back of his left knee causing him to kneel then pulls his head back by his scalp. Holmes leans in and whispers “Lights out bitch.” into his ear before pressing the mans’s index finger down, which was still on the trigger, ejecting a bullet from the barrel and directly into his skull. “No brainer.” Holmes mumbles to himself admiring his handy work. He then walks over toward the second guard who is trying to reach for the pistol he dropped when he fell. Deacon draws one of his guns and aims it dead center between the man’s legs and asks “What do you want in life?” The man immediately stops in his tracks. Holmes smirks at his temporary position of power and orders the man to stand up. His smirk blossoms into a full blown grin as he watches with delight as the obviously shaken man makes his way to his feet, both arms raised into ninety degree angles. After watching him squirm Deacon cocks back and pistol whips the guard so fiercely that he is knocked unconscious, then gives him a swift kick in the ribs for good measure before tearing off down the hallway so as to avoid any back up that may have been called. Holmes turns on the jets as he speeds down the hallway, making a mad dash through the corridor. As he approaches a section in which he can continue either continue to go straight or opt to go left or right the thick adrenaline casing subsides and a though is finally able to penetrate his skull. Stopping in the center of the intersection Deacon suddenly realizes he has absolutely no clue where he is going. Holmes screams “Yo John!” in a panic into his microphone. “What is it Deacon?” questions John in a voice that would suggest he’s in the middle of something. The footsteps of several people all running in tune with each other can be heard echoing through the hallway to Deacon’s left. He glances to his left and right suspiciously before quickly ducking into a nearby closet filled with cleaning supplies. Through the slits in the door Holmes watches as eight men high tail it through the hallway with guns drawn. A sigh of relief leaves his body as they pass. Deciding it’s now a safe time to continue his conversation with Zero, Holmes informs his partner “I need a lil direction.” as he steps back out into the narrow hallway after making sure the coast is clear. “Ok, you’re gonna wanna take a left, the next two rights, then another left.” comes back through the earpiece. Deacon nods and responds “Aight.” before looking upward at the shaft running across the ceiling above him. Just as he prepares to make his move “Hey Deac?” blares through his earpiece. Somewhat annoyed at the untimely interruption Holmes replies “Yeah?” only to be further harassed by Zero saying “Hurry your ass up, I need you to pick this lock.” Deacon’s face contorts with rage as he screams back “What!”! See man, see what I mean!” It’s cause I’m black, admit it. ADMIT IT!” Holmes? excitement obviously struck John as funny since he answers back “I got it man. Hurry up though.” while still chuckling. Deacon’s eyes widen and as nostrils flare as he exhales deeply while shaking his head. Having cleared his head, Deacon looks upward, then to the wall, then upward once more. A slight nod signaling to himself that he knows what he has to do is the only motion that takes place before he makes his move. Holmes runs at the wall, leaping into the air once nearby, then bounds off of it and places one of his legs on the side of the shaft while the other is propped against a higher section of the wall, lodging his body in between the shaft and the wall. Like sap down tree bark, Holmes feels himself slowly beginning to slide down and musters every ounce of strength he possesses to pull the vent cover off the side of the shaft, dropping it to the ground below. Deacon then pulls his right leg back away from the side of the shaft, placing it against the wall along with his left and then grabs the floor of the small opening with both hands. He then pushes off the wall with both legs and slide back into the cramped, hanging tunnels. Following John’s directions, Deacon comes to dead end with the opening covered by a vent. “Last stop on the get tha fuck outta here express.” mutters Holmes as he pushes the grate off then catches it before it falls to the floor so as not to alert any guards that might be nearby. He then leans forward and surveys the ground below for potential problems. Holmes then slides back inside as “Deac?” comes in through his earpiece. After giving himself a little space to work with Deacon builds up what little speed he can and catapults himself out of the shaft head first. Then flipping in mid air while replying “Yeah?” to John, he manages to land perched on top of a desk. Holmes takes a quick look to his left and right to make sure that the room is indeed empty before hopping down and crouching under the desk. “Where you at?” questions Zero. Hearing the door to the room creak open, Deacon whispers “Just reached the ground.” softly into the microphone. “Alright, with your back to the vent, go straight and follow the path of bodies. I’m one room from the exit. I’ll meet you outside.” Is the set of directions that come back through the earpiece. Holmes crawls from underneath the desk and watches silently as the guard moves through the dimly lit room. After a rather lax inspection of the area the guard begins making his way back across the room toward the door. As he crosses the desk Deacon decides to make his move and in one motion hops on top of the desk then dives off, tackling him to the floor. Once there Deacon draws one of his pistols and, grabbing it by the barrel, slams it into the mans skull, knocking him unconscious. He then checks the man’s pockets for anything of value, finding nothing, he steals his watch before surreptitiously moving through the room and out the door. Upon opening the door Deacon sees a path on bodies strewn throughout the hall, all either unconscious or dead. Looking around and finding the coast to seem clear, mainly due to the fact that John most likely either killed or incapacitated everyone in the surrounding area. Holmes walks through the hall stepping in between bodies along the way, studying each as he passes while wondering what John must have done to them. He then stops in front of one with a gaping laceration in his neck. Deacon’s face contorts as if the room were filled with sulfur as he remarks “Now that is some fucked up repugnant shit John.” In the distance footsteps can be heard rumbling through the hallway. A cold chill shoots through Deacon’s spine as he listens closely as turns around to hear the footsteps get closer, growing louder as they do so. Holmes turns back around and flies through the hall like a bat out of hell. “FUCK JOHN, get the DAMN car! I think the fucking army is behind me.” His head falls backwards somewhat as he pokes his chest out and pumps his arms running as fast as he can. The pace of the footsteps behind him picks up as they hear the loud sound of Deacon bucking through the hall. “Almost there D.” comes back through in bated breaths as John also sounds like he’s on the run. “Hurry tha fuck up John.!” exclaims Holmes after rounding a corner. Deacon sees nothing ahead of him but the exit, “Dig Deacon dig!” he whispers to himself as he manages to squeeze out a little extra speed as he bursts through the door and into the desert like parking lot. “Where are you John, I’m here and you’re not...” as he makes his way into the lot just as “FREEZE” is heard shouted from behind him along with the sound of several guns being cocked. Deacon sighs and his shoulders drop in defeat before raising both his hands into the air palms open. His head begins to shake as he considers that this may be his final day on earth. “What a way to go, death by inept security firing squad.” He squawks to the men behind him, the only reply is a few more guns being cocked. Then out of nowhere a black blur cuts across the lot, creating a whirlwind of sand in it’s wake, aimed straight for Holmes. The guards behind him begin to grow curious as to just what exactly is going on. A slight grin crosses Deacon’s lips, John is on his way. No more than a few feet away from Holmes the flash comes to an abrupt stop as it spins ninety degrees to it’s side revealing it to be a black Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder driven by none other than John Zero who is screaming “DOWN, DOWN, DOWN! Get down D!” at the top of his lungs as he swings the passenger side door open. Deacon dives onto the ground and slides behind the car door as the sound of gunfire fills the air. Holmes looks up to find Zero staring down at him with a rather snide expression on his face. He then smiles as he goes on to ask “Miss me?” Deacon responds irreverently “Yeah, sure, whatever man.” then asks “What are we gonna do about those assholes?” John reaches into the glove compartment and removes a small black box with a lone circular red button in the center of it, which he holds up with a smile, knowing that it’s one of Deacon’s favorite things. Holmes smiles as well then covers his head and ears with his arms as John pushes the button, causing the front half of the building to erupt in a burst of bright red flames and billowing clouds of smoke. As Deacon climbs into the car he notices a few of the guards getting up from the explosion with their uniforms engulfed in flames run around in a panic while other lay unconscious and covered by debris. He smirks at the sight as he mutters “Stop, drop, and roll.” while shutting the door. Exhausted he leans back into his seat and closes his eyes with John looking at him suspiciously as they speed off through the lot. “You have the disc, Deac?” He slits his eyes open and looks over at Zero before searching all of his pockets. He then returns a set of new widened eyes to John as he informs him “Shit, man, I think I dropped it.” Suddenly the car jerks to the left as John prepares to make a U turn, Deacon looks on while laughing hysterically as Zero slams his fist into the steering wheel in disgust. “Gotcha!” He exclaims while pulling the disc out of his pocket and showing it to John who fights to hold off a smile. “Asshole. Hold on.” he retorts as he flips the top of the car’s gearshifter and mashes down a little red button. The car jerks forward with a tremendous burst of speed. All of a sudden everything makes sense. “MOTHERFUCKER! That’s how you beat me to the HQ! I knew it, I knew it, I knew it!” exclaims Holmes, now obviously excited. Zero can do nothing but laugh as the two men speed off.
