Forsaken Pt.2

14 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-14-04 Forsaken Pt.2 Prologue. Ever wonder why this world has not gone to hell? Why the angels above have not swooped down and feel? Why have the devils below not risen and fled? Or is it all just a nother fiction story told for the head? In my eyes I see the world at a blank void. I question destiny and life, but I value both. An emptiness so strong and compact it makes me sweat, but so full of energy it makes me want to yell for joy. I see these as things that make me something else, just as I see them as thing that make me want to die. Is their reason to end your life? Is their another one after thus? Or will the world keep spinning, and you only be a matter of remains in a box left to be six feet under? Story Continues… Waking in his own vomit, Jesse rolls over and stares up at the ceiling. A solid white ceiling of void, it has no stains, no dots or speckles, no texture of any kind, just flat and plain. Reaching over for something to push himself up, he grabs thin air and his hand crashes down on the bed. Looking around he is in a room with only one bed, and an IV hooked to him but not connected to anything. Standing up he puts he catches his balance before he falls back down. Fortunately he’s not medicated or he’d have some real trouble. Walking forward he looks towards the door which is open, and so leaves the room. Where am I? What is this place? How did I get here? Walking out into an empty hallway, Jesse notices that he’s in a hospital. Lines on the floor, signs on the walls showing “Patience’s Area” He walks down the empty hallway, his silhouette from the light behind him shines onto the floor. He looks to see where the light is coming, but it’s only a room lit up. Turning towards it he heads in and finds what must be the most bizarre things ever in a hospital. Jars upon jars of body parts and fluids are lined on the wall, in the middle of the room is a metal table with blood stains on it. Jesse walks past the table and towards a desk in the far left corner. A computer sits, turned off of course, and papers upon papers are lying everywhere. The keyboard has a select few folders on it, with a cabinet near the folder exposing many more folders inside of it. Looking at the papers Jesse realized their apart of a journal of some sort. Reading one he finds something interesting. It’s now November 10, 2004, and the experiments we just did came back negative. Our research over the past 2 years has been worth nothing, and I’m beginning to feel pressured by these morons whom I work with. I hope to god I die, just so I can escape this prison I’m to work in. God save me! Staring at the paper with his eyebrows showing questions running through his head he drops the papers and heads to the cabinet. He looks for his name, as a gut instinct that he may be in here for a reason. Nope, not in this one… Ah, maybe this! Opening up the second cabinet he finds a location of folders with the label “F” on the outside of a tab. Finding his name ‘Fristik’ he pulls out the papers and walks over to the blood stained metal table. Laying the folder down he opens it after taking a deep breath and pulls out three yellow papers. Statistics, Notes, and Chemical Research Checkup… Whoa, what the fuck did they do to me? Opening up the Statistics, Jesse first sees something that catches his eye without taking a second glance. His birth date, and then… his death date. Staring at it, the paper reads he died November 12, 2004 at 4:13 A.M. At the bottom of the paper Jesse notices a log on what happened and reads it to himself. After a wicked outrage, his violent attacks had led us to use Novocain on him with a depressant drug, however it only caused for him to lose thought focus and end up choking the doctor whom administrated the IV in his arm… Looking down at his arm he rips the IV out and tosses it on the floor realizing it was meant to seduce him to a coma or death, and that Jesse did not want to think secondly about. After his death the building was evacuated and left to be destroyed on October 15, 2004. Mr. Fristik’s body will be forgotten and all traces of him as well. The newspaper will spill that his death was an accident and that he was involved in a terrorist attack. Why I’m writing this I know not, but I do realize that the medicine we used on him mixed with the new experiments were what caused these results. Mr. Fristik was perfectly healthy until we administrated our treatments. For that, I blame myself for his death. Smiling with laughter following, Jesse hints at what may be a new beginning. He rips the papers up and toss them up in the air, watching them descend onto the table in which someone was butchered. He was now unknown, nobody knew whom he was, nobody would guess if he was alive or not, but… one thing still remained unanswered and lacking recognition in his mind? Who did this to me? Not knowing that hurt him where it counted. He had to thank these people whom saved him, and the only way to do that was to repay the favor. It’s time I make my acquaintance with these men… They deserve at least that much. Laughing as he walked out of the room he got to the end of the hallway and turned heading toward a different light, and outside light. Walking to it, he opens up a door, and walks into a loading area meant for the ambulance. His eyes gazed up at a dark dull sky full of gray clouds of what would be rain, and he grinned. It’s time to let these men meet, the face they never seen. Walking out through the empty parking lot Jesse keeps walking until he disappears into the horizon of trees and distance, so far that the eye itself could not see.
