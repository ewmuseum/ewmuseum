Downfall: Meltdown

15 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/11/2005 The bathroom door slammed shut behind her with a sense of finality. Dawn pressed her back to the cool wood and slid slowly down until she was kneeling on the emerald green tiles of her bathroom floor. Twilight was coming and the last rays of dying sunset spilled through the bathroom window, reflecting off the pale blue walls and the porcelain tub, giving the space a light of its own. Dawn pressed her hands to her face, digging the heels of her palms into her eyes to forgo the inevitable spill of tears that threatened to overcome her. How long had it been? For the last several months, this had been her routine. She would work hard, train hard and focus hard on her goals’only to fail again and again, to constantly come up short when it came to everything she wanted. She’d throw her stuff into the trunk of her car, slip into the driver’s seat and make the grueling drive home with only herself for company’and then’when she got home, the first thing she would do is lock herself in the master bed room. Where no one else could see her, the Steel Rose would cry. Dawn’s shoulders shook as tears leaked around her palms, spilling down her cheeks and spattering against dark cargos or the hard tile floor beneath her. Her mouth twisted in a sneer of self loathing even as she fought against the tears that came on regardless. She hated to be weak, hated this powerless feeling of loss and failure that came with everything she did with her life. Even Lia was gone now’and not even God knew when she would return. “I’ll wait?” Dawn choked past the lump in her throat, “I promised I’d wait.” But the words seemed hollow, even to her. For all she knew, Lia might not return until Dawn was old and grey, with most of the light having passed from her eyes. “Failure?” The clung to her mind and to her ears like a black tar, clouding her mind and making focus all but impossible’except where her own failures came to mind. For Dawn the honeymoon was over, no magazines called for her anymore, no wrestling website sought her out for interviews or articles. She was the 2005 Mistress of the Mat for sure’but she had failed to capitalize, and no one wanted to write stories about the girl that might have been. Heaving herself up from the floor, Dawn staggered over to the sink and rested her hands against the sides for support. The cold porcelain dug into the palms of her hands, but she couldn’t tear her eyes from the face in the mirror. Her dyed black hair hung limply in her face, lanky and greasy from the blood and sweat she’d not had time to wash out before coming home. Her normally bright green eyes were clouded, the skin around them red and puffy, with large black circles denoting exactly how well Ms. McFarlane slept these days. She rubbed one hand across her eyes, trying once again to stem the tide that seemed to have become a constant thing in her life. With a trembling hand she reached out to the mirror, pulling it open and gazing at the contents within. On the surface it looked like most people’s bathroom cabinet: Toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, bandaids, polysporin and a host of minor cosmetic effects. Dawn pushed that all to one side, digging past the box of band aids to find what she was looking for. When she found what she was looking for, Dawn pulled her hand back out of the cabinet, holding a small razorblade between her thumb and finger. Light didn’t exactly “gleam” off the metal surface of the blade, since it was older and a little darker. The blade was immaculately clean though, and to emphasize that cleanliness Dawn ran the faucet until it was scalding hot and held the blade under. She rolled up her right sleeve, staring in dismay at the myriad of small scabs and scars normally hidden by the leather bracer she was. “I’m sorry daddy?” The edge of the blade hovered for a moment at her wrist’and at that moment a freight train hit the door to her bathroom. The hinges splintered from the force of the blow, startling Dawn and sending the razorblade clattering to the ground as the door slammed against the far wall from where it had hung mere moments ago. Rolling her shoulder from the force of the blow, Caitlin Valentine stepped through the remains of Dawn’s bathroom door, her face a contorted mixture of anger and concern. Spying Dawn by the sink, she stepped over the wreckage of the door and strode purposefully across the floor towards her. “Caitlin what are y?” SMACK! The sheer force behind Caitlin’s slap snapped Dawn’s head to one side, a small droplet of blood flying from the corner of her mouth to splatter against the mirror. Dawn lurched against the sink and stumbled to her knees, staring up at Caitlin in a mixture of hurt and anger, one hand going to her already throbbing cheek. “What’what?” “Dawn Catherine McFarlane!” Caitlin practically yelled down at her host, “What in the name of all that’s holy do you think yer doing?” “I..I?” Dawn stammered, stumbling over the words before they even left her mouth. Her eyes dropped to the floor, a profound sense of shame creeping into her mind as a blush of embarrassment crept across her cheeks. “It’s the only way’the only way to make it stop’the only way to ease the pressure out?” Dawn’s words were halting and broken, but with each one the look of anger slid further from Caitlin’s face as she stared down at her best friend. “Dawn?” A third voice joined the conversation as Alexis peeked her head around the corner, concern etched across her youthful face. Dawn looked up for a moment and then looked away again, dropping her gaze down and to the side as a tremour shook across her broad shoulders. “Don’t look at me Alex please’I never wanted either of you to see’to see me like this?” Caitlin crouched low in front of Dawn, trying to catch her best friend’s eyes. “What can Ah do” Ah want to help you?” “I don’t know’I’I just don’t?” Dawn’s shoulders began to heave, her whole body starting to shudder and shake as it was wracked by powerful sobs. Caitlin wrapped her arms around Dawn, trying her best to whisper soothing things. “I’m so sorry Cait, I’m so sorry for everything?” Dawn wailed incoherently, clinging to Caitlin’s back as though she was holding on for dear life. Easing Dawn up to a standing position, Caitlin kept her arms firmly locked around her one time lover. “It’ll be okay sugar’we’ll figure out something?” ********************************************************************************* It had taken several long moments of crying before Dawn could be persuaded to move downstairs. Now the Steel Rose had curled up into as small a ball as she could manage, her arms wrapped around her legs and a blanket over her shoulders. Nearby a fire roared in the estate’s antique fireplace, the dancing flames illuminating the room in a warm glow. “I don’t know what to say?” She whispered, her eyes lost in the fire. “Y’all don’t have to say anything” yet,” Came the reply, as Caitlin leaned forward in the arm chair she occupied. “But at some point, yer gonna need to talk to somebody ya know” It doesn’t have to be me darlin??just somebody.” Dawn nodded slowly, seeming, and feeling, as though she were in some kind of daze. “I don’t know what to do C’I feel like I’ve lost everything’like I’m wandering, or floating adrift with no anchor’like?” “Like you’ve lost yourself,” Alex said quietly, watching Dawn from her place on the rug. “Like you’re not even sure who you are anymore, never mind what you’re doing right?” Dawn nodded again, turning her head from the fire to regard her young guest. “That sounds’about right.” Alex nodded, picking at the hem of her jeans. “It’s tough, when you feel like you’ve had everything torn away from you’makes it hard to get back to things.” “What should I do?” Dawn asked, her voice ringing hollowly throughout the room. “You need to rewind I think. Go as far back as you have to go, until you’re back at where you were last really happy’and then go from there.” Dawn lowered her eyes to the floor. “I can’t go back that far’the people who were in that lifetime’aren’t around anymore.” There was a firm knock on the door as Dawn finished her statement, drawing confused glances from two of the women therein. Caitlin, however, grinned triumphantly and strode towards the door. “Ah know what you mean Dawnie, and we can’t bring them back’but Ah think Ah know someone who might be able to fill in.” She pulled open the front door and bowed low to whoever was on the other side, her Texan drawl momentarily stifled as she stumbled over foreign words. “Konbanwa, Mifune-sama, Daijobu?” ((Good evening, Mr. Mifune, are you well?)) Dawn’s eyes grew as wide as saucers, her disbelieving gaze fastened firmly on the front door. Through the gaping portal stepped an older man, his black hair having long ago become streaked through with a steely grey, his beard and mustache suffering the same fate. For an Asian man he would be considered tall, and even under the simple clothes her wore, powerful muscles could be seen rippling across his frame. A small smile tugged at the corner of his lips as he surveyed the estate, nodding once to Caitlin. “I am well, thank you. I like what you have done with this place, Dawn-chan.” For her part, Dawn could only gape in disbelief at her sensei, painfully aware of her own vulnerability in front of a man she both loved and respected. “M-mifune-sama?” He grinned broadly at this, white teeth seeming to split the weathered skin of his face in two. “It has been too long Little Dragon?” And he held his arms out to her. Dawn hesitated for only a moment before flying across the room and into her Masters arms, her head pillowed against his board chest. Tears leaked unbidden from her eyes, but her body had passed the point of needing to sob uncontrollably. “Master?” She whispered hoarsely, “I’ve failed you.” “Nonsense. The only thing you have failed, is the unrealistic goals you have set for yourself. That is why I am here.” Dawn looked up at him in question, and Mifune looked over to Caitlin with a smile. “Ms. Valentine and I agreed that it would be best if I came to see you as soon as possible, and I think I may have some ideas as to how to start getting you back on track.” “Master?” Mifune stepped back, holding her at arms length and nodding firmly. “You will come with me back to Japan. You will learn from me once again, and hopefully we can get to the bottom of this pain that seems to eat at you so badly.” Dawn’s eyes went wide in shock. “But Master, what about’about Alex?” “Your friend may come with us, provided she wants that.” Alex nodded from her place, smiling a little mischievously. “I’ve always wanted to travel to Japan.” “Lia.” Dawn said suddenly, looking up at her master again. “I can’t go, Lia might come home and find me gone?” “Ah’ll stay and watch over things while yer gone Dawnie. Besides, somebodies got to watch over these great honking beasts you call dogs.” Caitlin leaned down to scratch behind Damek’s ears, earning a cross between a grunt and a sigh for her trouble. “I’I don’t know?” Dawn stammered. “I do.” Her master replied, guiding her gently towards the stairs. “Now come, we have much packing to do, and you must fill me in on what I have missed.”
