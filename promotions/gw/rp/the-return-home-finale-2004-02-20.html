The Return Home (Finale)

20 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 20 Seemingly oblivious is the manner to which life passes us by. Live for the moment, don’t waste a second, Carpe Diem, all common phrases that we have heard at one point in time in our lives. But seemingly, meaningless words to us until we experience a great loss. Such is the case of many, the seconds, the minutes, the days, the weeks, they all fly by seemingly unnoticed and unmissed. Just another part of growing up. And so it goes until the world takes change. Until the wicked twists of fate of life, throw a curveball that knocks you right back down to the lowest time of your life. When a loved one is dying, or has passed on, all that can be felt is sorrow and sadness, and a thirst. A severe thirst for those minutes that passed so carelessly, a chance to have those days again. A longing to touch them one more time, one more laugh, or maybe, just maybe the chance to say goodbye. The brothers were standing inside the large foyer of their uncle’s large mansion. It had been a week since their successful defeat of Ishuzu and her backstabbing ways. When they had first found out they were surprised. They had defeated the pathetic bounty hunter that she had sent to the USA to kill them before the PTC Tag Title Match. And she had made several other failed attempts at ruining the business affairs of their father. They just could not understand why, she would try again. But, alas, it did not matter, for her retribution and justice came swiftly. As the two brothers stood they awaited their uncle. He had run upstairs to grab something for them, they had no idea what the hell it was that he was getting, but as they stood, Ichiro found his mind wandering. He had received a call the other day, he didn’t answer it. He was more concentrated on the mission at hand, and he had yet to tell Jushin of the call either. The only thing that kept him from answering was the name on the phone. McCoy. Ichiro flipped his cell phone open, he shook his head as he saw the small notice on the LCD display. “One Missed Call.” He knew who it was, they were taking longer then expected, but business could wait, he needed to deal with the family aspect of his life first. He quickly flipped the phone shut, he looked at Jushin who was still waiting impatiently. Ichiro began to raise his head to the ceiling, he closed his eyes in a sort of prayer. As he did the memories began to flow through his mind. I know you're listening? The brothers ran up the stairs. They heard the clatter in the room in front of them, their enemies were preparing for battle. The two Nakazuras drew their pistols and came in firing. They were met by a minimal opposition, surprisingly small. The battle was swift and easy, their enemies fell, fighting in a vain battle that they were never meant to win. Come to me in my time of need, when times are tough and those around me bleed. The brothers left none alive that is none except for Ishuzu. She did not have a gun, nor did they think she could even use one. She was a true slut, with no work ethic. Her ways of seduction and treachery had brought shame to all Yakuza families, not just their own. But, her methods and sins meant nothing to the brothers, they were merely warriors of vengeance. They brought her to her knees, a position she was most certainly not unfamiliar with. However, the circumstances were slightly different this time. Let not my enemies conquer me, give me strength so I can see. She begged and pleaded, but she was yelling out to deaf ears. They did not care for her forgiveness nor her sorrow. They were here to avenge the death of their father, and that’s what they would do. They smiled as they saw the tears running down her cheek, they laughed at the pain. They held her at their mercy, savoring every moment of her pain and suffering. Guide me in this final flight, from all the darkness into light. They unlocked the safety of both of their pistols. They smiled and took places standing behind the kneeling woman. They rested their pistols against her raven black hair, her neck and head, trembling as they did so. They closed their eyes, and rested their fingers against the triggers of their pistols. They raised their heads to the heavens and began to whisper a slight prayer to the Lotus. Through desperate days, through all the haze. Carry me to my success.' The prayer finished and their eyes opened. The pain, the suffering, the cries, the pleas they were nothing now. Their eyes fixated and their mind made up, almost as if possessed they pulled the triggers of their pistols. Each sending a bullet, skyrocketing from the barrel. The blast rustled Ishuzu’s hair, not milliseconds before an explosion of blood spattered as the lead passed through her flesh. She feel limp, dead immediately, and with that the brothers clicked the safety and? Ichiro’s mind was interrupted by the sounds of his uncle’s heavy footsteps bounding down the stairs. His head returned to level and his eyes opened, he smiled as his uncle came forward, with two small packages. He smiled and handed them to Ichiro and Jushin, they graciously accepted them and opened them up. Inside revealed a golden medallion with a small inscription on the back. Ichiro knew what it was immediately, and could not have asked for more of a token of their father’s memory. Jushin smiled and wrapped his fist tightly around the medallion and pressing it to his heart. Ichiro smiled at the glimmering medallion. The front showed a lotus flower, beautiful engraved. The back was a small inscription with the words “Honor, Pride, Family” written. “My father used to tell us of these all the time when we were around. We had calmly asked him where he had gotten them one day, but he never answered. He merely turned away, as if to say we weren’t ready to know yet,” said Ichiro. “I remember,” said Jushin. “He never told us where they came from.” “They are ancient heirlooms of your family lineage. Directly passed down from father to son, for generations. Easily, a thousand years of family pride is what you now wear around your neck. They are now your burdens to bear,” answered Gohei. The brothers smiled, before embracing their uncle. He had given them everything they could have asked for on their visit home. They let go and each of the two Souls put their hand on their uncle’s shoulder. He put his hands over each of theirs, and then the Souls of the Black Lotus bowed to their uncle. An utmost sign of respect. “You now represent the family pride, Gohei. You are the one who must rebuild the olden powers of the Nakazura name, it is your duty. As much as we should stay to help, I’m afraid our hearts now lead us in a direction. We must return to America, to Global Wrestling, for that is our new home,” said Ichiro softly. “I understand,” answered Gohei. “I cannot say that I am not disappointed, but I must respect your wishes, as that is the way your father would have wanted it. And I promise to you, with those medallions as my word, I shall rebuild the great Nakazura empire. And you shall be honored rightfully as the heroes you are, please take care.” “Take care uncle,” both of the Souls replied. They took their hands from his shoulder and made their way slowly to their black Mitsubishi that was waiting outside. Jushin smiled at Ichiro and pulled out the remote starter, he pressed the button and the car roared to life in front of them. They stepped into the car, and Jushin quickly threw it into gear. The tires squealed and the smoke filled the air as they sped off into the evening. Gohei could only wave as his two nephews left his sight and his life once again. He would rebuild their father’s empire, he would restore his family’s honor. He had to and with Ishuzu out of the way, he now had the opportunity to mold his brother’s empire to what it always could have been. But, there was still one thing that troubled his mind, why would Ishuzu have attacked the name of Nakazura, knowing full well that her assassin in the USA had failed, and knowing full well both Ichiro and Jushin would be back in Tokyo. Inside the car, Jushin turned to his brother who seemed deep in thought. Ichiro turned down the radio and pulled out his cell phone. “Who the hell are you calling now” We’ve done our business here.” “I know, I’m calling long distance to the US,” answered Ichiro. “Who the hell are you calling in the US”!” demanded Jushin. “Christina McCoy.” Jushin smiled slightly as his brother turned away and listened to the dial tones. The automated voice server soon picked up and Ichiro waited to be patched through to Ms. McCoy’s office. He turned the volume up so his brother could also hear, finally after several minutes he heard the first voice since he originated the phone call. “This is Christina,” came the answer. “Ms. McCoy, it’s Ichiro, we need to talk. About, business.”
