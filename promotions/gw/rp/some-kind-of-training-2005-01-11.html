Some Kind of Training

11 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/11/05 OOC: This was posted on the backup promo board 'cause at the time this board was being a whore and a half, but I'm just posting it here as well now to make sure it gets seen and all. An old, rundown gym in The Bronx, New York. That was where Mindy and her older cousin Michael found themselves two days before the first match of Mindy's wrestling career would take place. Mindy punched the punching bags, jumped with the jump rope.. but it wasn't going to prepare her for what it was like to be in a wrestling ring. Nothing could ever prepare her for something like that, except actually getting in the training and wrestling first hand. And with Michael with her, that was the exact plan she had in mind- or rather, the plan they had in mind. ''Ey, Mikey,' the slender nineteen year old squeaked as she rolled into the rickety wrestling ring at the center of the gymnasium, 'are you sure that I'm ready for this? I mean.. I'm not the biggest or strongest person in the world, nor do I exactly have the knowledge of wrestling that most others do.' Michael guffawed and quickly climbed into the ring after his cousin. 'This was your idea, wasn't it? To become a wrestler, just like me?' Mindy pouted and stuck her tongue out at Michael. 'You know what I mean! After all.. I said that stuff before I was put in the hospital. I'm not so sure if I'm ready for that stuff now.' Michael shrugged and stood tall, walking towards his little cousin as he rubbed at his bearded chin. 'Oh, so that's how you're feeling about this, eh?' 'Uh huh,' Mindy hummed with a childishly swift nod of her head, 'that's exactly how I'm feelin' right now.' Michael sighed. 'Well if you're getting scared to wrestle because you had a concussion, then yeah I guess this is the wrong place for you.' Michael was ready to continue speaking, but when he noticed his tone of voice and the facial expression Mindy had because of it, Michael stopped and took a deep breath instead. 'You've seen my matches, right?' Michael continued eventually, with a nicer tone of voice. The question that arised caused Mindy to nod her head once more. 'Well, you obviously must know the shit I've been through. And trust me, I've been in your position before. There were times I was thinkin' that I shouldn't be in the ring and I still look back on that shit and wonder if wrestling those times were the right things to do. But you know what? Sometimes you just have to push through that- and that's what you need to do for this match of yours.' Michael stopped speaking for a moment and looked around the ring, checking the turnbuckle padding and the ring ropes, making sure nothing was going to fall apart on them. Fortunately enough for them, aside from all the dust, the ring looked just fine. 'So,' Michael went on, 'who are your opponents for this Pay Per View, anyway?' Mindy paused, and one of her fingers tapped her chin in thought before she answered. 'Erm... a buncha people. Like, uh.. Choi Yen Lei, Ami Mizuno, Johnathan Drake, and Mike Biddle. There's some others, but I can't remember'em all. Those names ring a bell to you, Mikey?' Michael let the aforementioned names sink in for a few moments, and then shot Mindy a look of confusion. 'Nah. I think if I heard of a name like Mike Biddle, I would have remembered it. Not too many people sound like they get named after retarded flocks of pidgeons, after all.' Mindy giggled at the comment, enjoying her cousin's well renowned humor. Michael wasn't done there, though. ''Hey mommy,'' Michael said through a pinch of his nose, taking on an impersonating voice that for a second had sounded like Mindy's. ''Can I feed the Biddle?' 'Why yes, honey, here is some fiddle to feed the Biddle.' 'Yay!'' 'And you call me crazy sometimes, Mikey.' 'Well, that you are, cousin.' 'Meh!' Mindy ran over to Michael at the opposite corner of the ring and started to playfully bat at his shoulders, but he swiftly got her off with a rather large toss across the squared circle. 'That's called a hiptoss,' Michael pointed out helpfully. Mindy pouted as she clutched her ass through her pink hot pants, face contorted to express the amount of pain she had felt. 'Well,' Mindy sniffled out, 'that was mean.' 'Well those people you're facing at Reload aren't going to be very nice to you, either. And I doubt that they would flinch at those pitter patter slaps you were giving me.' That comment set her off. 'Pitter patter?!' She jumped to her feet, brushed the dust from her clothes, and ran back at her cousin. The result... 'And that, Mindy, is a drop toe hold.' 'Wah!' Before Michael knew it, Mindy was throwing a tear-soaked tantrum in the center of the ring. 'My nose,' she screamed, 'Mikey, you hurt mah nose!' 'That was the whole point,' the older one sighed, shaking his head slowly. Mindy forced herself to her feet, and growled in a girly growl. She was hurt, yes. But was she going to give up? Never. Michael threw her again. 'Arm drag.' And again. 'Back body drop.' And again. 'Belly to belly suplex.' At that point, Mindy was huffing and puffing. She was still on her feet and eager for more, but Michael had to wonder how much longer it would last. Maybe it would be best if he simply did something to make sure she stayed down. 'This,' Michael panted, grabbing his cousin's head and putting it in a front face lock, 'this is the Last Breath. Which means once I hit this, you shouldn't get back up.' He leaned forward and threw his right leg back, preparing for the momentum-filled DDT he was about to give. But then, the unthinkable- 'Fuck!' Before Michael knew it, he was on his knees, cupping his wedding tackle. 'And that's a kick in the-' 'Bo-fucking-jangles,' Michael interrupted through gasps for air, before he promptly smacked face first into the mat. Mindy forced her cousin onto his back and threw herself over him before she slammed her own hand onto the canvas. Clouds of dust erupted from the mat as she counted, 'One! Two! Three! I win, whoohoo!' 'Gahakdfhaksdf...' Gibberish was all Michael could form as Mindy stood up and leaped into the air, proclaiming her victory. 'You know somethin', Mikey, you're right! I can't let any injuries hinder my decisions! And I can't let myself be intimidated by my opponents just because I'm all... girly, and... well, cute.' 'That's the spirit,' Michael rooted on, still in pain, 'gah.. just remember, Mindy.. wear those wrestling boots of yours at your match instead of those platform boots...' Mindy looked down at Michael in a state of confusion. 'Wrestling boots?' Mindy glanced down at her platforms and grinned, turning her heels slightly so she could look them over. 'These are my wrestling boots, silly!' 'Gahakdfhaksdf!' 'So, ya think I'm ready Mikey?' his younger half asked, a mischevious grin etched on her features. Michael could no longer form a verbal reply; all he could do was take one hand from the crotch of his baggy black jeans, and give a shaky thumbs up. 'Yay!' Mindy skipped out of the ring and bounced off to the gym exit, to which Michael strained his head to try and see where she was going. 'Hey. Wait for... oh, fuck.'
