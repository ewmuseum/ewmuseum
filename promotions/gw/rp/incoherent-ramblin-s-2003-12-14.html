Incoherent Ramblin's

14 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-14-03 Some things just shouldn’t happen; some people just shouldn’t do the things that they do. Jesse Briggs isn’t one of those people, oh no, he’s a man who lives and dies by his actions. His decisions, while oft times rash, make or break the man. Tonight is no different. For the first time in damn near ten months he’s going to speak. Harsh will be his tone, fierce, the gaze he casts upon his opponents. He’s a man on a mission. He stands in the middle of a pre-constructed set, specifically built for him. The background is a large rebel flag hanging from a ceiling. Placed strategically in front of the flag is a display of Civil War era weaponry, notably a fully restored confederate canon and a rifle resting against it. On the opposite side of the set is a shoddy looking table with a model distillery and an old style shine jug. Jesse looks around the set, while it makes him uncomfortable to me amidst such a promotional setting, it does slightly thrill him to be surrounded by his heritage. He looks over the canon and rifle especially, after all he owns a still. What’s the point of checking that out? He takes his left hand and gently feels along the length of the canon, amazed at the craftsmanship, unaware the camera is already rolling. A grunt from off camera catches his attention and pulls him back to reality. He walks up to the camera, all decked out in his standard attire. The original Jesse Briggs work shirt, with name tag, his navy blue Sparks? trucking baseball cap, and worn blue jeans; he pauses midway to stare at the camera. Possibly unsure if he’ll have “it” after so long, but quickly he pushes that aside and moves to stand directly in front of the camera. His breathing quickens and small beads of sweat begin to roll down his face, which is beginning to flush with the sudden stream of blood and emotion surging through him. He slaps his hands together hard, making a loud clap, and begins rubbing them together as he prepares to verbally assault anyone he chooses to. Finally stops rubbing his hands together and begins to stroke his goatee, seemingly even deeper in though, then?.he speaks. Briggs: It’s been almost ten months, ten long months it’s been since anyone heard a damn word from me. How’s it feel to be back? Couldn’t tell ya, I haven’t whipped anyone’s ass yet, but oh BIGOD I WILL!!! All you lil? slickers can take that “un to th” bank. Ya see Global, I was gone, hell probly even forgotten, but I’m still alive! Blood stills burns in my veins, and what all that means is, I’m still able to cause a lotta chaos and raise even more hell! I’ve been doin? a lotta thinkin? since ya’ll saw me last. I done a lot of bad things back then, terrible things to good people. Hell, I hurt my best friend in the world, I beat the hell outta women, and worst of all, I attack people from behind. That ain’t how I’s raised GW. Where I come from ya fight to win, ain’t no lie to tell, but ya come at “em in front of their face, not from behind. I broke card’nal rule number one as far as the south goes, and for that, I’m sorry. Ain’t none of ya gonna hafta worry “bout me getting’s from behind, lest you out number me a good deal. But none of that matters right now, why” Cause we’ve got what somebody told me is called Showdown in Tokyo right down the road. I gotta focus on that, which I ain’t done real well, to be honest, but that’s ok. Ain’t no sumbitch here that can beat me. Not one single solitary person that walks the face of god’s green earth that can knock me on my ass, and make me stay. Arrogant, ain’t I? But I speak from experience. I’ve won more fights than Texas has executed people. This is one fight I intend to win, too. None of ya can stop me. Hell I’d go over a list of names if I cared, but I don’t. Ya see, I ain’t really got a problem with nobody in GW now, “cept my team cap’n, Brick. We got some unfinished business, boy. Soon as this is over, I say we how do the shrinks put it” “Find some closure?” I’ll whip your happy black ass after this show. We’ll see who really is THE man. But still, that’s me getting? ahead of myself again. The smart ones tend to do that. Back to the matter at hand. I’ve been told that the winner of this thing gets a shot for the World title, not to mention the International title. Well, that sounds mighty good to this ol? boy! I’ll be more than happy to kick, stomp, spit, bite, punch, slap, wedgy, eye poke, stab, or shoot and bastard that get’s in my way! I ain’t jokin? boys. Grandpappy Briggs taught me how to fight, and he told me, he says “Jess, when ya’s a fixin ta fight.” He did talk kinda funny, I’ll admit that, but he says “Ya better fight ta win, ain’t no man evah brung a knife ta a gunfight, got me?” And I told “im that I knew what he meant, so don’t be surprised when I start whoopin” ass like there ain’t no tomorrow. Read me? Cause I didn’t come back from this damn knee surgery to just lollygag around like some Chris Camero rip off. I aim to win boys, any way I can. Whatever it takes. However I gotta beat ya. I’ll do it. AND THAT’S THE BOTTOM LINE *BEEP*BEEP*BEEP* The screen goes black. Suddenly the GW logo appears and Steve Dinkler does a voic.e over.
