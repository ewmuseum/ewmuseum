Remembering That Day

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/25/03 The First Time I Held Her Was The Last Time I Held Her Oh Lord, Can't You End This Suffering? I Should Be Immune To This Pain Some Things You Can Never Get Used To Like The First Time I Held Her Was The Last Time I Held Her You Sit Back In Your King Throne And Laugh You Mock Me, Oh God Ironic or Hate Leaving This World Would Do You A Favor I'm Staying Here Just To Make The Believers Believer In Success Instead Of Faith They Will Believe In Me He raises his fist towards the sky, you could swear lightning surrounded it. The power of the fist, clinched...tight as ever signified the fury he holds up. Rain pours down from the heaven's...it's tears. The thunder rolls over the clouds...it's moan. Man verse faith. Faith verse man. Hoping one day, the conclusion would be solved. Answering those questions, the ones that are on all of our minds that no politician or celebrity mentions. Questions of birth, love, religion, death and many more. He stands up for his right, even though many have attempted to prove him wrong, when all they proved was their desire to change somebod's life to better their own. But not him, he is the hidden truth behind all of the lies. He is the solution to the problems. And he is the meaning of life. He screams into the heavens above with his voice, trembling, but furious... 'YOU POUR DOWN ON ME...RAIN DOWN SLEEPLESS NIGHT. SHED EACH TEAR, HOPING TO DROWN THOSE YOU LOVE. BUT THE ONES YOU LOVE HAVE FALLEN TO THEIR KNEES. PLEADING, BEGGING FOR AN EXPLANATION FOR SOMETHING THAT CAN'T BE EXPLAINED. THEY HAVE NO COMMON SENSE, OH LORD, COMMON SENSE IS THE MOST UNCOMMON THING THESE DAYS. SEE WHAT YOU HAVE SUPPOSEDLY CREATED? YOU HAVE CREATED MONSTERS TO DEFEAT THEIR OWN RACE? POLITICIANS, LEADERS, CELEBRITIES, SPORTS ATHLETES, MUSICIANS...THEY ALL ARE ROLE MODELS TO THE YOUTH. ROLE MODEL, OH LORD, THEY HAVE MORE POWER THEN ADOLF HITLER HIMSELF. SIT UP IN HEAVEN AND LAUGH. YOU SAVE THESE INNOCENT PEOPLE? LOOK AROUND YOU, OH LORD, THIS WORLD YOU CREATED. THIS WORLD IS NOT FULL OF LOVE, IT IS FULL OF PROSTITUTES, DRUG DEALERS, PEOPLE OF GREED, AND OTHER WASTES OF LIVES. SEE WHAT YOU HAVE DONE? THESE PEOPLE ARE NOT INNOCENT! THEY ARE GUILTY OF BEING PATHETIC AND WORTHLESS. PAT YOURSELF ON THE BACK, OH LORD, FOR YOU HAVE SUPPOSEDLY CREATED A MONSTER IN YOUR OWN IMAGE. HAVE YOUR CHILD LOOK UP TO THOSE PROSTITUTES AND DRUG DEALERS, THEN SEE WHAT THE FUTURE HAS ASTORE FOR YOU, OH LORD, THE WORLD THIRSTS ON SIN. WHERE WILL YOU BE IN 100 YEARS?' His voice breaks and he falls to his knees, weeping...begging. Not for forgiveness, but for mercy from above. His knees slam into the mud, his face falling forward, for nothing else matters. He begins to speak slowly and quiter now... 'See what you have done? Melissa, she lies dead. Just another person for you. Just another way to take my life, to take my hopes, to take my dreams and stomp them into dust. Lord, I strived for what you wanted. I followed your word, but then, your word was never written by you. It was written by your disciples. Melissa, oh Melissa, I would want her to be safe forever and ever...but you took her away from me. Greedy, nefarious, and disturbing. But again, you smile over your work. You have people bow to their knees, just to be closer to you. You have people put a bigger roll of money into the tray at church, just to prove themselves of worth to you? And you sit back and allow this to happen? Such a great and powerful God you are.' Lightning strikes a couple feet in front of him. He doesn't budge, just faces the fear... 'All I wanted was happiness in my life, but you took it all away with my father, mother, and Melissa. I try to patch these heartaches and to stitch up the crack in my heart. But you wouldn't let it...smile again oh Lord, you have done great things on this world.' He holds his left eye with his left hand... 'This scar I bare, it is a scar that I bare because of the fury that you've stabbed me with. A badge of honor, soaking in blood that was poured from my face onto her beautiful dress. Oh Lord, open your eyes for once and look what is before you. You waited until she said those three words and you ended it right there. You've taken my father, you've taken my mother, you've taken Melissa...now take me, oh Lord, I have nothing left. Please take me.' Lightning strikes closer to him. Instead of jumping back, a tear squeezes out of his eyes as he shuts them, awaiting the lightning bolt to strike him dead. He pauses for a couple seconds and waits...nothing happens. He patiently waits. The rain pours down harder and harder, drenching his clothing...soaking them in sympathy. 'JUST GET THIS OVER WITH' He screams at the top of his lungs. All of a sudden, the rain stops pouring and the clouds open up and the sun beams down on his dirty and broken face. The glisten of failure and disappointment flash across his face. He looks up towards the sky... 'What Lord? You can't finish what you started? Or because you want me to continue this life or misery, suffering in pain that will never go away.' A still frame memory of his father holding him over his head with a big smile on both their faces flash across his mind. A memory of Melissa's face riding in his car with a smile flashes across his face. Then a still frame memory of his father's tombstone flashes across his mind. Then a still frame memory of Melissa's bloodied face sends a sharp pain into his heart... 'Throughout my life, you have granted happiness, only to be replaced with the worst pain imaginable. Should I thank you? Or spit at your feet?' He pauses and drops his head towards the ground as the... SCREEN FADES TO BLACK ***** He looks up towards the sky with the GW International Championship Title across his shoulder. 'I've thrown away the still frames in my mind. And took just remembered the picture captions, for nothing else fits in my life except success. Not because of you, not because of anybody except The Nevermore and I. And I have realized, this faith I once had for you was blind. And now, now I laugh at what everybody else has fallen for. They are all brainwashed, thinking you are something when you are nothing. 'Success is counted sweetest to those who neer succeed' is what Emily Dickenson once said. But in my opinion, success is counted sweetest to those who dedicate their lives, striving for perfection. Striving for something better then the normal and the common. Success will be counted sweetest to me at Dead Zone when I step into the Hell in a Cell against Brick and knowing that nobody...not you, or anybody else can take this away from me. And when I hold the GW Heavyweight Championship up high in the air, I will look towards you and laugh. I will laugh because you will never be apart of my fame and fortune. And I will take that title and keep it as far away from you as possible. Lord, oh betraying father, you have betrayed me once too many times. The one who laughs first will not laugh last this time. For it will be me, standing in the ring over a bloodied Brick, making sure each one of his nine lives has been decapitated and buried in his own pain. It will not be you...it will never be you anymore. The tides have turned and this fire will blaze inside of me and never stop. For my adrenaline is what fuels the fire. And I am the adrenaline rush that never ends. Try to stop me if you can...because you, Brick, or anybody else can't. I am the solution. I am the meaning of life. And I am the hidden truth. The truth has been told, oh father, and the truth will hurt you more then it will Brick.' He turns around and chuckles...slowly and softly muttering...
