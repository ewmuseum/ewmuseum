Dividing Two Passions #6: Diversification 2004 #2

10 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 10th 2004 “What exactly do I do with this?” questions Arefiev. Igor Arefiev stands in a small sports hall wearing his basketball attire. He is wearing the wonderful Puerto Rican basketball jersey and shorts. In his hands he is holding an orange basketball. He doesn’t look comfortable at all. Zergolov is no around. Igor looks to his left and right and sees no-one near him. He is standing at the three point line on the basketball court. He looks up and sees the rim. He looks around him again. “No-one will see if I miss” Will they?” he whispers to himself. Igor fingers the ball in his hands, and then looks at the rim. Back at the ball. Back at the rim. Back at the ball. He limbers up and throws it at the rim. The ball floats though the air. ? ? ? Towards the net. ? ? ? And flies two feet over the backboard! Igor is motionless. “Shit!” echoes from his lips, as the ball crashes against the hall wall and then bounces back to his feet. “Ha Ha! Ha Ha!” bellows a hearty laugh from the doors of the sports hall. A footstep clicks on the hall floor. Arefiev, who was staring down at the ball, slowly tilts his head towards the door. Stepping across the floor towards Arefiev in stitches of laughter is Zergolov. “You suck!” exclaims Zergolov as he reaches his tag team partner, “I mean you completely missed the whole back board. Igor” I’m not going to lie to you, but that’s bad!” Zergolov smirks as he faces his tag team partner. Arefiev sarcastically smiles back. “You have a go!” exclaims Arefiev, with a look of intent in his eyes. “No way!” exclaims Zergolov, backing away from the ball and his best friend. “What not?” protests Arefiev, “I had a go” Why can’t you?” “Simple,” says Constantino, now standing six or seven feet from his partner along the three-point line, “I know I can’t shoot and I know I’ll clear that back board by even more than you so I’m going to not even try?” “Go on!” Arefiev lifts the ball and passes into Zergolov’s chest. Constantino takes into his hands and looks down at it. He looks up at the rim, and then back at the ball. “Go on!” encourages Arefiev. “Give me time!” snaps Zergolov. He looks at the floor and then at the ball. Back at the ball. Back at the rim. Back at the ball. He sets himself and throws it at the rim. The ball floats though the air. ? ? ? Towards the net. ? ? ? “Dude” You so missed!” laughs Arefiev. ? ? The ball crashes down on the rim. ? It hits the back board. ? Bounces on the rim again. ? Rolls around the rim. ? And drops? Into the net! “YYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEEESSSSSSSSSSSSSS!” screams Zergolov jumping up and down with his arms flying abut him. Arefiev has dropped to his knees with his head in his hands. Zergolov hops over to him and begins to dance around his partner. “LOSER! LOSER!” jokes Zergolov. *cough* Zergolov stops still. Arefiev looks up from his hands. Both men are meant by the small figure of Allen Iversen. “You guys must be “dem flying things!” exclaims the NBA star. “Yeah. We are The Flying Vergomovs!” exclaims Zergolov. He paces towards the USA basketball star with his hand out stretched. Iversen doesn’t even bother to shake his hands. “Now what exactly do you want with me?” questions Iversen as the two men stands close to each other. Arefiev stands up and shrugs. “I need to help me develop a workout routine?” begins Zergolov, “I hope to release?” “A workout routine?” interrupts Iversen, “Like them fit blonde chicks release at Christmas” Hell no! I ain’t getting involved in no fag’s plans.” Iversen drops the basketball he was holding and walks to the sports hall door. Neither of the wrestlers follow him. “Queer!” mumbles Arefiev with a slight snigger in his voice. “Fuck you!” bites Zergolov as he watches the sports hall swing closed. He then burns a hole in his tag team partner with his evil stare and then marches off. Arefiev shrugs and watches him go. He lifts the ball and looks at the rim again. ?
