Now See It Through My Eyes

12 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 12th, 2004 YOU’VE SEEN IT YOURSELF NOW SEE IT THROUGH MY EYES I just can’t seem to get away from these motherfuckers. I turn my car towards the right’tilting it on two wheels for seconds or two. Almost makes me feel guilty for trashing their warehouse. Fuck them, this isn’t a game. I’ll fucking teach them not to fuck with The Nevermore. I showed them what group is more powerful. They’re gaining on me? I punch the dashboard’fucking car isn’t as fast as theirs. They drive alongside of me. My heart beats faster then it has ever beaten before. For the first time, I’m scared for my fucking life. I slam on the brake and turn’I do a complete 180 turn. I hit the fucking gas before they can even do turn around. Now, more relieved, I travel alone. I wipe a handful of sweat off my forehead. I turn on the radio, nothing but fucking static. So I change the channel, kind of half-watching the road and messing with the radio at the same time. I look up and see two headlights coming straight at me. SLAM! My head flies back against the neck of the seat. It whiplashes forward and my head slams off the windshield. A dictionary can’t describe how much this fucking hurts. My car spins sideways off the road. It finally comes to a complete stop. I am relieved’glad I’m still alive. It could have been worse. I can’t move my right hand’its probably broken. I cringe in pain. My forehead starts to sting’more of a burning feeling. Snots drip down my nose, I wipe them off with my left hand. The red snot pours onto my hand. My nose is bleeding, its fuckin? broken. I reach over my body with my left hand, struggling to unbuckle my seatbelt. Those fucking car commercials. They act like having your seatbelt on will stop your head from smashing against the windshield. I plan on filing a lawsuit when I get the fuck out of this predicament. I unbuckle my seatbelt. I take a deep breath, drips of blood fall into my mouth. I haven’t tasted my own blood in about 2 weeks. Boy, do I love this fucking business. No matter how many times blood pours down your face, you never get used to it. I pull the handle on my car door. Fucking thing opens up about two inches and that’s all. I put my shoulder into it and smash it open. I try to step out, but my legs collapse underneath me. I can’t even hold up my own fucking weight. I crawl a couple feet into the grass. I roll over on my back, holding my head. I hear steps coming towards me. They are now in the grass in front of me. I can’t see who the hell it is. It’s probably the paramedics. About damn time. Right now, everything is going in slow motion. And my head just fucking hurts really bad. But I am just happy as hell to have the paramedics here. I can’t really hear anything, except mumbling’I think they are talking to me. I can’t speak, hurts too much. My bottom tooth has gone through my upper lip. Just knowing that its there makes it hurt 10 times worse. They continue to talk to me, the voices start to come clearer’sounds very familiar. It’s the voice of Maverick Reno. Suddenly, I remember what the hell is going on. I was running away from TUE. Everything that has happened with TUE wasn’t even in my head right now. The only thing that’s there are probably splintered pieces of glass. I don’t concentrate on what he’s saying. Nothing can be that important. I try to pry my eye open with all my eye power. That’s a muscle that I wish I had exercised a lot more. I finally get it open. I see John Zero, Deacon Holmes and Maverick Reno standing in front of me with fucking guns. Still, it doesn’t run through my mind of what the hell is going on. I’m in too much pain. All of a sudden, all I hear are explosions. My chest caves in. The pain in my head and in my right hand can’t even compare. My stomach starts to ache. I try to breath, but it seems that I can’t get any air. I’ve just been fucking shot. I can barely see anything. Everything is just a blur. But what I do see is Maverick Reno taking a couple steps forward. He puts a silencer to my forehead and pulls the trigger. My head slams back against a rock that is behind my head. Relieved that it’s finally over. I’ve finally’finally just let go. It feels like there are no more worries in the world. When people say that you’re whole life flashes before your eyes before you die, they are wrong. I didn’t know it was coming. Just the feeling of having no pain, that’s all I felt. I lay on my one eye wide open. I try to close it, but I can’t. I try to bring my left hand up to my chest to hold my gun wound, but I can’t even move my fucking hand. I can’t hear anything anymore. My heartbeat stops? As I lie there on my back, I slowly digest what has happened. And that, that is the worst pain imaginable. I wish I could have done something with my life. Being on television, being something in the Global Wrestling, being everything I was just wasn’t enough. I wish I could have fallen in love one last time. I wish I could have shared my life with a son or daughter. I wish I could have donated everything I won to charity. But I didn’t write out a will. When you are’well, when you were my age, you don’t think about writing a will. When you can run around, do a backflip, and do a split at my age, dying is the last thing you think about. The worst part about dying is. Every unfinished business is forced to be finished. I pray, for the first time in my life’I pray that me and God can work something out? IF CHRIST CAN BE SACRAFICED FOR THE SAKE OF MANKIND THEN WHY CAN’T SAGE SABBOT?
