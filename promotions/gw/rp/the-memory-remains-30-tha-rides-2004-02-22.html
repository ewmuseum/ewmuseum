The Memory Remains #30 -- Tha rides

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22204 I raised the glass to my lips and knocked the remainder of the brown tinted liquid back. It burned its good burn as it slid down my throat and into the empty pit of my stomach. Deac looked over at me from across the small gap between our respective bench seats in the back of the stretch Lincoln Navigator on loan to me from T.U.E. Rhaiza sat next to him, legs curled under her body. They each held in their respective hands a double old fashion glass still half full of Crown Royal. Ice bobbed in the middle of each glass. Personally, I was taking my whiskey straight. Deacon was fiddling with the radio controls, trying to get the perfect bass thump out of manufacturer quality speakers. Big Pimpin' by Jay-Z starting pumping over the speakers. Deacon and Rhaiza, both slouched and hanging limp in their seats with tipsy smiles covering their faces, start singing the song's catchy hook, their collective voices carrying over the words of Jay-Z. They gave each other a sly look and exchanged a quick kiss. Me? I helped myself to more of the crown, downing this portion in two big swallows. 'Damn man!' Deacon exclaimed, quickly rising from his seat and snatching the bottle up like a junkie hording a rock. 'You goin' a lil heavy on tha drink there Z, I mean damn, save some for me!' Deacon halfway slurred, working on the good buzz in his system. Then looking over to his right and noticing Rhaiza sitting there with an expecting look on her face he continues 'I mean us, shit... Everything alright man?' 'I'll be fine,' I said steadily, pouring yet another glassful. 'Is it Vic?' Deac asked, getting somewhat serious in his half-hammered state. 'Lil problem in the bedroom area? That why she ain't here?' he continues to pry, gently setting the bottle back down like a newborn in it's crib. 'No. She's out spending more of my money. Everything is fine there’It's just?' I stopped. 'It's just what?' 'I don't like riding in limos,' I stated flatly. 'How come?' Rhaiza asked, he green eyes wide with concern. 'You've got everything you need right here. You've got alcohol. You've got music. You don't even have to drive. What's so wrong with limos.' I gave Rhaiza one of my patented blank stares. 'Last time I rode in a limo for an extended period of time, I dislocated my driver’s wrist and shoulder, while fracturing his elbow.' 'Damn, Z. That's fucked up man. What did he do? Put one too many olives in your martini?' Deac asked. 'He startled me. I was?.daydreaming.' “That’s just wrong, man,” Deac started shaking his head. “You just don’t go and fuck someone’s shit cause he yelled boo while you were daydreamin”. And what tha fuck were you daydreamin' about anyway!” Guns? I bet it was guns!” He leaned to Rhaiza and whispered a drunk’s whisper into her ear, ? I told you he was into that weird shit.” Deac and Rhaiza share a laugh and another kiss, this one a bit longer but not full on makin out. Damn I wish Vic were here. I sighed. Deac might as well know the truth. At least most of it. Not the whole truth. No one will really ever know that. Not Deacon. Not Victoria. Only me. “A woman that I use to be involved with,” the words came out smoothly, helped by the Crown Royal. I picked the bottle back up and poured another hefty glass, preparing myself for the forthcoming harassment from Deac. “Oh ho ho!” Deacon exclaimed, excited as a kid who just got a new toy. “An old flame, eh” And what was her name?” A slight “Awwww,” escaped from Rhaiza’s lips. “Lily.” “Lily?” “And where, pray tell, is Miss Lily right now” I’d like to meet the bitch that fucked you up,” Deac laughed, pouring more of the Crown down his throat. Rhaiza tilted her head back and laughed some as well. I took a deep breath, letting the “bitch” comment slide. He doesn’t know, so I can’t ram my fist down his throat, like I would anyone else. “She’s dead.” That stopped them both in mid-laugh. Deacon’s face went slack, his jaw dropped. Rhaiza’s face had this “oh my god” look of utter shock and confusion. Rhaiza swats at Deacon’s arm, trying to get him to say something. Deac’s mouth started going up and down before actual words started coming out. “Damn. Shit, Z. I didn’t know.” “It’s okay, Deac. It was a long time ago. Before we started working together. I’ve moved past it.” For the most part, at least. “What happened to her?” Rhaiza asked, filling in the empty air left by the depressing conversation. “She was shot to death,” I said quietly. At this point, Deacon returned to his almost normal self. 'Someone killed her? Please tell me you got that dirty sonofabitch. I told you he was a vengeful bastard, didn't I?' he said, looking at Rhaiza. 'Come on, John. Spare us no details, man. The gorier the better!' 'I didn't get revenge on this one, Deac.' I said flatly. 'What!”! Why the fuck not? Man, shit, someone kills my chick, you for damn sure it's gonna be some furniture movin'! I mean damn, that right there is just a shit storm waitin' to shower. So why didn't you get them, John? And it better be a damn good fucking reason.' I looked Deacon square in the eye, leaning forward so that I made sure that I held his attention when I said it. 'It's hard to seek revenge on the person that killed your woman, when you are that person.' That really got them right there. No movement. No breathing. The stare that they gave me was so blank I could’ve sue them for use of my patented blank stare. A few seconds pass before Deac starts speaking again. “Yeah, so what kinda car you getting?” Leave it to Deac to come up with the smooth change of subject. “The morning after our little incident with Rokku, I had an Aston Martin V12 Vanquish special ordered,” I replied nonchalantly, actually glad for the change of conversation. It is still rather draining to talk about Lily for extended periods of time. “Special ordered?” Deac started. “Now aren’t you some hot shit, special ordering a brand new car?” “They handcraft these cars, D. Each one is designed for a specific person. You just don’t go to a lot and pick it out. You tell them what you want and they build it. That, and I had a few special modifications to request. Some that needed some time to have done. What about you, D” What you gonna get?” Deacon gave me a slow approving nod. “All I can say is that I can’t wait to get my hands on Lucille, Jr.” “Lucille”.Junior?” I asked slowly, cocking my head to one side. “You’ll see,” was all that Deacon said, his mouth splitting into a wide grin, while Rhaiza snuggled in close to him. Yeah, why couldn’t Vic be here right about now? Deac and Rhaiza turned their undivided attention upon one another, tuning me and the rest of the limo ride out. I carefully retrieved the bottle of Crown from its resting place and poured yet another glass. This one I savored, talking slow sips, letting the alcohol do its duty in deadening my senses. Outside, the trees passed by slowly as I gazed out the window. The alcohol coursed through my veins. It felt good. Unfortunately, however, it did cause my mind to drift. To drift back in time. “Lily.” It came out so soft, I didn’t think I had actually said it. Lily She was standing in the middle of the room when I came in. Just standing there, her auburn hair sweeping over her face playfully, a coy smile dancing on her lips. An angel standing before me. An explosion. A bang. A loud pop. I can’t remember which it was, but it happened all the same. And after that noise, that terrible noise, she fell. All the happiness fled from her eyes, replaced by terror. I ran to her, sliding down as I ran, catching her before she hit the ground. I held her close to me, looking into her eyes, those emerald green eyes, watching the life run away, seeking shelter elsewhere. I lifted my hand to her face, brushing the hair away, my fingers sticky with her dark red blood. Right before her eyes completely faded to nothing, she was able to tell me, the words almost a silent mutter, one last thing. “I did love you.” Then nothing. Her eyes went dark as the last little bit of breath her lungs held exited through her mouth in a small-exasperated sigh. I screamed, tears flowing from my eyes by the bucket-load. I raised my hands to my head, cradling it in my palms. It was then that I realized that I was holding the gun. I jerked out of it, my eyes snapping open and taking in a sharp intake of breath. My eyes shot around the inside of the limo. Deac and Rhaiza were still all over each other. If they noticed the little episode, they were playing it off. Before anything could be said, the limo rolled to a stop. A small buzzed over-voice came on the intercom. “We’ve arrived at the lot, sirs.” Deacon broke the kiss with Rhaiza, a large grin splitting over his lips. The door to the limo is opened slowly from the outside by the driver, and Deac leaps out of it, one hand grabbing Rhaiza and dragging her along with him, all the while giggling like a little boy in a candy store. I look at the driver, who just shrugged. Oh well. I stepped slowly out of the limo, letting the light slowly gather upon my body. We had been riding for what had seemed like an eternity. I reached into my pocket and removed a wad of cash, which I stuffed into the breast pocket of the driver’s coat. From the trunk, I removed an aluminum-plated briefcase. “We won’t be needing the car any more today. Go ahead and head back, and enjoy the rest of the evening,” I said, walking away, not watching the driver’s expression. The building didn’t look much like a car lot. More like a low-slung warehouse, the dull light gray metal walls collecting rust. It just screamed “ABANDONED WAREHOUSE,” but it wasn’t. Strolling into the building, I could see metal and machinery everywhere. Sparks flew. A heavy grinding noise was everywhere. I followed the barely noticeable path to the back office. I opened the door without even knocking. The man behind the desk stood up immediately, extending a hand. “There’s the man,” Tommy, the man behind the desk said. “Hey Tommy. I got a call sayin” she was ready.” “That she is, Mr. Zero. Let’s go take a look.” Me and Tommy exited his office by ways of a back door which led to a showroom of sorts. The room was empty, save for a few forms under gray drop clothes. At the far end of the room, we stopped. He reached out and pulled the cloth off of a form. “You’re Vanquish, Mr. Zero.” And I will Vanquish mine enemies?”
