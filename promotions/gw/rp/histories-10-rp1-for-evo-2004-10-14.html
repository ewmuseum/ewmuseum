Histories # 10 (RP1 for evo)

14 October 2004

Date: 101404 So, we’re not going to kill him? No, I didn’t say that. I said we weren’t gonna kill him right away. What about torture? That we can do. I’ll handle it though. Why you? Because you’ll just end up killing him right away. And? I can be more subtle. I laughed. Trust me. I laughed again. ***** The man was sitting on a metal chair, eyes moving about wildly. Good morning, Brock. Who are you? Who I am is of no consequence. But, I believe you know my friend over there. Zero pointed over to where I sat backwards on another steel chair. I raised one arm and waved. Wind! What the hell is going on here? You lied to me, Brock. I would never lie to you. I’ll be the judge of that. Zero held up a control box. Anything you would like to say before we get started? You’re not going to torture me, are you? You could say that. Zero thumbed a button on the box. The chain around Brock’s feet began to tighten, the hook attached to it pulling up. In a few moments, Brock was hanging upside-down. Zero pulled a knife out of his pocket and sliced Brock’s shirt down the middle, ripping it away from his body. Do you like fire Brock? Yes’I mean, no? Why? Because, I personally, think it’s interesting. It is so alive, but it is not. It is born, it eats, it dies. It has all of the characteristics of a living creature. But it is not. Where the hell was he going with this? I also think its interesting that it comes in so many different colors. Like blue, for example. He held up the business end of a torch in one hand and the lighter for it in another. He cranked the lighter and a reddish blue flame leapt out of the end of the torch. A twist and a turn of the knob, and the flame turned completely blue. You see, blue means hot. Hotter than hot. Not white hot. But still, pretty damn hot. Please? This should be interesting. Have you ever been burnt by a blue flame, Brock? He started to whimper. Brock’don’t make me repeat myself. “.No” I haven’t either. But I’ve seen it done. They say that it doesn’t burn. In fact, you almost don’t feel a thing. The heat sears off most of your nerve endings. It’s so hot to the point that it’s cold. The only way you know that your being burnt is by the sound of the sizzling of your own flesh. Zero disappeared behind Brock. I couldn’t see what he was doing. But from the look of Brock, it was not good. No?.no?.no?. Sweat began to pour from his skin. Tears pelted down upon the cement floor. And then he screamed. A high pitch whiny bellow that was a mix of a scream of terror and a desperate call for mommy. The smell of cooking meat filled the air. And sizzling flesh. Who was it Brock? Who did you betray us to? No’Nobody? So you wanted us dead yourself? More screams. No? NOOOO!!! Make up your mind Brock. NO! He’ll’He’ll kill me. The rank smell of evacuating bowels filled the air. Think about us, Brock. Think of what we’ll do to you. A final scream. OKAY! STOP! I’ll’I’ll tell you what you need to know. Zero came back around in front of Brock, the blowtorch still in hand. Talk. Or I burn your face off next. Look. He knew. Most of it at least. He knew that I supplied Wind with information. That’s it? He told me, that if I gave Wind the information? “keep talking” “that he would pay me a lot of money” “give me what I need” “and if I didn’t” “give me a name” “he would kill me. “all I need is a name. So you betrayed Wind. You fed Wind the bogus information so that this mystery man could kill him. No. He wasn’t after Wind. He wasn’t” He wasn’t? No. He was after you. Him? Me? Zero disappeared behind Brock again. He reemerged from the darkness with a half-cooked rib eye and a melted Popsicle. He threw them down on the ground in front of Brock’s face. The asshole cheated. Motherfucker. But he still had the lit torch in his hand. Alright motherfucker. No more fun. No more games. We were having fun? At least Brock had balls. Zero smirked. Then he pushed the flame onto Brock’s exposed stomach. This time Brock screamed for real. And no more smart ass comments, especially from the roasted peanut gallery. Brock whimpered. And now, it’s time for the sixty-four thousand dollar question. Are you ready Brock? Y..yes? Who was he, Brock? He..he said his name was Hector. You lie. Zero pushed the torch back into Brock’s abdomen, leaving it there for almost a minute. Brock’s screams echoed throughout the building. Zero removed the flame. Care to try again? I swear, he said his name was Hector. What did he look like? He was a little guy. Looked like he was about as wide as he was tall. Was in a wheel chair. Spoke with a Scottish accent. Three outta four ain’t bad. Zero turned off the torch and walked over to where I sat. Brock swung from his chain, crying to himself. Who’s Hector. Someone from my past. Lose our temper, did we? Just a touch. I nodded over to Brock. So, what do we do with him. Zero looked over at Brock, a flat look in his eyes. Kill “em. You want the honors” No. He turned and started to leave. Where you going? To think. He disappeared into the shadows? “The Numbers Man.
