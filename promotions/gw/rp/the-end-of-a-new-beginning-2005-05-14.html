The End of a New Beginning

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-14-05 Corvus passively went downtown to the police station with Detective Van Buren. During the ride he remained calm and silent; not willing to offer up anything that might lead her to believe he was guilty of anything. Of course he was at the club and the distinct possibility was someone got a hold of the same girl after he left then it did not look promising for him. He had a right to a lawyer which if he needed to utilize there wouldn’t be any hesitation at all. When they arrived at the station, the Detective took him for processing in which they took his fingerprints and a picture (which would be blurry because it’s a vampire protection mechanism and one can only be seen if they so allow it). The police were not amused when they took his photo and he extended out his middle finger. “Are you trying to be cute?” Van Buren said with disgust as they started to march him towards the interrogation room. “I thought you would have found it amusing Detective” I would think that you would laugh every now and again so your face muscles didn’t get as tight as the ones in your ass ...” He chuckled as she pushed him into the room and directed him to sit down in a chair. One thing she hated was a cocky asshole and she dealt with a lot of them during the day (or night). She really didn’t want to have to put up with it from a “wrestler” whom she thought was one of the worst professions anyone could have. “I hope you decide to help yourself out by being serious for once and stop with your crude comments and smart ass attitude. I know you don’t want to be here as much as I don’t want you to be here ..” She paused and walked over to her chair and sat down, crossing one leg over the other going over the report taken from the witness from the club. Corvus sat at the table with his hand folded together resting on the table waiting for her to question him about the evening. He was going to tell her the truth (or at least somewhat the truth) and hopefully she would garner enough common sense to let him go and leave it be. She was on the brink of finding herself embroiled into the vampire world of which she knew nothing about or when she found out would want to be involved in. Some vampires wanted to be as far away from what they were a lot of the time, himself included. “So tell me Corvus, where were you this evening?” “My brother just came into town and we decided to go have a drink somewhere. We wound up at a club ... but I’m not really sure what the name of it was. I just know that it was kind of a dive, looked almost like the inside of a warehouse.” The Detective jotted down some notes and looked over things a little more before continuing one. Things seemed to be lining themselves up and it would be a matter of time before she had an arrest. “From the witness I have on record, they said they saw you at a club called Absolution. Would you say that is about right?” “I told you I didn’t know the name of the place.” “You told me that you went with your brother. Where is he now” I didn’t hear or see anyone at the house other than you.” “We took separate vehicles. I left the club early because I was tired and I had some things to take care of for work and wanted to get a head start on it. I don’t feel like I have to keep tabs on him or baby-sit. He’s an adult ...” Van Buren writes a few more notes down in her tablet and referencing them from what she had in her report. She had quite a few more questions before she was going to put him in a line-up for the witness to pick out who he saw with the woman that was murdered. “Ok ... did you talk to anyone while you were at the club tonight?” “Detective, that is a bit of a redundant question. People usually talk to others in a club ... it is called socializing. You should try it sometime it might do you a bit of good.” “You’re walking on thin ice mister, if you expect me to try and get you out of here quickly you are going to answer my in a respectful manner from here on out. Are we understood or do I have to hit you with a steel chair for you to get me?” Corvus nodded slightly as a simple answer without needing to communicate with words. It was one of those things he just wanted to be over and done with but of course he’s a prime suspect so it would be that much harder to get out of the situation. “I want you to look at a picture. I would like for you to tell me if you saw this girl while you were at the club.” She slid the picture over the table towards him. He glanced at it and could tell right away that it was the girl he had taken into the bathroom and fed from. The man didn’t show any sort of emotion while looking at the picture to give off any sort of clue. “Yes, she looks familiar. She stopped at my table and we talked for a few minutes; told me her cousin was a fan of my employers programming.” “Ok, and during this conversation what did the two of you talk about?” “It was really brief; she basically recognized me for being in the public eye and didn’t expect me to be seen in a club of that type. We sat around for a little bit and that was pretty much all before I decided to go home.” Van Buren wrote down a few more notes before continuing. “Did you or did you not buy the girl a drink?” She tapped her pen against the desk waiting for him to reply to her. It was evident that she had some reason for asking him the question but there was a double meaning behind it. “Yes, I offered to buy her a drink and she accepted ... I bought her a Rum and Coke and she only really took a few sips of it that I saw before I got up and left.” More note writing came from the Detective before she looks up at him with a stern look on her face. “You know that is a crime to purchase liquor for underage persons?” She shakes her head. “I really expected more out of you but I’m not worried about a minor charge like that ...” “Well I don’t know what you think I did but I can assure you that after I bought her a drink and we talked a little longer not long after that I left. If you want to give me a fine or whatever you want to do for buying a girl that is underage a drink ... go ahead, but if you don’t have anything else then I’m going to take my happy ass back home where I belong.” He begins to stand up when the Detective motions for him to re-seat himself in his seat. “You’re not going anywhere for the moment, Mister.” She takes some pictures out of her folder and tosses them over in his direction and for the first time he unfolds his hands to take a look at them. In these pictures are images of neck wounds on the girl he had taken to the bathroom. He specifically knows that he did not leave anything on her when he left the room. Someone must have been watching him the entire time and when he went home decided to finish what he started either to stir some waves or wanted him to get in some deep ass shit trouble. Corvus puts the pictures back together and tosses them back to her. His eyes are sincere as he speaks to her in his defense. “Listen, despite what you may think ... I did not do that to the poor girl. She was in healthy condition when I left her at the club. Whoever you are looking for is a straight up fucking psycho.” He pauses for a second because he doesn’t want to paint an ill picture for vampires but then again what would she know to make a clear judgment. “I’m not your guy and you can have as many witnesses as you like tell you that I was at the club. I told you myself that I was there ... so while you are wasting time on questioning me there could be some psycho out there trying to find an underage girl buffet.” Detective Van Buren thought it over but she still couldn’t shake the fact that she had this odd feeling about him ever since the first time they met when she was in his house; also the fact that his skin is always so pale and she never sees him eat anything. He hasn’t been near any mirrors so far for her to try and convince herself vampires are real but she has that inkling in the back of her mind. “There are many strange coincidences in this case, Mister Corvus, and I just cannot dismiss them because you tell me to do so. You have made some valid points but how do I know that you are not just trying to sell me a line of crap to save your own ass from being put in jail?” Corvus leans forward in his chair and gives her an eerie look. He has all the incentive to reach across the desk and tear out her vocal chords but that wouldn’t prove anything to anyone. It would only prove that he was a vicious murderer and despite what anyone says ... he is more human than most of the real deal could be. “Listen, just because I go on television pretending to be a vampire and have the look and live the lifestyle doesn’t mean I go around biting people. You really need to look into this a little more carefully.” He says as he moves closer, opening his mouth for her to inspect it. “Do you see fangs” I didn’t think so ... how could I have bitten into her ... by hiding the fangs in my buttocks or my penis? Get real Detective ... you need to do a better job.” Having stated his case, he leans back in his seat and waits for her reaction. By his words, she has become flustered and is trying to hold back her obvious embarrassment over the situation. Vampires cannot be real, can they? What sort of monster could have done what they did to the defenseless girl? Corvus studies the Detective a little bit as he determines that he can smell the fear on her like a guy who has on way too much cologne. It wafts from her body towards him which prompts him to make a statement he could possibly regret. “Are you afraid of me, Miss Van Buren” Is that why you are throwing around all these accusations hoping something will stick?” She wasn’t sure how to answer it as it seemed like he was reading her mind. She shrugs it off and turns back to him and shakes he head. “No, I just want to find who did this whether it is you or not ... but you were at the scene and with the victim which doesn’t look good for you.” She starts to walk towards the door and he speaks to her. “Wait ...” she stops and turns to listen to what he has to say. “What if I told you that I could help you find who did it” I can tell you that it was someone who wants attention or someone who is looking to try and get me out of the way.” “I don’t tell you how to do your job, Corvus, or whatever the hell your real name even is ... but I refuse to let you tell me how I should or should not handle this case.” He wasn’t about to let this drag on any longer. The beast was beginning to come back which surprised him as he had already fed earlier but the small trace amounts of alcohol must have made him just that much thirstier. “I have something to tell you, Detective.” Corvus motions to her with his finger to bring her closer to him. She instinctively does as he asks as if deep in her subconscious it was what she wanted to do. A rush of excitement fell over her ... she was attracted to bad boys but usually not the suspected murdering types. She leans forward and whispers, “Yes?” “What if I told you that vampires existed ... would that make you anxious?” A devious grin came over his face as he brushes some of her hair away from her neck. She immediately starts to let down her defenses by moving closer and titling her head a little bit more to the side. The hunger took over not even giving him a chance to stop himself as he sat up in his seat and brought his lips to her neck, sinking his fangs into her skin and penetrating the soft flesh of it. She gasped a little as the pleasure from the vampire’s “kiss” took control over her. It was like a peak sexual experience only three fold in the amount of enjoyment that came with it. The blood flowed into his mouth and down his throat giving him that much more control as he was regaining some of his power just by the intake of her blood. He took only what he needed and nothing more; there was a possibility that he could make her one of his regular donors if things played out right. His tongue licked the wound closed; cleaned the area so nothing unusual would be suspected and then brought his lips to her ear and whispered to her, instructing her. “You will walk towards the door and forget about everything that just happened. The only thing that will be in your mind is what you were going to do before I told you I had something to tell you.” She slowly walks over towards the door and grabs her head as she is feeling dizzy for some reason. Van Buren looks over at Corvus who was sitting just as he was before like nothing happened. “I’m going to have the boys come in here and escort you to your cell. We’re going to hold you until morning unless you can find someone to bail you out of here, which if you have as many friends as you say you have ... I don’t see that happening.” Several minutes later, two police officers come in and cuff him one more time and lead him to where the cells are located. They escort him in and one of them undoes the cuffs and walks out as the other quickly closes the door so that he couldn’t try anything funny. The hilarious part is if he wanted out it wouldn’t take much for him to get out. Instead of acting like a nervous schmoe, Corvus walks over to the bench and has a seat. He thinks that it would be a good time to have a cigar or something to smoke for the hell of it, no matter what it was. Quietly he sat thinking about all the people who could have framed him and there was quite a list at that. There was the ex-wife for obvious reasons, her new found allies, and there were also a few others he had in mind due to some misunderstandings they might have taken personally. His senses began to pick up as he could smell another of his kind was there. There was not much he could do in the way of finding out what was going on without breaking himself out of the cell which would bring some unwanted attention to him when he was in enough trouble as it was. v v Rhianna had heard that Corvus was picked up by the police on the suspicion of first degree homicide after putting in a call to his brother Ash. She took it upon herself to go down to the station and correct matters with them if also to score points with the troubled vampire. Every single male in the precinct looked up as they heard the clicking of the stiletto heels she was wearing pounded the floor. She was wearing a business suit which when Corvus saw it would be impressed that she wasn’t dressing so much like a hooker. Her flaming red tresses were pulled back; she was wearing just the right amount of make-up to look sexy yet professional. With a briefcase in her hand she walked to the desk and asked if she could speak to the officer who was in charge of the case involving Corvus. Van Buren came out and introduced herself and Rhianna did the same, stating that she was Corvus? attorney and she was believable in the role. That was the funny thing about Toreador’s, they were good actors. “Do you have concrete evidence that my client committed the crime in which he was brought in for; also did you show him a copy of the arrest warrant?” Van Buren ran a hand through her hair and muttered under her breath. She was still waiting for the warrant, which did not come through so technically she arrested Corvus in an illegal manner. “I ... ? she hesitated and Rhianna jumped on it without hesitation. “I did not think so. I would like my client to be released before I sue both you and this department for negligence, am I clear?” Rhianna’s unyielding approach to get Corvus out of the situation appeared to be paying off. The Detective nodded slowly and walked away for a few moments to talk with the boss about what should be done. A short time later she returns to inform Rhianna that they would be releasing him from custody until they could find more evidence that would lock the fact that he indeed was the man they were looking for. She waited patiently for them to bring Corvus to her and noted the look in his eyes as he saw who it was that had liberated him from near doom. It was uncertain if there was any gratitude in his gaze but there was a sense of relief coming from him that much she could sense. v v The two sat silently in the car on the ride back to Corvus? estate. The only thing he could think about was finding out who killed the girl and wanted him to take the wrap for it. Rhianna’s mind was full of the want of redemption in her childe’s mind; to work out some kind of mutual relationship even if it meant that she would have to do more giving. The car pulled up through the gate which opened for him at the touch of a button on his key ring. The garage door opened as well in which the driver pulled into and left the engine running. “This would be your stop ... ? “That it is ... and before I go, I wanted to say thank you for coming to my aide. I know when to be appreciative for things that are done for me.” “You don’t have to thank me. I just wanted to make sure you were safe. If no one would have come for you there would have been a lot of trouble.” “In more ways then one ... would you like to come in” Your driver can just come back for you this evening. There are some things I want to talk to you about.” Rhianna seemed hopeful that this was the opportunity she sought to win his alliance with. She wasn’t worried about the secret war that raged on but just needed to have him around. “Sure, I would love to.” She instructed her driver to return at sundown and exited the car along with Corvus. They went into the house to have a quick drink and talk for a few moments before they retired for the remainder of the day. “So what did you want to talk about?” Rhianna stirred her glass of blood a little bit wondering what he had to say. It was an awkward moment for her because she hadn’t been this close to him without the feeling he was going to kill her. “This takes a lot for me to ask and I wouldn’t unless I didn’t think it was important to me ... I need help finding out about my son.” “Are you sure you want to continue bringing up the painful past” You are finally free from what bound you ... why retread back to something that may do more harm then good? Especially if people find out what you are up to?” He hesitated when it appeared evident that he was on his own in his quest. Maybe it was unrealistic for him to even think about it in the first place? That’s the thing about him though. He’s pretty stubborn when it comes to something he wants. “You’re not the only person who has said that, in fact, my brother said the same thing when I asked him to help as well. I see your points but it would be nice if you could see mine as well.” She saw his point but what kind of problems would that cause by him digging up the past and possibly alert the wrong people of his intentions. “I see your point; I’m just worried about what would come from the answers that your questions possess. I will help you Corvus, as long as you are willing to trust me from here on out.” “You bailed me out tonight ... you’ve earned my respect and trust. I do owe you one for that, well at least something within reason.” She smiled as she finished her drink. “I think I can manage starting out with something simple.” She stood up and motioned for him to give her his hand. He slowly put his into hers and stood up. “Finish your drink, you need your rest. We’ll talk about this when we wake up ...” Corvus brought his glass to his lips and finished it up and slipped the two glasses into the dishwasher and let it do its job as Rhianna walked him back to the master bedroom so that they could rest and recharge their batteries before they begun to work on finding out first of all who framed him for murder and also what might have happened to his son.
