The journals of Soma #3

26 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/26/05 “The art of evil. Not looking for perspective or contortions. A simple, naked front view so as to feel the attraction of the perverse.?-Luis Royo Everyone wants to know who I am. I am the shadow of mankind. I am all that man tries to become. I am perfection. And I was the first. Mankind and vampire-kind alike have told stories about me throughout history and nobody ever got the story right. Granted I killed all that almost did get the story right, that isn’t the important thing. The important thing is I have survived longer than any other creature in existence. I have loved and hated more than any other creature. I have taught many great men and vampires. And in all of my existence, I have never met my equal. There have been many women I have called wife, but none of them I actually loved, for I am a creature without love. I am a creature without remorse. I am the oblivion. I am evil in the eyes of man because I am superior to them. I, the avatar of suffering was created to bring pain to the ones who hurt the most high. But it is I, and my kind that are the evil ones” Writing in a journal is supposed to be calming and a way to clear the mind, but this always did seem a little odd, even for Soma. He stood there, journal in hand, pacing back and forth in the large, antique furnished bedroom. The bedroom, lavished in rare items from all over the world was a sight to behold. Over his bed hung three weapons, the first a scimitar laid with rubies at the hilt, the second weapon, a mighty sword unlike any other sword in creation, its light shines even in the darkest rooms, the last weapon was a lance that looked as if it had fresh blood on it. Over each weapon was a bronze nameplate. The Scourge. Excalibur. The Spear. He looked at the three magnificent weapons and tears began to fall from his face. His tears were so bloody they began to ruin his journal so he threw the journal on his bed. He once again reminded himself why he hated humanity and his “own kind” so much. He had seen so many things in the world he lived in and some how each thing he saw beauty in kept getting destroyed by man or his thin blooded cousins. But he had began to see hope in this woman who went by the name of Shadow. He kept pacing the room, now thinking about this unique beauty that had caught his eye. He remembered first encounter with her, at the wrestler known as Corvus side. The two of them, with two other wrestlers made up Soma and Sage Sabbot’s opponents at Crossover. They fought well, but not well enough. Soma smirks as he remembers his thoughts on them then: “Pitiful vampires who will die soon enough because they don’t truly know what they are or what they could become.” It was a harsh thought but that was the truth. And he still felt that way. He could see them bowing to human masters. He could see their hatred toward humanity but he could see the still holding on to their pasts and that they would never achieve the things Soma had accomplish. They did not know how to truly survive. But he saw more potential in Shadow. Was it because she was gorgeous in his eyes or the fight he could see inside of her? He knew the effect he was having on her and he saw the tension between them mounting. He offered her a life unlike any other vampire. He gave her a vial of his blood. “And as much as I hate humanity and what it has done to those of the thinner blood vampires, I need them. Out of their most viscous and sadistic, I shall build a family. They will be the guardians of eternal perfection, and I their patriarch. Once again we will rule through pain and suffering but with an order nobody will be able to match. But the quest will be a hard one; even so, the quest has already begun with Shadow. She will be the crimson queen to my sinister rule” His thoughts shifting once more on the only vampire to best him in combat, Soma knew he must have her. Shadow, the only vampire to ever best him in combat. In most situations where he was beaten, he would have tried to kill his adversary, but in this case it showed him exactly what he wanted to see. Did she drink his blood? The thought made him smile once more, for he knew what fate awaited her if she did. “Man is thoroughly stupid and dim-witted... and this condition is genetically inherited. Nurture seems unable to ameliorate what nature has here ordained. Here the idealist might argue that a eugenic solution mediated through genetic engineering could produce a more passive, thoughtful population by weeding out the stupid gene and replacing it'.-De Vere von Drakenberg Soma paced the room finally boring of his writing. He turned toward two large windows facing his bed. The windows were duck taped shut and the glass very tinted. Soma slowly walked over to them almost with a small ounce of fear. Fear for the only thing he was sure that could kill ninety percent of his kind. Sunlight. The only vampyres that could walk the day have all been put rest. Their essence obtained for the evolution that was about to transpire in Soma’s bedroom. He began to pull at the tape slowly, small glints of light showing on his black Armani pants. After pulling the tape from around all sides of the window he stood their, the windows seeming to be giants looking down on an inadequate creature. He didn’t move from that spot for several minutes. He smiled in assurance as he grabbed the handles to each door. Sunlight. It bathed him like a warm shower. The light showing the true color of his skin, as colorless as it could be seeing that it was brown. Once again tears came to his eyes as he realized he still did not have vision in the glorious light, the curse of the vampyre’s heart he had eaten to receive this dark gift. “It would seem Mr. Soma that there proved to be a flaw in gift you have received.” He already knew who it was, even with his vision gone for the short time, his sense made sure it was like he never lost it. He didn’t look toward the voice he just stared out the large windows into the sunlight. “Maybe, but you should come in and give it a try.” “Unlike you father, I still must be afraid of your new bright friend.” “What brings you here Max” The business not keeping you occupied enough?” Max didn’t say anything as if trying to come up with words for what he had on his mind. Soma sensing the uneasiness in the room he smiles turning toward his son, his eyes fully black. “Father, your eyes?” “I know son. What are you here to discuss?” “The council of vampires have issued a blood hunt on you as well as the few remaining vampyres have caught word of what you are doing and preparing their own attacks against you.” “Let them come. I shall over come my enemy yet again.” “This time father, it will be without me.” “I see. Even you have turned on me. After all you’ve seen. After all the hurt they have put you through. You still want to save them. You’d rather kill your father than watch my destiny unfold, to see Rex Deus spread across the land?” Max looked away from his father, his long black hair covering his facial features, while the body in the dark blue suit began to turn to leave, before he could take two steps, Soma stopped him. “Dad, please don’t force me to do something I regret. To see a dragon sovereignty take hold of this world would ultimately destroy us all. If your plan went through, many innocent deaths would be lost.” “Innocent” Hardly. These are the creature, who out of fear and ignorance, killed off most of your kin, and have started working on those thin blooded cousins of yours. You call them innocent. I see them as guilty.” “You are wrong father, and if you fight me I will kill you. Don’t make me.” “Maximillian Soma. I’m impressed to see that, like your father, you stand up for your cause, even if that means death. But you failed to consider one thing.” “What’s that” “If you’re so strong about your cause and are willing to kill me over it, don’t you think I’m just as strong about mine and would kill you too?” Before Max realized, Leinad had hold of his hands and tossed him toward the open windows. The sun instantly began to burn up his son, but with his speed he closed the windows right before the light consumed his son. He smiled as his son’s body was covered in harsh burns that left him unconscious. When Max came to he was tied up, spread eagle style to the front posts of Leinad’s bed, the windows closed. Max didn’t know how long he had been out but he did know it was long enough for Leinad to change into just a white suit shirt, his black Armani pants, a pair of boots, a heavy leather apron, and a pair of heavy black leather gloves. Fear. He didn’t know what to think. He trembled remembering the savage beatings of his youth. The punishments he got if he was unsuccessful at combat. He had gone through countless torture sessions but for some reason this scared him the most. He looked his father square in the eye and tried his best to spit in his face. Leinad only smiled while he opened the window a little, letting the sun touch a small portion of his now naked chest. The skin melted off the skin, bubbling and hissing as it liquefied and ran down his pant leg. Max screamed profanities and begged for his father to stop. The window closed and Leinad slowly walked over to Max. His left side open because of burns and wounds, Max cried out in pain as Leinad ran his hand inside the largest wound. He forced his arm all the way in until he had a hand on his heart. The two men stood face to face, one in tears, the other in shear enjoyment. “It is now time for you child to sleep. You chose this death not I. You had to watch in fear as your father squeezed the life from you. Sleep now and become one with oblivion.” Leinad pulled the heart through the chest, letting the blood and gore spill all over his apron. He looked at the heart of his son and opened back up the windows letting the sun turn the body to ash. Soma watched the body as he called one of his butlers to the room. “Vincient, I need you to clean this room. Oh and by the way, take this and have it put in one of the special tinted glass jars. Sit it on my mantle for all to see.” “Right away Mr. Soma.” 'A remnant of degraded human creatures persists... composed of those souls not sufficiently advanced to take part'. The Secret Doctrine
