Trials and Tribulations (1- Courage)

14 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/14/03 The winds whispered through the cool, mountain night. The twilight was beginning to fade into darkness, but that did not at all hush or shade the bright lights of the Shao-Lin Temple. The light from the temple illuminates in a radiant glow through the open windows and the small cracks in between the wooden frame. Soft voices can be heard as the entire temple is in a very quiet and relaxed time. The majority of the population sit engulfed in deep thought and meditation. Throughout every room children can be seen in deep concentration, an elder monc sits at the front on a small pillow chair, as he seems to be leading the class in their meditation. The same scenario occurs in many other of the rooms, although as the age of the children learning seems to grow, so does the length of the monc's beards. The monc's beard is a sign of his wisdom and age. The longer the beard, the longer since he has achieved his one-ness and has become one of the elder moncs. Those who instruct others in the ways of the Lotus. Then we hear a slight sound of grunting, panting and then a loud thud. The noises echo seemingly throughtout the whole temple. However, this does not seem to affect the many in deep meditation, as their faces stay still as paintings. There is a sound of a struggle down the hallway, but again it does not seem to affect the many concentrating in meditation. Down the hallway there lies a large room. One of the only rooms in the temple with full lighting, besides simply candles and incents. The room viewed from the outside seems large and very ancient looking. The walls are made of mere bamboo and paper. Through the paper we can see shadows moving, we can see what seems to be a fight going on inside. We see two shorter men engaged in combat with some type of ancient swords, as another; significantly taller then the others engaged in combat looks on. Inside the room, the two men continue there combat training. The man who is viewing is Ichiro Nakazura. A former member of the Shao Lin Temple of many years. He had since been gone for a few years, since leaving the temple to work in his father's Yakuza empire. He stands dressed in a traditional Shao-Lin robe. A black robe, trimmed with white and red, set on with black bamboo sandals. He stands arms crossed watching his brother engaged in combat with the eldest monc of the temple, Master Tanaka Ichizu. The Master of the temple, was very similar to the many other moncs of the temple. Well aged, and complete with the white and red robes of the moncs of the temple. His long, white beard, hung very low, lower then all other moncs. He was the eldest monc, the eldest and wisest in all arts of the Black Lotus. He stood in a defensive stance, with his samurai sword held in front awaiting the attack of the other combatant. The combatant was Jushin Nakazura. The younger and smaller brother of Ichiro. He faught against his former master, in an attempt to prove himself ready for the great task which was to be bestowed upon him, if he was ready. He wore a robe matching that of his older brother, and stood preparing an attack on his former master, an attack that he hoped could prove he and his brother's worthiness. 'Focus, young warrior. Harness your body's energy and spirit. Be one with the elements, and let the spirit of the Lotus, guide you. If you allow it, it will control your moves, it will embrace your body if you let it. If you let it, you will succeed.' Jushin hated the instruction, he wasn't a student any more, he was much past that. He grew furious and charged the older man, with one swing Jushin's sword was struck from his hand. He stood defenseless and defeated, staring into the eye's of his better, breathing heavily. 'You did not do what I told you,' Tanaka stated. 'Forgive me master, I was blinded by rage. Your words displease me,' replied Jushin. 'This I know, but you must not allow this to cloud your mind. For if you do, your task will fail, and failure cannot be accepted, for this task is of the up-most honour and importance. It will only be bestown to those who prove ready and able,' came the reply. 'Yes, master.' said Jushin, knowing he had been defeated. 'Now Ichiro, can you better your brother? Can you defeat me in combat with a steady hand, and an open spirit?' asked Tanaka. 'We shall soon see,' came Ichiro's reply, very mysteriously. 'Then prepare yourself for battle,' Tanaka smiled at the intensity in the older brother's eyes. Ichiro had always been the leader, the better of the two brothers. He knew that it would eventually be he, who would be the bearer of the mission. He knew that Ichiro was the right one to carry out the task of the Lotus, and it was with this in mind, that Tanaka took a step back, and prepared his own self for combat. Ichiro walked towards the sword, that lay on the ground. He kneeled down to pick it up, and as he raised it in his hand, he ran his hand up the smooth part of the blade. He concentrated heavily, and opened his mind and body. He stood up and walked into a direct path with the monc. He ran the blade slowly through the air, preparing his body, to be embraced by what was known as the Touch of the Lotus. He closed his eyes and steadied the blade in front of him. He maintained his shut eyes, and focussed all of his other senses, it was then when the touch came. It was like being electrocuted, he felt a surge of energy rush over his body. It was the after-effect that left him aware of the forth-coming attack. His sharpened senses, told him of the pound of footsteps that had begun to come towards him. He did not open his eyes. It was unnecessary, the touch showed him what he perceived as a shadowed version of the real world. In the darkness he could see the purple shadow coming towards him, he did not move, nor quake, nor shudder. He stood firmly, awaiting the attack as if he was oblivious. A rush of guilt came over Tanaka. It was not right to attack the man with his eyes closed. But he had told Ichiro to prepare for battle, and it was his own fault that he would be vulnerable to such attack. He closed in closer and closer. He was now about 10 feet away, he raised his blade, and took to the air. He jumped towards Ichiro, and swung his mighty blade, with all his might. The sound of steel sliced through the air as the footsteps silenced. Ichiro saw the shadow jumping towards him, he anticipated the swing of the sword and simply waved his hand, knocking the sword's blow astray, and opening his master up to attack. It was then when Ichiro's eyes shot open, and the real world came into plain view. Ichiro raised a foot and with a slight kick to the left, he pinned his master's sword to the ground. He smiled in victory, but was met by a punch. The punch almost connected, but lightning fast and without even thinking Ichiro's left arm raised and caught the punch. He then locked arms with his master and held him still. He smiled once again and raised his blade and rest it upon the shoulder of Tanaka. With a mere sweep, he could seperate the monc's head from his body, but that was not his intention. Ichiro closed off his mind and body. He felt the hold of the Lotus leave his body, his body slightly drained of energy. It was a natural reaction when the Lotus' touch left. It was the after affect of a strong adrenaline rush. He let down his sword, and let go of his master. He stepped back and bowed to the monc, and walked back towards his brother. The monc stood in awe at the older brother. He knew that Ichiro was strong in body and spirit, but he did not know of his mastery of the Lotus. He must have learned and studied much when he was at work for his father. All the better it was now almost certain. Ichiro was the chosen one, the one to bring the ways of the Lotus to the west. He was the Eternal Soul Warrior of the Lotus. 'Most impressive. I see you have learned to harness the Lotus' power. I congratulate you, for you are the youngest man to ever have mastered this rare art. And for that I believe that you and your brother may be ready. But there is a final test. So if you two could please meet me on the mountain over-look in ten minutes,' said the monc. 'Yes, master,' replied the brothers in unison. 'Feel free to change back into your original garments, for these robes are no longer needed,' instructed Tanaka. The two brothers bowed in respect and then walked towards the small change room attatched to the dojo. They walked in and found their suits just as they had left them. They quickly stripped the robes off, and showered. Then when they returned they found that the robes had been removed, they thought nothing of the courtesy of the temple, as they had been experiencing ever since they had returned. They both came out dressed similarly as they made there way towards the stairwells leading to the roof balconies. Ichiro the taller, older and stronger stood bold in his white suit, while his little brother Jushin was dressed in a dark black suit. The two men made their way towards the stairwell. They reached the stairs and mounted them slowly as both men were quite tired from their combat training. They reached the top and made their way across the roof area. The sun was now fully set, and the outer world consumed in darkness. They made their way towards the mountain overlook balcony. The darkness of the night time sky, impaired their vision but not their sense of direction. Although it had been years since they had made this walk, the journey was imprinted on their mind from years of repetition. It had often been their favourite meeting place, and it was for that reason they assumed that Tanaka had chosen this location. They rounded the corner towards the mountain-overlook and could see a smile fire had been started in the small pit. They walked over and sat in the chairs prepared for them. They looked around but could see no one, anywhere. The shadows seemed to consume all the roof but the fire. However, the two brothers had a feeling that all was not as it seems. They sensed another presence, lurking somewhere in the shadows. From the shadows behind their chairs, walked Master Tanaka. He smiled as he rested his hands on both of their shoulders. He laughed slightly at the jump and startle of the two brothers. 'Relax, it is merely me.' came the soft and comforting voice of their master. 'This is your final test. Through all of your trials and tribulations, this shall be the final test, to prove if you two are the rightful recipients, of this great task. Are you ready for this final test, my students?' 'Yes master Tanaka,' replied Ichiro. 'Yes, master,' answered Jushin slowly. 'Excellent, then let us talk now, as if old friends, with years of seperation,' said the monc. The two brothers smirked slightly at the monc's jest. It was not like Tanaka to joke and kid, but when he did it usually did not disappoint. They watched in silence as the monc sat on a large array of pillows on the other side of the fire, staring back at them. They tried to concentrate on their master, but the flicker of the flames kept drawing them in. Tanaka, smiled noticing their fixation on the flames. It had been long, since the three of them had met in this spot. He grabbed the bag sitting next to the fire, and opened it up. Reaching his hand inside, he pulled out many black and purple flower petals. He smiled as he dropped them into the fire. The fire died for a few seconds, and the three men sat in silence, as the dead of night consumed them. The two brothers sat focussed on where the bright, crackling flames had lived not seconds before. Then in an eruption of smoke, and flame a purple and black fire sprouted from the ashes. The two brothers' eyes remain focussed on the flames, their faces stern and fixated, their glare rock hard and inpenetrable. They stare into the flames and watch as their master smiles and leans back, also staring into the fire of black and purple. 'It has long been since we have sat here. Many days and many moons have passed since last, we watched the amazing spectacle of the Lotus flame. To watch it's dance, is to stare into the essence of true power. A power that you two shall bring to the rest of the world. That is, if you are ready,' Tanaka smirked and relaxed in his seat. 'Honour is our vessel, strength is our fuel, knowledge is our bearing, and let our souls be our guides,' replied the two brothers in a monotone unison. Their eyes had not yet left the intense black flames. 'Then now the final test begins. It has been many seasons since you have lived in this temple. During these several years you have been working in service for your father. A powerful Yakuza warlord. You must now, grant me passage through some of your many journeys as soldiers of your Father's fortune. It will be from these events that you will share with me which will tell the Lotus if you are the chosen ones.' Tanaka instructed the brothers. Ichiro's face took on a strange confused expression. His eyes stayed locked however, on the enchanting lure of the Lotus' flames. 'But Master Tanaka, how are our experiences in the Yakuza to prove our worthiness to the Lotus?' Tanaka smiled at the older of the two brothers. He had always been the inquisitive one, so eager for knowledge and power. Always willing to get the advanatge over all the other students. 'My student, these experiences will guide us through the enlightenment that the ways of the Lotus have changed your life. It is by these changes, that we may measure your worthiness.' Ichiro nodded, and for the first time his eyes left the flames. He looked to his brother, who in turn returned the glance. They both wanted this honour more then anything. Pride and honour was what made them unique from all of the others, they both knew it. That is why they had so readily accepted so many missions as street warriors in their father's service. With so many missions to choose from, they had no idea which would prove them most worthy. Those with much stealth involved, those with much killing involved, those with the most fighting, or those which needed the most intelligence. They could not have any way of knowing, and it was this that was part of the test. 'So, you wish to hear of our missions in our father's service, Master Tanaka?' questionned Jushin, just to make sure he was following correctly. 'I do. And I as well as the Lotus itself, shall decide whether or not you are ready my sons,' came the reply. 'But how shall we decide which stories to tell? For the novel which we have written during our time in service is long and detailed. There are so many to choose from!' inquired Ichiro. 'The Arts of Shao Lin and the Forgotten Arts of the Lotus, teach us many things. But few stand out strongly, those ten which do are most important. Wisdom, Pride, Strength, Unity, Speed, Courage, Justice and Redemption, Compassion, Stealth and Loyalty,' began the Shao Lin monc. 'Yes master, but we already know this. Why do you lecture us on that which we already know?' interrupted Jushin. 'Do not be so quick to judge. For I am not lecturing, I am answering your brother's question. For which missions you shall choose will be the decision of you own. But, you will tell me of one mission and one only, in which you have demonstrated one of the Merits of the Lotus,' began Tanaka. 'Each of you shall tell your own stories of the mission, each of you describing five to me. Those which you choose are up to you, but I must hear of each Tennet of the Lotus if you wish to pass this final and crucial test.' The two brothers stared at each other for a long time, neither speaking a word. But they did not have to, the looks in their eyes and the shape of their faces told each other what grounds they had. After a few minutes, the two were satisfied and turned back to their master. Tanaka watched as the two students turned back towards him. He knew they would not speak, for they would not have had to. They have had a bond ever since Jushin's birth, they somehow could understand each other without speaking. They had used it to fool many in the temple, students and moncs alike. This is it he thought. The moment had finally came. These final hours before daybreak would decide the future of this temple, and of these two young men. In his heart he hoped his assumptions were right. He hoped that they would be the two. He hoped they were the Souls of the Black Lotus. The monc leaned back in his chair. The test was about to begin. He looked at the two brothers, both stern-faced and focussed. He then began to speak to them, 'Now we shall discuss the first of these missions. Ichiro you will go first, then Jushin will follow. And so we will continue until all of the Ten Merits of the Lotus have been stated. Ichiro whenever you are ready you may begin your tale, by stating the Merit which your tale represents. Ichiro looked at his master, he smiled slightly and began with his favourite mission of all time. He spoke softly the merit which he would discuss, 'Courage.' He sat in the small chair, and the memories blurred into a vision in his mind. Flashback The task had been running through his mind all morning. He knew that they would be severely out numbered. That was why his father had chosen he and his brother. They were two of the most efficient killers in his empire. But, even this would be a true challenge for the brothers. The information on the mission was very limited. It said to expect at least 25-1 odds. The task itself was not entirely difficult. A band of young teenagers had been causing some problems with their father's employees in a shipping plant. They were to be terminated, all of them. Ichiro shook the thought from his mind. He concentrated on getting to the mission. He and his brother were in the small black Cadillac CTS that their father had given them, upon their entry to his service. The CTS rolled through the streets of northern Tokyo. The streets were unusually empty, as they cruised the streets. Ichiro knew his destination; a small warehousing unit in a vast array of complexes. After about ten minutes he reached the front gates to the Warehouse Complexes owned by Katao Incorporated. He pulled through the open black metal gates. He looked at his brother, who as always looked focussed and ready. He smiled, it was comforting to know that he could rely on his brother no matter what. He pulled into a small group of cars that were parked together. He backed in, leaving his car facing the gates. Then he killed the engine and stepped out of the car. The sunlight glimmered off of the white Armani suit he wore. From the passenger side he could see his brother emerging, the two walked to the back of the car, closing their doors as they did so. They got to the trunk, and Ichiro threw on a pair of sunglasses. 'Must you wear those?' asked Jushin. 'Why do you question, my fashion sense?' Ichiro replied. 'We'll be going indoors though,' answered Jushin. 'Let's just get ready!' Ichiro ended the conversation and opened up the trunk of the car. He smiled as he pulled out the two black duffel bags that were in the trunk. He passed one to his younger brother and slung the other over his shoulder. He then slammed the trunk down. The two brothers looked at each other, and began to walk towards their target, Unit 20. They walked side by side almost in perfect unison. They had only been walking for five minutes when they came upon Unit 20. The two looked at the small unit and the mass array of trucks and SUVs parked outside. Immediately they knew that there was to be much more then 25-1 odds when they entered the building. The two found shelter from the view of the rooftop scouts. The last thing they needed was to lose the element of surprise. They would be walking into the Lion's Den, and hoping to come out alive. The two men threw down the duffel bags and quickly opened them up. Jushin pulled out the sniper rifle which he had put in there earlier that morning. He smiled as he quickly assembled it. Ichiro pulled out two small sub-machine guns, and checked them over. Ichiro stood and began preparing to infiltrate the warehousing complex. He took off his suit jacket and put it in his bag. He pulled out his custom fit, leather upholster for his upper body. He slid his arms through it and clipped the leather over his black undershirt. He pulled out several magazines of two different sizes from the duffel bag. He put them all into a small puch on the front of the belt. He then loaded two of the larger magazines into the sub-machine guns and checked to make sure they fit. He took off the safety and put each of the guns into the front two gun holsters. Next he took two 9mm pistols out of the bag. He loaded clips into them, turned off the safety and placed them in two custom holsters located just over his back. He then put two more small pistols with clips into two holsters on his chest. He smiled satisfied and grabbed his jacket, throwing his suit jacket over the guns and hiding them fully from sight. Ichiro looked down at his brother who had now fully assembled the sniper rifle and was taking two pistols out and placing them in his jacket pockets. Jushin stood up, and with sniper rifle in hand stood beside his brother. The two began a quick prayer, before walking into the face of danger. 'I know you're listening,' began the brothers in unison. 'Come to me in my time of need, when times are tough and those around me bleed. Let not my enemies conquer me, give me strength so I can see. Guide me in this final flight, from all the darkness into light. Through desperate days, through all the haze. Carry me to my success.' Their faces became stern and focussed. They shook hands and then split up. Jushin ran to a back flight of stairs, there were five rooftop scouts, that they would need eliminated. Jushin climbed the stairs and laid in the prone position. He set his rifle on top of a small pile of bricks. He took aim, and fired five silenced shots. Each of the scouts fell upon the sound of the shot. Jushin ran down the flight of stairs quickly, he undid the barrel of the rifle, and quickly disassembled the rifle. He threw it in the bag and tossed the entire bag into a garbage can. He pulled out a small flask and poured its contents into the can, and pulled out a pack of matches and lit one. He then set the entire pack ablaze before dropping them into the can and igniting it in flames. Ichiro watched from behind a dumpster as his brother pulled out his two pistols and then the two began flanking towards the entrance of the warehouse. Ichiro lifted his pant leg and pulled out a small knife. He then walked close to the two guards standing in front of the door. He pocketed the knife and walked up to the guards. 'Who are you?' questioned one of the guards. 'Well, I was just wondering how to get to unit 50.' lied Ichiro. 'There is no such building here? Now who are you?!' demanded the other guard. Ichiro only smiled as he grabbed ahold of the knife and withdrew it. He stabbed the first guard in the neck with the knife before ducking a rifle butt from the other guard. He let go of the knife and rose punching the remaining guard in the face. He grabbed hold of his arm, and pushed outwards, the bone snapping as he went. The guard let out a harsh gasp of pain before receiving a knife to the throat from Jushin who had snuck up behind the last guard. 'Thanks,' was all that Ichiro could manage to say. Jushin merely nodded as he kicked the guard out of the doorway and grabbed the small assault rifle which one of the guards had been holding. He led the way as Ichiro walked behind him with his two small pistols in hand. Jushin approached the first door slowly and carefully. The two brothers entered slowly to check for any remaining survivors, there were none, six bodies lied dead on the ground, but they knew thatthe gun fire had attracted a lot of attention. Give me some cover fire!' yelled Ichiro over the sound of gun fire. And none else have come through the door to help them, these are final seven barriers in our mission.' assured Jushin. 'Okay, scoot and shoot. You cover me,' Ichiro instructed his younger brother. He realized he had not been spotted as all of his enemies fire, was still focussed on his brother. Ichiro dashed out again and took out two of the final three guards before his pistol ran out. Jushin watched as his brother hid helplessly. His gun had run out, and he had now drawn his enemy's fire. Jushin couldn't shoot at the guard anymore, as he was now ducking out of the way and firing at the helpless Ichiro. Jushin ran out from behind his shelter, he came running towards the crate that the final guard was hiding behind. He baseball slid feet first across the ground. He came into view and the surprised look on the guard's face told the story. As soon as he could raise his gun, Jushin had already unloaded four bullets into the guard. Jushin stood and walked over to where his brother laid hiding. He helped Ichiro to his feet, and then the two walked towards the ring of parked cars. Ichiro reached into his pocket and pulled out a wet rag. He opened the gas tank to the front most car, and stuck the end of the long towel down into the gas tank. He then handed Jushin the lighter and began to walk away. Jushin lit the lighter, and smiled at the death everywhere around him. They had overcome tremendous odds, much more then that was expected of them. He took the flame down to the towel, and set it ablaze. He ran to catch up to his brother, and the two began to run together. They ran through the flight of stairs and out the door. They never looked back as they heard the explosion of cars, and the crackling of fire and flames. They ran all the way back to the black Cadillac CTS, and hopped in. The car roared to life with the turn of the key in the ignition. Ichiro threw the car into gear and they pealed out into the Tokyo streets.
