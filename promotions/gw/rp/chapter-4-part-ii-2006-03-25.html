Date: 3-25-06 Four fighters entered The Battlefield? One is a legend who wants to reclaim his title along with his status as a champion. One is the most feared fighter and is the heavy favorite to win the title. One is trying to escape the shadow of his former self and wants to show the world who he really is. And one is a champion who wants to fight hard and win big. Ok, I think I did enough hyping for my recent fight at High Octane. But the people think that it does deserve all the hype since they’re saying that was perhaps one of the greatest fights aside from the International and World Heavyweight Title bouts. I must admit that High Octane was full of many surprises. One of the surprises being that not one fighter retained their title. Yes, I’m afraid that I was among the ones who did win their fights. Don’t really need to go into too much detail on what happened at High Octane. Well, for those who have missed the event you only need to know that someone made a fatal mistake near the end of the fight. I’m not going to mention any names since he knows who is. This fighter may have cost me the fight, but I have this saying that goes perfectly for just this kind of situation. “What goes around comes around.” With that being said there’s only one thing left to say. You’re going to be the next causality on The Battlefield, count on it! [:: ----- ::] The scene opens in a high-class restaurant where the Supreme Maiden, Megumi was sitting alone at a table, enjoying a hot cup of green tea as she waited for someone to join her. Someone approaches her table. Megumi lowers her cup from her lips. “I was a bit surprised when you called me.” She looked up at person who revealed to be Chi. “I presume that you didn’t call me here to discuss your destiny.” Chi sighs in frustration as she took a seat across from Megumi. “How many times do I have to tell that I’m not what you think I am. I’m an assassin not a Shinto Priestess.” “You say that isn’t your destiny, but deep down I think you know that it is.” Chi glares at Megumi. “You’re right, I didn’t call you here to hear your nonsense.” She sharpened her eyes into more seriousness. “I called you here so you can tell me what the hell is going on with Avenger.” “What?” Megumi looks with surprise. “Did Mr. Kushinaga transform again?” Chi raises a brow. “Again?” Megumi looks down with some concern. “The seal must be breaking.” “Seal, what seal?” Chi moved up in her chair. “I think you better explain.” Megumi blinks before she looked at Chi curiously. “Why?” Her curious look turned to content. “Can it be that you’re concern about him?” Chi shoots up from her chair and slammed both hands on the table. “Don’t be ridiculous!” Everyone in the restaurant stops what they were doing and looked at Chi with awkwardness. “I could careless what happens to that bastard as long as I’m the one who kills him!” Chi with a dark blush across her face glared down at Megumi. Megumi blinks before she formed an embarrass look on her face. “Uh, you’re making a scene here.” Chi looks around the restaurant and saw that the customers were in fact staring at her. She closes her eyes and sighed to herself before she sat back down in her chair. The customers soon go back to what they were doing. Megumi sips her tea for a moment before she looked at Chi with hesitation. “What is happening to Mr. Kushinaga is quite complicated to explain.” “Humor me.” Chi glared. ==|Part II: The Things You Do For Love|== In the meditation room within the secret headquarters of NEST was Mother’s Children who just came from honing their skills in the training room as they were still in wonder about their Lord’s plan on recruiting the Deathscythe Outlaw to the group. Smash was sitting at a table as he was drinking a bottle of beer, but he soon slammed the bottle down on the table. “This is bullshit!” Volt sighs softly with disappointment while he played a game of chess with Tsunami. “What is it now?” “I still don’t know what Lord Aion sees in that pretend fighter.” Smash glared. Bash was leaning back on a wall with arms crossed. “Yeah, there’s nothing impressive about him.” “You two just need to admit that you were impressed. I know that I was.” Volt moved a pawn one space forward. “The only he did was beat the shit out of a talking and walking garbage can I mean we could’ve done that without even breaking a sweat.” Smash drank his beer. Tsunami takes Volt’s pawn. “Well, I think he’ll make a nice addition to the group.” “You’re just saying that because you have a schoolgirl crush on him.” Bash stared at Tsunami with a smirk. Tsunami’s face turns red. “That’s not true!” She looked away in embarrassment. “I really do think he’ll make a nice addition to the group.” Bash scoffs. “Yeah right.” “What do you think about Kushinaga, Moon?” Volt glanced at Moon as he moved his knight. Moon was sitting on the floor with her eyes closed. “He’s dangerous when he doesn’t have a soul, but he will be even dangerous when he have one.” Volt scratches his cheek and looked at Moon with great confusion. “Okay?” “You all saw Kushinaga demonstrate his awesome power, but I sensed that he did all that while being unconscious.” Moon opened her dull colored eyes and looked at her group members with seriousness. Tsunami looks surprise. “No way.” “Come to think of it, he did become more powerful after being leveled with four cars.” Volt holds his chin and stared down at the chess board in thought about his next move. Smash scoffs. “I told you that he was nothing but a pretend fighter who has no place in our elite group of warriors.” “Lord Aion seems to believe that he does and we will obey his order on bringing him Kushinaga alive.” Moon glared at Smash and Bash with her eyes glowing bright blue. “And I mean alive.” Smash and Bash look at Moon with great fear as they nodded slowly, but some objection went with their looks. Moon’s eyes stop glowing as she looked at her members. “I want you all to show great caution when going up against Kushinaga. Conscious or not, he is still a dangerous fighter.” Tsunami looks around the room with curiousness. “Hey, where’s Widow?” “I could’ve sworn that she came back with us.” Volt moved a couple of a pieces behind Tsunami’s back. “She’s probably playing with her spiders.” Smash drank his beer. Bash laughs. “Yeah.” Moon looks with seriousness as she knew where Widow was at. [:: ----- ::] In a long corridor was Widow who was walking alone while humming a happy tune as she was going to see Aion in his private study. Widow arrives at the study where the door slid open as a look of disgust appeared on her face when she saw that Aion and Mother were sharing an intimate moment together. It’s no secret that Widow is deeply in love with Aion. She used to get all the attention from him that is before Mother entered the picture. He now spends his time with Mother and not her. She hates Mother for that. Widow hates that Mother took Aion away from him. She could kill Mother anytime she wants, but she knows that Aion will find out that she was responsible and kill her for it. Widow wants to receive Aion’s love and she would do anything to get it. Widow looks with heartbreak as she started walking away. “Is there anything you wanted, Widow.” Widow stops walking and stood in the doorway as she looked at Aion with a small smile. “It was nothing.” Aion stands before his desk as he straightened his clothes. “It must be something if you came to see me here.” “Uh?” Widow looked with shyness. Mother stares at Widow while she lied across the desk with a grin on her face. Widow glares at Mother with a growl, but she soon turned her attention back to Aion. “Lord Aion, I wanted to tell you?” Aion blinks and looked at Widow with curiousness. “Lord Aion, I have the analysis that you asked for.” Widow turns to Dr. White’s voice and saw that she was standing beside her within the study’s doorway with a single folder in her hand. “Good.” Aion took a seat at his desk with a smirk on his face. “Let’s see what Mr. Kushinaga’s Power Class is.” “I believe that his power will intrigue you as it did me.” Dr. White approached Aion and handed him the folder. She glances over at Widow then back at Aion as she had a curious look on her face. “Was I interrupting something?” “It was nothing.” Aion read the file with great interest. Widow looks down that made her long raven hair fall over her sad eyes. “Yeah, it was nothing.” She started walking away. “Well, well, Mr. Kushinaga is full of surprises.” Aion let out a small laugh as he had a thrill look on his face. “It seems that his power is equal to mine.” “I told you that you would be intrigued.” Dr. White smirked. Widow stands in the doorway with her back turned to the room as she had a look of shock on her face. “As powerful as Lord Aion.” Mother sits up from the desk and formed a serious look on her face as her eyes glowed sapphire. “Dear, I’m getting a report from the Ghost Man you had stakeout Mr. Kushinaga’s friend store.” “I presume that he’s getting close on discovering the secret origins of Mr. Kushinaga’s past.” Aion looked at Mother. Mother’s eyes return normal as she nodded. Aion closes his eyes with a soft sigh. “Guess we have to kill him before he discovers it.” “Allow me the pleasure of doing that, Lord Aion.” Widow entered back in the room as she bowed before him. Aion thinks a bit before he looked at Widow with a smile. “Alright, the assignment is yours.” “Thank you, Lord Aion.” Widow glanced up at Aion as she still bowed. “I also have a plan to bring you Mr. Kushinaga.” “Oh?” Aion raised a brow. Widow stands up straight and looked at Aion with great confidence. “Believe me, my Lord, it’s a good plan that can go without fail.” She smiled with some passion. “You won’t be disappointed.” [:: ----- ::] It was a late night in Okinawa where Jun was locked inside of his store continuing to search for the many answers on Kyo’s strange condition. Jun slams close a large book. “Damnit, it isn’t in this one either.” He leaned back in his chair as he closed his eyes and grabbed the bridge of his nose with a look of frustration on his face. “Maybe there are no answers to his condition.” Jun formed a serious look on his face. “Unless I’m looking in the wrong books.” He leaned forward in his seat and looked over at a metal box that rested on a nearby high shelf. Someone approaches the store. Jun has removed the box from the shelf and placed it on the counter as he stared down at it with some hesitation. “Could the answers be in here.” He opened the box after he entered the right combination to the lock as he pulled out a mysterious book that was wrapped in sacred paper. “The Book of the Damned.” ---- The Book of the Damned is a bible for demons. It said that it contains ancient scriptures of prophecies and also speaks about all breeds of demon which includes ancient breeds that thought to have been extinct a long time ago. ---- Jun pulls the wrapper off the book and started flipping through the pages until he reached the chapter that he wanted it. He begins reading the chapter. “It can’t be?” Jun’s eyes quivered in great shock. “Can Kyo really be a?” There was a knock on the door. Jun looks over at the front door where he saw a woman standing outside the front door. “We’re closed.” “My car just broke down and I was wondering if I could use your phone to call for help.” “Sure.” Jun closed the book and placed it somewhere underneath the front counter. He approaches the door where he unlocks the door and opens it. “Thank you.” Woman entered the store as it was revealed that the woman was Widow. “You’re a lifesaver.” She smirked with her eyes glowing emerald. [:: --The Next Day-- ::] Somewhere on the Pacific Ocean was the Deathscythe Outlaw laying on the rooftop of the anchored Wave Cruiser with his eyes locked on the clear blue sky as he was in deep thought about what has been happening to him as of late. Kyo’s thoughts are broken when he felt a great pain coursing through his brain as he was yelling and favoring his head. The markings on his forearms begin glowing that triggered fragmented visions of mass death and destruction with the haunting screams of the victims echoing in his head. Kyo soon comes upon a vision that consisted of a dark figure standing among millions of slaughtered humans and demons. A loud bam rang throughout the ocean! It was revealed that Kyo has bashed his head against the roof’s hard surface putting an end to everything. He slowly lifts his head with a distorted look on his face as blood dripped down from a cut on his forehead. “What was that?” Kyo soon sharpened his eyes in seriousness. “Who was that?” Sephiroth’s whimpers were heard. Kyo makes a curious sound as he looked down from the roof and saw that Sephiroth was sitting on the back deck looking up at him with concern. He hops down and kneeled before Sephiroth as he petted him on the head. “I’m all right. I was just remedying a bitch of a headache.” Kyo smiled as he watched his wolf enjoying the attention. The phone rings. Kyo stops petting Sephiroth and looked in the control room. “That’s probably Jun calling.” He stood up and entered the room where picked the phone. “Hello.” Kyo wiped the blood off his face as the cut closed up in result to his healing factor. “It’s me, Kyo.” Kyo goes to say something, but he paused and looked with some suspicion as he sensed that something was different in his friend’s voice. “Are you there, Kyo?” “Yeah, I’m here.” Kyo pretended that nothing was wrong. “So what’s going on?” “I managed to find out what is happening to you.” Kyo looks with seriousness. “Then what’s happening to me?” “I don’t think we should discuss this over the phone. Never know who’s listening in. Perhaps we can meet somewhere?” “Name the place.” Kyo leaned back on a nearby wall. “Shinjuku Station, fifteen minutes.” “I’ll be there.” Kyo hung up the phone as he stared at the phone with a troubled look on his face. On the other end, Jun stood behind the front counter of his store as he too hung up the phone. “You did well, my lovely puppet.” Jun looks over to the female’s voice and stared at the puppet master who revealed to be Widow. “Thank you, Mistress.” Widow approaches Jun with passion in her eyes. “But I’m afraid that you have outlived your services.” Her eyes glowed emerald as she started transforming into something more deadly while grinning. ---- Things appear to be getting very interesting now.
