The Awakening Of Sage Sabbot

October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October of 2004 I don’t recall my eyes ever being this tired. This sleepy. Ever. I don’t want to ever open them. It just feels too good to lie here and not worry about what I’ll have to deal with today. Because right now, nothing matters at all. I hear a noise, my eyes fly open. Sounds like rustling leaves, dancing around to the silent sounds of the wind whistling past me. A man leans over my body, but I can’t tell who it is. My eyes haven’t adjusted yet. I squint my eyes, and still can not make out who it is. I blink them a couple times. On approximately the fourth time, I look up and see? LEINARD SOMA Rage takes control of my body, I immediately dive at him from my back and wrap my fingers around his throat. I squeeze, harder then I’ve ever squeezed before. His neck cracks in my fingertips and it starts to feel like silly putty. But this my friend, this is not a joke at all. He is with The Echelon. And this is now an instinct for me. I will destroy them. I roll over on top of him. I jump on top of him and send him to his back. I stand on my tip-toes and put all of my weight on his throat. I want him to die, like they once killed me. I want you to understand what this feels like. I want your whole life to run through your head three seconds before your death. I want you to cry harder then you’ve ever cried before. I want you to beg for mercy. A combination of spit and drool flies out of Soma’s mouth. He attempts to mumble words? “What the fuck do you want?” He continues to try to talk, but the lack of oxygen truly makes it a difficult task. “What are you doing here?” His face turns purple, this is the greatest feeling I’ve ever felt. I’m about to kill Soma. It puts a smile on my face and merely gives me a chub in the pants. I’ve become sadistic’more sadistic then ever. He struggles to get out of my grasp, but its hard to break free now that I have my fingernails dug into his neck. I squeeze even harder. His eyes start to water. He grabs my wrists and force my hands off his throat. He lifts his knees up to my chest and kicks me off of him. I fly into the air from the force. I land on my feet and hurl myself back at him. Attacking him like an animal. An animal is exactly how they treated me. They shot me in the fucking head. Soma rolls to the right and I almost land on my face. He jumps on top of me now and holds my arms down. He is way to heavy for me to do anything, especially now that I’m so weak and tired. I suddenly get my second wind and my adrenaline rushes through my blood. A vein in my head almost explodes. Soma hits me in the stomach with his fist, my second wind just flew out of my mouth. “Will you stop fighting me?” He must be joking? I swing at his jaw but miss horribly, he throws my arm around and gets me in a back mount. He gets me in a full nelson, so I can’t move at all. Face first in the dirt, you don’t now how this feels. I take a deep breath and inhale dust and dirt. I start choking on it. But I’ll never give up. I know what these guys can do and they aren’t going to do it again. “I’m not here to fight you. I’m here’for you.” I stop fighting, purely out of curiosity. I take a deep breath and calm down a bit. I look in front of me and see a tombstone. I begin to read? Sage Tse Ling June 27th, 1961 ? October 12th, 2003 Gone But Not Forgotten Soma dug up my body from my grave. He woke the dead. Nobody wakes me up from the dead. No one. The anger builds up again. My blood boils inside my body and smoke seems to fog out my ears. He loosens his hold’enough so I can look back and backhand him in the face. My knuckles hit hard on his cheek. Hopefully, I broke his jaw. But he recovers quickly and locks the full nelson back in. I feel a warm drip of Soma’s blood land on my neck. Butterflies start flying around in my stomach. You don’t understand how this feels. “Sage, I was sent on this quest to seek you out. You are supposed to accompany me to accomplish it. Together, we will create controversy. And I’m not going to leave until you come with me.” I stop fighting once again. This time for real. No more playing possum just to wait for him to put his guard down. I actually listen this time. He finally caught my attention. “What the fuck are you talking about?” “I brought you back to life.” “That’s bullshit. God sent me back to heaven to take care of unfinished business.” “And that unfinished business is to help me on my quest.” “Well my unfinished business is to kill The Echelon for murdering me.” I bridge my back and flip Soma onto his back. He looks up in my face. Eye to eye with one of the people who killed me. I’ll never forget? “And to avenge my death?” I draw my hand back as far as I can. I clinch my fist tighter then I have ever clinched it before. This is pure hatred. I bring my fist down, as fast as a bullet and as heavy as a hammer. All of a sudden, a sharp pain stabs me between my ears. My head feels like its going to explode. I roll off Soma and hold my head’I can’t hear anything except? LOVE YOUR ENEMIES. FORGIVE THEM. A message sent from God. Maybe I should listen, he did grant me life after death. I make it to my knees. By the time I make it there, Soma is standing up directly in front of me with his right hand on my shoulder. If he lets me have my way, I swear I’ll tear him apart. I want to hate him so bad, but I can’t let myself. I’d die to kill him. But I can’t. Soma extends his hand in front of me. I grab it and he pulls me up to my feet. He turns from me and starts to walk. For some awkward reason, I follow him. I suppose God sent Soma to help me. Or sent me to help him. Whatever it is, my unfinished business has something to do with Soma. Maybe my plans have changed without me knowing it? Its like, God gave the blueprints to my life to Soma? Everything begins to unfold right before my very eyes? I just have to let my eyes adjust to see what it is? “to be continued.
