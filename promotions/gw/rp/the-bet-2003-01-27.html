The bet

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/27/03 RRIIIIING!!! went the Fosters? phone. Felicia briskly walked to the phone, holding baby Ashley in her arms. “Coming, coming,” she said, before picking up the phone, holding Ashley in her right arm. “Hello” Yes, he is, hold on. Aaron, baby, you have a call!” “Hold on for a minute! I’ll be right there!” “Okay, he’s coming.” Just then, Aaron walked up to her. “Who is it?” he asked. “It’s Jamal.” “OK. Hey, man.” “Time to pay up,” said Brick’s voice. Damn, Aaron thought to himself, he remembered the bet. “Fine,” he sighed into the phone. “I’ll meet you at the ATM.” He then hung up the phone and headed for the closet in the hallway. “Baby, I gotta run to the ATM. I’ll be back in a minute.” “Is this about that stupid bet you and Jamal made” You should have known better.” “Felicia, please,” he sighed as he put on his coat and gloves. “Bad enough Brick called to remind me, but I really don’t need you saying “I told you so”.” “Well, who held a gun up to your head, telling you to make the bet” Brick and Aaron had bet sometime last year on the Ben Affleck/Jennifer Lopez relationship. Aaron had bet $500 that the marriage wouldn’t last. Brick had bet double that they wouldn’t even make it to the altar. With the recent breakup, Brick had won the bet. Minutes later? Aaron walked up to a Chase bank and walked in. There he saw Brick standing a few feet away from the ATMs with his arms folded. “Bout time you got here,” he said. “Let’s just get this over with.” “Hey, don’t get mad at me. You should have studied J-Lo’s pattern when it comes to her relationships.” “What pattern” What the hell are you talking about?” “Look at the first dude she married. Barely last a year. Then she dated Puffy. They didn’t make it to the altar. Then that cat Judd, marriage lasted like two years. Longer than the first one but still. Then came Affleck.” “I still don’t get what you mean by a pattern.” “The famous dudes she dates, she never marries. But the less than famous cats, she marries then divorces. It goes like boy-girl-boy-girl. Only in this case, it’s “less famous dude-famous dude?” “You know, sometimes you scare me.” “Whatever. Just get my money.” Brick and Aaron then walked over to a free ATM. Aaron slipped his card in, then went through the usual routine of using an ATM. First he pressed the screen option for “English”, and then punched in his PIN number, leaning forward over the screen. “What are you doing?” Brick asked. “Trying to keep people from seeing my PIN number. That way, no one can clean out my account.” Brick sucked his teeth. “Suck your teeth all you want, but it’s happened before.” Aaron then went about choosing “Withdrawal”, “Checking”, and then punching in the amount of money he wanted. Less than 10 seconds later, he received 10 $50 bills. He then counted them off into Brick’s hand. “50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 350, 400, 450, 500. There’s your money. Why did I make that stupid bet?” “Because you’re stupid.” “Real funny.” As Brick and Aaron walked out of the bank, a thought crossed Brick’s mind. “Hey, Aaron, think I got a chance now that J-Lo’s single?”
