The Man-Ox Cometh (Reload Hype)

3 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-03-06 Jack Duncan stands before a camera, naked save for a loincloth. The camera slowly circles his massive, muscular body, showing every scar. The camera zooms in on a large, ragged scar on his right shoulder blade. .......................................................... Jack Duncan lays in the middle of the Arizona desert, completely unconscious. A vulture sits on his back, and thrusts it’s beak into Jack’s right shoulder and ripping out a chunk of flesh. A rock flies through the air and hits the buzzard, scaring it away. An old man walks over and checks on Jack. After seeing that Jack is alive, the old man begins dragging him away. .......................................................... The Camera zooms back out and continues to circle Jack, focusing on a large jagged scar that encircles Jack's entire body just below his ribs. .......................................................... Armed with only a spear, and with an oxygen tank strapped to his bare back, Jack Duncan floats out in the waters, several miles off the coast of South Africa. Suddenly, he feels something beneath him. Jack: Sub... Jack searches around frantically, places the oxygen mask back on, and dives under. The water is cloudy, and he cannot see much around him. He raises his head back up, and removes the mask. Jack: Where are you... WHAM! Jack is struck by a solid brick wall, and is launched into the air about a yard. He nails the water hard and is forced under, his oxygen tank ripped from his body and cast into the deep blue sea. Jack thrashes about and regains his composure, then resurfaces and readies his spear. Suddenly, Jack feels the very ocean give way below him. He jerks his head down and watches an enormous shadow jet towards him from the bottom of the ocean. Jack: Steady Jack, steady... Closer the beast comes. Jack: Almost time... The monster is almost upon Jack. He raises his spear. He knows the shark will try to grab him in its jaws and propel itself into the air, allowing it to stun him and drag him down deep. Jack: From the heart of Hell... Only inches away! Jack: I stabbeth thee! Jack brings the spear down right into the head of the shark, and it sinks in deep, driven by the power of the Man-Ox. However, it is not enough to stop the force of the great fish, as Jack is taken up in its mouth, and soars high into the air with it. Jack retains a tight grip on his spear, and digs in deep as he is inverted and along with the shark is driven back into the water. As Jack is forced down at a great speed, he opens his eyes and stares up at what will most likely be his killer. The shark seems to stretch on and on forever, seemingly...thirty feet. As Jack takes in the gargantuan size of his foe, his heart begins to beat. He is face to face with the SUB! Jack knows now that he must survive this encounter, must live to tell the tale. He begins to writhe and wiggle, but he cannot see in the now crimson waters. He relinquishes his hold on his weapon, and begins pounding on the face and nose of the shark, to no avail. The Sub's teeth are almost completely locked together, and Jack is almost completely ripped in half. .......................................................... The camera zooms back out and focuses on Jack's right forarm. There is a scar from an old stab wound. .......................................................... Jack Duncan is sitting on the grass, propped up against a wheel of the bus. He head hangs backwards, laying on top of the wheel, and his legs extend straight out in front of him. He appears to be dead to the world, save one eye which remains partially open. A shadow appears on the ground next to him, and a man steps out from around the side of the bus carrying a small dagger in his hand. The man creeps in slowly, then, just as he gets right on top of Jack, the man thrusts the knife straight toward Jack's throat. Jack swings his right arm up and the blade sinks into his forearm. The man keeps a hold of the knife, so Jack reaches over and grabs the handle of the knife, making sure the man can't pull it out and attempt to use it again. Jack pulls his legs up and leaps to his feet, driving the top of his skull into the man's face. Once on his feet, Jack headbutts the man a few more times, the backhands him, sending the man to the ground. Jack rips the knife from his arm, then walks over to the man and kneels down. Jack puts a hand over the man's mouth, then shoves the blade into his left side, driving it into the heart. .......................................................... The camera pans to show two puckered scars in Jack's abdomen, and one in his thigh. .......................................................... In Don Bob's mansion, Duncan kicks another door down and opens up with the Uzi, mowing down five men. After all the bodies have hit the floor, the Hoss realizes he took three rounds, two in the abdomen and one in the thigh. Jack winces and shrugs off the pain, then slowly begins to limp down another corridor. .......................................................... The camera zooms out and moves behind Jack, then zooms in on two more puckered scars in the middle of his back. .......................................................... At the end of the hall, Jack reaches a door. He throws it open, and sprays down the room with Uzi fire. As the gun runs out of ammo, Jack jumps in and grabs a wounder guard by the throat, then snaps his neck. Thinking everyone is dead, Jack hobbles towards a stairway at the center of the room, leading to Don Bob's personal quaters. Suddenly...two shots are fired, and Jack falls to the ground. Steve rises from behind a desk, brandishing a revolver. .......................................................... The camrea moves back to Jack's abdomen, focusing on a short, straight scar. The camera swings around and focuses on an identical scar on Jack's back. .......................................................... Don Bob thrusts at Jack, who parries and comes back with a slash. Bob, although a large man, displays good agility and jumps out the way. He strikes back, but Jack ducks under the attack and stabs at Bob. Bob brings his weapon down to block the blow, then moves in and punches Duncan. Jack staggers backwards and lands on Bob's desk, but quickly pops up and rushes towards his rival. Don Bob lowers his blade and prepares as Jack lashes out with his most fierce strike yet. Duncan stands completely still, his kitana at his side. Don Bob smiles...his sword completely through Jack's midsection. The Hoss struggles a bit on the blade, then hangs limply, staring at his adversasry. .......................................................... The camera moves to Jack's face, focusing on four parallel scars running from one ear to the corner of his mouth. .......................................................... Jack hears a noise behind him and spins around just in time to see another Dracon swinging it's razor sharp talons at his head. Jack tries to dodge, but the Dracon still manages to hit him in the face, leaveing four paralell slashes running from his ear to his mouth. .......................................................... The camera zooms out, showing Jack's entire body once more. He stares directly into the camra with his cold, soulless eyes.
