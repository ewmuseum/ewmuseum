Getting back

29 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Getting back Ronnie Long Global Wrestling Promo #561 Date: 08/29/03 Location: Various places Following Master Of The Mat, Ronnie Long had wanted nothing more than to go straight back to Global Wrestling, and beat seven shades of hell out of Sean Price for taking out his knee. But Deanna put her foot down on that, telling him he needed to take at least a few weeks off to heal up. Ronnie had, for once, listened to her, and consequently, when he went into the doctor’s office two weeks later, the report was good. “Well, Mr. Long. You’ll be wearing a brace on your leg for quite some time, but you’re operating at 90% efficiency. There’s nothing to prevent you from stepping back into a wrestling ring if you want to.” Although Deanna didn’t look thrilled with the prospect – anything but, come to think of it – she forced a smile onto her face as Ronnie turned to her. “Ronnie? This time, bring me home a title. OK?” Ronnie grinned. “It just so happens, my dear, that the person who’s going to be facing retribution, also happens to have the particular title that I think suits my needs best at this current time.” He was referring to Sean Price. ---- However, that wasn’t the way it was going to happen. “When I left, I was ranked 3rd out of the entire fed. Now I’ve been gone for two weeks, no one remembers me anymore, and I get to curtain jerk. I hope this is a temporary thing.” The uncharacteristic cheerfulness that had been in Ronnie following the declaration of his recovery had left by now. His hair was down over his eyes, and his voice was grim again. He walks up the stairs to his apartment, somewhat slowly due to his knee stiffened after two weeks of limited motion. Deanna following close behind, worry the prominent feature of her deep brown eyes. “Please, Ronnie… don’t get so worked up over it.” Surprisingly, he grins again. “Come to think of it, though, I can see why… how many times have I pulled that stunt of saying to the GW execs, ‘I’ll be back in a few’ and then being gone 3 months? Too many, I tell you that much right now. But it’s different… this time, I’m in this for real. I’m not putting this off as long as I can to collect a pay check for doing nothing… Deanna, I can’t wait to get back into the ring. I can’t. Are you SURE you don’t want to come with me?” Deanna shakes her head. “Ronnie, when I trained to wrestle, GW was GWF and it had a Womans division. I’m totally not ready to fight guys like… well, guys like you.” Ronnie shrugs as he unlocks the door to his apartment. Raising an arm in greeting to Jeff Andrews, who’s sprawled on the couch drinking a soda and watching TV, he heads into the kitchen and sticks his head into the refrigerator. Deanna heads off down the hallway to get the mail. “Gotta be something in here… ah.” Coming out with a pack of Ballpark Franks, Long tosses them into the sink, then starts rooting through the kitchen drawers. “Food is good. Anyway…” “Ronnie?” The door slams as Deanna walks into the room, a letter in her hand. “Ron, do you know a guy named Jack Cassidy?” He closes his eyes, trying to remember. “Yeah… guy who tried to mug me a few weeks back. Heh. I taught him better.” “Well, Ronnie, I don’t know exactly what you mean when you say you ‘taught him’… but you should know, he’s suing you.” “He’s WHAT?!” Forgetting about the hot dogs, Long drops the package, leaving the franks to roll around the floor. “I can’t fucking believe this…”
