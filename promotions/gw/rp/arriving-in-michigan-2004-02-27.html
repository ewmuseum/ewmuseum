Arriving in Michigan

27 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/04 'Yea, I know it was.......Tonight? Can't make it. I just landed here in Lasing, about fifteen, twenty minutes ago or so.....Yeah, I wanted to get here early, and get somethings done...... Yeah, that's right.' In a pair of tight, faded denim blue jeans, black NIKE sneakers, and his upper torso covered by a long, black leather trench coat, Ice walks, with his cell phone hanging from his ear, down a flight of stairs. The flight of stairs leads Ice into a large room filled with people. A large blue sign with large block white letters reads 'BAGGAGE CLAIMS'. Ice pauses momentarily as he reaches the bottom of the staircase, and oversees the room. He continues to hold the phone tightly to his right ear as he walks through the crowd of people, and walks up to the conveyer belt, circling round and round, with suitcase after suitcase on it. 'You know what you guys need to do. I've told you from the begining. This is me and him. Man to man. He didn't beat me last time, and he won't do it this time either..........Yeah, alright. When do you guys plan on getting in?' Ice continues to push his way through the crowd of people, until finally making it to the front of the crowd. He stands there eyeing the belt of luggage, while still listening to the conversation coming through his phone. A large grey suitcase, and small black gym back begin to come around on the belt. Once in range Ice steps forwards and grabs the two pieces of luggage with one hand. Slowly he sets them down on the floor side by side. Picking up the small black gym back, he slings it across his body, before reaching back down and picking up the large grey suitcase. After getting things situated Ice turns and makes his way back through the crowd of people pushing to get to their bags. 'Yeah okay. Well I'll see you when you guys get in. And if not, I'll see you Sunday...... Yep, you too.' With that Ice pulls the shiny, silver cell phone away from his ear and flips it shut, with one flick of his wrist. With the cell phone in his right hand, Ice burrows into his pocket, placing the phone securely inside. Methodically he makes his way back up the flight of stairs he took to get down to the baggage claims area, and re-enters the main lobby area of the small airport he is at. Upon reaching the top of the stairs, Ice stops and glances down at the sparkling silver watch on his left wrist. Ice slowly raises his head, and lets out a small sigh. Ice turns his head to the right and gazes at a large poster. The poster covers a quarter of the wall it is on. The poster had a purple tinted back ground, with two semi - trucks heading for one another. Between the semi - trucks two cards are shown flipping in mid air, with a third one speeing through. Just below the two flippling cars, in the haze of smoke, A lion's head, and a pair of two angry eyes are seen. On the trailer of the left semi is the word ICE, while on the trailer of the right semi is ROKKU. At the bottom bottom of the poster in large black print it reads.... SIXTY Minutes TWO Men ONE Champion GLOBAL WRESTLING : HIGH OCTANE appears on the top right corner of the poster in red block letters. Ice continues to stare at the poster, in almost a euphoric gaze. 'Hey stranger.' Ice's euphoric trance-like state is abruptly ended. He shakes his head clear, and turns to greet the voice. He turns to be greeted with a gigantic ear to ear smile, and warm loving hug. Softly Ice wraps his arms and returns the embrace. 'How are you doing Lara? It's been a while hasn't it?' Star, Ice's deceased girlfriend's sister Lara takes a step back after releasing her embrace of Ice and reassurringly smiles at him. A sad, solumn expression appears on Ice's face. 'It has been quite some time. And I am doing fine. We'll discuss things in the car. Stan is double parked and waiting.' Lara's smile grows wider as she turns away, heading for the exit. Ice shakes his head, and follows her out of the airport, and to a small dark green Sunfire. The trunk is already opened, as Lara and Ice approach the back of the vehicle. After tossing his things into the trunk Ice climbs into the back of the vehicle, as Lara heads into the passenger seat. The doors shut, and the car pulls out of the parking area, and into the open road. Ice gazes out the window at the scenary that passes by. The car is silent, except for the hum of the engine. Lara turns around in her passenger seat. 'So, how have things been for you Brock?' Ice lets out a low sigh before answering. 'Different. I try and keep myself preoccupied as much as I can. But lying down and going to sleep is almost impossible. And then when I do sleep, the dreams I have, they force me to wake up almost instantly, because they are too much. But other than that, I think everything has been fine.' 'I know what you mean. If not for Stan, I don't think I would be able to handle it all.' Ice just nods in response. An awkward silence fills the car once again. It lasts for minutes, before Lara decides to break it's monotany once again. 'I haven't told you the news yet.' 'What's that?' 'We're pregnant. I've known for ....well it'll be four months at the end of March.' A large, ear to ear, happy smile radiates from Lara's face, while speaking of the news. Ice half heartedly smiles back at her. 'We don't know if the baby is a boy or girl yet. And we don't plan to find out until the actual birth. But we do have some names in mind. For a boy.....Addison or Hunter. And if a girl....Star.' Ice's eyes slowly close, and he lowers his head. He pauses like that for a brief moment before turning his head and gazing back out of the window. He watches the scenary pass by without saying a word for moments. Again the car fills with an awkward silence. The car begins to slow down, as Stan pulls into a small housing sub - division. The small, one - story, houses pass by on both sides. The area looks like something you'd see from the 'Brady Bunch'. After circling and winding through almost the entire sub - division, Stan pulls the car into a driveway. The three step out of the Sunfire, and Ice stands and gazes at the small, elegant looking baige sided house. 'Come on, let's get you inside, and unpacked.' Lara and Stan, carrying Ice's bags, walk by him and into the house. Ice slowly follows, just as they reach the front door. Ice steps through the front, wooden door, into a large room, with all white carpeting, and all black leather furniture. A giant, 72 inch big screen tv sits on the far side of the room. Ice looks up and around at the room. Lara takes his hand, and pulls him into the house, and leads him out of the room, and into the kitchen. The kitchen is much smaller. It is all white, with wooden cabinates, and counter tops, and an old style, looking stove. Quickly they walk through the kitchen and into a long hallway. 'Far from the house you remember isn't it? We've spent almost three years trying to finish this up. When stand finally finishes the deck outside, and puts in the hot tub and pool, we'll finally be finished.' 'It looks very nice.' Lara smiles at him, while noticing, how intrigued he is by the many pictures that line both walls of the hallway. While Lara continues to lead him by the hand down the hallway, Ice continues to gaze at the pictures on the walls, until Lara stops. Ice's gazing at the pictures stops as well, as he turns and looks at Lara. 'Since we are turning the guest room into the baby's room. This was the only room left.' Her apologetic tone of voice, causes Ice to give her a confused look. Slowly, with her free hand, she opens the door, and leads Ice into the room. They enter the room. The walks are a mixture of gold and black, and the bed spread matches. Upon the walls are numerous pictures, glued directly on the wall, without frames. Each of the pictures have a common reoccurrance.....Ice is in each of them. His look of confusion fades, and his eyes widen, as he realizes now the reason for Lara's apologetic tone. We haven't touched this room since the day she moved in. And still do not plan to touch it either. If you don't feel comfortable using the room, I completely understand, but it is the only room we have free to offer you. 'No.........it's fine.' Ice takes a step into the room, and stands there, frozen, as he looks around at the pictures. Slowly he takes another step into the room. And then another, and another, and one more, until he finally reaches the bed in the middle of the room. A small blue book sits atop the small wooden night stand, next to the bed. Ice picks up the book and opens it. A looks of shock emerges on his face. Still staring at the book he takes a seat on the side of the bed. 'I will....uh.... leave you alone. Let me know if you need anything.' Ice doesn't respond to Lara. He simply continues to stare at the contents of the blue book, slowly turning page, after page. Ice's body begins to tremble, and suddenly the book escapes his grip, and falls to the floor. The book falls open, and it is now seen what Ice was staring at......a collection of pictures of him and Star. One picture, which is larger than the rest taking up a whole page on its own, stands out. The picture is of Lara and Stan in their wedding outfits on the left. Star in her bridesmaid gown and Ice in a black tuxedo on the right. Around Star and Ice in red is a hart, and in black marker across the bottom of the picture it says. Future Bride and My Groom Ice's head falls into his hands, and a single tear drop falls onto the picture. FADE
