Chaz Who? I've got bigger fish to fry(Leviathan, Bryant, Tragic Hero, and Quartz)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Wednesday The living room of Deacon Holmes? 1.5 million dollar penthouse is shown. The navy blue walls with white trim are covered in various Deacon Holmes posters, the most noticeable being the black and white one that reads “Deacon” in bold letters at the bottom and has a picture of him standing triumphantly with his arms outstretched atop a long set of stairs. Each poster represents a different movie but with the leading man substituted with Deacon Holmes, except one. A large framed picture of Holmes beaten and bloodied holding a belt over his head inside of a steel cage with ringropes made of electrically charged barbed wire. It sits above a trophy case with crowns carved out of the tops of the posts and glass shelves. Inside of it sits several golden wrestling trophies of varying size and shape, the largest of which sits alone on the top shelf. In the middle the room’s polished wood floor is a gray leather couch and two large matching reclining chairs. In front of these chairs sits a fifty-six inch flatscreen TV with surround sound. CW sits legs crossed in one of these chairs smoking a cigar while reading the newspaper clad in a loud italian shirt, dress slacks, and a pair of loafers. He also wears a long platinum chain. He slowly glances at his watch before turning to the next section and returning to his paper. Through the white framed windows of the quiet house a storm can be seen raging outside. Rain is falling in buckets with near hurricane force winds attempting to blow birds from the sky and trees on their sides. The rumble of thunder breaks the silence of the apartment soon followed by a flash of lightning that pierces the blackened sky. CW grabs the remote and flips on the TV to find out that there is a tropical depression quickly moving into the area before picking up the paper once again. At just that moment the front door bursts open with leaves among other debris from outside being blown in. CW leaves his chair and goes to the door to find a muscular figure cloaked in shadows heaving in front of the door. Lightning pierces the air once again and the figure turns out to be none other than Deacon Holmes soaking wet on the front door’s mat which reads “Don’t go away angry, just go away.” He stands there panting in a pair of AND1 shorts, JordanXVII’s, and a white tanktop with water dripping from his dreadlocks as he hangs his head in exhaustion. Lightning flashes again to show him now looking up shooting CW a threatening look. Holmes is fuming. The waters appears to be converting to steam as it touches his body as he steps through the front door slamming it behind him causing CW to back away in order to give him space and put himself outside of arm’s reach. Holmes just stares at him for a moment before storming right past him, past the full liquor bar, and off into the back of the house. CW looks on confused and puffs on his cigar before going back to his newspaper. Just as he hits the chair Holmes stomps back into the room in a new set of clothes consisting of a pair of red nike shorts and a black t-shirt with a large eye on it that reads “behind the twinkle in your eyes I can see the bitch in you.” He stares angrily at CW before opening his mouth. CW looks up from his paper. Deacon Holmes: Now that I’ve given you a moment to collect your thoughts well enough to form a decent excuse, you mind tellin? me what tha hell that was about!” CW: What do you mean? I don’t know what you’re talking about. Deacon Holmes: Don’t give me that shit, you know exactly what I’m talking about! You left me at the gym after you made me do that damn gauntlet again! CW: Well I figured you may want to finish up your workout with a light jog. Deacon Holmes: Light jog!” Thirteen miles!” That’s almost a fuckin? marathon! CW: Half D, stop being so damn dramatic. You wanna go in the back and have a good cry? We could hold hands...ya panzy! So you had to walk home, so what. Deacon Holmes: Walk!” Have you looked outside lately? There’s a fuckin hurricane out there! I was running for my life! CW: Tropical depression, stop whining. Those winds aren’t even gale force yet, that storm don’t even have a name and you’re in here cryin! How are you my nephew? Deacon Holmes: Don’t toy with me CW. CW: Alright then, I’ll be serious wit ya then boy. You’re in a world of shit right now. Leviathan, Tragic Hero, and Quartz are all ready to kick your ass after what you did on Monday and on top of that you’ve got that lunatic Chaz Bryant out running around telling people he knows your crimes. What tha hell did you do now? Deacon Holmes: Hmmm, Chaz Bryant, Chaz Bryant, doesn’t ring a bell. CW: What do you mean it doesn’t ring a bell? He’s your next opponent, you meet him in the ring in less than two days. How can you not know who he is? Deacon Holmes: I don’t know, probably has something to do with his utter lack of importance. I don’t even know why the GW is making me waste my time on this clown anyway, I’ve got more important things to do. CW: Like what? Chasing that lizard man around? Or maybe gettin beaten up by the sissy in the mask and the missing link before they skip off together into the setting sun whistling dixie like three cheerful assholes. You’ve already lost to that freak Leviathan. Deacon Holmes: Yeah but... CW: Yeah but nothin! You like losin? You like being laying unconscious on national TV while some other guy’s music plays in the arena as he’s having his hand raised? Cause that’s what’s been happening to you! Deacon Holmes: Don’t even try to pull that crap with me, I’ve whipped Leviathan’s ass twice since that match! Everyone knows he don’t wanna run into me again. CW: So what if you got him back, he got you first! Don’t forget who pinned wh... Deacon Holmes: Ooh! I just thought of what that clown was talking about! CW: Who? Deacon Holmes: Chaz Bryant. CW: Well what tha hell did you do? Deacon Holmes: I had sex with his mom. CW: You what? You’re just great at giving people reasons to kick your ass aren’t you? Deacon Holmes: I didn’t know, it was right before I came to GW. I was up in Georgia for Freaknik and met up with this chick named Elaine Bryant. We started rappin and that’s how it happened...she was a freak too. Holmes walks over to the bar, pours a glass of whiskey and then takes a seat next to CW in front of the TV. He takes a sit out of the cup and turns on the TV, Shaft is on. Deacon Holmes: No big deal though, he’ll probably get arrested before our match anyway. He’s been runnin around killin people for weeks now. CW: You’d better hope so, you’ve got enough problems right now without some doped out Punisher wannabe chasing you around. Deacon Holmes: Yeah, yeah, those guys just hate me cause they ain’t me. Everyone knows I’m the best thing goin right now in GW. I can’t help it if everyone wants a piece. CW: Well it seems like it’s going the other way around and you’re the one ending up with a piece of everyone. The foot, usually in your ass. Deacon Holmes: They’re all just jealous man, I’m blowin up like spontaneous human combustion while all these other losers are stuck. Basic crab mentality. If you can’t get out snatch and someone who can and pull him back down. Misery loves company and he’s about as miserable as they come. I kinda feel for tha guy though, I mean he’s goin nowhere and just wants to try and jumpstart his career by messin with me. Kinda late to try and make moves but I give him his props...even if he is only as talented as a bag of rocks and runs around masquerading in make up and spouting off haiku poetry. If he’s one thing though it’s lucky. He’d be in somebody’s hospital right now if it weren’t for the missing link showing up to save his ass on Thursday. What tha hell is that guy’s name anyway? Limestone? Graphite? Boron? CW: Quartz. Deacon Holmes: Yeah that’s the one, he should be off powering watches somewhere rather than trying to mess with me. Last time he tried me he got choked out and DDT’d right on the ramp. Now he’s facing me on Monday. He can’t possibly hope to beat me without anything short of a miracle. That clown’s probably off cowering under a pile of soiled mattresses somewhere praying to god I never find him after what happened on Evolution. I’ve got all these clowns right where I want em unc. CW: We’ll see... Deacon Holmes: Whatever man, hand me the phone, I wanna call Elaine up about stoppin through there on my way to Texas. Deacon Holmes finishes his whiskey and gets up to go get the phone to call none other than Elaine Bryant, Chaz’s mom.
