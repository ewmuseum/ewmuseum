Signposts Chapter 5: Restrained Conversation...

15 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/10/04 Winters awoke feeling decidedly groggy. He couldn’t forma coherent thought in his head, but despite the intense feeling of severe displacement, Winters quickly realised that he’d been restrained. As far as he could tell, he was in a sitting position with, what felt like, leather straps pinning his wrists and his feet down. It wasn’t the most comfortable of positions to find yourself in. The room he found himself in - and he assumed that it was a room - was almost pitch black, the soft caress of moonlight delicately massaging the outer extremities of his peripheral vision. Where the hell was he? Winters? memory swept over him, suddenly, like a tidal wave of its shore. She shot him with a tranquilliser dart. The woman who claimed to be his “guide” shot him with a tranquilliser dart, which - in truth - he could not blame her for. Ultimately, the lack of information provide proved to be his ultimate downfall. That and his paranoia, a trait that he’d now discovered owned a large section of his soul. Based on the information provided, his instincts had taken over, instincts that had ultimately been trained and chiselled into what they are, since adulthood. Winters truly believed that his instincts was the only aspect of himself that had been carried over through the amnesiac incident. His instincts were the only he could truly trust and if paranoia was a small section of his instincts then so be it. He must have had reason to be overly paranoid before losing his memory and it had become a sort of knee-jerk reaction. Not a bad knee-jerk reaction to have, truth be told. Essentially, the beautiful brunette had been speaking the truth, to a certain extent. She was there waiting for me upon my arrival, whether she was going to tell me more about my past was another matter entirely. She had a good number of goons with her that looked a trap to Winters, so her true intent was still very much in question. A number of good men have been put down due to the effects of a beautiful woman. Winters didn’t intend to be the next one in line for this impressive accolade. Winters attention was effectively captured by the sight of a silhouetted figured delicately brushing through the soothing glow of the moonlight kiss, and the sound of bare foot steps - trying to be disguised - on carpet, drawing ever closer to him. “Yes?” He said, now realising that the approaching figure was closer to him than he originally perceived. “Impressive.” The woman’s voice promptly replied. Winters instantly recognised the harsh, croaking voice of the brunette guide, who sounded as though she was only a couple of feet away, to his left. “Is it?” Winters quickly responded, violently pulling at the leather straps that pinned him down. Apparently, he hated being helpless. Then the lights flickered into life and he saw stood there in front of him, wearing practically nothing. He felt his boxer shorts shrink. “Yes,” She said. “You should’ve been awake for a good number of hours.” “Oh lucky me,” He replied, “care to tell me why I’m strapped to the chair?” The brunette smiled. “I’m sure you can work that one out for yourself. You hospitalised three people.” “Ah,” he replied, “it’s because I beat up those thugs right” You prepared an ambush for me and decided to perform a pre-emptive strike.” The brunette chuckled. “No, they weren’t - aren’t - thugs. They’re you’re body guards.” Winters couldn’t help but laugh at this. “Body guards” For me?” “Indeed. Good, no?” “But why?” “Because you need them and that is all that I can say.” “Okay?” Then, from the back room, a big bursts in. “Ma’am, something’s wrong. We’ve lost contact with our man on the outside of the building’I think they’ve found us!” “Who’ve found us?” Winters asked, obviously bemused by what was going on. The brunette unfastened the leather straps around his wrists, then began working on the ones around his ankles. “Don’t concern yourself with that now. Just follow Mr. Grey here and get the hell out of the building!” “Where will you be?” Winters asked, watching as she finished unfastening the ankle straps. “I,” she said, pulling out a rather hefty-looking side arm. “Will be completing my mission.” Mr. Grey took Winters by the arm and dragged him out of the room.
