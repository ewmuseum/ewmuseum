The Gray Area:<br>Transgression & Repenting

Date: 07.24.04 “When offered a choice of your own death or the death of another, would you choose to sin or to die a savior” Such a huge difference comes from such an answer although I wonder if, in the end, it’s really all just black and white?” -Mikhail Makya The birthplace of Maverick Reno from a bird’s point of view. The landscape is beautiful, the horizon is breathtaking, and the main cities are architectural wonders. Known for world-renowned athletes, clean air, and a really bad image in a certain animated movie, Canada is also known as one of the purest lands in the world. Although the city bustles with the speed of your modern day American metropolis, the countrysides far surpass America’s in their beauty. Canada has remained pure for hundreds of years and will remain that way for many to come. The devil himself grew here, developing first into the man we once knew as The Enigma, the Savior, and the Guardian Angel, and then later into the man who is quite different. This man, we symbolically call “Le Diabolique.” The view continues to pan over beautiful Canadian countryside until finally giving us a view of a marvelous modern mansion. Three stories complete with a single spire that is topped by a high glass dome, used as a sunroom. Two skylights are elsewhere atop the marvelous home, one made from stained glass looking down into the office of Maverick Reno and the other staring down into the dining room a few rooms away. Behind the home, a medium size home acts as a guesthouse. The home is nothing other than exquisite but to continue with the details would either bore you, or create very envious people. The nearest home is barely in sight even from this elevated viewpoint, making us wonder how much of this land Maverick actually owns. A few vehicles travel along the empty road, approaching the estate of Maverick Reno. Finally, the vehicles pull into the driveway. The first of the entourage is a black BMW, the second is a limousine and the third, a black hummer. The vehicles unload quickly: Christina McCoy is the first to step from the limousine, Naria and Tobias from the BMW and a few guards exit the Hummer. Maverick slips from inside the limousine and glances back. Scott Pierce is the next and last out. From this point of view, they’re like ants moving about in pointless patterns. It’s as if they’re just pointless specs; “dust in the wind” one could say. This must be how Maverick sees the world. ---- In four days, Global Wrestling will meet to determine the top two men in the federation. In the past month, Josh has been determined to be the indisputable World Champion and therefore, number one man in the federation. Nipping at his heels has been Maverick Reno after defeating him in singles competition and garnering a World Championship match more than a month ago. This would make Maverick the number two man in Global Wrestling. So what will Master of the Mat determine? The Tournament will determine the new number two man, be that it remains Maverick Reno, or even if Josh takes the spot in some turn of events. Eight competitors will have the chance to take the spot and all eight are ready to step up to the plate. The number two man (or woman) will be the Master of the Mat and will therefore earn his or her spot in the main event next month in Detroit, Michigan in the New Global Arena against the World Champion. Determining the number one spot is perhaps the tightest race of the evening. Josh is forced to step into the ring and defend his coveted Championship against the number one contender, Maverick Reno. Josh is perhaps one of the most popular and successful World Champions that Global Wrestling has ever known in its long and lasting lifespan. A member of the Elite, Josh has held nearly every title in Global Wrestling and has also won several awards. With friend Ken Partridge Jr., Josh stands tall against Maverick Reno inside the Global Arena. But a man’s ability to talk and trade insults with another is void if he’s unable to stand toe to toe with the same man in the ring. When the world looks at Josh, do they see a true Champion or do they see a boy afraid of the man who’s gunning for him? Maverick Reno has been known in Global Wrestling as several different things: The Enigma, The Guardian, The Avenger, Le Diabolique, and The Untouchable One. Maverick’s Global career began more than two years ago and has resulted in holding every single title at some point in time excluding the Platinum Title. For the past three months, he’s held the Tag Titles with extreme dominance in the division with two partners, Derek Draven and John Zero. The former World Champion wishes to return to his days on top of the wrestling world and sees that Josh is in his way. The lengths that Reno will go to take Josh out of the picture are truly unnerving and this alone inspires fear in his opponent. When the world looks at Maverick Reno, do they see a champion or do they see a murderer? ---- July 26th, 2004 ? Approximately 2:00 PM London, Ontario, Canada ? Reno Estate “This place seems to grow every time I see it.” Scott glances at Maverick as they walk through the main doors. Christina stands at Maverick’s side, remaining fairly quiet for the moment. Maverick allows his Cheshire grin to overtake his face, “It’s a veritable Rose Red, Scott. I suggest you not wander off alone?” Scott glances at him, laughing at his joke. “Your bedroom is upstairs. Naria, could you please show my guest to his quarters?” Maverick glances to Christina, “I have another guest to entertain for the time.” Scott shakes his head and glances to Naria who begins leading him up a stairwell. A pair of guards walk behind them, carrying Scott’s things at their sides. After the four are out of sight, Maverick smiles and turns to Christina. “It’s going to be marvelous. Just another aspect of our work coming to life!” Maverick gloats, prompting a mischievous little smile from Christina. “I’m thinking more of the last time we were here. The last time we were fully alone together?” Christina’s insinuation does not go unnoticed. Maverick wraps his arms around Christina’s waist and pulls her close; she leans up, her lips brushing Maverick’s earlobe. “Think we have time to slip away to the sauna for a little” relaxation?” Maverick glances at the stairway and to a pair of guards just out of earshot. He motions up at them and then calls out, giving him a few orders, “When Scott and Naria return, tell them that Miss McCoy and I have a bit of paper work to deal with in my office?” “Yes, sir!” one of the guards replies before walking back to his post. Christina leans up and kisses Maverick, her eyes closed softly as are his, both lost in the moment. Christina’s hand finds Maverick’s cheek and drifts down to grab his Mandarin collar. She pops the button, loosening the shirt just slightly before tugging and walking in the opposite direction. Christina takes a step back, letting her hand fall limp and then down as she turns and starts walking away. She grins even though he cannot see it, knowing that he’s watching her every movement. Maverick begins to follow and grabs Christina by the shoulders and tugs her back against him; she glances over her shoulder and up into Maverick’s eyes. “Yes?” Maverick, without so much as a word, picks Christina up and starts down the hallway. She laughs, shaking her head to allow her blonde locks to fall about face. “You know, you still owe me for everything I did for you?” Christina speaks, glancing back as she hangs over his shoulder. Maverick grabs a door handle and walks inside to reveal a beautiful restroom. “Then consider this the down payment.” The door closes again, leaving us on the outside. ? Reno Estate Basement “I’m warning you, Maverick, I’m not as fast as I used to be?” Scott admits, now adorned in a pair of Adidas pants and a tank-top. He may not be as fast as he used to be, but he’s certainly not let his exercising diminish too much. “limping all the time prevents me from doing a few of the old stunts, as well.” “No worries, Scott. I just need someone fast enough to catch me off guard.” Maverick states, standing before him. The two men stand on a large mat as would be seen in a gym. Maverick is wearing a pair of straight leg gray pants and no-shirt, his wrists and hands taped up. “Let’s start with you striking at me” and I’ll try and block you.” Scott shrugs and puts his hands up, both men staring at the other. Neither wear any padding at this time, so sure of the other’s abilities. Scott throws a right and Maverick blocks and spins in to put an elbow to the jaw of Scott’s face without actually making contact. “Cutting it a little close there, Mav.” Scott grins and leans back as if he had been hit, ducks under Maverick’s arm and delivers a kick over his own head to the back of Maverick’s. “You still rely too much on single strikes to stun your opponent. You blocks are great and well thought out but be ready. If they missed, so can you.” “This is why I came to you.” Maverick takes a few steps back, both of them returning to their starting point. “And you say you’re not as good as you used to be. I could never perform that kick?” “Sure you could” if you were as good as me when I was in full health.” Scott grins, his jab finding Maverick dead on. “Ouch, that’s cold, Scott. Really cold?” Maverick retorts, preparing for Scott’s next move. Scott moves in with a single strike again, this time, Maverick ducks the hit and comes up at Scott’s back to apply a modified cobra clutch. Scott moves slightly, attempting to free himself. “Yeah, yeah. I’m a cold bastard. I know” now, tell me what you’ve done wrong?” Scott asks, still in the hold. Maverick looks confused as he leans his neck back, attempting to examine the hold a little better, “I” I don’t know.” “You put a crippled man in a sleeper hold, that’s what you did wrong! Let me go!” Maverick begins to laugh and as he steps back, Scott turns with a back hand that Reno ducks quickly and comes up again. Maverick grabs Scott and quickly drops to his back, performing a reverse Russian leg sweep. Intentionally, Maverick fell to his side so that Scott wouldn’t get hurt. “Now that was good. Try to rely on moves like that, Maverick. The more you can hold onto your opponent and see them all the way through to the impact, the more likely that the move will take effect. And often, they’re going to cause more damage.” “Right.” Maverick nods and pushes himself up. He offers his hand to Scott who takes it and stands as well. “So, I’ve not been sure if I should ask this” but how’ve you been, Scott? Are things better for you now?” Scott pauses, his eyes on Maverick for a moment before stepping back and then back in with a midsection kick. Reno is caught off guard and is hit in the ribs. “Pay attention, Mav”.” Scott demands before moving back. Maverick nods and gets into position; Scott steps in and spins this time, delivering a twisting back-kick. Maverick again takes the hit like a man. “Well” I just hope no one kicks at you or you’ll be screwed.” Scott smirks before stepping back. “Let’s try this” you kick at me.” Maverick nods and performs the first kick that Scott did. Scott ducks below the kick and lifts his arms on either side of Maverick’s leg before lifting his body and tossing Reno to the floor. “That was” different.” Maverick states, reaching back to hold his head. “What was that?” “Capoeira. It’s not normally your style and you won’t be able to use it often but it could give you an edge against a few people who aren’t expecting it.” Scott offers Reno his hand this time and pulls him up. “You could also grab the leg and possibly break it from that position if you tried.” “Hmm, it’s worth a shot” the question is if I’m able to do it or not.” Maverick returns to his place and motions for Scott to come at him again. Scott performs the same kick and this time, Maverick attempts the block he had just learned. He ducks under and then comes up, holding Scott’s leg without throwing him backwards. “You didn’t answer my question before?” Scott lowers his leg and nods, “I know.” Scott motions for Maverick to attack him, “now the second kick.” Maverick takes a step back and spins, delivering a twisting back-kick in Scott’s direction. Scott lifts his knee at an angle, spinning on his left leg to deliver his knee right into the middle of Maverick’s extended leg. Maverick stumbles with the impact and Scott continues his spin, ending it with a high roundhouse, the bottom of his heel resting right above Maverick’s shoulder, his hands on the floor. “You see” I’m using my entire body to add more force to the kick. This way, even if you mess up and fall, you don’t have far to go? and it really helps with balance. Plus, if you do make contact, it could very easily knock them out.” Maverick nods and takes a few steps back again, his eyes on Scott. Scott delivers a straight punch and the two perform simple blocks and evasions as well as follow ups, receiving nods and nothing more. Finally, Scott speaks again, answering Maverick’s question. “I think about them every single day” and I know that when they died, part of me went with them.” ---- I can’t help but wonder, does every victim by association that I’ve effected feel this way? Does each one suffer this much torment? ---- Scott slows a moment and reaches down to hold his leg, stretching it for a moment. Maverick stops and watches his friend before the two continue on. Scott spins in and Maverick attempts the kick-block that he had just learned. However, Scott suddenly reverts his spin and comes from the opposite direction without any warning. At the same time, Scott drops low and sweeps Maverick’s feet right out from under him. “Hmm, misdirection is fun, isn’t it?” “You’re enjoying this aren’t you?” Maverick asks as he pushes himself up, his eyes on his old partner. Scott laughs a bit and shakes his head. “More than you can know” but a little hint, the eyes always give it away? the slightest twitch, the obvious glance. There’s also a tell-tell sign in the eyes. Oh, and if someone actually tries to do that spin twist to you?” Just put a thrust kick right in the center of their back. Simple solution.” Scott smiles and takes a few steps back, the two men again returning to their starting positions. Scott performs a straight thrust kick and Maverick slides one leg up and bends it to his chest as he falls straight to the mat, his elbows catching him before he can make complete contact. With his bent leg, he places the ball of his foot right against Scott’s other leg. “Not bad, Mav”. But how would you follow that up?” “You know, I’d probably wait for them to fall and then go after the leg a bit more.” Maverick answers him honestly, still lying on the mat while Scott stands at his feet. “Although, I assume you have a better suggestion?” “Well, kind of a go-between. Your idea is good and it’s the point, but you don’t want them to get away. So instead of letting them fall straight to the mat, bring your other leg up and whip them right in the ass with your calf. They’ll fall face first, and you’ll have the momentum to follow them.” Scott explains, gesticulating. “Hmm, I’ll give that a shot.” Maverick starts as he pushes himself up to his feet. “Where have you been working since you closed the gym?” Scott motions for Maverick to come at him and he does so, offering a straight elbow strike. Scott grabs Maverick’s arm and twists under and then jerks him down before using him to head-stand-flip over, wrenching his arm to a whole new degree. Maverick jerks away, “Damnit, that hurt!” “Sorry, Mav”.” Scott offers as he kneels aside his ally. “I’ve been working as a therapist in the hospital I went too” the staff there helped me so much, I dunno. I guess I just wanted to give something back.” Scott explains as Maverick again stands up. Scott motions for Maverick to step closer as he speaks, the two men locking up. “Ok, you lock up with a guy my size in the ring. He’s faster, more agile, and able to get around you if you take your eyes off of him for too long. What do you do?” Maverick glances down and then attempts to lock the two men up even more but Scott shakes his head, ducks down and plants a shoulder into Reno’s midsection. Reno staggers back and Scott quickly flips into a headstand and places his legs around Reno’s neck. “You were just laid out” might I suggest another approach?” “That’s what you’re here for.” Maverick retorts as Scott stands normally again and the two men lock up. Scott quickly jerks his hands and arms down and shoves them back up between Reno’s, wedging them farther apart. “First, use your strength to break free then throw his arms off of you before he can react. Next, your going to want to disable your opponent. You can do that a number of ways from here” a neck thrust, an elbow strike, a knee to the midsection, or a blatant head butt. No matter what, it has to last long enough for you to grab them and do something that’s going to last?” Scott explains, going through the motions as he does so. Maverick nods, “Makes sense.” The two men lock up again and Maverick ducks under an arm and applies a sleeper only to let the hold go and return to the starting point. Scott nods and again, they lock up. “Do you see your family much anymore?” “Not really.” Scott replies before stepping back and sending in a couple of kicks for Reno to evade. “Well, you obviously don’t need work with mat technique. You’re still number one when it comes to mat technique.” The two continue working for a moment more when a far door opens wide. Aiden sticks his head in, catching the attention of the two men. “Maverick” There are some papers for you to sign and the office needs them faxed in. Also, Mr. Pierce, there’s a call for you upstairs.” Aiden smiles and takes his leave of the room. Scott walks towards the door, his limp barely noticeable. Maverick grabs a towel and begins to wipe a bit of sweat from his forehead. Scott stops at the door and glances back at Maverick. “Mav, to answer your question, I am ok” it’s taken a lot to move on from what happened. I’ll never get over it? but I have moved on with my life. I’m able to enjoy myself again, to live my life. I still have to try and not hate at times? only because, it’s so hard not to blame myself.” Scott sighs and lowers his head for just a moment, “But this time working with you has really helped” although, I want to ask you a question, Maverick? it’s just something I need to know.” Maverick furrows his brow, looking at his friend. “What is it, Scott?” Maverick slowly approaches Scott, curious, “Anything you want to know?” Maverick gives a half smile as he grabs the door, reading to walk out behind Scott. ?” did you kill John Russell?” The question hits the ground with the weight of a thousand lies. Scott looks at Maverick who is speechless with shock. Scott looks down at the floor as the room grows tense and the atmosphere pensive. Maverick takes a step back finally and lifts his eyes to Scott’s, unsure of what to say. Before Maverick can speak, Scott speaks up again, “I shouldn’t have asked that, should I?” Maverick glances down for just a moment. The room is tense still, even with Scott’s half-apology. Finally Maverick looks up and speaks softly, “You have a phone call, Scott?” ---- Life is fragile. Throughout life, the being balances upon a thin line where one slip could be the end. There’s black and there’s white and no in between although, what determines the death of a person as good or bad is the reasoning behind the death. The death of Johnny Russell was a matter of judge, jury, and executioner and Maverick was all three. Had John’s death been the end of the evening, it would have been one death for a reason: punishment for crimes perpetrated. However, John’s punishment and then his death was based on an old principal, An eye for an eye. Maverick slew his family before him and forced him to watch and then proceeded to brutally beat him. John Russell died that night, not once, but twice. The first time he died was when he saw the house go up in flames, knowing that his wife and children were tied together in the foyer. John died when the lifeline attacked to his hand, as Maverick called it, stopped jerking. John would die again minutes later as his body could no longer hold out after losing so much blood. The EMT’s, police, and fire fighters arrived in time to watch him take his last breath. Maverick Reno may have been giving retribution but in his rampage, went a step farther. Maverick lowered himself to the same level as John had to begin with. It was on that day? that Maverick truly became a monster. It was on that day, that Maverick felt the power of the beast and was fully and forever tarnished. Vengeance led him that far, power and hatred took him over the edge. Since that time, Maverick has far surpassed that line time and time again. But in all of that time, this is the first time that Maverick has had to face up to the fact to someone whose opinion he actually cares about. ---- A million thoughts and a million questions. How do you answer that question? How do you tell a man who has lost everything that you committed the same atrocity to another man? Why did I even let myself get into this position, into this hell? The line has blurred? life is not black and white. Life and death have become as everything else: a gradient from white to gray to black as well as everything in between. Maverick stands in the center of the gray, looking from the white to the black, unsure of where he truly stands. When this all started, he stood closer to the white? although now, he fears that he belongs in the black. July 27th, 2004 ? Approximately 3:30 AM London, Ontario, Canada ? The office of Maverick Reno at The Reno Estate Maverick Reno sits in his office, dressed in a pair of sweat pants and no top. His hair hangs loosely about his head, his frame barely illuminated by the desk lamp. Maverick’s eyes show his weariness, his body sore and overworked from the extreme work that he and Scott have been undertaking in the last week. But now, even more than his body, Maverick’s mind bears the pressure of defeat. Softly, Maverick speaks as his hands lift to his forehead, his eyes closing as if the gravity of the Earth were pulling the lids down. “No” he’s safe? he understands.” Maverick sounds as if he’s not even there, his voice distant and yet, seemingly unconvinced. His eyes clench now and he glances up, peering at the beautiful skylight above. “He’s” my friend. He understands and more than anyone else knows why I did what I did? he’s not going to turn us in.” Maverick looks ahead ? The voice echoes throughout the room, as if speaking directly to us. “You know that he’s a risk, now. You can’t risk John and Deacon. All that you’ve worked for” you know what you must do, that it was your decision from the beginning.” Maverick shakes his head no wildly before burying his face in his hands. “NO!” Maverick screams out, denouncing the voice. “Then you truly are death incarnate” for you have brought it upon yourself, John, Deacon, and even Christina! Do not fool yourself? you know what must be done?” Maverick stands abruptly, tossing his chair backwards with his momentum and hurling his flat-screen monitor to the floor. The crash is loud and the silence that follows is strictly defined because of it. Maverick’s breathing finally overtakes the silence? “Do it” “No”.” Maverick clenches his eyes again, placing his palms down atop his desk, his body shaking. “I c’can’t.” Just then the door opens. Maverick’s eyes refresh and shoot up, spying the door before him as Christina steps through, wearing one of Maverick’s button-up shirts. She glances around the room, unable to see the monitor lying behind the desk. “Maverick” who are you just talking to?” “Myself, Chris” no one else.” Maverick glances towards a darkened shadow and then back to Christina. For her, he offers a smile. Christina motions for him to follow her as she steps back out into the hallway, “Well come to bed, I’m sure you’re tired?” Maverick glances down at the monitor laying in pieces about the floor, his eyes clearing quickly from his daze. If we were to ask him how the monitor got to the floor, he wouldn’t be able to recall at this very moment as he was in such a volatile state moments before. It’s as if Christina acted as a neutralizer. Maverick walks around the desk and to Christina, closing the door to the office as they step into the hallway. The two walk down the hallway and Maverick glances back? Suddenly the view changes; we see the world through the eyes of a monster, through the eyes of a walking sin. We see the world as Maverick Reno sees the world. The hallway is blanketed in the pedals of black roses, a blue fog hovering just above that. Maverick's eyes shoot from one direction to the next, Christina barely noting him. He glances back... blue smoke bellows from beneath the office door. The darkened hallway glows with the aura, fog consuming everything behind them. A figure... is it fear? No... is it guilt? A robed man appears, the fog emitting from his sleeves and from underneath his hood. Maverick's eyes light up and he turns away, trying his best to ignore the figure behind him. The voice... The voice echoes, deep and droning, the voice coming from every direction simultaneously. “It is I that comes for you.” ... maybe it is guilt...
