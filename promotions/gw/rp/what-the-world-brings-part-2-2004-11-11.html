What the world brings (part 2)

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/11/04 For today, Salaok had killed his own father, for real. Today Salaok finally stands alone. Dropping what is left of the burnt out filter of his cigarette, Salaok opens his pain stricken eyes and looks out onto the world that had caused him so much torment over the years. Pushing himself off the wall and moving towards the front doors of this mansion, he utters that words that The Lair had been waiting for him to say. “This world will pay once more for what it has done to me. And I will take great pleasure in it this time.” But if only the memories would have stopped there the night may have been able to be a normal one. But with his life as tormented as what is has been, how could the memories just stop? And now as he pushes his hand against the front door into the ice cold house that crime had paid for, the images of the fateful nights that plagued him come rushing back into his mind. Not wanting to relive that dinner again, Salaok moves straight to the stairs and then as a breath of air escapes his lips, he closes his eye to regain his composure the same way he had to every time he walked past the door to the dinning room. Was closing his eyes a good thing to do? No! He had allowed himself to open his mind to the memories tonight and now they just flowed to a head. ---- Moving through the house, trying not to disturb anything as there may be finger prints left around the place that would be vital to find out who was responsible for such a feat. That sound? The sound of humming and low toned voices floating along the dead air and alerting Salaok to what he thought would be the killers. Pulling a small hand gun out of the back of his pants, he lets it hang by his side, trying to conceal it and yet have it available at first sign. The air seemed stale and thick the more he moved towards the stairs and then up them. Each step brought the sound of a small thud as his big boots treaded the wood and then a slight scraping as Salaok had always dragged his feet when walking. But now, now he was picking up the pace as the sound of the low voices began to grow the more he got upstairs and once upstairs, the close he came to Darrell’s room. The thick red carpet was doing it’s best to muffle Salaok’s steps but the floor boards underneath it seemed to be working against him with every creaking that they produced. Swallowing the pain that was building in his heart, he takes a deep breath and begins to prepare himself for what he was about to find. Knowing full well that behind the door would be the body of his only son and the only thing that was left to remind him of Lisha. Another deep breath leaves his lungs and his eyes remain closed as his lips part as he speaks out loud to his lost wife. “I will never stop loving you Lisha. Never. But I fear I have failed you.” Swallowing the pain once more, he opens his eyes and moves his hand up to the door handle. As he begins to turn the handle and the opening between door and frame grows, the air becomes cold as if the room had been closed up for a long time. The voices which he had heard now become loud but are soon dispelled as the voices coming from the television that had been left on. And then Salaok sees him. Sees his son laying in the bed with his eyes closed and looking completely different to what the photos had shown. From where Salaok stands in the doorway, there is no mass of blood and numerous wounds over his body. Even his neck seemed to be untouched and as a tear falls from his check he speaks out to his son quietly, hoping that he is only sleeping and that this had been nothing but a cruel joke. “Darrell?” There is however no answer and so he begins to move into the room and again quietly calls to his son and again there is no response. Laying there with his eyes closed, Darrell looked so peaceful. So peaceful and yet pale. But that was no huge thing as Salaok himself was a pale skinned person and Darrell had taken after him in that sense. But the air, so cold. With each step bringing him closer to his son, the smile on his face grows as the signs of what would have been his murder are not present and the last few steps Salaok actually break out into a small jog, not being able to get to his son fast enough. Dropping to his knees beside the bed, Salaok grabs a hold of Darrell and holds him tightly but it is then that the realization strikes and pain swells to levels reach never before in his body. Darrell’s body was acting more like a rag doll than a sleeping human and it was then that fear drove Salaok’s memory. It was in this very same position that Salaok watched as Lisha died. It was in this very same position that Salaok was in when he found his father dead, or at least thought to be. And now Darrell. Jumping to his feet in the realization, Salaok moves quickly onto the bed and holds Darrell in his arms facing him and only now was Salaok able to see what was on Darrell’s neck. A large piece of tape, painted to match Darrell’s skin color was wrapped around his neck and with Darrell now being held upright, a small droplet of blood forms. Closing his eyes tightly, Salaok ties to dispel the image but he couldn’t. He knew now that the photo’s that had been planted for him to find where the real thing. Looking down at Darrell’s arms, it is only now with a closer look that the small cuts are visible which begins the tears following uncontrollably once again. He had lost Darrell too. His family was all gone now except for those he wanted nothing to do with. Laying young Darrell onto the bed, Salaok stands there in silence with the hatred and pain building within him and only with his body ravaged with such strong emotion is he able to reach down and peel back the tape which covered the wound. A move which he never should have made but once done some things can never be taken back. Slowly and carefully he pulls at the tape and it comes loose slowly at first but then comes off fairly quickly and easily. As the smell of blood wafts up into the air and up Salaok’s nose, it is almost enough to make his sick, but the sick is what puts him over. Well over. A movement from within Darrell’s throat which is slit from ear to ear causes Salaok to step back and for a brief second, hope arises thinking that Darrell may still be alive. But the movement isn’t one that Salaok wanted and would soon be the image that drives him. A chuck of Darrell’s insides slides out of the huge gap in this throat and followed by a countless amount of maggots which had ground and begun to eat the insides of the young innocent’s body. There little bodies a pinkish color as they had been stained with the blood of a Davis. The spilt blood that would need vengeance. Salaok steps back, horrified by the site of his son being eating from the inside and begins to stumble before crashing into the wall. Out of control, he slides down the wall with his hands now firming gripping his hair, he finally hits the floor. The roar that would boom from his lungs would be at a volume he had never produced before and as it shock the very foundation of Salaok’s body, the sounds of birds taking off from their trees around the house could be heard. But as quickly as the sound had exploded from his lungs, it stops and he sits in silence staring at his son’s deceased body. Through the uncontrollable tears, his heavy breaths and non stop shaking, he speaks but four words. “Lisha, I need you.” ---- A voice rings out through the quiet house and disturbs the peace. The peace which was cold. Again the voice rings out through the massive house, “Salaok” Cous, you in here?” The voice was one of pure and strength but it didn’t sound out again however was replaced by heavy footsteps as the man the voice belonged to moved through the house. The echoing thuds of the stairs grow louder and then become replaced by the sound of creaking floorboards and doors opening. And then into the cold darkness he steps. Pushing the door open on Darrell’s bed room, Lucas Foss takes one look around the room and is struck silent and still. With his eyes scanning the room, it isn’t long until he spots Salaok huddled against the wall, his eyes wide open and stubble growing around his jaw. The smell of urine, death and blood mix together to create a stench so vile that even Lucas finds it hard to breath and then realizes that the urine wasn’t from Darrell, but a stain had formed around Salaok’s body on the wooden floor. He swats down next to Salaok and tries to look into his eyes but they are blank. The usual pure white eyes of Salaok’s are dulled, lifeless and show no response as Lucas begins to speak. “Sorry it took me so long to get here Sal. But now I am, I think it’s time to get you out of here.” Without moving to acknowledge what his cousin had just said, Salaok mouth tilts open only slightly and the words that first were said days ago sound out again. “Lisha, I need you.” ---- Salaok had spent days in that room, unable to move. His body had been ravaged by the pain of the loss and the horror of the site of his child murdered in the house he owns. Soiling himself instead of getting up to go to the toilet. By the time Lucas had found Salaok, the smell in the room would have been horrendous but Salaok acted as if nothing had happened at all. But that was where that memories stopped because as soon as Salaok realized Lucas was there and was helped to his feet, he passed out due to the lack of food and the image of his murdered son’s now rotting body. With sweat pouring from his forehead now, Salaok opens his eyes and stares up at the door to the room where he found Darrell that night. There were still questions that needed to be answered and he didn’t have the slightest clue as to who to start with. The fear that Chris Davis knew of the killed had entered Salaok’s mind and now there was no way of ever finding out of that was the case. But there were others who might know. The same men who killed Lisha were high on the list. Or could it be the man who had sent him to a Mental Hospital with the help of Chris? Or was it The Lair? He didn’t know, but surely he would find out in time. Through the only way he knew how to get the information. Mind games and brutality would be the finally answer? or would it be the question?
