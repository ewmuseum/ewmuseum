Psycho Bunny in Jail...AGAIN~!

12 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Psycho Bunny in Jail...AGAIN~! Psycho Bunny Global Wrestling Promo #2127 Date: 07/12/05 Location: A Jail The scene opens in the local jail. Psycho Bunny is there because of his killing of the Trix Rabbit. He is now the O.J. Simpson of animal characters because of this incident. Children hide under their beds and women stay at their boyfriend's houses to fuck instead of going home because of him. Outside of his cell, he hears gunfire for some reason. Psycho Bunny: Fuckers... Psycho Bunny looks around his cell. There's a toilet, a sink, the bed he's sitting on, and some gasoline with a box of matches sitting next to it. Psycho Bunny smiles. Psycho Bunny: Fucking pyhromaniacs. Gotta love'em. Psycho Bunny grabs the gasoline and opens it up. He then grabs the box of matches. Psycho Bunny: Going down down down to a burning ring of fire... He then cackles evilly and lights a match. He then sets the gasoline canister on fire and throws it at the wall. One of the guards sees this. Guard: HEY~! Too late. The wall explodes, leaving a huge hole for Psycho Bunny to escape from. He turns around when he sees the guard and flips him off. Psycho Bunny: Later Barney. Tell Andy and Goober I said hello. Psycho Bunny runs out through the hole and finds himself in the midst of a battle between zombies and humans. Psycho Bunny looks around. Psycho Bunny: Did I miss something? One of the guards comes running through the hole Psycho Bunny made a minute before and hands him his assault rifle. Guard: HERE! TAKE THIS! Psycho Bunny smiles when he sees his rifle. Psycho Bunny: Fucking A! Psycho Bunny turns to the Guard, who is now shooting. Psycho Bunny: What the fuck is going on? Guard: Zombies! Zombies are everywhere! We must fight them before we all die! Psycho Bunny smiles. Psycho Bunny: How did this happen? The Guard shakes his head in dismay. Guard: Oh. Some dumbass named von Doom got a magic pen from some wizard or something and drew a zombie. They then started multiplying from there. Psycho Bunny shakes his head. Psycho Bunny: Fucking von Doom. I love that dude. The Guard turns and raises an eyebrow. Guard: You know him? Psycho Bunny nods his head. Psycho Bunny: He writes my scripts. The Guard gives him the eye. Guard: No wonder your wrestling promos are so fucked up. Psycho Bunny smiles and then loads his weapon. Guard: So what are you gonna do? Just stand there or help us kill these fuckers and send them back to hell? Psycho Bunny smiles and slowly creeps off the battlefield and into the woods nearby so that everyone else can kill themselves fighting the zombies as the scene fades out. Cut.
