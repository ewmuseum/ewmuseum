Date: 10.12.04 Wardrobe Gap: Charcoal & Black Trim Vintage 1969 Skull Cap Polo Ralph Lauren Purple Label: Black Luxury Pique Polo Polo Ralph Lauren Purple Label: Charcoal Wool Cargo Pants Prada: Black Leather Flat-Toed Tuxedo Boots The rain was pouring from the night sky when Ken finally stopped running and fell to his hands and knees on the cold pavement that covered the dark back road which he had been led to. Visions were running through his mind that he did not understand. Flashes of light stopped him dead in his tracks as if he had really just seen them and not imagined them. The burden of finding his past... finding who he was was becoming to great, and all he wanted to do was give up. He drew his dripping wet face upwards towards the heavens allowing the liquid needles to pierce his skin as he threw his arms into the air screaming with tears in his eyes. 'Why,' he chanted with a confused and angry tone in his voice, 'why must I be put through this test? Have I not proven myself time and time again? Was my reaching the fartherst-most outlying regions of near-death not enough punsihment? What is wrong with me? I just want my life back! I just want to start new! Why the fuck can't you just let me do that?' And with those words, Ken fell back to the concrete. The rain was beginning to fall heavier in sheets, but even with the cold drops consuming him, he never seemed to notice. He just lay there, crying, unaware of his surroundings and only aware of his state-of-mind. He had seen the face of his attacker, but his eyes were still blinded from the bright lights and the accident. Still, he noticed something familiar about the blur. Something eerie, even. There were scars across the attempted murderer's face, but that's all Ken could make out. ..::~::~::.. 'Hush little baby, don't say a word' The voice of Ken's mother was melodious as he rested in his bed. 'Momma's gonna buy you a mockingbird.' He felt safe, secure, and at ease. It was amazing how even at the young age of four these feelings could be understood. 'And if that mockingbird don't sing' A smile drew across his face as his mother softly sang him to sleep. 'Momma's gonna buy you a diamond rin--!' Ken's eyes shot open, only to view the faceless man in his mother's place as the room got dark and the only thing other than blackness surrounding him, were his sheets. 'Hush... little star... don't say a word!' The voice was creepy and soft with a rugged edge in the tone. Ken was afraid, but he did not scream... he didn't even tremble in fear. His fear was his strength. 'Daddy's gonna' give you a big... black... hearse!' Everything went black around Ken before he found himself in a casket, struggling to get out. As he pounded on the roof, it began to make way, only to have him puch through and find the coffin filling with dirt. He tried to scream, but his mouth only consumed the gritty earth that was devouring him. ..::~::~::.. Ken was catatonic by the time Gunner arrived. His eyes were red from the ammount of rain that had poured into them and all he could make out were blurs. She helped him to his feet and drug him to her car, where she put him in the passanger side before driving off to his SoHo home. He could only understand her crying voice as a mumble, but he knew it was her from her touch. He soft, glorious, beautiful touch. He felt safe... secure... and at ease.
