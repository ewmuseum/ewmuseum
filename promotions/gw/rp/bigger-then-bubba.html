Bigger Then Bubba

undated

Date: Unknown Looking up at the sky with my eyes wide open. I wonder if your eyes start to water when they are open to long when you are dead. I can’t feel anything, so I suppose not. Or maybe its just because I’m immune to pain. Everybody always says, there is no pain in heaven. But I’m not even in heaven’or at least yet. I question myself, to myself’where the hell AM I going? What’s the next step? Which religion out of every single religion in the world is right? I remember that in my life, I didn’t believe in anything, so whatever it is, I’m screwed. Right now, I’m staring into the sky’because it’s the only thing I can do. My heart stopped and everything stopped hurting. But I am still trapped inside this body. Now I know what prison feels like. In the clouds up high, a blinding light shines on me. So bright, I wish I could blink my eyes, but I can’t. White dots start falling down from the clouds. They are coming towards me, slowly’I have nothing better to do then watch them. Looks like something is about to happen. If I could, I’d take a deep breath and swallow my nervousness. Hope and pray for the best. Expect the worst. The tiny white dots start getting bigger, I can tell that they are human beings’or, at least they look like humans. They are, more like angels. They float above me now, I’m scared as can be. If you believe in Christ, you’ll go to heaven. If not, you are doomed to hell. I know what’s coming. And now, I wish I can sit and pray for forgiveness. For the first time in my life, I’d repent everything I’ve ever done wrong. If I could, I’d close my eyes. If I could, I’d put my hands together. If I could, I’d bow my head and pray for mercy. But instead, I think about an excuse to tell God. A white little fib, not really a lie to try to persuade him to let me go to heaven. But how the hell can you lie to God? He knows everything, right? I suddenly remember how I died. The Upper Echelon. Maverick Reno. John Zero. Deacon Holmes. Inc. If I’m going to hell, I wonder where they will go. Again I wonder, what if. What if I read the bible? What if I prayed more? What if? If I could, I’d shake my head, side to side. There are no what ifs. I look back at what I was instead of what I should have been. I try to think about all the good times I’ve had instead of the bad ones. But all I can think about are those’peons who mutilated me. If I could, I’d clinch my fist in anger. You don’t know what it feels like. I’m leaving unfinished business’unfinished. You don’t know what this feels like. Out of every regret, not taking care of The Upper Echelon is the biggest in my book of regrets. The angels draw nearer. Blonde women in their mid-twenties, as beautiful as can be float directly above me. Just like the movies and the books. They make models look like creatures. There are? 1? 2? 3 of them. If I could, I’d use my fingers to count. They’re eyes sparkle in glare of my blood. They’re teeth are as white as pearls. They are amazing. They are beautiful. They are’perfect. They wear white gowns of silk. Gorgeous white wings come out of their backs. The cleanest feathers in the world. The purest of pure. They swoop down next to me, smiling for some awkward reason. Maybe they enjoy putting people like me in hell. But that doesn’t make too much sense now does it? One of the angels puts her hand on my forehead. If I were alive, I’d shout to her and tell her to call 911 for help. But you can’t help somebody whose heart stopped beating several minutes ago. So forget it. “The Lord would like to have a word with you, Sage.” The angel says in the most beautiful voice ever heard. Sounds like a mix of Mariah Carey and Whitney Houston with a tad bit of Celion Dion’before the Titanic. Hearing her voice, so lovely, it feels as if my heart stopped beating’or, continued to not beat. If I could move, I’d nod my head. All of a sudden, a white light engulfs my vision. I can’t see anything. It is like the feeling you get when you wake up one winter morning and look out the window at freshly fallen snow. It just hurts your eyes. The white light fades. And I am standing in front of’God? I take a deep breath. Wow, I can move again. I raise my hands up to my face’possibly the greatest feeling in the world. The feeling of being alive. I’ll never take it for granted ever again. “Sage Tse Ling?” I look up at him. The deepest voice I have ever heard. Enough roar to shake mountains. Millions of Christians prepare their lives for this moment. They wait to die, so they can live again, forever in heaven. Eternal life is the prize for just a few decades of dedication. Damn, do I pat those guys on the back. Christians probably rehearse what they are going to say to God when they arrive in heaven. But me’I can’t think of a word to say? “Yes, Lord.” I could pat myself on the back now’but I won’t. God looks down on me’I wish I could describe to you what he looks like. But, you can’t describe him. He looks like Chris Rock and Keanu Reeves with a pinch of Santa Clause’if that makes any sense. “I wasn’t expecting you to come this early, you know.” “Neither was I, Lord.” Very friggen smooth, Sage. “But it looks as if things have changed. Sage, you know that you have not met the qualifications to enter the gates of heaven. You haven’t even tried, son.” I feel really guilty now. You don’t know what this feels like. Having God look at you, ashamed. Its about as fun as a shotgun blast to the face’or Maverick Reno walking up to you and shooting you in the cranium more then once. “I plead forgiveness.” “Its too late, Sage. Judgment is upon you.” “I plead guilty. But I beg for mercy, oh Lord. I beg for forgiveness.” “Son, I’m afraid it doesn’t work that way. I’m not going to grant you a one-way ticket to heaven, just because you are sorry for not doing what you should have done on Earth.” “Then grant me a ticket back to Earth, Lord.” God is stricken with curiosity’he puts his right hand on his chin. “I’m listening.” “If you could, grant me back to heaven. Heal me, Lord. I have unfinished business I must tend to.” God chuckles? “Such foolishness you speak of. Unfinished business” You don’t think mothers die, abandoning their children? You don’t think that military men die in war, and not have the glory to watch it end? You don’t think scientists die before they invent a cure for a deadly disease? There was a reason you died, Sage. Everything happens for a reason. And I won’t let you go anywhere until you realize what the reason is.” I look up at him in shock. It sounds so easy, to figure out why I died. There must be a reason but I don’t know why. I don’t have a lot of time to sit and ponder upon the reason I passed away. I sit back and think about what I regret doing in my life. Could it have been everything I’ve done to everybody? Treat others like you’d like to be treated’that could be it. But it just doesn’t click. Maybe it’s how I stabbed my father in the back when I was a child. Instead of taking care of the land, I moved onto my own life. But that doesn’t feel like the reason. I wish this was easier? Instead of thinking about what I did in my life, I think about what I didn’t. I never fell in love, passed on everything I knew to somebody else. I never donated money to charity. I never showed mercy or compassion. My life was a living wreck. And here I stand in front of God’wasting his time. I feel guilty, its the most horrible feeling I’ve ever felt. I wish I didn’t screw up when I was alive, if I didn’t’I would be in heaven and not in the situation I am in now. I regret not knowing God back when I was alive? All of a sudden, a light clicks in my head. I look up at God and he is already smiling back? “And your conclusion is?” He awaits for my answer? “I regret not knowing you, oh Lord. I regret not loving you.” “Now Sage, do you say this because you aren’t in heaven?” “Actually, no Lord. I regret not knowing you’for you. I regret not serving you’being foolish and greedy my whole life.” God chuckles’the deepest laugh imaginable. He makes James Earl Jones sound like Tiny Tim. “That’s exactly that I’ve been looking for. I knew you would figure it out. You are a very smart man, I made you with a lot of wisdom and talent. And you have been using that. I am going to send you back to the worldly realm. Don’t take life for granted this time. Now, now you know that you can die at any moment possible. And don’t forget it.” I look stunned? “Seriously”...” “Come on, give me a break. God doesn’t lie?” God laughs? A joke made by God’I think he forced me to laugh along with him. “I want you to return to finish your business. And make sure you remember the reason why you died before. You are my child, no matter how old you are. Your career should be to serve me. To evangelize to the world and teach them what you never had the chance to be taught. Share the love with the rest of the world.” I nod? “and that’s the last thing I’ll ever remember.
