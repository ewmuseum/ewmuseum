Budding Relationships

28 February 2007

Budding Relationships Jenny Callihan Global Wrestling Promo #3017 Date: 02/28/2007 Location: A Gym in Dallas In the gym by the bench press machine, a toned figure is pushing up a considerable amount of weight considering the preconception of her being a woman. The spandex shorts and sports bra protest at the rubbing as Jenny continues doing her reps. In the blue corner, we got men who are intimidated by a woman who can do such things on a bench-press machine, and take their insecure Johnsons away from her so they can try to recollect their shattered self-esteem. She was quite accustom to it since this was a gym where people with ideals of losing weight come to try to burn off calories and become superman. In the red corner are more insecure jackasses who seem to be sweating and panting either staring at her fit form, or trying to do over do themselves to impress her. Unfortunately, she doesn’t wear her wedding ring for professional purposes, and it was a mutual agreement between Jake and her. Could you imagine those diamonds grinding on someone skin? Ouchie. The door to the gym opens up, and a young man steps in- relatively young, at least. He has a fresh look in his light-blue eyes, and his dirty blonde hair looks a bit unkempt with its thick waviness, but is pulled away from his face into as unobtrusive a ponytail as he can manage. The other men don't seem to notice him- or, noting his rather effortlessly trim and toned physique, they take to making jokes about how gay he probably is. Unfortunately for their egos, Karma is not gay. Further misfortune is the fact that he actually knows the woman that many of them are drooling over, but he doesn't see fit, at the moment, to interrupt her on the press. He sets his bag down in a nearby metal folding chair and unzips it, taking out his weightlifter's gloves and his soft plastic thermos. She finishes the reps, and lowers the press down gently. Nothing annoys her more then when people are careless enough to let those weights slam down. Like it really improves your machismo when they make a loud clank, but to a woman like her it only shows you are insanely clumsy. She lifts herself up and takes the towel to wipe the sweat where her back had been on the bench before swiveling around to notice a new arrival in the gym. Thankfully for her, it was a more familiar face and one she was delighted to see. “Hey Karma.” He hears her voice before he looks up, and is already smiling by the time his eyes meet hers. He straps on his other glove, the Velcro making a thick ripping noise in the midst of the gym. He nods to her- life has been difficult enough lately, when all he wants to see is a friendly face, and doesn't get anything but bland blankness in return. 'Afternoon, Jenny.' It is about as casual a greeting as he can muster, but that isn't saying much; he's just not in the habit of speaking informally, not to anyone. She stands up throwing the towel into the bag, and taking a swig of Gatorade from a bottle. She walks over towards Karma that started an unintentional chain of stares from said insecure jackasses that one of the finest asses in the gym is talking to a man who was probably gay. “Want someone to spot you?” she offers her assistance. Granted, he would be incredibly screwed if it was more then she could lift, but still from what she seen thus far he doesn’t have a training partner. Back in the BACW, she would train with an assortment of friends from Lia, Denara, or Ivana but she knew that sometimes when you are the new dog in town that many people don’t even want their sweat being mingle with you on the superstition that it will because you become a noob, as some like to call it. A bigger smile comes over Karma's features. 'Actually, I'd love that. I've been having to cut back on my regimen a little because I don't have anybody to work with... I guess I don't make friends too easily,' he says, and seems a little sad for a moment, before forcing himself to chuckle. It's probably an understatement- Betsy could testify, after all. She nods although she did catch the look from his expression. However, she knew better then to press it when someone was about to lift weights. “Well, considering as you have been cutting back, maybe we could start off light. Some cycling, barbells, light dumbbells, and a cool swim?” she spoke to him with a nice smile. “Even throw in a nice massage to get you all ready to go.” He nods, smiling again. 'That sounds... well, pretty perfect, actually,' he says as he sits down on the bench. He glances at her curiously for a moment, 'Are you becoming a trainer or something?' Jenny chuckles a little. “Well it is not common knowledge, but I am a licensed physical therapist in Arizona and Washington. I might try to license myself in Texas too, I don’t know.” Karma raises his eyebrows. 'Very nice. I wouldn't have pointed fingers at a wrestler also being a physical therapist, that's for sure. How long have you been doing it?' He leans back on the bench, resting his back lightly against the bar. Jenny thinks for a moment. “About three years. I got injured very badly where the outcome wasn’t looking good that I would go back to wrestling, so while still bound to a wheel chair, I attended classes, and got a license. After that point, I split my time between being a wrestler and therapist for my co-workers. Someone told me to become a chiropractor, but the thought of snapping joints and tendons, no thanks.” She said looking at the weight bar. “Why don’t we start you here, and we will increase it later.” She moved a pin to a weight more appropriate for warming up. He lays back on the bench, and cracks his knuckles before gripping his fingers firmly around the bar. With gritted teeth, he pulls the bar with the weights up, and finds, refreshingly, that it's not much of an effort at all- a good way to start, certainly. 'Thanks...' he says, letting out a breath of air as he lets the bar come down to his chest, '...for doing this with me.' He pushes the bar up until his arms are extended all the way, and then brings it slowly back down again, careful to keep all of his muscles under control. “It’s no problem. Certainly don’t want you tearing something...hate seeing friends in agony.” If she were any other person, she would have said something about making sure he doesn’t screw up their tag team match, but she wasn’t callous. It’s tough to impress people, and injuries only make matters worse. “Relax your shoulders…too tense.” Karma's brows furrow for a moment, and then he takes her advice, closing her eyes and letting out another deep breath. The tension seems to begin working its way slowly out of his muscles. 'I am too tense,' he says through slightly gritted teeth, 'It's hard not to be.' He's alluding, naturally enough, to his relatively unimpressive record as of late. Breaking even isn't exactly what he came over to the States to do. “Anything I can do to help?” Karma does another rep, pushing the bar all the way up, and lowering it down again. He's getting to the end of his first sequence, and this probably seems like a rather awkward time to have a heart to heart conversation- but strange things can often happen in the world of wrestling. 'Tell you the truth; I'm just disappointed with myself. I haven't been performing as well as I wanted to, and I'm just trying to get up to the level I'm used to.' He pushes through another rep, and then aims the bar back at the stand for a few moments. 'I guess part of it is just being here in this new situation. You know what that's like, I'm sure.' Jenny nods. “Yea, I been in your boat more times then I wish to count. It sucks, but the beauty part is that it isn’t always going to be that way. If I must, I will crack a dirty joke to lighten the mood.” She said with a smile. “Seriously, lighten up your shoulders before you tear something.” Karma sits up, sighing softly. It's not that he's going to stop doing what he's doing- just going to take a moment to take her advice. He stretches his right arm across his body and pulls against it with his left, stretching out all the muscles along the back of his shoulder. He shakes his head. 'It's been driving me crazy, Jenny,' he says, open and honest for perhaps the first time since he came into the gym, 'I'm usually fine with doing what I have to do alone, but I guess after awhile, even guys like me start to get stir-crazy.' Jenny nods as she sits on the bench with one leg on each side. Most guys would get a hard on if a girl did that, but that wasn’t her intention. “Look, I know what you are going through. It happened to me in the AWA and plenty of other places before that. I knew Davey, but that was really all who I felt comfortable with…and he couldn’t always be there you know.” She chuckled. “Then Lia came, and gave me a home in the BACW…and finally, a family…I never felt alone when I was there. So look, if you need an ear to talk to, a shoulder to cry on, or even a home cook meal, don’t be afraid to see me all right? I am here for you, ok?” Karma's eyes light. 'I... well; I don't want to be trouble for you. But I could certainly use a little bit of company some time.' He chuckles to himself. 'It sounds suspiciously like inviting myself over, though... my mother always taught me that was rude.' “It is, but only if someone didn’t make the offer first.” She was quick to note. “You know, I’ve been meaning to ask…umm…I got this extra bedroom in my apartment. Don’t ask how, you don’t want to know…but anyways, I trust you…and hopefully you trust me not to food poison you. I was wondering if you want to share the apartment?” Karma appears to be entirely lost on how to react. His jaw drops for a moment before he recovers his composure and looks at her with a troubled look in his eyes. 'I couldn't possibly trouble you, Jenny.' “It’s no trouble at all. We work for the same company after all.” She said with a smile. “It’s up to you. Figure we could commute and hang together a lot more.” He nods once. 'I think I would really enjoy it... you're sure your husband wouldn't mind?' He pauses for a moment, and his face reddens slightly. 'I mean, I'm not implying that I'd try to take advantage of you or anything. Far from it. But, I just... well, people can be... you know how people act sometimes.' Wow. Now, in addition to being astounded by her offer, he also felt really stupid. She let a soft chuckle, but it was more warm and friendly then belittling. “Karma, I am a professional wrestler.” She said with a wink. “I think even if it was in you to try, Jake knows I could probably kick a whole lot of ass before anything was success. Don’t worry about Jake, it’s cool.” He nods, looking down at his hands for a moment, then back up at Jenny with a rather self-deprecating chuckle. 'It would be better than living in that awful, empty apartment I have.' She shrugs. “Well, I can’t judge cause I never been to your apartment, but I know a really good one is hard to find. And don’t worry; I won’t get too girly on you.” Karma's eyes widen at the thoughts of living in an all-pink pad. But still... 'Even all pink would be better than my stark white walls at the moment.' Jenny thinks for a moment. “Mayyyybbbeee…” she feigns a thought of consideration before chuckling. “No, I will not live in an all pink apartment. I will never be taken seriously in a biker bar or a truck stop.” 'A truck stop?' Karma's eyes widen, as though he's quite surprised, 'A lady like you in a truck stop? I could hardly imagine that...' Plainly, Karma doesn't have much experience with the 'tough girl' type. Jenny nods. “Sometimes you got to make do on the road. I am hardly one of those prissy little girls in a petticoat going ‘Yooooohhhooooo....’” she did in her best imitation of an annoying preppy girl. “I like to get my hands dirty, I have tackled the engine on my Harley more then enough times, and if I am going to play football, there is no touching involved. Take those dickweeds over there, they are like wondering if I am bull dyke cause I am pulling more weight then what they classify as appropriate, but the truth is, I am a woman, I love dick as any, but I like it hard and rough.” She shrugs. “Pardon my language.” Karma's eyes are wide, and he's partially unable to conceal the shock at hearing a woman talk like that. Then again, he's beginning to think there's quite a lot about American women that he just doesn't understand. He shakes his head. 'No, no, you don't have to apologize. I'm not offended by it, only... unused to hearing women talk like that. I suppose that's something I'll have to get used to here.' “Tis cool…are you more relaxed now?” He nods. 'I think so... I at least feel like some of that personal stuff is off my chest.' There is, of course, the disaster with Betsy, but then again... that's something he'll have to figure out on his own. She nods. “Cool, like I said...don’t be afraid to talk to me about any subject. Well, maybe not a bad film strip with people dancing disco, but that is a whole other story.” She chuckles as she rises from the bench. “Let try those reps again.” Karma lays back on the bench and cracks his knuckles loose. His hands grip around the bar, and he lifts it with a great deal more ease than he did before, doing his reps with seamless motions, and strong arms. Jenny smiles as she sees less tension in the reps. “Much better.” 'Feels better too,' Karma remarks, continuing through his second set. “Definitely. I once had to see a friend accidentally ripped her rotator cuff. I didn’t understand a darn word she said after that point.” She said thinking. “She had a lot of tension in her shoulder from a botched move, and she paid for it the next day.” 'Must have... been rough,' Karma said, his arms definitely beginning to feel the burn in his arms, 'I can't say I've ever had that... been lucky... but you always have to watch out.' “Well take it nice, easy, and slow and you will avoid a lot of it. I think a nice cooling swim would do you wonders.” She smirks a little. “Just wadding along the water, relaxing...sounds like fun.” Karma smiles, although it looks a little strained considering the weights he is lifting at the moment. 'It sounds like a plan to me... to be honest; I've been concentrating so much on the hard training that I haven't let myself relax a lot. I'll probably stress myself to the point of no return if I keep it up.' She nods. “Tell you what, after this set, why don’t we forgo any more weight training, and just relax in the pool. I promise, I won’t tell anybody.” She said as makes a mischievous face and gives him faux shifty eyes. “We can play hooky in the weight room.” The fact is she said it with a straight face, which is a miracle at some point. For perhaps the first time since she has known him, a wide grin comes over Karma's face, and even his eyes light- which is a rarity, considering that they usually remain sad, even when he smiles. 'I think I could agree to that,' he says, hooking the bar back over the stand as he does his last reps. “Cool, meet you at the pool?’ He sits up slowly, feeling the blood begin to drain out of his effort reddened face. 'Deal,' he says, standing up and starting for the men's locker room with a new energy in his step. She watched Karma make his way to the locker room as she made her own exit from the weight room. She likes the dude as a friend, and can seem a beautiful friendship blossoming before her. Although it didn’t help her with loneliness in bed despite solutions, it brought new warmth to her apartment. It wasn’t as bad as Karma described his, but it was definitely empty without her husband there. Now, with a friendly face to wake up to felt made Dallas, Texas just that more pleasant. <To Be Continued, Next Week..Same Cup time, Same Cup Channel>
