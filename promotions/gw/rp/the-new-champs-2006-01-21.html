The New Champs

21 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-21-06 11 P.M London England Shortly After Reload. Lots of shouts come bursting out of the locker room area of the Arena in England. The loudest are from the new tag team champions Gorn Fire Hammer and Gimley Ice Hammer. Quinton Spikes, a few girls a few other staff members, and a few lucky fans? are in the locker room as well. The Hammer Brothers raise their beers as they hold their titles on their shoulders. “Yeah!” shouts the Hammer Brothers as they slam their beers together. “I told you guys, The Hammer brothers are a unstoppable force!” Spikes yells to everyone in the room? A reporter comes running into the locker room. “How does it feel to be the new Tag Team Champions?” The reporter asks. “Pretty damn good” Gorn says as he slaps the reporter across the back, making him stagger forward. “Yeah. And to celebrate, we gonna party all night!” Gimley says The crowd cheers as they all head out of the locker room and down the hall? ---------- Outside a Limo with the Global logo pulls up. “What is this?” Gimley asks. The driver walks around and opens the door. “You are champions, this is one of the perks.” “Probably done it by luck”. A man from the crowd yells! “What!” Gorn says walking over to the man then picking him up off the ground. Gimley lets teach this punk a lesson! “Right away bro!” Gimley then picks up the man and him and Gorn dispose of him in a trashcan Quinton then runs up and shuts the trashcan doors. “Teach him we are the champions and we didn’t do it by luck!” Quinton smirks and starts laughing then runs and catches up with The Hammer Brothers. “Can we touch the titles?” Two girls ask one with blonde hair and the other with black hair? “Sure baby’s!” The Hammer Brothers say as the girls run up. “Well it’s all in a day’s work!” Quinton says out of breathe from running. “You know Spikes you were right this is awesome!” Gimley says picking the blonde hair girl up. “Ah!” The blonde hair girl shouts her voice full of laughter as Gimley picks her up. “You no what time it is now Spikes!” Gorn says. “Hammer time!” Quinton says. “Nope!” Gorn says. What Quinton says? Oh! “I know lets do it on three!” Gimley says this as he drops the blonde headed girl. “Ready!” Gorn says. “One!” Gimley says “Two!” Gorn says. “Three!” Gimley shouts. When Gorn shouts party time. “Lets do it again pretend yawl didn’t here any thing ready.” Gorn says tapping Gimley on the shoulder. “Oh!” Ready! Gimley says. “One!” Gorn says. “Two!” Gimley says. “Three!” Gimley shouts while Gorn shouts party time again. “Never mind just lets go party and lets take the limo.” Quinton says. 1:00 A.M London England A bar somewhere “This is the life Gimley shouts holding up his jug of beer!” Then him and Gorn slam their beers together. I’m going to grab a bite,” the blonde hair girl says. “Me two.” the black hair girl says. Then the two girls go across the street Yel Reco’s pizza joint. “Man beers are better when they have less filling!” Gimley says. “I don’t know what you smoking on beers taste great!” Gorn says. “Less filling” Gimley shouts suddenly. Then whole bar turns to watch. “Taste great!” Gorn shout back at Gimley’s face. “Less filling!” Gimley shouts making his head touch Gorn’s. “What?” Gorn says turning his head to the people who have been watching the fight all along. “Now were were we Gorn asks Gimley.” “I forgot!” But let’s drink Gimley says. “Yeah!” Gorn shouts and the two both down their drinks. Gimley, Gorn Quinton says. We have to leave because we have another match a main event match! “We have to leave to go to Dallas Texas were we will be staying in a hotel suite then on Friday at Fusion we have to fight Jake Hix the World Champion and Mike Biddle the International Champion,” Quinton says. “That sucks we were going to have a night with these girls!” Gimley says pointing over at the pizza joint. “Uh’Gimley that’s a pizza joint” Quinton says laughing. “I meant the girls inside the pizza joint.” Gimley says slapping Quinton in the back of the head. “Well we have to go right now.” Quinton says rubbing the back of his head. “We will leave the girls a note now lets drink to the main event match we will have in a few days.” Gorn says. “Hey bartender two more beers and right us an note right away!” Gimley says. “Hmm.” The bartender says with a weird expression on his face as he gives Gorn and Gimley the two beers. “Here is to the main event match!” The Hammer Brothers both say and down their one-gallon jug beers. “It “s Hammer Time!” The Hammer Brothers shout. “Now can someone call a taxi?” Quinton says.
