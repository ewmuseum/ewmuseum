A Fallen Angel: The Promise

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 27, 2005 Mike Biddle’s revenge against Jake Hix was almost complete with the beat down of the Texas Wrecking Crew; it would have already been complete if the committee hadn’t decided to forgo the last Evolution before Showdown in Tokyo for supposed production reasons. Of course, this was just a delay of the inevitable. One day Biddle would get a rematch of the Dark Rituals fiasco and would beat Hix into a bloody pulp. However, for the time being Biddle had to forget the Hix situation and turn his focus to Showdown in Tokyo and the possibility of a World Title shot. Unfortunately Biddle found himself on a team of has-beens. Ken Partridge Jr., the captain of the team, was a former World champion that had under-achieved his way to mid-card mediocrity. Brick, yet another former World champion, was once a top contender and GLOBAL poster boy...now he was nothing more than the lesser half of a tag team. Finally, there was Christian Darke, the new comer to GLOBAL and cast off of a long forgotten Primetime Central federation. The critics would say that Team Ace was the perfect fit of Biddle, after all, the former Primetime Central Extreme champion was nothing more than a shadow of his former self. Over the course of eleven months Biddle had won only eleven matches, nothing more than one victory a month, far below the standard he had set for himself years ago. It was clear that he was no longer “Mr. Main Event”, hell it was a stretch to even call him “Mr. Mid-Card” these days. Biddle needed to turn things around as soon as possible and Showdown in Tokyo was the perfect place to do it. But with the team Biddle had found himself saddled with it was questionable if he’d even reach the second round of the event, especially since the team that Team Ace was to face in the first round was made up of two current GLOBAL champions and one tough little emotional bastard. Could Mike Biddle pull off the impossible and prove once and for all that “Mr. Main Event” is more than some self-serving nickname? Perhaps, but the odds are against him and Showdown in Tokyo could possibly be the final nail in the coffin of Biddle’s storied career. All this weighed down heavily on Biddle’s mind as he watched the morning news from the comfort of the Italian leather couch in his living room. For the most part the news had been the usual drivel, George Bush sucks, people died in Iraq, and some new storm has destroyed a lot of people’s lives. Mike sighed as the insincere newscaster on the screen turned his phony smile towards Mike and started the next segment of the news report. “Our sources indicate that the investigation of Hillary Watson’s death is coming to an end,” the smarmy bastard said, “and that an arrest should happen by the end of the day. Mike Biddle is still considered the top suspect in the murder of his former lover and valet.” With a disgusted noise Biddle turned the television off, he was tired of hearing about the murder and hoped that the investigation was truly over. That way when Hagen arrested the real murderer the media would be off Biddle’s back for the time being. Biddle had grown incredibly tired of the melodramatic goings on of Beverly Hills and was considering a temporary relocation after the murder investigation was over. A depressed sigh escaped Biddle’s lips as he got off the couch and went into the kitchen to refill his coffee cup. Since Hillary’s death Biddle had found himself slipping into a depression. He missed Hillary far more than he had expected himself to. Even more surprising the realization that he truly had loved her, giving truth to the old adage “you don’t know what you got until it’s gone”. He hadn’t gotten an opportunity to visit her grave due to his early exit from her funeral. He wanted to visit her, he needed to say his final goodbye to the woman that was, despite the fact that he treated her like shit, his one true love. So he decided that later that day he would go the cemetery. ***** Harry Hagen felt he had gathered enough evidence to justify making an arrest in the Watson case and had already applied for a warrant in order to do so. However, Jon Goudy, the head of the precinct’s CSI unit had called him earlier saying that the lab results on the case were in. Hagen was confident with his conclusions but still felt the need to have them confirmed by CSI. Once Hagen walked through the doors of the CSI lab he immediately found Goudy hunched over a microscope. Goudy was a tall, lanky man that looked like a white Steve Urkel. Hagen stood beside Goudy quietly, not wanting to disturb the important work of the other man. After a few moments Goudy finally glanced up and noticed that Hagen was next to him. “I take it you’re here about the Watson case?” Goudy asked. “Of course,” Hagen said, “I need to hurry up and make an arrest so my wife gets off my ass about my vacation time.” Goudy chuckled, the on going battle between Hagen and his wife was the stuff of legend around the Department. Leaving the subject untouched, Goudy went over to his filing cabinet and pulled out the folder on Hillary Watson. “Okay,” Goudy began, “you already know that the time of death is estimated to be around the time that Biddle was seen leaving the apartment.” Hagen nodded, this fact alone was why Biddle was the top suspect at the start of the investigation. Goudy sighed as he flipped through the folder; he had spent more time on this case than he would have liked. “We found a fresh footprint on a windowsill,” Goudy said with a monotone, “naturally the print didn’t match the victim so this means that the killer came through the window.” Hagen thoughtfully nodded; so far the evidence was matching his theory. “Anyway,” Goudy impatiently said as he glanced at his microscope, “I won’t bore you with details that you already know. I’m sure you’ve already made up your mind who did it. The name of my perpetrator is highlighted several times,” Goudy said as he handed the folder to Hagen. “Thanks,” Hagen said as he shook Goudy’s hand. Goudy immediately went back to staring through his microscope. Hagen shook his head and left the lab. Once outside he opened the folder and saw the name of Goudy’s perpetrator. The name matched the one of the man Hagen was about to arrest. With a smile Hagen left the station for Beverly Hills. ***** The overcast sky added to the misery of the cemetery. A cool wind blew between the headstones, one cool enough to send a chill down one’s spine and make one wonder if the wind was in fact the cold hand of death. In the middle of all this was the headstone of Hillary Watson. The headstone itself was beautiful and carved from granite, a black and white picture of Hillary’s smiling face was attached to the stone. Multiple flowers were scattered near the headstone, offering a pleasing dash of color to the mundane scene. Before the headstone stood the somber figure of Mike Biddle, wrapped in a black trench coat to protect himself from the wind. Too bad the coat couldn’t also protect him from the guilt that he felt. As he looked down at the grave a whirlwind of “shoulda-woulda-couldas” danced through his mind. Things had been spectacularly better when she had been alive and by his side. He had been at the top of his game then, a perennial champion, a man that was supposed to have one of the brightest futures in game. Then he had been foolish enough to let her go and the downward spiral of his career had been no coincidence. He choked back tears as he wondered how different things would be if he had stayed with her. “I’d be the fucking World Champion,” Mike said under his breath. Alas, he wasn’t and at the rate he was going he never would be. That fact was, surprisingly, the last thing on his find at the moment. He felt guilty for the way he had treated Hillary and it upset him that he would never be able to properly apologize to her. If only he could make it all up to her somehow... Suddenly his dark blue eyes lit up for the first time in months. He knew exactly how he could make it up to Hillary and put his mind at ease. He would make a return to his former glory by winning Showdown in Tokyo and he would do it for Hillary. He would do it on his own with no help from his bodyguard, it was the only way to redeem himself. “I love you and I’m sorry,” Biddle said as he looked at the picture of Hillary on the headstone. “I’ll make it up to you, I swear.” He paused for a moment and cast a longing gaze at the photograph. He then left, he had a promise to keep. ***** Hagen twirled the handcuffs around his finger as he walked up the steps of the Beverly Hills mansion. With a satisfied smile he knocked on the door and waited for an answer. After a moment the killer himself answered the door. “Hello Detective,” the killer said with an unsuspecting smile. “Is there something I can help you with?” Hagen nodded and began state the killer’s Miranda Rights as he placed the handcuffs on the other man’s wrists. “What’s all this about?” The killer asked, incredibly confused. How could anyone have known that he was the murderer? He had covered his tracks so well. Hagen pulled the man out the front door, the killer’s red hair made a sharp contrast to the overcast sky. “Don’t play stupid with me Mr. Magee,” Hagen said as he escorted Charles to the car. “I didn’t do it,” Charles said. “That’s what they all say,” Hagen replied. “You just couldn’t handle the rejection could you” No matter what you did she always went crawling back to Biddle.” Charles offered no response, only a somber sigh as he struggled to keep up with the detective. “Then one night you decide you can’t take it anymore,” Hagen continued, “so you decided that if you can’t have her, then no one can.” Hagen opened the door of the car and pushed Charles’s head down as he “helped” the murderer into the vehicle. Hagen watched as Charles offered no defense to the accusations, the red headed man just cried his eyes out. Hagen shook his head as he went around to the driver’s side of the car. When would these damn millionaires learn that they weren’t above the law? Never, that’s when. Hagen sighed as he started the car and drove towards the county lockup. ***** Biddle was aware of the fact that Charles had been arrested for the murder. He was glad that Hillary’s true killer had been brought to justice, it meant that the world wasn’t such a dark place after all. Biddle smiled as he packed his things for Tokyo, for this first time in weeks he felt at ease. With his innocence proven he could go about the business of finally making an impact in GLOBAL. He closed his suitcase and left his room. As he descended the staircase he paused for one last look at the second story of his home. At the bottom of the staircase he paused again and surveyed the landscape of dustcovers that had been draped over everything he owned. The servants had all been let go after they had finished the task of covering everything. With a sigh Biddle made his way through his home. Before he turned off the lights he turned for one last look. After Showdown in Tokyo he wouldn’t be returning to Beverly Hills, he wouldn’t return home until he had finally reclaimed his past glory and when the melodramatic goings on of the neighborhood had finally calmed down. He switched the light off and left the house for perhaps the last time. -The End-
