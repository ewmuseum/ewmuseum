The changing of the tides #3

20 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/20/04 Silence. We sit in the limo in silence. My son making calls making sure the travel to New York goes smoothly with no “interference “from any visitors who are not welcomed. I sit there in my thoughts still thinking of who could have let word spread of my living in Texas, or at least having a house there and running part of my business there. What right do these younger vampires have to tell me I must abide by their rules or be punished” I who was around during the times of the pyramids, I who helped destroy cities, I who help create their way of life. Time has finally come full circle and children try to dethrone their fathers once again. We who were kings of the earth now lie in the ground and let our children destroy what we have worked so hard to build up. I am glad my own children, at least one of them are faithful and works with me to accomplish my goals. As for my daughter’she will know her place, but there is larger things to attend to at the moment. When we finally reach the house, Sabbot is already dressed and ready to go. I get out of the limo as well does my son and we head for the back of the large house where I hear the sounds of a helicopter. Each of us get on and the helicopter is off. “What’s goin on Leinad, I got a call saying to be prepared to go to New York” “There is a war coming and we are in the middle of it. At least I am and my son. I will need your services as well as some others will you aid me?” “Sure. Your helping me with TUE I’ll help you with this. Besides you brought my tiger back to me. What will you need me for?” “The usual. Magic’fighting”.backup that should be it.” “No problem. So, what are we fighting?” “Vampires. Maybe a ghoul here and there..” “Guys like you” Oh shit?.why? What did you do?” “Well ya see in vampiric terms I’m what you would call an antediluvian. Meaning I’m a very old vampire”.predating the flood of Noah?” “Damn you are old.” “Yeah. Well these younger generations thought all my siblings and I were in torpor meaning deep sleep. Until a time where we would rise up and destroy the ones we see as thin blooded.” “Ok.” “Well I unlike my siblings was cursed to never sleep”.I continue night after night without any rest. Well someone let a vampire named Almeric St.Francis, who is Prince of Texas. Prince meaning like a governor or such?. basically the law for this state?. someone let him know I was in his grasp so he issued a blood hunt on me.” ? That’s not good I’m guessing?” “No”.it means every vampire in Texas is out to get me. And by the time I got the call some of my distant relatives, if that’s what ya wanna call them were on their way to try and kill me. Which I can assure you is no easy task. And by now a lot of the princes know I’m up and kicking. And that is not a good thing at all.” “If we are gonna fight why are we leaving” Shouldn’t we just start knocking off vampires?” “Its not that simple’not all of them will be after us but a nice group of them will”.I’m going to call for back up or actually go get back up. I have very powerful friends in New York and I am behind some of the largest wars and treaties that state has seen when it comes to my kind.” 'So...we are living to fight another day i'm guessing?' 'Indeed.' 'What about our matches?' 'We will be back in time for them...I will go to the meetings and then we will fly back in plenty of time for them. I love my job at GW and all but I also like saying i've lived for thousands of years.' 'Most people would.' 'So how do we kill them?' I smile at the question and stay silent. We get very close to our destination...Soma Corp. Head offices New York branch. We all sit there in silence
