Tragedy

7 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08.07.05 For the first time in many months, Ken Partridge, Jr. had been able to spend a week at home, with no commitments. There was a break in filming 'The Death of Superman' and 'Superman Lives,' thanks to a healthy ammount of editing and post-production and a bit of excitment struck Ken when he learned that Peter Jackson would be on hand in an assistant-director's role for the latter. Ceci's tour was going smoothly and everything at Nameless Records was as well. He wasn't even going to have to pack for Master of the Mat, as Madison Square Garden was relatively close to his home and, aside from the day-to-day running of The Elements, which was now mainly taken care of by Karen Segars, the woman Ken had named General Manager, this past week had been nothing short of relaxing for KPJ. Even with his International Championship match lingering near and hanging in the air, he was sleeping well next to the beautiful Gunner Addison as the only illumination in the bedroom was coming from a bedside clock. 3:47 AM Ken shot out of bed like a bullet, the moonlight shining on his abdomen, as a ringing phone pierced the silence. Gunner was stirring as he reach over and grabbed it, pressing 'Talk' to end the loud ringing, and putting the phone to his ear. He was surprised that before he could even voice a groggy greeting, his ear was filled with the sobs of someone crying. The exhaustion that had filled him with the sudden wake was quickly replaced with worry. 'Hello,' he croaked into the phone, his voice not catching up with the rest of his waking body. 'Ken, she's gone,' cried the voice on the opposite end. 'Ceci,' asked Ken with a monsterous amount of concern hanging on his tone. An ammount that Gunner no doubt took notice of as she two sat up, holding the sheets to her nude body, and gazed worringly into Ken's eyes. 'Whose gone?' 'Anna,' she cried even more vibrantly. 'Anna's gone. She was going out to get us some ice cream and... fucking drunk drivers...' Ceci trailed off before really telling Ken what had happened, but he was pretty sure that he could fit the pieces into the puzzle. 'Drunk drivers,' he questioned, his voice beginning to crack with sorrow. 'Two of them,' Ceci responded. 'Apparantly, they were playing chicken or some stupid shit like that and disregarded the red lights that they had, slamming into Anna.' 'She's,' Ken cut himself off before he could say it, tears beginning to stream down his face. 'She's dead, Ken,' Ceci screamed in a mixture of overwhelming emotions. 'Anna's fucking gone.' Ken dropped the phone instantly, his eyes whelling up. Gunner had heard Ceci's screams and grabbed the phone from Ken's lap, placing it to her ear as she embraced him in an attempt to console. He was in shock, so much so that he wasn't even aware that Gunner was there anymore, let alone what she was saying to Ceci on the phone in an attempt to console her as well. Anna was so young, only 20, and she'd been taken from this world. The regrets immediatly began to swarm through Ken's mind, the first and foremost being time, and the fact that he'd only gotten to know her over the past six months since she and Ceci had been together. If only he'd gotten to know her sooner, they could have had more time together. But, now, this beautiful red-headed girl with a heart the size of the sky and eyes the same shade of green as the ocean had been taken from them. Then everything hit him at once. She was gone... Anna's was gone... forever. Ken lost it, crying uncontrollably in Gunner's arms and rocking back and forth. He'd snapped back into reality to notice that Gunner was also crying before remembering how he'd found out everything to begin with. 'Oh my God,' he sobbed. 'Where's the phone? I dropped the phone. Ceci's still on the phone.' 'It's okay,' cried Gunner. 'I talked to her, but she had to go... the police had more questions and she had to go identify the body at the morgue.' Just the sound of the word linked with Anna's name was more than Ken could handle. He pulled himself out of bed, sniveling and crying before hitting his knees and falling into a silent prayer. 'Dear Lord,' he began in his mind, 'please be with me... with us all right now. Please, keep a careful eye on Anna... please watch over her.' His mind was skipping all over the place again. He was shooting from anger to sorrow to confusion and denial. 'Why did you have to take her?!?! Why did you see it fit to take someone so perfect and so innocent?!?! I bet you let the driver live, didn't you! You let the two that were living in sin... you let them live! In fact, I bet they're absolutly fucking fine aside from their cars being in bad shape! So, why!?!? FUCKING WHY?!?! Why do you leave those that don't deserve it and take those that can spread so much happiness and love?!?! Why did you take her and not them?!?!' As if in the form of an answer, his mind was suddenly struck with memories from his past. Memories of his past addictions and struggles with drugs and alcohol. Visions of a car completly totalled because he found it fit to get behind the wheel of it after consuming three bottles of scotch and a half a bottle of champagne. Memories of a bedroom padlocked with a chain by his own parents so the they could allow him the misery of dismissing the China White and Venum from his veins. 'Why did you let me live? I didn't deserve it, God. I truly didn't. I was as bad as those two, if not worse. Why am I still here? Why am I still here without Anna? Why is Anna there and not me?' 'There's nothing you could have done,' came the loving voice of Gunner Addison as she had slid onto the floor with Ken and was beginning to rub his back. 'What,' staggered a word out of Ken's throat. 'I know you, Sweetheart, and I know how you take things. How you begin to place blame on yourself and question motives.' It was like she had read his mind. Or, perhaps, Ken had really been screaming out what he'd thought he'd been keeping in his head. The tears were coming too strong now for him to know or care. 'What happened to Anna,' cried Gunner, 'was something that nobody saw coming. Something that nobody COULD have seen coming.' 'I know,' whispered KPJ, 'but... God... I'll miss her so much.' 'I know you will,' said Gunner calmly back, choking back her own tears and pulling Ken's head to her, allowing herself to hold him tight as he cried into her. It's amazing how things can be said without words. How two people with such an amazing loving chemistry and bond can speak to one another non-verbally for hours. Gunner and Ken sat on the floor, holding one another and crying for nearly an hour and a half, rocking back and forth. Ken's mind had begun to race to many different things, such as Anna's family and her alcoholic mother. He seemed tof ind comfort in the fact that her mom could never hurt her again and that now she could be with her father, who'd passed away a year prior, forever. He soon thought of Ceci... poor Ceci. He had no clue where she was, Massachussettes, he thought... Boston, probably. He needed to hear from her. He needed to know that she was okay. He needed to hold her and let her hold him, much as he and Gunner were doing. He needed to cry on her shoulder and let her cry on his. He needed to help her start piecing things back together. The road ahead was a long, rocky one, but it was a journey that he realized he wouldn't have to take alone. The future wasn't going to be easy to pull together, especially for Ceci. Ken could only be there for her... and he would be. Then he realized that Anna would be there with them as well. Helping them in ways that they could have never helped one another, she would shine upon them. As this thought entered Ken's mind, calming some of his tears, the rain that had began shortly after Ceci call, interestingly, stopped, and was replaced by the early morning light. The sun, peaking through the sky and over and around the clouds, shined on his Ken's face, warming it a bit and drying the tears. 'Thank you, Anna.' [detail withheld]
