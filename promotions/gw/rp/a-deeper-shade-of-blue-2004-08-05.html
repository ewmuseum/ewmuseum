A Deeper Shade of Blue

5 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/05/04 I remember standing outside the store that morning, my eyes looking around; my mind looking beyond. It was a day that I thought would pass by like everything else; a fragment of my life that would vanish into the depth of my torn soul like almost every face I have to see in my life. I stepped out and into the real world, two white carrier bags hanging freely by my sides, weighed down with fruit, vegetables and other essential items. For the day that it was, I decided to dress a little more casually than usual. Rather than stand tall and look impressive in suits and ties, I opted for the basketball-style shorts, trainers and plain vest. With the sun beating down on the right side of my body, I turned and walked in the opposite direction, knowing that I'd cope better if the sun pounded down on my back... as opposed to the alternative option of my face. The world around me was always a fascinating place, filled with mixed emotion and perception. What one took to be an act of kindness or a friendly gesture, another would percieve to be an act of violence or a showing of vulgarity. I never figured myself to be somebody who had good intentions but carried a cold, chilling meaning. For years and then days beyond, I'd been somebody who said everything like it was. I'd be the guy who told you that you looked ugly if you chose something that looked rather bad; I'd be the guy who told you that you looked smooth if you put the effort into your showing and really did look good. However, in the instance of one conversation, despite the tone of voice used, the meaning that was taken from the words is something that wasn't what I actually meant when spoken. I didn't want to be completely unapprecitive of the help that I had recieved prior; I just wanted Ryan to know that I could handle things by myself... that I could fight my way through problems without having to depend on him or anyone else for cover. I walked down the street, looking around to see the various people in their groups; in their collectives; in their hives. I gazed happily in amazement as the happiness and joy spread like a viral infection. Children and adults of all ages, shapes and sizes, they played and helped with one another. They expressed true validation of what it was like to be a friend, what it was like to be accepted in some kind of circle without any complaints from the other side. I stood still a few paces away from the store and began to think back, looking at the times where it was just assumed that everything between Ryan and myself had run smoothly. History was no liar and I knew that it took a lot of work and dedication to move things along the first time. After all, here I was, professional wrestler to the very end, being forced to make a complete and utter moron of himself to please everyone except himself. '... I am of the unique population who don't need to depend on others to live through life. I don't need people to hold my hand when I cross the road, fight a fight when the odds are against me or try to tie my laces when they start to come a little loose.' I said to myself, chuckling at the thoughts of my previous experiences. I remained still for a few more moments, flushing it all out of my mind before carrying ahead, the white bags swinning by my sides; moving to-and-fro like the wrecking ball of an impending demolition. I envisioned myself as I walked along that road, trying to become the wrecking ball that would put nine men to rest in Evolution's Battle Royal of next week. I tried to think of the weapons and the stability I would have as this sole ball, this sole object of chaos and destruction. True, I didn't have the streak in me to annihilate people but I had a spark that would be more than enough to carry me from the starting post to the finish line... alone. The minutes passed and the hours followed as I continued to make my way home. The number of people looking happy and playing dwindled with every turn that I took. It was almost as though the world was showing me the last few years of my life, displaying all the friends that I had within this industry... and then the present, where few people were allowed to become actual friends of mine. I didn't really want to be the absolute loner that life was making me out to be, I just wanted a chance to be able to do things by myself... to be able to do things without knowing that the safety harness was going to be there to stop me from crashing into a wall or whatever. As the sun began to settle and night began to fall, I finally arrived home, exhausted and tired in both mind, body and soul. This walk had done more than exercise my body and help me shift a calorie here or there, it'd given me time to think about everything that I did on Evolution... and everything to do for the future, including next week on Evo in that rather large Battle Royal. Approaching the front door to my home, I was surprised to see the door wide-open, the lock almost ripped cleanly from its hinges. Dropping my shopping to the floor in a disturbed manner, I stepped cautiously into my domain, slinking along the wall in a slow and covert manner. I felt my heart beating faster and faster as the suspense and tension of the moment began to rise. Peering around the corner, I saw a masked individual rummage through what few items there were, screaming vulgarities and other obscene language following the lack of valuable materials found. I was prepared to strike, I felt ready to jump and deliever the singular blow that would put this entire situation of the window. I inhaled deeply and exhaled in a slow manner, building my self-confidence before lunging out to do battle with this fiend. Ready to leap around the corner and give the bastard the biggest surprise of his life, I felt a hand place itself on my shoulder; a firm but friendly grip following. '... I know that you want to stand here alone and refuse help from anyone, even the people who were once your friends... but can't you use the helping hand of another person at times? Can't you use the strength of a team to overcome problems?' The voice came and passed as quickly as the grip on my shoulder had wandered into our physical realm and then vanished back to whence it came. 'Can't I use the strength of a team to overcome a problem?' I asked myself, whispering so that I didn't cause any disturbance to the thief roaming around myself. 'Can I use the strength of another to win? The strength of another to acquire victory in everything?' I continued, asking myself various questions which related to both my performance and my chances at winning every match that I was given the opportunity to compete in. I chuckled to myself quietly after asking such a question, responding with a callous 'No' before stepping out from behind the wall to apprehend whoever was there. Launching around the corner, I prepared to strike... looking for the bastard who had tried to invade my home and rape it of everything it contained. '... and so you finally show.' comes the voice of disgruntled fiend. I turned around to see the being standing there, staring at me with the beady holes of their balaclava. I maintained my position, readying myself for whatever would come next, preparing myself for the final showdown. The seconds came and passed, the thief laughing loudly with the knowledge that he'd just caught one of the solo running characters off-guard. Ready to launch forward with an attack himself, a sudden crash meant that he could do no such thing. '... What?' I called as the body slumped to the floor, the same stupid expression ectched by the movement markings on the balaclava. I tried to look behind the thief to get a good look at the individual who now stood in his place but unfortunately, I could see no such thing. Leaving me with no room to answer or ask questions, the individual stuck out an arm and pointed to me. 'You try to live the life of a loner, desperately struggingly to win in your game of life. You try to close people out, wanting to achieve anything and everything on your own. I know of your plight and I know exactly how much you're wanting to win that Championship on Evolution. However, if you're not prepared to allow your friend to stick around and advise you as much as he can... you're not ever going to get far. Just take now for example... these last few moments before you could have been lying in a pool of your own blood. You didn't ask for my help... but without it, you might not have been alive for this conversation.' they answer, turning and walking away in a quicker manner than their arrival. I wanted to give chase to find out exactly who this person was, I wanted to see who was telling me all of these things. Unfortunately though, I froze... even more thoughts running in and out of my mind. What was I to do? What steps was I to take next?
