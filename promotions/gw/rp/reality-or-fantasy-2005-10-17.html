Reality or Fantasy?

17 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-17-2005 Sometimes the line between reality and fantasy can seem the same. When stress comes to much in our lives we try and think that maybe, just maybe, what we see is real. The taste, the smell, the touch, everything seems to fit right into the palm of our hands but to the very naked eye we can tell that it isn’t real. And when we do we simply shake it off. But what if you can’t? That is the question you need to ask yourself? What if you can’t? ------------------------------------------------------ Sunday October 16th, 2005 11:30 PM at night The night was crushing. Covering the wet night of Dallas like a sheet covering a bed. Outstretching the area for as long as the eye could see. The cold air had come in faster then anyone had expected. Dropping the temperature almost below the sixty’s. The road was filling up fast with the sight of kids almost to young to even drive which in this day or age wasn’t odd. The area was close to the clubs. It was always filling up with teenagers that came down to dance, sing, and drink till their hearts began to fade away. It was a slumber for sex and drugs. For me to be down here it was odd. The real Elia would not even come down to a place like this. She was far from the lustful scene were everyone was looking for their next high or the fastest way to get on their backs. She would rather of been next to a light in a leather chair, reading a book or listening to some country music. But now’she wasn’t in control. My thoughts began to race over the facts of what happen last week on Evolution. Losing my title which I only held for a week at that. The thought of being forced by the GW Staff to defend it in that type of match was ludicrous. But that was the way the very few that were trying to go their own way were being treated. Walking down the street, passing by the perfume smelling females with their eyes locking on my body, feeling their bodies hitting my skin as they pass by. “Hey you?” The sound of his voice came over me, making me stop dead in my tracks. “Yes?” Responding without even turning my head toward the man. “What is a good looking woman like you doing down in a place like this all alone?” His slick voice responds with a soft touch in it. Turning toward him with my dark blue eyes pouring out to see what this voice was connecting to. His face was a shattering part of something that you might step on walking down the road. His eyes, bulging out so wide, I myself couldn’t even miss them if I was a mile away. He ego, as big as the chip that I could see on his shoulders. Looking right at him, watching him as he steps out of the darkness a little more. “Looking for something.” Responding to his question, licking my lips just a little bit for him. “Looking for something” Well that’s odd because yours truly was also looking for something as well and I think I’ve found it.” Walking a little towards me with his attempt of a sexy smile. His eyes just couldn’t seem to stop looking at me. I could feel his body heat rising up in his body with every little step he took. “Baby, you look so good. Why don’t you come with Carlos and we can go find what you are REALLY after.” Laughing as those words came out. “But what makes you think it’s you that I’m looking for?” Running my hands through my hair, giving a little smile towards him as he came even closer. His hands, hot and sweaty, caressing my arm over the slick leather top I was wearing. My mind went a little crazy with every little centimeter that he was covering. Stopping right behind me before slowly talking into my ear. “Does it really matter if it’s me you are looking for?” Rubbing his hands closer and closer to my neck. “I’m here and that’s all that matters.” I could feel his hands wrapping closer and closer towards my neck. Going to their destination faster. My heart began to struggle to hold my adrenaline back. The fear was gone, the hard breathing was gone, everything that I use to feel was replaced. Lowering my head to look down while I felt his sweaty hands arrive to the proper place they were meant to go. “I don’t think you want to do that?” “And why not?” Replying to my little threat or what he thought of it. “Because you’ll make him angry?”, raising my head up, feeling my body changing deep inside. “Him?” Asking out loud while still holding on to my neck. The fire deep inside me was starting to come out. My mind, my strength, everything in me was slowly changing to the point were I couldn’t stop it. I felt his grip slowly loosing up. “Wh’What’s going on?” Twisting around with his hands still on me, looking at his eyes as they froze with fear in them. His hands come right off of me, backing up just a few feet away. All of a sudden, out of nowhere, his face began to change. Changing in to something that would even shock me. His face, familiar to every thing I had tried to forget. He was’he was? “Father?” “Elia?” It just couldn’t be. Hovering above me like a leaf in the night. “Father’why do you come?” Asking him with my eyes beginning to cry. “You must fight him. Don’t let him win?”, responding with his hands out wide in front of him. “FATHER!” Screaming as he began to float away. “WHAT DO I DO?” Looking towards him as his body slowly began to disappear from the area, from the sky, from my eyes all completely. But as I sat there, the world around me began to fade away. Everything began to go blurry. My body, going limp through out my body as the image that I was looking at was quickly changed to something that was’well’real. “FATHER?” Looking around the area, wondering what the hell had happen. I was laying in a dark area. My hands, laying in a wet spot of something. The light was off and I couldn’t see anything. My eyes were hurting from the time that I mist of been out. It was all a dream. A dream that felt so real and so unreal at the same time. Pressing up off the cold wet floor, feeling around in front of me before grabbing what seem to be a chain. Pulling it once, a bright light coming on to revile something that would shock even the most sane people. The walls, the floor, everything in the place was showering with one color, red. “What the hell happen in here?” Asking myself as I stood there looking around the room. Blood. The sight that no one ever wants to see. First a dream and now this. What was going on with me? Searching around with my eyes looking for anything out of the ordinary, till something caught my eye. In the corner of the wall was a little line of words that were wrote out in blood. Walking towards it, I slowly read the words out loud. “Full control is mine!” My eyes, pulling back as my chest and breath slowly began to stop before the shocking sensation of everything began to take control on me. What was happening to me? That was a question that I needed to find out and fast?
