Guys' night in

28 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/28/04 It was Wednesday night. Felicia was having a girls night out with some friends. So, Aaron found himself, hanging with Brick. The two decided to stay in and watch a few movies. Right now, they were in the middle of 'I'm Gonna Git You Sucka'. Cheryl: Jack, that's a gun! Jack, what are you doing with a gun?! Jack Spade: I'm in the army, remember? Cheryl: Jack, Mama's right! They're gonna kill you! Jack Spade: Well, Mr. Big is gonna pay. And, if I see those little Evans midgets, they ass is mine! I'm . . . payback, payback! And, Lois Mitchell, you know she snuck me, she snuck me, in the fifth grade . . . I was eating my Jell-O when you hit me, baby! Cheryl: Jack, Jack, they were just kids! Jack Spade: Look, a family full of midgets is not considered kids . . . that's a gang! 'I just don't think that was a smart thing to do, that's all I'm saying,' said Aaron. 'Maybe it wasn't smart, but it was the right thing to do at the time.' 'Cussing out your boss? You thought that was the right thing to do? I mean, this is someone with the power to fire you. She probably could have fired you right then and there.' 'Yeah, well, she could have, but she didn't. We got High Noon coming up and I'm part of the main event. Besides, somebody had to let her know what she did was messed up.' 'But why you?' 'Because Ely was out of it. And even if he was sober, he wouldn't have done it, because he has too much respect. I, on the other hand, don't really give a fuck.' 'Yeah, but are you sure that was the way to do it?' 'Look, I see my boy sitting at the bar drinking down Texas mudlsides left and right, I do something about it, you know what I'm saying? Besides, when have you known me to be afraid of the boss? I wasn't afraid of Ayers. I wasn't afraid of Lansheer. I wasn't afraid of Bubba and I ain't afraid of Christina.' 'Obviously not. Although I can understand why you'd be pissed. I saw Evolution. She was rough on Ely.' 'I told her straight up, if she wasn't cool about Ely and Gunner being together, she should have been up front with him about it. And then she uses it on the job? It was unprofessional.' 'Let me ask you a question.' 'If it's whether or not I'm cool with them being a couple, don't. I'm sick of having to answer it.' 'Well, are you?' 'Am I what?' 'You know what.' 'Yes, I'm cool with it. Why wouldn't I be? When she hooked up with that dude Kael Law, I was cool with it. When she hooked up with Partridge, I was cool with it.' 'Yeah, well, neither of those cats were friends of yours. Ely is.' 'And Gunner's a friend of mine, too. We were never really a couple.' 'OK, fine, I'll leave it alone.' John Slade: You go out there alone, boy, they gonna shoot your ass off. Jack Spade: Hey, I'm a soldier, man! I've been trained for combat! (On a rooftop are two snipers shooting at them with a machine gun. A bystander gets shot and a woman screams. Slade takes cover. Jack is frantic.) 'So, are you ready?' 'Am I ready for what?' 'You know. High Noon. I mean, you are in the main event.' 'Yeah, I know. Don't worry about me. I can handle myself when it comes to Deacon and Train.' 'I hope so.' 'I can. But just in case something goes down, I got a back-up plan. So how about Hollywood Shuffle when this is over?'
