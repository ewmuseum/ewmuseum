Revenge: Invasion

18 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 18, 2006 The hotel room was quiet and pitch black, the conditions worried the Brawler greatly as he entered. He reached out with one massive hand and flicked on the light switch. “Boss man?” He lightly called out as he carefully walked around the room. There was no answer so the Brawler checked around the suite. Biddle wasn’t in the bathroom, on the balcony, watching TV, or asleep. The Brawler paused in the bedroom and scratched his head, it was unusual for the boss man to be out at this time of night. Biddle had stopped frequenting the casino’s bar a few nights ago, instead preferring to drink alone in his room. Just as the Brawler was about to call for a search party he heard a sneeze that emanated from the closet. Startled he turned and slid open the closet door. What he found both amused and worried him, Biddle was crouched down in the closet gently rocking back and forth. “Whatcha doin’ boss man?” “Hiding from THEM you moron,” Biddle retorted as he his rolled eyes. “You don’t need ta do that boss man,” the Brawler said with a confused face, “there’s security all ovah the place...no way in hell those fuckers can get ya.” “I’ll believe that when they’re back in jail,” Biddle said. “How ‘bout ya train for ya match against that little smokin’ guy, that might make ya feel bettah,” the Brawler offered with friendly concern. Biddle sighed, he was sick of wasting his time with the dime-a-dozen flavors of the week in GLOBAL...he wanted a real challenge. Hopefully after he scared Josh away from GLOBAL again he’d be allowed his wish, of course that was if he actually lived long enough to make it to High Octane. “Stop jabbering and go do your damn job,” Biddle told the Brawler as he reached up and slid the closet door shut again. ***** It wasn’t that Ned didn’t like the new guy, he just didn’t feel that he could really trust the guy. Shawn was a big man covered in tattoos and he had something of a Hell’s Angels attitude and that alone really bugged the hell out of Ned. Shawn smelled like cheap cigarettes and he had spent the entire shift talking about ‘hot bitches’ despite Ned’s bored sighs. As Shawn was in the middle of talking about some woman of questionable character that he had spent the previous night with, Ned sighed loudly, looked at his watch, and then looked at the elevator door and prayed that the guys on the next shift would show up early. When Ned turned back around he found himself face-to-face with a small handgun with a silencer attached to the barrel, he became confused; why would his shift partner point a gun at him? “Say goodnight bitch,” Shawn said as he squeezed the trigger. The bullet hit Ned in the left shoulder, knocking him down and splattering his blood all over the wall. Ned fell out of his chair clutching his shoulder in pain. Shawn then stood up and positioned himself against the wall. He heard the knob turn and the creaking of the door as it opened. Out stepped the behemoth known to the world as ‘The Back Alley Brawler’. The massive man saw the blood splattered on the wall and Ned’s motionless body on the floor. “Holy shit,” the big man said. Shawn then sprung into action, pistol whipping the Brawler on the back of the head and knocking him out cold. The big man hit the floor before he even knew what had hit him, that caused ‘Shawn’ to laugh. ‘Shawn’ whistled softly and suddenly Duke and Hawk stepped out of the nearby janitor’s closet, they both wore security guard uniforms. Duke looked around at the destruction his longtime friend had just caused and smiled. “You just did a helluva job Jimbo,” he said. “Let’s go get that bastard,” Jimbo said as he opened the door to Biddle’s room. ***** Outside the hotel sitting in a dark van that was only lit by the glow of his cigarette was Charles. A devilish grin spread across his face as he kept his eyes trained on the window to Biddle’s room. He was waiting for the signal that his men had infiltrated the room. Once they had infiltrated the room they would capture Biddle and force him downstairs to the van under the pretense that the three men are his security escorts to the gym. There was the possibility that Biddle would simply scream for help once they reached the lobby but Charles had already thought of a way to prevent that, the three men would threaten Biddle with prison sex if he refused to co-operate. Charles knew that would be the one thing that would cause a homophobe like Biddle to surrender. Charles took a final drag of his smoke and stubbed the butt out in the van’s ashtray. He looked up at the window again and saw that the light flashed on and off three times, his men had succeeded in infiltrating the room! Charles chuckled quietly as he leaned back in his seat, it would only be a matter of time now before Biddle would be forced to die a slow painful death. ***** “Where are ya, ya rich bastard?” Duke called out as he flipped the couch over with a loud crash. “He’s gotta be in here somewhere,” Jimbo offered as he checked under the bed. Hawk kicked the bathroom door open while he sipped on one of the beers from the rooms mini bar. “He’s not in here,” Hawk said before a loud belch. Jimbo sat on the bed, he knew that Biddle was in the room he had seen him come into the room himself. Suddenly he heard movement in the closet. He went to the bedroom doorway and motioned for his partners to join him. When they entered the room he pointed towards the closet door. Quietly, the three men approached the closet. Duke quickly threw the door opened and right there cowering in the corner was ‘Mr. Main Event’ himself, Mike Biddle. The fear was apparent in Biddle’s dark blue eyes as he looked up at the three men standing before him. He silently cursed himself, he now knew why the new security guard looked so damn familiar. Biddle wanted to say something heroic, to get up and kick some ass...but he couldn’t, he was frozen in his little dark corner of the closet. “Ya remember us, don’t ya hoss,” Duke said with a smile as he cracked his knuckles. Biddle nodded, how could he forget these three men? He had screwed them over and sent them to prison after all. The story was long, complicated and included a whole lot of blackmail and other illegal things. Needless to say, these three men had more than a few good reasons to kill Biddle. Duke reached into the closet and pulled Biddle out into the bedroom by his hair. Hawk kicked Biddle in the back while Duke punched the pretty boy right in the face. Jimbo joined in with a few lefts and rights of his own. Biddle was on the ground, his hands over his face while the three men took turns kicking him, Biddle wanted to get up and fight back but he knew it would be futile. He would be dead by the end of the night, he was sure of it. “Alright boys,” Duke said as he wiped the sweat from his brow, “let’s get this little bitch down to the van.” Hawk propped Biddle up against the foot of the bed and looked the millionaire directly in the eye. “If anyone asks we’re escorting you to the gym. Got it?” Biddle weakly nodded his head. Hawk pulled Biddle up from the floor and the three men surrounded Biddle and ‘helped’ him from the bedroom to the living room. Suddenly the front door burst open and in hobbled the still bleeding form of Ned! All three men gasped as Ned pointed his gun in their direction. He squeezed the trigger not once, not twice, but three times and all three bullets hit Jimbo in the chest...killing him instantly. “Holy shit!” Both Hawk and Duke exclaimed as the lifeless body of their long time friend hit the floor. They both looked over at the door and saw that Ned had his gun pointed at them. “I knew we should have brought guns,” Hawk whispered. Duke didn’t say anything, he reached down and grabbed Jimbo’s gun. Ned squeezed the trigger again, discouraging Duke from grabbing an equalizer. “Let’s get the fuck outta here,” Hawk said before he ran past Ned. A few seconds later Duke followed. After they had left, Ned collapsed and later died from the blood loss. ***** A confused looked formed on Charles’ face when he saw that only two-thirds of his henchmen were making their way to the van. He became angry when he saw that Biddle was also missing from the group. How could they have messed up such a simple plan? The van’s side door slid open and Duke and Hawk jumped in, slamming the door shut behind them. “GO GO!” Hawk urged. Charles floored it, and once they were a safe distance away from Biddle’s hotel he let them have it. “Where the hell is Biddle?” He yelled. “We couldn’t get ‘im,” Duke said. “And why the hell not?” “Just as we was about to walk off with ‘im one of his guards busted in and killed Jimbo! And then that fucker tried to kill us!” Hawk said, his adrenalin still pumping. Charles swore under his breath, wasn’t Jimbo supposed to kill the guard? “Wasn’t Jimbo supposed to shoot the guard before the three of you went in the room?” “He did shoot ‘im first,” Duke said. “But that tough son of a bitch got up and shot at us.” “It’s back to the drawing board then,” Charles said with a sigh. ***** When he finally opened his eyes he had to blink a few times to actually focus. His head was pounding, he reached behind himself and felt a gigantic welt that had formed on the back of his head while he was out. The sound of an angry throat being cleared both caught his attention and caused his headache to worsen. He looked up and saw his boss, Mike Biddle, glaring down at him. “Where the hell were you?” Biddle said as he pointed his finger in the Brawler’s face. “They hit me from behind,” the Brawler replied. He knew he had failed, but the fact that Biddle was here to yell at him meant that the security team had done their job. “Did they get the bastards?” “They killed on of them,” Biddle said, “but that doesn’t change the fact that they got past you. If it wasn‘t for Ned I‘d be dead right now.” “Aww, c’mon boss man..” “You’re fired Joe,” Biddle said, “get your things and get out.” Before the Brawler could offer up any further protest Biddle turned and left the room. Alone in his room Biddle sat down on the couch, he leaned back and sighed deeply. He didn’t want to fire Joe, he had proven to be a good ringside ally in GLOBAL and his presence would be missed during his match against what's-his-name this week but that didn‘t change the fact that he had failed when Biddle had needed him the most. The phone rang, Biddle rolled his eyes...he wasn’t in the mood to talk to anyone right now especially after he had spent a good three hours talking with the police department about the incident. He picked up the phone anyway. “Hello?” He sighed into the receiver. “Mr. Biddle?” “Yeah, who is this?” “This is George Robinson, the manager of the hotel sir.” “What can I do for you?” “I want you out of the hotel by check-out time tomorrow.” Biddle sat up, angry all over again. “I’m a paying customer turd snap, you can’t make me leave,” he yelled into the phone. “I have the right to refuse service to anyone sir,” Robinson said, “and I don’t want the negative publicity that your recent exploits will give this establishment. Kindly leave.” The line clicked on the other end. Biddle slammed the phone down. He leaned forward and rested his head on his hands. “What else can go wrong?,” he asked no one in particular.
