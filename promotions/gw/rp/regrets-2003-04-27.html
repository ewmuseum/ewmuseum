Regrets

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/27/03 He wakes up with sweat pouring down his face. His heart races, he gasps for air. The nightmare was awful, it's the same one he's had for almost two years now. He's battling for the GW World Title, it's his last shot a greatness. The match is fierce, his opponent tough. They battle back and forth to a near standstill. The two men collide in the middle of the ring, they both go down. The ref begins the ten count. By the time he gets to six both men are stirring; they reach their knees at the same time. He starts to stand up, but his knee buckles under his weight. It gave out on him at the last second; he falls face first on the mat and loses. As the Champion celebrated in the middle of the ring he...Jesse Briggs, walks to the back in shame. The crowd taunts him as he makes his way behind the curtain, ending his career. He's glad it was just a dream, but it still makes his knee hurt. He wipes some sweat from his brow with his right hand. She stirs beside him, he looks down at her. God she so beautiful. Her glorious long black hair that streches down nearly to her perfectly round butt. She's his angel, his savior. He pulls the silk sheets away from him and sits on the edge of the bed. Jesse stares at his damned knee, it's cost him so much. He hates it, hates the fact that he has a weakness; an obstacle he can never hope to be rid of. He reaches down and grabs his jeans off the floor and slides them on. He stands up, and feels the familiar burning sensation as the ravaged joint bends to his will once more. He buttons his pants and limps out of their bedroom. His knee hurts him badly, it's almost gone. The doctors told him he'd be lucky to live the rest of his life without being in a wheelchair, but he knows better. He knows just how much punishment his body can endure. But it still haunts him, the damned knee. He wishes he had never gotten hurt, but it's too late for that now. All he can do is go on. He walks down the hall and into the living room of his middle class home. He sees his knee brace resting on his black sofa. He limps over to the damned contraption and sits down beside it. Carefully he unstraps all of the buckles and places it on his knee over top his pants. He pulls the straps as tightly as he can stand to and buckles them down. The pressure eases his pain physically, but mentally he curses the brace. Jesse is doomed to wear it for the rest of his life, there is no escape from that. It burns deep within his heart, such damning knowledge. ?I hate this.?He says pushing back the rage and tears. ?Damn knee brace! If I could I'd cut my knee off!? His heart races as he puts his head into his hands and tries to defeat the demon that haunts him. ?It's all McCoy's fault, that son of a bitch! I swear I'll make him pay!? She walks into the living room wearing only a nightgown. Her scent announces her, Jesse looks up into her angelic face. Just seeing her calms him somewhat, nothing ever fully calms him anymore. She sits down beside him and raps her smooth, soft arms around him. “It'll be ok Jess, I promise.” She tells him reassuringly. ?It'll be ok? That's bullsh*t Valerie, we both know it. It won't be ok, not until I make the piece of garbage Bubba McCoy feel the pain I hafta go through every damn day! It's his fault!? Jesse responds ragefully. Valerie pulls away from him for a moment and stares blankly at himand says. ?We both know better than that Jesse Briggs! Bubba McCoy was your best friend, he didn't do this to you, and you didn't do it to yourself either!? Jesse's face blushes with anger. ?Then who did? Was it some kinda freak accident? I don't think so Val. It was because I had to take the brunt of attacks meant for Bubba, that's how my knee got f**ked up! I'm tellin ya, it's his fault!? Valerie reaches out with her left hand and wipes a tear from Jesse's eye and then just wraps her arms around him again. Her scent envelops Jesse, and for a moment his hatred and anger subsides; but he forces it back into his mind and pulls away from his love. He stands up and walks over to the window. Jesse pushes back the curtains and stares out into the dark rainy night. “Valerie, I gotta do some things that you ain't gonna like, and that I'm probly gonna regret. Now don't say nothin' to try and stop me, it's gotta be done. I gotta fight Ely and some sumbitch named Eight Ball in a few days. I'm gonna hurt 'em both real bad. I gotta make examples of 'em. I got no choice at all in this. I made my bed, now I gotta lay in it.” He turns around to look at her. Their eyes meet. ?I just don't want you to think bad of me after I do it. I ain't a bad guy, I just gotta make these people understand I ain't foolin' around no more. Ok?? Reluctantly Valerie responds. “Ok love.”
