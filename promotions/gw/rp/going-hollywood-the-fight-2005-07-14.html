Going Hollywood: The Fight

14 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 14, 2005 Mike had left Crossfire immediately after Marvelous Matters had concluded and flew from Berlin back to Hawaii. When he had touched down on the island he was tired and grouchy. He had once again been foiled in his attempt to become the World Champion. Leinad Soma had won the title shot, Soma was aligned with Biddle but lately Mike had begun to question the need for such an alliance. The only time Biddle had spoken with Soma was the night Rex Deus had formed, after that he had heard nothing from the vampire and he had tired of waiting for action. A bad mood hung over Mike, much like a dark rain cloud. His only solace was the fact that he would be featured in the main event of Evolution for the second straight week in a match for the Platinum Championship. Mike would take out his frustrations on his nemesis Adam Myers. Mike was delighted by the chance for some sweet revenge on the man that had beat him for the Platinum Championship and at the same time cost him millions upon the termination of the contract with Richard Wood. For the time being Biddle had to push the concerns of Global Wrestling to the side, he had a movie to finish filming. Mike had met up with the rest of the cast and crew with only a few scenes left to film. Benjamin Johansen had told Mike when he arrived on the set that it would only take four hours to finish the scenes if they set out on the task immediately. Wishing to be done with film as soon as possible Mike obliged. “And that’s a wrap!” Benjamin exclaimed four hours later. With the filming completed both Patrick Warburton and Jason Lee left for their trailers, both mumbled quietly about how they couldn’t believe that they had actually been a part of this Hollywood blasphemy. Deanna and Bobby remained on the set and wrapped themselves around Mike; Jimmy Fallon glared at the trio from a short distance away. Over the course of the filming Fallon had become very jealous of Biddle. Fallon played the title character and yet Biddle got all the press and all the hot women. Johansen watched over the crew as they packed up the equipment. Once he was satisfied with their work he called Mike over to his director’s chair. Mike approached Benjamin with the two pornographic stars in tow. “Mr. Biddle,” Benjamin said once Mike stood before him, “I’d like to personally thank you for being a part of this project. You truly made the story great.” Mike smirked arrogantly and was about to reply to the director’s brown nosing but was interrupted by an angered cry from the throat of Jimmy Fallon. “What the hell do you mean he made the story great?” Fallon yelled from a few yards behind Biddle. “I’m the star of the movie, I play the title character damn it! He was nothing more than some small bit character’a foot note in yet another one of my great performances.” Mike’s bad mood suddenly became worse. He turned and looked Fallon directly in the eye, which cause the actor to shrink back in fear. “Look turd snap,” Mike said as he pointed his finger in Fallon’s face. “No one gives a damn about you or your damn whining. People are going to flock to this movie to see me and these two lovely ladies, not your pale pathetic ass. Look junior, sucking face with Drew Barrymore doesn’t make you funny’it makes you a disgusting D-Level celebrity.” Fallon’s jaw dropped with anger and the scrawny actor reached back and threw a weak punch at Mike’s jaw. After the punch connected Biddle laughed and replied with a punch of his own, a shot to Fallon’s eye the sent the man down to the floor with a loud thud. Mike then kicked him in the ribs once for good measure. “Oh my,” Benjamin said as he stood over Fallon’s unconscious body and watched as Mike walked towards his trailer with the female stars. Mike’s journey to his trailer was interrupted by Carlos running up to him. “Mark Bishop is here for that interview you promised GW,” the fat boy said between gasps for air. Mike turned to Deanna and Bobby and said, “Wait for me in the trailer, this won’t take very long,” he finished the sentence with a wink and then followed Carlos off to where Bishop waited for him. ***** “You’re not worthy of my presence.” His hateful words repeated themselves constantly within her mind. She had thought that he had changed, that he truly loved her. She hated herself for being fooled by him yet again. Yet, despite the pain and suffering she wished to be with him. She knew that there had to be some way to convince him to be hers. Hillary’s West Hollywood apartment was dark and lonely. She sat in the corner of the living room, still clad in the clothes that she had worn the night Mike kicked her out. Her nymph-like features were stained by tears and her angelic voice had gone hoarse from the constant sobbing of her sadness. She rose from the corner and walked over to the mirror on the opposite side of the room. She stared at her reflection and silently wondered why he didn’t love her; she was both beautiful and sweet. Many men desired her, how come he didn’t? She then thought of the other women that he always chose over her. They ignored him, treated him like trash, and never let him have his way. That’s when it dawned on her, in order to win over her unrequited love she needed to ignore him’treat him as his other lovers had, then and only then would he be hers. A smile spread across her face and she rushed into the bathroom. Mike was still in Hawaii filming his movie but he would return soon and she needed to be ready for his arrival. -To Be Continued-
