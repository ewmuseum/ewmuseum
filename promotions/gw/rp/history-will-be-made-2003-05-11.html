History Will Be Made

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/11/03 A Brand New Day A New Beginning What Lies Ahead Of Us Can Not Be Seen Because We Are Blinded By The Future. Tomorrow May Come And Tomorrow May Not The Only Thing Known About The Future Is Death, Sunrise, And Sunset. The World Never Changes “But We Do The leaves begin to grow on the branches. They haven’t seen the sun for months. A beautiful day, the sun shines high up in the heavens above, glaring down on the bright green grass. He looks up at the sky with a curious look in his eye. He takes a deep breath, indulging the fresh air that is taken for granted. It sooths his mind and relaxes him. His mouth opens and releases words. “It is about that time, the time where everything begins to look beautiful once again. The snow has melted and the temperature begins to warm up. This process is guaranteed. Every single year, the same things happen and we can’t stop it. Mankind creates technology and Mother Nature cannot be controlled. But Mother Nature can control us. We say we are strong. We say we are powerful. But who holds our fears in the palm of her hands?” He smiles. His enjoyment comes from making every single person watching feel like they aren’t good enough, he puts them down’and he laughs. He finds their weakness by targeting what they do not know and then pounds them to their knees. “The night begins to fall’slowly, the sun fades to nothing and the moon rises up in the sky. Why can’t we change this” Why can’t there be sunlight every second of the day? Why does there have to be darkness?” He smirks? “Because we are weak. And because nature is stronger then us. When tornados, hurricanes, and earthquakes roam her land, we flee from her wrath. For we have no control over her and we can not halt this process that happens every day of our lives. Night to day, and day to night. Winter to spring. Spring to summer. Summer to fall. Fall to winter. Why” Simply because this is the only way that she can keep us tame and control. We walk around with our hands covered with blood and glorify ourselves. But with one gust of wind, your life can be vanished from this world forever. A beautiful day? Or a beautiful moment? That is what I have been thinking about for quite some time now.” He looks in the sky and looks around and searches for something? “During the day, no stars are seen at all. But during the night, stars bury the night sky with drops of light. Nothing will ever change that’ever. But a great man, Winston Churchill, once said?'History will be kind to me for I intend to write it.' And he changed the world being the Prime Minister of Great Britain. And that is exactly what I plan to do.” He looks up and takes another deep breath. “At Evolution, another Evolution’I have nothing better to do then show the world that I am the best in Global Wrestling. I will change history by defeating Tragic Hero and Brick in a 3-way match. And then I will have the advantage going into the second round for the Heavyweight Title against Brick. I can smell it my friends, the taste of the title is right in front of my nose. And I know defeating Roz Blayze was easy, and every body else in the locker room knew it in their hearts. But defeating Brick might be a more difficult task, but nothing is too hard for me, especially when I put my mind on something and concentrate. Concentrate on the World’s Heavyweight Title.” He pauses? “In the past, many have held this title. From Justin Marks to Salaok to Silent Wind to Mani Shigeru to Christopher Camero, the list goes on and on and on. But when this whole entire tournament is over, another name will be added to the list. And it will not be Brick, and it will not be Tragic Hero. It will be me, Sage Sabbot’my dreams and goals are about to come true. And nobody can stop me.” NOBODY CAN STOP ME
