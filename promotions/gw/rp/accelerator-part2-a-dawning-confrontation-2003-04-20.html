Accelerator (Part2) - A Dawning Confrontation

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-20-03 'Sweet dreams are made of these, who am I to disagree, travel the world and the seven seas everybody is lookin for somethin, some of them want to use you, some of them want to get used by you' I could see their black suits in my head as I swung the door open, my finger was on the trigger of the gun I clutched tightly in my hand...Johnny Cruse...I gasped a sigh of releif as I looked into his green eyes. He stood there before me, not saying a word, the gun fell from my hand and I lifted my arms and embraced Johnny Cruse and broke down in tears. 'Jay...' He said calmly as I continued to weep like a six year old girl in his arms 'It's over...OmniTech is gone...no more running' 'He exclaimed in a soft voice as he patted my bare back with his hand, I let go of him and wiped my eyes, I felt so stupid at this point, never have I broke down in someone’s arms like that, I don't know what came over me, it wasn't tears of pain, but tears of joy that captivated me to do that 'Lets go inside...we need to talk' He said as he turned me around and escorted me back inside my hotel room. Darkness embraced the room as a dawn sun shed twilight rays through a blinded window, I grabbed a pair tan cargo shorts and pulled them onto my waist, I buckled my brown belt as I sat down on the soft bed and looked up at Johnny waiting to give me some words of wisdom which I knew he had churning around somewhere in that mind of his. I watched as he pulled a wooden chair up and sat down in front of me 'Jay...' He said once again as I dropped my head and looked at the white carpet between my feet' 'Valerie and Colin are missing...I have searched all over for her...I thought OmniTech might have had her...but they don't' He said to me in a depressed tone of voice as I looked up at him with my baby blue eyes 'Damn...' Was the only word that managed to break free of my mind as I watched him stand up and walk over to the window and pull the blind up and look out at the dawning sun 'I don't know who has them, but we will find them...you will help me Jay...won't you?' He said to me as he looked over his shoulder awaiting a response from me 'Yea' I said as I nodded my head ever so slightly 'What do we need to do Johnny?' I questioned to him as he looked out the window with a blank stare...He gave me no answer as I stood up and slowly walked over to the window beside him and looked out at the sun that was climbing higher and higher with every passing silent moment 'I don't know Jay...I just don't know' Johnny said with a sadness I have never seen before, hung his head low as I looked at him with concern 'They could be anywhere...anyone could have them, but I promise you this Johnny...I will be there at your side, from now until I die that I swear to you' I said back to him knowing full well that I would give my life for this man...another moment of solemn silence passes between us as we both look at the rising sun 'I fear my niece Cindy could be next' Johnny says out of nowhere as I once again look over towards him 'Cindy Frost...' I reluctantly say, with a confused look on my face 'Yes...' He exclaimed as he nodded his head and looked at me with fear in his eyes 'Have you talked to Derek?' I asked to him as he looked at me 'No...not yet...thats why I need you to be with me when I do, you two need to straighten out your problems, only you and him can do that, we are more powerful together than separate' Johnny said as he turned and continued to look out upon the horizon 'I know Johnny..I know' I said as I also turned and continued my gaze outward (To be Continued)
