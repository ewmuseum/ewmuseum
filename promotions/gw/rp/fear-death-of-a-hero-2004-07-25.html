Fear: Death of a hero.

25 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 25th, 2004. Death of a hero. Night had fallen and the sky showed evidence of this as it had turned from its pale robin egg blue to a dreary pitch black. Clouds masked the beauty of the stars yet the flickering streetlights more than made up for the blanket that Satan had cast over Heaven's sweet gleam. The streetlight was inconsistent flickering on and off never casting out its beam for more than fifteen seconds. It buzzed ominously serving as a reminder of what part of town I was in. The absolute worst part of town, yet the part of town that could best serve me. My bluish gray eyes pierced the streets, scanning the sidewalks littered with crackheads, prostitutes and the occasional drunk passed out on the steps of an apartment building, which looked even worse than the cheapest of all projects. The buildings that served as the ugly face of this part of town were marred by numerous marks of pride and prejudice. Random graffiti had been amusingly splashed across almost every building. Names, faces, phrases, and even the occasional gang sign littered this part of town, as their mock art seemed all too popular and all too acceptable. The heels of my black Chippewa boots clicked on the cracked sidewalk as I stared straight ahead avoiding any form of contact with the riff raff that served as nothing more than human garbage. I could sense almost every eye boring a hole into the back of my sleeveless white T-shirt, which bore the logo of 'Joe's Crab Shack' on the front. Or were they staring at the leather pants I wore and more importantly the wallet in which they resided? My last priority was to get mugged in such an ugly neighborhood yet it almost seemed likely that such an event could occur, after all, haven't you ever watched the news? Just picture the headlines; man found lying face down in the gutter with his throat slit. He has not been identified as of yet due to being stripped of all valuables. Great, now there's a thought to lull you into a sense of security. Progressing forward I kept my eyes straight ahead in an indication that I wasn't looking for trouble, yet it’s funny how even when I'm not looking for trouble…it's looking for me. This trouble was in the form of a young woman, no more than twenty years of age. Tall, brunette and pretty she seemed far too classy to be in her profession. 'Hi there.' She said, flashing me a smile that almost convinced me a heart of gold resided somewhere within. I didn't respond verbally but merely nodded. I wasn't interested in her forthcoming proposition and I didn't have the time for such petty delays. 'Looking for a little fun?' She asked, as she brought a cigarette to her mouth and began to destroy her body as she inhaled the revolting smoke of this cancer stick. 'Not interested.' I replied, with a cough as I fanned the offensive smoke away from my face. 'Hold on just a moment sweetheart…' She began, as I passed by her without so much as eyeing her over. Paying no heed to her bargaining I pressed on, seemingly not bothered by her presence, yet on the inside I couldn't help but feel for her. Something inside of me wanted to give her a cup of coffee and a twenty-minute lecture of why she needed to turn her life around, to let her know that someone actually cared…yet there were more important things at hand. My pace quickened as I turned into a familiar alley, this was it. With my heels clicking on the cement all of the way to the door I approached with caution and hesitance. Spying the solid metal door with a crude and strikingly ironic crucifix etched upon it I realized that this was the place. I hadn't realized this until now but suddenly a thought entered my mind. 'Why would an occultist have a cross on his door?' I whispered to myself, slightly perplexed by it's presence. Nevertheless I pressed on, reaching for the knob and turning the door until it creaked open on it's metal hinges. Stepping inside I closed the door behind me as an icy blast hit me internally. It seemed as if the blood was suddenly drained from my body and replaced by extreme cold. My knees weakened and threatened to buckle, my throat became dry…this place was already starting to make me uncomfortable. My eyes immediately turned to the solitary light in the room, a lone candle perched upon it's holder spitting forth a blue flame that sent shadows dancing along the wall and cast an eerie glow about the shadow man himself. The shadow man sat at the table on one of the stools; his hood was thrown back yet he still remained wrapped tightly in his raven cloak. His eyes were turned toward the table and he spoke without glancing up at me. 'I was expecting you.' He said in a voice so cold that it sent a shiver down my spine and Goosebumps across my skin. 'But I never told you that I was coming…' I responded, rather confused by the fact that he had prophesied my visit. 'You haven't told me a lot of things, yet I know you as well as I know myself.' He said in a voice that seemed to only creep me out more. 'Sit.' He commanded, as he motioned to the stool across the table from him. Upon his command I promptly sat. You would too if you heard the cold tone in which he spoke. A tone that he didn't need to raise to get his point across, the kind of tone that sent you into fear for your life. 'What do you know about me?' I questioned in disbelief, waiting for the shadow man to answer this question with his nearly omnipotent wisdom. 'Everything.' He said as he looked up. 'Would you like me to tell you of what hospital you were born at, your social security number, or how your first day at school went?' Staring into his haunted blue eyes I knew that he wasn't bluffing. 'How do you know this?' I demanded, a bit perturbed at the fact that a total stranger knew me as well as I knew myself…or perhaps even better than I knew myself. 'In the years that I have practiced the black arts I have learned many tricks of the trade, many secrets that few will ever know. And out of those tricks and spells…don't you think I would know how to read a pitiful human?' He smiled confidently…if you can call it a smile. His teeth gleamed evilly in the candlelight and I cleared my throat nervously as I placed my hands on my thighs. 'This place makes you nervous.' He stated in a no-nonsense manner. 'It makes every sane person nervous.' I shot back defensively. Glancing around the room I knew that he was right. If it wasn't the numerous books of black magic spells and potions it was the crystal ball that sat between us on the table and the dagger that was impaled into the wood of said table. 'You consider yourself sane?' He asked, with that disturbed smile still plastered on his wrinkled features. 'Any man who is willing to stand up to Maverick Reno can hardly be documented as perfectly sane.' 'Or it's because he's a hero.' I boldly stated in an attempt to show that I was not intimidated. 'Hero? You fear Maverick Reno.' He shot back, crushing my rising ego into a thousand shards. I said nothing. He was right; I did fear Maverick Reno. As much as it pained me to admit it to myself…I did fear Maverick Reno. He was a man guilty of murder on more than one account yet he was no common street thug. He was a professional, a businessman, and a man of great intelligence…even if he was playing jump rope with the fine line that resides between genius and insanity. 'There is nothing to be ashamed of, fear is perfectly natural. After all, courage isn't the absence of fear but rather the strength to carry on when fear is present.' The Shadow man said, the evil grin no longer upon his face. 'I came here to rid myself of that fear.' I whispered, my throat dry with terror. 'Foolish mortal…you should no better than to lie to me!' He angrily spat, with sparks flashing in his crazed blue eyes. 'I'm not lying!' I protested, my own eyes widening in shock of that accusation. 'Another lie.' He hissed at me in his dark tone. 'Fine, you’re the expert…you tell me why I came here.' I replied in blatant sarcasm. 'To learn of your future. You fear Maverick Reno and what he can do to you…and your fear has led to paranoia. Such paranoia has brought you into my lair, to my altar and in search of my wisdom.' He cackled, the evil smile once again tugging at the corner of his lips. 'That’s ridiculous, how are you supposed to predict my future?' I mockingly stated. He interrupted me by raising his feeble right hand and placing it upon the dim crystal ball in front of us…suddenly it came to life with a transparent glow! 'Look into your future…' He commanded, yet I needed no prompting as my eyes traveled down to the glowing orb undoubtedly fueled by the flames of hell itself. I wanted to look away this wasn't right, this wasn't socially acceptable! Common sense and logic screamed in protest yet they had lost the war to curiosity and my search of knowledge. Inside of the glowing orb a New World came to life, a world in which I was the main attraction. ---- Standing in the center of a parking garage dressed as I am now I made quick steps toward my Jeep on the other side of the lot. '22-B, 22-B, 22-B.' I repeated to myself as I sought after the Jeep. My eyes spotted 57-B…great, I had a lot more walking to do. Suddenly something stopped me dead in my tracks as I heard the motor of a car come to life. It came to life with an intense roar. Taking a step forward I thought nothing of it until I heard the car accelerate at a quick pace and the squealing tires warned me of approaching doom. Spinning on my heel I saw the car driving straight toward me at a breakneck speed! Black Mercedes going only God knows how fast and it's headed straight towards me…not good. With the car mere inches away I dove for safety with a leap so mighty it appeared that I was auditioning for a role in a Superman movie. Flying through the air I crashed hard onto the pavement the flesh tearing off of my elbows as loose pieces of gravel dug into my skin. Suddenly the driver slammed on his brakes and I heard four doors fly open. 'Did we get him?' Asked a voice which I could easily identify as the baritone of Tobias De Sarret, Reno's right hand man. 'No, he got out of the way!' Groaned another would be assassin of whom I had no identity to place with. 'I'm telling you…that guy can move.' Said the third voice, the more laid back of the group it seemed. ' I don't care how well he can move, he can't outrun a bullet…lets find him.' The fourth voice was also a mystery to me yet I didn't really care to stick around and take mental images of these guys, my number one priority was getting out of there. I couldn't care less if I could spot them in a line up I just wanted to run for my life. Forcing myself onto my feet I crouched slowly behind a green Buick LeSabre using its wide body to mask my frame, in hopes that I could escape what would surely be a painful death. 'Mav, isn't going to be happy if we come home empty handed, so lets find this guy, off him and get out of here.' Said the voice which I recognized as DeSarret's. I had four headhunters on my tail and Maverick Reno's bounty on my head, suddenly this situation seemed all too bleak. Dropping to my knees I began to crawl as I heard the sound of four men moving about in the parking garage, headed in different directions. At least this way the odds were better, yet even at these odds I'd be screwed if this were Vegas. The sound of approaching footsteps met my ears and with something of a six sense flowing through my body I knew that at any second my life would be in jeopardy. I wouldn't die without a fight though; they wouldn't take me down alone. To steal a line from a country song, 'If I'm going down, I'm taking you with me', and those were my exact intentions. The footsteps came closer and peering underneath the bottom of the car I saw a pair of black pants and Italian leather shoes on the other side! 'Not good…' I groaned in silence, as I rolled over and to the other side. With any luck, I would be able to crawl away undetected…then again, I don't believe in luck. Biting my lip I realized that there was only one feasible task on my agenda if I wished to escape with my life intact…to take him out before he took me out, it was that simple. I heard him mumble something softly to himself before I saw his feet change direction as he began to walk around the car. He may have a gun, intent to kill and a whole lot of anger but I did at least have one thing, the advantage of surprise. Then again, the advantage of surprise doesn't stand up too well against a gun. Moving my body into a crouched position I waited patiently for the headhunter to step around onto my side of the car, and here he came. With every muscle in my body taut and my structure coiled tighter than a spring I leapt into action! My left hand went to his throat in a knife-hand strike that left him gasping for breath, my right leg whipped into an outside crescent kick and sent his gun flying across the cement. Unable to even speak or call for help he was utterly defenseless and I put him down for the count with a hard right leg round kick to the side of the head that no doubt sent a parade of stars dancing circles around his head. He fell like a tree his body crashing to the pavement and his head painfully smacking the cement as his eyes closed. One down…three to go. Taking cautious steps I made my way to where his disarmed weapon had skipped away too. Kneeling down I retrieved the Nine-millimeter pistol and clutched it firmly, admiring the fine weapon that rested in my hand. The pistol grip was hot, moist with sweat from how tightly the man had been holding it. He had been nervous, meaning he was obviously new to this job. Great, Reno had sent boys to do a mans job. Fine by me, I wasn't against schooling a few rookies but I knew the moment I bumped into Tobias DeSarret that the shells were going to fly and someone would be taking a dirt nap before the night was over. Glancing over at the wall I read it off in a silent whisper. '52-B.' I still had a nice distance ahead of me. Glancing about cautiously I saw no sign of the other three henchmen as they had all vanished from sight leaving only the Mercedes behind. I examined it thoughtfully. It was a fine vehicle and one that I could easily use for a getaway car. They had even been nice enough to leave all four doors open for me. The keys rested in the pocket of Tobias DeSarret, yet hotwiring the car wouldn't be a problem, the fact that this car was no doubt being traced would be a problem however. It would be unlike Maverick Reno and his boys to leave me a perfect getaway car…it had to be bugged, traced or something. Whatever it was, I didn't dare step behind the wheel of that car. I would take my chances with getting to my jeep. Scanning the parking garage once more I walked away in a brisk pace. '51-B.' I counted to myself. '50, 49, 48, 47, 46, 45, 44…' I continued on, all the while keeping an eye out for Reno's henchman. My brisk pace evolved into a jog as I covered territory with more speed, moving by the cars while trying to stay as unnoticed as possible. Whipping my head about I saw no one on my trail so I once again accelerated my pace, this time into a run. '42, 39, 36, 32, 27…' I continued to count as I closed in on my vehicle. Suddenly my eyes spotted something and I came to an abrupt stop even dropping to my knees and rolling out of sight behind a parked Ford Ranger. Next to my jeep was a man dressed in a black suit. He sat casually on the bumper looking as nonchalant as a man in a barbershop. A smug grin was etched across his face and it was obvious that he was poised to halt my escape. 'Not so fast my friend…' I said grimly, my upper lip headed North in an angry snarl. Scanning the area once more I knew that this was it. Now or never, crunch time, ten seconds on the clock and down by a touchdown. With a sense of urgency I rose to my feet and flicked off the safety on the pistol I clutched. My hands shook slightly as I raised the gun… I had never killed anyone before and I didn't want to start now but if it came down to my life for theirs…so be it. My finger pressed against the trigger, waiting to send a shell into the mans skull and splatter his brains on the wall for the flies and maggots to feast upon. It was an uninvited entry and it would be the premature end of his life, but he should have thought about that before he decided to make an attempt on my life. 'An eye for an eye.' I whispered to myself, quoting the Bible in an effort to remind myself that this murder would be a righteous one. A righteous murder? How ironic. Closing one eye I held the pistol at an arms length and pointed it directly at the back of his skull. Praying that the bullet would fly straight from my shaky aim I squeezed the trigger. Apparently God didn't hear my prayer as the bullet slammed into the back of the mans shoulder and sent him flying to the pavement with a scream of pain! 'I knew I should've made my peace with God!' I growled as I took off on a dead run towards my jeep. The barking gun, the screaming victim, these had all served as an alarm to Tobias DeSarret and the fourth man. The two remaining headhunters were no doubt hot on my trail now and I had mere seconds to escape if I wanted to make it out of here alive. Bolting to my jeep I threw open the door only to see the man I had shot scrambling for his pistol. Reacting without even thinking I sent three more rounds into his shoulder, each shot punctuated by a scream of pain. Then I heard the bark of a gun behind me! Wheeling around I snapped off a lucky shot, which caught the fourth man in the right knee and sent him down to the pavement clutching his knee, screaming as if he were the victim of Lucifer's most horrendous torture. Turning once more to enter the jeep…time turned into slow motion. The clocks seemed to freeze, the time in between ticks and tocks had went from seconds to hours, my own cry of pain came out distorted as a bullet tore through the flesh of my left shoulder! Nearly dropping I staggered forward and spun around blasting away with my pistol. The first shot hit the pavement, because no one was in sight. The second shot zinged away into oblivion as my eyes spotted the gunman…Tobias DeSarret. He fired another shot, one that tore into my stomach piercing my innards and forcing a cascade of blood to spill from the entry into my stomach. Stumbling back I collapsed into a sitting position. I had never felt anything like this. The hot, sharp, sickening feeling…the stabbing pain in my stomach, a stomach ache so bad that it would send Tums and Rolaids running away in fear. I groaned in agony as I placed my left hand over the hole in my stomach, it was then that Tobias DeSarret fired his third shot into the left side of my chest. And then…everything faded to black. ---- With a gasp I recoiled in shock from what I had just witnessed. How could I be destroyed in such a barbaric manner? This was America, this kind of thing can't happen to me, we have laws! My breathing became sharp and my entire body quivered as I felt a stabbing pain ripping at my innards, the glowing crystal orb had now returned to it's deadened glasslike state, gleaming no more. 'That…that…can't happen…' I gasped, nervously twisting my ragged black locks a look of terror expressed on my face and enhanced by the candlelight. 'Oh, but it can. If you allow it…' The shadow man hissed in his evil manner, yet even as evil as he was, his evil seemed to pale when juxtaposed with that of Maverick Reno's. Swallowing my pride and choking down the vomit that had risen from the pit of my stomach I bowed my head in humility and forced out four painful words. 'Will you…help…me?' I stammered, pleading with the shadow man. His evil grin returned the wrinkles in his face highlighted by this terrifying grin, leaning across the table his familiar hiss struck my ears. 'I am the only person who hates Maverick Reno more than you, yet I cannot stop him. You alone will be hard pressed to stop him but together we will be invincible. To tear Maverick Reno down would be the culmination of my obsession. Every night I dream of seeing him wither like the grasses of the fields when winter falls. I dream of his blood upon my hands…now my faith lies in you to make the dreams of an old man come true.' Bringing my eyes up to meet the haunting orbs that lurked in his sockets I slowly spoke. 'Invincible.' I repeated, staring into his eyes. Looking into my eyes his grin grew wider and for the first time since crossing the path of the shadow man I realized my destiny. My lips turned up into a grin of their own as he and I spoke simultaneously. 'Invincible.'
