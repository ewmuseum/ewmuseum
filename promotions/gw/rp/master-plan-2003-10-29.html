Master Plan

29 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/29/03 OOC NOTE - This rp was co-written by myself and Chris aka Deacon Holmes. Credit to him as well. “Let’s go Jushin,” said Ichiro. “We gotta meat Deac in like twenty minutes.” Ichiro sat impatiently as he listened to the pipes of his brother’s apartment pumping water into his bathroom. He also heard the sound of the shower going, the sounds of the drops hitting the ground, and then sliding down the drain. “Hold on a minute, I’m almost done!!” yelled Jushin back from inside the bathroom. A few minutes later he emerged from the bathroom, clothed in a pair of blue jeans, and a white Armani T-shirt. Jushin looked down at his brother who was dressed similarly in a pair of blue jeans, a white Sean John hooded sweatshirt, and a pair of white K-Swiss shoes. “It’s about time bro,” said Ichiro. “Yeah, yeah, yeah, shut up. I’m here now, besides you’ve driven with me lots of times. Am I ever late for anything?”” “Good point,” said Ichiro. Jushin walked over to the counter top where Ichiro was sitting. He threw the towel which he was drying his hair with onto the couch, and then grabbed his keys off of a hook on the wall. He smiled, and grabbed a jacket off of a coat hanger, and swung it over his shoulders as he began to follow his older brother out of the apartment. He shut the door and locked it behind him as he walked to the elevator which his brother was already waiting in. He stepped inside and watched the doors shut as his brother pressed the button to take them down to the parking lot area. The elevator shook as it began to descend down the shaft and the two brothers waited in silence, until the doors opened once again. They stepped out into the soft sunlight of the afternoon sunshine shone into their eyes. Both men, instinctively reached into their pockets, and they each put on a pair of black Oakley sunglasses. They continued across the open parking lot area, until they came to their new ride. Jushin used his remote starter to unlock the doors, and to start the engine. Ichiro watched as the black Mitsubishi Eclypse Spyder roared to life. Ichiro opened the passenger door and stepped inside, he calmly buckled his seat belt, and leaned back. His little brother Jushin however, was more energetic and hopped inside. He didn’t bother doing up the seat belt, and he just turned his key in the ignition, pressed the clutch and through the car into first gear. He rolled through the open parking space, and was soon out of the crowded lot and into the open Dallas streets. Jushin continued his shifting, and the car’s engine continued to roar, as the RPM’s and miles per hour needles, continued to rise. Jushin smiled, he lived for this kind of thing, fast, speed, like lightning. His brother was not fazed, but was not as much of a speed demon as his brother, but he allowed Jushin to do his own thing, it was his car anyways. Jushin continued accelerating and the cars engine continued to purr. Ichiro looked at the clock, they had five minutes, and they were pretty close. Maybe Jushin was right, maybe they would not be late after all. Ichiro sat back and closed his eyes, he though about why Deacon had called them to this meeting, what had he needed to discuss and why couldn’t it wait? Several minutes later Ichiro opened his eyes, as he heard the distinct sound of the car’s engine quieting. It was apparent that Jushin was slowing the car down. “We must be close” thought Ichiro as they began to turn into the parking garage where Deacon had told them to meet them. Ichiro looked at Jushin who was smiling. “Welcome to the world of the living Sleeping Beauty,” he said in a sarcastic voice. Ichiro could only shake his head as they pulled into the parking garage. As requested the Souls of the Black Lotus, began to go upwards, and worked their way through the floors up to the top floor of the parking garage. They arrived on the top floor and saw that Deacon Holmes was nowhere to be found. They pulled into a spot near the edge, and the two Nakazura brothers, got out of the car. Jushin left the motor running, as he didn’t plan on straying too far from his new car. The two brothers sat and waited? Meanwhile A loud screeching sound can be heard as Deacon Holmes forcefully stomps his left foot down on the clutch of his black Porsche Carerra GT Turbo while slamming the car down into third gear causing the engine's revolutions per minute to dramatically increase while flinging Deacon backward in what would appear to be an attempt to force him into, and eventually through, his soft hand crafted leather seat. Low profile tires so thin they could be mistaken for not even being there at all continue to scream out in protest as the vehicle continues to race forward at blinding speeds. Holmes quickly jerks the car from one side of the road to the other as he delicately yet quickly whips around a slow moving cable repair van, through a red light, and into a busy intersection which he zips through while averting disaster on a numerous number of occasions in only a few seconds time. As he slams the car into fourth with the miles per hour gauge still rapidly climbing it would appear that the car is moving so fast that had God been trying to strike Deacon with lightning on this day he would have missed... terribly. 'Hustle' by Youngbloodz pumps crystal clear through all eight speakers of Deacon's custom made system made especially for such a small car. From the street sign quaking rumbles being expelled from Holmes' car it is apparent that he is using every ounce of available wattage in an effort to deafen each and every person he passes, fellow motorists and pedestrians alike. Although he is hidden behind the illegally tinted windows, which match the deep black paint job of his ride, some onlookers have grown accustomed to seeing such a spectacle on the road and know exactly who is behind the wheel although they cannot see him. Feeling the engine begin to whine a little, Deacon throws the car into fifth while rapidly closing in on the two hundred mile an hour mark, not to mention blazing a trail down fourth street and weaving in and out of traffic like a sewing needle through a piece of torn cloth. If near misses and close calls were bread and lunch meat Deacon would be able to pack a picnic basket big enough to feed the entire world twice over. Just before reaching a red stoplight Holmes jerks the steering wheel to the far right while simultaneously pulling up the emergency brake, causing the car to turn horizontal and continue to slide forward. Inches away from colliding with another vehicle, Deacon quickly drops the emergency brake, smashes the clutch down, throws the car into second gear, and slams his foot down on the gas sending the whirling black blur shooting into a barely noticeable parking garage. Once inside he continues to accelerate as he speeds up the coiling passageway leading to the top of the parking garage. Once there he downshifts and cuts a straight path toward a lone black Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder, much like that of John Zero, which sits in the center of the lot. Standing side by side while leaning back first against the car with their arms crossed are Jushin and Ichiro Nazakura, the Souls of the Black Lotus. Mere feet away from slamming into the men Deacon once again pulls the emergency brake and slides his car parallel to theirs, stopping it no more than a foot away from the two men who continue to look on, unimpressed. Deacon exits the car and looks at the Souls of the Black Lotus, who stare at him, unimpressed. He smiles, and nods and receives a similar reaction from the two brothers. They watch as Deacon stands beside his car, but then walks over to theirs. “Well what did you want to talk to us about?” asked Jushin, the younger of the two Nakazuras. “Well, I have a plan. A sure fire way that we can make sure that there is no way in hell that the Youngbloods make it to our match at Evolution,” answered Deacon. “Well, I like the sounds of that,” said Ichiro. “But how, are we going to pull this off?” “Well you see my fellow TUE friend, we have unlimited access to a wide variety of resources because of our TUE status. So I have devised a plan for us to divide and conquer. So I got a plan, if you guys are in?” “Sounds pretty dirty to me,” said Ichiro. “We’re in,” interrupted Jushin. “Great!” said Deacon and a smile spread across his face. “Aight, we never know who is watching, so we’re going to have to get out of here. So hop in that cute little import car of yours and follow me if you can keep up!” “Oh, you won’t be more than a few feet ahead the entire trip, Deac.” said Jushin. “We’ll see about that!” And with that Deacon ran back to his car. He jumped into the already opened door of his black Porsche Carerra GT Turbo. He slammed the door shut, roared the engine to life, clutched and then hammered the car into gear. The tires screeched and squealed as the car roared off on its way down the ramp towards the street. The two Nakazura brothers smiled, and jumped into their black Mitsubishi Eclypse Spyder and began slammed their doors shut. Jushin roared the car to life, and shifted into gear. His tires spun, and the car fishtailed around as he peeled out after Deacon. “I hope this guy has got a plan,” said Ichiro. “Have you ever seen this shit Deac cooks up” He’s a genius, I guarantee he has something great planned,” answered Jushin. “You don’t become the GW World Champion of looks!” “I know that, I just hope our faith is not misplaced.”
