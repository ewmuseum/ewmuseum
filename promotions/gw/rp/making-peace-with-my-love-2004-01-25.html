Making Peace With My Love...

25 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Making Peace With My Love... El Lunes Mascara Global Wrestling Promo #787 Date: 1-25-2004 Location: The Sanctum Of The Moon...Tijiuana, Mexico Female Voice-'Juan...Juan...' El Lunes-'Maria....Maria is that...is that you...' Maria-'I miss you....I miss you...' El Lunes-'You don't know how much I've waited to hear your voice...Maria I miss you, I love you..' Maria-'I love you too...but why...WHY DID YOU LET THEM KILL ME!!! WHY WOULD YOU LET THEM KILL ME, MUTILATE ME BEFORE YOUR EYES!!' ~/~What once was deep darkness with only their voices heard turns into a bitter flashback...a bitter flash of images as seen through the eyes of a man, watching on as a beautiful Latina woman is being mutilated, slashed gruesomely by means of a sharp knife, blood flowing out of her deep cuts like Roman fountains and her once beautiful and angelic face and body turned into a cutting board, images of her murder flashing through the eyes of a man like a slideshow until finally....her body lays upon the white carperted floor, dead and in a pool of her own blood.....the carpet and her white silk dress stained with her crimson lifeforce as her eyes stare back into the man's from his point of view....even in death locking with his in a frightened expression, lifeless and cold. Suddenly, the image of her staring back at the man cracks like fine glass, shattering into tiny shards as this 'slideshow' of murder ends and we are taken into the real world, the eyes of El Lunes Mascara opening wide with fear as he jerks up, looking about and noticing his is still within the Sanctum of the Moon, his shirt off which exposes his ripped and well defined chest and abs. He fixes the white mask setting upon his face comfortably looking about at the dim Sanctum, it's only light coming from the candles lit all about. El Lunes looks back, finding Justin laid out in his bed, sleeping comfortably and still in his workout clothes consisting of some Nike black and white sweat pants, an Offspring muscle shirt, and solid black wrestling shoes. His blonde and black streaked hair sprawls wildly on his bed, arms wide open as the welts from the previous day on his back, chest, and arms show how much work he put into his training, willing to take pain for his dream to be a wrestling star. El Lunes brushes his hand through his slick jet black hair and looks out the window, the city of Tijuana below resting quietly this day as he looks down upon it in envy, saying to himself quietly as not to wake Justin, running his finger over the dancing flame of one of the candles near him.~/~ El Lunes Mascara-'I envy you all below....being able to sleep so well at night...never having to worry about closing your eyes to rest only to be reminded of the single most horrifying day in your life. What I would give to be able to rest a single unstressful day in my life within the arms of my love....Maria, I would have saved you if I could...you know it....it's all my fault...if I had just...if I had just...' Justin-'If you had just what...' ~/~El Lunes turns his head sharply looking back as he sees a now arisen Justin, stretching his arms after being laid up for so long and looking to El Lunes, Justin waiting for a reply as El Lunes stands up, covering his aloud conversation from before by saying~/~ El Lunes-'I was just recalling a poem Justin...nothing to get alarmed about. I'd like to compliment you on your hardwork these past few days by the way, I can see the want you have for the art of lucha with each and every move you execute and take. With that said...I was called for a GW match in Dallas, against a man simply known as Ice....and either I have the choice of bringing ym best and winning...or bringing my best and going home in shame. Justin, I wish for you to come with me and see your trainer at work.. Come with me to America and learn from me, watch me bathe my opponent in the curing light of the moon and show him where the true power lies. Your answer young one....' Justin-'Ya know Lunes, my mom and dad used to think I sucked at everything. It didn't matter if I brought home all A's or if I had won a friggin PLATINUM medal at the Olympics, they'd never be impressed. They called my dream, my true dream...wrestling....stupid and idiotic. And I've found EVERYTIME I step foot in a ring, a peace of my self-confidence comes back man. When you go to America to take out that mofo, you can count me in cause Justin is looking to see how the JEFE(BOSS) gets down!' ~/~El Lunes lets out a simple laugh, accompanied by a grin as he continues to gaze out the wind, the Tijiuanan people still resting comfortably as Justin makes his way beside El Lunes.~/~ El Lunes-'I envy you Justin. Before this mask became my life, before it became my scapegoat...I used to be exuberant and jovial like yourself. Funny how we both fight for different reasons papito, you for your self-esteem...myself to once again feel whole.' ~/~Justin ties his hair up in a ponytail, the loose hair out of his eyes now as El Lunes approaches the left wall of the Sanctum, covered with news clippings from the local newspapers. His eyes lock on one in particular with a bold headline reading 'Festivale De Campeones'(Festival Of Champions). He looks back to Justin, ripping the clipping from the wall as he says~/~ El Lunes-'In two days time, I will be attending this event...Festival De Campeones....it is a grand festival two day festival held in Tijiuana where the best in lucha all come to meet. My invitation arrived a week ago and it is there where I will meet the best in Mexico, there will also be other events to look forward to and a rookie spotlight as well....I implore you Justin, this would be an event you would want to follow me on.' Justin-'Are there gonna be chicks?' ~/~El Lunes gives his young protege' a blank stare at his choice of question, Justin correcting himself sensing he said something wrong.~/~ Justin-'Sorry sorry....ES EN LA CHICA'S POR FAVOR?! I forgot how you wanted me to expand my Spanish vocabulary.' El Lunes-'Just be ready when I say it's time...' ~/~El Lunes shakes his head with a sigh as Justin looks at him shocked, wondering what it was he said wrong as obviously Justin saw no offense in his choice of question. He shrugs and heads back to his bed, laying out once again in comfort, looking at his picture of his mom and dad beside him, both of them holding each other lovingly with a smile, the photo looking slightly faded showing its age. With a smile Justin says~/~ Justin-'You may have never appreciated a da** thing I did in my life....but I still love you guys. Pity you guys never were the kind to say it back. But if I learned one thing from you two, it's that sometimes you gotta motivate yourself to do thngs and I won't fail at this...I will become the best and when I look back at all this, I'm gonna laugh cause then...I WILL be a star if not the biggest star anyone has ever seen. That I promise...good night mom and dad....' ~/~With a soft kiss to the glass frame of the picture, Justin lays his head back and drifts back within his comfortable sleep....his obsession with being the best a dangerous, yet driving motivator.~/~ On The Roof..... ~/~Meanwhile, on the roof of the Sanctum Of The Moon, El Lunes Mascara looks down upon the city, the wind blowign through his hair as he holds a pair of red roses in hand. The Sanctum is actually the penthouse of a rundown apartment complex, which El Lunes took the liberty of moving within and fixing up for his own needs. He thumbs through his thin moustache and seems unaffected by the cold winds blowing upon his naked chest, the bottoms of his wide-legged karate gi style pants flapping in the wind as he holds the roses high in the air as he gets down to one knee, looking as if he wants to propose to the night sky as he says~/~ El Lunes-'Sweet Maria...it is me again, your love. That can't be you who haunt me in my dreams, I know it isn't. The love we shared was so strong that even you wouldn't haunt me, even torment me in your death....I refuse to see you as a demon but as my lover as always and the day I die, will be the day I'm born again, for I will be in an immortal spirit and you will be right beside me...holding my hand in the gardens of heaven mi amor. Forgive me for the men I will hurt, the men I will snap at the Festival and when I reach the land of America...for it is only to gain some sense of comfort from your brutal death. El Lunes chokes up slightly, growing ever passionate as his monolouge continues. He stops to regain his composure, speaking yet again when he finally is able to let the words escape his throat without hinderance. El Lunes-'To be honest, when I heard I'd be facing Ice in Dallas...I was almost scared I wouldn't be ready to tackle the United States. But you would have told me to not be scared, to not fear something new. I don't fear it my love, I refuse to not as long as you still kiss me in the wind. The only fear will be from Ice himself as I proceed to SYSTEMATICALLY break him down and make him beg to join you in the higher plane. I won't let you down mi amor....not as long as de la luna(the moonlight) feeds my power. Guide my hand my love as I use it to bring pain to those who dare to challenge me and the young one. With that my love...I must depart....you forever own me Maria....forever. A mask can't even cover those feelings. ' ~/~El Lunes tosses the roses into the wind, watching them as they saunter in the wind, floating over Tijiuana with ease as El Lunes makes a gesture in the shape of a cross in the wind before heading back to the edge of the roof, where a metal stepladder awaits him for his return to his hideaway...his place of escape from a world he finds cruel and unloving without the one he holds dear...his Maria. Will he have the strength and resolve to best Ice come Global Wrestling Evolution? That question as well as many others have to settle for being...SHROUDED IN DARKNESS.
