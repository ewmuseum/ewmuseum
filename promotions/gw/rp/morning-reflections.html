Morning Reflections

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The sun slowly rises on a new day. It's rays pour through an open window and creep across the wooden floor of Jack Duncan's bedroom. The Man-Ox lays on a futon, which has been spread out on the center of the floor, his eyes wide open. He looks at the asian woman laying beside-his wife of 2 years, Misato. He looks to the other side and sees his katana, lying just with in arms reach. Jack stands up and walks over to a wardrobe and opens it up. He puts on a red shitagi shirt and a pair of black hakama. He looks into the back corner of the wardrobe and sees his .50 caliber Desert Eagle in its shoulder holster hanging in the back. Jack smiles slightly as he closes the wardrobe and walks away. He picks up his sword and puts it puts it through his belt, and then exits the bedroom quietly.] [Jack walks through the house, and out the front door. He surveys the area, looking across his compound. The whole thing looks like a samurai residence from ancient Japan. Besides the main house that Jack lives in, there are four other buildings-one is for housing the staff, two slightly larger ones are for quests, and a fourth smaller building is for storage. Just in front of the main house is a large garden with a small stream running through the center of it. A red wooden bridge arches across the stream to a small sitting area in the garden. The whole area is walled in. Jack slips on a pair of sandals and walks down to the sitting area. Jack takes a seat and looks back up to see a man walking towards him. The man is dressed similar to Jack, though his clothing is all a dark blue color. The man walks over to Jack and bows to him.] Jack: Rokusaboro, call into the kitchen and have them bring us some sake. Rokusaboro: Yes, sir. [Rokusaboro hurries off, then returns in a few moments. He takes a seat across from Jack, who is staring off into the distance.] Rokusaboro: You seem lost in thought. Jack: I was just thinking about how my life has changed. Less than a year ago, I was living hand to mouth in a bus, having to sleep with one eye open and my hand on my gun. I never considered going anywhere without at least two weapons, and was in constant danger wherever I was. Hell, I had to keep my own wife halfway across the world from me just to keep her from getting involved. [Rokusaboro nods.] Jack: Now, I have my own home, my own land, and my wife lives with me. My life is reasonably safe, and I don’t have to worry about where my next meal will come from. Rokusaboro: It is much more peaceful now that they are gone. [Jack nods in agreement. Two women dressed in kimonos come walking over. One sits down a small folding table, and the other sits a tray containing two cups and a flask of sake on top of it. They walk off, and Rokusaboro pours sake into each of the cups. Jack and Rokusaboro pick up their cups.] Rokusaboro: To your great victory. [Jack and Rokusaboro raise there glasses in a toast, and then both drain them. Rokusaboro pours them each another drink.] Rokusaboro: So I hear you have another match this week. Jack: Yes, against some guy named Markus Riley. I’ve never heard of him, but from what I have seen, he’s no Creature or Chris Mercer so he shouldn’t be too hard. I’ve fought many tougher battles and lived through them. [Jack takes another drink of sake. A look of concern crosses Jack’s face.] Jack: Truthfully, I’ve been wondering who my next opponent will be. Rokusaboro: Why would anyone in GW worry you? Jack: I’m not talking about GW. I was told once that this world still has a use for my soul. The only thing that I am good for is fighting, so if the world still has a use for me, than I must still have enemies to kill. [Jack finishes off his sake, and Rokusaboro pours him another cup.] Rokusaboro: Perhaps you will see some time of peace before your next enemy comes. Jack: Perhaps, but throughout the many lives my soul has lived, it has never seen peace. Once the conflicts are over, the body dies so that the soul can be reborn. It has never once died a peaceful death, and I doubt that will start now. [Rokusaboro nods as he finishes off his cup. Jack drains his cup as well, and stands up.] Jack: Now, if you will excuse me, I have some morning business to attend to. [Jack walks off, heading back towards the main house. Inside, he heads towards the nearest restroom. He shuts the door behind him, then after a few seconds, the sound of water splashing into water is heard. The Man-Ox lets out a sigh of relief.]
