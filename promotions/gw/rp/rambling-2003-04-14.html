Rambling Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #105 Date: 4-14-03 Location: Un-known ((During the first hours of the night. You see a lone man. His body is clocked in the darkness that envlopes the whole world around. The perspration is starting to dampen his hair. The moon's dull rays of light are glistning in the eyes of this man. Apprently the cool,damo night has began to get to him. He bends down and begain to pull on the zipper that hooks his pants. Turing them from break aways to Pants makeing them come together. He then takes one hand and puts it in the side of his pants and pulls out a Small white box. Pulling out a long slender white sticks of tabbacoo. Putting it in his mouth he brings out a Zippo lighter. On the front side it has the former GW wrestler 'The German Sensation' Hans Kruger. The other has the GW logo. The man takes the lighter to the end of the Marlboro light and Instantly there is a fire at the end of it. Then smoke begins to flow from his mouth. The man standing there is none other then Roz Blayze. Gw's New sensation. The Moon light then shines on his eyes. The reflection off his eyes is scary. He then looks at the Lighter. Roz: Hans Kruger, You learn alot from watching the German sensation. These words echo after hey have been spoken through the powerful lips of Blayze Roz: The German Sensation. again these words ring in yor head. like Blayze is speaking another Dilect. Something not of this world. Roz: I was in GW when it was GWF. When Names like Chris Camero,Hans Kruger and Jean Defoe were alltop line dont fuck with me names. Roz rolls his head to the left and right. Cracking his neck with every movement. A Wicked smile crosses his face for a moment but quickly fades. Roz: I was a Nobody. A Nothing. Excatly what i am now. A Nothing to any one who means anything But i dont mind. Roz's tone in this sentance varys from Livid to Calm from moment. From word to word. Like he was trying ti surpress anger. Roz: Honestly Nither are you Sage. You may be the Cruiserweight Champion but your still a nobody. Nothing caompared to the awesome names of Jean Defoe, Hans Kruger or Kully. Yes I remember the Draven Salaok Ladder match. In my mind one of the best ever. I also remember the Kully and Hans doing battle for the world title. I also remember a guy who diddent come into GW to far in front of me. I watched him rise up Becomming one of the greatest Superstars the GW has ever seen. I watched HIm become the Harvester of Sorrow. Yes Sage. I've got a history here. While my track record is not great I know that over comming you you would be a great Honor. Honor? Roz smiles Roz: Honor. What a funny word. What is Honor? Honor is for the weak. The Small. Respect is what i'm after. Sage I find that faceing you will be a Great obstacle. Not becuse you are good. Lets not kid ourselves. You are good but that not the reason. I have not another Human hart to follow me into battle. You have the Nevermore. I stand in front of the nevermore alone. I have nothing. You have me in speed. Quartz has me in power. Salaok has me in Wrestling Power. Kullyspo has me in Wrestling Knowdlage. Draven knows everything about me. So what do i have? Nothing. Not even my will to win will be enough to overcome That but backing out is not an option. I am not just going to bow out of this fight. Not Roz Blayze. See Sage they alaways say Honor before pride. Getting into a fight takes both. Sabbot do you have either. It takes Honor to fight and Pride to take an ass kicking if it comes to you. Roz hangs his head Roz: I try and understand why i am faceing you. Is this punishment for losieng Lisha? Sorry Salaok God willed me to find that house. God wanted Lisha to stay lost. Can i help that? I was the man that went blow for blow with you as you began to vent your anger and now you try and make a mockrey of me? Thanks. It makes me feel good that you would take a nevermore member and take the slim to none chance that i may beat him. Roz brigs his bowed head back up Roz: I dont expact much out of this match. Maybe to be moved up in the Cruiserweight divison. Maybe get me a title shot on down the line but other then this. Nothing. Roz's smile that has faded from earlier on in his speech reappres and when he says nothing the smile becomes bigger. Roz: Nothing. wow. That word is what got me to the point that I am speaking now. Nothing. I began talking about nothing. It's funny. How things begin and end in the almost same paraphrase. I am Nothing. I dont try to kid myself into thinking that i deserve a world title shot. Idont try and fool myself into thinking that i could beat every Nevermoe member. I dont even make myself think i can beat you but not trying is not an option. If i do then i'll chock it up to luck and go with the flow from then on. Roz stops for a moment and bends down. He grabs the strings on his boots and ties them. standing back up he flips teh end of a smoked ciggreett into the darkness Roz: Well Sage. Looks like Evolution is comming and there is no way we can stop it. The day is comming. The 17th. The New Sensation Vs. the Cruiserweight Champion. Non title becuse i havent earned it. There is nothing in the GW that I wasnt with out earning it. Roz's smile again fades druing these words. Now it comes back Roz: So far the harvesters went from Salaok,Quartz,Vyper and Sage to All those as well as Draven,Minia and Grendal. From a small Lion to a Herd owner. The beast that i now stand in front of. I may and most likly have to face all of the nevermore but none of that matters any more. The nevermore has began to make the GW their own personal Playground so far the only one's standing in their way is Silent wind,Brick and Braxon. Well you can almost add another name to this list. The name of Roz Blayze. I wouls stand with Brick. Then again i wouls also stand with Draven. I have known him alomost all my life and I would gladly stand with him or any of the harvester. SAage i dont hate you. If i did i would be hunting you like an animal. I have gotten alot better since my last outing in GW. I dont think you were here. So let me refreash some memorys and let me fill yours Sage. I diddenmt fair to well. Loseing eight of my ten matchs. I only beat Ruckus Inc with the help of Draven in the Death on demand Tag team and a guy named Acid. Now i have beaten One half of the tag team champions in Pete hardy as well as Brick and Tragic hero. Then i lost to Derek Frost. I wont call him one of the best but i damn sure wont call him one of teh worst either. so far two out of three isnt that bad. My record anit shit to Cameros 75 and 6 but still it's better then two and eight. I'm defnitly not the best in the world and Sage come Evolution That i dont scrape the bottem of the barrel The light in Roz's eyes hasent Swayed much in his talks. Again the small box is pulled out and Roz has a smoke in his hands. He pops it in his mouth and brings out the hans Kruger Lighter. Roz: Well Sagewe'll just have to wait and see who's better at Evolution I wish you the best of luck, Roz runs a hand through his damp hair on his head. he turns and begisn to walk away. You onyl see him for a moment then The darkness grabs him. Then he's seen once again in a small grasp of light In the distance a wifl howles and Blayze is gone.
