Getting To The Top

11 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Getting To The Top Judgement Global Wrestling Promo #410 Date: 07.11.03 Location: Haughton, Louisiana It's been weeks and I haven't heard from her. I don't know how long I can go on like this. I have been waiting for her to call me, but it seems she's never going to call. I figured she miss me and won't me back and accept me for me and not my career, but I guess I was wrong. I have to do something before I go out of my mind. She won't even call, I haven't turned my cell phone off for days and it hasn't even left my side for these weeks. Maybe this is why I have been losing and not be able to even want to move. I have to do something and I have to do something now. The room is dark and gloomy nothing can be seen, but the red of the clock that reads, '2:37'. Obviously, its the morning because no light is shining through the open curtains, but the blur of far away marquee lights from the street below and in the distance. Suddenly the comforter flies up and Judgement stands up draping his feet over the side of the bed with his head hanging towards the rough carpet hotel floor. 'I have to call or see her something,' Judgement says to himself staring at the floor. 'I have been up all night long thinking about her. I can't sleep it's even hard to eat without her, but I love wrestling what in the hell am I supposed to do?' Judgement stands up with only his Old Navy boxer's on and walks towards the chair by the balcony and grabs a pair of pants draped over the back of the chair. He quickly shoves his legs into the pants and grabs a shirt from the back of the chair as well. He gets the pants on slips the shirt on and grabs his car keys and cell phone and heads for the door. 'Where can she be?' Judgement says to himself walking towards the elevator searching through the phone book on his cell phone. Judgement steps into the elevator and looks up pressing the button to the lobby. He continues to search for, obviously, her number. After the elevator 'dings' he exits and goes towards the hotel parking area. He walks through the automatic door towards the valet parkers. Judgement looks up and jogs to the nearest worker and grabs his keys. 'Black 1969 Chevelle, get it fast!' Judgement says demandingly. The man nodes and begins jogging towards a large group of cars. As Judgement continues to search for a few minutes his black 69' Chevelle pulls up and Judgement quickly enters the car and drives off with spinning tires. With huge excitement Judgement yells, 'Yes, got her cell phone number!' Judgement quickly dials the number and waits as its assumed that its ringing. He waits and waits and also waits at a red light. Judgement starts to get restless and the red light turns green and he takes off. 'Finally,' Judgement says and then a short pause, 'Why haven't you called?' Judgement asks. Judgement sits on the other end of the phone as the women speaks, 'Well can I come over?' Judgement asks and waits for answer, 'OK, I'm on the way.' Judgement hangs up the phone and slams the car into 4th and the car roars and begins to quickly pick up speed. After about 20 minutes of driving Judgement turns into a small subdivision. He takes a few lefts and rights and then stops in the driveway of a brick two-story house. There is a light shining on from the house and Judgement switches off the car. 'Here we go.' Judgement says to himself before stepping out of the car. Judgement slams his door shut and casually walks up to the door of her home. He rings the door bell and Lyndsi opens the door. 'Hey, come on in no point in standing out there,' Lyndsi says in a much nicer tone then the last time we heard from her. Judgement walks into the room and Lyndsi walks towards the kitchen where she has a coffee cup that beams with steam as she must have just brewed it to keep her awake beings its three in the morning. She takes a seat at the chair and Judgement follows her as well. 'So, why'd you want to come over?' Lyndsi asks in confusion. Judgement begins to open his mouth when the heard of little footsteps are heard behind him. Judgement quickly turns around to his a little girl in a 'Sponge Bob Square Pants' pajama's holding a brown teddy bear by the arm. Judgement stands up and holds out his arms and the little girl comes running. 'Daddy!' she yells and embraces with her father. Tears begin to stream down Lyndsi's cheek as she covers her smile with her hand. After minutes of hugging Judgement picks up his daughter and sits back down with his daughter on his knee. 'Wow, she's so much bigger than the last time I've seen her,' Judgement says with a smile. Lyndsi whips her eyes and looks at Judgement, 'It's almost been a month since you came and seen her.' 'I was waiting for you to call, I was scared I lost you and Michele,' Judgement says with a scared and scratchy voice. Lyndsi takes a long look at her fiancé` and her daughter together, 'You can never lose Michele, but I can't say much for me. Michele is your daughter and you are always more than welcome to come see her, but I told you. It was wrestling or me, I am not having your daughter go with a daddy who can come see her when time permit's itself.' Judgement sits in confusion not even knowing what to say. He stands up and carry's his daughter back to her bed. He sits on the side of the bed after tucking her in. 'Daddy, are you leaving again?' Michele asks. Judgement looks at Michele and kisses her on the cheek, 'Just go to sleep baby.' Judgement kisses her once more and Michele rolls over clenching her doll tight. Judgement walks out of the door switching out the light. He stops at the door staring at her before cracking the door and heading back into the kitchen. He turns around in the hallway to see Lyndsi standing hearing everything and seeing everything that just went on. 'What am I supposed to tell your daughter where you went when you leave her? Tomorrow morning I have to be the one to break her heart because you aren't man enough to do it.' Lyndsi says with a strong voice. Judgement embraces Lyndsi as tears begin to fall from her cheeks, 'Don't tell her anything. Just tell her I will be back tomorrow.' Judgement says that kisses Lyndsi softly on the lips and gives her a long hug. He grabs her hands and slowly leaves with the arms extending as far as they can reach before they are forced to release as Judgement exits the house. He turns once again before exiting and getting in his car driving off. What am I to do now? I have a daughter who needs a father, a wife who needs a good husband and a great life. It's like this when you get started in a new place. It's a new job things are always rough at first, but it all works out in the end. I have to get myself to the top so I can give my wife and daughter the life they deserve. I will get to the top and it will be NOW!
