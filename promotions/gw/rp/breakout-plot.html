Breakout Plot

Date: #1 Crossfire 'Sir, we're going to need you to open your bag.' Damned if this wasn't ironic. Train could count on his fingers and toes all the times he'd walked through customs and one air port or another, with something he shouldn't have had. Usually something brick shaped...packed tightly...wrapped in a thick layer of saran. The first layer of saran covered with vasoline to dull the smell of whatever was inside to fool the K-9's if they came sniffing around. Then of course yet another layer of layer of cling wrap to keep the whole thing from getting messy inside the bag. After all he had his clothes in there and there wasn't a doubt he was going to need them...cuz there wasn't a doubt he'd get away with it. Of course he'd never been to England before. Now here he was...a stranger in a strange land...come not as a criminal, but a champion. Now here he was with this skinny, pale ass mothafucka with teeth that was half tore down with a cheap looking uniform and that pussy ass accent telling him to open his bag. And all knowing he didn't have anything that was illegal in his possesion. Ain't that some shit? He'd already spent what seemed like 20 or 30 minutes wading through customs. It took Train a good 3 passes through the metal detector, removing the layers of platinum chains, rolex and bracelet, all sparkling with diamond before security had decided he wasn't carrying any weapons. And of course just a second ago he'd be signing autographs and posing with the passengers and staff. Yeah....everybody loves the champ. So what was this bullshit all about? Train turned from the little podium as the woman looked over his passport to the voice behind him. The gangly man and two of his heavyset compadres stood at the end of the carry on conveyor belt. Train had gotten on the other side of the metal detector a couple of minutes ago, his sojourn interrupted by the autograph signing, posing and stripping of jewelry. He'd managed to snap the chain, Rolex and bracelet back on and step up to get his passport stamped before they called for him. Now he turned, everyone seeming to eye his suspiciously. They stared..he stared back with the GW Platinum Title laying casually over his shoulder. 'Excuse me?' Train inquired, hearing the declaration perfectly, but thrown off and offended enough to demand it be reapeated. 'We're going to need you to open your bag sir.' the man repeats, bolstering Train's indignation. 'What the hell for?' Train growls at him, cowing the officer's bold tone just a hair. 'We believe you have contraband in your bag and we will need you to open in it so we can inspect it.' 'What? Contraband?! What the fuck you mean contraband?! Do you know who the hell I am?! Apparently yo' punk ass wasn't paying attention when everybody walking past this bitch was asking for my autograph.' 'I'm well aware of who you are sir and I'm sure this is just a misunderstanding. If you will just open your bag and let us inspect it I'm sure we can clear this right up.' 'Oh for real? I'm just supposed to trust yo' ass, right? I don't even know you. I ain't opening a damn thing until you tell me what the hell you're looking for.' 'Sir there is no need for hostility. We have simply found a strange object that...' 'Man stop bullshittin' dogg. If you accusing me of something at least have the balls to spit the shit out.' 'Fine. According to international law it is illegal to transport a knife in your carry on luggage.' 'Knife?! Maaaaaaan.... Let me tell you something son. If I went through the trouble of trying to smuggle some shit into the country? I garundamntee it'd be a lot more than a knife.' 'Well sir we see a wooden handle, partially wrapped in duct tap with a long metal shaft so you can see what we believe you do indeed have a knife.' 'Wooden handle with duct ta....' Train begins, stopping midsentence. Suddenly it occurs to him what the problem is. It's a joke. Litterally a fucking joke...and it was starting to be more trouble than it was worth. Train just lowers his head and chuckles to himself as the security contingent looks on confused. 'Look out dogg.' Train brushes past the small group and grabs his bag. He can't help but laugh as he unzips his bag and pulls open the large front flap, exposing the contents for the world to see. What's inside seems to be neatly seperated. One side of the back is stuffed with combat boots and the fatigue pants Train wears to the ring to let the world know he's always ready to go to war. The other filled with Jeans and Polo shirts. A toiletries bag sits in between the two sections, unable to close with with the two large bottles of cologne sticking out the top. Next to that is the minirature steamer Train keeps with him to work out the kinks in his outfits as being on the road isn't an excuse not to style and profile. The guard looks into the bag examining it's contents for a moment, searching for what caught his eye. He finds it. The guard lifts the first pair of pants and the glimmer of metal catches his eye along with everyone elses. Their faces look terribly concerned about this threatening situation. Train can only snicker at them. The guard reaches in and very carefully takes hold off the 'sharp' metal object. The threat of injury is very serious...in his mind. clamping onto it gingerly he lightly tugs the metal piece out into the open. All eyes lock on...their suspicions of a weapon now confirmed...at least for a second...before they realize what it is. 'Sir,' The guard says, pausing...searching for his next words. 'Are you aware that you are...carrying a...a...a... Spatula in your bag?' 'Yup.' Train states flatly. 'Yes...of course. Well, ummm... Why?' 'What, are spatulas illegal or something?' 'No, of course not sir. It's just...somewhat........... Unusual for people to carry kitchen utensils.' 'Shit, I don't know why! Hell...you never know when you might get the urge to flip up a grilled cheese sandwich man.' 'Of course...grilled cheese... Yes... May I ask....why is it duct taped? Falling apart I suppose...with all the grilled cheese and whatnot?' 'Falling apart? Naw, I just don't want his ass running away.' The guards all take a moment to turn their eyes to eachother, silently helping one another evaluate the situation. Train can barely keep a straight face. 'Oh, of course, of course sir. We'd all hate for...him....to... run away. Ummm... Does he have a name?' 'Pen.' says Train, now realizing how crazy AWS Man has to be to tell people this and actually believe it. 'Pen?! You....named your spatula... Pen?' 'What else could I name him?' 'Oh, nothing obviously sir. What else could you do when naming a cooking utensil, but name it after a writing untensil? And this piece of tape wrapped around the bottom of Pen's metal plate?' 'Talks too God damn much.' 'Of course....... Sir you aren't carrying any medications required to regulate your mental state that you have not declared to us, are you?' 'I don't need no damn medication, I know it's a fucking spatula! Give me that shit!' said Train before snatching Pen from the hands of the guard and shoving him back into his bag. 'You looked, you didn't find shit, I got better shit to do, I'm gone.' He quickly zips up the bag and extends the handle before dropping it to the ground on it's rollers. Normally the guard might protest at the rude gesture... but how do you recover and become a figure of authority after holding a duct taped spatula? Train latches the Platinum Title on top of the bag and walks by the podium, grabbing his passport as the woman extends it to him without breaking stride. ===== ===== ===== ===== London slides past Train outside the window of the taxi. It amazed him how similiar and different things could be at the same time. The bright neon of the lights in the center of the city reminded him of Time Square. But then the buildings just oozed history and age like no others he'd ever seen. Now was this the life of a champion? Traveling the world... recognized wherever you go...on first class flights... staying in 5 star hotels...you every whim catered. Yeah... that's the life of a champion. Too bad Train wasn't living that shit. No doubt a Limo came to meet Partridge and Josh at the airport. They probably rode from there to Hyatt Regency where he heard they were staying sipping champagne all the way. Train just had to sit and stew in the back seat of this cheap ass looking taxi that wreaked with a smell that was likely reminicent of the one coming from the scuzzy looking driver's ass cheeks. Maverick Reno? Shit, that muthafucka probably owned a Bentley to drive him and some penthouse someplace. Pendragon? Who the hell gives a shit, how the fuck did he get put down anyway? They ask Train to rep GW and then they treat him some kind of second class schmuck? All Reno has is a tag strap, but he's the man cuz he can plant a car bomb. Those fuckin' suburbanite tricks Josh and Partridge were the fuckin' golden boys cuz their belts were made of gold. Fuck the lip service from McCoy...this place still had no respect for him. Didn't matter that he held the Platinum Championship high and dared somebody to try and take the shit from him. Didn't matter that even the dreaded Maverick Reno didn't have what it took to do that. Apparently no one had been listening a few weeks ago when Train told the world he didn't play second string for anyone. That's ok. The world obviously still had the shit twisted, but that was ok. This was his chance to untangle that shit for them. They brought Train here to be part of the team...to support everyone...to blend in. Yeah. Twisted. But Train would make it clear.... Fuck Josh. Fuck Partridge. Fuck Reno. Fuck Pendragon. Fuck McCoy. Fuck GW. This wasn't about them. This wasn't about the almighty GW honor. If they thought Train had come here to put his ass on the line for them they had another thing coming. When the wargames were over the only reason anyone would remember Global Wrestling was because it was where The Platinum Champion roamed. Because Train rolled there. The background was for suckers. Being 'part of the team' was for chumps. You come in this world alone and you leave the bitch the same way. In the middle... Fuck the bullshit people talk. Nobody feels your pain for you. Nobody else has to live with your regrets. Nobody else has to make peace with your what ifs. It was time for him to take center stage. It was time to take point. He didn't care who held what belt. After the smoke cleared on the war games people were going to realize any championship is only as good as the man holding it. Damn what the belt reads, after tonight there was only going to be one number one man in GW and it wasn't going to be Josh. It wasn't going to be Partridge. It wasn't going to be Reno. It wasn't going to be Pendragon. It was going to be a literal process of elimination and when all was said and done there was only going to be one name left on people's tounge... Train. ===== ===== ===== ===== The thick, dusky smelling smoke permiated the air. The aroma was intoxicating...literally. He didn't know where they'd gotten the shit from, but this was some good weed. As soon as he stepped though the front door of the bar the plume hit him right in the face. He was definitely in the Jamaican part of town. Just The Boy in Black told him to be. Train knew the name of his federal informant perfectly well, but he'd gotten so used to calling him that in code that he couldn't help, but call him that everywhere else. Luckily Train had earned enough favors, ie passed him enough cash, to earn a freebie tip for his trip to England. Guns were illegal in this country. Of course, like every other place on Earth, there is always a place to get what you want, no matter what it is. All you gotta do is have the cash. The Boy in Black had called in a few favors for him. He hooked him up with a name, an address and an appointment with some Jamaican mobster who could get Train a peice. He'd thought about trying to bring his own piece through the regular baggage shipping as opposed to the carry on. But that shit at the airport with Pen had only confirmed that wasn't the smart choice. He had the cash. No need to take a risk when he could easily get what he wanted without it. The dim lights hanging down from the cieling illuminated the haze over the non descript faces at the tables and boothes like fog over a city. Train's dreadlocks helped him blend right in here as they swung freely while he strolled up to the bar tapped the counter. The rotund bartender, hidden behind a main of thick dreadlocks and a scruffy beard moved over to take the order of this stranger. 'Whatcho have mon?' 'Michelob.' He's quick for a big guy. The bartender hits the cooler, grabs the bottle and pops the top in less than 10 seconds. The large man puts the bottle down on the bar in front of Train with a nod and begins to walk away, but Train interrupts... 'Hey big man... Let me ask you something dogg. Where can I find Spliff?' 'Who's askin' yankee boy?' 'Name's Train. I got an appointment.' 'Appointment? hahaha... What you tink tis is? A lawyer's office mon?! Spliff ain't here.' 'He's not here?' 'Did I stutter mon? Spliff ain't here?' 'That's funny man... See I heard he was here about all the time.' 'He's a busy man.' 'Well shit...that's fucked up man. See cuz I really needed some help and I heard he was the man to see.' 'Sorry mon. 'Me too...' Train quickly reached into his pocket and pulled out his wad of American bills. Train slowly thumbs through, of course starting from the outside working his way past Franklin...to Grant...to Jackson...to Hamilton...to Lincoln... to Washington.... Train doesn't bother looking up at the bartender. No need to... he can damn near hear the man salivating. 'Well I know you don't know how to get up with and all that, but I'll tell you what. If you know someone who does let 'em know I'd really appreciate the help.' Train slaps down one of the few he fives hi has sandwiched inside the mass of $100's, $20's and $10's. He grabs his beer and turns to walk away... 'Hold on mon... Maybe I can help you out.' Train casually turns back and takes a seat on the nearest stool as the big man walks to the far end of the dark wooden bar. He picks up the phone at the end and presses two numbers...which of course means that he was full of shit and Spliff is in the building. Just the way Train figured. The country might change, but the game remains the same. It doesn't take long. a 30 second conversation that involved a lot of nodding on the part of the big man. Finally he returns to Train as he sips his beer, watching the soccer game on the television above the bar as if he gave a shit. Cool as a cucumber. 'If you're looking for Spliff I think I can point you in the right direction.' the bartnder says just as casually, pretending to simply be doing his civic duty as opposed to looking for his own appointment with one of the Benjamin Franklin's in Train's pocket. Train is happy to respond in the man's language, laying another hundred dollar bill on the bar top that the big man quickly snaps up. 'Down the hall to the left...second door on the right.' 'Much appreciated.' Train grabs his beer and strolls to the far end of the bar and through the doorway right in front of him. He sips his beer the whole way down the hall. Before he knows it he's standing in front of the door he was directed to. Taking yet another sip, attempting not to waste what he's already paid for, Train knocks on the door. 'Come in.' says the voice inside Train turns the hand and steps into the room like he owns the place. But, more likely the scruffy dreadlocked man sitting behind the deshovled desk is the true owner. the two large, muscular bald men flanking either side of the desk are there to remind visitors of such. 'Close the door my friend.' Spliff says, toking on his cigar. Train takes another sip of his beer as pushes the door closed with his foot. His eyes never leave the men in front of him. The game is always the same. 'So you're the Choo-Choo man, huh?' 'That's one way of putting it.' 'You come with high praise from low places my friend.' 'I was just about to say the same about you.' 'Mmmhmm.............. Well before we get started I need to make sure there are no uninvited visitors to our conversation. You don't mind, right?' Train just sips his beer before holding his arms out. The big baldy on the left comes around the desk and begins the pat down. Train just stands cool and calm, knowing not to show any fear...because the game is always the same. 'He's clean.' declares the big man, returning to his perch behind the desk. 'So........ Mr. Train. I hear you need a gun? You've only been in our fine country for five minutes mon? Who you hate that bad?' 'Don't hate nobody man. Well...fuck all that, I hate alot of folks, but I ain't trying to make a body...just an example.' Spliff just look at Train for a moment, trying to determine where he's coming from. Finally Spliff smiles. He reaches into the top drawer of his desk and retrieves a solid black .45 and a full clip. Train steps up to the desk and fines one of the few open spaces to put his beer before going into his pocket and retrieving his stash. He counts out $500 in hundreds and drops it on top of a tall stack of file folders. Train reaches out to retrieve the pistol and it's clip, but Spliff's hand shoots out and covers them both. 'I don't guess I need to tell you that if you get caught with these...you and me? We've never met.' 'I'm sorry, do I know you?' 'Riiiiiiiiight....' Spliff says with a grin. He quickly trades his grip on the gun and trades it for a grip on the cash. Train does the opposite, grabbing the gun and sliding the clip into place before tucking it behind his back, lifting his shirt to cover it. Finally he grabs his beer and takes another sip. 'Good doing business with you dogg.' Train says before turning and opening the door to the office. But, before he can step out Spliff's voice beckons him back. 'Hey mon... I'm not one to get in other people's business, but just out of curiousity.... Who you trying to make an impression on?' 'Pen.' 'Pen? Who's Pen?' 'Pen's a spatula.' 'A spatula? You're going to pull a gun on a spatula?' 'No...I'm going to shoot Pen. That's gonna leave an impression on someone else.' 'Wait, wait, wait... You're going to shoot a spatula?' 'Yup.' 'Why?' 'He talks too fucking much.' Train lifts his eyebrows as he sips his beer and slips around the coner and out of sight.
