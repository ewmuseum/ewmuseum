Adding to the Confusion

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/14/05 ~A Week Ago~ ~Right After Dani's Match~ ~Last Evolution~ Danielle could tell she would feel it in the morning. She would be stiff and sore. Her face hurt from the impact of mat. She had given it her all, and it still wasn't good enough. She was starting to wonder what would it take for her to win. How many matches was it now? With only one win since coming to Global? This time she was out powered and out wrestled... but not by those in the match. But by herself. Once again. she allowed her mind to be effected by something outside what was in that ring. Her teacher would have her doing many hours of extra work out. Just to make up for the mistakes she made in that ring. She had a feeling that Gray wouldn't be so kind, after all he was there he saw the match and saw her mistakes. She knew he would have her in the gym working out for the next 5 days. But then again she also knew that she had a meeting with someone that even Gray didn't stand up against, but this person wasn't the lady herself. She was something different, something.. one would almost call evil or twisted, but beautiful She had heard stories about this woman, and her exploits in other places, she was sent when someone needed testing, and she held nothing back. Some say that what she does almost cost her sanity at some point. But then again, rumors fly at school and they get bigger and bigger and more twisted, as they change from something simple to the fantastic. Danielle knew that this week was important in anyway shape or form. She knew that she had to get home and rest up just so she could meet with this Lady later on. She some how knew that her life was changing for better or worse. She just hoped that Josh understood why she had to keep from being with him. Not now not until .......... A knock on her door broke her train of thought.... It also brought along a standard response to that same knock, ?What do you want, Gray?? Danielle crinkled her nose as she walked towards her room door, pulling the big bath robe around her. She knew she looked like a black and blue wonder with all the bruises, the robe would keep them from being seen. She could feel herself moving slower then normal as the stiffness started to set in from the sore muscles. She opened the door, and to her surprise there stood both Gray, and Josh. Gray, looking as stoic as ever addressed her, “You know I'm a valet and a trainer. I'm not a nanny, it isn't my job to collect or your stuff and bring it to you. We waited in the locker room for you. Then was told that you drove off in a hurry.” Danielle moves to let the two guys into her room, her eyes do not leave Josh, as he enters but doesn't speak. His body language says it all, he is in love with her. As much as she would love to give, she knows she can't. “Yeah, well it was a rough match. I needed a good hot bath, and wasn't going to get that in the locker room showers.” As Danielle turns around the deep black bruises on her legs show from under her robe. Josh's eyes affix on it and Danielle tries to cover it up fast with both her robe and her words, “They're nothing new. I know you have had a few also, Joshua, so before you start making a fuss over it just stop.” His only retort is, “At least when I am in the ring, I am not out powered three to one. That means they'll be harder to get rid of, and they'll hurt much more.” Danielle turns and looks at him, “It is my job you know, my life. I will not give it up for anything.” She starts to walk towards her small efficiency style kitchen as Josh says a response, under his breath, “Or anyone...” Josh walks past her and goes right for the small dorm style refrigerator, He gets out a beer for him, and brings Danielle one of her favorite wine coolers. Danielle takes it with out a word of thanks to him. As she does he touches his fingers to her hand, with a pleading look in this eyes..... Pleading for an answer to what is happening... Danielle just looks away and turns to speak to Gray. Before she can open her mouth to say something Gray speaks up, “You know what I don't care what is up your ass right now, but I do care that you are not focusing on matches. And that your risking your safety and the safety of the others in that ring because of it. You have a work out tomorrow morning at six A.M. sharp, I suggest both of you be ready for a hot and sweaty work out.” Gray turns to walk out grabbing his younger brother by the arm. “So I would suggest a good nights sleep and be ready you don't want me to have to come in here and wake your ass up.” Dani tries to say something, “I've got a meeting for a photo shoot at eight...” “It's canceled,” is all she gets back from Gray. At that Gray walks out and leaves Danielle standing there. with a wine cooler in hand, and a slightly surprised look on her face. She hadn't seen this side of him since school, and he was a nightmare of a teacher back then. She could just imagine how he would be tomorrow to her considering what happen between her and his baby brother. Danielle sighed, she knew what had happened and she was torn in two that it did. She sat down on the floor in front of the coffee table. Reaching under the table, onto it's shelf, she pulls out her leather bound personal journal and the implements she uses to write in it. She sits there, staring at it. She sits there some more. Danielle sits back and sighs. she should be happy. But she wasn't. She couldn't win a match to save her life right now, and she was loosing at so many other things too. She places her pen down and gets up and pulls open her dresser's second drawer down. She doesn't have to rummage long, as what she was looking for was right on top of the other clothes there. A shirt that's not hers, but she's worn before. Especially after waking up the next morning, naked and entwined with him on the couch. She holds it up to her face and takes a deep breath, inhaling the scent of him and his cologne. Her eyes start to tear, unconsciously and unknown to her. Almost on impulse, she walks over and places it on her pillow. She drops the bath robe and then lays her head down and closes her eyes, hoping for sweet dreams, but with tears rolling down her face. Sleep would not come well, nor would it be restful. ~The Next Morning~ Six in the morning rolls around too fast if you ask anyone who has to get up after a hard night on the job. Danielle wasn't ready to be out of bed yet, but she knew if she didn't Gray would be there and that is one thing she didn't want. The last time she slept in on a work out, a few years back while they were at the school, he dumped her into a bath tub filled with ice and water to wake her up. She didn't want to tempt fate again on that one, again. She was up, and dressed in a pair of biker style shorts and a tank top already. Although she had her other clothes in a shoulder bag to carry, she did have other things to do.. she would humor Gray with his early morning punishments for the time being. When Josh and Gray showed up, Josh looked like he hadn't slept all night, he carried two coffees one for him and the other for Danielle of course. Gray didn't say much they just followed him with out word or question. It was almost like being at school again. They quickly got into a car and headed to the place they were going to be using, a silent trip as it was. It was an old building, advertising that it was used for boxing and wrestling up on Charleston Ave, one of the major East-West through ways, about half a mile off the strip, near the Stratosphere. The blue and red painted mural on a white stucco side, tried to make it look like a big time patriotic boxing place, and the age of the design, very apparent, showed it to have been the same thing since the 60's or something. It had been here for a while. They walked in, and they were all alone. Dani headed to one locker room to put her stuff up and Josh and Gray to another. It wasn't much longer when all three were back in the gym. It was full of older machinery for working out, and a small ring with loose ropes. Gray started off, as he felt the tension already building up, “Alright you two, work out begins now: Complete, working every group. First Josh then Danielle. Dani your going to be his spotter and Josh your going to be hers.” Both of the two younger ones just looked at each other after hearing his words. Josh is the one to break the silence, ?And where are you going?? Gray answers him, “I have phone calls to make and a meeting to set up for tonight. But don't you worry little brother, I wont be too far away. I'll still be able to hear you two.” Gray turns and walks out of the room .... Danielle adds, ?God, what is he up to?? to which, she only gets “Who the hell knows,” as a response. The two meandered their way over to the aged weight equipment. Most of it was of the free weight kind. As instructed, Josh stated out. Dani as his spotter meant that she was at a disadvantage with Josh's bigger and stronger frame. She would need to be quicker if there was a problem. Fortunately for her, she knew that Josh was an endurance worker, he wasn't as big as his brother, and thus didn't go for the power workout. Smaller weights and more repetitions for him. She watched as he got stretched out first. Josh had a martial arts background, similar to hers. He was very good at it, and they both used it to keep their flexibility and their more aerial side to their fighting. As she also worked on getting loosened up for her workout, stretching as he did, and also keeping her legs moving as she spotted him, she couldn't help but keep chasing her mind around, as it tried to fill her with other thoughts. Between the smell of him, and other distractions, she wasn't a really good spotter, that's for sure. Josh's muscles didn't help with those thoughts either: his legs, chest, shoulders... Dani shook her head a few times while she helped him do his workout. Her turn came a while later, and basically while her time to get warmed up was spent while he was doing his stuff, his time to cool off had come. She looked at the butterfly machine, and then felt Josh's eyes on her. She decided that wouldn't be the best thing to do right now. She settled on starting with the leg press machine, it'd do it's damage and she could rest from it while she did the other work. She thought people who worked their legs last were bafoons, since they had to walk afterwards, didn't they? She started using it, and started her workout, but didn't notice too much just how much she was doing. She knew her normal routine though. She tried not to, but took quite a few leers in his direction with his shirt stuck to his body. She knew he was looking too, but she could hear, in the back of her head, him counting her repetitions. “One more and your done here,” came before she even realized it should have. Danielle grit her teeth together and pushed the leg lift machine one more time extending her legs to their fullest length with them slowly letting it down. Sweat drenched every part of her body, causing her shirt to stick to her torso. Her body still sore from last night, finally let her know that it was on the verge of rebelling due to the work out she would give it today. As she stood up her foot got stuck on the machine, pulling her slightly off balance as she started to fall Josh reached out and caught her, ending up in almost the same position as if they had finished dancing a number together. The two stared into each other's eyes and then he leaned in close... The kiss came, as she wasn't expecting it. It went for a few moments before she realize that she wasn't protesting, not in the slightest. Continue? Yes or No? Were the thoughts that passed through her head, quickly, rapidly. She was melting and quickly, and this time there was no alcohol to blame for it. Something in the back of her head screamed out to her to stop and she pushed him away, as she got her footing and stood up. She could barely speak as she protested, finally, “JOSH, NO! We can not do this...” She said as she turned and started to walk for the door. Just as she did, Gray stepped out of the gym's office, the owner not a few steps behind him. ?And where do you think your going? Get back to work!? he commanded of her, as he stepped in her way. Danielle looks right at him with out backing down. Tears rolling down her face, she knew she had to get away. Other wise the tension would rise to levels it shouldn't be at. Something was stirring in her, and she didn't feel she could control it. She didn't know what, but it wasn't like anything she had felt before. Boys in school or even the few after it, before she actually chose to become a wrestler, didn't do this. This was something more. But Josh was just a normal person, and he didn't need the heartache that came from all the other crap that he'd have to deal with.. Dani shouted out at Gray, “OUT! Now get out of my way, old man. I'm done for today.” Gray folded his arms across his chest unwilling to be moved, “Who said we are done in here today.” Danielle, on the verge of just screaming incoherent curses at him, tried to shove pass him, as she looked at him her eyes shifted, almost glowing. Josh looked on, taking his shirt off to use it to wipe the sweat from his eyes. As he did, he could hear things around him starting to rattle. ?If you don't move so help me, Gray!? Dani said, sternly at him. Gray obviously saw what was happening, or felt it. He pulled himself up to full height allowed his eyes and teeth to shift, staring directly at Danielle. He gave his own stern warning back to her, ?You will what little girl?? As he asked the question, he reached out and grabbed her by her upper left arm and pulled, maybe a bit too hard. ?Ow!? Dani added as she felt more then just his hand dig into her arm, but she didn't back off, instead she lashed out and slapped the larger man, hard, across his face. She had turned his head with the impact, and the left side of his face turned red, quickly to boot. As he turned his head back to eye her, a low, guttural growl escaped from deep inside of him Dani felt a shiver go down her spine as she realized what she could have just done. She watched as Gray's other hand came back, balled up. She was about to get the punishment that she'd never thought he'd ever use, but she was ready for it. She'd struck him first, while he was in a partial change, showing off as she liked to call it. Gray was unique in his kind. A throw back to a time when more then the Saquamish were in that area of Washington. His ancestors, although many generations back, before even Columbus had landed in the West Indies, were blond haired, blue eyed, and specialized in travel, and fighting. He was a warrior through and through. He was a teacher only of the arts and ways of fighting. She had reacted on impulse and expected no less from him. But it never came. It felt like forever. She could hear him breathing, labored, and heavy. “Mr. Brand, of all the people here, I think ye'd be the one who would know the rules. I'd expect ye to stand down and refrain from your course of action right now. The lady would not look good upon it in her report,” a voice, an English accent came. It was a woman's voice. Dani opened her eyes to see a statuesque woman standing there. A body to die for, her chest smaller then Dani's, but the rest of her was of model material. She was wearing flats and a business type suit. But one thing stood out more then anything. She was holding Gray's balled hand back, with one of her own. Dani shook her head, thinking she was just too tired for a moment as she looked at the blonde haired woman. She thought she smelled the faint scent of the ocean as the woman's hair appeared to go from a nice honey blonde to a sea green color, and then back. Gray's eyes and teeth shifted back to their more normal, human appearance as he realized just who it was who stood next to him. He then bowed his head in a respect that Dani had never seen him give anyone else before. “Miss Kaylee, it is good to see you. I take it your mistress isn't far away then,” Gray asks, as he gives Dani a glare for her impudence and insubordination. “My Mistress is where she wants to be. She figured I needed to come and check in on things myself. I see it was a good thing. I might have just saved your life, Gray Muzzle.” Gray's head went to the side with that statement. “Miss Kaylee, it's good to see you again,” Josh added, to try to break the tension. He'd already met her the night of his birthday, when he'd gone to talk to her Mistress. Kaylee turned to acknowledge him and to respond, “Good to see you too, Young Mr. Brand.” Josh nods and turns around to secure and lock down the last machine used. He was figuring out that the work out was done for the day. As he did, Kaylee's eyes widened and she blurted out, ?Where did you get those wounds?? ?Wounds?? Dani added, as she then turned, wrenching her arm out of Gray's hold, to look at Josh. Across his back were a couple groups of 5 stripes, still red with inflammation. It looked like an animal had rended it's claws across his back, multiple times. Josh looked in Dani's direction suddenly, not saying a word as to just how the marks got there. Kaylee let Gray's hand go, and it just dropped to his side. She walked over and touched Josh's back. “They're smell is distinct. You will need to come see me later to have those looked at. I can assume when they happened, and a week later they should not be looking like that,” She added as she looked at them as a scientist would look at a lab rat. Dani couldn't hold it back. She knew just how those marks had gotten there. She'd not seen them yet, he'd hidden them well. But they didn't look like something her finely trimmed nails could do. Before Gray could stop her, she rushed out of the room. She grabbed her bag from the locker room and headed right out the door. Gray didn't even have time to say anything to her, before she went out. “I'll go after her...” Gray tried to say as he headed for the door. But another voice stopped him dead in his tracks,?Leave 'er be, Mr. Brand. Ye've done enough today in confusin' th' poor girl.? Gray looked almost to sulk, upon hearing those words and the thick Irish accent. He just looked in the direction they came from. Another woman walked out from the side area. Her body was covered with clothing, a dress, stockings, and a scarf tied around her head in a kerchief. She wore sunglasses, but they didn't hide the bit of raven black hair that came out at the natural widow's peak she bore. The woman walked over, her high heeled shoes clanking and clicking on the tile floor. She bent down and touched one of the wounds on Josh's back. “Hmm, they still 'ave th' aroma of met'al in them. Not natural, bu' na' what I'd expected either. I'm beginning ta think tha' the prophets we're listenin' to, 'ave this all wrong. Very, very wrong. There is another smell in 'ere, too, but I can'na quite place it riogh' now. It's na' seeming right though. It's almost like we're...” She trailed off, as she looked at her assistant, the blonde haired woman, Kaylee. ?Almost as if we're looking at the wrong realm, Mistress?? Kaylee added, serving her purpose. The dark clothed woman just looked in her direction and nodded. Kaylee then moved to grab Josh's shirt and hand it back to him. “Kaylee, take young Mr. Brand back t' me suite, an' get one o' the others t' look at 'im. Make sure they get it all,” She said, in a defiant, and yet commanding voice. Josh could tell that her sunglass covered eyes were looking in Gray's direction. Kaylee grabbed Josh by his arm after he got his shirt on. The other lady started heading out the door. “Kaylee, also, pay th' owner 'ere fer the damages, if ye will.” “Yes, Mistress,” She added. ?Pay her?? Josh asked. Then he heard it from the side of the room, Gray had disappeared and he heard the loud crash of equipment being pushed over, “Oh.” Josh watched as the other lady walked out, and Kaylee pulled her purse up to pull out some money for the owner of the gym. ~Later~ Dani had found a restroom in the Jack in the Box down the road to change out of her work out clothes and into the set she had taken with her. She called Kris and canceled everything else for the day. She wasn't in the mood to look good and talk smoothly to potential sponsorship contract mongers and their ilk. A small mini skirt and handkerchief type top made her look almost normal for the hot summer here in Vegas. First thing she did was head to a store and actually buy some sunglasses for herself. It wasn't even a normal set she bought either. One of those new sets from Oakley that had a built in MP3 player too. She didn't even know why she spent the money on them, it just felt good at the time to do it. She ate at the fast food place, feeling like she gained about 5 lbs while she did. She's just have to take care of herself a bit more over the rest of the week. She then spent a few hours wondering down the strip, slowly heading back to her room at the hotel. That is until she decided to head down Flamingo, towards the mall. She figured by now, Gray would be back and the hotel, and it wasn't a place she wanted to be at. Walking the whole way wasn't fun, but she was glad she was in her running shoes still. It took a while and the sun was beginning to retreat and give some recourse to the heat of the day when she made it to an area that she didn't recognize much. That's when she realized that she needed a place to stop and sit or wind down a bit. She heard the music coming from one of the places over in a strip mall and the line of people outside. The sign above it said it was the “City Lights Bistro” but she could see that the crowd in front was anything but the typical 'bistro' crowd. Then again, this was Las Vegas, not Seattle, where Bistro meant coffee and bagels during the day. She started to take a second guess, when a man walked up to her, ?You look like you're lost, can I help you?? She looked down at him, as he was slightly hunched over in his posture. “No, just wondering if I should take a little rest in there. Maybe get a drink or two,” She replied as a man dressed in a mesh shirt and leather pants walked by with a similarly dressed woman heading right up to the bistro. “Well, tonight in there, is a special night. They hold an event called 'Sacrilege?. It's rather entertaining. Maybe you should go ahead and take a look around. Have a drink or two. In fact, walk up to the doorman and give him this,” The guy handed her what looked like a coin, with an odd marking on it, “It'll let you bypass the line and they'll take you to a good seat.” “I can't take this. It's yours,” Dani pleaded. “No, no, I've got more. It's not for me anyway. It's for you,” He added as he started to step away slowly. ?What?? is all she could say. She looked at the coin and then back up at the guy, but he wasn't there. ?What the hell?? She felt compelled though. She heard a few snicker at her, as she walked by the fetishwear clad people and walked right up to the doorman and handed him the coin. The large man, burly and down right ugly smiled a partial toothed smile at her. “We've been expecting you, Ms. Northsky.” ?What?? She stood there, slack jawed as the man signaled for another, almost a twin, to come up and offer to escort her to her seat. She found that she was compelled, almost as if against her will to follow him. He took her up a set of stairs to the balcony above the dance floor and pulled out a chair for her. ?What will Ms. Northsky be drinking?? the man asked. “Uhm...” Dani stammered, trying to figure out what she wanted as things were going weird again. “We'll be takin' two Pale Horse Ciders, an' a lot o' quiet time there.” Said a thick accented woman as she walked up. The woman was beautiful, with long black hair and pale skin. Her lips were painted a shade of red that resembled blood. Her eyes were a deep color, almost purple. She was almost unearthly. The lady took a seat across the table from Dani and looked at her. “I see ye know jus' 'ow t' make it t' a meetin' on time,” the woman added. ?Meeting?? Dani was still slack jawed and almost couldn't speak. “Yes, our meetin' fer t'nigh'. Ye made it riogh' on time. Ms. Northsky, me name is Aine Leanan. Bu' ye can call me Annie. We 'ave a lot o' things t' talk abou', ye an' I. I'd suggest getting confortable.” Dani shook her head, and tried to weigh in everything else. She couldn't believe it, this was the Mistress, the Lady that she was supposed to be meeting, arranged by Gray. But she had walked here on her own. “Don'na make yer 'ead 'urt t' much. At least na' this early,” The woman who had said to call her Annie spoke to her as two bottles were dropped off at the table. Dani could only say a few words, “Okay, I'll let you start. I'm still trying to figure this out.” “Yer na' th' only one, me dear. Yer na' th' only one. Bu' let's do this riogh'. A toast, t' figurin' things ou'.” The woman raised her bottle, and Dani followed suit. As they tapped, Dani took a huge swing from hers, more like 3 gulps, almost downing the sweet tasting beverage fully. She figured she'd need it by the end of the night. Ms. Leanan smiled at her and sipped from her bottle a bit before she started. ~End~
