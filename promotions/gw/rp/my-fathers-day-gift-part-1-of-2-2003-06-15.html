My fathers day gift...Part 1 of 2

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/15/03 To some people holidays are the end all and be all of family functions. For Sean Price, holidays are nothing more than regular days and family functions are no more than a distant memory. It has been well documented that Sean was never the favorite son of Lisa and Mathew Price, that honor was bestowed upon Sean’s younger brother Luke. As we all know many months ago Sean and Luke entered the GW together, as a tag team, wrestling under the banner of New Found Fame but after a few months of disappointment Sean had, had enough. He was frustrated and angry at his brother’s lack of effort and consequently their inability to win the tag team titles at a pay per view match involving then champions Shawn Corrick and J.J Dynasty. So when the opportunity came for Sean to move on from his brother and New Found Fame, Sean jumped at the opportunity and in doing so added to the demise of his brother’s short lived wrestling career. From that point on Sean became known in wrestling circles around the world as being one of the best in the business, while his brother lay at home with broken bones and migraine headaches. Than in late march Sean was given a letter from his mother saying that his brother Luke was pressing charges against Sean for “wages lost” due to Sean’s actions in the ring that lead Luke to be hospitalized and bed ridden for months. Sean tried to meet with his parents and brother in an attempt to solve this issue outside of court and try to patch together what little family feeling there was between Sean and the rest of his family but Luke and their father Matt would have none of that. Sean’s mother was the only person who stuck true to her son Sean and instead of taking sides remained neutral throughout the court proceedings. The end result of all of this was that Sean was out of the GW for two months and owed his brother a total of 1.7 million dollars in damages. Although Sean found this ruling to be a crock of shit he did pay it for the sake of the family. Sean has more money than he really knows what to do with. For him 1.7 million was pocket change, for Luke it was more than he would make in his entire life. It is now almost two months since the day the settlement was reached in court. Today is father’s day. Will it be a day in which Sean and his family put the past behind them and move on, or will today just be another reminder of how Sean is not only the family outcast but in his father’s eyes the family mistake. As the scene fades in we see Sean sitting in the back seat of one of the TUE limos watching the little television and drinking some Corona. The partition between the driver of the limo and Sean lowers as the limo simultaneously comes to a stop. The driver turns looks up in the rear view mirror at Sean and remarks that they have reached their destination. As the sentence concludes the back door closest to Sean opens up and Sean slowly gets out. He steps away from the limo and than stretches out for a second before walking upto the front door. He knocks on the door twice and waits a moment. The door opens and standing on the other side of it is Sean’s mother. A big smile comes across her face as she reaches out to give Sean a hug, Sean moves forward to accept his mothers show of love. Once inside Sean takes off his coat and wraps it around his arm. He follows his mothers lead into the family room. When he clears the hallway and enters the family room he sees his father and brother sitting in reclining chairs watching baseball on the television. Sean looks at both men without them noticing that he has entered the room. When they finally both Luke and their father jump to their feet in what appears to be surprise and disgust.) “What the hell is he doing here mom?” questions an angry Luke Price “Calm down Luke. I invited him, I thought it was time that we all sat down as a family and buried the hatchet once and for all. Today is father’s day, he is your son Matthew and I thought he deserved to be here as much as Luke did.” Replies Sean’s mother “Deserves to be here” After what he did to me, after what he did to our family? He doesn’t deserve to be anywhere but 6 feet under.” Sneers Luke “Now you watch what you say Luke, you may not be happy with your brother but he is exactly that’your brother. He always will be whether you like it or not. So you might as well put aside your petty differences and just accept things for what they are.” Quickly replies Mrs. Price “Do you think being bed ridden for two months is something I can easily put behind me?” remarks Luke “No but I bet that 1.7 million dollar check that I wrote you will help ease the pain won’t it dear brother.” Exclaims Sean (Both Sean and Luke give each other vengeful looks and then move one step closer towards each other before their mother pushes them both backwards a step.) “Listen fella’s this is your father’s day, it is a special day for him. Sean I invited you here in hopes that you could all put the past behind you, not to continue your family feud with your brother and your father. So please do me a favor and try to work things out.” Pleads Mrs. Price “I am more than happy to work things out mom. Why else do you think I came” Certainly not to hear Luke cry and complain, I came here because regardless of how the last few months have gone today is father’s day and today is the day in which I should be thanking my father for everything he did for me and for us as a family. So thank you dad, and happy father’s day.” (With that Sean’s father snickers, than turns around and walks into the other room, Luke follows suit leaving Sean and his mother alone in the family room.) “Don’t worry Sean, I know your father is appreciative for what you just said. It is just going to take some time that’s all.” “Time is not always on my side mother.” (The scene fades out as Sean and his mother continue to talk amongst themselves in the family room.)
