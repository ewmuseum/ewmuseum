The Melting Point Of Wax

13 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.13.05 09.13.05 | Unknown Location | 3:57 AM Light. Darkness. Light. Darkness. Michael's eyes flickered open and shut in a rythmless trance whilst he fought to retain whatever consciousness he had left. His fists clenched as they rest at his sides, body shaking from the occasional bump that the gurney took along the clean, tiled hospital floor. 'Stay with us,' a voice pleaded, although even with Michael's attempts to spot a familiar face watching him, he saw no one but strangersas they pushed him through the antiseptic halls. It was almost as though things were in black and white; the lights above Michael's face provided the white, and the only color to his vision came from the droplets of blood that lingered on his lazy eyelids that occasionally pattered alongside his crimson cheeks. Light. Darkness. Light. Darkness. Michael's lips moved as though he were trying to speak, but nothing came out. Inside his right fist, two of his fingers unknowingly played with an item concealed in his hand. One of the people wheeling him through the hospital took notice. 'He has something in his hand...' with the sole intention of wanting to help, the man took one hand from the gurney to try and pry the object from Michael's hand, but the wrestler's grip remained strong as iron even in his weakened state. That sole gesture sparked something in Michael; this wasn't the place he wanted to be. Frantically, he began to try and sit himself up. He could remember nothing; how he had gone from being in a wrestling ring to being bloodied up and dragged to an emergency room. That was not good, nor was it good for the people trying to keep Michael from wasting whatever strength he had left for the sake of his own worry. With all the blood loss Michael was sustaining, he certainly wasn't the only one worrying. 'Please, Michael,' they all seemed to say in unison, but with Michael's dodgy perception on what was going on, he couldn't be so sure. 'We're trying as best as we can to help, but you have to relax. Please!' A pair of hands firmly grabbed Mercer's somewhat broad shoulders and set him down onto the uncomfortable metal. The abrupt snap of Michael's back hitting the support underneath him caused the object in Michael's hand to wriggle loose from his grip. It fell from his splayed bloody fingers and rolled slowly from the edge of the gurney... and to the floor. ....clang... 'NO!' Michael shot up again, this time a crazed look in his eye that made one of the creeped out men jump back at the unexpected jolt. Michael sat upright with little hesitation, arms outstretched and aimed toward the ground at the object rolling away from him. Another one of the men looked at Michael's face; the blood-soaked bandage that dangled from his forehead, the rivulets of red that trickled down his face and matted his bangs to the sides of his face, the black eyes that stared in widened... what? Fear? Shock? Misery? There was no word that could accurately describe or provide justice for the horror captured on Michael's face. 'Jesus, those eyes..' one of the EMT's mouthed to himself, 'that's a stare that could kill...' Oh, he had no idea. 'Let me go!' Michael screamed, and the order did nothing to relieve the fear everyone was being injected with from Michael's off the wall burst of adrenaline. Why? Because no one could let him go, because no one was holding onto him in the first place. His muscles were strained, his limbs were tired, and his awareness was well out the window. Did Jake Hix really do all of that? Still, Michael attempted to throw himself from the metal table and onto the floor in pursuit of his possession, but the EMTs were lucky to get him into an unoccupied room, allowing the real doctors to take over from there. Never did Michael see anyone run away from him so fast as those men did from him just then, but it wasn't as though he was paying much attention to that, either. All that mattered was what Michael had left outside. 'I need to get out of here,' Michael said desperately; words weren't going to work on these people. Nothing Michael could say would convince those doctors that he should have been allowed to walk out of that hospital at four in the morning in the middle of Seattle. Who knew how many other people he would scare if he were let loose? 'Mister Mercer, you need to calm down..' But Michael did not want to calm down. That night was important to him, for more reasons than one, and those reasons transcended the meaning of wrestling. As pissed as Michael was at himself for taking such a hard loss at Whiplash, he would never put himself through so much pain over it. What he was so upset about, however... it was something that no one had known about, or could have guessed. People understood that Michael's wife was missing, but the press never interpreted it as something deadly, but more of a simple fact that Jaden had taken their child and left Michael, to simply never be heard from again. This, however, was much different. And Michael cared not for how much longer it took before people began to realize... but for how much longer it was before Michael was allowed to enjoy his life again. Because at this point, Michael's career was not in wrestling- Michael's career was in torturing himself. And at that moment, a deep exhale escaped Michael's lungs and his eyes seemed to glaze over. He remained upright for maybe a second longer, before that breath Michael took was gone, and Michael's body limply fell back onto the gurney. Light. Darkness. Light. Darkness.... darkness... darkness. That was the chance the doctors needed to move Michael from that uncomfortable metal and unto a slightly less taxing bed next to him. It took three of them to move all of Michael's two hundred and twenty plus pounds of muscle, but they managed to do it successfully. They were quick to remove the torn up bandage from Michael's face, revealing the true severity of the cut; it was no longer just on his forehead, but down the side of his face, about halfway down his cheek. Ouch. As they tried urgently to fix Michael's horrid face, the door to the room creaked open and one of the EMTs from before had slipped in; not to speak a word, but to place a certain object on a nearby counter. It wasn't until after that man had left that anyone sensed what had happened, and when one of the doctors turned to see what was left for them, he tilted his head in confusion. 'Does this belong to him?' asked someone under their surgical mask, picking up the small, round object. Through the caked blood, a glimmer on the item still shone in defiance. 'This is a deep laceration,' another doctor stated, 'patient's suffered severe head trauma, more than likely by repeated blows by a blunt object.. we're gonna have to work quick, here.' 'God...' another one grumbled; the worried look in his eyes and shaky voice was a blatant sign that he wasn't so used to such a sight. 'Who would do something like this to a man?' 09.13.05 | Key Arena, Seattle | 1:14 AM Michael placed the large bandage on his forehead, and groaned, looking at his own beat up reflection in the mirror. 'God, not again.' The look was something Michael could not bear. Why- no, how- how could he lose? This could not possibly be what Michael had expected from himself after all this time. He had a reason to exist, at least he swore he did. He had heart, passion.. a fire he refused to let burn out, no matter what bit of bad fortune came his way. Just why... of all the times... did he need to lose again- when it meant the most, no less? 'I'm sorry,' he whispered miserably, leaning forward to place his then-sweat-drenched head against the mirror in defeat, one hand on each side of the glass to support himself. 'I'm so, so sorry. This... this isn't me.' Blood laced the knuckles in his right hand, and much more of it immediately soaked into the bandage he wore so bashfully. The reflection that remained dejected with him was him, all right. Whether his spirit was the same or not, however, was a different matter. Michael had battled through everything; the torment of being single while having to worry about a troublesome wrestling career in SAWF. Jaden James was a heartbeat away, and she always was, but she never kept herself available for Michael to touch. Michael had fought an odd seventy-or-so matches, and every one of them held back nothing in terms of the fury and sadness in Michael's heart. And then that final confrontation... ...it had all paid off. It was over. Jaden could settle down with Michael finally, and the storybook was looking to close with a happy ending. However, someone- or some sick, psychopathic, and soon to be dead motherfucker, as Michael liked to put it- had taken all that good away from him. Would he see them again? Reality punched him in the face with the answer that night. Knock knock. 'Leave me alone,' Michael grumbled, his hulking frame still pressed wearily against the mirror. Michael's body did not budge one bit when the knocking continued. 'God dammit,' Michael roared, 'piss off.' Instead of 'pissing off', whoever was on the other side of the door had decided to make themselves welcome; quite possible the most moronic mistake anyone could ever make... at that time, anyway. 'Hmph, not going to invite me in? Where are the manners?' The accent rang a firm bell, and like a lion, Michael shot around to face T'sao Liang. Suddenly, Michael wasn't so tired. 'You again...' 'Who else would you expect? Who else cares about you, Michael, really?' Michael shot back quickly. 'At this point, I don't give a shit who cares about me. Makes no difference about the fact that you have done more or less nothing except invite yourself into my private life, rather than bother to help me retain it.' Michael pointed an index finger at Liang, shaking it emphatically with each scornful word that left his mouth. Kongde, however, only laughed at the trepidation. 'I don't know what you're talking about... if anything, you've been the one who has done nothing to help me. I have done all I could; I even helped you find your 'mommy and daddy'.' Liang added a sarcastic edge to those last couple of words, causing a spark to begin in the pit of Michael's stomach- something he thought had died during his match with Hix not too long ago. 'Either way,' T'sao continued, 'I came to my senses and realized that I can only help someone so much to the point where they can only help themselves. So, dear Michael, I simply came to say goodbye.' Michael could only stare with his jaw dropped practically to the floor at the sound of what he had heard. Was this guy really serious? Michael tried to find the words to describe his utter annoyance, while Liang pulled an object from his pocket and began playing with it; he threw it into the air with his right hand and caught it with his left, then transferred the item back to his right hand again. Michael didn't pay no mind to that, at first; granted, he was stil debating to himself whether to not to kick the shit out of the poor fuck for not only bothering him at the time, but for jerking him around for so long. However, slowly it became clear as day that T'sao didn't pull the object out to just play with it out of boredom. He wanted Michael to see it, and the sick grin that played out on his face had more than proven that. 'What is that...' Michael's eyes squinted to see, but his vision was already shot; seeing stars and the like just fine, once again, thanks to his aforementioned match. Still, he fought to see what it was. One by one, he picked out the look of the object. Small. Shiny. Round. Golden. The puzzle pieced itself together for him just had been thrown in his direction, to which he caught it out of reflex. A ring. But to add on to that, although its sparkle had been much better preserved, it did nothing to hinder the fact that it was otherwise identical to the ring on Michael's right ring finger. Just when Michael didn't think it possible for his jaw to drop any further, it did. 'So long, Michael.' Normally, a goodbye would have consisted of one person walking away from the other. To T'sao, it meant no more than a word he spoke before he killed someone. Out went the pistol. The ring clutched in Michael's hand fell to the ground, and the sound of it touching the floor by Michael's feet did nothing to drown out the BANG! that came next. Time seemed to crawl as Michael used the last bit of strength in his legs to dive for cover. The bullet whizzed just over Michael's torso and shattered the mirror that Mercer had been brooding in front of just moments before, raining tiny fragments of glass over Michael's already battered body. 'Urgh...' Michael growled in pain as his arms pushed him up off the floor. Adrenaline never paid off so much in Michael's life; not in the most meaningful match of the twentyfive year old's career. Michael shoved himself to his feet and lunged as hard as he could for Liang, and for the gun he held. Kongde was so taken back from the shock of Michael ducking out of the way, that he had no chance to line up a second shot before Michael got his hands on the weapon. Placing both hands tightly around the barrel, Michael tilted his head to the side and pushed upwards. Another shot was fired. If it weren't for the fact that most others had already left the arena, both men would more than likely have been fighting off police forces as well as eachother. And even if that were the case, Michael and T'sao were would have both been content as long as one of them died. Michael began to wriggle the gun from Liang's grip, and in a last ditch effort to keep control, Liang slammed the butt of the pistol into Michael's head. The angle of the blow ripped the bandage on Michael's head and worsened the cut underneath it, causing Michael to stumble backwards. In his worry, Liang opted to pistol whip Michael over the cut again, and again, until the cut was no longer just on Michael's forehead. Before long, blood was over everything. T'sao didn't seem to care that his hands were bathed in someone else's lifeline; over the time he had been killing people for a living, one would figure that he derived pleasure from it by now. Liang watched as Michael's body crumbled to the floor. It was over. All of that defeatism attitude Michael was forced to take in over the past few weeks had just culminated in him laying in a bloody heap in his own locker room, and in a few moments, he may not have been able to realize it any longer. 'Dirty has been.' T'sao squatted down, looking into Michael's glazed over eyes closely. Smiling, he knew what had to happen now. But before he could even think of trying to pull the trigger to his weapon, Michael's right hand shot up and latched around Kongde's throat. Sitting up, as though back from the dead like a zombie in a Romero movie, Michael's eyes weren't just glazed, but they were bloodshot. It was obvious he wasn't all there, but really... After yanking the gun from Liang and literally breaking it over the man's head, did that really matter? Michael stood and looked down at what he had just caused. The bloodshed in his match with Jake had- obviously- paled in comparison to Michael breaking a gun into thousands of pieces over someone's skull. It felt shocking. It felt surreal. It felt good. It felt very good... although it wasn't for certain if Liang held those same sentiments. Of course, however, one could make an educated guess as to how the Chinese man felt to being KO'd in such a destructive manner. It was then also, that Michael realized that the tables had turned. Now, Michael calmly moved over to the shards of glass, strewn about from the bullet that broke the mirror in its explosiveness. Picking up a nicely formed blade of glass, Michael stalked back to Liang's unmoving body, watching carefully. He was still breathing, and more than likely going to wake up in a half hour or so with a hell of a headache... unless Michael did something. '...No.' Slowly, the glass shard fell from Michael's hand and to the floor. 'I'm not like you. I'm no killer.' And suddenly, Michael dropped to his hands and knees, scraping along the floor for something. There, one of his bloodied hands picked up the ring that was discarded. And there, he walked. Where he was going, he did not know, but he walked and did so hurriedly; if anyone had seen what he had caused in his locker room with him still nearby, he'd definitely be facing some kind of charges. However, as fast as Michael went, the only thing he could say about what had happened was... 'I beat you...' sure, he did beat T'sao. Beat him into the ground, at that. But still... his wife... 'Oh, God...' Michael collapsed in the middle of nowhere. Where it was didn't exactly matter, because before long, it had once again become nothing more to Michael than: Light. Darkness. Light. Darkness.
