Furious (SiT RP 1)

27 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/04 Brick was furious. Once again, the security of his best friend Aaron Davis and Aaron's family had been compromised, this time by the very same people he hired to protect him. The guards who were supposed to be guarding the Davis home had instead allowed Salaok free access. Salaok was then able to steal the gun Aaron had for protection. The night of the 15th, Salaok decided to gloat to Brick about what he had done: 'Brick, you can try to protect your family and friends as much as you want, but no matter what you or Aaron do, I still will do what I want, when I want. I didn’t go there to hurt Aaron this time, as I simply went to pay the house a visit and prove to you that I can get past any blockage you try to set for me. I am The Harvester of Minds, Brick. And I will continue to fuck with yours, as you are nothing more than a little toy for my amusement.' When Brick attempted to rush down the ramp to attack Salaok and Derek Frost, the very same four bodyguards restrained him. All Brick could do was struggle and rage like a newly caged wild tiger. He was tired of the games. He was tired of Salaok screwing around with his life and the lives of those around him. One way or another, he had to deal with Salaok, once and for all. ---- Days had passed and Brick's mood wasn't any more improved. He had petitioned to have restraining orders placed on Salaok, so that if he came within 50 feet of Brick's family or the Davises, he would be arrested. Meanwhile, Brick had a private security firm to rake across the coals. 'No, I will not calm down!' Brick roared into the telephone, as he paced around his living room. 'I paid y'all to protect my friends and my family! You violated our agreement and you endangered the people you were supposed to protect! No, I don't wanna hear that shit! My friend has a wife and a one-year-old daughter! Never mind why he had a firearm in his house! That was supposed to be in case your people failed, which they did as I see it! You motherfuckers are gonna hear from my lawyers! Yeah, that's right, my lawyers!! I'm suing for breach of contract and unprofessional conduct! I'm also reportin' y'all to the Better Business Bureau! Oh, that's right! Before I'm done, y'all motherfuckers won't get jobs workin' security detail for little kids' birthday parties! You need to be thankful I don't press criminal charges for aiding and abetting! And for future reference, don't expect my business or the business of anybody I know!' Brick slammed the phone down so hard, he nearly cracked the plastic. Taking a deep breath, he picked up the phone and dialed a number. 'Yeah, Ma? I'm gonna be a little late to dinner tonight. Yeah, I gotta go a few places, first.' ---- Out in Brick's personal shooting range, cut-out after cut-out of the Harvester of Minds was perforated with gunfire. This wasn't the first time Brick had vented his anger with cardboard cut-outs and firearms. Definitely not the first time Salaok had inspired such activity. Suddenly, Brick could hear a car pulling up. Out of the driver's seat stepped Aaron. 'Let me guess, you figured I'd be out here.' 'Give me a gun.' 'What?' 'I said, give me a gun. Contrary to what Salaok showed, I can hold onto a firearm.' Brick shrugged his shoulders and handed him a sawed-off shotgun. 'Listen, I'm sorry about what happened at your house. You should know I'm suing that security firm.' 'You should. Who knows what Salaok could have done to me or Felicia or Ashley, and those assholes let it happen?!' Aaron exclaimed, firing shots into a Salaok cut-out. 'Salaok's a dead man, Aaron. This I promise you.' 'Not that I'd object to you venting your frustrations with Salaok, but don't you and the rest of Team Brick have a match with Team Jackson at Showdown in Tokyo?' 'Believe me, I haven't forgotten.' Then Brick set up cut-outs of the members of Team Jackson. One was of Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis. One was of Christian Zenith. One was of John Winters. The fourth, strangely enough, was a cut-out of Jim Carrey as the Riddler in Batman Forever. 'I know Jackson's gonna want a piece of me, especially. One, because I beat him last month at Dark Rituals and two, because of Brandi.' 'Well, you're not sleeping with her now, are you? Anyway, I didn't think he was too concerned over Brandi. Isn't he still obsessed with Kitty Mac?' 'Yeah, I've heard that. But it probably won't matter, one way or the other.' 'What's with the Riddler cut-out?' Aaron asked, pointing. 'That's supposed to be Jackson's mystery partner. It could be anyone so I made that one just in case.' 'Any ideas who it might be?' 'I know it could be anyone.' Brick then proceeded to open fire on the cutouts of Team Jackson. First, he hit the Riddler. Then came John Winters. Then came Christian Zenith. Finally came Jackson. 'Guess I'll just have to be ready.' ---- The rain came down in buckets worth of water outside. Inside the cemetery office, Brick stood at the front desk. 'Did you know the deceased?' asked the clerk. 'She was a co-worker of mine,' Brick replied. 'She's in lot 54.' 'Thanks. Which to your bathroom?' 'I'm sorry, sir. The men's room is out of service, right now.' 'Damn. Guess I can hold it.' Brick left the office and then drove down the path. Finally, he reached his destination. He walked across the lawn to the gravesite he wanted to see. There, he saw a headstone marked: LISHA DAVIS WIFE, MOTHER, SISTER 1973-2002 Next to it was a smaller headstone marked: DARRELL DAVIS GONE TOO SOON 2000-2003 Brick knelt down on one knee right in front of the grave. 'Hey, Lisha,' said Brick. 'It's been a while.' He then sighed. 'You know he's fucking with me again, don't you? It's funny, really. Part of me actually thought losing you and Darrell would actually change him. That it'd cause him to look at his life and think. But what the hell was I thinking? He pulled this sick shit when you were in his life. Now that you're gone, he's probably got nothing to lose, except his life. I mean, this motherfucker killed his own father in cold blood. And you...he killed your brother and you still stood by the son of a bitch!! Well, you know what? I just came to tell you that you and Darrell might not be alone for much longer. That's right, Darrell, your Daddy might just be joining you and your Mommy real soon. Because whether it's me or someone else, someone is gonna deal with Salaok and it just might be permanently. Nice talking with you.' Brick then stood up. 'Damn, I gotta go.' He then looked around for groundskeepers and other visitors. Then he looked back at Lisha's grave. 'Hmm.'
