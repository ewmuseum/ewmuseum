The changing of the tide # 2

16 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/16/04 The Long Night has ended and a new dark age dawns. An age of priests and princes. Of faith and damnation. An age of powerful ancient horrors and terrifying new threats. It is an age of excitement and danger, in which any Cainite with enough will can forge himself a kingdom over the cowering mortal herds and his own damned brethren. And many will try. -Cainite Proverb Silence. I sit in my office in silence awaiting the news from my scientist and from my son. They all hold very interesting news. News that will effect the future and my life as I know it. As I sit there in the warmth of my silence the phone rings. I pick it up carelessly as everything else in this world is to me and has been for hundreds of years. “Hello?” “Mr. Soma I presume.” “Indeed it is”.since it is the number you called.” “I see. My name is Almeric’Almeric St.Francis. I am with the council.” “And what council is that Mr. St.Francis?” “You know exactly what council I’m talking about. It has come to our attention that you are how do you say”.special unlike most people.” “I don’t know what your talking about. I am a simple businessman who happens to wrestle on the side. I’m a man making a living.” “A man you say’at no point in your life were you a man Mr. Soma. We know exactly what you are and you have violated many of our rules. For we Mr. Soma are just like you”.children of him?.” It seems finally my distant relatives have caught on to me. For so many years I have stayed in the shadows from even the ones who call the shadows their homes. For so long these so called children of Caine have never known of my presence until now. Who could have made them aware of my presence? Who could have let them know what I truly am? Granted I go on television every week saying I am a vampire but who would pay attention to that. Who would think that real? I keep my composure as would any businessman would do. “Mr. St.Francis you could not fathom what I truly am. You think I’m like you” You think I’m some mere vampire trying to make his mark in this world of darkness? You are wrong?.dead wrong, no pun intended. I am what you all wish to become. An evolved form of what you all call “vampire”. I can’t break laws which don’t apply to me. I suggest you forget you ever made this call. Have a nice day.” “Mr. Soma we are not through”.we know about your whole life and as we see it you have broken many rules and for that you must be punished. We have contacts everywhere in our world and theirs. We shall be watching you Mr. Soma and we will be in touch.” I hang up the phone aggravated thinking now of another problem that will have to be dealt with. I brush my hair out of my face and stand from my onyx desk and begin to walk around my office. I begin to contemplate all of my enemies in this world and who had the resources to actually come into MY world and begin to tear it down rock by rock. As I am pacing there is a knock at my door. “Come in.” Two men enter, one standing about 7’1 and the other about 5’6. The larger of the two men was wearing a black suit while the smaller man wears a white lab coat , a white t-shirt and jeans. I look to the taller man’my son’and motion him to speak. “Father, we have the results from the blood sample I got.” “And.” “It was yours’like you expected.” The smaller man steps forward and I look down at him. My anger at humanity once more boiling within my soul. He tries to speak but he trembled so badly he can not. He looks at me then to my son, then deciding both of us were equally terrifying he looks at the ground. “What is it scientist’it is ok you can speak.” “Well Mr. Soma, your son is right’it is your blood but only part, she is less vampire than you and your son. She is more human and the mixture of the blood will make her go insane’the vampire blood keeps her from getting sick and aging like humans, even giving her strength and other attributes like a vampire’but she lacks the mental capacity for the power. She will go insane and begin to kill like a rabid dog would?” Without hesitation I lunge at the small man and break his neck and begin to feed from him, my son in horror and disbelief steps a few steps back. I stand wiping the blood on my white trench coat. I look at him as a tear falls from my eye. I walk back over to my seat and sit down “Why did you do that father?” “He called your sister, my daughter a dog. We can’t have that. Besides I can’t control the monster with all the time’that’s something you, I, and she all have in common. With this new information I have to work quickly now. We have pressing matters to attend to. We must go to the house, pick up Sage, and head to New York.” “Why what has happened?” “Before you came in I got a little call from a Mr. Almeric St.Francis.” “But father isn’t he the?” “Yes son he is. He has probably already put the hunt on my head.” “But you can easily defeat anyone who comes for you”.all of the rest of your siblings sleep is that not correct?” “That is only hearsay and I can “t take that chance right now’I have too much on my mind. Besides I think I know who was behind letting them know what I truly am. Have someone clean this up I need to make a phone call before we leave.” “Ok father I shall return briefly.” I nod as my son grabs the body and runs out the door. I hurriedly picked up the phone and called the security guards “Yes Mr. Soma?” “If anyone comes to the gate besides myself or my son, shoot them at will with the special bullets I had made for you guys.” “Sir what’s wrong?” “JUST DO IT!!!!” “Yes sir.” By the time I hung up the phone Maximilian was already back in the room. I open one of the drawers of my desk and throw him a revolver. I check the other drawers and I take two. As I put them into the holsters I’m wearing I run to the closet and grab my sword. I look to my son and nod to him and he begins to head out the door. As we exit the office, I hear the ping of the elevator doors. Five men fangs bared running toward us with submachine guns. Time looks as if it stands still as I rush the first guy decapitating him with ease. The second guy I knock the gun from his hand and punch him through the chest pulling out his heart. The third guy I already can see as Maximilian slowly rushes him, shooting him in the head and in the heart. The forth guy doesn’t move except for dropping his gun I grab his head sticking my fingers through his eyes and throwing him into the fifth guy. Everything begins to speed back up to normal and I grab the fifth guy and throw him to my son. Max breaks his neck and shoots him twice, once to the head and once to the heart. I look to the newly blind fourth guy. I grab him by the throat and whisper into his ear. “All I wanted was quiet, and for the longest time I had that quiet”.that silence?.you tell your boss, or your prince’whatever you call the guy in charge that he is gonna have to send stronger vamps than that to stop me?.Thin blooded bastard?..find your way back home?.” I push him on to the elevator and I head further down with Max following covering my back. “I think it would be wise to take the stairs son.” “Yeah”.Dad?” “What?” “I have never seen you like that before?” “There is a lot you haven’t seen’but you will get to in the upcoming days”.A war is brewing and we are now in the middle of it.” We exit down the stairs in silence
