My Story: Chapter Two

19 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.19.04 My Story Chapter Two 'The UHWF Title' 2001 Ken Partridge Jr stood triumphantly in the ring, with his arms raised high inside of the 15-foot high steel cage and tears streaming down his cheeks. 'Ladies and gentlemen,' the ring announcer chimed with honey in his voice, 'the winner of this match... and FIRST EVER UHWF Heavyweight Champion of the World... 'Y2KPJ' KEN PARTRIDGE, JR.' The crowd was going nuts, screaming with everything they had. Their hero... the man which they had looked up to for so many years, had finally done it. He had finally acheived everything that he had worked for. All of the years... all of the blood, the sweat, and the tears... they were all paying off. 'FRAN-CHISE! FRAN-CHISE,' the crowd chanted as they had for so long at that time. It's a chant that's rarely heard in arenas presently, as the memory of where it used to be has been drowned out with chants of 'Sell Out.' The referee and senior official, Andrew Larkin, unlocked the cage door and stepped in, followed by an ethusiastic Special Kay who immediatly ran to Ken and threw her arms around his neck without caring about getting his blood and sweat on her pink dress, and Ken's trainer and mentor, 'Heartbreak Kid' Shawn Michaels. 'When will you learn,' sang Filter in the chorus of Ken's theme song at the time, Jurassitol. 'I'm not your boy,' the chords ripped throughout the entire building, yet they were still difficult to hear over the fans. 'You don't look back.' Special Kay, whom was now crying tears of joy herself, moved to the other side of Ken as the Shawn approached and embraced Ken as well. Both lifted his arms high into the atmosphere as Ken stood with his eyes closed and blood still pouring from his forehead. 'Why do I pick up your slack?' The owner of the Universal Hardcore Wrestling Federation, Anthony Pascal, dressed in his Chuck Taylor three-piece suit, made his was to the ring carrying the shiny new belt, as the cage was being raised. He made his way into the ring as the music continued to blast from the speakers and stood in front of Ken before shaking his hand and presenting him with the title. KPJ shot his arm high into the air immediatly, holding the large piece of gold for the world to see as Shawn picked him up off of his feet, lifting the strap even higher into the air. Ken continued to celebrate in the ring for quite some time, not even noticing how badly his broken ribs were aching until he made his way back up the ramp with Kay in tow and through the curtain to the backstage area. He walked down the stairs, still beeming, and stepped out into a standing ovation from his fellow in-ring warriors, trainers, agents, and suits. They all stood there, applauding Ken's efforts... not just on that given night... but throughout his entire career. Ken had finally climbed his mountain to the summit, and now it was time for him to avoid the avalanches and continue advancing up and to the peak. ------- 'Come in,' Ken responded to the knock on his door as he exited the shower in his locker room. The door opened just as Ken stepped behind his Japanese changing curtain, and Special Kay was quick to greet his family with open arms and smiles as Ken pulled on his black Epidemic slacks and tight white YMLA sleeveless zipper shirt. 'I'm so glad you guys made it back here,' said Ken as he walked back into the main area of his room. 'Did security give you any problems?' Ken was standing almost in as much astonishment that both his father and his new family and his mother and her husband were all in the same room as Ken and his sister's family as he was that he had finally won his first World title. 'None at all,' replied Angie, Ken's sister, as the Dave Matthews Band played quietly in the background on Ken's portable stereo. They went on to tell Ken how proud they were of him and how they all knew that he could do it. That they all always had faith in him. Looking back on it... Ken knew that they were lying. The only ones in that room on that day that had faith in him were Kay, Angie, and one of Ken's younger brothers, Daniel... and himself. From the rest of them, all he ever got was laughs and 'You'll never pull it offs'. A smorgesborg of 'You're to smalls' and a plethura of 'You need to live outside of your heads.' He never needed them, and he made it clear that night and has continued to do so every night since. Angie and Daniel... that's who is still allowed in his life from his family. They believed... they had faith from the very beginning. Daniel didn't do as Ken's dad did and laugh in KPJ's face and tell him he would never do it. Angie never told Ken that he dreamed to much as his mother did. Everyone else came along for the ride after the train had already proven that it was the best one on the track. He had no need for them... they meant nothing to him. But at that time... they were his world. Funny how things change. Funnier how they remain the same.
