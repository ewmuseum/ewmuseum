Another name on the list

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/30/04 As Christina McCoy sat at her desk, she perused the screen on her computer. She was reading the various Internet reviews of Clash of the Titans. Some were positive, others were negative. Her concentration was abruptly broken by the sound of Roberta the receptionist exclaiming, “Excuse me, but Ms. McCoy is very busy right now!” All of a sudden, the door to Christina’s office burst open with a loud crash and in stormed Brick. Many authority figures in the history of Global Wrestling had experienced Brick’s anger-driven intrusions into their office. Jason Ayers had experienced them. Eric Lansheer had experienced them. Even Bubba had experienced them, although, in his case, it was very rare, because Brick had a great deal of respect for Bubba. Now, it was Christina’s turn to experience it. “Ms. McCoy, would you like me to call security?” asked Roberta. “That would probably be best, wouldn’t it?” Christina asked, her eyes locked on Brick. “No, that won’t be necessary, Roberta. Brick and I can settle this.” Roberta then returned to her desk. “I think I have a pretty good idea as to what this is all about, but why don’t you enlighten me, anyway?” “Oh, you want me to enlighten you?! Is that it?! Well, how’s this for enlightening?!” he exclaimed, throwing a sheet of paper onto Christina’s desk. The paper was a printout of an article from Global Wrestling’s official Web site, which read as follows: The Return of Evil Steven Dinkler here with a breaking story! Former GW superstar Derek Draven has returned!!! You heard me right. After a year and a half, Draven has returned! Derek was seemingly never to be found as at COTT 2001 after losing the International title to Brick in a blood bath match up. Draven left GW for an extended period of time. No one has seen or heard from Derek in over a year, but on Sunday, March 28th 2004. Derek Draven made a surprise visit to Global Arena and walked out with a new contract!! Derek, formerly of the feared Nevermore, is set to return at GW Evolution... Who he will face is unknown right at this time. Derek has an impressive GW track record. Former Cruiserweight, International, Television and Extreme championships have his name plastered to them. Now that Draven is back one could guess that this sadistic man is once again out for blood!! Where has he been, none know. What will he do now that he is back? We shall have to wait and see. This is once again... Steven Dinkler signing out. “Brick, I can explain…” “Oh, can you? Explain how you could let that son of a bitch back into the company, knowing how I’d feel about it, knowing what I had to put up with from him and the rest of the Nevermore!” “Look, Brick, we always need talented wrestlers and fighters in the company. That’s all I was doing: hiring a talented…” “Don’t give me that, Christina! That’s what you said when you hired Makonie!” “Well, it was true then and it’s true now.” “Yeah, whatever.” “Anyway, I figured you could put your personal feelings aside.” “You figured wrong!” “Look, Brick, I know what you and your friend went through…” “Do you? Do you know how Aaron’s wife would cry herself to sleep at night because of Draven? Do you know it’s only by the grace of God that Aaron can still walk? Do you?!” “I’m sorry, Brick. I really am. But it’s not like I can fire him right after getting him to sign a contract.” “Why not?” “Well, he’d sue me, for one thing…” “Fine, but I’m gonna tell you right now. Draven had better get to me before I get to him. That’s all I’m gonna say.” With that, Brick walked out of Christina’s office, only slightly less angry than when he walked in. Christina could do nothing except sink into her chair and sigh. Brick had a new name on his hit list and things were just going to get ugly.
