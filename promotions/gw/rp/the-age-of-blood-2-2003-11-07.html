The age of Blood #2

7 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/07/03 Silence I pull my hair back so that my entire face can be seen….out with the old…in with the new. Showing the side of my face that hasn’t been seen in hundreds of years. The side of me that makes me a monster. The side of me that puts fear into the hearts of mankind. The second phase in the age of blood. “What are you Soma?” I turn to the shadows from where the voice is coming. As I run my hand over the part of my face I have kept hidden for all these years I try to answer with a calm manner. “I am a vampire.” “Yes but you are more than just a vampire. You are THE vampire. No other comes close to your powers except me. You are a God among men Soma. But being a God will bring you new enemies.” “Its nothing that TUE and myself can’t handle.” “TUE has nothing to do with this and they can not know of this for they have no part in this and would die if they tried to intervene.” “I understand master…” The words the voice had spoken chill even my body, the power of the voice strong, commanding, dominant. I listen as I am put in a trance by the voice. Its rhythm like a soft song that would calm the most viscous monster. “I wish to ask a question of my master?” “Ask what you wish Soma.” “May I see who my master is?” “You wish to see who your master is? Do you truly think you could handle who and what I truly am?” “I can handle anything…” The darkness seemed to subside and there stood a woman. She stood about five eleven, with shoulder length red hair but her bangs were dyed blonde, her eyes were a glowing dark red, her face lightly done with makeup to accent her already beautiful features. She wore a tight black spaghetti strap top with a see through long-sleeved shirt over it, also she wore a pair of tight black pants with knee high black leather boots that buckled on the sides. The outfit suited her more than average body just right. I sat there stunned for a minute not knowing what to say. The look of this woman showed she had to be one of the most beautiful women of her time…but when exactly was her time? Her eyes even as the glowed showed that she had age behind her even though she didn’t look a day over twenty-one. I begin to look over her body and I looked at her arms, both arms had angelic sigils tattooed on them. Truly a powerful creature is she. I stand there silent as she smiles and smirks quietly at the awe I have for her. “Surprised at what you see Soma?” “You’re a woman…” “Indeed I am. Do you really think there is any male on this world who could have the power over you that I do?” “There is only one and nobody had even mentioned his name in over two hundred years….only us vampires old enough to know who he is. And I of course know who he is since he was my father…” “Indeed he was. You do not speak of your royal bloodline often. Why do you think I chose you Soma? I have chose many of your kind before but only two were ever worthy. Your father…..and you.” “My father once spoke of a woman who taught him…..She made him turn away from everything he ever believed in and yet offered him ultimate power…..you….you are…” “Throughout the ages young one I have gone by many names. I have killed many things and yet I have only taught my ways to one. And he betrayed me. In the beginning was a man’s world. And I could not deal with that. Who says men have to rule over women? Who says that I had to be submissive to my husband? Out of all I have stood the test of time the longest my only rivals were the lord God and his angels. No mortal or vampire alike has gone through the age of blood until now Soma…you shall be the first. The first to know the pain of true eternity. The first male to know what its like to be in true power. The first vampire to contend with the Gods themselves. After the age of blood Soma, you shall reign. Your powers shall be unmatched and all you have to do is listen and learn the things I tell you. It has been told in angelic, mortal, and vampire lore alike there would be a time where a man would come that would shake the heavens and the earth. His power would be great and his power would come from the vitality of mortals. And through that none would be able to contend with him and he’d reign for thousands of years. Through his age of blood mankind would suffer for he who loves transgression loves strife and he who exalts its gates would know destruction. Soma you must realize this is your destiny. To be what your father could not.” “My destiny….You must realize I am closer to that destiny than you realize…I feed off the heavenly through my right to battle and victory. Each victory opens up the heavens to my mouth, that I may drink from the cup of the everlasting. I wouldn’t go as far as to say I shall reign for thousands of years or even being able to contend with the Gods. As much as I would love that.” “D.H. Lawrence once said “This is the very worst wickedness, that we refuse to acknowledge the passionate evil that is in us.” You need to realize what you destiny is. Soma with my help I shall make you a God. But there are some trials we must endure…are you ready to go through them?” “I am ready…” “There are two passages in the bible that let us know that know mortal is innocent even though they try. “As it is written: There is none righteous, no not one.” Romans three and ten. The second is “For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.” Romans three and twenty-three. Now your first trial will be to corrupt the innocent. There are certain ones you must corrupt. I have made a list for you. Do this and your power shall grow. This is the first test in the age of blood.” I stand there and I smile. To be what my father could not…..to contend with the Gods…I shall be the most powerful. Let the age of blood begin.
