First Night in Dallas

11 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.11.03 ~ALWAYS REMEMBER - 9.11~ 22nd Street was still pretty damp from the rain earlier, which irritated me somewhat considering my black Pradas cost a good $750.00 and I knew they were sure to be in need of a leather treatment at the end of the evening. I guess I shouldn't have parked the Porsche two blocks away. I glanced over at Gunner as we stepped over a puddle. She was truly more stunning than ever. She has hair that reminds you of sunrise and eyes that are so large and beautiful you can literally feel your insides warming up. When she smiled at me I became breathless, if there's one thing I've ever looked for in a woman it's a great smile. Lord knows that if there's a better smile than her's on this planet, I don't know about it. She was dressed remarkably... seductive in a very classy way. Her dress was tight and came to about mid-thigh, revealing just enough of her long beautiful legs. The neck was cut low enough to reveal clevage, but again... just the perfect amount. Her shoes were Gucci, a four inch heel if my judgment's correct, and black with ivory trim. The diamonds in her ears were enough to bring out the sparkle in her eyes and those around her neck to accent her smile. I can't believe how lucky I am. She's been out of my life for so long, and now she's back. I thought I had lost her for good last time, but fate's a truly curious thing. Now, here she was, showing me around Dallas, Texas. She said we were going to a pretty popular hot spot, a nightclub called Downtown. The truth is, I don't really care were we go, so long as we're together. Though, I do hope this place is pretty low key so she and I can spend a bit of personal time together. I hate to sound like I'm bragging, but I've definitly had a hard time in the past when I go out due to the ammount of celebrity a professional wrestler has these days. Let's just say getting noticed hadn't been a problem in a long time. The rumbling started as soon as we approached the club. The people in lines were obviously fans of Global Wrestling. Either that, or they had heard a few people around them who had recognized us. Nontheless, the cheers and calling had already began. 'I don't want to stand in that line with all of that going on. They're likely to stampede before too long,' I whispered to Gunner. She laughed and smiled at me. I would like to say she paying attention to the outfit that I had payed top dollar for, but not once did I see her eye wander to my black Versace slacks or my white silk button-up made by the same designer. Her eyes had settled into mine and at that moment I would have done anything she wanted and she knew it. 'Don't worry, Sweetheart. We don't have to,' she assured as she usured me to the doorman. 'They're used to that type of thing here, what with the organization based out of Dallas and all. So, everyone makes things as normal for us as they can.' She waved at the bouncer who pulled the red velvet rope aside and smiled at us as we pushed forward. 'How are you doing, Chaz?' 'Not bad, Gunner. It's a little crazy tonight, though. There've been a good bit of you guys in here and the crowd's eating it up. They're even calling up their friends and getting them here. Hey, I didn't know you knew Ken Partridge, Jr.' What Chaz had just said hurt me a little. She knew this cat, yet she had never mentioned me. I quickly grew out of my self-pity, though, when I realized that this was just a bouncer that she had probably never talked to for more than 2 minutes at a time. I reached out to shake his hand, 'Chaz, right?' 'That's right, and I know who you are. I'm a big fan,' he said, almost beaming. I couldn't help but smile as I responded, 'Thanks man. It's always nice to meet one.' We all laughed for just a bit before the crowd started get a tad unruly. Chaz, a big fellow that resembled Odd Job from that old James Bond movie Goldfinger, quickly slung the door open and showed us in. The club was fantastic, brilliant even. It was amazing how something that resembled a warehouse on the outside reminded me so much of my hometown of New York inside. Holding my hand, Gunner guided me to a section in the corner that looked onto the dance floor. We sat down with a small group of people that I'm only assuming she knows. The place was pretty crowded, and to be honest I was just happy to have found somewhere to be once we were in. Gun reapplied her lipstick and checked her face in her compact mirror. I quickly put my hand on her's, which was reaching for more make-up, I'm sure. 'You're absolutly beautiful,' I told her honestly and hoping that I had reassured her that she didn't need anymore make up. She smiled and removed her hand from her purse and placed it on my cheek. We moved in and kissed one another. It had been so long since I had felt her and it truly felt like home. The kiss wasn't that long and was more sweet than sexy, but it truly felt as if it had lasted a lifetime. I was beaming as we pulled away from each other, not saying a word and just staring into one another's eyes. We were quickly interrupted by our cocktail waitress, however. This was somewhat disappointing, but Lord knows I could always use a drink. 'What can I get for you, Ms. Addison?' Apparantly, everybody here knows Gunner, including the doorman and the waitresses. 'I'll take a Sour Appletini, I think.' She always spoke with the most honest hint of sweetness in her voice... I love it. 'How about you, si--' Oh no, she stopped. I've been in here for two minutes and I'm already about to have my first encounter. 'Oh my God... I'm your biggest fan,' she whispered to me as quietly as she could while she bent down to my ear. 'My name's Meghaan and I've been following you since you were in the Alliance of Hardcore Wrestlers. I actually had front row seats the night of your big title match, but the event got cancelled due to the company going out of business and all.' She had something of a sad look on her face that I couldn't help but feel sorry about. 'It was going to be my first wrestling event, too.. and I was even gonna' get to see you win the World Title, I'm sure. Unfortunantly, I haven't been able to see you live ever. I guess it's a kind of curse.' This made me feel real sad. I couldn't believe I had this big of a fan in front of me. I knew I had to do something. 'I'll tell you what,' I said, 'I'm making my Global Wrestling debut at Evolution. How about I put your name on the ticket holding list and get you a couple of front row seats.' 'Oh -- Oh my -- my God... you're kidding, right,' she asked with tears in her eyes. 'Absolutly, Meghaan. For a fan like you, it would be an honor.' 'Thank you so much,' the tears remained in her eyes and a huge smile was now on her face. 'Oh no, I completly forgot... ummm... what can I get you to drink?' I couldn't help but laugh at this. I mean, it was pretty humorous. I wondered if she had even remembered what Gunner had ordered. 'I think I'll take a double Scotch and 7 with lemon and lime for now. Macallan, if you've got it.' 'No problem, guys. I'll be right back.' She scurried off like an ant telling the rest of the colony about a picnic. Gunner was smiling at me. I didn't have to ask why. We always loved it when the appreciation for our fans came out of the other. 'I've got to go freshen up, Hun. I'll be right back.' She got up and headed to the restroom, which left me there by myself watching people dancing in front of me. Meghaan brought out our drinks before Gunner had returned and I quickly added my lemon and lime twist before taking a nice large sip. I love that taste. As I looked back up at the dance floor, I noticed something odd. A beautiful red headed girl leading out someone by the hand. But it couldn't be who I think it is... Kid Flash is still dating Amber.
