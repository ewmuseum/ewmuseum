Obstacle Set

21 March 2004

Date: 3-21-2004 Chris was sitting on his couch his feet were crossed on the coffee table at the ankles, his wrists were laid behind his head in an attempt to gain comfort while he watched television. He obviously was bored because his mind kept wandering, all he could think about was wrestling. By now he had moved past his public attention fear and had begun his 'high and mighty overconfindent' phase. This was not uncommon, to the few wrestlers who knew chris on a personal basis, they knew this was just an eventual stage. As he won more and got better, this syndrome would only get worse. In most cases this overconfindence had led to his downfall, he knew it, so did his friends. This time though he had been ready and anticipated it, and would use it to his advantage, he would use it to intimidate others. He would use it to gain fans and to gather his allies. Although he never appriciated the importance of his teammates he would use them as stepping stones. He would use them as objects to get to the top. He would not hesitate to betray someone if it meant a road to championship. Every possible situation had crossed his mind, every outcome. Even so, Chris had still not been assigned an opponent. But he had looked at them as victoms, pests in the way. He had begun to grow impatient, waiting for a call from the GW officials to give him the good news. Days had seemed like years he waited hour after hour. But when someone did call he would rush to the phone, picked it up on the first ring sometimes. Only to hear the voice of someone he didn't care for on the other line. A couple times this happened it made him angry. He had never been able to hide his anger fits, sometimes they would even give him consequences, they would fine him. Unless it was in the ring. In that case it served as his second wind, the ace up his sleeve. Although he could not trigger it himself, some things just seemed to set it off. He didn't view it as a curse instead, he used it to his advantage. Just then, the airwaves were penetrated by the sound that Chris had been waiting for, his cell phone. By this time he had no longer been hurring to the phone, instead he took his feet off the table, took his hands out from behind his head and reached to his phone, he didn't recognize the number. Just as soon as he answered the phone he realized who it was. 'Hello... O yes!... Exellent.. Okay see you then.' The conversation was short but it was still very important because he was just notified of his opponent. His wait was over and now he knew who his foe would be on evoloution. His obstacle had been set up and it was time to show the president what it was they had signed. And not only was he going to make an impact but he was going to show the full extent of the damage he could deal. He would even do it all between the bells. Now he had the call he had been waiting for, he could now get some rest, because if he was going to show everyone he had the skill to com,pete with the main eventers, he would need to be competing at top performance. After a short yawn Chris stood up, brushed himself off, walked down the hall to his room and shut the door....
