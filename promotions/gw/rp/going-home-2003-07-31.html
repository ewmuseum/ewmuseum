Going Home

31 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/31/03 The scene opens outside, on the side of a highway. The sounds ouf the cool air howling, and cars passing by raid the microphone attatched to the camera. The camera pans away from the highway and the speeding cars to a giant sign. The sign has a blue background, white trim, and big bold white letters that read ' WELCOME TO TORONTO'. The sound of crunching snow filters into the sounds of the howling air and speeding cars. The cameraman pans away from the sign to see Star, dressed in a long black over coat and a white scarf. Her face is red from the cold and the wind. Her hair, now blond with black streaks in it blows around with the wind. Walking next to her in a black trench coat, wearing black sunglasses, and sporting the same blond with black streaked hair style as his beautiful counterpart is Ice. The two stop their walking as they reach the cameraman and the welcome sign along the interstate. 'Glad to see you could make it. We've been waiting for you. You didn't have any problems getting here did you? Good. Now onto pressing matters....' Star smirks as she finishes her words. Ice turns and looks are her. She leans forward on her tip toes and gives him a small peck on the cheek. Ice calmly turns his head and faces the 'WELCOME TO TORONTO' sign. Ice adjusts his trench coat and stares at the sign for some time. Without his attention to the sign wavering Ice begins to speak. 'Ah, it's good to be home. Back to a place where the people appreciated your hardwork, your effort, and your talents. Being back home always, always gives me that little extra something that makes me want to do, or say something special. But that will have to wait.' Ice turns away from the sign and takes off his jet black sunglasses. He hangs the sunglasses from the from of his shirt that is peaking just above the collar of his trench coat. Ice looks into the camera lens and blinks, then blinks again, and blinks on last time before letting out a deep breath. The air fogs up in front of him from his warm breath hitting the cold air. Ice kneels down and picks up a handful of snow. 'Last week, some men took the liberty upon themselves to try and break me. Those men Deacon Holmes, John Zero, and Brick. Now I know Brick is going to say that he was trying to help me out. That he was going after Deacon, but in the process you cost me the PRIMESTAR TITLE! And I know why. Because while you were in the back watching your number one contender Deacon Holmes get his ass handed to him you realized that once I beat him, you were next. You couldn't handle that. You didn't want that. So you came out and screwed me. You came out hoping to crush my spirit, and break my pride. Well, unlike this snow, I don't break, and I don't melt. I am forever solid. In time, you will learn that, and so will everyone else. Now as for the question everyone has been asking. What do you mean Ice when you say leave your comfort zone and enter a new world. To that I will reply with this. In this life there are many trials and tribulations one must go through. Some that mean nothing, others that leave marks that last a lifetime. Many in this profession live inside their comfort zone, and go through, and experience those trials and tribulations that mean nothing, and leave no marks. I however have stepped outside the box, outside my comfort zone, and can still feel the marks that have been left on my body. I look at them and smile, then look at all of you. I am saddened, angered, and disappointed, that I am overlooked, and underrated though I have stepped out and conquered the things outside of my comfort zone, yet all of you, Deacon Holmes and Brick you stand, feeling safe in your comfort zone, and get all the praise, all the opportunity, and all the recognition in the world.' A sneer crosses Ice's face as he crumbles the snow in his hand and brushes off the reminents on his pant leg. He stands up and looks into the camera. His black streaked blond hair blows in the wind. 'In soon enough time, you won't be able to hide in your world of comfort, and you won't be able to ignore your fears, and your fate. Ms. McCoy, I'm tired of the world you have forced me to live in. I'm tired of being surpressed. I'm tired of men like Brick, and Deacon Holmes hiding in their world of comfort. So I'm going to go home.....,but I'm bringing you all with me.' A limo stops along side the road as Ice finishes speaking. Star walks up to him and wraps her left arm around his right arm. The two walk away from the cameraman and the sign. Ice opens the jet black limo door and Star slides into the vehicle. Ice follows her in and shuts the door. The cameraman watches until the limo is out of sight. He then pans back to the giant blue sign. The scene fades with the words ' WELCOME TO TORONTO' filling the screen as a single snowflake falls from the sky and lands on the camera lens. Fade to black.
