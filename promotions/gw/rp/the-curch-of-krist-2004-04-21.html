The Curch of Krist

21 April 2004

The Curch of Krist Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #1199 Date: 4/21/04((WC RP 2)) Location: Undisclosed. ((There weren't any lights on, It wasn't Dark because light Shined through the Stained Glass..The Picture Dipicted A Sunny Day. Trees growing the in Back were only to be covered up by an angel descending. His Golden Wings being Ripped at the Seems. The Pain on his face defiantly noticeable. Blood Pours from His right eye..His Mouth. a Small cut on his chest. The halo he once wore broken and cast to the side. His Once Angelic Robe lay in tatters around his midsection.)) 'This is my God.' ((The Voice comes at you from the middle of the curch. In the Third Pew there sits a man. His Back turned. Hands up as if they are praying. You notice the pews quickly. Not the Normal Royal Purple or Gold. Black, The side of them do not beckon welcome. They in fact command. Small portions of the Kdram are inscribed in the legs. The Stage is not covered by your normal Flowers, Pictures and Banners of a normal curch. There is but one stand. Black marble. on it sits an open Book. In the Curch of Krist i would guess the Kdram.)) 'I will be with you in a moment.' ((The Voice is barely audible in the fact that the stand Draws you...it send off a certain call. It Draws you to it. You reach the stand an the Kdram stands open. It Reads.)) Those who have Life, Have Life through me. They and you are but my fingers. You move but not of your own accord. I am the one and the only. ((The words mean nothing to some one who is not a follower..but you still keep reading.)) The Anarchy that ruled my Planet before i came to quill the small rebellion that had became a thorn in my side. I took Careful care to eliminate one by the the problems in these problems. 'I belive you came to see me you impudent filth.' ((The Camera turns and the Hard face of Roz blyze is the only thing it sees. His Clothing Simbling much the Curch. Black. A Black jacket that covers a Black Long sleeved shirt..The picture on it is of a man. Nailed though his Neck. To a Black Cross. His Pants very baggy. Also Black. 'You are here to Listen. Broadcast. Live if i let you.' ((I had Shaken this man now. Now i would toy with him as i willled. With No reactions what so ever. Right now if i wanted to pull This Ivroy and Glod Handled Desert eagle and Put one right between this Pigs eyes. I wouldn't..I couldn't stain the floor of the Curch of Krist. Blood cannot be spilt here unless it be Blood of a Kristian.)) 'You desire a Look into the Curch of Krist. Come, I will show you.' ((Roz turns and his jacket tail leads down into a hall way. )) 'There are Four Floors, You have seen the gathering room. The Next lower Floor is were we are going..The Floor below that you are not able to see. The Top Floor is the Krist baths. You will see The Curch of Krist as it is.' ((Roz leads down a set of steps. In the walls the Kdram is inscribe.)) ((The Book is Froced into our heads. We all know it by heart and are able to repeat any of it at any time i should choose. There are Incantations that are said to cripple men. When read aloud.)) 'Now we are going to the Black Rooms. This is serval Rooms were Kristians come to Pray, They are given anything they should want during this time. Women, Drink anything that your heart longs for.' ((You are being led down a set of steps, Black as much of the curch had been. You have seen no one but the Man standing in front of you. he turns left and into want looks to be a Black Hall..No light are on.)) 'We call this Sin's Playground. Normal Kristians do not Sin. In this Hall way we are allowed to do the smallest of things. Small sins. Like Human Little White Lies.' ((Much to my Dismay no one was in Sin's Playground. I felt it would be good to see what the man would think of a dead Body being found on the Floor. What joy i would have in Krists Baths.)) 'Down the Next hall way lays the Black Rooms. I will let you explore at your lesiure. Do not, i repeat DO not Disturb any Member Praying. If you Do. I will have to Kill you.' ((His Face was visible Shaken. I smiled at him lightly and went and took my Head booth of Prayer. I took a Glass of red Wine and began to watch the man. My Prayers began to illuminate along the panel. My Daughter. It had been such a long time. I long to see her again. My Angel. The Man began to look around. I seen members of the Religion. They normal prayers. For Life. They wanted to do more things for Krist. To be like me. They wanted to make them selves in the Head Preist of Kristism image. My image. he walked along. He suddenly stopped and looked down on Derodric. A Blond haired Blue eyes man. Very selfless..A Stout Believer in Krist. He was one of the few that i made Kristians. An Elloeha. He looked at him for a moment then pass him by. He Just simply browsed not caring. Not really interested. I could see his intrest slipping. I diddent feel like taking him out side and killing him yet. I just Felt that he needed something more. I exited my Booth and walked to him.)) 'Sir, Do you Wish to become a Follower of Krist? These things can be yours.' 'yes.' 'Then now you can see The Krist baths.' ((I would now lead him up. twenty nine stairs. I count them every them i walk up to the Krist baths. My Very, Very Favorite part of being the Head Preist. I get to give the babtisims. He followed. Dumb. He had even began Reading the walls. I never used to do it. Not until i became Governor of the house. Then i began making the Elloeha, do it every were they walked. They are Expected to be above the rest. They are the Care takers on the Curch of Krist.)) 'Once you enter here and are babtized. You are Kristian. Never can you forget that.' ((I pressed my face close to his.)) 'Never.' ((I twisted the large Bolt on the iron door. It swung open and i walked into a wave of steam. It smelled of fresh Blood. The Blood Of Krist and His Closest Followers. To become a Kristian you must be bathed in the Blood of Kroist. There were many on lines. I had a Elloeha pre forming a bath full of Babtizims.)) 'The Preist.' 'Belive in the Life Given to you by Krist.' ((They chanted it in Unison. I waved my Hand and they went back to their doings.)) 'Come.' He followed me to a bath, Made of gold and Silver. Inlaid with the Same picture on the Stained Glass..It was being filled with the Blood of Full Kristian. Our offering each week is a Blood offering. A Small one. Most of this Blood was from the Pure Kristians. The ones i Made. and myself. 'Take all of you clothing off and get into this.' I handed him a simple Garment that would cover his body and absorb the blood. He had to feel the Blood to become a Kristian. a small piece of the cloth you were babtized in must always be with you. he dressed and i grabbed him. Walked him to the end of the Bath. I took him off his feet. he stood levitated for a moment. I laid him down in the bath and put his head under. When he came back up. His eyes were as Black as my own. It is always fills me with Pleasure when i see Kristians become real Kristian. 'Go...Pray.' ((He nodded and went to his Doings. As soon as he went under. He seen everything that all Kristians have ever done. From back to the time Krist himself became our ruler.)) 'And Before Wrestlecade my Friends. You as well need to Pray. For i shall be much less forgiving and Much more foreboding. Trifle with me not. for at Wrestlecade. It may be your blood that is spilt.' ((Roz runs his hands over his head and lets the Blood trickle down his face. Then he smiles and turns. Giving another babtizim and repeating the Phrase said earlier. Pray. and i beg you all. Pray Hard.))
