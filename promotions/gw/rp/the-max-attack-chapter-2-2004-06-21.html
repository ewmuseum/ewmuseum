The Max-Attack: Chapter 2

21 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 21 Name: The Max-Attack. Other Names: Max Roswell. Job/Occupation: Super Hero. Other Info: Max Roswell also known as The Max-Attack was born in late 1979 in Connecticut. Since he was born fumes have gotten into his blood and have made him a super-human. Know one would have survived that crash but Roswell did. Since Roswell was 20 he was getting interested in pro wrestling. Then Roswell and Father Clark moved to California for a couple of months. In the hotel that Roswell and Father Clark were staying the same night Father Clark had been shot by a unknown person. Shortly after that had happened Roswell noticed a envelope on the table in his hotel room. Roswell opened it and it was a note saying to come to the abandoned parking lot a block from the hotel. Roswell prayed and went to that hotel only to find a man with a amputated arm sitting in a chair. When Max asked the man who he was there was no answer, when he asked the second time a old voice was heard. The old man tells Roswell to go over to that shack a couple steps back, and see the man inside it. Roswell walks away from the old man and walks over to the old brown shack. Roswell starts to knock on the door but nothing happened. He knocked again but there was no answer again. Roswell starts getting mad and punches the door and it opens. The man that was in the shack slowly got up from his chair and looks at Roswell with a weird look. A couple of minutes later the man and Roswell start getting along and talk for a couple of minutes. Then the man picks up a glass that was on the floor and pours it all into Roswell’s mouth. The man starts laughing and walks out the door while Roswell falls to the ground holding his neck and head. Suddenly from the ground Roswell is seen getting back up, he looks as he is stronger than ever. From the outside of the parking lot the man looks back and turns back again with a scared face. Name: The Blue Bat Other Names: Jack Acid, ?”” Other Info: Jack Acid who was born December of 1970 in the town of New Jersey. When Jack was 20 he left his family and moved to California. He never knew that was the place of Max Roswell. Jack was growing up fast and started a power plant where he worked for 5 and a half years. Then one day a terrible thing had happened. 50 galleons of acid dumped in the plant and hit Jack burning him up. There was a worker that was working late and saw this whole thing, but from outside of the plant. No one thought ANYBODY could survive a accident like that but... JACK DID! Jack rose from the ground and started walking around looking for the worker that had left him. Finally Jack found the worker hiding behind Jack’s desk in his office. Jack angry as he was started choking the worker until he died. No one saw the worker ever again, even though his body was probably still in the power plant. The scene opens up to outside the hotel Roswell was in. Roswell was seen leaving a ice cream parlour with a cone dipped in chocolate. Roswell then walks across the street and sits down on a bench licking his cone. Then Roswell sees a man with a long brown trench coat on but under the coat he sees a blue piece of amour shining. Roswell then sees the man take a gun out of his pocket and wiping it with a Kleenex. The man with the gun puts it back into his pocket and walks away. “Who would wear a big heavy coat in a time of day like this?” Roswell gets up from the bench and walks the way that the man was going. Roswell sees the man getting into a taxi. Roswell starts running fast and calls a different taxi to follow that man. The taxi driver steps on the gas petal and Roswell starts moving down the street. A couple blocks down the man in the other taxi stops and gets out. Roswell also gets out and pays the taxi driver and it drives away. Roswell starts running towards the man but not too close. The man is seen walking to the airport which is a couple steps away. The man goes into the airport and runs to get a passport. From where the man is he is talking to a lady behind a counter. The lady says that the only flight to Chicago is in 5 hours. The man says he will wait at the airport until the flight leaves. Roswell then says to himself that he will need some stuff because he is going to follow this man to Chicago. Roswell then leaves and walks out of the door. Roswell quickly gets into another taxi and drives away from the airport. Roswell then stops at his hotel and walks inside it. Roswell goes up the stairs and opens his door to his room. Inside his room Roswell grabs some stuff off of his desk and closes the curtains. Roswell then turns the light back off and walks out of his room. Roswell quickly runs down the stairs and out of the building. Roswell gets in the same taxi and they drive back to the airport. Roswell sees the man just getting on to the plane, and runs to the counter. Roswell asks the lady if there is one more seat to Chicago. The lady says that there is one seat left and gives the passport to Roswell. Roswell runs to the hallway and shows a different lady the passport and runs through the hallway. Roswell climbs up the stairs and walks into the air plane. Roswell goes and sits down in a seat right across from the man he was following.
