Date: 5.22.04 :| I once thought I saw a monster. But it was in my head. Not of imagination. But of fear and dread. Sadness is what it spewed. That is what I took. And clinically I went by the book. I once thought I saw a monster. It was gray and green. In my head anything's possible. But nothing is what it seems. But it seems like this monster took hold of me. I once thought I saw a monster. I wished it would go away. Nothing could stop it. It wanted to stay. But all it took was a few pills for my monster to disappear. Now I really wonder one thing - Who won, me or the monster? |: ?| The Right Hand Of God |? :| Tarnished silver bells of tainted serenity enlighten the walls of an unsanctified church. God's light worn down to just a dim of lightened, golden dust as it transcended through the other side of the huge anti-Christ painted class that decorated the walls of Satan’s church. An organ soon fades in giving the heinous sanctuary emotions of agony; preparing to hail the divinest melancholy: another form of Lucifer. And as if a Goth convention had came into town, dozens of eccentric humans personified by make-up and black Goth clothing akin to the devil filled the pews. On this unholy morning, Lucifer was in the mood for casting another blasphemous thought towards long-forgot Eden. |: 'Today, we praise the black angel who stabbed God to set us free! His melodic words played with a sinister twist tuning us in on the right key. Falling from grace, he exposed the choir of heaven as the mere cacophony it is.' Prophesying the fiery dark fiend, the demonic clad preacher with red pupils and painted on diabolic masquerade agreeing with his demonic robe, he continued to touch the population with the devil. :| Just then Geoff Hoon proceeds through the threshold of the church accompanied by Itagaki Freeze as the light from outside stretching down the long aisle to the podium before descending into darkness once more as the doors shut and shun the virtuous sun light provided by the heavens. Unaffecting anyone present, they find a seat in the back as the preacher preaches on. |: 'Unholy spirits touch the masses and we gather to implore that when Satan benights the world on Armageddon, he sacrifices our dark souls and reincarnates us into dark archangels. Armed to destroy the Celestial Hierarchy of the Heavens! Raging with the wrath of Hell, we shall live through death!' Hatred in his voice, he projected the beliefs of the tainted prophecy. :| A young small girl with death in her manner sitting in the front arose like a zombie. Her stringy long black hair draped over her face which held dreadful scars of abuse. And almost as if she was summoned, she headed up the stairs to the podium keeping her arms, misery, and pain to herself. |: 'Now present today, we have a child of the devil that needs to be possessed. Someone to relieve her of her pain and suffering. She's come to the right place; today she will be set free. Welcome Une Toile D'Araignee.' Introducing the little girl, the preacher backed down to present her to the podium. :| Approaching the podium, the little girl, also known as Une Toile D'Araignee, lowered her head refusing to face the eyes staring into her soul and began to release the pain she had carried for so long. |: 'the twisted ashy gloom of this fragmented world of suffering seems my vampiric sentence to rot. an empty shell in the dank prison that is my mind. thorny hands seek the throat of my soul. tearing, grasping to keep me from again knowing the unencumbered bliss of kisses most forgotten. left to a short life of pain endured like a fish's last flap, once on dry land. no one understands me...' And like that, articulating her soul to the multitude, she was a step closer to serenity. A step closer to being forgave. ...to be continued
