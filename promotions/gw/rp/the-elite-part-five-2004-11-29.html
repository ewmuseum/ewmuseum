The Elite (Part Five)

29 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.29.04 'Why couldn't Curtis make it here for dinner tonight,' asked a stuffed Bill Moresi as he, Ken Partridge Jr, Josh, and Ken's friends Ceci Braden and Matthew Moore sat in the den of Global's resident Superstar SoHo level penthouse apartment sipping on some of Ken's twenty-five year aged Johnny Walker Gold Label scotch and enjoying post-meal Macanudo thirtieth anniversary cigars, except of course, for Josh. 'He had his own celebration to head with The Connection at their home,' responded Ken after taking a puff from his cigar. 'He did want me to tell everyone Happy Thanksgiving for him, though.' 'You know, they could have all come here, considering he is going to be our teammate in Tokyo,' added Bill with a touch of frustration in his tone of voice. 'Don't start, Bill,' calmed Ken. 'We could have all gone to Casa de Connection as well, and just as easily as they could have come here. But we didn't, because this was more appropraite. The same with them. Besides, he's meeting us at the airport tomorrow. I wanted him to fly on our jet to Tokyo so we'd all have that much more time to go over things for Showdown.' 'Yeah, big man,' started Josh, 'calm down. This Curtis guy... he's a good kid. Soemone we can trust. I mean, think about it... he's one of the one's that was there with us in order to help drive back The Echelon. He's got our backs.' 'Look, Bill,' added Ken, 'I know that it's hard for you to trust people after... well, just know that I know that. But, Joshua... he's a friend of mine, and a good one at that. I trust him. I'm not asking you to do the same, but I am asking you to trust me.' 'I do trust you, Ken,' responded Moresi, 'it's just -- I don't know. I guess I just haven't gotten the chance to really get to know him, yet.' The conversation was suddenly put on pause as Gunner Addison entered and greeted everyone before sitting between Ceci, whom she'd formed a close friendship with since their first meeting, and Ken, whom she said hello to with a kiss. She looked up from her glass of wine, poured from a bottle of Ken's 1989 Preveli shiraz, and noticed the odd look on Bill's face. 'Gunner, let me ask you a question,' began Bill before Gunner could ask him what was wrong. 'Considering the fact that Joshua Curtis has pronounced that he is, indeed, attracted to Ken, do you still trust him?' Okay,' began Addsion with a smile on her face, 'whether I trust Joshua or not, I trust Ken. With that said, considering Mr. Curtis has a boyfriend in Zack Johnson... actually, even if he didn't, I trust him. Yes. I mean, if I didn't trust Joshua for that reason, then I couldn't trust Matthew, whom I love to death, but he and Ken used to date.' 'And,' chimed in Matthew for the first time in the conversation, 'whose to say that friends can't find one another attractive. While I don't know this Joshua guy, I know Ken well enough to know that he makes good choices.' 'Precisly,' continued Gunner. 'And I really couldn't trust Ceci-' 'Because of the fact that Ken and I were actually entertaining marriage before we broke up and how close we remain to this day,' finished up Ms. Braden. 'Look, Ken has great judgment.' 'Perhaps,' responded Bill. 'Ken, just know that I trust you with my life, man. This isn't about you. It's about me.' With that, the mountain of a man got up out of his seat and walked out of the room. Josh got up and started to go after him, but was quickly stopped by Ken and Gunner. 'He just needs to unwind for a while,' reassured Ken, 'blow off some steam. I'm sure he'll be fine.' 'where do you think he's going,' asked Matthew. 'The gym, I suppose,' answered KPJ. 'Which is fine,' he continued with a yawn and a stretch, 'he'll get in some training for Showdown in Tokyo while he's at it.' 'Why does he have such sketch trust issues,' questioned Ceci with a touch of concern in her voice over her new friend. 'I don't know if I'd necessarily call the sketch, Sweetheart,' he answered, 'as he has plenty of reason for hesitating to trust anyone at all in that ring.'
