Happy

15 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/15/05 The next morning, Mindy got herself back to Truth or Consequence as quickly as she possibly could with her hangover like headache. It was tough guiding herself home from the airport in her pink 'lammy', but the scrappy nineteen year old made it happen. She didn't know if it was her wanting to get out of the blinding sun or the longing for sleep in her own bed; whatever it was, though, it got her home. Mindy slipped into a parking space and climbed out of the car after barely being able to sling her duffel bag out with her title. When Mindy hung both objects over her shoulders, she was surprised to find her boyfriend outside on the front steps waiting for her. His always present hat covered his face in shadow, long black hair flowing about thanks to the morning breeze. A lit cigarette dangled from his lips and he inhaled greatly before his right hand took the cigarette out of his mouth, sending a faint wave of smoke out into the air with a small sigh. He had been studying the cracks in the sidewalk before him when Mindy pulled up in front of him. The slamming of the car door caused him to look up and into his girlfriend's eyes, and it was that vision that generated a smile from the giant stoner. 'Hey there.' Mindy lazily formed a smile, and with what seemed to be her last ounce of energy, she used Markus' shoulders to vault herself up to his face and kissed him on the cheek. 'Hi, hunny bunny,' she murmured in exhaustion. To Markus, he couldn't get enough of that sweet voice- even if she did call him 'hunny bunny' of all things- but still, he never wanted to see Mindy look as terrible as she did that morning. Knowing how much she hated him smoking around her, Markus dropped his cigarette and stepped on it, putting it out. 'You okay?' he asked dumbly. 'Want me to like.. get you something to drink or make you breakfast?' 'Markus... make breakfast?' That was a first. Still, Mindy was anything but hungry. 'I just want to get some sleep,' she groaned. Mindy didn't just want sleep, either- she needed it. And who was Markus to keep her from that? 'Oh, yeah.. sure, sure.' Markus opened the front door for his companion, but when he turned to step aside and let Mindy walk in, his reflexes had to take over. The teenager collapsed into her love's arms, to which Markus wheezed from a bit of the fatigue that he himself had been hit with before carrying Mindy- duffel bag, title and all- into the house. Once inside, he swiveled around and slammed the front door with his foot and soon took Mindy to her bedroom. The second he reached the bed, he gently placed Mindy down to which she squeaked upon impact. 'Mm.' The tall man from New Orleans slowly took off his hat and flung it like a frisbee, causing it to land perfectly on top of the computer monitor at the desk across the room. Then he slumped down, sitting over the edge of the bed as he softly took Mindy's foot and began to undo the laces to one of her platform boots. Mindy wiggled around slightly on the bed, but otherwise shown little resistance as Markus slid off her boot and placed it down on the floor before moving to the other one. 'No wonder you're so damn tired, you have to wrestle in these big muhfucks.' Markus could hear her giggle in response, just as he took off her other boot and set it next to the one he put down before it. 'Meh... I like wrestling in those.. I kick really hard with them.' Markus chuckled audibly, squirming out of his own sneakers as well. 'Yeah, I can imagine.' With a low grunt, Markus flopped next to Mindy on the bed and placed an arm around her, holding his girlfriend close. 'You're just tired, right?' he continued. 'You ain't hurt or nothin', are you?' Mindy snuggled into her boyfriend's grasp. 'I'm okay, I promise. Just really tired... had a real bad dream at the hospital last night.' 'Remember it?' Markus queried. 'What?' 'Your dream.' Mindy laughed, slightly embarassed from not realizing what Markus had been talking about. 'Oh.. well, yeah sort of. I don't know, it's not as clear as it was last night.' Markus approved of his girlfriend's remark by nuzzling her neck. 'Well, if it was a bad dream, I guess that's for the best.' Mindy let that comment sink in, and smiled. Markus had a point, indeed. Still, she wished she either could remember her dream, or just never have had it at all. 'Eh, I guess you're right.' Mindy's eyes slowly closed shut. However, she did not stop speaking. 'Hey, Markus?' Markus squeezed Mindy a bit tighter for a moment and kissed her on the cheek. 'What is it, sugar cookie?' 'Am I selfish for feeling bad for myself before the others?' Markus blinked and tilted his head upwards a bit, peeking over Mindy's shoulder. 'The others... Michael and his wife and kid? Well...' Markus trailed off. He was a bit taken back by the question, as he was never really looked to by many people to answer such serious questions. Most people looked at him as a joke, and so when anyone talked to him, all they usually did was joke around. So when Markus was actually asked a question that required some serious thought, Markus was a bit nervous. 'I really... I really couldn't be able to tell you, sweetheart. That's not something that can be either labelled right or wrong, I don't think.' Mindy frowned, not seeming content with the response. 'What makes you think that?' 'I dunno, sweetheart, it's just something you need to judge for yourself. No matter what, though, I don't think you should condemn yourself for being upset. Everyone has their problems once in awhile. And Michael's a tough bastard anyways, I don't think he needs you feelin' sorry for him.' Mindy stayed silent for a little bit, before she sighed loudly. 'I've just been feeling so bad in general lately.. I guess the stay at the hospital put things in perspective, I dunno.. and now I gotta worry about this Jake Hix guy, and the biggest GW Pay Per View coming up and all that... I'm just afraid.' Markus' hand reached out and held Mindy's, fingers sliding in between hers. 'You don't have any reason to be, love. You're not Michael, you didn't have anything to prove out of doing this. Hell, you're the smallest wrestler in GW, and you've defended that belt of yours week in and week out. I couldn't be any more proud of you.' Mindy could feel her pale skinned face turn a faint red. 'Really?' she yawned. 'Of course.' Mindy grinned and leaned into Markus a little more. 'Thanks, sweetie.' 'No need,' Markus answered, kissing Mindy on the cheek. Mindy's eyes fluttered open, and she blinked them a few times. Slowly, her smile turned upside down. 'I'm going to lose next week, though, aren't I?' Markus gently put a hand on his girlfriend's shoulder and eased her over. Mindy's body obeyed and she rolled onto her other side, facing her boyfriend. 'So what if you do?' Mindy shrugged. 'Maybe fans would feel let down if I lost the title after all this, I don't know. I just want to impress them as much as I can, y'know?' 'I can understand that,' Markus replied. 'I can understand because I felt that way when I wrestled, too. But you'll also have to realize like I did, that eventually you're gonna lose. One day or another, some dude or some chick will beat you. And yeah, it hurts, but it's not the end of the world, you know what I mean?' Markus slid his fingers through Mindy's highlighted hair, hoping Mindy understood what he told her. Markus could still remember how much it hurt when he won the World Title in UCW, only to lose it after injuring himself two weeks later; the last thing he wanted was for Mindy to feel the same way when the time came for her to lose. Mindy looked into her love's brown eyes, and for the first time in a long while, her lips met his. Markus seemed a bit surprised at the display of affection, but he sure didn't bother to question it. And when Mindy pulled away from him, she verbally answered Markus' advice. 'I love you.' Again, taken off guard. Markus' was pleasantly stunned once again, as Mindy cuddled her boyfriend and held on tightly. They remained in their embrace for a good few minutes, before she slowly rolled back onto her other side and closed to her eyes. Markus slowly began to sit up. 'Need some sleep, eh?' Not waiting for an answer, Markus was ready to leave. That was, until he realized that one of Mindy's hands clutched tightly onto the wrist closest to her grasp. 'Yeah.. just don't leave. Okie dokie?' Markus looked down at her, and refused to do anything but give in to the girl's wishes. He eased back down onto the bed and placed an arm around her, rocking her to sleep. 'Your name is Rosalina now,' Luis insisted to the little girl before him. 'We're going to start anew, and do everything we can to make you feel better. Soon, you'll forget about these past few months, and things will begin to improve. I promise.' Mindy's eyes shot open momentarily. Her body did not move in order to keep from worrying Markus, but she was startled all the same. 'You lied,' she uttered quietly to herself, but there was no way for Markus not to hear. 'Hm?' The large man held Mindy tighter. 'What's wrong, babe?' Mindy took a deep breath, and embraced the comfort from the body next to her. 'Nothing. Everything... everything's fine now.' Soon, she fell asleep in Markus' arms, and the harrowing dreams would not return to torment her. She would find the meaning to them one day, but only one thing mattered to her as she lay peacefully in bed- she would no longer sleep alone.
