The Return Home (Part 1)

24 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-24-04 .::There was no way that they could have prepared for it. When they arrived back home, they had agreed to try and reconcile their differences. It was the mature thing to do. They were brothers, and they had made it through a lot harder times than that. Through death and blood, their friendship and brotherhood had stood strong and unaffected. But when they returned home, it would be put to the ultimate test::. The plane ride was not a fun one. The two brothers didn’t talk very much and both of them spent most of their time catching up on their sleep. They hadn’t spoken since the match, they had made the agreements of a short term disappearing act before the match. In his heart Jushin was proud of his older brother, it had been a true battle and he had emerged as the better man. But, deep in his own heart Ichiro contemplated whether or not he should have let his little brother pick up the win. After the long flight home, they finally cleared through the customs. It was not hard to clear customs, as many of the men were on their fathers pay roll. However, many of them were giving the Nakazuras cold and concerned looks. The brothers figured it must have been because they were away so long. In fact they were only supposed to be gone a week, but they had been gone well over three months. They quickly found their car, it was of course and as usual, untouched and in perfect order as when they had left it. Jushin pulled the keys from his coat pocket and flicked the alarm system, disarming the car. The two brothers stepped into the car and within minutes the black Mitsubishi’s engine hummed as the car weaved through the afternoon traffic in Tokyo. The sunlight gleamed off of the black hood, and the light seemed to echo throughout the glass skyscrapers as the two brothers made their way towards their apartment building. They pulled into the underground parking complex for the first time in several months. They were surprised to see that their usual spot was taken. They wondered who would be stupid enough to piss off the warriors of Nakazura, but they did not toil long. It could have just been a mistake, they made their way out of the car, Jushin arming the alarm as they walked towards the elevator doors. When the key turned into the lock it was a long missed sound, Jushin pushed his door open and walked in followed closely by his big brother. They did not plan on being there for long, so Ichiro did not bother going into his room. He threw his bag onto the couch and made his way towards the flashing answering machine. He picked up the phone, and tossed it over to Jushin. Jushin lifted the phone to his ear, and dialed the number for his automated messaging service, before entering his password. He smirked and tossed the phone quickly to Ichiro, who raised the phone to his ear quickly to hear the automated voice speak. “You have forty-seven new messages.” Ichiro laughed and tossed the phone back to his brother, who quickly began to scroll through the messages and paced around the apartment as he did so. Ichiro laughed and leaned back, closing his eyes for some much needed rest. Although he pretended to sleep on the plane, he had not gotten much sleep and his body was beginning to wear down. Jushin shook him gently, waking him from his sleep. He looked around groggily and regained his bearings he looked at the clock. It had been 45 minutes, he must have dozed off when Jushin was checking his messages. Ichiro looked stunned when he looked back at his brother, who was already picking his coat up off of the table and heading to the door. “Wake up, sleeping beauty. It’s time to go!” “What’s wrong?” asked Ichiro. “Father, isn’t answering his phone.” “What the hell do you mean he isn’t answering, he always answers!” replied Ichiro. “I know, let’s go.” The black Spyder once again roared to life in the buzz of the evening traffic in Tokyo. All around them different people were on the streets driving to their destinations. The businessmen and women heading home, the shift workers off to their evening shift and the grandparents on their way to visit their precious grandchildren. The two brothers could not pay attention to that now, they were on a mission. The look of worry sat on both of the brothers faces. They had not known their father all that long, but the time that they had known him had been great. At first they were angry with him about shipping them off to the mountains, but they learned why. When they were let in on the family business, they knew why it was done, and now they were unable to let the Nakazura name be dirtied. They arrived outside the small office and warehousing complex where their father did business. They pulled into the empty parking lot, it was the weekend so there were usually very few here, but today the place was like a ghost town. Not a sign of life in sight. As the two brothers stepped out of the car they withdrew their custom pistols. They each were engraved with their initials and the logos of the Nakazura Yakuza family. They held the guns steadily, and made their way towards the door. When they entered the building they were unprepared and disgusted by what they found. The interior walls were covered in blood, at least a month old, the entire warehouse smelt of decomposing corpses and rotting flesh. The blood was dark and sour and in some places puddle. The two men stepped carefully inside and flicked the lights on, the site was definitely disturbing. At least fifty bodies littered the ground, the men of their father and the men of what must have been those who attacked the complex. The two brothers shuddered as they walked in, but they had to know for sure. They began to make their way down towards the small office and luxury pad where their father always conducted his business. They walked slowly, afraid of what they might find. But, they hid their fear from each other well, they gripped the handles of their pistols tightly and kept on guard at all times as they made their way. They walked over the dead bodies and through the puddles of blood, finally they saw the door to their father’s office. Ichiro reared his leg from the ground and kicked hard with all of his might, nearly kicking the door right off of it’s hinges. The two brothers carefully made their way inside, and saw their fathers chair occupied by some unknown person. The body was dirty and unclean, very much unlike the way of the father they knew when they left. But, the curiosity would overwhelm them and Ichiro walked towards the chair. When he reached the red, leather executive chair, he spun it around.
