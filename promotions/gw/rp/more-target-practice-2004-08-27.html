More target practice

27 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/27/04 Aaron hated driving out to this field. He figured Brick would be here, blowing off steam or getting into a certain mindset like he usually did. It was something Brick often liked to do when he had a big match coming. The instant he heard gunfire, he knew he was right. Brick had purchased the field years ago. He'd been using it since he came into Global Wrestling. It dated back as far as the first ever Dark Rituals PPV, where he faced Skee and Derek Frost in a ladder match for the now-defunct United States title, a title Brick won that night. Since then, he'd made very good use of the field. These exercises were very cathartic for Brick. When Aaron gout out of the car, he saw Brick holding a pump rifle, standing a few feet away from cardboard stand-ups of Sage Sabbot, all lined up in a row. At Whiplash, Brick would be facing Sage Sabbot for the vacated International title. The last person to hold it was Sabbot's tag team partner Leinad Soma. Due to an incident involving Soma, a high-ranking security guard and a plate glass window, Soma was stripped of the title. Now Brick and Sabbot had the chance to win the title. Brick had gone for months without a title, ever since Deacon Holmes beat him for the Platinum title. Now was his chance to hold Global Wrestling gold, once again. 'You know, I hate coming out here, and I hate it when you come out here.' 'Yeah, well, you didn't have to come out here, ' Brick said, as he took aim at a stand-up. 'I tried calling you at home. You weren't there. I called your mother, I called Monica, I called all the guys in DMAK, none of them saw or heard from you today. Plus, you wouldn't answer your cell phone...' 'I had it on vibrate.' Then, Brick took a shot at the figure and blew off a piece of its left shoulder. 'A little wide,' he said, locking and loading. 'So I figured this is the...' Aaron was interrupted by the loud gunshot that came. '...one place you could...' Then came another gunshot. '...be, because of your match at...' Then came another gunshot. 'Whiplash.' For a minute, Brick stood still and silent. 'Brick?' 'I still remember that night, Aaron.' 'What night?' 'The night of Dead Zone,' Brick said, firing a shot into the figure. He then reloaded and fired again. 'I can still see the tears running down Mom and Monica's faces. I can smell the flames on the cell, I can still feel the heat. I can still feel the pain from when Sabbot dislocated my shoulder.' He punctuated each sentence with a gunshot, shooting the various mock-ups and then reloading. 'I can imagine.' 'And worse, he did it all in my hometown!' That was when Brick truly started to lose it and began firing wildly, barely even caring if he hit any of the targets or not. He just wanted to vent his anger. 'You were distracted that night. Your mom and Monica were there. Your nose was broken. The cell was on fire. And besides, you beat him the month after.' 'He's not beating me again.' 'He won't.' 'He's beaten me too many damn times this year. He's not beating me again! And not in New York City! Not my hometown!' 'He won't. Just calm down, or you're gonna make yourself sick! You think Sage Sabbot is getting himself sick over this?' Brick had been on a definite losing streak lately where Sage Sabbot was concerned. In his qualifying match for the Master of the Mat tournament, Brick found himself without a partner, which Sabbot and his partner Jesse Jamester capitalized on. At the actual PPV, Brick had won the first lottery match for a place in the semifinals and went on to face Sabbot, Josh and Venneman Pendragon. On the August 9th edition of Evolution, the two had their first singles match in over a year. Brick lost. The week after, he and Donny McNasty were to face off against Manifest Destiny for a shot at the vacant Tag Team championship. Soma and Sabbot had already defeated the Elite and now it was Brick and Donny's turn. The match went both ways before Manifest Destiny finally won the match. Brick determined in his mind that he would have at least one victory over Sage Sabbot. Or die trying. Aaron looked around and something he saw on the ground caught his attention. It was a cardboard figure of Leinad Soma, laying face up. 'Is this...' 'Leinad Soma? Yeah, it is.' 'Funny, I thought vampires couldn't be photgraphed.' Aaron then looked over to Brick, who was walking towards the Soma figure. In his left hand, he carried a wooden mallet. In his right hand, he had a wooden stake. 'Well, apparently, this one can. Of course, I still have trouble believing he really is a vampire.' 'And if he were?' 'Still wouldn't matter.' 'So, why do you have a figure of Soma out here?' Brick knelt down over the Soma figure and placed the tip of the stake on the left side of its chest. 'Oh, just in case,' said Brick, before he began to drive the stake through the Soma mock-up and into the ground. 'Just in case.'
