Champion~!

22 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/05/04 [Narrator] ... On the distant planet known to the universe as 'Earth', an organization by the name of 'Global Wrestling' has existed, offering the world talent beyond it's wildest dreams and superstars beyond it's greatest desire. Many champions have stood on platforms, looking to stake their claim as the very best in their division... as the very best when compared to their fellow champions. For almost too long, Global Wrestling has waited... and waited and then waited some more, like the dying patient would wait for life saving treatment at a low budget community hospital, for somebody that would ONCE AGAIN, raise the level of competition. They thought this would happen when the runner-up of 'Suicidal Idol' won the World Championship in a most thrilling encounter. They thought this would have happened when Michael Jackson; pre bleaching, won his titles... and then they thought that something would have happened when the person I don't recall, won the title that I don't recall. Unfortunately, nothing came to pass, even with this titantic and often obese athlete's dropping hundreds and hundreds of pounds in various fat-removing operations. HOWEVER... When the lightning struck on one... uh, morning, there was a hero who emerged from the wreckage of car. For that instance, he stood alone, desperate to accomodate success on his own terms and with his own skills. As time passed on, he became something of a force in the organization, throwing various people around and winning as though it were some kind of hobby. To round off his accomplishments in this short period of time though, he managed to win a Mystery Championship two weeks ago... and then had it declared the Television Title, a belt that would be defended on EVERY television show that Global Wrestling was to have. RICH SHERWIN~!~!~!~!~!~!!11 was his name and victory was his game. The self-proclaimed 'Captain of Retirement' rushed back into the light with a storming run of victories, showing everyone within Global Wrestling exactly how to become a member of the ELITE~!~!~!~!, exactly how to be able to market the word 'pwnage' and not get mocked for being a complete and utter moron. Now armed with his flat-chested cheerleader, Ryan Moore... Sherwin looks forward to Evolution of next week, where recently crowned Cruiserweight Champion, Max Roswell would challenge for his own Television Title. Regardless, despite all of that intense and otherwise wonderful information, we now take you to the current residence of Rich Sherwin; the scene for today's action packed extract from his wonderful life. The picture opens in the said location, the home now looking like somewhere a family could live as opposed to it's empty and dire trend that had been shown previously. Colour had been splashed across the walls to inject a little life into the property, furnishings placed all over the place to give character to the property and carpet covering the floor to detract from law suits against the property. Rich Sherwin walked into the scene first, clad in his usual attire of blue jeans and a white vest. His facial expression looked somewhat relaxed, the victory that came with the Television Title doing a lot to boost both his confidence and general ego. Now, it's not as though Sherwin was actually a rather egotistical person... he was just a little deflated when he didn't have anything that showed how good he was or something. As the scene settled with Sherwin taking centre stage once more, we leave you to view the working life of this apparently amazingly athlete... [/Narrator] '... and you know what I said to all of those people? I said something along the lines of... and you REALLY thought you knew the limitations of mass pwnage. That'll teach you to bet against the guy with the fastest rising stock in the HISTORY of Global Wrestling.' Sherwin exclaimed, letting out a hearty chuckle following his words, turning around to look back into his home, a cell phone becoming apparent, held in view next to his ear. The Television Champion had been on cloud nine following his victory, despite the fact he was now in a position where he would have to defend the damned thing week in and week out. 'Yeah, yeah... yeah, I know what you mean and such. Don't worry about it, I'm sure you'll come close to winning some of your money back should you choose to put a few dollars down on my bout against Max Roswell on the impending Evolution. It shouldn't be too much of a stinker for your coffers, I've already trashed the guy at least two times. He's nothing compared to me...' Sherwin continued, his voice beaming with a level of confidence never before expressed. As Sherwin spoke, Ryan Moore entered the picture; clad in a pair of loose fitting black jogging pants and an old 'Pretty Cool Guys' T-Shirt from their days back in PCW. 'Anyway, I've got to get going for now, I've got a friend over and the two of us really need to talk about a few things. Yeah, don't worry about it... you know the result before I even know the result. Just don't bet too big though, Deacon Holmes lost against the same guy last week and that was something of a surprise. Alright? Take care of yourself... yeah, say hello to your wife and kids for me. Later.' Sherwin said, removing the phone from it's position beside his ear and closing the flap before tossing the phone over to Ryan; who now sat rather comfortably on the large three person sofa that wasn't quite in the view of things. '... So, who were you just talking to? J Shit Diamond is still trying to figure out why you're not losing?' Ryan said with a smile, chuckling a little after the words had finished flowing from his mouth. Rich returned the look with a smug smile, happily enjoying being what was commonly known as the decisive factor in the 'unfortunate' retirement of that skankwhore. 'Nah, I wasn't talking to J Shit today. In fact, I was having a quick word with Steven from the bookmakers. Apparently, he decided to bet against me when this Battle Royale took place and well, he happened to lose a lot of money. I told him to bet well, but not too well on me against Roswell this coming week. Heh, it actually feels like this match is going to be a reward for me or something.' Sherwin boasted with arrogance beginning to find it's way into his vocal cords. 'A reward?' Ryan asked with a confused expression, trying to understand how having to fight in any of these contests was really going to be something of a rewarding experience. 'Well, just look at how stupid I've beaten that Roswell character before. I've thrashed him like I thrashed your girlfriend last Summer.' Sherwin stated, continuing to sound very full of himself and extremely confident of an easy victory over Max. '... You did what with my girlfriend?!' Ryan yelled back, his facial expression changing from one that was somewhat lost and confused to one that was full of anger and disappointement. '...Woah, easy fella. It was just a... joke.' Sherwin responded, holding his hands up to fend off Ryan should he have chosen to lunge forward or something. '... You know I wouldn't do anything like that to you, don't you?' he continued, sounding a little sheepish. It seemed as though Sherwin had done something with a ladyfriend of Ryan back in the day but he didn't want to ruin the train of good feeling just yet. 'Regardless, are you that confident of beating Roswell this week?' Ryan asked again, trying to sound the nerves and make Rich think he was just completely full of himself and not partially full. 'Well, given my current track record against everyone at the moment, I'll have to be a little careful... but I don't see why I shouldn't be the one to come out on top. I mean, I've just found my spacing in this organization and next week could be really exciting. I mean, I have the prospect of facing another opponent, perhaps one that I've not faced yet here in my short time with Global Wrestling.' Sherwin boasted, looking forward to the bigger and brighter things that were going to come along with this Championship victory. 'Just remember though...' he continued, '... if I'm going to stand any chance of getting through almost all of these matches, I'm going to need you about the ring for a little moral support... and I'll need you just in the case that anyone tries to break through barriers and do any form of damage to me whilst I'm in control of things. The last thing I could handle is losing this Title because some moron comes about and attacks me.' 'Ok.' Ryan responds, turning to walk away from Sherwin and back out of the scene. A grin appeared across the face of Rich, something of a small plan seemingly beginning to form in his little mind. '... Now that I've got hold of this little number, these people are going to see a side to their hero that they've never quite seen before.' Sherwin said to himself as the scene slowly began to fade into the confinements of darkness. [Narrator] ... and there you have it folks, the latest segment of Sherwin's life with a little cliff-hanger added to make things look a little more interesting than they actually are. Will Rich really have some kind of plan that'll allow him to defeat Max Roswell easily? Or should we just look at his win-loss record to see exactly how easy this first defence of his title is going to be? Whatever questions could be laid out about this first bout and more, all should be answered in a few days time... on EVOLUTION~!~!~! [/Narrator]
