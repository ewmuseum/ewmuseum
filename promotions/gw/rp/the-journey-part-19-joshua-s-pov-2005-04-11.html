The Journey: part 19/Joshua's POV

11 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-11-2005 “The tears of the mourning are signs of satisfaction in the eyes of the wicked” Joshua Curtis on 10 February ,1993 It was an expression that Joshua lived by during his time before he became the Owner of the IWF later that year. The expression meant simply that those whom lost a loved one or that cried over insignificant things were weak humans that had no reason being around or in this case had not no right being allowed to participate in the profession of Professional Wrestler. On that night Joshua began his tear through the roster and beat down a good portion of the wrestlers that were within the company to the point where the Owner swore that if Joshua could beat him in a match then he would walk away from the company and Joshua would be the man to oversee the federation from that day forward. Joshua took the Owner up on his challenge and faced the man one on one for the rights to the company in a solid steel fence that reached 16 feet high and had a sturdy as hell padlock on the door. If anyone was going to be leaving the cold steel cell it wasn’t going to be by walking out of the cage under their own power but rather they would have to be carried out on a stretcher if not worse! Joshua went after the Owner as if he owed him money and the man in charge of the bills did everything he could to fight off the onslaught that Curtis brought at him and succeeded at one point to wound Curtis and bust him wide open’little did he know that by doing that he was about to see a side of Joshua that not many lived to tell about! Joshua wiped the blood from his forehead and looked at the crimson on his right arm before looking at the Owner and proceeded to give him such a beating that the match was stopped due to the fact that the opponent couldn’t defend himself and Joshua was awarded the deed to the company and it wasn’t months later that Joshua had to go into the cell again but for a different reason this time! This time Tina Davis was the reason for Joshua to get into the fence as she had been acting unruly as the IWF International Champion and Joshua wanted to see fit that she remembered whom she was representing as a champion! After asking permission from the Board of Directors ,Joshua got a match signed with himself opposing Tina Davis inside of steel cage and the fans were treated to one of the most exciting ,dramatic, emotionally charged matches in the IWF’s history! The match went back and forth between Tina and Curtis with Tina getting Curtis in her “Davis Lock” but the young Englishman wouldn’t give in to the hold and told the referee some unflattering things before becoming the first wrestler to break the hold without going to the ropes and the fans stood in amazement and actually chanted the Owner’s name as he got out of the hold and then turned and looked at the champion with the same look that he had given that of the former Owner before he beat the mess out of him! This time it was different? Joshua still beat her up as bad as the prior Owner but this time he wanted to make sure that everyone knew just how serious he was with discipline he took the Canadian blonde up to the top of the cage and put her in between his legs before grabbing her by the waist ,holding her high above his head in a power bomb position and then lunging off the cell while throwing Tina so that she landed on the back of her neck! SPLAT! Nothing but silence overtook the crowd until Curtis crawled over to the fallen warrior and draped an arm over Tina’s chest and the referee made the count? 1.” 2.” “. “. “. 3!!! The count had be prolonged on purpose and the feeling was that Tina could kick out and the ref wanted to see if she could before he made the final slap of the hand on the mat. The fans didn’t do anything but clap and chant Curtis” name ,which was when Joshua snapped out of his trance and called for the paramedics to come into the ring and get Tina out of there and somehow the young lady was able to wrestle again! This past November was yet another visit for Joshua Curtis into the apparatus known as a steel cage for his chance to become the Number 1 Contender to the World Title and he made what was the final two and in those two it was himself and the man that he would call out to the point where people were getting sick of it? Christian Zenith! Zenith would go on to beat Curtis in the match but contrary to what the now former World Champion would contend it wasn’t a “true” one on one match in that manner’no there were many other individuals in that match that they both had to go through to get to each other? Which brings us to now? Joshua has been longing for the chance to go after Zenith in a one on one match because it was back in October when the two gladiators fought before but thanks to big man in the fed named Jackson the match never had a conclusive winner and Joshua wanted to get at Zenith to settle whom the better man really was and now he was going to get his chance. The day after Evolution when Joshua and Ami went back to what is now the “Complex” they were greeted by the news that Joshua’s time for waiting was over and that on the 15th of this month Joshua was getting the chance to finally go at it like men with Christian Zenith in a 16 foot high steel cell to truly end any problems that the two have had in the past. Joshua is going after this man not for his International title ,he could care less about that piece of hardware BUT the one memory that is forever burned in the memory of Joshua is the one when Christian and his gang confronted Gunner Addison and he shot her with a tranq dart. If Christian thought that Ken was pissed off about that he didn’t know how that would affect Curtis and now he is getting a good indication because since that promo aired because since then Curtis has called out and cussed out the now International champion to try and get him in the ring and now on the 15th he knows he’ll get his wish! There is however another piece of business attached that not many people know about all that much? While they are good close friends Ken Partridge Jr. once made a comment that Joshua took a little bit of an offense to and he has yet to tell the man from the SoHo district in Manhattan. The comment is something that Joshua has kept private and will continue to do so but at the same time Joshua felt that it would be necessary to give a glimpse of his life to the world in a manner that ,while its not conventional ,is something that fits Curtis perfectly. As Joshua Viper pulls up into the garage of the main house he sits in the driver side for a couple of minutes to contemplate. Its not every day that someone grants you an opportunity to go into this high profile a match against someone the caliber of one of your enemies and Joshua wanted to make sure that he didn’t take that for granted. Those who take opportunities for granted are usually doomed to be second stringers the rest of their life and Joshua knew that he wasn’t “second string” material but rather a guy ready to break out and show GW that he deserves to be there! As Joshua got out of the car and walked up to the front door of his residence he gently knocked on the door with the back of his fist and was greeted warmly by the figure of one Tina Davis. “Good afternoon Joshua. I trust that you had a good trip?” The Disciplinarian of what has since been renamed the “Army of Old School” asked Joshua and the masked leader nodded before responding. “I had a very good trip and I got a lot of thinking done. I’m thinking that its time for me to reveal my hand to Global Wrestling that I’ve been hiding since I came into the company about 7 months ago.” Joshua explained to Tina and she nodded in agreement before Joshua walked through the main foyer of the home to the kitchen where a familiar face was waiting for him in the kitchen and I think you remember him now don’t you? “Welcome home darling” Zack said as he came from behind the counter where he was making a tuna fish sandwich for himself and hugged his lover tightly before Joshua freed himself long enough to take off his mask and kiss his boyfriend gently on the lips before Zack spoke again. “Sorry I haven’t been around much Joshie but you know how things are back home for me. Mom’s been sick and I’ve been taking care of other obligations that I had to finish before I could come back to you my love.” Zack told Joshua as he rested his head on Joshua’s shoulder and Joshua hugged him gently before rubbing his back gently before speaking again. “Shh its ok sweetie I understand completely. I have a favor I want to ask of you if that’s ok?” Joshua asked his Californian lover and Zack nodded before Joshua spoke again “Would you be willing to become the manager of the “Army?” I need a good ,strong ,handsome man to be the man to help guide us when we need it and you were my first pick.” Joshua told Zack and Zack was so overcome with emotion he nearly bear hugged Joshua before saying “YES!” after which Joshua decided that he was going to introduce him to some of the other members of the Army that he hasn’t met yet and these individuals were going to be coming to Evolution with him on the 15th. After saying their goodbyes to the room Joshua and Zack went upstairs and all the way down the hall opposite to their room to the door that lead to the walkway that would take them to the house that had the people that would be coming with Joshua for the week and on the way Zack decided to ask Joshua about these friends that he made and why invite them into the world of Pro Wrestling. “So Joshua” Zack started as he walked hand in hand with Joshua “how did you meet these people that we are about to go meet?” Zack asked with great curiosity and Joshua decided that he shouldn’t hide anything from his on true love. “I met these people when the IWF was on a break years ago and I was invited to go to this base where they all worked and yes it was a military base but they were all very much receptive to me being there. The one lady that you are going to meet first is named Lucrezia Noin and she is SUCH a darling. Lucrezia is kind of like the female version of you in that you can tell her anything and she won’t blab it anyone else if its really sensitive information. Lucrezia was the one that taught me that hiding things wasn’t ever a good thing and that I need to be more open with people.” Joshua told Zack and he knew that he was going to like Lucrezia already! “Anyone else I need to be briefed on?” Zack said as they were coming closer to the door of their home which appropriately enough was marked “Home of OZ” and Zack took the hint before Joshua spoke again. “Nah everyone is cool here’just watch out for the one person that we call Une. She has a split personality issue we think and I do my best not to piss her off if it can be avoided ,so I suggest you do the same.” Joshua warned before be knocked on the door and Zack nodded as the door to the home opened and a man with brown hair wearing a blue and white top ,white pants with a black belt and black knee high boots greeted them and Joshua raised his hand in a salute as did Zack when Joshua realized whom it was that answered. “Good afternoon Joshua” Treize Khushrenada began “how are you doing today?” the OZ General asked as he stepped aside to let his associate in along with his friend. “I’m doing fine Mr. Khushrenada?” Joshua was cut off by Treize as he realized the error of what he had just said but Treize felt it would be best to clarify the situation. “Joshua we aren’t on the base anymore. In your world that is the ring you are the superior and we are the ones that need to address you properly.” Treize explained and Joshua took what Treize said to heart and decided to try again. “Well I’m doing fine Treize. Treize I’d like to introduce you to my boyfriend ,Zack Johnson. Zack this is Treize Khushrenada.” Joshua said as he introduced the former OZ soldier to him and they shook hands before Joshua spoke again. “Is everyone else present Treize?” Joshua asked and the brown haired officer stepped aside to see the rest of the room standing at attention as Joshua got the book that does the attendance form the counter and decided that it would be a good time to show Zack how things work in the other rooms of the complex. “When I call your name give me some kind of signal that you are here’Lucrezia Noin?” “Present!” “Lady Une?” “SIR!” “Releena Peacecraft?” “Present!” “Milardo Peacecraft?” “SIR!” “Very good. I trust you know that I came today because I’m looking to take some of you into the realm that I know called Pro Wrestling and I am going to pick a couple of you to go with me in this room and I’ve informed one of the patrons in another room that he is going to be coming with us to a show called “Evolution” this week. The show is a part of a wrestling promotion called “Global Wrestling” and they are a promotion that much like us don’t get respected much. I think the reason that they don’t get respected as much as they would like is because of the fact that they are a little on the extreme side and we are more “old school”. When I look in this room right now I see a group of people that is very much loyal to the cause that the I am out to champion and I need people like you guys to help with that.” Joshua said as he walked down the steps from the second floor to the floor of the living room which was when he would properly address each member individually started with former Baronet that he met years ago. “Lucrezia Noin. Its been a long time since the first time since that first visit that I made to your base and I still have fond memories of that day. I remember that on that day you ,along with the rest of the soldiers there ,treated me with the respect that very few had done and its for that that you should be commended. Lucrezia would you do me the honor of coming with me to the show on the 15th?” Joshua asked and the black haired beauty that was Lucrezia Noin responded the only way that she had known for so long and that was to raise her right hand up to her forehead in a salute as confirmation that she would be coming with him on the 15th! Whom was next? Joshua looked over the group carefully and eyed them all up and down before settling on the young lady with the braids in her hair that made it appear in in a swirly type of design on the back of her head and in a scarlet type of top with a black collar that had a gold button on it ,white pants with a black belt and black boots and this was the woman that they called Une. “Good afternoon Sir. I trust that you have been doing well since I saw you last years ago.” Une said to Joshua and he nodded to her before speaking again. “I’ve been well Miss Une. I’ve been doing my thing in the wrestling business but as I stated I am looking for people to begin to help lay the foundation for the Army and that is why I started with this room and you guys first. Miss Une?” Joshua stopped as Lady Une had already accepted her invitation to come with him on the 15th to Evolution by standing at attention and saluting. “Very nice Lady Une. When you come I hope to see the good Colonel that I know can be a pit bull and ready to strike on those whom oppose us.” Joshua said and the good Lady saluted again before Joshua was getting ready to move on when a boy with Platinum Blonde hair and beautiful blue eyes came in wearing his traditional outfit of a purple vest ,pink shirt ,black belt with tan pants and matching loafers came in through the door all the way in the kitchen that connected the OZ residence with that of 5 other young men that Joshua had also asked to be a part of the Army and they all readily accepted and yes that included this young man whose name was Quatre Raberba Winner. Quatre was the heir to his family’s business when there was a war and he was forced to disinherit himself from it because he wanted to participate in the war. Quatre is one of Joshua’s closest friends and someone that Joshua looks at as a “younger brother” type of figure and that is why he worries about him A LOT! If something happened to the young Arabian Josh’s world would essentially go in the shitter because of the friendship and bond that they have. Josh has a special bond with another particular member of that clan and that is what Quatre wanted to come see him about but before he did so he followed protocol and saluted the leader of the Army before speaking. “Sir I request permission to speak on a matter of some importance regarding one of the members of the Army in Trowa Barton.” Quatre said and Joshua granted permission to his friend. “Sir as I’m sure you very well know Trowa suffered an injury a while back in which he hit his head and suffered temporary memory loss. I’m pleased to report that he has since recovered nicely as Catherine has been taking care of him and he has requested permission to take my place for this week since I came with you a couple of weeks prior.” Quatre explained and Joshua turned to the rest of the assembled and asked if any of the present members had any objections to which none were raised but they did want to hear from Trowa himself. Quatre complied and went back to their residence to get the former circus acrobat so that he could meet with Joshua and those assembled in the OZ room. Now for a little bit about Mr. Barton? Trowa is the brother of Catherine Bloom and was one of the star performers with a circus troupe that he was associated with along with his elder sister. One day while performing a certain aspect of an act that they had worked on Trowa had hit his head and suffered a good old-fashioned case of amnesia but was still allowed to stay with Catherine and the troupe because the Ringmaster a) didn’t want to lose him and b) because it would be too dangerous for him to go out with that kind of condition hanging over him. While Trowa recovered the shows did well enough to keep them going but once he started feeling better Catherine was teamed up with him again and the two became one of the best tandems in circus work of all time! So how did Trowa become associated with Joshua and the Army? Well it had to do with the fact that Trowa heard that Quatre was being recruited and despite the facts that he was still occasionally lapsing in and out every so often was willing to help in any way that he could but as we all know where Trowa goes Catherine follows and honestly that didn’t bother him! Trowa was instrumental in getting one of the other members to come into the fold in Duo Maxwell and Heero Yuy ,which already made him one of the most proficient members already in the group and Quatre ,or as Trowa calls him sometimes “Q” was pleased to hear that Joshua would take in the slightly amnesic acrobat. Joshua has a lot of trust in Trowa and is happy that Trowa finds peace in working in a different type of ring now and with the rest of the faction around he never felt more a part of a family outside of the circus troupe! When Trowa came into the room to meet with Joshua and the rest of the assembled he was wearing a t-shirt that Joshua ,Quatre and Catherine came up with that read “New School is dead” on the front and “Army of Old School” on the back ,Wrangler blue jeans and a pair of 20th Anniversary Jordans. Catherine Bloom ,his manager or “companion” if you will came in wearing a wearing an orange turtleneck with a black sports bra underneath ,a pair of “Buddy Lee” Jeans and a pair of K-Swiss sneakers that were white with a blue stripe. As they entered the room they also followed protocol (yes even Catherine) and stood at attention and saluted ,which in reality wasn’t all that different for Trowa from working within the OZ faction at one time. After the salute Trowa addressed Curtis as he had heard the situation from Quatre. “Mr. Curtis I am fully aware that you are concerned a little bit about my health and how it might affect me when I’m at ringside for a match of any kind for the Army. I understand those concerns ,but I would also like you to know that I have been prescribed a medication for that and I shouldn’t have an episode at anytime during the program. If I do I take the full responsibility for what it might cost you and I will not ask to go again. I would however ask that I be given the one chance and since Catherine will be with me she will ensure that nothing happens to me as far as my blackouts and she has also been training so if someone like this Dimitri fellow comes after her she won’t be Gunner Addison with a tranquilizer dart in her. We may have originally been trained as circus performers but you’d be surprised how much that can work for you in the ring or just to defend yourself with.” Trowa reasoned and Joshua was more than willing to take the gamble on the young man from Mexico City. “Very well said Trowa and as long as Quatre has no objections we will be having you ,Trowa Barton AND Catherine Bloom coming to Evolution on the 15th when I walk into the cell to attempt to get my first singles title in GW: The International Title! Do I hear any objections?” Joshua asked as he scanned the crowd and not even Quatre objected as he knew his chance was going to come down the line but it was going to come at a different time. This time belonged to Trowa ,Catherine and the rest of the Army assembled and Zack knew there was one more piece of business to take care of? “To those whom didn’t get picked to go this week don’t worry cos there will be plenty of opportunities come with me and see the fans of GW ,whom are THE BEST in wrestling today! They know a good stable when they see it and when they see us they’ll know exactly whose time it is in the spotlight and I damn sure know that the foundations of this federation are going to shake! We got The Elite in Bill Moresi and my good friend Ken Partridge Jr. and Christian Zenith’s stable running around thinking that they are the stuff well this week coming its OUR TIME! WE need to show the fans that we are here to stay and to be a force within GW because guys I’m not leaving GW without a fight and I know Ami won’t either! You are the greatest batch of talent that has been and will be seen in the coming weeks ,months and years in the various federations that we are going to take over and it starts with GW on the 15th with Zenith and his crew and taking his International Championship for the good of GW and the good of wrestling period! Between the different parts of OUR Army I think that we can do it’let’s start on the 15th with Zenith!” Joshua exclaimed and the group threw their fists in the air as a symbol of unity before putting their hands in one big pile and as customary with all the ends of the meetings in what WAS the OSC only four words were heard as the scene faded out! “TRADITION PREVAILS’ANARCHY FAILS!” Oh yeah and one other word came from Joshua? “DISMISSED!”
