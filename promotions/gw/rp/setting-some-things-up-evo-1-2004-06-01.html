Setting some things up... (EVO #1)

1 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/01/04 It may seem like a bummer that I didn't win the TV title last night, but right now I do have other things on my mind. In the recent months I been dealing with a certain foe in the name of Noah Hanson from WMW. The man is somewhat unpredictable, but then again so am I. He wants to challenge me to a Choose your fate match, he must have been watching some old tapes of mine during my days in the Southeastern Wrestling Federation. I will respond to his challenge, but I will do it in my own unique way. You see a few weeks ago when I announced on Overload, that I would be at Fuel, I lied. I did purely for the fact of having Hanson second guessing himself and looking over his back. It's amazing what anxiety can do to you. Hanson knows what I am capable of, and he knows that I do make my presence felt in unexpected ways. Noah, needs to build an Ark and stay away for 40 days and 40 nights until Hurricane McNasty calms down. Soon so very soon, he will feel the rath of McNasty. Everything is set, I just got off the phone with a great friend of mine named Patrick, he's hooked me up with something that is sure to get Noah's attention very soon. Still to this day, Hanson has pissed me off more than he will ever know. What I can say is that me and Harv will be making a trip to Cleveland very soon and I will be making my acceptance to Noah's challenge be heard loud and clear. You know this house is also pretty lonely in here with just me living here when I'm not on the road. I been looking for a woman to be a part of my life and well I think I found her. She's beautiful and fun to be around. People don't give her enough credit for what's she done in the wrestling business. I just hope she'll understand my intentions. Oh well I'll just have to wait and see...
