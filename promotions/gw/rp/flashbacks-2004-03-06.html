Flashbacks

6 March 2004

Date: 03.06.04 The weather is much windier than usual, this evening. So windy, in fact, that it made me change my mind about stopping by the street market and getting some fresh fruits and vegetables. Every night that I'm home, I stop by the same gentleman's market. He also usually has an excellent assortment of herbs and spices, namely Basil and Dill. Right next to him is a tattoo shop by the name of 'SoHo Ink.' This is the spot that Josh and I got our matching Superman tattoos. It was a great night. We were both in neWave at the time and had just participated the first ever NWW show at Madison Square Garden. All the boys were excited going into it. We sold out a month ahead of time and we were all feeling the nerves once we got into the arena. For some guys, it was their first time in MSG. Myself... I'd been to a few rodeos before this. I had even won the WCW and UHWF Heavyweight Championships of the World on this sacred ground; the same sacred ground on which Shawn Michaels and Scott Hall made history in 1994 with an amazing WrestleMania X Ladder Match. There was an electric atmosphere that night... the locker room was buzzing immensly. Brock, better known to most as Ice, Josh and I sat in our corner before the Pay-Per-View started, each attempting to keep the other calm. I was going after the United States Championship that night, and both Josh and Brock had big name matches high up on the card as well. A lot of pressure had been put on us going into this... saying that we were the backbone of this company and that it was up to us to make this thing work, on top of that, I was once again in front of my hometown crowd. We each overcame the tension and won our respective matches, thus a celebration was in order. Unfortunantly, Brock had a pretty big meet and greet very early the next morning in Chicago, if I'm not mistaken, so he was headed directly for the airport, leaving only Josh and myself to do the celebrating. We went on to hit every club in a ten mile radius before dawn... drinking, dancing, mingling. We both had our pick of anyone we wanted and were treated like royalty. I believe, at one point, Janet Jackson joined in on the fun at The China Club. I had suggested earlier in the evening that he come by afterwards and chill out at my place... that way he could save money on a hotel room and would get to check out SoHo for a few days. On the way back home, we passed by a new shop... SoHo Ink. We decided to go ahead and check it out before agreeing to get matching tats to solidify our friendship. Josh and I were, and still are like brothers. Since we don't share the same blood... we would share the same ink. It's a pretty well known fact by marks that we're both diehard Superman fans, and we both chose the Superman 'S' symbol, quite fitting since I had changed my in ring moniker from 'Y2KPJ' to 'Superstar' just a few weeks prior. I went with blue and silver, and he with the traditional red and yellow. Oh man... what a night. We were sore as all hell at the next house show. We worked a tag match and were both screaming obnoxiously loud at every arm wrench and head-and-shoulder lock up. Looking back... I believe it pissed our opponents of, but the hell with that... we were having the time our lives. Who knew it would evolve into this. An amazing... an incredible union, the likes of which has never before been seen. Wow... I'm home already. Good thing I suppose... I need to put together a little promo for three unfortunate souls.
