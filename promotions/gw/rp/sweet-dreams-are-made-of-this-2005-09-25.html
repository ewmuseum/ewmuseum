Sweet Dreams Are Made Of This...

25 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/25/05 Prelude: Vivica's Awakening ---- Click... Click... Click... Slowly, the cerulean blue eyes opened. Peering into the darkness... everything looked like a blur, waiting patiently for the room to come into focus. She sat up in a daze before rubbing her eyes. The room around her began to spin as she lifted her head away from her hands. Pushing off the covers, she sat up on the bed. Heavy eyelids weighed down on her still somewhat fuzzy vision as she attempted to make her way up to a verticle base. After a stumble, she found her center of gravity. She pulled her blonde hair out of her face and tucked it behind her ear on her way to the door. The 10 foot walk seemed like a mile at 4:30am... but who could possibly be up and about at this hour? Even the vampires tried to hide during this time of the morning, it wasn't long before the sun came up after all. She had to check it out, could be bad news. Click... Click... Click... The door screeched through the deserted hallway as she stumbled out of her personal sanctuary. As quietly as possible, she slid the door shut until she heard it latch with a click. Looking both directions down the long gothic style hallway... it seemed to be vacant. How could that be? She listened closely while adjusting her pink tube top. If it was an intruder, the last thing they deserved was a free peek. Click... Click... Click... Sure enough, even in an awakened state she still heard the footsteps in the distance. Carefully she walked down the hallway. The sanctuary was ahead in the distance, and she navigated through the pitch black by keeping her hand up against the wall. Who could it be? Should I grab something? Her mind whirled through a series of emotions as she crept closer. The footsteps began to grow louder and louder as she approached. By now, she couldn't be too far away. CLICK... CLICK... CLICK... With her eyes now quickly adjusting to the darkness she saw a crack in the door ahead. A dull light shone onto the floor. Most likely from the candlelit sanctuary in which the footsteps still came from. She snuck up against the door listening to the footsteps which came to a stop inside. She a light squeak she pulled the door open and peered inside. At the end of the purple carpeted entrance, beyond the pews stood a woman with her back to the door. The woman seemed to be fixated on the candle-lit alter in front of her. With a sigh of relief she opened the door with a screech. 'Wha?' The woman in the sanctuary turned around quickly, her long black hair flowing in front of her face as she glanced back at a familiar face, 'Vivica? What are you doing up at this time of the morning?' 'I was about to ask you the same thing.' Vivica J. Valentine replied with a slight smile, making her way towards Lia. Saying the word Lia still seemed so strange to her. She'd known her forever as Shadow, and she was okay with that. Everyone once in a while she'd still say Shadow by mistake, but she didn't seem to mind that much. Vivica just supposed she was just as used to Shadow as she was to Lia. 'I was just wandering about... thinking.' She replied looking down for a second, then looked back up at Vivica, 'I was just about to go...' 'Wait!' Vivica said, stopping Shadow from leaving the room, 'You want to talk about it? You havn't been the same lately... I just want to make sure you're okay. I mean, I know you'd do the same thing for me. We're so much different, I just don't know what to do to...' 'It's okay.' She stopped her, 'I'm just a beast anyway...' 'That's not true!' Vivica said walking closer to Shadow who turned her back on her. 'You know better than that, you're no beast. I don't see you walking around trying to kill people. It isn't that they think you're a beast... they just don't understand you like we do. People are afraid of what they don't understand, it's in our nature as humans I suppose. I was afraid once too Lia... but I'm not afraid anymore am I? I care about you, and if I have to go shove a hook down Emoticon's throat and drag him...' 'That's not neccessary.' Shadow said with a weery smile, 'Although that sounds very fun.' 'I'd do it.' Vivica said very seriously to Shadow, who always admired Vivica's over-zealous nature. There was always something about Vivica that made her chuckle. She was such a smart and talented girl, but she had one of the biggest mouths of anyone she'd ever came across. She'd always wondered where else could Vivica be if she wasn't fighting? Certainly not a school teacher. 'I know you would... it's very thoughtful but I'd just like to be alone.' Shadow responded, 'I'll be fine, just go back to sleep.' 'Okay...' Vivica narrowed her eyes a little bit before taking a deep sigh, 'You know no matter how much I try I'll never understand you. You certainly don't help things any, I'd say if I didn't know better that you...' 'Just go to sleep Viv.' Shadow replied, cutting the ramblings of Vivica J. Valentine short. 'If you need anything, just knock.' 'I will.' Lia replied before approaching the alter again. Vivica turned around and started walking back towards the exit again, wondering what she could do to get Lia to open up to her and talk to her like she did to Lia. When Vivica reached out for the door a screech caught her attention and made her jump as the room erupted in a burst of light. Quickly Vivica turned around and her eyes grew wide at what she saw. The body of Shadow ignited into flames. 'VIV!' Lia screams were almost drowned out by the roar of the fire that consumed her as she fell to the floor. Shadow crawled across the floor as quickly the rest of the HG Sancutary erupted. Pews and all were set a flame as Vivica sprinted towards her fallen friend. 'I'm coming Lia!' 'NO!' Lia screamed, 'GET OUT OF HERE!' 'NO WAY IN...' Vivica was quickly cut short. 'GET OUT OF HERE VIV! GET THE OTHERS AND LEAVE!' Lia screamed before collapsing limply onto the floor. Tears streamed down Vivica's eyes as a pillar fell from the ceiling above crashing down between herself and Shadow. Viv wanted to save the others but her sadness consumed her and she fell to her knees sobbing. 'NO! DON'T LEAVE ME!' Vivica screamed as her eyes shot open and she sat straight up in her bed. Beathing heavily she looked around the dark room in a frantic blur. Beads of sweat dripped down her forehead as she tried to take deep breaths. Running her hands through her long blonde hair she tried to calm herself down. 'It was just a dream...' She said to herself, fighting back he rapid heartbeat and the adrenaline now pumping through her veins. Laying back down and trying to go back to sleep wasn't working... she just had too much on her mind after she met up with Lia at the McFarlane Estate. Vivica's bags still stood over in the corner. Her eyes remained open for the rest of the night. When the sun came back up and it was a decent hour, she had to call. It would be the only thing to keep her sane. ---- First Movement: Helplessness ---- The door to Vivica J. Valentine's room in the Hunting Grounds Headquarters flew open at 8:00am on the dot. A surprised Jason LeMaster stood there in shock as the door flew open and banged up against the wall. Vivica marched out of the room, wearing a pink speghetti strap tank top and a pair of black and pink flame pajama pants. With a frazzled look on her face she looked over at Jason, who still stood there confused not moving a muscle. 'Have you seen my phone?' Vivica asked him impatiently, her blue eyes seemingly staring into his soul. 'Uh...' Jason stuttered still confused, 'I have no idea.' 'Ugh... you're about useless you know that?' Vivica said as she turned her back and marched away with purpose. As Jason LeMaster stopped and store, Christian Zenith walked up behind him already wearing his trademark black suit ensamble. 'What are you looking at?' Christian said as he walked past trying to catch up to Vivica J. Valentine, who marched towards the main lobby. Jason simply shook his head as he watched Zenith trail Vivica around the house. Vivica stopped in front of a leather couch before taking off the cushions and throwing them behind her across the room. Zenith took a step back dodging the flying projectiles as Vivica moved on. He simply watched in disbelief as Vivica pulled open the drawers of a nearby cabinet with no success. She screamed aloud before she turned around, shocked to see Christian Zenith standing behind her staring at her from the other side of the room. Quickly she tried to regain her composure as she looked into the icy cadet blue eyes of The Midnight Messiah. 'Chris, have you seen my phone?' Vivica asks looking back at Zenith with a slight smile. Christian just shakes his head in disbelief before reaching into his jacket pocket and tossing it over to Vivica J. Valentine. A huge smile crossed her face as she looked back up at Chrisitian. 'You're welcome.' Christian responded, 'Next time... try your best to keep an eye on it. This is the third time I've found it this week. The second time on the counter in the bathroom. Why you need a phone in the bathroom is your business. But I don't want a phone ringing at me while I'm doing mine.' Vivica's frazzled smile quickly turned into the woman look of death as she flipped open her phone. Turning her back on Christian she began to walk out of the room while dialing. She knew it was silly, but when the phone started to ring she just wanted to hear Shadow's voice. The vision of her friend up in flames was still in the front of her mind, even after the long sleepless night she just had. Whenever she closed her eyes, the flames were there... 'McFarlane Estate, this is Alexis speaking.' The chirpy voice of Alexis answered the phone, and was almost music to Vivica's ears. 'Hey Alexis, this is Vivica. Is Lia around? I really need to talk with her, it's important.' 'I'm Sorry Vivica...' Alexis paused before continuing on, 'Lia isn't here. Are you okay?' 'Yeah... I'm fine Alexis. What about Dawn? Is Dawn there?' Vivica asked out of desperation, needing desperately just to hear anything. 'No, I'm sorry she's out at the moment.' Alexis responded, 'Do you need to leave a message or something? Is something wrong?' 'Fuck...' Vivica muttered to herself away from the phone sitting down before answering Alexis, 'No... everything is fine. Do you know where she went? I could go and talk to her myself.' 'I'm afraid you can't do that, she left a note for Dawn and Virgil but didn't say exactly where she was going. I believe there was something for you in there... would you like me to read it for you?'Alexis said. 'Yes, please...' Vivica answered, at this point in time just wanting to hear anything that would confirm her dream was nothing more than a dream and everything was fine. 'Virgil, I've left you something on the desk. Likewise, Dawn. And Dawn... if you don't mind, please tell Vivica not to worry about the match, if you intend on telling her that I am gone.' Alexis read slowly off the note in front of her before continuing, 'But you can come here and wait for her if you'd like. I could make you coffee or something.' 'No thanks Alexis...' Vivica said closing her eyes with a sigh, 'Just tell Lia if she shows up or calls to get ahold of me. It's important.' 'Okay, if you change your mind I'll be here.' Alexis said on the other end, sounding bored out of her mind clicking a pencil on her desk. 'Thanks Alexis, but I have some errands to run... I'm sure I'll talk to you some other time.' Vivica said before getting back up to her feet and walking towards the door. 'Okay hun, bye.' Alexis stated before Vivica flipped her phone shut and slid it into her right pocket. Vivica made her way towards the front exit of the massive building, walking past Christian Zenith along the way. Zenith stopped where he was going to turn around as she zipped past towards the glass door. 'Viv.' Christian said aloud as the automatic glass door opened, stopping Vivica second before she left the building. 'What?' Vivica answered, tired of everything not going her way. 'What are you doing?' Christian asked her, not seeing her roll her eyes because her back was still to him. 'I'm going into town... I have something I need to do.' Vivica responded, 'Why do you need anything?' 'No.' Christian responded as he began to walk away, 'Just wondering where you were going in your pajamas.' Vivica looked down to see her wearing her pink tank top and her black and pink flame pajama pants. She closed her eyes and puts her hands against her forehead, trying to calm herself down. Everything just pointed to the fact that it was going to be a very long day. Especially if she kept running around like a chicken with its head cut off. She turned around and walked back into the building towards her room, wondering what in the world would happen next. If she couldn't talk to Lia, the least she could do is learn something about Lia. As long as she'd been around her she knew next to nothing about vampires... other than the fact now that they don't like fire very much. She'd heard stories of them not being able to walk in daylight and driving a stake through their heart would kill them. So much of it had been made into stories... how would she know what to believe? As she reached out to open the door to her room she came up with a conclusion. What harm would reading up on vampires do? Maybe if she knew a little bit more... she could keep her dream from becoming a reality. ---- Second Movement: Fact Or Fiction (The Anatomy Of A Vampire) ---- SILENCE! The sign on the front counter read clearly in bold lettering. Behind the desk sat a younger woman, appearing to be in her mid to late twenties. She wore black thick framed glasses and had her red hair down. Staring at the computer all day checking out books and looking up books sure seemed like an eternity... but it paid her way through college, and that's all she cared about. Suddenly, for the first time in nearly an hour, the door opened to her small library. Minimizing her game of solitaire she had going on her old 1994 style computer, she looked over at the door as a young woman quietly shut the door behind her. As she approached the front counter, the librarian thought to herself that she'd never seen this woman before... which was an odd sight around Truth Or Consequences, New Mexico. Forcing a smile, the librarian loaded up the locator program as the blonde bombshell approached the front counter. 'Excuse me...' were the first words that were uttered by Vivica J. Valentine. Little did Vivica realize at the time, that was the first time she said 'excuse me' and actually meant it in a little over a year. 'Yes?' The librarian asked of her, 'Can I help you with something?' 'This is going to sound really odd...' Vivica said, 'but, do you have any books here about vampires? I'm not talking about like Vlad The Impaler, I'm talking about like real vampires.' 'Real vampires?' The librarian answered with a blank stare.. trying her best to contain a chuckle she felt rising from inside, 'They'd be over in the horror section if we have it. Is there any book in particular that you'd be interested in reading that I could look up for you?' Vivica was disgusted by how anything about vampires were thrown together into the horror section. Before she didn't think anything of it. Then again, before she didn't really think vampires existed. She considered herself more educated now. Now what would she ask for? Spending so much time around Shadow, you'd think she'd mention something about a book. Nothing. Although one time, she did hear one of those insults to humanity mention something to Shadow about a book on the air... 'Is there a Book of Nod? Or something like that?' Vivica asked, not knowing exactly where to start. The librarian took long pause as she began to type on her computer. Taking a glance over at the horror section, Vivica saw it was rather large for such a small library. Perhaps all the mythology about aliens and such around the New Mexico area caused interest in that. Or perhaps they were just bored because New Mexico was so damn boring... 'I'm sorry, I don't see anything called a Book of Nod anywhere.' The librarian replied, 'But we do have a couple books about vampires. We have some Vampire Chronicles by Anne Rice... as well as The Vampire Book: An Encyclopedia of the Undead. Both of those are located in our horror section in the last aisle.' 'Thanks...' Vivica replied, wondering how crazy the librarian throught she was on the way there after that look she recieved upon asking of the Book of Nod. Thoughts of a vampire taking that bitch from behind and draining her body for substanence brought a smile across Vivica's lips. Pointing out her finger and scanning the books in the middle of the last aisle, she stumbled across the encyclopedia that the bitch had mentioned a minute or so earlier. Vivica took it off the shelf before seating herself at a table to begin reading. Well known folklore shares that many vampires shy away from crosses, others say garlic would ward them off, and even others say that vampires cannot step foot inside a church. Over the years these stories might have grown out of proportion and are presumably fiction. A much lesser known folklore states that vampires cannot cross running water. Vivica reads on in her mind, Although this has yet to be proved or disproved, many philosophers have no choice but to treat this information as fact for this story has been seemingly unchaged over the years. While it is nearly impossible to seperate the difference between factual statements and passed folklore, the one thing that remains constant over all accounts is that vampires can be killed by fire. Vivica pauses and flashbacks of her dream fill her head as she closes her eyes for a bit. Reluctantly Vivica reads on. We have no choice but to believe this as an actual account of how a vampire may be destroyed. This would coincide with other myths that state that vampires are unable to walk in daylight... which has a direct coalation with fire from the sun. Other methods such as driving a wooden stake through the heart of a resting vampire seem to be dramatized through movies and productions, although this is believed to paralyze a vampire. Another method that is rumored is beheading a vampire, although it is heavily believed that vampires have learned skills or evolved to a form where this can be avoided through craft. Vivica closes the book and carries it up to the front counter where the bitch has returned to her game of solitaire. Making the girl jump a bit by dropping the book with a thud on the counter, Vivica smiles at the girl. 'I'd like to check this out please,' She said. The girl at the counter pulls out a stamp and enters something into the computer before checking the book out and handing it back to Vivica J. Valentine telling her it needs to be returned in two weeks. With the book under her arm, she goes back out to her car... where she'd drive home and spend a majority of the day learning more and more about vampires and vampire lore. By the time she went to bed later that night, her mind was at ease with the ability to protect Shadow. All this time she'd never even through about it, because her partner was immortal. Little did she realize how fragile she really was. ---- Thrird Movement: Through Hell And High Water The electric crowd inside the Global Arena could be heard echoing off the busy hallways. It was only a matter of minutes until Evolution went on the air, and everyone was starting off what they honestly believed was their night. Buckling up the last buckle on her black boots, Vivica J. Valentine was ready. She looked up at the mirror in front of her. Cerulean blue eyes looked focused and intese as glared back at her. Pulling her hair back behind her ears, suddenly the locker room door behind her squeaked open. Looking into the mirror, she smiled as Shadow walked into the room in full ring attire. 'You ready?' Shadow asked as Vivica J. Valentine turned around and looked into the eyes of her tag team partner, alive and well. 'Yeah...' Vivica answered as she began to do some quick calf stretches on the cold concrete floor, 'Are you ready Lia?' 'As ready as I'm going to be...' Shadow answered before cracking her knuckles, 'Let's go... I think they're waiting on us.' Shadow opened the door for Vivica as she walked past, focused on the goal ahead... with a clear mind after seeing Lia well. Shadow followed as they made their way towards the entrance to the ring. Ahead of them was no easy task in the form of the Global World Champion and the Global Television Champion... but they weren't going to back down. Afterall, Hunting Grounds had waited months to get their hands on the Mercer's... and finally Shadow and Vivica were going to get their chance. 'So where have you been?' Vivica asked as they came to a stop, 'I've been trying to get ahold of you.' 'I've heard.' Shadow responded, remembering the message that Alexis had for her upon their last meeting, 'I've been doing a lot of thinking, a lot of learning.' 'I've been doing a lot of learning too.' Vivica responded with a smile, 'No matter how much thinking or learning you have to do Lia I want you to remember one thing.' 'What's that?' Lia asked inquisitively. 'I'll be here for you and have your back... no matter what.'
