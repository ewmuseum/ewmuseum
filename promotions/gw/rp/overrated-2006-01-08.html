Overrated

8 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-8-2006 Overrated. Most of wrestling is overrated. The big flashing lights. The over-selling. The huge pyros. All that is just for people who can’t showcase their true talents inside the square circle. For me, I don’t need it. I don’t need the high priced showcase that all the so called “Main Eventers” use every night. I prove that I am the man every night I wrestle. Global Wrestling? “The Main Event” David Crisp is here. -------------------- The weather was as cold as the point of a knife. Blasting the cold winds through each person who was stupid enough to be walking in it. The bark, ripping off the very foundation that they were being held on. It was one of the worst storms in the history of Quinlan. It was being named, “Queen B*tch”. Yes it was that strong. Inside was a different storm. Opening up inside the town’s workout gym, showing the chipped white walls that still stood up from the years of not getting taken care of. The broke down piece of crap machines that the less fortunate people of the town had to use. It was pitiful. Something you’d find down in the Bronx of New York, nothing against those people down there. His eyes wore a shield on them. Covering the entire room as he sat at the sit, lifting the bar bells up and over his head. “David?” Her voice said with a concern look. “Yes?” Replying. “I’m hungry. Can’t we go get something to eat?” Responding as she grabbed her stomach. Sitting the bar on the holders and turning around to look at her, his eyes became something other then happy. “Hungry” Can’t you see babe I got to work out.” Responding with a little flare in his voice. “But I’m hungry.” She responds. Who was this girl. Was it his girlfriend? His sister? His’well we aren’t saying that. David got up off the chair and grabbed the white towel that was sitting next to him. Wiping away the sweat that was pouring down his face. “Damn woman. Your just as winy as your damn mother.” Laughing as he turns away. “Excuse me! You’re the one who married me.” Grabbing him by the arm as she responds. Twisting around, David took a look at his shoulder, the same shoulder that her hand was on. Trimming upwards to focus on his eyes, David gave her a little grunt, sort of to make her let his shoulder go. “Take your hand off my shoulder now before you loose it.” Disappearing the smile from his face. She slowly let it go, pulling her hand away from the shoulder that she was grasping. Twisting away and walking towards the wrestling ring that was there, David tosses the towel backwards, letting it fly in the air to only land right on the face of his wife. “DAVID!” Screaming away. “Shut up babe. It’s not like your hair was anything special.” Laughing away from what he did. “Oh you say that now but just wait till you see the bill on what it cost.” Replying away with her toss of the towel. It wasn’t seen as to where the towel went. David gave a little bend downward to his feet, stretching away till he heard a small little ruckus behind him. Turning around his eyes locked on the sight of a huge male standing there. His arms, the size of the hulks in the comics. His legs, the size of Anna Nicole’s butt before Trimspa. David gave a sigh as the guy stood over his wife, breathing down her neck. “Can I he..lp you sir?” She said, backing away. “You made my drink spill all over my cloths.” Reaching down to the pants that he had on. David knew he had to go help. God only knows what his father in law would say. “Hey Tarzan.” Taping the man on his shoulder. Turning around, the man pointed his big huge eyes down at the littler man in David. “What in the hell do you want little boy?” Smiling in his response. “Well I know your like taller then me and all and well pissed off but you see, as bad as this is going to sound, that is my wife. Now I know she is a little off when it comes to’well, smart things”.” Winking at his wife who gave him an angry look. ??But you see, if you mess with her I’m going to have to fight you and then your manhood is going to get hurt and well, we don’t want that right?” It was a joke for sure. David hurt this guy? That’s like saying that a local Dallas high school football team could beat the three time Super Bowl Winning Team in the New England Patriots. The Tarzan little man gave a laugh before responding. “You know what mister, you’re a funny guy. I’ll tell you what. You buy me another drink and we’ll call it even. Just this time, don’t bring your little pet.” Pointing at David’s wife. “PET!” David’s wife screams out only to get a response from David. “Jamie. Can you hush.” Responding to his wife. David gives the guy a nod as to say yes to the offer before walking over to Jamie. “Listen Jamie. I’ve got a huge debut match on Global with all those so called legend in this sport. I don’t need to get my pretty little face smashed up before then. So what you’ll do is you’ll go right over there and sit your pretty little butt down on that chair while I go fix this. Understand?” Pointing his finger into her chest. “Yes?” Jamie nods. Jamie goes off, sniffling and brushing her little body in the air to the chair. David turns around, right into the face of the Tarzan look alike. *SMASHHH!* Oh the sound of flesh hitting flesh. David went right down with the power of that fist. The man stood tall right over the laid out Crisp who was holding his jaw from the cheap shot he got. “Now that’s for treating your girl that way. And it’s also for the simple fact, you need manners.” Sniffing as he steps away. David rolls over, looking around at the crowd that was watching. Jamie gave a small smile before she saw Crisp get up to his feet. “Jamie!” Screaming out. “We’re leaving.” Jamie got up off the chair with a smile. Knowing that David got what he deserved. David walked over to Jamie was grabbed her bag and David’s before he took her right hand and walked for the front down. His pride seemed hurt as he and Jamie walked out the front door, leaving the scene to slowly faded away.
