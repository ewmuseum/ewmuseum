Winner and still champion

29 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/29/05 A man suddenly whacks me with a kindo stick. He leaves and another man stands over with raising the belt. Suddenly a man yells out winner and still champion...... I sit straight up in my bed. Hoping this dream isn't prophetic in any way with my up coming title match against Mindy Mercer. I remember that match as if it were yesterday. Umbrage beating me at Blood Factory for his Heartland Championship. The man hitting me was former GW Star Kenji Lee. A man who I got retribution against shortly after and was the start of being a heatless new comer and on my way to becoming a loved face. I decide to get out of the comfort of my bed. I head down the steps in my t-shirt and boxers in a groggy daze. I go and sit down on the sofa and start to watch TV. As I watch X-Men on Toon Disney. I've been so tired past few days it's sad. This cold is kicking my ass. I could win at Evolution and stand in the GW spotlight and raise the title over my head. It won't be to a thunderous roar though like it would have been a few months ago though. I'm an outsider here in Global Wrestling. I know they all look at me as nothing more then a spy. Doesn't matter I can't let that hinder my success right now I know they'll come to accept me eventually. Really? Who can hate a face like this? I continue watching starting to doze off on sofa. Freddy Kick! Ducked! Enziguri Connects! The Ace Superior Champion is going to the top rope! She connects! Then over the roar of boos all you can here is winner and still champion! I wake up again looking at the TV which now showing The House of Mouse. As much as I liked to watch I simply get up and turn it off. Second Dream tonight... A flash back to my Wrestling Midwest stint. Another blown match that I could not pass off as losing to a run in like I had before. At that point in my life I had my head up my ass and quite frankly still do. That wasn't the only time I blown a shot at the title. The other being against the same person. The same person..... and the next time we met I paid. I gave it my all and paid with an injured leg. I fought threw it but it was the same outcome. Winner and still champion..... I get up and walk over to the computer. I sit down and turn on the computer. If I'm going to be up this late I may as well look at some porn eh? I sign on AOL 9.0 yes sadly everyone's favorite spy has this. I type in my password and click the sign on button and wait. Moments later the connection log pops up and I read Attempt 1: Modem was unable to connect 'Stupid dial up' I mumble to myself. The 2nd attempt goes through and I here 'Greetings Nerds'. Yeah I don't use the typical welcome sound. An Instant Message pops up from Owen. [detail withheld]: Hello Looking at this screen name always made me chuckle. How can you not? [detail withheld]? Awesome stuff. [detail withheld]: What's up Owen? [detail withheld]: Us and Vampires. [detail withheld]: Hey, keep it down I work with those things. I keep garlic in my suitcases for that reason. [detail withheld]: LoL What the hell you doing up anyway? You have a match tomorrow night and a flight in the morning. [detail withheld]: Nervous and nightmares I really didn't want to mention that part even to Owen but oh fucking well. [detail withheld]: Nightmares? Lol Monsters under the bed? [detail withheld]: No, in the closet just like you. [detail withheld]: I can't help it..... Besides who wouldn't want that guy Josh. Lol JK I start laughing at my loser friend but you can't help but feel sorry for him. [detail withheld]: Hey, I think you two would hit off perfectly. lol [detail withheld]: :-) [detail withheld]: .... You scare me sometimes. [detail withheld]: Lol So, what about these nightmares? [detail withheld]: I just think I'm nervous about this title match. I've blown four title shots in Wrestling Midwest and that's what been showing up in my dreams. Images of how they ended. Two so far.... I really don't want to think about the last two. [detail withheld]: Damn... I'm going to bed try to get some shut eye as well and good luck against Mindy. Yeah that's something I need right now.... Luck. Bucket loads at that. [detail withheld]: Thanks man night. [detail withheld] has signed off. So here I sit staring at the computer afraid to go to sleep. Because the worst dream will be the last one and I will not let that happen. Syberus will have a bruised ego after he leaves Crossfire. I won that match just took a risk I shouldn't have but let a lasting impression on him none the less. I refuse to go to bed because I refuse to hear four simple words. Winner and still champion..... Winner and still champion.... Winner and still champion.... I will not be hearing this at Evolution. I will not be hearing the words Winner and still champion......
