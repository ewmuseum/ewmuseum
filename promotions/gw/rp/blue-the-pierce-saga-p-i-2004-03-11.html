Blue (The Pierce Saga p. I)

11 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.11.04 'No doubt there are other important things in life besides conflict, but there are not many other things so inevitably interesting. The very saints interest us most when we think of them as engaged in a conflict with the Devil.' -Robert Lynd Saint Akodo, the Saintly Brothers Nakazura, and the Saint Kakita stand with hearts aglow, ready to stand toe to toe and meet Le Diabolique himself, standing before an army of demons; demonic variations of John Zero, Deacon Holmes, Tyler Pierce, and Naria Xiou. The battle field that these two minions shall come to collide atop, waging there war for epic peace, never lasting rule, and power, remains a simple one. The Global Arena. The battles have begun, revelations coming and going all around. Plays with words and words with play, games with a goal, goals full of games. The signs have come and nested before our very eyes; the inevitable has come to pass. That very war of epic proportions is thrust upon us, the battles taking place at intervals, the victories meaning little or meaning more than anyone could ever imagine. Law nor consequence stands before the limits of either faction, both rushing with guns flaring and swords swinging. The Lion rushes at full force, Diablo himself standing before him, his sickle in hand and ready to strike. The Lion: Shishi, noble and proud yet fierce and unrelenting. His style, vicious and powerful; very little can stand against it. - He rushes, his fur moving in the wind, his eyes narrowed and focused on his prey. The beautifully thick mane bouncing with his powerful stride, each step forcing a new direction. Dust flies from the ground beneath his feet, his heavy and powerful presence intolerably more than the ground can handle. With Golden fur, perfect movement, and grace unparalleled, he races towards his goal. The first clash of the architects. Le Diabolique: Akuma, emotionless and quiescent. His form strong and unmatched, his abilities unknown and his desire ten fold that of any creature known before him. -The aura of hatred; the grandiosity of authority and power. An unquenchable thirst for victory rests between the lungs of this beast. The silken strands of black hair, flowing about his face and his forehead. His face strong and sullen, his eyes open and locked onto his opponent. At his side, his trustworthy sickle, gleaming with a reflection of the fires all around. Shishi ga Akuma o sakarateimasu. combat Beautiful Engaging Enthralling Gruesome Fate March 11th, 2004 - Approximately 5:00pm New York City, New York - The Echelon Tower The scene opens to reveal the inner sanctum of The Echelon Tower, one of New York's more recent additions to the already legendary sky line. A large rounded room is in view, a table in the center with chairs all around it. The flat screens are off however; the chairs are pushed in and the lights are off. The room is silent and dark, sterile and cold. The creak of a door breaks the unmatched silence of the room and light floods the crack, illuminating the silhouette of a man. It closes before more can be seen, the thud as the heavy door returns to its resting place and then the deafening click as the lock slams into place. The echo of foot steps as the lone patron of the sanctum marches across the marble floor, one step at a time, the echoes fill the room killing the silence but enhancing the curiosity. Blue. Blue lights suddenly flicker on all around the room, barely illuminating anything. The mere presence of light helps to distinguish the room but nothing more can be distinguished about it. The man's back can be seen clearly, a fine Italian silk woven to make the beautiful black suit jacket that hangs on his shoulders. His long black hair has been tied into a ponytail. Reno turns, his eyes scanning the room. The wheels of one of the leather chairs is heard as it's dragged out slowly and then placed so that Reno may sit. He leans on the edge of the table, his eyes focusing on the keyboard before him before he lifts his eyes. 'Terminal Pierce.' The words must be picked up by a sensor in the room as the computer before him lights up with activity, booting and quickly giving life to the screen before him. The LCD flat screen ignites with a brilliant blue, glaring onto the face of it's user. His hands hit the keyboard with his fingers quickly finding the starting position. A few keystrokes later and the brilliant combination of wires, metal, silicon, and plastic has gone hard to work, hunting down the particular request made by Reno. Seconds pass and the screen informs the user that it has found his query but it is having to collect and organize it. Another few second and the screen loads up the file that Reno requested. Pierce, Scott Salem Age: 22 Born: August 7th, 1982 Born In: Boston, Massachusetts Hair: Brown Eyes: Blue Address: 27 Pinnacle Drive Suite 010, San Francisco, 94109 [former]; 1 Pierce Circle, Nantucket, Massachusetts, 02554 [current] Family: -Father: Mark Thomas Pierce -Mother: Debra Mize Pierce -Siblings: Kyle Trent Pierce, Tyler Xaviner Pierce [ECH] -Spouse: Alicia Malay (Buchanan) Pierce [Deceased - 2.16.03, DOA] -Children: Aiden Scott Pierce [Deceased- 2.16.03, DOA], Eric Mark Pierce [Deceased - 2.17.03, DOT] Career: Ju Jitsu trainer (The Prodigal Gymnasium) [former]; none current. ------ STATUS: Recluse. ------ 'Scott...' Maverick leans back in his seat, his eyes focusing on the screen as his hand does the work, clicking from this page to a page with a mug shot, photos and etcetera. Another page displays images of his beautiful late wife and his children. A few more clicks... 'This is not my fault. I don't care what that son of a bitch said.' Another click. A news paper article from the San Francisco Examiner. The headline reads 'Pierce Family Slain in Ritualistic Murder; Father is Lone Survivor.' Reno's thoughts could only be perceived. Is he feeling guilty for the death of these three people? Or is it simply a morbid curiosity... or a tribute of some kind to an old comrade in arms? The words on the screen fill forth, being read in the voice of Reno as he reads them for what can not be the first time. He sighs, his eyes locked to the words. 'In the early morning of February 16th, Police units were called to the scene of a high rise apartment complex in a wealthy neighborhood in San Francisco. Soon after, the area was restricted and Crime Scene Investigations were on the scene. Ambulances were called for the four victims who had apparently been the target of a planned ritualistic homicide. The only survivor of these horrendous events is Scott Salem Pierce, owner of the world class Prodigal Gymnasium in downtown San Francisco and son of export tycoon, Mark Pierce...' Reno stops for a moments, his eyes scanning over more of the text as if it's of no importance to his audience. He stops, reading aloud again. 'Even with such a high profile case, there are no leads. No warrants, and at this point, no suspects. The San Francisco Police Department have released no further comments on these events.' Reno sighs an leans back in the chair, losing all forms of posture as he slumps to a lazy position. His eyes roll back into his head as he closes his eyes and lets his head fall back. His hand has fallen to the arm of the chair, leaving the screen to find alternate forms of amusement in the form of a screen saver depicting The Echelon Coat of Arms complete with fades and other transitions. Like this, Reno rests for quite some time. His eyes are closed and his form quiescent, his mind shut off or so it would seem. His breathing is calm and serene but there is something about him; an aura or sense that something is not right in the great land of Reno. February 22nd, 2003 - Approximately 4:30pm Saint Luke’s Hospital - San Francisco, California The scene opens to the clean and sterile halls of St. Luke’s Hospital in San Francisco, California. The halls are quiet but busy with nurses walking from one end to the other, checking on patients and dealing with other issues. One nurse pushes a cart full of linens up the hallway, her mind set on one thing. A second passes and a man steps into view; he's dressed in a black leather jacket, black dress pants, boots, and a simple red shirt. Unmistakably, this is Maverick Reno a solid year before the formation of the Echelon. He walks slowly down the hall, his hair loosely about his shoulder and framing his face. He catches a nurse as she starts to walk away from the nurses center. His tone is quiet an calm, yet worried at the same time. 'Mr. Pierce's room?' The nurse pauses for a second, thinking to herself. 'Room 319. His family is here as well, Mr...?' 'Reno.' She nods and motions down a hallway, his eyes following the path. 'Well, Mr. Reno, you go down this hallway, turn left and it's right on the corner. But please, he needs time to recuperate. He's had a very difficult couple of days.' Maverick nods and turns, walking down the hallway. He slips his hands into his pockets, his eyes searching the room numbers. 'Difficult couple of days... that's an understatement if I've ever heard one.' 115. The room numbers continue to ascend as he quickly approaches the corner. He turns and there it is, a private and highly isolated room. Outside of the room stands Tyler and Kyle Pierce, the siblings of the wounded Scott Pierce. Reno approaches them slowly and they both turn, glancing at him. 'Maverick...' Kyle's voice is accepting. He approaches Reno and gives him a pat on the shoulder. 'How is he, Kyle?' Kyle sighs and glances at Tyler, who has taken a seat on a bench in the hallway. 'He's... alive. That's really all I can say. I've never seen him this distant. He hasn't spoken a word, Maverick. Not a single word.' Maverick glances at the door and then to Kyle as he continues. 'Well, while he's awake atleast. He cries in his sleep and begs for Alicia... We think he's just reliving what happened.' Reno sighs and glances down, his eyes on the floor before him and Kyle. 'Is anyone in there right now?' Kyle nods and glances at the closed door again. 'Mom and Dad. They wanted to have a little time with him. Mom's been a total wreck and it's really not helping for her to see him like this.' 'I would suspect not. Do you think they'd mind if...?' 'No, not at all. I'm sure he'd like to see you as well.' With this Kyle starts to and opens the door. Tyler glances up at Maverick and nods his recognition. 'Nice to see you again, Tyler.' With this, Reno walks into the room behind Kyle. The room is a virtual plethora of medical equipment. In the bed, in traction, is Scott Pierce. His eyes are closed and his mouth shut, a silent aura lies about the room. Debra Pierce is seated on the edge of the bed, her face in her hands. Mark Pierce is standing at the foot of the bed, dressed in an exquisite suit as usual. He glances up as the door opens, first looking to his son, Kyle, and then to the guest, Maverick. He takes a step forward, extending his hand. He speaks in a low whisper so as not to disturb Scott. 'It's nice to see you, Maverick. I wish this could be under better circumstance however.' Maverick nods, his eyes focusing on Scott as Kyle leaves the room again. 'What have the doctors said?' 'Very little. And then what they have said has been generic. We don't know much of anything about his condition except for the basics.' Reno nods and kneels slightly, his eyes peering over the bed to the face of Scott. 'He has a concussion... a few broken ribs; both legs are broken and his one arm is ... shattered. There's internal bleeding but nothing that can't be fixed, they said.' Reno sighs and glances down. 'This is so horrible. I wish I had been there... maybe...' 'We spoke with a Psychologist, Maverick. That's the first thing he told us not to say or let be said; we're not to blame for this. No one here is. We can't let that ...' Reno nods. 'I know. Just idle regrets, you could say.' A silence fills the room now, leaving all but the beeping of the monitors. Reno sighs and stands again, his eyes resting on Scott. He glances at Mark again; he searches for the right approach... 'This is a lot to ask but, could I have a moment with your son?' Mark thought for a moment and turned to look at Maverick, his eyes caring and compassionate. Being hurt like this was something that he was not used to in the slightest. No one would be used to this... He nods and walks to his wife. He leans down, holding her and whispering to her. A moment passes and they both leave the room, the door closing behind them. Reno pauses for a moment and takes his jacket off, laying it on a chair nearby. 'Scott...' There's no reply although, none was expected. 'I came here to see you. I'm very sorry to hear what happened to you and your... ' Reno stops himself, not wanting to bring up any names or details. Forcing Scott to think about it won't help anyone. He walks towards and leans over the bed, his eyes focusing on Scott's. His eyes are open and he seems to be staring straight up at the ceiling. 'Scott. I don't really have any clue as to what to say to you or how to help you. I want you to know that I am here for you, though. No matter what has happened or what will, I will be here for you. Time and time again, you've stood by me. Helped me through everything and now you're here and I can't do anything. I'm so sorry about this, Scott. I feel guilty and you know why. We both know where I'm coming from. I know it was him and so do you. It seems that... it seems that only the people I care about are the ones who get hurt. The ones who take the brunt of this feud. There's no way to prove it, Scott. We both know that. But I will, somehow, make him pay for what he's done. He'll regret this, Scott. I promise that to you.' Silence fills the room again as Reno lowers to his knees aside the bed, his arms on the mattress and his face nestled into the nook of his elbow. 'Melissa is with me, Scott. Scott... I... It's all about to boil over. I'm going right at them and there's not a single way they can stop me. You're here... I've hired a bit of protection to watch the hallway and had it approved with the hospital. They'll be here this evening. Everyone is safe now. It's time for them to pay.' Another silence fills the room as Reno stands. He wipes his eyes and shakes his head, beginning to march for the door. He stops and picks up his jacket before pulling something out; he places the object atop Scott's chest and glances into his eyes. Scott's eyes move slowly to see it... The Ivory Mask of The Enigma 'Kill them, Maverick.' The words shock Reno; he turns, just seconds from opening the door. He steps away and moves quickly to the bedside, placing his hands on the arm of Scott. He leans over the bed, staring down at him. 'I'll make there lives hell.' The scene quickly ends as Reno sits straight up in the chair, the Inner Sanctum of the Echelon slightly changed. The lights are on and Naria Xiou stands over him, her hand on his shoulder. 'Maverick?' He looks around, his eyes distant and somewhat confused. He stands and brushes himself off, hitting the power on both the monitor and computer before him. He walks past her, not commenting on anything. Quickly, he exits the door and starts walking down the hallway, his eyes focused on the large door that leads to his office. Naria steps out into the hallway, her eyes on him as he walks away. He opens the door and steps inside, closing and locking it behind him. 'Some Demons just don't die.' The scene slowly fades to black. I'm Coming For You
