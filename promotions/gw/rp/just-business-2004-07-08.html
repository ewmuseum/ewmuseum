Just Business

8 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/8/04 J-Gonz held the papers in his hand and out of anger slung them across his office. The two lawyers dressed in black suits with the briefcases opened looked on as J-Gonz paraded around the room. 'So we can't do anything? Nothing at all?' 'That's right Mr. Gonzalez, the seize action was only for your match and not the entire match.' 'You fools! It was meant to be for the entire night! You mean he could have come out there and cost me the Man of the Mat qualifier? You idiots! You're fired, get out of here!' The two lawyers in fear head out the door. As the door closes Paco enters the room. Not in his usual attire, the gangster has on a white-beater with a blue bandana around his forehead. He was easily recognizable as Paco though. 'Que pasa vato? Why the long face boss man?' 'Idiots bro, they can't ever handle a simple task! I don't even know why they call themselves lawyers, I'm gonna get me a pinche white lawyer next time.' 'Well vato, it looks like I'm gonna add to your sorrows esse. We are having some trouble on 23rd Avenue with Carlitos and his men.' 'What kind of trouble?' 'Carlitos thinks he's above the rest, thinks he's calling the shots.' 'I think I'm going to go and pay him a visit later on today. Is Chocolate out there?' Without even answering Paco opens the door and calls Chocolate. He walks in wearing the usual, and looks over at El Mariachi. 'What's going on boss man?' 'Get the boys ready, we're gonna take a trip to 23rd.' No words are exchanged, no ok's no nods. The two men just walk out of the office leaving J-Gonz in there alone. 'I've focused to much on the wrestling business too long and things are starting to slip out of my nose. I need to teach these little punks that they aint dealing with some punk youth.. Those kids think cause they're balls are growing hair they disobey orders. I'm El Mariachi! I own this city!' A knock at the door startles J-Gonz who looks up to see Rasberry. Her blonde hair has been trimmed and she's wearing it an a way that is obvious to recognize. J-Gonz being a man doesn't notice. 'Baby! Are you ok? You look bad.' 'It's nothing baby, just business.' 'Maybe you need a vacation baby.' 'A vacation mamasita? C'mon babe, I'm at the top of the world right now. Profits are strong, I got the gold around my waste.' 'You've just been so tired lately babe.' 'It's guys like Train and Sherwin mammi. Those punks are crossing the line and they're making things more personal. I've beat that Sherwin fool twice and he still won't get it through his head!' 'So what are you going to do about him baby?' 'Them mammi! Train and Sherwin are tough guys in the ring, they think they're something outside of the ring too. But them fools don't know who I am mammi! I'm El Mariachi, I'll put a cap in their head!' Another knock causes J-Gonz to slide his coat on. Standing up he gives Rasberry a kiss and a face of disappointment comes over her. She's nods as J-Gonz tells her he'll be in late tonight. She grabs his Television Title and heads out behind the men.
