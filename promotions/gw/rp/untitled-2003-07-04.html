(untitled) Ronnie Long Global Wrestling Promo #390 Date: 7-04-03 Location: Long's apartment They have said that the wheel that squeaks is the one that gets the most grease… and apparently, this is true. It is impossible for Ronnie Long to think about his match this week, and not be pleased. A year and a half in the undercard of GW… GWF at the time… always recognized as talented, as someone who could go places, but never able to get it together when it really counted, when it was time to put all the chips on the line, go out there, fight, and win. He couldn’t get that win, couldn’t bring the gold when it counted. It frustrated him… because, you climb, you climb to reach the top, and you get tired halfway there. The difference between a true great, and everyone else, is the ability to keep going. And Long, when he was last in the GW, did not have that ability. Does he now? That’s what we’re going to find out in the coming weeks. ******* Ronnie Long came back to GW, intending to fight The Nevermore. He saw a stable with a crushing grasp on the organization, and decided to do what he does best, one of the things that has, as of late, come naturally to him – set himself against the machine. Perhaps it’s just a mental hangup, or even his imagination, but Long always supposed that he did better, against so called impossible odds. All his greatest successes, are the ones in which he was never expected to achieve. But now the Nevermore has left, and Long doesn’t know what to do with himself. He was brooding, one day, thinking about returning to the indys, when his girlfriend, Deanna D’aimore, walked into the living room. “Ronnie, guess what?” Ronnie, his attention given to the television, showing one of the older James Bond movies, grunts, hardly looking up. He doesn’t care… and when dealing with Deanna, the words ‘guess what’ rarely proceed something good. “Ronnie!” This time, Deanna speaks much more sharply, and Long looks up from the television, regarding Deanna through the tangles of greasy black hair that hang haphazardly over his face. He makes a half hearted attempt at a joke. “Sorry, dear, didn’t see… you…” He trails off. Long’s not stupid, and he can tell that this isn’t going to do him any good whatsoever. Deanna walks behind the sofa, placing her hands on Ronnie’s shoulders. Bending forward, she whispers into his ear. “You’re still depressed because you don’t get to fight the Nevermore single handedly?” Ronnie shakes his head, briefly. It’s something more than that… it’s the same lack of direction, lack of ambition, that’s plagued his wrestling career since two months after is started. The inability to give a focused, sustained effort on achieving a goal. “Well, Ronnie, you’re gonna like this… your next match is in, they just telegrammed it to us.” A grunt, and Deanna’s voice once again becomes annoyed. “In Global Wrestling? You know, your job? The place you go and fight to earn us money? Your next fight, I have the details on it. Ronnie, are you listening?” Ronnie clicks the TV off. Standing and shaking his head, he turns around and takes the telegram out of Deanna’s hand. “Mark Kullypso… I remember that guy. Former stablemate, if I’m not mistaken… also, I think I beat him once, didn’t I?” Deanna shrugs. “You might have, it’s been a while. Either way, you CAN beat him. You’ve proved to me before, Ronnie, and really, to anyone who remembers you from last time around… if you want to win, you win… back to back victories over The Mercenaries, remember? You just need a reason to want to… and look at this.” Ronnie looks at the yellow sheet of paper. Scanning the information about Mark Kullypso – his current height and weight, his finishers and descriptions of them, and other useful information, he reads the bottom line of the telegram.” This match is the first round of a tournament for the recently vacated International Title. As Ronnie stands there reading the paper, Deanna slowly walks out of the room. Long sits back down on the sofa, but he doesn’t turn the TV back on. The last time Ronnie held gold was in the IWA, back in March of 2002, and that was a tag title. The last time he really held singles gold was all the way back in the early winter of 2001, when he was the GW Television champion. The high point of his career, unless you count when Neo smashed him with a razor wire wrapped chair and he shook it off. “Titles already… squeaky wheel indeed.”
