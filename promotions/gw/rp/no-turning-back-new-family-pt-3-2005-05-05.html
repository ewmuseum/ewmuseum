No Turning Back/New Family pt 3

5 May 2005

Date: 5.5.05 His body is kept warm and numb from the cold by the hatred which feuled every step he had taken in life. A life so filled with hatred that when love had landed in his arms, he didn't understand how to deal with it for so long. But with that love gone again, the hatred is his fire against the snow which falls around him. Although it was Spring, it still felt very much like Winter as the land was covered with the white covering of snow that had fallen from the sky in such a graceful manner. Like ballet dancers fluttering around, skillfully with every movement perfect and flowing, the snow drifted from above and rested on what ever it came in contact with, including the clothes of The Harvester of Minds. His journey had been a long one, and wasn't over yet as the man in whom he needed to speak to was a man who had man a living out of never being found when he didn't want to be. It was for this very reason that Salaok had hired him in the first place to work for his Nevermore, and would be the very reason as to why he sought his hidden image once again. For years they had stood side by side in the wars in which The Lair had taken part of against those who had dared to stand in their way of becoming the most powerful of the underworld groups of the time. With a fearlessness that had been spawned from their teachings as youth, these men would stop at nothing to get any job done that was given to them and in turn, a circle of respect was created. Each man respected what the others could do, even those who were contracted to work with The Lair, but not officially apart of it. The smell and stains of blood are hard to remove from clothes and skin, and in turn, they became so used to the smell, that when the air was clear, to them it smelt foul. The battlefields had been their homes for many a year that most had forgotten a life without death which had made their lives hard when the wars were over. Not willing to let go of the old days where The Underworld was the control of the nations and they had the right to do as they pleased. The adjustment to the world of the working, proved too much for some and it wouldn't be long until The Nevermore had adjusted the world to their ways. Death wasn't something that Salaok had found a pleasant happening, but was something that regardless, needed to be done from time to time. However, he had often found to easier to simply hire someone to dot he work for him and it was through these connections that he soon found himself rising up the ladder of control faster than most had expected. Seen as having his own army to a certain extent, Salaok would gain power quickly and use it in taking down those who opposed him and more importantly, those who found a need to attack his family. ---- Meanwhile, in Detroit..... I raise my head from the pillow on the arm of the couch. My eyes dart quickly around the room, not really looking for anything particular. My legs swing over the cushions, my feet touching the carpet. Before long, a hand covers my eyes. My hands instinctively shoot up, and upon contact I know the wrists. 'Did you have a good nap, hun?' asks my lovely wife. 'Yeah, what time is it?' I reply. 'I thought you said you were going to take a day off from the car. Derek you need to relax. You're spending so much time with that Chevelle, that I'm starting to get jealous. Last night, you were shifting gears in your sleep' A grin covers my face, and I think to myself. Should I say it, or not? Yeah, I'll do it. 'Well, maybe if you had an ass as stiff as the one I'm trying to fit under that frame, I'd spend more time with you.' Cindy knows I'm joking, so she simply slaps my shoulder and walks around the couch. As soon as she gets around in front of me, she sits next to me with her legs over my lap. 'I've been thinking.' 'Uh oh, this could be deadly,' I mutter. 'Shut up! Anyway, while you were sleeping, I was thinking about everything that has happened lately. Grandpa, and then your parents, and the news that Daddy and Uncle Johnny gave us not long ago.' 'And what did you think of all that?' 'Well, I also thought about how Dr. Mott said, about how you shouldn't be having any more hallucinations.' 'Um, maybe this isn't the time, but I think there's a leprachaun dancing in the fireplace.' I say, pointing across the room. 'Derek stop it, I'm being serious.' 'Sorry hun.' 'Anyway, I've decided that theres nothing that would make my father happier than him knowing that I have accepted him for his new......executive position.' 'So you're ok with being the daughter of a mob boss?' 'Not when you put it that way, no. Make a promise right now. Promise me you'll never refer to my father as a mob boss, crime lord, or anything else like that. He's a businessman. He said it himself, he's going to try to make CrusInc totally legitimate. I think Daddy may be well suited for this.' 'Ok, hun. From now on, you'll be my 'executive princess' instead of my little 'mob princess'.' Cindy smiles and lays her head on my shoulder. ---- Each step, heavier than the last as his legs begin to tire from the weight of the snow attaching itself to his boots as he walked. It had been some time since the two former foes and then allies had seen each other and the point of impact whent hey come face to face will be an unknown until it occurs. Much like the time when they had first come face to face without the intention of killing each other. It had been many years ago as Salaok remembered, and started with him counched in the bushes outside of what seemed like a manion by the sheer size of the building. Watching as the final light in the building was turned off before he rose to his feet, only to then feel the breath of a man standing behind him shooting straight down his neck. Not wanting to move, and yet, it was killing him to stay still, he waited for some sort of sign that the man behind him was either friend of foe. As a hand planted itself on Salaok's shoulder, he could feel the size of the man and it seemed to be basically the same size as Salaok which was strange as Salaok was not your average size man. Standing at six foot and six inches, Salaok knew that who ever was behind him wasn't going to be someone to be taken lightly but before he has a chance to turn around to see who had found him, the man's voice spoke quietly into his ear with confidence and power. 'Worry not Salaok, for you are not why I am here tonight.' The voice was one he knew, and one he had learned to despise over the years while The Lair had been involved in many a war. The voice, powerful, controlled and most importantly, cold. A voice which Salaok had heard on the battlelfields. A voice he had learnt to hate. A voice he had learnt to respect. The voice of Leinad Soma. 'Then what are you here for Soma?' Salaok responce matched the confidence of Soma, making it clear although he had not laid eyes on him as yet, he knew exactly who it was who stood behind him. The two men, were equal in most ways and yet, their methods and trains of thought at times could be miles apart which made them the best of foes, but for tonight, it would make them the best of allies. 'I dare say judging by the way you stood up as the light went off, that I am here for the same reason you are. Death in all it's glory.' Soma's voice lifted in spirit slightly with the word of death as even Salaok knew, it was something that the man enjoyed greatly, unlike Salaok. Although he would do the task that was required for him, Salaok had never taken enjoyment out of the death of another human being which had made his position as a hit man seem almost strange to those who were in the business. 'Business or pleasure?'Salaok responds, wanting to know if Soma's been contracted for this or has something in this himself. 'It's a personal thing, you?' 'A contract from Antonio Crusini.' Soma seems to scof at the fact that Salaok is still working for money and responds accordingly. 'You've gone so far as to work for the Mafia? Where is the art in that Harvester?' 'The art is in the green which they will hand over to me one this is finished. But with us both here for the same reason, I give you my word now that I will not make a move against you while you are here. The job comes first as you well know.' 'And I can respect that, but not the people who give you the contract. I know that death is something you do not enjoy Salaok, I've seen it in your eyes on the battlefields. You'll take part, but you take no pleasure from the act with confuses me to some point. What if I were to take care of business tonight, and you went home. The death would still have occured and the money that you so shamelessly seek would still be yours for the taking from the family.' Soma, wanting to have no part of Salaok in this kill, clearly was trying to rid himself of the Harvester's presence but there was just one small problem which Salaok was about to reveal. 'They want proof of his death. Mainly the part which rests on top of his shoulders.' Almost surprised at the fact that Salaok was going to cut off the head of the man who rests inside, Soma has to ask just to clarify. 'You, were going to cut of his head?' 'That's what the contract says. We both want this man dead, so why don't we just go up there and take care of the situation at hand. You can kill him for whatever reason it is that you want him dead and I will cut off the head and collect the money.' 'Well then, after you!' Salaok turns around to look at Soma for the first time, as if to question his motives for a moment, but there was no sign of anything on Soma's face. A dead stare up at the window where both men knew their target lay, hopefully sleep. Side by side, the two old enemies walk towards the house, both know of what will take place in the next five minutes. One man looking forward to the personal glory and enjoyment of the kill, the other merely there to do a job. ---- But that memory was of a long time ago and as he nears the building which he seeks, the visison of those dead eyes enters his head. They were watching him, even now before he had gotten to the company in which Soma had spent so many years of his life creating to become one of the wealthiest men in America. But what reason was there for Salaok to seek out Soma? Stepping under the cover of the buildings around him, the snow finally stops falling onto him, making the last few feet of his trip less unbearable. Reaching his hand out in front of him to push open the door, it suddenly opens for him via the hand of the man standing on the other side. There they stood, once again in silence and yet with respect. In a situation which seemed almost strange, both men extended a hand and cemented something that most never would have dreamed of. An alliance and an end to the wars in which they took part in so many years ago. ---- Bobby Cruse's office, in Cruse Manor, Sterling Heights, Michigan. The phone on the desk rings. 'This is Bobby.' 'Yes, this is Phillip Cartier, CEO of Primedia. We had a contract with your father, rest his soul. I am primarily calling to request a meeting with you, a chance to renegotiate our deal.' 'That will be unnecessary, Mr. Cartier. My father kept me closely informed of most of his business dealings, and in some cases asked me to advise him on them. I am aware of the contract made between my father and your company. Let me assure you, I will uphold my father's signature as if it were my own.' 'Still, sir, I must request your signature accompany his on this contract. May I fax you a copy to be signed and returned?' 'Of course, I will sign and fax it immediately back to you.' The fax machine rings once, then beeps a few times. The paper begins to shift, and soon enough Bobby has a copy of the contract on his desk. He grabs a pen and signs his name below his fathers. His father's signed name: Antonio Crusini.
