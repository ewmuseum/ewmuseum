Introduction (Part1) PPV1

28 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-28-03 For the life of me I cannot remember what made us think that we were wise and we'd never compromise, for the life of me I cannot believe that we'd ever die for these sins, we were merely freshman I have never been in love’I have only attempted it several times, I am a man with a cause, a passion, a lust, for women yes’but that is now on the back burner, something else captivates my dreams and sends chills up my spine, do you know what it is? I do? The clouds rolled overhead, grey, black, a gun smoke highlight, a gust of wind from the north, the smell of precipitation ever present in the atmosphere. I stood alone, by myself on this bleak afternoon, my knees hurt, my knees always hurt, but I paid it no mind as I continued my self driven stroll to make myself better, no’to make myself the best, I have proved it time and time again, but never when it really counts. I have had many chances that the GW title but every time I just blew it off, I wasn’t worried about being the Champion of Global Wrestling, but as I have aged and became more mature I have learned that not everyone has the talent that I do, a talent that I have been wasting up until now, I realize that I am no longer looking up at the top of the mountain’I am at the top, looking down on everyone else striving to be where im at. My mind was drifting off, my strides became less and less’I stopped. I raised my hands up and put them atop my head, eyes closed, mind open, heart racing, I took in a long breath of fresh air, the knife that was being driven in my ribs has now subsided, my white YB shirt soaked with perspiration. My baby blue eyes open asphyxiated on the sky above, grey clouds turning an eerie purple black as they roll and tumble over each other in the heavens’my eyes closed? “Your really going to be something great kid’I promise you that’I can see it in your eyes” My eyes were sunk back into my head, nothing but pain and agony, the feeling of self depression as I sat there on that hot wrestling mat, Eye of The Tiger blared from the stereo system set up in the corner of the large wrestling room, I was going to die, four hours’that’s how long it took to lose seven pounds, I couldn’t eat’I couldn’t drink, and now this guy is going to stand before me and tell me I am going to be something great, in most cases I would be over zealous that this man’my wrestling coach was telling this to me’but not now’no not now’I couldn’t even stand up, I was stuck there’no way out, did I wrestle because I liked it? No’I did it because it was a passion’a want’a need. I was Seventeen years old’what did the future hold for me then? The Oklahoma State Championship that’s what it held for me, it was reserved for me and only me. **Kaboom** A crack of thunder snapped me out of the past as I opened my eyes to see lightning streaking across the black sky, I took in a deep breath and continued to run, gravel shifted and crumbled under my every step, the shock of the ground pulsated up my calves, to my knees and into my thighs as a blustery gust of wind hit me, the hairs on my arms stood on end, a chill ran up my spine and goose bumps formed on my forearms, but like everything before I paid it no mind and continued to run’To where? I don’t know’but I was running nevertheless’as my mind began to drift into the past once again “Jay meet Derek Frost, your new tag team partner” Were the words of Johnny Cruse, his brown hair swayed on his forehead obscuring his hazel eyes as I looked across at this guy named Derek Frost, he looked brash arrogant and cocky no less, I out stretched my hand, he reached back and took my hand in his and as we shook I felt something different, this guy with ice blue hair soon became my best friend..in the ring’and out? The freezing drops of a beginning rainstorm brought me back to reality, thunder once again cracked in the distance followed by a bolt of lightening, my paces slowed again..i stopped rain drops blended with sweat, my breathing was heavy, my body ached, I could feel my heart pumping blood to every corner of my body. I raised my hands to my face and wiped the water and sweat from my brow as I ran my hands through my golden blonde hair, I was done running’I looked to the Arkansas River to my right being pelted by water falling from the heavens, I looked to my left and entourage of brown and green made up the scene of trees and bushes as falling water cascaded through the leaves like a painter creating a masterpiece’I turned around I was a good three miles from my starting point, I shook my head and let out a breath, I forced myself to begin running again, slowly at first, but as my mind began to wonder again I moved faster and faster’nobody was going to stop me but myself? Sean Price Deacon Holmes John Zero Leinad Soma The Upper Echelon Faster and faster’just at the mention of those four names, they are the ones I have something to prove to, they are the ones trying to end the career I have so desperately tried to keep from sinking, but then a thought stuck my mind like a light bulb hovering overhead’they won’t always be here’I will’I am the past present and future of Global Wrestling? A rock, a miscalculated step, I was running full speed but it all came to a crashing halt, my feet left the ground I was airborne, it felt like slow motion but I knew it was all going to end in pain’maybe like my career was going to end? In pain? Hopefully not, but that rock brought me back down to earth as my body skidded across the gravel covered ground as I came to a rest in the middle of the running trail. I turned to my back and looked into the grey clouds? The pain set in my hands and knees throbbed, I raised my hand and looked at my palm, blood ran down my wrist as I closed my hand watched the blood trickle down. The rain battered my bruised body as I laid there looking into oblivion?
