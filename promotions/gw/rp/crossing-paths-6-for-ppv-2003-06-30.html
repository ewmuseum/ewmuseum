Crossing Paths? (#6 for ppv

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-30-03 What a long day this has been. Early this afternoon I got a release from one man I thought would never let me in his office, yet alone let me leave, with or without a contractual termination. Then, I get home and spend the evening with friends and family. During the clean-up from that occasion, I receive a phone call from the wife of my former best friend turned arch enemy, Eliza Kullypso. She delivered the terrible news of the passing of Lisha Davis, Salaok's wife. Immediately after this, my own wife informs me that in 9 months, there will be a little child with the name of Frost. Tonight, I just wanted to get a good night sleep and relax, because tomorrow night I go in to Whiplash as the challenger for Salaok's International Championship. Not only that, but I am facing a man I have much respect for, and one who has been put through personal hell I couldn't not imagine. ---- I lay awake in bed, with my wife of a year and a half sleeping soundly next to me. It's hard for me to sleep, and I'm really not sure why. My wife and I are going to be sharing a child, the most sacred gift that can be given to a husband and wife. So why am I awake now? It has to be 4am at least. What in the hell is keeping me awake? The only time I am ever like this is when I am being watched. Don't know why, but I seem to sense things like somebody watching me. So why am I awake now? I look around the room out of instinct. Nothing is out of the ordinary, save the door to the bathroom being open a little. Cindy was up about an hour ago to get a drink, and must not have gotten it shut all the way. Finally, I can feel sleep starting to fall over me. I close my eyes and listen to the sound of Cindy's heart beating next to me. Slowly, I drift off. ---- 'Derek. Derek! It's me, Salaok.' 'What? Where are you?' 'I'm here, in the dark.' 'But what do you want with me? And why couldn't you have come to be at a better time?' 'There is no time. We must act now.' Fuck now. I need sleep now. 'Why now?' I ask the darkness. 'Because time is short. Whiplash is in a mere 14 hours. We have work to do.' 'What can we possibly have to do together?' 'We could do anything we wanted if only we would work together. You have pure talent. You wrestle because you love it, and emotion only makes you that much stronger. I have the experience and the aggression needed. Neither of us back down from anybody.' 'You may be right, but I don't do anything when I'm lacking sleep.' 'Then rest. Come tomorrow we will need to be on the top of our game.' 'But Salaok, why are telling me to get on my game if we are facing each other?' I hear a slight sneer in his voice this time. 'Not against each other, you fool. The Upper Echelon has both us and our respective stables in their sights. We have to be on our toes for them. Believe me, Derek. I know you and I have had our issues in the past, but we need to put that aside and come tomorrow ready to stand at my side. I'm not asking you to join The Nevermore, I'm not asking you to join The Harvesters. I'm simply asking you to stand at my side against a common enemy.' 'I'll think about it. Now let me go back to sleep.' 'You do that. Think about it.' 'Salaok, get the hell out of my house.' ---- I snap awake, gasping for air. I sit straight up in bed, looking around frantically. I jump a little as a hand is placed on my shoulder from behind. 'What's wrong babe?' 'Oh god Cindy. You scared me. That must have been a dream.' 'What?' 'Salaok. Asking me to stand at his side against TUE tomorrow.' 'It was a dream. Listen, you have a lot on your mind. Between Lisha, Whiplash, Aunt Val still missing, and now the idea of our family.' 'Cindy, I'm ok. There's nothing I can do about Lisha, we're trying to get whatever info TUE is holding against Val. Whiplash will be no problem. And as far as everything goes with our family, the only reason that would keep me awake would be if I had to be with you at your side.' 'Aww, Derek. Come on, go back to sleep.' With that, Cindy pushes me back onto the bed and lays her head on my chest. I smile and run my fingers across her back. Right before I drift off to sleep, I see a shadow move by the doorway.
