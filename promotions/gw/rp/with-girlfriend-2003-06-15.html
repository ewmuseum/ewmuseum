with girlfriend Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #305 Date: 6/15/03 Location: venom home With Roz and Dynamite leaving, I shut the door, and walks back into the kitchen where Jennifer is cooking up some food. “Smells good.” As I replied to her, as I walked over to her is what she was cooking. “Thank you, about that contract, for me being your manager you don’t have to sign it.” What she does not know it that I threw it away, reason being, last night after she left the restaurant I needed up catching up to her. From there you should get the hint. She came back to my place for the night. Well, she is still here. “I’ve been thinking I want you to be my manager again. No contract or anything thing that deals with signing my name to. If it doesn’t work out, you always have the choice to leave.” She had a smile of joy; I knew she wanted to hear that. I knew she wanted to be with me at ringside, cheering me on. She walked over to me, and gave my a kiss on the cheek. “Thank you, Venom you don’t know how happy I am. It has been three long years. Now I get to be with you again.” She went back into the living room, to sit down in the chair, she grabs the remote to turn on the T.V to VH1 classic. “And the men who hold high place Must be the ones who start To mould a new reality Closer to the heart. Yes, closer to the heart.” “Well, look at that Rush is on VH1.”I was amazed to see this video again. Good to now Rush gets respect. “You, seen them in concert didn’t you Venom.” “Sure did, it was great. Get to see a good friend of mine Alex playing.” Just then there was a knock on the door, and of all people, it was Alex, who I was just talking about. “Hey, Alex how it is going?” As I welcomed him into my house. Offered him a drink but he refused. “Going good. Hey can I talk to you outside for a minute.” As Alex was looking around in the house, making sure no one else was here, but Jennifer wasn’t in sight, she was in the bathroom when Alex showed up. So me and Alex went outside, he had a concern look on his like something was bothering him. “So what is it that you need?” I looked dead in the eye. Waiting for a response from Alex. “Its about my club, it burned down this morning.” I could not believe want I heard. The National Midnight Star gone, it was a place where I worked when I was 18, after my mother died. Alex was like a brother to me, when he offered me a job there. So many memories. “Damn, how it happen?” As I asked him, he paused for a minute. Alex was kind of teary eyed. “The police told me, a bunch of teen’s done it. But want I need from you is, some money to borrow to help me out.” Alex with his head down in shame of asking me for money. I couldn’t let a friend of my down, so I wrote him out a check of $10,000.00 to help him out. “Thanks man, I owe you big time.” Alex gave him a huge for helping him out. To hopefully help him get his club re-built. So Alex, left I went back inside, where Jennifer was standing behind the door with a cup, she was listening to me and Alex talking. She was probably pissed, for giving him that much. “That was the sweetest thing, you did.” She gave me a hug, and she walked back into the kitchen to finish cooking. I was shocked she was not mad, most women, would be like going off. Giving that much money away. She handle it good, I made the right choice making her my manager again. Plus, more important, having her as my girlfriend again. “Hey, Jennifer I’m going up stairs to pack, so we can catch leave for the air-port for Dallas, I think Roz and Dynamite is meeting us there. Tomorrow at Evolution baby, Sean Price, is going to get what he deserves.” I walked over to her and gave her a kiss. And headed up stairs, to pack to get ready to live for Dallas. This I my in ring return at Evolution, so I packed my lucky, Alf stuff animal for good luck. End Of Scene.
