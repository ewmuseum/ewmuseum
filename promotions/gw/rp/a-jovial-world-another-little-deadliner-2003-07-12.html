a jovial world. (another little deadliner)

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: saturday, july 12th, 2003 ooc: Little stranger than the usual, you’ll see why though. Quotes are from Fear and Loathing. “We had two bags of grass, 75 pellets of mescaline, five sheets of high-powered blotter acid and a saltshaker half full of cocaine.” Rub my eyes. It’s as if I was in a fairytale. Sniff sniff, puff puff, time to take the magical singing dragon into never never land. Flying through the air, thinking happy thoughts, smiling to the crowd as I pass by them all. One lump or two? I already have three throbbing on my head, and that’s without the bitter taste. Stare at my hand, watch it double, triple and turn back to normal. Repetitive process, repeat three times a minute and see me in the morning. “What are you so happy about?” I turn, and give a cold stare to him, her or it. Whatever it is, I guess. She sits there with a smile across its face, smug in her unnatural habitat. The black lights and strobes that crowd the living room only make her gold clothing shine even prouder. I read the writing across the front, and it made me smile too. Now I knew why she was so proud of herself, why she felt the need to make her presence known. “A whole galaxy of uppers, downers, screamers, laughers.” “You like at me like you’d have a chance,” she says and laughs. “Oh, I do indeed think I have a chance.” “What makes you think that Mr. Reed?” “Heh. It’s funny that you ask, because I have a number of reasons. There’s ? ? “No need to list silly ideals, I’ve heard all the pathetic ideas many times before.” “Oh, really?” I say and pull out a cigarette, a bit upset. I need to calm my nerves. “How would you know what I have to say?” “That you’re the best. Maybe that you know Jay Robson from the past, you’ve dealt with his type before. He’s not focused, he’s off partying all the time while you concentrate on the more important matters, like me.” “Hah. What is your name?” “That depends on who ends up with me.” “Oh, okay. Then I think I’ll call you Charlene. I must say, my naming skills got me climbing hills.” “And tell me, what do you call what you’re doing right now” You’re out of it right now, at a party, talking to me.” “Yeah, but this is a one time thing, just a little enjoyment.” “Uh-huh, right.” “I’m telling you, I’m ready for anything the pretty boy throws at me.” “Maybe I should leave.” Then, she stands up. “No, please. Stay, enjoy yourself. It’s a party.” “Bye, maybe I’ll see you around.” She stands, and walks off. Suddenly, someone taps me on the shoulder and I start to fly back, out of never never land at the speed of light. “What?” I flicker my eyes, and look around. It’s Ronny. “Hey man, what are you doing right now?” “Huh?” “You’re babbling, are you going to be alright?” Everyone’s staring, damn it. “A quart of tequila, a quart of rum, a case of beer, a pint of raw ether, and about two dozen amyls.” The things we do in vein. We all have our greedy eyes on the gold. Me, I’ve been using my imagination to look after the International title. Thinking about it all night, even when I’m trying to have a good time I tend to focus on the things that I find more important. Like Tuesday night, like having to face a guy that I have to team up with a week from then. And I know Jay. I know that Robson isn’t a guy who takes losing a match too easily. I’m thinking about “what if he doesn’t feel like teaming with me for MOTM if I beat him?” I’m trying to imagine what it would be like to get eliminated in the first round of a tournament, simply because two of the toughest competitors were seeded together. I’m thinking if advancing on in the international title tournament is worth risking the chance at throwing out the master of the mat tournament. I don’t want to face Davis Fury and Tony Stecchino by myself. I also don’t want to face Davis Fury, Tony Stecchino and Jay Robson by myself. I’m thinking about giving the match to Jay, for the better of it. But I don’t know, I have doubts. Withdrawals. Everyone has those, right? I mean, when I was at the party, looking at the title, I felt so good. Good as gold, Charlene was sitting so close to me. Right next to me, I could smell her scent. I could feel it coming closer to me, holding it high above my head. It felt right, it made me feel like I was strong enough to fight three or four or ten people off if I had to. It smelled like victory. But I’m still torn. A fork in the road, still unsure what to do. “Not that we needed all that for the trip, but once you get into a serious drug collection, the tendency is to push it as far as you can.” “Uh, I don’t think you want to know.” “This isn’t what I thought it was going to be. What is this Ronny?” “Forget about it Trey, let’s just have a good time.” “Damn, I’ve never felt like this before.” “Pretty good, isn’t it?” “I think I’m feeling sick, actually.” “Well, don’t worry. It’s just phencyclidine, it’s meant for you to have a good time.” “Wait, you’ve got me smoking Angel Dust?” “Relax man. We’re here, so just keep your head on your shoulders.”
