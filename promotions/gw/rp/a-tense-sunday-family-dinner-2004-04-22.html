A tense Sunday family dinner

22 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/04 It was another Sunday night at Coretta Randall's house. On one side of the table sat Kyle, Monica and the twins. At the head of the table sat Coretta. To her right sat Brick. Dinner on this particular Sunday evening was pork chops, Spanish rice, candied yams, steamed broccoli, cornbread, collard greens. For the twins, there were strained carrots. 'Father God, we come to you in the name of your son Jesus Christ with thanksgiving and praise. We thank you for this day and for this time for us to fellowship as family. We thank for our health and for our lives. We thank you for the food we are about to recieve and for the hands that prepared it. We ask that you bless this food to build the body, the mind and the soul. In Jesus' name, we pray.' 'In Jesus' name.' 'Amen.' 'Amen.' After blessing the food, they began to prepare their plates. 'So, how's Bubba doing?' asked Kyle, passing a plate to Monica. 'He's doing better than he was that night. I know he's still pissed at Salaok and Frost for that stunt they pulled.' 'I don't blame him.' 'I know. I still owe him for sneaking into our wedding and spying on our honeymoon.' 'Hey, look,' said Brick, 'if anybody needs to deal with Salaok, it needs to be me.' 'That's what I think we need to talk about,' Coretta chimed in. 'What do you mean, Ma?' 'Don't act coy with me, Jamal James. What is this I hear about you making some kind of deal with Train?' 'What?' said Monica, turning to Brick. 'Just last year, you were huffing and puffing about him being here for dinner and now you and he are the best of friends all of a sudden?' 'Oh, Ma, let me assure, it ain't like that.' 'Then what is it like? I'm curious.' 'Ma, let me handle it.' 'People are telling me they saw you in a park with two shady looking characters.' 'Is that where you were when Mom and I tried to call you?!' cried Monica. 'Monica, don't yell. You'll upset the twins.' 'Leave it alone.' 'I will not leave it alone. I will not let the idea of my brother, my children's uncle getting involved with possible criminals.' 'Listen, Jamal, anything you need to tell me...' 'Look! If there's a problem, I'll deal with it. Last time, I checked, I'm still the man of this family.' 'You are the man of the family, but I am still your mother and you will respect your concerns.' Brick sighed and bowed his head. 'Yes, ma'am.' 'You know I worry, especially when it comes to you and Salaok. I know you want to protect this family and you feel that you have to go to any lengths to do it.' 'Ma...' 'But please think about us. Think about whether or not we really want you to go to these lengths. Think about whether your father would want you to go to those lengths.' Brick then looked to his mother, then to his sister, then to his brother-in-law, and then to his little niece and nephew. 'Ma, before Dad died, he made me promise that I'd look out for this family, no matter what and I intend to. And if Salaok wants to hurt any of you to get to me, he will be dealt with...by any means necessary. Now may we please eat? The food is getting cold.'
