Revelations Part 2

1 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 1 The two brothers stepped out of the small taxi cab. They paid their fare and quickly walked over to the small entrance gates at their uncle’s estate. They buzzed the intercom system and waited for their faces to be recognized by the security cameras so that they could be buzzed through. Jushin looked around anxiously, extremely nervous over the events that had transpired over the last two hours. His brother, Ichiro was calm as always and was waiting to be buzzed through to tell his uncle. Jushin, however was not so calm. He was excited and somewhat scared. They had gotten the information that they needed, but the cost was huge. The whole meeting was a set-up, and they had been ambushed in their own interrogation room. The Americans had sent soldiers in to kill his brother and him, they did not know what they were getting themselves into. Then they even had the nerve to blow up his new car. They didn’t know what they had gotten themselves into. As Jushin looked around anxiously his mind wandered back to the events that had happened after they were ambushed in the interrogation room. The two brothers walked down the long flight of stairs that led into the Tokyo underground subway. They walked quickly, with the utmost importance on their minds. They had to make it back to their uncle’s quickly, they had important information and they had just been attacked. These new American investors weren’t kidding around, they were serious about not allowing anyone to find out what exactly they were doing in Tokyo. The brothers walked around the underground, searching for the northbound tracks. They were not avid users of the public transit system, especially the one in Tokyo and the search was long and tireless. They had been wandering the systems tunnels for about twenty minutes, before they finally found a directory map. They searched the map and followed it’s directions to the northbound tunnels about five minutes away. They walked into the northbound tracks and found that it wasn’t quite as busy as the other lines. There were people bustling around everywhere in the station, except for here. There was still a lot of people around, but here there was plenty of room to walk around without having to shove people out of your way. The two brothers looked around the tracks and saw a small sign indicating that the next train would not be around for seven minutes. The Souls of the Black Lotus walked over to a small bench that was right beside a pop machine. They sat down and began to look around, Ichiro merely looked down the tracks, but Jushin was nervous. He looked around, glancing the entire area over and over again. He was looking for any sign of danger, or any sign of information that he could draw from his surroundings. He looked at the small sign that told how much time until the train showed up. The sign read five minutes, Jushin sighed and continued his sweep of the underground tunnels. That’s when he noticed something a little bit out of the ordinary. There were shadows walking around on the far side of the tracks. Jushin stood up and moved closer. He looked across and his face grew a tinge of horror as he made out what was going on. He saw a group of ten men on the other side of the tracks, and from the looks of their shadows they were pulling something out of their long trench coats. And the shape of it, told Jushin it was a gun. He yelled out to the crowd. “Everybody get down!!” he yelled. Ichiro’s eyes shot open and the rest of the crowd in the tunnel merely stared at Jushin. However, they all took his warning two seconds later, when there was a loud burst of gunshots that echoed throughout the underground tunnels. The silence of the bustling tunnels was replaced with the screams of terror and fear. Jushin and Ichiro knew who they were, more hitmen from the Americans more than likely. They must have followed them from the building. The entire tunnel was now emptied except for the men on the other side of the track and the Nakazura brothers. Ichiro pulled out his two custom pistols and looked at the sign on the wall, three minutes until the train arrived, they only had to hold them off for three minutes. The two brothers nodded to each other and they quickly stood from there hiding spots and made a flank move to the left. They ran and shot across at their attackers as they went. They were chased by a stream of automatic rifle fire. They dove behind a fallen bench, and quickly reloaded. Jushin peered over the top and took counted their attackers. “Seven left,” said Jushin. Ichiro looked back at the small clock. “Two minutes until the train gets here.” Jushin and Ichiro nodded at one another and immediately ran back in the opposite direction heading for their original hiding place. Despite not being hit, again they did not bring down any of their attackers. Jushin dove behind the small bench, and looked at the clock as Ichiro slid in behind him. It had reset itself and now said next train in seventeen minutes. “Why is public transit always so fucking unreliable?” yelled Jushin. Just as he finished speaking there was a loud train horn, and the sound of the train approaching roared through the tunnels. The brothers stood and shot quickly, trying to take out any of their attackers that might be paying more attention to the train rather than their targets. The two brothers downed two more attackers. As they ducked back down to hide, they saw the light of the subway train’s front coming towards them. They heard the screech as the train came to a stop and they quickly made a break for the doors. They made it on and quickly ducked as bullet rounds came through the windows. These guys truly wanted them dead. They had attacked them in full public view, and now were shooting into a public transit vehicle. There were no other civilians in the cart but it was only seconds before the train pulled away. “Jushin, let’s go!” Ichiro said as he shook his little brother. Jushin was brought out of his fantasy world and brought back to the real world by the sounds of his older brother’s voice. Later on that night. Ichiro sat engaged staring at the computer screens in front of him. He had been watching the tapes for many hours now, and his eyes were starting to feel heavy. Something was bothering him, a sense of great uneasiness had grown over him throughout the course of the day and the attacks in the subway were not at all helping relieve the problem. He was staring at the security videos from the subway attacks today. The Tokyo underground had a surprisingly effective form of security control, no doubt due to the mass robberies and muggings that used to take place in the wee hours of the Japanese morning. Ichiro felt a sense of uneasiness when he found out the foreign investors were American. He had no doubt that they would be, but it felt strange to him for some reason. Also, the fact that they had organized hits did not sit well with him either. Was it these American bastards that led to the death of his father. He did not want to believe it, he wanted to believe that he and his brother had already fulfilled vengeance on those responsible for their father’s death, but something deep down was telling him that it was these Americans who organized the killing. Ichiro was about to call it quits, when finally something caught his eye. He was looking at the attack from a camera on the far side of the tracks, where his attackers were standing. He noticed something sitting on the back of one of the man’s necks. It was a tattoo or some form of ink marking. He quickly grabbed the mouse and begin to zoom in and use the enhance features on the computer to get a better look. He zoomed in and moved his face as close as he could to the screen. He looked hard and upon further view he noticed what looked like some form of coat of arms. Something went off in his head, he had seen that form of emblem before. But why? Where? How was it so important? He couldn’t figure it out, he knew there was a place close to him where he had seen a similar coat of arms. “Should I call Jushin?” he thought to himself. No, it would be better if I did not, I can’t guarantee this has any relevance at all. For all I know this stupid tattoo could be some form of family crest, or tribal design. He would weigh the options in his head, try to figure out exactly what it was. He would meditate on it, maybe that would help him figure it out. He did not matter, he and Jushin had a big match coming up, a chance to get a shot at regaining what was rightfully theirs, the tag titles. He took a final look at the emblem before he turned the screen off. He walked up the stairs quietly and retreated to his room for the night. The feeling of uneasiness did not go away. Even, in his last minutes of being awake he thought about it. The thought ran through his head over and over. I KNOW THAT COAT OF ARMS FROM SOMEWHERE
