'Sleepwalking.'

17 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 17th, 2004. The rain drizzled down onto the Dallas streets as I slowly walked along the sidewalk. My pace was slow because of my hesitation…my fear. I have never been one to fear anything, pain, my enemies…anything. But the fear of not being accepted was coursing through my veins as I made my way through the dripping tears from Heaven. Perhaps the angels were crying for the sins I had committed…or perhaps they were crying because they knew what was about to happen to me. I was admittedly fearful because even I didn't know. In fact my knowledge was very limited right now, as I was obsessed with one thing in this brief moment of my life…her. I had came to see her. It had been completely unannounced so it would be a surprise…perhaps a surprise to the both of us. I could only throw up prayers of acceptance and hope they didn't fall on deaf ears. The cold rain sent me into a shiver as it flowed down my skin before falling towards its gravitational calling. I was cold. Dressed in a pair of drenched and wrinkled Versace jeans, black Converse, Chuck Taylor sneakers and a black T-shirt bearing a small silver Celtic cross over the front of it. The shirt itself was rather tattered since I had ripped the sleeves off of. Reaching up to my neck I gave the cross pendant that hung about my neck, a squeeze for good luck. Shaking the water from my shaggy black hair I progressed down the sidewalk coming closer and closer to my destination…Alexia's residence. Ten yards and closing in. My feet seemed to grow heavier as I had to force them to move. I lifted one and then the other while they screamed in fearful protest. Forcing my body to overcome its fear I took a deep breath and then exhaled, slowly calming myself. 'What am I doing?' I groaned to myself rhetorically. I should have more important things to worry about in place of, Alexia. Nonetheless my angel strangely beckoned me with her soul and I knew that I must obey the inscrutable exhortations thereof. My feet progressed closer towards the door as I felt my hands begin to shake in fear. I had been through things ten times more traumatic than this. Yet…I couldn't overcome my fear. I had never once in my life worried or even cared about being accepted by someone else, yet now it was suddenly important to me. Raising my head my bluish gray eyes stared into the heaven as the rain fell onto my face. It ran across the features that so many describe as handsome…I only hope she'll think the same. The rain dripped from my face, down my neck, into my shirt as the cold liquid rushed over my thin frame. The angry storm clouds glared ominously down at me as I put my head down with a gentle sigh. I was at her door…it was now or never. Now was the time that I would take a leap of faith. And that leap of faith would do one of two things. It would either show me that she was there to catch me…or it would teach me how to fly. I bowed my head as I stood in the rain. The rain rushed down my face quickly or at least…I hope it was rain and not my tears of confusion, anger, bitterness and emotional torment. Taking a brief moment to compose myself I sighed gently. Go time. Raising my muscular and tattooed arm my finger paused right by her doorbell. Closing my eyes I pushed forward…only to have my finger miss the doorbell completely. It was at that moment in time that I realized my hands were trembling beyond all human perception. Each hand was shaking as if it were a leaf being blown about in the autumn wind. But these weren't leaves…this was my hand. And this wasn't autumn…it was the painful present. I had complete control for once…I could either push a single button and become the victim of even more heartbreak, or I could just walk away. It was risk/reward type of situation. The risk…my heart was on the line. The reward…if she would have me…Alexia. With this in mind I reached again for the doorbell yet my shaking hand missed it completely. Maybe this was a sign. Maybe this was God's way of sparing me from even more heartbreak. But maybe this was once again my own fear holding me back from my love. I balled my shaking hand up into a fist and I bypassed the doorbell. Instead my knuckles rapped three times against the hard wood of her door. Three hard knocks…surely she had heard them. Taking a step to the side I avoided the peephole in her door. Surely if she knew the man who was standing outside of her door she wouldn't open it…or would she? A flurry of questions ran through my mind. Question after question danced through my cerebral as my heart slammed violently inside my chest as if it were trying to spring from the body that encased it. My breath became shallow and it felt as if someone has just kicked me directly in the stomach, sending the contents of my dinner sailing about within. My body couldn't short circuit on me now. I had trusted it many times and it had always delivered. It couldn't fall apart in these few brief moments of anticipation…it couldn't. What were only mere seconds seemed to be hours. Time drug on as I lost all sense of it. To me every passing second of painful anticipation brought a flock of new worries. Then finally…my heart leapt as I heard the door being unlocked. A hand was placed on the knob and it slowly turned. The door opened wide before me as the wind whipped through the house. Then…I went numb. Everything stopped. I couldn't feel the rain dripping down my skin. My fingers ceased to tremble and my tongue was motionless. Standing before me was a woman who must've fallen from heaven…Alexia. My eyes soaked in her grand design, soft black hair…as black as the midnight sky and more beautiful than the sweetest of midnights. Large brown eyes that sparkled as they foretold the world of the joy that lived inside of her. Her eyes looked as if they had been stars plucked from heaven and crafted by God's own hand. If they were stars then I would forever be a devout astrologist for only one look into her eyes melted my heart. Above and below each of her gorgeous brown spheres her long eyelashes seemed to add even more to what was an already stunningly beautiful scene. My eyes ran across her face…a face of such magnificent beauty that it looked as if only the greatest sculptor could have chiseled it from the finest ivory. Her full, sweet lips looked as if they had been painted on by an artist such as Picasso for he would be the only man that could perfect such beauty…but the picture was too beautiful for him to ever paint. Her neck…a slender vessel of beauty that led the pathway to her physique of lovely curvature. The white Mickey Mouse belly shirt and black pajama pants she bad casually been lounging in only seemed to bring out her joyous demeanor as she stood there. She stood there slightly surprised as I gazed in awe of her heavenly splendor. My body trembled weakly as our eyes met. We stared into the others eyes before I was forced to look away unable to hold her gaze. But amidst all the fears that stormed about my poor troubled soul a passion emerged. Love…a powerful thing. Fear may give men wings, but love seems to make a man invincible. Love can make a man walk through fire, risk his life and do a number of other crazy things. I stood there a shaking, quivering mass of emotions…but it was time to be a man. My leap of faith must be taken now. I stood at the mouth of this yawning canyon called love as I prepared to leap off. Whether I would be caught or learn to fly was unbeknownst to me. I simply did the only thing I could do…I leapt. Raising my head I stared deeply into her eyes. Reaching forward I locked my left arm about her waist and pulled her closer towards me. Her body came out into the rain willingly. My right hand reached up past her shoulder and I gently touched her face as the rain began to roll down its lovely structure. My fingers explored the beauty of her face as I gently caressed the sweet artistry. My thumb slowly ran over her lips feeling the sweet moisture upon them and parting them ever so slightly. In my greatest leap of faith I overcame a giant hurdle, as I leaned in her eyes closed and mine did the same as I gently kissed her on the lips, softly…passionately…lovingly. I stopped hesitantly. I didn't want to stop but there was something so much more important that I had to do. Leaping into the wide abyss that is known as love, I spoke gently choking on emotion. 'Alexia…I love you.' Her large eyes seemed to widen and a smile danced at the corner of her lips. She looked pleased…she looked overjoyed. Perhaps this would be my moment of destiny. She pulled me in closer her arms around my waist. Standing on her tip toes to make up the five inches of height between us she whispered softly and lovingly into my ear. 'And Josh I...' She began but suddenly her voice was interrupted. Beep! Beep! Beep! Beep! Beep! Suddenly she was gone! My eyes opened wide and everything that seemed to be going my way had vanished! The rain was gone as thick blankets covered my body. The clothes were gone as I awoke from my nude slumber. In a violent surge of anger I punched the alarm clock on the nightstand beside my bed shattering it into innumerable pieces. Everything that had just mattered was gone. All that seemed bright and beautiful was nonexistent. Closing my eyes I pulled the covers over my head. Perhaps it was time to bar the door and lock the windows. It seemed that all the world outside had to offer was pain. Lying there a bitter and broken young man I hurt deeply inside. My purpose for living had been nothing more than an evil hoax of the subconscious mind. My dreams were just that…dreams. I had been awakened to cruel reality.
