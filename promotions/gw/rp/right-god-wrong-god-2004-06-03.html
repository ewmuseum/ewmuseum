Takahashi then leans back over to his right side, where the area around another group of candles, this time melted away on an old furnace, is illuminated with the rouch of Takahashi's match. The whole room is now lit up with the light from the candles, but the light does nothing to soften the damp, harsh look of the dreary home of Takahashi Jetsui. Takahashi sits on his poor-looking, wire-framed, spring mattress bed, and reaches down, lifting up a bottle of whiskey, and pouring the liquid into a dirty glass, that is cover in sut and finger prints:. What to do? .:Takahashi throws back the shot of whiskey, and chokes down the brown liquid, and a grimacing look takes over his face as he does so. Takahashi then reaches back down beside the bottle of whiskey, and picks up a tattered pack of cigarettes. After retrieving a white stick from the pack, Takahashi picks up one of the candles and uses it to light the cigarette.
