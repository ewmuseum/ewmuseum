Building A Mystery (part 1)

20 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/20/05 You want to tear me down You want to hold me down You can’t control me now You cannot take me out You cannot save me now Because I’m stronger now - TRUSTcompany *** Several days had passed after Corvus returned from his trips to both France and Detroit. He needed that time to recuperate not only his body but his mind. He had made a very hard decision in cutting himself off from the memories of his daughter but deep down he knew that it would only cause him more grief, more suffering. He began to change after all of this. His place began to come clearer and clearer, he knew where he needed to be. It didn’t take a Basement Match and all of the effects from it to make him realize that he couldn’t focus on anything but being himself ... a monster. Corvus knew the reason he couldn’t defeat Salaok was because he didn’t have complete focus. He dwelled too much on things, which always lead to his downfall in one form or another. It was the trip to France of course, but there were those other looming things in his mind ... how he became an assassin was one of them. The many torture sessions they used upon him to either break him until he died or bend him to do their bidding played in his mind for many nights after his first recollection of them on the plane. The dark and dingy dungeon in which he was housed, even the echoes of the dripping water that slipped through the cracks in the foundation when it rained. It was all coming back to him, every last second of it. Even their voices were clear even though at the time he was captured he didn’t speak the language. Arabic had not been his primary language since he came from Europe and had only planned on staying a short time when he was nabbed by a Falaqi of the Assamite clan (a Falaqi is a pack of a certain sect of vampire formed for a purpose). They had been sent to procure him, which was a reason he never understood. Why would the Assamites want a Toreador for? That question was answered eventually as they tried to break him into working for them. It was his nobility they sought, as they had heard of his father ... a great King. It did not matter to them that he had not come close to securing the Kingdom, but he had trained as a warrior due to his stature. This was something they thought could work out for them. The Assamites admired him even while they tortured him because of the will he had. For a vampire, he did have uniqueness about him. The humanity within him still after such a long period of being immortal impressed them. The main focus was to either purge him of this humanity before he died a final death or use it to their advantage. Months of torture ensued as he was chained up with small amounts of blood being given to him. It was just enough to keep him sustained but weak enough not to revolt against them. They had had enough problems with someone in their own group, which is the reason they sought to replace him with the mighty Corvus. They wanted to punish the defiler of their sect by giving his abilities to Corvus by having him drink all of the vampire’s blood, killing it and making himself stronger in the process. Just because the clan itself couldn’t diablerize, didn’t mean that bringing in someone from outside the clan to do it wouldn’t work. Finally, Corvus gave in and did as his captors wished and took the blood and the life from the traitor they had sentenced to be obliterated. Months of torture turned into months of training and learning all about the new things that he could do. It was the first step to him becoming the machine that he is today. He was introduced to a group within the Assamite clan known as the “Web of Blades” which specialized in assassinations. He learned everything there was to killing someone not only with weapons but also with a dark magic as well, making him ten times more lethal than he had been before and it gave him more reasons to be a monster then he was previously as well. It would lead to many quests and tallies in the victim column for the man who went from a vicious murderer to an entertainer of people. There is a story on why this is but it shall be saved for another time and only when that time is right. *** In between Corvus dreams or recollections of being tortured, which seemed to have replaced the strange dreams he was having about his long deceased daughter, the scent of a human came across his nose. He rose up from his bed and looked across the room where there was a silhouette by the windows of a woman standing there. “Who the fuck are you and how did you get in here?” He sat patiently as he awaited an answer before he went about ripping the person to shreds. The figure stepped a few feet away from the glass of the window and pulled something out of their pocket and held it up for him to look at ... it was a badge. One might think that something like this would bother him, but it didn’t. He knew that he would be able to get out of any situation that might arise if he were in trouble. They must have found some type of evidence linking him to the mugger or even the girl he made a meal off of. If that were the case, he needed to find better people to take care of the sweep and clears that took place after he fed. “Mr. Corvus or whatever your real name is ... I’m detective Elizabeth Van Buren, I came here to ask you a few questions regarding a body that we found recently that has been linked with being seen with you.” He shook his head as he looked at the woman. She was of average height ... around 5’4? give or take and had long, light brown hair which was pulled up into a pony tail. Long hair was a hazard in any job, just ask Corvus himself. He knew how much of a risk it was for his opponents or even victims alike grabbing it which didn’t hurt too badly but it was frustrating all the same. “I hate to sound like an asshole, but even with being a detective and all it would have been nice to call ahead ... so I would have had proper time to get ready and at least be a gracious host. I don’t suppose you have a warrant for being here would you?” He sat up in his bed and folded his arms as he waited for an answer from the detective. He could have made it easy for himself and took her out, but there would be too many questions on her disappearance. Someone had to have known where she was going and if she didn’t return that would only lead to more cops crawling around the place and that was something he didn’t need. “I don’t have a warrant ... but it wouldn’t take much to get one. A warrant is only good if I wanted to search your house and didn’t have your permission; I do not want to do that, I Just want to ask you a few questions and try and get some answers to this case that I am working on.” She did have a point, he thought. Warrants were only needed if they were looking for something specific ... say skeletons in the closet or bodies hacked up and stored in the fridge, things of that nature. He was intrigued about what her case was but more importantly ... how did she get in? “That’s well and good that you just want to talk ... but as you can see I was in a nice slumber until I could hear your breathing. A little excited I can see ...” Corvus paused a moment as he looked at his perfectly sculpted arms and then back to the detective. “It’s not every day you break into a celebrity’s home and catch them sleeping ... now tell me, Ms. Van Buren, how did you manage to get past my front gate and then the security system?” Detective Van Buren walked through the room until she found a chair and dragged it to where she could sit beside the bed. She made sure her hand was close to her standard issue police weapon as well just in case this guy was as ruthless as she had heard about. “You might find this to be a shock, but your front gate was ajar when I arrived this morning. I was able to walk through the front door as well. I’m not going to pull your chain but this is the truth whether you want to accept it or not.” The glance he gave her was not flattering in the least. He knew that everything had been locked up well before he had turned in for the day and to hear that she had gotten in without his security going off did not please him at all. “I’ll check into the security issue ... but I’m a little tired so if you could hurry it up with asking me whatever it is that you want to ask me, do it so you can show yourself out just as you showed yourself in somehow.” She rolled her eyes at him as she crossed her legs to make herself more comfortable. He was like every man she’d met, big muscles and bad attitude. He probably was not that smart she figured but if he was the guy she was looking for then he was rather good at what he did. “Maybe it wasn’t faulty security, Mr. Corvus, it could be the fact that you partied too hard last night and had a few ladies with you who left early this morning and you forgot to lock up after yourself ... I know that stars, even wrestling ones, can get a bit rowdy when they are not getting all sweaty beating up other grown men like they were in a fierce ballet.” Van Buren couldn’t help but put her hand up to cover her mouth to prevent herself from laughing at her statement, ridiculous as it was. She had never dealt with someone who was a professional wrestler before. With the little information she did have on him, it wouldn’t be easy to get him to reveal anything about his true self. “Sorry, I’m just not really used to trying to get answers from someone who can’t even give me a real name. I know you wrestler types like to keep your anonymity so I will just have to do the best I can with what I have. Where were you on the night of Tuesday, February 15th?” Corvus began to open his mouth when he heard a cell phone go off. He knew it wasn’t his as he knew the particular ring tone his was and it didn’t sound at all like what he was hearing. The detective held up her hand to stop him from speaking and answered her phone. She was only on it for a few moments before she put it back in her coat. “Shit, I’m needed at the station ... I would like to continue this discussion. Would this evening be fine for you, I promise I won’t break in this time.” “How about we do this someplace away from my residence so you are not given a second chance at breaking in” Eight or nine sounds good to me if you can manage it ... how about the Hard Rock? Since I’m one of those “stars” and all, I’d really not want to disappoint you detective.” She didn’t like the fact that he was being a smartass but she figured he was just like that or he was trying to play up to her. He was attractive, she noted that much in her head but he would never be anyone that she would get involved with, especially with how he was acting towards her. “You’re on; I’ll meet you there at 8:30 then ... see I can compromise.” The detective stood up and put the chair back where she had found it. At least she was considerate, but even that didn’t sway the fact that Corvus knew she had information on him and why she wanted to know where he was on the particular night in question made him think that when they found the body of the girl, he left something behind. He watched as she left his bedroom and after he heard his front door close, he sat up and walked out to the door and reset the security code and watched her walk through the gate and hit the button for it to close. During his walk back to his room he looked around to see if anything had been out of place, but it all looked just as it was. He began to wonder if he had been sloppy or the people he had working for him that disposed of his victims had forgotten to do something. He slipped back into bed and reached over for the phone on his daystand and dialed a number. A few rings in he received the voice mail. This infuriated him to no end when he had to leave a message, which wouldn’t be pretty. “I hope you guys didn’t fuck up on your last job for me. There was a cop here today wanting to ask questions about the night when I brought that bitch home that was hopped up on drugs. I have to meet with her tonight to discuss all this, you better hope this goes well or I’ll be finding someone else to dispose of YOUR bodies!” He turned off the phone and slammed it back onto the cradle. At least he had been smart enough to not use his full strength or he would have had to replace his phone, which has happened before in other places he’s lived when things did not go so well for him during a conversation. Corvus laid back and looked at the ceiling to try and ease back into sleep. Vampires didn’t need to rest for physical tiredness. It was for mental purposes since they used that more than any of their other parts which don’t need to work because they are dead. *** Corvus drove to the Hard Rock Caf? to meet the detective that had managed to make it into his haven earlier in the day. On the way there he had realized that he had been followed by someone in a Bentley since he had left his home. He found a parking spot and locked the car up drum tight just in case the person in the Bentley was casing his wheels. After he got out of the car he could tell that whoever it was that was tailing him, gout out of their car and was following him. It made him think that it might have been a cop that wanted to make sure that he had not bailed out on his little meeting with the detective. His mode of dress was a little different from normal. He was wearing a pair of black cargo pants and a hoodie of the same color. Corvus rarely ever wore any other colors besides black or red, they were his trademarks and he rarely strayed away from them. His eyes scanned the room and found the detective sitting at a table alone sipping on a soda. He slowly approached her and spoke. “Before I sit down, I want you to answer me something real quick.” The top of the chair was in his grasp but the only way he was going to sit down was dependant on how she answered his question. “What is it that you want to know?” She took another drink of her soda waiting for him to ask her what he was going to. “Why did you send someone to tail me when you could have been assured that I was going to come here and not skip out on you?” His eyes focused on her because he was not thrilled about the guy in the Bentley chasing him all the way from his place to the caf?. She looked at him as if he were an escapee out of a lunatic asylum. Leaning back in her chair she gave him a straight forward answer which she figured would be the only way to get him to realize she hadn’t done anything of the sort. “Excuse me” I didn’t send anyone to follow you. I actually took you at your word that you were going to meet me here. Is there something I should know about?” Corvus spun the seat around and sat down on it backwards as he looked her in the eye. He decided that he wasn’t going to put up with any bull crap, especially from a mortal. He read her thoughts to prove whether she was lying or not. The answer came to him that she was not lying. “Some guy in a Bentley was tailing me since I left my home to come to meet you. I thought it might have been one of yours to make sure that I wasn’t going to skip out on our appointment tonight. He may be in here somewhere so don’t look around. Let’s just get whatever you need out of the way so I can go back home. I have a match to get ready for soon.” Detective Van Buren looked at her drink and then to him and asked thoughtfully before she interviewed him if he wanted anything to drink. He shook his head negatively as he wouldn’t drink mortal beverages unless he absolutely had to due to their effects on his body since he didn’t have the proper ways of removing them. “Back to where we were ... you wanted to know where I was on the night if the 15th, right?” He said calmly as he put his hands together and watched her facial features when he would say something. He could always tell things by the way someone would react if he couldn’t read them or did not want to. “Yes, that is what I would like to know.” “I was actually out walking the strip. I have a fondness for walking around and looking at the people that are tourists and what not. I lead a very dull life as you can tell being a wrestler and all. I have odd habits you could say.” Van Buren wrote that down in her little notebook that she had for when she was getting answers from people she was asking questions to about cases. You could even say that this was a pretty casual way of interrogating him when they could have arrested him and really made things tough. She knew there wasn’t anything more than a small bit of evidence that linked the victim to him. “I see ... and what did you do after you were finished walking around the strip” Did you see or meet anyone that night out of the ordinary?” This was precisely where he figured she would go with it. They found the girl and of course he must have forgotten to do something. He figured he would play things down and if he needed to, he’d work some of his vamp magic and get her to forget about the whole thing and file a report that didn’t include anything to do with him. That was a last resort of course but when didn’t a vampire herd a human around to do his bidding? Happens all the time actually ... “I was walking past the Mandalay when this girl came running up to me and said she was being chased by someone. She was pretty frantic and she looked like she was freezing to death. I didn’t see anyone at the time, so I did the best I could to calm her down. Gave her my coat and everything and walked her to her car. She asked me about my coat and I told her that she could hang onto it. She insisted that she get my address so that she could get my coat returned to me the next day or so. That was about it; she got into her car and I decided to head back to my car. I still haven’t gotten my coat back either ...” Van Buren continued to right down notes on what he said before she spoke dryly while not even looking at him. “I doubt you will either ... we found her body about four days ago. That was the reason why I came to your house; I wanted to rule out anything since we found your address in her purse.” “Any idea what might have happened to her” She did seem really frightened when I ran into her. I suppose that since I was the only thing walking that way that looked like they might be able to help her, she gravitated to me. I have that effect on people it seems ...” The woman seemed like she didn’t want to divulge any information to someone that wasn’t on the police force, especially a guy like Corvus since she had heard some things about him from the other officers who were into watching GW programming. His type of character fit one of the odd occurrences that her body had displayed ... lack of blood. “Best thing I can tell you is that we think whoever was chasing her when you helped her found her and finished what they intended. The strange part was she was lacking a lot of blood and had trace amounts of a narcotic in her system. Are you sure you’re being completely honest with me, Mr. Morgan?” Damn ... he forgot that he put his alias on that slip of paper that he gave to the girl. It was starting to look like he might need to use his abilities to change the direction of how this was going, but first he was going to do his best to talk his way out of it normally. “I’ve told you everything that happened on my end of that evening. If you don’t believe me then take me downtown or wherever your cop shop is and stick me in a room where I’ll be bored to death and tell you the same thing I told you just now ... your choice but if you don’t have anything else, then I’m going to leave.” Det. Van Buren set her pen down and took another drink from her soda. She had to think of something because she didn’t really have enough to take him to the precinct. She relented a few moments more before speaking to him again since he was intent on leaving. “I have nothing on you right now that will give me enough evidence for an arrest but I will be keeping an eye on you, vampire boy, so you better be on your best behavior because I won’t hesitate to nail your ass if you cross any lines that shouldn’t be crossed. I know you play some supernatural creature on television but if you are bringing your work home with you like some sick pathetic freak ... we will get you. I’ve heard some things about people disappearing in Cleveland, Ohio when you were working down in that area ... there’s also been some information about some teacher that was killed that had your name written all over it. As I said ... I’ll be watching you.” Corvus stood up to his full height; being that he is seven feet tall it really put an impression on the female cop. He took the chair he was sitting on and returned it to its rightful position and started to walk away. He stopped in mid stride and turned to the detective and smiled slightly. “If you do recover my jacket, please call ahead ... I’d really like that back, it was one of my favorites.” She muttered something about him being an asshole as he walked away, which he heard very clearly and smirked like a Cheshire cat. Out of the corner of his eye as he was approaching the exit, he saw the fool that had been following him moving again. He had stuck around the whole time he was talking to the police officer. He walked out of the place and turned towards the parking area, which he shaded himself in the shadows. The man who followed him stopped when he didn’t see who he was looking after and began to get confused until suddenly out of nowhere Corvus comes and right crosses him in the face, breaking his nose into pieces with the amount of impact his fist made against the bone. Luckily this time he knew there was no one that was watching the incident. He dragged the man who was out cold towards his car and opened the trunk. Corvus stuffed the guy into the trunk and slammed the top down and hit the alarm so he could get inside. “Now I can get some answers ... the stupid moron should have stayed home tonight.” TO BE CONTINUED ...
