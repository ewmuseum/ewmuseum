The Meeting

1 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 1st **From time to time, we are all forced into situations that are not the most ideal situations to be part of. These situations may make us want to lash out on those around us, as the situation may be very frusterating to the person at the center. I have been through these situations many a time in the past, and have found myself walking through the same brain-cramping fields of dead-ends and meaningless short cuts once again. At every mention of my current situation, I find myself catering to the sudden pounding that ensues in my head** .:The room is dark and dreary. We can hear drips coming from various parts of the dark room, and we then see a cloud of smoke rise from a corner of the room and into the stale looking light that is hanging from the ceiling. A creaking sound comes from the corner of the room, and Takahashi Jetsui sits up in what appears to be and old spring mattress. The red paint seems to be worn off of the shirtless Jetsui's face, as he sucks back the nicotine from the cigarette clinched between his fingers. Jetsui releases another cloud of cigarette smoke into the damp air. Jetsui then puts his fingers through his hair, and begins to hack up a throat full of flem:. CAUGH! HACK! CAUGH! HACK! TAKAHASHI: Ugh. Another day in this rancid shit hole. .:Takahashi reaches down underneath the spring bed, and pulls out what appears to be a bottle of alcohol. Takahashi removes the lid from the bottle of brown liquid, and chugs back a good couple of mouth fulls before taking the bottle from his lips, and visibly shaking due to the taste of the liquid. With a long creak from the bed, Takahashi stands up and finishes the cigarette in his fingers before throwing the finished cigarette into a corner of the room. The cigarette hits the wall and a quick shot of sparks quickly lights up the corner before dying out:. .:Takahashi walks over to the opposite corner of the small room, and reaches into a duffle bag, retrieving a disgusting-looking tank top. Takahashi puts the tank top on, along with a pair of scuffed boots underneath a pair of bloodied denim jeans. Takahashi also retrieves a pack of cigarettes from the duffle bag. Takahashi then proceeds up a small flight of stairs, and opens a large rusty door, revealing another hot, humid day in Dallas, Texas:. .:Jetsui proceeds into what appears to be a side street of downtown Dallas, a street that is buzzing with the usual sounds of a fledging neighbourhood. Children dressed in baggy clothes are seen robbing a small convenience store at the corner of the street. As the kids run from the store, a black man comes charging out after:. BLACK CLERK: Hey you fuckin' punks! Jus' you wait 'till I find yer asses! I'mma skin botha' youse! .:There are several people on the street at this time watching the incedent, but none of the people on the street do anything about to twart the young thieves. It was almost as though these types of occurances were accepted in this society as a daily occurance. We can hear a few minor grumples from a couple of large women sitting on a porch across the street from where Takahashi is sitting:. LARGE WOMAN #1: Oh mai, what has this country come to? LARGE WOMAN #2: It's all because of dat' rap music dat is plastered all over da' place! LARGE WOMAN #3: Damn rap music. .:Takahashi shakes his head at the thought of these people balming their problems on a form of entertainment, and proceeds on his way down the street. After a few steps, Takahashi comes to a run-down bowling alley, with a woman standing in front. The woman is dressed fairly seductively, with fish net stockings, a mini skirt, a pair of large boots, and a revealing top. Lipstick was smeared very sloppily on the woman's face, along with sloppily applied cheek makeup, and eye shadow:. WOMAN: Hey hot stuff, you look new around these parts. You lookin' for somethin' ta do? Maybe some ONE? .:Takahashi looks at the female, who we now suspect to be a prostitute, in a confused manner:. TAKAHASHI: What are you proposing? .:The woman proceeds towards Takahashi, who stands there curiously:. WOMAN: My going rate is $120 for you to do with me whatever you want. TAKAHASHI: You're a prostitute. WOMAN: So they say. TAKAHASHI: You are swimming with diseases no doubt, and you are wanting me to pay you to allow me to do with you what I want? WOMAN: Excuse me? TAKAHASHI: Herpes, syphillus, gonoreah, and who knows what else is crawling all over every inch of your body. All of these diseases, these viruses of consumption have been placed into you by the dirt of the world, and you want to pass them on to others? WOMAN: Look pal, either you say yes, or you say no. I ain't in need of a life story. TAKAHASHI: My apologies. (Running his hand over the woman's face) I guess I could use an addition to my collection. WOMAN: Collection? TAKAHASHI: Allow me to explain. You see recently I have found myself collecting certain treasures that I have come across, female treasures. I love the smell of women, I love their looks, their every attribute, and the only way I am able to hold on to these feelings is by COLLECTING bodies. WOMAN: Oh my god! You creep! TAKAHASHI: What's wrong? No longer interested? We could have so much fun! .:With that, the hooker is speechless, and runs away from the low talking Takahashi. After this, Takahashi allows a sadistic grin to take over his face, before continuing down the street. After walking a little further down the street, we hear a voice from off the screen:. VOICE: THERE'S MY MAN!! .:Takahashi looks over and sees a visibly excited Juan Gonzales running quickly towards him. Juan crosses the street and approaches Takahashi:. JUAN: Heyyyyyy man! (Shaking Takahashi's hand) I've been waiting here all day! Hey, you want the rest of this burito I got from a seven-eleven around the corner? It's starting to give me cramps. .:Takahashi looks down at the sloppy piece of imitation Mexican junkfood with disgust:. JUAN: No? Hey, I don't blame you bro, this thing is wreaking havoc on my colon! (Throws the burito onto the roof of a bulding that Takahashi and Juan are standing in front of) So what's up my east asian brotha'?! How you hangin'? TAKAHASHI: Lets get down to business. JUAN: Right. So I was thinking we hop into my low rider with a couple of ma' boys, and then we head to the ring with the song- TAKAHASHI: Stop. Here's what is going to happen. You will go to the ring for the tag match, I will go to the ring for the tag match, and we will fight it out. No low riders, no Mexican hip-hop music, just a fight, you understand? JUAN: Ah, come on man! We at least need a ca- .:Takahashi grabs Juan by the shirt and holds him against the front display of the store that the two men are standing in front of:. TAKAHASHI: We go there, and we FIGHT, do you underst- .:Suddenly Juan pushes back and sends Takahashi into a beaten up Ford Tempo just next to the sidewalk on te road:. JUAN: I hope you aren't planning on bringing hostility into this partnership, essay, because if you are, you might as well walk away this instant. I want those tag belts, and this match is going to let me get one step closer. I like flash, I like style, and as far as I'm concerned, that's all part of the FIGHT. If you want no part of it, that's fine, but if you lay your hands on me again, me and you are going to have problems homes. Comprende? TAKAHASHI: (Getting into Juan's face) I'm not going to have problems this Monday, that couldn't be further from the truth. The simple fact you have to face, El Mariachi, is that me and you are not partners on a full-scale level. I have things to take care of that don't fit you into the picture, the tag belts are simply a stop for me to make on my way to my goals. So show up, fight, and leave, this is all I ask of you, anything thereafter between you and I is purely coincidental and means nothing. JUAN: I got it. Just make sure your head is in the right place amigo, or you'll be sorry. .:With that, Juan Gonzales departs from Jetsui, who is left in the middle ofthe sidewalk with look of distain pressed upon his face:.
