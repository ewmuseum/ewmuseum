Brandi needs help

23 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/23/05 The date: October 17th, 2005 The place: Global Arena, Las Vegas, Nevada Brick was returning backstage from his grueling match with Mindy Mercer. It was grueling, particulary for Mindy, who had just suffered a brutal attack at the hands of Bobby Brawler. Nevertheless, she was willing to go through with the match and even though she lost, Brick showed her the utmost respect. 'Brick.' Brick turned to see Brandi Sights headed towards him. The past few weeks, she had been berating Brick for his volatile backstage outbursts. One week, his partner Train was ambushed by Mike Biddle, who was looking to collect the bounty Christian Zenith had placed on Train's head: a shot at the International title to whoever took him out. Brick was out for blood: Zenith's, Biddle's, Shadow's, Vivica Valentine's, it didn't matter. Brandi had to calm him down. The previous week, he had been in a triple threat match with Blaise McClancy and Virgil Ambrosi for a shot at the Platinum title. He lost due to a mishap involving McClancy's mist getting sprayed in his eyes when it was meant for Ambrosi, not to mention Ambrosi capitalizing on it. 'What is it now, Brandi?' Brick grumbled, rolling his eyes. 'I've been good. I haven't been looking to start any shit with anybody.' 'That's kinda what I wanted to talk to you about. Listen, I'm sorry about the way I've been coming down on you for your tantrums. At least you had provocation. Not like that lunatic Brawler.' Bobby Brawler. The one man in the history of Global Wrestling who could be considered even more extreme, even more insane than Brick. Recently, he'd made his return to Global. Since then, he'd been on a tear, attacking various Global superstars. His first victim was newcomer Radiant on the night of his return. The second was Emoticon. Finally, he attacked both Mindy and her significant other Markus. 'I need someone to reign him in some and you're the only person I can think of to do it.' 'What do you have in mind?' Brick asked. 'At Dark Rituals, I'm putting the two of you in a special match, OK? It's no holds barred, no count-outs, no disqualifications, falls will count anywhere in the arena.' 'I was hoping to take on someone in the Hunting Grounds, especially since Virgil Ambrosi has a match.' 'Are you still on that?' Brandi groaned. 'You're damn right I'm still on that! I'd be the one facing Danielle Northsky if he and McClancy hadn't screwed.' 'You don't know that.' 'I don't not know it, either!' 'Listen, Brick, you're the only one who's tough enough and crazy enough to take Brawler on.' 'That's not necessarily true, Brandi. There's at least three people backstage who'd like a piece of Brawler. I just competed in a match with one of them.' 'Fine,' Brandi sighed. 'You're the only one tough enough and crazy enough who wasn't already booked for Dark Rituals. Radiant's in a dark match with two other newcomers, Mindy's in the Showstarter with McClancy and Elia Smith, and Emoticon has his Kendo challenge against Shadow. No one else is available' He knew Brandi must have been desperate to have someone deal with Brawler. This match seemed thrown together at the last minute. 'OK,' Brick said. 'I'll take on Brawler at Dark Rituals. You're right. I need something to do. OK, I'll take on Brawler.' Brick knew by accepting this match, he was sticking his head in a lion's mouth, but when had that ever stopped him? There was a reason they called Brick 'Mr. Nine Lives'. He'd survived drowning in a freezing river in Canada, a bludgeoning to the head with a crowbar, falls from heights of up to forty feet. He was pretty sure he could survive Bobby Brawler. And if he didn't, he'd go down fighting.
