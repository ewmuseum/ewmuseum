On the Edge of Sorrow

19 June 2005

On the Edge of Sorrow Ash Global Wrestling Promo #2103 Date: 06/19/05 Location: Evolution Roleplay “There is no greater sorrow than to recall in misery the time when we were happy.” - Dante The phone conference was getting nowhere and it left Ash with a rather bitter taste in his mouth as the record company continued to threaten him with litigation if he didn’t come through with what he promised them. “I’m doing the best I can, right now I have a million of other things on my mind so if you don’t mind I’m going to hang up before I say something to you all that I’m going to regret.” He hit the button on the speaker to cut the call and stood up, running a hand through his hair to try and calm himself down after listening to all the bs that he was being handed. It was strange but he was starting to dislike the music business far more than he ever imagined. If it wasn’t the music it was his brother’s obsession with wrestling ... fighting whatever you wanted to call it. The spectacle of what it entailed is what Corvus liked so much more than the physicality of it (although he enjoyed that too). Ash was in no way like his brother and wasn’t out to have himself in every little thing or be in the spotlight. He was a quiet, introspective type person who just wanted to do whatever he felt like and not be bothered with it; this was the reason why the record company was beginning to get on his nerves too. “Damn my brother for getting me involved in his shit again.” He lamented over the situation that his brother even came to him looking for help. Ash had been happy with his life before he received the phone call from Corvus asking him to come to Las Vegas. He walked down the stairs needing to get out of what he considered a prison of sorts. “I really am starting to hate this craphole of a city.” He didn’t realize that he wasn’t alone when he spoke. Rhianna was sitting with a book on the couch quietly reading. “But you must really care about what goes on in his life and for his safety as a reason for coming and actually staying despite the turmoil in your own life.” Rhianna set the book down next to her and looked up at Ash with her soft blue eyes trying to sympathize with him the best she could. Las Vegas was not exactly her favorite place in the world but it was certainly better than LA and what she left behind there. “We’ve always been close; it’s been that way since we were young.” He laughed as he spoke those words since it sounded like just yesterday but was really a very long time ago; so much has happened in both of their existences that most of their recollections have been washed away. “I just feel bad for him and what he’s going through right now; it’s probably best he found something to wrap himself around. His rivalry with Fury is going to get a lot uglier before the end.” Rhianna silently nodded. She wished not to speak about Fury after what transpired the last few days. Could Fury have really known what they were or was he trying to be funny? The truth could have been out if he would have really tried to hurt her and that was something she knew couldn’t happen. “If you wouldn’t mind ... let’s not talk about Fury.” There she said it and hopefully he’d respect her wishes. Ash nodded as he did his best to look himself over to make sure he looked good enough to go out for a while. He needed to take a ride on his bike to clear his head a little bit. There were a few other things on his mind other than his meeting with the record company, wrestling a singles match, and keeping tabs on his brother and when he told Fury he didn’t know where he was technically he wasn’t lying. He had a fairly good idea where he was after the events that transpired over the past month. “Do you think I look alright?” He said as he half ass modeled himself for her for approval. Ash was wearing a pair of old school black and white vans shoes, a pair of cargo blue jeans and a white wife beater and even though it was at night, he had on sun glasses. He was trying to look cool which he didn’t have to try very hard at all to fit in around Las Vegas. “I figured this would be ok just for taking off for a few hours.” She studied him for a moment and held back a chuckle as she replied, “You seem to dress with the times which is nice. Maybe not the cutting edge of fashion but presentable enough; are you going to a club tonight?” Rhianna thought to ask since she knew that clubbing wasn’t exactly a good idea for anyone in this manor to be doing at the moment with the incident involving the police. “Nah, I’m just going to ride around and see if there’s an exciting party or something. Maybe if I could find a poetry slam in this city I’d actually be able to relax a little.” He smiled as he grabbed his keys off a table. “I’ll be back; are sure you don’t want to come with?” It was a platonic gesture of course since he knew his brother was involved with her to his knowledge. That hadn’t stopped him before but ... that is not something that will be gone into. Rhianna scrunched her face at the thought of riding a motorcycle; she really didn’t like them. “I’ll pass but thank you ... I should be fine here by myself curled up on the couch with this book.” She smiled sweetly as she could to make sure that Ash wasn’t going to worry about her. Could she take care of herself if a situation came about? I guess one would never know unless this occurrence did happen. Ash walked to the door that lead to the garage and looked back one more time to see that Rhianna was ok and that it would be fine for him to go clear his thoughts out. “If you don’t have anything going on when I get back maybe I can play some of the material I’m working on if you wouldn’t mind?” That would be the second half of his cleansing of the mind: speed and music those were two of the loves of his life. “I’d love to hear what you are working on. If you don’t see me here on the couch, I’ll probably be in my room.” (of course she meant the one she shared with Corvus, which of course Ash knew) . . . . Los Angeles, California A big meeting had been planned and all the guests were now where they needed to be. Mr. Scott (as we know him by) sat at the head of the table while his guests were comfortably on the other end. Sitting in the other chairs were men who looked rather unsavory and of Middle Eastern descent. The first impression of them might make people think they were hijackers but they were a lot more dangerous than just that. “Gentleman, thank you for coming; I’m sure that with your busy schedules it was hard for you to be here but in the end it is completely worth it.” The guests turned to look at the host before going back to one another and speaking their native language amongst each other about the meeting. A consensus of what is said is that they were uncertain of why they were asked to come and that they disliked dealing with the kind of person Mr. Scott was. The man at the other end of the table that was the spokesperson or leader of the group glanced right at Scott and spoke; his English was not broken in any way and was clearly understood. “Mr. Scott, we do not have time for pleasantries. We have come to ensure that certain information in which we did not want to be made public is now secure in your hands.” The other men spoke in their native tongue to one another while the leader awaited a reply from their foreign proprietor. “Please, call me Douglas; I’m sure you will be pleased to hear that despite a threat of a leak to the source which we do not want this information getting to, it has been recovered.” He paused for a minute looking a bit nauseated before he continues. “However, a deal had to be made with a pack of Nosferatu in order to recover it.” The Middle Easterner’s made faces as they spoke once again in their native tongue. Their leader, whom Douglas Scott only knew as Asad Fateh Al-Asfan listened for a few moments to his cohorts before speaking back to them and then once more to Douglas Scott. “And what of the infiltrator that was sent to keep tabs on our former ... ally.” Scott could have choked at that moment as he didn’t have good news when it came to having to give them an answer. He calmed himself by gathering his words first before acknowledging the question. “Well ... unfortunately they have been compromised but don’t worry, it will be handled.” If kindred could sweat, he’d be pouring buckets at this very moment. “For a fee ... we could take care of this matter for you.” Al-Asfan said and consorted with his men again. “It is our specialty ...” A grin came upon his face and a tawdry laugh followed shortly behind him. You couldn’t put hiring an Assamite past the Ventrue clan but it was bad enough that they had to sink low enough to use the efforts of the beastly Nosferatu. He shook his head as if to say he was not interested and replied to make it more known. “I think I can take care of it myself; it is personal business you could say.” Scott adjusted his tie a moment as one of the men that accompanied Al-Asfan gave him a strange look. “Very well,” Al-Asfan said as he looked to his men and gave them a sign that the meeting was almost finished with. He returned his cold gaze upon Scott and wanted to make sure that his intentions were known. “I expect you to give me an update once you’ve fully recovered the stolen information; if this happens to get into Corvus’ hands we will be back for a visit ... but it will not be pleasant for you.” The men took that as their cue to stand up and Al-Asfan did as well. “My secretary will see you out gentleman; thank you for stopping by.” “We shall find our way out on our own.” The group then left the board room hurriedly as they had a lot of things to do most certainly. The door closed and Scott relaxed back in his chair glad that the meeting was over for now. He’d hoped that his assistant, Kyle, would bring back the information from the Nosferatu so he could return it back to the men so that they wouldn’t have to come back to his office again. “I truly hope I don’t have to see them again...contemptible creatures.” The business man returned to his office and made sure that he was not to be disturbed for the remainder of the evening as he claimed that there was important business for him to take care of. Scott sat down at his desk and pulled out an artists rendering of Rhianna and gazed at it longingly; with much obsession in his eyes. “My beautiful child of the rose, how far have you fallen? He places the sketch down on his desk and gets up and walks to a cabinet in his office and pulls out a flask and a nicely detailed chalice and brings it back to the desk; pouring the contents into the glass and taking a sniff of it before drinking with pleasure. “What makes someone cast something so good aside out of the blue?” Scott speaks towards the drawing. “How could you give up such a grandiose lifestyle to cavort with a brute, an eclectic brute at that?” He questioned her motives in the long term as it seemed like maybe she was using him to find her way back to her true lover despite the fact that the man hated her in his mind. “If only he knew what you were up to and who you truly are... his view of you would change back to the one that you feared most” A chortle comes from him as he drinks from his chalice. “And you would be left with how you truly feel inside without the dark, brooding, and pathetic twit: nothing” He sits back as he contemplates finding a way to destroy her without bringing upon himself any distress from the dangerous, foreign kindred. . . . . Little did Ash benefit from cruising around the city on his bike; it only grew upon his frustrations that he found nothing to do and couldn’t help but think about his brother. The man was so close to knowing something that he was desperate to learn but the consequences of it could shatter him and that was something that disturbed the younger kindred. The past could be a troubling burden and he knew full well that was the case; that was why he was out and about this night trying to chase away his own personal battles with the ghosts of the previous nights that were not as long ago as he wished them to be. Just outside of the strip he pulled up to a stoplight and there was a couple on a bike riding around the city as well. The woman looked over at Ash and he smiled and winked right about the time the man she was with caught him doing so. “What are you looking at, freak?” “A beautiful woman ... is that a problem?” Ash spoke with a grin as he was moving his eyes back and forth from the woman to the man. “A girl like this wouldn’t give you the time of day, bro, so you might as well put them eyes in the back of your head before you get yourself into some trouble.” Ash relaxed and brought his feet down onto the street and suspected the guy was interested in creating trouble that he might as well give him what he wanted and prove to make the night a little more interesting than it had turned out thus far. “Don’t be so sure, she’s looking me up and down pretty well ...” The guy could be described as one of those little racer geeks you would see in the Fast and Furious movies that needed to get punked because they deserved to be. He was attired in what seemed to be the latest racing gear: jacket and pants, gloves you name it. “Don’t make me get off this bike, dude.” He was aggravated enough with Ash’s comments and attitude that he wanted to fight but had no idea that he had little chance of succeeding and would have his ass handed to him literally if he pressed on the issue. Ash shook his head and knew how to handle this guy and was going to take a huge chunk out of his pride by his next actions. “You look like you’re big into racing ... why don’t you put up or shut up?” He reached and pulled out the pink slip to the bike and waved it at the guy. “Not that I want your Cracker Jack ride, but I could always use something new to work on in my spare time.” Now infuriated the guy was going to take off but still wanted to see how far he could go and looked at Ash again and thought to himself that it would be an easy target and get a new bike out of it for one of his buddies. “Go beat off ... my ride would whip yours hands down.” “Go beat off huh? Maybe if your girl offered to come along then I might consider doing that.” He paused for a minute just to let his soon to be rival stew on the words a bit. “So are you up for the challenge or are you just going to sit there looking like a cack master?” That was all the encouraging the guy needed besides the girlfriend saying “Take his ass, Tommy!” He looked back over at Ash and said, “You’re on!” The bike engine revved a few times as he pulled out his pink slip and showed it to Ash just to put their deal into motion. Tommy’s girlfriend moved off of the bike to be the race starter. They backed up their bikes a little bit and she stood in between them. They had agreed to race about 10 miles down and then back to where they started. Ash reached and put on his helmet just in case and if there was any sign of police he was definitely getting the hell away from there; it was bad enough one family member was being kept an eye on as it were. The girlfriend raised her arms and held them there for a few moments before bringing them back down quickly and spun around as the two bikes whizzed past her down the street. The two jockeyed for position until Ash took a pretty decent lead in the first little stretch but he knew not to get too cocky until it was close to being over. Tommy knew that there was a bit of road construction up ahead and hoped to use that knowledge to his advantage to help him win the race. They hit a spot where the road was going down to one lane and Tommy cut Ash off to try and get the edge but there was a ramp like structure which he sped up faster and jump his bike and did a 360 in the air just for the hell of it; to show off. He landed the bike on its front wheel and did a frontal wheelie until he spun the bike around to head back the other direction. The leg back was pretty much a no-brainer as Ash kept his speed up and there was no possible way for Tommy to catch up to him. It wasn’t that he didn’t try but his bike just didn’t seem to have the same get up and go that Ash’s had at the moment. When they returned back at the starting point, Ash was quite a bit ahead of Tommy and started flirting with his girl. He didn’t even bother getting her name and had her eating out of the palm of his hand. Tommy arrived and Ash was not about to let him skimp out on his bet and motioned for him to come and give him the slip to the bike. “You didn’t really want to go through with this bet did you man?” Tommy asked while trying to play cool thinking that Ash would just let it ride and tell him to go on his way without any harsh feelings. Lowering his shades, Ash looked at him with his emerald green eyes shaking his head. “Don’t be an ass ... you made the bet and was owned and you’re trying to welsh on the bet? I don’t think so. Hand over the slip and take your goofy ass walking!” Tommy reluctantly hands over the slip and says something to the girl and they both start walking until Ash speaks up in their direction. “Oh no, I said you were walking. She needs to drive this bike to where I need to take it to.” Tommy was a bit surprised she didn’t put up a fight about it and got onto the other bike and was told to follow Ash. The two sped off down the street and Ash waved back at Tommy before whipping him the finger. You can bet that the kid was pissed off that not only did some guy he didn’t know beat him out of his bike but his girl went with him and who knows what would happen. An hour and a half later... Ash takes the girl out to a wooded area after they stopped for gas and she decided she wanted to have a little fun. I guess poor Tommy wasn’t very good in the sack ... no ones fault but his own. The two wind up where no one can see what they plan on doing. They don’t waste much time before getting hot and heavy which takes at least another hour for them to copulate; before they are finished Ash is able to get a small meal in the process. It was around the right time of his dinner hour so even if she were able to scream nobody would hear it as the closest thing to the woods was a good fifty miles away. The deed was done and the girl whom he still didn’t know the name of had fixed herself back up was about to get back on the bike but Ash grabbed a branch that was sturdy in weight and whacked her hard enough to knock her out. He leaned her against a tree for the time being and pulled out his phone and dialed a number. “Hey Shaun, it’s Ash ... I need you to come out to those woods we found a few weeks ago; I have a bike that I won the pink slip for and a girl that you need to take home for me.” He spoke to Shaun for a few more minutes before ending the conversation and waiting for him to arrive. It took about fifteen minutes for him to get there and another fifteen to load up the bike and the girl into the back of his truck. Ash slipped him some money for his trouble and the two parted ways and would contact one another in a few days. . . . . “I know the night is not the same as the day: that all things are different, that the things of the night cannot be explained in the day, because they do not then exist, and the night can be a dreadful time for lonely people once their loneliness has started.” - Ernest Hemingway The manor was quiet when Ash walked back in from his previous activities. He took off his glasses and set them down with his keys on the table he retrieved them from when he first left. His mood had lightened a little bit while he was out but it was just too much to shake what was going on in his mind and Rhianna picked up on it when she came to see who had come home; she had hoped it was Corvus but was happy to see Ash as well. “You should have been out with me tonight, Rhi, it was amazing!” His voice was full of excitement about racing his bike. “I frustrated this guy enough to get him to race bike for bike and ruled the road all over the place.” He took a pregnant pause before getting into the more interesting part of the night (well interesting to him at least). “I ended up stealing his girl away and nailing her in the woods and then called my buddy to come and get the bike I won and take the girl home ... well I think he took her home.” He chuckled as he walked into the kitchen to pour a glass of blood. The hunger was still peaking in his body and he didn’t want to end up frenzying any time soon. “You must be really happy then?” She spoke with less enthusiasm then he had. Men sometimes really bothered her with some of their antics. “I can tell there was something on your mind when you left and I don’t think it has gone from you still.” He walked out from the kitchen leaving an empty glass behind him to clean up later. A pained expression overcame him as he approached the couch and dropped down into it; trying to find a way to ease his distressed thoughts. “I think it might help if you talk about it instead of holding it back.” Rhianna did the best she could to help him without trying to give him the impression she was fishing for information. “We may no longer live as humans but some of those feelings are still very deeply seeded into our conscience.” He nodded as it was true. He still felt a lot of pain that most kindred would pass as being unnecessary and foolish to have as a burden on their undead minds. “Loss is still a hard concept for me to deal with.” “Did you lose someone close to you?” It was a inane question to ask but there was really no other way around it in her mind. He slowly nodded and did his best to speak on it. “About four months ago.” If he was going to be there for a while then he might as well get comfortable; he kicked off his shoes and set them aside and out of the way. “Was she your girlfriend or something else?” These were basic questions that needed to be asked in order for any sort of meaningful conversation was to be had. Ash almost thought he was sitting in a psychiatrist’s office only decorated better overall. “She was more than that; she was my fiancée not to mention everything I had wanted in a companion.” “How did it happen?” She said in a soft voice as she knew she’d get caught up in the story being somewhat of a romantic and this was the way the story seemed to be going. “I’m sure you’ll probably give me the same crap about it that my brother did but ... she was a mortal.” He rubbed his face coaxing himself into going on because he had to let this out and vent about it to someone that wasn’t cold hearted like Corvus. “We met at a signing when I was still working for Wrestling Midwest; I had returned home for a week to Minneapolis and was scheduled to make that appearance.” “Go on...” “She was a fan ... was one of the many people I signed an autograph for and when I looked into her eyes things just seemed to click.” He smiled as he continued on while he reminisced. “I used a little bit of my abilities to get her to meet me after the session since I could see that she wanted to. That was the extent of the powers I ever used. Everything else was just natural chemistry if you could really call it that.” “So the two of you hit it off right away? Did she ever suspect anything about you because of the habits you had?” It was a habit she had of asking things like this because she always made sure that those mortals she surrounded herself with were kept in check. “No, I ate meals with her at the cost of getting sick in private to purge my system of it; I was as romantic as someone with the heart of a poet could be.” If he were able to sigh he would have certainly done so and heavily at that. “Eventually we became engaged because I wanted her to be at my side even when I knew she would age and one day pass on but” He hesitates a little bit before finishing the sentence, “I never had the chance to.” “Please tell me what happened?” “I found out just before I was in the last days of my obligation to Wrestling Midwest that she was sick.” He balled up his fists a little and the black painted nails of his started digging into his hand to try and give him some relief. “Cassie had been diagnosed with Breast Cancer but was able to get it under control before I met her ... it just kicked out of nowhere and the Doctor’s at the hospital screwed up some tests or something and they couldn’t treat it.” Rhianna began to feel the remorse of what she was learning and knew the hardest part was yet to come in his tale. How could she comfort him? What would she be able to do that would help him deal with it any better? She remained silent and continued to listen to him carefully giving all her attention. “I had this big event that I was scheduled for but the WMW team lost and I left the company to go back home and take care of my girl; and gave her the best days of her life. I proposed to her the moment I came back from finishing what I thought was the end of my wrestling career.” He leaned his head back trying to keep his emotions from going out of control but knew before he was finished that he’d end up walking out of the room. “We had six months together and she thought there was hope that she would get better so that we’d be able to have a wedding along the river walk.” “She became sicker much faster than you anticipated then...” Rhianna said as she brought her hands to her face and covered her lips feeling the deepest amount of sorrow for her lover’s brother; his plight was very emotional indeed. “Yes, her illness accelerated quickly.” Blood tears started to slowly well up in his eyes and he did his best to hold them back. “She was gravely ill and I told her about what I was and that I could help her and even went to the Prince of the city for permission to embrace her but he declined as did she; she didn’t want to live how I have and I couldn’t blame her.” He was almost through with the account but the weight was still there and maybe it would always be. “We brought in a Chaplain and with her best friend and my brother as a witness, we were married.” Tears began to ran down his cheeks as the hardest part of this story was now about to be revealed. “She died two and a half hours later.” The blood began to stain his face and it didn’t matter to him what Rhianna had thought of the situation at this point. He was hurting and it showed. “Oh my ...” She quickly found a handkerchief and handed it to him as he dried his eyes and started to come back to a more composed state. Rhianna embraced him lightly and rubbed his back. “Such a romantic tragedy my darling; no one can blame you for trying to keep it to yourself.” Remembering he mentioned that Corvus gave him hell about the relationship she pursued a little more information. “If your brother was there for you when you were married then why did he give you grief over your feelings for this woman?” “Because he’s always had this negative attitude for kindred involving themselves with mortals and the funny part is that he’s done so in the past or I have reason to believe he has.” Now that he was back to his senses he needed to go relieve some of the anguish through music to escape from this world for just a brief time even. “I’m sorry, Rhi, I need some time ... opening wounds that haven’t quite healed is difficult.” “Do what you must; just know that I’ll be here when you need someone to talk.” “Thank you.” Picking his shoes up from the floor, he quietly exited the front room and went up the stairs to his bedroom on the second floor. He tossed his shoes on the floor as he closed the door behind him and went over to where his guitar was on its stand. Ash looked at it for a moment and picked it up; then sat down on the bed. His eyes moved towards his nightstand where a picture of his dearest Cassandra was framed for all time. Turning away from it, Ash began to play a sullen song to try and work through his heartache one more night.
