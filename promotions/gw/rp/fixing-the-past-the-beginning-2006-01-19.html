Fixing the past... The Beginning...

19 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/19/06 My good friend, I have some updated news for you? no? I did not win. I know? you don’t even have to say it. On the upside though, I was in the final four so there’s something to be said about that. An old acquaintance ended up winning the damn thing though. Yeah? It was Kyo? The Deathscythe Outlaw himself. No, I’m not jealous? And I don’t hold a grudge. I am happy for him? What? You don’t believe me? I’m turning over a new leaf. Rather an old leaf or so to say. Speaking of old acquaintances? I’m traveling to one tomorrow. Can you guess who? Yes? It’s Erika. Oh yes? I don’t think everybody else knows the story either. Why don’t I just refresh your memory my friend? Back when I started in 4WW? before I even debuted? Upper management told me that I needed to change my image. I was too? real? I don’t know how to explain it, but I wasn’t enough of a character for them I guess. Well, they wanted me to be this dark brooding guy. Like? the Crow but without the make-up and none of the killing either. I was told to fabricate a story to give myself a hatred for life? something that would make me semi-supernatural. In doing so I would have to leave all of my past behind me. My friends? my love? my family. Well? my family was already dead. Except for a lone cousin, but more on him at another time. So there I was? I could either continue to wrestle and be given a shot at true greatness, or I could hold onto all that was connected to me. So foolishly I decided to chase a dream? a dream that I should have known was false. A dream that I should have known that I could not attain in such a place. Back on track though? Most important of what I left behind were my best friend, Justin Cage? and the true love of my life, Erika Kross. Why? could I be so plainly stupid? Now I am going to try to fix those mistakes though. First, Erika, I need to see her. I need to at least apologize, even if she wouldn’t take me back ever again. This is why I have a plane ticket to Houston, Texas. It’s where she lives now? I hope. It took me a good amount of time to track her down. After we split apart she left the public scene completely. I think I have the right address now? I have to see her. Well? The plane is leaving now? I will write to you again soon? Your good friend, ~ Kris ~ +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ As the sun blazed in the sky above, Kanton began to put a couple of duffel bags into his blue rental Explorer. Decked in Mountain Dew and Nike from head to toe, Kanton kind of stuck out? not so much, but he had that look and people knew he obviously had to be important. A few of the teenagers that passed by knew who he was. There were photos taken and various objects signed. The crowd thinned out soon though and Kris entered the car and began to drive. The sound of country music played through the radio as Kanton gagged at the thought of people like Kenny Chesney and Toby Keith. It’s not that they were untalented musicians? it was just that it wasn’t his style. Finding just the right tune, Kanton began his drive through the city streets. An interesting fact about this car? it had Onstar. Nifty little thing that GPS. You could go anywhere you wanted and get exact directions. Kanton plugged in the directions on the GPS? She was only a good twenty minutes away. Kanton then entered the suburbs before coming across a nice white and tan ranch house. It was the address he had found? but was it correct? Only time would tell as he began to work up the nerves to knock on the door. His heart began to thump loudly? The air in the car began to feel like a sauna? Every minute noise began to be amplified? Then? It stopped. All of a sudden the sensation was gone. He looked to the driveway and there was one lone car. It was a red Jeep Wrangler? somewhat new. The house was well kept, as was the yard. Music could be heard from inside the house. Evanescence it sound like? Yes, that is her favorite band. With a final bit of courage Kanton stepped out of the car and began to walk towards the front door. Kanton had a black HIM hat on? partially to block the sun? partially to block her vision of his face. He knocked on the glass front door, as the main door was wide open. He could see her? standing in the kitchen while cooking. “Just a minute!” Her voice was as heavenly as ever. Kanton’s heart skipped a few beats just then. She was wearing a pair of low cut blue jeans with a black camisole like tank top. Her hair was pulled back in a familiar ponytail. It was the same look she had sported for years, but even still she was breathtaking. She was beauty in every form. She turned around the as she placed a kitchen knife down on the counter. As she made her way to the door Kanton’s heart sped up? his palms began to sweat. He could hear her? she was singing. The glass door opened as she smiled? “Hello, can I help you?” Kanton looked up at that moment. Time then? well? froze, as it would seem. Neither person could believe who they saw before them. It had been a little over two years? the emotions were still there, or at least Kanton’s were. Immediately he wanted to take her. She on the other hand? didn’t know how to react? she just looked at him with mixed emotions, some hate? some love? some envy? some misery. It was Kanton who spoke first. “Hello Erika?” Time passed between the two for a bit while longer before any words were said, but their eyes were locked? there was a connection there. “What do you want Kris” Why did you come here? Why now? What did I do to you?” The questions kept coming as the tears began to flow from her eyes. She fell into Kanton’s arms just then. It was something she had wanted to do for a good long time now. “Let’s go talk.” Kanton had said? she nodded as she wiped away the tears. She took him inside then and the conversation started? as it would then continue for a good long time afterward. Many things were sorted out, but not everything could be figured out that day? there was a lot of pain to be sorted out? The sun began to set behind the house on Range Road? and the blue car remained? Yes, there were many things to be sorted out? Many things to discover? Many things to learn? +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ My good friend, I have some updates for you as I begin to head to the Global Arena for my first appearance of 2006 in the Global Arena. I am very excited, but this won’t be an easy task? My opponents are very dangerous? that they are. I have what it takes though to win? I believe again. Only the strong survive? On a personal note? things with Erika are at a beginning stage, but things are okay so far. It was so good to feel her skin again? smell her hair? hear her voice. It was all so sweet. I want more and I will do everything I can to make her mine again? I still love her? After all this time, I still love her. She said she had something important for me, but she didn’t know if I was ready yet. I assured her I was, but she said that it could wait? It kind of worries me? but what’s happened has happened. I can’t change any of it. I can only accept the situation that I have created? I need to speak to Justin again? I found out that he now owns his own wrestling school out in Anaheim, California. He’s running it out of the old AWS complex. I can’t wait to see him, but I need to spend some more time with Erika first. Hell, at least I’m not a dad. Until next time my good friend, ~ Kris ~
