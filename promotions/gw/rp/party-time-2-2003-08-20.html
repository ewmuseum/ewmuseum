Date: 8-20-03 Life is so boring, It's really got me snoring, I'm wearing out the flooring, In a cheap motel, But I don't have to work, And I might be sinning, But I never have to listen to The rings of school bells “Right?” My voice said over my cell phone as Ben was circling around me with his camcorder, but I paid him no mind as I continued my conversation “Yes’The Youngblood Mansion’right” I said as Ben continued to get closer and closer to my face with the cam, and was now starting to distract me “Hey hold on for a minute” I lowered the phone from my ear and covered it and looked at Ben “Asshole?” I barked to Ben as he lowered the cam from his face and looked at me “That’s not my name?” Ben exclaimed sarcastically as I cocked one eye brow at him “Ok’Benjamin’I don’t care what your name is’do you want to get laid or not?” I replied hastily to Mr. Pailthorpe as I watched him shake his head rapidly up and down as I put the phone back up to my ear “Ok then’leave me alone and let me do business’Eh hem’Im sorry are you there” I said back into the phone as I turned my back on Ben and continued to talk, I glanced back around to see if Ben was still behind me’which he was “Great’so about sevenish” Ok I see see you then’buh bye? I closed my flip phone and slid it into my pocket as I once again turned to Ben “Who was that..huh’huh” Ben said like a little kid as he looked at me “That was Veronica” I replied to Ben as I grabbed my can of beer from the bar and took a drink “Ohh’she shounds sexy” I finished off the beer can crushed the can in my hand and sat it back on the bar “Yes Ben’very sexy’her and about six of her friends who are just about as sexy” I said to him as I began to walk around the bar over to the floor fridge on the other side as Ben followed me step for step “And’and’they are coming here tonite mate?” Ben excitedly asked as bent down and opened the door of the fridge and grabbed another cold beer “Well’what should I wear’what should I say” What should I do?” BJP asked frantically as I popped the top on the can and took a drink “Well?” I started as I walked back around the end of the bar “Yea’yea” Ben blurted as his eyes widened waiting for me to talk “Just’act like yourself” I shrugged as I then took a second look at Ben and shook my head “Ok..on second thought’Ben” I said as I took a seat on the nearest leather bar stool, looking at Ben’s black baggy jeans and his torn YB shirt, and his scraggy black hair that was ties in a pony tail “You ever thought about getting a haircut bud?” I asked Ben as I took another drink and watched Ben’s jaw drop at my last comment “A hair cut” A bloody hair cut Jay? Chicks dig my long hair? Ben said hastily going on the offensive by my last haircut comment “Ok..ok’a haircut might be a bit much” I replied giving Ben the benefit of the doubt on that last comment, because I knew I wouldn’t be able to get him a hair cut which he desperately needed “No not a bit much a lot much” Ben said still trying to defend his hair “Ok ok’Ben’no hair cut, but we are going to have to do something about those clothes, you don’t go out trying to get laid by wearing baggy ass jeans and a ripped t-shirt” I exclaimed to Ben as I pointed out the big hole in his shirt and his baggy jeans “Ok I agree with you on that Jay..but what should I wear?” Ben asked me as I took another drink “Well look at me Ben” I stood up from the bar stool “Lets start from the bottom” I pointed down to my feet “Ok’these are Doc Marten boots” I showed Ben my brown leather Doc boots “Yea..those are made in England” Ben exclaimed as he looked down at my boots and I looked up at him and slightly nodded my head “Yes Ben I know, ok next’see my jeans” I asked Ben as I pointed at my faded blue jeans “Yes” He replied inspecting them closely “These are Abercrombie and Fitch boot cut jeans, you want em” a little tight in the front but not to much because you will look like a cowboy or something’but just enough to where they are comfortable? I explained to Ben as I watched him shake his head as he continued to look at my jeans “Ok next’look at my shirt” I said to Ben as his eyes moved up from my jeans to my shirt “This is a white and blue horizontal striped Abercrombie and Fitch long sleeved button up shirt with the sleeves rolled up to the elbows” I explained to Ben as he looked at my shirt and I rolled up one of the sleeves which fell down a bit “Ok’so if you wear stuff made by Abercrombie and Fitch’it will get you laid?” Ben asked me in a confused tone of voice “Well’not necessarily but it helps to look good for girls’so go up stairs and find some nice clothes” I said to Ben as I looked at the grandfather clock in the corner of the room, as Ben began to head up the stairs “Oh Ben?” ben looked at me from the stairs “It’s 5:45”.people should start showing up pretty quick so hurry?” I shouted up to Ben as I grabbed my beer of the bar “Ok mate” Ben said as he disappeared up the stairs **Ring **Ring “Yeap?” To Be Continued
