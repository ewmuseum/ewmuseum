Cashing In On A Favor

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-10-06 Eryc paces the hallway of the Eggdome with uncertainty to why he was there in the first place. It was pretty clear that after his “health crisis” and subsequent mental breakdown that he was unwanted in Global Wrestling. “This is ridiculous” was the thought going on in his mind as he continued pacing. If he paced any faster he’d likely put a hole in the concrete. Things had changed from the time he walked away from Global and the time he received a phone call from Bubba McCoy, who made it sound vastly important that the two of them meet in Japan. He wrestled a few matches after his departure but those bouts were nowhere near comparable to the type that he could have in Global. The rush of the crowd combined with the octagon was everything he desired but his philosophy clashed with that of those who were running Global in Bubba’s absence. “Come on McCoy, I don’t have forever ... at least not anymore.” Morgan had only time to think about his fighting career and where it was headed from here. It also reminded him of what has already past him. How would the fans react to him now? Would they accept him as anyone other than Corvus? The greatest and sweetest relief came out of the fact that he didn’t have to hide. The secrets he had kept for so long were taking a toll on him personally. Many have speculated there was something different about Corvus (the name he wrestled under before going the route of using his “real” name). His pale skin and his association with reddish fluid that he brought to the ring raised questions that the undead could possibly exist. They were not wrong. Little did they know that secretly the “Dark Prince” was truly a creature of the night (as was Ash). However, Eryc did everything in his power to hide his curse. Others of his kind call it “The Masquerade”. It was a time honored tradition of preserving their secret to the world for fear of chaos if they were discovered. Enter Jake Hix. Jake was supposedly a “warrior of God” and he was on a mission to rid the world of evil entities including vampires. Eryc’s secret was discovered when the two of them happened upon the same lair of vampires. It led to an uneasy alliance between them because Jake figured he could use a powerful entity like Eryc to aid him in his quest to topple his brother Sid, who was behind some of the evil both men were looking to destroy. This quest led them to New Orleans and a small battle with Sid’s goons. In this brawl one of the men injected Eryc with some unknown serum which had very diverse affects...in fact it reversed his physiology and he once again became mortal. This change led to his disappearance from not only Global but from the world. Eryc went into hiding and did not leave any clues behind as to why or when (if at all) he would return; even Ash who knew more than anyone else did about his brother did not have any idea of the whereabouts of his older sibling. During the time spent in isolation, Eryc modified his appearance by cutting off most of his long, black, locks and also added artwork to his skin. He was still recognizable in public but he did not go out all that much. The only thing he did was train to keep his body in shape now that he needed to and did what research he could to find out if his new condition was permanent or just dormant. The research led him to dead end after dead end which prompted him to make his journey back to where everything started, all with the help of a phone call from Bubba McCoy. * * * Unsure if McCoy was calling him to give him a job again or if there was something else, Eryc could only hope that it would be the start he needed to get closer to Jake Hix and find out what truly happened in New Orleans and why. The city went through devastation as a result of the hurricanes that took place there last summer and the idea of traveling there to search for information was a wasted one which would only bring up more questions. Eryc’s concentration is broken as he hears a familiar voice call out to him from just a few feet away. “You look extra sharp today, bro.” Clad in all black as usual, Eryc turned towards the direction of the voice to see Ash standing there. The younger Morgan didn’t have the same need to dress fashionably as his older brother; just wearing the basic motocross pants and tank top suited him fine. Eryc was speechless. He did not expect to see his little brother in the same place as him when Ash did not have nearly the same passion to be in the octagon as him. “It’s cool, don’t say hello then. I guess I will just tell you that Bubba wants to see you and be on my way.” Ash shrugged and left it up to Eryc who stopped him from completely leaving. “I didn’t expect to see you here of all places but if I am right about the feeling I am getting right now, it means the two of us seeing another is not by chance.” Ash acknowledged his brother’s comments but left them for Eryc to dissect on his own. “My suggestion is to just listen to what McCoy has to say and when you’re finished the two of us should have a nice little chat.” Ash adds to his statement after a short pause. “You owe me that much after ...” Eryc raises a hand up to stop Ash from continuing ... an ultimatum was the last thing he needed. Nothing would change the fact that he was a human and that he knew there would be consequences to everything that happened from the moment he vanished. “Just let me talk to the man and as you suggested and then we’ll talk.” The tone in Ash’s voice was positive as he slightly pleaded. “Keep an open mind to what Bubba has to say would you?” “Shut up and stay put”, was all that was said as Eryc turned and looked back with a smirk on his face. He then walked through the designated door that displayed Bubba’s name on it and while Eryc’s back was turned, Ash mimicked him like a child for his own amusement. Ash found it odd that he was around his brother again but things were different now. The uncertainty of how their ability to co-exist would come into play now that they were from opposite sides of the mortal coil. Mortals and kindred did not mix well due to the fact that the (humans) that did know about them normally tried to wipe them out without hesitation. Eryc was not likely to take that road but he was still a liability for what he knew and it made him dangerous. Ash knew he’d have to keep an eye on him regardless. After big brother took off, Ash stuck around the Las Vegas scene to keep up with bike racing and the occasional music gig (which did not go so well without his brother). He also stopped working for GW since there wasn’t much sense in being swept under the rug after everyone had their mind made up that Eryc was a tyrant. As they say it’s guilt by association. Ash’s impatience was becoming prevalent as he leaned against the wall; glaring at the door to Bubba McCoy’s office. Eryc had been in there at least a half hour if not longer which prompted him to wonder what was different about what Bubba was telling him unless the big guy was being difficult again. The door finally opened and Ash caught a glimpse of his brother and Bubba standing together. They shook hands before the head honcho went back into his office to deal with other business. The only thing that was said that Ash could hear was him congratulating Eryc on something. Ash decided to let Eryc tell him what happened inside the office instead of reading his mind (which was easier to do now that Eryc was mortal). ?(It) looks like we’ll be stepping in the octagon again small fry.” Eryc said as he rubbed his chin in thought about how the words he just spoke sounded. The younger Morgan was skeptical about how this was all going to play out but it would give him something to do and he could have access to just as many women if not more by being on television again. “You’re not going to have a hissy and bolt on me again are you?” Eryc silently shook his head no and this time he didn’t have a reason to leave, there was more incentive to stay around. “Everything will work out as it should just have a little faith in me. I’ve done some maturing since the two of us were last in the other’s company.” The statement prompted him to further comment about Ash mimicking him earlier. “At least one of us has ... because as far as I can tell, you haven’t changed any.” Ash felt the desire to throw a forearm but it was too early for them to squabble. Besides, Ash had the advantage now over his brother who was nowhere near the strength he once had. “What do we do now?” Ash inquired. Eryc was no longer needed by Bubba and was told to go have a great evening on the town or stay to watch the Showdown in Tokyo if he so desired. It didn’t fit his idea of a good time when he could be enjoying some of the finest cuisine and the most excellent sake he could imbibe. “I don’t know about you, but I’m going to a restaurant.” Eryc’s stomach rumbled even as he spoke. “I’m hungry and all the talk about returning to Global has made my appetite grow.” Ash frowned at his brother’s new found needs. They disgusted him and he didn’t want to have any part of it. “You go on ahead ... I’ll just catch up to you at the hotel.” Eryc nodded before strolling down the hall and was no longer in Ash’s line of vision. The choices of things to do were limited for Ash. He could go find some hapless Japanese girl for his meal ticket or stay and watch the event take place in the building. His need to feed was minimal and that left him with finding his way to the VIP area to take in SiT. * * * With a full belly and a nice buzz going on, Eryc arrives back at his hotel room and enters it with the electronic key card that has become so fashionable these days. He pulls off his coat and drags himself over to the bed and plops down with every intention of not moving until morning. “It looks like someone had a good time.” Eryc’s eyes widened and he sat up to see his brother sitting in a chair across the room. He was startled to the point that his heart was racing like a stock car at Talladega. The obvious question was how Ash got into the room. Knowing how crafty a vampire could be, Eryc let it slide. “Don’t you know how rude it is to creep into someone’s room like that?” Ash thought it over and then laughed about it. “This is not a private residence; I don’t need to be invited and you of all people should know that.” He was right about that one. They were in a public place in which all are welcome thus negating the “invite” clause that restricts a vampire from entering. Technicalities can be a pain in the ass sometimes. “Fair enough.” Eryc sighed as he resigned to the fact that he was going to have to bring Ash up to speed about everything that happened during the time he was away. “You probably agree with me when I say that this new run with Global is not going to be easy. I’m putting myself out on a limb just even talking to you right now.” Ash said hesitantly. “There are others that know of your crossing back into the land of the living and they are not big fans of yours.” “What is preventing them from finding me and then killing me” I’ll be honest when I say that I consider myself lucky to have lived this long without my former brethren knocking on my door.” Ash knew the reason why the other vampires were not hunting him but was reluctant to say. The subject was starting to make him uncomfortable but he did not allow himself to show it. “I’m supposed to keep an eye on you and if you do anything to jeopardize the Masq, I’m supposed to eliminate you myself.” Eryc was amused to think that vampires could be so stupid. He was one of the biggest sticklers when it came to breaking the laws of his former kind. If he knew of anyone that was infringing upon these rules he would personally see to their punishment with approval of the vampire that ran the city he was in at the time. “Right, I’m such a threat.” Eryc said in a sarcastic tone. “I can die just as easily as anyone, so I doubt the likelihood of going out to purposely look for a fight just to have my throat ripped out ... no thanks.” If only Eryc knew how dangerous he was to the kindred society he wouldn’t be making such joking remarks. When he was a vampire he was feared by many for the terror he spread across the world. He was a stone cold killer and rumors ran rampant that he’d even go after the blood of his own which many were leery of. “You know what Eryc” Just say what is on your mind and let it out. You’re not fooling me by pretending to be so calm about all of this.” A tone tinged with anger filled the air as Ash continued speaking. “Let’s just get it all out in the open man. You hate me for what I am and what you were and you think that I hate you because you’re supposed to be my enemy.” His words were not all that far off. They had always been close ever since they were young and after Eryc was turned he grew to view humans as nothing more than food. If he was asked he wouldn’t deny it. There was no blame that could be placed on Ash for something that is common as the tension that is built up between them. “Don’t make this out to be anything more than it is because we have a job to do and if that is all you want it to be then so be it. I have a reason for coming back here and that is to find some answers.” He paused for a minute; his cold attitude towards the situation made it hard for him to try and reach out to a man who was close to him but yet so far away. “Listen, we don’t have to be anything other than partners in the octagon, which is fine with me. I know that I wouldn’t be able to do this without you so let’s just make the best out of an uneasy situation.” “McCoy told you then?” “Yeah, he informed me that he’d give me a chance if I worked with you for a while and if that turned out then maybe I could have other opportunities.” Eryc didn’t care about the “probation” he was put on by Bubba because it meant less stress for him and more time to concentrate on gathering information. In time he’d find Jake Hix and get him to tell about what happened one way or another. As much as Eryc wanted to know about what happened to him, Ash was just as curious. Was there a cure for vampirism and if so would he be able to use it as well? There were way too many anomalies for him to get his hopes up at this juncture. “Eryc, do you think that your present condition is permanent?”
