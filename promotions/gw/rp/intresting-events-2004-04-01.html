Intresting Events Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #1082 Date: 4/1/04 Location: Conversation with myself.. ((What the Fuck was Going on..Four days ago i was sure that it was me walking into COTT and me walking out with a shot at the World title..I seen all that shatter about my Knees when a Combination things happened..One an Old Demon came back to haunt me and Now i begin to Haunt another..Once again this endless Cycle. Begins and Ends..I Know that this Has to be eating Brick up as well. Let me tell you something Old Man...I was watching the Night that Draven almost ended Aarons Life. It diddent seem that Ailen to me..I knew what Draven could and would do..It wasn't anything for him to go out there that night and almost Kill a Man..With out a problem..He could and would Kill you me and your little sister with out Remorse. It takes a man without a soul to do that. 'Such a Man used to be my friend. I know not what he thinks of me now. I still hold him in high respects..as a Friend i know not how far that little party man go.' ((Let me tell you a story of a man who in fact..In his Younger years was fascinated with Death. He would go out and kill things to see the life flow from their eyes..He Once told me that Killing took him to another place. He said it's almost like i die with them. I get a look at death without dieing..)) 'Mr. Derek Draven at the age of seventeen would in fact go out with myself and others. we would go in search of things to Kill. Rather Brutish..but thats how things were. We would sit and talk sometimes...Others times it was Kill anything we could find. Cows..Dogs. Frogs..Nothing was safe. Never once did i venture to take a human life..But I'm very sure that Dravens has had life slip through his hands very many times..' ((But to no Avil i have seen life slip through my hand..very different feeling..Dravens Held Life while i mearly watching it become and pass...Very Different Story. A Coming to Pass that is soon arriving. Will be Myself..Max Rosewell and a Mr. Rich Sherwin..Max and I know each other very well and i am very happy indeed that we get the chance to wrestle again.)) 'Well, I should say He gets the Chance to get his ass kicked..Again. C'mon max. It's almost not worth trying. So far your winning percent against me is Zero. Both me and you have Faced Twice I know. Twice at PPV's, Both times i came out with a Cruiser weight title shot and you came out a Loser. You want to feel like that again Max. Well. I know you do...You'll get that chance..Lets see if after that break you had you can come back as strong as Latino Lightning..' ((A Named i was dubbed in high school. But that is another story for another time. The Idea at hand is the one of my upcoming match. Now. It's been a long time since i held a title. It's been a long time since i won a match...I'm looking to star a Trend..Right here and Right now i claim to stop at nothing short of death to Claim a title. I wont be Picky..Anything will do.)) 'I'm a Latin Guy and I've noticed more and more that i am the only Latin wrestler in GW....Not that it does me any good but I've noticed. I'm the only one of my Race here. Not a problem. It just makes it more of a something to do in GW when i win a title. I'm not sure that there have ever been any Hispanic wrestlers in GW.' ((Which means that I Do things in a Very different fashion then your used to. To me. I'm all about the Money. I don't care..If I'm getting paid..I'll do it..For the right price of course..Every man has a price. Weather it be Money...Women Drugs...You name it men and women can be bought with it..)) 'People Like Derek Draven though. They cant be bought with Much. They do things their way. Never accept a Gift. Never give thanks..Be as Harsh and as Bad ass as you can be...All the time..' ((Draven's a Hard Man..Has been and always will be. The things that he has done will be with him the rest of his life. His deeds many times may be so Deadly and Ungodly..That they will be with him in his after life...His Deeds will go unnamed..Why? Because he wants them unnamed. He doesn't want you to know what he can come at you with. It's like fighting in the dark when you face him...But to me it's like bathing in the light. I know what he can and cant Do. All of it. I've seen his worst and his best...You have seen a glimpse of neither...)) 'But of Course Brick will say he's seen it all. Old Man you haven't seen anything...He could have killed you at will..He kind of have had a soft spot for you. As much as a thorn in his side as you were..I was for sure for about three months he was going to kill you.' ((Nothing said...Nothing done. Look at it like that....You cant say something and do nothing about it. In thus business you have to be all action and Big Talk. Here you heave to be the real deal. Some better then others and all better then most. Thats why everyone doesnt wrestler. Their not good enough. We are something made.)) 'We made ourselves. At first we wre just children with a dream..Now we mold our bodys and minds to our liking to take a beating. Some of the traning i've seen done by some has frightened me. Other times it has appeld to me. Something Dangrous..Something Fast Paced..' ((Thats whats i'm about. I'm about going all out on all occasions..Top make what i want happen. I'm about me. It's all about me. I care nothing about you or any one else. I do things my way. Mine MIne Mine..)) 'Now, Derek Dravens match is going to be Right after mine..The colsest we will have wrestled in ten years...I'll be watching.. I'm sure in your absesce you have grown many things...I'll be watching..I hope you'll be watching mine..You'll see max Rosewell and Rich Sherwin take their posistions below me in a title hunt..Becuse thats what i plan on doing. beating you both so bad that there is not a reasonable doubt that i'm better...Then both of you..' ((Theres no doubt in my mind. I'm Hispanic...I'm a mexican..Not a Mexi-can't... Your Life is Dictated by the choices that you make. What kind of choices do you make. Sometimes wrong...sometimes right. Good,Bad..Black, White..It's all the same now. How can you people tell if they make the write or wrong choices. It's impossible. You cannot say at the time. If that choice will have a negitive or positive impact on youre life. So what do you do. You make the desision you Think is right and go about your merry little business..)) 'I know that some of the things i do you may notlike. I know that sometimes. I talk alittle junk about your beloved American Youth Josh and other Morons that say they grace the wrestling busines by their presence. They dont know whats wrestling is about.' ((So you know what i say about it all. Lets do it. Lace up the boots anbd throw down. lets get on with it. I'm alaways ready. Are you Max..Rich you either. Maybe. We'll see. Now and then it looks good and it looks back. Somes times it's raning...Sometimes it's raning shit. Now i know how it goes. From times to time. Things look good. You think that your life is looking up and then BAM..Your dead.)) 'Dead....' ((it's something scary to think about right. Death. I think death with be a welcome blessing but right now we dont think that. Ther are times when you would howl and wish for death before you ever would think to stand and fight. Times when your down and out and you think theres nothing left to live for. Those are the times you must rallie your last rements of life and throw them head long into the attackers face and fight for all your worth..)) 'Fight for all your worth. Intresting consepot.. You lay it all on the line. UYour life on the line. To gain a very minumian profit. really for putting your life on the line in some cases..You recive very little Hazard pay..' ((Do you think any of us get such as that. Hell..Some times it unbareable to think about what happens in eveyday life.....I love wrestling but it's demanding. and i demand alot from my opponets. I dont just go in and beat you or have a great match with you. I like to test you. Beat you to no end and then make you come back for more. I'm like the greatest sales men. I'll kick your ass and make you think you have a chance. What a Show..What a world...)) 'Rosewell and Mr. Sherwin...They find them selves in a sort of prediuciment. In any case two of us could band on the other and get rid of him in a stately fasion...Or i could in fact beat them both hands sodnw and make it quick...I dont like quick...Long and drawn out..It's easier to understand.. Watching you slowly lose hope...Untill the end when you realize that you have no chance and then it's to late...Your hit with the Caja La Paradojo and it's over...' ((Slowly.....Slowly.....Slowly.......Hahahahahaha....Slowly...)) Fin!
