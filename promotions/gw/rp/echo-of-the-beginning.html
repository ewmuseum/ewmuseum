Echo Of The Beginning

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Before Oddities surrounded him, but he had never noticed them before. Layers of reality built upon each other to develop the common views of sanity, but he had seen the layers stripped away like so many cheap layers of clothes on the homeless until the dirty flesh beneath was exposed, raw and unwashed. After everything he had lived through he expected his life to have settled down, but the Lord had never allowed him a moment’s peace. He was always being tested, compared and submitted to the judgments of others. “Why should now be any different?” He asked himself as he stayed in the darkness of the alleyway. So much had changed in such a short period. He believed the turmoil had finally ended, but where there was a death he should have known a birth waited in the wings. The thickness in the pocket of his trench coat seemed weightier than anything else in the world. He had read the passages, notes and diagrams so much he could quote them. The tome with him was easily the most engaging of the lot, but he had ten more journals in his car locked in a secret compartment no one knew about except him. The rain had stopped falling, but the residual humidity hung in the air all around him just like the cloaking darkness he nestled himself in for safety. The steady drop of water rolling off the roof of the warehouse, collecting until heavy enough to fall like thundering drumbeats in his mind. The echo took him back to the Reservation’to times that he once thought were darker. Alleys were homes to one of the layers in society people liked to ignore. The rancid smell of vomit and human waste should have made him gag, but he had been in far worse places with far worse conditions. Now he only let the sound and smell keep him awake, preventing his mind from wondering. The moment needed exactness, and even the flask in his other pocket would not be taken out for this moment. All roads had led him here, divergences coming back around to the irony of the day. His steady march away from the business had brought him full circle until everything converged here where the only way he could get what he wanted was to go back into the business. The straightforward nature of the game called him back like an old lover. He missed the clarity his former job allowed him, but he had avoided these entanglements for so long because he feared losing his focus on the task he must perform. Roadblocks were easy to find, and he didn’t really want to be doing this anymore anyway. Some commitments transcended desire? Some promises could never be broken? Blood tied men together more than weakness could rip them apart? The car pulled up. Car was not the right term, rather a chauffer driven limousine. The man had taste and style, selecting a BMW for his journey. The man liked the trappings of his success. The man in the alleyway had been in those vehicles himself, surrounded by the leather and luxury features. Moon roof, bar, television, surround sound and all the liquor or other entertainments someone could desire. The scene was watched by the concealed figure as the occupant stepped out of his ride. He was strong, six feet four inches tall and a tight two hundred and sixty pounds. He had a strong, confident gait as he strolled over to the stairs. He stopped and looked back. The woman was gorgeous, she moved like an acrobat with smooth, athletic movements that brought forth images of a proud lioness on the savanna. Her body was string, but not buffed out like so many in the business, maintaining the femininity that he had seen on the television. Beneath the leather pants and clinging, red top he could see a body he once would have been drawn to. They both walked up the back stairs of the club to the guarded entrance, where the large bouncer stepped aside allowing them inside. Club Paradigm was a big place, and the VIP clientele were treated like royalty. The two of them would have a private room at the least, shielded from the noise of the club in an almost soundproof quality although the sound system within the room was just as powerful and deafening when needed. Decision made, he stepped for the out of the darkness into the subdued yellow light from the lamp on the side of the building. The long black hair concealed his face from view. He reached into his pocket, ignoring the flask as he gripped the other item he had resting beside it. He climbed the steps slowly. “Don’t you fucking guys ever learn!”!” The bouncer said as he shook his head, the yellow light gleaming off of the brown surface. “Well, time to teach another lesson isn’t it you pathetic piece of shit! This ain’t no fucking shelter!” The bouncer reached out with both hands. All he saw was a man in a dirty trench coat with black jeans and a button-up black shirt underneath. He should have noticed the trench coat was the only item looking worse for wear. Instead, he was too focused on getting to beat someone again for the fun of it. The bouncer’s arms were sent sideways by a quick double parry from the large man. Only now as he stood upright moving toward him did he notice the man’s size. The taser was slammed into the bouncer’s chest, the volts of electricity racing through his body. The man released the trigger as he grabbed the man by the throat and groin. He pressed him overhead and threw him into the street at the bottom of the steps. A kick to the back of the head ensured he wouldn’t be getting up anytime soon. The taser went back into his pocket. He opened the back door and proceeded down the dark hallway to the flashing, multi-colored lights at the end. The club opened up to him, and a small crack of a smile broke through his beard covered face as he looked around. The structure inside was huge. The box design was simple, yet highly effective with the center dominated by a huge DJ Booth manned by someone he didn’t recognize, yet the music was setting the fast, wild tone for the party. There were small stages to each side of the booth with young women dancing there, all club goers from what he could tell making them part of the evening. The two side were lined with tables and chairs, all filled by expensive looking types of people. The bar was massive, running the entire length of the back wall to serve as much alcohol as possible. The dance floor dominated the center. Of course, the club had a second story with spiral staircases leading to the top on each side of the stage. The two of them were moving to the right, taking residence in one of the private VIP Rooms for the evening. He proceeded through the dance floor, ignoring the looks of longing and wonder as he approached the staircase. No security at the top or bottom, but he felt a few eyes lingering on him in the background. He knew he would draw attention, and he was unconcerned because he wasn’t an obvious threat to most of the amateur eyes. The stairs went quickly, and he saw that his path would prove slightly more difficult now. The room had a security guard stationed outside. He would be easy to remove, but then the conversation he needed to have would be interrupted. That would simply not do at all. He surveyed the area looking for something to help him. He saw a woman and man leaning against the wall, with two men trying to converse with the woman who was ignoring them. He walked forward and leaned against the rail, feigning watching the club as he listened in to the people behind him. “Come on baby, what are you doing here with that loser” You should come party with some real men.” “Ignore them Harold, they’re just drunk assholes.” “I think I should get someone to remove them, or else I’ll ruin the night when I kick their asses!” “Enough Harold! It took forever for us to get into this club, and I will not have it ruined! Just ignore them.” “What a wimp. Woman needs to know what it’s like to be with real men!” “Yeah, we got something big and strong for you baby!” Hew turned around. The two men were sharing a laugh as they looked at each other. As he passed he grabbed the woman’s ass from behind. The move did exactly what he hoped. “DON’T TOUCH ME! HE GRABBED MY ASS!” She screamed. Harold had seen enough. He stepped beside his woman and slammed a fist into the man’s face, knocking him down. The other man fought back, and they were soon engaged. The bouncer, seeing a fight so close to the VIP rooms stepped forward immediately to stop them. The stranger simply walked past him and into the VIP room. The door closing behind him sealed them off into silence as the music had not been piped in yet. The room was very modern, with angled metallic supports bracing the walls with black angular furniture and red walls. The man was seated at a chair with an awkward looking triangular backing, looking up at the new arrival with barely interested dismissal. The woman was moving toward him with danger in her eyes. “No autographs. You slipped by security, but just get out of here before you bother me anymore.” Christian Zenith gave him a dismissive wave of the hand. The man smiled, “You are the arrogant prick everyone makes you out to be Christian. No matter, I can ignore that for the moment. You and I have business to discuss. You’re going to tell me what I want to know.” Christian simply laughed. The laugh was small, just like he thought the man was standing before him. He barely allowed anything to escape his throat as a sign of contempt for the man he was seeing before him. Vivica Valentine had heard enough. He was some simple street hoodlum, and they had all dealt with them plenty enough in their previous lives. She came around the chair, and threw a right cross at the man’s dirty, unkempt hair covered face. The blow never landed? The man caught Vivica’s fist with his open hand. He grabbed her under the arm with his right hand and twisted his hips. Vivica was sent flipping over with a simple judo throw. Vivica had not expected the move, but then the man did something stupid when he turned away from her to move after Christian. Vivica rolled back onto her shoulders, lifted her legs into the air and pushed up with her arms snapping herself quickly onto her feet. The man turned back around to receive a right cross that connected. He ignored the blow, and threw a right hand of his own. Vivica ducked easily, then bent forward bringing her boot over her back and slamming it into the man’s face. He stumbled back slightly. “You should have just left. It would have hurt less.” Zenith picked up his glass and took a sip as he watched the exchange. Vivica went for a kick to the side, but the man turned it away with both hands. He slammed a fist into the side of her ribs. Vivica nailed him in the side of the head with an elbow. The man grabbed her by the shoulder and hip, hoisting her light frame into the air as he threw her at the steel support. Vivica grabbed the support with her outstretched hands. She turned, her feet pushing off the wall and rebounded back at him. The man saw it coming. He grabbed her in mid-air and slammed her down over his knee. Grabbing a fistful of hair he pulled her head up as his other hand brought the 9mm pistol out from his belt. He put the barrel flush against her face and looked into the eyes of Christian Zenith. The man looked back at Vivica to see a surprise, a Desert Eagle just inches away from his temple. He locked eyes with the fiery Vivica who had a little bit of a smirk on her face. “They don’t allow firearms on premise here at Club Paradigm. I know, being a very special guest of the owner and all.” Christian Zenith said calmly as he leaned back in his chair. He took another sip of his drink. The man shrugged, “Rules don’t always apply.” “You’re telling me.” Christian was enjoying this. “Vivica, let’s see what the man came here for.” “No way. Let me smoke this Motherfucker right now. Clean, quick and easy.” Vivica offered. “He has position on you. Pulling the trigger could put off your aim. Let him be.” Christian replied. Vivica didn’t have much of a choice. She stood up slowly, and he took away the Desert Eagle. She moved over to the side where she would be out of the way. The man now had a clear line to Christian, with no distraction “Now, back to what we were discussing. You have information I need, and you are going to tell me what I need to know.” Christian laughed again, but this time he was not dismissing the man. “I hate to disappoint you, but having a weapon pointed in my direction doesn’t intimidate me.” “Really?” “Yes. This isn’t the first time someone has decided to take aim at my person.” “How many times have you been shot?” “That’s a rather personal question, and we don’t really know each other well enough for me to respond to such a personal question. You seem to know my name, and I am sure you are acquainted with the lovely Vivica Valentine. What is your name stranger?” The man was laughing now, “I think you know it, but aren’t sure if I am the man.” “Are you the man?” “I am.” He moved his hair back with his free hand. His face was plainly visible even with the heavy beard. “None of that matters though.” “You are well known. Will you really shoot me here when hundreds of people will see you come and go?” Zenith pointed out. The man adjusted his shoulder and squeezed the trigger. The bullet tore down the barrel, and the path went just beside Christian’s head. He could feel the air whip and the heat from the bullet on his cheek as it passed. The bullet struck the wall behind him, marring the serene surface the club had so perfectly maintained. The bullet was buried in the wall. Vivica made a slight move, but the man’s face stopped her cold. Christian’s face was still stony and calm, but something had obviously changed. He was not quite as easy going now that a bullet had almost struck him in the face. “Let me make my position perfectly clear. I am not going to take any bullshit, and any more dancing around and one of these bullets goes into you and into her. Then I will begin to get nasty about it all.” The man threatened. Christian Zenith smiled, “What is it that you want?” The man reached into his inner coat pocket and pulled out a picture. He held it in front of the man. “I am looking for this man.” Christian shrugged, “Who is he?” “His name is Todd Walker. You would have met him several years ago during the time you were enjoying your youth before. He was someone associated with your little ministry, and I want to know where he is. More than that, I want to know who he was, who he is and everything you might possibly know about him. Tell me where he is and I can let you live.” The man demanded. “And if I don’t?” Another bullet came out of the pistol’s barrel, slamming into the wall on the other side of his head. “What if I do” What I know is outdated at best, and if you are willing to go to these lengths then there must be something else you desire. I mean, what is this man to you? He was nothing when I knew him, and so he must still be nothing. There was no potential, no possibility of greatness and certainly no strength in that one. Why do you seek him?” “He is the next link.” Christian was very relaxed now, “The link to what?” “To what I need to find.” “Tell me what this is, and perhaps I can help you even more.” The man looked over at Vivica Valentine. Christian saw it, “You’re worried about her hearing?” “If you want my answer I will give it to you and to you alone.” Christian motioned to Vivica, “Give us a moment.” “Christian!” “Don’t worry. Leave us be for a moment.” Vivica Valentine looked angry, not liking this idea at all. She walked past the man, and he handed her back the Desert Eagle as she passed. She left the room, and they were inside the soundproof room alone. “Now tell me what it is you need.” Christian asked. The man told him. The door opened, and the stranger walked out. He gave Vivica a brief glance before walking away. Christian Zenith walked out of the room. “So, what did he want?” Vivica asked. Christian looked at her in the eyes, “He wanted to find the man to find someone else. I have offered to help him.” Vivica was surprised, “You offered to help?” “Well, let’s just call it an exchange of services. Don’t you know who he is?” Christian’s eyebrows were raised. “No.” “Let’s just say we have our very own big, bad dog from this point forward. Everything on the down low, but as long as we help him, he helps us.” Christian led her back inside to explain.
