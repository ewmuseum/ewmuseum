Date: 5.24.03 Regret Ace Cash Promo # 1 for GW Dead-Zone It was the happiest day of Ace's young life. His best friend had messed up real bad when he let lost this girl. Ace couldn't help but stare into her eyes for too long. It seemed to weird her out a little at first. 'Are you ok, Ace?' She would always ask, a look of concern on her face. Ace always felt like such an idiot when she said that, because he didn't realize until just then that he'd been starting at her. 'Yeah, I'm fine. It's just you're beautiful is all.' Ace replied, a meek smile on his face. 'I can't help but stare sometimes.' 'Ooooh, Ace. That's so sweet.' She would reply, cuddling close to him on her couch. There was no happier place for Ace to be at that point in his life. Having the girl of his dreams with him was all he ever wanted, and now he did. His friend Joe Leone had really messed up when he lost her. She was perfect. Ace can still remember to this day every little detail about that night. He remembered the perfume she was wearing, sometimes he swore he could still smell it. He remembered the way she was wearing one of his shirts. Every little detail to the night. It had been simple enough, just a movie at her house. But Ace couldn't help but feel as if it were something much more as he sat closer to her. Love That word kept rushing into Ace's mind! He couldn't control it. He was too young to be in love.. wasn't he? That night seemed to go by in a flash. It ended far too soon for Ace's tastes. He was more than a little dissapointed as she led him to her door. Why? He wasn't quite sure, he supposed he just didn't want the night to end. She stopped him at the door and looked up into his eyes, with a smile on her face. 'I had fun! That movie was kind of lame, but it was better with you here.' She says, rocking back and forth on her feet. She's nervous. Did he have fun, she didn't know. Will he kiss her? 'Yeah, me too.' Ace replies, not quite sure if she even really liked him all that much, 'I...uuuh..had fun.' The akward silence started right about then. Should he just go before it gets even worse? Should he apologize for being so stupid? What should he do!? 'So, did you study for that Chemistry test tomorrow?' Ace says, trying to ease the mood, but she only laughs at him. 'No. Did you?' She asks. Did he not want to kiss her? Was she not good enough? Oh, she hoped not! Enough of this. She couldn't wait anymore. 'Ace.. why don't you like me?' She asks, lowering her head a little bit. She didn't want to make eye contact with him, just in case she began to cry. 'What? Why did you ever think th-' Stopping in mid-sentence, Ace sees a tear running down her face. Smooth move, asshole. Now you're making her cry. You're an idiot!! Ace reaches and gently brushes the tear off of her cheek, and tilts her head up to look at him. There are those eyes again! Her beautiful brown eyes, he couldn't stop staring. Ace slowly leaned towards her, his face coming closer to hers. His lips... meeting hers. Ace had always heard that the best way to know if you were in love was by the first kiss. And now Ace knew he was. He started to pull away, but went forward again, kissing her again before leaving out the front door, heading to his car. He sits in the seat for a little while before starting the car- smiling. This was a very special girl. He wasn't going to let her pass him by, he thought as he started his car and drove away from her house. The girl? Tiffany Jo Murray. ---- ---- How fast things change. Back then it was so much simpler, so much easier. Tiffany would later become Ace's manager in his wrestling career, and they would spent several happy years together. It was't until about a month ago that they had a single fight. Ace doesn't even remember what it was about, all he knows is that she's gone. She left back for Proctor, Minnesota, leaving him in San Antonio to live in a house they bought together.. except now he was all alone. Everywhere Ace goes, there are pictures of him and Tiffany. He can't stand it as he walks around the house with a cardboard box, putting all the pictures in it. He misses her. He places a few pictures in the box before coming to his favorite. That was a good day. She looked so beautiful, we were in love. The picture was taken in Proctor, Minnesota. Both Tiffany and Ace are wearing heavy winter coats, standing in front of Tiffany's mothers house. Ace is hugging Tiffany from behind, his arms around her waist and his chin on her shoulder. Her head is leaning to the side, resting against his. Both are smiling. So genuine. So true. So... real. Ace holds the picture in his hand, and it starts to shake a little bit. He did that from time to time. It wasn't long until a tear gently rolled down his cheek. STOP IT! Get a hold on yourself, Ace. Stop this crying bullshit! You're too strong for that. Ace squints his eyes shut hard and hurls the picture into the wall, glass shattering. Why did I have to be so stupid? Why'd I let her leave!? In all the time he was with Tiffany, he had felt many emotions. Lust, love, honor, pride, loyalty- just to name a few. But this was new for him. It was a feeling he had hoped he would never have to experience from his relationship with her. Things used to be so happy...so simple. All there was... was happiness. Now, there's only regret.
