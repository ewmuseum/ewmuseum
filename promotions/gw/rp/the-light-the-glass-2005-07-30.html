The Light & The Glass

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.30.05 07.24.05 (9:00 PM) 'Well there... how d'ya do, mister World Title?' Mindy stared across the dining room table, looking happily at her newly won Global World Championship. The belt's glare from the lights above was near-blinding, as if a sign from God, saying that he assisted Mindy through her hellacious match the night before with Train. Mindy, however, couldn't see much of an otherworldly presence; as far as she knew, only one thing carried her to victory. 'Man, that Wonka Bar I had before the match was pretty freakin' chocolatey.' It was that simple recollection that caused a whimper to escape Mindy's lips as she leaned forward to take a sip of warm tea. Her head was still throbbing, there was no denying that, and a cup of warm tea was the closest to being a remedy in the house. 'This isn't even sweetened,' she muttered with a frown, 'I can't even remember the last time I had unsweetened tea. Ugh.' Then again, Mindy couldn't remember the last time she won anything more prestigious than a World Championship before, but that was because she simply hadn't. Mindy had never won much of anything ever in fact, which was why it was still a shock that Mindy was staring at the most heralded title in Global Wrestling. 'And to think, it only took a little bit more than one hundred and eighty-four days to accomplish!' The mere thought of that made Mindy take a celebratory sip of tea, to which she promptly spat back out. She tilted her head just enough to the right so the spray missed her precious title, and after seeing her title escape the haze of terrible tasting fluid, Mindy let out an exasperated sigh of relief. Meanwhile, Markus Riley stared at his girlfriend from inside the kitchen, safe from the oncoming mist of hot chamomile that penetrated the air as he blinked, immersed in dumbfoundedness. 'I hate hot tea,' he could hear the sugarholic whine, before she hopped up from her chair and ran to her belt which was propped up in the seat in front of her. Markus coughed as he watched Mindy glomp her title, and shook his head. 'You're really attached to that thing, aren't you?' The Caffiene Fiend looked up from her championship and nodded her head quickly, pigtails flopping cutely in front of her face. 'Uh huh.' 'If only you showed me that much affection,' he chuckled in joking response, although the twenty year old didn't seem to take the remark so lightly. Using reflexes that were usually only showcased in her wrestling matches, Mindy leapt at her boyfriend, landing comfortably in his clutches. 'Is that so?' she questioned, poking him in the ribs. Markus counteracted with wrapping the large fingers on his right hand around both of Mindy's skinny wrists. 'Yep.' With a pouty look on her face, Mindy hopped up enough so she could kiss markus on the cheek. 'Well once it sinks in that I'm the straw that stirs the drink in Global now, I'll try and help take care of that problem for you.' Markus laughed and released his gentle grasp on his love, allowing her to begin poking him again. 'I'm messin' around, hun, don't worry about it.' With that, he eased past Mindy and to her belt, picking it up and looking at the beautiful gleam it gave off. 'Man,' he continued, speaking in a soft tone of voice, 'I remember when I used to have one of these. That was a few years ago, but.. sheesh, the weight of that title on my shoulder, I'm never going to forget what that felt like.' Almost on cue, Mindy jumped up onto her boyfriend's back. Markus groaned slightly. 'Yeah, it felt kinda like that.' Mindy giggled heartily at Markus' subtle joke, leaning forward to nuzzle against his neck. 'Well, I may not be a lil' golden belt... but I'm cute and sparkly like one don'tcha think?' Markus craned his head to look at Mindy's face looming over his shoulder. 'I dunno about sparkly, but you're definitely cuter.' He kissed Mindy softly on the lips. 'Much cuter.' A heavy blush emanated in Mindy's otherwise pale cheeks as her arms squeezed in a loving embrace around the large man's neck. 'Thanks, sweetie.' They shared a few moments of silence together as Markus effortlessly held the feather-light woman on his back, before Mindy spoke again. 'Take me to the bedroom?' she requested, 'I think I should get some sleep.' Markus nodded at the sound of the question, although upon further thought, he replied. 'You sure? It's not really that late, can still watch some Vampire Hunter D and make some more notes of my resemblance to the Dunpeel who stole my hat.' Another sweet laugh came from Mindy before she sighed dreamily. As fun of a past time as it was for her to make note of Markus' likeness to her favorite anime characters, her exhaustion had forced her to decline. 'I would, but I'm really just so tired from last night still, hunny bunny.' 'No problem, off to bed you go.' Mindy latched onto Markus tighter, legs clinging around his sides as if for dear life. 'You won't stay with me?' That question never failed to make Markus feel happy. Whether he was upset or having the time of his life, a question like that from the woman he loved was just something that made his day that much better. He felt important to someone; a feeling that Markus craved for nearly all his life. 'I will, if you like.' Smiling, Mindy nodded her head 'yes'. 'Alright.' Markus carried the young woman into her room and set her down onto her comfortable bed. The second she felt the fluffy blankets underneath her, she curled up into a ball and closed her eyes. Markus chucked off Mindy's boots and placed them on the floor before doing the same with his, and he laid back next to her, only for Mindy to instinctively reach out and cuddle him as if he were one of her stuffed animals. 'Heh..' Markus smiled slightly at the contact, reaching over to run his fingers gently through his girlfriend's hair. 'Good night... champ.' Mindy's slumber, however, only lasted a couple of hours. As beat up as her body was, she was simply on such a high that she just could not stay asleep. 'Mmm...' she eased herself away from Markus just softly enough so she hadn't disturbed him as well, and sat up amidst the darkness around them. It only took a minute or so of thought for Mindy to decide that maybe the best way to get back to sleep would to be to write in her diary until she simply couldn't continue. It had been awhile since she'd written in it anyway... 'Hm, let's see here...' Mindy filed through her drawers quietly, eventually coming upon the book that hid neatly underneath all her socks. Grinning, she tip toed to her desk and sat down in the cushioned chair in front of it. One hand flicked on the desk lamp then flicked through to a clean page, while the other grabbed Mindy's favorite black pen. Slowly, the pen touches paper In the guidance of the words that you write Memories roll in of the things you once did And who you had shared them with: Is somebody thinking of you? 'Heya, Diary, Well, I've done it! Who woulda thought that in between the last time I wrote in here and now that I would have won the biggest, bestest title in wrestling? Certainly not me. I still can't believe it myself.. it's so unbelievable that I've gone this far in such short time. Sheesh, it's unbelievable that I haven't freakin' died yet. That Train... well, he wanted to hurt me, alright. I dunno, though.. I just feel as though I'm in some kind of dream that I can't wake up from. I mean, if this is a dream, I wouldn't exactly want to wake up... but I haven't had something so great happen to me without something bad take place in return. I'm just waiting for the part in the dream where I get scared out of it and wake up. Until then, though... I'm gonna enjoy every second with my belt that I can; it's the only proper thing to do.' 'I don't think Markus expected it, either. When I came home he told me that he practically fainted when he saw that I won! ^^; So cute. I have to admit that I was kinda nervous when first getting back together with him. I guess I'm naive enough to forgive easily. That, or the fact that I realize that he could have turned out worse. Either way, I think I did the right thing; he wouldn't have stayed here with me all this time, supporting me through all my troubles, and doing everything in the world to make me feel better if he didn't love me, would he have? And would I really have been focused enough to win that big shiny title if I didn't believe in the encouragement he gave me?' Slowly, Mindy glanced away from her diary to look at the man who slept peacefully in bed just a few feet away from her. She smiled. Did I bother telling you this With the words that cross teeth and jump lips? A poor choice of words in wanting to tell you anything But words don't come with ease They're forever my hurt 'Maybe it's best that I don't jinx it with any dumb ol' doubts I had. This is prolly someone's way of making up to me for the hurt I had started out with here, or for the terrible things that happened long before Global Wrestling entered my life. I know I'm not one to say that I deserve to be happy since I'm usually too worried about others before myself... but I'm so glad that I've been given an opportunity to be happy, and I'm going to make the most of it. If it goes away eventually, that's fine... just as long as I get to make good on the times I spend while this happiness remains.' Would it really matter If you were to count the days left with your hands? Your focus secure, and the loves you left, well Smiles staged in photographs, here until... 'The only thing that could make this better is if Mikey were here. I know I don't need to worry about him, but whenever he disappears like this, I can't help it. He did so much for me before he even knew how much torment I had been going through. I wasn't his cousin to him at first, I was just some crazed stranger who popped up on his doorstep...' 'Come to think of it, maybe some of what he's dealing with is my fault.' 'No. I promised I would try and be happy. I need to be happy for everyone. Mikey will cope... even though I'd rather him be okay than have to cope.' 'These people are my real family. I love them all so much.' Markus stirred to the sound of a dull thud nearby. He fought the urge to go back to sleep, sitting up to find Mindy not lying next to him, but passed out over her desk. The large man chuckled warmly, before he took the girl into his arms and placed her back in bed. Upon doing so, Markus stole a glance at the little pink book that remained still underneath the light that Mindy's desk lamp had provided. He wasn't going to go out of his way to read through his girlfriend's private thoughts, even if the temptation at times had crossed his mind; however, something on the page caught his eye. Markus peered down, looking past the words and at the drawing beneath them: a small, anime-like doodle of a girl with wide, bubbly eyes and bouncy pigtails kissing the cheek of a tall man with a giant hat on his head, partially covering his own long hair. Over both of them fluttered what must have been a dozen tiny hearts. And just below this cutely childish drawing were five simple words, which the tall man read aloud. 'The love of my life.' Markus could feel himself blush as he calmly closed the diary's pages, and slid back into bed. By the time he woke up tomorrow, he'd hope to think of a nice way the couple could spend the next day. ---- 06.15.05 (6:00 PM) Michael watched his cousin walk out the door with her boyfriend. Somewhere upstairs, Luis' wife remained out of sight. She didn't matter; for all Michael cared, if Arielle felt badly about what Michael was going to say or do, Michael had the comfort of knowing one thing in the back of his mind: 'This is exactly what you deserve.' If Jaden was around to hear Michael's thoughts, she'd would more than likely have agreed. You, you left the light on... There's a chance I might've tripped, girl You were there to hold on Michael took the butcher knife he had been playing idly with, and brought it with him out into the living room. There, he watched as Luis immediately tried to plead his case. Michael had none of it. 'Shut up.' He promptly stuck the knife into the cherry wood coffee table he was sitting on, and a whole new sense of urgency arose in the aging man's mind. He continued to try and explain his situation, against Michael's wishes. This only fueled Michael's anger further. However, Michael stayed quiet; in return he merely wound up and punched his uncle as hard as he possibly could. 'What did I just ask you?' Michael continued solemnly, disregarding the blood he got on his hand. Luis cried in incoherence as he spat red fluid from his mouth and onto the floor. Amidst the crimson wad of saliva, the solidity of what was once a perfectly good tooth. Michael watched carelessly as his uncle struggled in his restraints, but there was no way he was escaping. Luis pleaded, crying out something that best resembled the sound of a 'please', but Michael once again turned a deaf ear to it. 'You listen to me, first,' Michael ordered. 'You've had your fun at the expense of others, now it's my turn.' Michael took his knife and began to twirl it menacingly in his right hand. But you couldn't last a lifetime Caught between here and the days of it Carving her name across your arm With every wish, it's hit or miss her I told you so; I measured distance in lines Departing the rest of my life Before Michael knew it, it was night time, and he was knee deep in what had quickly become a mix of water and blood. He had found himself surrounded by the calm rustling of leaves in the night sky, the trees creating enough concealment from the rest of the world as Michael dumped the dead body into the stream. He watched it float away slowly, the calm waters taking their time in dragging it away before it started to sink beneath the surface. Michael gritted his teeth and drove both of his bloody hands into the earth, pulling out two handfuls of soil, clenching them in anger. Someone, however, had been watching the entire thing take place... remaining in the shadows, so much as to not get in Michael's way of comitting the horrid act that he did. But that didn't mean Michael didn't know they were there. 'Why did you follow me here?' Michael inquired, not even bothering to turn around as he did so. He knew the face of who it was, and he shouldn't have let himself be surprised when he saw him. Michael was just so taken over wiwth his rage... the house his uncle directed him to hadn't been as guarded as Michael would have thought; there were three guards, and the first two posed no problem. The third one, however, well... 'Hey,' the Chinese accent replied, and soon the face that the voice belonged to had stepped out of the shadows, 'if it weren't for me, that man would have gotten away.' T'sao Liang approached Michael, grabbing him by the back of his shirt and helping him up off his knees. 'You should be happy I came here when I did.' 'I am,' Michael replied slowly as he stood up, turning to face his 'friend'. 'But this isn't exactly a happy... time.' Kongde tilted his head in legitimate confusion. 'Why do you say that? Your parents are safe... you should be happy.' Michael promptly grabbed T'sao by his black shirt, holding on in a vice grip. 'Not when I have to kill someone to be happy.' The emotion behind Michael's voice almost had him work himself into tears, but he forced his eyes to remain dry. 'I just put someone's body into the fucking river, and no one will ever see it again...' 'And you have two more bodies to dispose of in the same manner,' Liang cut Michael off coldly, as his startling green eyes fell upon the hand that gripped his shirt. Michael didn't notice; he merely noticed the offensive sentence he had recieved. 'Fuck you! I just took three lives away.. you're an assassin.. you should know how that feels.' 'They were the lives of scumbags,' T'sao answered with little interest, 'you know that yourself. No police officer would break a sweat to look for them, they are the scum of the Earth, and the world is now a better place with them gone. I know your parents will be thanking you when you are able to catch up on old times with them.' With a guttural howl, Michael shoved the smaller man hard, causing Liang no other alternative than to fall on his backside. 'Don't talk to me about scum, you hear me?!' Kongde sighed as he lay on the ground, brushing himself off lightly. 'That's right. I guess I should talk about that with your uncle then. Because of course, you refused to kill him.' A dark look entered Liang's eyes, and his lips curved into a sick, twisted smirk. 'It's so much easier to kill people you don't know, isn't it?' 'Shut up!' 'Well, did you kill him?' the smaller man persisted. Michael's cheeks flushed with red. 'No... no, I didn't. All I did was tell him...' If you get put to sleep Like an old dog, you're better off If you get put to sleep... I've been cautious with the words I extend Allow this year before the world starts to end '...Why does it fucking matter to you if I let him live or not, anyway?' Michael questioned angrily. In return, all he got was another question. 'Why do you care about what I say anyway?' Liang muttered as he rose to his feet. 'I'm only a worker, doing what he is paid to do.' Michael gritted his teeth in frustration, watching helplessly as the Chinese man began to walk away. 'But... but I haven't paid you a fucking dime.' Unlike everything else Michael had said, this remark seemed to spark something. Liang turned to face Michael once again. 'I know,' he replied with a smile. A very uneasy smile. Michael blinked at the sight of it. '...What are you getting at?' 'That's what I wait for you to find out.' Michael stepped back, staring in a speechless silence as Kongde turned at the heel and disappeared into the woods. Michael was left with nothing more but the relief that he once again was united with his parents, however burdened he would become by the actions he took to achieve it. However, Michael did take a slight bit of the Chinese assassin's advice; he caught up with his mother and father, taking the time to re-establish their relationship. They'd stayed in a hotel in Verona for a couple of days before they decided to take a flight back to New York, if only to see the sights there together one more time. The day they were headed to the airport, Michael had looked in the newspaper; even though it may have been true that no one with a heart would have wanted to find the bodies of those assholes who had held his parents captive all this time, he was deathly afraid of someone stumbling upon them. However, although he was relieved to find that he found no news of a murder anywhere near the area that Michael had been in over his stay, he was only given more grief when he had read the obituaries. How was he going to explain this one to his cousin? Your father's dead, he passed in his sleep And I woke to the sounds of her crying Lyrics: 'The Light & The Glass', Coheed And Cambria
