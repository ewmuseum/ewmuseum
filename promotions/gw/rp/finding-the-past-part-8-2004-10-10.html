Finding the Past (Part 8)

10 October 2004

Date: 10.10.04 Wardrobe Gap: Charcoal & Black Trim Vintage 1969 Skull Cap Polo Ralph Lauren Purple Label: Black Luxury Pique Polo Polo Ralph Lauren Purple Label: Charcoal Wool Cargo Pants Prada: Black Leather Flat-Toed Tuxedo Boots 'Oops, I'm sorry,' apologized Ken as he accidentally bumped into a stranger as he shopped through downtown SoHo. He quickly put out his hand to shake, but the person's eyes were already looking directly into his with much anxiety over a timidly smiling face. 'You're Ken Partridge, Jr., aren't you,' asked the well dressed man in his white t-shirt and charcoal washed jeans, both by Oakley, with a slight tone of excitment in his voice. 'Actually, yes,' responded GW's resident Superstar with a smile on his face, 'yes, I am.' 'I knew it,' said the still anonymous patron. 'Wait a second,' began Ken with curiosity ringing throughout his voice. 'You look familiar yourself. You're... you're the new rook, aren't you? Josh... Curtis?' 'Yeah, that's me,' confirmed Curtis. 'Well, welcome to GW... eventhough we are still standing in this dreary drizzle.' 'And,' laughed Josh, 'why exactly are we still standing in this rain?' The two laughed a bit as the rain began to pick up slightly heavier. Ken clenched onto his bags and looked around a bit before bringing his attention back to Joshua. 'What say we sit down to a cup of coffee until this calms down, huh,' requested KPJ. 'I think that's a good idea,' confirmed GW's newest star before the two began their run into a near-by Starbuck's. The two dried off a bit and found a seat before continuing their conversation. ..~::~.. The lights had brightened to a blinding effect before Ken blacked out. His body went through hell as his unconcious mind chose to erase it all. His weakness became his strength, and laughter was all he could hear as he finally came to and attempted to pick himself to his hands and knees only to fall back to the wet cement face first. The laughter got louder, more ferocious, and closer as Ken opened his eyes to a face that was only a blur. 'My job is finished,' chanted the voice before going into the last thing that Ken actually comprehended for three months. 'Our Father, Who art in heaven Hallowed be Thy name Thy kingdom come Thy will be done On earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day or daily breaD And forgive us our trespasses As we forgive those who have trespassed against us Lead not into temptation But deliver us from evil For Your's is the kingdom, the glory, and the light Forever and ever!' Ken held on to life for a little bit longer with all that he had. 'Rest in peace, young Superstar.' ..~::~.. 'Are... are you okay,' asked Joshua bringing Ken back to the now. 'Yeah,' responded a shaking Ken, 'but I have to go... now... I'm sorry. I'll see you at Evolution, Joshua.' Ken picked up his bags and his Honey Ginsing Green Tea before running out the door with an obvious purpose in his face as Josh sat at the table perplexed. 'Sure,' he whispered to himself, 'See you at Evolution.'
