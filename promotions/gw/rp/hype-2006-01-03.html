Date: 1-3-06 “I did not choose my destiny” destiny chose me?” ~ Kris Kanton, Jan 1 of 2006 ---------------------------------------------------------- An image of a globe fades into the screen as the following message displays over the top of it. “The following is a GW production?” The image fades out and there is a large stretch of desert with a line of pavement running directly down the middle. A yellow jeep comes into view and then it quickly passes as dust is left in it’s trail. We now find ourselves inside the Jeep and in the company of a man? a well known man? he is a former PCW, 4WW, and GW superstar? a man who is seemingly retired? This man is Kris Kanton. The scene changes again and we find ourselves in what seems to be the city of Louisville, KY. The yellow Jeep has reached it’s destination. Kanton stands in front of a large church. The sky begins to glow red as the sun sets. Kanton slowly creaks the door open and enters the large building. His footsteps echo against the walls as he proceeds down the aisle until he reaches an unoccupied pew of his choice. Kanton steps in and kneels down? he has come to share his words with god. He slowly brings his eyes closed, his hands together, and words to his surroundings? “Lord” I have done many things that I regret. I have shunned the ones I love. I have lost the respect of my peers. I gave into greed, lust, gluttony, and hate. I thought I was bigger than everything. I thought I was bigger than the business that I loved? now? I have money and fame? but it comes with a price as I truly feel as though I have nothing. I feel as though I have lost it all. I want to be what I once was? I want to love life again. Please forgive me?” With a deep sigh Kanton opened his eyes and stood up? feeling as though a huge weight had been lifted he looked around with a new look on everything. It was time for the old times to return. Kanton stepped out of church just then and reached into his pocket. He pulled out a cell phone and a small blue business card. It said Global Wrestling on one said and the other simple said the following? “Kris” call me? ?~James~? Kanton smiled as the sky brightened a bit? things would change? after all? it was destiny. ---------------------------------------------------------- “I did not choose my destiny” destiny chose me? And destiny said to kick your ass!” Kris Kanton True Legend? True?
