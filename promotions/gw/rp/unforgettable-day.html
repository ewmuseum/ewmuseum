Date: Sometime After Death Walking down one of the longest hallways of my life, I try to remember the directions on how to get to my point of destination. Soma told me that he would like to meet with me in the piano room. I’m actually starting to get the hang of this place. I only got lost’well, too many times. But that’s not the point. The point is, I know how to get from my bedroom to the dining room and the dining room, back. This place has rooms that I don’t even think Soma has been inside. I walk down the hallway, each wall is covered with painted portraits of Soma. Well, not the Soma that you see every day, more of like the Soma you would have seen if you lived 2,000 years ago. I stop in front of one, it has Soma sitting in a gigantic wooden chair, draped in the fanciest of clothes and fabrics. He sits, straight with his head tilted towards the ceiling. His long black hair was curly’kind of looks like a girl. I’ll write that down in things to make fun of Soma about? I turn around and continue down the hall. I turn down another hallway and at the end are a set of stairs. I take them’three floors, I think. I get to the top and open a large wooden door. Set before me is a hall with red carpeting, with statues of knights to my left and to my right. Doors are between the places that don’t have statues. I go in front of the first door, tired of playing “Where’s Waldo” with Soma. I close my eyes and hum an old hymn. I raise my arms up, pushing towards my shoulders. It’s heavier then what I remembered it was. I must be getting old. I struggle a little, but succeed. I’m very drained, but relieved. I open the door and see Leinard Soma, sitting on a bench in front of a black grand piano in a giant white room. Nothing is inside the room except the piano, the bench and Soma. The room doesn’t have a drip of black, green, blue, or red paint. Quite fascinating to tell you the truth? “I’ve been waiting for you?” “I almost got lost, but I ended up taking a shortcut.” “A shortcut” I’ve lived here quite some time and there are no shortcuts that I know of.” I shrug my shoulders? “It was magic.” He shakes his head side to side, then concentrates on the ivories and plays a beautiful sonata. “I didn’t know you played the piano.” “I’ve played for awhile.” “Did you teach yourself?” “No, I took lessons in 1422 from a rich man in Florence.” “Fantastic, so you’ve got a lot of practice in the past, oh say, 500 plus years?” “Something like that.” Soma laughs a bit. I walk over beside him and watch him play. His fingers, moving faster then I’ve ever seen’but fluent and graceful somehow. He turns his head, not even looking at the keyboard and talks to me while playing? “I wanted to talk to you about your powers. But I already see that you can do remarkable things. So apparently, you decided to use it again.” “What, you think I’m too stupid to do remarkable things without magic?” I laugh? “Yeah?” I stop laughing? “I’m glad you took that path. It’ll make our journeys a lot easier.” “I don’t mean to change the subject here or anything, but you’ve been talking about this mission since the moment you woke up me from the dead. You still haven’t told me anything about it, just that once I help you, you will help me. And you said something about the year of the dragon. I’ve had some freaky stuff happen that has to do with a dragon and all, but I don’t understand what’s going on.” “And every time you ask me, I always reply with “You will learn in time”. “Yeah but you see, I’m not as patient as you are. I’m more of a, you know’hurry up and tell me what the fuck is going on type of guy.” Soma chuckles? “If I told you know, it would only spoil the surprise. You youngins” have a lot to learn.” “I’m over 40 years old dammit!” “Well I’m over 1,000, you are a puppy compared to me.” Damned vampire? “There is another thing I called you in here for.” “And that is’what?” “Cherish.” “Oh, the maid. Yeah, what about her?” “She likes you.” “Wait a minute, what do you mean?” “Come on Sabbot, you haven’t had a woman in your life in decades, and you still don’t know what “she likes you” means?” “But she is like 18 years old?” “24.” “I thought you said she was like, 17 or something?” “No, that was my daughter that I’m on the verge of finding her at this very moment. My son has done a great job taking over the business so far, so we have more time to search for my daughter and finish our mission.” “That’s good to hear. I can’t wait to get my hands on them.” “Anyways, anyways’she is 24 years old. She has a great personality and I think that you two would click immediately.” “Okay, she is 24 years old! I’m in my 40s. That’s not right.” “Sage, Sage oh Sage’you have much to learn. Love has no boundaries. Age has nothing to do with love. The heart wants, what the heart wants. With love, there are no laws and there are no limits. I’ve loved many of times in my life and I’ve grown to see my wives die time and time again. It’s the most excruciating pain in the world. But I still never regret loving them. I’ve loved women from the age of 20 to 60. And I haven’t seen any differences between them other then their experience. Don’t let age put love aside old friend.” “So’she likes me huh?” He winks at me? “She wouldn’t stop talking and asking questions about you. I swear, I was about to fire her if she mentioned your name one more time.” “Really?” Maybe this won’t be as bad as I thought it would be. “Of course.” Soma starts to play a familiar tune? Water Runs Dry by Boys II Men? “You can play Boys II Men?” “I’ve had so much practice, I can play songs that haven’t even come out yet. You can go now, I just wanted to tell you those things.” “Okay, I’ll see you at dinner.” “Great, we are having lamb chops soaked in wine for 3 years and a side of homemade mashed potatoes.” “Sounds scrumptious.” I walk out the door and hum Water Runs Dry... Great way to set the tone? LATER THAT NIGHT I grab hold of the wooden stick on the side of the chair and pull it. My legs fly out in front of me. I stretch and put my hand behind my head. The yawn of a lion comes out of my mouth’or maybe that was Brylou. I look down at my feet, the enormous albino Siberian tiger rests at my side. I drop my arm down on his head and pet him. His big white eyes shut and he rests his head on the floor, preparing to take a nap. I think I’ll join him. I close my own eyes and try to clear my mind. But there is so much to think about. I have my powers back. I can use my magic again. Then I think about Cherish. I still can’t believe she likes me. I think about Soma, and how he has been such an amazing friend to me. He has brought me Brylou, let me live in his home, and takes care of me. He really is a decent guy. I wonder why he even got involved with The Upper Echelon. Those guys are heartless bastards. They hold their egos much higher then anything else. They are greedy’I think about how I’m going to put an end to it all. This angers me. I think more and more about it. I grit my teeth, just thinking about Holmes. I clinch my fists just thinking about Zero. And a shiver of hatred runs down my spine when I think about Reno. I will seek revenge and it will be sweet. I can’t sleep’thinking about TUE was just fuel for the fire. I have all of this on my mind right now. And I still’can not sleep. The room is as quiet as can be. If a pin dropped, you would hear it from the other side of the room. I attempt to let my mind drift away, into a dream of some sort. I try to think of something relaxing, something to calm me down. “so I think about her. I imagine her being beside me, watching over me while I sleep. I imagine her head, resting upon my shoulder. I imagine her eyes’those beautiful” “green” “eyes” HOURS LATER The sound of someone humming wakes me up. I don’t even open my eyes, it sounds too beautiful. The hum turns into lyrics. I continue to lie there and adore such an amazing voice. I wonder if I’m still dreaming? “We don’t even talk anymore.” The voice sends a chill down my spine. It sounds so majestic and angelic, it makes you want to cry. Kind of like the same feeling you get when you heard the National Anthem after 9/11. It meant something to you. I don’t know how, but it feels like the same way. “We don’t even say what we argue about.” The voice rolls each word off the tip of the tongue. This truly is, the greatest voice I’ve ever heard. “I wonder who it is. I sit up and squint my eyes and look around for it. I see Cherish in my room, dusting knick knacks that Soma had put on a table for me. She picks one up in her hand and cleans it” “Don’t even say I love you no more.” She sounds’perfect. She is singing? “the same song Soma played on the piano the last time I spoke with him. He sure knows how to set the mood. She puts the knick knack, or a sorcerer down and turns around? “Cause?” She catches me watching her? “Oh, Sage. I’m sorry, did my singing awaken you?” “Yes’it did.” “Oh, I’m so sorry. I’ll leave now if you’d like.” “No, nonsense. I was having a difficult time sleeping and your singing was helping me relax. You have such an incredible voice.” “Oh, why’thank you.” You can see her face turn red in a matter of seconds. Her rosy red cheeks? Nothing can compare. “Actually, if it wouldn’t be too much trouble, would you continue?” “I really don’t like singing in front of people. I have a terrible voice.” “That’s ridiculous. I think both you and I know that you sound great.” “You really think so?” She smiles like a child after opening a Christmas present on Christmas morning? “More then you’d imagine.” “Well, okay then.” “Thank you?” She picks up a small wooden chair and carries it over to where I’m lying down. She places it beside me and sits down. She starts to hum? Patiently, I wait to hear her voice once again. I look into her eyes, and she looks into mine. For once, something feels right. “Lets don’t wait til the water runs dry. Lets don’t wait til our lives pass us by. Lets don’t wait til the water runs dry. We’ll make the biggest mistake of our lives. Don’t do it baby.” She starts to hum again? If I could, I would tell you what happened after that. But the truth is, I fell fast asleep. And I’ve had the loveliest dreams I’ve ever had. When I woke up, I found myself covered in a blanket up to my chin. You don’t understand how this truly feels.
