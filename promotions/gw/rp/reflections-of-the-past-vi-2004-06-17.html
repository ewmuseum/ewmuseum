Reflections of the past: VI

17 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 17th, 2004. Shoving off of the concrete I felt the gravel digging into my palms grinding my callused skin to raw and bleeding flesh. Pushing myself up again I gasped out a number between my cracked lips… 'Forty-nine…' I said as I strained to continue my push-ups. The muscles in my sweat-covered body began to tremble with fatigue as my body was once again lowered until my nose was mere centimeters away from the gravelly pavement that I stared into. 'And…fifty!' I groaned loudly as I shoved my exhausted body out of it's position and straight into a sprinters stance, without hesitation or even bothering to regain the depleted oxygen supply my burning legs took off on a quick burst. Forget about the gym, the streets of Dallas had become my playground. There was nothing like a three hour workout routine in the bustling city as spectators watched you doing chin-ups off of street signs, wind sprints next to cars, and push-ups with everyone walking by and nearly stepping on your hands. I wasn't in the mood for the confines of the gym, I needed freedom, serenity, and I needed to be out in the open where I felt no boundaries. I needed all of this for one of the biggest matches of my career, Ken Partridge Jr. versus Josh, for the World title…the rematch. My mind was clouded with the fog of confusion as I entered this contest. Had Ken mapped out my strategy from our last encounter? Would my game plan even work, coming into a match with such bizarre stipulations? And the most haunting question of all; how could I once again raise my hand against my partner…my friend…my brother? Ken had indeed been like a brother to me, there at all times even when my brother by birth was not. Ken seemed to be the only family I had; he was much akin to an older brother. Always there to support me, guide me and uplift me. He was in essence my only true brother…And this relization came as my mind went back to days gone by and took a little adventure down memory lane. ---- Rewind. November 12th, 2002. Lincoln, Nebraska. - St. Mary's hospital. Stomping the snow from the soles of my brown Doc Marten work boots I let out a sigh of relief as the warm air of the hospital heated my frozen body. 'I really should invest in a better jacket.' I mumbled to myself, frowning with disdain at the leather jacket I wore. Of course the fact that it was unbuttoned didn't help either. Nor the fact that the harsh Nebraska wind bit through my heavy BKE jeans as if they were nonexistent and whipped through my tan Billa Bong T-shirt. 'Nebraska winters just plain suck…' I sighed to no one in particular as the hospital seemed devoid of all life. Turning my eyes to the nearby maternity ward my boots clanked on the tile floor as I made my way across, and eventually through the door. The heavy steel slammed loudly behind me and I winced, praying silently that I hadn't brought every child in the ward to consciousness. My bluish gray eyes quickly attuned themselves to the large glass window in the center of the hall, and behind this window rested every newborn in the hospital. Tingling with anticipation I grinned eagerly as I peeked through the window. Pink hat…girl. Blue hat…boy. Pink, blue, pink, blue, pink…and there it was…blue. Wearing only a pair of blue pajamas and a blue stocking hat to keep him warm lay a six pound nine ounce bundle of joy, and the name at the end of the bed proclaimed him as; Nathaniel Logan. There he was…and a sight to behold. Grinning fondly at my newly acquired nephew I whipped the gray beanie off of my head and pressed my forehead against the glass proudly looking him over. I was an uncle, and despite the responsibility it bore it felt quite amazing really. 'Now just to find that mother of his.' I said smiling as my eyes turned to gaze down the hall where I spotted the name 'Josh' next to a door. Walking quickly with a proud beam on my face I made my way to the door and once there, knocked lightly before stepping inside. 'Hi May…' I said in a barely audible voice. Looking up at me my sister in law's face suddenly went through a strange series of emotions…surprise, confusion and finally happiness as she broke out into a broad smile. 'Hey, what are you doing here?' She asked in a tired voice that matched her tussled blonde hair and bloodshot eyes. 'Can't a guy come see his nephew?' I teased with a playful smile as I dropped comfortably in the chair next to her bed. 'Of course, I just didn't expect to see you here. After all you have other things to do and your parents…' She stopped suddenly, knowing full well that I didn't want to discuss my parents with her. 'I took a few days off from training to fly back to the States, after all…I need to see that kid sometime, don't I?' I said calmly trying to completely bypass the fact that she had ever even mentioned my parents. 'Yes, well…it's nice to see you.' She said in her lovely little way, which was still quite soothing despite the fact that she no doubt felt horrid. 'Maybe I'm not such a bad guy after all, eh?' I joked completely avoiding any lame shows of affection. 'Your brother will most certainly be surprised to see you, the last time he heard from you I believe you were in Scotland.' She said, now on the same wavelength accepting the fact that I didn't want to hug and cry and all of that sentimental bull shit. 'Right, I'm still traveling Europe and being paid to do so…it's the life I tell you.' I proudly stated explaining my endeavors in the Wrestling world which at this point had led me to an independent Wrestling company located in Europe. 'Well…it is for you.' She softly stated pointing out to me that neither she nor my brother showed any support for my chosen profession. 'You life your life, I'll live mine.' I shot back flashing a confident smile. At that moment I heard a pair of sneakers squeak on the tile floor of the hospital room and looking up…there stood my brother, dressed in Tommy jeans, a gray polo shirt and the white sneakers that had announced his entrance. 'Jackson…' I said with a nod as I rose from my chair next to the bed. 'Josh.' He responded as he returned my nod. 'Been a while, slick.' I chuckled, already needling him about his receding hairline. 'I guess it has.' He said in return in a rather straightforward and no-nonsense manner, showing that perhaps my parents disdain for their youngest child had been handed down to him. 'So, what's it been…two years?' I calmly asked not bothered in the least by his abrupt tone. 'Almost three.' He replied with hesitation. 'Then what has you by the balls?' I queried searching for an explanation to his unfriendly behavior, although deep down I already knew. He raised and eyebrow and glared, ignoring my question completely. 'So what do you want?' He asked with all the brotherly love of a police interrogator. 'Dude, I'm just here to see my nephew…I'm not looking for trouble so I advise you not to start any.' I said, still keeping a relaxed smile on my face. 'You've already started it just by being here.' He spat out his steely gray eyes narrowing with anger. 'Jacker, don't be like this.' I groaned at him now frowning in disappointment. 'Josh, did you ever stop and ponder why I haven't called you since you punched out dad?' He asked his tone now increasing in volume a hint of anger easily detectable in his voice. 'Oh please, your going to tell me he didn't deserve it?' I gasped at him a bit surprised that he would so readily take my fathers side, but of course…why wouldn't he? He had always been a little kiss ass, so now it was perfectly logical that he too would turn his back on me. 'You punched your own father, that is unacceptable!' He angrily barked at me. 'Dude, just chill. You and I both know he deserved it.' I said defensively, now becoming slightly perturbed by his holier than thou attitude. 'No, your just an arrogant little punk!' He shot at me in a loud voice. 'Just look at yourself in the mirror…long hair, tattoos, piercings…you look like a freak show. You're pathetic!' Pathetic. That word seemed to stick with me. Pathetic? Pathetic. 'We'll see who's pathetic…' I snarled at him the anger in the room beginning to become infectious. 'When are you going to give up this stupid charade Josh? Look at yourself…look at what you've became. This Wrestling business is nothing but a cancer, and your drive to succeed is eating you alive. Your going to become nothing Josh…your going to burn out…' He growled at me in an attempt to cut down my career aspirations. 'I'd rather try and fail, then give up and be a quitter. Then I would be a nothing…a nothing, just like you.' I said, my eyes now shooting sparks. 'Josh, your going nowhere…you'll never amount to anything.' My brother mockingly sneered. 'The hell with you…' I said, brushing pasy him and giving him a hard shove as I stepped back out into the hall. I wanted to hit him…to break his jaw…watch his teeth fly…hear him scream…feel his warm blood on my knuckles. I wanted to hurt him…I wanted to crush him…I wanted to spit in his face…I wanted to break his body like a twig. 'You had better listen to those older and wiser, you'll never be anything special!' He screamed as his voice stalked my quick paces down the hallway. I wanted to go back in there and make him regret everything he had said, but I would do something even better…I would prove him wrong. ---- 'Watch where your going you little prick!' Screamed an old man as I suddenly snapped back to reality, my shaggy black hair trailing me in the Dallas wind as I sprinted down the street. I had barely missed the old man that yelled at me and his protest had returned me to my current frame of mind. Training. I would train…I would push myself…I would go far beyond my limits. Because I must do reluctant combat with my brother… …my true brother. There were still things that must be proven…and prove them I would.
