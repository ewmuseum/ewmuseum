A Breath of Familiar Air

21 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.21.04 So it starts. All of the doubts... all of the trouble... begins once again now that I'm back. It seems the only difference is, I'm no longer one to solve a conflict... simply one to end it. The conflict has been documented... my in-ring return... a match at High Octane pitting myself and two of my most trusted cohorts, Josh and Ace, against the likes of Roz Blaze and Destruction, Inc. Will I solve it? Probably not... nor do I particulalry care to. Will I end it? One way or another, yes. The Elite... which has already been dubbed 'a cancer'... it seems, is already misunderstood. We are not here to take over Global Wrestling. We are not here to put any companies out of business. Call us a cancer if you like... but we are truly a salvation. We are here, in some significant way, to save Global Wrestling. We are here to save it's superstars... even if that means destroying their careers... their well-being... their families. Roz Blaze has referred to us as a cancer, and my guesses are, he's looking at us another version of an nWo type organization. Roz needs to look at us as something different, though. Mr. Blaze needs to look at us as an obstacle that he must overcome. Not just any obstacle, mind you, but as an apocolyptic chaos... an E.L.E. ... Extinction Level Event. One which, if his cards aren't played correctly, he and Destruction, Inc. could very well put them out of action. I don't believe that Roz actually realizes what he's up against. He's too busy trying to discover who the leader is than to find out who The Elite is. I'll make it easier for him... there is no one leader. Not Ice, not me, not Josh or Ace... we are all four on a level that no longer requires leadership. He's also a bit busy comparing himself to me and trying to decide whether or not to play a human game of chess with your's truly. Roz... THERE IS NO COMPARISON! Comparing yourself to me would be like comparing Ronald Reagan to Theodore Roosavelt. Sure, Reagan was beloved... the most beloved president of all time... but he didn't get things done. He didn't have the passion... the drive... the spaldings to crush those that needed to be crushed and to conceive what needed to be conceived. I am that Theodore Roosavelt, Blaze. The Elite needed to be conceived, and I conceived it. Jesse Jamester was in the wrong place at the wrong time... he needed to be crushed... and I did it... same as I'll do to you. You see... I am that JFK... and you're that Marilyn Monroe that needs to be erased out of the picture... and I will do that. I will do that to you and your new buddies Destruction, Inc. Roz, you had sure as hell better learn how to play a little chess, because in the event that you don't, I'll capture you in a Layman's Checkmate. Three moves Roz... that's all. My Ken Music will be the pawn... The Cure will serve as my bishop... and The Epidemic will be the queen. You've made a very ill move, my friend, as you are now singled out. Every step... every breath... every single movement you make, I'll have my eye on... every... single... one. So, Mr. Blaze, as I look at this chess board I see that I have conveniantly put you into check... your move.
