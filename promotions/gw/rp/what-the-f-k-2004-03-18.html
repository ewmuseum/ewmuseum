'What the f--k?'

18 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today 'What the F--k?' March 18th 2004... Time... it doesn't really matter... My debut for GW had been a disaster and everyone knew it... even myself for once. I went into that ring looking to rely on everything that had come to me over the last seven years or so and for whatever reason, I was punished for thinking that the past would help overcome the future. Thrown into a Triple Threat match wasn't something to be happy about but at the time, I figured that it'd help match fitness settle in... that'd it allow me to develop some kind of understanding of what it's like to fight inside that ring once more. To a degree, I was right about helping. Fighting on that night helped me to understand exactly how much it hurts to be beaten... again. Wind and ability wise, I knew I still had a long way to go before I'd be back to winning ways... especially since I seem to spew too much shit in these rather cool little extracts of my life. It'd been about three days since that impro-loss to Arik Idol and I wasn't exactly feeling the rays of sunshine on my youthful ass. I'd spent the last two days resting up in some bar, drinking away whatever I wanted and only moving when I needed the bathroom. It wasn't the most intelligent of things to do, nor was it the most hygienic of things to do but when you're in a mood, I don't think there is anything better. Going back to this morning, it must have been about seven or eight when I got my usual wake-up call from the owner of the joint. Dressed in the same jeans, t-shirt and jacket combination for the last couple of days had been somewhat counterproductive on my part... although I'm not going to bore with those exact details at this very moment in time. Painfully pulling myself up from the seating booth that I was curled up upon, I raised my head and looked in the direction of the bar owner. Vision distorted and voice adjusting, I managed to ramble, '... W-wh-hat... t-t-ti-time... is... it?' 'What time is it?!' he yelled back in his usually gruff, texan accent. 'Why, it's time for you to get the f--k up and think about going home to get washed and such. Being in the same clothes for the last couple of days isn't exactly going to help your career... nor is it going to help you get any date in the near future.' 'Blergh' I responded, my body refusing to shape up words to voice disagreement when it could get the same result through gibberish. The texan owner stared at me for a few moments before shaking his head and carrying on with his own business. I'd love to tell you kind people exactly what business he was up to but with the blurred vision and everything, I couldn't tell whether he was cleaning pipes or himself. Removing myself from the seating booth, I staggered away from the 'comfort zone', moving slowly toward the doors of the establishment. It didn't take too long, even in my unbalanced state. The world was spinning inside my head but outside, I felt like I was flatlined. As I exited this bar, the picture around me began to get a little clearer and I stabilised myself somewhat. The sight of the sun wasn't as painful and the light breeze didn't feel like an artic wind. As I trudged along that small pathway I began to talk to myself. Not exactly the greatest of comforts for my mental health but those who knew me understood why I spoke out aloud, why I spoke to myself. It wasn't to prove a point to anyone, it was to get things into perspective and perhaps get some understanding on a few of the issues in hand. '... What happened last week? I mean, what really happened in that ring? You were supposed to show yourself off to a degree, especially since it was your first appearance on a GW show. What happened though? You were beaten down like a piece of meat, you were beaten into the floor with another loser and subsequently made to look like roadkilll. What was the point in coming back, if the only objective was to get beaten senseless week in and out?' I said out aloud, hoping that the external thinking would somehow shape an answer for me. I didn't know what had happeend last week and I really didn't want to have to think about it any more. Arik Idol wasn't the greatest of people and the easy loss didn't make my own personal case any stronger. Your debut in an organization is supposed to be a brief representation of the things that people should be looking forward to in your time with the company. Your debut is supposed be some kind of statement, some kind of paragraph that says what you are and what you actually aim to bring. '... Now, things aren't as bad as you're currently thinking. This upcoming week on the same show, you've got another match that contains two opponents. True, Arik Idol isn't going to be one them... but if you put your finger down and just think about the situation, it's not all that different from your first match.' I rambled, trying to focus on the fact that in effect, this was actually going to be a repeat of the contest that I'd lost the prior week. True, the opponents were now going to be Nikki Covas and Ace Harding but that didn't mean a whole lot of anything at the end of the day, when it was all said and done. 'This is probably going to be the only other opportunity you'll recieve when it comes to actually proving yourself. You don't know what next week is going to bring and you don't even know if you're going to be walking around this same locker room in a week. You're going to need to evolve yourself and attempt to be funny but have some kind of serious element. You have to be able to know when to laugh and where to express the coldest Winter.' I felt like I was trying to get through things with a simple-minded pep talk. Not exactly the greatest morale boosting tactic in the book of life, but it was doing something of a job and for that, I guess I should be thankful. As the morning came to it's eventual close, I found myself walking further and further away from the bar that I'd appeared from. This next match was being treated as my absolute last... this next match was being treated as my last opportunity to show value. End.
