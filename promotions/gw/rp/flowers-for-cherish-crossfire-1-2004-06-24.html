Flowers For Cherish (Crossfire #1)

24 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/24/04 Pacing back and forth in my own room, I look like a fool. I just can’t get her off my mind. Her beautiful smile and her lovely black hair. Her eyes, how they shine like the stars in the skies. I haven’t felt this way in decades’since Margo and Jessica. Since so many heartaches and tragedies. They were so hard to get over, still’I think about them all the time. I feel the butterflies flapping around in my stomach. You don’t understand how good this feels. I have always been one to fall in love too easily. From the first time I seen her, I knew she was something special. Just the sound of her name? CHERISH Makes my heart sink to the bottom of my chest. I just wish I could tell her that the feeling is mutual between us. That I care about her, as much as I hear that she cares about me. I already know it sounds like I’m some kind of pedophile. I know that I’m over 40 years old and that she is only 24. But many say that love has no boundaries. And that age has nothing to do with what the heart wants. Look at me now, I’m talking about love. Would you ever have thought that I, Sage Sabbot would be sitting inside the house of Soma, talking about love? Months ago, I was the GW World Champion. Months ago, I was in The Nevermore. Months ago, I was ruthless and unstoppable. I was feared and devastating in the ring. I was’evil. But now, I talk about love. Who would have ever thought? I don’t know how to tell her. I don’t even know how to ask her for a date. I don’t even? KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK I turn my head towards the door’while wiping the sweat off of my forehead. “Come in?” The door swings open and Cherish comes walking inside. I quickly swallow my nervousness? “Hey, come on in and take a seat?” She smiles? Such definition of beauty. I wave my hand over to the leather chair. My leather chair. She walks over to it and folds her skirt underneath her legs and takes a seat. She puts her hands on her lap’like an angel. “I just wanted to stop by and see if you needed anything Sage.” “No’well, actually’yeah. I kind of, well?” I put my hands behind my back, thinking for something to say. A light bulb appears above my head? I cough, a silent cough. Enough to create a dozen roses in my hand. “I’m glad Soma talked me into bringing back my magic. I pull them out in front of me and hand them to her. Her eyes light up and sparkle, shinier then ever before. Her smile spreads wider then normal. A dictionary can’t describe how she looks? “These are for you.” “But’Sage, you shouldn’t have.” I can’t think of anything to say, I just sit there, smile and enjoy the scenery? “Thank you so much.” “You’re welcome. Umm’Cherish, I was wondering if uhh, you wanted to?” “I’d be delighted.” “sometimes some things feel too good to be true, especially when she finishes your sentences for you. ??you, you would?” She giggles? “Yes, I’d love to.” Wow, I can’t believe she said yes. And it was THIS easy. Remind me to thank God tonight in a prayer. “Alright, well Thursday night at 7 o’clock then. I’ll come and pick you up.” “We live together Sage. So we don’t have to worry about that.” We both share a laugh, how stupid of me. “Sounds fantastic to me.” “Okay.” We both sit back with our faces red. Neither of us say a word’because I can’t think of anything to say. When you’ve just witnessed the greatest thing happen to you right before your very eyes, you speak in gibberish. “Well, I guess I better get going. I have some work to do.” “Sure thing, let me walk you out.” She laughs and stands to her feet. I follow behind her and guide her to my bedroom door. I open it and she turns around and looks down at her flowers. She looks back up at me and quickly kisses me on the cheek and shuffles out the door. I shut the door once she leaves and I fall to the floor. Miracles come in 24 year olds named Cherish. “I think my heart just skipped a beat. Instead of looking at my watch, I already know that it’s Monday night. Again, picturing Cherish inside my mind, it helps me go to sleep as I lie in the leather chair in the middle of the room. My leather chair. My room.
