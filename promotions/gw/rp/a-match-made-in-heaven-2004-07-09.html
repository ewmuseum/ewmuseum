A Match Made In Heaven

9 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th July 2k4 A Match Made In Heaven? The 'Master of the Mat' tournament has been known to bring some of the strangest people together with a bond that no other person could describe or deny. Despite being a pairing that existing for just ONE round of the tournament, it was that one round that in some cases... brought a new level of respect to exist between competitors. Known to most people walking the world, Rich Sherwin was something of a joke. He was something of a joke... and he couldn't quite be taken all that seriously... EVEN with everything that was going on with Juan Gonzalez and what seemed like every single Mexican on the block. Sadly, this reputation wasn't going to be evading the athlete at any point in the immediate... or near future. Granted, you'd have thought that Rich Sherwin would have matured with a tournament of this size... of THIS level. Battling with serious athletes in an attempt to get a shot at the World Heavyweight Championship! Although, Rich has a weakness for women... he has this need to seriously impress every female that he gets to spend more than a few moments with. Who would have known that when the brackets for MotM were officially announced, Sherwin would have found himself paired with a very attractive female athlete? So... with that taken into account, we find ourselves suddenly transported to a McDonalds restautant located somewhere in America. Nobody quite knows where... and not many people really care either. The picture as a whole is a seperate booth of the McDonalds interior, a table made for four... but tonight, only actually serving two. Everything remained vacant and silent for a few moments, a smartly dressed Rich Sherwin coming into view shortly afterwards. He looked rather smooth tonight, having seemingly put all of his problems out of mind for the rest of the night at the very least. Clad in nothing less than a James Bond-esque tuxedo, he sits down at the table and rests his elbows upon the top of it. '... Excuse me, sir. Would you like to place an order?' came a voice from outside of the scene, a smartly clad waiter coming into view. He looked down to Rich, who returned the look with a rather 'smooth' glare back. There was a period of silence between the two, Sherwin apparently trying to soak this very non-dramatic moment for everything that it was worth. 'Do I want to order?' he finally said in a loud tone of voice, trying his best to sound like some kind of posh individual. The smartly dressed waiter nodded his head in a slow manner, wondering whether Sherwin was actually some kind of mentally incapable person. '... At this very moment in time, I don't want to order anything. I'm sitting here at this table and I'm waiting for a VERY good friend of mine to come along. You may know her, she's the hottest and newest talent to join GW.' '... Well, when you're ready to come along and order, just shake your behind down to the desk and I'll be happy to cook up anything!' the waiter yelled back with some kind of overly excited fashion. Rich moved up the seating a little, trying to get away from a waiter that he thought was looking to try it on... especially with Sherwin's smooth tone of voice and this individuals use of the phrase, 'Just shake your behind.' 'Yeah, well... when they come about, I'll be sure to look for somebody else to serve us. No offense, but you're starting to scare me a little.' Sherwin responded in a rather fake cheerful tone. He wanted to lay the line down without sounding angry or annoyed. In fact, he was hoping that this rather positive tone of voice would help the waiter to understand that he was something of a freak and shouldn't be working here. The waiter nodded somewhat in agreement before moving away from Sherwin and the table, apparently returning to the dark void from whence he came. Rich waited at the table for a few more minutes, slowly starting to think that the entire cause had been lost... and that nobody was going to show up. In the moments that followed, a spotlight seemed to appear on the entrance to the restaurant. It whirled around for a few seconds before settling on the door. It opened slowly... creaking forward to give some kind of overly dramatic entrance. The people working the tills of this elusive McDonalds restaurant stood still and gasped, their mouths wide open... absolutely wide open! 'Oh... My God!' 'Who is that lovely vixen coming through?' 'Oh... My God!' 'Is this the date for that Richie Sherwin, foo'?' Singing in tune as though the world was suddenly some kind of wonderful musical, three members of staff jumped out from behind the tills and began to dance like they'd never danced before. After some funky kind of waltz, the three people moved aside and allowed the beautiful guest to walk through. She looked stunning, she looked amazing...she looked like a thousand billion trillion gazillion dollars. Mackenzie Geller was her name and looking spanktastic was her game. Having been invited to this place by Sherwin, she looked rather disturbed upon entering what was supposed to be a back-alley global fast food chain. She gazed around in surprise at the sheer level of effort that had been put into this entire affair... and suddenly, she began to wonder why she'd dressed up so well. '... This is just starting to freak me out.' she exclaimed, looking at the rather robot-like employee's of the establishment as they clapped their hands and ran away out of sight; moving back to the darkness behind the tills. The musical scene stopped for a few minutes, allowing everything to return to a state of normality. Rich stood up in his seat and looked over to Mackenzie, who looked back and gave a gentle wave to the significantly over-the-top one that had come from the obviously infactuated Sherwin. She walked over to the table and took a seat opposite Sherwin, the former TV Champion looking on in awe... almost drooling at the beauty that was seated directly opposite him. Of all the ladies that Rich had bumped into over time, none had looked quite as good as Mackenzie did. 'Thanks for coming...' Rich said, his eyes looking lovingly across the table at Mackenzie; who returned the look with a smile... but not one that was encouraging the mood; just appreciating the kindness that was being expressed. 'Well, you left a message that saying tonight was somewhat important... and with our tag team bout around the corner; I thought it would be best if we met up to talk about things.' Mackenzie informed Rich, sounding as beautifully plain as no other lady walking the face of the planet. Rich nodded his head somewhat and giggled like a schoolboy, lost in his heart and incapable of thinking properly. '... Y-y-ye-yea-yeah. I th... mean think, we should t-t-talk about this match and stuff. Those two pe-people we have to face aren't going to be... like, easy or anything.' Rich managed to splutter out, messing up in a lot of places and stuttering like complete fool. Mackenzie didn't seem to be bothered by the lack of stability that was being carried by Sherwin but deep inside, she must have been hating this whole situation. 'Exactly what do you want to talk about?' Mackenzie shot back to Sherwin, looking to get straight to business. From the word 'Go', it was becoming apparent that both people had completely different ideas as to what the basis for this whole meeting would entail. On the one side, Sherwin was expecting to use this to score with a very lush lady... and on the other, Mackenzie was simply expecting to talk about any last minute issues before her and Rich went into their 'Master of the Mat' qualifier. '... Well, I was thinking about things Mackenzie... and I'm trying to bring some kind of secret techniques and skills that could help us in battle on Evolution.' Sherwin began, suddenly regaining his composure. He looked around for a few moments; trying to think of something to carry the evening along and not give Mackenzie the impression that he just wanted her out her for a little lovin' and romancin'. 'Rich... I can tell by your tone of voice that you don't even know what you're going to be doing for the duration of our contest.' Mackenzie stated, shooting a bullet of accusation across the table to Sherwin; who acted as though he'd been shot with seventeen million rounds from a very old school machine gun. His facial expression changed to look like a completely false expression of horror. '... Of all the things that could have been said... all the things that could have been pushed in my direction. I know exactly what we should do against these two people... primarily because one of them happens to be something of a mate of mine. I'm sure he doesn't give a damn about the tournament... so, how about we just start to talk about something really good and ignore this whole thing.' Sherwin states, a level of confidence shining through with his obvious blagging. Mackenzie doesn't look impressed by Rich's cheap attempt to throw the match aside and move onto something that was a little more juicy. She may have thought that Rich was something of a nice guy at heart... but she didn't have the kind of interest in him, that he had in her. 'So... the whole point of this meeting has been to get me here for some personal dinner?' she asked, her tone of voice not sounding very happy but not sounding all that angry either. Mackenzie appeared appreciative of the attempts being made to ensure that there were no troubled waters going into this tag team contest... but at the same time, she was quite reserved about Rich's actions and how he expected this meeting to actually go about. 'Well... you know, I'm not REALLY all that high on wrestling when it comes to ladies... but you know, tonight isn't just about getting to know one another. It's about something else... something rather crazy!' Sherwin exclaimed, trying to make this seem as though it was the best thing that could ever happen in the WHOLE WORLD. 'This is a joke, isn't it?' Mackenzie replied in a very attitudal-way, seemingly not appreciating anything that was happening now. She prepared to get up from her chair and move away from things, leaving Sherwin to ponder the nights events... but before anything could happen, Sherwin jumped out of the booth and began to dance; the three earlier members of the piece coming out to sing and dance too. Was this to be the musical that nobody could have ever imagined? 'Joke? How could THIS be a joke to you? Do I look like I could EVER joke... with you?' 'Does he ever looks like he could... joke with you?' chimed the three apparent backing singers, dancing in the same odd way that Rich was. '... My love for you is like some kind of... tiger flower... it looks really cool and imports from Japan...' 'It imports from Japan...' 'As strong as Jackie Chan... who is apparently not from Japan...' 'Not from Japan....' After fifteen more seconds of dancing and moving around, everything seems to go fuzzy... moving around, moving around... moving... when... 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!' His eyes shot wide open and he was finally awake. Rich jumped up from what was apparently a sofa and looked around, sighing loudly after realizing that nobody was anywhere. 'To think...' he began, talking to himself in the same way that any psycho would, '... I thought that was really, really cool.'
