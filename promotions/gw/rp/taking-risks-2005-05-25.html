Taking Risks

25 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-25-2005 The garage was cold and wet, the recent high level of rain having seeped under the door and spilled over most of the available area. Outside the wind howled through the cracks in the door and windows, as if trying to find any available way to sneak into the, otherwise secure, McFarlane estate. Dawn’s bare knees scraped against the cold pavement, her grey boxers providing no protection from the cold or the wet for her long legs. She hugged her arms around her torso, trying desperately to hold in what little warmth was provided by her tight black tank top, teeth chattering as much from the effort as from the cold. Her recently dyed black and red hair hung raggedly around her feet, as though she’d just risen from a particularly restless sleep, one that left her hair tousled and her eyes red with strain. She huddled on the floor, shivering form the cold, stubbornly refusing to lift her eyes from the floor to look at her surroundings, already knowing in her heart of hearts what she would see there. She wasn’t supposed to be here. Not again. The sound of the slowly swinging chain grated on her ears, each slow pass feeling like it was grinding right next to her ear, grinding against her mind. In desperation she clapped her hands over her ears, leaning towards the ground as she screamed in frustration and fear. “LEAVE ME ALONE!!” She howled, “I CHANGED MY MIND’I DON’T’don’t want’this?” Her voice trailed off near the end, turning into more of a desperate pleading then an agonized screaming, her voice choked with tears shed too many times. For all her begging the sound continued, the merciless sound of steel grating against steel, the slightly clinking as each link in the chain wound up slightly and then spun away. She shook violently, this time not from the cold, and bent nearly double, her forehead pressed against the unforgiving concrete. Hot tears slid once again from her eyes, slipping stubbornly between eyes squeezed shut so tightly that the flesh around had wrinkled in protest. Slowly, as if being forced, she opened her eyes, looking behind her with eyes gone wide. She could still see them, it wasn’t the evil trick she had silently begged it was. Pale feet swung mere inches from the floor, shifting back and forth with each swing of the chain that bound them. Unwillingly her eyes trailed up long, pale legs that seemed to shine with a light of their own in the dimness of the garage, up to a pair of terribly familiar bunny rabbit boxer shorts. She jerked her head away, tearing a small gash in her forehead against the rough floor. Behind her the door into the house opened, artificial light flooding into the garage, illuminating a small silhouette that stretched to just past where Dawn lay, shivering. She felt the dread built inside her, reaching up from the pit of her stomach to grab a handhold in her quivering throat. “NOO!” She screamed again, curled nearly fetal by this point. “Not again! Just LEAVE ME ALONE!!!” Dawn came awake with a scream on her lips, sitting bolt upright in bed, one hand clutching at her collar just below her neck. Pain flared along her right side, turning the scream into a strangled groan as she felt something grind against itself. She folded forward in agony, one hand pressed to her side, the other fumbling for the box of pills she kept on her bedside table. She found them before her hand hit the table, held in another’s hand; the flesh cool under Dawn’s fevered touch. With a gasp of surprise Dawn pitched herself off the left side of the bed, coming into a rolling crouch as she glared in a mixture of anger and surprise at her sudden intruder. “Did I startle you?” Shadow asked in amusement, a slow grin spreading across her face. “God fucking dammit!!” Dawn spat, slowly pulling herself back up to her feet. “Don’t you people ever use the fucking door?” “Language dear, language.” Shadow tutted, placing the box of pills on the bed between them. “I DID try your front door, but it happened to be locked’unlike the balcony door into your bedroom.” Dawn snatched the box from the bed, snapping it open with her fingernails and popping a pair of small pills into her mouth. “Well it is the middle of the goddam night you know?” She grumbled, swishing the pills around before swallowing. Shadow folded her arms under her breasts, leaning against one of the posts of Dawn’s bed. “Would you have rather I came calling during the day” I sunburn even worse then you do dear.” Dawn sat heavily on the edge of the bed as the pain in her side flared anew, taking her breath away for a long moment. “You could have called?” She gasped out. “Christian and I didn’t think you were likely to answer you phone at this hour. Provided you could even fight your way out of your drug induced stupor.” Dawn’s head snapped up and around, fixing blazing green eyes on her midnight visitor. “I take those pills for pain, and nothing more. You of all fucking people should know better.” Shadow held her hands up, palm out, in a placating gesture. “Peace Dawn, put your hackles down. Believe it or not I’m here because we’re all concerned about you.” “I’m fine.” The response was short, almost rehearsed, and drew a sigh of exasperation from Shadow. “No you’re not. Barring any of my own expertise on the subject, the fact that you’re popping pain killers in the middle of the night and doubling over from sudden fits of pain shows that you’re far from “Fine”.” Dawn grumbled a moment, staring off into the darkness of her room before answering. “What do you want?” “Christian asked me to come here and keep an eye on you Dawn. And judging from the state you’re in, I think he was quite right to do so.” Dawn sighed, wrapping both arms protectively around her aching torso. “What are you going to do” Take me to the hospital?” Shadow’s laugh caught Dawn off guard, seeming to shatter the smothering quiet that had formed in the darkness around both women. “Hardly. I think I’d likely have more luck attempting to drag an angry Tiger back into her cage. I COULD do it, but it would be a lot of pain and effort I don’t need to take.” Dawn looked back over her shoulder quizzically. “What then?” Shadow sat on the edge of the bed, looking intently at Dawn’s right side. “Well, for starters I’d like to take a look if you’ll let me. I’ve had a lot of experience with treating injury, and I need to know what the problem is before I can advise you what to do.” Dawn stared off into the dark for several moments before nodding reluctantly, turning her back to Shadow and raising her right arm up over her head with a wince. Shadow slid over the beds covers in behind her, gently raising the bottom of Dawn’s tank top until she could get at the wounded area. Shadow’s cool fingers slid over the abused flesh, gently pushing and prodding against Dawn’s ribs, earning a shiver from the taller woman. “Take a deep breath.” The vampire instructed her charge. Dawn nodded, sucking in a large breath as Shadow pushed against her side. “Does it hurt when I do that?” Dawn gritted her teeth, her breath hissing out between them. “Yes.” “But you can bend and move alright” You can turn your torso without too much pain or discomfort?” “Yeah. As long as I don’t make any sudden moves it’s just a general feeling of discomfort.” Came the short reply. Shadow looked up at Dawn, reaching out one hand to lay it on her cheek. “You’re flushed; do you feel cold or clammy” There’s a chance it might be infected and you might have a fever.” Dawn swatted Shadow’s hand away. “I’m not feverish” She snapped back, “I’m just a little embarrassed to have been caught in my underwear.” Her cheeks burned, but she kept her face away from Shadow. Shadow laughed, taking her hand from Dawn’s side and letting the tank top fall back into place. “You’ve got nothing I haven’t seen before Dawn, I’m a woman myself remember?” “Yeah and I bet you don’t take to kindly to sudden visitors either.” Dawn retorted. Shadow smirked, leaning up against the headboard as she crossed her arms once again. “Your brought this on yourself darling. If you’d only come to Christian earlier then he wouldn’t have had to get me to drive all the way out here to make sure you were going to take care of yourself.” “I ALWAYS take care of myself. Besides, I’ve got Tessa here if something were to happen as you suggest.” There was a derisive snort. “Please, do my intelligence some credit. Christian and I both know that she’s away visiting her children, if she was here I’d be able to catch a smell of her at least.” “Ergh’damnable vampiric bullshit?” Shadow laughed again, reaching out to play with the ends of Dawn’s hair between her finger tips. “Most of the rest of the time you don’t seem to mind them?” She purred. Dawn jerked her head away, pulling her hair out of Shadow’s grasp. “That was a long time ago, before I knew better then to trust the majority of your kind.” Shadow placed a hand over her breast in mock hurt. “I’m wounded.” “I didn’t say you.” Dawn fired back, sliding off the edge of the bed and standing up. “If I didn’t trust you at least a little I doubt we’d even be having this conversation.” Shadow looked up at Dawn in curiosity. “Going somewhere?” “No, but you are.” Dawn turned back towards Shadow, folding her arms. “You’ve done Zenith’s task and you know I’m fine. So I’d say you can head back home.” Shadow gave another short laugh, shaking her head in disbelief. “You’re a real piece of work and not much of a hostess either. I’m not going anywhere Dawn, you didn’t bother to hear my diagnosis yet.” “And?” “Your ribs are cracked; I’d say two, maybe even three. It probably happened when you put all your weight down on Mercer’s knees. So Zenith was quite right to send me here’and ask me to stay.” Dawn’s eyes narrowed. “What do you mean’stay?” “You’re not taking care of yourself, that much has become fairly obvious. Christian told me to keep an eye on you, and that means that I’m going to be where you are most of the time. So either you come back to my house, or I’m going to be staying here with you.” Dawn rolled her eyes. “I don’t need a babysitter. I’m fully capable of taking care of myself.” “Most of the time I would have agreed with you’but something’s different now Dawn, there’s something about you that isn’t right. You’ve been taking a lot of risks, and not giving yourself time to patch up from them. You jumped into this weeks match knowing DAMN well that there was something niggling you.” Dawn shrugged uncomfortably, sitting back down on the bed. “So I’ve been pushing myself, it’s not a big deal. I made a mistake; it’s not going to happen again.” Shadow’s hand on her shoulders made Dawn jump in surprise, but the other woman held on tightly, slowly kneading the muscles there beneath her fingers. “Is that all there is to it” Just a mistake?” Dawn’s head lulled forward and she sighed. “That’s all there is?” “Liar.” Said the voice in Dawn’s mind. “Why don’t you tell her the real reasons” Tell her the whole story about why you feel you have to take all those risks’why you have to be like that spotmonkey you hate so much.” ??I won’t pry.” Shadow was saying. “But if you feel the need to cut the bullshit and tell someone, you know where to find me.” Dawn nodded absently, already falling asleep under Shadow’s pleasant massage. “Mhmm’I know’know where?” Shadow leaned forward, her lips brushing Dawn’s ear. “Sleep.” She whispered, adding just a touch of power to her voice. “And rest your body’you’re going to need it.” Dawn’s eyes slid slowly shut and her body sagged backwards into Shadow’s arms as the other woman lowered her gently to the bed. She tucked the covers up around Dawn’s chin, slipping off the bed and running one hand down the side of her face. “One of these days’I’m not going to take “I’m fine” for an answer you know.”
