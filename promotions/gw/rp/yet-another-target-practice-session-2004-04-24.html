Yet another target practice session

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/24/04 It was that time again. Whenever Brick wanted to get psyched up for a big match, or just let off some steam, he would drive out to an empty field with an Army duffel bag full of firearms and cardboard cut-outs of the superstar or superstars he was facing or had angered him. He'd then take shots at each of the mock-ups. Often, these little sessions would be videotaped in order to send a message to his opponents. Sometimes, the mock-ups would be of people who reminded him of the person he was facing. This was true in the case of Mark Makonie, who hadn't been in the company long enough for the guys in merchandising to make a cardboard standup of him. Besides, who would ever buy Mark Makonie merchandise, aside from the Nazis and skinheads that compromised his fanbase? So he shot at mock-ups of Adolf Hitler, Tom Metzger, Byron De La Beckwith and other racists. This time, the mock-ups were of Deacon Holmes and Train. He was facing these two men in a sixteen-foot-high steel cage at Wrestlecade IV. At stake: Deacon's Platinum title and Brick's Wrestlecade winning streak. As he set up the figures, he thought about the events that led up to this moment. Brick's rivalry with Deacon Holmes had gone on for about a year. It all started with Deacon's scathing comments about Brick on an edition of Overload. Since then, their rivalry has escalated into epic proportions. There have been times when the two men didn't deal with each other. Brick's animosity with Train was fairly recent. Since his debut, Train had been a major troublemaker. He had constantly challenged Christina's authority and started fights with several of the other wrestlers. Brick figured sooner or later, Train might start something with him. But what really got Brick's gall was when Train teamed up with the racist Mark makonie. The following week, Brick had publicly called Train on it. Their excahnge of words resulted in a huge brawl and later led to them meeting in an eight-man tag match. There had since been numerous altercations between the two. The brawl that broke out between these three men on the second edition of Deaconpalooza made this match inevitable. “Brick?” a voice called out. “Aaron?” Brick replied. Aaron walked right up to Brick’s left side. “Figured you’d be out here. Getting ready for Wrestlecade?' 'You know it,' Brick replied, after pulling out the sawed-off shotgun. “And how is this supposed to get you ready?” “Puts me in the right mindset for the match.' 'Yeah, well, hopefully that mindset will be focused.' 'What do you mean?' Brick asked, as he took aim. 'Both of these cats have a way of getting under your skin. I've seen it before. Deacon knows how to get to you. And apparently, so does Train.' 'Well, you forget. That goes both ways. I can get under their skin just as easily. And I have. Remember the time Deacon came out and tried to ruin my celebration” Remember how I got him with the pack of diapers?' 'Didn't he set your Navigator on fire after that?' 'Yeah, he did, but I've managed to get him back after that.' Brick then shot the Deacon Holmes figure in the left shoulder. 'I'm telling you, Aaron. If I lose to Deacon Holmes again...' 'Don't think like that, man. It's a three-way match, so it can go either way.' 'That's not what the insiders say. According to them, I ain't even a factor.' 'And since when have you listened to the critics?' 'Never.' 'Exactly. So why start now?' Brick thought about it. He then took aim and shot the Deacon Holmes figure in between the legs. He then went to his duffel bag and pulled out an Uzi. 'And even if you don't win, it's not the end of the world, is it?' 'Actually, you know the only thing that would make me happy about losing this match?' 'No, what?' 'If Train gets pinned by Deacon. Then he won't be able to say a damn thing to me!' Brick then shot 'Train' repeatedly in the chest. 'Do you ever get tired of this?' 'Tired of what?' 'Coming out here and firing off guns when you're pissed off.' 'Not really.' Brick then grabbed a pump rifle and proceeded to shoot holes into both figures, causing them to crumble. 'Come on, man. Let's go.' The two men then walked away from the refuse left behind. Which would be nothing compared to what Brick planned to do at Wrestlecade.
