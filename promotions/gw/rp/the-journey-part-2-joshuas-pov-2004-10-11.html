“The Journey” (Part 2/ Joshua’s POV)

11 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-11-04 Friday October 8th, 2004 7:00 A.M. Today is a good day to be alive! Today is special because of the fact that LIVE in New York City the fine folks of ESPN2’s “Cold Pizza” were about to get a treat. It turns out that the “powers that be” had decided to ask Joshua Curtis, the leader of the “Old School Connection” or as you know it in GW “The Connection” to appear on this show for a “special edition” and its special because for the entire show its nothing but a conversation with the man, myth and as some would dare say “legend” in the business and if ya don’t believe him ask around! When the trio of Woody Page, Skip Bayless and host Jay Crawford were told that they were going to the home of Joshua Curtis and Zack Johnson admittedly Jay had the most reservations about going there because of the fact that its going to be somewhat touchy to go interview an openly gay wrestler and he didn’t want to offend Joshua or his boyfriend Zack. “Ok guys here’s the deal: We were told to do this interview at the request of ESPN because its not too often that wrestlers get interviewed on TV and because of the uniqueness of the situation with the fact that the wrestler has been open in the past with his sexuality so what was I gonna do” Refuse? I don’t think so!” Jay said with a kind of annoyance that made the other two kind of upset with him. “Jay” Skip started “I think you are kind of overreacting about this. For one thing this isn’t an everyday thing and for another I’m SURE that Joshua is more a gentlemen than people in production have let on about him and besides I think this is just what the ESPN audience needs.” Skip said and Woody was quick to back up Skip’s argument “I’ve covered Joshua before when he has done shows in Denver and he was nothing like the stereotype that people have let on about him. Joshua asked about the family and how work was going but not ONCE did he hit on me! Now I don’t know about Zack but my understanding is that he is away on business so he won’t be home to make you uncomfortable Jay!” Woody told the younger reporter as they went to the door and no sooner did they get there that Joshua was waiting to greet them! “Good Morning Mr. Bayless, Mr. Crawford and Of course Mr. Page and welcome to the home of the “Connection. As you know I am Joshua Curtis and Mr. Page as I told you on the phone Zack will not be here because he has to deal with some personal issues at home with his mother in the hospital and everything.” Joshua explained as the trio walked in the house and Josh couldn’t help but smile when Woody came in because he was the only reporter to really understand his situation even though Skip was pretty understanding about it also as while he never had the pleasure of meeting the 23 year old New York native before today he was told from Woody about the young man and knew that it was going to be a fun time today! The quartet walked through the house to the kitchen where they sat at the kitchen table and Skip decided to offer the first question to Mr. Joshua L. Curtis? “Joshua” Skip began “Let’s get right to the heart of the matter if you don’t mind. We were made aware of the fact that when you were going to be signed by the fed known as “Global Wrestling” that you pretty much told the management there that you were gay. Why did you feel like you had to do that?” Skip asked and Joshua knew that it would be best to answer the question. “Because if I didn’t nip it in the bud now then people would be spreading rumors about me and I didn’t want that. My mother once told me that having secrets is a bad thing, so why bother” I’m gay so the best thing to do is for everyone to deal with it!” Joshua said to Skip and Woody nodded before he began his question. “So now it is nipped in the bud and word has it that you have befriended a roster member in GW named Ken Partridge Jr. and I guess the question is why him” Weren’t there other people on the roster other than this man whom seems to me anyways to have lost his way?” The elder statesmen of the trio asked Joshua whom, while only 25 years old is still wise for his age as he carefully crafted the answer to the most simplistic of questions. “Well that is a good question Mr. Page and I think it has to do with the two of us being kind of the same path. I have burned bridges with people in different feds much like Ken is trying to get his friends back in Global Wrestling. I want to become his friend and not so much as a lover but just as someone he can talk to’yes Mr. Crawford you have a question?” Joshua asked as the youngest member of the trio began to speak. “Well thank you first off for allowing us into your home and I want to say that its an honor being here to talk to you because we have heard so many good things about you but I have a concern that I’d like to address with you right now. You said that you have burned bridges before and we all know about that but as far as Ken goes do you honestly believe that you can be just friends with him” What would happen if you had the urge to try and date him or something of that nature?” Jay asked and that didn’t phase Joshua one bit as he sat there contemplating the question that was asked of him. “First off it’s a pleasure allowing the three of you into our home and now to your question Mr. Crawford. I like Ken’a lot but I am committed to Zack 110% and I know that might be somewhat disheartening but I have to face facts right now. Ken is best friends with “American Youth” Josh and I respect that as much as the American Youth might not respect me and I can wholeheartedly understand that because I am a 110% homosexual and it feels good to say that let me tell you! The fact is that Ken is a sweet guy and I hope he finds someone whom likes him for him and no other reason. To be honest if I didn’t have Zack I might be inclined to go look for Ken even though he is best friends with the American Youth!” Josh told Jay and to say a pin could drop in that room would be a drastic understatement! The rest of the interview went well after that with Joshua speaking highly of the staff and the roster in GW and Jay taking some notice to the fact that when KPJ (Ken Partridge Jr.) gets brought up Josh perks up greatly! Is a friendship there already? Maybe and maybe not but as Jay put it before they left he summarized Joshua as having an “understood respect” for the roster of GW! Is this the end of “The Journey?” Hell no and what’s gonna follow next may very well shock you...or not! Au Revoir and sweet dreams dearest ;)
