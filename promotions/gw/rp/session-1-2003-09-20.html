Session 1

20 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/20/03 Three men in long white coats, all wearing glasses, and going bald, open the door, and courtiously greet Ice and Star as they enter the building. Ice angrily looks over the three men, turns and briskly walks away from them. Star politely smiles, and hurries to catch up with Ice. Ice and Star walk down a long white hallway, which appears even whiter that what it normally should, as the bright white lights hanging from the ceiling shine down upon them. Ice walks briskly, his focus forward, not looking around at any of his surroundings, just focused on where is going. Star, slightly begining to breath heavily from having to quicken her pace to keep up with Ice, is right behind him. The hallway opens into a large black and white checkered room. To the left is a small green ping pong table, that is occupied by an older man and a young girl. The two gently tap the ball back and forth, and appear to be having a good time with one another. Ice sneers at the sight, and turns his head and looks to the right. To the right is what appears to be a lounge area, containing two brown couches, and three mustard yellow 70s style chairs. The furniature is accompanied by a small seventeen inch color television. A younger, blond haired man is sitting alone in the lounge. An orderly walks up to the blond haired man and taps him on the shoulder. The blond man sitting alone in the lounge turns, two dark purple rings circle his hazel brown eyes, and a goofy look engulfs his face as he stands up and is escorted away by the orderly. Ice turns to Star. 'What?' Ice doesn't respond to her question. Slowly he turns his attention straight ahead of him, and to the front desk, which has a lovely looking brunette sitting behind it. Ice takes in a deep breath and walks towards the desk. He stands silent in front of the large brown wooden structure, and waits for the young lady to notice him. Realizing the lady is paying no attention to him standing directly in front of her, Ice loudly slams his right hand down on the desk. The brunette jumps, and quickly looks up to see the large frame of Ice standing before her with a menacing look on his face. 'C..C...Can I he..help you?' Ice reaches into the back right pocket of his blue jeans, pulls out a small piece of paper, and hands it to the woman. She looks over the document, and stands up. She looks over Ice from head to toe, and Star shoots her an evil glare. Hurrily the woman exits the room through a door that is located directly behind her chair. 'Can you believe that little bitch?!' Ice turns towards Star and looks at her with an annoyed look on his face. Star stares at the door with a pissed off look consuming her face. 'Did you not see her checking you out? That bitch had better keep her eyes where they should be before I... 'Before you what? Look, I don't want to be here to begin with, so if you wouldn't mind, would you please shut up and let me get through this with the least amount of annoyance as possible.' Ice continues to angrily stare at Star as the door behind the desk opens, and the brunette who was ehind the counter, and a man in a brown suit walk out from behind the desk. The brunette walks over to Star, and the two walk off back down the hallway Star and Ice came from. Ice and the man in the brown suit walk over passed the lounge, and enter a small interigation looking room. Ice takes a seat in one of the metal folding chairs in the room. 'My name is Dr. Gruzielata. You can just call me Doctor. Miss McCoy informed me that you would be coming for a period of time to our sessions. We weren't sure if you should be in group, or individual sessions, so today is more of an evaluation session to see which would better fit your needs. What that means is that I am going to ask you a few questions, and some other general things. This will go as fast as you cooperate or thirty minutes at the most, because that is the lenght of the session that was paid for today.' The doctor takes a seat across from Ice in a similar metal folding chair. On the desk is a small pale tan folder, and the doctor opens it. He pulls out a medium sized note pad, and a black ink pen. Dr. Gruzielata rests his left leg on top of his right knee, and sits back in his chair. Ice sits back in his chair as well and awaits the doctor's questions. 'Ooookay Mr. Ice? What is your real name?' Ice tilts his head to the left and looks at the doctor, but does not respond. Over the top of his glasses, which are hanging on the tip of his nose, Dr. Gruzielata gazes up at Ice and awaits an answer. Ice tilts his head from the left over to the right, but still does not respond to the doctor's question. The doctor looks down from his gaze up at Ice, and looks back at his note pad. 'Okay, not real talkative, that's alright. Do you know why you are here?' Ice shifts in his chair, and stairs down at his left hand. He begins to pick, and dig at his fingernails, while ignoring the doctor, once again, all at the same time. Once again the doctor, with his glasses barely hanging off the tip of his nose, looks over the top of them and stares at Ice. Ice continues to pick at his fingernails, and ignore the doctor. 'You know Mr. Ice, this would go by alot faster if you would cooperate and answer some of the questions I am asking you.' Ice snaps his head, and attention from his fingernails to the doctor. Angry, and perturbed Ice pushes his chair back, and leans across the table he is sitting at and gets into Dr. Gruzielata's face. 'NO! You don't get it! I'm here because I am being forced to be here. I don't want to be here! I don't want to cooperate. I could care less about the questions you have to ask, and anything else you are going to try and do. All I have to do is serve my time, so I can get back to doing what it is I do.' Ice pauses as he begins to breath heavily from his outburst. The doctor begins to speak, but Ice cuts him off before he can utter a single word. 'People like you, you can't handle the things that go on in my mind. You ask, because it is your job, and you are paid to, but deep down, you don't care, and you really don't want to know. The things I think about, the things I actually do, those things scare you deep down, but you don't want to admit it, in fear of sounding inferior to me. But I already know. I on the other hand, don't really care, about you, Jin Akiyara, Christina McCoy, or anyone else. The only person that I even remotely care about is Star.' Ice is breathing even more heavily now, as he sits back down. A small smile crawls across the doctor's face. 'Good. Good. Now we are getting somewhere. We have at least established, that you do have feelings for someone. Maybe we can build from that.' Ice angrily sneers at the doctor. The doctor looks down at his watch and sets the note pad down. He reaches over for the folder, and pulls out a stack of papers. Slowly he goes through the stack, and randomly sets aside sheets of the paper. 'Now our time today is winding down. I will remind you we have another session scheduled tomarrow at 1. Before I let you go, I wanted to do one more thing. I have pulled out five ink blots. You will be shown them, and as you look at each one, you will tell me the first thing that comes to mind. We have ten minutes to complete this, and I will keep you here until you cooperate.' Ice sits back in his chair. The doctor pulls out one of the ink blots. Ice stares at it but does not respond. The doctor calmly sets that one down, and holds up another one. Again Ice stares at it, but this time turns his head to the left, and still does not respond. The doctor, begining to get a little upset at the lack of cooperation, sets that one down, and holds up another one. Ice stands up, turns his back to the doctor, and folds his chair up placing it up against the wall that is now in front of him. 'Mr. Ice, would you please turn..' Ice quickly grabs the chair, spins, and drills the doctor in the side of the head, knocking him to the floor. Ice sickly smirks as the doctor lies on the floor. Slowly Ice raises the chair above his head, and slams it down on the doctor. Again Ice slams the chair onto the doctor. Ice tosses the chair aside, and it clips the corner of the table, causing the note pad to fall on top of the doctor. Ice reaches down and pulls the note pad off of the doctor. A large blot of blood covers most of the paper. Ice smugly looks at it and then back down at Dr. Gruzielata. 'What do you think of that ink blot doctor?' Ice drops the note pad, and exits the room. He walks back down the hallway he came and meets up with Star and the brunette who are sitting on a small bench talking. Star notices Ice and quickly comes over to him. 'So?' 'Tomarrow, 1.' The brunette stands. 'Where is Dr. Gruzielata?' Ice smirks. 'He's still in the room, gathering his notes.' Ice wraps his arm around Star and the two exit the clinic. The scene fades.
