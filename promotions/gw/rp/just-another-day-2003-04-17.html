Just another day

17 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/17/03 The GW offices were quickly becoming a place which Salaok enjoyed as people who he had hated before had no choice but to do as he says or find themselves out of work. Power that seemed to satisfy some of his need. But not everyone was confirming to the ways in which things were to be done. And he never thought that the major problem would arise within his long time friend and fellow member of The Lair. Quartz was for some unknown reason causing problems and helping the one man who he has no right having anything to do with. The match last Evolution was the be the match which destroyed Tragic Hero as he placed him in a match where his partner would clearly be attacked and taken out of the match. Placed into a match against one of the best tag teams in the federation that had ever existed. It was a masterpiece. Places those who he wanted to get rid of, in a match against people he hated just as much. The idea of making his enemies do his bidding for him would normally make him happy. But not now. Quartz had gone and helped Vincent Welch out. Helped him when he had no right to do so and put a hitch in Salaok's plans. But why? The thoughts run through Salaok's mind as he sits in a meeting, trying to stay focused on the topic at hand but finding it all too boring. That was the only problem Salaok had with Global Wrestling. It was legitimate. It was the first business that he had run that was controlled by crime and illegal dealing. That's not to say that illegal dealings didn't take place inside of Global Wrestling, butt he fact was that it wasn't controlled by it which Salaok simply didn't enjoy. 'Listen, I am going to appoint a commissioner to the federation next Monday so would you get off my fucking back.' The man who sits on the other side of the table, flanked by other men wearing suits sits up with a confused look on his face. 'Sir, you already have a Commissioner. Stephen Perdue. Remember?' A smile comes over Salaok's face as he looks up at the men. 'How foolish of me. Of course I do. Has anyone seen Perdue lately?' 'Well no sir. To think about it, I haven't.' Salaok raises an eyebrow at the men before glancing down at his wrist, looking for his watch. He pulls the sleeve of his trench coat back and sees the time. 'Well gentlemen, it was nice having this talk, but I really must run.' The head man coughs before looking Salaok in the eye with a bemused look. 'Salaok, sir, no offence but you haven't answered a single question we have asked you. We need to know the arrangements for Wrestlecade so we can begin the extended advertising and the guest appearances.' Salaok stands up and fixes the collar of his coat around the back of his neck while looking out the window. 'Well, as long as Brick can continue to win his matches, then it will be myself and that fool in a Hell in a Cell match. Now run off would you?' 'I don't think you are understanding. We need the full card.' 'Well I don't have it. Now get the fuck out of my face would you? You know where the door is, make sure you shut it as you leave. Good day gentlemen.' The tone in Salaok's voice had become the mono-toned beed and ruthless sounding voice that the world had come to know and fear throughout the years. He wasn't in the mood to deal with the business of the federation at the moment. For now he needed answers. For now he needed to talk to Quartz and find out what his intentions are and if his helping Vincent was a game or something more. Walking over to the window and turning his back on the men at the table, Salaok peers out on the world and watches at the sun sets dirrectly infront of him. The men in the room give up and lead by the man at the head of the table, get up and leave. Their suits and briefcases made Salaok sick to the stomach. This wasn't his world. Business deals yes, but corporate business bored him. No, Salaok world was out there on the mind of everyone who walks, drives and taxis their way home from work. His world is the world that the people had learnt to fear and he loved it. No matter what it was that was the means of their fears, Salaok always seemed to be at the every heart of it. The world of organised crime isn't the same as what it was ten years ago. Now it's all about the dirt and how much you are willing to use it in order to get what you want. How far you are willing to go in order to make sure you got what you wanted, and that those who stood against you, coudn't talk up about it. Domination of a world is what The Lair wanted and finally they were getting it. Darkness and corruption was the order of the day to day lives of the people of this city. Salaok was home. But Quartz, his long time friend, and partner for most crimes was becoming a headache and Salaok needed to find out why. Salaok knew where Quartz would be. It was the same place he always was at this time of the day. The castle of The Nevermore had two great cooks who had been hired and work solely for them. Each meal was one of greatness and when you have as much money as The Nevermore had, you can afford to eat like this every night of the week. And with Quartz being the size that he is, there is only one way to know where he is and that is during meal time. Quartz would be at home and that is where Salaok needed to be. Turning from the window and quickly swipping his keys, cellphone and a pile of important papers from desk, he moves towards the door. Click He hits the light switch as he opens the door and sees Liz still sitting there at her esk, taking phone calls and filling out the forms that she knew Salaok needed done. Normally he would be doing them tomorrow, but she had been able to tell what Salaok wanted from day one. He rearly had to ask Liz to do anything as she was all ready on top of the situation by the time he had asked about it. He closed his office door behind him and stopped at her desk, resting his hands on the table top and waiting for her to finish with the phone call. 'OK, well I will have Mr Davis get in touch with you as soon as possible. He's already left the office for tonight so will tomorrow during office hours be good for you? ...... OK, well I will let him know. Thank you and have a nice night.' Liz puts the phone down and looks at at Salaok who has a smile etched into his face. He had quickly found a place in his heart for this woman. Not a love feeling, but one of respect for the job she does. He was also thankful that she was so much better than Amy who worked for him at one of the Casinos. She would be putting calls through to Salaok even when he had left to go home hours before. 'Let me guess Salaok, your going home for dinner?' Salaok chuckles to himself for a second.'How'd you guess?' 'You've told me about those cooks you've got. I would do the same thing if I were you sir.' The thought of inviting her around for dinner crossed his mind for a mere second before remembering what was waiting for him at home. He wasn't going home for the food. He was going there to talk to Quartz. 'Well Liz, you know you are welcome around anytime. Lisha has been wanting to have you around for dinner you know.' 'I know sir. Maybe another night. I have a heap of work to do.' Salaok raises an eyebrow in question of her statement before walking over to the wall and turning the main lights out, letting only the small wall lights give life to the room. 'You've finished for today. Go home Liz. We can do what ever needs to be done in the morning. I have a feeling I will be here early tomorrow anyway so I can get some of it out of the way before you get here.' With a smile growing on her face, Liz shuts down the computer and turns off her desk lamp. She gathers her stuff from her desk and walks over to the door with Salaok and they leave the office together. Moving through the large building they finally get down to the car park which is poorly lit but both had walked through it enough late at night to become used to it. Not that anyone was ever going to mug Salaok and get away with it. 'Well, the hearse is over there. I will see you in the morning.' 'OK Salaok. See you then.' The two walk off in different directions as they head off to their awaiting cars. The thuds his heavy feet made on the concrete floor echoed in the stillness of the car park, but were soon drown out b the sound of Liz's car starting up in the background. Or at least that is what he figured it to be. He too had soon reached his car and with a press of the key pad, the alarm system disables and the doors unlock. Opening the driverside door, he plonks himself down in the detailed leather seat and shuts the door. A sigh and his body seems to melt into the chair. The days of Salaok were long, but the nights seemed to be forever longer. He reaches forward with the keys in his hand and inserts them into the slot and turning them. First the stereo comes on and the heavy metal sounds from one of his many CD's explodes into the air drowning out all sound from the outside world. Which right now was exactly what Salaok was needing. He pumps the peddle to let some gas through the pipes and then turns the key to hear the perfect purr of the V8 engine spark to life. The seat belt goes on and clicks into place, the car is clicked into drive and the peddle pushed down. The car moves out of the parked position. SMASH He had pulled out of the parking spot without looking and now a Suzuki Esteem seemed to be connected to the front of Salaok's hearse. Seriously, this couldn't be happening. Out of all of the nights that some jerk had to be speeding through the car park, tonight had to be the one that Salaok came in contact with him. The pile of papers which Salaok had placed on the seat beside him, now lay in a heaped mess on the floor with a packet of cigarettes which had also spilled open. Reaching over and grabbing one of his precious cigarettes, he places it in his mouth and lights it before getting out of the car. With great effort, he pushes the door open as it clunks a part of the body work out of the way which causes Salaok to sigh again. He pulls himself out of the car and looks at the damage, only to shake his head. The front drivers side of the car had taken a blow and was going to cost him a bucket load. But the other car wasn't much better as it seemed to have buckled completely at the front. The drivers door of the other car opens and a man steps out wearing sunglasses. There was a part of the problem straight away. What kind of moron wears sunglasses in a poorly lit car park? As he emerges further out from the car, it's clear to see that the moron in question is Jason Dynasty who looks to be as pissed off as Salaok. Both cars were still running and look like they could be driver for a short distance at least. 'Listen Jason. Get in the car, drive to wear ever you need to drive it, get a taxi or what ever. I will cover the costs. Right now I just don't need any shit from insurance companies.' A grumble sounds from Dynasty as he gets back into the car and backs it up as pieces begin to fall to the ground and the hubcap of Salaok's front wheel rolls away. Salaok just plonks himself back into the car and before Dynasty can drive forward again, Salaok pushes the peddle to the floor and speeds out, leaving Dynasty behind and clearly in a foul mood. Did this matter to Salaok? No. He could cover the costs of both cars easily and didn't need to get caught up in an arguement about who's fault it was and everything else. Money would solve the problem. It always had and always would. You throw enough money at someone and they will see your point of view soon enough. However, that didn't apply to what Salaok had to do now. Now it would be a matter of getting home and confronting Quartz. Money wouldn't buy him. The Lair had already made him rich, as it had done Salaok. Answers was all Salaok was wanting and answers were now only a drive away.
