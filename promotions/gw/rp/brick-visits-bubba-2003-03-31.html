Brick visits Bubba

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/31/03 Bubba McCoy lay silent in his hospital bed. He has been in a coma since for a couple of weeks now. Since then, Salaok, who was recently revealed as new co-owner of Global Wrestling, had run roughshod over the company, firing various GW superstars and toying with others. One person who fell into the latter category was the man who had come to visit Bubba that day: Brick. A man who had the distinct 'honor' of being on Salaok's most hated list, Brick was now under the stipulation that should he lose a match, he would be suspended from Global Wrestling until such time as Salaok saw fit to reinstate him. Of course, knowing Salaok, that day would probably never come. 'May I help you?' asked the nurse. 'I'm here to see Bubba McCoy.' 'Are you immediate family?' Brick merely stared at her and replied 'Do I *look* like immediate family? I'm one of his employees.' 'Oh, right. You're Brick. My son is a huge fan of yours.' 'Really?' 'Yes, in fact, I wonder if I could get your autograph. It would really mean a lot to him.' 'Yeah, sure,' Brick replied, as he reached into his pocket, looking for a pen. 'Ah, found one!' The nurse handed him an old Brick poster. 'Oh, you came prepared.' 'Actually, when I told my son that Mr. McCoy was in the hospital, he asked me to hold onto this, in the off chance that you came to visit him, hoping that I could get you to autograph it.' 'Your son's a smart kid. So what's his name?' 'Jeremy.' 'OK. 'To Jeremy. Brick.' There. Hope Jeremy's happy.' 'Actually, you're not the first Global Wrestling personality to visit Mr. McCoy while he's been here.' 'I'm not.' 'No, there was his friend Logan Creed. Plus, there were two friends of his, Jesse Briggs and Ely Braxton.' 'Really?' 'In fact, they were here before Mr. McCoy slipped into his coma. His room is down the hall to your right, by the way.' 'OK, thanks.' As Brick walked down the hall, he pondered why Briggs and Braxton were there. No one had seen them in GW for months. Then it hit him. It was at that moment that he reached Bubba's room. He peered in through the window at Bubba. He saw him hooked up to a respirator, lying very still. It wasn't a way Brick was accustomed to seeing Bubba. 'Hey, Bubba,' he said. 'I've been meaning to come around, but I've been putting it off. By the way, my mom's been praying for you. Well, I don't know if you've been aware of this, but Salaok is fucking up the company. I mean, seriously.' Brick then paused to sigh. 'He's been going nuts. First, he fires Price, Dynasty, Stecchino, Fury and Reno. Then he makes up this bullshit stipulation where, if I lose a match, I get suspended until he decides to bring me back, which you and I both know ain't never gonna happen. In fact, he booked me in a triple threat match for this coming Evolution. Motherfucker's trying to screw me. He ain't slick. That don't mean I won't do what I have to. And now I heard he fired that cat Lance Daniels all because o' that Sharpshooter he placed him in at the end o' last week's Evolution. It's a seriously fucked up situation. Now, Perdue is missing. Ain't nobody seen him since the last Evolution. Now I know you, Bubba. I know you ain't the type o' cat to just roll over and die and just let Salaok get away with shit like this without a fight. Now you know me. I ain't scared o' Salaok or Quartz or any of them cats, but the company needs you.' Suddenly, a doctor walked up to him. 'Are you acquainted with the patient?' 'Yeah, he's my boss. Doctor, tell me the truth. Is he gonna be OK?' 'I wish I could give you a straight answer, sir. This is one of the most unusual cases we've encountered. There are high levels of toxins in Mr. McCoy's bloodstream.' 'And you don't know how they got there?' 'We haven't a clue. But we're doing whatever we can.' 'Thanks, Doc.' As the doctor walked away, a thought sprang into his head. How did those toxins get into Bubba's system? And who would gain the most if Bubba were gone? One person immediately sprang to mind. 'That motherfucker!' Brick stormed out of the hospital as fast as he could. He had a match to prepare for...and a suspect to confront.
