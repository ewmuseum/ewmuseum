The change...complete

25 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-25-05 My eyes began to wonder throughout the dark and moody room. Cold air coming out of my warm lips. My body, feeling every little inch of clothing that was strung on my body. Black leather covering my warm silky skin. Tightly wrapping around my legs and arms, milked on over my chest and letting my nipples come out for you to see. For some reason I was changing. I couldn’t see it for myself but I knew I was changing. The little fire that I had going in the broiler, smoking out and scorching my face with the heat it was letting off. Looking down with my bluish eyes at a box that was sitting at the front of my feet, lowering my left hand down to grab a handful of clothing that was in it. “To the death of her?” Rattling out words before tossing them right into the fire. Sometimes we change, I know that, but for this it was different. Ever since the red eye monster had come into my life my actions have been unwritten by me. My mind was wondering off and coming back with a more attitude that made me scared. My heart, the very written foundation of me wasn’t even there anymore. I was not Elia Smith. I was not me. “To the death of Elia Smith.” Tossing in another handful of clothing into the fire. I kept on tossing away before the door that was leading into here came open. Light began to shine right on in, casting a shadow over the wall in front of me of the person standing there. “You in there Elia?” Screams the person. Nothing came out of me. Not a response. Not a reply at all. “Elia?” Repeats the person as they came walking in to the room, shutting the door behind them. My head twirls around to see the person walking down the stairs. My lips, moving over my tongue as it come in and out, rubbing over the dark lipstick that I had place on. My hands, rubbing on the baseball bat that was leaning on the wall next to me. I could feel the cold air mixing with the warm fire from the broiler as he came into focus. “Elia?” He says, walking into the light. “What!” Responding to his words. “What are you doing down here?” Response my Uncle in his rough words. “Burning a past that isn’t needed.” Turning away from him as my words came out. Tossing in another handful of clothing, also picking up a picture in the process. Sunny day, a single tree standing behind me and my Uncle as he stood there in the path of the camera. I didn’t know where it was nor did I really care right now. My emotions were gone and all I felt was what I needed to feel. “Burning who’s past Elia?” My Uncle response before walking a little closer to me. After throwing the stuff into the red and orange blazing fair, reaching over and grabbing the bat before letting my words come out through my lips. “Stay away!” Saying in a low angry tone. “I do not know you anymore.” “Huh?” Uncle Johnson says. “What are you talking about Elia?” “I’m talking about your weak and pathetic life you are trying to toss on her.” Turning around, finally showing the white face and dark coloring that I had placed on my face. His face, twisting from confusion to fear of the unknown. Raising an eyebrow to the words that had come out of my mouth. “You are not ok Elia. You need help. This is not you, this is not like you..” Pausing for a moment as he saw the baseball bat come up. “You are right, this is not me. This is not your little girl anymore. This is something’different.” Walking closer towards him with my eyes sparkling all over his fearing body. Fear was not something I knew at this moment. Anger, frustration, and the changing inter self was the only feature I knew right now. Looking deep into his eyes, feeling out everything he was thinking in his mind as I walked up closer to him with that baseball bate. “Uncle, what you see in front of you is the beginning of who I was destined to be. I was born to be a plague in this world yet I was raised as a butterfly. I was forced to be something I was not meant to be and you were the one you helped make that reality happen.” Rubbing the bat closely against my chest, licking the top of it like it was a lollipop. “What are you talking about?” Backing up against the brick wall behind him. “What am I talking about” I’m talking about the sweet heart that you have changed me into being. The small little puppy that you portrayed me to be to the entire world. Look at me Uncle. Look at the woman that he has helped me to be.” Rubbing the baseball bat against his chest now before licking my dark lips. I could feel his heart racing deep inside. Pounding to get free from the small area of fear that he was covering in. Watching him look down at the baseball bat, I slung it back and right against the wall, making him scream a little out loud. “ELIA!” Laughing out loud as his response, I began to press up again his body, talking into his small ear where he could hear me best. “Uncle. Your little girl is gone for good. The only think that remains is the shadow that has been cast by her now blacken heart. He has made me realize the true nature of who I need to be. He has shown me the way to my next target. And my next target is?” Sighing for a moment before pulling back away from him. ??You.” His eyes went as wide as the Pacific Ocean, knowing that the baby girl he use to train was fictionally dead. Swinging the bat right at his chest and slamming it right into the gut, doubling him right over to the floor, watching blood slowly trickle out from his mouth. His hands began to pull forward for the stairs. “Uncle Johnson, you are a disgrace to him and to me. You have been cast away by me and the true fear of your mind has made you weak beyond anything.” Screaming over him before raising the baseball bat once again. “For that I must strike down upon thee.” Raising the baseball bat high, looking down at his body as it crawls for anything to protect himself. Nothing could save him, not even Elia Smith, driving the bat right down and striking him in the back. His mind and body was struck out, laying there in nothing but hay and blood. It was over. It was done. Dropping the bat down beside him before turning away and walking towards the fire once more. Leaning down to the box once more, seeing the final piece of me in there and picking it up. A picture of me when I was three years old. Smiling as I was being held by my parents. Tossing the picture into the fire without hesitation, my eyes watching it burn away to nothing less then ashes. The turn was complete. Elia smith was no more?
