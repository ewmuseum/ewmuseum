A conclusion (short) Stan Stone Global Wrestling Promo #315 Date: 6/16/03 Location: Hotel, six hours before Evolution His suit was fresh and clean cut, black jacket with a black shirt and a white tie, all silks fresh from Italy. Stan Stone was clean shaven, impeccably groomed, dark sunglasses hang low on his nose as he surveys the daunting hotel looming above him. He lets a long whistle escape his lips, rarely did Stan spend this money on a hotel but as he looked at the layout he was glad that he did. Tossing his keys to the teen-age valet he grabs up his bags and heads inside along a red carpet layed out at the entrance. The marble floors covered in thick oriental rug's and the walls covered with intricate murals dipicting some event or another. 'Can I help you' the hostess behind the desk inquired of Stan as he arrived with an awestruck expression on his face. 'Uhh, y-yeah I have a room reserved under Stone, first name Stan.' For a few seconds she goes to her keyboard typing in the name to pull up the information, but every now and then Stan could catch her gaze as it shifted over to the handsome chisled face of the new arrival. When she handed him the key along with his room numbed she held his hand a little longer then regulation and Stan smiled, it was appearant that this would be a great night. Nodding his head and walking off towards the elevators his spirits were in high order and each step showed his happiness... yes, it would be a great day... **** I always hated those people who had everything given to them on a silver platter, those men and women who ride there talent to success that I can only dream of. I hate those people who are given gifts from god. Everything they do goes against the principle 'if you work hard you get rewarded' but that's bullshit to them, they could care less how hard they work, they don't need to work, they've got talent. It's just god spoiling his sons, and it makes me angry every day of the week to see men and women like that walk down the street while I stand as the opposite to everything they could ever be. I don't go a day without working, I train for my job ten hours a day five days a week, I've got the strength of an ox and the endurance of hell, when it comes to endurance I fall into my own category. Everyday that I see those people I wan't to wring there necks, I sit here at the bottom of the barrell, waiting to prove myself again, I'm clean and new and will work my way up, talent doesn't mean a damn thing next to work.... and I'll prove that. Sweat beaded at his forhead and flew forward everytime his fist smashed into the punching bag. As every muscle strained and flexed, his power rushing through his who arm coming together at one point and being released with all the hitting force of a professional boxer. It sent the bag lurching as Stan Stone backs off, grabbing up a white towel draped over a metal chair in the corner of the hotel gym and wipes the excess moisture off of his shoulders and neck. Dressed in a white sleevless t-shirt and black sweat pants with running shoes, Stan Stone had all the looks of a fitness nut, perfectly toned and chisled muscles could be seen all over his upper body, looking at the wall to wall mirrors he admires himself for a moment while taking a break from the workout that had started only six hours ago and had it been any other day but a match day then Stan would be returning to the gym for another six hours. I guess it's about time I grab a bite to eat. he remarks to himself while glancing at the clock above the doorway on the opposite end of the room. Tossing the towel over his shoulder he walks out of the gym with all the grace of a man who knew not just every detail of himself but every detail of his surroundings as well. A few minutes later, a few rooms traveled and he was sitting quietly in a booth, his towel deposited at the laundry and his clothes changed to that of a pair of jeans and a t-shirt. He ate only a sandwich with a glass of water, it was important to eat lite and not bog yourself down with heavy foods or alcohol. He'd warmed up and would rest until seven at which point he would arrive at the arena and get ready. No one in the GW had probably ever heard of Stan, no one had probably ever seen Stan and tonight would be a first for many, even he felt a little nervous about this, it was something he hadn't expierenced in a long time, coming in a nobody, coming in at the bottom. But hardwork was what Stan Stone was all about, the one thing that had gotten him through life no matter what was in his way. ***** His muscles felt tense as the hot water rushed over him in the shower, the steam building up and creating a cover over his more private extremities. He stood, his entire body sculpted by years of hard training, underneath the oncoming rush. The water was hot but Stan never felt that it was hot enough to wash away his past sins, as he turned his face up into the water he felt it run over and through every line and every scar. Strong as an ox and as tall as most basketball players, Stan Stone was an impressive sight to behold out of the ring but every fiber in his body had begun to ache to get back in the ring. Every muscle strained to do his job. The hardwork that he had done all his life, the pain he felt and the disrespect he had received would soon all be worth it, every last minute of frustration was about to be released, he was about to usher into a new world of oppertunities and Stan Stone had every intention of leaving his mark. As he steps out of the shower, clean of all the sweat and grime built up, he knows that tonight he was going to leave an impact....
