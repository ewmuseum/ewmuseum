Thug 101: Refresher Course

12 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Evo #1 (6/12/04) There were three ways this could go. Half decent... Piss poor... To the hospital. The neon lights of the bar glowed brightly in the dark of night. Train sat behind the wheel of his rented BMW X-Type SUV, the tip of the cigar glowing in the same darkness that occupied the world around him. The smoke drifted lazily on the air inside the car as Train pulled the smoke into his mouth...but just to his mouth as Train never inhaled smoke... from tabbaco. This was a regular cigar, if not couting the fact that it was hand rolled in the keys of Florida, by men who apparently couldn't bear to be too far away from their native Cuba. Normally Train would've been smoking a different style of hand rolled cigar, but, despite the fact he was preparing to enter a bar, there was no room for intoxication. Not tonight. The tipsy leprechaun danced a little jig while toasting with his hefty beer mug and blowing bubbles, all the action glowing in technicolor. The animated neon sign of the Drunken Leprechaun must've been new. Train hadn't remembered seeing it the last time he was here. Then again, the last time he was here, the sign, decorations, decor nor atmosphere meant a damn thing. He'd only been here for one reason and one reason only... Someone needed to learn a lesson. Looking back on it Train could only see the episode in a haze cause, he determined, either by the time that had past or the weed he'd been smoking. Fact is it hadn't been that long ago...only a few months, not long after he'd come into GW. The short time frame suggested the smoke as the culprit. But, then again, he'd blown lightyears past where he'd been at the time. Train was no longer the mark... the quick win... the new kid on the block that everyone thought they could just fuck with, with impunity. He was no longer someone to be played with. He was a champion. Easy to understand how it might seem a lifetime ago. Yeah.... Back then it was a different story. Back then McNasty thought he was slick. It started off as an issue between Christina and Train. She'd been mad at him...cool... whatever. Oh, but then the bitch walked up in his home like she owned it and proceeded to talk shit like she was the one to lay down the law...in his home. Yup... a lot of people had shit twisted. Back in the day. Train had gotten it untwisted for them though. Christina wanted to come into Train's home and tear shit up. It's amazing how complicated it had gotten from there. Especially since all he'd done was return the favor. She came into his home and disrespected it... He did the same to Global Arena and disrespected her. Christina's office? Fucked up. Christina's security? Fucked up. Her ride? Fucked up. The broadcast? As closed to fucked up as you can get without being. And she knew who it was...who had done it to her. She knew...but like every detective Train had ever encountered, she couldn't prove it. She tried to bounce him out of his contract early on some moral terpitude wrap...she wasn't the only one with lawyers though. She had no evidence against Train so she had no legal cause to release him. And she wasn't going to get any evidence either. That was the plan anyway. Hell, it should've been fool proof. Gloves mask...no id. Of course Train would up like those cops that beat down Rodney King...he hadn't counted on the random video camera. Especially one held by Donny McNasty's good buddy Hardcore Harv. From what Train had gathered from the tape Donny and his buddy came to the arena that night looking for him...trying to get a little revenge for Train handing him his ass on a silver platter the week before. Apparently Harv decided it was a moment that needed to be saved for posterity. They didn't find Train. Officially. Harv walked outside just in time to see Train take his mask off before hopping into the Nav and rolling out. Train was about a quarter pissed at himself. Taking the mask of...that was some rookie shit. He knew better and it never should've happened. Train underestimated the folks down here...how damn lucky the bastards could get. It was alright though... lesson learned. Oh, but it was over though... Train was about three quarters pissed at Donny and his buddy. They had the tape. Could've turned it over to Christina and had him bounced. But they didn't do that...naw... They didn't want Train gone. They thought it would be fun to let Train know they had the tape and could get rid of him any time they got ready. Naw, they didn't want Train gone. They just wanted to be someplace they shouldn't have tried to be. Inside his head. Somebody should have told them though. Train wasn't one to let people to invade his space. Back in Chicago if he'd just let people get away with that it wouldn't have been long before the only space he occupied was a hole about six feet deep. Naw...that shit wasnt' allowed. And Train's head? That's more his personal space than any place else on Earth. Someone had to learn a lesson. Train had fun watching the tape though. He almost wished they had given McCoy the tape just to let the bitch see the look on his face. If she had, she would have known he wasn't the one to be played with cuz his face said it all as he strolled from Christina's limo, where he'd graciously left the sledgehammer sitting in her winshield in case she felt like she need to use it, back to his SUV. It was funny watching himself on the video. Just calmly swaggering away like he'd done that shit a million times. Fact was he hadn't. He'd never been the one for such trivial games. He'd always figured a malotov cocktail through the window made a bigger statment than a broken windshield or a scratched paint job. Surely a message from the devil came through a little clearer through the flames as opposed to some broken glass. But Train decided he should take it easy on her. Trashing her office, beating the shit out of her security detail, nearly knocking out power to the arena with an axe to the breaker box and a sledgehammer through the windshield... It got the point across...even it it was a little more subtle than usual. Yeah...it was a fun tape. Didn't take long to burn though. One metal barrel, a dash of lighter fluid and add one match and it's a recipe for burning evidence. Train was sure that McNasty and his boy had comforted themselves after they lost the tape that it hadn't been easy to get. Bullshit. The dumb bastards had called him right from the Leprechaun. No cloned cells, no pay phone down the street. Just them and what they thought was a fear inducing threat. If they had known it was just going to piss Train off they probably wouldn't have bothered. They would have done the smart thing. They would have turned it in and been rid of him. But then Train's first visit to The Drunken Leprechaun was bound to happen though. It was pretty much unavoidable. If they'd have turned in the tape he would've have come for them too. And it probably wouldn't have been near the happy ending it had been before. Looking back on it now, Train surprised himself thinking about how it had gone down. He'd gone though a lot of trouble sneaking in through the window of the men's room and waiting till the Leprechaun shut down. The blunt made the wait for the patrons to leave easier though. And it hadn't really been that hard to stroll out into the bar while Harv was doing his last minute book keeping before heading home. The stereo was decent so it bumped pretty good when Train put the cd he'd made just for the occasion into the player and poured himself a drink. Heck, it bumped so loud Harv had to come out of his office in the back to figure out what the hell was going on. He didnt' even know who Train was at first. That changed pretty quick. Train had a little respect for Harv. He wasn't a push over. He didnt' scare easy. He didn't want to give up the tape. He was a good friend. Loyal. He had his mind made up. He knew Train was gonna hurt him if he didn't give it up, but Harv was apparently of the opinion that it was worth enduring a little pain to protect his buddy Donny. That was ok though. The Glock had it's uses when it came people's minds. Changing them...and blowing them. And there'd been at time Train wouldn't have given a shit whether it changed a damn thing. A time where the 9mm really only had one use. Yeah...he'd come a long way in a short time and luckily the guns uses had doubled by the time Harv had met Train. Train had always thought of a gun like a Dragon, but he kept it from spitting any fire that night. Someimes the metal was actually better cold. The simple touch of it to the back of Harv's skull sent a visible shiver down the man's spine. There was no more resistance. The safe under the bar popped open quick like after that. The second it did the tape went from theirs to his. It was exactly what Train wanted. Or he was sure Harv thought so. The tape was only half of it. A message had to be sent. He knew McNasty wouldn't really hear him just cuz Harv got smacked around a little. But that was ok. It was also ironic. Harv had been the one to pull out the bat to try and protect himself. Instead it was the thing that did him in. The bat came down on the bar with Harv's hand trapped in between... Train didn't have to say much after that. The snap, crackle and pop of flesh and bone spoke volumes. Train's mind finally came back to the future. Or at least to the present... contemplating the future. This wasn't supposed to turn into a fight, but he knew damn well it could. And if it did, he'd just have to handle that. But seeing as it was a busy night on their home turf. Getting out might not be nearlly as getting in. All that required was the front door. The prior might require swollen knuckles. Luckily he was used to those. Normally this wouldn't be the type of plan Train would endorse. But, just like the last time, a message had to be delivered. Normally he'd have been happy not to have to endure grating of McNasty's voice on his eardrums, but this was different. Master of the Mat was Train's opportunity smash the concrete of his status and one of the best superstars on the roster and reset it it in stone as THE best superstar on the roster. Of course it didnt' make a hell of a lot of sense why he'd need some other asshole to help him do it, but thems was the breaks. Now if McNasty knew how to play ball the way he should have by now this face to face wouldn't have been neccessary. Train had already delivered several messages to McNasty. But he was incited to show up in person because a string of unanswered voice mails was less than encouraging. There are a few things you can get away with when dealing with the cooler, slightly calmer Train. Blowing him off isn't one of them. McNasty made a face to face neccessary. Train figured he had about a 75% chance of catching him here. Yeah, McNasty would be pissed, but that was ok. Donny liked to play that happy go lucky bullshit to the hilt, but having faced with him on several occassions Train knew, if he wanted a shot to pass to the next round of the MOTM he didn't need the drunk and happy McNasty. He need the pissed off Irish bastard with fire in his eyes. That's who his partner needed to be and he'd get him. One way or another. ======= ======= ======= The brightly charged sound of old school rock n' roll blew out of the door as Train pushes it inward. The unintelligible hum of random conversations echoes beneath the music, the smell of cheap beer and cheaper cigars permeating the area as Train steps in. The place is packed and, just like he remembered, the bar is only a few feet from the door. The security up front was a young guy, couldn't be more than 22 , shaggy blonde hair with a scruffy goatee...probably 6'2'... 250... He was making time with a few of the girls in the crowd, but Train still picked him out of the crowd. Especially since, the second he saw him, Train had the funny thought that he'd seen him someplace before... couldn't place him though... Whatever. He needed to watch out for him, bottom line. But was it just him? Train's eyes continued to scan the crowd, taking a complete assessment in just the few seconds it took him to casually stroll up to the bar. He didn't spot anyone else. He might have been surprised if he didn't know the owner so well. This was a step or two above a shithole honky tonk so he might be the only bouncer on duty. Especially since Harv was one of those original tough guys. If someone needed to be thrown out of his bar he'd probably be obliged to do it himself. If this went wrong he knew Harv wasn't anyone he needed to worry about. Unless other folks had Train wrapped up there was no way he'd get froggy...try to put his hands on Train. Anything other than that and Harv would do everything he could to keep his hands as far away from Train as possible. Not because he was neccessarily scared. But the sound of your bones breaking leaves an impression... a scar. Train cuts out a space to call his very own at the crowded bar. The girl tending bar, with her tight top is cute, but likely the Altoona version of a supermodel. She was ok to look at, but Train was looking for another face. Two of them of in fact...and neither one had a set on them like hers. Train just grinned, holding his hand up, the universal signal for attention. She glanced down in his direction, responding with a little grin that translated into 'I'll be there in a second'. She's cute and good to her word. She quickly pops the tops on five Miller Lights down the bar and quickly hustles down to the other side, greeting the non-regular with a smile. 'What can I get you?' she asks loudly while managing to keep her sweet, helpful tone. 'Just a Coke baby.' Train responds with his own charming grin and deep tone. 'Just a Coke?!' she asks playfully, feigning shock/outrage. 'You seem like a big boy. You sure you don't want anything with that?' 'Oh, I do...but I don't think I'll be able to read your phone number after you stick it in the coke.' 'Cute... One Coke coming up.' Her pleasant grin just a bit bigger the lady quickly clutches the drink gun next to her and shoots a cold stream of chipped ice into a glass, the two clinking together like a set of wind chimes. After filling the glass halfway a fizzing stream of soda goes in next. In less than 20 seconds Train has a cold glass of Coke set gently on the bar in front of him. He shows his appreciation by reaching into his pocket, retrieving a fiver and dropping it into the giant cognac snifter that doubles as a tip jar. 'Thank you,' she says, acknowledging his contribution. 'Call me if you need something else.' She turns to head back to the other end of the bar, but finds a large hand, rough, but it's grip fairly gentle holding her back. 'Hold up a second. You can help me out...' Train begins. 'You seen Donny in here tonight?' 'Donny who? McNasty?' 'That's him.' 'You a friend of Donny's?' 'A friend? Naw... I ain't his friend.' 'You're not? So.....what do you need him for?' 'I ain't got to be his friend to be his partner.' 'Wha....... Ohhhhhh.... I get it. You're one of those wrestler guys, right?' 'Nope. I'm one of them champion guys.' 'Mmmm... Well... Donny's not here... Hasn't been in all week.' 'All week, huh?' Train inquired... momentarily convinced until she tried to add on the bit about him not having been there all week in a burst of what he was sure she thought was great inspiration... knowing damn well she was lying. His mouth opened to ask the next logical question, but he didn't really need the answer from her, the mirror behind the bar answering it for him. Yet he asked anyway, not wanting his new/old friend to be thown off by realizing he didn't have the element of surprise the way he likely assumed. 'What about Harv...... He around?' As if he needed to ask. The woman's head and body leaned to the side, obviously looking around Train's massive frame to something of more interest behind him. Train turned on his heels, trying his best to feign a bit of surprise as he locked eyes with 'Hardcore' Harv...his old friend the 'the bat' gripped in his hand. 'Damn Harv... Where'd you come from?' Train asked, doing his best to play into the man's ego. He'd be less prone to actually jump him if he thought Train was somehow scared. 'Long time no see man.' 'Fuck you.' Harv stated flatly, his hand gripping the wooden club that much tighter. 'You got 10 seconds to tell me what you want before I take this bat and...' 'Careful Harv.' Train retorted harshly, cutting off as if with a knife. 'You and bats ain't never really seemed to get along. I'd hate to see you get hurt..... Again.' 'That's 9 seconds.' 'Tell you what...' Train begins, feeling his heart and his soul begining to ice over, the cold rage that seems to possess him at times creeping up his spine. However... he grows cold, but he stays cool. 'You wanna play Billy Badass again you can feel free to skip the rest of that bullshit and just call it zero. Or...you could stop setting yourself up for another ass whuppin' and tell me where Donny is.' 'I dont' have to tell you shit.' 'True. You don't have to go to the hospital either, but it's gonna be one or the other.' 'I'm not scared of you, you arrogant prick.' 'Oh really?' Train asked his question, letting his cold eyes cut through the bullshit and to the heart of it all. He said he wasn't scared......... Yes he was. Not a whole hell of alot... but he was scared. But it made sense that it wouldn't be crippling. This was his house during peak hours. Plenty of folks to watch his back. If Train took out the first couple with a broken bottle or bone they'd stop flooding in. However, Train called it right. The next voice he heard was deep, but still carried the distinction of youth. The scruffy big boy he spotted earlier didn't take long to interject himself in it. 'What's up uncle Harv?' Asked the young man, his eyes locked on the problem as it stood at the bar, staring back. 'You want me to show this guy the door?' 'Uncle Harv?' Train says, feigning befuddlement. 'You mean your parents had more after you?' 'Up yours pal.' responds the young man. Train just turns to him, staring him the eyes for a moment...his question takes the young man off guard. 'Don't I know you?' asks Train. 'Huh?' retorts Harv's nephew, dumbfounded for a moment. 'Look pal...that line ain't gonna work. Especially if you ain't bought me a drink.' 'That's cute.' Train responds. 'Now walk away, the adults are talking.' 'Make me.' 'You don't wanna play with me dogg.' 'Do you want me to call the cops?' asks the girl behind the bar, trying her best to difuse the tension that's is slowly, but surely attracting the attention of everyone in the room. 'Naw Amy...' Harv says. 'We got this.' 'You sure?' asks Train. 'You got a lotta nerve man.' states Harv's nephew plainly. 'There ain't no reason I can play with you pal. I play with a lotta guys and most of them wind up on the ground half broke.' 'Oh, for real?' 'For real asshole.' says Harv. 'You may think you're a tough guy, but Mikey here is one with some kind of respect. All American. Notre Dame.' 'I'll be damned...' Train said, almost to himself, suddenly remembering where he'd seen this kid before. It had been a long flight up to Pennsylvania...the sports section kept him occupied for most of it. 'Mike O'Malley. Notre Dame middle linebacker. Runs the 40 in what...6.5? Not bad for a big boy, man. All the papers are saying you're gonna be in the running for the Heisman this season. That's a helluva thing for a defensive player kid.' 'Damn right.' he responds flatly. 'And My uncle Harv is good enough to let me earn some cash over the summer bouncing bums like you. So you can get walk out or get bounced out.' 'Right, right... But seriously, I hear you're gonna go pretty early in the draft after the season. I guess you wanna play for the Steelers right?' 'Where I play isn't any of your business.' 'Where you play? Naw I guess it ain't. Oh, but whether? That's a different story.' 'What?' 'Whoa....hold the hell on...' Harv interjects...suddenly not liking the direction the conversation was headed in. 'Let me ask you something Mikey... Did you know it only takes 16 pounds worth of pressure per square inch to pop a knee out of it's socket? It's only 18 pounds in the right spot to tear and ACL. I know, doesn't seem like much right? I mean that type of shit could almost happen in the blink of an eye, right? But, naw, that's all it takes. And that damn AC Ligament man... Once you fuck that up... I mean yeah, you can come back, but shit rehab on something like that could take damn near a year to get back to a hundred percent. And really?! Once you done had it ripped in half...you ain't never really a hundred percent. I mean, not like you used to be. You wind up wearing a brace... you never get all your speed back. Hell, that type of injury... it's gonna take at least 3 years off your career if you're an athlete. Yeah man... It's crazy to think how much damage can come...so fucking quick.' Mikey stares at him for a moment, finally catching his drift. 'You threatening me you son of a...' 'Mikey, back off,' Harv said, knowing for a fact it was a threat that this trained fighter was capable of pulling off despite most efforts he'd be able to muster to stop it. 'Uncle Harv, let me...' 'Shut the fuck up and back the fuck off Mikey!!!' Harv yells, at the top of his lungs, knowing it would startle his thick headed, macho nephew enough to get him to listen in the short time he had to save him from a career threatening injury at the hands of a man he knew was capable of such a cold hearted act. It works. Mikey is startled along with everyone else in the room. Even the music stops at Harv's bellowing. Train stands and slowly strides over, meeting Harv toe to toe... man to man. 'I'm not gonna bother asking you where he is.' Train says. 'You ain't gonna tell me if he's here and I ain't gonna fight my way through everyone in here to find out. And if he's not you ain't gonna tell me where he is. But I know you're gonna tell him exactly what I have to say. So... You tell him... I want Master of the Mat. When the smoke clears everyone's gonna know who the best man doing is because I'm gonna be standing in the center of the ring with my arms raised when it's all said and done. You tell him that I said I know he's jealous of me. I know he thinks he deserves to be where I'm at even though he ain't proven shit to anyone. You tell him I said that I know he'd rather eat glass than lift a finger to help me... But you tell him I said...that if he wants to fuck with me? To do it in round 2. You tell him he'd better do his fucking part come Monday night. You tell him that I said if he wants to prove he deserves to be where I am then let him show the world when he faces off against me, but trying to do it in our match on Evo fucks him just as much as it does me. You tell him Donny... Train.... The Platinum Champion told him... If he wants to ignore me and play like he doesn't hear me, it's all good. But you also told him I said... Come game time? His punk ass better be ready to play... cuz this ain't no fucking game to me.' With that, all eyes firmly focused and burning a hole through him, Train glances over the room and all the hostile faces once more before pushing through the doors and disappearing into the dark. The tension takes a while to dissipate. As it slowly drifts off and the room returns to normal the door behind the bar slowly swings open and out steps the man of the hour.... 'The Nomad' Donny J. McNasty. he strolls up the the bar and pours himself a Jack and Coke on the rocks. Harv quickly joins him, getting one of his own. 'You hear him?' Harv asks. 'I heard him.' Donny says, taking a few sips of his drink. 'What about it?' Harv asks. 'You don't even want to talk a little strategy with him?' 'The strategy is simple. When Evolution comes I'll be ready and do my job. He wants to win as bad as he says he'll do the same. Until then?' Donny finishes for the moment, downing the rest of his Jack and Coke, slamming the glass back to the bar. 'Let the bastard sweat.'
