Date: 032004 That night?.I died. “I love you, John,” she managed to whisper before I pulled the trigger. Her body jerked once and screams filled the air. Lily was dead, yet the screams continued . . . because I was the one screaming. I lay there, next to her, not wanting to move, not wanting to go, not wanting to live. I have to let her go. I just got her back. I just lost her again. I begin to rise slowly, putting pressure slightly on my arm. Oh. My. God. The. Pain. Moves. Fast. I. Try. To. Ignore. It. I. Push. It. Away. I block it out. Breathe. In. Out. Breathe. Move. Ignore the pain. Shove it aside. I walk out the exit and look at the fiery heap that was the Escalade. Sorry, Alessandro. You were the father I never had but always wanted. I’m sorry it had to end this way. The sound of scraping metal. Vinnie slithers out of the wreckage. One of his arms is missing. His legs are battered and torn. He looks up and sees me. His face blackened. I raise the glock and shoot him in the forehead. Past the former Escalade, the guardhouse burns slowly. Looks like they missed another block. Oh well. I leave the burning facility and head towards my doctor. Not a hospital doctor, but a doctor who I know and who I trust. Someone who can keep their mouth shut. I move in and out of consciousness. A nurse over me. She places pills in my mouth and pours water into my throat. “Sleep, child,” she soothes. “Sleep.” I can’t sleep, for fear I’ll dream. It feels like swimming. Not in water. But some silky-smooth liquid that flows a little thicker. But yet, it wasn’t really swimming. More of a floating. A deep nothingness surrounding the body in all directions. A small light. Just enough to play tricks. A dark shadow passing. Almost brushing the body. Voices. From the past? From the present? Maybe both. Who knows. Calling. Ever calling. The shadow passing. Closer. Yet still far away. Tricks of the light. I hear my name called. Yelled. Screamed. Whispered. I turn. I look. Nothing. Always nothing. Behind me now. I can feel it. Deep down, past my soul, past my very being, where we feel true pain. I turn. I see it. The shadow. Its coming for me. Right towards me. A chorus of voices call. “We want you.” The shadows of my past, combined. Everything I’ve done catching up with me. The shadow is not a shadow, but a dark form. A sleek body gliding effortlessly in the liquid. A mouth opens. Rows of jagged teeth calling to me. I do not turn. I do not run. I do not try to hide. I move forward. Towards it. To embrace it. I hear it call my name, as its jaws close and I start to scream. A shadow. Creeping. Crawling. Slithering. Encompassing all. Consuming all. Nothing penetrates its darkness. Its blackness. Its hollowness. Its nothingness. Coming for you. Coming for me. Nothing can escape. You can run. But, eventually you have to stop. Take a breath. And that’s when it gets you. It still comes for me. At night. I feel it. Moving. Stealthily. In the shadows. Lurking. Waiting. Patiently. It moves closer. In my dreams. It wants me. It wants my soul. A giant vacuum, sucking the life out of everything. I It calls for me still. It screams. It whispers. It’s voice raspy, shriek-like. Nails on a chalkboard. I shudder. Because I know the truth. It is coming for me. And won’t stop till it has me. It lurks. In the darkness, in the shadows, in the corners of a blackened room, in the recesses of my mind. I know its there. I know its waiting. Waiting for me. Waiting for you. Patiently. It laughs. Because it knows. It knows that time is on its side. It knows that in the end, it will win. It knows that it will overcome. No matter where you run. No matter where you hide. It will find you. Don’t move. Don’t whisper. Don’t even breath. All of it will give you away. And when it finds you, when it gets you, it will never, ever, let you go. It’s like a leach. Sucking away at you. Sucking the life right out of you. Till you have no life, no soul to sell. I close my eyes. Try to rest. Try to sleep. But I can't. It's there. It's always there. Watching me. Waiting for me. It taunts me. Beckons to me. I resist. I can't go. No. Not yet. Soon. When the time is right, I will go. But, I will not go alone. Others shall come as well. And we will walk to it, like soldiers marching in a line, one-by-one, accepting our fate, almost welcoming it. But, that is the future, many a mile down the road. For now, I, we, must concentrate on the present, concentrate on the time at hand. For now, we must concentrate . . . on rest. I died’only to come back. Though I only was a shell? Just a shell? There were women. Always women. But, they were nothing. They weren’t Lily. No one could replace Lily. The fighter stands on unsure footing. I cock my head to one side. He does the same. We bow towards each other. The crowd, silent, roars in approval. I motion to the man to remove his mask to that I might see the man who almost bested me. He hesitates before removing the heavy mask. Long raven black hair falls away, revealing light toned skin and almond shaped eyes. I take a step back. The fighter was a woman. I feel a hand clamp down on my shoulder and Deacon’s voice in my ear. “Damn man, how does it feel to have your ass kicked by a girl?” he practically yells, making me wince slightly. “Technically, Deac, nobody won, it was a tie.” I looked at the woman before me. Damn, she was gorgeous. She had pulled off the bulky fighter’s uniform and donned a pair of black silk pants and a red silk Asian style wrap-around shirt. Her Americanized body was highly accented by light Japanese features. Almond shaped hazel eyes with flowing raven black hair. Her lips a reddish-pink slash across her mouth, serious but the ends twisted up in a light smile. The rounded curves of her ass pressed against the light material of the silk, stretching it slightly. Damn though, if I had to admit to being beat by a woman, this was definitely the one to be defeated by. I still hold the glass of champagne in my hand, which she begins to reach for, once again. Raw emotion takes over and I rush her, knocking the glass out of her hand, pressing my lips to hers, my hands running over her back and down to her ass, feeling her body pushed against mine. Then I realized what I was doing and whom I was doing it to. My body flew through the air like a rag doll and I bounced twice off the ground before rolling onto my back. I could hear her shoes clicking softly against the hard wood floor as she approached. One leg stepping over my fallen body, she slowly sinks down, sitting on my abdomen. “Sorry, couldn’t help myself,” I mumble softly. “Not without my permission,” she says, before leaning heavily down, her lips locking mine, her groin grinding erotically against mine. She leans up and whispers in my ear, “Yes,” while her tongue reached out, caressing my earlobe, her teeth nibbling at it. I had to know. My index and middle finger dug into her flesh, right under her jaw line. I held my breath. Closed my eyes. And counted. Seconds ticked. Slowly, my shoulders began to sag. And then, a slight pump against my fingers. My eyes snapped open. It was slight. Hell, I almost missed it. I adjusted the position of my fingers on her neck and waited. Another pump. And another.
