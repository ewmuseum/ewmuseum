A New Concept. (motm1)

23 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-23-04 07-19-04 It looked like an action movie. It was as if everything paused at the climax of destruction, and you could walk around examining the smallest detail. The raindrops froze and looked like a jail cell from the rest of existence to the two scraps of metal that were at this point forging into one. When the glass shattered, it was almost immediately followed by a crimson red liquid splurging over the entire place. This was not in the middle of nowhere by some forest grove in the absolute dead of night, mind you, it was illuminated by skyscrapers, lampposts lining the streets and the red of the stoplight. It was written across the face of all the spectators walking innocently down the road that night...whoever is in that car, is fucked. A man driving a Lincoln Navigator with two passengers ran a red light, and hit the front driver’s side fender of a man driving a rented Toyota Echo. The Echo reached a good ten feet off the ground and went crashing into a marble fountain thirty-something feet away in the front of a skyscraper business building. The Navigator flipped and scraped along its side straight into the traffic light post. All three were drunk on the way from a bar, headed towards another bar. They were young adults, seemingly trying to bring back memories of their adolescence. It was easy enough for everyone to pass off as a tragedy for all families? involved and then to move on with life. “Not many would have walked away from that day, much less be back to this hellhole a year later looking for more, you know.” I sighed, “Yeah Trey, I’ve only heard the story a million times now.” It was still an amazing story to hear, about the night he got in that car accident. But what was more intriguing, was watching it happen, being there almost eleven months ago. And unbeknownst to him, I was just one of those spectators, innocently walking down the street that day. And at the sight of collision, I knew that everyone in those two vehicles were dead, on impact. The horror on their faces as their bones shattered into millions of pieces made it that obvious. But no, though three people died, the fourth was alive. Not only alive, but aside from a few minor injuries, he was well. But it couldn’t be possible, he HAD to be dead. A part of him did die that day, anyway. This is my bond to Trey Reed. That was the day that linked us together, forever. My name is Dennis Winston, and can be proven with official court documents. What I witnessed that day was a picture that will never escape my mind. It haunts me every second I close my eyes. And it draws me closer and closer to a black abyss...at the same time, bringing me closer and closer to him. Trey looks across the street, “The Get Bus?” He sighs. “I guess I’m starting from the bottom rung once again.” He walks sulkily towards his boarding, with a duffel bag laid in each hand, trying to force himself to cheer up and regain that confident look of his. ---- 07-16-04 The first time I officially met Trey Reed, would have been towards the beginning of March, this year. He was a mess, no longer strolling about in the silk suits you’re used to seeing on television. His eyes were not the blue that you had known, but became a pale gray. His hair was always matted, and his face was never shaved clean, which made him look like he was always just waking up. He was scraggy, lost most of his physical power and most of all, a desire to continue. He was abandoned by all he knew. When he left the hospital, he was not in the shape to return to GW, so he was placed on the inactive list until his contract was to expire, then he would be released. He knew that they had turned their backs on him, and it was not the same federation he knew from months before. He found solace in wrestling the mid-west, Chicago Indy circuit. I found him in a gym, trying to come back to strength, preparing for a tournament. I gave him the impression that I was a sports agent, and he bought it. He had hit rock bottom, and decided that he would swallow his pride and take whatever help he needed to return to the top. This time, however, his goal was different from just becoming the best. He had found something in a journey, and it changed his entire outlook on life. He was determined to bring his discovery to the forefront of the media...and the best way to reach out from who he is ? wrestling. So he got on to an indy program with a small local television deal, and worked his way up to the top. He became World Champion in weeks - literally. But just as soon as he was going to deliver the secrets of his findings to all WGN viewers, FCW went bankrupt and abruptly closed for good. That’s what brought us to this discussion, what seems to be the falling out of his plans. “It’s over,” Trey said while hanging his head between his knees, hiding his frustration-red face. “There are still ways,” I tried to console him. “You’re not done just like that. FCW was only a small possibility; it’s time to move on to bigger things.” “There is nowhere else for me to go, Dennis. Alas, I am an outcast forced to wander the seas of time alone and forever forgotten.” I figure this is the time to cheer him up, so from my briefcase I procure a manila folder, and toss it onto his lap. “Your ace in the hole, Mr. Reed.” He gave me a have smirk, as if to say are you serious? “I don’t even know how you get this kind of info. GW?” “That’s right,” trying to enforce the idea in his head while at the same time moving from his awkward statement. “You have one chance to make every move happen like clockwork, Trey. It’s returning, you know. A year has passed since that last opportunity.” “You mean ? Master of the Mat?” Now, he smiled for real. “That is something that I admit would tempt anyone.” The thought of what happened at MOTM4...When Sean Price ousted Trey Reed from the tournament in the semi-finals thanks to help from Deacon Holmes and John Zero...It is one of those bad memories of the place that is constantly in the back of his mind. Slowly, his smile turns back into a state of reality. “Let us not forget that I was banished from there as well, Dennis. Not literally, but the way they handled my wreck was basically the same as a middle finger to my face. They left me to rot, and not one person stood to see me while I lay in the hospital, or returned from the physical therapy. I doubt the federation even is as it once was.” “The tides have shifted. Your friends TLM and BJP are gone, yet so is your rival Price. People like Maverick Reno run the show of evil now, and Mr. McCoy has returned to office.” His eyebrow raises, “So he’s finally back to full health” I can see how talking to him would give me more of a chance than that self-preoccupied daughter of his.” He grins, “And this is GW...so we are talking H-B-O.” “You’ll be back in the primetime.” “Well, I suppose that I can’t miss out on an opportunity like this.” ---- 07-19-04 And so he arrived at his destination. The Global Arena. A million thoughts could be running through his head at this time, mainly if he will be remember by those fans who truly adored him only a year ago. Because if you don’t have an audience, you can’t put on a spectacular show of course. And the first step of this, Plan B, has become complete.
