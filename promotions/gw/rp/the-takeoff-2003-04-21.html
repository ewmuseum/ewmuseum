The Takeoff

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21 Standing in line to board an airplane Deacon Holmes is about to confront his greatest fear. Flying. Impatient, he anxiously fidgets with the zippers on the black leather Avirex jacket worn over a white tanktop. In an attempt to keep his mind off of the tasks at hand he finds some lint to pick off of his black pants while nervously tapping his black Jordan XVII’s against the linoleum all the while remembering that the flight is only the beginning, Quartz awaits. Finally he reaches the front of the line. He removes the headphones from his ears and shows the attendant his ticket before proceeding through the tunnel. I can’t forget the bumps on the way up this hill, and I’m scared to fly but surely Deacon Holmes will. Holmes enters the plane’s hull and slowly works his way down the aisle constantly checking his ticket. He finds a his seat at the end of row 17 and slides in stowing a small leather bag under the seat ahead of him. He leans back into the headrest eyes wide open. Shit and I ain’t slept in like a month of Sundays but still I’m wide awake as we coast out on the runway. The flight attendants assemble in front of the passengers to announce the safety procedures. Holmes scans the back of the chair in front of him and pulls out the manual for evacuation. His eyes widen as the captain comes over the intercom. And now the captain says we’re number one for take off. Been trainin? all this week but I ain’t strong enough to lift this weight off. After being situated in a new seat in order to escape the exit row Holmes once again buckles up his seatbelt. Fear turns to adrenaline as the plane accelerates, I’m headed to my destiny, how long you think it takes? To get there... The plane slowly begins to ascend into the air as Holmes tightly grips his arm rest occasionally glancing at the men to his left and right in an attempt to read their expressions to see if this is supposed to be happening. The in flight movie today is Gladiator but I can’t afford the headphones, it’s cool, I’ll catch it later. One of the men recognizes Holmes from TV and strikes up a conversation after announcing who he is to the other man. I’m crammed between two dudes, one from each coast, yeah we all agree a safe flight is what we need most. Just then the plane begins to shake gently. Everyone remains calm as it doesn’t interrupt anyone’s actions. Holmes on the other hand seems ready to jump out of his skin. A flight attendant walks down the aisle with Deacon’s eyes watching her step after step. She flashes a smile on her way to the back of the plane. Hey stewardess I can’t handle all this turbulence, the ride is getting bumpy and that shit don’t help my nervousness. She returns moments later pushing the food cart up the aisle. After a few minutes Holmes looks back to find her serving the people three rows behind him. Now then fin ta serve tha meal, I think I’ll choose steak. Every choice is crucial and we all know the breaks, in this game. After finishing his meal Holmes exhales a deep breath. He seems to be feeling better as he stares out the window in a daze. I barely even notice but we’ve covered every state, plus I’m slowly turnin from feather to heavyweight. It’s about time... Holmes scans the area and begins to undo his seatbelt. He stands halfway before sitting back down. He resettles in his chair and glances at his watch. I need to take a piss but I’m still scared to get up. Please lord protect those up in this air wit us. He decides to give it another go and stand once again. He gets halfway to the bathroom before being instructed to take his seat and rebuckle his seat belt by a flight attendant. The captain comes over the intercom once again. The captain just started our approach into the sunset. I’ve flown a million miles but this shit still ain’t fun yet. The plane lowers and makes a smooth landing. As the plane rolls to a complete stop Holmes is the first one up and into the aisle. He maneuvers his way past everyone in an effort to exit the plane as soon as possible. He takes a deep breath as he makes his way down the steps and back onto solid ground. With this journey now complete all that’s left is Quartz. Tonight I meet him face to face and I’m giving him my worst. I swear on every once of blood flowing through my veins I’ll give him all I’ve got and all I’ve got to give is pain.
