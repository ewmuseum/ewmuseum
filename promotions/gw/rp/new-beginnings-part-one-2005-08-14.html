New Beginnings (Part One)

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08.14.05 The funeral was beautiful. It was held outdoors where the warmth of the sun could caress their faces as they sat in silence. Everything was white hinted with the vibrant green of the grass, from the chairs to the beautiful marble coffin laying at the front to the magnificent flowers that made everything smell of wonderment. Even Ken's suit was white, as were the outfits of most in attendance. It was the most beautiful summer's day. Many of the chairs had already been filled by family and friends. Ken, Ceci, and Gunner filed into seats in the front row, opposite Ceci's parents, with Ken in the middle. There was a perfect breeze echoing through the grass and kissing them on their cheeks. Gunner clinched Ken's hand even tighter, causing him to look into her beautiful face. She had been to too many funerals with him, and all for people she didn't really know. Todd, Stephanie, his grandmother and his grandfather, and, now, Anna. At each one, she had been his beacon of light and his source of strength. This would be no different. He couldn't help but find solace as he saw her beautiful blonde hair flowing gentley in the breeze and her gorgeaous green eyes staring back at him. His visual rested on her soft skin and her caring gaze. A tight grip on his left hand prompted him to take his eyes, flinchingly, from Gunner and to the face of one of his two best-friends in all of the world, Ceci. Her black hair was short and vibrant and her brown eyes were tear-stained, as one would have no doubt expected. She wasn't returning his glance, instead looking directly at the casket that was before them, her olive skinned face wet with sorrow. She was bighting her lower lip, to keep from sobbing, Ken was sure. Her stare took Ken's eyes to the entire reason they were there. As he looked upon her tomb, the overwhelming emotion finally took Ken over, allowing tears to stream from his eyes as a priest, the only person there dressed in black, began to speak. Ken and Anna had met only six months earlier. Ceci had been telling him about this new girl she'd been seeing, a short and spunky tomato with a warming personality and Ken finally convinced her to bring her to the apartment. He couldn't deny Ceci's judgment, as Anna had a way of finding her way into your heart instantly. It was a comfort to know that good people like that still existed in the world. He'd only wished Gunner wouldn't have been visiting family in Minnesota at the time so that she could have met her as well. Anna became commonplace in Ken's life, especially as Ceci's relationship with her began to get more and more serious, which was causing Ceci to smile more, something that Ken would trade nothing for. Gunner did eventually get the opportunity to meet her and, as all were, was immediatly drawn to the pure happiness and intense love for life that she exuded. Anna had this ability to capture everyone's attention as she walkd into a room, filling it with wonderous beams of light from her smile, and Gunner had recognized that just as easily as Ken and Ceci had. Over the course of time, as he and Anna grew closer, Ken began to notice a different person than the always cheerful one that he'd met at the beginning of February. As she allowed him into her life, he gained the benefit of knowing the Anna Nicole G that few were afforded. The deep-thinker and philosophical person that had sandy blonde hair and green eyes was more complex than one could gather at a first meeting. The struggles that she'd made it through spoke abounds for her and her will. Her mother was an alcoholic that could never let go of her bottle, no matter how hard Anna tried to help her do so. Her effort to her mother was uncanny, especially for someone of her age. At twenty years old, it was more like Anna was the mom and her mother was her child, a rebelious one at that. Through abusive relationships and menial jobs, however, she'd always put her best effort forward. Unfortunantly, the burden was added upon due to passing of her father the previous August. The one person that she'd been able to confide in and call her best-friend was taken from her at the young age of nineteen. A realization struck Ken with the suddeness of an electrical storm. Anna passed on nearly a year to the day after her father. Oddly enough, the put a smile on his face, but he couldn't figure out why. Perhaps it was that he knew that Anna would be happier now. Maybe it was because he knew that she and her father were finally able to embrace again. Moreover, it could have quite possibly been that he knew that she would always be with them in one way or another, living in their hearts and dwelling in their dreams. The friendship that they'd shared would be one that lasts eternal. The priest's words had finished, many of which Ken had paid little to no attention to simply because he found no comfort in them. The comfort that he had came from the memory of Anna. It came from those around him in Gunner and in Ceci. It came from friends such as Josh, Bill, and Ace and it came from family in his parents and his siblings. Ken's refelection upon his relationship with Anna was brought to an end as movement began to once again occur. He looked over his shoulder to notice that the seats that were empty when they sat down had been filled with those that were now getting to their feet to pay their last respects to Ceci and / or Anna. Ken noticed that the tears that had been rolling from Ceci's eyes had stopped as she clinged to his arm. He looked over at Gunner as Ceci became bombarded by a line of those wanting to hug her or tell her they were sorry for her loss. Her eyes were serene, like the ocean after a storm, as cliche as it is. Ken's face contorted back into the smile he'd fought back just minutes earlier. He knew why he was smiling. He was smiling due to all of the things that had just croassed his mind and that Anna's body may be gone and he may never get to see or speak to her again, but her spirit is still just as vibrant and just as alive as it ever was.
