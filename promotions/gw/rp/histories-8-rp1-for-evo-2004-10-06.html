Histories #8 (RP1 for Evo)

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 100604 Exiting the club, I started to walk over to my car. Wind stopped me with a hand on my shoulder. “No, let’s take mine.” I shrugged and followed him to the back of the lot, all the way back. Nestled between two trees was a jet black Dodge Magnum. At least the fucker knew how to travel with a little style. I looked over the sleek lines of the powerwagon and noticed something almost disturbing about it. “You know, this thing looks like at hearse.” Silent Wind looked at me through those black pits he called eyes, cocked his head over to one side and gave me a resounding, “And?” Smartass. “Nothing. I’m just saying that it looks like a goddamn hearse, that’s all.” “Don’t you think it’s fitting though?” He gave me a smug look, followed by a shark-ish grin. Yeah. He’s definitely smartass material. But, he’s not as bad as Deacon was. Damn, I miss that kid, wherever the hell he is. I slid into the cushy leather passenger seat. Fiddling with the controls, I created a comfortable reclined position, and closed my eyes. My nostrils picked up the faint scent of new leather. The big guy must’ve just got it. I could hear him struggling into the low car. I bet he had them remove the driver’s seat so he could sit in the backseat to drive. Cracking my left open, I turned my head and looked at the big guy. “So, are we there yet?” Silent Wind turned his head and looked at me, hitting me with one of those evil eye dead stares. He didn’t respond, only cranked the engine and slammed at massive foot down onto the gas pedal, letting the oversized Hemi-engine do its thing, throwing us both back in our respective seats, peeling out of the parking lot in a mixture of dust and rubber particles. “I say, goddamn.” Silent Wind could only laugh. “Great, isn’t it?” “So” Where we going?” I asked. “Not far, just down the street actually. We should be there in a few minutes.” “Okay,” I replied, looking out the window. “So, what’s down the street?” “A building,” he chuckled. Asshole. “Shall we continue playing this game or can you just be patient and wait?” “Just wanna know what I’m walking into, that’s all.” “You know, trust is a beautiful thing.” My turn to laugh. “That’s asking a lot. Especially coming from a contract killer who’s loyalty can be bought with a suitcase full of green.” “That hurts John. That really hurts.” He started to slow the Magnum down a bit before jerking the wheel hard to the left, nearly spinning the vehicle around and stopping in another dirt parking lot. “Besides, we’re here.” We exited the vehicle simultaneously, both of us staring at the seemingly harmless looking old warehouse of a building. Hell, the thing could be abandoned. But then, if it were abandoned, all of the cars parked out in front of it would be out of place. “So, what is it exactly that I’m looking at.” “That would be a brothel. Don’t you think its interesting that brothels always seem to spring up near stripclubs?” “Yes. That is rather interesting.” I was eyeballing the building though. From this distance I couldn’t see a lot of security measures. “And the plan?” “We go in, leave a present, and leave with as few people noticing us as possible.” “A present?” “Yes. A present. A bomb, to be more precise.” “And the people inside doing their business?” “Marks. The whole lot.” “All of them?” “Yeah,” he said, circling around to the back of the Magnum, me following behind him. “Security isn’t very tight. One guard, two tops at the door. A third wandering the halls. Besides that, just the occasional lost john trying to find something to bury his dick in.” At the back of the vehicle, he shrugged out of the red and black plaid jacket he was wearing as well as the t-shirt, pulling a black long sleeved shirt over his head. The material was conforming, hugging closely to his body. He then pulled a shoulder holster on, and added a H&K to it, chambering a round and making sure that the silencer was snuggly in place. “You got some stuff in there that I can borrow, big guy?” He looked up from the back of the Magnum, staring at me with disbelief. “You mean you didn’t come carrying?” “Not guns, no.” He gestured to the back of the car, in a help yourself manner, while slowly shaking his head, muttering, “And you call yourself a professional. They say I’m crazy.” Inside the back of the powerwagon, I found myself a nice shoulder harness that held two guns, one under each arm. I slid two silenced H&Ks into the open space. Personally, I’m a glock man myself, but hey, you gotta roll with the punches sometimes. A few spare clips went into my jacket pockets. I also managed to grab a set of goggles that could switch between night vision and infrared. Silent Wind took a backpack out of the car and put it carefully onto his back, before looking at me. “Ready?” “Forgetting something?” “What?” I tapped the side of my face. “Masks?” “Oh’yeah.” He reached up to his neck, his fingers digging deep within the folds of his flesh before jerking his arm up and back, peeling the skin away from his face. The rubber mask came off and was tossed into the back of the wagon. Underneath the fake face? The infamous mask of death. “Ready?” I sighed. “Got a spare?” He rummaged around for a second before coming back up with a silky black mask. “Sorry. You don’t get one with the red lines.” “I’m not worried about it.” I pulled the mask on. We crossed the parking lot with no difficulties, stopping to scan for any additional guards or arriving patrons. Nothing. Not even a single camera. Odd. Before the last stretch to the front door, I donned the goggles and switched it to infrared mode. Through the front of the building, I could see a multitude of things. Several large shapeless masses with limbs jutting this way and that’things that I could only assume were group sex sessions going immensely well. But, those things didn’t really concern me. What did concern me were the two red human shaped forms glowing near the front door. The doorman and a friend, perhaps. Then one of the shapes turned around and exited down a hall. That just left the doorman. I looked over at Silent Wind, who was crouched next to a car, looking at me expectantly. I held up a hand and motioned that we should go forward. I ran across the space between the lot and the building, staying low and fast. Easing up against the left side of the door, I watched the guard sit on the other side of the door. I pulled one of the pens out and popped the cap off. I could feel Silent Wind eyeing me and the pen in my hands. Knocking on the door, I watched at the human form stood up and went to the door, the head leaning in to examine the peep hole. Jumping out in front of the door, I thrust the pen forward, the sharp point breaking through the glass of the peephole and ramming into the doorman’s eye. With the infrared vision, I could see his body shaking and convulsing, his body pressed against the door. Silent Wind sided up next to me. He extracted a set of lock picks and went to work on the door. A few seconds later, it swung open, the body still attached. Giving a quick tug, we laid him down on the ground next to the building. We entered the building. Inside, I could hear the echoes of sex all around. The moans. The groans. The slaps. The smacks. The yes’s. The more’s. It took me back. All those years ago. All those lifetimes ago. The phone was ringing. It had gotten dark. The hours just seem to slip away if you are not careful. Becky stirred a little bit when I picked up the receiver, but stayed asleep. “What?” I said into the phone. “They’re on the way up.” It was Hector. “Three B. The one next to yeh.” The line went dead. About that time I heard the door open and close along with the giggled whispers of one of the ladies. The man grunted something that I couldn’t understand. Thanks mainly to the thinness of the walls, I could hear just about every moan and groan in the other room. “Oh, baby, just like that.” “Harder.” “Faster.” He grunts. She moans. It was like watching porn except there was no picture. They kept going at it. I started to zone out and would’ve missed my cue, if it weren’t for her. “Don’t do that,” she said. And I heard it. The unmistakable sound of flesh striking flesh. And again. She screamed. I shook my head, trying to keep the images out, forcing them out. The thumps of footsteps to the left. I turned, my right hand already drawing out a pistol. Silent Wind was already ahead of me, his gun out and firing, dropping the john and his sex partner with four well placed shots. “You’re getting slow, John.” “Yeah, yeah, whatever.” Another ten or so yards in we stopped. Wind was about to unshoulder the backpack when the sounds of multiple guns being cocked “That’s far enough, gentlemen.”
