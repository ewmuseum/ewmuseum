Purple - Business or pleasure? (Sub Saga Pt. 2)

6 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.06.04 'Feelings are not supposed to be logical. Dangerous is the man who has rationalized his emotions.' -David Borenstein In the lives of many men, they'd reach, strive, and even grasp for greatness, barely able to even feel its presence. For Maverick Reno, it was a daily task to stand, bump his head on that big brass ring, shake if off and push it aside for he, standing amongst few, reaches far past that ring and towards a ring that again few have ever deemed imaginable. Reno is silent as they sit, enjoying the dinner placed before them. The room is very atmospheric; a lattice hangs overhead with greenery mingling in and out of the criss-crossing wood. The walls are white but covered in the same lattice, lacking the greenery. Small cafe tables are scattered about the room. A swinging door is located against one wall. A bar comes out in the back of the room, creating another area for people to sit. Above the bar is a television which is currently off. There's an awkward silence between the two men, one that seems to have been in place for quite some time. Finally, Reno sighs and begins to speak, his eyes searching Ryan's composure for his attention. 'We speak on a few conditions, Ryan.' Maverick's words are calm and collected. Ryan nods and sits back in his chair, quickly wiping his lips after the meal. 'Whatever you say. What are they?' 'You do not record this, you do not make notes of this in your files, you do not speak of this to anyone outside of this room. If this happens, I will be forced to take preventative measures.' To say that Reno's words are collected and well thought out would be quite the understatement. He reaches towards the bar and grabs his jacket and from inside, pulls both a white and a black rose. He places them each on the table, the white rose just a bit closer to Ryan. 'Deal?' Ryan nods, seeing no problem with these arrangements although, finding them peculiar. 'Deal. Maybe we shoul...' 'Amendment to the conditions, Ryan. I'll tell you what I want; no offense, but there's nothing you can throw at me that we both don't know. I just need to let off a bit of steam.' Precaution once again takes a step forward as Maverick adds another ground rule for this conversation. Ryan nods and sits back in his chair, he glances up suddenly and smiles a thank you as the waiter comes and cleans away the plates. Reno motions for him quickly and the man pauses. 'After this, lock up the room. It's going to be off limits for a few hours.' The waiter nods his understanding and takes the mess from them, leaving their glasses full. Reno speaks softly, his eyes falling to the table for a moment. 'Do you remember Scott Pierce?' There's a moment of pause as Ryan glances up, searching. 'I... Yes, I do infact.' Reno nods and lifts his glass, his eyes focusing on the ripples of the Chardonnay as he flails it gently about in his hand. 'He and I remained good friends... also got into business together.' Ryan nods as Maverick continues, speaking cautiously. 'He helped Stephen, you remember Stephen?' Ryan nods. 'He helped Stephen and I build Reno Enterprise. Then after all of this, he joined with me when I entered the sport.' Again Ryan nods, leaning in slightly. Reno sighs and continues. 'That's when I met Anthony Stecchino. From the moment we met backstage, he proved to be a veritable thorn in my side. And vice versa, might I add. The company decided that they'd pit us against one another because of our combative personalities but what came out of it was much less business oriented and much more personal.' Reno's story begins to flow now as if he read from a book instead of stringing it together from memory. 'It began over his treatment of a young boy, Matt Brown. He assaulted the boy and put him in a hospital. I went after him for this and ever since, we've been head to head. A year and a half ago, Anthony stooped to a new low. He and John Russell began working together to make my life a living hell. They started well, striking in obvious places. I built up my defenses however and soon, I became an impenetrable fortress. Neither of them could stand against me... so they struck at Scott.' Ryan's brow wrinkles, watching as Reno tells his tail. 'They found Scott. They killed his entire family, Ryan. They may have lost here, standing against me. But when they struck at him, they got something more than a physical victory over me. They... won my sanity, for lack of a better way of putting it.' Ryan curiously looks at Maverick, not sure what to say. 'When it was all over, Stecchino fell. John had fallen before him. I remained, standing as the winner. But my victory came at great cost; I had lost much of my concern for my actions. My moral ground had been shaken and the foundation weakened. By pure will I had kept my eyes on my goal and succeeded. Slowly, my life would reshape itself. I ... it did not last long.' Ryan speaks up finally, interrupting Maverick. 'You speak as if this haunts you. You have not sunk so low as they, Mav. You acted in defense.' Reno ignores him and continues, speaking from the prompter in his mind. 'Salaok made me his personal target; for a length of time, he and I battled with one another regarding my position in the company, what I earned in the company and so on and so forth. Eventually, Salaok found a way to rid himself of the thorn I had become. I had to fly in for a show... but Salaok had the flight detoured. I obviously couldn't make the show, everything was taken care of. I had no way to make it... as was his plan. He fired me for this. I had been outdone, out witted.' Ryan simply nods now as Maverick speaks, the story coming easier than before with much less emotion. It's as if Reno is reading from an article in a magazine. 'I fell into an abyss. I had my company, my lovely fiancee Melissa. But it didn't matter; it ate at me, it got to me. I had been beaten. I was not the best... ' Life is not lost by dying; life is lost minute by minute, day by dragging day, in all the 1000 small uncaring ways. -Stephen Vincent Benet 'When this happened, I lost interest in everything. Nothing mattered to me anymore. Melissa grew tired of it and before too long, she left me. I digressed even more. Before Spring could even begun, my love had withered and died by winters harsh breath. No sun nor any of its warmth could give birth to what I had lost. This to, I blamed on Salaok, his nevermore, and the Global Corporation.' Slowly but surely, emotion began to fill Maverick's words. 'Bubba's failure soon became a personal insult; failure after failure, month after month. It was nothing but a series of slaps in the face. I had lost every except for my business.' Ryan leans back in his chair and nods again, surveying Maverick. 'So you blamed Salaok at first and this progressed to Global and thus, its owners? It's a somewhat logical strand of thought, I must give you that.' 'It did not end there, Ryan. My pride stepped in, it would not allow my failure to be the final milestone of my life. It would not let me end my career that way.' Reno pauses, his forehead finding his open hand as his elbow rests upon the table. His eyes lift again suddenly as he straightens his posture, his story now continuing. 'For this I was pleased but to what end would I go? I fear that my mind is a veritable wasteland of hatred and unforgiving scorn. I fear myself yet dread not what I do, nor am I sympathetic for what I have done.' 'Wha..what have you done, Maverick?' Ryan's question goes unanswered now as Maverick picks up his story. 'I made a few calls. I called Tyler, Scott's brother. I needed a bit more financial backing and I knew he was looking for a risk to dive into; he needed it. I called Sean Price, a man I had worked with briefly in the company... wealthy and willing to get the job done. Something I needed. Next, we contacted someone new to the company. Talented young guy although, not really my style and at first, got under my skin. It was worth it though because he's become one of the cornerstones of this great institution. Deacon Holmes. Others entered as background assistance, including Naria and the de Sarret's. Finally a fifth entered, John Zero. A professional to say the least... he's proven himself more than once, let me assure you. Infact, without John, Deacon, Tyler, and Sean, The Echelon would not exist. The five of us are the original cornerstones. Numerous others would stand at our side. Jason Hughes is the only one of these to reach such high rankings as those I've mentioned before. He's the sixth cornerstone...' Ryan nods and speaks again, using the pause that Maverick provides. 'So John, Deacon, Sean, Tyler and Jason were assembled... and together you built Echelon Incorporated. What did you need these men for? What did you need The Echelon for?' Again, Ryan's question hangs in the air. Maverick continues, his words circling through the air now. 'I know that I will not stop now, that we can't be stopped. But I wonder, Ryan. Have I taken a pure form of justice and turned it into a plaguing death?' The words hang silently in the air for a moment, no one speaking. Finally Reno sighs and leans back in his chair. 'But what really is bothering me is Scott. I feel as if ... as if it's my fault. My original fight was pure and then he was hurt; if I had simply not confronted Anthony and John, would they still have assaulted his family? In essence, I am to blame for their death.' 'It sounds to me like you want to pass the blame but you don't know how to do so. I would suggest that you look back at how you felt when it happened and compare that to how you feel now. Figure out which one is truthful and, if you did not feel guilty then, why now?' Ryan's words are intelligent, his methods careful and secure. 'What I want you to do, Maverick, is take time away from the company. Give your self time to reevaluate everything.' These last comments had sank in but had not done so well. Reno looks up slowly, his eyes focusing on his companion. 'Take time away? Take time away!? You have to be joking me. I don't have time to take, Ryan. I only have time to do everything that I possibly can, everything to make sure that my life is as good as it can be. Look around you, I did not gather all of this beauty that surrounds you by taking a break.' Ryan inches back just slightly, the abrasive attitude from Maverick shocking him. 'Maverick, I didn't mean for it insult you. I'm only looking out for your well being; that's why I'm here.' Ryan had slipped up. 'That's why you're here? Would you care to explain yourself, Ryan?' With this, Reno stands. His eyes watch Ryan carefully as his hands lock together at the low of his back, suddenly abandoning his frail and emotionally wrecked frame and patronizing a stern, incriminating, and powerful shell. 'You didn't just show up to see an old friend, did you?' 'Maverick,'Ryan stands and pushes his chair away, attempting to open himself up with body language as he speaks. 'you have to understand that she called me because she was concerned for you.' 'Leave, Ryan. Before I choose otherwise.' At first, Ryan pauses not sure how to react. Reno watches him, not batting an eye. His blue eyes look to have been covered in winters frost, his frame stiffened by time and his demeanor, that of death. A sickle in his right hand would complete the image of death personified. Ryan still stands there, not sure of what to do and, as if in response to his stupor, Reno pulls a glock from under a nearby table and aims it at Ryan's forehead. 'I'm changing my mind with every moment you wait.' That too, could complete an image of death. Ryan steps back quickly, tripping and falling to the ground over the chair where he once sat. 'Ma..' Reno stops him, reaching up and jerking on the glock's slide, being sure that a round is in the chamber. He aims down at Ryan then pulls the gun away. A new weapon rises from Maverick's side, a small flip phone. 'Miss Xiou, have Tobias and Aiden come up. They need to escort Mr. Purgason from the building.' There's a pause with Reno's demand and then a quick nod. 'Also, schedule yourself an appointment tomorrow Miss Xiou. We should speak of today’s events.' Evil always turns up in this world through some genius or other. ?Denis Diderot With this, guards entered the room and soon after, Tobias and Aiden de Sarret. They approach Ryan quickly and each grab an arm, hoist him up and drag him from the room even though he's willing to go. Reno grabs his silk dress coat and throws it on, now pacing about the small bistro. The doors close following the exit of the small fleet of guards that accompany the de Sarret brothers. Reno's anger bursts suddenly as he grabs a small table and hurls it up and over. The table flies up into the air and then back down, slamming into the ground with a fevered thud. Another table is kicked to the side by Reno as he stalks around. 'I can't fucking believe them. A snake in my own fucking nest. I trusted you, Naria.' Reno's words are not exactly eloquent or chilling alone, his tone however is the second. The tone to which Reno growls out the previous statements would send chills up the back of any man who heard him. Again he reaches into his coat and flips out his cell phone, making a quick call. 'Aiden. I'm sure he's still in view so have him followed; he's a viable leak now. If you see anything suspicious or dangerous to us, have him picked up and brought back here.' There's a pause now as Aiden speaks on the other end. Reno grimaces and nods. 'If you have to have him picked up, Aiden. Try to make it look like a sloppy kidnapping.' Reno snaps the phone shut and grabs the glock from a nearby table, still standing after his ravenous outpouring. 'It always comes down to a choice. Business or pleasure. When those with whom you seek pleasure turn it into business, then there is no choice. When those for whom you deal with in business make it pleasurable, then there is no choice. Business and pleasure are like oil and water, they never fully mix no matter how long they are paired. Today, they were abruptly strewn together. I hope that tomorrow does not bring another mixture together for sadly, blood and water do mix... they mix quite well.' I'm Coming For You
