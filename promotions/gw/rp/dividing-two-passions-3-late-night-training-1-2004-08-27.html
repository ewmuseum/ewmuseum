Dividing Two Passions #3: Late Night Training #1

27 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 27th 2004 “Constantino! Wake up!” The whispered words of Igor Arefiev sound through the door of Constantino Zergolov’s room. “Constantino”!” There is no reply. Igor is bent down looking through the door keyhole. “Constantino”!” Again there is no reply. Arefiev peers through the keyhole at pitch black. He groans, and wipes some sleep from his eyes. In the distance, the grandfather clock in the main foyer strikes three o’clock. Igor yawns. He is wearing a dark Vergomov tracksuit and a baseball cap. “Constantino”!” he exclaims again. The silence extends as again Igor is left without a reply again. Again Arefiev groans. “CONSTANTINO!” Igor raises his voice and sounds a little more agitated. There is no reply. Igor reaches up and carefully twists the handle of the door. Eventually the door clicks and Arefiev pushes the door open. He is weary of any creaking as he looks over his shoulder back into the hall and then steps into the darkness of his tag team partner’s room. “Zergolov”! Get up!” growls Igor, with a much more normal tone. He steps towards the bed, which is marked out by the moonlight coming from the window off to his right. In the bed lies a body, its shape clearly marked out by the sheets. A groan comes from the body covered by the bed sheets. “Constantino! We have training!” motions Igor as he pulls the sheets off the bed. Zergolov groans and fights for the sheets. He rolls over to face his partner, with his eyes still closed. The pair fight for the sheets for about twenty seconds with Igor finally managing to pull them away. “What do you want?” moans Zergolov, slightly opening his eyes and looking at the silhouette of Arefiev. “We have late night training!” exclaims Arefiev, “Now come on!” “Why bother?” snaps Zergolov. “WHY BOTHER”!” snaps Arefiev, “We have a wrestling career to think of, that’s why we should bother?” “You have a career to think about,” replies Zergolov, “not me.” Arefiev seems confused and looks down at his partner. “What?” enquiries Igor, “Why do you not have a career?” Zergolov opens his eyes fully. He stares at Arefiev, then rolls away, not replying. “Constantino” What did that comment mean?” continues Igor. “You should know!” mumbles Constantino. Igor puts his hands on his hips and peers down at Igor. “You’re the only one that is getting anywhere,” remarks Zergolov. “What?” snaps Igor. “Master of the Mat” I wasn’t entered in it,” explains Constantino, “The Whiplash gauntlet” I’m not entered in it? Do you think they realise there is two people in this team?” “Don’t be so ridiculous!” returns Igor, almost immediately. Zergolov sighs. “Then why are you getting all the singles opportunities?” bites Zergolov. “I’m not?” “Yeah, you are,” remarks Zergolov, “I only fought one singles match against El Serpenti!” “Which you lost!” smiles Arefiev. Constantino rolls over and throws his tag team partner an icy glare. “What?” grins Arefiev, “You did! You lost! Maybe that’s why!” Zergolov’s icy glare is unrelenting. “I have only been given one opportunity to showcase my skills! You have been given numerous openings!” “What” Two?” asks Arefiev. “It’s more than that!” rejoinders Zergolov. Arefiev shakes his head. “Nope,” exclaims Arefiev, “Master of the Mat was my first ever appearance by myself and then I fought Juri.” “Yeah a girl!” snaps Zergolov, clutching at thin air for a comeback. Arefiev looks puzzled at his partner. “You’ll get your chance, I know you will. And if you want, when I win, I’ll give you the first shot!” Zergolov kinda smiles. “So” Are we going training?” suggests Arefiev. “Nope!” exclaims Zergolov. “Why not?” asks Igor. “I have two weeks off. Why should I bother?” smiles Zergolov. Igor sighs, and not wanting another little spat with his tag team partner. He turns on his heel and leaves the room. He pulls the door gently behind him. “I can’t believe he is being like this?” Igor is whispering to himself as he steps through the empty halls of the Arefiev Mansion. “So what” I got a shot at the cruiserweight title. It’s not like I’ll make it! It’s not like I’ll be crowned a Champion. I’m the first person in the ring. Why isn’t he supporting me? He is being a big baby! Aarghhh! He makes me so mad some times! They gave me a shot at the Cruiserweight title! It’s an eight person gauntlet! I’m going to have beat six other wrestlers to have a go at a ladder match for the Cruiserweight Championship? And he cries that he isn’t in the match! Aarghhh! I won’t make it! Will I? I did manage something like it at Master of the Mat? And it was against heavyweights. But this is for a title. There is more at risk. It is wanted more. Hell, Tyler Pierce and Trey Reed will want to get back to winning ways. They will want it more! But I want it! Why am I doubting myself? Because I feel like I don’t deserve to be in the match. I feel like I’m only in it as one half of that tag team! I’m making up numbers! No I’m not! He really does get in my head! Aarghhh! Shit!” Igor reaches the end of the hall and is standing in the centre of a corridor that goes to his left and to his right. “Which way did I come up?” he mutters, as he scratches his head. “Shit. Shit. Shit.” He exclaims and looks around. BANG! Suddenly a gun shot fills the air and a loud thud on the stony floor echoes around the corridor.
