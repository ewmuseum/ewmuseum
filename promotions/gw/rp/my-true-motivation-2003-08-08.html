My true motivation...

8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/8/03 **A few days removed from completing my first job for the Salamone family and only a matter of hours removed from committing my first cold blood murder with Deacon Holmes things are running wild in my head. I am doing things now that I never thought I was capable of. Murder’cold blood murder’crime without reason’killing just for the thrill. These are the words of a sadist not me or are they?** (As the scene fades in we see the main gate to South Beach Cemetery. The cemetery consists of at least 20 acres of nothing but tomb stones as far as the eye can see. Towards the back of the cemetery are little stone buildings that also contain coffins. The sun is shining and the flowers are in full bloom making a place filled with death almost seem nice. Suddenly Sean Price’s hummer pulls into the cemetery and drives down a dirt path for about 100 yards before turning into the cemetery parking lot. Sean then gets out of his hummer with an arrangement of flowers in his hand. He walks through the little church that is setup in the direct center of the cemetery. He kneels down besides one of the pews and makes the signal of the holy cross with his right hand. Once inside the pew he kneels down again, places the flowers carefully on the pew and then takes a moment to pray. After Sean is done praying he lifts up his head to reveal tears streaming down his face. He picks up his flowers and then heads back out the church and down a few dirt paths before stopping at a tombstone that reads Stacy Marie Harden. Beloved daughter, sister and fianc’e. Born July 5th 1977, Died August 1st 1999. Sean drops to his knees infront of the tombstone and begins crying. In the midst of doing so he neatly places the floral arrangement against the tomb stone.) “My dear Stacy’it has been 4 years since you have died, and in 4 years what have I become?” (Sean leans in alittle closer to the tombstone, he kisses his hand and then presses it up against the tombstone signifying a kiss to his long since dead fianc’e.) “When we were together Stacy I was the happiest I have ever been. Everything was going perfect for me. My grades in school were good, I was having an incredible career in football at Princeton, and I was in love with the girl that I thought I would spend the rest of my life with. But then that all changed in the blink of an eye.” “Four years ago we were a happy couple bound for great things. You were intelligent and beautiful’intelligent’or so I thought. Me, I was a star football player bound for the NFL. I was a nice guy, kind and caring, the kind of guy that you deserved, the kind of guy you loved.” “And now”.what am I? I’m not kind’I am not caring. I am the guy that goes around killing people just because I am asked too. Most of the time I don’t even know the person whose life I take, I just do it and I don’t look back.” “My how much I have changed these past 4 years, for the good or for the bad” I don’t really know, only god can decide that I guess, for he is the one true judge.” “But you know the more I look back on it, the more I realize that who I am now, is no different than who I was before. The difference is that while we were together I was living an illusion, a life of happiness and pride. Happy to have you, happy to have my career, happy to be the guy that would make all your dreams come true, but at the same time I was masking my dreams, and never giving them a chance to be accomplished.” “Then you went along and did the best thing that you ever did for me’you killed yourself. You ended your life and in the process you ended mine. The little goodness that I had in my died along with you that day. But I suppose you didn’t think about that when you jumped out the window head and smashed onto the concrete killing yourself instantly did you” You didn’t think about me, and what I might go through’no, you only thought of your self.” “You know, now a days I hear the word selfish a lot when describing me. And to be honest with you, I have to agree with it. I am selfish, selfish because the day you died I stopped caring about others, I stopped worrying about putting others ahead of myself. No, on that dark day I began a life that has lead me to where I am now. I think only of myself, I do only for me and no one else. Yes I am selfish, but I learned from the best’you!” “I am glad you killed yourself, I really am. Because with your death came my birth. The man I am today is the man I always was at heart when you died I got the chance to let out everything inside me that had been hidden for so long. When you were alive I did what was best for you. I did everything you wanted me too. I was the perfect boyfriend, sure you paid me back in your own ways but your never really went the extra mile for me. But that was ok, because I was living in a world that was almost too good to be true, but I saw past any possible flaws, all I saw was the typical All American Dream. A house with a white picket fence, two kids playing in the yard, and a long life of happiness ahead of me, that is what I saw.” “But not any more, Stacy you were my true motivation, everything I did and thought I did because of you. But now, everything I do and think is to forget you and distance myself from you and the memories we share. I came here today to let you know that when I leave those gates I won’t be coming back. Not tomorrow, not next week, not ever. The day you died is the day we ended, but today is the day we officially part. Im glad your out of my life, because now I can move on with my own.” (With that Sean stands up and takes one last look at Stacy’s grave. He picks up the arrangement of flowers and snaps them all in half, he then throws them on top of the grave site and walks away without looking back.)
