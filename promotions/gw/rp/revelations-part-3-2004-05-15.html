Revelations Part 3

15 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 15 “Final boarding call for Flight 275 nonstop Dallas, Texas to Tokyo, Japan,” the voice boomed over the airport’s PA system. Ichiro and Jushin were already standing in line and handing their tickets to the air port security officers and flight attendants as they heard the announcement. They smiled as the flight attendant nodded them through. She handed them the end of their flight tickets and then greeted the next people preparing to board the plane. Ichiro and Jushin made their way through the tunnel and into the plane. The plane was quite cramped as most usually are before take up. People putting their stuff in the overhead compartments, children wining about seating, the businessman complaining to the flight attendant because his business wouldn’t fly him first class and the shuffle of people trying to just find their seats. The two brothers looked at one another and sighed as they walked towards the first class section and found their seats quickly. Twenty minutes later they were in the air, and on their way home. There was nothing left to do, but wait. Jushin was busying himself by reading a recent copy of GW Magazine which featured “American Youth” Josh on the cover. Ichiro however, was sitting with his eyes closed, seemingly in deep thought. Finally, Jushin turned to his brother; he had to ask the question, it had been on his mind for a long time now. “Ichiro, why the hell are we going back here again so soon” You know we didn’t figure much out for Gohei, and what we did find for him, he probably hasn’t found out any more information about anyways!” Jushin questioned. Ichiro opened his eyes, he did not want to get into this conversation with his brother on the plane. But, it did not look like Jushin was going to let it wait any longer. Ichiro turned to his brother, and kept his voice down, so as to not let anyone else in the area hear is words. They were flying first class so there was a lot of room in between seats, and it was not hard for anyone to avoid hearing them. “Look, there is something I found before we left. I didn’t tell you, and I did not tell Uncle Gohei?” Ichiro started. “What!” Why wouldn’t you tell Gohei, more than that, why the hell wouldn’t you tell me?!” demanded Jushin. “Shhh, calm yourself brother,” Ichiro started. “There is no need to be so loud. I did not tell you because, at first I did not think it important. But, now I can’t seem to get it out of my head, and it is driving me mad. I need to go back and find out more information, but Gohei cannot know my true purpose. All he needs to be concerned about is that his two nephews are back in Tokyo for a few days.” “Well, what is it that you saw” What is it that has been on your mind this past week? What is it that makes you want to return to the turmoil and danger that currently resides in Tokyo?” furthered Jushin. “I can see that you are not giving up easily my brother,” Ichiro said. “If you must know, it was a tattoo on the necks of one of the Americans that attacked us in the subway last week in Tokyo.” “A tattoo, you’re flying over fifteen hours, and across the world, because of a tattoo?” Jushin said with a hint of snickering in his voice. “It was not that he had a tattoo, it was what the tattoo was. It seems to have some sparking resemblance to something from my memories. I do not know why, but I feel as if I have seen it before,” Ichiro answered. “Listen, Ichiro. This is not like you at all,” Jushin started. “I have never seen you act upon something unless you were sure, unless you knew deep down in your gut what you were doing. And now, you act so foolishly, so childishly and return to danger over something so small and insignificant!”” Ichiro did not respond, he took his eyes away from his little brother, he could not stand to see the confused look on his face any longer. Ichiro knew that his brother was right, he knew that this was completely out of his character. But, he could not explain why he was doing it, it just seemed like he needed to. He knew that he had seen that tattoo somewhere before, he was positive of that. But, he could not explain to his brother why he needed to find out so badly. Ichiro, needed to know, because he needed to know if they had done the right thing when they took out Ishuzu’s empire. In a crime of anger, vengeance and passion for those who had fallen, he, his brother and his uncle had single-handedly crushed Ishuzu’s entire empire. They had done what entire clans had failed to do, in a few hours they had destroyed everything that Ishuzu had ever built. But, he could not tell Jushin that this was the reason he was going back. Jushin would think of him as weak, maybe even pathetic. And Ichiro could not take that, he could not live without his brother’s respect and love that was one thing that he could not do without. And so, he needed to come up with something to say, something to ease his brother’s mind. “You do not speak to me, brother. Will you not any longer?” asked Jushin. “I am sorry, my silence was not meant to offend,” replied Ichiro. “Well, now that you have your voice back. What exactly was it that you saw” What was this tattoo?” furthered Jushin. “I, I cannot say for certain. For I would by lying if I knew exactly what it was,” answered Ichiro again. “You do not even know why we are going”!” demanded Jushin. “I do know why we are going brother, at least I know why I am going. I am going because the Lotus is telling me that I need to. I have meditated on the subject many times and it has told me my path, so that I must follow. Why you are going, I am still unsure!” exclaimed Ichiro. Jushin was taken back by this; it had been many, many years since he had last seen his brother go off like that. Ichiro usually was the calm and collected of the two. Ichiro’s recent behavior was more suited towards Jushin’s attitude and not that of his own. “I am sorry brother, I did not mean to anger you. I go because you go, because I will follow you and protect you as you would follow and protect me if I needed it. But, I only wish to know why, what is the cause for the risks that we are now undertaking by returning to Tokyo,” came Jushin’s subtle reply. “The tattoo, it was some form of crest or shield of some sort. But, I feel as if I know it. As if I have seen it somewhere before, but my memory fails and I have only seen half of it, so far I have not been able to figure out the mystery,” Ichiro informed his brother. Jushin looked puzzled as he replied to his brother. “Ichiro, I will not lie, I do not believe why you are going back to Tokyo. But I do believe in you, and I will follow you, and be with you as you need.” “Thank you, my brother,” answered Ichiro with a smile. He took his hand and placed it onto his brother’s. Jushin smiled back, but quickly went back to reading his magazine. However, Ichiro merely closed his eyes. He was weary from arguments and thought right now, he needed to rest before they arrived in Tokyo. It was his only chance, if he wanted to complete what he set out to do when he arrived. Half an hour later, a flight attendant walked by with the drink cart. She pushed her way through the aisle’s of the aircraft asking everyone if they required anything to drink. When she reached Ichiro and Jushin she saw that both were sleeping, and decided to push on without waking them. However, something stopped the cart’s progress, something was on the ground and it was stopping the cart from going forward. The attendant bent down and picked up the small magazine that was in her way. She looked at the magazine confused. The page showed some sort of ad for a wrestling show. The poster talked about a show called “GW Overload” and from what she saw, this week’s episode was featuring an interview with TUE’s Maverick Reno. Whatever, the hell that meant. She looked into the background behind, the picture of the man, who she assumed was Maverick Reno. It was a beautiful insignia, a beautiful looking silver coat of arms. It was beautiful and majestic, it reminded her of one the crest’s of a knight. She closed the magazine and placed it back onto Jushin’s lap and moved forward down the aisle once again.
