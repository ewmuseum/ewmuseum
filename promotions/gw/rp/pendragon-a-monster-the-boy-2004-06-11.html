Pendragon a Monster? The Boy....

11 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-11-2004 “He who fights with monsters might take care lest he thereby become a monster. And if you gaze for long into an abyss, the abyss gazes also into you.” -Friedrich Nietzsche Darkness filled the hero’s mind, and Pendragon wandered the streets aimlessly. Through the back alleys and dark places he trudged, heedless of where fate might eventually lead him. Confused and bewildered, fighting unseen enemies in his own head, he walks. Past the homeless; past the troubled youth who take to the streets when home life becomes too much. Each visage is emblazoned on his mind. And it has come about all because of two men: Akuma and Freeze. And the boy, lest we too soon forget the atrocities committed upon the boy? **********FLASH************ The sign outside the hospital reads EMERGENCY. Soon, a wailing ambulance races into the lot and stops in front of this particular entrance. The doors fly open and two paramedics hop down from the truck, followed by Venneman Pendragon. He stands and fidgets nervously as the two men try to pull the gurney off of the ambulance. The wheels stay locked, and the thing won’t budge?” “NOW!!! FOR THY FATHER’S SAKE!! GET HIM OFF NOW!!” roars Pendragon, barely able to contain himself, barely keeping himself from falling into a full blown rage? The roar startles the paras, and one of them accidentally gets the lock done and the wheels finally turn. They pull the gurney from the ambulance and we see that it holds the boy that Freeze tried to munch on during the last Evolution. He is pale, and a bloodstained bandage wraps his neck. The two men rush him into the hospital with Pendragon hot on their heels. “Get Dr. Soames’NOW!” Pendragon looks on surprisingly as one of the para’s voices mimics his own. The nurse who received the shout hurries off, but soon returns with a doctor in a white coat. He shines a small light into the boy’s eyes, first the right, and then the left. He checks the pulse and shakes his head’Pendragon could take the silence no more? “Will he pull through” Dost thou believe him to be in any danger?” “I need to get him to the O.R.?Then, we’ll see. The boy has lost a lot of blood, and he needs a transfusion. Has the boy’s mother been contacted?” The doctor’s stern tone is directed at the nurse, who again hurries off, before stopping and turning around? “But I don’t even know his name’Let alone his mother’s!” Pendragon reaches into a concealed pocket in his shirt and pulls out a tattered piece of parchment. He hands it to the nurse. She looks at it and nods, once more running off. The doctor turns to Pendragon as two orderlies show up to take the boy into the operating room? “I’m afraid you have to stay here, lad?” Pendragon’s eyes grow wide, but he nods?.“I do expect to know as soon as I might see him’The boy is’Special to me?” “It will be so?” The doctor turned away and Pendragon’s great shoulders sagged noticeably. Looking around, he finally spots a waiting area and strides over. He plops down in a chair and drops his face into his hands? “The boy’It was all about the boy. Seeing Freeze sucking his blood was bad’The ride to the hospital was worse’But there was something even worse waiting to leap upon me?” At last, after what seems like an eternity to Pendragon, the doctor returns. His eyes are bright, and he places a hand on Pendragon’s shoulder. Pendragon jerks into alertness and stares at the doctor, who stands smiling above him? “He will pull through. He is more rattled than anything else. We got some fresh blood into him and he seems to have gotten over the shock. I’d say that he is going to need some counseling. And that wound! What kind of blade made that cut” It took a miracle to stop the flow of the blood from that slice!” Pendragon rises to his feet and claps the doctor on the shoulder?“Aye??tis a most noble blade, but that is unimportant. Thou hast saved him, and in doing so, have saved mine heart. My thanks go to thee’And now’Might I see him” I promise thee that I shall disturb him not.” The doctor smiles and motions for Pendragon to follow. They enter an elevator, and the doors slide closed. The shot switches to show the doors opening on another floor, and Pendragon follows Dr. Soames down the corridor, and finally, into a room. The boy lies in a bed, peaceful looking enough, and not quite so pale. Pendragon walks over and pulls a chair close. He holds the boy’s hand in both of his and he begins to speak softly? ??Twas not right, what the animal has done to thee. “twould be better if thee stays away from him from here on out. Things have gotten messy, and I am going to make sure that things grow even messier. Akuma and Freeze shall not get away with the terrors they have committed upon thee. Too long I have merely been passive, and things got out of hand. It is high time that I righted some wrongs, and took out the trash. I swear to thee upon mine life that the transgressions against thee shall be avenged?” From the hallway, the shrieks of a frantic woman can be heard as her footsteps echo down the hall? “MY BABY!!! WHERE IS MY BABY?””” And following that, the cool soothing tones of Dr. Soames??Maam?.He is fine. He is resting now, but I’ll take you to him’Just try to stay calm!” The door flies open and standing there is the a woman who seems to have two conflicting emotions gripping her. Fear for her son’s well being, and rage for the man who now sits with him? “What are YOU doing here” You’You’wrassler!” The last comes out in a mocking tone and Pendragon bows his head before addressing the woman? “I was worried for thy son, madam. The lad has a special place in mine heart. I never wanted to see anything bad ha?” He is cut off as the woman crosses the room and stares defiantly up into his face? “You had no right! You lied to me! You said he would be ok! That nothing bad could happen to him! You said that you would watch him! You said”.You said?” The last breaks up into sobs and Pendragon embraces the shaking woman, holding her against his chest. She accepts at first, but soon grows rigid and pushes him away? “I want you to stay away from my family! I don’t want your kind to even be near my son!” “But madam, please!” Pendragon’s tone is hurt and dejected, but she stands firm? “You failed to protect him from those hooligans, and one of the bastards drank his blood’HIS BLOOD!!! Drank it on TV where anyone could see. Do you realize how long it will take a boy his age to get over the trauma of something like this?””” Pendragon casts his eyes down, unable to meet her gaze?“I only thought’I mean, it should have been a grand time for him”.” “AND IT WASN’T!! Stay away from my son”.Your insanity is just as bad for him as those other mindless fools?” She turns from him and crosses her arms, no longer speaking or looking at him. The boy stirs and reaches out, taking Pendragon’s hand once more. Pendragon grasps the small hand and looks down at the boy. He smiles and finally leaves. At the door, he looks back once more and a single tear runs down his cheek from his eye? *********FLASH********* And so the hero found himself here, amongst the dregs of society. They left no imprint on him. In his black mood, he wished to be among those with black souls. The people that he passes almost seem surreal. Not really there. The only true living being around is Pendragon. He seems to blaze with an inner strength, though it appears he has no goal, no place that he wishes to actually go? “And the worst thing is that the woman was right. I had no right. I had no right to place the boy in that kind of danger. Once more, too confident for everyone else’s good. Am I a monster” Was his mother right about me? Am I insane? These questions, though disturbing, matter little. What matters is that I shall destroy Freeze. If I have to stoop to his level, justice shall be done. I shall have mine vengeance’And yet, it seems that those who hold the power in GW wish me to take down good men. They have set me to destroy Donny McNasty on the next Evolution. Whatever makes the money for them, I suppose. I shall be merciful upon his poor soul. He is a decent chap. Only a bump in the road that takes me to Itagaki Freeze. Once mine hands close upon his throat’Things shall be ok’And all manner of things shall be well?” Headlights illumine the screen, and a car screeches to a halt inches from hitting Pendragon. A door opens and the voice of “The Bastard” Ben Davis rings out?“I’ve been lookin” all over for ya, man! What the hell are you doing?” “Thinking”.Just’Thinking?.” Ben shakes his head and opens the passenger side door of his Mustang’A simple car for a simple fella, ya know? Pendragon drops into the seat and Ben shakes his head?“Good Christ?” ~TO BE CONTINUED~
