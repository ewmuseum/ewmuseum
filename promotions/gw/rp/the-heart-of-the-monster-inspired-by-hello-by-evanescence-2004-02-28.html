The Heart of The Monster ( inspired by 'HELLO' by Evanescence)

28 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/04 Ice's head falls into his hands, and a single tear drop falls onto the picture. The tear is followed by a long, loud sigh. Ice continues to sit there, staring at the the ground between his feet as his head still rests in his hands. His hands slide back from his foreheard, through his hair, and finally resting at the back of his neck, as he slowly raises his head. A blank expression consumes Ice's face, as he pulls his hands away from the base of his neck. A moment passes, with Ice sitting there, staring straight ahead, no expression on his face. His eyes are glossy, and it appears as if his mind has wandered to another place. Slowly his eyelids slide down, and his eyes close shut. The sound of his breathing is the only sound that can be heard. Moments later his eyes open back up, and Ice quickly rises to his feet. He walks towards the door, and exits the room. Ice turns, and looks back into the room briefly, before turning back around and heading down the hallway, and into the main living area of the house. Lara is in the kitchen, doing the dishes, when Ice reaches the room. 'Hey, you okay?' Ice looks at her and pauses before responding. 'Could we.....' Lara gives Ice a quizzicle look. 'Could we what?' Ice turns his head to the left, and looks down at the kitchen floor. 'Could we go......you know.....I haven't since.......' 'OF COURSE! Let me grab my purse and keys, and we'll go right now.' Lara rushes by Ice, heading for the coat rack by the front door, to grab her purse and keys. Ice turns and sluggishly follows behind the rushing woman. She quickly rips her purse from the coat rack, and snatches her keys from a small hook on the wall. Lara quickly pulls the door open, and heads towards her car. Ice slowly follows, and reaches the car just steps after Lara enters the vehicle and starts up the engine. Ice slides into the vehicle, and shuts the door. Just as the door closes, Lara backs the car out of the driveway, and speeds away from the house. FADE OUT........................................................................ ............................................................................FADE IN The car begins to slow down. Slowly taking a left turn Lara turns the car down a long gravel road. Dust bellows from behind the car as it drives down the road. Lara again slows down the car. As the car slows it approaches two large concrete pillars, with a faded black metal sign, arching over them. The arching, metal sign reads. 'DEEPDALE MEMORIAL PARK' 'Well,.............we're here.' Ice slowly takes in a long, deep breath. He holds it for a few seconds before letting it slowly seep back out between his lips, as his eyes move about, anxiously observing all in front of him. His right hand raises, and reaches for the latch to open the door. A soft click is heard, but booms through the silence in the car, as Ice opens the passenger side door. Ice slides out of his seat, and out of the car. He stands next to the partially open door, and takes in another deep breath. Ice turns and looks in at Lara before closing the door. 'I'll leave you two alone. You have your phone on you?' Ice reaches into his pocket, pulls out his shiny silver cell phone, and holds it up so Lara can see it. 'You have my number right?' Ice gives one single nod in response. 'Well, give me a call when you are ready to come back home. Or if you need anything.' Ice gives two quick nods in response, and then shuts the passenger side door. Lara puts the car in reverse, and backs away from Ice. Ice turns and walks into the cemetery. Slowly he walks down a gravel path, observing each name on each headstone as he walks past them. Alicia M. Baker, Jacob A. Suttcliff, Colt A. Rowland, Clay L. Hibbard, Mary S. Potts, Victor K. Bahler, Alma L. Bahler, Joshua O. Bordon, the names stick out, one by one, as they are passed by. Ice reaches a large statue of an angel. The angel's head is bowed and to the left slightly, and her hands are together in a praying manner. At the bottom of the statue, it reads. OUR ANGEL Amelia Star Peterson May 9, 1984 - December 12, 2003 Ice stands frozen, and his body begins to slightly tremble as he stares at the statue. Suddenly his legs begin to shake. His legs then give out, and Ice collapses to his knees. His eyes turn a light shade of red, and begin to swell up with tears. Ice tries his hardest to fight them off. He takes in a long deep breath, and tilts his head back, staring up at the blue sky above him. But all his efforts are all for not. The emotion is too much to fight back, and the tears fall down his face at a rapid pace. One by one, in an unrelenting barrage, the tears continue to fall. Ice doubles over onto all fours, as his body convulses from the emotion running throughout him. Ice lies there sobbing into the earth, for a few minutes before finally regaining his composure, and sitting straight up. Still on his knees, but sitting straight up, Ice wipes the remaining tears from his eyes. 'I miss you sooo much..................' Ice again begins to sob. 'I can't sleep, I can't eat, I can't live without you...........I......I...I......I don't know .......I just wish that you were still here.' Ice once again regains his composure. Slowly he takes in a long, deep breath, and just as slowly lets it back out. 'And I promise you that I will make it right. I will not eat. I will not sleep. I will not stop......until that son of a bitch pays for what he did to you. I swear to you, that as long as my heart is still beating, as long as I have a breath in my body, I WILL MAKE HIM PAY! And he will continue to pay until he has suffered the mysery, and the anguish that you and I suffered. HE WILL FEEL THE PAIN HE CAUSED ONTO US!' Ice pauses and sighs, in attempt to calm himself. He slides off of his knees and onto his ass, before lying completely on his back, cupping his hands behind his head, and staring into the bright blue sky above. He closes his eyes, and lies ontop of the grave Star is buried in.. 'Our retribution is soon to come. I promise you, I will not break my promise.... I will not fail you.' The bright blue sky, quickly dulls, and fades into a dark purple. The low rumble of thunder echoes throught out the sky. One drop, then another, and then another, and then another fall from the sky. Another rumble of thunder bellows throughout the dark purple sky. The bright sky is no more, and everything has become dark. There is a loud CRACK and a bolt of lightining darts through the sky as the heavens open up and the rain begins to pour from the skies. Ice does not move and inch. He remains lying there, his hands cupped behind his head, and his eyes closed as the rain pours on top of him.
