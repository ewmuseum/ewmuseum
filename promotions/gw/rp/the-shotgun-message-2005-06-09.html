The Shotgun Message

9 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/09/05 'This is where things go downhill.' Michael sat in his locker room, reflecting on his defeat to La Herencia de Herederos. Perhaps it wasn't as much of his defeat as it was compared to The Enforcers, but it was a loss regardless; he failed to get the pin, and that made him a failure in conjunction. Maybe with what he was finding himself going through, it was a big mistake to team up with someone he barely knew in favor of his cousin. 'Fuck that.. it's a mistake being here and wrestling at all.' Out of all the things he could be doing while his wife was missing, it was the one thing she despised. No wonder he couldn't be doing his best; with such a thought in the back of his mind, how could he? 'God... if that woman knew that I needed to wrestle for this guy to want to help me get her back, she'd rather drop dead.' That joking thought made him smile inside, but not out of finding good taste in it- instead realizing simply how true that statement was. Truth be told, however, he had no other alternative than to take the word of this one person. If he didn't, Michael would be left with nothing other than to continue waiting hopelessly for police to get their heads out of their asses and come up with a find. Wrestling not only would get this Liang to help the search for his wife, but it was a way for Michael to take the law into his own hands. It was his way to strengthen himself for the day when his wife's abducter would be found. It was his way to legally vent his anger on any living, breathing human being that had the bad luck of being booked in a match with him. 'May I come in?' asked a voice that came with the abrupt rapping at Michael's locker room door. The faint Chinese accent that came along with the question provided from outside, though, gave Michael enough knowledge as to who it was, and he could care less if his strange associate came in or not. Michael was just getting ready to leave, anyway; it was merely those few thoughts that plagued his mind that kept him from standing up from the wooden bench he sat upon. 'Knock yourself out,' replied the Bronx native, and in came T'sao Liang, dressed in casual black clothing and hair slicked back neatly. He was a stark contrast to the gruff Michael, clad in a wifebeater, blue jeans, and his Converse high tops. Just noting how formal the Chinese man appeared, Michael couldn't help but feel weird knowing that he'd have to team with someone so incredibly different. Michael was the type who jumped into any fight; sometimes for the sake of fighting, others for much stronger purpose. Kongde, on the other hand... he had a calculating way about him- the kind that made him look harmless on initial impression.. but a further look into the man's green eyes showed someone that was more dangerous than most. It was probably that simple look in his eyes that got him a Global contract. 'I just thought I'd say that you did just fine tonight, Michael,' said Kongde. 'You and I have never fought together, never trained together.. we haven't even had a real friendly conversation... and yet, we functioned quite well as a team. I'm very pleased, I was expecting a much slower start to developing our teamwork.' Michael looked up at T'sao as if he were the craziest man in the world. That was another thing that made the two men different from eachother. 'Yeah, well, we didn't pick up the win, either.' Michael didn't care at all about teamwork- in the end, a win was all that mattered to him when it came to the world of wrestling. And that night, they didn't get one. 'You're being quite the negative person tonight. I would have thought you to be pleased with your performance. I know it will only be a matter of time before we dominate Global.' Michael blinked. A matter of time? 'And will it be before or after that time until we find my family?' Kongde looked back at Michael, and smiled. Another thing that made them both different; T'sao didn't appear to be the least bit worried, unlike his counterpart. For all Michael knew, Liang's amusement was actually coming from his partner's serious demeanor. 'Relax, already. You're not helping your own case by acting so down and out. And besides, I can assure you that your wife and child are safe.' That remark filled Michael with just enough hope to get him on his feet; it also piqued his curiousity. 'How can you be so sure?' Liang snickered at the question, as if he'd been waiting for it to be asked. 'Obviously whoever's behind this wants something from you. As long as it remains in their best interest for them to give you something in return, they'll be safe.' Well, that was one way to observe the situation. Thinking about it that way did help Michael feel at least a little relieved, even if it certainly didn't take so much as half the weights off of his shoulders. 'I need to know for sure, though,' Mercer sighed. 'I need to see them. Telling me they're alright doesn't mean anything.. not until I see them.' Maybe T'sao understood what his brash friend was trying to say. He had no idea what he must have been feeling, but at least perhaps it would be best if he tried to garner some sort of idea of what Michael meant. 'I think he's trying to tell me my word isn't good enough.' 'For now, Michael, I can only request that you trust me. Please... surely you can believe that I know what I'm talking about, right?' Michael couldn't argue. This guy was his only shot, even if it was a long one. 'Yeah... yeah, I can believe that,' Michael groaned in defeat. 'There just.. just has to be a way to know for sure.. someth-' Brring, brring. '...What the fuck?' Michael turned around, looking at his duffel bag stuffed in his locker. From it, came the sound of Michael's cell phone. T'sao tilted his head slightly at the sound, a bit curious as to who could be calling. Michael sensed Liang step forward slightly and gave him a confused glance, which caused Liang to raise his hands and step back. 'Sorry.' Mercer's attention turned back to the bag, and he reached right for it. The zipper was slid open quickly, and it only took a moment for Michael to feel his phone in the grasp of his right hand. He lifted the phone up, and spoke into it hesitantly while keeping an eye on the person across the room. 'Hello?' The voice he heard on the other side initially didn't trigger any sense of familiarity; as much as he had been thinking about the person behind the voice, his hope of hearing from them again was long gone. 'Michael? ...Help me!' Michael's hand grew shaky, and it nearly dropped the phone to the ground. 'Muh.. mu- mom?' Liang's ears perked. 'Hm... could we be getting somewhere?'
