The Beast of the Hunter

13 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-13-03 The Beast of the Hunter Thoughts: When the sickness builds up inside of you your heart explodes. When life shatters beneath your feet you fall. And when life comes to an end you feel like giving up. Emotion is the pride of being a human, of being that walking time bomb of emotion that could go off at any second. Yet it’s something we control, and at times, express when it should be held back. One of these things are anger… The anger I express is a lot worst than that in which a kid could express. Being rather a bigger person, I can concentrate on the weaknesses of someone, and hurt them when they are trying to be my friends, even if I know I truly don’t want to. It’s something that overcomes you as a person, you just can’t control it at all. The anger jumps from your head to your hands or your mouth and you just shoot off with punches or words. These are things I regret, things I want to learn how to control, things I must learn to control or everything will be gone. I’m not weak, I am not mean, I’m just not strong enough to control my emotion, and I need help. I need that help and it’s hurting me so much to admit it. I need the strength to know that when pain comes I will not cry, when someone dies I will not suffer a depression, and the strength to say sorry when everyone else says it. I need the strength… I need it… NOW! Prologue: Every man learns from his mistakes. Though they will never admit to it, it’s true. Just like they say, an old dog can’t learn new tricks. That’s false. It’s the fact that they just don’t want to take the time to teach an old dog new tricks when it already has some. The point is what can a man learn if he’s already experienced it all? Well surprisingly a lot. When someone says they know it all, or have learned it all, or even gone through every thing life has to offer, it’s just a testimony to how lazy they are. There is a new lesson behind everyday of life. You open a door, and you meet someone new, you learn a new name, you learn a new word, or even learn a lesson. For some it’s harder than others, for many it’s a second nature. Everyone learns, it’s nature, and daily is just the way it’s described. Even those who have trouble with a mental disorder or physical disorder, they still show that they can learn how to work around those disabilities, or a way to accept them. And each day they accept the fact that others will make fun of them, that some will laugh at them when they fall or drop a book, or even when they have to leave for a doctor’s appointment early. Those who laugh are the fuel that these people gain and build up for when it’s there time to shine. This fuel is the reason kids bring guns to school and shoot others; this fuel is the fire behind all hatred. This is harassment and it’s not allowed in this man’s lifestyle… Story Begins: Around every corner is someone being put down, laughed at, or hurt in some shape or form. It may be words, it may be action, but that doesn’t matter for it always happens. Every time someone does something to boost their ego, to feed there ignorant pitiful self, they show there weak, the show they have to do this to live, and that is why they are weak. There walking upon the street side is a man, long hair, hanging down over his shoulders and over his face, only a part of his chin can be seen as it shows a brown goatee matching his hair color and his eyebrows that are revealed as he pulls the hair back out of his face. Wearing a pair of black tight jeans, black leather boots with no lace, and a T-shirt that has a picture of a heart and it being surrounded by roses on the outside, with “A True Champion” in the middle, the guy just walks down the street standing out from everyone else. His face seen to many as a normal person, yet his build, and his height a defiant target to prove he is not the average man. His piercing blue eyes gaze at the street below, and he walks to the corner and turns with the same pace he started with. Turning the corner his eyes meet a child who has a face that is misshaped, for his chin has been either dislocated or moved to the left a lot. His lips are large, and he is young, maybe eight or nine. The boy just stares at Jesse as he walks by, not caring at what he stares at as many have stared at the young boy, and he most likely doesn’t know it’s rude. The boy just stays there sitting as the man walks by. Around the next corner the man sees yet another child, this one a young girl who is completely bald, and has an old appearance. As though she was sixty or seventy, and was aging ten years for every year she lived. The man just looks at her and keeps walking, as though he knows how it feels, and tears appear in place of the pain. He cannot control them and will not, for he can express himself and is doing so. He keeps walking as he goes around yet another corner, this one a left turns however. He sees an older man, about mid-thirties, and a young child is feeding the man food. Though not paralyzed, he just seems to have a problem with his eye-hand coordination. The man just keeps walking and now he is nearly balling, for such pain is too hard to keep in. His eyes are flourished with anger, for he sees a group of kids down the street pushing around someone whom is just helpless and is being treated different for how he looks. The man sprints down the street and grabs the biggest bully he finds, lifting him up by the neck he stairs into his eyes, and the kid just starts shaking in fear. The kids around the man run, including the one who was being picked on, and they just run away. The man finally drops the biggest bully and then looks around, windows are opened, people are staring, and he feels stupid. As he shakes his head he continues to walk, wiping the tears from his face, then from the side… WHAM! A car nails him and the man goes through the windshield, into the driver! *** *** *** The man awakes in an alley, his face bloody, his back cut, and his shirt stained red from the blood. Looking as if he’d been shot or just did open heart surgery, he is in no shape to be called normal. His face is nasty, for his nose is bloody, his eye is black, and it looks that he may have a concussion. Rolling over off the garbage, and pulling a hamburger wrapper off his back he begins to get up and walk down the dark alley. Then through a window where light comes, he sees a TV and himself on the TV. For the first time, he remembers his name, Jesse Jamester. Jesse looks down at his hands and then at his wrists, the vines that are green now are red, the name Julie is now a dead black, and he remembers it all… He remembers who he is, and what he is doing. I've felt the hate rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander out where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... Goodbye! “I’ve been locked in this dreadful mood for so long. Never to be reopened, or even called upon. I’m sorry to those who have loved me before for my skill, but tomorrow I will have to cheat to win it all. You can bury a man beneath the surface but he’ll always remain in the heavens above. I’m sorry to those who tried to change me, but tomorrow I’ll do what I once thought was impossible. Please forgive me.” I wipe it off on tile, the light is brighter this time Everything is 3D blasphemy My eyes are red and gold, the hair is standing straight up This is not the way I pictured me I CAN'T CONTROL MY SHAKES! How the hell did I get here? “This is my life, this is my passion, I live this dream, as if it’s happened. You’re hate has caused me pain but now that pain will be my strength. I’ll be the one to overcome it all, to release the energy of darkness upon all. I will be superior. I’m going to win the Global Wrestling Battle Royal and take GW by it’s neck slowly suffocating it of every stopping me!” Something about this, so very wrong... I have to laugh out loud, I wish I didn't like this Is it a dream or a memory? GET OUTTA MY HEAD CUZ I DON'T NEED THIS! Why I didn't I see this? “Life won’t be sweet for any of my partners. Sure you will help me into the battle royal, but everything will remain as was. I’m sorry to them but life is not fair, you’ll fall to my evil wrath as I just stare. No I’m not Satan, nor am I god, I’ve just been possessed to beat the hell out of all. You have to realize, this is not wrestling; this is a fight, not for man, nor wife. Now another name is within, Jarone that is.” I'm a victim - Manchurian Candidate I - HAVE - SINNED - BY - JUST Makin' my mind up and takin' your breath away GOODBYE! “Jarone you’re a sick man… Just like these kids. You hurt others by the pleasure you bring in there pain. By kidnapping, laughing, or just hitting them, you cause more pain than anyone I’ve ever known before. Your’ the devils mercenary, and I’m the bounty hunter who’s going to take you down. I’m your slayer Jarone, and it’s time you became a trophy on my wall! You haven't learned a thing I haven't changed a thing My flesh was in my bones The pain was always free “Wake up Jarone! Wake up! Wake your ass up! Your fighting me a man who is a 6’6” beast and I will rip your head off your limbs before I’ll tap or submit. Listen to me… Jarone, your not the sin within that bothers me. You’re the saint from above that I hate to listen to, and now since I’ve been unleashed, I want retribution, and you’re the one who will receive it.” I've felt the hate rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander out where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... AND IT WAITS FOR YOU! *** *** *** Jesse awakens in his bedroom, the pillow over his head, and his stereo blasting “Wait & Bleed” by Slipknot. He gets up and walks over to the mirror. Knocking the stereo off the shelf, he sees himself in the mirror, bloody from the face down, wearing the same T-shirt, and the same pants as in his dream… Was he dreaming? Or was he awake? Smashing the mirror with his fists he turns around and his face is full of anger. He is not a man, he’s a beast, and he’s just awoken from hell! Turning to the wall he’s emotionally distraught, no need to calm this man he’s already off his rocker. Jesse hustles down stairs and looks in the sink, the blood just trickles off of him and appears there upon the steel covering. He’s not sure whether to wipe it off or just stand there, but he knows that something is wrong. As he takes his left hand and wipes the blood he notices the dots in the sink form some words. Those words appear as the following. ”Victory is Fate.” Jesse turns on the faucet and washes the rest of the blood off his face. He makes his way to the living room where he turns on the Television and sits down to watch some TV. However within moments he is asleep again. *** *** *** Their are few things a blind man can see. One is the laughter of ignorant youngsters who know no better. The other is to see the evil lurking within. Some people will laugh but a blind man can see the worst of them all. His site was taken from him for the mere fact of him doing wrong, so in return he has to help others by finding the evil and killing it. “I’ve got little do worship in this world… But one thing I do worship more than anything else is my talent to wrestle, and so help me god, come tomorrow night I’m giving it my all!” Jesse prays after saying this and then fades away into the darkness, where the evil lurks.
