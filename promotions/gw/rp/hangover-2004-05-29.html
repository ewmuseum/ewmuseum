Hangover

29 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 29 RING RING RING Some kind of alarm clock had just rung right into my ears. I opened my eyes, but I wasnt at home in my comfortable bed, I was laying on the floor of the Dark Horse Saloon. The hard wood was driving into my back, so I sat up and looked around. Jesse was lying on the floor too, with a beer bottle in his hands. Brandi Sights was laying in the middle of us, I guess last night we got a bit of a hangover. I got up off the hard wood floor, because I had already got cut from it. Jesse suddenly awoke with a startle. He wiped his eyes and got off the floor. 'What happened to us last night?' he asked me. 'I think we got really drunk and just fell asleep here on the floor, but the only question is, why is Brandi sleeping beside both of us?' I asked him. 'She probably got a bit drunk too, so she must've have followed us and just lay down.' he said back. Suddenly, Brandi awoke off the ground. There was a broken beer bottle right beside her head, and she standed up and also loocked around. She walked over, and sat down on the same chair that she sat on yesterday. Me and Jesse walked over to her and gave her a smile. 'How did we get onto the floor?' was the words that came out of her mouth. 'We must've gotten drunk and fell asleep. I have a really bad headache right now.' I said. I rubbed my head a bit, it really did hurt. It was a throbbing in my head, that wouldn't go away. I closed my eyes, maybe the pain would go away if I put my mind to something else, other than the pain that was ripping through my head. I opened my eyes, and suddenly the aching stopped. My head feeled perfectly fine. I didn't know what happened, it just disappeared. 'Ok. My head does hurt a little bit to.' she said. 'What match are you in at the PPV?' asked Jamester. 'I'm teaming with Vennemen Pendragon taking on Jetsui and Itagki Freeze. Some freaks, eh?' I said. He laughed and so did Brandi, but when she laughed, there was somehting in there that made me feel really good. I've never felt like this for a girl before, maybe we might get into more than just friends, even though she just came out of the hospital, from the beating she took from TUE. They brutally attacked her with a baseball bat, and blood was pouring out of her mouth. She still had a cast on her arm. I could see the pain in her eyes. 'Well, me and Jamester should be going now, got to get to that show for High Noon.' I said. 'Bye, see you at High Noon.' she said. Me and Jamester walked out of the saloon, kind of wobbly from the night before. It was about 8:00 in the morning, so the sun was out and blazing down on the people below. We started walking down the sidewalk, and back to my car as the scene started to fade.
