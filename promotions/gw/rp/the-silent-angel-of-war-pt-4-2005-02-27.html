The Silent Angel of War Pt.4

27 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-27-05 Blood. It was everywhere. The room was stained like a crimson mask, and not even the heavenly waters could cleanse it. Handprints marked the door, as it showed the instinct of someone scratching for their very survival. Yet, the middle of the room was the scene of it all. There, on his knees, the Jackal sat with the same stained body as the room around him. On his lap lied what would be the sin of all sins, the slayed female body of an eight year old. Her eyes dull and lifeless, as her face began to pale, and her fingers and hands lifeless as they lie motionless hanging over her sides, like an angel feeling hells wrath at full force. In the awe stunning scene of events though, there was a guilty conscious. Their Jesse sat, calves under his thighs, as he peered into this young one’s face, weeping for forgiveness. “Forgive me father” I’ve not meant no harm. Please, take this pureness and form an angel of love and forgiveness.” His words fall short, as he realizes he’s begging for something he doesn’t deserve. Tears swell in his eyes, as they shade a blister red, and the veins show through the white of his eyes. His lips form words, but only a silent prayer is given. Laying the slain young one upon the cement, he lays her arms and legs straight, and then puts his hand over her eyes. Their fragile hollowness still shines as she stares directly up at the ceiling. “Sorry young one. I didn’t mean any harm. I” was possessed by the darkness.” Closing her eyes he lets her rest in peace, and her soul can now escape. The tears steadily roll down his cheeks like water fountains. “Everything is all wrong” I was suppose to kill you and then leave you here. But when I walked out that door and looked back, I couldn’t help but feel attached. It’s a sick feeling, knowing I killed you, this pure little innocent thing? What in gods name have I done? I’m not only committing sins, I’m begging for forgiveness after doing them. This isn’t me? this is a monster TBC created. But?” The words almost escaping his lips but he stops himself as the door opens up and in walks two guys in nice black suits. Grabbing Jesse by the arms they hoist him up and drag him out of the room. His legs leave two trails of blood as they slide out the door, leaving the room vacant and void of any life. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** “Tisk, tisk, tisk?” Staring down at the floor, TBC waves a clipboard in his left hand before spitting on Jesse’s feet. “You disgust me?” He spits again, but this time in the face of Jesse. The white fluid, tainted with a dark substance seeps down the cheek of the Jackal before Jesse wipes it off with his hand. “Your not even able to handle the simple killing of a child” someone who doesn’t have much of a past to remember, nor a future to look forward to.” A man pushes Jesse against the wall, where he then straps his wrists against it, making sure they aren’t able to move. Placing his palm on Jesse’s forehead, he pushes it back and then wraps another strap across his neck, tight enough to stop him from moving, but not enough to choke him. He continues this process with the legs, and the waist, securing Jesse to the wall like a prisoner bound to his death chamber. “I see your stats here-? He points at the clipboard as he flips through the sheets and looks at the statistics from all the tests they ran. “Your strength, endurance, speed, and mental capacity is all off the charts. Yet your mental state of mind, your blood level, and heart beat are all unstable” hmmm? Placing his hand out he waits for something to be placed in it, and after a few moments, a leather whip is. A meek smile overcomes the lips of TBC, before he grabs the handle of the whip and lets the tail of it fall to the floor. “But you know what” I think I can solve your problems with a special therapy.” Slapping the tail of the whip off the floor, his grin grows, and his eyes narrow, as the heartless man inside him is reflected through the black eyes of evil that he has. “Everyone leave the room” I have business to attend to.” As the six or so other men in the room leave, the tail of the whip slashes against the floor, causing a smacking noise to be heard. From the outside of the room, the men listen as screams echo through the cracks of the door, and fade only after entering the ear drum. Yet inside the room, the man who takes this abuse, can only grit his teeth as the slash marks across his chest begin to split, and blood begins to slide down his chest and drop from his abs onto his pants. After twenty or so whips, blood begins to drain through the cracks between his teeth, and seep down his chin. “AHHHH!” One yelp is heard from him, as the whip scratches his face, scarring his cheek leaving a red line. As the whip drops to the floor, his face does the same, and his arms hang limp as his body aches. Blood scurries down his beaten red body, as the hope in him drains through the wounds, yet not enough to let him go from reality. ?” hope you learned your lesson.” The smattered voice grumbles as it leaves the room, and two of the men who left come back in and unhook Jesse from the wall, and drag him by his arms, out of the room, and down the hallway. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** This is my life? there is no denying it now. I’ve become the used man, who gets the end of the shaft nobody wants. That, I cannot change? I have to ponder on these thoughts now, like I ponder on life itself. Why am I here? Why am I being treated like this? Is it hopeless? All of these questions have made be forget the truth, and forget what is really happening. Whether it be the truth or not, we’re going to have to settle our problems sooner or later. I’d rather it be sooner, but we’ll have to wait and see? TBC can make me ache, but he has yet to kill this beast inside of me, and I don’t think he can. God I wish these aches would go away! Each scar will be a foot in his mouth, and a fist that pounds on his heart. TBC, just like the global roster, has yet to feel any of my rage? has yet to feel the true force behind me the Jackal. It may seem like a game to them, but this is my life, and I live and abide by one code? “There is no quitting in life.”
