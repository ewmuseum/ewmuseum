A Familiar Voice

Date: Today It was the first time I had seen him in a long time. It was almost as if seeing the devil in person. His every movement, his every action ever so slight, tantalizing. He demanded attention, attention from everyone. But, no matter what he did, he would always have my attention. He would always have my attention because he made it that way. He did not have to make things this way. He did not have to single-handedly hold the focus of all my anger, hatred and rage. And now, now that I had finally found him, he did not have any escape. He paced around the large apartment. Once again the place looked like a wreck. He had been taking medication, he had been getting lots of sleep, he had been doing everything the doctor had told him to do, but it wasn’t totally helping. The worst part was, before he would remember the struggles, the battles within him for control, now he didn’t remember a damned thing. He walked through the kitchen. Broken glass from plates was scattered across the counters. Knives were sticking out of the wall, and out from the cupboards. The place looked like a scene from a horror movie, how convenient for this fine Hallows Eve day. The television set was on, loud, bright and seemingly broken. It was not broken however, instead he had the entire tape looping the same scene. The same part of the show. The scene was from GW’s Dark Rituals, and he seemed to be studying it. Watching it intently in between his breaks when his mind would wander away. It had happened a lot lately, a side effect of the drug’s he was on, he supposed. It had become harder and harder to concentrate and focus for long periods of time since he had begun taking the drugs. He looked at the glass on the counter and began to gather it up into a large pile. He stacked and clumped the shattered glass together before placing it against the small ledge of the counter. He looked down on it, pain had become the only release for him lately. A way to return him to reality from the dream world the drugs put him in. He breathed in heavily. Panting almost. In? Out? In? Out? Then he pulled his arm back. He clenched his hand into a fist, and he let his arm fly forward. With all of his might he swung forward with a punch that would knock the head off of a small child’s shoulders. His knuckles hit directly in line with the pile of glass, it was what he had wanted. Upon impact the skin began to pierce. The layers of skin opened up, and blood began to flow freely. The glass shards dug right into his hand, through the skin, right into the nerve endings. The pain shot through his entire body like electricity, the adrenaline began to flow as his body was consumed with the pain he felt in his hand. He did not cry. He did not pout. He did not whine or complain. No. He merely accepted the pain, and began to focus himself on what he had been doing. He walked back towards the small couch placed in front of the television set. The couch was nothing to be proud of. Not a lush velvet or leather, it was a ripped up, carved up, piece of junk. The rips in the upholstering did nothing to make it look better, nor did the foam piling out from many of those rips, nor did the scratch marks that seemed to have been carved in with some form of knife. He plopped himself down. The blood was still flowing freely from his hand, but it did not matter anymore. He was focused. Not on the pain, but on the task at hand. The one thing he had to do before his time was complete. The one thing he wanted to complete before he joined the rest of his family. “You stole everything from me. You took everything I lived for, everything I ever had, everything I ever wanted. You stole my life from me. And now, it’s payback time,” he spoke out loud to no one. However, it was not his usual harsh and raspy voice. Instead, it was replaced by a soothing and calm, somewhat familiar voice. “Your time is up, I’m coming for you!!” The scene on the television set was finally shown? The arena slowly dims. The opening piano rifts from In The End by Linkin Park begins throughout the arena then comes to a sudden stop. Darkness overtakes everything. It's as if the building has undergone a sudden power outage. The opening of Unforgiven II begins to play, building to the climatic toll of the bell. With this, the stage entrance from the back is illuminated by blue candles, placed carefully all about the platform. The candles range from the floor to sitting atop stands, well at 6 feet tall. The crowd gives a mixed reaction, leaning towards a chant of boos. The arena remains in blackout as the song continues. Lay beside me, tell me what they've done Speak the words I wanna hear, to make my demons run The door is locked now, but it's open if you're true If you can understand the me, than I can understand the you Slowly, the Upper Echelon's coat of arms burns into view upon the Globaltron. This is quickly replaced by the burning eyes of Maverick Reno. The scene zooms back to show a wolf, a cross hanging from its mouth as it paces towards the camera, red eyes glaring. Lay beside me, under wicked sky The black of day, dark of night, we share this paralyze The door cracks open, but there's no sun shining through Black heart scarring darker still, but there's no sun shining through No, there's no sun shining through No, there's no sun shining... The blue candles seem to grow stronger as they illuminate the entrance, the song building to climax once again. What I've felt, what I've known Turn the pages, turn the stone Behind the door, should I open it for you.... With this, black lights flicker on above the arena, giving a purplish blue glow to everything and everyone. What I've felt, what I've known Sick and tired, I stand alone Could you be there, 'cause I'm the one who waits for you Or are you unforgiven too? On the drawn out too, Maverick Reno emerges from the back. He's adorned in a pair of black straight leg pants and no shirt, his black leather trench coat hanging over his shoulders. His head now shaved completely and a tattoo on the back of his head resembling the classic ankh of Reno. There's a renewed look of passion in his eyes. Lay beside me, this won't hurt I swear She loves me not, she loves me still, but she'll never love again She lay beside me, But she'll be there when I'm gone Black heart scarring darker still, yes she'll be there when I'm gone Yes, she'll be there when I'm gone Dead sure she'll be there... Maverick makes a slow walk towards the ring, the arena remaining dark excluding the blue candles at the top of the ramp and the black lights above the arena. What I've felt, what I've known Turn the pages, turn the stone Behind the door, should I open it for you.... What I've felt, what I've known Sick and tired, I stand alone Could you be there, 'cause I'm the one who waits for you Or are you unforgiven too? Lay beside me, tell me what I've done The door is closed, so are you're eyes But now I see the sun, now I see the sun Yes now I see it Maverick, as if suddenly garnering energy, breaks into a furied rush to the ring, candles along the ramp that weren't seen before lighting with blue flames. He hits the ring and rolls inside and in one fluid motion, removes his trench coat and tosses it over the top rope. He paces the ring slowly, the song continuing to its completion before the candles all at once flicker and blow out. The black lights die at the same time and within seconds, the lights return to normal in the arena.
