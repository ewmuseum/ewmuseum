The Night After....

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/8/03 Change is a good thing. It always has been whether you want it or not. Most people regret it and wish they could go back to the way it was before. Others are happy about moving past it. Celebrate leaving it behind. Wishing they would forget it all. If things were only that simple. You get a new job, you leave home, you move into a new place, you start a new life. Change is everything that way. The journey of a lifetime always begins with one step. *BLLLLLLEEEEEEEERRRRRRGGGGHHHHHH!!!!* Or for Johnie Ringo, it was a kneel. *BLLLEERRGGHH!! BLLEEERGHH! BLL... BLLL....* It stopped, for now. But you can’t expect much with hangover days. His eyes squeezed shut from the pain, if you can call it that. Just that grueling uneasiness quivering within his intestines. He can just tell that if it wanted to, his entire insides would jump out of his mouth if it could. Right now its too tied down. Maybe some other time. Feeling he’s settled enough, Johnie looses up the clenching of his eyes and flutters them open in pain as the florescent light shines off the porcelain. “UGH!” He leans his head onto the toilet seat to rest a bit, closing his eyes in pain. He opens them again reluctantly to look down at the creation he’s made into bowl below him. The multicolored modern art thrown together on the floating waters. Bits and pieces of food scraps surrounded mostly by phlegm-like liquids. He tilts his head to the side slightly as he stares at it. “I don’t remember eating that...” Johnie collapses backwards and slams his body against the wall, shuddering with ache. Straightening himself out, he looks up at the cheesy fixtures of his new bathroom. Some place. Glancing at the light blue curtain half-hung on the bar beside the shower, he chuckles to himself and half groans. Hugging the wall, he struggles to his feet and tries to use the doorknob for leverage. Instead, his hand slips and sends him crashing back down to the floor. Back to square one. Crawling, he heads toward the sink. Grabbing hold of the curvature, he lifts himself up with it. Tilted to one side, he looks down at his feet noticing only having one shoe on. Great, now I have to find the other. Putting most of his weight on the sink itself, he looks a grizzly faced mess in the eye. If only he could keep them open. Shaking his head, he turns the hot water knob as the stream gushes beside his hands. He cups them and splashes his face with the scolding waters. Clearing his sinuses, he snorts quick and detects a scent of repulse. “Fuuuuuck.” Johnie leans to his left and flushes the toilet. Feeling fine enough, Johnie reaches for the doorknob and opens it reluctantly as the sun shines though the door. Somebody opened the curtains. It certainly wasn’t him. As he walks out of the bathroom, he steps into the war zone. Bottles and cans scattered across the entire floor. Glancing at the coffee table, he sees filled ashtrays along with other objects made into ashtrays. Cups, cans, plastic Mt. Dew bottles, tinfoil, the table itself. I guess it was anything goes night. But when isn’t it when a party’s going? Hearing the echo of cheers, he glances over at the TV screen to see Trey sitting in his somewhat plush chair. It must be Saturday. College ball is on. “Is he alive or just the walking dead?” Trey says as he turns his head over towards Johnie. “Kinda in between,” Johnie mutters as he heads towards the nearby couch and plops down hard. He grabs his head instantly, realizing that it wasn’t smart to do down fast. “Us zombies have a way of flipping back and forth between.” “Well I hope you’re gonna live,” Trey replies, turning his head back to the screen. “I’d hate to be the one to dig a grave in the backyard for ya. Too much effort for such a small cause.” “It wouldn’t keep me down anyway. I’d just climb back out and we’d do it again next weekend.” “What the fuck happened to you, anyway?” Trey doesn’t turn his head away from the screen. Kinda hard when you’re addicted to the game. “You were doing so well until you did that shot contest with Ray.” Pulling a pack of Camels from under the couch, Johnie readies his light. “Well Jack and I have had our differences over the years. We liked each other when we first met, but we went downhill kinda after that.” The flame flickers before the cigarette as he breathes in the smoke and lets it pour out his nose. “I’m sure we’ll meet to try and settle it all again.” “Whatever man. Just make sure you can handle it next time. That way I won’t have to pull you off some skanky bitch next time. “What are you talking about?” “Last night, man!” Trey even turned his head for this one. Its gotta be big. “You were slobbering all over this fat piece of ass. Don’t ask me why.” “The fuck?” “You know. You kept grabbing her tits, leaning all on her. Shit, son. If you’re gonna mack, be a little more selective next time. Shit!” Johnie leans back taking a drag as he goes over the memories of the night before. Faces. Figures. Shots. Beers. Names. All of it comes in a haze, not clear at all. Then it comes to him...he thinks. “You mean that big tittied chick?” “Her tits weren’t the ONLY thing big on her! She had junk in every direction and not just her trunk, bro.” He doesn’t get it. Johnie puffs again, shaking his head in submission. “Whatever man. I don’t remember it. But if you’re saying she’s bad, then she must have been bad!” Trey tilts his head back in question. “What does that mean?” “Nevermind...never mind.” Johnie chuckles to himself and glances at the screen himself. Tennessee vs. Miami. Already half through. It must be pretty late already. “What time is it?” Trey glances at his watch and replies. “3:23” “Jesus!” Johnie exclaims as he throws his feet up on the table. “I gotta get on a normal schedule. This is bullshit.” “Maybe if you got a real job, you could live real hours.” The tone was different. Kind of a protective tone that Johnie’s heard before. “Fuck you!” he fires back. “You sound like my mom. And you KNOW I don’t need another one of those.” Trey recoils a bit and gets back to watching the game. “Yeah, yeah. I know. I’m just giving you shit.” *BEEP EEP EEEEP* A faint echo of Johnie’s cell phone shouts out from under the cluttered mass on the floor. UGH! What now? He struggles to lift himself off the couch and crawls his way over to the pile of half-eaten Doritos and cardboard pizza mats. Its not there. *BEEP EEP EEEEP* It rings faintly from behind him. “Where is the fucking thing!” He scrambles to turn around and picks apart another pile of cluttered crap. Mostly just empty cigarette packs. *BEEP EEP EEEEP* “FUCKING THING!!” This time he hears it to his left underneath his University of Arizona sweatshirt, stained from the night before. He tosses the shirt aside and flips open his phone. “Yeah?” Simple answer says it all. “Is this Johnie Ringo?” The voice is unfamiliar to Johnie, but he can’t expect to remember every voice. “Yeah.” He rises to his feet and heads into his “office.” Most people call it a bedroom. “I have a job for you.” It seems that word has gotten out. I guess Stecchino gets news out easy. “What do you have in mind?” Simple business. In this line of work, things don’t always have to be formal. “Something simple. Just to get my point across.” “Do you have anything for me to say” Just in case he’s not sure who this might be from.” Strange how this has to get brought up, but some people are stupid. Or just have more than one guy after them. “Just that he’s overdue. That should be clear enough.” “Where?” Johnie paces around the room, waiting. “Tulsa.” “Tulsa?” His voice changes a bit, but not enough to notice it seems. He stops pacing for a second. Oh god...why there? “Yes.” The voice sounds firm, but a little reluctant. Not his first job call, but he doesn’t seem like he does them often. “The target hasn’t been paying his dues lately. Its about time someone gets him up to speed.” “Name?” “Luther Treadwell. You’ll find him on campus.” “Done. You know the price and place?” Johnie says it nonchalant. He hopes the guy knows. He’d hate to repeat it over the phone. “Of course.” “Good. Later.” He flips his phone back and sighs. “This is getting too easy.” Its funny how easy the job can be when you have the simple facts of it. Its not so hard. Dirty work, but its gotta be done. Keeps the people in line. Johnie swings open the door and puts together his gear. This might be a long trip. Trey is walking back from the fridge with a Corona in hand. “Going somewhere?” “Yeah. Gonna be a while.” Rushing along, Johnie tosses in a couple shirts lying near the couch. He knows he’s gotta go soon. Sooner he’s out, the sooner he’s back in. Trey squats back down in his chair and reclines back. “Where to?” “...Tulsa.” Silence, but sometimes silence is its own answer. Johnie zips up his bag and marches to the counter beside the stove for his keys. Jumbling them he grabs his bag and starts heading out the door as quickly as possible. “I’ll catch you in a couple days.” “Aight, later.” The door slams behind him, leaving Trey to sit back and watch the game. He throws back a quick swig and suddenly gets why. “Fuck...”
