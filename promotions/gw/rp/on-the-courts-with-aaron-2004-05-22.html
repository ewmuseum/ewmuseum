On the courts with Aaron

22 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/04 It's a warm day in May. It's been unseasonably warm, as a matter of fact. It's been more like summertime weather than springtime. And on warm days like this, Brick and Aaron hit the basketball courts for a little one-on-one. Today is no different. Brick is now on the offensive, getting ready to go to the hole. Aaron puts forth the best defense he can, given the relative size differences. The score is tied up, 9-9. The game ends at 10. 'Come one, man. You ain't got no better D than that?' 'Oh, you wanna see D? I got your D!' Brick fakes left and then spins around to the right and heads for the basket. One lay-up shot and Brick wins the game. 'Good game, man.' 'Yeah, I needed it. 'Listen, man, I know you probably don't want to hear this, but I've made a decision.' 'And I wouldn't like it, why?' 'Because I've decided to forgive Derek Draven for what he did to me back in 2002 and the rest of the Nevermore, too.' The idea doesn't sit too well with Brick, and understandably so. The Nevermore had been a constant thorn in Brick's side for years. It all started in January of 2001, when Jonathan 'Scarecrow' Davis bludgeoned him repeatedly with a crowbar. Months later, Salaok screwed Brick out of his first-ever World title shot. In the summer of 2002, Brick's war with the Nevermore, or the Harvesters as they were known, began in earnest when Aaron was used as a human chess piece. A year later, Salaok became co-owner of Global Wrestling and proceeded to make Brick's life hell. 'Brick, say something, man.' 'You decided to forgive the Nevermore.' 'Yes.' 'You decided to forgive the sons of bitches who kidnapped you, who tortured you, who damn near killed you?' 'See, I knew you'd react like this.' 'You're damn right I'm reacting like this! Those motherfuckers damn near killed you, trying to use you as a weapon against me!' 'I know.' 'No, I don't think you do!' Brick then opens Aaron's duffel bag. 'What are you doing?' Brick pulls out Aaron's wallet and pulls out a small photo of Felicia. 'You see this woman? You know who this woman is?' 'Yes, Brick, I know who my wife is.' 'Yeah, glad you do. Because this is the woman who came crying on my shoulder night after night, worried that you might never walk again or might be killed! This is the woman who almost became a widow before she could really be a wife because of those bastards!' 'Look, Brick...' Brick then pulls out another small photo. This one is of Aaron's daughter Ashley. 'You see this little girl here? Thanks to the Nevermore, she might never have been born!' 'Brick!' 'What?!' 'I'm not forgiving them for them. I'm forgiving them for me.' 'For you?' 'Yeah, for me. It's been two years now. It's not doing me any good just keeping it in. It's burning me up inside. I'm not doing anything to them, holding onto a grudge. And just because I forgive them doesn't mean I'm ever gonna be buddies with them.' 'I hope to God not!' 'You think Felecia would be cool with this?' 'Who do you think suggested I forgive them?' 'See, I always knew you were whipped,' Brick jokes. 'Shut up!' 'Seriously, though, if that's the decision you want to make, I'm behind you. I may not like it, but I'm behind you.' 'Really?' 'Yeah, man. You're my boy.' Brick and Aaron pound fists. 'How about you, Deacon and Train?' 'Don't push it.'
