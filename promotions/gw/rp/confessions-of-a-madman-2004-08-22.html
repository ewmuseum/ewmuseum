Confessions of a madman

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/22/04 Candlelight served as the only illumination of the abandoned Inn's dim loft. The candles forced shadows to dance on the walls as wisps of gray smoke ascended toward the heavens. Amidst the shadows that ran across the wall as if they were possessed by a satanic force, stood a man who may have very well been possessed by a satanic force himself. Dressed in his black cloak with a heavy sash tied in the middle, his pale blue eyes, dull and weary beneath the candles flame, stood the man I know only as the shadow man. Confusion enraptured me as I stood before the shadow man in bewilderment. I couldn't even begin to fathom where I was; much less how I had arrived at this destination or the big question…what was my purpose here? All of those questions seemed merely rhetorical as I fired them at myself, my mind racing to stumble upon a conclusion, yet the self interrogation was in vain as no answer greeted me. 'Sit down, my child.' The shadow man, softly ordered. Glancing at the small wooden table resting next to an oaken stool I knew better than to make an attempt to set there, instead I would settle for the floor. Leaning down slowly the cartilage in my knees popped and my tendons creaked as I took my seat upon the floor. Still standing, the shadow man now seemed to tower above me as I fired off a question at him. 'Where am I?' I asked, crossing my legs Indian style. 'I've already told you, you are home.' He gently replied in a tone that seemed far too human for such a being as that. 'Fine…where is home?' I asked with a sigh, making the decision to play along with whatever game this madman had initiated. 'This little inn is nestled in the beautiful forest of Romania.' The shadow man responded, leaving me quite surprised. 'Romania?' I asked, blinking in surprise. 'The most remote and desolate forest of Romania.' Said the shadow man as the candles highlighted the wrinkles in his aged skin. 'Then…why am I here?' I asked; now twisting at my ragged black locks in confusion. 'How did I get here? What is my purpose here?' The questions seemed to all flow at once and my accomplice chuckled evilly, apparently amused at my ignorance of these surroundings. 'You are here because you were attacked by Leinad Soma, I brought you here so that you may recuperate, revive, lick your wounds and retaliate. Your purpose is to bring justice to Leinad Soma…many have tried and all have failed, yet in you I see a hero.' The shadow man said with a chilling tone to his words. 'Leinad Soma?' I asked in confusion, with both hands now tugging at my hair. 'The vampire.' He flatly stated. 'Oh please…I don't believe in vampires, it's just a stupid myth.' I spat out in denial. 'Don't be so foolish, my child. The world is full of many things we cannot understand…the world is full of many things that strike fear into our hearts.' Said the shadow man as he slowly took a seat upon the wooden stool in the attic of this bizarre inn. 'The world is also full of lies and myths.' I sneered, refusing to believe that such a creature actually existed. 'Then tell me what you believe.' The shadow man coldly responded. 'Wh…what do you mean?' I inquired, my confusion and interest piquing. 'Explain why you are here. How you reached the very place you sit at this moment. I am telling you that you were attacked by a vampire and I brought you here to guide you along in your destiny, yet you choose to follow the unknown rather than to believe the truth?' He bravely challenged. 'Fine Xavier…tell me what the truth is if you have such wisdom of these things.' I sneered, waiting for him to answer. 'The truth isn't pretty. It's not beautiful pictures, roses and babies breath, but rather a tale of blood, heartbreak and death.' He grimly stated. 'Sounds like a best seller.' I cheerfully replied with sarcasm dripping from my voice. 'You aren't taking me seriously.' He murmured, his voice not yet taking on a tone of anger. 'That's because you aren't making any sense. You bring me out here to…to God knows where…' I began, before he cut me off. 'Romania.' He inserted, using his geographical knowledge. '…to Romania, and for what? To chase some goofy guys in Dracula costumes around the woods? I'll start taking you seriously when this doesn't seem like a joke.' I said, finishing my original sentence. 'You were delivered here so that you may conquer the evil that roams this universe, you are the hero of the new age. Your birth marked the dawning of a new era!' Said the shadow man, his voice rising in excitement. 'That's all I hear, you are the hero, you must conquer evil…explain to me why I should! Explain everything to me. I mean…why should I trust you? I don't even know your name, where you're from or anything about you. Tell me why I should trust a stranger.' I snapped; yet to my surprise the shadow man remained unmoved by my emotional outburst. 'You want to know who I am? I used to be the man that you are…' He replied, his voice trailing off slowly. 'The man that I am? There is no need to speak in parables…' I said in turn, my mind racing with confusion. 'Close your eyes.' Commanded the shadow man. 'Why?' I inquired, untrustingly. 'I'm going to tell you of a hero I once knew.' He replied, his voice softer than I had ever heard it. With a moment of hesitancy I grumbled to myself before lowering my eyelids over the bluish gray orbs that resided in my sockets, while folding my hands and allowing them to rest on the black leather pants I wore. Suddenly I felt cold and the soft white cotton T-shirt over my torso did little to comfort me as a shockwave of cold fear rushed through my body, rippling my skin with goosebumps. ---- Suddenly everything seemed to change. No longer was I sitting in a dusty attic but now the opening was in the clouds. The sky was as blue as purity would allow the birds were singing their praises to the sky and the squirrels scampered about. Yet…I was not present, at least not in a physical form. Instead I bore witness to a young man in a meadow. Romania seemed to be the setting of this meadow, so full and beautiful as a gentle breeze sent flowers swaying to and fro amidst the grass. It was a beautiful explosion, a myriad of colors and this young man was enjoying it. The young man was of stunning appearance. Handsome with blonde hair that fell across his face, dressed in a black cloak he seemed all too familiar and had it not been for his blue eyes so full of life then I would've have mistaken him for a warrior. On his side, a sword hung in it's scabbard, the sword, a work of art, decorated by the finest silversmiths and encrusted with only the most beautiful of jewels. 'Years ago, this man was once like you.' Said the shadow man gently, yet my concentration remained unbroken and the scene did not fade away. 'Young, bright, exuberant and full of life. My joy overflowed, it was abundant and never ending, until the day in which his family was killed by a group of thieves.' Now the young man in the meadow pulled out his sword and fell to one knee holding the sword to the heavens, the sun bouncing from the sharpened tip of this beautiful instrument. 'And when his family died, he took an oath. An oath to protect those who could not protect themselves. Great or small, rich or poor, young or old, it mattered not to him, his job was only to protect.' The shadow man continued, a weary sound in his aged voice. The young man in the meadow kissed the blade gently and closed his eyes, letting his long blonde hair fall over the blade as he rested his head upon the flat of the blade. 'Upon the grave of my father…upon the grave of my mother…I do vow to protect those younger and weaker than I. To uphold righteousness, glory, honor and virtue…' The young man softly said, his voice strong and youthful. Raising his head he looked to the sky and finished his vow with four softly spoken words. …so help me God.' 'The man that you see before you was a true hero of the masses. He was celebrated by all that knew him loved by all that he protected and loathed by all of those that dared oppose him. A true virtuoso with the sword he never lost a battle, slaying villain after villain. He truly was a superhero came to life before the time of comic books. Yet his power was a gift from a heavenly source. A true warrior for God, this young man waged his battles. Fearless, he raged on ridding the world of evil foe by foe. Yet one man cannot go it alone…no matter how hard he tries. Eventually the weight of the world became too much for this man to bear.' The shadow man said in a choked voice, had my eyes not been closed I would have no doubt seen a tear escape and race down his ancient cheek. Now the scene had changed, gone was the blue sky, the meadow and the singing of birds, now it was a sky dark with sin, a fearsome forest and the eerie hooting of owls served as the soundtrack for this setting. The young man now looked different. The sword that had once gleamed with silver and precious stones now bore the stains of blood down its long blade. The blade itself rested against the ground and the hilt was clutched in its possessor's right hand. 'The hero lost his way. Evil surrounded him at every turn until he could combat it no more. His pride had vanished, the killing of the evil ones had ceased and the death of the innocent would begin in his absence.' Said the shadow man, served as a narrator for what I saw in my minds eye. The young hero threw his sword into the woods and fell upon his face, tears streaming down filthy cheeks and his once shining blonde hair streaked with dirt and grime. 'The hero simply walked away, believing that if he left evil alone it would run it's course, and nothing would change. Such a foolish thought, for he had never heard that good men are only required to do nothing for evil to triumph.' The chilling voice of the shadow man hissed. The young hero closed his eyes, sobbing as he lay face down upon the hard packed terra firma of the forest. ---- 'Open your eyes.' The shadow man ordered and I did so at his command. The world I had seen in my mind vanished and the dark loft replaced its memory. 'That hero never realized his mistake, yet he was far too old to remedy such an action.' The shadow man told me with tears streaming from his eyes as he choked on emotion. Staring down at the tips of my Chippewa boots I avoided looking at the crying shadow man, yet it seemed as if that wasn't what he wanted as he garnered my attention once more. 'When he realized his mistake, the young hero was old…yet his wisdom was sufficient to bring up a new hero. Living over countless years, God had punished him with immortality. It was punishment, for this immortality was unwanted, the hero wanted to die to end the constant suffering and guilt he lived in day by day.' The shadow man said as I raised my eyes to meet his. 'That young hero, was a man known as Xavier Crysto…' The shadow man began, a lump of emotion in his throat delaying his sentence. '…and I am…Xavier Crysto.'
