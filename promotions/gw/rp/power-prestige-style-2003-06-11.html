Power, Prestige, & Style.

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: .6.11.03 June, 11th, 2003, a dark hotel room The scene opens slowly, the darkness of the hotel room very obvious from the start. Lights are not completely absent however as the moonlight comes right through the window, illumination the outline of the bed and the masses occupying it. The time is 4:24 am which can clearly be seen on the digital clock placed on the night stand aside the bed. We watch as the minute’s change, the lighting of the digital numbers changing just slightly to convert what was once a four into a five using only a slightly different pattern. Movement One mass in the bed turns, causing the bed to fluctuate and shift very slightly; the other person moves just a hair with the change. We hear a voice, whispered, obviously spoke aloud out of desperation. “I wish I could sleep.” The voice belongs to one Tyler Pierce. He turns over again, pulling some of the blanket with him as he does so and lies there, restlessly for a moment. A minute at most passes, the light pattern on the digital clock shifts again and Tyler has turned once more, facing the opposite direction. He sighs audibly and moves again, this time ending up on his back. Another voice rings out, this one of a slightly sweeter? and sleepier persuasion. “Tyler, is something bothering you?” He turns his head on this time, glancing to his left. He sighs a bit and looks back towards the ceiling. “Nothing’s wrong, Ember. I’m just” I want to know what’s going on with this Blayze match. Preston called me a few hours ago? said there were “complications.”” She reaches towards the nightstand on her side of the bed and flicks it on then leans over, draping an arm over his sculpted chest. “He said there were complications” Like what?” Tyler shakes his head a bit, speaking quietly. “Didn’t say, just that he was going to look over the agreement that was signed” see what the deal is and get back to me. He said something about a loop hole.” Ember places her head on Tyler’s shoulder and closes here again. “I can’t believe this. I’m getting screwed out of this cruiser match again, I know I am. Salaok’s pissed because we fucked with his plans and he’s taking it out on me. Hell, there’s no telling what’s going to happen.” Ember is silent as Tyler speaks, his pace slightly quickening as the thoughts that have been filling his head file out one by one. “He’s got the power to change shit and he knows it. I get the feeling we’re making one hell of an enemy in Salaok and Nevermore” not that we can’t handle the psycho cultist bastards? but that’s not the point. It still makes things hard on us career wise.” He sighs again? and in the silence, Ember refrains from responding. Atleast, we think she does until he glances at her and realizes that she’s fallen asleep again. Gently he removes her head from his shoulder and lets her rest back on her pillow. He whispers to himself. “Just because I can’t sleep doesn’t mean I shouldn’t let her.” With this, Tyler pushes himself from the bed as slowly and easily as possible. He stands and stretches before the window, gazing out at the vacant pool area downstairs. Tyler stands before the balcony’s sliding glass window in a pair of Adidas white and silver shorts. “Hmm” might as well try and be somewhat productive since I’m up.” Tyler’s whispers are amusing only in the fact that he’s speaking to himself; a sign of instability, insanity, other mental psychosis? or simply insomnia? Tyler walks slowly across the room and pulls a drawer open from the large Chester drawer. He pulls out a sleeveless white t-shirt and pulls it on. Within a couple of minutes, he’s also pulled on a pair of white tennis shoes and grabbed his keys and wallet from a nearby desk. He pulls the door to the hotel room open slowly and then closes it gently; letting the woman, now known as Ember, sleep. We hear the door’s lock click and we fade to black on the room. The scene fades in on a clock, a clock with the hour hand on three and minute hand between four and five. The scene pans back slowly, showing a glass room excluding the one wall. The hotel pool can be seen just outside the window and then the towering visage of the actual hotel just yards from there. The sound of running can be heard as we pan back and of breathing, although not rushed or hurried. The scene begins to pan to the side very slowly, revealing Tyler Pierce with a now wet shirt running on a treadmill. He has his eyes closed, his mind elsewhere as he runs. Tyler Pierce; a rich man’s son, a Dad’s small piece of immortality, a Father’s delinquent. Tyler Pierce was one of a kind, a true problem child. He had a million problems growing up, between his inability to do well in the earlier grades no matter how brilliant he may have been, his attitude towards most other people who he came in contact with, and the drug problem that would consume six years of his life; he had managed to become the black sheep of the Pierce family. A brief summary of The American Pierce Family Tree His Father is Mark Xaviner Pierce. Mark Xaviner Pierce came over from England in the mid 1960’s; he had done well for himself with business in Europe and felt it was time to expand his family fortune to America. He quickly found that America was not quite the easiest place to expand to. He assumed anyplace that would consume McDonald’s food by the tons daily would buy almost anything ? he had been wrong. After a few failed attempts at franchises and small businesses, he landed into a fresh and developing field, computer engineering. With a few wise investments, he quickly bought a nice bulk of shares from three companies: Microsoft, Apple, and Macintosh. With shares in each and continuing to make a small living through an art gallery in Boston, Massachusetts, he quickly became the powerful Mark Pierce that he is today. In the early 70’s, he met his wife, Debra Rochelle Trenton. Within a short couple of months of their marriage in 1975, Kyle Gordon Pierce was born. Kyle was the first blood Pierce to be born in America. Kyle led a simple life and caused no problems as a child, he was the ideal child for an up and coming business man. Eventually he went to MIT and went on to teach Computer Programming and Engineering at Boston University. He lives in Boston today with his wife and two point five children. A clich? life for a clich? man. Tyler Xaviner Pierce was the next to be born but since a summary is not needed, we’ll move on to our next Pierce. Aiden Scott Pierce was born in 1980. Although his given name is Aiden, he’s always gone by Scott as it’s more common. Scott was not quite the same as either of his brothers; he was neither business oriented like his brother Kyle nor party oriented like his brother Tyler. He was in between, a shy guy until one got to know him. Scott felt the need to get out from under his parents wings, his own desires not matching what they had set out for him. With this, Scott left home at 18 and moved to New York, New York where he joined with Trevor Sinclair and Brandon Mikhail. Trevor, a true luchador in a now defunct wrestling organization and Brandon a powerhouse to be reckoned with, he trained to compete in the tradition squared circle. After only a few years of competition and a few championships under his belt, injuries forced him out of the business. He now teaches self defense and martial arts in San Diego, California. There you have it, the Pierce family tree. You can see how Tyler is easily the black sheep of the family; although Scott’s career wasn’t one that Mark and Debra had originally conceived for their son, it was much more respectable than the wild parties and vodka soaked life that Tyler had been leading. Tyler was always under pressure by his parents to follow in Kyle’s footsteps or to “be more like Scott” and commit himself to something. Neither business nor hard work was Tyler’s style; he lived for the moment and nothing more. Things changed however. Things always change? that’s how life works, you know? Someone lost their life. And Tyler changed his life. Now, Tyler follows in his brother’s footsteps. He admires the business style of his brother Kyle and follows in the footsteps of his brother Scott. He’s taken his parents advice and twisted it, molding it into a lifestyle he can stand. With each match in Global Wrestling, Tyler Pierce takes one step towards becoming as popular as Scott once was. With each move he makes with the rest of TUE, he shows how his business sense is there. He’s truly a mix of both worlds, a business man who knows how to handle himself in the ring with the best of the best. That’s why he’s here now; why he’s running himself to death. Why he’s soaking his shirt at four in the morning. Tyler runs still, his shirt getting damper. He has a match in one week against Roz Blayze, he knows that. There’s an issue. Something has changed? it seems that Salaok is striking back for the assault on the warehouse. TUE is about to get a little return fire? but how much? He has an appointment in three and a half hours; he’s supposed to meet a limo out front of the hotel. Inside the limo, Preston Banks will be there to explain what’s going on. Ten hours ago, Preston Banks, Attorney at Law representing the organization known as The Upper Echelon, dove into the paperwork signed just a week before in regards to the mishap at Dead Zone. Banks and Capo had been able to pull getting Tyler a singles match with Roz Blayze for the Cruiser Weight Championship. But what now? Something came up? and Preston’s going back to work to figure out just what is going on. Tyler steps off the treadmill and pulls his shirt off, wiping his forehead with it. He tosses it to the side and moves to the weights; he lies down and grips the bar above his head. Whatever Nevermore had done to the match wouldn’t make things easier for Tyler. After all, he is a member of The Upper Echelon. TUE isn’t exactly Nevermore’s favorite group of people right now. Whatever Salaok may throw at him, Tyler was going to be ready. But what could he do? What could be done to this match to make it more difficult? He’s prepared to run straight through Blayze? he’s been preparing for two weeks now. Evolution was just a step on the way to the match, not a challenge? just a step. He was ready for Blayze then. He’s more than ready for Blayze now. Tyler pushes the barbell into the air without as much as a grunt. He holds it there, breathing in and out slowly before lowering it for only a few seconds and lifting it again. Speed and agility are Tyler’s strong points? and from the looks of things, he’s adding endurance and strength to that list. In the past month, Tyler has begun to bulk up just enough to increase his game. Increase his game for the coming months? a war is brewing. Both diplomatic and on the battlefield. The Upper Echelon is one of the major factions? and they intend to win. Tyler intends to win. The scene fades to black as it slowly zooms back, Tyler continuing to lift the weights. A few seconds pass of pure blackness. In white the following appears: 7:30 am The scene fades from black to show a black limo moving into place outside the hotel. A black Escalade is directly behind it, its windows tinted, its rims 22 inch chrome, and the body lowered just inches from the ground. Power, Prestige, and Style. That’s what TUE is all about. A moment passes and the glass doors of the hotel open, two guards walk out both dressed in black suits with white dress shirts and black ties. Just a step or two behind them is Tyler Pierce. Tyler is dressed for success; his dirty blonde hair is hanging neatly about his face, he’s adorned in his usual black Italian silk suit with alabaster white dress shirt and tie, the golden cufflinks embellished with what is now known as the The Upper Echelon’s coat of arms. His Armani wingtip shoes click on the cement as he walks towards the limousine. He carries a briefcase in his left hand and slips a pair of mirror tinted sunglasses on with his right. Power Prestige Style Tyler exemplified all three, the image of him making his way towards the limousine convoy, the Escalade containing up to four guards, the bodyguards walking about him in his stylish suit, the simple image says it all. Tyler represents what The Upper Echelon represents. One of the two bodyguards opens the back door for Tyler and he slides in and onto a plush leather seat. He places his briefcase next to him and sits back, gazing up at Preston Banks and another face he hadn’t expected to see quite this early. “Sean. Nice to see you, man.” Sean Price is seated across from Tyler in the limousine, also dressed impeccably in a black suit, blue dress shirt and black tie. His suit is ornated with the same cufflinks and tie clip as Tyler’s. “Tyler, what’s up?” “Trying to figure out this shit. What’s going on?” Sean shrugs and motions to Preston Banks, a small man in a simple business suit and glasses. “Dunno yet, the prick won’t tell me; said we had to wait for you. Fine with me but I’d like to know why I got a phone call at six in the damned morning.” Tyler nods. “Well, I’m here. Let’s get on with it.” Preston Banks nods and looks from Tyler to Sean. He pulls a small briefcase into his lap and pops it open and withdraws a sheet of paper. He speaks to them in his own feeble voice. “The documents we signed a couple of weeks ago with Global Wrestling Incorporated states that on the Evolution of June 16th, 2003, Roz Blayze is decreed to face Tyler Pierce. This match was granted due to the unpredictable and fully unpreventable absence from Dead Zone. Well, Salaok apparently didn’t want to give you a cruiser weight match this week.” “But he can’t do that” we have a contract.” Preston shakes his head and sighs, “Yes, he can. You see, Salaok for one keeps all power to change the card at any point in time as he sees fit. However, he was bound by written agreement that you and Roz Blayze face in a match. The agreement did not state that it had to be a singles match or for the Cruiser Weight Championship; it was just understood.” Sean glances at Tyler and then at Preston Banks. “Ok, cut the legal bullshit. What did ol” stick up the ass do?” Preston clears his throat and glances at Price. “Well, to put it bluntly, Roz Blayze and Venom Alexander, members of the newly formed Clique, will face off against Sean Price and Tyler Pierce, members of the newly unveiled TUE.” “What!”” Tyler exclaims, his anger building ? it’s evident by the twitch in his cheek. We’ve only seen it twice now, this instance and when his Corvette was totaled in Dallas just a few weeks ago. “You mean that asshole is putting us in a tag match instead of giving my damned Cruiser Weight Match?” Tyler starts to continue but Preston cuts him off nervously; “Well, Mr. Pierce. Yes. Regretfully I must say that instead of the cruiserweight title match, you and Mr. Price will be in your first official tag team competition, however?” Before he continues, Tyler slams his fist into his briefcase. “This shit isn’t right! The McCoy bitch couldn’t fix this before it hit the air” I mean fuck? how the hell are we supposed to catch a break if he’s in charge of what happens?”” Sean motions to Tyler, “Chill out, Ty. Let the man finish what he has to say” I agree that this is bullshit, you were promised that shot? but just let the man finish.” Preston smiles a bit, still anxious. “Thank you, Mr. Price. You see, Tyler. We were able to talk with Global Wrestling’s legal beagles and we worked something out. You will get your Cruiser Weight Match” it’s just being pushed back by one week. They’re saying that this card has already aired and can’t be changed without full permission from Salaok, which we won’t get.” Tyler sits back, gazing at Banks for a moment. “This guy’s going to be a thorn in our side, Sean. This is small and fixable” but what’s next? What more can he do?” Sean sits back, “I’ve seen this guy work his hands up the corporate asshole enough to know he’ll stop at nothing to tear us a new one if he wants to. But we’ll come out on top, Tyler. With Deacon, Capo, and Preston here... we have the legal backup and the physical backup. Not to mention the small army we have following us around. Salaok’s limited” this is really all he can do to us unless he fires us. Let him have his fun? let him throw these challenges our way; we’ll just take them down one by one. Prove why we’re the best? why we’re The Upper Echelon. In the end, he’ll be the one all out of cards.” There’s a moment of silence in the limo. “You practiced that didn’t you?” Sean grins a bit, “Yeah. Did I rush it” I felt like I rushed it.” Tyler shakes his head, a slight laugh coming from him. “No no, I liked it.” They laugh for a moment before Sean rings in again, “Ok, now that the pile of shit’s out of the way, let’s grab breakfast” I’m starving.” The scene fades to black as the privacy window lowers and Sean names a destination. The three continue to talk as the scene fades to black.
