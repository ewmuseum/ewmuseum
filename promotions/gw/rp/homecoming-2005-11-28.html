Homecoming

28 November 2005

Date: 11/28/05 ---- Chapter One: The Escape ---- Paperwork... such a dull, mundane, yet essential task to running a successful business. All day today had been noting but paper work, but if everything is to run as planned... this mundane task won't last for very long. Soon enough he'd have the money to hire people to sign his paperwork for him. Ah, dreaming away at what it would be like to be rich and infamous. It was almost like being a child all over again. It had been almost five minutes since he'd signed a paper. Apparently his aspirations had taken him off the task at hand. A stack of papers in front of him quickly reminded him not to count his chickens before they hatched. Paperwork... 'Could it possibly get any worse than this?' He said aloud, hoping that his question wouldn't be answered. He was disappointed again. Almost on cue the door to his office swung open. Abruptly he looked up at the man who entered. It was Logan, his head of security. The disturbed and frightened look on his face told everything. He pulled himself away from his paperwork, looking at the shock that had crossed Logan's face. It looked like he was trying to speak but he couldn't. The words were there, they just never broke the plane of his lips. 'What's the matter Logan?' He asked, becoming somewhat frightened himself. 'Maybe you were right Mr. Hathaway...' Logan paused trying to regain his calmness to rationally explain the situation at hand, 'Maybe we should've left her inside that box.' 'Why, what's wrong?' Logan reached into his pocket and pulled out the scrapnel of what was once a marvel of modern invention. Turned to ruble. Mr. Hathaway saw the damage of what was once one of his favorite devices, destoyed beyond recognition. Logan didn't have to say what had happened, both their worst fears had been realized. 'She's gone.' ---- Chapter Two: The Hunt Begins ---- Click... Click... Click... The sounds of footsteps echoed through the busy hallways. Everyone wanted a piece of their own 'destiny.' A word that seemed to be used often these days. Everyone believes that since they were born with a large frame and a big mouth they deserve something. Something is owned to them by God, Buddah, Satan, or whatever self-serving idiotic beliefs they may have. Religion was nothing more than an excuse, and excuse that people have do that they can fall asleep at night. Thinking that they couldn't kill anyone, it was the work of Satan. The Jesus would protect them against a rapist. A silly facade people put on in order to escape their own fears. If people could only see the idiotic things they do in order to live with themselves. Falling to thier knees, closing their eyes, and talking to a statue. Holding crucifixes tightly to their next in fear... as if it's going to do a damn thing when a bullet went threw their skull. Click... Click... Click... He was amazed at how these people didn't see so clearly what made them below him. They had spent their entire lives not fully accepting themselves as human beings... living in fear of some divine mythical creature that send their souls to incenerate for all of eternity in the underworld. In order to fully encompass your destiny, you must first throw your fears and weaknesses to the wayside. You must evaluate yourself and learn how you are weak, and spend every living and breathing second turing your weaknesses into your strengths. Becoming that much better than everyone else. That was the true meaning of survival of the fittest, the true meaning of what Hunt or Be Hunted stood for. Once you transcend the average... you become a God amoung men. The king of the mountain, the master of your domain, an honest to god legend. 'Right there.' Christian Zenith suddenly halted and pointed at the wall in front of him. A bare spot above the posters that lined this particular wall. Many referred to the contents of this wall as the things legends are made of. Historic moments in time that truly showed the ability of one man or woman to be head and shoulders above those to be formerly considered their peers, and become on a plane of existance of their own. Where they were worshipped by millions, statues were purchased the reinacted those very events in order to facilitate the worship of mindless sheep who'd follow them to the ends of the earth at the blink of an eye. That was power my friend. 'That's where my picture is going to be, that is where I take my spot in history not along side... but above and beyond the expectations set by those who stood here before me pointing at this wall. Those who said that they would be right there, and then did exactly that.' Zenith looked back at Vivica J. Valentine, who studied the posters on the wall... seeing the familiar faces of the 'greats' to ever grace this place, 'Angelo Deville, Doctor Curiosity, Seymour Almasy. All these men will be my competition on the road to claiming my spot of immortality. For the rest of time, idiots will marvel at the sight of whomever may grace the ring and some of the dumbest amoung them will go 'he's one of the greatest that's ever lived.' and the one beside him will go... 'but he's no Christian Zenith.'' 'This is what the thrill of competition is about...' Christian paused looking up at the spot he's already claimed as his own, 'It isn't about my shiny belt. It isn't about 'doing your best.' No one will give a damn about sportsmanship when they take a look at the records of events years from now. It's about winning, it's about power, it's about being the single greatest fighter to ever step foot in all of existance. No one can take domination away from you. This is why we are all here, this is why we play through the stupid little games of Jean Claude Dafoe, Christina Reno, and Brandi Sights. Now, let's go take our place in immortality.' Vivica J. Valentine nodded as she looked at the pictures of the former tournment champions in front of her: Angelo Deville, Doctor Curiosity, Seymour Almasy. Christian Zenith lead Vivica, Demitri Miasnikov, and Jason LeMaster into a room right past the posters. Inside a man sat behind a desk, a list in front of him... with all the eligible names to compete in this year's GTT5 tournment. He adjusted his custom black suit as he approached, a group all in black followed. The man looked up, he'd seen menacing men all day at this point in time. Although his previous encounters with Christian made him cringe, this particular day had really already been a freak show... and made Zenith look relatively, normal. 'Christian Zenith.' He said as he flipped to the last page of the paper, 'There you are... welcome to GTT5.' 'What does it stand for?' Vivica asked the man, who looked back at her surprised with a blank stare. 'Well... uh...' The man tried to hesistate as he flipped through his packet, trying to find it spelled out. It wasn't there! How could it possibly not be there, 'A long time ago it used to stand for the Golden Turnbuckle Tournment but now... we really...' 'You don't know, do you?' Vivica replied before he finished. The man quickly tried to change the subject in order to not look like the moron Vivica had already made him out to be... 'So uh... is your sister joining as well?' The man asked adjusting his collar looking up at Christian. 'Are you on something? I don't have a sister...' Vivica replied. 'No no... not you, him.' The PTC Employee said, looking over at Christian Zenith. 'I'm afraid you're mistaken.' Christian replied, 'My sister is dead, and I'd rather appreciate it if you not mention the name of that back stabbing bitch in my presence again.' 'My condolences, how long a...' The man stopped halfway through his sentance before looking up at Christian and decided it was in his better interest not to go further along with the conversation, '...well, good luck Mr. Zenith. I suppose I'll see you in the tournment.' Christian Zenith took his paper and left, with Hunting Grounds trailing him. How did that guy ever find out that he ever had a sister... she'd been dead well before he pursued a career in the fight industry. It had been a long time since Tera had legitamately crossed his mind. It was one of those chapters in his life he'd closed a long time ago. What could've possibly provoked that? Who knows... he had more important things to worry about. While Tera's remains were rotting out in the desert, he had immortality right in front of him. All he had to do was grasp it. ---- Chapter Three: Desert Rose ---- A cloud of dust sweeps across the terrain, encompassing everything that lies around. He marched along... covered in black from head to toe, having only his arms exposed as he tried desperately to follow the trail into the middle of nowhere. This had to end here and now he throught to himself, there was no other way. High above him the sun beat down like he was inside of a microwave oven. His arms were red and badly burned from the sandblasting wind which never seemed to cease, making it feel as if someone had rubbed sandpaper against the side of his neck. He cuped his hands over his eyes as he made it to the top of the dune. Squinting to see through the exhausting heat and raging winds, he thought he could see figure towards the horizon. Was it real or just a mirage? Perhaps his mind was beginning to play tricks on him. His instincts had never failed him before, why would they now. He had no choice but to trust what his eyes as he began to descend the dune towards the image. As he approached he say it was the form of a human... dressed in an all white hooded gown covereing her from head to toe. Her white gown blew violently in the breeze but she stood still, unmoving, almost unnaturally strong against the excessive winds. He fought off the terrain and stood five feet away from her, his cadet blue eyes fixed on the woman who stood motionlessly and silently right in front of him. Suddenly his sight began to grow blurry due to apprent dehydration and heat exhaustion. Under him his knees began shaking, but he stood as long as he could. The face of the woman in front of him was casted in complete darkness by the hood that hung over her head. She grasped the sides of her hood with her hands, and slowly began to pull the hood back to expose ger face. He waiting anxiously to see whom it was he was following, perhaps she could answer the questions. How he got here, why he was following her. Nothing added up, nothing logically made any sense at all. Her white face glistened under the sun, unblemished from the violent desert. Dark brown hair blew in the wind like a flag. Suddenly she opened her cadet blue eyes with a scowl... no... it couldn't be... Christian Zenith's eyes shot open and she slowly sat up in his bed. Rubbing his arms feeling around for irritation he felt nothing... same with his neck. Half-awake his eyes navigated around the room. It's safe... he's safe. It felt so real though. 'It wasn't real.' He said aloud looking down at the bed sheets that surround him, 'She's gone, she's dead rotting in the desert. She's not coming back, it's over.' It was that stupid PTC Employee who took his name earlier that day. Yeah, that was it. By telling him he had a sister he'd simply brought her image to the forefront. That was simple enough. Now it was time to return to a peaceful sleep, and make a mental note to give that PTC Employee the most traumatizing experience he'd ever had in his entire life for reminding him of her. Problem was... he never did return to a peaceful sleep that night. She was in his dreams... waiting for him to come back. ---- Chapter Four: A Little Bit Of History Repeating ---- 'Why are we going to Tokyo now? We have a couple days... and I hate flying fucking commercial airlines.' Vivica J. Valentine spoke over the airport commute. Christian Zenith peered back at her through his black Oakley Sunglasses. He didn't say a word as they marched forward on the blue carpet. People glared at Christian Zenith, Vivica J. Valentine, Demitri Miasnikov, and Jason LeMaster as they made their way over to the flight to Los Angeles that connect to the flight to Tokyo they needed to catch. 'It's best we get there a couple days early...' Christian responded, 'Our pilot is gone for the weekend, and I'd like to get away from the business for a few days to train for GTT5.' That's what he told Vivica anyway... truth of the matter is, there was no way Tera Zenith was ever going to be seen or mentioned in Japan. Perhaps a vacation was what the doctor ordered afterall. Zenith tuned out the world around him as they made their way towards the airport terminal. There wasn't anything in the world that would stop him from leaving the country now. Easy Sailing. Suddenly Vivica J. Valentine came to a hault. Demitri and Jason stopped with her as Christian Zenith continued to walk, before he realized he lost his friends back in front of a television. Zenith turns around and starts walking back to his teammates glued to the television in front of them. 'We have a plane to catch gu...' Suddenly Christian Zenith was cut off by the sound of a news report on CNN. 'This just in ladies and gentlemen, early this morning two african american men were found shot and killed on the streets of Chicago two blocks west of the Zenith Corporation Headquaters. Both deaths are reported to be a single shot in the head, supposibly from above.' The woman reporter continued on, 'Police have said that this more recent murder has very clear indications linking these to the sniper style killings that plagued Chicago around two years ago. The Die Erntemaschine Murders.' 'No...' Christian said out loud as the report continued on. 'These murders were tried back in 2002, with the suspect being the owner of Zenith Corporation... Christian Ulrich Zenith. During the trial it became apparent that the tip to police was made by an anonymous source. Christian Zenith on the other hand was found innocent of all charges. Late last night when the shootings occured, Christian Zenith was suspected to be at PrimeTime Central Headquarters, far away from the incident. Yet the police would like to have a few words with Mr. Zenith, and would like you to dial the number at the bottom of the screen if you know anything about his whereabouts.' Christian looked at the screen emotionlessly as the people around him watched the horribly history begin to unfold once again. The dreams... the murders... could they all be connected in someway? Was someone trying to send him a message? Or was it destiny? 'Get the pilot...' Christian said looking back at Vivica from under his Oakley sunglasses, 'We're going back to Chicago.' ---- TO BE CONTINUED IN GTT5...
