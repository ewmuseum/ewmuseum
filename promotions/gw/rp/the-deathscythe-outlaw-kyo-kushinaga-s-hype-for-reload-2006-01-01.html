'The Deathscythe Outlaw' Kyo Kushinaga's Hype for Reload

1 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/1/06 The camera fades in what looks like to be a large land area on a dark cloudy night. There stands a single structure far away in the area. Camera slowly moves closer to get a better view of the mysterious structure? ...He came to 4 Winds Wrestling as a defender of justice and peace? Shows a black and white clip of a man; a Japanese man in black with a black metal mask that completes his sleek superhero attire steps out from behind the curtains with millions and millions of people cheering and chanting one single word to him, “Avenger”. Closer? “He fought and vanquished many arch villains” Many clips show of The Masked Avenger demonstrating his superb martial arts skill on the many villains in the 4WW roster. Closer? “He became a real hero by vanquishing one of his most worthy villain and ended the villain’s reign as West Wind Champion” This clip shows is the Flaming Hell in a Cell match for the West Wind Title at a StormFront event which shows the referee raising one of The Masked Avenger’s burnt hand, as he was proclaimed as the new champion after defeating “The Extreme Bastard” Fnord. Avenger having a crimson mask over his metal mask took his newly acquired belt and slowly held it above his head, as he fell to his knees and clutched it to his chest. Closer? “The great hero soon met his untimely end” The next clip is of the infamous King of the Hill match for the West Wind Title at a StormFront event which shows the monstrous Tarrant delivering a vicious chokeslam on The Masked Avenger off the side rope. Avenger fall many stories down until he crashed on many hard wooden tables that was underneath the suspended ring. Adding further insult, after Tarrant connected with the Destiny’s End on the barely conscious Avenger, he took off the Avenger’s mask and crumbled it, then threw on the dying chest of the fallen hero. Closer? “The Outlaw was born” This clip shows dark figures with lit torches in their hands that stood on opposite sides of the ramp that was covered in thick smoke, as the chilling Deathscythe Outlaw Kyo Kushinaga slowly walking down ramp with death in his eyes. Closer? “The Outlaw unleashed hell and carnage to all” Shows random clips of the Deathscythe Outlaw performing cruel acts of brutality to the 4WW superstars that includes him connecting with a vicious chokeslam on Kris Kanton, connecting not only one but two superkicks to Patti Anderson-McClancy, his most legendary act of nailing and crucifying Tarrant on a large crow steel form that hung high over the Battle Dome, and many more. Closer? “With all the Outlaw’s hell and carnage he won one championship” The next clip is the legendary Battle Dome match for the West Wind Title that happened at Maelstrom IV: Destiny which shows the referee raising the hand of the blood covered Deathscythe Outlaw, as he was proclaimed as the West Wind Champion for a second time. Outlaw grabs hold of his West Wind Title, as he climbs up to the nearest turnbuckle and holds up his title with a crucified Tarrant hanging in the background. Getting closer? “Another championship” This clip is the three-way match for the North Wind Title that happened at Maelstrom V: Winds of War which shows the Deathscythe Outlaw being proclaimed as the North Wind Champion. Getting closer? “And another championship” Clip is the tag team ladder match for the East Wind Titles that happened at a StormFront event which shows the Deathscythe Outlaw was holding onto the twin titles, as he soon unhooked them and becomes one half of the East Wind Champions. Getting closer? “But it all soon ends again” The final clip is the infamous North Wind Title match that happened at Maelstrom VIII: Down “n” Dirty which shows 4WW superstar, Shawn Singleton burying the Deathscythe Outlaw and another alive. Camera is now at the standing structure. Still couldn’t make out what it was due to being too dark. The clouds soon slowly drift away, letting in some light from the full moon that hangs high in the dark night sky. It is reveal that the structure is none other then a stone headstone with an engraved crow along with a name on it. The name is of the Deathscythe Outlaw’Kyo Kushinaga. Out of nowhere, a black boot stands on the top of the headstone? The camera moves its? view up and reveals a familiar man who is dressed in black, as he lights a cigarette that rests in his mouth with his silver lighter. He takes a deep drag from his cigarette and sighs out a long flowing cloud of nicotine in a blissful manner. The man turns and looks at the camera, as he tilts his head to the side, curling his lips in a sarcastic smirk. “What” You thought I was dead?” “January 11th marks the return of the Deathscythe Outlaw” “He won’t be unleashing his wrath in a four-sided ring this time around” “He will be unleashing his entire wrath in a Roped Octagon” Kyo kicks over his headstone. “No Graveyards” The night sky gets even more clearer which makes more light from the bright moon and shows that the entire area looks like a war has just happened with destroyed cars with some skeletons inside a couple of the cars, crumbling and collapsing buildings, and the ground charred beyond recognition. “There’s only the Battlefield” “He will make Global his Battlefield” “At Reload” Without another thought, Kyo swiftly turns around and kicks the camera. The camera falls, revealing the lens is all crack up, but it is still on, as it is looking up at the standing Kyo. “The battle has begun. There will be casualties.” Kyo grins. “Eighteen of them?” Camera quickly shorts out.
