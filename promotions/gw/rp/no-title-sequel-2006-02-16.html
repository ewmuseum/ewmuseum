No Title (Sequel)

16 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Febuary 16 2006 On his way to dinner when it took him by surprise, And with one pull of the trigger he would vanish overnight. Dancing by the roadside, holding on for dear life. The a gun from out of nowhere made a widow of his wife ? Ozzy Osbourne I awoke from the sounds of pouring rain, I heard the wind whispering me in the ear urging me to arise from my sub conscious prison, a cold feeling enveloped my body as I laid motionless on the water saturated trail, grains of gravel pierced though my back like needles in a pin cushion as my blue eyes slowly opened. A dark figure stood over me as my pupils struggled to focus upon the figure, a woman?...No’a man stood over me, I once again closed my eyes tight and opened them back to trying to force myself to see “Hey’are you alright?” A voice exclaimed as I sat up and wiped the gravel from my arms, a sharp pain stuck me in the hand as I turned it over palm up to find my hand coated in a crimson red, I slung my hand away from me and watched splatters of blood fly from my finger tips as I arose back to my feet. Stumbling as if I was an inebriated bum, I felt the hand of the man place his warm hand upon my shoulders, bewilderment, embarrassment’I turned and shrugged the mans hand from my shoulder as I looked at him “It’s ok..im fine” I said quietly, I don’t know why I was embarrassed but I was, I guess its due to the fact that I fell’On second glance this looked to be a younger man close to the same age as me, with his hair a scruffy golden blonde and a pair of sideburns that need to be cut, but his baby blue eyes had the glint of wisdom as he stood there opposite of me on the narrow running trail. The rain seems to have subsided for the most part, but the sky was darker than it was before, I could picture the sun setting behind the clouds in my mind as I raised my hand to my aching neck and tilted my chin up and saw a few birds passing over under the grey canopy of the rain clouds “Are you sure?” He questioned to me in a concerned tone as I watched the birds disappear into the distance’wishing I was one of those birds flying away right now, I looked back down to the man and simply nodded my head and turned away from him “Yea’thanks anyways’brah” I said to the guy giving him my salutations as I walked away. The pain in my hand has reached a scratching, irritating feeling as I looked down and pressed my blood glossed hand against my wet shirt, staining it with a smeared hand print “I’m the only one that can help you now, I know what you want” Memories, confusion as I heard the words from the fellow reach my ears’who was he talking about? I needed to find out’I needed to know, I stopped dead in my tracks and spun around with a question on the tip of my tongue “What do?” Nothing but a narrow winding trail, the man was gone, where could he have gone’all these questions surged though my brain as the last few words slipped out “You want”...” A gust of wind blew the first fallen red and brown leaves of Autumn across the trail as I stood there holding my bleeding hand with the tail of my shirt awaiting an answer’but none came The walk back to my car took a lot longer than it took me to run to my destination with the strange man, by this time nightfall has set in, the air was chilled, the ground was wet with dew drops littering the green grass around my car, their was an eerie felling in the air, I didn’t know exactly what it was but I could feel it, combined with the chirping of crickets, the sounds of tree frogs, and the songs of hoot owls’the symphony brought a completion to this night that just didn’t seem right. What was left of the black rain clouds have now blended with the starlit sky silhouetting a patchwork portrait of the heavens above me. The full moon’s light shown half heartedly through the mask of clouds as I made my way around to the driver side door of my Black Chevy Camero. I placed my hand upon the door handle as I felt a chill run up my spine’the feeling of someone watching me sent me into a state of paranoia as I let go of the handle and turned around’no one there’or was their? I focused on a point in the thick timber awaiting the slightest movement from something behind the trees’I quickly shook my head and snapped myself out of my self induced coma as I turned back around, heart racing, adrenaline pumping, I pulled the door open and got in as quick as possible, hastily shutting the door once inside. I grabbed the steering wheel with my hand while keeping my other clutched in a fist on my leg, reaching over to the passenger seat I picked up a white cloth and wrapped it around my blood ridden hand as I pushed the clutch in with my foot and turned over the key. The massive three fifty engine fired right up, I reached up with my now bandaged left hand and took hold of the wheel, wincing in a pain as I felt it shoot up my arm, I slid the shifter into first and headed to my house. The road was dark and desolate, same as inside my car save for the dashboard lights and the radio’Invisible Man’by Theory of a Dead man quietly pulsated from the car speakers as I listened *Haven't seen me lately Staying hard to find Well I know it ain't easy But you know I don't mind I somewhat sang along to the words that I knew as I thumped my thumb on the steering wheel, I pressed down a little harder on the accelerator with my foot, the inertia pressed me back in my seat putting tension on my neck as I looked down and watched the tachometer move upwards from sixty to eighty, and on a wet paved road that wasn’t very smart on my part but I didn’t care *I always know where you are, you never know where I am You got me sneaking around like the invisible man You'll never know where I've gone, you've gone and done it again you've got me sneaking around like the invisible man going once, going twice, fuck this I'm gone Headlights in the distance as I looked at them staring back at me like a pair of illuminated eyes, a trance, a zombified state I found myself in as I glared into the lights, a snap, a quick break in reality, as I shook myself out of it and yawned, I must be getting tired I thought to myself as I continued my journey to my house *You've been looking for days now Oh but I'm not around I've been finding new ways how To see that I'm never found I glanced down I was approaching ninety miles an hour as I looked back to the road a figure stood before me, as my head lights illuminated his body, I recognized him, the man, from the trail, what was he doing in the road? I can’t stop? “Fuck?” I shouted out in terror as I slammed the breaks, a tail spin, a loss of control, smell of burning rubber, my eyes locking onto his as I cut the wheel, my car cairned off the road. My body shook and tossed inside the car as I tried to regain control of my vehicle, I looked up through the windshield’a tree’I can’t stop **Fade Out Fade In** I awoke from the sounds of pouring rain, I heard the wind whispering me in the ear urging me to arise from my sub conscious prison, a cold feeling enveloped my body as I laid motionless on the water saturated trail, grains of gravel pierced though my back like needles in a pin cushion as my blue eyes slowly opened. A dark figure stood over me as my pupils struggled to focus upon the figure, a woman?...No’a man stood over me, I once again closed my eyes tight and opened them back to trying to force myself to focus “What in the hell happened to you?” A voice exclaimed as I sat up and wiped the gravel from my arms, a sharp pain stuck me in the hand as I turned it over palm up to find my hand coated in a crimson red, I slung my hand away from me and watched splatters of blood fly from my finger tips as I arose back to my feet. Stumbling as if I was an inebriated bum, I felt the hand of the man placed his warm hand upon my shoulders, bewilderment, embarrassment’I turned and shrugged the mans hand from my shoulder as I looked at him “Johnny?” It was Cruse, my good buddy..how did he find me? What the hell happened to me? Was it Dejavoo? I was just in a car wreck? No’a dream? If it was a dream..it was a fucked up dream at that “What happened to you Jay?” Cruse asked me, my mind was still spinning, I couldn’t shake the cobwebs as I looked at him “Man..I will tell you on the way’how did you find me?” Jay put his hand on my shoulder as I asked him and we began to walk down the trail “I’m your manager” Or did that slip your mind to?” Cruse said to me as we walked along the trail, I shot a glace back to see if their was anyone behind us “But..you were just in my dream and I didn’t know who you were’it was wicked strange I tell you brah” I said to Cruse, I couldn’t get my mind off that dream as I racked my brain trying, wanting, to remember “Haha’I want some of whatever you have been taking bro” Cruse said to me as I rubbed the back of my head and kicked a few rocks on our venture up the trail
