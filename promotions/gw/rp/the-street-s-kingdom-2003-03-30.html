The street's Kingdom Quartz Global Wrestling Promo #47 Date: 3/30/03 Location: The streets A long long time ago, Quartz never existed. Which is a good thing, as a world other than today’s never could have survived the giant. Only in a world of hate, violence and evil running wild, could a beast like Quartz manage to go by without being noticed by all that see him. A world that was as full of corruption as he was. A world that was as full of hate and the love of violence that filled Quartz's mind. Quartz was truly at home in this world. And with this world came the people that went in it which was the only problem. A world that held his son. A son in which he had learnt to hate over time. A son in which he vowed to protect with his life, and yet now he finds himself standing face to face with him as they trade punches. For a man full of hate, he never expected his son to turn his back on him. On the Nevermore and the combined men of The Harvesters. A group which he had only just been accepted into before he turned his back on his father and those around him. But now Quartz had a purpose. Had a reason for his existence. The Lair had gained even more power over the past few months due to business deals being done everyday. The men behind Omni-tech were no longer a threat to The Lair's organized crime and no longer would Omni-tech be a business threat. With everything in the world wrong again, Quartz still finds himself wondering through the streets. His long black hair hanging down over his face, hiding his identity and flowing in the breeze which had become a part of life in the big city. The giant buildings constructed in a grid pattern throughout the city area didn't allow for the free movement of even the smallest breeze. Therefore, no matter how soft the breeze, it would always turn into something stronger as it is forced to squeeze between the walls of the giant structures of the skyscraping buildings. The city no longer had color to it, only gray. Everything here was dead. Breathing, yes. But where the people who moved throughout the passages of the streets really alive? Did they live the life they wanted or were they simply stuck behind desks, watching as one piece of paper is replaced by another. Watching as the news appears on their computer screens and telling them of death and decay. Reports about crime taking control of the city and wrapping it's death like hands around the necks of those who try to stop it. This was the home that The Lair had created. This was the place for Quartz. No one would look you in the eye. No one would say 'Hello'. No one would care anymore if they passed an alleyway where a man was having death beaten into him. Yes, this was the world for Quartz. And yet, the streets never truly belonged to The Lair. The men who struggled through their life belonged to a different world faction. A faction that was known for not caring and simply sitting back and watching. These were the lost souls of the Kingdom. A kingdom of lost souls and minds that read the world differently. Eyes that looked at how the world behaved and found it easier simply to turn their back and not care. Or at least their eyes didn't used to care. Now they watch. Their pitiful existence had found life in this world. They had found something to believe in. They had found new light in the Kingdom that was described as tragic. Would they truly ever cause a problem? Would they some day rise up and take control? Not likely. Not in Quartz's mind they wouldn't. Alone is how they fought. There was never any unity and that would be how The Lair came to control them the first time. The time before they got away. His trench coat flowing behind him, almost cape like as people walk past. Their eyes not turning up to meet his as he looks down on them. Even the skies above were gray. No life here. The Kingdom was there, but weren't strong enough yet. The one who had stood up for them had left them behind. He had stopped trying it would seem. Happy to disappear like he told his people to fight again. Happy to spend his life looking for a love that he drove away. In a world of despair and gray, there is no color. In a world of hate and violence, there is no symbol of love. But somehow, in the world of the kingdom, in the world of Vincent, there was but one symbol. A symbol which was once his and now is no ones. Quartz stood there looking down at the concrete where he had stopped. An old quote had been changed for the times of today. 'The world is but a rose, and we are but petals. Huddled in together, knowing should we break free, we would surely die.' The reason for the kingdom of the street, no longer having direction was right there on the ground. The quote meant nothing, and yet held one word that meant so much to man one. With Vincent, still lost in the world of Rose, he knew that the Kingdom would never rise against them. Not unless someone where to bring it to life once more. Looking up from the concrete and the quote, Quartz looks over to the park. Even the green of the park's grass seemed gray and lifeless. And there in the park was all that the kingdom still held. A man, wearing jeans and a black Pearl Jam T-shirt. His long blonde hair, messy and yet neat at the same time as it is tucked behind his ears, with a swipe of his hand. Happy to not be noticed and yet no longer wanting to disappear into the nothingness that the rest of the world had. If only there was a way to gain control of the Kingdom's army. If only. Quartz knew where the key to the Kingdom's army lay. It lay in the mind of Vincent.
