The Loose Ends

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.26.03 (Dead Zone) Saturday, May 23rd, Dallas, Texas. The scene opens to the inside of a suite in The Adolphus Hotel in Dallas, Texas. The room is as elegant as the last time we saw it, very little having changed. Two major changes are apparent from the last time in which it was shown to us: The towel hanging at the foot of the bed has disappeared as has the beautiful brunette who once sat near the towel, brushing her hair monotonously. Strangely enough, without this beautiful add-on, the room has lost much of its luster; and probably most of the male audience. Remnants of her stay have been left about the room, the brush in which she was using has been placed perfectly aligned on the night stand aside the bed. Suddenly, we hear the voice of Tyler Pierce coming from off camera. “What do you mean we’re meeting up tonight” Isn’t that kind of short notice, Capo?” Tyler Pierce walks into view, his light brown hair is parted and hangs down, covering a little of his face. He turns towards us and something catches a glimpse of light; an eyebrow piercing on his right eyebrow glimmers of an exquisite gold. “The others are already on their way” Eesh, Capo? why can’t you warn a guy in advance?” The sound comes from the receiver of the phone, loud and sudden. Pierce flinches a little as it does so and changes his somewhat condescending tone instantaneously. He speaks again, his eyes quickly shifting about the room. The cell phone remains next to his ear, a golden ring, a new addition, has been placed on his finger; a crest of some sort, the base of the center being white and the crest itself black; a diamond is in the center of the crest. “Yeah yeah, give me a little while. I’ve gotta change.” Pierce turns, and walks towards a built in, oak closet and opens it. His eyes feasting on the contents inside; a wardrobe of clothing hangs before him, his for the choosing. With a simple gesture, a nod, he flips the silver phone shut and sends it sailing through the air, spinning in its gravity defying motion until it lands, safely and gently atop the bed. Tyler is quick to remove his shirt, a black polo shirt accented in red, and drops it onto the floor. We fade to black as he continues. The image returns to the screen in full Technicolor just moments later; the same closet door stands before us, its doors open but Tyler no longer stands before it. Seconds pass and the door just two or three feet aside the closet opens and Tyler steps from within the room, now dressed elegantly. He stands before us in a black suit ornamented with golden cufflinks and a golden tie clip. The ring remains on his finger, the crest of which is also an embellishment on each of the cufflinks. He wears a white shirt underneath, flawlessly ironed and added. In fact, not a single flaw remains in his attire, even his shoes glimmer in the light as do his ring and cufflinks. Pierce’s hair is now combed back, giving him a slightly more serious look, truth be told, the only aspect of the Pierce that stands before us that would separate him from any other, run of the mill, white collar worker is the single golden piercing in his right eyebrow. Tyler steps back in front of the mirror, quickly adjusting the tie that needs no adjustment. Perfection is the key at this moment and he is a perfect exemplar of it. We watch from behind, viewing his back and his front via the mirror. A smirk crosses his face as he pulls a pair of custom Armani shades from an inside pocket. “The difference between me and them.” He slips the glasses on, smiling now as the thought of his phrase catches hold of his sense of humor. “I make this look good.” With a quick flash of a smile, Tyler turns and starts towards the night stand. He reaches down, grabs his wallet, a set of keys, and a watch. He quickly snaps the watch onto his wrist and adjust it appropriately. “Better make sure my ride is here?” With the thought, Tyler reaches for and takes the cell phone in hand. Before he can even flip it open, the ring sounds announcing a new call. With a smirk and a flip of his wrist, the cell opens and the call is answered. He holds the phone to his ear and smirks. “You’re downstairs” You guys sure know your shit. Alright. Be right down.” With this, he walks towards the door and brings the key from inside his pocket. He flips the light switch, cutting the lights to the suite and steps out into the hallway. Within seconds, the door has been locked and Tyler is on his way towards the elevator. He presses the button and slips his hands into his coat pockets, waiting quietly. The elevator doors open and the same bellboy from a few days earlier begins to walk out backwards, pulling a cart. Tyler steps to the side and smiles at the gentlemen, holding the door for him. “Thank you.” He speaks as he notices the hand holding the door. Pierce leans in quietly, speaking aloud to him. “You’re very welcome.” The bellboy jumps and trips over his own feet, falling flat on his rear before the Prodigal Pierce. He smirks and holds back a snicker before stepping into the elevator and allowing the doors to close. The scene fades to black as the bellboy stands and dusts himself off. The scene fades in again, this time to the backseat of a plush limousine. Tyler Pierce is seated on the leather seat, his legs crossed with his black wing tipped shoe bouncing slightly with the beat of the music in the limo. Eminem’s Solider plays inside the limousines passenger area. The plexi-glass window between the passenger side and the driver side is up, putting Tyler in complete solitaire. A hand falls to the side and lands atop the small bar placed near the back wall. The fingers on Tyler’s hand begin to rise and fall, one after the other in perfect timing and rhythm; one and then another, one and then another. The drumming becomes repetitive in the room, the sound not mixing with the music pulsing within the prison of tinted glass and steel. Suddenly Price turns and pulls down part of the seat nearest him to reveal a laptop mounted on the back of it. He flips the screen up and powers the computer on, his eyes waiting. A second passes. “Pleas identify yourself.” Tyler, with precision responds to the mechanical female voice by typing his last name into the computer via the keyboard: Pierce, Tyler. A moment passes the cybernetic voice speaks again, this time asking a somewhat different question. “Please authenticate yourself, Mr. Pierce.” Within a second, Pierce has begun to enter his pincode, his fingers flying over both the alpha keypad and the numeric keypad. The letters and numbers appear as asterisks until finally, Pierce hits the enter key changing the screen to black. A moment passes. “Welcome, Mr. Pierce.” The mainframe appears on this highly protected and networked laptop. Within a few clicks, we are seeing a folder complete with photos of all of the men set to face off against one another in the upcoming twelve man battle royal, excluding Pierce himself. Each photo has a name below it and a small bar to the side of that ranging from green on one side to red on the other. The first two men, the only ones whose bar is in red, is Ben J. Pailthorpe and Derek Frost. Following behind him are Kid Flash, Ace Cash, Roz Blayze, and Max Roswell all in no particular order. “Hmm.” Tyler quickly places his finger on the touch screen and chooses Pailthorpe. Once again, the computer screen darkens and the word “Processing” appears. Another second passes and suddenly, a file folder window opens including a biography type report, a moving window off to the side complete with match clips, and a mug shot type photo near the top left hand corner of the screen. Tyler smirks, his eyes wandering over each of the facts about his soon to be in ring nemesis. Tyler reaches to the side and grasps a glass, bringing it to his lips and sipping from it as he reads, his eyes examining every word on the page. His every thought focusing on Pailthorpe until “Music off.” The words spout from the lips of Tyler suddenly, coming from nowhere. The voice activated system does it magic, cutting the CD off and restoring silence to the limousine. Tyler replaces the glass, his eyes continuing to read over the information of the man he will soon be facing. “Interesting fucker, aren’t you Benny?” He continues, his lips mouthing the word “champion.” Pierce sighs and places his hand to the screen again, hitting a small “Folder Top” icon near the bottom of the screen. Suddenly the original folder view comes up, showing the eleven other competitors. With quick precision, he moves his finger over, touching the pixilated image of Derek Frost. The screen again goes black and then returns, displaying the same type of information about Derek Frost. “Frost” What is this, a Blade or a Batman rip-off?” He continues reading, his eyes scanning down the liquid crystal displays projection of words, his mind taking the information and understanding it. His eyes continue, never stopping as he reads. “Shouldn’t be a problem” as long as he doesn’t bubble up like a balloon or try and suck my blood, we’ll be fuckin? peachy.” With a few quick movements, Pierce lowers the folder and returns to the initial page. His eyes dart over the words, his eyes moving from face to face. The question is there: Who is next? Which one should we study now? Pierce is quick and precise, his fingers moving over each face. “Duck” duck? duck’duck?” His fingers walk over the screen without touching it, his eyes studying each face. He speaks softly still. “duck” duck?” He finally grins to himself. “Green-haired goose.” The choice has been made and within seconds, the screen turns black. Another few seconds pass and the screen reloads, showing the mug shot, video capture, and rough bio of one Ace Cash. “Colorful fuck aren’t ya” Let’s see what your deal is, boyo.” With his fingers walking down the page, he reads to himself silently. His eyes scroll the words, stopping at “Cruiserweight Champion” for a moment before continuing. He smirks to himself, reading a few details of Cash’s illustrious career in Global Wrestling. “Cash has been reported as being snobby, self righteous, arrogant, obnoxious, and overbearing.” A pause is taken after these words as Pierce examines the paragraph alone. “Not a bad guy really. Too bad all I care about him for now is seeing his ass on the outside of the ring at Dead Zone.” He smirks again, touching the LCD monitor at the “Top Folder” icon again. “Not impressed so far. What’s next?” Tyler’s eyes continue over the page, giggling as he notes Logan Creed is amongst the participants. The childish game of choosing his next topic pursues once again. “Duck” duck? duck? duck?” He touches the screen, choosing Kid Flash quickly and surely. “Goose.” He smiles, watching the screen as the information loads. A smirk crosses his face again as he reads, his eyes wandering the text aimlessly. His thoughts unknown as he reads over the valued information before him. “Aren’t you a clich” kid.” The words have no explanation, our imaginations are our only ability to tell where this came from. He reads on and quickly clicks back to the main page and then another face, this time?. The face of Roz Blayze. “Blayze eh” Interesting character you are.” Pierce sits back, again taking the glass in hand and sipping at the unknown contents. His eyes pan over the screen as he replaces the glass and leans in a bit, his eyes panning over the page quietly. His sense of humor has left him for the moment? or has his simply ceased his attempts to amuse himself? Either way, Blayze’s profile rests before him on the screen and he reads on quietly. Finally he speaks with a tone to his voice. “Blayze eh” Cute. Just another face, another three count, another paralyzed victim of the Prodigal.” The words have no humor, just a business like sense. It’s as if Tyler Pierce, the playful jester who we’ve been watching has suddenly changed into a business man, a man who knows how to and when to get things done. A man who will stop at nothing to get things done. And that’s what he is? a man with a goal, deadset on victory. Nothing will stand in his way. Not today? not tomorrow? not at Dead Zone? not ever. The driver’s separation glass lowers and Pierce glances towards it. The chauffeur’s voice comes from the other side, the words summing up Pierce’s homework. “We’re here, Sir.” With no response, Tyler closes the laptop. The mechanical voice is heard again as he closes the seat up, hiding away the computer once again and returning the vehicle to normal. He grabs his glass and downs the rest of its contents before placing the glass inside of a drawer in the built-in bar. “Now powering off. Good bye, Mr. Pierce.” The door opens and he slides out of the limo and as he stands, slips his sunglasses onto his face. The scene pans around now, showing three other limousines parked in front of the one that Pierce stood out of. Another man stands next to the door, a large man also dressed in a black suit, black dress shirt and black tie. The only colored object on his attire is a red handkerchief neatly folded and placed in his coat pocket. The man is bald and stands at approximately 6’2, 250 pounds. He’s a brooding individual as well as an armed one, we note the handle of a stun baton protruding from the beneath his suit coat. We continue panning and from a new angle, notice a black earpiece in the ear of the large man. Pierce glances around and then looks forward just as the scene pans to show his view; a large tinted glass building in the middle of the city stands before us. Pierce, with a quick tug on his suite starts his ascension on the steps towards the building. One he reaches the top of the steps, he steps inside. We fade to black.
