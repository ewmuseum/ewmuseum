Talking things out

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/06/04 'Life is such bullshit sometimes Harv' a clearly distraught and confused Donny J McNasty tells to his long time friend and business partner Hardcore Harv. Harv nods and smiles as he understands what Donny is going through inside his mind. Donny's first title defense for the Platinum Title is against none other than his good friend in GW Brick and the biggest rising star in GW Pendragon. 'I'm a man of respect Harv, I respect my friend and tag partner Brick and well to be perfectly honest I have nothing against Pendragon. Whose idea was this shit? I haven't been able to sleep real well at night since the matches were announced.' Harv knows how much this is eating Donny up inside, Donny hasn't been himself all week, he doesn't want to hang out at his own bar, he's been distant with Alexis and the man won't even think about picking up a golf club. Harv talks to Donny. 'Look, Donny there is not a damn thing you can do about it. You just have to go out there and give it your all. I don't think Brick will hold it against you if you beat him in your title defense. Have you even talked to him about it?' Donny looks at Harv and Harv already knows the answer that Donny is about to give. 'No, I just don't feel right about calling him about all of this. I mean your right about it, he's not going to care about me beating him to retain the title. I just don't feel right having to face him. If it was me one on one with Pendragon it would be a different story, I wouldn't feel bad about fighting him, me and him aren't friends, we're competitors. It's a different story' Harv knows how difficult it would be especially if it was him facing the same situation. He also knows there is another factor involved here and that's what Alexis thinks about all of this. 'Has Alexis said anything to you about it?' Donny shakes his head yes and goes into greater detail about what she said. 'Basically all she said was that no matter what happens that she would be proud of me regardless. She told me that in the situation that Brick wins, don't hold it against him, and if Pendragon wins, go back after that belt. She says I just need to loosen up' Harv seeing that Alexis who may come off as a ditz has her highlights sometimes and nailed it right on the head on what Donny needs to do. 'Donny, I know you love her, and I know you listen to her sometimes. Now is one of those times you must shut up and listen. I'm not being mean or cold, but you need to loosen up and just take it as it all comes. You have the ability and the brains to do it. This is going to be one hell of a match and the fans are going to love it. Don't think about Brick, don't think about the belt and don't think about yourself. Think about the fans and go out there in that arena and deliver. Give them what they want, and that's 110%.' Donny looks at Harv as they arrive at the Airport, and shakes his head in agreement. 'Harv I think you got a point there. I have to be me. Me and Brick will be friends no matter the outcome and I can take care of Pendragon and I will definatley not dissapoint the fans.' Harv smiles as Donny parks his 2005 Black Chevy Monte Carlo SS. They both get out the car, Donny goes to the trunk and gets his bags out, shakes Harv's hand and gives him the keys to the car. 'Alright Harv, take care of the bar for me, and I'll be back in town by Friday' 'You got it Boss' Donny walks into the airport terminal and begins his trip to Dalls. Harv gets back in the car, drives off knowing he just gave Donny the shot of courage that he needed.
