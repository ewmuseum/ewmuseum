After the tornado

16 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/16/04 It was a hard night for Brick and Jesse Briggs, to say the least. They were competing in a tag team tornado match against Terminator and Pitbull Pete Hardy. The previous week, Terminator and Hardy brutally assaulted them during their Street Fight. This week, they put whatever differences they had aside to deal with the two returning wrestlers. Brick’s issues with Terminator were a thing of GW history. Briggs’ current issues with Jesse Briggs are relatively new. In any event, Brick and Briggs attained a victory over the two, but not without consequences. Brick’s shoulder was injured and made worse by repeated attacks on it by both Terminator and Hardy. A foiled diving attack led to Terminator giving Briggs a Termination through a table. However, while Terminator was distracted, Brick gave Hardy a Bricklayer through a table and got the 1-2-3. After the match, a fight occurred between the two that took a large number of officials and referees to break, in spite of their relatively weakened conditions. Eventually, the referees and officials did manage to break up the battle. Terminator and Hardy were escorted backstage in order to prevent any further altercations. Brick made his way over to where his partner lay amidst the broken pieces of wood and metal. “Jesse?” he said. Meanwhile, down the ramp came Ely Braxton. Brick knelt down and started to rouse Briggs, why Braxton made his way over. “Hey, Jesse, come on!” Brick kept nudging his unconscious body until finally he started to groan. “Jesse!” “Did we get them sumbitches?” he asked, groggily. “Yeah, we got him. Got a piece of Terminator afterwards, too.” “Come on,” said Braxton. “Let’s go on back.” Brick attempted to help Briggs to his feet, when Briggs waved him off. “Get off me, dammit! I can do it myself!” Briggs slowly sat up and then went to one knee and slowly rose to his feet. “Good match, you two.” “Yeah, you didn’t have to compete in it.” “You know this thing between you and Terminator ain’t done yet.” “Tell me something I don’t know.” Yes, the conflict between Brick and Terminator was far from over. The next time the two met, it would probably take more than a handful or referees and officials. “OK, who’s up for headin’ down to the Dark Horse for a few drinks?” “Some drinks sound good right about now. How about you, Jesse?” Briggs moved rather slowly, still sore from the Termination through the table. “Yeah, whatever.” The three men made their way up the ramp towards the backstage area, followed by the referees and officials. “Y’all don’t have to follow so damn close, you know.” “Christina’s orders. She figured that somethin’ might rise if you and Terminator ran into each other backstage.” “Yeah, well, she was right.” “Will you two just shut the hell up?” Briggs exclaimed as he slowly walked a step behind them. Brick and Braxton just looked at each other and said in unison, “Same old Jesse.” The whole time they made their way backstage, there were no altercations between Brick and Terminator(much to both Brick’s delight and chagrin), or between Briggs and Hardy. The three could enjoy the rest of the night. Another day would be different.
