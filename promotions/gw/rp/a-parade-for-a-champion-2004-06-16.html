A Parade for a Champion

16 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/16/04 Sitting reclined against a solid blood red love seat, J-Gonz sits with his hands stretched out in his white suit, with blue vest and blue tie. The Global Wrestling Television Title sits beside him foled up. 'Mamasita! Look what papi brought home.' Rasberry walks into the room wearing a short pink dress, he blonde hair is pulled up. She walks over to J-Gonz who is smiling. 'I told the world I would do it babe, I told you I would do it and look what I did!' 'We should celebrate baby! We need to let the world know that J-Gonz is for real!' 'Hmm....that's a nice idea mamasita, but what can we do?' J-Gonz claps his hands and in walk in Paco and Chocolate. Both men are adourned in there solid black suits. 'Que pasa boss?' 'I need an idea boys and I need you guys to help me. You see J-Gonz won the Television Title and we need to celebrate.' 'Well boss you could make a billboard of yourself.' 'Nice idea Paco, what do you say Chocolate?' 'Well...you could take over a parade and ride through the parade.' 'A parade? What?' 'Yeah boss, theres a father days parade later today. You can take over it.' 'Hey that's not that bad of an idea Chocolate.' 'Ooh baby! That would be great, we can pull out the Impala Low-Rider and cruise in the parade!' 'Okay vatos, lets do it!' The scene fades out and then reappears with J-Gonz sitting on the back of a cherry red Impala. Both Gonz and Rasberry are in the back seat, in the driver side is Paco and in the very front in another vehicle is Chocolate. They arrive at a busy and crowded street in downtown Dallas. The parade has already started, J-Gonz pulls in front of a marching band and soon finds himself being yelled out and trash is being thrown in his direction. 'Ladies and gentleman Gonz Enterprises is proud to present, the new Television Champion...J-Gonz!' Chocolate blares through a PA as the crowd begins to bood. J-Gonz pulls a microphone to his lips and holds his title up. 'You look at this long and hard vatos, cause this is the real deal! This is J-Gonz your new Television Champion! I beat Drachen for the title and now that punk Rich Sherwin is making things personal. Look at this, I'm J-Gonz!' The Impala moves through the street and soon it stops and begins to hop up and down. This calms the crowd down some and soon they are actually cheering J-Gonz. 'Paco hit em with some tricks.' The car moves from side to side and continues to hop. Little kids gather around getting the autograph of J-Gonz. 'Ah si! This is what it's all about. See babe, I told you I was big time. I'm the real deal and I'm on top of the world. Nobody is going to take this moment away from me!' The scene continues with the car jumping up and down and soon fades out. When it fades back in J-Gonz is behind his desk with Rasberry sitting with her legs crossed on top of the desk. 'So soon I see! The white man want's to keep J-Gonz down. But I have a surprise for them. Leinad and Itagaki are no match for J-Gonz, I'll see to it that both men don't make it to Evolution!' J-Gonz hits a button and soon Paco and Chocolate are in the room 'Boys, we got two buffoons coming for the title next week, I need you to take them out. Go out tonight and see if you find either of the two and bring them back to me.' 'You got it boss.' The two men disappear and soon J-Gonz is up looking out of his penthouse window down at Dallas. 'From terrorist to crime lord. I'm running this city now babe and soon I'm gonna run Global Wrestling. Ay! You better it believe it.' The scene fades out with J-Gonz taking a drag of his cigarette.
