Finding the Past (Part 7)

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.06.04 ~WARDROBE~ (Ken) Giorgio Armani: Black Tuxedo Pants with Black Silk Trim Giorgio Armani: Black Ankle Boots Exhaustion settled into Ken's state-of-being as he leaned back into his white Italian leather sofa, allowing his eyes to close the bare skin of his torso to absorb the coolness of the calf-hide. A small smile etched across his face as he breathed in the aromatic fragrance of the four Dulce de Leche scented candles that sat atop his den's greeting table. He felt a warmth ente his body as he took a sip of his hot Honey Ginseng Green Tea and allowed the music of Faure to flow throughout him as his Requiem 'Sanctus' emitted powerfully soft from the silver slimline Bose stereo hanging on the opposite wall as his large widescreen LCD television by the same company. All of these factors were finally bringing Ken to a point of relaxation that he had not been in for some time due to a tirade of events being battled about in his mind and soul. There were countless conflicts that he had been through in recent months regarding memories forgotten. The fact that he would have people call or visit just to see how he was, only for him to have to admit to them that he had no idea as to their identity had caused him more pain than he could have ever imagined. The wars that he had been waging within himself were beginning to take their tolls, and he was nearly ready to surrender to them and throw up the white flag. However, he was now overjoyed to say that all but one memory had returned. The exhaustion which Ken was suffering had been caused due to an amazing rush of past visions and thoughts all at an immediate surpluss. In just a few short moments, it was if his mind had gone into hyperdrive and allowed every memory that he'd ever had to come back to him. Memories as far back as early childhood when his parents were still together... memories that he had been blockling from himself for so long... all of them had come back. All of them save one. Ken still had no clue as to who was attempting to run him over... who was trying to murder him. But the fact that someone had in fact been attempting to murder him was in his mind without a shadow of a doubt. As sure as Ken was that he would find himself with those which he had lost at a garden of honey and wine once he left this world, he was sure that somebody had already given their best to let him experience that early. Why such vengeance, though. Was their yet more in his past that he had not recovered. Perhaps a wrong-doing that he had done someone that had caused a spiteness towards him to the point of murder. His mind raced and he struggled to develope a notion that, even as the type of person that he was at that time, he would have been able to so something to that extent. The smile remained upon Ken's face. His struggle would be over soon, but for now he reveled in the fact that everything he once was had returned to him. He was his strength.
