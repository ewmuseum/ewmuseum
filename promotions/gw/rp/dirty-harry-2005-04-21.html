Dirty Harry

21 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/21/05 You're like an infantile I knew it all the while You sit and try to play me Just like you see on TV I am an oversight Just like a parasite Why am I so pathetic? I know you won't forget it -'Sooner Or Later', Breaking Benjamin Michael was too frozen to place his foot on the gas of his black Pontiac Firebird. He sat in disbelief of what was happening to him. He hadn't known the truth of his wife and daughter's disappearance, and rightfully so. How was he to know? Michael knew no one who would- or could- possibly wish to bring harm to his family. No one could really be that stupid... could they? 'I should have known...' Michael mumbled, just like he'd been mumbling all day in that car. It was growing towards night time, and Michael still couldn't bring himself to even move- he was just too taken over by emotion. He glanced at the dashboard, and took notice to the object that lay there next to his daughter's stuffed teddy bear: a pack of cigarettes. He never smoked and as far as he knew, Mindy didn't either, which caused Michael to assume they were Jaden's. 'Hmph. The least you can do is take your shit with you if you're going to leave.' Michael grabbed the pack and chucked it over his shoulder, scattering cigarettes all over the back seat. It was then that Michael's hands slammed against the steering wheel, gripping it tightly; as though he were going to rip it off any second. 'If you hated me so much... you should have just told me so.' Michael revved the car's engine. It was too late to grieve about things; all Michael wanted now, was an answer as to why she left him without a reason- again. If Jaden didn't want to be a part of him, at least this time Michael would be ready for the rejection. He put the car in drive, unknowing of the real reason behind his family's disappearance. 'There was no reason for you to go and do this to me again,' he continued, as his hands clenched the steering wheel in a rush of emotion. 'If you wanted to leave, all you needed to do was tell me. I thought after everything else, that I didn't deserve to go through this again.' His eyes blinked with lack of expression, only to look in the rear view mirror at his house, which got smaller and smaller by the second. 'I guess I was wrong.' Michael got out onto the main road and upon doing so, tried to wonder where he should look first. He was so dead set on finding his wife, that he hadn't even thought about where she might have been. 'Airport...' he mumbled, 'yeah, she's probably at the airport or something.' It was the only place Michael could think of. Hell, it was the only place that truly made sense to him. Sure, she had family in New Mexico, but they definitely weren't the kind of people she'd have wanted to go to- no matter what the circumstances. 'Although, if she went to the airport, she's probably gone already...' As Michael slowed to a stop at a red light, he shook his head in disgust. Black bangs of hair fell over his dark brown eyes as he tried to think of something- anything that he could do. For all he knew, Jaden could have left days ago. Would that deter Michael from looking for her, though? Never. The second that light turned green, Michael was gone, literally leaving the car behind him in a cloud of smoke. 'Let me see you run away from me this time.' Michael paid no attention to the car he took off in front of, but maybe he would have benefitted from it- even if it wasn't really something he was interested in either way. Markus Riley blinked at all the dust that got kicked up in front of his windshield. In truth, however, he was way too baked to make much sense of it. 'Holy shit... Tazmanian Devil's in front of my car.' He fumbled a badly drawn map in his hands, while one foot manned the pedals and the other hooked itself around the steering wheel. He had somewhat forgotten why he was in New Mexico in the first place, but it wasn't like he purposely forced it out of his mind, either. With a sigh, he placed the map in his lap as his right hand reached over to grab a joint in his mug holder- or rather, his ashtray. 'I really should have saved this shit for Four-twenty Day... hey, that sky is pretty fuckin' pink.' Markus took his large black samurai hat off and wiped a bead of sweat from his forehead. From the blistering heat he was hit with, Markus had assumed he got lost in Tijuana, Mexico. 'No wait.. this is that... that 'other' Mexico. Come on, Marky Mark. Why are you here again?' As his feet effortlessly drove and steered the car, Markus racked his brain for a reminder of what he was doing lost in 'the other Mexico'. He had a map... maybe looking at where it led would be an assistance. He looked at the maze of lines that represented the town's streets, and the big red 'X' on the map in the lower right hand corner. It was there that Markus found the name of his destination, and he audibly attempted to acknowledge it. 'Minnie... Minty... Mindy. Mindy's House. Hm.' He let those two words sink in. Deeper... deeper still.. and then- 'Ooooh. Wait...' Markus looked up at the street sign to the corner on his left as he drove by it, and quickly darted his head right back to the map. 'Hm, I'm going the wrong way, aren't I?' The smoke-filled red Grand Am swerved a hard right, and with it, garnered Michael's attention as he looked in his rear view mirror and noticed the car. '....I fucking recognize that pussy wagon.' Michael was insistent on finding Jaden, but even that could rest while he beat the shit out of the man who hurt his cousin. No one hurt Michael Mercer's family. Not even his former best friend. His car made a loud U-turn, and it generated many loud honks and obscene gestures in his direction. Did he care? Not really. 'What the hell?' Markus mumbled to himself, hearing the screeching of tires behind him. The added noise from the angry drivers didn't help Markus' ears, either. He already felt like he was hearing things, and didn't need anymore sounds to influence those thoughts. When he turned around, however, Markus could confirm with his eyes that the noise behind him was indeed real. Hell, he was stoned- but not that stoned. 'Get back here, you piece of shit!' Michael screamed, even though he knew Markus couldn't possibly hear him. It was all academic; once he saw that car, the smoke that poured from it, only one thing was in Michael's mind. 'God, it is fucking on.' Worry raced through Markus' mind. He wasn't even in Truth Or Consequence to try and start trouble; all he wanted to do was talk to Mindy, and hopefully get some kind of good out of it. At that point, though, Markus didn't expect much more to happen other than getting his teeth knocked out before he even made it to the house. And before he knew it... BAM! Markus kicked open the driver door to his car and crawled out, blood coughed up from his mouth. He had absolutely no idea what hit him until he rolled onto his back to actually look. It was then that he noticed his Grand Am was folded up like an acordion, and a large telephone pole was the source of the crash. That sight alone made the blood Markus spat up taste all the more bitter. Then he heard the car behind him skid to a stop. Then the opening of the car door. Then the faint sound of sneakers clacking on the concrete. Then that unmistakable voice. 'You motherfucker... get the hell on your feet!' Michael grabbed Markus roughly by his shoulders, and even though Markus had a good four inches and fifty pounds on him, Michael lifted the stoner to his feet as if he were a child. Markus coughed and sputtered, but could only emit a soft groan over the overwhelming pain that he was enduring. 'Shut up,' Michael said, refusing to let Markus get even a noise out of his mouth. 'The hell are you doing here? What the hell are you doing here?' Michael watched as Markus tried to respond, but could only double over and clutch his ribs. Michael shook his head in disgust; he was through with feeling pity for anyone. 'Come on,' he gestured, 'answer my question.' Markus tried and tried, opening and closing his mouth in repeated attempts to so much as lip sync an answer, but it was no use. His legs were too rubbery, and he finally crashed backwards onto his ass in the middle of the street. An overweight old man then stepped out of his house, still in his bed clothes- a white tee shirt that was obviously two sizes too small, a pair of smiley face boxers, and a fuzzy pair of pale blue slippers. 'Hey!' the man yelled, 'what in sweet chocolate Christ is this ruckus?! You better get that goddamn pussy wagon out of the road!' 'Pussy wagon...' Michael trailed off, taking a moment to chuckle to himself at the connotation he and the old man shared toward Markus' car, but it was only a moment. And a brief one at that. 'Shut the hell up, penis wrinkle. You want me to ice you?' 'You're crazy!' the man cried back. 'I'm calling the cops!' Michael glared daggers at the old man, and even at the distance between them, the old man felt those dark brown eyes pierce right into his soul. 'You do that, I will take my fist and play Ro Sham Bo with your face. Rock Rock Rock, motherfucker.' With a stare like Michael's, the man was in no condition to test Michael's word. '...Okay. At least put out the pot, I know marijuana when I smell it!' The moment he uttered the last syllable in that sentence, the man was back in the safety of his home. And come to think of it... Michael did smell something. He turned towards the wrecked car, and walked over to it mockingly. He gave one quick glance at Markus, who looked understandably confused. Markus obviously had little comprehension of what was going on; a car crash, Michael showing up out of nowhere... for all he knew, he was just having very bad hallucinations. That was when Michael pulled out the joint. He sighed as he stared at it, still perfectly lit. 'I don't even smoke, and the sight of you here just makes me want to toke up.' And Michael did just that. 'My...' Markus stammered, reaching a hand out towards Michael as the wrestler calmly made his way back to Markus and squatted down next to him. He watched Markus' hand travel towards him, and try to grab the joint from his hand. All Markus got, however, was a slap on the wrist. 'Don't touch,' Michael ordered. 'Tell me what you're doing here. And maybe then I'll at least consider letting you walk away.' Markus shook his head slowly, his long black hair dangling in front of his bloodied, pale-skinned face. 'I.. I just wanted to talk... to, to your cousin.' 'Bullshit,' Michael snapped back quickly. 'You upset her last time you both talked, why would I think you'd come here to make friends?' 'I didn't say you'd believe me,' Markus winced in defense, 'I'm telling you what I'm doing here, like you wanted me to. Not my fault you can't... ugh, can't handle the truth.' Markus coughed up and spat out another wad of bloody saliva, and it just barely missed Michael's Converse sneakers. Michael stared a hole through Markus, the temptation to actually hospitalize the man- hell, even kill him- was rising slowly by the second. Michael felt hatred, but it was a good three, maybe four years since Michael felt the kind of hatred he was feeling at that moment. That was when his angered and fogged up mind began to speak for him. '...Did you talk Jaden into leaving me?' Michael barked, the mere thought of such a thing bringing a fire to Michael's eyes that Markus had never saw before. 'You did, didn't you?' Markus' eyes widened. Knelt in front of him was a man that used to be his best friend- maybe even his only friend- and he actually looked ready to kill him then and there. For the first time ever, and legitimately so, Markus was afraid. 'I don't...' he hesitated.. He wanted to tell Michael the truth, but he knew that Michael would want much more than that. Still, Markus couldn't lie; he didn't even have an idea what the hell Michael was talking about. 'I don't know what you're getting at,' he said finally. 'Don't lie to me,' Michael warned again. The sincerity in Michael's voice chilled Markus to the bone; it was enough to scare the stoned shit right out of him. 'Because if you are...' 'I'm not lying!' Markus yelled back as he tried to get to his feet, but to no avail. Michael's look took on one of unbridled fury, as he slammed a fist into the ground without so much as a flinch to show for it. 'Goddammit, you did not come all the way down here to talk to my cousin! What the fuck are you doing here!?' 'Michael!' a distant voice called. Michael was too drunken over with hatred to notice it. 'Dude, I was your friend for all this time, why can't you believe me now?! Because of one freakin' thing, why can't you listen to me?!' A small dose of Michael wished he could sympathize with the words Markus was saying- the man was actually crying at that moment, but Michael just did not care. 'Please,' he continued, 'please, just don't hurt me...' 'Hey, Michael!' the voice called again as it got closer. Michael's ears picked it up, but he did not turn away from Markus. His former friend was just in a harsh car crash, spitting up blood and openly weeping right before him, but Michael adamantly refused to turn away from the man in fear of him getting away. He no longer trusted Markus Riley for a second, and it was blatantly apparent. 'Michael!' the voice cried loudly for a third time, but on that occasion, a sharp punch to Michael's shoulder came with the voice. 'Dammit, Mikey, didn't ya hear me screamin'?' Mindy. Michael sighed. 'What do you want? I'm about to figure out where my wife is.' Mindy nodded slowly. She didn't know whether to laugh or cry at the irony to that comment. 'Yeah, Mikey.. you are.' It was then that Mindy handed her older cousin the note she'd found just before she was attacked. And a second after that, Mindy's eyes looked right into those of the man who was once her boyfriend. 'Markus,' she growled. The first thing she wanted was bloodshed; but she soon noticed that Markus had spilt more than enough blood already. Mindy could be a fighter- but underneath her GW persona, she was still a caring little girl at heart. 'Sheesh, what happened to you?'
