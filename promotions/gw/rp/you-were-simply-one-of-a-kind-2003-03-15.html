You Were Simply One Of A Kind

15 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/15/03 Beauty Lies In The Eye Of The Beholder He looks up towards the sky. Thinking about his father just made him miss his family even more. He spoke words about his father that I’m sure if he were here, would never be forgotten. But he isn’t here in this world, but he may look down upon his son from the heavens above and smile, smile because his son accomplished something. Someday, someday his father would be proud of him. That someday is today. He rarely mentions his mother. Maybe the thought of her leaving him, her own son, for another man shortly after his death plagued his mind with anger and astonishment. Maybe he did not want to bring it up? His mind wonders, back to the memories when his father used to work on the field while his mother would fold and wash laundry. She used to cook every single meal from scratch and delight him and his father with her wonderful cooking ability. His dad used to always say that, “She can make a piece of paper taste like a scrumptious pastry.” The good ol? days, when his father and mother were still around. His father’s whereabouts are known’in the afterlife, heaven or hell’it doesn’t matter, he isn’t on this Earth. But his mother’s whereabouts, they are a mystery. Perhaps she has died and passed on along with his father. Maybe she is happily married with three children, making them the greatest dinner possible. But when he thinks about his mother, the words “if” and “maybe” continue to pop up, because where she is, is simply a mystery. “I do not know much about my mother’other then I miss her. It is very difficult for me to recall the past and what she was like. But, in my memories, I remember her long black hair was usually tied in knots. Her lips weren’t the rose red that the perfect woman is said to have. And her eyes do not shine like diamonds or glisten like morning dew. Her skin, more of like hide from an animal, as hard as can be from manual labor, eliminating the dishwashers, washing and drying machines. Her odor was never compared to perfume. But the love she once had for us could last a lifetime’she was one of a kind. She was my mother and no matter what happened to her, whether she married another man or whether she is sitting at home watching American television, I will still love her through every situation and problem I have come across in my life that she has caused.” He reaches down and picks up several blades of grass and drops them on the ground? “Mom used to try to teach me how to whistle with a blade of grass. She was very amused by it’s hidden talent. I would try, but I would always fail. But that didn’t matter to her, she loved to sit back and watch me, if I could whistle with a blade of grass or not.” He picks up a single blade of grass and positions it sideways and places it gently into his mouth. He takes a deep breath from his nose and breaths out through his mouth? “no sound comes out of the blade of grass. He takes it out of his mouth and crumbles it up into a small ball and throws it in front of him. “I never learned how to do it. It was something that I just could never do. Because she didn’t last long enough to teach me. My father passed away, and she left home months later because she wanted to meet “another man” and to be cared for. See, Mom was always somebody who had to have somebody love her at all times. She had to have somebody appreciate the hard work and dedication she put into her work. And I think she did not want a child to appreciate her work, so she left. Left me alone, starving, with no place to call home except the building I lived in. This wasn’t home. This was my house??home is where the heart is?, that is what everybody always says. But where is home if you don’t know where your heart is?” He pauses for a moment? “I know where my heart is now, and it lies in Global Wrestling’in The Nevermore. Months ago, I was left without a family, now I am faced with a new one. Salaok, the father of The Nevermore. Quartz, my brother. And others, we are all a family. My heart is still unknown’for I am a Heartless Bastard. But if I did have a heart, because of The Nevermore, it is now dyed black as can be, separating the truth between good and evil within my soul. Now, now I have a family. Only if you were here Mother, then you would be proud of me like Dad is.” He stands to his feet and looks up at the brightest star in the sky. “Mother, wherever you are’thanks for being there when you were. Thank you for the memories and thank you for everything you have done for me. Your laughter, your smile, and your happiness, that has been passed onto me. I am the product of your marriage. I am wise and noble, because of you and my father. Thank you once again Mother. For I have inherited all my blessings and skills from you and Dad.
