Catch Me If You Can

23 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22304 'Catch me if you can!' The words lag behind as if tossed from the back of the motorcycle as it speeds away like a stowaway from a cruise ship in the middle of the ocean. The sound of these words are soon cast aside to make room for the roaring engine of an Aston Martin V12 Vanquish as it slides past the garage gate on onto the open road before taking off after the Ninja, it's tires screeching in protest. ***** And I will Vanquish mine enemies?. The drop cloth slid effortlessly off of the cars sleek form. It started off slow, like a strip tease, the grill peaking its way through like a woman’s cleavage before revealing all the goods. The cloth then slipping away like the dress off of a woman’s body, revealing all the delicate curves in the right places. I stood there and stared at the beautiful machine, oblivious to everything around me. Finally, Tommy spoke up. “Now, Mr. Zero, a few modifications that you request were fairly unusual, but I believe that you’ll be most pleased with the results.” I turned my attention to Tommy for a moment. “Mind if I put them to the test?” “Be my guest.” The words had no sooner come out of his mouth than I reached under my loose fitting suit coat and whipped out one of my trusty two glocks and fired off a complete magazine, letting the bullets fly at the windshield hood and quarter panels. The magazine clattered to the ground noisily as it was ejected, a fresh one locked in place, round chambered and the gun slid back in place. I buttoned my coat back as Tommy just stared at me and then at the car. I slow walked towards the Vanquish, running my hand over the flawlessly smooth hood. Only a few black marks scarred the dark charcoal gray surface, but they wiped away at my touch. The windshield received the same amount of attention. The only difference between it and the hood was the lack of the black smudges. I nodded my approval to Tommy. “Very nice.” Reaching into his pocket, Tommy tossed me the keys. “Check out the inside.” Opening the door, the smell of new leather greeted me. I slid in behind the wheel, the black leather squeaking softly against my body. I fiddled with the seat controls for a few moments, getting the seat to my preferred liking. It felt so right. “Now, with the standard V12 engine that comes in these bad boys, you can go zero to sixty in about four and a half seconds. You can hit one hundred miles per hour in about ten seconds. This baby right here has a six speed manual transmission. Now, instead of your normal gearshift, it has a fingertip shifter right behind the steering wheel,” Tommy droned in his salesman type voice. As if by magic, the key found its way into the ignition, the engine rumbling to life before settling into a timid purr. I gunned the engine a few times, the purr turning into the roar of a lion. “Now, we did turbo charge the engine for you, giving you that extra little oomph with a small nitrous system, which I highly doubt that you’ll use.” I turned and gave Tommy a long blank stare, telling him not to doubt anything. He gulped nervously and continued. “We also installed a high end stereo system, including a head unit, two Audiobahn immortals, two Audiobahn flame series amps, new six by nines, dynamats for the trunk, doors, and license plate holder, and a capacitor. This think will thump like its nobodies business. We also made the pull away compartments in the backseat, as per your request. This thing is cocked, locked, and ready to rock.” I leaned back in the seat as Tommy droned on and on. Out of the corner of my eye, I watched a motorcycle come into view, Deacon and Rhaiza on its back. It flew through the warehouse and slide to a screeching halt right in front of the Vanquish. Deac threw me one of his big goofy smiles before gunning the engine and tearing off again, leaving tire dust to settle on my brand new car. Oh hell no. I picked up the briefcase and tossed it to Tommy. “Money’s in there. Any problems, I’ll find you.” I slammed the door, encasing myself inside the luxurious machine. I jammed the gas petal, the engine wailing as the tired smoked before friction caused the machine to go. I raced the car out of the warehouse, across the small parking lot and out the gate. Deacon was waiting in the middle of the road. I saw his mouth moving, already knowing what he was saying, he took off like a bat outta hell as the Vanquish went flying through the gate, the ass end swinging wide, tires protesting before giving hot pursuit. Catch me if you can, my ass. ***** Looking back over his shoulder, Deacon finds that Zero is hot on his tail. This brings a smile to his face as he cranks back even harder on the throttle, abruptly jerking the bike forward. Noticing this, Zero immediately downshifts, causing his engine's revolutions per minute and his speed to rapidly increase. Another minute of fast paced driving and the two vehicles are neck and neck, each one taking turns sliding into first place as they jock for position. On each side of the road trees, sidewalks, street signs, passers by, stores, and neon signs all fly by as formless masses. All of these things stretch, skew, and blend together in an abstract blur of random colors and shapes that looks like an epileptic nightmare. Two yellow lines in the center of the road are the only thing dividing the two speeding vehicles as they continue to punish the pavement while rocketing down the road under the setting sun. With their engines roaring the two vehicles fly up a hill somewhere in the neighborhood of one hundred and fifty miles per hour. As they approach the summit of the hill Deacon finds himself on a crash course for an oncoming truck. Realizing they have relatively nowhere to go, Rhaiza's eyes grow wide and worried as she realizes her life is in Deacon's hands and there isn't much of anything she can do about it. At this point in time Holmes is presented with an interesting proposition... Either slow down and take the safe route by driving behind Zero on the right side of the road, and in the process more or less forfeit the race... Or do something incredibly stupid, and borderline suicidal at the risk of not only his life, but Rhaiza's as well all for the sake of bragging rights. With the truck getting closer and it's horn bellowing through the air Rhaiza's eyes grow even wider, as Deacon's do just the opposite... Holmes' eyes scowl at the oncoming truck and a maniacal grin forms on his face as he grits his teeth while still fast approaching. With his speedometer showing no signs of him stopping, and Zero still pushing heavy on the gas, it would appear Deacon has made his decision. 'Brakes are for sissies.' he mumbles to himself in a gruff voice before making his move. Throwing caution to the wind Holmes throws his ZX-12R into a lower gear, causing it's rear tire to screech as he pops the bike up into an almost full on twelve o' clock wheelie and rockets into the neighborhood of one hundred ninety miles an hour... in between the two vehicles. As the front tire is once again reintroduced to the pavement Deacon's speedometer turns a little further to the right, completely breaking through the hundred and ninety mile an hour zone as he zooms to a blazing two hundred and five miles per hour. The near death situation he's just thrown himself into and emerged from unscathed has done nothing to shake his confidence as he takes the time out to knock on Zero's heavily tinted window to draw his attention then give him the finger in his passing. A cocky smirk grows on Deacon's face as he realizes that at over two hundred miles an hour John will never catch up in a Vanquish, as it's top speed is only one-ninety. And with that thought another comes crashing down. By no means a complex or deep though , this one was of the single word variety... Modifications... Deacon's eyes grow wide at the prospect of what John might have possibly had done to that car. Then looking over his shoulder he notices the charcoal gray vehicle steadily closing the gap. 'Son of a bitch!' These words fill the air and ring out like an old west gun shot as Zero pulls side by side with Holmes' bike. With Zero estate looming over the horizon Deacon's cell phone begins to ring. Never ungluing his eyes from the road, Holmes reaches into his pocket and removes his phone then places it against his ear. 'What?' Deacon yells into the phone. 'Back-off, you know we can't go through side-by-side. There's only room to go through one at a time.' 'You back-off, I'm winnin' this race.' 'Deacon, Deacon, Deacon... haven't we had this conversation before?' Inquires Zero in a rather relaxed tone. 'Yeah...' 'And remember what happened last time?' 'Yeah... I guess I'm gonna have to do a little rewriting of history then cause I ain't losin'!' 'Wanna bet?' Zero asks, hanging up the phone. Inside his Vanquish a faint grin falls across John's lips as he says to himself with a shrug 'Alright Deacon you asked for it...' Zero nonchalantly flips a switch on the steering wheel with his right thumb, injecting nitrous into his fuel line, and easily propelling his Vanquish past Holmes' Ninja. He offers up a casual wave and a nod as he makes his way past Deacon with his speed rapidly increasing as he flies through a red light. 'Not this time Johnny boy!' Holmes exclaims as he mashes a small button in the center of his console. Immediately Deacons bike is shot forward, causing he and Rhaiza to be thrown backward as a result of the abrupt burst of speed. The two fight tooth and nail against the wall of wind resistance against their bodies as they blaze through the same intersection Zero just did, weaving in and out of traffic coming from each side of the road all the while closing the gap on John. Just as when Zero thinks he's got it won, a black white and red flash blows by with only one thing being distinguishable... Two middle fingers... Holmes manages to regain the lead and then just as they are set to enter John's hidden driveway Deacon cuts Zero off right before flying down the entrance to Zero estate. ***** “Man, I’m about to fucking starve!” I sat down on one of the brown leather sofas in the living room across from where Deac and Rhaiza sat. They were both slowly sipping on tall glasses of orange soda. In my hand, I clasped a double old fashioned glass with two fingers of Jack D. in it. “D, when the fuck aren’t you hungry?” I leaned back into the leather, allowing it to suck my body back. Deacon looks over at me, and eyeballs the glass in my hand. He turns and looks at Rhaiza. “See this shit” This is tha shit I be talkin? “bout. This bastard doesn’t share shit with anyone.” He turns his attention back to me. “That's real fucked up John!” Back to Rhaiza. “You know how many times I done saved that motherfucker’s ass?” Back to me. “And I can’t get a motherfucking drink” Ain’t this a bitch!” Deacon gets up and storms away, leaving me to sit with Rhaiza. She looks in the direction that Deac took off in, watching him disappear behind a slamming door. She turned her attention back to where I sat back in the sofa. From my side, I lifted up two glasses empty glasses that Deacon never saw. “Care for a drink?” She breaks into a laugh before speaking. “Sure.”
