And Game (Actual end of gaming series)

25 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2.25.04 The scene opens to an oversized gymnasium complete with everything a modern day high-tech gym could ask for and then some. The room made to hold a capacity of somewhere near thirty people is currently only occupied by two: One man is standing atop a marked grid on the floor; he's adorned in a pair of black thick wool pants and knee height boots. The joints however are not wool but silk, made so that flexibility and agility are not compromised. He's also wearing the same type of long sleeve shirt with a mandarin collar that fastens to the side. The specialized fencer's attire is complete with a solid black fencer’s helmet. The only emblem on the uniform is that of a small crest on the breast of the jacket. The fencer is putting a pair of thick black gloves on; a fencing rapier rest upon a nearby table. 'It's been awhile since I've done this, Maverick. I may be rusty.' The voice is very familiar and may be recognized although somewhat muffled. A moment passes and Maverick Reno walks into the shot, in the exact same attire but without the face mask thus far. He holds his face guard underneath his right arm, his rapier in hand. 'I'm sure you're still in top form, John. Now c'mon, let's get this on it's way.' Reno pulls his face guard on and steps onto the grid, standing a solid distance from the man now only known as 'John.' A moment passes and Reno steps back, feet together and holds the rapier vertically before his frame, the blade directly between where his eyes are. John does the same. Reno is the first to salute his opponent and John quickly follows; They assume position, with knees bent and back perfectly straight, the left arm up in the air, and right arm bent with the rapier facing their opponent. 'On guard.' John is the first to thrust in although it's quickly parried by Reno's rapier. We can now see that the same crest that appears on John's uniform is on the same position on Reno's. Perhaps it's the emblem of this private gym? Reno again parries and reposts, thrusting towards the knee of his opponent. John leaps backwards, barely escaping the blow from Reno's blunted blade. Another quick thrust by Reno and John nearly loses his balance. He is able to parry however, catching Reno off guard and thrusts in with a repost of his own. Reno side steps and slams his blade into John's, beating him over and causing him to snap back with too much force. John overestimates and comes back too far, giving Reno the advantage and with a single quick disengaging thrust, smacks his blade into the side of John with a horrendous swipe. 'Point.' 'Yeah. Like I said, I'm rusty.' 'I believe you, John. I do... now prove you know what you're doing.' 'Oh, you think you're the shit now? S'that it? Alright... bring it on.' Both men retreat to where they started, both watching for the others move. Finally, Reno advances and makes a half thrusts, stopping before the actual lunge. John reacts however, giving Reno the perfect opportunity and with a quick snap of his wrist, a point is awarded. '... don't say it, Maverick. Don't even say it. I'm just warming up.' 'I'm sure you are, John.' Again the two men retreat to their starting points and advance for a third round. This time however, John attacks first with a high swipe towards the side of Reno's head; Reno is quick to parry and drag his blade down the blade of John's and with a quick beat, pushes it out of the way so that he may get a shot. John isn't about to be fooled twice however and instead of bringing it over too far, controls it and brings it up, knocking the point of Reno's rapier up and with a very quick repost, scores a point. 'Very nice.' 'Like I said, only warming up.' --- May 7th, 2003 - Approximately 7:00am New York City, New York - 4077 Crabapple Cove Lane The scene opens to show a large octagon shaped table made of a cherry stained oak. The edges are marble as are all accents of the room, including the six pillars that seem to support the roof. Three chairs are at one side of the octagon table with a fourth on the opposite side. A large plasma television behind the single chair showing images and schematics about a warehouse. At the bottom it says, 'Dallas, Texas.' The three chairs at the end closest to the camera are occupied by three gentlemen of different statures, each dressed in their own style and choosing. on the far left we see a smaller framed man with long blonde hair wearing a sports shirt and a pair of khaki pants. Next to him, a larger man wearing a t-shirt and jeans and beside him, a young black man wearing jeans and a t-shirt. The faces of these men can not be seen due to the camera angle. A flat screen LCD monitor has been placed before each man, giving them a close up and manipulatable version of the larger screen. A fourth man enters the room; Maverick Reno appears adorned in an Italian silk black suit complete with blue accents and silver cufflinks and tie-clip. His long jet black hair is tied back and his goatee is neatly trimmed. In his hands, he carries a single folder. A fifth person, a young Asian woman by the name of Naria Xiou accompanies Maverick into the room; she's dressed in a woman’s business suit and is carrying a stack of folders. She approaches the three men, placing one folder in front of each. As she passes the youngest of the three, he leans back in his chair, his face moving completely off camera before it could be distinguished, so that he is able to examine her... closely. 'Gentlemen, I believe you've all met Miss Xiou. She'll be assisting us today. Let's get down to business... we all know why we're here.' The youngest of the three raises his hand and Reno quickly acknowledges him. 'Yeah, why the fuck are we here?' 'Because it's time to let the world know. You see gentlemen, our planning and our precision are what makes us a formidable crew - it's what holds at a level above all others. It's our willingness to make people feel and fear our presence that makes us dangerous; our ability to strike anywhere and at anytime. That's why we're here. It's time the president of Global Wrestling felt our presence so that he may learn to fear us.' The middle man speaks up, his voice strong and curious. 'So what's the plan?' Reno takes a seat at the fourth chair and opens the folder. He types a few keys and the screens change, showing a detailed schematics of the warehouse. He begins to speak... 'This is a warehouse in Dallas, Texas. Currently, it is owned by Salaok. We have information that inside of this warehouse, there may be valuable information that the McCoy family as well as a few others would pay dearly for.' The middle man speaks again. 'Yeah, why are we going after Salaok first? He's got the biggest backing, he's got the power to really fuck us up, and he's not exactly an easy target.' 'Exactly. You're trying to capture a chain, what do you aim at first? The weakest or the strongest link?' Reno presents the query to the group until the man with the long blonde hair responds. 'The weakest link... it's the easiest and it gets you footing.' 'Wrong. Although a good, solid plan. It is not the most direct method to take over this chain. You attack the strongest and most powerfully supported link. Why? Simple. If you take over the weakest link then that's what you then have; the weakest link. If you attack this chain... and win... and take it with power to spare, then you have destroyed your opposition and become the strongest link, leaving the other weaker lengths in place and therefore, under your power.' The youngest man's head drops suddenly and the middle man nudges him midway through Reno's spiel about links and chains. The man's head jerks up and he speaks quickly, although quietly. 'What tha fuck man!” I was payin? attention, honest... I'm just a nocturnal learner, that's all!' Reno gives a half smile towards the three. 'Here's what I need from you three. I need you to gain access to the warehouse and seek out the main office; more than likely that's where any information we wish to acquire will be. The place will more than likely be protected so you may want to be armed to some extent although, I'd prefer if you didn't use any firearms... too loud and messy.' The man with long blonde hair speaks again. 'Yeah. Won't anything of importance be in a safe or something like that?' Reno nods and motions to the younger of the three. 'That's why we have our young friend over there. From my knowledge, he should be able to get into any safe, vault, or passed any security system they may have. You two are backup and support on this run.' 'That's another thing.' The middle man speaks, his voice breaking the silence in the room. 'I thought there was a fourth going with us? You said you had a real pro to help out with the takedowns?' Reno nods and sifts through the papers in the folder. 'Yes. Regretfully, however, John is taking care of a few loose ends back in Los Angeles. He won't be able to assist at this time. He will be joining us later on, however.' Silence overtakes the conference room for a moment before the youngest speaks. 'Yeah, you said this would be challenging? This is muh-fuckin' child’s play, man. When's the real shit gonna hit the fan?' Reno smiles and leans back in his chair. 'Like I've said in previous meetings. We're only warming up.' --- The scene returns to the gymnasium. Reno, distinguishable dur to the hair hanging out of the back of the face guard, has turned and taken a few steps away from the grid. His opponent, John, is crouching. 'Twenty games. What's the score now, Mav'?' 'Er, I believe 11 to 9. Not too uneven.' 'No... not at all. But you're mine now.' There's a slight chuckle in Reno's voice. 'You're a very competitive man, John.' 'Of course I am. That's why you hired me right? Best in the business... you can't be the best without being competitive.' 'That's true. I guess that makes all of you competitive.' With this simple trade, Reno returns to the grid and assumes his position. John stands and assumes his and with a simple thrust and lunge, the match is underway. The first lunge is of course parried and a repost is not made, Reno simply retreats a step and waits for another attack. John readjusts himself before stretching, popping first his back and then his neck and in one very fluid motion, lunges again, aiming for the feet of Maverick Reno. Reno jumps and brings his rapier around and to the side, aiming to bring it across the side and front of John's mask. John forces a grounded jump however, ducking as he falls backwards and knocks the blade slightly to the side. John curls his knees to his chest and thrust them forward, leaping from his back to a standing position. Within the time-span of a second, he is back in on guard stance and staring down Reno. 'Very nice, John. Very nice indeed. What do you say we up the ante?' 'How so?' Reno steps back and lowers his rapier; he walks slowly away towards a bar where numerous other fencing weapons are held. He puts his rapier in place and holds his hand up for John's. John tosses his over and Reno catches it by the hilt. He quickly returns it to its place and pulls two epee's from the rack. 'Epee, Mav? C'mon, Rapier is much more of a challenge.' 'Not just epee.' Reno proceeds to toss both epee's to John. 'Two-handed.' 'Now that's a challenge. You're on, Mr. Reno.' Reno grabs two others and returns to the grid with John. The two men stand, feet together. They put their left hand behind their back in the same position as their right, their right in the same position as before with the blade perfectly dividing the mask in two. 'On guard.' With this simple command, both men spring into action. Their forms are different however. John stands in the traditional fencing form with only slight alterations in his left hand so that the second feeble is pointed towards Reno. Reno however stands with his right arm slightly angled away from John although the feeble points directly at him; Reno's left arm is parallel to his back calf, the feeble pointing away from John. John quickly lunges forward, extending the first epee and then the second although Reno parry's the first and disengages under the second, merely moving his body out of range and with a quick beat, locks John's arms by pressing against the top. Within a moments time, the second epee is brought forth directly into the abdominal region of John. The two men unlock and take a step back, John shaking his head. 'I certainly wasn't expecting that.' 'Now that's the point isn't it?' 'Yeah yeah, old man. Let's just try this again...' The two men assume their positions and before the words, 'on guard,' can be said, John has lunged forward, thrusting for the right kneecap of Reno. Reno leaps up and crouches in air, swiping below to knock his blade out of reach. John's second blade comes for Reno's throat as he's coming down. Maverick quickly parries however and spins, thrusting for John's exposed side. John however is not going down this easy; with a quick jerk of his wrist, his right epee comes over and knocks the blade out of range. With this spin, John has his back to Reno and flips his left epee so that it's feeble is facing Reno. He thrusts backwards only to have it knocked away by a parry with his left epee. Both men jump, Reno back and John forward. John spins, ready to defend himself and Reno assumes on guard position again. John advances a step and facades, planting his right epee towards Reno's chest; Reno of course replies with a parry for nothing, John's left epee flies in for Reno's head but Reno simply drops a knee and brings his left epee in for John's unprotected abdomen. John, having foreseen his miss, leaps backwards and also takes a knee to defend against Reno's lowered position. Both men quickly stand and touch the feeble’s of their right epee's. --- May 30th, 2003 - Approximately 8:30pm Dallas, Texas - The Global Arena's VIP room (Approximately 30 minutes before Evolution) The scene opens up to show the VIP room. Inside the room, Maverick Reno stands at the one way mirror, his eyes downcast on the Octagon that makes Global Wrestling so unique, adorned similarly to his apparel in the previous scene, in an black Italian silk suit with red accents. Maverick's hands are clasped together behind his back. Naria Xiou stands beside him, her eyes facing the same as his. 'It's beautiful. Months and weeks of planning, errands and issues dealt with precision. Four men working in perfect unison and harmony. A council of men set so that there can be no problems. Perfectly elite and flawless in everyway, miss Xiou. Don't you agree?' The assistant nods her head, smiling pleasantly. 'Most definitely Mr. Reno. As you requested, the crew has been sent for.' 'Very good. And what of John? Has he returned from Los Angeles yet?' Naria shakes her head. 'No sir. But he did call in and confirm tonight’s events. He's very upset that he's having to miss these events.' Reno nods. 'Very well.' A knock comes from behind followed by the sound of a door opening. A man in a black suit, complete with an earpiece and what appears to be a stun gun and holster on his belt walks into the room. 'Sir, the crew has arrived.' Reno turns along with Naria, both facing the entrance. A moment passes before another guard walks in and takes his position on the other side of the door. Three men enter the room, each dressed in an exquisite suit. First Tyler Pierce, in a black suit with white alabaster accents, then Sean Price, in a black suit with blue accents, followed by Deacon Holmes in a black su... in jeans and a t-shirt?. 'Welcome, Gentlemen. Welcome to your future.' The scene fades to black with the four men, standing amongst one another in the Global Arena for the first time. --- John thrusts his left epee towards the abdomen of Maverick while he thrusts his left towards the side of John. John quickly parries as does Maverick, both locking and then tossing their opponents epee into the air. Both epee's hit the ground but before they can, Maverick and John both press the feeble’s of their right epee's against their opponents throat. 'Stalemate, Mr. Zero.' 'Stalemate, Mr. Reno.' The two men laugh and put their weapons down before both remove their masks, and for the first time revealing that the second man is actually John Zero. 'The Upper Echelon rises again, John.' 'And we will rise to the top again.' The doors open and both men turn; Deacon Holmes walks into the room in his normal attire. 'Awww great, you bunch of fuckin' sissys in here playing with your toys and I'm out doing all the work. Yeah, anything else I can get ya massa? Yo sho do look like a fag, massa. Damned motha-fucka's and ya pansy ass fuckin' sports. When you gonna learn, huh? When ya gonna learn?' Reno laughs a bit, shaking his head. 'It's damned good to be back, boys. Damned good.' The three men walk towards the main door, at first silently until Holmes speaks up. 'So are we still callin' you Il Capo or what? Honestly, I prefer Mavy. You like Mavy? I fuckin' like that; you like that John?' There's a slight pause. 'I definitely fuckin' like that.' The three walk out of the room and out of sight (and ear shot). The entire way we can hear Holmes continuing; 'Would you like a drink Massa Mavy? So how the fuck you doin' Mavy? Looks like we're gonna kick some muh-fuckin' ass, Mavy. Hell yeah I like that... you like it, Mavy?' --- 'Two men locked in an epic battle of agility, dexterity, concentration, and instinct. An epee slams against the second, a third flies through and a fourth parrys. No points. Intensity. Veracity. Determination. Survival. Adrenalin. Skill. The drive to advance. The art of fencing. Intensity. Veracity. Determination. Survival. Adrenalin. Skill. The drive to advance. The sport that we live for. Two men locked in an epic battle of agility, dexterity, concentration, and instinct. A lock up leads to a turn around which is only blocked and then rebounded; a firemans carry but the second man flips to his feet and turns. No points. The battle is not of strength or speed, but of strength of mind. The determination and drive to survive and advance to the next level. When you see the chance, the opportunity, you take it. You wait till you get that one hand, then you play big and you take the house. You work your opponent into a mindset. You deal a blow and pin him in a corner; he reacts incorrectly and the game ends. Checkmate. The battle for superiority begins with a salute, respect for your opponent. Always respect those beneath you; when you lose respect for the person you're victorious of, then you lose respect for yourself and then, the victory is no longer yours. Strike and strike fast but defend when necessary. On guard! Point, thrust, lunge - parry. Point thrust, disengage, facade, cutover, lunge - point. The game ends; your opponent is defeated. In fencing, you wait till that moment when you know you can take your opponent and you take him so that you win the final round. In fencing, you work your opponent into a corner, you get him defending, watching and expecting your attack. Then you give him something he was not expecting; a loss. In fencing, you fight for honor.
