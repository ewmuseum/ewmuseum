Uncle Sage

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/29/03 He is seen, sitting down with Lisha’the father of The Nevermore’s wife. Drinking coffee, black’the only way to drink it. He takes a sip, then puts the cup down. He takes a deep breath, for he hasn’t done too much breathing these days. He speaks’softly and quietly, hoping for help from her, a more wise woman about things that men never speak about. “Thanks for coming. I’ve been wanting to speak with you for awhile now.” “You seemed very anxious to have me over for some awkward reason. So I figured I’d rush over just to help out a good friend of Salaoks.? He nods” “I appreciate it a lot Lisha. Thank you.” “So what was it you had to speak to me about.” He twirls his fingers around on the table? “I don’t know, I’ve been through a lot with my life, and I’ve never had anybody with me. My father passed when I was young and my mother deserted me, left me with nothing at all. I lost loves in Melissa, Jessica, and Michelle. And there is a crack inside my heart, and it can’t be filled with any title belt or anything besides the love and care that I need. I don’t know, I get kind of jealous or something, I don’t know how to phrase it, but when I look at other people like you and Salaok. Or’or like even Derek and Cindy Frost, I sit back and wonder if I’ll ever have that same feeling in my life that you all have. It just hurts to sit back and realize nobody cares about me or anything.” “Sage, that crack in your heart can only be filled with love. There is an empty spot’and I know a great way that it can be filled.” He looks up surprised? “How?” “Your family.” He shakes his head side to side? “No, they are gone now. I am left with?” “No, your family in The Nevermore. Sage, you aren’t just a nobody. It doesn’t matter if you win or lose. It doesn’t matter what happens in the ring or with your career. You are one of us, blood Sage, blood is thicker then water. You are our family.” He pauses to think about it. She calls? “DARREL!” “DARREL!” Salaok and Lisha’s son, Darrel, comes running over to Lisha and hops on her lap. He gives her a big hug. “I’d like you to meet somebody.” He smiles, he is so carefree and loving, the age that mother’s wish every distraught teenager would return back to? “This is Sage’your uncle. Uncle Sage.” The first feeling of love, a family love, returns into that crack in his heart’for decades, that has never had anything there. But those words, Uncle Sage, made him feel special’like he is somebody. Like he is cared for by a group or person or anything at all. It doesn’t matter to him. Lisha, the wife of Salaok, cares about my well being and cares enough to help me. Why? The little child looks up and looks into his eyes. Without motioning or saying anything, a chill runs down his spine. This feeling, his non-related nephew’means more to him then any female ever could. He has a family’more then friends. More important then lovers. Darrel smiles at him, a 5 year old smile’ear to ear. Usually, people look at him and fear runs down their spine. Seeing somebody of such dominance that he has, makes them turn away, because they are not used to it. But not him, he smiles’because his mother, Lisha, tells him it is alright. Uncle Sage’it has a ring to it. A ring happier then wedding bells. He smiles back’it feels good for once. “Hello.” Darrel speaks in his 5 year old quiet voice. “Hey Darrel. How have you been?” “Good.” “That’s good to hear.” He nods his little head and squiggles out of her arms and jumps onto the floor and runs off into his living room. A smile falls across Lisha’s face. “This might not be the love that you are looking for, but it is the only love we can offer. The Nevermore, we have always been close Sage. We aren’t like any other stable or group that stays together for business purposes. We are together to better our lives, to be there for each other. Why” Because we aren’t just a group of people who conquer the ring or get paid to be on television. We are a family, we are The Nevermore.” The thought of what she said runs through his mind’finally, he has found his place in this world. He has filled, filled that crack in his heart that has bleed’that has spilled for decades to come. Oh, this feeling’nothing can compare and nothing can be more precious then the smile Darrel gave him. It was the initiation of his welcoming into his new family. “It’s all over.” “What is?” “The search to fill that crack in my heart.” “We will be here for you. You are a brother to us now, this is how it is.” A thud of unreleased tears burst down his throat? He swallows hard, trying to swallow reality. It has never choked him this good. “Thank you.” A single tear falls from his eye’a tear, something to remember this moment by. He quickly stands up and gives Lisha, his new sister in his newly found family a hug. It never felt so good. Love? This feeling is more then love. Emily Dickenson once said, “Success is counted sweetest, to those who ne’er succeed”. Such an amazing quote, he has never had the success he has thrived for. Love, love was his goal’now, now love is a feeling he has deep within himself. Thank You I Can Never Repay You Thank You I’ll Never Let You Down Thank You
