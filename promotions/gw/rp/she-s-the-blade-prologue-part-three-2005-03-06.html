She's The Blade: Prologue part three

6 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 6, 2005 It was a beautiful problem, one that he reveled in every time it crossed his mind. Two women, one vying for his affection while the other too stupid to accept her true feelings. The only dilemma was which one should he choose? He knew that he’d easily defeat Mindy and win an evening with her, which would give him the opportunity to do the only thing he wished to do with her. There was also Lisa, the rich woman that had a bad reputation because of the local gossip columnist. Lisa was more his type, she was beautiful, wealthy, and had large breast. He pondered all this as he sat in his living room with his newly won Television Championship that rested on his lap as he polished it. He wore a tuxedo; his blond hair slicked back, his fingernails neatly trimmed, and an ever so slight scent of his favorite cologne drifted from his body. Mike Biddle was dressed to kill tonight. Mike had become so wrapped up in polishing his championship belt that he didn’t notice his saucy maid, Belle, enter the room. She cleared her throat, an action that grabbed his attention instantly. He looked up at her and the very first thing he noticed was that her uniform seemed to fit her more snugly and it left very little up to the imagination. “What do you want?” Mike asked. “You know what I want,” Belle replied in her accented voice. “I wish to do my job...clean your pipes.” Mike smiled; nymphomaniac French maids were a beautiful thing. He looked up at the clock that clung to the wall to see if he had time to have his “pipes cleaned”. Unfortunately, it was 7:28, which meant that the limousine that was going to take him to the benefit dinner would be there very soon. He frowned as he placed his championship belt on the arm of the chair he was seated in and rose from the chair. “Not today Belle,” Mike stated, “I have somewhere to be right now. Perhaps we can do this later.” He left for the driveway; Belle stood alone in the living room with an upset look upon her face. “Damn Americans,” she cursed under her breath before she went about her business. When Mike made his way out the front door he immediately spotted the limousine that Lisa had sent for him. Once Mike stepped out onto the lawn an older man emerged from the front driver’s side seat, he was the man that had hand delivered the invitation to Mike earlier in the week. The man walked to the very back of the vehicle and opened the door; he then motioned for Mike to enter the vehicle. “Good day sir,” the man said as Mike entered the limo. He sat down and relaxed as the vehicle accelerated and sped off to the Beverly Hotel. Mike noticed that there was a mini-fridge next to his seat with a small note attached to it. The note read: “Mike, inside is a dry vodka martini; shaken, not stirred just the way I heard that you like. Lisa.” “Just what the doctor ordered,” Mike said to himself as he reached inside the mini-fridge and grabbed the martini. As he sipped on his drink he looked out the window and watched the beautiful scenery of Beverly Hills pass him by. Before he knew it the car had come to a complete stop in front of the Beverly hotel. He set the martini glass down on top of the mini-fridge and waited for the driver to the open the door for him. The man opened the door and Mike stepped out into the cool California night. Without acknowledging the driver he made his way up the front steps of the hotel and entered the lobby. He glanced around the lobby for a few moments as he tried to figure out where the benefit was being held. “Can I help you sir?” Came the helpful voice of a young bellhop. Mike turned to face the pimply-faced boy. “Yes,” Mike began; “I’m looking for the benefit dinner for the Breast Cancer Awareness Association.” “It’s on the top floor sir. Are you Mike Biddle?” Asked the bellhop. “Yes.” “Miss Jameson phoned ahead and the desk manager told me that if I saw you to let you know that she’d be late” the bellhop said. Mike nodded his head and walked towards the elevator, as Mike walked away the bell hop suddenly realized that he had just spoken with the Global Wrestling Television Champion and mentally kicked himself for not asking for Mike’s autograph. Mike stepped into the elevator and pressed the button that would take the small box to the top floor. Seconds later the doors parted with a ding and Mike was thrust into the benefit dinner. The room was well lit and the atmosphere accented by classical music that played in the background. The room was crowded with the people that Mike expected to see at the average Beverly Hills benefit dinner; actors, actresses, musicians, and all other kinds of celebrities from the Los Angeles area. He made his way into the room and came to a stop at the velvet rope that kept people out of the dining hall until they made their donation. ?$1000 is the minimum donation to enter sir,” said the middle-aged bald man that stood behind the rope. Mike rolled his dark blue eyes in annoyance as he reached into his back pants pocket for his wallet. He unfolded the wallet and produced ten $100 bills. “Here,” Mike said in an annoyed voice. The man produced a fake smile for Mike as he pulled back the rope and motioned for Mike to enter. Mike stepped into the room and the first thing he did was grab a glass of champagne from a waiter that passed by. He then began to mingle about the room. He stopped to chat with those that he knew and introduced himself to those he didn’t know. This went on until a short Hispanic woman that wore a short red dress approached him. “Mike Biddle I presume?” She asked with a smile. “At your service,” Mike replied with a seductive tone. “I’m Alice Martinez,” she said, “I work for the Beverly Hills Star.” “Of course,” Mike replied, “you’re that gossip columnist.” She blushed at the recognition. “I need to talk to you Mike,” she started, “I heard that you’ve been in contact with Lisa Jameson and that she is interested in dating you.” “What woman isn’t?” Mike asked with a wink. “Look,” Alice said in an annoyed voice, “she’s only interested in you for your money! She’s going to do to you what she did to all those poor old fools that she married before.” “Is that so?” asked a calm and elegant feminine voice that originated from behind Alice. Alice turned around and found herself face-to-face with Lisa Jameson. “I think it’s time you run along Alice,” Lisa said, “you’re intruding on my date with Mike.” With a frustrated growl Alice turned on her heel and stomped off. “So I take it she bothers you often?” Mike asked Lisa. “Yes,” Lisa replied, “she’s been following me around ever since my first marriage.” “Well,” Mike said, “you have to admit that is a bit suspicious when a beautiful young woman marries an old man that’s on his last leg.” “I did it for the money,” Lisa replied with a smile, “and now I have that money. All I’m looking for now is someone that makes me happy.” “So is that why it seems like you’ve been following me?” Mike asked. Lisa blushed slightly. “Yes,” she replied, “you’re just the most attractive playboy in the bunch.” She finished with a wink. As a waiter passed Mike exchanged his empty glass for a full one. “So what you say we skip this and go do something interesting?” Mike asked. Lisa put her arm around Mike, an action that caused her bosom to rub against his stomach and forced him to think of dirty little things to do to her. “So soon” You just got here.” Lisa said with a giggle. “Yeah,” Mike said, “but I’d rather be alone with you and get to know you better than mingle around with a bunch of uptight assholes.” Mike fibbed. “Then let’s go,” Lisa said. The two ducked out of the dinner together and little did Mike know that he had just started what would become one of the most horrible chapters of his life. -To Be Continued-
