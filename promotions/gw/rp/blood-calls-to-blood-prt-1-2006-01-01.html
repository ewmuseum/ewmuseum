Blood calls to blood prt 1

1 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/01/06 Day 1. Spain, 0500 Hours The rising sun fractured across the tree tops sending beams of light through the chopper as it tore through the sky like the devil out to collect. The winter cold still held strong in the air, biting to the core of the man standing in the helicopter. His back was to the sun and his head hung low, the light rise and fall of his chest gave the impression of a man asleep but Micheal Cervantes hadn’t been able to sleep for almost twenty-four hours, not sense he received the message from his mother, not sense he learned his brother had disappeared in these forests and hill. Checking his Ak-98 more out of habit than necessity he mulled over the discoveries he had made regarding his brother. Leaving home, in an attempt to follow his older brother, Tomas had begun taking an interest in international military affairs. Joining up with several different renegade companies operating out of the Spanish, French border he had tried to make a name for himself. As the helicopter slowly came to a hover over a small clearing in the naked woods. Cervantes turned his head to look back over his shoulder, he knew these woods well? he grew up no more then five kilometers from the place. In a way he regretted having to bring violence to a place that reminded him of such a wonderful childhood. Thrusting his cares aside through he gripped the cable tight tossing the end over. Without a word to the pilot he zips down and hits the grown with a thud, bending his knees at impact to lessen the shock. Not even looking up Cervantes darts for the woods finding cover in a wide branched tree, there he stops and waits removing a small GPS device from a pocket in his tactical vest. His brother had come here to be trained, he remembered that there was a guerilla training camp only one kilometer east of this position resting ride on the border lines, it was a rough place to train and its no wonder his mother had become worried after a year and no word. Checking the map he made sure of his location, and that his gear had made it with him. He had his assault rife with scope, back up pistol, combat knife, tactical vest an experimental SEAL wet suit hijacked from the Navy R&D department to keep him warm and provide a little extra protection; Three frag grenades two flash bang, one claymore, three clips for the rifle plus what was loaded, a backup flip for the pistol, two days worth of dry rations, a compass, a watch and finally his map. Placing the GPS map back in the vest Cervantes begins to march to his destination, his feet falling surely, stepping over dry twigs and moving like a man accustomed to spending his time sneaking through the forests and jungles o the world. As he walked he could remember his younger brother wanting to follow Michael into these very same woods. “And now he has?” Cervantes says to himself. Tomas had hoped to find the same people who had taught Cervantes after he had left the military. Cervantes only hope was that if he had found them that they hadn’t killed him yet for coming unannounced. Guerillas were not men to attempt to sneak up on, Michael had lived with them a long time and new that many would shoot first and ask questions later. This reckless style of action is what drove Cervantes out of the revolutionary circle and into the Private Military Companies of America and Great Britain. With access to better gear and working with far more professional officers, Michael has earned himself more than enough money to live out his days comfortably? Cervantes had hoped to retire to Cuba before Johnathan Davis decided to finally call on him to return a favor. But here he was, thousands of miles away from his retirement ready to die and deal death all for a little brother who was more than likely dead already? and if that was the case Michael would have a bloody couple days ahead of him. He grinned, at least it would be nice to do something he was good at again. As the sun crept up over the trees glistening through the bare tops yet despite the bright sky the wind was wicked and tore through the forest around him. Michael was thankful for the new wet suit underneath his gear, it kept him remarkably warm despite it being the middle of January. As he crept up on his destination Michael could see what looked to be a permanent camp, several short stone buildings surrounded by fence and a bit of foliage for camouflage. Bringing his binoculars to his eyes he began to examine the buildings. “Odd..” he whispers. There was no sign of life.. No one standing guard, no one patrolling no one even taking a morning piss. Beginning to creep forward Cervantes flicks off the safety on his rifle his eyes now darting rapidly among the buildings and the woods. For all purposes the camp appeared empty, arriving at the closest square brick building he peered through the window? darkness. Sidling along the wall he steps through the doorway and turns on the light on his gun. “Dear god!” he hisses, stepping back for a moment. There, sitting in a chair, his face covered in frozen blood and puss was a man or what was once a man. No breath escaped the dead mans lips, his body had been decaying before winter set in and now sitting in front of Cervantes was a preserved rotting corpse. He frowned, now things were getting him worried, looking at the room more closely he noticed the place was turned inside out, desk tipped over, a mattress torn open and a bookshelf reduced to kindling. Checking the body again to make sure it wasn’t Tomas, Michael stepped back out the door and headed deeper into the camp. With breath steaming he began to see the camp more clearly, here and there he saw blood, frozen to the ground and the wall? something had happened here. He stopped suddenly, pressing his back to the closest building. He heard voices coming from the building he had just passed, sneaking back he pressed his ear to the wall. “N’no” I’m fine! See! No sores! No sores!” begged a voice in poor French. “My friend we have our orders this plague is to be burned from this area.” BANG! Cervantes eye twitches at the gun shot. Sneaking around to the doorway he peers in, standing in the dark over a corpse is a man dressed much like Michael only on top of the tactical vest and black fatigues he wears a combat helmet and gas mask. Turning towards the door he sees Cervantes and slowly brings his gun to bear. “Don’t try it” Michael says in French. The man nods and drops the pistol on the ground, un straps his machine gun and lets that fall as he brings his hands to the air. Cervantes walks forward slowly. The man begins to laugh and before Cervantes can turn a second man, formerly in the corner steps forward and drives the but of his gun into the back of Michaels head cause his head to swim and his stomach to turn. He wills his legs to keep standing but they don’t listen and he drops like a sack of bricks his face smashing against the stone floor busting his nose and leaving him to lie in a pool of blood. All went black. Time passes in a dream, a haze of delirium partly from the blow to the head, part due to the torture of the soul. Nightmarish images of those slain and those he’d been a fool to let live flash in successions. Johnathan Davis looms over the world his arms extended his graying hair flying in all directions giving the nightmare persona the look of a warlock, his smile wide and his teeth white. “Soon Cervantes” Soon it will be time to walk as I fly above? our enemies are weak and we have been patient?” Blood? everywhere blood? a boy? in the jungle no a castle... Dead in his arms strangled. A man in black, blonde hair hanging and evil laughter ringing? A man with eyes of the dead in despair? a scream through the castle.. A scream tears through the masses. And still everywhere blood? “You have slept, you have been dormant without me” your potential sits beneath your skin and soon I will bring it out? I will tear off your fleshy mask and let loose the demon you are?” Plague and petulance fill his mind? gore surrounds him, the dead are impaled upon the trees, there decomposing flesh falls to the wolves. “Come back to me Michael Cervantes” take up the mantle I have prepared you for? come back and Harvest your enemies fear!” “. It couldn’t have been more than a minute or two that he was out. His head ached and his legs wouldn’t listen. Opening his eyes Cervantes could see a small pyre ahead with three men standing around it making sure the bodies that they had thrown on the pyre were staying on. Cervantes was being dragged and he heard the man to his left yell. “Colonel, we found someone snooping around. No I.D. possibly mercenary from the different gear.” he reported in French. “Bring him forward” The voice was like a hammer, this was a man who never asked questions and would never tolerate being refused. His bald head reflected the paling light of the winter afternoon. Looking at him the man was dressed much as the others but wore no helmet or mask, he was at least a head and a half taller than the other soldiers and his shoulders were built like a Spanish bull’s. The man looked over his shoulder at Cervantes, his up curled mustache would almost be comedic if it wasn’t matched with a pair of eyes that Michael was sure could kill you if he gave it enough thought. “What’s your name!” “Where’s my brother?” It was like lightning and felt like a blast of thunder, the Colonel had turned with break neck speed and brought his foot forward kicking Cervantes square in the chest and sending him back a good two feet. Michael gasped for several minutes feeling the vomit well up in the back of his throat. Frowning he swallowed it back, refusing to show this man any weakness. “Your name!” Cervantes slowly staggered to his feet. “Michael Cervantes?” he gasps, slowly turning dried blood on his face sweat dripping off his forehead. He slowly takes several steps to the Colonel. “Was that so hard” No, I didn’t think so? the Colonel replies. “My name is Colonel Jaques Martel formerly of the French Foreign Legion now a member of Le Administe Francais Militaire.” he steps forward, graceful for a man of his size and offers his right hand. “Pleased to meet you Mr. Cervantes.” Cervantes shakes his hand, unsure of how to take this turn of events. “Now what can we do for you Mr. Cervantes.” he says, releasing his hand. “I came here looking for my brother.” Martel raises an eyebrow and strokes his blonde mustache. “We came here because of illegal PMC activity in the area, we had tracked this particular group for some time after several interferences in French affairs. But when we arrived we found the camp much as you have, most of the soldiers still here were infected with some kind of virus, nothing our medic could even identify. A sample was sent back to the labs in France but I’ve never seen anything like this. We have been forced to execute each soldier here and burn the corpses. Now if you’d like?” There’s a sound of metal against metal as Martel lifts up a small sack. “These are full of dog tags found on the corpses of those in that pyre” you are welcome to examine them if you like.” Sitting in the dirt, several meters away from the rest of the soldiers, Michael Cervantes dumps the dog tags into the dirt and begins to sift through them? one by one? hundreds of them, Jaques Martel stands some distance off his eyes on the man who just come into there midst and the problems that this aroused just as Cervantes sat looking at tag after tag, knowing he would not be allowed to leave. But neither man knew the extent of the problems they faced and it wouldn’t be too long till Johnathan Davis would come to collect his prodigy.
