Yet Another Journey: Part 1

26 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th August Children... At one point or another even though most don't like to admit it we have all been children. We have all done stupid things as children like drawing on the walls or running around with nothing but your potty on your head. Even the so called dark loner types here in GW were kids at once and have undoubtedly had their own potty fun. Children are so honest and so inquisitive. Now this can be endearing or it can be very, very annoying, they can ask a simple enough question such as 'Why?' and turn it into a weapon of torture. Now don't get me wrong kids are this world's future and without them we wouldn't have anyone else to look after our world when we are gone. Children are possibly one of wrestling's biggest fan base, I mean when I walk out to that ring I often spot kids yelling at the top of their lungs for their favourite superstar. Of course there is that age-old argument that children shouldn't watch such violent things like wrestling. Now I see this argument as a load of rubbish, I mean it takes the viewpoint that children are mindless idiots, okay I will admit children in the beginning of life are blank slates and do look around them to learn how to behave and interact. But for a child of 12 or 13 to be called a blank slate it not fair, I mean they are little people and by that age have already made their own decisions and opinions on things. So all these new stories that say so and so did something because of wrestling is a load of crap. The reason children do stuff like that is for attention and it is because the parents are not willing to blame themselves they look to something like wrestling to blame instead. In my opinion it truly is pathetic... I have told you stories of my childhood well stories of since I was 7 anyway. As I have said my childhood was a very protected childhood, I was literally wrapped up in cotton wool by my mother. I knew my mum was simply protecting me and doing what she thought was best but after 18 years of not being able to run and play with the other children, 18 years of never being let outside, it can get a little tiresome if you know what I mean. I am willing to admit I am still slightly bitter about the whole episode but at the same thankful. As if it weren't for my mother almost tyrannical rule over my safety I would never have rebelled against it. I would have never started my training, I would have never gone to the academy, I would have never have met Peter Willows, I would have never gone to Mexico and Japan and I would never have come to the GW. Now here I am just a few days away from Whiplash, GW's next Pay Per View, where I stand the chance of becoming the next GW Cruiserweight Champion. It has been a while since I last fought for a title, in fact the last one I thought for was in Japan and that was the Japanese Championship, which I won and held for 4 months before leaving for the American Indy Circuit. However now I face winning a title that has been held by all the greats of GW and I aspire to join those ranks at Whiplash... 'I can't believe it Adam, I am so proud of you.' I was standing in Peter's office as he sat down behind is large oak desk with papers scattered everywhere. I had just told him the news that I had received a shot at the GW Cruiserweight Champion. I was smiling broadly behind my mask not believing that I had received such a shot. 'It is just incredible, I mean you spent what? 2 weeks in the GW and yet already here you are with a GW Title shot.' 'I think they were just impressed by my time in the GU and obviously thought that I was ready for the Cruiserweight title. There are 7 other people in this match though it is going to be one hell of a match. Especially when the final 2 have to enter a ladder match.' 'You going to be ready for it?' Peter asked concerned by my last statement. I sat down and leaned over to Peter, even though he couldn't see it I was trying not to smile. 'Peter come on, it is me, have I ever never been ready for a match. Remember that time in Japan during the King Of The Deathmatch?' 'Yeah, what about it?' Peter said knowing where I was going with this. 'I broke my arm when that guy hit me with that lead pipe covered in barbwire and what did I do?' 'You carried on with the match and went on to win.' Peter said pretending to yawn. 'Exactly. This match is going to be tough I know that, I mean all of the 7 other competitors will want the Cruiserweight title just as much as me, but I am not afraid Pete and do you know why?' 'Why?' 'Because as much as the other competitors will refute it, I am the only one out there who will be willing to take a risk. And I don't mean jumping off the ladder because any mindless baffon can do that. The risks I talk of are the risks of going for what I want, not holding back, letting the punches being thrown, getting beaten down and getting back up. People may say it is too early for me to hold a title in GW but I say it isn't and I am going to go for it Pete, hold nothing back!' 'Good for you Adam!' Peter shouted standing up, obviously getting excited by my little speech. 'Anyway Pete, I better get off and tell Rachel the good news.' 'Oh how is she?' 'She is great, she told me she pumped into you a few days ago.' 'Oh yeah I was just shopping for some essentials.' Peter said looking flustered. I couldn't help but chuckle. 'Don't worry Pete, I know it can get lonely on the road. I will see you later.' I said as I left his office. As I got outside and entered my car, I felt an air of satisfaction come over me... My life is great, I have a great job, I have great friends and I have a great wife. It felt like this was all a dream and that one day I would simply wake up and I would find myself back home with my mother looking over me, concerned as always. If this was a dream, I sure didn't want to wake up anytime soon because everything was so perfect. I pulled onto my drive and saw that Rachel's car was placed on the drive also. Again I smiled; I could already see her reaction when I told her the news. I got out of the car and locked it and strolled towards the entrance door. I pressed the button and after a few whirls from the mechanism inside the air vacuum released and I stepped inside. Again I pressed the button of the same thing happened this time a mechanical voice came. 'Entrance and house sterilised, welcome home Adam!' I pulled off my mask and took in a big breath of air; it felt good to be home. I stepped through the next door and entered my house. I moved towards the lounge and there relaxing in front of the TV was my gorgeous wife, Rachel. She lifted her head and smiled at the sight of me. She quickly got up and kissed me and hugged me. 'Hello gorgeous.' I said kissing her again. 'Hello handsome' She replied. 'How's your day been?' 'Tiring, I had to produce this stupid programme about Justin Timberlake's life, I mean he is only 21. He is only just an adult!' 'Well I am sure all the teenage girls are thankful for it.' I chuckled as I held her. 'It's not funny.' She said smiling and playful hitting me on the chest. 'I'm sorry sweetie, I am sure it was very meaningful.' 'Yeah right,' She said holding my hands and staring directly at me. A smile then crossed her face, 'Besides I have some news.' 'Me too, and you are going to be so proud babe.' 'Ok well you tell me first and then I will tell you mine.' 'Okay,' I said taking a deep breath, 'Right, well you know the GW are having their PPV in the next few days?' 'Yeah?' 'Well guess who is involved in a match for the Cruiserweight Title!' 'That is fantastic sweetheart!' Rachel said as she threw her arms around me and hugged me. 'Oh I am so proud of you! It is just what you were asking for, they have obviously seen your talent and put you up in this match.' 'Thanks honey,' I said as we continued to hug, 'So what about your news?' Rachel pulled away and looked at me with the biggest smile I have ever seen. She grabbed my hand. 'Come sit down.' She said as she dragged me towards the couch. We sat down and Rachel just stared at me smiling. 'Honey, what is the news?' 'Oh right,' She said snapping out of her trance, 'Well, Adam...we...are...going...to...have...a...BABY!' My mouth dropped, I couldn't believe it! I always thought because of my condition we could never have kids but here I was being told that I was going to be a dad! 'Sweetie, are you ok? Are you happy?' I realised I had been staring into the distance for the past 10 seconds. 'Sorry honey, it is just shocking! I mean I thought we couldn't you know...' 'I know but believe me this baby is all yours honey!' 'Oh my god!' I said feeling the excitement wash over me, 'How far are you gone?' '2 months!' '2 months! How come you never said anything earlier?' 'I just put it down to the stress of the new job.' 'This is fantastic Rach!' I said grabbing her and squeezing her tight.' 'Are you sure you are happy?' 'I am over the moon sweetheart! I can't tell you how happy I am!' 'Good because I have to ask you to do something?' Rachel said changing her tone. 'What?' I asked sounding concerned at her new tone. 'I need you to go see your mother!' 'What?!?!' I screamed outraged. 'Just listen to me Adam, I don't want this baby to grow up never knowing its grandma! I mean both my parents past away but you still have your mum and...' 'Stop, just stop Rachel!' I shouted interrupting her. 'There is no way, I am going to see that woman who made my life a living hell!' 'Why not?' 'You wouldn't understand Rachel! I left her and never looked back. She said she never wanted to see me again and I never want to see her again.' I was now standing up and angrily pacing up and down the lounge. Rachel got up and moved over to me and placed her arms around my waist and placed her head on my chest. 'Adam I love you, and I have heard all the stories, I have heard the hatred in your voice when your mother is ever mentioned. I understood when you didn't invite her to the wedding but it doesn't change the fact that she is your mother. Please honey go for me, go for your family.' I felt my heart melt, she was right of course. If I cared for her and I cared for the baby I had to go. I sighed. 'For you and the baby, I will go...' Rachel yelped excitedly but I held my hand up. 'But I am not promising anything!' 'Oh thank you sweetheart, thank you so much. I do love you, you know that don't you?' 'I know,' I smiled, 'I love you too.' We stood there and hugged as I felt dread and fear fill my body. After 9 years I was going to see my mother and I felt sick... So I am off to England, back to see my mother, back to see the woman whom 9 years ago I turned my back on. Will this distract me from my training for Whiplash? No it won't, I will continue to train throughout this journey, I will be ready for Whiplash.... And that is a promise!
