The Enforcers - II

19 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.19.05 SoHo, New York June 7th, 2005 'Well, is he okay,' asked a worried Bill Moresi on the phone with Ken Partridge, Jr. as a somewhat confused Ace Harding looked on. Bill had received the call while he and Ace were finishing up watching their most recent Tag Team Triangle match in order to fully come to an understanding of what they did wrong tow in the match. They were halfway into it when the phone rang. The past two and half minutes had consisted of 'yes' and 'no' answers from Moresi in the cases where he wasn't being cut off by Ken on the other end. Ace could do nothing but sit and listen... wonder what was being said that had transformed the normally cool Bulldozer in a bundle of nerves. 'We've gotta' go,' announced Bill as he clicked his cell phone shut and reached for his keys. 'Whoa,' resounded Harding. 'What's going on? I mean, you need to let me know something here.' 'It's Josh. He attempted suicide and held a gun to Ken's head,' explained Moresi quickly. 'What,' shouted Ace. 'He did what?' 'Look,' began Bill, 'they're at the hospital right now and Ken wants to go to his new place and get the mess cleaned up before they get home. He doesn't want any reminders waiting for Josh once they get him back home. He' already got a cleaning crew on the way and Gunner's going to meet us there with the key. 'Is Josh okay,' asked a now very concerned Harding. 'The doctor's said he would be fine, according to Ken,' huffed Bill. 'I'll go into detail on everything on the way, man, but we've got to go.' Ace simply nodded as the two exited Ken's training facility, The Elements Underground in the underbelly of his club and jumped into the yellow Hummer that awaited them in his parking area. SoHo, New York June 8th, 2005 'Thanks for all of your help, guys,' cried Gunner Addison to both Bill and Ace. 'I can’t see the four of you without each other and I just wish I could put into word how much the two of you mean to both Ken and myself.” To both Ken and myself. This struck Ace quite hard directly in to the heart. The fact that the three of them had been able to form a bond as more than just stablemates, but as friends really spoke volumes. Especially when you considered the bitter rivalry that he’d carried out with KPJ for so long. After all, Ken was the man that forced him out of wrestling for a year. Then, you also had to factor in the time that he’d nearly broken Gunner’s neck when he decided to piledrive her directly in front of the “Superstar” while KPJ was handcuffed to the ropes. “Gunner,” began Ace, “you and Ken, along with Josh and Bill” well, you mean the world to me. You are my family and I would do anything for you. I’m just glad that Josh is alright and that things didn’t get worse for all involved, namely Ken.” “Speaking of which,” spoke Bill Moresi, breaking his silence, “how is Ken?” “A mess,” said Gunner. “He’s staying strong for Josh, but he’s a ball of mayhem inside. He’s breaking down on a regular basis when it’s just the two of us.” “Is it because Josh held him at gun point,” asked Ace. “No,” responded Addison, “the gun he could handle. He would die for Josh” for any one of the four of us, really. What got him was the thought of losing Josh? the thought of going through life without him. Then, take into context that he blames himself, and you’ve got a mess.” “But,” began Bill, “there’s nothing he could have done to prevent this.” “Yeah,” seconded Ace, “and he did everything he could have done to prevent things from being worse.” “I know that,” began Gun, “and you know that, but we all three know how stubborn Ken is.” SoHo, New York June 15th, 2005 “So, how are the two of you feeling,” asked Ken Partridge, Jr. to both Bill Moresi and Ace Harding while Josh sat on the bench next to him in The Elements Underground. “Well, enough,” responded Bill. “But, dammit Ken, are your dropkicks always that stiff?” “Nah,” replied KPJ. “Yeah,” resounded Ace, “he took it easy on us tonight. They’ll usually knock your head off.” The four men poured into laughter at the comment of Ace regarding the Tag Team spar they’d just finished. All four of them were dripping with sweat, allowing it to pour down each chiseled physique. “I’m hitting the showers,” stated Josh with a smile on his face as he got to his feet. His black Raven ankh shirt was clinging to his body as he stretched largely, allowing the scar on his arm to be seen in plain sight. As he opened his eyes, he noticed Bill and Ace both looking and quickly pulled his arm into him. “See you guys in a bit,” he finished as he finally made his way into on of the six lush full-sized bathrooms of Ken’s gyms. As he exited, Bill and Ace huddled a touch closer to a smiling KPJ, who stood in a soaking wet white wife beater with a Superman “S” symbol on it in a black spray paint design and a pair of dripping blue workout pants with white stripes down the legs. “Well,” began Bill, “how’s he been?” “Who, Josh,” asked Ken in response. “Of course,” said Ace. “Look guys, Josh is fine,” began Ken. “He was going through a rough time, he hit the bottom of the well, and now he’s scraping he’s way back to the top. All three of us have been there, myself included. But, he’s smiling again and we’re working on making him happy again. You two need to worry about the Parking Lot Brawl against The Syndicate that’s coming up.” “We’re just concerned, Ken,” said Ace. “We want Josh to be okay. He” been dealt a harsh hand, and we realize that, but this has happened before.” “No,” stated Ken quickly and plainly, “this hasn’t. The Rome Incident was just that, and incident. He wasn’t attempting suicide, he wasn’t trying to hurt anyone, he just made a bad choice. This was his wake-up call, and had we all not already gotten ours, I’d say it was one for us as well.” “Look,” began Bill, “the reason we worry is because we care.” “I know,” said Ken. “Trust me” I do. And you two have no idea how grateful I am for everything you did at the house. However, I just think that this is a subject that should be left alone for the time being. Josh is a big boy and he’s learned from his mistakes. I say we let him keep following his light and living his life? he would do the same for us once we were back on the right path.” Josh stood in the hallway to the bathrooms with a tear in his eyes when Bill and Ace agreed with Ken and began to talk about the two moving from California and New Mexico, respectively, and moving to New York so the entire group could be close. These three men, especially Ken had put all of their faith into him. Josh made his way into the bathroom and turned on the shower before getting in and the three outside in the main gym began to make their way into three of the unoccupied five. The Elite was strong again, and it’s strength was brotherhood.
