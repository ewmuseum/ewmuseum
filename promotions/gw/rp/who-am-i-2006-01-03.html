Who am I

3 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-3-2006 It was the tale of one man’s life. The tale of what he was going to do. The tale of what he wanted to be. And the tale of what he could be. In the mist of the shadow’s of the room, a man stood walking alone. Foot over foot, step over step, he came through the darkness. Wearing his black button up wind breaker pants with a Motley Crew shirt on. His face, smooth with a red goatee. “What if I told you all I had an ego. Would you believe me by my own words?” His hair, short and black, nothing to connect to the red goatee that he was carrying. He was not of the Global type. His image was that of a young rookie fresh out of wrestling school. Something you’d find in some bar dressing out the ladies. “You wouldn’t would you” No. Though you’d believe the words that came out of a priest. Or maybe the words that came out of your own mother’s mouth. You see that is almost belittling me and calling me a liar. How do you think I feel?” Pressing his arms tighter together at his chest. Covering up the Motley Crew words. “Frankly I don’t give a s*&t! Because I’m not hear for you fans. I’m here for the money. For the gold. To bring the ages of oldness out of the light and into my world. Into the world that they were once at and that’s at the bottom of the piss bucket. Guys like Josh?” Grinning slightly. ??Jake Hix?” Spitting against the floor as if Jake Hix was there laying down. As if to say I don’t give a crap. ??Or even Bubba McCoy himself. It doesn’t matter to me because to be frank, no one intimidates me. No one can place the blanket over my eyes and force me to fear them. No one!” Walking out and towards the camera just a little more. Letting his baby blue eyes focus in on the middle of the camera lens. “To all that doesn’t know me, I’m David Crisp. The Gift. The Savor. The One. And at Reload, everyone in the back will know exactly why I am?” Walking passed the camera, letting the shadows behind him be the only thing that the lens sees. All that could be heard was the very footsteps of David and his rough voice in the background. ??The Main Event, David Crisp?”
