First Time Flyer (RP #1 for PPV)

27 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/27/04 On the way to Chicago for Maximum Carnage, Donny McNasty is traveling solo, his girlfriend Alexis Steele already had to be in Chicago earlier in the week for GW. Donny is sitting in first class as usual, he's reading a magazine called, 'Potato Farmers Weekly' his mother suggested it to him for the long flight. Next to Donny in first class is an old lady who is around her mid 80's in age. She sitting clenching the seat like there is no tomorrow while sitting next to the Platinum Title Champion as he's as cool as the other side of a pillow. Donny catches the nervous lady out of the corner of his eye, and asks her what's wrong... 'Are you alright over there?' The Old lady smiles nervoulsy, and answers Donny. 'Well just a little bit, this is my first time on a plane' 'Ah, there's nothing to worry about, I been flying in planes for years, from sea to shining sea' The Old Lady begins to relax somewhat and is looking at Donny as if she knows him. 'You know you look vaugley familliar. Where have I seen you before? Are you a porn star or something?' Donny obviously very uncomfortable with the question on the table, answers the old lady with class. 'Actually no, I am a proffesional wrestler for a promotion called Global Wrestling. We're on HBO every Monday night.' The Old lady's eyes light up when it finally dons apon her as to who this is. 'Oh my heavens yes, now I remember you! You're Donny J McNasty, your the King of Hardcore' Donny smiles in recognition. 'Well I guess that's where the porn star question came from.' The Old Lady blushes 'So what are you doing on your way to Chicago?' 'Well I'm going to be on a Pay Per View called Maximum Carnage. It's going to be a great show, you should tune in.' 'I just might have to do that. Pay per view you say, I don't know if I can stay up long enough. I usually watch re-runs of The Golden Girls and off to bed I go.' 'Do you think you could stay awake, say if I got you tickets for the event?' 'Oh my heavens that would be delightful' 'Well good. When we land and get out of here, I'll make some phone calls and you'll be well taken care of. It would be my pleasure to have you as a guest' 'Wait till I tell the girls from Bingo about this!' 'You a little more relaxed now?' 'Why yes, thank you so much' 'Well I know something else that will make the ride a little bit easier' Donny Stands up... 'Stewardess, yeah, I need two double whiskeys on the rocks' As Donny orders the drinks, they continue talking until their plane finally lands in Chicago. No matter where Donny goes, he always makes a friend for life with his generosity, and compassion...
