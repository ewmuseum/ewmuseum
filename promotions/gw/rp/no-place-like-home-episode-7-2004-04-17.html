No Place Like Home Episode #7 J. C. Diamond Global Wrestling Promo #1168 Date: 4/17/04 Location: Martin Residence, Miami NO PLACE LIKE HOME- THE RATINGS [Scene: Outside of the Martin Residence. J.C is to the right of the porch, sitting down in a green lawn chair. Ynonick is to the left of him, sitting in the green thin grass. The camera‘s zoom in on Jayson‘s firm grinned face.] Yesterday, the Martin’s had found out about Jayson’s DRUG ADDICTION. Sure it is something to worry about, but doesn’t everyone like to puff the magical dragon once in a while? Jayson has always been influenced by drugs and everything else associated with him. He was and probably still a gangster. Doesn’t every gangster do drugs? HELL YEAH THEY DO. The thing about it was, the Martin’s have lowered their standards to let a drug addict live amongst them. They were disappointed in Jayson. They expected better things from him. Well actually, they still don’t know if that bag of marijuana they found was his. They have a pretty good idea it was related to J.C, a guy who can not be trusted in there home- in there residence. J.C stares down at Ynonick as he leans back in the lawn chair, feeling the soft plastic rubbing on his back isn’t a good sign. “What do they expect out of me?” shrugs J.C,” “I mean, I have won everything they asked right? You have seen me; these cocksuckers keep hounding me about bringing the ratings of our show up. Ynonick is the only one home- besides J.C. The air around them is very thick and very humid. Both of them bare through it. “Well Jayson, what are you going to do man?” asked the young son of the Martin’s who was now playing with the top of the grass. Jayson turns back to the young child, who has recently, became a man. He felt kind of stupid talking to such a young child, with youth under his belt, but he had to get this out to someone. Sure the producers of this show were pissed at J.C, down right furious at his ass, but he didn’t give a damn about them. “Let me tell you,” Ynonick says, standing up and walking toward the large J.C. Jayson returned a huge smirk on his face- telling Ynonick to stay far away from him as possible. “I know you can bring these ratings up, you are J.C Diamond! You are a star above everything else. I am sure something will pop in your head. J.C was very silent. “Jesus Christ!” The superstar begins to protest after hearing Ynonick’s speech. “That’s the dumbest fucking thing I have ever heard in my entire life. Sure I am J.C Diamond, but I am stupider than you can imagine- drugs did that to me.” Ynonick smirks, not letting this slip out of his own little hands. “Jayson you know you can pull something through, I mean you are the one that brought me my man hood, I am sure something will brace your mind long enough for you to take action,” Ynonick continued, “trust me Jayson, I bet you have an idea in your head right now.” Jayson’s mind was completely blank when Ynonick said that.
