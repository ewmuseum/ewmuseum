Different?

5 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/5/05 Son of a bitch. That was about the only phrase Bubba McCoy could think to describe his day so far. It didn’t bode well for the rest of the day as technically the day hadn’t even started yet. But, as young as the day was, if the morning he’d already had was any indication, it was gonna be one of those days. First he about froze his balls off this morning as he was rudely alerted that the water heater at the house had conked out after jumping into a shower that would normally have been the temperature of a nice dunk in a hot tub after leaving it running for it’s normal 5 minutes. Instead he might has well have joined the Polar Bear’s Club as it felt more like he was diving into a hole in a glacier. Then of course there was his favorite greasy spoon where he sopped up a little breakfast every morning before heading into the office. Seemed like the only place in town that could serve up bacon, eggs and grits the way it was supposed to be. Not to mention it was pretty much the only joint in town that served up a decent cup of coffee the way it’s supposed to be. Strong, hot and black... not all that frilly horseshit they charge you 30 bucks a cup for every place else. Too bad the place was closed now. God damn health department. Jesus, a couple of damn cockroaches and all of the sudden everyone turns into a bunch of damn panzys. Crap, there what gives it the flavor. Looking at the sign on the door Bubba realized how glad he was to be a true to life Texas boy instead of one of these damn whiny bastard yuppies who ain’t got the constitution of a damn nat. It might not have been so bad if he’d been able to at least get the second best cup in town, but of course the truck stop where he went for that in emergencies had a broken coffee maker. Bubba was about as ashamed of himself as he’d ever been as he sipped on the Grande Mocha Latte he’d had to pay a damn arm and a leg for. Fucking pirates... as the little bell chimed and the door to the elevator at GW headquaters finally opened as it hit the executive floor Bubba promised himself that he ever ran across that bastard Starbuck he was gonna stomp a mud hole in the little yuppie prick. He swallowed his pride and another gulp of the fu-fu crap he was stuck with in his cup and stepped off the elevator, taking a quick stride down the hallway to his suite. Bubba cinched up the cup of coffee and the briefcase in his hand as he dug his shoulder into the door and pushed it open, stepping into the waiting room of his office. Maggie, secretary and the other half of his brain, greeted him with her normal smile. Or at least what seemed to be normal, though, if he’d been more observant he would have seen the strain in it. “Morning Maggie.” Bubba rumbled, not wanting to let his bad mood land on her. “Ummmm, good morning Mr. McCoy. ? she responded nervously. “Hold all my calls for a while, I got some paper work to look over this morning..” Bubba insisted, taking his norm path towards his office door. “Sure Mr. McCoy... but Mr. McCoy before you go in your office, there’s a little problem...” she began, moving from behind her desk and following him towards the door. “No, no, no... No problems this morning until I finish this cup of fu-fu garbage and get this paper work taken care of.” “But Mr. McCoy...” “Later Maggie!” Bubba growled as he turned the knob to his office and pushed the door open. That’s when he saw him. Yup.... One of those days. Maggie and Bubba both paused in the door way as Train, leaned back in the seat behind Bubba’s desk, his feet propped up on the fine, cherry wood finish of Bubba’s tabletop work space, sat with his arms casually folded and a arrogant smirk etched on his face. “I didn’t know what to do Mr. McCoy,” Maggie interjected before Bubba could ask what the hell he was doing there. “He got here about 15 minutes before you and wouldn’t leave. I was trying not to cause a scene... do you want me to call security?” Bubba just stared for a few moments. He wondered if the hot cup of glorified hot chocolate would be better used as something to burn the arrogant little bastard in his office than something to drink. Finally his scowl broke into a smirk of his own. “No.” Bubba said simple. “Close the door on your way out.” Maggie looked uneasy, but knew she’d be better off just following instructions. The door shut gingerly behind Bubba as he and Train locked eyes. “Get the hell out of my chair.” Bubba said bluntly, not trying to muster any sense of cordiality. “What chair” This chair?” Train questioned innocent, as if taken aback by the gruff reception. “I’m not interested in playing these games with you Train.” “What man” I’m just sitting in the chair that’s meant for the cat that’s running shit up in GW. I mean since that ain’t you I didn’t figure you’d mind.” “I don’t run things” Hahaha... What, did you fall and hit your damn head? This is GW my friend. GW belongs to one man... Me.” Train spins around, lifting his feet of the desk and planting them on the floor before standing to his feet. He slowly crosses from behind the desk and positions himself directly in front of Bubba, no more than 5 feet between them. “Oh, for real?” Train questions, now dropping his pretense of mannered speech. “See cuz you could’ve fooled me. Seems like Mr. VP’s been pulling the real strings lately.” “Oh,” Bubba says, shaking his head as he steps around Train and steps behind his desk, setting his briefcase and his coffee down on the desk top. “So that’s what you’re here whining about?” “Man fuck that!” Train rumbles, his smirk turning to a sneer. “Don’t talk that whining shit to me, I ain’t whined a day in my fuckin” life. I only speak the fuckin? truth when somebody’s trying to pull some muthafuckin? bullshit cuz they think they can get away with it. Well I ain’t one to let some little punk get over one me cuz he ain’t willing to pay me my fuckin? due! And this bullshit with Dafoe? First he sets up the main event at Wrestlecade with Me versus Frost and Brick versus Salaok... and then has the balls to turn around and make me and Brick team up with them bastards on the next Evo?! Yeah.... that’s some of that ol? bullshit I was talking about! Shit... if I didn’t know better? I’d say it was a damn set up. We going up against The Elite who’s a damn united front while the only people me and Brick got to watch our back is two cat’s who’d rather see us get hurt so they got an easy pass at Cade? What, is that supposed to be some sort of damn joke?!” “One,” Bubba begins to respond on many a level actually understanding where the man is coming from. “The Elite have a mystery partner and their ain’t no garauntee it’s gonna be somebody they’re fond of. Two the match is made. I can’t do nothing about it now, but...” “Oh, my bad.... I didn’t know you couldn’t change the shit. Hell I only thought you was the dude in charge around here.” “BUT...... You don’t need to worry. I’ll see to it Dafoe lives up to his obligations and makes sure you and Brick... and Salaok and Frost aren’t walking into a trap. Last thing I want is the main event at the biggest pay per view of the year for the biggest belt in the business to be tainted by some screw job a couple of days before..” “Mmmhmmm... Well I’ll tell you what. You do that. But while you’re at it” You give the VP a little message for me. I ain’t gonna let him or nobody else play these little games with my career... especially now that I’m about to be the biggest thing in this business when I put that world title over my shoulder. You tell him he needs to be careful about playing games. Cuz I don’t play... period.” Train, having made himself clear as glass, turns and whips the door open, the metal knob slamming into the wall behind the door and charges through the door back out into the hallway towards the elevator. Bubba doesn’t even bother telling him to close the door as he doesn’t see the need in wasting his breathe. Bubba slowly crosses towards the door to his office as he watches Train step into the elevator and the doors slide closed behind him with a ding. Of course the second ding was almost indistinguishable from the first, the second coming nearly on top of the first. Bubba pulls the door up to close it, but knows that’s a waste of time now. As the doors close behind Train the second set of elevator door slide open and out steps another visitor that Bubba doesn’t even have to question is there to see him. Brick rumbles down the hallway, his face painted with that usual pissed off look that it sports when he visits here. Knowing the door is just going to burst open in a second or two anyway Bubba just steps out into the waiting room. “Bubba!” Brick says stepping through the door of the waiting area “We need to talk. I...” “Wait!” Bubba yells, cutting the big man off. “Let me guess. You think this match on Evo with you and Train teaming with Salaok and Frost is a set up and The Elite have this huge advantage cuz they’re a real team. You think The VP is playing games with your career, but you’re not around to play. You want me to make sure the whole thing doesn’t turn into a screw job. That about sum it up?” Brick just looks Bubba in the eye for a long few moments. Finally he steps toe to toe with Bubba and peers down at him. “Well... if you know so God damn much... Do something about it!” It’s a tense few seconds before Brick finally turns, heading back down the hallway to the elevator. “Geez, “Maggie says shaking her head in confusion. “How in the world do guys like Train and Brick work together being so different?” “Different”!” Bubba asks trying not to burst out in laughter. “They might as well be God damn twins.”
