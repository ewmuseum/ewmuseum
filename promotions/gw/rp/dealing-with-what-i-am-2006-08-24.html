Dealing With What I Am...

24 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th August Struggle... It is a concept that has been the focal point of most of the world's history. Struggle in history usually concerns the minorities fighting against the majority for rights and such, the most well known of these struggles was that of the black people, there have been some great names that have stood up to those that oppressed them such as Martin Luther King and Malcolm X, even Muhammad Ali. These great speakers and great boxer showed the world that they would not take the awful treatment those ignorant people were giving them anymore, they stood up and said it like it is, they are human, they are equals and should not be treated any differently. That is all it takes, for a few select people to stand up against the majority and show them that they will not take it anymore. Unfortunately there are still a lot of ignorant people out there who are not willing to listen. Although we are all equal, idiots refuse to believe this and that is why we still do not have peace in the world. It is ignorance and pure stupidity that stops many from stopping and taking a second to thing about the world today. As that is all we need to do, think. It is a simple enough thing to do; yet we continue to struggle. I am no politician but I see the world before me being torn apart by politics just because they are unwilling to except someone else’s viewpoint. Would it hurt politicians so much to just listen? If they could do that, then we could stop struggling... Throughout my life I have struggled to come to terms with what I am. On the inside I am no different from any other man, I feel, I think, I love, but on the outside although again I am no different from any other man I am still treated differently. I have learned to deal with the strange stares as I walk down the street, I have learned to deal with the fact that some ignorant fighters are unwilling to fight me as they believe they will become infected by me, I have even learned to deal with being called a freak. All I have ever wanted is to be excepted for what I am, I am not saying that I haven't met people throughout my life that have excepted me, I mean I have Rachel, Peter and those many people who were willing to train me. In the federations I have fought in I was excepted but that was only because of my skill in the ring that people were willing to look past my disorder and see the real man within. Now I am in the GW and yet again I come across the matter of being excepted. My match at Evolution is my chance to be excepted as I know I am very capable in that ring and to be announced GU Valedictorian would tell me that I had truly found a place that was willing to except me for what I am. Even if I lose the match but have given it my all and that has been recognised by the crowd that night, I will still be happy. However till Evolution comes I live with the fact that at the moment to myself and the rest of the world I am nothing but a freak... I jerked awake in bed, yet again my heart was pounding and sweat was dripping from my body. I looked across and there lying peacefully like a sleeping beauty was Rachel. She had a smile on her face; obviously dreaming a happy dream whereas I sitting up in bed was still haunted by nightmares. I never usually sleep well before a match, as I am too pumped up and raring to go. However I had never felt this unsettled before and it disturbed me. Perhaps it was just the dreams or perhaps I had doubts about the match...no that couldn't be it. It had to be the dreams, I just needed to take my mind off it. I kissed Rachel on the forehead and placed the covers on her and slipped out of bed. I silently pressed the air release for the door. As it hissed I quickly checked it hadn't woken Rachel up, she had stirred slightly but only to move into a more comfortable position. I slipped out of the room and quietly closed the door behind me. I moved towards the bathroom and opened the door. Again I carefully closed the door behind. Making sure there was no stirring from Rachel I switched on the bathroom radio, to its quietest setting obviously. It was already pre-tuned to my favourite station, Wrestling FM. The DJ came through the speaker quietly as if knowing of my situation. '...And that was good old Nirvana with Polly. Now onto the News...GW is soon approaching their next Pay-Per-View, Whiplash where we will see current GW World Champion Josh defend against current GW International Champion Leinad Soma it is sure to be one brutal match. However before GW head into Whiplash they have another edition of Evolution to air and much talk has been going on about the opening match up between Kitty Mac, Rich Sherwin and Adam Myers. It is said that this match up is going to showcase the GW's future, all eyes will be on these 3 GU graduates to see who can come out on top.' I switched the radio off, I didn't need to hear anymore, I knew that the GW Front Office would be watching the match, I knew I would have to prove myself, I didn't need some DJ telling me that. I switched on the shower and stepped in. As the water ran down my face and back, I sighed deeply, something was going on in my head that I didn't understand and if I didn't find out soon it would drive me mad. I stood there in the shower just letting the water wash over me. I switched the shower off and stepped out. I dried off and headed for the spare room. I didn't want to disturb Rachel; I didn't want to concern her. As I switched on the light I was confronted with pictures of my past hanging on the wall. There were pictures of my training in Mexico and Japan, there were pictures of my triumphs and my failures, there were even pictures of my wedding day. I sat down on the chair in the corner and simply stared at the pictures letting the memories wash over me. As the memories came and went one particular memory kept repeating itself. It was a memory I hadn't thought about for years, it was a memory I wish I had forgotten, it was a memory of a time when I was first confronted with what the rest of the world thought of me, a time when I realised that I was a freak... It was when I was in Mexico, it had been about a year since I started training with Juan Diaz and I was just getting to know my local area. Juan had kept me cooped up in my specialised apartment, not letting me be distracted by anything. Being only 18 I saw this as unfair and was itching to explore Mexico, I mean how often was I going to get the chance to see Mexico, it is a once in the lifetime opportunity. Well that was the logic I tried to use but Juan made me realise that I was going to spend the next 4 years here, so it was hardly a once in the lifetime opportunity. However after a year Juan finally loosened my leash and allowed me to go explore. I was like a kid in a candy store, I wanted to visit everywhere all at once. However I had taken one thing for granted, my appearance... It was a brisk night as I walked down the street. Juan said that it was best that I go out in the evening or late afternoon as it would reduce the risk of getting spotted and hassled. The sun was just setting and it was casting a beautiful orange glow over the houses and buildings. I sighed satisfied, I was quite content with how my life was panning out, I was becoming a wrestler, and I was doing what I wanted. I looked up at the town clock, it was 6 o' clock, time to check in with Juan. 'Hello?' Came Juan's voice from the other side of the phone line. 'Hey Juan it is me, just checking in like you told me to.' 'It is 5 past 6 Adam.' 'So?' 'I told you to call me at 5:30.' 'It took me half an hour to find a payphone, they are pretty sparse around here you know.' 'I know I am sorry Adam, it is just I worry. People aren't as understanding as I am about your appearance.' 'It is alright Juan, I am pretty well covered up, I am wearing a cap and Mac, I don't think anyone is going to spot me.' 'That is good Adam, well make sure you are back at your apartment at 10 I will give you a call to make sure you are home safe.' 'Hurry up with the phone amigo!' A rough voice came from behind me, I chose to ignore it. 'Okay Juan I will talk to you later.' I hung up the phone and turned to look at the man behind me. He had a shaven head and a nasty looking scar on his right cheek. He was a few inches taller than me and was using it to tower over me. He was looking at me with complete disgust on his face, it was a look of disgust I had never seen before, it was like his disgust was mixed with hatred. I simply shrugged it off. 'The phone is all yours now amigo!' He didn't say anything, he just looked at me still with the same disgust/hatred on his face. I could still feel the look as I walked down the street. I turned around and he was just stood there with phone in hand looking at me. I continued to walk trying to ignore the look. I walked to the bay and walked towards the end of the walkway. I looked over the water at the sun setting behind a valley. I sat down on the end of the walkway dangling my legs over the edge. The look of the man soon flooded from my mind as I felt my content return. What a wonderful life I had here... 'Hey amigo!' A familiar voice came from behind me. I should have just ignored it but instead I turned around and there stood the man from earlier with a few of his similar looking friends. 'You got any money?' The man asked trying to intimidate me. 'No' I replied trying not to be intimidated. 'Don't lie to me!' 'I am not lying!' 'Yes you are! If you wear a something like that you have to have some money!' He said pointing at my bio-suit. 'This suit was given to me!' I said defiantly. I was now standing up as they circled around me. 'You know something amigo? I don't like you!' 'You don't even know me!' I said logically but if fell on deaf ears. 'You know what you are amigo? You are a freak! Look at you in your strange suit, who do you think you are?' 'A human being!' 'No, no, you are not a human being, you are not normal,' The man said poking me, 'You are not like me and my friends, you are not like anyone, you are a freak! And we don't like freaks around here.' 'Well if you class you and your friends here as normal, then you know what? I don't think I want to be normal.' 'Oh that is big talk from a freak. You want to back up your words, freak!' I didn't say anything I just stood there. The circle tightened as the man was right in my face. 'We are going to teach you a lesson and when we are through freak, you are going to wish you had never been born.' As the last words came from the man's mouth one of his friends behind me swung and cracked me round the head with what I later found out was a bat. As you can expect I fell to the ground and then all of them began kicking me, and punching at me, screaming at me over and over again 'FREAK! FREAK! FREAK!' The last thing I saw was the last bit of the sun setting behind the valley and how wonderful it looked. Apparently someone had seen the gang following me and had informed the police. By the time they got to me even though my suit had protected me I was still bleeding. All I truly remember after that is the immense pain I went through as the doctors reset my bones. I was never allowed out again, not until I had completed my training, which as you know I did. Surprisingly whilst on my 10th tour of the Mexican circuit I saw the man whose name was Antonio White and lets just say I showed him what a real freak could do! Now why did I tell you this story? To provoke sympathy? No. To provoke fear? No. I told you this story because throughout my life I have struggled to become what I am and I am proud of what I am. The thing is you can kick me down, you can call me a freak, but I will always get back up. You know what they say, you can't keep a good freak down!
