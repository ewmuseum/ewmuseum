The Last Good Bye Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #216 Date: 5-20-03 Location: Greens Cemetary ((As I look at all the Tombstones. I feel less and less like living. They all seem to resolute. All so Peaceful. The way that I'd like to feel. I am kneeling down in front of one. My hands folded across the top. Then i put my head down and begin to weep. This is the Grave of my Son. He has been dead now for one Night. The Hardest Night of my Life. I see my son fade away to be with the Creator. I am all alone in the world. On the stone that my tears now fall on, is Written. 'Samual Venages Azrul.' He was Only Seven Years old. Seven. His lef had not even started. His wordl hadent even began but in his death. MY world began to end, and the one that should be here now with me. My wife, Laura. Is Not. I have not heard from her since she left me. My Daughter Turns four in Eleven days. My Son turned Eight. In Three Hours. He would have been on this earth Excatly Eight years in Three hours. His Life cut Very short by Cancer. My Grand Father Had it. His Grave Next to my Son's. He was a Full Mexican Man. As well as My Mother and Father. As well as my self. My Son. Wasent. He was Half. His Mother bveing white. Half Mexican. Half White. He had what it took. He was on the Baseball Captin. He was on the Swim team. At Seven. My son was gifted. That Gift was Taken Tragicaly on May 17th at Three o Five in the after Noon. As i watced from the Glass room up top. Most Parents wouldent be able to sit there and watch as their Son's Neck was split open and they began to work on getting the cancer out. I sat there. I would almost be willing to bet that an imprint of my fingers was left on a chair there. There wasent a tear that streemed down my cheek. I thought that he would come out alive. I thought that my sonw ould fight as hard as my self would have. I knew that he did. he just diddent have the Know how that i did. I stopped weeping. Then stood up. He had just been put in the ground. The Dirt Fresh under my feet. The grave ston just as cool and un weathered as it had been when it was brought. I knew that there was nothing in the wordl that could replace my son. Nothing. Then suddenly There was a hand apoun my shoulder. 'Please. Dont Cry any more.' I spun around to see who was standing there and lo' and behold. My wife of Five Years, Laura Vendacrat Azural. If she hadent alaready changed her name. Was standing there. a Black veil covering her face. How had she come to know that her only Son had died. I knew that he was sick. She left me when he was sick. 'How could you? You show up here. Put your hand on me and Tell me to stop crying? Since you left i havent shed one tear. Nowwhen my son dies. I begin to cry and you Tell me to Stop!' There was more then intensity in my voice. There was pain,Greif and Sorrow. 'Please, Robert. Dont.' 'Dont? Dont what? Tell you what I think? For Five years. I have had tosit back and liten to what you thought. To what you said. I fucking through with it. Now your going to listen to me.' She had never heard me speak to her with this tone or with those words. She shrank back with fear the first few. Then came back knowing my morals I wouldent hit a lady. 'But....' 'But Nothing. You left me. For No reason. You took my Now, Only Child. and left. Just up and hauled ass. Took every thing that i had even bought for you. The ear Rings that your wearing now. That necklance. Even that dress. Everything. And left. Laura. The sight of you Disguts me.' She had nothing to say to these words. She knew that i ment them. I delivered them with such intensity that there was nothing left to say. I had stated every thing that she planed to throw back in my face. i Turned my back on her and left. I walked away from all that shit that she waas going to try and feed me. Eight days ago if you would have asked me if i loved her i would have said with allmy heart. Now. I hate her. She is with out a shadow of a doubt. The First person that i'd like to see dead. This may sound more then mean but i dont care any more. I am filled with Hate and there is no other reason to live. Then a thought filled my head and i stopped. 'When do i get to see my Daughter?' 'This will be the last time ever. She's in the car.' Bitch. I swear to god i had a good mind just to take Angel and Leave. I dont know to were but just to go. Thats it. I had nothing left to live for. I was taking my daighter and leaving. For good. For ever. Laura would never ever seen her again. Nor My self. Shje diddent care though. She had all the money in the banks. She took the check book and all my credit cards. I had nothing. Then again i wouldent have to pay them. That residence was gone. Burth to the ground by a freak fire. This brought a quick smile to my face. I was about a fifty yards from the little green civic. sitting inside i could see the head of my Daughter. She looked just as Angelic as the very first day i had ever seen her. Thus i named her Angel. Angel Gabrial Azural. I loved her dearly. Now i had the chace. I walked to the car. and knocked on the window. She was drawing something on a paper. She looekd up with her big blue eyes and a Big smile corssed her face. 'Daddy' She jumped up and hugged me. then i pulled her away. 'So. How do you like liveing with Mummy?' 'I hate it. She never takes me way were.' 'Wanna come home with Daddy?' her eyes light up. I knew that she loved me more then her mother. Her mother i guess had become a drunk. What did i care. 'Ok then. Sit here and write mommy a Good Bye note and i'll be back.' 'Ok' Now i would be happy to a point again. My Daughter was with me and i knew she was safe. I walked around to a Black and Silber Hummer, and opened the door. Then i hopped in and drove it to beside the Green Civic. Angel was standing outside of the car with a note in her hands. When i opened the door she handed it to me. Iopendd the note and read aloud what it said 'Mommy. I hate you. I hope this is the last time i ever see you. Good Bye.' I diddent know what to do. Be happoy becuse she hated her mother. Or be sad becuse she was so hatful. Fuck being sad. I was happy. I throw the note in the drivers side of the car and we were off. Never to see Laura Azural again. Ever. Fin!
