Family Greetings: Gangster Shit

undated

Date: #1 High Noon Strange that he felt so mellow. As Train sat behind the wheel of his car it suddenly occured to him that this was the first time he hadn't felt on edge in weeks. The pressure on his shoulders had been mounting steadily since Wrestlecade a month ago and hadn't let up since. Now going into High Noon, the biggest night of his career so far, where there should be assurances, there were only question marks. And he was staring down the barrel of what is likely to be the toughest match of his life. Yeah... It had every reason, obvious and subtle alike to be a pissy day. For one thing.... Train normally had no patience for stupid drivers, always about 3 wrong seconds away from an attack of road rage that would likely send somebody to the emergency room. As it was the Lincoln Navigator was only lurching along at about 30 miles an hour on a freeway who's limit was twice that. Train sat, cool as a cucumber, dressed comfortably in his black velour Sean Jean warm up suit, his dreads pulled back into a loose pony tail, behind the half ass flow of half ass driver, the exit for Dallas International Airport he had set out for an hour early seeming to crawl closer, bit by bit, in John Woo like slow motion. This was normally cause to be irate. Second of all... How was he gonna get rid of that anchor he had wrapped around his neck called The M.O.B. When Train thought of all the bullshit he went through with the whole Makonie thing to be part of that clique it made him wanna puke. All that bullshit so he would have to carry, not only his jock, but everybody elses? Talk about some bullshit. Terrelle Greeniso... Yeah, he talked a good game. Too bad he couldn't ever back that shit up. Title shot after title shot after titles shot... What's it gotten 'em. Nothing. He can blow shit up outside the ring but can't get the 1, 2, 3 inside it. Same for Micheal... Same for Blayze...who should never have been put down on the set in the first place. Bunch of punks... They ain't accomplished shit and now they think they can say where Train can and can't go? Fuck all that... Yeah, he popped in on WMW. So what? Terrelle might be 'The Godfather' but he ain't Train's fucking momma. He don't owe no one any explanation about where he goes or why. Besides... Pacino played the role better. But, all jokes aside Train knew the Greenisos. He knew what they did and what they were capable of doing. He knew it was a mistake getting wrapped up with an outfit like that..lying to himself that it was just wrestling they'd keep the action confined to the arena. Train had known that was bullshit from the first time he told himself. But it's like they say... If you lie to yourself enough you start to believe it. But it was getting clearer to him. He knew what The Greenisos were about because, as much as he wanted to change his life...that's the same amount he didn't want to change his life. But M.O.B. life had served his purpose. It reminded him of all the crap he'd tried to leave behind back in Chicago...and all the reasons he wanted it to stay there. Too much drama. Train understood what he was going to have to do. He was going to have to cut them loose. Talk about drama. This beef would get ugly. It was stupid he'd come here to escape the life and wound up creating a situation where he had to escape all over again. They were going to be on him inside of the ring and out. Fuck 'em. Don't matter how much money or how many people they have. Train had the advantage because he knew Terrelle's arrogant as had the whole thing twisted. When it all goes down the M.O.B. is going to think they're dealing with some kind of changed man. Naw... The man wanting to change and the man that has changed? Those are two very different animals. What the fuck else could be riding on this High Noon match? Train casually took a sip of his 20oz Cherry Coke and pondered that loaded question. Let' see what else do we have... Oh right... The Platinum Title. He'd been after the strap from damn near the jump. He was about to waste his time with that Primestar bullshit when it existed. He'd had that title in his sights from the second he stepped through the door. Now... It was time to put up or shut up. Tough spot considering he'd have to beat two cats he knew was gonna come to play...especially in the main event of a major pay per view. Especially that little bastard Deacon Holmes. Brick was a tough son of a bitch, but Deacon was the one he was worried about. He's the bastard that'll damn near kill himself just to hurt you. There wasn't no telling what type of shit he was gonna pull out of his ass. Oh well, whatever he wanted to pull out, Train knew what he was going to have to pull out when all was said and done... That piece of Train's shoe that was gonna get broken off in his loud mouth, show boating, monkey ass. What was the last problem? Oh right... Train didn't just want to win this match. He HAS this match. At first he thought that bitch McCoy was just trying to play a little hard ball. Bust Train's balls a little as payback for all the beef they've had in his relatively short stint in GW. But Train only had a week from High Noon to get a new contract or he was out of the company. And even after the wake up call he tried to send showing up in WMW...nothing. Not even the hint of an offer for a contract extension. It was some petty as shit...but it was real. She didn't want Train around. He was too hard to control. But that wouldn't mean a thing if Train put the Platinum Title over his shoulder. After having to vacate the Television Title, wasn't no way she could afford to lose another champion. She'd about have no choice but to offer Train another deal. He had to win The Platinum Title... gainst two cats that wasn't about to lay down... At one of the biggest pay-per-views of the year... In the main event. No pressure. But still...it wasn't that bad. That sense of calm had been with him constantly for a couple of days. Probably since the moment he found out his baby sister was flying all the way to Dallas to see him. Train had never been the type that needed a whole lot of approval from people. The more people didn't want him to have something the harder they worked to get it. And when people he didn't like, didn't like what he was able to accomplish the more he knew he must be doing the right thing. That's why the GW fans didn't like him. He didn't feel the need to kiss their little ass and tell 'em how much he loved 'em for their fucking approval. But he had to admit it. When everyone in the arena would be booing him at High Noon.... It was nice to know there would be one voice cheering him on to the end. The fans were just water. Blood is always thicker. =================== The double glass doors slid open with a woosh that was barely audible below the normal hustle and bustle of a terminal at the Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport. Train steps through the double doors amid the steady flow of people and faces, making him almost anonymous as a woman's voice reminds the commuters driving past the front of the terminal of the illegality of parking in front of the building. Lines at the ticket counters stretched out aways as the memorial day weekend quickly approached. Train walked through the terminal towards the gates and the security check point as, every now and then, heads turned in his direction, followed by finger pointing towards the GW Superstar. Generally he just nodded to the kids who were excited by his prescense as he made his way through the security stop (only having to step through the metal detector three times after removing more and more platinum from his person). As Train continued to storll, seemingly drawing more and more attention with each foot he strode, he took note of the time on his platinum Rolex. His sisters plane wasn't going to be here for at least another 20 minutes. That gave him plenty of time to get rid of that bottle of cherry coke that was no pestering him. Train spotted the sign for the mens bathroom and ducked into the opening, walking through the short hallway into the inner sanctum. Despite the volume of people filling the airport the restroom was relatively clear. Train immediately spotted an open urinal and pulled up into place to handle business. It takes a few seconds for train to free his partner in crime from it's confines, before he can do his thing. Train lets out that short grunt all men give when the harrassing of their bladder ceases. The high ring of a steady stream of liquid hitting porcilean hums through rest room as Train continues to relieve himself. Train's mind waunders. He almost doesn't notice the man who pulls up a stall down from him. He probably wouldn't have even noticed if not for his stature. The guy had to be at least 6'2'. He had to weight 250 if he weighed anything. Plus their was something else... The way he was dressed in the slightly shiny dark blue suit...the way the guy moved... 'Don't I know this muthafucka?' Train thought to himself. Slowly...subtly Train cut his eyes to his left. It only took a second to register. Yeah... he knew the son of a bitch. Now, the question was...what the fuck was he doing here? Could be a coincidence. The bastards being pissed with Train cuz he didn't clear his trip with them...like he was some sorta kid on a fucking field trip. Still it could be a coincidence. Even though he knew they were all jealous... two ppv's...two title shots. And what had they done? Jack shit and jack hopped his ass on a greyhound and dipped outta town a long time ago. Now they didn't have shit to look foward to while Train was staring at the Platinum Title. Now...knowing all that... It really could just be a coincidence...that this muthafucka showed up at the airport...the same day Train was there to pick up his little sister. It could be.... Bullshit. The relief process is complete. Train gives his large lambs tail the customary two shakes and tucks it back into it's customary spot. Train quickly backs away from the urinal, turning to the man he figures must've followed him here. His appearance had given Train a few questions. It was time for Micheal Greeniso to give a few answers. 'What the fuck are you doing here?' Train inquired in a quiet, almost in audible tone...the only tone he could muster to keep from yelling and making a huge scene. 'What?' Micheal retorted in an innocent tone, turning to see who had asked him the question...as if he didn't already know. 'Ohhhhh... Train, what the hell is going on my friend? Long time no....' 'Don't play fucking stupid with me.' Train growled back, still quiet in tone, but now spiting fire. 'What the fuck are you doing here?' 'Whoa,' Micheal spat back. 'You keep talking to me like that and I'm liable to stop being so fucking friendly.' 'You don't tell me what the fuck you're doing here I'm liable to not to give a shit what you do or say son.' 'Number one?' Micheal says, zipping his fly. 'I ain't your son. Number two? You need to calm the hell down. I'm here to pick up Terrelle. He had to make a quick trip to New York. His flight should be in, in a couple of minutes.' 'Really?' 'Yeah...what did you think?' 'I don't know what I was thinking then...but I know what I think now. I think it's probably been decades since Terrelle flew commercial so tell me another one.' Micheal turns to Train, both sets of cold eyes locking firmly into place... firmly on the other set of eyes. There is a short silence, at least as far as voices are concerned. It's a toss up as to whether the electronic hum is from the florescent light fixtures or the tension in the room. 'What the hell is your problem man?' Micheal inquires, with a tone that almost made it seem genuine. 'Ohhhhhh... I get it now. Yeah... What's the matter Train? You a little paranoid? You thinkin' maybe me and Terrelle might be a little mad about your little trip to Wrestling Midwest? Huh? You think we might try to take a little revenge for your betrayal? Is that it?' 'Betrayal?' Train asks. 'I think yall need to stop watchin' 'Goodfellas'. The shit's starting to make you melodramatic.' 'Melodrama? hahaha... Is that what you think this is Train? Melodramatic? Terrelle and me show the respect in your abilities to include you in The M.O.B. and then you go out and start negotiating with another company without a word to us and you think us calling that a betrayal is over dramatic?' 'Yup.' 'Just how the fuck do you figure that Train?' 'Easy. You ain't my momma. I don't owe you know fucking explanation, I don't owe you no heads up, I don't owe you shit. I ain't got to clear a damn think with yall. I do what I want... when I want... why I want... because I want. Simple as that.' 'Oh no, it ain't that simple. See when you stepped into The M.O.B. you joined a family. And...' 'Man, shut the fuck up with that wanna be Godfather shit! This ain't no fucking movie and I am not to one to be played with.' 'Fuck you! You won't be played with cuz this ain't a fucking game! You knew who the fuck we were when you joined up! Don't get this wrestling shit mistaken with who we really are Train. The second we think you're a liability to the family you will be taken out...and I'm not talking about in the arena.' 'Oh, for real?' 'Yeah, for real. Now look... I've gone to bat for you with Terrelle. He flew way off the fucking handle about this WMW shit. He was talking all kinds of shit.' 'That's cool. I been waiting for yall to try all kinds of shit.' 'Yeah well it don't have to come to that. I just told Terrelle, what I knew you would say yourself. That you were still M.O.B. and you would prove yourself to us again.' 'Is that what I was gonna say?' 'If you know what's good for you.' 'Man, fuck that! Me prove myself to yall?! Muthafucka, when are yall gonna prove yourselves to me?!' 'What the hell is that supposed to mean?' 'You know what the fuck in means Mike! I didn't hook up with the M.O.B. for the fuck of it. Power...money...titles... Yall muthafuckas ran your fuckin' mouths about a lot of shit...and what? Huh? What the fuck has that gotten me?' 'A title shot in the main even at High Noon?' 'Boy.... I suggest you check that shit at the door if you want me to stay as civil as I have been. Don't even think about taking credit for the shit I got by working my ass off. See, that's the shit I'm talking about, right there! You muthafuckas talk a good game, but when it comes right down to it you can't back up any of it. You wanted to talk about taking over GW and stealing the power away...when was the last time you even won a fucking match?!' 'Watch yourself.' 'Fuck that. Why do I need to watch me when that's about all you bastards do is watch what the hell I'm doing?! The only reason anybody still talks about the M.O.B. is cuz they know Train is down with it and, quite frankly, a brotha's back is starting to hurt from luggin' yall around on his fucking... coat tails.' Micheals visage has slowly grown from detatched to scowling, the scathing review of his performance and that of the M.O.B. cutting into his gut like a knife. He starts to step forward when the patter of large footsteps and the jingle of metal quickly fills the hallway behind him. Two airport security guards quickly step onto the scene. Both gaurds position themselves across from the two GW Superstars, still facing eachother, but turning their glance to the officers. 'We heard a lot of yelling,' said the older of the two officers, the third stripe on his arm signifying that he's in command here. 'Is there some sort of problem gentlemen?' there is a beat as Train and Micheal lock eyes one more time. Each knows what the other is thinking. Another time...another place... We will finish this. 'No, no, no, officer...' Micheal says, expertly diffusing the situation. 'We're old buddies. Haven't seen him in a while, just catching up. Little too loud I guess, sorry about that.' 'Didn't sound like a friendly session.' responds the younger officer. 'Huh? Oh, I'm just breakin' his balls a little officer.' Micheal casually remarks. 'Is that right sir?' The older officer inquires of Train. 'Yeah, we're just fucking around.' 'Well, the airport is not really a place for that.' says the older officer again. 'I know you two are GW guys and all, but we can't let a fight happen in here. I think you boys need to move along.' 'No problem officer.' Train says, brushing past Micheal. Train strolls towards the hallway leading back out into the terminal, wishing he'd had the chance to use Micheal's face to mop the floor when he hears the voice of the man that's intruded on his calm. 'Say hello to your sister for me.' Says Micheal flatly, the hint of menace, hidden from the security guards, but by no means lost on Train. Train had known it all along. This wasn't a fucking coincidence. Train didn't know how Micheal had found out, but he knew from the jump they had somehow expected him to be here. He knew how to handle it. The cell number would be changed...he'd have some one sweep the phone at the house and house itself for bugs. And, if they found bugs in the house the security system was getting a serious overhaul. It didn't take him long to formulate the plan of action...he had plenty of time as he froze for a moment...the whole time, amongst formulating a plan to keep this from happening in the future, also reminding himself that Micheal Greeniso wasn't worth going to jail for. Wasn't worth what would happened if he followed his instincts and ripped his fucking throat out in front of these cops. Finally, gathering his cool he slowly turned back to the small group. 'Yeah...' Train coldly responded to Micheal, looking him dead in the eye. 'You just be sure to say hello to your mother...your father, your sister your brother, your uncle, your aunt, you cousins and your children for me. Actually better yet... One day...if I have a good reason? I'll say hello in person.' Train turns, walking away as Micheal looks on, his heart beating just a little faster as the scene fades to black.
