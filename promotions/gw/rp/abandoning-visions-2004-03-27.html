Abandoning Visions

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 27 The jungle walls were thick, an impenetrable wall to all those who were not supposed to be there. It kept the dangerous people out, and it kept the dangerous animals in. Inside the jungle walls, was there own little world. A perfect society, unharmed by the cruel hand of man. Inside was the true future or the world, the great circle of life. The animal kingdom. Walking through the thick brush came the so-called King of the Jungle. The huge lion made its way, lumbering slowly through the jungle. Not a care in the world, the animal was about as arrogant as an animal could be. Not a care in the world, not a fear inside the barriers of the jungle it slowly walked calmly. It’s golden brown fur shimmering under the rays of sunlight coming through the overhanging branches of tress. His huge mane, a symbol to all of his power. In the small waterhole far downstream, laid a crane. Whimpering in pain, very hurt. Somehow, it had been hurt by another, something had gotten a hold of the beautiful bird, and left it crippled to the world. The lion was making its way towards the small waterhole. And it seemed as if the beautiful white crane, with its silken looking feathers rustling in the wind was due for its end. A merciful end at that, but as the lion got closer, it was apparent that it had other things in mind. In a strange manner, the lion threw the crane onto his back. The crane gratefully accepted and took the ride. Its soft feathers rustling in unison with the fur of the lion, the wind whipping through both. The lion did not change, it kept the same slow lumbering pace, but it began to make its way from view. It began to walk towards the walls of the jungle, and it began to try and find a way out. To a place where neither the lion nor the crane had ever gone before. They were going to become free together, they were going to leave everything they knew behind? Ichiro opened his eyes and smiled, his meditation had gone well. He was indeed calm, cool and collected. He smiled as the answer to where Rokku and Yoshiko had gone, now seemed slightly clearer. He smiled a smile of relief as a tension of burden was removed from his shoulders. He got off of the ledge quickly and made his way back down to his apartment. He made his way through the hallways, making his way back down to his room. When he got there, he saw that Jushin was just about to leave his room. “Where you going?” he asked. “I planned on getting some training done,” answered Jushin. “Come inside for a second, I need to talk to you,” came Ichiro’s reply. Jushin nodded and followed Ichiro, into his apartment. Ichiro opened the lock and quickly flicked on the lights, and threw his keys onto the counter top. He walked over and sat down on the couch, Jushin followed suit. The two brothers now sat opposite one another. “Well, what do you need Ichiro?” asked Jushin. “I need to know what you’re thinking about this whole Legion situation,” said Ichiro. “The Legion is dead, what about it?” answered Jushin. “Well, that’s just it. We have lost Jin, Rokku and Yoshiko. But that does not mean the Legion is dead. We still have Brick, we do not have to let The Legion die,” Ichiro started. “There is nothing to let die, for if you can’t see that it is already dead, then you brother are blinded by a false hope. The Legion was great, a strong unit that was solid, unified to stand up for what we believed in, but those things are all in the past now. There is nothing left of The Legion. All that is left now, is The Souls of the Black Lotus. You and me,” exclaimed Jushin. “Brother, that is all it has ever been. That’s all GW has ever been about, The Souls of the Black Lotus, the Nakazura brothers, us staying true to one another. Rokku and Yoshiko did not abandon us, they were forced out. By TUE as I suspect, whoever set us up. They needed time away from that, and we can’t blame them for needing a break. For a family of that style, it would be much different than it is for myself and yourself, Jushin,” said Ichiro. “What do you mean it has only ever been about us”! Have you already forgotten about the Legion, or our time with TUE?!” Jushin’s voice rose slightly, but he was far from angry. “No brother I do not forget, nor am I senile. I merely believe that no matter what faction we have been in, you have been the only one that I have trusted fully. You are the only one that I can trust fully. I would trust you with my life, and I would hope you feel the same way?” Ichiro paused for a moment before he continued speaking. “When TUE divided us, I felt terrible, having to face you was one of the most difficult battles of my life. Jushin, no matter what stable, gang, group, clique, no matter who we have been with, to me it has always been about our success, not the success of all. It has always been about the Souls of the Black Lotus.” Jushin could only stare blankly at his brother. His words were deeply touching, and surprisingly Jushin was humbled by his big brothers words. He could not reply, only stare as Ichiro kept going. “So, just like TUE we have broken apart from The Legion. But, do you remember what has happened since we left TUE. The titles that we wear to the ring every time we compete now, a small part of what we have done since we left TUE. And now without The Legion, we have the opportunity for more. We have the opportunity for a World Title, no matter who wins the match, it will be a tribute to both of our skills,” Ichiro said. “I am with you brother, know that. Through thick and thin I will be here for you, and at Clash of the Titans, I will be with you,” answered Jushin. Jushin put his hand out, and Ichiro placed his palm over top of Jushin's. “Yunaiteddo itsumademo!” said Jushin. Ichiro nodded, before repeating the words. “Yunaiteddo itsumademo!” With that Jushin stood and began to head towards the door, Ichiro watched him as he left. Jushin still reminded him of the boy he once was. Playful at heart, but trying to portray a strong, stern and serious exterior. Ichiro, smiled as he watched Jushin go through the door, most likely heading to do some training for the PPV this weekend. Ichiro sat and thought about his brothers words. They were a great comfort blanket, and it was just the sort of assurance that he would need going into Clash of the Titans this weekend. He remembered back to the days of their youth when they used to fight with many other boys. He chuckled now, but as he thought about it, he could see their close bonds forming early on in their lives. Ichiro watched as the crowd of boys gathered around his little brother. Ichiro shook his head, he did not like fighting at all. Nor did he like that the other boys seemed to think that he and his brother were favored. Even though Jushin and himself went through much more strict and tough training than the normals. Ichiro quickly ran over to his brothers side. He stood tall, behind his brother as the group of boys started to stand in their tracks. No longer advancing. Ichiro smiled arrogantly, he looked across the group. They would have had no problem beating on either one of them individually, but with both Nakazuras, the boys did not stand a chance. The stare down lasted only a few seconds before the group of boys dispersed. Ichiro smiled at a job well done, life was tough in the temple sometimes, but he would not have enjoyed it any other way. The brothers united were a force to be reckoned with, and from the rumors that had gone around and the way the group of boys had reacted today, Ichiro knew that people took the brothers seriously. That respect, in the unity of he and his brother, made him extremely proud. And that pride was still certainly on his mind, and would be going into Clash of the Titans?
