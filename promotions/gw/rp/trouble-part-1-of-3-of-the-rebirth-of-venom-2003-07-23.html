Trouble(Part 1 of 3 Of the rebirth of Venom) Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #456 Date: 7/23/03 Location: somewhere It was, a dark and stormy night, after I heard about the miscarriage, I had a good amount of beer in me, along in the car was my girl. Out of nowhere, a deer jumped out in front us, I turned to miss not realizing at the time I was heading down a bank, the girl was throw her from the car and I just got a few cuts on me. The police finally arrived and I was charged with a D.U.I when the officer told me about my girl friend I was shocked I broke down and cried. After all that happen the officer drove me home. I managed to get a few hours of sleep after a long night, now Cobra is gone and Jennifer to. However, things are starting to look good in a way. Roz is about to make his return. Plus, Jay Vyper is back a good friend of mine. -The next day- Morning, the night after my accident, wondering how much my fine is going to be? So I made my way down to the courthouse to pay for my fine, I arrived earlier which is good thing to show for the judge. “Your fine, Mister Venom Alexander for driving under the influence comes to $10,000.00.” I could not believe my ears on how much my fine was, but I deserved it, for what I did. Luckily I have tones of money so I wrote him out a check, and it was over with. I decided to hop in my dodge, to think. What should I do, hopefully I will be back in the ring, as I was thinking to myself. When Roz returns, we are gunning for the tags titles. Since both of us are former champions, Roz being a cruiserweight champ. And I being a former platinum champ also a Iron Fist winner. I feel, we have something to prove in the tag team department. The current tag team champions Genisis are going to be a good test for Roz and me. That is if we cross paths somewhere in the future. However, right now I am more focus on the present. Who will be my next victim? It really doesn’t matter as long as am in the ring doing what I love to do. That is to wrestle with the top GW superstars. After hours of thinking I made my way to the newly rebuilt National Midnight Star now know as The Orbit. I made my way in, I notice Alex at his usually place behind the bar. “Well if it isn’t Venom, welcome to the orbit.” Alex then handed me a class of milk. He must have heard about my little run in with the cops. “Milk, something I wouldn’t expect in a bar.” I gave Alex a odd look when he handed me the class of milk. Reason why, I got the milk. Alex went up and turned on the T.V and on CBC news it showed my picture about the D.U.I and that the girl with me was killed in the accident. “What were you thinking Venom? Drinking and driving, on top of that you had Jennifer with you and you crash; now she is dead.” I could tell Alex was upset with me, I did not blame him. Since being, Jennifer was his cousin. “I feel bad what happen, it just that Cobra passed away put a lot on hold. Then she had the miscarriage I felt everything was falling apart for me. Therefore, I took upon myself to have a few so her and me got in her car and we talk. Then next thing I know down the bank doing 80 around the turn she flew out of the window.” Alex then stopped me, he could not hear no more, and I was about to shed a tear of sadness. We just looked at each other. The look he had in his eyes, he wanted to beat the hell out of me. “I think it’s time to change the subject. When do you go back into the ring again?” Alex said. “Next week being the soonest. After Master of the Mats being the latest.” I then handed Alex my empty class. He asked me if I needed a refill, I told no thanks that I would catch me later. I made my way outside, and the rain just pouring. When things could not get even worse, my truck was broken into. My CD/DVD player was stolen, something I saved a lot of money for. Whoever in that kind of mind would take something from me, must be asking for a ass whooping. I look all over for clues but none. So I made my way back home, got into bed. T.B.C
