Undelivered Letters

16 June 2003

Date: 6-16-03 are the sins getting staler? does every moment move past you? does it feel like forever? and shouldnt you be laughing too? ----------------- I sit down at my desk, with a pen in my hand. Blank paper lays on the desk before me, nothing but the date on it. After what seems like forever, I take a drink of the Dr. Pepper next to me, then dig in to the letter. To one of the guys I want to kick the shit out of most, What you did was absolutely dispicable. Hans and I did jack shit to you and Jay. And what do we get in return? Chair shot for Hans. I got a god damn broken arm at the hands of two guys I thought had my back for life. Guess all you had my back for was to stab it, eh? Why? Why did you feel it necessary to kill the group you said has always been a part of you? You said it yourself, Young Blood has always flowed through your veins. Wrong. The only place you had Young Blood that night was on your hands after you and Jay attacked me and Hans. Luckily, Jay and I faced off as soon as my arm healed to where I could use it again. You, however. You escaped. I never got my chance to show you the real meaning of what Jay and I used to say all the time when we were known as Cold Blood. Ben, you don't fuck with Young Bloods. Derek Frost I set the pen down, get up and walk out of the room. A few minutes later, I plop back down in the chair in front of the desk and slowly eat an oatmean cream pie. I flip the paper over and start a new letter on the other side. To one of the best friends I've ever known, We've been through a lot. From the day I met you, I never thought you'd turn your back on me. Guess I was wrong. That night, everything got turned upside down. Sometimes in life, you do something you have to do for yourself. The only time I've ever done anythingthat would benefit me and only me was.. well, I really can't remember. Man, the only thing you had to do to get you chance at the world title, was ask Hans. Yes he was still kicking ass, but he would never intentionally keep his tag partner and good friend from acheiving his goals. Instead, you and Jay turn your backs on Hans, Johnny, and also me and Cindy. We were one big family. If you had asked me to drop out of the ranking to give you a chance I'd have done it, and Kruger was the same way. But no. You let Jay convince you that the only way to get anywhere was to follow him. Look at me now. I'm just starting to step out of his shadow and its paying off. He over shadowed me my entire career and I got nowhere because of it. Did I stab him in the back? No. I waited patiently for it to be my turn, and now that my time is approaching, everybody is out to get me. Which brings me to my next point. Kullypso made a mistake. He let me believe he had Cindy, when really she was with her dad like planned, but her phone died and I couldn't reach her. Kullypso used his wife Eliza, or Syn if you will, to make everybody--even me--think he had Cindy. Now that I know the truth, there's nothing keeping me from holding back on him. Kullypso is a marked man, and his fellow Harvesters know it. They WILL help him get me out of the picture. Salaok and I have had a personal grudge since our days in UCWF, so he wants me gone too. And as for the world champion.. well, Sage has what I want, and there's not a damn thing he or anybody else can do to slow me down right now. Which is exactly why on the next card, I'm going to make the announcement of my match that I'm planning for the next PPV. You remember at Dead Zone when I won the match vs Kullypso? Well, that contract let me put me and MK in any match I want. It never said I couldn't put other people in it. I know you want to know what match I have planned, but just keep watching. Well man, I just wanted to write you a letter and let you know everything I've wanted to say to you over the past year or so. Sincerely, Derek Frost
