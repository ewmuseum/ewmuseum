Wrestle at Wrestlecade: Episode II

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/04/04 Wrestle at Wrestlecade Episode II: Ultimate What?! '... Rich, Rich, Rich... you must come to harness the power of Ultimate Muscle! You must learn the ways of the only thing powerful enough to get you through Wrestlecade. Jay Quickness, Max Roswell, Mike Storm; among other people... they're all going to have Chojin levels far higher than your own UNLESS you're able to get your head about the ways and mannerisms of the Ultimate Power.' screamed the Korean trainer as the scene began to open for episode two of this explosive run in to Wrestlecade's 6-Man Cruiser Title contendership match. As already known by far more people than required, the winner of this contest was going to be pressing ahead for GW's Cruiserweight Title. The picture finally began to open in the back of some cold, horrible gym. The surroundings weren't the best that money could buy but since Rich Sherwin was having to earn his way through the ladder from the bottom of the list, neither he or his trainer had much say about the level of facility that could be used. Despite being an employee of Global Wrestling, Sherwin was considered too much of a joke to be granted permission to use the companies own personal gym section. Rich stood still in these cold quarters, squatting and standing up again with apparently huge weights held together with a bar passing over his shoulders. The athlete bobbed down and then rose up, looks of strain crossing his face as the going was getting tough. 'Keep it going, Rich! Keep moving! You know you're going to have to do a lot more than this if you're wanting to be the ultimate chojin!' he screamed, continuing to throw oodle upon oodle of encouragement in the way of his student... in the way of the man who could move to things that nobody dare imagine. Prior to this point in his life, Rich may not have had all the tools of the trade to make him a Champion... and he may not have had all the intensity to become a Champion... but he did have the heart of one. 'Nine-hundred and ninety-seven... Nine hundred and ninety-eight... Nine hundred and ninety-nine... One Thousand!' Rich bellowed, a new number coming through with the completion of each and every squat. For once, he was pushing himself to the very limit. Right now, there were no jokes to be made about his competition... there were no slurs to throw toward athletes and absolutely no homosexual remarks about J. C Diamond. The Ultimate Chojin continued his training, squatting like a man possessed. 'Congratulations, you are pushing through these training sessions as though they hardly exist. I knew you had the kind of power, desire and heart to fight hard where it counts. Just like four weeks ago at the fated 'Clash of the Titans' show where you managed to finish fifth, you will break through the mould and move to WIN a shot at the Cruiserweight Title. Granted... it's not the title I wish for you to be wearing but in the words of mother nature herself, anything good HAS to be worthwhile.' the Korean trainer babbled, his words having no actual influence on the Chojin now. Instead, Sherwin was running his way through the zone; sprinting his way through barriers and walls alike. To him, failure was not going to be an option. It was not going to be a concept: Roswell - Defeated. Blayze - Defeated. Diamond - Defeated. Storm - Whatever. Quickness - Too Slow. Wrestlecade wasn't going to be about the whole point of taking part and looking good for the people. Oh no, Rich knew that his fans weren't going to get the same kind of kick out of failure considering his complete performance last month. He knew that if the start to his Global Wrestling career was going to be deemed a success, he was going to have to run through all five people and show the world that he can be more than a joke, that he CAN be the greatest wrestler walking the lands. 'Wong Kenobi... this is probably the first time that I've been through a training session without any hillarious goofs or bowls of food.' Rich said, finally stopping his exercising, placing the equipment on the floor in a gentle manner. Kitted out in a pair of shorts, sandals and a vest, Sherwin looked to be in the best shape of his entire career. So much for this apparently being his first real training session, eh? A few moments of silence existed between the trainer and student before Rich decided to continue, '... When I go into that ring on Sunday, this contest is going to be for a lot more than just a shot at a Championship. This is going to be my way of thanking you for spending all the time in the lead to train me. This is going to be my way of repaying you for showing faith in me.' '... There, there, you make it sound as though you've fought and won the contest already. Rich, I can feel the power being exerted from your body and I can understand how confident you have become since the start of your career here. True, you have also managed to defeat over fifty percent of the competition... but still, you have yet to truely know the power of both Storm and Quickness. These two men, as mysterious as they are to you, could prove to be a couple of your biggest challenges yet.' Wang Kenobi was quick to point out, desperate to ensure that his student was able to keep his level of confidence in tune with his level of sense. After all, the over-confident are always prone to make mistakes at the worst possible times. 'I know what you're saying... but I can't afford to be too cautious in this contest. According to polls and votes, I'm very close to being the favourite going into this bout. Those other guys are going to be looking for a fight, looking for a way to pull one over me and eliminate the strongest at an early point in the bout.' Rich stated, maintaining his level of concentration and concern. 'Still, despite going into this bout as one of the marked men, you shouldn't feel so confident until you have managed to pull over everyone involved previously. Storm and Quickness are the threats... and for all you know, they may be the bodies that you focus on first. Nobody likes different people... especially when they're people whom they've never encountered before. Have faith in man's desire to destroy the unacceptable.' Wong Kenobi stated, his voice almost demanding in tone. It was as though he was telling Rich that one of the only ways he can win this is by believing in the brutality of man, the burning hate for anything and everything that fails to fall into place. 'Very well...' replied Rich, trying not to dismiss his own ideas too soon. He wanted to show Wong that he could branch off with his own mannerisms too, almost a complete form of independance for a second or two. '... I'm going to listen to everything you're telling me, but I'm not going to step down on the confidence scale. I'm a Chojin and I know no limitation to skill.' he told himself, over and over. 'Hmm, given that you've been able to pass through these hardships so easily... well, easily in comparison to what I assumed you'd take them at, I think it's about time I give to you a few skilsl that could help you in your quest. Their power is unforetold... unknown to man and have never been executed in the real-world. The concept's of these moves scare even myself at times for their damage to the body can be extensive and beyond all comprehensions of scientific repair. Granted, I assume you will only certain techniques in certain situations... as some, are FAR more devestating than others.' Wong speculated, bringing further attention from the Chojin athlete. Rich could only gawp in amazement at the revealing of new moves. '... You mean, you have new moves that you're going to allow me to add to my own inventory of destruction?' Sherwin asked, his voice carrying a tone that was both beyond amazement and fearful. Never before had anyone offered to teach him moves that could be deemed as extraordinary. For many years, he'd sat back on the moves of his family... but with evolving times, comes the need to run that extra mile for worthwhile results. As the scene ended for yet another day in the preparation for Wrestlecade, we can only begin to ask ourselves what these moves Wong speaks of actually are. Never performed in real-life? Just when you though EVERYTHING had been done. =x :: END :: Next time... Episode III: Chojin Power~!
