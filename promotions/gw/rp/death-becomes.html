Death becomes?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have seen death and I have seen it walk. I know of the pain that it brings and exists with. That is why I will not ask for you to be brought back.' - Salaok Davis after Lisha's death(2003) ---- For years, their blood had marked the earth. For years, they had done as they had wanted, with little to no resistance. They were the powers of the underworld which was controlled by darkness, hatred and vengence. They were The Harvesters and through the recent battles from within the group, caused by blood shared, they came close to losing all dominance which they once had. Some say that time heals all wounds, but some wounds are unable to be corrented and there is but no choice other than to turn a back and pray that one day things will work out. Ignoring the problem until it rears it's ugly face again and dealing with it the only way they knew how. Destroy it before it causes too much of a problem.. The war of blood between Quartz and his family was one half of the major problem with this group of nobel warriors as both of the giant's son's were powerful enough to stand along side their father, and yet chose not to. Their reason was because of their mother and wife of Quartz. A wife he had loved but grew apart from as she learnt of what he was truely like. The other war of blood was that of Salaok and his brother Mani. Salaok hated his half brother as he was the result of his father's cheating ways and this blood kin was all that was left of the old man. Chris Davis had been killed, his asian girlfriend had met with the same fate some years ago, but Mani had escaped. But now with backs turned on those who had wronged them, The Harvesters had vowed to rebuild. Building back their legacy of donination and control of so many. With vengence running rampant with his thoughts, he sits in the lounge room gazing into the fire as it's tongues lap up any oxygen within it's grasp. His throne like leather chair, seems to almost dwarf Salaok with it's massive arm rests and a back that seems to stand a good six foot high, positioned perfected in the dead center of the room giving the feeling the room is more like a den than a lounge room. However, similar chairs are placed in a semi circle stretching off from Salaok's chair but those are all vancant, and yet he is not alone. 'Times have changed my old friend, changed more than we ever thought would have been possible but they have.' 'You need not to tell me this Salaok, for I too have seen it with my own eyes.' Reponds the low tone voice of Quartz as it carries across the room to Salaok's awaiting ears. Quartz's hand pressed firmly against the window, feeling the slight chill of the night through the glass pane as he looks out over the fields which this massive building had been built on many many decades ago. His eyes continuously returning to a small grassy hill some distance away from the house, and yet, it had been proven that it was still close enough for a snipe to send a bullet through the window and hit it's purposed target. Neither man really in tune with the thoughts of the other as their minds wonder off on their own seperate paths and yet, still will some contection to each other. You don't spend most of your lives together without having some sort of link between you form and your thoughts slowly become the same over the years. The glass of red wine reaches Salaok's lips and a small mouthful is taken as he tries his hardest to enjoy and respect the drink but these days, it is nothing more than alcohol. The tastes and smells that so many people relate to the different red wines are of no interest to Salaok these days as it merely enters his mouth and after he swallows it, it's gone. Why mellow in something so small and pointless in life? He was once told that he had to learn how to enjoy the finer things in life, but enjoyment was something of the past now. 'What happened to the time when we controlled everything? You know, when The Lair owned so much and had so much power that people didn't even both to challenge us. These days, it seems like we are walking around with targets painted on our backs. Even McCoy has grown enough balls to stand up and fight against us for what he believes in.'Salaok says while looking at the glass of red wine in his hand, swirling it around, trying to understand how people can see different colors when all he sees it red. 'Actually Salaok, he was willing to stand up against you for what he believed in, not us. McCoy still doesn't know that I am even alive, but soon he will. McCoy can stand up against you because you are just one man and it's easy to keep an eye on one man, but much hard to do so with two, or three or more.' 'If you are trying to say that once The Nevermore is rebuilt, then things will change. Change in our favor for once?' There is a silence as Salaok waits for the responce from Quartz, and Quartz thinks on his answer. Their backs both turned towards each other and yet knowing that the other is there. He pauses as images of his thoughts form and haunt him, forcing him to close his eyes tightly, wanting them to go away and never return, but he knew they wouldn't. 'I can not guarantee anything Salaok, you know that as well as I do. But time will tell, much like it has in the past. It was only while we stood side by side that people have feared us, for alone we are only half as strong. And now Orthane has returned to our side so strength increases again, but not only does strength, but the powers in which he holds. He has spoken about an army in which we could tap into should the situations be right and us to be willing, but it maybe become uncontrolled. Plus, Kullypso still stands with us does he not?' Nodding his head before he replies, 'He does, and always will. Kullypso will never faulter The Lair nor any of us which makes him a piece of the puzzle, more valuable than most would think him.' 'You two must not forget about Frost.'Orthane walks into the room with his hood pulled over his head shadowing his face. Both Salaok and Quartz turns their heads from their position of gaze to look at Orthane but then go back to their stares. 'I would never forget about Derek, he's proven to be an ally that although doesn't understand our background, does believe in the same hatred in which has feuled us for centuries.'Salaok lets out, making sure that both Orthane and Quartz know that Frost is indeed one of them incase they were unsure at anytime. 'But their is more to him than his hatred and his recent cold heart, I have seen some things that the future will hold for him, and although his recent past will haunt him always, new connections will cause him great power. A power that we would be foolish to walk away from and could possibly end a war that has claimed the twentieth century as it's victim. I can not speak on it any further as I am yet to see the details, but he is important to us.' With Orthane's explaination lacking some, if not all detail, both Salaok and Quartz sit silent as their personal thoughts begin all over again. Orthane, although used to the silence which has been apart of his life for basically every day of his existance, seemed to find a need to speak about what he knew and how the future would be strong again for the group which once held the country by it's throat. 'As for the army you spoke of Quartz, you are more than correct. not only do I know of a way to build you an army, but I know those who would best suit what you need. Your thoughts are plagued by those of the past, with you Salaok it will forever be your family. And for you Quartz, it will be your family and their friend Shigeru. But these are not the images of the past in which we need to reclaim for they are times of failure and that is something I will never dwell on. Look to the fireplace.' Salaok and Quartz, respecting the words of the man who knew more than they, both turn and look at the fireplace as it's flames dance around like ballerina's on speed. Orthane points his hand towards the fire and hold it out with his palm to the room as a small ball of blue energy forms in his hand and then shoots across into the fire. What was once just a fire, now is more like a monitor into what Orthane sees and wants as a part of his plane to help the duo rebuild this army they were once apart of. Images of the likes of Jeremy Irons, Salaok original mentor in The Lair appear and then are followed by others. Their old master and his brother appear in the flames only to then be replaced by another set of images, this time there is an image of Chris Davis. A growl emits from Salaok at the mere site of his father and then it changes again, this time to the brother of his beloved wife, the man knows as Mephiston. But Mephiston doesn't stand alone, he has others behind him. 'Stop! Why Mephiston? Why his allies? Do you not remember that it was us who killed him?'Asks Salaok is annoyance in his voice. 'Although dead, Mephiston is also in pain over the death of his sister and the fact that they will spend their afterlifes at different ends of the universe. He knows of your pain Salaok and for the sake of his family, wishes to help.'Orthanes' voice, calm and in control of the situation as always, however, not liking the eyes in which Salaok shoots at him. 'I respect you Harvester, but the images of the warriors you've displaed to me are all images of the dead. What use is a corps to me?' 'Firstly Salaok, control your tone in whch you direct towards me, secondly, a dead corps is of no good to you. However, a live corps is!' Salaok and Quartz both turn their attention back to the flames, their faces showing the exact same thing that their minds are going through. Complete and utter confusion. Could it be possible? The way in which Orthane intended to bring life back to The Lair was through death and now Salaok understood what Quartz was refering to when he mentioned it could become uncontrolled. Salaok had only seen it done once before through The Lair and what returned was a darker, demonic version of what had originally walked the Earth. It was the very reason why Salaok had never requested that Lisha or Darrell be brought back to life. Because it wasn't life. That was something worse than death.
