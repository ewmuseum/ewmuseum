A meeting with the boss

A meeting with the boss Stan Stone Global Wrestling Promo #313 Date: 6/10/03 Location: GW Headquarters, before the Coma 'Damn,' Stan thought 'My watch has stopped...' he shakes the gold rolex next to his ear a moment to check if anything was loose. He'd been waiting fifteen minutes in this lobby for his appointment and boredom was beginning to take it's toll. Dressed in a black Armani suit with gray tie, with his hair impreccably groomed, he looks about the lobby, it was nice, marble floors with the company logo engraved in the center, wonderful furniture and the drapes brought it all together. Stan smiled, it was a nice day in Texas and the sun was high overhead, not hidden behind clouds like Seattle. His smile vanished though and was replaced by a calm demeanor when the oak door opposite him opens quickly, a stout man, barrell chested and bald walks out of the office, gives Stan a look and leaves the lobby. 'You may go in now Mr. Stone.' states the young secretary in a dry tone. Stan smiles at her as he walks by and steps into the office of Global Wrestling owner Bubba McCoy's office. It was big, big is rather an understatement but gargantuan is an overstatement. It had a wonderful rug (orinetal or not Stan wasn't sure) and the biggest, most expensive looking desk Stan had ever seen. Huge, probably made from oak, looked sturdy and behind it was a man who looked just as solid. Bubba McCoy was a large man by all means, built like an ox from his years of ranching, a real cowboy if Stan ever saw one. He looks up from a piece of paper lying on his desk, 'Take a seat Stan.' he says with warm albeit gruff voice. 'It's been a long time since I've seen your face in person, of course I catch you every now and then when I flip over the WCWF but those are only fleeting glances, I haven't really gotten much of a chance to see you perform recently.' Stan sits down, his chest swelling as he smiles revealing pearl white teeth as he speaks in a confident tone, 'Well Bubba, I think you've been missing a good thing then. I'm not the same person you knew five years ago.' he laughs to himself. 'Look Bubba, I know you and my uncle never got along but don't think I'm my uncle or anything like him, I only saw him a few times in my life and they weren't the best of times so know that I intend on coming to Global Wrestling with every bit of proffesionalisim expected.' 'You're right Stan, you have changed. I used to know you as a punk kid, now you're going on being thirty years old. Well, I have the paperwork here, all you have to do is sign and you'll be receiving an anual pay-check from Global Wrestling...' he slides the paper forward taking a pen from a cup that says A+ Dad. Stan smiles as he glances at the cup, the tough as nails cowboy always was something of a sofety at times, glancing down at the waiting contract he thinks to himself 'Remember the basics Stan, do what needs to be done and keep your foundation sturdy' he reaches forward to grab the pen clicking it a couple times and spinning it between his fingers. A few fleeting seconds later and he passes the paper back to Bubba, his name written cleanly along the dotted line. Bubba slips the contract into a manilla folder and places it into a drawer underneath his desk locking it closed. Standing up he walks around the desk and picks up Stan's hand in his shaking it. 'Welcome aboard Stan, welcome to Global Wrestling.' Both men say there goodbyes and Stan leaves the building, walking out into the hot Texas sun wearing the look of a man who just made the best decision of his life... ***** The plane touched down on the tarmac at seven-thirty in the morning a light fog hung deathly still in the air masking the comings and goings of the busy airport. With the stairs in place the side hatc opens and through a cloud of smoke steps a haggard looking Stan Stone; unshaven, his face has a five O'clock shadow offsetting the clean black suit with white tie he's wearing. He puffs on a large cigar, a duffle bag in his other hand as he disembarks from the plane walking over to the transport vehicle that would take him to his car. He sits down shotgun, removing a small flask from the inside of his jacket pocket unscrewing the top and taking a few swigs inbetween puffs of his cigar. 'Things are about to change' Stan said to himself as smoke poured from his open lips, and Stan beleived it would be for the better, he knew he couldn't get involved with the personal affairs between wrestlers in the GW, he had no intention of fighting any type of crusade for any damn righteous cause. It felt good to be home though, he had a few weeks to prepare and there was no place like home to get ready, he takes a deep breath of cold air through his nostrils, the wind whipping his hair into a frenzy as he was driven from one end of the tarmac to the other. Every day he thought back to those days in Japan, all the blood he spilled in those arenas just so he could come back to America and do the same thing... funny really, made him laugh every now and then when he thought about it... he rubs his chin moving his fingers along the faint white scars on each cheek. Stepping off with his bag Stan walks out to the parking lot, passing a few security guards. As he nears his car, a V-12 Aston Martin Vanquish he drops the cigar butt onto the ground grinding it into the asphalt with his shiney black shoes. Yeah, it was good to be home, but it would be even better to get to work again, he missed the strain on his muscles and he knew if he didn't return to his gym then he'd get soft and when you got soft you're foundation began to crumble and when the foundation crumbles you crumble, Stan worked his ass off and because of that he knew he would never crumble. Ducking low and stepping in and sitting into the drivers seat he grasps the wheel, flexing his powerful hands, hands that had helped him win, hands that would help him win again... Yeah, things are about to change, yeah they were about to change for the better... and yeah, Stan couldn't wait.
