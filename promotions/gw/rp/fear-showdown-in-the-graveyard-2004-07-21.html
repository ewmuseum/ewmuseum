Fear: Showdown in the graveyard.

21 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 21st, 2004. The night sky that served as Earth's ceiling hung above me, darker than the Devil's heart as clouds blotted out the moon and stars, while raindrops fell as if they were the tears of angels crying for the sins of mankind. Seated in the drivers seat of my new Jeep Wrangler, painted gray with a shade of black marking each side with a lone stripe, I could barely make out the road in front of me as the cascading rain bounced from the windshield before me. The windshield wipers were being put to quite a workout as they served their purpose of keeping my vision free of obstruction, nonetheless the highway that set before me was a mass of darkness. The anger of God was almost visible as lightning illuminated the entire road and the forest that was lying next to the road. The treetops danced with electricity as a thunderous crack could be heard, punctuating the flash of heavenly luminescence. The rain itself seemed to fall harder by the second as the pouring skies proved to be weather that was fit for neither man nor beast. No idiot should be out here in this type of weather, but then again, I'm not the everyday idiot now, am I? The wind rattled the leather roof of my Jeep as my wheels spat water from the road showering the fenders of my vehicle and a sudden chill shot through my skin, sinking into my bones and raising the hair of my arms on end. It was painfully cold…especially for July. Had I not known better I would have been forced to believe that Extraterrestrial forces had turned the night skies into their own personal playground or perhaps even a battlefield. Yet such was not the case, instead it was a cold, rainy night and one that I was determined to enjoy, come what may. The cold however was rather enjoyable and it forced me to reach for the Caf? Latte that I had procured at a local Starbucks before hitting the road. Pressing the sixteen ounce Styrofoam cup to my lips I took a slow sip of the scalding rich and creamy liquid, letting it roll over my tongue and delight my taste buds with it’s delicious tingle. It trickled down my throat quickly before hitting the pit of my stomach and strangely with a mere sip of this wonder-liquid I felt as if my inner thermostat had already been kicked up a few degrees. With a satisfied smile on my face I returned the cup to it's resting-place before turning my eyes elsewhere and to the glowing green numbers of the digital clock placed in my jeep. '12:42.' I read aloud, interrupting the beautiful strains of 'Sea of faces' by Kutless that echoed throughout my jeep as the CD player pumped out the lovely tune. With a satisfied smile on my face I glanced up from the clock that set in the center of my dashboard and returned my focus to the road, and that's when I saw…it. A shadowy figure stood in the center of the road no more than fifteen feet in front of my vehicle. The figure came to life as the beams of my headlights shined upon this person. It was someone rather small, wearing a black cloak with a hood tossed over their head to wick away the falling rain. The hood came to a point and cast this person's face into a dark shadow leaving their identity a mystery to me. So intrigued by this enigma I nearly forgot to hit the brakes until I had reached the distance of ten feet away. My heart threatened to spring from my chest as it pounded violently, ignoring all of this I closed my eyes as I slammed a black scuffed Chippewa boot down on the brake pedal, inwardly praying for the safety of the poor victim in my path. The water rebelled against my brakes and I slowed down much slower than I would normally, before finally skidding to a stop fifteen feet past where I would have hit the man or woman in my path. Opening my eyes I unbuckled my seatbelt and thew open the door of my Jeep, springing into action I swung from my seat and onto the highway, slamming the door of my jeep behind me without even bothering to turn off the ignition. I paid no heed to the fact that I was dressed in a pair of 330dollar Versace jeans with a sleeveless black shirt made of the finest silk pulled over my torso. The freezing rain bit into my skin, leaving me chilled on this late July night yet I somehow managed to ignore all of this. And I ignored it because my mind lay with the person in my path that was undoubtedly lying on the road in a crumpled heap of quivering flesh and broken bones, the victim of an unfortunate accident. Yet what kind of people would deliberately put themselves in harms way? Had it been an attempt at suicide, or was there something more at play? Suddenly stricken with a keen sense of worry I balled my right hand into a fist and proceeded with caution as I made my way to the point where I saw the skid marks begin. Glancing down at the pavement and the aforementioned skid marks I noticed something conspicuous by its absence…the body of the victim. Had this person risen from such harm as if they were an invincible entity? Had I somehow crossed the lines of paranormal and ran over a minion of Satan? Or had I became the unintentional hit man of an angel that was watching over the safety of humanity? Lost and confused my mind raced as I looked for a logical explanation or anything to determine what had happened. Had I been seeing things? Was I losing my mind? Could that be it? Had I gone mad? No, I hadn't, because it is well known that one who questions their sanity is no doubt completely sane, for a lunatic never beseeches the answer to his mental instability. Yet…what could have happened? Brushing the shaggy locks of black hair from my eyes that had been plastered there by the rain I spied something out of the corner of my bluish gray eyes. There! In the woods a shadowy figure had just stepped behind a tree. It all seemed too suspicious. Something didn't feel right about this. The victim disappears and suddenly I exit my vehicle? This one had horror story written all over it. Visions of being robbed, beaten or even murdered flashed through my mind as I recalled a half dozen horror movies that I had watched in my adolescence. 'Run!' The voice inside my head screamed at me as my common sense appealed to my curiosity. Yet something was compelling me…it almost seemed as if something called to me. The shadowy figure was no longer in sight yet I could almost sense it's precise location. 'Get out of here…' Logic and common sense pleaded once more as they fell upon deaf ears. Slowly taking a step forward I realized that I must obey the inscrutable exhortations of my soul and pursue this enigma into the woods to discover the mystery of the invincible shadow. Taking off on a run I followed my inner radar into the woods, splashing across the mud next to the highway rain soaked into my skin as I began to shiver. Stepping into the forest I bypassed a tree before being slapped across the face with the wet branches of towering pine. Whispering a grumble I took another step forward as a twig snapped beneath my feet…the shadow had heard me, no doubt. Pausing to listen I realized that my suspicion had been confirmed as I heard a rustling in the trees that lay to the north as the shadow picked up its pace, hustling through the woods in an attempt to lose its hunter. So the shadow was human after all? Surely it must be, for an angel or demon would no doubt vanish into thin air or float away, yet the shadow had to walk and even run like a mere mortal. 'Stupid…of course it's human, what else were you expecting?' My own inner sarcasm hissed in my mind. Hot on the trail of the shadowy figure I raised my hands to ward off the branches as I made haste through the thick forest neglecting to absorb it's beauty of sniff the lovely fragrance of the tall pines. Inside I could feel myself growing closer and closer by the second, and that's when I saw it…the shadow! The shadow figure stood only ten feet before me and once it heard my breath whistling through the night air it spun on it's heel and once again began to run away from my approach. 'Stop!' I yelled, at the shadow in vain as it continued to elude my presence. Why must this shadow run? And more importantly why do I feel that I must follow him, her or it? These were all rhetorical questions for the answers were far beyond my knowledge yet within my grasp if I could find the shadow. Suddenly the forest ended, as I stepped into a broad meadow…with the shadow nowhere in sight. Glancing about the field I could see no further than a foot in front of me as the sky was still blotted out, almost as if God had cast a curtain over the sun, moon and stars, leaving Earth to suffer in the darkness. I had limited vision and with such there was no possible way that I could spot the shadow in this broad field. It was almost as if I had been hunting for the proverbial needle in the haystack. Yet still my determination has long been my greatest attribute and my Achilles heel at the same time, so with rigid defiance I took a step forward in the field as I began my battle with the dark night. The ground beneath my feet gave a little as the mud welcomed itself into the cracks on the soles of my boot, occupying these lines and weighing my boots down. Taking another step forward I knew that the mud had caked to the sides of my boots and they would no doubt be in serious need of leather treatment as soon as I found my way back to civilization. Or should I say…if I found my way back to civilization? A third step took me further North before my foot slammed into something hard and unmoving. The Shadow?! No, it was too solid to be a human figure, but rather some immovable object. An object anchored firmly in the ground despite the mud that seemed to surround the field. Dropping to my knees in the mud I ran my fingers over the object. It was solid, smooth and cold. Made of a very hard rock, granite or marble no doubt and it curved strangely at the top. Emitting a gasp I collapsed as I tumbled over backward pushing myself away from the object as I realized that it was…a tombstone! My eyes widened despite the rain that stung them and I quickly rolled onto my knees and forced myself onto my feet, yet even as I stood I placed my hand upon another tombstone before jerking it away quickly. The shadow had led me to the graveyard, what could this mean? Standing to my full height I glanced about in the rain still unable to see even a foot in front of me, and then…God must have turned on the light switch of Heaven for the sky once again flashed an eerie shade of white. And as the sky lit up it revealed the Shadow standing no more than ten feet in front of me! Using my great speed and resiliency I leapt over a tombstone in front of me and made steps toward the shadow at a quick run. I was running blindly but the Shadow was as well for I hear it cry out in pain as it must have crashed into a tombstone. Using the sense of hearing I quickly grasped desperately at the place where the noise had been emitted from and I felt a piece of cloth in my hand…the shadow's cloak! Clutching this tightly I refused to let go as the shadow forced itself to it's feet and made an effort to scramble away. 'Stop!' I demanded, as I grabbed the cloak with both hands now. 'FEAR!' The shadow hissed in a cancerous voice! 'Who are you?' I said with all the force of an interrogator as I clutched the shadows cloak. 'Fear!' It hissed again, as it grabbed at my hands struggling to wrench free of my grasp. I felt blood on my hands as the fingernails of the shadow dug into my skin sharply lacerating my skin. 'Stop!' I screamed at the Shadow as it suddenly broke free of my grasp. The lightning flashed once more as I was permitted to see the face of this enigma… Long white hair danced before a face of wrinkles and a crooked nose, the face was that of a man, an old man. His pale blue eyes bore a sick color in them as they met mine and suddenly the lighting ended…the rain ceased…and the Shadow was gone.
