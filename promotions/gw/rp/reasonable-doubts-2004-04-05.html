Reasonable Doubts

5 April 2004

Reasonable Doubts J. C. Diamond Global Wrestling Promo #1099 Date: 4/5/04 Location: Skylight Hotel Everything is quiet, no music on the radio, no whistling. Only thing that can be heard is the wheels of vehicles running throw the wet streets. The sun has fallen and the moon has taken its shift. The eyes of oncoming cars shined and the streets lights has awaken. “Sir Diamond, you seem oddly, are you okay?” “I’m fine Jeffrey, thanks.” I was not intending to mislead Jeffrey my limo driver, because see, something indeed was bothering me. I am not the one to share my stress and my hard times with others, not until I get over them anyway. I don’t want anyone to feel sorry for me or hurt with me, so I first get over the stress and hard times then maybe talk about it later. But this, this is something that will maybe be with me for as long as I wrestle. “Sir Diamond, we have arrived at your stay for the weekend.” Jeffrey said as he pulled into the Skylight hotel. Jeffrey, dressed in his usual straight black, parked the limo right in front of the opening to Skylight. He quickly got out of the car and let up the black umbrella he held and opened my door. “Thanks Jeffrey and don’t worry about my bags till the morning.” I said as he shut the limo door. “Just find a nice place to park the limo, I’ll go set-up the rooms.” I obtain the umbrella from Jeffrey as he nodded; signaling his ears heard my instructions. I turned and walked towards the Skylight hotels front doors, I heard Jeffrey drive off behind me in search of a park. I entered the Skylight motel; everything was decorated nicely on the inside. There weren’t many people up walking about, I guess because it was after one am here. I notice the pretty clerk at the counter staring, so I stepped up to the desk. “I would like two rooms please.” The clerk was very pretty; she had long black hair, nice brown eyes, full lips and a flower in her hair. I think she was from the Island of Hawaii because she has there look. “How long will you be staying?” she asked with a smile. “Book me for five days.” I said knowing usually I like to stay over a bit. I notice her gold name tag on her green and white clerk dress and it read, Amy. “Alright, here are the keys to rooms twenty and twenty one.” she said as she handed me the room keys. They had big green cards with the room numbers on the key ring. “Thanks.” I accepted the keys and I heard the front door to the Skylight motel open, making the bells ring. I turned and it was Jeffrey. “Hey Jeffrey, here is your key.” I handed him the key to room twenty one. “Thanks sir Diamond.” Jeffrey said as he took the key. After we reached our rooms Jeffrey fell fast asleep, I could tell by the snoring I could hear through the walls. I got into the bed, still dressed in my blue-jeans and white t-shirt and socks, but I couldn’t sleep. It wasn’t the clothes and it wasn’t the room, it was nicely set-up. The rugs were white, the king size bed covered in white blanketing and matching pillow slips, the bed post was black steal made into a flower. Had the lamp shades and all, ya know? I’m sorry if I am being a little short, but I’m not feeling so well… Alright I guess I’ll let you know what is bothering me. The truth is I’m getting a little home sick. Yea, I got the picture of my family right here on my chest as I lay. You see, my mom is sick, my father is sick and right now there living together. Well, my mom doesn’t have her license because my dad always took her anywhere she needed to go, almost like my limo drive Jeffrey. My father treated my mom like a queen. Well, now that my dad is sick no one could take her shopping or anything except for me when I was living there. My aunts and uncles are helping them the best that they can, but my mom always says it felt better when I took her because it didn’t feel like she had to rush. My dad, well with him you have to take him in out of bed, help him get on and off the toilet, clean him up and give him baths. While I was there, my mom and I did that mostly and he felt comfortable with us because he knew we didn’t mind. My pa, as I like to call him, always said he never mind anybody helping him but he didn’t want to make them tired of him. I could understand, sometime people smile and all in your face but turn around and say other things to different people. Then, there are my brothers and sisters, my nieces and nephews. My niece Dominqua called me on my cell one Saturday night. She told me how much she misses laying down with me and watching movies. She even said she cries sometime because it seems like she want ever get to see me again. Dominqua is only eight, but she is a very smart girl. She doesn’t like kiddy movies or anything, she likes movies like Vanilla Sky and the whole time she will ask what does this mean or what was the reason for this and that. Sometime, I think this is maybe the wrong business for me to be in, this wrestling business. I barely have any time for a personal life. I have no time for family, no time for outings, can’t go out and meet some nice girl and maybe make her my wife. But, what I don’t get is how can something that’s going so good be so wrong for me? ...You know, I’m a little thirsty and there is a soda machine down the hall come take a walk with me. I stepped out into the hallway which was floored with a black carpet. I looked right and could see the 7up poster on the front of the soda machine. I walked down the hall briefly looking at some of the paintings on the wall and checking out some of the plants alongside each second painting. I approached the soda machine, inserted a dollar bill and purchased a sixty cent can sprite. “Prices are getting higher everyday, isn’t that right sir?” I heard a voice say. I turned and it was Jeffrey, “Indeed Jeffrey, they are.” I notice Jeffrey was out in the hall in his normal all black but he just had white socks on his feet. “Had enough sleep already, I suppose?” “Not quite, I was taking a trip to the washroom and I heard your door open.” he said. “I took a peek out of my own and seen you here. But, now in return, are you having trouble sleeping sir?” “Yes kind of.” I admitted. “May I ask what is troubling you sir.” Jeffrey asked. “And if you want we can sit on this bench and talk about your troubles.” I turned to the way Jeffrey motioned and to the right of the soda machine there was like a straw bench. I figured since I would be riding around with Jeffrey for quite a while I might as well open up to him. “Sure Jeffrey.” I said as we both headed to the bench by the long black tinted window and set facing the elevator. “So sir, what is bothering you? Is it your match with Steel?” Chris Steel, he was on my mind too but not my main concern. “Not really, it’s just…Just I’m getting a little home sick, if you know what I mean.” “Oh, yes sir I do.” “You see, I missed my family. I missed helping my dad out, driving around and fussing with my mom about which way to go to get to a place the both of us have no idea where it’s at.” I continued. “Then sometimes I feel as if, as if I walked out on them you know? 'And why do you feel that why?” asked Jeffrey. “Well Jeffrey, I’m not there to take my mom shopping or help my father.” I said. “Then I miss my nieces and nephews, my sisters and brothers.” “Awe, I see.” Jeffrey said. “But sir, there are other ways to show you want to help and that you are thinking about someone instead of just being there all the time.” “How is that Jeffrey?” “Well for your mother, you can hire her a driver.” Jeffrey said and making a lot of since. “Your father, you can get twenty four hour nurses, get him a work out gym where he can visit and work on his muscles.” again Jeffrey had another great idea. “That’s true.” I agreed. “But would it be the same thing as me being there?” “Sir, I am sure they know you wouldn’t mind being there, not at all.” Jeffrey said. “But, they do know you are a grown man now and you got to focus on your future. And if you had not taken this job, how would you get all that stuff for them? How would have bought your mom those pretty dresses? And your father those nice jogging suits he cherishes so much. Let me tell you sir, every time they see you on smash holding that belt high in the air after a victory, your parents smile because they know you made something of yourself. They know if they leave here tomorrow you can take care of yourself. I am sure you miss them and I know they miss you, but that’s how you know you love someone and someone loves you.” For first time this today and this whole night, I smiled. “Jeffrey, you are right.”
