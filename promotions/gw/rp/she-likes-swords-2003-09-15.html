She Likes Swords

15 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 15th, 2003 Four days. It had been four days since Yoshiko arrived in the States, and Rokku had to admit, times were good. Aside from the hell of moving everything Yoshiko had shipped over from Japan in -- just how much clothing did she OWN anyway? Regardless, it was nice to have a friend around, especially one so easy on the eyes. Now, if she would just let up on the training? Akodo Rokku stood amidst a group of hanging sandbags in his basement. Eyes closed tightly, he imagined each bag as an attacker, and begins to strike out at each of them in turn. As the minutes passed, he attacked faster and faster, until he was striking each of them in rapid succession, never stopping long enough for one to swing back and hit him. His hands and feet become blur of motion, and a sheen of sweat forms on his body as he punches and kicks the bags over and over, never allowing himself to slow or stop. The minutes continued to fly by, and still he continued to pummel the bags relentlessly. As the pummeling continued, the bags began to show signs of wear and tear, until finally Rokku slammed his fist into one of them, and it exploded in a shower of sand. Undaunted, he struck another bag, and it too burst apart at the seams. One after another he destroyed the sandbags with his hands and feet, until he stood in a cloud of swirling sand, only one bag remaining. Growling like his clan's namesake, Rokku lashed out, sending the last bag flying across the gym with The Emperor's Right Hand. Slamming into the far wall, it exploded like the rest. Breathing slowly but heavily, Rokku stood amidst the swirling cloud of stand, as if waiting for another attack. Without warning, he whirled around, knocking aside a knife hurled at his midsection, and snatching one aimed at his head from the air. Smirking, Rokku opened his eyes. As the cloud of sand settled, Kakita Yoshiko appeared, applauding softly and wearing a smirk of her own. 'Not bad, Lion' said the Silken Crane, 'Forty minutes of constant training, and you're still fast enough to snatch knives out of midair. I think we're making some progress!' 'Some?' asked Rokku, blanching. 'Yoshiko, you've had me training non-stop for four days now. Weights, bags, aerobics, groups of jobbers? I'd like to think I've made more than just 'some' progress.' Yoshiko's smirk turned into a wide grin. 'Well, alright,' she said, 'to be honest you've been doing incredibly well. I've amped the difficulty every day, and you keep performing beyond my expectations. I'd almost forgotten how good you are.' Rokku grinned, and leaned back against the wall, arms crossed over his chest. 'Damn right you did! This stuff is child's play for a guy like me, or have you forgotten that I was the youngest Heavyweight Champion in IWL history?' Yoshiko shook her head as she walked across the basement to a large black cabinet next to the last ruined sandbag. 'Oh, I haven't forgotten,' she said, smiling devilishly, 'But since you're so cocky, why don't I introduce some new elements to your training today?' Yoshiko opened the cabinet, revealing a wide variety of weapons. She selected a pair of bokken, and tossed one to Rokku. 'Kendo training?' said Rokku quizzically. 'What for?' 'I know you've been practicing Kendo as a meditative art,' said Yoshiko, strolling towards Rokku, 'but I think it's time you got some serious combat training. With a pair of assassins AND a vampiric C.E.O. out for your hide, every trick you can know helps. Plus, if you learn more about the art of the sword, you might learn to temper your ego a bit more.' Rokku raised an eyebrow, then laughed. 'Ego, eh? Okay woman, just remember that you asked for this, and get ready for a whuppin'!' Rokku grasped the hilt of the bokken firmly, holding it out in front of him. Yoshiko held hers loosely at her side, in a relaxed position. With a thunderous kiai, Rokku charged her. Chuckling, Yoshiko easily ducked under Rokku's wide swing, and rapped her bokken hard against the back of his calves, sending him sprawling. Shaking the stars out of his head, Rokku sprung back to his feet, swinging at Yoshiko's legs. The Silken Crane leapt over the attack, and brought her kendo stick down on the back of Rokku's neck. As the Steel Lion staggered back, stunned, Yoshiko jabbed the point of her stick into his stomach, and swept his legs out from under him. Rokku fell flat on his back with a resounding thud, and groaned as Yoshiko stood over him, resting the point of her kendo stick on his chest. 'Ugh?' Rokku moaned, 'where did you learn to handle a katana like that, Yoshiko?' Yoshiko's eyes widened, and she let out a lilting peal of laughter. 'You didn't know? My father is an absolute perfectionist, Rokku. And one thing he's a stickler about more than almost anything else is Kenjutsu. I was practically born with a sword in my hand, and I've been winning kenjutsu tournaments since I was five.' Slowly, Rokku pulled himself back onto his feet, shaking the last of the cobwebs out of his head, and cracking his aching back. 'Well,' he said, 'I never saw you do anything like that back in IWL. It could have come in handy during contract negotiations.' Yoshiko chuckled, resting her kendo stick on her shoulder. 'Excellence in ALL things, that was always dad's motto. If I hadn't gotten perfect scores on both my business AND kenjutsu tests, my father would probably never have let me out of the house!' Rokku chuckled softly, and held the kendo stick out in front of him again. 'Well all right,' he said, 'I'm impressed. So how long are we going to be doing this? You beating me senseless, I mean.' 'That's simple, really,' said Yoshiko. 'As soon as you can hit me, you get a break. You already know the basics of kenjutsu, and a few of the more complex moves, I'm sure. So that shouldn't be too hard for you. Not that I plan on making it easy.' 'So,' said Rokku, 'all I've got to do is hit you once? Then lets go!' The two friends and comrades in arms fell into their stances again, and Rokku made the first move. Hours flew by as they duel over and over. Again and again, Rokku found himself flat on his back, with Yoshiko standing over him. Normally, that would have been something he could enjoy -- but not know. Four hours later, Rokku was gasping for breath, and sore as hell all over from the constant shellacking he had been receiving. Yoshiko showed tiring herself, as strands of snow-white hair stuck to her sweat-slick forehead. Still, both student and teacher were smiling -- Yoshiko because she saw that Rokku was learning quickly, and Rokku because he had finally come up with a way to pierce Yoshiko's defenses. 'Ready for another go, Rokku?' said Yoshiko, between breathes. 'As? as I'll ever be?' gasped the Steel Lion. 'All right then.' said Yoshiko and the two of them fell into their stances again. This time it's Yoshiko who struck first, thrusting the point of her kendo stick at Rokku's chest with lightning speed. But, to her surprise, Rokku sidestepped, letting his weapon slide down the length of hers and push it away. Yoshiko spun around, but after four hours of sparring, too slow -- Rokku ducked under her swing and with a quick snap of his kendo stick, finally struck her -- right on the behind. 'Whoooooo!' cheered Rokku, pumping his fist in the air and celebrating his victory. 'I finally got ya, teach.' Yoshiko rubbed her butt where Rokku had hit her, and grinned evilly as she walked towards the stairs. 'Oh, you got me all right. And true to my word, you get a break from kendo training -- in fact, it's over for today.' 'Great,' said Rokku, letting the bokken clatter to the floor, and following her up the stairs. 'So what's next?' 'Dinner,' said Yoshiko, 'which you'll be making. And after that, more training. And then, I might let you sleep. Rokku rolled his eyes. 'You have got to be kidding me.' 'You know me better than that Lion,' said the Silken Crane, 'I don't kid about business or dinner. Now get in the kitchen and start cooking!' 'Yeah yeah, whatever?' grumbled the Steel Lion. Yoshiko raised an eyebrow, and Rokku suddenly found himself with a kendo stick pressed to his throat. 'What was that?' she said, smiling sweetly 'Uh, uh,' stuttered Rokku, the color draining from his face, 'I mean, yes ma'am!'
