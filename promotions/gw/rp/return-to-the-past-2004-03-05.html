Return to the past..

5 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-5-04 The Black Ford truck pulled up into the gravel parking lot of the Darkhorse Saloon. Ely slowly climbed out of the Drivers seat. Along with him from the passenger’s side climbed Jesse Briggs and Logan Creed followed him. Ely looked up at the sky that was growing cloudier by the hour. “Storms a brewing” Stated Logan running his hand though his thick beard. Ely only nodded “Yep” Ely replied as the three continued into the Saloon. A couple girls passed them on the way in. “Hi Jesse!” The two said and started their circling. Much like buzzards circling a piece of meet. “Umm” Hi Ladies.” Jesse replied looking at Ely for help. “Sorry partner. Your going to have to go it alone with out a partner this time.” Ely said with a smirk. Jesse lowered his head and looked like a defeated man, as the ladies dragged Jesse Briggs off into the bar. “Poor Jesse. They are going to devour him.” Logan said. “I don’t think Jesse will mind. Hell he needs some stress relived after watching over Talon for the last week; like he has.” Ely said as the two continued on. Ely looked over where the workers were re assembling the fight cage in a corner of the bar. Ely stood there remembering his day in that cage. His musings however ended as he was poked in the leg.. Ely looked down to see Logan pointing. Down the stairs from the office above was walking Christina McCoy. She was heading start for him. “I am out of here Ely. She doesn’t look to happy.” Said Logan as his vanished into the crowd. “Yeah.. Umm Logan.” Ely said slowly. Ely watched as Christina Power walked to his location. “So Christina. I see your having them reinstall the cage. I thought you did not like that cage. ? Ely asked stopping her as she came close. Christina stopped and looked from the cage location to Ely. And the anger in her face started to show. “You know Ely. I don’t like that cage. My father fought in it while I was a little girl. I saw him bleed and be beat up.. But one thing my father thought me over the years is never give in. Some times to earn respect you have to knock it into some one. Well Monday Ely. Train is going to learn respect. Do you know that he had the gall to walk in her.. In to my fathers place and disrespect ME!” Christina screamed causing the workers to stop and look at her. “What are you all doing” GET BACK TO WORK!!!” Christina screamed yet again. Ely stood there looking down at this small woman. Never did he see her so angry ion her life. What.” Ely started. “And YOU! Where have you been all week!” Christina Demanded. “Me” Well. I told you I was going to go out to Talons cabin in the woods. Right?” Ely said. “Yeah.. You did.” Christina replied. “Well Jesse and Logan came along. Seams Logan wanted to do some hunting and Jesse wanted to go fishing. So we all three went. We just now got back.” Ely said. “Well.. Well Ely while you three were off having a good time.. That Train guys has been nothing but a pain. He been harassing me, and just earlier tonight he came in here, causing trouble and acting a fool. He even insinuated that me and you were having sex!” Christina exclaimed with a disgusted look on his face. Ely raised a eyebrow. “He did” That a bad thing?” Ely asked. “Pish. Ely! Don’t encourage him.” Christina said. “Okay” sorry. What did you say?” Ely asked. “I told him the truth. We are friends, and that is all.” Christina said walking over to the cage.. Ely sighed and lowered his head. “Friends.. yeah.. That is all we are.” Ely said saddened. “Yeah and Train needs to learn how to treat people with respect. SO” I booked a match.” Christina said a smile coming to her face. Ely looked up his eyes looking at Christina from under his cowboy hat. “Christina.. What kind of match?” Ely asked with a bit of surprise in his voice. “A match to teach him.. I booked you and Train in a Bar Cage match!” Christina said spinning to face Ely, her smile big and proud. “Christina.. No.. That.. that match” Yo..” Ely started.. but Christina cut him off with a hand. “NO! Ely. You’re going to teach him respect for the family. Respect for me! My father would if he was here, but he is not. So Your all I have to depend on for this.” Christina said. “Me” Christina. That fight I don’t wish to go back to the guy who fought in those types of fights. That man was not a nice guy. “Ely explained to Christina. “Why me” Why not Brick?” Ely asked. “Why! Because of what he implied. Your going to do this Ely. Just don’t mess up this time.” Christina said stomping her foot and storming off. Ely looked at Christina walking away and slowly staggered to the bar. Why? Why would Christina do this.. She knew what he did when he fought in bar pit fights.. How he grew into a blood thirsty monster. Ely took deep breaths thinking about it.. His military training started to boil to the surface. Seek and Destroy. That was his mission statements? Seek and Destroy. Ely shook his head trying to force the monster inside back down. He was no longer that man. “LOGAN!!!!” Ely bellowed causing the Dwarf to pop up out of no where. Logan Looked at Ely his face showed he knew.. “Ely.. I just heard.. I am sorry.” Logan said. “Me to my friend. Look. This is wrong.. I don’t like it. Look I need a fave pal.” Ely said looking into Logan’s eyes “Anything.” Logan replied. “Find Train. Tell him I want to talk to him before this fight. And find him fast.” Ely said.. “Gotcha.” Logan said. Just as quick as he appeared he vanished into the crowd. Ely followed him but his eyes stopped as standing there in a pair of jeans and a black T-shirt. Stood a tall gorgeous blond. Ely and her eyes met and locked. She smiled and approached. As she did Jesse stepped in front of her. “Hey Ely. I just heard.. I got your back you know. This Train guy, I here is not one to mess with. “HI Jesse” Said the blond from behind Briggs Jesse Froze. Slowly he turned around and looked at the woman. “Umm” Hi Gun!” Jesse said to the woman and turned to look at Ely. And under his breath said “I got to go.” Jesse left causing the woman named Gun to shrug. “You know Jesse?” Ely asked. “Yeah, We were once very close.” Gun said. “You must be Ely Braxton.” “Yeah. How you know?” Ely asked. “Well a former GW International Champion has her ways.” Gun said with a smi8le. “So you’re the one and only Gunner Addison.” Ely said with amazement in his voice. “Yep. I am”. Sooo.. You gonna buy a lady a drink or stand there looking as if you been hit by a bull.”” Gunner asked. “Barkeep. Give this lady what ever she wants.. On the house.” Ely shouted over his shoulder. The two talked and shared drinks and laughs. Strangely this woman he just met made every thing feel better.. and he knew things were going to be okay.. From her office window Christina looked down to the bar watching Ely and Gunner. Anger. Anger flashed in her eyes. And she snapped the blinds shut. To be continued?.
