The Life: The Story of Ami Mizuno(Volume 3)

3 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-03-2005 Early Morning Wel its about 6:45 in the morning in NYC and its about 12 degrees outside on a chilly Monday Morning. Is it evident? Yeah considering the fact that the population is out is wool hats, really heavy jackets and sweaters with padded long johns! Makes you wish you were in Florida right now huh? Well for one particular person she absolutely LOVES this weather and I think you know who she is... Ah yes the beloved “Ice Princess” of the “old School Connectiion”, Ami Mizuno is out and about on this particular day out near Times Square and Rockafeller Center taking in the sights and sounds of a busy Monday morning in “The Big Apple” and that always entails some odd moments for the young prodigy but she also likens it to being back home in the Rishada District of Japan where her apartment was that she lived in with her mother. Today Ami was out and about to do a couple of things for the Connection as well as possibly take in some ice skating to make sure that she hasn’t lost her touch at a hobby that she fell in love with back home.(National Champion in Tokyo thank you very much) As Ami walked across the not so busy streets of New York City to get to the Center Ami spotted a friend of hers that hse hadn’t seen in a while, so she decided to stop for a minute to speak to the person before going on her way. “Hey Makoto” Ami began “what brings you to New York City at this time of year?” Ami asked the tall brunette and Makoto, whom was dressed in a wool sweater, a pair of Canyon River Blues brand blue jeans and a pair of boots that went about up to her ankles cleared her throat before speaking “Well hello Ami” Makoto began “I was on break for the New Year from the Academy that I’m attending, so I decided to pop in and see how you were doing.” Makoto said with a pleasant smile which always warmed the heart of Ami because before she left to go live with her brother she knew that Makoto was sad to see her go but at the same time she wanted to explore the interest that she had in the sport of Pro Wrestling. “I’m fine Mako-chan. I’ve been studying about the sport of Pro Wrestling and Josh has been teaching me things about holds and what not to use when I get in the ring so I’ve been as busy as when I lived in Japan.” Ami told Makoto and she couldn’t help but see a smile across the face of her tall friend and had to ask “Something wrong Mako-chan?” “Huh? Oh no Ami nothing’s wrong at all. I was just thinking about what I heard from the others about you wrestling in the states and I wondered if it was in fact true. I guess it is and I wish you all the luck with chasing this dream of yours.” Makoto told Ami and she smiled as she knew how sincere Makoto was being with her feelings and that if it came to lying that Makoto was HORRIBLE at it. “Well I’ve already held a Women’s title in one organization here in the states and now I’m working with Joshua again in a promotion called GW ,so I guess you could say that this brother/sister tandem is somewhat inseperable huh?” Ami asked as she turned to lean up against the raling of the ice skating rink. “Yep just like when Joshua teamed with you in Japan before he came to the States. I know cos I was at the match that you had teamed with him and no one could believe the chemistry that you two had in your first match and all. A lot of promoters wanted to sign you two on the spot to keep you both there but Josh left because he was given an offer here in America that was a little better...” Makoto started before being uncharacteristically cut off by Ami “But he is and always been a hero in Japan. Everywhere we went before moving to be with his boyfriend people looked at him as a National icon and when we went to the Egg Dome for the GW PPV ‘Showdown in Tokyo’ you couldn’t hear yourself think due to the noise in the arena that night. Some say that Joshua’s reception that night combined with his upset pinfall of Maverick Reno is cause for GW to start to take notice of his abilities in the ring.” Ami pointed out and Makoto nodded in agreement and she would know of Joshua’s national status, for she was at a lot of the matches that he had in Japan when he freelanced for companies. Its no secret that when Joshua wrestled in Japan people came to see him and he gave them as good a show if not better than the World Champion that would be fighting that night! Ami isn’t a slouch thiguh because when she went out for her first match in Japan with Joshua in her corner she stunned the crowd with the technical prowess that would have made even the most storied of Legend stand up and take notice in the arenas that night. A lot of people took Ami as a short, skinny know nothing punk kid but when they saw her first match that preception quickly evaporated and because of her cool demeanor some say that is how her nick name of “Ice Princess” came to be...or it could have also come from the hobby that Mako-chan and herself were about to partake in: Ice Skating! Ok now I don’t profess to know a lot about the sport of Figure Skating ,but I acknowledge as a sport as I will for Golf and NASCAR amd also let’s just say that the two girls are good...scary good! Ever watch “Search for Bobby Fisher”? I think even though that was about a chess prodigy someone could’ve made something out of these two dynamos! Ami was usually in a close competition with Makoto because their skills were so close together that if you tried to pick one over the other it was always a hard thing to do! So the girls skated for what seemed like forever and really it was only about an hour and a half but let me tell you that the people that were on the ice that day watched an exhibition unlike anything that they saw on TV and that’s saying something because of the fact that the girls performing never competed in an Olympic Competition or anything like that but they put their hearts into the performance and that was all that mattered. After the duration of the performance there were swarms of guys and gals looking to get the autographs of the two young ladies and while they did it without hesitation they also knew that they had to get back to their respective homes before it got too late! As the girls left the rink area Ami gave Makoto a quick hug and a kiss on the cheek before she went on her way back to the Residence and on the way she saw that familiar Viper and she knew who the driver was almost instantly! “TINA!” Ami cried out and the car stopped instantly at the curb for her and when she got to the driver’s side the window came down and she saw the face of the driver and she knew that she was right! “Good Morning ‘Prodigy’.” Tina said with a twinkle in her eye as fir those of you whom don’t know Tina is pretty much like the “Mother Figure” for Ami in GW and she couldn’t be happier! “Good Morning Tina. I’m sorry I didn’t get to talk to you last night but after the study session I had to go to bed you know?” Ami said almost apologetically and Tina knew that she didn’t mean it but accepted the apology all the same. “Its ok Ami. Come on and get in so you can get out of the cold for a couple of minutes and I’ll take you home ok?” Tina asked and without a moment’s hesitation Ami went and got into the passenger side of the car and off they went and onto the home of the “Masters” as some call them but to them its just the home of the old school. the “Old School Connection”! Connection Started! Au Revoir!
