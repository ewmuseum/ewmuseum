The Joruney: Part 14/Joshua's POV

25 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-25-05 Late Night/Early Morning New York City - The “Big Apple” and the melting pot of the United States. Its in this city that one of the more tragic events occurred when some suicidal mother fuckers went and took down the “Twin Towers” otherwise known as the World Trade Centers and since then the city has undergone a healing process that is running on 4 years. At this point in time its dark and eerily quiet’so quiet that if you dropped a pin you wouldn’t just hear it hit the ground but the echo would reverberate throughout the home. Why is it so quiet? Well for starters its about 4:30 in the morning and most everyone is asleep in the house. The only light on or noise being made is coming from one of the few rooms that gets little use in the house: The Study. Now don’t misunderstand this but that room gets little use because when the gang is all there they generally are dealing with wrestling stuff. Today was a little different because Joshua had already come back from his “Platinum Series” match against Conner and Deathsun and wanted to spend a few days in the study before he went out to Vegas where Evolution was to take place and he just wanted to reflect on some things in his life such as his relationship with Zack, his stable and the coup de gras: his mother! Let’s start with Zack? God what’s it been about almost 12 years officially. Zack basically came into wrestling through a training camp like Joshua did and was an all-around nice guy’for a heel! Yeah you heard me right Zack was at one time a heel in a fed called IWF and when he would hear people boo him lustily it ate at his very being! He wasn’t the kind of guy that did BAD things he was just someone that the ownership at the time wanted to market as a heel. The thing of it was that while Zack was in IWF as a “loner” he confided in certain people about his sexuality and believe it or not both good guys and bad guys got behind Zack and wanted to see him happy and with someone but there wasn’t a soul in the fed that would WANT to be caught dead dating him was there? This is where Joshua came in? Weeks before the match was even going to happen between Cummings/Clarke and Curtis/Johnson Joshua pushed for Zack to go through a “turn” so that he wouldn’t be so depressed anymore. Joshua had heard from Zack about the fans booing him and calling him names that were TOTALLY unacceptable to Joshua and the fact that the ownership allowed for it made him sick! The writers actually agreed with Joshua and without telling President Daniels wrote it so that Zack would start to slowly but surely undergo a process where the fans begin to accept him for who he was and not WHAT he is and everyone that knew about it loved it! The wrestlers in the back watched as Zack matured right in front of their eyes and started noticing that one of the wrestlers was taking a notice in Zack’s change and that of course was Joshua L. Curtis. Joshua was watching Zack from afar and the fact that Zack was slowly becoming happier made Joshua smile because of the fact that Zack was such a good guy in the industry that he deserved to have the fans cheer him and not make the rude comments that they were making. As Zack’s popularity grew the writer’s started pushing Zack themselves as a kind of “champion” for a cause that was very much overlooked in the industry and Zack accepted that role but as we all know in some cases 10 pounds of gold don’t hurt either! As Zack started to get pushed in the singles divisions he was looking to see if it would be possible to get himself a tag team partner but the writers and ownership once they found out about Zack’s turn told him in all sincerity that they didn’t know who Zack could team with that wouldn’t be offended to go after the tag titles with him and that was kind of depressing. Then “The Night” happened? On the fateful Throwdown in which Chester Clarke/Brad Cummings came out to the ring unexpectedly and started braiding Joshua Curtis because he had befriended a homosexual Joshua came out and was ready for the tag team match and was going to go at it handicap style but as he was about to walk down the ramp to face them by himself a voice came from behind the curtain in Wembley Stadium. “Wait Joshua.” Out walked Zack Johnson and side by side Joshua and Zack stood united by the prospect of stopping the hate that was prevalent in the tone of Cummings and Clarke. When they walked down to the ring together that is honestly when the OSC chants started and the fans really got behind the two. As the match was going down the fans noticed the way Zack looked at Joshua and while Joshua took notice to it during the match he knew they had a job to do and would worry about it later as the job at hand was dealing with those two homophobic ingrates! When the 3 count rang out and Joshua and Zack’s hands were raised with the belts in their possession a lot of people began the “OSC” chants and Curtis was seen weeping openly because it was his first title in the company and he got to win it with a friend of his. As they stood in the ring and took in the cheers as the show was to be going off the air at that point was when Zack got the microphone from the ring announcer and got down on one knee to make the offer to Curtis of a relationship. As the fans were chanting “Do it! Do it!” Do it!” Joshua told Zack to get up and that was when he planted the kiss on him in front of the world the crowd absolutely roared with approval and a lot of the writers and ownership came out and applauded them for doing that. Now onto the story of the “OSC?” This one was a little more complicated but as we all know wrestling today is in a state of extremeness where you need tables, chairs, frying pans and flaming tables to get a point across to the people. When Joshua took over the IWF he wanted to find 3 or 4 people that would be willing to help him spread the word of the “Old School” in case the fed closed and he found them ALL in his fed! Now Zack was a lock in the stable because he was going out with the owner but the other 2 or 3 people were going to be difficult to find and Josh knew that! How Joshua found Tina was by dumb luck more than anything. One night during a match that Tina was having against an up and comer in the fed a band of goons called “The Extremists” hit the ring and put a beat down on Tina until Zack and Joshua came out and cleared the ring of the morons much to the approval of the fans and to the gratitude of Davis. Curtis offered Tina a chance to become someone in the IWF because he knew that when the old ownership was there that Tina hadn’t gotten the chances she wanted in the fed. Tina was a little hesitant because of the fact that the two charter members were homosexuals and she believed herself to be straight but told them that she’d think about it. The two took that for what it was worth and left her alone. When Tina had her match Brittany for the International title ,which at the time was IWF’s equivalent to the World title, Joshua brought a friend with him to ringside to watch the match and Tina didn’t recognize the woman until she got up of the canvas and realized that it was Juri Arisugawa, a woman that she had befriended back in a place called Ohtori Academy she put it into overdrive especially after the International champion went after Curtis when Curtis took over officiating the match. After she won the title that was when a lot of the fans took to the idea of an “alternate lifestyle” stable and yes that is what exactly its been termed. Why would the fans do such a thing is beyond a lot of “insiders” minds but at the same time people also realized that the times were really changing in the wrestling industry and for Curtis to pull this move off was something that needed to happen. Ami came into the OSC because of family reasons with her brother being the leader of the faction and because she believed in the cause that Joshua was attempting to champion and she felt that it was the right thing to do. Michiru was a woman that Ami had befriended and fallen in love with in Japan and she decided that since Joshua’s cause was worthy enough that she would contribute in her own way and be the “spiritual advisor” for the faction. Joshua grew to be really good friends with the “Miss do-it-all” and asked if she’d like to join the stable. When Michiru agreed to join the stable it made Joshua’s day because of the fact that the woman meant a lot to Joshua and was always good for guidance when he needed it. How’d you like retrospective? Well there’s more to it than just that I’m afraid? When Joshua finished looking at some of the pictures of his stablemates and reminiscing he put the album back on the shelf and pulled the one off the shelf labeled “confidential” and with a heavy heart opened the album and did the best he could holding back his tears as the album had pictures of his mother in it. Now let’s make a few things clear here right now: Yes Joshua and his mother got along despite the sexuality issue for a while but she was also a prostitute that slept with most every guy she met and nearly contracted every disease if her own son whom had never had sex in his life basically made her use protection! A lot of the pictures that were in the album were from when Wendy was in high school and college. Had Joshua not gotten a hold of the album before a point then his mother was going to junk it! Joshua managed to get a hold of the album before she could do such an asinine thing and kept it hidden from her. When Wendy found out about what Joshua did she went ballistic and nearly threw Josh out of the house but Joshua to his credit stood his ground and snapped back at his mother. The two never really spoke after that and Joshua felt kind of bad but at the same time he knew that it had to be done. What does that have to do with the here and now? Well during Joshua’s tenure in GW so far he befriended a woman that bears a striking resemblance to his mother back in the day and her name is Ms. Gunner Addison! For those of you whom might be new to GW or might not remember who Ms. Addison she is the woman that is a very important part in the life of the current reigning GW World Champion in Ken Partridge Jr. as they are a couple and Joshua respects the hell out of her for sticking with Ken through better and worse and heaven and hell. To a degree Joshua is somewhat envious of Ken because he has a woman that can help pick him up when he’s down and while Joshua has Zack he never really had a female figure to look up to as a “mother figure”. Does Joshua have a “Mother Complex?” Hard to say because Joshua ,while open on a lot of things, never really touched the issue and when Ami has approached Joshua about it he always played it off like he didn’t but the fact remains that Joshua to this day wishes that there was a woman in his life that he could turn to when the shit hit the fan! Problem is that Joshua has not had any contact with Gunner since the Evolution before SiT and that kind of hurt him in a way that he would never vocalize on the air ever because there was the belief that people didn’t really care but at the same time there were fans that snet Joshua and company fan mail and a lot of it dealt with the issue that Curtis had about talking to Gunner again and whenever Joshua answered the letters about the situation he always said that he wished he could at least talk to her one time down the road but knows that the reality is that that’ll never happen and the best that he could do is pray that someday, somehow he would be able to face “Miss GW” Gunner Addison!
