The Final Begining Part 5 - Through the Angel's Eyes

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/29/03 Darkness Pain Tear... As he sat on that hill holding the rifle in his hands he could hear the words in the room through his mind... Hear everything... Man - God Machine.. that's what the raZed project called him... He could hear thoughts.. Hear whispers... Hear... everything... 'My name is Mani Kai Shigeru.. I am a murderer... My life has been rife with death and hate.' Jude looked down at me... 'Mani, something inside you has changed...' I looked up at him white hair in my eyes as I perched on one knee.. 'Jude.. I am not the man you knew. I am not Kai... I am not the Lost Angel... I am not the loving husband of Kiana Shigeru.. I am not the savior of Gunner Addison... I am the heartless... I am the Man-God.. And my last orders from Maxis Incorporated were simply 'Revenge' So my interpretation came back as revenge for my father...' I turned my head back to the room as Lisha turned her head slowly.. her eyes locked and the flashback hit... - 'Mani' I turned face exposed 'Mani I don't know what happened between you and Salaok but whatever must be done to make it right... do it..' I looked into her eyes and smiled 'I fear you may be hurt in the process Lisha.' She looked down her eyes sad 'Mani, if it will make your pain subside then so be it...' Surprising how much she seemed to care for me... Almost as if it had to be false.. 'Mani, I don't want to see Jon, Salaok and yourself at each others throats for eternity..' My eyes shined slightly as I whispered 'No, Lisha you don't want to see the two of them die... I know he is a proud man but Salaok knows nothing can protect him from me if I decide to kill him...' Her eyes lowered as I snapped back to reality - A smile... she knew what I was set to do.... BANG! Her smile faded.. and my minds eye saw into her mind... 'I'm sorry... I know this will most likely kill you and the other... But my father is dead... my last possible hope for humanity... and it is his fault' 'Mani I hold you no ill will... Now run Salaok will most likely send quartz and Diasporo after you and my death is already more blood on your hands than an angel deserves... I know you meant me no ill will' Her words burning like a thousand fires as my mask came loose blowing in the wind as I mouth 'I'm Sorry Lisha' Second later I turned and headed away as did Jude... Darkness our beginning and darkness our ending... This is my last connection to the place known a Global Incorporated.... and now Maxis would issue me another order...
