A Tale of Woe...

16 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-16-04 Imagine a world where cold evil rules supreme’where good people are driven hard beneath the yoke of slavery. That would give you a portrait of “The Land”. The inside of the old woman’s house was very neat, with each thing occupying its given place. One room is all that the hut consists of; bedroom, kitchen, hearth and bathroom all in one. Pendragon and the old woman sit on the floor, their shoes removed. They sip some sort of steaming concoction from clay cups, and the palaver goes on? Pendragon: This, Akumancha’Thy lord’Where did he come from? What is he doing here? Woman: that is a long story, but any story well told is started at the beginning? Pendragon: Truer words never have I heard? Woman: The land has always been lush, and remains so. We, the peasants, lived under peaceful rulers for centuries. These kings were fair and mighty, and would just as likely be seen farming the land with us as attending parties of state. People’s people they were, and they were very much beloved. The trouble started in the last twenty years. *******FLASH********** A strong castle stands in the distance, banners waving mightily in the air. A peaceful calm surrounds the place, and it exudes and aura of regality. The shot suddenly switches to show a large man clad in a red shirt and black pants working through the sword routines with a small boy. The man wields a glowing sword and the boy uses a smaller one of the same make? Man: Thou must flow smoothly when thy danceth this death dance, for death is what this is made to deal. There is no other reason to draw sword unless death is thy plan? The boy is a bit awkward, but already the blonde youth shows great poise under the eye of his father. He moves his small sword this way and that, and his father smiles. Finally, he stops and looks at his doting dad? Boy: Do I improve, father? Man: Aye’thee most certainly do’Thou shalt make a wonderful king one day’Both just and powerful? The man hugs the boy, and without warning, the skies grow dark, and a rain drops from the heavens. Lightning crashes and thunder thunders through the land? Man: (Shouting) We have to get inside!! The boy nods his agreement and the two rush for the waiting doors of the castle keep. As they approach, shouts can be heard coming from within. The man holds the boy back and shakes his head?. Man: War is upon us, by the sound’Go to thy room in the tower and await me there? He kisses the boy on the forehead, and the youth sheds a single tear’It is the last time he will see his father alive, yet he knows it not. The boy runs to another smaller entrance to the keep and passes across a huge square. Soldiers run here and there, but they pay the lad no mind. He makes a b-line for an enormous tower, and enters it at a door in the bottom. Up the circling stairs he goes until he reaches his room. He runs to his window and looks out upon the scene. The army of his father is engaged with an army attired quite strangely. They are Egyptian in look, but surely there are no Egyptians in the land? They battle back and forth, and the sword his father carries flashes in the distance. The Egyptian army is huge, and finally the boy can watch no more as they close in round his father. He slides down to the floor and puts his face in his hands. A small bit of time passes, and finally his door is thrown open, and the boy looks up, startled? Boy: Merlin!! Merlin smiles at the lad, and puts his finger in front of his lips? Merlin: Shhhhh?.We have to get you out of here, laddie’Things are not going well? Boy: but what of my father? Merlin doesn’t answer as he moves to the boy and throws his own cloak around him? Merlin: Sometimes other worlds are indeed better? With those words, the screen goes blank? *****FLASH****** Pendragon sits dumbstruck at the Old Woman’s story’He sips his drink slowly, and he looks very thoughtful into the eyes of the oldster? Pendragon: How does thee know so much of this story? Almost it seems that thee were there at that black hour? Woman: Indeed, I was’For years I was the nanny to the kings. And I saw you taken from the kingdom in the blackest of times? Pendragon shakes his head?. Pendragon: SO thou thinkest that I am the boy? Now grown to manhood and come back to claim mine throne? The woman smiles at him and nods? Woman: I know you are’after all, I helped to raise you as a boy? Pendragon shakes his head again? Pendragon: But I know thee not. Before this day I have never set eyes upon thee? The woman smiles again, and sips her drink, probably tea? Woman: What do you remember of your childhood, my lord? How far back does your memory stretch? Pendragon looks at her for a moment as her words sink in. He looks to the ceiling, gathering his thoughts before he is set to give an answer? Pendragon: My father was a teacher. He taught me all there is to know of legends and valor, honor and glory. When I was nine? Woman: What about before you were nine? Do you remember that? A frown crosses Pendragon’s face as he answers? Pendragon: No I cannot? Woman: Merlin saw fit to save you from the fate that Akumancha had planned. He placed you with the right people, people who would know how to raise a boy who would be king. Your values are older than you know, my lord. You are the boy who was taken from here so many years ago, and it is time you took back your rightful state? Pendragon: And what of the great army? What of that? Woman: Armies grow complacent when the people they rule over offer no resistance. The hoard has grown fat off of our toiling, and people grow restless. They seek a leader? Pendragon loses himself in thought?. Pendragon: Mine own army could be raised against the vermin’Glory of battle’The just cause? Woman: Aye? “Everything I am tells me that this must be a dream. Indeed, there are many worlds and many planes of existence, but can this be” Can I be a king from another land? And has fate, and Merlin, led me here to free the people of mine ancestry? This world is still of kings, and yet what I know is a world where I am a relic to be tossed aside at the mercy of the masses’Battle though. True battle. Raising and training an army’this could be mine greatest hour?” The woman rises and goes to a small chest. She reaches in and pulls from it a medallion and a map. She comes back and hands both to the Lord King? Woman: The medallion will prove to people who ye are, if’n your sword fails to. The map is a map of the land?. Pendragon unrolls the map and looks at it. A large wooded area lies to the east of where the woman points that they are? Woman: In Cravenwold Forest, you will find the beginning of your army. Lead them. That is what they seek. Their right king to lead them to the light from the darkness’This is the task Merlin has given you? Pendragon: Merlin’Even now he pulls the strings that move men and mountains’Were that he were here’Things might be easier? Pendragon rises and the woman again kneels before him. He Pulls her to her feet and kisses her forehead? Pendragon: Thee has shown great kindness that shall not be forgotten’Now I must away, for great deeds and glorious battle awaits me? Pendragon leaves the hut and finds himself once more in the sun. He heads to the east, away from the road, towards a green smudge on the horizon that must be the forest? Pendragon: A journey sooner begun is sooner done’More Merlin wisery? Pendragon laughs as he takes off at a fast trot through the grass. His size belies his speed and soon he grows small in the distance as the scene fades to black?. “Heroes come and go, and yet when they come, they leave such a mark as to be remembered for ages to come. Songs are written of their deeds, and great stories are told about them. In the modern world, heroes are forgotten relics of a more peaceful time’But in The Land, heroes can endure, and with them, the hopes of the masses”..” TO BE CONTINUED
