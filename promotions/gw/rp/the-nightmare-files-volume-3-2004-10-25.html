The Nightmare Files: Volume 3 Joshua Curtis Global Wrestling Promo #1729 Date: Monday, October 25th, 2004 Location: Outsider of Rockerfeller Center Early Afternoon Ok this one is a little more traditional in that guess what? IT’S ACTUALLY SUNNY OUT! Now I know that that might not seem important because the last two times that we paid a visit to the “Connection” residence we have been inside the house when Juri had her little “panic attack” and to say that she worried darling Ami when that had happened would be an underestimation on your part! Ami and Juri have a bond that is pretty much unbreakable and how that happened is still a mystery to a lot of people but to the two “Connection” members just the fact that they work and live together peacefully is all Joshua ever asked of group. On this day we find Joshua, Ami and Juri walking up and down the streets of New York City taking in the sights and sounds of the “Big Apple”. Joshua is wearing a red dress shirt, Khaki pants, and black dress shoes while Ami is wearing a light blue sweater, blue jeans and a pair of Keds. Juri is wearing a white turtleneck, Wrangler Blue Jeans and a pair of British Knights sneakers. The trio is seen walking past Rockerfeller Center and its ice skating rink when Joshua walks up to the top of the wall and leans on it. Joshua is seen with a great deal of disgust and thought and when he actually shows that kind of emotion it can’t help but worry the two young ladies. “Josh” Ami began “is there something wrong?” the blue haired genius asked the “leader” of the group and Joshua knew he couldn’t lie to his sister because if he did that meant basically that the trust that those two had built up over the years would basically be shattered! “Sis” Joshua began “This past week has been hell for me. I feel like someone has been playing some type of game with my head and I can’t help but think about whom might be at the controls of this game.” Joshua told Ami and that caused Juri to jump in at the tone of this conversation “Josh do you think ‘Dark Angel’ is doing this to you?” Juri asked which caused Joshua to spin around and look directly into the soul of the two young ladies! “Not only do I think he’s doing this but I also believe that he is causing the visions to appear in our head and the timing is something that is impeccable with how close its coming to the ‘Connection’s’ debut at ‘Dark Rituals’.” Joshua explained to Juri and that is when little sister Ami couldn’t help speak up about something that had happened the night prior! “Josh” Ami began “I think there is something you should know.” The younger sibling told Joshua and this is where we begin a story that I think might garner us a rating so high the FCC comes looking for us! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- This story takes us to the “Land of the Rising Sun” and by that I mean Japan and to be specific we are in Minato-Ku, Japan which is the now former hometown of the former NoW Women’s champion Mizuno Ami. Ami had just gotten home from school for the day and was walking in the door when her mother, the elusive Dr. Mizuno, was walking out of the house to go to one of her medical conferences for the weekend, which meant two things: 1. House to herself for the weekend 2. Ami gets to have a sleepover! Sounds good right? Sounds like something that is really typical and therefore ordinary right? The thing about this day was that there would be an interesting sighting and its of a family kind! Ami’s father whom was a famous painter was going to be in town this week and Ami so wanted to see him! Its not every day that her dad would be in town and for him to see her would make a lot of people’s days! Only glitch would be that she wasn’t coming for any reason that would be “normal”… It turns out that there was going to be an “exhibition” of sorts in a museum on this night and Ami had received a note from her mom that she was being allowed to go as his guest! Don’t get it yet? Don’t worry Ami didn’t either and it almost cost her! Over the next couple of hours Ami had been deciding on what to wear to go to the show and that is when it hit her to wear her dark blue outfit that her mom bought for her about 2 years ago! When Ami made her decision she hung the hanger on the knob of the closet in her bedroom and went to go get a shower… BIG MISTAKE! Anybody that has been reading these stories know exactly how they have been running with right about now knows how things usually go right now right? Keep reading! At about 6:30 on this particular Friday afternoon a man with slicked back black hair, a muscular build and dressed somewhat like a businessman walked into the residence of the Mizunos through the unlocked door and began looking about the house to see if his prey was there and unattended to… BINGO! The man, whom later would be identified by people as Ami’s father walked into the bedroom of Ms. Mizuno and hid carefully so that she wouldn’t expect anything! [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] To make a long story short Ami had lost something that she knew she wasn’t gonna get back and the fact that it was her father just made life that much more confusing for the blue-haired girl from Japan! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Present day… “WELL THAT SICK SON OF A BITCH!” Joshua said as his ears were burning from what Ami had told him and I just gave ya a beginning and an ending! IF you knew all of what happened I personally guarantee the FCC, GW and any other governing body would come looking for me in a minute! The fact that Joshua heard this story made him happy to be the loving family member that she chose to stay with…well that and Dr. Mizuno had asked Ami to go to the states to stay with Joshua after the incident in question! When Ami got done telling her story and Juri calmed Joshua down it was time for the 3 of them to head off towards “Destiny’s Door” and hopefully at the PPV Joshua L. Curtis of New York City could prove all of his critics and “Doubting Thomas’” wrong in that he isn’t a choker and the time that he spent traveling the circuits paid off for the best. This has been the “Nightmare Files” and after Dark Rituals is over with we will return to our regular scheduled story and this time it’ll be about “The Connection” finally together in GW! Au Revoir dearest and I’ll see YOU at “Dark Rituals”!
