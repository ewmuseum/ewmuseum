Finding the Past (Part 10)

She lay him down in front of the fireplace before turning it on and heading to his room to fetch a few blankets to wrap him in. As opened the bedroom door, she looked around at the well decorated and very chic home. 'Nothing's changed,' she said outloud as she headed over to his closet. As she reched in, pulling out two of his favorite grey wool blankets, she smiled to herself and became a bit flushed at the fact that she still knew where everything was. She stood there for a minute, lost in her thoughts, before closing the door and heading back out to a now coherant Ken. He was shivering as she wrapped the blankets around his shoulders, trying to take away as much of the chill that the cold rain had poured upon him as possible. As she was doing so, she was close enough so that Ken could smell her hair. It was as sweet as it had ever been, heavenly almost. He put his hand upon her's as it was on his shoulders and squeezed softly, catching Gunner a bit off-guard as she quit to look into his eyes. 'Thank you,' he uttered quietly as he gazed at her. She simply looked at him and delivered an uncomfortable smile before finishing wrapping him. 'Any better,' she asked as she turned the fireplace up even more. 'Yeah, much,' he responded, still shivering to the point that he had to slowly enunciate his words in order to make them audible. 'What happened out there, Ken? I mean, I was driving up here to see you, and the last thing I expectedwas to find you laying in the middle of the road in the middle of storm,' questioned Gunner with great concern as she sat in the floor next to him in front of the fire. 'What's going on?' 'I,' began Ken, 'I'm not one hundred percent sure, Gun. I had this odd black out earlier, at the coffee shop and I began running. I didn't know where to, just wherever my feet were leading me. I kept going until I had nothing left. I'm not sure how long I was running. It could have been thirty minutes or three hours, but when I gave out... I just remembering crying to God. I remember releasing all of this anger that's grown deep inside of me, and falling to the ground. I can't recall anything between that and you pulling me to the car.' 'When you blacked out, Ken,' began Gunner, 'what did you see? What happened? Try hard to remember.' Ken sat staring into the fire, which was supplying the only light in the room. He allowed the flames to overtake is eyes, almost blinding him, much like... 'The light,' said Ken. 'There was this bright light. Nothing heavenly or super-natural, just this blindingly bright light. I remember seeing it before I was hit by that semi. It distorted my vision, and I couldn't really make anything out. After the accident, I remember looking up at this scarred face, but I couldn't really make it out. There were a few other things, involving him, like... -- ...Wait a minute, why were you driving up here to see me?' Gunner sat confused and tongue-tied. She looked into Ken's eyes perplexed and searching for an answer. 'I... was coming to visit an old friend... to make sure he was doing alright,' she stumbled out. 'Is there anything so wrong about that?' 'Well,' began Ken, 'no, but I figured Ely may have a thing or two to say about it.' 'Ely and I aren't together anymore,' she whispered. Ken looked at her in silence and confusion as whether or not to be happy or sad. He wanted her to be happy, and he could tell that the situation was weighing on her. 'What happened,' he asked on eggshells. 'He asked me to marry him,' she responded matter-of-factly, fighting back tears. 'How in the hell did that cause the two of you to break-up?' 'Well,' began Gunner, 'I told him no... turned him down.' 'Why,' asked an ever curious Ken. 'I mean, I thought you were happy with him.' 'Happy, yes, but in love, no,' responded Gunner. 'I love someone else. I guess I just always thought I could stop loving him until that very moment when I had too. When Ely asked me to marry him, as hard as I tried, Ken, I just could not stop loving... you.' Ken was stunned. His heart was in jubilation and his mind had suddenly become crystal clear. All of the chill his body had sustained subsided as he filled with warmth. He stared into her eyes as she stared into his and they shared that moment for what seemed forever, with the fire doing the only talking in the room. 'Maybe... maybe I should go,' Gunner said. 'No... why?' 'Because I've obviously just made a fool of myself, Ken,' she said as she returned to her feet. 'I mean, I just told you I was still in love with you and all you could do was stare at me like a deer in headlights.' Ken quickly got to his feet and rushed over to her as she was almost at the door. 'Wait,' he said as he grabbed her arm and spun her around. 'What,' she cried with tears in her eyes. 'What can you say to make this better?' Without though, Ken rushed in and kissed her. As their lips met and he tasted her in the first time in years, he felt whole again. He wrapped his arms around her waist as she threw her's around his neck. All he wanted was to hold onto to that moment forever... and she wanted the same. As they pulled away, still holding on another and standing a breath apart, gazing into one another eyes, Ken spoke again. 'Don't go... please, don't go. I've wanted you back for so long, Gun. Now that you're here... please... just... just stay.' She smiled in accordance to his words. 'Why?' 'Because, Gunner... I love you, too' His love was his strength.
