The second Encounter

22 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/22/05 Tribulations of Drake, Continued? -The Second Encounter- At first all I could hear was the sound of rain all around me and could feel the drops splashing all over my body. I slowly opened my eyes and arose; my back popping as my spine grew long. I looked down and found my self with no cloths. There was no one else in sight so I didn’t think much of it. As I gazed around I realized I was somewhere in the city’but something was different. I observed one of the buildings closely and that’s when it dawned upon me. All around me, everything was outlined only by rain. The clouds looked to be made purely of water and I could see the lightning striking furiously inside never breaking through. The street its self was made up of water and was pitch black underneath. I began pacing myself down this cold dark street, which appeared to go on forever. The water beneath my feet rippled toward me with every step. The further I went the colder the water became. Suddenly I felt a strong wind blowing from my right and another blowing from the left. I noticed the outlined buildings beginning to sway towards one another and slowly tip over. They collided seemingly melting together and within seconds had formed themselves into water tunnel. Step by step I went on. Everything was quiet for quite some time but then I heard something in the distance. A scream. Without warning I was engulfed with the screams of pain. Following the screams were their sources, which appeared on the other sides of the water. I saw all aspects of life that make us scream and make us cry. It was almost as if the evils of the world were surrounding me directly. Also surrounding me were them? The blacked cloaked figures from my previous dream. Their haunting red eyes watched me from behind the horrific images. What did they want? Why were filling my brain with such horrible displays? That’s when I felt the blood in my veins growing cold. I tired to block out the screams but they were too much. As I watched on, my legs felt weak but I had to continue on to get out of this god-awful place. I tried to run but my legs wouldn’t allow it. I didn’t know how much more I could withstand. Just when I felt as if I were going to die I spotted something. A wall of water. Was this the end? Or was I trapped? My legs were now strong enough to jog and I was filled with a small dose of hope. But I quickly stopped dead in my tracks! A figure slowly approached the other side. It appeared to be another of the cloaked figures but something was different about this one. It stopped inches from my face and I tried to see it but was too well concealed. Just when the thought of reaching through the water and ripping the hood off entered my mind, it slowly began revealing itself. She smiled at me. Katie looked more beautiful now than ever. We gazed into each other and at that moment all the screams where gone and everything was so peaceful. She raised her hand to the water and I followed her lead. My hand was only an inch apart from hers when? The cloaked figures grabbed on and dragged her down into the darkness. “KATIE!” I shouted jumping through the water following after. Everything was dark and I heard the sound of a waterfall. I couldn’t see an inch in front of me. Then, with a sudden splash, I found myself under water. I swam up but couldn’t reach the surface. How far under could I have possibly sunk? My heart began to race. I now found myself sinking so I looked down to spot those horrible red eyes. I tired to break free from their grasp but it was no use. I couldn’t hold my breath much longer! Just as I lost my breath I started to fall again. As if I fell into a floating pond or something of the sort. I was falling much faster this time and it went without reason as the figures still had me and where still pulling me down. The air whipped by faster and faster; I felt like I was turning into an ice-cube. The cold air became painful as I felt my skin tearing open. Moments later I stopped falling and found myself floating. Things were a little brighter now, or, at least bright enough to see why I had stopped falling. I was dangling like a puppet by the long streams of blood that escaped my body during the fall. I felt paralyzed. Then one of them appeared in front of me. He held my neck in his hand studying me with those evil eyes. His breath was freezing. As quickly as he appeared he was gone. Then the most intense pain I’ve ever experienced shot through my body as the figure drove one of its razor sharp nails into my back. He slowly pulled it down and I could feel my spine being sliced like bread. AHH! I screamed in pain just as? To be continued?
