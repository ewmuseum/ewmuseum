Brick goes off

12 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-12-03 It’s been said that the safest place to be when Brick is pissed off is far enough away. If Brick had a right to be pissed off, it was tonight. While he and Jesse Briggs had beaten the Souls of the Black Lotus in their tag match, the end of the night wasn’t quite so much a reason to celebrate. He, Jesse and Ely Braxton had run out to the ring to aid Akodo Rokku and the Youngbloods against the Upper Echelon. Unfortunately, as the saying goes, “the best-laid plans of mice and men often go awry”. All three men put in good attempts but were taken down almost immediately after. This defeat burned Brick even more, because he was once again taken down by Sean Price for the second time in two weeks. As Brick, Ely, Jesse and GW security made their way backstage, the rage in Brick began to build. Then he stopped short in the middle of the hall and stood still. Then, he started to tremble, his fists clenched “Brick, you okay?” Ely asked. “Brick?” asked Jesse. But Brick couldn’t hear them. All that was registering in his mind at the moment was blind fury. Finally, Mount Brick erupted. He knocked over and threw objects, kicked crates and soda machines. He stomped and he fumed and he spouted obscenities. Nothing that wasn’t bolted down was safe. “Aw, shit, there he goes!” “Motherfuckin' TUE!” Brick roared. “MOTHERFUCKIN' TUE! GODDAMN MOTHERFUCKIN' TUE BASTARD SONS OF BITCHES!! I’M GONNA FUCKING KILL EVERY LAST ONE OF THOSE PIECES OF SHIT!!” “Shouldn’t we be trying to stop him?” one security guard asked another. “You feel that suicidal?” Ely is the first to make an attempt at calming Brick down. Ely is probably the only person alive who will attempt this unarmed. Considering he used to kill people for a living, this was probably the best job for him. “Damn it, Brick! Calm down!” “Fuck calm down, Ely! I’m sick of those motherfuckers!” “Them or just Price?” “All of them! I’m sick of Price! I’m sick of Stecchino! I’m sick of Holmes! I’m sick of Zero! I’m sick of Soma! I’m sick of Ice! I’m sick of Black Lotus! I’m sick of Pierce! I’m sick of them all!” “Look, I don’t like any o” them bastards, but what the hell is throwin? a damn tantrum gonna do?” “And in case you forgot, they laid a beatin” on us, too!” Jesse added. “Look, like they said, this is just the beginning and we gotta be ready for “em. Especially now that Christina’s got a new commissioner comin” in.” “Any idea who she’s bringin” in?” “No, all she said was that it would be someone who could come in and rally the troops, but most of all, it would be someone Stecchino hates with a passion.” “Only one cat I know fits that last description. You don’t think it’s him, do you?” “Wouldn’t surprise me. Plus, I think he’d jump at the chance to screw Stecchino over on a regular basis.” “I’m actually surprised you’re not the new commissioner, Ely,” said Jesse. “I mean, if anybody earned the right to run the show, it’s you.” “Hey! What about me”!” exclaimed Brick. “Yeah, well, I asked Christina the same thing.” “Well, anybody would be a relief when it comes to dealing with TUE.” “Yeah, even you.” “Watch it, redneck. I ain’t that calm, yet.” “Well, I guess we’re just gonna have to wait and see. Who’s up for a drink at the Dark Horse?” “I could use one,” said Brick before starting to walk away. “You know that son of a bitch Soma has his eye on Gunner?” “Yeah. Somebody needs to deal with that bloodsucker.” “Oh, somebody will.”
