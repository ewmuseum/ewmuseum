Pigeons and Firecrackers You Want a Monster Part 2

14 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/14/03 The black nothingness fades in to a close up shot of Ice sitting on the edge of a rooftop, overseeing a small city. Ice's legs are dangling off the edge of the building, as he looks out into the night sky, and over the city. Next to him on the ledge is a small red papered package. In his hand is a book of matches. Slowly Ice pulls out one match, lights it, and watches at is burns down to his finger tips. A calm expression fills his face, as the yellow glow, of a single security light shines directly above him. The night wind howls, and the stars twinkle in the sky as Ice again pulls out another match, and watches it burn down to his finger tips. Ice twirls the book of matches inbetween his thumb and index finger while slowly intaking a long, deep breath. He holds it, and looks up before exhaling his breath. 'I hope you realize what you are doing by your actions Christina. You want the ratings, so you put me in two matches in one night. You are hoping that THE MONSTER comes out and gives your fans the things they pay to see. At least that is what you say. But deep down, I know your real reason behind this week's plan, to see me wrestle twice in one night. I know the truth behind your actions, I am the only one not fooled.' Ice lowers his head, and again gazes out into the night. The security light begins to flicker. Ice's attention is not altered, as he doesn't even notice it, and continues to stare out over the city, and into the night. 'You think so much of yourself. You think so much of what you have done, and the fact that no one has caught on. Well, I think that you think TOO MUCH of yourself. I know that the only reason for me having two matches, is that you are trying to get rid of me. You are trying to stop THE MONSTER one way or another.' Ice stands up from his seated position on the edge of the building. He adjusts his pants and his shirt, steps off the rooftop ledge, and back onto the roof of the building. He picks up the small red papered package and puts it into this pocket. Ice turns and looks directly into the camera. 'The facts are this. You are only fueling the fire with your actions Christina. You are the one who is unleashing THE MONSTER. And I WILL NOT BE HELD ACCOUNTABLE!' Ice turns from the camera, and walks away. The camera quickly follows as Ice walks across the rooftop, and over to a small cage filled with pigeons. Ice stops and gazes at the pigeons. 'Pigeons. Exactly how Christina thinks of her superstars. Pigeons, caged in by their contracts. Pigeons, waiting for Christina to open the cage door and allow them to spread their wings and fly. Evolution, one pigeon, is being sent to do Christina's dirty work. Does that pigeon really know what they are getting into, or is that pigeon just doing what he is told, so he has a chance to spread his wings?' Ice pulls the small red papered package out from his pocket. He pulls off the red paper and reveals a handful of firecrackers. Ice lights one of the matches from the book he holds in his other hand, and lights the firecracker. Ice watches the firecracker burn down, and tosses it at the cage just before it explodes. A loud pop echos through the night, and causes the bird to squawk and flutter in fear. Ice sadistically smirks. 'Frightened Ben? Did the minor explosion cause you to jump? How are you doing Ben?' Ice pulls another firecracker out from his pocket. He lights it and just as he did the one before tosses it at the bird cage just before it explodes in his hand. 'Ben, I am going to say this now, before I forget to say it later. What I do to you this week, is not my fault. I am not going to be held responsible, for the actions of Christina McCoy. You have been forewarned.' Ice walks over to the pigeon cage, and opens the door. Flock of pigeons burst through the opening, except one, which Ice manages to keep in the cage, by suddenly slamming the cage door shut on it. Again Ice smirks to himself. 'The lone pigeon, thinking that being singled out by his capture is going to allow him to spread his wings and fly, when in all actuality it only cages and confines him more. I saw what happened to you. In fact, I found it quite amusing. It gave me chills to see you as that explosion went off. I just wished I had thought of that myself. And because of what happened last week, I'm sure you have this match as some kind of deal for retribution later on, should you do as Christina asks.' Ice lights another match and sticks it through the tiny holes of the bird cage. The lone pigeon in the cage flutters around in fear of the flame. Ice's cold blue eyes glimmer with the reflection of the flame. 'I know you are not up to this task that is before you. I know you are still in pain and agony from what happened to you just one week ago. But that does not change what I plan on doing. You see, through you, I am going to send a message to Christina McCoy that she will never EVER forget. And through you, I am going to show the entire world just what it is that I am capable of.' Ice pulls off the last match from the book, and twirls it around, inbetween his right thumb and index finger. 'You want your ratings? You want THE MONSTER? Then you will have to live with the consequences.' Ice lights the last match, and presses its flame gently against the tip of the last firecracker. Ice tosses the firecracker into the bird cage. 'POP!' The firecracker explodes and a puff of smoke and feathers shoot out from inside the cage. Ice turns and evilly snarls at the camera. The camera zooms in on his cold, icy blue eyes, then turns to the random parts of the pigeon that remain from the explosion. The scene fades.
