Date: 11-12-03 “OK, let’s go over that again, from top,” Brick said over the intercom as he and Aaron watched from the control room in the recording studio. This week, the group was recording their cover of Billy Joel’s “The Longest Time”. They had been working this track for hours now, recording take after take. This was to be their fifth take on the song.
