Date: 2-1-06 I came, I saw, I kicked ass, and I claimed the damn title! January 23rd, 2006 was the night that my dance partner obviously didn’t think that he would be laying flat on his back, staring up at the dim lights of defeat. He should’ve known that I was going to take his ass to the limit. Once I step foot in the roped octagon, the thought of being in Global disappears, as it becomes my battlefield. I guess he’ll remember that the next time we dance. “Jay Robson” He said that he was going to prove to me and the Global fans that he was for real. It doesn’t get as real as me, junior. And the only thing that he proved to me was that he has an ass pirate for help. He also proved that he needed help to defeat me. Despite the annoyance of that so-called interference he still felt the wrath of the Deathscythe Outlaw’literally. Pretty boy did do quite a number on the knee, but I believe the cool platinum from the title will ease the pain. This was a glorious victory to say the least. It would’ve been much better if I was able to get my hands on that ass pirate and shoved that towel down his throat before I gave him an ass kicking of a lifetime. Oh well, beggars can’t be choosers, or something like that. With that battle said and done, it is time for the next one. So, there’s only one thing left to say. Who’s the next causality? [:: ----- ::] ==|Part I: Who’s An Angel|== The scene opens somewhere on the Pacific Ocean on a clear sunny day. Seagulls were out flying and song their smoothing tune to the sea. The boat that the Deathscythe Outlaw has been burrowing from the mysterious island for some time now was sitting motionless in the middle of the ocean. Kyo was at the boat’s back deck. He was lean back in the kind of lounge chair that would be seen on a cruise line. Kyo had the second volume of the underrated anime manga series, Hellsing drape over his face, as he was using this to shield himself from the bright hot sun. However, this wasn’t the only reason why he was hiding from the sun. “Damnit, this damn headache won’t go away.” His manga fell off his face and into his lap after he sat up in the chair. Kyo has dark circles under his eyes that made him look like a raccoon. It appeared that he and a couple of stagehands from Global did some hard partying in celebration of his victory at his dual life. He leaned back in the chair and stare up at the blue sky with his hand over his face. “This would’ve been a perfect time for my healing power.” Kyo was right. His healing power can heal everything and that included hangovers. Kyo rests his right hand over his forehead and closed his eyes, as a soft sigh escaped his lips. He opens his eyes halfway and took notice of some bandages that was sticking out from the end of his right gauntlet. Kyo removes the long gauntlet off his arm, as he stared at his bandaged arm. The memory of the horrifying death of his mother soon enters his mind. He sees her handing him the mysterious blood covered sword to him while her blood spewed out from the large hole in her abdomen. Kyo also hears her speak her last words to him in his mind. ---- “My gift to you is the sword that has been forged with the Spiritual Energy of Heaven’s Light. Use the sword responsibly, my loving son, Kyosuke?” ---- Kyo still stares at his arm, as he started to wonder what was so special about the sword that was infused in his own arm. He also wonders who was responsible for intrusting the sword to his mother long before he was even awakened from his crystal tomb. His thoughts are broken by a sudden jerk of the boat. Kyo sits up and looked around with puzzlement on his face. “What the?” Did something hit the boat?” He soon heard the boat’s power engines were activating. “What the hell?” Kyo got up and quickly entered the boat’s control room. He sees that all the boat’s controls were turning on by themselves. It was like the boat had a mind of its own. Kyo quickly approaches the control panel and tried to deactivate the boat, but it was to no prevail. All controls are soon on, as the boat started to sail him somewhere. Kyo didn’t know if all this was a good thing or a bad thing. Nevertheless, he equips his firearms after he made sure that they were fully loaded and they were. He wasn’t going to take any chances. “Mr. Kushinaga?” Kyo looks around the room with puzzlement. “Mr. Kushinaga, can you read me?” He notices that the soft woman’s voice was coming from a small television screen right beside the steering wheel. Kyo approaches and saw it was the maid from the castle at the unknown island was on the screen. “Mr. Kushinaga, can you read me?” Kyo sees that a phone was hanging beside the screen, as he picked it up with his eyes locked on the screen. “Morgan, what the hell is going on” The boat just started moving and ?” The maid, Morgan closes her eyes with her trademark cheerful look on her face. “Don’t be alarmed, Mr. Kushinaga. I activated the boat’s navigation system from here on the island and it’s transporting you back here.” “Really want to be alone right now.” “Her Highness has returned from her spiritual journey just this morning. She asked me to fetch you, so she can speak with you.” “That’s good and dandy, but I’m not in to much of a mood for small talk right now.” Kyo was about to hang the phone up. “Her Highness says that she has the answers that you seek.” Kyo stops midway. “Please hear Her Highness out, Mr. Kushinaga.” Kyo closes his eyes with a soft sigh to himself, as he lifted the phone back up to his face. “What a weakness I have.” Morgan looks with puzzlement. Kyo opens his brown colored eyes halfway, as a smirk appeared on his face. “I can’t say no to beautiful face.” Morgan has a please smile on her face. “Thank you, Mr. Kushinaga.” “But only on one condition.” “Yes?” “You know how to make a Prairie Oyster?” Morgan blinks, as she had a puzzled look on her face. [:: --Later-- :: ] It was mid-afternoon at the unknown island. The boat sails to the island where it soon docked on the golden beach. Kyo steps out of the boat’s control room, as he straightened his long black trench coat while looking at his surroundings. He hops off the boat, as he soon saw that he was greeted by the cloaked assassin, Chi. Chi stares at him with her crimson colored eyes. “Man, you look like crap.” Kyo sighs, as he pulled out his orange lens sunglasses from his trench coat and put them. “Well, you’re no sunshine either.” He headed in Chi’s direction to only pass her and began walking on a long path that leads to the castle. Chi sniffs the air. “You reek of alcohol.” She began to follow him. “Were we getting wasted?” She smirked. “If you must know a couple of fellas took me out partying. Now I got a damn hangover that would kill a moose.” “Aww, you poor baby.” Chi laughed. “Bite me.” Chi has seduction in her eyes along with a dark grin on her blue colored lips. “Tell me where.” Kyo only response by holding up one of his large handgun that has white cross screwed on the long barrel. The handgun with the white cross was fully loaded with Pure Spirit Shells that can easily eliminate anything from the world of darkness which included Chi. Chi nods with a small laugh. They continued walking down the path. [:: ----- ::] They arrived at the castle, as the maid, Morgan met them in the castle’s Main Hall. Morgan soon escorts both them to the castle’s Great Study to finally meet Her Highness. When arriving to the Great Study, they all saw that machines were organizing and putting books on the wall to wall bookshelves. Morgan resumes escorting them through the large room. “My client appears to be quite the bookworm.” Chi looked around her surroundings in awe. “When Her Highness isn’t doing her duties as a Light Maiden then she’s reading her great collection of a Spiritual Spells.” Chi blinks. “Didn’t know they were so many Spiritual Spells.” Kyo looks at Morgan with puzzlement. “She’s a Light Maiden?” Morgan nods. “Yes, a High Shinto Priestess.” Kyo begins to wonder what a Light Maiden would want with him. He also wondered if she did in fact have the answers that he seeks. They soon arrive to an open-spaced area that was in this room. Morgan bows. “Your Highness, they have arrived.” Kyo and Chi was staring at a young woman in an all white kimono who sat on top of a small ladder with a thick brown colored book on her lap. Her Highness looks at both of them gentle emerald eyes. “Hello, Mr. Kushinaga and Ms. Luxandra. I’m glad that you decided to join me here.” She smiled. “I’m sorry that I couldn’t greet you two before, but I was in China performing some purification that needed my attention. I’m Light Maiden, Athena Moon.” Chi has a look of puzzlement on her face. “Athena Moon?” Kyo shares the same look. “That name sounds familiar.” Athena stands up from her seat after she placed the book back on the shelf. “It should since this isn’t the first time we all met.” Kyo and Chi look at each other puzzled looks on their faces and then back at Athena. “Perhaps you two don’t remember me in this form.” Athena’s entire body suddenly glow a bright light. The bright light disappears and revealed that Athena has shrunken to the size of a little child. “How’ll about now?” “YOU!” Kyo and Chi looked at Athena with great shock, as they remembered Athena from their past and they should since that’s how they first met. ---- It all happened two years ago. Kyo was still with the Spirit Guardians as the Masked Avenger. He was assigned to protect a young sorceress, little Athena, but of course this task wasn’t going to be an easy one. The Yazuka sent their assassin, Chi to take the young girl’s life. Avenger and Chi fought each other. This was how Chi got that scar that’s just over her navel. Avenger managed to defeat Chi with help from little Athena. After Chi’s defeat, Avenger took little Athena to the Light Maidens that transport her to Eden Isle that is located somewhere in the Caribbean where she would develop her skills to become a Light Maiden herself. Little Athena was never heard from her again until now... ---- “What the hell are you trying to pull here, you little brat!” Chi glared at Athena, as she was feeling used and that was something that she doesn’t take too kindly to. “Keep it down.” Chi glares at Kyo. “Keep it down!” Aren’t you as pissed as I am about this!”” She pointed at Athena. “This brat could’ve been playing us for fools!” “SHUT UP!” Kyo favored his head, as the hangover got worse after his outburst. “Damn it.” He glared at Chi. “Look, you need to calm down. Surely, she has a very and I do mean very good explanation about all this.” Kyo turned and look at Athena. “But I’m beginning to think that this was all a ploy to get us together like this.” Athena looks with surprise. Chi looks at Kyo with puzzlement. “What the hell are you talking about, Avenger?” Kyo lights a cigarette with his silver lighter, as he took a drag from it before he breathed the smoke pass his lips. “Think about it. Did you receive any information about the girl when you were assigned to kill her?” Chi ponders. “Come to think about it, I didn’t. I didn’t receive any type of information about the brat.” Kyo has a serious look on his face. “I was in the same boat too. The Spirit Guardians gave me the assignment and that was all. No information. No nothing.” Chi looks with surprise. “Wait, are you telling me that the assassination was staged!”” Kyo slowly nods. “It was nothing more then a test.” Chi stared at Kyo. “Test for what?” “A test to see if both of you would be ready to face the great darkness that would soon plague the Mortal Realm.” Kyo and Chi notice that a tall woman soon in same kind of kimono as Athena has stepped out from behind the bookcase that Athena was standing in front of. The tall woman stares at Kyo with a gentle smile on her face. “It’s has been a while, Mr. Kushinaga.” “Yeah, it has.” Kyo didn’t look too please to see her after all he just found out that he was right about everything. However, he was never wrong when it came to stuff like this. Chi glances at Kyo. “You know this woman?” Kyo nods. “Forgive me, I haven’t introduced myself yet.” Tall woman bowed to them. “I’m Light Maiden, Megumi Wind.” “If you’re here then that means we’re on Eden Isle, right?” Kyo stared at Megumi. Megumi nods. “Correct.” Chi gasps, as she looked around with surprise. “I knew this place looked kinda of familiar. This castle is the Light Maiden’s Grand Shrine.” Chi blinked, as a look of puzzlement appeared on her face. “But wait isn’t this place supposed to be full of Light Maidens. I haven’t seen any since being here.” Athena soon reverts back to have grown up form. “I wasn’t really in China.” Kyo and Chi look at Athena with puzzlement. Athena sits back down on the top step of the ladder with a sadden look on her face. “I was searching for other the Light Maidens to only discover that almost all the Light Maidens throughout the world have been haunted down and killed. I, Megumi, and Light Maiden cadet, Morgan are the only ones left.” Kyo knew that this was Aion’s doing, as this was a classic strategy. Wipe out all threats that could delay someone from achieving their goals. In Aion’s case, killing all mortals to start a new world for beings like him. He takes another drag from his cigarette before he breathed the warm smoke through his nostrils. “I thought all you Light Maidens were supposed to be powerful.” Chi stared at the three Light Maidens. “Guess not.” Kyo sighs, as he scratches his raven colored hair with an inpatient look on his face. “I’m really sorry about your lost, but I still want to know what the hell this has to do with me.” Chi glares. “The same goes for me too.” Megumi stares at Kyo. “What if I were to tell you that you’re a divine angel from Heaven?” Kyo chuckles a bit before he gave Megumi an amuse look. “Then I would ask if you have been drinking?” “Light Maidens don’t drink. We tell the truth and I’m telling you the truth. You are a divine angel.” Chi crosses her arms, as she laughed. “This rude, gun-happy, thick-headed, smoking outlaw who is perhaps two steps away from becoming a full blown alcoholic is a divine angel. That’s funny.” Kyo glares at Chi. “I’m still in the room you know.” He sighed. “In any case, I don’t buy it.” Kyo suddenly felt his right arm mysterious pulsing within the trench coat’s sleeve. He has puzzled look on his face, as he grabbed hold of his arm, trying to stop the pulsing. Megumi notices this, as she stared at Kyo with seriousness. “The sword that is infused in that arm is telling that I speak the truth.” Kyo soon feels the pulsing stopping, as he hid his arm behind him while glaring at her through his sunglasses. “How do you know about the sword?” “Because I was the one who entrusted your mother with it.” “So, you were the being that shine a bright golden light that my mother spoke of.” He nodded, as he let out a soft sigh pass his lips. “Ok?” Without another thought, Kyo quickly pulled out one of his handguns from inside his trench coat. It was the handgun with the red colored cross screwed on it, as this gun was fully loaded with Dark Spirit Shells that can kill anything that came from the world of the light. The gun was aim right at Megumi. “Give me a good reason why I shouldn’t drop your ass where you stand for what you did.” Chi and the two Light Maidens look with surprise. Megumi stands there with an unafraid look on her face. “Your mother was aware of her own death before I even appeared to her those years ago. She told me that she wanted to help you on your fight against darkness.” She looked with sadness in her cerulean colored eyes. “I was skeptical to know that this woman had accepted her fate without even a trace of regret. It made me feel guilty for placing the sword within her for it to only drain her own life force.” Kyo forms a content look with his eyes closed, as he lowered his gun. “That’s the kind of person she was. She always put her children first before herself. I always figured that she was God’s mother.” Megumi stares at Kyo. “Then you know that she wouldn’t want her death to be in vain. She would want you to do what is right.” Kyo sighs to himself, as he slipped the gun back in his trench coat. “I still don’t believe the fact that I am some kind of an angel from Heaven.” Athena looks at Kyo with great content. “You’re not just any angel. You’re the legendary divine angel!” She knew this since she read ancient books that have been written about him. Chi scoffs. “If he’s so legendary then why is here instead living it up in Heaven right now.” “The annoying vampire has a point there. Why am I not in Heaven right now?” Kyo stared at Athena and Megumi with a puzzled look on his face. Chi glares at Kyo. “Who’s annoying!” Athena looks away from Kyo. “I’m not sure why?” Kyo looks at Athena with a puzzled look on his face. He looks over to Megumi and saw that she was giving the same distant look as Athena. Kyo also notices that Morgan was too. He soon realizes that they were hiding something from him. “Ok, you answered the divine one’s question. Now what my question.” Chi glared at Megumi and Athena. “What is my role in all this?” “I would like to know that too after all I almost tried to kill her.” Megumi looks at Kyo. “But you didn’t.” Kyo smirks. “Only because I ran out of bullets.” Megumi shakes her head. “You wasn’t going to kill even if did had bullets. Self-consciously you were aware that she was someone important to you from the past.” Chi looks at Megumi. “But I don’t know him. I only know that this is the same guy who left an ugly scar on my beautiful body.” “How could anyone tell by you wearing that cloak all the time.” Kyo had his eyes closed with both arms folded behind his head. Chi glares at Kyo. “You wouldn’t know how to control yourself if you saw what I looked like.” Kyo smirks. “Doubt it.” “The attempt assassination isn’t the first time you two met.” Megumi looked at Kyo and Chi. Kyo and Chi look at Megumi with puzzlement. “Ms. Luxandra is Rain’s younger sister.” Chi blinks. “I’m who’s sister?” Kyo looks with great shock. He faintly does remember that his beloved, Rain had a younger sister, but he wasn’t sure if the vampire assassin was actually what they said she is. “Rain, the woman that Mr. Kushinaga had fallen in love with years ago in the Feudal Era.” Megumi looked at Chi. Chi laughs. “Come on, I would remember something like that. Besides, the only family I have is my vampire family. I don’t know any Rain neither do I know him.” “You and Kyo are the same. Somehow, both your memories were completely rewritten with new memories.” Megumi stared at Chi. Athena ponders a bit. “I believe their memories were rewritten the same time of Rain’s death.” Megumi nods. “It was. Both of you took high risks to gain supreme power to achieve revenge for the death of a love one.” Kyo has Rain’s wedding band in the palm of his hand, as he was staring down at it with a disbelief look on his face. He gives Chi a silent stare. Chi blinks, as she looked back at Kyo. She didn’t know rather to believe the Light Maiden’s words or not. Kyo turns away from Chi, as he closed his hand and started walking away from them. Athena notices Kyo leaving. “Wait, Mr. Kushinaga.” Megumi places her hand on Athena’s shoulder. “Let him be.” Kyo continues to walk away in silence. He soon blurs away from behind a bookcase, as he wanted some time alone to think the things that the Light Maidens laid upon him. [:: --Later -- ::] It was a clear cool night at Eden Isle. The Deathscythe Outlaw was lying on top of the boat’s roof with folded arms behind his head, as he was staring up at the stars with a serious look on his face. He was thinking about what was said to him not too long ago, as he could hear their voices echoing in his mind. ---- “You are a divine angel.” “You’re not just any angel. You’re the legendary divine angel!” “If he’s so legendary then why is here instead living it up in Heaven right now.” ---- Kyo still stares at the stars. “Was I banished from Heaven” If was then what was the reason for the banishment?” He closed his brown colored eyes halfway, as they had seriousness in them. Kyo begins to hear more of their voices. ---- “You wasn’t going to kill even if did had bullets. Self-consciously you were aware that she was someone important to you from the past.” “The attempt assassination isn’t the first time you two met.” “Ms. Luxandra is Rain’s younger sister.” ---- Kyo holds Rain’s wedding band in front of him, as he was staring at in deep thought. “Rain, could your younger sister really survived when the Shinto Shrine was burned down” Could that vampire really be, Misato.” He continued to stare at it, as it was almost like he was hoping that the ring would reply to him. Kyo’s thoughts are broken by the whimpering of a wolf. He sits up and sees that Sephiroth was staring up at from the boat’s back deck. Kyo closes his eyes with a smirk on his lips. “Yeah, I know. We’re heading out now.” He slipped the ring back within his black shirt. Kyo hops down from the roof and landed on the back deck. He enters the boat’s control along with Sephiroth where he activates the boat’s controls. Kyo soon begins to sail away from island, as he was heading back to the states to resume his dual life as a professional fighter. ---- An atom bomb has been dropped on Kyo and Chi. But the question was what the Light Maiden’s words fact or fiction? Is the Deathscythe Outlaw really a divine angel? Is the vampire assassin, Chi really the long lost younger sister of Kyo’s beloved?
