One on one with Aaron

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/24/04 It's a grey, cloudy day in July. Even though there's a chance of rain, Brick and Aaron hit the basketball courts for a little one-on-one. The game they're currently playing has been going on for about a half-hour. Brick is now on the offensive, getting ready to go to the hole. Aaron puts forth the best defense he can, given the relative size differences. The score is tied up, 9-9. The game ends at 10. 'Come one, man. You ain't got no better D than that?' 'Oh, you wanna see D? I got your D!' Brick fakes left and then spins around to the right and heads for the basket. One lay-up shot and Brick wins the game. He and Aaron then bump fists and head for the bench. 'Good game, man.' 'Yeah, I needed it.' 'I know. I saw Evolution. Man, was Deacon pissed.' 'I know,' says Brick. 'That was the whole point.' 'Brick, be honest with me. This is your boy talking to you. Did you really have sex with his girlfriend?' 'Man, how long have you known me?' 'That doesn't answer my question.' 'It should, because you should know when I'm bluffing and when I'm serious.' 'Yeah, but...' 'Look, I know what I'm doing.' 'All I'm saying is maybe it's not such a good idea to piss Deacon off. I mean, TUE seems pretty tight. And from what you've told me, they've already killed Akodo Rokku and the Nakazuras. They might decide to stand behind their boy and ' 'What, I'm supposed to be scared o' TUE? Fuck TUE, especially Deacon. I ain't never been scared o' Deacon Holmes before, an' I damn sure don't plan to start bein' afraid of him. Besides, I got other things to worry about.' 'Oh, you mean Master of the Mat? I thought you lost the qualifying match because of...' 'I did,' Brick replies, cutting Aaron off. He knows Aaron is about to mention Ely Braxton's severe beating of Deacon Holmes, which cost him his match. 'There's two lottery matches for some of the guys who didn't make it into Round 2. Each one is a six-man elimination gauntlet match. It starts off with two fighters. With each fighter that gets eliminated, a new one enters the Octagon.' 'Which one are you in?' 'I'm in the first lottery match.' 'Lucky you.' 'Yeah, lucky,' Brick replies, rolling his eyes. 'So, who else is in this match?' 'It's me, Trey Reed...' 'Wasn't he in the company before?' 'Yeah, he decided to come back. I barely remember the cat.' 'OK, who else?' 'John Zero, some new cat named Dagoth Kinslayer...' 'Dag-what?' 'I know. As if we didn't have enough cats in the company with weird-ass names. I mean, we got Soma an' Pendragon...' 'Who else?' 'Ely's in the match.' 'Is he?' 'Yeah, booking added him at the last minute.' 'Is everything cool with you two? I saw that Evolution when he attacked Deacon right before your match.' 'We squashed that issue a week ago. Besides, Deacon had it coming.' 'That's good, but...' 'Look, we can kep it professional. No anger or ego in it. Besides, we might not even have to face each other.' 'You said the match is a six man slimination gauntlet, right?' 'Yeah.' 'Well, so far, you've only mentioned five guys, including yourself 'Train,' Brick grumbled. 'Uh-oh.' 'What do you mean 'uh-oh'?' 'Well, it's just that Train gets under your skin almost as easily as Deacon does. I remember on the second Deaconpalooza when he was ragging on you about losing to Deacon...' That last comment causes an angry grunt to come out of Brick. Brick hates to be reminded that he's lost to Deacon twice, so far. 'Oh, sorry.' 'Yeah, well, he was raggin' on Deacon, too. Even though he was clownin' me at the same time, I had to laugh. Cause it was funny.' 'So, either way, you might have your hands full at Master of the Mat.' 'I'll deal with it. I always do.'
