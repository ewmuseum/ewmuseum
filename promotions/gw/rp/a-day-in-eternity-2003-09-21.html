A Day In Eternity

21 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/09/03 Jushin walked away from the small bonfire burning in front of them. He had done as his master Tanaka had requested. Jushin was pleased that they had passed their test and after the long hours in front of the fire, he was ready to leave anyways. He walked across the roof, and then down the stairs. He met and talked to a few of the elder monks, that he had known as a child. Ichiro sat in silence. Nervous to see what it was that his master had wanted. He was also very anxious to get away from the temple. His mind and body were all exhausted from the day's tests, and he was happy to be through with them. Even happier having passed the tests of mind, body and soul. Tanaka had called the brothers the Souls of the Black Lotus, it had a ring to it, and it declared their place as the chosen ones. He sat in silence watching as his master sat with his eyes closed. He wondered what his master could possibly be thinking about. He and his brother had passed the tests. They were the chosen ones, what else was there possibly to say? The monk opened his eyes slowly, and stared at Ichiro. He knew that he had asked him to stay, but was indeed surprised that Ichiro had sat in silence for so long. He looked into Ichiro's eyes and saw the weariness, but he also saw the happiness and relief in his eyes. 'Congratulations,' Tanaka finally said. 'THAT'S IT! That's all he wanted to say,' thought Ichiro as he sat there. He looked into his master's eyes and now realized in relief that he was not done speaking yet. 'You and your brother have passed the tests. However, I must warn you, that your brother is not as strong as you are Ichiro. Jushin is still far from his full potential. His mind is clouded with anger, rage and fury. He allows this to hurry his mind and body. You must watch over him, and help him to grow,' continued the monk. 'Yes master,' was all that Ichiro could manage to put forth. 'You are strong Ichiro. Stronger and wiser than any I have ever seen. You have double the knowledge and the strength that I had at your age. You are the chosen one. You could pass these tests on your own, but not without Jushin's help. Jushin is your motivation. You seek to surpass where he can go no further. He is your brother and the connection that you two share is a bond so strong, that it pushes each of you to be stronger, and to help each other.' the master monk continued his speech. 'I understand,' said Ichiro. 'However, soon enough your love for each other will be tested. Jushin's soul is dark and black. He will be one to push your love for him to the limits. There will come a time when you will have to save him, or your task will fail. It has been shown to me, and I must tell you this.' Ichiro could not stand it any longer. He wasn't just going to sit here and listen to Tanaka make comments about his brother. His brother had always had his back, the only person who ever had. And he wasn't about to let someone talk poorly of him. 'Jushin is my brother! He would never do anything to hurt or betray me! We have always been there for each other and that is something that will never change! Not for all eternity!' declared Ichiro. 'And so it shall bind you to your word, Ichiro. The Black Lotus is always listening, and now it shall hold you to your declaration. Your faith in your brother has surpassed any expectation that I had. And so I shall now tell you of what the Lotus says of your soul,' said Tanaka. 'Okay,' answered Ichiro, not knowing any other words with which to reply. 'I do not wish for you to speak now, my son. Just listen with open ears and open mind,' requested the monk. Ichiro nodded and closed his eyes. He listened to the sounds of nature. The chirping of the midnight owls, the wind whistling through the trees, the soft whispers of the trees speaking to one another, the sound of the night sky eliminating all light from the mountain atmosphere. Then the soothing sounds were interrupted by the words of his master. 'You, Ichiro are strong and wise. Strongest and wisest the Black Lotus has ever seen. You spoke of your faith in your brother as lasting for an eternity. So it is with this vow in mind, that you shall be called the Eternal Soul. Your strength and your mind are your power. You fight with a full use of the Lotus' power and it is this that will get you far in your task, however the Black Lotus cannot help you in your vow to your brother. All of Eternity, you said, and so you shall be called. For you are the Eternal warrior of the Lotus, the one who shall bring the light of the Black Lotus, to all others in the world.' stated the monk. Ichiro kept his eyes shut, and awaited to see if there would be more. 'Jushin, full of rage, anger and impatience. He is the darkness to the light which you shall bring to those who oppose us. It is with a quick reaction of body and not of mind and soul of which your brother acts. This darkness has earned him the title The Dark Soul. Do not forget my words, or think that I dislike your brother. For I share the same love for him that I do for you. His anger and rage can be great tools, if he can learn to harness them and use them to aid him. And not to use them to his disadvantage.' Ichiro knew these words were true. He had tried to speak to Jushin about it before, but Jushin was stubborn and would not listen. He was now beginning to see what it was that the Black Lotus had already seen. He must make sure he kept his brother under control. It was his brotherly duty to do so, and now it was a responsibilty that the entire temple depended on. Ichiro sat in silence and awaited the finish to his master's speech. 'So god speed now to you Ichiro Nakazura. The Eternal Soul, the strongest warrior in our service, and the strongest in your father's service. Go forth now, and fulfill this great honour which has been bestowed upon you. For it is your duty, and this mission of mind, body and soul, will eventually fall onto your shoulders. Bring the words of the Lotus to the western world, and do your damage there. Show everyone, that the ways of Eastern Shao Lin, and the Forgotten Arts are a strength like no other. The unity of the Souls of the Black Lotus, leaves intact, but none now no whether it will return that way. Good luck Ichiro.' concluded the monk. Ichiro opened his eyes. He stared at the monk's face. In all of the years that he had known his master he had never seen a sign of emotion. He had always seent the same focussed expression, through the dark times, the sad times, the happy times and all those which came in between. He stood and bowed to his master. He turned to leave, and left his master sitting down. 'Don't look back Ichiro. You can't look back, and remember what you're leaving.' thought Ichiro. Despite his hardest trying he turned back to face his master again. That is when he noticed it, a tear on his master's face. Ichiro didn't know what to do, he had never seen expression on the man's face. Throughout all the years, his master's face has been like a statue. He walked back towards his master. He didn't know why but something was pulling him back. Tanaka has not noticed Ichiro's turn. He slowly stood up and gathered the pillows which he had been sitting on. He wiped the tear from his face. He was truly sad to see them go. He had watched the brothers grow up. From the loners in the group, who had often fought side by side against many other students for only respect or sometimes less. He had seen them flourish into the great men and the great warriors they now were. He stood and turned to follow Ichiro, pillows in hand. But when he did Ichiro was standing there in front of him. 'You startled me,' said Tanaka with a tear still on his cheek. And struggling to keep from bursting out into tears. Ichiro took the pillows from his master's hands. He looked into the monk's face and smiled, as a tear ran down his cheek also. He dropped the pillow and embraced the monk. Without hesitation the monk, hugged him back. For so many years, Tanaka had been like Ichiro's father. Ichiro knew that Jushin would never show such emotion, but he did not care. Jushin was not around to disapprove. After what seemed like only seconds, but had been much longer, the two men parted. Both wiped the tears from their faces and from their eyes. Then Ichiro bent down and helped his master carry the pillows back down the stairs. They came down and Ichiro saw his brother waiting impatiently near the front door, talking to a few of the younger students, who had awoken. Ichiro handed the pillows to his master, who smiled and took them from his former student. Ichiro bowed to Tanaka, a great sign of respect in the Shao Lin Temple, and then the two parted. Each man knew in their hearts that they would see each other again, but they did not know when. Ichiro smiled and turned, walking towards his brother. He walked up to his brother, who glared at him, obviously upset that he had to wait and speak to the youngsters. All around the few children awake were talking about all of the stories that they had heard about Jushin and his brother Ichiro. 'Ready to go, Dark Soul?' asked Ichiro smiling. 'What the hell are you talking about? Dark Soul?' demanded Jushin. Ichiro could only smile as he remembered that Jushin did not know of that nickname yet. 'I'll explain it in the car. Are you ready, bro?' asked Ichiro. 'I've been ready for half an hour! Are you ready to go finally?!' asked Jushin arrogantly. Ichiro smiled as he recollected on his master's words. He saw the flaws but did not care, Jushin had always been there for him, why would that change now? 'Let's go then.' Ichiro's words were cut short, as they began to walk out the door of the temple. Both turning to the temple and bowing in respect as they walked towards Jushin's car............ Ichiro's eyes shot open in a flash. It was the second time this week that he had dreamed about that night. He shook the thought out of his head and looked sleepily at the alarm clock on his night table. 10:17 'Well I guess I should get this day going.' Ichiro said out loud to himself. He stood up, and shook the covers of himself. He looked at his laundry hamper, and decided to forget about getting dressed. He would do a quick workout before he did anything else. He walked towards the kitchen, wearing only his pair of black Fruit of the Loom boxer briefs.
