Civil War

13 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-13-04 The setting is a dark and dank gym, fog and a general grayness covering its entire scope. Dust sits in every crevice and corner of the room, especially the ring, which is torn and in poor condition, looking neither fit for performance or to take any further abuse. Its foundation is crumbling and it creaks loudly if even the slightest amount of weight is applied to it. The weight systems look out dated and old rather than sturdy and strong. An aging bench is covered in cobwebs along its feet and dust along its surface. Ace sits on the bench with his head drooping down, his shoulders covered with dust and his arms slung over his knees. Ace: For so long I asked myself, “Why do I do this?” I asked other people, “Why do I do this?” I even asked God, “Why do I do this?” And the only answer I could come up with, was, “Because I love it.” But love is something I’ve never understood, and it’s something that can easily be shattered, then rearranged like a puzzle, and turned into something worse’hate. I no longer live for that feeling of walking down that aisle, feeling the rumble, the intensity, the LOVE of the crowd, that rush of adrenaline that overtakes you when you step into that ring. That feeling you get to know, that once again’you’re home. That feeling is gone. And it’s been gone for awhile, no matter how much I wanted to believe it wasn’t. The love for this sport has passed on into darkness, and the hatred for everything that makes this BUSINESS is left. It’s not about who wins or loses, who’s the better man or the better wrestler, and it sure as hell isn’t about who the better human being is. It’s about the money, the fame, who you are and who you know, how many titles you have, how many times you’ve beaten somebody, how many awards, how many submissions, how many pin falls, how much merchandise you sell, how much DVDs you release, how many T-shirts on the website, how many fan sites you have. It’s about who you are, but not in terms of character, but in terms of how much MONEY you can make for other people. Are you famous? Do the fans want to buy all the worthless SHIT that happens to have your name on it? This isn’t a sport anymore’maybe it never was?.this is a business. And I’ve been shoveling shit for free in it. Ace picks his head up to look into the camera. Ace: A lot of people have asked me, why I joined up with the Elite. A lot have asked me why I would be so willing to admit my own hypocrisy, by joining up with a man I loathed more than life itself. You would think they were right, if you only understood “why.” It took me 10 years to finally realize the truth, that I would always be a nobody if I continued on my current path. If I continued to believe that by sacrificing my body and mind on the physical “wrestling” aspect of the business, that being a just and decent person would get me to where I deserved to be, then I would be where all those old farts and never-were nobodies like Darren Drozdov are, sitting around with shattered ankles, knees, broken necks, backs, chipped elbows, and half of my front teeth gone. Because this business is NOT worth dying over, no matter WHO you fucking are!!! But’instead of becoming like those people, I decided to finally make a difference in my career. I decided that the name Ace Harding would no longer be the name you see at the bottom of a secondary show, against some other no-name wrestler you couldn’t give two SHITS about. And the one who could do that for me, was Ken Partridge, Jr. For so long Partridge, I hated you, because you reminded me of myself. But more importantly, it was the parallels of what I COULD have been. You’re young, a great wrestler, a master of submission, and many, many years of experience under your belt. I was the same. The only difference, is that you had what I fought my whole LIFE for, and NEVER earned’RESPECT. You were a multi-champion, a womanizer, a celebrity, a showstopper, a ratings grabber, a main attraction, and now, a leader of the most influential stable this fed has ever seen. And who am I? I am JUST as capable, as experienced, as young, and as smart as you are. But what have I gotten? NOTHING. Absolutely NOTHING. I haven’t won a single fucking title in my 10 years of this shit. NOT ONE!! What does that say about me? It says that I’m a nobody, a nothing, a never-was and a never-will-be. The Elite should have been my salvation, my stairway to heaven, my guiding star on a moonlight night through a desert of nothingness and frailty. But ever since I’ve joined this federation and stood side-by-side with you and your compadres, I’ve been chokeslammed off a cage, embarrassed and outwrestled, lowered to pummeling on a woman, lost a match after a SINGLE move, and a DDT no less, then got the shit beat out of me by two shit heads while you were off twiddling your thumbs or fingering your ASSHOLE SOMEWHERE, and FINALLY, to put a nice big fucking CHERRY on top, knocked out of my opportunity to become a number one contender, by?.YOU Ken. Of all people, YOU!! Do you think I don’t see what’s going on here, Ken? Don’t you think it’s a little strange that the moment I come to you asking for help, asking you to help me become a somebody and help pull me out of this hole I’ve dug myself in, that I suddenly get the raw end of the deal? You know what I think Ken, I think you had this planned all along’you found a way to hook me in, because I had a lack of self-confidence, and now you’re fucking me over while you have the chance. You really think I give a shit about your friends, and whether they live or die? I told you when I came here, that I was only doing this because I was desperate. Because I felt my career coming to an end, and I wanted to revive it from its zombified state. I NEVER said a damn thing about giving a shit about your friends, unless it involved a match or some backstage attack. And I find that in every situation, you’re never there, and neither are they, to back me up. Why should I give a damn about you, Ken? Why? When I’ve been getting the short end of the stick the entire time!! You must really think I’m an idiot, don’t you? You think I’m going to stand for this shit? DO YOU?!”! Ace breathes in and out, then relaxes himself. Ace: Ken Partridge, I admire you. You have everything a man can ask for in life, and more. I asked you to help me become a little more like you, and you agreed. I kept my end of the bargain, and I played the fall guy when you asked me to. But that’s going to chance, and now. I’m no longer gonna wait around for you to stick your dick in my ear anymore. My career is at an end, there is no doubt about that. But I’ll be damned if my “legacy” receives any further taint at the hands of somebody like you’you had better be careful the next time we tag up, and be sure that you don’t so much as LOOK at me the wrong way’because I WILL send you straight to hell and back in an instant, whether you want to go to that level or not?
