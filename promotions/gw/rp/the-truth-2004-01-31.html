The Truth

31 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31/04 'Jimmy, what time is it?' 'Five thirty.' 'Is your watch fast?' 'Give or take a couple minutes. Don't worry Brandi, you look great, the camera is ready, and we have a full reel of tape. When he gets here, we'll be ready, and won't miss a thing.' Brandi Sights nervously looks from side to side, while biting her bottom lip. Her red hair is pulled up into a low pony tail, and rests gently on her shoulders which are covered by a thin, black leather jacket. Continuing down her gorgeous figure, is a long pale white skirt, with a light blue flower pattern. The camera focuses in on her uneasy expression, and the large white van behind here that reads GLOBAL WRESTLING across its side in black letters outlined in gold. 'I know.....I know Jimmy. You know, he just kind of creeps me out. I mean you've seen the things he's done in the past. He even put you in the hospital Jimmy. And for some reason, he decides to call Ms. McCoy and request MY SPECIFIC PRESENCE for an interview. Then he makes the appointment for this....this...cemetary.' The camera turns away from Brandi Sights and surveys the surroundings, seeing nothing but old, dull, grey and black headstones sticking up from the grass that covers the bodies of the dead. The sky is a shade of light blue, with the occassional white, faintly visible cloud, lightly strolling across the large blue ocean above. 'I know, and believe me I'm not excited about this either. The guy is a psycho, but we have a job to do. So if Ms. McCoy is going to offer me double my pay to do this one, then I have to be out here. Reguardless if I like it or not.' The camera continues to pan around the scenery. The grass, the sky, and the headstones all seem to go on forever, and the camera gazes in on that sight, until the sound of an engine causes the cameraman to suddenly turn to see if the man who requested this interview is arriving. A large black hearst slowly rolls through the gates of the cemetary. Deathly slow, it begins to drive down the long gravel path, leading to the spot where Jimmy, Brandi, and the GW production van currently are. The hearst seems to take forever to reach them, but finally it does and the engine is killed. Brandi visibly begins to breath heavier in nervous anticipation of their guest. Quietly she stares at the large, black, vehicle of death, and awaits for something. 'Waiting for something?' Brandi jumps as the silence is broken by the booming words, and suddenly spins around. As she turns she sees Ice, leaning up against the side of the GW production van, with an emotionless expression on his face. His short blond hair is spiked up in random places, looking as if he just rolled out of bed. Casually he is dressed in a pair of blue jeans, and a white shirt that is partially covered by a long, black trench coat. 'Where'd you come from? And what's the deal with the hearst.' Ice smuggly smirks at Brandi and her startled inquiries. He pushes himself away from the production van, and walks towards Brandi. As he does, she quickly shoots an uneasy look at the camera, but stands her ground. 'Where I came from is of no importance to you. Let's just say, I've known you were here for quite sometime now, and was just waiting for my ride before I made my prescence known.' Brandi opens her mouth to speak, but stops before she can even get a word out when Ice's hand reaches forward. The hand grabs the tips of her glowing red hair, and begins to gently message them between its finger tips. Again an uneasy, and unsure look engulfs Brandi as Ice continues to play with her hair. She slowly raises her hand, and pulls her hair away from Ice's grip. 'Do I make you nerveous Brandi? Because you seem a little uneasy. A little stiff. Almost as if I frighten you. Is that so Ms. Sights?' 'N..No. I am just unsure of your reason for asking for me specifically, your reason for this location, and what you had to say. Playing with my hair does not answer any of those questions, and makes this interview seem very unprofessional.' Ice smirks and nods at Brandi's comments. 'Fair enough.' Brandi meekly smiles back at Ice's gesture and comment. 'To answer your questions, I specifically asked for you because I feel you are the best of the GW staff. That pink haired bimbo Alexis Steele, was frankly, getting on my nerves. The fact that you are quite the pretty picture to look at while talking to is what also lead me to my request.' Brandi's slightly blushes at Ice's remarks about her. 'Thank you. That was very kind of you to say.' Again Ice nods in response. 'I speak nothing but the truth. With that said, that leads me to your second question. Why this location. In life there is only one thing that is for certain. There is only one thing, no matter what else you say, or do that will always hold true. That one truth is death. Everyone dies, at some point. There is no escaping that. Just like there is no escaping the truth.' Brandi's eyebrow raises in confusion, and Ice quickly notices her expression. 'It appears as though you aren't following.' Slightly embarrassed Brandi's cheeks once again redden. 'Well, it's not that. I mean I do see the metaphoric reasoning for the location, but I was wondering what the literal reasoning was.' Ice inhales a long, deep breath, and slowly allows it to escape his body before answering Brandi. 'You see Brandi, the reason for my choice of location is the cemetary is the link...the final link from the FANTASY known as the 'real world', to the INEVITABLE of....MY WORLD. I brought you here, to allow you a chance to take in what I already know, and accept, so that you have a better understanding of the difference between myself and THE HYPE, as you call our match.' 'Then, what does any of this have to do with PTC Global Champion Seymour Almasy?' An evil smile crawls arcoss Ice's face. Just as quickly as it appeared, the evil smile disappears, as Ice walks past Brandi, and heads towards the hearst. Brandi quickly follows in step. The two reach the hearst, and Ice begins to caress the side of the vehicle. The driver's side door creaks open. An older man in a grey suit steps out, and walks around towards the back of the vehicle. He opens the back door and pulls out a large, silver casket with Ice's assistance. The two pull the casket completely out of the hearst, and set the casket down on one of its ends, leaning it up against the back of the hearst. Ice stares at the metal structure, while gently caressing it. 'Almasy, you come from distant lands. You have battled them all, and proclaim yourself to be the greatest warrior this world knows. I'd like for you to however, bare with me just a moment, and look at this fine piece of work next to me.' Ice turns back from the casket and grabs the camera's lens. 'No, not Brandi. The casket.' Brandi giggles at his flirtatious comments. Ice briefly glaces over at the beautiful red head, before turning his attention back to the silver casket. 'Now look deeply at it. Drink in its look, let it's meaning engulf your mind. Look past the barriers of your mind, and see what it is I am showing you. Look at the truth that is of MY WORLD, and see that you cannot escape it.' The camera pans from Ice over to the casket and begins to zoom in. As the camera stares at the casket, a feint outline of a figure can be made out. The camera zooms in closer, to try and see the outline better. As the camera zooms in the feint picture becomes more and more clear. The figure of a man is lightly outlined on the top of the casket. From the man's shoulder hangs a belt of some kind, in his hand is a sword. The camera continues to zoom in on the belt, and the sword, as there are smaller letters on them. On the belt, the world ALMASY can finally be made out. The camera then pans to the sword, to see a haunting messege. 2 - 2 - 2004 R.I.P. The camera suddenly pans to Ice who has an intense look etched on his face. He stares deeply into the camera, not saying a word. Brandi cautiously walks over and meekly approaches Ice. 'Wh..What....' Ice places his right index finger on Brandi's lips and silences her words. A sadistic smile appears on Ice's face. 'Seymour, you have entered a battle you cannot win. Because you see, this is not the distant lands you are so accustom to. There is no hiding yourself from the truth. This is MY WORLD, and in MY WORLD, there is but only one truth, and no one escapes the truth. Global Reload......THE TRUTH WILL SET YOU FREE!' Ice opens the casket lid. As the lid flies open a bloody figure, which looks exactly like the outline on the casket lid appears. The blood covering this figure is splattered all over the inside of the casket, writing out a messege. <red>WELCOME TO MY WORLD</red>
