Pulled Over

5 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/5/03 ******NOTE!!!!****** To catch what happened prior to this, read my previous RP. This takes place immediately after that. ******NOTE!!!!****** Red and blue flashes in the rearview mirror as Johnie snaps back into normality. He swaggers his head around to check out his side to see a black Crown Victoria speeding to catch up with him. “Son of a bitch!” Johnie shouts as he takes a tosses the bottle of beer from his right hand to the passenger side. “Like this night could get any worse.” He sighs loudly rolling his eyes as turns the wheel to the right pulling over to the side of the road. He calmly slows the Ram to a stop on the shoulder gravel and puts it in park, slamming himself back into his seat. “What else could happen?” Johnie mutters. The police car pulls over about 10 feet behind him and the cab light flips on as an outline of a cowboy hat leans down toward the lower dashboard of his vehicle. Johnie looks back at him in his mirror and scoffs. “Now what is this dope doing?” Tapping impatiently on the wheel, he stares out at the nearby tree line. “This guy’s taking his sweet ass time, that’s for sure. Jesus. I didn’t even do anything.” A beam of light flashes between his truck and the car behind him as the figure holding the light comes towards him. The officer shines his light into the bed and rear cab before stepping to the window. Johnie turns his head to him as he knocks on the window. Slowly, Johnie moves his hand to the window button and rolls it downward. “Something wrong?” Johnie sneers at the officer. The officer tilts his head back a bit at that shot, frowning his lower jaw. “License and registration.” Standard question for a speeding driver, but this didn’t sit well. “Doesn’t a please usually go with that?” Johnie teases, but just gets a glare from the officer. “License and registration.” Johnie leans over reaching into the back pocket of his jeans and obliges with the information. The officer reaches out and takes the plastic and papers, turning back towards his vehicle. “I’ll be right back.” “Great...” Johnie leans back in his seat and stares up at the ceiling, breathing deeply. He scrolls his eyes back and forth when he snaps into motion towards the back cab trying to nudge the empty beer bottles underneath his seat as quickly as possible. But before he can get all of them, he sees the cablight flash back on from the cop car and he snaps back forward with his rolling over the bottom of the wheel and looking blankly forward again. His footsteps clap on the tar as he approaches the truck again looking down at the information. He turns his head back up towards Johnie raising the paperwork to him as he scratches through his cropped blonde hair. “Do you know how fast you were going, son?” Son?! Oh, great. “I don’t know,” Johnie answers back as he tilts his head over putting the papers away in the armrest and looks down towards the passenger side at the beer bottle barely visible under the seat. “I think the coke I snorted a few hours ago affected my vision. Maybe you can tell me.” Johnie laughs at himself turning back towards the officer, but unfortunately he didn’t find it as funny. “Excuse me?” The officer growls as he steps closer to the truck and flashes light into Johnie’s eyes. Johnie only winces his eyes as the officer grills again. “You were saying something, boy?” Again with the kid cracks. “I said that I don’t know.” he answers with some force behind it. “Something wrong with that...officer?” Johnie lifts his hand over his eyes trying to block the light. “And will you get that damn thing outta my face.” So far, not good. The light stays on Johnie and the young face of the officer scowls hard at him. “Step out of the vehicle,” he barks at Johnie with a speck of southern drawl to it. “What” What did I do?” Johnie answers innocently with a small smile. Sarcasm never hurts, I guess. “I want you to step out of the vehicle,” he says with a little more stern tone this time. “I want to know what I did first. There’s no reason why I should get out unless there was something I did. It would be nice to know.” “Out of the vehicle now, son!” he hollers this time with his eyes burning with anticipation. I guess it doesn’t take much to set him off. Johnie opens the door and starts to slowly climb out. “I’ll ask you...not to call me “son.”” As he climbs out, he looks the officer up and down at his slightly taller frame. His arms are a light build with a little bit of definition. He has a little bit of age to him, but not enough to tell his how old. He turns and steps towards the cop angrily. He looks intently at his strong blue eyes. “Do you get me, bright eyes?” The officer doesn’t budge, but steps to Johnie smugly. “Step back toward the vehicle...son.” Johnie tries to stare back at him, but he can’t see beyond the glare of the strong flashlight. Instead he just turns and steps back near the bed of the truck. As he heads over there, the cop steps toward the open door and turns his light from Johnie to the cab, shining it around. “If you’re looking for the dead body, you’re too late. I already buried it.” Johnie leans back against the back bed of his truck, turning his attention to the gleam in the rear window. He breathes deeply waiting for this inspection to be over with. Glancing over the floor, the cop shines his light over the front cab. Candy wrappers cover parts of the middle seats and cigarette butts are scattered over the mud caked floor. He scrolls his hands over the seats and between them to try, searching for the usuals. Paraphernalia, drugs, anything that might be hidden for a reason. Johnie twitches his cheeks up a bit and clasps his knuckles. The joints crackle through the night air. His eyes roll once more and clenches his jaw as he glares towards the bent rear of the officer. He repeats the cracking of his other knuckle and stomps over towards the cab slowly. “Are you through with this bullshit yet?” Pulling himself out of the truck, he turns towards Johnie and nudges him backwards with his free hand. “I said back, boy!” He jerks the flashlight up and points it at Johnie’s forehead. “Don’t make me whoop your ass, here. I’m checkin” you out.” “What the hell for?” Johnie fires back. “I didn’t do shit.” “You were 15 miles over the speed limit and you were swerving a bit a few miles back.” “Is it against the law to do that” When you’re driving as long as I have been, you get a little tired. Of course you’re gonna swerve a bit. Jesus!” “Bullshit! I don’t believe that, boy!” Johnie clenches his fists hanging on his sides and looks fiercely at the officer. “Enough with the kid shit! Either you book me, give me a ticket, or be on your merry fucking way. Take your pick.” Assertively, the cop takes a step forward and looks down to Johnie. His mouth clenched trying to hold back his fiery aggression to keep his composure. So far its not working well. “I will address you how I want, kiddo. And if I feel like tearing your pickup apart, I will. I am the law around here. And the Law runs the show. You get me?” Johnie stands his ground and scoffs at the threat. “Whatever, big shot. Are we done here, or do you want to lecture me some more about how THE LAW does things around here. Other than chase around people like me.” As his cheeks turn red, the cop clenches his face and steps extends his arm with a slip of paper. Speeding ticket, obviously. “Take it and get the hell out of my sight!” Johnie bends a slight grin as he accepts the ticket. “With pleasure, redneck.” He brushes by the cop as he walks toward his truck and steps up in the cab. “I just hope I don’t swerve too much on the way out.” The officer starts to stomp away until he hears a tinkling of a glass container hitting the ground. He snaps his head around and sees an empty beer bottle rolling beneath the truck. Instantly he smirks and turns back. “Out of the truck, tough guy.” Johnie clenches his eyes shut as he lowers his right leg back down on the ground and swears under his breath. The cop bends down and gradually picks up the bottle. Lifting it up and smiling at it, he looks back at Johnie. “Well, well, well. What do we have here?” Johnie turns around and shrugs his shoulders down as he glances at the bottle. “It looks like a bottle to me,” he remarks. “Is that something you don’t see much around here” Or are you not still drinking jugs of moonshine?” The officer’s smile bends a bit, but he still remarks with a high tone. “No, but do you know its illegal to have an open alcohol container in this state?” “No. I don’t live here, remember?” “That’s a fine, you know.” “It should be a fine for you to have that haircut,” Johnie counters. “Well, I’m gonna have to ask you, have you been drinking tonight?” Johnie bites down and clenches his teeth. “No, I haven’t.” “Would you object to a breathalyzer test then?” he smugs. “Yes, I would in fact. Its an invasion of privacy and you have no right to do so.” “Are you declining this test?” “Yes, I am.” “Fine with me.” The officer puts down the bottle with his right hand and with his left he whips out a set of handcuffs. He promptly spins Johnie around and pins him against the truck twists his right arm backwards. “You are under arrest for Driving Under the Influence.” As the cop pulls Johnie’s left wrist back into the cuffs, Johnie tries to struggle out but its too late. He’s caught. “WHAT”! No fucking way!” “You have the right to remain silent.” The officer continues with the recitation of his rights as he pushes Johnie back and walks him towards the back of his police car. He shifts hands to open the backdoor and shoves Johnie into the seat. Johnie grimaces hard as the door is slammed in his face. “Another dirt bag off the roads.” The cop laughs and turns to the front door and climbs in. TO BE CONTINUED...
