The Gray Area:<br>When the Fine Line Blurs

26 July 2004

Date: 07.26.2004 “There’s a fine line between genius and insanity.” -Oscar Levant The master bedroom filled with darkness although but even without the needed light, one could tell that something was amiss. Darkness consumes every last inch of the room but it does not end there; it is as if, the darkness has consumed everything inside as well. Even the whites of Maverick’s eyes are smothered by the shadows. This unnatural darkness creates silence as well, aside from the breathing coming from a sleeping Christina McCoy. It has to be atleast four in the morning as there’s not a conscious soul throughout the home. Thoughts race through Maverick’s mind, his eyes closing to distance himself from the perpetual darkness. The door creeks open and Maverick stirs, his hand slipping beneath his own pillow. “Maverick.” The voice? Maverick’s eyes clench and he buries his faces, ignoring the call. “Maverick.” The voice. Maverick bolts up right in his bed, jerking his gun from underneath his pillow and aiming it at the far wall. With a flick of his wrist, a small light comes on to illuminate the other wise sinister room. There is no one there and Maverick takes a breath, shaking his head. “I really am losing it?” Maverick mutters as he places the gun down on his night stand. His eyes move to focus on the beautiful Christina McCoy as she sleeps, prompting a soft smile upon his lips. “Maverick.” Not the voice? Maverick grabs for his gun but it’s no longer there. Scott Pierce stands before him, holding the gun and aiming it directly at Maverick. “What’s going on, Scott?” Maverick asks, his eyes growing wide. “It’s time for you to be stopped, Maverick.” Scott says clearly, his voice dry and monotone. It’s as if Scott is mechanical, without an emotion in his entire body. “It’s time for retribution.” The gun fires? Maverick bolts up in his bed, sweating and breathing heavily. With a quick flip of his wrist, he turns the light that was on a moment ago on and looks about the dimly lit room. The moon’s light shines through the French doors, leaving the room with a blue tint. Christina rolls to her other side as her reaction to Maverick’s sudden movements. Maverick reaches under his pillow and removes the gun, his face still unsure of what’s going on. Quickly, he glances at the sleeping Christina and then around the room again. “A nightmare?” Maverick grumbles before flipping the light off. Readjusting the blankets, Maverick lays back down and closes his eyes to enjoy the rest of the night’s slumber. ? Hours pass? Christina gently nudges Maverick until he comes to, his eyes slowly focusing on her beautiful visage. Christina smiles and plants a soft kiss upon his lips before whispering in a voice not her own. “You know what you have to do?” The voice. Maverick bolts up again, the room still dead silent and barely any more illuminated than the last time Maverick was woken from his night’s rest. His eyes quickly move to Christina who has once again turned in her sleep, now lying on her side facing Maverick, a cute smile gracing her lips, signifying pleasant dreams. Some people just get lucky that way? Maverick shakes his head, his eyes moving over the room before lying back down. 4:15 The clock reads and Maverick closes his eyes again, allowing Morpheous to take him. 4:20 Maverick sits up again, his eyes scanning the room anxiously. There had been no nightmare or dream of any kind. He sighs and glances down at his hands. Today is the thirtieth of July. In twelve hours, he will be on his way to the Air Canada Centre? but right now, Maverick is facing demons that even he knows do not exist. Maverick lies back and glances at the clock again before furrowing his brow. 3:30 Maverick sits up again and lifts the clock, now beginning to doubt his own eyes. He glances around again and then opens his nightstand drawer and pulls out a watch. 3:31. Maverick puts the watch down on the night stand and glances around before lowering his head to the pillow and closing his eyes. Time is relative when you’re in control but when you’re the puppet, time is just another mind game. The room is dark again, darker than it was at 3:30 or 4:20. Maverick’s eyes are wide open, staring up at the ceiling to his bedroom. ---- Tonight is a big night for me. Victory is within my grasp, all I have to do is take what is rightfully mine? take what I have earned and deserved. Tonight, I’m going to sweep the entire company. Tonight, I take the Master of the Mat Tournament and the World Championship in one fowl swoop. Tonight is the culmination of all that I have worked for, the coup de gra. Tonight, everything comes together. ---- A flickering light comes from outside, catching Reno’s attention. Quickly he sits up and grabs his gun, his eyes passing over the alarm clock. He stands and then stops, his eyes reverting the clock. 3:10. Maverick again lowers his eyes but is quickly returned to topic when the light flashes again. Slowly, Maverick walks towards the French doors and pushes them open, the light continuing to flicker more and more. Maverick steps out onto the balcony, his eyes attempting to focus enough to see what might be causing the interruption. Maverick’s eyes narrow as he gazes out into his courtyard, watching as Scott Pierce and the hooded man stand together, a flash light in Scott’s hand. “He’s conspiring against you, Maverick.” The voice. Maverick shakes his head and clenches his eyes shut, muttering over and over again, “I don’t believe you, I don’t believe you?” Finally, Maverick opens his eyes and gazes out again. The two men have disappeared and the flickering light has vanished. Maverick takes a sigh of relief and glances down at his bare feet. “Maverick.” The voice. Reno narrows his eyes and pulls the gun up to his chest, spins and aims it in the direction of the voice. Standing before him, with a shocked expression, is Christina McCoy. “What are you doing!”” she questions in her own voice, her hand finding her chest. Maverick lowers the gun and takes a deep breath of his own before stepping forward and taking Christina into his arms. “I’m sorry, Christina” I’ve been unable to sleep.” Maverick closes his eyes, the gun hanging at his side. “You know what you have to do.” The voice. Maverick looks up and into Christina’s eyes. Christina looks back blankly, confused about the expression on Maverick’s face. “Did you just say something?” Maverick asks calmly, attempting not to show the anxiety that ravages his mind. Christina shakes her head no and replies, once again in her own voice, “No, are you okay, Maverick?” Maverick nods and motions to the bed, “Let’s just get a little sleep?” Maverick leads Christina to the bed and watches as she crawls in on her hands and knees, grinning back at him as she teases a little. Maverick offers a half-smile and places his gun atop the nightstand before slipping into bed and beneath the blankets. Quickly, and almost without thinking, Maverick glances up at the clock and bolts up right again. “2:56” Maverick mutters, unsure of what he sees. Christina looks up at him. “It is almost time.” The voice, coming from the lips of the beautiful Christina. Maverick sits up in bed, breathing heavily again. Christina lay asleep at his side and he drops his head back, heavily annoyed at his own imagination. He clenches his eyes and faces the clock without looking at the time. He forces his eyes to open and moves his hand to the table, moving a roll of duct tape to the side before reading the clock and taking a sigh of relief, “Thank God?” Maverick mutters, reading “4:54” on the face. Maverick closes his eyes again and takes a deep, refreshing breath. ---- Scott would not betray me. To think so would be paranoia? although, why was he standing there? Why was he meeting with ? with the hooded man and why so late? Why with a clandestine shroud? I must not question Scott’s loyalty. However, his query worries me. Why would he ask such a question? If he has figured it out, has anyone else? Will anyone else? And if not, will guilt lead him down a judicial path? A path that finds me locked away, a path that condemns his avenger!” ? no, Scott stands by me. Scott would not betray me. Scott would not betray me. ---- Maverick’s eyes flutter open. “Scott?” he mutters, his vision blurry from sleep. “Is that you?” Maverick attempts to keep his voice low, knowing that Christina lay asleep at his side. He sits up on his elbows, gazing up at his friend. Scott Pierce stands at the foot of the bed, staring directly at Maverick. “Maverick.” Scott’s voice. Maverick’s brow furrows and he reaches for the gun on his nightstand. His eyes quickly shoot to the side to see that it’s no longer where he placed. Christina grabs Maverick by the arm and smiles up at him, her eyes narrowed and menacing. “Maverick.” Christina’s voice. Maverick jerks away from Christina and stands from the bed, looking from one to the other. His eyes jump to the clock and reads the time aloud, “1:59” Maverick stumbles back against the wall, his eyes moving from Christina to Scott. “Maverick.” The voice. They both speak yet there is only one voice. There is only one voice? Maverick shoots up in his bed, his eyes scanning the room in a panic. He jerks his head to the right, his eyes focusing on the digital clock, “5:27” “I suggest you remain very quiet, Mr. Reno.” a man in a black suit presses the barrel of his gun against Maverick’s forehead. Reno’s eyes shoot up to the man’s face before duct tape is pressed around his mouth and he’s forced down onto the bed. He looks to his side to see Christina laying in a fetal position, her hands and mouth taped, a gun held at her head. July 31st, 2004 ? Approximately 5:32 AM London, Ontario, Canada ? Reno Estate The man speaks in the voice of his nightmares, prompting Reno’s eyes to widen. “You’ve crossed that line too many times, Maverick. Now, it’s too late to go back” for you, the line has finally blurred.” The man slides the track of the gun back, pulling a single bullet into the chamber. “Now say good night.” The man forces the gun between Maverick’s lips and releases the safety. The clip falls from the handle of the gun and onto the bed, leading the man’s attention away from Maverick. Reno’s hand comes into view, a Glock 32 suddenly in his grip. He slams the gun against the side of the man’s head, sending him tumbling to the ground. Reno lifts the gun and holds it on the far man, prompting him to release the gun. Without removing the tape over his mouth, Maverick speaks carefully, “Don’t move, remain silent, and you’ll make it out of here, understand?” Maverick reaches down and removes the tape from Christina’s mouth. She opens her eyes suddenly, looking at Maverick in fear as he leans over her, holding his gun on the man behind her. “When I say so, hit the floor and cover your head” and don’t come out until I come back and get you.” Christina’s eyes grow wide in terror as Maverick fires a single shot, forever silencing the man he was just speaking to. Christina takes this as his command and drops down to the side of the bed, screaming in terror. Maverick stands on the bed and jumps off and into the center of the room. “What’s going on!”” Christina screams out, afraid to look up. Maverick slams his shoulder into the bedroom door, bursting through it and into the hallway. Several shots are fired in the hallway before the sound of running footsteps is all that remains. Maverick rushes down the hallway, gun in hand. “SHOW YOURSELVES!” Maverick screams before rounding a corner and ducking, again several shots are heard and granite flies from the wall above his head. Reno cringes and stands to open fire, unloading an entire clip across the length of his living room. Smoke clouds the air before Reno, making it difficult to see. Footsteps? and a scuffle. We see Maverick Reno stumble back into vision before lifting his gun and firing into the smoke. ---- Sometimes, seeing the world through another man’s eyes is like seeing a completely different world. Other times, it’s like taking a glimpse at Hell. ---- Maverick lowers himself behind the couch and lifts a clip from his pocket, as if he’d been ready for this to happen. Maverick closes his eyes and aims up and over the couch, firing against the far wall. One of the bullets strikes an aquarium, sending water spewing forth all over the living room floor. Maverick stands and leaps over the sofa, rolling to the floor and aiming again into a far hallway. ---- And sometimes, you see the world as you wish to see it. ---- Maverick glances up at a clock located on an end table and narrows his brow at the time. “3:27” Maverick thinks little of it and stands, rushing the hallway. Again, we see only Maverick as he slips his gun beyond our vision and a shot is heard, prompting Reno to bounce back and take cover behind the wall. “1:53” the clock now reads, Maverick no longer noticing it. Again he spins around the corner and takes aim, firing out of our view. Shot after shot rings forth until his clip falls from the handle and hits the floor without a sound. Another clip from nowhere and the gun is ready to go again. “2:82” the clock now reads. Maverick rushes down the hallway, drapes blowing heavily as every window has been opened, snow pouring in from outside. Maverick rushes and then barrels into a far door, smashing it open and revealing his own office. Without a second glimpse, he lifts his gun and fires into the room, several rounds before spinning on his heels and moving to another door. As we pass the room, we see a large clock positioned on the table that reads, “0:AG” Maverick puts his boot into the door and steps in, now dressed in a pair of black jeans and a t-shirt, his long black leather trench coat flowing in about his calves. “27:00” a clock on the wall reads. In the distance of the home, a grandfather clock chimes a baritone chime, demanding attention. Maverick walks slowly through the room, his long black hair tied back in a pony tail. “42:NO” a clock positioned near the wall reads and a small bird shoots from the center, calling out to everyone in ear shot, “Cookoo, cookoo.” Maverick turns and blasts his twin Desert Eagles with all of their might, blowing the clock into pieces. “Cookoo” the clock continues to call out, even though it’s no longer in one piece. Maverick rushes from the room “RE:NO” A clock reads. Blue fog bellows from a doorway, prompting Maverick’s attention. He lifts his glocks 20’s and reloads their clips, ready for what may be waiting behind the door. “Bastard” you can’t come into my home? you can’t threaten my life? my safety.” Maverick rushes the door and kicks it in, his eyes searching the lamp-lit room for anything, anyone. Standing before him is a man in a robe, blue fog coming from every direction. A clock behind him reads, “Do:nt.” Maverick lifts his guns as the man picks a black rose off of the nightstand and appears to gaze down at it. “It is time.” The voice from behind Maverick. “What’s this here for, Mav?” What’s going on?” The voice of Scott Pierce comes from the robed man. Maverick narrows his eyes and pulls both triggers, unleashing the fiery fury of his mechanical sickles. ---- ---- The grounds of the Reno Estate are a luscious green, the summer having been gracious this year. Night blankets the sky however and the moon’s light provides little to no light. Footsteps echo through the night as we watch Aidan DeSarret rush across the grounds, a gun in his hand. He reaches the luxurious guest how that is currently inhabited by both John Zero and Deacon Holmes. The time is 5:40 in the morning, July 31st. Master of the Mat V has come and gone and night has once again engrossed the world. Aidan breathes heavily, gunfire heard behind him in the main house. Maverick bangs on the door loudly and begins to call out, “JOHN! DEACON!” Inside, the elegant house is divided with two bedrooms and John is the first and only to wake from the noise. He pulls a robe on and trudges through the small house and to the front door. He pulls the door open and delivers a dead stare to TUE’s head security officer, Aidan DeSarret. “Aidan, but there had best be a damned good reason for?” Another shot rings out and John looks up at the main house. “What the fuck is going on!”” John rushes back into the house and grabs a holster strap and his six Glocks. He rushes the door and attempts to head for the house but Aidan grabs him and pushes him against the outside wall of the house. Aidan looks frightened and utterly struck with fear, his eyes wide and his face pale. “John” something’s wrong with Maverick.” “No shit and you’re helping! I have to go help him!” John calls out as he shoves Aidan back and draws two of his glocks. Aidan calls out, not sure of what to say, “You don’t understand” he’s?” Aidan stops and roughly runs his fingers through his hair, unsure of what to say. “SPIT IT OUT!” John demands, unsure of if he should rush the house or Aidan. Finally, he turns and starts running towards the house and reaches the back deck. He grabs the handle to the sliding glass door and jerks to the side and then simply slams his shoulder through the glass, forcing his way into the home. Aidan stands there, unsure of what to say or do when Deacon wanders to the opened door of the guest house in a pair of boxers and a Care Bears t-shirt. “White people?” he mutters before turning and walking back into the house. The gun fire stops and all is silent. Aidan looks up and begins his sprint back to the main house, clothing moist from perspiration. He steps through the glass door that John just destroyed and pulls his gun, looking through the house carefully. Aidan rounds a corner and stops, seeing Maverick on the floor next to Scott Pierce’s body. Maverick has his back to the wall, his hands limp at his sides and his head pressed back against the wall. He’s dressed in a robe and a pair of jogging pants, a single Glock 32 lays several feet away. John Zero stands nearby, his eyes staring a whole through Maverick unsure of what to make of the situation. He glances back at Aidan and quickly asks, “What the hell happened here!”” “That’s what I was trying to tell you” John, when I heard the gunfire, I flipped through every camera in this house.” Aidan begins as he picks up Maverick’s gun. Christina McCoy wanders nearby, now partially hiding herself behind a corner. “He was shooting” and he was moving around to get away from the people he was shooting at?” “And this is strange?” John asks, demanding that Aidan get to the point. “Would you prefer he get shot?” “That’s just it, John” there was no one else!” Aidan explains, prompting a ridiculing expression upon John’s face. Maverick is simply staring up at the ceiling, unresponsive and dazed completely out. “John, Maverick” needs help. He was shooting and chasing these people but ? John, they weren’t here. He’s the only one saw them?” Aidan glances down and John slowly looks towards Maverick who has yet to respond or say anything in his defense. He doesn’t even appear to be conscious. Scott Pierce lay in the middle of his bedroom floor, half dressed, with a black rose in his right hand? “Scott’s the only person he shot at that we could actually see on the cameras. I thought that maybe someone had scrambled our security but I ran an exhaustive check of the perimeter, ran motion sensor checks, and even a body heat analysis” John, I swear to you? there wasn’t another person in this house aside from those of us who were supposed to be here.” Aidan continues to explain, hoping for some form of understanding to come from the facts. John kneels down in front of Maverick and holsters both of his weapons. He leans in closely and speaks to his friend. “Maverick” Maverick? are you okay?” Maverick lifts his head slowly, staring directly into John’s eyes. Maverick does not speak and his movements are at the slowest speed possible. He blinks and glances at Scott’s body, a slow smile coming over Maverick’s lips. John glances at the slain Scott Pierce, the black rose in his hand wondering just how this all happened. “We’re safe now, John?” ---- When the line between genius and insanity blurs, there is no black or white.
