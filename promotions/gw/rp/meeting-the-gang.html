Meeting the Gang.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The scene opens up to the doorway of the warehouse where Max and Tyler are standing. They start looking around the huge warehouse and start walking forward. Max and Tyler look to the left and see a long hallway that has about 50 rooms down it. They look to the right, and see once big door with a nameplate on it that reads, “The Gang.” “They must be in here.” Says Tyler. “Let’s not go in this side. Let’s sneak around the other side, there has to be a back entrance into the room.” Max and Tyler start walking forward and see some rats run down the long hallway. There are cobwebs all over the walls and some of the walls having cracks in them. There are some pipes sticking out of the walls, and also some nails. Max and Tyler walks up some stairs and then walk to the right again because the left is blocked off. There is a huge window that Max and Tyler look into. They see about 50 people all standing in one area talking to each other. Max looks past the gang and sees a table full of pipes, guns, and knifes. Max and Tyler turn around and see a guy in a black suit walking towards them. His shirt says Security, and Max and Tyler run and hide behind a big crate. The security guard passes them and walks down a flight of stairs leading into the huge room with the gang in it. “It’s time to bust there party.” Says Max. Suddenly from a different room another security guard starts walking past Max and Tyler. It passes them but Max jumps out and kicks the guard in the back of the head. The security guard turns around but Max and Tyler both punch him at the same time. The guard slips over something that was on the ground and falls through the window the ground below. The guard hits the concrete with a thud as other guards start climbing the stairs to where Max and Tyler are. About 10 guards come up the stairs as Max and Tyler charge at all of them. Max kicks two of the guards over the edge and both fall to the other one on the ground. Max sees Tyler charging up for the Twister, so Max hides behind another big crate. Tyler jumps into the air and starts spinning really fast sending all of the guards over the edge of the broken window. All hit the ground as Max and Tyler start running down the stairs towards the gang. “So we have company.” Says one of the gang members. The gang members start circling Max and Tyler. They smile at each other charging at the gang members. Max jumps into the air and stays there. Tyler jumps into the air again taking out most of the gang members with the Twister! Max goes back to the ground and uses his feet to catapult Tyler at the remaining members. Now, only 5 members are standing face to face with Max and Tyler. “I think the one in the middle is the main leader of this gang.” Whispers Max to Tyler. 4 of the members start running at Max and Tyler. They all miss them and get kicked in the face by Max. Max throws one of the guys another and says something to Tyler. Tyler smiles and then backs up from the 3 guards after him. Suddenly Tyler opens his mouth and says something really weird out loud at the guards. “Power of Water, Fire, and Earth. Bring the wind back to it’s birth!” Suddenly a wind comes out of now where and makes the 3 guards get thrown into the brick walls. The wind takes the guards off the ground and disappears through the brick wall. “How did you do that!” yells the last member of the gang. “Magic.” Yells Max. Max gets off the ground and him and Tyler start chasing the leader all over the room. The leader runs by the wooden table and grabs some kind of gun off it and starts firing at Max and Tyler. All the shots miss them and Max finally catches the leader by the shirt. “Remember me?” asks Tyler. “No, I don’t actually.” “Well, to bad then.” Max throws the leader at the brick wall as hard as he can. The leader smacks the brick and falls to the ground bleeding from the head. Suddenly an explosion is heard and a part of the brick wall collapses. Suddenly 5 men, that look like security guards grabs the leader and take him onto a helicopter. It flies away as Max and Tyler just stand there. “We have to go catch them.” Says Tyler. “Just wait. Let’s rest. They can’t go far, because I put a tracking device of the leader of the gang.” Max pulls a little mini T.V. out of his pocket and him and Tyler look at it. They can see the helicopter flying towards the Sears Tower. “Awesome. Now we can just follow them with this.” Max and Tyler get off the ground and jumps through the hole in the brick wall. Tyler jumps into the air and starts spinning towards the helicopter and Max jumps into the air and starts flying towards the helicopter. They catch up to it no problem and both of them grab onto a little handle on the bottom of the helicopter. The helicopter starts to land at the top of the Sears Tower as Max and Tyler let go and land on the side of the tower. They both hold onto a ledge and wait till the helicopter is fully landed. It lands as the leader and 10 other people get off and walk down the little stair case that was inside the broom closet. “Well climb up in 5 seconds.” 5? 4? 3? 2? 1? Max and Tyler start climbing up the side of the Sears Tower with all there strength. Max gets on the top first and he helps Tyler get up. “We need to destroy the helicopter so they can’t leave anywhere.” Says Max. “Okay.” Max and Tyler walk over to the helicopter and start pushing it. It gets closer to the edge. Max then kicks it as hard as he can and it flies off the side of the Sears Tower. All the people below start screaming as Max and Tyler walk down the stairs in the broom closet. Max and Tyler start running down the stairs and they finally catch up to the gang. They get off at the same floor the gang does. Max and Tyler start walking like normal people getting closer to the gang. “Let’s just attack them from behind. And get it over with.” Says Tyler. “Wait. Take someone’s shoe and throw it at them.” Says Max. Tyler walk over and grabs a little kids shoe and then runs back to Max. Tyler throws the shoe straight ahead and smacks the leader in the back of the head. Max and Tyler hide behind a lady and her husband and daughter. The leader looks at the daughter who has a smile on here face. The leader gives the little kid the finger and it starts crying. Max and Tyler pass the lady as the gang stops at a door. They knock but just open the door and walk inside. “We have to go in there.” Max opens the door a little bit and sees the gang sitting on chairs with papers on the table in front of them. Tyler opens the door all the way and him and Max walk inside. “Not you again.” Says the leader. “Yes, it’s us again.” “Boys, kick these guys asses.” A whole bunch of security guards rush at Max and Tyler. Max punches one in the face and then throws him into the wall. Tyler kicks two of the guys In the face and another in the balls. The leader gets off the chair and runs at Tyler punching him in the face. Max turns the leader around and throws him straight through a wooden table in the far corner. Max picks Tyler up and the both hit a double clothesline onto one guy leaving him inside out. Max walks over to the leader and starts stomping him as Tyler jumps into the air hitting the Twister! Papers fly everywhere and another table flies and hits the wall breaking it in half. “This is kind of fun.” Says Tyler. Suddenly from behind Max and Tyler a guard gets up and picks up and axe that was lying in the corner by some papers. He turns Max and Tyler around and swings at them but they duck and the guard hits another guard cutting his stomach open. Max kicks the guards into the wall. Tyler and Max look around and all they see is blood and a whole bunch of body’s. “Well, I think are work here is finished.” Says Max. SCENE FADES
