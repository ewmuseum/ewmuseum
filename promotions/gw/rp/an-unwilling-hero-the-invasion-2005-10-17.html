An Unwilling Hero: The Invasion

17 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 17, 2005 The atmosphere of the house occupied by the South Side Locs was one of embarrassment and fear. Their embarrassment was due to the fact that they had completely bungled Stomper’s kidnapping scheme. The fear, an emotion felt only by Lazy and Spanky, came from the unexpected murder of Creeper at the hands of their leader, Stomper. Stomper had been angry and heavily influenced by drugs the whole week, making his amigos increasingly nervous. As the days wore on Stomper had become more violent. They had watched Evolution and during Biddle’s promo Stomper shot the television and then in a moment of extreme rage threw the device out the back window. Stomper claimed that Biddle had mocked him during the segment. After the outburst Stomper had disappeared into his room and refused to speak with anyone for two days. Finally, at the end of the second day he emerged from his room. He was as haggard and wild-eyed as the average conspiracy theorist; stubble protruded from his face and dark circles had cemented their legacy underneath his brown eyes. He had found the surviving members of his gang seated on the couch, they shivered with fear when he entered the room but he did not notice. The murderous gang leader looked at the two of them the way a battle weary General would before leading his men into the final battle. “I’m going to kill that fucking gringo,” Stomper said through clenched teeth. The threat was nothing new to the gang members; Stomper had been repeating those seven words ever since the night of Biddle’s escape. Lazy and Spanky were silent and careful not to make any sudden movements as Stomper began to pace back and forth in front of them. “We’re going to his casa tonight,” Stomper ordered, “and after I kill him we’ll steal all his shit.” Stomper then glared at them for approval of the new plan. “S-sounds good to me,” Lazy said as he nodded his head and nudged Spanky until he did the same. “That’s what I thought,” Stomper said. “Get your shit together cuz we’re leaving in an hour.” ***** The Back Alley Brawler was bored but did his best to hide that fact from his current employer, Mike Biddle. The Brawler had no clue what was going on or why Biddle had insisted that he accompany him back to Beverly Hills. All he could do was wonder why all the blinds in the house had been pulled down and why Biddle kept peeking through them like a paranoid dictator looking for snipers. However, the Brawler had his theories and he bet it had something to do with the annoying fat kid that lay on the couch like a beached whale. During the car ride from the airport Biddle made sure that the fat kid had kept his head down, this perplexed the Brawler since the windows of the limousine were tinted. He didn’t question Biddle’s strangeness though; the money Biddle paid was enough to keep the Brawler’s mouth shut no matter how weird Biddle acted. When they had reached the mansion Biddle whisked the fat kid inside and ordered the Brawler to do a walk around of the grounds for security reasons. Once he was done with the menial task Biddle then instructed the Brawler to stay close-by just in case, and that’s just what the Brawler has done for the past three hours as he silently wondered to himself “in case of what?”. ***** The banged up Impala eased to a stop by a curb that was a few blocks away from Biddle’s home. The three men that emerged from the vehicle were dressed in gray overalls that made them appear to be gardeners. The overalls had been Stomper’s idea, residents of Beverly Hills were naturally suspicious of minorities but they would go unnoticed if they appeared to be workers of some sort. Stomper’s amazingly simple plan worked and the trio was in Biddle’s backyard with no questions asked. “How are we going to get in?” Lazy whispered. “We’ll climb the ivy up to that open window vato;” Stomper motioned towards the open window and the ivy that led up to it. The climb was easier than it looked and both Stomper and Lazy made it up with no trouble. However, as Spanky climbed through the window he slipped and fell to the floor with a loud thud that could be heard throughout the house. “You fucking idiot,” Stomper hissed. “Don’t trip man,” Lazy put a reassuring hand on Stomper’s shoulder, “we’re going to kill the gringo anyway so who gives a shit if he knows that we’re here?” That seemed to calm Stomper down as he motioned for them to follow him in his search for Biddle. ***** “Did you hear that?” Biddle asked as a paranoid fear rippled through his body. “It was probably just a bird boss,” the Brawler offered. “N-no,” Biddle said, “I think it’s them they’re here to kill me.” All of Mike’s bravado from earlier had disappeared for now he knew that these gang members were serious! Mike pushed Carlos off the couch and shooed him into a nearby closet and locked him inside. Biddle then franticly searched the living room for his trusted nine iron. The Brawler watched this with a disheartened look on his grizzled features. There was something depressing about watching a grown man give in to paranoid delusions. The Brawler was about to offer a few words of advice when suddenly the words, “Where the fuck are you gringo?” Echoed from downstairs and startled both Biddle and the Brawler. Biddle shot his bodyguard a look as if to say “see” I told you so?. “I’ll take care of this shit boss,” the Brawler said with clenched fists as he approached the stairway. Once the Brawler disappeared around the corner Biddle bravely leaned against the wall and hoped that his bodyguard could take care of everything himself. Sounds of fighting drifted down from the upstairs hallway and it sounded as if the Brawler had a rather large advantage over the intruders. Suddenly Biddle felt a cold object jab into his neck. He looked over and saw the leader of the gang standing next to him with a gun pointed in his general direction. An angry sneer had attached itself to the gang leader’s face, making him even scarier than he already was. “Did you really think you could get away with making a fool of me?” Stomper whispered as he reveled in the fear that emanated from Biddle’s body. Biddle didn’t move, at this point in time he felt that any sudden movements would cause Stomper to squeeze the trigger and prematurely end his life. Suddenly a sound that was a mixture of breaking bones and cries of agony echoed throughout the house. The sound caused Stomper to take a step back as he tried to figure out the source of it. Biddle took this split second as the time to jump into action. Acting on pure adrenaline Biddle swung his nine iron at Stomper’s hand and knocked the gun across the room. Stomper immediately held his hand in pain and didn’t notice that Biddle was winding up to swing at him again. This time Biddle connected with a shot to Stomper’s right knee that sent the gang leader to the floor as he yelped in pain. Biddle took this as an opportunity to gloat. Biddle kicked Stomper in the ribs and spit on him. “You picked the wrong millionaire to mess with,” Biddle calmly stated before he kicked Stomper in the face. “Way tah go boss!” the Brawler said as he entered the room and saw the scene before him. The Brawler dragged the unconscious bodies of the other two gang members behind him. Biddle smirked, “It was nothing really.” The Brawler added Stomper to his collection of gang members. “Don’t worry “bout deez guys boss,” he said, “I’ll make sure they don’t bothah ya no more.” “Just make sure no one sees you,” Biddle said as the Brawler dragged the gang members towards the garage. “Don’t worry boss.” Once his bodyguard had disappeared Biddle walked over to the closet he had thrown Carlos into and opened the door. “Is it okay now?” Carlos asked. Biddle nodded his head and the portly young man came out of his hiding place. “Put your shoes on,” Biddle told Carlos, “we’re going to go see your grandfather.” ***** As Mike looked across the desk at William he saw a mixture of happiness and sadness in his face. With a smile William addressed his grandson, who was seated next to Mike. “Carlos,” William said, “I think it’s time you went back to Boston to live with your mother. Taking recent events into consideration I no longer think it is safe for you to be here.” Carlos somberly nodded his head in agreement. He didn’t want to leave Los Angeles but he knew that it would be for the best if he did. “Go and tell my driver to take you back to Mike’s house so you can pack your things,” William instructed, “and then tell the driver to take you to the airport. Mike’s private plane will be waiting there for you to take you home.” Carlos got up to leave, before he walked out the door he looked over his shoulder at the people he was being forced to leave behind and then he left. “Well now that that’s out of the way things can back to normal,” Mike said with a smile. “Actually lad,” William said with a somber voice, “there is something else I need to talk to you about.” “What?” Mike asked, he hoped it wasn’t something bad or trivial. William took a deep breath, “I can no longer accompany to the ring or to Evolution for that matter.” “Why not?” “I’m getting older lad,” William said, “my health isn’t what it once was and my doctor thinks I can no longer physically handle the rigors of managing a professional wrestler. Don’t fret lad, I will continue to be your agent and advisor.” “But what about my match against Train” Who is going to help me win?” Mike asked, fear was present in his voice. William chuckled. “You have your bodyguard now lad,” he said, “he’ll be much more helpful in the octagon than I ever could.” Mike thought back to earlier when the Brawler had single handedly defeated the gang members and he realized that William was right. “Well,” Mike began, “I can’t say I’m not disappointed by this decision but I understand. I’ll be in touch.” The two men shook hands and then Mike left the office. Even though he had been unwilling to save Carlos he felt good about the deed and was happy that the end result was that Carlos would be out of his life, hopefully for good. -The End-
