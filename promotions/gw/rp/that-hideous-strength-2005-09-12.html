That Hideous Strength

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.12.05 09.02.05 | The Mercer Household | 9:18 PM 'Hey cuz, have you seen Markus?' asked the twenty year old World Champion. Every now and then for the past few weeks, Mindy found her boyfriend Markus leaving the house without a trace. Needless to say, along with that and the recent attacks made by the Hunting Grounds on her and her cousin Michael, she was beginning to worry. Unfortunately, Michael seemed to know just as little about Markus' whereabouts. Why would he know? 'Nah, thought he was sleeping already or somethin'.' With a pout, Mindy walked out into the kitchen to get a late night snack- preferably something sweet. 'What the hell could he be doing now?' she cried softly as she walked off into the kitchen, leaving Michael by himself. 'Like I fucking care about him anyway,' Michael muttered angrily, before he tried his best to keep his frustration restrained until he made it to his bedroom. He barely succeeded, just holding back the urge to throw his bedroom door off its hinges. He sat himself down over the edge of his bed, and sighed. 'What the hell is happening to me?' the voice in his mind had asked, and it was a question Michael knew the answer for all too well: anger. Anger that not a lifetime worth of Yoga could relieve. Michael gulped, and reached for the nearest picture on the nightstand. A picture of Michael by himself, before he had reconciled with his wife.He could remember the day that picture was taken like no other. 'I'd been training that day...' 01.30.04 | Hix Family Ranch | 7:02 AM Michael had arrived at the ranch two minutes late, his feet shuffling along the dirt as he approached the large area of land. Two bags were shuffled over each shoulder, and they all assisted in weighing the younger Michael down. He was so different then; his hair was long and slicked back, his body was much smaller than it was now, and most importantly... 'Heh, you could never shut me up back then, could you, Jake?' Michael knocked on the front door to the enormous home, and it wasn't long before those doors opened. On the other side to greet him was the man who would be training him: not Jake, but his brother, Pitt. 'About goddamn time you showed up.' Michael blinked. What a way to be welcomed, eh? '...I'm two minutes late,' Michael grumbled in defense. Pitt scoffed in return, wanting to hear none of it. 'I don't give a shit how late you think you were.. you want to be trained, you get your ass here on time.' Michael tried not to laugh. It was his choice to be trained, yes, but not by a man like Pitt. The guy was gigantic; as far as Michael remembered, the man had to have been over three hundred pounds of pure muscle. 'But back then, I could've sworn he was a fucking monster.' Of course Michael would think that; he wasn't nearly as big and tough as he was over a year ago. Michael was an underdog, almost like his cousin was in Global. Then, Michael was a shade over six feet tall, and didn't weigh much more than two hundred pounds. All the easier for the Hix brothers to break. 'And besides,' Pitt continued, 'you're an hour and two minutes late.' Michael Mercer stared in disbelief at Pitt, growling as he brushed by. 'The hell's the point to training me at such an hour?' Michael asked. 'Are you trying to keep this shit a secret?' Quickly, Michael was on the ground. His bags and a few objects that were in them had been strewn along the ground, as Pitt immediately dragged Michael to the floor. Michael shook at his arms to get himself free, but there was no use. Pitt was three hundred pounds of muscle, and they rarely gave leniancy to anything 'You're under my roof, you're under my rule. I say you come here at six, you're fucking here at six. If that's a problem, go back to New York. I'm sure they have all the gyms in the world up there to keep your skinny ass in shape.' Almost reluctantly, Pitt had allowed Michael to get back to his feet. 'Way to break my arm,' Michael groaned, holding his right shoulder in pain. His grimacing made Pitt crack a smile. 'That's only the beginning, little guy.' Michael's eyes narrowed, staring back at Pitt as a mix of emotions rushed through his veins. Mainly anger and annoyance, but a little worry as well. He certainly had a right to be worried. Grabbing his bags, Michael was shown the room where he would be staying. It wasn't so bad; the guest room was as big as Michael's old bedroom in his parents' apartment, sure, but at least the bed looked comfortable. 'You won't be finding much time in here,' Pitt said, 'but for what it's worth, you'll be sleeping here... when I say you can, anyway. Enough sightseeing, though... unpack all your shit and join everyone in the dining room for breakfast.' Michael watched as Pitt left the room, and only one thought had entered his mind; a thought so prominent that he mumbled it aloud unknowingly. 'What the hell did I just get myself into?' At breakfast, Michael was introduced to the rest of the Hix family; including Jake, a man who didn't seem all too friendly to begin with. In fact, from Michael's memory of him then, Jake hadn't spoke a word. He simply kept himself to his heavy breakfast, and the word 'heavy' was quite the understatement for the breakfast they had that morning. Stacks of french toast that practically reached the ceiling, enough bacon to have needed a dozen pigs to make, more pancakes that Michael had ever witnessed in an IHOP, and enough biscuits to end hunger in a third world country. Oh, and there was the large ham in the center of the table, also. 'Jesus H...' Michael whispered under his breath, meekly flopping a stack of five pancakes onto his plate, along with a couple strips of bacon. 'You guys always eat like this?' he asked, looking out at the group before him. He then remembered that just about all of them were a good hundred pounds heavier than he was, and he quickly brought his dark brown eyes to his plate of food and the glass of orange juice next to it. 'What's the matter?' Pitt questioned. 'Afraid to put some meat on your bones?' Michael chuckled. He was a big eater also, but he never ate that much... especially for breakfast. 'Well seriously... ham at eight A.M.?' Pitt sighed. 'Don't tell me you're a vegetarian, too..' 'Oh, sweet chocolate Christ,' Michael said finally, 'I'll friggin' eat, damned hosses.' Ten pancakes, seven strips of bacon, four slices of french toast, six biscuits, a large slice of ham and a tall glass of juice later... 'Alright, buddy. Ready to start your training?' After the huge breakfast, Michael was suddenly green to get in the ring. Was the man going to be man enough to admit it, though? 'Ready as I'll ever be.' And that wasn't very ready at all. Pitt started Michael off on the basics, testing his resilience with some simple bumps, and even Pitt would have had to admit that Michael seemed sharp at first. But then again, that was how everyone looked at first. Then it happened. 'Jake, show him some suplexes.' Three or four overhead throws later, and Michael rolled out of the ring. 'Hey!' Pitt yelled. 'Where the hell are you going?!' Too late; Michael disappeared from sight, only returning about ten minutes afterwards, one hand tugging at the ass of his pants while the other was waved in front of his face. 'Want to explain what that was about?' Pitt asked, trying his best not to jump out of his boots and throw the kid down again. 'Well sooooorry,' Michael roared back, 'but I'd rather wrestle wearing a clean pair of pants. Wow I feel like I just dropped enough bombs to kill the Czech Republic.' That did it. 'Okay, okay! Uncle, goddammit, uncle!' Jake snickered as he let Michael out of the harsh camel clutch, allowing the screaming Michael to breathe easy. 'I can't train if you're going to hospitalize me, gawd!' Jake stood up, and it was quite obvious how much he showed scorn for the childlike wrestler at his feet. Michael hadn't seen much more of The Big Dawg during that time, but for what he was designed to teach Michael, he was pretty damn good at doing it. Suplexes, submission holds.. it definitely didn't help matters that Michael was so much smaller than Jake was, but Michael couldn't deny that Jake was a damned beast then. 09.02.05 | The Mercer Household | 7:23 PM The real question, though, was if Jake Hix was really that same beast? Oh, that I could scream And the world would stop and listen And these scars could speak in volumes But who has ears to hear? Or eyes to see? Again, I scream Before Michael could answer that question, his bedroom door creaked open. On the other side, was another mountain of a man, who went by the name of Markus. 'Hey, dude.' Far from the presence of The Big Dawg, indeed. Michael nodded back. 'Hey. Come on in.' Markus smiled and stepped inside the room, closing the door behind him. In hopes to start a conversation of any kind, Markus pointed at the picture in Michael's hands. 'That the one of Jaden?' Just the thought Michael needed... only not really. 'No,' he slurred back, giving off a slight growl as the word left his mouth. Markus frowned in apology, but said nothing in return. Sorry was such an overused word to that point, he was afraid of only making things worse by speaking it. Instead, he remained silent, which to Michael, was that much worse. 'You...' Markus blinked, surprised to hear Michael still speaking; he was much more surprised, however, at what was said next. 'You love my cousin. I think you've proven that enough by now.' Taken aback by the subject of Michael's speech, Markus gulped. 'Michael...' 'I'd assume that after staying here for so long, that you'd want nothing more than to marry her, huh?' Michael cut him off. Boy, Michael sure knew how to hit the nail on the head, no matter what the situation. Markus was so stunned from Michael's remarks, that he couldn't speak. 'Well,' the slightly smaller man continued, 'I'm right, aren't I?' But my voice is buried in an unearthly silence Like in nightmares when ghosts steal your breath Markus groaned. Oh, how true it was... '..Yeah,' Markus blushed, 'yeah, you are. I love her a lot...' Michael thought deeply. In the back of his mind, he remembered how much he loved Jaden when he'd asked to marry her for the first time, and the expression on his face was no different from the one Markus had. Michael loved Jaden... but the thought in the back of his mind told him that if he kept living only thinking about her, everything was going to end very badly for him. He needed something good to happen... And that was why he made the offer he made to Markus. 'If you need any money.. let me know. Get Mindy something nice.' Markus couldn't believe what he was hearing. For a second, he thought that surely this was a trick. 'God, if you're not being serious right now, Mi-' 'Thank me later,' Michael sighed. 'Just.. don't fuck it all up. That's all I ask of you.' Markus quickly nodded his head in understanding. 'Yeah, I know... I won't. I swear ta God I won't, Michael.' 'Good,' Michael replied. 'I'll write you a check in a little bit, just let me get my shit together, okay?' 'Yeah yeah, sure.. no problem.' And before Markus had Michael change his mind, he bolted from the bedroom, leaving Michael to himself once more. 'I guess...' Michael mumbled, to no one at all but himself. 'I guess this is the happy ending I was looking for.' Happy ending. Michael saying those two words about himself and situation he was in, made him laugh. He hadn't been happy in a long time. 'Then I suppose it's time to just end the show here and roll the credits.' Taking one last look at the picture in his hands, Michael couldn't help but break down. Forgetting was the worst part to any relationship, but he feared that here, if he didn't forget, things would be finished- and for good. 'Jaden...' I pray that power be not in my words But in truth that supercedes the mind of man And in our dead hope, and our blind faith In means that look to justify the ends In SAWF, the fire that fueled him to keep winning was his wife. The anger of her not wanting to be with a full-time wrestler, the misery in the inability to hug and kiss his daughter goodbye when she went off to school. Against Jake Hix at Whiplash, the anger came from somewhere else. A place that only the bravest of men dared to go, deep in the back of the psyche, where Michael Mercer's most dangerous inner being dwelled, waiting for all but one chance for his anger to be unleashed. Jake Hix grabbed the steel chair, but when Michael staggered to his feet, it was too late for him to realize it. The punishing shot reverberated throughout the arena, steel slamming into Michael's skull with the most unforgiving of thuds. The only thing Michael could do then was fall to the ground, as much as his heart struggled to keep him on his feet. 'This... this can't be happening,' his mind told him, but it did nothing to save him from hitting the mat. The bell rang, and it was all over. Michael's eyes closed, and when they opened, they showed the defeat in them. No way would Michael let that happen again. This was not a young and foolish Michael just stepping into the doors of the Hix Family Ranch... this was a grizzled Michael Mercer stepping into the arms of fate. Someone was going to leave Whiplash hurt, and for all it was worth, Michael was already a beaten man. But if he was going to die, he was making sure he took Jake Hix with him.
