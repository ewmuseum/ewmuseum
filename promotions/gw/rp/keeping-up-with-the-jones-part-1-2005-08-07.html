Keeping up with the Jones' Part 1

7 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 7th, 2005 Fred Grest is seen standing in a wrestling ring with a young wrestling student. The young student charges Grest and shows some athleticism by getting up high and taking Grest down with a dropkick. Grest sells the bump extremely well as he gets back up and looks at the kid. “Nice work there , Tony.” Grest says. “Thanks but it’s the only thing I do well right now.” Tony responds. “Don’t worry you’ll get better and even if you don’t there are plenty of people who have made it too the big leagues and done the dropkick well.” Grest chuckles. “Yes, but what have they ever done?” “Good point, but probably more then me at this point.” “And he finally admits it.” Coming over from the far corner of the empty training facility. A man a bit taller and much more muscular then Fred Grest approaches the ring. Grest narrows his eyes and mutters. “Mike Jones.” “So you do remember me I guess the history lesson won’t be nearly as painful then.” He replies. “Grest why is it every time you try to help train me some asshole interrupts?” Tony responds. “Kid, if you train with losers you become a loser.” Mike Jones smirks. The kid doesn’t take kindly and wants to attack but Grest puts an arm out and says “Stop” “He’s speaking from experience, kid. You mess with me you get hurt.” “Is that why you’re retired?” Grest asks. “You knows me. I retire and then come out and kick your ass. Who knows I might join Global Wrestling just for shits and giggles.” Jones responds. Grest doesn’t say a word and just stares a hold threw Mike Jones. “Freddy I think you’re busy training one of the future jobbers of America so I’ll leave you be.” The kid is a lot like Fred Grest as he doesn’t take kindly to be calling a failure. He knows that right now he is not ready to take on a pro wrestler though. Mike Jones walks out of the front doors. The kid looks over at Grest and says “Don’t you ever lock anything?” “Bah!” Is Grest’s only response to that question. “Well, I think you should go calm down before we continue.” “Alright” Tony steps through the black ropes and hopes off the ring apron. He goes and gets a drink of water. Grest walks over to a corner of the turnbuckles. He sits down and leans against the yellow pads. Wondering if defecting from Global Wrestling was really worth it. He went from a WMW mid card wrestler to GW dark match competitor. Grest may as well fakes the facts. He is an enhancement talent. He felt so close to being successful and he blew it. He couldn’t even win that damn second chance battle royal. He has been in this wrestling for almost 5 years now and all he does is lose. No matter where he goes it’s the same story. He just focuses in on one person and become obsessed with beating them. Dante, Mike Jones, Syberus, Stevie Franchise. All have one thing in common. They made a living out of smacking Grest around. Now all he has left to wonder is who will it be in Global Wrestling? It can be pretty much anyone since half the roster hates him. Maybe that’s what he needs to do piss off everyone there is to piss off?..
