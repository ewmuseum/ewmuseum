Tee time (PPV#1)

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-21-04 It’s a wonderful day in Central PA, Harv and McNasty decided it would be nice to not worry about things as much and do a little golfing. It seems almost as if golf is the one way McNasty can clear his mind sometimes. He wondered what in the hell happened to White Rose, he was supposed to face them Monday night at Evolution, and instead he had to face to women in Black N White. Nothing seems to make sense, he employs midgets as Leprechauns, and he’s getting sued. He never did anything to WMW and the next thing you know they’re trying to make an example out of him. McNasty and his partner to be announced tomorrow night have to change there scheme now when it comes to Wrestlecade, they’re now facing those meatball eating assholes The Syndicate, McNasty never has liked the Mob and he never will. Something just doesn’t seem right with the climate in Global Wrestling, it almost seems as if someone has a death warrant out for Donny. Who’s to say that it was White Rose who destroyed his car, it could have been WMW, the Syndicate or least likely Black N White. It seems like ever since he said he plans on creating a Tag Team, everyone is after him? Coincidence? Maybe. We see Donny lined up to take his tee shot on the Par 4 18th hole. There is a quite that can’t be unmatched anywhere else, he swings and WHACK there it goes 300 yards straight down the fairway. “Well my good friend Harv it looks like you’re buying when we get back to the clubhouse”. McNasty Said. “I have still to take my shot yet, just cool your horses” replied Harv. Harv takes aim with his shot, swings and it’s 10 yards short of Donny’s also on the fairway. “Now you see I’m not far away from your tee shot,” said Harv. “You still have to put in the hole, you know this hole isn’t over” McNasty said. “Trust me I know how to put it in the hole, I have 5 illegitimate children remember.” quips Harv. The two make their walk to the ball and are talking on the way down. “I’m looking forward to tomorrow night from the bar having it being broadcast all around the world” McNasty said. “Think of all that advertising were basically doing” Replied Harv. “True. It’s too bad that Gunner Addison chick won’t be in the bar with us; I think she’s pretty damn hot. She’s one hell of an athlete on top of that.” McNasty said. “So what’s your theory on White Rose and why you’re now facing The Syndicate.” Harv Said. “Something smells like a fucking rotten meatball. Lets get this all sorted out, first it was those cat eating Chinese guys in White Rose who assaulted me and then probably blew up my car, if it wasn’t them it was WMW or hell it could be The Syndicate. Something just isn’t right about all of this. Someone is out to get me, someone wants me to suffer I just can’t place it right now, but if I get my hands on who it is, they are going to pay.” McNasty said. “So who is your tag partner” You haven’t told me yet??asked Harv “Well you’ll find out tomorrow afternoon when he gets into town. I think you’ll get a kick out of him, what I can tell you, is that he’s not in Global Wrestling just yet, I mean he did sign a contract and all but he hasn’t been there before. In fact he’s never did any professional wrestling before, but he’s one tough S.O.B. I told him that maybe Tomorrow night or Thursday night were gonna go back to the Jiggly Juggs and see Bubbles, I haven’t seen her in awhile” McNasty said. “Sounds like a plan to me” Harv Said. The two come up upon their balls and since Harv is the furthest back he’s next. He lines up his shot, pulls out his 8 iron and swings, the ball is high and it’s looking good it hits the green and it’s in for Eagle. “Asshole” McNasty said angrily. “It’s not over yet if you get an Eagle here then well it’s a tie,” responds Harv. McNasty pulls out his 8 iron as well and swings, it’s a nice shot, it’s on the green and he misses the hole. Harv has won this round of golf and McNasty has to buy the drinks. “Like I said I know how to get it in the hole” Harv said. “Shut up” replies McNasty. Tune in tomorrow night on Overload to find out who McNasty’s partner will be.
