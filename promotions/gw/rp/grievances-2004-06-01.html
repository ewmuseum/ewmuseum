Grievances

1 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Takes Place June 1 Their uncle’s funeral had just finished the day before, and a sad song still played through the winds as they spoke to the two brothers. They had taken the two urns in which their uncle’s remains had been cremated and placed and were allowed to be taken wherever they so wished. The two brothers knew exactly the two spots. The two brothers looked at each other solemnly. It was a sad week indeed. There was not much left to be hopeful for. Their uncle, the only remaining member of their bloodline had been killed at a Yakuza meeting. Obviously, the alliances in the Yakuza could not be trusted any longer. The two Nakazura brothers would have to figure out what they wanted to do with the Empire of their uncle. The street warriors had not checked in, why would they, they were abandoned and most likely fearing for their lives. Many of the security guards at their uncle’s estate had stopped showing up. The two brothers stepped out of their uncle’s house, carrying a black metal canister in each of their hands. The canisters were each marked with a Lotus petal on top of the lid. The brothers were as usual, dressed in black and white business suits. They walked towards the small, black Mitsubishi parked in front of the estate. When the brothers got to the car, they turned around and faced the large, empty, lifeless estate. “They will pay dearly for what they have done to our family!” exclaimed Jushin, obviously pissed off with what has happened. “Be calm my brother, the time for vengeance will soon come. But, that time is not now. Now is a time for grieving and for decision making,” said Ichiro in response. “You are right my brother, I am sorry for my outbreak,” Jushin said softly. “Well, here is the first decision that we face. What are we to do with Uncle Gohei’s estate?” asked Ichiro. “Get rid of it,” said Jushin. “I have no need for it, I have no longer any want to return to this terrible and corrupt place!” “How shall we just get rid of it” We have no one to give it to, there is no one left in our family my brother,” came Ichiro’s response. “Then, we should sell it,” answered Jushin. “That sounds like a smart enough decision to me, I shall contact the realtor tomorrow to discuss the details with him,” said Ichiro. Jushin could only nod as he turned away from the house. The thought of what had happened to his entire family angered him greatly. He wanted nothing more than revenge, but he agreed with his brother. It was a time to grieve their loss, not a time to kill. That time would come soon enough. Ichiro made his way around to the other side of the car. He took one final look at the large mansion and ducked into the passenger side of the car. He held the canister in his hands tightly even as he sat, he watched his younger brother, who was still outside the car. Jushin turned back towards the mansion. The tears began to well up in his eyes as he opened the canister. He twisted the lid off and held the canister high into the air as he felt a gust of wind begin to blow. The wind whispered and spoke, as Jushin turned the canister to it’s side and allowed a stream of ashes to be taken by the wind. The strong wind carried the ashes away. Taking them on a journey to wherever the wind wished. The ashes floated away, soon to land in the estate that their uncle loved so much. Jushin leveled the canister when he was sure that it’s contents had been completely emptied. He tightened the lid, then turned back and placed the canister carefully in the back seat of the car. He ducked into the driver’s side of the small sports car. He quickly turned the key, and the engine roared to life. Jushin looked over to his brother and nodded. “Are you sure that’s where you want to do it, you know Gohei has never been there.” “I am sure, it is not whether he has been or not. It is a sign of respect for him, and for our background,” answered Ichiro. Jushin did not answer, instead he threw the car into gear. The tires squealed as the car did a rather large burnout before heading towards their destination. It was not a terribly long journey to Mt. Kidake. A quick trip into the Eastern Mountains, about an hours drive at most. The car drove quickly, but inside the time seemed to stand still. The car was silent, Ichiro felt a strange sense overhanging him. The two brothers had no idea what dangers would come to them, they only wanted to finish their business and head back to the United States. Fifty minutes later the brothers could see the temple in front of them. They breathed a sigh of relief and quietly parked the car about a half mile’s walk from the temple. They did not wish to be noticed, only to pay their respects. Ichiro and Jushin simultaneously stepped out of the car. They shut their doors quietly and began to walk towards the large temple where they spent their childhood. Ichiro was still holding tightly to the metal canister while Jushin’s remained in the car. They finally came over the final hill that led to the temple. Jushin stopped and watched as Ichiro walked into the open plain in front of the temple. There was a lush garden in the background, but that was not important to Ichiro. This was where he wished to pay his respects. Ichiro began to open the lid of the canister, twisting it off as the cool, mountain wind began to blow. There was a loud buzzing in the air as Ichiro did so. Jushin looked around trying to find the source, but paid little mind after finding nothing. The sound got slightly louder, but the brothers paid no mind. The mountain winds began to blow very strong. Ichiro raised the canister into the air, and prepared to tip the canister with his uncle’s remains in it, when he was suddenly surprised. A large, black helicopter emerged over the tree line of the mountains. A large coat of arms decaled on the side of the helicopter revealed that it was The Upper Echelon. The two brother’s clothes began to ripple as the winds from the lowering helicopter became increasingly stronger. Jushin yelled out to his brother. “Let’s get out of here!” he yelled. “Where will we go, the car is too far away,” said Ichiro calmly. Jushin looked nervously at his brother, who tilted the canister over completely anyways. The ashes began to pour out of the metal canister quickly. The contents began to swirl around in the air, caught by the whirlwind from the massive helicopter. Ichiro saw that the canister was empty, and merely dropped it to the ground. He walked over towards his brother, and looked up. Puzzled and bewildered they stood, maybe the time for vengeance would be here sooner than they thought. They waited as the helicopter began to descend towards the ground?
