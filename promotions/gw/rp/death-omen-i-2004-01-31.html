Death Omen I

31 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-31-04 Prologue: Can you put a price on love? How about dreams? Or even a goal? Some say you can. In my eyes, I see no price that can amount to either of the three. A man I know, one very similar to me, and very complete in nature, for he does everything at one hundred percent, thinks exactly that too. He once fell in love, and was broken by that woman’s loss. Since her loss he’s never been able to regroup. After a year of suffering he tried to regroup, all he could care for was this woman he met on the road. Feeling her love for him, the caring she brought to him when she smiled, and the lovely hair that blew freely in the wind when he saw her waiting outside after each of his matches; Jesse was sure this was the one. For weeks at a time, he’d been getting to know her, not caring to train, no will to work, he’d lost sense of what he should be doing. Until finally, she stopped appearing outside, and she stopped calling, and every time he looked in the mirror, he saw a saddened man, lost in a world of hurt. Hurt inside, the man could no longer hold it. He was sick of taking this abuse. Wrestling was a dream of his that he’d busted every bullet of sweat for that is until he met this woman. Only last week Jesse was hurt during a match by two unknown men. Some said he was attacked from behind, but Jesse knows he wasn’t focused. He admitted that to the press, and with broken ribs, and a week off, he decided to take a break and collect himself. However in that time a man, one from a rival company, threatening the very survival of Global Wrestling where Jesse worked, had began to challenge his role in the company. Jesse could not with stand the pressure. It was way too much, and so he accepted, not caring whether the man agreed or not, this would be a match of pure anger from Jesse’s part. Though, one must question himself on Jesse’s part. Can a man who’s lost all confidence in his life, who’s been busted up and bruised be any challenge for a man who seems to be the most confident man on mother earth? Or does that only push a man to the edge, and bring the best out in all he does? You figure it out after reading this. Story Begins: ‘The white eyes of my hero echoed throughout the world, as I had seen no more a tragedy than what had taken place there before my very eyes. It could have been a dream, yet the reality of it was too harsh for me to believe that. Looking to the very element of truth, I had been directed into a dark hole unknowing of its contents. I was not free, nor safe, yet felt comfortable and with that, I could not stay still.’ Raining like bullets the ground was broken glass as I stepped onto the rooftop. I peered over the edge to see what may be my destiny, never knowing whether I could really finish what I once was. My hair a weave of messy hairs, covering my face as the rain dampened it until it was completely soaked. The sky cracked open with bright yellow and white thunder, each time giving light to the near endless dark that had overcome the heavens. ‘I was more than unsure, I was more than afraid for I was completely lost. Everything in my body said to just take that last step. The people would not care my mind said; they were not worried about me. Nevertheless, I could not believe that.’ Down below cars and couples occupied the streets. It was a festive evening as many gathered at restaurants and went to friends, only to avoid being alone on such a night as this one. Building lights were darkened by the shades, as it had seemed no one wanted to be witnessing his life end. ‘I have thought to myself, where be the torture to stop. I have captured many prizes and conquered many goals, but nothing was as personal as this was. I'd taken a leap of death into the night sky, plunging myself into the heart of a woman who had only been there to hurt me anyway. It was nothing I couldn't deal with, but I could no longer with stand this threshold of pain.’ Stretching his arms out like a crow upon flight, he takes one step forward onto the edge of the brick ledge, fearing no more, he can only die with his next step. ‘I question life as it is. Sure I’m successful and have all I ever wanted in financial status, but is that enough to buy my affection? Can I use my money to pay my broken heart a new shell to hold itself? I do not think so, and therefore this is my last step to my life. Forever my heart shall lay, an unsettled man of undisputed and unplaced anger, until I’m no more alive than the man who I once killed with my bare hands is revived. God bless my soul…” Agony reaching out from with in, the brink of suicide so brutal as the body falls from the heavens as it may seem and lunges into the street below, crumbling the ground as the body goes limp, it is over for the once proud and loyal wrestler Jesse Jamester. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** Six days later upon the burial of Jesse Jamester the rain pours again, and the thunder opens the skies. It is no more that of which one man died that another shall take his place. And so above the tombstone that shows the memories of Jesse Fristik, stands his cousin and adopted son, Julius. A trench coat down to his knees, with the middle buttoned up, and a black hood over his head, he kneels over the tombstone and says a prayer. In his left hand he lights a candle, as dark and gloomy that it may be, he still lays it on the edge of the tombstone. With a white rose underneath the candle for the remembrance of his cousin Jesse, then leaves the tombstones’ place as the rain continues to shatter the process of thought on this very night. Thundering and pouring still, clouds dark overhead as it begins to be a black hole in the sky, the wind keeps blowing, until all else is not heard. Within a minute or so after his leaving of the graveyard the winds stop, the downpour goes silent, and the thunder fades away. Their upon the ground where the tombstone lies, the candle goes out, and a rumbling in the ground begins. A hand reaches out of the ground, and then another, reaching up the ground so muddy and displaced it piles up high as a body disperses the ground from beneath. An almost lifeless body appears coming from the ground, not just a man anymore, but a sub-human species. He’s a zombie or something of the sort. The man once known as Jesse Fristik is now back, but not the same, or anywhere near that as before. His body muddy is washed off as the rain begins to pour again and there as he turns he sees the candle, but more importantly, the white rose. As he takes the damp flower he holds it in his hand and the rain just runs right off of it, washing the mud off his hands and showing his white dead skin. Jesse stands up, his black hair hangs down in his face messy and tangled upon his uprising from the earth. He tucks the rose in his pocket and walks out of the graveyard as the moon begins to appear behind the shadow clouds that hid it, and it’s full essences are only a blessing to see in Jesse’s eyes that are a dead blue. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** ‘It sometimes can be too much to hold inside you, the guilt, the suffering, and all your misplaced anger. You just want to burst open from inside and unleash all of it. I did that, and now I realize, no matter how much I want to do it, I’ll never be able to perish in comfort, for my time here is much too long, and I’m dreaded to live a life of suffering. For that… I will make others suffer, the ones who made me, starting with her…’ Still dark and only the moon to shine through the dark room, Jesse sits on the balcony peering through the window at his girlfriend who’d left without any notice. He tries to cry, but his body is completely shaking and he can’t release any of his emotions in such a state as this. Jesse places a hand on the window, and then looks way, he knows he can’t do this. He’d never forgive himself. In his heart, his mind, he could never live knowing he hurt an innocent woman. But the rage, the innocence, the unforgiving emotion inside must be released, and that is when he knows how to do it. ‘Akodo Rokku…” Maybe not the best way, but to protect himself from hurting the innocent, from hurting those who he cares for, he has to do to others that are waiting in line to challenge him. The one place he can do anything he wants without being hurt for his actions, without suffering any consequences of his doings, basically the place he has no constitution attached to him… the wrestling ring. ’I’m sorry my friend, but it is the way I must play. Rokku, you’ll be… the next… victim...It’s not my choice Rokku, I have to protect the people I love, I have to protect myself, and so… it is your fate that will be decided when I face you in that ring. ’ With that the tears of a man so emotionally displaced fall from his cheeks and he begins to realize his future will be no more than that of his past, a place of suffering, distraught, and more pain. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** The midnight sky is once again seen, but this time, clouds are upon them, and the beauty of the moon is less fulfilling than that of any other night. Peering a hole in the sky Jesse Jamester sits in the park wearing all black, just staring up at the sky. The moons glare creeks through the clouds, down through the tree branches hanging briskly above, and shins down on him just sitting there on the bench. After a few moments, he slowly gets up and walks out of the shine of moonlight, and into the dark shadows of the trees. Echoes of his footsteps shatter the silences, but there is no one around to notice. Just walking his mind begins to speak through his mouth, for it does not seem Jesse is sure he’s speaking his mind, however he keeps walking and talks as he does it. ’It’s been too long for me to realize this feeling. Being left alone, not having anywhere to go, for I’m dead, and that is a problem. I may have spooked Rokku on Evolution, but I know as well as anyone else, this is just another fight. I may be dead, but I’m sure Rokku’s not intimidated. His eyes told me different, and I defiantly could tell he wants to keep that championship… (Sigh) I just can’t realize losing… How can I, a man of the walking dead, incorporate this win in myself? If I lose, then I have nothing left to give… So I how god, how did you expect me to use this to my advantage? HUH!?” His words just spread throughout the empty forest for a moment, and he realizes he is talking out loud. Turning to the right to go back a gunshot is heard and Jesse turns quickly, only to be hit square in the head right between the eyes. He touches the bullet hole, and then in what seems an eternity, he falls flat on his back, not moving nor breathing. (To Be Continued…)
