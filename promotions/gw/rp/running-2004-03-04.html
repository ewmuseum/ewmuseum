Running.

4 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 4th, 2004. I took a long sip of my Caf? Latte savoring the rich flavor as I stepped out of the 'Cellar door' coffee shop. I closed the heavy oak door behind me as I stood on the sidewalk directly in front of the store. I took another sip of the Latte and begin to walk away from the store trying to look inconspicuous as I prayed that no one would notice me. I probably stood out amongst the crowd for a number of reasons. One would be my striking appearance, a handsome guy with shaggy black hair, bluish gray eyes, and a chiseled physique. I wasn't your average Joe…far from it. Two would tie into my appearance seeing as the second would be the clothes I wear. A pair of wrinkled Versace jeans, Converse sneakers, a black shirt with the sleeves ripped off and a deformed smiley face on the chest as the sleeveless shirt showed off my trio of tattoos. A black Jetta cuff locked onto my wrist and a silver cross pendant was dangling from a leather cord around my neck. Three would be the amount of fame that had been heaped upon me. I was a face that was easy to recognize among the Dallas population. My nine months in Global Wrestling had gained me worldwide recognition but in Dallas it was easy to spot me. Those were just a few of the ways to notice me amongst a crowd but I was praying with every ounce of spirituality in my poor twisted soul that no one would notice me. I didn't want to be noticed because I simply didn't want to be bothered. I enjoyed my life as quiet, anti-social recluse, my life as an iconoclast, a castaway, a social misfit…and to quote many others, a freak. Keeping my head down I took a third sip of my Latte as I let the hot liquid rush into my mouth and slide down my throat quenching my thirst. As the coffee followed the internal trail of organs to my stomach something felt wrong down there. It wasn't the coffee but rather a sick feeling I had in my gut, or a gut instinct as many like to call it. My mental radar was beeping frantically as I raised my eyes and my vision locked on to a breath taking sight. Shiny hair as black as a raven's feather…but so much more beautiful. A face that simply could not be done justice with even the sweetest and most loving words. A body wrapped inside a figure fitting white T-shirt and jeans the amazing body provoked thoughts of the sexual variety. She stood only twenty feet away from me…my angel, my life, my love…Alexia. I froze as my tongue went numb, I stood there mute, unable to communicate and unable to move even a fraction of an inch. The hand that gripped my Caf? Latte begin to shake as the rich brown coffee spilled over the side of my to go cup. I didn't know what to do…I was completely at a loss for words. Her head was down as she looked at a paper she held in her hands, her steps came closer and closer as my body began to slowly tremble out of nerves. My Caf? Latte was almost half-gone now as I shook uncontrollably sending the creamy liquid from the cup. Her footsteps were coming closer by the second and I was frozen like a statue. What could I say…what should I say? She hadn't returned my calls for weeks, I had vanished from the earth as far as she was concerned. I wasn't oblivious to the fact that she knew I was dating both her and Amber at the same time…yet there was nothing to remedy that now. If it wasn't seventy degrees I would believe I had been frozen solid because with every step she took I couldn't move…at least not aside from my involuntary trembling. Finally my latte dropped from my hand and hit the concrete sidewalk. I heard a female voice gasp as the remnants of the liquid splashed onto her. I wasn't concerned with that however, I was focused on Alexia. Slowly I saw Alexia discard the paper she held in her hand as she passed by a wastebasket and simply dropped it in. She looked up and despite the numerous bodies on this busy street her big brown eyes sliced through every solitary soul until she saw me. Her eyes widened and she stopped abruptly as our vision was locked on one another. She too was frozen in place as my heart beckoned her in like a tractor beam. However the beam quickly died as she sharply turned on her heel her gorgeous black hair swishing behind her almost in slow motion. She didn't even want to walk my way…what could I do? Still trembling I took a slow step forward and then another. Building my courage I decided that it was now or never. I had to talk to her, I had to apologize and even if I must stoop so low…I had to drop to my knees and beg for her forgiveness. I saw her back vanishing through the sea of bodies and I realized I couldn't let this chance go by. My slow steps became quick steps as I effortlessly shoved a man out of my way and sent him sprawling to the sidewalk amidst his yelling and cursing. Ignoring this man I brushed past a lady and rammed my shoulder hard into another man to remove him from my path. I could see Alexia walking quickly as I gained on her. I rushed forward battling through the mass of bodies as we came closer. Held back by the nearly unstoppable tide of humanity my progress was delayed. Then I saw her looking back at me and our eyes met. Sparks of anger shot from her eyes as she quickened her pace. I shoved a man out of my way and finally saw something of a clear path as I quickened my pace to a job in an attempt to catch up to her. Looking back at me once again Alexia saw me jogging and she set off on a dead run in an effort to escape me. Her small body weaved through the mass that populated the streets as she slowly began to vanish. However I was determined to catch up to her. Using a bit more force than I probably should have I crashed through a line of people knocking several over as I gave chase. A smile came across my face as I could see the back of her head and her hair bobbed as she cut through the crowd. I was gaining on her and getting closer by the second. Brushing by an elderly gentleman I sent him to the concrete where he landed painfully on his hip with a scream of pain. I had probably just fractured the man's hip but that was his problem to worry about, not mine. I had far more important items at hand…such as catching up to Alexia. That's when I realized, when I catch up to her…what am I going to say? Putting this worry aside I pumped my legs and cut around three bodies opening up a path as I began to close the gap between Alexia and I. I had to catch her, it was now or never. My legs pumped as I showed the speed that had gained the interest of a few colleges that wanted me to run track for them. I had politely turned down several full scholarships and ignored my free ride to college to become a Professional Wrestler. Nonetheless I can't recall ever running through this many bodies any time I ran track. One foot slammed down onto the sidewalk and then the other as I propelled myself closer and closer. We were only fifteen feet apart…if she would just stop I could talk to her. With my eyes locked on her, as I grew closer I couldn't help but notice the balding, middle aged man in a business suit that walked directly into my path. My body slammed into his with a fierce crack as I heard his ribs breaking when I hit him at top speed and drove him into the sidewalk. I flew off of him as my momentum carried me head over heels and then back down onto my face where my head collided violently with the concrete. I saw stars and circles whirling around my head but more importantly was what I saw dripping onto the concrete. The texture and color of it would have normally tipped me off but when I felt the warm blood flowing down my face I knew what it was immediately. Ignoring the small gash in my forehead I pushed myself to up to a vertical base. I was gasping for breath, winded from my pursuit of an angel and battered from my unexpected collision with an innocent bystander. I staggered back still a bit dazed from the impact and dizzy from the severe blow to the head. I quickly realized I couldn't stand and I cursed my limbs as I slumped to a knee. Using every last resource of strength and energy I had I pushed my love crazed carcass off of the sidewalk and onto my feet again. Placing one foot in front of the other I started off wobbling across the sidewalk. That's when I saw her black hair…she was standing with her back to me. She was calm and relaxed, unmoving. Taking a few steps forward I gasped for breath and gently placed my hand on her shoulder. 'Alexia…' I softly began in an exhausted voice. The woman spun around and I realized the hard blow to my head must have rattled something because it wasn't my angel. The unknown woman freaked out at my bloody appearance as I bore a crimson mask. She let out a loud scream and gave me a hard shove that sent me staggering back as she quickly scrambled away looking for safety. Her shove hadn't been that intense but as scatter brained, as I was that was all it took as I toppled over lying on the sidewalk in a daze. My eyes stared up to the heavens as the blood ran down my face, closing my eyelids that's when cruel reality hit me. It was then I realized… I had lost my angel. …Again.
