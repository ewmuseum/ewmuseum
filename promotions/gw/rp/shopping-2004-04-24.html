Shopping.

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 24 The scene opens up to the inside of the Connecticut mall. Me and Joey were walking right out of SportChek. I had bought a pair of shoes and some wind pants. Joey had bought a real NFL football. We were both carrying bags that had the SportChek logo on it. We started walking by all of the other stores. I stopped but Joey kept on walking. I got a cart to put all of the things I bought in it. I pushed the cart to where Joey was. Joey looked back and saw me not struggling with what I had. “Hey, where’s all your stuff.” he said. “Look.” I said. He looked down at the cart. All of my stuff was inside it. No offense to my friend, but Joey sometimes, was dumb. He didn’t know where my stuff went when he could have just looked at the cart. He dropped his stuff and ran over to get a cart of his own. He pushed it back and put all of his stuff inside it. “My arms hurt like hell.” he said. “Well, that’s why I got a cart.” I said. “Oh.” he said. See what I mean by him being dumb? We pushed our carts right by, McDonalds, A&W, and Burger King. We found a bench in the middle of the mall. We put our carts beside the bench and sat down. We had only been here for a few minutes and we were already tired. “Where our we going next?” I asked him. “Who knows.” he said back to me. We sat there for a few minutes and then got up. I got my cart and he got his. We started walking with them thinking about where we were going to go. We walked for a bit and then we started talking. “We could goto the food court. I didn’t have lunch.” he said. “Why not?” I said. “Ok then. Where in the food court?” he asked. “I don’t know. We can both just pick a place and pick a table to sit at.” I said. We walked all the way to the food court. By the time we were there our feet and legs were so tired. I went to Burger King and Joey went to Arby’s. We met at a table and started wating what we bought. We started chatting. *10 Minutes Later* I shoved my empty garbage into the garbage can that was beside us. Joey was still finishing up on his fries. He finished and shoved the empty garbage into the can. We got up and got our carts yet again. We started walking down the mall as the scene started to fade to pitch darkness.
