Retirement? Bah, I am far too bored.

2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: xx-xx-04 After many months of doing nothing but sitting on the sidelines, the man that many people came to love and know as 'Rich Sherwin', decided that it was time for him to put those 'Professional Wrestling' boots back on and try his luck inside the ring with the next generation [in some cases, the prior generation too] of the business. His career had been long and decorated for all those who can truely remember. For those who don't, here's a quick summary of each fed he was in and the position that he held... NTICW... Jobber PCW [1]... Jobber LOW... Jobber PCW [2]... Jobber The X... Jobber ... and about seven thousand other organizations to follow that, all of which start different but end with the same six letter word. Sherwin couldn't claim to have had the greatest career in the world, nor could he lay claim to having a career that he could be truely proud of. Often switching organizations made people doubt Sherwin's ability as a whole and this, in effect, had dramatic impacts on every one of his matches. Regardless, the past, is exactly as it sounds... a time and place that nobody gives a flying f--k about at this very moment in time. '... Ladies and Gentlemen, my name is Rich Sherwin and despite the fact about nine and a half million of you actually tuned in for my last ever match, I'm sure you've pretty much forgotten who I am.' As the scene of this exciting little life-sequence opened, we found ourselves in some kind of... conference room. It was neither grand, nor plush... but, enough for a couple of die-hard fans and bottom-line 'Internet Newspaper Reporters'. The room could have easily been mistaken for some n00b's office but at the moment, that minor detail didn't matter... and whoever was supposed to be here, obviously wasn't. As the pimply faced geeks sat in their seats, awaiting the arrival of quite possibly, the greatest thing since sliced bread, another pencilneck walked out onto a cheaply raised platform to get a few formalities out of the way. He looks rather nervous, almost unable to composure himself in a mature way. Despite the error's though, they choose to press ahead with business. '... Today, we have something of a major announcement for you. It might not be all that big in reality, but I'm here to make it seem like something big. After many months of contemplating what to do with his life, Rich Sherwin has decided to come out of retirement... and sign for Global Wrestling on an undisclosed contract.' The Geek smirked somewhat to this audience of people and then turned to his right, where Rich Sherwin was currently standing. The former PCW, X and what-not superstar inhaled deeply before exhaling in a calm, slow manner. He stepped out onto this platform dressed in typical street attire, partly expecting to be blown away by a rousing applause... partly expecting to be abused and degraded. Sherwin strut his stuff confidently, hands waving around in the air, but to the surprise of a few, the athlete saw no reaction from the people. 'Yes!' he powerfully exclaimed before choosing to continue, '... It is not an almighty ghost that you see before you. It is not some kind of fancy equipment that only Batman and myself could devise. Instead and in fact, this is the original Rich Sherwin... and the only one too. I am the person whom you have chosen to... do stuff.' Nobody in the room looked at that impressed with the opening of this athlete. Needless to say, it's not as though he was going to be recieving mass amounts of exposure. Take for instance every other professional that was currently on the GW Roster for example. Some of them were close to the top and didn't gain the kind of reputation and/or following that they had expected to get. The geek, desperate to do something about the way the crowd had simply been killed off, turned to Sherwin and posed a snap-question. 'So, Sherwin... or, Rich. Exactly what made you decide to come to GW? I mean, I heard there were some other random places trying to acquire your siggie too.' Sherwin looked bemused at the question posed but decided to play along with it regardless. '... I don't really know to be honest with you. I think it had something to do with the amount of money I was being offered... but, meh, I really don't recall otherwise.' The geek tried to hide his face amongst his hands and trudged off stage, leaving Sherwin to do whatever he wanted. The amused wrestler remained still, looking down at these peonic reporters before strutting his stuff around the platform, trying to dance his way out of the situation. '... What the Hell are you doing?' 'I thought this was going to be something worthwhile!' 'NO! NO! NO! That's just disgusting... are you mentally well, sir?' The comments flew onto the stage like daggars thrown from afar and they pierced the armour of this usually cool human. He stopped dancing and looked at everyone with a strange degree of anger burning in his soul. '... You came here for some kind of interview? You came here for some kind of story? Some kind of reason as to why I decided to come to GW and call myself, Captain Retirement, correct?' Sherwin asked. As the people nodded, Rich continued his story, 'You see, I trudged along to this organization because I was getting bored without any real need for a fight. The X taught me how to fight in certain situations and until now, I have never had the need to use any of those techniques. This is a fighting organization, or so I was told... and I intend to fight my way through everyone.' '... You're so generic!' screamed one of the reporters, standing up to voice his hate. Rich could only laugh at this individual, throwing a little chuckle here and there before removing himself from the platform. It was quite obvious that the athlete wasn't going to stand for any of this. Upon leaving the stage top, the geek bumped into him again and asked what the hell the problem was. 'Why are you leaving now?!', he began, almost flying into a rage at the sudden choice to depart. 'I told those people they could question you for something in the region of ten minutes... and it feels like you've only been out there for something like a few seconds.' 'Do you think I should care about that? Do you think I should give a flying f--k if you think that I've been out there for five seconds or five minutes? You're forgetting what the whole point of my name is... and I can assure you, it's not some pretty little joke that you get to tell friends. This whole name, this whole persona... it's an extension of the pain you're forced to endure when the realization of retirement slaps you in the face. I break bones, break arms, break legs... all for the enjoyment of the weak and the satisfaction that I seek. Do not get in my face again.' Rich stated, his voice full of a cold and relentless chill. As the scene slowly faded out into the depths of darkness, we could only stare and wonder what the true intentions of this comedic/violent athlete were.
