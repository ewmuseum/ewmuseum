Courage

26 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 26 One of the most dramatic words in the English language is the word courage. Courage is often found in the midst of storms; in times when you least expect it. Courage can be very remarkable. People often associate the word courage with the color red. Courage often leads to bloodshed. Courage is an indispensable part of the world. Without Courage the rights to freedom of speech and the civil rights of many nations would have never been fought for. Without courage society will not last, without courage one cannot survive. When I speak of courage being an indispensable part of the society we live in, what exactly do I mean? I mean courage to stand up and to fight for what you believe in. The courage to be involved, the courage to stand up for freedom. I could go on endlessly about areas where we all need to show courage. To tell you the truth, we all need to show courage in everything we do, in ever aspect of our lives. Courage is one of the cornerstones that make our freedom so great. Courage creates freedom. Fittingly and greatly can freedom and courageousness not be one and the same? Tokyo Words and rumors had begun to surface. The rest of the Tokyo crime syndicate was beginning to learn the story of the Souls of the Black Lotus? victory over Ishuzu. The truth was beginning to surface, no longer could Gohei act like he had no part in this, people had begun to point their fingers at him. He was strong now, his forces were ready and able, but he was not strong enough. He could never be strong enough, for as long as he was evincible, he was never strong enough. And that simply would not do, he needed more power, more pride, more fighters. However, there was one more rumor floating about, one that could crush he and his nephews with one sweeping stroke. Word was beginning to rise, that Ishuzu might not have been the only one who had a hand, in the death of his brother. Word was beginning to rise, that she may have had an outside accomplice. Now to be truthful this was only speculation, and currently without evidence they were only words. But despite the old saying, these words threatened to break the credibility of the Nakazuras and in turn crush the backbone of its family empire. But who else would be apart of such an act? Who else in the Tokyo underground would be stupid enough to take on the Nakazuras, knowing full well the wrath of the deadliest warriors to grace the streets of Tokyo would still be waiting with a vengeful watch. However, what if it was someone outside Tokyo, someone without knowledge of the Souls. Someone who did not fear the vengeful sword which the brothers carried, Ichiro and Jushin. Or maybe it was personal, maybe it was someone who had no reason to do anything, more then an opportunity overseas or a chance to get back at those who did what they could not. The possibilities were endless, but for right now, Gohei needed to get back to work. Much preparation needed to be done, not only for the next few weeks, but also for the long-term life of the Nakazura Empire. Dallas “Well that wasn’t so bad, at least he’s got a nice place,” said Jushin as they walked out of the Lion’s den. They were still dressed in their business suits, and still had their golden tag team titles slung over their shoulders. “True say brother, but does it not seem as if something amiss?” asked Ichiro. “What, it seemed to me like Rokku and “Shiko knew what they were talking about. I mean sure you get distracted by the Crane, she did look mighty good in that business skirt and blouse?” “Not that brother, but something somewhat distant. Do you remember our conversation from a few days ago?” said Ichiro. Jushin could only look at him confused. He had a dazed look in his eye and Ichiro could tell that Jushin had no idea what he was talking about. Ichiro shook his head slightly as they approached the black Mitsubishi. The two brothers quickly climbed in and began to drive off, but the conversation did not die with the roar of the engine. “Remember how I told you I had a sort of weight on my shoulders, well it has not gone away. It seems to be getting stronger,” said Ichiro. “Oh, I remember now,” said Jushin. He did not make eye contact, he kept his eyes focused on the road as he continued to make his way down the Dallas streets. He pressed the clutch and shifted up a gear, getting a slight purr of delight from the engine. He gripped the wheel tightly and remained focused but looked over at Ichiro, he seemed to be deep in thought. “What do you think it is all about?” “I am still not sure, I have meditated about it several times since, but all I keep getting is garbled messages. I see images of us killing Ishuzu, images of our past and images of something which I can only assume if the future.” “What do you see in the future?” asked Jushin. “I cannot say for sure, because to be honest I do not know. The Lotus has only shown me symbols, it is trying to depict a symbol to me, but it has not come through clear yet. I shall meditate about it again, there must be something that I am missing.” “Well, what do you see so far?” “It’s, well?” Ichiro’s face grew long, his eyes flickered slightly. He closed his eyes, and Jushin looked over concerned. His brother did not often lose his train of thought so quickly, in fact not at all usually. He kept a steady eye towards the road, but kept glancing back to his brother. He saw his brother’s muscles begin to pulsate. “Ichiro, what the hell are you doing”!” Jushin didn’t understand, he was allowing the Lotus to take him over. But why? It would only peak his energy level and make him aggressive, why would he need that when they were driving, especially when there was no danger around. Jushin continued watching, this was when the transformation began to differ. Usually, their muscles would go down after the Lotus took over, but now as Ichiro’s eyes were beginning to flicker once again, his muscles stayed swelled up. His veins popping up, nearly ripping through the skin. Ichiro leaned his head back and let out a muffled scream, however it was not his usual voice. No, this was a deep, grueling voice, almost as if it was growling towards him. In it’s first muffled sound, Jushin could make out no words, but that was not of importance right now. He looked to his brother, and began to grow greatly concerned. It had been about three minutes, much longer then it ever took the process, doubling his longest time at least. “ICHIRO!!!” His brother’s eyes shot open and Jushin looked into them, but did not see the comfort of his brother’s usual eyes. No instead, Jushin looked into the piercing gaze almost god like. Ichiro’s eyes were dark black everywhere, not a sign of white visible. Inside where his Irises should have been, it was replaced with a purple smoke. Almost still swirling inside his brothers eyes. Finally, where his brother’s pupils should have been, Jushin saw the Lotus itself, the petals of the Lotus. “There is no Ichiro now, he is my vessel now. Thou art to weak to be my carrier, so thou hast done been chosen as my messenger.” “What the hell are you talking about brother”!” demanded Jushin. “I am not your brother, I am a much higher power. There is a conflict that thoust two will soon face together, united, fighting as one. Am I not correct?” “Yeah sure, we defend the GW Tag Team Titles at High Octane this week,” answered Jushin. “Thy battle is of the utmost important, do not fear thine enemies. However, thou will soon learn of a great hatred towards them, they have stolen from you. But what they have stolen I cannot say, but know this, that it has been stolen and shall never be replaced.” “What the hell are you talking about!” Stolen, who stole something Terrelle and Michael, those two pussy Americans!” questioned Jushin. “Looks can be deceiving, they are not as simple as they seem. Do not take them lightly, for they will not spare you the same measure. This battle is not merely professional, its boundaries rage far outside that of Global Wrestling, it is personal.” Jushin looked in horror and could not speak. Beads of sweat had begun to form on Ichiro’s face and his hair was very damp. Jushin pulled the car into their own apartment complex quickly. The black Mitsubishi pulled into the underground garage, and pulled into their normal parking spot. Jushin looked over and saw one of the most horrific scenes of his life. Ichiro sat completely still, his eyes barely open, as if the life was draining out of him. His muscles were finally beginning to swell down, and underneath his fluttering eyelids Jushin thought he could see white emerging through the blackness of his brother’s eyes. And then as if a surge of electricity had been sent through his body, Ichiro began to shake and convulse violently. After a minute of convulsions they ceased. Jushin hopped out of his seat quickly, he ran to the far side of the car and ripped his brother’s door open. He slung Ichiro’s limp body over his shoulder, and felt both of the tag team titles simultaneously dig into opposite shoulders. He closed the door quickly with his foot, before moving away. With Ichiro on his shoulder, he struggled to reach into his pocket and pull out the car remote, he did so successfully finally, locked the door remotely and quickly pocketed the keys once again. He then ran off towards the elevator as quickly as his legs could carry him.
