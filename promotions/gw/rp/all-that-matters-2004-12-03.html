'All that matters.'

3 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 3rd, 2004. 'Cecil!' Shouted the aged voice of Lester Rawlings, at his best friend Cecil Roadlin. 'Cecil!' He said again, trying to get the 80 year old man to look at him. 'What do you want Lester?' Grumbled Cecil, staring back at the old dog across from him. 'Do you see what I see?' Grumbled Lester in an anxious tone of voice. 'Probably not, ever since you got that new prescription for your spectacles you've been a regular ol' Eagle eye, boy howdy!' Cecil croaked back in response to Lester. 'No, you'll be able to see this too...' Began Lester, before Cecil cut him off. 'Is it one of those fine hoochie-mamas, running around in their teeny clothes again? I love the clothes they make these days, they've got body parts hanging out everywhere. Where is she?' Cecil asked, tilting back the tan fishing hat that rested on his head so that he could have a better look. Craning his neck he peered out into the crowd. 'No, no Cecil, I haven't seen any hoochie-mamas...' The grouchy Lester started before the equally grouchy Cecil cut him off. 'Then why in the sweet name of Jimmy Carter did you wake me up? I was having a dream about this fine hoochie-mama who liked...' Cecil began only to have Lester interrupt him. 'You'll never believe this. See that feller' out there?' Lester asked, pointing a bony index finger out into the crowd. The eyes of Lester and Cecil converged on the man that was being pointed out. He was fairly short at the mark of five feet, ten inches but his athletic body, slightly north of the two hundred pound mark made up for that. He wore a gray 'Number one gun', t-shirt, with a pair of comfortable jeans and black Chuck Taylors, over his right shoulder was a backpack and from the looks of his hair, a comb or brush hadn't touched it in years, as the black mane shot everywhere. His dark locks spilled over into his face and on occasion he would reach up to brush the hair out of his bluish gray eyes. 'I don't believe it Lester, you really are an eagle eye...he must be a good thirty feet away!' Gasped Cecil, surprised at his 82 year old friend's ability to see over the twenty-five foot mark, a feat which Cecil was envious of. 'Well, don't you recognize the hoodlum?' Lester asked, quickly losing his patience. 'How am I supposed to know him? I've never been to Miami before!' Cecil angrily retorted. 'We aren't in Miami yet you ding-dong...we're still in New York. Grumbled Lester at his friend. The young man under their microscope was oblivious to their presence as he leaned against a pillar in the airport, opting to stand before his flight to Tokyo. 'Look at those tattoos.' Sneered Cecil as he noticed the two tattoos on the young mans arms. The first tattoo was on the left arm, The word 'freak' in black letters on the underside of the left wrist. On the opposite side, the underside of the right wrist bore a red Arishikage symbol and had the young man not been wearing a short-sleeved shirt the elderly men would have spied the Superman tattoo on the higher section of his right arm. 'Well, don't you recognize him?' Lester asked, his patience for Lester completely depleted. 'By golly, how am I supposed to recognize some New York hoodlum?' Cecil snapped back his patience also gone. 'No, no Cecil he isn't a New York hoodlum...' Lester began before being cut off once more. 'Well he looks like one to me!' Cecil roared back at his friend. 'Now I'll be the first to say he's a hoodlum, but that young punk right there is none other than 'American Youth' Josh!' Lester said, a smile growing on his face as he nodded his head vigorously. 'Oh he's that...yeah...he's that feller' from Global, ain't he?' Cecil asked, his white eyebrows raising in surprise. 'Yup, he's that feller'!' Lester excitedly responded. 'The punk with all those tattoos? The one that comes out to that fast bang-bang music, jumps around like he's on crack, hurts himself by jumping off of things that God never intended, and then for some reason the fans cheer the disrespectful little snot?' Cecil asked, his surprise growing by the minute. 'That's him. I tell you Cecil that is exactly what is wrong with America today. Tattooed hoodlums are allowed to run around in public. Look at that...he should be locked up somewhere. He beats people up for a living, then runs around in public all bruised up, with his head looking like a big ol' purple grape.' Lester snarled, growing even crabbier with every passing moment. 'Locked up? Locked up where? He doesn't need to be locked up in jail he needs to be locked up in a mental institution!' Growled Cecil, agreeing whole heartedly with his friend. 'I'm telling you Cecil, that is what's wrong with America today. Tattoos, piercings, Nike commercials and that no good hoodlum Josh!' Lester mumbled. 'All he does is promote decay and the destruction of society, look at the way he dresses, wearing rags...he ought to be ashamed of himself. When I was his age if you were on TV and you didn't wear a suit and tie your mama would slap you.' Cecil said, nodding in agreeance. 'I bet his mama never did slap him. No siree, he probably never got spanking number one when he was growing up. Look at that...spoiled little brat as a child and when he grows up he's even worse. Now Cecil that's why they ought to bring paddling back in the public school system, some of those youngsters need some sense beat into 'em.' Muttered Lester, shaking his head. ---- 'Da-nuh-nuh-nuh-nuh -nuh!', began the ring tone on my Nokia cell phone. I knew the tone...Batman, the tone I had reserved for my comrade in arms, Ken Partridge Jr. Reaching behind me into the side pocket of my Olive green backpack I pulled out the cell phone. 'Da-nuh-nuh-nuh-nuh -nuh!' it repeated, the tune becoming more annoying by the second. 'I really must change that ring tone.' I said to myself while flipping open the phone. 'Yeah?', I answered, awaiting Ken's response. 'What's up?' Ken asked inquisitively, his voice coming across the phone smoothly. 'I'm at the airport, about to hop on a bird for Tokyo. How about you, amigo?', I inquired of him. 'Just wanted to call and check on you, you seemed a little stressed out at the way Bill was acting the other night. How are things?', Ken asked, his voice laced with genuine concern. 'I'm okay man...I'm okay. I guess I was just a little bothered because I thought Bill was more understanding than that, you know?' I replied, my tone bearing a slight tinge of annoyance. 'He was just upset, it happens.' Ken reassured me, before continuing on. 'But Josh, you know its that hot-headed nature that makes him such a dominant force in the ring.' 'Your right Ken.' I admitted.'You are right.' 'So, no worries then?' Ken asked, his voice showing the true concern that made him a quality leader and even better friend. 'Makuna patata.' I said over the receiver with a chuckle. 'What..?' Ken asked, chuckling at my temporary lapse of maturity. 'You remember the Lion King don't you? Timon and Poomba? They always said 'makuna patata', it means something like...no worries.' I explained in complete seriousness. 'Josh...have you been getting enough sleep?' Ken asked with a laugh. 'Yeah, I'm okay, what makes you ask?' I responded, slightly perplexed by the question. Oh nothing really...other than the fact that you are...umm...well, quoting The Lion King?' Ken laughed again, as he said this. 'Oh.' Was my simple response. 'Good point, there probably aren't a lot of world renowned athletes who make their living by fighting for their life and then go around quoting a kids movie.' 'Right, for some reason The Lion King wasn't exactly a cult classic in our profession.' Ken stated, still amused by the fact that I had used a quote from a Disney movie. 'You've got a point Ken, but you know...that's exactly how I feel makuna patata, baby, makuna patata. I'm not worried about things, I'm not stressing out over things, for once in my life I'm just taking things as they go, and you know what? I'm happy Ken. Happy.' I said, a smile spreading across my cleanly shaven face. 'And I'm happy for you Josh.' Ken said, in a way so genuine that you could tell it was the truth. ---- 'Look at that...talking on his cell phone like he's Mr. Big Shot. Look at me, I'm so important I have to carry a phone around with me! I tell you Cecil that is what is wrong with America today, all of the youngsters have to be with it and carry around a cell phone.' Sneered Lester, shaking his head in disgust. 'Oh I hear you Lester, it makes me sick. Why, back in my day if you wanted to get in touch with someone when they weren't home, you called and left a message with their wife, or you wrote them a letter. But did you ever call them up on some phone they carried around? Nosireebob, you did not!' Gasped Cecil in an upset manner. ---- 'I don't understand it Ken, but I'm happy. I don't have anyone special in my life, I'm not at the top of Global anymore, and when I lost my title I lost my friendship with Ice, but in spite of it all...I'm happy.' I replied, the smile on my face growing larger by the second. 'Don't worry about the title Josh...' Ken began, but I cut him off, my voice flowing with energy. 'I'm not. I'm not worried about it all. I loved being at the top of my profession but all good things must come to an end sometime, and you know...there are so many other things that I want to accomplish in Global. The title was great to have, it was a great run and its nice to have my name written in the history books of Global, but hey...I haven't even made half of the impact that I want to make.' My voice bore an unusual amount of enthusiasm as I spoke, and Ken seemed to pick up on this. 'That's good, and you'll have many more opportunities coming your way.' Ken stated pleasantly, and even though I couldn't see him, I knew he was smiling on the other side of the line. 'And Ken...' I started, but was quickly interrupted by a tap on my shoulder. Turning around I saw a man standing there with a broad smile across his face. Dressed in Khaki shorts, a blue T-shirt, black Red Sox baseball cap and white sneakers the man held a little girl in his arms. The little girl couldn't have been over the age of four, her brown hair was adorably put up in pigtails and she wore a pair of Jean overalls with a white t-shirt underneath. 'Hey Ken, can I call you back?' I asked, smiling at the man and his daughter and raising the index finger on my left hand in a gesture for them to be patient with me for just a moment. 'Something up?' Ken asked, seemingly puzzled at my rush to leave. 'Yeah, but its nothing bad. It looks like someone wants a photo-op, so I'll catch you later amigo.' I said, smiling at the cute little girl in her fathers arms. 'Ok buddy, you take care.' Ken stated, seeming to completely understand the situation, which was understandable due to the legions of fans that he had. 'Always.' Was my cheerful response, before adding; 'I'll see you in Tokyo.' With that said I pressed the talk button on my cell phone and returned it to the side pocket of my backpack which hung lazily by one strap over my right shoulder. 'Sorry for the delay.' I said in apology to the man and his daughter. ---- 'Now do you see this, Cecil? Look at that...that man is going to make his pretty little girl have her picture taken with him, this is downright despicable!' Lester ranted, waving his hands about as he spoke. 'Oh it sure is, it makes me sick to my stomach. I don't even see how a loving father would let some freak like that even get close to his daughter, it turns my stomach Lester, turns my stomach!' Cecil spat out angrily, obviously disgusted at what he was bearing witness too. ---- 'No sir, we're sorry to inconvenience you, I hope the call wasn't important.' The man apologetically stated, although his eager daughter didn't seem to feel too bad about making me get off of the phone as she smiled up at me from her position in her fathers arms. 'Nothing too important, my only question is...sir? Who were you talking to?' I joked with a playful wink. 'I'm sure you know my name so...Josh, that's what my friends call me, and every fan of mine is also a friend.' 'Well Josh, its very nice to meet you.' The man said, adjusting his daughter over to his left arm so that he could extend his right hand. Gripping his hand I shook it firmly, in a symbol of respect. 'And who is this lovely young lady?' I asked, freeing my right hand from her fathers grasp and twirling my finger about in the air in front of her. 'Bzzz!' I playfully exclaimed as I poked her arm, her reaction was one of amusement as she shrieked in joy. 'Kimi, say hi to Josh.' The father stated, to the little girl. Glancing up at me, she spoke softly. 'Hi.' Was the only word that her tender little voice uttered. 'Awww, how cute.' I replied, with a smile, my face beaming at the sight of such a cute picture. 'Actually, we were wondering...would you mind taking a picture with her?' The man asked politely, bracing himself for what he assumed would be a negative answer. 'No, not at all. In fact it would be my pleasure.' I responded, the smile on my face seeming to be permanently attached. 'Although, would you mind taking one for me as well?' I asked, once again reaching into the side pocket of my backpack, and extracting my digital camera. 'No, that would be great!' Replied the giddy father who couldn't have been a day over thirty. Leaning down he placed Kimi on the floor where she promptly ran over to me and grabbed the leg of my BKE jeans, as I handed her father my camera. 'My, aren't we the eager one?' I said, my face beaming as I scooped her up in my arms. ---- 'Look at that...that sicko is holding her. This makes my skin crawl, Cecil. Do you see that? Cecil...Cecil? Wake up Cecil!' Lester shouts, going so far as to reach over and smack his friend on the knee to wake him up. 'I was just resting my eyes!' Cecil proclaims as his head snaps up and he looks about in confusion. 'I was awake...really, I was!' ---- Kimi beamed brightly as she pressed her adorable little head against the bottom of my chin, holding her cautiously I smiled for the picture and her father quickly snapped three photos with his own camera and then quickly switched cameras and snapped one with mine. 'Take another, please.' I requested, and he promptly did so. With an overjoyed smile he let my camera dangle about his wrist by the strap as he opened his arms, and I returned Kimi to his arms. Handing me the camera, he seemed speechless yet he didn't need to say a word, I knew exactly how he felt. He felt like I was this amazing person who could change the world and he was touched that I would take just a moment out of my busy life to spend with him and more importantly his four year old daughter. I understood how he felt, because I almost felt the same way. I was touched that his four year old daughter would think enough of me to allow me into her life, to have our picture taken together. There is no love more true, more pure or more innocent than the love of a child, so as overwhelmed as this father may have been by my star presence...I was even more overwhelmed by his daughters innocent admiration. To her it didn't matter if I was 'too small' for the business, it didn't matter if there were many in the business who considered me arrogant, it didn't matter that I was no longer the Champion, it didn't matter that all of the hype had ended when my title reign did. It didn't matter that some people saw me as a tattooed degenerate freak, it didn't matter that I had been cast out of my family, it didn't matter that I was a loser in the game of love. There was only one thing that mattered and that was the fact that I could put a smile on her face, and to me...that really is, all that matters.
