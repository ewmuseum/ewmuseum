Guess who's coming to dinner

15 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/15/04 It was another Sunday night at Coretta Randall's house. Brick, his sister Monica and her husband Kyle were setting the table. 'So, Jamal, when's your friend getting here?' asked Kyle. 'I just got a call from him a few minutes ago. Said he was on his way.' 'I'm actually surprised you'd want someone from work here considering what happened the last time,' said Monica. 'Yeah, well, that was the second last person I wanted here near my family.' 'I guess I don't have to ask who the last person you'd want here is.' 'No, you don't.' 'So, who are the teams in your match on Evolution?' 'It's me and Donny, Manifest Destiny and the Elite. There was already a match last week. The winning team from each match faces each other for the tag titles at Whiplash.' 'Didn't you already hold the Tag titles?' 'Yeah, but that was a long time ago. It's been too long since I've held a title. Not since...' 'Since Deacon Holmes beat you for the Platinum title.' Brick growled. He didn't like being reminded of Deacon's victories over him in the ring. 'So, Jamal, is Leinad Soma really a vampire?' 'I don't know. I kinda doubt it, even though he's 'proved' he is. I know I won the one match we had when he was in TUE. Last I heard, vampires were supposed to be superstrong. And I may be strong, but I ain't exactly Superman.' Just then, Coretta stepped out of the kitchen. 'Jamal, Kyle, I could really use your help in here.' 'Be right there, Ma. Come on, man.' Brick and 'Is Monica helping you two out there?' 'Yeah, she just set a few places on the table.' 'Why did you let her? 'We tried to get her to sit down and relax, but you know how she is.' 'I don't suppose I can blame her. I was like that when I was pregnant with both of you, but I still took care of myself.' 'Well, Ma, she's just setting the table. That's not exactly strenuous labor.' 'So, when will your friends be here?' 'They should be here any minute.' 'Do they know how to get here?' 'I e-mailed Donny a map to here.' 'How did you get a map to this house?' 'Had one made on the Internet.' * * * Meanwhile, outside the house, Donny McNasty and Alexis Steele made their way up the path to the front door. 'Brick's mom has a really nice house.' 'Not surprised, since Brick told me he bought this house for her.' 'You think Brick's family will like us?' 'After having dinner with Train? I'm pretty sure,' he said, ringing the bell. * * * 'I'll get it!' Monica called, as she headed for the door. 'Oh, hi you must be Donny. I'm Monica, Brick's sister.' 'Yeah, I think he's mentioned you a few times.' 'Won't you come in?' 'Hey, Monica, is that Donny and Alexis?!' Brick called from the kitchen. 'Yes!' she replied, as she led Donny and Alexis into the living room. 'So, Brick tells me you and your husband are having twins. Congratulations.' 'Thank you.' 'Oh, I just love babies,' said Alexis. 'They're so small and so cute and so soft. Oh, Donny, when can we have babies?' 'Someday, babe. Someday.' Brick walked into the living room. 'Hey!' He and Donny then bumped fists and embraced. 'Let me introduce you to the rest of the family. You've already met my sister Monica. This is her husband Kyle...' 'Nice to meet you,' said Donny as he shook hands with Kyle. 'And this is my mother Coretta Randall.' 'Thanks for having us here, Mrs. Randall.' 'Oh, think nothing of it. It's been so long since Brick invited a friend to dinner, especially one from work.' Donny sniffed the air. 'Is that pasta sauce?' 'Yeah, it's Ma's own recipe.' 'Would you like spaghetti or penne?' 'What's penne?' Donny whispered to Brick. 'It's like ziti.' 'Ah. Penne sounds good. Alexis?' 'I'll have penne, because it sounds like the name of this girl I used to know.' 'Uh, yes, ' said Coretta. 'Well, if I can get some help from my son and my son-in-law, we can have dinner as soon as possible.' 'Right. Come on, Kyle.' * * * Time had passed. Brick and Donny were on, not their second, but their third plate of food. 'So, it's almost time for the end of the match. Donny lifts Makonie up and Brick comes in with a diamond cutter...' 'What was it you called it?' 'A McNastylayer.' 'How is it,' asked Monica, 'that I'm the one who's pregnant, but you two are the ones eating like there's no tomorrow?' 'What can I say? We're two hungry cats.' 'Plus, we've had good food. My compliments to the chef.' 'Thank you, dear.' 'So, are you two ready for your match?' 'More than ready. Right, Donny?' 'Right.' 'By the way, Jamal told me about what happened last Monday night. Are you all right?' 'I will be, ma'am, at least come Whiplash.' 'What happens at Whiplash?' 'Donny gets Train in an Iron Circle match for the Platinum title. Only way to win is by KO or submission.' 'Like the Pit?' 'Exactly. Can't wait till then.' 'So, Brick, are you gonna face Deacon Holmes?' asked Kyle. 'Nah, Bubba's got him defending his title in a ladder match against Sage Sabbot and Juri Hyobanshi. Not that I would have minded facing Deacon at Whiplash.' 'I know the way you've been messing with his head, he definitely wants a piece of you.' 'OK, Jamal, tell the truth. Did you really sleep with his girlfriend?' 'Monica, you know Ma doesn't like this talk at the dinner table.' 'Actually, Jamal, I'd like to know, myself.' 'Well, I guess I can tell all of you.' * * * A half hour later... It was time for Donny and Alexis to leave, but not without some 'Once again, thanks for having us for dinner, Mrs. Randall.' 'Oh, you're welcome. You should come again.' 'Oh, I plan to.' 'Thank you, Mrs. Randall,' said Alexis. 'Well, we'd better get going if we're gonna make the airport in time for Evolution.' 'Yeah, I'd better get going, too. I'll call you later, Ma,' said Brick, as he kissed Coretta on the cheek. 'OK, you be careful.' 'Bye, Brick,' said Monica, as she and Brick kissed each other on the cheek. 'Bye, sis.' 'See you later,' said Kyle, as he and Brick shook hands and embraced. 'Good night, everybody.' Brick, Donny and Alexis all headed out the door and down the path to the sidewalk. 'Hey, Brick, wanna tag along?' 'Yeah, sure. We just need to swing by my place and get my bags.' 'Can't wait till Evolution.' 'Neither can I, Donny. Neither can I.'
