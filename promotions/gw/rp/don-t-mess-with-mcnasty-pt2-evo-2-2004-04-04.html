Don't Mess with McNasty pt2 (Evo #2)

4 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/04/04 Last night things got a little crazy at the Drunken Leperchaun, McNasty had to show a man by the name of Justin that it doesn't pay to piss McNasty off in his own bar. Now they're down at the Altoona Police Department sorting out the mess and the assault charges Justin is trying to bring up against McNasty. What Justin doesn't know is that McNasty is very good friends with a lot of the Police Department, especially Sgt. Todd Sprinkles. Sgt. Sprinkles is addressing Justin who is sitting on the left side of the desk and McNast is sitting on the right side. 'So let me get this straight, Mr. McNasty assaulted you for no reason, other than you were talking about wrestling.' Justin who is all bandaged up begins to respond. 'Yeah he did, he just turned around and punched me and I went flying into a table or something, then the next thing I know is him picking me up and throwing me out the window. Which I suffered bruises, cracked ribs, and many lacerations. I had to get 135 stiches because of this man and his aggression problems, something needs to be done about him' McNasty just shakes his head chuckling away. Sgt Sprinkles asks for his take on the story. 'Mr. McNasty is what he said any bit true.' McNasty smiles as he begins to speak. 'Sgt. Sprinkles, lets be honest, if I wanted to just assault this man for no reason, I would have hurt him a lot worse than he his now. In fact he would still be in the hospital bed. The honest fact was that he pissed me off, I told him to shut his mouth and when he wouldn't do as I say, I showed him the fastest way out of my establishment.' 'Mr. McNasty, what was it exactly that made you angry' 'He kept running his mouth about Train, I guess Justin here doesn't think that what I do is real. So again I told him to shut his mouth, he kept running it so I shut it for him.' Justin replies as if he's just been proven right. 'You see, this man needs to be arressted and thrown behind bars. Do something about it right now, if not then someone else's life will be in trouble.' Sgt. Sprinkles takes a deep breath and then tells Justin what he's going to do. 'Look sir, this is how it's going to be. I am arresting you for wasting my time with this bullshit. I know Mr. McNasty well enough to know that it was all your fault to begin with' Justin has a stunned look on his face. 'What in the hell are you talking about, I am an INNOCENT MAN!!!! You can't do this to me, you'll be hearing from my lawyer' Sgt Sprinkles gets up and then proceeds to cuff Justin and take him to a holding cell inside the main part of the Police Department. As the two walk away, McNasty leaves a wad of twenties on Sgt. Sprinkles desk. As you know it doesn't pay to mess with McNasty. Paying Sgt. Sprinkles was just really icing on the cake for the way things are in Altoona. If McNasty wanted to he could run for Mayor in this small town in Central Pennsylvania. Lets just see how well McNasty is able to handle Train and Makonie tomorrow night at Evolution only on HBO.
