I'm not the only one

10 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-10-2005 To the untrained ear you might say that I was more grown up sounding. Weird yes but more grown up then the young girls that walk around these parts today. The fashion styles of today with the black leather suits and the red high heels to go with the goth look. Those girls were just scary to me and I’d never fall into that category ever. But for some reason, something, my dressing was a lot different. I had went from the short cut off blue jean shorts with a shirt that was showing more then my parents would like to the tight long pants that would press both of my but cheeks out as far as you would want them with a shirt that cresses the bottom of my “B” cup breasts. It was the new me and it was scary. Even my uncle was wondering what was going on. Looking at the time that I use to train in the ring as it slowly went down to almost nothing. Still I never even was picking up on it and I don’t think I would of unless that incident happen? *********** September 10, 2005 2:30 PM in the afternoon The tree’s on the outside blow with every little breeze that whistle by. Pressing them to the very edge of their breaking point. The storm was coming in, taking the full blow right into Dallas, Texas. It was the storm of the year and the weather man knew that even before the first signs had come in. Tearing down power lines, tree’s, fences, and other things at will. A few sky scrapers in the downtown blunt of the city could feel the most effect’s. Windows braking as things came flying through the middle of the window and slamming into the thick wooding of the walls. Good thing no one was inside them or they’d be getting ran to the nearest hospital. On the far side of town, waking up to a very noisy house I could hear the screams coming into my very sensitive ears. My hair, as wild as the seventy’s were with the hippy days. It was all in self pride today and to be blunt, my self pride was out the door. Pressing the few covers that were laying over me, feeling the sudden chill of cold air come flying through the foot of the doorway and slamming into the front of my feet before they came crashing down on to the oak flooring we had on the house. “Good morning?” Turning as quickly as I could to the corner of my room to see the face of death. It was bitter looking and it didn’t take anything I was going to say nice. “So you say.” Responding with a sudden feeling of scariness. He was in the dark, the shadows of the corner were their was no light at all from the window which had been close for the last few days. He rose up towards me, still in the chair. Showing his face which to be honest was as ugly as a woman who was torn up into little pieces which had been raped several times and left for dead. He was a monster and their was only one thing he was going to have’ME! “What do you want anyways?” Asking with what bravery that I still had left. His red eye was glowing even brighter this morning. Shining right at my eyes with an intensity that would make anyone become scare’s. It was like my heart began to race out of it’s own shell. Breaking the outer skin and running away from me, trying to kill me spirit. Covering my eyes with both hands, trying to make him go away but it wasn’t going to happen. “You know what I want Elia. I want your full cooperation with everything that is and will happen. From my words to my actions that I tell you to do.” Responding with his eye still glowing towards me. I might have been acting as if my afraid side was loose and running away from me so that my brave side could take over but to be frank it wasn’t. I was like a little girl trapped in a corner, afraid that she is going to be done in by a mad man. Or a man who is running from a murder that he didn’t cause. Either way, my heart was speeding into high gear and I could only look right at him. “I can’t?”, Responding with a low voice that only he could hear. “You can’t or you won’t?” He says, almost cringing with anger in his eyes. “I can’t. I just can’t.” Feeling the water beginning to come out of my eyes. I just couldn’t do it to people that didn’t cause me pain. I was not going to let him win this one. “You have no choice. Your father didn’t believe me either and then he finally wised up.” Raising up off the chair and moving forward with little bitty steps. “My father?” Asking with a shocking voice. “What about my father?” Moving closer to me as I sat on the bed still. What did he mean by my father not believing? How did he know about my father? How did he know him I kept on asking deep inside. “Your not the only one I’ve come to in this family. I’ve been to your grandpa, grandmother, your great aunt and uncle’s, and even to your Uncle Johnson and your father.” Getting right next to me as he continues. “You can ask him but as we all know he is gone off and away from you and if you could find him, do you really think he’d be the same man?” “YES!” Screaming at him with anger for the first time. “I can assure you Elia he wouldn’t be. You see, after that night in the alley, he’s been a different man. His soul is different. His heart has stopped beating. And his mind hasn’t seen the light of day since your mother left him.” He says with a smile on his face for the first time. He reaches down from his standing point in the room and places a hand right on my shoulder. That night, what did he mean. I knew I had to talk to my father now and find out what he really meant. Looking over at his hand, reaching back with all my anger and blasting it right off my shoulder. Like a country on it’s heels of a war, the eye began to turn a deep blue. Twisting through out my body as a feeling of pain, ripping through my brain, twisting my heart and releasing the blood that ran through it. Screaming as loud as I could, I fell back down on the bed, not able to move before the door came crashing open. “ELIA! ARE YOU OK”!” “ELIA!” Thomas and Eric, my two best cousins came running in to see me laying on the floor. My eyes, reaching the back of my eye sock it’s. Looking up off the pillowcase to the end of the bed, right back into the dark corner to see the red light fading away. “You will learn just like he did.” That voice began to fade away as Thomas and Eric scream out to the living room for help before the world that I saw with my eyes became a dark circle of nothingness.
