Cherish #1: Ties that bind

Cherish #1: Ties that bind Leinad Soma Global Wrestling Promo #1880 Date: 02/06/05 Location: soma's mind 'Suffering is permanent, obscure, and dark, and shares the nature of infinity.'-William Wordsworth For a mortal she fights quite well. I remember was my first thoughts when I saw her. Her hair was like fire and her eyes like the smoothest blue diamonds. She was intent on killing me and I gave her the chance. It's rare that a vampire of my age would still have been up at all, but as I was always told: I am not like the norm. She swung her axe fiercely as any person from Gaul would have, aiming for the chest, back, and neck. I stood my ground firmly I knew she held no chance but she tried harder than any other warrior I encountered in quite some time. The thought actually made me smile. This warrior, this female could fight as hard as she did. Before she could swing the mighty weapon once more, I was upon her gripping the arm which held her axe and my other hand around her throat, slightly closing in on her windpipe. She stood there in my grasp not saying a word, but I could see the fear in her eyes. The want to run away. The want to survive. But to survive is to suffer. This is well known. I looked down on the young woman, smiling as I usually did in such an occasion. A kind of triumph over one's prey. I felt a single tear hit my cold murderous hand, her face never showing a hint of fear. I wanted to scold the girl. Yell at her asking if she knew who I was, what I was. Why was she not screaming or crying in fear. I then remembered the Romans had looted, pillaged, and raped this land till almost utter destruction. She has known pain. She has known suffering, the ripping of her dress shows that. For the first time I let my prey loose after toying with one as I did her. She looked at me a moment, just standing there waiting for me to attack her. I sat down on a nearby stump and looked at her. I really hadn't realized how beautiful this young woman was. Her hair was long and the color of a poinsettia, and her eyes the bluest of blue. Under the rough hand of the seasons, you could see very smooth skin almost milky white in tint. I watched her move cautiously toward me. 'You may not share the color of most of those roman bastards but I know they have sent you and I won?t rest till you are on your way to the underrealm.' I smiled at her. 'I can assure you child I am no more roman than you yourself.' 'How can I believe you?' 'You really can't, you will have to trust me or die because you didn't realize that I offered you life when I could have killed you and fed of your blood.' She opened her mouth but in fear could not mumble a single word. I smiled once again. 'Do not fear child. I wish to offer you a job that will give you benefits beyond your understanding. I am offering you the chance to be under the tutelage of one who has been around since the dawn of time. In my care you shall learn all forms of fighting, languages, and mathematics. You will learn sorcery and the darkest of arts. I offer you power. You may think me a demon, but I am merely a creature who wants to live on in memory not eternal life. It is your decision to make, but if you refuse you shall surely die.' Those rare thoughts of those who came before my personal attendant Cherish, the one who when had to decide, found happiness in the arms of the man she loved than the man who gave her life. Cherish never realized I had been preparing her. She required all the skills she needed to carry on the Soma legacy. She was to be the next Morningstar, keeper of oblivion. Since there first meeting on a cold night about four years ago Cherish had gone from a prostitute to a business woman, she spoke over seven languages and all of my private accounts. At what I was paying her, she was making double what a NBA starter would make. She has a black belt in Jeet Kune Do, Hung Gar, and Tai Chi. I trained her to be able to hunt the so called 'elders' of my species. She could have been unstoppable but this man, the man I brought back to life, has set my plans back a little. I should have stayed with TUE and left Sage Sabbot dead. That's how I got paid for trying to help the world. Not again. Never again. I sit now in my mansion alone, fully recuperated from the staking I took at Overload last year. I walk around in the halls. Those empty halls. As I look at the future, I see tensions flaring, alliances made, mighty pillars being raised up upon high and being crushed to asunder. But patience is a virtue I have long been a master of. The hunt has began and the hunter has already its time to rekindle old ties and making plans within plans. And once my Cherish is back I shall finish her transformation. I stand on one of the halls in the east wing of the mansion looking at a painting from France. I take a closer look at the painting and smile. 'I think it's time to see who can be trusted and who will be dealt with accordingly. It is time to show people the path to oblivion.'
