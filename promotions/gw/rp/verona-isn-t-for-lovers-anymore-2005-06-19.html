I may not have gotten a perfect freakin' grade in every single one of my classes, but I did as good as I could none the less. Of course, you, father, happened to be the one to threaten to kick me out of the house for flunking a measly little test, which I would have aced had I not been in the hospital the week before with the flu. I took the car that you gave me for my eighteenth birthday, and it being my car, I decided to get it painted pink. Surprise surprise, you threatened to sell it for that reason alone.' The tension in the room slowly began to build. Mindy nitpicked every single thing about her father that she could gather from her memory, and all her parents could do was sit and take it. The private schools she was forced to attend. The swimming lessons he forced her to take. The job at a shop that Luis' friend had run that he made her take, so she could sit and pay rent for the house that he owed money on. 'Oh,' the teenager continued, 'and I'm sure I don't need to remind you about Lucia, do I?' 'What do you mean?' Luis stated plainly. He knew all too well what his daughter meant, he simply wanted to hear it from her. Maybe he'd get the truth out of her. 'You know what I mean!' Mindy screamed, almost lunging from her chair and at her father. 'My best friend got thrown out into the streets because of someone she liked. And because that person was me, she requested to stay here for just one weekend until she could find a place to stay with other relatives. What did you do? You told her to piss off and live in the street, because you'd rather watch her die than risk me turning into- what you called- a carpet licking whore? The question shouldn't be why I ran away without a trace, dad.. it should be why didn't I run away without a trace sooner than I did?' Luis shook with anger as Mindy turned to her mother. And unexpectedly, with every venomous thing she had called her father out on, she managed to look at Arielle and smile. 'It had nothing to do with you. No matter what this heartless bastard says... it had nothing to do with you.' Arielle paused for a second. After drinking the last of her wine, she smiled back at her daughter, which made Mindy feel much better than she had expected to feel through the meeting. She was positive that her mother was going to wordlessly side with Luis once again- like she always did- but this time, it did not happen. 'I will always respect the decisions you make.. Mindy. You know that if you ever need to talk about anything, I would be happy to help.' In utter disbelief of what he was hearing, Luis stood up from his chair. 'I cannot believe you are encouraging this spoiled, disrespectful brat.' 'You know, Luis...' Mindy started in reply, standing up as well. 'I had the decency to give you what you requested from me, now it's your turn. Why didn't you tell me about my parents?' Mindy glared daggers at her father in wait for an answer. For not getting one quick enough, she yelled again. 'Why not?! For everything you put me through, why didn't you tell me?!' A knock at the door saved Luis from further humiliation. How long that safety lasted, however, was about to be tested. Seeing his means of escaping the question, Luis went for the door. Big mistake. The moment he opened it, a pair of hands firmly grabbed him by the collar of his shirt. 'You have some fucking explaining to do, buddy.' The words were spoken with such a cold, sinister touch, that it rendered Luis speechless. 'Michael... what, what are you...' Before Luis could even attempt to find the words to finish a coherent sentence, the man behind Michael chimed in. 'Where's Mindy?' The face of Markus Riley came up to the front door to join Michael.
