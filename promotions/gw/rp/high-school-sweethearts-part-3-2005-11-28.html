High School Sweethearts: Part 3

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28 The Day After Thanksgiving Fred Grest is found asleep in his chair and a half eaten turkey sandwich on his lap. He snores loudly with drool coming down his chin. A loud knock comes at the door which wakes Fred Grest up. He gets up and the turkey sandwich starts to slip. He quickly grabs it and sets it on the table. He wipes the drool off of him and gets up as someone continues to knock. 'I'm coming you inconsiderate bastard.' Fred Grest says. He walks up to the door, reaching for the handle he turns and opens it seeing Owen. Grest smirks as Owen walks inside. 'What's up Owen?' Grest asks. 'Not much man. How was your Thanksgiving?' Owen replies. 'Boring' Grest says. 'Didn't bother to visit the family again did you?' Owen asks. 'Only under the rarest of circumstances will I visit them. Remember now I've pretty much been disowned for not being what they wanted them to be.' Grest responds. Owen nods. 'I know and I completely agree with you too.' 'Yup, I don't get out they're great catholics yet racist at the same time. The homophobia I get.... there told to be.' The Original Pranksta says. 'So what did you do for Thanksgiving?' Owen asks. 'Slept alot. I drove back up from Kentucky. Had a blast with Karen and now inside the store is a life size cut out of me holding a super soaker.' Grest says. 'Cut out? Cool.' Owen replies. 'Yeah, I'll be going back there before Christmas as well.' Grest says. 'So how do you think your chances are in Tokyo?' Owen questions. 'Meh, I have no idea I'll just go in there and do my damnest to win the thing. I'm the darkhorse as always. I'll make the most of it.' Grest responds. Owen nods. 'You'll do fine' 'I know.' The Extreme One responds sharply. Something didn't seem right with The Original Pranksta at the moment. Since when has doing fine been okay with him? He's always had a certain confidence about him. He knew he was the underdog but the words on his arm defined him. Hope. He seemed to have little hop.... little desire to past what people thought where he should be at. Fred Grest almost seemed to have lost his fire. The only thing he seemed to care about right now was Karen.
