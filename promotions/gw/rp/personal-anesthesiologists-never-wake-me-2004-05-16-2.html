?| Personal Anesthesiologists: Never Wake Me |?

16 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.16.04 ---- :| And just like that, the sun peaked the next morning as if God was apologizing for the nefarious night last night. Itagaki woke to an eerie silence throughout the asylum. No screaming, no beating, no flesh ripping and blood splattering. It was abnormal for this place. Itagaki sat up as his white stringy hair flowed with his movements before steady footsteps approached his cell door. Keys rattling soon unlock the metal materialized door as SWAT team looking men bombard the room and grab Itagaki. Swiftly taking him out the room, another shot is injected into his neck resulting in him passing out as everything goes blank. |: -Later That Day: Arizona Dessert, 6:32pm- :| Itagaki's eyes slightly split open as his blurry vision struggled to focus. The intense heat reigned down over the dirt and cacti as lungs inhaled dry air. His mind slipping in and out, Itagaki soon realized he couldn't move his hands nor feet as they were tied once again. Held Jesus style bound to a cross, a group of SWAT team looking men stood in front of him as Geoff Hoon soon interjected himself through them and stood before Itagaki. Drowning in heat waves, Geoff Hoon took off his sun glasses and stared Itagaki in the eyes. |: 'Itagaki Freeze, welcome.' :| Complexity overwhelmed Itagaki's mind as of who Itagaki Freeze was. |: 'Oh, that's right, you probably don't know who that name belongs to. Well let me introduce you to your future Mr. Freeze.' Geoff paused to let Itagaki process. 'Before this, your life was filled with meaningless memories and no purpose. Now, I've given you life, a reason, a purpose for being. But ultimately it all depends on whether you accept or decline. Accept and you will live forever by my side. Decline and you will have proved you have always been dead.' :| Hearing these words, Itagaki understood them, believing them to be true. Like a child who has not yet lived by experience, Itagaki fell victim. |: 'I leave you now Itagaki. Whatever path you shall choose I will be back to collect my property.' Referring to Itagaki, Geoff continued, 'Hopefully, you do the logical thing.' :| And with that, Geoff turned and headed back to his awaiting parade of black cars. Climbing in, he soon took off along with the others down the cracked dirt crust of beaten Earth disappearing in the heat waves. As Itagaki watched with blurry eyes and a dying mentality, Itagaki spoke his final words. |: '..careless of consequence I fling my battered and beaten soul over the cliffs of merciless memories. Emptied of all life, all hope, the aftershock of vampiric intrusion. I am left an unwilling witness to the cruelties of the heart. The very fiber of my being shriveled and withered like a lone frozen rose in the winter. I condemn thee, guardians of superficial plasticity, peddlers of cheapened broken dreams. I die to live. I accept my... reincarnation...'
