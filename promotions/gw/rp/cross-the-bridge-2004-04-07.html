Cross the bridge

7 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/04 The place was Rome, Italy. The date was March 29th. Brick and Train were reluctant partners and reluctant tag team champions. They only dealt with each other when they had to, thanks to a mandate from Global Wrestling owner Bubba McCoy. One of their most recent outings as a team involved assaulting chief custodian Adam Grey. Train stood in front of the soda machine, trying to pick a soda. 'Train.' Train turned to see Brick standing right next to him. 'What the hell do you want?' Train growled 'We need to talk.' 'Look, if this is about the Rumble, we may have to be partners an' defend the titles, but tonight, all that shit goes out the window. It's every man for himself.” 'It ain't about the Rumble. Listen, I know you have some connections. I need you to get me in touch with some people.' Train paused for a moment, frozen in his tracks by a statement he never thought he'd live to hear. 'Excuse me?' 'Did I stutter?' 'Hold up, hold up...' Train said, trying to catch his breath for a moment. In the pause, Train stared into Brick's eyes trying to gauge him. What did he mean? Is he looking for the hook up on a new stereo, a flat screen... maybe a new suit or car? Naw... naw, this was more than that. When he said connections, he didn't mean hook up, he meant 'connections'. 'Mr. Black superhero himself wants me to connect him to people I know?' Train asked in a half chuckle. 'Is this a joke?' 'I don't see how,' Brick replied in a steely tone. 'The only one laughing is you.' Train paused again, taken aback for the second time. Maybe it shouldn't have been such a big deal. One phone call and Brick would have gotten what he asked for, no real skin off Train's nose. So what was the problem? Who the hell was he kidding? He knew what the problem was. 'Do you even have a clue what you're asking me for? Yeah... I know the shit between you and Salaok is serious and all that, but... You about to take this shit to a whole 'nother level that there ain't gonna be no coming down...or back from. You better think long and hard about what you say next. Watch what you ask for. You just might get it.' 'Salaok's been threatening my friends and my family for years. I need him to understand that I ain't takin' his shit no more. You know, I could have sworn, some time last week, a cat who looked just like you said we need to set an example, let cats know not to mess with us. Now I wanna do just that and you won't help me? 'Look, you need to handle your business. I ain't in it.' 'The hell you ain't in it!' Brick roared, stepping in Train's path. 'What affects one member o' this team affects the whole! Or did you forget about Salaok's little bitch Frost jumpin' you in the hallway just last week in Honolulu?' Train sucked his teeth and started to walk away. 'Donny wouldn't leave me hanging like this.' That last comment was enough to make Train stop in his tracks. No, this motherfucker did not just go there, he thought to himself. Train and Brick weren't friends by any stretch of the imagination, but this was especially true of Train and Donny McNasty. 'I'm sorry, did I hit a nerve?' Train then turned back around and looked at Brick. 'Fine,' he sighed, 'I'll call some o' my people.' Train then pulled out his cell phone and began to dial a number. 'Hope you know just what the fuck you're gettin' yourself into.' ***** ***** ***** The world quaked as the butt of the shotgun caught him in the temple. Even after the blow to the head the man still had it in his heart and balls to run. Unfortunately his body refused to cooperate beyond the point of impact. The alleyway was dank and musty, over ridden with menacing shadows. The odor of trash and other various forms of filth didn’t improve within the claustrophobic, brick lined space as the man’s limp body was lifted of it’s feet and sent careening to the side, finally crashing back to Earth through a set of three tarnished, steel trash cans. The metallic clatter of the impact echoed down the narrow, open slot between the two sides of the huge apartment complex and reverberated in the large, open, inner square that contained the trash dumpsters for the building. A moment ago all the man had really been able to hear was an amalgamation of his feet hitting the asphalt and his heart beating through his chest. Once the rattling of the trash cans died away the sound of his footsteps were replaced by the sound of the footsteps of the cat that had been chasing. As the man slowly opened his eyes to the world, every place he looked covered in a thick glowing haze he knew he was in trouble. It occurred to him he should’ve known how long he’d been laying there, but he didn’t have a clue. It had just been a second or two, but it felt more along the lines of a week as his head swam, searching for the answer. Suddenly the sound of the foot steps was covered by footsteps that were much closer. Mustering all his remaining strength the man manages to turn his head. His eyes manage to focus a bit and finally sees his assailant. A heavyset figure in black steps out of the cut, a recessed slot in the brick wall that may have been meant from some actual purpose at one time, but now only severed as a good spot to smack him upside the head without being seen. The figure hit a slow, confident swagger as he moved in the downed man’s direction. He could only lay there and try to figure out who the hell this was. Let’s see.... At a distance His frame was frame was broad shouldered and heavy set. A few steps closer and the his black denim jeans and jacket almost helped him blend into the shadows, which was likely the point. One more step and now the figure was standing over him. It was sheer force of will that cleared the haze enough to focus his eyes. The man peered up from beneath the pile of garbage that covered most of his torso into a cold, dark set of eyes that pierced as they stared right back at him. Oh shit. The man had really hoped it was the cops that had caught him blind side down here. Sure they weren’t supposed to, but it’s not like it would have been the first time. Oh, well... no such luck this time. No cops. Just Street. That’s what everyone called him anyway. It occured to the man that he didn’t even really know Street’s real name. No surprise since anyone that had ever really dealt with Street in a situation like this was far more concerned with their lives than his name. Honestly there really wasn’t much you needed to know about him. His head was completely bald while his face was covered in a thick black beard that crept down from where the sideburns would have started had he had a head of hair. His dark brown skin seemed to be etched with deep wrinkles despite the fact that he couldn’t have been much more than his early 20's, mostly due to the constant scowl he wore. It also wasn’t often you caught Street out of his self adopted uniform either. Black. Didn’t matter whether it was a suit or of the Mecca denim jean, jacket, t-shirt set he’s sporting now it was unlikely you’d find another color on him. His friends love to break his balls about it, but they all knew why. He’d been at the street game a long time. There weren’t too many people who knew much better than Street that you never know when you’ll need to slide into the cut to get away from somebody who’s after or sneak up on somebody you’re after. There weren’t too many people who knew the game period. Nobody really even new when he’d gotten down on the set. When he’s started running with the Southsiders. Whenever it was he’d made the transition from run of the mill gang banger to true to life gangsta? as the Southsiders were molded into The Southside Syndicate. Now whenever the syndicate needed someone handled. they called Street. As he layed staring up at the Chicago version of the grim reaper he didnt? need anyone to tell him what time it was. It was his time to get handled. Now the set of footsteps that had been to his rear scampered up and pulled to quick stop. The heavy breathing was a clue to how hard he’d been pursued. The man mustered up another push and turned his head back to the direction he’d come from. A tall thin man in a dark blue, double breasted suit stood with his hands on his hips, his shoulders and chest heaving. His black, silk shirt hung open to the second button revealing a thick platinum chain with a platinum cross hanging from it. The man could only lie there and chuckle to himself a little. He must’ve surely been important. They hadn’t just sicced on him, but the mouth too. JJ was the smooth talker of the crew. You could pretty much tell that before he ever even opened his mouth. Just like Street had a uniform so did JJ. If Street was was gutter... down and dirty... JJ was fly.. The cat that liked to shine. If you ever caught him out of one of his tailored suits he was known for then you best believe the jeans and shirt he was sporting hadn’t cost him less than $500. And wherever he was the Rolex or the Fossil would always let him know it was his time to shine, his response to let the platinum and diamonds shine. More than that, saying he had a silver tongue would be an insult. More likely the tounge matched the jury. Platinum. Street was the gangsta. Which means he wasn’t a terribly social individual. Nor talkative, nor communicative. That’s why, despite all the reasons they irritated to hell out of each other JJ got teamed with Street. He was everything Street was the gangsta.... JJ was the hustla?. “Muthafucka”....” JJ panted, trying to fill his lungs with the oxygen he’d lost following the man down the fire escape. 'Why the fuck yall bitches always gotta run? Yall muthafuckas see I'm in my nice suit!' It wasn’t like he wasn’t prepared for it to happen. Him and Street had the building scouted. They knew that would be the only viable way out when JJ knocked on the door. Too bad he hadn’t been on the treadmill any time recently. “What the fuck you running from nigga?” JJ demanded to know, irritated he was forced to put forth more effort than he really wanted to. “Wa.... wa... wa.........” the man muttered doing what he could to sit up and speak before Street’s black boot slammed into his chest, putting him right back down. “Wait a minute!” he managed to demand, the impact from the boot the chest seeming to knock loose some impediment to his speech. “What?” Street growled. “What the fuck you got to say” Snitch ass nigga.....” “I..... I didn’t tell “em nothin” man.” the man said in a fit of desperation, convincing himself there was something he could say that would change the inevitable. “C’mon now dogg.....” JJ said shaking his head with a smirk on his face. “You mean to tell me when the spots up 30th and Kindrek got hit that shit ain’t had nothing to do with you” You trying to tell me when the chop shop out in Wheeling got popped that wasn’t nothing you said?” “I.... I... I ain’t said shit about nothing real dogg. I swear that shit on my momma man!” “Man, fuck this nigga!” Street growls again quickly rasing the military styled, tactical shot gun before having it slapped back down by JJ. “What the fuck is wrong with you nigga”!” JJ demands to know in a muffled yell. “We done already made enough noise son. What you wanna do send up a signal flair or some shit”!” “What I wanna do is spread his nigga all up on the wall.” “Hey, I didn’t do nothing yo! I didn’t do shit! I ain’t told the cops nothing” “Oh, for real?” JJ questioned, slowly turning back to him with the same trademark smirk. “So let me get this straight. Two days ago you get busted with two keys in your trunk. Yo” monkey ass already got two strikes on you. Next thing anybody knows you back out on the street laying low in the second apartment you rent in another name that you think nobody knows about. But you ain’t said shit?” “I swear to God......” the man begins, before every breathe in his lungs is knocked out, not by an impact, but by the sight of the shot gun not 2 feet from his face. “Tell the Devil that when you get to hell bitch.” Street rumbles, his finger poised on the trigger. Suddenly the man’s chest pumps out 3 violent sprays of red liquid. The silencer on JJ’s 9mm turns what would be loud pops into harsh whispers. Street jumps back wiping the blood from the barrel of his shotgun. “What the fuck nigg”!” Street bellows as he turns to JJ, still pointing the smoking pistol at Chicago’s newest murder victim. “I was just about to handle my fuckin” business so why you all up in mine?!” “Nigga, we just went through that” JJ responds as he tucks the pistol back behind his back and heads down the alley way into the darkness. Street just stands for a moment glancing down at his would-be quarry before turning to JJ as his form grows smaller as he moves further away. “One of these days I’ma whup yo” ass son.” Street says before following suit, disappearing into the shadows. ***** ***** ***** The delivery man steps off his truck and trots up the stone steps to the front door. His hand quickly taps on the door and he waits. He uses his electronic pad to scan the bar code, on the small, brown box, getting it prepped for delivery. Finally after a few moments he hears the locks on the door popping open. The delivery man puts on his normal smile, looking straight ahead to lock eyes with whoever comes out to get what’s been sent to him. The door swings open and the recipient steps out. The delivery man has to tilt his head back and the smile seems less a greeting than a stunned reaction at the sheer size of the man he’s looking for. Brick looks down at the much shorter man with a much with a small grin that passes for a broad, friendly smile on the visage of “The CEO of Ruckus Inc.” “Package for a.... Jamal Randall.” “That’s me.” “Sign here sir.” Brick quickly grabs the stylus and signs his name on the line visible on the screen of the electronic pad and the brown box is dropped into his massive, free hand. The delivery man thanks him and invites him to have a good day before heading back to his truck. Brick examines the box a bit before stepping back inside. “What the hell is this?” Brick asks himself, not expecting any kind of delivery today. Brick pulls the box open, spilling a few of the packing peanuts onto the floor before finding a small, silver cell phone. He wants to ask what the hell again, but he doesn’t have much time as it starts to vibrate in his hand. Brick just stares at the phone a few moments, suddenly feeling like he’s in an episode of the Twilight Zone. Reluctantly Brick flips the phone open and presses it to his ear. “Who this?” Brick asks bluntly, not much caring for the theatrics of all of this. “Your new best friend.” the voice on the other grumbles. Brick pauses for a moment, his face curling up in annoyance. He knows who it is, but it damn sure ain’t his best friend. “Train...... What the hell is all this?” “What’s all what?” “The delivery and the phone man. What the hell is all this?” “Oh, that” That’s the game man. You wanted to play it and now we’re gonna play it right.” “Uhuh...... And just how we supposed to do that?” “Simple. We play it safe. First off, the phone you just got” It’s basicly disposable. Nobody will be able to find my call to you on phone records on this phone cuz it’s prepaid and they dont? have records. So... when this conversation is over you’re gonna walk your ass down the street and throw it in the dumpster. Got it? Cuz if you don’t the game stops here.” “Man, I got it.” “Good. Now....... I’m gonna do you a huge favor right now.” “Yeah, and what’s that?” “I’m gonna give you one last chance..... to walk away.” “You know what” I don’t need no damn morality lessons coming from you.” “Maybe not, but you damn sure need a reality check! Look.... I know I ain’t the voice of restraint or nothing like that, but..... Look man. I went through whole lot of shit to get out of the crap you’re asking me to get you into. More than that... you’re about to take something that’s in the arena and take it to the streets. Dogg.... there are some bridges that once you cross” You ain’t got to burn them. They burn themselves behind you. You’re about to escalate this shit to a level you won’t be able to bring it back down from. You do this... you use the dudes I got for you... There ain’t no going back. Now... I’m asking you one more time. You sure you wanna do this?” “Let me tell you something. I don’t give half a damn if Salaok puts his hands on me. Hurts me. Attacks ME. But see, now” He’s threatened my friend and my family. I didn’t take the shit here, he did. Now let me ask you... if somebody took it there with you.... What would you do?” There’s a long pause before anyone says another word. “Ain’t dogg.” Train says somberly. “It’s on you now.” With that the call ends, the shrill buzz of a dial tone taking over until Brick flips the phone closed. Suddenly there’s another knock at the door. Brick quickly moves over and swings the door wide open. There, on his front stoop stand two men. One heavy set with a thick bushy beard, a cold expression on his face as he stands like a dark shadow in his black suit. The other is tall and slender, his silvery suit shining in the bright sunshine as Brick sees his relection in the man’s shades. “Yeah?” Brick asks, narrowing his eyes at the two rather unsavory looking strangers. “Yeah....” begins the man in the silvery suit. “I’m JJ. This is Street. A homeboy of ours said you needed a favor........”
