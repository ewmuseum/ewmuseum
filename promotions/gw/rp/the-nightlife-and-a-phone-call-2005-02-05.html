The nightlife and a phone call

5 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/5/2005 Nightlife is once more full of life. Life of people enjoying what they have with their significant others. Then there is those who wish to find that one thing that is lacking in their life...love. Love...noone knows what love really is until it is taken away from them. Recently, that has been the case for several of the Harvesters but none most than Kullypso and Salaok. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 'If I may Salaok?' Kullypso asks The Harvester of Minds as he stands infront his respected chair. With a nod from Salaok, Mark begins to talk. 'As you all know there has been a disturbance in my personal life but has been linked with us. For the past four months, Syn has disappeared with no notification of her whereabouts and with the abduction of my two children. I will raise the price if need be, but I need more information on the whereabouts of Syn but more importantly have the kids be brought back to they belong...With me!' With that said Mark sits back in his chair and puffs on a cigarette that is infront of him. As Quartz stands up next and begins to speak his business. Mark glances over and examines the entire room and his fellow Harvesters. Salaok seems like he is in his own world. Flare and Jackson Diasporo seem to be interested and taking notes when Quartz said something important. Silent Wind has his arms crossed and seems like he has other business to attend to. Afer about ten minutes of Quartz speaking, he takes his seat and taps the Harvester of Minds on his shoulder so he could end the meeting. As the meeting is over, Kullypso is seen leaving the meeting at Nightclub Nevermore. While the rest of the Harvesters stay and live the life that most would wish to have (money, the girls, and the rides). Kullypso leaves the nightclub with a half-lit cigarette in his hand and enters an awaiting black limousine. As the driver closes the door and makes his way over to the driver's side, Mark reminisces about the past four months. The past is the only thing he can think of now since the disappearance of his entire family. The past is slowly taking control of everything...his thoughts, his actions, his ability to perform in the ring, and recently his business decision out of the ring as Comissioner of Freedom Alliance. Pulling out of the parking lot, Mark takes out some papers that need to be reviewed and glances over them. As the limousine stops, Mark's cell phone begins to ring. Picking up his phone Mark takes a look at the number and not recognizing the number, Mark lays the phone back down. picks his phone back up dialing a number. Holding the phone to his ear waiting for an answer on the other side, Mark begins to drive down the long stretch of highway. Reaching a voice message on the phone Mark leaves a message. 'Hey I need to know what's the latest that you got on the plan. Call me back when you get something. You got the number.' Putting the phone to his side, Mark picks up a folder with contracts that need to be looked over before the next show. While reviewing the fine print of one contract his phone begins to ring. Picking the phone up and realizing the number is from someone he was expecting, Mark switches the phone over to speaker phone. 'So what information do you have for me?' 'Well...all I can find out is that....' The phone begins to cut in and out while Mark can catch only bits and pieces of what the person on the other end of the conversation had to say. Mark clicks the phone off. As the limousine begins to stop Mark gathers his belongings and sorts the papers. When the door opens Mark steps out and heads to the front door of his mansion. As he unlocks the door and opens it halfway, the phone rings yet again. Mark closes the door behind him as he answers the phone.
