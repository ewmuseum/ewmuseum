A New Dawn

6 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th November 2004 Pain... It goes hand in hand with suffering. In the GW we are used to pain and suffering, we are used to having matches where we are thrown around the ring like a rag doll. We are used to being beaten until we are nothing but a bloody pulp. However what most of us are not ready for no matter how many times it happens to us, is the pain of losing. To be defeated is dealing with the fact that in that particular match you met someone who had you scouted. A man who obviously knew you inside out defeated you. It hurts but you have to rise above it and carry on. That is what I have always believed, I have had many matches in my life and I have had my fair share of defeats. Each time I was able to shrug it off and carry on, I say to myself that I wasn't fully prepared but I will be next time. Defeat always taught me to keep my feet on the ground; defeat taught me that I can not always be at my best. I have never let a defeat get to me...that is until I faced Jackson... I went into that match knowing that I was facing a monster, knowing that Jackson could break my back with one swift move, so I trained extra hard I added a few more impact moves to my arsenal so that I could fight power with power. I went through every counter I knew and added some more. I was ready for that match; I was ready to show Jackson that although he is a beast of a man he can still be taken down like any other man. You all saw me throw myself into the match, giving Jackson everything I got. At times I had Jackson down, I had him scream in pain, but he kept coming so I continued my attack and brought him down a few more times. Then suddenly it turned and Jackson was the one on the offensive, smashing me to the ground with one blow and breaking my body in two with a simple suplex. Then it was all over and as I lay there, my body aching I was fine, I accepted the defeat, that was until I saw Jackson walk over to Brandi Sights without any difficulty, not even looking out of breath. In fact Jackson had no signs that he had been in a match at all apart from a little bit more sweat than usual. That is what really got to me, I had that guy screaming in pain at one time and there he was not showing any emotion or any exhaustion as he ask Brandi out, yet again! As I picked myself up and made my way to the back, fans shouted at me that I would get him next time and that they still loved me etc but I didn't even acknowledge them. My mind was racing with questions, had I actually given Jackson my best? Did I hold back? As I sat in my locker room it soon dawned on me, perhaps I did hold back, perhaps I wanted to save myself after my exhausting match at Dark Rituals. Subconsciously I had held myself back because of Rachel, because of our baby. I didn't want to spend another month in hospital and worry Rachel again. But I am Adam Myers! I live by the saying, 'Take The Risk, Forget Everything Else!' I can't hold myself back, not anymore, I need to step it up, I need to take more risks, and I need to become vicious in my matches. I face another monster of a man at the next Evolution, Brick, who is a veteran in the GW and is one tough man to beat. If I am to beat Brick I can't hold anything back... 'Again!' SMACK! 'Again!' SMACK! 'Again!' 'I don't want to Adam!' 'God damn it James, hit me again or so help me I will kick your ass from pillar to post. Is that what you want?' 'No.' 'Then hit me again!' SMACK! I was in my gym in a private area I had reserved after Evolution. I was with a wrestler in training, James. I had asked him for his help and he was more than willing to lend a hand, of course I hadn't told him what I needed him for but he didn't seem to care. When he found out what it was he seems apprehensive but I was able to talk him round... You see the reason I needed James was because after Evolution and after my revelation, I had decided to do something about it. So I asked James to hit me with a standardised ringside steel chair. Now this may sound crazy to you but it does have logic to it. After the beating Jackson had given me, it made me feel like I couldn't even take a punch from an opponent. Therefore I decided to test my stamina and recovery by being hit by a steel chair. I believe that if I could keep getting up from a steel chair shot then there was no punch in the earth that could keep me down. It seems a stupid idea but during my time in Japan I learned that it is all a matter of mind over body. If I tell myself to get up and will it then it will happen, no matter how much my body aches if my mind get pass the pain then I can keep going. SMACK! James landed the 20th chair shot and I fell to my knees. My body was screaming with pain begging me not to get up to stop this madness. However my mind was screaming just as loud to get back on my feet and take another. Unsteadily I got back on my on feet and looked James in the eyes. His face was full of concern as he sheepishly held the chair, which had multiple dents in it from my head and body. 'Again!' I said my voice now gravely and showing the pain I was in. 'Please Adam stop this!' James pleaded. 'Again' I screamed pushing passed the pain again. SMACK! The chair landed right across my head as I fell to the floor. James was only a kid of 18 and was quite scrawny but he still packed a hell of a punch with the steel chair. I crumbled to the floor again, my body now shaking with pain. Nevertheless my mind screamed at me to get back up and slowly but surely I got back up. James again gave me the same look he had given me the last 20 times, I didn't say anything and simply nodded. James took a big gulp and lifted the chair above his head... 'WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON HERE?!?!' Came a scream from the entrance of the private gym. I took a quick glance at James who had now dropped the chair to the mat and looked relieved that someone had finally interrupted this madness. I painfully looked to my side to see Peter Willows standing at the entranceway with a face of thunder. He looked at me and then at James as he stormed towards the ring. 'I asked a question Adam! What the hell is going on here?' 'Go away Pete.' I said knowing that if I explained it to him he wouldn't understand. 'No I won't Adam, not until you explain this to me,' Pete was looking at me with such intensity that I was glad when he switched his gaze to James, 'James, Terry is looking for you he wants to talk about your next match.' 'Thanks Pete.' James said thankful that he had an excuse to leave. He quickly rolled out of the ring and ran to the exit towards the rest of the gym not looking back. I moved over to the ropes and leaned on them as Pete jumped onto the edge of the outside. 'Before you say it Pete, I have my reasons. So I don't want to hear any lectures from you.' 'Well at least tell me what is going on here?' 'James was just helping me.' 'With what? Help give you brain damage?' 'Don't be stupid Pete! I told you I have my reasons.' 'What reasons Adam?' I moved over to the corner of the ring where there was a piece of paper. I picked it up and handed it over to Pete. 'That is this week's Evolution card.' I said as I handed it over. 'What on earth is the Tournament Of The Worthy?' 'Did you not see last week's show?' 'I only saw your match.' I cringed as the memory of my match flashed before my eyes. 'Well, Maverick Reno has been stripped of the World Title and by orders of Bubba McCoy, six GW fighters have been chosen as the worthy to lay claim to the World Title, and apparently I am one of those worthy.' 'That is fantastic Adam!' Peter shouted at the top of his voice. 'My god you could be World Champion by the end of this month!' 'Slow down there Pete! First look who I am facing.' Peter's face dropped slightly he looked down at the paper again. 'Oh, Brick.' 'Exactly, Brick! A veteran to the GW, a guy who had held the World title, a guy who if I am not ready for will tear me apart!' 'That still doesn't explain why you had James smack you over the head with a steel chair!' 'You saw my match against Jackson right?' 'Yeah.' 'Well you saw me throw everything at that monster and yet still he was able to beat me, and do you know why?' 'Why?' 'Because I held back, because I let his moves hurt me. Brick is going to destroy me if I do not know how to take such powerful moves.' 'Adam,' Peter said obviously understanding, 'You are one of the most skilled wrestlers I have ever known, not only that but you are the biggest daredevil in the business. You take risks and you don't care. Jackson was just lucky that is all! Believe me next time you will beat him!' 'I can't take that risk Pete! If I want to be champion then I have to break the boundary, I have to become so extreme that nothing will be able to keep me down.' I stepped down from the ring and onto the floor, Peter jumped down also and looked me straight in the eye. 'Please Adam listen to reason.' Peter said showing the same concern as James earlier. 'I have made up my mind Pete. From this day forth when it comes to the GW I will live by my saying, 'Take The Risk, Forget Everything Else!' This is a new Adam Myers, Pete. An Adam Myers that will stop at nothing. An Adam Myers that is going to prove why he is worthy!' I nodded at Peter and picked up my kit bag as I made my way towards the exit. 'Please Adam!' Came Peter's last plea. But it fell on deaf ears as I continued walking towards the exit with only six words going through my head... Take The Risk, Forget Everything Else!
