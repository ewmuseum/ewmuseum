'The Journey' (Part 1/Zack's POV)

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-6-04 Tuesday September 28th, 2004 7:30 A.M. Another early morning, another bad night? Doesn’t sound good does it? It almost sounds like the team of Curtis and Johnson are on the ropes already huh? Here’s the strange thing of it all: Its not as bad as you think! This is the time that our beloved Zack Johnson of a team called “The Connection” wakes up everyday and this morning he woke up from a sleep that included him remembering a night years ago in which he had a messy break up with his former boyfriend? September 19th, 1991 Well it was a pretty typical day for our good friend in Zack Johnson in San Francisco, California as he was up and dressed before the hour of 8 in the morning and mainly because of the fact that he was programmed to be that way by his family and his boyfriend lived and no his name wasn’t Joshua Curtis but rather Brian Watson, a slender, blonde haired 18 year old that Zack met at the library one day and things had clicked! Now do I mind telling you that Brian was a little demanding? No but at the time Zack thought nothing of it and if you ever heard the Eve song “Love is Blind” then you’d get it! Zack was going to get some breakfast and he had seen a light next to the “message” on the answering machine in his house and the message went something like this: “Hey baby its me Brian. Listen I am at class right now and I didn’t want to wake you because you looked sooo cute asleep’anyways don’t worry about me and I’ll see you later!” Now did this kind of make things uneasy? No but then again Zack was young and naive back then (ok so he’s not that much older now but hey) and the fact is that he loved this young man with all his heart because well’he didn’t know any better! At about 1:30 in the afternoon Zack went to San Francisco University where he was going to pick up Brian so the two could go get a late lunch before Brian was to go back to class, but it was at that time that Zack found out that his tender heart could be betrayed by someone that he cared about! Now what could possibly have happened? Well it just so happens that when Zack got to the campus off the bus he usually would see Brian sitting at a bench on the lawn waiting for him and when he didn’t see Brian he kind of panicked and decided to go looking for his lover. Five Minutes later and Zack heart would be torn to pieces! It appears to us that poor, misguided Brian decided that playing with one’s emotions would be the best thing to do and he played with the emotions of Zachary Johnson with such skill that no one could figure out that he was doing it’except for Zack that is. And that brings us to the present? Zack had pretty much forgotten about what Brian had done and that was a good thing. The fact was that Joshua was perfect for him because he understood completely about the heartbreak that comes from a betrayal but I’m not about to bore you with that little side story! As Zack got up and dressed for the day ahead thoughts of his mother ran through his head and that almost brought him to tears except for the fact that he promised Joshua that he would remain strong for him and I think its safe to say that he has done that without hesitation! Today Zack was to go back to San Francisco to go see his younger sister Valerie at the San Francisco Memorial Hospital which is where his mother is staying after suffering a major migraine headache the made her really sick! While Josh knew about it Zack kept some things hidden from his younger boyfriend because for now he didn’t want to worry Joshua with his problems! When Zack got to the airport there were a lot of fans wearing shirts that had “OSC” on them but then there were also the fans that had the new “Connection” logo on them and Zack signed the autographs with a heavy heart knowing that a woman that meant a lot to him was in the hospital. As Zack went onto the plane he closed his eyes and went to sleep knowing that someone that he cared deeply about was sick and he didn’t know what brought it on! As the plane touched down a few hours later Zack first went to the hotel that he was to be staying at and truthfully I don’t think he did it with a lot of conviction! The next day was the day that while Zack was looking forward to it made him also kind of nervous but mainly because his sister DIDN’T know the truth! Now Zack made it a point to go to the hospital first thing in the morning and while he was mainly there to see his mother he had to tell Valerie the truth also. “Valerie” Zack began “There is something that I think you should now about me: I’m gay.” Zack told Valerie and while the shock was there for a little while it was all gone within like 5 minutes! “I know Zack. I thought that there was something wrong when you fell out of love with that girl that you had a crush on and well it also didn’t hurt that you had that boyfriend in Brian before the incident happened!” Valerie told Zack whom was the most relieved homosexual that you will ever find in the history of mankind! An hour and a half later and Zack called Joshua which he did for a couple of reasons and one of them was because he was doing a “Best Damn Sports Show” the next day and I know you wanna know how that went but you’ll have to wait until later to find out how that went! Au Revoir ;)
