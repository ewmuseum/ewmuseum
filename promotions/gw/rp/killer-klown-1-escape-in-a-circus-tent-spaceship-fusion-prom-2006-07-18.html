Killer Klown #1: Escape in a Circus Tent Spaceship? (Fusion Promo) Killer Klown Global Wrestling Promo #2591 Date: 07/18/06 Location: Darth von Doom's Desert Palace Dungeon Storeroom Inside the deep, dark bowels of Darth von Doom's Desert Palace, I am in the dungeon digging something up. It looks like a big circus tent. Alas, it is a big circus tent. It's my old spaceship. It has been hidden here for months since before I became Dark Harlequin. That means nothing to me now. Now, I know, I must go back home. I can see the tattered and torn edges of my ship and the burn marks from when we plummetted down into the dense atmosphere of this planet. That was when me and some others went on a fact finding mission for the museum of my home planet. Then, I got lost and stranded here, with all the fuck-ups and rejects of the planet. The humans are what they call themselves. I merely call them food. Few are my friends. The few who are, however, will not be eaten. The rest are fair game. Such is the life of a man-eating cannibal clown. I dig through rubbish that has been down here for god knows how long. I hate the trash that covers my ship. I wonder how long von Doom's kept all this stuff down here? There's boxes filled with wrestling videos, books, and other assorted goodies lying all around me. Such filth. Yet, this is prettier than half the things I see walking the streets. The trash and scum that live in this city have become unbearable. I pull parts of my ship out faster now. I can start to make out the sides of it. The circus tent ship is what it is. My vehicle, my transport, my home away from home. My prison. It is like a prison, keeping me here until I can fix it up and go fly back to the stars from wench I came. Oh, how I used to relish the days where we would fly here and eat the unsuspecting humans but now, all is lost. At least, until I fix it and leave this God forsaken planet before it's too late. YES! That's what I have to do. Fix the ship and go before they find me! Before HE finds me. von Doom, the one who I tormented for so long and the man who now has an evil grip over me. I must escape from him. Captain Jack Morgan is another matter entirely. He is my best friend. I can't leave him here. He took me under his wing, made me a champion. NO! They'd eat him when we arrive on my home planet. I might eat him on the way there. SHIT! There's no way to win at this game of life. What should I do? I have to think. My best friend could be eaten and he also, he may not want to leave. There's rum here and not on my planet. CRAP! Now I don't want to leave. I'm thinking too much now. Don't think clown, just dig. And that's what I keep on doing, throwing dust everywhere as I get all the pieces of my ship together and place them on the floor beside the core of the tent. After I'm done, I look over everything and nod my head. 'Yep,' I mutter under my breath so that one of von Doom's goons doesn't hear me. 'That's everything.' I look over and see a box of tools lying in the corner. I go over and grab it. Then, the work begins. I must leave this place. I HAVE to leave this place, for my own sanity. I'm an outsider looking in, like so many on this world. I always was and always will be. And that's why I must go, before they kill me out of spite. (/To be continued.../)
