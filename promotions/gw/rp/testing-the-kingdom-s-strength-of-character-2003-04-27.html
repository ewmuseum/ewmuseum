Testing the Kingdom's strength of character

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Testing the Kingdom's strength of character Quartz Global Wrestling Promo #155 Date: 4/27/03 Location: Street The Lair had infiltrated his life at an early age and throughout the years, there has only ever been one time in which he regretted it. One time that something happened because of his connections with the underground crime group that had dominated the hidden world for so long, even then. But these days, he wasn't just another warrior of the Lair, he was a corner stone of it. He was the man who seemingly held the keys that no one knew about. The night air was cold and seemed stale as the smoke from the fires, which burnt through the town, clogged up the skies. Around a corner and in the darkness he waits. Waits for one man. One man who's hope and fight still exist. He had to find it. He had to find what made these people so much different to the next man. He had to find the key to The Kingdom which had been named as Tragic for so long. His hand resting against the brick wall, tilted backwards letting the smoke trickle out of his mouth and into the dark air. His hand wrapped around a single red rose, squeezing it tightly as the thorns push deeper and deeper into his hand. His eyes blanks as he stares forward give no sign of the pain which must be related with the blood with drops to the ground from his fist. Footsteps in the distance took Quartz's attention and his grip lessened slightly, pulling the thorns out of the skin and opening the wounds even greater. He drops the cigarette down to the ground and it burns there for a while before his boot gently presses down on it, making sure that no sounds arose and let his unknowing victim, suspect he was there. The footsteps become louder and louder. With every step, the unknowing comes closer to the knowing. For a brief second, Quartz closes his eyes and clears his mind of the things which haunt him. The things which have made him who he is. He closes his eyes to shake the images of the eyes of those who have died in his hands. Foot steps, louder. Breathing becomes audible. A shadow creeps across the ground as the street light behind him still burns deep into the air. Footsteps all most around the corner. BAM. Quartz's hand reaches out directly beside him, grabbing the unknowing man by the neck and pulling him into the alleyway. With eyes wide open, the man looks at Quartz with fear which brings a smile to Quartz's face. He had found one. Vincent had tried to explain this to Quartz, but until now, he didn't understand that it could still exist within the world that the corrupt rule and the innocents have no choice but to play a part of. But here it was, right in his hand. A living example that those of this Kingdom, do feel as though they have something to protect and that along was enough for Quartz to take the advantage of. The man's head cracks back against hte wall of the giant building which appears run down and useless in it's age as a small flick knife replaces the rose in Quartz's other hand and shoots forward to the man's neck. 'Beg.' Quartz's voice was clear as day and his order was a shock to the man. If he begged, would he be let go? If he begged, would it only make the event more fun for the giant who holds his feet of the ground and his head against a wall. 'Do you want to die?' 'N, n, no.' The man's responce was fast and predictable. 'Then tell me, why should I let you live? What is it you have to live for?' 'My wife and daughter sir. Please don't hurt me.' Quartz allowed a smile to creep across his face in the darkness, knowing that the man who barely be able to see it. For the man had began to beg, and better still had given Quartz the information he required to prove that those who have fight left, are those who can be controlled by the underground groups, like so many others. 'So, you have a wife and daughter? How nice. Do you love them?' The voice coming from Quartz, seemed almost sorry for a mere second before turning back into the harsh low toned voice which was his usual. 'Oh I do love them. They are everything to me.' 'Everything you say.' 'They are the reason I get up and go to work in the morning. They are the one's who I live for. I work for them, so they can eat and have the things that they need in life.' Quartz's eyes roll as the man spits forth his little rant which gains no attention from the giant anyway. 'Well Steve, do I have a surprise for you!' The man's eyes open in shock. Looking around to see if this is some sort of joke by those assholes at his workplace, Steve's face changes to one with confusion painted onto it. 'You, know my name! How? Is this some sort of joke?' 'Well, depends what kind of jokes you like now doesn't it. For me, it's great laughs, for you however, I doubt it will be. But I know your name because I know your daughter.' The confusion was straight away replaced by fear as the giant lifts the man a little higher, cutting off his air way's a little bit more. The knife is removed from Steve's neck and points off to the side but nothing can be seen. Suddenly a light shines on the area and standing there are two figures bundled up on the ground and two more figures standing above them. The two on the ground are unknown, though one is a man, and the other is a female, both who are young of age. The two figures standing are Mark Kullypso and Grendel who stand side by side with smiles on their faces. The Harvesters of Pain and Suffering. Quartz's voice turns from one of deep tone, to one of slight cockiness. 'Well well. What do we have here? Well Steve, it looks like it's your beloved daughter, and her beloved boy friend.' The man cuts Quartz off quickly with a threatening tone. 'Don't you hurt them.' 'I would never dream of it Steve. No, you see, I am going to leave that up to you.' Keeping Steve pushes up against the wall with his feet dangling a few inches off the ground, Quartz slides his along, close to the two hostages. Finally as they drawn near, Quartz throws Steve to the ground between the two who now are seen to be bound and gagged. 'What do you want of me?' Steve lets out as he crawls to his knees with tears in his eyes. From behind him, Kullypso rests a handgun with a silencer attatched to it, into Steve's hands. Quartz steps forward into the light, allowing Steve to see his fully for the first time. Quartz long black hair framing his face in the darkness, giving him a darker look than normal. His eyes, red and glowling in the light focus on Steve and do not move. 'This is what I want Steve. I want you to kill your darling baby girl's boyfriend here.' 'NEVER!' Steve's reply is fast and direct. 'That's mighty rude of you there Steve, I hadn't finished what I was saying. You see, there is but a small situation we have here. I thought you would refuse the offer to kill the boy, so I let the two men behind you have guns. You can either shoot Chris here, or you, your daughter and Chris will all be shot dead where you rest. The choice is simple, shoot Chis, or watch as we execute them and then feeling the bullet destroy you.' Silence falls over the area as Steve knows not what to do. Does he kill a teenage boy, or does he let all three of them die, leaving his wife alone in the world? A choice which causes tears, not only to flow, but to run a river over his face as his head turns from one side of his to the other. The eyes of his daughter and Chris, wide open and full of fear. How could he choose? He turns his head up to face Quartz and looks at him through his tear blurred eyes. 'Will you hide his body?' Nodding Quartz responds. 'Of course we will. No one will ever know about this other than you and your daughter.' That seemed to seal the deal and the fate of the young man at the same time. He couldn't let his daughter die. She would be able to grow up and find other boy friends, but what Steve didn't yet know, was she would never find anyone to replace Chris. Rising to his feet, he looks at his daughter before mouthing the words, 'I love you.' Turning to Chris, the boy-friend of his beloved daughter he looks him straight in the eye and raises the gun. The gun shakes from side to side, not allowing Steve to get a clean shot and finally it drops to the ground as Steve drops to his knees to pick it up. 'Steve?' Quartz's booming voice gains the attention of the broken man and he turns around to see Quartz standing there with a gun to the head of his daughter. 'Do it or she dies.' Fumbling with the gun, he finally gets a grip on it and slowly gets to his feet. Walking around behind Chris, as to avoid anymore eye contact, he grips the gun with both hands. 'Sorry Chris. Forgive me as I have no other choice.' BANG Chris lays on the ground with a hole in the back of his head, in a pool of his own blood, spredding quickly across the ground. Quartz looks at Grendel and them Kullpso and nods before turning away and beginning to walk off. But he stops, and although he doesn't turn around to look at the howling Steve, breaking down mentaling on the side of the alleyway, he speaks to him. 'Steve, I must let you know. Chris and your daughter were on the way home when we found them. Tonight they were going to tell you and your wife something but I feel as though I might as well tell you now and ruin the surprise.' Quartz pauses for a second, thinking for the best way to phase what he has to say. 'They are expecting a child. Steve, you just killed your grandson's father.' The howling escaping Steve's lungs increases as Quartz falls back into stride, walking off away from the mess, smiling to himself. This man was just like all of the others. Just because he is down in the lower regions of public view, doesn't mean he doesn't have things which he is willing to fight for. And in his willingness to fight, he opens himself up to be used. 'Vicent, your army of the Kingdom are just like everyone else. Fighting for what they believe in and in doing so they allow themselves to be corrupted. Just like me. Vincent, whether you like it or not, I am home.'
