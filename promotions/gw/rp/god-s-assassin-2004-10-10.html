God's Assassin

10 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-10-2004 As he walked down the crowded street, Pendragon’s hand constantly rested upon the hilt of his scabbarded blade. He eyed the milling masses warily, and because of his garb: golden armor, flowing black cloak and a sword’Doesn’t quite jibe with modern day attire. Pendragon is totally conscious’No face misses his gaze, after all?”. “Any one of these ignorants could serve the Shadow. That man over there selling pretzels’The little boy with the ice cream. The Shadow is not very selective when it comes to its servants; just so long as they have a soul. That is the price. And times have not been the best for the Lord King here lately. Seems after my attack on Sims, the entire dark population knows of me’If they know not mine face, they know me as I am. Constant guard is the key to staying alive. As with any battle, the man who keeps his head, keeps his head.” ***************************FLASH*************************** Alone in the darkened street, Pendragon strolled confidently. After all, who would be so brazen as to bring an attack to the Lord King? Suddenly, the sound of footsteps is heard; MANY footsteps. Pendragon barely has time to draw steel and whirl around to face a charging mob. He gasps as he looks into eyes that are devoid of emotion’Men women and children charge him, clubs and bats, knives and rocks in their hands. The mob is one, their intention is to run him down. So many faces in the mob, and only one blade. Without thinking, the Void drops over Pendragon and he begins the dance. He wades into the mob as it envelops him. Each slash drops an enemy’Each thrust doubles over one of these poor souls. The mob, though, is not without its own strength. Sheer numbers dictate that Pendragon will take wounds. A gash bleeds on his forearm, and a slash has gouged his cheek. A hundred small wounds cover him’In the Void, he pays them no mind. Finally, the mob begins to give way a bit. Even mindless followers can be made to see that their efforts are futile. At last, a lone warrior emerges from the group? “I could not believe it. The small fellow approached me slowly, his eyes full of fear. He reminded me of Benjamin, but that was another life. Slowly, my blade lowered, almost as if on it’s own accord. The child drew closer and closer’Soon, he stared up at me, sadness and fear mixed within his gaze. In an instant, the look changed?” The boy’s face becomes a snarl as he pulls a dagger from behind him. Lulled into unwitting softness, Pendragon barely has time to see it. The dagger streaks toward his thigh, the tip smoking with the poison that is meant to be his doom. All of this was but a ruse to allow this young soldier of the Night to get close enough to plant this tainted blade. Just as the blade is about to strike home, Pendragon strikes out with Lucien. The small head is separated from small body and the boy falls harmlessly to the ground? “I watched it all with horror. “Twas not mine fault. Mine hand is trained to protect mineself against whatever danger there may be, and this was certainly a danger. Damn the bastards’Sending a small boy against me. The Shadow seeks weakness and builds upon it. It preys upon it’Mine weakness is the weak. I have always had a spot in mine heart for those who would need to be protected. The boy was one, and he nearly killed me?” Gasping, the crowd rushes forward, and in a fury Pendragon is driven under the moving mass of bodies. He cannot be seen as the pile moves this way and that, occasionally dropping a limp carcass from the group. A low roar can be heard from inside, and finally, with a scream of both anguish and triumph, Pendragon throws the Shadowsouled from his back and stands amongst the ruined flesh as a shining colossus. Blood stains his blade and his clothes. He staggers as he views the devastation he has wrought, yet it does not last for long. A hard look drops over his eyes and He looks at the dead as though they are nothing’In a way, they no longer are? ***************************FLASH************************* “The penalty for failing the Shadow is more than thy life. An eternity in hell awaited those who died upon mine blade. None better for them, and they deserve no less than to burn for all eternity, to die a thousand deaths, remembering each as if it were yesterday. The Shadow has no heart’It is but blackness that does battle with the light. Souls lost trouble mine soul, and yet, victory over evil gives mine plight strength.” Those thoughts and remembrances make him even more wary as he walks along the sidewalk, seeming to look for something. At last, he stops before a hugely tall building? “Computech” he mutters to himself?“Sims” company?” He smiles as he passes this building by. A job already finished, and good riddance to bad rubbish. He had a list, and there were many on the list. Each man bore a punishment, and he was to dole it out. He had never been a mercenary or an assassin, yet now he felt like one?“God’s Assassin?” He says this to himself and stops, laughing. It builds slowly, but soon he is roaring laughter right there on the sidewalk. Passersby skirt him with even more room than they normally give him’His eyes hold an insane light, and even those who might know him for what he is dare not to disturb him in this mindset. One man, however, is different. A slight man, balding and gray haired, approaches the massive laughing man on the street. He touches his elbow and immediately the point of Lucien is pressed against his neck’Unafraid, he speaks softly? “My name is Derek De Santos’I know some things’I am supposed to share them with you?” Pendragon lowers the blade and follows the fellow up the sidewalk. Finally, they stop at a small shop? Derek’s Old Books Quaint name, if simple, and the two enter. Derek turns the sign to closed and walks into the recesses of books. Pendragon smells and his eyes light up’The thought of so many books, many of them archaic, excites him. He has always been an intellect, and would prefer knowledge to strength any day’He also realizes that knowledge is strength, and that the two go hand in hand. Derek leads him to a small table and takes a seat at one end. He motions to a small pot and Pendragon smiles? “Coffee’It has been awhile since last I had a good cup?” “The Joe’s alright here, but my conversation is gonna be better”.I assure you, My Lord King?” Pendragon almost slips at the title, but he takes a cup and sits down?“Speak, man’I would have what thee know.” “Namenlos is a demon. He is straight from hell’And I see that you a champion of the light’Jackson is a champion of the Darkness’The difference is that you are a champion among men as well. Jackson is not. You are a champion twice over. It is your humanity that will lead you to triumph. Jackson is a beast. You are not. The shadow is twisted and it twists those that it gets ahold of”.The Light preserves?.” Pendragon takes a sip and gives Derek a look that seems to say “This coffee tastes like shit!” He grimaces but speaks?“Derek, thy coffee reminds me of the time that I had to survive a desert on mine own urine’You say that I can beat him because I am human” But as an imperfect being, why is that a strength? What is it about man that can defeat the Shadow? Is it not all the Light?” “The Shadow is hungry’You have something it wants. A few somethings in your case. You have prominence’Your two titles are enough to have the Darkness drooling’You also have your soul. That is what the Shadow covets more than anything’Plus, you are a Champion of the Light. You are set to do battle against evil. What better trophy to take to the dark master” Jackson wants you beaten, but not broken. You would make a most excellent gift.” Pendragon rubs his chin thoughtfully, avoiding the terrible coffee. “So Jackson will not come full force” Why would he not choose to unleash his darkness? Why not do battle with the Light right there where all can see? A victory for either side will be a crushing blow to their cause. I know that when I crush him, I will show the world that darkness is weak’Bob is the way. He has anointed me his weapon, and I do plan to do his work. Glory has been promised, and what better glory than to take this upstart Demon down?” Derek chuckles a bit, but straightens his face quickly at a look from the Lord King?“Nice to see you are still humble. Nice to see you are careful’Jackson is not one to be trifled with. His soul is older than you or I know. Power is his, and it is dark. He can be beaten, but you must be meek’You must be humble, and you must walk in the Light.” “And the way was still unclear. Jackson would fall’Bob promised it, and in turn I promised it back. Evolution would be the battle ground of good and evil’It would be a main event for the ages”.” ~TO BE CONTINUED~
