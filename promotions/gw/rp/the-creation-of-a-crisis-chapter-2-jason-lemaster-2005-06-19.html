The Creation of a Crisis (Chapter 2: Jason LeMaster)

19 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/19/05 Click... Pulsating in a brilliant display of beauty on the wall, a dead object came to life. An erray of colors danced on the walls atop the stairwell. His brown eyes focused on the light as he quietly climbed the stairs. The stone stairwell was almost identical to one that you might see in a Dark Ages castle. Cold and gray, but warmed by the flickering flames which lined the stone bricked walls. Click... Click... His footsteps resonated through the staircase. Perspiration started to trickle down his dark forehead from the heat builtup inside the tower. Suddenly a silhouette appeared atop the stairwell as he drew near. A DVD case clinched in his right hand. A pulsating beating was heard from above. He watched as a man exchanged a series of punches with a bag hanging down from a poll. He picked up his pace as he saw the silhouette. Click... Click... Click... He took the last step forward into the room. He looked around to see footage of Mindy Mercer's last match playing on a flat screen plasma television. He watched as Mindy flew threw the air and the crowd jumped up to their feet. But where did... CLICK! This click however... was a different kind of click. He turned around slowly to find himself looking down the barrel of a chrome magnum. His heart skipped a beat when he looked into the man's cadet blue eyes. Slowly the man dropped the gun pointed towards him and slid it into the inside of his holster, which was fasted over his right shoulder. 'You're lucky you're not dead.' Christian Zenith said as he looked at the man in front of him. 'Not the first time you've said that...' Jason LeMaster replied as he held out a DVD case labeled 'Josh.' Christian took the DVD out of Jason's hand and walked it over to the DVD a few paces away. 'I'm sure it won't be the last either...' Christian replied looked back at Jason, 'You've always had a knack for getting yourself into compromising positions. I can't count the number of times I've bailed you out. You're damn lucky you're good with a computer.' 'I got other redeeming qualities too, you know.' 'And what's that?' Zenith looked back at Jason questionably. 'I'm damn sexy for one...' Jason said with a smile. The lights reflected off of his bald head as he looked around the large room atop the stone tower of the Hunting Grounds complex. The Hunting Grounds had finally returned after all those years. Jason found it ironic, the two times were very similar to each other after all. 'Did you ever notice that this is a lot like the old times?' Jason said looking over at Christian who'd made his way back over towards the punching bag. 'When?' Zenith replied, his eyes focused on the punching bag in front of him. 'Back in Chicago man on the streets. No matter how many people we'd overrun or how many territories we'd take over, someone was always there to tell us we weren't shit. And then we'd have to take them down. Just for some other bastard to step up and tell us that we're overrated and they were taking over. No one ever seems to get the hint.' Jason said. Christian Zenith looked over at Jason, taking a small break from the bag for a bit. 'Yeah,' Christian replied, 'The world is full of elitist bastards. Everyone who can throw a punch thinks they're Muhammad Ali, and everyone who steps into an octagon thinks they're Christian Zenith. One by one, they fall down in line in this endless cycle of ignorance and incompetance. When the dust settles it'll be the same result as Chicago. The Hunting Grounds will reign supreme, and Train will be trapped like the rat he is on the Southside shithole. Using second best as his claim to fame.' Jason laughed at the mention of Global's beloved (and under educated) hoodlum Train. Zenith had known of Train for the longest time, and had no idea why Train was so passionate about Chicago's ghetto. Christian never really had much interest in it afterall, it was not much use to him. He doesn't do prostitution, he doesn't need to live in the project, and he sure as hell don't need to smoke drugs or skip school. Having 17 children wasn't exactly Christian's idea of good business or a good time. But the boys had their hood... and for whatever reason they loved their worthless pieces of real estate. But like they say, one man's trash is another man's treasure. All this talking about Chicago brough back memories of when everything suddenly got complicated for Christian... and Jason was right, the similarities were almost scary in reality. ---- Another dream that will never come true Just to compliment your sorrow Another life that I've taken from you A gift to add on to your pain and suffering Another truth you can never believe Has crippled you completely All the cries you're beginning to hear Trapped in your mind and the sound is deafening Let me enlighten you This is the way I pray ---- Date: September 8, 2000 Place: Chicago, Illinois The sun was beginning to set over the Windy City. Breezes swerved whimsically inbetween the towers of downtown, cooling of the earth from the burning red rays of the sun above. High above, the sky was painted beautifully like a canvas with shades of red, orange, yellow, blue, pink, and purple. Down below, the glass door of the Zenith Corporation Insurance Company closed and locked shut. Black Oakley sunglasses shielded a pair of cadet blue eyes. His black hair blue in the breeze as he heard the door latch behind him. There was a swagger of confidence and arrogance in his step, even for a teenager he was amazingly brilliant. A prodigy, a kid wonder. Many had already voiced his name as being the next big business man. The next Henry Ford, or even Bill Gates. Of course this worried his boss, who was also his father. The more people were impressed with his son, the less impressed they would be with his own business stylings. Although it was true that all press is good press, it is bad press when you're completely ignored. Afterall... he was still a kid, and leaps and bounds ahead of his sister at this age. The one woman other than his mother he still loved, his sister had left home and never returned. At times he felt as if he was the only one who still cared about his older sister... or remembered. That of course comes with the times unfortunately, everything was fast paced and yesterday's news was tossed aside like a broken toy. Everyone was out for the quick buck, and no one seemed to be out for anyone other than themselves. At least not in the business world or in the streets. It was amazing how much the two were alike. Everyone always said crime was a very profitable business, but with big rewards come big risks. Names like Al Capone are immortalized for what they did, and how they did it. The depressing thing is that 100 years from now, there is a better chance that people will remember Jack The Ripper before they ever remember Mother Theresa. Thus is life... it's a brutal cycle full of cynics, selfish pricks, and greedy jackasses. But in the end, that was the name of the game. It was the life you live... so you either took what you could or lose. It's true that nice guys finish last, don't ever let anyone tell you any differently. With thoughts of his sister filling his head, Christian Zenith looked across the streets. When something crosses your mind for some reason you're always under this impression that it'll be easier to find. But as usual, no luck. Something did strike young Christian though. A young African-American man his age across the street had a table set up, and was playing a game with whomever would give him the attention. Just most it was just another pathetic example of a man trying to scam the ignorant out of a buck... but to Christian, it was the sign of a business man. Zenith crossed the street, adjusting the collar of his midnight blue dress shirt as he marched on the fading crosswalk. The young man had already spotted Christian Zenith... and saw big bucks coming his way. As Zenith approached, he could almost see the young man licking his chops. The man stood up, wearing a black FUBU baseball style jersey with neon green lettering, and a pair of baggy black jeans. His hair was made up in cornrows, and his eyes were a deep brown... almost black in color. Christian stopped in front of his little stand, much to the man's delight. 'Are you interested in playin a game today sir?' The man said to Christian. 'What kind of game do we have going on here?' Christian replied... looking down at 3 SOLO cups upside down in front of the man. 'It's easy you see, I take this ping pong ball and place it under one of these cups. I move the cups around and you tell me where the ping pong ball is. Sounds easy enough right? Only $2.00 to play. I'll even give you $4.00 if you win. You play again for another $2.00 I'll give you $8.00 if you win. But my limit is $20.00 man, if you can't win by then... I think you should just call it quits. I ain't not saint, but I'm not supporting any gambling habits.' 'Why not... I'll take a crack at this.' Christian said, reaching into his pocket with a grin that would now be considered a rarity. Not so much back in 2000... a very different ballgame indeed. Zenith handed the man $2.00, and watched as the man placed the small white ball under the middle cup. Suddenly, Christian became amazed by the man's speed. Moving the cups around with such velocity, no wonder he took part in such a simple game. This ceased to exist as a game, and almost became art. The cups came to a suddenly halt, and the man looked up at Christian with a smile. 'Where did it go?' The man said with a smile. Christian simply shook his head at the man's speed and looked him dead in the eyes. 'Do I still get $4.00 if you put it in your right pocket?' Zenith replied. The man looked back at him in shock. 'What do you mean?' The man acted confused looking up at the teenage heir. Christian then knocked all the cups off the table, revealing no ping pong ball in sight. The man looked up at Christian... seemingly impressed, pulling the ping pong ball out of his right pocket. 'I've been doing this for a while man... you can see I've practiced for years. You're the only man I've ever seen pick up on that.' The man reached into his other pocket to pull out $4.00. 'No, no... you keep that. You did a damn fine job.' Christian said, 'Let's call it an investment for the future. What is your name anyway?' 'Jason LeMaster.' The man said with a smile, 'and yours?' 'Christian Zenith.' Christian responded. Jason almost fell out of his chair when he heard the name. He straightened himself out and everything as Christian looked on with a slight chuckle... 'Oh man! CHRISTIAN ZENITH! You're THAT Christian Zenith?!' Jason replied, 'I can't believe this man! I've heard a lot about you. You're some kinda business phenom or some shit right? Oh man... the boys will never believe this, I gotta get an autograph or something.' 'Please... don't waste my time, anyone in the world can sign my name.' Christian said, 'But you... you have something special. I'll tell you what, I could use a man like you in my company. You've very street saavy, with some schooling you can be business saavy. You got a bright future ahead of you. I'll set you up in a house, you and your family could move in.' 'Oh I'd love to... but... I can't.' 'Why not? I'm offering.' Christian replied, confused. 'I kinda got a special family. That stuff just wouldn't work out... thanks but no thanks. I can't leave my family like that.' Jason said with his head down. 'What do you mean leave your family? You mom and dad would be thrilled I'd think...' 'It's not THAT kinda family...' Jason replied, lifting up his sleeve to reveal a black 'G' tattooed into his right bicep, 'It's a different kinda family. They don't approve of me goin off and doin my own thing. It's not the kind of family you can just leave, they'll come and find you. You have to go back and support them. Take one for the team... everything is about the family' 'I understand...' Christian replied, 'but hey... let's say after work I'll come over here and give you some business tips for your game. But you have to do me a favor in return.' 'What's that?' 'I want to know how this family works...' Zenith said, 'Street business has always intriged me. Hell I might even learn a thing or two. Maybe I could even meet the family sometime.' 'Just might Mr. Zenith.' Jason said with a smile. 'Don't call me that... I'm not an old man, I'm about the same age as you. Call me Chris.' 'Aight... see you tomorrow Chris.' ---- Living just isn't hard enough Burn me alive inside Living my life's not hard enough Take everything away ---- The quarter moon hung over the Chicago skyline. Winds off of Lake Michigan blews leaves througout the streets, paining the otherwise bland neutral colored landscape with splashes of red, orange, and yellow under the bright white street lights. A shadow cast into the street under the light, throwing black onto splashes of color. The shadow belonged to Christian Zenith, wearing a black dress shirt with the top two buttons unbuttoned, and a pair of black slacks. His cold eyes navigated the surrounding area, as he walked slowly down the street. 'Yo Chris! Over here!' Zenith heard from the darkness. Christian began to walk towards the voice, which seemed to come from a narrow alleyway nearby. As he entered, his eyes adjusted in the darkness. Across from him stood Jason LeMaster, who made a waving motion. Quietly they made their way through the pitch black night. A small flame appeared ahead, being burned in an old steel trash drum. As Jason LeMaster poked his head out of the alley, a group of men made their way over towards him. Greeting each other they gave each other five, and everyone seemed to be wearing nothing but black. Christian stepped out slowly through the darkness... 'Who in the hell is that cracker?' A man said as Zenith moved forth closer to the flame. 'That's my friend Chris, don't worry he's cool.' Christian Zenith looked over at the man who spoke. Zenith didn't make any facial expressions at all as he moved confidently to Jason's side. Many of the members stood in their corners, away from Jason and the new comer. Suddenly a large African-American man came out from the shadows. He looked over at Jason and Christian as he drew near. He stood almost 7 foot tall and was built like a sculpture. 'I don't remember inviting anyone here Jason.' The man said. 'I'm sorry G-Dawg, this is my friend Christian. He's cool.' Jason explained. 'I know who in the hell he is. He's that Zenith Corporation kid who all those uppy bastards talk about in them newspapers. Why in the fuck is he here? He was born with a fuckin silver spoon in his mouth. He don't know a goddamn thing about the streets, and when people like him come trying to fuck with our territory they get fucked up. So you get his little pansy rich ass out of here Jason.' G-Dawg said. 'But...' 'I said GET THE FUCK OUTTA HERE!' G-Dawg said as he pulled a knife out of his pants pocket. Christian Zenith looked at the man unphased before looking over at Jason. 'We gotta jet man...' Jason said to Christian. 'Why?' Zenith replied. 'Dude, I'm not fuckin around, we need to go.' 'It's a goddamn street Jason, I can stand here if I want to.' 'It's MY GODDAMN street! You got that?!' G-Dawg yelled as he came over towards Christian. Zenith stood and watched as G-Dawg came closer with knife in hand. Jason stood next to Zenith and began to shake in anxiety. 'Let's get the fuck outta here Chris!' 'No.' Christian replied, much to Jason's surprise. Power rushed through Zenith's body, and he liked it. He was in control... he was the better man. 'You asked for it white bitch!' G-Dawg said she he came lunging forward towards Christian's abdomen, but Christian stepped to the side and slammed his forearm down on the back of G-Dawg's skull sending him down to the ground. The 'family' began to watch in shock as Zenith planted his boot on the side of G-Dawg's skull, pressing it against the pavement. 'What the fuck are you doing?!' Jason screamed at Christian, whose icy demeanor scared Jason beyond belief. He'd never seen Zenith fight, he'd never seen Christian so cold. Zenith's eyes looked down at G-Dawg. 'Don't move or you'll break your neck.' Christian said. 'Let him go Christian!' Jason screamed as Zenith shot a glance up at him. The anger in Zenith's eyes let Jason know that this was no time to run. LeMaster began to back away slowly as Christian looked back down at G-Dawg. 'You better get the fuck off me!' G-Dawg screamed in pain from the pavement. 'You better stop moving or you'll break your neck, do you not understand that?' 'You little fu...' G-Dawg said as he tried to force his way up to his feet. Suddenly a snap echoes across the buildings. It was a noise that Jason LeMaster remembered to this day. What a sickening noise. Zenith felt the crunch under his dress shoe. Zenith took his foot off of G-Dawg and looked down in confusion. 'Damn...' Christian said as he backed up. A series of emotions began to fill Christian's body as he looked down at the limp body of G-Dawg in front of him. The thought of killing a man sickened him, how could he do such a thing? No one could find out. What happened if someone did find out? Would they kill him... no, no. He could just pay em off... he could give them anything they wanted. Suddenly, a sick sense of pride came across Christian Zenith. He took a life, he was stronger, better, smarter. It was survival of the fittest... it was only natural, right? Yeah... it was completely natural. Only the better man should've lived afterall. Christian Zenith looked up as all the members crept out of the shadows and went down to one knee looking at Christian. He looked around them in confusion as Jason to, fell to one knees. Zenith stepped forward, walking towards the flame as they bowed their heads. ---- Another nightmare about to come true Will manifest tomorrow Another love that I've taken from you Lost in time on the edge of suffering Another taste of the evil I breed Will level you completely Bring to life everything that you fear Live in the dark and the world is threatening Let me enlighten you This is the way I pray ---- 'What in the hell is going on.' Christian asked as he looked over at Jason. 'G-Dawg was the Alpha Male, he was our leader.' Jason replied with his head bowed. 'Looks like you need a better leader then.' 'We have one.' Jason replied. 'Who?' 'You're the Alpha Male now.' Jason said. 'What?' Christian said while he looked around at the men bowing before him. The shock was quickly taken over by a sick sense of accomplishment. As much as he knew what was going on was wrong... he couldn't help by feel invigorated by the power and the glory. Adrenaline began to pump through his veins, and a sadistic smile grew across his face. 'Rise.' On command they all rose to their feet and Jason ran over to Zenith, who surveyed the men before him. 'Okay there are only two rules to go by around here Christian...' Jason said, 'Number One... we're a family and we operate as a family. A family doesn't stab each other in the back or rob one another. We also don't rat each other out to the police. You let us take care of G-Dawg.' 'And second?' 'Number Two, you always wear black from now on. That's our color, when you see black you know it's family.' 'I think I can handle that...' Christian replied as he looked around. He cracked his neck and looked back at G-Dawg lying on the ground. It was at that very moment he realized there was no turning back now. He was here to stay... and he had to learn to love it, he didn't have a choice anymore. 'And one last thing Chris.' 'What?' 'Welcome to the Hunting Grounds.' Musical Credit: 'Prayer' by Disturbed
