Intervention (SiT RP 2, with Team Brick)

5 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/5/04 It's a cloudy day in Tokyo. Donny McNasty and Alexis Steele stand outside the Tokyo Egg Dome. Donny looks down at his watch. Donny: C'mon, Brick, c'mon. A black Hummer drives up to the stadium. Out of the driver's seat steps Brick. Brick: Hey, Donny. Brick and Donny bump fists. Brick: Everybody esle here? Donny: Yeah, Adam and Ven are inside. Brick: Good. As Brick walks into the building, Donny and Alexis look at each other before following. Adam Myers sits in a backwards chair, leaning on the back, his arms folded. Venneman Pendragon sits in his chair as if he were sitting upon a throne. Brick: Hey, fellas. Myers: Hello, Brick. Pendragon: Greetings, friend. Brick: Well, now that we're all here, let's get started. Donny: Wait, Brick. Before we discuss our team strategy, there's something else that needs to be talked about. Brick: What's that? Donny: Brick, we're worried about you. Brick: Worried? What are you--? Pendragon: We fear, friend, that the villain Salaok may have you vexed to the point of distraction. Brick exhales. Brick: He's got me vexed, all right. If it was any o' y'all, you'd be vexed, too. Myers: Listen, Brick, we all feel for what you're going through with Salaok, but we gotta know where your head is. Showdown in Tokyo isn't that far away and we can't afford to lose to Team Jackson. Brick: Don't you think I know that? Myers: We're just concerned you don't let certain persoanl feelings cloud your judgement. Brick rises from his seat. Brick: Is that so? Tell me something, Adam. Your wife, she just had a baby, right? Myers: Yeah. Brick: Now, let's say someone threatened your wife and your baby, and basically told you there was nothing you could do about it. How would that make you feel? Myers: I'd want to tear his bloody head off. Brick: Exactly. That's exactly how Salaok makes me feel right now. That said, I understand your concerns. Let me tell you right now. Any of my issues with Salaok, I plan to table it as far as Showdown in Tokyo. Because it's too big a deal for all of us, and I won't let him or anyone else ruin it. But that means I need all of you behind me. Brick stands in front of Donny. Brick: Donny, you're one o' the toughest cats I know. Not many cats I know who could take a beating from and still smile and say I'm their friend. You're someone I'd definitely want watching my back. Donny: You can count on me, Brick. Brick then walks to Myers. Brick: Adam, you're the Cruiserweight champion. The night of our match, win or lose, I knew you were someone I wanted on this team. Because you and I are alike, in a lot of ways. We never let the odds tell us we can't win. That's the kind of person I want on my team. Myers: That means a lot coming from you, Brick. Brick steps in front of Pendragon. Brick: Pendragon, you're a double champion and probably the only person in this room who's beaten both me and Donny. And if that's not someone who should be on this team, I don't know who is. Pendragon rises from his seat and stands like a king. Pendragon: If, by standing with you, I can achieve victory, I will. You have thy Lord King and Excalibur at your side. Brick: Thanks. Now, let's talk about Team Jackson. Now, each of us is gonna have to be prepared. I know Jackson wants to even the score. I know he's got two things against me. One is that I beat him last month at Dark Rituals. Pendragon: And the second? Brick: I've slept with Brandi on a number of occasions. Donny: Who hasn't? Alexis shoots Donny a glare. Donny grins sheepishly at her. Donny: Other than me, of course. Heh heh. Myers: I'm happily married, mate, so I haven't. Pendragon: I also have never lain down with Miss Sights. Brick: Guess I'm the only one here who has. Alexis clears her throat and looks around. The others all turn to her. Alexis: OK, one time, but it was late and we were drunk and kinda horny... Brick: That's okay, Alexis. You don't have to elaborate. Donny: Speak for yourself, Brick. Brick: Look, there's no time for this. Now, Zenith has been on a losing streak, lately and two of the cats that have beaten him are in this room. Pendragon: True. He may fight harder than all the others. Brick: The reason we can win this is because we can be a team. Jackson and Zenith can't stand each other. We can maybe use that to our advantage. Myers: What about this mystery team member of theirs? Donny: He's right, Brick. We don't know who it is. It could be anybody. Brick: Yeah, there's a lot of cats who haven't been accounted for. Fushichou no Nazo, Dagoth Kinslayer... Donny: Speaking of cats, whatever happened to Kitty Mac? Brick: You don't think she could be the mystery team member, do you? Myers: No bloody way! She's a good friend of mine and, even if she weren't, there's no way she'd have anything to do with Jackson. Brick: OK, let's not worry any more. I think we've all done enough of that for one day. Let's get down to the ring and practice. Team Brick leaves the locker room and heads for the ring. Brick: So, Alexis, you and Brandi, huh?
