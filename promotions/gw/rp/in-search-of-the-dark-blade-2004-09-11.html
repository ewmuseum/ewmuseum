In Search of the Dark Blade...

11 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-11-2004 Things change. It is inevitable. No matter how we wish that things would stay comfortable, that things would stay’Predictable’It is the nature of the world for things to change. And now, change is on the horizon in a big way. The underlings in the world known as GW have been comfortable with an unfocused king. The throngs have gotten used to a ruler who spends more time worrying about what he cannot change rather than attacking the problems that he can. No more. Thy Lord King is on a mission from God? Trudging through the jungle with a dark skinned native looking fellow, Pendragon stands out amongst the green in his burnished golden mail shirt. Pendragon has never been one to blend in. The other fellow, Pendragon’s guide through this lush land, blends in as if he is a part of the jungle. A tan loincloth hides the man’s, umm, manhood, and he is tattooed heavily on his arms, chest and even his face. His ears are decorated with bone carvings, and he leads on as if he knows each footfall that he makes. “If only the man would acknowledge me whenst I speak at him. Even heathens such as he should know that to speak to a king is both an honor and a blessing”.A king on a mission from Bob is even more a feat! And yet?.” “HO! Stop there! I have a question for thee! I seek a dark sword, in a dark temple, in a dark forest’We walk in the light. I do hope that thee has not led thy Lord King, anointed of the Light, astray!” Pendragon uses his best voice of command, but the dark skinned fellow marches on. Caught up in listening to himself, Pendragon almost doesn’t seem to notice, as he keeps up his end of the conversation, almost as if he is speaking to himself? “My friend Bob, thy Lord and Creator, puts thy Lord King Venneman Pendragon on a mission to destroy evil. To fight darkness with darkness. The fellow could control everything if he wished, and yet he looses his hammer on the world without a thought as to how said weapon would be received. GW is to be mine staging area, for evil darkens it’s once noble halls of valor. This has been foretold by the Creator himself. But now, the kicker. After mine acceptance of mine path, a decent man has been set upon mine way. Brick. The man personifies what once was noble of the world of GW, and the higher powers send him my way as if for a sacrificial lamb. The shadow-men must hold more sway than even I had imagined. Why would I not be given those who are actually evil to do battle with” What of Soma, a vampire? Reno, a murderer? Hell, even this Jackson fellow is a demon reaver’I, however, get Brick. God’s Hammer gets to pound the nail known to GW faithful as Brick. I guess it is at once everyone’s time to fade, I only lament the fact that this noble fellows downfall begins with me?” Pendragon sighs as he notices the native still pays him no mind, merely trudges on to wherever it is that he takes the armor clad hero. The jungle, however, has begun to change. The trees seem to be getting older the deeper they trek’The trunks are becoming twisted and knarled, with branches that seem to want to come together over the path. Patches of darkness are passed through here and there, as the branches come together to keep the light out. In these shadows, strange beasts dwell. A dog sized creature with eyes taking up nearly the whole of it’s head peeks up and disappears just as quickly? “What in the name of the Creator”.” Pendragon begins with a start, but the native shows no fear, only marches on into the darkening jungle. Pendragon shivers a bit and shakes his head, visibly gathering himself. To have shaken this great man, it must have truly been unexpected. An unknown species’A threat. He loosed his hand made blade in the curved scabbard, dragons inlaid with gold twinkling on the hilt in the patches of sunlight that make it through the thickening canopy. With almost a smile, the native leads on. Pendragon follows as strange jungle creatures scurry across the path in the shadows’Sometimes, the smaller creatures are scooped up by strange birds of prey’Sometimes they make it to the relative safety of thickened undergrowth. Pendragon shakes his head in amazement as the strange things pass, but he leaves the Katana sheathed. The further they trek into the jungle, the darker it gets? “It would appear that we are indeed on the right path?” Pendragon mumbles this, almost apologetically, but not too apologetically. The native remains stoic, however, and barely cracks a smile. Suddenly, he halts and points. Ahead, in extremely dim light, is the mouth of a cave, seemingly sliced into the side of a sheer cliff rising straight out of the jungle. Pendragon gasps as he sees it, and the native nods as Pendragon draws his sword? “You are right to be wary, Crusader. None that have ever entered that evil place have come forth as they were?” Pendragon gapes openly at the native? “The man speaks! And here I thought thee a godless heathen?” Pendragon smirks as the fellow frowns at him? “Godless I am not, nor a heathen. I have been your guide, but my guidance stops here. Bob speaks not only to you, Hammer. He does have many tools.” “But what cometh next” After I enter, what should I expect?” The native shakes his head sadly?“That is not for me to know. I have never entered, nor do I wish to. Of the Hundreds that have sought this cave, a mere five have come out alive. Of those five, none remained with their sanity. Fear lurks within, and usually too great for the mind to bear. You must enter, as has been decreed by our god, and you will hopefully come out with what you seek. May the light shine on you, Hammer’Good hunting.” The man claps Pendragon on the shoulder and turns to go back the way that he came. Pendragon stares after him, and the man stops?“I shall camp one mile from this spot. If you are not emerged after five days, I shall leave, for you will not be coming back. Again”.Luck to you?” Pendragon looked after long after the native fellow had blended into the jungle and vanished? “Strange fellow to ignore me the whole way here and then leave with such a cryptic message. If only I knew what I had to do other than to go into yonder cave. What horrors await” Will I keep mine ownself sane?” As he says the last, there is a bit of a twinkle to his eye, almost as if he is already too late. With a chuckle and a shrug, though, he makes his way to the mouth of the cave and enters. As he disappears, the scene changes to something entirely different? **********FLASH*********** Blinding white hits the screen, as we find ourselves in an immaculate office. Seated behind a white desk sits a white bearded man. The man’s kindly face is lit by a smile, and he appears quite regal in a snow white suit. “It has been awhile my children. The Hammer is loosed upon the world. Seeking the dark blade, Lucian, he will fight his darkest fears and face his most bitter foe. There are a number of markers to guide him on his way, so fear not for the King of men. The King of Heaven watches out for him, and seldom do I choose an inferior vessel to hold my faith. Pendragon is the perfect man for his purpose. To destroy evil in all worlds. His task is simple, if far reaching. Yet, the best tasks are those that are broad based. Why muck up the works with too much information” Keep it simple’Why do you think it took a mere seven days to create the world?” With a chuckle, Bob waves his hand and the screen goes black?
