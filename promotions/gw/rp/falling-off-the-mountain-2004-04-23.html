Falling off the mountain

23 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 23 (#3 for PPV) The scene opens to a small room where cheering is heard. Inside is Roswell on his knees inside a wrestling ring. Roswell gets up and put’s the belt around his waist and waves to Larry the owner of this place. He slides out of the ring and looks as if he is going to cry, he walks over to Larry and shakes his hand. Roswell walks over to all of the competitors who were in the tournament and shakes there hand. Roswell: I know this title means almost everything to me but...........I’m not going to be coming here any more! I have achieved everything I wanted to here and right now I would like to give the title up. I would like Larry to come up here and decide who should be the champion! I will always love this place but I have no more time. I have to pursue a career in GLOBAL WRESTLING! From that said Roswell takes of the belt and lays it on the mat of the ring and leaves out of the ring. Larry suddenly appears and slides into the ring to pick up the title. Larry watches as Roswell walks into the shower room for the very last time in his little career here. Larry: Well you guys I know you weren’t expecting that but it had to happen. As for the new champion I would like to call all the competitors into the ring and I will have the privilege to announce the new champion. All the people who were competing in the tournament slide into the ring hoping that he will pick them. All the people go into different corners of the ring and listen on to see who the new champion is. Larry quickly slides out of the ring and walks into his office. 1 second later he walks back out with certificates wraps up. Larry slides back into the ring and puts the title on the ground. Larry: Joey Flash would you please come up here. Sorry man but you are not the new champion. Larry hands Joey the certificate and tell’s him to go wait so we can get a picture. Joey walks over to the right turnbuckle and waits there silently. Larry: Frost would you please come up here. Sorry Frost you are not the new champion. Larry also hands Frost the certificate and also tells him to go wait where Joey is waiting. Frost walks over to Joey and starts whispering to him. Larry: Fire would you please come here for a second. I’m really sorry but you are not the new champion. Please go stand by the other two. Larry: It is between Red Dragon and Fang. Whoever I choose will not be the champion. The last man standing will be the champion. Larry: I’m sorry but RED DRAGON you are not the champion. From the other side of the ring you can see Fang jumping all around because he is so happy. Roswell who is in the shower room can here this and starts to clap even though there is nobody there. Roswell walks out of the shower room with his gym bag around his shoulder. Roswell sees ever body staring at him but he pays no attention and walks out of the door. Roswell is seen walking through the lobby of the building for the last time, He says goodbye to everyone at the long counter and goes out the spinning doors. Outside Roswell is walking down the street when all of a sudden he hears police and fire fighters sirens go off. Roswell: What could that be? Right beside Roswell is a lady running, Roswell starts running after her down the street and starts yelling at her ear, but the lady does not listen so Roswell runs even faster shouting at her again. Then Roswell slows down and starts to wonder why everyone is running and not walking. Roswell starts running again and sees a firefighter. Roswell: Why is everyone running? Fire Fighter: On the mountain side a man is about to jump for no reason. Roswell: Now why would someone want to do that? Fire Fighter: I have no clue, here hop in we can give you a ride there. Roswell hops into the front seat of the car and Fire Fighter starts speeding down the street not caring for traffic. Finally Roswell and the Fire Fighter arrive at the mountain side only to see a man on a platform about 1 million feet over top of shallow water. Roswell starts running to the scene and jumps almost to where the man is. Roswell starts climbing up the mountain dropping his gym back down into the water. Roswell who is at the top of the mountain jumps onto the platform were the man is. Roswell: It’s ok don’t jump. Man: Why shouldn’t I? For the last year was nothing but shit everybody hated me and didn’t care for me. Not even my wife, so I thought is this keeps on continuing I’m just going to kill myself! Roswell: What did your wife do to you? Man: You know what she did! She slept with another man and didn’t tell me that’s what she did. She ignored me when I wanted to talk to her, she thought I was a nobody. So that’s why I’m jumping and there’s nothing you or anybody can do about it! All of a sudden from the platform the man jump’s to his death. Everybody on the mountain watches in terror! Then from the platform Roswell jump’s off trying to help the man. Roswell watches and so does everybody else as the man his feet off from the groun, then Roswell pushes himself to go aster and is also feet away from the man. Roswell grabs onto the man and holds him as they hit the water with a splash and a thud! Father Clark: ROSWELL!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Everybody from the mountain start running down it, people start jumping down the mountain and landing on there stomach. Father Clark who is ahead of everybody else quickly runs down the mountain and reaches the bottom, but Roswell and the man are nowhere to be seen. Everybody starts searching for the body’s hoping that they are not dead. The police bring in a suction and start sucking out all of the water that was in the little river. Finally after a couple minutes all of the water is drained out. Everybody jumps into the dried up river and starts searching for them. Then a woman screams and all the people rush over to see what she was screaming about. Everybody looks down as they see the 2 body’s. Then after minutes Roswell who was laying as if it looked like he was dead rises from the river and screams to the people. Everybody starts cheering as they run back over to the body’s. They see Roswell alive and standing but the man still lays there unconscious. Then a ambulance speed through and stop’s right before the man. 2 paramedics bring out a stretcher and put the body of the man onto it. 1 Paramedic: He has a heartbeat! The paramedics start asking Roswell of what happened as another paramedics takes the stretcher and put’s it into the ambulance. Roswell limp’s to Father Clark to tell him what happened when they hit the ground. Roswell: Well what happened, when I grabbed onto the guy I went underneath him so when we hit I did hit the ground first. But when I hit my body kind of bounce and the man went flying and that’s all I new because I lost my memory when I hit. Father Clark: So you are alright? Do you know who the man was? Roswell: No I don’t but I think I might have seen him before. Father Clark: Where did you see him before? Roswell: Oh my! He was the man that stole from the church! Because when the robbery happened I got a glance at his face and was pretty sure that was him. He shot me! Father Clark: Oh dear, I’ll have to tell the police that. Roswell and Father Clark start walking up the mountain and leave. They start walking in the dust and then down a old street. Roswell and Father start walking down a newly painted street and then walk into Roswell’s house to sit down. SCENE ENDS
