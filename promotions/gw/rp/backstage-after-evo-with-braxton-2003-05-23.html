Backstage after Evo with Braxton

23 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/23/03 Ely Braxton walks backstage from the arena, followed by Christina McCoy. All night, he had been chasing what everyone else had been telling him was a ghost. Ely and Brick were discussing the matches they were set to compete in, assuring one another that they would look out for each other when Ely looked over Brick’s shoulder and spotted a black haired man sneaking out of Salaok's office. He thought, or rather knew, it was Sean Price. Both Brick and Christina tried in vain to convince him that it wasn’t who he thought it was, or rather who he knew it was. The only person who would corroborate was one crew member who he had to pin against a wall. His hunt led to a confrontation between the two men in the middle of the ring and then to Salaok taking advantage of the situation by booking the two men in a match at Dead Zone. “What happened to keeping your temper, huh?” Christina asks. “That son of a bitch did it! I know he did!” “Well, what good was choking him gonna do?” “Nobody says what he said about you! Nobody!” “Do you think I care about that?” “Hey!” a voice calls out. Ely and Christina turn to see Brick coming towards them. Brick has his own reason to celebrate. He has advanced to the main event of Dead Zone. “Hey. Congratulations!” “Thanks. So, it was Price.” “Don’t worry. I ain’t gonna say I told you so.” “Well, that’s good because then I’d have to kick your ass.” Ely tries to force back a smile. “Is that a smile I see?” “No.” “Yes, it is.” “Ely could stand to smile,” says Christina. “I told you to calm down, didn’t I?” “Yeah, she’s right, man.” “Wait a minute,” says Ely. “I need to keep calm” Who was it beat the hell outta Deacon Holmes earlier tonight?” Brick remains quiet after Ely mentions his altercation with the arrogant young wrestler earlier in the evening. Brick had already been offended by Holmes? comments about him on the last edition of Overload. Brick had taken it upon himself to go down to the ring during Holmes? match with Roz Blayze and sit in the commentators? booth with Brandi Sights and Mark Bishop. Sometime near the end of the match, the Nevermore came down to the ring and assaulted the two cruiserweights. Brick intervened, hitting all three members with a steel chair. After the Nevermore retreated backstage, Deacon took that opportunity to get in Brick’s face, which elevated into an argument and then a shoving match, ending with Holmes slapping Brick upside his head and Brick beating the hell out of him. “Well, that was different. He put his hands on me. He was already on my shit list, before.” “Oh, and like you didn’t go out there, looking for trouble?” “Hey, the boy was talking shit. I went out there to see if he could back it up.” “You could have watched it back here on a monitor.” “Could have. I just didn’t want to. Plus, I was just making trouble for the Nevermore, if anybody.” “Speaking of which, you know I’ve got your back at Dead Zone. I know you’re gonna have problems with the Nevermore if you get Sabbot.” “I’ll probably have to deal with them if I face Tragic Hero. Salaok and his boys just like to start shit. And I’m a be ready for their asses. Just wait and see. But the same goes for you. When you face Price, just in case he tries anything, I’ll be there if you need me. Say, why don’t we head down to the Saloon and get something to eat?” “Sounds good to me,” Christina replies. “Fine,” grumbles Ely. “Brick! Brick!” The voice belongs to a crew member who runs up to him. “What, man?” “Just wanted to tell you. Sage Sabbot won his match against Tragic Hero. You’re facing him at Dead Zone.” Brick smiles on the inside. Now he can accomplish two goals at Dead Zone: finally winning the World title and beating the hell out of another member of the Nevermore. “Thanks for telling me. Come on guys, let’s go to the Saloon.”
