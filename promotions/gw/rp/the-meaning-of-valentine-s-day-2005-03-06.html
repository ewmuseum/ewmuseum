The Meaning of Valentine's Day

6 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/06/05 'Well... happy Valentine's Day, Mikey!' Mindy barely gave her cousin Michael a chance to so much as draw in a breath to speak before she jumped and wrapped her arms around him, choking him in a near-deadly bearhug. To Mindy, Valentine's Day was meant to be the happiest day of the year. To Michael, of course, it was just any other day; never celebrated any different than the day before, aside from maybe an extra amount of hatred for the happy spouses in the world- however small or large the amount of those were. As far as he was concerned, it was another excuse to eat chocolate, and a sad one at that. It didn't help that matters that he never did eat chocolate in the first place, so one could imagine his chances of warming up to a Valentine's Day now; in the quiet, dark confinements of the New Mexico home, with the only illumination coming from two lit candles on the dining room table to the left of the living room. However, when Mindy clung to him, all he could muster was a loud grumble. Words hadn't been able to describe his annoyance with the holiday- and Mindy's love for the day only fueled his anger. Hiya, Diary, Well, another depressing day at the house. I feel dirty in a way for not thinking of how to celebrate. I mean, today's Valentine's Day! Everything should be all happy and cute, not bleak and reeking of death. Still, that doesn't mean I'd choose my real home over staying here. Speaking of which, those stupid phone calls tonight... urgh, I want to tear my pretty pink hair out just thinking about them. Why'd they have to call now? I should be happy tonight, and not writing out another little rant. She had stayed quiet all day up until that time, when the clock above the living room television hit nine-thirteen PM. Mindy simply got too nervous when it came to watching her cousin constantly so upset; he had been seated on the couch as if he waited for something to happen, but nothing did- nothing other than the few times he bumped into Jaden when she came back and forth from her breaks from work. And when that happened, all that came from him were few words. Not many, and nothing overly welcoming. It made for little surprise when the uncomfortable silence continued after Jaden went off to sleep. It grew upsetting as such that Mindy had to do something to cheer her cousin up. Maybe for once it would actually work. 'You shouldn't be sitting here all gruffy,' the girl stated with a pout, 'today's a day to be happy!' Michael smiled faintly and eased his cousin away from him. 'Don't have much of a reason to be happy.' Mindy stepped back and rubbed her chin, as if confused by what he said. No reason to be happy? It didn't take long for Michael to delve deeper into an explanation. 'This day is barely a holiday. Since when is Valentine's Day something that has meaning anymore? Besides... you're supposed to celebrate it with the person you love.' Michael worries me a lot. Sheesh, come to think of it, there's never a time where he hasn't left me concerned as to what his thoughts are. Or more importantly, what he thinks of me. He probably could do without having me around to bug him, but I just want to help! Even though he doesn't realize it, Michael did so much in helping me get through my life this far. He's as good a role model as anyone else, in the sense that if he doesn't like something, he's not afraid to say so. But still... it just feels like I don't understand him at all half the time. Mindy gasped at first. Was he taking a cheap shot at her- after the cruel, yet ultimately heartwearming outbursts they threw at eachother in that restaurant the week before? No... he couldn't be commenting on that. 'Wh- what is that s'posed to mean?' Mindy questioned, rightfully worried to know the answer. In return, though, she got a hesitant reply- not the usual witty quip that Michael was more than used to throwing out. Instead, it was something surprisingly low on the level of controversy. Not spoken through audible words, it was a mere sway of the head and trail of the eyes. They led Mindy's attention over in the direction of the closed bedroom door in the distance, cowered in the darkness of what was a swiftly approaching night time. Mindy stared into the emptiness of the hallway, as if something was going to soon emerge from the dark to deliver her cousin's answer. However, nothing returned from the dark to comfort her knowledge. He seems to want someone to help him, but he just doesn't open up enough to let anyone guide him. Maybe he's too proud to spill his heart out to people... I don't even think his wife knows why he gets so depressed. I swear, he can have a big mouth or be as quiet as a little teeny tiny mouse. The moment of silence that followed was one that didn't make for much more of an ease to the tension in the room. Luckily for them, though, the ringing of the telephone was enough to jar them both out of their tranquility. 'Who the hell is that?' Michael groaned, squinting through the growing darkness to stare at the telephone across from him. With a sigh, he began to push himself from the couch. 'This better be important.' Before he could get himself onto his feet, Mindy pushed him back into his seat with a heave. 'Relax,' she exhaled, 'I'll get it.' The girl scampered over to the still ringing phone, and picked it up quickly. 'Hello?' The voice on the other end was one that gave her a rush of mixed emotions. Anger, sadness, confusion, regret, all rolled up into one blunt sentence. 'Did you get my gift?' 'Markus...' she trailed off. The name she whispered was enough to have Michael hunched over in his place on the couch to hear. 'Who?' Michael asked. 'I hope that isn't who I think it is.' Mindy turned around and put a finger to her lips, motioning for Michael to keep quiet. Michael folded his arms and shook his head, but complied none the less. 'No,' Mindy continued, 'I didn't get anything from you.. aside from a ride in an ambulance, but that was a couple months ago.' 'That's right,' the voice answered, 'it was a couple months ago. And considering it was an accident, I figured you'd be ready to talk. Maybe I was wrong.' Mindy nodded slowly, not caring for the excuses presented to her. 'Yep, you're very wrong, hun.' 'You know,' the voice started again, 'I said I was sorry.. I fucked up, okay? I'm really sorry. Just do me the favor and talk to me. I deserve that much, don't I?' 'Funny, I don't think you're that innocent,' Mindy mumbled lowly. She always tried to put a leash on her anger, but half the time it was for naught. Oh well, at least she tried, unlike Michael. 'I didn't say that,' the voice pleaded, 'I just asked to talk to you, that's all. Christ, God forbid I care about you, girl.' Mindy bit her bottom lip. Markus cared about her... maybe that was true. He had cared... 'He did care. That doesn't mean he cares now.' I gave that boy all the time in the world. He could tell me that he had tickets to Disney Land and that he was going to propose to me on the Universal Tour when Jaws popped out of the water, it wouldn't change the fact that the bastard cheated on me. And he couldn't even do that without being so puzzled about it! God, if you're going to cheat on someone, least you should do is be sure about it. Meh, people these days. 'If you cared, I wouldn't have been in the hospital over what you did.' The voice on the other end of the phone was silent after that. What else was there to say? Markus dug his own grave, and it was the truth. He didn't have to put his hands on Mindy; he didn't have to hurt her at all. If only he had kept himself in line, and hadn't been caught. No... he shouldn't have done what he did, period. He knew it, but of course, he'd never admit to it. Apology or not, however, Mindy would hate him all the same. 'As far as I'm concerned, we have nothing more to talk about.' After a few moments of continuing silence, Mindy shook her head and was ready to hang the phone up, but the voice sounded just as she looked to do so. 'Mindy?' The girl rolled her eyes in repulsion. 'Yes?' 'Have fun with Mike Biddle.' Mindy gasped. So much for caring. 'Fuck you!' she screamed, although it was too little too late; Markus had already hung up. 'Son of a bitch...' Yep, the more I think about it, the more I cannot believe I had gone out with that mongoloid. Ooh, I hope I spelled that right. Down the hall, a door could be heard creaking open. What came from the door was what Michael had guessed to either be Heaven or Hell itself. At that moment in time, he predicted it to be the latter- and he was right. 'What the hell is goin' on?' Jaden mumbled in exhaustion, staring at Michael's silhouette with focused blue eyes. 'I'd really like it if I could get some sleep.' Michael's eyebrows scrunched up in annoyance. 'Now she's getting on my nerves,' he mumbled in a sentence only he was able to hear. Did everything always have to happen at the worst of times? They didn't know why he was so short-fused that night, not even Jaden. However, it wasn't something that he just inherited on this one Valentine's Day. It was how he reacted to every Valentine's Day, for the past eleven or twelve years. And every year, Michael came closer to losing his cool and doing some things he debated he'd regret or not. He probably would, but more than likely it would happen way too late. 'What a dickhead,' Mindy continued, either oblivious or unknowing to the furious woman who stood just out of her sight. She slammed the phone down on the reciever and pouted in frustration. 'I swear to God I can't win with guys. First Markus the chain smoking vampire, now that Keebler Elf lookalike Mike Biddle. Who's next? Logan Creed, the resident GW Papa Smurf?' 'You said it, not me,' Michael coughed. I wish sometimes Michael would concentrate a little less on being so opinionated and a little more on trusting others to know more about him. I mean he's funny and all, but he can be rather discouraging. 'What a nice comment to make, Mikey.' The younger of the two cousins turned and glared down her older relative. She didn't seem to be the playful, energetic Mindy anymore. No, she was much more serious. Whether that was for better or worse, however, only time would tell. After all, if unleashed, she did have a temper that could rival Michael's. 'I'm not always going to be nice,' Michael replied with a shrug, standing up and scratching his stomach through his white tee shirt. He yawned and stumbled almost drunkenly past his cousin, and over to Jaden. 'Sorry, hun, I think I'll join you. Pretty tired myself.' Michael's remark came one split second too late. Just as he finished the sentence, a silhouette much smaller compared to the ones around it, had wandered around the corner of the hall and in front of Jaden. 'Momma? Daddy? What's goin' on?' Jaden's hands went to her hips. There went a good night's sleep. Jaden reached down and lifted her daughter into her arms, rocking her gently back and forth. 'Nothing at all, sweetheart, we were all just about to get some sleep. Weren't we?' By the tone in Jaden's voice, it was obvious who the 'weren't we' comment was directed to. The person in question also overheard it. 'Oh, uh, yeah.. sorry.' Mindy poked her head out into the hall, over at the faceless shadow of Jaden far in the distance. Jaden's a nice girl... I can see how she can have a short fuse, considering she married a man who has a problem with his temper. I love Mikey to itty bitty bits, but I'd be a big liar if I didn't confess that he had a problem controlling himself sometimes. 'Good night, cousin,' Michael stammered, rubbing the back of his head as he turned to go to his room. That could have been the end of the night right there, but it wasn't- the second series of rings from the telephone did not allow that. Both Michael and Mindy's heads turned towards the phone. Mindy looked at her cousin, as if looking for advice as to what she should do. 'Ignore it,' he said, which was good enough for her. That was, until she gave the phone one final glance. Perhaps she should pick up... it could have been important. When Michael realized Mindy hadn't followed him down the hall to their respective rooms, he turned and sighed. 'Mindy, what did I say?' I knew he didn't want me talking to anyone else, and I know now I really shouldn't have picked up.. but curiousity always killed the kitty cat, I guess. Mindy heard what Michael had said, but she just couldn't go without picking up the phone- maybe it was something she needed to know, by some weird chance. 'Hello?' she started, lifting the phone to her ear. Moments later, though, the girl recoiled in shock as she had to hold the phone away from her due to the yelling on the other end. Michael had shrugged it off- all he wanted was to forget that Valentine's Day ever existed and catch up on some much needed sleep. Maybe that wouldn't be much to ask any other time or place, but in the Mercer household, such a request was next to impossible to be fulfilled. Mindy on the other hand, well, she was too worried about everything to ever get sleep. The occasional cup of soda always kept her in line anyway. 'Slow down for a se-' 'Please... just calm d-' 'Oh, for fuck's sake!' I swear, though, if there's anyone in this family that couldn't possibly get any angrier, it'd have to be... Mindy fought relentlessly to get a coherent sentence in to her ill-tempered father, but it was practically no use. The repeated shot down words were just enough to have Michael stop and look back in sympathy of his little cousin. 'Mindy...' The second he muttered her name, the phone was slammed back down on its reciever once again. She wasn't crying, but Mindy rubbed at her eyes in hopes to prevent such a thing from happening. 'Mindy?' Michael mumbled again. His instinct told him that Markus called up again, but he quickly dismissed the thought. He didn't seem important enough for her to cry over anymore. 'My dad...' she stammered, biting her bottom lip. Christ, I can't even write the word 'father' down, not in reference to him. It makes me sick to my stomach to have to think of him as my dad.. or so much as acknowledge the fact that he's related to me. 'He found out that I'm wrestling. He wants me to come home right now.' Michael didn't know what to say. Even though he more than knew about how it felt to have someone to wanted them to stop wrestling, it was still so different. Michael loved Jaden, and wanted to keep her happy. Mindy, however... 'I, I'm sorry,' he stuttered finally. 'Don't be,' Mindy answered as she shook her head, pink and black hair swaying in front of her face. 'It wasn't your fault I ran away from home.' They both looked across the room at eachother; the darkness was too overpowering for them to truly see into eachother's eyes, but that wasn't needed. Just as long as Mindy knew that someone was around- someone who gave a shit- it was all just fine. 'What am I going to do?' she questioned. Michael hesitated, before he wavered his arms in an unsure manner. He couldn't tell her with a clear conscience that it was okay for her to keep running from her parents, but with how upset she was, he couldn't exactly up and say that everything would be fine in the morning if she caught the first flight back to Italy. 'Sleep on it,' he whispered. 'Give yourself some time to think before you go doing anything else. That answer good enough for you?' Mindy gave a small pout, before she nodded quickly and gave her cousin a quick hug. 'Yup, I think so... thank you, Mikey.' 'Don't mention it, cousin.' It was good advice at the time... but now I just wish I could get to sleep. How can I with everything that's going on right now? I wish I had a concrete decision to go by; a way to get rid of my father for good, and a way that doesn't involve bloodshed. Is that so hard to come up with? It can't be that difficult... Whirling around in her seat, she was surprised to find Jaden standing in the doorway. 'Mindy, come out here, please, I need your help. It's Michael...'
