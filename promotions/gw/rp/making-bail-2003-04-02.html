Making bail

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/2/03 About an hour has passed since the brawl that broke out in the holding cell involving Brick, Jesse Briggs and Ely Braxton. They’ve since been placed in a separate holding cell. The bruises and cuts show just how intense the brawl really was. Of course, the three managed to give more than they got. An officer comes down to their cells with keys. “Braxton! Briggs! Randall! You’ve just made bail!” he says as he unlocks the cell door. “Good ol” Logan,” says Jesse. “Yeah, well, “good ol” Jesse? took his sweet damn time gettin? here with our bail money!” Brick replies as they exit the cell. “Aw, quit yer bitchin”!” shouts a voice coming down the hall. The voice belongs to Logan Creed, the Hardcore Dwarf. “You know how long it took me to get the money together to bail you three out?” “Yes, we know, you damn munchkin!” “Dwarf, damn it! Dwarf! How the hell many times I gotta say it”! I ain’t a midget! I ain’t a munchkin! Got it?!” “I ain’t in the mood for semantics right now, Creed!” “You an” Jesse are always in bad moods!” Creed shoots back “Look, let’s just get the hell outta here,” says Braxton. “Yeah, we got a match to get ready for.” The four men walk out of the jailhouse towards Creed’s car. “Well, one thing’s for sure,” says Brick. “We got a lot of planning to do. Cause, knowin” Salaok, he’s probably thinking up a hundred ways to screw us over next week.” “No kidding.” “You really think he had somethin” to do with Bubba’s coma?” Briggs asks. “Would you really put some shit like that past Salaok, of all people?” Brick replies. “If he is behind Bubba’s coma,” says Braxton, “I plan to make that son of a bitch pay and pay dearly!” “Now who’s taking shit personally” Aren’t you the one who told me we have to keep it strictly business?” “Know what” Fuck what I said! You didn’t hear Christina’s voice when she called me that day! Now Bubba McCoy’s one of the best men I know! And I promised myself that I was gonna make whoever harmed him pay!” “I know how you feel, man,” says Brick. “I felt the same way when Salaok and them put Aaron through hell just to get to me. But, like you said, we can’t let personal shit sidetrack us!” “He’s right, Ely,” says Briggs. “We gotta keep our minds on what’s important. We lose this match, those charges stick, and we go back to jail.” “And I’m out of a job, and I ain’t goin” out like that! Salaok ain’t puttin? me out like that! I’m ready to fight. Are y’all with me?” “Oh HELL YEAH!!” Briggs and Braxton shout. “Good! Now I gotta get my stuff from the Arena.” “Yeah, and get your ass thrown back in jail,” says Briggs. “I ain't got no choice. My hotel room key and a whole bunch of other stuff are in my duffel bag, an” my duffel bag is in my locker.” “Well, they’ll give you another key. Don’t worry.” “Yeah, you’re right. It’s Salaok and his little bitches who better start worrying.”
