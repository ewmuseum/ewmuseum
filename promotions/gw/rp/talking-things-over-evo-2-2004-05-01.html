Talking things over (Evo #2)

1 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/01/04 Almost a week after their awesome performance at Wrestlecade IV, Donny and Charles McNasty are having a pep rally of sorts at the Drunken Leprechaun. A local band by the name of Bad Daze is playing their covers of the 80’s and 90’s rock and roll tunes in the background, Donny and Charles are up at the bar enjoying the sights and sounds tonight, the two brothers are also trying to do some catching up and figuring out some things involving Charles’s new career in professional wrestling? “Just listen to some of my ideas Chuck, you might like some of them.” Donny Said. “Like what, last night you come up with the bright idea of me having midgets in business suits carry me down to the ring’I don’t feel like getting into the same trouble you’re in now little brother” quipped Charles. “So that wasn’t the best of ideas, but listen you need to come up with a catch name, like me I am The Nomad’Let’s see you could be’THE AGENT’no’hmmm’The CEO’no not good either’How about the Apprentice?” asked Donny. “Uh Donny, The Apprentice is a reality tv show on NBC hosted by Donald Trump?” answered Charles. “Damn that Trump! He’s always getting in the way of things” Donny said. “That’s the truth, if it wasn’t for Donald Trump, well over half the buildings in the Big Apple would be mine’Real Estate is tough business Donny, it’s all about location, location, location.” Charles said. “Hey, what about The Big Apple Charles D McNasty” Donny replies. “Um no, I don’t want the name of a fruit, which could be taken in several different and wrong directions.” Said Charles. “Well that’s a valid point, hmmm well I guess when the time comes for a special nickname we’ll know it. Now lets talk entrance music.” Said Donny. “Well what about it, I mean I like Whiskey in the Jar for when we come down to the ring what did you have in mind?” asked Charles “I was thinking with your personality and one of your favorite groups growing up was KISS, that you could use a KISS song?” replies Donny. “That’s about the first idea in the last 24 hours that’s made good sense. Let me think of a KISS song, Tears are Falling is a good one, but not for Wrestling, I got it, God of Thunder.” Charles Said. “Sweet song, I love that song. That’s perfect your own entrance music will be God of Thunder by KISS. See now were cooking with Gas” Donny Said. “So how is the training with your Leprechauns going?” asks Charles. “Well it’s been interesting to say the least’I didn’t realize how hard it would be to train these guys. I mean they’re drunk 24/7. Timmy, the one more outspoken leprechaun I was trying to teach him how to do Irish whips and reverse them, well he ended up puking from getting so dizzy. I don’t know how he got dizzy, I only Irish whipped him once. The room was probably already spinning when I did to him, I’m not too worried about them losing though” replied Donny “What happens if they do lose though?” ask Charles “Trust me they won’t lose” replies Donny. “I wish I had your confidence about everything Donny, ever since we were kids you were like that. Some things just never change” Charles said. Two very nice looking blondes who God obviously blessed with beautiful brains, come up to the two McNasty brothers, and ask them to dance. “Hell yeah I’m up for it ladies, what about you Chuck, come out and dance.” Donny said. Charles lifts his left hand up to show his wedding ban and the girls acknowledge it and tell him that it’s his loss. The two buxom blondes and Donny get out on the dance floor and dance the night away as Charles just sits at the bar and watches still taking in the energy of the night. If the McNasty Boyz win the Tag Team Invitational it’s sure to be a shake up in the Tag division in Global Wrestling, tune in Monday night to Evolution only on HBO.
