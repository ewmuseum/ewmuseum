Date: Wednesday The inside of an upscale gym is shown. Several people fill the building all working out on different machines in various areas. Deacon Holmes is seen working out on a bench situated in front of a large mirror. CW puffs a cigar as he half watches Holmes who is attempting to push up his final rep. Sweat pours from every pore as veins bulge from every inch of his body. He begins to grunt as he slowly pushes the weight half way up shaking all the way. He takes short quick breaths as he pushes the bar a little higher before getting stuck. Holmes can’t seem to get the bar up any further and needs help. He manages to call CW in between breaths causing him to stare at him oddly before kneeling down while sucking on his cigar. After a minute of watching Holmes attempt to push the weight off he blows smoke right into his face causing Holmes to shoot the weight right up and immediately sit up coughing and holding his throat. After recovering he turns his attention to CW who is really laughing it up. Holmes? arm shoots out snatching the cigar from CW’s mouth and casting it aside before crushing it with his foot. Deacon Holmes: What tha hell was that for? You forget the meaning of the word spot? To detect or discern, especially visually you idiot! You weren’t even looking, and why tha hell do you have a cigar anyway? This is a gym damnit! CW: Stop cryin?, you got it up didn’t you? That’ll help you push the guy who’s pinning you off when you’re so exhausted from getting your ass kicked you can’t breathe. I can guarantee you Chaz Bryant is training as we speak sunshine. And I’m pretty sure he’s not very happy about you screwing around with his mom. Poor son of a bitch keeps tryin to tell everyone she died giving birth to him. Both men look at each other in silence before bursting out in laughter Deacon: Yeah, what he doesn’t know is she put him up for adoption. Smart move on her part, psycho bastard’s out runnin around killin people. And not even surreptitiously, this asshole is killin people in broad daylight right in front of witnesses. Why that nutbag isn’t locked up right now is beyond me. I hope some mob boss knocks him off before I have to lose one of my shoes on this guy. After our match he’s gonna be carted out on a stretcher with three shoes on. Two on his feet and one lodged firmly in his ass. CW: Hahah, good one D. Have you heard what that chump said about you yet? I don’t understand it but apparently he says he’s going to fry his fist in your face. Deacon Holmes: Huh? CW: Don’t look at me I don’t understand it either. I hear he’s trainin pretty hard you ya’lls match on Thursday though. Deacon Holmes: Hahaha, training for what!” To attain an as yet unknown level of mediocrity!” Is that even possible for him? I mean I know no one in GW is quite on my level but Chaz Bryant is really scrapin the bottom of the barrel, they don’t come much worst than him. I don’t know why I’ve even gotta waste a whole three minutes of my valuable time on him Thursday. CW: Three minutes D? Deacon Holmes: Yeah, two minutes and fifty-seven seconds to do pushups. CW: Good thinkin. Deacon Holmes: As usual. I’m a step ahead of all these clowns and tomorrow night Chaz Bryant is gonna find out first hand. He might as well not even show up. I mean I dazzle the eye and boggle the mind but what does he do? Give all my fans someone to boo and throw their empty cups and popcorn buckets at? Bryant is a walking garbage dump and come Over, I mean Deaconload, I’m gonna put him out like the trash he is. CW and Holmes clear the bench and move on to the next station while laughing at Chaz Bryant jokes.
