Better Days (Through the Eyes of Weakness)

26 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/26/05 'Nobody knows, the trouble I've seen...' Michael Mercer sat in reflection inside his black Firebird, playing the recent events in his mind repeatedly. The result was a feeling of helplessness, and the confrontation he had endured did not help one bit; if anything, it merely helped Michael convince himself more and more that he was going to have to 'choke a bitch', as his unsong hero Wayne Brady had put it. Michael felt he was only inches away from his wife, but yet he came up with nothing. No lead, no names, no whereabouts, nothing. Simply put: there was no way Michael would find Jaden on his own. And without having any clues to give to police, how would they be able to do anything more than him? They were trained professionals with weapons and numbers, sure, but unless they were keeping secrets from the public, they sure as hell weren't psychics. Life wasn't always fair, but to Michael, life was as bitter as an unpaid whore- without the STDs. 'Nobody knows, my sorrow...' Michael put his car in drive, and took off towards one thing that he hoped could at least take his mind off things. A good beer always managed to do the trick, why wouldn't it now? Michael would soon find that out for himself. What's the use in hoping When you always get let down? What's the use in smiling When all you wanna do is frown? I'm drowning in a sea of hate-filled eyes And she's screaming But no one hears her cries... Michael and Gabi sat across from eachother on the little girl's bed, looking down at the vacation magazine in between the two of them. They had been debating that entire day where he should take both her and Jaden on vacation for the summer. They had narrowed it down to only two choices but they both seemed equal in appeal, thus making it quite difficult to come to a proper decision. 'Well,' Michael uttered, throwing a lopsided glance at the teddy bear clock on the wall above Gabriella's dresser drawer. 'It's going on ten o'clock, and not only do you have to get to bed in a soon, but mom's gonna be home from work, too. 'Daddy, why do you say in a soon?' Gabi shot back, completely ignoring the decision Michael had tried to put on her. Michael smiled and scratched his head. 'Because daddy's taken too many bumps to the head and thinks 'in a soon' is a funny phrase for some reason. Now, Italy or the Bahamas? Quick.' 'Weeell... Italy is fun, and we have family there and we've been there before and we've all had fun there... so I'd love to go back there...' Michael smiled. 'So it's settled then.' And then came the word Michael had been dreading. 'Buuuuut...' Michael's smiled turned upside down in a flash. 'But what, sweetheart?' Gabi's tiny hands reached out and fingered through the pages of the magazine in front of her. Her eyes went into a dreamy gaze when they fell upon the pages she had been looking for. She took the magazine and flipped it over, allowing Michael to look at the picture; a crystal clear ocean, sandy beach, beautiful sunset- the perfect picture of what a vacation should look like. 'But the.. the..' 'Bahamas,' Michael cut in, helping his daughter out. She grinned at that one. 'Yeah, that place... well, we've never been there, and it just looks so purdy!' Michael chuckled. 'Well, hun, you're gonna have to pick between whether going to a place that you already know you're gonna love, or a place that looks like it'd be something you're gonna love.' 'Well, whadda you think, daddy?' Gabi prodded further. Michael tilted his head, smile growing wider. 'Heh, daddy's opinion isn't all that matters, hun. This is a vacation that I want you and mom to be happy on, I shouldn't really have to have influence in deciding where you would want to go for vacation.' 'So then why not let mommy decide with us?' 'I already told you, this is our little secret. I want her to be surprised, and I already have a feeling she would be happy with either of these two places, so it's just a matter of picking which one.' Well, Michael had a point there. Gabi stayed silent for just a moment. 'I think...' Michael leaned forward slightly to catch Gabriella's answer, waiting for it anxiously- he needed time away from New Mexico almost as much as they did. 'Yeh?' 'I think... erm, how do you say the name of that place again?' Michael laughed and sat back, opening his mouth to say the word his daughter was thinking of, when... 'What place?' 'Uh oh,' Michael exhaled. Jaden. 'What place?' his wife questioned again, arms crossed. 'Is there something you've been planning... Mikey?' Michael looked up at Jaden. The couple both exchanged smiles. 'Now you see, you called me Mikey, now I'm definitely not going to tell you anything.' Gabi acted fast, and quickly slid the magazine behind her back. Jaden noticed the shuffling of paper, both from the sight of it and the sound of it alike. 'Don't make me come over there, Michael James Mercer...' Jaden grinned. Michael grinned right back. 'Or what, wifey? What can you possibly do to me to have me tell you about these plans of mine?' Michael looked over at Gabi, who looked back at him. That moment, Michael could see his daughter's eyes shift to something on the nightstand next to him. A can of silly string; the very object Jaden despised more than anything. Michael tried hard not to laugh. 'Okay, Mikey, now you've asked for it...' Jaden started in, diving at Michael and landing on top of him. Michael fell backwards onto the bed, and nonchalantly reached over to the silly string. 'I told you not to call me Mikey.' Before Jaden knew it, caught a wad of multi-colored silly string right in the face. Instinctively, she screamed in absolute horror. 'Dammit, Michael, you know I hate it when you guys use that stuff!' She meant to be angry by that, but she was too busy laughing to seem the slightest bit enraged. Michael took advantage and rolled over, in essence sending the lovebirds tumbling out of bed and to the floor. More silly string flew, and more screams were to be had, but in the end, both Michael and Jaden shared a soft, loving kiss. One that neither of them had gotten to share in a long time- one that made Gabi 'oooh' and 'aaah' at the sight of it. In fact, it was the last one they had shared at all. That was their night before Christmas. It was the next morning when Jaden learned about the trip to the Bahamas, and naturally she was overjoyed. Michael had never seen his wife so happy, and that in its own, made him happy. And between that time and the time that Michael was driving to the nearest bar in Truth Or Consequence, came the pleas from his cousin for him to go back to wrestling. It was that moment, where things went downhill. Fast forward to Neely's Bar, where Michael found himself drowning away his sorrows with the help of a bottle of Wild Turkey. He wasn't fully gone yet, but he wished he was; that any second, he'd let the drunken asshole inside of him take over, maybe get into a barfight, and he'd soon be worrying about things that didn't involve his wife. If only it were that easy. And instead of drinking the sadness under the table, it simply overwhelmed him. The bartender obviously was contemplating taking Michael's booze from him, but who was a three hundred pound butterball to get in the way of a tough-as-nails wrestler like Michael Mercer? Darren sure as hell wasn't that person, and he wasn't going to be that person. Instead, he would be content watching Michael sip anxiously at his alcohol, and test himself by not snickering at the numerous mopey faces Michael made with each passing chord of 'Better Days' by Thrice. Is anybody here to see a band? Did anybody come to hear a song? Does anyone believe in magic? Is anybody here to sing along? 'Who the fuck put this on?' Michael groaned in drunken annoyance, glaring daggers at the bartender. He soon noticed he was being watched by more than one person, however, and turned to look around at the people who surrounded him. Mainly young college kids, mostly of the emo type, and one or two hanging back in the shadows. Come to think of it... '...Was I really in need of a drink this badly to come to a Coheed And Cambria convention?' 'It's just the randomized playlist we have on now,' said the big boned man behind the bar tentatively. The last thing he wanted out of Michael was a fight, much less an intoxicated Michael. His bald head was beginning to generate a bit of sweat, both by from working as long a shift as he had been working, and the fact that he was growing more worried for his safety by the second. 'Urgh, turn it off...' Michael grumbled. Darren shook his head. 'I, I can't right n-' 'Turn it the fuck off.' People shove me down They're coming from all sides And as I look around Something just don't seem right I feel so all alone Though the people inundate I see no joy or desperation Only senseless hate 'Hey, man,' said one of the nearest college kids, 'how about you chill out? Maybe get a breath of fresh air, or get laid? Better yet, get a life?' Obviously, this man did not know who Michael F'n Mercer was. Slowly, Michael rose out of his seat. He was wobbly at first, as would any man be who had been chugging down Wild Turkey like he had, but quickly gained his balance. In his inebriated state, he slurred a sentence in the direction of Darren the overweight bartender. 'What if I punch this kid in the face?' he asked. Darren stared in disbelief. 'Here we go.. I'm going to get hospitalized.' 'Nothing fancy,' Michael continued, 'just calmly turn around and give him a clean right hook to the jaw?' 'That... will not be necessary,' said a voice from one of the men towards the back of the bar. One of those who sat in the shelter of the darkness. He had a hint of Chinese in his accent, which therefore made it catch Michael's attention. However, do not mistake that for Michael actually heeding the voice's advice. 'This isn't your fight, douche solution,' he called into the dark. The voice did not yell back in defense, no fists had flown... only a silhouette made its presence known. The figure stood up, and it was obvious that he was of a smaller build than Michael, but the man began to walk towards the drunk with little hesitation anyway. Normally, that would've meant Michael's right foot got introduced to someone's crotch, but this time, something was different. Is anybody here to see a band? Did anybody come to hear a song? Does anyone believe in magic? Is anybody here to sing along? 'I know,' the man stated calmly, 'but I think it would be better interest for me to step in here, rather than allow you to get your knuckles covered in this man's blood.' Out of the shadows he came, and a face was soon brought with that voice. Black hair, green eyes, a black suit... the Chinese man seemed rather out of place compared to the rest of the people in the vicinity, that was for sure. Michael huffed, and flared his nostrils. 'Yeh? What's it to you if I beat the shit out of this guy?' Michael balled up his right hand, while his left one brought the bottle of Wild Turkey to his lips for him to take a hard swig. The emo kid whose life was very well in jeopardy. 'Believe me,' the man started, 'I'd prefer to see a fight. But you will simply do me no good in jail for destroying a man who clearly isn't worth your time.' Michael's bottle of alcohol was slowly lowered from his lips, and the man unalarmingly took it from the tall Bronx native, placing it on the bar behind him. Michael blinked, trying to shake the drunk out of him. 'The hell do you want?' 'Simply to have a talk with one of my future co-workers.' The smaller man stepped past Michael, and over to the door. 'Let's go for a walk.' 'Now remind me,' Michael stuttered, talking through the cold night air that swept past both men as they walked down the main street just outside of Neely's Bar. 'What makes you so eager to talk to me again?' 'I'm very lucky to have gotten in touch with you, Michael Mercer,' replied the man. That calm speech of his did not make Michael feel any more relieved about the situation. He was drunk, upset, angry... and he had a headache; half from the drinks, and half from being told to get laid by a long-haired, acne-ridden emo kid. 'I believe that although we are quite different from one another, we could possibly help eachother out.' 'HAHA!' The loud, hilariously sarcastic laugh that Michael screamed out caused the few people also walking the streets that late evening to turn in the direction of the two men. Not that Michael cared, though, he was just being the dumb, obnoxious New Yorker that he always was. 'You help me? I highly doubt that. No one seems to know how to help me other than myself, and I completely botched the chances of doing any good for my own behalf already. I'm fucked as it is, pal, so take your Tony Little 'YOU CAN DO IT' speech and stick it in your urethra.' The Chinese man let out a sigh, almost as if disappointed by that remark. Michael stared at the smaller man, and shook his head. 'Where in Japan are you from, anyway?' 'I'm from China,' the man in black replied. '...Where in Japan is that exactly?' The man groaned and kept walking, turning at the corner. Michael noticed and soon followed right behind. 'Oh, nevermind, it's not important anyway.' A brief silence followed, but it wasn't long before the mysterious man spoke again. 'Oh.. forgive me for the rude lack of introduction. My name is T'sao Liang... it's a name I'm sure you have not heard before.. but soon, I'm sure it's one you will grow to appreciate.' 'I can feel the boner springing in my pants already,' Michael joked dryly. 'What the hell is it that makes you think you can help me?' 'Well,' Liang began, 'as I've already told you, I have signed a contract with Global Wrestling. It is my goal to be quite a successful man here in America, and defeating the best fighters here would be as successful as one can get.' 'Fighters...' Michael sighed. 'We're not fighters, we're wrestlers. Wrestlers compete for their love of the fans.. because it's safe.. if you want fighting, go to a place that isn't mired by bullshit. Those are the words of a man who wrestled for five long years. And look at me now, I'm drunk like fuck and I might as well drink myself dead. Wrestling did nothing other than give me a little leather belt with a piece of golden tin on it and told me to buy some new rims.' T'sao stopped in his tracks, a bit surprised by what the man had to say. When he realized the person next to him stopped walking, Michael did the same and turned around. 'What?' the New Yorker coughed. 'For a man who used to be quite famous, I must admit that I'm confused as to what you could be so sad about. May I ask, what has gotten you in such a decrepid mood?' Michael gave the man a look of repulsion, before he started walking again. 'None of your business.' 'That's fine.' Liang nodded slowly, and began walking as well. They came to another corner, and they both turned onto a quiet sidestreet. Between the darkness and the silence, it became too much for Michael to stand. What did he have to lose anymore, anyway? 'My wife and daughter.. someone...' 'Murderer?' the Chinese man asked slowly. Michael shook his head. 'Kidnapper.' Liang paused for a few moments, before a small smile formed on his face. In the darkness, Michael couldn't see it, but he knew something was up. 'Maybe I can help you after all,' he said thoughtfully. Michael sneered. 'You would be able to find my family's kidnapper? Even though a police squad can't, some Joe Schmoe like you can?' 'It wouldn't be the first time,' Liang answered with a shrug. 'And, what would make me want to believe you, of all people?' It was almost as if T'sao was waiting for that question to pop up throughout the entire conversation. 'In China, those who knew me called me Kongde. Sleeping Virtue... let me simply say.. back in China, I made a job out of putting hated people to sleep... permanently.' Michael was silent, as they both rounded the next corner, and slowly approached the main street once more. Upon rounding that corner, Michael guffawed. 'You're kidding, right?' Like really.. you're kidding?' In the bright streetlights shining in the distance, Michael could see the look on T'sao Liang's face- and it was not one that was joking around. 'If this was a joke, I would have had no reason to come and waste your time. It's simply a matter of whether you would like my assistance, or simply reach out into the darkness and blindly, stubbornly complete the search for your family on your own. Of course, I do not promise your family's lives, but at least with my help, you will find them much faster. That, my friend, I can promise you. I merely query.. is this an offer you want to take advantage of? Or are the lives of those you love not as important as you make them out to be?' Michael replayed those words in a dumbfounded shock. If anything got him out of his drunken haze, it was definitely that. 'Help me become a successful man in Global Wrestling, Michael Mercer, and I will give you back everything. Your wife.. daughter... the satisfaction of washing your hands in the blood of their attacker. Of course, you can take your time with this, as I'm sure you have many things on your mind that must be sorted out. I will come in contact with you another time, whether on your initiative or not.' The Chinese man continued along into the street, crossing it calmly as Michael stood out in front of Neely's Bar, watching as the rush of emotions surged through him. How he longed for the happy moments with his family once more... how he needed that vacation to the Bahamas with them. 'Wait..' was all he could muster out of his voice, but it was far too late. Michael sighed- the knowledge that he would have another day to give T'sao his answer was a welcome thought, but the fear that when another day came that his family could be dead, was not. Michael Mercer drove off into the night as a more bitter man than he was a few hours back, as the words to that song he hated played through his mind, complete with their somber guitar notes that coupled with them.
