Return to Regis and Kelly, pt. 1

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/3/03 “Live with Regis and Kelly” returns from commercial with the camera fading in and zooming on Regis Philbin and Kelly Ripa. Regis wears a grey suit and white shirt with a yellow tie. Kelly wears a sleeveless powder blue turtleneck and a black leather miniskirt. They look into the camera, smiling. “Welcome back,” says Regis. “For four years, our next guest has been one of the most well-know superstars in Global Wrestling. Recently, he achieved his dream of winning the World championship at their pay-per-view event Whiplash. Here he is, Brick!” As Regis and Kelly rise out of their seats to greet him, Brick comes out to his music with the World title over his right shoulder. He then walks over to Regis and shakes his hands. He then walks to Kelly and they give each other a peck on the cheek. Kelly then rubs her hand across the title belt before all three sit down. “You know, there’s something different about you,” Kelly quips. Brick chuckles a bit. “Yeah, I know. I gained thirty pounds last week,” Brick jokes, patting the title belt. “Yeah, we can see,” Regis replies. “Thirty pounds, huh?” “Yeah.” “Just be thankful you didn’t have to get pregnant to get those thirty pounds,” adds Kelly. “Now the last time you were here, after your segment was done, I remembered what you said to us.” “Yeah, I promised the two of you that I wasn’t gonna come back to the show until I won the World title. I felt I had something to prove. That’s what I thought of myself. That the next time I came, I wanted to have something to show. Last time I was here, I had just lost a chance to become no. 1 contender. And I have such respect for you two, I was determined not to come back until I’d won the title.” “Good thing you won it.” “Yeah, or y’all probably never would have seen me again. Kelly’s kids would probably be in puberty by the time I won the title.” “Well, the way she goes, she’d probably have an army by then.” Kelly gives him a faux shocked look. Brick puts up his hands and goes, “Whoa, I didn’t say anything. That was him.” “You’re not scared of her, are you?” “Don’t start, Regis.” “Now, everyone knows, you’re not exactly a rookie. You’ve been at this for four years.” “Yeah, four long years. This was the fourth actual shot at the World title that I’ve gotten in those four years.” “And there was word going around that you wouldn’t get another shot after this one.” “Yeah, there was that going around. But I worked hard to get to that point, and I was gonna see to it that all the hard work paid off.” “Well, that it did,” says Regis. “We’re gonna take a break and come right back with Brick.” The audience applauds as the scene dissolves to footage from “Legally Blonde 2”, specifically of co-star Regina King and the announcer saying “Tomorrow on Live: From the new movie Legally Blond 2, actress Regina King.”
