Underworld Business Journal Tony Stecchino Global Wrestling Promo #646 Date: 10/10/03 Location: NA THE UNDERWOLD BUSINESS JOURNAL Stecchino Takes Over Yakuza Shipping In a huge business venture, Anthony Stecchino of Stecchino Investments has taken 100% control of Yakuza Shipping. Stecchino, 24, is currently working for The Upper Echelon of faction declaring war on Global Wrestling based in Dallas, Texas and could not be reached for comment. Stecchino Investments opened just over two years ago when Anthony Stecchino was released from prison after the alleged murder of his wife in the winter of 2001. After a shocking turn of events it was discovered Stecchino plotted the whole thing out to gain sympathy from those around Global Wrestling. It worked. Then upon his release from a mental health facility in the Seattle area of Washington State, he returned to GW and dropped the biggest bomb shell on everyone in GW. Melissa was still alive and Silent Wind had not committed the murder, but was an accessory to murder and it was a huge plot between the two. Anthony Stecchino has amounted a huge reservoir or business contacts from all over the world. America to Italy to Germany to Russia to the Orient. He is a true master of business and tact. And has netted a worth of nearly three billion dollars in the past two years. Already adding to the millions he made through professional wrestling and television ventures before his 21st birthday. Yakuza Shipping was a world renowned shipping company that shipped everything from auto parts to automatic weapons and drugs. Yakuza had an absolute clean record as far as not being criminally prosecuted by authorities and remains that way today. Yakuza has over twenty two thousand employees to date. “As far as I know all the jobs are going to be saved and no one will be laid off with out reason.” Said new VP of Operations Yoshii Traumata. “The company is really excited about the change in ownership and should progress nicely through these hard and troubled economic times, perpetrated by the inimitability of the President of the United States of America, George Bush.” Yakuza has offices all over the world, 173 to be exact. 24 in North America, 31 in Central and South America, 27 in Europe, 14 in Africa, 23 in the Middle East, 16 in Australia and 38 in the Orient. Before his untimely death Mr. Yakuza had established a worth of nearly 25 billion dollars, which in business signings left all the money to the companies majority stock holder, Anthony Stecchino. He pulled off one of the biggest business deals in the history of the world; he more than quadrupled his net worth in a single meeting. He has a nose for business. Bill Gates should watch out. Tony Stecchino is quickly becoming a force reckoned with in the business world.
