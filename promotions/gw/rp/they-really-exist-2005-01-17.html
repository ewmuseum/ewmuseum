They Really Exist?

17 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/01 -August 27th- 2004 'Good Morning Mr Lei, I hope you enjoyed your flight over to England. It's a beautiful country. The people there are to be respected Choi Yen, do not besmerch them!' We find ourselves listening to the same well spoken British gentlemen Choi Yen is working for - Charles Gates. The rumbling sound of an aeroplan taking off is heard in the background as Choi Yen sits inside the airport. His lap top resting on his thighs. 'Listen carefully Mr Lei. This is a clear picture of the son of a b!tch who as my treasure. I must warn you now... he is some what of a freak.... I believe he claims to be a Vampire!? He is crazy Choi Yen... his name is Ethan Rayne.' Lawrence looks at the picture, and studies the rest of the information Charles has sent him... 'And finally... I thought I would give you a little something. I don't know if you will really need it, ha, I'm quite certain you're already equiped. If you look in the left sleeve of your bag you will find a key to locker 606 at your hotel. Good look, and I hope to see you back here very soon!' Lawrence sighs before shutting his laptop. He reaches inside his bag and feels for the key. As soon has he grips it he laughs then shakes his head. He places his laptop carefully back inside his brief case then pulls himself up to his feet and heads for the exit. Two Hours Later Lawrence inserts his key in to locker 606 and pulls the door open. He reaches inside the locker and pulls out a silver barretter, much like his own two golden guns. He looks carefully at the gun then shakes his head. 'Cheap!... so very cheap!' He says as he tooks the gun in the inside pocket of his jacket. He looks around for anyone that may have seen him but nobody else is in the room.... so he thinks. He feels a slight draft on his left ear, as though somebody is blowing on him... quickly he turns around holding his ear. He looks around the room once again and slowly walks towards the exit... nothing! He walks in to the next section of the locker area, but he is the only person in the room. Lawrence relaxes for a second... 'So... you here on vacation' Lawrence turns around quickly. There standing infront of the 606 locker is an average sized man, wearing a hat, his head is tilted preventing Lawrence from seeing his face... He is leaning on the locker, his hands are hidden inside his pockets. 'Just visiting family...' Lei says, happy to stick with his faulse excuse. 'That's cool Lawrence... or would you prefer to be called Choi Yen?' The strange man says as he slowly looks up at the confused Chinese Man. Lawrence squints his eyes thinking to himself as he examines the mans face. Quickly he clicks... his face turns red as he is now staring in to the eyes of Ethan Rayne! Maybe Ethan doesn't know why Lawrence is actually in England... but how does he know his name?... all these thoughts are running through Lawrences head... 'How is it that you know who I am!?' Asks Lawrence as he takes a couple of steps closer... 'Oh I've seen you before Lawrence...' Ethan says, this time standing behind Lawrence... Lawrence is a little startled by that just happened. How is it that Ethan can just dissappear then reappear in a split second behind him? Lawrence finds himself backpeddling, Ethan begins to follow him... 'I've seen you on the streets of LA... I use to spend quite some time watching you while you work. You are quite the Assassin... But then you disappeared for quite some time. Which was really impressive. I have never lost track of anybody except you!' Exclaims Ethan as he rubs the stubble under his chin. Ethan knows Lawrence is an Assassin, but he is still unsure of why Lawrence is in England... Lawrence places his hand inside his pocket. Ethan stares down at his hand, then smiles. 'I could have caught you Lawrence, If I was in charge of all of the murder investigations you can bet your ass you would be in jail! I was the best at what I did... which is why I enjoyed watching you so much. I loved how the FBI struggled with every investigation.' Ethan pauses for a second as he sees Lawrences hand move as though he is pressing the safety cap on his silenced weapon. 'Somebody else was also watching you Lawrence! A man named Charlie Gates. I followed him around too, and learned about all of his plots... one of them was to kill You! Another was to kill the man responsible for the death of his Brother! and the current owner of this...' Ethan pulls open his jacket to reveal a gold medaleon... In a split second Lawrence pulls the gun out from his pocket and begins to shoot! But before the bullet could even leave the cannon Ethan disappears without a trace! Lawrence runs in to the second row of lockers and ducks... he peeps around the corner and sees Ethans shadow... 'So! You are working for Charlie Gates... I had a strange feeling that I am the reason why you are here in England Lawrence. But before you go jumping to too many conclusions, there is something else I want you to know... I want you to know the reason why Charlie Gates wants you dead. And I also want you to know that...' Ethan stops speaking to dodge more bullets from Lawrence's gun... Lawrence as quick as a cat caught Ethan off guard. But Ethan dispite being hit is able to find another hiding place... Lawrence ducks infront of another row of lockers... 'You are good Lawrence I'll give you that... So I'll just go ahead and tell you this now. Yoshimitzu was working for Charlie Gates! So... Charlie Gates is the reason why you and your Father are separated from this planet... You can either choose to believe me Lawrence, or you can continue wasting your time trying to kill someone that cannot be killed by the weapon of a mortal!...' Exclaims Ethan as he vanishes from the locker room. Lawrence is very confused now. He knows Ethan has gone but does not know whether he should believe him or not... so he just sits there, his gun still in his hand, his head bowed down...
