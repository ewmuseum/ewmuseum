The Road to Retribution: Part II

Date: Today The Road to Retribution Part 2: Conflict of Interest '... I'm going to leave now. I'm going to go along to Evolution and I'm going to try and sort out as much of this matter as I can.' '... but, why do you want to do anything more than you need to? Why do you want to try and put yourself in the line of fire. I can understand how angry you are... but the more I think about it, the more I have to wonder why you can't just press ahead with the future. I'm angry too, but you'll get your chance to fight for the prize when the time is right.' 'This isn't a matter of being angry or annoyed anymore. After thinking about it yesterday, I came to realize that guy only came down to attack me because he want's to start a fire. He wants to start a party... and I'm the invited guest.' The scene opens inside the hotel room of Rich Sherwin. He'd been staying there for most of the last week, struggling to both come to terms with and understand the predicament which had led to his defeat at Clash of the Titans. He'd wasted through five days just trying to think of a reason why Dylan would come down and do such a thing. Nobody knew; nobody understood... and most likely, nobody cared. They had a winner of the contest and that's all that really mattered. Sherwin stood at the door to the room, his back facing his sister; who had stayed the last night as their conversation ran on for a longer duration than expected. He was dressed in a pair of black Nike trainers, baggy blue jeans, white vest and a white visor. At this moment in time, Rich didn't have any baggage but it'd be safe to assume that anything he did have had already been taken down to the foyer of the establishment. '... Rich, you know how much we were all against your decision to start wrestling again. You know that it's these kinds of vendetta's that bring the family down. We both know that while somebody does one thing to affect you, you're always going to attack back in a manner that is ten times more destructive!' she screamed, placing extreme emphasis on the latter of her words. Dressed in loose fitting blue jogging pants and a zipped up tracksuit top, she flung her arms down in anger, stomping her foot at the same time. Rich pulled grabbed hold of the doorknob with his right hand, stepping back as he pulled it open. 'Would you rather that I let this slide to the side? Would you rather everyone who has supported me through my career think of me as being nothing more than a coward?' Sherwin sounded a little bemused, unable to comprehend why his sister would want him to turn away and look so small in the eyes of the world. 'Captain Retirement' turned around, his facial expression being close to hysterical. He waited a few moments for the words to settle before continuing, '... This business, this line of work; it's all about the reputation that you have and the actions you take when confronted by a stupid son-of-a-bitch. This isn't a playground, you can't tell a teacher and hope that everything is going to be OK a few hours later. If you want somebody to understand that they did wrong, if you want somebody to realize that they messed up, YOU need to take the initiative, YOU need to pass sentencing AND execute it.' Jo turned around, thrashing her arms about wildly before crossing them over and looking down to the floor. Within moments, the sounds of her sobbing could be heard. Rich didn't care though. He didn't have time to care and he didn't have the emotion. Instead of walking over to his sister to console her, the GW athlete turned around and walked through the open door. He was walking out of her care, he was walking out of her sorrow, he was walking out of her life. '... If you don't appreciate it, don't concern yourself with it. I didn't ask you to come along and I didn't ask for your sympathies.' Rich said, his voice trailing off into a whisper as he walked further and further away from his heartbroken sister. Now, the man had taken his destiny into his own hands. He'd made a decision on what he wanted to do, he'd made a decision on what he wanted to accomplish. All he'd really need now is a means and a time for when the wheels of a plan were going to be in motion. Passing through corridors and walking down stairs, Sherwin made the journey to the foyer alone. For the first time, he'd recieved no 'goodbye' from his sister, no hug from his sister and no love from his sister. The determined man paced through the foyer, people looking at the expression on his face; trying desperately to identify what could be on his mind. '... Perhaps he's having woman problems?' 'Maybe he's quit wrestling again?' 'Apparently, GW cut the room service to his room and he's walking out because he couldn't have his way.' The voices continued to buzz in the background. The rumours, the lies, the jokes, they continued to fly around the establishment, even after the superstar had pushed his way through the entrance. Taking what was his last few steps before he approached a bright yellow taxi on the busy streets, Rich turned around to look at the doors from whence he came. '... I suppose that was the last time I'm going to see you for the immediate future.' he said, taking a few more seconds to simply stare at the entrance before turning and getting into the back of the taxi. Even then though, he turned and looked to the entrance of the hotel, secretly hoping that... just maybe, his sister would have come down one last time. Both parties were angry and upset with one another, their conflicting ideals always the cause of many battles. This time though, it wasn't just a petty arguement that went beyond their level of control, it was an issue of pride. 'Where to?' interrupted the taxi-driver in an unusually gruff voice. Rich turned his attention from the hotel entrance and looked ahead, his eyes meeting those of the driver via a mirror on the dashboard of the taxi. '... Take me to the nearest airport.' Rich replied, his voice lacking his usual enthusiastic and often sarcastic attitude. Nodding somewhat, the driver turned his key in the car's ignition and proceeded to drive off, Rich giving the hotel entrance one final look before the scene faded deep into black. Today, Rich had been unfortunate enough to lose a link in his family chain. Prior to Clash of the Titans, everything had been going in his way to a degree. Even during the match, Rich felt that he had what it took to get through everyone else and be the brightest star of the night. Unfortunately, something happened... and this something is setting off of a chain reaction that is going to have casualties for many weeks to come. : End :
