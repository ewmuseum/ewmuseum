The Memory Remains #14

22 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 082203 John Zero struggles up the stairs, shirtless Deacon Holmes leaning heavily against his right shoulder. Slowly, they climb the mountain of stairs, one step at a time, Zero obviously straining at the extra weight of the nearly unconscious Holmes. After reaching the top of the stairs, both men collapse in a heap, Holmes unaware and Zero cussing a storm. “Remind me to kick your ass when you wake up,” Zero mutters to himself. Zero stands up, looking down at Holmes. “The hell with this,” Zero says, reaching down and grabbing Holmes by the ankle, dragging him down the hallway. Zero pulls Holmes into a bedroom, picks him up and throws him onto the bed. Turning, Zero exits the room, turning off the light. Zero walks slowly down the hallway, back down the stairs, into the main room of the mansion. He crosses the large space, heading towards the bar he had setup for Holmes? birthday celebration. Help me. Zero retrieves a double-old-fashion glass from the bar and a half-full bottle of Jack Daniel’s. He pours a tall glass of the brown colored liquor. He picks up the glass, raising it to his lips, downing half the glass. Heaven help me. What am I doing? I don’t even know anymore. Everywhere I turn. There she is. Still taunting me. Pointing at me. Laughing at me. Zero downs the rest of the glass, filling it once more. He raises it once again, downing it this time in one long pull. He holds the glass to his face, turning it over and over in his hands. Zero turns, hurling the glass across the room, watching as the glass shattered, and sending a shower of shards across the room. Zero picks up the bottle and begins to lift it to his lips, pausing just before taking a pull. First Matthew. Then Robert. Zero places the bottle back on the bar. A coincidence? No. It couldn’t be. Zero cocked his head to one side, replaying the past in his mind. Johnnie Vincent. The villain that Deacon and I have been plaguing for the past several months. But how? How does he link back to Damien Vincitti? How? How does he link to Lily? Zero nudges the bottle of Jack Daniel’s, tipping it over, spilling the contents all over the bar. Maybe its not all over with like I thought. “Problems?” a feminine voice calls out behind John, who whirls around, facing the voice. A scantily clad redhead stands before John, her small black leather bikini barely covering her well-endowed body. John raised an eyebrow, approaching the woman. “Not anymore, no,” Zero said with a smile, placing one arm around her shoulders and walking away with her.
