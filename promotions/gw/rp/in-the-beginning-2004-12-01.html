In the beginning

1 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/01/04 I'm an original thinker, that's what people say. A person who will lead people to oblivion one day. A person of charm , with a talent for speech. That no goal shall be altered or out of my reach. With a smile favored by the masses, and a heart of pure gold, It was too late to know what divine destiny would hold. With an unspoken knowledge of how to manipulate the mind, The masses come to my aid with great loyalty refined. The skilled hand of a surgeon, is my hand with a pen, Showing all those who read the true darkness within. The universe's most lonely of creatures, living in a world of shame, Hoping to reap of th of the world's terrible riches and fame. My destiny decided as the morning star fell from heaven, To perform acts as viscious and vile as the unholy seven. Great works will I do, seen as the future's new light, My true nature unseen by humanity's dim sight. None care of those deeds, that in mind and body touch them not, All of humanity forgets it is the human spirit i've sought. For it is in the spirit I see my greatest desire, A land of hatred, brimstone, and fire. I've been seen as great evil for having pride in how I was made, But in this new era of life , those thoughts have begun to fade. I am the shadow of the morning star that faded into the coming dawn. Showing humanity my true glory as time marches on. Doing that which all creatures do, protecting all like them and true. To always love and mean good, but it is sin we love to do. And having done those most sensational of deeds, humanity falls short; weakend in the knees. So I teach not to be more than one's self, being the highest of sin. As the morning star once did and did not win. So as a mortal of mortals I shall always be, Being neither good nor evil than what is divinely expected of me. And with that it is why humanity always gives me a smile. For it is without a doubt they all look with denial. That none can be of great horror and monstosity. Or none could fathom the most guesome atrocity. But it is by divine eye that onle sees the darkness of humanity, And that which alters mortals hint of sanity. That we are only as good or evil as seen in the mind's eye, But the mind lies, corrupts, and yet simplifies. To see the thought of becoming more than we are. The same thought that punished the great morning star. Thereby making his thought the counter part of good, Or our want for the same, viewed misunderstood. And in that same thought making us like him. In that showing too, humanity is quite grim. To having the thoughts of the morning star viewed from his eyes. Giving limitless possibilities that the mortal mind can demise. Which is why humanity sees me with a heart of gold. As I have not crossed the limits as divinely foretold. For I am as I am, a mere mortal of mortals. An original thinker with the keys to humanity's darkest portals. For once again humanity doubts; They look with denial. And all I show them is an innocent smile. Knowing I have thoughts of horror and monstrocity. Committing such vile acts of gruesome atrocity. Being no more good or evil than what is divinely expected of me. And the only one whose mind has been truly set free. As I see what we are and dare reaching for that prize. Pleasure in insanity and climaxing in lies. Do any wish to stand the test of time? Seeing if there is truth of this disturbing rhyme. To see if one can be as powerful as he? The original thinker that is incarnate in me. For now is the time to show it can be done. And in that, such power shall be found and won. I'm an original thinker. What will you say? Will you quote this rather unique rhyme one day? Will you stand in front of others and make the same point as I? That in thought of becoming more, one shall eternally die. Shall one such as you see the way it takes back to me? The violent, angry, sadistic path it takes to be free.... He stopped reading the letter, not knowing what to think about it. It's not often a letter comes to someone with no return address or without saying who it is from. And yet he could not look away. None of this made since to him. He ran a hand through his long white hair as he lay the letter down on a small coffee table. He looked down at the black envelope that the letter came in and slowly reaching for it and opening it to see still in the envelope a small business card. All it said was 'The Morning Star'. He looked at the card curiously before turning it over and seeing a phone number printed on the back. He quickly picked up his cell phone and without ever taking his stone colored eyes off the card; he began to dial the number. 'I have been waiting on your call.' 'What's going on? I get a weird poem in the mail that from what I gathered was some sort of invitation.' 'You are correct.' 'So who are you guys or should I say what are you guys?' 'We are the Morning Star. For the moment that is all the information you require.' 'Why me?' 'Our operatives have been watching you for sometime sir. We know you possess incredible power and we also know you try and hide it as much as possible.' 'How do you know about my...' 'It's what we do. We know all. We see all. Although now and then someone catches are attention more than anything else. You are one of those things. Please come and see us. Hear us out and you will learn more.' 'Where do we need to meet?' 'There is a car outside waiting for you.' 'And if I refuse?' 'Nobody refuses the Morning Star. But if you were to refuse...things could go bad for you. I would suggest you walk outside and get into the vehicle.' The phone hung up and the man stood pacing the small, well lit living room. What could all this mean? What or who is this Morning Star? The only way to really know was to venture outside the protection of his apartment and get into the vehicle. He once more ran a hand through his hair before grabbing his jacket and walking out of the apartment. He stood outside looking at the black Navigator and the man that stood outside of it. “You must be Lucan. Please get in.” The driver said with no emotion or feeling. Lucan made his way into the Navigator as the driver closed the door and walked around to the driver’s side and got in himself. They drove for what seemed to be hours. Lucan’s patience began to wear thin when they finally stopped. Lucan’s door swung open and he got out looking at his surroundings. He stood there for a moment perplexed at what he was looking at. The house of his family. The house of his past. His house. It had been a very long time since he had been there. It had been even longer since he had talked to any of his relatives. He stood there wondering why was he brought here? As he stood there, his phone rang. He looked at it almost not wanting to answer it. It almost seemed like he knew whatever was on the end of the other line was sure to tell him why he was brought there. “Answer the phone sir.”The driver said seeming annoyed with the incessant ringing. Lucan startled hurriedly answered. “Hello?” “Lucan, what you will find in that house may disturb you but know this it was because of you that this happened. It was because of your ability. The people who did this”..travesty were looking for you.” “What do you mean?” “Go in and find out.” Without a second thought Lucan ran in not bothering to hang up just yet. He rushed over to the grayish blue two-story house and stopped before its burgundy door now seeming to be more menacing than anything he has seen in his life. With a small tremble in his hand, he turned the knob to the door slowly before fully opening it. At first glance he became even more pale than usual. The walls almost painted in blood. He stood there before checking every room. Downstairs he found his twin sisters still laying in their beds, looking to be only dreaming until he saw they had no color in their face. He pulled back each of their covers to reveal that their bodies had been tortured very badly. Without haste he ran upstairs to find his parents tortured in the same fashion. “Who did this you piece of shit?” “Temper temper my dear Lucan. As I said before this was your doing not ours. We have merely come to assist you in finding the evil persons who did this; if you can do something for us of course. It took everything he had not to hang up or to lash out at the person on the other end of the line. “What is it that you want me to do?” He could almost feel the person’s wicked smile over the phone. “There is a man in Amsterdam who could most likely lend you assistance. Find him. Tell him you know someone who can tell him what he truly is and then call this number and I will set up a meeting ? “And if I accept these terms?” “You will get more power than you ever imagined and be able to avenge your family.” “Fine, but there is one thing I need to know before we go any further.” “And what is that Lucan?” “Who are you” I mean what’s your name.” “I am the Silent One. I am the Morning Star.”
