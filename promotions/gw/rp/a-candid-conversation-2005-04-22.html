A Candid Conversation

22 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Week Of 4/22/2005 Jake Hix had plans for the evening. He was meeting his agent for a late dinner to discuss some new business opportunities and marketing ideas. The entire affair would be boring, and his mind was elsewhere at the moment. None of that mattered though, because there were still duties to fulfill to keep the cover story in place. Everything was becoming jumbled. He needed to continue the quest for the man he once considered his brother, but now his career was coming on track. He needed to pay attention to his future in Global Wrestling because that would be expected of a rising star in the league. Not paying attention would be considered strange, and he didn’t want anyone to begin wondering. He was cleaned up, and because the dinner was at Le Cirque he had laid out a black Armani suit with blue knit shirt. He never knew what his agent would pull, so it was always best to be ready for anything. Jake matched it with the new pair of Armani shoes he had received via UPS the day before. All ready to get going, Jake used the Dolce & Gabbana cologne he had brought on all the best spots. He rubbed some on his wrists, neck, under his arm pits, behind his ears and just above his most precious body part. Jake liked the smell of the Dolce & Gabbana. They used a very fragrant, natural blend that explored the essences of lemon, orange and lavender blended in with notes of sage, cedar and tobacco. Next, the blue silk boxers went on, followed by the shirt and suit pants. He put the black belt through the loops, tucked his shirt in and looked himself over in the mirror. Satisfied, he put on the socks and shoes. The duffel bag on the bench was filled with his toiletries, wrestling gear and street clothes he had worn to the arena. Jake slipped his jacket on, pocketed his personal items and then grabbed the bag as he headed out the door. The arena still had plenty of people inside. Jake got some nods, and gave some nods. He was still a little too new to get too many looks or be spoken to that much. However, as he approached the back door he saw Noi Tsunami lock her eyes onto him. He simply kept a smile on his face as she approached. Noi motioned her cameraman over, and like the true pro she was she didn’t start speaking until the camera was rolling. “Jake Hix, how are you doing?” “Feeling strong Noi. Getting ready to head out onto the town.” Jake knew he couldn’t slip away from her. “That’s great! Lots of nightlife in Vegas.” Noi nodded, “While you’re here, can we get a few moments to answer some questions?” “I’m always ready to answer questions Noi. Go ahead.” Jake set the duffel bag down and looked at her. Noi smiled, “You’ve been on quite a roll since you came into the league. You had a great debut, followed by victories over Ice, Jesse Jamester and now Mike Biddle. The fans are beginning to grow in their support of you. Did you expect this?” “Well, risking being labeled as arrogant I did.” Jake laughed a little. “I didn’t come out of retirement for no good reason. I came back because I had been training hard to be a monster once again, and I think it all shows. I turned into both a better wrestler and a better fighter. I’m looking for a fight that brings me to an even higher level, and I’m looking for any and all comers.” “Ice made comments recently, and there have been plenty of rumors swirling about those comments. Do you think he is jealous of you” Afraid of you?” Noi was enjoying the interview. Jake looked right into the camera, “Of course you’re afraid of me. Of course you fear what I’m going to do to you. The fear courses through your entire body, and I’m going to kick your ass the moment we step into the octagon again.” “Okay, so you think he’s afraid of you, but what about jealous?” “All of the news, rumors and Internet sites leading up to the Clash Of The Champions were abuzz with the news of his return. He was the second coming, and so when he gets eliminated he is pissed. Then everyone is speaking about the huge newcomer to the scene. He is jealous of me stealing his thunder because I’m better than he is.” Jake didn’t stop, “Worse yet, he keeps deluding himself into thinking I did not beat him. I beat him at Clash Of The Champions when I tossed his pathetic ass over the top rope. Then I beat him and Jamester when I won the three way dance. If he was so good, he would have walked away a winner instead of a whiner.” “Well, what about?” Noi began to ask, but she was cut off. “Wait, and now let’s focus on the biggest thing that makes him a jackass!” Jake was riled up now. “I may be new to the league, but you don’t betray the league, the fans and everything you were by running over to the other side. I don’t know what this shit was tonight, and I don’t care. I’m saying it now to be absolutely clear’bring me Ice. Put him in with me. I’ll take him down, break him down and make him my bitch!” “Thos are strong words, have you expressed that to GW management?” Noi looked at the man’s eyes, and knew he was very serious. “I think we just did Noi.” Jake picked his duffel bag back up, “Now, if you’ll excuse me I need to head off to a late business meeting. We should sit down for a long talk sometime Noi, I’m a fan of your work.” “Thanks Jake” Noi responded as she watched him walk out into the parking lot. Jake Hix moved out to the parking lot. John opened the door for him, and he slipped into the plush backseat. John moved to the driver’s side, and rolled down the partition. He turned to Jake, “Was the location ever finalized for the meeting?” “Yes, we’re headed to the Bellagio. So we kill two birds with one stone.” Jake explained as he poured himself a drink of water. “I’ll get you there at once.” John put the car into gear, “Jake, how goes the house search?” “I closed on the deal, and the house is officially mine. This week a cleaning crew is scrubbing it from top to bottom, a gardener is fixing everything in the yard and I will meet with an interior designer this week.” Jake explained. He liked visiting with John. “An interior designer?” John was surprised. ? You’re hiring an interior designer?” Jake could see the raised eyebrows in the rearview mirror, “Okay, I need furniture, a bed and everything else. I don’t have the time to do all that myself, and I might as well hire a professional to do this for me who knows what they are doing. We’ll meet, they’ll hear a lot about what I don’t like. Then they’ll find stuff that doesn’t piss me off.” “Maybe I should get a job like that.” “They’ll find stuff that doesn’t piss me off that’s color coordinated!” Jake looked into the rearview mirror, “You color blind asshole!” “I see how it is.” John shook his head as drove through the Vegas streets, “You think a win would put you in a good mood.” Jake became slightly withdrawn, “I’ll get in a better mood when I get my hands on something real. I miss being in the main event, competing for titles and having half the arena wearing my shirts. I’ll be happy again soon enough.” “I hear you.” John kept driving. They settled into a comfortable silence, John realizing Jake liked to be alone with his thoughts when he was planning something. Jake didn’t know who the new manager was, only a name to reference for the table. Hawk had arranged it since he was taking over the other operations. John took them to the hotel quickly. He pulled into the line of cars and limousines and one of the attendants opened the door for him. “Welcome back Mr. Hix.” Bernie, the attendant said. “I hear it was another good evening.” “It was, thank you.” Jake handed Bernie a five dollar bill as he passed. The interior of the Bellagio was impressive. On entering, you are assaulted by an amazing sight as you look up to witness a ceiling covered by two thousand glass flowers. Reading the material in his room, Jake knew the work of art was the Fiori di Como created by Dale Chihuly. The entire entrance was designed to capture the imagination and grandeur of the entire hotel. Next, Jake walked through a kaleidoscope of colors as he stepped into the Conservatory and Botanical Garden. Row upon row of exotic flowers seemed like a tapestry of nature. He enjoyed the fragrant aroma of the flowers as he continued through to the crossway. Jake headed left to where Le Cirque was located. Le Cirque, the famous New York restaurant, had been transplanted to Las Vegas as the crowning jewel of the Bellagio. The restaurant was set upon an eight acre lake filled with dancing fountains that were time to music with lights. Jake walked across the pathway to the restaurant. The entryway was something he loved. There were polished woods with silk tenting, and the two items would also be featured throughout the rest of the restaurant. Jake walked up to the man in a suit behind the small podium. “Welcome to Le Cirque, how may I help you?” The man said with a slight French accent that seemed genuine. “I’m Jake Hix, there is a reservation for me and Deacon Manning.” Jake explained. The man reviewed the reservation list. Finding the entry he looked up, “Yes sir. Deacon Manning and three guests. Mr. Manning has already arrived. Can I show you to the table?” “Thank you.” The maitre d? led him through the dining room to the table. Standing as he did, Jake got many looks and stares from the crowd. Not too many recognized him, but he could tell from the looks some did. The maitre d? led him to a table where a small man was sitting. He was maybe five feet eight inches tall, and a thin one hundred and forty pounds. He was in his early thirties, and had all his hair along with horn rimmed glasses. His face spoke of cool self-confidence as he stood up to greet Jake. “Mr. Hix, a pleasure to meet you!” Deacon stood up, offering his hand. Jake took the man’s small hand in his own, and was shocked by the limp hand and wrist that greeted him. Jake didn’t lessen his normally firm grip. He saw the surprise in the man’s eyes when his hand was virtually crushed. “Good to meet you.” Jake replied. They sat down. Jake cocked his head, “The maitre d” said that there was a party of four. You and three guests.” “Yes, well, the publicist I have on staff had to cancel. Another client had an absolute emergency that she needed to deal with.” Deacon explained. Jake thought about it, “That means one person is missing.” “I’m not missing.” A voice came from behind. Jake turned, and stood up on seeing the third guest. The man was older, in his late fifties with thick, silver hair slicked back. He was wearing a black suit, red shirt and black tie. The man’s left hand leaned on the silver topped cane for support. The man was tall, six feet four inches and two hundred and fifty pounds of well-maintained muscle. They shook hands, then the shake broke into a full hug. John “Hawk” Peters was like a surrogate father figure to most of the family, and a brother to Pitt Hix. Jake had relied on him many times, and the embrace helped relive tension and hurt that could never be vocalized. “I didn’t know you would be here.” Jake said as they broke away. “I’m full of surprises.” Hawk said as he moved around to Deacon. “It’s been a while Deacon. How has life been treating you?” They shook hands, “No complaints Hawk.” “Well, let’s have a meal shall we?” Hawk held his arms out wide before sitting down. You have to be quick witted and thick skinned inside a locker room.” “Okay then, would you be comfortable holding a weapon?” Deacon kept making mental notes. “Is it aimed at you?” Jake smirked. Deacon assumed it was a joke, “No, not at all.” “Pity.” Jake wasn’t hiding his dislike of the man. “Look, you don’t think I’m good for you. So Deacon, explain what you bring to the table.” Jake continued locking his eyes on the small man. Deacon smiled. The small crack of his lips was the smile of confidence and satisfaction. His voice was more powerful now, “I have no experience in the world of wrestling and fighting. I have never managed anyone in this business, and don’t plan to seek other clients at this point. The business is dangerous, bloody and not very attractive to my marketing partners.” “I represent actors, models and singers. We look for opportunities outside their current comfort level. We create cross-cultural appeal using every avenue available. Our successes in this endeavor are well-known, and the returns are quite handsome for myself and my clients. We can take you to a new level of success. There are places you can go, if someone opens those doors to you. We open those doors.” Deacon took a sip of water, “You can act, you have appeal to women who want you and men who want to be you. You need a larger stage than the simple auspices of Global Wrestling. If you want to expand your reach, and become an icon in the media then allow us to help you. If you don’t 3want this, then we can a simple and pleasant dinner, then part company.” “A man who speaks plainly, often speaks the truth.” Jake leaned back in his chair now, “I like that. You may have potential yet Deacon.” “Well thank you.” Deacon backed up his chair, “If you will excuse me for a moment gentlemen, nature calls at inopportune times.” They both nodded as Deacon went to find the restroom. Hawk took the lead, “He can help you become even greater.” “On a practical note, considering some of the actions I am forced to take, is more notoriety a good thing?” “That’s a fair point, but people are more forgiving of a celebrity.” Hawk lifted his glass of wine, “And you need to quit taking such direct action. Jeopardizing yourself does not help the cause. We have friends, we have associates and we have people who owe us favors.” “I can’t entrust this to anyone else.” Jake’s eyes took on such a sad state that Hawk flinched. “We did that once, and it came back to haunt us.
