Revelations Part 4

16 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 16 Jushin was sitting inside the large, black SUV. They were parked in a small parking lot across the street from the Holiday Inn Tokyo. The large thirty-four story building was currently being occupied by the Americans. According to their meeting with Yoshahiro Li, the top three stories were currently occupied by the Americans. All rooms being used as living quarters, as well as offices to house their illegal activities. Jushin felt uneasy about being so close to the place where they were unwelcome. He did not fear the Americans by any means, but he did not know how much they could trust Yoshahiro, he seemed sincere, but nowadays none can be trusted. They were so close to where he said they would be, but could it be a setup? Were the Americans currently watching Ichiro and him, instead of vice versa? Ichiro was sitting in the passenger seat of the SUV. His eyes were focused on the private parking garage located to the side of the hotel. The garage could only be used by guests of the hotel, and he figured that if the Americans were going to leave, this is where they would come from. His mind began to recollect on the meeting Jushin and himself had conducted with Yoshahiro Li earlier on that day. Ichiro and Jushin walked through the doors of the small tea shop. They looked around and quickly spotted Yoshahiro sitting on the far side of the restaurant. They quickly made their way over to his table and sat down in the two chairs opposite of him. Yoshahiro looked terrible, his face was pale, and he looked like he hadn’t eaten or slept in days. His large black sunglasses hid the bags under his eyes; he did not look towards the two brothers as they sat down. He took a sip of his green tea and continued to stare blankly out the window. He did not turn back to the brothers, even when he began to speak. “Thank you for coming, I trust that you two both know what has happened?” he began. The two brothers nodded in unison. “Good, then you know why I have called you here today. You are the only two that I can trust to seek vengeance for my father. I know what the two of you did to Ishuzu when she killed your father, and I know that the Americans attacked you last week. I can only hope that you will do the same to the Americans for my father, the same that you did to Ishuzu for your own father,” Yoshahiro started. The two brothers leaned in, listening carefully. They did not care about his father, he meant nothing to them. But, their hatred would allow them to listen to the sad story to find out what they needed to find out. “I’m sure you heard what happened to him, he was attacked. In broad daylight, in one of his warehouse complexes. He and several of his personal officers were killed, but I found him before he died. He was supposed to meet me after, but he never showed, so I came to find him. I did find him, lying in a pool of his own blood. I rushed to him quickly,” he stopped suddenly. He sniffed a bit but soon continued. “I apologize, I am still bothered by the image. Anyways, he told me that when the Americans were checking the dead bodies, they were being sloppy and talking about their plans.” “Look man, we don’t need the sad story. I’m sorry for what happened to your father, and I know how you feel, but this will be a whole lot easier if you just tell us what we need to know!” Jushin exclaimed. “Jushin! I am sorry my friend, my brother does not mean to be rude,” Ichiro tried to explain. “No, he is right. I should just tell you what you need to know,” Yoshahiro began once again, he lit up a cigarette and took a drag before continuing. “He told me that he overheard their plans for tonight. They are currently residing in the Holiday Inn Tokyo. From, what he understood that was the current hideout of their operations. They planned on doing a pickup at the harbor front tonight, something about a shipment from America. Unfortunately, that was all that he managed to tell me, before, he passed away.” “Thank you, we will do what we can my friend,” Ichiro said as he stood up. Jushin was already walking out the door, before Ichiro was even out of his seat. He patted Yoshahiro on the back, and left his hand there for a moment. Yoshahiro turned to Ichiro for the first time in their entire meeting and took another drag of his cigarette. For the first time, Ichiro saw truly how bad a wreck he was. His face was pale, and his skin was hanging from his bones. His fingers were shaking as he inhaled the nicotine. He still managed to force a smile towards Ichiro. “Thank you, my friend.” Ichiro was quickly brought out of his recollections as Jushin shook him. “Look, look!” he pointed towards the garage. Just then, the garage opened up and a convoy of white pick-up trucks pulled out single file. Ichiro watched as the trucks began to pull out and make their way up the street away from the hotel and towards the waterfront area. Ichiro waited and watched, about six truckloads left the garage before the doors closed. He looked back towards Jushin, who was staring at the doors. “Let’s go,” said Ichiro. Five minutes later the two brothers exited the SUV. They were both wearing street clothes over top of their stealth gear. Each man dressed in a hooded sweatshirt and a pair of jeans, with black boots. They made their way quickly across the street and began to head towards the elevators. They quickly hopped inside, and began to take the elevator up to the thirtieth floor. That was as high as they could get, without using the stairs. It was a measure of security for those who used the presidential suites on the top floors. They exited the elevators and quickly began to head towards the stairs. They made their way up the stairs and came out into an open hallway. They looked around; there was one security camera in the corridor, Jushin quickly reached behind it, not allowing anything in sight of the lens. He pulled the small wires out from the back, killing the feed, they quickly made their way down the hallway. They came out into another hallway, and again another video camera. Jushin once again tugged the wires from the back of the camera. They quickly looked for the room marked 3417. According to the blue prints of the building, that was the largest office suite that the hotel had to offer, and they guessed that was where they could find the most information. They walked down the hallway, quietly not wanting to alert anyone left, if there was anyone still around the hotel. They passed many rooms as they went. 3405, 3407, 3409, 3411. Finally at the end of the hallway they found it. Jushin quickly took a step back from the door, and delivered a stiff kick directly on the door knob. The door did not budge, however, the cheap locking mechanism of the hotel clicked slightly. The small light on the keypad went green. Jushin smiled at his older brother, and pressed the handle down and then swung the door open. The two brothers entered silently. Ichiro quickly began to scan the walls and roof for any signs of cameras, but as he thought there were none. How would they be able to get away with putting them in people’s private rooms? Ichiro quickly began to make his way over to the desk and Jushin made his way to a small filing cabinet. “Don’t mess anything up!” Ichiro said quietly, but firmly. “Okay, I got it!” said Jushin. Sarcastically he saluted his brother, before he returned to work on searching the cabinet. “I’m serious Jushin, we can’t leave any signs that we have been here,” said Ichiro. He began to search through the desk, but found nothing but empty drawers and stationary. Jushin however, was having much better luck. He found that a section of the filing cabinet was divided chronologically by weeks. He began to search for the most recent weeks, and he came up with the previous week. “Brother, I think I may have found something. Come here, take a look,” said Jushin, waving his brother over to his side. Ichiro quickly shut the drawer of the desk that he was looking in and walked over to the cabinet where his brother was standing. He pulled out a small flashlight and focused it on the file folder that Jushin had just pulled out. The manila folder featured the same coat of arms insignia that he had seen on the man’s neck. Two horses surrounding a shield and crown. Jushin opened the folder and the two brothers began to scan the top document. It was a receipt, some form of fax. An invoice maybe, something about receiving a shipment. Ichiro read the contents but it had nothing important, except that the parcels were arriving from America. He then looked at the header, it had the company name and information. Sakana Ichimiro Company. He shrugged, it did not ring any bells. However, when Ichiro began to read near the bottom things began to fall into place. He read the signature on the bottom, is was almost completely illegible. However, luckily the contact’s name was typed underneath. Ichiro read the name and bells began to go off in his head. “Justin Kirk,” Ichiro read out loud. “The name sounds familiar,” said Jushin. “It should be, Justin Kirk used to be an associate of ours,” Ichiro started. “If I remember correctly, he was the Foreign Manager of Pierce Imports.” “Pierce Imports” But isn’t that run by?” Jushin stammered. “Echelon Incorporated,” Ichiro finished his brother’s sentence. His mind began to race, if this was true, their return to GW would be very interesting. If TUE truly was involved in this, there would be hell to pay. He did not want to mix family life with professional life, but it looked as though TUE had broken that line. And that was something that he would not allow to go unpunished. “Take the receipt, and leave everything else inside the folder. Put it back into it’s proper place and let’s get out of here, we need to get back to Uncle Gohei’s and tell him what we have found out, before our flight tomorrow,” Ichiro said. He was already on his way out of the office.
