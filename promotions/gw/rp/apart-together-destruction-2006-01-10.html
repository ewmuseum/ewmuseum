Apart...Together...Destruction

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/10/06 Apart There had been scattered showers in the late evening, but so far the cloudy sky had been held at bay since the sun graced the sky. He had hoped the precipitation would stay away, although he was excited that the rain wasn’t snow thanks to the unseasonably warm weather they had experienced as of late. The meeting wasn’t necessary, at least not for the stated purpose, but there were always wheels within wheels. As he sat on the green bench, he took another bite of the hot, salted pretzel he had purchased from the street vendor. The first pretzel was already gone down low into his hungry stomach, and this one would soon follow. Surveillance was a known factor. He assumed he was currently under watch because to think any differently was foolish. No, someone had trailed him to New York, seen him take a cab down to the park and they were watching him eat the pretzel. I hope you’re watching me and starving? Pitt’s bitter thoughts were understandable. He kept looking around, giving the impression he was being cautious. In truth, the looks about were simply a distraction from the continual stares he cast down the yellowing grass hill to the scene below. He was watching someone who could not know he was there. Common sense told him not to be here. Risk versus reward was simply an equation in your mind, and no matter what he did or how he adjusted the equation the result never changed. The risk was not worth the reward, but he did it anyway. Another man came walking along. He was dressed sharply in a gray suit and black overcoat. The man looked at the assembled strangers before sitting down next to Pitt Hix on the bench. “I thought you weren’t coming back for me.” Pitt finished chewing the bite of pretzel. He swallowed it before speaking. “I believe my exact words were I wouldn’t come back to kill you, but a visit is never out of the question. Not paranoid are we?” “You mean that the man I stole from wants a meeting with me?” He gestured around, “At least we’re here in the open. If you were going to kill me, I would have expected something more private.” “I wouldn’t kill you for stealing my money. I may have beaten the shit out of you, but what kind of man do you take me for?” Pitt smiled, returning to his lunch. The man sighed, running his hand through his carefully combed coif of blonde hair. His friend across the way got the signal that he was safe for the moment. “That was clumsy and easy to spot.” Pitt laughed. James turned to Pitt, “What are you talking about?” “That fat, greasy, sweaty and thoroughly obvious man across the way was there that night we had our’disagreement.” Pitt crumbled up the paper in his hand. He tossed it across the opening into the trashcan, “You brought back-up, but that’s okay. I can understand your’trepidation.” “What do you want Pitt?” James didn’t like this situation. Pitt had always carried an air of arrogance, but he assumed it was just a face for the public. When he had been caught embezzling funds from the New York restaurant, James quickly learned Pitt had a n aura of arrogance and danger. He had earned both. Pitt leaned back, stretching his back out as he leaned over the top of the bench, “I hear you’re thinking of opening a restaurant here in New York City.” James felt the cold trickle of sweat in the middle of his back, “I don’t know where you could have heard that from. I have been advising some friends on certain matters of design and style?” “For a percentage of the restaurant, and I understand you can become majority owner within the year.” Pitt kept close eyes on a few of the passersby, but then he kept looking down the hill. “I see you still have plenty of friends.” “I do, and this violates the agreement we had between us.” “There’s no contract.” James regretted the snotty tone and choice of words the moment they were out. “Contract?” Pitt leaned forward now, his eyes locked on the fat man across the way. “Since when was a contract needed between men of honor” You see, when you betrayed me it was a debt of honor that I repaid to your father by taking no action against you. I should have, by rights, taken that damn cleaver and chopped your balls off. I could have had Sal fry them up and serve them to you.” “I decided to let you pass out of my old friendship with your father. Now, as part of that, we made an agreement on what you could and could not do. Your father was an amazing man and fabulous chef, and allowing you to work in New York at a restaurant is an insult to his memory. So, you have two choices.” James snorted in the way of a man used to hearing ridiculous ideas from a class of people he generously called plebian, “Do I?” “Oh yes, you do. You can go back to whatever little town or city you were hiding in these last few years and resume that life of boredom.” “Or?” Pitt pointed at his fat friend, “Do you think he can save you from the cleaver so to speak James?” James looked across, and knew he didn’t have much of a choice. “Can I finish helping with the restaurant before I leave” They are my friends’and I did promise?” “You are an obnoxious, arrogant little fucker.” Pitt nodded. “Yes, but you sell your percentage back to them for one dollar. Paper drawn up as soon as possible.” “One dollar!”!” The bitter bile rising in the back of his throat also affected his tone. He was being’insulted! “Friends do favors James, not jobs. You’re father taught you that. Now get out of here before I decide you already forfeited your father’s good will.” Pitt brushed him away, nothing more than a nuisance. James stood up, walking away in a huff as his fat friend joined him. Pitt remained for a bit, looking down the hill again. At the bottom of the hill, there were children playing. Two of the children, bearing a striki9ng resemblance to each other, were among the rest. Thomas and Alexandria, fraternal twins, were the children he had fathered with a New York socialite. He was away from them, and less than five people knew he was a father. He had run to protect them, hidden himself away to prevent foolish sentimentality to endanger them. Yet here he was, staring down at the twins while he knew there were men keeping watch over him. A phone call to James would have had the intended effect. He would have been frightened into submission without the cost of a trip to New York. Pitt stood up, pulling his trench coat around his muscular frame. He began walking away, now feeling the cold wind pushing around his suddenly hallow insides. His family’his future’was so close, and yet he couldn’t even reach out and touch them. He had a plane to catch? Together Cold caused his shoulder to tighten up, but thankfully Texas had been warm. Unusually warm for this time of year, even in Texas. Eighty degree Christmas and eighty degree New Years was not normal, not even in a state where the heat from the bright sun was as legendary as the size of the landscape. He was lying on the soft green grass in the backyard, staring up into the blue sky. The bullet wound had healed nicely, and his shoulder was only sore when the weather turned’or when he slept on it wrong. Luckily, so far he had pain free for several weeks. Xavier turned, looking through the glass door at her. She was seated at the table, lazily reading a book. He knew it would be something brilliant, like poetry or philosophy. She read books that stimulated her mind, and that was part of her charm. Beautiful was a word tossed around so much, but she was just that. Beautiful Bella was a beautiful, a special soul at odds in her worlds. Her tight, lithe body had comforted him so many evenings with her muscular frame softened at just the right points by her feminine charms. Of course, the first part he had fallen in love with was her wit and aloofness. She was so complex in ways he learned a little more everyday, challenging him while always welcoming him into her life. That’s what hurts so much? Xavier had accepted her love despite his doubts and second thoughts. She had come to him when he was reborn through fire, nursing him back from the brink and fleeing with him into the darkness. They had been on the run for three years before returning home. The most recent attack had shattered what security he had mistakenly believed them to have. No, they would never be safe, not until he buried the other half of himself. He thought everything was finished on the reservation, but he had returned. Sid wanted him dead. Sid would not rest until he was dead. The enormous truth only hurt him even more. So now, he was forced to put the woman he loved in danger if he wanted to remain with her. Xavier was a coward for her though, and had tried to force her to leave. She had stubbornly refused to leave him alone’and he loved her for it. Xavier stood up. He stretched out, trying to completely waken his muscles. There was a training schedule set for tomorrow, and Xavier was looking forward to it. For now, if he couldn’t send her away, he wouldn’t be without her. Xavier walked across the perfectly cut lawn to the back door. He walked in as she looked up; her gorgeous eyes always seemed to penetrate straight through to him. “You done being lazy?” Bella asked him with a playful smirk. He walked over to her. Xavier stared down for a moment before kissing her on the forehead, “I love you.” “Where did that come from?” “Nowhere, I just wanted you to know.” Xavier pulled her up by her wrists. “And as for your question, I’m not thinking about being lazy at the moment.” They embraced, kissing passionately like young lovers stealing a moment from the world while they could. He scooped her up into his arms, carrying her out of the kitchen. Away from the world? Away from the danger? If only for a moment? If only for an instant? Destruction The third floor of the old headquarters building held plenty of training equipment. The free weight stations, cardio machines and dumbbells were also paired with punching bags, yoga mats and a full sized wrestling ring. Now, an octagon would have been more appropriate, but they hadn’t put one in. Yet? Pitt thought to himself. He planned on adding one assuming he and Xavier got back into the swing of things. Today though, he had an enormous chip on his shoulder. Surprisingly, he had noticed his brother had the same thing. Xavier seemed in a word’pissed. Anger could be good for training, and with such a massive tag team match at the Pay-Per-View they both needed to be in top shape. Pitt walked over to the ring, and slid in under the bottom rope. Outside, all around the ring were young hopefuls from the wrestling and fighting world. They were from all around the Metroplex, locals looking for some exposure. The smallest one was two hundred and fifty pounds, and the two large bikers were six and a half feet tall and three hundred pounds each. They had a good group for the training. Xavier jumped up onto the ring apron from the floor in one graceful move. He hurled himself over the top rope into the ring and joined Pitt. Pitt began explaining what was happening, “Okay guys, this is how it goes. We start with two at a time, and when you are pinned or tap out you cycle out for the next pair. We’re not fragile, and this will be full speed training. Everyone has head gear to avoid any serious injuries during?” “What a bunch of crap.” The biker with the shaved head interrupted. Pitt had spotted him as a problem case, lots of attitude in he and his friend. Xavier stepped up, “Excuse me?” “Fucking pads” What kind of pussies are you two?” The biker and his friend laughed. Xavier could feel the tension coming, just enough to keep him ready. He looked the man up and down, “You’ll notice we don’t have pads. Those are for your protection so we don’t hurt you.” “Like that could fucking happen.” The second biker added his two cents. Xavier thought they deserved their money back after listening to these two. Pitt came forward, “Well, I think we have our first volunteers.” “Sure thing grandpa.” The biker took his shirt off and threw it aside, revealing ink all over his torso. His friend did the same. “Fine, then ding-ding and come on in.” Xavier wasn’t playing around. The rest of the men around the ring could tell this was trouble. Pitt and Xavier were well known,. And prodding them like that was stupid. If anyone here did well enough in the sparring, these were the type of men to help them out. You cross them though? The first biker slid into the ring. Xavier waited for him to stand up. He moved toward the tattoo-covered punk, and easily dodged the sloppy punch. Xavier slammed his knee into the man’s midsection. He could tell he was soft despite his size. Another knee, this time to the face, sent him flying into the corner. Pitt wasn’t as gracious as his brother. When his biker slid into the ring he speared him to the outside. The assembled men moved out of the way as he climbed on top, slamming lefts and rights into the man’s unprotected face. Inside, Xavier went to work with his elbows. Each blow looked like a punch, until at the last moment he turned his arm, slicing across the man with his elbow. He fractured the man’s nose, and then ripped open two long gashes across his face. Xavier began slamming knees into the man’s face when he fell down to the mat. As his left arm came back, there was blood covering it and dripping off it. Pitt grabbed the biker’s wrist after pulverizing his face. He turned it over, and simply bent until he heard the crack of bones breaking and the pop of tendons tearing. The biker’s scream was like a child looking for his mother, but Pitt’s final diving forearm to his face sent him into blissful unconsciousness. Xavier was still slamming his knee into the unconscious man. Pitt slid back into the ring and pulled his brother off. “NO! LET ME TEACH HIM A LESSON!” Xavier yelled angrily. Pitt yelled right back at him, “YOU DID!” Xavier pushed Pitt away and walked to the far side of the ring. He wiped off the blood, throwing it sideways to the mat. Pitt looked down at the two men, then out at the others. “Anyone else have anything to say about the padding?” One brave man raised his hand. “What do you have to say?” The man’s voice cracked as he spoke, “Thank you.” There was a brief, awkward moment, and then they all burst into laughter. “Good answer.” Pitt laughed along with them, but Xavier wasn’t laughing. He bent over, waiting on the next two to come into the ring.
