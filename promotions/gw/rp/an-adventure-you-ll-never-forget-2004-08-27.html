An Adventure You'll Never Forget...

27 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/27/04 The new Global arena. Just the thought of it blows my mind. You have to give McCoy props, he is putting a lot of money into this company. Whether it be advertising, merchandise, or spending an extra dollar just to better it. He is doing whatever he can to make Global Wrestling the best organization in the entire world. New York City hosts the new arena. And the new arena will host Whiplash next week. I wonder if Bubba is going to have me defend my new tag title at the Pay Per View. It really doesn’t matter to me. A challenge is a challenge and a victory is a victory. And a loss, well, a loss isn’t even in my mind at this moment. Of course I sound cocky, if you only lost three matches in over four months, you’d be a little arrogant as well. The truth is, if you had my abilities, you wouldn’t be able to contain yourself. And deep inside, you know it. Brr? Brr? I reach my hand towards the middle compartment of my Aston Martin DB7. You know, one of those cars that you only see in magazines. You see, when you have such talent as I have, you get paid for it. No wonder why you don’t have an Aston Martin. I flip the lid of my cell phone with my thumb while turning down the volume of my unique stereo system, which is more money then the entire car that you drive? “Sabbot.” I nod my head? Speak of the devil? “Alright.” ? “McCoy, I know you are on a high horse and stripping everybody of their titles and everything. But you know who not to mess around with. You know who the hell I am.” ? “Forget my tone of voice. Forget that I’ve changed. I’m back to my old self once again if you like it or not.” ? “International title shot” Keep talking?” ? “I just beat his ass two weeks ago. You’ve got to be kidding me.” ? “Who should I prove myself to” If Brick doesn’t know that I’m better then him, perhaps I’ve knocked more screws loose in that giant head of his than I thought.” ? “Once I beat Brick again, I want you to strap that belt around my waist, personally.” I shut the lid of my cell phone and throw it to the carpeted floor? I can’t believe McCoy is putting me in another match with Brick. There is only so much of beating Brick’s ass a man can take. I know I said a victory is a victory, but this is a slap in the face. How many times do I have to beat him until McCoy realizes that he isn’t in my league? I know he defeated me for the GW World’s title, but come on, that was over a year and a half ago. And I know it’s hard to believe this, but I’ve gotten better then what I was back then. I know you are thinking, how can the best get better? I’ll show you at Whiplash? I pull over to the side of the street next to the curb. I take the keys out of the ignition and reach down towards the floor to pick up my cell phone. I open my car door, between flying cars, and step out. I close the door and lock it with my trusty handheld security device and place the keys in the breast pocket of my black and white Armani suit. I take a pair of sunglasses out from my pocket and put them on and walk around my car onto the curb. Whiplash, such an important Pay Per View. And I can’t believe I’m wasting it on a match against Brick. I thought Bubba would at least give me some type of challenge for the GW International Championship title. But instead, he gives me Brick. Again. That’s alright. I’ll make short work of him. I never thought the International title would be this easy to? I fall to my hands and knees after a hard blow to my kidneys. So unexpected. I lift my head and look forward and I see what just happened. I stand to my feet and reach in my pocket’my wallet is gone. ---- The adrenaline runs through my veins like gasoline. I run as fast as I can with the black leather wallet inside my hand. I look back, to see if the old fucker is chasing after me? He is standing to his feet and putting his sunglasses back on his face. Maybe he doesn’t even know I have his wallet yet. This is too fuckin? easy. If it wasn’t for the adrenaline rush, I’d sit back and smile. Like stealing candy from a baby. I turn down a small alley and sprint to the end of it where I am faced with a metal linked fence. I climb up at an Olympic rate and jump over it. I turn back to see if the old bastard seen where I went. To sight of him? Now I have enough time to sit back and smile. I snicker, because I’m that good. I turn around and walk to the end of the alley and make a right down another alleyway. I don’t even have to run anymore, I walk as my nerves begin to calm down. This was easier then the last time. I pick up the wallet in front of my face and kiss it. I open it and quickly count the $100s, then the $20s. I don’t even bother with the small bills. This old bastard had over $800 in his wallet. Now, I can buy Mona that necklace she wanted at Sears. And have some money left over to do whatever the hell I want. I smile, once again. I look forward, down the end of the alleyway, the safe haven. The closer and closer I get, the closer I can see my car. I don’t even feel sorry for what I do. The old man had an expensive car, he can spare a few hundred dollars for somebody like me. He should feel good about this, it is like donating to charity. I look up to see a silhouette of a man standing at the end of the dark alley. I continue to walk, probably just some stranger. The closer and closer I get, the more I realize that he looks like the old man’I start to get nervous. “What the fuck are you lookin” at?” Hoping to scare him away? But the man doesn’t say a word, or move an inch. I continue to walk towards him, growing more and more nervous. “If you have a staring problem, I’m sure I can help rearrange that for you.” The man doesn’t say a word, or move an inch’still. I stop dead in my tracks? “What the hell do you want?” “My wallet.” The man replies in a deep and mysterious voice. SHIT! I turn around and start to run down the alley. I turn my head and look back, the old fuck is walking? This is fuckin? scary as hell. Reminds me of Jason or Halloween or some shit. I reach the end of the alley and turn down the other alley, meeting the metal linked fence once again. This time, I’m more afraid then I was the last time. I reach inside my coat and pull out a 9mm handgun. I slap the magazine inside the chamber and cock the charging handle back, loading the handgun. I sit there, and I wait for his ass to turn the corner. Sometimes, you have to do what you have to do’ya know? I wait a couple seconds, which seem like an eternity, but there is no sight of the old fucker. I slowly walk to where I can see down the entire alley. No sight of him’good. “My wallet, please.” I turn around, face to face with the old man in his expensive business suit with his sunglasses on. I take a couple steps back and hold the gun up to his face. “You ain’t getting” it back.” “I’d like my wallet.” Sweat drips down my face, burning my eyes. I hesitate, then pull the trigger. Shooting the man the chest, he stops and looks at me? He takes his sunglasses off to reveal the darkest eyes I have ever seen. “My wallet?” What the fuck? I pull the trigger again and put another bullet in his chest? The man takes a couple steps back from the impact, but remains on his feet. He takes a couple steps towards me? “Once again, for the last time. I’d like to have my wallet.” “FUCK OFF?” I’m breathing harder then I have ever breathed before. I pull the trigger again? “and again” “and again until the magazine is empty. The man moves back with every shot. But he still, remains on his feet. I throw the gun down towards the ground” “What the fuck are you?” The man smirks? “I’m the harvester of your tears.” The crazy fuck snickers and raises his left hand up in the air? I hear noises all around me, I look around. I turn my head to the left, and to the right’shadows start to move around on the walls. Shadows of’what? The black shadows begin to come together and form a giant square on the side of the wall. I take a couple steps back to the other side of the alley, scared for my life. The square turns into a dragon and it jumps off of the wall onto the ground. “What the fuck is this shit?” The dragon crawls up in front of my very face and opens it’s mouth, exposing it’s dark teeth. It lunges at me, biting my shoulder. I jump back and slap the back of my head towards the wall, falling to the ground. I look up, and the dragon is gone. I look over to the man, and he stands in front of me with a sadistic smile on his face? “Learn your lesson yet, my friend?” The crazy asshole laughs? “Fuck you.” I spit out the words along with a tooth and a pint of blood. The man looks at me and shakes his head side to side. He raises his right hand in the air, and once it reaches the peak’I start to hear voices in my head. Like, a room full of children’whispering and giggling. I start to panic. I look at the man, he lifts his head and laughs at me? “What the fuck are you” Get outta my fucking head!” “Listen to what you hear, my friend. Perhaps you’ll starve for silence and a mediocre life afterwards.” Bullshit? “Just get it the fuck outta my head, please!” “Asking for favors now, are we” I asked for something simple, my wallet. And you refused, so must I.” “You want your wallet” Take your fucking wallet, mister! Just please, get this out of my head.” I slide the wallet on the ground beside his feet. He reaches down and picks it up, counting every single bill inside. He looks at me and nods his head. He takes a couple steps towards me and kneels down beside me. “The voices haven’t gone away yet.” “I know.” “FUCKIN” MAKE THEM GO AWAY.” “It’s remarkable how you believe one act of kindness can fix every problem in your life.” I try to tune him out, but I’d rather listen to his voice then the children’s voices in my head. They are getting louder by the second? “You honestly can’t expect me to forgive you, can you?” “No, sir. But please, I’m sorry. Please’take these voices away.” I figure if I calm down and speak in a decent tone, he’ll help me? “Well, my friend, I’m definitely not going to forgive you. But I know somebody who will.” I sit in silence, listening to the voices? Listening to the man? “He is a forgiving man who loves you. He is a merciful God, one that you should get to know on a personal level.” “Can this guy help this go away?” “Yes.” “What do I have to do?” I feel like my head is going to explode. “Repent, ask for forgiveness. Bow your head, close your eyes, and pray unto him.” I nod my head, anything is worth a try right now. I bow my head, close my eyes and whisper a silent prayer to a God that I have never prayed to before? “Ask him to forgive you for the things you’ve done. Accept him into your heart.” I continue to pray as the voices slowly fade? “That’s it. Soon, everything will be better.” The voices continue to fade, almost to the point where they aren’t there? ---- “Soon, everything will be’just the way you want it.” I watch the thief as he bows his head, praying for forgiveness as I pull a switchblade from my breast pocket and slice his throat. The man looks up at me in shock, or maybe that is the shock kicking in. His eyes, wide open, ready to bulge out of his head. Those’forgiving eyes help me breath easier. “That’s it. Soon, there will be no more voices. Soon, there will be no more pain.” The man falls to his side while the blood pours from his throat. Everything is silent, except for the gurgling sound? “When you get to heaven, send me a postcard.” The man stops gurgling. He lies there on his side, motionless. I lean over his body and roll my fingers down his forehead, down his eyebrows, and then closing his eyes. “You can never pray too much.” I clean the switchblade with my fingertips and I place it back in my breast pocket. I stand to my feet and say a silent prayer for the lost one. I put my sunglasses back on and I turn around and walk away from the man. I walk away’from my problems?
