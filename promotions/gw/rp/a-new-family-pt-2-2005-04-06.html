A New Family pt 2

6 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4*6*5 '...some of y'all still haven't grown into your face, and your face doesn't quite match your head, and I'm waiting for a brain to fill that dead space that's left...' ---- Here I am. Standing with my wife in her parents' new mansion. She's at my side, with her father in front of us, and his little brother Johnny standing with him. 'Bobby, don't do it. They don't need to know. Their lives have been affected enough, don't do it to them too,' says Johnny. 'Yes they do. It's Cindy's heritage, and Derek is part of the family. They both have to know the truth. Cindy, your grandfather wasn't mugged by a teenager wanting money, or by a junkie looking to pay for his next fix.' My wife and I stand there totally lost in thought, or shock, or some deep mixture of the two. Finally, Cindy gets enough back to speak. 'Then why did Grampa die? Who killed him?' Johnny's eyes drift downward, hiding behind his eyelids. A hand comes up to brush his long hair out of his face. He breathes deep then rubs the bridge of his nose, wishing his brother would stop. 'Cindy, your grandfather was murdered in cold blood, a deliberate attack upon our family. I know, you're asking why anybody would target our family,' says my father-in-law, Bobby. Johnny steps forward and speaks up. 'I am totally against the idea of you two knowing. It's too dangerous for you. In fact, it would be best if you were nowhere around, at least for a few months.' 'Uncle Johnny, I can't just abandon my family. Why would you ever suggest I do such a thing?' says my wife. 'Calm down, dear. I'm trying to protect you. You're my only niece, I'm going to do my best to keep you safe. You remember the night after Dad's funeral, when you two were driving home?' My head snaps up at this. Everything flashes through my mind again, as vivid as it was that night. The sleet falling from the sky, the bright blue glow of the Lincoln's headlights as it sped up behind us. The sound of the bullets exploding out of the gun. The shattering of the windows on my Chrysler 300, and the way the glass landed in my lap. 'The black Lincoln that shot at us. I can see it in my mind,' I say. 'The police confirmed that the car was reported stolen earlier that night. They had no reason to suspect anything else,' adds Cindy. Bobby answers us. 'But did they say who the car was licensed to?' Cindy and I look at each other, racking our brains to think of the name. 'Roberto Bianchi.' I know the name. I can't think of where I've heard it before. 'Head of the Bianchi family. Has a large dealing in, how can I say it politely, underhanded business practices.' Cindy, being the ever perceptive one of the family, asks 'You mean he's a mob boss?' Johnny answers while Bobby's face flushes of color. 'Yes, Cindy, he's a mob boss. The car was reported stolen a matter of minutes before the shooting. Police didn't tell you anything because the one you talked to was more than likely on the Bianchi payroll.' 'Daddy, is it true?' 'Yes, dear. the Bianchi family was responsible for that.' 'Are they the ones who killed Grampa?' 'Yes. They hired an outsider to kill him. We don't know who the shooter was at this time, but by God I'm going to find out.' This sparks my interest. We? I'm? 'Are you saying that you're personally involved in the hunt for the shootist?' 'Well Derek, this is what I've been trying to tell you two. I'm being encouraged to step up and take my father's place, not only in the business, but also in all of the dealings that are made behind the scenes of CrusInc.' 'Daddy!?! What.........?' her voice drifts off as her jaw drops like a rock. The weight that holds her mouth open pulls her whole body down, as she slips onto the couch. Bobby sits down in his arm chair, and without looking at anybody, continues. 'Listen, I'm not too proud of some of the things that happen. But how long did my father run the company without you knowing there was anything illigitimate about it? The public doesn't know, and I'm going to keep it that way. The Cruse family has a good name, I will not do anything to tarnish that.' Johnny sneers. 'Then hand the shady practices over to somebody else. You're at the top, Bobby. You can divide things up and pull the family out of it! Keep the company, ditch the bullshit, and we're safe!' 'Johnny, you know I can't do that. It's not that simple. Some of the people we deal with are very powerful. They wouldn't take kindly to my backing out of our deals. I won't tarnish the name, but I won't put the family in danger by cutting off contracts with these men.' I decide to speak up. ' I don't know much about the 'business' but I know that your daughter and I were shot at on the highway. We're already in danger.' 'Yes, I realize this. I've taken it upon myself to hire some help to keep you, my daughter, and my grandson safe.' He gets up and walks to the back door. He walks back and four men in black suits follow him. Each man has on a solid colored dress shirt under the suit coat, one white, one yellow, and two dark red. My father in law stands before us, and when the four men stop to stand at his side, he waves his hand toward them. 'Cindy, Derek, these four men are your body guards. They are being paid to protect you as well as do what you desire.' 'I don't know if I like this....' Cindy starts to say, only to be interrupted. 'I'm not asking you to like it. I'm asking you to do it. Cindy, I'm still your father, and I'm doing this to protect you from the choices I'm going to have to make.' Cindy looks at me. I gaze in to her eyes, and see our son, Damon. She knows what she has to do. 'Ok, Dad. We'll do it.'
