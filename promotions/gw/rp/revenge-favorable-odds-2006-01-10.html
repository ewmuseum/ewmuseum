Revenge: Favorable Odds

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 10, 2006 Due to the constant melodramatic happenings of Beverly Hills Mike Biddle decided that it was time for a change of scenery. Without any hesitation Biddle took up residence in Las Vegas, it was the perfect location. No one from his Beverly Hills social circle was there to bother him and the city’s motto of “what happens in Las Vegas, stays in Las Vegas” appealed to his lifestyle greatly. Sin City was the ultimate escape from the personal disasters of 2005 and offered the perfect beginning to 2006, hell and it was damn close to work too. Ever since he arrived in Sin City Biddle felt free from the guilt that surrounded him following Hillary’s death. Even his horrific loss in Showdown in Tokyo didn’t bother him anymore, it was all in the past now. Of course, an obscene amount of martinis every day can really help a man to forget his problems. It was this particular “indoor sport” that Biddle participated in every night in one of the many bars inside the MGM Grand Hotel, where he had booked a large suite for the next few months. On this typical evening Biddle sat at the bar, already incoherently drunk, with a half finished martini next to him. He couldn’t help but notice that the brunette next to him continued to look better and better as the evening progressed. The woman, the classic big beautiful woman, who sat next to Biddle, noticed that as the evening wore on the attractive man next to her kept casting a seductive gaze in her direction. The gaze caused the all too familiar longing to return to her belly and she couldn’t help but blush as she wondered what a man with movie star good looks would want with her. Biddle grabbed his martini glass and finished his drink, instead of ordering another one he shifted in his seat and faced the woman next to him. He didn’t even notice that she was the sort of woman that he always considered to be beneath someone as great as him; all he knew was that he was horny. He gave her the eye, that sexy look that he had used countless times to woo actresses, and she blushed so badly that she looked like a big tomato. “Hey there,” Biddle said with a sly smile. It wasn’t his best opening line but it was the best his intoxicated mind could come up with. “Hi,” she said back with a small nervous voice. She was sure that she was dreaming a man like this had never even given her a second glance much less talk to her! Biddle ran his hand through his blond hair as he searched for something else to say to this woman’something that would convince her to go upstairs to his bed. “What’s a pretty woman like you doing in Sin City?” He asked, he silently cursed himself for not coming up with something better. She looked down at the ground, she hadn’t been this nervous in her life. “I’m here for business,” she said, “the advertising firm I work for just signed a contract with the casino and I’m here trying to come up with ideas for our campaign.” Biddle nodded his head with fake interest. “What’s your name pretty woman?” He said carefully, he could feel himself begin to slur his words. “Carrie,” she answered. “That’s a beautiful name,” he said with a smile. “Say, are you doing anything for the next few hours?” “No I’m not,” Carrie answered as she smiled and nervously looked down at the floor. She knew that if he asked her to go to his room she wouldn’t be able to resist, he was just so damn attractive’not the kind of man she was used to being with. “So’uh’would you like to maybe go play some slots with me then?” Biddle weakly asked. “Okay,” Carrie’s face lit up with the answer. Not only was this handsome stranger picking up on her but he wanted to be seen in public with her! Biddle smiled as he rose from his barstool, he was about to walk away with this “beautiful” woman when he suddenly felt a heavy hand on his shoulder that held him back with brute force. Carrie looked worried as she looked at the owner of the hand on the handsome man’s shoulder. Biddle sighed and turned around to see his bodyguard Joe Balboa, better known as the Backalley Brawler, standing behind him with a grim look plastered onto his street tough face. “Can’t you see I’m busy Joe?” Biddle said with an impatient tone as he brushed Joe’s hand off his shoulder. Balboa sighed; this was the tenth time this week he had to save his employer from his own stupidity. Normally he wouldn’t care what an employer would do but Biddle had given him implicit instructions to not allow him to take any ugly/fat women up to his room. Joe sighed heavily as he looked down at the burning anger residing in his boss’s eyes, he knew damn well that Biddle was about to throw a hissy fit. “It’s time for ya to get back up to yer room boss,” Joe scolded, “You’ve had too many damn martinis to be out in the casino wastin yer money.” The anger in Biddle’s well tanned face grew, he knew that in his drunken state he was no match physically for the Brawler but perhaps he could at least find a way to outsmart the brute. “And who told you that this was your decision?” Biddle carefully stated, trying not to slur a single word. “You did boss man,” Joe answered. Biddle stroked the stubble on his chin for a moment as he thought; he vaguely remembered telling Joe to prevent him from doing something stupid’something that involved ugly women. He cast a glance over at Carrie he decided that there was no way in hell she fit the description of an ugly women. She was beautiful brunette that could have been a model if she so wished. Biddle looked back up at Joe, “You must be confused; I don’t think this is the situation I was talking about.” Joe looked over at Carrie’s large form and then back at his employer and frowned. “This is exactly tha thing ya told me about boss,” he said. “So are ya gonna come back nicely or do I hafta be mean about it?” Biddle’s right eyebrow arched in anger, “Neither, you’re off for the night Joe.” Mike turned around and was about to take Carrie’s hand when he felt the heavy hand on his shoulder again. He turned around to scold his bodyguard but was silenced by a sweaty meat hook covering his face in complete blackness. Joe then put Biddle in a sleeper hold so strong that the drunken movie star couldn’t even struggle. Carrie watched on in a mixture of horror and excitement as this went on, she also wondered why the other patrons, and even the staff, of the bar weren’t saying or doing anything in the handsome stranger’s defense. Once Biddle was asleep, Joe looked down and whispered, “You’ll thank me in the mornin boss.” He then turned to leave. “Hey,” Carried nervously called out to the large brute that was carrying away the man of her dreams. Joe flinched at the sound of the voice and turned around. “It’s’uhh’way past his bedtime ma’am,” he said with a weak smile as he inched away. He saw that she was about to say something more so he turned around and quickly made his way to the exit before she caused a scene. Left alone at the bar, Carrie didn’t know how to react. She felt as if the rug had been pulled from underneath her. The Grand was big but she knew the odds of her running into the handsome stranger again were pretty good if she kept an eye out for him. She motioned to the bartender, a short man with brown handlebar mustache, for another drink. When he served her she asked, “Who was that charming man that was next to me?” The bartender chuckled; Carrie was the tenth woman to ask him that question this week. “He was Mike Biddle not that long ago, but now he’s nothing more than a drunken idiot with an enormous pocket book now.” Carrie smiled, the name sounded familiar. “He sounds familiar, what does he do?” The bartender politely chuckled; he was starting to get sick of explaining who Biddle was. “He’s some kind of wrestler in GLOBAL but he probably sounds familiar to you because he played Mr. Howell in that awful Gilligan’s Island movie last summer. How that movie ever made any money I’ll never know.” So that was why the handsome stranger looked so familiar! Carrie remembered seeing the movie; she was one of the few people that actually enjoyed the film. Now that she knew his identity there was still one question left to be answered. “Who was that large man that took him away?” She asked with a concerned voice. The bartender sighed. “That tall drink of water is his bodyguard, always comes in here just before I close up to make sure Biddle gets up to his room okay. He’s an alright guy in my book.” “Where can I find Mike?” Carrie pressed. “He’s always here unless he’s out in the casino playing slots,” the bartender answered. Carrie smiled again, now that she knew how to find the handsome stranger she could let him finish seducing her tomorrow and hopefully this time his bodyguard wouldn’t show up and ruin things again. ***** It’s hard to fit in with ruffian peasants when you’re a rich white man from Beverly Hills, but Charles managed. The only thing that kept him going during this lonely time was the knowledge that soon he would crush Mike Biddle beneath the heel of his boot. Every day Charles had been beaten up in the prison yard by the other inmates for various reasons, he hadn’t sustained any serious injuries’yet. It seemed that every time the beatings were about to get severe the group of rough looking white men that hadn’t joined in on the name calling the day he was brought to Nicola, would step in and stop it. Then they would disappear before Charles had a chance to even thank them for their help. It seemed that the group was testing him, letting him know how badly he needed them if he wanted to survive. One rainy afternoon in the prison yard, just after lunch, the group of men finally formally introduced themselves to Charles. The group that approached him was made up of three men, the leaders of the white faction in the prison. They were large, scruffy, middle-aged men that looked like they just got out of a Hell’s Angels convention’a far cry from the men that Charles rubbed elbows with in Beverly Hills. With a worried scowl Charles shivered as two of the men took seats on either side of him on the bleachers and the third man stood in front of him with a lit cigarette hanging from his mouth. “So you’re Charles Magee?” The man with the cigarette asked as he took a puff, being careful not to singe his salt and pepper beard with the smoke that hung from his lips. Charles nodded his head. “The same Charles Magee that killed Mike Biddle’s little bitch and almost got away with it?” The man asked as he put his cigarette out. Charles winced, he would never describe someone as beautiful as Hillary Watson as a “bitch” but didn’t feel like having an ethics debate with a man that had never heard of such a thing so he just nodded his head yes again. “Hell, then you alright in our book guy,” the man said with a smile. “We don’t like that rich little shit,” the scruffy man on Charles’s left said. Charles wasn’t surprised by this admission; he theorized that no one that had ever met Biddle actually liked the bastard. He was curious why men like these would hate someone of Biddle’s social position; he doubted that these ruffians had ever crossed paths with the self professed “Mr. Main Event”. “You see,” the man in front of Charles said as if he had noticed the quizzical looked on Charles’s face, “Biddle screwed the three of us over a few years ago. I’m not going to bore ya with the details but I’ll tell ya that we were happier than pigs in shit to find out that someone had finally stuck it to “im.” The men on either side of Charles nodded their agreement with that statement. “My names Duke by the way,” the man in front of Charles said as he held out a friendly hand. Charles shook the hand gladly, for the first time during his sentence he felt accepted. “And on your left is Hawk and on your right is Jimbo.” Charles shook the hands of these men also. “We was wonderin” if you’d like to join our little group,” Hawk said as he shook Charles’s hand. “You could definitely use the protection.” “I accept your offer,” Charles said quietly, “but on one condition.” “What’s that?” Duke asked with a raised eyebrow. “That you help me escape this hellhole so I can complete my revenge on Mike Biddle,” Charles said with an evil sneer. “Do you actually have a good escape plan,” Duke whispered. “Yes I do,” Charles answered. The three men leaned in, expecting to hear the plan right this minute. “But I’m not finished hashing it out just yet,” Charles said, “I’ll share the plan with all of you once I’m sure it’ll work’I just want to know that the three of you will help me when I do attempt to leave though.” “Shit hoss,” Duke said, “You get us outta here and we’ll help fuck up Biddle’s shit.” Hawk and Jimbo enthusiastically nodded their agreement. “Good,” Charles said. ***** Biddle had woken up to a monstrous headache, something of an everyday occurrence since his relocation to Las Vegas. As he rolled out of bed he realized that he didn’t remember anything from the night before’another everyday occurrence since his relocation. He looked over his shoulder to make sure that there wasn’t anyone else in bed with him, there wasn’t. Somehow he had been fortunate enough to not wake up with any unattractive women in his bed’a problem that he used to have in Beverly Hills when he drank heavily. He got up and went into the rest room, it was time for him to get ready to enjoy another day of his vacation before it was all over and he had to return to the octagon. First he’d hit the casino, have some complimentary drinks and then hit the bar’maybe get laid. Once Mike was dressed he left his suite and went down to the main gambling room. He smirked when he laid his eyes on a large golden slot machine that looked like a winner. He fumbled in his pocket from some change to feed the slot machine as he made his way across the casino floor. In doing this he was too distracted to notice the overweight brunette that was making a beeline towards him. She watched with a huge smile as the handsome stranger from the night before stopped at the slot machine and fed the device his pocket change before he pulled the lever. He pulled the lever but the three images that he pulled up didn’t match and he didn’t win any money. As he mumbled unhappily to himself she made her move. “Hey,” she said with a low voice as she tapped his shoulder. Biddle turned around to see who was talking to him and when he saw the fat brunette before him a disgusted look contorted his features. The woman didn’t seem to notice, she was too busy oozing over him. “Does that offer from last night still stand?” She asked with a nervous smile spread on her face. “What offer?” Biddle asked with an annoyed voice. Good God what had he done last night? “The one about playing slots with me silly,” the woman said with a playful laugh as she bit her lip. Biddle looked at her with disbelieving eyes; there was no way that he would ask a woman that looks that bad to do anything with him. The woman pouted a little bit. “Come on don’t you remember?” She asked. “You said I was beautiful and everything last night.” There was no holding it back now; Biddle began to laugh out loud. He laughed so loud that he was beginning to attract stares from the other patrons of the crowded casino. The woman didn’t know how to react to this, so she laughed along in a nervous manner. There were a few murmurs in the crowd of “Not this again”. This was the tenth time this week that the guests of the casino had been treated to Biddle humiliating some random woman. “What’s so funny?” She quietly asked after she had gotten tired of laughing.. Biddle had to take a deep breath before he could answer. “The idea that you think that I would ever want to be seen with a woman as fat and ugly as you are,” Biddle answered in-between arrogant giggles. “I-I don’t u-understand,” the woman said, she was starting to cry. “Y-you w-were so nice to m-me last n-night.” Biddle laughed again, oblivious of the fact that a small crowd had gathered around them. “I was drunk last night,” he paused to look her up and down, “I must’ve had more to drink than I thought.” He continued laughing as the woman in front of him began to cry. The woman continued to stammer and cry as Biddle laughed hysterically at her plight. The crowd around them had grown larger and was starting to get almost as ugly as Biddle thought the woman in front of him was. A few of the men in crowd wanted to intervene and teach Biddle a lesson on how to properly treat a woman, but Biddle didn’t notice this nor did he care’he was having the greatest laugh of his life at this woman’s expense. Biddle didn’t even find it in the least bit odd that this sort of thing seemed to happen to him every day. Fortunately for Biddle his trusted bodyguard was watching over him as per the usual. Joe Balboa used his massive six foot seven frame to break through the crowd that had gathered around his boss and stood beside the laughing man. “There ain’t nothin” to see here folks,” Balboa said with a firm tone. However, the crowd did not disperse as he had hoped so he had to reiterate his demand. “Get a fuckin” move on,” the Bronx Behemoth bellowed at the top of his lungs. This time the crowd was startled and finally took notice of Balboa and his demand that they disperse. The crowd left, some of them muttering that someone should do something about the Biddle nuisance. So with the crowd finally dispersed and the two stars of the dog and pony show not noticing, Balboa tapped Biddle on the shoulder. “Hey boss man, what’s goin” on here?” He asked. Biddle wiped the tears from his eyes and took several deep breaths before he answered. “This silly bitch,” Biddle said as he pointed a finger at the woman, “thinks that I would actually lower myself by talking to her while sober.” This made the woman sob even harder and Balboa instantly recognized her as the woman from the night before. Balboa sighed, this was the tenth time this week that he had to disperse a crowd and prevent his boss from getting beat up. He nudged Biddle and motioned that perhaps now was the time to leave before this broad caused another scene. Biddle looked at the hysterical woman and concurred with his bodyguard’s observation. Once they were safely tucked away within the confines of the casino’s expanse, the Brawler spoke again. “You need to cut down on the sauce boss man,” he said with a stern voice. “And miss out on all these laughs” Hell no,” Biddle replied as he chuckled again. The bodyguard realized that this was a losing argument and that he should probably change the subject. Then he remembered the phone call that he had gotten earlier, perhaps that would get his boss to knock it off for awhile. Balboa paused for a moment before he continued. “That Barfart’Bastardfar’whatever his name is called today boss,” he said, unhappy that he had once again stumbled over the name of Biddle’s business manager, William Bastafar. A surprised look overtook Biddle’s face, he hadn’t heard from William since the day he left for Showdown in Tokyo. “What did he want?” Biddle inquired. “He said dat he got a fax from GLOBAL about tha Reload card boss man,” Balboa said with a sly smile. Biddle sighed, yet another sign that his vacation was rapidly approaching its end’still it would be nice to get back in the octagon. “Let me guess,” Biddle mused, “I’m in the opening match again aren’t I?” Balboa shook his head. “No way boss man, you’re gonna fight that Josh punk for tha International Title.” “Well it’s about damn time,” Biddle exclaimed. This was the best news Biddle had heard in a long time. “And against Josh even’why they’re practically handing me the title!” Biddle’s bodyguard joined him in his laughter at how ridiculously easy it would be for him to finally claim the International Title. “That’s not all boss man,” Balboa said, “you an” me are teamin? up for the Tag straps too.” “Well then,” Biddle said as he patted his bodyguard on the shoulder, “this good fortune is cause to celebrate. Let’s go have some drinks on me,” Biddle said as he headed towards the bar. “And then we train?” Balboa asked with an expectant tone. He had something to prove and didn’t plan on wasting valuable time getting drunk. “Perhaps,” Biddle replied. Balboa stopped in his tracks; he didn’t like what he was hearing from his boss. “You ain’t gonna train boss?” “Why should I?” Biddle laughed, “It’s not like Josh will even bother to show up. He sure as hell didn’t show up the last time he was supposed to fight me.” “What about the tag match?” Balboa inquired. “Don’t even worry about that,” Biddle said, “knowing the state of the GLOBAL tag team division I bet there’s an extreme lack of talent in the match.” Feeling somewhat assured, Balboa joined Biddle for an early celebration drink. Biddle felt arrogant as usual as he thought about the year ahead, if only he knew of the trouble that would be waiting for him in the coming weeks.
