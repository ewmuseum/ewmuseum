Wesley Snipes made it look so easy

10 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/10/05 A decaying no man's land populated by condemned buildings and hungry homeless. Steam rises from manhole covers, drifting across the litter- lined streets. Suddenly,a black Mercedes 850 appears over the crest of a hill, stereo system belting out Filter. Traci, a wasp-wasted woman, sits behind the wheel. 20s, rich, sickeningly attractive. Hungry eyes. Squirming around in the passenger seat is Kenny, a model/actor boy- toy with a sub-zero IQ and a 'fuck me sideways' grin. Kenny: So where we going? Traci: It's a surprise. Kenny: I likes surprises. Traci eyeballs Kenny. Traci: What do you have down there, little man? Dennis: Heat-seeker. Traci: I'll bet. She slides a manicured hand up his thigh, squeezes his groin. Kenny moans. She pulls her hand away, downshifts. The 850 threads a narrow alley into a vacant lot and brakes hard. Traci and Kenny climb out. She leads him into a meat packing plant. Traci leads Dennis around the back of the plant, where a host of workers are loading refrigerated trucks with product. Kenny: What the fuck are we doing here? Traci just smiles, heads on into the plant via a loading door. The workers ignore her. Kenny follows Traci through the bowels of the plant, catching glimpses here and there of carcasses being rendered or hacked apart. Through one partially open door we see what might be a line of bodybags being trundled into the back of a truck via a hook and chain pulley-system. But Kenny doesn't have enough time to be disturbed by the vision, because he's being pulled away by Traci, led down a stairwell into the basement. At the end of the hall is a steel door, with perhaps, just the faintest hint of music heard coming from beyond. Traci knocks. A 'peep-hole' slot opens and a black light shines into Traci's eyes. A voice behind the door offers a verbal challenge, speaking a strange language, laced with a devilish cadence. Traci responds in kind. The door opens. Traci gives Kenny a knowing wink and enters. Dennis follows. Traci and Kenny move past a hulking doorman, making their way down a narrow stairway. Dennis is suitably impressed. The club is elite, underground. White-tiled walls and floors for easy hosing, chromed fittings, run-off gutters, drains. No bar. Bodies writhe on the strobe-lit dance floor. A heavy S&M scene. Leather. Latex. Tattoos. Body-piercings. A D.J. wearing head-mounted spotlights orchestrates the tunes on twin- decks. A beat pounds so heavy it could jar the fillings from your teeth. Brutal 'DARKCORE' along the lines of Prodigy or Underground. Traci pulls Kenny out onto the dance floor. They sway. A lupine-featured girl with a streak of white running through her raven hair moves in behind Kenny, pressing up against him. Her name is Arly. She flicks her tongue against Kenny' ear -- it's been pierced with a silver post which clicks against her teeth. Tattooed across her back in black is a swirling, tribal vortex. Kenny is now sandwiched between Traci and Arly, the three of them dry-humping their way to every man's glory. The beat gets LOUDER. The action heavier. The atmosphere more narcotic. People are stripping off their clothes, sweating like fiends. It's a virtual orgy. Kenny laughs, reveling in the hedonism. Everything rises to a fever pitch -- Kenny(over the music): Fuck, I need a drink!!! Traci just smiles. Then Kenny notices a drop of something spatter his hand. It looks like blood. Kenny looks up, concerned. More blood droplets are falling. Traci's face is sprinkled with them now. Kenny stops dancing. Traci turns her face toward the ceiling, as if washing herself in a summer shower, now the other club goers are looking up too. Blood showers down from sprinkler heads in the ceiling, onto the dancers. The club goers love it, thrusting their heads back, mouths open wide to receive the crimson offering. Horrified, Kenny recoils, turning towards Traci, whose face morphs into a sinister snarl. Her canines extend, tapering to razor-sharp points. Her tongue flicks, lizard-like as fingernails sharpen into claws. All this while the whites of her eyes bleed red, pupils oscillating hypnotically. Traci: What's wrong, baby? Kenny SCREAMS, pushes away from Traci, only -- Arly has fangs now too. In fact, everyone in the club except poor Kenny does. That's because they're all vampires. Kenny: Shit, you're all vampires! Traci: You mean you couldn't figure it out? We're in a fucking meat packing plant, you idiot! Kenny tries to run, but the burly Doorman blocks his exit, brutally smashing his fist into Kenny's face. Kenny falls, dazed. The club-goers close in around him, cackling like hyenas about to rip apart a dead wildebeest. The strobe lights quicken to a seizure-inducing intensity. Kenny spins, tumbling into Traci's arms. She shoves him forward and he lands on the floor, falling at someone's boot-clad feet. He looks up. A dark figure sits in the shadows, unnoticed until this moment. The figure stands, moves into the light as time screeches to a halt, a large black man towers above Kenny, wearing dark glasses and a leather longcoat, a sneer of cruel contempt etched upon a face tempered by a lifetime of horror. It's Brick, dressed up like Blade. Brick whips open his long coat, shrugging it off, revealing an arsenal of high-tech weapons strapped to his body: 6-point adjustable body armor, a modified CAR-15 assault rifle with an ultra-violet entry light, two Casull .454 revolvers, a 'Demon' automatic cross-bow, a bandoleer of mahogany stakes, an Indian-style katar punching dagger -- and last, but certainly not least, a silver sword which is secured in a back-scabbard. The vampire club-goers stare back. Nuclear silence. And then -- All hell breaks loose. With a SNARL, Traci charges at Brick, moving at superhuman speed. Brick draws his Casulls, FIRES in multiple directions. A round roars through the air towards Traci. A silver-tipped dum- dum bullet which explodes on contact. WHAM! The round punches a fist-sized hole through Traci's chest, continuing on into the vamp behind her! Vampire blood fountains. Both creatures tumble forward, their bodies liquefying into puddles of black oil which go gurgling down the run-off drains. Brick continues FIRING, then -CLICK!- magazines empty. Next. He holsters the Casulls, swings up his assault rifle, calmly flicks on the UV entry light mounted above -- Quicksilver leaps twenty feet straight up into the air. We've never seen anything move so fast. She CRASHES through a glass skylight, disappearing into the night just as a shaft of blinding UV 'sunlight' cuts across the vampires. They rear back, skin smoking from the light's corrosive effects. Brick opens fire, pumping round after round of wooden fragmentation bullets into the crowd. The strobe lights flicker as the mayhem mounts. Some of the vampires try to flee, scurrying up the stairs, but the exit quickly becomes clogged with liquefying bodies. Then Brick's CAR-15 jams. The remaining club-goers see their opening, surge forward en masse. Brick drops the rifle, reaches over his shoulder and unsheathes his sword with a double-handed grip. Brick moves like lightning, hacking his way into numerous vamps, cutting them into pieces. Brick suddenly finds himself wrestling with a feral-faced six-foot- something nightmare named Finn. The vampire rears back its head, jaws stretching wide. Brick forces an elbow against Finn's throat, trying to keep him at bay. With his other hand he reaches to his bandoleer, pulls out a stake -- CRUNCH! Brick shoves the stake through the vampire's larynx. Finn gurgles, clutches at his throat. Brick rolls out from under, unholsters the cross-bow secured to his leg. With a flick of a switch the arms of the bow -SNAP!- open, drawing the bow-string taut. Brick shoots a bolt in Finn's shoulder, throwing him backwards and nailing him to the wall. As Finn reaches over with his other hand to pull out the stake, Brick FIRES AGAIN. A second bolt slams into Finn's other arm, effectively pinning him like a butterfly to a board. UP ABOVE, mounted in one of the corners, is a security camera. Brick fires a cross-bow bolt straight into the lens. Brick strides over, placing his sword against Finn's chest. Brick: Where is Corvus? Finn glares, trying to speak, gagging on the stake still lodged in his trachea. Brick: Got something in your throat. Brick yanks the stake free. The vampire laughs, air whistling through his ruined larynx. Finn: Fuck you, Rottweiler, I ain't saying shit! Brick: Corvus. Finn responds with a slew of rapid-fire obscenities in the vampire tongue. Brick sees he's getting nowhere fast and calmly sheathes his sword. He unclips a white phosphorous grenade from his combat harness. Finn: You won't stop him, Brick. The Platinum Series is coming, and the Dark Prince is gonna -- Brick shoves the grenade in Finn's mouth, pulls the pin. WHOOSH! Finn goes up like a roman candle. Brick turns, surveying his work, ignoring the howling pyre behind him: All evidence of the vampires is gone, with the exception of a few oily-black puddles. Kenny sits huddled in a corner, having pissed his pants. As Brick approaches, he cringes back. Kenny: Please don't -- Brick simply grabs Kenny by the jaw, tilting his head upward, rotating it from side to side --looking for bite marks. There aren't any. Brick moves on, leaving Kenny alone amidst the carnage. Kenny: Wait! Brick stops mid-step. Kenny: What about Adam Myers? Brick is silent for a moment. Brick: He knows the score. As Brick starts up the stairs, he pauses in mid-step. A cockroach scurries out from underfoot. Brick adjusts his footfall, sparing the roach. He continues on up the stairs, disappearing in the smoky haze.
