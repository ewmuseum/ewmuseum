The Max-Attack: Chapter 18

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The scene opens to the hallway of the hotel Roswell was in. We starts running after Ben and Katherine. Ben and his mom start running faster down the hallway and are almost at the elevator. Max cathes up to both of them and starts talking to them. “I’m really sorry. Is there any way to make it up to both of you??asks Roswell. “No. Stay out of our life’s.” “What if I took you to the next Evolution show when I defend my title against Kid Flash?” asks Roswell again. “Are you serious?” asks Ben. “I’d do anything to make you feel better.” “Mom, can we goto the next show with Roswell?” asks Ben. “Well I don’t know. He didn’t take us last time.” “Pwease!!!” “Oh, Ok.” “Yes!” shouts Ben. Suddenly the roof starts to fall and the rocks start hitting Roswell. Max looks up at the hole in the roof and sees that there is a blue little dot flying in the sky. It comes closer and Roswell jumps up through the hole and stares with his arch enemy, The Blue Bat. Ben and his mom run to the elevator and push the lobby button. They run outside and get into there white Sunfire and look at Max who is standing staring down with The Blue Bat. “Be careful Max!” yells Katherine. Max doesn’t reply and just stands there staring him down. The Blue Bat comes onto the roof and runs at Max kicking him in the gut sending him backwards. Max flips onto his feet and throws a few punches at The Blue Bat. He dodges all of them and kicks Max again sending him back. “MAX!” yells Ben. Max flips onto his feet again and gives the thumbs up sign to Ben and then hits a side kick sending The Blue Bat sideways. The Blue Bat smiles and grabs a little ball from his pocket and throws it straight at Roswell’s stomach. It explodes in a cloud of dust and sends Roswell off the building hitting the ground with a thud. When the dust clears The Blue Bat is now where to be seen and Ben and Katherine rush over to Max to see if he’s hurt. “Are you alright?” asks Katherine. “I’ve had better falls then that.” says Roswell. “Like what?” asks Ben. “Well I fell off the Sears Tower.” Ben’s eyes widen and so does Katherine’s. “How long were you in the hospital?” asks Ben. “I didn’t goto the hospital.” “Why?” “Stop being rude Ben.” says Katherine. “I’ve also fell off a huge mountain.” Suddenly a wind pushes Roswell forward into Katherine and they kiss! Max gets up with a shocked face and starts blushing. Katherine starts blushing to. “Why did you do that?” asks Ben. “I didn’t do that on purpose.” says Roswell. “Wow.” says Katherine. Roswell looks at his watch and it is 2:00 in the afternoon. “Uh-oh. I have to go.” says Roswell “Bye!” says Katherine and Ben at the same time. Roswell and Katherine smile at each other and Max walks back into the hotel and Ben and Katherine sit on a bench resting there legs. Roswell walks to his room and into the bathroom. He walks back out and goes to his window. It is filled with black smoke because of the explosion. Max looks around the sky and sees the same blue dot going the other. It look like it’s going out of town but Roswell ignores it and lays down on his bed. 5:00 MINUTES LATER Max gets up from the bed and walks balk out of his room and out of the hotel. Ben and Katherine are not on the bench but they are across the street walking into A&W. They walk back out with a couple hamburgers and they walks down the sidewalk. Max goes and sits down on the same bench and holds his head. Suddenly he opens his eyes but he isn’t in Chicago. Roswell gets off the bench and starts walking around. After a while Roswell notices that he is walking in the middle of a highway. He sees a smashed up car and an ambulance with a stretcher beside it. Roswell walks to the car and sees a little baby sleeping in the back of it. The paramedics come and take the baby to safety. Roswell walks over to the stretcher and sees a man laying there not breathing. The paramedics cover the body up and put it in the ambulance. Roswell closes his eyes and then opens them again and is back in Chicago. “I think that was a flashback!” yells Roswell. SCENE FADES
