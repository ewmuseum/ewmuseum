A Mother and Her Son...

13 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/13/04 Still a couple days removed from his phone call from Christina McCoy informing Donny that he was given a shot at the World Title, the feeling of excitement of and accomplishment hasn’t gone away. Donny has the butterflies in his stomach and in his mind he has determination. He realizes Josh is one of the best there is, but this also means Donny has to be one of the best there is as well. This is what he really got into wrestling for, he wanted to win the top gold in the business, The World Championship. Donny has been in the business for almost 3 years, and finally someone somewhere outside of his inner circle is recognizing his hard work and dedication. With all these thoughts in mind, Donny wanted to get a down to earth prospective of everything, so he went and spent an afternoon at his Mother’s house. If anyone can keep Donny from being overwhelmed with the situation, it would be his Mother. While at his Mother’s house, Donny was sitting on the couch looking at old photos of himself and his family. All these pictures tell the story of who Donny is and how he came to be. All these family members, each one had a role in his life. But none had a bigger role than his mother. His Dad as you know left the McNasty family months before Donny was even born. It was 1978 and his Mother is now left to raise three kids, and run a potato farm all by herself, because of the coward William McNasty was. Truly Donny’s Mother was a miracle worker. As Donny grew older he recognized everything she did for them, and vowed to make it big to help take care of her. Which is what he’s doing now. As Donny’s Mom, Diane sits down next to him, they begin to look at the pictures together, they do a lot of laughing and sometimes they frown over loved ones they lost. Diane tells her baby boy, that he was the one she worried about the most ever since he was born. Mothers always have that sense on who is going to have the difficult time out of their children. Her senses were right when it came to Donny. Things were never easy as ABC with him? While elementary school, Donny didn’t exactly fit in with the other kids, for starters he was taller and bigger, and well his mind was always in different places than kids that age would be. Donny was more concerned with World Politics at the age of 10 then he was with long division. As you can surely well imagine, Donny was definatley different for a long time. As the years went on, Donny and his Mother were closer but yet lurking in the shadows was those darn teenage years. Donny was nothing short of a hellion. At least once a week in Junior High he would have after school detention, mainly for not doing his homework and talking during class. Then again there was also the occasional fist fight which of course Donny finished. Of course at that age Donny took interest in the girls, but the girls didn’t take interest in him. As he went through his years in high school Donny changed as a person which is expected of course, but Donny became a man in those years, he helped out around the farm, he learned to take responsibility and he also learned how to help others. He learned a lot of the qualities and morals his mother has always possessed. Donny and his mother put down the photo album and talk. Donny talks to her about his upcoming moment in the spotlight where he has a chance to become the World Champion. Donny tells his mother about his excitement and even the butterflies in the stomach. His mother looks at him with a look of pride. She tells him that if he puts his mind to it and works hard at it he will become the champion. Simple advice from a mother, maybe something we all have heard before from our parents, but when it comes to Donny it rings true. Diane tells her son some other words of encouragement, that no matter what happens Monday night on Evolution, that she will always be proud of her son, and he will always be a winner in her eyes. To Donny, his family is the one thing that is more important to him than the World Championship. With nerves of steel and a heart of gold, Donny J McNasty would make a great champion that everyone can be proud of?
