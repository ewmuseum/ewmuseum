Saturday Night Live

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/24/04 Fade in. We see the backstage area of Studio 8H. We see Rachel Dratch standing by when Lorne Michaels walks up to them. Lorne: Hey, Rachel. Have you seen Brick? Rachel: Yeah, Lorne. He's down that way. Lorne: OK, thanks. Lorne walks down the hallway as the camera follows him to Brick. Lorne: Brick? Brick: Oh, hey, Lorne. Lorne: I just wanted to thank you for hosting the show tonight. Brick: Well, it's good publicity for Global Wrestling. I just hope everything goes ok. Lot of cats in the company would just love to ruin this. Lorne: Well, I had the same talk with Christina McCoy and she assured me that she had the situation under control. Voice: Yo, Michaels! Enter Finesse Mitchell and Maya Rudolph, dressed as Deacon Holmes and Rhaiza. Finesse: You know this bootleg, right? Maya: You know my man is the real star of GW! He's the one who should be hosting the show. Brick: Deacon, what are you doing here, man? Finesse: You know why I'm here, old man! I should be hosting Saturday Night Live! You should have come asked me to host the show, Michaels! Lorne: Well, I didn't. I asked Brick, so please...don't hurt me. Lorne then runs off stage. Brick: Look, Deacon, I don't feel like dealing with your crap tonight, okay? This is a very important night for me. We already got Wrestlecade, so just go to your hotel room and get some sleep. Finesse: What, you afraid o' me? I don't blame you. Voice: Choo choo! Enter Kenan Thompson, dressed as Train. Brick: Aw, come on! Kenan: Man, how you gonna come an host Saturday Night Live when you can't even beat this skinny little no-talent punk right here? Choo-choo! Finesse: Who you callin' skinny, punk! Kenan: You, punk! Choo-choo! Maya: No, you don't talk about my man like that! Kenan: Shut up, you wanna be J-Lo! Brick: AIGHT! OK, now all o' y'all need to go somewhere. I'll deal with y'all in Nebraska. Finesse: How bout you deal with us now? Brick: I don't feel like dealing with y'all now. Kenan: What you scared, punk? Choo-choo! CHOO-CHOO! Brick: Aight, that's it. Brick 'punches' Finesse across the jaw and then 'elbows' Kenan in the gut. Maya runs off-camera. Brick then grabs Finesse and starts 'punching' him in the gut, repeatedly. he then 'clocks' him across the jaw, 'knocking him out'. He then puts Kenan in a full nelson. Brick: Aight, now say it! Kenan: Ahhh! Brick: SAY IT! Kenan: LIVE FROM NEW YORK, IT'S SATURDAY NIGHT! Cut to the opening montage, where we see the various cast members. Don Pardo: It's Saturday Night Live! With Rachel Dratch! Jimmy Fallon! Tina Fey! Will Forte! Darrell Hammond! Seth Myers! Chris Parnell! Amy Poehler! Maya Rudolph! Horatio Sanz! Featuring Fred Armisen! Finesse Mitchell! Kenan Thompson! A cartoon by Robert Smigel! Musical guest Joss Stone! And your host, Brick! Dissolve to the set of Saturday Night Live, where the band plays. Don Pardo: Ladies and gentlemen, Brick! Brick enters through the door and jogs onto the stage. The band reaches the crescendo and then ends. Brick: Thank you! The audience is cheering raucously. Brick: Thank you, thank you! Aw man, it's great to be hosting Saturday Night Live! Never did I think I'd be hosting this show. It's not like I have a movie coming up or anything. I just have this pay-per-view Wrestlecade coming up. The audience lets out another cheer. Brick: Now, this is my first time here on Saturday Night Live. But the funny thing is somebody told me I was here before. I didn’t understand. He said it was back in “77. I was three in 77. Anyway, he said there was footage proving him right. If we could roll it for a second... Fade out. Fade back in to footage from 1977 of the 70's funk band Brick as they perform 'Dazz'. Dazz: Everybody go on and dance If you want to Music makes your body move Well all right Funky dancing get up Get down, shake your booty Music makes your body move Well all right Fade out. Fade back in to Brick (the wrestler). Brick: Yeah, yeah, I get it. That’s the old funk band Brick. I get that a lot. Suddenly, Amy Poehler comes out. Amy: Um, hey Brick. Brick: Uh, hey, Amy. What’s up? Amy: Oh, nothing, nothing. Brick: Uh, Amy, I’m kind of in the middle of the monologue. Amy: Oh, this won’t take long. I just wanted to know if you could do something for me. Brick: Uh, sure. What is it? Amy reaches into her pocket and pulls out a wad of cash. Amy: Listen, there’s this guy in the audience that was giving me trouble earlier and I was wondering if you could, ah, She places the wad in Brick's hand. Amy: take care of him. There he is, right there. Cut to a male audience member. The audience starts laughing. Cut back to Brick and Amy. Brick: You wanna pay me to beat up some guy in the audience? Amy: Yeah, sure. Brick: Amy, I can’t do that. Amy: What, that’s not enough money for you? I’ll give you more after the show. Brick: No, Amy, it doesn’t matter how much money you give me. I’m not gonna beat this guy up. No, I won’t do it. I’m sorry. Suddenly, Jimmy Fallon enters from the right. Jimmy: Hey, Brick. Brick: Hey, Jimmy. Guys, I have a monologue to do. Jimmy: Yeah, yeah, I know. Listen, I need you to take care of somebody for me. Jimmy points to Amy. Brick looks to where he points and gets exasperated. Brick: What? Oh, come on! I am not gonna beat Amy up for you! Jimmy: Why not? Brick: Wha-what do you mean “why not?” Look at her, then look at me! Amy: What, you don't think I can take you? Brick: What? Take me? Amy-- Jimmy: I can pay you double. Brick: Man, no! Jimmy: Aw, what’s the matter, Brick? Don’t have what it takes to be a hero for hire? Brick: Real funny, Jimmy. Some of you may know that I’m trying out for a movie called Luke Cage: Hero for Hire. That's the joke. Look, y'all, I'm not gonna beat somebody up for money, okay? No matter how much you give me. Jimmy: OK, fine. Come on, Amy. Jimmy and Amy exit stage left. Amy: You were gonna pay him to beat me up? Jimmy: I thought he'd take it. Brick(sighs): Anyway, so I was... Suddenly, out comes Maya Rudolph. Maya: Hey, Brick. Brick: Uh, Maya, what's up? Maya: See, it's like this, Brick. I was wondering if... Brick: Look, I already told Jimmy and Amy, and I'm telling you now, I'm not gonna beat somebody up for you for money! I'm just not! Maya: I was gonna ask if you were coming to the after party. Maya exits. Brick: Oh, I'm sorry. I'm just under a lot of stress, trying to get through this monologue and... I feel like such an ass. Anyway, we... Enter Will Forte. Will: Hey, Brick, I was wondering if you could help me out. Brick: Will, if you're gonna ask me to beat somebody up for you... Will: No, it's not that. Brick: Oh, good. Will: I was wondering if you could pretend that I beat you up. Brick: What? Will: Well, there's this woman that I wanna impress and... Brick: Will, listen to what you just asked me. Who's gonna believe you beat me up? Will: What, you don't think I can take you? Brick: What? Man, get outta here! Will exits stage left. Brick: OK, for the record, to anybody here in the studio, I am not taking money to beat people up tonight. So please don't ask me to. Cut to backstage, where the other castmembers and the crew start putting their wallets away. Lorne Michaels has a silver briefcase handcuffed to his left arm. Cut back to the set. Brick: Anyway, we got a great show for you tonight! Joss Stone is here! The audience cheers. Brick: So stick around! We'll be right back! The camera zooms away from Brick as the band plays. Fade to black.
