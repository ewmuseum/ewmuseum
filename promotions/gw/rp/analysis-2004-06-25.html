Date: 06.25.04 June 23rd, 2004 ? Approximately 1:00 pm Los Angeles, California ? Carlton Press 'And while the law [of competition] may be sometimes hard for the individual, it is best for the race, because it ensures the survival of the fittest in every department.' -Andrew Carnegie “I hate negotiations, Mr. Carlton.” The voice of Maverick Reno can be heard before the scene even comes fully into view. “So why don’t we cut to the chase, yes?” The setting is a lush office, enlargements of tabloids and news papers hang in frames all around the office, each magazine baring the same name, “The Tribunal.” Maverick Reno is adorned in a very vibrant red suit, the collar of Mandarin style as it wraps about his neck, the button resembling the coat of arms that Reno bears so often. With this, a pair of red dress pants as well as a freshly polished set of Armani wing-tips. The suit is adorned in small intricate designs in certain areas using black to accent the red. Reno’s hair has been pulled back into a tight pony tail, his face uncovered for this meeting. Sitting across from Reno on the receptive side of the desk is Max Carlton: trash news entrepreneur. Max is dressed in a white short-sleeve dress shirt and a loose tie, his receding hairline winning a battle that began when Mr. Carlton turned thirty. Max drums his fingers on his desk, each finger featuring an even larger ring studded with more diamonds and gems in as gaudy a design as possible. The office truly compliments the man; modern and edgy meets grimy and disgusting. There’s something dingy about the room even though it’s been kept nicely by the cleaning staff. Perhaps it’s the furniture that looks as if it was bought at an 80’s surplus store? That’s it. The room is the pure embodiment of the 80’s business man. Cut-throat, grimy, disgusting, and lacking in any ounce of tact. “You’re a strong man, Mavy Baby! Not many men have the cahones to walk right into my fortress, and sit right before me. Course, you’re just like all the other big-wigs I’ve had to deal with. You think money can buy me! See, Mavy Baby, what you don’t get is that this story will be huge! You own Reno Company, work with hundreds of businesses, and you’re a big-name wrestler with Global! Mavy, Baby” you have to understand, this story is going to boost my sales!” Reno smiles his dry smile, his eyes showing little to no emotion as he speaks, “Mr. Carlton.” “Mavy” baaaaby. You can call me, Max. After all, you’re the man who’s making me a millionaire!” Carlton spits off, offering a cheesy grin, capped with a few gold teeth. Ah, to be stuck in a time long forgotten (and thankfully so.) “Right. Max. Reno smirks a bit as he glances about the office, his words forming precisely as always. “We either strike a deal on this matter or my lawyer is going to have a field day taking everything you own. I suggest the former rather than the latter; it’s much easier to cleanup.” Max laughs a bit as he leans back, kicking his feet up onto the desk. He pulls a pack of Turkish Gold from his pocket, the extra long, extremely filtered version, and puts one between his lips before lighting the tip. “Mavy, babe. No lawyer, no matter the training, or law team for that matter has ever been able to break my team! Let’s face it, Mav, it’s Freedom of Speech! It’s the American way! The American Constitution.” Maverick smiles. “The constitution also states that each man has the right to bear arms.” Reno stands and pulls a silenced glock from his back and trains the laser directly between Max’s eyes. Max goes cross eyed and then looks at the gun, the cigarette dropping from his lips. “MAVY! BABY! Why” we can work this out! What happened to that ? that lawyer of yours, Mavy!”” Carlton is shaking, his heart pumps. His adrenaline rushes through his blood stream, sweat stops, he’s ready to run. He evacuates his bladder, soaking his pants in urine. “My lawyer” This is my lawyer. Certified by Gaston Glock, he never loses a case. His opening statement is often the last statement made. You chose to play the tough guy, Mr. Carlton. You chose to ignore my requests for a peaceful resolution.” Reno pulls the trigger, blasting a silent bullet through the air and through the white shirt. The shirt splits followed by the skin, blood blasting in all directions inside and out as the bullet lodges into Carlton’s right lung, forcing it to instantly collapse in on itself. Blood drenches his shirt as his breathing becomes raspy. “Now, I can walk out right now and call a doctor. Or I can put another bullet into the other side of your chest and be done with you. It’s your choice and all you have to do is tell me where my file and any backups are.” Carlton breaths heavily, his hand motioning towards a file cabinet. “Th’there” hurry.” Reno smiles and slowly approaches the file cabinet, his eyes moving from A to R quickly. He pulls the handle and smirks as it’s locked. Again, he lifts the gun and fires at the locking mechanism, the sound a little louder than the first bullet had been. Reno pulls the drawer out and smiles. There’s a reason Reno had requested to meet Max Carlton at one in the afternoon on Wednesday; it’s not a normal work day for the Tribunal staff. Reno pulls the drawer out and begins going through the files; he finds his folder and pulls it out. Slowly Maverick turns to look at Max who is still breathing heavily, his body growing visibly weaker as he slowly passes from life to death. “You sure this is all of it, Carlton” If I read anything in the Tribunal for the next twenty years, I’ll be back to finish what I’ve started.” Reno walks towards the desk as Carlton nods quickly, his hand moving to a drawer in his desk. “Smart man, Max.” Reno pulls the drawer open and grabs a small box, full of documents and tapes from the sound of it. Reno places his gun down atop the box and grabs his trench coat, slipping it on. “Sorry I can’t stay. Busy day and all.” Reno grabs the box, slips it under one arm and with his gloved hand, picks up the gun again, turns and fires three more shots. One of the shells lodge into his left side, shattering a rib, the second kills him as it passes into his neck, severing veins and muscle. The third is overkill; slamming into his face, the leverage able to knock him backwards and onto the floor behind his desk. Reno sighs and places his gun on the inside of his trench coat, the body now hidden behind the desk to some extent. Reno walks down the hall outside of the glass office, shielded from outside view by blinds and the occasional fichus plant. Reno takes a side exit, using the stairs to go down the main floor. He looks around and then walks out of the semi-vacated building. He turns a sharp corner and walks to the back where a black limousine is waiting for him. Tobias DeSarret stands outside, leaning back against the limousine, what looks to be a hand-made cigarette hanging from between his lips. “You do know that you’re putting that out before you get back into my limousine.” Reno speaks aloud to Tobias, the comment floating on the air as Tobias looks up, his eyes slightly blood shot. A moment passes as he holds his lips tight, pursing them almost before exhaling, the aroma filling the air around them. “Finished?” Tobias smiles and nods slowly as he presses the tip of the blunt against his finger, making sure it’s completely out. “Must you spoil all of my entertainment, Maverick?” Tobias presents, a smirk crossing his face. “Yes. I must, actually.” The door to the limousine opens and Maverick slips inside, Tobias pockets the paraphernalia and slips in behind him. Naria Xiou is seated Inside, her eyes blank as she stares at the two men. “Naria, I need you to use one of our blocked cell phones and call the local paramedics. I promised Mr. Carlton I’d call a doctor for him and I am a man of my word.” Maverick smiles his own sadistic smile as he allows his trench coat to drop from his shoulders again. He sits back in his seat as the limo moves down the road. Naria picks up a cell phone, sighing as she does what she’s told. Quickly she dials. “Yes. Hello. Someone’s been shot at the Tribunal office. Right. That’s the address. No, I don’t want to give me name. I” ? Naria smirks as she applies a bit of emotion to her voice, “I’m scared?” Quickly Naria hangs up the phone and looks up at Maverick, her eyes returning to their glassy state. “Taken care of. I just want to remind you, Maverick, your plane leaves at four. We have to be in New York on time to catch your flight to London.” “Yes I know. We just have one final stop to make before then.” Maverick states as he pulls a laptop unto his lap and opens the top, his eyes quickly looking over the data that appears on the screen. “We really should go straight to the airport, Maverick. You know we couldn’t secure a landing strip for our own plane and these don’t tend to wait on people, no matter how much money they can offer.” Naria barks, giving Reno a look. He glances up at her slowly and smirks a bit, his eyes returning to the screen. Tobias kicks his feet up on the seat across from him and reclines, his black suit complete with blue turtle neck conforming to his body. “So why are we heading to the old apple anyway?” Reno glances up at Tobias quickly, his response rather straight forward, “WMW.” “WMW” Whoopty-motherfuckin-whoop, Mav. What’s that supposed to mean?” Tobias laughs aloud, waiting for Maverick to respond. Reno closes the laptop and pushes it off to the side before leaning back in the seat, his eyes closing lightly to shield his eyes from everything. “Wrestling Mid-West. I’m assuming Bubba had something to do with this; basically, we have to waste our time defending “Global honor” against these other five miscreants.” Reno states his words almost drifting off with him. “And before you ask, it’s a favor.” “Wait a damned minute. What one mother-bitch-ass-fucking minute. You” Maverick Reno? are doing a FAVOR for Bubba McCoy? Woah, wheeeew. Where the hell am I!” I know one thing, I’m not in fucking Kansas anymore cause this shit’s not as kosher as my ?” Reno cuts Tobias off by lifting his hand quickly. Tobias looks at Reno and then across at Naria, neither of them speaking yet. Reno opens his mouth slowly, his tongue moving inside to form the first word, “The favor isn’t for Bubba, Tobias.” Reno utters, ending the conversation there and then. His hand lowers and time passes, silence consuming the vehicle as if some technical error took out the sound. Finally Maverick reaches off to the side and grabs a small phone. His thumb finds the power button and then the number 1. The phone begins to ring and Reno holds the phone to his ear. “Aiden. Maverick.” There’s a pause as Aiden speaks on the other end, Tobias? attention being captured with the mention of his brother, “Yes. Everything is under control. Have you prepared the flat for our arrival?” Tobias looks curiously at Naria and mouths without making a sound “he’s preparing a flat?” Naria looks away from Tobias, rolling her eyes in the process. “Very good. We should be arriving in New York late this evening and then immediately boarding for London. Thanks for the help, Aiden. We’ll be in touch.”Reno quickly listens as Aiden wraps up his end and both men end the call. Carefully, Reno returns the phone to its place near the wall and leans back in his seat. Tobias looks from one to the other, neither speaking. Reno rests his head back against the seat as his eyes slowly close, Tobias growing more and more impatient. Tobias sits back, waiting patiently for an answer to his unspoken question. Naria picks up a small book and opens to a marked page, her eyes scanning the text as she reads silently. The silence is finally broken. “Why the fuck do you have my brother ? in London” giving someone a flat tire. I mean, I’m not the man behind the desk but it seems to me that if I were going to pay for his flight to London, I’d have him doing a bit more than preparing a flat!” Naria places her book down and looks, more than slightly annoyed, in Tobias? direction. Maverick opens his eyes with a sigh and glances towards Tobias. “Well!”” “Tobias, a flat is an apartment.” Tobias smirks and looks at Naria quickly, “Your brother is preparing one of our properties for us so that we have a place to stay while we’re in London.” Reno rests his head back against the seat again, his eyes closing. Tobias sits back, a bored look on his face as he looks around the limousine. Naria returns to her book when the phone rings. “Damnit. Am I not going to rest?” Reno snatches the phone from the side of the limo and pulls it to his ear, his voice carrying through the receiver. “Maverick here. Who is this?” There’s a pause and a half-smile forms on Reno’s lips.“So you had the security tapes sent to the tower” Marvelous.” Naria and Tobias shoot one another a look, both a little curious before looking back to Maverick. “Yes yes. I’ll be sure to have our security look it over. Thanks for the help.” Reno pauses in the conversation, the other end speaking before he continues, “Yes, I understand. I’m sure we’ll be in touch” yes? goodbye.” Reno quickly replaces the phone and directs his attention to Naria, “The security tapes from the Global Arena are being sent to the Tower. When we get to the airport, I need you to have a detail start reviewing them. I want every bit of footage of this ass who’s been after me.” Naria simply nods and returns to her book Every man has his flaws. Every man has his talents. To say that a man exists without on is to denounce his existence in the same breath. Maverick exists as any man on earth does with a multitude of flaws and even more talents. Maverick is a skilled business man, a master in his field, and a superb athlete. He’s been trained in the martial art Jeet Kun Do as well as in the many wrestling styles made perfect by the legends. Maverick has been trained in the arts surrounding a multitude of weaponry extending from fencing blades and sabers to bo’s and tonfa. As for his flaws? A proverbial drawback exists that has plagued the mind of the great Global warrior for much of the last three years. Maverick Reno suffers with the emotional guilt relating to the death of Alicia Pierce and her two children. Maverick suffers the mental anguish of having to watch his best friend plummet through madness, having survived a brutal assault that took his wife and children away from him. Why does plague Maverick so heavily? Because Maverick feels blame for this incident; he feels that the event occurred in an effort to strike at Maverick; that Maverick was the target in the first place. Over the past few months, Reno has deteriorated into a psychotic state known as schizophrenia. No, there aren’t two personalities within the same body, there are no voices; simply delusions. Type I Schizophrenia has turned what was once a morality driven individual who devoted his life to the people in his life; his friends, his family, his co-workers. Hell, he would have done anything for anyone at certain points of his life. As a reference? He once vacated a championship and handed it off to a child in the audience, stating that, “To be a champion is to be a person who has faced true turmoil. To be the best, you must prove to the world that you are the willing to stop at nothing to succeed. That is something that I have not done for all my life, I have suffered no great dismay as detrimental as to compare with this young child.” The child in which Reno spoke of was delivered to ringside by a nurse who pushed his wheelchair. It has turned this man into a proverbial monster. Reno is a success. He is the head of a billion-dollar organization, he is a former multiple-time World Champion, and has beaten former Global Champions. To say that Maverick Reno has earned his pay in this business would be to speak untruthfully. To say that Maverick Reno deserves sympathy, however, is to state incorrectly. Maverick, with a PhD in abnormal psychology, failed to spot his own plummet into the wonderful world of delusion. Maverick lives in a world of delusion, plagued by the problems that exist in reality. As reality builds up against his delusion, he sets out to keep his world as he sees it. He alters what is real to fit his delusion and in the process, becomes even more frightening and all the more powerful each and every day. Maverick Reno is not just a man living in a dream world. He is forcing the world that we all live in to become his delusion. He is forcing us to exist in a nightmare. The limousine stops finally and the three patrons in the rear rouse from their selected tasks. Reno reaches over Tobias and grabs the door handle and pushes the door open. Naria turns to look out the window as Reno slips from inside to close the door again. He clasps his hands behind his back. Maverick walks slowly away from the limousine, the scene panning out to show the area. Reno walks through the grassy plains, his eyes focusing on something in the distance. Maverick continues walking as the scene continues to pan outward, showing a small number of gravestones. Reno stops at one with an intricate ankh standing high above it. Reno kneels slowly and bows his head, his hand placing a pair of yellow roses on the ground. Reno whispers, his words inaudible. The scene pans and then zooms in on the markings, Reno’s whispers have turned silent as he clenches his eyes shut, paying respect.
