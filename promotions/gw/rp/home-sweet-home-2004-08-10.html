Home, sweet home.

10 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 10th, 2004. My eyes opened, cracking the sleep still attached at the corner of my eyes. My eyes had hardly began to focus in the dark room, which served as my residency. Glancing around it seemed as if everything about this room was wrong. Pulling my hands to my eyes I balled them into fists and sleepily rubbed at my eyes as I yawned widely before shaking my head and sending my messy black locks flying everywhere. Kicking off the silk sheets off this large four poster bed I rolled my legs from beneath the covers. Clad in only a pair of black boxers I gasped as my bare feet touched down upon the cold stone floor. Wait a second…I didn't have silk sheets, I didn't have a four poster bed…and I most certainly didn't have a stone floor! 'What the..?' I mumbled, awakened by this odd turn of events. This wasn't my apartment, my bed, or anything that seemed even remotely similar. I didn't recall going to bed in such a place either. In fact, if this room wasn't so dark it could almost be mistaken for a castle. 'A castle? That's ridiculous.' I silently told myself. My eyes refused to focus in this dark room, devoid of all light. Rubbing at my eyes once more I stood wearily to my feet with confusion taking over my emotions. My eyes may as well have been closed for the darkness rendered me blind. Reacting as any blind man would I began to feel around with my right hand. Next to the bed I felt a table…this was progress. My fingers continued on in their exploration of the fine Oaken table that stood next to my bed and upon that table they discovered something that seemed foreign to the touch…a piece of paper? No, it was more than just a piece of paper. It was very thin cardboard, in a triangular shape. My fingers batted at it before I pulled the piece of cardboard into my palm. Feeling the piece of cardboard I realized that there was something inside…matches! A matchbook, so that's what the piece of cardboard was. Opening the book of matches I tore one away and lit it, immediately light flooded the dark room and my bluish gray eyes were greeted with peculiar objects that danced beneath the light of this burning match. Stonewalls, a stone floor, an enormous four poster bed, silken sheets, ancient tapestries that decorated the walls with their strange crests and insignias. Finally on the wall I spotted something that seemed all too familiar, a cross. Only this was no normal cross, instead of the barren wooden cross it was a silver cross with Jesus still perched upon the cross. A Catholic cross? My eyebrows raised in curiosity as I studied this cross and it was at the bottom of that cross where I noticed that this cross was merely a highly decorated dagger. A cross…particularly a silver cross in the form of a dagger? It could mean only one thing, protection from vampires. 'Vampires.' I snorted, refusing to allow myself to believe in a creature that had merely been the brainstorm of Bram Stoker and his classic, Dracula. Sneering in contempt at the very thought of a vampiric creature I immediately dismissed the idea. I don't believe in vampires…what kind of fool would? Suddenly a sharp pain struck my fingers and I yelped in pain! 'Gahh!' Was all I could manage as I jerked my fingers back and tossed the burning match to the stone floor where it died out in a desperate flicker and a puff of smoke. I had been so consumed by discovering the room, which held me within its walls that I had forgotten completely about the match in my hand. Extracting another match from the book in my hand I struck the match against the corner of the bed and it immediately came to life with a flash of light and the stench of sulfur hanging in the air. Glancing about I spied a candlestick; complete with a white candle resting on the table juxtaposed with the four poster. Introducing the candlestick and my flaming friend I brought light about as the room brightened thanks to the candles flaming wick. Blowing out the match I dropped it on the stone floor and firmly grasped the candlestick in my right hand as I surveyed the room. The ceiling was high and identical to the walls and floor due to the heavy stone that had been placed there years ago. The room boasted only one small window, covered in curtains of crimson trimmed in gold so beautiful that it must be reserved only for royalty. Walking slowly across the stone floor I threw back the curtain to reveal a small opening protected by vertical steel bars. Suddenly it seemed as if I had stepped back into the medieval ages. Staring out into the land beyond the window I realized that it was night and the stars danced throughout the sky, yet this pattern of stars seemed different from the stars that I had observed on so many occasions. Scratching my head thoughtfully I turned a way from the window and began to explore the rest of my quarters, it was then I noticed a wooden stool at the foot of the four poster bed. Upon that stool sat neatly folded clothes. 'Are these for me?' I whispered to myself in a voice so silent it seemed as if I feared disturbing whatever ghost that haunted the premises. Answering my own question I sat the candlestick upon the stone floor and picked up the clothes. Slacks of the finest black silk and a matching silver tunic of the same material. Surely these were not for me…or were they? Holding these items against my body I realized that they were no doubt a perfect fit. With a bit of hesitation I slowly pulled the shirt over my head, shoving my arms through the ruffled sleeves. Stepping into the pants I buttoned them before dropping onto the bed that sat before me. In a seated position I adorned black silken socks before tugging on a pair of leather shoes. These leather shoes had a silver buckle in the front and I could only picture these shoes as something that had been worn hundreds of years ago. These clothes were all a perfect fit, yet none of this made sense…what had happened to me? Had I suddenly been transported back in time, doomed to live life in the past? Where did these clothes come from? Why did they fit me? Where was I? How could I have ended up here, and why did I have such an excruciating headache? Putting these rhetorical questions in the back of my mind I felt around my throat searching for the cross necklace that never left my possession. As skin met skin I felt the cross necklace between my thumb and index fingers. Raising it to my lips I kissed it softly and prayerfully before letting it drop back to my neck as I reached down for the candlestick. Raising the candle aloft my heels clicked upon the stone as I walked away from the bed and towards the large door carved into the wall. Not even bothering to heed caution I reached for the knob and turned it as the solid door slowly creaked open upon aged hinges. The noise of this was loud enough to alert anyone of my presence, yet did that matter? In fact I could very well be alone in this strange place. With a lump of emotion in my throat and my inner radar going off like an alarm clock I stepped out onto the stone floor of the long hallway that sat before me. Closing the door behind me I grimaced as the aged hinges once again creaked in protest. My eyes stared off into oblivion, as the hall seemed to be without end. Numerous tapestries and coats of armor decorated the hall of this strange castle, and it only made my mind run faster as it's investigation of how and why I arrived here came up with no conclusion. Arching an eyebrow in frustration my heels clicked against the stones as my pace quickened, making my way down the hall I passed numerous doors. Each door sat in the wall with a door parallel too it. The doors were all identical, solid oak with jutting hinges. Finally after what seemed to be a thousand steps but in reality was no where even close to that mark, I reached the end of this hall where a spiral staircase loomed ominously before me. Proceeding with caution I transferred the candle to my left hand as I balled my right hand into a fist. My steps were steady and hesitant as I began to climb the stairs. Click, click, click, click, was the sound emitting from my heels, warning any and all bystanders of my presence. The further I climbed these stairs, the more I realized what this place was like. It wasn't a castle as I had expected but rather an old inn! An inn that had seemingly been unoccupied for some time, yet it held it's own against its battle with age and Mother Nature. Still in good shape this inn was dark and dreary yet held a strangely enchanting element about it, that contrasted with the evil nature that seemed to surround this inn. I continued up the stairs and without warning I was plunged into immediate darkness. I glanced down at the candle in my hand yet I was met with nothing but darkness as black with sin as Lucifer's soul. Placing the candlestick upon the stairs I groaned aloud, frustrated with myself for not bringing along the book of matches that I had discovered. Guiding myself with my hands stretched forth I continued to climb the stairs, oblivious to my destination and baffled by my journey. Such confusion seemed to consume me, as it became my primary emotion. My muscles had drawn tight with anxiety and my body had coiled as tightly as any spring a hint of panic rested at the back of my mind and a heavy dose of curiosity took charge at the forefront. My eyes seemed to have been closed as the darkness blocked all light from entering my orbs, yet as I rounded the spiral staircase I saw a flicker of light! With renewed curiosity and the voice of common sense screaming in my ear to run the other way I charged up the stairs at a faster pace. Rounding the staircase I entered what could only be the attic of this ancient inn. The walls bowed towards one another to form a low, sloping 'A', those walls weren't stone as the ones below were, but they were made of wood, as was the floor. Three candles illuminated the room. A candle on the left side which cast its beam throughout, a candle on the right side which provided sufficient light and finally a candle in the middle where the flame danced across the candle itself. The candle in the center brought one figure to life…the shadow man. The aging man sat there at a small wooden table, perched upon a stool. In his hands he held cards to predict the future and his cold blue eyes studied these cards deeply. He never even bothered to look up and acknowledge my presence as he stared intensely into the cards he held in his hand. He was dressed in his typical hooded cloak and his elderly face seemed decorated with even more wrinkles than usual today. The corners of his thin lips turned downward into a cruel frown. With a thousand questions flooding my mind, my inquisition was the first thing that escaped from my lips. 'Where am I?' I asked in bewilderment. The frown upon his face made the transformation into an equally cruel and evil smile as he hissed a solitary word at me. 'Home…'
