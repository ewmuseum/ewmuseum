Wake Up (Part I)

21 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.21.05 10.16.05 | The Bronx, New York | 10:16 PM 'Mindy?' From the darkness of her and her boyfriend's bedroom in the somewhat unkempt apartment, Mindy Mercer could hear Markus calling her name out in the dimly lit hallway. The silence emitted afterwards was all that could successfully project the mood in the air- and it wasn't good. 'Mindy?' It was almost a week following Global Wrestling's Night of Champions edition of Evolution, and the pain in Mindy's ribs still refused to subside. Until it did, however, it was safe to say she was also going to remain in a non-talkative mood; either that, or it would take the sting of the loss of her title going away to get her to speak. As it were, Mindy had no will to communicate with anyone but her cousin, and he was long gone by then; where to, no one knew but Michael himself. Forcing back tears that hadn't let up in what seemed like forever, Mindy curled up under a pile of blankets on her bed and lied there. She could only pray that fatigue would eventually do her in and force her sadness to wait another day to torment her, but if only there was more to numb the pain... she relied on staying in bed in hopes that the monsters would finally give up and allow her to return to her old, happy self, but even a naive little girl like her had to admit that such a plan could not- and would not- hold out forever. One could consider it unfortunate that Mindy would have to leave the confines of the room tomorrow either way for her match against Brick the next night, but it wasn't unfortunate- it was her job. 'Stupid wrestling... I should have never opened my big mouth.. Gods, why do I have to be so pathetic?' Mournfully, the former Global World Champion closed her eyes to try and think of something happy; bunnies, chocolate, life-sized cuddly teddy bears, Brad Pitt... but her innocent green eyes were forced open by the sight of her now deceased uncle, standing amidst the faeries and teddy bears and candy canes, telling her exactly what Mindy would have expected him to say: 'You can't dream forever, you stubborn little brat. Your life is just as miserable as anyone else's, don't ever forget it.' And she most certainly did not. At that moment, Mindy remembered the place she truly lived in. This wasn't New Mexico anymore; the beautiful, peaceful desert landscapes no longer comforted her... she was in a dark, messy bedroom in her boyfriend's apartment located in The Bronx, New York. Outside, the rain was as prominent as ever. Droplets of water pattered on the windowpane behind the bed, and they nearly were enough to kill the noise of her down-and-out boyfriend who remained in the hallway. He stood just opposite of the door to his very own room, defiant, but kept out by the fear of getting his future wife riled up even more. All he could do was knock on the door some more, hoping that his persistence didn't catch her bad side. 'Mindy?' Mindy's ears perked just a little bit, if only for the fact that she wanted to be sure that her boyfriend was really calling for her and that it wasn't the wind outside tricking her into thinking that someone still cared for her. There was no trick to it, though: there was actually someone outside that door who begged for her permission to let him in. All Markus wanted was one chance to hold his girlfriend tight, to be able to tell her not to beat herself up over the little things... that all that mattered was them, and nothing else. As long as they could be there for eachother, they just had to be okay. However, all that crying was making Mindy tired, and rest was beginning to grow on her mind. 'I'll just... rest my eyes for a minute.' From there, it shouldn't have taken long for her to drift into what would hopefully be a peaceful dream. Instead, a harmless voice impeded Mindy's chance at rest. 'Come on, Mindy, I know you can hear me.. so what if you lost that title? It's not like it's the end of the world.. you had a nice run with it, and you're probably due to get a rematch in the near future. It's no big deal... did you even read the letter you got the other day yet? The one from HSW Headquarters that I've been asking you to read?' Sharply, Markus could hear his girlfriend's voice hit his ears like a hammer hit to the skull. 'I could care less about HSW and I could care less about that stupid title! It was too heavy for me anyway...' By that, only Mindy knew the true meaning behind what she said. It wasn't the title that was so heavy, as much as it was the importance of it, and how much that importance had whittled down Mindy's priorities to two things: the title, and defending the title. With a sigh, Markus knew all too well of the only other thing that could possibly have Mindy so upset. He took a deep breath, and drew in the strength to find his voice one more time. 'Listen, I know Michael's gone. I know you miss your family, and it hurts. I only wish I cared so much about my family. The fact of the matter is, however, that your cousin is a grown man, and he's able to take care of his own problems. I said it once, I'll say it for you right now- he's going to be fine, okay?' That time he had to strain his ears to hear anything on the other side of the door, but he could hear a whimper eminate from inside his bedroom. 'Words aren't good enough.' Strike two. Fortunately for Mindy, though, she had a boyfriend who never liked quitting on people he cared for. Frantically, Markus ran into the kitchen and threw open the refridgerator door, hands filing past half-filled (yes, through the black clothes and pale complexion, Markus was an optimist) beer bottles in desperation. 'Corona, Miller, Budweiser, Zima... how the fuck did a Zima get in here?' In bug-eyed horror, Markus took the beer from Hell and promptly disposed of it in the best way one would in that neighborhood: he opened the window above the kitchen counter and chucked it out, letting it fall three stories to the sidewalk below. 'Now, where was I?' Back Markus went to looking for what he wanted. He hadn't drank much other than beer in the past couple of weeks, he wasn't so sure he even had anything else inside. 'God damnit,' he muttered, 'there's gotta be something in here... ah hah.' With a knowing smirk, Markus returned to the closed bedroom door once more, his right hand holding exactly what he hoped would get Mindy to show herself. 'Please, hun, just come out. You can't keep yourself in there forever, you know.' He still had a faint hope that perhaps mere comfort and consolement would get Mindy to open the door, but that method was as useless as before. Sighing, Markus went to his last resort. 'Besides, babe, I was going to have a beer as usual, but why get drunk when there's a can of Pepsi in the fridge? One last can of Pepsi.' He stated those last few words firmly, tauntingly. And to Markus' triumph, it was able to inspire another noise from the bedroom. 'Huh?' Markus' smile shifted into a grin as he could distinctly hear the sounds of slipper-clad feet shuffling to the door. Not a second after that, ka-schink went the lock, and the doorknob rattled. The door opened, a tiny hand poked out, found the soda can Markus held, snatched it, and withdrew into the bedroom. The door closed, and Markus tilted his head. 'What the fungus?' In defeat, Markus knocked on the door some more. 'Can I come in, honey, pleeeease?' Markus could hear the soda can being popped open, followed by his girlfriend's somber voice. 'Why?' 'Because you need a hug,' Markus answered nonchalantly. 'No I don't.' 'Yes, you do.' 'No I don't...' 'Yes you do!' Oh, how the tables had turned. If only Markus knew how incredibly Mindy-like he sounded, offering his cuddling services to his girlfriend in her time to need; only for Mindy to be the one to push away. The irony stung like a hornet sting to the face. 'I don't need a hug,' Mindy cried, 'I need my mommy.' 'Now don't go that far,' Markus sighed in response, still tapping on the door in half-heartedness. It was obvious that Mindy didn't want to be bothered and even he had to admit that. But yet... he couldn't let himself hear his love sound so stressed out. 'You've been through worse times in your life before, why go back to saying things like that? Isn't my willingness to help enough?' Another deep breath. As big and strong as Markus appeared on the outside, he needed all the strength in the world and more to say what he needed to say to someone a foot smaller than him. 'Don't you care at all that I'm here to help?' No answer; exactly what the six and a half feet tall Markus Riley had expected to hear. Nearly a year of trying to fix things with Mindy had led up to her locking herself in Markus' own bedroom. As much as she loved him, she couldn't take it having to look at her boyfriend with tears in her eyes. She'd dealt with that before, and knew how much it hurt her to know that she was letting Markus hurt himself to see her crying. And it was that mindset, that Markus could no longer take. 'Look. I know you, and I know that as childlike as you are, you have a side of you that is strong and determined. It's okay to be childish and to only want to have fun.. shit, I know how that is. But if you're going to let all of this get to you and not bother to take one step out of that room, then think about this for me. Will you react this way if something bad happens to me.. if something bad happens to your cousin? Or would you keep going, and try your damndest to make everything right?' By the end of his little speech, Markus had sank to his knees, shifting to lean his back against the door. Perhaps exhaustion was getting to him as well. After a few moments of contemplation, the man nodded. 'Well, I guess I'm sleeping on the couch again.' Markus remained on the floor for just a second longer, trying to push his laziness aside so he could stand himself up. Instead, the door he had been leaning against had gave, and the large man tumbled backwards. 'Ugh...' Glancing upwards, however, Markus realized that the door didn't give- it was opened, and the twenty year old caffiene fiend stood over him. Her eyes were still somewhat red from all of the crying she had been doing, and she more or less seemed quite far from being the happy girl that Markus had grown so used to up until the last two weeks. Oh well. In any case, it was a start. 'Somehow, I knew that was going to happen,' Markus grumbled, rolling onto his stomach so he could get to his feet. Mindy couldn't help but at least smile at the sight of her boyfriend lying on the ground. Once he stood up, it took one calm gaze into Mindy's eyes for him to feel better about the whole situation. 'I missed that.' The sugarholic gave Markus a confused glance, rubbing the last bit of moisture from under her eyes. 'Missed what?' 'That smile of yours.' So cheap. So cheap, yet it worked like a charm. It worked so well in fact, that he could've sworn Mindy had to stop herself from letting out a giddy little giggle. 'I try. Do I still look like I need a hug?' Markus chuckled, growing more and more comforted at the feeling of tension beginning to go away. 'No... but I think I need to hug you anyway, just to be sure of myself.' Mindy could feel her cheeks turn a faint shade of red but she no longer cared, allowing herself to be gently squeezed in her boyfriend's arms. She sighed dreamily, before resting her head on Markus' chest. 'I'm sorry. I shouldn't have tried keeping you from me. You just wanted to help.' Gently running a finger through his lover's pink and black hair, Markus nodded. 'I still do.' 'Would you come to Evolution with me tomorrow?' Mindy requested bashfully, 'I don't want to be there alone.' Always the voice of reassurance, Markus was quick to accept the subtle invitation made. 'As long as it makes you feel better, I'll do anything.' 10.17.05 | Las Vegas, Nevada | 11:09 PM It all happened so quick, that Mindy had little time to defend herself. Between being caught off guard and her ribs still bothering her from last week's match, there was little the former World champion could do when her boyfriend had his blood spilled at the hands of a chair wielding maniac that she didn't even know. Bobby Brawler was a Hall of Famer in most eyes of Global Wrestling, but to Mindy, she only saw a crazy man who wanted nothing more than to hurt people. Thinking about it, maybe she should have saw that coming with someone who had the last name 'Brawler'. As she tried to think, however, she was rudely interrupted by the running spear that connected wholly with the same beaten up body that had been put through the ringer seven days before, and had the timing to happen just five minutes before Mindy had to be back up on her feet for another match. Ouch. So much for the chances of things improving that night. There was nothing Mindy could do as Brawler stepped out of the locker room with a sinister laugh signalling his departure. Mindy coughed and forced herself to crawl to her fallen boyfriend just feet away from her. Her lungs ached for air, but with each breath she took generated more and more pain in her stomach. Of all the times this had to happen... Still, Mindy tried to fight Brick that night. It was a futile attempt and it showed, but she couldn't let herself succumb to pain anymore. She had promised Markus she wouldn't sit back and cry like that again, and although that didn't change the fact that she lost, Mindy cared not about the match, but about what would happen afterwards. 'Where's Markus?' Mindy asked, wincing in pain as she wrapped her arms around her ribs. The man she was speaking to had seen the fight that broke out between Bobby Brawler and Markus, and had pointed the girl down the hall toward the trainer's room. 'Thanks.' From all the way down the hall Mindy could hear her boyfriend's voice, yelling in pain from the shots he sustained; the last thing she would have thought to hear would have been Markus in pain, but again, it all happened so fast that she could only hear the blast of the chair hitting his head before she, too, was on the ground. When she opened the door to where Markus was being taken care of, it was worse than she thought. Although he had been cleaned up, blood still trickled down his forehead through the bandage he donned. It was much more damage than what would be expected from a single chair shot, that was for sure. Upon further examination, however, Mindy also noticed what seemed to be burn marks on his face. 'Oh my God...' Markus then saw his girlfriend standing in the doorway, a look of obvious concern in her green eyes. He tried to stand up and walk over to her, wanting to prove that it wasn't as bad as she thought, but the EMT members working on him wouldn't have it. 'Fucker..' Markus murmured through gritted teeth, 'fucker and his fucking C4 chair. Let me find him and show him how to fight without any weapons, we'll see how he does... ouch, fuck! Shit like this really ruffles my potatoes.' 'Markus..' Although Mindy knew the answer to the question she had kept in her mind, she figured she'd ask anyway. 'Is he going to be okay?' Calmly, one of the EMTs turned to face the inquisitive girl, nodding his head. 'We just want to get him to a hospital and stitch him up. He was lucky.. it could've been much worse than it was, as is usually the case in this business. I'm sure you already knew that, though, right?' Frowning slightly, Mindy nodded in agreement. 'Yeah.' 'I'll be fine, babe,' Markus said, 'just head home and get some rest. I'll be there in the morning.' Slowly but surely, a glimmer of hope returned to Mindy's eyes. 'Promise?' 'Promise.' Although words weren't enough for her one night before, Markus' promise to her was enough to calm Mindy's nerves. She ran up to the large man and kissed him on the cheek, before leaning over to whisper into his ear. 'Seeya tomorrow then, hunny bunny.' Mindy kept the look of hope on her face until she left the room. After that, the look transferred into one of annoyance. 'I'm going to get someone back for this.' 10.18.05 | The Bronx, New York | 8:01 AM A tired Mindy Mercer walked into the third floor apartment of her boyfriend's, quietly closing the door behind her so she didn't aggravate any next door neighbors. The first thing she wanted to do was get some sleep- no, hurting Bobby Brawler took more precedence. Well, maybe if she had to make a list of top things Mindy wanted to do, the list would look as follows: 5.) Go on a sugar binge, taking in any source of caffiene to be had inside the apartment. 4.) Take a hot bubble bath, relaxing her muscles and preparing herself to be in good condition for the upcoming Global Pay Per View 'Dark Rituals'. 3.) Wait for Markus to return home, and have a nice, peaceful cuddling session. 2.) Try to get in contact with her cousin Michael. 1.) Beat the living christfuck out of Bobby Brawler. Something told Mindy, however, that she had left out one important part. What it exactly was, though, was what bothered her; she couldn't remember. With the last bit of adrenaline (or sugar, Mindy was too tired to tell at that point) left in her body, the caffiene fiend ran around the apartment in hopes of finding what it was that she needed to do. '...Oooooh.' Mindy could practically hear the flashbulb turn on inside of her head as she rushed over to the dining room table and looked at the mail cluttered atop of it. 'Bill. Bill. Junk. Bill. Here we are, HSW.' Tearing open the envelope, Mindy then yanked the letter out of its resting place and began to read, disregarding another piece of paper from the envelope that took an effortless glide to the floor. 'We at HSW are aware of your work, you have a bright future, yadda yadda yadda, we'd like you to help kick off our new show with a bang, etcetera.. and would hope you sign our contract agreeing that you will have a match with Jonny Kae on the twentyfirst of October.' Slowly, Mindy placed the letter back on the table. 'Contract?' It was then that Mindy spotted the piece of paper at her feet. She squatted down painfully thanks to her ribs, and picked up the paper in question. It was indeed a contract. Mindy wished that it had Bobby Brawler's name on there instead of Jonny Kae, but it was still a contract. Well, if she couldn't take her immediate frustration out on Bobby Brawler, there wasn't much wrong with finding a substitute for the person she hated, if at least for a little while. If anything, she'd at least get to beat something up. That was all that mattered, right?
