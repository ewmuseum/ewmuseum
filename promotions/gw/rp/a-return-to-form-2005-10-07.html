A Return to Form

7 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 7th How long had it been? How long had it been since the world last witnessed violence performed with such sadistic glee and gusto? How long had the killing grounds ran dry, the sacrificial lambs allowed to grow fat and comfortable? Not long enough, perhaps. For whatever reason that monster lives is perhaps the same reason he returns time and time again to those places that would rather forget His presence. If only He'd stay gone. If only people would learn from the past. If only Bobby Brawler couldn't hurt people anymore. Oh, but he can. Oh, but he can. Vanished without a trace? That's what they said about Bobby Brawler, the man who collected accolades by creating carnage and pain, the man they called the Human Atrocity, the Inhuman Monster and sometimes the 'Extreme Legend'. They said he had gone away never to return again, just as 'they' always do. And yet, he emerged from the ashes a pheonix reborn. The same man who crushed living human beings for a living for seven long years of blood, sweat and tears, the same man who took the industry into the next level, brought Global Gold higher than it had ever been, single-handedly legitimized the Hardcore division, defeated THE Insane Cain, shredded flesh with nothing but broken glass and a dream... 'Bobby...' The most sadistic motherfu- 'Bobby...' Beyond the limits of human flesh, more than human a God among- 'Bobby, you have visitors.' Bright lights enter the safe room, Brawler flinches slightly and takes his head off the padded wall just long enough to consider the cameras. He shuts his eyes uncaring and looking almost serene in his straitjacket. 'Sir, we've been allowed to interview you before your release. The doctors have been gracious enough to give us this chance to get into the head of one of Global Wrestling's most famous Hall of Famers. Do you have anything to say about the rumors of your return?' asks a reporter. 'I never left...' he says, his eyes remaining closed. 'But, Global just found you this past week...it's been over two years since we've last heard from you.' 'Did it leave you with a despair traveling through your body like a disease? Did your lust for blood finally grow so wild you hunted me down like a whore to bare your burden? At what point did the name Bobby Brawler come to mean nothing more than a harlot of violence? I spent seven years giving the fans what they wanted to see, I broke men in the prime of their lives, took upon my body enough punishment for seven lifetimes much less SEVEN FUCKING YEARS. And now you have the audacity to come back to me and ask for more? You want more from the man you labeled a monster?' Brawler slowly rises to his feet, the restraints of his straitjacket coming undone. The cameraman jerks back mildly afraid of the off-kilter man before him. 'I checked myself into this place seeking a chance to learn what it was exactly I had turned into. The hype, the cameras, the things I said to those I would face and destroy inside that ring, was it all true? I spent two years confined inside this padded cell, of my own free will...trying to figure out what it all meant. The transformation into the creature that the darker recesses of my brain called 'Broken', the atrocities I committed in and out of the ring...the day I forever destroyed the innocence of a woman just to get into the mind of my opponent. I did some very horrible things in and out of James' house...I exposed so many to pain, unimaginable pain. I reached into my soul and found darkness, is that who I was? Am I really the kind of man who can tear apart families, destroy lives and cripple young men and women just to give a few people in a crowd a moment of entertainment? Am I really that much of a monster?' 'Yes.' He states matter of factly. 'It took me two years to realize it, a few seconds to accept it. I am what I am, what I always have been. Those that stand in my way would do well to to appreciate what exactly I'm capable of. There's no turning back now.' Suddenly the straitjacket drops to the floor, Brawler's massive physique now in full view. The cameraman and reporter clear a path as the intimidating specimen of strength and pain makes his way out of the room. His body, a roadmap of pain and suffering highlighted by fossil-like imprints of barbwire and long jagged scars. His mind, clear and free of the demons of his past. For those demons are simply part of him, what make him who he is.
