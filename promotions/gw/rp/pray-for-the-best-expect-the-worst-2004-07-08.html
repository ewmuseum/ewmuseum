Pray For The Best, Expect The Worst

8 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/8/04 Brrr? Brrr? Brrr? I struggle my cell phone out of my pocket, twisting my hips side to side, wiggling it out. I push myself up from the arm of the couch with one arm. Brrr? I grab it with two fingers and pull it out? Brrr? I flip the lid? “Sabbot.” I wipe a handful of sweat off of my forehead. “Just calling to give you your next assignment.” “Alright. Watcha got McCoy?” “Well, you already know that you are out of the Master Of The Mat tourn?” “Yeah, get to the point.” As you can tell, I’m still a little pissed about that whole ordeal. “And I know that you are angry that your partner didn’t do anything to help you out in that match.” “He tapped like a little bitch. He didn’t even do anything. He actually cost me the match. That’s okay, because I broke his friggen knee. I heard from Soma that the doctors said that I tore his ACL and his PCL. Which means that he won’t be wrestling for quite some time.” “I know, I know. I heard and he got what he deserved. I know what the Master Of The Mat means to you. I think you got the worst of the situation between you both’even afterwards.” “Yeah, there’s always next year, right?” “Yeah, right, next year.” I hope he realized that what I just said was the most sarcastic thing I’ve ever said. We both don’t say a word for a couple of seconds? “Jamester is stuck without a partner for the tournament.” “Sounds familiar.” “So I was thinking of pulling you in and having you take his place.” “Wait a second. Can you repeat that again?” “Well, its either I pull somebody who deserves to be in the tournament or a random person.” “Well, alright. Count me in.” I try not to sound excited, but I am. I am reborn in the tournament, revived once again in this life. This is another shot to win the Master of the Mat. And I know that Jamester is going to do his part, I’ve been watching him from locker room and he does his part. So that won’t even be a problem. Right now, I’m the happiest man in the world. I’ve only lost two matches since I’ve been back, about two months ago. One was against Josh, who is the reigning Heavyweight Champion and Partridge, who is the current International Champion. So I wasn’t really upset about that because we gave them both a run for their money. But last week, last week was a joke. Every single time I faced Partridge, he got the upper hand, but not the next time. I’ll make sure of it. Soma and I have been tagging for quite some time. We’ve defeated a lot of opponents. Especially the handicapped tag team match at Crossfire against The Kindred. That was one of the greatest matches of my career. “Alright, good. You and Jesse will be facing Brick and Deacon Holmes this week.” “Oh really?” I grin an evil grin. “Yeah, this is for the Master of the Mat.” “Trust me, I know. I’m not going to let history repeat itself.” “Good, that’s what I wanted to hear. Alright, take care.” “Sure thing bossman.” I shut my cell phone and don’t even try to put it back in my pocket. I just throw it on the sofa. My sofa. I yank back on a little lever on the side of the chair and my feet go flying in the air. I fold my fingers together and place them behind my head. I yawn’wanting you to know that I’m the most relaxed man in the whole entire world. You go about your life, working day to day. And me, hell, I work a couple days a week and I’m still making more then you. God? “err, Gosh I wish you can all enjoy some time to relax. I’m starting to get the hang again of this money making thing. Since I moved in with Soma, I don’t have to pay any expenses for anything whatsoever. I don’t have house bills, electric bills, phone bills, heat’I don’t have to take care of anything. Soma has everything and he is sharing it with me. I’ve been saving up my money and placing it in a local bank account. I haven’t been spending a penny of it because Soma covers everything I need’or want. Soon enough, I plan on checking out some places to stay at. I kind of just want to move out, nothing against Soma, but I feel like an 8 year old child living in another man’s home, or one of his homes. Now this, this is no easy task. I’m going to have to find a place big enough for material possessions. You know, things that I need and not want. Things like this comfy chair that I love so much. My comfy chair. Things like my king sized bed. I never knew they have a bed as big as this. My bed. Or the handmade quilt that was made by an old deprived woman in the late 1500s. My quilt. All of these things, these are things that Soma bought for me. And I’m definitely going to take them with me. I wonder what people will think when they see a giant albino Siberian tiger wondering around aimlessly. I shrug. I really don’t care. I’m Sage Sabbot and I do what I want. Perhaps this money and power is getting to me. What can I say, so little things to do in so much time. I yawn again, hopefully starting to piss you off. Don’t you wish you can kick up your feet during a lunch break in the comfort of your own home? Instead, you drive to the nearest fast food restaurant, grabbing a bite to eat. For me, I can have my food brought to me. Hell, I can have it spoon fed if I wished. Nothing can stop me from doing what I want. If I want to relax, I relax. And not one person can stop? Knock, Knock, Knock “me. “Dammit.” I whisper underneath my breath? “Come on in.” I hear the door knob shake back and forth. “It’s locked.” “great. I just out of the chair. My chair. And I walk over to the door and unlock it. I swing the door open and am greeted by the monster, Leinad Soma. (OOC: I spelled it right that time, ehh? Ehh? Ehh?...*gets knocked out by Marq*) He stands a couple inches taller then me and a lot heavier then me. “but I can still kick his ass. Except that one time he choked me out. And there’s that time when he kicked me in the ribcage. And’well, maybe I’ll get him someday. “Ahh, Sabbot. So nice to see you.” “Ahh, Soma. So nice of you to interrupt my afternoon nap.” He snickers and walks inside? “May I come in?” “Yeah sure, why not. You’re already in.” “Be happy I asked, you are living in my home.” Damn vampire always puts a foot in my mouth. He takes a seat in the comfortable black leather chair. My comfortable black leather chair. He crosses his legs and waves his hand for me to sit down on the couch in front of him. I walk over to the couch and take a seat. “Got some good news?” “McNasty got killed by midgets.” “Now, how foolish of you.” He looks around the room. “That hasn’t happened quite yet.” I nod. “I got a shot against Partridge this week for the belt.” “Oh, quite a belt as well. You know, I carried that belt when I defeated Tragic Hero right before I won the GW Heavyweight Championship, which was the same night I was inducted and became a Harvester.” Soma gives me a look. “THEE look! ??well yeah, I had it once’before?” I look around, awkwardly? ??yeah. Well, uhh, good luck. He kicked our asses once, beat me last week, I don’t want to hear that you are going to lose this thing. This is a big match, you already know this.” “Of course I do. This is one of the biggest matches in my career.” You see, Soma might own every single thing known to man. But the things he values the most have a meaning to it. Something that has sentimental value of any kind or have some respect in having. Soma cherishes his family like nobody else. He loves his son and the rest of his family. He would take a silver bullet for them all. He would never take any GW Championship for granted. He knows what it takes to have one and he will someday make a great champion. Hopefully it happens at Evolution. “Well my friend, I wish you luck of all kinds.” “Thanks, I appreciate it.” “I also got some good news.” “What’s that?” “Jamester’s partner dropped out of the Master of the Mat competition.” “So, why does that affect us?” “Easily, I got eliminated because of Angelus Sorrow. He tapped like a little bitch.” “I wonder what he’s doing now with a broken knee.” “Torn ACL, PCL, shattered kneecap, torn ligaments, ripped?” “I get the picture.” “Anyways, McCoy just called me and told me that he wants me to take the open spot.” “Go for it. Hopefully Jamester does his part.” “I have a feeling that he will.” “Yeah, me too. Did he say who you guys are facing?” I snicker and smirk? “You wanna guess?” “Somebody in The Echelon?” I nod my head. “I got Deacon.” “Good, here’s your chance. Your first opportunity to get some blood on your hands from them.” “Oh, trust me. Blood will be spilled and it will be pouring from the forehead of Deacon Holmes. I’m not too sure he knows what he is going up against. His best defense is to just not show up. I think I want to send a reminder to Reno and Zero at Evolution when I make Holmes beg for mercy at my hands. And at the end, I will raise my hand up in the air towards the heavens above and proclaim victory over such a pathetic individual.” “Shit, I’m starting to hear some of the old Sage Sabbot come out of you.” “You will see some of the old Sage Sabbot at Evolution. The ticking time bomb that’s just about to explode. The adrenaline rush that never quits. The best damn wrestler to step foot on this world. I’m bringing back some old school shit here against him. And if he doesn’t think he is in for a ride against the old Sabbot, he should ask his partner.” “Whose that?” “Brick. About a year ago, I faced Brick in a hellacious hell in a cell match for my GW title. It was a battle like no other. For every punch I gave, I received one. For every drop of sweat and blood that poured off of Brick’s face, the same happened to me.” “What happened?” “What else would have happened” Of course I won, I made Brick tap worse then Angelus Sorrow last week. I wrapped him in a crossface, wrenched back further then ever before and ended up breaking his arm and almost breaking his neck. The referee had to literally pull me off of him. That was one of the most despicable things that the Global Wrestling has ever seen? “until Evolution. I said it once, pray for the best, but expect the worst. And that’s what Deacon should be doing right now. Sitting beside his bed and giving thanks to everything that God has given him. He should pray for his sins, that they be forgiven and that his slate it wiped clean. I’m going to make what I did to Brick look like child’s play. Because when he steps into the ring with me at Evolution, he is signing his death will in blood. His own blood.” Strong Words From Such A Little Man He Has Said Them Before And Everything Came True Pray For The Best, Expect The Worst
