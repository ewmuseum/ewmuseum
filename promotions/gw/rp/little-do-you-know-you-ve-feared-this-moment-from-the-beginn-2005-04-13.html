Little Do You Know, You've Feared This Moment From The Beginning

13 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/13/05 I look towards the skies above, here lie the heavens. Now beginning to clear from the horrendous storm. Leaving puddles and the thick smell of downpour behind. Acid rain mixed with must. Hope with a scent of revival. You breath through your nose, the smell seems to replenish you. You breath through your mouth, the smell seems to suffocate. Oddly enough, you wish you can only breath through your nose. But you enjoy the discomfort. Like a blister on a toe, you feel like it is your duty to agitate. Even though it hurts, you want to draw blood. You want to pick that scab until it begins to scar. Scratch that bug bite until your eyes water. It is human nature to enjoy self-punishment. But if we can enjoy causing pain to ourselves, it would explode the brain’s frontal lobe of happiness and satisfaction while exploring the boundaries of punishment towards others. That.. that was my intention when Salaok Davis and I first created The Nevermore. It wasn’t a struggle for power or authority because quite frankly, we had it wherever we went. We were one of the most powerful underground cults on the globe. “An elite group to take into the next generation.” .. and I’ll never forget those words that Salaok spoke so many times. From then on, we’ve recruited members upon members. From Jay Vyper to Silent Wind. From “The Scarecrow” Jonathon Davis to Mark Kullypso. We reigned supreme. We called our elite and strongest members, the Harvesters. The Harvester of Souls. The Harvester of Pain. The Harvester of the Mind. Together, we held together like a family. A well oiled machine that manufactured anguish. From around the Globe, we took the form of something incredible. This group being formed would be known as The Nevermore. Members from America to China, we maintained strength in numbers. A brotherhood bound by a common goal, to watch others squirm in pain and to take the world to the next step. And then suddenly, an abrupt halt confronted the group. The death of Salaok’s wife and mother to us all, Lisha, was killed. And the entire thing, I predicted. I knew this was going to happen years before it took place. Don’t get me wrong, I wanted to stop it, but there was nothing I could do about it. Changing things in this sinful world can only make things worse. Ironically, it was Lisha’s death that tore us apart. .. And it will be her death, that will bring us back together. I look down towards the lawn below me. A simple shade of green grass stained with tears of the lost. I grasp the stem until the thorns protrude into my hand as a chill runs down my spine. Until my blood runs down the stem. That, that is what pain feels like and I love every second of it. I kiss the red petal and inhale its aroma. I then place it onto the grass before me and I let my mind wander as I find words to say.. “I miss you..” I huff a tad, choking back a tear. I feel his hand on my shoulder and I nod my head in agreement. “We miss you.” I rock on my heels and stand back to my feet. I turn around and face him.. my best friend in the entire world. My.. brother in many ways. Salaok Davis, father to Darrel, husband to Lisha, friend to us, enemy to many. I put my hand upon his shoulder and nod my head.. “It’s time.” He looks back at me, looking directly into my eyes. “The reformation of The Nevermore. There is no time like the present.” “Will we be strong?” I smile, hoping to settle the nervous look upon his face. “Stronger than ever.” Such a powerful man he is. The Global World Champion and he respects me more than anybody in the entire world. He truly is a man of dignity and honor. A man of valor and one that you never want on your bad side. I taught him well. He taught me well. He nods his head with a grin. I guide his shoulder and he turns around along with me and walks away from the past. And sometimes, its so difficult to walk away. But sometimes, what lies in the future can take your mind off of yesterday. “Durian, I never thought I’d see you again.” “Never have any doubts in life. I knew this day would come. A declaration to The Harvesters to stand back up and continue, that is why I’m here. So many have fallen and it seems that there wasn’t anybody to pick them up.” “None have fallen. Some.. some just gave up.” I shake my head.. “Harvesters don’t quit.” I look into Salaok’s eyes. “Harvesters are forever.” He pauses. “You see, there are leaders and there are followers. And then there’s The Nevermore. United and hell bent on destruction. I couldn’t sit back and see us crumble. We have to succeed once again. With you as the Global Heavyweight Champion, it makes things a bit easier. We need to claim as much as we can. Stop fighting within our walls. We have Derek Frost aboard and he is a mighty warrior. He does not bleed the same blood anymore, Salaok. He is not a Youngblood. He is one of us. Guidance, that is all he needs. And together, we will recreate a legacy for all of eternity to remember.” “What made you change your mind?” “.. I knew you wanted it more than anything ever. Together, you were happy. Divided, you weren’t. I came back to bring back the honor and the name of The Nevermore.” “But you swore you’d never return.” “Perhaps I miss it a little.. perhaps it sickens me to see everything the way it is.” We reach the limousine, I open the door as Salaok steps in. I look back towards the grave with a sigh. The poor man hasn’t cared for one things since Lisha passed on. But who can blame him. The man has anything he could ever want but what was most important to him, was his family. It is like taking oxygen from any other human being. You took the only thing that he needed for survival and put it in a coffin and buried it underground. I suppose that is a reason why I’m here. To help him take his mind off of things. Such a strong man, but even the strongest need a pat on the back. “To your home?” He asks me.. “I don’t have one.” “I haven’t seen you for five years, Durian. You have slept somewhere over these years. With the wealth you obtain, you have to have a home.” “Home is where the heart is. I don’t have a heart, therefore, I don’t have a home.” He nods his head. “Don’t worry, we’ll find you one.” “A heart?” “.. a home.” He points toward the limousine driver and the driver raises his hand and lowers it. “Our home.” He says in the calmest voice ever. Without thinking, I knew what he meant. A new home in The Nevermore. His home. My home. Our home. As the driver presses the gas pedal, dust and dirt trail behind us. Rocks kick up from the ground. And a new leaf has been turned over. This is The Nevermore that you remembered.. .. this is The Nevermore that you’ll never forget.
