Contender (Feat. BJP)

Date: 7-30-03 There's a shadow just behind me, shrouding every breath I take, making every promise empty, pointing every finger at me. Waiting like a stalking butler who upon the finger rests. Murder now the path called 'must we' just before the son has come. Jesus, won't you fucking whistle something but the past and done? Why can't we not be sober? I just want to start this over. Why can't we drink forever. I just want to start things over. On my quest to become Master of the Mat once again there is many obstacles I must over come’one being my lack of motivation, two being taking on my stable mates in matches, and three being trying to stay sober long enough to make it all happen again, but I assure you the magic is back’The Righteous Superstar is back. My quest to become the best in the federation once again. GW has become littered with young cocky punks and never will be’s filled with piss and vinegar. Myself and BJP are here to stop that GW belongs to us’If anyone has a right to be cocky its us’because why? Because we can back it up we are the cornerstones of Global Wrestling we are the pinnacle we are what you want dream of being, we are Youngblood we are the future. Myself and BJP have had more world title reigns than the whole current roster put together. Am I boasting? Yes I am, because im Jay Robson I’ve earned my right to do that unlike The Upper Echelon who couldn’t fight their way out of a wet paper sack, and I will prove that once I get my hands on that two timing wanna be gangsta Pricey Poo’Ok well since we are on the subject of Sean Price, let me tell you a little story about him. One day way back when, Mr. Price came to me for help’because why? Because he wanted to be good and at the current time he was teaming with his brother Luke who didn’t have any business in this federation. Well..I thought about it for a while and I saw a little bit of myself in Sean Price, cocky, talented, and egotistical, all the characteristics of myself, so I was like ok Sean I will tell you what, how about I resurrect The Mile High Club? Sean was all for that so that’s what we done, I took the fed over once again with help of Dynasty, Ace Cash and Sean Price’The MHC was on the brink of becoming one of the greatest stables ever formed in GW’but what happened? The rookie Sean Price let all the fame and fortune I gave him go to his head, just like I did at one time in my career. Sean Price thought he was bigger than the MHC, Sean Price tried to take the reigns of the stable I built, but what happened? He bit off more than he could chew and it blew up in his face and thus the MHC is no more, but more importantly Sean Price will be no more after give him an ego check when the time comes. %%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%% **Pop **Pop **Pop The chain jingled and jangled from the wooden cross beam as I juked and jived my way around the black Everlast punching bag, my taped fists connected one after another as the bag swayed to and fro from the impact of my punches. My thighs were hurting my arms felt like spaghetti, but it didn’t matter because I blocked all the pain out as I danced around, my bare chest gleamed with a shiny coat of sweat in the hot gym, I was in my own little world, just me and the punching bag when I was so rudely interrupted. “Jay?” ? Hey Jay?” I heard the shouting voice echoing though the gym..it was BJP, I gave the bag one last hard punch and turned around to see coming through the door, black hair tied back in a pony tail, red YB shirt, black baggy jeans and cam in hand, I looked at him but I didn’t respond I reached down and picked up a white towel off the concrete floor and ruffled it though my golden blonde hair and over my face as I looked back towards him. “Hey man..whats up” What are you doing?” He said to me as he stopped the bag from swaying with his hand. I swung the towel over my shoulders and held it with both hands as I looked at him. “Are you ok mate” You look..you look?” Ben hesitated in his words as he looked me up and down with a confused look on his face. “Intense” He finally exclaimed as he put a goofy smirk on his face and nodded slightly “I haven’t seen you like this in a long time, you must really be wanting that MoTM Title” Ben said to me still nodding his head, I took in a deep breath of the hot gym air and look back at BJP “Na Ben’its not only that, I want to show everyone in the world that im no washed up has been in this biz, I want to show everyone im still in my prime, and that I am still a top contender in this fed” I said to him’my voice was crackly, Ben was the first person I have spoken to all day and I was wore out from working out. I cleared my throat and headed for the door as Ben followed closely behind “Hey that’s the TLM I like to see’but you know’im in the tournament to?” I placed my hands on the cold steel of the white double doors as I head his voice catch up with me, I pushed the door halfway open, sunlight beamed through the door and glistened off my sweating body, I turned around and shot a look at BJP with my baby blue eyes “Ben’I know, listen man’whatever happens out there is pure competition for the love of wrestling, you beat me..I won’t hold it against you, I beat you and you don’t hold it against me, we are bros till the end..you understand?” He understood me..I could see it in his eyes as I talked to him and gave the words from my heart. Ben blinked a few times and stared at me, an awkward silence passed before he spoke again “What” No im TLM im going to kick you arse blah blah’no smart arse remarks? Man..who are you? Your not the Jay Robson I know?” Ben replied sarcastically, he knew where I was coming from that was just his way of telling me that he knows. Ben bowed his chest and stepped up to me as I looked back at him with a straight face, he lifted his hand up to me chest high still staring me in the eyes, I lifted my taped hand and grasp his. A true moment of friendship in the hot gym, I nodded my hand and Ben nodded back as I let go and backed through the door as sun rays now engulfed me and BJP as we headed out the door “So whats the big plan tonite Jay” Gonna go home and do some push ups and eat raw eggs?” Ben said to me jokingly as the heat and the sun tortured my already hurting body “Hah..na man” I replied to Ben chuckling a bit as I opened the door of my Silver Camero SS, the heat of the handle burnt my hand as I pulled it open and looked over to Ben who has his cam up to his face video taping me “Then tell all the people what we are going to do” Always one to put a show on for an audience I put a cocky smirk on my face and looked in the camera “We’s gonna get fucked and fucked up! Wooooo” I shouted as I let out a much need cry of joy as I sat down on the hot black leather interior of my car, the heat seeped though my blue shorts and burned on my back but I knew it would end in a few seconds as I pushed the clutch in and fired up the 350 Vortec engine, the dual pipes let out a beautiful rumble as I tapped the accelerator and the rumble became louder “That’s what B’J’P likes to hear’that’s the Jay Robson I know” Ben exclaimed as he took a few steps back from my car still with cam to his face “Haha..yea man’hey I will give you a ring later on after I rest up a bit and then will will hit it up like two sailors at port” I said as I grabbed the blistering hot shifter and slid it into reverse and backed up into the street “Ok’mate I will be around”.” Ben said from the parking lot looking up from his cam and waving me a goodbye. I pushed the clutch in and shifted into first and popped the clutch, my Hoosier racing burned rubber on my chrome rims as I sped off into the setting sun leaving Ben in a fury of smoke
