Hunter 1 Prey 1

12 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/12/03 Silence. I stand there in silence on the road outside of the Dark horse Saloon till I look up and see what looks a 2002 Ford Mustang cherry red with Akodo Rokku driving slams into my body. It knocks me to the ground and I begin to try and get up but before I fully realize the situation I think I was hit with a Louisville slugger. Now I have gone through many pains in my long existence but being hit by a car then with a baseball bat has to be one of the most interesting. I stand, my clothes ragged now and I begin to laugh maniacally. “Well played! You doubled back over your own trail, making following your scent useless -- and then the car, and the bat. You might just be a worthy challenge after all! “I don't know what the hell you're talking about, you sick bastard. But shut the hell up and FIGHT ME! Here, I'll even make it easier for you!” With that, Rokku stretched his arm out and let the bat clatter to the ground. But I only laughed again. Slowly, I walked towards Rokku. 'You have absolutely no idea what you're dealing with, child.' Rokku scowled, his muscles tightening in anticipation of a fight. My lips split into an animalistic grin, and Rokku finally saw something that frightened him? fangs. My tongue darted from my mouth, licking blood off his face. For an instant, Rokku's resolve faltered as I grin widener. 'You're not ready for me yet,' Soma said plainly. 'If you fought me now you would surely perish -- and I when we fight in the eyes of the lord, I want you at your best.' Rokku froze solid, and for the first time in years, felt real fear. I just turned and walked away; I begin to walk off as I think to myself. “A car and a bat”.that’s brilliant and funny. It was like a cartoon’But that bastard tore my clothes?.he will pay. I keep walking as I hear footsteps behind me moving fast toward me. I stop and turn around to a female. This woman is average looking. She has long brown hair, with an average athletic build, light gray eyes, and a soothing voice. She runs up to me touching my chest and feeling my face. “Are you okay mister” I saw that guy attack you. Most people would have not been able to get up from such an attack.” “I am not most people.” “I see that. Could I be of some assistance in someway?” “Why yes’yes you could’How about you come with me?” “Ok.” “Splendid. Absolutely splendid. She follows me to a hotel about a mile down from the Dark Horse. The name of the hotel is The Goodnight Kiss Hotel and Resort. She looks up at the large hotel and smiles. We walk in and the clerk stops me. “Hello Mr. Soma, will you be staying with us tonight?” “Oh no Thomas’just stopping in for a quick bite’have the maids ready in about an hour or two.” “Yes sir.” “And Thomas?” “Yes sir?” ??send up some bottles of wine and some of the special.” “No problem sir.” We take the elevator up to the penthouse. The girl looks at me while we take the trip up. “Who are you, sir?” “I am Leinad Soma, president of the Soma Corporation and owner of this hotel and you are?” “My name is Lisa.” “Nice to meet you Lisa. Shall we continue this conversation in my room?” “Sure.” We make it up to the lavished penthouse.The Drop-dead gorgeous room featured new Belgian linens, down duvets; oversized, square European pillows; Godiva coffee products, Gilchrist & Soames bath accessories, and high- speed Internet access through the guest's television set. The 16th floor balcony, garnished with palm trees and sleek Adirondeck chairs, is the ultimate venue for rooftop retreats whether gathering for cocktails or seeking solitude in an over- stimulating city. I gesture to the bedroom and she walks in. I think to myself?.?Should I seduce her or just kill her?” She beckons me to the other room. When I get in there she is naked. So easy it is to get these women?.I need a challenge. She walks over to me and put her hands around my neck. “I grow tired of this’Let’s just do this?” She smiles and begins to kiss me and I pull her away from my lips then pull her close and begin to bite her throat. She begins to scream but I muffle the sounds with my hand. I feed on her, the sweet liquid flowing into my mouth like a fine wine. The woman tries to fight back but to no avail. Soon her body becomes silent and I drop it, as soon as I do there is a knock on the door. “Good timing.” I thought to myself. The maids walk in and begin cleaning the blood that’s all over the floor. “Its so good to have a hotel run by my servants”.” I say as I begin to smile the usual wicked smile. I pull out a cell phone and make a call. I go back downstairs and the limo is waiting on me. I get and head to the TUE headquarters. I finally arrive and get out of the limo and make my way in. Holmes is sitting there laughing at Zero. They both look to me as I sit down at the table. My clothes still torn up and now blood all over them too. “Man, what truck hit you?” Holmes asks. “No truck. It was a Mustang. And a Louisville Slugger, if I read it correctly before it hit my face.” “WHAT”!” both Holmes and Zero exclaim. “Yeah man. You heard me right’hit by a mustang and a baseball bat. I have to agree it was pretty funny.” “That’s not funny shit man. We’re gonna have to fuck a dude up.” Holmes says getting a little upset. “No, no, no our list has grown yes but I will handle this myself. You do realize who it was?” I say calmly. “Akodo Rokku.” They both say. “Indeed. I have plans for him’Believe me’It will be taken care of.” I say as we all sit there and there is silence.
