Poession Is Nine-Tenths Of The Law

13 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/13/05 After everything she’s been through over the last 4 years... you’d think she’d be used to this. The moon shimmered into a distortion as it’s reflection beamed back at the sky in the Sandusky Bay. The seagulls were perched atop every street lamp, to those who weren’t familiar with the creatures it seemed quite the scene, but to the locals it was an everyday occurrence. No one quite understood what made them do it... perhaps it was something about the street lights that attracted the seagulls to come. Julia Arcador-Starr could relate to that as she drove across the lengthy Sandusky Bay Bridge, surrounded by shades of midnight glistening underneath the raised pavement. Her entire life seemed to be a magnet for chaos, some would call her a glutton for punishment, others a walking bad luck charm. But for all the downs in her life... it never came out THAT bad. Sure she’d been nearly killed on several occasions, abandoned and abused as a child, and the target of nearly every demonic presence in the Western Hemisphere (thanks to Malgior)... but she escaped it all. With grace, fame, fortune, and a sense of fulfillment. You can’t say things turned out quite so bad for Julia, plus things had even seemed peaceful after the unforeseen closing of her beloved Super Athletes Wrestling Federation. She’d gotten back together with her husband, the one man she’d truly loved more than anyone in the world, and learned a lot from her deceitful ways. She knew it was the kind of lifestyle she never wanted to return to again, and for once in her life... she was happy. With the window down and the sound of “Not Listening” by Papa Roach resonating loudly over the bay, Julia’s light brown hair flapped in the breeze. Her face with a smile that some would compare to that of an angel, and her heart beating with excitement from the news that had been bestowed upon her just that evening. She couldn’t wait to get back to her Sandusky home to tell Billy, he’d be so happy... but the look on her face and even the way she held herself made it very hard to hide. Inside the 2005 Metallic Blue Ferarri F550, Julia wore a pink baby-t with the word “PRINCESS” written across it in silver with a pair of denim colored tight fitting jeans. Her hand maneuvered around the stick shirt as her foot pressed down hard on the accelerator, making the powerful engine roar to it’s fullest extent. She sang at the top of her lungs into the sky at her hearts content: “I'm not listening, not anymore! The more I hear, the more I ignore! I told you before, wont listen anymore! I told you before, lets settle the score! I told you before, wont listen anymore! I told you before, lets settle the score!!!” Julia sang joyfully before suddenly a huge pop broke her fake American Idol moment. She slammed on her brake with a loud screech as the car rocked violently. The vibration in the steering wheel became unbearable, sending the super sports car spinning out of control. Julia screamed as she saw her and her car screaming towards the steel barrier separating her from the depths of Lake Erie under her. The Ferarri finally came to a halt, jerking her forward and cracking her neck. Julia’s heartbeat raced as she looked up out the windshield and saw the nose of her car just millimeters away from the steel. The smell of burning rubber consumed the air around her as she took deep breaths trying to calm herself down. “Relax Jules... it was just a flat tire.” She told herself over and over again as she looked up into the night sky, looking for sanctuary from her fears, “All I have to do is change it... and go home...” Julia opened up the door and walked out onto the empty highway with a little shake in her step. She moved carefully along the back of the car, where she immediately noticed a shredded back right tire. Dropping down to one knee, she observed what was left of what was once a tire... but now just looked more like a disaster; or at least some kind of very odd abstract piece of artwork. “What in the world could do something like this?” Julia questioned as he got back up to her feet and looked back at smoking tire tracks that lead back into the darkness. With her head looking down to the concrete, Julia walked away from the handicapped Ferarri... trying to find out what could’ve possibly caused something like that to happen. Up ahead, a shimmer underneath a street lamp caught Julia’s attention as the strong breeze blew her long light brown hair across her face. Julia approached the object and went down to a knee... her green eyes grew wide as she held in her hand a Japanese style dagger. The metal blade shone under the lights as she ran her hand across it’s cold surface. Not even a scratch... there was no way she could’ve ran over it. Suddenly the screams of seagulls erupted over the bay, leaving their perches and flying away vigorously from the bridge. Julia shot up to her feet... heart racing and dagger firmly in her hand. “Whose there”!” Julia yelled across the bay. She surveyed her surroundings... the street lined with lights and outside, water as far as the eye can see. “I must be imagining things.” She told herself in her mind, hoping that this was all some kind of freak coincidence and it’d all go away if she just closed her eyes. It was worth a try anyway. With her eyes firmly shut Julia stood on the side of the highway, her arms wrapped tightly underneath her breasts and her back to the breeze. Suddenly... the chill turned into a warmth, and Julia felt a presence behind her. With deep breath consumed by fear, her lips quivered out an exhale before she turned around and lunged forward with the dagger. “...WHAT?” Julia said as she found herself on her knees, alone. Julia got up and spun around... dagger in hand. The adrenaline pumped through her veins and a rush came over her, like nothing she’d felt since her retirement from in-ring action after the closing of SAWF. It was just like old times... old times that were all but forgotten. A shadow cast across the ground in front of her, blocking the street light for the slightest of seconds. Her attention turned to the sky where she looked for something... anything moving that my explain her apparent loss of sanity. Just as sudden as the tire had blew, Julia found the instrument which caused accident flying out of her hand and skipping across the concrete. She held her hand in pain as she slowly turn around to face her nemesis. Their eyes met, Julia’s emerald green eyes narrowed as she looked back at her nemesis... surprised to see a woman with brilliant blue eyes. The woman was slightly taller than she, and had black shoulder length hair that blew slightly over her eyes, obstructing her vision just a bit. “Anata to kiteimasu. (You’re coming with us.)? Sada spoke in Japanese as suddenly Julia was grabbed from behind. Jules kicked and screamed trying to break free but it was to no avail, as a needle was stuck into the back of her neck. Her sight grew blurry and faded to black, before she laid limply in Nariko’s arms. ---- Can you see her standing there Well shes trying to find just anywhere Flowers in her hand, yes She doesn't know why Offered is advice to you And all you do was fake it And she's only yours tonight Oh she never cries But I know there's pain inside There's Julia ---- Dear Mr. William Masterson/Starr, We have something that belongs to you, but there is a price. Sincerely, An Interested Party The letter sat in the lap of William Masterson, better known to the public as Billy Starr as he looked down onto the desert below him. Thoughts of Julia ran threw his mind, he’d thought this period of his life was over and now it was time to relax and live a normal, simple life. He’d through wrong. The envelope which was sent to Billy was sealed with a silver Z, a symbol in which was unfamiliar to him. He’d expected such things to be done to him by the likes of Slick Wilson or even Purple Tongue... but from someone he didn’t even know? “What could the POSSIBLY due with the compound?” was a question Billy Starr constantly asked himself with anxiety as the stainless steel briefcase laid next to him with the requested document. Up again the window rested Nightmare Weaver... his trusted crystal staff which had brought him through so many incidents similar to this before. Although he worried deeply about his wife, he remained calm and collected. It was apparent that he’d been in so many situations like this before... the demon trials, his roasting at the hands of Jake Hix, his rafters fall by the hands of Julia’s brother Jedah Arcador, the test of his love for Julia by Xavier Black, and not to mention the constant pressure put upon him by Flash Redux and Slick Wilson throughout his Hall of Fame career. Starr looked down at the complex as the helicopter began to lower down for a landing next to the modern day castle. His cerulean blue eyes surveyed the complex which he hadn’t seen in well over a year. A tall stone tower stood triumphantly in the air on the west end, where he’d spent so much time training with Kinslayer. The sanctuary on the east end, where Silas Creel had snapped and destroyed everything Divine Light had built. Suddenly he awakened from his memories with a jolt, as the helicopter landed on the sand below. The Epitome of Xtreme opened up the side door and jumped out, with Nightmare Weaver and briefcase in hand. The wind of the helicopter blew about his long black hair, his cerulean blue eyes fixated on the entrance under a pair of mirror-lensed Oakley Sunglasses. A tight fitting white Structure shirt latched onto his 6’2? 222 pound frame, and a pair of bright blue nylon Kikpants with white stitching completed the outfit as he marched down the gravel path towards the complex. In the tower, he noticed a pretty blonde girl watching his every move through a pair of binoculars. He couldn’t remember the last time he was so sickened to see a beautiful woman checking him out. Billy Starr stood in front of the massive wooden door, beads of sweat dripping down his forehead under the mid-day burning desert sun. With a motion, Billy Starr took a step backwards and hit the door squarely with a savate kick. The door swung open and Starr jumped inside with Nightmare Weaver out waiting for battle. Much to his surprised, everything inside seemed peaceful. He looked around the newly black carpeted floors and the baige colored walls. In the distance, he could hear an organ playing what sounded to be “Moonlight Sonata”. Heading towards the sound... Billy Starr turned the corner and focused at the heavy wooden doors at the end of the hallway labeled “SANCTUARY”. Billy Starr stopped in front of the door with a deep breath, when suddenly the doors opened... and opening them were two men. One was large as a monster, nearly 7 foot tall and pure muscle, the other was a slender yet ripped African-American male... with cornrows. Both men wore black tightly fitting no-sleeve shirts and black jeans. The Vigilante of Virtue was surprised they weren’t ready for a fight, their arms extended welcoming towards the center of the room. The chords of “Moonlight Sonata” resonated around the cathedral, which remained intact from the last time he’d seen it... when they’d finally finished the rebuilding process after Crusher Creel wrecked the joint. The only thing different was a large crushed velvet black curtain that hung from the ceiling, blocking the immaculate stained glass. He could only assume what was behind that curtain as suddenly the music stopped. Billy Starr looked around the room, when suddenly a man up on the stage in front of the black curtain caught his attention. “Our guest of honor has arrived!” The man announced as he straightened his black almost trench-like suit jacket. The gold chains connecting the jacket shone under the skylights above. He stepped forth into the light, his cadet blue eyes staring deep... almost seemingly into the soul of Billy Starr. “Do you like what I’ve done with the place?” “It’s not yours to change, and I’m more interested in what you’ve done with my wife.” Billy Starr replied with great confidence. “We’ll get to that. First I want to make you an offer Mr. Masterson.” The man said as he approached Billy Starr, “My name is Christian Zenith, I own Zenith Corporation... the militaries largest manufacture of weaponry. I’ve spent the last couple months in Global, showing the world what true power really is... so you probably know who I am. Shadow is the lovely lady playing the organ over in the corner... she’s very beautiful, but just as deadly if you’re wondering. But that isn’t why we’re here is it” I am willing to offer you a million dollars cash for this place... it’s a very important purchase to me, I have big plans that I’m not willing to alter. If you refuse, I’ll be very disappointed.” “It’s not on the market...” Billy Starr said in response. “It’s a shame you won’t work with me the easy way.” Zenith said in response, as Demitri Miaskinov and Jason LeMaster shut the doors behind Billy Starr, locking them from the outside. “I don’t do things the easy way Christian...” Billy Starr replied as he prepared his crystal staff for battle. “It’s a shame your wife can’t say the same.” Christian replied cold and heartless. He could see the anger flow across Billy Starr’s face at the comment. Everything was going according to plan thus far, it’s amazing how easily the “great ones” are manipulated. Sheep they are... really. “You son of a bitch.” Billy Starr muttered before he charged as Christian Zenith with the staff. Suddenly, two female ninjas dropped from the ceiling and held Billy Starr, who squirmed violently to break free. Christian walked up to Billy Starr’s face and grabbed it with his hand. “I don’t think you understand...” Christian said before he let go of Billy Starr’s face and began to walk away as Nariko and Sada bound together Starr’s hands behind his back. “This place... this complex is already mine Starr, whether you like to admit it or not.
