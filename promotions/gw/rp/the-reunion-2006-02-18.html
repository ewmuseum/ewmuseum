The Reunion

18 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/18/06 The damp air in the abandoned warehouse made it seem like being in the middle of a tropical rainforest. The weather outside was a different story, a famous North Easter? storm was in its early stages and a light flurry had begun to fall on the already wet ground. The cold steel floor sent chills through Shaun Styles that woke him up and put his mind into frenzy. His eyes open wide, his mouth heavily dry. He tried to spit but all that came out was blood. As he wiped his mouth, it began to come back to him’Laproni, Tony, and Young had finally found him. After going into hiding for almost a year now he had been pinpointed. Shaun Styles lifted his head off the icy ground and looked around. Too many bad memories here. Not exactly the ideal position he wanted to be in, struggling to regain his composure. He felt his face?.it was heavily swollen with bruises and distinctively marked with cuts. It seems that Tony and Young had a little fun with him before dumping him in this landmark of sorts. As Shaun looked at the old, broken windows all the way atop the walls next to the ceiling, it began to come back to him. Flashes of his boxing match in this very complex went through his head. He saw J.T. Thunder lay a direct hook to his jaw, the canvas followed. It all seemed surreal or at the very least like it happened ages ago. But no’this was all too real. He vividly remembered the look on Laproni’s face as Styles landed on the mat, his head bouncing off. The sight in front of Shaun Styles was enough of a wakeup call for him. A very quiet gasp came out as he returned to a vertical base and saw a mysterious figure tied to a chair. Blood covered his face to the point of making him almost unrecognizable, the bandana tied around his face, covering his mouth didn’t help out too much either. Unfortunately, there’s nothing they could do to this man that’ll leave him unnoticed. It was Jade Taylor, Shaun knew that the second he laid eyes on him. You don’t forget your friends, especially the ones that you spent a good portion of your life with’at least a very important portion. Shaun Styles staggered over to him, limping heavily and dragging his feet. The snow was picking up, the broken windows allowed an opening for it to enter. Styles ripped the black covering over his face, seeing his friend for the first time in who knows how long. The year didn’t do much to change Taylor’s appearance, even with the blood all over him, he kept his baby-face looks. His wide, brown eyes were only half open and his lips were heavily cut up. “It’s been too long, hasn’t it?” Shaun Styles murmured to his partner while building up a faint smile. Jade Taylor wasn’t able to respond much. He just bobbed his head back and forth uncontrollably before letting out a loud and raspy cough, spurting out a heavy amount of blood. It splattered over the snowy floor giving it a much needed burgundy color. A style was going to work untying him as quickly as he could which he found to be quite slow. Taylor was finally able to get his hands free and the first thing he did was dig deep into his inner coat pocket, scrimmaging fierce looking for something of great importance. Success’He pulled out a pack of cigarettes and pulled the last one out of the pack before throwing the remains on the floor. “You got a light?” That voice was music to Shaun’s ears. He pulled out a lighter from his torn denim jeans, leaned in forward and slowly lit Taylor’s cigarette. Jade took in a deep drag before pulling it out of his mouth and letting the smoke escape slowly and thick, climbing up towards his face and gradually fading away the higher it got. “You know, those things will kill you.” Shaun said to his buddy trying to keep a calm exterior. On the inside he was breaking into little pieces. “Not as soon as you will.” Was the reply from Jade Taylor. Unfortunately he didn’t seem to be kidding around. Styles felt responsible for his close friend being in this position. This was something he should’ve dealt with on his own, even if the two of them got into it together. Jade wasn’t the one to lose the match’he just set it up. ??There’s not much I can say to that’I’m sorr----? Shaun Styles was interrupted by the loud sound of the warehouse door flying open. He caught a peak of the storm now raging on outside. The heavy winds pushed the snow in a daze of bright white lights. The almost black-and-white scenario seemed thrown off by Don Laproni’s red suit. He walked into the structure with a bundle of papers in his hand. His brisk pace got the point across; there was business to conduct. He threw the papers down towards Styles allowing it to slide towards his feet. “Congratulations’You got yourself a job’I expect to be paid very soon’with interest.” Laproni’s voice seemed to travel on the wind and hit Styles like a ton of bricks. His menacing character was exposed more than ever as he showed his teeth in a crooked smile. His long, greasy hair was slick back and his brown eyes held a tint of red in them. Maybe it was just Styles? imagination. No sooner as he came’he turned around and left the cold infrastructure. Shaun Styles bent down and picked up the stapled pack of papers. He looked over them, turning the pages quickly. The logo on the front said it all and if that didn’t do it the first two words definitely did. Global Wrestling Shaun Styles threw the contract in Jade Taylor’s lap. “How “bout it, buddy” Ready for one more run?”
