Waking up...

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/14/2003 Waking up... 'The Nomad' Donny J McNasty Global Wrestling Promo #878 Date: 2/14/2003 Location: Nomad's Apartment... (We begin inside the Nomad Donny J McNasty's apartment on an Early Saturday morning. We take a look around and see a lot of different things, from empty bottles of Guinness all over the place, to pictures of Notre Dame Football games on the wall. We peek into the Bedroom and we see the walls are all painted Dark Blue and Gold for the colors of Notre Dame. We see the Nomad in bed with a girl who obviously wasn't all that great looking at 7pm but at closing time was the hottest thing in the bar. The Nomad's sheets on his King size bed are shamrock green.) (The Nomad begins to wake up a little bit, he turns over to see who is in bed with him and he jumps a little bit. The Nomad then turns the other way as if he was facing the camera and has a look of worry on his face. He then finds his robe and puts it on, and walks out of the room. He picks up the phone and calls Hardcore Harv.) Nomad: Harv?....Are you awake?.....Listen man I have an ugly ass problem on my hands.......What do you mean you knew this was going to happen?.........You shit fucker why didn't you warn me........you did huh?.........Damn it Harv next time shoot the girl not me.......How the hell am I going to get this thing out of here......What's the number for animal control?........I'll tell you what I am going to do, I am going to wake her up and tell her she's got to go...I have something that came up....and while I'm at it, I will get a restraining order or something against her.....all right,I'll be down later,....yeah I'll let you know how things go....alright talk to you later.....bye... (The Nomad then hangs up the phone and walks back into the bedroom and begins to wake up the girl.) Nomad: Hey, I need you to wake up, I have to get going, I just talked to Harv and me and him need to get going, something came up with GW. (the girl now turns over and as you can see she's no SI Swimsuit model. She quickly gets out of bed and hurrys getting her clothes on. Once her clothes are on she finally leaves his bedroom and then tries to give the Nomad a kiss he turns his cheek to her and she leaves his apartment.) (One the Nomad knew she was gone, he goes back into his bedroom and sprays his bed with a can lysol and then has a sigh of relief) Nomad: What in the holy hell was I thinking?
