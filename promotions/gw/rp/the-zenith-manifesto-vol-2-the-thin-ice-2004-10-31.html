The Zenith Manifesto (Vol. 2): The Thin Ice

31 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/2004 Momma loves her baby And Daddy loves you too And the sea may look warm to you Babe And the sky may look blue Date: June 12, 1982 Time: 18:27 Location: Rammstein, Germany 'PUSH!!!!' A man screams across the sterilized hospital room. A white mask over his face hid his emotions, as many children as he has seen born in his life... everytime was a brand new experience. This one was no different, the woman before him had been in labor for nearly 12 hours. She screamed loudly in pain, but her scream was soon joined by another... the scream of a beautiful baby boy. All of 7 lbs, 6 oz laid in the doctor's arms as the cord was cut. The woman sighed and her head landed back on the pillow, as her husband clinched her hand tightly. 'Congratulations Mrs. Zenith, it's a baby boy.' The doctor said as he handed the little bundle to his mother. Wrapped tightly in blankets, he was still a little upset about the whole experience. She cradled him in her arms and rocked him back and forth, before looking up at her husband. With a smile he looked back at her, acknowledging everything she just went through for her baby boy. 'What should we name him?' She asked, her face simply glowing with pride. 'Christian... his name will be Christian Ulrich Zenith.' He said with satisfaction. It wasn't long before the boy quickly closed his eyes and fell asleep. After an overnight stay in the hospital, the family quickly rushed home, where Nikolas Dietrich was watching 3 year old Tera. Upon arrival that morning, Tera was up and about. She quickly ran to her father, who picked her up and spun her around. 'Tera, this is your baby brother, Christian.' Ulrich said with a smile. Tera looked down at her baby brother and back up at her dad with confusion. 'Why is he so small?' She said before looking back at little Christian, sleeping soundly in his mother's arms. 'Well, when babies are born... they are real small, and they grow up.' He said, with a small chuckle in his voice. Tera looked very inquitively at the baby, and then back up at her father. 'How fast?' She said, 'Will he be bigger than me?' 'Oh, he just might one day' Ulrich said with a smile, 'but not any time soon dear.' Ooooh Babe Ooooh Baby Blue Ooooh Babe 3 Years later... 'Christian!!!' Tera shouts across the German mansion. It echoes through the hallways, and the sound of footsteps could be heard as a little man came charging around the corner. His black hair was all combed forward and his cadet blue eyes shined under the house lights. He ran up to his sister with a smile... 'What?!' Christian squeeked back, wanting to know what possible reason Tera could have from driving him away from playing with his action figures. 'Wanna have a race? I'll give you a head start!' Tera said, with a big smile across her face, 'We'll race around the upstairs... we'll run over to that wall right there, touch it and run back and break this jumprope like a maraton runner! It'll be fun!' 'Okay... as long as I get a head-start!' Christian said, always competitive but knowing in the back of his mind he's probably going to lose. Christian runs back into position in front of the rope with his sister. She stood next to him and looked down, as he squatted down, looking at the far wall. The only thing between him and that wall, was 20 feet of wooden floor. 'Okay, as soon as you touch that wall, I'll start!' She said with a smile, 'Ready! Set! GO!' Christian took off with a small stumble but quickly got his balance back. He was very quick for a 3 year old boy, Tera knew that... but then again she was never planning on winning in the first place. Christian picked up speed and began to stop only in his socks. He slid into the wall and smacked it with his hand, second before Tera took off. Tera ran across the floor, at least 3 times the speed of her smaller brother. As Christian reached the halfway point back, Tera stopped and smacked the wall. She could see Christian in her sights, she began to run... then she stopped. Staring at Christian run across the floor, growing closer and closer to the jump rope. 'BREAK THROUGH THE RIBBON!' She screams as Christian holds his arms in the air, mimicing a marathon running as he approaches the finish line. Seconds before he realizes what is about to happen, he stops... but his socks keep him moving. His little eyes grow wide as the clothesline catches him right under the chin, clothslining him. He grabs his neck and slams the back of his head on the wooden floor. Tera chuckles from the other side of the room as little Christian screams out in pain. Tera walks towards her little brother, but sees her mom and dad running up the stairs and hides in her room. They run over to Christian, who is bawling on the ground. Tera peeks out her door and watches the scene... 'How come they don't come to me like that when I'm crying?' She mutters before she puts her head down and walks away. Deep in the back of her mind, she knew Christian was the favorite. Everything she tried to do to get their attention failed. That's all she ever wanted anyway, was to have attention and to be loved. It didn't even matter if it was bad attention... as long as she got it. If you should go skating On the thin ice of modern life Dragging behind you the silent reproach Of a million tear stained eyes 1 Year later... 'We're here!' Ulrich screamed from the front seat of their small car. Christian and Tera screamed in joy from the back of the car... bringing a smile to their parents' faces. When the car came to a stop, they bolted out of the car, bundled from head to toe as they tredged through the snow. They both had jackets, hoods over their heads and scarfs covering their faces. They ran over to the ice-covered lake, their parents followed, with ice skates in hand. The Zenith family puts the little ice skates over the little feet of their 4 year old son, and 7 year old daughter. Quickly Tera sprang up to her feet and began to skate on the ice. She skated in elaborate figure 8's and did quick spins. She was very proud of the way she was skating, and her parents were too... even though they thought her to be quite the show off. Christian wasn't far behind, stumbling at first being such a young age. It didn't take long for him to get the hang of things, he was very intelligent, especially for a small child. He would try to keep up with his sister, but she was much too fast... and her mechanical skills were obviously better. 'Mommy Look!' Tera screams, getting the attention of her mother, who looked on with a thermos full of hot chocolate. Tera leaped into the air and did a single spin. She stumbled a little bit on the landing, but stayed upright. Her parents clapping in approval as Christian watched on, it wasn't long before Tera looked back at her little brother. Christian's eyes shot quickly over to his parents... 'MOMMY MOMMY, WATCH ME!' Christian shouted, angering Tera a little bit, trying to steal her spotlight. Christian started skating faster and faster towards Tera, obviously attempting to do a spin as well. Like most siblings they had a bit of a rivalry, and Christian wasn't to be out done. Christian began to attempt the spin right by Tera... and suddenly bad thoughts crossed through her mind. Tera waited for Christian to be right by her before she stuck out her foot. Christian went to jump into the air, but struck Tera's foot before getting completely off the ground. Christian flew through the air, before smacking the ice hard. The ice shattered underneath the blow and Christian fell into the artic water. Ulrich sprinted towards his son, crying and strugging to keep afloat as Tera watched on with suspicion. A gurgle was added to the screams of Christian, he couldn't stay afloat any longer. His mother screamed from the other side of the lake in horror, she thought for sure she was going to lose her baby boy. Ulrich jumped into the icy cold water and grabbed his son. He swam valiantly to back to the snow banked edge of the river. Christian began coghing and such, and he was certainly going to catch pnemonia. Ulrich cradled his crying son in his arms, and shot a look up at Tera that she'd never forget. Little did he know, that's exactly what she wanted. 'I didn't mean to.' She said through fake tears, looking down at the ice. 'We'll talk about this when we get home, get in the car... NOW!' he shouted. Tera skated back towards the car with her head down, but as she looked down. A small smile crossed her childish face from cheek to cheek. She knew how to get the attention she wanted now, all she had to do was do something to their little pride and joy... and there was nothing she enjoyed more in life than being better than Christian.
