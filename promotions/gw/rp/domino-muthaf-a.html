Domino Muthaf*%^a!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday “I could see it everytime I closed my eyes. The gigantic foot of Quartz, a runaway train with worn brakes on a collision course with my face, and to make matters worse, sometimes I can still feel it. The big boot, the chokeslam, the thundering clothesline. All of these things play in surround sound on a perpetual schedule in my head. When I blink from the camera flashes that follow me wherever I go. When I lay my head down to rest after an exhausting day of training to remain the epitome of excellence. No matter what I do I can’t escape it. Now for some people this would be debilitating, resulting in lack of sleep, loss of self worth, a lesser man might even turn to alcohol and swallow it all wallow and drown in his sorrow, but not me. You wanna know why” I like it! Three words, Deacon Holmes wins, that simple little phrase makes everything worth it.” Tyler Chambers looks on, dumbfounded by Holmes? utter lack of respect for anything but winning. He sits pen and pad in hand at the head of a small oak coffee table that Holmes and CW are playing dominoes on. The two men trade off taking turns slamming the small rectangles down on the table. CW is dressed as usual in full 70's pimp attire complete with cigar. A translucent haze of smoke fills the den of Deacon Holmes? penthouse. Holmes grabs at his tank top and takes a whiff. “Damnit CW, this tha last time I let you smoke in here man. It smells like them cheap ass cigars you smoke in here for days after you leave.” Holmes exclaims. Chambers looks uneasy after CW responds to Holmes? complaint by blowing more smoke directly into his face. The two men look to be right on the verge of jumping down each other’s throats as Tyler breaks it up by changing the subject and spitting out another question. “So you’ve got your work cut out for you at Wrestlecade huh” Holmes stops cursing at CW in mid sentence and turns to face Chambers. “What did you just say?” he inquires. “I was just saying you’ve got your work cut out for you. You’re wrestling in two, possibly three of the matches on the card and they’re all against some pretty stiff competition.” Holmes cracks a slight grin out of the corner of his mouth and then bursts out in laughter. “Are you serious” I’ve had drinks stiffer than that, what’s the word? It’s right on the tip of my tongue..” Chambers answers back somewhat annoyed “competiton?” “What’s that” Never heard of it. Shit, have you even looked at who I’m facing? The Primestar title is as good as mine. “The Flamer” Steve Kincade and Roz Blayze... the family man. At least Kincade is smart enough to keep his washed up mouth shut, Blayze on the other hand isn’t quite as smart. He actually thinks he has a chance. What a joke, I hope he keeps his wife and those two dirty ass kids at home. It’d be a shame for them to have to see the their daddy get rolled out of the arena on a stretcher. He won’t even get a chance to be a has been like Kincade, he’ll be a never was. Chambers scribbles Holmes? comments onto his pad awestruck by his arrogance as he continues to gush about his grandeur. “But you know what Ty, those two should be happy. Although the hell that they’ll endure will do more harm than good to their careers, they’ll always have the honor of being beaten by me, the new face of GW. It’ll be like the bright light in their lives, like god shinin right outta heaven.” “You can’t be serious.” Chambers blurts out almost surprised that it came from his mouth. “Very serious, Deacon Holmes merchandise sales are monumental and my appeal is continental, I’m the only one around here with fans that aren’t related to him. Shit, I’m the reason anyone even watches Evolution and Over, I mean Deaconload. I blew breath in this fed, and I am the new face of Global Wrestling. It’s a simple mathematical formula, Deacon Holmes equals ratings. It’s just that easy. Everyone’s eyes here are on me, but not cause they wanna beat me, it’s cause they wanna be me. I’m the best thing goin, no need for discussion, Deacon Holmes is blowin up like spontaneous human combustion. In short, the Primestar title is in the bag.” Holmes returns his focus to the domino game while awaiting the next inquisition from Chambers. It finally comes in the form of “How do you feel about the possibility of having to face Leviathan again” And this time with a belt on the line.” Holmes slams his domino leaving only one in his hand, a double six. “I’m counting on it, and unless he’s gotten soft-er he’ll be there waiting for me in the Primestar finals. From then on though his night will be down hill, I’ve got a score to settle with him that no number of backstage brawls or match interruptions will even. At Wrestlecade I will attack him with no remorse, no repent, and most definitely no respect. I’m gonna give it like I live it till my blood sweat and bones ache. There’s a certain price that comes with blemishing my record, and at the pay- per- view he’ll find out he has no credit with the bank of Holmes when my foot forecloses on his ass.” “Well what about the cruiserweight contendership ladder match” Are you at all worried about that one?” asks Chambers. Following CW’s move Holmes slams down his final domino causing all the other to jump up slightly from the table. “Domino muthafucka!” he exclaims as CW angrily sweeps the remaining pieces off the table to the floor and walks out of the room. Holmes then turns his attention to Chambers. “Hold up, let me ask you a question first. Why” Why should I be worried? You’ve been here in my house now for quite some time and yet you still don’t seem to understand, I’m that shit that legends made of. I mean I’m sure all those clowns in the match with me are special in they clique but they ain’t nothin next to me. Pailinthorpe is the only one even opening his mouth about that match and when he does he’s extra careful not to let my name pass through it. He’s just an old man mumbling to be heard. As far as I’m concerned they might as well just let all those clowns stay home and book me in a match with Sage Sabbot at Wrestlecade. What’s the point in beating around the bush? I don’t understand why they don’t just cancel both the matches right now and declare me the winne... Chambers interjects with “Aren’t you entered in the tag tournament too” You certainly are keeping busy.” Holmes shoots him a dirty look for cutting him off and continues. “Yeah, I hope I end up without a partner though.” “What!”” shouts Chambers. “You heard me Ty, I hope I don’t have a partner in the tournament. I’m better by myself, I mean you can’t really improve on the epitome of excellence. I can handle any tag team that forms out of this whole thing all by myself, crushin” duos sittin waitin on tha next two.” Tyler finishes copying down Holmes? last few words before thanking him for the interview and leaving. Holmes begins to pick up the spilled dominoes. “CW! Time to get your ass whipped again!”
