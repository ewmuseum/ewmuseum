A Message Delivered

15 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 15th, 2004 **Behind the actions of every man is an even greater force provoking these actions, whether the force be human or spiritual various from case to case, but in any scenario, these powers to influence a man's behaviour are greater than any nuclear weapon or military core. A greater threat than any violent hostilities is an unstable man, powered by unstable emotions. When one become blinded by these emotions, only dark skies lie ahead as every action will not be targeted towards any one particular entity, but whatever unenviable entity stands in the path first.** - DOWNTOWN DALLAS, TEXAS - .:A familiar dwelling lies in front of us, the run-down dwelling of one Takahashi Jetsui, located in, to say the least, not the most eye-catching part of Dallas, in the downtown core. We see a tall, lanky man dressed in women's clothing approach the rusty, barely-there door of the dwelling. The drag queen man in a skanky looking, one piece rubber skirt and a feather scarf around his neck picks something from his teeth and adjusts his breasts before knocking on the door:. KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! .:After a couple of moments we hear and see the knob of the door jiggle, as there appears to be a struggle to get the door opened. A struggle to open the door ensues between the door itself and whoever is trying to open it on the inside, then suddenly:. WHAM! .:The door is almost sent off its hindges with a loud slam from the person on the inside, who, once the door creaks open, is revealed to the inhabitant of the gutter-hole, Takahashi Jetsui. The shirtless, face-painted man shows up to the door in a black pair of sweat pants and a cigarette clinched between his lips, but not yet lit. Takahashi rubs out his eyes, and then notices the tranvestite standing before him with bad make up, lipstick smeared almost everywhere but the lips, and drawn on, crusty eye lashes. In disillusion, Takahashi removes the cigarette from his lips:. TAKAHASHI: Is there something I may do for you? TRANSIENT (Very deep voice): Well don't you remember me silly? .:Takahashi eyes the cross dresser up and down a couple times before responding:. TAKAHASHI: I'm sorry, but you do not trigger any memories. Maybe the female clothing is throwing me off? TRANSIENT (Very deep voice): Ha, ha, ha, oh you. You don't remember back in Japan, honey? They used to call me Brian Dantrell, I used to train with you in the underground? TAKAHASHI: You? You were a member of the Hikawa Clan of Hiroshima? Forgive me, but if my thoughts serve me correctly, we in the army would be-head those who betrayed their given identity. TRANSIENT (Very deep voice): Brian Dantrell is my name. I fought alongside you in many battles darling, and celebrated with you many victories. After I left Japan, I discovered a new side to my character, and the massibe amounts of Japanese opium may have helped me out a little in my discovery. TAKAHASHI: Where's the mark? TRANSIENT (Very deep voice): The mark? TAKAHASHI: Anyone who has ever passed through the graces of the Clan has been branded with the Hikawa Mark of the Great River of Fire .:As Takahashi speaks of the mark, he points to a Japanese symbol on his own hip. After a few hesitant moments, the transvestite lifts up his skirt, and just above a dirty pair of women's underwear, and underneath a thick sheet of hair, lies the same mark that Takahashi was previously speaking of:. TRANSIENT (Very deep voice): Satisfied? TAKAHASHI: So you bare the mark, (pulls out a lighter, puts the cigarette in his mouth, and sparks the cigarette, then Takahashi releases a short puff of smoke) now what the hell is your business with me? .:The transient reaches into his cleavage, and retrieves a small folded up piece of paper, which he then hands over to Takahashi Jetsui. Takahashi takes the piece of paper and unfolds it:. TRANSIENT (Very deep voice): I haven't talked to anyone in the old Clan for a while now, so when I received a message from Master Goshi Wong, I was surprised. But then it turned out that they didn't want a thing to do with this little transvestite, they just thought I'd know where to find you, and since there aren't a whole lot of Japanese men with red face paint walking around Dallas, when I saw you from my corner one night, I decided to follow you to see where you're living. When I found out you were living in this shit hole, well sweet heart, I had to deliver a message from a few of our former comrades. TAKAHASHI: So I guess that will be all (takes a puff of his cigarette)? Because after all, I have some things I must tend to shortly. TRANSIENT (Very deep voice): He, he, you always were impatient Takahashi. .:Takahashi then steps out of his doorway, and grabs the fake, greasy hair of the transvestite, forcing the transient against the brick wall around the doorway face first. Takahashi pulls the transient's head back, revealing the Adam's apple visibly wedged somewhere in the transvesites Trachea:. TAKAHASHI: And as far as I'm concerned, you were always a bottom-feeding, disgusting excuse for a fighter. So here is my offer, you forget what you ever knew of, and found out about Takahashi Jetsui, and go back to the filthy streets you came from, or I add you to the list of rates and numbers dealing with the per annual homicides of trans-gender prostitutes. .:Takahashi takes a strong puff of his cigarette, and then clinches it between his middle and fore fingers of his right hand, slowly bringing it towards the throat of the trans-sexual man in his clutches:. TAKAHASHI: Do you comprehend what I am saying to you? TRANSIENT (Struggling deep voice): Y- yes! I- COUGH! Understand! .:Takahashi throws the trans-sexual man to the hot Dallas pavement, and then began hauling on the cigarette again. The transient grabs his throat as he gasps for air, and Takahashi re-enters his house. Once inside, under the grungy light of his damp-looking low-rise house, Takahashi unfolds the piece of paper given to him by the transvestite, and reads it. In English lettering, the note reads: IT'S BEEN A WHILE COMRADE WE ARE IN DALLAS, WE MUST MEET 1254 CONWAY LANE, TONIGHT 2 AM PLEASE MAKE AN APPEARANCE Takahashi glances over the note a couple of times before pulling his lighter out one more time, and egniting the small piece of paper, as allowing it to disintegrate quickly into the air:.
