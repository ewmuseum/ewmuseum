Blast from the Past (Part IV) Finale

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/09/06 Interlude: After a chaotic year, things seemed to be all just settling down for Shane, that was until last week when he was arrested after being in the wrong place at the wrong time. When Shane went to pick some money of his long time cousin, he realised that he could have possibly picked a worse time. As he was in the scene where Huey Lucio and his crime-fueled buddies liked to hide-out from the cops, aswell as storing fire arms and other illegal substances, the police burst through the door catchin' Shane Wethers with the group. Police quickly assumed that Shane was in on the deadly deeds that Huey and the gang had committed in the past. As of press time Shane is spendin' most of his time behind bars with a lawyer, trying to figure out a way for justice. The good news for fans is he has been given 'leave of abscence' from the jail so that he can compete in his match against Tyler Davidson this Thursday Night. Yet there are more important things on the mind of many people today, was Shane set up by Someone? if so, then who? was someone really following him? Many people are pointing their fingers at Shane's mischevious brother 'Silus' who caused him many problems earlier in Shane's career. So today we take you on a trip back in time, when Shane had been framed for an expired visa and had his ex-girlfriend kidnapped. He later was cleared off all charges from jail and re-captured his girlfriend, yet terror was still to come when he heard about the death of his grandfather. Who was responsible? Silus.....The same brother that was thought to had died earlier in his childhood by a street attack in the Wethers hometown of Leeds, England. Shane was shocked when Silus revealed that he was still alive on a video tape, sent to Shane to let him know that he was in the firing line, Silus was going to try to kill him. In this Flashback we take a look at the closing scene that shocked the wrestling world, still seen by many as one of the biggest hard-hitting stories of all time. Let's take a look at the closing chapter of ... 'Replacing the missing pieces'. *The street light shine’s brightly on the corner of a street in the town centre. Cars roll by as the voices of many conversations mix in the atmosphere, like a large public get together. People are on their way home from work or from where ever they have spent the day, looking forward to a good Friday night. There is a musty smell in the air, like a mixture of exhaust fumes, food from the local take-away and the silent smell of winter pine. Shane Wethers leans on the wall next to a public footpath, he watches people pass and looks to the faint horizon of the moon rising high. Shane is wearing dark blue jeans, white and fluorescent yellow trainers, and also along black leather trench coat. He also is wearing dark sunshades, as he usually wears to protect his eyes. Sometimes Shane’s eyes become very bright and unusual, sort of white like those of a creature of even a demon. It’s usually caused by anger, almost as if someone has set of a time bomb ticking inside of him and it won’t be long till he explodes, letting go of all the anger within. He looks at his hand, from the back of it to his palm, observing it like a small object. He feels uncomfortable with the microchip implanted in his hand, he feels like he’s been injected with a virus, with pure evil. Shane looks up and walks across the road. He keeps walking until he reaches the tarmac pathway leading to a regular sized building. There is a sign on the front of the door that reads “California District Hospital”. He continues walking again and walks up to the door and into the hospital. Shane walks over to the reception desk where there is a small, fat nurse sat filing her nails wearing a blue uniform. Shane leans over* Shane: I’m here to see a doctor. I can’t remember his name. *the nurse sighs? and opens a small red book. She looks up at Shane* Nurse: Doctor Hiddlepontildeon, Cociaoyo or Patterson? *Shane looks confused* Shane: Errrmm?..Patterson? *The Nurse nods her head and scribbles something down in her book.* Nurse: Are you sure? *Shane shrugs* Shane: I dunno. *the nurse then looks up at Shane* Nurse: Ok. It’s down the hall and your second right after the double doors. Shane: Thanks. *Shane walks off down the hall* ------------------------------------------------------ (meanwhile) Date: 23/11/01 Location: EW HQ *The room is faintly lit but is well secured. Cecil Cassidy sits at his desk on the leather seat. His face can not be seen because his desk is in the dark part of the room. There is one light and that is a small lampshade on the desk. The face of Silus can be seen, as he continues smoking a cigarette and drinking a small glass of whisky. He has large dark circles under his eyes, as if he hasn’t slept in many weeks. He rocks back and forth in his chair before speaking.* Silus: Y’see, I was thinking. *Cecil looks up* CC: did it hurt? *Silus laughs and breaks in to a hard throaty cough half way through* Silus: It’s been almost a week since I’ve caused any problems for my little brother. Maybe its about time that we gave him another little surprise. *Your can see the faint outline of Cassidy’s head, nodding up and down* CC: What do you suggest? *Silus leans forward and grins, revealing a silver tooth* Silus: Well, now he has that chip implanted under his skin thanks to myself, I think it would be a good time to track him down at home and plant a bomb in his last car. This time we shall finish him for good. CC: I think your going a bit too far. He’s a member of EW, we need him’for now. *Silus grins and rocks back and forth again* Silus: But Cecil, it will be so good to get rid of the loser. CC: It’s Mr Cassidy to you. I don’t think killing your brother is going to make you any better. Your still a fat oaf who stinks like an over-grown mule. *Silus? smile fades* Silus: Ah c’mon Mr Cassidy, I’ll come work for you. The fans wouldn’t miss him. *Cecil laughs* CC: You? Work for me? Haha, come off it! You have nothing to offer. *Silus frowns* Silus: That’s a lie! I made Shane become the frightened man that he is. I shocked the world when I turned up and killed mine’and his..very own grandfather! What more do you want? I’m a mastermind. If you had any sense you’d of signed me up straight away. Put me in the ring with this new fella, what’s his name? Kid Kash? CC: That’s Kay Kash and no, he would waste you in under ten minutes. The only way you’d win is if it was a Skunk match. *Silus stands up* Silus: I don’t have to listen to this! I gave Eternal Wrestling the storyline of the week and this is how you treat me? You don’t deserve to be in my company. I’m insulted Cecil’er’Mr Cassidy. I have so much respect for you, that’s why I did this to Shane. I wanted to be the next member of your very own alliance. You’re like a role model to me. *Cassidy thinks for a moment and then replies* CC: Ok then, go out and make Shane pay. *Cassidy hands Silus a small device* CC: This gadget will help you track down Shane, wherever he goes. I want you to take a small video camera with you, just so I can see Shane’s very own demise on video. *Silus grins with a sadistic smile and he leaves the room as Cecil Cassidy Shakes his head and laughs* (Later) ----------------------------------------------------- BACK AT HOME: *Shane walks through the door of his home and shuts the door behind him. He hangs his coat on the rack at the side of the door. He then walks into the kitchen where Elektra is cooking over a hot stove, she is wearing a long blue skirt and a black tank top. Shane walks up to her from behind and puts his arms around her. He looks over her shoulder, watching her stir what looks to be some sort of casserole. Elektra smiles at his presence.* Elektra: Hiya hun. Where’ve you been? I’ve been making tea. Shane: I’ve been to the doctors to see about removing this netbios chip. *Elektra stops stirring the food and turns around to face him* Elektra: Oh yeah? What did he say? *Shane scratches his head and scrunches his face* Shane: They said it’s not going to be easy. The chip is so small they can’t find where it has been planted. I know its in my neck but they can’t find it. Who knows? It’s probably irremovable and besides, there’s 100% chance of ending up blind like Skylar. I’m doomed to eternity, and followed by people I don’t even know. There’s gonna be hell to pay. *Elektra turns back to the stove and adds some crush peppers into the casserole* Elektra: What about the next immortals after this week. Who do you think you will be facing? I mean, Bobby Stormbringer has been saying some harsh words about you. *Shane sits down at the kitchen table* Shane: Well, ya can’t help sore loser from being’well’sore losers. I beat him once already and he got the early advantage, still he couldn’t get the job done. So what chance does he stand now? He has no chance at all! He is weak and pathetic, he doesn’t deserve to be in the same ring as me. He keeps going about with his but-lord butler and saying? *Shane does an impression of Bobby Stormbringer in a strange but funny voice* Shane:..?Carl, get me another chocolate milk please’oh Carl! Can Yer remove my head from my ass?” *Shane shakes his head* Shane: This guy is obviously in way over his head, they say don’t run before Yer can walk, well he’s a prime example of that. Nothing will stop the fella from talking crap, it’s the only talent he’s got. Look at me, I’m the New Era. Some people don’t even understand what I’m getting at by saying that. I guess in time I will reveal just what the New Era is. *Elektra keeps stirring the casserole* Elektra: What about Cassidy and Silus? Shane: What about them? If they want to exchange sexual favours then they can do. Cassidy seems to like doing so with Bobby Bore-inger. He handed Bobby the number one contender’s shot for the CyberSlam belt. All because this loser can’t win a match by himself, well that title is going to be taken off him as soon as I’ve dealt with my immature brother. Elektra: Something has gotta be done about that. Can’t you challenge him? Shane: What? Bobby? Didn’t Yer hear what I said? I said “he doesn’t have what it takes to beat me”, that’s a fact. If he did, then he wouldn’t have backed out of the match against Dexter Thomas and me. *Shane gets up and walks over to the kitchen cabanet and takes out a glass. He fills it with water and takes a drink, he laughs and nods* Shane: This time it’s payback. ------------------------------------------------------ (Meanwhile) *Silus is stood outside laying on his back under a car. The car looks to be Shane’s Mercedes and it seems like Silus is fitting a small device underneath the car. He then gets up but still crouches down a little as if he is hiding. It becomes apparent that Silus is stood outside Shane’s house. Silus is wearing blue ripped jeans and a leather coat. He walks over to the garage door and picks the lock, it clicks open and Silus walks inside, closing the door behind him.* (Back in the house) *Shane is stood drinking water from a glass as Elektra stirs the casserole in the stove* Shane: So how longs it gonna be? Elektra: What? Shane: The food. Elektra: Oh, about 10 minutes. *Shane nods and drinks the rest of his water, he then puts the glass in the sink* Shane: I better go get Grandma then. Elektra: Ok. *Shane walks into the hall and puts his coat back on. He then opens the strong wooden door and walks out of it, closing it behind him. Elektra continues stirring the casserole and adding additional spices. She walks over to a cupboard, just then she hears an almighty explosion. Panic starts flowing through her veins as she rushes to the window to see the Mercede’s blown to small burning pieces. There’s no sing of Shane, her eyes fill with tears as she puts he hand to her mouth. She runs towards the door when do arms reach out and grab her from the back. She screams loud as the person behind her puts their hand over her mouth. The voices whispers..* Voice: Hello baby, did you miss me? *Elektra manages to turn around to see Silus stood grinning at her. Elektra throws weak punches at her but Silus manages to restrain her with minimum effort. She breaks down into tears* Silus: Elektra my dear, you punch just like my little brother. *Elektra puts her hands to her face and weeps softly* Elektra: What have you done to Shane? Tell me! *Silus peers over her shoulder and looks out of the window* Silus: Shane has taken a time-out, but this time its permanent. No more Shane Wethers! Just Yer and me baby, the way it should be. Look, it’s the perfect picture. Shane didn’t renew his Visa, he blew up two of his own cars for the insurance claim to pay off the fine and then it backfired in his face. I’m a new man, I’ve got a different identity! *Silus takes his passport out of his back pocket and shows it to Elektra* Silus: I’m now known as? *Silus looks at the card himself* Silus: Miss Mary Lee Johannesburg? Damnit! I picked up the wrong passport. *Silus feels around in his back pocket of his jeans and takes out an identification card. He shows it to Elektra and then looks at it himself with a smile* Silus: I am Mr. Ricardo Luis Ferdandnez or something like that. *Elektra looks up at him* Elektra: B-but your not even Spanish! *Silus frown and pushes her onto the floor* Silus: Hey!!! The police will think Shane killed you like I said, picture perfect! I gave you a chance to be with me but now I realise something, your nothing but a slut. *Silus tightens his grip on Elektra’s throat* Silus: Say goodbye! *all of a sudden there’s a smash and a vase is seen crashing over his head and smashing to little pieces. Y-y-you were s-s-supposed to be d-d-dead! *Shane grins and shakes his head* Shane: Ah my brother, Yer' so gullible. That’s probably why my family didn’t want you around *Silus grits his teeth in anger* Shane: Ya see, as I left the house I kinda got to thinking of the discussion I had with Elektra earlier on, about how that this chip meant I would be followed and doomed to eternity. I knew Yer’d 'av something up Yer sleeve, I mean how unusual would it be for Yer to 'av done something to my car for the third time? So I decided to take a little look under the most common place for a bomb to be planted, that’s underneath the car, and sure enough there was a small little device. I could of called out the police of even tried to disconnect it myself, but that would 'av been too easy, I wanted a little bit of fun. So knowing that the door had to be opened and slammed shut for the bomb to trigger I found a long roll of nylon string, strong enough to slam a door. I just tied it to the inside door handle of the drivers seat , guided it through the passenger seat window and made sure I wa' a good few meters way from the car. I then just pulled the cord and the door slammed shut, hey presto. Picture Perfect! *Silus thumps his hand down on the floor. He then gets up to hit Shane as Elektra watches on in relief. Silus runs at him and Shane moves out of the way and throw’s Silus into the wall, then he continues beating him with a series of punches to the head, opening a cut on Silus? forehead. Elektra looks up at Shane* Elektra: I was so scared, I thought you were dead. *Shane laughs* Shane: No, I wa' just chillin?, watching the sh!t blow up in front of my eyes. *They both laugh and walk out of the room as the scene fades*
