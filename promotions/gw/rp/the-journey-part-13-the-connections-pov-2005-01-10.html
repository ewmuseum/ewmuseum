The Journey: Part 13/The Connection’s POV

10 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-10-05 Early Morning Its been a long time friends and I don’t mind saying its been TOO long to be away from you one and all. The story that never ends is back and by now I think that you know the players involved so let’s get to it shall we? Its early morning in New York City and its evident by the taxis running at full force and some people seen screaming obscenities at one another over the general dumb stuff that makes a person nauseated. One such group that lives in a house on the outskirts of town doesn’t believe that you need to be a big meanie to get anywhere but as long as you are yourself its fine and that is’The Connection! I’m sure you remember the gang right? Joshua Curtis, Zack Johnson, and Ami Mizuno are the on screen fixtures that you get to see week in and week out while Juri Arisugawa, Tina Davis and Michiru Kaiou stay in the background unless they feell they need to come to the forefront for a huge announcement and that is a rarity in and of itself. Today is one of the “early riser” days for Zack and Joshua but that’s only because Josh and Zack have appointments to keep at a hair salon at about 8 o’clock in the morning! Joshua was the first up at 6:30 in the morning and when he looked over at the sleeping frame of his lover he couldn’t help but smile as he knew the return of one of the best tag teams in GW was quickly approaching and sleep was all but hard to come by at this time. *Better let him sleep just a little longer* Joshua thought to himself as he got up and walked to the bathroom in their bedroom and closed the door behind him before turning on the water to start his shower. Joshua let the water run for a good 5 to 10 minutes to get the water hot as it’s a known fact that the water was going to be ice cold from the weather that they had been getting in New York for the past couple of days before he got in finally and undertook the task of cleansing his body. Problem is he wok the dead? Now mind you Zack gets up at about 6:45 every morning anyways and it was about that time to begin with but when he heard the water running in the connected bathroom to the shower Zack couldn’t help but play a little prank on his boyfriend? Zack very carefully snuck into the bathroom and found the toilet at which point he did the one thing anybody would hate that was taking a shower: he flushed! *AIEEEE!!!* Joshua screamed as the really hot water hit him not 3 seconds after the swooshing sound was made from the toilet and Joshua stuck his head out the sliding door of the shower to give Zack what for! “HEY!” Joshua yelled out as he stuck his head out the glass sliding door “What’s the big idea”!” Joshua asked somewhat annoyed at his boyfriend’s playfulness. “Sorry” Zack said in a mock-pout even when he knew that part of the reason that he did what he did was because Joshua took the liberty of taking the shower without him. “Ah its ok hun’give me like 10 minutes and I’ll be out ok?” Joshua asked and Zack nodded before leaving the bathroom and taking his spot on the bed. When Joshua got out of the shower Zack went in soon after and finished the task of bathing in the same amount of time as Joshua except Zack didn’t get the nasty surprise that Joshua got but then again Joshua knew better! After about 10 minutes Zack walked out in a white undershirt and cheetah boxers and took a seat right next to the man that makes his life everything worth living for in Joshua L. Curtis! “Ready to go back to work Monday?” Zack asked as he sat cross-legged on the bed next to Joshua and there was really only one real answer to that question. “Of course Zack! I know it was only a week away from the fed but that week was like hell for me without being able to walk out to see the fans and hear them chant our names was like complete and utter torture because it was those people that kept me motivated at times.” Joshua explained to Zack and he nodded in agreement before speaking “How very true Joshie. I know that I haven’t been around the game all that long but I know that when you and I wrestled in England as a team hearing the fans calling for the “Blackout” was one of the most exhilarating feelings I ever had in my life with the first of course being our coming together as a couple.” Zack pointed out with a wink towards Joshua and it was at that moment that Joshua got up to take a look in the walk-in closet to look for his outfit for Monday. “What do you think we should do for backstage attire this week Zack?” Joshua asked the more fashion savvy part of the couple and that is when with a smile Zack got up and walked towards the closet where he saw the “perfect outfit” according to him. “Right Here.” Zack said as pulled out the outfit that Josh wore to an anime convention about a month ago and when Joshua saw the outfit he had a big smile on his face. “That’s perfect Zack!” Joshua said before looking at the clock next to his side of the bed “Oh crap we got to go hun so let’s get ready because I hate keeping Michael waiting.” Joshua said as he got a white Patriots polo shirt, Arizona blue jeans and British Knight sneakers while Zack picked out his San Francisco 49ers sweatshirt, Canyon River Blues jeans and a pair of Vans sneakers that Josh bought for him a couple of weeks ago when he was away on a trip with Tina Davis. When the duo was dressed and after Joshua checked to make sure that everything was in place they walked out of the room and towards the garage where Joshua opened the passenger side door for Zack before getting in the driver’s side and starting the car up to leave for the salon that they known better than anyone! Now you may be wondering who this “Michael” character is am I right? Michael is the owner of the salon that Joshua frequents and was the person that Joshua was first to confide in about his sexuality when he came to the United States after one of his matches in England. To everyone that has ever encountered the 27 tear old owner Michael is a very normal person outside of the fact that when Joshua came overseas he was the first one to hit on Joshua and was somewhat heartbroken when he found out that Joshua had taken on a lover but then again he also wanted Joshua to be happy and when he saw how he was around Zack how could he begrudge him. About 30 minutes later and The Viper pulled into the parking lot to which was greeted by Michael coming out to greet Joshua and Zack before they went into the salon and every worker that was there stopped what they were doing for a couple of minutes to say their greetings to Joshua and Zack which always brought a smile to the faces of the two lovebirds because it made them happy to be accepted there. “Welcome back guys” Bradley started as he turned to face the duo before continuing “what brings you to our neck of the woods today?” the young apprentice asked the two before resuming his duties with one of the customers there. “Oh not much really. Just came because I think its time that I did something with this hair that is on my head.” Joshua said semi-jokingly and while Zack somewhat stifled a giggle Michael was surprised by the statement that Joshua had made. Michael knew that Joshua was a little funny about his hair on occasion but to make a statement that sounded like a complete overhaul was almost befuddling to Michael but luckily for his heart that wasn’t going to be completely the case. “Relax” Joshua began as he looked the expression on his favorite owner’s face “all I’m thinking of is dying my hair. I don’t know what color I’m going to do it but I’m thinking maybe do it the color of Lieutenant Zechs from Gundam Wing.” Joshua explained to Michael and he thought about it and smiled sweetly as he knew of Joshua’s Anime fetish and sent him over the cute red-headed girl that started there about a week ago named Emily and as Josh made his way over to the girl Michael turned his attention to Zack. “So Zack” Michael began “how have things been for you and Joshua since you two began going out?” he asked and as he did he could see the expression on Zack’s face turn to one of pure joy just thinking about the man that is his partner in life. “Very well Michael.” Zack said as he began his explanation “I think that Joshua has helped me understand the way things work in the places that we have been and he’s been a very caring person when I was under the weather a few months ago and I know how much it pained him to have to leave me when he had to but I think he knows that I understand that when business calls you have to answer or else.” Zack told Michael and the owner of the salon nodded as he heard Zack’s words about Joshua. “Does it worry you that he has developed a friendship with the World Champion while you were sick” I know that Ken is a friend of Joshua’s and that during the weeks before your debut Joshua had been watching him from afar.” Michael told Zack and Zack to his credit just shrugged off the notion of Joshua betraying him for the World Champion. “Joshua is loyal to his friends Michael. I learned that overseas when Joshua had a chance to betray me to get a title shot and all he had to do was hit me with a chair when the ref was out cold and all he did was throw the chair outside of the ring and walked out to the delight of the fans and the disgust of the person whom believed he bought Curtis” services but found out what the power of love was all about!” Zack told Michael and seeing the firm commitment from Zack told the open Owner about all he needed to know about the young man from San Francisco, California. Meanwhile on the other end of the salon? “So Joshua” Emily began as she was getting the Connection “leader” ready to put the bleach in his hair “tell me about Zack. What’s it like living with a man that was an opponent for so long in the ring against you that is now your lover?” Emily asked with a certain glow about her and that made Josh all the more eager to answer her question. “Zack is dream come true Emily. He is the kind of guy that every night when I lie awake in bed and look next to me I can be assured that I have someone that is in this with me and isn’t afraid to face challenges with me.” Joshua said assertively which was one of the few traits that he rarely showed to people. “Well that’s good to hear. I know what its like to have both the heartbreak and the euphoric nature of relationships as there is this one special person in my life now that helped me when I was feeling like complete crap after the other relationship I was in had a disastrous ending!” The red-headed employee explained to Josh which got him wondering. “Who is the person that you have in your life now?” Joshua asked hoping that he could get the answer out of the young lady and she wasn’t too willing to give up the answer easily. “Well the guy that I dated was a jerk and he treated me like complete garbage which is what basically caused the breakup and then with the emotional and physical abuse afterward I just said “That’s IT! I’ve had it with straight guys and now me and this girl that I met a little over 4 months ago are dating and she treats me with the respect that the guy never did.” Emily explained to Joshua as she put the finishing touches on the peroxide that was to go on Joshua’s hair and when he went under the machine he closed his eyes for a couple of minutes to think about life without Zack and it wasn’t pretty to say the least! Put it this way Joshua would have been dead by now cos very few people try to understand his situation outside of Zack and another person by the name of Ken Partridge Jr. Is that bad? Considering that the climate of the federation that he is in is not that keen on having wrestlers whom are open about their sexuality Joshua needs all the support he can get when it comes to the matter. Zack is a great example of a support system that makes it easier for Joshua to go on with his life and if he can ever get the friendship back with Ken Partridge Jr. that he felt like he lost for a little while then he could really be happy. There is one person that very few people know about though that Joshua still holds a place for and he is in a hospital in San Francisco, California in a coma that goes by the nem of Adrian but that my friends is another story for another day. As Joshua finished Emily gave him a mirror to see if he liked what he saw and of course Joshua is never hard to please so he graciously paid her and went out to see Zack and Michael one more time before the two young lovers left for the day. “Well guys how are you two making out?” Joshua asked and Michael stopped for a second to stifle a giggle before answering the question. “Oh just fine hun. Zack was telling me about you and how much he cares about you. You have a good man here and I think that when the time comes you two will really begin to understand the bond that you two have and then you two will really be happy together. Well I bid you a farewell and good day gentlemen. Talk to you later ok?” Michael said and the tow young lovers nodded and both kissed him on the cheek before leaving the salon hand and hand much to the moans of a lot of people but not because they didn’t get it but because they never know when they are going to get to seem them again?.after all Lightning never hits the same place twice right? Au Revoir darlings and God Bless!
