The Search for Steel...The Quest For Gold!

3 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-3-04 Mountains’Crowding in from the distance until they seem to encroach upon the entire shot. As the shot pans down, a lush valley springs into view, and a lone horseman. Tall in the saddle, he wears a black cloak, and his hair flies back in the breeze that blows through the valley from the mountains. The shot comes close, and we see Venneman Pendragon. His familiar sword is absent from his side, and he rides with a grim look set upon his face? “I find myself back in the East’The noble land of Japan. Here, as in no other places on earth, do some people hold to the old ways; the old beliefs. I am without friends, I have not even mine sword’Yet, still there is hope. One could never hope to replace the power that a sword of Excalibur’s caliber brings with it; it bore the grip of all mine forebears, and that is an irreplaceable commodity. And yet, to be swordless is to wish for death, and thus I am unwilling to go as such for too long of a stretch of time. Ahh’Were it so that I might get mine hands around the neck of Itagaki Freeze. Would that I could take the head of his master, Akuma. That, however, shall not be at this time. In life, there are always other things that need attention. Revenge is nice, but it rules the world not. I need steel. Strong steel, from the mountains here, forged in the small town I seek”.Tatara. There lives the master sword maker’Yoshindo Yoshihara?” As he rides, smoke drifts up in front of him towards the horizon. The forge at Tatara, famous for its swords and strength of steel. It is a place where swords are smithed as they were during the times of the old masters. Pendragon rides on and finally comes to a village marked by distinctive Japanese architecture. The curved roofs of the buildings, and the openness of the spaces. Pendragon rides in and catches some strange looks from the local population. It is not often that Westerners are seen in the borders of this small village in the Shimane Prefecture. He pays them no mind. He is drawn by the deep ringing of hammer on steel. He reins in his horse in front of the smithy, and dismounts in a fluid motion. He pushes through a flap of canvas that serves as a door to the workshop, and enters into the heat. Putting the finishing touches on a beautiful Katana sword is Yoshindo Yoshihara. A tenth generation sword maker, he is the master of his craft. Small of stature, but solid, he has a kindly face from which the hair has started to draw back. He squints up at the intruder and smiles? Pendragon: I seek the master? Yoshindo: And you have found him, my Lord? Pendragon stares regally at the old man before a smile crosses his lips and he moves across the room and embraces the smaller man, lifting him off of his feet? Pendragon: It has been a long time, master? Yoshindo: Too long, my son’Too long have you been away from the forge. Pendragon rolls his eyes but keeps the smile upon his face? Yoshindo: Your sword? Excalibur? Where is he? “Swords are he’s. One doesn’t go into battle with a wench at one’s side, unless one plans on dying beside said wench. Yoshindo and I see eye to eye. The master has taught me much, and it is here that I return to forge the blade that shall take the place at my side, if only for a little while?” Pendragon: Stolen. Taken from mine side by a trio of impudent whelps who failed to best me in hand to hand combat. Yoshindo: Then the cowards have sealed their fate, it would appear? Pendragon: So it would. “Tis ruination for them. They have affronted the Lord King, and I will not stop until they are crushed beneath mine boots. Yoshindo: You come seeking steel then” Of course you do’I see the gleam in your eye that this blade has brought out. You and I shall forge the blade together, but it is your task to find the steel. So it was in the old days of the Samurai, and so it is for true warriors of our time. Go to the mountains, and return when you have found a suitable iron. Then we shall shape it? Pendragon shakes his head and smiles at the old man. With a twirl of his cloak, he turns and leaves the forge room? ***************FLASH***************** In the mountains, high above the land below, Pendragon sits alone at the top of a sheer cliff. He looks out over the land, as daylight fades to dusk. Gathered behind him is the makings of a fire, and he sets the blaze going with flint and steel. An adept outdoorsman, he smiles approvingly at this minuscule accomplishment. As it becomes dark, he begins to look to the heavens? Pendragon: Fathers before me. I come to you once more, yet changed. Disarmed I am for the first time in mine life, and it is nothing that I am proud of. I underestimated the cowardice of mine foes’It shall not happen again. Mine whole life, I have sought honor’Glory’I have sought out places to perform great deeds. Friends have fallen beside me, and yet I toil on. And now, friendless, I once more bestride this great world alone? Thunder rumbles in the cloudless sky, almost as if something, or someone greater, is indeed listening. Pendragon just nods at the sound? Pendragon: Aye’Gold has been a major driving force for mine deeds and actions’The lust for gold and for immortality. To be remembered when I am gone. In GW, it seems that I have been presented with an opportunity for at least one of those dreams. King’s Gold has been set before me, and all mine hands need to do is to reach out and take it. A warriors prize, and one that many shall covet’The Television Title. And yet it is more’More than the small man, Drachen, who holds it now, and more than any others save mineself who might hold it in the future. It represents to me a coming about. People said I was finished’Washed up. They said I had not the fire, no desire. And yet, here I am’Once more emerged from the shadows to claim that which will be mine. To me, it represents a rebirth of sorts, and yet it is even larger than that. This title is a warrior’s title. Defended constantly, with many undeserving trying to usurp the crown’Usurp the King’s Gold? Lightning flashes in the distance, streaking down from the cloudless heavens, and striking ground far in the distance. Pendragon’s eyes widen and he smiles, throwing more wood upon his fire? Pendragon: EXACTLY! The TV strap is but a stepping stone to grander things. Drachen stands in mine way, and thou art correct. Freeze and his lackey, Jetsui, will probably have something to say about the King wearing gold once more. I have destroyed greater men than these who banded against me. This time shall be no different. Flies they are, who buzz around the head, waiting to be splattered by some quick hand. Common trash they be, and like rubbish I shall cast them out. They will try to stop me, and they will fail. They have mine sword, but they do not have mine power. I persevere when others fall, and I am relentless. I get what I want, and I deserve all that comes to me. Power. Glory. Honor. Gold. Drachen will fall. Though he tries to be strong, his weakness will show. There will be no one to save him this time. It is he and I, and once the battle haze drops, I pity the result for the new champ’To lose gold so quickly is a travesty. But that is what happens when one unworthy holds gold in the first place? A huge burst of thunder shakes the heavens and the mountain, and Pendragon looks skyward, a touch of fear and awe etched in his gaze? Pendragon: Yes’I veer from mine task. What kind of a King has no steel? What king has no blade with which to smite his enemies? And yet, for mine new blade, I need an alloy of unmatched toughness. Durability unchecked’Where shall I find such a thing? Divine intervention rarely strikes, and when it does, it is usually something small. It is normally meant for one person to witness, not for an entire world, or a large group of people. This night, things go against the norm. Across the sky streaks a blinding fireball, a meteorite. It comes it at a slight angle, beginning far out on the horizon. As it flies across the sky, it leaves an afterimage that follows it all the way, as it passes over Pendragon’s head and crashes into the side of the mountain, further towards the top. Pendragon sits, and astounded look etched upon his face as it races towards him. He follows it with a crane of his neck as it passes overhead and crashes behind. As his retinas return to night mode, and the afterimage begins to fade, he lets out a small? Pendragon: hoooooo?..Good CHRIST!! With a final look to the sky, Pendragon stands, bows his head and places his right fist over his heart? Pendragon: I thank thee, fathers, for this most wondrous sign’For glory’For honor’For gold? The scene fades as Pendragon turns to face the smoking crater left by the meteor, his long blonde hair blowing back from his brow in the night breeze?
