The Struggle Within (Part 1)

undated

Date: Today He had been resisting more and more lately. There were more struggles, more violent urges, more shaking, more black-outs. It was getting out of hand, I knew that he wanted to regain control. I knew it. He wanted revenge and he wanted to do it himself, he didn’t know that he was too weak. I was what he needed. He wasn’t strong enough. That’s why he had failed the first time. And in the slumber after his failure, his subconscious created me. I am what he needs. I am everything that he wishes he could be. I am the epitome of what he needs to accomplish his goal, and that is why I am here. No matter if he wants to do it by himself or not, he can’t. I won’t let him fail again. I am in control now, and I will never let him back. Fushichou no Nazo walked to the back of the room. His face was uncovered, which was unusual for the masked superstar. However, none of his facial features can be made out as his shaggy hair hangs messily all over the front of his face. It does not seem to bother Nazo however as he continues about his business as usual. He walked to the back of the small apartment, and pressed his hand against the wall. He begins to rub and feel against the wall with his hands. A confused look comes across his face, as he apparently cannot find what it is that he is looking for. “I know it is here, he showed it to me in his memories,” Nazo speaks out loud, but still to himself. He continued rubbing his fingers along the wall. Finally after several minutes he gave up. He appeared to be quite frustrated. He slammed his fist into the wall, the wall was sturdy and must have been reinforced on the opposite side. Nazo looks down at his hand without making a noise. He sees the blood trickling out of several points on his knuckles and begins to smile. “I know you felt that, not me. I am in control, I control when you take over, you don’t,” Nazo said once again out loud. Who could he be talking to however? Closer inspection of the room still proves that the room is empty. “I’m going to need to find this room, I am going to need to find my gear for this week. No matter how much I despise you, I need your help. We must work together. We mustn’t let anyone know the truth. And for that, I need the special equipment that I know you have somewhere in this apartment,” Nazo chuckles slightly after he finishes speaking. Nazo’s breathing became heavier. He panted and grunted. Eventually, his body began to shake slightly. His head shot backwards, and for the first time a look at his face can be seen. It looks to have a slight scar across the bottom of his jaw-line, but it can’t be told for sure. The rest of his face appears to be a blur. Finally his body stopped shaking. He slumped to the floor, his knees up and his head buried in between. His shaggy hair fell back over his face, and his breathing finally began to return to normal. Suddenly, the sound of tears and sobs came through the silence of the room. “No, no matter if you’re trying to help accomplish this goal or not. I can’t, I won’t. I will not allow you to control me,” the words are soft but harshly spoken over the soft sobs. Nazo stood back to his feet, but he appeared to be a different person. He walked slowly, his back slumped over. His entire body portraying a man defeated. A change from the proud and confident, seemingly invincible monster that was once in his place only minutes ago. He reached the small sink on the kitchen counter. He turned the knobs and cold water instantly began to rush out. He quickly put his right hand underneath the water. He let the water wash the blood off the already purple and bruising knuckles. Satisfaction began to take over, Nazo once again appeared to be at peace with himself. He turned the taps off and reached for the small hand towel. He wiped his hand off, but soon another change came over him. His hands immediately shot to his ears. He began to block out noises that were seemingly non-existent. “NO!! SHUT UP!!! I WON’T LET YOU CONTROL ME!!” his screams echoed throughout the empty apartment. However, the actions did not make sense. Except for his screaming the apartment had been as silent as a graveyard. Nazo began to make his way towards the door. It appeared as though he were trying to get away from a being that was not there, something was haunting him. Something that nobody could see. His hand reached out and grabbed hold of the door knob. He went to turn the handle, but suddenly his body began to shake once again. “NO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” the scream was blood-curdling. It sounded like a man being threatened for his life. Nazo’s body became limp. His eyes fluttered behind his hair, and his legs turned to jelly. The rest of his limbs went limp and the world became limp. He fell to the ground, and landed with a loud thud on the apartment floor. The thud was deafening in the empty apartment. Suddenly however, Nazo reappeared. He stood tall and proud once again. Once again a great change from his prior state. He laughed slightly as he began to walk back towards the apartment wall he was scanning earlier on. “Thanks,” he spoke aloud. “As long as you think you’re in control, your mind exposes all the secrets that you don’t want me to know.” This time it appears as though Nazo has a better understanding of what he is looking for. He walked to the wall, and takes a few sidesteps to his right. He kneeled down in front of the wall and extended his hand. He pressed it firmly against what appeared to be a solid wall. However, the wall swung open like a trap door as he did so. He chuckled satisfied once again. He crawled through the small opening, just large enough for himself to fit inside. When he emerged on the other side of the tunnel he was shrouded in a room of darkness. He ruffled through the room, noisily as he ran into the walls. The room seemed so unfamiliar to him. Finally, he felt something dangle against the top of his head. He reached up and discovered a steel chain, he tugged the chain. The room instantaneously became engulfed in light. He smiled as he looked straight ahead. It was exactly what he had been looking for. “I knew you had this hidden in here somewhere. They had always fascinated you, they have just recently taken on a new meaning though, haven’t they?” his words seemed cold and gripping. Nazo began to laugh sadistically as he reached out to the rack in front of him. On the clothes rack was an ancient Samurai body suit. The armor was deep red and it glistened in the light. He felt along the small shin pads and the shoulder pads. “They are too heavy too compete in,” he announced as he lifted the gear up. Nazo soon noticed something he had not noticed before. There was a small wooden table, with a stool in front of it behind the clothes rack. Nazo made his way to the table. On the table he saw the traditional, underwear of a Samurai warrior. It appeared to match the Samurai body suit in color perfectly. The red arms bands were tightly fitted, protecting the warrior’s arms completely from the cold. However, it was another item on the table that caught Nazo’s attention even more. Beside the arm bands was a small mask. Beside the mask was an encyclopedia opened up, to the area about Samurai. The page showed a picture of a mask, often worn by high ranking members of the Samurai community. The mask was red, and featured a dark and sinister face with a large smile on it. The mask appeared to be an exact replica of it. Only instead of being made out of heavy leather like the ways in the past, this mask was made out of a tight-fitting latex type material. Nazo smiled greatly and grabbed the mask off of the table. He began to laugh greatly as he leaned his head back. His hair fell away from his face, and he soon covered his face with the mask. He looked back down and grabbed the arm protectors off the table. He pulled the string and the room returned to darkness. There was no more noise, there was no movement there was nothing. Suddenly there was a loud sadistic laugh, followed a few seconds later by a loud thud.
