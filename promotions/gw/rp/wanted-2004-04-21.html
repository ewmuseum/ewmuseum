Wanted

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21/04 The engine hummed, the leaves of the passing trees blurred by, and the wind howled as I drove up I - 80. I had decided to take the Expedition on a little road trip. While my friends, and fellow Elite members 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr., and the man I would be facing in only a matter of two days 'American Youth' Josh took the Elite helicopter to the site of WRESTLECADE IV, I decided to make the trip on my own. Being around Josh was not what I wanted, and not what I needed right now. There was too much going on between the two of us. He was not my friend right now. He was not the man who I travelled with. He was not my stablemate. Right now, and until our match at Wrestlecade was over, Josh was THE ENEMY. So while Josh and Ken took their fancy little helicopter ride to Lincoln, I took the trip the old fashioned way. I hopped behind the wheel of my vehicle, and I started up the engine. I needed this time alone. There were alot of things running through my mind. Alot of things that hadn't ran through my mind in what seemed like an eternity ago. Josh and I had been up and down the road together a million times. Josh and I had traveled around the world, spilled each other's blood, and broke one another's bones. We have had a countless number of matches, but this one stood out from the rest. This time everything was different. Whether best friends, or arch rivals Josh and I have always had one thing in common, and that was our want, and our drive to be THE VERY BEST at what we do. I have reached that goal. Twice. I had become the World Heavyweight Champion. I have become THE VERY BEST at what we do. The journey was long. The path I took was a bumpy one, and I sure as hell did not come out unscaved. The shitty hotels, and hole in the wall joints that called themselves hotels spoke for themself. The pain I have put my body through. The blood I have spilt, alot of it at the hands of the man I would be facing in merely two days. The bones that were broken, the sweat that poured from my body, and even the damnation I have put my personal life through has been all worth it. Not a day goes by that I keep from looking back, and am glad that I went throught what I have. With out the trials and tribulations that I have been put through, reaching the top would not have been as sweet. Reaching the top of the mountain, and grabbing that brass ring would not have meant as much, if I had not suffered the worst possible things a man's life can suffer, just to make it. I however did not make that journey alone. There was one person, day in and day out. Through every city I travelled. Every match I had. Any suffering that occured. There was one person that was there with me to experience it all. Josh was that one person. He travelled to the same cities that I did. He suffered most of the same suffering that I had been put through. Yet like myself he never stopped, and he never gave into that pressure. For almost three years, we were inseperable. Whether it was because we were at each other's throats, spilling the other's blood, or breaking one another's bones, or whether it was because of the mutual respect, and friendship we had gained. Josh and I could not be pulled apart. It's kind of ironic. After almost three years, things have finally come full circle. It was Josh who I won my first championship EVER, against. It was against Josh, that I broke in, and made a name for myself as someone to be reckoned with. It was with Josh that I trained, and trained, and trained, and finally acheived my dream. He stood by my side the entire time, proud of my accomplishments as if they were his own. Yet, now, I am the last obstacle standing in the way of my best friend achieving the one thing he has worked to accomplish his entire life. I now stand in the way of my best friend doing the one thing in life, he's always dreamed of doing, but has never been giving the opportunity to do. Josh's dream of becoming THE VERY BEST at what we do, and becoming World Champion rests in my hands. I sat and gazed out the windshield of my Expedition, driving along the interstate in a daze, thinking of what WE have been throught, where WE have been, and what WE have done. My dazed thinking, was abruptly interrupted as my cell phone let out it's high - pitched screech it called a ring. I really did not want to be bothered right now. I needed this time to get all of these thoughts, all of these emotions out of my system, and clear my head so that I could focus on what I HAD to do. But alas, the annoying screeching got to me, and I unwillingly answered the call. 'Hello?', I said with a slightly annoyed tone to my voice. On the other end of the line was Ken Partridge Jr. He had called just to let me know that he and Josh arrived alright, and was wondering how my trip was going. My answer took a while to develope itself, as I had wanted to spew out the things that were running around my mind. I wanted to get out the emotions that were flowing throughout the veins in my body. However I merely replied with a simple, 'Fine.' I could sense that Ken felt a little awkward with his two best friends at such odds at the moment. I could also sense that he was interperting the tone in my voice, as he quickly said his goodbye, and wished me a safe trip before hanging up the phone. The sun was begining to set as I continued my journey, figuratively and literally. Slowly I leaned back in my seat as I tossed my small, metallic silver cell phone onto the passenger seat next to me. As I rested my head on the small cushioning behind it, I released the grip of my right hand from the steering wheel and turned the volume to the radio up, and slightly pressed down on the gas pedal, speeding up the Expedition a little. As the sun continued to fade below the horizon, and the darkness began to creep in, engulfing the earth, Bon Jovi's 'Dead of Alive' hit the radio. The slow melodic tune soothed the racing mind. I sat there, staring at the road in front of me, again in a daze, as my mind honed in on the lyrics of the song. The words were eeriely like a soundtrack to the path on which Josh and myself had taken, and were still on. As eerie as it was it really didn't stick out to me how much the lyrics told OUR story, until the solo at the end of the song hit. As I stepped on the gas, to finish my journey, as the night took it's hold on the earth, these words played over and over in my mind, as my memories of Josh, Ken, and myself played like a highlight reel in my head. Now I walk these streets This six string in my hand Still playing for keeps It's the same old me, same old band We've been everywhere, standing tall We've seen a million faces..... We've seen an awful lot of faces
