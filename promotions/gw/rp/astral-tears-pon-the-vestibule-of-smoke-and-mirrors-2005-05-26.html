Astral Tears 'pon the Vestibule of Smoke and Mirrors

26 May 2005

Date: May 26th 2005 May 25, 2005 Prelude: The sun’s descent, The moon’s dissapperance, The stars fallen existence Shall never be a token Unbroken, Of hope and memory yielding For each day passing Silently, silently..... Through the melody of eternal passion, To the swansong of our time In this garden of delight....” - Excerpt from “Garden of Delight (Melody of Eternal Passion)? By Gabriel Hampshire ******* The chill of the night lingered in his heart. It was almost impossible to hide from it. The bustling town, now died and almost became a ghost town in itself. There was a dull odor of sweet roses in the air as the wind passed through him. Tranquility.... how beautiful he thought. Never has he held a state of calmness ever since the dissappearance of his parents. Nor has he ever gotten more than a couple of hours slumber out of it. It weighed down upon him like poisonous daggers to his heart. He was almost dead inside. No. He is dead. His one goal now was to find out what happened. But he could not do that without money. As much to his families chagrin, he had found a job. But it wouldn’t matter anymore. He was alone. No. He is alone. “T’sao?” A woman’s voice broke the silence. The emptiness inside his heart all of a sudden filled. Filled with many emotions that he could not comprehend. He turned his head only to catch a glimpse of her from the corner of his eye. She was beautiful. The moon glared upon her grace, making her presence radiate. She was an angel. And he was enthralled. She giggled as he turned to face her completely. She was shorter than he. She only reached the span of five feet six. But it mattered naught. “My dear, what is wrong?” She asked. Could he tell her how he felt about her? Never has he been with a girl before. She was the first for him. And maybe the last. “Nothing is wrong my little flower.” He answered. He inched his way towards her. Simultaneously, she too approach him. His right hand reached forward, but the action made her step back. She did not want him to touch her for reasons she wanted to keep to herself. “What is wrong my arabian queen?” He couldn’t understand. She knew this, but she couldn’t allow herself to be touch. It was too soon. His mind was too fragile. She turned to face the night’s sky. The glittering stars shined through her jeweled eyes. Again, he tried to reach for her. She shrugged her shoulders this time. “Tell me Malika... please. I need to know.” He muttered. The wind grew and blew through him. He wanted to tell her then how he felt. But he was afraid. Afraid to show such an emotion to another human being. After all, everyone before her who he had loved died, or disappeared. “The sky looks so beautiful tonight. Like your soul, T’sao.” She said walking towards a towel laying on the soft grass. “You are beautiful Malika... everything about you.” He replied following behind. “You don’t understand... something has come to pass my love.” A long pause held the air heavily between the two. He could not understand the meaning, but deep down inside of him, he knew it was something very important. Something that may ruin what they have. “How long does it take for us to be honest about our feelings?” He questioned approaching from behind her. “Have you found another?” The question drew forth a surge of anger within her as she turned around. “How could you ask such a thing” No... it is not that at all...” “Then what?” His hand gently inched to her right shoulder. She was cold. He felt this even through the fabric of her coat. She did not reply. All she did was grab his hand, resting her cheek upon it. Even her face was cold. Very cold. “What is the one thing you desire most in your life?” She questioned. He had no reply. Before, he was sure. He wanted a family. He wanted a good job. He wanted wealth. He wanted what every normal human being wanted. But now his number one desire was to find the truth of the disappearance of his family. That, and going through such a time with the owner of his heart. “To live... with you.” Was his reply. Relinquishing his hand, she turned to face him. How beautiful is she? How mesmerizing is she? Can it be that I found true love? Can it be that I can love again? Is this true? Is this a dream? His mind began a conversation inside that kept his eyes fixed upon hers. Her beautiful jade eyes can leave a single stranger in erotic melancholy.... thriving on living just to fix his eyes upon such a rare sight again. This was how he felt towards her. She lowered her head and pushed forward ending up laying her head upon his chest. He embraced her. She was shivering heavily. She embraced him back. A tear almost fell, but he desperately tried to held it in. He did not want to cry in front of her. This was a happy moment. “T’sao.... I love you, I really do. But....” He stopped her. He did not want that sentence finished. He was completely satisfied in the moment that he did not want it ruined. There eyes met once again and within seconds, his lips fell upon hers. Many things flowed tumultuously in his mind. Beauty, elegance, her lips were perfectly symmetrical to his. He pulled away first as she followed suit. “I’ve been meaning to do that for a long time...” She stopped him. A tear had escaped from her eyes and slowly trickled down her cheek. He wiped it off. “T’sao, I have to leave you now. Please know that I love you, and I will always love you.” The statement left him hypnotized in fear, and in an emotional crescendo. “What!... What do you mean?” He replied shockingly. “The moon... it’s sway holds our love eternally. Always look at the moon my darling. I will be watching you.” The words were enough to send the tear that he held so hard inside to escape his eye. And it followed with more. It was at that point that she disappeared into thin air. “Malika! Malika! Malika come back!” He screamed into the night air. He fell to his knees and screamed in agony. No reply was recieved. Nothing but the wind. ******* May 26th, 2005 The Clarion Hotel. It’s structure was based on Victorian style. Beautiful it was to his eyes. Many people entered and left through the busy entrance. It was only a few more days until his big debut in the wrestling world in America, and his stomach inside felt like it was knotted. He proceeded forward with his luggage. Entering the hotel, he proceeded to make his way to the front desk. “Hello sir, and welcome to The Clarion Hotel, my name is Justin Smith, may I help you?” The man questioned. He was a small man, about 5’4 scrawny and his face looked to young to be working the front desk. Even his voice echoed like that of a child. But he placed his luggage on the ground and replied, “yes, my name is T’sao Liang and I have a room reserved in this lovely hotel of yours.” “Yes, excuse me Mr. Liang, let me check it in my computer... ah! Yes from Global Wrestling right?” “Yes that’s right.” He replied, surveying the lobby. Many different types of people sat, some arguing with there spouse, others waited in half a daze for something. Justin excused himself and walked to the back. A few moments later, he came back to his post with a key in his hand. He planted the key firmly on the desk. “The number of your room is 517. I hope you enjoy your stay here at the Clarion Hotel.” “Thank you.” And nothing more. He took the key from the desk, picked up his luggage and proceeded towards the elevator. ******* Later that day He was awakened from his meditation by the roaring of engines outside. Even at the fifth level of the hotel, they still echoed firmly without wanting to disappear. Sweat had poured down his face. Nightmares appeared whilst his session. He could not escape from them, he could not rid of them. They appeared to him as if they were real. Quite suddenly he rose to his feet and walked into the bathroom, flicking the lightswitch on. He stared deep into the mirror. Her face still haunted him even in his waking hour. How was he suppose to concentrate for his first match? He could not. The very thought of her made his heart weep and his soul ripping through his flesh. “I love you Malika... I truly love you...” ******* Later that Evening The phone’s ring vibrated through his head as he leaped up from his sitting position on the bed, trying to train his mind. He reached and grab the phone after the second ring and held it close to his ear. “Hello?” He said in a questioning tone. “Ah, T’sao... it has been awhile has it not?” The voice said. He knew exactly who was at the other line. It was the man that brought interest into the wrestling world in America. “My friend. What is the purpose of this call?” He indeed wanted to know. He wasn’t one to speak to someone on the phone for very long. If he had to, it had to be of importance. He loves a conversation with someone face to face so that he would know exactly what they say is true. Through a phone, one cannot tell if something being told is a lie. “Always to business...” There was a pause and a sigh that sounded on the other line. “Are you ready for your debut?” The voice grew to the line of worriness. “Of course I am.” There was a sound in his voice that wasn’t sure of his statement. It was for a split second, but he picked it up, and he knew that the man on the other line did as well. “Everything is in order.... as it should be. You are wrestling a man who seems to be rather, shall I say to troubled by many other things other than wrestling. You should not have a problem with him.” The man said. “This... Emoticon. His intelligence is vast, and his personality humble, but as you said. His concentration is blocked from his agenda of bringing back chivalry into this forsakened society that we live in. Unfortunately for him, the knowledge of the truth is that what he is fighting to bring back is a myth... unless he decides to wipe out the population of the ignorance, and populate the world with his genes, his battle to bring back chivalry to society is futile.... just like his wrestling ability against me.” After a moment of silence, the man on the other line laughed as he now stood, approaching the window, peeking to see if anything outside could hold his interest. Much to his chagrin, there was nothing but many cars and many more people walking to there destination. “Ruthless are your words my friend, I do enjoy hearing them.Your frame of mind seems to be in the right place, that puts me more at ease. Are you ready for what else is in store for the night?” A question that made him snicker. “Yes... I am ready. Ready as I’ll ever be...” As his voice drifted there was yet again a miniscule amount of uncertainty. “Good... I shall see you in the arena then.” The man on the other line said. The sound of the line die followed, as he hung up the phone. “Ready as I’ll ever be...” A statement that he was sure to be right. Then again a statement that filled much uncertainty in his mind...
