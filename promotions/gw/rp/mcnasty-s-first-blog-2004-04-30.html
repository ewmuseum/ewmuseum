McNasty's First BLOG

30 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/04 (EVO #1) You know I seen a lot of people doing these things called BLOGS or something like that on the internet, so I decided why not, I could do one for Global Wrestling on their website, let my fans know what’s happening. Sometimes in certain situations I feel like the fans are my family. And as I’ll mention later, family is the most important thing in my life. So this is my first entry into my BLOG. So who ever my fans out there, give me feedback at [detail withheld]. So let’s move on with this. Life has been different since my brother Charles came back to Altoona. We haven’t seen each other in like two years. My brother is a pretty good guy, he’s married with two wonderful kids, eventually they will be making their moved back down the keystone state once school is done for the year. I think it’s pretty cool though, not everyday in your life do you get a chance to work with your brother in something you love so much like professional wrestling. Two of the most important things in my life wrapped up into one, my career in pro wrestling, and more important my family. When it comes to family, I have a large family. We’re also Roman Catholic and well we’re not supposed to use contraceptives, so that explains the large family. I wouldn’t trade my large family for anything in the world. It’s such a great support system. I’m not talking about money, but I am talking about when you need someone there for you in rough times. The more people who love you, the better? I know it seems weird coming from someone like me, the king of hardcore, talking about emotions and family and such. I am a human being like all of you, I feel pain of the non physical kind. This is one of the main things I wanted to touch upon in this BLOG, I wanted you the fan, the viewer to know who I really am. Sure what you see in the ring a lot of times is really me. I am that tough son of a bitch, I am that guy who likes to have parties, have a few drinks, and have a good time. But I also wanted to set the record straight, I am a mere human being. I guess what I can do now is give you more of a background on my brother Charles? Well he’s a few years older than me, he’s the owner of a real estate company called, Luck of The Irish Real Estate, right in downtown Manhattan. Sorry ladies he’s married and has two kids. He left home when he was 20 years old, went to work for a real estate company, eventually he learned the ropes, and now here he is owning the second largest real estate company in the New York City area, yep you guessed he’s 2nd to Donald Trump, that bastard. As different as Charles and I can be sometimes, were pretty close as brothers. He’s one of my best friends. I was blessed to have him as a brother. God must have known that when I would come a long I would need someone to help me out in tough situations. Our Dad left our mother alone when I was still in my Mom’s tummy, and I have only ever seen him just a few times in my whole life. Charles is the one who taught me that eventually in life I will have to forgive my Dad for what he did to us. I eventually did, that was one of those lessons Charles gave me on becoming a man. One of them was to not hold a grudge, I still have a hard time with it, but I am working on it. It’s a tough issue to work on especially in this world of professional wrestling, when you have people who attack you backstage because they don’t like the shampoo you use. Here is another instance where Charles was a life saver, [detail withheld]. My family was a big help when it came to it, especially Charles and my Mom. That’s one of the many reasons Charles and I are so close as brothers, he’s not afraid to tell me how it is. Everyone needs that in their lives, someone who you can trust and will listen to, that will tell you when your shit is stinking. So now getting a chance to work with Charles is an honor really. I think Charles and I working together as the McNasty Boyz, we can do some serious shaking up in the tag division in Global Wrestling. Well kids, it’s getting close to opening time here at the Drunken Leprechaun. Duty calls, so be looking for this BLOG once a week on the Global Wrestling website ok? And on top of that cheer the McNasty Boyz on this Monday night on HBO, ok? Until then take care of yourselves and have fun. Cheers!, Donny J McNasty
