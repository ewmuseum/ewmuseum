The Astonishing Tales of the Horny Mathematician, Episode III: Revenge of the Nerds!

2 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/2/05 'There is a Chosen One from the wrestling prophecies.' [An old, dusty book is shown lying in the sand somewhere. The wind blows the dust all around it.] 'It is said he will bring balance between the rudos and the technicos.' [The book suddenly flutters open and pages begin turning.] 'He will come from the desert and bring equilibrium.' [The pages suddenly stop turning and it ends on a picture of said hero.] 'His name will be James.' [The scene quickly fades out.] -Taken from the James von Doom promo 'Dark Lords of the Sith,' UWF (6/7/05) ------ HAWAII 5.0 [After the attack on the Island of Misfit Wrestlers, Adam and Matt followed James' shuttlecraft to the island of Hawaii and, more specifically, the Mount Kilauea volcano, which just happens to be the most active volcano in the world. As James' shuttlecraft lands, they sneak behind his with theirs and land theres in the jungle but get all tangled up.] Matt: Damnit! I hate islands! Adam: This is an island? [Matt looks over at him.] Matt: You didn't know Hawaii was an island? [Adam shakes his head.] Adam: No. Matt: Moron. [Adam slaps Matt.] Adam: Bastard. [Matt grabs Adam and puts him in a headlock. Adam tries to fight out of it by punching Matt in the side. Matt then lets go and they brush themselves off in preperation for the confrontation that lies ahead.] Matt: Well, let's do this. Adam: Yep. [They get out of their shuttlecraft and head toward the volcano where von Doom is.] * * * Darth Death [On Mount Kilauea, the Comedy Leadership is having their meeting. James von Doom the Handler is doing his usual rambling as Darth von Doom comes walking in wearing a black robe with the cloak over his head to cover his face. All of the leadership looks over.] Nute Dumbray: Lord von Doom. We've been expecting you. [Darth von Doom motions to the doors with his hands, closing both exits so no one can leave.] Meanwhile... [Adam and Matt are about to get blown up before their match even has taken place. They are puffing and weezing as they run through the jungle. Matt is doing better than Adam.] Matt: 'Look at you! You run like a girl! Run like a man! Lift your knees!' [Adam looks over at Matt and growls as he runs, continuing not to lift his knees.] As that's going on... [Darth von Doom is using his lighstaber to slash up all of the Comedy leaders using his evil Force powers of DOOM~! Once he's done with almost all of them, he leaves one for last...James von Doom the Handler. The Handler begs off as Darth von Doom force leaps into his direction.] Handler: But I created you! I am your true Master! [Darth von Doom then slashes him and the Handler falls in a very badly acted death sequence. Darth von Doom then deignites his lighstaber and has an evil look in his eye as he looks out the window to the lava before him.] * * * The Duel of the Heroes [Lord von Doom then notices that another shuttlecraft has landed nearby.] JV: Hmmm...I wonder who this might be? [Lord von Doom uses the Force to magically open up and von Doom goes out to the landing platform where he finds Adam and Matt, his two best friends, waiting for him with the lighstaber prototypes. Von Doom becomes very angry that his so-called 'friends' followed him there.] JV: YOU FOLLOWED ME HERE YOU LITTLE SLIMES! [Matt motions around.] Matt: Little slimes? LOOK AT WHAT YOU DID! YOU KILLED JUST ABOUT EVERYONE CLOSE TO YOU! Adam: Yeah. You pissed on us all and now, we're back to get some payback. [James turns his back to them.] JV: You must not understand. I have brought peace, freedom, and justice to MY new circus of comedy! Adam: Your new circus? Our allegiance is to the Island of Misfit Wrestlers! TO PROTECTING THEIR NATURAL HABITAT! [Darth von Doom pays him no mind.] JV: If you are not with me, then you are my enemies. [Matt and Adam ignite their prototype lightsabers.] Matt: We will do what we must. JV: You will try. [James then does a backflip in mid-air, igniting his lightsaber and charging at them both. The duel begins. The two best friends of James attack him from both sides but he is able to fight them off and push them both back into the conference room. While in the conference room, where there are bodies strewn about everywhere, Adam and Matt continue to attack as one but James is too powerful for them. James uses his lightsaber to knock away some debris and then uses the force to throw it at Adam.] Adam: CRAP~! [The duel then ends up in the control room and Darth von Doom pushes Matt and Adam back using the force. He then leaps and lunges at them with his lightsaber but misses and nails the control panel instead. Flashing lights start going off and they end up outside on the ravine in front of the lava coming out of the volcano. They all then look around as the entire facility begins falling apart.] Matt: Oh no... JV: Crap... Adam: Mommy... [The structure begins falling apart and going down into the river of lava below. Matt and Adam grab a hold of some cables and hold on for dear life. James grabs a cable and swings across, battling as they go on. Adam and then Matt jump, followed by James who is still relentless, attacking the moment his feet hit the ground. Matt and Adam attack James who almost is at the edge. They then leap off onto a moving lava boat. James follows, jumping at the last second before the facility falls into the lava. They float over the lava river.] JV: I should've know you all were not my real friends! Adam: James, The Mathematician is evil! JV: From my point of view, he's my only real friend! Matt: Then you truly are lost! [They continue to float over the lava river.] JV: This is the end for you, my friends. [James leaps over them both and comes down, almost falling off the boat they're on and continues to duel it out with them. Adam then leaps for the shore and then Matt follows him, both of them land. James stares a hole through them. He then positions himself to leap at them. They motion for him not to do it but he leaps anyway and they use their advantage. Adam slices one of James' legs off and Matt severs James' arm and the other leg.] Matt: You were supposed to help all the comedy wrestlers, not destroy them! Bring balance to the UWF, not leave it in darkness. JV: I HATE YOU! Adam: You were the Chosen One! You were supposed to guide us to the light, not leave us in darkness! [von Doom claws at the ground as he suddenly bursts into flames. Adam and Matt can't watch, so they just simply throw up and leave. To James, the stench of the barf is worse than the burns of the flames. Adam and Matt then run off toward their ship as another ship lands. This one with the Horny Mathematician and his Circus of DOOM~!] * * * RISE, LORD VON DOOM On the volcano... [Barely alive, James is rescued by the Mathematician, and brought to an evil medical facility. There he is kept alive, submerged in healing liquid, limbless and horrifically deformed. James, burned within an inch of his life is resuscitated by the Mathematician's personal medical droids, who heal him by building him into an armored suit that keeps him alive. Now more machine then man, the new and improved Darth von Doom. Darth von Doom arises from the table where they were working on him.] Darth von Doom: Where...where am I? [Darth von Doom looks over at the Mathematician.] Mathematician: You arae alive, von Doom. But now, you can create objects and characters only using your mind instead of relying on your own creativity. You now are the ultimate weapon... [Darth von Doom lunges out of his restraints and raises his hands into the air.] Darth von Doom: BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA~! [The Mathematician smiles at the evil creation that he has made.] * * * Killer Pirates Later that day... [The Killer Pirates have docked their boat on a remote island after escaping the zombie death squads under the command of the Horny Mathematician and his henchman James von Doom. They are sitting on the beach, staring out at the sea when they see a shuttlecraft heading in their direction. The craft sees them and hovers down to the ground on the beach. Jack and the 'Killer Klown' both ready themselves.] Jack: Be ready, mate. It might be the Mathematician and his goons. KKFOS: Rightiando. [The doors on the shuttlecraft open and out come Adam and Matt. They smile and stroll over to the Pirates, who greet them.] Jack: Glad to see we weren't the only ones who made it. Adam: What's the casualty report? Jack: Unknown. Matt: Damn von Doom and Mathematician. All of our friends now are either dead or in hiding... [They all look grim until Jack smiles.] Jack: No worries mate... [Jack pulls something out from behind him.] Jack: We've got rum. [And they all spend the rest of the night drinking rum.] Cut End Episode III
