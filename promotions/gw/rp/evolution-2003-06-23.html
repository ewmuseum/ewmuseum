Evolution

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/23/03 'Oh yes, Evolution is upon us. Only a few short hours away and the show will being. I remember what occurred last week. How could I forget the cold scaley hand you gripped around my throat. The feeling of being hoisted high into the air, and then slammed onto the canvas. The roar of the crowd as my body bounced off the mat, and I layed there in pain. Oh it was wonderful!' The scene fades into a extreme close up of a smile. Slowly the camera zooms out to a head shot of Ice's face. His long, shoulder length blond hair covering half of his face, and a sick smile ,glowing from his mouth, fills the picture. The camera again zooms out, and Ice's entire body, can now be seen. Ice doesn't say a word as he turns and walks away from the camera. The camera follows Ice as he makes his way towards a crowd of people who are standing in line waiting for tonight's Evolution. Ice leans against a tree in the outskirts of the parking lot, over-seeing the crowd. No one takes notice of the superstar, in his casual white t-shirt, and black jeans. Ice folds his arms, left over right, in front of his chest as he looks over the long line that winds around the arena. 'Look at that pathetic mass of humanity.' The camera turns and pans over the enormous mass for a short time, then turns it's focus back towards Ice. 'They work their fat asses off day in and day out to come to this place, and spend their hard earned money for nothing! Nothing! Look at that card! Who in the hell booked that crap?! Doesn't matter. However what does matter is what is going to occur in that arena tonight. What is going to occur inside that ring. Because although I enjoyed every single minute of the exhilerating feeling of being chokeslammed, I did not appreciate the unprovoked attack.' A stern, serious expression resides over Ice's face. He steps away from the tree, and leans into the camera lens, staring, piercing a hole through its lens. 'This past Friday, I guarenteed that I would be victorious over both Angus the Bull, and Christopher Canada. After I kept my guarentee, Salomane Vation decides to walk his narrow ass down the aisle and proceed to take advantage of my weakened state. So I decided to show up tonight, and find out, just what Angus would do. To find out if Angus would accept the little bitch's proposal to be his wife. And after all that Bull was finished, I was going to see if Vation wanted to man up, face to face, and see if he could chokeslam me again, when I wasn't already in a twenty minute match. But apparently Salomane has taken a little trip, and isn't going to be at Evolution tonight. What a pity. Looks like someone got a little case of the limber tail. Looks like someone who wanted to make such a big impact, wanted to be known for something, wanted to get out of the shadow of darkness, and cold that I was casting, isn't going to be around to see any of that occur. Well Salomane, if you ever feel froggy again, step up to the plate, and your fate will be like the rest.' Ice leans back, away from the camera, raises his left hand, and slowly pulls his hair out from in front of his face. After gliding down the back of his neck, his hand slides around the side of his face, and Ice slowly begins to scratch the bottom of his chin. Ice takes in a long, deep breath, and slowly exhales it out. He tilts his head back and gazes up into the sky. 'Now seeing as I have stuck around this hell hole, and Salomane has decided to not show, I'm not going to waste my night. So be forewarned. Tonight, Evolution, all those involved, heed my warning. I am here. I am watching. I am listening. You won't know when, and you won't know where, but I will make my presence felt, one way or another. I could take action towards my opponent on Overload Venom, or remind Angus of what I did to him last week, or not do anything at all, and just leave you all sitting, waiting in anticipation like a pathetic puppet, waiting for its puppeteer to pull his strings.' Ice's gaze wanders down from the heavens above, and proceeds to make its way back over to the line of people, which has shortened quite a bit since last glance. The camera zooms in on Ice as he stares over the crowd as it continues to dwindle down. The scene fades to black.
