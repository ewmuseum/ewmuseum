Dividing Two Passions #4: Late Night Training #2

30 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 30th 2004 BANG! Suddenly a gun shot fills the air and a loud thud on the stony floor echoes around the corridor. There is silence. The silence extends. “Did you get him?” questions the shivering voice of Mrs. Arefiev. “Of course I got the little fucker, honey!” confidently beams Mr. Arefiev in the darkness. The hall lights flicker on. The corridor of the Arefiev Mansion is fully light. Standing at a corner in a dressing gown, confidently holding a steaming shotgun is Igor Arefiev’s father. Behind him, sticking her head around the corner is Mrs. Arefiev. She is also wearing a dressing gown. On the ground, ten feet away rolling around clutching his arm lies? ? ? ? Igor Arefiev. “SHIT!” shouts Igor’s father as he rushes to his son. Arefiev is rolling around on the floor clutching at his left arm. A look of real stress is across his face. Blood is seeping through his fingers. Almost immediately Mrs. Arefiev begins screaming. Igor’s father drops to his knees as his son’s side. Arefiev’s eyes are closed and he is unaware of his father’s close presence. “Igor! Look at me! Please Igor! I’m so sorry!” panics the old man. Constantino Zergolov suddenly appears from down the hall. “What happen?” he questions, as his eyes scan the scene. His head answers his own question. Mrs. Arefiev is now crying freely in the corner of the hall. Her hands are covering his face. Zergolov pulls Mr. Arefiev away from Igor. Constantino rips off the t-shirt he was wearing, to showcase his washboard stomach. He then rips at the bottom of the shirt, creating a makeshift bandage. He falls to his knees. “Igor! Give me your arm!” Mr. Arefiev stands back looking down at his son. He is silent. “Mum” What happened?” Rushing around the corner appears the youngest Arefiev, Anna. She drops to her mother’s aid without taking in the rest of the surroundings. “I heard a gun shot and I” She stops as she turns her head to face the three in the corridor. Zergolov is putting the finishing touches to his bandage on Arefiev’s arm. Igor has stopped rolling around but seems to be a state of shock. Mr. Arefiev is silent. He is still holding the steaming gun. “What happened?” mumbles Anna, her eyes wide open with disbelief. Mrs. Arefiev continues to cry. Her words are mumbled. Mr. Arefiev doesn’t reply. His attention is now turned to the gun he holds, and then back to his injured son. Zergolov is currently in life-saving mode. He has no time to answer questions of the rest of the family. “Do you have a licence for that gun?” enquiries Constantino. He tilts his head up towards Igor’s father. There is no reply. “Does he have a licence for that gun?” asks Constantino, now directing his question to Mrs. and Anna Arefiev. There is no reply. “DOES ANYONE KNOW IF THERE IS A LICENCE FOR THAT GUN”!”” screams Zergolov. “What does it matter?” sharply replies Anna. “Well,” begins to explain Constantino, “if there is no licence for the gun, he can’t go to the hospital?” “Why?” “Because then, your dad will go to prison?” explains Zergolov. Anna nods. Mrs. Arefiev cries even louder than before, at the thought of her husband going to prison. “I” I? I? didn’t?” mumbles Igor’s father. “Why would he go to prison?” enquiries Anna. “Well, how do we explain the big bleeding wound?” grumbles Zergolov. “I” didn’t? mean? I didn’t? know?” continues Mr. Arefiev as he mumbles. Zergolov glares at Mr. Arefiev, then down at Arefiev. Igor is now unconscious. “Shit!” snaps Zergolov. He slaps at Igor’s face. “I” I? don’t? I? know? mean? to?” Zergolov again glares at his tag team partner’s father. “What is he doing?” snaps Constantino. “He’s in shock!” argues Anna. “Then get him out of it!” Anna sighs. She stands to her feet, and takes her father’s arm. “Come on dad!” whispers Anna, “you better come with me!” Mr. Arefiev nods and walks with his youngest daughter around the corner. He is still carrying the gun that he shot at his son. Constantino and Igor are now alone in the corridor as Anna takes both parents with her away from the pair. “Am I dead?” enquiries Arefiev as he rolls over. His eyes partially open. He peers into his tag team partner’s eyes. Zergolov smiles and shakes his head. “No” But you probably will be at Whiplash!” Arefiev groans and turns away from his tag partner. He reaches over and feels the once-white-now-red bandage. He groans again. “What are we going to do?” asks Arefiev, “I can hardly fight” Can I?” Zergolov shakes his head. “I guess I’ll have t take your place” At least have a go?” Igor grimaces and grabs his arm. “Is this the bullet still in me?” Zergolov shakes his head. “Nope” The bullet hit the wall over there!” Zergolov points at the wall behind him. Arefiev strains to see where he is pointing and then groans as he gives up.
