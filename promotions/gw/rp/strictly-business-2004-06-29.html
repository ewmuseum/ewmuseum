Strictly Business

29 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/29/04 A gleaming glass door swings open as J-Gonz, outfitted in a solid black suit, including his tie, burst through. His two henchmen behind him, both adorned in solid white suits and solid white ties. On his left stands Paco holind an oozi near his chest, to J-Gonz's right is Chocolate holding a small barrel 12-guage. The three men march in and catch Roberto Gomez off guard. The chunky, bald druglord almost falls off his chair when he sees the three men standing in front of him. Gomez's henchmen don't even have time to react, they turn and back up against the wall. 'Well look what we have hear, we have fat baldy and his two queers.' Paco and Chocolate hold there weapons out as the two other henchman make a move to grab theres. 'W-W-What brings you here Mariachi?' J-Gonz scratces his head and pulls out a small 9-MM out of his side pocket and lays it on the desk. Roberto Gomez swallows hard and looks up at J-Gonz. 'I told you from the beginning Gomez, I'm not one to screw over. I told you from the start that you were either going to be loyal or you were going to be dead. 'I don't know what you're talking about...Mariachi all I've done has been for you. I haven't broken my word. I'm a man of my word!' 'Now theres one thing I hate more than a man that can't keep his word, and that's someone that wants to take me for a fool Gomez. Right now, you've done both.' 'What are you talking about Mariachi? I've kept my word. Look sit down, tell your men to put there weapons away. lets talk it over.' 'My men aren't putting anything away. We're talking now Gomez and you want to take me for a fool.' 'No Mariachi!' Paco pulls the trigger to his oozi and it launches a fury of fire and nails one of Robertos henchman in the arm and another bullet goes to the head. The henchman falls to the ground. The other henchman sweating and shaking holds his hands up. Roberto looking more nervous then before looks over at Paco who now has the oozi pointed at him.' 'Now listen Roberto. We had a deal, you and me. I get 50-50 of what you get...' 'I do...you get 50...' SMACK! 'Now listen Roberto. Theres been a leak and someone has some information that only you and I would have. There are papers floating around right now that could ruin me, that could send me back to the hole. Now Roberto, you have to options...no make that one. Tell me why you gave out that information!' 'Mariachi...I didn't!' Roberto has enough time to utters those words, and then a solid lead bullet leaves a his head split right down the middle. Chocolate looks over at J-Gonz who nods in approval. 'All I wanted was an answer Roberto and you couldn't give me that. You left me like a hooker without an orgasm. Stupid Roberto! 'Boss, what do you want us to do about him? J-Gonz looks over at the other henchman that is shaking in fear. 'Whats your name?' 'Loco...' 'You want a job?' The man nods his head furiously as J-Gonz motions for him to follow them out. All four men head out of the building. Again the door glimmers at it opens. This time however there are four men coming out. J-Gonz reaches into his pocket and he flips his vibrating phone up. 'We just took him out babe.' '....' 'What do you mean there were cameras?' '...' 'So Roberto had nothing to do with it?' '...' 'Well I can't say the same for Roberto...' '...' 'Two matches...What? Well babe we'll be back soon. We have to hit the club before we head back.' J-Gonz hops into his limosine with the other three men. The driver taps his brake lights and goes down the road. --- 'Look I don't care what you do...you find him and you bring him to me. Man this wrestling thing is starting to get int the way of the business. But atleast the workload is down some, except for this week when I got two matches.' 'Well atleast you took care of that Sherwin punk boss.' 'You're right Paco. And Pedro revealed his true colors and I got a great attorney working for me now. Honestly, how stupid was that Rich Sherwin character to think he could trust a lawyer with the name of Pedro Gomez? I hope he's not mad that his daddy is dead! Ha Ha!' A knock at the door startes both Paco and J-Gonz. The both men turn to see Pedro Gomez standing there dressed in a blue suit and holding a solid black suitcase. 'Speaking of the devil! Sit down bro...take a load off.' 'Why'd you do it...' 'Do what?' 'Don't play stupid with me Gonz...' Paco stands up and grabs Pedro and slams him against the wall. 'Let him go Paco. He has a right to know...have a seat Pedro.' 'I just want to know.' 'Well it was an accident. We made a mistake. Word leaked out and somebody had to take the fall. Your dad was the most likely canidate and we had to take him out before the whole job came down hard and fast.' 'And what...why him?' 'It's business Pedro. It's business.' 'Yeah business...' Pedro stands up and walks out of the room slamming the door behind him. 'Hey he took it better then I though...but eh, he'll come back he needs a job!' 'Yeah boss. Hey what are you going to do this week boss, you have two matches.' 'I don't know yet, I'm thinking I can buy the second match. I heard a rumor that giving McNasty a few dollars for some Samual Adams usually is enough. But I don't know. I might have someone take him out or hey, I'm a bad bad man so I might just do two matches.' 'Things are getting harder aint they boss? With that Train guy.' 'Train...oh that fool with the pictures. Well it's like a game of chess Paco. Right now he has the upperhand, but hey next week who knows...I'm gonna have him by the balls.' 'Ah boss...' Just then another knock and then Chocolate enters the room. 'Boss, a certain Ken Partridge Junior is here to see you.' 'Ah let him in. Paco, excuse us my friend. Business.' J-Gonz stands up and walks to the door, Paco slips out as KPJ walks in and stares at J-Gonz. 'El Mariachi...' 'This is all about business!' The door closes and the scene fades out.
