Coming on board...

9 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/9/05 What's this? A man comes into your world and rocks it. A man who's an unknown to the Global arena, comes in to your main event last week, to help someone out after they were given the good old screw job attempt, and he not only causes the Main Event to have one of the highest Nielsen ratings in quite some time, but he's supposed to be a one shot deal. An indy wrestler, a man who recently left another federation to get his life together... Blaise William Todd McClancy, a man who is known as 'Hazard'. A term that means many things. haz’ard n. 1.A chance; an accident. 2.A chance of being injured or harmed; danger: Space travel is full of hazards. 3.A possible source of danger: a fire hazard. 4.Games. A dice game similar to craps. 5.Sports. An obstacle, such as a sand trap, found on a golf course. *Provided by Dictionary.com He takes the name based on the place where he grew up, the home of Global itself... Las Vegas, Nevada. A dice game, similar to Craps. Very fitting, since it's the Old English term for Craps. Some dictionaries try to hide the fact that it was fully what now is one of the hottest games of chance in the casinos. But the other meaning are there, and they're things that he tries to live off of in the ring. He is dangerous. He is something that most don't like to meet. His old home, he worked for his family. It got to be quite a bit when every time he would win a title, either with his girlfriend Ukyo in his corner as a partner or as a valet, someone would scream foul that he was getting stuff because of his family association with the owners. But last week, he showed the world by taking on one of Global's best, that he isn't a fluke. Maybe that's why Global has made a move that some might not have guessed they would. For is you look at the booking for this week's upcoming show... Blaise is on there. But it was only for one night? Global thought they would be safe to only have one night of him. But no, something happened. Why would Global let something like that pass through their fingers? They're not stupid, are they? Virgil Ambrosi, one of the two who were on the other side against the man this last week, gets a chance to pay back Blaise for what he did after they attempted to use a third person to secure their win. And the Global mainstay, Brick... a man who's just recently come back and has problems with the Hunting Grounds, specifically one Christian Zenith. But this match has a prize in it. For the winner will be the top contender for the new unified Platinum Title. A man in his second match in Global is being given the same chance as the former Platinum Champion, and a man who has done almost everything there is to do in Global. Why? You want to see why, just watch the replay of last week's show, and you'll know why. And if you don't want to do that, I invite you to 'hazard' a guess as to the answer. There is one question that will need to be answered by both of these men... Are they really willing to 'Take the Risk' of being in the ring with a man who's had the shackles taken off. A man who's got something to really prove. A man who once had the world in his hand when it came to the ring. Most will only remember his time in his cousin's owned federation. But there are some that will remember his performance in PTC's GTT4 last year, having to pull out only because of other obligations. Some would remember when he was the mainstay, the staple to a network of Federations that were under the umbrella of Revolution Studios. Their highest paid champion, on their highest rated show, in their highest rated federation. Their longest reigning World Champion, a title actually called the Superior Championship. He was the epitome of that moniker for that federation. A few years out of the spotlight? Yes. But now it's time to get back in, and climb back up to the top. The first two parts of the foundation of this new era in the man who's first name means “Firebrand” will be built in with brick, and the very will and nectar of the gods. But not everything is all wrestling or fighting in that octagon ring of Global. There are some things that are beyond even the most renowned and worldly people who follow the weekly soap opera of the likes of Shadow and Emoticon, Zenith and Brick, Elia Smith and her demons, and the like... ^^A Retrospect: Last week after the end of Global's Evolution...^^ Blaise was just finishing up using the locker room to get cleaned up to leave the show. Ukyo was already changed from the special dress she had worn to valet him, and both were now sporting their usual jeans and t-shirt look that they wore on the outside world. The red-haired, green-eyed Amerindian tossed the last bit of stuff into his over sized gym bag and then sat down to finish tying on his moccasin boots. Ukyo was ready, and waiting for him. “Well, this was a nice diversion for it all. At least we have a certified nanny available when we need one.” Blaise just smiled as he finished tying his second boot. “Although I found it funny that Kaylee was in the area and would offer like that. I know she was the first nanny for Fey's babies a year ago. But what are Annie and Kaylee doing here” Do you have any clue?” Blaise put his foot down and made sure the soft leather was in place and conforming around his foot before putting his weight down on it. “I have an idea about it.” He said that just as he looked up, and then he greeted the one that walked into his locker room without knocking. “Princess Leanan, how nice of you to join us. I take it you wanted to come see me tonight. Although I can see that the manners of courtesy still haven't brushed off on you.” Annie smiled wide at him with her white, perfect teeth that contrasted with her raven black hair. “Ah, but Prince Turren, ye know tha' I'll always take a cheap look at one o' th' males in yer family. I'm sorry I showed up t' late t' see ye changin'.” The two called each other by their titles for what they called their mantles. Aine “Annie” Leanan was a business woman on the outside, the side she showed the human world all the time. But underneath all of that she was a member of a race of beings that most humans in the old world called “Fairies”. She was a Leanan, specifically, a creature that was known as a water spirit from eastern Ireland that was known for driving men insane with but a kiss from her lips. As she's one of their kind that cares very little about others, and the world... only about her own fun and getting her stuff done, she's a member of the court known as the Unseelie. Blaise, on his father's side, is a member of the species called the Tautha De Dannan to their kind. A race of beings connected to nature and also a member of the Seelie court. His specific kind makes his full heritage a bit of a battle of the opposites, as he's what the Greeks called a dryad or wood spirit. Forget the whole thing about them only being women, the Irish knew all to well what they really were. Then again, when was the last time you had giant oak trees in Greece. They worshiped the damn things in Ireland. His other half is something completely different, though. A cold, hard killer that allowed Blaise McClancy, the bearer of the mantle of Turren MacNauda to become something in the organization he works for outside of the wrestling ring... what they call a Hunter. A being that actually goes out of his way at night to find creatures that aren't human who have violated the rules imposed upon them all about hiding behind what they call the veil. Those that violate those rules, and show the human world what they truly are without the possible premise of being television camera tricks... are removed from their corporeal existence, sometimes at the very blades of Mr. McClancy and Ms. Kushinaga. For Annie, she works in the Order's research department. She actually works directly under Blaise's cousin, Fey who is also known as the Mistress of Secrets after her wedding to the person in charge of the Order a bit over two years ago. At one time, Annie was almost to become the wife of Fey's brother, but that fell through. Sean and she didn't fully mix well. But Annie was still in a place of at least the same rank as Blaise, and occasionally she took that to mean she could ignore rank all together. “What do I owe this intrusion to, Ms. Leanan?” Blaise was a straight shooter as it was. Annie feigned being injured, covering her heart with both hands. “Ye 'urt me Prince Turren, ye 'urt me. Ye know tha' I'd only come bargin' in with yer Kami woman 'ere if I 'ad business t' conduct. An only if such.” She used the term Kami, the Japanese word for spirit for Ukyo as if it was almost an insult. However, since that's what Ukyo was, it wasn't that much of one. Annie still had a hard time grasping the non-western world and people who were from it. “What's your business then?” “I wanted t' ask ye what ye thought of my little group 'ere?” “What little group?” “Th' Brands an' Ms. Northsky.” Blaise slung his gym bag over his shoulder, showing that he didn't have much time for this. “What do you want to know” The younger Brand looks like a real temper case. He needs to learn control. The Older Brand acts like he's got a stick up his ass. The girl, Dani, seems nice, but a bit naive.” Annie chuckled. “Yer rather astute. Do ye not recognize them at all?” Blaise shook his head to indicate the negative as he spoke. “Nope.” Annie didn't believe that one. “Ye've na' 'eard th' buzz around th' Order channels abou' our find 'ere in the desert” Abou' our little 'Angel'?” Blaise squinted his eyes at her. “No, I've not. I've been kinda pre-occupied with legal battles and finding a new house here over the last few weeks since gaining my freedom.” Annie sighed. “So how did it feel?” “How did what feel?” She gave him a serious look. “Look, Blaise. Ye know damn well wha' I’m askin'. Ye jus' mentioned yer freedom. Ye were workin' fer Sean, 'imself up in Seattle. I'm na' dumb. I read the papers, Fey 'erself granted yer release. I've checked in on things, an' I know abou' yer legal troubles over yer little girls. Bu' one thing, ye came 'ere t'nigh' t' 'elp ou' a few people who were in trouble... 'Ow did it feel t' get int' tha' ring and jus' let loose an' try yer damnedest t' win?” Blaise sighed this time, before giving her an answer. He hesitated a few seconds, appearing to roll it through his mind a bit then he gave the answer that she was expecting. “It felt pretty damn good.” “Well, I'm glad. Because it could lead t' a bit more fer ye 'ere. Yer makin' yer move back t' Vegas a permanent one?” “As permanent as I can make it. My Kaylie and Kiley, both of them need stability for these next few years. They're young and need to not be moved around anymore. First I just need to get full custody of them.” “Well, I guess it's time tha' I cut t' th' chase then. I need ye on this assignment.” Blaise shook his head with how quickly the Dark Fairy had changed her tune. “What” Why?” Annie sat down on the bench across from him. “I've ga' somethin' tha' could turn int' a mess 'ere. An' yer th' perfect addition t' keep things a bit under control.” Blaise sat down, Ukyo stood behind him. “Alright, I'm getting interested, fill me in.” Annie reached into her large purse and pulled out a couple of papers. “Th' girl, Dani, she's a fokin' throw back. Confirmed b' Suriel itself. She's seven generations from a Sioux legend called Morning Star. A being tha' came down from th' sky an' mated with one o' 'er people.” “Sounds like the Neophilim, the Grigori, and that, hub bub. The two hundred angles that took a permanent vacation for a little action on this world.” Annie chuckled, a bit uncontrollably with the way Blaise put it. He wasn't anything like his Irish cousins. But then again, he'd grown up in Sin City, USA. “Well, seein' as 'ow this girl recently 'ad a bit o' trouble an' somethin' 'as moved t' protect 'er. The symbol tha' it uses is tha' of one of' those fallen, lustful angels. One named Kokabel. We've been tryin' t' find all o' the legends around it all. Everything, we don't know wha' culture to use. Bu' she's got some enemies from the looks of it. Yer th' only 'unter I know of t' 'ave faced one of the fallen from 'eaven and won...” She knew about Blaise's trials from last year, where the fallen Arch Angel Zadikiel was returned to the spirit world by Blaise himself. But it wasn't the power of the Tautha that had made it happened, but by Blaise embracing both sides of his blood, including that of the beast within, the Wendigo of the Paiute Indians. “Okay” What is she? She's not exactly big enough to be a gibborim. She's no giant half breed.She's full grown, right?” Annie's eyes went big as she explained it. “She's fokin' winged in 'er true veiled form. She's a bloody angel tha' was born on this earth. We've never encountered one tha' was seven generations away. We know tha' 'umans usually toss back a bit in their genetic ladder every time tha' number is reached. 'Er fifth great grand father was a Virtue in charge o' the movement o' the stars in the sky. Th' thing is, it appears tha' 'e was allowed back int' the graces o' th' Creator after th' flood. So we don'na fully know wha' s goin' on there.” Blaise looked a bit shocked. He knew all about Angels from his run in with the Archangel of Destiny. But he was moving into business mode. “What about the other two?” Annie moved to answer him. “Th' younger Brand, Josh, is the son o' Margarette. Our 'ead researcher in Seattle. She was th' second wife o' a Saquamish Native man. 'E appears t' share some traits with 'is mother. I'm sure you've met 'er before.” Blaise just nodded an affirmation. ?'E appears t' be empathic like 'er. Josh an' Dani are a bit more though. They're na' unlike ye an' Ms. Kushinaga 'ere.” Ukyo blushed a bit. She was still a bit naive herself when it came to her relationship with Blaise. “And the older Brand, Gray?” Annie really got a serious look for that one. ?'E's only a year 'er two older then ye. 'Is 'air color is a byproduct o' 'is secret side. Ye an' 'e' share somethin' in common. Where ye are the inheritor o' the spirit of th' Wendigo. 'e is the inheritor o' the spirit of th' Get o' Fenrir.” Blaise got a confused look on his face. “But he's native?” “Ah, yes. Bu' in tha' area, quite a bit back, there was a landin' o' People from Northern Europe, Scandinavians. It appears tha' one o' them brought th' curse with them. An' someone on 'is mother's side, th' first wife of the elder Brand, 'ad contracted it. Tha' stick up 'is arse, as ye called it, is 'is attempt t' control 'is anger. 'E's a former teacher a' the Academy 'ere in th' states. An' 'e's th' Guardian assigned t' Ms. Northsky.” Blaise shook his head. “So he's the other one” That makes this quite interesting. So what do you need?” Annie smiled. “I can'na be 'ere all th' bloody time. I need someon' t' take charge o' this when I'm na' 'ere. Someone who knows th' rules an' procedures o' the Order. Bu' I 'ave a feelin' tha' th' others, th' Grigori, will na' take kindly t' Dani's existence. I need someone who knows 'ow t' fight them if needed.” “What in it for me?” Annie got a shocked look on her face. But Blaise looked dead serious in his question. “Well, we're negotiatin', now” This request is comin' down from the Mistress o' Secrets 'erself. In includes th' proper respects of an assignment leader. Ye would be th' officer in charge, an' I'd go back t' being a researcher, an' na' a field officer. I'd go a'ead an' get everythin' setup fer ye. Yer new 'ouse an' everythin' . Ye'd need t' be a neighbor t' them, so ye could keep an eye on them. But I need them t' still be seperate. Let th' Order 'elp take care o' ye t'. I'll be leaving Kaylee 'ere with ye, t' act as a Nanny when ye need one.” “And what will I tell the courts?” Annie laughed. “Why Mr. McClancy, since when 'ave ye ever 'ad a problem with anythin' o' the sort” Yer na' exactly broke. Ye never 'ave been. If ye 'ever need a spokesperson fer th' company, ye 'ave them call me. Alriogh'?” Blaise looked to ponder it for a few moments. “Blaise, I don'na 'ave all nigh'. Neither do ye. I'm promisin' ye a job tha'll keep ye in the good graces o' the Order, an' keep ye assigned 'ere t' be with yer baby girls.” Ukyo squeezed Blaise's shoulder a bit, and he turned to look at her. All she did was nod that if he was up for it, she was too. He then turned back to Annie. “Alright, I'm good for it. You get cracking on your part of the deal.” Annie stood up and smiled at him. “Very well, Lieutenant...” Blaise looked up at her. “I'm only a sergeant in the Order.” Annie smiled wide. “Wrong, I told ye... ye'd be the officer in charge. I was given the authority if he accepted t' grant ye the priveledges needed to carry ou' yer duties. Yer in charge o' a cell now, a field officer. Get used t' it. Get yer backside back t' yer 'otel an' let me 'ave me 'and maiden back so I can get workin' on yer stuff. Tomorrow we meet fer details.” Blaise nodded to her, as she then gave a slight bow with her head. “I'll see ye then, Prince Turren.” Annie turned and left the locker room. Blaise stood back up and adjusted the strap on his bag, so he could carry it. He took Ukyo's hand in his own and they started to walk out. As they approached the exit to the parking garage, they're interrupted by Ms. Noi Tsunami, with a ragged and haggered look about her. She appears to have run down in her high heeled shoes. “Mr. McClancy...” Blaise stops, and addresses her. “Yes?” Noi takes a moment to catch her breath. “Would you happen to have a moment” Ms. Sights would like to see you up in her office.” Blaise doesn't look to be too concerned with it all. “Maybe later. I've got to get back to my children.” Noi tries to implore him a bit. “Mr. McClancy, I'll have to ask if you could call and make arrangements. Ms. Sights has been dealing with a conference call with the committee ever since they saw you in the ring tonight. You're not exactly a small name to suddenly just come in on a one time thing. I think they want to offer you a job with the federation. I know when she called me in to try to get me to find you, they were saying something about putting you in a match this next coming week.” “Look, Ms....”” “Noi, Noi Tsunami. I'm Global's head reporter on camera, and I do quite a bit of work behind the scenes.” “Okay, Ms. Tsunami, I'm here in Vegas to make it a permanent move. I'm not looking to get back on a travel schedule again. I have personal things to think about, as in my children.” Noi smiles wide at him. “Well Mr. McClancy, don't you need to make sure you've got an income also” The good news for you is... Global does everything from this arena, except for our Pay Per Views. That's the ONLY time that we travel. Basically one weekend or so a month to go someplace else to show off the product.” Blaise appears to be pondering that offer, when Ukyo speaks out. “C'mon, I'll call Kaylee and tell her that we'll be a bit longer. It can't hurt to at least hear the offer, now can it?” Ukyo, the voice of reason to him on a regular basis. Sometimes Blaise was as much a slave to his emotions as anyone could be. “Alright, Ms. Tsunami, lead the way.” ^^Back to today^^ There you see it, Blaise had to make two big decisions in one night. He took it upon himself to become the field officer on the assignment of Danielle Northsky, a woman who's proven to be quite something else all together from what others thought. And then as we can see, his answer to Ms. Brandi Sights when it came to Global had to have been a yes as Ambrosi and Brick can see from the booking sheets for this coming week. Does Blaise still have what it takes to fight in such as this match up? Can he reclaim what he once was? Well for that answer look at the match last week. Yes, Shadow pinned him, but he also pinned her. She's the international champion, and has only not won something like 2 or 3 matches since she came on board the Global roster. The thing is, is Global ready for him? Blaise now stands in his new house, a week later. It's a large house, a block or so down the road from the one that the Brands and Northsky were set in by the Order. He's looking at a sheet of paper with numbers and a diagram on it as he's in the dining room for the place. Ukyo is standing in the kitchen, as she's putting away things. “So, what are you doing” You've been looking at that for a good hour or so.” “I'm trying to figure out how much insulation I need and that. I'm going to turn that room into a practice room. The insulation is so you don't have to listen to my guitar wailing at all hours of the day and night when I'm concentrating on things. The new furniture for the girl's room will be here in a few hours though. So I'm taking this time to concentrate on this, instead of heading out and just buying it.” Ukyo starts to put out some bottles and cans onto the counter top, before sliding them into one of the two refrigerators in a particular order. “What are you doing?” Ukyo smiles at him as she keeps working. “A little birdy told me that the girl, Dani, was planning on coming by today with a little house warming type thing. She's still a back woods homebody apparently. So I figured I'd have enough stuff here for us to handle a slight get together. I don't know their drinking tastes and that. Kaylee said that Dani was cooking up some stuff to bring over.” Blaise looked like he didn't really matter on the subject. “Oh, and Gray mentioned that their gym in their house was already setup with a ring and such, so if you need to use one to get ready, all you need to do is come over. They'll give us a key to the back door to the spa area, where it's located.” He just nodded as he worked on his paper. Then something comes flying over, and bounces off his head. “OW!” He grabs his head and looks in his lover's direction. “What was that for?” “You're ignoring me. Did you hear what I told you?” “Yeah, but you didn't exactly ask a question that required a response.” He smiled and dodged some odd shaped fruit that came in his direction. Quickly he came to his feet and headed into the kitchen where Ukyo was at. She moved to get away from him. “Don't you dare.” “What, I can't get you back?” She turned to try to get away, but he grabbed her by her arms, and spun her back around. As they then look each other in the eyes... A small voice comes from the other room, one asking something about... “having to go potty.” Ukyo smiles at him. “Saved by Kiley.” Blaise moves to go take care of it, but Ukyo puts her hands up to him. “No, I've got it. You handled the last call, at four thirty this morning.” Ukyo turns and heads into the master bedroom that's on the first floor. The door had been left open so they could hear the girls as they played in their play pen. Blaise shook his head slightly as he watched the tall, but beautiful Japanese woman take to helping out with two girls that weren't hers, but came from another woman who was in her place before her. Yup, things looked to be about right in today's world for him. Now to keep them that way.
