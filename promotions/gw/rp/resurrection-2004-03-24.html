Resurrection

24 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-24-04 A large hole dug deep sits in the middle of a dark valley. Sitting at the very bottom is an extremely purple, bruised Ace Harding. He has a swollen eye, bandages on his face, and looks almost inhuman. He sits with his legs forming a triangular shape, one arm stretched on top of them and the other in a sling. His shirt, face, arms, and legs are covered in dirt. His breathing is hoarse and heavy, as though he has some sort of lung disease. Ace: Do you have any idea what it’s like, to be me? Do you? Have you the slightest idea what it feels like to be so deep in a hole, only to discover it was you yourself that dug the hole, and you yourself who stood in it until you could get out? That the concussion you have, the burning pain that sears through your flesh so badly you feel like you’re going to have a conniption, that EVERYTHING bad that’s every happened to you is ALL your FAULT, and NOBODY ELSE’S?! Ace breathes in and out a few times in agony. Ace: It’s an incredibly humble thing, to look in the mirror and see behind your own skin, deep into your own soul, and be disgusted with what you see. To have no lens, no mask to hide behind. To see you at your worst and most insignificant. Down in this whole, I see where I am. I see who I am. In Global Wrestling, I am a nobody. In Global Wrestling, I just lay down for somebody even lower than me. I’ve been doing this shit for ten years, but I just stayed down for somebody who wasn’t even on the radar! When I first got here, I played the whipping boy, I played the weak link. I was the joker of an otherwise flawless and credible deck of cards. I took a chokeslam off of a cage, when I was supposed to be the Enforcer. What kind of Enforcer constantly lies on his back for the ten count? Huh? What kind of “Elite” am I? I’m not an elite. I’m not even a fucking human BEING. I’m a worm, crawling through the fucking DIRT. Ace grabs a handful of wet, slushy dirt and grips it tightly in his hand, it’s black oozy slowly pouring between his fingers. Ace: Dirt is so cold. Cold as death. You ever want to know what death is like, just lie in a hole for a little while. Because that’s where I’ve spent the last 10 years of my life. I’m a fucking corpse that refuses to DIE. Ace flings the mud aside. Ace: But do you wanna know something? All of that shit is gonna change. For once in my life, I’m gonna take control. I’m gonna finally plant my foot down, and shove this fucking business up God’s ass. Ace rips off the cast from his arm. Ace: It’s about fucking time I show you stupid pieces of crap who the fuck I AM. Ace rips a bandage off of his face, showing his bloody, bruised, purple and red face, complete with stitches. Ace: Because behind this humble exterior is the power of a GOD?.the power of a being not from the same Earth you know and love. Inside me is a demon waiting to be unleashed, a soul tarnished by everything that is dark about humanity. I am not Ace Harding. I am a fucking ELITE!!! Ace begins to climb out of the hole. He stands up and brushes himself off, a wild look in his eyes. Ace: It’s time to stop fucking around. I’ve dug my own grave for too long. It’s about goddamn time I climb out and finally live up to the bullshit I’ve been talking. I am going to enter that Global Rumble, and turn the entire federation on its head. When I am best, is when I’m thrown into a situation I did not expect. It is said that a sudden and profound change is the most horrifying thing a man can experience. On Monday night, in that Global Rumble, many sudden changes will occur all at once, but the BIGGEST change of them all will be that the man you see right now, staring down the gaping maw of his grave, will no longer be a man. He will no longer be a wrestler. He will no longer even be a living, breathing, BLEEDING human being?.he will be an ELITE’and there isn’t a damn thing anybody can do about it? Ace spits in the deep hole and kicks some dirt into it, then walks away.
