My way is the best way

3 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 3rd, 2005 'The Symbol of Supremecy, The Professor of Perfection, The Bronx Bomber, The Inovator of Intellect , and the often imitated but never duplicated, Man With Talent on Loan From God' 'Primtime' Eddie Ross rolls off those words with precision, and arrogance. Each description is accompinied by a growing smile. He knows he dosnt have to say this. He knows everyone knows this about him already. However, he isnt the type to miss a chance to rub it in, and when your in a match with eighteen other guys, its nice to make sure they all know where they stand. Obviously, he feels they definatly dont stand on his level. '19,18,17. . . .so on and so forth. In a match starting with 19 and ending with 1 alot needs to happen.'1' has to have alot of things go his way, alot of luck on his side. He probably will rely on help from many other people to get him through. Thats the only way someone wins a Gauntlet match right? With a little luck mixed with some help?' Eddie brings his hand up to his chin and strokes it a bit as he ponders some deep thoughts. Trying to find the exact words which will be the vessel with which his message is delivered. 'You see though it takes all that for the normal man to win this type of match, but I am nothing close to normal. I am above normal, above average, not generic, but superior. I do things no one else even thinks they are capable of. I dont take the easy way, I take my way. Thats why I'm asking to be entry number 1 in the Gauntlet. I want there to be no doubt that i stand a cut above the rest of these 'new blood'. I need not either luck or help. I just need the opportunity.' Eddie nods his head in approval and confidence of the words he speaks. 'So this message is to you Suits in the back. We can go about this the easy way or the hard way. You brought me here because you knew I was something special, that I stood out. I dont want you to do the spineless thing and try to protect your investment by having me come out last. You make me a star, or I will myself. Trust me, its in the best interest of you and your league to just be on my side. I rest assured you will make the right decision. You already made one great on by signing me, and you will make another at the PPV. It's the best option. . . .trust that.' The scene fades with Eddie Ross' eyes locked in.
