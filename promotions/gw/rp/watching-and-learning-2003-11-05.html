Watching and learning

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-5-03 Normally, on afternoons like this, Brick watches videos of matches in order to prepare himself. Not this time. This time, he is studying a recent interview of the current World champion, Ice. It holds a certain personal stake, since it was Ice who eliminated Brick from the main event at the last PPV event, Maximum Carnage. Since then, Brick has been hoping for the opportunity to face Ice for the World title, for a chance to regain the glory he worked four long years to achieve. So far, nothing has happened. “I can’t believe I’m sitting here with you watching this,” groans Aaron as he sits next to Brick. “Yeah, well, I’m not exactly holding you here at gunpoint,” Brick replies. “If you want, you can go home to your wife and daughter.” “I probably should, but we’re supposed to be looking at more cover designs for the new album.” “I know. I just want to see the rest of this interview.” “Why” What’s it gonna tell you that you can’t learn watching a match?” “I can get a glimpse of Ice’s mindset.” “You already know his mindset. He’s a crazy motherfucker.” Phenus: Moving on. How did if feel to win the Global Wrestling Heavyweight Championship of the World? Ice: I’ve been in a lot of companies, and have been screwed out of my fair share of World Titles. So for it to finally happen, against 7 of the best this business has to offer. On such a big stage, and in such a crazy match, it meant a lot. Being able to call myself World Champ, is me sticking it to all those promoters who screwed me over in the past. Or those who said I couldn't do it. I pride myself on being the best. Holding a title doesn't always mean it. But everyone who enters this business aspires to hold the World Championship once. I now have done that. I always felt I was the best before, this is right here, is proof. I can honestly now say, I have nothing left to prove to ANYONE. Phenus: Who do you see as being the biggest threat to your title reign? Ice: My unborn child and its mother. Ha-ha. Seriously though, I believe, that there isn't a single person in this company, ONE on ONE, that can beat me. Rokku, Jin, Holmes are all tough competitors, but I just don't think they have what it takes. The only thing, in my mind, that could end this title reign, is something happening at home, causing me to leave, or lose focus on the task at hand. “Cocky mother?” “Shhh?” Ice: Ha-ha..... Phenus: With Showdown in Tokyo 4 coming up in just under a month. Who do you see yourself facing to retain your coveted championship? Ice: Any of the three I mentioned before, are the Top Contenders in my mind. I know Rokku feels we have some unfinished business. Jin and I haven't closed the book on our feud. And I know Deacon wants his title back. But honestly, I don't really care. I won't duck anyone. “He didn’t even mention me.” “So what do you think that means?” “It means he doesn’t consider me a threat to his title.” Just then Brick begins to smile. “Perfect.”
