Reunion

14 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/14/03 It was two in the morning when the knock came at his door. Two in the morning when he was awakened from a fitful, but much-needed sleep. Two in the morning when Akodo Rokku's eyes snapped open and saw only ceiling. He ignored the pounding at first, hoping that it was just a figment borne of dreams and the midnight storm's rolling thunder. Hopefully, the sound of the rain on his windows would drown it out. But as he stared at his ceiling, the pounding continued. Grimacing, Rokku rolled out of bed and pulled on a kimono. Hustling across his town house, the Steel Lion stopped just as he reached for the doorknob and winced at a sharp pain in his bandaged ribs -- a reminder of his new 'friends', The Souls of the Black Lotus. With a bitter grumble, Rokku unlocked and swung open the door. She was silhouetted by the hallway light, wet T-shirt and jeans clinging to her like a second skin. Rokku had to blink, hard, and still wasn't sure she was real until she dropped her bags and threw her arms around his neck. 'Lion,' whispered Yoshiko, blinking rain -- or was it tears? -- out of her eyes, 'Oh god Rokku, you're alive!' Tentatively, Rokku wrapped his arms around Yoshiko, pulling her close? then staggered back a step as pain lanced through his injured ribs. Yoshiko looked Rokku in the eyes, then reached out and pushed aside his kimono, revealing his bandaged midsection. She brushed her fingers over the bandages and frowned. 'They got to you already, didn't they?' Rokku raised an eyebrow. 'How'd you even know anyone was after me? No, don't answer that yet. It's great to see you 'shiko, and I'm sure you have a fine story to explain why you're here -- but you need to go dry off first. Yoshiko's lips curled up into a smile. 'Ever gallant, Lion. Worried I might catch cold?' 'That,' replied Rokku, smirking, 'and you're dripping all over my hardwood floor.' Yoshiko rolled her eyes. 'I fly across the planet for you, and you're worried about your floor?' 'Well also, you're kind of poking through your shirt there.' Yoshiko's eyes widened. She immediately snatched up her bags, and made a beeline for Rokku's bathroom. An instant later, Rokku heard the sound of a hair dryer. Chuckling to himself, Rokku fished a bottle of sake from his cupboard and poured two cups. A few minutes later Yoshiko returned looking very little like the waterlogged creature which had appeared at the Steel Lion's door not long ago. Wrapped in a blue silk kimono, with her freshly brushed hair cascading over he shoulders in waves, she was as lovely as ever. Rokku couldn't help but admire the view as he held out one of the cups. 'Drink?' 'God yes.' Smiling, Rokku handed over the cup of sake, then grabbed the bottle and the other cup, and retired to the living room. Yoshiko followed, and the two of them settled down on the couch. 'Okay 'shiko,' said Rokku, refilling her freshly drained cup, and then his, 'tell me what's going on.' Yoshiko took a sip of the sake, and then a deep breath. 'It began about a week ago. Ever since you left, the Thunders have been doing their best to keep Genbu in check. I hired a computer hacker to keep an eye on his internet logs and phone records -- and it's a good thing I did, or I wouldn't be here right now. About a week ago, Genbu received a call from the SomaCorp office in Dallas.' Rokku's eyes widened. 'SomaCorp? That son of a bitch?' Yoshiko nodded. 'Anyway, almost immediately after that, he called an unlisted number -- which, with the right palms greased and favors cashed in, I managed to track down. It was an arcade. An arcade that, if you ask the right people, you'll find out is a front for Yakuza operations. I immediately went about hiring people to tap Genbu's phone after that, hoping that he might talk to whoever was on the other end of that line again. And he did -- a day later they called him. All the voice said was 'Your price is acceptable. The Souls are ready.'' 'The Souls of the Black Lotus,' said Rokku, clenching his jaw. 'Right. I immediately started digging up everything I could find about the Souls -- and the more I find the worse things sounded. So I packed my bags and caught the first flight out here. I had hoped I might get to you before they did but?' Yoshiko's eyes trailed to the bandages around Rokku's midsection. 'Yeah,' grumbled Rokku, 'you didn't. I met them just a few days ago, in fact. Good kids, if a little psychotic.' Yoshiko frowned again. 'Well Lion, I'm here now. And I plan on staying here as long as you need my backup. Er? you do have a spare room, right?' Rokku laughed. 'Yoshiko, I've got three. Pick your favorite.' Yoshiko smirked. 'So generous. I'm going to go get some sleep then, alright? It was a hell of a flight, and the sake's not exactly helping keep me awake?' 'Sure,' said Rokku, rising from his seat as Yoshiko did, 'I should get back to bed too.' Yoshiko picked up her bags, and started heading for one of the spare rooms. 'G'night Lion!' she called over her shoulder. 'G'night Crane,' Rokku replied. 'And, Yoshiko?' Yoshiko stopped in her tracks, and turned to look at Rokku. Rokku smiled softly. 'Thanks.'
