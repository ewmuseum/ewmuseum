The past can be very disturbing

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/6 <font color='Orange'Lisha...dead! Salaok....not the same anymore. Johnathon Davis....disappeared like usual. The rest of the Harvesters....not here. I, myself, will never be the same! Mark Kullypso repeating himself as he writes into his one true friend, Book. Book was given to him by a psychologist one day three years ago during Kullypso's inner battle with himself. With Book in hand, Kullypso could write down his inner most thoughts and fears for relief and sanctuary from the cruel world of reality. Throughout these years I have changed myself. No longer am I the father that I used to be. How can I? My two sons would hardly recognize me now! Nor would I recognize them! They've grown so much and spent most of their life without me. I missed out on so many firsts for them: their first words, the first time they walked, their first days of school. Leaning back against the wooden chair that he's sitting in with his head tilted back looking up at the ceiling. Kullypso wipes a single tear rolling down the left side of his cheek. Taking a break from his writing, Mark gets out of his chair and begins to look around at his surroundings. A dimly lit room, the only light is that coming from the streetlights piercing through the cracked wall directly in back of Kullypso. To the left of him is an old worn-out army cot and his bag of clothes. To the right is a paint-chipped brown door, a card table with some old pizza boxes stacked on top of it. After the small break to relax and calm himsel down, Kullypso sits back down in the wooden chair, pulls up to the desk and begins writing. Pathetic how I live! I have all this money invested in other corporations and into stocks. I'm a millionaire but I choose to stay in these hell hole motels. But I wouldn't want it any other way. I've always chose the unorthodox choices. Even when I was a child. They chose to play baseball. I played baseball with mailboxes. They wanted to have fun and do childish things. I wanted to create chaos and bring pain to the world. My mom wouldn't believe me that my friend 'Kullypso' was in on it. She thought I was just a spoiled little brat who didnt show respect to her or anyone in my path. Well she was right on that part though. Stupid bitch! But she wanted to take me to every god damned psychologist in the entire state of Florida. Those whacks only gave me medication and said I was schizophrenic and needed time away from everyone. They sent me to a mental institution...aww my second home! Well, Book its time for me to wander the streets. Its time for them to feel the Pain from a Harversters viewpoint! With that being written, Kullypso closes the book and places it back into his duffel bag next to his cot. Stretching out into the open air, Kullypso throws on his cloak and walks out of the room in search of his prey for the night. ------------------------------------------------------------ Hours later... As light from the morning glow of the sun pierces through the tattered curtains; a door opens and Kullypso walks in slowly and lays down on the cot. As he closes his eyes for his nights slumbers dreams of his childhood begin to flow throughout his mind. Light begins filtering through a dark room as a door opens. A shadowy figure of small stature slowly walks into the room, standing still for a minute and then quietly proceeding forward to the farthest wall. A clanking sound can be heard as the figure grabs a metal object from a ledge. Turning back around the shadow walks back towards the light silently mumbling to himself. The shadow is that of a young child with short brown hair. The child looks like he is in a trance as he slowly moves away from the closed door dragging a shovel. 'Though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death,'The boy begins to speaks slowly moving towards his destination, a kitten in a box scratching and trying to escape. 'I will fear no evil.' says the boy as he begins digging up fresh dirt from the ground. 'For Thou art with me.'The boy says again. This time grabbing the kitten and burying the poor animal neck high into the freshly dug earth. 'Thy rod,'the boy says as he slowly lifts the shovel into the air, 'and Thy staff they comfort me.' the boy says as swiftly the shovel swoops down and smashes into the kitten's skull. The kittens lets out a high pitched squeal then goes motionless and limp. The boy then begins to throw the rest of the unused dirt over the lifeless kitten's remains. The expression on the boy's face remains emotionless through the entire event. Once the kitten was fully buried, the boy quickly drops to his knees and tears begin to shed from the boy's face. 'What did I do!?!' the boy cries as he notices the mound of dirt just inches in front of him. 'Why di-id you let me do this!?! I-I-I-I th-thought we were going to have some f-fun!?!' the boy stutters. No noise is heard for the slight pause the crying boy gives. 'Marcus, what's that noise out there?' questioned from a lady's voice from the window sill. The boy now known as Marcus quickly tries to maintain posture and get in control of his tears as the woman stretches her head out of the window. 'I-I don't know Mom!' exclaimed Marcus as he tries to stand up dusting off his trousers. 'Come inside young man it's time for dinner.' the lady says as she puts her head back into the house. As Marcus finishes wiping the dirt off his trousers; a low sadistic laughter slips from Marcus' mouth. Marcus quickly covers his mouth and runs toward the house. Sadistic laughter begins to fill the room as Kullypso wakes up from his sleep. Staring blankly at the wall infront of him wandering what he thought was lying dormant for all those years may have finally woke back up.
