The Memory Remains #27

1 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 020104 My body came awake before my mind. It stretched, yawned, grumbled to life. I laid there in a daze, my brain begging my body for a few more blissful hours of nothingness. But my body ignored my request. Moments passed before the first eyelid began to creak its way open. A harsh muted light made its way in and I winced slightly before my eye adjusted. The second eyelid followed suit, creaking its way open, pausing slightly to allow for light to pierce its way to the core of my brain before finishing opening all the way. Soon, consciousness followed, my mind becoming active, noticing things all around. My body under the sheets. Victoria’s nude form pressed against mine. The overstuffed comforter hiding our entwined bodies. The sun peering its way into the bedroom. My body gave one last final stretch before demanding in its own way that I get out of bed. I leaned my head down and kissed Victoria on her forehead. She grumbled and went back to sleep. Gently, I pulled my limbs from our entanglement and got out of bed. The air was brisk compared to the warmth of her body under the blanket. My body shivered slightly and goose bumps formed on my upper chest. With the master bathroom just steps away from the bed, I didn’t bother putting any clothes on. My feet sunk into the plush carpet as I padded my way quietly to relieve myself. The tile floor was did its painful duty as its sent shockwaves from the bottoms of my feet to up and down my spine. Standing before the toilet, I aimed with my right hand, allowing my upper body to lean forward, my head resting on my left forearm, which was in turn resting on the wall behind the toilet. With as little as I had to drink last night, enough piss left my body to fill a rather large like. I felt like I had been standing there most of the morning. From the toilet, I made my way to the shower, sliding the glass door opening and turning the water on to a medium temperature, not hot, but not cool, but just right. I slipped inside, letting the water rinse over my body. I lathered my body with a gel and let the soapy bubbles slide their way down my body and to the drain before I stuck my head underneath the jet of water, wetting down my head and hair. While I was under the jet, I reached down and cranked the temperature all the way up, letting the heated water massage the back of my neck while creating a playful steam. The door to the bathroom opened and closed. Not very loudly. Just a small click. Victoria trying to sneak up on me. And doing a fairly damn good job. I almost didn’t catch the noise. I blocked out the sound of the water rushing out of the showerhead and listened for her footsteps. They were barely there. The door to the shower slid open and I felt her presence behind me. “Mornin”, Sleeping Beauty,” I called out softly. She gave a quiet mumbled reply and her hands encircled my body, her head pressing against my arched back. Her hands roamed up and down my stomach, fingertips tracing over my abs before going lower and grabbing me gently. I turned around slowly to pull her close to me, but she was already on the floor, taking me in her mouth. She continued to engulf me for several minutes before I couldn’t take anymore. She rose back to a standing position and nuzzled her head against my chest. “That was a hell of a wake-up call,” I whispered gently in her ear. “Mhmmm,” was the only reply that I got. “So what shall we do today?” “I think you should take me to Fredrick’s, like you mentioned last night.” “I think we can manage that,” I replied as I saw the my bank account total in my head take a quick dip before rising back up. Damn I love interest. I gave her a quick kiss and exited the shower. “Don’t have too much fun without me,” I called to her. Her response was a wet washcloth that sailed over the top of the shower and hit me in the back of the head. Back in the bedroom, I quickly dressed. Nothing too fancy. I opted for blue jeans today with a black Christian Dior dress shirt, leaving the collar unbuttoned and the tailed untucked. Fully dressed, I left the bedroom to try to find Deacon. The first place I checked, and the most obvious, was the fountain out by the front of the house. The last time we had a party like this, Deac had ended up waking up in the fountain wearing nothing but his birthday suit. It wouldn’t surprise me to find him passed out there again. Wow, I’m impressed. He actually managed to pass out somewhere else other than the fountain. Hmmm. The hunt continues. The backyard was relatively empty, except for a few scattered paper cups, the occasional passed out person, and the crew that was cleaning up the mess. Unfortunately, Deac wasn’t among the unconscious on the lawn. What the hell, maybe he actually stayed in his new pad on his first night back. Wrong again. The pool house was littered with human bodies, but no Deacon. Where the fuck was the kid? The bastard must’ve pulled a ninja vanish during his party. And he calls me an asshole. I left the pool house to think over the whereabouts of Deacon over a stiff drink. Shutting the French doors behind me, I pause at the sight of several large men in loose fitting business suits standing in my den. One of them stepped forward. The cut of his suit was cheap and a little too tight as it showed the bulge of a gun underneath his right arm. The wireframe glasses were crooked on his beak-like nose. Cops, I wonder? Or crooks? “John?” he asked. “John Zero?” “Yes,” I replied, instinctively reaching for the glock that should have been in the hostler at my back but was not there. I lowered my hand back to my side, playing the reach as an itch. “What can I do for you gentlemen?” He gave me a crooked toothed grin. “Pleased to make your acquaintance. Don Vincent sends his regards. Get that motherfucker, boys.” Ah shit. Now I really wished Deac hadn’t pulled that ninja vanish. Damn his strong kung fu. I fell back into a light-fighting stance, and whirled a spin kick from the hip, dropping the fastest of the five me across the face, snapping him to the floor. The remaining four men took a moment to pause. I think they were having second thoughts. No time to waste though. The four men stood three abreast, with the fourth man standing behind the middleman. I rushed forward, jumping at the man on the right, grabbing him around the neck with my left arm, using him as a pole to swing myself around, planting both feet into the side of the head of the man standing in back. I continued on my swinging path, kicking the middleman in the back while jerking my left arm, snapping the right man’s neck. I land just in time to catch an elbow to the face, which sends me reeling with stars in my eyes. Falling backwards, I use the momentum to do a backwards summersault, rolling into a squatting position and launching myself at the closest opponent, spearing him in the gut with my shoulder. I fire two stiff rights into the man’s jaw before slamming my palm in an upward strike, driving the cartilage of his nose into his brain. Two down, three to go. Looking up for the next target, I catch a foot in the face, which sends me to the floor. Before I can even think of moving, the three remaining men are on me like a pack of wild dogs, stomping and kicking and spitting on me. I try to curl up into a ball to present a smaller target, but the hits just keep on rolling. The world is starting to fade when I hear; “Aww hell naw!” followed my running feet. Then everything stops. No kicking. No stomping. No spitting. I remove my arms from around my head and take a look. I see Deacon laying on top of the three attackers in a form of a dog pile. He looks up at me and grinning, he stands. “Just in time to save your ass. Again, I might add,” Deacon laughs. “Yeah yeah, that’s one less you owe me. The fuck have you been?” “Out. Who are these bitches?” The sound of a gun cocking is heard. Both of us turn and see the man with the crooked glasses standing there. “Forget about me?” he laughs. “Haven’t I taught you anything Deacon” Always take out the leader.” “Fucker wasn’t there a minute ago.” I start to get louder, hoping that Victoria will hear the noise and come down. “Yeah, and I’m catholic.” The man clears his throat at the two of us, but we continue to ignore him. “Well call the pope, cause that bitch was not here when I got here.” “Well he’s hear now, what are you planning on doing about it?” Another clearing of the throat. “Well, obviously nothing. Motherfucker’s got a gun. What about you” Any bright ideas?” “I was thinking?” My thought was cut short by the gunshot to the ceiling. That surely got Vic’s attention. “Sorry to interrupt gentlemen, but Don Vincent did ask that we dispose of both of you,” the man said. Me and Deac looked at each other. “Now, without further adieu.” Two shots ring out simultaneously, but not from the man with the glasses. The red bloom of a bullet wound expands on his chest as the top of his head exploded. Two well placed shots from two different people. The head shot came from Victoria, her small hands griping the glock I keep in the bedside table. The other shot came from behind the man. Vic tossed me the gun, and I caught it one handed, taking the time to pop the man in the chest one more time as he fell before switching the barrel of the gun to the outlined figured standing in the doorway. “Drop it or I drop you,” I yelled at the person. Friend or foe, I didn’t really give a fuck. This was turning into a shitty day already and personally I wasn’t in the mood anymore. “Three seconds?” I yelled again. “Two” one?” I started to squeeze the trigger. Deacon lashed out and hit my gun hand, causing my aim to go to hell as the bullet exited the barrel. A small vase exploded as the bullet rammed into it. I paused long enough to look at my friend, giving him a “What the fuck” look. A moment later, the sound of a gun clattering to the floor filled the air. I looked towards the door and saw the young Spanish girl that Deac was chasing yesterday as she stepped closer into the light. And the plot thickens.
