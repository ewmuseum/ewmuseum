Damn kids

18 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.18.03 Shards of glass and wood cover embed themselves in Ace's already blood spotted hair as he lays in his hospital bed back home in San Antonio. Maximum Carnage had proved to be quite a show for Ace, with the TUE brutally breaking a bat over his head, and Ice beating the living hell out of him backstage.Ice's attack had been worse, however, as the TUE were forced to run when some police officers were about to arrive on the scene. 'Ugh...' Ace makes a sound of grogginess and pain as he sits up in the rather uncomfortable hospital bed. At first, Ace isn't quite sure where he is, or what's going on. His eyes flutter a few times as he tries to get a firm grip on the reality of his situation. It all came back to him in a flash. He remembered the attacks, and he was remembering some random images of the lights of an ambulance and Christina McCoy calling out his name. 'Heh..maybe she wants me. I've always had a thing for business women.' Ace mumbles. Just then, a nurse comes walking into the room. She gives an audible 'oh' when she sees Ace sitting up in bed. She seems happy. 'Hello Mr. Cash. Do you know why you're here?' The nurse speaks slowly and pleasantly. 'I think so. A group of spoiled rich kids took me out, and then I got my head put throw a mirrow by a Vanilla Ice lookalike.' Ace says with a slight smile. Oh man, it hurt to smile.... how does that work? 'Sir, it's actually quite serious. Dr. Krueger and myself weren't sure exactly how long it would take you to wake up. You've suffered some serious head trauma from your..eer..incident.' The nurse says. 'We have already contacted your place of work and informed them you are not to compete for at least three weeks.' 'Whoa! Wait just a freakin minute there, Nursey.' Ace says, his adrenaline flowing. 'There's no way I can't work..I'll go poor...well..actually, I won't. In fact, I probably could live without a job.' Ace looks confused for a minute, before remembering what it is he was talking about. 'But that's not the point! There's no way you people can take me away from my job!' Ace bellows. 'You would still be paid in your absence, sir. Just no matches.' The nurse replies curtly. 'Oh come on, lady. Believe me, I have plenty of money to get by. I'm not worried about that! I want to get back in business here.' Ace says, taking monitors off of his chest. 'Umm, excuse me, but where do you think you're going?!' The nurse says, suddenly dropping her polite manner. Ace jumps out of the bed, and realizes he's wearing a hospital gown. 'AHH! You put me in one of these things!?' Ace screams. 'It's standard procedure. Please go back into your bed, Mr. Cash. I can't allow you to leave this room.' The nurse replies. Ace is desperately looking for a way out of the room, even if he has to wear the hospital gown. Ace cannot find anything to help him escape. Desperately searching, Ace comes across the only thing that can help him- a rubber hammer. 'Back off, bitch! I aint afraid to use this!' Ace yells, waving the hammer in the air. 'Sir...get back in the bed.' The nurse says, unimpressed with Ace's weapon. 'WHAT!? Don't you realize I'm holding a weapon of....uhh, MASS DESTRUCTION!!? If they found this in Iraq, that would be some serious stuff!' Ace screams, inching towards the door. Ace is at a standoff with the nurse now, shuffling back and forth, before deftly dodging to the side and bursting out the door, laughing his ass off that he got away. Ace begins to run, with a huge smile on his face. 'That shows you not to mess with the most luchadorable guy on the planet! You're lucky I was in a good mood, otherwise this hammer would have done some serious damage!' Ace yells, his head turned backwards at the nurse who is frantically yelling for him to come back, waving her arms. Ace's laughter echoes throughout the hall as he runs, but there is an even louder echo of skull smasking against steel as little Jimmy JoHanson opens a door after getting his stitches in his arm from a dog bite. Ace staggers for a second, before dropping like a sack of potatoes. 'Ugh..' Ace says for the second time in under 10 minutes. Jimmy looks down at the fallen GW Superstar, and looks hopefully up to his mother. 'I think that man needs stitches too, mommy.' Jimmy says, pointing to the fresh cut on Ace's forehead. Damn kids.
