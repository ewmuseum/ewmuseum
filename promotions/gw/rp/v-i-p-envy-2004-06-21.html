Date: #1 Evo 6/21/04 Her skin felt like silk under his fingertips. Train knew the rules said he wasn't supposed to touch, but damn.... What else could he do? Besides...those rules were for your average chump who had to blow half his rent money to lay eyes on a set ofHe knew she was into it. His hands cut a stark contrast on her fair skin as Train slowly slid his hands up the outside of her strong thighs. The redheaded beauty felt soft and warm as she leaned back, laying her undressed back against his massive chest, which she had so playfully exposed, gleefully unbuttoning the front of his Polo shirt. Both the building and her petite frame pulsated, grinding itself into his, the bass of 'Slow Motion' by Juvenile pounded through the stereo system. It was a captivating spectacle but event that could quite hold his full attention. Train could feel her on top of him, but his eyes had to travel over to the two women next to him... The raven haired, caramel skinned latina, her tight, curvacious body winding sinuously the to methodical beat, sat between the legs of a milk chocolate complexioned, ebon siren that doubled as her cushion. The girl on the bottom sat, sliding her creamy, brown hands up and down the others' body while the latin honey stroked, what was perhaps the sexiest thing in the room to Train... The GW Platinum Title belt draped across her, covering only one of her well proportioned breasts. The blue, purple, and hot pink lights glimmered and beamed brightly as they reflected in the bay of mirrors above red velvet, half-moon booth where Train and his new friends sat. It was nearlly visualy overload. Train's eyes scanned the entire club the veritable rainbow coalition of womanhood as it wiggled and jiggled for him. Train slid forward and leaned towards the table in front of him. His arm slid past the slender body on top of him and his hand took hold of the Crown Royal: Special Reserve bottle that sat on the table. He tilted the bottle and poured the dark, reddish liquid into his glass and the waiting ice, finally placing the bottle back on the bottle back on the table. Train took hold of the glass and slid back into the cushiony velvet, his new best friend in tow, and casually raised the glass to his lips, quickly downing the finely aged whiskey. As he he sank farther back in the seat it became obvious... It was good to be the champ. It was a whole new experience. When you walk any place and you're the champ people fall all over themselves to be near you...to say they met you. No matter where you go...it's your world. Like this place for instance. Train had heard tale of 'The Lollipop' backstage in GW, but he'd never been here. He'd never been much for strip joints...after all he always needed money for himself, so what did he look like shoving it down some bitches thong? But tonight, he'd finally decided that it was his time to cut loose. He had the belt...the tight contract...and the knowledge he'd done what everybody said he couldn't and put Maverick Reno in his place, which was on his back for the 1...2...3. The shit might not've been easy, but it got done. And since it got done he checked the bank statement this morning and saw the $150K Nakamura had promised for dropping his ass. It was all good. With that type of luck, what else could Train do, but his civic duty. It was high time Train helped some highly intelligent, D-cup chick pay for a semester of junior college. The second Train stepped in the joint, the Platinum Title slung over his shoulder, even with every curve that was on display, all eyes turned to him. Being recognized was becoming common place. Every time he stepped out someone always knew who Train was. Not that, that had never happened before. Folk knew who he was back in Chicago too. Only problem is the only people who came up to say hello were usually carrying badges and a warrant. Tonight he had barely been inside the club 30 seconds before the owner walked up with a handshake and a pat on the back. No kissing ass, handing out tips to get in good... Nothing but a few handshakes and pictures and they hand you a free bottle of whiskey as they unhook velvet rope of the V.I.P. Section to let you in. Sure there were other guys here, but the truth was obvious. It was all about him. The rest of these fools probably had to take out one of those pay day loans to even afford the cover in this place, let alone get the girls to pay attention. Hell all Train had to do was take his seat and they flocked like moths to a flame. Brick... Reno... Deacon... Josh... Partridge... Fuck all of 'em. The wouldn't occupy Train's head tonight. Being the champ came with just as many problems as benefits, but Train knew he spent too much time worrying about the problems...keep that up and he'd forget the benefits. To hell with that. Mo' money is mo' problems...but they could wait til' tommorow. Train looked back to the two girls keeping themselves entertained next to him. 'Damn girl,' he began, catching the eyes of the woman now attempting to shine the belt with her body. 'I like the title and all, but you messing up the view. Put it around your waist.' 'But it's cold in here...' Said Angel, the carmel complected latina, coyly as the girl who's lap she was sitting in continued to rub and nuzzle her as she continued to stroke the Platinum Title. 'If I put it down how am I going to keep warm?' 'Oh, you need someting to keep you warm?' Train asked, being just as coy. 'Tell you what....' The champ slid his large, muscular hands around th petite waste of his red haired admirer and shifted her over to one side so he could go into his pocket. It only takes a moment for him to retrieve a thick wad of bills. Like an expert he uses his thumb to flip the folded mass of paper open and slides one of the bills that was once on the inside, to the outside. He reaches around the girl on his lap and pulls the newly freed $100 dollar bill away from the rest of the pack. Trapping the finger between his index and pointing finger he reaches over and slides the bill up her toned thigh. 'Why don't you use this to keep you warm?' She grins senually, nibling on her lower lip as she slides the belt down to her waist, revealing the rest of her c-cup chest. Train grins back and manuevers the bill between her tanned skin of her curvy hip and the strap of her thong, creating a snug home for the crisp bill. 'What? Nothing for little old me?' asked Strawberry, the red head on Trains lap, pouting her red lips that seemed like they'd be more cherry flavored despite her name. 'Oh, my bad...' Train said, realizing how insensitive he'd been. He quickly flipped out another $100 and slid the second crips bill down the center of her chest, brushing one of her breast with only partial intent...more like hope... This doesn't set off any alarms though, unless her high pitched giggle counts. The bill slowly travels down past her stomach before finding a permanent home tucked into the front of her g-string. 'There you go girl.' 'Thanks for the tip.' she purred seductively. 'You just keep doing what you doing,' Train responded. 'I'll have a real tip for you in a minute.' 'hahahahaha... You're such a bad boy.' 'Damn right.' The vibe of the club speeds up as 'Lean Back' by Fat Joe and Remy Martin fades in, the tense cello riff pulsing through the air. 'Uhoh!!!' screamed Coco, the petite, ebon princess who was big in all the right places. 'Look out!' she said to Angel, gently nudging her forward, freeing herself to stand. 'This is my song right here!' Coco holds out her hand and Strawberry takes it, knowing it's time to let her girl work her magic. 'You're in trouble now...' giggled Strawberry as she stood, quickly settling back into the the open space to Train's right. Both girls know what's coming... Coco, finds her beat and she's gone. Her body twists and bows, accentuating every curve she has...intermitently dropping it all to the ground and popping back up as if she had a spring underneath he voluptuous frame. Train couldn't justify not showing his appreciation. 1...2...3...4...5....6 $20 dollar bills drift to the ground at her feet as Train happily depletes his cash reserves. She doesn't seem to show any regard for the money, not even bothering to collect it. She simply kicks off her heels and steps up onto the velvet seats, standing tall, straddling Train's wide frame. She slowly drops down into his lap, her body facing his. Their eyes lock...she slowly reaches behind her and releases the strings of her bikini top. Her only covering, skimpy as it was, fells into his lap as he looks on with a small grin. Coco continues to grind to the music as a long legged waitress strolls up the stair to the raised V.I.P. section. Train mind is a little too preoccupied to notice her at first, but as the girl walks towards the booth the chime of ice and glass colliding together inside the silver bucket the waitress carries in front of her catches his ear. Train wraps his arm around Coco's waist pulling her close so he can easily peer around her. Spotting the waitress with the champagne Train takes Coco off gaurd, sprining to his feet, clutching her body as if she was a small child. He spins and sets her down gently in his former spot in the booth, then turns back to the approaching waitress. He quickly takes the bucket, the golden necks of the two bottles of Cristal jutting out from the ice inside, and exchanges it for a $100 dollar bill. The waitress grins as she places the three champagne flutes on the small, circular table that's been pushed out from the booth. The woman walks, tucking her hefty tip into her bra as Train frees the first bottle from it's icy prison and tears away the golden paper, exposing the cork. 'Oooo,' purs Angel. 'Is that for us Papi?' 'Well yall doing your thing pretty hard up in here,' Train responds. 'I'd hate for yall to get dehydrated or something cuz of me.' 'What are we celebrating baby?' Strawberry asked. 'hahaha... Let me tell yall something...' begins Train, dropping the bottle down to his side, inperceptively shaking it's contents. 'When you the champ? You don't need no reason to celebrate beyond just being the man.' 'Damn right.' Coco said, lending her support to his affirmation. 'Besides... We'll just call it an early celebration for when you beat down Brick.' 'Ohhh... Do I have a fan?' Train asked smuggly, already knowing the answer. 'You know you do baby,' Coco resonds vibrantly. 'He's so damn old and still running his mouth. Everybody I know that watches is tired of his played out ass.' Train can only respond with a gale of laughter. It's nice to hear folks echo your own sentiments. 'Tell you what girl...' he said, finally regaining his composure, but still fighting back laughter. 'After I put his ass on the mat for the 1..2..3, I'll let him know you said so. Now...forget about that chump. I'll take care of him later. It's back to business...' he said, raising the bottle back to chest level, finally begining to work on the cork. 'Oh, are we business baby?' Strawberry asked, flashing her green eyes at him. 'The way yall lookin' tonight? Hell yeah! Shit... I mean damn... Yall got a brotha kind of excited, you know? I mean I kinda feel like...like...' Suddenly all the girls are covered in a spray of white foam...the cork flies into the back wall and bounces off as the champagne sprays from the mouth of the bottle. All the girls fall back, flailing their arms and legs, attempting to cover up as they scream in surprise and bubble with laughter. The spray only last for a moment and Train quickly grabs his belt back and puts it back over his shoulder where it trully belongs. 'Oh my God!' Yelled Strawberry, brushing off the expensive sparkling wine as her mouth hangs open. She does her best to sound angry, but the giggle in her voice gives her away. 'I can't belieeeeeve you did that....' 'Damn.... My bad... I swear that's never happened before!' 'Yeah right!' playfully protests Angel. 'God, that stuff was cold.' said Coco. 'I'm sorry girl. Just let me know if you want something warmer.' 'Oh man... You are sooooooooo bad.' said Strawberry, shaking her head. 'Like I said before... Damn right.' Train turns back to the table and scoops up the three champagne flutes, holding them between the fingers on his left hand, pouring with his right, and tipping them at just the right angle to avoid anymore foamy hijinks. That's when he felt them. For most the dancing of the lights and bodies in the club would have been to distracting. But when you spend so long, knowing your survival depends on you staying focused on what's around you...you begin to develope a sixth sense... A silent alarm in your head that let's you know...when someone's watching you. Train slowly glanced up and saw the two men in black suits. They were sitting near the front of the stage watching to gorgeous women rub eatchother down with baby oil. Or, at least, they should have been watching. Instead both sets of eyes were focused up on the V.I.P section and the man standing at the railing, pouring his companions a drink. Of course it could've been all innocent. Train stood with the Platinum Title on his shoulder, splashing and pouring sparkling wine that cost $500 a bottle. Surely that type of thing draws attention. But even Train was having a hard time looking at them as the two women on stage entertained the rest of the crowd. He didn't figure he could ever be that attention grabbing. Besides that, one of the two men...both latino...the one on the right, heavy set and dark skinned, his jet black slicked back... was pointing towards Train. He leaned over to the smaller man...lighter skinned, rocking the bald head and a rather scraggly looking goatee... telling him something as he continued to point in Train's direction. Fans? Could be. But fans don't usually dress in matching black suits. If Train didn't know any better he'd have to say the two men were somebody's muscle. And the problem was... he didnt' know any better. Amazing how quickly life can go from good to bitch. It only took a second for all of this to roll through his brain before he knew... This shit was gonna be a problem. Train turned back to the girls and handed them their glasses. He did a good job disguising his irriatation as his mind pondered. Who sent them? Reno? Christina maybe? He knew it wasn't Brick. Wasn't his style. Train turned back to the table and put the bottle of Cristal at one edge of the round table. he quickly reached into the bucket and pulled out the second bottle before placing the bucket down on the floor beneath the table. Tearing the paper away from the bottle Train pops the cork without incident, simply taking a few big swallows from what he inteded to be his personal container. The belt slid from his shoulder and he folded it, placing his prized possession at the center of the table before pulling the table back towards the booth, nimbly keeping the other bottle from falling as he does. Coco scoots over and Train settles back into his seat before placing his bottle of cristal directly across from the first bottle at the opposite edge of the table. The stage is set. 'You got quiet all of the sudden.' pointed out Strawberry. 'What's wrong? You're not getting bored are you?' 'Huh? Oh...naw girl. How could I? Naw...I'm just expecting company.' 'Comany? Like who?' It was a fateful question...and it drew the response Train had been anticipating. At the bottom of the steps he could hear some kind of argument at the bottom of the stairs. Gleaning the information from bits and pieces it seems the security guard watching the entrance to the V.I.P section has taken a little exeption with two men that insist they know someone up the steps. He could hear two different voices from that of the security guard...both with thick barrio accents. A few seconds later heavy, stomping footsteps trudged up the stair. A large, barrel chested security guard quickly made his way to the booth. 'Excuse me,' he said. 'I've got two guys that say they know you. Paco and Chocolate I think?' 'Paco and Chocolate? heh.... Send 'em up.' 'You sure? They seem like they're looking for trouble to me.' 'They found it. Let 'em up.' Train said, his former joviality giving way to the coldness he's better known for. Reluctantly the man nods before heading back over to the top of the stairs. With a quick motion of his head he clears the entrance of Train's new admirers. The guard slowly strides back down the stairs, likely trying to decide whether or not this is a wise idea. It's likely not, but he continues on his way disappearing down the steps.
