Climbing the Ladder

29 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Climbing the Ladder JJ Jarone Global Wrestling Promo #680 Date: 10-29-03 Location: In a gym Today is a new beginning for my life. I have graduated from the underground promtions to the pro's. After fighting my way to the top for four and a half years I have finally made it. In the beginning going through the Primetime Wrestling Academy I can remember thinking where I would be three years from now. All I could do was dream of being in a high promotion like the Global Wrestling. It was all a dream, and at times a certain goal. I finally made it, all the pain and structure in my life has paid off. Good-bye Excite Pro Wrestling, hello Global Wrestling. Yet I may be a low carder, I have time to improve, time to collect the skills I will need to make it to the top. Now I sit here like I did when training at the academy, pondering to myself on where I may be in three years. Will I still be a cruiserweight? will I have held multiple Global Wrestling championship titles? Most importantly, will I still be with Global Wrestling? All these questions begin to repeat through my mind as I sit in a gym with lockers towering above me as I bench press, laying down on a black padded bench. Today was a good day, I woke up feeling great knowing I signed a contract with the Global Wrestling. I decided it be in my best interest to hit the gym early in the morning, I will need to train much harder, and more often every week if I want to keep up with my fellow GW superstars. As I continue to bench press the weights I could see an older man possibly in his thirty's preparing for his training. With him he has a black Nike gym bag full of water bottles and such. The man sat down next to me, slowly lying down preparing to bench some weights. I could see out of the corner of my eye that I recognize him from somewhere. 'How's it going Jarone?' The man said. 'Good. Do I know you?' I replied. 'You most certainly do, my names Derek Greenfield. We went to Primetime Wrestling Academy together, don't you remember.' I continued to bench press as I pondered to myself. I can see the man begin to lift the weights with ease. 'Oh Dirty D. How's it going man, what promotion you wrestling for now?' 'I'm not wrestling anymore Jarone, I play Arena Football now. Manchester, New Hampshire is supposed to open up a franchise, I'm hoping to become a player for that team.' 'That's cool.' What was I thinking? I knew damn well it wasn't cool. I remember back in the high school days dreaming of being a pro football player. Always wanted to be wide receiver for the New England Patriots. But I never got the chance, instead I went with wrestling. Oh well, either sport I get to kick some ass. 'So what are you doing for a living, still wrestling in underground federations are ya?' Derek asked. 'Nah I wrestle for the Global Wrestling, you ever heard of it.' 'Oh yea, I heard of it. It's part of the Primetime Central, right? Well, I'll be damned, you're a professional wrestler now. Good job Jarone, I guess you were right in the academy. If you train hard enough, you will get there. You see that's the way I feel about football. Just because I don't wrestle anymore, doesn't mean I can't train.' He replied. 'Yea, I hear that. I use to want to play football.' I decided that was enough bench pressing for one day so then I got up, and picked up my water bottle that was placed on the bench beside me. I then moved over and began to curl some weights standing up after placing the bottle on the brown hardwood floor. 'So when did you sing to Global Wrestling?' Derek asked. 'Well I just singed a written contract a couple days ago. I'm set to debut at the pay-per-view, Showdown in Tokyo. I'm hoping to push my career forward and set my goals, and sight for the Global Wrestling Cruiserweight Championship within a couple months.' 'Oh yea, that's what I'm saying. Don't waste anytime proving yourself to the fans and superstars in the backstage area, ya know?' 'Yea.' I replied with a grin. I continued to curl the weights as I pondered about the things Derek said. It repeated over and over in my head. Derek was right, I shouldn't waste anytime proving myself. Infect come pay-per-view I will serve a grade A ass whoopin' to whoever my opponent is. He's gonna have to step up and swing for that long homerun, when in the end results he will go down swinging. I droped the weights forwards and picked up the water bottle again, sipping on it. I grabbed the towel and began to wipe the sweat off my face, I could feel it poor down my chest. 'Well Derek it's been a pleasure, but I have to get going. I'll see you around man.' 'Oh yea definitely, we have to do this again sometime.' I picked up my gym bag after throwing the towel and water bottle in. I began to walk away turning my head nodding with a grin. I guess you could say I was excited to be able to say I am official apart of a big promotion, and I will be airing at a pay-per-view. With that said, I walked out to my car and sat in it throwing in some Nelly in the CD player. I reved the engine and took off peeling the tires.
