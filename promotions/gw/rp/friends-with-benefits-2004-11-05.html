Friends with benefits

5 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/5/04 Friends with benefits: two friends who have a sexual relationship without being emotionally involved. Typically, two good friends who have casual sex without a monogamous relationship or any kind of commitment. ---- Background music: 'Girl From Ipanema' by Stan Getz and Astrud Gilberto The date: July 2002 The place: Honolulu, Hawaii The first-ever edition of Global Wrestling Evolution was over and done. Brick had lost the International title to Silent Wind, thanks to outside interference from Quartz and the returning Derek Draven. Some months back, he and Brick had competed for the International title at Clash of the Titans. In retaliation for Draven trying to hang him from the rafters with a noose, Brick carried him to the top of the Globaltron and tossed him off into a dumpster filled with light bulbs. Now Brick was at the hotel bar, drowning his sorrows. 'Hey, sailor. Buy a girl a drink?' Brick turned to see Brandi Sights. She wore a red halter top and black leather miniskirt. 'Hey, Brandi.' 'This seat taken?' 'It's a free country.' Brandi sat down on the stool right next to Brick's. 'For the lady?' 'Sex on the beach.' 'Coming right up.' 'You okay?' 'I'm still pissed about Draven and Quartz screwing me out of the International title. Not to mention, I still owe Silent Wind an ass-whippin' for that shit he pulled with me and Aaron.' 'Yeah, I heard about that. Are you sure it was him?' 'Positive.' 'Well, Bubba did give you a rematch with him at Master of the Mat. That's at least got to make you happy.' 'You'd think.' 'Your sex on the beach, ma'am.' 'Thank you.' 'Anyway, they ain't the only ones got me pissed off.' 'Who else?' 'That little punk bitch Steve Maverick. I didn't appreciate the way he ran his mouth about me an' this company. Plus, I gotta work with his ass in the 'So how come you're here alone? I figured you'd be with Gunner, right now.' 'What? Nah, me and Gunner ain't like that.' 'But I thought you and she--' 'Nope, just friends.' 'Friends, or friends with benefits?' 'Just friends.' 'Really?' 'Yeah.' 'Well, are you looking for more?' 'Not really.' One year before, they weren't exactly the best of friends. At the time, Brandi was dating Terminator Mike Page, whom Brick was feuding with at the time, and whom Brick had recently had an extremely grueling two out of three falls match with at Genesis. Their relationship at that time was somewhat adversarial. Brandi was one of the few women Brick had ever given the Bricklayer. 'Well, I know you're really tense right now. Any ideas on how you plan to deal with that tension?' 'This is one way I was planning to deal with the tension. Why? You have something else in mind?' The sly smile on Brandi's face obviously signified a 'Yes'. ---- Background music: 'Soft and Wet' by Prince The place: Brick's hotel room. Whenever Brick did anything strenuous, he liked to put his all into it, no matter what. This occasion was no exception. And Brandi Sights was learning this. They hadn't even bothered with foreplay. Because foreplay was what someone did when they were about to make love. What Brick and Brandi were doing wasn't making love. It was raw lust. A half hour later, Brick and Brandi lay, her back towards him. 'So, how's your tension?' 'Oh, I ain't all that tense, now,' Brick chuckled. 'Brick, there's something you should know. I'm not really looking to get involved with anyone, especially not after how things went with Terminator.' Brick shrugged his shoulders. 'I can understand that. I'm not really looking to commit, either. But this has to make us more than friends.' 'Well, friends with benefits.' 'Is there any dude in this company that you haven't been friends with benefits with?' 'Hey!' she exclaimed, hitting Brick on his shoulder. 'I'm just asking.' 'Well, up until now, you, for starters.' Brandi then rolled on top of Brick and looked him right in the eye. 'OK, big boy, round 2.'
