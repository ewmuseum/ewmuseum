A new path#2

30 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/30/04 Sage Tse Ling June 27th, 1961 ? October 12th, 2003 Gone But Not Forgotten I sit in the graveyard in silence. This is not an unfamiliar seen. The once great vampire known as Leinad Soma sitting in a graveyard collecting his thoughts for the task he is about to do. It seems befitting that this is where I would end up beginning my journey. I mean a vampire is already dead so here I sit in a graveyard looking at the grave of none other than Sage Tse Ling otherwise known as Sage Sabbot. The Warlock himself?.The man who died at the hands of myself and the rest of TUE last year. I remember those events and I shed a tear looking back on what I was?.what I had become’I pick up the shovel that sits next to me and stand and begin to dig. I begin to remember what I once was?.before I became the monster known as Leinad Soma?. Egypt 1332 BC My name back then was Amenhotep III but then I changed it to Akhenaten. I was a pharaoh way before the time of the Christ. It was in those days that I was happy, a young vampire with anything I wanted at my fingertips. What people didn’t understand about us Pharaoh’s were we had all been vampires?.children of Ra?.children of the gods?.back then I was a tad bit boastful and had enough of claiming I was the child of the gods?.I changed the old ways even though I knew it would be the end of me one day?..from many gods to one god and I was his only son. I made the people wonder and speculate my origins if I were the only son of this “one god”. But at the time it didn’t matter to me. My sorcerer Imhotep, a man way before his time encouraged me to think about what I was doing?..the “consequences” of my actions?.the repercussions?.I thought nothing of it just as I didn’t think anything of it when I had a hand in killing Sage Sabbot. Present day I snap out of my memories and I’m half way finished digging. I hear approaching footsteps but right now I could care less. I have a job to carry out and nothing will stop me from carrying out. I keep digging as the footsteps get closer and closer. I begin to dig faster and faster?.dirt being flung everywhere as if I’m a dog unearthing a bone. Suddenly as soon as I hear the cling of the shovel hitting the coffin I hear the cocking back of a gun. I turn around and I know how the scene must look. I mean if I were him I know what I’d be thinking. I’d be thinking what the hell was going on. I’d be scared to see a man or what looks like a man digging up a grave. I’d yell freeze and flash my light on him only to be even more scared when I see the man’s face. “FREEZE!!!!”The man yells while flashing his light on me. I do not turn around but keep working on getting that coffin out of the ground. “I SAID FREEZE!!! DON’T MOVE OR I WILL BE FORCED TO”..” “Forced to what” Shoot me? Sir I can smell your fear?..I can also smell the scent of pepper and I do not smell the scent of metal so I know you don’t have a gun. And sir I don’t want to be forced to kill you. But if you make me I will. You have no clue what I am and I am really busy right now. I turn and look at him showing my whole face. He cowers at the sight of my horrible scarred right side of my face. I can tell he wants to run for his life but for some reason he can’t. The fear is so strong in him he can’t even move. I smell the scent of urine in the air realizing the man just wet himself. I shake my head and lift the coffin over my head and push it out of the grave. “Sir I shall give you two choices”.the first you can help me take this body to that mausoleum over there and the second you can die right here?.right now?. never seeing your family again.” “I have no family mister”.but I don’t want to die.” He lets a hand out to me and helps me out of the grave and we both carry the coffin to the mausoleum. Once inside we sit the body on a prepared table. Candles lit in a traditional ancient Egyptian mystic’s ceremony. I cross the dead body’s arms as if he were a pharaoh and walk over to the edge of the table and grab a goblet and I set it near his head. “Are you some sort of devil worshipper” What are you gonna do with that body? Are you some sort of cannibal?” “No I am not a devil worshipper’a cannibal”.some might say yes?.as far as this body I plan to raise it from the dead?.You have a lot of questions?.Now I have some for you.” “W’What?” “Would you give your life so that many others would be saved because of your sacrifice” Would you appear as a savior for mankind and die for them? Would you die so that many would live?” The man looks a little more scared than previous. He begins to shake a little and tears begin to fall from his eyes. As I watch the man tears also begin to fall from my eyes. He knows what I have intended to do after telling him I would not kill him if he helped me. Please Lord, forgive me but I am doing your will. The man stops shaking and smiles calmly at me even though tears still run down his face. “Sir if it would the Lord’s will for me to die so that many would live I would do it whole heartedly. So do what you must but all I ask is to make it quick. God bless you sir.” I give a small smile as I cry that this man is willing to give his life at my word he would be dying for the greater good. I begin the invocation. I begin to pray to the god Anubis to revive this dead body I slit my wrist and pour my own blood into the goblet “I call on you Anubis to let go of your hold on this man for his work is not done. I invoke your powers to bring this soul back from the land of the dead. With the blood of the forsaken eternal I give him blood to become immortal. And with the blood of mankind I grant him his youth, his strength, and the mind to be as he once was and not have the craving of blood.” I pull a knife from my back pocket and I lunge at the man and slit his throat and then plunge the knife into his heart. As the blood spews forth I catch it in the goblet. I stand once more and go over back to Sage’s body and continue the incantation. “And from the fires of the eternal sword that drove Adam and Eve out of the Eden I cleanse his body and burn away all his sins. I call forth the soul of Sage Tse Ling to come back and to finish what has now been started. I call forth this avenging angel to do what must be done. I call for this soldier of light to do the biding of you Lord.” I light the blood on fire and sit the flaming goblet by his head. I wipe the tears, sweat, dirt and blood from my face and cover the hideous side of my face with my hair once again. “SAGE TSE LING??COME FORTH!!!” The man’s eyes begin to open?..I stand there as he slowly begins to wake in slience?
