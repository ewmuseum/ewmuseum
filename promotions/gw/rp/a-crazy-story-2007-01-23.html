A Crazy Story.

A Crazy Story. Reginald Atkins Global Wrestling Promo #2957 Date: 1/23/07 Location: The Past “So, chap, long time.” The film is gritty, not because this is another home video, but because the bonfire in the shot is raging, sending flickers of ashes in random and sporadic gusts. It takes a second to adjust to the shot before we can find our focus, who is of course Reginald Atkins. He is looking to his left to the man who made the last comment, a man who is making his debut on Global Television (not too much of a surprise, granted, as this is only our second glimpse into the world of Atkins), Nathan Boswell. Boswell is a man large in stature, almost as big as Reggie’s manager Lyell. He pushes his auburn hair back and awaits Reginald’s reply. “Yeah, I know,” Reginald sighs, inhaling a cigarette and looking at the fire solemnly. He is wearing a pair of boot-cut jeans and once again deciding to go shirtless for the occasion. I guess you can get away with that sitting next to a bonfire. “I’ve been gone awhile.” Nathan Boswell laughs, a deep and booming sound. He nods and looks over to his old friend, a man he met through this crazy sport, a man who he bonded with because they both enjoy the same sort of things: destruction, carnage, and the ever-present sarcastic wit. “I know that, you fucking tard, or I wouldn’t have asked the question. I’m not concerned with the when. I want to know the what.” Atkins nods again and flicks the ashes off of his cigarette, then looks down at his feet. He smiles wanly at Boswell, then takes one more inhale before chucking the stick in the fire. “You want the what.” “Yeah, I want the what.” “All right, but I must warn you,” Reginald sighs, stretching out his legs and leaning back against the logs he and Nathan are sitting on. “The what, my friend, is a crazy story.” ---- The quick cut is alarming, as we suddenly find ourselves headfirst in the middle of a mugging. Masks, guns, money, it’s all there. The shorter of the two large masked men is shouting at the man in the middle, his left hand raised to take money, his right hand holding a pistol to expedite the process. While this ruckus is occurring, we hear Atkins’ accented voice in the background. “Well, you know how me and Lyell got our kicks before we found wrestling. Little of this, little of that, you know.” Boswell answers back over the scene, a hint of amusement to his voice. “If by little of this and that you mean torturing animals and trying to secretly kill Jetters’ butler, then yeah, I know.” “Exactly. Well, when IWE went under, Lyell and I tried that. But something had changed.” As the man fumbles with his wallet, the man with the gun stops shouting and then simply mutters “Schlag” to the bigger one, and the bigger man punches the small man in the face, quickly knocking him unconscious. The two men grab the money and run. “Richard had quit being Jetters’ butler, and there’s only so many ways you can skin a cat, we found out. Or a dog. Or a cow. Anyway, Me and Lyell realized that we couldn’t just keep doing small things. So we started mugging.” This scene is followed by a few more quick scenes involving muggings, beatings, and other crimes. Lyell, no longer wearing a mask, is holding a man up to the wall with one hand while Atkins continues his voiceover. “Small things at first, you know. Blokes on the street, bints looking for a good time and instead finding their pocketbooks lighter, that sort of thing.” “But you had all the money you needed. Jetters is loaded, and even if he wasn’t the fed gave you a bit of bank. Why money?” “You know it wasn’t about the money, Bozzy. It was a thrill, it was a rush. Fuck, it was just something to do. And it wasn’t really cutting it, but at least it was better than sitting in the mansion all day, or God forbid getting jobs. So we were prepared to keep doing it until either of us could catch steady at another fed.” In the scene, Lyell is still holding the man up, watching him scream. However, suddenly the man stops screaming. In fact, he stops moving all together, confusing Lyell. Lyell looks up at the man and weighs his options, then ascertains that, without a doubt, the reason the man stopped moving was most likely because of the new bullet hole in his left temple. “But something happened.” Lyell Jett turns around, dropping the man, to see who shot his territory. Standing in the doorway is Reginald, his arms slightly in the air. Behind him are three men of roughly the same size, two of them with guns. Lyell’s eyes show anger and confusion, so he looks to Reginald as, in present day, Boswell inquires. “What?” And we focus on Reginald’s eyes, which show a glimmer of a smile. “We were discovered.” ---- Jett and Atkins are walking down the street with the three men now, guns thankfully put away. The two men in front of our focus look around quickly and cautiously, making sure there are no suspicious people in sight (or, rather, people who would find them suspicious). Then they nod to the man behind Atkins, who gestures them to a nearby car. The three of them slide in, and Atkins looks back at the two large men, who are still surveying the streets. It is here we abandon the voiceover from before and stick to the events and dialogue at hand. “We waiting for our friends?” he asks, not yet closing the door. The man who accompanied them in the vehicle lights up a cigarette and shakes his head. “No, they’ll stay outside. Close the door.” Reginald shrugs and does as he’s told. Nodding, the man with the cigarette begins. “Hello, boys. It’s good to meet you, Reginald. Lyell.” Lyell suddenly looks to his friend and partner, who matches his confusion. “Yes, those are our names. How, might I ask, do you know them?” Chuckling, the man inhales a puff of his cigarette, then puts it to the side for the time being. “You gents are easy to find dirt on. How can you not be? You worked in a fucking wrestling promotion. It’s not like you’re goddamned spies.” “Maybe not, but that still doesn’t explain why you have escorted us to your lovely station wagon to have us watch you smoke.” Lyell lets loose a hearty laugh at this, as the man pauses for a second, as if deciding whether to be angry or amused. After the pause, the man chuckles again, keeping his head nodding like a bobble-head. “You guys have been doing a lot lately. You’ve been mugging. You’ve been stealing. You’ve been hurting. More precisely, you’ve been cutting into my territory.” “Deine Platz? Wie ist es deine Platz?” Lyell’s sudden entrance to the conversation takes both parties by surprise. The man looks at Lyell sharply, then looks to Reginald. “What’d he say?” Reginald raises his eyebrows and leans back, gaining comfort in the situation. “My friend Lyell here is just curious on how this little place is your fecking territory. Come to think of it, I am as well.” “It’s my territory because I know where the money is, I know where to get it, and I know how to get rid of people who know what I know,” the man answers, not taking his eyes off Lyell. “I’m James Eugene Parker, and I own these streets.” Reginald nods. “Pleased to meet you, Jimmy Gene, but aren’t you in the wrong field of business? I thought you had to have a name like Marinelli or Spicole to get into a mob.” Once again, Parker laughs and takes another hit of his cigarette. “This isn’t a mob. We’re definitely not organized crime. We just have a bit of investment in this city. And we don’t need it going to waste by some morons who think it’s cool to hold people up five feet in the air.” Parker glances at Lyell, who shrugs. “Fick dich, Schweinhund.” Smiling, Reggie leans forward. “He said…” “I get it, I’m sure.” Parker sighs, then leans forward as well. “The real reason I’m bringing you two here is to discuss some information I just found. There is a very important artifact coming to Farmington. And I don’t want the two of you continuing to scare people. We want there to be a, what do you call it…a false sense of security.” “So are you telling us to stop robbing people? Is that it?” “That’s part of it, yes. But instead of just telling you that and letting you go, I think it’s a better idea to keep you under surveillance. And the best way to do that…is to make you part of the team.” Reginald cocks his head to the side, then looks at Jett. The big man has made no movement since telling Parker to fuck off, and still doesn’t break this to look at his partner. Nodding, Reginald turns back to Parker. “What is this team, and what do we do?” Smiling, Parker lays his hands out. “We are, big surprise, interested in procuring this item. We have a lot of guys in intelligence to help us get it, but we need the muscle. We need guys who like to beat up weak and innocent people, like, maybe, security guards or staff workers. So I think you two would fit in nicely. “You have time to think about it. The artifact isn’t going to be in Farmington until the night of September 13th. That’s about six weeks from now. But if you’re in, let me know in three weeks time. We have a few things to go over.” Reginald puts a hand up in the universal “stop” gesture. “What if we don’t want to?” “Then I’ll simply ask you to leave the city or get hurt. I hope you don’t doubt my sincerity on this one. The city is ours, gentlemen. And if you don’t believe me, try to turn down my offer and go back to Jetters.” Parker pauses, the finality in his voice obvious. After a moment, Lyell leans forward, his hands clasped, a glint in his eye. “Geld?” Parker looks to Atkins, who translates. “He’s asking if money is involved.” Parker nods. “Tons.” “Koenne ich Leuten toeten?” Once again Parker looks to Atkins, who smiles as he asks, “Can he get to kill people?” “Only if things go wrong.” Noticing that Atkins is not speaking in German, he glances sidelong at both of them. “Why aren’t you speaking it back to him?” Reginald smiles wider. “He understands English.” “Then why doesn’t he speak it?” Lyell chuckles. “Weil ich die konfusen Blicke auf Gesichtern der Leute sehen mag, wenn ich Deutsch spreche.” Parker’s face once more becomes a mask of confusion, and he doesn’t need to say anything to Reginald to let him know he needs a translation. Opening the car door, Reginald crouches in the awkward position of trying to stand up in a small space. “He said because he loves to see the confused looks on people’s faces when he speaks German. See you around, Jimmy Gene.” And Reginald exits the car. As Lyell goes to follow, Parker grabs the big man’s arm. “Listen, you fucking Kraut. Tell your partner that this is not the kind of situation you want to take lightly. You guys may be muscle, you may be able to kick my ass, but I can and will have you killed if you two try to make a fool out of me. Understand?” Lyell looks at him for a moment, then follows Reginald out of the car. Parker claps his hands together and watches the two of them walk down the street. ---- “So that’s why you’re in Dallas? Because you were run out of town by an Irishman who thinks he’s a kingpin? Weak.” Nathan Boswell shakes his head, tossing about his auburn curls. Reginald shows no shame or embarrassment on his face. In fact, save for the bitter face he is making upon each inhale of cigarette smoke (as he has lit another), his face is nearly devoid of emotion. “Not exactly.” Reginald does not elaborate, instead letting the answer slowly sink into Boswell’s brain. Then, after a moment, it does. “Wait…so you helped him pull it off? What was it? What happened?” Reginald looks through the fire and sees someone walking up, and shakes his head. “Tale for another time, Boz. You’ve got someone blinking for ya.” Boswell looks up and sees Arianna, his girlfriend/charity case. Atkins laughs as Boswell stands up and whispers, “Give the punks hell in that battle royal. If you need help trashing staff members, give me a call.” Reginald laughs. “I’ll give you a call if you can get out of your seeing eye dog duty.” Boswell’s eyes widen, and he is able to harshly whisper, “Shut up, ass!” before Arianna walks up, feeling for him. As she feels his skin, a smile comes across her previously blank face. “Hey, baby,” he says, looking down and kissing her head. “Hey. Who’s that I hear talking with you?” Arianna looks around uselessly, her dull eyes not taking in anything, the only thing obvious to her the heat of the fire. “Hey, love,” Reginald offers, his words dripping with charm. Upon hearing his voice, Arianna’s face goes sour. “Hey, Reggie. How are you,” she asks dully and rhetorically before looking up in Nathan’s direction. “Can we go, babe?” “Sure.” Boswell brings Arianna to his stomach, then looks down at Reginald. “You going to tell me the rest of that story sometime?” Reginald winks at him. “Right-o, boss.” As they walk away, Reginald shakes his head. “You minge-whipped git, you.” Standing up, Reginald spends a few moments watching the fire, then looks over the horizon pensively. After a moment, he sighs and begins walking up the hill to his car, his face still trying hard to show emotion. His passive look betrays a troubled mind, one that can’t get his mind on his work, because he always has in his mind the still messy results of his last job gone bad.
