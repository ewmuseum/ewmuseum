Living the Lie, Part II: Come Here Often

3 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 3, 2006 I have never been so embarrassed in my life than I was at this exact moment in time. I was sitting in the middle of a restaurant, a nice one at that, all by myself none the less, and I was trying to hide something from the rest of the upper class who was dining there. Now I know it's natural for something like this to just, pop up, but why the hell does it always happen at the worse times? I shifted around in my chair, doing all I could, but nothing seemed to work. Then when I thought it couldn't get any worse, my waitress came to my table. 'You have an idea of what you might want tonight sir?' She asked me, doing her job. My face turned a bright red, and I could feel my ears start to burn. She smiled at me, and her cheeks turned red as well. For some reason, that made mine get even worse, and I was certain she knew what my problem was. I glanced at my menu really fast, trying to avoid thinking about it. 'I'll take a few more minutes please. But if I could get another water, that would be great.' I kept my face buried in the menu as she grabbed my glass, and started to walk off. 'I'll be back with your water.' She said, and I breathed a heavy sigh of relief as she walked out of sight. But I still had the rest of the restaurant to contend with. Who might glance over and see my problem? What good looking young woman would be the first to point and laugh with all of her friends? I tried to think of anything I could to make my problem go away. But the my mind kept coming back to the simple fact of why this was happening to me, and why the hell it had to happen now. Whatever it was that karma had against me on this day, I was going to try and fight it. I looked around for a moment as to where the bathroom was located. When I spotted it, I didn't hesitate, and I headed straight for it. I was unsure of what I looked like to others, but I'm sure my face paced walk was accompanied by a slight slouch of my back. I could only explain how I thought I looked while basically running to the bathroom as how you would see one of those nerd types walking the halls of your high school. Arriving at the men's bathroom, I was lucky enough to find and empty stall inside in which I occupied quickly. I sat down on the toilet to gather my thoughts for a few moments, and to wait for my little friend to go away. After taken a few deep breaths, and thinking of anything that would take my mind off of it, my little camping trip had ended. A voice from the stall next to me called out, though. 'You alright over there buddy?' He asked. I giggled to myself before answering back. 'Yeah, just fine now, thanks.' I said. 'Being twenty-three years old, I don't figure things like this to happen still.' I finished. Laughs came from both stalls on either side of me, before the one to my left, the one occupied by the guy who had previously spoken, spoke once more. 'It happens to the best of us kid. There's quite a lot of good looking tail bringing food to the tables out there, I wouldn't expect anything out of a youngster such as yourself.' He joke. The sarcasm was a tad embarrassing, and I exited the stall quickly with the sound of laughter being heard as I left the bathroom. I walked back through the restaurant towards my table. I avoided looking at anyone, but about halfway back, I caught eyes with a young woman sitting in a booth with two of her friends with her. They all three were smiling at me, one of which looked like she couldn't keep from laughing. The one I caught eyes with, however, had red hair, while the others had blonde. I didn't keep the eye lock long, however, as I quickly made my way back to my table. Upon arriving, I noticed a new glass of water waiting my arrival, and I quickly downed three-fourths of the glass. My waitress then arrived again as well, doing her duty 'Have you decided yet?' She asked. I simply shook my head, and she smiled before walking away. I noticed, though, as she got further away, she started shaking her head. All I wanted was food, and I'd turned into a circus freak of this place I started to look at the menu again. The Parthenon Restaurant, the place I just happened to be in at the moment, was said to have the best authentic Greek cuisine. I told myself I'd try it, just to see if they did it as good as Mom did. But the way things were going, I wasn't sure if I was ever going to order, or keep my sanity before I just ran out of the place. What else could happen, right? Well, that red head who I had caught eyes with not five minutes ago found herself hovering over the only other chair at my table. 'You waiting on someone?' She asked. Both her hands placed on the back of the chair, and her head was slightly tucked to her left shoulder. She looked cute as hell, but I was still too damned embarrassed. I shook my head as I felt my cheeks turn red. 'Well, mind if I join you? She asked another question, this time pulling the chair away from the table. I shook my head and shrugged my shoulders as I buried my face into my menu. 'How's it going?' I said from behind the fold of laminated paper. I was hiding my red faced self from her more than anything. She smiled and looked down at the table. 'Alright, I suppose.' She said back. I nodded my head to her. She spoke back up. 'So yeah, me and my friends noticed you when you came in. I told them I thought you looked like you weren't from here, and they thought they had seen you before. I was just wondering...' She didn't finish, as if to ask, without asking, where I was from. I sat the menu down onto the table and looked at her. 'Well, you were right. I'm not from here. I'm not even from this country.' I said to her as I looked out through the people dining at the moment. She smiled back. 'Where are you from then stranger?' She asked. I thought to myself about how she likes to ask a lot of questions, but I figured I'd go along with it anyway. 'I'm from Athens, Greece originally.' I said before continuing. 'My mother is actually from Michigan, though. So I guess you could say I am from here, through relatives and all, but I've lived my whole life in Greece.' I finished with a nod of my head. She smiled and looked back down at the table. She had started to play with a spoon that was sitting at her place at the table. 'What brings you to America, then...' She paused. 'I didn't catch your name.' She said with curiosity to her voice. I smiled. 'I didn't tell you my name, but it's Christopher. Most people call me Kaden though. It's the middle name.' I said, before realizing I had probably said too much. But I proceeded to answer her question anyway. But as I started to say it, I realized I wasn't even telling the truth. 'Well, you see, I'm big into freestyle wrestling and all. A big sponsor company here in the states offered me a contract to come over here and wrestle for them. Tournaments and stuff all over the US. I said, not even being close to the truth. In fact, it's the same story I had been telling people since I had lied to my father about it before leaving Greece. Ms. all smiles across the table continued to show her pearly whites as her inquisitive ways continued. 'That sounds interesting. Not a lot of free time then I suppose.' She said as she got a little embarrassed herself. 'I don't suppose you come here often do you? You know, to where I may see you around again?' She asked. I shook my head and broke a smile of my own again. 'Should I let you keep asking questions, or should I just give you my number?' I asked, a bit of sarcasm to my voice, but mostly seriousness. Her smile didn't fade one bit, and she nodded her head as she slid me a piece of paper that she had pulled from her purse. 'Here's mine.' She said. She also slid a pen over. I noticed a blank spot on the paper, and I tore it away from her number. I wrote my number on the blank piece, and slid it back to her. 'There you go...' I paused, realizing I hadn't caught her name yet. She smiled and said. 'Jaime.' I wrote the name down onto the piece of paper she gave me with her number on it, and then slid the pen back to her. 'Jaime. Nice.' I said as she stood up from her chair. She pushed the chair back into place and gave me a wink. 'Hope to hear from you sometime Kaden.' She said as she started to walk off. I nodded to her and smiled. 'Likewise.' She smiled back, and walked off with haste. I watched her as she exited the restaurant. Outside I noticed her start acting like she was still in high school as she met back up with her two friends from earlier. I shook my head and smiled as I picked the menu back up from the table and started to look at it. With nothing on my mind worrying me, I found it very easy to pick out what I wanted to eat. My waitress then came back to my table. 'That looked uncomfortable for you.' She said with a sarcastic smile on her face. 'Anyway, know what you'd like tonight?' She asked for what would be the last time. I looked at her and nodded. 'In fact, I do know what I want.'
