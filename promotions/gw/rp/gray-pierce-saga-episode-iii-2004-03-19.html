Gray - Pierce Saga [Episode III]

Date: 03.19.04 'Anger and jealousy can no more bear to lose sight of their objects than love.' -George Eliot March 16th, 2004 - Approximately 12:10 AM New York City, New York - The Echelon Tower The scene opens to show the dismal quarters that contain the ornate office of the one, Maverick Reno. The moon's glow radiates through a nearby window, giving nothing but a bluish-gray glow to the already dark office. A black marble motif is not something that tends to allow much for illumination without artificial light; especially in the dead of night. Maverick Reno is seated at his desk, his form draped in the same black suit as the day before, minus the Mandarin jacket. Stained onto the window behind him is a new addition, the Ankh of Reno. His eyes focus on it and then past it, the beautiful skyline of New York his current muse. He leans back in his executive chair, his mind wandering far beyond the city before him. 'You're letting things get to you again, Maverick.' He speaks to himself, acting as his own therapist. 'You're letting guilt get to you in ways that it hasn't in more than a year. Alicia's death was not your fault, nor was the acts of her murderers.' Reno reaches to his desk and picks up a goblet, sipping at the white wine. He glances up again, his eyes focusing on the moon above. 'Scott knew the consequences of dealing with those men. There's nothing that could have been done and I am most certainly not to blame.' Another sip. 'If anything, my influence was positive. I believe that I helped him a bit after the incident.' The self assurance seems to be working. Reno replaces the goblet, his eyes wandering still. He folds his arms behind his head, reclining back. A few moments pass as silence fills the room. Reno reaches over and presses a button and then another. Finally, a third is pressed and Hiru No Tsuki begins to play throughout the room. The eerily melodic tones fill the room, the language of a foreign land. Music is the ultimate language... 'So this is my final on the matter. I am not guilty and I will not punish myself for what happened. That was then and this is now, tomorrow is my future and my future lies within the halls of victory. There is no room for sorrow and defeat... not in my life. Not in this world.' There's a moment where the music continues to play, but all else simple stops. There is no movement in Maverick, no fluctuation of light, not even a cloud moves in the sky above New York City. Not a creature was stirring, not even a mouse. Another moment passes and the same occurs; finally the silence is broken as the music comes to a halt in a beautiful end to a beautiful song. 'My World.' Again, Reno reaches over and presses a few buttons. Finally, another song begins: Beethoven's 5th, a classical piece of art. Reno leans back, his eyes closing as he brings the goblet to his lips, sipping at the exquisite liquid inside. 'I simply need a way to keep my mind off of the matter. A way to focus on the things to come.' He places the goblet down again and braces his hands on the desk chair, his eyes still closed loosely. The sky outside casts a purple light into the office now, colored by the movement of dark clouds and man-made signs. Reno's face is highlighted by the purple essence, showing little but his features and even then, barely that. The Path of Morpheous is being tread by our Host as he slips into a world of slumber. Moments pass and the scene fades. Peace. Serenity. Harmony. Memories. Fear. Torment. Torture. Every moment that I lie awake before I sleep, I fear the moment my eyes shut in wait of the next day. Every moment that I sleep, I pray that I not wake to face the torments of what may come. Every moment that I live and breathe, I am thankful for this chance to survive. There is no way to take a second chance at sleep - no way to 'make up sleep.' Life is an irony in and of itself. All you hear about are 'second chances' and 'I wish I could rewind time.' These statements of wishes are so often fulfilled in our society that we are fooled into believing that we will actually have a choice regarding the truly important things. There is no second chance, when you are born, you are stuck there. When you die, that is how and when you die. 'To Sleep, Perchance to Dream.' When I dream, I dream of death and blood. When I wake, I spawn death and blood. But when I first wake, I scream for the peace of mind that will never come. Would I change anything? If I had one chance to go back would I? No. I am inescapable, unavoidable. 'Death is the only inescapable, unavoidable, sure thing. We are sentenced to die the day we're born.' -Gary Clark Gilmore And that is the truth about Second Chances. That is the truth of life. Every moment that someone thinks of me as I sleep, they fear the moment my eyes open wide to feast upon the new day. To think that I am the man that so many fear is not only believable, but increasingly common. I am the man that haunts the dreams of competitors, the man that brings misery and anxiety to the hours of wake to many. Something startles him and he sits up in his chair, grabbing suddenly to his left. A gasp and then the sound of shattering glass is heard. Reno slowly turns, his eyes focusing on his assistant, Naria Xiou. She seems frightened and stares down at Maverick, a stunned look on her face. On the ground before her is a single glass, shattered, the contents of which have flooded the area in an almost perfect circle. She pulls her hand away, stepping back. 'What are you doing in here?' There's a long pause before Naria responds; she gathers her wits, the sheer aggression radiating from Reno says more than any idle threat. She steps back, rubbing her now red and slightly bruised wrist. 'I... I just thought you might like a glass of Zinfandel.' Reno glances out towards the city lights again, his eyes focusing on the view before him. 'You normally get a glass of one of our premier wines about this time when you work late. I'm sorry, I had no idea that you had ...' 'That I had what, Miss Xiou?' 'Well, fallen asleep, Maver...' 'Maverick? Quite casual at this moment are we? Do we think this is... a moment of pleasantries or a moment for apologies?' 'I.' 'Yes?' 'I'm sorry, Mr. Reno.' Reno nods, his back nesting into his chair again. He glances down as he pulls a sterling silver pocket watch from his pocket, his eyes feasting on the time. 'Leave a message for Mr. Zero. It's been a few days since we've spoken and right now, I believe a few things must be dealt with in regards to next week. He'll agree and understand.' 'Yes, Mr. Reno. I.is there anything else I can do for you?' Reno stands and pulls his full length black leather jacket onto his shoulders, his eyes examining the skyline outside again. His mind is working, mulling over Miss Xiou's offer. 'Are Tobias and Aiden in the building still?' 'I believe so, Mr. Reno.' Reno nods as he places a small black leather satchel inside of his trench coat, fastening it with buttons and straps. He glances down again and then speaks softly, not directing it towards Naria in the later stages. 'Have them meet me there. Close.' The curtains slowly slide to a closed position as Reno continues. 'Miss Xiou, also have two of the guards downstairs with the Porsche. And if you need to reach me, you'll need to reach me through those particular guards. Understand? They'll accompany me in a town car.' 'I understand, sir. Is there anything else?' 'Yes. Contact the Global staff first thing in the morning. I'll leave you a list of issues to clear with them as well as a few contract issues. Make sure that these are dealt with.' 'I see, sir. Thank you. Have a good...' 'Also. get a phone number for Scott Pierce. His file seemed to be lacking one earlier.' 'I'll see what I can do.' Reno has begun his walk towards the doorway, his eyes downcast the entire time. Two bodyguards step in behind him as he starts down the hallway. Quickly, Naria pulls a walkie-talkie from her jacket and speaks into it. 'Quarters, this is Xiou from CC. Stadther, Overby. I need you two down in the parking garage A.S.A.P. with keys to a black town car, be waiting for Mr. Reno and both DeSarret's downstairs near spot number 03.' There's a moment filled with static and then a response. 'This is Tucker. They're on their way via the express elevator, Miss Xiou.' Naria nods and flips the handheld to standby and then flips it shut like a cell phone. She quickly slips that back into her jacket and picks up a phone on Reno's desk. After pressing three digits, she holds it to her ear and waits for the first ring. Quickly she presses intercom and hangs the phone up again. 'What's the deal, Mav?' 'This is Naria, Tobias. Maverick has requested you and your brother meet him in the parking deck.' 'Did he say anything else?' 'Not really.' 'We're on our way down...' With this, Naria quickly presses a button on the machine, ending the conversation. She glances around the room, her eyes getting used to the lighting finally. She sighs and sits on the edge of the desk and closes her eyes. Suddenly she turns and picks up the phone again, this time dialing a full number. A moment passes and she speaks into the phone. 'Excuse me for calling so late, Doctor. Is there anyway I could get you over sometime after...' Naria leans over Reno's desk, flipping on a light. She runs a finger down a sheet of paper, her eyes following every line. 'Two pm? Mr. Reno needs to speak with someone. And please, don't let him know that I called you... let him think that you're just dropping by?' There's another pause before Naria smiles, nodding as if that's a response to the person on the other end. 'Thank you, Doctor.' With this, Naria replaces the phone on the base and sighs. She glances about the dimly lit room and reaches for the light, flipping it to the off position thus making it even darker. A moment passes as she moves slowly, finally beginning her leave of the office. 'I don't know what's wrong with him... but he needs to get it off his chest.' The scene fades to black. March 16th, 2004 - Approximately 2:00 AM New York, New York - Echelon Tower Parking Garage A pair of impeccably carved oak doors slide open, revealing a red carpeted elevator complete with marble walls and wood accents. Inside stands a single man; he's adorned now in a black long sleeve shirt, long black jeans complete with straps, and a pair of boots. His hair is hanging loosely about his shoulders where it meets his trench coat which is barely shy of dragging the ground; a look not recently seen from the founder of the Echelon. Reno steps out of the elevator and pulls a pair of black leather gloves on, his eyes moving about the deck slowly. Two guards had taken their stand aside the elevator and step in behind Maverick as he begins his trek to space 3. The two guards are dressed as usual, in a pair of black suits complete with earpieces. Reno turns a corner, his eyes searching the area. A large '3' can be seen painted carefully on a wall, aside it '2' and '4'. In this spot, a silver Porsche Boxter with a black convertible top has been parked. Two men stand at the back of the vehicle. The first man stands at approximately Reno's height in black dress pants and a white turtle neck; he has jet black hair, neatly cut. A black blazer rests over one arm and around the other shoulder, a holster and a single pistol; what kind can't be made out. The second man is slightly taller than Reno. He's dressed in black cargo pants and a black turtle neck. His hair hangs the same length all around his face, barely covering his eyes and blood red in color. Hanging at his sides are what appears to be batons, although, they can't be made out due to the fact that they're mostly hidden by the holsters. 'Maverick, what's this all about?' 'Let's just say that I'm glad you came prepared.' Reno reaches into his trench coat and pulls out a pair of black roses and places one in the strap of each man's weapon. He glances at each and nods. 'Care to fill us in, Maverick?' 'In the car, Aiden. No need to rush the matter.' 'But why so sudde...' 'In the car.' Reno walks around and opens the drivers side door of the beautiful vehicle. Aiden and Tobias shrug and Aiden slips into the passenger seat. Tobias nods to the two and motions to another spot just out of eye shot. 'They're following?' 'Mhmm, always right, Aiden?' 'Right.' Tobias sighs and slips into the backseat of the sports car, positioning himself so that he's comfortable. 'It's going to be quite the ride, boys. Quite the ride indeed.' 'Uh, will we be back in time for Family Guy tomorrow?' 'Why are you so addicted to that show?' 'Well, I picture myself as a Stewie without the delusions of grandeur. It's sort of a 'your dreams can come true' situation.' No one comments as the doors close and the engine roars to a start, the top of the convertible folds back, revealing the men inside. Lights illuminate their path and the car starts out, moving fairly rapidly for such a contained area. Another car, a Lincoln Town car, pulls in behind it. When they reach the concrete barrier that keeps the interior and exterior separate, another guard calls out for Reno to swipe his card; he does so and then quickly swipes his own, allowing the large doors to open down the center, allowing access to the ramp and from there, to the street above. The two vehicles with tags marked 'Ech-3 and Ech-17' pull off of the ramp and onto the main road before heading right and towards an interstate. The camera switches to the view inside the Boxter. The three men are comfortably seated, Reno at the helm. Tobias is stretched out across the back seat, his boots kicked up on the side of the vehicle, and his arms folded beneath his head. In the front, Aiden has an arm draped over the door frame casually. He glances back at his brother and then to Reno, speaking above the whistle of the wind. 'So what's the reason for this outing?' Reno glances at Aiden quickly and then returns his eyes to the road. 'We're taking care of an old friend.' Tobias leans up and then over the seats, his head hanging between both Reno and Aiden. 'Care to be more specific, Maverick? You have lots of old friends and most of them need to be taken care of.' Reno nods, his eyes watching the town car behind him. 'This one is a real gem. Names John Russell.' Aiden turns his head, glancing at the two men before speaking his mind; something few can freely do around Reno in these times. 'Russell? He's been dormant for over two years. Why him and why now?' There's a moment of silence in the car as Aiden's question hangs in the air. Time passes and it becomes obvious that Reno is reluctant to answer. His words finally form however, forming a new chill in the air. 'His actions in the past are nothing to me. His actions towards one however, they haunt me at night and day. At one time, I was too full of delusions of moral grandeur to deal with him properly but now... now his time has come to truly pay for his crimes. Just as his employer has done. His time is now... while he rests, unknowing, trying his best to nurture a family in this world. Tonight while they sleep, we take it away from him just as he took it away from another. Tonight, business takes a break while vengeance reeks havoc.' There's a silence amongst the three men in the Porsche at that time, Reno having said his piece and the others not quite sure how to respond. Finally, Tobias barks up. 'Sounds like a creativity test. Let's see how bad we can fuck up the rest of his life, boys? What'do'ya'say?' Reno smiles at Tobias' remark. 'I knew you'd like the idea, Tobias. You're always up for a good mind-fuck.' 'So, what about the guards? I'm sure we don't want baby sitters for this job.' Reno nods and glances in the rear-view mirror now, his eyes focusing on the headlights behind him. 'Already thought of that.' 'Hey, uh, Maverick?' 'Yeah, Aiden?' 'If we're going to Saratoga Springs... why are we going south?' 'Because we have a tail.' Aiden and Tobias look at each other and both duck suddenly as Reno swerves around, maneuvering a 180 degree turn while navigating the small amount of vehicles on the interstate. The Lincoln Town Car swerves and spins on it's wheels, trying the follow the powerful sports car. Suddenly, Reno switches gears over and over, slamming his foot on the accelerator as he speeds through oncoming traffic and off a ramp. The Lincoln tries to follow, only to be nailed in a side as it turns by a small Miata. Horns sound and a guard staggers out of the passenger side, shaking his head. We cut back to the vehicle where Tobias is leaning over the edge like a dog enjoying his first car ride. 'THAT WAS FUCKING AWESOME!' '... you're insane, Maverick. Completely and diagnosably insane!' Reno grins and jerks his back, popping it all the way up. He turns suddenly and heads up another ramp onto the northern expressway. On the opposite side, both guards can be seen standing while the entire road is blocked off by the accident. Cars are beginning to pile up from the lack of movement. 'Completely, yes. Diagnosable? I highly doubt it. Besides, we lost them didn't we?' Aiden rests back in his seat, enjoying what has become a fully pleasant ride. Tobias once again stretches out across the backseat, his head resting in his arms. the scene fades. March 16th, 2004 - Approximately 4:30 AM Saratoga Springs, New York - An isolated suburb The scene opens to a long and winding road with snow pouring from the heavens above. The top has now been placed over the heads of those in the vehicle, protecting them from the snow above. The car slows in the harsh conditions. Finally, it turns down a large drive way in between rows of barely living bushes. The car drives slowly still, even this driveway is unkempt. A large garage is seen ahead but the driveway also circles, taking the Boxter and its occupants just twenty feet from the door of a magnificent home. A large Victorian half-mansion stands before the vehicle now. A half mansion because it's more of a large manor or estate than a mansion. Although, it is complete with two guest houses that rival villa's in California. Reno quickly gets out of the vehicle and approaches the door, the brothers DeSarret at his heels. They are all wearing their jackets, concealing anything that they may not wish be seen at this time. Reno knocks, first, at the door and then rings the doorbell. Time passes and Reno does this a second and third time. Finally, a light can be seen coming from the foyer. The door opens and there stands an elderly man in what appears to be night clothes. 'Mi..Mister Reno?' 'Yes. I'm very sorry for disturbing everyone this late but is there anyway I could speak to Mister Russell?' 'Mr. Reno, I ... Mr. Russell has turned in for the evening.' 'I wouldn't have come if it weren't a matter of life and death, Hugh.' There's a pause as Hugh contemplates then nods. He motions for them to come into the entrance hall where all men shudder and relish in the warmth of the home. 'Wait here and warm up a bit while I go wake him. I truly hope this warrants this much unrest, Mr. Reno.' The man now known as Hugh moves up the stairs as the three men stand in the foyer, shaking off the cold. Tobias watches him walk up the stairway then glances at Reno. 'A matter of life and death? Foreshadowing, my friend?' Reno simply grins and motions for the two to be ready. Aiden slips his arms out of his jacket sleeves but keeps them in his coat, hugging himself so that it looks as if he's trying to keep warm. Tobias rubs his hands together and blows in them while Reno stands quietly, his eyes feasting over the beautiful and elegant Victorian Architecture. Finally a voice calls from above. 'If it weren't a quarter to five, I'd be glad to see you Maverick!' The three men glance up to see a rather large man coming down the stairwell. He's dressed in a pair of flannel pajama pants and slippers. His hair, formerly a sandy blonde, now graying at the edges. He stands at 6'4 and 250 plus pounds without an ounce of fat on him. He smiles to the three and puts a pair of glasses on at the final step. The man known as Hugh is behind him. 'What brings you to my doorstep tonight?' 'Just shooting the breeze, John.' There's a pause in the conversation as John approaches the three. 'Whiskey for my guests, Hughes. Speaking of guests... who are these two men, Maverick?' 'Ah, how rude of me.' Maverick begun, stepping up and motioning between the three men appropriately. 'Aiden DeSarret and his brother Tobias DeSarret. Both men work with the Echelon.' John nods, waiting to be introduced. 'And this is your mark.' John smiles, the words not crossing him at first. His eyes widen suddenly as his hand extends and two silenced-blasts are heard. The black blazer that draped the shoulders of Aiden now falls slowly to the ground; accompanying it are two spent shells. Aiden with his wicked gift for accuracy has placed a pair of matching bullet wounds into the inner-folds of John's arms, shattering the elbow from the opposite side. 'SHI-AH!' 'Came to talk about Scott Pierce, John.? John has taken a knee, his arms practically useless now. Blood drains from the wounds, conforming to the muscles of John's lower arms as it drains all the way to his palms and then trickling to the floor via his fingertips. John's eyes are welling with tears and it would seem that he's in an amazing amount of pain. Finally he starts to speak, his words low and angry. 'Why...?' 'Because a large man like you is a threat. However, without his arms, he's, well, excuse the pun, disarmed.' John purses his lips, his eyes making the same motion. He bows his head and glances up suddenly at the sound of a door swinging open. Hugh stands there, an awe struck look on his face. John begins to speak. 'Ru' That's all that is heard, a blast masks the rest in a loud blanket, this time non-silenced. Hugh throws a silver platter complete with four small glasses and a bottle into the air as he slams backwards into the door, triggering it, and falling through it. The door hangs open as his lifeless body blocks its closure. Footsteps can now be heard upstairs as John's tears begin to stream down his cheeks. Suddenly he shoves himself to his feet with an adrenaline rush and bum-rushes the man with the twin guns. 'Tobias...' Tobias is very fast as he spins, pulling an escrima from his side satchel, and slamming it into the mouth of Johnny Russell. Russell flies up into the air and then down, blood being spit above his face and then landing on his cheeks as he hits the ground with a thud. He turns his head to the side, spitting out a bloody chunk of what used to be his teeth. With a sudden silence downstairs, footsteps are heard above the heads of these three. 'Ah, the pitter patters of little feet, John. Isn't it truly a blessing?' A tough act comes over John as lifts his head. He speaks with difficulty now, having lost over half of his teeth. Blood seeps from between his lips, his nose is no doubt broken, and he'll be lucky if he can have his jaw wired and reset. He somehow manages words through all of this. 'Whath thu you have in min'd? Thu fuckin' basthard!' Reno motions towards his face. 'Finish what you started, Tobias. When he can't talk, I'll be happy.' With this, Tobias steps in and in view of the camera. He lifts an arm and brings it down harsh and fast. The crack of the weapon is sickening and it only happens once. Toby steps out of the way so that we can see the aftermath. John lies unconscious on the floor, his jaw twisted down on one side. He still breathes with a huge hiss however. 'Get a lil morphine in his blood. Make sure he stays alive long enough for the paramedics.' Aiden holsters, what he calls, his twin deaths and quickly pulls a needle from his coat pocket. He injects Russell in the arm. Reno steps forward, his eyes peering at the top of the stairwell. He glances around again, his eyes focusing on the broken bottle of whiskey. 'Go get the rest of Mr. Russell's American Dream, Toby. I'm going to get a little of his finest chardonnay. No use in it going to waste, yes?' Toby rushes up the stairs quickly, tapping his twin escrima's together. At the same time, Aiden pulls a rolling chair in from a nearby computer room and pulls Russell into it. He ties his head back so that it doesn't lull over so that he can see the festivities. Reno walks calmly into the next room. A few seconds pass as Toby makes his way downstairs, a young blonde haired boy in one arm, a small knife in the other hand. She jumps and runs to her husbands side, dropping to her knees and crying into his lap. Her husband looks more like a Tzimisce Prank than a human being at this moment. Toby drops the young boy onto his feet and Aiden draws his guns, aiming one at a child and the second at the mother. 'Let's form a nice neat little row, shall we?' Trinity quickly grabs her two children, both in tears now, and holds them to her, kneeling next to them. Tears are welling in her eyes as well. 'What are you going to do to us? We've done nothing to deserve this!' With this, Reno steps from the kitchen and grins, holding up a bottle of chardonay and four glasses. 'Ah, Trinity. A lie told often enough becomes the truth. Lenin said that...' Trinity jerks up, standing and hiding her children behind her as her eyes focus on the man now standing before her. 'Maverick... you. But... but why?' 'No the question is how. You see, how can you say you are innocent when your own cousin was taken from his home, beaten until inches from death, his family slaughtered, and then you marry his assailant?' Sure, this may be a bit extreme for an angel but eh, it's all good.' Trinity screams out, her eyes full of tears. 'You're no angel, Maverick! A devil perhaps!' 'I prefer Le Diabolique, Trin. Toby, take her tongue away from her.' Trinity spins just in time to catch an escrima to the side of her face, knocking her to the floor and completely unconscious. 'I hate to destroy your lives and run but I really must, early meetings you know.' The two children and quickly grabbed and their hands tied together to their mothers. Their mothers hand is then quickly tied to the staircase. The other hands of the children are then tied to small strings that are hooked to the ears of John. He is then carried from the house and carried to a small clearing in the front yard where he has a full view of the home. Aiden quickly gets into the car and moves it away from the house. 'You really should watch, John. This will be fun.' Reno smiles and pulls a black rose from inside his trench coat. He attaches a small weight to the bottom then lights the pedals. He grins and hurls the weight, taking the rose and the flame through the air and into the house. Tobias quickly closes the door and runs back to Reno and John. '... by the way. Found the gas line downstairs.' John's eyes widen and he jerks a bit, only causing himself more pain. The back of the house explodes suddenly, fire shooting into the air in all directions. 'When the strings stop jerking, John, that's when the fire has reached them. Pray they don't stop jerking before the fire department arrives.' Reno walks away now, Tobias at his side. The Porsche glistens, the reflection of the fire visible in it's paint. Reno walks to and gets into the car, quickly taking the drivers seat. Tobias jumps into the back again and the car speeds out of the driveway and off into the darkness. The house burning still. The scene fades. March 17th, 2004 - Approximately 12:00 PM New York, New York - The Echelon Chamber The scene opens much differently than the last ended. There is no snow and no fast car, no pile of mangled flesh that used to be a man. Just a large room complete with random artifacts to give it a comfortable touch. The walls are a dark finished oak and the floors, a black marble. The ceiling is quite different however; it's a stained glass room in one of the upper levels of the Echelon Tower; one of many rooms designed for the comfort of the Echelon. This particular room has a large rotating wall behind which furniture and other items are stored. Portraits of the current Echelon members adorn three walls while the forth is owned primarily by a black marble fireplace, the sides of which are sculpted dragons coming up from the floor. The Coat of Arms of the Echelon hangs above this. A fire burns diligently in the fireplace, hundreds of embers floating from the charred ruins. John Zero, dressed semi-casually in a white dress shirt, the sleeves rolled up, and the top two buttons undone, leans against the fireplace. His eyes are focused on the flames. Not far away is a black leather arm-chair seated atop a large black and gold area rub. In the center, a round table complete with a bottle of Chardonnay, two wine glasses, and an untouched crystal chess set. On the center of the board, The Coat of Arms of the Echelon has been etched in. Off to the side of this, another black arm-chair. This one, however, is occupied by Maverick Reno. He's dressed in a pair of black dress pants and a red shirt. His hair has been tied back, keeping it out of his face. He drums his fingers on the arm. Reno speaks quietly, breaking the silence. 'How is she?' 'Doctor says she'll be fine... they had only just started when i walked in... she passed out due to shock and fatigue, not pain and torture...: Zero walks back to his armcahir and pours a glass of wine for himself. he offers the end of the bottle to reno, who nods, and zero fills reno's glass as well. Reno speaks again. 'And Vincent?' 'Not a concern of ours anymore... permanently. What about you Mav? You look like shit.' Reno leans back, trying to find a comfortable position for his head. 'Very long night. Dealt with an issue that seems to interupt my sleep as of late.' There's another pause in the dialogue. 'Make sure to have the hospital send the bills here. I'll have Naria make sure that's taken care of.' Reno lifts the glass that Zero poured and nods his head in thanks. 'A toast in honor of Saint Patricks, John?' John nods. 'To the health of your enemies enemies.' 'Well said, John, well said.' Reno brings the glass to his lips, drinking of the liquid. John does the same. They sit the glasses down and Reno makes a gesture to the waiting chessboard, nothing more than a flick of the wrist. 'Shall we?' Zero turns his armchair to face the board, picking up a pawn moving it in place. Zero speaks up. 'The little bitch thinks she's smart, doesn't she?' 'But even with this truth, we must admit. She is quite the perfect advisary for us. Cold, heartless, efficient, and lacking in any way of escaping our reach.' Zero speaks again. 'And don't forget cunning... she had some forethought of what would happen by placing us in the same bracket....' 'She knew how it would turn out and she planned it so. The only problem is that we should give her a show she's not expecting... and most importantly, not let the competition divide us. The comradery amongst the Consiliu is what makes us strong.' Reno reacts again, watching John's most recent move, and begins to block his path in the center. 'Something unexpected...something unusual....something that we are both very good at....' Zero picked up his rook and slides it several places over. He continues. 'A duel perhaps?' Reno responds, his eyes on the chessboard. 'Brazen move, John. A duel? You're on the right path but we need something with more finess.' Reno plays quickly. He slides a rook into line, defending his back line. 'What about something that can show our many talents? ' Zero appears to abandon the rook, bringing the opposite sided bishop out. 'A showcase duel? Something featuring many weapons, allowing us to show off.' Reno watches the board, his mind wandering and mulling. Finally he responds. 'Yes, I like it. But it needs more... plus, we need to avoid the cliche of those old hardcore matches.' There's a pause in conversation as Reno slides a knight over, protecting his rook twice over. 'I always hated those things.' There's another pause before Zero responds. 'They are a permenant pain in the ass that requires no skill. Just a large blunt object and a fool to break said object over. We need to be able to make this more sportmanlike.' Reno watches the board, mapping move after move before he responds. Suddenly, Zero picks up his bishop and slides it across the board, taking out a pawn on the king's side of the board. the king is slightly boxed in. 'Check.' 'Damn you. But why don't we show up with weapons that actually require skill? More of the artform weaponry? Katanas, gunseens, etcetera.' Reno moves his king forward to where his king pawn once sat. A silence fills the air before Zero responds. 'Exactly. On both accounts.' 'But we'll need a method of drawing attention from the plebeians. Any ideas?' 'We could always hold it on a rooftop... they always hold a flair for the dramatic' Reno responds quietly, placing his hands on his clutched fists as he watches the board. 'It sounds like a good idea. So a roof top melee?' John nods, speaking quietly. 'Yes. A rooftop melee it is.' Reno nods and leans back in the leather arm chair, sipping at his drink. 'May the best man win, John. And may he become the champion.' 'Yes' Zero raises his glass. 'To the champion.' Reno raises his in return and lightly clings them together. 'To the Champions. To the Echelon.' With this, the scene pans back as the two men continue their game of chess. The scene slowly fades to black. I'm coming for you.
