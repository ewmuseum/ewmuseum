An Unwilling Hero: A Fat Burden

10 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 10, 2005 A long, long time ago Carlos Fraga was the lackey of one of professional wrestling’s biggest stars. Night in and night out Carlos would put his body and pride on the line to make sure that his boss won every match. Carlos was good at his job; he has the scars to prove it. Everything went well until one fateful night when Carlos’s boss, Mike Biddle, ordered him to take his place in an extreme match. Carlos was greatly outmatched but still tried his hardest to win. His reward for his efforts was a botched piledriver that put him in a coma. Carlos did not awake from the coma for months, and when he finally did awake he had lost the ability to walk. Unable to walk, Carlos slipped into a deep depression. He tried to drown his sorrows in Twinkies and gained a tremendous amount of weight in the process. Biddle took pity upon Carlos sent him to the best rehabilitation clinic in the country. Within weeks Carlos regained use of his leg muscles and returned to Beverly Hills with the hope of regaining his job. Unfortunately Biddle informed Carlos that his services were no longer required. However, as a favor to Carlos’s grandfather, William Bastafar, Mike allowed Carlos to stay with him at his Beverly Hills home since William had no room for the portly young man in his apartment. As time went on Mike began to regret his generosity. Carlos spent his days glued to the television while eating every form of junk food known to man. The only times Carlos would get up off the couch would be to use to the restroom and get more food. It had gotten to the point that Mike could no longer bring female company over to the house because of the grotesque nature of his housemate. When a woman laid eyes upon Carlos the usual reaction was a queasy look followed by a lame excuse to leave the house. Mike had had enough of this and had resolved to fix the problem once and for all. He couldn’t kick Carlos out but he could force the idiot to change his ways. Mike arose slightly earlier than was his custom and dressed for his morning jog. As Mike descended the staircase he heard the familiar sounds of Saturday morning cartoons, a sigh escaped his thin lips as he wondered whether or not Carlos had gone to bed yet. Biddle walked into the living room and found Carlos passed out on the couch, his upper body fat jiggled endlessly as he snored. A disgusted look distorted Mike’s handsome features as he laid his eyes on the horrible scene before him. Potato chips were scattered all over Carlos’s sleeping body, the couch, and the floor. A soda can laid on its side on the coffee table, the contents dried and sticky to the touch. Biddle could stand it no longer and reached down with an open hand and slapped Carlos across the face, an action that sent drool flying all over the leather couch. “What the heck,” Carlos exclaimed as he held his cheek in pain. He looked up and was further startled when he saw Mike Biddle standing over him with his arms crossed, impatiently tapping his foot and a very unhappy look carved upon his features. As Carlos became more awake and aware of his surroundings he became increasingly worried. “Why did you wake me up?” Carlos asked with a quivering tone. Mike looked at the disaster area before him and then glared at Carlos. “Because it’s time for a change you little asshole,” Mike stated firmly. Carlos began to wonder why things needed to change, from his point of view things were great. “Why do things need to change?” Carlos inquired. A great sigh escaped Mike’s thin lips as he lost his patience with Carlos’s stupidity. “Because you’ve turned my house into a disaster area. It’s so bad that I can’t even bring women over,” Mike paused and saw that Carlos clearly didn’t understand what was being said, “I’ll put it to you this way, if it wasn’t for your grandfather I would have kicked you out a long time ago.” Mike smiled with satisfaction when he saw that the fat idiot finally understood what was going on. “You’re not going to beat me up are you?” Carlos asked with a frightened tone. “I wish I could,” Mike answered solemnly, “but I promised William I wouldn’t. So instead I’m going to force you to change your lifestyle, from this point forward you are going to be training with me every day.” A horrified look came over the pudgy face of the lazy young man. “You mean I have to exercise?” Carlos asked. “Yes,” Biddle answered, “and today is the first day so get your fat ass off that couch because its time to go for a jog around the neighborhood.” Mike then grabbed Carlos by the collar of his gray t-shirt and pulled him up onto the floor and then told him to put his shoes on. Biddle then dragged Carlos out the front door and out into the hot California sun. Carlos squinted his eyes due to the intense brightness of the sun; he had not been exposed to sunlight in months. Biddle pushed him forward from behind, forcing him to maintain a quick pace down Crescent Street. Many tourists stopped to stare and take pictures of the duo as they pushed their way through the crowded sidewalks. “I think I’ve learned my lesson,” Carlos wheezed. Biddle simply ignored him and continued to push the overweight and sweaty young man in front of him forward. ***** The houses of Compton made for a very stark contrast to those of Beverly Hills. Compton was a place Mike Biddle had never actually been to; he had only driven past it on the freeway and occasionally heard its name on the news. Likewise, the population of Compton rarely gave any thought to Mike Biddle. As far as they were concerned Biddle was just another in a long line of rich idiots from Beverly Hills, the sort of man that the police force actually bothered to help out. One group of four young Hispanic men, members of a violent street gang known as the Southside Locs were plotting ways to make money so they could afford to buy some new guns to aid them on their quest to totally annihilate their enemies. The room they sat in was almost bare except for a torn couch, an old coffee table, and a small television set. The air of the room was smoky from the cigarettes and other things that the group smoked while they thought of ways to “earn” money. All four of them vacantly stared at the television; most of their best ideas came from the television. The show they were watching suddenly went to a commercial break and the first commercial was an advertisement for the film Gilligan’s Island; a movie that was now dedicated to the memory Bob Denver. Scenes that featured Mike Biddle flashed across the screen and the announcer’s voice explained Biddle’s greatness to the folks at home. “Hey vatos,” said Stomper, the leader of the group. “I have an idea!” Stomper waited for his three amigos to snap out of their stoned stupor and pay attention to him, once he was sure he had their attention he explained his idea. “I bet that Mike Biddle fool has a lot of money,” Stomper began, “and we should jack his shit or something.” “Yeah vato!” Exclaimed Spanky, the smallest of the group. “Or we could kidnap his family and make him pay.” An idea had finally been found and the four-gang members congratulated their leader on such a wonderful idea. “I heard that he jogs around Beverly Hills this time of day man,” another member offered, he was known as Lazy. “You sure we should jack someone during the day man?” Asked the second in command, known to his friends as Creeper. “Don’t trip about it homey,” Stomper replied, “all we have to do is back him into an alley away from all the tourists. Shit I bet that fool probably carries hella money on him while he jogs too.” “We should get going if we’re going to jack his shit now man,” Lazy said as he reached for his car keys. The members of the Southside Locs rose from the tattered couch and went out front to where Lazy’s banged up blue Impala rested in the driveway. They then sped off towards what they hoped would equal a lot of money. ***** Mike couldn’t help but feel satisfied as he continued to push Carlos down the street past the many curious tourists. The duo now jogged down Wilshire Boulevard, Biddle only allowed Carlos to stop once every fifteen minutes for a drink from the water bottle that Mike carried with him. During one such water break a banged up blue Impala slowed down as it drove past them, the four occupants turned their heads and looked at them with menacing stares that even caused Mike to gulp from fear. “We’re going to take the shortcut back home,” Mike said as he watched the Impala drive off into the distance. Carlos said nothing as he followed Mike into a nearby alley, he was just glad that he didn’t have to jog anymore. There was an eerie silence as the two made their way down the alley. The silence was broken by a Hispanic voice that said, “How are you doing gringo?” Mike’s worst fears were realized when he looked up and saw four men standing around a banged up blue Impala. They appeared to be gang members. They all wore khakis and white t-shirts, the man that was obviously the leader had a short sleeve blue flannel shirt on with just the top button buttoned. The four men were also covered in tattoos that proclaimed their loyalty to their barrio and their faces were covered by blue bandanas, confirming Biddle’s suspicions that these were gang members. As a rich white man, this was the scariest moment of Biddle’s life. The leader of the group walked towards them, one hand in his pocket presumably holding a gun or some other weapon. “Give us your money,” the leader said. Mike was able to identify the leader as Stomper due to a tattoo that was on the man’s left forearm. “I, uh, don’t have any money on me,” Mike said as a nervous sweat dripped down his forehead. “What the fuck do you mean you don’t have any money on you?” Stomper yelled. Mike gulped. “I don’t carry money on me when while I’m jogging,” he answered. Stomper silently cursed to himself as he began to wonder what to do. He was about to suggest that they go to the bank when he noticed the quivering figure of Carlos cowered behind Mike. “Who the fuck is the fat ass?” Stomper asked with a raised eyebrow. Mike looked behind him and began to worry that Carlos would cause him to get shot. Mike then turned and looked at Stomper and spoke, “He’s my manager’s grandson, why?” Stomper’s brown eyes lit up when he heard Biddle speak these words, for a great idea had suddenly come to him. Stomper turned and motioned for his three amigos to come over to him. “Grab the fat kid,” Stomper said as he pointed a finger at Carlos. Stomper restrained Biddle as the other three gang members grabbed Carlos. The portly young man struggled while they tried to force him into the Impala. To solve this problem one of the criminals pulled out a gun and pistol whipped Carlos on the back of the head, effectively knocking him out and allowing the gang members to load him into the backseat of the car. The three men then got into the car and the driver started the engine. The man in the rear passenger side seat motioned for Stomper to get into the car. Stomper leaned over and whispered into Biddle’s ear. “You’re going to have to pay out the ass to get your fat ass friend back you rich bitch.” Stomper then released his grip and got into the car. The gang members then sped away and just like that Carlos was gone and Mike was completely alone. A smile stretched across Biddle’s thin lips when the full gravity of what had just occurred hit him. He was rid of Carlos for at least a week or two, which meant that he could now bring women over to his house without the fear of embarrassment. However, as Biddle left the alley and began walk towards his house he began to feel guilty. He had to at least inform William of the situation so that it could be dealt with properly. Perhaps he could even talk William into sending Carlos back to his mother once the ordeal was through. Mike decided he should stop by William’s office before going home so he could make his long time manager aware of the situation. ***** “It all happened so fast,” Mike said to William after he was done explaining what had happened to Carlos. William leaned back in his chair, a very worried look painted upon his face. Mike had burst through the door of his office and claimed that there had been an emergency, William had never thought that it would be something of this magnitude. “Should we call the police?” Mike asked. William leaned forward on his desk and looked Mike straight in the eye. “No,” the older man began, “if we get the police involved then the media will pick up on the story and my daughter will find out that her son is being held hostage by a group of bloody hooligans,” William shuddered at the thought of what his daughter would do if she knew of the situation. “Let’s just say that it wouldn’t be good for our well being if she knew about this,” William finished with a nervous laugh. “Then what are we going to do then?” Mike asked. “No,” William said, “what are you going to do?” “What do you mean what am I going to do?” “You’re the only one that can rescue Carlos lad,” William said. Biddle slumped back in his chair, he knew William was right but he wasn’t happy with the situation. Mike rose from his chair, if he was the only one that could save Carlos he might as well start by trying to find the identity of the kidnappers. “Be sure to let me know of your progress lad,” William said as Mike walked out the door. Mike said nothing as he left William’s office, the last of his good mood had disappeared. Why should he save someone that he doesn’t even like? ***** When Biddle returned home he noticed an unmarked manila envelope taped to the front door. He grabbed the envelope as he walked into the house. He sat down on the easy chair in his living room and opened the envelope. What he pulled was a piece of paper with letters cut out from the magazine and newspaper. The note simply read: If you want to see your fat ass friend alive again meet us in the alley again next Saturday with one million dollars! Mike sighed when he read this, there was no way in hell that Carlos was worth a million dollars. Mike tossed the ransom note into the garbage and went to go clean himself up, after all, with Carlos gone he could bring women to his house without the fear of embarrassment. ***** At the same time in Compton, Carlos Fraga had started to come around. As he awoke he winced in pain from a rather large headache. He tried to reach up to hold his head but found that his arms were restrained. Carlos looked down and found that he was bound to a wooden chair with duct tape. He looked up and saw the four gang members sitting on a torn couch watching television and he suddenly remembered what had happened. He let out a loud gasp when he realized that he was no longer in the comfort of Beverly Hills. “Well look who decided to join us,” Stomper said when he noticed that Carlos was awake. “W-what do you want from me?” Carlos asked. “We don’t want nothing from you,” Stomper explained, “you’re just here so that your friend will pay us a million bucks to get you back.” Stomper finished his sentence with a winning smile. Carlos began to worry even more. He wasn’t sure if Mike would actually pay to get him back, especially after some of things he had said today. “What if he doesn’t pay?” Carlos asked. “Then we’ll kill you,” Stomper replied. Carlos began to cry, his fat jiggled in a disgusting fashion as he sobbed. This grossed the Southside Locs out immensely and finally Stomper said, “Will somebody shut that fat ass mother fucker the fuck up?” Spanky rose from his seat on the couch and produced his gun from his pocket. He pistol whipped Carlos on the exact same spot as he had earlier in the day. The four gang members laughed as they watched Carlos pass out once again, they knew it was only a matter of time until their big pay day came. For now, all they had to do was wait. -To Be Continued-
