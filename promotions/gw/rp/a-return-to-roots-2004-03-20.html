A Return To Roots

20 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.20.04 It had been a while since I had been upstate. I really never had a reason to. Well, I did, but I just avoided it. It hurt to much. But I needed to go see Nick... I hadn't seen him since Todd's funeral some months ago, and it hurt too much to talk to him for that long. Nick was his boyfriend of about four and a half years when Todd passed away. He's been a good friend of mine for many years before the two of them even began dating. He's one of the few people that I can just sit and talk to about anything, and now that Todd's gone... I need that. I simply hope he's doing okay. He sounded well enough on the phone, but, unfortunantly, you never can tell. He was pretty shaken up at the funeral... quite pale... but that's to be expected. The last time I talked to Todd's mom, she said Nick was pulling through okay. I'm glad she's doing better, also. Jackie had been like my own mother growing up, so her well-being was pretty much always on my mind. I'd almost forgotten how lovely the trees were this time of year. Of course, after being in the car for nearly four hours, you really start to appreciate things like that much more than usual. As I pulled off on the Hudson Falls exit, my anxiety only got worse. Here I was, not fifteen minutes from Nick's house, and I had no idea as to what I was even going to say. I knew that there was going to be the everyday small talk... that we would discuss Todd for some time. However, I knew that I'd been compelled to go up there for some reason in particular. I just had no idea what it was. Perhaps to cleanse my soul. Maybe it was just to adhere to my conscence about making sure Nick was okay. Regardless, it would come to me when I got there. The trees really are quite lovely. I pulled into Nick's driveway just at dusk. I suppose he saw me pull up, because he was coming out of the door as I was exiting my car. Things were still a little odd for me as he made his way over, but when he welcomed me with a hug, for some reason, all of the anxiety went away. 'Nice Mercedes. Is it new,' He asked. 'Yeah, I bought it just after I went back to GW,' I responded. 'I saw that. Jackie called to let me know about it. You're entrance still gives me chills,' he laughed. 'Thanks, but I think it may have gotten a little stale.' 'I think it's great.' 'Well, once again, thanks.' Nick's smile quickly faded, however, upon the continuation of the subject. 'Your return speech... it was... eh... a little harsh, huh?' 'I don't know if I would go so far as to say harsh. Honest is more like it. I carried all of those people on my back for years, and how did they repay me? By forgetting me. They spat in the faces of my friends because of who they are, only to turn face when they found out we'd known each other for so long,' I responded, referring to the occassions Todd and Nick would come to my shows, only to be heckled by all the worthless piled of elephant dung in the crowd that claimed to be my fans. 'Ken, people will be people. Not all of your fans are like that and it didn't really happen that often. You turned your back on the people that put you on the top of the mountain...' I cut him off before he could lie to himself anymore. 'The only people that carried me to the top of that mountain are myself and a few select others... none of which are quote-unquote fans. Those people never put a title around my waist, Nick. They didn't bleed in the tournaments for me. They never fell through ten stacked tables from the top of a scaffold to win me that Death Match trophey. Not once, did they ever do anything for me.' Suddenly, it was quiet again. Neither of us looked at each other... just at the ground. As if we were having a conversation with our feet. 'I'm sorry,' I said. 'I didn't come here to talk about this.' But I had. I realized that instantly. I had to tell someone whom I knew didn't see things through the same eyes as me the truth. How I felt and why I had done the recent things in passing. 'I know,' Nick responded, 'but it does sound like you needed to get it off of your chest. Why don't we go inside and have a drink. You still sticking with Scotch? I think I have some Macallen.' 'That would be great.' We went inside his house and talked about various things over the span of the next hours... life in general, careers, Todd... but my mind never once escaped the true reason for my being there. For all of those years, those sheep followed me only in hopes of pushing me off of the mountain once we made it to the summit. They never cared... not about me... not about themselves... not about anyone else. My dislike turned to distain and I grew to loathe all of them. They were the ones that made what should have been the happiest times in my career the most stressful. They were the ones that had driven Special Kay away. They were the ones that formed the wedge between mine and Gunner's relationship. They were the ones that pitted myself against two of my best friends in the world in Brock and Josh. They were the ones that formed what used to be a hatred between myself and one of the men I trust and respect more than anyone else now in Ace. They were the ones... who killed Todd.
