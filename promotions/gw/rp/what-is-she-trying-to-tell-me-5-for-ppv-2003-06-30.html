What is she trying to tell me? (#5 for ppv)

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-30-03 It's been said that the sport we all live for is a sort of soap opera for a different breed of person. I don't think they meant those of us who put our body on the line week in and week out. ---- Home. I don't know what could make tonight any better. My wife and I are cleaning up after a small get-together with one of my brothers, my best friend, and their respective girlfriends. 'That steak was very good hun,' comments Cindy, while picking up the plates from the table. 'Nobody left any of it on their plate.' 'Well, you prepared it, all I did was grill the damn things.' 'Yeah, and you did that very well.' 'Why thank you,' I reply, giving her a small kiss on the cheek before going back to scraping the grill clean. rrrriiiinnnngggg....rrrriiiinnnngggg.... I set the scraper down and run over to my cell phone. 'Hello?' 'Derek, this is Eliza, Mark's wife. What I have to tell you is very important. Do you understand?' 'Yes.' 'Ok, well I understand you were in contact with Salaok earlier in the day.' 'Yes, I spoke with him earlier today.' 'Well, he and the Harvesters are at the hospital nearest Salaok's house. Lisha has been shot.' I gasp for air a little, then manage to choke a few words out. 'Oh my god! How did it happen?' Eliza pauses for a second, and I can hear a voice in the background. 'It was an assassination. They all know who it was, but refuse to tell anybody that wasn't there. Mark hasn't even told me who. I'm telling you everything I know.' 'Oh my god. I can't believe this.' 'Please tell Cindy, I know the two were friends outside the ring, as well as former opponents inside. Also, I ask that you don't tell anybody besides your wife. The Harvesters, minus Salaok of course, talked about it and decided only people he was close to outside the ring should know, and one of the first names that came up was yours, thanks to Mark. I know he's put you and Cindy through hell, but at a time like now I ask that you can forgive us for everything.' I bite my lip, trying to hold back the choice words I have. Later. I'll save them for later. I can't do that right now, not with Lisha so freshly gone. Eliza continues. 'But Derek, please keep Salaok and Darrell, as well as the rest of the family in your thoughts. Just that would mean so much to all of us.' 'Yes, I will do that. Thank you for calling and letting me know.' I hear a click on the other end, so I too flip my phone shut. I drop it to the table, and slowly turn to face Cindy. She comes over to me and looks into my eyes. 'Derek, what is it? Is everything ok? Who was that?' I don't know what to say. So I do the only thing I feel is rightHe pulled me close to him and gave me a hug. 'Derek you're scaring me. What was that all about?' I step back a little, so I can see the look on her face when I tell her this. 'Cindy, that was Eliza.' Yup. Pure surprise. Not an exciting surprise, more of a 'why the hell' surprise. 'Eliza? Holy hell, what did she have to say?' I try to explain this to Cindy in a way that she would take as lightly as possible. 'Mark asked her to call. He's busy, otherwise he'd have called himself. This isn't business stuff, Cindy. It's personal. The Harvesters are all at the hospital nearest Salaok's home. Lisha...' I pause to wipe my eyes before a tear runs down my cheek. 'What about Lisha? Lisha what?' I sigh and look deep into her huge brown eyes. 'Lisha was shot. They know who it was, but can't say.' I frown a little as my wife's gorgeous eyes darken with the news. 'Oh my god, is she ok?' Now comes the bad part. 'Salaok and Darrell were at her side when she passed. Eliza did, however, tell me one other thing.' 'That is sssooo horrible!' cries the woman pressed against my chest. This next part will hurt me almost as much as it will hurt my beautiful wife. 'She was pregnant also. She was shot during the celebration dinner.' Cindy cringes and sobs, her tears soaking into my shirt. After a minute or two she looks up and tries to dry her tears. 'Derek, I have something I have to tell you.' What does she mean? She's never really said anything like that before. Before she had always just come out and told me. 'Well? What did you have to say?' 'Derek.. I love you so much. I love you more than anything. And I'm so glad you got your release from Global Incorporated. Now you can spend more time with your family.' 'Cindy, I'd love to spend more time with you. Especially after what happened to Lisha, it's a reminder that life is so fragile. I would like nothing more than to spend more time with you.' 'Well, can I prove you wrong?' asks Cindy. What does she mean? There IS nothing i would like more. 'If you can, go for it.' 'I would have to say that I know you better than everybody else. Maybe even as well as you know yourself. Now, I know you say you would love to spend more time with me. But, knowing you as I do, you will freak out after I say this.' She pauses, leaving me hanging on the edge of sanity here. I know she can see the question in my mind, and luckily she doesn't leave me like this long. 'Derek, I'm pregnant.' Instantly, thoughts run through my mind. Holy shit. I'm going to be a father. Are we ready for a child? These thoughts are shattered just as quick as they come to me. Of course we're ready. Our love is stronger than anything else. Nothing can change that. Love conquers all, even if the recent events with Salaok and Lisha don't prove that immediately. 'Oh my god, that's great!' I say, totally overwhelmed. Cindy looks deep into my eyes with a look of sheer radiance. I smile and lean down to give her a soft kiss on the cheek, but she turns her head an inch and we lock lips while sharing one of the tightest embraces ever.
