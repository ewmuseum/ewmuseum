The Journey: Part 11/Joshua's POV(Parental Advisory

7 January 2005

Date: 1-07-2005 NOTE: For this promo I attained the permission of the person whom is used in this promo to use his character so its all good.. Early Afternoon Well good evening friends and loved ones and welcome once again to the story that doesn’t end. This is the story, which began back in October of last year and now has actually gone into its 11th part as of this point in time! Truly the longest that a story of mine has ever gone and we show no signs of stopping anytime soon! This is the story of how one man’s journey truly has started to become somewhat of a legend! Now on this day in particular we find Joshua sitting around in his office practically bored to death! I mean if there was a crime for “Murder by boredom” he’d be guilty of course his would be a suicide but you get the idea right? Joshua was trying to keep himself as juiced up as possible for the PPV, which is in a couple of days and honestly he knew that if he confined himself to his bedroom all day he’d end up going nuts! Joshua decided that it was best if he went out of the house for a little while and decided also that a shower would be necessary if he was going to leave the house for a little while. Did he tell anyone? Yeah he told Ami and Michiru. Did he want it kept secret? No but if Ami and Michiru decided to keep it secretive for him then that was cool too because that was a sign of respect that Michiru showed her friends and she has for the longest time considered Joshua such. When Joshua walked into the bathroom he brought his change of clothes into the bathroom with him, which wasn’t always that uncommon for him but its what was with that stack of clothes on top of the pile that was! Ok now here is a warning from me to all of you right now: IF any version or variation of religious items being read in these things offends you then you know that you can stop at this point OTHERWISE the only thing I can and will say is this: Let’s continue? So Joshua has his clothes with him and on top of the pile was a chain that he scarcely wore but at the same time wished that he had worn it more because it was a chain with a cross on it that at one time or another (or so a relative of his says) it was blessed by the Pope in Rome, Italy! Now was Joshua L. Curtis, the “Golden Boy” of Professional Wrestling deeply religious? Not exactly but at the same time whether you knew it or not Joshua prayed to that man up in the heavens every night and not necessarily to pray for forgiveness because of what he was but because he mainly wanted to thank him for allowing him to be alive to achieve the dream that he has set out for and that is to become one of the greatest wrestlers and one of the nicest human beings alive today! I think he’s got a good start on that already don’t you? Survive that? Good cos we aren’t done yet and I want you attention for the rest of this story?. So as Joshua turned on the water to the shower he put that case that the chain was in on a separate side of the sink so that he would put that on last (he likes to have it hanging out of his shirt in case you are wondering) and then he walked into the shower and stayed in there for a good couple of minutes (30 or so) and he took care of his task of cleansing and also thought about the family that he has assembled and every time he did it just put a smile on his face that no one’NO ONE could erase no matter how abrasive they were! After about an hour total Joshua had dried off and done the whole nine yards before putting his cross on and walked out of the bathroom. No one has ever seen Joshua wearing his cross or if they did it was rare but a welcome sight by all that met him and instead of being ridiculed for it a lot of people praised him and said that it didn’t matter whether he like guys or not but the fact was that God was watching over him and would forgive those whom judged him. As Joshua walked out of the house to get in his Viper, Ami came walking out of the house to give her brother a kiss goodbye before he left for a little while and Joshua always cherished these moments because lord knows they are very few of them for him in this day and age! As Joshua pulled into the city he thought about where he wanted to go eat and there was a restaurant that was outside of Time Square that he wanted to go to that he hadn’t been to in ages so off to the “Restaurant Gilbi” he went! Now for those that are wondering this restaurant wasn’t so much a “high class” place but it was like a 4th home for Joshua when he wanted to get a good bite to eat plus since the Owner knew his mother sometimes he’d cheat and give Joshua the food on the house! (SHHH! Our secret ok?) After about 20 minutes Joshua pulled his car into the parking lot and a trio of big, white dudes that looked like they were from a bike gang walked up to Joshua and Josh, whom outside of the ring doesn’t fight much was legitimately scared for his safety, yet the leader of the group didn’t seem like he wanted a fight’at least not yet! “Hey son” The leader of the trio said and the guy was a big, white, bald-headed dude that wore a black bomber jacket, with a black t-shirt that Josh couldn’t read at the time and basically the whole rest of the outfit was black but Josh knew that he might have to do something that he didn’t want to “do you know where the **** you are”!” The leader asked Joshua and Josh knew not to be smart with him but at the same time not to be bullied by the man. “Yes I do actually” Joshua began but might I ask what’s a dashing man like yourself picking on someone like me for?” Joshua asked and when he said the thing about the biker being a “dashing man” he could tell that that infuriated the man whom got so pissed off that he swung as soon as Joshua got out of the car but Joshua casually ducked out of the way and then gave the biker a spin kick to his back with such force that the biker went down to one knee! “Inpudent little?” was all the biker said as he got back up and charged at Joshua again but again Joshua dodged him and this Joshua elbowed him in the back of the head which really stunned the biker and all the while his two lackey stood there and watched the fight as they couldn’t believe what this short, scrawny, 190-200 pound kid was doing to their leader and when Joshua decided to get aggressive they still didn’t do anything even when Joshua took the guys arm and put it in a position to break it! “Now then darling we got two options here: either you leave me alone and this charade ends or we continue this game and I break your arm to the point where a prosthetic is your only option.” Joshua informed the biker and even when the biker was begging him to get off that didn’t work as Joshua wanted to hear him surrender? It never came? CRACK! Joshua had just snapped the guy’s arm in about 6 different places and Joshua felt bad but at the same time he warned the guy what was going to happen. One of the remaining two had decided that they wanted to avenge their comrade decided to come after Curtis but Curtis ever being the coy one let himself be caught for he saw something that apparently this guy didn’t?. While Joshua was fighting the leader someone came up from behind the only other comrade and laid him out with move that cracked the person’s head on the concrete and so there was no backup for the other one. “Hey fag!” the second biker said as he picked Curtis up by his shirt collar and dangled him in the air “What do you think you were doing to my buddy” Do you know who the hell we are?” the biker said and Joshua with a Cheshire grin on his face gave the only response he felt was appropriate at the time? “How’s your head feel darling?” “My head?” The biker asked quizzically before continuing “Ah just shut the **** up fag you hear me”!” The biker said but alas that was the last thing he got out of his mouth as the figure that had incapacitated his friend had his sights set on his next victim? KO! Joshua managed free himself before the biker fell forward and as he straightened himself up he looked at the figure up and down before deciding whether to say thanks or fight the person and boy was he in for a shock! Standing before him was a man that, while to his own admittance, Joshua had only met one time before but he knew him well? Josh, a former GW World Champion and World Crusierweight Champion had pretty much just helped save Curtis? backside in more way than one! “Thank you” Joshua began before he introduced himself just to be polite “my name is Joshua Curtis and as you may now I’ve been in GW for a couple of months now and I guess you pretty much know my deal as it was pretty much screamed out by these hooligans!” Curtis pointed out and the young man that stood before him nodded before speaking. “Indeed I do” The young man began “the name is Josh and I’m friends with a guy that you might know from GW. Yo familiar with Ken Partridge Jr.”” the American Youth asked and Joshua’s eyes lit up at the mere mention of his friend. “Do I” Kenny was my first tag partner in GW and a guy that I owe more to than any amount of money in the world could contain!” Curtis exclaimed before regaining his composure before continuing “Say I was just about to go get a bite to eat sir?” “Please call me Josh.” The American Youth told the young upstart and Curtis smiled before continuing “Very well Josh. Would you like to join me for lunch” I was just about to go and grab a bite to eat when these’people went after me. Luckily for me the cops know that I don’t start trouble and there were like 3 cops around there to see it so I shouldn’t be getting sued or anything in the near future.” Joshua said jokingly as the two turned to walk into the restaurant. “Mr. Curtis” The Hostess began “welcome back to the restaurant. Will you be taking your usual table?” The young lady asked and Curtis thought for a minute before turning to the American Youth and informed him that it was a booth in the back. The American Youth decided that maybe a little bit more of an open space would be needed and Curtis agreed as they settled on a table that was near the middle of the room. “This way please” The young hostess said to the duo as they made their way to the table and as they did the American Youth couldn’t help but notice that they were getting some interesting looks from observers and Curtis just turned to the American Youth as they got the table and decided to find out what was on the mind of the former champion. “Penny for your thoughts.” Curtis said jokingly and as the American Youth took his seat across from Curtis he openly divulged them to the rookie. “Do you always get looked at like this when you come into the place?” The American Youth asked and as Curtis got the two menus and handed one to the American Youth he smiled and answered his friend’s question. “I think its cos I came in here with someone or the fact that Zack isn’t with me. I swear we are as tight a knit a unit as they come but sometimes I wish he wouldn’t be so protective!” Curtis said as he opened the menu and without looking picked his selection already. “One of those relationships at times huh?” The American Youth asked as he noticed the chain around Curtis? neck “Who gave you that Joshua?” “Huh” Oh this?” Joshua said as he looked at the chain that the American Youth was observing “This was given to me by my grandparents back in London, England and I wear it a lot less than I should you know?” Curtis mentioned and the American Youth made a note of that before speaking again. “Do you not wear it because you don’t want people asking 20 questions about it or you just don’t want to start a big “Is Joshua Curtis really gay thing?” ? The American Youth asked and Joshua liked how pointed a question he asked so he HAD to answer it. “I don’t wear it a lot because there are people that really look at me like I’m nothing’NOTHING but a sinner because I date guys. Look I used to date women and it didn’t work out with the one girl that I really liked years back. When I told mom that I was gay she accepted it quicker than anyone else that I ever met! I don’t look at myself as a sinner and I don’t judge people for what they do and who they are. I serve his will but I do it in a way that is different from many others and a lot of people everywhere I go applaud me for it.” Curtis explained and the American Youth took all of that in with great interest. “I understand that completely and Joshua you have my word that he knows that you are doing your best and that is all he’s ever asked from anyone.” The American Youth said before he decided to bring it back to a more pleasant conversation “Want to know why I came here to see you?” The American Youth asked and Curtis’s interest was peaked “Sure.” Was all Joshua simply said and the American Youth decided to tell him that he had gotten an invite to come to Reload in some capacity but what capacity he couldn’t tell the young superstar. The two ended up having a nice lunch together and after that believe or not the restaurant picked up the check for what those two did prior to coming in. As the two left and went their separate ways Joshua Curtis had a feeling that somehow the American Youth was going to make his presence known at the first PPV of the year for GW! I will see you in part 12 Au Revoir!
