An Unwilling Hero: The Great Escape

9 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 9, 2005 The young, portly secretary with long curly brown hair kept staring at him with a longing gaze, and why shouldn’t she? He was damn good looking, or at least he thought himself to be. Mike Biddle’s medium length blond hair was perfectly combed, his bangs barely obscured his dark blue eyes. His blue Hawaiian shirt and khaki pants made him look out of place in the formal setting of the waiting room he sat in as he read an old copy of Reader’s Digest. He had been seated in the waiting room for the past twenty minutes as he waited to see his manager, William Bastafar, who was currently on the phone with his daughter trying to explain why Carlos hadn’t called her the past few weeks without letting on that her son had been kidnapped. Mike hoped that the phone call would go well, he didn’t want William in a foul mood when he had to explained that he hadn’t made any real progress in his rescue efforts. “Mr. Bastafar will see you now,” the secretary said with a dreamy voice. She sighed deeply as she watched Mike get up and walk into William’s office. She smiled at as he walked by but he ignored her presence like he always did it caused her to frown. Would a gorgeous hunk of man like Mike Biddle ever acknowledge her existence? The first thing Mike noticed when he entered William’s office was that the older man was seated in his chair facing away from the door. “Have a seat lad;” William said when he was aware of Mike’s presence. There were two chairs in front of William’s desk; Mike chose the one to the left. William then spun his chair around and faced his favorite client with a somber face. William’s resemblance to Sean Connery never ceased to amaze Mike and often made him wonder if William was Connery’s long lost twin brother. “Now before we get into the current situation with my grandson I feel that there are a few things we should discuss about this upcoming Evolution,” William said, his hands folded in front of his bearded face as if he was praying. Mike leaned back in his chair and crossed his left leg over his right one. “I’m the number one contender for the International Title,” Mike said, “there’s nothing to really discuss.” “Actually lad,” William said, “there will be a unification match between the Platinum Champion, the Television Champion, and the Cruiserweight Champion along with the top contender to each of those titles. That makes a total of six competitors...” “So what?” Mike asked. “You’re in the match and listed as the Television Title contender, that’s what,” William shot back. Mike’s arrogant facade instantly melted away at this news. “Why in the hell aren’t I in an International Title match?” “Because Christian Zenith is in the match,” William replied. Mike’s jaw dropped. “But Zenith gave me his title shot when I beat the crap out of Train for him.” “Apparently the committee in charge of GLOBAL doesn’t think you’re quite ready for the task of fighting for the International Title,” William said as gently as he could as to not wound Mike’s ego too much. Mike’s face turned red when he heard this. “I’m “Mr. Main Event” damn it! How dare they insult me this way!” “Calm down lad,” William said, “you could prove them wrong by winning the title unification match. You can also remind the public of the fact that you have a contract for an International Title match.” This seemed to have a calming effect on Mike, but the blow that had been dealt to his ego was evident in his eyes. “Now lad,” William said, “I need to know what your progress is on the situation with my grandson. His mother is starting to ask questions you know, it kills me to have to lie to her.” Mike took a deep breath and tried to think of a way to make it sound like he had actually been searching for Carlos instead of partying. “Well,” Mike began; “I did manage to find out that the gang that kidnapped him is from Compton.” William’s face lit up instantly. “Jolly good lad,” William said, “so you know his exact location?” Mike tried to avoid looking William in the eyes. “No I don’t,” he said, “but once we’re done here I’m going to go find out.” “How do you plan on doing that?” William asked, his tone suggesting that Mike better have a damn good answer. “Oh I have an idea,” Mike said, “and I’m planning on taking my bodyguard with me as back up.” “No,” William said, “don’t involve him in this just yet. Search for the house yourself and rescue Carlos yourself. Only involve the Brawler if things get violent.” Mike nodded his head in agreement; he would call his bodyguard over from Las Vegas and tell him to wait for further instructions. Mike looked up at the clock on the wall. “Well I best be going,” he said as he rose from his seat. “I’ll give you a call once I know where Carlos is.” The two men shook hands and then Mike set out on his unwanted quest to save Carlos Fraga. ***** The population of Compton was privileged to see a rare sight that day, a well-dressed white man walking the streets. He knocked on every door and spread the good word of God, he was a Jehovah’s Witness...or so they thought. The attractive young man that had walked all over the neighborhood handing out copies of The Watchtower was in fact Mike Biddle in disguise! The blond hair that normally hung over his eyes was slicked back, black sunglasses covered his dark blue eyes, he wore a cheap suit, and carried a book bag that contained copies of various religious literature printed by Jehovah’s Witnesses. It was an ingenious disguise and no one suspected his true identity, save for a few real Jehovah’s Witnesses that he had ran into during his performance. Why would a rich and powerful man like Mike Biddle disguise himself as a Jehovah’s Witness and knock on every door in Compton? The answer is quite simple; it was the only way that Mike could search for the gang that had kidnapped Carlos without arousing suspicion. The only drawback of the plan was that it had been hours and Mike had not made any progress. As Mike walked up the front steps of a very dirty white house with garbage strewn around the yard, he felt tired and disgusted. As he knocked on the door he decided that if this wasn’t the house he was searching for then he would just go home and try to think of a better plan. As he waited for someone to answer the door he reached into his bag and produced a copy of The Watchtower. A young Hispanic man looked out the window to see who was at the door, Mike became curious since the man looked very familiar but he couldn’t attach a name to the face. “What the hell do you want?” The man asked when he answered the door. Mike found it odd that the man kept looking around to make sure that they weren’t being watched. “I’m here to spread the good news of Jehovah my friend,” Mike said as he held a copy of The Watchtower in the man’s face. The man swatted Mike’s hand, which allowed Mike to get a good view of a “SSL” tattoo on the man’s arm. “Get that shit out of my face mother fucker,” the man said before he slammed the door in Mike’s face. Mike sighed heavily, that was the forty-fifth door to be slammed in his face today. He wondered to himself how the real Jehovah’s Witnesses did this every day. As he walked away from the house he suddenly remembered why the man had looked so familiar; the answer was in the tattoo. The man was the one that had pistol-whipped Carlos that day in the alley. A banged up blue Impala roared past Mike and pulled into the driveway of the house, this confirmed Mike’s suspicions. Mike looked over his shoulder and made a mental note of the house number. He then walked away with a smirk plastered on his thin lips; he now knew Carlos’s location. ***** Mike wasted no time in staking out the kidnapper's house. He parked a large windowless black van down the street from their location. His post was far enough away to not arouse suspicion yet close enough for him to observe the house unnoticed. He was crouched behind the front driver’s side seat with a pair of binoculars aimed at the house. His attire matched the decor of the van, all black. A black beanie even covered his precious blond locks. He had observed the house for hours; he was waiting for at least the majority of the gang to leave the house. As soon as they were gone he would spring into action and put this unpleasantness behind him for good. Sadly it seemed that those losers never left their house and Mike began to fear that he would have to wait for days on end until he actually got a chance to rescue Carlos. Finally, at around one-fifty in the morning Mike’s long vigil was finally rewarded. All four members of the South Side Locs made their way to the banged up blue Impala, piled in, and took off. Mike was shocked that they had finally left their house. He cautiously left the van and made sure that the gang was out of sight. Once he was sure he quietly crossed the street and stepped over the fence in front of the house. Once he was safely behind the house he pulled out a flashlight and began to search for a point of entry. He found a window that was pulled open; no screen was in place to block his entry. Mike grasped the windowsill and pulled himself through. He made no noise as he climbed down onto the floor inside. He look around, it appeared that he was in the bedroom from the video. “Carlos!” Mike called out into the barren wasteland that the kidnappers called home. Biddle listened carefully for a reply but only heard a muffled cry from another room. Mike left the room and followed the cry until he found Carlos in the living room. Carlos was tied to chair next to a small television showing America’s Funniest Home Videos; a piece of duct tape covered Carlos’s mouth. Carlos’s dark eyes became wide with excitement when he saw Mike and he began to wiggle with uncontrolled joy at the sight of his rescuer. “Duct tape?” Mike chuckled to himself, “I wish I had thought of that.” Mike then knelt down behind the chair and attempted to undo the knot. He was so engrossed in his work that he didn’t hear the sound of a car pulling into the driveway, he also didn’t hear the footsteps of four men coming towards the house...he simply assumed that it was whatever was on the television. However, the sound of the door being slammed shut startled him and he looked up to see the four kidnappers with a twenty pack of Budweiser. Stomper looked at the scene before him and had to pinch himself to make sure he wasn’t dreaming. “Well if it isn’t the stupid gringo,” Stomper said with a smile spread across his face. Mike didn’t know what to say he just stood there with his jaw dropped as he suddenly wished that he hadn’t of listened to William and had brought The Brawler with him. Stomper pulled a gun out of his pocket and pointed it at Biddle. Mike’s immediate reaction was to put his hands in the air and not make any sudden moves as he silently cursed Carlos for getting him into this situation in the first place. One of the other gang members approached him carefully and then forcefully grabbed him. The gang member then took out his own gun and pistol-whipped Mike on the head. Suddenly, everything went black. ***** Hours later Mike returned to the world of the living and he had a horrible headache that put any hangover he had ever experienced to shame. He tried to rub his head but quickly discovered that his arms were bound. It all came back to him now; he had been captured during his poorly planned rescue attempt. He looked up and saw that the South Side Locs were seated on their couch, empty beer bottles scattered all around them, and they watched at him intently. “Well look who decided to join us,” the leader said with an arrogant smirk on his face. If there was one thing Mike hated more than anything else, it was arrogant assholes. The gang leader continued, “Now we can get ten times as much now that we have your dumb gringo ass. Now fucking tell me who to get a hold of to get money in exchange for both your lives.” Mike rolled his dark blue eyes in annoyance. “You can call my bodyguard,” he said with a smirk on his face, “and if you can survive the ass kicking he’s going to give you then I’ll gladly pay your hospital bills.” Stomper growled as he threw an empty beer can at Biddle’s head that barely missed it’s target. “Don’t talk shit mother fucker,” the gang leader said through his teeth. Stomper then rose from his seat on the couch and approached Mike, obviously to take his aggression out on the smart-ass millionaire. The gang leader placed one hand around Biddle’s throat and lifted the other hand in the air. He was about to strike when suddenly a loud barrage of bullets hit the house! “DRIVE-BY!” Exclaimed one the gang members as they all hit the floor. Biddle leaned back in his chair until he hit the ground, he yelled for Carlos to do the same. Even though the attack only took seconds it still felt like hours to the occupants of the small house. Once the shooting was over one of the gang members looked out the front window to see a red car speeding off. “It’s them chap bitches from Inglewood,” he said. Stomper got up from the floor and dusted himself off. “Spanky,” he said, “stay here with the cash cows and the rest of us will take off after those little bitches.” “Three of us against them?” Creeper spoke up. “Vato, I tied that rope hella tight. They ain’t going nowhere so don’t even trip about it. It’ll take all of us to take care of this shit that just happened.” Stomper took a moment to think about this and decided that Creeper was right. “Fine,” he said, “but if those two are gone when come back there’ll be some shit.” Creeper nodded his head. The gang members quickly checked themselves to make sure that they had their weapons before they piled into the Impala and took off towards their enemies. Now that they were alone Mike laughed at the stupidity of his captors. Not only had they left him unsupervised but in reality the ropes around his wrists were not tied tightly at all and he easily slipped his hands out of them. Biddle got up and undid the knots around Carlos’s wrists, which were actually tied tightly. Mike then ripped the duct tape off of his Carlos’s lips, causing the portly young man to flinch in pain. “Thank you so much for rescuing me,” Carlos said with a choked up voice. “Never mind that now,” Mike said, “we need to get out of here before they come back.” Carlos said nothing and followed Mike out the front door and to the black windowless van parked down the street. “What if they come looking for us after they find out we’re gone?” Carlos asked with a frightened tone. “That’s why we’re going straight to Las Vegas after I pick up some stuff from the house,” Mike answered. Carlos smiled for the first time in weeks when he heard this. “You mean I get to be backstage?” He asked. “No,” Mike replied, “you’re going to be locked inside a hotel room.” “Why?” Carlos whined. “Because I have an important match and the last thing I need is to worry about you wondering off and getting re-kidnapped,” Mike said with a stern voice that silenced Carlos. Mike pulled into his Beverly Hills driveway and quickly ran into his house to grab his luggage. He then jumped back into the car and sped off towards the airport that housed his private jet. Mike got William on the phone and told him to meet him there, they were leaving for Evolution early. ***** An hour later the South Side Locs returned to their home, they didn’t even notice that the black van that had been parked down the street was now gone. The police had obviously not been at the house, they never responded to drive-by calls that didn’t include injuries. The gang had gotten revenge for the attack and they were comforted by the fact that there was one less rival gang to worry about now. Stomper entered the house first and when he saw that the two chairs were now empty a string of Spanish curse words escaped his lips. The other gang members came in to see what was wrong and they too were surprised by the lack of kidnap victims in the living room. “This is your fault,” Stomper screamed as he pointed a finger at Creeper. “I didn’t think...” “You never think bitch,” Stomper interrupted. Stomper took out his gun and pointed it at Creeper’s head. Creeper shrank back in fear, he knew Stomper was crazy enough to pull the trigger. “There’s no need for that vato,” Lazy tried to calm Stomper down but it was no use, his pleas for sanity were ignored. Three shots were fired and three bullets entered Creeper’s skull, splattering blood all over the room, the murderer, and the witnesses. Creeper lifelessly slumped down to the floor in a pool of his own blood. “Clean this shit up,” Stomper told the remaining gang members as he went to the backyard for a cigarette. Lazy and Spanky did as they were told, they didn’t want to suffer the same fate as Creeper. -To Be Continued-
