Reflections of the past.

20 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20th, 2004. Pressing the 'stop' button on the remote to my DVD player I quickly flipped both the television and the DVD player off simultaneously. 'That's enough tapes for today.' I wearily told my student, as she sit next to me on the couch. Glancing over at me, her nearly black eyes bore a curious look in them. 'So…why do you do it?' She asked. Glancing at her my bluish gray eyes met hers and I quizzically raised an eyebrow. 'Do what?' I asked my newest interest. 'Wrestling. What made you want to become a Professional Wrestler?' She asked me for the second time her full lips pursing into an impatient pout. Glancing over at her I seemed to be lost in thought as I absorbed her beauty. Since I had taken her under my wing she had slightly altered her appearance. Her long hair was now cut shorter than before as it hung just slightly past her shoulders and was now adorned with red streaks. Dressed in a pair of Capri jeans, a white wife beater and with her shoes kicked off she awaited my response. It was odd how people are brought together. This female who was no taller than five foot six and couldn't have weighed anything over one hundred and thirty pounds had shown enough skill to become a personal student of mine. And shockingly enough she had garnered my interest by besting me in the Wrestling ring. It seemed unlikely…yet it had indeed happened, much to my chagrin. It was all too bizarre…a scrawny kid named Josh had became a huge star and now a girl named 'Raki' from California was set to do the same under my tutelage and guidance. 'Well?' She impatiently asked, punching my arm playfully as she snapped me from thought. 'There are a lot of reasons.' I said, answering her question indirectly. 'Come on, spill.' She demanded as I reluctantly did so. Raising my feet clad in black ankle high Chippewa boots to the coffee table I smoothed out the wrinkles on my baggy black cargo pants which led up to my sleeveless back 'Social Distortion' shirt. Propping myself up on my elbow and with strands of hair falling across my vision I began to tell her the story. 'I've always been a very defiant person…someone who was forced to do something just to prove others wrong. I don't know why, but there is some insatiable appetite for danger that possesses my mind.' Closing my eyes as I said this my mind traveled back in time. ---- July 9th, 1999. The screeching of my alarm clock jarred me from a pleasant slumber and I reacted by jolting upright before dropping back to the bed and slamming a fist into my pillow as I groaned unhappily. It was nine-fifteen in the morning…an hour that I usually never saw thanks to my love for the nightlife. In fact I had to question myself as to what possessed me to set the alarm clock so early. 'What is today?' I mused silently to myself before I once again shot upright this time with a smile across my face. 'The ninth!' I joyfully yelled. Kicking the covers from my nude figure I sprung up in an instant my bare feet landing simultaneously on the hardwood floor. Standing erect I bounded across the room with a smile on my face for today was the biggest day of my life…July Ninth, my eighteenth birthday. Reaching onto the floor I procured a pair of black boxers and tugged them on as I proceeded to my closet. Opening my closet I saw it bare, save for a few essential items, those items being the clothes that I would wear today. Pulling out the black gym shorts and the white Nike T-shirt I stepped into the shorts and jerked the shirt over my head. Looking about for my shoes I spotted a pair of silver trainers. Dropping back onto my bed I pulled the trainers closer. Inside I found a pair of socks, which I of course pulled on before stepping into and lacing the trainers. 'Today is the big day…' I half-heartedly mumbled to myself as I stood from the bed once again, dressed comfortably. Walking across the floor my trainers made no noise as I stopped, knelt and shouldered the heavy black backpack that rested on the floor, inside were all of the things I treasured as well as some of the items I couldn't live without. Perhaps even a trivial item or two had somehow made its way in there but nonetheless the heavy backpack was now responsible for everything I owned. Without even bothering to shower or brush my teeth I was off, on my way, on my long journey to independence without a hitch…or so I assumed. The problem with assuming is bringing parents into the equation for often times they don't act as you have assumed they will…such was the case in this scenario. Stepping out of my room I quickly made my way down the stairs at a quick gait taking them two at a time, and when I reached the bottom of the stairs…I suddenly wished I was still sleeping peacefully. I sheepishly smiled at my parents as my father placed his paper on the kitchen table and peered at me over his glasses. 'Where are you going?' He curiously asked as my mother sat down her coffee and turned to look at me a perplexed stare on her face. 'That's what I need to talk to you about.' I said in a soft tone, causing them to look at one another questioningly. 'You haven't told us of your plans.' My mother informed me as she took another sip of her coffee. Pulling up a chair at the table I dropped my backpack onto the floor and rested my elbows onto the table. 'I haven't told you…because I'm not sure exactly how well you'll take them.' I said, punctuating my sentence with a sigh. 'Oh great…' My father moaned his demeanor already showing signs of impatient anger. 'Mom, Dad…' I began, looking at each one of them as I spoke their name. 'I'm eighteen today, so I've decided that I can't stay around here any longer…I have to heed my life's calling.' I said, knowing full well that I had just struck a match in a powder keg. At first there was stunned silence but I knew that wouldn't last long…not long at all in fact because less than five seconds after this revelation my father's booming voice shattered the silence. 'Your life's calling?' My father asked in a tone trapped somewhere between angry and confused. 'I'm going to become…' I paused as I took what could very well be my last breath before I finished my sentence. '…a professional Wrestler.' And that's when it happened…the explosion. It started with just a hateful stare…and then an angry twitch of the lips. Then the yelling would begin… 'A Professional Wrestler?!' My father screamed, seemingly outraged by my career choice. 'Yes Dad a…' Before I could even form a full sentence his voice cut me off. 'What kind of idiot are you? He screamed. 'You have a scholarship to the best college track program in the nation and you want to throw it away for that…that…Wrestling crap?' He was now standing apparently enraged. 'You're not serious about turning down the scholarship to become a Wrestler are you?' My mother gasped. 'Yes, I Am.' I said as I was quick to defend my chosen profession and of course my own freedom to be anything I so desired. 'No.' My father angrily stated in a matter of fact tone. 'What?' I asked him, rather bewildered by his statement. 'No, you're not going to become a Wrestler.' He stated as he glared at me from across the table. 'Yes I am, it's my life…I can do whatever I want.' I said trying to keep from growing angry and make this situation worse than it already was. 'No, I forbid you to Wrestle!' He screamed as his face twisted into a mask of anger. 'You can't tell me what to do!' I defiantly said in retaliation. 'Josh, don't talk back to your father.' My mother stated quickly taking sides with her husband. 'What are you going to do ground me?' I sneered at her. Yet it was mere seconds after that had been said that I was the unsuspecting victim of a hard slap across the mouth from my father that sent me from my chair to the floor. I hit the floor hard, instantly wiping blood away from a split lip. 'What the fuck?' I demanded as I rolled over onto my back. 'Don't you ever use that kind of language in this house! As long as you live in my house you will abide by my rules!' My father screamed at me as I slowly stood to my feet, shouldering my backpack. 'I don't want to live in this hell hole anyway!' I shot back as I brushed by my father. Apparently he wasn't content to just let me go as he grabbed me by the shoulder and slammed me hard into the kitchen wall sending dishes on a nearby shelf to the floor where the shattered. 'What did you say?' My father demanded as he towered a full three inches over me and weighed significantly more than the one hundred and forty pounds I weighed at that time. 'I said I don't want to live in this hell hole, so fuck your stupid rules and fuck you too!' I screamed at him as his eyes seemed to widen juxtaposed with both shock and anger. I tried to leave but he placed a hand on my chest and shoved me back into the wall with enough force that I heard the sheet rock behind me crack. That was it…I could take no more. I had been pushed too and beyond my breaking point and I hadn't spent all of those years studying Taekwondo for no reason at all. A situation had risen…and now it must be taken care of. Reaching up with my left hand I grabbed his wrist and twisted it firing off a snap kick that hit him in the groin at the same time. He slumped to his knees and with my free hand I punched him hard in the mouth and then blasted him in the face with a knee that sent him onto the floor on his back with blood now pouring from his nose. 'Leave me alone!' I screamed at him, my bluish gray eyes flashing with anger. 'Leave me the fuck alone! I repeated as I turned away from him. Storming away I walked through the living room where I kicked over the coffee table covered in family portraits. 'Stay out of my life you worthless piece of shit! You can burn in hell for all I care!' I screamed as I angrily stomped across the portraits hearing the broken glass crunch beneath my feet. Tears flooded my eyes as I grasped the doorknob and threw the front door open, rushing out into the oppressive July heat. My vision was blurred by the tangles of hair that had fallen across my face and the tears that rushed from my sockets. Wiping the blood from my mouth with the back of my right hand I ran across the sidewalk in front of my former residence and stepped out into the street in front of my parent's home. Secretly I was praying that a car would hit me to end my misery but with no vehicles in sight I quickened my face to an all out run, showing my track star speed as I ran. As I ran the problems seemed to roll off of my shoulders…I was running away from my problems, leaving them all behind. A new life would begin, a life of change…my life. ---- 'Josh?' Raki grasped my shoulder and shook me softly jarring me from the painful memory that held my imagination. 'Sorry.' Was all that I could force myself to mutter as I traveled back to reality. You never did answer my question…' She said forcing her full lips into a playful pout. Bowing my head I exhaled loudly in a state of mental anguish and emotional exhaustion. 'It's just been something I've always dreamed of.' I mumbled in a barely intelligible sentence. 'And sometimes…dreams do come true.' Closing my eyes yet again I leaned my head back against the leather couch as thoughts of what had happened four years ago shrouded my mind. It seemed miraculous as to how I had defied the odds. I had thrown everything back in their face and climbed to the top of the Professional Wrestling world. Whether or not they supported my decision didn't seem to matter because now I stood defiant, but best of all…now I stood victorious.
