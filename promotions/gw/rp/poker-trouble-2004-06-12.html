Poker Trouble

12 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/04 A little past Seven O'Clock and the crowd has already gathered in downtown Dallas. The locale for this great event, The Palace. Bystanders look on as some of the cities biggest men sit around a poker table, each with chips in their hands. However one seat is empty, and the crowd is whispering as to who exactly this seat belongs to. They don't wait too much longer before their guesses are answered. 'Horale! Horale! Horale!' Those at the poker table look up, and the crowd opens up as J-Gonz, the former Global Wrestling Television Champion walks up. His two men, Paco and Chocolate both behind, each in a solid black suit. Neither of the two look like happy campers. Gonzalez on the other hand seems to light the room up as he strolls to his seat. His black hair slicked back, and his purple suit lighting up the room. Theirs no question to how much money this crime lord has in his account, more to say around his neck. Gold chain after gold chain dangle from his neck, three rings on each hand and a solid gold rolex. 'What's going on homes?' J-Gonz takes his seat between two of Dallas' biggest club owners, Rafeal Lopez and Chico Morales. Both keep their clubs running from the protection of Gonzalez Enterprises. Rafeal lifts his hand as Gonzalez settles in. Chico looks over. 'Why the smile today?' 'It's a new day Chico, a new day.' 'You held the tournament up about twenty minutes.' 'No need to go hard Rafe! I had my reasons.' 'Having another business with Train again?' 'Hey look Chico, what I do on my own time is my business.' 'But when you get a lot of people mixed up, you get a lot of people exposed Gonzalez. You know that.' 'You don't think I know what I'm doing man? I've been doing this for sometime now. I'm not some two-bit kid just starting out.' 'I just don't like it man.' 'You don't have to Rafeal, it's my business. I watch out for me, you watch out for yourself.' The conversation between the the three men is interupted as the dealer shuffles out the cards. Theirs six people at the table all together, along with Chico, Rafeal and J-Gonz are prominent business owners, Howard Douglas, Phillip Dean and Pete Bass. 'Good-luck to all of you men tonight, remember antes are 1000 and 2000, also blinds are 5000 and 10000. All of you are starting out with 100,000.' All three men nod as the dealer calls out the rules and the blinds. The starting hands are dealt out, and J-Gonz finds himself with a pair of eights. The betting goes around with Chico and Phillip folding there hands. Pete calls the bet, and Howard folds. Rafeal calls Pete, and Gonzalez being in the big blind checks. 'It's a three way dance esse, what you got?' Neither of the two men answer J-Gonz as the dealer lays down the flop, an 8 of hearts, a Jack of spades and a king of diamonds. Pete goes all in with his bet, Rafeal folds and J-Gonz calls Pete. Both men lay down there cards and the club goes silent as J-Gonz lays down his eights, and then Pete lays down two eights also. 'What the...' Just that instant J-Gonz falls back kicking the table in the face of Pete Bass as he pulled out an M-9. The crowd in the club scatters and Pete Bass pulls the trigger to his M-9. The bullets blast up in the ceiling, a stray shot splits the head of an innocent bystander. 'Paco, Chocolate, load up!' 'Cocked and loaded jefe!' Behind Pete who has crawled behind a post, a door flies open and an FBI SWAT team hurries into the room. About ten of them come in shooting their gun. Paco and Chocolate dive behind the bar and unload their MK-57s. They take out the whole front roll of the SWAT Team. 'You pussy cats wanna play! Lets play homes!' As the words ring out of J-Gonz mouth he pulls out from behind a folded over table and in the line of fire takes out three members of the swat team with a super charged double barrel 12 guage! The other two members of the swat are taken out by Paco and Chocolate. 'It was a set up jefe! Some one leaked the word!' 'Lets get out of here.' Just as the three men turn to head out the back way, another team of seven men comes through the door. Pete Bass is still behind the poll. The first three men are taken down by the MK's, but the other four men dive behind tables and break their rounds. Paco and Chocolate dive out of the firing lane and watch as J-Gonz takes out two of the guys with his super charged 12 guage. 'Who hired you out Pete?' J-Gonz is barely heard over the gunfire as the room is being hit with more and more members of the SWAT team. 'Pete who hired you out!' 'Theirs a lot of people that want your head Gonzalez!' 'That's not what I asked! Who hired you out!' The room is covered in bodies, both live and dead. J-Gonz crawls on the floor trying to get to the exit. Behind him Paco and Chocolate are clearing the way. 'Jefe get to the door!' 'I aint leaving without you boys!' Just as things are looking dim for the three men, from the exit a masked man, covered in complete black fades into the scene. He uses a grenade launcher to blow the entire back end of the room. 'C'mon bro!' 'How'd you know I needed you?' 'Word leaked out on E-Bay! Now come on, I got a van parked in the back.' 'Pete I'm gonna find out who hired you, mark my word esse! This isn't the last time you hear of J-Gonz!' As smoke fills the club, Paco and Chocolate continue to unload until each one is safely out of the room. Both men hop into the van, with J-Gonz and the masked assistant. 'You got yourself into some trouble there didn't you bro?' 'Word leaked, they want my head.' 'Well now me and you are working together bro.' 'That's alright though homes, they want to war with J-Gonz, we'll go to war!' The van speeds off into the distant.
