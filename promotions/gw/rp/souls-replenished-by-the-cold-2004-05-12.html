:| Souls Replenished by the Cold |:

12 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.12.04, EvO match rp1 'The entire purpose of human existence is to kindle a light in the darkness of being. We are a nation of hollow stuffed people; bleeding together, heads stuffed with straw. Like wind in an empty street, or rats feet over broken glass in a dark and forgotten cellar. So it is written. So it shall come to pass.' :| Or so I thought. I woke up with sweat turning into frost upon my head before seeped out into a liquid state. Wind rustling: echoing at a haunting chorus. It all seemed dream real to me. There I sat in the middle of the snow half buried as if fate was trying to shun me away from the world, to kill off my existence. But I couldn't let that happen to me. I couldn't let society win this battle of the known vs. the forgotten. My hair whipped wildly in the wind, as I begun to stumble to my feet, looking around for some sort of savior but that day, the cold was my savior. |: 'Looks like you need help.' Some figure stood before me at whose face I couldn't quite make out. My vision was blurry and thoughts where razors in my mind when I tried to think of a reply, so I mumbled: 'Who are you?' The one question you ask when your state of perplexity is so strong you do not even trust your own instants. 'What happened to you? Do you know where you are?' He kept asking me questions I knew nothing about. I felt as if my memory was deleted purposefully from my head but then again, I felt a lot of things... 'Is there a reason you stand before me? I sense the winds of fate have brought you to me to be my savior but I will not accept a gift from that of which have taken so much from me already. If you cannot turn the tides of destiny that chosen me, me, me, only me-' loosing it for a moment, I didn't let emotions get the best of me. Like any true person, emotions mean nothing when standing face to face with fear. They only make you weaker; 'Chosen me to live a life of torture.' :| At this point there was really nothing left to say to this stranger that stood before me. The wind bit away at my face like ramped fire ants whose purpose are to strike terror before a sudden sharp pain struck the back of my head and black filled my deleted thoughts. |: 'It was for your own good, when you wake up, all will be clear of your soul purpose.' Spoken like fate her self, the figure spoke to my unconscious body in a monotone that echoed through empty head. ---- :| The storm had come back in my life. Thunder streaked through pregnant clouds like stomach cramps as the rain began to forecast the day to be a day of unfortunate mishap. I don't know where my head was, like thoughts of a blank mind. It was one of those days where reality and fantasy intertwined proving everything you thought to be right, wrong. My chapter concluded in PCW; happy endings don't always happen. My understanding of being unstoppable was ephemeron like the life of a housefly. A tear ran peculiarly up my face as my mind stumbled to answer what was not logical if I was right side up. Slowly splitting my eyes open, in a dazed state, I could make out other images hanging around me in this dark room before the sound of a huge metal door sliding open and light piercing through the benighted room. Footsteps approached. |: “Well, well, well, Mr. Itagaki. We thought we lost you for a moment.” The scientist said with a smirk before I heard his pen scribble on his clipboard, “we would’ve hated to loose you before we finished testing on you.” :| “Testing?” I thought before I felt a needle puncture my flesh and like ice invading my veins, the chemical they shot in me swallowed my whole being and made me forget everything up till now as I began to shake violently. Shifting as I jerked by the rope binding my feet, the chemical calmed my nerves and put me in a mellow state. My white stringy hair flowing to a steady wave as it brushed the floor beneath me, one of the scientists called over as three images I could make out in biohazard suits started to take me down from my suspended state. “BE CAREFULL! If you ruin my work I will make sure you never see the light of day again!” Cautious about his “experiment”, he watched eagerly as they took me down and dragged my body along the freezing floor out the room. “Valencia,” the scientist directed towards his assistant nurse, “get the Freon chamber ready.” “Ok doctor.” “Put him in there correctly and plug him up. And please, make sure it’s tight this time. We don’t need another mishap like last time.” Geoff Hoon, the head scientist, instructed his staff. Then slightly under his breath, said to him self, “I will live through him, my creation, my manufactured seed?”
