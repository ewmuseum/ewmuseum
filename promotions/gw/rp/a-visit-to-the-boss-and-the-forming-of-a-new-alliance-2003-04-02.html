A Visit to the Boss, and the forming of a new alliance

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-02-03 The door of the rusted up Blue work truck of Ely’s pulls into the parking lot of the Hospital.. We know it a hospital due to the fact of nurses and doctors walking around going to work or going home from there. BANG!!! The sound of the backfiring truck makes a few nurses scream and run, then realizing it was just a backfire they scowl at Braxton for a bit and head off? ? Damn, I thought it was a drive by.” Says a short nurse. People don’t shoot up hospitals Trisha. We repair the ones they are out shooting.” Says a large fat nurse. “True” Says the smaller one. The nurses keep talking as Ely locks up the truck and heads in to the hospital pulling off his black cowboy hat. “May I help you?” Says a receptionist. Looking over the rim of her glasses. “Umm. I would hope so darlin; my name is Ely Braxton. I am here to see the McCoy Family. I am a friend of the family.” Braxton says with a very stern and concern look on his face. “Yes” McCoy? Ahh Room 510 D Wing floor five.” She says with out looking up. Braxton nods; and then heads to the elevator. He taps his boot as he waits impatiently. Soon the elevator dings and the doors open. Standing right in front of Ely is the one and only Jesse Briggs. Next to him is Logan Creed the hardcore dwarf. “I tell you they have the best burger here I have seen in a while Jess. Damn goo” OOFH!” Says Logan not paying attention to where he is going and walks into the leg of Braxton. “Hay PAL! Watch who your” OHH!” He says looking up. ? Hi Ely. Glad your hear.” “Logan. Take me to the Boss.” That’s all Braxton had to say to make Logan jump. “Yeah, Sure Big guy” Follow me.” Braxton had casually met Jesse Briggs a few times while he trained at the ranch or came to the bar. Mostly Braxton was doing more work for Bubba and had no time to chitchat. But now the two men stood staring each other. Both man good friends of Bubba McCoy. Braxton knew much about Briggs from his talks with McCoy. McCoy Respected and liked Briggs. That’s all Braxton needed, and with one jester a new friendship bloomed? Braxton shook Briggs’s hand in silence. And both rode up to the floor with Bubba in it. None had to ask how he was doing; they all knew it was bad. Bubba was notorious for hating doctors and going against their wishes. For Bubba to even be here in a hospital? Ely did not even want to thank about that. The silence cut off with the sound of a ding and the doors opening. Both walked out and followed the little dwarf. ELY!!!! The sound of a females voice raised Bubba’s head. All of a sudden he was leaped on by Christiana McCoy. Her long dark red hair flinging into his face, but he did not mind. Truth was he loved this woman, but she would never know it if he could help it. She was Bubba’s daughter. And Bubba was like a brother that Ely never had, She was his only family. And loving her the way he did was wrong? So very wrong. Even so he held her tight. Pulled in the sweet perfumed sent of her hair, Strawberry. She cried into his shoulder. And he let her. Partly to comfort her, and partly to hid the tears coming from his own eyes. “Ohh Ely! The doctors say they don’t know what’s wrong! They say.. They Say..” Christina cried out. “There there little one” Boomed out His large hands petting her head smoothing her hair. “Bubba will be okay, I know he will. ? “He is right Lass.” Said Logan. Bubba is tougher then a cornered rattlesnake and meaner to. This illness whatever the damned thing is, Bubba will kick the crap out of and ask where is his beer and steak when he wakes up. You’ll see.” Logan said with a face so serious it made every one laugh. “You little man I could kiss you!” Said Christina. “What yall laughing at. I am serious, NO! I really am.” Logan said a bit confused? Ely took this time to sneak away and wipe his face. He found his self-standing in the room with Bubba. Bubba lying there still; with a breathing machine keeping him alive. Not a way he wanted to see his boss. And not a way Christina should look at her father. Braxton walked up to the edge of the bed. He stood there in silence for a while. “Bubba. Man it should be me there in that bed not you. I owe you my life Bubba. DON’T YOU DARE DIE BEFORE I CAN REPAY YOU! Your all the family I have. You better pull though this; I don’t think I want to come to the after life looking for you. But I would do it in a flat second to keep Chris from crying. She hurts Bubba. I love her so much” Seeing her hurt like she is pisses me off. ? Braxton bellows out slamming his fist into the wall. “Bubba you have to pull though, to many need you. I am going to find out who did this to you, you bet your ass! You just fight the biggest fight of your life.” Braxton says now calmed down a bit, not much but just enough control to get by with. Braxton lowered his head and prayed silently. Little did he know, Christina McCoy was standing at the door. She turned and left before Ely could turn to head back out. Braxton talked with Logan for a bit and soon him Logan and Briggs headed back to the elevator. Braxton stopped and looked at her. “It’s going to be okay darlin.” He said. And strange, Christina believed him for some reason? that reason was he loved her, and she knew it. And she loved him. But how would she let him know? +--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+ “SO do you know who did this?” Braxton asked. “Not a clue my large friend” Logan said. Stuffing more burger in his mouth. “I think I know” Said Briggs. “Well I have a few suspects. The three discussed the possibilities as they ate.. “Do you really think it is him?” Asked Braxton. “Yep. Think About it. Who else would gain more, And have you seen GW as of late” I tell you it is him. ? Said Briggs “Then I say we kick his ass!” Screamed Braxton. “Not that easy. He is well protected. Also, to do so we would have to re enter GW.” Said Briggs. Ely and Jesse both looked at Logan with smiles. “What?” Logan said looking up from his plate of burgers.” “Hurry up and eat.” Said Braxton “Yeah, We have a plan you little trashcan! Said Briggs “Well speak while I eat! DAMNIT!” Screamed Logan and the three started talking plans.
