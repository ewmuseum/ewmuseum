Em-Bare-Assed..............The Exam!

16 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-16-2004 As Pendragon walked away from Our Sisters of Mercy, it almost seemed as though nearly every step caused him just a bit of pain. Each step he took from the door brought more color into his cheeks as well’If one didn’t know better, one might believe the Lord King was blushing. His eyes, though, are a bit wary, and his frown scares the passersby from his path. “What is the matter, Venneman?” The voice, accompanied by a blinding flash, tells us that Bob is on the scene. His white hair and beard give him a kindly look, as does his attire: white shorts, white cardigan sweater, white deck shoes today’Seems he has hung the suit up for the time being. Pendragon casts him a look as he continues down the street. Bob smiles ambiguously as he walks alongside the larger fellow, a twinkle in his eye. “You fought well, Crusader. Namenlos?” He spits after saying the last before continuing?“Is probably one of the strongest demons you will face. He failed to best you. Your strength surprises even me, and it is hard to surprise the all seeing deity that I am?” He smiles wryly and casts Pendragon a considering look?“I do mean all seeing”.” *********************FLASH*************************** Our Sisters of Mercy hospital. Dr. Littlefield, still wearing his mask, dances and capers about Pendragon’s room. Pendragon, for his part, sits through the childish display stoically’He is, after all, a hero. Finally, the doc tires of his charade and removes his mask. He still wears a smile as Pendragon eyes him warily. The doctor grabs the clipboard at the foot of the bed, reads it and frowns before placing it back on it’s hook. “Is thy brain gone, knave” Have thee been too consumed by thine bitter alchemy that thee would bounce about this room as if thee were a court jester rather than a DoKtor of’Medicine?” Pendragon frowns as he speaks the last’Medicine is new to him. Leeching is understood, but this business of medicine is some tricky stuff. “I was merely tryin to raise your spirits, my boy!” Pendragon frowns at the fellow, but it doesn’t seem to faze the Doc? “I see no boy here, DoKtor. Thee would do well to refrain from that kind of talk”.” He crosses his arms across his massive chest, but the doctor continues to stare at him as though he were a child. With a sigh, he shakes his head. “Ok’I’ll send for the nurse to help get you prepped for surgery?” He turns to go as Pendragon’s eyes nearly pop out of his head? “SURGERY!!!””” What surgery?”” Pendragon is at the edge of the bed now, his robe barely concealing his sword’err’yeah. The doctor turns and smiles? “Nothing major, son. Just a cut here, a snip there and a shave here and there, and the procedure will be finished. I’m proud of you’Enduring through the years, feeling as though you were a girl trapped in a boy’s body. And your parents’Finally they have enough money to help you’That pesky extra X chromosome just got in the way?” again the doctor turns to leave and Pendragon leaps from the bed and catches him by the shoulder. Frantic, he turns the fellow around and peers into his eyes? “Who is it thee thinks that I am?” The doctor looks puzzled, but shrugs his shoulders and smiles? “Why, you’re Jamie Johnson’And the name even will work afterward.” He reaches out and pats Pendragon’s chest?“Hmmm’Hormone therapy will be in order for you, missy. A little too hard there in the chest?” The doc tries to pull away, but Pendragon’s grip is a vice. He holds him steady? “I know not where thee has gotten thy information, but thee art sadly mistaken. I am thy Lord King Venneman Pendragon’Defender of the realm, anointed of the White’Bob’s chosen. Art thee a fool” Art thou blind? What is it you think to accomplish?” Pendragon squeezes the shoulder a bit and the blood drains a bit from the doc’s face. He manages to stammer?.“Pendragon” Your clipboard says Johnson. This must be some kind of a joke. If you are not the Johnson kid, what did you do to him?.her?.ummm?.What did you do with Chris?” Pendragon drops the hold and throws his hands into the air?“I know not who thee speak of! Get someone on here who knows what the hell is happening! GW sent me here for a’Check up.” He frowns. He was tall, but why would he need a check up? Surely it meant something else? “I’ll send someone else’Just allow me to leave?” Pendragon arches an eyebrow at the fellow who now cowers before him?“My Lord?” With that, he scampers from the room. Pendragon pads to the closet, but before he can open the door, another doctor enters the room. Pendragon, feeling rather exposed in the backless gown hurries to the bed and sits upon what was only a moment before on full display. The new doctor is tall, and he holds himself a little more confidently than his predecessor. Pendragon eyes him as the doc looks him over? “Allow us here at Our Sisters of Mercy to offer up our apologies. Somehow, your stats were switched with another patient. We can examine you and have you out of here in a jiffy.” Pendragon smiles and the doctor visibly relaxes. “Aye’Thy Lord King would be most upset if he was parted from his king’s iron. So. When do I start this’Examination?” Again, he rather stumbles over the word. It isn’t one that he normally uses. The doctor smiles? “Immediately.” The door opens once more, and a burly looking nurse walks in. Her sleeves are rolled up and Pendragon gasps?“Meet Nurse Helga. She will be taking you for your exam, and even performing part of it. You two have fun!” The doctor smiles as he leaves, and Helga eyes Pendragon like a starving dog eying a steak. “We must get you to the exam room, yes?” It is not really a question though, merely the way she talks. She rumbles over to the closet and pulls from it a folded wheel chair. She pulls it into it’s open position and motions for Pendragon to have a seat. He looks from the chair to her, and then back to the chair? “Would thee mind turning “round” I would like to see some dignity?” Pendragon says this almost meekly’Familiarly. The huge nurse just smiles? “I have seen them before, no” But very well’I will turn if it will get your behind into the chair.” Amazingly, she does turn around and Pendragon bolts to the chair? “Let us be done with this as soon as possible”.” For any other woman, he may have added the customary “wench” to the phrase, but he holds in the insult with Helga. She smiles and pushes him out the door. As the pair move through the halls of the hospital, some people cast knowing glances at the pair, as if they know Pendragon. Not odd, since he is a bit of a celebrity. A few of the male doctors look pityingly at Pendragon and shake their heads. He pays them no mind, however, as he the chair stops before a door marked “Examining Room A”. Helga pushes the door open and wheels Pendragon in. The room is simple, containing a counter with some drawers, and an examining table. The table is a bit odd, though, as it seems to have restraining straps attached to it. In a flash, she heaves Pendragon onto the table, face first? “Such tension in your muscles?” She whispers this as she straps him down, quick as lightning’Pendragon struggles, but the straps were made to be struggled against’He never had a chance. “What trickery is this” Unhand thy Lord King, wench! This is a mistake!” He can do little more than lie there, though, as Helga moves to the drawers, her eyes riveted to his bare and muscular behind. She reaches into a drawer and pulls out a surgical glove and a tube of?.Something. She stretches the glove over her hand and begins squeezing the contents of the tube onto the glove. Pendragon flinches as she smacks his behind with her bare hand, and a smile passes her lips?. “Yes’It will be very good, no” A full cavity exam’With an emphasis on the prostate, yes?” “The what” What is it that thou meanest to do to’Wait’It cannot be!” Pendragon’s eyes are huge as he peers over his shoulder at her. She squeezes some of the lubrication into his exposed crack and he flinches again at the coldness. “Please no’Do not do this!” She only smiles as the shot suddenly switches to show the closed door of the exam room? *SQUISH* “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” The scream echoes through the halls. The men who hear it grimace’That scream means only one thing to them, and it is not a pleasant thought. Not a pleasant thought at all, brother. Passersby hurry past the closed door, trying not to look as the screams continue to grow? *SQUISH* *SQUISH* *SQUISH* “WHYYYYYYYYYYYYYY?””!!! OH GOD, THE HUMANITYYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!” The scream fades to nothing as the shot?.. ********************FLASH************************ Jumps back to Pendragon and Bob on the street. Pendragon limps a bit still as Bob tries to hide a smile?“Yes, Venn. I saw even that’A most’Unfortunate occasion for you.” Pendragon looks at the smaller fellow? “Why did thee allow it to happen” It felt as though the wench was digging for gold up there! Good Christ!” Bob frowns at the blaspheme, but Pendragon goes on?“Nothing wrong of course, but all I really needed was rest! I needed not a cavity exam!” In his anger, he doesn’t even trip over that unfamiliar phrase. “I defeated a demon!!” Bob smiles at the last and pats Pendragon on the shoulder. “That you did, my boy! That you did’But to get back to the present, at least you weren’t parted from your’Blade.” Pendragon’s hand drops to the hilt of his sword and Bob smiles?“Not that blade.” Pendragon’s eyes widen in surprise, just for an instant. Bob chuckles as the larger man rubs his sore backside, not noticing the looks people are giving him? “Now. Now you fight John Winters. Meaningless. If only I were a booker, I could set the world on it’s ear.” Bob puts his hands in his pockets as Pendragon looks down at him? “It is to defend mine King’s Gold’It is also a doorway to get more. And to take down a murderer at the same time’Zero.” Bob’s eyes light up. Evidently he is not so knowing as he lets on. “Aye’A dark soul if ever there was one, and he currently holds mine international title. Winters is but a bump in the road. I bump to be ridden down’Trod upon. Predictable to the last, he is just like all the other so called adventurers that end up in GW. And like them, he too shall feel the wrath of a king?” His hand stops rubbing his ass and grips the hilt of his sword. His knuckles are white with the pressure of the grip?“The fool realizes not who he deals with. He will not be ready.” Bob eyes him, a humorous look to his gaze?“Will you be ready” I mean, the way you keep rubbing your ass, one might think you need to keep a free hand in the battle’One hand for Winters, the other, for your ass!” Pendragon stares ahead, stoically. He is not offended that Bob said ass, nor is he surprised. Nothing surprises him about Bob anymore. Bob chuckles at the reaction?“You will be ready mine son. The world has had a taste of what you can do. But only a taste. The wins pile up, along with the bodies along the way. The perfect vessel for mine influence’The perfect man?” Bob smiled, but Pendragon frowned’The perfect man? Imperfect by nature, man can never achieve perfection. Pendragon, however, planned on being as perfect as humanly possible against Winters. A hard victory would not be forthcoming. He would roll over the man like a tidal wave. No way he was going back for another?.Check up?. ~TO BE CONTINUED~
