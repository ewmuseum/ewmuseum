The Attorney (DUI series pt. 4)

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/23/03 Johnie is walked down the hallway by the gray haired goon that’s on duty. He’s quite a bit shorter than him, but that never says much about a person. Neither does his age-creased face. Strength can be judged by more than just their appearance. Its hidden so well by what’s held on the outside. Men have the ability to lift cars of their fallen family or friends when the time arises. But who does Johnie have to count on to do that for? As he walks with Fuzzy the elder cop, he stumbles still adjusting to the cuffs since he’s used to swinging his arms off the side instead of having them hang in front of him. The mini-cubicles for the men in blue are now filled with slack-jawed officers more concerned with their Rubik’s cubes or game boys than whatever may be on their plates for fighting crime. Unless that’s all they have to do. Approaching the door at the end of the clutter holds a sign above it declaring “Counselor”. To Johnie that doesn’t mean lawyer. It reminds him more of his high school guidance counselor with the lisp that told him he needed to look beyond the sports and into an education. Instead of listening to the feminine male, he shrugged him off and laughed in his face. Never underestimate the intelligence of a prissy man in a suit, I believe the lesson was for him. The window into the room is crossed with shatterproof glass. Or what they allude to be shatterproof. Must have been put in to provide a sense of security for the lawyer inside. Or for the officers on the outside if the prisoner didn’t like what he got to defend him and tried to smash it out of aggression. Either way, the door opens and Johnie is escorted inside as Fuzzy holds him by the arm. Hunched over the tablet in front of him, the attorney sits with his brown hair slightly parted to the right as some of it hangs down above his brow. The stench of imitation Old Spice tingles Johnie’s nostrils causing him to sneeze suddenly over his left shoulder. Taking a handkerchief from his right pocket, Fuzzy begins to wipe the drops of spray that landed on his face and uniform. Instead of apologizing, Johnie can only let out a quick snicker. Upon hearing the door creak shut, he snaps his head up quickly and nervously starts piecing together the material before him. Fuzzy steps to face Johnie and proceeds in unshackling the cuffs around his wrists. “Well, hello there Percy. I see you got stuck with another one.” Letting out a soft chuckle, Percy rises to his feet, holding his tie to his chest. The chair squeaks backwards and tumbles to the right nearly falling. Twisting around awkwardly, he catches the wooden seat in midair and sets it back down with his eyes wide, hoping no one saw. Fuzzy didn’t, but Johnie did. THIS is what they sent to represent me? Oh grand! Crossing his eyebrows, Johnie observes his lawyer reset his surroundings and clumsily walk towards him. “Yes sir. You can’t expect me to take all the great ones,” he mutters in half chuckle. Fuzzy steps to the side with the cuffs and gives a genuine grin to Percy as he stands before him. Percy eyes up his new client and smiles brightly, breathing heavily. “This one’s quite the catch though. I heard he’s been giving you some problems.” “No, he wasn’t all that bad,” Fuzzy recants gruffly. “He was just a little rowdy when Frank hauled him in the other night. Despite being a little noisy here and there, he hasn’t been too awfully bad.” Slapping Johnie on the back, he looks up with a wide smile. Johnie simply stares forward, hardening his eyes and trying his best to hold back. Teeth mildly clenched, he knows he’s going to leave after the small talk. “Doesn’t talk much now does he?” Percy responds a bit jittery. Must have had his third mocha chino of the morning. Whatever gets a person up it seems. “I hope this is a mere formality and we can become good friends.” That’s enough, jackass. “Can we get on with this anytime?” Johnie chimes in, interrupting the banter. The smiles disappear from their faces. Fuzzy turns and makes a quick exit while Percy twitches his fingers through his hair as he turns to go back toward his notes on the thin wooden table. “Please, have a seat.” Johnie steps forward as his spins the chair around, straddling it. Letting out a light cough, the suit begins his address as he raises the notebook up to go over the script before him. “Now Mr. Ringo, it seems that you’ve caused quite a ruckus over the past couple nights. Hauled in for a DUI, while also picking up a few charges on top of that. Alcohol consumption of a minor, open container in a motor vehicle, and reckless endangerment. You have quite a load over your head. This could get you a heavy fine and possibly more jail time.” Keeping his head low, Johnie stares at his hands folded over each other. Not saying a word. Lowering the tablet, Percy looks over at him questionably. “Mr. Ringo?” No response. Raising to his feet, he leans over toward Johnie. “Mr. Ringo?” Nothing. Sliding out from his chair, he steps toward him cautiously. “Sir”!” Jerking his head up startles Percy enough to jump backwards a few inches and grab his chest tightly. “There will be no jail time,” Johnie orates calmly as he glances up at his startled defender. “You understand me?” Fidgeting with his tie, Percy clears his throat and continues on shrugging his shoulders. “I’m afraid there is a possibility of jail time. You see with the reckless endanger...” “There will be no jail time.” Johnie interrupts with calm demeanor passing off a cold stare. “I can’t guarantee that...” Johnie lams his fist against the table, shaking the wobbly legs almost to the point of falling over. He rapidly goes to his feet and stands before Percy, but instead of meeting his gaze, he staggers backwards against the concrete wall, crossing his arms for support. Lowering his head, Johnie stalks forward and watches his lawyer shiver in fear as he approaches closer. “You see Percy, I don’t do well in prisons. They aren’t my style.” Raising his hand over the counselor’s mouth, Johnie pulls him forward and smacks his head back against the wall as a loud crack echoes in the cramped enclosure. Percy’s loud moans are muffled into a soft groan as he winces his eyes shut. “Look at me. LOOK AT ME!” Johnie shouts into his face until the fluttered eyes wince open enough to see his dilated pupils peak out. Letting a slight grin fall across his face, Johnie continues. “And if you think that I am just going to be willing to spend anymore time than I already have in some concrete box with bars as air holes, you are kidding yourself!” Shoving him back a bit, Johnie releases Percy to hold the back of his head while crumbling to his knees. Checking his hands to see if any blood is trickling from the large lump forming at the base of his skull. “Oh please,” Johnie scoffs. “If I wanted to make you bleed, I would have done so. Trust me. There are many ways to make them bleed without it being seen.” Crouching forward on his right knee, he flicks his finger on Percy’s chin making him look up at him. “So this is what you’re gonna do. You’re gonna leave here and put the case together so that I don’t end up getting any jail time and those other charges get dropped. Call in favors, talk to judges, whatever. There will be NO jail time. Got me?” “B-B-But...that’s impossible,” Percy staggers back to his feet as his knees are as weak as his crackling voice. “They’ve been filed and they can’t be...” “You’re not hearing me, are you?” Stumbling further, Percy holds up his hands stopping Johnie from getting any closer. “Ok! Alright! I’ll get them taken care of. Just don’t hurt me!” Letting a small smile creep over his lips, Johnie nods in satisfaction. “So good that we can understand on another now.” He steps over to the table and collects up the lawyers scattered notes. Grasping them with both hands, he holds them vertical and shoves them into Percy’s chest, nudging him back a step or two. “This could be the start of a good working relationship.” Johnie turns back toward the steel frame door and knocks three times against the hard glass. “This is the end of our meeting, Percy.” A small sigh of relief comes across Percy as he walks over to the door, clenching the paperwork tight against his chest. “However.” Percy stops dead in his tracks and closes his eyes tightly. Johnie turns and stands directly in front of him tapping his index fingers together. “If you happen to say anything...ANYTHING...to these brainiac cops out here, to any judge, even to some random bum on the street and I find out about it...I will find you.” He lifts his right finger to the papers Percy is holding and thumps them hard that they thud softly. “I will find you....I’ll find your family...I will even go as far to find whatever cat and dog you might have picked up on the side of the road and taken in...and I will fuck them up. You get me?” Before he can respond, the door swings open letting in the bright artificial light from the hallway as Fuzzy steps in with cuffs in hand. Pulling Johnie’s wrists to his waist, he snaps the metal clasps together and escorts Johnie out into the hallway. Percy, following close behind, turns to go around the cubicles and head straight out to get a fast breath of freedom. Nearly stumbling over two officers and an old lady, he liberates himself into the open air as he charges out the open front door. Johnie can’t help but smile as he’s escorted back to his cell. Despite still having to spend the rest of the day in his cell, he knows that tomorrow he will be free again. Unleashed back into society where he can resume a normal life. Or what can be considered that anyway... TO BE CONTINUED...
