The Max Attack, Back?

30 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 30 Whatever happened to The Max-Attack? Who knows, I might have killed the force that told me to be it. Or maybe it just left my body, because I wasn'y using it. Nah, it wouldn't do that, because I can still feel the same force I did before, when I used to be it. I still have the mask of it, and the suit, and it still fits. I wonder if I could ever use it again? It would probably work. I got up off of my bed, thinking of what to do with my costume and mask, and the force inside me telling me to go back to The Max Attack for one more thing, but I don't know what. I opened my drawer, and grabbed the costume and mask our. I put it inside a plastic bag, and walked out of the room. I walked down the stairs, and into the living room. I sat down on the couch, thinking of what I could do with this stuff. I got off the couch, and walked into the bathroom. I closed and locked the door. A few minutes later, I walked out, with the mask and costume on. I ran to the front door, and quickly snook out of the house, so no one would see me. I jumped up, and flew to the top of my house. 'This is more like it.' I said. I jumped off my house, and started flying throughout the air. I passed a few buildings on the way, and lots of people on the street. I stopped at a big screen television, that was at the television shop. It was on the news. It said that there was a man, that had gone crazy, right in this part of town, and they need to capture him. Mauybe, for me to get back into The Max Attack, I could capture this guy, and give him to the police, and then I would get a reward, and then people would actually want a super hero in there town of Connecticut. I jumped off another building, down to the ground. I went into a back alley, so I could take my costume off and goto the cops, and ask where this guy was last seen, and everything, just so he doesn't expect anything. I walked to the police station that was only a few houses down from where I was standing right now. I walked into the front door, as the officers were watching the news story to. One of the officers saw me. 'Hello, I'm looking for the chief of officers. Could you please tell me where he is?' I asked. 'Just a second, hes in the back dealing with something. He should be out in a few minutes.' one officer said. *15 Minutes Later* The chief officer walked out from the back of the station. He didn't look to happy. I sat down on one of the chairs, in front of his desk. He looked at me, and sat down at his desk. 'What do you want?' he asked me. 'Well, I was wondering, if I could go on a lookout for that guy that escaped from prison, and that the cops don't seem to get. But I know that I can catch him.' I said. 'Whatever, but just come back alive.' he said. 'Ok, thank you.' I said. I got off the chair, and walked out of the police station. I went into the back alley again, and got back into my costume and mask. I jumped, and flew to the top of the building, that the back alley was in. I started running across the building, jumping at all of the gaps. The cop had also told me where the man was last seen, so that was where I was going. I jumped up into the air, and started flying. It was better than running across all of the buildings and shit. I got to a big warehouse, and stopped on the roof. I jumped to the ground, and went to the back of warehouse to see if there was a back door. There was one, but there were a few guards guarding the door, and I could see guns in there hands. I jumped back onto the roof, and then started climbing down the wall, above the officers. I had to be quiet or they would hear me. I got to just above them, and punched one in the head. He fell to the ground, as I kicked the other one. I quickly went into the door, and locked it in the inside. 'That was close.' I said. 'Not close enough though.' said a voice from above me. I looked up and a few more guards were coming down a rope to get me. I jumped up the wall, and kicked one, and then neckbreakered him. I started running, because one of them started shooting there gun at me. A bullet barely missed me, as I jumped up onto a ladder that led to the top of the warehouse. I got to the top, as the guards kept shooting. Then they stopped, they must've not seen where I went. I started walking, very quietly. I saw a few more guards in a room, eating donuts and drinking coffee. I saw a stun gun, lying on the floor, so I picked it up, and aimed for the guards that were eating. I shot it, and quickly shot the other guard. They both fell to the ground, like nothing. I snuck past that room, and saw another room, with guards guarding someone. It was probably the guy. I hid behind a barrel as the scene started to fade.
