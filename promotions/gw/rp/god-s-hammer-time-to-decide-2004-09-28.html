God's Hammer...Time to Decide Venneman Pendragon Global Wrestling Promo #1669 Date: 9-28-2004 Location: An alley...Mostly...MC#2 God’s Hammer. The name itself implied that he was to be a tool of the Lord God Bob. A tool. Who else would have the power to use a king as a tool other than God? Pendragon, however, had memories that he could not explain…Memories that could not be, and he wished answers from the God Bob. As in so many cases, answers from the almighty can be fairly confusing and simple at the same time, yet bob did what he could… As he patted the massive Pendragon on the shoulder, a concerned look came upon Bob’s face. It was not like Pendragon to show this kind of weakness…He was one to bludgeon ahead until he accomplished whatever it was that he intended to accomplish. Perhaps Bob had misjudged the man. Perhaps he needed to know the whole of his past, rather than the parts that he was able to remember. Bob had two choices…First, he could tell him and hope that the man would see reason, or second, he could destroy him utterly. After all, a fellow who rejected the touch of God was not a fellow he would have loosed upon the world. Only trouble was that in telling him everything, he might destroy him that way as well… “Venneman…You have always been strong, stronger than any man I have ever had a hand in creating. Your sense of drive and purpose have always been what defined you. Why now, with so many important tasks at hand, do you balk at who you truly are?” Though the question was blunt, it was not asked unkindly. Pendragon looks up with red rimmed eyes and shakes his head… “Though mine greatness has far exceeded even mine own extravagant goals, mine truest wants have always been the most simple. I wish to have a family…A wife, and a son. In mine mind, I remember a wife and a son, although I know that something such as that cannot be. I remember their deaths, and I remember mine own death hundreds of times. I have died upon the spear, and I have died of old age. Accidents have happened and murders. Why does this drivel fill mine head? How can it be?” Pendragon shakes his head and laughs ruefully as Bob crosses his arms and purses his lips, thinking. At last, Bob sighs, resigned to his choice… “Never in all the worlds has their been a soul such as the one you possess…In medieval times, kings spoke of the Divine Right of Kings. Though a foolish notion in many cases, in some cases it was quite true. Some kings were indeed anointed of the Light by none other than myself. These were always good and strong men, able to do what was right for the most people of mine precious worlds…” Pendragon looks up at him, one eyebrow arched, and Bob smiles thinly. He carries on undeterred though, and Pendragon frowns with each word… “It’s true. I rule all the worlds, though some I tend to pay more attention to than others. Although I am divine, I am still one being. I can be many places at one time, but not everywhere at once. Even God has limitations…” Pendragon interrupts suddenly, and god frowns at him… “So thee were the one who watched as Benjamin and Sarah were beheaded before mine own eyes? You allowed it to happen?” He makes as if to rise, but Bob puts a forestalling hand on his shoulder and shakes his head… “I watched not as your family was slain, and though they were your family, they were not YOUR family. That Pendragon is dead, along with so many others.” Pendragon looks confused as God scratches his head, unsure of how to explain this complicated mess to the massive fellow… “Look at it like this…Heaven has so many souls. When a person dies, unless that soul is an agent of the Dark One, the soul returns to heaven to await a rebirth. Some people remember these lives, most do not. The soul is recycled and reset, so that the life it is given once more is new. Most souls are bound to the world in which they first appeared. If not for this, there would be very few people living in all the worlds. Some souls, however, are different. Yours is one of these oddities. You are neither an agent of the Dark One nor a soul that returns to heaven. I have no idea where your soul goes when you die. The destiny of your soul is to be reborn again and again, in different worlds. It matters not the world in which you find yourself, though, for you have the ability within you to walk between the many worlds. The fabric of reality is thin around you, and events have a way of molding themselves to you because of your uniqueness…” Pendragon raises a hand…”So when I die, there is no place in heaven for me? The Great Lord Pendragon has no spot reserved in the afterlife?” Bob frowns and runs a hand through his white hair. It stays in perfect style as his hand leaves it…Bob is quite powerful, or he uses a fantastic hairspray… “I have done some research on this phenomenon, and the only thing that I can surmise is that there is a being even higher than myself…A creator of the creator so to say…Though this is troubling, I guess that to be I would have to have been made. Otherwise where did I come from? I have created everything you’ve touched in your many lives, yet I have only created you once. After you first life, you have never come to me. You go to a different place. You are not recycled, but remade the same each time. Each birth that makes you, you forget your last life, yet the strength of heart and character that I first blessed you with remain the same. As time has gone on, the strength of man has faded…It is my fault, and yet it is not. A piece of me goes into each soul that I birth onto the world. With so many roaming the worlds, I am stretched quite thin. You, though…You are an original. Imbued with all the strength that I put into the few when I first began my work. Something has snatched you up, bound you to yourself…I only ask that you remember where you originally came from and help me…” Pendragon looks up at the kindly man. He has never really asked him for his help, only assumed that Pendragon would do as bidden. Astonished, Pendragon still questions… “But why this world? Why now? Why have mine other existences been allowed to be played as they were? Were I not important enough to thy plans then? Was I not strong?” He stares defiantly up at the Creator, who only smiles down upon him and claps him on the arm… “You are the strongest man left.” He says this simply, with no hint of subterfuge…”It is not only this world that I need cleansed…It is all worlds. As mine sway has grown weaker, so has the Dark One’s power grown stronger. He is the same in all worlds as well as I…As men have turned their back on me, they have embraced him, however unknowingly. His agents have never been some numerous as now, and only now do I have the power needed to fight him…Only now do I have you.” Pendragon gasps….”But you are God! Always superior, always strong! What need have thee of me? To face the emissaries of Evil, you need someone stronger….” “There is no one. You are pure, Pendragon. As pure as the first group of men that were fashioned by my hand. There is no other way. I feared that I would destroy you with the knowledge I gave, yet if I did not give it, I feared that I would be forced to destroy you anyway. Man doesn’t turn his back on me…Yet, even now, I am unsure if I have the strength to destroy one such as you. There is something in you, Pendragon. Something that amplifies everything that I originally placed within you. Someone above me has sway with you…I only wish to guide you…I wish for you to do the most good for the most souls. I want you to save that which I created.” Pendragon studies his hands for a moment, still getting used to what he is, or what he may be. Though it is God Bob, he doesn’t know that he fully trusts the fellow as yet; after all, he allowed Pendragon’s family to be executed in front of him…”That was another life…” he whispers under his breath, and Bob pretends that he did not hear. “This world is dieing. It is one of two that I have dealt in. What is worth saving? Perhaps it would be better to wipe it clean and start anew. Destroy all that man has wrought and set a new man to rebuilding. Would that not be better than saving…..This?” God, er, Bob chuckles a bit at that…”Were I the vengeful god you would have me be…Were I the vengeful god that the books have made me out to be…I cannot do what you suggest, even if I were to wish it. Evil holds sway, and evil will not let go so easily of the things it has wrought. Science replaces me in the lives of men. Science explains things to the masses…” Pendragon cuts him off, and shakes his head…”Talk to me not of science…What is there that would make me wish to save this place?” **********************FLASH************************** A darkened alleyway appears, and Pendragon and Bob stand side by side in it’s mouth. From the dark recesses of the alley, a small voice is heard, muffled, trying to scream… “What is this trickery? Where are we?” “You need a reason. I will give you as many as it takes. You will see things as they truly are for the first time in all of your existences, Pendragon. Go.” Bob points into the alley. Pendragon shrugs and draws Lucien as he walks into the darkness, toward the small voice. As he reaches it, he gasps in horror. A small girl in ripped clothes lies on the ground, covered in blood. She tries to pull her knees to her chest, to just make herself into a ball as she whimpers, her mouth gagged. That is not what horrifies him. Above the girl, a huge lizardly beast looms. A demon if ever there was one. It wears the clothes of a man, and it’s hands/claws are covered in the blood of the girl. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] “HALT THEE, DEMONSPAWN!!!” Comes the command from Pendragon, and the beast jumps in surprise as it looks his way, taking him in for the first time. With a snarl, it flings itself at him. Pendragon nimbly sidesteps and his blade is a blur. The demon thing streaks past, stopping and turning back a few paces away. It’s huge arms flex and wane as it snarls at him. Pendragon holds his blade up so the thing can see the blood that covers it. It looks down at it’s torso and sees blood welling from a gash there. With an astonished look upon it’s face, the thing falls into two pieces. Pendragon had sliced it in half so neatly that it did not even realize until it died. “Good Christ…” He whispers in stupefied tones, and he looks toward the girl. He slowly walks to her, but she scrambles up and away from him. Terrified, she runs from the alley. As she reaches Bob, he waves his hand over her and her blood disappears, the terrified look leaves her eyes, and the clothes mend themselves. “Have you seen my mother?” She asks him in small tones, scared, though certainly not terrified…. “I believe she is that way, searching for you…You should not keep her waiting….” The girl smiles, relieved, then runs in the way he pointed. Bob smiles and walks into the alley. Pendragon studies the body of the beast, poking at it with the end of his blade. “You see? If for no other reason, you can rid the world of those such as this.” He waves his hand in front of Pendragon and the thing that was sliced in half becomes a fairly ordinary looking man. Pendragon gasps and jumps back… “Save thy alchemy for those who would understand it!” Bob scowls at him and Venneman nods his head…”I agree. Remind me never to question thee again, if these are the answers that thee wilt provide me.” Bob smiles, and snaps his fingers…. ************************FLASH******************************* A brightly lit courtyard, with flowing fountains meet them now, and Pendragon smiles, his sword going into scabbard. “I thought this would be better for you…I am not without understanding…” Bob says this as he sighs himself at the beauty of it… The two men take a seat on a stone bench, and weariness seems to wash from Pendragon’s face. He even smiles, a little, and that is unlike the man. “Maximum Carnage…Though I dislike the title, the Cup match provides an excellent chance for you to reveal some strength to the world. You will be matched against evil there, no matter how the foes are sorted out.” “And when I face a good man? A true heart who would serve thee also at thy call?” Bob frowns, but says “Show mercy…Do not destroy, merely defeat. You must make that choice when it is presented. Evil though….Plow though the evil that is set before you as if it is your goal. Winning matters not, except for you glory…Making an example of evil though….THERE is a goal. Prepare yourself well, my young king…And rest while you can.” Bob passes his hand in front of Pendragon’s face and he falls instantly asleep, his face a picture of serenity. “If only you are as strong as I believe you to be, this will be a prosperous endeavor….If not, you’ll die.” Bob snaps his fingers and disappears in a white flash, leaving Pendragon to his dreams……… ~TO BE CONTINUED~
