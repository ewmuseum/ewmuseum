'From The Ashes Like a Phoenix' Feat. Johnny Cruse

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 10, 2006 Oh mirror mirror, you're coming in clear, I'm finally somewhere in between, I'm impressed, what a beautiful chest, I never meant to make a big scene. Will you resign to the latest design? You look so messy when you dress up in dreams. “The cold steel was now warm between my clutched fists as I lifted the bar overhead, veins pulsated out of my arms as I felt the pressure on my body more than ever , abstract thoughts swirled through my head as I tried to focus on the task at hand. As I lowered the bar the weight expanded my chest on the black padded bench as I gazed into a light bulb that was hanging from the ceiling with my baby blue eyes. I blocked everything out of my mind except me, the bar, and the light. Up, down, up, down repeated repetitions as sweat began to seep from my pours; I couldn’t help but think about Reload, and my opponents, the Platinum Championship, and to once again gain respect in a place that has forgotten me. My goal here is to once again be the best, to once again hold that World Title around my waist. My breathing slow and synchronized with the bar which I was lifting off my chest, just one more I said to myself as I tried not to arch my back, my muscles spasmed and shook as I inched the bar to its resting place. The sound of steel on steel as I dropped the bar into the grooves and sat up, with my mind cleared everything seemed somber, the smell of sweat and a hot weight room filled my nostrils. I sat relaxed on the end of the bench and looked myself into immense mirror which encircled the walls. Sweat ran though my golden blonde hair, down my face and dripped from my chin leaving a small puddle on the blue rubber floor in between my feet. Veins ran from my shoulders to my wrists as I sat there, Scar Tissue played elegantly from the speakers set up in the corner of the room. I breathed in”. And out slowly, as my chest arose and fell, my gaze into the mirror became somewhat hypnotic as I sat there’still thinking’about everything, about what I should have done, about what I could have done and about what im going to do, about what I should do, and about what I can’t do. Everyone says I’m a washed up’Everyone says my glory days in Global are over’everyone that says that is a punk’Everyone that says that is a never will be’they will never be what I am’they have never experienced what I have been through? Adrenaline rushed though my body, from my head to my toes I could feel it, I missed that feeling, I flexed my pectoral muscles and let out a long breath as I laid back down under the bar, I let my arms hang limp at my sides. Thoughts were flowing uncontrollably through my mind, thoughts that needed to be locked out for the time being. Bulls on Parade the song of the YB blared through the speakers in the corner? I closed my eyes? Johnny Cruse, Shawn, The Soup, BJP, T, Hanz Kruger, Derek Frost, TLM, all Youngblood, all legends in their respective federations. My mind began to wonder off with visions of the Young Blood crew coming down the ramp to the Theme of Rage Against the Machine, the old YB is dead but it will never die. I placed my hands on the bar and lifted the weight now heavier than before’my eyes still closed I lowered the bar faster than before Faster? Faster? My breathing once again in synch with my repetitions, one after another I bounced the bar off my bare wet chest, the pain becoming more and more, my arms hurt, my mind was focused soon I will be ready for Reload? “Jay’Jay Robson” Eyes open as I dock the bar back into its respective position above me. The familiar voice knocked me out of my self induced coma as I looked up to lay my eyes on a man that I haven’t seen in ages it seemed like. His grey suit and black Ray Ban sunglasses accompanied him well as he looked upon me brushing his scraggly blonde hair to one side “Haha’Johnny Cruse” I chuckled as I sat up from the sweat glazed vinyl and stretched my shoulder out. “How ya been man” Long time no see? I said as I stood up and shook Johnny’s extended hand “Trying to end the never ending story my friend’but like they say, you only live once and if you don’t do it while your young you will never get it done.” He proclaimed to me in a raspy voice as we continued to shake hands. This was the great thing about Cruse, he always knew what to say and the right time to say it, and probably why I have always looked up to him and thought of him as my teacher and father figure. I wouldn’t be here today if it wasn’t for him. “I saw your last match Jay” He said to me as we stopped shaking hands and he removed his black glasses to reveal his piercing green eyes that I felt were always staring a hole directly through me every time I looked at him “Yea’I know it wasn’t the greatest performance” I exclaimed, as I dropped my head a bit in shame “Wasn’t the greatest?” Hell Jay you were running around out there like a chicken with its head cut off’I was watching that match on tv and couldn’t believe that you were the same guy that I actually put my heart and soul into. It looked like your very first match’ever?” He said, basically scolding me for my past effort in SiT, but I couldn’t blame him for it either and I offered only one rebuttal “Johnny man’that was the first time I have been in a ring in over a year’the ring rust was a major factor there” I said to him trying to disguise my poor performance with an even poorer excuse “Jay’I don’t care’your better than that’you know it, and I know it. Hell son, your in the Hall of Fame, Hall of Fame athletes don’t go out there and get kicked in the mouth’you’re the one that’s supposed to be doing the kicking” Johnny proclaimed, my head and shoulders dropped a bit more in disappointment as I stared down past my grey Nike shoes to the blue rubber floor “So what do you suppose I do?” I said in a low distraught voice as my shoulders shrugged a bit “You mean what do you suppose we do?” He exclaimed, confusion ran through my brain as I looked up at him with a glimmer of hope in my eyes “I’m going to help you Jay’you were my best and greatest student of the game’no I take that back you are my best student” Johnny said to me as he placed his hand on my shoulder “Together we can once again be atop the mountain and all of this and the past should be forgotten because together you and I are YB’we are family Jay I never want you to forget that again” He proclaimed as he put his arm around my neck and pointed at my chest with his other hand as I nodded my head a bit “I’m with ya brah’im with ya?” I told Johnny as we walked out of the gym together arm in arm’thinking only of what the future holds for us both’hoping dreams of Young Blood happen sooner than later.
