Just Chillin!

4 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-4-06 January 23 6 A.M. Dallas Texas Quinton hers the noise of children screaming and people laughing as he waits for Gorn and Gimley in the hospital. In time we will get are revenge Quinton thinks to himself. He then looks around then goes and gets himself a bag of chips and a pop. After a while Quinton goes to get another pop but he sees Gorn and Gimley walking out mad. “Stupid doctor all we had was a bloody head and he examines are foot!” Gorn shouts. “Well we have another match at Fusion another Main Event match against Jake Hix, Mike Biddle, The Back Alley Brawler, and the Texas wrecking Crew.” Quinton says. “Those guys are gonna pay!” Gimley says. “Especially Make Mix!” Gorn shouts. “It’s Jake Hix Gorn.” Quinton says laughing. “Not for long!” Gorn says. “Well I’m going back to my Hotel and going to sleep since were staying here.” Quinton says. “Yeah lets go to sleep.” Gimley says. “Already starting to fall asleep.” Gorn says his voice sounding tired. The three head back to Quinton’s hummer. The three get there right when the Sun comes out but head into the house immediately and fall to sleep. January 24th 2006 5 P.M Quinton wakes up looks around for Gorn and Gimley but sees only the covers of where they slept. “Probably went to some dang bar!” Quinton says to himself He turns on the TV and watches Dragon Ball GT on cartoon network. After that goes off Quinton turns off the TV and looks at his watch “5:30 already I have to get up!” Quinton says. He gets up curses a few times then puts on shirt with flames and baggy cargo pants with some black K-Swiss shoes. “This the life no tuxedo and no dress pants just a day to relax.” Quinton says out loud. When Quinton finishes tying his shoes. The Hammer Brothers walk in with swim trunks on. “Why me God! Why me!” Quinton says looking up. “Is he okay?” Gimley asks Gorn. “I don’t know.” Gorn says to Gimley. “Of course I’m fine but today is a day to relax before the match you bozos!” Quinton says. “So were do you want go Spikes!” Gimley says “Lunch, A movie, Dinner,.” Quinton says. “Bah Spikes what about a night club!” Gorn says. “I was about to say that before you rudely interrupted!” Quinton says looking at Gorn. Gorn just shrugs. “Lunch, Movie, Dinner, and a club it could work.” Gimley says “Good Idea Spikes!” the Hammer Brothers say then they bear hug Quinton. “Ok well get dressed.” Quinton says out of breath from the bear hug. Gimley and Gorn get dressed in similar pants but with a shirt with the Hammer Brothers logo on it. “We got it from the manager!” Gorn says “Lets go Spikes!” Gimley says The three then walk out the door. Quinton then locks the door and the three get in the hummer. The get carryout for lunch at Arby’s. Then after a gay man tries to put the moves on Quinton and Gorn and Gimley teaches him a lesson by throwing him in a garbage can. The three then start walking to the hummer. “Spikes I want to see Harry Otter 4.” Gimley says “Me two!” Gorn says. “You can’t even pronounce the name so lets see Zathura.” Quinton says. “No Harry Otter!” The Hammer Brothers scream. “Ok fine you win we will go see Harry Pothead.” Quinton says with a scared look on his face. “Thank you Spikes and its Harry Otter.” Gimley says. “No it’s Harry Pothead!” Gorn says. No Harry Otter!” Gimley shouts. “Enough!” Quinton shouts stopping the hummer. The two stare at Quinton. “Lets go watch this dang movie and I don’t want to hear any more talking out of the two of you!” Quinton says restarting the Hummer. The three arrive at the movie theater. “Hello says the girl at the booth three tickets for what?” The girl asks. Quinton just stares at the girl until Gimley hits him in the back. “Three for Harry Potter.” “Ok the total is $42.83.” Quinton hands her a hundred dollar bill. “Keep the change.” Quinton says smiling at the girl with his mouth open. After a few minutes Quinton’s mouth is still hanging open. “Were you in Special Ed?” The girl asks putting the $57.17 into her pocket left over from the amount. Quinton doesn’t reply. “Ok well enjoy your movie.” The girl says. “You mean its ours to keep?” Gimley ask. “No just go in and see it and come out.” The girl says “Ok!” says the Hammer Brothers dragging Quinton by the arms until they get inside. Quinton then finally comes back to earth. “Heh you two.” Quinton says pointing at the Hammer Brothers. “Yeah!” The Hammer Brothers say running over. “Go on in the movie and I will get us some popcorn and some drinks.” Quinton says pointing at the left side. “Ok!” Gorn and Gimley shout then run around the corner. I hope they know were the place is Quinton thinks as he’s paying for the popcorn and the drinks. Quinton then walks around the corner and sees the Hammer Brothers beating the wall. “Stop that!” Quinton yells “We can’t find the movie place.” Gimley says attacking the popcorn. “Follow me and I will show you.” Quinton says handing the three pops to Gorn and jerking the popcorn away from Gimley. “You know I let you have that popcorn.” Gimley says. “I know but if you didn’t I would not have showed you.” Quinton says opening the door. “Fine!” Gimley says The three then go and set at the front seats and watch till the end of the movie. January 25th 2006 9:45 “That was a good movie!” Gimley says. “Yeah!” Gorn says slapping Gimley on the back. Quinton’s phone starts ringing. “You two go play games.” Quinton says pointing at the Hammer Brothers and giving them each a ten-dollar bill. The Hammer Brothers then run of in happiness while Quinton walks in the restroom. After about thirty minutes Quinton comes walking out of the restroom with a smile on his face. He walks over to the Hammer Brothers. He sees them lying to the gaming guy trying to get more quarters. “Gorn, Gimley we have to go.” Quinton says walking out the door with the Hammer Brothers following. ? Go where Spikes.” Gorn says catching up. “Yeah were?” Gimley says also catching up. It’s a surprise. Quinton says unlocking the hummer. The three then get in and go to a raggedy looking place looking like it came out of a old horror movie. The three then go inside. Inside there is an old punching bag some old tables two old couches a dried up swimming pool some broken weights some rusted water fountains and old painted walls Quinton then takes the Hammer Brothers into a room were there are old nasty toilets and showers. “Umm”. Spikes what are we going to do with this?” Gimley ask? “Were going to fix up the place and this is going to be our gym.” Quinton says with a smirk. “What?” the Hammer Brothers says. “Yeah I bought the place for 25 grand.” Quinton says with an even bigger smile. “Let me kill him!” Gorn says walking over to Quinton. “Yeah but lucky for you two we are going out to eat first”. Quinton says trying to reason with the Hammer Brothers. “Fine we will agree if you give us our money.” Gorn says. “I gave it to you the day after yesterday.” Quinton says turning around looking at the Hammer Brothers. “What?” The Hammer Brothers says again. “You mean you don’t have your money you each had 25 thousand dollars!” Quinton yells. “We gave the restaurant guy 25 dollars for are meals that’s all you gave us!” Gimley says. “No you gave the restaurant guy 25 thousand dollars all the money I gave you.” Quinton says pointing a finger at the Hammer Brothers. “Oh.” The Hammer Brothers say stupidly. “But lucky for the two of you I still have my money and since you spent all of your money you will work cleaning the place up we will talk about the rest of this later now lets go eat.” Quinton says. “We will take him out later”. Gimley whispers to Gorn. “I heard that!” Now your not getting any food. Quinton says getting his keys. “No Spikes don’t do that.” Gimley says. “Because if you do we will hurt you!” Gorn says popping his knuckles. “Even though we love yah!” Gimley says also popping his knuckles and smiling. “You two are scaring me and it was a joke.” Quinton says opening the gym door. “Oh!” Gorn and Gimley say running to the hummer. The three then leave the beat down soon to be gym and go to eat dinner at Golden Corral. Were they pay with Quinton’s money.
