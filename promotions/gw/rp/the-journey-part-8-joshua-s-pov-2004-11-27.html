The Journey: Part 8/Joshua's POV

27 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-27-04 Early Evening Greetings one and all! I’d like to welcome you back to the land that has no exit or enter just a vastness in the land that belongs to the “Connection”. Now on this day its about 2 days after “Turkey Day” and we actually find Joshua in the spacious kitchen of the OSC residence getting some of the food from the previous Thanksgiving ready and he “dressed down” for the occasion as he was going for the pretty casual look in the home in which his family lives in. The thing about this day was that it was going to be Joshua’s “last meal” before he went into the match upcoming at SiT. A part of him cursed himself for being in a match like this but he knew that he was doing it for a friend of his: The incomparable Ken Partridge Jr.! Now its been about a month or so since Joshua has come into the fed and for the most part has found the patrons of the establishment to be accepting of his lifestyle. OK so they don’t exactly agree with the idea of two men dating but Josh has found a certain...I dunno “niche” in the roster where he can be himself and people don’t really notice it all that much. Joshua for the most part has been not so much in hiding with his family but its just how to have them appear to the public. Its one thing for them to see Zack and Joshua because they are the ones under contract but what would Joshua do if the fed ever asked to have the entire “Connection” stable on GW Television! Its not like “Sex in the City” where some sex crazed bitch is trying to get laid every episode but more like a “Queer Eye” version of say...The Seavers from “Growing Pains”where Joshua has pretty much taken the role of Jason and Zack in some ways I guess you could say is Maggie (Jason’s wife). Now Joshua doesn’t usually do these “Goodbye” dinners where he gathers the stable around the table and do a speech about him leaving for a little while but tonight Joshua doesn’t so much look at it as a “Goodbye” dinner more like a “I’m going away for a week” type of occasion. While Joshua was in his little thought process he felt a pair of small hands cover his eyes and Joshua knew EXACTLY to whom they belonged. “Hey sis.” Joshua said without turning around “Hey bro” the blue haired genius began before spinning Joshua around into a dizzying 360 which almost had the unofficial leader of the OSC losing the little lunch he had eaten for the day. “Did Zack tell you to come check on me?” The older brother asked Ami as he knew she couldn’t lie and she sheepishly looked into Joshua’s eyes and said “Yeah...he did.” “Would you mind taking me to where he is hiding so I can get him out here to help me set the table for dinner?” Joshua asked and when he saw the devilish look on Ami’s face that was all he needed to see! Now you may (or may not but will play along anyways like you are) be wondering what Joshua and Ami do when Zack is trying to hide from helping Joshua with dinner. Well Joshua has Ami run into the bathroom and make sure the curtain is closed in the bathroom and then Joshua flushes the toilet and the water becomes ice cold and I mean like freeze you ***** off its that cold! Anyways... This always prompts Zack to come running out of the bathroom at about 45 mph (someone figure out what that is in km/h cos I ain’t got time to do it) and then he makes a beeline for the covers of the bed that he and Joshua share...usually! Apparently Zack was waiting for Joshua to stick HIS head into the door and when he did *BAM!* a pie right to the kisser! How Ami didn’t trip it not even the great Zack Johnson could figure out buut when Zack did come out of the shower Josh made sure his punishment was a good one! I’d tell you what it is but the FCC would come looking for me for indecency (Damn Terrell Owens and Howard Stern! ) So when Zack was done having his fun and he got cleaned up he went downstairs to help Joshua with dinner and suffice to say while Joshua was glad that Zack came down to help he was a little annoyed with the prank! “Gee thanks hun!” Joshua said mock pouting as he wiped the whipped cream off of his face and washed the towel in the sink and Zack couldn’t help but give him an apologetic kiss on the cheek and whispered into his ear “Sorry sweetie.” So dinner came and went and as the time drew closer for Joshua to make his announcement it felt like a huge piranha just started nibbling on his insides and made him want to vommit! When the announcement time had come Joshua, clad in his “Censorship is Unamerican”, Arizona nrad blue jeans and white Hanes socks tapped the glass that was in front of him and stood up at the head of the table before making his speech to the group... “Fellow OSC members, the time is upon us to have to get ready for a war of great importance. I have been your leader for a time now and I feel that I have to do soemthing for the good of this family. I was recruited by a friend of mine named Ken Partridge Jr. to go into the match at Showdown in Tokyo as one of his partners. Now does that mean neccessarily that I am in his stable? No it doesn’t and I will tell you that the main reason that I am doing this for him is because he needs backup and I could think of ALMOST no better than the “Rookie Sensation” as the darlings in GW like to call me to go into this match...Yes Tina?” Joshua askes as the “Disciplinarian” of the stable decided to stand up and ask some questions about the situation. “Josh Why? Why Ken and ‘The Elite’? Do you not see this as a way to have Kenn turn his back on you and then leave you a bloody heap in the middle of the ring?!” Tina asked and that prompted Zack to reach up and put a hand on Tina’s shoulder to sit her down before something happened that everyone regretted! “Tina I see where you are coming from I really do but that is a part of the structure anyways. By the format the winners of the two matches go into a match at the end where its every man for himself and Ken is a good friend and someone that if it came down to it I just don’t know if I could bring myself to hurt him bad. I talked to Zack and he understands my situation because Ken was my first ever tag team partner in GW and this was the least I could do for him. Look Ken’s been through hell and he’s trying to get back things that he lost when he turned his back on his friends once before. He has the American Youth, Addison, and Modesi and I know that I can’t technically join them but I feel like if Ken needs me then I’ve got to be there for him and people just don’t realize that. *sigh* Look I know that Zack is my boyfriend and I wouldn’t dream of cheating on him but and I told Zack this before if he never came to GW with me I might have done the unthinkable and persued Partridge! I know its against what I stand for but I would have done it and believe me when I tell you I wouldn’t have thought twice about it which Zack also knows. Zack is my boyfriend and he will be forever and a dayu but I have an ‘inspeperable bond’ with Ken and if he were here right now I’d want him to know that I wouldn’t hurt him because see while physical scars heal over time the mental scars that I would have sustained for eitehr getting beaten by him or me hurting him would NEVER go away!” Joshua said as he walked away from the table and with Zack in tow slowly walked up the stairs and into the bedroom where as soon as Zack closed the door and turned the lock you could still hear the sobs of a man that had to come to grips with something that ate him up bad! Good Night...my friends! Joshua L. Curtis
