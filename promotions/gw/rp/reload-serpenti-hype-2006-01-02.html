Reload Serpenti Hype

2 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-2-06 The scene opens revealing a small town in Mexico? A man dressed in black silk pants and a black trench coat and a matching Black mask with a red and greed cobra head on the face walks though the dirt streets. The town seams almost empty, almost deserted? Except for one building that cheers are heard coming from? The Man slowly heads to that building. Masked Man: This is where I spent my teenage years. Learning how to fight? learning the art of Lucha Libre. Here I started the path that has lead me to Global? I am proud of my family’s strong tradition and history of this sport. I was proud when I got my first mask? It was here that I became the Mexican Wrestling Champion? LPA, Lucha Pro Arena; My first federation. The man turns and the small town morphs into the city of Tokyo Japan, Neon lights fill the night sky as the man walks down the street? Once again the man stops in front of another building A sign flashes showing fighters facing each other permoting a Show.. More cheers are heard coming from the building.. Mask Man: Ahh Japan. I learned more here and added this to my style of fighting. Here I learned Martial Arts, I also learned and added other highflying styles to my arsenal? I loved my time here. Although unlike Mexico, I never became a champion, but I gained one thing that I hold more important? Respect. The Man turns away and the background changes again? this time Washington DC? Home of Primetime Central? The Sun shines as the man approaches the Offices of PTC. A poster hangs in the windows promoting GTT: 5 Masked Man: Ahh PTC. A place that has connections to many places of many styles. This is here I began my tournament fighting. Which I still love to this day, Here I gained even more respect. Looking forward to the next Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. The Masked man turns once more and the background changes?. Las Vegas? The Neon lights flashing all over the place. Mask Man: And now we come home? Las Vegas. Sin City. Home to Global, A place that has some of the greatest fighters I have ever seen. Some of the very best have walked though these doors.. The Man walks though the doors of the Global Arena. And heads to the backstage area. Man: Walked though theses halls? The Man turns and steps out onto to one of the two stages leading to the global arena floor. Man: Stood on this stage. The Man then walks down the ramp and heads to the octagon stopping to look around at the empty arena? That slowly begins filling with fans? Pyros explode and the cheers fill the arena.. Glo Bal!!! Glo Bal!!! Glo Bal!!! Glo Bal!!! Man: Looked into this crowd.. and walked this isle? The Man steps into the octagon? And the crowd fades away. Man: And many legends and superstars have stepped into this octagon? Everyone has his or her reasons. But all including myself have one common desire. The respect and honor that goes with being a Champion! I must admit I crave the gold? After all I am not called the Spanish Pirate for no reason at all. I love treasure. And not all is lost and have to be searched and found? Some are in the open and ready for the taking. ON January, twelfth, two thousand and six. I shall step into this Octaon.. I shall fight with honer and Respect.. As I have three fighters who have the ability to defeat me.. But I shall not be defeated. To long have I not pushed my self to the fullest of my abilities? ON this night I shall not be holding back.. I shall give it my all. To my three Opponents.. Best luck, For you shall need it when you cross paths with a Serpent? El Serpenti spins and the background changes and Serpenti is now standing on deck of a old pirate ship? El Serpenti: Some call two thousand and six the year of the Dog? No I say. Two thousand and six is the year of El Serpenti! Global Beware The Snake Bite And The Pirates Noose Awaits you.
