The long road home.

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 24th, 2004. The long road home. Sometimes we are faced with difficult decisions in our daily life. These decisions often times revolve around far more important matters than our routine from sunrise to sundown. Decisions can be life altering and completely turn everything you thought you knew upside down and inside out. I had made such a decision only four years ago at the tender age of eighteen. I remember every single thing about that day…about that moment… It was a sunny July day, the tenth of July actually and one day after my eighteenth birthday. You couldn't have asked for a more peaceful day…the sun burning down across the dry Nebraska plains, the wind whipping about sending a refreshing breeze over the flat land. I never could quite discover where the wind came from…but wherever that wind blew from it seemed to entice me. The wind was without form…it could not be captured, it could not be calmed and it could not be tamed. It was just the way I wanted to be…free to do whatever it chose, whenever and wherever. So that day I made my big decision, I broke away from my chains of social and parental oppression and I ran freely to the great unknown which, is commonly referred to as the Wrestling industry. So from a small Nebraska town to the top of the world…it seemed I had came full circle. It was a little bit crazy that Global Wrestling had never promoted an event in the great state of Nebraska and when they chose to do so it was the biggest event of the year…headlined by the only Nebraska native with his name on a Global Wrestling contract. My journey to greatness had began in Nebraska…and it appeared that it was here I would climb to the pinnacle of success that I had broken bones and tore muscles to finally reach. Beautiful Nebraska. I couldn't help but smile widely as my bluish gray eyes peered from the passenger jet I rode on and down to the never ending plains of Nebraska. The state had quite an interesting shape from the air…it was cut into squares where farmers worked tediously day in and day out to feed, not only the state, not only the nation but the entire world. Nebraska was an interesting place to live…a good place to leave…yet I never thought it would be so great to return too. After all, what was so special about the state? All that we had were farms, never ending plains, great hunting and of course…the pride of Nebraska, our beloved Nebraska Cornhuskers. But despite the hard work…despite the miniscule towns…despite the dust and dirt…despite it all, it was a place I had grown to love. My childhood home had grown into my obsession. It was in Nebraska that I had made the choice to become a Professional Wrestler. It was in Nebraska that I had begun honing my mind and body to the sharpness of a razors edge in my Martial arts skills. It was in Nebraska that I had grown to become a man…and it would be in Nebraska that I would become a World Champion. Shoving the fond memories of my native state to the back of my mind I felt warm inside from the reception I would no doubt have from my friends. Friends that were closer than brothers and for the very first time would see me live in competition. I had already gave out tickets…many of them. Three of my best friends would be at ringside, my Martial arts instructor, and a host of other acquaintances that I had grown to deeply cherish and respect. It would no doubt be a night to remember…for one reason or another. The reason to remember it would of course be what I had worked for the last four years…the World Championship that had eluded me. If I won that World championship then everything I've done…everything I've worked for would suddenly be brought to light. The broken bones would be worth it; the torn muscles would no longer ache. In short…I would be on top of the World…or at least on top of the Wrestling world. But another way it could be remembered is if I lost…if my shoulders were placed on that canvas for a three count, it would be a bitter pill to swallow and one that could ultimately destroy me. If the referee's hand dropped for the three count and the bell rang then suddenly the world as I have known it would change forever. The broken bones would have been in naught, every time I stretched I would be reminded of my torn muscles as they cried out in inaudible pain. If I lost…my world would come crashing down around me and I would be left to pick up the pieces and place them back together…losing was not an option. I let out a soft sigh as I closed my eyes and dropped my head back against the seat my shaggy black locks shooting everywhere in an order of disarray. I rested my hands comfortably, palms down, on my wrinkled Versace jeans that served as a medium between my black Converse Chuck Taylor's and my light gray, skin tight sleeveless shirt that hugged tightly to my torso. A black Jetta cuff around my left wrist and the cross pendant that dangled from my neck completed my attire as I rested comfortably aboard this flight. Yet as comfortable as I was I couldn't shake that uneasy feeling in my gut…it seemed as if someone had just kicked me right in the pit of my stomach and I was feeling the effects. Something just felt…wrong. Perhaps it was the fact that I would step into the Global Wrestling Octagon and wage war with one of my best friends…or perhaps it was the fact that I had never came this close to destiny before. I felt my hands start to tremble as my mind ran down this path of second-guessing. Since the day I stepped into the Wrestling ring I had wanted to be the best…and I had repeatedly been told that to become the best you must be the World Champion. Well truth of the matter was…I am the best. There isn't another technically master on my level of proficiency, there isn't another man out there that can go airborne as well as I can. In fact there isn't a man in the entire business that can match me move for move…but I had never been given the opportunity to prove that. I could count a total of one World title shot in my entire career…and it was a World title shot that had came in an Independent federation, a title shot in which, I lost. So after being cheated of destiny here I was…staring the monster called fate dead in the eyes. It shot back beams of hatred and anger as I fell into the role of perennial underdog a place that I was all too familiar with. 'You can't beat Ice.' The critics had said, then again that's what they had said about Jesse Jamester, that's what they had said about Deacon Holmes, that's what they had said about nearly every single man that laced his boots and stood across from me. I had defeated Ice before and I would do it again. Train put the shoulders of Ice on the canvas…John Zero put the shoulders of Ice on the canvas. And a mere week before Clash of the Titans Ice had been the man who cost our team a match against Souls of the Black Lotus when he was pinned directly in the center of that Global Wrestling Octagon. The rest of the Wrestling world hadn't made the same realizations as I…they hadn't realized that Ice was the human hype machine. It wasn't his presence in the ring that took a man down but rather his braggadocios and boastful nature outside of that Octagon. He claimed to be the best…yet he had lost two straight weeks. He claimed to be the greatest Wrestler alive…yet I had defeated him enough times to know the internal workings of his mind, better than my very own. It was all hype…every single bit of it. My mind traveled down this littered path before suddenly snapping from it's thought process as the plane lurched violently, turning in the air high above the Nebraska plains. Glancing out of the window I noticed a town in view…it was the city of Lincoln, my home. I twisted around in my first class seat as I stared through the glass pane that provided vision to the outside world, looking across the city of Lincoln an interesting thought came to mind… The prodigal son had returned…and he had returned for his moment of glory.
