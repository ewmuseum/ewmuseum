CW's surprise, The Gauntlet

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Wednesday The inside of a relatively large yet somewhat rundown gym is shown. The walls are painted in chipping beige paint with brown trim near the bottom. Dozens of posters showcasing famous prize fighters and wrestlers adorn the walls serving as tribute to those who have trained here while also helping to cover holes and hold the chipping paint to the sweating walls. There are several people training at any one station as well as aspiring fighters working with the medicine ball, hitting the speed bag, or jumping work. Today the gym’s ring which usually has people lined up waiting their turn to spar is empty. The ring is surrounded by an anxious crowd looking on curiously at the four large men that stand at each turnbuckle. The gym’s impressive surround sound system is blaring “24's” by T.I. as everyone’s attention turns toward the steel double doors of the gym which have just opened. In walks Deacon Holmes prodded by a slap to the back of the head by CW who is dressed in Polo from head to toe and smoking a cigarette. Holmes looks at the gym to notice everyone staring at him and offers a head nod. He then turns to a chuckling CW. Deacon Holmes: What tha hell is going on here? CW: I told you I had a surprise for you. I can’t have my nephew runnin around lookin soft on TV. It’s disgraceful, embarrassing, and I won’t have it! So I set up this little training seminar for you. Deacon Holmes: Well which one am I supposed to be going up against? CW: AHAHAHA, all of em. Deacon Holmes: Are you kidding me!” I’m not running the gauntlet against these clowns in here! I’m not even getting paid! CW: Well this is what you get for losing, should’ve wrestled somebody your own size. Deacon Holmes: You saw what happened last time, the poor bastard killed himself. There’s no comp for me, Leviathan just got lucky. CW: Well go prove it in that ring. The crowd begins to chant “Deacon!” growing louder and louder as CW starts to laugh again and gives Deacon a slap on the back of the head to send him on his way. Holmes cuts a path through the chanting crowd while taking off his black “If you don’t like it, BEAT ME!” shirt exposing his tattoos and pulling the rubberband out of his hair allowing his dreads to fall to his shoulders. He climbs onto the apron before hopping over the top rope and into the ring in a pair of black AND1 basketball shorts and matching Jordan XI’s. He dances around a little to get loose and does a couple quick stretches. His first opponent is Duke Irving, an ex pro wrestler turned drug dealer after losing his job due to steroid use. He stands a stocky six feet three inches and has just stepped into the ring. A bell sounds and both men go at it. Irving runs in with a clothesline which Deacon hooks and turns into a crucifix going for an early pin. After a two count both men get to their feet and reset themselves. Holmes shoots in low and hits a double leg take down, taking advantage of the situation he then flips forward holding both legs and bridges. After another two count Irving wraps his arms around Holmes? waist and begins to rise turning Holmes in the process setting him up for a powerbomb. He goes to lift Holmes over his head but Holmes blocks it by clipping the back of Irving’s knees out landing him in the same position as before. Holmes bridges and gets the three count. Holmes pops up and informs the cheering crowd that he’s just warming up as his next opponent Felipe Sanchez ambushes him with a missile dropkick to the back sending him sliding across the ring. Holmes looks up to find the five foot eight luchador going for a somersault splash which he avoids by rolling out of the way. Both men quickly hop to their feet and lock up with each other. Deacon Holmes comes out wrenching Sanchez’s arm with a hammerlock. He manages to lift Sanchez into a spinning torture rack while still barring his arm before dropping him down across a waiting knee. Holmes then applies a cross knee back breaker hold causing Sanchez to submit. As soon as he yells “I quit” Gary Nelson, a six foot ten inch bouncer from the club on the corner, hops in and nails Deacon with a double axe handle in the back. He then takes the opportunity to put the boots to Holmes who eventually trips him up sending him to the mat. Holmes grabs both his legs and goes for a stretched boston crab that has Nelson’s legs over Deacon’s shoulders and his neck supporting all of his body’s weight. Holmes leans back with his knee resting on Nelson’s head to inflict maximum pain. After about a minute of this Nelson’s arm finds the ropes and Holmes lets go of the hold, ending it with a swift kick in the ribs. With Nelson on his knees Holmes dives on his back and applies a kataha-jime with a leg scissors. Nelson struggles for almost two minutes to crawl to the ropes before passing out with them just inches out of his reach. A now exhausted Holmes props himself in the corner while awaiting his next and final opponent, Talon MacPhearson, a six foot eight monster. MacPhearson removes a his robe and steps into the ring. The two men circle each other briefly before locking up. MacPhearson gains the upper hand and whips Holmes into the ropes and comes at him with a big boot. Deacon sidesteps and goes for a rolling crosshold but MacPhearson counters with a facesmash leaving Holmes slow to get up. MacPhearson drags him to his feet by his hair and hits a slingshot half-nelson suplex. Holmes lays on the canvas face down breathing heavily trying to find his strength again. MacPhearson doesn’t wait for that to happen though as he drags him to his feet once more and gives him a few knee lifts. He then throws Holmes over his right shoulder and runs toward the corner to smash him into the ringpost. Just as they are about to make contact Holmes slides off MacPhearson’s shoulder and pushes him into the turnbuckle. MacPhearson stumbles backwards into a waiting Holmes who hits a desperation Final Flash. Both men lie lifeless on the mat for a seven count before Holmes manages to drape an arm over MacPhearson. After a slow two count MacPhearson, the gym favorite, kicks out and wraps his hand around Holmes? neck. He chokes Holmes as he gets back to his feet pulling him up as well. Once there he lifts Holmes high into the air and prepares to finish him off. Just as MacPhearson is about to drive him through the mat Holmes applies a triangle hold. MacPhearson his a thunderous chokeslam but is dragged down to one knee by Holmes who is still holding on. There is a stalemate for several minutes as both men are being choked with no way to escape. They both refuse to give up their hold on the other as they stare each other down. After an impressive showdown MacPhearson keels over to the side unconscious. Deacon Holmes is announced as the winner and an overjoyed CW pushes his way to the front of the crowd and climbs into the ring. When he gets there he simply kick Holmes in the ribs in disgust...he is unconscious for the third time in a row this week.
