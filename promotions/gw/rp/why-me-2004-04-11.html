'Why Me?'

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th April 2004 'Why Me?' There remained all of a day until I'd be forced to compete in another triple threat contest on Evolution. All of a day until I'd be required to fight my way through another two individuals. I often wondered if these matches were devised to help me grow as a 'tough' competitor, I often wondered if these matches were created to help me ascend through the ranks of GW. Brick, a GW legend in his own right; being forced to lower his target range to the newcomer, to the underperformers of the growing organization. I asked myself if being booked against this fellow was something of a gift, something of a reward for surpassing ALL expectation at 'Clash of the Titans', something of an incentive to continue my push through the ranks. Every match up until this point had been in the same setting. Three men walk into the ring and one man walks away a winner. Fortunately, I'd been able to be the one walking on two of the last three occasions, my only blip coming in my first venture into this kind of activity. 'Threesomes' had never been my kind of adventure but with the number I was being presented with, I was beginning to wonder if this was the only kind of action that I'd ever really recieve. The scene for the day was a local park. I'd jogged through in preparation for Evolution; in preparation for another test from the boys of Global Wrestling. Clad in a pair of loose-fitting black jogging bottoms, black trainers and a loose-fitting black hoodie, whilst I didn't look the greatest of characters, I was comfortable enough to be pushing through this training session. A few people decided to be polite on my journey, waving a few times and sending a few cheers for my upcoming contest. I could never quite get my head around why these people were nice to me but I always welcomed it. Being appreciated for something actually felt somewhat good. 'Bricks and Stones... er, Diamonds... may break my bones but words will never hurt me.' I sang, trying to accommodate the little verse to my current situation. I'll admit, I was never the type to make good of anything but this little number sure brought a smug smile to my face. Continuing to jog along, I repeated the same line, each time a smile coming to my face and a little chuckle to follow. Eventually though, after a few minutes of this continued jog, I stopped moving along and sat down on an old friend; namely the conveniently placed wooden bench. This time though, I didn't have the benefit of a fat person babbling about something... nor did I have any kind of person coming to me, trying to talk to me in some retarded form of english. For the first time since I'd started to run, I was able to sit down alone... without the hassle or bother. 'Brick and Diamond...' I said to myself, thinking about my opponents, trying to establish some kind of plan for these very unique characters. As most people probably knew, I'd not been able to come up with anything to defeat either Diamond or Brick. Sure, I'd managed to watch about seventeen years of JC's life on VHS but Brick, well... the only thing I could think of with regards to him was to use some kind of sledgehammer and pray that he cracks a little with every connecting blow. '... I have absolutely no idea how I'm going to contend with the big guy... and even less of an idea as to how I'm going to deal with a guy who chose to put his entire life on VHS and most likely, DVD.' I'll be honest now, probably for the first time in a while. The idea of facing a couple of people that I couldn't find all that much about, was somewhat threatening. It's true, Brick was a man who should have galleries of information to study and prepare for... but there was nothing that could especially help me in my quest. I didn't have the physical strength to throw him about or thrash him like a tiny Cruiserweight. Diamond, I could carry about and throw down... but Brick, are these people starting to think that I'm superhuman or something? '... You really know how to confuse the Captain.' I said, leaning back on the bench, my arms stretching out into the air. The cold breeze began to blow against my face, it's gentle hand almost stroking me. I wanted to get up and press ahead but I really couldn't be bothered to move. The lack of company, whilst good to a degree, never really made me feel any better. It was at this point that a couple of voices seemed to bring my attention around. 'Like, woah! Woah! Can you believe this? Are you like, seeing what I'm seeing?' a female voice screamed. The collective scream that followed was one of the worst things that you could have ever heard. Nails on a chalkboard? Compared to this, that was child's play. Another female seemed to scream after that... and another followed. I felt like I was listening to the 'Symphony of the Damned' or perhaps something from episode five of Dairanger. Regardless, it was really bad and I didn't like it at all. 'Oh my God! It's Rich Sherwin! It's the man who's had more threesome's in his GW career than anybody else!' one of the girls chimed in, bringing more of this annoying fan-girl music to my ears. I wanted to jump up and sprint off into the distance but what would that do? I'd only stop at some point in the distance and get jumped by even more of these annoying types. Disinterested in the masses of girls that had crowded me, I sat up and looked at the character that appeared to be the leader of the pack. She looked rather nice... but at the same time, a little too young for my own personal preference. She might make one person happy in her life but that sure as hell wasn't going to be me... at least for the next five years or so. '... Yes, I'm Rich Sherwin... but I'm afraid we're looking at threesome in the wrong context... at least, that's going to be the most probable side of things. I mean, aside from one bout, I've had to have these TRIPLE THREAT matches with guys.' I tried to sound as serious as possible, looking to get these people to understand that I wasn't actually all that into real threesome's and that I really wasn't all that interested in constantly having to fight my way through two people instead of the one. For a moment, it seemed as though the information had gone directly to their brains as every single one of them took a step back. I felt inclined to get up and walk away, content that my mission was complete... but no, in all truth, it'd more or less only just begun. 'Woah, so... you're like, a homosexual or something? Always playing with the boys and stuff? Does that mean that Brick and Diamond are homosexual too or something?' asked one of the girls, a very large hint of curiousity underlaying her tone of voice. I have no idea how the conversation got to this point but for some reason it did. I tried to believe that there was no possibility of women being as perverse as the guys... but, it dawned upon me that they were probably much worse... just, a little less outspoken than eighty percent of the current male population. 'Believe it or not... I'm not homosexual and I really don't know what the preferences of Brick and Diamond are. Come to think of it, I really don't think that I care either.' I uttered, loud enough for everyone to hear. There seemed to be a gasp among the group of rabid fangirls, a surefire sign that they didn't buy the truth for one second. I don't know what the deal is but it's almost like these girls seem to appreciate homosexual people more. It's not like they're going to be able to give them any loving, is it? 'You're not a homosexual?' came the first response to my damning statement, obviously shattering a lot of their dreams. I'm sure that many of these people would have loved to take me shopping or something... but alas, they'd have to find somebody else to have their vile ways with. 'What do you mean, you're not a homosexual?' another persisted, almost sounding desperate. 'Well, what it means is that I actually enjoy the company of women and have a door that I'd like to keep virginal for the rest of my life, amen.' I stated in a stern tone of voice, the whole moment causing displeasure as opposed to anything else. I picked myself up from the bench and looked around at the mass of female admirers that had got in my way. Some of them looked shocked, some of them were upset... and some of them had this rather wicked gleam in their eye. I thought it was going to be something worthwhile at first but at second glance, they appeared to be trannies. 'Now..' I began, pressing my arms outward to create some space for myself before continuing, '... Since this issue has been dragged through the air and thrown into my face, I'm going to take a few moments to talk to you all about something rather important. You see, I have a match against these two people on Evolution... and like you, I don't quite know what way they actually swing. Now, I'm not actually implying anything but you know how the boat rocks, don't you?' The masses gave something of a positive response, almost cheering at the sign that there may just be some hope for them yet. I felt inclinded to continue, inclined to lead these troops along the way. I didn't have much reason to do so but as long as they didn't think I were batting for the other team, everything was good. '... Ok, since I'm going to have a bit of trouble against this really big guy, I think all of you will need to jump into the ring and help me if things get a little out of hand. I mean, you should know exactly how good I am... but, even the best need a little help here and there.' The mass cheered and proceeded to run back off to whence they came, believing that everything was over. Confused and slightly bemused, I was very skeptical of anyone showing up at Evolution to help me... regardless of whether something happened or not. Today's training had not been in complete vain, although it did feel like I didn't actually accomplish anything. Regardless of what actually happened though, I still had no gameplan for the disposal of Brick and I still had no idea of how I was going to contend with Mr VHS. Work with Brick? Work with Diamond? All I hope is that I come away with another win. Wish me luck! : End :
