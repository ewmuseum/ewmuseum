Having lunch

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/03 Brick and Aaron sit in their favorite soul food restaurant, having lunch. Aaron has a fish sandwich, while Brick has baked chicken. Brick has just gotten through telling Aaron about his match with Leviathan. “So, Salaok actually cost Leviathan the match?” asks Aaron as he bites into his sandwich. “Son of a bitch hit me in the shoulder with that damn shovel,” Brick replies. “Referee gave me the match by DQ. Salaok was pissed. He even tried to make the ref reverse the call. The ref refused. Ah, it’s his own fault, anyway. Stupid bitch should have stayed out of the match.” “So, then what happened?” “Anyway, he threatened to fire the ref if he didn’t reverse the call. The ref still refused. So, Salaok threw him across the ring and was about to hit him with the shovel when Leviathan grabbed it out of his hand and threw it out of the ring.” “Get out!” “I’m serious. So I rolled up behind, then Leviathan pushed him and made him trip. I started to beat his ass good when Draven came down. I got what I wanted, so I didn’t want to stay behind.” “You should have stayed and whooped Draven’s ass.” “The thought crossed my mind. I guess I paid for it, though. After we left the ring, Leviathan chokeslammed me on the ramp. Yeah, he was looking to make some impact that night and he did. I might have to get back at him for it, but I got bigger fish to fry.” Truer words were never spoken. Brick is in a match that was years in the making. Since November of 2001, when Salaok robbed Brick of his first-ever World Title shot. Since last summer, when he kidnapped and tortured Aaron to get to Brick. Since last October, when True Evil assaulted Brick backstage at Dark Rituals, and Salaok made a thinly veiled threat concerning his sister’s upcoming nuptials. Since last November, when Salaok made an appearance at the engagement party on the one year anniversary of the night he screwed Brick. Since the past month of Salaok booking matches designed to get Brick suspended indefinitely from Global Wrestling. This Hell in a Cell match at Wrestlecade has been a long time coming, and Brick was ready to savor every moment of it. “Salaok ain’t as slick as he’d like everyone to think he is. I know this is a set-up. Everybody knows this is a set-up. Stevie Wonder could see it’s a set-up. The hardcore match with Humane was a set-up. The triple threat match with Tragic Hero and Roz Blayze was a set-up. The handicap tag match was a set-up. Every match he puts me in is a set-up. He wants me out of GW, and do you know why?” “He’s afraid of you.” “Exactly, because he knows I’m not afraid of him. It’s like Derek Frost said on Overload. Salaok is just scared that his precious Nevermore will fall apart at the seams, so he’s just trying to eliminate anybody who might be a threat when he has the chance. And I apparently qualify as a threat. But you know what” Cats have tried to take me out before and failed miserably. Jason Ayers tried and failed. Eric Lansheer tried and failed. The Scarecrow tried and failed. And Salaok is just a cheap carbon copy of the Scarecrow. What makes him think he can take me out when his brother couldn’t even get the job done? And now, it’s payback time.” “You gonna be ready for Wrestlecade?” “Why do people keep asking me that” You, Braxton, my mom, Monica. I’m ready, OK? I’ve been ready for two years now.” “I’m just saying, you know. You and I both know what he’s capable of. I think you should be careful.” “I’ll be careful. But just in case Salaok has his little Nevermore bitches try something, I do have something planned for their asses.” “Good, and when you beat Salaok, Me and Felicia are gonna throw you a party to celebrate.” “I don’t think Mom’ll be there. She’s not too happy about me going through with this.” “I think she understands that you feel you have to go through with this. Still, you should know she ain’t gonna be happy about it.” “I do have to do this. At Wrestlecade, it’s all or nothing. I’m gonna beat his ass from one end of the cell to the other and back.” “Hey, what time is it?” Brick checks his watch. “5:45. Why?” “Oh shit! I have to take Felicia to Lamaze!” “What time is the class?” “7 o’clock.” “OK, let’s go.” The two men scramble to leave the restaurant. One has a family to start. The other has a score to settle.
