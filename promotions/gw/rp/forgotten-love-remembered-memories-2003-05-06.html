Forgotten Love, Remembered Memories

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/6/03 He holds hands with her. She is beautiful. Her curly red hair flows down her back and bounces with every step. Her beautiful brown eyes glimmer from the sun in the sky. She is beautiful to the world, but amazing to him. He stops in his place. He helps her sit down on her knees, hoping not to stain her marvelous dress. He soon follows, he smiles’because he can not stop smiling. The love he has isn’t a love that everybody has. It is a desire that every one dreams about. And that desire sits right in front of his eyes. His heart pounds every second he stands by here and he often slurs his voice. He takes out the piece of paper folded in his pocket and unfolds it. She looks puzzled? “What is that?” “Just something I wrote for you.” “You wrote something for me?” She smiles, the same smile that he wants. That smile, it means more to him then every star in the sky. It is all he has. She leans over and attempts to read the piece of paper. He draws it back? “No need to rush, I’ll read it to you.” She sits back and waits for him. He clears his throat? ? Alone With No Place To Call Home Waiting To Hear Your Voice On The Phone You Don’t Realize The Twinkle In Your Eyes You Have No Clue My Smile Has Extended Its Boundaries All Because Of You The Touch Of Your Skin The Smell Of Your Hair The Taste Of Your Kiss Oh, Nothing Can Compare I Never Want To Wash Your Aroma Off My Hand I Love You More Than Every Grain In The Sand I Love You More Than Every Fish In The Sea Than Every Star In The Sky Than Every Drop In The Rain And You Don’t Know Why? She smiles, she attempts to hug him’but he chuckles? “Sit back, I’m not done yet.” She chuckles too and sits back and awaits for the rest of the declaration of his love for her. You Are Everything I Want You Are Everything I Need You Are Everything Little Thing My Dreams Said You Would Be When I Say I Love You Love Is An Under-Estimate For A Dictionary Can’t Describe How You Make Me Feel My Love For You Is Unreal Love, Oh Love, You Are All My Heart Is Beating For She smiles. Her smile sends a chill down his spine, he has finally read her the poem he has been working on for such a long time. And it was worth it, she hasn’t stopped smiling yet. “Sage, did you write that?” He takes a deep breath and shakes his head side to side? “No, I had a little help.” “From who?” He slowly looks up at her, she is confused as can be. Her smile has faded? “Your heart.” The smile reappears. She leans over and kisses him on the cheek. “Sage, I love you. You are all MY heart is beating for.” He sits back and smiles, he is too happy and nervous at the same time to do anything else. “I know you do, that is why we will be together forever.” “Forever and ever.” Together Forever And Ever Its all that runs through his mind. Day and night, night and day. He can’t stop thinking about those words’forever and ever, together. Margo and Sage, they will never split apart. Margo wasn’t like Jess’she accepted his love. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- She stands next to her parents’pleadings and begging for their acceptance of him. “Dad, but I love him more then anything.” “You don’t even know what love is Margo. You are only 20. Your mother and I started dating when we were 23’you still have time Margo. Don’t jump into relationships now, you have your whole life for that.” “But what would happen if you met Mom earlier then you did and fell madly in love with her, like I am in love with Sage.” “Then we would wait.” “No you wouldn’t Daddy, you would fight it. You would do what I am doing now.” “No I wouldn’t, because I don’t fall for pathetic people like Sage.” “Is that what this is all about” Is this all because Sage isn’t like one of your star athletes, or one of your million dollar princes? All of that doesn’t matter to me daddy, what matters is his love. And no other guy has shown me that love.” He raises his voice? “No guy has shown you that love because you haven’t waited to feel it. Sage is just the first, and he is a sad one to be a first at anything.” “Daddy, why can’t you see that he bleeds like every other person and that he is normal, just like us.” “That piece of garbage isn’t like us at all. He lives in a god damn shack for Christ sake. He plows fields and plants vegetables. Where do you see yourself in 5 years Margo” Helping him plant cucumbers?” “He might not be able to give me a diamond ring or a magical wedding, but he can offer me love’and in the end, that is all that matters.” “You live at home, I forbid you see that peasant once again.” “But daddy?” “NO BUTS ABOUT IT MARGO!” She puts her head down in defeat. She has failed, she accepted Sage for who he was and not what he had, something he has never had before. But society, this time, Margo’s parents did not accept him for who he was. They thought of what he owned and what he did for a living. They looked on the outside, when Margo was looking on the inside. It hurts to realize everything God has blessed him with, always falls apart right before his eyes. Those blessings always end up in pain and sorrow. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- “Sage, you know I love you’but things just won’t work out because of my parents.” “Do the words, Together Forever and Ever, mean anything to you?” “Sage, it isn’t my fault, only if you can realize that.” “Together to me means me’and you. Forever means each dying day and every breath. And Ever means through every problem and every pain. Margo, I love you forever and ever?” “Sage, it has nothing?” “Is this goodbye?” “Forever and ever.” A tear falls from his face. He takes a couple steps backwards, studying her. Remember the memories, for they will never fade. But these memories, they are the ones that he wants to fade. The love he has for her, it was only temporary’he is used to it, so it seems. He has grown immune to this pain and it is starting to become a ritual. Everywhere he looks and whoever he finds, he is not good enough. If only they would accept him, then he will have that missing spot filled once and for all. There are no more pictures on the wall, no more stories told, but the memories my friends, they will last forever and ever.
