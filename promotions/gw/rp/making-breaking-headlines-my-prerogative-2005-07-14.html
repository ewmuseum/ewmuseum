Date: 07/14/05 'Vivica J. Valentine is nothing more than a glorified barbie doll who needs to stay way from a Global Wrestling Octagon. Her aerial skills are dime a dozen and she's pushed incredibly beyond her own real ability because of her stunning beauty. Don't get me wrong, I think she's one of the hottest girls I've seen in my life. But the girl is average at best, I think they should make more room for some real talent in Global.' -- Bruce Carnagy (Fight Night Illustrated) Flourescent lights beat down on the barren locker room. Almost deserted in appearance... a far cry from the almost chaotic pace in the Las Vegas arena on a Friday Night Evolution. A clock on the wall ticks away the time until all the questions are answered. As life became shorter, opportunity grew neared. Anticipation racked the mind of a beautiful young girl, whose breath-taking cerulean blue eyes were unsheated and glaced upon the clock on the far side of the room. Tick... Tick... Tick... A deep sigh escaped her small pink lips. Brilliance once again hidden behind peach walls of forbiddence, making her entire world drop into temporary nothingness as she pushed herself off the stiff oak bench. Throwing a towel over her shoulder, she opened her eyes once again... moving strands of golden blonde hair out of her face. Most would never take a face like that to be that of a fighter... but then again, most would be ignorant fools. Slowly she stepped towards the door. The sounds of gossiping outside let her know what was awaiting her. She had to leave sometime... 'It's time.' ---- People can take everything away from you... ---- 'Overall I think the hunting grounds is amazing. Christian Zenith makes a great leader along with some phenomanal talents... Shadow, Dawn McFarlane, and Tessa Windsor. Your typical lackies in Demitri Miasnikov and Jason LeMaster, but then you have Vivica J. Valentine. A complete waste of space, she must be great in bed for Christian to keep her around. Eye candy and nothing more... there are plenty of pretty girls in the world, at least half of them have to have more talent than Vivica.' -- Maurice Young (The Wrestling Edge) Her hand plunged into the wooden door, sending it and her open to the wolves. A frenzy of cameras and media quickly attack the young woman, dressed in a speghetti strap top and a pair of baggy black Oakley shorts, embroidered with white O's on each side. A roar escaped from the mouths of the collective media personalities. She blocked it all from her mind as she tried to march past them. Quickly they made a human wall... barricading her against the very wood door in which she escaped earlier. 'Miss Valentine what are your thoughts about Danielle Northsky?' 'Excuse me Miss Valentine, do you think you're ready for singles competition in Global?' 'Miss Valentine! How did your booking come about? Did you expect a singles match for this card?' 'Pardon me Miss Valentine, what kind of ring training do you really have? It's rumored that you have relatives in the business, is that true?' Vivica J. Valentine crossed her arms eyes the press before looking down at the ground. All this press over one stinking match? You'd think she was part of a major terrorist attack. Then again that shows you how much of a life wrestling reporters and fans really have outside of their world. Why can't I just train and go home like everyone else? Why does everything I do have to be under a microscope? This isn't the end of the world, it was just one... fucking... match. Right? ---- But they can never take away your truth... ---- 'It's amazing what a big mouth and a pretty face can get you in today's society. Global's Vivica J. Valentine is a prime example. A trash talking, ho-bag who Global absolutely refuses to put in the ring on her own. Tag team after Tag Team that never seem to work, you think there might be a reason for that? It's cause she's completely useless... and if she's not the next one fired from Global, their management just made a huge mistake.' --David Moreau (Pro Wrestling Today) The lights glowed on Vivica's angelic face as she looked back at the media attention like a deer in headlights. The media held out their microphones towards her... they were like guns in a way. They were used as weapons to force you to confess, or to do as if they wanted you to. Nothing more than a gang of thieves... 'What in the hell is wrong with you people?' Vivica said aloud. As the press simply looked at each other momentarily before firing questions once again. 'I just want to train and go in there and fight. I don't think that's too much to ask... I'm not some little girl you know. I can handle myself just fine, thank you. Now if you'll excuse me I have a life. Now I understand you're not familiar with the concept, get the fuck out of my way and maybe just MAYBE you'll have one like me one day.' Vivica plunged into the wrestling media circus and escaped through the other side. Vivica walked down the hallway towards the exit as Global Security began to push back the media trying to stampede her from behind. A quick glance at the wall was a reminder to Vivica... Tick... Tick... Tick... The countdown to showtime had begun... ---- But the question is... Can you handle mine? ---- 'Vivica J. Valentine vs. Danielle Northsky... is Global insane? Vivica J. Valentine is going to get mutilated, maybe Dani can beat her into submission and teach her what a real woman is like... so maybe she'll stay out of an octagon in the future.' --Michael Jensen ([detail withheld]) The exit sign above the door ahead seemed like a beaming light coming down from heaven. An escape from the madness... a chance to collect her thoughts and emotions. She really didn't know what it was like to fight in Global, but it wasn't like she hadn't fought before. She'd fought for most of her life... and if Mindy Mercer can get a World Title shot it can't REALLY be too hard. 'I'm not going to choke...' Vivica said to herself as he approached the door, 'This is my triumphant debut. This event will forever change the future of Global Wrestling... I don't care what the fuck people say, they don't know me and they never will. This is my life to do as I choose, no matter what Tim Gibbons from Boston thinks about while he strokes his pecker at night... I'm not some little giddy cheerleader.' The words spewed from Vivica's pretty pink lips as she shoved the exit door open, and the hot Vegas air blew through her golden blonde hair. Once again she closed her eyes and felt the air blow over her body... peace of body is peace of mind. With a deep breath she enjoyed the not so silent Las Vegas surroundings. 'Sounds like Timmy Gibbons has it made...' A man said, breaking Vivica's escape from reality. Her eyes shot open and looked over at a rather round man with a tape recorder. 'My name is Nathaniel Chambers, I'm with PWP... Pro Wrestling Press?' 'I know what PWP is... no comment, I'm done with interviews.' Vivica said as she walked past Nathaniel, trying her best to completely ignore his presence... trying desperately to keep pace with her. 'But the adoring public wants to know what's on the mind of Global's new hot topic... the girl next door making her debut.' 'Do you not know what no comment means? Get that thing out of my face before I shove it up your ass.' Vivica said glaring over at Mr. Chambers. He knew the woman look of scorn well, but little did he know exactly how threatening it could be. 'I guess that means Christian's not exactly approving huh? I'd hate to have anything happen to that face too...' 'Fuck off.' Vivica said as she shoved Nathaniel Chambers into the brick building. He let out a grunt upon impact, dropping the tape recorder out of his hand. He squinted and fell to the ground grabbing his right arm. Vivica looked down at his pain for a moment before quickly moving back towards her car, 'This is all I need right now...' ---- ...They say I'm crazy... ---- The ominous glow of the television pulsated around the room... 'And now our top story. Late last night outside the Global Arena in Las Vegas, a professional wrestling media personality was assulted by the wrestler in which he was covering. Vivica Julia Valentine, a 21-year old wrestler from Highland Park, Illinois is the suspect. It is assumed that upon leaving the arena that the media personality, Nathaniel Chambers asked Miss Valentine a question and she responded by shoving him into a brick wall, breaking his shoulder on impact. She quickly fled the scene, and an investigation is currently underway to find out exactly what happened. It is understood that Mr. Chambers is filing charges. Police are currently looking for Vivica Julian Valentine to ask her questions. If anyone has any information on where she may be at this time, please contact the Las Vegas Police Department.' The sudden shut of the door announced her arrival into the room. She looked around the room, investigating the newly build Zenith Manor West in Lake Tahoe, Nevada. Slowly she approached the couch, she saw the television through the window and knew that pratically everyone in Nevada knew by now. She stopped behind the couch and began to watch the remaining news before a clap started to echo through the manor. She closed her cerulean blue eyes and gulped as a voice broken the awkward silence... 'Well done Vivica.' A man said sitting on the couch in front of her, never taking his eyes off the television, 'Now tell me again exactly how you kept from getting caught all these years? Did the people just not ask you the right question?' 'Shut it Christian...' Vivica said before she turned around and started to walk out of the room. Before she could leave he grabbed her wrist. Vivica sighed before trying to yank her arm out of his hand with no luck. 'I don't think you understand...' Christian said, 'This is exactly the reason I wanted to go with you. I knew this freak show media would show up and give you the third degree. I also know how you are Vivica... I wasn't sure if you could handle it on your own. But I guess now I know that you can't. You're a smart girl, can we try to act like it please?' 'Your not my dad Christian, I can do things on my own without your help you know.' 'That isn't what the news is telling me, plus you have no idea how much this puts everything in jeopardy. You assulted a MEDIA PERSONALITY Vivica. He has a tape recorder to catch the whole thing. This is concrete... we have an agreement that you might've just broken. We pay for pretty new squad cars, we don't mess with the police and the police don't mess with us. How can the police ignore this? It's a media circus.' Christian Zenith said with his cadet blue eyes locking with Vivica's. 'I'll handle it.' Vivica said, shooting the woman look of scorn towards an unphased Christian Zenith. 'Right, like you handled this media personality.' Christian replied, 'This is a job for me to take care of. If I want something done right I have to do it myself.' 'No!' Vivica said before she walked over and got into the face of Christian Zenith, 'I'm not a little fucking girl anymore Christian. I can do this myself... and I don't need your damn permission to do it. This is my mess... and I'm cleaning it up without your help. You got that?' Christian Zenith let go of Vivica's hand and turned his back and walked away. Coldly, he sat back on the couch and tuned into a police scanner listening to see if anything about Vivica came up. With a deep breath Vivica walked back across the room towards the door. Slowly she turned the doorknob and walked into Nevada's grace. The door slammed shut behind her. She could feel her heartbeat quicken while she pushed golden blonde locks away from her face. Marching towards the door of her black Chrsysler Crossfire, Vivica knew what had to be done... 'Snap out of it Vivica...' She said to herself when she reached out and grabbed the handle to the car door, 'You have a job to take care of. I can't afford to blow this one...' ---- I really don't care That's my prerogative Some ask me questions Why am I so real? But they don't understand me I really don't know the deal about my sister Trying hard to make it right Not long ago Before I won this fight ---- The moon hung high in the Nevada sky. Coating the surface with a pale white glimmer, making the surface seem almost magically in appearance. Perhaps this is what the explorers spoke of when they said the streets were lined with gold. The gray concrete sparkled, as asphault was blown across in the desert wind. A beam of light struck the concrete, almost yellow in color. Light became shrouded by darkness as a silhouette became apparent on the glittering neutral surface. A plump man walked through the door, almost ball shaped in appearance. He would instantly remind many of the Penguin character from the Batman Comics. His right arm lie in a swing as he wobbled his way out the door, which is then shut behind him. He made his way towards a long champagne colored cadillac. Wearing a black XXXL T-Shirt, with a 'PWP' logo on the front and a pair of black jeans, he approached the vehicle and opened the door. Very cautiously the man walked around the car with the interior lighted... looking as if someone may be hiding in the back seat. After a careful inspection, he sqatted down into the drivers seat and turned the ignition. Sparking the nearly brand new luxury car to life. Turning on talk radio and pressing down on the accellerator he left the parking lot of Pro Wrestling Press Offices, which shared a building with many other media outlets... leaving what appeared to be a sleek black Chrysler in the rear view mirror. At full throttle and his head lights leading the way, he felt relaxed for the first time all day. Suddenly the sound of a 'Superstar' by Saliva ringtone caught his attention as he turned down his local talk radio station to plunge into his pocket. Out he pulled a small Motorolla flip phone, singing the song of his favorite wrestler... Ken Partridge Jr. He flipped the phone open and put it up to his ear as he drove with one hand. 'Nathaniel Chambers here.' He said listening for a response. 'It's going real well so far. They fell for the broken arm thing hook, line, and sinker.' Nathaniel said with a smile, 'Zenith has some deep pockets, so I'm sure this will be settled out of court so getting medical files isn't important. From what I know of Christian, court is the last place he wants to be... he'll pay you can bet on it.' 'Well even if not it's some well needed exposure for Pro Wrestling Press.' Chamber said before a pause, 'If they thought that whole Kenny Rogers pitching thing was some media hype they havn't seen anything yet. People assulting media personalities is a huge public concern now. They love watching people who have it all self destruct. Every woman in the world is glad about Vivica's bad press... with an ass like that, they have to have something on her.' The two having a conversation shared a laugh as Nathaniel listening to what the person on the other hand had to say. Suddenly a huge bang could be heard from the back of the cadillac and the back seat fell down flat. Nathaniel swerived out of shock as his heart began to beat out of control. 'What in the hell?!' Nathaniel screamed before he flipped the phone shut and threw it on the passenger seat. Suddenly someone jumped out from behind him and grabbed the steering wheel, swerving him off the desert highway road as he pressed down hard on the brake pedal. He began to breathe heavily as he felt cold steel against the side of his temple. He closed his eyes and his throat closed up, making his breathing shaky. Beads of sweat poured down his face as he waiting the end... or just a word or two from the person behind him. 'You fatass liar...' Those three words have never been said so sweet. Nathaniel immediately knew the voice, but he always thought their questioned background was all just a facade to get them over with the public... perhaps it still was. His hands began to shake and the sling fell off that was holding his not so broken right shoulder. He heard the gun click and took a huge gulp of saliva. 'It would be a damn shame if all of a sudden no one could find Mr. Chambers to get him to comment. Not to mention he'd never get the chance to press charges,' Vivica J. Valentine said from the backseat... pointing a black Desert Eagle into Nathaniel's skull, 'But you're lucky I'm too tired to kill you right now, so I'm feeling generous.' 'W... wh... what do you want from me?' Chamber's squeaked out as Vivica's blue eyes glittered in the darkness. 'I want you to pick up that cell phone...' Vivica said in Nathaniel's ear. Chambers sat there in shock, not moving a muscle. With that Vivica J. Valentine fired a shot into the dashboard and Nathaniel screamed out in terror, 'I SAID GRAB THE DAMN PHONE YOU FAT FUCK.' The car began to smell a bit like urine as Nathaniel Chambers reached over and grabbed the cellular phone. 'Take your fat little fingers and dial up the Las Vegas Police Department and drop all charges.' Vivica said planting the gun hard against Nathaniel's cranium once again. 'If you don't... well I can knock you out and we can go back to Zenith Manor. I'm sure I can have a nice little room for you there until either you feel like listening or I get tired of waiting and kill you.' 'You wouldn't get away with it.' 'Please piss pants, you have no idea how many times I've heard THAT line...' Vivica snapped back at the urine soaked journalist, 'Just fucking do it.' Nathaniel Chambers began to dial and Vivica waited in silence as the phone rang... 'Hello, this is Nathaniel Chambers... No I havn't swung by the doctors but I'll get that check-up...' 'Umpf...' Vivica said quietly to herself, about the police questioning his fake injury as they went on. 'Yeah, I was just thinking about it... it was really my fault. I'd like to drop all charges... Yes, I'm sure. I'll be in there in the morning to fill out the paperwork... You too, goodbye.' Nathaniel Chambers flipped his phone shut and suddenly the Desert Eagle shot a whole through the cell phone while it was in Nathaniel's hand, making him scream once again. He watched as the plastic and circuit board turned to scrapnel and ash. 'Why in the hell did you do that?' Chambers questioned of Valentine who replied with a smirk. 'I felt like it. Now... tell me chubby what's gonna happen if you don't fill out that paperwork tomorrow morning?' Vivica asked. 'You're gonna kill me?' 'Good boy.' Vivica in a sarcastic tone before throwing a snausage on Nathaniel Chambers' pee-soaked lap and exiting out the back door. Chambers began to take deep breaths of relief as he watched Vivica walk away in his rear view mirror. 'What an ass on that girl...' He said to himself before suddenly Vivica turned around and shot a hole through 3 of his tires. He felt the car sink to the ground and slammed his fist on the steering wheel. Opening the driver's side door Nathaniel looked back at Vivica halfway down the highway and almost out of sight by this point in time. 'Why in the hell did you do that?' He screamed into the night towards Vivica who seemed to disappear into the darkness. 'Fuck off.'
