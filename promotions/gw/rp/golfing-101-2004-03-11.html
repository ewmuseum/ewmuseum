Golfing 101

11 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-04 (Earlier on Wenesday afternoon the Nomad Donny J McNasty decided it was time for a round of Golf and he called up his childhood friend Tim, who used to live down the road from the McNasty Potato farm. McNasty is preety happy after his first win in Global Wrestling, and is finally starting to relax. Although he knows that him and Train aren't over yet, he knows that he can beat Train at his own game. Life for the Nomad is getting more complicated of late, with getting back together with his ex girlfriend Julia who he dated for a long time, but he knows there are two things that can make his mind clearer, the first being Alcohol which is a given, and the second being Golf) *McNasty is on the tee and is pulling out his driver, it's a par 5 that is about 525 yards to the pin with a dog leg left* McNasty: Tim as you know the Irish were one of the first people to play the game of golf, in fact my super duper great grandfather, Connor L McNasty was one of the best back in the 13th century. It's been written that he was able to knock the black plague out of the ball everytime he hit it... Tim: Black plague? McNasty: Yeah it was some nasty shit back then, it came from people not having toilets or something. Tim: Ewww McNasty: Yeah considering what some of my shits smell like. *McNasty lines up to take his shot and does the wiggly butt thing all golfers do, and he swings away, he hit the ball hard, in fact the ball is traveling straight for 200 yards and all of a sudden it takes a complete turn to the left, and the sound of a golf cart crashing and people screaming can be heard.* McNasty: As you can see I didn't inherit the golf genes in the family. Tim: Yeah really... McNasty: Tim, let me tell you something, there are reasons, why us men NEED to go golfing. Number one it could be used as a tax write off. In fact me and Harv have reciepts from here, in January of this year... Tim: Donny, there was snow on the ground in January how did you go golfing and how did you get a reciept for it? McNasty: SHHHHHHH, The IRS doesn't know that ok... Besides I think they been watching me and Harv after last years tax return. We grossed at the bar, 1.3million dollars ok? Well we ended up getting a refund for 2.1 million... Tim: How in the fuck did you do that? McNasty: If I told you I would have to kill you... Anywho, as I was saying, one of the other reasons why men NEED to go golfing is to get away from the women for a few hours. You're married you know what I mean... Tim: Yep sure do... McNasty: And the last reason is really simple, it gives an almost legal reason to get tanked... *McNasty pulls two Guinness's out of his golf bag and gives one to Tim and pops one open himself and they both begin to chug.* *McNasty finishes first and lets out a loud belch* *Tim puts his ball on a tee and picks up his driver and takes his shot, it's straight down the fairway the whole way.* Tim: Donny that's how you hit a golf ball... McNasty: Yeah yeah, fuck you and the horse you rode in on. Lets go look for my ball, and I'll write out a check for that golf cart while I'm at it... (The two begin to walk away, it was a long day on the golf course for these two, Tim finished -4 and McNasty finished +30, three dead birds, 5 golf carts, one person with whiplash and many other with grapefruit size welts from where McNasty hit them with his stray shot.) *Scene fades to black*
