Whips, cat o nine tails, raggedly sharp looking tools hang from the walls, while the room also sports things like the iron maiden and a rack. Upon the rack is bound a scared looking man. After a few more strokes, the scene cuts back to the journal, leaving Pendragon in the dungeon with the infidel? ***********FLASH*************** ~ONCE MORE, THY JOURNAL~ The fellow seemed to not garner any satisfaction nor gratification from that, and I found that odd, for I really whaled upon his prone body. The rumble of the engine joins the thunder of the great warhorse’s hoof beats in the distance. The engine stops, and Ben Davis hops out of the car. His ponytail blows in the soft breeze, and he wears a sleeveless black shirt with khaki shorts. Sandals hide his feet. He walks across the drive into grass the greenest imaginable and the shot changes to show Pendragon riding hard towards him, Xerxes at the full gallop. He pulls up just as he reaches “The Bastard” and Xerxes rears back on his hind legs. He settles and Pendragon smiles down at his friend? “Hile, Bastard! Ben smiles up and in a tone a bit more harsh than intended? “I hate it when you do that, Venn. You know horses make me nervous?” Pendragon frowns as if injured and Ben raises his hands to show he is only messing with the big fellow and the smile returns? “Hast thou thought upon mine problem, Benjamin” I am at mine wit’s end. The mustang is parked in this alley, and in the distance a neon sign reads “The Pearl”. Must be some kind of a nightspot, if we know “The Bastard” at all as of yet. The tuft of hair that sticks out of the bottom of the mask belies who the person it. But what the hell is Ben doing wearing a gimp suit? “Come on, Venn.
