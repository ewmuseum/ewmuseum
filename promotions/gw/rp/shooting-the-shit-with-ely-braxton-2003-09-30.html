Shooting the shit with Ely Braxton

30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/30/03 The mood in the Dark Horse saloon was at an all-time high. People were dancing, drinking, riding the mechanical bull, etc. Or rather one man was riding the mechanical bull. That man was Ely Braxton. It was a normal pastime for Ely. Lately he had been busy recruiting new talent for Global Wrestling, frequenting Then the door swung open and in walked Brick. The crowd cheered “HEEEY!!!” after he walked in. He looked around the bar and saw Braxton on the bull. “Typical,” Brick said to himself, before walking up to the bull. “Hey, Ely!” “Brick!” Ely called from the bull. “Be down in a minute!” Just then, the bull began to slow down. And “Gimme my usual.” “OK, that’s one Texas mudslide. And how about you?” “Let me have an Angus Bingham.” “One Texas mudslide and one cranberry juice coming right up.” “Heard you been busy doing some recruiting.” “Yep. This guy Caspian Tilleli that I’m bringin” in, I got a feeling he’s gonna make some dividends. You should’a been with me to see “im fight.” “He’s that good?” “And then some.” Then Brick’s demeanor became serious. “Listen, man, that’s not the only thing I wanted to talk to you about.” “What’s up?” “Well, I don’t know if you’ve heard, but there have been some rumors going around backstage about Christina.” Ely inhaled deeply upon hearing what Brick had to say. He didn’t care to hear anything negative about Christina. Ely loved the whole McCoy family in general, but Christina in particular. Once, he nearly strangled Sean Price for calling her trailer park trash. “You mean the stuff about her and that new kid Akiyara.” “You know about it?” “I may be a redneck, but I ain’t stupid. I hear things.” “Look, I don’t know if there’s any truth to it. I hope there isn’t any truth to them. I thought you should get a heads up, since we’re boys and all. Cause a lot of cats are gonna be lookin” to test you.” At that point, the bartender returned with Brick and Ely’s drinks. “OK, that’s one Texas mudslide and one cranberry juice.” “Thank you.” The bartender then left when another customer called for him. “Probably nothing to these rumors,” Ely shrugged. “So, she looks out for “im. He’s a young kid. She’s probably just lookin out for “im.” “Deacon’s the same age, and she don’t look out for him the same way. Of course, Deacon is a little piece of shit, so?” “Yeah,” Ely chuckled, “what was it you said about him that one night we were all here?” “Boy’s an ass whipping waiting to happen!” they exclaimed in unison. “And he is,” added Brick. “Boy doesn’t know it yet. Only reason he hasn’t gotten more ass whippings than he’s gotten is because of TUE and those damn bodyguards.” “Yeah, well, he’s gonna have to deal with you an” six other guys at Maximum Carnage.” “You mean me and four other guys. Zero’s in that match, and he and Deacon are boys. Plus that stunt him and Ice pulled last Evo.” “Know anything about this mystery man who’s in the match?” “No, not a damn thing.” “You think it’s Chris Camero, like all the rumors say?” “Wouldn’t surprise me one damn bit. I know I gotta be on my game this time. But I’ve beaten nine other cats in the same match, one of which was Robson, so?” “Just go in and kick ass.” “Same to you.” Then they toasted to their prospective victories at Maximum Carnage.
