Date: 06.17.04 'Sandstorm' blasted loudly throughout The Quest as lights flashed and flared, illuminating the sillhouettes of countless bodies moving their bodies in trance to the beat. Glowsticks were dancing through the foggy atmosphere of the club as they were guided by the hands of those captured by the music and reflected in the mirror that constituted the dance floor wall. In the midst of it all, in the center of the dance floor dressed in a pair of Hot Topic plaid bondage pants with the flare of the legs unzipped over a large black leather pair of platform bondage boots by the same company and in a black sleevless top with the word 'Hollywood' etched across in dull white block letters, is 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr. The crowd of bodies had gathered around, and were dancing for him. However, he paid no mind to them. He hadn't come there to be hounded, he had come there to put everything that was haunting him in the back of his mind. The fact that, once again, he was being forced to step in the ring with his best friend... that he was being torn by the fact that, as much as he didn't want to wrestle Josh, he wanted the World Title that was around his blood brother's waist. He was haunted by the current situations resting between himself and the love of his life, Gunner Addison... and whether or not she truly wanted to see him again after so long. All of this was plaguing him, and for one night, if only one night, he wanted to forget it. He wanted to concentrate on nothing more than the music pounding it's way into his soul andcausing him to follow his feet and body wherever they may have taken him. 'I can't believe this,' a voice next to him bellowed, 'I'm really dancing with KPJ. This is amazing.' Ken opened his eyes for them to reveal a stunning young Asian dressed in a pair of Mudd jeans that higged her hips and a black fishnet top with the black bra that envelopped her perfect breasts showing through. His eyes moved down to her amazing stomach and a smile actually etched across his face... he'd always loved a nice stomach... in fact, it was his favorite part of the anatomy. His eyes moved back to her face, where he realized that this girl was so young that it was almost illegal for him to even be talking to her. 'I'm Kat, by the way,' continued the girl. 'My friends call me Kitty Kat.' 'Really,' began Ken with the smile still on his face as he bore witness to a head of the most emaculate black hair that he'd ever paid vision to. 'That's interesting.' Kat's hand reached out and touched Ken, as did a few others that he brushed off before she moved closer and began to move her body against his in rhymatic session to the music. 'Kat,' beckoned Ken as she turned her back to him and began to rub it gently up and down his body, 'how old are you?' 'I'm eighteen,' she responded moving her back up Ken's chest, 'but a mature eighteen.' 'Do you know how old I am, Kat,' asked Ken over the music. Ken was attempting with all of his will to resist the fruits that were laid before him. 'The question is,' she began as she turned to face him and moved her lips very close to his, 'do I care?' Ken pushed her away and held her arms to her side as she responded with pouty scowl. 'I'm in my mid-twenties... and I do care,' answered Ken as he left the girl standing in disbelief, watching him walk off of the dance floor and towards the outside patio. He exits through the door as the bouncer gives him a slight nod. The new area isn't much better than the last. A universe of bodies moving to the sounds that have strummed outside and into the motions of those around him. He sat down in a chair close to the corner in an attempt to discard the looks and comments already traveling throughout the outside area regarding the celebrity of the current GW International Champion. He sat, leaning back in the sterling silver seat and gathering his thoughts. He could have had the girl on the dance floor... along with many others at the club, and a few years a go, he would have capitalized on that. However, he knew the reason that he hadn't was one simple name... Gunner Addison. He hadn't even spoken to her again yet, and he already felt like just talking to Kat was cheating on Gun. When the fuck did morality re-enter Ken's life?
