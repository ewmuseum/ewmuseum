Not Dead?

4 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 4th The scene opened inside of my house, inside my bedroom. Everything was neatlly placed in order, now that I have gotten all new stuff because of the robbery's that had happened in my house. There was about 3 or 4 of them that had happened, and to tell you , I wasn't shocked to find out that it had been broken into. It was stupid, why just my hgouse, does somebody, or people have something against me. I don't do bad, I'm a Christian Beast, except for the Beast part in realy life. But when I'm wrestling, I give it my best, and try so hard to get the win over whoever I'm facing in the match. I sat there, on the chair that was in the corner of the room. No lights on or nothing, but there was some lights on, candles. There were candles surrounding me, as I sat on the chair. Some were on the dresser, others on the floor, and even some on my bed, but they wouldn't fall, never. If they did, they would burn everything that I had from when I was born till now. And if that burned, I'd burn with it. I would burn with my life, and no one could stop me. I had my eyes closed, I was praying to god, I was praying for my mother, and father, who had both died at a very young age, but it is not my fault or anybody's fault. God, simply needed my mother and father in heaven for something, so took them up there, so they could be angels, and they could protect me from anything. 'Please pray for my mother and father, so that they can feel good in heavrn, and so that they don't cry when they see me down here. I want them to feel so special, you can't even feel it, and right now in my heart, I can feel there hearts thumping with mine, going at the same beat. Sometimes, at night, I just listen and here them talking in my heart and telling me that they love me, and they want to be with me, and I always tell them, I'll be there soon. And one more thing to pray for, Juri Hyobanshi's soul, I am against him at Evolution, and I will show him why they call me The Christian Beast. I will kick his ass, so I ask for you to pray for his soul, cause he should be up there after I'm done with him on Evolution.' I said aloud. I wobbled on the chair I was sitting on a bit. I had almost fell over, but something had helped me get back up. I opened my eyes, I had totally forgotten about the candles, and I totally forgot about that I had the candles set up there. I must've have been to busy praying for my mother and father, I had missed them so much. I was also praying for Juri Hyobanshi's soul, because on Evolution, I will beat him, but luckly, if something comes up and I lose the match, it wouldn't matter, because it is not for the Cruiserweight title, so I wouldn't lose anything to Juri Hyobanshi. That was a good thing, because I am goin to keep my title for as long as I can, and I'll try not to lose it the next day just like the Primestar title, I won it and then lost it the next day. Wouldn't that suck? The door to open my bedroom, I had locked it, so no one would interupt my conversation with God. Suddenly, there was a knock at my bedroom door, I did not know who is was, and I didn't know how they could get into my house, because I locked it. Why would someone come right now, its like 1:00 at night. Everyone should be sleeping, well everyone except me, I have a priviledge. I got off of the chair, and walked over to the door. I slowly put my hand on the knob, and turned it slightly, to make it open. Standing there, was my mother! She was dead, how could she be here? I was confused. I smiled at her as she walked in, it looked like she was perfect, and nothing had happened to her. She walked over to my bed and sat down. I was dumbfounded. I walked over to the chair and sat down right beside the bed. 'How are you here?' I asked, really dumbfounded. 'I am an angel, don't I have a right to be here?' she asked me. I was totally confused, as to why she was here. Yes I understood she was an angel, but why did she come down and visit me, or maybe she just wanted to say hi to me because she hasn't seen me in so long. Butshe looked totally normal, no bruises or scratches. or anything. and she wasn't white like a real angel, she was just there, totally normal. Of course, right now I was not normal, as I was wondering as to why she was here. I haven't seen her in so long, I almost started crying in front of her, right now. When I looked into to her eyes, it looked like she was going to cry too, I think I had just saw a tear fall from her cheek. Wait, that was just mine. I wiped it away and smiled at her. She was wearing a dress, it was the dress I gave her on Mother's Day. She wore it mostly every day since I gave it to her, and she never seemed to get rid of it. It was a light blue with some kind of design on it. I couldn't really make it out because she was sitting on tne bed. She was wearing high heels, and had her hair in a ponytail. 'Why did you come?' I asked silently. 'I needed to see how you were doing. It has been oh so long since I've seen you last. The last thing I remember was looking straight into your eyes, and smiling, and then my life started passing through my head, and then it was black. Couldn't see anything. I went to heaven, and let me tell you, its an awesome place to be. No house work, and no doing chores, you could pretty much do whatever you wanted up there. And besides, everyone has to die in life, and I guess God wanted me up there, so that's why he called me up there. Except, I've only seen God once, he's always been so busy with people and all.' she said. The room was still dark and gloomy. The candles were the only light in the entire room, which only gave off a little light. My mother didn't seem to care though, she just sat there and stared at me. I figeted with my hands a bit and then saw that one of the candles had blown out. It had melted down. I didn't care about it, for two reasons, one, is because I had about 20 other candles, and if just one went out, it wouldn't matter. And two, is because my mother was here, and I wouldn't ditch her to fix a stupid candle. We kept chatting as the scene started to fade.
