The Quest to find the Key of Solo Success #2 (MOTM#2)

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 22nd 2004 Warsaw Wrestling Academy Wednesday 21st July 2004 Igor Arefiev appears at the grand gates that lead into the academy. He is wearing brown hiking boots, green combat trousers and a green t-shirt. He is reading a small map. As he reads the map, Mr. Salem voice echoes through his head. “The key to solo success is a quest not even I have been able to succeed on. You should know that now. No one in the last two hundred years has discovered the key and its reasoning?” Igor steps off walking down the road that runs past the school. He is soon met by a troop of circus students out running. They are quickly past and Igor continues walking. “For your quest, if you so wish to take this on, you are required to bring no supplies and wear only the clothes you are wearing when you leave the gates here?” The Flying Vergomov is reading the map as he walks. “Right” I have to cross a gate somewhere along here and climb that mountain. After that, the map stops?” “For the key on a quest you be?” Igor is stopped by an unknown voice in his presence. Arefiev looks down in the ditch to his left. “YODA”!” The small green Jedi master stares back at the circus performer. He blinks and tilts his head to the right. “Mmm”!” exclaims Yoda. “What are you doing here?” enquiries the startled Arefiev. “On the path lead you I must?” Yoda “stands” up and waddles past. “With you is strong the force?” Igor seems confused and follows Yoda. Mr. Salem’s voice echoes through his head. “On the path you will meet your guide in the form of someone you greatly admire. Trust them to lead you where you want to go.” Igor and Yoda walk in silence, the Flying Vergomov taking in his surroundings. “So where are going?” Yoda doesn’t respond and continues to lead the way. Igor shrugs. -------------------- “Your test this is first?” exclaims Yoda as the pair of mismatched travellers reach the top of a hill on the road. Igor looks around him. He sees nothing but roads, hills and grass. “Where is my test?” asks Igor. He looks down to his left, and Yoda is gone. He turns around. No sign of Yoda. “YODA”!” There is silence as Igor scans his immediate surroundings in search of the Jedi master. “During your quest for the key, you will be set five challenges by people or things that you have the utmost respect for or fear of. These challenges will thrive on your fear and try to expose your weaknesses, and toy with your mind. You must be ready to face everything thrown at you.” “I am afraid the Jedi master is not around, young adventurer.” A new squeaky voice is heard behind Igor. He turns around to be met by a dwarf standing in the last position that Yoda was in. Igor takes a startled step back. The dwarf is wearing a red hat, a green jumper and brown string-tied trousers. “Be not afraid, young adventurer. I am test-master number one.” “Your one of the seven dwarfs!” exclaims Igor stepping back once more. The dwarf nods. “I’m Sleepy.” Igor is silent as he looks at the dwarf and then looks around the dwarf. “Where are the rest of the dwarfs?” There is silence. Igor’s question lingers in the suddenly frosty air. Igor gets a chill up his spine. Arefiev looks down at his test-master. Sleepy is? yup, you guessed it? asleep. “Sleepy! Wake up!” The dwarf opens his eyes. “Sorry. I just feel really tired.” Igor smirks. There is silence between the pair. Arefiev looks uncomfortable. “So?” “What?” snaps Sleepy. “My test?” questions Igor. “Oh yeah,” remarks Sleepy, “take a look around you.” Igor turns his head and looks around him. Expecting to see the road hills and grass, he is shocked to find himself standing in a forest with the dwarf. “Welcome to test number one.” Igor continues to look at the area. “Beside you is the start point.” Igor Arefiev look to his right and is met by a red flag beside a balance beam. “You have to follow this balance beam path and without falling off reach the green flag.” Igor nods and begins to sweat. “These challenges will thrive on your fear and try to expose your weaknesses?” Igor swallows hard. His mind begins to replay previous episodes in his life. “These challenges will thrive on your fear and try to expose your weaknesses?” “You may begin when you are ready,” finishes the dwarf, “I will see you at the finish line, but remember the forest is watching so don’t cheat.” With an unpleasant looking smile, the dwarf disappears into a bush and Igor is alone. Igor wipes his forehead with the back of his hand and then stands onto the balance beam. The beam rocks as Igor tests his weight. Igor nearly falls off immediately. “CAREFUL!” calls out the dwarf from some unknown location. Igor smiles sarcastically and continues on the beam. The wind howls past, a branch striking Igor in the chest as it flies by. Igor lets out an audible groan. “You didn’t expect it to be easy did you?” laughs the dwarf. Igor does not acknowledge the comment and continues to walk along the beam in silence. “Come on! You should be finished by now. I thought you were a professional tightrope walker.” Igor is silent as the dwarf talks. “Oh yeah. You don’t tightrope walk anymore do you Igor?” Igor shakes his head. “No you don’t. You’re a wrestler, a tag team wrestler. You really can’t do anything by yourself can you” You have to bring someone else down with you don’t you?” Igor shakes his head. “You can’t do anything by yourself! You have to bring someone else down with you!” Arefiev continues along the beam. His face becoming more and more stressed. “You can’t do anything by yourself! You have to bring someone else down with you!” Igor shakes his head. “You can’t do anything by yourself! You have to bring someone else down with you!” The taunts from the dwarf continue. Igor repeatedly shakes his head as he tries to compose himself. “You can’t do anything by yourself! You have to bring someone else down with you!” “IT WASN’T LIKE THAT! IT WASN’T MY FAULT! NO ONE BLAMES ME! NO BODY!” Igor screams into the open air, and breaks down on the beam in tears. “Dude” That was rough.” Another voice joins the dwarf in the open air. The voice is slightly deeper. “I know,” replies Sleepy. “You were only supposed to rough him up. We can make him cry later,” continues the unknown voice. “I’m sorry.” “You should be?” There is silence. Igor did not hear the conversation as he was crying. “Igor!” exclaims the unknown person. “Igor!” Igor looks up from his hands to the surrounding forest. “Igor, you may step down from the beam. You have passed test one.” Igor looks around. He is silent. The silence extends. Then Igor steps? along the beam. “Sleepy! What’s he doing?” “He’s going to pass the first test.” “No one supposed to pass the first test!” Igor continues to walk the beam course. It is light work to a former professional tightrope walker. He reaches the green flag, touches it and jumps down. As he reaches the ground he is met by an applauding Sleepy. “Bravo!” Igor smiles and nods. He touches the ground and lands on the tar of the road. Yoda is standing in front of Igor. “Young adventurer done well!” exclaims Yoda. Igor is back on the road in Warsaw. “Get going we must,” exclaims Yoda. He cracks his back and turns away from Arefiev and starts walking. Igor follows behind all the while his head is replaying something in his eyes. -------------------- The pair reach another hill top on the surprising very straight road. “Yoda?” “Yes, adventurer young?” “Is it me or is this road really straight and the fields all the same?” Yoda groans and looks away from the Flying Vergomov. Igor doesn’t enquiry any further. Suddenly it starts to rain. Igor pulls his jacket over his head. He then stops and looks down at his clothes. “I wasn’t wearing a jacket when I left the Academy, was I Yoda?” Igor looks up but Yoda is not there. In his place stands Jasmine from Aladdin, in a sky blue boob-tube and sky blue trousers. “I am afraid the Jedi master is not around, young adventurer,” smiles the Disney vixen. Igor stands in his jacket as the rain pours down. There is a silence between the pair. The silence extends. “These challenges will thrive on your fear and try to expose your weaknesses?” The rain continues to bucket down. Jasmine is getting evermore wetter. Igor’s eye are drawn a single rain drop as it rolls down he chest and in between her breasts. Jasmine coughs. Igor looks up at her face, and receives a telling smile from Jasmine. “These challenges make take some strange forms and may even require you to take the first step?” The rain continues. “These challenges will thrive on your fear and try to expose your weaknesses?” Immediately, as if the penny dropped, Igor removes his large coat and wraps it around Jasmine who was previously five feet in front of Igor. The rain immediately stops. A large round of applause is heard around Igor. “You took your time,” echoes the sweet voice of Jasmine, “but you got there.” Igor looks up at the sky as the rain immediately stops. The coat drops to the ground and a small hump appears under it. Igor looks at the hump and lightly kicks it. “Off fuck!” curses Yoda. “I apologise, Yoda.” “Lucky young adventurer are you. Can of whoop-ass to open was I ready.” Yoda turns on his heel and marches off from the Flying Vergomov. “Jackass fucking,” mumbles Yoda as he storms off. Igor sighs. -------------------- The pair walk in silence for a solid hour along the never ending straight road. As they reach the top of another rising, Yoda stops and turns to face Arefiev. Igor collapses onto the grass verge at the side of the road, and moans loudly. “Let me guess Yoda, another-? “I am afraid the Jedi master is not around, young adventurer.” Igor looks up to greeted by a tall dark figure standing in front of a fire. The being’s voice is deep and rough. Igor sits up as he finds himself sitting around a camp fire, except the only two scouts at this special fire are Igor and the creature being silhouetted by the fire. The sky has got dark very quickly. “Who are you?” The person moves closer to Arefiev, and moves their face close to him. It is the Beast from Beauty and the Beast. Igor lets out an audible shudder. “Trust me, you aren’t the prettiest either,” jokes the tall Beast. Igor smiles sarcastically. He attempts to dry his still wet clothes by moving close to the fire. “These challenges will thrive on your fear and try to expose your weaknesses?” “Test number three, Mr. Arefiev.” Igor looks up from the fire over towards the beast. “Somewhere that direction?” The Beast motions out towards the trees beside the fire. “There is a small silver ball, about the size of your hand” Go find it!” The Beast sits down on a log, and watches Igor. Arefiev stands to his feet and looks out into the dark. “These challenges will thrive on your fear and try to expose your weaknesses?” He cautiously looks out into the dark forest. “I can do this?” he mutters to himself. The Beast smiles and groans with delight. Igor steps forward and then stops. He peers into the dark. A twig breaks under his foot. Igor jumps out of his skin. The Beast lets out an audible smirk. Igor steps forward again. He laves the camp fire clearing, and trips over a stone. He stands still the dark forest. There is silence around Igor. His heavy breathing is the only noise he can hear. Thirty seconds later, the Beast hears footsteps. “Back so soon?” “Nope.” Igor paces into the clearing holding a log. He runs to the fire and shoves the stick into the flames. The wood suddenly ignites. Igor turns and runs out into the woods. Almost immediately, Igor returns holding the burning wood in one hand and a silver ball. “Somewhere out there” I tripped over it the first time I went out there.” “Adventurer young done well.” Yoda walks out from behind the fire. The Beast is gone. “Tests three of which completed by you, but two more pass you must and key hold you will.” Yoda turns on his heel and steps away from the fire. Igor Arefiev is once again on the seemingly never ending straight road. “Seriously. Can Polish people not make turns?”
