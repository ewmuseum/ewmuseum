The little things piss you off. The big ones make you snap.

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.15.03 June 15, 2003, The Adolphus Hotel Who is Sean Price’s partner on Evolution? Who will stand beside TUE’s own against the Xodus fools? Will there be a fourth member added to the ranks of TUE? and if so, who will it be? Il Capo stated himself that it was someone already signed with Global Wrestling. But who? Salomane Vation? No. He’s not The Upper Echelon’s style. Perhaps someone is betraying Xodus from the inside? You can never underestimate the power of a man with connections. There’s no way of knowing who it may or may not be. There’s no way to tell with all of the new signers in Global Wrestling. With the size of GW’s unaligned roster, there’s no real way of finding out before Evolution. The scene fades from black. Black leather couches and chairs line the walls of the large room, tables along the center and a marble counter near the entrance. A bartender in a black vest and white shirt stands behind it, tending bar. Beautiful women highlight the room dressed in semi-formal attire. One woman we recognize; Ember sits at the bar with a strawberry daiquiri in hand. Her beautiful brown eyes move over the room, watching the other people. She glances at her watch and sighs. She’s obviously waiting for someone? but whom? You know who. She’s waiting on a member of the Elite, Tyler Pierce. The music is new and keeps the room jumping with people although, this is obviously an upper class VIP room located in a local Dallas club. She’s dressed in a strapless red dress that fits like a glove to her luscious body. Her perfect complexion and sultry body have caught the eyes of quite a few of the men in the room although; none of them are catching hers in return. One tries as we watch, he approaches her with a slight grin on his face. She glances to her side at him and places her glass on the bar where she sits. He walks up and leans over the bar, nearly brushing her with his arms and sides as he speaks to the bartender. “Another strawberry daiquiri for the lady.” The man has a tone of assurance about him although, you have to wonder why. He’s not exactly a handsome man? he’s more like a Tzimisce(***) prank than he is a clich? “good looker.” “That’s er, that’s ok, bud. I’m waiting on someone and this one is going to last me awhile.” Ember speaks with a soft smile and tone, trying to be polite to the gentleman. He glances at her, his breath reeking of the scent of vodka. “You don’t understand. I’m buying you a drink.” The man reaches into his pocket and tries to sit at the same time on the barstool aside her. He pulls a dollar out and slams it onto the bar. “BARTENDER! Did you hear me!”” The man has begun to cause a scene in the lounge, embarrassing Ember. She turns away, her cheeks turning a beet red. A few other patrons turn to look but then return to their own affairs. “So what’s your name baby?” Ember attempts to ignore him, her eyes turning towards the glass she holds in her hands. The man grabs the bar as he gets a little dizzy, the alcohol in his system putting the part of his brain that gives a human being inhibitions. He reaches forward and places a hand on her knee; “My name’s Joe, baby. What’s yours?” He smiles at her; his breathe sending a sense of disgust through her entire body. She scoots down a stool and faces away, hoping he’s not so drunk that he ignores her overly obvious hint. The bartender sighs as Joe begins to take the stool next to her, he lightly touches his shoulder. “Look, bub. Maybe you should leave the girl alone, eh?” The Canadian accent of the bartender was nothing more than a clich? reminder that we’re no where near Canada. How that reminded us of that, I’ll never know. The man jerks away, knocking the stool over and causing a loud clanging noise inside of the lounge. He staggers back. “YOU DON’T TELL ME WHAT TO DO!” the drunken man belches out, catching the attention of everyone regardless of whether they wanted to notice or not. A new member has been added to the list of patrons however; one Tyler Pierce. He stands at the entrance to the lounge, his eyes dead set on the drunken slob causing trouble for Ember. “You know, it’s the little things people do to piss you off that you can look over.” Tyler speaks aloud to the room as he walks through the small crowd of people. Ember begins to approach him and the man known as Joe grabs her arm, jerking her back to his side. Tyler stops and gazes at the man? the left side of his face twitching. “Where the hell do you think you’re going?” The drunken patron questions Ember as she barely catches herself from hitting the floor. Tyler glances at the tears forming in her eyes and then at the man with a grip on her arm. “But it’s the big things that make you?” without finishing the sentence, a final twitch sends Tyler forward with a single thrust kick that knocks the man back onto the bar and flipping over it! His feet smash into the glass bottles behind the bar as he lands in a crumpled mass on the floor at the bartender’s feet, “snap.” There’s a moment of stunned silence from the patrons before a sudden applause. Ember rushes forward and throws her arms around Tyler as he stands there, the twitch lessening but still there. She hugs him tightly and he returns the gesture, his eyes focusing on the man as he slowly pulls himself up from behind the bar. Tyler suddenly lets go of Ember, stepping towards the bar and grabbing a bottle of 20 year old cognac. He lifts it above his head and the bottle is grabbed by the hand of another man. Tyler glances behind him, seeing the face of Deacon Holmes. “No, Ty. Let it go” ? Tyler glances at him for a moment, his eyes full of rage after witnessing how this man treated Ember? two TUE bodyguards grab the man and escort him out the front door. Tyler releases the bottle and takes Ember’s hand in his? “Let’s get out of here.” He tosses a wad of bills onto the counter and nods to the tender, “Sorry about the mess” that should cover it.” The tender smiles and nods. “Anytime. Thanks for the show.” Tyler smirks and slips an arm around Ember’s waist and leads her towards and out the door. Deacon picks up the bottle of cognac and winks at the tender. “Should be enough in what he gave ya. Catch ya later.” Deacon grins to the tender and walks out a moment later. Outside the club, the city lights of Dallas, Texas, illuminate the night. A black limousine is parked at the curb, one guard stands at the backdoor to it dressed in a black suit with earpiece. The drunken man has been lain out on the sidewalk, his nose busted. The two bodyguards quickly climb into the custom Escalade parked behind the limo. Deacon climbs into the back of the limo first, a bottle of cognac in hand. He’s dressed in a pair of black suit pants, dress shoes, a white dress shirt completely untucked and a tie, extremely loose about his neck. Tyler lets Ember into the backseat before slipping in beside her; he’s adorned in a black suit with a royal purple dress shirt and white tie. Once they’re inside, the bodyguard closes the door and gets inside the passenger door. The limo pulls away from the curbing and the scene fades. Within the second, the scene fades up showing us the back of the limousine, Deacon Holmes on one side, pouring a glass of cognac for himself and Ember and Tyler on the other. “So where’s Sean?” the sweetest voice possible speaks up. “He said he’d catch up with us later” that he had something to take care back home.” Deacon shrugs a bit, “pretty vague if you ask me” but ya didn’t ask me.” Tyler snickers a bit and knocks on the driver’s privacy window; the window slowly lowers and he glances back at the two for a spit second as he drives. “Where to, Mr. Pierce?” Tyler glances at Deacon and then back, tilting his head back as he speaks. “Abacus” I’ve heard that’s fairly decent. Sound ok with you guys?” Deacon shrugs and Ember is silent. “Fine with me.” Deacon utters as he throws back a glass of cognac. The driver nods and speaks, “Abacus it is.” The privacy pane goes up and the scene fades to black. The scene remains black: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- It’s the little things people do that piss you off. Those little things that build up? one by one, one by ten. The little things that can drive you crazy about a person? Think about it. Fights are always about something silly; “He got the wrong thing at the supermarket.” I’ve actually heard that one before. That’s not why they’re angry, however. It’s just built up and that’s how we, as immature and tactless humans release our frustration. By arguing? fighting. Tonight I had just cause to do what I did. This man had insulted my girl. He hurt her and embarrassed her in a public place? all because he was drunk. Completely inebriated. I can’t say that’s wrong? I’ve been in that position a million times. But not once have I accosted a women I didn’t know? hurt her? and caused a scene like the man did tonight. I may have taken it a step too far? but by the mere responses of the patrons in the bar, you must admit that my actions were warranted. Xodus stands back, talking about their team and how they’d rather have no one behind them than who they have. They talk about how they were going to take out Nevermore? but we got in there way. Hah! Nevermore would have wiped the floor with them? completely and totally demolished them! The only thing we’re doing is saving them from losing at their initial goal. From getting beaten by the top dog. These fools want to stand back, talking about how they’re going to take us down. Step up, boys. Step up. On Evolution you’re going to find out exactly what the deal is! You can act big and bad, tough and rough, all you fuckin? want. But when the door closes and the music is off, you’re the ones who’ll be in the ring with the best of the best; the Elite. Venom and Dynamite, you two will be standing in the ring with Sean Price and another man. Yeah, I know who that other man is. No, I’m not telling you. You get to be just as surprised as the fans watching the show. You two are going to wish you never set foot in Global Wrestling when they get done with you tomorrow. And Roz Blayze. What a fucking prick. You know that thing about the little things pissing you off? Well, I’m pissed off. I got shitted out of my match at Dead Zone? and you got the title you filthy little worm. What you have around your waist belongs to ME! Now you go around, talking about Xodus this and Xodus that. Fuck Xodus. You guys don’t stand a chance in the ring against the true men. The Upper Echelon? You’re nothing to us. Nothing but an obstacle Mr. Salaok threw in our way. When this is over, you’re going to be the most humiliated group of people after Evolution. Not only will Price and partner have walked over Dynamite and Venom. But, I will have walked into the ring with Roz Blayze, walked right over him, and then right out carrying that Cruiser Weight Championship over my shoulder. The pain I’m going to cause you will be warranted? because Xodus is a fly on the wall; an annoying gnat that keeps getting in our way. You’ve pissed me off enough, Roz. Now it’s time to pay the piper? The Piper Pierce. (*** Tzimisce ? In White Wolf’s Vampire: The Masquerade, a clan of vampires with the ability to mold flesh and bone. A Tzimisce prank would be someone they molded and changed to look monstrous.)
