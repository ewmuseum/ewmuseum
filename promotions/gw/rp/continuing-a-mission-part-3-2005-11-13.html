Date: 11-13-2005 5 Day’s ago? It was the night before Christmas and all through the house, nothing was moving, not even a mouse. That was what I use to hear when I was a kid. The words of a mother and father, reading to me in my very small bed. The days of innocent dreams, toy playing, and all other things that a little girl would do. But those days, those wonderful days are like the winter season. Gone. Now things were different. I was different. Not by choice either. I was different by a force that was not of my own but of someone else. Something else. Something much stronger then anything of this world. As crazy as that sounded, it was true. And I was getting weaker and weaker by the days that passed by. Sometimes, sometimes late at night, screaming at the top of my lungs for help but the demon that controls me, the soulless and heartless demon that lurks inside of me, taking control of my either movement, wouldn’t let anyone hear. Walking through the skinless night. The fresh air that was lurking by my body, startling my skin to tense up just a little bit. It wasn’t the cold that was making the demon inside of me tremble. No, it was the demon that was trembling because I was slowly starting to see the light that he was shining for me. My eyes were looking around at the surroundings that I was incurring. The shaky tree limbs that were processed by the green leaves, the dirt that was flying at my feet, covering my foot prints behind me. Nothing new except for the fearful feeling of the unknown that I was getting. Traveling down the dirty road towards a place that I hadn’t been to in years. A place that I was very familiar to but very distant from. It was my grandfathers house. A banded with the death of him. Forgotten from everyone’s head but one’s? Standing in front of the torn up house, looking at the holes, the chipped paint that had fell from the holding of the sighting that was once put on. It was as if a tornado came through and took every bit of life from this place and began pouring death to it’s soul. “Home?”, Saying before walking towards the front down. It was so hard to even imagine how it once was. Pleasant, lights lifting the image of the place. Everything that a godly person, a family that was loved by the world to be taken away. It was a horror to see but maybe it wasn’t a bad thing at all. As I got closer to the door I couple feel a sudden presence in the air. Someone that knew me. Someone from my past. “Hello Elia.” Turning to the other side that I was looking. Seeing the dark cat like eyes that were glowing in behind the tree’s that came towards me. Blue speckles from the painfully stair that she was giving me. My heart began to race through at the thought of how she was her. I was far from home. No one knew I was here. How did she know. “Hello mother. What are you doing here?” “Looking for you my dear.” Walking out to revile the dark color clothing that she had on. She was different. Not that it was a bad thing but she was different from the last time I saw her. Dressing in a way I hadn’t seen her dress before. Walking towards her, feeling the cold breeze still as it began to rush up the back of my body. “And why would you be looking for me mother” Have I done something that you feel is “To different.””” Coming within a few feet of her dark and breezy breath of words came rushing out of her mouth. “Don’t make me laugh my darling. You have never been that funny.” Laughing for the first time in this little encounter. “Then save me of your tries to make me more “Human” mother. I don’t need to do what I did again do I?” Making her remember the beating that I gave my Uncle Johnson and her just a few months ago. I could see the fear in her eyes, the pressure of her self realistic chances of me going smooth with her breaking. Backing up a little to make some room between us, “Your threats are not needed Elia. I’m not hear like I’ve been. I’m here because you grandmother has just died and we are getting ready to tear down the house tomorrow.” Died? The second mother I’ve always known. Gone from this world like a piece of trash that has been finally tossed away. I could feel a single wet tear coming from my darken heart. Trying to fall down the smooth face that I was showing now. But all those memories. All those no selfish feeling that I had always held deep in my heart. I could care less now. “I guess now she can stop being so weak.” Turning away from the awful sight of my mother. “Weak?” Screaming at me as if I was someone who was hurting her. But little did she know, I wasn’t even close to hurting her’YET. “That’s the person that helped you out when you were little several times. Now I might not be in control of you now a days but you will respect the one person in this family that loved you.” “Respect?” Turning back towards her with nothing but hate in my mind. “I’ll respect the one person that has helped me see the light in the tunnel. And you will respect him or else?” “Or else what Elia. Are you going to beat me and beat me till I’m dead to?” Pressing the silk leather outfit that was slung over my breast and body. Everything else, all the breezy cold air, all the noise that was coming from the birds in the tree’s, all that was shut off. My head began to turn, twisting like a doll to the head and eyes of the grand ole mother that I had. “I’m not going to kill you mother because to me’you are already dead.” Saying those words to her before beginning to walk away. “Your right Elia, I am dead. Dead because the daughter that I had has been over taken by someone I wish was dead.” My mother said as I was walking away. Stopping, like someone was shooting at me or like a car was coming my way, feeling the words as they were said. Everything around me began to slow down to a point were I was seeing or hearing on my mother. Lowering my head before responding. “Your right mother. I have been over taken. But over taken because of a world that needs the dark that I possess.” Continuing to walk towards the house and the darkness that was blowing in. ******************************************************** 1 Day before Evolution? The dark night was over taking me eyes. Yet I was just able to see what was standing in front of me. The blood stained hands of Jake Hix walking out of that house and towards the shed. 'Does it look good on you?' Asking with the darkness of the night, tilting my eyes just a little for Jake Hix to see. Jake turned, knowing well who it was who haunted him like some dark vision from the darkness. He took a threatening step her way, 'I have blood enough on me tonight, but perhaps sending you back to Hell would be another duty to perform.' 'Threats...', those were not my reasoning to be here. Emerging from the pain free darkness were dreams are taken and blood twisted to another color. 'I didn't come here Mr. Hix to fight. Your threats are not needed because we are now on the same side.' Fully stepping out into the light that was shining right me, shining down on to the dark hair that I was wearing. 'The very same darkness that I breath you have come to know tonight. So I ask, does it feel good.' 'Same side? No matter how twisted the world gets, take a loony demon freak and see how much more twisted it becomes.' Jake shook his head, circling the thing wrapped in flesh. 'I'm on God's side, and this is work he would approve of, I know. The man tried to kill my family, and in doing so he would kill the world. I kill him for the sin he knows, and the sin he never knew. Greed is a pale excuse. Now, unless you want to be burned you should get the fuck out of dodge.' 'God?' Hearing what everyone would think was the most powerful none flesh man that was ever said. 'You think that your killing, your urge for death for one man is because of your holy connection with God?' Stepping closer towards the confused and now angered Mr. Hix. 'Darkness and light are not the same Mr. Hix. You were meant to be one with the darkness. Just like that man was meant to die.' 'There's a time for everything under his heavens. A time for everything Elia.' Jake walked over to the old shed. He kicked the door in and looked around. There it was, just like any place in America, the usual red plastic container for gas. he grabbed it and walked back out, 'Even a time to kill Elia.' 'A time to kill...', lowering the eyes that I was gassing on him. 'A time to kill for him or a time to kill for you is the question. Though even I know that your so-called 'God' wouldn't like you taking his job. ' 'That's a discussion between me and him. I don't see your place in this demon.' Jake walked back into the house, trailing gasoline behind him as he went. He dropped the container on the kitchen and then walked back out to where Elia was waiting on him. 'The difference is you kill for fun or something darker, I do it for something better.' 'Better?' Still standing in the snow covered darkness that the midnight sky was falling down. My heart, sitting at a steady beat. 'Fun...something better...Mr. Hix, why pass by the true nature of what you have become? Why don't you embrace the gifts that you possess and become one with the dark side that is in you.' 'Well, if it isn’t the modern incarnation of fucking Emperor Palpatine. Try to find some new material. You and I aren't the same, we aren't friends, and we certainly don't do things for the same reasons.' Jake pulled a lighter out from his pocket. He kneeled down and lit the gasoline trail, 'No matter how indestructible you think you are, a cell will rot the rest of you. I find you following me again, and I'll send you back to the darkness you came from.' Squeezing my eyes shut a little to press out the light from the fire away from my eyes. The heat, blazing through the outer layer of my skin as I stood there. 'Foolish words from a foolish man. You tell yourself you are doing gods work yet you don't open your own eyes to see what's really there. Darkness. Destruction. I know this because he has told me...' 'I'll let Him judge me, not you. Break a few eggs, burn a few down and so on. I never go after the innocent, only those who have joined the darkness to tear down the heavens.' Shaking my head. 'Have you not hear the voices. Have you not felt the true meaning of what you are meant to do? Even the one voice that I hear, the one name that rings to me...' Pausing as the heat from the torched house became a pain in the side of my face. '...finally gave into the darkness and so will you Mr. Hix. In due time...' Jake backed away from her. 'I'll see you soon enough, of that I'm sure.' Watching his eyes backing away, following his every step. 'Soon enough even you will understand...', pausing for a moment before stepping back into the deep smoky filled darkness that was coming up. 'Your true calling, your true destiny, just like hers was.'
