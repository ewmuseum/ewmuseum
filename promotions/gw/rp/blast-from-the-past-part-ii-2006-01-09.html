Blast from the Past (Part II)

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/09/06 Prelude: The past week has been a tough on for Shane, in fact the last TWO weeks have made the star Fatigued. Not only has Shane had the heavy thoughts in his mind as to who was behind framing him, but also mentally and physically preparing himself for one of the biggest matches of our time, against Stormbringer at EW Legends. But still anothe obstacle stands in his way, that being Dexter Thomas. Thomas will be included in the triple threat when Wethers meets Stormbringer this Friday at Legends and bookies already believe Thomas will be tough competition for the Eternal Punk, who is currently EW's #1 contendor for the US Title. With that in mind Shane has been on a mission to find out some answer and in the last epic we saw him question Thomas, things got heated between the two stars and there were no prizes for guessing just how much tension there was between them. Another week has passed and still no answers to take him further up the ladder of conclusion... what will this week have in store for him? Interlude: In todays Epic Shane continues his work as an un-official EW detective, trying to solve the crime that has been rotting in his mind non-stop. Today Shane interviews another culprit but will it come to any avail? or will we have to wait until Mecca? Read on and find out! _____________________________________________________ Date: Thursday 26/09/02 Time: 07.12am Location: New York *Its an early Wednesday morning and the sun is slowly rising upwards in horizon. The streets of New York are busy with people rushing to work or trying to make the early clearance sales in the high street shops, rushing like headless chickens. The smell of exhaust fumes is highly distinct as the road fills up with cars, queuing all the way down the long winding road. The loud mixing of peoples voices and the harsh sounds of car horns are enough to send your mind in a spin, but even this early in a morning New York feels like the place to be. Shane Wether sit in the cafeteria of a large hotel, gazing out of the window. He is wearing black baggy jeans with a chain hanging from the pocket, a black T-shirt with the words “Fear Inc” on the front in blood red writing. He is also wearing grey and aluminous yellow trainer and his hair is in the usual spikey yet messy fashionable Shane Wethers style. Shane holds a large warm mug of coffee in his hand as his weary, sleepy eyes stare at the busy roads outside. Shane yawns and scratches his head, as if pondering some troublesome question that has been weighing on his mind. Shane picks up the mug of coffee again and finishes it off. He then stands up leaving a $20 bill on the table for his breakfast that he enjoyed earlier. He walks out of the cafeteria and towards the exit where there is a guard on the door who greets him and smiles in a friendly way, even though it would take a lot more than a smile to comfort Shane after the hectic few weeks he has endorsed. Shane walks down the pathway and down the busy, noisy streets. He wanders on thinking to himself* {Another day, yet another challenge. No closer to my target of finding out who is behind this set-up that unwisely put me behind bars, the list is as long as I started with.. wasn’t much use talking to that jack-ass “Die Hard” yesterday, but what more could I expect from someone with such small intelligence? Then we’ve got dark justice thinking he can walk into my world and start talking trash like the pickledick he is. I’m already visualising the duct tape over his mouth. That guy is definitely depriving a village of an idiot. I’m involved in a investigating mission and he’s at circuses humping clowns like a the sick freak he is. Although, him being at a circus must make him feel at home. I’m glad Sean has been released from jail, I can’t believe those @sshole’s kept him locked up just because they thought he had some involvement with the mafia since he was seen talking with me. Maybe me staying with him and his wife, the night I was released from jail, was a bad idea. Well, Sean said I was welcome to stay, but I’m sure he didn’t expect this to happen. Luckily the charges have been dropped, but what happens when I’ve gotta go back to court? What if I have to be locked up just because those brain dead detectives can’t tell a criminal from an innocent party? This government sucks ass!} *Shane continues to walk down the pathway until he reaches a small car park and walks towards his light blue Aston Martin. He pulls the keys out of his pocket and pushes the button on a small device on the key ring. The device unlocks the doors automatically and Shane opens the car door at the driver seat, stepping inside the vehicle and closing the door after him. He puts the keys in the ignition and the car radio starts up. “Minority” plays out of the car speakers as Shane reverses and drives out of the New York Plaza car park. Shane continues onto the busy roads for approximately five minutes until he reaches the Fdw Headquarters. Shane pulls into the entrance of the private car park where there is a barrier blocking him. The barrier is controlled by the security guard in the box to the left of him. Shane winds down his car window* Guard: Evening Mr Wethers! How are you today? Go on’say it, “Your all that and a whole lot f’n more?” huh? *Shane looks at the guard for a moment, with the look of total bewilderment.* Shane: Just lemme through yer' fat grease monkey. *The guard looks apologetic and shocked at Shane’s harsh response* Guard: Uh’sorry Mr Wethers, right away sir. *Shane winds the window back up and drives forward as the barrier rises. He pulls into the busy car park and parks his car in an empty space on the ground floor. He puts the car out of gear, pulls on the handbrake and turns the keys in the ignitions, removing them as he opens the car door and steps out. He closes the car door behind him and presses the lock button on his key ring. The lights flash three times and the car makes a bleep sound as he walks away. Shane walks through the entrance doors of the car park and proceeds to walk into the main entrance of Fdw H.Q. As he walks through reception a guy walks over to him, the guy looks familiar, he has a beard, glasses and a suit on. He stops Shane in his tracks* Shane: What the?.” Do I know you? Cos if I don’t then you better take your hands off me otherwise I will lodge your teeth down the back of your throat. *The guy lets go and steps back* Guy: I’m Bill Gates *Shane stares at him, finally recognising who he is* Guy: I’m the guy who created Microsoft and?. *Shane interrupts him* Shane: I know who you are you damn fool. What I don’t know is what your doing here, unless Omega got his genitals caught in the zip drive after jerking off over clip art. *Bill grins* Bill: Actually I’m here as a messenger. You see, I was sat down once creating Microsoft Pootang organiser when an angel zapped me up and told me if I didn’t be your guardian angel, then they would have to castrate my balls. *Shane looks at Bill in utter disbelief. Shane responds sarcastically* Shane: Oh yeah? and who was the Angel? Ronald Trump? Bill: No, he was the devil?.actually the angel was Hewlett Packard. *Shane looks at Bill with a frozen expression of disbelief. All of a sudden Shane knocks Bill to the ground* Shane: If that’s right then where were you guys when Microsoft Word crashed last night? *Shane goes to the ground with an elbow drop, you here a sick thud and a cry of pain coming from Bill Gates* Bill: aaaaaaaargh! My liver! *Shane stands back up* Shane: And how comes windows XP is a total piss take? *Shane drops yet again with another elbow drop, and the screaming of pain from Bill can be heard again* Bill: Ow! You got me in the eye! *Shane stands up again, brushing himself down* Shane: Now how’s that for a taste of revenge? Bill: ~Groan~ *Shane walks away leaving Bill down, dazed and in pain. Shane walks through reception and through a set of grey double doors on the other side of the room. The scene cuts into an advertisement*
