Tasting the Sun

4 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th June 2005 Tears? How many I have shed over the passed few months is impossible to count. As I sat in my dark steel room with nothing to comfort me but my mind’s eye. Tears streaming down my face as horrible images of my wife and daughter in pain and torment flashed through my mind. It was enough to drive a man crazy and to be honest with you it nearly did. Of course I had no idea what state my family was in but that did not stop my mind tormenting me with the worst. Day after day all that was on my mind was my family, questions flooding my mind, where were they? Are they okay? Are they still alive? I had to get out not just for my own sanity but for my family. 4 long months I have not felt the warmth of my wife’s body, I have not heard her beautiful laugh, I have not held my baby Emma in my arms and looked down as the precious angel sleeps. 4 months without the 2 people who mean the world to me. Now I am free and I about to be reunited with those that I love? It was not an easy journey, as I said it has taken me 4 long months to reach the position I am in now. For 4 months I was under the thumb of one Doctor Richard Wood. Day in and day out he would put me through useless experiments which according to him were all in the interest of science. I saw no escape from my own personal hell until one day I was able to connect the dots and find my key. The key was none other than a fellow GW roster member who goes by the name of Mike Biddle. You see for a couple of months Wood had someone follow me whilst I was at the GW arena and GW events so he could keep track of me. I always had the sense that someone was following me but I just put it down to paranoia. Until 1 day when I discovered that Biddle knew more than he was letting on and so started the rivalry you see before you today. I was fighting a battle on 2 fronts, one against Wood and another against Biddle. After another month of battling both, the answer came to me. At Wrestlecade along with Corvus, we were booked in a Ladder Match for the vacant GW Platinum Championship. Now I knew Biddle couldn’t refuse a challenge so I added a stipulation of my own. That stipulation was if I won then Biddle would have to tell me the location of my family and also my contract to Wood would be made null and void. If Biddle won however I would agree not to touch him whilst he was Platinum Champion. Like always I was placing it all on the line and it paid off? Now I am the new GW Platinum Champion and now I am on my way to hopefully be reunited with my family. My life is truly on the up and with this Gold and Glory Tournament coming up it could get a hell of a lot better. Now I know after the beating I took thanks to the new stable Rex Deus or to give them their real names Leinad Soma and Mike Biddle, that the possibility of me competing in the first round of this tournament seems unlikely and I would agree with you that is why El Serpenti has been called in just in case I cannot fight. However I do intend on being there and if I am there then there is every chance I will fight. So all I have to say to my opponent, Mindy Mercer is be ready because you don’t know who is going to be coming out of that curtain? The exit doors of the hospital slid open as I stepped into the California sun. It had been a couple of weeks since I was officially released from Richard Wood’s “care” but it still made my skin tinkle when I stepped into the outside world. Of course I couldn’t feel the warmth of the sun but it was simply the fact that I was outside and I was free to do as I pleased. I looked down at my right arm which had been tightly wrapped in bandages. I was still able to move my arm but the doctor had told me that it was best that I didn’t compete in the Gold and Glory tournament to give it time to heal. I simply said “We’ll see?” I have respect for doctors and they do a good job but they don’t have much concept of a human being’s will. This is the second GW tournament I have been in that involves the GW World Title in some form. In the first tournament I got as far as the second round but in this one I intend to go all the way. I have waited a long time for a chance like this to come my way and no damaged arm is going to stop me from competing. I appreciate Serpenti stepping in for me but this is my tournament and if I am to win it then I need to do it for myself. As I continued to examine my arm I heard a large beep from in front of me. I looked up and saw my friend, Peter Willows staring back at me in a pair of tinted sunglasses in his Mercedes 500. He had a huge grin on his face as I made my way to the car. As I got in Pete jumped on me and wrapped his arms around me in a tight hug. I was a little shocked as he leaned back. “Sorry.” He smirked. “What was that for?” I questioned. “Just whilst you were in that facility of Wood’s, I missed you man. It made me realise we have been through a lot together and well it was technically my fault that you got in that mess.” “Hey, don’t blame yourself Pete. I signed that contract, I agreed to those terms and so if it is anyone’s fault it is mine.” “But?” I held my hand up interrupting Pete. “It’s water under the bridge Pete. All that is important now is that we get Rachel and Emma back. You got the address?” “Right here.” Pete said holding up a piece of folded paper. “Then lets go.” With a turn of the ignition, the car roared to life and we were back on the road. Pete had taken the roof down so the Californian sun was filling the car. As I looked up I heard the birds call as they flew over head, I couldn’t help but smile. Life was finally getting back on track; all I needed now was my wife and daughter by my side. “What did the doc say about your arm?” Pete said as he changed lanes on the freeway. “He said I’ll live.” I chuckled. “Seriously Adam, what did the doc say?” Pete asked looking at me with a stern look. “Alright, he said that I shouldn’t compete in the Gold and Glory tournament.” “So your not going to compete right?” I didn’t reply and simply looked out of the car. “Right?” Pete persisted. “I don’t know,” I sighed, “I mean, how many times will I get a chance like this Pete” The doctor only advised me not to compete. I feel fine, I can still move my arm and I am sure it will be fine by the time Evolution comes around.” “But you don’t have to compete Adam; I mean El Serpenti has got your back. He promised to compete for you until you can compete yourself then he would step aside.” “I know but what if Serpenti gets through the first and second round and then I take over and I win. Will I have gotten there on my own” No, and unless I compete in every match myself then no one will see me as the true No.1 Contender to the World Title.” “But why compete and risk injuring yourself more when you can let someone else do the fighting giving you the chance to heal?” “Because as I said Pete I need to get there on my own! Serpenti is a great friend for what he is doing but this is my battle to fight not his. I need this tournament to show that no matter what that Biddle and his gang throw at me they can’t keep me down. I mean can you imagine the look on Biddle’s face when I win that tournament even after he tried to sabotage my chances” This is my chance to wipe that smug look off his face. This is my chance to stand up and be noticed by the GW and no injury is going to stop me.” “As long as you are sure Adam.” Pete glanced over at me with a questioning look. “I’m sure.” “Well as you know I will always support your decision. I just hope this decision doesn’t put you out for another month.” “It won’t.” I laughed. We pulled off the freeway and onto some dirt roads. The dust flew into the air as Pete put his foot down and the car screeched and roared as it drew close to 100 mile per hour. As we drew closer to our destination my heart began to pound, the palms of my hand became sweaty, my mouth became dry. I felt like I was about to sick, after 4 months of hell I was about to be with my angels. I looked down and stared at my hands, they were trembling with adrenaline. Suddenly the car screamed to a halt. I looked across at Pete who was beaming with a huge smile as he nodded to something to my left. Slowly I raised my head and looked to my left. There as Wood had described was a log cabin in the middle of a baron desert. There was absolutely no one around, no people, no cars, just nothing but this log cabin. I quickly swung open the car door and as fast as my feet could take me I ran over to the cabin door and swung it open? IT WAS EMPTY! I felt my heart sink as I looked around at the abandoned cabin. The only thing that occupied this cabin was a bed. “Rachel!” I screamed in desperation, hoping she was hiding in some secret compartment but there was no reply. “RACHEL!” I screamed again my eyes frantically darting around the cabin. All this was taking me back to when I first arrived home and found they had been taken away and I felt sick to my stomach. Suddenly I spotted something in the corner of my eye something hanging from the end post of the bed. I quickly ran over and grabbed what was a pink blanket. It was a blanket my mother had knitted for my daughter Emma. It had the name Emma stitched on it. With my gloved hand I ran my hand across the name. As I let my eyes follow my hand across the name I fell to my knees and burst into tears as the “a” of Emma was positioned next to a red stain, a stain I had seen many a time in my day, it was a stain of? BLOOD! Streams of tears rolled down my cheek as the realisation of why this cabin was empty hit me. The tears continued to fall as I held the blanket to my mask; this was all I had left. This blood stained blanket was all that remained of my wife and my baby. I was alone? “or was I”... “Adam?” Came a voice I hadn’t heard for 4 months from behind me. With tear soaked eyes I turned around and as the sun blazed in through the cabin door a figure the same shape as my Rachel stood at the doorway. The figure stepped forward and as my watery eyes focused I saw there holding my precious baby was my precious wife in perfect health. Without any words and with tears still streaming down my face I grabbed my wife and child and hugged them close. No words were spoken...Nothing was said’We just stood there holding each other.
