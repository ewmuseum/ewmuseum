A Letter and a Rose

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/20/04 Still reeling from his last encounter with Trey Reed over the TV/PHONE, Gonzalez leans back on his chair. The sun blast through the back veranda and shines all through the room lighting it up. Gonzalez leans over to and grabs his Television Championship Title. 'OK vato! You want this title, come and get it!' Just as Gonzalez is scoffing at the idea of Trey Reed a knock at the door startles him, a UPS woman dressed in the traditional brown walks in. She has a small package in her left hand. Slowly walking up towards the desk she lays the package on the table. 'Sign here sir.' Gonzalez leans up and grabs the pen, as the ladie leaves he pulls out a small blade and begins to open up the package. As he opens it up he holds the box up and a white rose falls out. He looks it over and notices that a letter is also in the package. Gonzalez, You sent your boys after me, and you thought that they could end this. You're fooling around Gonzalez, I've got proof! The man is after you Gonzalez, and I'm gonna get that Television Championship Title! Here's a rose for your troubles, smell it long and good, it may be the last rose you ever smell! The man is getting iratated, you've got till Monday! Let me know what you're gonna do. In anger Gonzalez throws the package off his desk, and then grabs the rose and crushes it with his left hand. 'OK vato! You want to play games! You want to send me letters and a rose! OK esse! Lets play games!' Just then the two men that Gonzalez had sent to take out Trey Reed show up, they look a bit upset as they approach Gonzalez. Just then Gonzalez pulls out his two pistols and with a flick of his wrist shoots both men in the head. THey fall back and hit the ground. 'You imbasils! You screwed things up esse! You screwed things up! I need men that can work like men! Carmen! Carmen!' A young Mexican girl walks into the room and gasp as she sees the dead men in the floor. 'Get a camera! Take pictures, I'm gonna show that vato that I mean business! You can't have anyone do things now adays. What are you waiting on? Take pictures?' The girl sobs as she grabs a camera and takes several pictures. 'Paco! Chocolate! ' Gonzalez's two normal henchmen walk into the room. They look down at the ground and see the two bald men. Gonzalez nods and they nod back. Both men walk out of the room. 'Trey Reed, you want to play games esse? Well I'm gonna play a little game with you homes! It's called war!' Putting both pistols into his holsters, Gonzales steps over the two men as he walks out of his office.
