An Eventful Night II

6 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/06/05 ~Getting up to speed~ When we last left our boys, they had decided to follow Dani on her 'date' with the researcher, Jaymes. Jaymes appears to be not only brought in by their sponsors as a specialist on Native Mythology. Last week, while trying to conduct a ceremony to help determine what was going on with Dani, the group was attacked by a demonic entity that Jaymes claimed was working for the other side. This was a side that both Gray and Josh didn't know too much about. However, the signs of a similar attack had been recorded by Josh the week before that in Danielle's own room at the hotel. Dani had still not talked about that night, where she was found unconscious and wrapped in a towel still, as if she had passed out coming out of the shower. However, after banishing the demon, Jaymes had asked Dani to join him for a night on the town. He used the excuse that he was using it to get Dani into a relaxed mode while he asked her for more information. But Josh saw it as a sign of him trying to move in on his territory. He's in love with Dani, that's something that he knows, and she knows it too. Josh, though, also thought that Jaymes was a slimy snake of a man, who was into this for something a bit more. Something gave Josh a feeling that this outsider was after something else, and he was hiding things from them, and Dani. So, for his night with Dani, Josh convinced his brother to take Dani's truck, her Toyota Highlander, and follow them for their time. They had followed behind them when they went to the Bellagio Hotel and Casino, and Josh had lingered a bit as they took their dinner at the 5 star restaurant called Picasso's. Thanks to the kids toys, that denote that they promote kids to spy on others, that he had purchased from two perverts who were spying on the girls by pool side, Josh had listened in as Dani had ordered the Braised Fallow Dear, as well as other items... including him taking in a couple bottles of wine. He'd also listened in on the small talk as he asked Dani questions about her past and her training. After that, they followed as Jaymes had driven his Jaguar XJ8 to the parking garage of the New York, New York Hotel and Casino. They had made the 7:30 PM showing of Zumanity, but unfortunately they couldn't stick around and ended up going back up to the top floor of the parking garage to wait for them to leave at Gray's insistence. “I don't like this Gray. We should be down there.” Josh spoke out, for the um-teenth time tonight. They had Jaymes' Jaguar in view, thanks to the night vision goggles that Josh had bought along with the shotgun microphone. Gray, himself needed no such assistance due to his other side. “Shush, I can't have you in a Casino making a scene. We'd get kicked out and watched by the damn cameras. They'll head out here about 9:00 O'clock. The next show after that is 9:30, and the staff has to clean the place up.” Josh didn't like it still. ?What if he has a room here?? Gray countered. “What if they stop to play the tables or something? Not our business. I promise, if they're not up here by 9:30, we can start to head in and take a look around. Just chill out.” Josh sat there with the notes of Jaymes' character that he had already figured out tonight running through his head. He remembered the scene where Jaymes picked Dani up from their hotel, when he walked up and handed her a single red rose, and then hugged her. Then again the scene of Dani in that dress wasn't a fun thing for him either, for she wasn't dressed like that while going out with him. Jealousy, envy, call it what you will, but he wasn't a happy man right now. Josh suddenly went to mimic what could only be Dani's voice for a slight moment. ?Jaymes, why are we parking way up here, couldn't we just use the Valet?? He then switched over to a voice that was nothing but to mimic Jaymes' usual tone. “I don't trust the valets with my baby here. The Las Vegas valets are the worst in the country. I'd like to have a car waiting for me. The top floor of the parking garages usually have less people in them, and thus less cars. Keeps it all safe and secure...” He then trails off back into his own voice. “What a prick. Everything about him is his money. Thousand dollar suits, expensive little sports car, 5-star dinners, and a show afterwards. This guy makes me sick.” Gray chuckled, it was overflowing with the distaste for his apparent rival. Josh took some offense to his brother's glee. ?What's so funny?? Gray just sighed. “Nothing, little wolf, nothing. Just remember, it was an Indian who made up the saying about letting something go if you love it. Even I can see that this man is superficial and plastic. He would throw her away in a heartbeat for the next best thing to come his way.” Josh shakes his head. “That's not the only thing there though. He's a native, a shaman from the looks of it. Dani's special to her people, maybe even to all the Nations. We don't know yet. But something tells me that...” Gray reached out and put his hand on his brother's shoulder. “Joshua Bodaway Brand, you're feeding things into this that don't need to be. I have joined you in your subterfuge tonight, to lay to rest your fears. But you know that if what you are truly fearing comes to pass, then it won't be a good thing for you. You need to calm down, not everyone out there has evil plots to carry out.” Josh didn't look at his brother. ?This guy isn't just after a beautiful woman, he's got other things on his mind. I don't know how I know it, I just do... okay?? Gray chuckled again. “Alright, little wolf, alright.” The two sat there for a bit longer, time passing as Josh fumed more and more. ~At About 9:20 PM~ Jaymes and Dani stepped out of the elevator right on time for what Gray had said they would. A bit of relief appeared to come from Josh who then trained the shotgun microphone on the pair, while he watched them. ?So, was that fun?? Jaymes asked Dani, as he walked behind her. Josh could tell by his angle that he was checking out how she walked from the back. Josh himself had done this many times. She normally had a slight swing to her backside when wearing heels. “Oh, yes. The show was rather good. Although I can see why I waited for it to be the last one of Cirque Du Solei's shows I saw. That was rather... racy.” Dani answered him, loud and clear over the speaker in the truck, as they listened in. If Josh could see in color through the goggles, he'd see the slight bit of blush that had come to Dani's cheeks. “Nothing wrong with it, adults need their diversions also. And I've always found that things dealing with mostly taboo subjects tend to open the mind and the mouth a bit. You've answered all of my questions rather well tonight.” Jaymes was apparently still trying to make it seem like taking her out was part of his job. Right at that moment, as Josh watched the man size Dani up from behind, he wanted to just reach out and punch him right in that chiseled chin of his. Jaymes flipped his long hair back, out of his face in a slight gust of wind that came up on the 8 story structure. Gray focused in on the man also, as he listened to the speaker on the toy microphone. “Thank you, Jaymes. I must say that this night has been a pleasure. I guess you're right. I don't feel at all like when I've been asked all these questions before about my family and that. I'd recommend this type of technique to anyone, including Ms. Leanan.” Dani stepped up close to the Jaguar, near the trunk area. “Oh, no problems there. So, shall we continue our evening or do you wish to retire? It's only about 9:30...” Josh's hands clenched into a fist. He might as well have asked her if she wanted to go back to his penthouse room at the Wynn Hotel and make like wild beasts the way the words rolled off his tongue. ?No, I've had a bit too much wine, I might have to admit. I think maybe I should head back and get some rest. As I understand it, I've got to head to Seattle in the next few days and be ready to face this chick I call Cowboy Barbie for our third confrontation. I guess Global has it out for her or something. I hope that's not a disappointment to you?? Dani looked innocent with her body language and that. It was almost as if she half knew what he was implying in his offer, but she wanted to play it safe. Jaymes showed no dismay for it though, and moved to open the back door on the passenger side of his car. “As you wish, M'lady. My coach shall take you home then. You'll excuse me while I check something really quick though.” Jaymes leaned into the back door on his car and appeared to grab something from under the back of the passenger seat. He turned and smiled wide at Dani as he then handed her a small box. “A little something for you my dear...” Dani took the small box, with a pink ribbon and bow around it. “Son of a bitch...” Josh said as he watched in the black and white view of the goggles. Dani opened the box to show a piece of jewelry in it. A locket type necklace with various stones around it in a design of a heart. “No, you shouldn't have. I can't take this.” It was obviously worth a good amount of money, but Jaymes put his hands up in protest when Dani moved to hand it back to him. ?No, no... not a problem at all. I must insist. Especially after you open the locket. It's the least I could do for the daughter of Daniel and Lynn Northsky. You do know you were named after both of them, right?? Dani gasped as she opened the locket. Inside were two pictures, one of her father and mother, shortly after they were married... a few short months before she was born. The veil over her mother's eyes showed that the pictures this was made from might have come from their actual wedding reception itself. Dani stood, admiring the pictures of her parents, things that she had to live with since she was so young... they had been murdered by a mob of people off the reservation when she was only a baby. She tried to hold back a tear, but failed miserably and it escaped down her cheek. “Stay where you are, Joshua.” Gray stated, him having full color vision at this time. Dani put her clutch purse and the box to the locket on top of the car and then moved to pull the necklace out of it's box. As she did that, Jaymes takes his handkerchief from his breast pocket and turns from her slightly. “I apologize if it brings back bad memories. I just thought you might want something a bit more to remember them by. I know you don't have much, being raised by your grandfather and that after the full ravages of those that did that cruel deed to them.” Dani didn't answer him right away, so he continued, turning back around to look at her. “Although that would lead me to one question that has been gnawing away at me since I got here...” Dani chocked on the words to acknowledge him. ?Yes? That is?? With a serious tone to his voice, Jaymes asked her... ?How or where did you learn the Christian Prayer, in Latin none the less?? Dani blurted out her answer. “After I lost them, I spent quite a few years being raised in a Catholic Orphanage, until gran-pa came to find me, and take me out of... there.” It suddenly hit her what she was actually answering. No one knew that she still remembered the ways of the nuns and that at the convent orphanage. She'd never advertised it. But she had used the Lord's Prayer just recently when she was attacked in her room. Josh, back in the truck suddenly spoke out... ?What type of question was that?? Dani took a step back and went to turn towards Jaymes... Who hadn't moved in his closeness to her. “That's good to know...” Dani started to turn to bolt away, her alarms going off now, but she was stopped. From the backseat of the car came a collection of what could only be tentacles... grasping arms that grabbed her by her arms and legs, and around her body. The same type that had been on the entity that had attacked her in her room, and then had tried to attack her during the ceremony in the grave yard last week. The same type that Jaymes has banished during that ceremony. ?What are you doing?? Dani asked as she began to struggle. Jaymes smiled as his plan appeared to be finally taking fruitation. The sound of a car or truck door opening and slamming shut in the distance can be heard. “PATER noster, qui es in caelis, sanctificetur nomen tuum...” Dani started to speak the Lord's Prayer like she did that night that she had been visited in her hotel room. But it was cut off as Jaymes took his hand and put it over her mouth and nose. The handkerchief covered most of her face. ?MMMPH!? The only sound that came out as Dani breathed in the chemicals that he'd put into it while she wasn't looking. Slowly her breathing slowed and her eyes rolled back into her head. Her struggles lessened and then she slumped into the creatures grasp. “Hold her tight, I need to get her to the right place to use the jar. Once that's done it'll be just a little trip back into the city and all will be taken care of. We're about to be free my servants...” Jaymes shut the back door on his car and went to walk around to the other side to enter. A flash of movement caught his eye, coming right at him... “YOU SON OF A...” Jaymes, his jacket unbuttoned from removing the bottle of chloroform from his inside pocket, reached down to a pouch on his belt and then withdrew it. He tossed a cloud of powder right into Josh's face as he comes in. It almost instantly dropped him, as he began to cough violently. “Ah, Mr. Impulsive. Guess my plan wasn't fully idiot proof. But as you can see, I adapt rather well. Don't worry, loverboy. She'll be in good hands. In a few hours all will be over with.” Jaymes pushes Josh over with the heel of his boot, and then gets into his car. He pulls out, dumping the case to the locket and Dani's purse onto the ground. Josh takes the goggles off his face, he'd forgotten to take them off when he ran out of the car. However, with a witches powders needing a path to the eyes to make a full effect, they just saved him from anything more then a breathing irritation. Quickly he grabs the dropped stuff, watching as the Jaguar pulls out of the top parking area. The sound of the approaching Highlander builds, finally Gray had gotten the truck started and moving. Gray slowed as he pulled up near his brother, and Josh dove into the back seat through the open windows. “Okay, little wolf... I guess I owe you an apology...” Gray turned down the winding exit way for the parking garage, trying to keep his driving like a mad man in a place that could have just about anyone walk out in front of them. ?Apology accepted, now if you don't mind, catch that bastard so I can castrate him!? Josh said that as he flipped over onto his back, in the backseat. He finished pulling his legs int through the window, just in time to miss a few other vehicles in a tight turn that was heading out onto Flamingo Road, where you exit the Casino's grounds. “You'll have to beat me too it, little brother.” The Jaguar came out of the parking garage and avoided a near wreck by switching lanes at the last second, and then ran the red light, turning right and heading away from the Las Vegas Strip and it's large mass of traffic. The metallic blue Highlander came out, right behind it, slightly going over the curb as it turned to follow. Unknown to all of them, a Ducati motor cycle was right behind that, making sure that it's presence wasn't known to anyone else in the chase. ~End Part 2~ ~To Be Continued~
