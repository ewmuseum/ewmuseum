Crusing home/Bad memories

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/19/03 Headlights shine ahead over the tar road in front of him as Johnie drives on towards what he can call home. Staring off into the nothing that is the darkness of night before him. He heads straight onward back from just another job. Still several hours ahead and a long ways from his destination. Redness darkens his eyes from what he’s already covered and passed. His eyes stare blindly on the road, while his mind is on other things. Over the past. Still having the same questions pondering him. Same issues, same problems. What’s happened to me? Johnie snaps his mind out of it for a moment as he turns his attention to the speedometer as it glows “72 mph” in florescent green. Still too slow Boredom sinks in as he turns towards the radio as its been off for a few hours now. It clicks on with a blaring screech of a fiddle. If you’re gonna play in Texas, you gotta have a fiddle in the band! That lead guitar is hot, but not for Louisiana man. “Some fucking country station again,” he scoffs as the song continues. “I must still be in Texas then. Pfft. Corny bullshit.” He twists the knob to the left through the static and buzzing until he hits another stop. The strange twang of a guitar plays along with a slight drum beat and tambourine as a song starts to play. “Heads Carolina. Tails California Somewhere greener. Somewhere warmer.” “How about somewhere else,” as he hit’s the knob again. “More country shit. There’s gotta be something else on.” A hard drumbeat hits as the guitar follows afterward with a hard beat starting the lyrics out. “She thinks my tractor’s sexy! Its really turns her on.” “WHAT THE FUCK IS THIS SHIT”!!” he shrieks hitting the scan button this time. “Jesus. Is this whole fucking state obsessed with country”! And why the fuck would a chick find a tractor sexy? Goddamn this is bullshit. There HAS to be something good on here.” The scanner spins through the countless numbers over the FM dial until it has to start over at the beginning. “Good lord. Let there be something on.” The scroll finally stops on a “94.5” as a soft beat comes over the airwaves with an enticing voice to back it up. “I’m a genie in a bottle baby. Gotta rub me the right way, honey. I’m a genie in a bottle, baby. Come, come, come on and let me out.” A small grin creeps across Johnie’s face as he hears Christina moan out the next part of the chorus. “Heh. I could rub you the right way, babe. But that doesn’t mean that you can sing. NEXT!” He hit’s the scanner again and comes across a slow thump of a good set of drums coordinated guitar. “Some people call me the space cowboy. Yeah. Some call me the gangster of love. Some people call me Maurice.” Johnie laughs as the song continues on and bobs his head to it a bit. “Steve Miller Band, yeah. Maybe someone does have a little taste in the sticks.” He starts to sing a long, getting some of the words right. Hit and miss. “Cause I’m a picker. I’m a gritter. I’m lover and a singer. Playing my music in the sun. I’m a joker. I’m a smoker. I’m a midnight toker. I’m get my lovin? on the run.” The guitar riff continues as Johnie turns back towards the empty highway as the moon hangs high and shines across his hands on the wheel, tapping to the beat. He pulls his hands to the right pocket of his and pulls out a unopened pack of Camel Filters. Johnie taps the pack on the back of his hand, before tearing off the wrapping and getting the first cigarette out. After rolling down the window, he lights it up as the song ends and the radio announcer comes on gabbing about the usual. “This is Classic Rock WKOR, the way rock was meant to be. We have just gone through a solid hour of your requests as it comes to a near close with “Joker” by the Steve Miller Band. Awesome tune, by the way. But before we let you go on this night, I’d like to put on one last track for Jenny from Amarillo. She wanted to hear some mellow Def Leppard, so here you go, Jen. This is Cactus Jake and I am outta here.” The song starts in a soft guitar intro with scattered riffs and jingles. Johnie turns his head over towards the radio. His smile turns to a hard grimace real quick. He knows the song. “When you make love, do you look in the mirror” Do you look down? Does he look like me? Do you think twice?” Johnie slams his hand down on the radio and shuts it off right away, turning back towards the road. His eyes wide and bulged out as far as they could go. It struck a nerve. He knew it did. As he takes a drag off his cigarette, he stares as far as he can forward but its not far enough. He wants to go further back. So his mind does it for him. ************************************************************* Johnie lies in his bedroom, glaring at the opposite wall that holds his trophy shelves. Originally when he put that up, it was meant to motivate him. Now without a good knee to stand on, it’s a razzing curse. They laugh at him. He let those trophies down. He was meant for better things. Not lying in some bedroom with a heavy brace on his knee. He turns left to look over at the clock as it shines back at him. Of course he already knows what time it is. He checked three minutes ago. She’s late. Way late. Smacking his head against the headboard draws his mother into the bedroom. “What’s wrong, dear?” “What do you think is wrong”!” he snaps back at her. Might be a bit harsh, but it didn’t matter to him. “How should I know?” she retorts as she goes over to pick up the shirts scattered over the floor. “I don’t quite pay attention to all you do. Not like you do much anymore anyway.” She chuckles at that. It wasn’t funny. “What’s so funny”! Huh?!” He’s riled up now. This is a bad time for that. “The fact that I’m lying here fucked over for the rest of my life”! HUH?! Is that funny to you?” With the veins popping out, he turns away from firing a glare from hell at his mother towards the door as...she stands in the doorway. He knows he just messed up. Too late to take anything back now. Instead he tilts his head down in disgust. His mother sucks her lips into her mouth and bites down hurt. She heads towards door and slides out of the way. “Maybe you can talk some sense into him, Trisha. He won’t listen to me at all.” “Why should I listen to you” All you do is nag at me,” he utters under his breath as his mother leaves. Trisha heads towards the bed reluctantly while Johnie keeps his head down on his hands shuffling through his sheets. She sits down near the edge of the bed around where his feet are. Usually she has a smile on her face and she rubs his feet some. But this time, its different. Her thin lips are frowning a bit and her eyes don’t really even look at him. He knows it because he feels it. Something’s changed in her. “How are you Johnie?” “........” At first, he doesn’t want to answer, but he knows he has to eventually. Maybe not right away though. Sometime. “Johnie?” She needs to say something, but she wants his attention first. “You’re late.” To the point. Why waste time. “I-I know. I just...” She’s stalling, he knows it. “Just...what?” His voice is growing harsher. His eyes are still down at the sheets, but he quit fiddling with them. But now she is. She gets to be the nervous one. “I just...Johnie...I’ve been thinking.” Oh great. “Things have changed. You’ve changed. And...I don’t like what’s happened.” She gets up and walks towards the trophies and looks over them with a small smile and a chuckle. “I mean...we were going somewhere together. You were doing well with football and we were looking forward to being together past college. We had a plan.” She turns back towards Johnie, but looking at the carpet instead of him. “Now, the plan’s gone. You got hurt and you’re different now.” “So what are you saying, huh”!” He picks his head up from twisting the blankets with his hands. His eyes are quickly filling with rage and he glares right through her black heart. “That you think we’re through” Just because I fucked up my knee, that we’re through? FUCK YOU!!” She turns her head towards Johnie now and walks towards him. But once she sees his eyes, she stops. “No. Its just that...” “Fuck you! You were using me to go somewhere. That’s it. You were using me! If you wanna leave me” FINE! Be a fucking bitch then and leave me. Leave me here in this hole.” She’s not moving. She just stands there in whimpering tears. Trying to bilk it for all its worth. Johnie won’t have any of it. He picks up a pillow and throws it at her head. “GO!! Get the fuck outta here!” She turns and goes towards the door running with her hands over her face, crying hard. Johnie twists himself to the side of the bed and starts to run after her in rage, but falls flat on his face shrieking in pain. “AHHHHH!!!” As he lies there, he picks his head up and hears the front door slam hard. He whimpers a soft cry as a tear trickles down his cheek. “Fucking bitch...” TO BE CONTINUED.....
