An Eventful Night III

10 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/10/05 ~The Streets of Las Vegas~ Two cars going down the Las Vegas roads, away from the busy hustle and bustle known as the Strip. The car in the lead, a black 2005 Jaguar XJ8 SV8; a 390 horsepower, supercharged V8, 6 speed automatic car . Inside of it is a man that the Brands know only by his first name, Jaymes. A Nakota wise man... witch... shaman. They thought that he was here to help, but what they just witnessed a few short moments ago, leads them to know otherwise. Behind him, in a Metallic Blue 2003 Toyota Highlander SUV. Not the speed demon that the Jaguar is, but by far not a car to wince at on the streets or off road. Inside this car are both Joshua and Gray Brand as they follow the other one. Josh, the only one who didn't see Jaymes as a good person, due to some feelings and hunches of his own, sits in the back seat. He's still having a slightly strong cough as he recovers from the powder that had been thrown into his face when he rushed to try to stop Jaymes from making his get away. Fortunately for him, the goggles he had been wearing at the time kept the hallucinogen from getting to his eyes, a focal entry point to allow passage of the chemicals in it to the brain. However, it still had some side effects and they allowed Jaymes to get into his car, with Dani apparently unconscious in the backseat. Gray, the man who isn't really a man, but something else drives the car at a safe following speed, using all of his supernatural senses to ensure that everything is safe. He doesn't need to cause an accident or hurt someone else, causing them to loose Dani. Right now, the only thing they do know is that he has her, and he's trying to get away. As for his plans? They know nothing of them. “Can we get to the side of his car? This is a four-lane road.” Josh was antsy. He wanted to get his hands on the man who had done this. ?Not safely. There is to much traffic on this road. He's heading east, probably towards the outskirts, the desert. I don't need to try anything stupid to cost us our tracking, or force him into an accident, now sit down and shut up, will you?? Gray maneuvered around a couple of smaller cars, expertly. His days of training as an agent of the Order coming through. Gray had never made it into the elite group known as the hunters, but he was a guardian. He was Dani's guardian, assigned to her. Although she didn't quite know this yet. The car suddenly turned a hard right. Josh, not in a seat belt after jumping back into the car as it came by him in the parking area, slammed head first into the other door. They both felt the back end of the truck begin to fish tail. ?Ow! Will you watch it?? Gray didn't answer. Josh felt the vehicle straighten out and then regain it's course. Josh's head came back up and he noticed they were on a dimly lit road beyond the high priced area of Spanish Trails. Gray was right, they were heading to a scarcely populated area. The traffic started to thin out considerably and it looked like the sports car was getting ready to make a run for it. ?Gray... ? Gray reached over and hit the button on the dashboard that put the SUV into 4 wheel drive. He then dropped it from 4th to 3rd gear and floored it as the smaller car started to pull away. “Gray...” ?HOLD ON!? Josh's hands went up to the roof, in an attempt to make himself more rigid. His legs flexed and pushed against the floor. Gray pulled up, nose in line with that of the smaller black car and then reached up and took off the 4 Wheel Drive. The truck lurched forward with the change of power train, gaining a few feet on the smaller car. Just as the street went down to a one lane in each direction road, with other cars on the side of the streets, packed away for the evening, Gray suddenly pulled the wheel to a hard left... ?GRAY!? The Highlander fully fishtailed, doing a bootleg reverse of sorts, now aimed in the other direction. They both watched as the Jaguar turned also, and ran up onto the sidewalk, hitting a stand of three free magazine racks. Advertisements of “In Room Asian Massage” and “Get Laid Tonight” flew everywhere as the car came to a stop. The front end, mostly fiberglass and plastic, showed signs of needing some major body repair... but that wasn't the concern that the Brands had. Gray hit the gas and pulled the SUV right up next to the car, turning slightly to come up along side of it. “You get Dani, I've got the bastard.” Josh opened the door and stepped out, right next to the car. Gray climbed out of the driver's seat and then walked around the car. Jaymes was also making his exit from his vehicle. He came up and looked at both men with distaste. “You know, this is a hundred-thousand dollar car. I think you two need to really reconsider this. I'll sue your asses off for this.” Josh gave him a shocked look. It was if he was trying to play off that he hadn't done what he had actually done! “You, sir, are in violation of the Order's directives on this case. And you have my charge. As a Guardian of the Order, you know what my duties would entail.” Gray gave him the warning, stern and from the book. That was sometimes his problems. In trying to control his own rage, he was sometimes too much from the blasted book. Jaymes looked at Gray and smiled as he opened his business coat. “Ah, so, you are here to challenge me. You... Good Saquamish cousin, should know that you don't get in the way of a shaman. Let alone someone of my age. I've been waiting over a century for Ms. Northsky to come into being. I'm not going to wait again...” At that, Jaymes pulled something out and threw them in Gray's direction. Two small objects hit the ground and the smell of sulpher immediately permeated the air. As the creatues that looked like a bad cross between a spider and an octopus appeared out of thin air, Gray assumed a fighting stance. “You're so predictable, Garou...” Jaymes snarled as he said that. He had shown his prejudice against those who were both man and wolf, like Gray, before to Josh. Gray let part of the transformation take him. As he seemed to actually get bigger, and fangs and claws formed from his normal teeth and nails. This was a minor move for him, but one that he could still control. Gray lunged at the two creatures... Both of them grabbed him with their tentacles, but he knew that he had to face them one at a time. Just as he reached one, the other seemed to be getting the upper hand. Then it appeared, a lone rider on a motorcycle came riding in. She dropped the bike, letting it slide up and trip the extra entity that was there. The rider, wearing a tight fitting jump suit, tossed her helmet off as she came up to her feet. Her long blonde hair cascaded down to reveal the hand maiden of Lady Aine Leanan, Kaylee. ?I'm here Mr. Brand, hope you don't mind a helping hand!? Kaylee reached down to her boots and pulled out some items that weren't obvious to the naked human eye. Quickly she heads in on the second creature and starts to attack it with what would appear to be some form of front dagger, designed to be used when punching. ?GET DANI YOU KNUCKLE HEAD!? Josh heard his brother's orders. Jaymes started making some motions with his hands at the two supernatural humans who now faced him. That left Josh... normal in all aspects that he knew, to get the woman he loved to safety. He snapped back into thinking and opened the back door he'd seen Dani disappear into when they were on the roof of the parking garage. As soon as he did, the smell of sulpher almost gagged him. He could see Dani's form laying there, almost bound in place by one of those things as it took up a good portion of the back seat. The handkerchief that he'd used to knock her out, was stuffed in her mouth, to ensure that she'd not awaken soon. He reached in, but noticed the nasty looking business end of the creatures claws could get in his way. ?You're one ugly son of a bitch, that's for sure. ? He started to look around, then reached in. The claws snapped shut, just inches from his fingers, as he withdrew his hand. “Shit...” He could hear the fighting and Jaymes start to chant something. He had to act quickly. He reached behind him and felt something in the truck... Dani's clutch purse. She always had something in there. He unclasped it with one hand and then reached in. The only thing that took up more room then her wallet in her purse was her small hand taser. He grabbed it and pulled it out. It was rated for 900,000 Volts of stopping power, he hoped the advertisements were right. He reopened the door that had shut slightly on the car and gave a test zap with the taser. The smell of ozone overtook all that he could sense when the powerful jolt went off in the air. “Yeah, I got something for you... you fugly son of a...” Josh reached out towards the creature, and it sent one of it's claws in his direction. Quickly he hit the button and again the bolt of electricity arced across the test electrodes of the small box. Instantly upon coming in contact with the creatures claw... it let him know how it felt with a high pitched scream that could only mean it felt pain. “Yeah, my guess was correct. You're in this world enough to hold someone hostage, you're in this world enough to get your ass kicked by me.” The creature withdrew the claw that had been hit, and Josh could see the limb had become slightly translucent, as if the creature had slipped slightly back into the ether of the veil. He then took the opportunity to lunge at it, holding down the button he hit the mass of tentacles that held Dani in place, wrapped around her body. Josh's ears filled with a sound that was reminiscent of fingernails scraping across a chalk board. He thought his ears were going to start to bleed with the noise, but he held the button down and continued to put the electrodes right onto the creature's skin. He held it and the smell of burnt flesh started to permeate the air, overcoming the smell of ozone and sulpher. Eventually, he witnessed as the spider like entity vanished before his eyes, once again taken in by the ether of the veil, and removed from this plane of existence. He sighed in relief as he pulled Dani up off the seat, her unconscious form having no support anymore. He pulled the chemical filled handkerchief from her mouth and then moved to pull her out of the car. He got her legs out and then lifted her up onto his shoulder. Subconsciously he ensured that the dress kept her modesty and then he turned to survey his surroundings. Kaylee, her long blonde hair streaming about, was taking on two of the spider things herself. His brother was engaged in a test of strength with one of his own. That left 3 more there. He saw Jaymes as he was making like some bad ass, waving his hands around and shouting in the language of a Sioux witch. ?I've got Dani, you guys can get your part done now!? Josh yelled, in his brother's direction. However, he drew attention he didn't want. Jaymes turned to him and looked at the fact that his captive was over Josh's shoulder right now. “You impetuous whelp! You will not cost me my prize and my prestige. The grand-daughter of Morning Star will be mine...” Jaymes waved his hands and threw something in Josh's direction. He ducked, and heard what sounded like small rocks hit the side of the truck and then bounce into the street. ?Missed me, bitch!? Josh turned on his heels as he sounded off and looked to be making to get Dani into the SUV, but as he took a step, he fell flat onto his face. He managed to have Dani land onto himself, making sure she was safe. The feeling around his ankle told him that his friend from the car was back. The fact that Dani was also being grabbed by another set of tentacles told him that, he'd come back with friends. Josh rolled, trying to bring the taser up to bear again, but when he hit the button and touched the tentacle that had grabbed his ankle, nothing happened. The batter had finally given up, especially after the last continuous jolt that Josh had given Dani's captor. Josh struggled as he was pulled towards the claws of the creature... He rolled and tried to grab a hold of one of the vehicles, trying to ensure that he couldn't be fulled any further. As he tried to look around for another weapon, he witnessed his brother be sent across the street and land on top of another car and then roll to the sidewalk on the other side. ?Crap!? He could hear Kaylee as she fought, as she was beginning to sound winded. Jaymes actually started to chuckle. “As I said, I'll not be stopped. I'll thank you two, even though you have tried to ensure that my plans won't go through, thanks for the new car. I must say, you've probably rendered mine unusable.” Jaymes began to walk around his car, heading to where Dani was laying on the ground. He got with in a few steps of Dani, and Josh when he stopped in his tracks. ?How did you get here?? Jaymes seemed taken aback and stopped in his tracks. Josh tried to look in the direction he was looking. It was then obvious as to what had happened. Kaylee was there, that meant that Lady Aine (Annie) Leanan knew also... Standing in the middle of the road was that very person... Josh looked up from her shapely legs, up to her stern face and raven black hair. She didn't look at all happy either. ?Ye know all t' damn well why I'm 'ere. Ye've broken our trust, Jaymes!? Jaymes chuckled again. “No, I didn't. I'm working within the boundries of my contract. You hired me to find out what she is. I'm doing that. But the opportunity to work with the 5th great-grand daughter of Morning Star is quite the opportunity. And I'm an opportunist. You know this, M'lady.” Annie didn't change her expression. She knew he was telling the truth. Open contracts were open to interpretation. Josh could almost see Annie making a mental note to flay the person responsible for the contract in this case. ?I'm sorry, Jaymes. But as th' senior officer on this case, I can assure ye tha' yer contract will be invalidated. Ye've jeopardized Ms. Northsky's health with yer antics t'nigh'. ? “I'm not the one who ran my car off the road.” Annie suddenly raised her voice. ?ENOUGH! Ye'll also violated th' rules of workin' with th' Order. Ye've ripped the veil enough t'nigh'!? Suddenly Annie's eyes turned black, no pupils visible. A slight glow, almost as if it was under black light came from them and the noticeably decline in the odor of sulpher dissipated in the air. Josh didn't feel the hold on his ankles anymore from the creature. He came up to his feet, as Jaymes moved around to another position to survey the situation. The mass banishing by Annie was something he wasn't expecting. ?Ah, Lady Leanan, I see you came prepared? Did some studying from your last encounter with my servants?? “They're actually na' tha' 'ard. Remember, I'm a researcher. Once I saw th' bloody things, it was jus' a case o' lookin' 'em up. An' when I found tha' they're actually minor demons, with no real master ou' there, it made it even easier.” She cracked a smirk in his direction. Josh scrambled as they had their stand off and started to gather Dani up in his arms. “Now, Jaymes, let's jus' take care o' things 'ere. Ye've served th' Order well befer. Ye'll get yer payment , an' we'll let ye go. Jus' leave Ms. Northsky alone an' we'll take 'er from 'ere.” Annie took a few steps towards Jaymes, and he showed his nervousness in the case of having the woman from the Fairy world there. He knew that she had some power backing her up. “You see, my payment I’ve chosen this time isn't any of your rare alchemy materials that the Order keeps around for those like me. My payment is your subject. Dani and I have a date with a Las Vegas Minister in about an hour or so.” Josh asked the question upon hearing that. ?A minister?? Jaymes looked at Josh with disdain on his face. “Yes, whelp. A minister. Ms. Northsky is to be my bride. Many decades ago, when Fallen Star cursed me into my current life, I knew that it would be one of his decedents that would help me break my curse. Now that one of his family has taken upon them the Wakan Takan traits, I know what can save me and allow me to have my life back. Once Ms. Northsky and I are joined, and then we consummate... I will be as them in marriage.” Josh hissed at that notion. ?Hey, Moron. How did you plan on getting her to say those magic words of 'I do' when it came down to it? I mean you kidnapped her and all. What a way to woo your future wife. Maybe you aren't that smart after all?? Jaymes chuckled “Actually, whelp. I had that all planned out. Once we were joined under man's law, it would be only the act of consummation that would matter to the spirits. You see, I'm a man with lots of worldly wealth. I do work for others and get paid. And sometimes that payment can allow me to get other things that I would normally not find accessible to me.” Jaymes suddenly reaches under his coat, to an inside pocket and pulls out what looks like a small flask of clay. The markings on it don't look tribal at all. ?Wha' th' 'ell is tha?? Annie asks as Jaymes takes the container and throws it in Josh's direction. The container hits the ground and something that looks like a shimmering comes out of the broken pieces, a cloud hovering in the air, almost like it was full of glitter and starlights. Kaylee comes up behind Josh. ?Bloody Hell!? The shimmering appears to take an almost humanoid shape in front of Josh. Josh holds his ground, as he holds Dani in his arms. The new entity, something not Tribal or Native by a long shot, reaches out and tries to touch Dani. As it gets close, the pendant around her neck, the one that Josh had given her on the sly after some old lady had given it to him... began to glow. The creature's hand couldn't get to close to her when it started. “What the...” Josh exclaimed as he was almost blinded by the light from the pendant. The entity reached out again and it was again prevented from touching Dani. Kaylee stepped in next to Josh and moved to help him if needed. “M'lady, this is an Archon. A possessing demon...” The creature, which Kaylee had identified as an Archon, reached out again, and this time it managed to grab a hold of Dani's arm. The pendant came to life again and what appeared to be not unlike lightening leaped from it and struck the ethereal beast. It howled as the energy coursed through it. With the surprise, Josh let's Dani's legs go, and drops her slightly, Kaylee reaches up to help him, so that he doesn't drop her fully. ?Where th' 'ell did ye get one o' those, Jaymes?? Annie asked him as she moved in closer to the thing. “If you know the right place to buy, you can buy anything.” The pendant continues to defend Dani from the Archon, until it appears to have taken too much. It takes a semi solid form, looking not unlike a night in armor from the medieval world. It lashes out and hits Dani on her back, down by her left kidney area, knocking her with enough force to pull her out of Josh's arms. Josh falls back into the side of the SUV, near the passenger side door. ?Fok, this shite!? Annie stepped up into the way of the creature and floated up off the ground slightly. Her eyes went fully dark, and a sphere of energy formed around her. Her hair whipped around by the wind that didn't exist. Josh looked on as Annie's entire visage changed to that which was her true form. The woman was beautiful, but also dangerous looking. Her sharp feminine business suit changed into a long flowing gown, but the breasts were bare. Leather, and lace mixed into something that looked like it was some fetish ball gown. Her pale skin looked a sharp contrast to it's red and black colors. A pair of small dragonfly type wings came off her back shoulders, denoting her connections to the land of the Faery in the old country of Ireland. ?BACK! I am Lady Aine Danue Breanna Leanan, Lady of Dunabary Castle, Princess o' The Unseelie Court of Tir Na Nog, Chief Loremaster of the Order o' the 'Ammer and personal servant o' the Mistress o' Secrets! I order ye t' back off yer assault on my people. Yer kind 'ave long standin' treaties tha' yer violatin' with this assault on th' woman!? Annie yelled her commands as she, herself, ripped the veil asunder in this location. The Archon backed off a bit, not wanting to get within her area of influence for her magics and glamories. Josh looked down and saw that Kaylee was checking in on the still unconscious Dani. Seeing her in good hands, he opened the passenger side door and reached into the floor board area. He pulled out a small case and ripped the velcro open. Josh pulled out a pair of wooden sticks, each about two and a half feet long. As soon as he had them in hand, he flipped them around to the ready position and then went the long way around the wrecked Jaguar. Josh skulked around, as Jaymes was paying attention to Annie's attempts to hold off the Archon, which appeared to be working so far. As soon as he was in range, Josh reached out with one of the Escrima sticks and tapped him on the shoulders. Jaymes turned his head and ran right into a forearm strike that had the stick going along the arm for an added impact. Jaymes' mouth began to bleed almost instantly. Josh then spun around with both sticks and impacted Jaymes' knee with a loud crunch, dropping the man down for more impact. Josh then spun the sticks in mid air and proceeded to start to strike the downed man in various places, as if he was a practice dummy. Josh got in about 15 really good hits before Jaymes brought his hand up and with a waving motion, was sent back into the car with a thump. Jaymes started to try to come up to his feet, but his knee looked like it couldn't hold any weight on it anymore. As Jaymes moved over and started to use the car for support, he looked up and found himself looking into the black eyes of the Unseelie Mistress. “Jaymes, me dear. Ye know, if all ye were after was the kiss of a pretty lady, all ye 'ad t' do was ask.” ?What?? Annie reached down and grabbed Jaymes' chin. She then planted a full on kiss on his lips. It all looked normal for a bit until she opened her eyes and they were glowing with the black light again. Jaymes opened his eyes shortly after that and his were also glowing with the same negative radiance. Annie let him go, and Jaymes fell back with a silent scream as his eyes returned to normal. As he landed back onto the ground, he started to twitch like he was having a seizure. His mouth gaping and trying to vocalize his mental anguish as the glamorie of the Leanan Faery took it's course, robbing him of his mind, sanity and reason. Kaylee called her mistress over. ?Mistress, we have an issue here!? The vision that was the princess of the Unseelie Courts of the old country changed back to the one that was normal for the realm of earth and she rushed over to Kaylee's side. “Mistress, there is a bone like protrusion coming from the open wound on her back. But it's not left over from the Archon. It's coming from her.” Annie bent down and looked at the open wound on Dani's back, near her left kidney. She shook her head. “That's natural lookin'. But there should'na be a bone there tha' looks like tha'. This event migh' cause a manifestation. We may need t' get 'er t' a nexus tha's actually safe. Get 'er int' the car.” Kaylee, the small woman, moved quickly to pluck Dani's body up off the street and then place it into the back seat of the SUV. As she shut the door, they all heard Josh's next question. ?Uh, guys. These Archon things, what's it take for them to possess you?? Annie turned to him, to answer. ?Ye must be mortal still, na' awakened. So tha' riogh' now leaves me an' Kaylee ou'. Ye also must be asleep. So yer safe. It looks like Jaymes' plan was t' possess Dani an' use tha' time t' get Married. They can only last on this plane fer a few hours. After tha' it would 'ave been getting everythin' else setup... Why?? Josh stood up from where he had hit the car and was looking across the street. ?What bout Garou?? “They're still mortal...” Josh's question hit Annie like a brick as she answered. Her head snapped over to see where the form of Gray Brand was laid out on the sidewalk on the other side of the street. A glow had descended down upon him, and his limbs were beginning to move about slightly. ?Ah, fok!? As Josh stepped up next to Annie, Kaylee shut the door to where she had put Dani. ?So, what can those things do when their inside of someone?? Kaylee was the one to answer. “They gain full control over all bodily functions while they're here. A full powered Archon can last maybe a few hours on this plane before they have to go back.” ?What if they possessed a Garou? Can they change their form to the wolf side?? “Yes.” Josh didn't like that idea as he noticed the creature starting to get Gray's body to a position to get up. Kaylee looked back over her shoulder, they were right in front of a gated YMCA building, with an outdoor pool sitting there. ?Mistress?? Annie turned to look at Kaylee. “I have a plan.” Annie looked at the location that Kaylee was, and nodded her confirmation. Kaylee then turned to Josh. ?Can you drive?? Josh hesitated with the answer. “Uhmm... well I don't have a driver's license.” ?I bloody well didn't ask if you were allowed t' drive. I asked if ye could!? Kaylee got very stern with him. “Yeah, I can drive. They just didn't like me doing a hundred and twenty down Interstate 18, on a mountain, in a corvette...” Kaylee cut him off. ?Good, you're going to be taking Mistress Leanan and Dani to the nexus. You know it as that rock she likes to go to on the reservation. You're driving, so get ready!? Josh nodded, but took a few moments to watch as Kaylee turned a hundred and eighty degrees around and ran right for the gate for the YMCA pool. She didn't stop at the gate, well her body didn't. Her clothes did. Josh watched as the woman seemed to flow around the bars, almost like she was water herself, and her clothes be left behind on the ground. Kaylee ran across the concrete around the pool and then dove in with a perfect no splash dive. Annie broke his shock. “Get ready, Josh. I'll need ye t' drive us ou' o' 'ere as fast as tha' thing will take us.” Josh complied and ran around to the drivers side door. He watched through the windshield as his brother's body stood up and then started to run at Annie, fully starting to change to the half human, half wolf form that was nothing but a killing machine. Gray got within about 10 feet of Annie, when she made a few waving motions with her hands. Gray appeared to keep running, but he went over her, in the air. Almost as if he was running on a bridge that left him falling right into the pool that Kaylee had jumped into. As soon as the man-beast fell in, the pool erupted with activity. A combination of wolf and what appeared to be seaweed or kelp came to the surface a few times. Annie jumped up into the passenger seat of the SUV that Dani owned and slammed her door. ?Alriogh', ye can drive now!? Josh, who had already started the engine, threw the truck into gear and left some rubber on the ground as he peeled out. Annie stumbled to get her seat belt on and grabbed the handle above the door as Josh headed up the road, breaking the speed limit by quite a bit, heading north, for the Paiute Valley of Fire Indian Reservation and Atlatl Rock. Annie was already looking green due to his recklessness and speed... She actually managed to cross herself and looked to be praying a bit for a safe trip. ~End Part III~ ~To Be Continued~
