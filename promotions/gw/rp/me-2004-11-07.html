Me

7 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-7-04 Not many know me. I am a fighter, a charismatic young man on a mission. I am determined and unrelenting. I am making a return, and I'm going to make it big. There are, however, a few who know me. I am one of the most decorated veterans in this company. I have been here since before this company became what it is now. I am a foundation, and I'm not moving for anybody. I'm Derek Frost. This..... is me. When I was just starting out in the wrestling business, I was very young and inexperienced, desperate to find a palce to fit in. UCWF President, Jason Ayers, saw my application on the desk of one of his friends, who was owner of another federation. Ayers had seen me in a few independant shows and saw my potential. He knew he wanted me to join his company. My first match ever in a national company was a non title match against then UCWF Intercontinental Champion, Iceman. Very few remember what happened during the match, but it is the ending that made it so special. My undefeated streak began, only to be attacked post match by my opponent. Later that night, one of the dominant factions in the UCWF was in the ring after one of the members had a match. Johnny Cruse, Shawn, T, and 'The Ladies Man' Jay Robson were then attacked by a handful of rivals. One of their attackers was Iceman. I dashed down the ring, eager to get some vengeance against my opponent from earlier in the night. After the dust settled, I was left in the ring with the men I was fighting alongside. Not only that, I was handed a tshirt. I was a Young Blood. Being part of a group requires giving back. I teamed with one of my newfound friends, Jay Robson, and took off to clench the tag titles. Time went on, as did my undefeated streak. I ended up going on to win the Television title, defend it for a few months before finally having my streak come to a screeching halt in a non title match. No shame in this, however, as Mr. Ayers had personally congratulated me on making such an impact. Not to mention, I still had my Television title. This impact, however, was deadened by two men who continually stopped Jay and I from winning the Tag Team Championship. These roadblocks were the Champions, Salaok and Quartz, collectively known as True Evil. In our time in UCWF, True Evil never once was handed a loss by Cold Blood. Jay and I could not beat them. Then one day, our beloved President Ayers shook our world up. We were merging with another federation, FWL. Everything changed that day. Our roster size doubled, our titles merged with theirs, and our leaders worked together. The Young Bloods stayed a constant. At the PPV where the merging happened, I defeated Duane Gill who was standing in for Diamond D Luxx, to unify the Television Titles. My tag partner unified his Cruiserweight Champion, and together we became the undisputed number one contenders for the Tag Team Championship. More time went on. Before things really settled, I was embroiled in a bitter feud with former FWL star Moneytime. We had many classic matches, including one that was underrated in the Match of the Year standings. We bloodied each other in ladder match, and kept going back for more. Nobody could break us up, and nobody really wanted to. As we were clinging to the ladder, reaching for the title, I jumped for the gold, causing the ladder to tip. Moneytime went down with it, landing on the concrete floor in the audience. I hung from the title, finally able to unsnap it and claim victory. A few weeks later, Moneytime took that title from me. My title reign had come to an end, but not before setting a new record, one that stood for 3 years before being broken. Over time, the Young Blood roster changed. Shawn and T left, allowing us more room to adopt new stars. The most important 'new' recruit was Ben 'Firestarter' Pailthorpe. Ben had been in the original Incarnation of the YB camp. Later on, my girlfriend decided to try her hand at wrestling also. She entered the GWF with the blessing and training of her uncle and my good friend Johnny Cruse. Yes, the world became aware of the fact that I, Derek Frost, had been friends with Cruse for years before joining GWF. In fact, it was my girlfriend's uncle who tipped Ayers off to my status of unemployment. Thanks to Johnny Cruse, my dream had come true; I was a GWF Superstar. Cindy and I became one of the premier couples in GWF. I was past my second TV title reign, had held the Cruiserweight title once or twice at this point, and was well on my way to becoming a main eventer in the company I helped build. She, meanwhile, was winning her first of two Women's titles. To make everything better for us, We had just become engaged. I was marrying my high school sweetheart, girlfriend of four years, coworker, and neice of the man who taught me how to wrestle. The downside to us being in the same business, is that my rivals began to use her to gain an advantage over me. One such example of this is my biggest rival to date, Mark Kullypso. Mark was one of my best friends before he had his mental breakdwon of sorts. Mark and Eliza Gunthree were great friends to Cindy and I. Then Mark suddenly reverted to his alter ego, Kullypso. Cindy and I were separated for a few months because of Kullypso's mind games. He made tried everything to break us up, brainwashing her into thinking she had cheated on me, and using video editing techniques to make it appear so to me as well. In the end of that battle, Kullypso and I met in a 6 level cage match for the Pacific Title, but more importantly, for my beloved Cindy. By the end of that grueling match, Mark and I had both fallen some 60 feet through 6 stories of chain link fencing and broke the base of the ring with our impact. As fate would have it, I ended up retaining my Pacific Championship, and more importantly winning back the love of my then fiancee'. After that match, I was elevated to a new status. Fans saw me as a man who would do anything to protect the ones he loves, and my coworkers began to see me as more of a mainstream competitor, capable of fighting the best of them. I went on to hold the International title for a few weeks, losing it in a tournament to Jason Dynasty. After this, my career sort of topped off. I was feuding with my former tag partner, TLM, and his new group the Mile High Club. I can't say I've really forgiven Jay and Ben for turning their backs on me not once, but twice. After things died down there, they came crawling back to the Young Bloods, which was much appreciated because TUE and The Nevermore were both running rampant on the GW. The YB seemed to be the only group capable of keeping some level of control. Suddenly, everything got turned upside down for me. I found myself having disagreements with staff backstage, as well as disliking the way my career was heading. I went in to talk to my long time rival, co-owner of GW Salaok. He and I agreed that we should have a singles match for his International title before I leave, so we signed a contract. Not only was I booked to face Salaok in a PPV Main Event, undoubtedly the biggest match of my career, but I had received the best news of my life. My wife was pregnant. At Whiplast 2003, I met my tag team rival in our first singles match in the four years we had worked together. By the end of the match, we were dual International Champions as result of a draw, giving me my second International title reign. Not only had we pushed each other to our limits and come up equal, but we shook hands and showed the world our mutual admiration for one another by hugging in the ring. Our celebration was cut short, as TUE decided to come down the the ring. Luckily for Salaok and myself, our respective tag partners, Quartz and TLM were at ringside to even the odds. Salaok and I had our wonderful match, show of respect, and our very first chance to prove to the world that when we put our differences aside, we could be an unstoppable force. Now I am back in GW after a year off to be with my wife, watch my son come into the world, and let my body rest. I came back alongside the man I left with, a close friend of mine. Salaok We are going to prove to the world that we are every bit as good as the rest, if not better. My wife is staying at home with our son Damon, and I wouldn't want it any other way. They are both too precious to me to risk losing. I have so much to lose. One thing I used to take pride in but now simply wish to leave behind, is a nickname I've been given by a few of my old UCWF coworkers. 'Best to never hold the GW World Title' Not many know me, but hopefully have a better idea now. I am a fighter, a charismatic young man on a mission. I am determined and unrelenting. I am making a return, and I'm going to make it big. I am one of the most decorated veterans in this company. I have been here since before this company became what it is now. I am a foundation, and I'm not moving. Not for anybody.
