Get Well Soon

4 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/04/06 'Oh my God! The UCW World Champion just got cheated out of his title!' 'Markus Riley won the title valiantly two weeks before at the Meltdown Pay Per View, but it seems that he's just not the same since then.' 'Agreed. He's been playing things very risky competing with that bad knee, and once Kasey Slade ripped off that brace Markus had been wearing, it seemed as though the champion didn't have it in him to mount a comeback.' 'Brace or no brace, however, that's far from the heart that's needed to fight through the pain to keep that belt. Perhaps that title win of his was nothing more than a fluke after all.' - Diagnosis for: Markus Riley June 30, 2002 On the fourteenth of June, Markus Riley sustained a torn quadricep in his right leg. Failure to approach a doctor immediately gravely worsened the the injury, and has therefore, given me no choice but to recommend that he be unable to compete in Underground Championship Wrestling, and any other wrestling federation for that matter, pending a thorough recovery and rehabilitation process. -Dr. Steven Jacobs - 'She did it! Mindy Mercer has just defeated Train to win the Global World Title!' Three months later... New York Times, Tuesday, October 25 'Former Champion - Soon To Be Mommy?' Some may know teenage superstar Mindy Mercer by shocking the wrestling industry and becoming one of the youngest Global Wrestling World Champions, along with being the first ever female to accomplish such a feat. Now, however, after losing the belt to the man she defeated for the belt, has been seen with her boyfriend Markus Riley, frequenting numerous stores and buying baby clothes and toys. Witnesses say they discovered the couple easily, by the tub of strawberry marshmallow fluff she had been cradling in her arms, and the outlandish giant hat that only Markus seems to wear in New York City. And that's saying a lot. - It's a shame she can't do what she loves anymore. S'almost like me in a way... being told that I can't wrestle anymore. It would almost be the exact same thing as her case... but I don't love wrestling as much as she does. So why the Hell would I risk blowing out my knee again, yeah? Well the answer to that is simple. My fiance loves wrestling, and it kills her not to be able to get back into that octagon and wrestle. And one day, while we're sitting down, watching Adult Swim on Cartoon Network, eating a bunch of chocolate chunk cookies, she turns and tells me... 'The next best thing that I could ask for after me getting back to Global Wrestling.. would be for my cousin Mikey to return instead. And since he's got his own lil' girly to take care of, it wouldn't be right to ask him that.' ..And so that left me. No, I'm not supposed to do it. No, I don't really even want to do it. And no, I couldn't give a shit whether I became a superstar or just showed off that I really do suck from doing it. So why do it? Why put my livelihood at stake again, when I proved to myself that I wasn't cut out for it already when I fucked up my knee? Love makes you do some crazy things sometimes, that's why. Markus Riley, making his Global Wrestling in-ring debut on January 12 at Reload.
