Suprise Suprise Suprise

14 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/14/03 Silence. We sit on the Soma Corp. jet in silence. John Zero, Deacon Holmes and Myself. I sit there drinking a glass of blood, Zero is drinking vodka, as Holmes is drinking a glass of hypnotic. Holmes finally getting upset at the lack of conversation begins talking. “Where are we goin?” Deacon looks Zero. “I don’t know. Where are we goin Soma?” Zero looks to Soma. “Well if you guys must know, Deacon we are going to Japan for two reasons. The first being I felt bad about not getting you a birthday gift, so I have one waiting for you in Japan.” I say with a sadistic smile. “Oh shit for real?” Holmes says smiling. “This could get really interesting really fast.?Zero says quietly. “I’m hoping so, if not Holmes can shoot me again.” The jet begins to land and each man with his entourage of body guards gets off. Each man gets into his car and follows Soma to a large estate about two minutes outside of Tokyo. Reaching higher than about eighty to one hundred meters above the ground, consists of a ground floor surrounded on three sides by stone and six more floors built on top of the stone foundation walls for a total of seven floors. Two pillars, east and west, rise in the center of the castle from the ground floor to the floor of the sixth level (point ninety-five meters wide at the base and twenty-five meters tall). The central support consists of three through pillars, one extending from the ground floor to the second floor, the second pillar from the third to the fourth floors, and the third pillar from the fifth to the sixth floor. The through pillars are held in place by girths that support each floor. The girths and beams on top of the through pillars are inserted into median pillars to unify the three blocks. Other pillars are placed every two meters around the tower; when the median pillars are included, pillars are spaced one meter apart for an extremely sturdy construction. In the corners, diagonal braces as thick as the pillars are inserted to provide resistance to earthquakes. I look to Holmes and smile. “How do ya like your home away from home Deacon” Happy belated birthday man hope ya enjoy it. ? “WHAT!” You go this for me!” MY NEGRO!!” “Damn Soma, can’t wait for my birthday.” Zero says laughing. “You guys go ahead in’There is more surprises in store. Have ya seen that new R Kelly music video with all the asian/black girls in them?” Holmes look to Zero and Zero looks back to Holmes. Holmes take off running into the house, as Zero laughs and walks slowly in. “You coming in Soma?” Zero ask standing in the door. “Nah I have work I need to handle’I’ll be back later though?” I walk back into the car and drive for about thirty minutes. After that I come to a nicely sized house, more modern that Japanese. I get out of the car and walk up to the door. Knock. Knock. A small yet attractive woman in her mid forties opens the door. I look at her and smile and she gives me a peculiar look. “Do I know you?” The woman asked meekly. “Um yes and no. I use to be your husband’s boss. I am Leinad Soma, Mrs. Rokku. It is a pleasure to meet you. “Oh”.please come in. What can I do for you Mr. Soma ?” “Have you seen your husband lately Mrs. Rokku?” “No’not at all. Why what has he done?” “Nothing at all” I must speak with him’Its about your son?” “Why” What’s wrong with Akodo?” “Nothing. He has just bit on more than he can chew is all.” She looks at me and then takes a closer look. “You’re Leinad Soma, the wrestler too.” She said looking a little angry. “Yes, that I am.” “You’re also a’a?” She steps back as she tries to finish her sentence. “Yes’Mrs. Rokku, I am. But don’t fear.” “Mr. Soma I don’t fear you I loath you. You are the reason my husband was not in my son’s life and now you are trying to destroy my son. Why are you trying to destroy my family Mr. Soma?” Tears begin to swell in her eyes. I look at her and move closer to her. The fear and the anger become stronger in her eyes. I look over her body and I think to myself she is gorgeous for her age. I smile at her “Mrs. Rokku’I’m not here to destroy your family’on the contrary. I am here to bring it back together again.” I smile as I pick up my cell phone. I dial a number and put the phone to my ear. “Send him in?” A man walks into the door behind me. He is quite tall and doesn’t look a day over twenty-four. The man is quite muscular, long blonde hair, green eyes, dressed in a black suit. Mrs. Rokku stands there in utter shock. She begins to walk toward the man then stops as the man smiles’Fangs showing from his mouth. “May I introduce the new and improved version of your husband Mrs. Rokku’I want you to tell your son that his father will be at his next match. We will bid you adieu.” I walk out and the man follows me he gets into the limo. We begin to ride back to the estate and once we get there women are laid out everywhere. I stand outside the doors as I see Zero on one of the couches with a couple of women and Holmes on the other with a party hat that says “Birthday Boy” on it and about twenty women all over him. I smile and tell Mr. Rokku to stay here. I walk outside and begin to walk around the estate. I smile after the events of tonight. I sit out in the gardens and look up at the night sky. “Soma two”.Rokku one?” I laugh slightly and sit there looking up at the sky in silence.
