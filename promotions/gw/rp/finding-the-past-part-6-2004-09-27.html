Finding the Past (Part 6)

27 September 2004

Outfit ~ Josh: Calvin Klein: Black Calf Jacket DKNY: Charcoal Cotton Flight Suit DKNY: Black Leather Gloves DKNY: Norm Boots Hugo Boss: Titanium Shield Sunglasses Ken quickly shook off a chill as he walked down the busy New York City Street with fellow Elite member Josh. The two still had the fresh taste of the Thai food which they had just dined on in their mouths and trying their best to accomadate the groups that would crowd them from time to time in search of an autograph or photo-oportunity... or both. Ken was having trouble keeping the smile off of his face as the fans flocked to the former International Champion and current World Champion on the sidewalk. His renewed sense of faith in the fans and in himself had made a huge difference in his attitude towards life in general. It was causing a turn-around in his ring work as well, as the KPJ of old was beginning to take-shape in the octagon once more. Hopefully, his success rate would pick up soon due to this, and he could think of no better place for it to begin than the Global Cup Tournament. He realized that it would be no walk in the park, as the best the world had to offer in GW was going to be in the ring with him at the same time vying for the same prize. However, for Ken, it was much more than a large golden trophy with his name engraved on it. It was the come-back from his current state, and new life breathed into the career that many, including KPJ himself, was beginning to believe was winding down. 'What's on your mind,' questioned Josh as he finished scribbling his name upon an 8x10 photograph of himself holding the Cruiserweight Championship belt high over his head at a previous Pay-Per-View. 'You look a bit troubled,' he finished as he handed the picture back to the small girl that had handed it to him. 'A million things,' responded Ken, signing his own John Hancock to a similar photo of him holding the International Title. 'Mostly the Global Cup, though,' he followed up as the small girl mentioned previously grabbed back her autograph with a smile, finishing up their current cluster and leaving the two some time to speak. 'You know, Josh, less than a year ago, I was riding high... holding that International Championship in the air for everyone to see and renewing a vigor that hadn't been seen in me for some time. I was damn near unbeatable, save one man... and I couldn't even muster it up in myself to give him a decent match as wrestled probably the worst I ever had. Since then it's been a downward spiral. I had a short stint as Television Champion, but I was to caught up in my own head to give it the proper respect which it deserved. Now, here I am, with no title and on a losing streak, and I don't even have the memory of what it felt like to be one of the best anymore. But, now, I have the chance to remember. I have the chance to remember what it's like to not only be the best... but to be the best in the world.' 'Has anything come back, Amigo,' asked Josh, somewhat grimly. 'I mean... have you gotten any new memories?' Ken was quiet for a bit as he closed his eyes, allowing the brisk Autumn breeze to tickle his face and play with his hair as the two resumed their walk. As he did so, a million different feelings and senses and memories came to him all at once, each one more pleasant than the next, but none of which new. His smile widened as he saw a beautiful vision of Gunner laughing in front of a sunset... it was the last thing that he saw before opening his eyes. 'Some things, I never forgot. You and our friendship was never erased from my mind, nor was my friendship with Ice, Bill, Dean, or Shawn for that matter. The memories of Todd and Stephanie remained with me and, unfortunantly, so did their deaths. I receive new and more glorious memories of Gunner daily... but the one that remains with me the most is that she is with Ely now. And, lately, well...' Ken paused, his smile disappearing as the two approached the building that housed his penthouse home. As they walked up the stairs to the fornt doors, the silence remained, and so did Josh's intrigue. They both greeted Pierce, the doorman, as he tipped his hat and held open the door for them to enter. As they got closer to the elevator, Ken pulled Josh aside and began to speak in a whisper. 'I've been having more and more intense memories regarding my accident, Josh, and it's kind of beginning to worry me. I come to from them, and I have no idea where I am, how I got there, and how long I've been out.' 'Jesus, Ken,' responded Josh with a true sense of urgency before lowering his voice to the same tone as his best-friend's, 'you're blacking out from you memories?' 'Only the ones regarding the wreck,' admitted Ken quickly. 'There almost more like dreams... or nightmares. I don't know what to make of them. It's all so real until the end, until I see the truck. It's all real until I look into the rearview mirror and see a familiar face before getting hit. The strange this is, while I know the face, it's blank and dark... but it's evil. Josh... I think there was more to this than your everyday hit and run.' 'You're saying,' began Josh before getting cut off by Ken. 'I think somebody was trying to kill me.'
