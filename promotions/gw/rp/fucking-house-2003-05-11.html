Fucking House Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #192 Date: 5-11-03 1:53 A.M Location: Home ((I Take A Good Hard Look Around. There Is Nothing. Nothing About This House. Nothing About These Clothes. Nothing. There Is Nothing In This Whole Space That Means Anything Any More. Walking Around A Shambled House. Pretty Much All My Doing. I Walk Past The Room That Laura And I Shared. I Walk Past The Ring That Sam Slept In. Not Three Feet Away The Room Were Angel Slept. The Doors Kicked Off Of All Three. I Look Down The Hall At The Living Room. You Can See The Love Seat Is Flipped Over On The Big Screen TV That Is Laid Down Face Front On The Floor. I Take Three Steps And Am Standing There. Interestingly Enough The Phone Is Ringing. I Grab The Phone And Yank It To My Ear With One Thought In Mind. 'hello, This Is Tela Zapper. Hate Annoying...' If It's Not Her I Don't Want It. Then I Throw The Phone Through The Glass Door That Sits In Front Of The Flipped Over Breakfast Table. In The Kitchen That Lays Not To Far From The Living Room. There Are About Twenty Knives Stuck In The Stand Alone Freezer. The Fridge Is Face Down. Every Cabinet Door Is Ripped Off And Slung Ever Which Way. The Computer In The Den Is Slung On The Floor. As I Walk To It. I Have More Then A Hatred In My Head. I Walk By And Kick It. Putting My Foot Through The Side. Cutting Through My Think Leather Boots. There Is Blood On My Red 'hatebreed' T Shirt And Red Adidas Rip Off Pants. The Only Thing In My House That Is Not Messed Up Is A Radio That I Have Full Intent To Fuck Up In Every Which Way. As I Walk By The Bath Room I See The Towel Rack. I Take A Step Inside, Over The Door And Rip It Off The Wall. The Mirror In There Is Not Broken So I Say What The Fuck. I Sling The Rod At It About Six Times And Break Every Particle Out Of It. I Step Out And Over The Door Again. As Walk I Brandish The Rod As If It Were My Key To Life. At The Point It Is. If I Dont Mess Things Up. I'll Just Commit Suscide. I See The Green Radio Sitting There. It Was Used For Listening In My Work Out Room. There Wasn't Anything In There Now. I Was Working Out When I Got The News. After I Hung Up The Phone I Began To Throw Weights Into The Walls. Now There Was Nothing Left But A Few Holes And A Radio That I Left. For What Reason. I'm Really Not Sure But It Was Still There. I Stood And Looked At It. A Memory Popped Into My Head. I Heard Laura's Voice. 'honey. I Got You Something.' From My Solid White Work Out Room I Spoke Back To Hear. 'what Is It?' God. It Was This Radio But I Diddent Know At The Time. I'm Glad She Got It For Me. If She Diddent I Wouldn't Have Made It This Far. I Diddent Know What I Was Going To Do After I Broke This. Maybe Fuck Up The Camreo. I Would Fuck Up Her Little Green Honda Civic Car But It Was Gone. For Good. 'I Fucking Hate You!!!!!!!!!!!!' I Yell This As I Beat The Little Green Radio. It Turns On And 'fucking Hostile' Is Playing On The Cd I Left In. I Leave It Be. Good Song. Lucky Fucking Radio. I Walk Away. As I Walk To The Gragei Think Quite A Bit About Every Thing That Has Happedn The Last Few Hours. First I Get A Doctors Call That Sam Is In The Hospital. Then About Twenty Minuets Later I Get Another Call Saying That My Bank Account Has Been Drained. Then. I Get A Call From Laura. It Went Something Like This. 'divorce. I Hate You. Your Never Home. I Hate You. I Hate You. I Fucking Hate You. Never Ever Try And Find Me. I Have Enough. I Have Angle And You Know Were Sam Is. The Car. Good Fucking By.' I'm Seething As Think Of The Recap. Now I Am Standing At The Grage Door. With A Lightning Fast Kick It Flys Off The Hinges Into The Floor. Both Cars Are Parked Out Side. Inside The Hummer Is About Enough Gas To Burn The Whole Fucking Place Down. The Camero Is What I'm Interested In Though. I Walk Quickly To The Grage Door And Deliver Kick After Kick Untill It Starts To Cave. I Know That I'm Not Going To Break The Door. Kicking It Makes Me Feel Better. I Stop For A Second And Press A Bottom On The Side Of The Wall. The Door Wont Go Up All The Way. My Fault. I Kicked A Dent Big Enough To Sit In Almost. I Grab It. The Door Is About At My Waist. I Begin To Open. Giveing Me Just Enough Room To Get Under. I Only Have To Bend My Neck Slightly. I Walk To The Car And Open The Door. Inside Sits A Pack Of Pall Mall Smokes And A Set Of Matches. I Take The Two Items Out And Take Another Item Out Of The Pall Mall Box. As I light a smoke i don't put the match out. I put it in the Box. Instantly it begins a flame. I open my Gas Door and throw it inside. I know i have to get away fast. I don't though. I don't care any more. I don't care. Nothing matter any more. Not winning., Not losing. Not being a champion. Not even dieing bothers me any more. There is nothing. I've lost my wife and daughter. My Son is very Ill. He's the only reason that i back up more then three feet and don't get in the car. I sit there and watch as the car explodes The hummer gets a small scratch on it that I find later. I look around at the house. Shambles. The front door is kicked off. almost every window is broken. There are a few holes in a part of the house. Weights little the ground outside. There is a Car burning in the drive way. A Silver and Blue hummer sitting close to it. Now that i look around memoirs and words fly through my head. The word Hate more then others. The word love not far behind that. I have no money. I have no wife. I have no daughter. The only thing i have is a very sick on which i have to go see. I would have already been there is the doctors hadn't had to oprate on it. Fuck. Sam i hope your all right. My world was falling down around my fucking ankles and I have a match with Derek Frost as my Tag team Partner. Then i have a match Facing his former or still tag team Partner in Cold Blood. TLM. I'm out of the world title tourney. I knew some one had to be. I don't mind. sage is one of the better here today. What the fuck. why am thinking about work. These thoughts soon leave my head. The only one that remains is the one that says Die. I have to begin o dismantle something. Anything. I remember that there is Gas in the Hummer. I have such a sick idea. I'm not even going to question my Motives. I'm just acting now. i open the hummer. I can feel the heat coming from the Camreo. I grab two of the gas jugs in one hand and two in the other. Just enough i think to my self as i walk to the house. When i get to the front i open one of the gas jugs. I begin to pour some just as i walk into the house. walking first around the living room and kitchen. I run out. I open another and begin to pour. The Weight room and the dining room. Next of the bed rooms. When i am finished with this small task I begin to look around for a lighter. I find one. It has Quartz on the side of it. define Irony. I'm about to burn a house down with a lighter that has the face of one of my next opponents. That was irony. I diddent care to much about that. when the Nevermore was involved i diddet get to much of a chance in the matter any ways. Plus i had Derek Frost as my Tag team partner. If i ever got tagged into this match. it would tickle the shit out of me. again i find my self thinking of work when i should be throwing this lighter down light and burning this place of memoirs that would only bring rage and cost me more because i would vbreak every thing in this house every day if i lived in it. every day until i got to this point and burnt it down again. I'm a very compulsive person. So i throw the Lighter down after i light it. I finish off my smoke and throw it down into the flame. it has now reached the dining room and has only three more to go. The bed room of my self and Laura. The bed room of Sam and then the one of Angel. I watch this as it happens. I don't care to much any more. I've thought about standing here and letting this shit burn down around my damn knees. I cant do it. Sam needs me. I must go now. Leave this god forsaken place. yes. Leave. every to come here again. Fin!
