This Monster Lives

24 November 2004

Date: 11/24/04 The highly trafficked hallways of the Global Arena had began to clear after their relentless parades of humanity. The parking lot outside began to look like the usual apocalypse horror movie that it always seemed to mimic. Backstage inside the arena, few people still stuck around... some didn't have much of a choice. While some were forced to stay behind by Bubba McCoy, others didn't have the ability to walk out on their own just yet. The door to the medical station swung open and bounced off the white concrete wall. Out walked Christian Zenith, with black cargo bag hanging over his right shoulder. Christian held his ribs a little bit as he closed the door with his foot. The door slammed shut, averting the attention of some of the stragglers backstage. Zenith had a very slight limp as his footsteps echoed down the concrete hallways. Some attempted to stop and stare but just one peer into Zenith's cadet blue eyes forced them to continue on with their business. Well that was except for one person... 'Hey Zenith!' Christian heard a voice yell from the other side of the hallway. Zenith quickly stood up straight and made eye contact with that man, well... loosely a man, it was Mark Bishop. Bishop stood with two GW Officials, he didn't until he grabbed Zenith's attention anyway. The officials quickly made their way towards the exit as Bishop made his way over to Christian Zenith. 'Nice job out there, but let me give you some advice kid. If you know... actually win some matches, you might be around here for a couple months.' Bishop said to Christian, with a bit of arrogance. Zenith's hands were balled into fists as he cocked his head a bit at Mark Bishop's ignorant comment. 'Just some expertise from someone who has been around here a while. If you take it you'll do well around here, but if you just keep doing what you've been doing around here... I might starting filling out job applications, but that's just me.' Bishop said as he turned around to leave. Bishop suddenly bumped into what at first seemed to be a brick wall. Mark lost his balance and landed on his ass on the floor. Bishop looked up at Demitri Miaskinov with his eyes wide open. 'Pardon me, I didn't see you there...' Bishop said, trying to avoid the glare from the 7 foot monster. Mark got up quickly and turned to see Jason LeMaster to his left... and Vivica J. Valentine blocking his path on the only remaining side. Bishop adjusted his glasses and suddenly became claustrophobic as he peered around at the wall of athletes surrounding him. 'You know Mark, you've always kind of looked like a rat. But when you're trapped like this... your rodent side really shines through.' Christian Zenith said, after Mark Bishop gave up any ideas of trying to escape. 'If this is about the winning comment, I didn't mean it that way. I meant you look like you SHOULD be winning more matches, and I can't figure out for the life of me why you keep losing. You must be royally getting screwed over around here or something...' Bishop said out of desperation. 'Let me give you some advice Mark.' Vivica J. Valentine said with a smile, 'I've been around Christian for a while now, and well if you want to live for lets say... another 30 or 40 years, I'd recommend shutting your smart ass mouth before we pull it over your head.' 'Thank you Vivica but I'm not sure if we can do that to mark...' Jason LeMaster said, 'We'd need a proctologist to remove his head from his ass first.' 'Now that wasn't really necessary...' Bishop interrupted, 'Now if you'll excuse me, I really do have to get going. I have a... reservation! For... dinner with my girlfriend.' 'Awww, that's a shame Mark.' Vivica replied, 'We'll make sure to cancel for you.' Next thing Mark Bishop knew, he saw nothing but white. The smell of hickory and rain consumes your senses. Soon the scene begins to come into focus, and you see a bright crescent moon shining down upon you. You sit up slowly in confusion, unaware of where you are or how you got here. A chorus of noises seem to greet you into their home. A somewhat deafening roar of a swarm of crickets averts your attention somewhat as you look around and find yourself in what appears to be a forest of some sort. The hooting of an owl seems to alert the others that your presence is welcoming. You look up, to see where the owl may be, but the darkness clouds your vision. You get up to your feet slowly, the leaves cry and the branches break under your weight. Leaves cover your chest and legs, but are very simply brushed off, as you stagger a bit slightly off balance. How long had you been lying there? Certainly there had to be a reason why or a time when you became engulfed in wilderness. Suddenly a sound echoed through the trees that was unfamiliar to your eyes. A roar of some kind of animal, beast, or perhaps monster. Beginning to walk slowly away from the roar, trying to not make too much noise as you move, it suddenly dawns upon you that perhaps not all woodland creatures may be so friendly. Who knows what kind of wild animal wonders through these woods? Surely you don't. You stop at the thought, only to head you're not the only one trudging through the woods. The snapping of branches and the ruffling of leaves grows closer and closer. You can't pinpoint the direction it's coming from, you spin around looking in every direction. The ruffling seems to crescendo towards you as you pick a direction and run. Through the woods you scurry, but the other being seems to be on your heels. Unexpectedly a stump jumps out of the ground and catches your foot, sending you sliding and somersaulting through the leaves that cover the dirt floor. The creature draws right upon you, as you see it coming down the path. It scatters away in fear when you once again brush all the leaves off of your body. Scared of a raccoon in fear, snap out of it you tell yourself. Every forest has been explored except for the rainforest, and you could already clearly see this wasn't one. There isn't anything in the woods that wasn't already captured and studied. Surely the most horrifying thing you could come across was a coyote at this time of night. The important thing after all is, how in the hell are you going to get out of here? You get up and continue to walk along the dirt path. The moon guides your way along the newly found dirt path. Finally your eyes were beginning to adjust, and that of course will speed this process along quite a ways. Navigating through the tress, you felt like a mouse trying to find cheese in some sort of experiment. A grimly vivid reminder behind you encourages you to pick up the pace, in the form of that familiar roar. Your walk becomes a jog, and your jog becomes a run as you shove branches out of your way down the path. A couple branches swipe you on the arms on the way by, perhaps scratching you, but you didn't care... you wanted to get the hell out of there. Thankfully, the cheese was right in front of you, up a ways in the distance you could see an opening. An empty grass field it appeared to be, hopefully there was a road nearby you could get a ride home. Maybe not, but at least you'll feel a bit safer along the roadside than you would in the maze of random trees. You break into the opening and look around with your hopes high. Trees... Trees... nothing but trees! How could this be, this field is nothing but a circle of grass in the middle of an unending wilderness! As you walk into the middle of the opening, you begin to hear footsteps not your own. You stop to listen as they seem to come from all around you. Looking around you see nothing... surely they have to be coming from somewhere, you're in the middle of an opening. You must be going crazy, all this time in this forest has surely driven you insane! 'HELP!' you scream into the darkness, 'I can hear you! PLEASE come out and help me!' Your screams echo back at you off of the trees, and also silence the footsteps. In frustration you drop down to your knees and begin to pound away at the ground with your fist. As you hit the ground in frustration, a chorus of clapping surrounds you, as if you are some kind of cruel and demented show for someone. Standing up you look up, and then spin around trying to find out where it is coming from. 'STOP THAT!' you yell in frustration, 'I don't know who the hell you are or what you want from me. But I am NOT here for your amusement and I DEMAND that you stop this at once!' At your request suddenly an orange dot, roughly the size of the head of a nail, sails over your head and lands about 5 yards in front of you. With it's landing, a huge fire bursts out of the ground. The shock of the flame and the wave of heat send you stumbling away for your own safety. I think it was worthy of an Academy Award.' A man said as he walked around the flame. The gold light of the flame flickers off of his black suit jacket. A smile crosses his lips as he claps at your expense. The fire reflects off of his black sunglasses, giving an illusion that he was perhaps Satan himself... or the Devil's Advocate at least. He adjusts his black tie underneath his black suit vest as you grimaced at his sight. 'What in the hell do you want from me Zenith?!' You screamed, feeling like a puppet at his disposal. With those words suddenly you were grasped from behind and wrestled to the ground, a 3-person controls your every move. You try to break free, struggling with every ounce of strength you have... Everything is planned through, everything is accounted for, there is NOTHING that I can see that I haven’t already seen come to fruition up here you see?' Christian Zenith spoke as he pointed at his brain. It wasn't long before you found yourself bound helpless in the grasp of an unforgiving strait jacket. Buckled and stood up, you had little choice but to listen to him speak... 'Zenith if this is about...' you tried to speak when suddenly your mouth was covered by a huge hand. 'You don't talk unless I ask you to fool.' Zenith responded as you were being gagged by the same large hand, 'Don't you dare interrupt me, because I won't think twice about setting you ablaze in that fire. As you've seen in the seclusion of this forest, no one will ever find you so just sit back and relax, and enjoy my company. If you do that for me stooge, I may let you live.'
