The history of The Blue Bat.

27 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 27 The scene opens up to the inside of a nuclear power plant. On the 1st floor of the plant there are about 20 rooms covering the side of the walls. On the 2nd floor are more than usual because there are 50 rooms covering the walls. At the top floor there is only one room in particular. On the plate in the window is a name, the name reads “Jack Acid.” Inside the room is a man sitting on a brown leather chair writing on a white lined piece of paper. Suddenly a man walks into the door looking all confused. Man: Jack, we need help! Jack: What, what happened? Man: One of our workers accidentally hit the danger button! Jack: Oh dear GOD! Suddenly Jack gets up from his chair and him and the man run out of the room down flights of stairs. At the 2nd floor the door opens and Jack and the worker are seen entering the door. Inside the 2nd floor is a big room where probably 100 workers are seen pushing buttons and getting stuff ready. Jack and the worker run over to a glass window and look inside it. Inside the window is a man laying on the floor shaking. Jack then opens the door and helps the man to his feet. Jack then runs back out of the room and yells to the other people. Jack: Everybody get out of this building now! The Grey cans are going to explode in the next couple of hours. Go save your families, I’ll stay and try to turn it off. Everybody in the room start going crazy all running in a different direction. Jack and his worker start undoing their ties they had on and start running over to the door they came in. They run back up the stairs and open the 4th floor door. In the fourth floor there are about 3 Grey cans with flesh burning acid inside them. Jack and his worker run over to the cans and push the button on it. Jack: This will only hold it off for a couple more hours. Jack suddenly hears a cracking noise inside all of the cans. Then the cans tip over with all the acid falling out. Jack’s worker suddenly runs out of the room and out of the building. Inside the room Jack starts running but cant get to the door and the acid pours all over him. A couple of minute’s later Jack’s worker comes back into the room looking shocked. But from the ground where Jack lays, he gets up. His worker gets all freaked and tries to run out of the room but Jack grabs him by the waist and punches him in the face. Jack rises from the ground and takes his worker again and kicks him in the face. Jack: YOU LEFT ME! You left me with the acid! Worker: I’m so sorry. Jack: Sorry is not enough. Sorry to do this but I’m going to have to kill you. Jack grabs the throat of his worker and chokes him to death. Jack then starts turning blue and starts getting red eyes. His body turns into a coat of blue armor, with him walking out of the room. At the top floor Jack is seen walking into his office. He sits down onto his chair and takes a sip of his coffee. Jack: Stupid worker. Oh WELL! I’m back and stronger than ever! You no what, I’m going to call myself The Blue Bat! Jack then looks on his desk and sees a picture of Max Roswell with a baby in his hand. Jack spits onto the paper and throws it into the garbage. Jack then gets up and looks through the window in his office to the carnage where the acid came. Jack: Pfft, This Roswell guy thinks he is the best. I bet you I could beat him. You know what, I’m going to visit this Roswell guy. The Blue Bat then makes himself blue and jumps out of the window smashing it. Outside the building Jack is seen soaring through the sky using nothing to hold him up. The people on the ground are all looking confused and wondering how he does that. From the sky a blue ball falls blowing up half of the street. The people on the ground start throwing rocks and hard rubber trying to hit the flying guy. Then The Blue Bat stops at a big building which is The Presidents Inn. It is the hotel where Roswell is staying. The Blue Bat then starts looking in all of the windows trying to find Roswell’s room. The Blue Bat stops trying to find the window and makes it so he isn’t blue. Jack jumps down from the top of the hotel and walks into the lobby doors. Inside Jack walks over to the brown counter and asks the lady what room Roswell stays in. The lady says 11th floor room number 1524, from the counter Jack walks over to the elevator and pushes the white button. Jack walks inside the elevator and walks back out of the 11th floor. Jack walks down to the back of the hall and finds the room 1524. Jack knocks on the door but there wasn’t an answer. Jack then knocks on the door again but there wasn’t an answer again. Jack gets so mad that he kicks the door making it break. Jack walks inside the room and sees the bathroom light on. Jack knocks on the door of the bathroom. Jack: HELLO! Get out here now! Roswell: Who are you? What do you want with me? Jack: Just get out here! Suddenly the bathroom door opens and Roswell walks out with a towel wrapped around his waist. Roswell then ignores Jack and walks over to his dresser and puts some clothes on. Jack walks over to Roswell and sits down on his bed to stretch. Jack then walks over to Roswell and shoves him into the TV. Roswell: Hey what was that for! Jack: You don’t know who I am do you? Roswell: No, and I frankly don’t care. Jack: Well you should care. Because I was the one who shot Father Clark! Roswell: What! Roswell then punches Jack in the face and smashes his head through the TV. Glass shatters all over the floor but Roswell ignores that to. Jack then turns around and gives Roswell a low blow. Jack then punches Roswell in the nose making him bleed. Jack: You want to try to ambush me again. Try ME! Roswell: What do you want with Father Clark and me. Jack: What do I want? The question is what do you want. Roswell: What do you mean? Jack then grabs Roswell by the shirt and throws him through the window to the pool area. Glass shatters all over the poolside, one even touching a little girl. Jack starts laughing at Roswell, who is lying on the ground. The people in the pool start looking at Roswell wondering what happened.
