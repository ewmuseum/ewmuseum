Following Traditions

6 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/06/05 Sometimes you don't know the full story... Sometimes there are things that are forgotten in the annals of time, things that make and define a man. Things that shine through to show what and who they really are. Sometimes they're good. Sometimes they're not so good. One upon a time there was a man named Mike Marvelous. A man who had climbed to the pinnacle of another federation. A former Primetime Central Extreme Champion, a former TV level and Tag Team Champion. A man who stood a good chance at being a world level champion. But then something happened. He had joined a stable that become one of the most dominant factions in that other federation. But one day he thought of something other then himself in a match. He put one of his stablemates over. That stablemate then turned on him. When the dust had settled though, Mike Marvelous stood tall, and was ready to claim that which he felt he should have had. Then he ran into a stint of bad luck, and for some reason he decided to take his proverbial ball and go to someplace else? Why would these issues need to be brought up here? Why would the recent history of one Mike ?Marvelous? Biddle need to be brought up? It's because about the time he was leaving from that other federation, Blaise McClancy had just arrived there. And before anyone tries to play connect the dots... there is a truth to be told. This coming week, Mike Biddle, now having been in Global for a few months or so... has a challenge ahead of him that some could bill as a battle of their careers. Mike Biddle vs. Blaise McClancy. Biddle was a star in the other place that McClancy just came from. He was a big time player. The biggest road block he ever faced was a man with the last name of McClancy, Blaise's cousin, Sean. A man who took that PTC Extreme Title from Biddle. Then after retiring, about the same time that Biddle left that last company, Sean McClancy stepped in as the President. There are some that have hinted that the McClancy rule over there drove Biddle away. There are some that are looking at this match and salivating at the thoughts of another Biddle vs. McClancy fight. Would Biddle get one over the family now, or would he fall to someone who's been one of their most successful? Well while Biddle deserves all of the respect in the world... whoah? Did you just see that? Yeah, Biddle deserved respect... who am I to argue with a family tradition? Mike Biddle started his downfall with a McClancy on the other side of an old Irish Pub. It'll continue on his slide down with a McClancy helping to push again. Mr. Howell? Movies? Being 'given' contenderships when you can barely win one? What's happened to him? Mike Biddle is a shadow of his former self. He's no longer anything near the almost world level champion he could have been. Let alone that 'wrestler of the year' accolade he will remind you of if given an opportunity. However, for a man who was, or maybe still is, considered a contender for the Global International Championship... he's got a target on his back right now. A target that's going to get hit. Mike Biddle must go down again. He's nothing but a facade riding on past glories. While it's sad that it would have to come down to this, destiny is a cruel mistress. Mike Biddle's destiny is to come face to face with a man of a family that has had his number in the past when he needs to bring about a change to his record in the biggest way. 35% win rate in Global. It's pathetic compared to what it once was. ^^^^^Last Friday, November 4th, 2005^^^^^ The sounds of Shinedown's newest song, “Save Me” starts to dye down and fade out on the speakers inside the room. A small woman with very short hair, currently dyed platinum blond sits in one of the chairs with the sound board in front of her as well as a series of microphones. ?And we're back here on K-O-M-P-92-point-three, the rock station! And you're back with me... CAR-LOTA and I've got a special guest coming into the studio with me today. Sitting with me here is none other then Las Vegas' own hometown wrestling hero, Blaise McClancy! How's it going Blaise?? Blaise is seated across from her, in a chair with only a microphone setup dangling down into his face. “I'm doing good. Good to be back on KOMP for a little chit chat.” Carlota, the small woman with strange tastes that she is rather open about while working as the midday disc jockey at one of only two Las Vegas modern rock stations smiles as she flips a few questions. KOMP though, unlike 'X-Treme Radio' isn't afraid to play some of the older rock too... and doesn't have the same 20 songs they play over and over again. ?Well Blaise, things have recently changed for you. You've moved away for a while, admittedly while with another federation. But in the last few months you've come back and you're not wrestling out of the Global Hotel and Casino up in North Las Vegas, just a little bit out of the Metro area. And you've now got a few new friends, right?? Blaise sighs, knowing full well how these interviews normally go. “Well, those new friends are actually part of the reason why I relocated back down here. It's inevitable when you have a group sponsored by the different academies that when they prove to be worth their salt that they get the offer to bear the Flaming Horse in their motif. However this one is special, in the fact that I've been granted it's leadership role... and I'm the only real ego trip in the bunch. But that's neither here nor there. I've got a good crew here, and they'll prove the Brigade well.” He adjusts in his seat as Carlota continues on. ?So, let me take a look-see at what you've got going on this week. Oh my! Mike Biddle! Global has made the dream match for those who have followed you and your cousin, Sean McClancy, now haven't they?? Now Blaise really sighs. ?Let me guess where you're about to go? Biddle will want to beat me so that he can get a win over a McClancy under his belt. And how am I going to live up to the legacy. There are also the nasty rumors about why he left our former federation that we both share. One that he ran when I came in, which was quite the coincidence. There are also some who say that he left because Sean took over.? He stops for a breathe, Carlota just lets him continue. “But truth be told... He left for partially the same reasons that I did. The same person...” Carlota takes the opportunity to chime in. ?The same person?? “Yeah, the same person. The same person who kept telling the others that he'd NOT fight me and went out of his way to ensure that even when booked, he'd not fight me. That person was the World Champion or equivalent at the time, and he pretty much didn't want to fight Mikey Marvelous, either.” ?You going to give me a name?? “Nope, but I'll give you a hint. I was sooooo freakin' relieved when I found out that Ukyo's family had adopted him instead of him being genetically related to her.” Blaise gives a smile that can almost be heard over the radio airwaves. ?So, are you saying that you have something along the lines of respect for Mr. Biddle?? ?Yeah, he's done good things in this business. He's a class act sometimes. But I know that lately he's been kinda bland. He's always been a man to take shortcuts if they come up, but other then that, contrary to popular belief, there is a reason why he's a high rated talent. But, lately I've seen the beginning of his downfall. Unfortunately for him, I'll have to uphold a family tradition and help him on his way back down to the opening acts in Global.? Carlota laughs. ?So you are going to do the traditional wrestling thing and claim you're going to give him an ass whoppin' in the ring?? ?It's clich’d, yes... but I'm not here to play around with bad actors. I think that's the one thing that is going to kill him. He's more concerned with his image anymore with the movies and that. I'm a third generation wrestler, but I'm also a trained fighter. I'm not here to have my picture taken or try to impress some third rate Hollywood company that they need to cast me in a movie. I'm here to work my way up to the top. Biddle just basically lost his international contendership. Maybe he still has it, I don't know. I just know that he's recently really become a disappointment to anyone who followed him over the last few years. I'm just coming to Global and I know I'm the #2 contender for the Platinum title. Could I take the Platinum Belt? I have no doubt in my mind that if you put me against Virgil Ambrosi that I'd be able to kick his teeth in. But first KPJ has his shot coming up. And I've got Biddle. I can't allow myself to fall prey to someone like Biddle. A man who my cousin flushed his head into a toilet in an Irish pub... respect or not, I've got to beat him. I know this, and he knows this too.? Carlota cuts in. “Well, I've got to play some music here, and when we come back I'm going to have at least one person from U2 on the phone with me here. A good Irish band. You going to stick around? They're in concert this weekend at the MGM Grand Garden Arena.” Blaise doesn't hesitate. “Ah, for something like that I think I will. I know that a few of them have admitted to being Wrestling fans before. And with me being the first of my family born over here, the rest are pretty much right from the same area in the Olde Country.” With that Carlota hits a button and the sounds of Staind begin to fill the studio. ^^^^^Fast forward a few hours.^^^^^ Blaise's Chevy Avalanche rolled up into the driveway of his Queensridge home. The automatic garage door opened up to his car based on the small transmitter located in the front grill, making it a non-necessity for him to even hit a button. As his rear bumper passed a certain point, the door started to silently creep back down to the closed position. He climbed out and made his way passed Ukyo's new black Chrysler Crossfire SRT6 Roadster in black with the soft top on it. Silently he slipped into the house and found no one there to greet him. Straining his ears he heard what he then expected. A week ago they had moved the two girls to an upstairs room with a full layout for the girls as they started to get older. Everything Blaise had done recently for those two little red-heads had been in preparation to get granted full rights to keep his daughters. Especially with the now criminal cases brought against their mother and and her new husband. Blaise kicks his shoes off out of habit onto the mat set aside for them next to the garage door. While not really a 'custom' in the house with Ukyo being Japanese and Blaise having lived in that country for some time... it just made sense. Why trace all that dirt and that through a house like this. He got to the kitchen and opened up the smaller of the two refrigerators. Insider were assorted bottles and cans for the drinks and other beverages that the family would partake in on a regular basis. He grabbed a bottle of Sobe? Fuerte? and popped the metal cap off the bottle as he then headed for the steps to go upstairs. That's when his ears filled with the sounds of... three girls... giggling. He pushed the not fully latched door open a bit and peered in as he took a drink from the bottle of sports/energy drink. Inside was the woman he'd taken on as a lover, a valet, and a partner in more ways then one, bouncing on the large bed that his twin daughters shared as she 'mock wrestled' with the two young children. While they were now 3 years old, going quickly on 4. She took one over onto the bed, prompting the other to jump on top of her. There were some out there that said that Ukyo would never be a good role model for them. She had grown up a street tough in the bad parts of Tokyo with her real brother, Haru and after a while her 'adopted' brother, Kyo. While instilled with the values of her family and all, following a form of Bushido similar to a man who sat in her family's far past... she was from the new culture in Tokyo that was very western in their tactics and disdain for the very old ways. It was a mixture of what was over here, to what was long over there. But between her penchant for different, unnatural, hair colors to her refusal to act as a lady in the presence of all but her closest friends and family; some said that she would never make a good mother. And Blaise looked at this and thought about how much she had supported him over the last year and now how she had taken to being just what others, like her adopted brother had said about her. And knew them all to be wrong. She was unique, that's for sure. But she was nothing like what he and the others had claimed. Whore, slut were words they liked to use now. Yes, she was dating a man from over on this side of the country. But other then his red hair and green eyes, trademarks of his Irish heritage he was Native American in his looks. And to quote a movie about the sacrifices that the men of the Navajo nation had given in World War II... “The only difference between you and them is that uniform.” Blaise watched as the three girls in his life played, and bonded even more. He just leaned against the door frame, drinking from the bottle he'd brought along. All went fine until one of the girls landed wrong. She didn't land wrong to hurt her, but landed at the base of Ukyo's neck. The last night of action for both of them in their previous federation, Ukyo's 'brother' had taken it upon himself to hurt her. He had sent her out of the ring, where she landed on her back, the main impact point being the base of the neck at the shoulders. Ukyo winced, and then stopped as the pain shot up and down her spin, prompting Blaise to move in and get one of his twin daughters up off of her. Feeling the girl come quickly off her back, Ukyo rolled over and came up to a seated position, holding her neck with one hand. ?Blaise! You're home?? Blaise held his daughter close to him, who looked to be worried about Ukyo; concern on her face. He set her down softly on the bed and both girls came over as Blaise went to check on Ukyo. “Yeah, done at the station. Although it was pretty cool due to my scheduled time. I got to talk with Edge from U2 on the phone. I know we already have tickets for tomorrow night and all. Still pretty cool. Was nice to hear that he was a fan as it was. Said they've all pretty much followed the likes of my grandfather, uncle and dad in the ring too. Not to mention my cousins. They wished me luck against Biddle this week.” He sat down next to Ukyo and started to rub her neck for her. Normally she wouldn't allow this, but Blaise touched her hand with his other and let her know what he was doing. After a few moments, she sighed and then twisted her neck to the left, followed by a twist to the right. “Ah, natural faery magic pain killers. Better then any pills the doctors keep giving me. I guess I'll keep you around for a while.” She smiled wide at that., knowing it was a joke and such. Especially with the real, underlying reasons for them staying together so long. The fact that mortals, humans, would consider her desires to be with him, something similar to having a virus or sickness. ?You do that. Anything go on while I was gone?? “Yeah, Lady Leanan called, she said she was bringing the last set of packages for your project in the back yard's garden area. So I guess that project will be done. Other then that, Dani asked if I could go hang out a bit today and early tomorrow before we go to the concert.” “No problems with that. Especially when I'll have a lot of work to do in the back yard tonight.” Ukyo's face went soft. “Well, she asked if we could take Kaylie and Kiley along.” Blaise was taken a bit by that. “It was actually my idea a bit. But Dani's been coming over during the day while you and Gray have been working up on the reservation with your grand dad. She's taken a bit of a liking to them too. We're thinking about taking them to the Adventure Dome at Circus Circus.” ?Yeah, yeah. No problems. I don't need them fully seeing what's going to go on in the back yard as it is. They're years from being awakened if they carried any of my genetic code for that. I don't need them growing up around it like I did. Not until they're old enough to accept it. When are you two heading out?? DING DONG! Actually more like an oriental gong going off, could be heard. The doorbell... “I'll go get it.” Blaise stoop up from the bed and took a few steps. ?Mommy, you okay? We wres-tle some more?? One of the young girls suddenly spoke up, causing Blaise to stop in his tracks and turn around. Ukyo appeared to be in a bit of a shock too. She looked to her right at the young girl, identical in looks to her sister on the other side. ?Yeah, Kiley. Maybe a bit later on, okay? Right now you and Kaylie need to get ready to go out. Dani from down the street wants to go out with us today. You guys up for that?? ?We go circus?? The other girl asked. Her voice not fully inflexing right with the words, and circus almost missing the storng 'r' sound in it. But being almost 4 years old, it was forgivable. The two were learning to speak fully, stilling trying to piece full sentences together. They'll start school next year, and it was a sign that they were almost ready. “Yes, just like I mentioned before, we're going to the circus.” Both girls sang out in unison. ?YEAH!? And then they dropped to their butts and bounced as they then came off the large bed. The both tugged at the second drawer from the bottom on their dresser and pulled it open. Blaise smiled wide at Ukyo. She looked up at him, a lone tear falling down from her eyes, apparently unnoticed by her. He winked at her and then turned to head back down the stairs as he heard the door bell ring again. He took the stairs, 2 at a time, and headed right for the front door. As he opened it, he was looking at the woman who was currently his boss within the Order, Aine “Annie” Leanan. “About time ye opened th' door fer me. Beginin' t' think ye an' Ukyo were up t' something.” She gave a wide, sarcastic smile. “No, she's getting the girls ready to head to the Adventure Dome with Dani. She made arrangements for taking care of them away from here while I finished up the centerpiece of the garden.” Annie's smile dropped a bit as she then handed him the small, flat, packages that were in being held by her outstretched hands. “I take it she told ye tha' I was bringin' the final pieces ye needed.” He took the packages with one hand under them, and brought them to his chest. He then slide the top one, about an two inches thick, open and looked inside. “Perfect. I'll go ahead and head out there.” He turned around and headed towards the kitchen and the door to the back yard. Annie followed close behind and actually stepped in front of him when he hit the sliding glass door, so he'd not have to adjust much to open it. He walked out and was right on the deck with the built in barbecue grill and the shallow side entrance to the pool. He kept walking, Annie following behind him towards the left of the pool, to where a garden had been setup with many walking paths made from hexagon shaped tiles about. It looked like all of the paths that went through the various rose bushes, orchids and other flowers went towards a centerpiece that had a circular walk way around it. In the middle of this was a tree. A small tree that looked like it was of the traditional yucca tree found in the area. Hanging from one of the branches was what looked like part of a birds wing that had been smoked and mummified in the traditional native manner. “It's still a shock t' me t' see this.” Annie was looking at the tree. It was special, that was no denying from the way it had been made into the central part of the garden. Blaise walked over to a spot that actually had a missing tile in the walkway. He put the boxes down and then got down on his knees. He separated the 4 boxes, as much identical as his two daughters were to each other. Opening each he found the one he wanted. He pulled a metal plate, about the same size and shape as the various concrete ones used in the walkways. He dropped the plate into the indentation that had already been made for it to fit into. The plate, an off color close to gold, but not quite as shiny stood out with it's engraved symbol that was in it's middle. Annie watches and Blaise repeated the process on the other three spots that remained that needed their plates. Each was the same type, except a different symbol graced them. When he was done, he stood up next to Annie and then looked on at his garden creation. “I cen see it, Blaise. If I shift me eyesight over t' the veil I can see the circle o' protection around yer tree. Na' only is th' piece o' the wing o' Zadikiel there t' guard it, but now a magic circle. I must say, interestin' idea.” He smiled. ?I got it from my grandfather, actually. There are a few spots on the reservation that they've done this. But she's commented that they're always having to go through and redo the markings. I wanted a way to make them more permanent. I'm a dryad, a tree spirit, connected to nature. This tree is the one that has my spirit in it. I wanted it here so I could guard it myself. With my daughters too, they may soon show signs of having gained that attribute too. I can plant their trees in the safety of the circle.? Annie shook her head. “Some dryad. Ye don'na 'ave an Oak tree, but some modified cactus as yer tree.” Blaise chuckled. “You have to remember, I wasn't born in the Olde Country. I was born right here. The indigenous tree is the Yucca. Not my fault I'm connected to the land here, and not some Oak grove over in Tir Na Nog like my father and the rest of his family.” Annie smiled so show she wasn't trying to insult him. “Ye new world people an' yer new fangled trees an' tha'.” He laughed out loud at that. “I like my tree better then your rock.” She didn't want to continue that one. Quickly she changed the subject. “So, I'll now leave ye. I just heard Dani's truck pull up. I'd guess Ukyo an' the girls are leaving now. I'm going t' leave ye t' the rest o' yer ritual. Tha' tree 'as been moved abou' 40 miles from where it was planted. Yer going t' need t' nourish it 'ere. Feed it, rebond with it. Bu' make sure it's safe at all costs. Tha' is the one weakness tha' our enemies could use against ye.” Blaise nodded at her. ?Good thing that it only effects half of my nature, huh?? “No shite. I'll head out now. I migh' try catchin' the show this week. I'll be in town till Wednesday, then I'm off.” “I'll see you around then.” Annie started to walk away. “Annie...” Blaise called her name and she stopped, and looked over her shoulder at him. “Thanks for your help in this.” ?Bah! I'll be takin' yer ideas back t' the group down in Texas. Tha's a pretty good idea fer a few of our places tha' need a but o' a physical buffering up on maintaining their protections. I can see even keystones o' buildings could be used fer tha'. Ye jus' make sure everythin' is setup 'ere. Tha's yer job Lieutenant. Na' mine.? Blaise nodded as Annie walked away. He then turned and looked at the garden as it was setup. “First things first...” He outstretched his arms to the side a bit and put his head back. His red hair started to move as if a wind had come up right there, and a slight gleem, almost a glow came from his eyes as he concentrated. The assorted bushes of flowers around his chosen centerpiece then began to grow, rapidly. They got to the point where they were straining at the trellises that held then in place. “Now it's not as visible from the ground. But there is one more thing I need before I do this.” Blaise walked inside his house really quick and went to the room that he'd set aside for practicing his guitar. The same room that Joshua Brand had started using for his drums. He grabbed a large item that was covered in a sheet, long, about 4 feet or so in length. He headed back out and walked back towards the garden area. He didn't unwrap the item so much as he grabbed it by a handle that popped out of one hand. Quickly the sheet burst into flames as the sword that was held inside of it become a tongue of fire. Blaise held the sword up. It was designed in the same style as an old broadsword, with a hand and a half pommel on it. The pommel and cross guard were unique in they formed the image of a man, mostly naked, with a pair of wings that shot straight up along the base of the blade... an angel. The flamed of the sword died down and Blaise took it with one hand as he approached the circle around the tree. When he got there he reached out and took the mummified wing in his hands. ?Zadikiel, Archangel of Destiny... my fallen foe. Your wingtip grants my homestead the defenses of your power. You will help me consecrate the land that my spirit now resides in. And you'll do it with my other trophy that I claimed from you when I removed you from the physical world... Your weapon.? He let go of the wing and then held the sword with both hands, point down. He slammed the sword down into the dirt that currently held the yucca tree in it's fixed location. A grin on his face, he turned and then walked towards the eastern rim of the circle, standing on the metal plate that now sat in that spot. “Time to get started.” His hands rouse from their sides, behding at the arms. His hands went out at an angle, but almost straight, palms up as he then began to speak the words that included his true name to the people of his father's line that sat over in the Irish land of Tir Na Nog. ?Guardians of the East... I, Prince Turren MacElatha beseech you!? ^^^^^To Be Continued^^^^^
