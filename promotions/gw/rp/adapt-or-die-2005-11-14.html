Adapt or Die

14 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14/05 Why? Some would ask that simple question... They wouldn't like the answer. The answer to the question: why? It is truly simple. Because he opened his big mouth. ?I still owe McClancy an ass whippin'! I want a piece of him for what he did! I'm Brick, I'm the best Global's ever seen!? Got news for you big boy... you're not. But you'll be facing him at Evolution. That's right. First Match: Double pin with the current and unstoppable International Champion Second Match: Your dumb ass got pinned by Ambrosi. Blaise? Nope. Third Match: Elia Smith... victim, down for the count. Now the #2 contender for the Platinum Title. Fourth Match: Mike Biddle... had to uphold that family tradition. Biddle is down. Biddle was supposedly the #1 Contender for the International Title. We'll see if that one gets recognized there. I told people when I showed up... you want some, just ask. And you not only asked, but you demanded! I don't care if it's Shadow, Zenith, Ambrosi, Train... it doesn't matter who it is. Just ask for a match. It's how I've done business for years, why stop now? **Sigh** But what do I have to gain by this? Yes, Brick is a former Tag Team Champion with that other walking Gimmick in Train. Yes he's a former World, International, TV, US and all those other title holder... a Grand slam winner. A win over him looks good on my Global Resume. Yes, yes it does. But you really have to ask, when you look at the man lumber around the ring and try to make it look like he's wrestling, how? Just how did he get what he has had? The answer is an obvious one when you look at the long standing locker room, and his good friendship with Brandi Sights. How many other guys have a one on one pep talk with the Spokesperson for the board of directors every week? I'm not here to be handed everything on a silver plater. I'm not a walking stereotype or gimmick. I can see through the haze there, I can see the truth. If Brandi actually appears to care about him as much as she does... then she'll need to make sure she gives him a warning... Blaise McClancy isn't one to fool around with. C'mon Brick... look at what I did to you last week. I offered you the chance to either take me face to face, or from behind. I turned by back on you and offered you the chance to pay me back. You didn't bite the bait. You had your chance. Then I took mine. It's funny that one of your sayings is: “Don't start none if you can't bring none.” I brought you a very healthy serving of crow. I hope you enjoyed it. I know I enjoyed being at your service for one evening. You want it... you've got it. This week, you and me. Regular rules for Global. That's right, I didn't ask for some gimmicky crap. No, you want to face me, let's do this dance by the rules first. Oh, and I'll be leaving the stuff that helps make the mist outside the ring. In fact I'll leave it in my bag in the locker room. Scratch that... I'll leave it at home. You know why? Because I really don't need it to beat the living hell out of some washed up has been! Face it Brick, you were the shit back in 2002... This is 2005... It's the future... Prepare for the FutureShock. **Fade** Blaise wasn't quite sitting, but he also wasn't quite kneeling either. He was in a strange stance, sitting back on his heels, but his full weight being supported on his toes and the very ball of his feet. He was barefooted, and wearing on a pair of loose fitting pants in a dark gray, cotton-like material. Obviously something to reflect on his martial arts background and continued training. Around the waist of the pants was a belt of a heavier weave of the same material in a black color, with four red bands adorning the very tips of the belt, spaced about half an inch apart. Sweat beaded on his forehead as he sat there, his head looking up. It also gleaned off the various tattoos that he had put upon his body over the years. The prime one was on his right shoulder. It started on his chest, down by his lower ribs, the snake like body of the oriental dragon in red and gold coiled up his right pectoral to the shoulder. Where his arm came out of the shoulder, the mouth sat there, open. The other tattoos on his body, a tribal world turtle on his back, and a tribal lizard on his left arm were dull compared to that, in basic black. A new tattoo though, went on his stomach... it gleamed in the almost lavender color, the color of Ukyo's hair. In kanji, written there was her full name and his own. In front of him was something strange though. Everyone in the world knew that Blaise was a practitioner of the martial arts. So his getup and typical practice routine could be guessed rather easily. However, today was a bit different in his current meditative process. In front of him was a setup with what looked like the same type of red bricks that you use for doing the siding on houses. Of course they're not used as the main building material anymore. The red clay being rather brittle and unable to withstand the weather properly. There was a base made from two 4x4 pieces of wood and then to stacks of five bricks setup in parallel towers right on top of it. He comes to a partial crouch and extends his right hand out to the side, tucking in the thumb to draw it away from that part of the hand. 1... 2... 3... He lashes out, coming up out of the crouch slightly, and then brining the hand, inverted so that the thumb side is down into a ridge hand strike onto the bricks. The two towers of 5 break apart. The ones on top only appear to crack at first, while the bottom two or three crumble away. As it all falls apart though, each is in at least two pieces, if not more. As he comes back down to a resting position, he can hear a couple of pairs of hands clapping for him, of queue, time and key with each other. The nonhuman side of Blaise can make out one set of hands. He's heard it many times both inside and outside the ring. Ukyo's hands have a distinctive sound to them. Things that someone who's senses are sharpened by the side of him that was just pure... animal. The other pair wasn't one he recognized. It was a pair of ladies hands though, smaller, softer and more gentle then a man's clap. He cracked his left eye open and looked over towards the glass doors that led from his patio, back into his house. Standing there wearing one of his t-shirts, and a pair of jeans was his girlfriend, as noted... Ukyo. The other person was wearing a women's business suit. She looked familiar but couldn't place it. ?Impressive, Mr. McClancy. Am I to take it that's a specific message to your opponent this week?? As soon as she spoke it hit him, Noi Tsunami, one of Global's reporters, as well as one of their HR people. “If you wish to make it down as such, then please do. I've not got much to really speak to him about right now.” Noi walked over to the larger patio table, of course with an umbrella, and grabbed the towel sitting there. She walked over and handed it to him. ?Of course, you know who I am, correct?? Blaise took the towel with a nod of his head and started to dab at the sweat that rolled down from his red hair that had been getting longer over the last few weeks. He used to keep it cut short, but recently taking to growing it back out to the length it used to be at when he started wrestling in the states a few years ago. “Noi Tsunami: One of Global's book worms, and reporters. As well as one of their HR and PR people. I see I'm not important enough to get personal invites and visits from the speaker for the board of directors.” He smirked at that last statement. Noi returned it with a feigned look of shock. “You're taking another shot at Brick and I don't even have my recorder on. Although I must say, the McClancy dry wit is evident in you. I've seen your family before, but I'm not to familiar with you. And after a month, I don't know how much the Global fans are either.” Blaise dropped the smirk and draped the towel over his shoulders. ?So is that why you're here? You want to get to know me better?? Blaise gave a sly look towards Ukyo who giggled a little bit. “Uhm...” All Noi could get out before she was at a loss for words. Ukyo walked up behind her to comfort her a bit. “Don't worry about him. I'm sure he'll play nice.” She turned to Blaise and winked at him. “Don't bother the reporters, dear. You'll scare them all away.” Noi looked a little relieved that Ukyo had stepped in to save her. She had to admit that for a split second she was thinking of other things, she didn't know why, but it was pretty strong. A smell filled the area, like that of fresh foliage, like you'd smell in the woods on a spring day. She shook her head a bit, and Ukyo broke in again. “Maybe I can get you something to drink. Although I'd have to ask you what flavor of that damn Lizard Logo'ed drink that he constantly has in the refrigerator you'd like.” ?Huh?? Blaise broke in as he slid a chair out. “I've had a promotional contract with the drink brand known as Sobe? for some time. Since I was the SCWF world champion over in Revolution Studios. That was about three years ago, right after I came over to the states.” Noi turned her head towards Ukyo. “Uhm, anything citrus I guess.” At that, Ukyo spun on her heels and headed towards the kitchen. Blaise motioned for Noi to have a seat in the chair he had pulled out. “Please, put your back to the sun. You'll be more comfortable.” Noi took the seat and allowed Blaise to push her back in towards the table. He then picked up a pair of sunglasses from the table and put them on as he sat across from her. ?So, what do you want to know for your little website and magazine there, Ms. Tsunami?? Noi pulled out her gear from her purse. A small tape recorder and her pad of paper and a couple of writing utensils to ensure she'd never be without one. Quickly she started to scribble stuff down. ?Well Mr. McClancy, let's start off. Cause Global has of course been around for a while. You said three years ago you were the champion of another federation in at the time, was the strongest competitor to PrimeTime Central, correct?? Blaise leaned back, as if he was reminiscing. “Yes, I was only using the name Hazard at the time. It was shortly after that when my real name came out. Namely when my cousin called me out, wanting that World Title back, or as it was called, the Superior Title. I held that title longer then anyone else ever had or ever would. Then my cousin, Sean, decided to teach me some humility when he took it from me.” Noi suddenly sparks up some recognition. “Yes, I know now. I remember watching that. You were about ten pounds smaller then, and your hair was in a pony tail.” “Yup.” “So, for those who are looking at your stampede into Global... currently you've not been pinned or made to submit since you got here. In fact the only match that was a loss to you, you weren't the one in the three way match that got pinned.” Blaise smirks again. “No, it was that idiot Brick.” Noi pulls back and looks at Blaise after he said that. “You do know that Brick is a former grand slam champion. He's held just about every title in Global.” Blaise just looks at her, and smiles before giving his answer. ?So?? Ukyo comes out and puts a couple of bottles in front of both of them, and then takes a seat herself. Noi answers Blaise's answer with a similar question. ?So? You're saying 'So?' about a man like Brick?? Blaise leaned forward. ?Ms. Tsunami, I've got a few mental conditions based on today's psychology classes, but one thing I don't do is stutter. Yes, I said 'So?'. The reason why is because we're talking about a man who I've not seen do anything worth a damn since before I got here. Since the PTC tournaments over the last few years, I've paid some attention to the people who are now in Global. I almost won a bracket in GTT4 last year against another 4WW member... But one thing I've seen is that I really need to not fear the man who everyone speaks so highly of.? Noi takes a quick drink, as he's speaking. “You see, Brick has been given everything here. He's one of the old generation of wrestlers around here. But there is one problem with people like him. He's given everything, is best buddies with the old President, and still hangs out with the head honcho-lady... and you know what... he's never had to grow as a fighter. He's never had to adapt to something new.... well I'm something new.” Noi tries to continue on. “Well, you bring up the GTT4. Some would point out that you didn't make it past the bracket final round... you still lost. To Tarrant I might add.” Blaise shakes his head. “Tarrant found my one weakness last year...” ?Oh, and what might that be?? “I worked for my cousins at the time. When Sean and Fey came up to me, and whispered in my ear, it wasn't about encouragement. It was about my loyalty to the company and keeping rumors down.” Noi shakes her head a bit at that. ?Are you implying...?? ?I'm saying that I was told to put Tarrant over, because at the time in 4WW, he was the top contender for their big belt. And if I beat him, the one thing that they always tried to keep from me, to keep their reputation clean would have to go to me. Based on their rules, if you ever beat a contender, then he lost his spot. I'm a loyal company man, but there is a difference now. I'm out for myself and the KB:NAN now. Not as much for Global. I'm back to the way I was when I was the top. When I was the man that not only had one of the highest rated federations sitting on my shoulders, but was helping an interfed challenge the big boys. Now I'm here, Global, PTC... and the only way I see to help them out, is to do it all again. But I have to work to get there. Shadow and Ambrosi: Check. Smith: Check. Biddle: Check. Next stop... Global's Golden Boy who couldn't even beat a pansy like Zenith... Brick.? Noi speaks up with shock in her voice. ?You'd call the World Champion, Christian Zenith a pansy?? ?If the shoe fits? Look, I'm below his radar right now. But after Showdown in Tokyo, that's going to be very, very different. First Brick. Next week someone else. The week after, yet again someone else... After that, Showdown in Tokyo, doesn't matter. All I know is that eventually I'll be sitting there looking across the ring at someone wearing something that is going to be mine. I don't care who it is. I don't care what belt it is. True, just as the McClancy's always say... a title doesn't make a man. But a man can make a title. However, if you want to get noticed in this business, you'd better have a belt that doesn't hold your pants up around your waist. There is a new generation of people here in Global. You've got fighters like Shadow, MacFarlane, and a few others like Emoticon, who over the last year have just wowed the Global crowds. You know why that is?? Noi shakes her head no. “No, I can't say I do.” ?It's because they're not the same old, same old. They're not the walking gimmick. The 'Street Tough Gangsta'. They're not the hardcore wrestler. They're not the slick snake oil salesman trying to make you think he's something else by day, and a wrestler at night. They're consummate fighters. And after the first batch, Global was on it's knees. Shadow went how long without a true defeat? Unheard of, right? Those old dinosaurs like Brick floating around here, in their little tidy bowl of talent are about to be extinct. It's an Extinction Level Event called... adapt or die.? Noi continues to write as he answers her. She quickly makes sure the micro tape has enough space left. ?In fact, Noi, let me make it really simple for you. Don't look at my past. Titles? Big deal... really. Former World Champion? Years ago. Former mid card level champion? A year ago... Former Tag Team Champion? Just maybe my partner sitting here carried my ass in the ring. Maybe she didn't. Simple fact: Those who don't learn from their past are destined to repeat it. The problem is, those who don't learn from their past may also find themselves surpassed by next years model. Brick hasn't adapted, he's not grown in his abilities. He's stagnated. He wants to go calling me out? I thought when I announced the formation of the KB:NAN that he knew how I felt. No, I didn't mean to spray him in the face. But I feel no remorse for it either. And then he called me out. So last week, I went out there and offered him the right thing. Pay me back. Don't look, don't think twice. Hit me. He didn't do it. Why? Because he's soft. He looks to the crowd after he came back and took on Zenith. Look at him though. He's still holding onto that gangsta crap, and he's still talking about how much in Bubba's pocket he is. That's fine, really. But Bubba isn't in the ring fighting for him. Neither is Brandi Sights. If the man wants to call me out, that's fine. He doesn't know me from Adam... yet.? Noi nods as she puts the period in on that. “Any final words for you there, Mr. McClancy? I'm sure this is enough.” Blaise smiles as he puts his own bottle of drink down onto the table with a slight glass on glass clang. “Yeah...” He pulls the towel up and wipes the side of his mouth quickly. ?Brick needs to learn the definition of a word that came to mean a tag team like no other. I speak of adapting, of evolving. If you don't, you face one thing. Eventual extinction if you're an animal. But humans? They have another term for it.... And the way that Brick has been running around like a chicken with his head cut off, calling out people. I can only see that he's a true case of something.? ?What's that?? Blaise hesitates for a split second. Then he smiles as he says the world. “...Future Shock.” Noi writes that down and then turns off the recorder. “You'll excuse me Ms. Tsunami.” Blaise turns and gets up. He walks into the house, not waiting for a response. ?I take it that's my cue?? Noi looks at Ukyo. “Yeah, I think it is. He'll be at the casino tonight for the meet and greet if you need more.” “Alright. I must say, your husband is a bit into himself.” Ukyo laughs outright. ?Did I say something funny?? “Well, not really. But first, he's not my husband.” “Oh, I'm sorry, I didn't mean to.” Ukyo puts her hand up to her. “No, no. Even Dani made that mistake at first. We're dating, we're close. But nothing permanent yet. But let me finish... Second, he's not conceited, he's convinced.” Noi chuckles at her comment as the end comes up for today's chapter. **Word of the Era** Future Shock: A condition of distress and disorientation brought on by the inability to cope with rapid societal and technological change.
