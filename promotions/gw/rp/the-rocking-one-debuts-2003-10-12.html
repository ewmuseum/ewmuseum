The Rocking One Debuts

12 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Rocking One Debuts Jack Rocker Global Wrestling Promo #648 Date: Sunday 12th October 2003 Location: Halfway through what has been a terribly busy day for the staff at the airport they now find a small amount of reporters, cameraman, photographers and the like waiting at one of the terminals for the 2pm plane to land. Finally the plane land thirty minutes late and passengers make their way out of it and enter the terminal, one of these passengers in a young lad who looks to be no older then nineteen. He carries a suit case with his right arm, and has a sports bag on his left shoulders, he is surprised to see a man dressed in a white shirt black trousers and black jacket with an ear piece who comes over to me and takes his suitcase. Jack Rocker: “Who the fucking hell are you?” The security guard continues to show the same stone face expression but does shake his head slightly. They continue to walk down the corridor, until they make it to the terminal. The group of reporters, cameraman and photographers rush over to the two and instantly begin to ask questions to the young man. Several flashbulbs go off as well and the young male shields his eyes obviously blinded by the camera flash. Jack manages to hear one of the questions from one of the news reporters. News reporter: “Jack, are you looking forward to debuting in the wrestling federation, Global Wrestling?” Jack Rocker nods his head as the man dressed in a suit tries desperately to keep the group of reporters, photographers and cameraman away from Jack. Jack makes a “Keep on rocking signal” to several cameras before answering the question from the news reporter. Jack Rocker: “You bet your fucking ass I am. It’s my dream job, getting paid for kicking some bastards’ and some bitches’ ass, you going to see more violence then you ever thought possible when I hit the ring and take on anyone or anything.” The muscled man dressed in the suit who is obviously Jack’s bodyguard finally makes a path through the crowd and he walks alongside Jack who looks at a few cameras as he goes by. Eventually they both manage to get through the crowd and walks out into the airport’s parking zone where Jack sees a chauffer holding up a name card that reads “Jack Rocker” as he leans against a black limo. Jack and his bodyguard walk up to the chauffer who smiles. The chauffer opens the passenger door for both Jack and the bodyguard, he watches them enter, then closes the door behind him and enters the driver’s part of the limo. Chauffer: “Where would you like to go sir?” Jack thinks for a few seconds before rummaging in his trousers pocket. Both the chauffer and bodyguard seem to be a bit surprised by Jack wearing a pair of blue jeans and a red Nike jersey. After a few minutes of rummaging Jack pulls out a note, which hays the address of the hotel, he is staying in. He hands it to the chauffer who takes it and reads it before turning the keys in the ignition. Jack opens a cooler in the car and smiles when he sees a bottle of beer, he reaches out to get it but the bodyguard stops Jack. Bodyguard: “I know in Britain you are old enough to drink but you’re not out here, and anyway you should try and keep in shape not become an alcoholic.” Jack shakes his head and sits back in the leather seats. He opens his green suitcase and pulls out a compact cd player and some headphones, which is connected to it. Jack puts on the headphones and presses play on the cd, he turns it up so loud that the bodyguard and the chauffer can both hear it, they scrunch up the face as they hear the sounds of Limp Bizkit. After half an hour of sitting in the black limo, Jack is finally able to get out as they have finally got to his hotel. Jack is breath taken by the outside and is completely shocked when he enters the hotel. He looks down at the marble floor and looks around what is defiantly a very expensive hotel most likely a five star hotel. Jack turns to see a woman behind the reception desk and walks up to her before finally making it to the desk; he leans on it and looks at the woman. Jack Rocker: “I believe there is a room reserved for me? My names Jack Rocker from Global Wrestling.” The woman looks down at the list in front of her until she finds his name. She smiles and heads over to the rack of keys, she pulls out on with a blue key ring and hands it to Jack. She then hands over a form and a pen to Jack and tells him to sign at the bottom. Jack showing how young he is doesn’t even read the form and just signs at the bottom, he hands it back to her and she then gives him directions to his first floor room. She then tells him about an interview he is expected to be at in the morning. Jack Rocker: “Damn, no late night for me then. I’ll be there at nine in the morning, though I may be running a little late. Could you give me a wake up call at half eight just to make sure I’m up?” The woman nods her head and writes it down on yet another sheet of paper. Jack smiles before heading of up the stairs and into an elevator with bodyguard entering as well. The elevator quickly reaches the first floor and both Jack and the bodyguard guard walks up the corridor before Jack turns around to face the bodyguard. Jack Rocker: “Will you stop fucking following me? Next you’ll be getting in my fucking bed, look I don’t want you so piss off!” The bodyguard looks shocked and angry at the same time. He gets nearer to Jack but can see the anger flaring up in the young male. After a brief stare down the security guard turns away from Jack and walks down the corridor to he finds the elevator. Jack just shakes his head and unlocks the hotel room door, he then enters without looking back and shuts the door behind him…
