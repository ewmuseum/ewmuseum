Memories of Blood (part 2)

28 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/28/05 Corvus sat in the comfort of his library enjoying a glass of finely aged bloodwyne. He could not help but think about what his sire had said to him about Lia turning her back on him. The stinging sensation came back when he realized that she decided to play around with a mortal. This really helped him figure out that he didn’t need to associate with mortal or kindred alike. He was on his own and needed to do what was best for him. The fact that there was not any consolation in this turn of events was mind numbing. He swirled the contents of his glass as he stared at the flames which danced so beautifully yet were so dangerous to his kind. It helped him forget about the troubles he’d been experiencing as of late. The temptation of leaving the mortals game had crossed his mind. Why exert so much energy for their amusement when all it did was cause strife? After all it was a dangerous bit to be a vampire pretending to be one. Just one slip would cause a horde of kindred to come after him for breaking the Masquerade. It was the only thing that protected their kind from mortals who fear what they don’t understand. “Brooding like that will not get you anywhere my dear ... I always thought you to be much better than that.” A voice like that of a siren came from the dark shadows of the back of the library. Corvus brought his glass to his lips and finished it off quickly and set it down on the table next to him. The sneer was back again on his face in response to hearing the voice of his maker inside his haven. “I thought I told you that if I saw you again that I would not be generous with your unlife?” There was a great risk in that as there are laws in their society that really prevents them from bringing other kindred to their final death without the approval of the Prince, which is the leader of all kindred within a specific city as every city has one. He had barely been allowed to continue on with performing in a ring much less been granted the ability to take out his sire for leaving him stranded an eon ago. That was something that always was a mystery to him after he learned the core rules of what it was to be a vampire. Once you embraced a mortal into the world of darkness you were responsible for them until they could be on their own, something Corvus never had the pleasure of. Celine had left him to fend for himself and that was the simple truth. Was it a part of a much bigger plan or was she just that bad at being a sire? He knew he was not the first instance of her failure to give her childer the proper knowledge they needed to survive in their new surroundings. “I’m usually not one to say I told you so ... but I believe that I was right about a matter in which you vehemently swore would never happen.” Rhianna had a cat like grin on her face as she poured more salt into the wound. “How does it feel to know that your so-called beloved wife decided to stick it to you and gallivant around with a group of mortals” Sickens you doesn’t it?” Slowly she began to move closer to him knowing the danger that came along with it. He might have become a powerful kindred in the years he’s accumulated but there was one thing he failed to realize: She had just as much power and knowledge to make it a fair game. “Do you really think I care about that” I suppose it is only fair that I get what was coming to me. Not even a year ago I had turned my back on her out of frustration. This was her way of proving to me that I was wrong. Ok ... so I’m wrong, time to move on and deal with other things.” Rhianna moved closer to the chair and placed her hands on the top of it without making any such contact with him. He was aware of her movements and would make sure that if she moved any closer that he’d set her back a peg. She wanted so much to make things easier for him but their views on the world around them were such a contrast. “There’s one thing I’ve noticed and that is you are very stubborn. One might think that after all this time you would not brood like a mortal. I had higher hopes for you than that.” Her voice had that tinge of disdain upon it. “Did your father ever tell you that you were a disappointment” If he didn’t ... then as your “mother” I have no problems saying you are a failure.” With his fists clenched, Corvus considered standing up and socking the woman but he knew that even though his desire was at a level of intolerance he still had to respect her. However, the one thing that he objected to was being called a failure. Certain things he excelled at more than others but he never considered those that he was not being failed. If Kindred were allowed to speak of themselves to anyone other than their kind it would be found that Corvus was one of the most sick and twisted killers of all time and influenced some of the most ruthless mortals in history. Names like Elizabeth Bathory and the man who had inspired the whole Hollywood vampire concept or so it was said: Vlad (the Impaler) Tepes. If anything he was an inspiration. Maybe he was not a role-model you would want your kids looking up to but he has done some good deeds in his later years. Playing a lesser part in the vampire wars he felt was necessarily so that the sides could be balanced. All he cared about was entertaining, which was considered one of those failures by Rhianna and he knew it. “Don’t speak of my father ever again. That is a closed chapter do you understand me” I may not wish to kill you but I can strike up enough courage to hurt you so that you retreat and rethink about what you say in my presence.” His tone was obviously bitter and sharp, but he still held contempt for her actions even though he had only himself to blame for accepting the dark gift bestowed upon him. “As for you, mother, labeling me is not a very flattering thing when you know so little about me.” “I think I’ve made some contributions to this world, both good and bad but the one thing I can say with good conscience is that I never gave anyone a choice whether they wanted to live or die and have a horrible existence placed upon them and then when I’ve had my way just throwing them to the proverbial wolves as you did. That is a fine way to earn respect in the kindred community is it not, Rhianna?” He knew he cut right into her by once again throwing it at her that she was a failure at bringing others of their kind into the world. This was one of the edges he had on her because in all his years he had never attempted to bring anyone into the darkness, not even those he cared about. “There is something I would like to know ... how exactly did you get into my haven” I have state of the art security. If I didn’t want myself to get in, I would not be able to get in.” Rhianna ran her fingers along the top of the beautiful leather chair and let out a soft cackle. “You really have no idea do you” You are simply too predictable for words.” She paused for a second to let it sink in. “It is not hard to put in a security code when someone knows that yours is the date of your deceased daughter. You are not fooling anyone, Aric.” He sprung up from his seat and looked at her, fists were clenched which seemed to be a constant when in her presence. The iris? of his eyes glowed an eerie red color to express his anger even further. “I thought I told you that I preferred being called CORVUS” Secondly, a very rare amount of people would even know that information.” He grumbled and decided that it was time to cut to the chase and see what the real reason was for her being there in his haven. “Besides stalking me all over the city or however long it is you’ve been showing your voyeuristic tendencies, what is it that you’ve come here for?” He unclenched his fists and folded them across his chest. Rhianna moved from the chair and began to walk towards him then placed a finger on his shoulder and traced it along him as she walked behind him. She knew that it was bothering him. If he were still mortal it would bring a shiver across his spine as if he had something crawling upon his skin. “I think you know the reason why I am here. This has nothing to do with the fact that you are pandering to every dull-minded flesh bag on this forsaken planet but has everything to do with your birthright.” She continued on as he brought his head back trying his best to tune her out which she knew but did not stop. Rhianna would be as persistent as she needed to bring the point across that she needed him, that the cause she was apart of needed him. “You’ve been on the sidelines for too long ... you need to snap back into reality.” “Oh no, not this again ...” he thought as he opened his eyes back up and looked at her. He knew that she wanted him to join up with the Sabbat again to continue the Jyhad against the Camarilla. It was a long time since he paid attention to what either side was doing. Essentially he was a free agent in the vampire society not adhering to rules of either sect. He was “happy” with his undeath as it were; there was no reason for him to get back into it and risk everything he has built. His was an empire that would be talked about for ages to come but it was still nowhere near being complete to where he wanted it. “Listen, I am flattered that you want me to get back into the fight but that is just not who I am. I am not taking sides and as you can see I would not take sides when things with Lia and I were in a state of acquiesce.” He moved away from her and picked up his glass and the bottle that sat next to it and poured it nearly full and took a drink of it. Rhianna kept her eyes on him fully to listen to what he had to say but in her mind she already had a devious plot brewing to get him involved again. “Speaking of which, don’t you want to get back at her for causing all this strife?” She said as she stole the glass right from his hands and took a drink from it, savoring the taste of a vintage. “Mmm, this is rather wonderful. You do seem to know how to pick your wyne. Let’s not get off track though, the man known as Corvus from what I heard about never let any grass grow under his feet. He was someone that gave back far worse than he had received.” He pulled the glass out of her hands and finished it setting it down onto the table. There was no denying that she had a point but the fact of the matter was that he did not do such things anymore. No longer a cold vicious murderer, assassin, or anything even remotely close, he had spent the better half of the last two decades in the public eye. Partly as a musician who had some success to a wrestling promoter then a wrestler himself. He could not fathom breaking the masquerade that held the kindred society together. It would have created one huge shit storm that would never have been able to be sorted out. Never mind that though, the fact was that it was Lia she was working him towards getting revenge on. Technically, he was the first to strike and this recent happening was only her getting back at him. They were even in his eyes but Rhianna was still going to push that button as hard as she could. “So you are willing to turn your back on your kind in order to satisfy those who become your dinner?” She shook her head as she took the bottle he had sitting on the reading table and poured some into the glass now that he was finished. “I shouldn’t have even spoke about it, knowing that you like to leave those you care about behind ... need I mention your son?” There was a slight smirk on her face if he managed to catch it before she changed facial expressions. If a vampire could be any more pale than normal, Corvus would fit that bill. There was one thing that was off limits in a conversation and that was his family. He never spoke about them or even acknowledge he had one other than the fact of his marriage to Shadow which was now in the tank. For him, he wouldn’t care now if she had taken those divorce papers he signed and turned them in. That was the way he felt about the situation or any situation as of late. His life was not going the way he wanted; he was beginning to hate his job not to mention that even going to work pissed him off. It was not like he needed it to pay his bills or feed some illegitimate kids like some wrestlers have. He had a rather good stash of money in various banks around the world, not to mention he had assets that would make Bill Gates look like a two dollar whore. What’s even better than that is the one to make him a vampire was back into his life bugging the fuck out of him and bringing up things that she really had no right to bring up. This was a great way to endear herself to him if she wanted him to do anything remotely close to what she was asking. In all likeliness, he was not going to lift a finger for her or the Sabbat ... he could give a fuck less about the war. “Let’s make something clear here ... we are not to discuss any part of my family past or present, is that clear to you” There are only a few people who have that right and last time I checked you certainly were not one of them.” For a moment his cell rang and he pulled it from his pocket, answering it when he saw the call was coming from his brother. He did not want to pull him into the situation until more was known so he had to figure out how to brush him off without alerting him of something being wrong. “Hey, do you mind if I call you back sometime later” I’m in the middle of something and it requires my full attention.” He listened for a few moments before responding. “Thank you for understanding, I’ll call you later.” That was the duration of the conversation before he closed the phone and placed it back into his pocket. “Ok, where was I” Oh yes, I remember now. I was just about to tell you to go fuck yourself and your cause. That is about right isn’t it?” Corvus sneered as he looked at her and saw the shocked expression on her face that he would actually talk to her like that. “I know I may not physically or emotionally be able to do any damage to you because there is not much of either to work with and you may have made me but with all that I have been through you certainly have no rights to be my keeper.” He started to walk to the door of the library, turning to look at her one more time. “Since you’ve managed to find your way in ... you can certainly find your way out. Good evening, Rhianna, or whatever the hell you call yourself these days.” She stood there silent watching him leave and closed the doors to the library behind him. Crimson tears began to flow down her face but where they because of what he said to her or was she just trying to console herself from the fact that he wouldn’t accept any subtle advance. Rhianna could no longer sense his presence in the manor. He left and she knew better than to follow him or have anyone follow him for fear of their safety. She realized that he was more like a human than any vampire she knew in her entire existence, which is what made him different; special. One thing she did know is that this wasn’t the last they would see of each other, she vowed to that as she picked up the bottle that was sitting on the desk and took a long swig of it before setting it down and walking towards the exit herself, heels clicking along as she did. * * * The one thing that Rhianna was truly right about was that Corvus was more human than sometimes humans themselves were. This was something of an aberration as he has been a vampire for a long time and most kindred lose their humanity right at the beginning. The monster could tear apart most people to shreds but sometimes that came with a price. His conscience was always his guide and even then it liked to boot him in the ass here and there. There have been a few exceptions with things he’s done, including hacking up Rhianna’s little errand ghoul, Jack. That was just a matter of being at the wrong place at the wrong time. Some words stung quite heavily and his maker’s words especially hung over him. He stood upon the rooftop of one of the nicer buildings along the strip, watching as people milled along without a care. Some of them were just tourists enjoying the sights while others were hardcore gamblers who didn’t care if they bankrupted their families. The lights and activity were signs of life, something that Corvus knew he’d lost a long time ago. These were just reminders of what he had but this was at a time when things were a lot different during his natural life span. Marriages were arranged and done at very early ages, much younger than people marry or are allowed to in present times. Child birth was also a time when girls were near still being children themselves. It seemed like young men and women learned to grow up faster than they should have had to only for the need to survive in that time. There were even times when children perished before their parents, he could tell you that much to be true. But rarely ever did the parents die much before their time, even if the life expectancy was much shorter than in today’s world. The talk of family made him uncomfortable again after he had just come to terms with the fact that his daughter would never see him again as he was damned to an eternity of horror and when that was over it would be the torment of whatever hell he was sent to which also made him uncomfortable. Instead of his daughter, Angela, the thoughts of his second child and the only son he would be given were trapped in his mind. He never quite knew what befell his boy and that was something he could not ever bring himself to research. The last he could remember of the boy was several weeks after his embrace. He tried to return home to see the remains of his family ... one last time. It was a chilly night, perfect for ghosts and things unnatural to prey upon unsuspecting people. I returned to my estate to find most of the workers relieved of their duties and my brother had taken over as the head of the house. He and his soon to be bride were taking care of my son whom knew very little of his mother or that he even had one and the memories of his father had also began to fade. I had asked my brother to look after things if anything were to happen to me as he knew that in my drunken state that the possibility of me not returning some night could happen. I was able to move as if I were one with the sound of the wind that beat against the foundation of the home. I crept in shadows until I found what I had been looking for all along: the bedroom chambers of my youngest son, Brennan. The little one was fast asleep probably dreaming of the next day’s excitement and playing outside as the plague that had swept our land had gone and it was safe to roam free. I watched him as his chest rose and fell in such beautiful movements. It was little things like these that you come to miss when you no longer have the ability to do them. I wanted to move closer but I just could not allow it ... I feared that I might do something I would regret and bring an unwanted fate onto someone who did not deserve to be punished. Brennan was far too innocent to have a foul creature as I had become for a father. His mother had run away, his sister dead, and the only family he could count on was his Uncle. It was not that I didn’t trust my brother to take care of Brennan; I just did not imagine him being the fatherly type. He was much more concerned with his wealth and his lady to make a lasting impression on my son to become something great like his grandfather and a complete opposite of his father (at the time). I couldn’t stand it any longer; I began to move towards the bed and must have made too much noise. Brennan did not wake up but several moments later I heard the door begin to creep open and I hid back into the corner where most of the shadows were. He carried a lantern in his hand and he must have known that something was there. “Who go-eth there?” My brother Christian (who has gone by many names over the years) looked around and must have caught a shadow of my frame as he stepped back quickly. I could tell he was frightened ... hell I could even hear his heartbeat going a mile a minute. “Show yourself!” I did not move an inch from my spot as he wouldn’t go any closer and I did not want my child to wake up in the middle of this exchange, the only thing I could muster was to speak softly. This had to be good because it was my last goodbye to my mortal family. “I don’t think you want me to do that, Christian.” “Aric, is that you” Where have you been, everyone thinks that you died!” “They are right in thinking that brother, I came here to say goodbye to my son but I wish not to disturb him and let him see what has become of his father. I want you to promise me something ... ? I stepped out of the corner and allowed the light from the lantern to display a pale version of my former self and by doing that knew there would be some sort of reaction from my brother. I thought he was going to have a heart attack on the spot when he nearly dropped the lantern. He moved closer to me after the initial shock wore off and the flame of the light made me nervous. It was an instinct that I had grown to realize that fire could harm me just as easily as sunlight. “You are a monster, you do not belong here! I deem thee no longer welcome in this house!” My eyes glared at him and if I had known then how to better control a mortals mind I would have done so. I began to back up more and more towards the window in which I had entered. I gave one last plea to him so that he could tell Brennan what I wanted him to know. “If you need to tell him that I passed, then so be it. Just let him know that I did love him with all that I was. Do the best you can so that his good memories of me will not fade ... can you do that for me, brother?” I could see his skin crawling, knowing that I was not the brother he knew. I was no longer the person I knew ... I was a creature of darkness, a wretched shell of what once was. My clothes were the same as I had left with the last night I was seen only dirtier from crawling myself out of the grave I was placed in by Celine. The words that came out of his mouth were expected but being that I had just been turned not long ago ... it still hurt. “You are not my brother, demon. Be gone before you are vanquished!” “Not before you promise me what I asked of you, it is a simple task. JUST DO IT!? “I know that this young boy’s father did love him; you don’t have to tell me that but you are not his father and you most certainly are not my brother. Now be gone ... !” I flew out of the window and disappeared into the blackness of the night. I watched as Christian closed window back up and also heard my son speak. The talking must have woken him and when he asked my brother what was happening all he said was “Go back to sleep lad, it was nothing at all.” * * * Corvus continued to watch from the rooftop as he recalled the last moments he had with his son. The course of time had always made him curious as to what happened to him ... if he had lived a full and productive life and if he had children of his own. There were certain things that he and his brother never discussed and this was one of them. Christian could not have been born into darkness too long after him; he didn’t look at all like he had changed since the night he was told to leave his own home. He reached into his pocket; pulled out his cell phone and pressed a few buttons to make a call to one of the numbers on his speed dial. The other end answered and Corvus hesitated to speak and nearly hit the end button. After a moment more, he decided that this was something he had to do for his own peace of mind. “Sorry about earlier, I had a house guest and I could not talk at the time. There is something I need to ask of you ... a favor of sorts.” He waited for a reply even though he may not get a positive post response it was worth a shot. “Do you think you can come out for a few days” I need your help with something.” Ashe as he was going by now, hesitated for a second before he said he could spare a few days but wanted to know what his older brother was cooking up. Corvus was not about to tell him now because it would jeopardize his whole plan altogether. “Are you in some kind of trouble” I heard you had a run-in with the local authorities but nothing else concerning you has been said in the kindred community lately concerning you which is a little unheard of.” A chuckle came from deep in the elder vampire’s throat. Kindred spoke about him because he was reviled and hated all at the same time. They didn’t care for him much but knew that if something needed doing that he was there to get it done. “Just get here and I will brief you on everything on the way back to the house. That is all you need to know right now; think of it is being a matter of importance for the family’s sake.” He didn’t want to keep talking because his intentions would come out the longer they went back and forth. “I need to get going. I have to make a few arrangements before I get in the ring this week. I’ll see you when I pick you up from the airport, later Ashe.” Corvus closed the phone as soon as he stopped speaking. That would not give his brother a chance to get another word in about anything. He placed his phone back into his pocket and began fidgeting with his family ring which he always wore. It could have really given him away if anyone that was a historical buff would recognize it but family was important to him and so it stayed loyal on his finger. When he reached the back of the building, he simply stepped off and floated down to the ground slowly and continued walking towards his car once he hit the ground; he could not help but speak to himself out loud. “I will find out the truth ... whatever it may be.”
