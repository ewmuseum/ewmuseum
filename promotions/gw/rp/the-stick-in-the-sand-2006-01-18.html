The Stick in The Sand

18 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 18 2006 Adjectives on the typewriter He moves his words like a prize fighter, The frenzied pace of the mind inside the cell. The man on the street might just as well be. The man on the street might just as well be. The man on the street might just as well be. *The Dream The tips of my fingers tingled, hair stood at attention on my arms as I glared into the epiphany of a iced over landscape, a dead frozen water ridden wasteland of nothing, feet becoming numb, as I broke into a cold sweat, wondering when it would all end’if it would ever end. Eyes closed, face cold, the negative degrees shoot a disturbing pain though me’must be what I deserve like stepping into a tub of ice water. A voice trailed through my brain, like a pianonic crescendo, kind of like a song? “I’ve tried so hard and got so far but in the end’it doesn’t even matter.” But it wasn’t that, it was just pain, a knife though the eye, or an eye for an eye? A chilled wind waked through me, the opposite of summer heat, I stood alone, ears blood red, face and nose with the same tint. White below, grey above, this must be hell frozen over? *Reality Buzz? Buzz? Buzz? A never ending noise in a repetition as my baby blue eyes opened to a white ceiling skewered by rotating fan blades, I let out a yawn as I turned and knocked the clock from the bedside stand without bothering to see what time it currently was. I pulled the cream sheet from my sweltering body as I arose and placed my feet on the moderate wooden floor as I wiped my eyes. “Yawn” I let out as I opened my eyes and looked at the rolling white caps from the South Atlantic Ocean as my pupils focused on the beautiful scene from behind the window of my bedroom as the white drapes fluttered elegantly in the breeze. I stood up, my body cracked and popped as I erected, but I shrugged it off and dragged myself into my bathroom, I popped my neck a few times and I looked into the mirror guarded by the porcelain sink as I turned the water on I let it cascade though my hands and hair. My shoulders dropped as I splashed the clear liquid onto my face and ran my hands though my golden blonde hair as I arose I looked myself over in the reflection. A cocky smirk crossed my face as water raced from my neck to my chest, I took in a long breath of the Atlantic air, I felt my lungs opening and collapsing again as I pulled my red boxers from my waist and snatched the blue swim trunks hanging from the shower bar and clad them to my naked body, popping the elastic around my waist as I stepped from the bathroom “Robson!” I heard as I slipped my grey Nike shoes onto my feet and looked up to see Johnny Cruse standing before me wearing only a pair of blue windbreakers “Yea man?” I questioned as I stood up and looked at him “You ready?” He asked me as I rolled my neck and popped my fingers relieving the stress. “I’m always ready brah” I said to Cruse as a smile crossed my face “Well let’s go then’we have a lot to do today” I followed Johnny out the door only to be met with the unrelenting rays of light from the South American Sun. The heat was almost intolerable for the first few moments but my body adjusted as my pores opened and I wiped the first beads of sweat from my forehead. “Awful hot out here isn’t it Johnny” I exclaimed to him as we began stretching out our legs on the white sand beach which was almost blinding to my sensitive eyes “Quit your whining Jay’we haven’t even started yet” Cruse said to me with a scolding look on his face. I merely nodded back and continued stretching my body. “We will jog to Rio De Janeiro Point and back.” Cruse announced and I simply nodded my head, but inside I was pondering to myself as to why we had to run that far’and back in this sweltering heat which I despised so much’but if it’s going to make me better than so be it. I thought as I let out a long breath and started out in a jog before Johnny. I rolled my shoulders a bit getting the last glitches out of my system. “Well were off on the right foot” I announced to Cruse as I felt the sand shifting and changing under my feet with every step. “How do you figure that?” Johnny asked me as he caught up along side “Well, I already won a title in my second match back you know” I said as we kept pace with each other “Now Jay don’t already start getting a big head on me, you can get that title stripped away from you just like that” Johnny said as he snapped his fingers “I know’I know’I’m just saying” I exclaimed, trying to muster an ill fated rebuttal as I wiped the sweat from my brow. My legs were in the beginning stages of exhaustion as I tried to keep up with Cruse. I glanced out to the ocean and watched the brilliance of the sun and the water as it sparkled with each wake and churn. “I know you are, but Kyo Kushinaga is no slouch, he won that battle royal to get an opportunity at your belt and believe me he will come out firing and if you’re not careful he will punch you in the mouth and won’t think twice about it” Johnny proclaimed, his voice knocked me out of my trance with the shimmering water. I focused once again on the miles of white sand before me dotted with random specks of Brazilians and tourists. “Jay’you have to have a game plan going in, use your wrestling abilities to counter his martial arts ability, you know what to do in there. Instincts are crucial in this point of the game” My mind began to wonder. I envisioned myself inside the octagon with Kyo, thousands of screaming spectators and jeering patrons? I could almost feel the electricity running though me as I ran along side Johnny on the beach. The intensity and passion started to take hold of me, my legs taking longer and longer strides as I caught my second wind. “Jay?” “Jay?” I heard Johnny’s voice catching up to me from behind, I paid him no attention as I continued to run at full speed I could feel the suns rays beating down on my shoulders and sweat poured from my face. I wasn’t going to be denied in my first title defense no one can stop me now?. A stick in the sand, a miscalculated step, and my feet left the ground, I was airborne. My mind went into slow motion I knew I was going to be picking myself up out of the white polished sand. A crash, I skidded into the ground. I opened my eyes and laid there on my back looking up into the high rolling cumulous clouds over head, heart was racing, blood was pumping as I listened and felt the beat of my body in the hot sand. “You ok man?”” Johnny’s voice rang out has he cast a shadow over my sand covered body and extended his hand. I let out one last gasp of breath and grasp him as he helped me from the white sandy beach. He brushed my back off as I ran my hand through my golden blonde hair ridding myself of the itchy substance. “Yea I’m alright” I said to Johnny in a dejected voice, I turned and looked at his and saw the concern in his face, I wiped myself attempting to expulse the sand that clung to my sweat soaked arms. “Come on were almost there” Cruse exclaimed, as he began to run away from me and I just stood there still in shock from my recent predicament “What’s wrong Robson” Can’t keep up with an old man?”” Johnny shouted back to me, as he continued into the distance. I chuckled a bit at his remark, shook my head, and put one foot in front of the other, starting in my pursuit. Could this be what motivates me to once again reach the top tier in Global? Or will Kyo be the stick in the sand that sends me crashing into oblivion once again?
