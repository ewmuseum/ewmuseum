what price can they want from ME?

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-11-2003 “God DAMNIT!” Johnny Cruse says as he slams down the phone. Johnny’s Apartment in Dallas has become a total disaster area. Seeing as how in the last few months Johnny’s life has been hectic. The past month the most. Two months ago his son Collin and his wife Valire had turned up missing. The Police were useless. No leads found in their investigations. Even with the stalking reports received from Derek on Mark Kullypso on his wife. Johnny’s own niece. Mark had been cleared of any involvement. Johnny looks at the place and takes a deep breath, and dials a number on his cell phone. “Yes, Advantage Maid Service.” Rings out a voice. “Yes. My name is John Cruse. I need another maid at my apartment. Yes Use my Card account I gave you for the bill. The Desk clerk will let the maid in. Thanks” Johnny clicks the phone off and heads to the door. He opens it just as Ben J Pailthorpe is just about knock on the door, Ben is dressed in shorts and a black t shirt. Standing next to him is Jay Robson. Dressed similar but instead has on a tan button up shirt over green cargo shorts. “Hey mate!” Says Ben a bit startled that Johnny opened the door before he knocked. “Hay Guys.” Says Johnny in a depressed voice. “Johnny, Man brighten up bro. Things will get better. We will find Val.” Says Jay trying to be encouraging. Johnny takes a deep breath. His fist clench and unclench. ? I would know where they are if Tyler Pierce and Sean Price would have just helped. But noooooo. They have to be greedy son of a bitches.” Johnny says in a very serious tone. “You know I hate Sean. Ever Since I Reformed the Mile High Club and got hooked up with that Prick, things have been screwy.” Jay Says. As the three start heading to the elevator. “Yeah, like Derek not trusting us.” Ben says. Johnny stops and looks at them both. “Guys, You know Derek. He been true, he has been burnt one to many times by the both of you. The only way I can see to solve things is to face him.” Johnny with that statement leaves both Ben and Jay as he enters the elevator. Johnny stands in side a old friends Dojo in Dallas. Dressed in a black GI he stretches. Soon the door bell sounds and in walks Derek Frost. “Your late.” States Johnny. Derek drops his bag he has on a pair of trainers and a loose black T Shirt with a dragon on the front. “Yeah, I know.” Says Derek as he puts on some pads. ? I had to make sure Cindy was safe.” “How is my niece?” Ask Johnny as he climbs into a ring in the center of the dojo. “Still shaken cause of this Syn and Kullypso shit.” Says Derek as he follows. “See, The Nevermore were my Prime suspects. But now.. now I wonder if Price and Pierce had any thing to do with the Disappearances of Colin and Valire.” Says Johnny as he circles Derek. ? I know. What could they want that they don’t already have.”” Asks Derek as he blocks a kick from Johnny. “That is the big question is it not.” Says Johnny as he leaps into the air and spins to the floor taking out Derek’s Legs with a sweep kick. ? Your going to have to be Sharper then that if you plan on beating Salaok Kullypso and Sabbot this PPV. You do know it is a trap. Do you not? ? I know but Johnny. I have to do this.” Says Derek flipping back up. ? I know. I know.” Is all Johnny says as both get into there sparing session fully.
