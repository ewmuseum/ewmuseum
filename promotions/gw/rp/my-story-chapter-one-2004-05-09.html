My Story: Chapter One

9 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.09.04 There are times in one's life when the only person that truly understands... is themself. 'Have you ever gotten to the point where you're just tired of trying? Have you just gotten so tired of trying to prove who are each and every day, eventhough your mind isn't cloudy in the least about your the identity of yourself and your soul. Have you ever gotten so tired of trying to rebuild something that once exsisted, something like trust or respect or self-worth, that you feel like you're soon to crumble under the weight of the foundation which you are so galiently supporting yourself? Have you ever gotten to the point where you're just tired of trying... and want nothing more than to give up?' Silence sometimes become your closest friend. 'These are the battles that I've faced my entire life,' voiced Ken Partridge Jr out of the dark recesses of what looked to be a warehouse. His footsteps could be heard echoing through the desolate hangar as he slowly paced back and forth. On wouldn't notice if he was moving more than his legs simply because he didn't bother enough to step out of the shadows. From the clicks of his shoes, an afficiando could easily point out that it was a pair of Pradas tapping the cement as Ken continuously placed one foot in front of the other. The small ammount of light that was available shined through five small windows, two high above the floor on either wall of the structure and one at the far end, and would, at times, clinch to the white silk YMLA shirt that was tucked into Ken's Epidemic brand leather pants. You could see him turn on his heel as soon as this happened... it was almost as if he was afraid that the sun would burn through his outer-shell and expose the core of his soul. Perhaps, in the back of his mind, that was what he wanted to happen. 'Seemingly, the battles never end... the only continue to grow. When I was young, I battled alcholism and drug addiction. Now, as an adult, I battle myself and my worth. As a child, I was at war with society for looking down on me because of my age. Now, I'm wage war against them for looking down upon me due to the simple fact of my being bisexual. My entire life had become a shell... and live inside of it, allowing very few to visit. I'm no longer battling my parents' trust for me, but battling the Global Wrestling faithful because they lost MY trust. Well, no more. It begins on Monday, when I take the un-named Championship and break the morales of nine other men. That's when I prove that--' The lights in warehouse begin to kick on and Ken begins to look around a bit, showing that it was unexpected. Voices can be heard echoing throughout the building and it becomes obvious that a crew is coming in to begin a shift. When the line of vision returns to where Ken was standing however, all that remains is a closing door and unanswered questions. --To Be Continued--
