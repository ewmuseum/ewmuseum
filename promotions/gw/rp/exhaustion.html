Exhaustion

undated

Date: Sundizzle The pool house at Zero Estate lies in rigid motionlessness with nothing more than deafening silence to inhabit the frigid air. The entire area remains mute until the minute jangle of loose keys can be heard rattling just outside the door. An utterly exhausted Deacon Holmes stands outside the door under a blazing sun patiently sifting through the ring of keys in his possession while searching for the one that can open the door. At this point though he’d just as soon pass out right in the lawn or behind the bar on the pool deck as he would continue searching for this key. His fatigue had cost him motor skills, causing the key to slip from his hand several times before he’d grained a firm grip on it. Now grasping the key Deacon gazes up into the sky with a sigh as he plunges the key deep inside the lock chamber. He pauses for a moment before slowly turning the key, muttering “Home sweet home.” as he does so. After cracking the door slightly Deacon feels a powerful stream of chilled air pouring from the small crevice and proceeds to push the door the remainder of the way open. Allowing the icy blast of air of wash over him like a wave, Holmes looks up with his eyes closed and inhales a deep breath before trudging into his humble abode. As he laboriously plods across the floor the entirely worn out nineteen year old wipes a bead of sweat from his furrowed brow reciting “No pleasure, no rapture, no exquisite sin greater... than central air.” in a tired yet triumphant voice. Holmes then begins to undress while still in motion lumbering through the room. He casts his hat aside like a hot murder weapon before discarding his blazer as well, slithering out of it’s grip the same way he eludes everyone else’s in the ring. Holmes continues his impromptu strip show by kicking off both of his shoes, yet another color of the shoes he endorses for Nike. He then gives the coup de grace by slipping out of the polo and plain white t-shirt he’d had on, taking them both over his head and hurling them into the air and across the room with the last bit of his now entirely depleted energy. Now clad in only a pair of antique custom faded jeans and white ankle socks, Deacon Holmes approaches what would appear to be his final destination... the bed. Holmes looks longingly at the blanket covered mattress with soft puppy dog eyes and a flat smile, almost in disbelief that he’s finally made it here. Holmes gingerly does an about-face and stretches both arms out and as he closes his eyes a victorious smile stretches across his lips. Deacon simply slides his legs out from under himself and allows his body to fall backward, ultimately plummeting and sinking into the butter soft mattress of his california king bed. A sigh of relief escapes the young man’s lips as he slowly waves his arms back and forth over the ice cold sheets, gently caressing them as he does so. Holmes yawns over “What a day” while taking his face in his hands before once again letting silence overtake the room, leaving him to be alone with his thoughts. The first of those being “Sometimes I wish I weren’t famous.” It had been quite a day indeed, filled with enough obligations and hassles to break any camel’s back. The new fame Deacon was experiencing had a different taste from that which he’d earned. This time it was thrust upon him. As soon as he was recognized as “somebody”, to be watched and listened to with added interest his input ceased. Deacon had gone blind and deaf in his over animation, but had yet to realize it until now. One can either see or be seen, and Deacon had been in the dark for quite some time now. Fame had become a mask that gradually ate it’s way into his face. Day in and day out Deacon schedule was filled with personal appearances, photo shoots, interviews for both television and magazine, promotions, and commercial shoots, not to mention scheduled as well as impromptu autograph signing sessions. He’d long lost his anonymity, and was no longer able to enter or leave any building unnoticed. Deacon missed being able to go places without being hassled and rushed by swarms of adoring fans all frothing at the mouth at the opportunity to score an autograph and a handshake, or maybe even a possible photo op. He didn’t mind signing the odd autograph here and there, but one often bolstered to one hundred. What used to be a five minute trip to the store to scoop up a loaf of bread or some other trivial item was now a two hour event in which several pens were bled dry across anything a random fan could get his or her hands on in the form of the young man’s signature. It wasn’t uncommon for Deacon to leave a store with a single item and a sore wrist from repeating the same motion over and over. He would never dare claim he didn’t enjoy the complete and utter adulation he seemed to receive from everyone he came across, but at the same time he’d never claim he didn’t miss having time to himself either. The pool house he inhabits at Zero Estate had become something of a fortress. The only place he had to hide away from the prying eyes and the shrieking voices. His current situation was exponentially better than how his life would’ve been expected to turn out, but Holmes was beginning to learn that there was also a steep price for the luxury he enjoyed. Money was rolling in from all directions, yet there was no time to enjoy it. Lying across the bed, the nineteen year old superstar let out a deep sigh as a revelation befell him. Deacon slowly moved his hands upward over his face and rested them on the top his head with their palms near his hairline, as though they were attempting to prevent an explosion. As Deacon slide his hands even further back, bringing them down over his ears en route to the sides of his neck he utters to himself “It’s like I’m giving my life away...” His eyes grow wide as a result of this realization. It couldn’t be any closer to the truth. Even though only nineteen years old, Deacon was forever young, always just wanting to have fun and never opting to be overly serious. Now with all these added obligations, fun and games were over. They’d been traded in for large sums of money he’d never have time to spend. A nearby calendar attached to the wall became Holmes? focal point for a split second, as the block representing each day contained Deacon’s agenda for it. A new club was opening downtown and as a known frequenter of clubs, Deacon had been asked to appear so as to generate star power. He groaned at the thought of doing anything other than remaining still as a corpse and slowly watching the hours pass in silence. His time had become a hot commodity, everybody wanted a piece yet he didn’t even have enough for himself. Just as his eyelids began to grow heavy and his body numb, a muffled melody of high pitched tones configured to resemble a popular song sounded out. Patting around his pant legs without looking, Deacon’s hand finally fell upon a slight bulge near his left thigh. Rolling his eyes back, Deacon snaked his hand into his pocket and retrieved a small cellular phone, pulling out the lining of his pocket as well due to the lackadaisical manner in which he’d removed it. Brining the now blaring device into his line of sigh, Deacon proceeds to flip it open with a single hand then deftly press a series of buttons in order to operate the speaker phone option before dropping it on the mattress alongside his head and unenthusiastically answering. “Hello?” A familiar voice answered back, it was the one, the only... Maverick Reno coming across a bit choppy as a result of the speaker phone feature being used. “It’s five o” clock, do you know where your Deacon is?” sounded into the air as Maverick inquired in a condescending tone, leaving only a split second of dead air before continuing, making sure not to leave a big enough gap for Deacon to retort before saying “I know where mine is, and it’s not where’s he’s supposed to be.” Of all the trials and tribulations involving the public Deacon went through during the day, nothing compared to TUE. Which also just happened to be a rather large reason for him being where he is today. Without them he would have probably ended up being destroyed by the Nevermore rather than helping to run them out of Global Wrestling. Without TUE Deacon never would have come into contact with John Zero, and as a result of that never gained his current living arrangement... or met Rhaiza for that matter. The exhausted teen could do no more than roll his eyes and let out a groan inaudible to Reno over the phone before finally mustering up the strength to reply “What tha fuck are you talking about?” in a rather weak and breathless tone. An exasperated sigh comes from the other end before Reno once again chimes in, “Alright, I’ll give you a hint... we discussed it just the other day.” Deacon’s face shrivels up into a grimace of confusion before he fires back “I don’t know man... floral patterns for your new dress” A dramatic pause fills the air before Reno decides to respond “If I found you in any way humorous I’d probably be inclined to laugh, maybe next lifetime, try again...” with a bit of audible movement coming over the line as well. Deacon exhales as he rests both his hands behind his head to create a pillow for it before answering back “Hell if I know man, decorating tips” How to make your roses bloom bigger?” with a bit of a smile forming on his lips that soon turns to laughter. No matter how tired he was, he could never pass up an open shot to drill Reno. “Deacon?” Maverick responds, now in a much more serious tone, prompting a “Yeah?” from Holmes. He then continues on after gaining Deacon’s complete attention and adding “Remind me to tape your mouth shut before you say something like that again.” Deacon remains silent as a result of Reno’s quip, allowing him to go on “Now since you’ve obviously forgotten, allow me to refresh your memory. Does the word meeting ring a bell to you?” Away from the phone Deacon utters an inaudible “Fuck.”, leaving his mouth agape and thrashing about the mattress for a moment in disbelief before deciding to reply “I’m not goin, I’m playin” my get out of boredom free card.” while sitting up on the edge of the bed. As Deacon turns his torso so as to put the phone within arm’s reach several cracks and pops ring out like gunshots, prompting a sigh of relief on his part. After scooping up the phone, Deacon holds it up against his ear just in time to hear “Oh?” coming from Maverick Reno. He just knew something bad was coming, and could only roll his eyes and give a jacking off motion with his free hand as he waited. “Well then don’t be surprised when you find that I’ve attached a get out of paycheck free card to the back of it.” Holmes simply grunts, as he is far too frustrated to muster up an actual coherent response. After a pause, Maverick tosses out another barb, as he already knows he’s won. “So how long will you be?” he asks, with the closest you could get to a smile being heard in is voice. Deacon’s face contorts into a scowl as he cheerlessly rises to his feet while replying “As soon as I get dressed...” with a groan. Just as he goes to clamp the phone down so as to end the call Reno chimes in “You sound a bit down Deacon, what’s the matter” Can’t handle the new girl?” Rather than responding, Deacon simply closes the phone and returns it to his pocket, mumbling “Let’s see if you can handle my foot in your ass.” while hobbling across the floor in search of his clothes. “Fluke” Against me?, pfft! ...no contest.” Deacon muttered to himself while scooping the shirt he’d thrown upon his entrance up from a nearby chair. Juri Hyobanshi had been the only person whose speed rivaled Deacon’s enough to give him any sort of a problem. He’d always been faster than his opponents whether they were his size or smaller, it was what he thrived on. It was also how he lived. His speed had always been what made the difference against much stronger and larger opponents, but against her that would all go flying right out the window. In their first encounter she managed to edge him out with a narrow victory and Deacon has ever since referred to her as “Fluke” because of the manner in which she won. It was the same way Roswell had beaten him a few weeks prior to that for the cruiserweight title. Deacon got caught showboating and allowed his opponent to creep up on him with a low blow and a quick roll up. In no way a decisive win. At first chance, Deacon came back and defeated Max Roswell in order to claim the cruiserweight title for a third time, and also establish himself as one of the greatest cruiserweights Global Wrestling had ever seen. His will to win had always been sharp, but getting a rematch with someone after a win he felt was a fluke took it to the point that it could split atoms. Especially when gold was involved. Deacon’s accolades meant so much to him that at one point in time he actually named them, only to later abandon the practice as he felt he grew too attached after doing so and was a wreck after losing one... no matter how many others remained as he was known for holding multiple titles. After slithering back into his shirt, Deacon snatches his shoes, which he’d spent much of the day shooting a new commercial for, and once again takes a seat on the bed. Just as he goes to slide his foot into one of the shoes he hears a knock at the door followed by a feminine voice asking “Are you home babe?” Rhaiza. Another important piece of the wishing for a more normal life puzzle. Due to all his engagements he rarely had time to be with her aside from match nights, as she accompanied him to the arena as well as the ring. He’d met her at the welcome home party Zero had thrown for him months ago, and found himself growing continuously more enamored with the young woman. He loved everything about her, to the way her jeans hugged on her hips and the way that she walked with a seductive switch in her swagger, to how she always licked her lips after they kissed. More so than anything though, he loved the way she treated him... like a normal person. When they met she barely even had a clue who he was, and even after finding out her attitude toward him never changed. With or without the money, she was his and he was hers. With John getting engaged and handling problems of his own, the vast majority of Deacon’s time could only be spent with Rhaiza, allowing her to become something of a support system for him as well. His greatest fan and biggest cheerleader even though she cares nothing about wrestling. And through all of that, Deacon still found himself afraid to fall for her. Dropping his shoe down to the ground, Deacon stood up from the edge of the bed and made his way over to the door, where after flipping the lock notch over, he swung it open. The blazing heat of the outdoors hit Deacon like a slap in the face as the beaming sun shone into the room, partially blinding him in the process due to the rapid adjustment made by his eyes from the dimly lit room to the light of the sun. Standing there awkwardly and squinting, Deacon looked rather ridiculous as he’d been taken by surprise by the blinding light of the sun. Unable to see clearly, Deacon felt the soft caress of two hands brushing against his face, one on each side. They stopped near his ears, and were slowly withdrawn, sliding across his cheeks as they returned. Deacon opened his eyes completely and found himself able to see perfectly fine, greeted by Rhaiza’s smiling face. She’d slipped the sunglasses away from her own eyes and given them to Deacon, who smiles back somewhat nervously as the two hadn’t seen much of each other lately. Placing a hand on her hip, Rhaiza asks “Well... are you gonna let me in or do I have to work on my tan a little more?” before stepping forward into the doorway. Deacon’s smile quickly breaks as he realizes he’d been blocking her from entering and steps aside. Rhaiza plants a soft kiss on his cheek before announcing “I made us dinner, hope you’re hungry.” and holds up a basket with a smile as she passes into the room. “I thought maybe we could have a picnic.” she continues as she walks into the room followed closely by Deacon after he shuts the door, once again sealing the room in quasi-darkness. Holmes? facial expression immediately tells the tale that something’s wrong as he mopes past his girlfriend and once again resumes his seat at the edge of the bed. Setting the basket in the seat of a chair, Rhaiza walks over to Deacon and takes a seat next to him, brushing up against him in the process. Leaning over into his lap and looking up at him, Rhaiza flashes a megawatt smile in a failed effort to cheer Holmes up before asking “What’s wrong” Where are you going?” Breaking his silence, Deacon grumbles “Take a guess...” while tying up his second shoe. Without a club in the world as to what it could be, Rhaiza shrugs before answering “I don’t know, you said you’d be free after five.” while looking him in the eye hoping that was still true. Shaking his head in disappointment, Deacon replies “T.U.E.... a meeting.” in rather curt fashion. The attitude wasn’t directed at her though, but rather at the idea of having to go. “But I thought...” Rhaiza starts, only to be cut off by Deacon as he stands up. “Yeah, I thought I’d be free to, but apparently I was wrong.” he says indignantly, with a scornful look directed toward the floor. Rhaiza’s posture melts in dissatisfaction as a result of having once again missed being able to spend time with Deacon. Noticing this, Holmes leans in and gives her a kiss on the cheek and a reassuring rub on the shoulders. “I’ll be back as soon as I can, I promise.” he informs her with a wink, before turning his back and walking toward the door. As he passes his dresser, Deacon picks up the television remote and gently lobs over to Rhaiza in order to help alleviate some of the boredom involved in sitting around waiting for him to return. As he took the door knob in his hand, Deacon yelled out over his shoulder “Keep that dinner warm, we’ll eat when I get back.” Rhaiza sits rigidly still while watching him walk away, waiting for him to turn around and yell gotcha or come tackle her onto the bed. He was always playing around, and she hoped this would be another one of his jokes. Unfortunately, this was not one of those times. As Deacon closed the door behind himself Rhaiza tried to speak but got choked up, only getting out a “But...” before hearing the door close behind him. “But we were having sushi...” she says now to herself, as she realizes Deacon was really leaving, and she was really going to be stuck there awaiting his return as their dinner slowly went bad. A solemn Rhaiza now inhabits the pool house at Zero Estate, patiently waiting for her boyfriend’s return.
