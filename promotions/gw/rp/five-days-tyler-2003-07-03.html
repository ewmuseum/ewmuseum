Five Days Tyler

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/03/03 The scene opens inside of the lobby of a prestigious hotel. The cameraman, carrying his camera, and Brandi Sights, enter the hotel lobby through two enormous glass doors that are trimmed with gold plated, brass frames. Brandi in black suit pants, a white top, and has her red hair hanging down, which sways side to side as she walks, makes her way through the lobby, to two people who are sitting in chairs reading newspapers in the back of the lobby. As Brandi makes her way towards the people, they drop their newspapers and watch her walk towards them. Sitting in the chair that is to the left is the blond woman, name still unknown, that has been seen with Ice the last couple of weeks. She is wearing a pair of red tinted sunglasses, a purple turtle neck sweater, a light purple almost see through skirt, and black heels. Sitting next to her, wearing white Converse tennis shoes, a pair of blue jeans, and his brand new blue t-shirt, with white block lettering that looks like blocks of ice that read 'ICEMAN COMETH', is Ice. Ice and the blond both watch Brandi as she walks over to them. She stops right before she reaches them. Ice stays seated. Stands stands there waiting for one of the two to stand up and make their way over to her. Neither move, and Brandi is forced to pull up her own chair and make her way towards Ice and the blond. As Brandi gets situated, and ready to ask her first question Ice interrupts her and begins to speak. 'Five short days until the clock stops, and fate takes its massive toll on you Tyler Pierce. Five short days and the questions that have been asked go away, as the answers come forward from the shadows and rear their ugly heads. Five short days, and I take full claim of the name that is rightfully mine. Tyler, you can live a lie all you want, you can live in envy for all I care, but not by stealing my name. You see boy, you put the name CHAMPION to shame. And just as I punked out Mysterious and Ben J. Pailthorpe, and beat them by myself, I am going to punk your ass out, and beat the living hell out of you, all by myself. As I have said time and time again, Tyler, you are just another victim in the wrong place at the wrong time. So I will give you the warning, and give you the option I have given everyone else this past month. Do not walk down that aisle, do not step into that ring, and do not get in my way if you expect a healthy life ahead of you. The blood that you will shed, should you not heed my warning will not be on my hands. The physical pain, and the long standing agony you will go through will not be on my hands. The permenant damage to your physical well being, will not be on my hands. Because you have be forwarned, and I have given you the option to save yourself now. So it is on your hands, should you decided to sign your death warrent and walk down that aisle, step into that ring, and get in my way. And should you not heed my warning, don't worry, I will finish you off for good.' Ice folds up his newspaper and sets it onto the floor next to his black gym bag, which is leaning up agains the side of the chair he sits in. Ice looks over at the blond and takes a long look, before slowly returning his gaze to Brandi Sights and the camera. 'When I'm done with Tyler Pierce, I will make my way to the back and before Star, and myself begin to celebrate, I will give a little message to the TUE, and everyone else in this company. Hell, why wait til then. TUE, GW, right here, right now, I am serving notice to everyone in this company. When I am finished with Tyler Pierce, when I become Cruiser Champion, I am going to move up that ladder, and one by one each of you will stand face to face with me, and one by one I will go through you all until there is only one left. I am not here to play anymore games. I am not here, to sit back and watch as others talk their talk, but forget to walk that walk. I am here to do what it is that I do best.' 'Tick....Tock......Tick.....Tock. Time is running out. This Monday night on Evolution, I will walk into that ring and I will walk out victorious. On that night my prophecies will come to realization. On that night, the promise I made only four weeks ago will come true. On that night, I will show you, Christina McCoy, Tyler Pierce, the entire GW locker room, the GW fans, and anyone else who doubt my words as I run rampage over the best cruiser superstar this company has to offer. And when I'm finished throwing him around, and I take that title, I will be THE ONE left standing tall in the middle of the ring. And it will be known then once and for all that this is MY WORLD. And in MY WORLD no one has the tools to hang with me.' Ice stands up and the blond, now known as Star, reaches down and grabs both of their bags. Ice walks up to Brandi and the camera. He leans in and intensely looks at her, and into the camera's lens. The camera zooms in so that his cold, icy blue stare is all that can be seen. ICE: TICK.......TOCK. TICK......TOCK. TICK.....TOCK. Ice closes his eyes and the scene fades to black.
