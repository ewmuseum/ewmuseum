Finding Humane

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Finding Humane Salaok Global Wrestling Promo #10 Date: 03/21/03 Location: Offices Leaving the office which he had so quickly made his own, he begins his search with the papers in hand. The papers that would solve two problems with the one match. It had been reported that Salaok had re-signed Humane to the Global Wrestling Inc, however, the papers were not yet signed. And those papers are exactly what Salaok needs to get signed in order to order two men to fight in a match where it was high possible that one of them would end up in hospital and make Salaok happy. So the search begins for the giant retard known as Humane. But why did he want him back? Was it because that Humane could easily be manipulated? Was it because Salaok hated everything about Humane and knew that by having him work for the company, then Salaok would own him? Or was it simple because Humane was good for ratings as the federation found out just after the Global Turnbuckle Tournament which they entered Humane in as a test of sorts. 'Fucking idiot.' The words escaped Salaok's mouth as he thought about Humane. But what a match it would be as Humane and Brick, two men that Salaok simply couldn't stand, would be made to fight in an extreme match. A match which Salaok, and the world for that matter knew would be filled with blood and pain. An owner of a federation couldn't ask for two better people to put into this kind of match. It was just a shame that Salaok had to hate them both. Moving through the halls of the GW HQ, sliding in and out of people talking and trying to get to where they are needing to go. But where would Humane be? But that question would soon be answered as soon as Salaok heard a scream of a young woman and the word 'Freak' being yelled out repeatedly were heard. Only one man could cause a reaction like that, but Camero was n longer working for Global wrestling. Therefore, there was but one name which ran through the mind of Salaok. Humane. His feet quickly begin to move under him and pushing past the crowd which had began to gather in front of the elevator shaft which is were the trouble was found. The doors of the elevator shaft were held open by a couch and Humane was inside jumping up and down. With every jump the elevator shook that little bit more. THUD THUD THUD. 'Where are you Mr. Singer? Me can't see you.' Humane yelled at the top of his lungs at the roof of the elevator. 'Me hear you singing. Me get you out.' Humane had been let loose inside the GW HQ for only a short amount of time and by the looks of things had been heading towards Salaok's office when he had found music playing in the elevator. Not just any music was played in the elevator, but it was the theme songs from GW and the voice was confusing Humane. Somehow, in his small twisted little confused world Humane believe that the band was stuck in the roof and was going to get them out. Salaok had to move fast before Humane caused any more damaged to the shaft and so begun to push himself through more people. It wasn't an easy task as people were interested in what the freak was doing and their screams of fear, were quickly turning into chuckles and full out laughs. 'Get the fuck out of my way or you're all fired.' Salaok's voice booms out over all others in the area and within seconds, a path had cleared all the way to the front of the crowd and Salaok began to move through. Although not seen as a huge man when in the ring, Salaok's frame was huge compared to the workers in the offices and those who were still in his way were quickly picked up and moved to the side. 'Humane! What are you doing?' Stopping his rescue attempts dead, Humane turns around and looks at Salaok. A confused look began to form on Humane face, not quite believing it was Salaok who now stood there in front of him. 'Salaok?' His voice was that of a man, but had the tone of a small child asking a question that he wasn't sure if he should be asking.'Yes Humane. It's me. Now what the hell are you doing?' Humane points at the roof with a single finger as his confused face is replaced by a worried one. 'Band is trapped. Me get them out of roof.' Salaok steps forward from the crown and to the door of the elevator. His arms folded over his large chest and the tattoos on his arms seemingly blending into each other with the tension. Trying to put on the most comforting voice he could, Salaok speak to Humane quietly. 'Listen Humane, I will have the repair men get the band out. You don't have to do that kind of stuff here Humane. Your here to play in the ring right?' Humane nods his head furiously with a huge smile forming on his face as he steps out of the shaft. A sigh of relief escapes from Salaok mouth as he couldn't have Humane destroying the elevator, or hurting himself before getting hurt in the ring on Monday night at Evolution. Leading Humane back through the GW halls and towards his office, Salaok begins to talk to the man who he honestly can not stand. But if it wasn't for this man having a history with Salaok's father and also playing a part in Salaok's training in PAIN, Salaok wouldn't have anything to do with this freak. But now Salaok found himself, hiring him and booking him into a match that would spill blood in order to make himself happy. 'How are you Humane?' Salaok asks as if he really cares. 'Me good. When me play in ring?' The brain of a child meant Humane would be easily handled for most of the time, which would make life for Salaok easier and a lot less work. Humane wouldn't ask questions about the match, he would do what ever he felt like doing. Making this match including hardcore rules and falls anyway was only because it would have ended up that way as long as Humane was in it. Rules didn't make a world of sense to Humane. 'You will get to play in the ring as soon as you sign some papers.' Salaok couldn't believe he was doing this. Talking with Humane was something he never wanted to do again and now he is walking side by side with the freak discussing him employment. Leading Humane, back to his office, Salaok tried his best at making small talk but it only seemed to confuse Humane even more. But they had finally made it and they passes Liz at her desk and walked straight through before she could stop them. Liz called out to Salaok as he opened the door to his office. 'Salaok, there is a man in your office waiting for you. I told him you were busy but he said it's news you have been waiting for.' Stopping and turning to look at Liz, he thinks to himself about asking her who it was, but decided to simply walk on into his office and find out for himself. There, with his back against the large window looking out on the city below stood a giant. His frame was nothing more than a silhouette as the sun shined from directly behind him but Salaok knew who it was and hoped that the info he brought with him was what he had been wanting to hear for three weeks now. But tension in the room grew as Humane and Quartz laid eyes on each other, and yet not a word was spoken until Salaok asked Humane to sit so the contracts could be signed. 'Humane, sit would you. We have to deal with business revolving around your contract.' Humane sits in the chair which Salaok's hand was pointing to, but rarely took his eyes of the monster in the window.
