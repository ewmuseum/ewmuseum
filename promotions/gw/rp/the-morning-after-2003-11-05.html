The morning after

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/5 The morning after... Birds chirping back and forth amongst themselves as they hop from tree branch to tree branch fluttering their wings along the way can be heard by those that are actually coherent at such an hour, I am not one of these people. For some reason, on this particular day all the color seem to be especially bright, vibrant even. The colorful plumes of the birds squawking overhead seem to glow as sunlight cuts through the tree branches like ultraviolet machetes slashing their way through a vine infested marsh. Come to think of it, all of this stuff happening in the same place at the same time makes for a pretty picturesque scene, although I can’t see it nor do I have any knowledge of it even existing at the current moment, but bear with me here. The birds singing, the sun shining down on the evenly cut bright green blades of grass and beaming through the thin layer of dew droplets slowing making their way into the ground. Sounds a lot like the cover of a hallmark card huh? Well add a sprawling mansion complete with long curving driveway and a yard that could win even the most upscale neighborhood’s “best lawn” award and you’ve got a pretty good picture of what’s going on outside. Now take it one step further and add a nineteen year old millionaire with dreadlocks that hang down to his shoulders and countless tattoos laying spread eagle and not to mention naked smack dab in the middle of said award winning lawn and you’ve got exactly what is going on outside Zero’s house. So here I lie, oblivious to everything happening around me wearing the same thing I came into this world wearing, my birthday suit... and I don’t even know it. Suddenly I feel a cold, wet sensation all over my body and wakes me from my foliage cushioned slumber, which until this point, bordered on an amalgamation of hibernation and coma. Instead of slowly being gently coddled from my incoherent state to one of full awareness I am violently shaken to a conscious state when I pry my eyes open to find myself staring directly into the sun. Talk about your rude awakenings. I immediately close my eyes and roll over onto my stomach still unaware of my surroundings, including two rather interesting facts. The first being that I’m naked, and the second being that I just woke up in the front yard. Ignorance is bliss, and unfortunately these shocking revelations cannot be held at bay any longer. As I lay yawning and stretching in the yard I also begin my morning scratching routine. Everything is going according to plan... until I go to slide my hand under the waistband of my boxers for the final scratch of the bodily tour. It is at this exact moment that I realize I’m naked... and laying in the middle of the yard with the sprinklers on. Damn this water’s cold. Wasting no time I quickly kick up from my horizontal position and effortlessly make a seamless transition into a vertical one before aiming myself at the front door like a human missile and taking off toward it at full sprint. As I expediently make my way to the front door while unsuccessfully trying to cover my manhood with my hands frigid streams of water from the sprinklers pelt my body and do a good job in making the worst of a bad situation. Once near the door I mover over to the right and hide. Shivering behind a patch of six foot high foliage I stretch out as far as I can in order to reach the door bell, then press it several times in rapid succession to insure a speedy response. Soon afterward I hear the faint creak of the door opening and a female voice repeatedly asking “Who’s there?” John must’ve sent Debra to open the door. I creep over to the edge of the line of bushes and look towards the door to see her peeking her head out the door scanning the area. She’s about to find somethin alright. Just as she steps completely out the door to get a better view I burst from the bushes running full speed and dive inside the door, well, more like ran in the house and slipped on the hard wood floor because my feet are wet. But I played it off well. My effort to get up and continue to my room before anyone finds out are quickly foiled by the sound of laughter coming from behind me. You know those dreams people have where they’re at school in front of the class giving a presentation only to realize they’re in their drawz? Well this is worse than that. I turn around to find John laying across the couch laughing hysterically with his head resting in the lap of that chick from the party who is now wearing one of his long sleeve dress shirts, and probably not much else. John continues laughing in between eating scoops of peanut butter from a nearby jar while she continues massaging his head. Although, I doubt that’s what she’s truly focused on as her jar is currently dropped and her eyes widened to the point that they may be at risk of falling out. Just when it seems things couldn’t get any worse Debra walks in but is stopped dead in her tracks as she gasps due to find me standing in the middle of the room wearing nothing more than a few blades of grass that stuck to me because of the water from the sprinklers with a small puddle now accumulating at my feet as the water runs down my body. At this point John is laughing so hard he has to put the peanut butter down and sits upright on the couch next to the woman. Half talking, half laughing he manages to blurt out “You’re looking a little young down there Deac.” to which I respond “Those fuckin” sprinklers are freezing? What tha hell did you do? Melt a glacier in the backyard and run the water straight through the sprinkler system... ya’ll rich people are sick!” That said I continue through the house, but walking now as the need for stealth is no more. I can practically feel the two sets of eyes from Debra and that other chick. As I make my way into the hall in which my room can be found I hear Zero in the front room telling the two women something to the effect of “He’s just a little guy” finally calming down from his laughing fit. Although he probably doesn’t know I heard him I respond with a resounding “Fuck you John!” that echoes through the hall... I also shake my dreads in the middle of the hallway causing water to spray from them in a myriad of directions. Now in a normal house such a thing wouldn’t be that big of a deal, but when you’ve got paintings worth more than all of the wealthiest of businessmen’s lives combined it could create a problem. Oh well... I stroll into my room, also know as the guest room, although I’ve taken the liberty of adding some personal touches. One of which being the graffiti covered walls and menagerie of hip hop and black power posters hanging from them. I doubt he even knows I changed anything seeing as how he never comes over to this wing of the house. I begin to wonder what would happen if one day he did come in and discover all the alterations I’ve made to this room but then just as suddenly as I began I am dragged to a halt by a nagging thought. I’m still naked. Once again aware of my situation I proceed to knock out the three s? of the day. Shit, shower, and shave. Save the shaving part as I don’t have much in the way of facial hair... just a little chin action, but not enough to shave. After getting myself ready for the day I step back into the bedroom and sit on the edge of the bed while checking out some music videos. Apparently in the process of doing so I passed out because I wake up and glance at the clock which is now three hours ahead of where it once was. Curious as to whether a prank is being played on me I walk out into the front of the house to find John fully dressed and preparing to leave the house. In the middle of buttoning his shirt he stops and gives me a blank stare before asking “Is that what you’re wearing” If so, gutsy move.” Puzzled, I ask him “What tha hell are you talkin” bout?” Fastening the last button in the mirror he replies “We’ve got a meeting with Il Capo in an hour, I told you about it earlier. Your door was closed but I could’ve sworn you heard me.” It’s amazing the things you miss while unconscious. I begin to get a sinking feeling as I realize that if I’m not on time I’m in for the bitching out of a lifetime. Wasting no time I race back into my room to prepare.
