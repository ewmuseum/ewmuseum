Surreal Story (Part1)

27 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-27-03 I'm having trouble trying to sleep, I'm counting sheep but running out, As time ticks by, still I try, No rest for cross tops in my room, On my own, here we go Every cowboy sings a sad song, about his girl he lost or his dog that died, there are hundreds of reasons they may be singing that solemn ballad, but im not singing to anyone or anybody, is my pride gone? No’are my feelings hurt’yes, but everyone’s feelings will be hurt at some point in their lives, and before your final fall their will be only two things that you will come to realize’either you laid down or you got knocked down, simple as that. *Beep *Beep *Beep And the sounds of a working respirator as I pushed that cold white door open the hospital room, darkness enveloped me as I took a step inside save for the small lamp which illuminated the upper body of my fallen friend who was laying there lifeless on a bed upon which God knows how many people have died in. I took in a deep breath, I was careful not to make a sound as I stepped carefully across the white tile floor to the bedside of my friend. His eyes closed tight, tubes and tape protruded from his arms like an extension of his body, his face covered by a clear mask, I watched as his breath condensed on the inside and disappeared, and once again, continuing in a non stop repetition. A white bandage wrapped his head trapping his sandy blonde hair inside, as I sat down slowly in a black chair, the leather was cold on my backside and the springs inside creaked a bit as I put all of my weight into it. This is my friend Trey Reed, a car accident has put him in his recent predicament, a tragic accident which never should have happened but I hold myself partly responsible for this, it was my house he was leaving when it happened, after a long night of boozing and mingling with the baddest bitches on the planet, it was bound to have happened, but not here’not like this. I took in a deep breath and closed my eyes, the feeling of guilt surged though my body as I sat there thinking of what if’s and could have’s...seconds turned to minutes’minutes turned into hours’hours turned into sleep? “Mr. Robson” “Mr. Robson” A warm hand on my shoulder, a sudden shake, I fluttered my eyes a bit and looked up to see a brown headed woman clad in white standing over me, my baby blue eyes were blurry trying to focus upon her face, I closed my eyes and opened then once again this time bringing focus and clear vision to my pupils “Yea?” I bellowed in a tired cracking voice as I sat up in the chair and looked over to Reed who was in the same state he was before I took a journey to slumber land “Im sorry visiting hours are over” She exclaimed to me in a sweet tone of voice as I nodded my head and stood up slowly, I tilted my head trying to get the kinks out but it didn’t help, the same feeling of guilt coursed though my body as I turned to Reed “Im sorry buddy’good luck” I said silent and somber as I turned back to the nurse who had woke me up and looked into her soft hazel eyes “Take good care of him” I said with a compassionate concern in my voice as I patted her on her shoulder and walked out of the room, stopping at the doorway to get one last glance at Reed before I left (fade out) But Trey Reed wasn’t the only burden on my mind’I started to wonder if I was the only one rising up to challenge our rivals in the Upper Echelon, Johnny Cruse let me down?.he let me down hard, there are other ways of getting to your adversary than giving into them’and that’s exactly what Cruse did, he gave into them and let everyone who is against TUE down, he told nobody about his predicament, I think now if he would have painted the picture better then this wouldn’t have happened, he wouldn’t have given Price and his goons what they wanted and that was his share of stock in Global Wrestling. “Hi’This is Johnny, Im not available to answer your call right now so if you could leave your name and number and a brief message I will get back with you’thanks’Beep” The scratchy recording of a answering machine as I held my cellular phone to my ear as the glass sliding doors to the hospital slowly opened for me as I stepped out in the concrete and metal jungle known as a parking lot “Hey bro’This is Jay again’get a hold of me, we need to talk” I said as I drew my phone from my ear and flipped it closed. This wasn’t the first time I have tried to reach Cruse’I have tried many times before and each ending in the same result, I don’t know where he is, I don’t think anyone knows where he is, but if he wants to deal with this problem so be it, he knows he fucked up, its his problem to solve’if he wants help he knows where to go. A sharp gust of chilled air bit at my face as I winced a bit and sunk my hands into my blue jean pockets as I continued to walk across the asphalt, the first cold front of quick approaching winter, and Autumn just started I hate cold weather, I wish it was still summer. Absent thoughts in an absent mind as I saw the ass end of my silver Camero protruding from the parking space. I let out a scratchy cough as I looked down at my gold Rolex, the time said 8:55 pm but it felt more like midnight as I looked up at the waning moon casting an eerie light upon the sea of cars around me. **Ring **Ring Johnny...I thought as quickly I pulled my phone from my pocket and looked at the caller ID “B..J..muh fuckin’P’what up brother?” My voice quickly went from joyful to angry, as my face did the same, as I listened to what Ben had to say from the other end of the phone “Are you serious?” Their was a definite change in my voice as I questioned to Palithorpe, whose voice was in an utter state of desperation “Hey’yea’stay there Ben?” I exclaimed out to Ben trying to get a word in, between his drunken rants and raves from the other side of the phone, as I glanced back down to my watch “Ok’I will be there in ten minutes, don’t do anything stupid you hear me” Ok?” I said into the phone as I once again flipped it closed. Damn Ben, this really tops off my night, all I need is two to my friends in the hospital? (To Be Continued)
