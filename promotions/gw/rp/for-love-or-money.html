For Love Or Money?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Spanning the past couple of months... “Igor?” The husky Russian voice echoes around the small trailer. The trailer is narrow, yet bright. There is a couch that bends around the corner of a small table, with a tiny T.V. on a shelf high above the sofa. The curtains are drawn and light floods into the “lounge.” At the opposite end of the trailer are the brown, wooden doors to the bedroom. In between are a sink, cooker, and a couple of cupboards. A short, bulky man is standing in the “kitchen” wearing a navy tracksuit, as he stands at the sink. He has short spiked black hair and a rough beard. “Yes”!” A loud call comes from the back of the trailer. The voice is a lighter higher, but still has a Russian vein to it. Stepping out the “bedroom” appears another man of similar build. He is clean shaven, and has short brown hair. He is wearing a matching tracksuit to the other man. “Did you say something Constantino?” asks Igor. “Why do we always accept to these stunts for stupid American soap operas?” questions Constantino. “We are the Flying Vergomovs. The most exciting and best circus performers in the world today. Why are not millionaires by now” Why are not retired by now, living comfortably? Why?” Constantino’s previous questioning tone has now turned into a rant. “Simple.” Igor’s response is immediate. Constantino seems surprised by the quickness I which his partner responds. “We are still circus performers,” explains Igor as though Constantino should know this. Constantino now seems puzzled. “We have always been performers. Are you trying to tell me we are in the business” That we should have stayed in our homeland and became miners?” “No, comrade.” Igor turns and walks into the bedroom. “What do you mean?” Constantino calls after Igor. After a number of seconds Igor re-appears holding a sheet. He passes it to his fellow circus performer. “Wrestling”!”” Constantino seems puzzled. “You expect me to give up this art for wrestling!” Igor nods. A grin on his face is slowly growing. “Within months we will be millionaires.” Constantino’s puzzled look does not disappear. “Trust me. Millionaires. We will own those companies. We are the finest two athletes in the world. What we can do on tight ropes, and from a standing position will rock the place.” “But it’s wrestling!” Constantino now seems concerned. “I know. I understand. It is nothing compared to the art. But think of the money. Think of the publicity. Think of what we could for the circus. Week in, week out, we will be performing on television. We will let the circus out there on a national stage. We will be legends. Greater than the great Vergomov twins. We will be the pinnacle of the business. We could have our own circus schools. Just picture it.” “No.” Igor had drifted into a day dream as he spoke. He is suddenly ripped out of his trance. “What do you mean “no?” Did you not hear the dream?” “No. It won’t happen. I want the money. I o not want to fall out with my family. I will be disowned. Then our schools will be useless if I am the black sheep of the Zergolov family, and you, the Arefiev clan.” Igor is speechless. It only now seems to have dawned on him that he may be blacklisted by his family. “Igor, think of your father. Think of your grandfather. Think of your great-grandfather. You are from a long string of legends. Is it really worth?” Igor does not respond. He has turned his attention towards a picture to his right, of four men. One is Igor. “Don’t get me wrong. The money is amazing. But when we are alone, who will we spend it with.” Igor looks at the picture, as Constantino bows his head, drops the wrestling flier in the bin and steps out the door of the trailer. --------------- “Get in!” Constantino Zergolov looks down and into the window of the red Ford Mustang that has just pulled up. Sitting in the driver’s seat is his long term circus performer partner, Igor Arefiev. Both men are wearing navy “The Flying Vergomovs” tracksuits. Constantino nods and gets into the car. They pull off. “So where are going?” asks Constantino “Somewhere.” Igor’s response is sharp and abrupt. “We have rehearsals in an hour. Remember that.” “I know. I know. Don’t worry.” “So where are we going?” “Somewhere.” “Igor! Tell me!” Constantino seems to have lost his patience already. “Where” are? we? going?” “You will see.” Igor smiles. Constantino sighs and turns away from his friend and looks out the window. The rest of the car journey is silence as Igor weaves his way among the streets of Chicago. Igor pulls the car up to a gym. He hops out, and beckons his partner to follow. Constantino reluctantly follows suit and steps out of the car. “Come on!” Igor steps into the dark gym. He looks around the foyer. A small desk is at his left side with a blonde receptionist on the phone. In front of him are the signposts. He follows the sign reading “Great Hall.” He smiles. Constantino steps into the gym and follows without looking at any signposts or any of his surroundings. Constantino turns a left corner. “Igor!” He shouts after his partner as his turns a corner at the end of the corridor, 200 feet away. Constantino begins a quick jog down the corridor. He turns the right corner and bursts through double doors. Constantino finds himself in a dark room. He can barely see three feet in front of himself, thanks to the lights in the corridor. He reaches out to his side for a switch. After a number of seconds, a click I heard and the room becomes bright. Constantino is standing a large sports hall with grey stone walls. In the centre of the hall sits a small wrestling ring. Surrounding the ring, there are a number of folding chairs. Gradually growing backwards into simple stands. The “arena” probably holds about three hundred people at most. Constantino’s attention is suddenly drawn to a figure crouched in the middle of the top rope. “Igor. What are you doing?” Igor stands up on the top rope, and begins to walk towards one of the corners. “Look Con. These ropes are three times thicker than a tight rope and barely four feet tall. We would be brilliant at this. We could own the place.” “Yeah, but look around. This place barely holds three hundred people. How are we supposed to be millionaires working in places like this?” “No. No. We won’t be working here. We will be the main attraction in places like Madison Square Garden.” Igor suddenly somersaults twice and lands on the mat. “No problem.” Igor smiles and runs towards his partner and hops the top rope, landing on the mat, outside the ropes. He then drops to the hall floor. “But what about your family?” Constantino seems anxious. “Constantino. I am 23. You are 24. We have so far done everything we possibly can in the circus world. We are the youngest ever members of The Flying Vergomovs. If our families don’t realise we need to do this, to be as big as we can, then it doesn’t matter. Start thinking for ourselves. Do you want to be broke forever?” Constantino is quiet. “This is the only chance we have.” Igor brushes past his partner. “I’ll see you in the car.” Igor pushes the door open and leaves the hall. Constantino looks around the hall, sighs and then leaves. He switches off the light as he goes. --------------- “Dad. It’s Igor.” Igor Arefiev is sitting in his trailer on his cell phone. He on the couch in the lounge. Constantino is standing in the kitchen looking very worried. Both are wearing “The Flying Vegomov” tracksuits. Igor’s is red. Constantino’s is blue. “Stop!” Constantino suddenly breaks into action and shouts at Igor. Igor looks puzzled. “Yeah Dad. I’m doing well.” “Hang up!” Constantino is now whispering very loudly. Do you know the way people can whisper, but it’s really loud? That’s what he is doing. “Listen Dad. Can I ring you some other time?” Constantino smiles. “Yeah. See you soon. Bye bye.” Igor closes up his flip cell phone. “Why did you make me hang up?” “Because I have it!” “Because you have what?” “I have a plan. We won’t have to tell our families. My family live in France, and are doing tours of Russia and Australia in the next three years. And your family live in Spain and are doing tours with my family, right?” Igor nods. “Well then. We don’t need to tell them. We can wrestle all we want, and if they come to visit, we can wear masks. Like the ones we wore years.” “Which?” “My red mask with the yellow strip for me and the blue one you have. It could be part of our persona in the ring.” “No.” Igor is very sharp in his response. “Not a chance.” “Why not?” “That blue mask is the Arefiev colours. If I wear that mask, it’ll be like an advertisement for me to be caught.” “Right okay.” The two performers are silent as they seem to ponder. “What if we get masks made?” If it were a cartoon, a light bulb would have just appeared above Igor’s head. “Whenever we do public appearances, we can wear masks. That way no one will know us.” “Perfect. That way we don’t have to tell our families, and we can stay highly rated in the circus world, and become millionaires.” Both start laughing. Constantino breaks into a really bad impression of an evil super villain’s laugh. “Okay, now we have to find a mask designer.” Igor jumps up and walks into the kitchen of their trailer. He roots in one of the cupboards for a number of seconds, before pulling out a thick phonebook. He walks to the dinner table and drops the phonebook onto it. “Mask designer, mask designer, mask designer.” He is muttering to himself as he flicks through the pages. “We can design the masks.” Igor looks up from the phonebook. Constantino stares back. “We can design the masks. We used to design our costumes for our shows, why don’t we just do the same with this. How hard can it be?” “Let’s try it. If we can’t put together a decent costume, we get a designer in.” “Okay.” Constantino smiles and gets up and leaves the trailer. Igor closes up the phonebook and walks into the bedroom. --------------- “ACTION!” A large shout is heard, and then there is silence. The TV set is quiet. Nothing seems out of place in the office block or the caf? beside it. The street is dark, and only the street lights give the area some life. Suddenly out of a second floor window, there is a large crash. The window smashes and small pieces of glass fall everywhere. As the glass falls, it becomes clear it is not alone. A body is falling with it. The body somersaults twice and spins, and then lands on a conveniently located safe mat. Igor Arefiev rolls from the mat, in the wake of the crew’s applause. He is met by Constantino Zergolov. They “high-ten” and hug. “That was amazing!” exclaims Constantino. Igor just nods. He is out of breath. The pair walk off the set, through a shower of trailers, cables and crew members. “That was the highest jump we have done for this.” “That was the last jump we do,” exclaims Igor. “I am not going to continue doing that for thirty years just to make money.” The Flying Vergomov members reach their trailer. Igor, wearing a grey sweater and blue jeans collapses onto the sofa around the kitchen table. Constantino is wearing a green sweater and black trousers. He grabs a stool and sits down. “What does that mean?” asks Constantino. Igor looks up. He reaches into a bag on the ground and pulls out a number of pages. He then slides them across the table towards Zergolov. “What’s this?” Constantino lifts the pages and begins to scan read the first sheet. “It’s a contract,” exclaims Igor. “I sent a couple of videos to some federations and one has asked us to join their school. We’ll get training and see how things.” Constantino nods. “So we are definitely going for it?” asks Zergolov. “Yup.” Igor collapses back onto the couch. Constantino is silent for a number of seconds. He then jumps up and leaves the trailer. --------------- “What are you wearing?” Constantino is bursting with laughter as he rolls around the couch of his lounge. Igor Arefiev walks out of his bedroom, in the pair’s flat. “What do you mean?” Igor is standing in front of his tag team partner wearing red tights, red tightrope shoes, and a red mask that engulfs his whole head. Compared to the white minimalist d’cor of the flat, he stands out like a sore thumb. “What is that?” “It’s my wrestling attire.” Igor seems shocked by his partner’s reaction. “What’s with the flame?” Constantino has stopped his erratic laughter and is now taking note of the costume. “Which flame?” asks Igor. “That one,” points up at Igor’s mask. There is a flame growing up the front. “Why have you got a flame?” “I don’t know. When I was working with the designer, he said it would look cool. And I trust him.” “Okay.” Constantino nods. “Why have you written “The Flying Vergomovs” down the leg?” The pair’s attention is drawn to the left leg of Igor’s tights. “The Flying Vergomovs” is written up the left leg, in flame colours. “Oh,” exclaims Igor. “That’s our tag team name.” Constantino jumps up from the white sofa in disgrace. “Are you mad?” Igor shakes his head. “I thought we discussed “Arefiev & Zergolov!” Or, as per usual, you just made up your own mind and didn’t bother really caring about what I wanted!” “No.” Igor is trying to calm his friend down, although his facial expression is not visible due to the mask. “You just decided, I know. I’ll screw Con over again and risk both our futures and publicise the fact that two circus performers are entering the world of wrestling. Are you stupid”!”!” “NO!” Igor has had enough. “I went in to see the Global Underground design team and I said we wanted to be called “Arefiev & Zergolov” but they said no. They said we would sell better as “The Flying Vergomovs.” And, we are only about the money. I’m not doing it for the money. So I agreed. The better we sell, we quicker we can get out of this business.” Constantino is silent. Igor is silent. A pin drops. “Right, okay. I’m in it for the money too.” “Great, “cause your attire is in the bedroom.” Constantino looks up, and follows Igor’s arm into one of the bedrooms. As Constantino disappears into the room, Igor looks up and down his outfit. He walks around the lounge, striking a number of “muscle man” poses. He kicks and punches the air a few times, and then groans in a manly manner. Igor walks up to a full-length mirror on the far wall from the bedroom door. He begins to inspect his look. He pushes his fingers in the mouth gap of his mask, and then in the eyes. He turns round and looks at his butt. As he stretches the waistband, Constantino re-appears from the bedroom. He is wearing a dark blue mask. Encircling the mask there are a number of diagonal yellow and white stripes. He is topless, and wearing blue tights, and blue trainers. The tights and shoes have white and yellow stripes down the side. “So what do you think?” His voice is a little muffled due to the mask. “Very smooth,” laughs Igor. The two men chuckle and strut around the living room. They are both punching and kicking the air. Igor stands up on the sofa and somersaults onto the white rug. Constantino turns round. “I don’t know,” exclaims Igor, “that mask feel weird when I’m in the middle of a somersault.” “I was watching some wrestling tapes. There aren’t many somersaults performed. You’ll be okay.” “Hope so.” Constantino moves towards the mirror. He strikes a muscle man pose in front of it. “Grr!” “What?” Igor swings round to face his partner, “did you say something?” Constantino, in a startled manner, arches his head round to face Arefiev. “No, I didn’t utter a word.” Igor nods. The two men continue to study their new outfits. “It feels really different from the circus costumes we used to wear.” “I know,” agrees Arefiev, “it just feels?” “Weird.” Both men finish the sentence together. They look at each other. “There’s no turning back now.” “I know.”
