The Work of the Devil

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He had been living in this strange world for a couple of what they called months, and during that time he had learned a lot about these strange people and their costoms. They were so different from the time in which the Huns were alive, the time when the Roman empire had been the most powerful on earth. He had read, and watched, and learned to fit in, but he decided against it. He was immortal, he could do anything he wanted to do. So he always had his sword at his side, he always carried what he had found that belonged to his time. The clothes, the shoes, the jewelry, everything where from his time. He hated to remember that time, but he knew that it was his time and nobody could change that. He had learned of religion, and he laughed, because he had seen the devil. His very sword, and the source of his immortality was given to him by the devil himself, that wonderful gift that is eternal life. How could the devil be so bad if he would give him that wonderful gift. There were definately some strange things, things he could have never predicted that would exist, but then again, this was two thousand years after his time. It was easy to for him to learn new thgings at first, but the more time he lived in this world, the more he adapted to it. He had learned english easily, I guess that immortality was not the only thing that sword was good for. The question for him remained, could there be other like him? Could there be other immortals? It was certainly plausible. If he was immortal, then there could be others. Would they try to kill him? If they wanted to, they would have to put up one hell of a fight, because he kept his sword always by his side. His eyes were like an eagle's eyes, always ready, always thinking of his next move. Years of leading an army to war would do that to you. He led the Huns in three mayor wars, two victories and one defeat, though the defeat was caused by Attila's overconfidence. He led them against the Visigoths, the Ostrogoths and then all the way towards Rome. After being betrayed by Attila, he left the Huns only to be found by the Barbarians in Germania, they helped him get revenge, if he trained their men. After getting his revenge, destroying the race of the Huns completely, he had to kill two other people and he would be complete, but it was impossible for him to do it by himself, he needed help, but he was not going to trust anyone after what had happened to him. He went to a soothsayer and told her his plight, she agreed to help him get to the only man that could help him. The devil in the flesh. He followed her instructions and visited the devil. He went by the name Ur, not many people knew he was the devil, but he was. He told Dagoth that if he helped him, he would have to give him his soul. He agreed. The devil then created an exact replica of Dagoth's sword and in it put powers that every mortal man would have died for, immortality was the most important one, but he made Dagoth a true warrior for the Devil. Now he had to do his bidding. He walked through the streets of Texas as he got nearer and nearer to the house he was trying to find. In this house was his first mission after reincarnation. He had to kill a Catholic priest who believed so much in his faith that he was disturbing the Devil's plans. It was child's play, these missions were never a challenge to Dagoth, but he had to do this in order to keep his promise. After he repaid the Devil he would be free of him and on his own again. He walked through the sidewalk, looking at the people walking past him, admiring the way they forgot that they were used to play a game of chess between heaven and hell, that they were just pawns in the game. He looked at himself and he saw another piece in a board being moved by a huge hand. It was all a game, but he did not like being used. He moved through the night swiftly, getting glances only because of his build and the sword at his side. He finally got to his destination. He walked towards the door of the house and knocked on the door. The door opened and the priest was standing there waiting to be killed. He seemed surprised to have seen this huge man standing in front of him, then he saw the sword, and his eyes told a whole new tale. Dagoth pushed him inside and closed the door behind him. 'Ill Diavolo! Ill Diavolo!' He tried to scream, but he didn't have air on his lungs. 'I am not the devil, but I come here in his behalf. You have been a good man, lived your life following the doctrines of your God and you have made people walk trhough the path of righteousness, so now you are to accompany God in heaven. You have but one chance to save your life, and that is to give your soul to Ill Diavolo,' said Dagoth towering over the short italian priest. 'No, I will never give my soul to ill Diavolo,' was the priest's response. 'You brought this on yourself priest,' Dagoth answered drawing his sword. The light from the moon reflected into the metal of the sword shining Dagoth's face. He looked at his sword and doubts came over his mind, should he do it? Was it worth it? He looked at the priest and decided to do what he had to do. 'If you see my wife and daughter up there, tell them that I'm sorry,' he said with a suden fear in his voice. He had to do it. The priest nodded and started praying in his mind. He closed his eyes and raised his head up towards the sky. The motion was as quick as the blink of an eye. The body of the priest stood there for a few seconds until it slumped down to the floor and fell sideways, his heasd rolling down to his side. There was a trail of blood left on the sword and a pool of blood below the disembodied head of the priest, the white tiles no longer white but crimson red. Dagoth put the sword in front of his face and smelled the blood, the smell invigorated him, but at the same time sickened him. He had smelled blood countless times before, but never the blood of a man without a weapon. He cleaned the blade with the clothes of the priest and left the house, he had to meet with Ur to give him the news. He sheathed his sword and was on his way. He knew he had to get used to this, used to killing people with no weapons, so at least this was a good iniciation for the work that was coming. While he walked he remembered the people he had killed, he remembered the faces of those that had helped in the assassination of his family, Kamer, Cassius, the Caesar, Attila, all dead, all dead by his sword except for Attila, who died for his greed. Ur's house was close by, he had killed some unlucky fellow for it. Dagoth kept walking, his eyes lost in thought, lost in time and space. In no time, he was where he wanted to be. He opened the door to an dark house, there was a single red candle burning in the living room, and an open window with the full moon right in front of it where you could have see it's splendor, but this night made the moon take a reddish color. A slim tall man sat in a chair in front of the candle. 'It is done,' Dagoth told the man. 'Good job Kinslayer, I don't have anything else for you to do this night, but I will come calling very soon. Until you repay me you are mine,' he said never turning around. He put his hand on the flame of the candle and it immidiately made the flame move from the candle to his hand. 'It's fascinating what you can do with your powers Dagoth, it was my gift to you, remember that! You owe me,' he told Dagoth finally turning around. The handsome man that stood in front of Dagoth was exactly like he remembered him from before only with clothes that made him fit in in this world. 'I see you have not left the old tradition of your sword. You could use a gun you know, much easier and less messy,' he told him with a grin. 'I hate these guns. There is no honor in killing with a gun, the sword is the ultimate weapon of warfare, anyone who uses guns is scared to face a man who can handle a sword. I would never part with my sword,' Dagoth told him while he rubbed the hilt of the sword. 'I will be going now, I have other things to do,' Dagoth told him as he turned around and dissapeared into the night. The only thing in Dagoth's mind right now were the words, those dreadful words...'I own you.' 'Nobody owns me!' He started to walk down the street and into his hotel room. Back at the house, Ur stood there with the flame dancing in the palm of his hand. He laughed and closed his fist to make the flame dissapear. 'Great job immortal, there is plenty more to do.' The laugh that came after that, was one of the most chilling things one could ever hear, the Devil laughing is what it was, nothing to be joking around with.
