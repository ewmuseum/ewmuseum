One More Time

27 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/04 [Start Scene] [The camera fades in to show the Chicago penthouse home of Core Wrestling’s own power couple, Jason Hughes and Veronica Paige. It is nighttime and the two are seated comfortably on one of the large black leather couches with Hughes seated on the edge cushion with his arm around Paige who is curled up on the middle cushion with her head against Hughes? muscled chest. The couple is watching as the credits of some old movie begin to move across the TV screen. Paige turns slightly to change her position, the pressure she creates eliciting a moan from Hughes. Paige quickly sits up, looking at Hughes with a concerned expression.] “Oh baby, I’m sorry.” “No, it’s ok. I’m fine.” [Hughes says slowly as he pulls his shirt off to reveal a very painful looking bruise across his lower stomach and around the left side of his ribs. The bruise has taken a black and blue color to it and Hughes grimaces softly as Paige touches the spot gingerly.] “Jason, please go see a doctor about this. It looks really bad.” [Paige says with a hint of pleading in her voice as Hughes shakes his head in response.] “It’s ok Roni, I am fine.” [Paige looks at him angrily and pulls away from him, sitting on the middle cushion of the couch with her head in her hands as she silently pouts. Hughes sighs and moves closer, his hand holding his ribs carefully as he moves until he is close enough to run a hand through Veronica’s long red hair. He whispers softly into her ear as her head remains down.] “Roni’what’s wrong?” [Paige looks up at Hughes slowly before speaking.] “Jason, you went into the Pay-Per-View expecting to referee a match and you ended up taking a Running of the Bulls and a Stairway to Heaven from Matt Epic. I mean, you could barely stand for the hours following Ringmaster’look at what it did to your back and ribs. If that wasn’t bad enough’you’re competing for Global Wrestling in a couple of days and have a match against Nick Worrell on Wednesday. I’m just worried about you, that’s all, I know you are going to do what you have to do but I am just worried that you are pushing yourself way too much.” [Hughes smiles and slowly reaches under Veronica’s head, moving her head up gently with his hand under her chin until he is staring at her amazing gray eyes.] “Roni, I know that you are worried and maybe I am pushing myself but this is the time when I have to push myself. I lost to a guy names “Smiley” in what was to be MY Pay-Per-View and now I have not only Matt Epic and Nick Worrell to deal with but probably Munson also.” “I know’believe me I understand, but I just hate to see you hurting Jason. I just’I just love you too much. Why not cancel this GW match” I just don’t see the point of it.” [Hughes cannot help but smile at her tender words and he thinks for a moment.] “The point of it is loyalty.” “Your loyalty is gonna get you bedridden one of these days.” “Just as long as you’re there with me then I won’t mind.” [Veronica giggles softly and smiles as she moves her arms around Hughes? neck and kisses him gently.] “I promise, there is no way I would pass on a few days in bed with you.” “Good, then why don’t we relax for now” I have my suit on already’why don’t you put yours on and we can go chill out in the hot tub. It gets so little use lately.” [Veronica’s eyes light up at the suggestion and she quickly gets up, giving Hughes kiss before hurrying up to their bedroom to change, calling back as she leaves.] “I’ll be right down baby; don’t have any fun till I get back.” [Hughes shakes his head and makes his way past the kitchen, opening up to the enclosed sunroom at the rear of the penthouse where the hot tub he had given to Roni as a gift now stood. He quickly peeled off his jeans and shoes to reveal a long black and silver swim suit and stretched slightly, grimacing from the pain of the deep bruise, before stepping up into the hot tub. The hot water hits him perfectly and he sighs a bit as he slowly sinks down into the heated up, his back against the soft plastic of the pool. His words are slow and direct and being heard as he relaxed in silence in the hot tub, with thoughts of the weeks past and future racing through him.] There are points in a person’s life where they are conflicted. They begin to worry about whether what they are doing in their lives is right or wrong. There are times when a man, perhaps a wrestler, wonders if he is working to hard on doing what others want of him. There are times when a wrestler may begin to doubt his own abilities. There are times in which a man may believe he no longer deserves the chances that he is being given. There are times like this in the life of every man. This is not one of those times. [Hughes? hand comes up through the water, cupping some as he splashes it lightly over his face. He is exhausted; exhausted from what might as well have been a war last week and what will no doubt be a war still to come.] This is not one of those times because this past week cured me of any doubts that I could have had. I cannot stop going over that moment in the ring with Matt Epic’it was something out of ancient times, months have passed since the debacle where he stole my Legacy Title. You had two men, both legends’no less than Hector and Achilles’in the ring at the same time. Only Achilles didn’t stab Hector in the back. He looked him in the eyes as he killed the warrior. There is a shortage of warriors on this planet, a shortage of men loyal to the core and loyal to their country. You have the soldiers, but whom else? Who in wrestling? That’s why I immediately said yes when Roni came in and told me that some guy named Maverick was on the phone about a match. They say family is all about blood. I don’t have any blood relatives besides my three-month old son and Roni. But blood is not the only way to judge family. You look towards your friends. You look towards your loyalties. When you’re Jason Hughes you look towards Maverick Reno, Silent Wind and John Zero. You look towards the Upper Echelon. [Hughes slowly pushes himself away from the wall, moving around the hot tub on his knees as only his head is visible, trying to at least to some extent dull some of the pain he is feeling. The jets of the hot tub begin to go and the vibrations of them against his lower back must be working to some point as his eyes are slightly glazed over as he tries to just relax. He is facing away from the penthouse and looking out at the large window overlooking the city of Chicago, everything looking so small and far removed from the little water world he is in at the moment.] [Suddenly a pair of hands wraps gently around Hughes? eyes from behind and he grins as he hears the seductive voice of the woman behind him.] “Guess who baby.” “Um’the most beautiful woman in the world” [Hughes smirks as he turns around to see Veronica standing behind him wearing a small pink bathrobe.] “That was a perfect answer.” “I know.” “You got enough room in there for one more?” “For you, hell yes” [Veronica laughs a bit as she pulls off the long pink robe to reveal a small black and pink thong bikini that hides almost nothing as its painted-on appearance clings to every curve of Veronica’s body. Hughes? eyes widen slightly and he shakes his head as she takes his hand to help steady her as she enters the hot tub.] “You look’wow’you look amazing in that.” [She laughs and kisses him gently on the lips as she slowly sinks into the warm and inviting water, causing her tanned body to glisten and shine as she moves to the seat next to Hughes and lays her head softly against his shoulder.] “This water feels so great Jason’is it helping your back?” “Yeah, it’s helping a bit but it won’t get much better until after the show when we can hopefully relax.” “I can’t wait to just be at home with you and not have to worry about anything else.” “I know darling but I still have two shows left to do before we can relax but then I promise’just you and me for a week.” “You better live up to that buddy.” [Veronica says with a smirk as Hughes? hands lightly move across her back and shoulders, causing Veronica to lean forward slightly as he rubbed her back gently, kissing her neck every few seconds as they sat in silence in the hot tub with only the noise of the jets to lull them.] I am a very’passionate person. I am passionate for this sport, for my reputation, for Veronica, for Cassian and for other lesser things. I am passionate about these things because I put everything I have into them. My blood and my sweat and my agony go into this point of competition that we call wrestling. Who truly know what I have done? There are some’these rookies for instance who see me with all these accolades to my name; eleven titles including five World Heavyweight reigns, the Ringmaster Trophy, one Hall-of-Fame induction and perhaps another one in the future, a Wrestler of the Year award and being a member of the Upper Echelon. These are the pieces of my legacy that they see and it attracts them to me’perhaps not to cheer for me, but to respect what I do. But the question remains’do they truly know what went into all that glamorous gold? I doubt that there are many, even in Core, who recall my bout from years past with Steele, Wyld and Law. They never saw my frustration at losing to Harris not once, not twice, but on three separate occasions. They don’t know about Sage Sabbot and what my matches with him did for my career. They don’t recall over one hundred matches across the United States and Europe with stops in Australia, Africa and Japan along the way. These are not the things that they will remember; these are the things that my body and my mind alone will take to my grave. My legend will only grow with gold’nothing more and nothing less. That’s a truth of life. I’m beyond that. This title I hold, this Legacy Title that has been mine for all but a few weeks of the past year, and it will be my last. I’m done with gold, all I seek is glory. The Upper Echelon is mine as much as any others? but being back in Global Wrestling, back where those few fans only remember a man named “Dynasty” there isn’t the prestige factor of my name as there is in Core. It’s not the Legacy Champion. It’s not the Ringmaster. It’s the member of the Upper Echelon. It’s the glory, not the gold. [Veronica leans back against Hughes as he runs his hands over her shoulders, turning slightly to meet him in a kiss. They slowly break apart, their bodies still close and Veronica speaks softly, sighing slightly as Hughes? touch makes her body react.] “I was just thinking?” “Yeah?” “I’ve never felt as safe as I do when you are holding me. I mean’it’s just that when you touch me it feels’I don’t know’right.” [Hughes doesn’t say anything but instead wraps his arms around Veronica’s small waist as she scoots closer to him and they just sit there in silence.] She has faith in me. I need that I guess. I have never thought it was important to have someone backing me up but to know that she is going to be there for me’it does make it easier for me to know what I have to do. The Nevermore. The MOB. The Lion’s Den. Ruckus Inc. The Elite. There always seems to be a faction against the Upper Echelon. The only fact that matters those is that TUE is still around. The Elite is the newest issue and Maverick needed a fourth man to be the problem solver. He couldn’t rely on those in GW besides Wind and Zero so he came to me. I of course said yes. The chance for one more stand as TUE is something I cannot and would not pass up. The chance to move away from “Old School” and back into the days where I handed out pain and delighted over bloodshed is what I need. One more time to quench my thirst for death and destruction. One more time with my “family”. One more time. Just One More [Hughes sighs lightly and then smirks as Veronica slowly turned around, getting comfortable on his lap and wrapping her arms around his neck as they kiss softly, simply enjoying their time together.] [The camera pans back slowly to take in the full view of the room until the shot begins to slowly fade out, darker and darker until finally only darkness remains’only darkness and then?] [Nothing] [End Scene] [?
