Making a Future

24 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.24.04 WARDROBE: ~Ken~ Calvin Klein Black Label: Black Crewneck Sweater Calvin Klein Black Label: Charcoal Flannel Slim Pants Calvin Klein: Black Leather Lace-Up Shoes ~Gunner~ Levis: Black and White Stripped Sawtooth Shirt Levis: Denim Premium Easy Rider Jacket Levis: Denim Superlow Jeans Puma: Black Leather Sneakers Ken and Gunner were still signing autographs outside of Fenway Park, alongside the likes of Steven King and Ben Afleck and Jennifer Garner, forty-five minutes after Boston had won Game One of the World Series. The crowd of people surrounding them put a huge smile across Ken's face as he scribbled his signature on everything from wrestling magazines to baseball programs and ticket stubs. He glanced up at Gunner as he handed one autograph back to a particularly happy guy and his girlfriend. She was so beautiful and generous. The smile that was etched across her face was beyond genuine, and Ken fell in love with her again, for the hudredth time that day. She lifted her eyes up to his and warmth filled his bdy as the two shared a romantic moment. He began making his way over to her while signing the slew of items that were being pushed to him and fially after about fifteen more minutes they broke free, making their way down the Boston streets that Ken was too familiar with. 'You know, if I could move back to any one place I've ever lived,' Ken began, 'it would be here.' 'It is nice,' replied Gunner, walking arm in arm with the Superstar while leaning her head on his shoulder. The two continued their stroll down the crowded street and through their fellow raucus Red Sox fans. They both turn, laughing, as a few kids, followed by their parents, run through cheering at the tops of their lungs while flying Boston pennants behind them. They turn back around with huge smiles on their faces as their eyes come upon a horse and carriage that's empty minus the man behind the reins. Ken looks down at Gunner and winks before leading her to the carriage. He held her hand, helping up into the beautiful wooden holder and then pulled himself in beside her. 'Where can I take ya' two young loveboids,' asked the driver in heavy Bostonian as they covered up their legs with the blanket supplied in the seat. 'Anywhere,' answered Ken. 'Anywhere at all.' The driver nodded at him before turning back around and whipping the reins a few times, putting the horses into motion. As the sounds of their feet clopped on the cobblestone, Gunner and Ken wrapped their arms around each other. 'I've missed this,' said a trembling Gunner with he head laying on Ken's chest. 'I've missed this so much.' 'So have I, Sweetheart,' admitted Ken with a warm smile on his face. He continued rubbing her shoulder and arm in an attempt at keeping her warm, but what he didn't realize was the just being with Ken warmed her inside, just as being with her did the same for him. Their lovers' embrace was passionate and felt like something out of a fairy tale film. It was like Ken was a young Humphrey Bogart and Gunner was a beautiful young Katherine Hepburn. The seen was perfect and the night was electric. 'So... what do you do now,' asked Gunner. Ken sat, silently holding her for a bit longer before answering. 'Well, I've had a past, ruined it, lost it, and found it... how about now... we just focus on the now and work on making a future.' She looked up at him smiling. 'I think that's a brilliant idea.' The two embraced and shared a long passionate kiss as they continued down the cobblestone street. At last, Ken's life had come full-circle. At last, he was where he belonged. At last... he was happy. She is his strangth.
