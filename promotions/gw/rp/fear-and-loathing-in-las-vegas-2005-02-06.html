(Strata) Friday 5th February, 2005 Corvus? Manor, 9:12 PM “Anyone home?” A voice echoed through the halls of the manor. There was absolutely quiet throughout. I was just a baby when I got married to you, how in the hell was I supposed to cope with losing the child that made us both so happy? Do you think that I just let my mother tell you to go to hell when you came all the way to Zdice to get me? SHE made that decision, not me! They were keeping me locked up in an upstairs room of the estate like I was the village madwoman, and I didn't even know you'd come to get me until Anezka broke down and told me because she felt so bad keeping it from me. I never forgave them for that, and I would have done something about it, but you'll forgive me if I didn't get back to you on it, I was being tried for witchcraft.' She bit off the last part with a growl, the emotion rising up in her throat and turning quickly to anger. She was rooted to the spot, adrenaline coursing through her despite her age. Pushing the Beast down was going to become difficult if she continued at this clip. ?And what about our son? Would it have killed you to have at least try and stick around for his sake? It was bad enough he lost his mother but eventually because of what happened in Zdice I came back home and drank myself into oblivion and that is probably one of the reasons I’m still standing here ARGUING with you over this! Not like it would make any big deal to you but if I had actually died when I was supposed to I might have reincarnated myself over the generations and been able to partake in trivial things like knowing what a fucking cheeseburger and fries with ketchup taste like or even going to the beach and getting a tan that wouldn’t make me explode into little pieces. Have you ever thought about things like that Lia? I highly doubt it and as much as you accuse me of being selfish...I just seem to hear in my mind, what about me? What about poor Lia? To tell you the truth if the only little part of Aric that is left in my body didn’t care for you I would have killed you 4 years ago!” This was certainly a venting match between them. It was probably one of the best things to happen in a long time between them. They would at least understand why they felt the way they did. Corvus spent too long harboring things and this was the biggest of them all, the grand daddy if you will. Anger growled through her, ripping her apart. 'Oh please, are you even paying attention? I couldn't come back because my mother decided that I wasn't leaving Zdice ever again, or did you forget so quickly that the Vlaszy line had loyalties to uphold. It was my family that made the decision to put me back in servitude to Laanto. They changed their minds, and in case you don't remember, it was a hell of a lot harder for women back then to do anything about something once the family made up its mind to keep her from doing it! I loved him too, but it was all a lot for a young woman to handle! I've never said I didn't make any mistakes! You're the one who seems to find it a hell of a lot easier to believe that because it's easier than admitting, even at our ages, that there were things back then that we didn't have under our control.' She paused for a moment, her limbs beginning to shake. 'I don't want MY mortal life back, so why the fuck should you? Even if I did, there's not a damn thing that can be done about it now, now is there, Aric? Are you going to start blaming me for siring you too? Because I'm not the one with that wretched blood running through my veins, and I didn't curse you with your wretched immortality! And back to this issue of coming back to you! You KNOW I loved you, so don't EVEN accuse me of being selfish about it. They were trying to BURN ME AT THE STAKE, Aric, and the ONLY reason I was saved from that fate is because Vaclav Ionescu chose to bestow his gifts on me. I HAD NO CHOICE!' She finished at a fair roar, her eyes sparking with anger. “I have to ask you, who is the new guy” Not like it is any of my business but I’m just curious. Most people would be when something like this happens. Though I’m not going to argue with you...you want rid of me then I’ll give you what you want. It just gives me one final reason to kill the rest of Aric that lingers on. Don’t worry about finding something sharp...I have my dagger.” Corvus finds the right paper and walks over to the table that is there towards the balcony. He pulls out his dagger which looks very intricate and antique out of the back of his pants. Corvus brings it to his hand and slices it open allowing blood to flow onto the point. He finds the line and signs it in his blood to make it legitimate. In his mind any document such as that should be signed in ones own vitae. She bit her lip, her incisor biting sharply down on it, but it didn't matter at this point. This was one of the things she hated the most about him, how he was always marginalizing her feelings. She paused, trying to calm herself. 'I didn't say I wanted rid of you, Aric, I'm trying to do what's best for both of us. Considering how much you obviously despise me, despite many attempts by both of us to make this work, it's obvious that we're just making each other miserable. Maybe we loved each other in the past, but as you said, you'd rather kill Aric than BE Aric.' She shrugged, slightly, but was actually letting some emotion show through this time. It was plain that this WAS actually hurting her, contrary to her anger. 'The guy you're asking about is Virgil. He's the younger brother of the pack ductus for the pack that I was priest in before my memory got all fucked up. I'm entirely certain that you're implying he's my romantic interest, but he's not. He's a friend, a friend who took care of me when I needed someone to do it. I almost got murdered back in Cleveland by a pack of Hunters before he came into the picture. And he's the one who took me back to Prague and made sure I remembered who the hell I was before I did something stupid to get myself killed. I can't just tell him to piss off, because I owe him.' Corvus nodded as he licked the wound on his hand closed and looked for something to clean his knife with. He walked into the bathroom and took care of that and came back out. He looked at her trying to clear his thoughts of all this craziness but it wasn’t working. Every other glance at her he took reminded him of her way back when. “I can’t make you feel anything you’re not going to or want to. That is why I signed the papers. Once of us wants to be free...but which one of us is it really” I’m sure we’ll be in touch again down the road...that’s inevitable...but what side of the road we’re on is the tricky part.” Corvus figured they both said everything they needed to so there was no need to posture any more than was necessary. He began to make his way towards the door; there was still plenty of time for him to get home without worry about becoming a dust particle. She paused. 'Aric... wait. Can I say one more thing? I still don't think I've managed to make myself clear... it's hard, the Beast being so close to the surface for Fiends...' God, Lia, what the hell are you doing? That was the only question that ran through her mind right now. “You can say whatever you like...I hold no sway over you as I’ve turned over any rights I had to that little paper sitting on that desk. I may sound cold, I may sound cruel even, but at least you understand me just a little bit better now.” She narrowed her eyes. 'Fine. If that's the way you want it, fine. I didn't come here WANTING to get you out of my life. I came here wanting to make you happy, because you've always acted like I was such a chain on that ever since we met up again. Do you want me to say it? A part of me will always love you, Aric. A part of me, standing here, right now, is still very much in love with you, just like when we were children in Gaul, just married. I'm not trying to get away from it, but you said it yourself. You want to kill the part of you that cares about me, and you've just about done it. What else can I do but let you go? I don't understand why you're standing here acting like I'm trying to get rid of you. You say so often that you hate me that I thought this would make you happy.' Her green eyes zeroed in on his. Damnit, Aric, I know you're in there, no matter how cold you're acting. But it was stupid of her to hold out hope like that, wasn't it? It wasn’t easy for him as he had thought it was. He didn’t want to have that hate inside of him for her...he wanted to channel it and use it against people who deserved to get pummeled into batter. Corvus ran a hand through his hair and realized that more of his old life was still there at the very bottom of his non living vessel. “We wouldn’t have married again if there wasn’t anything there...I want you to do what makes you happy. We both want to do what makes the other happy yet we don’t know what that is. I don’t want to turn this into something more than it isn’t. We were Romeo and Juliet before it became a twinkle in Shakespeare’s eye. I can’t ask you to stay here; you have commitments elsewhere...I can’t take those away from you regardless of how selfish I could be. I signed those papers to try and ease the pain of all the things I did to you because of something that happened long before anyone these days even remember. For two people who seemed to care for each other as we have...we sure spent a lot of time trying to cover it up.” She closed her eyes momentarily, then opened them again, nodding. 'Ano... we did. I don't know why things went wrong, and I suppose that has been bothering me for quite some time now. We lead very different lives, and I know that it's not easy for two people with as different blood flowing through us as we have right now to get along. You're right, I can't stay here. I'm carving out a destiny for myself, but as only a Tzimisce knows... deep ties stretch beyond any distance that might sever them. It's been hard for me to figure out what happened between us, even harder to figure out how to fix it. I am a different entity than I was then... I'm not the little girl who fell in love with you, and I've been blaming that on myself for a long, long time now. But there's nothing that can be done about it... Metamorphosis affects us all, and I've realized that since going back home. I thought maybe that's how we could fix things, to change with our age, but maybe I was wrong... It's hard to be sure what to do when I don't even know if there is anything either of us could to do fall in love and be happy again without completely lying to ourselves about it.' She wasn't attempting a pity party, or acting on childish melodrama as she often did out of habit with him. This time, her voice was changed, it was that of a woman trying to realize some very hard truths, and cope with them as best as she could. “As much as I wish I could ask you to give this a chance and stick around...I know that something else is waiting for you. That’s the hardest part of it really but the one thing that we have always had the most trouble doing is finding where Aric and Lia begin and where Corvus and Shadow end. You have the papers now...it’s your choice to do with them what you wish. I just have one request left out of what is left of me. I need to stay with you one last time so that I can finally piece things together.” He didn’t expect a positive answer from his request but it was the one thing he thought might solve some things going on in his head and possibly hers as well. Fate had come and gone in their lives so many times in and out and this could possibly be the finality to that. For a moment, she stood silent. Then, her expression softened. 'I don't want you to think that I've given up. Anything can work, if that's what both of us want. But the hardest part is doing what is best for both of us while still managing to love each other, isn't it?' She gestured to the room around her. 'You're free to stay tonight... I just want to call Virgil, and let him know that I'm okay. He worries a little too much about me since The Den almost ate me alive in Cleveland...' She paused. 'I want answers just as much as you do.' He nodded in understanding. If he had known about it he would have been worried as well. For the moment he felt somewhat relieved that they were at least going to see if they couldn’t work out their differences at least one more time before allowing the fate that kept them apart so long swept them up in the current. Before she walked into the bedroom area he grabbed her lightly by the arm and slid his hand down to her wrist. A villainous smile was sported on his lips as he spoke to her. “You know there was one thing about you that I always liked...you were able to use the key that unlocked the beast. Things are going to get rather interesting around this city of sin...guaranteed.” He let go of her wrist and watched her walk out of the room. Slowly Corvus walked to the balcony and opened it, stepping out to feel the air wisp against his body. All the fancy lights that adorned the strip were aglow as he watched as people were mulling about even at such a late hour. Such an unsuspecting lot they were...and this was only the beginning that has a much bigger playing field waiting to be untapped.
