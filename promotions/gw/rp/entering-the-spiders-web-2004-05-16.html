Entering the spiders web.

16 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 16th, 2004. The stinging rain bit into my tan skin as the wind blew it directly into my face. I batted my eyelids squinting slightly into the misty black night that was set before me. Looking around I spotted the large apartment building that loomed ominously in the night. Walking slowly up the steps through the swirling rain I glanced at the thick oak door that barred me from entering this building. I twisted the knob…but to no avail for alas it was locked. Gazing at the door I paused momentarily as I considered knocking it off of its hinges but as thick as that door was the only thing that would break would be my shoulder. I knew better than to buzz someone to gain access for I wanted this visit to be quite a surprise and I had the feeling that it would be. With the door securely locked I smiled, reveling in the knowledge that I had came prepared. Extracting a small lock pick set from the pocket of my black leather trench coat I knelt down the knee of my tattered Ralph Lauren jeans resting on the cold cement. The wind blew violently threatening to rip the remnants of my torn up sleeveless white shirt from beneath my unbuttoned trench coat. Inserting the lock pick into the knob I turned…but there was no result. Shoving in another lock pick I turned again and this time to my satisfaction the door crept open, squeaking in protest as it swung on its hinges. Stepping inside, my feet, clad in black Converse Chuck Taylor's felt the soft carpet below them as I dropped the set of lock picks into my jacket pocket and shut the door behind me as it once again groaned it's disagreement. With the door closed and the wind no longer inviting itself to whip through the hallway my bluish gray eyes focused throughout the dim hall. The only salvation from darkness was the light at the end of the hallway and from that light I could read the reflection of an apartment number. '1-A.' The large gold letters on the brown oaken door gave me the impression that this would be the managers residence and a place that I must surely avoid so as not to be detected. Uninvited guests while frequent weren't exactly greeted with great enthusiasm. My steps were careful as I slowly plodded down the hallway until I reached the staircase that sat in the middle. Looking about carefully I brushed a strand of my messy black hair from my eyes as I reached into the pocket of my trench coat once more. Procuring a small white slip of paper I opened the folded note and read what had been scrawled therein. '8-B' The note gave me the right apartment number…now to only gain entry there without detection. After all, the Dallas police department didn't exactly smile upon breaking and entering. Reaching the top of the stairs I returned the slip of paper to my pocket as I gazed about the hallway. Suddenly…I heard footsteps. Thinking quickly I dove back into the shadows of the stairwell, ducking down out of sight, as a middle-aged man with a rotund beer belly waddled past my position oblivious to my presence. Holding my breath and waiting patiently I didn't move until I heard a door slam, signaling that the stranger had returned to his apartment. Waiting to the count of one hundred just to be safe I stepped back out into the hallway my eyes darting about anxiously as I waited for someone to pop out at any moment. 'I've been reading too many damn spy novels.' I muttered under my breath to no one in particular as I read the numbers on the apartment doors. '1-B, 2-B, 3-B…' Skipping ahead I looked toward the end of the hallway where I would be sure to find it and…bingo. 8-B. There it was. The apartment I had been looking for, and finally my journey had brought me to this conclusion. Getting this far was the easy part…the tricky part of it all was just about to begin. With cautious steps I quickly walked down the hallway keeping the collar of my leather trench coat flipped up in the case that anyone spotted me and possibly recognized or even reported me. The chances were slim…but it was a risk I wasn't going to take. Finally standing in front of apartment '8-B' I knocked lightly on the door…and then waited. Glancing down at the Fossil watch with a cracked leather band on my left wrist I noted the time, 11:53 P.M. Since no one had answered my knock I rapped harshly on the door once again, much louder this time and once again I waited. Glancing around the hallway I realized that no eyes were locked on me, so I would gain interest in whatever way necessary. With a sly smile creeping across my face I once again retrieved the lock pick set from my pocket and shoved it into the lock of the door before me. A simple turn and almost instantly the door swung open without giving me any trouble. Perhaps I had been reading too many spy novels…but it was paying off. 'Please don't have a dog…' My plea rang on deaf ears as I stepped inside the apartment and closed the door behind me locking it at as well, as the darkness quickly encased me, the light barred from entry. My hand felt along the side of the wall but no light switch resided there. 'What kind of idiot builds an apartment and doesn't place the light switch next to the door?' I grumbled to myself, taking a step forward to look for the light switch. My step forward proved to be a wrong step as I stumbled over only God knows what, lying in the middle of the floor. 'Damn it!' I cursed loudly as I staggered about before steadying myself against the wall…and it was there I felt the light switch. Flipping on the solitary switch, the room was instantly flooded with light as I looked about letting my eyes absorb everything as they were forced to readjust themselves to the harsh light. The apartment was nothing of vast size; it boasted a large living room, a small kitchen to the side, a bathroom of miniscule size with the door ajar and a closed door that no doubt led into the bedroom. I slowly twisted the knob to the bedroom door and opened it slowly waiting for any surprise that would come my way. Thankfully surprise came, as the room lay dead and silent. My hand located the light switch on the wall and I flipped it on as the room instantly lit up. 'Great…they make this light switch easy to find.' I sneered, still a bit angry from my prior tumble across the room. Looking about, the room seemed fairly normal. A bed, a desk which a computer rested upon, a bookshelf stocked with various novels and classics of days gone by, a closed closet, and a stereo covered in CD's were the content of the room that weren't worth noting. However, what was worth noting was the eight by ten picture frame that resided on the desk and held a beautiful sight within. It was a female, and a gorgeous one at that. A beautiful smiling face, large brown eyes, and brown hair that fell across her pleasant face. She was indeed a lustful, thought provoking thought. Returning the picture to its rightful place I spun on my heel and promptly exited the room turning off the light and pulling the door shut behind me. Bypassing the living room I went straight to the kitchen. Nothing in the kitchen was worth noting, save for the large white refrigerator, which I immediately threw, open. I could use a midnight snack and when it was at the expense of others I decided I might as well indulge myself. Pulling a bottle of water and a Pizza box from the fridge I sat them both on the counter. The fridge slammed shut by itself as I opened the pizza box and smiled, happy to see the contents of a ham and pineapple pizza crying out to be devoured. With the pizza box in one hand and a bottle of Evian water in the other I returned to the living room where I dropped onto her hunter green couch. Throwing my feet on the table I dug into the contents of the pizza box filling my mouth and beginning to slowly satisfy my appetite. No further than half way through a slice of pizza I heard a noise…someone was at the door. I smiled slightly as I anticipated what was no doubt to be quite an interesting showdown. I heard the jingle of keys…then I heard them being placed into the lock on the knob. The knob turned slowly and a young lady stepped into the doorframe. She slammed the door behind her and then quickly looked up rather bewildered. Her face was more beautiful than it had been yesterday…it was more beautiful than it was in the picture. With rain drops clinging to her cheeks and her hair hanging about in wet black strands she was a picture of beauty. Her brown eyes flashed with anger as she spied me lodging comfortably on her couch indulging myself into her goods. 'How did you get in here?' She snapped, looking both angry and confused at the same time. 'That's not important.' I said with a smile. 'What is important is the fact that you have something I like. You have spunk, you have fire, you have courage…but most of all you have talent.' She looked at me in a rather untrusting manner before nodding as she spoke. 'Go on.' 'I would hate to see that talent go to waste…and since I'm a well respected man in the World of Wrestling I have a proposition for you.' I told her as I had now captivated her full attention. 'Shoot.' She demanded in a no nonsense way as she waited for me to get straight to the subject at hand…I liked that about her. 'I didn't track you down just to let you know you were talented…I tracked you down to cultivate that talent. Basically it's like this; I'm offering you a chance to see the business from the inside. You train with me, you learn from me…and I can make you a star.' I said, my voice taking on a convincing tone. 'And you'll get what in return?' She suspiciously asked. 'We'll cross that bridge when we get too it, wont we? So are you in…or not?' I asked her knowing full well that she was strongly considering my offer. Pausing, she thought hard upon her answer before the words slowly passed over her full lips. 'I'm in…' I smiled widely…feeling almost as if she had entered my web where I was the spider. With the smile still on my face I spoke softly. 'Well then…let's get down to business.'
