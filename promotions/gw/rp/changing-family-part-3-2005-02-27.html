Changing Family part 3

27 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2:27:05 FLASH! I'm standing on the side of the road, next to a black Dodge Viper. The paint on the hood is bubbling, like blackened butter in a cast iron skillet. The heat emanating off the burning cloth convertible top was enough to make a marshmellow run for its life, and when i reach out to try to open the door, the top layer of my skin fuses to the metal door handle. I tear my shirt off and try to break the glass, but even the immense heat cannot weaken the bulletproof glass. I use my shirt to smother part of the flames on the top, then pull my pocket knife out and cut the top open. This new inlet of oxygen flares the flames inside higher, illuminating the dark cabin. The thick black smoke pours out of the hole, but for a brief second I see the charred bodies of my parents. FLASH! I wake up laying in the hospital. The pasty white walls feel eerily cheerful considering I have no recollection of what happened to put me in the hospital. I look around the room, trying to find something familiar. Nothing. Nothing but bleach-white walls, floors, bed sheets, pillows, blankets, and a navy curtain over the brightly lit window. A nurse walks by the doorway, then reappears after a few seconds. 'Oh, Mr. Frost, you've come to. Good, I'll send your nurse in to check on you in just a second,' she says, chipper as a fucking bluebird. 'Excuse me Ma'am, can you tell me what I'm doing here? I mean, I realize I'm laying in a hospital bed with these goddamn wires and IVs in me, but what happened? Did I take a bad bump at work?' 'No sir, I'll ask your nurse to tell you. You know with all these new privacy laws I can't give out information like that,' she replies. Bullshit, its all bullshit. I'm in the hospital, why the hell am I here? Privacy laws my ass, it can't be so bad that I can't find out what's wrong with me. 'Alright, whatever. Just as long as somebody gives me a fucking clue.' I look up at the clock. Twelve fifty-five. Must be afternoon, either that or there are some extremely bright street lights in this neighborhood. My wife's head pokes in the doorway and she slightly smiles at me. 'Aww, good. I was hoping you'd wake up soon. The doctors are saying you'll be out of here in no time. How do you feel?' 'Like shit a la mode. My hands are stiff, it's a little hard to breathe, and my right knee feels worse than ever. What happened to me?' Her expression changes instantly. She looks like she is about to cry, but instead she walks over to me and puts her hand on my arm. 'Derek, there's been an accident. We were following your parents to their house so they could watch Damon while we met with the lawyer.' She trails off, leaving me hanging. 'Oh my god, were we in a car wreck? How are you unharmed if I'm like this? Wait, what about Mom and Dad? How are they? I need to talk to them.' I try to sit up and get out of bed, but Cindy puts her hand on my chest and holds me down a bit. She bites her lip and looks at the floor, a tear running down her soft cheek. 'Your parents were in Johnny's car. Something went wrong, and they...... they didn't make it.' 'What do you mean? No, this can't be. They.......' My voice trails off as I choke on my tongue. Everything hits me, a flood of lost memories coming back to me. Following them down the interstate. The fire flaring up in front of me. The shockwave of the explosion as it ripped through my body. Standing beside the car, helpless as my parents burned inside. The thick, black smoke. The smell of singed flesh and hair. Burning gasoline. The waves of heat drifting skyward off the flaming car. I fall into the hospital bed, my gauze wrapped hands in my face. A wave of tears well up in my eyes, my heart pounding so hard I can hear it. Cindy leans down to comfort me. I push her away. I don't want to see anybody. I yell at her to get out of the room. I want to be alone. My wife looks at me and wipes her eyes, then turns and walks out of the hospital room. I cry myself back to sleep, and I relive the whole explosion in my dreams. I have the same dream over and over. Although, it's not really the same dream every time. The first time is exactly as it happened. Every recurring dream changes a little. During one of them, the windows are blown out from the explosion, and when I get up to the side of the car, I can see my parents corpses, blackened from the fire, their skin dripping from their faces. In another, my father's head flies back and shatters the windshield right in front of me. It then proceeded to talk to me. 'We love you Derek. Your mother and I love you and your brothers so much. Tell David and Donnie we will always love them. Above all, son, cherish your loved ones. Nothing else matters.' When I finally come back from my world of nightmares, I am still laying in the hospital bed. I notice a new IV bag hanging from the rack next to me. I can feel the needle in my wrist. What a shitty job that nurse did. I haven't seen the needle and I can tell exactly where it is, how far into my hand it goes, and holy shit does it hurt. Cindy looks in at me through the glass on the door. My eyes meet hers, and I look away, remembering the way I shoved her away when she told me what happened. She slowly opens the door and whispers to me. 'Can I come in?' I nod, and she slowly walks in the door and immediately comes over to give me a hug. I hold my wife in my arms, and as I close my eyes with my head on her shoulder, my father's dismembered head reappears in my mind. 'Cherish your loved ones. Nothing else matters.'
