So Now What?

8 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/8/04 The statement is very true, behind every bad man, there is a bad bad woman. This statement is so true with J-Gonz who took a week off to rest his body after the attack from the midget and after the match that saw him lose his very first match in GW. Since then J-Gonz has taken time to think about his future and to refocus on his goal and since then, J-Gonz has found the perfect woman to compliment him. The scene opens up outside the Dallas Jamacian Dance Floor, J-Gonz and his two clonies, Chocolate and Paco are standing outside. J-Gonz being the formal man that he is is set up in his usual attire, the white suite, velvet vest and the collar shirt has been left unbottoned at the top. His two clonies both which are notorious gangsters are dressed in the stereotypical white-beater and the brown dhiki pants with the blue bandana around there black slicked hair. 'I've told you two boys about smoking around me, I can't stand the smell!' 'Sorry boss, we're just nervous.' 'Yeah boss, we've never come to a Jamacian night club before, especially not to find you a wife.' 'Keep it down boys, I think here she comes.' With that being said a young blonde bombshell steps out. Dressed in a red satin dress, the blonde hair moves like in the movies, with the wind russtling it back. Her hips sway back and forth, letting us know she knows she looks good. From behind J-Gonz appears, stumbling and tripping over his shoes. 'Excuse me lady in red, I know a fine lady like you don't need to be walking by herself at this coon joint.' 'Huh?' 'Yeah lady in red, you need a man like this to protect you.' 'Who are you?' 'I'm El Mariachi baby, I'm J-Gonz...' 'What do you want? I don't have any money.' 'Nah baby you got the wrong idea, I don't need money look at the sign up there that reads Gonzalez Complex, I own that babe and I wanna share it with you.' 'What? You must be drunk, here's a nickel, go buy a salty dog.' 'Ah c'mon mamasita, look at this babe, take a long hard look, I'm J-Gonz.' 'Oh you wanna have sex? Well it's gonna be twenty-five bucks! Wow this is so great! I'm gonna lose my virginity outside a night club and I'm gonna make money!' 'Nah babe you got me all wrong! I want you, I want you to be my wife.' 'Oh! Well I don't even know you...' 'You don't need to babe, I know all that there is to know! Just say yes and follow me...' The strangest thing, two strangers meeting on a night like this. Already engaged, already set out to be together for the rest of their life. The scene fades with J-Gonz walking her to his white stretch limo. The scene returns with J-Gonz in his office complex looking out into the city of Dalls, the run down join that has been infested with what authories are calling the Gonz Bug. A city of crime run by J-Gonz. 'All this is mine, all this I own and it's going to be your's mamasita. This city here is run by Gonz Complex and I run it with an iron fist babe!' 'What do you do?' 'I'm a lord babe, I'm a drug-lord, a crime-lord, a fighting club-lord, man mamasita I'm any kind of lord you can think of, except I'm not the Lord. The man upstairs has things in check.' 'So you wrestle? Where do you wrestle?' The conversation is interrupted by Chocolate entering the room wearing this time a black suit and an ear piece with a head set. 'Boss, this just came in...J-Gonz is going to get a rematch against Jonathan Drachen...for the Television Title!' 'Ay-Hay! That's what I'm talking about esse! I knew my flare, my money would get me what I want. See babe, this Jonathan Drachen kid beat me two weeks ago at High Noon but I wasn't brining my A-Game. I did'nt have this lovely lady by my side babe, you're gonna make everything that much better.' 'Wow! Does that mean I get to accompany you to the ring? Do I get to stand and watch you fight? Wow! Tell me J, what's so big a deal about the Television Title.' 'Babe you don't get it yet, but you will. It's a stepping stone, it's a stone that I need to step over to get to the World Title and J-Gonz can do it babe, I got the people, I got the money! Baby I'm gonna dance! We're gonna dance together.' Seemingly excited the blonde bombshell turns to face J-Gonz, holding her finger to her mouth. 'Well don't just stand there J! We need to buy you clothes, we need to buy me clothes! We have so much planning to do, we gotta plan a party, you need to get out on the streets and promote yourself babe! People need to know who you are! Lets go! Lets go!' 'Calm down Rasberry, it will come. But right now you have something you need to give me...' 'Babe we aint married yet, and I told you I wanted to wait till we got married!' 'What are you talking about? I was referring to a back rub while I sniff up my coke! Arriba la raza!' 'Oh...' The scene fades out as J-Gonz rest his shoulders on the hands of Rasberry. Both smiling, both happy...the two newly engaged couple...Now What?
