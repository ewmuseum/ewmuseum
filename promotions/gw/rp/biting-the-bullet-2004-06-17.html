Biting The Bullet

17 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.17.04 Traffic had been terrible in Dallas due to three major car accidents and a slew of road work, and after sitting in it for around two and a half hours, a cursing and raving Ken Partridge Jr was glad to get out of it before he attacked someone. He took the exit to his left and began his ride down the small two lane road that led to the Global Center, the base of operation for all of Global Wrestling. His white BMW Z3 glided along the curves as the sound of 'Dirt of Your Shoulders' vocalized by Jay-Z bounced throughout the air. The top was down, causing the wind to whip his hair about in a slightly unruly manner and allowing theanger and frustration that he had just gone through to exit him and remain with the tress that he was passing. The warm Texas wind was splashing him in the face and actually had a calming effect on KPJ. The warmth felt good, alive even, and was shooting even more energy through his body as he reached over to straighten the very large bouqet of Lillies, Roses, Birds of Paradise, and Tulips that were residing in the passenger seat with a card reading 'Gun' on it. The International Champion took in a large breath of the sweet Dallas air, and a calmness secured itself upon his handsome. Even the butterflies that had taken up residence inside of his gut were no longer felt by the Grappling God. The beautiful white car pulled up to the Global Center at around 7:15 that evening and the sun was just beginning to set. It was the perfect time of day resounded in Ken's mind as he looked up at the large building that was his home office. KPJ parked the car in one of the front spots that read 'Reserved for Global Wrestling Champions' and cut off the engine. As he opened his door and took a step out of the car, a feeling of relief shot through his legs and up throughout the rest of his body. It had been two and a half hours since he had embarked from the Dallas condo he was residing in while in town, and his limbs were in need of a stretch. The Superstar stood up and raised his arms high above his head, letting out a long sigh as he pulled them as hard as he could, releasing the stress and kinks that were in them. Ken was dressed in pair of Versace jeans that laced up the front with a flare at the foot of the leg. Hanging over it was a large collard black button-up with three-quarter sleeves by the same designer. He reached into his car and pulled out the flowers, rearranging slightly due to the effects the wind had taken on them. As he closed his door, he swallowed his pride and walked into the front doors of the large shining silver building standing tall before him. The receptionist at the end of the large great hall immediatly looked up as Ken entered. The tower was unusally empty, Ken guessed it was due to the hour that it was. The receptionist's smile broadend as Ken walked closer, allowing her to mak eout who it was. 'Hello, Mr. Partridge,' beamed the receptionist. 'Can I help you?' Ken flashed one of his million dollar smiles to her as he took off his Tag Hauer sunglasses, setting the fglowers on her desk in the process. 'You certainly may, Elizabeth,' responded KPJ after looking at the name plate that was resting on her desk. 'You see, I'm looking for someone and I was hoping you could help e find them.' 'Oh, absolutly, Mr. Partridge,' she immediatly resounded in an excited tone. 'Please,' he said calmly, 'call me Ken.' 'Okay... Ken,' the gorgeaous brunette smiled behind her reading glasses, 'those sure are pretty flowers.' She was actually quite striking in her black power suit and skirt and her hair pulled up. She had wonderful definition in her face, nice cheek bones and jaw structure, and a body on par with a Demi Moore. 'Thank you,' he responded. 'They're for someone very special, and, if you help me, I may bring some for you next time.' Elizabeth blushed and looked down slightly with a smile across her face. 'Who are you looking for,' she giggled. 'Gunner Addison.' 'Oh,' she responded, straightening herself some. 'I'm so sorry, Ken, but she's already left for the day. However, I'm sure that there's something I could help you with.' Her implication fell on deaf ears as Ken stood in disappointment. Two and a half hours, not to mention the flight from New York, for nothing. He motioned the flowers to Elizabeth. 'Just, uhm... just give these to her when you see her, please,' he said in a much more low brow voice than he had entered with. He turned, neglecting to even pay attention to her response, and exited the building. Unable to resist temptation, Liz pulled out the card to read it as Ken pulled away. 'Gun, I realize that I did a lot of things wrong, and I just want to make that up to you now. I love you more than words could ever say and I'm ready to start over new. We can take things as slow as you want, I just just need you back in my life again. Maybe this time you'll stay. Love, Ken'
