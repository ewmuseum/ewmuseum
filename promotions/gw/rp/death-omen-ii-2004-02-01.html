Death Omen II

1 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 01, 2004 Prologue: When dreams have faded… when hearts are shattered… when the silences has hit… does it mean the ending? Do you call it quits? Or do you dive for that extra yardage? Does the pain that you feel bother you… or does the blood shooting through your body push you enough to ignore that pain? Chance… Stardom… Victory… Defeat… They are four elements within one person that have always been in the spotlight. Without the chance, he’d never believe he could reach stardom. Without Victory, he could only know he was to taste defeat. Adrenaline… Risk… Fear… Resilience… These are four elements within one person that have risen from him in past happenings. Without the adrenaline, he’d never have taken the risk and beat Jin Akiyara to get this shot. Without his fears coming true, his resilience to use his fear to help him would be bound to be no factor at all. Death… It is the mere word that one can say they are afraid of such happening, yet realize they will not be able to avoid it. Jesse Jamester… a man upon men, was given a chance, which may decide his victory over Rokku, or is his stardom defeat at the next PPV. With the adrenaline he can muster, the fear will be only in the challenger. Risks will be taken and resilience will be the key factor to winning. Story Continues: When the bullet hit, a hole was seen, and blood leaked. But afterwards, after Jesse fell, there was an odd silence to the area. As if no bullet was shot, as if the man was never hit, and then with only the echo being heard it was gone, as if never happened. However, Jesse was already dead, and the bullet that hit him was just a miscalculation and sign to him. As Jesse stood up he felt the blood trickle down between his eyes. It was something he felt many times in the wrestling ring. The blood drops trickled down his nose and then off the end of it dropped to the ground below forming a small circle of dots. Jesse looked around, and through the darkness saw only the tree branches. As he felt the bullet hole again, he noticed it was steaming; yet at the same time, he felt nothing. His eyes showed an unknowing feeling, as if he was unsure of what was really going on. He knew he was dead, but did that mean feeling was unnoticeable or obsolete to him now? Wiping the blood with his hand he smeared it around on one side of his face, leaving the other pale, and giving him an eerie presents. Frustrated and confused, he began to speak out loud in the dark woods late at night. ’I may not see you my good dear friend, but hunting the dead is not a game I would want to play. See… When you shoot the already shot, they just come back and bite you that much harder in the end. Believe me… I’m not one to take grudges lightly.’ With those words said out loud a disturbance is heard by some trees, and Jesse turns and sprints towards it leaping through the bushes and turning to avoid trees he dives and tackles a man wearing a hunters vest and a hunters cap, dressed in complete orange, he’s frightened to death. ’YOU DID NOT SEE ANYTHING! YOU HEAR ME? I’M NOT SOME EXPIEREMENT, SO DO NOT SAY A WORD ABOUT THIS OR I WILL FIND YOU AND I WILL –‘ But he could not finish the words for as he was to he fell backwards and felt his chest area, and then his head, and with a brisk pause between him staring at the man and the ground, he realized what he was doing. He was torturing an innocent man, a man who did nothing wrong but try to hunt and mistake Jesse, a man he’d thought was a deer, or whatever he was hunting, and shot. Yet there was Jesse, taking it upon himself to disturb a man who had nothing but a hardy meal planned out for himself as someone he was going to hurt. ’Go… GO I TELL YOU, GO!!! Leave now, and don’t mention this… to anyone.” The man barely gets to his feet as he scrambles away from Jesse quickly. He bursts out sprinting after he finally gains balance and doesn’t stop at all. Jesse just sits there in the leaves with a tune playing in his head that he whispers the words to.
