Checkmate

17 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 17, 2004 The scene slowly fades from black to a candle lit table covered by a marble and glass chess set. The flickering lights allow us to see the placement of every pawn to the twin knights to the oh so important Queen and finally, the King. --- All deciding pieces in a game of chess; a kingdom without a pawn is a kingdom without time to prepare. It is a kingdom without a shield or a delay in their opponents attack. The knights represent the front line - the warriors who race from the castle gates to confront the foe head on, knowing that every time they ride in honor of their king, it could be there last. Next the bishops, the King's trusted advisors. There to distract and somewhat defend against any forces who make their way into the royal chambers. The twin rooks, the King's elite guard. Ready and willing to sacrifice themselves so that the king might live to wage and win war over his loathsome nemesis. The final defense; the Queen herself. Powerful in her own right, proud and respected. Feared by pawns and peasants to other Queens alike. Her abilities to move quickly through and around a battle have proven to win a war in a matter of minutes. Her influence can end an epic battle, or doom a kingdom. Finally, the King. The king is a figure head with mobility, if not speed. Powerful in his own right, to protect the king and completely defend him is to win the war for he is what allows victory in the end. This is chess. It is only a game. But what if the king were a combination of every other piece? What if the man behind the lines; waging the war; condemning his enemies, was standing on the front line, bastard sword held high, mounted on a black stallion, and rushing the king of the opposite side? What if the rules did not apply to this one king. It is simple; this king and his army would rule all others who dared to oppose them. --- A hand hovers over the board and moves king-pawn 2 of the clear glass side; white always moves first. The Alkaline Strategy in it's earliest form. Another hand moves forward, using king-knight and taking a highly defensive step. Frosted glass has moved; black is always second. 'This is perhaps one of the most relaxing games.' A moment passes before another voice breaks the silence a move or two later. 'It's very relaxing on the exterior; but if you try to win, try to compete to the fullest of your ability then this game is not only rather unnerving, it can be pretty damned stressful.' The first voice rises again, sounding somewhat familiar although, without a face it can't be placed. Infact, both voices sound familiar. 'Yes, but this is a random game amongst allies, yes? No need for stress.' Another moment passes as we watch the pieces move, block each other in the center then quickly resort to a second plan; attacking at the side via a pair of pawns and a ferocious knight. The scene transitions. March 21st, 2003 - Approximately 5:20 am London, Ontario, Canada - The home of Maverick Reno A fire burnt in the hearth that night, the embers rising from the flames and fading to nonexistence as their life force drained with its attempted escape. Miniature phoenix’s on an accelerated cycle. A bottle of Gray Goose has been placed on a sterling silver tray, a tall glass sits beside it. A pair of black boots are strewn before the couch and stretched out on the elegant leather sofa is Maverick Reno; adorned in a pair of black jogging pants and a black sweater. The look on his face is distant although, not quite dazed. The fact that he's been drinking for quite some time is obvious. On the television, a broadcast signal screen is showing although, the sound has been muted. Slowly, Reno reaches over and retrieves the tall glass and shakily lifts it. He grabs the bottle as well and begins to pour another glass full, his eyes focusing on the two objects in his hands. He pours a small bit and stops, his coordination severely damaged by the alcohol in his system. 'Fucking piece of... where's Lissa when you need 'er.' The slurs in Reno's speech patterns were growing more and more severe as the hours passed. His release from Global Wrestling did not set well and it seemed that he had found some type of haven within the walls of inebriation. He voices breaks the somewhat serene setting. ''LISSA!' A moment passes before Melissa Phillips walks into the room, fully dressed and wearing a coat. 'I told you, Maverick. I'm not helping you. You've been slumming around the house for a month, now! You can barely hold a fucking conversation without getting angry. You have to either learn to live with what Salaok has done or do something about it!' Reno stares at the glass as he finally begins to pour it somewhat steadily with the occasional gulp going into the glass then balancing it out. 'Are you even going to ask why I'm dressed?' Again, Reno doesn't respond. He continues to pour until the alcohol runs over the top of the glass. 'Fine. I can't take it anymore, Maverick. Call me when you get your life straight... you're on your own till then!' With this challenge, Melissa turns and opens the door. She walks out towards a waiting taxi, slamming the door behind her. 'Who the fuck needs 'er, right? I got the tube, I got the ... fire... I got the house, fuck! I even got the goose!' A chuckle lifts from Reno at his own joke. A disgusting state for a disgusted man. 'I'm so going to regret this tomorrow morn... afternoon.' And with this comment, he gulps and guzzles the last glass of Gray Goose, killing off the bottle and his ability to remain conscious. Within moments, he's lain out completely unconscious on the couch. The scene fades to black. Voice: Rock bottom. The scene opens again to the chess board. The game has progressed quite a bit since our last look at the candle lit battle of wits. It is white's turn again and a brazen move is made; Queen threatens King. 'Check, My friend.' A grunt is heard from black's side of the table. 'Touch?, Mr. Reno. Touch?.' With a simple move, Mr. Black is able to move the fragile king out of danger, giving the white Queen a clear and Scot free shot at the black Rook. A fork in the road; one path must be chosen and the other abandoned. March 22nd, 2003 - Approximately 11:00 am London, Ontario, Canada - Home of Maverick Reno The room is dark, void of light excluding a single stream of light piercing the line that separates one side of a set of curtains from the other. The stream barely illuminates the figures in the room, leaving those far from it's path to the shadows. The major piece of furniture in the room can be made out, however. A large King size bed is placed against the far wall, complete with a single patron. The single beam of light crosses the patrons face, revealing it to be a barely conscious Maverick Reno. At some point in the night, he must have woken and made it to his bedroom. Next to the bed, a blinking red light signifies that there's a message waiting. The beam of light finally influences it's victim, causing Reno to stir; first he sequences his eyes and shifts then slowly, opens them and looks around the room. He raises a hand, pressing his palm to his forehead and attempts to 'knock the cobwebs out.' A moment passes and he sits up slowly, the comforter falling off of him. He reaches over and clumsily nails the button on the answering machine; a moment passes and we hear a beep followed by the voice of Melissa Phillips. 'Maverick... I know you're not awake yet. That's probably why I'm calling now; I really couldn't actually hear your voice. I don't know why... it's either because it would hurt too much, or because I'm afraid you'd be drinking again. Either way, I'm calling to beg you to get help; get yourself out of this rut! Do whatever you have to do. You know that I'll stand beside you in whatever it is you choose to do. If you want to go back to GW, then I'll be with you. But I can't stay in that house with you... not if all you're going to do is drink day in and day out. I can't take it. When you get your life straightened up... when you have a game plan. You call me on my cell and I'll be home. I love you no matter what, Maverick. I don't love how you've been acting... or treating me. I need stability and deep down, so do you..' Another beep is heard. The room falls silent excluding the soft and soothing sounds of Reno's breathing. He pushes the cover away and gets out of bed, standing at his full 6'0, in the same clothing as the night before. He walks slowly and carefully through the bedroom door and down the hall, his eyes dragging from the floor to the room before him. Without a second thought, he goes towards his glass and the empty bottle of gray goose from the night before. He picks them both up and sets them down, his eyes shooting to a liquor cabinet on the other side of the room. He walks over and opens the cabinet, his eyes rummaging over the half empty and more than half empty bottles inside. Three; one white wine, one Jamaican Rum, and one Gray Goose. He pulls the Jamaican rum from inside and examines the label, his eyes festering on it for a moment. He lifts his eyes suddenly, his doorbell ringing throughout the house. He sets the bottle down and goes to the door. Upon looking outside, Reno watches as a delivery van drives off into the distance through snow covered hills and plateaus. He kneels to the box and opens it slowly; it's from Global Wrestling Incorporated. They had sent his possessions from the locker room he once commanded. -A picture of Melissa... -A picture of Stephen Perdue and himself... -Postcards from all over the world... -A picture of Scott Pierce. Poor Scott, his entire family murdered because of this fucking 'sport' -A few left over pieces of merchandise from Reno's many tours. -An iron or silver mask; the one he wore as The Enigma. -A small book, full of phone numbers to the many stars he had the privilege of meeting during his stints. -A picture of himself; standing in the ring after winning the World Title, Melissa Phillips at one side, Stephen Perdue at the other. Reno slowly walked back inside, his eyes moving about the entry hall and living room. He tosses the package onto the couch and returns to the bottle of Jamaican rum; he quickly pulls a glass from a shelf and places it on top of the liquor cabinet. He opens the rum and begins to pour... but stops. He glances about the room, his eyes narrowing. The noise from earlier filling his mind, his ears, and this time: his heart. 'Almost a fucking decade in this sport and what does it get me?' He looks down at the glass. 'Nothing. And why? WHY!? Because I always did it their way; I always played by the rules. Well fuck the rules. This is my time... I am going to make it Hell on Earth for Salaok and Nevermore! They're going to wish they had never seen the fucking Global Arena!' With a jerk of a wrist, Reno slings the glass against the French doors that separate the living from the back patio; The Jamaican Rum is next. He grabs it by the neck and hurls it, shattering the glass of the French doors and sending it, flailing and spilling all over the back yard. One by one, the two remaining bottles are tossed to the snow covered earth via the whole in the stained window. He clears the glasses from the shelf with a foul swipe; glass shatters and spreads. A crash here and a crash there. The entire bar area has been cleared... foreshadowing? Is this what is to come of Global Wrestling? --- The scene transitions and we return to the chessboard. The Alkaline defense is in full swing and is clearly seen however, it's time for a new direction; a new misdirection. A new force of unknown strength. The white Queen moves to the far right of the board, out of danger. Tunnle vision allows the player to see that the Queen is now threatening a valued knight. The knight is quickly defended by a Bishop. No one trades a Queen for a Knight. Tunnle vision has succeded; White moves a rook to black's side of the board, putting the King in check. The white Queen prevents movement on the other side. The perfect team has been formed. --- April 2nd, 2003 - Approximately 12:00pm New York City, New York - 4077 Crabapple Cove Lane The scene is now set on a large office inside of Reno's corporate headquarters in New York City, New York. The black marble floors match the marble and oak desk which accentuates the oaken designs embodied on the side of the black marble pillars that appear to hold the room up, keeping it from collapsing. A large picture window shows a beautiful view of the Atlantic Ocean via New York's harbor. Maverick Reno, adorned in a black silk suit, is seated at the desk, pen in hand with his eyes focused on an L-Canyon flat panel monitor just a foot or so away. He is taking notes, his eyes quickly darting back and forth between digital words and pen and ink. He is truly in his element; his hair has been tied back into a tight pony tail, he is neatly shaven and kept. His suit is exquisite, embelished with a dark silken blue and a marvelous sterling silver. A door opens and then closes out of view; Reno doesn't flinch or move in response. Footsteps immediately follow and continue as we see Melissa Phillips walking towards Reno. 'Mav, Naria is here. She'll be taking over while the others are here.' Reno nods and stacks his papers before shutting the monitor off. He slips them into a folder and hands them to Melissa. 'Great. Drop those off with Anthony and we'll get this ball on the move.' Melissa smiles and takes the folder 'What about the four outside?' Reno leans back in his chair and nods. 'Send them in... the future cornerstones of the elite, standing in one room at one time... for the first time.' She nods and walks out of the room as the scene fades to black. I'm coming for you
