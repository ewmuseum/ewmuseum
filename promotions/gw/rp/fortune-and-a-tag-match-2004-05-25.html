Fortune...And a Tag Match?

25 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-25-2004 Pendragon, sword drawn, fled across a grassy plain as dawn spread her fingers over the Land. Behind him, the howls of the hungry pack drifted up to his ears, making him run ever faster? “These bastards are mighty hard to shake. Seems like everything I run into is designed to keep me down.” Across the plains he goes, the shadows of some fairly large beasts getting closer to his heels. Finally, a beacon in the growing dawn’A formation of boulders lies but a mile away?. “One more mile’I must make it. There is no way I can stand against this unholy pack’They are driven by evil and they seek mine blood?” With a howl, a beast that looks like a cross between a wild dog and a and a tiger takes a swipe at the Lord Kings leg’Pendragon anticipates the attack and savagely spins, bringing his sword around, lopping off the beasts head and continuing with the spin until he keeps running towards the boulders’With a last gasp, Pendragon pours all that he has left into the race. Reaching the boulders, he scrambles to the top and stands tall, looking down upon his pursuers who have gathered round the boulders. The beasts stare up at him with red eyes’Bloodlust clouds their vision. “Good Christ’Lord Akumancha truly possesses glammer. And his glammer is strong to entrance beasts such as these to do his bidding. The question now, though, is what shall mine next move be?” With that, he turns from the beasts and surveys the boulders. Set in the middle, where three boulders come together is set a door. Ironwood, with a steel ring in the middle. With a shrug, Pendragon pulls the chamber door open. To his surprise, a deep tunnel stands before him, with steep stairs leading down into the gloom. With a last look behind at the beasts, Pendragon starts down the stairs, holding his sword in front of him, both hands upon its hilt. As he descends into the darkness, a faint light begins to grow ever more clear. Finally, he comes to a second door, with torches set upon the outside. This door has but a huge golden doorknocker in the shape of a lion’s head? “It seems that everywhere I go in this land, I am met with both wonders and mysteries of the past. And here, now, I feel torn. As though I am constantly in two places at once’The real world’The world where I am a combatant in the ranks of GW, and this world. This world where I am a man who would be king. There are similarities betwixt the two worlds. For one, mine kingly stature. My sword. Mine enemies. It is almost as if when an event strikes over there, it echoes over here; as if the membrane between the two worlds is thin, and evil can slip through with the Lord King. Right now it is Lord Akumancha and his minions; blind men who follow his evil and his glam heedless of the danger that he truly is. And between the worlds thou findest me’Were that Merlin were here. I need some answers?” Pendragon takes his right hand from his sword and holds it with his left. He raises his hand to the knocker and knocks three times? *BOOM* *BOOM* *BOOM* Ominously, the door opens, and Pendragon walks into a gloomy chamber lit by a fire. The smoke escapes through a hole in the ceiling, at an unseen height above. Pendragon looks around at the various items in the room. Books, different pieces of armor and weaponry. At the far side of the chamber is another door. This one with a handle. Pendragon scratches his head, when the voice of an old man rings out? Old Man: So at last you find me? Almost from thin air a man appears seated Indian style beside the fire. Pendragon’s hands betray him and swing the sword in a heavy arc towards the old man. At the last moment, the sword is turned away by an unseen force? Old Man: You have no need of that? Pendragon: And yet I wish answers? Old Man: That way destroys answers forever. Sit. I am sure your questions are many. I will do my best to give to you the answers you seek. We will palaver, and I will feed you. And then, when you are ready, you will go forth to meet your destiny on a fuller stomach. The man motions for Pendragon to sit down. Pendragon sheathes the sword and sits opposite the man. The man smiles kindly and holds up a spit that holds two skinned rabbits. He places the spit over the fire, resting it upon two forked rods. Pendragon leans back on his elbow and stares at the man? Pendragon: Why am I here? Dost thou know that? Old Man: You were needed? Pendragon: Needed? And thus was I ripped from the only world I have ever known? I was thrust into this conflict because I was needed? The old man nods and smiles, and turns the rabbits over the fire? Old Man: You have been invested with a great power. You may be the single most unique warrior any world has ever known. You have the power to be wherever you are needed most. You can walk between the worlds like you would walk down the street. You are free to do your own thing’Like your precious GW, but you will feel a pull when you are needed elsewhere. You are too noble and honorable to not heed the call. It is one of your greatest strengths and perhaps your greatest weakness. Now eat. And then I will tell you your fortune? Pendragon begins to munch on the rabbit. He pulls the steaming meat off of the bone with his bare hand and pops bite after bite into his mouth. So much for manners, but hey! There ain’t no silverware! Pendragon: So you are a seer then? Old Man: I am a very powerful wizard! Pendragon: Like Merlin? The man shakes his head? Old Man: My kind has not been seen for many hundreds of years. My lore was passed down to me from my father, as his father did to him and so on, and so on. So it has been with my sect for an age. You interest me. It was thus I placed myself where I knew you would surely stumble upon me. I wanted the chance to read your fortune. I doubt I will ever have the chance to read one such as yours for many years to come? Pendragon: My fortune? Bah! I take no notice of the things that fortune dictates. I make my own way. I always have? Old Man: The humor an old man who fed you. And who knows? Perhaps you may gain something useful. Pendragon: Very well’My fortune then, Wizard? The man opens his hands and a very worn deck of cards appear? Old Man: The tarot. Simple, and yet effective? The man begins to deftly shuffle the cards, and soon they are flying. Pendragon stares, transfixed by the man’s dexterity. At last, he lays a card down? Old Man: The King. This card represents you. Somehow, I knew that this card would appear? He lays another card down and Pendragon gasps? Old Man: The Ace of Swords. The Ace of all swords you carry?.Excalibur. Here though, it is the journey signifier. The Sword here signifies that your time will be a constant battle?.Tumultuous. Next, we have your goal of the short term? The man flips a card, and Pendragon laughs? Old Man: The Snowman? I never had that card in this deck. It doesn’t even exist! But? Comprehension dawns on the man’s face and he smiles? Old Man: Freeze. And Akuma. They are your goal as of now? Pendragon: Akumancha! Old Man: Call them what you will’Their aura is one and the same, though the inhabitants of that aura may be different. Freeze is your short-term goal, that much is clear’Next, your karma? The old man flips a card? Old Man: The Mistress of Wands?.The Healer. That is your destiny. To heal the hurts of a thousand wrongs’And next, your heart? He flips one more card?. Old Man: The hanged man’Here though, it does not signify death. It signifies strength, or even entrapment. You are a man of lofty morals and honor, and you are bound by those ideals. To separate from these ideals would mean insanity’And finally’The outcome? He flips one more card and places it on top of the King. Old Man: The star. The star represents immortality, but this cannot be! You are but a mortal man, and a mortal death you owe? Pendragon shakes his head and looks into the old man’s eyes? Pendragon: Each of us owes a death. But our deeds in life echo in the afterlife, and they echo in the worlds we touch after our passing. With remembrance comes immortality. So maybe I die, and maybe I do not’But mine name shall persevere for eons to come? The man nods his understanding at Pendragon’s words? Old Man: You are wise for one so young. But your battle to determine the seat of power in this land may not be fought here. When you go into battle against Freeze, worlds will hang in the balance? Pendragon: And yet, it may not be I who determines the outcome’I have been forced into a pairing with a man named Jamester, and Freeze with a man named Jetsui. Old Man: I see’a kink in the works it is then. Fear not, my young warrior , for if your heart is true, good will overcome? Pendragon: Aye’I will force good to overcome. I shall give evil something it has never seen before?. “And thus, it appears a showdown looms upon the horizon. Would that I could wipe the mat clean with the three others in the battle, yet I cannot. I am forced to work with Jesse Jamester for a common goal, and that goal is to destroy Freeze. To destroy Akuma. To free the land, I must free mine ownself from their hideous curse. Each action they take is more grievous than the last, and the time comes when I shall collect on the bill that they add to with each passing day. I will stand and be true, and when I meet Freeze”.When I meet Jetsui’they will not stand?..” THEY WILL NOT STAND!!!! ~TO BE CONTINUED~
