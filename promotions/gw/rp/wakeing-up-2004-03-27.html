Wakeing up Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #1055 Date: 3/27/04 Location: New York ((It was growing night as rapidly as it could as i looked out the window of the dim hotel room. Sleeping in the bed in which i sat was the girl i had met but three days ago. Her Name Amber Horn. She lay sleeping after a full night and day of debotchry. I was sitting up in a pair of Black calvin cline boxers with a Blend 27 smoke in my mouth. I would fly out in mear hours. It was my last night in New york. The building had been amended to their liking and in two years i will be able to bank with these people for free. What perks life brings. Like in the rumble you may find some one in a bad posistion and be able to kock them out and have them become a less potential thret. There is no margin for error. I mean you look at it as if your in a bad way there are thirty seven other guys. One of them is bound to see it. And that make you weak. You cant go through the entire rumble with out almost getting in trouble a hundred times. It's vital to show that you can hang with the best. 'It happens, You standing in the middle of the ring and the next thing you know there is a right eating up the side of your head. It leaves you in another world for about three seconeds and then your coming back. Thats how Hardcore the rumble is. Were all clawing like rats on a big cookie.' amber stirs as i take a puff off my smoke. her naked body laying agnist the white cotten sheets. I smiled lightly. It had been fun but that would end for a small time and untill i saw her again. I would exisist with another..It was a life less lead and a life much less liked. For me it was a way to be for now. I was no longer married. There was no hope of getting back together...Why not have a little fun. Hell i'm only twenty seven.. 'All seeming to have a bigger goal in mind but really wanting the momentary glory that we bask in for such short periods of time now-a-days. Your on top and then your off. something tells me that things will happen at COTT. Something that you all will never expect.' Now you want to know from whom i would guess..Well just look and you will see. I dont really care what happens. There will be some things you like and some thing that you are going to dislike. You know why becuse there are people in wrestling that you dislike...You know. Those people you cant stand at all. There are going to be some of them there and for that simple fact you will be unhappy with some protion of the GW PPV COTT. Now in the rumble there are so many diffrent types of wrestling styles. many times i get asked how i prepare. You really cant. There is no way you have enough time to get in the ring and make yourself a specilist at everything there is in wrestling in one week. There is no way to do it. Now you take a look at people Like Chris camreo and hans Kruger. Those were Athleats. They never had to be questioned about their talents. Now-a-days you dont know if you'll make it in the business anymore.I remeber the day that even a skinny hundred and fifty pound man got into wrestling. So long ago anything was possible. You could make yourselfwhat you wanted in the wrestling world. Look at Ric Flaier. Harley Race. Then countless others that made them selves and never aksed any one for the help to do it. 'They would have been the men that would have won the rumble. Self made men with more talent that any five wrestlers of today. Slowly we all become burned out though. Time takes it toll on our minds and we cant think stright anymore. You begin talking to your self in your sleep. You cant understand some of the thouyghts that go through your mind.' You become a walking breathing Bag of skin Bones and mecanic movement. Not to say wrestlers arnt smart guys. We do have times were we all have dicussions on how things are going in the world of business. Sometimes i'll sit down with a few of the guys and we'll go out and have some lunch. I'm not above hanging out with someof these guys. They are the most like me out of many of the people i have met during the years. You kind of get used to a certian kind of person and then you can stand them all. Many wretlers are pretty much the same way. We like haveing fun and we dont give a shit who says what we are ready to do what we want at any couse,reason or rhyme. Never do you have to ask us twice. 'we know what we are ment for. Like Rome needed the Game. America needs Wrestling. It is a sport of pure raw power agnist more Raw power. You just have to know what your doing. Wrestlingt is a fity fifty business. You win you lose and you go home. And you know that you are expendable. There are millions of other peoplewho would die to take your place and get off the Indy monkey go round thhat they got him oon. I really feel bad for the independant wrestlers of today. They have it kind of hard. Little pay and lots of driveing..I remember my days. The days in which me and Draven were still very good friends. Were me and Jon Blood used to travle the roads together.' The good ol' days. When i was full of piss and viniger and could win any world title that was set in front of me. I was the man you wanted on the cas.e Like sherlock fucking holems. I was there. and in rare form the entire time i was there. Never once did i miss a beat and if i did it was once and you may never see it happen agaian. Thats whats so funny about life. You have to blink but if you blink at a cetrian moment. You may miss soemthing that you'll never again in your life be able to see. and you missed it becuse of a function that the body has to carry out in it's daily work. 'so during the rumble dont Blink. You may see something that the wrestling world talks about for years. Stars my be born and others may fade. catch a glimpsof a falling star..You know what they say...Make a wish. Well I wish that I was destine to win this rumble. God how i wish that i knew how these things were going to work out my way.' Now, everything considered..I've had a good time here in new york..I hoped things went that ay as i strode to get on the plane later on..The rumble was apoun me. It was a very short time away. and through the loking glass i bet you wouldent see me. Through the looking glass i'd just be another sparkle in the back...Now and then i would pop up but would quickly be put out as an underachiever. How's that feel. I dont know...I've never thought of myself as that. I know that i get what i can done and at times it is not what i think it should be. Thats is why i go out and work so much harder. Now and then i think that i may never see the day were i will win another major title. I may never again see the say were I am in the spot light. But that is just all part and par of the entire wrestling biz. 'Thats is how it goes. We bath in the light for as long as we can becuse after a while it starts to get cold and the Warmth doesnt come back often...The Lights begin to get dimmer and dimmer..and the waves get smaller and smaller. Today you have to be something . Right then. Thats all wrestling is now-a-days. Mr. Right now. No one wants Mr. Right any more. it's not good enough for them.' They want what they cant have. Our talent. Our looks. They want us. To live for therm. To do the things that they never had the guts to do. NThats why i have a real dream Job. Kids sit at home and say to their buddys. hey man. Were gonna be wrestlers. yeah. it's nice to say and hell. Even to do in your back yard But There is a diffrent kind of presedent to wrestling when there's seventeen thoushand people there. Sitting there cheering you on or wishing you'ed lose. 'And in thsi rumble we will all have our own fans. None will have a great bearing in this kind of match. The fans are so divided in some cases that no cheers are given when a person comes to the ring.. So we will see boys. Time grows short..' as does our minds. We all see it diffrently. We all look at the glassMost of you look at the glass as half empty. Well. Ilookat a half empty glasss and think. How can i fill it up. I dont think of the good the bad or the ugly. I'm thinking of a way to make things better for me and my daughter. I dont care what happens to any one else..No one. No one holds a candel to me daughter. I would kindly bow out of any game to help my daughter. 'Wouldent any of you. There are many who know what it is to their daughters..If they seen their daddy on TV. It kind of takes away from that relationship. I mean I get beat but i never have my daughter watching. She barly know i wrestle. I try and keep her away from it untill she's old enough to understand. It;s funny to me now. She;sturning six and for her brithday i'd like to bring her home the COTT rumble championship.' That'd be nice. And it can happen. If i make it. Thats a driveing force behind many of my endevors 'My Daughter. So young. She is just learning how to make her own desisions. Like my desisions in the rumbe. It's all about picking yuour spots. All about looking at the situation and then takeing it to another leavle and excuteing your ideas.' It's nice to be able to think on your feet. It's very easy for me. I try and make it a game., Every day i begin to think of things that could happen. I start to think of everything....Plane crashes. What do i do. carw wresk what do i do. Mugger. Building falls. I have thought about it all. Thats whats going to help me in the Rumble. 'My calculations never stop. Never. I am alaways on my toes. I'm going to grap ahold on every little snag that i see.' I have to be one of the men that is fixed on what is going on. I have to know that out of all the things happning. I can make things happen for myself. I dont have to wiat for the opening. I will make the opening. Then dive in head first. 'Becuse thats what i do.' Fin!
