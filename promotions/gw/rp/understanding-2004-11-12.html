Understanding

12 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th November 2004 Underdog... There is nothing quite like an underdog story is there? A person who has been underestimated, who has been seen as a complete zero not worth a second glance, makes it to the top, prove everyone wrong and wins the heart of the most popular girl in school...okay maybe not that last bit but you get what I mean. A true underdog is someone who is willing to stand up for himself, who is willing to fight the popular opinion and not care what anyone thinks. If an underdog caved to popular opinion then there would be no underdog stories, instead there would just be sheep stories about how people follow the popular trend and never upset the system. You see without underdogs, this world would be full of sheep and nothing would ever change. Democracy for example would never have been a realised idea because no one would be willing to speak out; no one would be willing to upset the system. Underdogs are needed in our society and especially in this game... All this talk of underdogs is for a reason. In the GW even though I am Cruiserweight Champion I still consider myself an underdog because I am always underestimated. Take last week's Evolution when I was facing Brick in the Tournament Of The Worthy first round match, everyone predicted that I would be squashed like a bug in a matter of seconds. To be honest I did think for a brief second that Brick was a hard man to bring down and that it was going to take a little extra from me to come out the winner. However I did pull out that little bit extra and proved the critics wrong and came out the winner and a semi-finalist in the Tournament Of The Worthy, a tournament that could see me as the next GW World Champion. But before I reach the final I face a man who only 2 weeks ago opened my eyes to the fact that I was holding back, that I wasn't extreme as I was in the past, that I needed to improve my game. This man or as some see him, this beast will stop at nothing to come out of the match as the victor. In our last encounter he left me an utter mess and he walked out of that match without any sign of struggle. He probably thinks he has it set in this match, he probably thinks that he will be facing the same Adam Myers he faced 2 weeks ago. Well Jackson let me tell you this, because of our last encounter, you have awoken a monster within. You think you are a monster Jackson? Wait till we are in that ring. Get ready to meet an Adam Myers who will stop at nothing to beat you, an Adam Myers who is going into Evolution and coming straight out as the winner. Be ready Jackson because I know I will... Monday 8th November 2004 Just after Evolution... I flopped onto the chair in my changing room. I was exhausted; my head ached, as did all the muscles in my body. I wiped some extinguisher foam from my arm and flicked it across the room. It landed in front of the door just as Rachel stepped into the room. She was glowing the maternal glow. The bump had become quite big as the due date rapidly approached. She was wearing a ghastly flower patterned maternal dress but she still looked gorgeous. However at that moment in time I wasn't concerned with how she looked... 'What the hell you doing here Rachel?!?!' I shouted at her as I stood up. I hadn't told Rachel about my new direction concerning my fighting style. I didn't want to worry her, especially in the condition she was in; any unwanted stress could cause a premature birth, which none of us wanted. She had phoned the GW offices to book a ticket so she could come see my match, when she told me I told her she couldn't come. It was a 2-hour discussion before she agreed to stay at home. I should have known with Rachel being an independent spirit that she was going to come anyway. Rachel looked at me with concern and anger in her eyes. 'Don't you dare shout at me, Adam Jacob Myers!' Rachel replied just as angrily. I knew I was in trouble, as soon as anyone says your full name, you know you are in trouble. 'I thought I told you not to come tonight.' I said sounding calmer and trying to ver the conversation away from the inevitable. 'You knew I was going to come Adam. You knew as soon as you told me not to come, I was going to come. I knew you were hiding something from me Adam.' 'What? What do you mean hiding something? I wasn't hiding anything from you.' 'Yes you were, from what I saw in that ring...' 'That was just me wrestling Rachel.' 'No it wasn't.' 'What?' 'I have seen you wrestle before Adam and that was not you. That was not my husband wrestling out there, that was something else, someone else.' 'I came out the winner didn't I?' 'Is that all that matters to you Adam? The win?' 'In that match...yes! Don't you understand Rachel? I need to be at my best if I am to win this tournament. If I am to become champion then I need to be ruthless.' 'Adam, I know how much this tournament means to you, but you have got to realise how much you mean to me. If you keep fighting like you did tonight then...' Rachel placed her hand on her bump, '...our little one is not going to have a father to look up to.' I hung my head... She was right, she was always right. But I couldn't hold back, I couldn't let my concern for my wife and child cloud my mind when I am in that match that would mean certain defeat. I knew my next opponent was Jackson and I needed to prove to myself more than anyone else that I could bring him down. I needed to fight like I did against Brick, because if I can bring a veteran like Brick down then there is no doubt I can bring Jackson down as well. On the other side of the coin I didn't want to worry my wife but I couldn't lie to her, she would see right through that. I moved over to Rachel who was looking at me with more concern now than anger. I wrapped my arms around her and hugged her. Her head rested on my chest as I rested my chin on the top of her head. 'I just want you to understand how I feel Adam.' Rachel said as she buried her head further into my chest. 'I know.' I said sighing. 'So will you promise me that you will stop?' I pushed away from Rachel and turned my back to her, I stared at the wall and sighed. 'I can't Rachel, I'm sorry.' 'But why Adam?' I turned around. 'Because I have to Rachel. You want to understand how you feel? Then you need to understand how I feel. This is my work Rachel, this is my life, and this is what keeps us in such comfort as we are now. I can't let myself slip otherwise we can kiss goodbye to what we have now. I am doing this for us, for us and our child.' 'It doesn't mean you have to go out there every night and be suicidal.' 'It does if I am to beat people like Jackson next week. I am not going out there to try and kill myself. I am going out there to be my best and if being suicidal means I am at my best then I guess I have to be suicidal.' 'But Adam...' 'Please Rachel, just let me do this. Just say you understand, I don't expect you to back me on this but I just need you to understand.' There was a moment of silence as Rachel just looked at me with her curled lip of frustration. She knew she couldn't change my mind on this. She slowly moved to me and wrapped her arms around my waist and put her head on my chest again. 'I love you Adam Myers, you know that, don't you?' 'I know and I love you too Rachel Myers.' 'Then even though I don't like it, I understand. You just remember what you are putting on the line when you make that 50 foot drop okay?' '50 feet? Now that is a bit extreme!' I chuckled. 'You say that now.' Rachel laughed as she jokingly punched me. 'Come on let's go home.' I said as I picked up my kit bag and put my arm around my wife and walked out of my changing room. Suicidal, extreme, call it what you want but I call it my ticket to victory. Beware GW because this underdog has one hell of a bite!
