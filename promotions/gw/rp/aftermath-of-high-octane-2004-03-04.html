Aftermath of High Octane...

4 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aftermath of High Octane... Mark Makonie Global Wrestling Promo #959 Date: 3-4-04 Location: Dark Place The place is dark and the sounds of thunder can be heard outside. The lightening strikes the ground as the bolt lights up the whole dark sky. There is rain that is coming down from the sky and you can hear it drip on the metal roof above. Every single drop of water can be heard. There are isn’t any candles that are lit or any lights that are on. You cannot see what is around or where you are. There is a flashlight that turns on and Mark Makonie puts it under his chin so that it lights up to his face. “I have been beat by a man that I hate the most.” Mark starts to say as there is a wall behind him. “This man has beaten me and he shouldn’t even be roaming this earth. For days I haven’t been able to show my face out in public because of you. I have gone six long weeks, fighting you. I have the marks to show how much I have put into this and this is what I get out of it! Is this the future of my wrestling career?” Mark starts to rock back and forth while he is sitting on the ground. “NO! This is not going to get to me as you think Brick. There will be hell to pay in the future. You go around parading that you beat a white guy Brick, but I’ve been in GW for only a couple of weeks and I already had a title shot. Did I think I was going to win? HELL YES! I thought that I could easily beat you, but it was your time to shine. Next time there will be a different outcome. I hope that Deacon Holmes takes you out on Evolution. I will be there looking around for you Brick. This will be the first time since High Octane that I have been out in public.” Mark gets up off of the floor as he points the flashlight down to the ground. His feet have a dark dirty look to them as you can tell he has not worn any socks or shoes for a while. His jeans also seem to have a brownish tint to them. He starts to pace around the place as he collects his thoughts in his head. It seems as if Mark ahs gone into a trance and that he is over thinking and taking this loss too hard. “Now coming up in a couple of days I will be in a triple threat match with Jesse Jamester and who knows who else there is. We all lost at High Octane and now we need to rebound to get our momentum going for the next major show. All my anger will be taken out on this match and I don’t need any help. I will not trust anyone in Global, the only reason I would be teamed up with someone is for a push. I don’t see this happening anytime soon either. My self esteem has been lowered and it will just keep on lowering. Just wait until Evolution when I come out into the open and I show what I have been keeping up inside of me.” Mark starts to walk over to the only bedroom that is in the house it looks like. He turns off his flashlight as the whole place goes dark once again and then the door creeps closed. The creeping sound from the door sends an eerie chill down the spine of the camera man. The sound of thunder can still be heard as well as the rain drops on the metal roof. The camera man heads outside as the door closes and the camera goes off.
