The Journey: Part 21/The Army's POV

28 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-28-2005 7:25 in the morning You have to love early mornings in New York City’well more like you have to LIVE in NYC to appreciate an early morning in the “Big Apple”. On a nice spring day you can usually see people walking through Times Square ,strolling through Central Park or catching a cab to Manhattan to visit the SoHo District and visiting some of the shops there. On this particular early morning we find a few of the members of the “Army of Old School” at the Virgin Megastore ,which is across the street from the famed 1515 address that MTV resides at. The 5 members are Joshua Curtis ,Zack Johnson , Quatre Raberba Winner ,Trowa Barton and Releena Peacecraft. Now why are they there this early when the store doesn’t open until about 10? Well Joshua wanted to see if he could get a hold of some CDs that Miliardo wanted and seeing as how Releena IS Milardo’s sister Releena felt her obligation to come along with Josh to make sure that he didn’t forget the list. Joshua had on Trowa’s “Wife Beater” t-shirt(don’t ask Trowa doesn’t even know who got it for him or even WHY) ,a pair of Old Navy cargo pants and a pair of Timberlands while his partner in Zack had on a white tank top with a pair of Levi’s Blue Jeans and Pumas. Quatre went for his “traditional” outfit ,which consisted of a purple vest ,pink shirt ,brown belt and tan pants with brown loafers to finish off the ensemble while his Latino Lover had on a shirt that Trieze and Lucrezia made for the “Army” ,Wrangler Blue Jeans and a pair of “Old School” Converse(all hail the “Chuck Taylors”)! Releena was wearing a white blouse with a sky blue mini skirt and a pair of high heels that had about a 3 inch heel to the estimation of the 4 men that were in attendance and sometimes she honestly cursed wearing a heel but eh shit happens right? “Well gang” Joshua began “last week “The Old School Connection” returned for the most part successfully and interestingly enough this VP in Jean-Claude-Dafoe didn’t try to stop Zack from getting into the ring against Zenith and his team. Does Dafoe really hate Zenith that much?” Joshua asked as they were standing outside the store awaiting the opening patiently. “It shouldn’t come as too much of a surprise to you though Master Joshua. You and Dafoe have a common enemy in Hunting Grounds and he wants to see them go down as much as you do.” Quatre noted and Zack raised an eyebrow when the young Arabian called Joshua “Master”. “Mr. Winner is there something more to the friendship that you have with Joshua that you aren’t telling us” The Californian lover asked Quatre and it took Quatre not three seconds to start blushing three shades of red. “N-no Zack not at all. Where I come from there is a group of people that look to me as their leader they call me “Master Quatre?”” “Its basically a sign of respect where he comes from.” Trowa finished trying to save his Arabian lover from any further prodding into their relationship or even sex life! Of course we all know that it didn’t work? “Oh” Zack said in mock amazement “I thought the “Master” title came from when you and Quatre did S&?.?Zack didn’t get to finish his comment as smartass as it was to be due to Joshua elbowing him in the ribs to silence him. “Easy hun we don’t need to prodding like THAT into someone’s relationship.” Joshua said to Zack before looking over at the young couple “My apologies Mr. Winner and Mr. Barton for Zack’s comments. He’s been away from the group for a while so he’s new to having more men in the group than we initially had.” Joshua explained to the young couple and Trowa nodded his understanding and Joshua noticed that a couple of weeks ago the signs of Trowa’s Amnesia had all but subsided and that he had truly begun to find himself with Quatre by his side helping him as well as Catherine Bloom and the rest of the “Army” but to the young Latino this didn’t feel like an “Army” but more like a Family. Was that the intention all along? You bet it was and the reason it was to feel like such is because there aren’t many factions around that the people live together ,work together and grow together! It just doesn’t happen like it should because what ends up happening is that you see a stable together on the TV and then off the camera they are a bunch of individuals. Joshua wanted to stop that his feeling is that with this faction he has or that he can because as long as people see the “Army” for who they really are and that IS a family then Joshua knows that part of his mission is accomplished. Now there is a school of thought that says that Joshua doesn’t have/want many friends outside of the “Army” and that can’t be denied or proven because Joshua is pretty mum on the subject and anyone that tries to talk to him about the subject usually gets a “no comment” for their response from him because Joshua stays close to the vest on the topic. So who could Joshua consider a friend outside of the “Army?” Well two people that Joshua COULD consider live in the SoHo district in Ken Partridge Jr. and Gunner Addison. What’s the hang up you ask? Zack isn’t too fond of KPJ because to him Ken shunned Joshua as it pertains to the faction known as “The Elite” and to Zack that is just wrong considering now that Joshua has taken up Ken’s fight the least that Ken could do is show some gratitude towards the young man from the “Outskirts of Times Square” as they call them and don’t think that the rest of the faction is too pleased with Ken either’oh no they want at Ken in the worst way but Joshua has informed them all that he wants to give Ken a chance to correct the mistake of turning him away or else Mr. Trieze Khushrenada will be given permission to sick the dogs on Ken and Co. and even be allowed to enter discussions with the faction known as “Hunting Grounds” to go after Ken! 2h and 15mins later and the store opened with the 5 being the first in there and going about the task of finding the items that they wanted to get and Joshua found the music that Milardo wanted in about 5 minutes but it as always fun to go looking around to see what else he could find for himself and naturally he went to the anime section so Lord only know what he found there? “What did Lucrezia want again?” Quatre asked Trowa as they were in the “Country” section and by now they had about 4 or 6 discs in their respective baskets when they got the Baronet’s choice. “I think it was the CD with “Courtesy of the Red ,White and Blue” on it ,but I’m not sure exactly Quatre.” Trowa explained but Quatre remembered no sooner than he said that anyways so it all worked out in the end and Quatre could wipe the sweat off of his brow as he thought that he had forgotten his friend’s pick. Releena had gone into the “Classical” section as she was one of the only people to actually listen to the music in the family with Quatre ,Joshua and Trowa being the others. When she went walking through the section she wasn’t looking for one particular CD’that was until she heard the melody that was played over the sound system in the store and it was the melody that Quatre and Trowa played that one faithful day in the music room of Quatre’s place and you know something: She always pined to hear it LIVE! *Ooh this is torture* Releena thought as she walked through the store with the melody buzzing in her head and not surprisingly the rest of the gang had been doing their shopping when they heard the music play and when the young couple that made the song heard it playing a furious blush crept up on the blonde’s face! “Is this what they mean by “They’re playing our song?”” Quatre asked and Trowa just smiled and held his young lover for a couple of minutes’yes in the record store and yes there were people looking but they didn’t care and why should they? They weren’t doing anything wrong and neither was thinking perverted thoughts’well none that they would share publicly like they had a tendency to do but you get the idea right? Joshua had just finished his checkout when the melody had begun to play and he couldn’t help but smile as he is to date the ONLY member of “Army” have heard the song LIVE and he wanted the rest of the group to get to hear it for themselves. The only question that was left was could Quatre and Trowa do it again or was it literally a “spur of the moment” thing that could only be captured one time and done? His answer didn’t have to wait long? As everyone finished up with their shopping they all waited patiently for the bus to their destination and Joshua looked over at Trowa and Quatre whom seemed to have been planning something but what not even the leader knew and it didn’t really bother him that much considering he knew that whatever the young couple had planned would probably good. Approx. 3 hrs later? It was about 3 in the after noon when everyone got settled from the trip and a lot of time actually was spent by most of the group listening to the music they had bought earlier ,but Joshua’s mind was admittedly elsewhere. What had Trowa and Quatre been talking about on the ride back and better yet was he to be involved? Josh had to do his investigating so he went straight to the source and that would be room “Gundam”. Or it would have been if the door wasn’t locked up ,which was somewhat surprising since Joshua knew himself that they weren’t THAT private and that made Joshua wonder even more what Quatre and Trowa had planned. That night Joshua was in his office when he heard a gentle knock at the door and he knew only one person that knocked like that? “Come in Quatre.” Joshua said and the Blonde walked in alone and while it didn’t raise suspicions it did make Josh think a little bit as was evident by his next question “Where’s Trowa?” Josh asked and that was when Quatre got up and locked the door to the office to speak with Curtis. “Master Joshua” Quatre began “remember when we were in the music store today and they played that melody that Trowa and I performed about a year or so ago in front of you?” Quatre asked and Joshua nodded before his friend continued “Well back when you heard the song when you were in my home we had a drummer already and now we were thinking that maybe you could?” “I’ll do it!” Joshua said and Quatre smiled that ever bright smile of his ,which always made Joshua’s day and with that his Arabian friend walked out of the room but not before telling Joshua to tell everyone to meet in the Courtyard in front of the stage in about 30 minutes and Joshua nodded again before he shut the door. Now it should actually come as no surprise that Quatre would ask Curtis for this favor’after all Curtis is one of the best drummers in the country and he had been expertly practicing with the song on his iTunes night in and night out to make sure that he didn’t miss any of the timing. When Joshua was finished with his business he pushed the button for the intercom and left the following message for all the complex to hear: “Meeting in the courtyard in 10 minutes. All members are to attend.” With that Curtis opened up his top drawer that had his drumsticks and walked out the back entrance to the west entrance of the courtyard and walked calmly up the steps on the side of the stage where Quatre and Trowa were waiting and as expected the rest of the group arrived shortly thereafter. When they all took their seats Joshua stood up behind the drum set and began to speak? “I know you all are wondering why I called you all here am I correct?” Joshua asked and as the crowd didn’t say anything he took that as his cue to continue “When I was in Saudi Arabia some time back I was over Quatre’s place and he and Trowa played a piece of music that was one of the most stunningly beautiful songs I ever got the chance to hear. While the song has no “Official” name we like to call it the “Shining Melody” and I hope you all like it as much as I did years ago.” Joshua finished and then the melody began? As the song began to play the crowd assembled watched in awe as Quatre began to play the violin with Joshua plying the drums behind him in sync and Trowa followed soon after with the flute. For about the next 45 seconds or so the crowd watched 3 experts at their preferred instruments and when it was all over the crowd stood and gave the biggest reaction to that piece that was ever received! The night continued on like this for a while and everyone even got to hear Quatre sing! That was something to behold you can be assured and by the time it was all said and done the members of the faction had a new respect for Curtis ,Quatre and Trowa and not just as competitors but rather as musicians also. By the time the concert was all over it was about 2 in the morning and everyone knew they had to get up the next day but Curtis told them not to worry about getting up early because for this occasion it was excused and believe me they were all grateful to be sure! As Joshua walked off the stage Zack was waiting for him at the bottom and he gave him a big kiss before they walked hand and hand into the house again where they did a little more than kissing ;) Au Revoir!
