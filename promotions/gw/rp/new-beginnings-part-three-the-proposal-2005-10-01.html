New Beginnings (Part Three: The Proposal)

1 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.01.05 The gardens in California's San Fernando Valley were absolutely beautiful and the day couldn't have been more perfect. Ken had finished filming The Life and Death of Superman and his commitments with NBC and Showtime for both Will & Grace and The 'L' Word, respectively. The only things on his plate for the time being was Global Wrestling and his quality time with Gunner. His goal in Global had become well known and publically published, his eyes were set on the Grand Slam, and the only two title left for him to acheive this were the Platinum and Tag Team Championships. His effort for the time being was to get noticed by the new Board of Directors for a shot at the Platinum Title and not a ranking in the World Heavyweight or International categories. That was an obstacle to him, but not an unbearable one. However, his goals for his relationship with Gunner had begun to seem stagnant to those looking on in the outside world. The question of family was one everybody's tongue. Well, everybody, that is, with the exception of Gunner. Ken was grateful at her restraint to add on to the pressure that he already had, but he knew that the stars of young children and a stable, 'normal' life lingered in her eyes. That was one of the reasons he'd brought her to one of the most beautiful areas he'd ever been. They'd chosen to spend the day in a small vineyard right outside of Angeles National Forest. The crisp air brought a perfect addition to the amazing mixture of colors and fragrances that surrounded them as they walked and sipped on their freshly made Cabrenet Sauvignon. It wasn't the best wine that Ken'd ever tasted, but for a vino that hadn't the opportunity to yet be aged, it a wonderful subtle oak to it that lingered on the tongue delicately. 'You know,' began Ken as they approached a small wooden bridge, 'there's been a lot on my mind lately.' 'I've noticed,' responded Gunner. 'You've been distant. I don't mean emotionally, or from me... just, well, with yourself.' 'Well, it's...' 'It's to be expected, I know,' she interrupted. 'I know everything you've been going through. Ana, your grandfather, your commitments with Warner Bros., NBC, and Showtime... I know you've been spreading yourself thin, trying to be Superman for everybody but yourself. I just want you to take care of yourself. I want you to realize that you can't take care of anybody if you're not taking care of yourself first. Ken, I know you're drained. You can see it when you look at you. It's like you have the weight of planets on your shoulders.' 'Things are getting better, you know,' retorted Ken in a somewhat uneasy tone as he scratched the back of his head. 'I mean, the funeral was the finality I needed with Ana and my grandfather pulled through his surgery. I've finished most of my commitments and am going to be able to spend more time at home now... more time MAKING it a home.' Gunner simply smiled and looked into the brilliant blue eyes of the man she loves. She couldn't help but notice the gentle breeze whisp through his black and red tipped hair and the way it ruffled his white silk button-up by Donatella Versace. The way his balck slack fit perfectly around his waist, secured by a black leather belt with a hollow gold buckle. Ken was definitely the movie star material that so many looked for. If only she knew that Ken looked at her in much the same way. He loved the way her beautiful and long blonde hair flowed in the breeze and the way that everything she wore, even the red salsa dress she was currently adorned in, always hung onto every curve of her body perfectly. He was constantly lost in the serenity of her green eyes and the wonders of her heart-stopping smile. If only she knew these things. Then again... maybe she did. 'Gunner,' Ken continued, 'I've been thinking a lot more about us lately, and, well...' With that one general pause, Ken finally found the strength to lower himself to one knee. 'I have a question to ask you.' The tears immediately began to stream from Gunner's eyes. She was sure of what was to come next and the atmosphere couldn't have been more perfect. Here they were, in the most beautiful and serene and wonderous place on earth... nearby, the sounds of a brooke was echoing through the beautiful lillies and lilacs that colored the canvas of the valley. The smells of flowers and wine atuned their senses and Ken had never looked so handsome... or so nervous. 'Darci 'Gunner' Addison,' he began as he reached into his pocket and pulled out the most beautiful Tiffany's stone she'd ever seen, 'I love you. I love you more than I could ever begin to put into words. You are my heart, my hands, my eyes, and my soul, and I want to spend every moment of every day of the rest of my life with you. I want to grow old with you. I want to take care of you and hold you and be the man that you always envisioned I could be.' She couldn't believe it... and neither could he. He had just overcome the most intense challenge of his life, and his heart was in a vice of jubilation and anxiety. He looked in the amourous face of his beloved, streaked with tears, and continued. 'Gun... will you marry me?' A smile spread across Gunner's face warmth spread throughout her body and soul. She began her response with a slow, but feverish nod before finally letting out a sigh of happines and whispering 'yes.' Ken's face immediatly erupted into jubilation as he slipped the ring on her finger, lept to his feet, and swooped her up in his arms with a long embrace. As the lips met, Ken realized that he'd never been so happy as he was now. She was his strength. To Be Concluded...
