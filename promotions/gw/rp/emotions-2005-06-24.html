Emotions

24 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.24.05 A tear strolled from Ken’s beautiful blue iris, down his chiseled cheek, and onto his powerful chin. His normally well-defined face had become puffy and dark beneath the eyes and his usually perfectly groomed black hair was mussed rabidly, allowing streaks of red to fly through it in an uncanny manner. As he sat, head in his hands, his body began to tremble. His already bad heart was going into calisthenics and racing at an amazing pace. The pressure had finally become too much. Ken had been carrying too many burdens on his back. As the captain of Team Global, he’d had to keep his spirits high and face the fact that he had to get along with Christian Zenith, Dawn McFarlane, and Shadow at CrossFire. Dawn was an amazing talent that he was more than happy to work with on an individual basis. She also wouldn’t have been any threat to the team had it remained her and the four members of The Elite. However, as it stood now, he and Josh, half of The Elite, were to be in War Games at the same time with three of the charter Hunting Grounds members, along with the five Team WMW faithful. At least when he took on the entire Echelon in the cell, he could trust all of his team-mates. This was as hostile an environment he’d ever stepped foot in. Unfortunately, the War Games match was the least of Ken’s worries. Josh, his brother-in-arms and running-mate for so many years, was worrying KPJ sick. His recent suicide attempt troubled Ken immensely and the Superstar blamed himself for letting it occur. It wasn’t that Partridge was held at gun-point by the man that was more than his brother. He’d faced death before and no longer feared it. It was that he had allowed it all to happen in the first place. Now, he was nothing but happy when American Youth was around. Ken wanted there to be no negativity in the house, for Josh’s sake. But, it was taking its toll on The Franchise. His entire life, the former World Champion was constantly the “happy” one. He was never allowed to show sorrow, or weakness, or vulnerability. His friends and family had always looked to him for strength, so he was to be their rock. It was a burden that he could not bare much longer. Nobody could be genuinely jovial all of the time. As if all of this wasn’t enough, the weight of a new home, the running and handling of his ever-growing nightclub in The Elements, the film, and his overall fan-base; Ken had become drained. He sat there, on one of the white Italian Leather sofa that sat in his new living room, in nothing but a pair of black silk boxers, allowing his bare feet whisper on the cherry wood floor. A few rays of early sunlight dripped across his chiseled abdomen as the front door opened. He looked up to see one of the most beautiful sights that had ever graced his mind. There, in a tight, white backless dress by Versus and a matching pair of heeled sandals by the same designer, stood Gunner Addison with the sun light beaming behind her. Her Michele Bidault earrings simply extenuated the absolute loveliness of her smile. Her hands were filled, one with a holder that housed a cup of coffee, a decaf skinny caramel macchiato, and vanilla nut frappe all from Jack’s Stir Brew Coffee, a dog-friendly SoHo coffee shop that serves only organic, fair-trade beans. In the other, a bag of fresh bread from the Vesuvio Bakery, also in SoHo, and another from Katz’s Deli, which was immortalized by Meg Ryan’s orgasm scene in When Harry Met Sally, that held a corned beef Reuben on marble rye for Ken, a turkey club on potato bread for Josh, and a Mediterranean on wheat for Gunner. As she entered the home, she seemed to move in slow motion in Ken’s eyes, her hair flowing with the wind behind her. She almost immediately noticed the look of sorrow on KPJ’s face and sat down the coffee and the bags to tend to him. Gunner walked to Ken and put her hands on his head, allowing them to get tangled in his mussy hair. She stood there, holding his face to her lower stomach, with a feeling of utmost love in her heart. When he hurt, she did, as well. “What’s wrong, Sweetheart,” asked Gunner. “I can’t keep doing this,” Ken responded. “I can’t keep holding everyone up on my pedestal with me. Eventually, the weight” it’s gonna? get to where it’s too much, and it’s gonna? pull me down.” “What are you talking about,” asked Gun as she fell to her knees, coming eye-to-eye with the Global Icon. “Nobody’s trying to pull you off of any pedestal, Hun.” “I know,” cried Ken. “I know that nobody’s trying to, but I’m not strong enough to carry everyone on my back anymore. I’ve got enough room on there for the troubles and woes of you, Josh, Bill, and Ace. Ceci and Matt’s find their way on there, as well, but I can NOT keep carrying the pain of all of my family, all of my friends, and all of my fans. I love them all with more emotion than could ever be put into words, but I’m only one man.” Gunner’s face developed a warm smile as she pulled Ken’s face in deep to her’s and gave him a kiss on the forehead before holding his head to her cheek. “You’re one of the strongest men I’ve ever known, Ken,” she began. “And, you’re right, you’ve bore this stone for far too long. However, the only person that can dismantle it is you.” “I know,” said Ken, the tears beginning to subside. “That’s what I’ve already begun to do.” The two sat and held each other on the sofa as a just rising Josh began to make his way down the stairs and into the den. He stretched as he entered the living room, which housed the two lovers. “Good morning,” yawned the American Youth, clad in a pair of Ken’s San Antonio Spurs basketball shorts. “Good morning,” replied Gunner with a smile on her face. Ken, who was quickly stammering to pop the smile back on his face only succeeded partially as he released a “morning, Buddy,” to Josh. “You okay, Amigo,” asked Josh. “You look like something’s wrong.” “Nah,” replied KPJ. “I’m fine. I just didn’t get much sleep last night.” ??His back was bothering him, again,” included Addison as she reached onto the coffee table and handed Ken his macchiato and Josh his plain coffee before snagging her frappe for herself. What Gunner spoke was truth. Ken’s back and knees had been giving him more and more trouble lately, and were the physical reasons that he hadn’t been able to sleep well the night before. “Well,” replied Youth with a smile on his face. “I guess we’ll just have to work those kinks out of you at the gym, eh.” Ken laughed. Josh had a tendency to make him do so. “Yeah,” began The Franchise. “Bill and Ace are supposed to meet us here at around eleven-thirty. We’ll all eat and then head on.” “Good plan,” shot Josh. “I’m gonna” head to the shower. Are we all still attending your monstrous gala at The Elements tonight?” “Yeah,” replied KPJ. “Which reminds me, I got you a new outfit the other day. It’s not your usual ratty tee-shirt, torn jeans combo, but I promise it’s you.” Josh emitted a sigh. “Fine, Amigo. But, just for you.” “Aww,” joked Ken. “You’re a Sweetheart, Pookie.” Josh laughed his way back up the stairs as Ken and Gunner began to put away the bread and tidy up a bit. “Are you gonna” be okay,” asker Addison. “Yeah,” finished Ken. “I’ve got you, don’t I?”
