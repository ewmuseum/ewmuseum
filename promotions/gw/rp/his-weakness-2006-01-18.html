His weakness...

18 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/01/06 ?” You guys came so close to winning those PCW Tag Team Titles!” “Oh man, I can’t believe you two almost came away with those NTICW Tag Team Titles! It was almost unreal!” ? It was almost unreal? it had all been almost unreal. It was as if something magically occurred overnight when both of those events happened. Something that I couldn’t even pin-point. I’d go out on a limb and say that fate made us the “almost-guys” that we were? but I don’t think it was that cut and dry. I really don’t think that those situations were so black and white. Over the years, my speciality has been singles wrestling? and now, I’m coming through into the realm of singles fighting. Tag Team had been? something of a small hobby that existed for a very short period of time. Do I know why I chose to participate for such a short while? Not really? but if I had to put it down to anything, I’d have to put it down to the lack of need. I would have to say that I’d done what I needed to even if it didn’t mean I was celebrated or riding it high with the championship of every company I worked for. Much like a tennis player may play doubles to perfect a shot? I played doubles to improve my game. Of course, I improved? to the point where I didn’t think I needed to improve anymore but with the announcement of Fusion, I can’t help but wonder? Is this a subtle message? Is this a kind reminder of the things that never were? Am I being ushered into doing something I really didn’t think I need to do? Here’s to hoping that everything works out for the best. ? I dragged the mouse down to “submit” and clicked it before confirming the content and publishing what I’d typed. Yeah, after all the times I’d tried to write a journal? I finally settled down and found something a little more interesting; The Blog~ An easy way to express myself without the need for a biro and a notepad. Quite ingenious if I may say so myself. Regardless, the importance of the blog isn’t what this chapter of life is about. The true importance today exists in the fact that I’m going to have to do something that I haven’t done for quite a while. In fact, it’s been longer since I wrestled tag-team than it has since I last wrestled; period. The nerves are shaking? even more so than when I made my Global return this past week at “ReLoad”. Walden did somewhat well for himself? making himself known in a bout where the odds of winning were low. Sure, I guess I went some way to announcing myself but I didn’t come anywhere close to where I wanted to be. For somebody who hadn’t wrestled competitively for around a year, I suppose my finish wasn’t as bad as I’m thinking it was. Still, I can’t take any kind of positive note from a defeat? not when it was my own mistake that put me in such a bad position in the first place. I can’t help but wonder if things would have gone better for me if I didn’t get into such a weak position. I committed proverbial suicide on that night? and virtually handed the elimination to everyone else in that octagon. Stupid Rich? stupid move. ? and what could have made things even worse? The guy who got eliminated with me; the one who I had to practically hold on to in order to save myself; I’ve got to face him on this Fusion show. So, in one hand, he’ll be trying to get revenge for what is probably going to be classed as me ending his chance to become the contender to the Platinum Championship? a reward that Kyo currently holds. Heh, I almost forgot to mention that things didn’t look any brighter, even with the inclusion of that Crisp guy. It seems that I’m going to have to lock eyes with that Calix person again. Sure, I played a part in their elimination at ReLoad? but I’m not sure on the effect that is going to have come show time. Will it play out as my advantage? Or will it simply serve as motivation for revenge? Either way, I’m walking into a bout with two people who are going to potentially want their own serving of revenge. Walden really is one lucky-gun? even though he was involved in the bout as well, it’s not like he had any major beef with these people. Oh my, now I’m left to truly ponder if being teamed with Walden is actually the greatest play in the book. I don’t mean to take anything away from the guy? but you know, it isn’t looking particularly good. Anyway, enough of the pondering? this isn’t going to get me anywhere. So, there I was, sitting in the comfortable confinement of my room after posting my blog. This was my first entry and it should have been filled with a lot more joy? but whatever, I posted what I could about the night and left all of the gory stuff out. Since my visit to that little gym, I hadn’t heard anything from anyone. I didn’t know if they were all laughing about my defeat or looking down? expecting me to charge right in to take my pent-up frustration out on anyone. To be honest, I don’t have that kind of problem anymore? but it’d been a little while since they’d seen me in such a state and so, it’s not like I could fault them. Looking at my computer screen was starting to bore me. In truth, it had been boring me for the last half an hour but the sudden urge to become “popular” and have one of these blogs was somewhat overwhelming. I was almost ready to get up and leave when somebody sent me an instant message using the very popular messenger that was known as AIM. [Global Fan06]: Wow, I can’t believe you actually use this service. Even more so, I can’t believe you’d be willing enough to have your status on anything other than private. Looking at the monitor, I was a little surprised. Of all the times to be messaged, now wasn’t one of the times I expected? and to add to the mystery, I did wonder why I was suddenly visible to the public. Don’t get me wrong, I don’t hate people but I don’t really like having anything other than a small circle of people knowing that I use a certain service. Intrigued, I felt it only polite to answer. Rich of GW: You’re not the only one who is slightly surprised. I’m a little surprised myself? since I could have sworn I’d turned my privacy to “Buddy List” only. I hit the enter key and waited patiently for a response. Something told me that this conversation wasn’t going to be like others. I can’t explain the reason for the feeling? but I just know that it felt a little odd. [Global Fan06]: LoL, maybe you turned it off or something? Anyway, I’m not interested in that. As you can tell, I’m not just a typical fan. After all, it’s not as though you flaunt this screen name of yours, right? Thought so. Besides, I’m more interested in a question that I have to ask. Rich of GW: Oh yeah, whatever could that be? Intrigued was now an understatement. What this “fan” had said was true. I don’t flaunt my username all over the shop and so I wasn’t talking to a typical fan. Unfortunately though, that still didn’t give me any real kind of light as to who this person was. [Global Fan06]: You’ve never actually made it as a tag team wrestler, have you? How on earth do you expect to team with somebody you don’t know? if you couldn’t even make it when you set aside time to actually have a tag team career? How on earth? I didn’t know the answer to that question. It wasn’t as though the question actually caught me off-guard but I just didn’t have a sufficient answer to give. I mean, truthfully and honestly, I just didn’t know what kind of answer I could give to this non-typical fan. Crisp and Calix are no-more a tag team than Walden and myself? although some teams don’t need to have spent years with each other. Some teams are just able to get the job done when it’s needed. Some teams? despite their differences, have the abilities and the talents. This guy? this person, they certainly knew how to get down to the raw stuff. Like I said before, I’d never made it in the tag division but when I left to concentrate on what I did best, I’d always believed that I left because I didn’t feel I could improve anymore. Heh, misguided thoughts lead to a misguided decision. It was never about my improvement but my ability to coexist with another. Rich of GW: You pose an interesting question. An interesting question that I don’t have an answer for. I don’t know if I’m going to be able to coexist with Walden. At the same time though, I don’t know if Crisp and Calix are going to be able to keep it together. You should really keep this kind of question for when I have to fight an established tag team? Not the best answer? but by any means, I think I could have done a lot worse. Now all I’m going to have to do is worry about when it comes to actually facing a real tag team. After all, if things between Walden and myself work out? will we be forced to team again the following week? [Global Fan06]: I figured you weren’t as confident as you were in your whole “bottle” promo. Oh man, this is going to be something. Watching Richie-boy struggle to readjust to the doubles contingent of fighting life. Do you think you could handle the two of them alone? Crisp and Calix that is. Rich of GW: Who knows? I guess we’ll see when we come to it, won’t we? I didn’t feel like answering any more of this persons questions, especially when they seemed to delve a little deeper with every answer. Being asked about how I’m supposed to adjust to fighting in a tag team again? to how it’s going to be if my singles instincts take over and I start fighting both people by myself. On top of that, I’ve now got this idea creeping into my head that I won’t even be able to trust Walden. Not cool, Rich. Not cool at all? doubting your own tag partner for the night before you’ve even really met him. Great? Feeling a little unstable, I closed AIM and pushed myself away from the computer. The chair that I’d been sitting on rolled back and came to a sudden halt against the side of my bed. Exhaling, I got up and walked out, lazily traipsing around the house until I eventually came to the living room. She was standing there again? that girl from before, the one who’d been around when I was getting ready for ReLoad. As I stood in the doorway and stared, she returned the look with a rather upset expression. ?” I hear you lost.” she uttered, looking down to the floor. “Yeah, I didn’t do as well as I had hoped?” I replied, continuing to look in her direction. ?” but more importantly, I’m not really sure I understand why you’re here.” “To be honest, I don’t really understand it either. I was minding my own business when a friend of mine commented on how you were going to be fighting again on a show called Fusion. Out of curiosity, I looked at the scheduled line-up and it said that you were down for a tag team match.” she said, her soft voice sounding even sadder and sadder. “Yes, I’m going to have to fight with somebody” not my preferred choice of match but one I’m going to have to get through all the same. Now, is there anything I could do for you?” I asked, sounding a little harsher than usual but who could blame me? I’d just had a couple of rather odd questions thrown to me by a somewhat unusual fan. ?” I was serious when I asked how you expected to do any good in a tag team match. You couldn’t even give me that answer. I tried to ask you as a complete outsider and you still didn’t give me any kind of ground. What is it with you? At first, I thought you were just being uncooperative but now? now it’s become even worse than that.” she said, her soft voice picking up in tone. I stood still and looked at the floor? a little shocked and dismayed but when I thought about it, it had been completely obvious. She was the only one that bothered enough to want to know about me. Charging over, she pushed me back a couple paces before running up to try and hit me. ?” You want to know why you were defeated? You can’t work with anyone. You want to know why you’re going to lose again? YOU CAN’T TELL ANYONE ANYTHING! YOU CAN’T WORK OR BE WITH ANYONE OR THING! EVERYTHING HAS TO BE DONE BY YOU! YOU AND YOU ALONE!” Her punches had little to no effect against me and I neglected to respond with an attack of my own. She was right though, I’d never truly been able to work with anyone. Even when I tried to run around the world with my old tag partner, I could never work with him. I had to lower myself to his standard? and he too would get dismayed when I wouldn’t reveal anything about myself. She was right? I could work with anyone, I couldn’t be with anyone. Anything and everything that I did, I had to be secure in knowing that I did it alone. ?” I? I?” I couldn’t say anything. Nothing would come out from my mouth nor would it travel from my mind to my mouth. I didn’t need this kind of pressure, not so close to the match. Lost in everything that had happened, I pushed her hands down and walked back to my room, closing the door behind me. I don’t know how long she remained there for but just as I closed my door? I heard her final words. “What are you going to do” How are you going to establish yourself like this?” ~~[ END ]~~
