Lets get ready to Drink. Part one.. [Featuring Donny McNasty and Hardcore Harv]

26 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-26-04 Monday?.. After Evolution had gone off the air.. Logan Creed and Hardcore Harv walk in to the locker room at the Global arena where Donny McNasty and Ely Braxton are talking and playing a game of poker. Both Logan and Harv talking very excited about something causeing the two to Stop playing. “What be going on here?” Donny asks Harv. “Yeah Logan, what has you two so excited?” Ely also asks. Harv and Logon both try talking at the same time making a confusing mess out of things. “One at a Time, I cant bloody here you two!” Donny shots. “Okay Donny, Here is what Logon and my self have asked Christina permission to” Host a Drink off. Ely and Donny look at each other, confusion showing on their faces as they turn back again. “A Drink off?” The two ask at the same time. “AYE! A Drinking Competition! Two to be telling the truth. One at the dark horse one at your bar Donny, The Drunken Leprechaun” ? Logan explains. “So what does that mean for us?” Ely asks. “Simple really” You both have been named team captains of the Bars and will be finding Team members to challenge each other in a Drinking contest.” Harv says, a smile coming to his face. “Aye!, And then Christina went and said that the finals of this tournament deal would be drinking at the PPV and The winner is names Grand Champion for this event.. And a Trophy goes to the winning Teams Bar.” Logan says a bigger smile on his face then Harv has on his. “Really now. That be intrusting.. ? Donny says musing at the thought of his bar having a trophy in its possession. “Yes, Well good luck, Cause The Darkhorse is going to win.” Ely states downing the rest of his beer. Donny snaps out of his ream state and looks at Ely? “Ohhh Really, NO MY Boy.. I say The Leprechaun be winning this” ? Donny says. “Well then looks like we have a contest to get ready for. ? Ely says and looks down at his cards. A Smile creeps across Ely’s face” “And the Darkhorse will win, Just like I have won this game of poker” Read em and weep Donny.” Ely says laying out his cards. A long whistle sounds from Logan’s lips? “Strain Flush! That’s gonna be hard to beat Donny” Logan says. Donny Gets up and takes a deep breath looking defeated. “Well Ely, The Leprechaun will win this , And you will come in second best just like now.” Donny says and lays his cards down on the table and walks away. Ely looks down in disbelief, as on the table is a Royal Flush. “Damn Irish Luck!” Ely shouts and Donny smiles as he walks away whistling an Irish drinking song tune. Harv and Logan shake ands and Harv follows Donny out of the dressing room. “Logan I need some good Drinkers” ? Ely says. “Ohh Yeah” You’re in it. Same with Donny, and you both are going to be in the Big Rumble at the PPV. ? Logan says. Ely looks happy to here this. And he quickly gathers up the poker cards and chips and places them in his box. “Nice. Well looks like I have a lot of things to do.. “See you tomorrow. ? Ely says and walks out of the room. Logan sees a case of un-opened bears and smiles and heads for them” “Dwarf’s have luck to my boy” Come here my beauties!” Logan says rubbing his hands together and he sits on the floor and starts to drink them?
