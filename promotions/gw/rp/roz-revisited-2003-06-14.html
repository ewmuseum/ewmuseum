Roz Revisited

14 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.14.03 - Evo 2 June 11th, 2003 ? just a few hours ago The scene fades from black, this time showing Tyler Pierce on his cell phone. Tyler is seated comfortably in the back of his limo, absent of any other patrons. He’s adorned in the same black Italian silk suit with alabaster white shirt and tie. The sun’s bright rays blast through the open sunroof, glistening off of the golden cufflinks and the mirror tinted sunglasses that Tyler wears. He has his Armani wingtips kicked up onto the adjacent seat, relaxing as it would be. A duffle bag is placed aside his briefcase in the opposite side of the limousine. “Yeah, Banks. I can hear ya. Damned reception. That’s why I don’t like cell phone’s too much. Anyway, what’s the deal?” There’s a pause in Tyler’s voice as he listens to Preston Banks on the other end of the phone. A grin comes over his face. “You mean the McCoy Bitch really did do some work” So you made another call after we had breakfast this morning, huh? Who was that to?” There’s a pause. “The midget” Whatever for?” Banks speaks again, his mumbled voice nothing more than a blur of words from the earpiece of the cellular phone. Tyler nods. “So he got a hold of Christina and she changed the card around” Not bad, Mr. Banks. So what’s the deal now?” Tyler nods as Preston explains the changes to him. It seems that once Preston got in touch with the Bitch McCoy, she got in touch with the production team and then a few other GW officials. Within the hour, she began repaying part of her debt to The Upper Echelon. But at what price would Tyler get his match with Roz Blayze? Preston explained the situation fully; Tyler Pierce would once again be scheduled to face off against Roz Blayze for the Cruiserweight Championship? which excluded both men from the tag match. Unable to rid the Evolution card of the match and thus leave a few wrestlers without a match, the setup was changed. Dynamite X and Venom Alexander would now face Sean Price and a mystery partner. Although this was a step forward for Tyler in his career, what about Price? “What a minute. What about Price” Who’s he going to tag with? I think Deacon’s already booked.” Another pause awaits the questions in which Tyler has presented. Preston speaks, not giving the answers Tyler really wanted. “Look, you and Capo know I want this title” I want Roz Blayze in that ring on Evolution. You know that. But if it means that Price is screwed in the process, Roz can wait. I’d rather stand beside Sean.” Tyler’s noble gesture is a surprise, his normal self serving attitude put aside for a moment as a stable mate may be put in the dog house so that he may get his chance? So what now? Preston speaks to Tyler again? eliciting this response. “OK, so I can’t pull double time. I don’t care what you have to do, make sure he has a partner or I’m going to go out there with him. You got me” There’s no doubt in my mind that Price can handle himself against those plebeians but in no way in hell am I going to let him stand a chance of being double teamed.” He nods and glances around the limo for a moment, not really focusing on anything but merely attempting to collect his thoughts. “Look, I’ll be down at Crabapple in a few minutes. Is Sean still around?” Tyler nods and glances out the tinted window at the passing city. He quickly rolls the security glass down and speaks up. “Turn it around” we’re meeting the others at Crabapple Cove.” The security glass goes up again and Tyler returns the phone to his ear; “Alright. I’ll be there in a few, Preston.” The scene fades to black June 14th, 2003, the office of il Capo The large cherry stained oak desk of Il Capo is positioned facing the door to his office; the floors are a black marble, the door frame made of the same. Golden candelabra’s accent the walls, perfectly positioned in the same spot on each side of the wall. The scene is visible from an angle aside Capo’s Italian Leather executive chair. We can see his arm lain across the table, a black fountain pen in his hand as he works it over a small stack of papers. The papers are inside of a folder marked “Candidates” as can be seen on the tab lying at the side. Suddenly the heavy oak door opens and Capo shifts, looking up at Preston Banks as he enters. “Mr. Pierce wishes to speak with you.” The folder is closed and the chair tilts back, the camera changing angles so that all that can be seen is the main part of the room and the arm of Il Capo. “Send him in” the voice is quick and to the point. Preston nods and turns, walking out of the office. Seconds later, Tyler Pierce steps in and closes the double doors. He turns to face Capo now, his hair pushed back and kept neatly out of his face; he’s dressed in his usual black Armani Italian silk suit with alabaster dress shirt and tie. The wingtip’s tap as he crosses the marble floor, causing a vibration just short of being called an echo. A small golden chain connects from each side of the shoe to the other, giving a very faint glimmer of light. “We need to discuss this upcoming match.” Capo’s chair tilts again before turning towards the large windows that engulf the far wall of his office. “What about it, Tyler” ? you go in, you win, the second Championship finds its home with The Upper Echelon. What is there to discuss?” The words are simple yet questioning, Il Capo’s tone revealing his complete and utter seriousness. Tyler takes a seat and places his hands on the desk. “Not that match. Sean’s. I don’t want him going into the ring with The Clique by himself.” The chair tilts again. “Tyler, I respect your concern” and honor your nobility in this situation but you do realize who the Clique consists of? I mean, Venom Alexander and Dynamite X are not exactly the highest caliber of competitors? especially not for a man like Sean.” Tyler nods and sits back in his chair, gazing at the small stack of papers on the desk of TUE’s head honcho. “I still think he needs a partner.” Again a sigh is heard as the Italian leather chair turns. A hand reaches forth, a golden ring with TUE’s coat of arms imprinted on it can be seen, and he nudges the folder marked “Candidates.” “Look at that, Tyler. Do you think I’m going to let him walk into a possibly dangerous situation” Not only am I scouring my resources for a partner? but he will have two guards on the outside of the ring if anything goes wrong. Not to mention yourself and Deacon in the back watching with another four guards. He’s covered, Tyler? you need to worry about Roz.” Tyler picks up the folder, flipping through the pages of printed information. Tyler nods and leans back. “You’re right. I should know you have things covered” and I’m sure you have contention plans for every possible mishap. Looks like you’ve narrowed it down to three for this match?” “Yes” I’m fairly sure of who it’ll be. I’ve already had the calls made and the paperwork signed.” Capo lifts a cigar from the desk and holds it up. “I really shouldn’t smoke” these things will kill you.” Tyler smirks as he drops the cigar back into the box placed at the center right edge of his desk. “So which one, Capo?” A slight chuckle rings out, “You’ll meet him tomorrow or the next day” along with Sean and Deacon. Preston left to pick him up right after you got here? they took the jet.” Tyler nods and tosses the paper onto the desk. “It’s someone in Global Wrestling already” that’s all anyone needs to know.” Tyler nods again, glancing down at the desk. “Thanks for clearing my mind. I know it’s silly considering the competition but I really didn’t want to abandon Sean” I could’ve waited for the shot. Roz won’t be a problem to mow over? you know that. That championship will be mine as of Evolution.” “I wouldn’t have let you. This is Global Wrestling” you get a shot, you take it. Sean and I are thankful of your concern but you have to begin to trust the company, Tyler. I have everything under my thumb? more things than you know.” Tyler grins a bit and nods. “I bet you do, sir. I bet you do?” The scene fades to black.
