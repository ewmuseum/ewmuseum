A Renewed Interest

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-11-04 Nothing's absolutely definate til it's absolutely, definately gone. - Brand New The cold wind blows gently, not enough to rustle your hair, but enough to sting your face whenever you raise your chin from your chest. Above, the clouds are hardly covering the sun, giving the illusion of warmth when the lack thereof is overpowering. I stare straight ahead, doing my best to avoid shivering in the frigid Detroit autumn. My mind and eyes are focused on one thing. The Young Blood gym. My former training ground, and one of the only places I could go and talk to the guys I worked closely with. That time has come and gone, as now I'm tag teaming with Salaok. Don't get me wrong, he's a good guy once you get to know him...and when he's not burning people alive or blowing their house up. He's one of the most talented wrestlers I've ever seen. Not only that, he's one of the most dedicated. After losing his entire family, he is still kicking asses. Unfortunately, we didn't work too well as a team this week. Last week we never had a match. Our opponent never stood a chance without his partner. This week, however..... This week was different. The Connection got the best of us. They isolated me and weakened me in order to win. They used me. But why? Did they see me as the weaker half of the team? Am I the person that is more likely to be beat? Or was it simply luck on their part that they managed to trap me? Either way, Salaok and I are not happy that we lost. The only thing that we are aware of after being beaten by two guys who probably practice on each other, is that we need to do something so we don't get beat again. I am going to work out, hopefully polish my ring rust off. I've been sitting out for a year, and am obviously out of practice. As for Salaok, he has his own plans, as he demonstrated with Brick and Gunner. Whatever that man has up his sleeve next, I don't know. That's the biggest misconception people have about he and I. We are a tag team. Two guys who respect each other very much. We are not, however, working together outside of the team. We do not always agree on how to start a match, how to deal with those who piss us off, or even on who gets to start the match. We share a common goal in educating most of the roster on how you have to earn your dues. Along with this goal is the tag team championships, which we will gain eventually. In the mean time, we have plenty of work to do. I'm on my own, as it was mostly my fault for our loss, being the one who was pinned. So, here I am, standing outside the YB Gym, freezing my ass off. Finally, i get the courage to go up to the doors. I pull my key out of my pocket, insert it into the lock, turn it until I hear the click, then push the door open. Everything has changed inside. What once was a mass of young wrestlers is now nothing. The bright white paint is dusted over, the neon green banners are worn, and the ring ropes are saggy. The light bulbs are all broken, with some of the fixtures hanging from the ceiling crooked. I set my bag down as my eyes glance from side to side. 'Holy shit...' escapes my lips without warning. I can't believe this place is actually closed up! On second thought, I can. For about the past year, the Bloods have been off on their own. I left the business to be with family. Jay and Ben are who knows where. The only thing sadder than the gym closing is the fact that I've lost touch with two of my best friends. TLM and BJP were still in GW when I left. Only time will tell, because the three of us are bound to meed up at some point in time in the future. Thats the way things work for us. Other than them, there was only other man who would have ever been to this gym. And I haven't seen him in ages. 'This place looks pretty sad, I know. But what can you expect?' calls a voice from behind me. My head snaps up from the dirty concrete floor and I stare directly in front of me. 'Don't act so surprised. You knew I'd find you sooner or later. Looks like things have finally come around for you. You're back in GW, your family is perfect and happy, and your damn hair is as blue as ever.' I know that voice. It can't be though. No. Not him. I spin around on my heel, almost afraid to learn I'm right. He stands in the doorway, his eyes set strongly on me. He too has a bag at his side, which he now sets down. As the sun reflects off his hair, I catch a glimpse of the purple tint in it, mostly faded out down to the bottom three or four inches, right about his shoulders. I lock my eyes on this man, and we simultaneously take steps toward each other. We meet outside the hallway to the locker rooms and stop about three feet apart. Our stares show no emotion as we stand there, but the ensuing embrace says everything. 'It's good to see you again, Johnny.' 'You too Derek. How's my neice doing?' A smile comes over my face, prompting my best friend and uncle to inquire. 'What does that mean?' he asks. 'Well, she's Cindy. I don't know if that girl ever has a bad day, she's always so happy and beautiful.' Johnny pats me on the back and answers, 'It's love. The same way I feel inside whenever I think of my Valerie. I might not show it all the time, but she's my everything. If not for her, I wouldn't be here. Despite everything that's happened to our family, all of us, even you and Cid, the one thing that never fails any of us is family.' I look down, thinking about his words. After a few seconds of silence, JC goes over and picks up his bag and carries it over. He sets it on the dust covered desk and opens it. He turns around and hands me a hammer. 'What do you say we make this place look new again?'
