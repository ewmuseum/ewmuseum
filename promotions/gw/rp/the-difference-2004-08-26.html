The Difference...

26 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/08/04 This past week on GW's primetime show, 'Evolution', the returning superstar, Rich Sherwin, found history repeat itself as he lost his 're-debut' of the sort. However, despite the loss that had come his way; the match had won rave reviews and even though this was not a physical victory... in more ways than anyone could ever imagine, this was the biggest mental victory for the fallen former champion. Despite what appeared to be something of a breakdown as the telecast went on, Sherwin was able to showcase himself to the world. He was able to show the people in and about the profession that something had changed within him... something for the better. Adam Myers and Kitty Mac had proven more than capable competition, showing themselves to be valuable opponents for future battles; be they on the same side... or the other end of the table. Unfortunately though, with every positive in the world... there is a negative to even it out. For Rich, this negative existed in the form of Guerrera; the man who laid claim to enhancing Rich Sherwin beyond his wildest dreams. He laid claim to the murder of our fallen hero... and he laid claim to being the source of his advancement. Exactly what kind of state would this leave Sherwin in when the next physically demanding contest came around? These next few days were to prove vital to his stability... + Thursday 27th August 2004 Dear Journal, As far as I can remember, I have never actively kept any record of my life. As far as I dare recall, I have no written memories of any moment in my life. Anything that I have been fortunate enough to keep is stored deep within the innermost sanctum of my mind. I'm not quite sure why I'm even writing something like this now... granted that I'd never done something of this kind. However, with the changes that were forced upon my life, I cannot allow myself to be in a position where I am forced to look down upon everything that has happened to me. Like many people walking the world, even though every step hurts... even though every breath is a painful reminder of what was, I have to make some good of my life... I have to make some good of the position that I'm currently stuck in. This journal, as private or as public as it may be... is going to be a view from within. It'll be a page by page recollection of everything important that has happened to me. Every birthday, every death, every birth, every marriage... all of it. Everything that I am able to have the priviledge of being around for, I will make note of it. Even this coming Sunday, when I am due to ride into battle once more... mental notes and images will be kept of the event. As well as those though, I will have material images to further recall these memories from. Guerrera may have done a lot to me; plenty of which I don't know if I will ever come to understand... but this book, these words, these feelings... [The extract ends] The scene slowly begins to open inside a bedroom; found in some kind of house somewhere on the face of earth. The room itself is plainly decorated; white walls and a window offering the only major features of this quarters. To finish off the almost non-existent ambience in the room, a small matress rests on the floor, a sleeping bag sprawled out on top of that. Across the other side of the small 8x10ft box room is a work desk; complete with a little draw. Sitting at the table is our fallen athlete, Rich Sherwin. Clad in a white vest and a pair of black jeans, the unshaven GW athlete looks a shadow of his former self. However, as seen with an exciting performance this past week on Evolution; the looks of the situation are very, very decieving. Sherwin appears to be making some kind of entry in a book but stops as a short and sharp knock eliminates the silence that had been in control of the room. Quickly, Sherwin closes the book and opens the main drawer of the table. He throws the book into the drawer along with a pencil and closes it, removing himself from the chair; walking over toward the matress and sleeping bag combination. '... Come in.' he says, hesistating with every passing second. Seemingly, the athlete was still unsure about his interaction with other people... even though before all of this, he'd been an athlete that many people could talk to. The door to the room slowly begins to open and an individual walks inside. They're dressed in rags, perhaps indicating that they are one of the many unfortunate people who are without a stable home. With a face hidden behind the hood of their tatty clothing, it is impossible to see what gender they are exactly. The baggy clothing that shrouds the body in a mysterious darkness even prevents the identification through traits of body size and proportion. Looking toward the stranger with a warm smile, Sherwin welcomes them into his very humble accomodation. 'Before we begin, I must 'Thank You' for agreeing to come and see me on such short notice. I must admit, I didn't expect to be able to find you... but now that I have, peace is once again restored in a tiny element of my mind. Please, come on in and take a seat.' Sherwin beckons the stranger into his 'room', getting up from the sleeping bag/matress combo; walking over to the desk and pulling out the chair. The stranger accepts the chair, nodding their head in appreciation before taking a seat. Rich walks back over to his weak bedding on the floor and sits down, looking at the stranger as they remove the hood to their tattered attire. To his own personal surprise, a beautful woman appears from behind the darkness of a dire fate. Her natural radiance seems extremely undeserving of her current surroundings. The female character is slightly tanned, suggesting an extended amount of time out in the heat of the summer. Her wonderous facial structure and the fading shine of her hair leave our GW athlete speachless, completely unable to say anything. The female individual looks bewildered at first, completely taken by surprise at the response that she was gaining from Rich. 'Please... you're making me nervous with your staring. Aren't you supposed to be against showing my kind of people such looks of affection and wonder? After all, we're below you in the eyes of the world... far below the ground that you walk.' she said, her voice as soft as anything that anyone could ever imagine. She looked sincere with her words, a fragile look now starting to come across her face. It was almost as if she'd never been able to recieve this attention before. So impressionable... and so unknowing of the things that have evaded her grasp. '... I'm sorry, I didn't mean to stare like that. You see, it's not all that often that I'm able to place my eyes upon beauty. Ever since my unfortunate accident, many people have... become scared of what I have become. Even though I'm still the same person that I was; they appear to fear me. As it stands, the only people willing to show me any kind of understanding are the people that I have to face. To be honest with you, I just don't know why people have turned me away so quickly. What is it that I have done to them?' he asked, questioning the young girl as though she was an expert in the field. As cruel as it seemed, Rich knew that the only way he was going to be able to understand everything in his path was to take each step as it came along. His matches were not quite as needing of information or help. After all, the skill to fight was something that everyone has within them... not to mention, it helped that over the course of time, GW has employed some of the strangest people to exist in this realm. 'Like me... they dislike you because you are different. They dislike you because you are no longer of the same variety that they are. Sir, you are no longer a human. Not even somebody like I can relate to the problems that you have. We... we are different beings, different forms of life. You have died... and have come back to us as something else. I'm nothing more than a homeless person... a human being without a stable home. I'm sorry... but our problems are vastly different, on all fronts. Not even we have masters to control our movements...' she responded, putting the hood back over her head before getting up from the chair and walking toward the exit of the room. Rich stopped and thought for a few moments, the words of this female character buzzing through his head like some form of highly reactive chemical. 'Are you saying that the... the quota portion of the world will no longer accept me because I'm not a pure human? Are you saying that these people will never ever grow beyond the fact that I'm something different?' he questions in response, his words full of holes and worry. The answer to this would change a lot of things in his life... a lot of things with his perception of people. Would this track of thought lead him straight into the desires of Guerrera? Would complete banishment from the normal populous be enough to drive him into the pathway of true evil? 'Sir... I cannot tell you the end of the day. Today, we begin to look at you for something that you have now become. The people who fear you now... they still see you as being the dead body that was fished out of a river. How is it that the dead are able to walk? How is it that somebody like you was allowed to come back? These people dislike you because you have been given that second chance. These people dislike you... simply because of the fact that you have not remained a part of the worlds past.' she responded, her voice full of sorrow and displeasure. Regardless of how she felt and regardless of how Rich Sherwin was perceiving this now found attitude being displayed by the people of the local area, she was right about his second chance coming to pass. Despite being killed for the sheer benefit of science, Rich Sherwin was given that chance to come back as something else. He was given that opportunity to come back as something new... something improved. With her final words, the young female left the room and closed the door behind her, leaving Rich to contemplate the meaning of everything that was said. 'Not even the true outcasts of society can offer the help in which I desire. The people dislike me because of my second coming? They show dislike and disgust because I was killed and then brought back, all the time against my own will? How can these people truely hate something that wasn't even within my control? How can these people show such... such anger?' Rich said aloud, looking up to the ceiling of his room as though he were trying to find a source that would provide some kind of answer... any kind of answer. Just something that could put his mind at ease... something that would allow him to look to Sunday and appreciate the challenge in mind without these needless thoughts and worries. Whilst his contest is not currently the biggest problem running throughout his mind, it is something that will need to be addressed when the next chapter of his life comes around. With the evil Guerrera looking over his shoulder, laying claim to the existence of this Sherwin... what trouble will come next? Stay tuned.
