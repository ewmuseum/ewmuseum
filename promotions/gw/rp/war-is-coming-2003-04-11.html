War is coming

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-11-03 Ely Braxton had to get away, the pain of seeing his Best friend in a Coma; staggered him. He never thought he would see Bubba McCoy laid low by something unseen. They had a good clue who done it but no proof, this frustrated Braxton to no end. So he entered the Federation his friend had built. Last week he had the worse beating he had ever had. “They are worse then dammed cockroaches!” Braxton bellowed out destroying the silence that was present in the Bronco. “Who are what?” asked Briggs putting down his beer, looking over from the passenger seat, a confused look on his face. “The dammed Harvesters Briggs, I hate those guys.” Said Braxton; His irritation showing in his face. “I mean Hell Brick said the others had not been seen in ages, there was only Salaok, Quartz, Vyper and Sage, Hell John had not been seen in recent weeks. Then low and behold! Here they come pouring out of the depths of hell like a bunch of fucking cockroaches!” Braxton enforced the statement by slamming his fist on the steering wheel. Briggs downed the rest of his beer. And looked at Braxton “Yeah I know, but worse, Cause you can always hire a exterminator to take out the roaches and they are gone. But those guys just keep dying and coming back, dying and coming back.” Briggs shook his head. “Dammed roaches! AHHH!! Finally we are here.” Briggs said tossing the beer-can in the back. “HAY! Who the hell hit me with this dammed can” Was it you Briggs!”!” Shouted the voice of Logan Creed from the back. “Yep I guess it was.” Said Briggs with a smile on his face as the Bronco stopped. “I will kick the crap out of you ifin you do it again!” Logan shouted back. “Come on bring it I need a good workout!” Shouted Briggs. “YOU TWO SHUT UP!” Shouted Braxton sending both into a state of total silence. The large man got out and moved with speed one would not associate with a man his size. He ran strait to the front of the Bar. =========================================================== Both Logan and Briggs climbed out looking at each other puzzled. “What was that all about?” Asked Logan. “Hell if I know Logan?” Briggs started then went silent. As they turned both let out an ominous gasp. Now they could see the explanation for Braxton speed. They stood in front of the Saloon that belonged to Bubba. And in effect belong to them. The doors were lying on the ground, looking as if ripped off. The smell of smoke could be smelt. Both looked at each other. “FIRE!” The both shouted and raced in? The place was soaked. And burnt in places smoke still rose, but the Saloons sprinklers seemed to have put out the blaze not before the damage was done, however. “Braxton!” Briggs shouted. “Stay here Logan, who ever did this may return.” Briggs said, seaming to sober up at long last. “Right. An ifin they do I am gona run them though.” Said Logan grabbing up a broken chair. Briggs then speed off looking for Braxton. =========================================================== Braxton Had smelt the smoke as he pulled up, and old reflexes kicked in. He raced into the Dark Horse Saloon. The place soaked, he cast about his head making sure no one was around. ~ God damn if Christina is hurt, I am gona kill some one. ~ Braxton thought to his self in complete silence. Braxton silently moved though building searching, old training kicking in. Dormant reflexes coming back into existence. Braxton was once a soldier. Delta Force. The elite of the elite. Now all that he had tried to put away, tried to disregard. Came pouring out in a flood. And Braxton was grateful it did. He stopped as he heard the groans coming from the office of the Saloon. He approached gradually. His fears of Christina’s harm growing with each step those fears leading into silent rage. He slowly picked up a mettle pole and held it like an expert. Braxton slowly pushed to door open, the office was completely destroyed and papers lay all over the place. Brixton’s face turned from a looks of caution to grim, a face that you saw before you died. We can strike at any time and any place, this is a warning do not think you can withstand our might. Was painted in dark red on the far wall. Braxton cracked his knuckles, then froze as a cough sounded under the toppled bookshelves. Braxton moved over to the wreckage and tossed the bookshelves aside laying there beaten and bloody was Donovan McCoy, The son of Bubba McCoy. “Don! DON!” Bellowed Braxton. “Come on boy” Who did this!” Braxton asked clearing the broken furniture “You know who did this Braxton.” The voice made Braxton freeze He looked over his shoulder and standing there next to a broken window was Silent Wind. Current GW world champion. In a flash Braxton spun and drove Wind into the wall holding him they’re with the mettle bar across his throat. Face to Mask both stared and did not budge. “Were you part of this Assassin?” Said Braxton in a steely voice. “In this” No. Is it a result of things I have done? You could say yes.” Said the Masked Assassin “How do I know I can trust you Wind?” Asked Braxton. “You don’t. But you and I both know who is responsible for this. He has returned, It me he wants, but Salaok has targeted him at you.” Said Silent Wind. Braxton released his grip, and turned away his head low. As he did Silent Wind quickly placed the dagger back in his arm sheath. “Shigeru!” Shouted Braxton. “How do I beat him Wind?” Asked Braxton. “BRAXTON! Where are you!” Rang the voice of Jesse Briggs. “Your going to have to find that answer your self, Check out the Omni Tec facility, search for anything under the name raZed. That is where you will start to learn of the opponent you face. Beware Braxton, The Harvesters have eyes everywhere now.” Said Wind. Braxton turned to face Wind. When he did Silent Wind was gone. “Braxton!!!!!” Shouted Briggs again. “UP HERE!!! QUICK! Donovan is Hurt bad!” Braxton shouted out! =========================================================== Quickly Briggs was in the room. Braxton grabbed him by the arm as he entered. “Get him to a hospital, and make sure the McCoy’s are safe. This is going to be a war Briggs. You best sober up.: Braxton said. Looking into Briggs Eye. “I’m no dumb ass Braxton. I know what to do.” Said Briggs. Braxton released Briggs’s arm, and turned to leave. “Wait! Where are you going Braxton?” Asked Briggs as he kneeled down to an unconscious Donovan McCoy. Braxton stopped. “Back to my roots Briggs. Something I did not want to do, but now. Now I have no choice. Take care of them Briggs, contact Brick. Tell him what has happened, and to meet me at the airport Saturday at ten o’clock am. I am counting on you Briggs. Don’t let me down. This is important.” With that said Braxton descended the stairs.. Briggs looked up and saw the writing on the wall. “LOGAN!!!! GET YOUR ASS UP HERE!!” Briggs shouted ============================================================ Braxton shut the door and hit the Bronco in reverse, and spun out of the gravel parking lot. He headed to face his past, one he tired to forget, one they were making him remember. They want war. He would give it to them.
