The Worst Night Of My Life (Part 2)

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/03 Thoughts still running through his head. So many, his mind can’t contain them. Everything and anything has left his mind except her smile. She is his new everything and anything’if she lets him. The thoughts spill through his head? If I say something stupid, will she think anything less of me? Maybe I shouldn’t say anything the whole way down. But then she might think that something is wrong. But nothing is wrong. Everything is perfect. Or isn’t it? He clears his throat’searching for something to say, begging his voice box to just speak the most beautiful words to her. Her heart, that is his goal. To be with her, that is his dream. He thinks? She is everything I’ve hoped for. She is everything to me. She means more to me then the first snow. More to me then a freshly planted tree. More to me then my family. Only, only if she knew this. Only if she’d give me a chance. Will she give me a chance? Will she give me a chance? Too long, he waits’he spits out the first thing in his mind? “We are going to have a lot of fun tonight.” “Yes we are.” “This is going to be the greatest night of my life.” “It is your senior prom, it should be the greatest night of you life.” He sits there, thinking of something to say, his eyes open up at another opportunity to capture her heart? “It is going to be the greatest night because I am going to spend it with you.” “Sage, you are killing me over here.” You overdone it. You are pushing it. You are only going to throw her off course. Don’t let her fall away from your grasp. Never let what you love fall away. “I’m sorry.” She chuckles? “It’s okay.” It is really okay? Does okay mean she forgives me? Or does okay means she enjoys being complimented. Don’t let it ruin you Sage. Don’t ruin it. He can’t concentrate on anything except her. The steering wheel in his hands, what steering wheel? He wishes he can drive straight into her life and to be with her forever and ever. If only it were that easy? He glances over into her eyes one more time. He can’t control it, she is just too beautiful. Never let it go. He focuses his attention on the road ahead. He spots a driver who swerves all around, off the road, then back on’trying to maintain control. He turns out of the way and tries to avoid the car, but the car turns directly into his car’the lights go out? ----------------------------------------------------------- He wakes up in the middle of the wreck. He looks at his clothing, drenched in the blood pouring from his eye. A cut running directly over his eye won’t stop bleeding. He shuts his eye, trying to see, but the burning sensation in his eye is his main attention. He turns around’she is nowhere to be found. Suddenly, his focus fades off of any wound or bruise he can ever have and focuses on her whereabouts? Is she alright? I feel like crying, but it hurts too much. Where is she? Please God Almighty Don’t let this happen. He slowly pulls himself out of the window. His car, flipped and broken into pieces. He painfully stands to his feet’looking around. Screaming at the top of his lungs with a hoarse voice? “MELISSA!” He coughs, he can’t even speak without breaking in a mouthful of blood. He spits it out and screams again? “MELISSA!” Please Lord. Please don’t let this happen. She is my everything. Don’t take her away. He turns around and limps over to the other side of the car to see her, fallen and bloodied, lying helpless and motionless on the ground. His heart sinks, her dress’covered with blood and ripped from the impact. Her eyes, closed? Her beautiful smile, covered with blood? Why Lord? Damn you Lord. You have done it again. You first take my family. And now, now you have taken her. Why Lord? He rushes over to her side? He jumps down to his knees and scoops her up in his arms? “Melissa” Please, please answer.” Her eyes slowly open’he holds her tighter? “Everything will be okay. You can’t leave me.” “I won’t leave you.” He pouts like a newborn baby, trying to force out every word? “I need you here with me. You are all I have left. Don’t leave me here alone by myself anymore.” Please Lord. Don’t take her. She is all I have left. “You have me Sage.” “I know?” “I love you.” Those words, now spoken? Oh Lord, you show no mercy. And you have no pity. But thank you for those three words. I finally know what I have? But please, Lord, let her be with me forever and ever. He does all he can to force out the words? “I love you Melissa.” Let her be with me forever and ever. “I’ll be with you forever and ever.” “I know you will.” “In your heart.” He body falls limp’he lifts his head towards the heavens? “Mock me, oh Lord. Let me fall, you have taken everything else’take me. You left me with nothing’you gave me no hope for a future. But you want me to thank you. Lord, see what you have done’see what you have DONE TO HER?” He screams at the top of his lungs as his eyes start to produce tears’a sign of sorrow. A tear falls from his eye, mixing with his blood and lands on her beautiful dress. He looks at her face, still as beautiful as can be. He slowly, softly kisses her lips. The taste has never been better. Her hand, growing cold’he holds it and grasps her in his arms? In my heart just isn’t good enough. You play with me God. You mock me God. You use me. You hurt me. You took her away from me. He can’t let her go. Out of his grasp, he can’t let her go. You let her die in my arms. You want me to proceed with life. Take me for as I am. Nothing? His tears drench her soft skin while cries on her shoulder as the? SCREEN FADES TO BLACK
