Date: 07.10.04 'So glad I could pleasure you again, Ken,' rang the seductive voice of a beautiful red-headed woman wrapped in a towel. 'So am I,' replied Ken, face down on his stomach with a large smile across his face. 'I don't know what it is you do, but it's magic, that's for sure.' The woman laughs, causing her large breasts to bounce slightly as Ken rolled over to his back, his body glistening with oils and lotions in the candle light. The aroma of lavender and butternut filled the room, making an unlikely pleasant combination. KPJ pulled his hand behind his shoulders and sighed. This was the most relaxed that he'd been in years. 'So,' asked the lady, 'am I still JUST your masseuse, or can I be a little something more yet?' She was pulling her towel off slowly as she crawled onto the bed with Ken, who quickly cut her off with his hand in her face. 'Listen, Jennifer,' stated Ken bluntly, 'we had something in the past... it's over now. You work for the same company that does my massages... a coincidence. There is nothing more there. Now, your money is all on the table downstairs. Please, collect it on your way out.' It was amazing to all who knew him just cool and calm Ken could remain in any situation. He definitly wasn't going to let a crush ruin a damn good... hell, a perfect massage. She wrapped back up as she put her clothes on. 'Yeah, well, it had better be all of it,' mumble Jennifer in an angry tone so low she hoped Ken hadn't heard her. 'It's your usual $15,000. Not bad for an hour's work and if you want to keep it that way, never question me again.' She opened the door and walked out, leaving Ken laying alone in his bed. When relaxed, anything could smack Ken's mind with a close fist, and he still wouldn't be affected. This was a good thing, because at times like this, his mind seemed to wander most. Master of the Mat was the first thing to cross his mind. The prestige behind the tournament and what it would mean to his career if he were able to pick up the crown. The opportunity of a lifetime was laid out in front of him and he was feeling more primed and ready than ever to take it. However, what if he had to face Josh. Hell, it had happened before, why wouldn't it be different this time? Because they were on equal ground. They weren't going after one another's straps... they would be striving for a Championship that may as well be declared vacant. No, no... things between he and Josh would presume as always. What happens in the rings stays there... just like the blood sweat and tears that they lose each and every night. Then his mind quickly ran into the roadblock known as Leinad Soma... the man that would be challenging him for his International Championship in less than a week's time. Still, though, the vampire could not erase the smile from his face. Ken would beat Soma... and he didn't care if it was going to take a wooden stake through the heart to do it. Ken's relaxed mind then saw utopia... and it was in the form of Gunner Addison. Her hair, her smile, her voice, her smell... it all made him melt. He couldn't wait for the day the two of them could spend forever. That's when the muscles began to tense again and the stasis of relaxation began deteriorating. Whatr if that day never came? What if she truly was happy with Ely Braxton? What if... what if she never wanted to speak to Ken again? He couldn't go on with the mindset of never again seeing her sticking to his mind. It stung him simply thinking about it. For the first time in his life, Ken could say the he was in love. Unfortunantly, he didn't know if the pther party felt the same way. He couldn't believe it... he let a crush ruin a damn good... hell, a perfect massage.
