Date: 1-19-05 ::::::(Chapter 2: You Awful Me):::::: It pains me to feel, it hurts me to see it. My hope has no meaning, my life is nothing more then your fears. I lay here dead or alive sometimes kicking to stay. Your fear the innocent yet you praise me for my pain that none of you seem to want to take credit for. Life is nothing more then my playground, I knocked you off the monkey bars. She dreams me again and again, I'm nothing more then her fiction, but time after time she reaches out for me and I can't do a thing about it but stare back longing for her to want me back. She fears what others will think, and she fears that I am to perfect for her. I charm her to often, or I don't do it enough. Yet it pains me anyway, love is this game that often is just one big joke. Guilty by design, she is nothing more then fiction. Nothing more then my fantasy, nothing more then my hopes and desires. Yet I'm blindfolded, and everyone is spinning me around to my destiny. What is destiny? What is mine? Do you fear me yet? Do I scare you yet? Have I gotten threw to you yet? You see only what you want to see, you don't see me in front of you until I tap you on the shoulder and I force you to look. You're guilty of your sins, you take no credit for what you have done. I stand alone Broken You AWful Me! With nothing but my rage to guide me to my doom and self pity. Dream the black crow dream, dream the black crow dream. Don't look, don't look! Don't absorb the image you see of me as I am this monster I don't want you to see. I often can't make up my mind whether if I'm good or evil, I play with the idea to often. I'm misunderstood I'm scared of what I might do next. Dream the black crow dream. Quote Me No More..You Awful Me. - Rafe Corvin ---------------------------------------------------------------- Images flash in black and white. What is a scene? It's nothing more then you describing thoughts making it some sort of image for everyone to see and to understand. Why do you need to understand what has already happen? What makes you truely care what happens? What draws you to me? You hear her tears, do you feel her pain? Flash! Flash! Flash! 'AHHHHHHH!' I screamed waking from my dream. I am only sixteen years old this is my foster home, but its not a home its my prison as this has become hell. I am much older then before, just as stupid thinking people actually care out their. They sent me here after my parents died, and after the mob thought I died, but you know that story already, maybe too well. After everything happen I was sent here, no family to speak of as the mob killed everyone to make sure nobody could tell a dirty little secret...no little white bird was going to carry a message to those who could help because they all died left me for the courts to decide. 'God damnit Rafe, I told you to clean everything! I told you to work!' as a whisky bottle wizzes past my head. Meet Mark Daniels, my foster father. He knows not what love is or shows any to his wife nor I. He stands taller then I am now he is fat and out of shape, he drinks all day and all night. He makes me live in the basement where rats fester and whisky bottles lay broken. My bed is a clump of blankets and a cardboard box matress. 'I told you, I am your master not your dad. You do what I say or you get nothing!' screams mark as he unlasso's his belt. 'You're gonna learn boy, you're gonna learn the hard way!' Mark says with a sick chuckle Mark races at me and forarms me to the wall as I bounce off and lay on the ground. He grabs me by my pants and throws me to my so-called bed. He takes off his belt and and starts swinging it. My head is busted open and I can hardly see, but I hear that swinging sound that haunts me to this day, that whistling sound it makes, that smell of rotten eggs that is forever imprinted in the belt due to him never showering. 'WHACK! WHACK! WHACK!' goes the belt over my exsposed back 'You gonna learn boy! You gonna learn you do what I say!' He screams after ever whip of his belt over my back. Marks begin to be shown, then cuts, then it bleeds. 'BLEED FORE ME!!!!' Mark screams again and again until I black out. It was several hours later, I start to move but everything hurts, blood stains are everywhere but thats common. I finally get enough strength to get up and crawl outside. You see Mark never does anything, he has some sort of suit that he was injured on the job. I often wonder if Mark was always this way or was he ever happy? Why did he turn to the bottle? To forget? The bottle makes you forget, but it also makes you forget everything that may of been good as it does bad. 'Oh My God!' I hear behind me, its a females voice...one I've never heard before. 'Come with me, please we gotta get you to a hospital or something!' She is in fear of my saftey, something nobody has been for some time.. 'I don't go to hospitals, Mark doesn't allow me' I say barely getting the words out. I have yet to turn to her, I don't know her but I can smell her as if she is a dozen roses. I can imagin her by just the smell as beauty by just the smell. She walks up to me I can feel her footsteps as she touches my back, more the cuts on my back then my back itself. 'You are hurt, please if not the hospital come to my house and I'll clean the cuts' She doesn't give up so easily, I like that. I have yet to see this girl, or turn around, she knocks the shovel out of my hands, as I been digging since crawling outside, I am covered in dirt and blood. She puts her hand in mine and makes me turn to face her. AS I finally do her smell agains swurls into me as all these feelings come back to me as if I had forgetten. I open my eyes to see this beautiful girl with fear on her face, it wasn't the last time I saw that look on her face... 'Please, come with me!' She still has a hold on my hand and drags me with her. Mark never let me venture very far from the house, he was smart but even I sometimes got away with stuff when I thought for sure I'd be dead. This girl had no idea who I was, nor did I have any idea who she was. Mark didn't allow me to go to school, he told the courts I was home schooled by him since he was unable to work due to his accident...that never seemed to stop him from beating me to a bloody pulp every now and then. We come to this giant white house, with white picket fences, with roses everywhere. It was one of those houses you saw in magizines she was dressed like someone you read about, being rich and wealthy. 'My name is Allison, whats yours?' She says while we walk to her front door. 'My name is Rafe' I barely get out, as I am so caught up looking at the house. 'Thats a cute name, hehe well hurry get inside.' She drags me again inside. Allison I kept thinking, as she grabbed many different things and had me stand facing the shower as we stood in this large bathroom covered with pictures and mirrors. I had a mirror behind me as I stood facing the shower then I noticed a mirror in the shower as I looked into it. This was the first time I saw my reflection in three years, my hair was to my shoulders as I studied myself I felt this pain in my back and I wince. 'I'm sorry did that hurt?!' Allison eyes dart at the mirror I'm looking into. 'It just suprised me thats all...' I muttered. I studied my body it seemed so surreal, I didn't recognize myself as I was trapt like a slave for years in a damp basement for no reason but because I was who I was. Then I looked at my back all the cuts, all the bruises. Allison was dabbing them all with something, I can't really recall what it was but it hurt. She told me I could take a shower, her parents where gone on vacation. She always did like to talk, I myself was never very open about many things but with her words just seemed to always find a way out of my mouth. I began to undress and to my suprise she just turned away as I jumped into her very large bathtub and turned on the shower head. 'How long have you lived here?' Allison questioned. 'Three years..' I answered. She asked many questions about my past, and I always second guest telling her anything, it took many more weeks and months before I told her what really happen to my family, and who I really was. After fourty five minutes I finally was done showering, my hair for the first time felt puffy as I dried off. She had cleaned my pants and shoes, well I think she had one of her maids do it myself but she told me she did it. She handed them to me as I slipped on my pants and shoes and faced her. 'You're safe here Rafe.' She smiled. 'I'm not safe anywhere...' I replied. She handed me some old shirt her father has, and I put it on and she showed me around her house, showed me pictures of her family, friends it was like walking back into to when I was eight. My mother did the same things with my old house, my old life...I realized right then I'd never get my old life back. My old life was taken from me, and that just didn't sit well. To Be continued.....
