Tales from the Outside: Episode II

15 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/04/04 Tales from the Outside Episode II: Don't forget, you're on camera!! Following the success of day one, where I was able to get the better of Jarvis Cocker and the black community of some annoying town, I looked toward emulating J.C Diamond in an attempt to boost my own personal profile and settle a little better with the people of the world... especially my fans. Much like in the way that the Osbourne's had become something of a TV revelation, I saw that Diamond was going to follow the same road, trying to make things look good and dramatic for the camera. Hell, if I remember correctly, I was watching one episode and it looked just like 'Days of our Lives'... with the crashing, the banging and the 'OMFG, is this guy dead?!?!' screams from millions of teenage girls. As said earlier, today was going to be all about the emulation of a TV product that one member of the GW roster had already started to do. JC Diamond, the man with the plan... and a whole bundle of other stuff, was my inspiration for a piece that would become a true legend in the eyes of the masses of people who give a damn. The first scene, as unoriginal as it was, opened with a few friends and I inside the living room of a rented room. The place looked rather plush with latino/mexican/immigrant flavours buzzing around the place to create a surreal and special sense of homesome-ness. I guess many of you are wondering why I'm in a home like this when I'm supposed to have so much money. Well, the truth be known... I don't have that much money and this was the cheapest 'pretty' that the production team were willing to rent. '... Ok, so what's going to happen is that we're all going to run this really cool episode I saw on 'Days of our Jayson' and there's going to be some commotion about black people or something. Somebody is going to say 'white power'... and then, whilst totally painted black, I'm going to come through and say something like... 'I pity teh foo' who messes wif Mista T'. You know it's going to work because EVERYONE is a closet A-Team fan.' I said, pitching my ideas to the friends as the cameras rolled in the background. The three friends looked to one another for a few moments, their faces twisted and contorted, most probably because my genius was affecting their ability to think also. 'Well, do you really think this kind of idea is going to work? I mean, Jarvis Cocker didn't exactly win you any worthwhile opinion polls when it came to the black community. Instead, desipte everything you did to make Jarvis look like some kind of racist bastard, you're still the most hated white-man in Harlem.' said the first friend, whose name just happened to be something like Miguel... or Paulo... or Sanchez? I didn't really pay much attention but he looked and smelt like an immigrant, so he has to be one. 'That's true, friend. If you're looking to do anything specific with this video, you're going to have to make yourself a lot more presentable and you're going to lay off all the insulting terms. People don't appreciate you because you're always abusing somebody in one way or another... especially with the commentaries of your stories.' the second friend said, his name being something like Raul or whatever. He was like friend number one in the smell and look department so it was safe to say that I'd be calling immigration on his ass once this entire production was through. My father always said that the best way to punish a man was to take away his visa. The third friend, who I didn't even care about at this point, sunk off into the background as he was refused permission to add to the general mood that was floating around the room. '... No, I don't care what you have to say.' I informed him, making it clear that I really didn't care if another person was going to agree with me on the matter or not. At the end of the day, this was going to be my video and I was going to make it in the way that I saw fit to. Looking at the remaining two members of the cast, I figured that I was going to say something else to convince them in their ways and allow me to present myself in the way that I had previously suggested. 'Ok...' I began, my voice trailing off as my eyes moved up toward the ceiling. A thousand and one thoughts escaped my mind in that instance and as my mouth opened, I could hear my only genius idea stay behind. '... Since you're both a couple of people who could do without background searches by the FBI or the CIA, I'm going to be kind and give you the chance to pick one of two SPANKTASTIC options! Either, you can tape this amazingly cool version of J. Shit Diamond's life... or you can refuse to do it and get carted off to some haven for homo-like people who'll bone you in the showers.' I thought my words for the moment had done the trick, the whole deal with the homo-people being nothing more than a stereotype gone wrong. I couldn't honestly give an them any truthful information but I'm sure that something like nine in every ten inmates that go into prison will come out as... 'queens'. 'You're going to throw us into some rabid farm for homosexual's if we don't agree to your whole video idea?' one of the nameless faces said, a worried expression beginning to cross his face. I could tell that he was seriously worried by my threat, something which nobody had done for quite a while to be honest with you. His associate just stood there and laughed, not seemingly worried about the prospect of having his anal cavity enlarged to the size of a few human fists... and then some. '... Don't think you can scare me with talk about having my bum-bum ripped to shreads by a couple of sick, disgusting homosexual people. Everyone knows how wrong is it to have that kind of thing going about inside the cells... even the Wardens frown on it completely.' he stated, standing his ground and preparing to go to war. 'You're not scared of a whole number of these sick people sticking their things in you? Well, how about I just make the call now a...' 'No, don't do it!' he screamed, his bluff not being the strongest weapon within his verbal arsenal. I looked toward the individual and chuckled somewhat, knowing that my threat had worked to it's maximum effect. 'Ok, so with all of these very uncool events moving swiftly into the background, are the two of you going to agree to my most awesome plan when it comes to copying J Shit Diamond's entire lifecycle? I'm really going to need this commitment for video one... and perhaps two, three and four also. I have a feeling that this is going to sell really well... and people don't like it when cast's change around. Just imagine what would happen if Neighbours was completely replaced by a whole town of new people?!' I informed the duo, hoping to have done enough with the threats to make them realize and understand that they weren't going to win and that they were going to work with me for more than this video alone. '... Fine.' they both responded, nothing but sorrow and displeasure lingering in their voices. I don't know why they were so upset about the arrangement, it's not as though this whole situation couldn't have been avoided. At the end of the day, they chose to be illegal... and I just managed to use that to my advantage. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The Scene... As the scene had been set up and such, we decided to press forth with the actual filming. For this segment, the two immigrants were going to be called Chris and something like, Joseph. I was going to be J Shit Diamond, an obvious attempt to rip off the GW athlete who I'll be facing on Evolution of this forthcoming week. Remember, take a breath when you're looking at that last line. The picture opens in the same room as before, nothing changed and nothing jumped about. The two immigrant people were sitting down on a sofa, the walls of the room bare and the floor being nothing more than laminated wood and a cheap fake fox rug. The brothers, as they were supposed to be, watched a television that was out of view, or pretended to watch one as this case happened to actually me. 'You know, I don't think J Shit is going to be able to defeat on Evolution of this week. For some reason, he just happened to get really lucky against Prick and Bitch Sherwin. I don't think he's going to defeat that guy this week... just because, or this feeling... this urge that I have inside of me. I want to stand up and scream out to the people of this world, exactly what I'm thinking and exactly how I'm feeling.' the immigrant playing Chris said, getting up from the sofa that he'd been sitting on, punching his hand into the air... a real feel of victory and sensation coming with each of his words. 'Brother, I know of what you speak... and I wish to share the same feeling. How can J Shit ever defeat the man who knows all of his moves? How can anyone defeat Bitch Sherwin after they fight him in the ring on one time? I don't think anyone can beat him on second time... because of WHITE POWER!' the other immigrant screamed, making no sense at all because he was of course, anything but your average white man from suburbia. TA-DUM-DUM!!!!!! It was the cheap but effective banging of trash-cans and other various garbage that I'd picked up to use as dramatic effect to save on the costs of editing. I walked into the scene, covered in this thick layer of completely black bodypaint. I looked like one of those golliwog dolls from many, many years ago. Still, despite the obviously ridiculous look, I turned to the two brothers and looked as stern as a charcoal G.I Joe would. They dropped down to their sofa immediatly, cowering in fear at the sight of the 'WHOLE FUCKING RUTH' J Shit Diamond. '.... Wat's dat I here u cay 'bout me n' mi match on Evo tis week? Wat's dat I here u b cayin 'bout me n' mi chance wif Sherwin?' I shouted, in a very thick and very fake accent. The two immigrants looked to one another, trembling with fear, shaking as though they'd seen some kind of vile ghost. 'U b finkin' dat ey don't hav a chance 'gainst tis foo' do ya? Y, I pity teh foo' who fink's ey don't stand no chance. Ey'm teh trufe n' nothin' gonna change dat!' 'We're sorry! We always knew that you were going to win... what with your completely soap-opera life and completely random ways of living each day. How could we ever think that you were going to LOSE against that joker, Bitch Sherwin?' one of them cried, tears beginning to steam down their face. For those unknown to this trick, the guy was crying to make the scene seem as though it was scarier than anything ever imaginable. 'Gud, becoz Ey iz black... n wot ey cay iz guna b teh law 'rand tis 'pace. Doez u's hear wats ey b cayin'?' I said, again trying to put on that thick accent as perfectly as possible. The immigrant actor continued to sob as the scene faded out, the actual picture coming to a close... everyone but me disappointed with the way that the scene had actually moved along. For some reason, I was beginning to feel a little like J Shit Diamond amongst this complete and utter mess. I don't know whether this was because his life was nothing like a lie... or whether it was because of the fact the 'Episode of my Life' was set to knock whatever he had to offer on primetime television, RIGHT out of the window. Regardless, the days to Evolution were starting to run thin and I for one, was starting to run out of ways to further humiliate J Shit and amuse myself. What's going to happen next time? I have absolutely NO idea.
