Hallucinations

23 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd, October Bobby stepped into his hotel room, his tired sullen face a reflection of his soul. In the past week he had somewhat gotten a grip upon the insanity that seemed to plague him, and yet he was still far from any sensible definition of 'sane'. Hitting the gym had at first helped qualm his increasingly intense bouts of rage and yet, when he had visited the last Evolution he had completely lost control. Where had the methodical genius gone? Where was the Bobby Brawler of Legend? He had no answer to these questions, only the dark hallucinations that had started to take control of his psyche months into his stay in that dank sanitarium. Without warning the familiar feeling of vertigo took hold and Bobby's vision blurred. Adrenaline pumped through his veins in such great amounts he soon felt himself invinceable, almost godlike in his ability to push his mortal body beyond any reasonable limits. The world was swirling around him now at a hundred miles per hour, his head was rushing with an almost euphoric sense of excitement. As soon as it had begun, the world became still again and Bobby found himself before a camera. How familiar this all seemed, hadn't he been known best for the promos he had recorded for his matches in the past? It only made sense that he reach out into the televisions of his fans across the world and educate them on the finer points of the psyche. A sick, sadistic grin found itself planted firmly upon Brawler's war-torn face as he glared into the camera's lens. He could feel his persona traveling through the wires and transmitting thousands of miles away. 'And I thought to myself, what a beautiful world.' he began, 'A world where such things can happen, a world where I can fulfill the prophecies I once set before you...the people who believe in me. Let us not forget the deeds I have done, great deeds, lest we find ourselves victim to the acts of aggression the lesser beings call 'violence'. It is important that when seeing an act, we let ourselves open to the reality that the act represents. What I did to Radiant...that wasn't hatred, that wasn't violence...that was love, Bobby Brawler's brand of love. He's not dead, he's stronger for what I did...as are the others. The man called Emoticon...I gave him the greatest possible gift I could have given to anyone...motivation, the gift of blood. Now, as he goes up against the demon known to us as Shadow...he will have a true reason to fight, a true reason to want victory. Before ME...he only had confusion. I took the man and I gave him direction...whether or not he finds a way to secure his place in history at Dark Rituals is not my concern, nor even his...for it is not victory that gives life, but the pursuit of it.' Suddenly the camera vanishes and reappears to his side, he turns, his eyes filled with madness and doesn't even think to question this anomaly. Instead he reaches down into his pocket and pulls out a silver zippo lighter and begins to cackle. 'And Radiant, the fire burning so bright, I will complete his transformation come Devil's Night...and nothing will ever be the same...again. Such is the way of the world, things change. It's important that they change for the better and that is exactly what I aim to do. I will take a lost soul and give him the chance to find his greater potential...I can only imagine how overjoyed he is that I have chosen him as my protoge...what did he think when I wrapped his body in barbwire and showed him the meaning of humiliation? What went through his mind as I bled him before all the world to see. I remember the pleasure I felt the first time I awoke in a pool of my own blood, the stuff of life, the strength it gave me. Baptised by my own blood...just as he was baptised in his.' Brawler sighs, walking carefully over to the hotel window. Beside it sat the barbwire wrapped steel chair and the box of c4 explosives that men and women had learned to fear. He smiled wickedly as he examined the weapon, noting the burn marks on the center of the chair. He wanted to giggle, but instead he held it in not wanting to show the fans his pleasure. Memories flowed through him as he stood there, and when he began he began so quietly, that even if there had been an actual camera in the room it would never have heard what he had to say. 'Markus...Mindy...heh, how odd that such a little girl could manage such a feat as defeating Train. Pathetic. If it had been Brick with the title, he wouldn't have given the little whore a chance. And yet...wasn't it Brick who felt my wrath so many years ago? Could he have grown since then? Strengthened? Will he stand in my way in James' little graveyard brawl? Will he remain just another worn step along the path to enlightenment? Brick...what were you thinking getting yourself involved in my affairs? Perhaps you want to prove something? Perhaps...perhaps you will you fool of a man, perhaps you will. You will help me prove to the world...' Louder now, 'THAT I AM THE TOUGHEST, MOST HARDCORE MOTHERFUCKERS ON THE FACE OF THE PLANET! Yes...you're going to serve a purpose after all...congratulations Brick...just another soul that will end up the way of everything else. For in the end...we are all...' 'broken...'
