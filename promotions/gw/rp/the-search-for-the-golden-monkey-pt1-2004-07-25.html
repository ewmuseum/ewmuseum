The search for the Golden Monkey - pt1-

25 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-25-04 The sky flashed with the pending storm that was coming to the city of Dallas. This caused the air to grow hot and heavy with the humidity. A lot different from the dry heat the man walking down the ally was used to.. The man dressed in all in black, from his dress shoes to pants to black silk shirt. His long black hair slicked down with gel causing it to seam permanently wet. The only thing identifying him was the mask he constantly wore. Dark green with touches of dark yellow and blue. El Serpenti. The man known as Serpenti slows down and stops at the end of the ally and checks his watch. Serpenti takes a deep breath and slowly lets it out. “So you have arrived on time snake man.” Comes a deep voice with a strong Jamaican accent. From the shadows steps an older man with long dreadlocks and dressed in a black suit with a black trench coat on. The man walks with a cane with a cobra’s head as the handle and his neck adorned with more necklaces then most would normally wear. His eyes are what stand out the most, as they are solid white; The Eyes of a blind man, Appearances though can be deceiving. Serpenti felt the hair rise on the back of his neck. He did not wish to come here. But had little choice. This man he found out had part of the map he needed. Although he was unsure of the price the map would cost him, He hoped is was not his soul though. “Man I hate dealing with Sorceress and Voodoo Priest.” Serpenti thought to his self. “I have, did you bring what I seek majestic one?” Serpenti said in a calmer voice then the thoughts running though his head. “Heh, heh, heh, heh!” Laughed the white-eyed man. His deep half crazed laughter sending more chills to run up and down the spine of Serpenti. “I have what you wish snake man. But the question now is what are you willing to give for this prize?” The white-eyed man says pulling out a piece of leather that looks as if it was ripped from the others. The leather is safe in side a sealed plastic bag. El Serpenti upon seeing the leather peace knew it was genuine. The map to the tomb where the Golden Monkey of Karak Zull laid rest was not known by many to have been drawn onto leather. Serpenti surmised it was part of a tent. “Well. Depends on what you seek Majestic one. Money. I am sure is not the price as you have plenty of that from your other ventures.” Serpenti said running his hand though his black hair. The white-eyed man smiled at that. Flashing his golden teeth. ? You know much Snake man, No I do not wish money for this. But do not fear, You soul is not what I wish either.” The White eyed man said placing the leather back under his coat. Serpenti took a sigh of relief at the sound of this. “Heh. Heh. Heh. NO snake-man your soul is safe today. What I seek is an amulet. It rests on the neck of a corpse that rests in a tomb in the Dallas Commentary.” The White-eyed man replied flashing another smile. “Just curious but I have to ask. You have plenty of men at your disposal. How come they don’t get it?” Serpenti asks tilting his head sideways. “Ahh.. Wise to ask. If you did not I would think you were foolish. There are powerful spells protecting it from me and those of my bloodline. Plus. None are skilled enough to pass the other obstacles. No this will take a man of skill. Believe me I have sent men to capture this treasure for myself. None have returned.” The White Eyed man said with a eerie frown. Normally this would have made any normal person run away screaming, a jaunt into the Dallas graveyard, taking a guarded amulet from a dead man. God only knows what other dangers. But no to El Serpenti this was a challenge. Much like the challenge. Plus should he succeed he would have a second piece of the map, and a clue to the location of the next piece. “Very well Majestic one I shall retrieve this amulet for you in exchange for the map.” El Serpenti said flourishing a dramatic bow. “Ahh Good. Then it is a deal. You bring me the amulet and I give you me peace of the map for the treasure you seek” The white eyed man said and he spun and walked into the shadows?. Serpenti turned and headed back out of the ally to his motorcycle waiting at the end of the ally. Upon arriving he sees what is left of what one would call a motorcycle. “ARGH! Robed and striped. Damned thieves! I hate them. Why can anything not be simple.” El Serpenti screams in frustration. Serpenti kicks his motorcycle and walks over to the bus stop and waits. Slowly he lifts his eyes to the large billboard on building across from him. An Advertisement of MOTM V displays the MOTM title and the last four champions with a shadow of the fifth. Serpenti takes a deep breath. He would need a good showing to gain more money for his adventures. To do so he was going to have to over come a lot of obstacles, much like his quest for this amulet. Serpenti knew the odds were high. All he could do is face the challenges and try to overcome them. Serpenti took another deep sigh as the Bus pulled up cutting of the view of the billboard. Serpenti steps aboard and pays the driver and takes his seat as the bus takes off. Serpenti pulls out his cell phone. “Hello” Yeah. It is I El Serpenti. Give me Talon Eagleclaw please.” Serpenti says into his cell. “Yes I can hold while being patched through.” Serpenti says again. Serpenti sits and waits as the buss moves on to its destination. Serpenti moving along to his destination with it. All it takes is some patience time and a little luck. Serpenti knows this, that if he has those things he will arrive where he is meant to be. Serpenti knows with those he can over come almost any odds against him.
