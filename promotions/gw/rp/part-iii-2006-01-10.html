Security was at their posts. They were checking out every room in the facility from the many television screens that covered the entire front wall. As they wanted to see who was responsible for tripping the alarm. The security room’s door soon slides open? Security looks at the door. As everyone immediately stopped what they were doing. They quickly got up from their seats. And they stood firmly. Who was this person who had such an influence on these people? “What’s going on here?” A man who looked to be in his mid-twenties entered the room. He straightens his white necktie that completes his black business suit. As he look at the security with dissatisfaction. “I was in the middle of some research.” In other words the man had his fun time cut short. “So where’s the alarm coming from?” The security goes back to what they were doing before the man entered the room. “Research Lab 13, sir.” “Research Lab 13?” The man quickly remembers who was inside of this lab. As he thought that someone was busting Kyo out from captivity. “I want this place on lockdown now!” Security started locking down the facility. As this would truly stop anyone from escaping. Wouldn’t it? “You won’t be taking that bastard away from me that easily. I want to watch this.” He walked to the front wall with a grin on his face. “It would be even better if Kyo was awake to see this. I would love to see the look on his face when he witnessed the pathetic excuse of a rescue team be shot to hell.” He laughs. The scene changes to a long hallway. Group of five guards were standing before a circle-shaped metal door. As they were armed with semi-automatic machine guns. They point their guns at the door. The scene switches to the security room. The man was still staring at the screen with the same grin on his face. “Open fire.” The scene changes to the long hallway. They start firing! The guards soon stopped. As the door now looked like Swiss cheese. Obviously nothing could survive that. Could they have? The door slides opens with the assistance of the people in the security room. Two guards approach the door with caution. While the rest of the group stayed where they were with guns pointed at the door. Both guards stopped. As them along with the others sees that they were facing an empty hallway. The scene switches back to the security room. “What!” Not there!”” The man glared at the screens. The scene changes to the long hallway. “They’re apparently not here. We should move on to the next section.” One guard walked away. The others were about to follow. But someone didn’t want to them leave. Two loud yells of pain were soon heard! Three guards almost broke their necks by snapping their heads so fast. And shocking saw a man who was hanging upside down from the ceiling. As this man had just killed both guards by stabbing them in their necks with two surgical knives. The guards fell to the floor? This man soon flips down from the door. As he landed on the floor on one knee with his head lowered. He was unharmed from the previous shooting due to that clever move. The scene switches back to the security room. “Who is that?” “Is he one of us” I mean he is wearing lab clothes.” The man slams both hands on a nearby desk. “IT DOESN’T MATTER! I WANT HIM DEAD! KILL HIM! KILL HIM NOW!” The scene changes to the long hallway. The guards were about to shoot the man down. But the man powerfully kicks a corpse at the guards. Corpse knocks the guard in the middle down. The other two guards prepare to fire. The man instantly swings one arm to side. As two more surgical knives struck the men in their heads for instant kills. The last guard manages to move the heavy corpse off him. He soon sits up. And aims the gun at the man’s chest. A gunshot was fired! The guard was still sitting up. As the man was still standing. Did the guard kill the man? The guard soon coughs up blood. As he fell dead on the floor. It was revealed that man has shot the guard with a handgun that he took off of one of the decease guards. The scene switches back to the security room. Security was shocked after they witnessed their allies being easily killed by this person. But the still question remains. Who exactly was this individual that didn’t even break sweat when killing these guards? They wouldn’t have to wait any longer to see who this man was. What will be their reactions to the man’s identity? Especially this man’s. What will be this man’s reaction? The scene changes back to the long hallway. The man was loading himself up with the weapons of the decease. He soon senses that he was being watched. As he realizes that a camera was watching his every move. He looked at the camera. And reveals that he was Kyo Kushinaga to whoever was watching. He grins. It was like he was taunting them. The scene switches back to the security room. Security was shocked. The man’s eyes were quivering at the sight of Kyo’s mug. “Impossible! He shouldn’t be awake! Who the hell awakened him!”” They saw Kyo nod at them. The camera feed soon goes out after Kyo shot the camera. As everyone were now watching static. The scene changes to the long hallway. Kyo still kneeled before a decease guard with the gun still held out. “I’m coming?” He gets up. And he runs down the hallway with death in his eyes. The scene switches back to the security room. “Sir.” The man didn’t respond. “Sir.” The man still didn’t respond. As he was in a state of complete shock. But among being shock he was also scared out of his mind. He would’ve probably pissed his pants if security was here. Since he already knew that Kyo was gunning for him. And Kyo wouldn’t rest until he was killed. He knew all this. But he still didn’t know how Kyo managed to get this far. He soon realized that he must’ve had help from somebody. Was it somebody from the outside that hacked their way into their system to awaken Kyo? Or was it somebody from inside the facility? Could there actually be a traitor? “Yosho!” The man was revealed to be Kyo’s most hated foe, Yosho Hiroshima. He was the mastermind of the secret group, The Organization. As Yosho snaps out of his thinking trance. “Should we report this to Mother?” Yosho looks with fear. As he knew that Mother would have his head if anything were to happen to Kyo. But his animosity for Kyo soon surfaces. “No. Alert the men. Kyo is to be killed on sight.” He figured kill him before he kills me. “But Moth?” “JUST DO IT!” He glared. Security makes the order. Yosho grins. As he was confident that Kyo was going to meet his end instead of him. But there was still Mother to worry about. Since this Mother character wanted Kyo alive for some special reason. Yosho begins thinking up a good alibi to use on Mother. Could he think of one? Or would he and Kyo be sharing a grave? “Sir!” Yosho has a pondering look on his face. As he still try to think up a good alibi to use. “What is it?” “I think you should take a look at this!” Yosho turned to the screens. As he was watching the camera feed that came from the dark room. “And what am I looking for?” Yosho had a not interested look on his face. “This room has been off limits for some time now. But there’s someone in there using the computers.” Yosho looked surprised. “What was that!”” He soon sees the mysterious person that the guy was talking about. As he begins to realize that the help that Kyo was getting was from someone in the inside. As there was a traitor among The Organization. But who was this traitor? What did this person have to gain for helping out The Organization’s worst enemy? “What do you want me to do, sir?” “Tell me where the room is. I’ll handle this.” Yosho grins. As he just figured out what his alibi will be. Or should it be who his alibi will be? “Meanwhile” The scene changes to a long hallway. Kyo was on pursuit on finding Yosho. But he stops at the sight of guard standing in his way. He soon noticed a guard appear behind him. “I don’t have time for this.” Kyo lunged at the guard in front of him. The second guard runs after Kyo. While the first guard was about to shoot Kyo. But Kyo stopped the first guard by knocking him down with a powerful roundhouse kick to the chest. Kyo soon does perhaps an impossible move that he could only do. As Kyo pushed off the first guard before he fell to the floor. And manages to knock the second guard down with a vicious spear. Kyo soon resumed his pursuit. The scene switches to the dark room. The mysterious person still watched Kyo’s exploits. But the camera feed mysteriously freezes up. The person tried to fix it. Still the camera feed was frozen. The room’s lights come on! The mysterious person who was soon revealed to be a woman with long hair. As she looks around the room. Yosho stands at the room’s entrance. “You can’t trust computers.” The woman gasps. “As you can’t trust humans either.” Yosho held a small gun in his hand. “I should’ve known that you had a hand in this. Since you have been eyeing that bastard since he came here.” He approached her. She stands up. As she began to back away from him with fear in her emerald eyes. “He is not your weapon!” Yosho laughs. “What is this!” Have you actually fallen for him!”” Yosho continued laughing. “It’s not like that!” She glared at Yosho with some fear in her eyes. “He destined for great things. And he can’t accomplish it by being locked away here.” “He destined for great things?” Yosho laughed. “The only thing that he is destined for is death.” “But Mother wants Kyo alive.” The woman looked behind her. And sees that she was cornered at the wall. “If you kill Kyo then Mother will kill you.” Yosho chuckled. “That would be true if I didn’t have an alibi.” The woman has a curious look on her face. “You killed Kyo by shutting down his life support system and I killed you to avenge Kyo’s death. And I will be made out as the hero in Mother’s eye.” Yosho glared at her. “But for that to happen’you can’t be here.” He pointed the gun at her. Her eyes can be seen quivering through her glasses. “But before I kill you?” Yosho kept his cold eyes on her. “I want to know who you’re really working for. Are you working for the government” Perhaps the Galaxy Police? Or maybe you’re working for a spoil brat?” The woman glares at him dead in his eyes with some fear still in her eyes. “I won’t tell you. So you mad as well shoot me.” “As you wish.” Yosho placed the gun’s barrel on her forehead. The woman closed her eyes. As she braces herself for certain death. But someone was about to make their grand entrance to this party? The room’s door burst open! The woman jumps. But she soon realized that wasn’t dead. “What the hell!”” Yosho looked at the door with anger in his eyes. “I thought I smelled your cologne here.” Kyo entered the room with a grin on his face. “Le Piss of Coward, right?” Yosho looks at Kyo with great fear. Kyo approaches him with guns in both hands. “I’m here to get back what your so-called trophy for capturing me. And to burst you up.” He grins. Yosho shoves the woman at Kyo without a second thought! The woman gasped. Kyo catches the woman. Yosho used this opportunity to leave the room in a hurry. “Get your ass back here!” Kyo ran after Yosho. But a guard enters the room. “Hey, what’s going on here!”” He soon noticed that Kyo was heading right for him. As he prepares to shoot. Kyo leaps onto a nearby desk. And leaps off it. “Out of my way!” As he knocked the guard down with a hard forearm smash to the face. He soon pursued Yosho once more. The woman stands there with a look of confusion on her face. But she snaps out of it. As she uses this chance to make her escape. Who was this woman? And who does she work for? This probably won’t be the last time we see or hear from this woman. The scene changes to a long hallway. Yosho makes a right at the end of the hallway. Kyo was about to do the same thing. But he stopped at the sight of four guards that came from around corner that Yosho just ran to. The guards were about to shoot. But Kyo soon notices a fire extinguisher right beside him. As he fired a single shot at the extinguisher to form a smoke screen before him. The guards fire regardless of the smoke. They soon stop. As they approach the smoke with caution. Was Kyo dead? Gunshots were fired! All the guards fall down dead with gunshot wounds to their heads. Kyo could be seen dropping down from the ceiling within the thick smoke. As he used this distraction to jump up to the vent that was above him. And kill the guards from there. Kyo soon steps out of the smoke. He glared at the corpses. Kyo senses something behind him. He looks behind himself. But he gets tackled from behind by a guard that came out of the smoke! The guard smashes Kyo’s face on the solid floor. “This is for killing my friends!” He smashed Kyo’s face on the floor again. But he soon goes to snap Kyo’s neck. Kyo glares. “They shouldn’t have joined the wrong side!” A surgical knife slid out of his coat sleeve. It slides into his hand. As he stabbed the guard in his groin with it! The guard’s scream of pain echoes throughout the hallway. And who wouldn’t be in pain after that? Hitting a sensitive spot like that would make anyone be in pain. Kyo flipped the guard over his shoulder with a common snapmare move. As the guard hits the floor hard. Kyo soon knocked the guard out with a strong punch to his skull. Kyo resumes his pursuit. He was preparing for a special attack. Since he saw Kyo was heading his way. As he was waiting for Kyo to come. Once he did then that will be the end it. Will it? The guard falls dead on the floor with blood oozing out from the back of the head. Kyo was standing in the opening to the area with a smoking gun held out. Suddenly, one of the elevators dings?
