One messed up night (Evo RP)

10 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/10/04 Brick had had a rough night. He had competed in a triple threat match with his friend and tag team partner Donny McNasty and Venneman Pendragon for the Platinum title, which And on top of that, Brick didn't get a chance to pay the Greatest Sins of All back for what they tried to do to him at Maximum Carnage. John Zero claimed it was punishment for Brick's 'crimes against the Upper Echelon'. But in Brick's mind, he hadn't begun to commit crimes against TUE. Fortunately, a post-match shower managed to improve Brick's mood...slightly. Brick had just gotten his clothes on when there was a knock at the door. 'Who?' 'I'm surprised you feel safe, runnin' water in here,' said Bubba. 'That ain't funny, Bubba,' Brick growled. 'Sorry, you're right. It ain't funny.' 'Aren't you gonna do something about it?' 'No, I'm not.' 'What?!' 'Look, I can't fire 'em, thanks to Christina's damn contracts. Besides, I figured whatever you got in mind for 'em is a lot damn worse than...' Before he could finish, his cell phone rang to the tune of 'Wanted: Dead Or Alive'. 'Yeah? Jack, what's up ---- Ely Braxton should have been the happiest man in the world that night. But he wasn't. Everything should have gone Ely's way that night. But it didn't. That night, he should have been celebrating Gunner Addison accepting his marriage proposal. But he wasn't, because she didn't. Instead of saying, 'Yes', she said: 'I'm sorry. I can't.' Then she quickly ran out of the office, but Ely quickly followed her. When he asked for an explanation, she tried to let him down as gently as she could. When he asked if there was someone else, all she could do is look at him with tears welling in her sky-blue eyes, say 'I'm sorry' and walk away. Now, Ely could do nothing except sit at the bar in the Dark Horse Saloon, downing Texas mudslides, one after the other. ''Nother mudslide, Jack.' 'I think you've had enough, Ely.' 'You're cuttin' me off?' 'You've already had more than a human should have.' The doors swung open and in stepped Brick and Bubba. 'Now, look here, dammit, I say I wanna nother mudslide...you damn well better gimme another mudslide, NOW!' 'Ely!' 'Leave me alone.' 'Come on, Ely,' said Bubba as he grabbed a hold of Ely's arm. 'Let's go get some fresh air. 'Git yer goddamn hands off me!!!' Ely roared, yanking his arm loose. 'We know you're hurtin' right now cause o' Gunner turnin' ya down, but, Ely, this ain't the way to deal with it.' 'Is my damn bui'ness!' 'Ely, we're trying to help you, man.' 'Is you, ainnit, Brick?' 'What?' Ely staggered towards Brick, his intoxication very apparent. 'I asked her if there was someone else...she didn' say a thing...but I know it was you she meant...' 'Ely, you're not thinking clearly...' 'You never liked her an' me together, did ya? Ya talk 'bout Christina, but yer jus' as bad. Bet you're glad she dumped me, huh?' 'Look...' But before Brick could get any more out, it happened. The palm of Ely Braxton's hand went smack across Brick's cheek. Brick merely stood there, barely keeping his anger in check. 'How you like that, huh, ya dirty bastard?!' 'Ely, you're drunk, so I think I'll let that slide, but don't think I'm just...' And then Ely slapped Brick again. This time, it had really set something off. 'Muthafucka!' Brick lunged towards Ely, but Bubba managed to step between the two men and hold Brick back. 'Nah, led 'im go, Bubba! Led 'im go!' Ely drunkenly shouted. 'Jack, get Ely upstairs an' give 'im some dark coffee!' Jack and a few others managed to get Ely up to the top floor of the Dark Horse. 'Why'd she do it?' Ely wailed as he went up the stairs. 'Why'd she leave me?!' 'Brick, dammit, calm down!' 'Why the fuck did you stop me?!' 'He was drunk, Brick! It wouldn't have been a fair fight! Plus, he's been through enough already.' Brick took a deep breath and sighed. 'You're right. Guess I should just let it slide. Besides, he ain't the one I'm really pissed at.' Brick then began to walk towards the exit. 'Let me know how Ely's doing.' Needless to say, this night had done very little to lighten Brick's mood.
