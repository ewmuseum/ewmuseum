The walls have ears...

1 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/1/03 (The spot light is something Sean Price is very much aware of. All his life Sean has been the guy that others looked up to. Whether its on the football field, or in the class room, Sean was always number one. However when Sean Price came to the GW everything was different. Sean had to start at the bottom and has been working his way up ever since. Now as a member of the TUE, again Price is not the number one man in the fed, however he is a member of the most dominate organization professional wrestling has ever seen. The TUE is number one, therefore Sean Price is number one.) I am the Appex. I am an All American. I am a Master of the Mat. I am a man to be feared!! It’s a wonderful day outside. The temperature is not too cold and not too warm, its just right. The sun is shining through the trees surrounding the Price mansion. Suddenly a loud sound can be heard as we see Sean Price pulling into his driveway atop his Suzuki motorcycle. Sean reaches a stopping point and then turns his motorcycle off, After dismounting from the motorcycle Sean takes his helmet off and hands it to his butler Bernard. “Mr. Stecchino is inside sir.” ?Inside? Did you offer him a beverage or something to eat?? “Yes sir. He has a drink, and cook is fixing him something right now.” “Good good. You know that I like it when my guests are taken care of. Especially one such as Mr. Stecchino.” “Yes sir.” With that Sean walks through the garage and through the door leading to the inside of his home. Sean walks into the kitchen, opens the fridge and grabs two bottles of Corona. Sean then continues through his home and up his grand stairs and then to the end of the hallway onto the back balcony where we see Tony Stecchino standing with two TUE bodyguards at his side. Sean walks past the two guards acknowledging them as he does, he then taps Tony on his shoulder and then hands him one of the Coronas. 'Here you go boss, I figured you probably need another one.' 'Sean Price you always keep me waiting don't you?' 'Well Tony I am a busy man just like yourself. Be thankful that I can fit you in at all. Now shut up and drink.' 'Sounds good to me.' 'I thought it might.' 'So Sean I have a few things to discuss with you. Do you have a moment?' 'All I have is time.' 'Ok.' 'Would you prefer we go inside and talk.' 'No, lets stay out here. The walls have ears.' 'Very true. So what is it you want to talk about.' 'Well first off, SIT...TUE is being represented by two teams as you know. I want you to pick the guys that will wrestle with you this way I can pick my own team.' 'Done. I already know who my team is going to be. I just had to wait for the final piece to fall into place, and sure enough it has.' 'Wonderful, were you planning on telling me who your team is anytime soon?' 'Yes actually I will, on Evolution when I announce it to everyone. Yourself included.' 'Are you kidding me? You are going to make me wait that long? Just tell me now so I can get to work on my team.' 'Tony, Tony, Tony, you know thats not how I work. I prefer not to give out any information until I absolutely have too.' 'Fine, because I trust you I will let you go this time. Plus I have something else to discuss with you.' 'What is it?' 'Well Sean, with each passing week our stronghold over the company gets bigger and bigger, the grip we have over McCoy gets tighter and tighter and soon the G.W will be officially under T.U.E rule. Until then there are some things we have to take care of.' 'Such as?' 'First we will start with this coming Evolution. Last week Sage Sabbot thought he could make a gallant return and stop us but we took care of him. The Youngbloods have never and will never be a threat to us. But that bastard Bubba McCoy just doesn't know when to quit. Now he thinks he can save the company by bringing in some big shot commissioner. And to top it all off the crippled bastard disappears.' 'Tony, you and I both know that no matter who Bubba decided to bring in as the new commish it is only a matter of time before he or she either realizes how powerful we are leaves or like the saying goes...'if you can't beat em, join em.' Everyone wants to be part of the greatest organization known to man. Just give it time, this company will be ours.' 'Oh believe me, I know that. But why wait?' 'Tony, taking over this company is like a game of chess. All we have to do is sit back and watch as McCoy makes his next move, and after each mistake he makes we will be there to let him know it. Between yourself, myself, Soma the rest of the T.U.E as well as Preston and Ching we are not just a group bread for destruction and dominace, not just a group full of wrestling talent, but we have the brains to do and control anything we want. It's just a matter of doing it at the right time thats all.' 'Your right Sean, with alittle bit of patience, all will pay off.' 'Thats right. Besides I have a few ideas of my own for this weeks coming Evo. I want to talk to the boys first before we decide on anything.' :'Sounds good to me.' 'I knew it would.' As the scene fades out Sean and Tony continue to comiserate outside on the balcony of the Price Mansion.
