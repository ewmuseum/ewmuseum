Yet Another Journey: Part 2

28 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th August Fear... It is a word many of us in this profession do not like to talk about. To have fear is to have a weakness and to admit that you are fearful of something shows this weakness to your opponents. However fear is a common emotion in the world we live in today, everyone fears such things as Nuclear war, okay there are a few crazy people who want Nuclear war but most of them are locked up. Anyway some fear is a good thing because if you fear nothing then you are not prepared; you underestimate the challenge or danger of a situation if you have no fear. We all fear something even if we do not like to admit it but to be honest it is the fear that drives us on. It is strange viewpoint I know but just think about it? If you fear something then when you come face to face with it you have 2 choices, you can either run away or you can stand and fight, and if you chose the latter then you become stronger and learn to deal with your fear. However if you chose the option of running away then you begin to let the fear control you. The fear almost becomes you, controlling your every action, always on your mind; it becomes impossible to escape. The reason I talk of fear is because of the fear I ran away from 9 years ago... Last time I left you I learned the fact that I was going to be a father, in fact the amazing fact that I was going to be a father. However my forgiving wife asked me to do her one favour for her and our child and that was to talk to the woman who I had not spoken to in 9 years...my mother. Now if you have been following my story then you will know that the relationship between me and my mother is non-existent, it stop existing 9 years ago when I first told my mum that I was leaving for Mexico in a week to begin my training as a wrestler. Now up until then my mother had no idea that I wanted to be a wrestler and so you can guess her reaction, the fact of my condition also didn't help matters. So after a yelling match I simply turned my back on her, my own mother, and headed for Mexico. Recently I have been having a recurring dream where I have a very strange talk with my mother and it always ends with her screaming at me that she will never forgive me. Now I am not one to take dreams literally, as they are just random thought processes in your mind. However when I finally got on the private jet Peter's company had supplied me with, I felt my stomach turn. Questions began racing around my mind, what if the dreams had been an eye into the future? What if that was the conversation I was going to have with my mother? Was my mother still living in the same house? Where could she be? There is no way that she will be happy to see me, I mean 9 years with no contact, not even a phone call from your only son. Who would be happy to see a son who did that to you? As the plane took off and left America behind, fear began to fill my body. I had to take my mind off the fear so I moved to the plane's gym to train for Whiplash.. I was on the running machine with the monitors bleeping away showing my heart rate and muscle build. The thoughts of my mother were beginning to fade as the captain’s voice came over the intercom. 'Excuse me, Mr. Myers sorry to disturb your training but a Mr. Peter Willows is on the phone.' 'Shit!' I said to myself. 'Should I hang up?' The captain said obviously hearing my outburst. 'No I will take it, put it through to the gym will you.' 'Certainly' There was a click as the intercom switched off and a red light appeared next to the phone. Hesitantly I moved over to it... The thing was I hadn't actually told Peter I was going on this little adventure to England, he thought I was using the plan to go back down to Mexico to sharpen my training. I couldn't tell him the truth; he would blow his top if he knew. He had obviously checked up on me and checked when the flight was landing but was surprised to see that it was charted for England. This was not going to be a pleasant conversation... I picked up the phone. 'What the fuck do you think you are doing?!?!' Peter screamed down the phone. Peter only ever used curse words when he was extremely pissed off. I tried the gentle approach. 'Hey Pete, how you doing mate?' 'Don't Adam, I am so pissed off with you right now!' 'Pete, I had to...' 'Had to what? Lie to me! Why the hell are you going to England?' 'Well if you would let me explain myself Pete, I think you would understand.' 'Fine, you've got a minute.' 'What?' 'Go!' 'Erm okay, well,' I was stumbling for words, I hate speaking under pressure. 'Well first of all Peter, you will be so surprised but me and Rachel are expecting a baby. Anyway...' 'Wow slow down, you are expecting a baby? How? When? How?' 'Wait what about the minute?' 'Forget it! Just answer my questions, how pregnant is Rachel?' '2 months.' '2 months! How come she never said anything?' Peter said sounding as shocked as me when Rachel told me. 'She wanted to be sure.' 'Oh my god that is great Adam, congrat...wait what has this got to do with you going to England?' 'Well that is what I was trying to explain to you. Rachel being the peaceful woman that she is, wants me to go talk to my mum and...' 'But you haven't spoken to your mum in 9 years!' Peter said stating the obvious. 'I know but Rachel wants me to try and reconcile with her. She doesn't want our child growing up, never knowing their grandmother. So being the loving husband I am, here I am on this plane, heading for England.' 'So how you feeling about this whole thing?' 'Honestly?' 'Honestly.' 'I feel sick to my stomach, I have never been so scared in my life Pete. I mean I left 9 years ago, it would be fine if it were a few months ago or even a year would have been better. Adding to the fact that she hates my guts and to be honest I hate hers.' 'So why go?' 'Because of Rachel, I need to try for her.' 'And?' 'And what?' 'And there is something else isn't there?' 'Well yeah. You know I dream I was telling you about? I think this trip will actually help me forget about it and move on.' 'Well Adam, I got to say I am not happy about this at all but I know you love Rachel and you need to do this, but what about your training?' 'Oh don't worry, there is a gym on the plane and I have set up some training time at the local gym in Lancaster. In fact that is probably where I will spend most of my time, I will fax you the number.' 'Okay good, but don't take your mind off Whiplash, Adam. This match is far too important, you put on a great fight at Evolution but I know you can walk out of Whiplash with the Cruiserweight Title.' 'I know Pete, I know. Listen I got another 6 hours flight time left, so I am going to put in as much training as I can before we land. So I better be going.' 'Okay Adam, good luck and take care.' 'I will, see you Pete.' I hung up the phone and moved back over to the weight machines. I was not going to let this trip stop my chances at Whiplash that was for sure. We landed safely and I was set up in my old specialised apartment, the one I shared with Pete. Each minute that past as I looked out of the window at Lancaster, I felt my stomach turn again. The time was now I had to see my mother or I would feel this way for the rest of my life. I picked up my keys and stepped out into Lancaster. This was it... We face many disturbing events in our lifetime and I was about to face my most disturbing. If I can face this then there is no doubt that I can face what awaits me at Whiplash. So to my fellow competitors I say this... Be ready!
