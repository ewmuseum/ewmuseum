Far from over

7 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/7/04 The date: July 17, 2004 It had been a week since Brick had lost his Master of the Mat qualifying match to Jesse Jamester and Sage Sabbot. This wasn't totally through any fault of his. His partner in the match was longtime rival Deacon Holmes. Lately, Deacon had been verbally abusing Brick's close friend Gunner Addison. Unfortunately for Deacon and for Brick, Gunner's boyfriend is a short tempered Texas cowboy named Ely Braxton. Ely brutally assaulted Deacon right before the match. Brick was so caught off-guard that he got double-teamed by Jamester and Sabbot. This week, Brick had been trying to relax, but he also had an agenda. It was at a soda vending machine where it all went down. Brick was just hanging out at the Arena as he hadn't been booked in a match for the evening. He was purchasing a soda when he heard Deacon Holmes call out to him. Deacon had blamed Brick for the loss the previous week. His argument was that Brick should have been able to keep Ely in check, therefore they would have won the match. Of course, Deacon mouthing off was more than Brick could take. He proceeded to manhandle the poster child for 'Don't Try This At Home' and issuing a bit of a warning. Brick then stormed off, leaving Deacon on the ground, gasping for air and his girlfriend Rhaiza tending to him. And then something odd had happened. Brick had unexpectedly returned. As he did, he pulled a pair of black thong panties out of his pocket, tossed them to Rhaiza and claimed she had left them in his hotel room. Needless to say, Deacon was less than enthused by hearing this, even though Rhaiza denied that they were hers. Later that evening, Brick was leaving the Global Arena to head for the Dark Horse Saloon. He had just sown a seed and he wanted to celebrate in anticipation of the fruit that would grow from it. On his way out, he passed by a dumpster when something caught his eye. He walked over to the dumpster and looked in. Reaching his hand in, what should he pull out but a pair of black thong panties, the very same pair he dropped in front of Deacon and Rhaiza. 'Oh, Deacon, Deacon, Deacon,' said Brick, shaking his head with a sly smile. 'So damn predictable.' He then placed the panties in his pocket and walked off. ---- The date: July 26, 2004 Brick had already been eliminated from the MOTM semifinals. He had won the first lottery match off the evening, being the last fighter to come out and engaging in a heated brawl with Train. However, his fatigue, coupled with Josh, Sage Sabbot and Venneman Pendragon However, Brick considered himself fortunate that he even made it to the semifinals. He and Donny McNasty had been discussing Train's unexpected visit to Brick's mother's house while the family was having Sunday dinner. Just then, Deacon came up, wanting to confront Brick. As soon as Donny left, Deacon accused Brick of fabricating the whole story about sleeping with Rhaiza. Brick then confirmed Deacon's suspicions that he and Rhaiza never slept together. As Deacon was walking away, feeling rather satisfied with himself, Brick then said that they didn't sleep together because they were up all night long. This remark caused Deacon to fly off in a frenzy and start a brawl with Brick. ---- The date: August 2, 2004 Brick sat and logged onto the Internet. The first place he went was the official Global Wrestling website. He then saw a link marked 'The GW Lowdown, Vol. 1' in the Columns section. This intrigued him enough that he clicked on the link. He then scrolled through the column to find: Big Point: It's a long way from over Like most GW observers I could have sworn I'd seen the last embers of the Deacon Holmes, Brick war burn out months ago. Well, apparently, all that means is me and most GW observers need some new contacts! These two men have taken great pleasure in pounding eachother's skulls in, in the past and it looks like we're about to take a trip back in time as the war between these two superstars, that has been so damn entertaining in the past, is about to erupt in another volley of violence. It all started a couple of weeks ago when Deac and Brick had to team up to try to make it into the next round of the Master of the Mat. Needless to say that turned into an unmidigated disaster with Ely Braxton putting his hands on Deacon even after Brick said he wouldn't let it go down. Deac took that hard and the two words. Brick responded, not just by throwing Deacon around a bit...but by producing a pair of lacy panties he claimed belonged to Deacon's chick Rhazia!!! Deacon, as you can imagine go pretty heated about that even though Rhazia said they weren't hers. Oh, before I go on, might I offer up a solution to the mystery of are they hers or not... I say we pull a Johnny Cochran... bring her down to the ring where we can all judge for ourselves and let her try them on... If the thong don't fit, you must acquit! I mean geez...if a little latin booty doesn't make the ratings spike, at least most of us will get a spike of our own. This idea had intrigued Brick. Perhaps he could use this idea to his advantage. The idea of Rhaiza publicly exposing herself would certainly Anywho... Deacon came to Master of the Mat and got in Brick's face about how he didn't believe him and was just playing games. But you got to wonder who Deacon was trying to convince with his apparent faith in his relationship, because when Brick confirmed Deacon's suspicions that they hadn't slept together....cuz they were a bit too busy to sleep Deac proceeded to try to tear Brick's head off. So... Is Brick tappin' that ass... or is he just tappin' some insecurities? Don't know...but there is one thing I do know. You'd be well advised to stand back cuz this thing is about to blow!!! 'Shadow,' said Brick, 'you have no idea.'
