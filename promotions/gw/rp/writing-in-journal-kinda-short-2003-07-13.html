Writing in Journal(kinda short)

13 July 2003

Writing in Journal(kinda short) Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #422 Date: 7/13/03 Location: unknown Dear Journal, Day 1: I arrived at Dallas about five hours ago, to get ready for a hard fought match with Ace Cash, but when I arrived I got news that I was pulled from the match with Ace I must say I am a bit nervous after having a few weeks off with family issues, and Jennifer at home getting over a miscarriage. Seems as if my life is falling apart, but I have to pick my head straight and focus on what is ahead for me in GW, getting back the platinum title which will take some time, and wait for Roz to get out of the hospital. Speaking of Roz, I visited him at the hospital; he was doing fine, and said he will be back soon. I told him about Jennifer how she had a miscarriage, he gave me his blessing. I was thankful, he been there for me when I needed him. So here in Dallas I will be watching from the backstage, waiting for the show to get done, I invited a good friend of mine to show up for overload, hopefully he makes it, since being a, I guess a former GW superstar. Who, is the guest I invited no other than the man himself Jay “The Stray Dragon” Vyper. We have been keeping in touch ever since my knee surgery. He been calling seeing how Jennifer and me where doing. Nice to know I have a friend like him. I told Jay Vyper I was going to try and get a hold of another friend to as a guest, the name will be unknown to due his issues he had in TWF. But no luck getting a hold of the other friend. Day 2: Well, the time has arrived I packed my suitcase to head back home to be with Jennifer, and when I arrived home, after a long plane ride, she had a surprise for me, she brought two tickets to the SARS benefit concert in my home town of Toronto, on July 30th. It was the greatest gift anyone could have giving me. Therefore, I just look her in her eyes then I popped the question. Will you marry me? She paused for a few moments, and then she said yes. I realize things where going good for me, Roz will soon be back in GW and then we will be down to business. Also during the long week, I had a few calls from UWF they are interested in me. However, I was like no thanks that my home is here in GW, and I plan to retire in GW. Whoever, it was that called understood my decision. From bouncing from Fed to Fed, GW and all the staff treat me like family. I finally feel at home now, being in GW still a rookie in GW, I see myself one day being the World Champ. Something I never done. Sure, I won the hardcore title in TWF, the tag titles in HCWF, and here recently I held the platinum title, plus an Iron Fist Award Winner. However, I feel there is something missing, perhaps, me and Roz when he comes back, go for the tag team gold. However, until he does, I am going to focus on each match. To prove to everyone that a 33 year old Venom is on top of his game. Yours truly, Venom Alexander
