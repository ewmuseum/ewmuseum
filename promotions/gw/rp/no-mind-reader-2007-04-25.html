No Mind Reader

25 April 2007

No Mind Reader Nathan Boswell Global Wrestling Promo #3087 Date: 4/25/07 Location: Boswell's home Nathan Boswell doesn’t know what is going to happen a week from now. He’s no mind reader, no prognosticator, no swami; nothing like that. He’s a wrestler. So instead of acting differently, he prepares for his match against Reginald Atkins in the small gym behind his home. He thinks of Atkins as he lifts weights. He thinks of Atkins as he does push-ups – two-handed, one-handed, clapping between. He thinks of Atkins as he bounces off the ropes of his ring. He doesn’t think of Adriana, however. He doesn’t think of her sitting inside, listening to the radio. He doesn’t think of her as she looks up vestigially, wondering where her man is. He doesn’t think of anything but wrestling. And that means Atkins has already won. *** Boswell wipes his face with a towel as he makes his way inside. He sits on the edge of his coffee table, his mind reflecting on everything he’s been through. His father. His uncle. Flourishing in the NWC… And then destroying it. And now this. He’s never dived into this sort of thing alone. He’s always had help from someone, Atkins usually. Being alone scares him. Again, he doesn’t think of Arianna. *** So he makes a phone call. “Hello.” A calm, deep voice answers. “Devin.” “Who is this?” The voice sounds subdued. “A voice from the past.” For some reason, Nathan’s always reverted to mystery when he talks to his old friend. “Jason?” “Think smaller. I was a friend, a confidant…” “Colin?” Nathan sighs. “A lackey.” “Oh. Hi, Nathan. How are things?” “How do you beat someone who is trying to break you without giving way to the evil inside of you?” There is a pause. “That sounds like a Linkin Park lyric.” “I’m serious.” The voice on the other side pauses again. “Trouble in the ring?” “An old friend is trying to get me to hurt people. To be like I used to be. And the only way to get rid of him is to do just that.” Devin doesn’t answer for a minute, and Nathan thinks he lost the call. Then: “These games…” “What?” “These silly wrestler’s games. Hicks want entertainment, so you put yourself in unreal situations to get a reaction. Chair shots become murder, low-blows rape. An evil wrestler rates on the scale of a petty robber doing six months for holding up a 7-11.” Nathan smiles. “Spoken like a true retired man.” “I’m serious. Let loose. Become a ‘killer.’” Devin says the last word sarcastically, the first true show of a personality. “Nothing will happen, especially in whatever tiny aquarium you’re trying to play big fish in.” Nathan shrugs. “Maybe you’re right. How’s Jessica?” “We’re not personal, Nathan. Not anymore. I’m doing fine. We’re doing fine. That’s all you need to know.” Click. Nathan hangs up the phone and sighs. It’s easy to cast stones when you’re not the one tied to the stake. But Devin has a point. Maybe he has changed. Maybe he can let it out and not do the things he once did. Too much to think about, he finally decides, and he lays on the couch. Falling asleep, he still thinks of everything but her. If he was a mind-reader, he would kiss her, hold her, try to change to future. Instead, he closes his eyes and rests. He has a big day coming up.
