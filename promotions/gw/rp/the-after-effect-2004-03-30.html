The after effect.

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 30th, 2004. Disgusted. Discouraged. Distressed. Disheartened. Disposable. Those were the feelings and emotions that ran through my body. My entire being was a nothing more than a mere bundle of out of control nerves. It seemed as if the dreams that haunted my sleep had only plagued me further…when and if I slept. Since my 'incident' in Rome I have been dazed and confused, looking everywhere for a direction. My path had only seemed to grow darker as a perpetual fog had replaced the light that shone down my life's way. Now everything had gone from being somewhat fuzzy to dark and nearly impenetrable. My minds eye was blind to the path I traveled so I hesitantly put one foot in front of the other stumbling along my journey of destiny. I was living in a constant darkness never knowing which way to turn. I never knew what to say or what to do…since my experience in Rome I was forced to look in the mirror every morning and confront a stranger. I didn't know whether or not I had attempted suicide and no one else knew either. The details weren't exactly crystal clear…I had awakened in a hospital in Rome to be told I had overdosed on the 'forget-me pill'. I certainly had forgotten. So with no idea as to whether or not I was a junky or a suicidal maniac I had pulled myself up and embraced my destiny…my destiny that brought me to battle a man whom I considered a brother. Ken Partridge Jr. my friend, my ally, my comrade in arms and the man I consider a brother. A real brother…not like the brother that was born eight years before me and tried to control my path. Not my brother by birth that mocked my career choice and told me I would end up falling flat on my face. Well my brothers prophecy had rang true time and time again. In Japan…I had dislocated my knee and broke my leg all on the same move and nearly ended my career. In America I had been nothing more than a mere stepping stone for bigger stars. I was constantly matched against Wrestler's that many thought to be superior…but in truth they were only bigger stars. And oftentimes had it not been for my own mistakes I would've easily came out as the victor. Such was the case most of my career. In the late summer of two thousand and three I had been nothing more than a springboard to glory for Jin Akiyara, and once Jin climbed to the top of the Cruiserweight division I was neglected. Did I ever receive a shot at that title? Not while he held it, not even once. Jin was hidden from he…he was protected and babied by Christina McCoy. Jin stole my spotlight…Jin stole my airtime and Jin stole my destiny away from me. Robbed of my glory, stripped of my honor and blatantly disrespected I held my head high and with my pride intact I won the Global Wrestling Primestar title. But was I allowed to revel in that glory? Was I allowed to make my mark amongst that division? Of course not…for only mere weeks after I won that title my recurring knee problems once again rose up to combat me. And as I asked Christina McCoy for time off she shoved me into a match…a match in which I lost my Primestar title. Stripped of gold and glory I was nothing more than a wasted talent with a bum knee. Surgery would repair that but it could not repair the internal demons that seemed to grow stronger as I fed them with bitterness and resentment. Bitterness at the booking squad for constantly shoving me down the card. Week in and week out I Wrestled hard and earned my spot, I paid my dues repeatedly. Yet when it all came down too it…I was replaceable. I wasn't the 'mega-superstar' they sought after. No one would deny my ability to captivate the audience with my jaw dropping aerial maneuvers and my awe inspiring technical abilities or even my talent as a martial artist. But despite all of this talent I had one thing working against me…the audience just couldn't seem to connect with me. They didn't care if I could suplex a human body in twenty five different ways without even having to blink an eye, they didn't care that I could pull off moves in utter defiance of gravity that left them reeling. They wanted a star…so I gave them their star. I called in Tommy Rock to be my special advisor and in the biggest regret of my life…I became one of them. I became just another mutant. I mutated the person that I truly am to be their hero. I conformed to their standards, I conformed to their desires and worst of all I conformed to their rules. It even garnered me a Cruiserweight title reign, but despite all of the above they couldn't find a place in their heart for me. Then suddenly one day I just snapped. I broke away from the grip society had on me, I broke away from their social stranglehold and I became my own person. My own person…a man whom was exactly the opposite of what Christina McCoy desired. She never wanted me for anything more than a curtain jerker when she inked my contract. She expected a fearless, death defying kid who would probably end up in a wheelchair on his thirtieth birthday. What she got was more than she bargained for…she received a man without fear, a man that couldn't be stopped and a man that wouldn't let office politics hold him back. I had paid my dues. I had earned my spot. I had bled for this company. I had undergone surgery. I had climbed the ladder…fallen off and climbed straight back up. I had put my body through unimaginable pain. My blood…my sweat…and my tears had all been poured into this company. I had sacrificed my well being, I had sacrificed my love…and I had almost sacrificed my life. So now as I sit here dressed in camouflage shorts that have been ripped off as they hang around six inches below my knees, a sleeveless black shirt with the Latin phrase 'Non sequitur' scrawled across the front in silver. In addition to scuffed black Chippewa boots that only climb to my ankle and a cross pendant dangling around my neck, I sit here hurting deeply. It isn't so much from physical pain as it is the pain coming from the demons that possess my mind, heart and soul, demons of self-consciousness, paranoia and hatred, demons of bitterness and strife, demons of anger and even of self-loathing. I sit here in my apartment poring over old tapes of Global Wrestling studying each and every match that Jesse Jamester has participated in, when asked of my diligent study the answer is strikingly simple…I refuse to be a stepping stone again. After nearly a year of hard work it's all thrown back in my face. I do the one thing that I can do to reach the peak of Global Wrestling's mountain yet once again…they try to take it away from me. I've scraped and clawed my way to the top and for three long hard years I have had the rug pulled out from under me countless times. I live with the knowledge that I have a job due to my Wrestling ability and not to my star power. While my charisma has been praised the fans wish for someone more fun loving, someone more entertaining…a despondent freak isn't a man they wish to see in the spotlight. So after earning my title shot Christina once again tries to take the wind out of my sails as she matches me up against Jesse Jamester. After all of my hard work this is how they repay me…again and again. I have been tested and I have been tried and I have proved myself on a consistent basis, yet Christina McCoy still wishes to take the spotlight away from me. I will not be deterred. I will not be defeated. Slowly as these thoughts of bitterness and anger flash through my mind I realize I am no longer paying attention to the battle on the screen that stars Jesse Jamester. With one quick motion I rise from the black leather couch upon which I am seated and I turn off both the Television and the VCR. I can't concentrate. I can't focus. However to win I must do both of those. I'll have to dig deep inside of myself and defeat Jesse Jamester. Destiny is singing the sweet song of victory a sound that has long been foreign to my ears. All I have to do is beat Jamester and my shot will be there…yet that task is rather arduous. Turning away from the TV I feel the churning in my stomach as an invisible hand seems to clamp on my throat…the panic attacks are returning. The contents of my stomach seem to bubble about violently as my air comes in gasps my body temperature seeming to soar to unearthly heights. I staggered forward nearly tripping over my glass coffee table but quickly putting my hand down I saved myself from collapsing onto the blue carpet as I staggered into the bathroom pushing the door open. I turned on the sink as I dropped to my knees and grabbed two handfuls of cold water splashing it across my face. It cooled me yet still didn't relieve me, the contents of my stomach churned faster and faster as I spun around on my knees, facing the toilet. In one quick motion I threw the lid open and hung my head over the bowl. Suddenly the churning in my stomach reached a point of no return as I felt the contents begin to rise through my esophagus. It departed with more haste than it arrived as multicolored vomit landed inside of the toilet with a resounding splash. I fell back slightly with my temperature only seeming to grow worse as I slammed the lid of the toilet down and quickly flushed it. Placing my hands atop the lid I forced myself up as I turned to face the sink. The water was still running and I once again stuck both hands beneath the faucet and splashed water across my features. The rohypnol had taken quite an effect on me and I was still suffering the consequences. Now my question was simply…had I defeated the grim reaper, or lost a power struggle with God? I didn't know if I had overdosed intentionally or if I was completely innocent…and not knowing was haunting me daily. As I looked up into the mirror above the sink a pair of pained bluish gray eyes with shaggy black hair falling into them, stared back at me. My reflection was quite a sight to see…a face that was so pale it was as if I had just risen from the dead. Then again…I almost had. I stood there panting softly the oxygen entering my lungs in short audible gasps. The rohypnol hadn't killed me but the after effects seemed ready to finish the job. With my pride dashed and my sanity dwindling I could only pose one question as I stared at the disturbed young man in the mirror. My rhetorical question was simple and to the point and a question that may never be answered. With my eyes meeting my reflection the words stumbled over my lips in a soft voice. 'Who are you?' Perhaps I was a man who had made far too many mistakes…perhaps I was a suicidal junky or perhaps I was just a troubled youth. I guess I was just 'American Youth' Josh…the man who had to beat Jesse Jamester and the man who must become the next Global Wrestling World Champion thereafter.
