Waking upto a nightmare

22 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/22/03 His eyes slowly open, his vision blurry. His memory is rather fuzzy. The last thing he remembers is feeling a sharp blow to his face and being knocked unconscious. His mind is now a haze and he feels a certain numbness. “Well, here we are,” he hears a voice say. Brick groans and stretches. He doesn’t realize the predicament that he’s in right now. He doesn’t realize that he’s perhaps in for the most humiliating night of his life. “Wha-wha happen?” “I got a lot of money to get you here on time.” Brick can hear a car door being opened and he can feel a hand grab his and pull him. “Well, this is the place I was supposed to bring you.” Brick sets his blurry eyes on his left. He can barely make out the shape of another man. “Want I should walk you to the door?” “Hmm?” He can feel himself being led somewhere. He can barely make out some neon lights. He then hears another voice. “Oh, you must be the entertainment.” The voice sounds male and rather effeminate. “The person who called said you’d be here. Oh, you’re a big one. The crowd is going to love. Although, I think you need to work on your make-up, sweetie.” Brick can hardly understand what this person is saying to him. He does understand the word “makeup”, though. Little by little, Brick’s mind starts to get clearer. His vision starts to steady. He can see that he’s being led to a stage. “And now Club Fantasia proudly brings to you the evening’s entertainment’MISS THING!!” The DJ starts to play an old disco song. The song is “You Make Me Feel Mighty Real” by Sylvester. Through the blinding light, Brick can make out more shapes. He sees an odd assortment of individuals. He sees men dressed in scant leather biker gear. He sees what appear to be women. But all he can hear are male voices cheering him. Brick looks out into the audience where he sees bikers and drag queens cheering him on. “Where the hell am I?” “Come on, honey! Show us what you got!” Brick looks down and realizes he’s dressed in drag. “WHAT THE FUCK!!” Brick looks around and realizes where he is and what’s going on. Brick immediately storms backstage and grabs the nearest person he sees by the collar. “What the hell is going on here”! Where am I?” “C-Club Fantasia!” croaks the frightened club owner. “How did I get here and why am I dressed like RuPaul”!” “You were dropped off in a cab! Somebody called the club and said they were sending some entertainment!” “WHO”!!” “I DON’T KNOW! THEY DIDN’T GIVE ME A NAME!!” Brick shoves him away. “Where’s the nearest pay phone” I have to make a call.” The frightened club owner points to a pay phone on the wall. Brick picks up the receiver. “I need a quarter.” The club owner reaches feverishly in his pocket for a quarter. He pulls one and gives it to Brick. Brick dials a number. He hears some ringing and then? “Hello, Jayson” Yeah, I need you to come get me, man. Where am I? Place called Club Fantasia. Think it’s some gay club. I don’t know how I got here. Just ask for directions. Look, never mind how you’ll look. Somebody dressed me in drag and left me here, okay? Yes, in drag. Stop laughing, Jayson. This shit ain't funny. That’s what I’m trying to figure out. You found my clothes? Near whose duffel bag? That motherfucker! Look, you and Duzza just come and get me and bring my clothes. Oh, and one more thing. I think it’s high time the three of us arranged a’business meeting. I’ll see you when you get here.” Brick then hangs up the phone. He then hears another effeminate voice say, “I’d like to arrange a little business meeting, myself.” He turns to see a man in leather flirting with him. “BACK UP OFF ME!!” Brick roars. The man in leather jumps back in fear. After giving the man a glare, Brick storms off, yanking the wig off his head. “I’ma kill somebody for this!”
