Tirbulations of Drake

7 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/07/05 Tribulations of Drake Fade in Here we stand on a skyscraper over looking Los Angeles; the moon has taken over the crystal clear sky joined by thousands of stars, which glimmer in the moonlight. Everything is so peaceful up here, no wonder Drake has chosen this spot as a safe haven, who wouldn’t? Well maybe someone afraid of heights. Drake wears black pants, no shirt, and a black-torn-sleeveless trench coat that rides every gust of wind. “What has the world come to” Almost nothing is as it should be anymore and at what cost? People are so caught up in their own bullshit that they blind themselves from the bone chilling truth. Perhaps they’re afraid to face reality? I can understand that and I don’t blame them. I do, however, feel that they have become weak and for some reason they can’t seem see the obvious truth and allow things to get worse. Then they feel sorry for themselves but I have no sympathy. Like a girl who starves herself, people feel sorry for her’but I do not. Because she chose to do so! When things go wrong in your life you can’t sit around and mope. You must stand up and do something about it. In this day and age you have to be strong and never let your self be a victim or let someone con you into believing you can’t do something.” Drake leans his head back, closes his eyes, and inhales the cool breeze. He slowly makes his way to one corner of the scraper and takes a seat allowing his legs to dangle off the edge. “As I said, people are caught up in their own bullshit and don’t see things for what they really are. Take celebrities for example. People are so fascinated they treat them like they were gods and rules do not apply to them. If a celebrity goes out and gets drunk it’ll be all over the news, in magazines, and you won’t be able to even go for a walk with out hearing someone talking about it. If an average “Joe” gets drunk in public and beats his wife nobody cares. Instead of people focusing on important issues it seems like all the majority of them care about is someone else’s sex life. Brittany spears isn’t a virgin? What that surprises you? Even if she was what difference does it make? She’s only human like the rest of us. Another thing I can’t figure out is why people look up to them? They make millions of dollars, which is way more than they should be making, and blow it all on pointless possessions. They buy four or five million dollar homes, and put fountains in their driveways, which is something no driveway should be with out.” He says sarcastically. “They buy car after car and other pointless objects they don’t need or use and sometimes only look at once. To top that, they go to events and pay ten thousand dollars for a single plate of food” Personally I choose burger king. It’s funny that these people actually wonder why they go bankrupt. Then they have the audacity to do these charity events to make it look like they care and want to help society. The sponsors pay them thousands of dollars just for that and for what? So they can go blow more money? It seems to me with all this supposed “charity” things would be getting somewhat better but guess what’there not! Another line of bullshit people are into now is this notion of being “patriotic”. Now don’t get me wrong, that’s fine, but they way it has all come about is what makes it a little ridiculous. Think about it! You didn’t see all these American flags in front of people’s homes or hear everyone talking about how great it is to be an American nor supporting our troops. No. None of that started, to the extreme it's at today, until after September Eleventh. And I find it sad that it takes an event of that magnitude to make people “patriotic”. People have talked about our government being able to stop it but they didn’t. Why would they? If you stop and think about it, an event like that gives them the perfect reason to go to war and war makes them money. I know it’s hard to believe that they would let people die like that for money but hey people are cruel!” Drake looks back down at the city below and sighs. “Luckily my father, God rest his soul, taught me to look at things for how they really are and not let myself be fooled. My father was a wise man and I learned allot from him in the time we had. He was truly a patriot and truly someone who cared! He always thought of himself last and would do what ever he could. He stuck up for people when no body else would no matter what their situation. If construction workers were making fun of the guy cleaning the outhouse my father reminded them “if it wasn’t for this guy you’d have no place to crap!” He always used to tell me that every job had to be done and never look down at someone because the may be cleaning an outhouse or picking up the trash. It was also my father who got me into Martial Arts when I was ten-years-old in hopes that I would never be a victim. Unfortunately he died when I was thirteen but I still had my mother who, much like my father, was very loving and wise. She taught me to respect all life and to try and avoid conflict. But sometimes it is unavoidable so “always be ready for it” she used to say.” All this thought of his father and mother brings tears to his eyes. “When I was fifteen my mother died, much like my father did, and I was sent to live with my aunt and uncle. They were allot like my parents in a way but more fun and living with them was great! But nothing that great is meant to last’I came home from school one day, I was sixteen at this point, and found my aunt and uncle dead. Murdered! They had slashed my uncle’s throat and shot my aunt in chest three times. It was at this point I realized two things: first of all my parents deaths were not accidental or natural, someone was out to hurt my family. The second thing I realized was that who ever it was would be coming after my cousin Katie and I next. So I made a vow that day a vow that I wouldn’t allow us to suffer the same fate as our parents. I started training to the extreme with not only hand to hand combat but close quarter combat weapons as well, which included: staffs, nunchaku, escrima sticks, swords, and knives. I worked wherever I could and made barely enough money to pull through, but somehow, I did. Years went by. I turned twenty and Katie was now sixteen. I was working steady and bought us a nice two-bedroom house. I was putting Katie through school, a strait-A-student I might add, and had more than enough money to spare for fun. We were doing just fine and it seemed like we had left our past behind. Until they came?” To be continued?
