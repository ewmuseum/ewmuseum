Odd Couples

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Evo #1 (8/23/04) The silence was deafening. The just a few hours ago the room of GW arena had nearlly come off. Fans from miles around poured into the concrete structure, with their signs and lungs poised at attention, waiting to release either at just the right moment. Cheer and root on their hero when he needed that one last burst of energy... to boo and jeer the man that stood as a villain in their mind, as if the very prescence of their noise added to the rest could throw him off just enough to make sure his ends were never accomplished. It was all empty now. About the only thing that stirred now was the occassional janitor and whatever might be luring in the vast shadows, just outside the ring of dimmed light that shined down, illumining the multi-sided ring that had become Global Wrestling's signature. Emptiness echoed from the rafters, reverberating back and forth across the arena, washing over deck, whistling through spaces between the ocean of empty seats. But something still stirs in the dark. The pad of the the footsteps are almost inaudible at first, but they continue to grow until it can't be missed, drawing closer and closer to the ring and the light. Slowly a figure in the dark turns from shadow...to silohette...into a champion. Train steps into the ring of light that circles the ring of canvas and steel. His black Adidas warm up with with the white stripes down the legs and his gleaming white, ribbed, cotton wife beater moved effortlessly with him. His dreads swayed from side to side as he strolled into the light, the smoke from his 'Black n' Mild' cigar drifting in a trail behind him, despite the 'No Smoking' signs plastered all through the arena. Train took another puff on the cigar and the cherry burned red...the red glow and the glare of the lights above gleamed on the surface of the GW Platinum Championship belt that sat over his shoulder. Train paused at the edge of the ring of light staring out at the vastness of the arena, taking it all in. He had to do this every now in then. Sometimes he just had to remind himself of just how far he'd come. He was living a dream nowadays. There was a long time there he figured it was dead forever. Chicago streets love thugs and gangsters, but they don't seem to favor dreamers much. When they see you have the streets do whatever they have to, to choke them out of you. But.... here he was. Train took a deep breath as he continued to scan the arena. He hefts the weight of the Platinum Title higher onto his shoulders as he can almost hear the crowd roaring. No illusions about them chanting his name however. Train knew most would only call out his name if it was preceeded or followed by some kind of expletive. That was ok though. He made no bones about giving a shit what the morons who pay, rather than get paid, to be here thought. Opionions were definitely like assholes....there were plenty of them out there. The hell with it. He'd spent what seemed like several lifetimes coming up in the street game and about every law on the books was there to tell him how wrong he was for doing it. If the law could go fuck itself so can the fans. Let them talk what they want. They may hate him, but that's half the reason the put their money on the counter to by the ticket. They wanted to see him get his. Trains hand clutched the leather strap on a belt a little tighter. They got what they wanted. Just not how they wanted it. He'd come a long way. Most would have just looked at where he stood and been happy to be there. But Train's eyes finally fell from the empty arena to the ring apron. His face slowly contorted into a scowl. Fuck being happy. Train looked at the skirting that carried the name of GW's flagship show 'Evolution'. There was no doubt the McCoy's new what high quality was. The graphics and stitching was grade A. Including the pictures of the superstars that apparently felt defined the show printed on it. Christina McCoy. Deacon Holmes. Maverick Reno. John Zero. Oh... and of course.... The great and powerful Josh. Mr. World Champion himself. 'Little fuck.' Train uttered to himself staring into his well printed, oversized eyes. Train stomps up the steel steps and whips the belt down from his shoulder before stepping through the ropes the way he's done a million times before. He slowly strides out to the center of the ring and looks out at the arena again, realizing how addicted he's become to this. Train looks up to the lighting rig hanging above the ring. The GW and Evolution banners hang from the steel grating. Everyone one of them bearing the face of 'The Champ'. Train took a puff of the cigar as Josh hung from the rafters...looking down on everyone.... Train sneers. A high pitched chime tears through the air, seeming to echo through the whole arena. Train reaches into his pocket and produces a silver cell phone. He slings the belt back over his shoulder before opening the phone with a flick of his thumb. 'Sup?' Train inquires, finally lowering his eyes from the image of the champ and blowing a plume of smoke. 'My main man!!!' yells the nasaly voice on the other end of the phone. 'Train, where the hell you been all week?! I been trying to get hold of you for forever and a friggin' day here!' 'I just been chillin' Jerry.' Train stated plainly to is over exuberant agent. 'I needed some time to think about my next step.' 'Next step? Hey baby, I'll tell you what your next step is my friend! Now that you whipped that King Arthur wannabe's but and kept the Platinum Title the Giovanni Wheels people have been ringing me off the hook. We closed the deal for a quater mil over the next year. All you gotta do is show up for a few photo shoots every few weeks and it's a done deal. As a matter of fact, I even got them to throw in some free 22' inchers for your Navigator. C'mon... tell me... Who's the man? Huh?!' 'Tell you what, Jerry,' Train replied curtly, cutting him off before next insistence on adjulation. 'If you're such the damn man why am I getting slept on like a fuckin' punk?' 'What?!' Jerry asks, his shock not feigned for once. 'What do you mean you're being slept on?! You're the champ! No one can just ignore you.' Train lifts his eyes to the rafters again. The World Champion stares back at him. 'That ain't what it looks like to me.' says Train. 'Train... my man...' inserts Jerry, trying to stall for time to formulate the proper response. 'What is going on here? I call you up to tell you you're mug is gonna be plastered in ever car and hip hop magazine from coast to coast for the next year and you tell me you're getting ignored? I gotta know what's going on inside your head before I can give you answer answer to this.' 'Oh, you wanna know what's going on inside my head, Jerry?' he asked, almost feeling the man on the other end of the line tensing up for what he had to know would be a barrage. 'How bout how am I the Platinum Champion for months now... but Mr. World Champ, who won his shit hourse after I won mine is Mr. 100 days...everybody going ga-ga over the shit when I done had my title for longer than him? Every God damn place I go I gotta look at his fucking picture plastered all over everything.... You wann know what's going on inside my head Jerry? I'm wondering how the fuck it is I'm going into one of the most dangerous matches in this business...a damn cage match... and the only person I got to watch my back is that wanna be superhero Brick?! A muthafucka who hates me almost as I hate his punk ass! I'm wondering how it is I wound up getting sucked into this Survivor Series shit at this PTC pay-per-view following Josh like me and everybody else on the team is his fucking side kick or something?! That's what's going on inside my head Jerry.' There is a moment of semi silence as Jerry takes a deep breath and produces a sigh. He knows Train. He knows most would interpret this as jealousy, but it wasn't. It was his will. It was his heart. It was his soul. It was his will to be the best at anything he does. Many thought he was. It's just no one was saying it. That's what he needed to hear. That's what he had to tell him. 'Train....' Jerry began, treading lightly. 'I don't do the booking so I don't know about this Brick thing. But I know this. Whatever you may think about it Brick isn't being asked to rep the promotion like you are. And there way his little career is going half the crowd has forgotten about the guy as it is. He may not like you but I garuntee... in the back of his mind?! He's thanking God they teamed him with a champion somebody might actually think he's championship caliber again. As for Josh... who cares what people say? If he's the face of GW, let him be...for now. Let him think that he's on top. Let him think he's the best around. While he's busy patting himself on the back he'll be too busy to hear you sneak up behind him. Let me tell you something. You might not wanna be bothered with this PTC ppv, but One? It's a paycheck. Two? You don't want to be his side kick? Don't be. This your opportunity to stand out. Put out more guys and drop more bodies than that little Goth wannabe could even dream about. You understand? Embarrass Mr. 100 Days. Let him know there's a penalty for not paying attention to you. Ok? You got it?' ......... 'Yeah,' Train says, pausing as he looks back up the rafters and the image of the champion trying to eclipse him. 'I got it.' Train flips the phone shut and shoves it back into his pocket...all the time staring up at Josh as he stares back with a smug, unwaivering look. 'Yeah... there's gonna be a penalty.' Train takes another puff of the cigar before tossing it to the canvas. A few flecks of it's burning embers spark off as it makes contact. Train steps over and grinds the burning tobacco into the mat with the heel of his boot. 'Some muthafuckas are about to get burned.' Train steps through the ropes... stomps down the steps... disappears into the darkness.
