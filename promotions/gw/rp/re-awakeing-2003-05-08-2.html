Re awakeing

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May-08-03 The sounds of a flat tone jolt Ely awake from his slumber? It had been several days since Tyler Pierce and Sean Price gave Christina McCoy the antidote to the poison in Bubba’s system. Since that day Ely Braxton has stayed in Bubba’s room. Ely had several reasons for staying with Bubba. On the top were these. He did not trust Sean Price. He knew who ever poisoned Bubba would attempt to finish the job. Ely looks around making his self wake up. Bubba is not in his bed.. With panic rising inside of him Ely is quick to his feet. For a large man Ely is surprisingly fast and agile. In a few short seconds the room is searched. Still no sign of Bubba. Just as soon as Ely reaches the door it opens. In walks a small old nurse. Who is startled at the presence of Ely who looks ready to kill. “Excuse me sir” Says the old lady in a frail voice. “Visiting hours are over. Would you please go home.” Obviously this woman had not been on duty in the last few days. Or she would no he was not a visitor in the normal sense. ? Sorry mam. But that is not a option. I have the doc’s permission to stay.” Ely said with a more harsh tone then he wanted to come out. “Now if you please. Can you tell me where Bubba McCoy is at!” Ely bellowed out. The old Nurse took a few steps back started at the sound of Ely’s voice. “I do not know. I just got off of vacation and was told to clean this room.” The nurse said, her already frail voice faltering as she swallowed hard. Ely took a deep breath. ? I Apologies mam. It is just I am worried about my friend. I did not mean to scare you.” Ely said with as much calm as he could muster. The nurse put her hands on her hips and looked Ely in the eyes. “I sir, am not scared. I was just surprised! Now there is a doctor out in the hall talking to a lady. He may be able to help you. NOW if you do not mind I have a job to do. Now please leave the room so I can do it! ? Ely tipped his hat and stepped out of the as quickly as he could. Sure enough standing across the hall was a Doctor talking to Christina McCoy. “I am amazed. How did you come by that formula to counter the toxins in Mr. McCoy’s body?” Asked the doctor. “I told you no questions asked Doctor. Lets just say I had some help from some powerful people” Christina replied. Ely walked over rubbing the sleep from his eyes. “Umm I hate to intrude. But where is Bubba, Chris?” asked Ely concern spreading across his already tired face. Christina’s face brightened at the site of Ely. “Ohh Ely he woke up this morning. IT worked the stuff Price and Pierce gave us worked! Father is awake!” Christina said. As she wrapped her arms around Ely hugging him tight. Ely for a third time soaked in the hug greedily. If only he could tell her how he felt.. With a sigh he separated his self from Christina. And looked at the doctor. “Umm Yes. Mr. McCoy is in therapy.. He has suffered a lot of muscle damage and he has to” Umm As I was telling Miss. McCoy here. Bubba has to relearn how to walk and things. The toxins caused a lot of damage.” The doctor said nervously as he adjusted his glasses. “Will he recover?” Ely asked with a glare in his eye. “Well in time. Yes. But?” The Doctor never got to Finnish. “Where is he now!” Ely demanded. His temper rising. “Umm yes. Follow me. I will take you to him” The doctor said. Ely started to follow but Christina grabbed his arm and stopped him. “Ely father has been though a lot. We know who did this” But the cops. They said?” Christina started to break down in tears. Ely grabbed her small face in his large hands and looked her in the eyes. “Chris. I know what the cops are saying. Those basterds in the Nevermore will pay. And they will pay dearly! You father will return to his former self. I will make sure of both of those things.” Ely said as a tear ran down his cheek. Christina wiped her eyes and smoothed her dress in a attempt to regain her composer. With a knowing nod Ely turned and followed after the doctor. ~They will pay big like none have paid for making her cry.~ Thought Ely.. Soon the doctor stopped and opened a door reading Physical Therapy. Ely followed in and saw Bubba sitting in a wheel chair staring out a window. The doctor and a nurse talked a bit, and then the nurse turned to Ely. “He has been like that since this morning. I do not know what to do to get him motivated.” She said with a defeated look in her face. “Leave me and Bubba alone” Ely said. The nurse looked to the doctor and he nodded. And both left. “Good luck” Said the nurse before closing the door. “Hello Ely” Said Bubba his voice not as robust as it used to be. “Bubba.” Replied Ely walking to stand next to his friend. “You’re wasting your time Ely. I give up.” Said Bubba with a deep sigh. ? I’ll be damned if you are!” Replied Ely. “Look Ely. Salaok and his goons have won. LOOK AT ME!!! I mean Hell. I wake up and Logan is in ICU! My Son has been assaulted. I KNOW Salaok and his family. Who is next. My Daughters. My Wife! NO! I do not want no part in this no More. Let Salaok have the federation. It is not that important to me!” Bubba says turning his wheel chair around. Bubba begins to head away but Ely grabs his chair. “Bubba. I am not going to let you quit. Do you remember how we met?” Ely asks. There is a long silence. “Yes” Bubba replies.. “Yes I do.” “You saved me, Bubba. You did not let me quit. God knows I wanted to. All I wanted to do was crawl under a rock and die. But NO Bubba McCoy could not let me do it. And now.. NOW It my turn. You’re not going to quit.” Ely says moving around Bubba’s chair where he could look Bubba face to face. “But. What. If?” “NO BUTS BUBBA! You’re going to pull though this. If it kills me you will.” Ely said placing his thumbs in his belt loops. Bubba thought for a while.. “Fine. Have it your way. ? Bubba said. “Good. But not here. You’re coming with me! ? Eley stated. Ely grabed the back of Bubba’s chair and stated pushing. “Wha.” Was all Bubba could get out before the doctor walked in. “Umm where are you takeing him?” The doctor asked. “Out of here.” Said Ely. “But that patent is under my care1” Replied the doctor. “Fine then you’ll have to come along! ? Said Ely as he pushed past the protesting doctor. The Doctor rushed to catch up.. “Just where are we going?” Asked Bubba. “To see Talon Two Eagles” Said Ely. As Bubba and Ely got to the bottom floor two men in black suits head up to Ely.. The first man stands in front of the two. ? Are you Ely Braxton? says the first man. “Depends on who is asking. ? Replies Ely. “My name is Michael Spinks. I am here to let you know your part of a invite tournament only. Here is your paper.. Be there. Both men walk away leaving Ely to hold the envelope. “Looks like you will need My help as much as I need yours Ely.” Says Bubba with a slight smile. Ely places the envelope in his pocket. “I recon that’s true Bubba” Says Ely as the Two head out of the hospital.. A Upset doctor following suit Both then headed to the truck. ------------------------------------ As the three speed away in the truck, a man with long black hair, dressed in a trench coat, steps out from behind a tree camera in hand. He takes a few snap shots then starts writing down some notes? A small smile coming to his lips.
