Nocturnal Activities...A New Man...

12 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-12-2004 A prince should therefore have no other aim or thought, nor take up any other thing for his study but war and it organization and discipline, for that is the only art that is necessary to one who commands. Niccolo Machiavelli, The Prince The room was small, yet elegant. A great bed occupied most of the space, along with a small vanity, a chair and a large window through which moonlight streamed. Upon the bed lay the Lord King Venneman Pendragon. Rather modest accommodations for royalty, yet he seems content as he stands at the window staring into the night. Wind blows through and blows his hair from his forehead. He is dressed simply, wearing a flowing white shirt with ruffled cuffs and black leather pants tucked into black riding boots. As he stares into the night, his right hand continually drops to the familiar spot where his sword hilt normally lays against his hip. Each time, he draws back unexpectedly at finding the place empty of his mighty blade, Excalibur. Anger smolders in his eyes, and his face is a conflict of emotion’Anger vs. sorrow. In the moonlight, he is truly a picture of the noble king? “After Ben found me, he took me to a room at an Inn, and there I stayed’For a short while, though the room was of an elegance that was a bit beneath a king. Staring into the moonlight, it seemed like all the trials and tribulations I have faced of late were bearing down upon me. The losses, the injuries to the boy’That poor, poor lad...Freeze. I slept not, and kept rerunning scenarios through mine mind. “What if I had done this” What if I had done that?” Questions that have no meaning and yet the human mind is loathe to let them slip away in times of great trouble. I have lived with the ideals of honor all mine life. I have never dishonored an honorable enemy, and I am a servant to the weak; their protector. And yet, here, in this lonely room, I felt my conscious at a crossroads. A simple man, quick to action, has been the description that I would have given myself in the past. Headlong into danger I would run, regardless of the consequences. Passion has been mine fuel. Yet, in the world that is GW, it seems that I have been without passion. I have shunned mine darker side in hopes that the Light in me would overcome all obstacles before me. I have presented only half of a man to mine foes. A leader is both good and bad’Good to his people, devastating in his efforts to protect them. Bad for his enemies, devastating in his quest to destroy them. Thus far, because of mine relentlessness upon being good, I have failed. I have failed mineself, and I have failed mine fans. No more!” Pendragon leaps from the window, and the shot switches to show him flying from a second story window in a stone building. He lands in a kneeling position, head down. He raises his head, and his eyes blaze with what can only be described as glory. Power seems to leap from his visage, and a smile plays about his lips. For the first time ever, we look upon Pendragon as he should be. Unhindered by useless thoughts for the welfare of his enemies. He stands and makes a fist with his right hand, looking at the fingers as they clench. A fist is an amazing thing. The very muscles involved to make such a simple gesture, the signals from the brain that make the muscles do as the mind wishes? Pendragon looks at the moon and smiles?“The whelps have mine sword, and yet, what is the sword without the strong arm to wield it” Man is the true weapon’I am the true weapon. Forged of generations of Pendragon’s I come, and now I unleash mineself against mine enemies. Freeze, Akuma, Jetsui, even Drachen. Each man has served to dishonor me, and I chose only to sit by and allow it to happen’No more. The time has come to FIGHT! Ice with FIRE! Dishonor with the most brutish force possessed by a man! The whole of GW shall witness mine might, and the whole of GW shall tremble at mine wrath!” Strong words spoken to an unseen adversary. Yet the message is clear. He places the fist to his heart and bows his head before smiling and striding into the night. He walks along illumined streets, drawing odd looks from the few passersby he comes to. He pays them no mind as he continues his walk. As he goes, the neighborhood becomes less nice, until finally, he comes to what can only be described as “the bad part of town”. He smiles and eagerly walks into the darkness that is this messier side of town. Small gangs of young men stand here and there, each of them eying the Lord King’After all, he does look like he has money; yet he is one huge brute. Nobody makes advances towards him’These weak fools only attack when they know they can win. Even in numbers they realize that the huge blonde haired man could tear them limb from limb. And finally, music to Pendragon’s ears? “GET AWAY FROM ME YOU BASTARDS!!!” comes a scream from the distance, followed by a horrified wail. Pendragon’s eyes light up, and he begins to run. He rounds a corner and comes upon four street thugs attempting god knows what to a young woman. A Toyota Camry with a flat tire seems to belong to her, and would explain what she is doing out and about in this unsavory neighborhood. One wears a red bandana on his head, one on his arm, one on his leg and the other about his throat, like he is some kind of outlaw. “Aww’Don’t be mean, mama!” Exclaims bandana head. He smiles and pushes her to his buddy, the Outlaw. The outlaw smiles and grabs a breast? “GET OFFA ME YOU ASSHOLE!!” Pendragon shakes his head and walks straight into the scene?“Now lads’There must be some way to end all this before someone gets hurt! After all, thee lads are so strong, and she is but a wench! Surely thy wish is not to inflict harm upon her person?” “What the FUCK is this” What kinda fuckin FREAK are YOU?” The outlaw seems to be the voice of this “intelligent” quartet? “Now, now’Who I am is none of thy concern. The pain that will befall you should you choose unwisely in this matter should be all that occupies thy mind at this time.” The four men laugh, falling over one another in their mirth. The woman seizes her chance and races over to Pendragon, burying her face into his massive chest. He puts one arm around her and casts his gaze menacingly over the bandana quartet’The laughing stops? “Look here, fool! She was ours, and we are gonna have our fun! Ain’t a damned thing you can do about it! You fuckin fairy!” At last, bandana leg speaks up and the four men advance on Pendragon and the woman. Each of them pull a knife, and Pendragon shoos the woman in the direction behind him. He squares his shoulders and stands relaxed as the men advance on him? “Oooh yeah, boy! We gonna cutchoo! We gonna cutchoo SOOOOOOO fuckin bad!” Bandana head exclaims as he slashes at Pendragon. Blindingly fast, Pendragon’s hand shoots out and grabs the man’s wrist. A quick turn and bones can be heard crunching. With a scream, the man lets the knife go and grabs for his wrist. Pendragon keeps his grip and catches the knife with his free hand, and in one fluid motion, throws it so it points hilt out of bandana leg’s thigh. The second thug goes down with a scream, and now only two remain. Pendragon releases the wrist and gives the thug a kick. He then stands to his full height and faces the two remaining men who look just a little bit unsure of themselves? “Thee lads should have chosen a bit more wisely! Thou hast granted me that which I most wished! A fight from dishonorable opponents! What other way to hone the skills necessary to devastate mine enemies?”” The last, Pendragon exclaims in almost a mocking sort of tone. “Come on, bro! We still got him two to one!” Exclaims the outlaw, and the two men rush in. Outlaw swings his blade, and Pendragon ducks, driving a fist into his sternum. He flies backwards as Pendragon sidesteps to avoid bandana arms swipe, and reaches up, grabbing the poor fellow’s scrotum with his left hand? “Two on one, eh” How about’FIVE ON TWO!” Pendragon laughs as he twists, really applying the pressure. In his pain, the man swings again, and Pendragon drops the bag, catches his arm, and bends it in a way it wasn’t meant to bend. Again, bones snap and the man drops to the ground, screaming, his arm broken in three places. The Outlaw shakes out the cobwebs and stands on wobbly legs. Pendragon advances on him and grabs him by the throat. The knife drops to the ground, and Pendragon lifts the man into the air by his throat? ??Tis a sad day indeed when a young woman cannot even fix a flat tire’Thou should never have set upon the lady, my lad’I gave thee the choice, and thou hast chosen’Poorly. Were I not a man of honor, I would crush thy neck and leave thee for dead. As it stands, I am a better man than thou art, and so thee can live. I have things to do?” He tosses the outlaw who lands in a heap and doesn’t move. With a nod to the young woman, he strides over to the car and looks into the open trunk. He reaches in and pulls out a jack and a tire. With a smile and a gleam in his eye, he sets to work on the tire’and on becoming the greatest force GW had ever seen?
