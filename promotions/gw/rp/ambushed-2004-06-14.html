Date: #2 Evo 6/14/04 The city flew by in fast forward. The music pounded through the system in Train's black Navigator as it cut a path down the Dallas/Ft. Worth expressway. He needed it loud. The aggressive beat of 'You Don't Want Drama' rattled the entire truck as 8 Ball and MJG spoke coldly to any potential opposition. The music helped to keep his synapsis popping as he drove. Otherwise the steady beat of the drizzle falling from the sky, along with the intermitent sliding of the windsheild wipers across the glass in front of his eyes might have been soothing enough pattern to lul him to sleep even as the luxury SUV did 75 down the road towards his Dallas home. Train was tired...drained. It had been a long flight back to Texas after his informative trip home to Chicago. The weight of the jet lag sat heavily on his brain weighing it down like a bar of lead. But it wasn't just the flight across the hundreds of miles between his old home and new that had done the damage. It was an acute case of information overload. His eyes and ears both strained as they spent most of the flight trying to keep up with the explosion of windows on his laptop. Everyone filled to the brim with incident reports, clips of grainy security tape, black and white surveillance photos and long recordings of wiretaps, all flowing like water from the disc his federal informant had passed to him for a hefty price. And all of it a testament to one man. Maverick Reno. It'd be a stretch to say that any of what he'd seen surprised Train. This is a man that, rumor in the back had it, was directly responsible for the death of two GW superstars as it was. And fact it is it was only half a rumor. Everyone knew for a fact what Reno did to Akkodo Rokku. Everyone with eyes and a brain watched it live, in living color. Crushing a man's throat with a bat. That was some cold blooded shit. No if's, and's or but's. But more than cold blooded...it was blatant. Now all of the sudden the only highlight reel they have to play for Jushin Nakazura is the one where his ass is still breathing. That's the rumor part...but Maverick Reno's name is all up in the mix again when cats talk about what killed 'Bright Soul' or 'Dark Soul' or whatever the fuck he called himself. When Train drummed up business for funeral directors and medical examiners he at least had the good sense to cover his tracks. Wear gloves, avoid witnesses, dump the weapon, dispose of the body...this was basic shit. Reno? He must've been on some advanced type shit cuz he didn't bother with any of that. What's more... The motherfucker wasn't in jail. Yeah...this cat had some balls. But really, there wasn't anything advanced about what he was doing. It was still basic. If you're gonna do stupid shit, have folks that can make it go away. Reno obviously had some backers. This cat was protected from up on high by the angel of darkness to be able to do what he did for the world to see and walk away a free man. If Train hadn't been so thoroughly focused on handing him his punk ass on a silver platter he could've half way respected that shit. Oh, but if he thought Train was gonna be the next victim he had the game twisted. Respect be damned, when it came time to step between the ropes the only thing Train had to show Reno was the boot he'd be getting broke off in his monkey ass. Fact is...it didn't even have to be between the ropes. There were two ways this could go. Either Reno, ever the arrogant bastard, could keep this thing confined to the ring...go gentlemen and champions with it. Duke it out mano-e-mano... Or he could do what he seems to do best. He could try to take him out before it got to the ring. Well... There were no garuantees, but Train pretty much knew he was safe. Reno wanted the Platinum Title back in TUE. You can snatch titles outside the ring, but you can't win them. And that's what Reno really wanted. To show the world just how good he was...just how much better he was. He probably wasnt' going to come after Train in the street. He wanted to world to see him win. And, being the pompous ass he was, no doubt he just knew the common thug he percieved Train to be couldn't stand up to a true technician he percieved himself to be. This was about defending TUE's honor...showing up Train. Maverick Reno hadn't said a word, but Train's blood began to boil. 'Ok muthafucka....' Train mumbled to himself. With men like Maverick Reno and his Upper Echelon in the picture you will need the proper orginization not only to survive, but to rid yourself of him once and for all. I simply wish to give you the proper resources to do so.' 'Proper resources?' 'I have $150, 000 in an escrow account, waiting to be transfered to your account the moment you pin Maverick Reno. With this you will have the funds you need to begin creating your own organization. With the power to combat the evil that you face now.' '......... hahahahaha....... I would have done it for half that. But don't worry Mr. Nakamura. Make sure you have your tv tuned to Evolution this Monday. Trust me... You'll get your money's worth.' Train pushes the power button and tosses the phone back to Yellow, who flinches as he draws back, catching the phone at his chest. Train simply turns and stalks off to his truck. They might have tried to pulls something... But he knew they wouldn't. The simply watch as he climbed back into the truck and put it in reverse. He tore out past the Jag and cut the wheel to the right, screaching to a halt right behind them. The driver's side window slides down and Train leans out to offer some words of encouragement.... 'Yall drive safe now.' Train says with a smirk. The music begins to pump through the Navigator again as it pulls around the warehouse, disappear into the night. Green and Yellow can only lower their heads in humiliation as the scene fades to black.
