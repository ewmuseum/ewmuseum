The Return Home (Part 6)

6 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 6 The sun began to fall behind the thick cover of the clouds. It’s purple and orange rays began to paint the evening twilight, a beautiful portrait of light and color. Ichiro looked up as the sun fell, he smiled and walked back to his uncle’s black SUV. The thoughts of the violence that would soon ensue ran through his mind. When he arrived back his uncle was making some last minute adjustments to his pistols. He was about to double check on the plastic explosives that he had made. Ichiro smiled and nodded to his uncle as he passed, he received a similar response from his uncle. Ichiro made his way past the vehicle and made his way to the small, metal ladder at the side of the building. Ichiro made his way up the ladder, he made sure to be quiet as he ascended. He reached the top and peeked over the top of the cement ledge. Ichiro quickly hopped over and crouched down walking towards his brother as he went. Jushin was lying belly down on the ground, still staring through the long scope of his .24 caliber sniper rifle. Ichiro patted Jushin on the shoulder, Jushin’s head rose from the gun and he smiled. The two brothers looked at each other for a few seconds before Ichiro finally broke the silence. “How does it look?” “Well brother, to tell you the truth it doesn’t look like this is going to be all that difficult. They’ve got a rotating patrol that circles the building, every hour the patrol switches to two new guys. Add to that they have one guy come on the roof every once in a while, I haven’t pegged down an interval yet, but I’m guessing about once every three hours. Then finally there are two to three guys at the front door at all times, standing guard.” “Alright, you got a good enough vantage point up here to clear them all out?” “Well, not from here, but I figure I can still move over to the ledge, provided I take out the rooftop sniper first, I should be able to go unnoticed long enough.” “How long do you need to take them out?” “Ummmm, it’s tough to say Ichiro. Could be anywhere from two to three minutes, four or five at most.” “Alright, get ready then, get into position and get ready to start when I give you the signal.” “Okay.” Ichiro crouched low once again and began to make his way towards the back of the roof. He duck walked towards the ladder, but did not make it very far. He felt Jushin’s hand grab hold of his arm tightly. He turned around to look back at his little brother. “Ichiro, I’m sorry about what happened in GW. I was wrong about the Echelon and I’m sorry.” “It’s alright Jushin, just worry about getting this done.” “Ichiro, honestly, just listen to me. You’re supposed to be the leader, I was wrong to think that you would take us in the wrong direction. You’re my older brother, from the time I was first born, you have always been there for me, to defend me and to guide me. And now with dad gone, you’re all that I have. I’m sorry to have doubted you my brother.” Ichiro could not reply, his tongue was swollen in his throat. He had no grasp for words or grammar right now. He merely walked back towards his brother, he wrapped his hand around the back of his head, and pulled his head close to his own. The top of their heads touched one another. They embraced each other for a few seconds before breaking and going their own separate ways. The lights were completely gone from the sky. The only light came from the brightness of the full moon, partially covered by the clouds. As well as the light that gleamed through the glass window of the complex’s observation room. The lights shone brightly down onto where the guards were standing guard near the front door of the complex. This was a huge mistake as it was giving Jushin an easy sight to his targets. Ichiro was sitting with his two pistols in his hands. He was ready to fire, no matter what happened, he waited leaning against the black SUV. That was when he heard the whisper come from the roof of the building. “I have a visual on the rooftop sniper and the patrol is behind the building they should be out soon. What do ya think Ichiro?” “Take your shot, Jushin. Take it!” Ichiro began to move closer to the front of the building. As soon as Jushin had downed all of his targets he would be down quickly. Ichiro and his uncle Gohei began to creep closer to the front. They heard the first shot go off, to the guards at the door it would sound like a distant whistle, but to the guard who it hit, it would sound like a bullet tearing through his entire body at lightning speed. Ichiro watched as the guard fell to his knees, in his mind he could hear Jushin reloading the cartridge and sound of the empty shell falling to the ground. Ichiro watched on as his brother continued his task. Ichiro watched carefully as the two patrolmen came out from behind the building. This would be a difficult shot, as he needed to down both of them without making a commotion. He heard the first shot and before the second man even knew his partner was dead, he too was lying in a pool of his own blood. There was only the guards at the door left. Ichiro imagined that he was atop the roof with Jushin. It always calmed him down to see his brother work the magic of his sniper rifle. He imagined the click of the bullets going into the chamber and the satisfaction of a well fired shot that would within milliseconds down his target. Ichiro opened his eyes no more then ten seconds later to see that all three of the guards at the door were lying dead. Ichiro smiled, as he heard his brother pick up the sniper rifle and running towards the ladder. They had the element of surprise but they had to move quickly. Ichiro and Gohei waited anxiously for the arrival of the youngest of the team. Jushin slid quickly down the ladder with his sniper rifle carefully in tow. He felt the tips of his toes touch the ground and he quickly let go of the ladder. He saw that Ichiro had left the back door to the SUV open, Jushin quickly ran over and put the sniper rifle carefully in the backseat. He looked into the back and saw all of the plastic explosives rigged up in the trunk. Jushin smiled and hurried to meet up with the other two. Jushin was already grabbing for his custom pistols as he came running around the corner. His dark business suit camouflaging him with the shadows. He arrived and was met with two nods, he nodded in return and without speaking a word all three men knew that it was time. The three men began to run at full speed, they made a sprint towards the front door of the complex. This mission was nearly suicidal, the slightest overlook or misstep could cost them their lives. But, that thought did not cross any of their minds. For Gohei, this was a chance, an opportunity. A chance to prove to himself and to Tokyo that he still had the strength to hang with the Nakazura brothers. As well as a great chance at power in the Tokyo underground crime syndicate. To the Souls of the Black Lotus the future was not on their mind, nor was the past. For the Souls of the Black Lotus, this was about retribution. The chance to clean their families name and to taste the sweet juices of freedom and vengeance. Their father’s wrath empowered them, the Nakazura name would never be tarnished again.
