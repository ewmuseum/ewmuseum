I release the grip and help him to his feet. “Sorry about that, doc. You startled me,” I quickly apologize. I help him straighten out his white lab coat when the world starts to spin. My feet have trouble maintaining their grip on the floor. Leaning against Vic’s bed, I try to regain my balance. “I was afraid of this Mr. Zero,” the doctor began. “The swelling of your eye and the minor swelling in your brain has not gone down yet and it is effecting your balance and the ability to move around.” I close my eyes and regain my composure. “So, what do we do?” “We patch it.” ***** “This thing is fucking with my peripheral vision.” “Well, its either this or get woozy every twenty or so minutes.” Fucking doctors. I looked in the mirror in the examination room, almost taken aback at what I saw. My hair, normally kept quite neat was now disheveled. My face, usually clean cut, sported a nice seven or eight o’clock shadow. And now, covering my left eye was a giant black circle, with a thick black strap going around my head, keeping it in place. “This fucking this is gonna have to fucking go.” “Your choice. Personally, I’d rather where the damn thing than get sick,” the doctor replied. “But, like I said, its your call.” Fucking doctors. If I was gonna have to wear this goddamn thing, some adjustments were gonna have to be made. “Are we done here?” I asked in an annoyed sigh. “Yes, we’re done.” ****** I walked back into Victoria’s room, still getting use to not being able to see anything coming up from the left side of my body. The door pushed in easily to the room. Standing over her, with his back to me, was a man in a long black trench coat. Reflex. My right hand jammed in the small space between my body and the black overcoat that I wore, snapping the glock out of its holster, thumbing the safety. I held a steady bead on the center of the man’s back. Every single nerve in my body begged me to squeeze the trigger the last millimeter but I didn’t. Slowly, the man raised his arms up from the side of his body into the air, letting me know that he was unarmed. Slowly, slower than he raised his arms, he began to turn. And I saw who it was. “Hello, Maverick.” “John,” he nodded. “Good to see you my friend.” “Likewise. What can I do for you?” I asked. “First, do you mind putting that thing away?” he motioned with one of his still raised hands towards my gun. “Sorry. Nerves are about frayed.” I smoothly slid the gun back into its holster. “It’s okay. I saw what happened Monday night. I thought it best to come to see how you were holding up.” “I’m fine. I’ve pretty much been here since Terrelle came to visit her. Fucking prick.” “You look tired, John.” “I told you, I’m fine.” “Let me help you.” “Help” How?” “Let me move her somewhere, John. Somewhere out of the way. Somewhere where you can still watch over her. Somewhere safe.” “Where?” “The tower.” Maverick replied. I cocked my head to one side, starring at Reno’s eyes behind his dark glasses. “The tower doesn’t have the facility to keep her, Mav. And even if we started today, it wouldn’t be ready till next week.” “I know. That’s why I had them start on it last week. It’s ready for her today.” I looked at my friend. One of my two friends. I felt my shoulders slump as if a great weight had been lifted. “I don’t know what to say, Mav.” “Say nothing. Let’s just get her safe.” ***** “What the hell is going on in here”!”” the doctor yelled as he re-entered Victoria’s room. Fucking doctors. The move had been going swiftly, efficiently, and neatly. Reno called in the TUE private medical staff, and the white coats had arrived less than a minute later. The medical staff didn’t speak to me nor Reno, making their own private gestures and muttering off medical terminology. I caught most of it, but they spoke in low tones, and I am still pretty off my game for the moment. “What does it look like, doc?” I asked sarcastically over my shoulder. “We are checking Victoria out of this hospital.” “The hell you are,” he practically shouted at me. Slowly, I turned and faced the doctor. I looked over to my left and Reno was at my side. Apparently, the doctor’s tone had struck a nerve with Mav. “I’m afraid we are, good doctor,” Maverick spoke before I could respond. “With full support of the management team of this facility, I’m afraid. So, I will kindly ask you to get out of our way.” The doctor raised a finger and jabbed it into Maverick’s chest. Wrong move. “Fuck you,” he spoke to Reno. Two in a row. The doctor swung his finger around and pointed it into my face. “And fuck you too, Mr. Zero.” Three wrong moves in a row. “I don’t know who the fuck you think you are, but Ms. Kim is still my goddamn patient. I will decide what is and isn’t done with her. You will have to move her over my dead goddamn body.” You had to give it to him, the little prick had a little spunk in him yet. The words escaped Maverick’s mouth in a bare whisper. “As you wish.” It happened in almost super slow motion. Both of us, me and Reno, flipping our outer coats to the side, revealing our pieces. Our respective right hands going for the grips of the guns, snatching them up, safeties clicking off. The doctor pissing the front of his pants. “You see, doc, this move is going to happen one way or another. It’ll be either us taking Victoria with us while you standing in a puddle of your own piss, or you’ll be lying in a pool of your own blood and piss. It’s your call. Doesn’t make a bit of fucking difference to me,” Reno spoke to the doctor while looking over the sights of his glock. The doctor swallowed hard. And at that exact moment, before we reached the complete climax of the situation, Reno and I ready to extinguish the life of this poor pathetic man, in walks Deac, in one a plate of hot wings, the other holding a wing to his mouth, nibbling away at the spicy flesh. “Damn, these hot wings are the shit. You need to try this shit, Z. The kung-fu of these wings is very fucking strong. And from a fuckin hospital, if that don’t beat fuckin all.” He stopped in the doorway when he noticed that we had a doctor pinned down with our guns. “So” What will it be, doc?” I asked He swallowed again, his eyes intent on the two guns pointing at him. “Get out.” “And your state” Will this still be over your dead body?” Maverick asked. “No. I’ll be co-operative.” “Good,” Reno and I said in unison. “Leave.” “Y’all just hold the fuck on for one goddamn second,” Deac spoke, sitting the wing back on the plate, licking his fingers clean of any excess spice, blocking the doctor’s path. D removed the doctor’s wallet, emptying the cash out of it. Deac then removed the doctor’s rolex from his wrist, putting it in his own pocket. D started to walk away but paused, noticing a fancy pen sitting out of the doctor’s white lab coat’s breast pocket. D quickly snatched that as well. Picking the wings back up, D took his last wing up and motioned to the doctor with the wing to go. The doctor turned and left the room. “I have to admit, that was rather amusing,” Reno said. “Yeah, it was so funny I almost pissed myself,” Deacon added. He paused in mid-chew, sniffled the air, and checked the front of his pants. “Whew, almost scared myself for a fucking second there.” We all laughed. Deacon stopped, his face growing as serious as it could possibly get. “You alabaster motherfucka's and your guns. You really need to find a better method of dealing with your emotional angst. Perhaps yoga?' Reno and I looked at each other briefly before returning duel blank stares to Deacon. “Me, personally, I prefer a good wing.” ***** I sat in the room that Maverick had prepared for Victoria. She lay next to me, somewhere between life and death. Floating. Hovering. There, but not. I cradled my head in my hands, my elbow resting on my knees. What to do? What to do? “John?” “Come in, Maverick.” I heard his feet pad softly across the tile and a chair being dragged over next to where I sat. Reno slowly lowered himself into the chair. “It was inevitable, Mav.” “What was?” I removed my right hand from my head and leg and gestured to Victoria. “This. Anytime I let someone in. This. This is what fucking happens!” I stood up, picking the chair up, hurling it across the room over Maverick’s head. He instinctively ducked down to avoid the flying object. “What’s on your mind John?” “Have you ever loved someone, Maverick” And I mean really love someone? Loved them so much that you had to kill them?” “It's a common saying, John. If you love someone, you should let them go. If they come back to you, then they are yours to keep. But if they don't, then they never were. This is a delicate situation, John. Her life and her love are one thing while yours are another. But do you really think that she would wish to submit” Do you believe that she would lie there and die if she had the choice in the matter?” “No. Vic was’is” a fighter. She’s been fighting all of her life. She is not one to give up. But’I don’t want her to suffer. I don’t want her to suffer because of my choices, my problems, my conflicts. I don’t want my demons to hunt her as well.” “Then your choice is not whether she should die, it is to love or not to love. And even then, it is both your and her decision to make, John.” I slowly nodded. I was about to add more when Deacon walked in. “What the fuck are you two bitches up to?” “Just having a heart to heart with John, D,” Mav spoke, not removing his gaze from mine. “Ah. I see. So should I go get the guitar so we can sing fucking Koombaya and holding fucking hands?” I broke Reno’s gaze to look at Deac. “Do you have any other suggestions?” “Hell yeah. I say we go tighten their bitch ass’s up and make them feel like they’ve just been gang-raped by a bunch of perverse elephants.” I could feel it moving through my system. It came slowly at first; almost working it’s way down from my toes all the way through my body and to my mouth. The ends of my mouth slowly curling up into a small smile.
