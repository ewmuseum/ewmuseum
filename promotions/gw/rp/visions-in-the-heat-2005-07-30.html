Visions in the Heat

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/30/05 July 28th, 2005 A man of his word, that is what Gray is. As he told her he would do, he reported her dream to his superiors. No sooner did he do this but she got the notice loud and clear with the delivery of a old looking scroll tube sealed with wax. Telling her that she is to report any more “dreams” to Gray. Not even her grandfather would be able to keep her from that task. So again, she sat alone in her room, over her bed hung a dream catcher; elaborate for it's kind, but beautifully made with black with silver beads making it look like stars shining as the light hit it. The use of it: the good dreams getting through the web made by the celestial spider, Unktomi, to the person sleeping under it, while the nightmares and bad dreams are caught in the web to become nourishment for the spider at a later time. The simple dreams she was use to were being replaced by the same dream over and over. One of her ancestor. As it seemed to her the closer she came to becoming of age, less then a week from now, the more intense her dreams seem to be getting. Some would ask how a legend would effect someone, but in all legends there is a touch of reality, in one form or another. Her teaching at the school were more then wrestling, as it was a school they did teach her more. That is where she met Gray, in class. He was fresh back to the school, after the loss of his wife and child. In his heart break he turned to the one thing he knew best: Teaching the art of wrestling and hand to hand combat. Hardest on a young man named Josh, he pushed all the students to their limits. But as Danielle would find out, Josh was Gray's hot-headed half brother. Josh carried no love lost for his older brother and guardian, and Josh seemed to be at the school under great protest. While the rest of the students worked hard to stay, he slacked off and only seemed to work hard in one area: Self Defense, which happen to be his brother's class. It was to seem that Josh had something to prove to him. And would go through great means to do such. The day Danielle bested him in the ring, you could of sworn someone had punched Josh in the gut. He refused help from anyone. But he knew that he was bested by himself not anyone else. What made that day worse for him was that he was not only bested in front of his older brother and that whole class of students, but also by the headmaster himself. From that day forward things between Dani and Josh had always seemed rough at the best of times. But that was the least of Dani's worries right now. The people back at the school had ordered her to keep Gray up to date, and she would. She wanted to know what was happening to her just as much as anyone else did, if not more. In but a few days she would be 21, the age of majority. She'd be considered an adult and would be able to do many things, including finally 'enjoy' the likes of Las Vegas, her new home, without feeling like she needed a kiddie menu or supervision. On top of all of that, she was working out of a slump now. After her first few loses, last week she took her team in the Master of the Mat tourney to victory. The Sirius Star flew as high as she could that night. The camera flashes, the crowd behind her. The adrenaline push was a rush. The same rush she gets every time she steps into that ring, but for some reason it was different. This week it was more so, the lasting effects. But she knew she had to keep focus. Her winning this meant she would be facing her partner this week... or so she thought. The now familiar sound of the feather Pen scratching the paper of her leather bound Journal. The thick heavy hand made pages, gave the book a aged look. Danielle, in her ever familiar form was laying on her stomach on a pallet on the floor in front of the TV in the living room area of her suite. The TV looked to be on Food TV, with some old replay version of the Iron Chef playing. She was, as usual for her when not expecting much, was dressed for comfort, which to her was not much; a tight baby doll tank top and black boy shorts, as she carried out her almost impulsive/compulsive event for every day... recording everything in her books. Journal: I did it, well we did it.. But I am the one that got the pin. Actually I'm the one who did most of the damn work. Now I go to the next level of this Master of the Mat tourney, facing my partner of this week. Sounds like fun doesn't it? While Gray and Josh stand and watch on the side lines waiting for their first match in Global, still. I have to keep my feet firmly on the ground. A win is nice but cant let it go to my head, Zenith isn't a slouch. He is the trainer of the woman that beat me soundly last week two shows ago. There is no doubting how good he is, he's a former multiple time champion here. Just knowing I have to be ready for anything, tagging with him gave me an idea what He could do to me in the ring. But for now I wait for the official word from Kris, as he has been faxing the matches from headquarters to Gray. Stupid Computers are a waste of time if you ask me. But Josh loves his laptop and Gray seems to have a knack for them also. I guess thats what happens when you grow up in the back yard of Microsoft. Me? I'd rather to stay with pen and paper more personal. But, grandfather sent me a birthday gift going to have to have Josh show me how to use it, sooner or later. She looks up at the box sitting open with a laptop computer sitting on the table next to it. Screen up looking at her, black as it's power isn't even on. The plastic sheet still stuck to the screen to protect it.. She shakes her head and goes back to writing. But before she can touch the pen to the paper again there is a knock on the door. That causes her to jump. ?Who is it?? She asks our loud. She knew it wasn't Kris, he'd just walk in. She knew it wasn't Gray or Josh, as they had a distinct knock they both used. A voice filled in from the other side of the door. “Sorry to disturb you Miss. Northsky, but the office just received this message for you via fax and they asked me to bring it to you,” the man implored to ensure he wasn't going to get into any trouble. Danielle jumped up grabbing her sweat pants that she kept near bye at most times, and pulling them on before rushing to the door and opening it. The sweat pants were more for modesty's sake after her first night on the road so many months ago, answering a hotel room door in just her lacy underthings lead to some very embarrassed room service people. Dani opened the door and then looked at the young man on the other side. He'd probably volunteered to run the message up to her on a whim to get to see her. He looked almost like he had stars in his eyes as it was. She took the piece of paper out of his hand, as he stood there and asked him, ?Thanks, I thought Mr. Brand was picking these things up?? “Sorry, but we were left with word that both Mr. Brands would be out for the day. Oh, they left you this message also,” he said as he pulled out another sheet of paper from his jacket inside pocket. She takes that also as the young man, while trying not to be obvious, looks her over. His eyes stop and linger at the low cut of her tank top, and at that point, Dani had decided his star search was over. She slams the door in his face with a quick and unmeaning, “Thank you.” She walks over and just drops down into the Russian blue upholstered love seat and then unfolded the papers. She then looks down at the paper and reads it. Master of the Mat Tourney, Round 2 Danielle Northsky VS Mike Biddle She then scanned down the paper and saw an extra bit hand written in above another listing from Kris. by the way, happy Birthday Josh. You and Gray finally have a match Absolutely Merciless v. The Brand Brothers ~Kris She noted the use of the hearts over the “i” in every instance, as well as a few other personal points that she knows Kris always uses when writing. It was legitimate. Dani shook her head and reads this over and over again. Not a reason why, but the match was changed... No explanation. while the words 'Card Subject to Change? come to mind, as they're on the bottom of every wrestling card by any company out there, it'd still be nice for her to know why Zenith was not booked against her. But why Biddle? Why him? Someone who she knows about, that's for sure. He's a former PTC Extreme Champion, as well as having titles in many other feds. She's not seen him do too much so far h ere, but she's not been around too long either. Biddle was an interesting choice, that's for sure. There was going to have to be a phone call made, that's for sure. She knew someone who knew Biddle better then he probably did. But first, time to get out of the room and go see something... Something she was being drawn to. She grabbed her backpack, tossing 4 bottles of water in it, changed out her sweats into a tight set of jean shorts, put on some hiking boots, and ran out the door. Stopping long enough at the front desk to leave a note for Josh and Gray to let them know where she was heading. Something told her in the back of her mind that this would be a good idea.. ~A little Later.... ~ ~Valley of Fire~ ~Paiute Reservation, North of Las Vegas~ We find Danielle in a place where the Red Sand Rock and Red ground would say that she is in the Valley of Fire. A place seeped in the rich native history. The Petroglyphs on the walls shows she is standing at Atlatl Rock if anyone has ever seen the tourist post cards of the are. The heavy black Patina1 covered walls riddled with ancient drawings the most notable one is the one where the rock gets it's name. The crude drawing of a man holding a long notched stick, that was used to add speed and distance to spears when thrown, the beginning of the bow and arrow. The deeply carved pictures show a time and place that the main worries are nothing like today. The life was simpler but more complex in other ways. Danielle slowly walked taking her time to look and read the signs placed out for the visitors of this wonderful place. She smiles at a little girl passing by as her mother stops and stares at Danielle. “You... you know you look a lot like that Northsky woman that is a female wrestler,” the woman states as a matter of fact notion. Danielle just smiles and nods her head. Dani answers her with, ?I get that a lot since I moved here. So do you watch wrestling a lot?? “No my husband watches I just catch a glimpse here and there of the show. Usually to busy to sit and watch it,” was the woman's response. Sweetly smiling at the little girl, Danielle looks up at the mother. “She looks like she can keep you on your toes,” Dani adds, trying to change the subject. The woman takes the bait and answers Dani's statement, ?She can but as all kids they are a handful at times and a complete joy at other times. You have any kids?? “Oh no...” Dani almost looses her cool, it's always been a tight subject with her. She saw some of the females in the sport already loose their careers due to getting knocked up due to one stupid night of passion. She quickly tries to recover her statement, “Not yet, I should say. Maybe someday when the time is right and I am married. It's a decision that I've not decided to make a commitment to yet. I'm still pretty young too.” “Sorry didn't mean to make it sound like...” The woman starts to get slightly defensive. “You didn't. It's just a touchy subject. I'm sorry. I've got a career to think about and everything else. My grandmother and the others in my family are always asking me on the phone when I'm going to settle down. They're still in the past where a woman is a baby making machine, not someone who had goals in life,” Dani filled her in. “Oh, I understand that. John wants another one. But I don't know if I'm ready yet,” the woman continues. Dani smiles and looks down at the little girl again. Her heart strings being pulled already, she decides to give in. She walks over and kneels down to her. “She's going to be really strong one day. She'll be sure to show them all that she can be right up there with the rest of the pack.” Before the woman could answer, a man approached them. ?Okay, time to go. Are you read....dy?? The man stops in the middle of the word, and clears his throat, staring at Danielle, who just smiles. The woman who takes her husband staring as checking out the breathtaking young lady standing before them in a annoyed voice answers him quickly, “Yeah, lets go. I think this young lady was enjoying her quiet time before we came along.” They turn and leave as they do Dani pulls a blanket out of her sack and spreads it out to sit on. It's time for a rest. She sets the blanket in the shade of one of the larger boulders, to protect her from the high desert sun. Pulling out a bottle of water she takes a few shallow drinks, and then pours some down her front to act as self efficient air conditioning in the 120 degree heat. In the shade of the rock Dani Sits quietly wondering about her match this week.. Biddle isn't a new name to her. She has seen the name come across other wires. That was something she needed to do once she got back from her little day off. She wondered where Josh and Gray went off to with out her. Feeling a little left out she was just a little mad that they didn't bother to tell her they were actually going some place. Then again she didn't mind a little time to herself. The last few weeks things have been coming to mind, the dream, josh,his birthday, her birthday, the Federation. Her mind raced to pick one subject. She tried to quiet herself, center herself and find peace. She was in the land of the Anasazi,The Fremont people, and now the Moapa Paiute, they deserved her giving them her full attention. This was not a day for business but a day for relaxing. Soon she found herself not awake, but not fully asleep either. As she sat there, leaning against the rock in the desert she dreamed ~The Dream~ Heartbroken, lonely,unhappy.. these are the feelings that this dream has brought. Great sadness like non-other. Laying on the cold ground looking up at a shooting star, the morning star. A woman dressed in white buck skin with a baby in her arms, crying, wailing, morning her loss. A loss of the heart. A loss of deep love. Alone on the open hill the woman sits and cries, while holding the baby close to herself. Yelling for him to take her with him. The answer is heard in the back of Danielle's mind, telling her no that it was to late she could never comes back again with him, this set her off again and she vowed never to love again. Dani can feel the pain of this woman as she stands there unable to move or say anything of comfort. Then Dani realizes the child is him, her ancestor and the woman his mother. Dani looks up and sees the star has a face and drops to her knees. It is him, the Morning Star himself, the man who came from the Wakan Tanka to the Earth to take a maiden as his bride. She watches him as he turns and leaves the woman and her young child. That child was to later become a man who was considered one of the greats of her kind. Supposedly he was immortal but he disappeared about the time of the stand at Little Big Horn, where his decendet Sitting Bull had his fight with the US Calvary. And many knew he was gone well before Wounded Knee, when women and children, as well as the men dancers, were massacred for gathering to perform the Ghost Dance that had been brought up by Fallen Star from the Hopi and Zuni to the south. She watches as Morning Star leaves his bride back on earth with his young offspring... and then the dream seem to fast forward it's self to a different time. A long pole erected with a large nest sitting on the top of it. Danielle had the sense of Dancing in circles, the voice not her own but of a man, singing. The dance goes on for what seems to be hours. Constantly dancing in circles, circles and more circles around the pole in the middle. She can't seem to stop and listens to the chanting continues. Suddenly, sharp pains of her skin being pierced by skewers near her breasts, and the feel of the skin tearing as she realizes that this body is actually strung up to the pole by ropes. The skin tears as the weight of the body against the ropes finally weakens the spots of the piercings, causing her to collapse from hunger, thirst, and pain Her mind starts to reel and spin...... a voice can be heard....... ?Ma'am? You okay? Miss Northsky?? Dani's eyes open standing next to her but looking down at her is a man in a uniform. As her eyes focus on him she sees he is a ranger for the Valley of fire, one of the local Paiute. She stutters, barely able to make a few coherent words as her mind starts to grab hold of reality again. It's not enough, as the next time she's coming to again, she has the sense of laying on a bed and the inertia of moving in a vehicle at the same time. She's in an ambulance. She looks at her arm the IV tells the tale she must have been in the heat longer then she thought, she pushes herself to sit up but her body feels like it's in a Gravitron Carnival ride being spun and forced back to into the bed she gives in and just allows her body to stay put. Giving a forced smile to the man standing over her. She feebly tries to talk to him, “I'm just fine, I must of dozed off or something.” The paramedic just hands her a bottle of what looks to be a Gatorade like drink, ?Here, drink this slowly. If you drink to fast you might make yourself sick. She takes the bottle and manages to roll slightly so she doesn't choke as she drinks. She sips it slowly her body seem to reel at the feeling of the cool liquid. Her hands began to shake. “I said I was fine, was just enjoying the shade. Must of dozed off for an a little bit. I just need to go back and get my truck and I'll head back to my room.” The ranger, sitting next to the paramedic, interjects, ?Actually ma'am, it's closing time. When your friends couldn't reach you on your phone they knew you had headed here. They called the ranger station. I remembered seeing your truck heading down the road to the trail head to Atlatl Rock, so I headed out and saw your truck was still there. That only means one thing, you were still out there. It's after 6 P.M. Which means you were out there for quite a few hours. But don't worry the meda-Vac is coming to take you to sunrise for observation you have to be dehydrated by now. The ambulance will be taking us as far as the edge of the park, we needed to get the first aid started. If you hesitate in a case of possible heat shock or stroke, it can be fatal.? Dani tried to sit up. ?Heat stroke? What?? She tried to comprehend everything all at once, and it wasn't working. “Lay down, it'll be better for you. Your friends are going to meet you at the hospital, and your truck will be sent back the the Hotel for you. You lucked out that you had been sitting in the shade for most the time. Just so you know the temperature today hit about a hundred and twenty out there. It's not safe to go hiking by yourself in the high desert.” Dani again protested, “I told you I was fine, just a little tired...” She reaches for her chest her skin still burning from the dream,she looks down and there is no blood, no holes, no tears. But she knows what she had seen, what she had been a part of the Sun Dance. A painful ritual that was so scared that all the objects used would be left behind to allow nature to take it's course, not one would ever be used for private use again. But it was also a ritual that was reserved for a man. It was his coming of age ceremony, to show that he had the strength to fight for the tribe and hunt. Why would she have seen and appeared to have gone through that? The ambulance stopped, and the ranger opened the door to the back. He and the paramedic pulled the gurney with her on it out of the converted SUV for helping in the desert areas. The thumping of the propeller on the Medical Helicopter could be heard as it echoed off the stone walls of the Valley. From where she was sitting she could see the tow truck driving off with her car, she knew it would be to late to try to convince anyone she was okay enough to drive. The ranger and four other people are standing just outside the door waiting, but one man seems to be way out of place. Dressed in white buckskin breeches with no shirt, he bares the mark of the Sun-dance on his chest. His face kind, familiar to a point, her mind trying to figure out what she is seeing. Why now is he coming to her. She closes her eyes then reopens them and he is gone just leaving the men in the Emergency response uniforms standing there as the helicopter lands to take her to the hospital and take her into the reach of Gray Brand who no doubt will have some questions for her. But maybe some answers as well. If not then it is time to call grandfather, he might know what to do. ~End~ Word to know: 1Patina:This coating usually consists of iron and manganese dioxides mixed with other things such as the by products lichens. This is also called a 'desert varnish' . With a patina or varnish the rock surfaces often appear shiny or wet. It can also have a burnt look to it.
