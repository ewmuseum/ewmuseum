Rp for Evo Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #11 Date: 3/21/03 Location: Toronta With war going on over in the middle east, Venom is overwhelmed by his so called lost to “TEG” cause of Cobra using a chair and costing Venom to get DQ, is Venom upset with Cobra, big time, but he will take out his angry on The English Gent at Evolution in a Cage match for the platinum title, Venom doesn’t need to worry about Cobra coming down to the ring to give him a hand. Know on with the story, Venom is at home, has the rain is pouring down, he is flipping threw channels, his luck he only gets four stations and all of them are carrying the news on the War over in the middle east, so he decides to leave and head over to the National Midnight Star. 30 minutes later…Venom arrives at the National Midnight Star, and notices Cobra at the bar talking to a sexy girl, Venom walks up to them both and chats a little. Venom: “Hey, Cobra it’s me Venom what are you doing here, why haven’t you return any of my calls?” Venom acting kind of funny here to get the girl to leave. And it works. Cobra is pissed at him for acting stupid. Cobra: “What the hell was that, man I was about to get with her tonight for a little fun and you show up acting gay, what the hell.” Venom: “Pay back is a bitch, that is what you get for screwing me the platinum title, I had that match won, and your stupid ass, came down to the ring and hit The English Ass with a chair, which the Ref saw and DQ me, and now I’m in a cage match, with that goof.” Cobra: “Hey man I’m sorry, I didn’t know that the ref would turn around and see me hit him.” Venom: “Sorry, my ass, I don’t won’t you to be at this Evolution, I’m going solo for one night, I don’t need your help, I’ve in this business for a long time, to where I don’t need a ones help. Not even from my partner.” Cobra: “Ok, I won’t be at the show this week; I will chill out at my mother’s house.” Venom: “Good, but if for any reason you do decide to show up in interfere in the match in anyway like climbing in the cage and costing me the match, I will be force to knock your ass down, you hear me?” Cobra: “I hear you.” Venom: “Good, now if you excuse, I have to look for someone.” Venom leaves The National Midnight Star after getting in Cobra’s face, now the Snakepit can’t be having any troubles they been friends for a long time, or is Venom’s ego getting the best of him. Is Venom starting to think he is a high class sob, who knows, back to the story here folks? Venom, is out looking for a certain someone who could it be? Hell, if I know let’s see what Venom is up to. Venom: “Where the hell can she be, I’ve been driving for minutes now.” While Venom is looking for some girl, he steps on the brakes and turns around at a local Wendy’s as he notice a red Caviler, could this be a girl he was talking? He pulls into a gas station where the car is parked at and waits, and a girl walks out of the gas station and yes, it was the girl he was looking by the name of Kim. Venom: “Hey, KIM” Venom shouts out loud for Kim. Kim: “Not you again, did I tell you not to long ago you weren’t in my league?” Venom: “Yes, you did but I believe you my dear were wrong, you see I know you noticed me and I noticed you that day at the mall. I would like to give you this ticked to see me in action at Evolution as I will be in a cage match for a title.” Kim: “Really, you don’t look like the type that should have any kind of title.” Venom: “Really, well for your information I happen to be the Iron Fist Champion of GW.” Kim: “Ok, should I be jumping for joy right now?” Venom: “Woman, you don’t know what you are missing; any girl would kill to be with a guy like me.” Kim: “Well, I’m not like all girl’s maybe a guy to me, needs to be more polite, like a Englishmen type guy.” Venom: “Englishmen, lady I whooped a Englishmen ass a week ago and I’m bout to whoop the same one again, in a cage match for the platinum title.” Kim: “I know who you are talking about Lawrence Stanley.” Venom: “That would be the dork. He is going to feel the Venom strike.” Kim: “Well Venom, I would love to stand here and chat with you, but that would be wasting my time here, I got more important things to do.” Venom: “Whatever lady.” Kim drives away, and Venom just stays parked for a while to watch the sunset, as the scene fades away..
