Reflections of the past: Part II

27 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 27th, 2004. Sometimes there are memories, which we shove into the vast recesses of our minds because we simply don't want to bring these memories to light. We don't want to acknowledge these thoughts due to the pain that they bring us, therefore we hide them, and we tuck them away, praying that they'll never resurface from the repression we have forced them into. But alas, such memories do remain, such memories do resurface and when they do so they loom ominously seeming even more disturbing than ever before. Such is my story. It all started innocently enough when my new 'apprentice' beseeched how I started out in the Wrestling business, or rather…why. Immediately memories of the past sprang up, memories that incensed me, memories that threw my entire system into a rage. Memories of hate…memories of pain…memories that had nearly shattered the fragile World I had built for myself. Yet now it seems those memories refuse to leave me alone. Where there was once peace there is now turmoil. Where there was once silence now there is the screams of anguish emitting from my soul. And today…such is the case, my mind has seemingly abandoned my body as it drift's aimlessly to thoughts of days gone by. ---- July 9th, 1999. The midnight hour was approaching fast and the later it got the slower I became. I had started off by running through my front door and not looking back, that run hadn't lasted quite an hour…before it became a jog…then it finally devolved into a walk and now a tired stagger. The weight of the backpack thrown carelessly over my shoulder seemed insignificant compared to the heavy burden of stress that rested there as well. My white Nike T-shirt was soaked with sweat despite the fact that the sun had dropped over the horizon nearly four hours ago. My black gym shorts were dusty from the swirling dust blown onto me from cars that aimlessly whipped by. My silver Nike trainers now housed a pair of sore and blistered feet, and my messy black hair was plastered to my forehead by a thick layer of sweat that seemed to coat my entire body. My breath came in shallow, painful gasps, as the Oxygen seemed to escape my lungs. Such was to be expected; after all I was in Colorado now…even though the sign bearing mention of that was no more than a mile behind me. My ears alertly picked up the hum of an approaching engine as I saw the headlights a split second later. My heart skipped a beat in hope that a ride would be offered and as the car approached me from behind I turned and thrust my thumb out…only to watch the car zoom by, showering me in a cloud of thick dust. I coughed painfully my lungs feeling as if a knife had been driven through them. As I coughed I felt my lips crack courtesy of the dry climate which I was in. Reaching up I wiped my mouth with the back of my right hand and as I did so I felt the dried blood peel away and my split lower lip begin to seep blood once again. Licking the swollen wound with my tongue I batted the blood away from it and swallowed, cringing at the thick iron taste of my own blood mixed with the dust of the Colorado countryside. That's when I heard it again…another car. False hope had proven to be the catalyst for a lot of disappointment today so it was with despondency that I greeted this car, thrusting forth my thumb once more only to see the car zip by yet again. 'Yeah, well screw you too…' I grumbled incoherently between chapped and bleeding lips. It all seemed far too strange…I had never pictured my journey to becoming a Professional Wrestler like this, in fact this was far from what I had pictured it. Yet I would play the hand that fate had dealt me, and perhaps if I held my cards right I would come out as a winner. In an ironic twist of fate I had began my day in high spirits only to be slapped and degraded by my father and chastised by my mother. In a fit of rage I dropped my father and stormed away never to be seen again by my parents…all of this because they didn't agree with a decision that I had made. It was my life after all and one day I would make something of it…I would show them. I would throw everything right back in their face. I would make them regret the way they had treated me; they would be the ones who ended up looking like fools…not I. I had my pride, and that was just one of a countless number of things that forced me to press on as I climbed up the steep hill on this Colorado road making my way closer and closer to Colorado Springs. At Colorado Springs I would meet a legendary Wrestler by the name of Tommy Rock and it was there I would train to become a Professional Wrestler, it was my destiny…no matter whom disliked it. My thought process was broken as I heard the hum of yet another engine approaching from the South. With a disgruntled mumble I turned my head and it was there my bluish gray eyes met the headlights of a quickly approaching white Chevy pick up. With a slight moment of hesitation I thrust out my thumb for what seemed like the millionth time today, thus far it had only garnered me three rides but I had to press on until I finally reached Colorado Springs. Much to my surprise the truck lurched to a halt right next to me and the window rolled down as a grizzled old farmer with a graybeard and baseball cap looked at me over his glasses. 'Hey kid…you need a ride?' The farmer inquired, and due to his attire of blue jean overalls and a white T-shirt it was easy to name his profession. 'Yes sir!' I jubilantly exclaimed almost a bit taken aback by this mans kind offer. 'Throw your bag in the back and hop on in, son.' The elderly farmer said pleasantly. I simply nodded in response as I dropped the backpack from my shoulder and tossed it into the bed of the pick up, rubbing my shoulder slightly from where the strap had been biting into my skin for the past few hours. With my bag taken care of I threw open the heavy door to the truck and dropped onto the gray interior inside. 'Thanks.' I softly said as I leaned my head back against the rest. 'Well you looked like you were plum tuckered out.' The farmer said in his peculiar manner, as he stomped on the accelerator. 'That I was.' I admitted as I closed my eyes wearily, happy to finally let them rest from their countless hours of being filled with dry Colorado dust. 'So what's a youngster like you doing out hitch hiking?' He questioned as I could feel his kind old eyes absorbing the exhausted sight that sat next to him. 'I'm headed toward Colorado Springs, sir.' I said as I answered his question politely, grateful for the ride. 'The big city.' He snorted with a bit of disdain in his voice showing me how much he cared for 'City living'. 'Yes sir.' I wearily responded, as I opened my eyes trying not to drift off to sleep. 'There's nothing good in the City, kid. It's full of drugs, booze, gangs and whores…it's nothing but trouble, just chock full of trouble.' He said with a strong level of disdain in his voice. 'I have some business to attend to.' I softly told him hoping to cure his angst toward the city of Colorado Springs. 'Business, eh? A job perhaps?' He asked in typical farmer fashion as he tried to get the scoop on everything. 'School, actually.' I responded my eyelids drooping wearily. 'College? Now that's respectable.' The old farmer said with a nod. 'Well, it's not exactly college…' I slowly stated. 'No? I though college in July was a little strange, what kind of school might you be looking into there, son?' He asked as he glanced over at me his eyes full of suspicion. 'It's a Wrestling school actually.' I told him, punctuating my sentence with a smile full of charm. 'Rasslin'? He asked with a laugh. 'You mean to tell me you want to be one of them…professional 'rassler's?' He said as he eyed over my five feet ten inches and one hundred and forty pounds of lean flesh. 'Yes sir, that's my intention.' I stated with a nod. His response next was something that seemed to be all too typical from those whom I informed of my dreams…he laughed. I had become aquatinted with such responses yet it unnerved me nonetheless. It still hurt when people laughed at my dreams. 'You’re a scrawny little cuss.' He said with a big grin spread across his face. 'Maybe so, but pound for pound I'm as tough as they come.' I said, quickly defending my chosen profession. 'Yeah, but pound for pound I think I've got sheep that weigh more than you do.' He snorted, adding a hurtful laugh to his sentence. I quickly doused my temperamental flame before it could consume me, taking a deep breath I slowly counted to ten inaudibly before responding. 'Well…just remember the name Josh, because one day I'll be a star.' I said with pride. He raised an eyebrow curiously as he looked over at me. 'Josh…a star in professional 'rasslin, wont that be the day?' He said with a doubtful laugh. Closing my eyes I tired to push the memories to the back of my mind. I knew I could become a star…one day the name Josh would be plastered on marquee's everywhere. I would go on to prove him wrong…I would prove them all wrong. ---- And as I look back on that memory…it seems that I have proven them all wrong. Josh…a star.
