Leaving the Hotel

1 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/1/03 The scene opens outside of a hotel door. The door is white, but is chipped in spots and the tan coloring of the wood beneath the paint can be seen. The numbers on the door are gold plated and indicated that this room is room number '316'. The door opens and the blond that was last seen in Ice's gym walks out of it. She is wearing a pair of black sunglasses, her black streaked blond hair is pulled back and up into a bun, and she is wearing a light blue gym suit with white stripes down the side. A small bell boy is carrying her bags for her as she walks past the camera and down the hallway. The camera follows her down the hallway as she enters an elevator. The elevator is silver, and everyone in the elevator has a reflection from it's mirror like presence. The elevator bell rings and the bell boy leads the blond out of the elevator and through the all glass front doors. As the woman exits the doors and enters the parking lot GW commentator Brandi Sights runs up and begins asking her question after question and shoving the microphone in her face. Brandi: Who are you? What is your relationship with Ice? Where is ICE? Why isn't he with you right now? What does Ice think about being igorned and overlooked by the stars here in the GW? Blond: Why don't you ask him yourself? Brandi gives the blond a confused look. She turns around to walk away and runs right into Ice, who is standing in a long black trench coat, his blond hair pulled back into a low pony tail, with his arms crossed in front of his chest. He is staring down at Brandi with a look of intensity on his face. Brandi begins to back peddle away from Ice but runs into the blond haired woman. Blond: What? Now that he is here you have nothing to ask? Or are you just like everyone else, and just plain scared of him? The blond smirks at Brandi and Brandi shoots her a dirty look before regathering herself and walking back up to Ice. Brandi: ICE..... Ice puts his hand up to Brandi's face and cuts her off in mid sentence. 'So you want my reaction? Well, I will make this short, sweet and to the point, because frankly I have better things to be doing right now, but since you wasted your time to get out here and bother us, I will give you a little something. Angus, you can say you have more important things to do, but let's look at the things you will be concerning yourself with. Salomane Vation???? Salomane Vation??? Yes that is quality talent to be concerning yourself with. Going into long deep battles beside or against that man will surely get your career nowhere in a hurry. But if you want to concern yourself with him, go right ahead, but however I will concern myself with you, and when you least expect it BOOM! That is when the storm will hit you, and that is when I will hit you. Maybe then you will concern yourself with your fate.' Ice tilts his head to the left with a slight jerk and cracks his neck, and then to the right with the same result. His focus never leaving Brandi's eyes. 'Now as for the rest of the GW. You can run your mouthes like the little punks you are. You can overlook me, and not even metion me, but before you start your pathetic, what has he done, why should I care about him, take a look in the mirror, then take a look at me, and tell me if you actually think YOU desever the slightest bit of time in MY DAY? So from this day forth, everyone in the GW is on notice. I don't care who you are, where you are from, or what title you hold. Because, to put it in simpleton terms so you can understand it, I WILL HURT YOU. No ifs, no ands, and no buts about it.' Ice turns to walk away, but Brandi stops him just as he turns around by grabbing his left arm. Ice snaps his head and stares at Brandi. She smiles seductively at him, and the blond notices. The blond rips Brandi's hand off of Ice's left bicep and shoots her a evil look. Brandi: Just one more thin Ice, that is if you don't mind. What about Stan Stone? What about Vortex? 'Who?' Brandi: Sta..... 'I know who you are talking about, but again I ask the question who? You really want me to worry about and waste my time thinking about two men who have yet to say one word. I have said it before, and I will say it again until someone figures it out. This is MY WORLD, and when you enter MY WORLD things aren't what you are used to. In MY WORLD we play by my rules. Many have tried and enter it and take it over. But to take it over you have to beat me. And there isn't a man alive who can do that one on one... man to man. So Vortex, Stan Stone, you walk your little narrow asses down that aisle and you step into the ring and get ready to face your fate. The gods have spoken, and the crystal ball shows that your fate holds only one thing. ICE!' Ice walks away and the blond latches onto his left arm, tightly holding it with both of her arms. She looks back as they walk away and evilly stares at Brandi. Brandi smiles back at the blond with a devilish smile. Scene fades to black.
