The Opinionated Are So Opinionated

30 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/30/05 Michael hadn't heard from Mindy immediately following Evolution. By the time he went to sleep that night, she hadn't returned home, either. 'She better not have done something stupid,' he mumbled at the thought of it, and such an action didn't help. Saying such a sentence about Mindy Mercer was not the greatest thing that could be uttered. So Michael slept, albeit reluctantly; Mindy was practically suicidal the last few days, and her not being home yet was not a helpful sign. He couldn't remember being so worried about her, and he never was- Markus Riley incident aside... --- -January 27, 2005 Michael looked up from his plate of bacon and french toast to watch his cousin amble lethargically past the dining room and into the kitchen. In between ravenous chews, he leaned back in his chair to see what Mindy was going to dig out of the refridgerator. Michael was somewhat surprised- she had been sleeping until very late in the afternoon all that week up until that day. 'It's not noon yet, so call me a dumbass, but I'm surprised to see you awake, 'Mope Master' Mindy.' Mindy paid no mind to the bittersweet remark Michael gave her. Instead, she merely snatched a two liter bottle of Pepsi from the fridge with one hand as the other grabbed a plastic cup from a nearby cabinet. With the realization that he'd get no answer, Michael lowered his head and returned to his meal. He didn't think he would lose sleep over Mindy's attitude; every now and then he'd have to hang around some woman with PMS, whether it be his wife, a friend, or in the said case, his cousin- and every time was the same. After about a week, they would get over it and become approachable again, give or take a day or two. But this time it was different. Somehow, someway... Before Michael knew it, Mindy sat down across from him at the dining room table- with Mindy at one end of the table, and Michael seated at the other. Michael hunched over in his chair to look at what Mindy had in front of her. 'Hm,' he crooned, 'soda and strawberry pop tarts, cornerstone of any nutritious breakfast, ah?' Mindy didn't even look up from her meal- all she did was whisper with a mopey voice. 'Is Jaden at work, Mikey?' Michael winced as he bit into a piece of bacon. 'Don't remind me about that.' It began to get to the point where Michael derived physical pain from the thought of his wife working. How she worked all the time and he was stuck at home was a mystery to him, but it was one Jaden probably had an answer for. Maybe an answer Michael wouldn't understand, much less want to understand, but an answer all the same. Mindy didn't seem very impressed with Michael's response, and simply nibbled on the pastry that rested on the paper plate in front of her. 'Why did you become a wrestler, Mikey?' Michael sighed at the question. Not more wrestling talk... 'Why I became a wrestler?' Michael reiterrated the question, before he coughed momentarily on a piece of a pancake he swallowed wrong. 'Ahem, I uh.. I became a wrestler because it was the only thing I was really good at. You know, coming from The Bronx, you're raised to think that you're not good at anything. I was good at fighting, but fighting didn't get you anything other than shot over where I lived. Then my parents died and my uncle taught me how to wrestle and.. I'd been doin' that ever since.' Michael was pretty much talking to the floor by the end of his little speech, but Mindy could careless- all she wanted was a straight answer, and she got it as far as she was concerned. 'Not enough of a reason to stick it out because Jaden told you to leave?' Michael took the fork he was holding, and placed it down on his plate. It was going to get ugly... again. 'No, actually. No, it wasn't.' Mindy took another small bite from her pop tart. 'Do you think I should still wrestle?' 'What do you think?' Michael asked, obviously upset. With Mindy living up to Michael calling her 'Mope Master', added with the repeated questions about wrestling, it wasn't shaping up to be a good conversation. In fact, Michael was ready to snap at any second. 'Do you always question others for decisions you should be making? Or is this something that you're doing to me only because I'm your hero? Huh?' And there went the tears. 'I'm sorry!' Mindy cried emotionally, 'you're the only wrestler I care to talk to, and yeah, you're my hero, okay? It doesn't mean I want to annoy you! I just want to have someone to talk to, and after Markus I've had no one.. no one but that idiot from GW constantly harassing me! So I'm sorry if I just spoiled your mood, but you're not the only one with tough decisions to face!' Michael rolled his eyes and glanced down at his partially eaten breakfast. 'Figures. I just lost my appetite.' 'Tough decisions, Mindy?' The older Mercer looked up into the nineteen year old's watery eyes. His glare was like stone compared to her feverishly blinking green orbs. 'What tough decisions do you assume that I have? I have nothing to decide. Every decision I've had has been made already.' 'Only to Jaden,' Mindy sobbed, biting her bottom lip, 'but I know and you know that you want to wrestle again.. you're just afraid of losing your family! Why?! Why are you afraid?!' 'I'm not afraid!' Michael slammed his fists into the table, growing more and more enraged by the second. 'I have nothing to be afraid over!' Mindy blinked in astonishment. She literally could not believe what Michael had told her. 'Well in that case...' Mindy trailed off, searching for the heart she would need to finish the sentence. It took a moment, but she found it- and Michael's reaction was one she had never seen before. 'Why don't you do something with your life?' Michael reeled back in his seat as if he'd been shot. Never had his commitment been judged about anything, ever. 'Are you fuckin' kidding me?' 'Go back to wrestling, Mikey,' the girl pleaded, throwing her upper torso onto the table in a fit of sorrow. 'Please... at least be my manager or something, I just want us to be happy. I haven't seen me, you, Gabi, and Jaden share a happy moment together.. not once. You owe it to yourself to go back to wrestling.. please... I can't do this without any help...' It sounded as though Mindy was going to keep talking, but she was so distraught that she couldn't form another word from her quivering red lips. Michael saw the defeat in her eyes, as he watched her crying hysterically in the otherwise still dining room. It was a depressing sight for all the wrong reasons, and yet... 'You're not going to take no for an answer, are you, cousin?' Michael cared for people when he saw them hurt. If they were in physical danger, Michael would be the first person to assisst them. Emotions, however, usually had no effect. For the first time since he could remember, Michael truly cared. --- That was what led him up to the dreary Tuesday morning, the morning where he hoped he would run into his cousin on his way to the bathroom or the kitchen- depressed or otherwise. He needed to talk to her.. she deserved that much. And Michael drew a light smile from the sight of his cousin in the living room- bright and early at seven-thirty- watching Gundam Wing with her niece on the couch. He said not one word, however- as Jaden stood over the two ladies in wait for Gabi to pry herself from the cartoon to go to school. Mindy could feel the look they were getting from Jaden, and motioned for Gabi to get up. 'Alright, sweetheart,' Mindy said with a smile, 'we'll watch more when you get home from school, I promise.' Little Gabriella pouted her protest, but Mindy would have no tears. She gave her niece a big hug, before allowing Jaden to send her off. It wasn't until that moment when one of them actually saw Michael standing in the hallway. And at the sight of him, Gabriella pulled away from her mother's grasp to run and leap into her father's arms. 'Bye, daddy,' she squeaked, 'I'm going to school.' Michael couldn't help but grin- his daughter always brought him joy, no matter what was truly on his mind. 'Okay, darlin', you go right ahead.' Michael put his daughter down safely on the floor, and Gabi took one step back. 'Seeya when I get home?' she asked. Michael's smile grew bigger. 'Of course, hun. I'll seeya when you get home.' 'Bye, daddy.' Gabriella waved to her father, before she turned and ran to her mother and allowed herself to be led out the front door. Michael's attention slowly turned to his cousin, and she swiftly returned the favor. 'Are you alright?' Michael asked, walking towards Mindy as he elicited a soft yawn. Mindy thought briefly about the question, before she shrugged and gave her trademark childlike smirk- it was then obvious to Michael what the answer would be. 'Weeell, seein' as to how I won my match last week, yeah, I think I'm feelin' a little better now.' Michael meekly brushed back his spiky black hair, tilting his head in slightly feigned surprise. 'You won? That's awesome, cousin.. I'm proud of ya.' Mindy gave a big, toothy grin. 'T'ank ya.' 'No problem...' It was then that Michael was going to tell her what he needed to tell her. Something that was probably going to cause problems with Jaden, but something he needed to handle none the less. 'Hey, I was gonna tell ya last night but I fell asleep before you got home, but...' 'But what?' 'I decided, that you were right about what you said a few days ago.' Mindy gasped. In her time of knowing Michael, he had never told her that she was right, not about one single thing. 'I was?' 'Yeah,' Michael nodded. 'Yeah, you were... and I would not mind being your manager in GW.'
