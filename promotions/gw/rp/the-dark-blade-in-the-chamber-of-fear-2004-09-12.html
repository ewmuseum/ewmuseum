The Dark Blade....In the Chamber of Fear

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-12-2004 Darkness fills the screen. No light to pierce the black. Soft breathing is heard, and little else. Finally, a soft murmur penetrates the darkness. The black screen was rather boring, after all? “Bob’Bless this tool to fight the darkness. Take me to what I need”.” and suddenly, a light springs up as a torch begins to blaze. We see the King, Venneman Pendragon. In his left hand, he holds the now blazing torch. In his right is the curved Katana sword that he smithed with his own hands. The blade looks fragile compared to the broader Excalibur that his hands are used to, yet this blade is special. Folded one hundred and fifty times, the steel is as hard as a blade can get without delving into the magical. I guess we can only hope that it can hold against the dark blade if it comes to that. Pendragon stands still, surveying the temple. Of an ancient western design, it appears to be Christian in it’s builder’s beliefs with crosses and the like adorning the halls. Pendragon pays the artifacts in the corridor no mind, save for uneasy glances. Running friezes adorn the wall. Pendragon holds the torch close as he begins to move through the corridor, peering at the wall. In one scene, a blonde man in gold does battle with a white haired man in black. Blue light flashes from their swords at each picture of them clashing. At the end of this panel, there are two outcomes. In the first, the man in black stands over the golden fellow, and on his sword point rests what appears to be the earth. Pendragon shivers at this and looks to the second outcome. In this, the man in gold stands triumphant over the man in black, the latter’s dark blade raised high in his right hand. In his left, he supports the world, and a ray of light shines on it from above?. “And so nothing is certain. The man in gold is certainly me’Yet I wonder who the man in black is. He must be the guardian of Lucien. Two fates’In one, the world lives in the light, sheltered by mine hand. In the other’I fall?” Pendragon shudders as he says this last and bows his head silently for a moment. He raises his eyes once more and continues down the corridor. The seemingly endless corridor is filled with murals of prophecy. Men in gold slay hideous demons, whilst men in black slay children’Women. Pendragon walks by them all, caring only to glance at the scenes played out before him. No need to worry himself over what only MAY come to pass. As he walks and is about to pass from the corridor, a sight catches his eyes. A man in shining golden armor holds two swords high over his head. One is white with power, and the other is black with the power of malice. The frieze almost seems to move as the man brings the swords together to form an all powerful blue blade. Children surround this huge man, and he bestrides the world as a colossus. Pendragon gapes in wonder at the beautifully wrought rendition. With a start he comes back to himself and peers into the opening ahead? “Two blades become one. Lucien, the black twin of Excalibur. Prophecy. I wish I had not seen this. Prophecy is a difficult thing. Things do not always mean what they seem, and a man who places his faith in a prophecy may be doomed to fail or forced to change the prophecy because he thinks he does know the way to fulfill it. Forced prophecy is a dangerous thing, and best left untouched. Sometimes walking blind is a much better way to see the light?” he says the last with a glance at his blazing torch. The torch doesn’t illumine as one would believe it should. The darkness seems to swallow the light until all that is left is a small, dim pool around the man. He sighs and holds his blade at the ready as he walks into this new chamber. As he does, he pauses to listen. His eyes widen for a moment, yet he continues grimly on. This is why he is here. As he does, blinding light illumines him for a moment, and the scene abruptly changes? ***************FLASH******************** Clad in a white tunic bearing his Dragon Sigil, Pendragon sits on a throne of gold. Excalibur at his side, he does appear to be the content King of his domain. Petitioners line the throne room carpet, and his eyes widen suddenly at where he is? “This cannot be! I was just in the dark, and now I am in mine own Kingdom” A kingdom that does not exist as of now?.” The thought is cut off as a woman lays a hand upon his shoulder. Recognition dawns in his eyes and he smiles? “Petitioners, I have done with mine proclamations this day. Return tomorrow, or wait in the guest quarters. I have other things that I must see to?” He commands in his loudest voice, and the room clears. He stares up at the woman, perhaps the most beautiful he has ever seen. His Queen, the lady Sarah. Dark hair frames her face, and her skin is the most beautiful color of white. “Of course’My queen. How could I believe what I was thinking! Of course this is right! This is mine kingdom!” “My husband’Your son Benjamin wishes you to watch his advances with the blade. One day, Excalibur shall be his, and it will be for naught if he knows not the approval of the man who wields it now?” She caresses his cheek as she states this fact, and he smiles openly? “Of course. Benjamin is a good lad, and I would hate to disappoint.” He rises and as he does, the doors clash open and armed men stream into the chamber. Pendragon draws steel, but is soon overpowered and thrust aside’He crashes into the wall, and Excalibur falls from his grasp’He stares at his hands in disbelief. How has he become so soft? The Lady Sarah backs into a corner and a white haired man smiles at the prostrate Pendragon as he runs a sword through her. She merely gasps as the blood begins to flow and she slumps to the floor, eyes accusing even as the life drains from them? “NOOOOOOOOOO!” Is the roar that escapes from Pendragon and he reaches for the sword, intent on dieing as he would like, in defense of his wife. His hands close on the hilt, and abruptly the scene changes once more? ******************FLASH********************** Pendragon’s hands close on the sword he made with his hands and he slashes at the air. With a start, e realizes he is back where he belongs, yet the memory of his slain Queen will not leave him? “Her eyes said that “twas mine fault. I could do nothing. I had not the strength. Content as I was, I had grown soft. Light have mercy’No wonder men emerge from this place tainted.” With a deep breath, he gathers himself and in the distance, a chuckle is discerned’Pendragon wheels about, sword in one hand, torch in the other, searching out a target. “Who is out there” Show thyself, coward!” And from the darkness, a reply comes in a richly melodious bass voice?“Not yet, I think. Thee must see more before facing such as me?” Pendragon rushes forward blindly, and after two steps the scene changes once more. This truly is an interesting place? ***************FLASH****************** The sword thrust took the man full in the chest, and as he pulls Excalibur free, smoke begins to pour from the wound? “Demonspawn’I now return thee to hell. The hammer has fallen, and so shalt thee!” Pendragon speaks the last as he swings the sword and lops off the man thing’s head. With a bow of his head, he says a silent prayer to send the reaver on his way, and from the darkness behind him jump ten figures. He turns smoothly, not even giving a “WTF” look as he does. His sword is a blur as body parts are chopped and hacked and blood spills onto the ground. The numbers are never ending. Fangspawn, half men, half beasts whose soul purpose is to serve the dark lord. At last, overpowered, Pendragon is carried into the darkness. The shot follows and shows the Fangspawn chaining Pendragon to what appears to be an altar. Once secured, the white haired man emerges from the shadows, his face a blur. Pendragon gasps as he sees him, but the man only shakes his head? “Thee can be turned to me whether thy wishes it or not. This place adds to mine power, and I wish to have thee for a slave. God’s hammer”.PAH! Didst thou thinkest that thee could defeat me? This dagger?” He pulls an ornate weapon from a small sheath at his side?“This dagger can turn thee to darkness with one slice. Even thy precious Creator cannot save thee then?” Pendragon flexes against the chains and stares defiantly up?“This is not real. Thee art not real, though thou speakest the language. I will overcome thee?” With a roar, he rips one hand free and grasps the white haired man’s throat. As he begins to squeeze, the shot abruptly changes once more? **************FLASH***************** The dark chamber once more, and Pendragon’s hands close on air. There is simply nothing there to grab onto. With a roar, Pendragon shakes a fist at the dark and is met with the mad laughing of who can only be the White haired fellow in black?. “DAMNIT! If only one of these doors would take me to GW”.There is important business for me there. Defense of the King’s Gold is not shirked by the Hammer.” Almost as if he willed it to be so, an opening appears in the reality. Screaming throngs await on the other side, and the Global Arena stretches out before him. Pendragon smiles as he starts for the opening, ignoring the groan from the darkness?.. ~TO BE CONTINUED~
