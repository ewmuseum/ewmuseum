Fatherhood: Part 2

3 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd January 2004 Parenthood... At one time or another we have all been someone's son or daughter. Many of you will say that you never knew your father or mother but does that stop you being their son or daughter? No it does not, you could hate your mother and father but that does not stop you being their son or daughter. No matter what we are all someone's child whether they wanted us or not. Personally all I had when I was younger was my mother but I have never forgotten my father even though I never met him. My mum used to tell me stories about my father, such things about how they met and how dad had proposed to her. My mother never had a bad word to say about my father and so in a way I grew up respecting and loving my father even though he was no longer around. Even now I mirror my mannerisms on him, respecting a woman's wishes and knowing how to treat a woman. Now a father myself I can't help wondering what my father would have been like if he was around when I was born... However before we get to that I need to finish a story I was telling you last year just before our last PPV, Showdown In Tokyo. I had just finished an intense training schedule with the leader of the team I fought alongside at the PPV, Brick. I felt in the best shape of my life and felt on top of the world then I got the call. The call was from my friend Peter and instantly he told me that Rachel was having our baby. I rushed out of the gym and into the street but soon realised that I was 5 states over from my home and had no means of transport. I was in trouble and here is how the rest of the story unfolded... Tuesday 30th November 2:30pm 'You have got to have a flight?!?!' 'I'm sorry sir!' 'Not one damn plane? Not one aircraft that will take me home?' 'No and again I am sorry but all flights going to Dallas are booked to fly tomorrow.' I was in an airport and had been arguing with the woman at the ticket desk for the passed 20 minutes. The woman at the desk wasn't helping things either as she looked at me every so often with a glance of concern. She was your typical blonde who was extremely beautiful but didn't have much going on upstairs and only knew how to press a few buttons on a computer. A huge queue had formed behind me. I was beginning to feel extremely anxious as people began pointing at me and whispering about my suit. I never usually attract this much attention as I usually have a private mode of transport anywhere I go. However this time round I had not arranged any transport and so I was stuck. 'Please my wife is having a baby, I have got to get to Dallas! Is there not a private jet I can use, cost is no object.' She pressed a few more buttons and stared blankly at the screen for what seemed like hours before turning back to me. 'No sir there are no flights. Now would you please move out of the way as I have other people to serve.' I banged my fists on the desk and looked at her with such anger that even with my mask she could feel the intensity. 'Thanks for nothing!' I shouted through gritted teeth. I moved over to the arrival lounge and sat down on the seats. I put my head in my hands and tears began to stream down my face. Of course no one around me could see it and for that I was thankful. My whole world was crashing down, I could see Rachel screaming my name wondering where the hell I was. My heart sank why had I been so stupid to go to a gym 5 states over when my wife was due any day. I had no way of getting to her in time, I needed a miracle... 'Hey mister, excuse me mister?' A little voice came from in front of me. I looked up and saw a little girl of about 8 in a pink dress with her long blonde hair staring at me with amazement. 'What is it kid?' I asked not really in the mood for talking. 'Your Adam Myers aren't you?' 'Yeah?' 'Wow you are one of my favourite Global Wrestling superstars.' I couldn't help but smile as it is always nice to meet a fan. 'Can I have your autograph?' 'Sure, you got a pen?' 'Oh no! Hang on my dad will.' She sprinted over to a man dressed in a business suit that was already walking over to her. He was a very handsome looking man and looked very successful. He pulled a pen and pad out of his pocket and handed to the little girl who came back running over to me. She handed me the pen and pad. 'Who shall I make it out too?' I asked. 'Emma.' She said smiling back at me. Another tear rolled down my cheek as the name Emma was what we were going to call our baby if it was a girl and here I was miles away while that baby was being born signing autographs. I signed the piece of paper and handed it back to Emma. She smiled as she looked down at it and then looked up at me with concern. 'Hey Mr. Myers why you upset?' 'How did you...' I said amazed that she could tell. 'The sound of your voice.' Emma said seeming proud of herself. 'Well Emma,' I wasn't sure whether I should tell her about it all as it might raise to many questions for her dad, I decided not to care, 'My wife is having our baby and she is 5 states away and unless I can get a plane I won't see our baby born. And there are no flights going to Dallas.' 'Dallas you say?' The girl said with a smile on her face, 'I might be able to help.' Before I could reply the girl ran back over to her father and dragged him over. 'This is my dad,' The girl said pointing at the man by her side, 'Dad this is...' 'Adam Myers,' The man interrupted as I stood up and shook his hand, 'My name is Richard Wood. I hear you are looking for a flight out of here, well me and my daughter are flying to Dallas ourselves so you are more than welcome to join us.' A huge smile washed across my face. I needed a miracle and I had found one. However the name Richard Wood sounded familiar and how the hell did this guy know my name? I thought nothing of it for now as he was the one who was going to get me back to my home. 'Thank you so much.' I said nearly shaking the man's arm off. 'Well my plane is all ready so if you want to follow us.' As we walked through the airport I no longer cared that people were staring at me all I knew was that I was going home to see my baby being born... 5:30pm The name Richard Wood was still bothering me as I burst through my door. The voice recognition went off but I didn’t have time and I burst through the sterilising entrance. Alarms began to ring out but as I burst through the door the only noise I heard was the cry of a baby... My baby... My Emma was born! Now I bet you are all wondering what this has to do with my match at Reload? Well the fact is up until that moment in that airport I have never given up in life. But thanks to Emma and Richard I found my determination rekindled. I am the GW Cruiserweight Champion and I am sure Josh Curtis will give me a run for my money but I will not give up Curtis and I hop you don't either. I want you to give me your best and like always I will give you my best. At Reload we will see who deserves to be Cruiserweight Champion, at Reload I hope I will see the true Josh Curtis because you will see the true... Adam Myers!
