Mambo Italiano: Ain't That A Kick In The Head?

13 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 13, 2006 Things had more or less returned to normal for Biddle after the horrific episode with Charles. He had elected to stay in Las Vegas for the time being and had recently taken up residence in yet another casino resort on the strip. Unknown to Biddle, the casino he currently resides in is owned by the Rizzonelli family, an alleged crime family that supposedly controls all the mafia interests in Las Vegas. Even if he was aware of the supposed connections of the casino owners, it wouldn’t bother Biddle for tonight he was far too busy knocking back martinis with his friend Frank. Biddle and Frank were friends in boarding school and had randomly ran into each as they both wandered about the casino. Frank was shorter than Biddle and had gained a good deal of weight since Biddle saw him last. They sat at a table in the casino’s lounge, it was nearly evening and the warm up acts were still on stage. Biddle was in mid-sentence when an attractive brunette with an olive complexion sat down at a nearby table. “I’d definitely hit that,” Biddle smiled as he pointed out the girl for Frank. Frank chuckled to himself, would Biddle ever change? “It’d be better for your health if you stayed away from that one Mike,” Frank said. “Why?” Biddle asked as he arched his eyebrow. Frank smirked, surprised that Biddle didn’t already know the identity of the young woman. “That’s Belinda Rizzonelli, her family owns this place.” “So?” Biddle chuckled. “SO?” Frank practically yelled as he leaned forward in his seat. “The Rizzonelli’s are the top mafia family in town you idiot. If her brother Gino caught you talking to her he’d break your neck.” Biddle rolled his eyes. “I’m sure I could beat up some wannabe gangster, I think you’re forgetting that I’m a professional fighter.” “And I think you’re being cocky again, same old Mike...always getting into shit because you don’t stop to think before you act. You thought it was bad when Charles took the fly swatter to your family jewels” What the Rizzonelli’s would do to you if they caught you drooling all over her would make that look like a walk in the park.” “I think you’re over reacting buddy. I’m sure her family would be ecstatic if I let her be seen in public with me,” Biddle replied as he eyed the Italian beauty. “You shouldn’t risk it Mike,” Frank sternly replied. “Oh what’s the worse that could happen?” Biddle asked. Meanwhile, a few miles outside Las Vegas... Isaiah Federman had been the accountant for the Rizzonelli family for over twenty years. The pay was good but he had always felt he should have been paid more due to the risk he was taking by helping out that family of criminals. That was why he had recently started embezzling money, nothing fancy...just a few thousand dollars here and there. It was enough to make up for the family owed him for his services. He didn’t think it was a big deal, there was no way any of the Rizzonelli’s would be smart enough to realize that he was stealing from them. In fact, his embezzlement wasn’t even on his mind as he stood atop the diving board directly above his recently installed swimming pool. He dived off the board and splashed down deep within the azure water of the pool, he would be underwater for more than just a moment, he was currently diving for some of those neon sticks his wife had bought at the grocery store yesterday. Unseen by Federman, several well dressed men emerged from the bushes in his backyard and rushed their way to his pool. One of the men carried what appeared to be a can of gasoline and the man next to him had box of kitchen matches in his hand. The man with gasoline quickly set to work by tipping the can over and running around the pool in order to spread out the deadly liquid. The man with the match simply lit one and nonchalantly tossed it into the pool. Within seconds the pool looked as if it were the entranceway to Hades, Federman was still underwater and wasn’t even aware of the inferno awaiting him overhead. With closed eyes Federman swam towards the surface, looking forward to a gulp of fresh air. It was with a bit of twisted luck that Federman just happened to come up towards the edge of the pool, in a spot where there was no fire. He opened his eyes and finally noticed the flames and screamed as he scrambled for dry land. Suddenly a large fist struck him in the nose and a rough voice yelled out, “Get back in the fiery water ya God damn thief.” Everything went black as Federman fell back within the depths of the pool. Later on that evening when the flames had died down his wife would find him floating face down in the water. “That’ll teach that bastard to steal from my family,” the large man that had earlier punched Federman in the face said. “Damn straight Gino,” the henchman that had lit the match said, “no one messes with the Rizzonelli family and lives to tell about it.” Gino smiled the grin of killer that had just gotten away with a nationwide murder spree. He pointed to two of his men. “You two find the money that damn Jew stole, the rest of us will be back at the casino waiting for you.” Back at the Rizzonelli Casino Biddle was alone at the table now, Frank had to leave so he could meet his wife for a show at another casino. Belinda Rizzonelli was still seated at the same table, she too was alone and unaware of the fact that Biddle had spent the entire evening watching her with drunken lust. The warning from Frank still rang in his ears, would he really want to risk another life threatening dramatic episode just so he could try to slip the salami to this Italian beauty? Belinda dropped something and bent down to pick it up, revealing her ample cleavage. Biddle watched with wide eyes and it was if God Himself had answered the dilemma for him. Without even thinking Mike gets up and walks over to the Italian girl’s table, she looks up at him and smiles. “Can I help you with something?” She asks in a sweet voice. Biddle smirks, “Is this seat taken?” he asks, indicating the seat next to her. “No, go ahead,” she replies. He sits down. “My name is Mike Biddle,” he says trying his best in his nearly inebriated state to sound suave. Belinda’s eyes light up in recognition, she had heard her brother speak of this man before. “You’re that fighter that’s staying in the hotel aren’t you?” Biddle nods his cockily nods his head, the fact that she recognized him did nothing but inflate his ego even more. “Hopefully my brother turns up tonight then, Gino is a big fan of yours...he’d love to meet you,” she said. Biddle took a glossy 4x6 photo out of his jacket pocket and quickly signed it with his pen. “Here,” he said as he handed the photo to her, “you can give that to your brother.” “Oh he’ll love it,” she said with a smile. “You’re really sweet.” Before Biddle could respond he felt a rather large hand grasp his shoulder and pull him out of his chair. He heard Belinda object but he wasn’t really paying attention for he is face to face with a rather large man that looked like a younger Luca Brasi, Biddle had nearly shit himself when he came eye to eye with this man. No one in the lounge looked over at the table, for they were all used to this happening when some idiot hit on the Rizzonelli girl. “Who the hell said you could talk to my sister ya God damn little fudge packer?” The Luca Brasi look-a-like demanded. “Leave him alone Gino,” Belinda demanded, “don’t you know who that is?” “I don’t give a damn who he is, this little bastard shouldn’t be talkin” to ya,” Gino replied. “That’s Mike Biddle, Gino,” Belinda said quietly. “What?” Gino said as he checked the face of the man he had been yelling at. He instantly recognized the famous face of one of his favorite GLOBAL fighters and politely help Biddle back into his seat. “Sorry about that Mr. Biddle, I thought you were someone else,” Gino said with a smile. “No worries,” Biddle said with a nervous tone. Gino Rizzonelli looks directly at Biddle and Mike noticed that there is some kind of respect in the other mans eyes. “I wish you luck in the Clash of the Titans Mr. Biddle and I hope that you enjoy Rome...it is very nice this time of year. Just don’t let me down sir, I have some money on you to win,” Gino said in a nice, respectful voice. “Well I hope I don’t disappoint you then,” Biddle said. He really, really, really hoped that he won now...no way in hell did he want to be on this guys bad side. “I’m gonna go ahead and leave you two alone to talk then,” Gino said. He turned to leave but before he left he gave Biddle a look that screamed “Don’t try any funny stuff with my sister!” “I’m so sorry about that Mike,” Belinda said after her brother had left. “Don’t even worry about it,” Mike said with a smile. He sighed lightly as he wondered what he had gotten himself into this time.
