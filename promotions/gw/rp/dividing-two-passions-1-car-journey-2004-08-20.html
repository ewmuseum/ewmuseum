Dividing Two Passions #1: Car Journey

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 20th 2004 ??Surrounded by the green fields of the Polish countryside, lies the large grey Arefiev family mansion. The large forty bedroom house was built in 1900 by the great Ivan Arefiev. Ivan was the first inductee to the Flying Vergomovs after the great Vergomov brothers? “I thought you were the only Arefiev inductee into the Flying Vergomovs?”” interrupts Zergolov. Arefiev shakes his head as the pair drive along the Polish roads in their very own red Ford Mustang. Zergolov is at the wheel. Arefiev is in the passenger seat with a road map unfolded in front of him. “No,” explains Arefiev, “it turned out that Ivan wasn’t born an Arefiev, but at that time he had to be from a respected family to make it into the circus. Hence, he became an Arefiev” Therefore officially I’m the only Arefiev Vergomov.” Arefiev smiles. Zergolov nods with understanding. “The house was a way for him to give something back.” Igor continues. “The great mansion lies on an 80-acre estate of green fields and a small forest. Each room is decorated in an authentic English Victorian style that is preciously preserved by the current inhabitants as a symbol to the founder of the great house. The banquet hall can host events for over 500 people and the kitchens are run by a team of five hundred” “Dude?” interrupts Zergolov again, “you sound like an estate agent. Give it a rest.” Arefiev is silent. He moves into a huffing position. “I was just sharing a little of my knowledge. I’m sorry for disturbing you.” There is silence in the car as the pair travel on. Arefiev sulks as he looks out the window. Zergolov begins to drum his fingers on the steering wheel. The silence stretches. Ever so faintly, Igor Arefiev begins to hum a tune into himself, as he watches the fields pass the car. Igor slowly builds in volume. Zergolov sighs. “Right okay,” he exclaims, “tell me about the house?” “No,” snaps Igor, “I don’t want to bore you?” Constantino sighs again. “You won’t bore me,” he protests. “Yes I will,” answers Igor, “I’ll sound like an estate agent.” Again, the driver of the car is caused to sigh. “No you won’t,” he exclaims, clearly trying to please his partner. Arefiev shakes his head. Zergolov glances to his right and sees the shaking head of his tag team partner. “Why not?” bites Zergolov. “Because?” mumbles Arefiev. “Because what?” “Because?” again mumbles Arefiev. “Because what?” “Because?” again mumbles Arefiev. “BECAUSE WHAT?” screams Zergolov, seemingly very annoyed by his partner’s unwillingness to answer. Arefiev is caused to turn away from the window by the scream and he looks blankly at his partner. “Well?” suggests a much calmer Zergolov. “Because, I kind of finished everything I had to say?” Zergolov sighs. Arefiev slides out of his huffing mood and begins to study the road map. Zergolov drums at the steering wheel with his fingers and sings into himself. The silence extends. “Remind me again why we agreed to visit your family the week before our biggest night as wrestlers?” enquiries a pessimistic-sounding Zergolov. “It’s my sister’s birthday,” replies Arefiev. “Really?”” Zergolov’s tone has chirped up. Constantino turns and smiles at his partner. “Gloria’s?” asks Zergolov with a telling smile. Arefiev returns the smile with an icy look. He holds his expression motionless for a number of seconds. “You should know,” snaps Arefiev, “you went out with here for two years. When was her birthday?” Zergolov’s smile drops and a look pf panic and fear swipes across his face. Zergolov returns his eyes to the road and is silent. Arefiev smiles. “You don’t know!” He laughs out loud. Zergolov is quiet and motionless. He concentrates on the road. “So glad she got rid of you if you can’t even remember her birthday,” jokes Arefiev. “Shut it!” Zergolov snaps. Arefiev retreats back into his seat. There is an icy silence between the two men. “It’s Anna’s birthday by the way,” mutters Igor. Zergolov nods. A blanket of silence once again engulfs the car. “Could you have not sent a card?” mumbles Zergolov, “at least then we wouldn’t suffer any jet lag?” “Emm” NO!” snaps Arefiev. Zergolov turns to face Igor Arefiev. “Why?” “Because,” bites Igor, “It’s her twenty-first birthday?” “Why did I get invited?” asks Zergolov, “Your parents hate me?” Arefiev shrugs. “I don’t know they just wanted us to come. Our American tour hasn’t started so we are doing nothing anyway?” Zergolov nods. “So when we leaving?” enquiries Constantino. “Friday, I think?” shrugs Igor. “FRIDAY?” The car swerves across the road. Zergolov takes it under control and looks over at his best friend. “What?” Igor steadies himself in his chair and look over at Arefiev. “Training” Manifest Destiny aren’t like the other teams we have faced. They beat Josh and Ken Partridge to get here,” explains Zergolov. “We beat Jamester and Roswell. I’ve beat Soma in a singles match at Master of the Mat.” “You didn’t really beat him. You lasted longer than him in the elimination match.” “Therefore I beat him,” snaps Arefiev. “You’re just jealous that you weren’t in the Master of the Mat.” Zergolov sighs. “I was just saying. Why do you have to turn it into a fight?” “I wasn’t turning it into a fight,” sulks Igor. “Yeah well, I’m just worried about training. I don’t want to let this chance slip away.” “It won’t,” explains Igor in a reassuring tone, “this is the mansion of one of the greatest circus dynasties in the history of the world ever?” “Yeah and so?” Arefiev sighs. “Let me finish. Do you not think that we have a gym in the house?” Constantino’s face is covered in an understanding look. “But what about our move training” I was kinda hoping to unveil a new move on Monday night.” Arefiev looks puzzled. “A new move?” Zergolov nods. “You’ll see it later?” Arefiev shrugs. “I don’t know. I suppose we can do weight training and stuff. The move work can be done on Sunday or Monday morning.” Zergolov shakes his head. Arefiev looks puzzled. “What’s wrong?” asks Arefiev. “This is the Global Wrestling Tag Team Championships!” exclaims Zergolov. “Yeah I know.” “Well then. This is our chance. This is our time to shine. To be the pinnacle of the division. Don’t you realise that?” “Yeah, “course I do!” “Well then. Why have you dragged me the whole way to Poland the week before a major match?” Arefiev shrugs. The pair continue in silence along the road. “Turn in here?” mutters Igor. Constantino slows the car a little and then turns in right between a set of grand white gates. The car disappears between the trees down the driveway.
