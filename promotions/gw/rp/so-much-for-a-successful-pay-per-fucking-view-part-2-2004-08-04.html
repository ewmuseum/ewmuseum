So much for a successful Pay-Per-Fucking-View Part 2

4 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 4th 2004 “Get up!” Constantino Zergolov stands at the door of Arefiev’s room with his arms crossed. He looks sown at the bed, with a look of impatience in his eyes. Arefiev groans and rolls onto his front. “Igor?” snaps Constantino. Another groans resounds from beneath the covers. Constantino sighs and turns away from the door. “Just because he’s a fucking star now!” mumbles Constantino as he crosses the studio plan apartment. Zergolov lifts a blue sports bag onto his shoulder and pulls open the door. “Igor” I’ve gone to training!” exclaims Constantino back into the apartment, not really trying to let his tag team partner hear him. Zergolov steps through the door and closes it behind him. There is silence in the apartment. The silence extends. Igor Arefiev stumbles out of his bedroom thirty seconds later, wearing a pair of silky boxers and nothing more. He wipes some sleep from his eyes and crosses the apartment watching for the unpacked brown boxes that line the walls. He groans as he steps into the light of the kitchen window. He turns on the cold tap and bends down to search in a brown box. He pulls out a small glass and fills it with water. “Constantino” Where are the pain killers?” His voice echoes throughout the white apartment. Silence is the only reply he receives. Igor shrugs and opens a cupboard above his head. He looks around the shelves and pulls down a small box of headache tablets. After struggling with the silver seal for a number of seconds, he finally pops two tablets into his mouth. The tablets are quickly followed by a mouthful of water. Igor places the glass onto the sink and turns back towards the leather sofa in the centre of the room. He throws himself down onto the plush cushions and lifts the newspaper, beginning to flick through the pages. Quiet engulfs the whole room, expect for the occasional rattle of newspaper being turned and groans sounding from Igor’s throat. The front door clicks open and Constantino Zergolov bounds into the kitchen without even paying attention to Igor on the sofa. Igor immediately sits up and watches Constantino walk back across the apartment. “Where are you going?” enquiries a hoarse Arefiev. Zergolov stops dead in his tracks, evidently not expecting anyone to be in the room. Constantino turns to face the voice of his best friend. “I was just on my way to training but I forgot my elbow pads,” exclaims Zergolov as he stands in the apartment stuffing two pieces of cloth into his “Vergomov” sports bag that matches his navy “Vergomov” tracksuit. Igor seems disappointed. “Thanks for waking me?” “I did” Look at the clock, it’s one! I’ve been calling you since ten but all I get is a groan and then you roll over!” Igor groans and returns to his newspaper. Constantino shrugs his shoulders and leaves the apartment. “Don’t wait for me to get changed?” grumbles Arefiev. “Just because I’m getting a bit of recognition, he’s acting like a bitch” I showed the world what we are capable of and he holds it against me? I let people know what we are about and he doesn’t like me for it? He knows I know he could do the same? He holds it against me that I get the recognition and that he is left in the cold? It’s not my fault? That’s the public’s doing? Not mine!” Igor rolls off the couch and saunters into the bathroom. He wipes at his face, and goes about his grooming routine. He begins to shave with an electric razor. “If it weren’t for me, he wouldn’t be in this world” If it weren’t for me we wouldn’t be regarded as the greatest prospects in this federation? I came up with the idea to become wrestlers and he persists to be pissed at me? It was only natural that one of us would have a big break? He would have reacted the same? He would have went to the parties? He would have done the interviews? So I maybe forgot him? Not intentionally? We are a team? We will be the ultimate team? We will be the pinnacle of a generation? Master of the Mat has just guaranteed us more coverage than we had expected so soon?” Arefiev wipes down his face and splashes his neck with aftershave. He walks into his bedroom and begins to get dressed. “On Monday night, we have an opportunity to practically guarantee ourselves the tag team titles and he doesn’t want to know” He’s trying too hard to prove to everyone that he is a bigger star than I am? I mean look at our opponents? Jamester and Roswell?” Igor audibly laughs. “J-Gonz and Train?” Arefiev laughs again. “They have just threw teams together” People that have tagged maybe once before have to go up against us? It wouldn’t look good to hand us the titles so they are going to make us work for them? But this won’t be work? We are a pure breed tag team? Does Constantino give a shit?” Arefiev zips up his training top and walks back into the apartment, grabbing his wrestling attire. “No, he doesn’t” He is thinking about one thing? Himself? He wants to prove himself to the fans? Rather than realise we can prove ourselves by holding the tag titles for an eternity? No, no? Just because I did what I threatened? Just because I entertained and done well in two matches he is huffing? He is acting like a child? Like a baby? Why is acting like such a tool?” Arefiev stuffs all his wrestling gear into the bag and zips it up. He throws it towards the door. “So what if I get recognised on the street” We get recognised in our tracksuits as a team? The Flying Vergomovs? People don’t know my face? We wear masks or doesn’t he realise that? I know he’s just as talented? I realise he could have done as well? We both couldn’t enter the competition so they had to flip a coin? Aarghhh! I’m so fucked off with him! So much for a successful Pay-Per-Fucking-View!” Arefiev grabs his keys, and walks to the door. He lifts his wrestling gear and steps out the door, slamming behind him.
