Back in the studio

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-5-03 It was yet another week of recording tracks for DMAK’s upcoming album of cover songs. The previous week, they had just gotten through recording their cover of the old Manhattans song “Kiss and Say Goodbye”, the very song they sang when Brick had discovered them in the West 4th street station. This week, they were recording a cover of the Enchantment hit “Gloria”. There was already one previous remake by Jesse Powell On this track, Andre, the group’s second tenor, would sing lead. “OK, let’s go over that again, from the top,” Brick said over the intercom as he and Aaron watched from the control booth in the recording studio. “Remember, we start straight from the piano intro?” “I think they know where to start, Brick,” replied Aaron. “We’ve been doing this for three hours now.” “And we’ll do it for another three hours if we have to. Now, from the top.” Andre: Gloria My Gloria Things ain’t been the same Since you went away, yeah Gloria (DMAK: Gloria) My Gloria, yeah (Ooh’Gloria) I don’t wanna see Another day (Ah, ooh) Seems like only yesterday I beheld your sweet face (Wah?) And in my lonely room Are memories (Memories) of your embrace, oh? Gloria (Gloria) My Gloria, yeah (I love you) Things ain’t been the same Since you went away, yeah Gloria (Gloria) My Gloria (Ooh’Gloria), yeah I don’t wanna see Another day (Ah, ooh) Isn’t it funny how time can change All the things you once believed (Doo, doo, doo, wah) But time won’t change the way I feel “Cause in my mind (In my mind) it’s you and me (You and me) you and me, oh” Gloria (Gloria) My Gloria, yeah (I love you) Things ain’t been the same Since you went away, I just can’t go on Gloria (Gloria) Oh, baby (Ooh’Gloria) I don’t wanna see Another day (Ah, ooh) I’m so lonely, girl Isn’t it funny how time can change All the things you once believed (Doo, doo, doo, wah) Whoa, but time won’t change the way I feel “Cause in my mind (In my mind) it’s still you and me (You and me), you and me Gloria (Gloria) My Gloria, yeah (I love you) Things ain’t been the same Since you went away, I just can’t go on Gloria (Gloria) My Gloria (Ooh’Gloria) I don’t wanna see Another day (Ah, ooh) I don’t wanna see, no Another day (Ah, ooh) Without your love I don’t wanna see, oh Another day (Without) It’s a real sad thing, but I don’t want to see, no Another day (Ah, ooh) Without your love, I don’t wanna see, no Another day (Ah, ooh) It’s gonna hurt me, but I (Don’t want to see) no, ooh Another day (Ah, ooh) I just can’t wait (Don’t want to see) no longer (Ah, ooh) I just can’t bear to be (Don’t want to see) without your love, no (Ah, ooh) “So, Brick. Another three hours?” “No, that was just fine.”
