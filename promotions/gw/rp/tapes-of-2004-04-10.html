'Tapes of...'

10 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10th April 2004 'Tapes of...' Usually, I find myself appreciating the work of somebody who is filled with charisma and entertainment value. Usually, I can appreciate getting twenty hours of tape if it's all of something that is particularly enjoyable. Material from great people like Steve Coogan, Jim Carey and the sort. They won't give a headache or any form of strain over a twenty hour period, they won't make me regret doing what I did and they won't make me ask myself if watching another one of their video promo's is going to cut my expected life-span by twenty-five percent. Now, to the average person, you have to wonder why I'm bothering to mention something of this sort. You have to wonder why I'm standing on a soap-box to talk about the length of a few videos and the length of time it would take to get through everything. This though, is what the story of today will focus on... and at the same time, it's exactly what is currently driving me from one end of the bridge to the very edge of the other side. The story begins in my apartment, a small and luxurious pad that I'd purchased with the downpayment of another contract with another firm. It held a large chunk of my personal items, most of it contained in multiple brown boxes and crappy plastic crates. The majority of the gear had been dropped off by removal people, shifting the items from storage at my sister's home. I took a few steps into the apartment and looked around momentarily, soaking up a view that I had not seen for many nights and many moons. 'It feels good to be home... it feels good to be in the comfort of my own living arrangement.' I said out aloud, talking to myself in the way that any normal human being would to another. On paper, it'd make me look like something of a psycho but without anyone else around, I don't think it really mattered. At least, nobody told that it mattered if they actually were snooping around the place and the sort. I trudged through the packed hallway, the walls bare, parcels and boxes all over the place. The smile on my face was warm, almost appreciative of the things that were just strewn everywhere. This was of course, until I'd made it into the deeper confinements of the living room, where a nice stack of labelled videos were sitting. 'What would these be?' I asked myself, wondering what videos had found themselves into my apartment. Picking them up one at a time, I began to read the labels, my smile changing from a look of happiness to an expression of sorrow and depression. Apparently, during my time trying to find a way to get around Brick's enormous frame, an individual by the name of J.C Diamond had decided to cram forty-eight hours of his life onto an entire stack of videos. To his credit, each of the videos had been nicely presented and given something of a 'decent' label job. Hell, I bet Disney would even be impressed at this video packaging. 'Impressive...' I said to myself, picking up the stack of tapes; moving them to the TV/VCR combo that'd been previously plugged in, assumingly by the people that'd brought all my stuff to this place originally. I browsed through the tapes with a curious interest, looking for whatever could be identified as the first of this mini-saga of life footage. 'Hmm, this would appear to be the first tape...' I thought to myself, dropping the other tapes to the floor before pushing this 'first' tape into the VCR. A few moments passed as the tape was read and following that short break, the screen filled with the image of JC and presented his life to me in a number of various stages. As the tape played through, I stepped back and plopped myself onto a leather chair, allowing myself to sit back and chill as the video footage played from beginning to end. Immediately following this, my cell phone began to dance around and freak out like the little techno-whore that it is. Reaching deep into the back pocket of my pants; which wasn't easy, considering the fact I was sitting down and everything; I managed to pull out the technological wonder. Thrusting it downward as though it were a morpher of a mighty GaoRanger, the flap of the phone opened and I raised it to my ear, pressing the green telephone button to accept the incoming call. 'Hello?' I asked, sounding curiously interested again. I was something of a simple soul, curious with anything that I couldn't give a definite answer to. The voice on the phone remained silent at first, their deep inhaling and exhaling being the only hearable things. 'Not exactly the best way to start conversation' I said to myself, thinking it'd be best if I didn't use that one line at this moment in time. '... Excuse me?' they responded, sounding a little aggressive and forward with their tone of voice and selection of vocabulary. 'Would you happen to be the one they call Rich Sherwin?' the voice stated, asking if I was this sexy beast of a wrestler called 'Rich Sherwin'. 'I'm sorry but Rich Sherwin isn't here at the moment...' I replied in a very squeaky and false voice, the intention behind this being that I wanted the individual on the other end of the phone to simply hang-up and get on with their own little tardy life as though they were just another statistic. 'Rich Sherwin isn't in at the moment?' the individual on the other end of the line asked, reiterating the prior point that I'd made about me not actually being around to take the call. Answering the posed question with a solemn 'mmm-hm', the being across the line continued, 'Well, could you please get Mr Sherwin to call me back at some point in the near future? I'm just calling to ask for his opinions on all the tapes that we sent over with JC Diamond all over. I figured it'd be good for him to do a little research into the lifestyle and the habits of his opponent.' I stopped and waited for a few moments, pondering whether to let it be known that I was actually here or not. I gazed at the screen, watching pointless scenes from the life of one leg of the competition on Evolution and figured that I may as well bring it to light now. 'Oh look, Mr Sherwin has just arrived home.' I said, the same mockingly-female tone being used to decieve and dupe the individual in question... whomever they should be. Pounding my feet on the floor a few times, I tried to make it seem as though I was coming across to answer the phone. 'Hello?' I said, thrusting the reciever back up to my head, my cool look complementing the 'cool' accent used. 'Mr Sherwin, I'm going to assume that you've been able to sit down and watch all of the tape's that I've been able to send you?' they asked, sounding a little too anxious for my own liking. Nodding somewhat and responding with a 'Yes', I could hear a sudden move of enjoyment on their end of the line. The voice took a few moments to calm down from the rousing applause but I soon heard the next part of the conversation. 'Mr Sherwin, I was wondering if you could quickly take a few moments of time to talk about the footage that you've been able to see. JC Diamond has something of a reputation and from all the videos that he'd originally posted to GW, perhaps you've found something that could be useful for your bout?' I looked around my immediate area momentarily, trying to think of a few things to say that would neither leave me looking a fool or a complete and utter moron. A few one-liners came to mind but there was nothing that could be deemed worthy for use in a situation such as this. Regardless, I tried to press forth, drawing the conversation out with some minor noises but eventually, I found exactly what I wanted to say in response. 'Well, whilst I've been able to look through the videoes of this athlete, I have yet to conclude if I should really be worried about something like this. I mean, in all truth and honesty, it looked as though the guy could have been having a family lunch as opposed to really concerning himself with the contest that'll be coming on Evolution. One mention? Just a single mention of two people that are likely going to make or break his career? Don't you find it just a little odd that in all of those waking moments, Mr 'Just Cranky' is more concerned with his insignificant personal life as opposed to his very significant professional life?' The questions that I'd thrown forward were shaped like daggers, thrashing and tearing through anything and everything that just happened to be in the way. For a while, the voice at the other end of the line was dead, an echo of shock being the sentiment carried from their reciever to my cell. 'Well, JC does have something of a very busy life... wouldn't you say so?' came the eventual reply, an attempt to justify the lack of interest attempted to be displayed by whoever was on the other end. 'A very busy life? You appeared to have sent me a mass amount of tapes, none of which actually coming to help with my bout against the guy on Evolution. How can you people expect me to do well if all you're actually doing is sending tapes of stuff that is probably going to help with kitchen care rather than ring welfare.' I answered in an angry tone, very unimpressed at the sheer lack of quality that had been compressed into all of these videos. I knew that I was taking a BIG risk with my poison words, espcially when compared to the fact that WE know I only just managed to look at the first five minutes of the video prior to the call. 'Not one of the taped contained anything that you'd be able to use against JC Diamond when it comes to your contest on Evolution? You mean to tell me that I took those tapes from the mighty offices of GW and you're not even able to use a second of footage from one of them?' the individual at the other end replied, their tone of voice beginning to fill with bewilderment and amusement. I almost felt compelled to laugh and joke about how they'd wasted their time... but for whatever reason, I really didn't want to seem like some kind of arrogant bastard. 'Look, I can appreciate what you have done... well, what you have tried to do for me. I can appreciate the fact that you're just trying to inform another athlete of what to look out for in contests with certain people... but unfortunately, twenty or so hours of 'fly on the wall' material that is simply dramatic garb, it's not information that is going to help me out. It's not information that is going to honestly help me win... is it?' I replied, trying to sound as cool, calm and under control as possible. To say the least, I wasn't happy at the way that I'd got all of these videos... but at the same time, now extremely proud that I didn't have to sit through so many hours of the exact same thing. '... I'm sorry.' they answered, hanging up the phone at the end of the line moments later. I closed the flap of the phone at my end and dropped the device to the floor, a stern look beginning to spread across my precious face. My previous attempt to discover the perfect plan to rid myself of Brick was nowhere close to being completed and my attempt to watch the 'game' of JC Diamond and discover his true words, was nowhere close to even starting. Evolution was all of three days away and without any information, I'd be walking into another match blindfolded. The scene faded into the depths of darkness, my quest for information still dominating the movement of my life.
