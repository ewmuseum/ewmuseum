Going Hollywood: The Script

8 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 8, 2005 The sound of the private jet’s engines filled the air as it was boarded by its only passenger, Global Wrestling superstar Mike Biddle. Once inside the cabin Mike removed his dark sunglasses and smiled as he took a sip from the martini that had waited for him. For the first time in months he was in a legitimate good mood. Despite the fact that he had lost to Dawn MacFarlane the previous evening the brash young man was of the opinion that his slump was about to come to an end. He had allied himself with Leinad Soma, a partnership that was certain to bring certain benefits to Biddle. He had also learned that he had been entered in a small tournament to crown the next number one contender of the World Championship. The greatest contributor to his newfound mood was the movie contract he had recently signed, he had not yet read the script but the mere fact that his face would be viewed on theater screens across the country brought him great joy. All he had to do now was sit back and wait to be showered with success. As the jet began its ascent skyward Biddle buckled himself to his seat and reached into his carry-on bag for the script to the movie. He crossed his right leg over his left and began to read. ***** With her beautiful nymph-like features and slender body she was more than just another face in the crowded airport. The blue jeans she wore appeared to be painted on, her red tube top left little to the imagination, her make-up was flawlessly applied, and each strand of her blond hair strategically placed. Many men gave her more than just a glance as they passed her by but she paid them no mind, there was only one man for her and she patiently waited for him. Her name was Hillary Watson and she waited for her unrequited love, Mike Biddle. She had not seen him since the evening of his wedding to Lisa Jameson. He had promised to call her but he must have been so busy that he had forgotten. So she had decided to surprise him at the airport when he returned from his weekly trip to Las Vegas. She had met up with his limousine before the driver had left, and she planned on accompanying Biddle home. The wait seemed like an eternity but Mike finally arrived at the exact time the limo driver had said he would. When she saw him her light blue eyes lit up and she frantically waved at him as she shrieked his name. When Mike laid his eyes upon her he heaved a sigh and his shoulders slumped, naturally Hillary was too stupid to notice his dissatisfaction and embarrassment. She ran through the crowd and when she reached him she threw her arms around his waist and gently cooed, “I’ve missed you so much Butter Muffin!” “Good for you,” Mike said as he pushed Hillary aside, his mood obviously not as good as it was when he boarded the jet. Hillary ignored Mike’s distinct displeasure of her presence and wrapped her arms around his left arm and walked with him outside. When they reached the limo Mike shook her off his arm and removed the yellow Hawaiian shirt he wore in favor of the white one he wore underneath. Hillary then followed him into the limo. When he was seated he removed his sunglasses and for the first time Hillary noticed that he was displeased. “Is there something wrong?” She asked as she put her hand on his shoulder. He glared at her. “Nothing that concerns you,” he snapped at her. “Why the hell are you even here?” “I just wanted to see you?” she replied, “I’ve missed you.” Mike smirked slightly, he knew what she was after and was more than willing to give it to her but it would have to wait; business before pleasure and all. “How about this,” he said, “you can wait for me in the bedroom while I go and talk business with William.” Mike finished his sentence with a playful wink that made Hillary’s heart melt. Hillary did not need to give a verbal reply, her goofy smile and the fact that her face turned a bright shade of red was answer enough for Mike. Mike looked out the window and saw that the limo was near William’s office. Mike pressed the button that rolled down the mirror that separated the passengers from the driver and told the driver to stop at William’s office. Moments later the limousine pulled up to curb and came to a stop. Mike turned to the driver and gave him these instructions, “Take the young lady back to the house and then come back here and wait for me.” Biddle then turned to Hillary and said, “Don’t start without me.” He gave her a quick kiss on the lips and exited the vehicle with the movie script in his hand. He flung the door of William’s office building open and glared at the secretary, Miss Moneypenny, with the fury of a thousand supernovas. “Is he in?” Mike asked with a snarl. “Of course he is,” Miss Moneypenny stated with a disinterested tone as she stared at the clock on the wall. Mike took that as his cue to storm into William’s office. He kicked the door open, an action that startled the older man a great deal. “Are you trying to give me a heart attack lad?” William asked with a chuckle. “Is this some kind of sick joke?” Mike yelled as he slammed the script on William’s desk. “What’s wrong with it?” William asked with an arched eyebrow. “Have you even read it?” Mike shot back. “Of course I have lad,” William answered, “what exactly is your problem with it?” Mike took a deep breath and sat down on one of the chairs in front of William’s desk and calmly stated his case. “I watched that show when I was a kid and the only thing that script had in common with the show was the names of the characters.” “I know,” William said with a devilish smile. “It’s like this lad,” he said as he rose from his seat and leaned over the desk, “once this film is released people will flock to it and come back disgusted.” “And how is that supposed to help my career?” Mike asked with an incredulous voice. “They’ll all be talking about you lad,” William said with a bemused tone. “The more they talk the more your star will shine.” Even Mike couldn’t argue with William’s logic, the greatest moments of his career came in the midst of public controversy. A smile crept upon his lips and the good mood that he had earlier in the day was restored. “When does filming start?” Mike asked with a cocky smirk. William sat back down and leaned back in his chair. “Next week you are to meet with the rest of the cast and go through the script together. Then you will be fitted for your wardrobe and I assume filming will begin afterwards,” William said. “Excellent,” Mike said as he rose from his seat and grabbed his copy of the script. He shook William’s hand and exited the office and made his way outside. William called after him, “Don’t even worry about the movie this weekend lad, and concentrate on taking your first step to becoming Champion of the World!” “Of course I will,” Mike said with a wave as he let the door close behind him. The limousine waited for him outside, he entered the vehicle and ordered the driver to get the house as quickly as possible’he needed to celebrate. -To Be Continued-
