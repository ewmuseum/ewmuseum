Histories #11 (DR RP#1)

20 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 102004 I circled around Brock. His body swung slightly from the chain that held his body aloft. The back of his pants were wet and dark from where he had defecated himself. Running down his back were the remnants of Zero’s Popsicle trick. His face was starting to turn a darkened red color from being hung upside-down. Please? Begging? Help me? Pleading? It had all come to this. I picked up the control box and thumbed the button to lower Brock. Why Brock? They were going to kill me. Why didn’t you tell me? They were watching. And listening. Brock was laying on the ground now breathing heavily. I undid the chains at his feet and helped him up. I’m sorry, Wind. So am I. I slammed my right fist into his head, coming down across his left temple. Brock crumpled into a heap on the floor. Unconscious, but alive? “for the time being. ***** I cracked the smelling salts underneath his nose. Wakey, wakey. His eyelids slowly opened. “Wha” He looked around nervously. What happened? You collapsed. So I put you on this table. He struggled around for a little bit. Why can’t I move? Because you’re strapped down. Do I want to know why I’ve been strapped down. It’s a personal thing, actually. I’ve found, when possible, it’s easier to kill someone when they’re strapped down. It cuts down on the squirming factor. WHY ARE YOU GONNA KILL ME?!”!” I slapped him hard across the face. Do I really need to honor that with a response? Tears began to well up in his eyes. No. Good. From the table I picked up a cup and a paintbrush. Have you ever been to Jamaica, Brock? He gave me a confused look. What? Da islands, mon. Jamaica, asshole. Have you ever been? Yes. What did you do there? What do you think? I got high and got laid. I gave him an approving nod. I went a few years back, and did some work for a drug lord down there. I dipped the paintbrush into the cup and pulled it back out. The drug lord was a ruthless man named King Willie. The brush dripped thick red liquid back down into the cup. Besides running a lucrative drug empire, King Willie was also a feared VooDoo priest. I started drawing odd characters on Brock’s naked chest. What’s that? Blood. Now, King Willie was so impressed with my work, that he showed me an old VooDoo ritual. Brock’s eyes started to grow wider with fear. He taught me how to steal a man’s soul before killing him. He started to struggle against the restraints. Which is what I am doing to you now. I dipped the brush back into the cup and shook off the excess blood. And often in his secret soul he prays to sleep through barren periods . Now at last word I bring, witnessed by every dead and living thing. With thy blood, and thy blade, rest may now be obtained. I set the cup of blood down and picked up the knife on the table next to me, holding it up for Brock to see. Sleep child. Feel no more joy. Feel no more pain. Drift throughout eternity. I maneuvered the blade to his left side, and thrust it deep within his chest, up to the hilt. Brock gasped a deep breath. When I removed the blade, I could hear a slight hissing noise. Air seepage from the lung. I moved the blade to Brock’s right side, and buried it hilt deep once again. Finally, I took up the blade and rammed it straight down into his heart, leaving the knifed lodged there. And with the opening of the gateways, flee spirit, flee soul, escape into the confines of the weapon that freed you. I watched. I waited. There was no glowing light. There was no unearthly aura from the body. I was highly disappointed. If it actually, worked, I would’ve liked to of seen a show. But then again, perhaps Willie was smoking too much of his own ganja.
