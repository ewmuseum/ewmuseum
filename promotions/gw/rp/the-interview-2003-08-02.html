The interview

2 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/2/03 The scene opens inside of a hotel. The hotel is rather small and run down. The outside of the building is covered in vines with shabby green pannel peaking through at different spots. There is a single door that leads to the inside. The cameraman is standing at the front desk. No clerk behind the counter. A large man with a scruffy beard, white tanktop which is spotted with stains, and bald walks out of the small bathroom behind the cameraman and walks around behind the desk. 'BURP' Can I help you?' 'Uhh...no, I'm waiting for someone that's all.' 'Why you need that camera then?' 'I'm meeting this person for an interview? Why do you care?' Just then the door swings open and Star walks into the room. She is wearing a long silver over coat, buttoned all the way to the top. A light blue scarf is wrapped around her neck and hangs down over her shoulders. She has on a pair of black sunglasses. Her face is red from the coldness outside. Her black streaked blond hair hangs down and rests lightly on the light blue scarf that hangs over her shoulders. Without saying a word or motioning for the cameraman, Star turns around and walks right back out the door. The cameraman quickly follows her. She walks down the sidewalk along side the hotel, passing numerous hotel room doors along the way. She then turns and walks up the two flights of stairs that lead her and the cameraman to the second story. Star walks past four more rooms before stopping in front of room 45. She opens the door and walks inside. The cameraman enters the room through the opened doorway. He quietly shuts the door as Star removes her over coat, scarf, and sunglasses. A pair of hip hugger blue jeans and a grey sweater is now the articles of clothing covering up the blond bombshell. 'You were late.' Star doesn't even acknowledge the camera as she begins to rummage through a navy blue gym bag. 'Frankly I don't really care if you were late or not, but Ice is the type of guy who likes things done when he asks them to be done. He doesn't like others to compromise his schedule, and routine.' As Star finishes her sentence the door opens and Ice, in a long black trench coat, walks through the door. He takes off his coat and throws it onto the bed. It is now seen that Ice is wearing a pair of black jeans, his brand new black t-shirt, with large white block letters across the chest that read 'WELCOME TO MY WORLD'. His black streaked blond hair is pulled back slighly. Ice gives Star a quizzicle look and makes his way towards her. 'Speaking of the devil. How are you hun?' Star smiles as Ice leans forward and gives her a kiss. He reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a small black book. Star looks down at it and her eyes get wide. She looks back up at Ice and smiles. Quickly she runs into the bathroom slamming the door. Ice slips off his shoes, and sits down on the end of the bed. Ice looks up at the cameraman. 'Ah how nieve the feeble minded can be. However it does seem I am starting to get noticed just a little bit, at least by a few. I tried to come out and help out Ice, but missed and hit him instead of Deacon. Then that little punk had the nerve to slap me when I was trying to make ammends. Jesus Christ! You sound like a freaking school girl bitching about some girl stealing her date for the school dance. Get your pink panties out of the bunch they are in, stop bitching, and do something about it, if you don't like how I make ammends.' Ice stands up and walks over to the bathroom door. He reaches down and pulls out a pair of blue gym shorts from his black gym bag and tosses them on the bed. He then bends back down and reaches into the bag again, pulling out a tan shirt. Ice tosses it on the bed as well and begins to speak once again. 'You see, it is that which I am talking about. Brick talks the big talk, thinks he is big sh!t, but needs and wants the comfort of his GW boys having his back, should his mouth write a check his ass can't cash. It is guys like Brick, who feel the need to make a name for themselves by running off at the mouth, yet biting off more than they can chew at the same time. It is guys like Deacon Holmes, who sneak by life, because someone is always there helping them along the way, yet he speaks like he is the one who did it all himself. That type of ignorance, and that type of incompatence is exactly what distgusts me. Boys, I want you to get something straight. The fun and games are over. I was playing in the beginning but the mood has now changed. My words are not of chips on my shoulder, or of an ego unwilling to fight this battle. My words are not to be taken lightly. Because with my words I fortell the future which you all behold. With my words I speak to each and every one of you, and warn you of your fate that will be. I hope you all continue to be so nieve. I hope you all continue to be so ignorant. Because my job will only get easier the more you fight hearing the truth in my words.' The bathroom door opens and Star walks out wearing a ravishing purple silk nightgown. Ice smiles at the sight of her beauty. Star blushes as she grabs her coat. Ice opens the door and grabs the cameraman before heading out the door. 'Master of the Mat 4. A ladder match. Cruiserweight title on the line. Max Roswell, been there, beat him. Do I really need to say anything more about him? I think not. Jay Vyper. OOOOO.... SCARY!!! Mr. Vyper, I don't know you, frankly, I don't care to know you. However, I will give you this little warning, stay out of my way, or you will get hurt. Now onto my final opponent in the match, Jin Akiyera. The new rookie phenominon that is hitting the GW Cruiserweight division. I'm man enough to give credit where credit is due. Jin, you are one hell of a competitor. You do have more guts than brains, but that's pretty much it about you. So fellas, let's get this straight before you all get hurt. That belt, by all means, should already be mine. Tyler Pierce decides to no show. He decides to go and take care of some family matters, and forfiets the match. Then the bitch comes down to the ring, and says that even though the rules say that I should be the Cruiserweight Champion, she takes it upon herself to say I don't deserve it that way and need to prove myself. Well Ms. McCoy, you want me to prove myself, show I deserve it? Then the blood is not on my head or my hands. It will start in the ladder match. Then it will move to the actual Master of the Mat Tournament. Where first I will take out my friend Kid Flash. One by one, Brick, by Brick. I will put each and every competitor that gets in my way, in their proper place. Summer is fading away. Those warm days are slowly growing colder, and in time there won't be denying the facts, or the truth. Ms. McCoy, GW, the world as you know it is changing. Master of the Mat 4 you will find out, that this is no longer the place you have come to know, and the place you have grown accustom to. An evil smirk grows across Ice's face as the scene fades to black.
