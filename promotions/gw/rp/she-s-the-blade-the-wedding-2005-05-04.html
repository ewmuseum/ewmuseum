She's The Blade: The Wedding

4 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 4, 2005 Hillary Watson’s Diary May 1, 2005 Today I met the person that sent me the clipping of Mike’s wedding announcement. Her name is Alice and she works at the local paper. She’s been writing about Lisa for a long time now and told me everything she knows about her. After talking with her I knew that I was doing the right thing by trying to put a stop to this wedding. I’ve been following my Butter Muffin around lately too, he hasn’t noticed me though. He doesn’t act like himself anymore. Normally he acts like an arrogant jerk (which turns me on!) but now he acts like someone completely different’it’s like he’s hypnotized or something! I think this is why he’s been losing so many matches lately! Well, I better go get some sleep’tomorrow is a big day! Lisa Jameson’s Diary May 2, 2005 Today is the day! By this afternoon I will be Mrs. Lisa Biddle! Not only that but I’ll be a whole lot wealthier too! I’m also confused as to what to do now too. Should I go with my original intentions and stay with Mike and get that stupid paper off my back? Or should I divorce him after a month and take all his money? I’ll probably divorce him; I’ll have enough money to buy that stupid paper afterwards! The ceremony is in a few hours, so I better stop writing and get ready. That Afternoon? The Chapel of the Bells was filled to the brim with Mike and Lisa’s closest friends. Mike stood by the altar, he wore a black tuxedo. Beside Mike was his best man, James Loewen. James nudged Mike to grab the groom’s attention. “You sure you want to do this man?” James asked. “Sure I do,” Mike replied with an unsure tone. “You did get her to sign the pre-nuptial’right?” James asked with a low voice. Mike turned red and tugged at his collar. “No,” he replied with a nervous chuckle. James reached over and slapped Mike on the side of the head. “You’re a moron,” James said, “You know damn well that she only wants you for your money!” Mike turned and glared at his best man. “That’s not true,” Mike growled. James chuckled. “You’re so whipped it’s depressing,” he said. As Mike was about to reply he was quieted as the organist began to play “The Wedding March”, the music that signaled the entrance of the bride. Everyone in attendance turned towards the large doors in the back and waited for Lisa to enter the room. There was a large applause as Lisa walked through the large doors wearing her long, luxurious, and white wedding dress. She was closely followed by her bridesmaids, all of whom wore baby blue low-cut dresses. The procession made its way to the altar and stopped the moment that Lisa stood beside Mike. The two looked at each other, Lisa smiled broadly under her veil and Mike returned her smile with a nervous smirk. The music stopped and the reverend performing the ceremony cleared his throat and began the festivities. “If it be your intention to take each other as husband and wife, you will manifest it by uniting your right hands,” the reverend said with a formal voice. Lisa held out her right hand, but Mike paused for a moment to think. However, Lisa just grabbed his hand and the ceremony continued. The reverend turned to Mike, “You now take this woman, whose hand you hold, to be your lawful wedded wife. Do you solemnly promise, before God and these witnesses, that you will love, honor, and cherish her; and that, forsaking all others for her alone, you will faithfully perform to her all the duties which a husband owes to a wife, so long as you both shall live?” Mike looked from the reverend, over to James. James shook his head, and mouthed “Just say no numb nuts!” to his friend. Mike took a big gulp and just as he was about to give his answer the large doors in the back swung open. Everyone in attendance turned to see what had caused the noise. A beautiful blonde woman with nymph-like features stood in the doorway. She wore a short strapless red dress with matching stiletto heels and heavy makeup on her face. Mike slapped his hand to his forehead when he recognized the woman as his former valet/girlfriend Hillary Watson. Hillary walked up the aisle and stopped in the middle of the room. “I’m sorry Mike,” she breathlessly said, “I can’t allow you to do this to yourself.” “Why the hell are you ruining my wedding?” Lisa asked with a furious voice, it was obvious that her face had turned a beet red under the white veil. “Because I’m saving my Butter Muffin from a mean bitch that only wants his money!” Hillary yelled at Lisa. Those that were gathered for the ceremony gave a mixed reaction of laughter and gasps at Hillary’s comments. A few even applauded. Lisa turned to Mike, “You’re not going to listen to that’that’that’bitch are you?” she asked with a desperate tone. Mike heaved a sigh. “Well,” Mike began, “I must admit that I’ve had my suspicions’especially after you wouldn’t sign the pre-nuptial agreement.” “See?” Hillary called from the aisle. Mike held up his hand to quiet Hillary down as he continued to look at Lisa. “In fact,” he said, “I don’t care if you want my money or not?” Lisa looked hopeful as she thought that Mike would ignore the ramblings of his old lover and sign his fortune over to the angelic brunette that stood beside him. Mike turned to the reverend, “To answer your question sir,” Mike took a deep breath before he finished his thought, “I DON’T!” Once again the crowd that had gathered for the ceremony gave a mixed reaction to Mike’s response. Lisa let out a furious scream and ran from the altar with tears in her eyes. As James watched this, he let out a great laugh and slapped Mike on the back. “Way to go man,” James said with a wide smile. With the show over the crowd began to disperse. Once everyone had left Mike still sat on the steps of the altar; a pleased smirk rested on his handsome face. He wasn’t alone for long; eventually Hillary came and stood before him. “So are you going to thank me?” Hillary asked with a goofy smile on her face. “No,” Mike replied, “I knew the whole time that she only wanted the money’I was going to say no anyway.” “Oh,” Hillary said. Mike rose from the steps. To Hillary he said, “Come on, I’ll take you back to my place and you can get out of that little dress.” Hillary excitedly followed him as he left. With Lisa Jameson out of the picture Hillary could only hope that Mike would be hers once again. -The End- Epilogue For the first time in months Mike’s head was clear and he was finally relaxed. He was also focused for the first time since he had won the Television Championship and was determined to end his six week skid. He had left for Las Vegas a day early and had spent his time training’a practice that made his manager, William Bastafar, very happy. Mike had not heard from Lisa since her exit from the chapel, but he had heard that she had moved to Europe and had no plans of ever returning to Los Angeles. Hillary had once again become a part of his life, but not as a girlfriend. Mike was amazed that she could even forgive him for leaving her at the altar all those months ago. She took any chance she could to try to prove her love to him’something that he took advantage of as often as possible. Belle, the saucy French maid, had been re-hired and just about everything in Mike’s life had returned to normal. The only thing Mike had left to do was finally win a match and get the title shot that was rightfully his. Credit for the Reverend’s words during the ceremony go to Edward T.
