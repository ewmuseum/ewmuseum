Desperate Awakenings: Chapter 1

7 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/7/04 Prologue: When you awake in the morning does your eyes feel covered in a mask? Are they hard to open as you look out the window to see where the sun is or if it’s raining? Do you smile when you view in the mirror? Does life feel like a struggle? Do you fight to live? Or does living come as a thing you oversee day-in and day-out? In the mind of Jesse Fristik he was underestimated, un-respected, and un-cared for. However when he twisted his mind from the darkness to the light he began to remember what he was doing and now past the darkness his eyes can stray upon his goal… To become the best of any man to ever step in a squared circle. But will his goal be too much for him to surpass? Can a man overcome a dark path of misery and betrayal to finally come out into the world whole again? Find out… Story Begins: His eyes looked into the mirror glaring a deep tired look as he brushed his hair out of his face with the swipe of his hand. Looking as though ill, he quickly turned an threw up into the toilet next to the mirror and sink. Jesse was in the state of a hangover, his mind slower than normal and his motions the same. Pacing himself, he wiped his face off with a towel and threw it into the dirty hamper. Then turning on the faucet he stared in the mirror again as he took his hands and cupped a bowl of water with them. Flipping his hands towards his face the water splashed on his cheeks and forehead running down the cracks of the skin and slowly edging towards his goatee as he bent down into the sink and spit. Jesse looked like a man who’d been in a fight and afterwards partied. For his face was slightly bruised on one side and the corner of his eye nearest the temple was purple. As he stood there glaring into the mirror he shook his head. Turning around he grabbed the washrag and wiped the cold water on his face off. Leaving the bathroom he heads to the bedroom where his clothes have a path leading to his bed starting with his shoes, then his socks, afterwards his jeans, then his shirt, and finally his bed where a nice young sweetheart of blonde with brown rooted hair lies asleep with her right arm being exposed because of how she sleeps. Jesse smiles as he puts on some clothes and cleans up the mess. His curtains block the sun; only until he lets it in with an opening of the left side curtain, letting it cease the room so he can see his closet. The other curtain he leaves alone so the woman isn’t bothered. Jesse gets a shirt on and leaves the room letting the woman sleep. He heads to the kitchen and turns on the coffee, but doesn’t drink any for he is not a caffeine person. Instead Jesse pulls a bottle of water from the fridge and guzzles it down as he sits down at the table and tries to awaken from his hangover. As he goes to open up the newspaper the woman who was lying in his bed comes gliding across the kitchen floor to where Jesse is and kisses him on the cheek. Wearing a small pink shirt that is cut so her belly button is seen and a pair of pink Victoria’s Secret underwear, she is nearly naked. Jesse kisses her back and continues on opening up the newspaper to the sports section. The woman grabs some coffee for herself and sits down next to him at the table. She smiles as the sun shines through the door nearest them and melts the door outside. A smile so bliss that if you were to try and yell at her you’d punish yourself afterwards for doing so because of it’s beauty and it’s essence. Jesse slowly moving his hand towards hers and she doing the same, the two meet and they both smile even larger. Finally Jesse puts the paper down and speaks. ‘Oh… it’s such a beautiful morning. You are so beautiful… Ah, it’s so refreshing to see you here in front of me, and yet fulfilling.’ ’Last night was beyond life to me. I can’t believe I gave you that bruise though. Are you okay?’ ’This little thing? I’m good. I’ll have to make up some reason for having it with the boys at work, but I’m good.’ ’Just say you were wrestling with a much more aggressive person than they are,’ she giggles and then kisses him. ’So… You don’t have a problem with me wrestling? Because it’s my job and I don’t want to have you run off with some other guy because I’m not here all the time.’ ’I’m fine with it as long as we can do what we did last night every night! Anyway, who else would look as good as you?’ she giggles again. Her blue eyes are like ice, for they freeze your mind to focus on it. ’Well that’s good to know, it makes me happier than when I lost last week to Ice. Luckily I have a chance to win my International Title back in this tournament starring the best of Global Wrestling.’ ’I thought you tied with Ice? And don’t you face Mark Makonie and Sergei this week?’ ’Yeah that’s who I face and I did tie, but it’s more of a loss when you don’t win you know what I mean? Oh by the way, I got you tickets for front row to watch me during the match. I’m still talking with people about getting you to be my manager like you asked, but for now it’s still in the air.’ ’Great! I really want to be apart of your life Jesse, and you know… Since we just met, this will be a great way for me to get to know you more. I totally support you, and this will be a great experience for us both.’ ’Yes it will be Nikki. I mean it’s been so long since my last…’ Jesse stops halfway through the sentence for he doesn’t want to say it, and changes the topic, ’Well, it’s going to be great having you next to me and supporting me as I do what I love. It’s something I can only hope happens all the time and is what I hope to do for the rest of my life, you know?’ ’I understand completely. It’s all about learning things Jesse, and we both will learn about life this way. I can’t hope but to become a better person because of you. You’re like a new dawn to me, for everything is emerging out in front of me and is new. The light I saw this morning was so beautiful I almost went back to sleep. You make my life more peaceful Jesse, and I hope I do the same for you.’ ’You do Nikki, and I love you.’ With those words she is stunned, the three words every woman wishes to hear, and she being taken off balance completely by them. ’I… I… love you… too,’ she says so stunned that the words me out piece by piece. Jesse leans over off his chair and onto his knee slowly realizing he has to do it because he has already said the words, and you can see in his eyes he wants to. He touches her hand and gently takes it from the table and puts it near his hand where he kisses it. Then with a breath of fresh air he opens his mouth to speak. But the phone rings and Jesse is broken of his concentration and moment to do what he wants. He looks at her and she looks back, and then the phone rings again. He shakes his head angrily as he gets up and grabs the phone. ’Hello?!’ The person speaks with Jesse and then he replies. ’Nikki, it’s for you. How does your boss know this number?’ ’My boss is my girlfriend Clair. I told her last night I’d be at your place before I left the bar with you. I probably have to go to work.’ Nikki talks to Clair on the phone and after a few minutes hangs up and tells Jesse she has to leave. Going to the bedroom she gets changed and heads out for work. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** There in Jesse’s bedroom he lies on the bed staring at a picture of his late wife. Her eyes a diamond pair of blue angels, and her cheeks pale but have a soft gentle nature. Jesse’s eyes begin to seep tears. He looks at the picture and speaks. ’I love you… But now, I’ve got to move on. Hopefully, you understand… I’ve got to move on. I’ve held back for so long, that now if I don’t move on I will only be a chain that rusts in the rain as it holds on to the vault that has my past in it. I love you and will never forget you honey… Remember that please, because in here (Points to his heart), I wrestle, I live, and I care for you.’ Jesse puts the picture back in his closet where he also has a small box. He acquires a smaller box of a soft leather type shell on the outside. Opening the box slowly a ring is inside, and the tears he had shed before now drop down onto the ring. He wipes his eyes with his shirt sleeve before saying something. ’Julie… I gave you this ring a long time ago, and you told me if I ever fell in love again to give it to that woman. So in my heart when Nikki wears this ring, I’ll remember you, and I’ll live with her sharing two lives in on. God bless you…’ He closes the box and places it in his pants pocket before closing the closet. Wiping the tears from his face he sits back on the bed and looks out the window into the sunlight just lingering.
