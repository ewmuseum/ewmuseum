The Nightmare Files: Volume 2 Joshua Curtis Global Wrestling Promo #1713 Date: 10-22-04 Location: 'Connection' residence (Not where Joshua and Zack live) Early Morning Oh what a beautiful morning…well beautiful if you like gray skies, a downpour and oh yeah mad thunder and lightning! Welcome to what we will call “The residence”. This is where Juri Arisugawa, Tina Davis and Mizuno Ami live, work and play as they do their part to help spread the word of the “Old School” to the masses. Now today my pretties is an interesting day. Much like our last adventure with Joshua Curtis and Zack Johnson at least one of these ladies has been having problems with sleep and believe me when I tell you on this particular day it showed! “Juri-sempai?” Ami asked as she walked up to the former duelist from Ohtori Academy and noticed that the slightly older woman was moaning slightly in her sleep “Huh? Oh Ami-Chan what’s up hun?” Juri asked rubbing the sleep out of her eyes “Not much except I heard you making strange noises, so I decided to come and check on you.” The blue haired genius from Juuban Junior High told Juri as she sat next to her and rubbed the back of her hand across the duelists face. “Oh dear your burning up…here let me get an ice pack for you sweetie. You just lay back down and I’ll get everything ready.” The future doctor of the “Connection” told Juri and Juri was too weak to argue as she laid her head back down and closed her eyes but the problem with doing that is that is what “he” wanted her to do! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The story I am about to tell you is one that will make you question the integrity of a student council and for that matter that of a school. The scariest thing about this story is while it happened there are people that can’t talk about it because doing so would have resulted in their expulsion from the Academy of Ohtori! Here’s something to consider: School is an institution in learning correct? If that is the case than we should be more concerned with getting said education than getting “knocked up” right? Stay with me on this one… On a picturesque day in Hou’ou-shi, Holland at the Academy, Juri was with some members of the Student Council and noticeably absent from the group was the Student Council President in Touga Kiryuu but somehow I have a feeling we will be seeing him later on anyways. Now Juri was dressed in an outfit that was a little different than what we are used to seeing her in. Rather than her Student Council uniform Juri was in a light pink shirt, white miniskirt and a pair of Keds. Is this really all that different for her? Yeah but to go into why would be another like 6 pages in and of itself which is not what I think this is supposed to be about right? So on this day in particular its Juri Arisugawa, Miki Kaoru and….UTENA TENJOU?! If this isn’t a shock to you it kind of should be but let me give a real brief synopsis of this for ya: Juri has had her heart broken before and she is never one to share her emotions with people but one day while on a lunch break she had spotted the pink-haired duelist and asked her to come with her to a little shack that very few people knew about! How secret is it? I DIDN’T know about it until…I’ll save that for later ok? So Juri had decided to try and be open with her emotions one day and of course she tried doing it with the “Social Butterfly” of Ohtori Academy in Utena Tenjou and…it worked, don’t ask me how but it worked and the two are now closer than you can imagine! “So Juri” Utena began “any cute guys ask you to the Christmas Dance?” Utena asked and Juri lightly blushed under Utena’s gaze. “One guy and like four girls! Kozue, Shiori and two other girls but I forget their names right now…matter of fact I think you wanted to ask me but you chickened out!” Juri said as she playfully ribbed Utena about her shyness around her. “Well I would’ve but…” Utena stopped herself as she unfortunately had to remind herself that she was engaged to a girl named Anthy, Whom I don’t mind telling you is a darling of a girl and if you get to know her she’s not that “mysterious” as people make her out to be! “Utena you know that no one would care if you asked Juri out to the dance! Ok so you are engaged to be with Anthy but I know that you want out of that and well Juri is a sweetie!” Miki said and Juri turned 6 shades of red in like 45 seconds from the compliment! “I-I know that Miki but well…” Utena stammered trying to get out of the predicament but Juri pounced on it like a cat! “Aww Utena has a crush on me and she won’t admit it!” Juri said as again she ribbed Tenjou but this time Utena didn’t take kind to it and Juri was about to hear it for sure “STOP IT ARRISUGAWA! DO YOU HEAR ME STOP IT RIGHT NOW!” Utena screamed at Juri and no one had ever seen Juri’s eyes well up the way they did in front of Utena and to say that Utena felt as big as a micro machine would be the understatement of all time! Utena, realizing what she had done tried to apologize but got shoved away from a tearful Juri and Utena knew right then that she crossed the line but fixing it was going to be hard! About 15 feet away Joshua Curtis, a visitor of the Academy saw the incident and immediately ran up to Utena afterwards to do two things: Find out why Utena acted that way and give her a piece of his very fragile mind! “UTENA TENJOU!” Joshua screamed as he ran up to the situation “DO YOU HAVE ANY IDEA WHAT YOU JUST DID? THAT GIRL I THINK LOVES YOU TO DEATH AND YOU DID THAT?! YOU KNOW THAT SHE ONLY PLAYS WITH YOU LIKE THAT AND SHE MEANT NO HARM BY WHAT SHE SAYS…OOOH NOW I GOTTA GO FIX THIS!” Joshua said as he went on after Juri. Ok so it wasn’t so much to find out what happened as it was to berate Utena for yelling at Juri but I figured the former would bore the shit out of you ok! As Joshua went to go chase after Juri the fact that he couldn’t follow her because well she runs twice as fast as Joshua and Josh is pretty fast himself but anyways… “Excuse me sir but you didn’t happen to see an orange-haired young lady run past here did you?” Joshua asked a slightly shorter student and when that student gave him directions to the “Hideaway” Josh made the first beeline he could because if he knew Juri as well as he thought he did trouble was about to come find her. I hate it when he’s right! It turns out that someone had scoped out the facility and decided to lie in wait there for the little birdie to come home without mama… It worked! As Juri walked into the little room that very few people knew about a figure jumped out from the shadows and grabbed her in kind of double arm chicken wing and threw her on the couch in that room. Despite the protests of the girl the attacker jumped on the couch and using his full body weight pinned Juri to the piece of furniture and hoped that her struggles would cause her to blackout so he could have his way with her… It worked…unfortunately! As Joshua made his way over towards the figure he pulled off a black mask that covered the person’s face and of course you had to know it was Touga! “OUT NOW!” Is all Joshua could say and Touga was conscious enough to get his scrawny ass out of the room and Joshua then turned towards the unconscious Juri. “Juri I know you can’t hear me but listen ok? Utena is sorry for yelling at you and if she can make it over here she’ll be with you…oh shit look at what that fucker did!” Joshua exclaimed as while the attack looked harmless apparently Touga had to make some incisions on her body to make her pass out from some kind of pain but as luck would have it Joshua carried some stuff for emergencies like this! As Joshua took Juri into the adjacent room Utena was just making her way there and when she couldn’t find Juri on the couch she panicked if only for a second as she saw Joshua tending to Juri and keeping an eye on her. As Joshua turned to see Utena he didn’t say anything but he did brush Juri’s hair back and kissed her forehead softly before getting off of the bed and walking towards Utena. “She does care about you Utena…a lot and I think you should give her a chance and if you are worried about Touga I’LL handle ‘Pretty Boy’ you just take care of Juri and please don’t play with her emotions. One girl did that and she hasn’t full recovered from it yet.” Joshua cautioned as he walked out of the room and took one final look as Utena took her shoes and top off and snuggled next to her friend and what some argued at that time to be, her lover! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Present day… “Has she been like this all day?” Joshua asked as he sat with Juri’s head in his lap and stroking her face softly. “Since early morning Joshua and the moaning got worse not 45 minutes ago.” Tina told Joshua and the newest GW star knew that he was powerless to stop “him” from causing anymore pain today but he told the girls that he would be staying the night with them to keep an eye on Juri and if any more of these attacks hit anyone an I do mean ANYONE he wanted to know about it!
