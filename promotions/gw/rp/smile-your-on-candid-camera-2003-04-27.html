Smile, your on candid camera

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/27/03 His office at the school of pain was somewhat of a home away from home for Salaok. A place where he could escape the world without having to go home to those who would ask him about him. The School of Pain was his escape and what a place to escape to. The walls has long since been painted black with a slight tinge of ruby through them, causing the color to change as you moved around the room. His desk was and always had been the centerpiece of the office as it's large nature and hard dark red wood finish was something that people envied. The desk alone spoke for riches and power that no normal man could ever hold. And yet for Salaok, it was a day to day situation. This was his place. A place that he wished he could run the Global Wrestling Inc from but it would make him inaccessible which seemed to be a problem with some people. Not that he actually cared about what those other people thought, but was more due to the fact that he couldn't be bothered driving in and out of the damn GW Offices when ever he had a meeting. He would never have let those stiffs into the School. His fingers tapped the table through his large gothic rings, which covered all of his fingers and acting more like a metal glove than a set of rings. The fine detail on each ring was well crafted and many rumors had arisen about the rings. The mere fact that the rings used to be Johnathon's and now they rest on Salaok's fingers were enough to start people talking. But the rings were the only things that Salaok had been given by his older brother as the wide brimmed hat also sat on Salaok's table in the far right corner. It had been a while since he last wore the hat and that was during the time that Brian Welch was around. The pet crowd, which Johnathon had, still flies out to visit Salaok everyone and then, but never carries the news like Salaok wishes that it would. Johnathon had been missing for a long time now but it had happened many times before and Salaok simply didn't have time to waste on his brother who found ways of disappearing so that even The Harvester's themselves couldn't find him. With Johnathon not around however, it did make Salaok more powerful within The Lair and the power was showing. His business deals which saw him buy Global Wrestling Inc was simply one of the many tasks that he took to after his brother went missing. He sits at the table with his fingers tracing some of the lines in the wood, his eyes looking up at the cougar whose upper torso and head sits above his door. The day that he killed the beast was still fresh in his mind, as it would be the day that The Harvesters would reunite and begin to cause a world of hurt for those in their way. It would also be the day that the old leader of The Lair would pass away, leaving control to the brothers. He couldn't belive that he had waited too long to act. If he had done something a day or two eariler, things would have been right again. Deth would never have died, or at least would have been able to receive medical help to try and save him. But it seemed impossible until that day. Many had asked Salaok what was going through his head the day of the attack on Mephiston's castle and Salaok had never been able to give them a straight answer. The simple fact of the matter was that on that day, Salaok finally snapped. On that day, Salaok simply lost control and took everything into his own hands. He made sure that a fury like no other would engulf that castle and kill those who occupied it. But his crowning moment was when he finally had Mephiston's right hand man in a room alone. It wasn't enough for Salaok simply to let the man die a quick death. no, pain and suffering is what was needed on that day and Salaok delivered it with pin point accuracy. His sword slicing into every body part that could be seen, and then some that couldn't. And finally, after his head had been severed, Salaok took pride in carving the 'L' symbol into his enemy's forehead. On that day, Salaok proved why he was once The Harvester of Pain. But with The Harvester reunited, he would show the world why he had taken grown and took the Harvester name, former taken by his older brother. The Harvester of Fear. With all of the Harvester together again, hell would soon follow for those who stood in his way. One man had always gotten in the way of The Harvester's and still does so today. A man by the name of Brick. Many a time was it thought about as to how to effect Brick the most. And then it finally occurred to Salaok that with Brick, it was about attacking him in person, but attacking those around him and making his life a living hell. For it is one thing to be punished for what you have done, but it is a whole different story to know that those around you are being tortures for the choices that you have made in the past. Knowing that if it iwasn't for you, then your friends would never be in the situation of being stuck in a cage and having pieces of his body filled with pain, day after day. Reaching forward and grabbing the remote for the television and DVD mounted into the wall, he presses the small round red button which soon filled the screen with life and kicks the DVD into action. The screen takes a few seconds to warm up and start showing a clear picture, but when it does, it shows what looks to be a home video of a wedding. But this isn't just any old wedding as we see Brick with his family waiting at the front of the church for his sister to arrive. And then she does so. Out of a white old fashion limo she steps and her dress trails along after her. She is truely beautiful and she slwoly makes her way up the steps out the front of the entrance to Brick who wraps his arms around her and gives her a huge hug. A voice comes from behind the camera and it's tone gives it away straight off. 'Oh Brick, good to see that you can still relax and have fun. Your sister does look nice today. Such a shame that she will not look so......well, it could be put down as a shame that she will not look like this tonight.' The scene cuts to a little later on that day and everyone is in the church and seated, part from the important people up the front. Salaok who holds the camera pans it around the room, making sure to get everyone on film before turning it around to look at him. 'Stunning day, isn't it Brick? I went to such trouble to get here today. I brush my hair, cleaned my teeth, oh, and I had to bride my way in here. Seems as though you had a security company hired to make sure I don't turn up. Well Brick my boy, it is always best to make sure that the company you are hiring, doesn't actually work for me. If you had, I would have had to pay a lot more than what I did today.' Salaok grins before turning the camera back around to the wedding ramblings at the front. Salaok voice comes from behind the camera again. 'You see Brick, it doesn't matter what you do, I can and will always be able to find you. Always be able to get at you. And you know what's so much better than all of that? I now have every single one of your family and friends on tape in order to make my identifying of people easier. I know what they all look like Brick. The only question is, what on earth would I do to them?' The camera stays on the wedding events up the front of the church and then suddenly cuts to darkness. Slowly the camera starts to get light at the stop of the screen as it moves up on what looks to be a window. A bush is moved to the side by a hand behind the camera and we see Brick's sister and her new husband retiring to bed and beginning to take off their clothes. Salaok stops the DVD right there and then takes it back a few frames. He ejects it a few frames and then gets up out of his chair after grabbing a large yellow envolope. Knowing now exactly how far in to cue the DVD, he smile to himself as he walks over and removes it from the player. 'Well Brick, you and your family are sure going to love tomorrow night.' He seems almost to be enjoying himself as he speeks out loud to himself and seals the envelope. Tomorrow night would finally be the straw that breaks Brick's back if everything goes to plan. And Salaok couldn't be happier.
