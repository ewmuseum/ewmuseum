Afternoon exposure

19 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/19 Maximum Carnage has come and gone, and with it, my run as world champion. A severe double team by “The Aluminum Tabby Cat” Akodo Rokku and Ginger Akiyara culminating in a gigantic top rope double knee drop finale that left me kissing canvas spelled the end of my reign as the youngest world champion ever in Global Wrestling history. The impact of two knees crashing down across my torso from somewhere up in the lights left me with two cracked ribs, and an ever increasing whirlwind of incertitude. “Should I have wrestled twice in one night?”, “Did I celebrate too much backstage after winning the tag straps with Zero?”, “Did I train hard enough?” all these questions swirl around in my head, each revolving around a solitary central doubt, the eye of the storm if you will. “Am I good enough?” Now on any other day before now the automatic computer generated response formulated inside my head would be “Of course”. Even before such a ridiculous thought could be conceived. Since being signed to Global Wrestling at the end of March my confidence has swelled tremendously with each passing week as I’ve soundly beaten opponent after much larger and more experience opponent. Much to the dismay of those around me. But now it seems as if all my boasting has come back to haunt me as I’m left to wonder the question again, this time audibly, as if I truly don’t know. “Am I?” I whisper to myself in an inquisitive tone as I ease back into the soft leather of my Barco-lounger. Well, in actuality the chair is Zero’s... this being his house and all. But seeing as how I’m sitting in it and he’s not, it’s mine for the moment. So here I sit, blinds shut, wasting the day away in the darkened living room of John’s house while quickly approaching full blown inebriation and doing something I’ve never done before, doubt myself. I know I was the first person in the ring and immediately jumped into the fray with whomever else entered, but I just can’t seem to get over the fact that not only was I pinned, but I was also the first one eliminated. And to make matters worse, the loss of my title came at the hands of Jin Akiyara, a man who just last Evolution I practically murdered. I mean seriously, I matched him move for death defying, jaw dropping, hair raising, eye popping move, and then I got serious... It was evident, at least in my eyes that I overwhelmed him. In the end our match was ruled as a no contest due to outside interference from Akodo Rokku and Kid Flash, but he knew the score. Or at least I thought he did... until Maximum Carnage. After his performance in that main event I’m no longer so sure that even I know what it is. “Could he have been holding back?” I wonder to myself while considering our meeting at the last edition of Evolution. Impossible. He was trying just as hard as I was, if not harder. I could see it in his eyes, he wanted to win. We pushed each other to our limits, maybe even past them. And to be perfectly honest, I liked it. For the first time in my short career I’ve come across someone just as fearless as I am. To say the least this comes as a shock, and the loss of my title. The same title that I tore a hole in anyone who dared step in my way to get a shot at. The same title that I almost died trying to win in my first encounter with Brick, one in which we both fell from atop the Globaltron and plummeted through a reinforced steel stage. The same one that I choked the life out Brick for after driving his head into the pavement. The same one that I’m getting a second chance at next Evolution. And if history has taught us anything, it’s that you can’t afford to give me second chances. Obviously Global Wrestling has yet to learn this lesson. This coming Monday I will be looking to reclaim my spot atop the pecking order as world champion and prove that I am truly the new face of Global Wrestling. “I can’t lose.” I murmur to myself as I lean forward in the chair, causing the foot rest to once again collapse back into the front of the chair. Normally when such a thing comes out of my mouth it’s said with an air of confidence, but this time it comes as more of a warning. I can’t afford to lose, not again, and especially not with what is rightfully mine once again on the line. Once fully upright I reach out onto the table in front of me and take a half emptied bottle of Yukon Jack in my grasp. As I lift the bottle up from the surface of the table a small square of moisture can be seen where little droplets of water cause by the frost melting from the base of the bottle have begun to accumulate. I quickly look over both shoulders to make sure John is nowhere in the vicinity before wiping the wetness from the table with the cuff of my hoodie, as he would most likely have a fit if he thought a ring were going to develop on his table. After opening the bottle I slowly fill the lonely shot glass sitting next to where the bottle previously sat. As I stare into the thick brown liquid filling the translucent black shot glass with the silhouette of a zero lined in gold the smell of what I am about to ingest fills my nostrils. A less than enjoyable aroma to say the least, so horrid in fact that most anyone else who has smelled the same thing would put the glass down. I simply open my mouth and toss the shot into the back of my throat. That much closer to intoxication, I gently set the bottle back down on top of the table. This time on a coaster. A burning sensation slowly works it’s way down my throat then grinds to a scorching halt at the center of my chest as I pull my hood up over my head and lean back once again, now feeling sufficiently buzzed. A deep sigh rumbles up from the pit of my stomach, squeezes through my esophagus, and makes a mad dash for open air through my partially pursed lips before echoing through the large empty house. I chuckle to myself a little at the faint noise repeating over and over. Just then, nature screams. I try to ignore it as am in quite the opposite of the mood of someone who wants to get up, but apparently not only does nature know where I am, but that bitch has my cell phone number as well. I lean back further into the chair and pull both of by legs into the air as I press both my palms flat onto the armrests. Once I feel I’m in a sufficient position, I thrust all of my weight forward in an attempt to use the chair to aid in the task of catapulting me to my feet. To my relative amazement, it works, and if only for a moment, I feel like I’m flying. But then again, that could easily be attributed to the fact that it’s two thirty in the afternoon and I’ve been up drinking all afternoon. Eh, oh well, it’s still fun... while it lasts. As I coast forward into what on any other day would be a five point landing I find that my landing gear has yet to be deployed and that both the pilot and co-pilot are in the midst of a drunken stupor at the helm. If it weren’t for those damn unions I’d fire both their asses. And if I weren’t hurtling toward the ground right now I’d probably shake my fist in protest of their existence, something to the effect of what an old man does toward the kids throwing the frisbee in his yard. Damn kids. Finally the rust riddled copper cogwheels of my slow motion descent grind to a screeching halt as the front of my right foot strikes the floor with the rest of my body coming down at an extremely odd angle behind it. At this very instant I realize that I am not buzzed. But I’m not sober either. As a matter of fact, I’m drunk. Indeed, I’m actually pretty fucked up right about now. What a time to find out. Somehow I manage to plant my right foot flat on the ground and use my left arm to catch the rest of my weight, landing in what could be considered the red headed stepchild version of the three point stance. Picture spider-man... but drunk. That how I look right about now. Who knew such a simple task as getting to one’s feet from an easy chair could be such an accurate gauge of how fucked up you are. When my foot hit the ground it felt like I was stepping down into a vat of watered down jell-o pudding... needless to say, Bill Cosby would not approve. Rather than muscling my way up into a standing position I opt to simply drop down onto both knees and work my way up from there. Too bad the trip from knees to feet include a pit stop in Layingonthefloorville, USA. I don’t know if it’s due to my equilibrium being entirely maladjusted at the current moment or for the simple fact that the floor seems to resemble a water bed filled with freezing cold water on a hot summer day, but that’s where I’m headed. No finesse or gingerly working through the motions about it, I just keel over to my left side and collapse on the floor. Soon after I assume a fetal position, complete will full range thumb sucking motion. Babies don’t sleep this good... Suddenly and excruciatingly rudely I’m awakened from my alcohol induced, daydream or reverie or what have you by the sound of nature once again screaming in my ear by way of a police issue megaphone. My eyes pop open as if pried open by crowbars as I quickly roll over onto my stomach and use my arms to hoist myself up, much like your standard issue push-up. I then push myself off the ground and pull my legs up under myself and during my landing transform into a world class sprinter then take off in a mad dash for the bathroom. Now I know I’m fast, hell, everybody knows I’m fast. But I don’t think I’ve ever run like this before, not even when being shot at or on the verge of being chopped to pieces by a gigantic salad shooter replica doubling as a set of fan blades. I gallop out of the living room like a bat out of hell being chased by a Russian race horse with a glue truck behind it bordering on the speed of light on a three day crack binge. Once I hit the hallway I get the same feeling I do when I realize that me and Zero are home free, usually the same one I get around the time John or I blows up a building. Probably the same one a junkie gets while taking his first hit fresh from detox. I tear down the hall kicking up rugs the whole way until finally I reach the door. Once there turn the nob while still trying to bring myself to a complete stop. Much to my disappointment... it doesn’t turn. Doesn’t even budge, nothin?. With the situation continuously more critical by the second I proceed to jump up and down in an attempt to somehow stall the inevitable, the draining of the main vein. Still in disbelief of what is going on I try the door once more only to hear the words “I’m in here.” come through the door in a muffled female voice. One word comes out of my mouth before I race back up the hallway, and that word, ladies and gentlemen is fuck. Say it with me now, f-u-c-k, fuck! Although I doubt Debra even hears it until I’m a good three quarters of the way back down the hall. I burst back out into the front room of the house and nervously look back and forth across the area evaluating options. Finally something clicks and it dawns on me to go outside. If lightning were to have struck the exact spot I was standing, at the exact time I looked toward that door as my only hope... it would’ve missed. Words can not describe how fast I got from the end of the hallway and out the front door. With time being of the essence I decide that in this instance finding an optimal spot for the outdoor deployment of urine is less than necessary as long as it gets out. Basically, as soon as I get out the door and down the small set of stairs in front of it no more than three or four steps past that I decide to let it fly... just in time to see Zero’s car pulling in. This day just keeps getting better... I just know he’s gonna kill me for pissing in his orchids.
