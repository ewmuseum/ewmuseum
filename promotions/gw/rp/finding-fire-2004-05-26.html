Finding Fire

26 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.26.04 Finding Fire After the GW International Championship Change Backstage in the Global Arena An elated Ken Partridge Jr walks through the curtains and down the stairs to the backstage area with his newly won Global Wrestling International Championship hanging proudly from his shoulder. As takes his foot off of the final step, he stops to the sounds of a cheering crowd. Curious, KPJ peaks his head out of the black area curtain surrounding the stage to see that the Global Arena is still filled to capacity with screaming fans. As he looks above the ring, he sees a constant replay of himself winning the strap, which he had held for no more than 10 minutes at this point, playing over and over again on the four-way screen perched from the ceiling in the center of the colliseum. He couldn't believe it... these people that hated him so much... that loathed beyong all belief... were cheering him for winning the International strap. 'Typical,' responded Ken disgustedly to the situation as he quickly pulled his face back through the curtains and closed the area that he'd been peering through with a hard jerk. 'Now they all want to jump back on the fucking bandwagon.' Shaking it off, Ken turned on his heel and continued his march back to the locker room that he was sharing with fellow Elite member and GW World Champion, Josh. As much as he didn't want to show it, Ken was on cloud nine along the entire way. A smile crept across his face that wouldn't leave. It had been so long since he had been a champion, and now, in one night, he'd captured the International Title and secured a shot for Josh's belt at High Noon. In one night, the largest step in proving that he was the absolute best that wrestling world had to offer had begun. 'Good job, Ken,' congratulated a suit standing off to the side of the hall. Ken gave him a small nod, still not erasing the grin from his face, before shrugging him off and entering his dressing room. 'Dammit, when I guarantee something, you'd better take that shit to the bank,' screamed Josh with a light-hearted tone in his voice as Ken walked in. No sooner had Josh made his statement then Ken was being sprayed with champagne, both men laughing as Josh continued to shake the bottle as KPJ held his mouth open to get some in. Josh through the bottle aside and hugged Ken, who returned it with a firm grip, yet never releasing his newly won Championship. Josh pulled away, gave Ken a slap on the shoulder, and stared at him... only to begin laughing with joy shortly thereafter, which Ken followed with. 'Man, I'm so proud of you,' spoke Josh with all the true candor and sincerity of a saint. 'You really did it.' 'Thanks, buddy,'responded Ken. 'It's been a long time coming.' 'Yeah, how long has it been?' Giving a large sigh Ken responded with, 'Almost a year if you count my two week stint as Primestar Champion. Longer if not.' The smile quickly resurfaced on Ken's face. 'But, man, it's like this huge burden has been taken off of my shoulders now and all of these walls I've been hitting... all the mental blocks that have been plaguing me... none of them are there anymore. Josh, I've found it again... and it's amazing.' 'Sounds like I've quite a match set up against me at High Noon, then,' laughed Josh. Suddenly, with that one statement, the room became serious for the first time since Ken's match earlier in the evening. Ken simply looked at Josh in front of him, holding the World Championship over the same shoulder as which Ken's International Title hung. Almost a mirror of two spectacular athletes that knew each other better than most people know themselves. 'Josh,' began Ken solemnly and slowly, 'I -- I'm not going to hold anything back at High Noon. You should know that I want that World Title hanging on your shoulder more than anything in the world, and I will do absolutly anything to obtain it. That one belt, Josh, represents the fruition of all the new goals and dreams that I've ser for myself since I've returned to the ring. It my lifeblood, and it will be mine. Take it as you will, I'm looking to cause no rift between us, as you are and will continue to be after Sunday, my best friend in the entire world. You're my brother... in fact... you're more than a brother to me. But we've been put in a situation now that I've awaited in some form or another for some time now.We've met in the past, Josh, and I've always come out on top, but you had also not reached your potential yet. Well, now you have, and something continues to linger there for me, and I'm sure it does you as well.' Ken steps a little closer to Josh before finishing his rant withmore confidence than he's ever bled in his entire life. What he said, he said slowly... he said calmly... he said surely. 'We need to know... Who is... the best?'
