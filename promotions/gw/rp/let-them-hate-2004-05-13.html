Let Them Hate...

13 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.13.04 'How did you convince me to come here,' questioned Josh, oddly adorned in a clean and untorn red t-shirt with a black leather jacket over his black Armani slacks which were brushing the tops of his brand new black Prada loafers. 'Because I was tired of pizza and hamburgers,' rebutted Ken Partridge Jr quickly, 'and so were our bodies.' Ken was attired as usual... white Versace slacks and jacket over his baby blue on royal blue striped shirt, which remained untucked fashinoably, and an addition in the sandal department to his seemingly never ending collection of Parada footware. Looking at Ken prompted Josh to look down on himself, making an odd face while shifting uncomfortably. 'Well, why in the hell do I have to wear these clothes?' 'Because there's a dress code here, Josh,' answered Ken as he reached down to sip on his '98 Shiraz. 'Besides, I bought you a new outfit, you should be grateful.' 'Grateful, I am,' responded Josh, 'but you know how uncomfortable I am in this type of atmosphere. This is you, Ken. You're the glitz and the glamour, I'm the--' 'You'd better get used to it, Chum. That Heavyweight Championship is going to prompt you into a lot of situations much worse than this when it comes to stardom. Charity events, political campaigns, special appearances and the like... they're pretty much all gonna be formal. Which means you're gonna' have to dress like that,' forewarned Ken. Josh frowned a bit more and looked out the large windows of the restauraunt that Ken frequented enough to wn stock in, Bottega, before thinking business again and boiling down to brass tax as usual. 'How long's it been since your last Ladder Match?' Ken sat, pondering the question while sipping on his wine and awaiting the arrival of his Prime Rib. 'Longer then I remembered, I guess. It seems that the last one was in neWave against Mike Golden.' Josh laughed, 'that 'Movie Star' guy?' 'Yeah, that's him. My body took a stern beating in that match... probably the worst at that time.' Josh simply stared back across the table at a reminiscing Ken... 'But you won.' Ken portrayed half of a smile across his face, 'Of course I won... I always won. There's yet to be a Ladder Match I've lost. But, they still hurt... for weeks... sometimes months... you know that.' 'Yeah, you're right I do. There are times when you wonder if the pain will ever go away at all.' The two sat quietly for a bit as their meals arrived. As the steaming medium rare Spare Rib and Josh's Shrimp Limon were sat in front of them, Josh took the opportunity to shed the jacket and muss his hair a bit. Ken, of course noticed, but didn't care, nor had he ever. One of the things that bonded he and Josh so tightly together was the fact that they were both completly comfortable letting the other be themselves. Josh immediatly began to twirl the linguini around with his for before stabbing into a shrimp and stuffing it in his mouth. 'It's Benjamin's first match back,' replied Ken as he began to cut into the Spare Rib. 'He's going to be looking for a 'W' moreso than usual.' Ken finally dipped his bite in au jous and placed in his mouth, making a heartfelt sigh at the bold and succulent taste of the rib. 'He's gonna try to play mind games with you. I'm sure he'll note your two losses in your other specialty matches,' gunned Josh. 'He won't say how you lost those Battle Royals, just that you did.' Ken finished chewing before retorting, 'True, but let him. The people will believe him anyway... eventhough they saw with their own eyes. I lost the first to you, fair and square... eventhough I had been in there a considerable ammount longer, that's the luck of the draw. But they saw the Pearl Harbor job that eliminated me last Monday. Thus is more proof that they need other people to think for them.' 'So, what are you gonna do,' mumbled Josh through a bite of food. 'Me,' questioned Ken as he finished his recent glass of Shiraz before filling it back up with the half-full bottle. 'I'm going to let him hate.' Josh looked at him somewhat puzzled before Ken contnued. 'However,' Ken followed with another bite of his rib... 'I'm going to make him fear.'
