Amazing Love

31 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/31/04 The most important question I have ever been asked, without even thinking, had to be: “When God looks down upon you on your judgment day, what will you say to him?” Honestly, that question has racked my mind for decades. What am I going to say to somebody so powerful like him? Can I even think of the words when I stand before him. When I stand before’my creator. Tonight, I think I’ve found the answer that I’ve been looking for. When that time comes, I will look the Lord into the eye and say? “Merciful God, I’ve brought forth many angels’for you. I’ve sacrificed my judgment, for the sake of others. For I care, Lord. Merciful, and forgiving God. I ask you to feel pity for what I’ve done.” Yes, I’d get on both knees, and I’d beg for my everlasting life in the heavens above. When I stood face to face with God many moons ago, I honestly didn’t think I deserved to even have the honor of standing before him. But now, after helping those two people get to know the Lord in a personal way, I know that I will stand tall. <hp> I stand before the new Global arena. The hours count down before this arena is packed with an exploding audience. The question that boggles my mind is, am I ready? Standing here, I worry about injury. I worry about winning the GW International title tonight. I worry about Leinad and his quest to defeat Joshua for the GW World’s Championship. I worry about Cherish, as I do every morning until every night. I worry about the world in its entirety. I worry? “about you. But like I told the prostitute, one must have enough faith in the Lord. Place your burdens, your troubles, and your worries in the palm of his hands and he shall provide. Walking into Whiplash tonight, I have the Lord by my side. Walking away with another title around my waist or not, I am the winner tonight. I bow my head and fold my hands over my stomach. I close my eyes, and say a prayer” “Lord, tonight, I place my life in your hands. I’d like to give you everything, and let you work another miracle in my life. I’m giving up everything to you, oh God. I’m laying it all down before you, asking you to provide. I’m laying down, tonight, for the sake of you my King. I’d like to pray for Cherish and her ongoing struggles with school starting. And Leinad, give him the power he’ll need for tonight. For me, to work through you, oh Lord. And for Brick? “because tonight, he’ll need a lot of prayers to walk out of this confrontation alive.” I open my eyes and lift my head towards the arena. I look towards the sky, as the sun begins to break over the clouds up high. I slowly walk towards the entrance to the arena? ??I’m forgiven, because you were forsaken. I’m accepted, you were condemned. I’m alive and well, your spirit is within me. Because you died and rose again.” I sing the lovely hymn as I walk, slowly, towards the arena. “Amazing love, how can it be” That you my King, have died for me? Amazing love, I know it’s true. And it’s my joy to honor you? “and all I do, I honor you.” I continue to sing, a lovely tune as my voice fades from the distance. Faded, like my worries and problems in the hands of the Lord?
