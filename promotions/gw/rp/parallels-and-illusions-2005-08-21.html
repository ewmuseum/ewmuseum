Parallels and Illusions

21 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/21/05 Parallels and Illusions In the mind of Joshua Brand, not much is making sense except for a few small things in the world. He's seen a lot in his life, a lot that others would have to rub their eyes, as if they were thinking they were still half asleep. Between a brother who's not wholly human, but something else entirely, to his mother who worked as an ancient cultures and archeology researcher out of the University in Seattle, he's seen almost everything. Then again, what some didn't know about his mother was who had paid for her scholarships and all that. Sometimes the man thinks about his brother and wonders if there is some resentment there. Josh isn't what he is, not in a long shot. The doctors of the organization they both serve, the one that Josh's mother served, has tested him again and again. They determined that the line that gives Gray his special 'features' was from his mother. Josh never knew Gray's mother, she died shortly after Gray was born. Sometimes Josh thinks his brother is just an 'ass' because of their two different mothers, especially with Josh's mom being Caucasian, she was a French exchange student, can't get much more Caucasian then that. But sometimes, even as much as he loves his brother, he finds him to be a major pain in the ass. Why doesn't he understand what's going on with the woman that Josh is in love with? Yeah, Josh isn't a bad looking guy in one bit. He could probably land just about anyone if he really tried. But that's not what he wants. But everyone else over the last two weeks has just beaten down on Dani for what happened that next morning, when she kicked Josh out. He won't deny it hurt, but Josh appears to be the only one with the unique touch that knows just what Dani is going through inside, and the internal conflict it puts her through. She's always been taught that for a woman to succeed,s he's got to put everything above her personal life. And the stories told around the academy by the 'stupid', Josh's word for them, instructors to try to scare the women out of the training if possible, didn't help either. Josh also knows just what Dani's been going through with her recent record. She expects herself to do the best, and when she falls short, it tears her up inside. So just why would she even try to cloud her mind with other things? A person can only handle so many things at once. Between her career and the other changes going on in her life... Josh just wishes it would all get better. Gray, on the other hand just appears to be wanting to push her further and further. He's one to talk about not giving up on personal things though, truly. Joshua “Fire” Brand, the Firebrand, yeah... Hotheaded, impulsive, and down right flamboyant on the outside. Focused and determined in the inside. An internal hypocrisy that only he knows and realizes. Others can think what they will about him, he's good with that. Because, between the three of them, he just might need to be the foundation to keep things from crumbling to the ground. Him, and the two people he loves more then anything; his brother and Dani. ~Flash back to last week~ ~Ms. Leanan's Suite at Ceasar's Palace~ Like a good boy, Josh allowed Ms. Leanan's Handmaiden, Kaylee, to take him to the hotel with no questions asked. All the way there he really wanted to know how she, as little as she is, stopped Gray with one hand on his arm. Josh hoped this wouldn't take to long as he wanted to go looking for Danielle himself, to make sure she was okay. The surprised look on her face when she saw the marks on his back pretty much said it all, she had no clue that she had done it. Josh really hadn't looked at them after Gray cleaned them, he had pretty much forgotten about them being there. It's not like they hurt or anything. When they reached the suite, one of the side bedrooms has been set up like an examination room, she'd called ahead. It looked like the doctors office in some bad horror movie: over head spot lights, a small table that looked like a table for massages, trays of sanitized sealed items and four Coke-bottle-bottom-eyeglass wearing, pocket protector, “nerds” for doctors or scientist standing looking at him like they were going to attack him at any second. They seemed just a little too happy to be there. Kaylee acknowledged them all with a nod, but then turned and spoke to the side of the small group of 'men', “Jaymes, it is good to see you again. I see your team is already setup.” As she spoke another man with super model length black hair, dark brown eyes, and a complexion that rivaled Gray's stepped out. He was dressed to kill in a pinstriped suit that mostly cost a easy four grand if not more. He walked into the small room from the living area, and immediately went up to Josh. He looked like some guy that just stepped out of a God Father Movie. He looked Josh over for a few moments and then turned to Kaylee. He gently grabbed her hand and gave it a kiss. Josh just chuckled at it. The man, Jaymes apparently, greeted Kaylee, ?As always, I am at my lady's beck and call. I was told that my expertise might be needed on this case. Is this the specimen?? Josh didn't even hear him being put off as a lab rat, he just looked the man in the eyes and asked him, ?What nation are you from?? James smirked at the question, but did offer an answer. “If you really have to know, I am from the Bad Lands of South Dakota. I'm a Nakota, but I was once a liason between my people at Cheyenne River and the Rosebud reservation. Yes, I know Miss Northsky, personally. I also know her grandfather and parents before they were killed. I also know you, Mr. Joshua Brand, so Introductions are now done. Shirt off please.” Josh looked at Kaylee and she just nodded her head. Josh pulled his t-shirt off as he did, Jaymes walked around behind him, followed by the geeks in white jackets. Jaymes suddenly asked, ?What type of Animal attacked you?? Josh wheeled around at James, a shocked look on his face. ?Animal—What do you mean Animal?? Kaylee quickly stepped in to give the answer that Jaymes was looking for, and Josh couldn't or wouldn't give, “These were done by Miss Northsky, during acts of passion. Neither one of them can seem to tell us exactly what happened, and by the way Miss Northsky looked when she saw them I am guessing she didn't even know they existed until today.” Jaymes looked a bit surprised on his face to find out that a human had done the animalistic marks going down the young man's back. He appearered to try to recover his own composure by just adding, “Really, do we have any more clues on the young lady's bloodlines? With this it would seem that we might want to be ready.” Kaylee again responded, “Not yet. My Mistress and her teams are still working on it. Miss. Northsky is still not aware of what is fully going on, the longer we can keep her in the dark the better. We don't need to set her off even quicker.” James leaned in close to look at one of the marks closer, “Are you sure that she hasn't been infected by something else? She has been hanging around 'him' for a while.” At that Josh turned around and looked at him, he know all too well who that 'him' he was referring to was, “I'll assume that you're speaking about my brother. He would never, infect Danielle, she is too much a little sister to him. You have no right to imply...” Kaylee quickly steped in between the two men, and takes Josh's arm. As gentle as she is being he could feel her strength and decided not to pull away. As she did that, Jaymes, ignoring the display of bravado from Josh, walked over to a bag on a table pulled out a camera and note book. Kaylee looked Josh in the face and tried to make things go a bit smoother, “Josh why don't you allow these fellows to take a closer look, and maybe find out why it is healing the way it is.” Josh shrugged but didn't take his eyes off the man standing only a few feet from him. He didn't trust him, not in the slightest. There was just something that irked him about this guy. As he laid down the the four other men, converged on him like hungry buzzards. Josh just tried to lay there ignoring the comments being tossed around. ~A few hours later~ Gray appeared dressed in jeans, t-shirt and boots, walks in looking at his brother laying on the medical bed, “At least your not there due to me.” Josh opened his eyes, using a half asleep method to ignore the other things going on, “No but Danielle could have been. That was too much, old man. What you did was inexcusable. I can't believe you almost... She thinks of you as her brother and you almost betrayed that.” One of the Doctors interrupted the touching family moment, “Well one thing these are going to scar and scar badly, but they make no sense. None at all.” With that news, Josh stood up and grabbed his shirt, and started to put it on. He gave his own smart-assed commentary as he did, “Great I guess I better start looking for a new tattoo to cover up those scars then.” Jaymes, who had moved to the side to let the physical doctors look at them suddnely spoke out, ?Who said we where done?? As soon as Jaymes spoke, Gray looked in his direction and almost snarled. Jaymes visibly got uncomfortable. Josh just continued getting dressed, sliding his already tied shoes on, “I did. You guys have poked, prodded, measured, and photographed every inch of those marks. A couple of hours, and all you can tell me is they are going to scar, I could of told you that before I laid down there.” Gray just shrugged his shoulders at Kaylee, as Josh shoved pass the doctors who's faces were beginning to look like children that just had a new play toy taken from them. Josh looked up at his brother, “At least Lady Leanan is with her, so I know she is okay.” Kaylee looked at josh with surprise she hadn't said anything about Annie finding Danielle, ?What makes you say that?? Josh fixed the belt on his jeans, after tucking in his shirt as he answer her in a matter of fact way, ?Maybe because she hasn't called us yet. Maybe because your boss hasn't called yet asking about her. It's just simple listening, and learning. I know Dani and I know your bosses type. If she hadn't found her yet after what had happen she would have had half of the Order looking for her by now. Josh walked away from his brother and headed for the door. Gray tried to stop him, but missed, ?And where do you think you're heading?? Josh just turned and looked at him with a little bit of anger on his face. Josh snapped at him, “If you really need to know, I am heading back to take a nice, long shower. I feel like I have been violated in the worst ways, over the last few hours. Then maybe to the tattoo shop to see about getting a new one, to cover the marks once they finally heal. I might take a hint from the Keeper on his tattoo choices, he was forced to get one on his back to cover up some old wounds too.” Gray just nodded, ?Okay?? But Josh continued on, ? Then hopefully, to go see Dani and apologize to her for your actions. As I am sure you would want to do yourself, but most likely too ashamed to face her. If you would have punched her Gray Muzzle, you would have done more damage then any of her wrestling opponents have at that time.? Josh shoved passed everyone in the room heading for the door. Right before leaving he turned and looked at Jaymes and blurted out, “Oh trust me, the Mistress, and that's not Ms. Leanan, already knows how hot headed I am. I am sure your calling her about it won't surprise her any.” Jaymes looked shocked at Josh, but Josh, himself visibly didn't care. He tried to slam the door on his way out, but Gray was tight on his heels. ~Back to the present~ Memories are great, except when you're looking at the your brother when he is pissed off to the point he could rip you limb to limb. In such a case, Josh falls back on a old line from Star Wars, the good first movie. When Han Solo comments to the big gold colored robot about him winning some holographic chess match... “That's 'cause droids don't pull people's arms out of their sockets when they lose. Wookiees are known to do that”. He knew Gray's Strength when he was mad and in the middle of change. Not to mention his temperament when doing so. If only those damn vikings hadn't landed in what was now Pouslbo, just a few miles south of the Port Madison Reservation a few hundred years ago. But there was something about Danielle that day that he just couldn't put his finger on. . Josh used times like this to contemplate things going on. To keep his head straight. He was waiting for his brother to get back. they were supposed to goto the gym this week again, but Gray had decided to give Danielle time off. He still hadn't spoken to her since the incident at the gym. Josh was starting to think that his brother was almost ashamed of himself. But, then again he had no control of the animal in him, it had it's own mind. Danielle knew it and Josh did too. No matter how much gray denied it, it ruled him hence the reason he left his wife and family. He felt sorry for his brother most of the time. A man who when he reached the age of majority found out that he wasn't exactly normal to the point that he wasn't even human. Already with a wife and kids, he sent them away... threw his life away to live the life of a social hermit. But, then again he felt sorry for himself also. He knew Danielle's feelings towards him where not as she said. Just knew it, some how, some way. She spoke one way but thought another, completely, he could feel it. She had been the one that had initiated that night as it was. He remembered it all too clearly still. Memories that he'd savor until they could be replaced with new ones. Maybe this week apart would be good for the both of them. Kaylee, on Annie's orders, was taking care of Danielle's schedule. Kris, being away for a few weeks to a month hadn't left them with a good choice in Gray to decide things. That wasn't Gray suit, not matter how much he tried to pretend it. She insisted that the two trained separately. Maybe because of Gray, maybe because of Josh and maybe because of what has been happening with Danielle. That he didn't fully know. Josh did know that a week ago he had really messed things up. He didn't even know why he did it. When Dani fell at the gym he couldn't help it. He kissed her. The feelings had welled up while he had been working out, and then more while she did. As far as he knew, she had wanted it too. He slid out of bed and slid a pair of sweats on. Looking around the room that had been basically trashed over the last week. His brother hadn't spoken to much to him either. It was eating away at him that he had almost shifted and done something terrible, almost in front of the 'normals' out there. He'd almost broken one of the rules he was sworn to protect. But then again, Josh had noticed other things going on. It was all to much like the days when he'd come home from school and he'd mess with some of his mother's things, some of those weren't fun, and being scolded for some of it wasn't any bit better. He looked around again and kicked a pile of clothes that had been building up. Maybe it was a day for a more focusing warm up then he was used to as of late. He appeared to be scouting, to make sure no one was looking as he went over to one of his suitcases that was still in the hotel room corner. He pulled it up and then took out a small black box. Opening it, revealed a pair of black and red painted sticks, each about 2 and a quarter feet long. Spinning them, he took a spot in a clear spot on the floor and brought them to his sides. Soon he started to stretch, going through the motions, but also with the sticks coming into play. The sticks were from the art he had taken as a little boy in Seattle, Escrima, a Filipino art form that specialized in hard strikes and the use of short staves that were used not unlike batons at times, and swords at others. Working through the motions, he began to ignore his surroundings. Once his body was most limber, he moved to a splits position and then leaned backwards. As he pulled his body up into a handstand, his eyes were almost glazed over in their own little world. Once he had staid in that position long enough, he pulled his body down and then came up into a fighting stance. Once ready, he started the slow, strong moves to the form, what most in martial arts would call a 'Kata'. The form's feigned blocks, attacks, parrys, and side steps never too him out of a 5 foot circle on the floor. For the first time in weeks his mind is calm and focused. His pace quickened with the sticks in his hand, his muscle quiver as he pushes them harder. ~About an hour later~ Josh didn't realize his brother had came in, as he finished his kata and bowed a loud clapping from the side made him jump. Gray, knowing that he had a pair of sticks in his hand made his presence known another way, “Bravo little brother, haven't seen you that focused in a long time.” Josh just walked over and placed the Escrima sticks back on the case where they belonged. He kept his eyes on his brother though, watching him as he stepped towards him. ?Mind if I join you?? Gray asked as he took off his shirt. “If you think you can keep up with me,” was Josh's reply. Gray got into a low, horse type stance. His time in the arts wasn't anything compared to Josh's. Gray was a late bloomer, and had learned all of his at the academy that he later taught at. Gray's specialty was more grappling and power based. But then again, his body was quite bigger then Josh's. ?So what brings you to actually do a joint workout? Inspired by my awesome performance or feeling left behind? Or did Dani crack a comment about the gut forming, yet?? Josh asked with a snide look on his face. Gray looked at this brother with a slightly off look. And at the same time blocked a sweeping left hook that came in his direction. “Easy little bro, and by the way, neither of us has time to laze around this week.” Josh took a hard swing at his brother's legs, trying to take him off his feet. Gray jumped over the move and brought a hammering fist down, which Josh blocked quite easily with a parry. Gray tried to continue the conversation while they went at it, ?You going to let me tell you what I found out?? Josh's retort had nothing to do with work, “What? That you're a bastard. I already knew that. I told you that the moon phases where getting to close for you to keep a clear head. Someday, they'll realize the truth. I'm the cool one, and you're the berzerker.” Suddenly, Gray grabbed Josh's arms, and pinned them to his sides. He pulled his little brother up close to his face and started in on him, “Excuse me, look you. Get this bug out of your ass and say what you need to. This week I need your head clean, when we get into that ring.” Josh adjusted himself for breathing, and kept going, ?We have match?? Gray dropped him down, “Yea, what do you think? Against the tag team champions. But we are not getting a title shot just a regular match.” Josh regained his footing, ?How fun. Should prove interesting I guess. As for the bug up my ass as you call it, you really want me to give it to you?? Gray stood up straight, “Might as well get it out in the open. We're brothers, we've fought out whole lives. Give it to me.” Josh stepped up to his brother, so he was as eye to eye as he could get with the man who was four inches taller then him. With his most stern voice he then shot, “If you ever TOUCH Danielle like that again you will deal with me.” Gray almost laughed, ?You will deal with me? I cant believe I am hearing this? What is this? The little boy got a piece of ass, and he's finally getting balls?? Josh smiles and looks at his brothers eyes one more time, “You know what? If I didn't know better, I would say you still needed to cool down a little. Maybe back out a bit. I think your mind is still a little primitive for your own good. Figured that the moon phase would have you feeling better by now. But guess I was wrong.” Gray growls and grabs Josh by the front of his shirt, lifting him off his feet, ?For your information, little boy. I'm an Rahu, meaning born under the full moon. That was just a few days ago. But as for that, backing off? What do you mean?? Josh has been in this predicament before. He'd used both hands, so he was still in check, ?Easy, big bro... I can tell why you're pissed. You've lost some of your pull on Dani's case. I called in Lady Leanan. You wanted to make it all your find when it was figured out what she was. You wanted to reclaim what you thought you lost!? ?What have I lost?? Gray set him down a bit. Josh didn't miss a beat, “That you never made it past the trials to be a hunter. Because you're too much the animal, no control. Lord Malik didn't want you on one of his teams, because you could have fucked a mission royaly.” Gray let his brother go and turned around. Josh didn't stop, “You always seem to forget, while I was initiated only 2 years ago, I grew up around all of these people that you've dealt with now for 7 years. My mother was one of them. I've met them, I've dealt with our sponsors for longer then you. I was just a little boy. Doesn't mean I didn't learn things. Dani's case is for the research arm, it's in Annie's hands now. They know that Dani looks up to you though. So it would be stupid to pull you off, even though they can see that you tried to hide things from them.” Gray looked over his shoulder, ?You just had to remind me, huh?? Josh gave out a sigh then continued, “Hey, it's not your fault. Dad loved both women. He even knew that Laura was what she was. You still do him proud....” Gray turned back around to look at his brother. Before he could speak though, Josh gave him a really serious look and then added in, ?Just too bad the first of your kind to make the hunters was a few months later and he was, what... a Theurge?? Gray lunged, but not in a powerful way, ?Don't remind me!? Gray suddenly found himself laying face first on the ground, Josh on top of his back, with one arm wrenched behind him. Josh started laughing. “Funny, very funny. Now get up,” Gray tried to look behind himself at his brother. “No,” is all he said. “Fine...” was all he heard after that, as he found himself suddenly forced back, and into the ottoman chair that sat in the middle of their living area in the hotel room. Gray stood up and brushed some of the stuff from the floor off his front. ?I'd like to remind you, little brother, that it appears you really messed up last week also. From what I've heard, you had pushed Dani to leaving after trying to kiss her again?? Josh looked up at him, and then resided the facts, “I didn't know what I was doing fully. She fell when trying to stand up and I caught her. Just then, as she was in my arms, I was just compelled to do it. And for the record, she kissed back until she suddenly pushed me away and started to head out.” “She makes no sense sometimes. Even human females give off pheromones. Just not as much as animals. Her's are targeted for you, but she keeps...” Josh cut his brother off, his voice going stern again, “I understand her though. I'm cool with it for now. She's got a lot going on, all around and inside of her. You aren't exactly the prime model of social or romantic relationships. You've not seen your ex-wife and kids in years. And I might add, you forced them out after you started to change. It's not a position you can argue. If I have to wait, I'll wait.” Gray shrugs, “You could be waiting for a long time, little brother.” ?So be it... ? is all Josh said as he got up and slid a t-shirt on, ?Still early, I think I'm going to go check the buffet for breakfast, going to join me?? “No, maybe later we'll do lunch. I already did the McDonald's for breakfast,” Gray said as Josh hit the door. “That sucks, you'll get fat,” is all he heard when Josh shut the door. ~Downstairs~ Josh made it downstairs in a good time, and noticed that the buffet's line was wrapping around the waiting area in a huge snake like pattern. “Damn, everyone else in Vegas not wanting to leave to eat. Don't blame them, though, the damn heat.” He said as he looked around a bit. Suddenly his shoulder was tapped from behind. He turned and noticed a disheveled old woman standing there. She looked like one of the cleaning ladies that had seen much better years. ?Sir, did you drop this?? She asked, as she handed him something. “No, not that I know of,” he says as he takes it from her. The item is a small pendant, in a circle with a concentric star pattern in it. Around the wide outer circle are words inscribed in a language that Josh is far not familiar with. “I don't recognize this, couldn't be mine.” “But if fell from your pocket when you were messing with your wallet, good sir, I think it is. Unless you're wearing someone else's pants,” The old lady smiles as she starts to walk away, “It's pretty though, if you don't want it. Maybe you have a female friend that would appreciate it.” Josh looked at the woman as she walked away, and then down at the trinket. He leaned up against the wall as another person walked up to him. ?Are you in line sir?? A young man asked him. ?What?? ?Are you in line?? Josh looked up above his head and noticed that there was a separate line, much smaller for hotel guests. “Oh, yeah, guess I am.” At that, Josh moved to catch up with the rest of the people in the line, the other person following him. He continued to look down at the trinket the old lady had given him, saying it had dropped from his pocket. ~End~
