Meeting Old Friends and Making New Enemies

9 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.09.03 As Ken and Gunner wander backstage, it's apparant that the two are spending more time catching up on the past than doing their assigned task. They're laughing heartily as they bump into Kid Flash and Ice. The four stand and stare at one another for a bit with very stern faces before bursting into smiles and laughter. They all hug and it becomes apparant that they too are old friends. Ice: Damn... what a surprise! Flash: I'll say! What the hell are the two of you doing here? Gunner: Well, President McCoy and I just came to an agreement, and I have re-signed with the company. But, guess who the freshest face on the GW roster is? Flash and Ice's smiles both get wider as the look to Ken. Flash: Ho-ly shit! You're back in action? Ken: It seems that way. I can't wait to get back in that ring either. Flash: Hell yeah... it'll be just like old times. Wreaking havoc and not worrying about names. Man, this is gonna' be great. Ice: Flash is right. I can't wait. But, we've actually gotta' run. We've got to catch-up, though. Why don't the two of you hang out with us after the show. Have a few drinks, talk about old times... Ken: I'd love to, but I think that Gun and I have a bit of catching up to do ourselves. Ice's demeanor changes slightly. Ice: Oh... well... some other time then. Gunner: Definitly sometime soon though. The two tandems split up and begin to go their separate ways as Ken notices ice's new haircut. Ken: We'll see you guys later. Oh... Ice... I like the new cut, man. I'm diggin' the spikes. Ken and Gunner turn and begin to walk away as Ice stops dead in his tracks and turn back around to their backs. A wild look develops in his eyes and he gives Ken a hard rabbit punch to the back of the head, knocking him to the ground. Flash actually begins to laugh maliciously as I ce taunts Ken. Ice: What the hell was that supposed to mean, huh? 'Like the new 'cut! Diggin' the spikes!' Why is that so damn funny to you? You make light of me directly to my face, yet you still have the balls to call me your friend? Unbelievable! Ice delivers a quick kick to Ken's ribs as he begins to crawl up on all fours, knocking him back to the concrete floor. Flash find even more humor in this and begins to stomp on Ken's back as Ice continues. Ice: Some friend you are! Hell, you can't even find the time to talk to us when you haven't even seen me or Flash for the better part of a year! For what? For HER? Ice points at Gunner and splits his attention between the two. Ice: If it wasn’t for me and Flash, here... you would have never even have met this tramp! SMACK!!! Gunner slaps Ice across the face and Ice grabs Gunner by the shoulders and begins to yell at her. This causes Flashes attention to turn focus on the two of them as Ken attempts to get to his feet. Flash: Yeah, but you've gotta' admit it... we did a damn good job setting these two whiney bitches up with one another. Ken has finally made it to his feet and spears Flash to the ground, pinning him down and barraging him with heavy rights and lefts. Ice immediately drops Gunner and lifts Ken off of Flash, but is met by an array of big time haymakers by Ken. The two are going back and forth, exchanging fisticuffs.. Gunner and Flash trade blows as security finally makes it's way to the back. When they pull him away, Ken is in full control of the fight, pounding away on Ice. The scene fades away with the four former friends struggling and taunting each other in the arms of the uniformed guards.
