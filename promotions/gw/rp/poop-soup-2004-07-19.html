If they didn’t, I’d be hearing ridiculous name calling for hours upon hours. I think they know that if they didn’t shut up, that I’d beat the poop out of them. I’m bigger and stronger and smarter and older and everything. Not too much older, but when you are 8 years old, one year seems like an eternity. In fact, it’s almost all of your whole entire life! I know I might sound a little cocky and arrogant, but when you are 9 years old, hanging out with 8 year olds, you know that you are the boss around this place. I can play dodgeball better then them. I can cut the grass faster then them. I can even peel ears of corn. Mom won’t even let them hold one. I don’t even know why I tag along with these losers. I am a grown man. I can fend for myself. I don’t need them. I get sick and tired of hearing them whine and moan and groan like little 5 year old babies. I can do what I want and nobody can stop me. I’m 9 years old for crying out loud. I think he has a thing for her. He is always in the kitchen, helping her out with whatever she is doing. He is one heck of a suck up, always kissing her fanny. That took us forever, but it’s okay, Dad helped us out. Then, we had to get leaves and smaller branches and build a wall and a roof over the bigger branches. We left one little opening so we can all fit inside of it. I really want to camp out inside of it some night, but Dad said that if it storms, a big wind can knock it down and crush us to death. Dad’s such a kidder sometimes. Each step, I crunch leaves and twigs under my feet. I feel like I’m Goliath out here. I stomp my feet, making the noises louder. I feel superior and bigger then the world. I run to the house now and wave hello to Dad. Now, I feel small again. You see, Chinese people are usually short. But Dad is big. He is like 30 feet taller then everybody else. So right now, I feel like I’m 9 years old again. I run into the kitchen without even knocking or asking to enter. I can do whatever I want, it’s my house. Mom and Dad pay the bills, and I’m property of Mom and Dad, so in some way down the line, I’m paying the bills’or something like that. Ryan is standing next to Mom on top of a wooden stool, stirring something in a big pot. “Hey Mom.” She turns around. He black ponytail flies back and smacks Ryan, dead in the face? “Oh, I’m soooo sorry.” I snicker. Good for him, I wish it would have knocked him out. She grabs Ryan’s head and checks it for bruises. “for cuts. “for brown stains on his nose. I snicker again. “What do you want Sage?” “Umm’just wanted to know what was for dinner Mom.” “You’ll find out when it’s done.” “Ahh, come on Ma. Not even just a little hint.” “Not even just a little hint. Only Ryan and I know. It’s our little secret for now.” He smiles at Ryan, who turns around and sticks his tongue out at me. If I knew about the middle finger, I would have used it at this time. “Dinner is almost ready, get Chris and Seth and tell them to come in and wash their hands before they eat.” I put my head down and storm out the door. Mom always has me doing the dirty work. I can’t believe that woman. She is always favoring Ryan. Such a big, tough, 12 year old Ryan. I can’t wait til I’m old enough to help Mom stir’well, whatever she is making. I stand outside, in front of the screen door? “CHRIS’SETH’GET YOUR BUTTS INSIDE. By the time I get back to the kitchen, Mom is carrying the big pot of’whatever to the table with her big pads on her hands. She looks funny. Dad is already sitting at the table, in his seat. Nobody sits in Dad’s seat except him. Not even Mom or Ryan. That’s Dad’s seat and if anybody ever got caught sitting in it’they’d be in for it for sure. I jump into my chair and pick up my fork with a smile’I can’t wait to find out what’s inside that big pot. Mom picks up the big ladle and pours’whatever into my bowl. Mom KNOWS I hate kidney beans. Why would she keep it a secret from me. She knows that I hate them” “Thanks Mrs. Tse Ling for the delicious meal.” “You’re welcome, Ryan.” Mom smiles as she pours Chris and Seth, the dumby twins, their bowls of poop soup. “If it wasn’t for you, I wouldn’t have be able to make it anyways.” Ryan smiles as a carrot pops out of his stuffed mouth from smiling. Dad says that if I complain about Mom’s food, that Mom will stop making it and we’ll all die of starvation. Talk about putting a world of guilt on a grown man. I shut my mouth after I see the look in Dad’s eyes. I scoop a giant helping of poop soup on my spoon and stuff it into my mouth. I figure if I don’t chew it, I can’t taste it? 29 MINUTES LATER I take the last spoon full of my bowl. I feel nauseated. I feel like I’m going to blow chunks. I’ve almost choked over 8 million times during the duration of this dinner. But at least Mom will keep making them. “Everybody done?” Mom never let anybody leave the dinner table until we were all done together. Like a family. But Chris and Seth and Ryan aren’t even related. I look at Chris and Seth, they are playing with the spoons and putting them on their noses, trying to balance them. That is so last year. I look at Ryan, he has been finished with his soup, and his second helping for quite some time. He looks annoyed that I took my time. I really don’t care what he thinks. He’s a stupid head. “Everybody enjoy their meal” I know Dad did.” If she only seen the look in his eye when she just said that. You could have swore he was going to kill me. I know that look. And it scares the poop out of me. “Well, everybody get ready for bed. I want your teeth brushed and your pajamas on in a half an hour.” “fallout. A 9 year old Heavyweight Champion of the World. Chris and Seth fight over which one is theirs. Ryan picks his one up, it is black and white, two colors instead of one. In some way, he always has to out-do all of us. When we are done, I jump into my pajamas. I have black pajamas with white footies. They are my favorite pair, I always wear them when the guys are over, to show off, why else” Chris and Seth have matching blue pajamas, they aren’t even twins, but their Mom dresses them like it.
