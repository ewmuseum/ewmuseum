Memory Remains #8 (The Chase Scene)

24 July 2003

Date: 07/24/03 I got my twin glock .40s, cocked back Me and my homies, so drop that We rollin on twenties, with the top back So much money, you can't stop that Twin glock .40s, cocked back Me and my homies, so drop that We rollin on twenties, with the top back So much money, you can't stop that John Zero and Deacon Holmes walk through the restaurant parking lot, getting into Zero’s black Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder convertible, the top already down. Zero is about to key the ignition when an explosion rocks the area, the heat of the blast creating small beads of sweat to form on Zero and Holmes? faces. “The fuck was that?” Holmes asks as the wreckage of a car settles, just as another explosion goes off, not too far from the first one. “I believe that was our TUE bodyguards passing GO and collecting their two hundred dollars,” Zero replies, checking underneath the steering column for any extra wiring. “There’s the little bastard.” Zero takes out a small knife from his pants pocket, cutting at several wires before pulling out a small black box. “And that is?” Holmes asks, with a concerned look on his face. “This would probably be a present from Johnnie Vincent.” Just as the words escape Zero’s mouth, four black BMWs screech in front of the Eclipse. All sixteen doors open in unison, and sixteen men step out, reaching under their coats. “Fuck! DRIVE! DRIVE! DRIVE!” Holmes yells as Zero cranks the car, popping the clutch, spinning through the BMWs, hitting one or two of the armed men. The remaining men draw their guns and begin shooting at the speeding vehicle. Holmes returns fire to the men, taking two more out. “Goddamnit! Il Capo just had to have us go get that damn briefcase!” Zero exclaims as he slams a fist into the steering wheel. Holmes turns around, peering over the seat, scanning the street behind them. “We got two, three, no, fuck, all four on are muthafuckin” tail!” Zero looks in the rearview mirror, ice glazing over his eyes. “I got “em.” Zero starts scanning the street. “Their coming quick, John.” “I’m thinking, I’m thinking.” “Well think a lil fuckin faster, if you don’t mind.” “See that car coming up” The blue convertible?” “Yeah?” “I’m gonna throw this bomb into the back. You shoot the gas tank. Big boom, gives us time to regroup. Got it?” Holmes takes out his gun, twirling on his finger. “Got it.” “Ready?” “Cocked, locked, and ready to rock.” “Almost there, almost there, NOW!” Zero lobs the bomb into the air as Holmes raises up in his seat, a steady two hand grip on his desert eagle, his finger pulling back on the trigger as a flurry of bullets escape from the barrel, littering the floorboard of the speeding car with shell casings. KABOOM! The car explodes, sending flames into the street, filling the air with smoke. A burning hunk of the blue car lands on another car, causing that car to explode as well. “Hot damn I’m good.” “Yeah, yeah, yeah.” Zero whips the Eclipse into a tight ninety-degree right turn, backing into a warehouse, with the rolltop door open. Zero presses a button on the dash, and the convertible hardtop behinds to fold up into place. What in the world is in that ROOM, what you got in that ROOM? A couple a gats, a couple a knives, a couple of rats, a couple of wives Now it's time to choose Zero looks at Holmes. “Thirty seconds.” Zero gets out and heads to the trunk of the car. “Thirty seconds, what do you mean, thirty seconds?” “Deac, those are professionals. That little stunt we just pulled will buy us about thirty seconds.” “Aww fuck.” “Exactly.” Zero pops the trunk, reaching in and pulling out two bulletproof vests, tossing one to Holmes. “Put it on.” Both Zero and Holmes put the vests on over their clothes. Zero reaches in, pulling out extra magazines for his glock, placing them in his coat pocket. He un-holsters on of the glocks, racking the slid to make sure it was chambered. Zero reaches in again, tossing Holmes a Tec-9, who racks the slide. “Fifteen seconds,” Zero says as the BMWs fly past the warehouse opening. Zero and Holmes get back into the Eclipse, the hardtop securely in place. “Five, four, three, two?” The BMWs screech to a halt in front of the warehouse, coming from the opposite direction. “Damn’they’re early,” Zero remarks as the BMWs cruise into the warehouse, the cars barely having enough time to stop before the passengers within exit. Zero sits calmly as the men begin to pull out an assortment of weapons. “Um’John”. Can we get the FUCK outta here now?” Holmes practically yells. “Wait for it.” “Wait for it” WAIT FOR IT! We’re about to be turning into swiss muthafuckin cheese and you say “wait for it.” I don’t know about you, John, but I’m lactose intolerant and therefore can’t be no fuckin? cheeseman.” John slowly turns his head to Deacon. “Shhh. Wait for it.”. The men raise the guns in unison. “Wait for it.” “Aww fuck.” The warehouse erupts with the sound of gunfire, as the armed men unleash heaven’s fury upon the black Mitsubishi Eclipse. The men fire upon the car for ten to fifteen seconds, some stopping to reload, only to fire again. As the last shell falls from the last fired gun, the smoke begins to settle, and the black car is seen, slowly as it fades into the light, unscathed. Are you custom-made, custom-paid, or you just custom-fitted? Playstation 2 up in the ride and is that Lorenzo-kitted? Zero looks at Holmes. “Aren’t you glad I sprang for that bulletproofing special the dealership was offering when I bought this little gem?” “Yeah.” Holmes nods. “Hell motherfucking yeah.” “Shall we?” “We shall.” Zero and Holmes roll down their respective windows, Zero sticking his Glock 40 out and Holmes extending his Desert Eagle, both emptying their magazines into the vulnerable men. Ejecting the magazine, Zero starts the car, tossing it into reverse, backing away from the scene of carnage as the unhurt men rally together, climbing into three of the BMWs, abandoning the fourth. Zero sends the car into a slight backwards spin, shifting out of reverse and into neutral, turning the car around. At the end of the warehouse, a giant window is shown, and a street is seen beyond the window. “Left or right?” “What?” “Left or right” I’m going through that window, Deac. Should I go left or right?” “Right.” “Right?” “Right.” A car goes from right to left. “No! LEFT! GO LEFT!” “Left, right?” “Right.” “You mean right?” “Right. No, LEFT! FUCK THIS, TURN LEFT GODDAMNIT!” Holmes exclaims. “Left it is then.” Zero presses down on the accelerator, speeding the car up before bursting out onto the street, cutting hard to the left, sending another vehicle skidding off the road in a shower broken glittery glass. Seconds later, the three BMWs fly through the hole, onto the street, two of them making it, the third exploding as it collided with a stationwagon. Holmes peers out the back glass as bullets from the speeding BMWs thump into it. “Down to two.” “Care to practice your math skills?” “Math?” Zero shifts gears then reaches out and taps the Tec-9 that Holmes is holding. “You know. Two cars minus one.” Holmes grins mischievously as he rolls down the passenger window. He extends his body out the small opening, half propping on his side, wind blowing back his dreadlocks as he tries to take aim against the weaves of Zero’s Eclipse and the back and forthness of the approaching BMWs. The Tec-9 erupts in a stream of bullets, striking the grill of the leading BMW, causing the hood to fly up, smashing into the windshield. The BMW weaves back and forth, smashing into a car on its left, causing both cars to spin out of control. Holmes slides back into the Eclipse. “Down to one.” “Good. I have an idea for the other.” Zero points up the road, where a drawbridge has begun to raise slightly. Zero upshifts, causing the car to lurch forward, the engine beginning to whine before Zero downshifts, causing the engine to settle, adding a couple extra miles per hour to the speedometer. “What are you doing?” “Haven’t you ever wanted to jump a drawbridge before?” “Hell no.” “Why not” It’ll be fun.” “Bullshit.” “And if I do it anyways?” “If we survive, I’m gonna kill you.” “If we don’t?” “I’ll kill you anyways. “Promises, promises.” The Eclipse jars slightly as they head up the incline, losing some a slight amount of steam. “We’re not gonna make it!” Holmes exclaims. “The hell we’re not.” Zero thumbs the car of his gearstick, revealing a small bright red button which he presses, causing the car to fly forward as the blast of nitrous his the cars engine. “Go, baby, go.” As the peak of the incline, the car leaves the bridge, traveling the one hundred feet separating the divide. “FUCK!” Holmes yells as they travel the long distance, Zero sitting in grim silence, till the car drops down hard onto the pavement, sparks flying. “Holy Shit we made it!” “You had doubts?” Zero slams on the brakes, also jerking the emergency brake up, bring the car to a halt. “Get out.” The two men exit the vehicle, Deacon slamming a fresh magazine into the Tec-9 while Zero pulls out two Glock 40s. They wait, as the bridge begins to lower from the impassable incline, the BMW flies over the much shorter gap, barreling down the ramp. Zero and Holmes raise their weapons, firing at the windshield of the approaching vehicle. The glass spiderwebs with an intermingling of red blood. The black BMW weaves hard to the right, crashing through the barrier and dropping down into the water below. “That was fun,” Holmes says with a grin. Zero shrugs, “Just another night out on the town. Let’s get out of here.” The two men get back into the Eclipse, driving off into the night.
