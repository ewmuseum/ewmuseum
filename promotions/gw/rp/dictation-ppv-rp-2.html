Dictation (PPV RP #2)

Date: Sunday The rapid pounding of nimble feet against the floor echoes through the hallway followed closely by the click of high heels trailing not too far behind. These two sounds combined with a bellowing squall of laughter mesh together to form a raging storm of amalgamated noise. This auditory atmospheric disturbance rumbles throughout the halls of the fourth floor of echelon tower serving as a warning to those in the vicinity of what was fast approaching. Deacon Holmes goes tearing down the hallway and somehow miraculously manages to keep himself going at full speed while taking a corner and emerging from it horribly off kilter as a result of his hysterical laughter. What’s even more surprising is the fact that his girlfriend Rhaiza remains no more than a few body lengths behind him while running in heels. Entering the proverbial home stretch as it were with his office looming at the end of the hall, Deacon exclaims “I told ya you couldn’t catch me!” before glancing over his shoulder. What he finds causes his eyes to widen dramatically as he sees Rhaiza holding one shoe in her hand while hastily removing the other with a smirk. Realizing that one or both of them would soon be hurtling toward him at breakneck speed, Deacon hunches down a bit as best he can while still maintaining full stride. While rapidly approaching his office an ear to ear grin sneaks across Deacon’s due to an overwhelming feeling of victory. Whether it be a foot race or a stick fight, Deacon loved to win. No matter what the event or opponent. Of course, the biggest reason he enjoyed winning so much is because in victory he was granted the key capable of unlocking the door to what he does best. Talk trash. Deacon could jaw with the best of them, but when everything he says about being better is proven true beyond the shadow of a doubt he enjoys it that much more. In fact, it wouldn’t be out of character to talk trash after winning a foot race with a paraplegic. If his grandeur were a bed, Deacon would wallow in it... Still running, with one hand stretched out ahead of him, Deacon reached desperately for the handle to his office door, his long thin fingers deftly weaving through the cooled hallway air like snakes in a pit while looking to wrap themselves around the knob. Finally reaching their destination, Deacon’s fingers form a flesh and bone enclosure around the knob that exists only long enough for him to fling the door open with every bit of his might. With another glace over his shoulder Deacon catches a glimpse of a scarlet red pump flying directly at him on a collision course with his head. His eye widen a bit before instinct takes over and he scurries inside the room, leaving only the door behind to absorb the impact. It would either be that or his head, and it wasn’t gonna be his head. Now that he’d successfully escaped there was only one thing left to do, showboat. Knowing Rhaiza was no more than a few steps behind him, Deacon continues running once inside his office without hesitation. Approaching his desk, Deacon leaps over the back of the chair in front of it and uses his momentum to carry him to the front edge of his desk where he plants one foot before pushing off once again. This time Deacon’s descent lands him in a standing position in the seat of the large black Italian leather chair situated behind his desk. As a result of his continued momentum, Deacon’s chair was now careening toward the impressive scenic view of the city granted to him by the nearly wall encompassing window behind his desk at an accelerated rate. This unfortunate circumstance leaves Deacon to attempt to “surf” his way to a stop against the wall, where upon impact he immediately drops down into a proper seated position. At that instant Deacon notices Rhaiza standing in the doorway, as well as a second shoe in mid air on a frozen rope for his chest. Acting quickly, Deacon propels himself forward by pulling against the ground with his feet, then, with the chair now rolling back toward the desk Deacon turns his body in order to take the chair with him and use the back of it to block the shoe. Disaster averted, the back of the chair knocks against the desk with a gentle nudge and Deacon smiles to himself before spinning the chair back around to face forward, where Rhaiza was now sitting in the chair on the other side of the desk. Deacon sits straight up with perfect posture while giving his best attempt at a serious facial expression even though he’s on the verge of cracking up. Deacon eyes up the young woman sitting across the desk from him, then tilting his head to one side in a state of faux quandary as to why she’s there he asks “May I help you?” in as professional a voice as he can muster with his slight drawl. Rhaiza smirks before deciding to play along a bit and replying “I’m here for the sex, I mean the secretary position... is it still open?” in a suggestive tone. Deacon grins like a cheshire cat before placing both his elbows on the desk and resting his chin on his fists and cocking her a curious eyebrow. “Well that all depends...” he begins, giving his sad attempt at a goatee resulting in a tuft of short baby hairs a stroke while asking “how well do you take dictation?”. Rhaiza gives a seductive lick of her lips before slowly rising from her seat and strutting around to the back of the desk and joining Deacon in his chair, making sure to give him a slow grind on the way down into his lap. Once in position, she leans back into him and responds “I’d say it’s one of my strong points.” Deacon’s eyes become glassy and begin to retreat to the back of his head as Rhaiza climbs up on top of him and attacks his neck with soft kisses and gentle sucking. His posture melts as her arousing assault continues at full force while he sits with each of his limbs limp and completely devoid of strength. Summoning what little concentration he has left, Deacon manages to utter “Good...”, prompting Rhaiza to respond “I know it is.” during a brief break. A few more moments pass, during which Deacon searches for the strength to continue his sentence “cause I’ve got some fan mail to reply to... and I don’t feel like typin” that shit up.” At the realization of what Deacon had just said, Rhaiza freezes in place for a moment before sitting more upright so as to look at his face. A megawatt smile eclipsed the majority of his face and after staring into it for a few moments in silence Rhaiza begrudgingly agreed, but on her own terms with “Alright, but now we’re definitely going to Kyoto’s” Deacon then sat upright as Rhaiza stood up in order to pull her skirt back down to it’s normal length before once again joining him in his seat, this time sitting on his lap rather than straddling him. After booting up the computer sitting atop Deacon’s desk, his official website hosted by Nike loads and Rhaiza immediately takes control of the mouse and opens the fan mail area. “Alright, it’s open” she informs Deacon, hopefully prompting him to divert is attention from her ass and onto the screen in front of them but to no avail as he replies “I can’t see it, could you read it to me?” Rhaiza peers back at him over her shoulder with an “Uh huh” to let her know that she’s caught him before clearing her throat and reading the message. “Andy G. from Boone, North Carolina writes... Deacon, I just wanted you let you know I think you’re the man. I also wanted to know when your next Nike commercial would be hitting the airwaves. The first one was awesome and I’d love to see another one.” She then looks back at him to get his response, causing him to lean back in the chair with his arms folded behind his head and a thoughtful look in his eyes before speaking “Okay, take this down... First off, you’re right, I am the man. Thank you for the positive reinforcement.” The clicking of computer keys quickly follow each syllable out of Deacon’s mouth as Rhaiza’s manicured fingers deftly punch out each word as Deacon continues to speak in response to the fan’s mail. “Now as far as commercials go, Deacon’s everywhere. Just turn a corner, boom - Deacon. Turn on a light, there I am again - Deacon. Not in a creepy way though, you know, just cool. Pretty soon T.V. shows will be short interruptions in-between half-hour Deacon commercials. That’s how I see it. You can expect the next one to be appearing on a T.V. near you sometime in July when they start rolling out the new limited edition colors though.” After completing the typing of his response, Rhaiza looks back at Deacon with a befuddled look on her face as to why he suddenly began referring to himself in the third person while replying to fan mail before replying “You’d better put on some weight, cause if your head gets any bigger you’re not gonna be able to stand up” while making a motion with her hand similar to that of someone falling down. Deacon begins to reply “But you know I...” emphatically before being abruptly cut short as Rhaiza begins reading the next letter in a tone louder than his own in order to drown him out until he stopped talking. “Benjamin Polychronopolis from Miami, Florida writes... Dear Deacon, why weren’t you on Evolution last week” The show just isn’t worth watching without the poster child for don’t try this at home on it.” Deacon couldn’t help but smile after hearing what he’d been saying all along coming from someone else. Once again taking on a facial expression leading one to believe that he was deeply entrenched in thought he begins to reply. “You’ve probably never been more right in your life than you are right now. Deacon Holmes is the new face of Global Wrestling, so of course without me Evolution isn’t going to be worth watching. But there’s something you’ve got to understand, Deacon is a busy man. I’ve got commercials to shoot, interviews to give, photos to pose for, and at any give time a sea of autographs to sign. Plus, every once in a while Deacon’s gotta step back and let some of those other albeit god awful, so called athletes on the Global Wrestling roster get some air time. Otherwise they’d be getting paid for nothin”, in which case McCoy might as well fire them all and devote the entire show to me... which for the record, I wouldn’t mind. Deacon tends to steal the show. I mean, that’s one day... you get Deacon the other three hundred and sixty four!” As she finishes typing the reply Rhaiza can’t help but laugh at her boyfriend’s narcissism. Deacon laughs a bit too before Rhaiza regains her composure and informs him “Okay, last one.” before reading the final message aloud. “Jimothy Louis from Newark, New Jersey writes... Hi Deacon, I’m a huge fan and I just had two quick questions. What is your career highlight, and what do you do in your free time.” Deacon sits up a bit in his chair snakes one arm around Rhaiza’s waist before replying “Well Jimothy, Deacon’s whole career is a highlight reel. They may have to make an all Deacon cable channel just to fit it all in. But if I had to choose just one, it’d be the first time I faced Brick for the world title. I didn’t win, but neither did he, as a matter of fact we were both injured horribly as a result of that match and ended up having to go to the hospital... but DAMN those nurses were fine!” With that Deacon starts laughing as Rhaiza takes a swipe at him with an open hand in between words, causing him to inform her “Girl you know I’m just fuckin” around.” before continuing on to the next question. “Deacon is constantly striving to be a better Deacon for his fans When Deacon’s not wrestling one of his match captivating audiences that not only dazzles the eye but boggles the mind as well, he’s training for whatever’s next. Modeling” Movies? Who knows. The sky is the limit for Deacon, and if there were something higher than the sky... that would be the limit.” After adding on the final bits of punctuation to Deacon’s response she begins clapping and cheering enthusiastically, causing him to shoot her a confused look. “What’s all that about?” he asks, without a clue as to what he’d done to garner such a response. Hopping up from his lap and handing him a stapler from his desk, Rhaiza informs Deacon “Congratulations baby, you’ve just won the asshole of the year award.” with a wry smile. Standing to his feet as well, Deacon shoots back “Someone doesn’t wanna go to Kyoto’s tonight.” as he heads for the door. Rhaiza watches him for a moment before taking off after him before he makes it there. Once within range she leaps into the air and straddles his back, holding onto his shoulders and neck to stay up. “Oh no ya don’t!” she begins “We’re going out tonight, and you’re going to dress up, and it’s gonna be special whether you like it or not!” Deacon drops his shoulders a bit in defeat before replying “Fine, fine...” unenthusiastically. As she clings to her boyfriend’s back, Rhaiza finally realizes she’d never been in the room they were exiting before. “Who’s office is this?” she inquires. “Mine.” Deacon answers matter of factly. “I didn’t know you had an office.” “Neither did I until last week.” They both laugh as Deacon swipes his hand over a light switch to darken the room as they exit into the hallway they’d wrought havoc through earlier. Following a motorcycle ride home at speeds in excess of two hundred miles per hour that made entire city blocks appear as nothing more than multicolored blurs, Deacon and Rhaiza arrive back at the guest house he inhabits at Zero Estate. They open the door and step inside. Silence. The cool air of the room as an eerie chill for some reason, and when Rhaiza flicks the light switch Deacon immediately finds out why. Peering across the room he finds none other than Leinad Soma sitting in an easy chair as comfortable as can be as though nothing were wrong with this picture. Rhaiza quickly retreats behind hey boyfriend as he glares at the intruder before beginning a line of questioning with “What tha hell are you doing here!”” Rhaiza, filled with panic, desperately searches through Deacon’s drawer until her hand lands upon one of his guns. Removing it from the drawer with her hand shaking, she begins to aim it at Soma only to have it pushed down gently by Deacon and taken into his hand. Soma stands from his seat and begins stepping closer, but quickly stops as Holmes trains the pistol on his position. “Hello Deacon.....an interesting first name don’t you think....Deacon.....are you religious at all?” Soma asks calmly, still not seeming to grasp the gravity of the situation. Deacon furrows his brow and stares Soma down while replying “I’m sure that in all your years someone has told you at least once that it’s infinitely rude to answer a question with a question. I’d say it’s right up there with breaking into someone’s home.” Holmes cocks the pistol and begins again. “Now what are you doing in my room?” Do it....you know you want to.” Soma replies, baring his fangs as his eyes flash red before continuing. “But you know what will happen.” Leinad then turns his attention to Rhaiza, and begins to take a step in her direction. “She must be new.....hello dear” Deacon keeps the gun on him and orders Soma “You stay away from her! Soma’s expression becomes somewhat confused as he looks back to Deacon. “Deac is this how you treat an old friend?” A cold chill shoots up Deacon’s spine as Soma has always given him a bad feeling. His mouth quivers slightly before he speaks. “No, this is!” With that, Deacon squeezes the trigger, ejecting a bullet from the gun’s barrel, causing it to bury itself in the upper right quadrant of Soma’s torso. Leinad immediately falls backward onto the floor and Deacon relaxes his posture while looking down at the man with a cocky smile. “Pardon my rudeness... Leinad, Rhaiza, Rhaiza... Leinad Soma.” With introductions over, Deacon goes to hold Rhaiza in his arms when all of a sudden “We can either do this the easy way or the hard way” comes from the ground and before Deacon can even move Soma springs from the floor and lunges at him. Exploiting his strength advantage, Soma grabs Deacon by the wrist of his gun hand then places his other firmly around Deacon’s throat and forces him up against the wall in such a fashion with his eyes now blood red and fangs even longer than before. “But since I know the answer to this...” he continues, when out of the corner of his eye he catches Rhaiza slowing making her way to the drawer in an attempt to fetch Deacon’s other pistol. Paying her no mind, Soma continues talking to Deacon “If your bitch tries anything I'll bite out your throat, and you know I can....so listen good....consider this my resignation from TUE and consider this the warning.....I am coming after TUE....they must pay for their sins. Goodnight sweet prince.....” With that, Soma swings Deacon away from the wall and hurls him into his girlfriend causing them both to fall onto the bed as he makes his way out of the room. Unhurt, but extremely frightened, Rhaiza looks on in fear as Deacon springs up from the bed and rushes out the door after him, only to find nothing there...
