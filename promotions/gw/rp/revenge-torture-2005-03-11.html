Revenge: Torture

11 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 11, 2005 The redheaded murderer paced up and down the length of the small hotel room he shared with his henchmen. Not only had his gang of miscreants failed him, one of them had managed to get killed! Not only that but the object of his revenge, Mike Biddle, had relocated and he didn’t know where he was now. “Shit!” Charles yelled as he searched the room for something to throw. The first thing he found was a group of empty beer bottles that his remaining two henchmen had left on the table. “Those worthless fools,” Charles muttered as he picked up the bottles and hurled them at the wall, making a loud crash. Outside the room sat his remaining henchmen, Duke and Hawk. Both men were still buzzed from the drinking binge they had embarked on after the death of their longtime pal Jimbo at the hands of a random security guard. They both knew that they had failed miserably and were both lucky that Charles had not killed them on the spot for their failure. “You hear that hoss?” Duke asked in a low voice. “Sounds like the boss is havin” a temper tantrum,” Hawk replied. “Yeah,” Duke sighed, “maybe we should wait a minute so he can calm down before we tell him the good news.” “You betcha,” Hawk said as he leaned up against the wall and lit a cigarette. As he inhaled the calming smoke he smirked as he remembered how they had come across the information. Biddle had been forced to leave the casino he was staying at after their first attempt and no other resorts in Vegas would allow him to stay anyway near their accommodations, or at least that’s what Hawk thought considering where he and Duke had seen him. It seemed as if Satan himself had smiled upon their doomed souls by hand delivering the object of their revenge to them. “Sounds like he’s calmed down,” Duke said after a few minutes, he was obviously anxious to get this all over and done with. Hawk nodded as he took a final drag from his cigarette before tossing it away. They entered the room without knocking. The first thing they saw was Charles seated on the foot in the bed, his head buried in his hands. The stress of last weeks failure had taken it’s toll. “Hey...uh...boss,” Duke said with a quiet voice. “What?” Charles growled irritably. “You know how we...uh...messed up last week?” Duke inquired. “Do you really need to remind me?” Charles said with an annoyed voice. “Well...uh...we just...uh...found a solution to that,” Duke said. “You’re going to save me the trouble and commit suicide?” Charles asked in a serious tone that startled both henchmen. “No,” Hawk interjected, “you know how no one knows where Biddle is staying now?” Charles nodded, this fact had haunted him for several days now. None of his contacts knew the whereabouts of Biddle. “Well,” Hawk said, “we know exactly where he is.” “WHERE?” Charles yelled as he got up off the bed. Hawk smirked, he knew what he was about to tell the boss would totally blow him away. “He’s staying in the room down the hall.” Charles walked up to Hawk and placed his hands on the biker’s shoulders. “Are you sure?” Charles asked with barely contained excitement. “I’m very sure,” Hawk said with a laugh. Charles turned to Duke. “You saw him too?” “Sure did Hoss,” Duke said, joining Hawk in laughter. Charles grabbed them both by the arm and led them to the door. “Take me to him now,” Charles said with a smile, “we’re going to finish what we started.” ***** Mike Biddle didn’t like his new hotel room. It was what the kids called “ghetto”. The small room was definitely a far cry from the luxury that he was used to. Sadly, this was the turn things had taken after the invasion by Charles’s henchmen. Biddle could no longer get a room at any of the casino hotels in Vegas and after the death of Ned the security guard there wasn’t a single security agency in the country that would hire themselves out to him. The lack of security made Biddle extremely nervous, sure the local police drove by the hotel every hour or so but that wasn’t adequate protection. Alas, Biddle had no choice but to stay in the small hotel room for the time being. It would take a week or two for his home in Beverly Hills to be set up for his return and there no vacancies in any of the hotels in the area. Biddle blamed this current situation for his recent poor performance at the last edition of Fusion. The International Champion hoped to have this situation resolved before his match against Josh. The last thing he needed was to be distracted during a title defense, of course that was if he lived long enough to defend the title. Suddenly Biddle’s thought process was interrupted by a knock at the door. Naturally suspicious Biddle called out , “Who is it?”. “This is the cleaning guy, I’m here to clean up yer room,” a gruff voice from behind the door answered. “Well it’s about damn time,” Biddle muttered to himself. He had spent the whole day calling the front desk trying to get someone to come up and at least change the sheets on his bed. Without even thinking he got up and opened the door without even checking through the door’s peephole. The man on the other side of the door definitely wasn’t employed by the motel and before Biddle could react a blunt object struck him on the head. The world went black. ***** Biddle awoke with a start and quickly found that he was tied down to a cold metallic table. “Oh for crying out loud when is this shit gonna end?” Biddle thought as he struggled against his shackles. It was at this point that Biddle realized that he was nude. Great, not only did he have a massive headache but his tallywhacker was hanging out in a cold room. He looked around the room and tried to figure where he was. Sadly, the room was completely black. Biddle sighed loudly, he knew that this was the end that had been looming over him the entire year. It was a shame that he would never be able to defend his International Title and finally prove that he truly was meant to be a part of GLOBAL’s main event. “I see you’re awake,” a familiar voice called out from the dark. Suddenly a light over the table turned on, momentarily blinding Biddle. Once he was able to see again Biddle looked to his right and saw Charles emerge from the shadows with a wide grin planted firmly upon his lips. “I always knew you were a little queer,” Biddle said to the murderer with a sudden rush of unexpected bravado. Charles laughed. “I take it you’re referring to your lack of clothes” Don’t worry Mike, I didn’t do it for sexual pleasure. You’ll find out the reason for your nudeness soon enough.” Charles laughed again, a dark evil laugh that caused goose bumps to form all over Biddle’s body. Finally Biddle worked up the courage to ask the question that had been on his mind. “What the hell is all this about” Don’t tell me you’re still mad that I made you piss yourself at that club last year,” Biddle said with a nervous chuckle. Charles slapped Biddle hard across the face. “This is all about revenge Mike,” Charles hissed. “You’re the one that made me kill Hillary, if you had just left her alone I never would have been forced to do what I did. If you had just left her alone she would still be with me.” A single tear ran down Charles’s cheek. “I didn’t make you do shit you moron,” Biddle yelled, he had finally had enough of this and he knew he was going to die anyway. “You’re just mad because she never loved you as much as she loved me.” “SILENCE!” Charles yelled as he wagged a finger in Biddle’s face. Biddle bit the finger, causing Charles to draw it back in pain. Charles disappeared into the dark again, this time emerging with a fly swatter in his hand. “It’s time you learn why your clothes have been removed,” he said with an impish grin. Charles stood in front of the table and raised the flyswatter high above his head and brought it down with a loud slap over Biddle’s genitals. An animalistic scream escaped from deep within Biddle the minute the pain set in. Charles used the fly swatter again, and again each time Biddle’s screams became louder. “THIS IS WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU FUCK WITH ME,” Charles screamed at the top of his lungs as he continued the torture. Each time Charles hit him, Biddle wished for death... ***** A random squad car with two police officers drove through the warehouse district. The two men were silent, they were listening for any signs of possible trouble. Their diligence was answered by a loud scream coming from a deserted warehouse directly to their left. “Sounds like some hookers getting raped again,” the driver said to his partner with a sigh. “Want me to call in some back-up?” His younger partner asked. “Nah,” the driver said, “I think we can handle some drunk horny bastard ourselves.” With that said, he reached over and turned on the siren. ***** Charles couldn’t hear the siren over Biddle’s screams. Biddle’s genitals had turned a lovely shade of purple, Charles smirked as the bruise spread with each slap of the flyswatter. Suddenly, Duke and Hawk burst into the dark warehouse. “We gotta get outta here hoss!” Duke exclaimed. “The cops are here,” Hawk added. Charles gave Biddle a final slap to the balls and then dropped the flyswatter. “How many?” He asked. “Just one car with two of “em,” Hawk said. “Then kill them before they can call for back up,” Charles said with a dismissive tone as he went back to torturing Biddle. The two henchmen exchanged pained glances at each other after seeing what Biddle was going through. Sure the guy was an asshole, but no one deserved to have that happen to his family jewels. Just as they were about to go back outside and carry out their orders a loud voice yelled “LAS VEGAS POLICE, FREEZE!” “Take care of that you idiots,” Charles said to his henchmen as he continued to use the flyswatter on Biddle. Duke and Hawk pulled their handguns out of their jackets and began firing in the direction of the policeman’s voice. The fire was returned, bullets could be heard ricocheting off of the walls and pained cries were heard as someone was shot and fell to the ground. Instead of doing the smart thing and leaving, Charles continued to slap away on Biddle’s genitals. Biddle had stopped screaming when the cops burst in, he had passed out from the pain. Suddenly, a bullet flew out of the darkness and hit Charles in the back, causing him to drop the fly swatter and fall to the ground. The two policemen emerged from the shadows and their eyes widened, the situation was definitely not what they were expecting. On the ground in a pool of blood was a man that they had only seen on wanted posters back at the station. “Well that explains the screams,” the younger officer said as he pointed to Biddle’s genitals. “Jesus Christ that’s bad,” the other officer said as he bent down to put cuffs on Charles. “Free that guy while I go call for back-up.” He said as he pointed to Biddle. ***** The nightmare was finally over. Both Duke and Hawk died from wounds sustained during their brief gunfight with the police. Charles is now paralyzed from the waist down after being shot in the back and has been returned to prison. After a short hospital stay Mike Biddle has fully recovered from his groin injury and has put it all behind him. Biddle’s mood improved greatly once he was released from the hospital, it was a few days before High Octane which meant he had survived long enough to defend his championship. With Charles back in prison Biddle felt that he no longer had anything left to fear. He was also invited back to the casino that he had stayed at when Charles first attacked. This was were Biddle relaxed now as he waited for his limousine to the airport to be prepared for him. As he relaxed he heard his cell phone ring. He answered it. “Mr. Biddle this is Jack from Warner Brothers Studios,” the voice on the other end began, “I was wondering if you’d be interested in being the star of our next superhero franchise?” “Tell me more,” Biddle said with a cocky grin. Things had finally gone back to normal, but for how long?
