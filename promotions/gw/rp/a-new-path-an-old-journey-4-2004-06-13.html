A new path an old journey# 4

13 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/13/04 'Know that you are made to be unmade You are the white lamb The gentle sacrifice You are the greatest part of the bounty of Caine And on your shoulders shall fall the greatest of sins For alone amongst the children of Caine I have sought The answers to the greatest of riddles For alone amongst the children of Caine I have walked The breadth of the entire cosmos For alone amongst the children of Caine I have asked The One Above for forgiveness For alone amongst the children of Caine I have confronted The worst of the One Below’s minions Those snakes which bit me whilst I slept Those foul wyrms that drank my blood They learned the horrible truth by this greatest of sins I learned to cleanse the blackness from the blood The wounds of the flesh, the mind, and the soul And though I may live no longer I pray that certain of my childer live on Open thine eyes, my brood, and see the world truly And know what thou must Live on to heal another generation Live on to guard the world in her final days’ The one thing that has always been constant in my life was my silence….It was silence that saved Bubba McCoy….It was silence that kept my inner monster at bay….and it was silence that has kept me lonely… I sit in my bedroom in silence, laying on the large bed with black silk sheets. I lay there thinking of the past…..thinking of how alone I have been in the years and how there was no reason for it…..I think of something I have not thought of in years….. My Children In my thousands upon thousands of years on this earth I have had three children….Enoch, Zillah, and Irad….In honor of my grandfather I named them after his own three. Its so sad for I have only seen two of the children and never the third. Enoch my first son born during the time of the knights of old, during the time of King Arthur and his famous knights of the round…..he took on the name Safir and became part of the round table and served under Arthur. I only met him once but he was a great, great man. A man worthy enough to call my son. He knew what he was and instead of letting it drive him mad, he accepted it and used his power for good….every now and then he sends word to me that he is well and that he is proud to be my son. The next child I had was during the late 1800’s. Irad was born in my native lands of the middle east….When he figured out who he was he became mad with power, as I did before I realized I didn’t have to be a monster….he went on a killing spree that was only rivaled by one man, myself…Enoch hunted his brother down and slew him. Every night I blamed myself for what he became…Every night I thought if I had been there would he have turned out better and then I realize, I would have only encouraged him…..made him mad…made him just like his father and at that thought I feel sick…My last child my darling Zillah, my only daughter was born not too long ago. She was born in Japan. Her mother died while giving birth to her. She, my precious Zillah…..born into death and blood like no other child for it wasn’t the fact her mother couldn’t deal with her death it was the fact that the child had drained her mother dry…which was odd of a child who was only part vampire…I never knew the name that was chosen for her and I am glad of that…..I don’t deserve the love of my children….I lay there and I realize a most disturbing fact…..I stand quickly and rush into Sabbot’s room. He was sitting there with his back turned to the door watching television. His tiger sitting on the floor next to him. He pets him as the tiger stands and looks to the door with a soft growl. “What’s up Leinad? Can’t sleep?” “I don’t sleep.” “That’s right the whole vampire thing, gotcha. So I go back to my previous question, what’s up?” “There is some things we need to talk about. Things involving why I brought you back, why we went and saved Mr. McCoy, and why this is the year of the dragon. But first of I want to tell you about who I am and my family…why I joined TUE instead of the Nevermore, and why I have changed my ways. Are you prepared to listen my friend?” “Sure. I think its about time I hear some of this anyway.” I begin to explain my past to him….where I was born and how I was raised. I tell him of my brothers and sisters and what has happened with the vampire race since the last time I saw them. I explain to him how I met Saloak and why I didn’t join the Nevermore. And I explain why I joined TUE. He sat there quiet trying to take it all in. He turns around to face me and looks me right in the eyes, something he hasn’t done since I’ve brought him back. “I have a question for ya.” “Sure ask away?” “Your children?” “What about them?” “You said the last one was born fairly recently?” “Indeed.” “How recently?” “About seventeen years ago, why?” “Do ya think she could have came to GW?” “I know what your implying but no I don’t think so…” “Ya sure?” “No.” “It something you may want to look into. I mean its kind of odd that with you coming back….she showed up…” “I don’t think its her.” “Ok…So on to the year of the dragon.” “There is a man we need to seek out in GW. He will help us achieve the ultimate goal. The goal in which I have brought you back for.” “And that goal is?” “The utter annihilation of the evil that plagues GW….starting with the biggest of the bad….” “TUE…” “Precisely.” “Well what makes you think this dragon guy is gonna help us” “It’s simple….I and one of my son’s have been loyal to his family for years…and now in his time of need we shall be of assistance once more.” “I see. As long as I get to inflict the pain that is deserved upon those who killed me I am ok with your plan.” “That is good to know my friend.” “I still say you need to look into that girl though….she seems a lot like you. Is there anyway you could find out?” “I would need a sample of her blood.” “Then we should get working on that.” “Indeed we should.” We sit there plotting and I pick up my cell phone. “Who are you calling?” “My son. I am asking for him to come here to become my new bodyguard and Senior VP of Soma Corp.” “Ah.” “Oh, and how would you like to be VP under him. The pay is pretty good” “Sure, why not.” “Excellent. I shall draw up the papers immediately.” I walk out of the room in silence.
