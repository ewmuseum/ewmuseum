El Manana

5 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/05/05 'Talk! Talk or you die!' Even Mindy was shouting in protest by that time, but Michael was having none of it. The man who threatened Mindy's life was on his knees begging for his life, face only covered by the cloak he wore. Michael glared down at the poor sap, showing no remorse in the figure's sign of misery. 'Quit your goddamn snivelling and tell me what is going on! Speak!' The figure in black whimpered for mercy, but it was no use. Michael was well beyond having an edge of reason- the man before him must have sensed that. 'I r-really don't know,' confessed the figure in between choked breaths that were obviously ragged from the attempts at holding back tears. He didn't sound like a ruthless kidnapper at all, and he definitely didn't sound like a killer. If anything, he seemed like nothing more than a big baby. ~~~ When Michael heard the screeching of tires outside, it didn't rattle him for a second. His mind was beyond the point of noticing anything around him. The room he sat inside of, the noises outside of his bedroom window, his child's abandoned toys strewn along his floor. The pain of everything that surrounded Michael caused him to grow numb to it, until there was nothing. He saw nothing, heard nothing... his mind was a clean slate. Would he ever get to see his wife again? Would he ever get to watch cartoons with his daughter? 'Why did I have to leave them again? Why must I be so unhappy with what I have? I'm such a fucking waste...' Maybe his mind wasn't so clear after all. It wasn't as though he knew what was going to happen if he went back to wrestling, and he had absolutely no idea that his wife and child would be in danger if he left. How could he have known? Either way, it didn't change a thing. They were gone, and Michael had no idea how he was going to get them back. Hell, he probably wouldn't get them back at all. 'Maybe I deserve this after all these times I refused to stay with the family... fucking wrestling.... fucking wrestling...' 'Michael!' It was that voice that snapped him out of his thoughts. However, not only was it the voice itself, but the initial thought that the voice was Jaden's, crying for the help of her husband. 'Michael!' the voice screamed again, and before the thought could develop, Michael realized that it was his cousin. She had been yelling for help outside over the screeching of tires against pavement, and Michael was too upset to care. Repeated cries eventually got him to his feet, though, mainly out of defeat rather than anger. With his luck, Markus was probably just fucking with Mindy's car or something. Boy, was he shocked when he stepped outside. CRASH! The moment Michael's frame filled the front doorway, he felt himself jump instinctively from the noise of the limosuine scraping against the fencing that circled around his family's property. The stretch kept on going, but it didn't get far. Before long, it skid to a halt at the corner of the street and stopped. If it wasn't an early morning on a weekday, Michael would have expected spectators looking on from their front steps or through their windows in a heartbeat. However, adults were mostly at work and kids were at school, therefore there was not a soul on the empty side street that cared about the situation other than the people involved. Markus held Mindy close, who was shaken up way too badly from the close call she had just encountered. Just a couple more inches to the right and Mindy would have found herself taking another trip to the hospital- and with her recent winning ways in GW, that wasn't something Mindy could have happen to her. Not as though she truly cared about GW anymore; her family was much more important than holding onto a leather belt. That, and her well being, of course. Oh, and she couldn't forget about Markus. 'Michael, oh God..' Mindy ran to her cousin and immediately held onto him for dear life. 'He almost hit us.. Michael...' She trailed off before she could finish. She wanted to voice her reasoning that maybe this had something to do with Jaden, but she couldn't say for sure. It was weird; just not weird enough to establish a concrete reason to think this was relevant to the kidnapping of Michael's wife. That was, until a figure, all in black, slithered out of the driver's side of the limo. Mindy's eyes widened as she saw the person, and Michael's attention was soon diverted to him as well. It was that one sight that had Michael charging down the street. Before the man could realize what was going on, Michael had him in his grasp, dragging him back to the house against the kicks and thrashes from the cloaked figure. 'Perhaps you and I should have a little chat.' ~~~ 'Really,' the figure stammered, 'I was just forced into this.. I had a gun to my head! I swear, I had nothin' to do with what yer talkin' about!' 'Oh bullshit,' Michael shook his head in disgust. 'Haven't you had your fun yet? I want to see my family! Now where are they?!' To Michael's surprise, he heard what for a moment had sounded like a straight answer. 'You... you mean the lady and the kid? Th- they were in the limo, too! See for yourself! If he hasn't gotten away yet, at least...' Michael blinked at the remark, especially taken back by the end of it. Maybe this guy wasn't bullshitting him at all. 'Watch him...' Michael said to his cousin, who stood silently next to her long-haired boyfriend, who seemed as clueless as one could possibly be. They both took glances at Michael as he went to one of the living room windows that overlooked the street, and looked out. They waited impatiently for the reaction in his face to pop up, and when it did, they both let out sighs of exasperation. That look could have only meant one thing. 'There's nothing there...' Michael mumbled to himself, in no way loud enough for any of the others to hear him. He paused, shifting his weight to one foot, then the other. What to do, what to do... 'There's nothing there,' Michael stated again, this time in a manner that caused everyone to shake their heads quickly from the breaking of silence. Michael looked at the cloaked man, who was still knelt down and in no position to fight. That fact was made even more clear when Michael grabbed the hood and yanked it from the man's head. Underneath was the tear-stained face of a very scared human being; one who looked like he had just escaped Death himself... one who looked like he just had a gun to his head. 'I sw- I swear,' the man stuttered, 'I had nothing to do with that.. that psycho taking your family. I, I wish I could help more...' Michael stared blankly at the man. How he wished the man was lying. Oh, how he wished he had the bastard responsible for his family's disappearance in his grasp. But no, he got away again. 'Such, a fucking, waste,' Michael thought coldly to himself. It was that thought that carried him to the front door, and that thought alone that compelled him to leave the house. And he did. Mindy opened her mouth to speak against it, but it was no use. Why even bother? Even if she'd said anything, would Michael really have stayed? She wasn't as important to him as Jaden and Gabriella both were, and understandably so. But what was she supposed to do by herself? Sure, she had Markus, but he had no idea what was happening. Perhaps, that was the best thing for him. 'Go...' Mindy whispered, looking down at the innocent limo driver at her feet. 'Go home, everything's going to be okay.' The man froze; he was still quite shaken up from the ordeal he'd been put through, but so was Mindy- and she was unnerved even more at the sight of this person in her home. 'Go!' she yelled, and it was that sense of urgency that forced the man to pick himself up and run out the front door, slamming it swiftly behind him. 'Geez,' Mindy pouted, slowly reaching out to Markus who comforted her quietly with a simple embrace. 'What am I going to do now?' ~~~ Not since then had Mindy seen her cousin. She was going crazy when she entered the arena all by herself; her match with Nariko that night would quite possibly be her most difficult one up to that point, and Michael Mercer was nowhere to be seen. Where could he have been? 'I really don't mind that you're gone... just as long as you're not hurt. Please, just be alright, that's all I ask.. I need my family, too.' Mindy felt almost as if she was lying to herself. She did need Michael around, or at least someone she could talk to. It made her wish all the more that she hadn't told Markus that she'd be fine all alone that night. With a sigh, Mindy trudged to her locker room, duffel bag and Television Title in tow. She reached her locker room, but before she could enter it, a little kid stopped her. He couldn't have been older than twelve; he still had that innocent look in his eyes that most kids lose at his age these days. He held out a pen and paper towards her, smiling from ear to ear. 'Hi, miss Mercer,' the boy greeted shyly. Mindy couldn't help but giggle. 'Heya, lil guy. What's your name?' 'Tommy,' he replied. 'Gee, such a cute name, Tommy.' Mindy glanced at the paper and pen, and that was when the sadness hit her. Tears threatened to stream down her face, as the realization that she was by herself was even more apparent. As hard as it was to fight off, however, she forced a smile with every ounce of strength she had inside of her left over from the past few days; not much, but just enough to get by. 'You want an autograph, huh?' 'Yup yup, if it's not too much trouble.' Mindy smiled. 'No trouble at all.' She took the pen and paper and leaned against the wall, signing it and handing both items back. She never broke that smile on her face. 'There ya go, hun, enjoy the show tonight, okie dokie?' 'I will!' Tommy grinned, running off presumably to join his parents. Mindy watched the boy until he was out of sight, and the second he was gone, her smile immediately went upside down. 'Michael, where are you?'
