OJ, OJ, OJ

5 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/5/04 Ten years ago, Nicole Brown Simpson, ex-wife of former football great O.J. Simpson, and her friend Ron Goldman were brutally murdered in her Brentwood home. The number one suspect was Simpson, himself. In his highly sensationalized murder trial, he was found not guilty, but in a later civil trial, he was declared responsible for their deaths and was ordered to pay the victims' families $33 million. To this day, Simpson remains a pariah. Today, two friends discuss the matter. 'Did you see the O.J. interview on Dateline last night?' asks Aaron. 'I saw parts of it' replies Brick. 'O.J. done lost his damn mind. He really has. That comment about how he was a little angry at Nicole.' 'I don't think O.J.'s crazy. I think he's just stupid. I mean, anybody who'd license his name to a steak knife company while he was on trial for a double murder...' 'Yeah, that was stupid. You're on trial for murder and you're gonna license your name to a company that makes the murder weapon. What kind of foolishness is that? I didn't see Jeffrey Dahmer come out with food seasonings.' 'Yeah, I can just see that,' Aaron laughs. 'Dahmer Seasonings. Especially good on dark meat.' Brick and Aaron enjoy a good laugh at that. 'If I was O.J., right after the murder trial, I would have packed up my shit and left the country.' 'You know O.J. wasn't gonna do that. He was too obsessed with White people liking him.' 'Then he could have moved to Europe.' 'I'm serious, man. White people loved O.J.' 'Because he made Bryant Gumbel look like Malcolm X?' 'That's Wayne Brady.' 'I know.' 'I'm serious. O.J. was on top of the world. Now he's like right under Osama.' 'I always thought the whole civil trial was bullshit. They found him not guilty in a criminal trial and then they take him to court and find him liable. What kind of shit is that?' 'And you know the Browns and the Goldmans wanted to take O.J. for everything he had.' 'I know, right? Hell, they wanted the breath out of his lungs. And you know they're gonna try and get O.J. on whatever they can get him on. O.J. better not so much 'And it's happening again with Kobe. Meanwhile, nobody's saying a damn thing about Robert Blake anymore.' 'Say, Aaron, do you think O.J. did it?' 'Do I think O.J. killed Nicole and Ron? He could have. I'm not gonna just say he didn't do it because he's black. I mean, he really could have killed them. I'll say this much, though. If he killed them, he wasn't alone. Those murders were not the work of one man.' 'Yeah, I know. I don't think even I could have killed two people in the time frame they say it happened. But, yeah, he probably could have done it. And he probably didn't do it. But if he didn't, he probably knows who did.' 'There are only three people who will ever know what happened that night, and that's O.J., Nicole and Ron. And neither of them can say.' 'Well, Nicole and Ron can't. O.J. just won't, for whatever reason. Either because he did it or he's afraid of the people who did, but he's tight-lipped as hell.' 'Let's talk about something else.' 'OK. So, what do you think about Michael Jackson?'
