Thoughts of The Past

19 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th August Pride... It is considered one of the seven deadly sins and I have always wonder why that is? I mean what is it about pride that is so deadly or for that matter sinful? Is it not good to have a little pride in yourself? If you didn't have pride then you wouldn't have hope; you would not push yourself to be the best that you can be. I am not saying always be proud because proud people can be very irritating and I would know as I have met quite a few proud people in my time. However I digress, if it weren't for pride then I would not be here in the GW, if it weren't for pride then I wouldn't be bothering with my match at next week's Evolution against my fellow GU graduate, Kitty Mac and Rich Sherwin. You see pride is essential in every person, especially a fighter like myself. As I said before I am not a proud person but I do have pride in my work. I have said before that I am well aware of what I am capable of in the ring, I know when something has gone wrong and I know when I have not been at my best. If I was proud then I would not be able to be as critical as I am and that is why come Monday night I know I will be at my best. I am not going to start talking trash as that is a ridiculous waste of time and I will leave that to my opponents. Most of you probably have no idea, who I am, as most of you have probably never even bothered to look at the Global Underground. Now this would probably piss a proud person off, as they always wish to be recognised and believe they should be the centre of attention. However I do not think like that as I see my obscurity as more as an advantage than a disadvantage. You see people underestimate the unknown, it has been the common mistake throughout history, for example when the King of England, Charles the First refused to give up the throne when Parliament asked him, he underestimated what the Parliament would do next. Led by Oliver Cromwell the Parliamentary army destroyed the Royalist Army and executed Charles the First. Now this was a simple matter of Charles underestimating Parliament. You see underestimation is a dangerous thing and in a way is an ally to the so-called deadly sin of pride. Therefore to all those who see me as just another worthless speck on the GW roster, be warned underestimation and pride are a deadly combination, and when I hit that ring be ready to be surprised. Sure you could shrug this off with a simple matter of me being overly arrogant, but let me ask you, how do you know who or what I am till you get in that ring with me? The answer is you don't, so do not judge what you do not know. I have recently brought you more or less up to date with how I got into wrestling and how I came to end up in the world of Global Wrestling. Now after 1 and a half months of proving myself in the Global Underground I am finally where I belong and that is Global Wrestling. I enjoyed my time in the GU and I enjoyed proving myself to the Global Wrestling bosses, now here I am only 4 days away from facing 2 fellow GU Graduates with a chance to be named the Global Underground Valedictorian. Now I am not one for titles but to win this match and be known as the best of the Global Underground, to be known as the hottest rising star in GW is something that you shouldn't turn your nose up at. However I will not let this 'title' control me, I will not let it be the be all and end all of me. This is my first match in the GW and to show the rest of the federation my skill is more important than becoming GU Valedictorian, it is simply a bonus. The GW is my new home, I have worked hard to get here and I will work even harder to go further... 'How was training today?' Peter Willows said as he sat down at the table in my home. Peter had come over to tell me the news of how I had been promoted to the GW. Unfortunately for him I had already been phoned by a GW representative. I felt guilty especially when he arrived at my door with a huge grin on his face and said 'Guess what?' I could have just let him tell me and act surprised but I have never been a good actor and I believe it is best to be truthful, a lesson I learned years ago when I left my old home. To soften the blow I offered him a coffee because for some reason he loves my coffee because it tastes 'clean.' Anyway there he sat looking up at me as I stirred the coffee. 'I got to be honest it was sort of tough.' I said as I dropped the teaspoon into the sterilised sink. 'Tough? How do you mean tough?' Peter said with a concerned tone in his voice. I placed the coffee in front of him and sat down on the chair opposite. 'I don't know, I guess my mind wasn't focused enough. It was like something was just nagging my subconscious, but I couldn't put my finger on it.' 'Strange,' Peter said as he took a sip of his coffee, he screwed his face up, 'Could you put a little more sugar in that?' I grabbed his cup and moved back over to the kitchen area. I placed another teaspoon of sugar in his cup and began stirring. I watched as the spoon went around and around, getting lost in the swirls, then there it was that nagging feeling, an image flashing through my mind but it was too blurred. Suddenly Peter's voice snapped me out... 'Erm, Adam I think that coffee is stirred.' I jerked out of my trance and quickly turned around to face Peter, with a smile on my face. 'Sorry man, I just got that feeling again.' I placed the coffee in front of Peter again and sat down, Peter took a sip of the coffee and sighed satisfied. He looked up at me with that concerned face of his. 'Are you worried about your match at Evolution?' He asked hoping to find the root of my problem. 'No not at all,' I said confidently, 'In fact I can't wait for the match. It is a great opportunity to show that the GW made the right choice placing me on their roster. It should be a good match, I know Sherwin will be the favourite going into the match as he already had a stint in the GW and even held the Television Championship. But I do not feel threatened by that as that is all in the past and Evolution is the present, so in a way Sherwin is in the same position as myself. As for Kitty Mac she has fought hard to get into the GW and is going to put up one hell of a fight. As I said I look forward to the match as it should be a great showcase for our talents.' 'Okay so it is not the match,' Peter said thinking, 'So what could it be? I mean you have never had any trouble with training before.' There was a few seconds of silence, which felt like an eternity as we both stared into the distance and thought... The truth was, although at this moment in time I had no idea what was wrong, but later that night it dawned on me. Guilt was what was affecting my training, the guilt of my past, the guilt of leaving my mother behind. For those who have not been keeping up with my story let me give you a little recap. Ever since I was 7 years old I have wanted to become a wrestler, however because of my condition I was held back by many things especially my over bearing mother. She was always overly cautious when it came to my safety and so even the thought that I was to become a wrestler would make her a nervous wreck. Therefore when I reached the age of 13, I began my training secretly. When I hit 18 I was offered a contract by the former Peter Willows, which took me to Mexico and Japan. Of course before I left I had to confront my mother about it. Let me just say she didn't take the news too well and it ended up with me simply leaving my home without turning back. Now I have always told myself never to regret a single thing I have done and for the past 9 years I never once thought about who I had left behind. However now that I had finally hit the big time, it suddenly dawned on me on what I had sacrificed to get where I am. Now do not take this as a sign of me wanting to quit wrestling, as there is not a chance in hell I would ever even think of doing something so stupid, wrestling is my life and nothing will ever change that. However last night my dreams opened up my mind... I walked through a bright white doorway into an even brighter room. In the middle of this bright room was a dark black table with two dark black chairs. 'Hello?' I shouted into the emptiness, 'Is anyone there?' There was no answer; I felt a cold chill run up my spine and for the first time in a long time I felt fear. I moved towards the dark table and sat down. As soon as I did the chair came alive and straps came flying across my torso and legs. I was constrained and I couldn't get free, the more I struggled the tighter the straps got. I tried to scream out for help but I couldn't, it felt like my voice had been muted. Suddenly to my left a dark rectangular shape, the shape of a door, formed and out stepped my mother. My heart began to beat like a drum, I could feel my breathing getting heavier. She stepped towards the table and looked at me with such disgust and disappointment that I bowed my head in shame. She sat down on the seat opposite and looked down at her hands, which were crossed in front of her. Again I tried to speak but nothing came out. 'Adam, Adam, Adam.' My mum said shaking her head still not looking at me. Again I tried to speak but nothing. 'How could you walk out on me like that?' My mum said finally looking up at me. 'You broke my heart. Did I not treat you right? Did I not protect you?' 'Yes you did,' I said finally finding my voice, 'But you never understood...' 'Understood what?' My mum interrupted angrily. 'You never let me be myself. I had to follow your rules, I had no choice. You never understood my want to be a wrestler; you never encouraged me to follow my dreams. You held me back.' 'Held you back? You lied to me for 5 years, not telling me why you were doing all this 'training'. Do you not understand that I just wanted you to be safe? I had already lost your father to cancer, I didn't want to lose you as well.' 'I am not yours to protect anymore mum! I run my own life can you not understand that? I am in the GW now, I am at the top of my game, can you not just be proud of me.' 'How can I when you left me 9 years ago! You are dead to me Adam Myers; I will never forgive you. Do you hear me, NEVER!' I jerked awake in my bed with the last word echoing through my mind. My heart was bounding; sweat was dripping from my body. I simply sat there staring into the darkness... Our minds can show us our true feelings, mine was showing my true regret. But I couldn't dwell on it, I have to train, I have to be at my best for Evolution, and I promise you... I will be!
