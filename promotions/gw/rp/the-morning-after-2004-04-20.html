The Morning After

20 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 20, 2004 **Pain only exists in ones mind. Something that may be painful to one man, may not be as painful, or painful at all to another man. Pain is a feeling that can be shared with others, and depicted by men who have felt man, but pain truly cannot be shared by one person and have it felt by those it is being shared with. The only true way of sharing one's pain with others and have them understand exactly what one went through in experiencing that pain is to attempt to inflict the same physical or emotional pains on others. This recreation may not equal the same pain that the original victim suffered, but it is human nature to allow pain to be appeased through the pain of others** - DOWNTOWN DALLAS MOTEL ROOM - .:We left Takahashi as he tortured himself and passed out in a thick pool of his own blood, blood that had been spilt by Takahashi inflicting his own wounds with shards of a broken mirror. As Takahashi comes too, we can see thin rays of sunlight peering through the shredded sheets hung in front of a small window in the very small bathroom of the room. Takahashi's red-painted eyes struggle to open, and when they do finally peer open, the diallated pupils take a minute to adjust to the sunlight of the hazy Dallas, Texas morning:. TAKAHASHI: Ugh! Argh! .:Takahashi begins to stir, and rolls over onto his stomach were he is surprised to find himself in a puddle of his own vomit. Takahashi quickly brings himself up to his hands and knees, and then looks down to his own arm. Upon looking down to his bloody arm, we see that Takahashi appears shocked. Takahashi's arm appears to be very mutilated due to the self-inflicted cuts. Takahashi touches his left arm, but as soon as he does the dried up blood crumbles, and Takahashi visibly feels a sting from the flaking dry blood. Takahashi lifts himself up with the help of the old bed of the motel room:. CREEK! .:Once to his feet, Takahashi grabs his arm and proceeds to the tiny bathroom. Takahashi turns on the water in the washroom sink and begins to rinse the blood away from his forearm. Once the dry blood has been rinsed off the arm, we can see numerous cuts sliced deeply into Takahaashi's arm. The cuts visibly need stitches, but instead Takahashi removes his dirtied-white under shirt, and wraps the shirt around the wounded arm. Suddenly, there is aknock at the motel room door:. KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! .:Three sharp, loud knocks are slammed into the delapidated door. With each knock we can see several paint chips fall from the door onto the ground. After a couple seconds, while Takahashi gathers his items, we hear a couple more slams on the door:. KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! .:This time the knocks are louder, causing the door frame to be slammed so hard that it seemed as though it might even come crashing to the floor. Shortly after the knocks, we hear a voice from the other side of the door:. VOICE: Hey mother fucker! What is going on in there? Answer this fucking door right now! .:This shouting from the other side of the door causes Takahashi to look up, and proceed to the door of the motel room that he had purchased just the night before. Takahashi opens the door, and we see the owner of the motel on the other side:. TAKAHASHI: What the hell do you want? I'm renting this room, what I do in here is my OWN BUSINESS. How dare you approach me with such distain! MOTEL OWNER: Well don't you go usin' them fancy werds with me boy! This is my godamn motel, an' when people goes and tell me that they's is gonna' be leavin' here by morning an' they is still here, that makes me get all concerned! TAKAHASHI: I'll leave when I want to. I will leave when I am damn well and good. And if that isn't good enough for you, well then that is to bad. I paid for this room, and I shall stay here until I feel it is no longer necessary. MOTEL OWNER: Well we got some complaints about a big bang in this here room last night. I want to check it out, so get out ma' way. .:The motel owner barges into the motel room, pushing Takahashi out of the way in the process. Once inside the room, the owner is shocked at what he sees. As soon as he walks in, the owner sees a mixed puddle of vomit and blood, and a smashed mirror:. MOTEL OWNER: What in the hell went on in here?! (Turns and points to Takahashi) You's got some essplainin' to do you punk foreigner! TAKAHASHI: Foreigner? I suggest to calm down, before things are forced to get ugly. MOTEL OWNER: What the hell did you jus' say? See, I knew you was trouble as soon as you walked through them doors downstairs. You with your face all painted and such. That's the problem with you zipper heads, you's is a violent breed. I'm not even to sure if you's nippers is real humans! .:Takahashi reaches for the pudgy, balding motel owner, grabs him by his greasy wife beater, and throws the motel owner to the floor. Takahashi goes down with the motel owner, and gets in the owner's face, still hold the collar of the wifebeater:. TAKAHASHI: Racism is the wrong path to take. That seems to be the problem around these parts, all you slack-jawed yokels use hateful speech in displaying your ignorance. I'm not sure why you feel such hatred, but believe me, your hatred isn't going to get you anywhere with me. Now when I rent this room, .:Takahashi reaches to the floor and picks up a bloody shard of mirror, then holds it to the motel owner's throat:. TAKAHASHI: I expect to be left alone with my own pain, you have intruded on my space, and now I'm not sure what I am to do with you? You have insulted an unstable man, you have insulted Takahashi Jetsui, for that, I should slice your disgusting body into ribbons. MOTEL OWNER: N-, no, p-, please, don't go killin' me, do whateva' you want, jus' please don't go cuttin' me up! .:Takahashi continues to hold the piece of broken mirror to the man's throat, as we can see the beads of sweat sliding off the motel owner's head. Finally, after a couple of seconds, Takahashi takes the sharp piece of mirror away from the man's throat, and stands up. Takahashi drops the sharp object onto the fat stomach of the large, bald owner, and proceeds to grab his trench coat. Once Takahashi has grabbed his trench coat, he leaves the motel room in shambles. Takahashi walks down the set of stairs to the main floor of the run-down motel:. .:Takahashi walks through the front lobby of the motel, and enters the front lobby in front of the motel's front desk. As Takahashi walks through the lobby, a disgusted looks comes over his face. We can see numerous flies buzzing around the disgusting front room, and then Takahashi exits the motel through the front doors. Once Takahashi enters the hazy Dallas streets, he walks out to the curb of the walkway, and picks up a nearby piece of concrete. Takahashi then turns around, and hurls the hard piece of concrete through the window of one of the front doors on the motel:. SMASH! .:The concrete piece makes the front window disintegrate upon impact. After the act by the wrestler from the land of the rising sun, we see a couple of locals come rushing into the street. One man in a pair of ripped shorts and a pair of sandles rushes up to Takahashi:. LOCAL MAN: Hey there fella'! Why did you go and do that fer'? .:Takahashi looks down at the curious man, and appears to be disgusted. Without answering, Takahashi walks away from the man, and proceeds up the street. Takahashi continues to walk away from the dumbfounded local Dallas man, leaving the man scratching his head. Takahashi proceeds down the street and into the Dallas afternoon:.
