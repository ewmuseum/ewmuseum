The Burning Flames

The Burning Flames Leviathan Global Wrestling Promo #103 Date: April 12th, 2003 Location: Detroit, Michigan ﻿Global Wrestling Chronicles Chapter Two: The Burning Flames Date: April 12th, 2003 - 11:29 P.M Location: Detroit, Michigan “Deacon Holmes....he destroyed all I had....all I knew on this God Damned earth for the past twentythree years of my mortal life. These very ashes I stand upon, was once my house.” Leviathan says as he walks around the yard, that once was the five story warehouse he called home. Leviathan looks around the fenced in area, to see only some of the framing still standing, but debris everywhere else. He walks through the debris, and under the framing as pieces of drywall still fall from the once stories above. He walks deeper into the debris to find a huge sheet of drywall, covered in dirt and shit of the sort. He kneels down, and grabs onto the drywall piece, and lifts up. He stands and pushes up, then tosses it over to reveal his once loved room. He walks down the badly damaged mini staircase and steps onto the not so same floor. His bed is destroyed, but can be distinguished that it was once a bed. His chair is complete history, as well as his little table. He sits down on the corner of the bed and stares out into nowhere. “I felt all those human feelings in this room. Those undescribable feelings that you don’t mean to feel, but they are there. I experienced every possible thing in this room, and look at it now,” Leviathan says, sorrowful. Leviathan dips his head down and a smile breaks across his frozen face. He stares at his bible, and picks it up. Oddly enough, it is in perfect condition. He flips through the pages, and yet none are damaged. “It says here, in this very bible, in the revelations that the dark lord will once again be set free from his prison. That time is now, as the flames of hell are breathing heavier than they ever had before, lighting a fire with in me. It cannot be stopped.” Leviathan says. “Deacon was the first to feel the burning flames as I drove him to the depths of hell. He learned to not play with fire, and in this case, he was burned. This week, Cold Blood will pay for the ignorant actions of Deacon as well.” Leviathan shouts, as he stands up. “Tragic Hero is my partner in this quest to destroy Cold Blood. The quest of taking two more victims, and adding them to the list. Before, these damned beings were just names to myself...now they are my passion. The burning fire deep within my black heart has made destroying people my passion...my life desire.” Leviathan says to himself. Leviathan drops the bible in his hand to the ground and stares at it. He walks up the staircase, back up into the debris from the fire. He dips his hands into his pockets and slowly walks, looking through what was once his. He walks through the yard and exits through the ripped fence. He looks back once more, for what will be a last time. “Things are different now...my past has been ruined...but my future is blazing...Cold Blood...things are starting to heat up, so watch out.” Leviathan says, walking into the misty night.
