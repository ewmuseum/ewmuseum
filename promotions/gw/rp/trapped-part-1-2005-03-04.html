Trapped (Part 1)

4 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th March 2005 Freedom? It is amazing how we all take it for granted. To walk down a street and breathe the fresh air is seen as freedom. To talk to another fellow human being is seen as freedom. In fact everything you do in your life no matter how minor falls under the title of “freedom”. Freedom is a cause that people have both fought and died for. The most well known is the American war of Independence, where millions of Americans fought for their freedom and won. Nowadays most of the world is seen as free, there are still conflicts in countries where there are tyrannical rulers but the majority of the world is free. However whilst the rest of the world walks around basking in their freedom, there is one man who is not free. There is one man who for the passed week has been locked up, there is one man who has no escape and that one man is me? Let me bring you up to speed on the events that have happened in my life recently. It all began about 2 weeks ago when I was coming back from Australia after wrestling in a Platinum Series match, which I lost to the man who is now No.1 Contender. Anyway I finally thought my life was back on track and that I had finally solidified my place in the GW roster, how wrong I was. After a car journey with my friend, Peter Willows, I arrived home and was ready to spend some quality time with my family. That was not to be however as I entered my home and found that it had been trashed. Worst of all was that my wife and child were nowhere to be found and all that was left of them was ripped clothing and drops of blood on my kitchen floor. As I took in the horror, I was knocked unconscious by someone from behind. That is where I left you last time and here is the rest of the story? The people that had trashed my home and had taken my wife and child were associates of a man I met last year named Richard Wood. He and his daughter were kind enough to give me a flight home so I could see my daughter being born. However something about the name Richard Wood bothered me and so I asked Peter to look it up for me. After a few weeks of searching Peter found that Richard Wood was actually the man who owned the rights to my bio suit since the original manufacturers were bought out by Richard Wood. This meant that not only did Wood own the suit but in a certain way he owned me as well. Peter thought that Wood had no idea of the suits existence but after the scene at my home, it was obvious he did. Wood has brought me in for tests and to test the suit’s strengths and weaknesses. He told me that I would still participate in GW events but under the watchful eye of his business, this explains the scenes you saw at the last Evolution. You are probably wondering why I am going along with all this and not putting up much of a fight? Well I wasn’t given much choice as Wood gave me an incentive as to why I had to go along with his plans, my wife and kid. Wood said they were perfectly safe but would only release them once he had finished with me. Therefore I have no choice but to go along with what Wood says. You may think this is against human rights but because of the contract I signed 10 years ago it is perfectly legal. Wood has told me that I need to prove to him that I am testing the suit to its full potential before he will even consider letting my family go. I am stuck in a nightmare I can not wake up from? “Adam?” “Adam?” I could see Rachel’s face smiling in front of me as she said my name in her angelic voice. “Time to wake up Adam.” I reached out towards my wife’s face but her image faded away and was replaced by a bright white light shining into my face. “Rachel!” I shouted feeling disorientated. I quickly sat up and took a look around me. My heart sank as reality began to set in. I was sat in what can only be described as a sterile steel box. The “bed” that a few seconds ago I was lying on was nothing more than a steel bench with a pillow at one end. The floor and walls were all steel and in front of me was a two way mirror that I knew had people behind it watching me. I shivered as a chill ran through my half naked body; I crossed my arms in a vain attempt to keep warm. “How are you feeling today Adam?” Came a voice over the speakers positioned in each corner of the room. “Terrific,” I replied sarcastically as I moved over to the sterile sink and began to brush my teeth. I spat the tooth paste out and looked at the two way mirror, “Am I going to get to see Richard Wood today?” “Mr. Wood will be down shortly to start the tests, Adam, why don’t you try and relax before then.” “Relax!” I said feeling my anger grow, “How the hell am I supposed to relax in a damn steel box!” I smashed my fist against one of the steel walls to rid myself of some of the frustration. Getting angry wouldn’t help Rachel or Emma, but I couldn’t help it? For 7 whole days they had kept me cooped up in this steel box, being monitored 24/7 not getting a moment’s privacy. Every time I went to the toilet, what ever I produced was taken away and examined. I mean what the hell are they going to learn from my urine? But that wasn’t the worst part, the worst part is the tests. Every day they concoct some stupid test, like yesterday where I had to do fighting moves in water to test the suit’s movements in water. God knows what they had in store for me this time round. Plus the fact that I fear for my wife and kid, Wood will not tell me a thing about where they are, he just keeps telling me they are fine and thinks that will satisfy me. My child isn’t even 1 year old and yet they are keeping her captive and for what? Just so they can dangle her in front of my face like some proverbial carrot. I hate them so much but if I am to ensure the safety of my family I have to play along, for now. “Is there any chance of getting some clothes?” I asked now standing in front of the mirror and noticing all I had on was some boxer shorts. “I’m sorry Adam but Mr. Wood has insisted that you wear nothing but the suit.” “But why?” I asked wondering why a T-shirt and a pair of jeans would affect anything. “I can not give that information Adam, I’m sorry.” “Fine.” I said counting to 10 in my head to subdue my anger. I headed over to the “bed” and sat down and just as I did I heard the door clamps slam open. The air pressure released and the door slid open, for a split second I thought about charging at the door but I knew any struggle would be futile. I stood up and stared at the door already knowing who was coming through it. Richard Wood stepped through with a smug smile on his face. Every fibre of my being wanted to lash out and smack that smile off his face but again it would be a futile effort as he was followed by two huge men who were built like brick houses. “Good morning Mr. Wood!” Came a chorus of voices through the speakers. “Good morning,” Wood replied holding his hand up at the people behind the mirror before turning to look at me, “And good morning to you Adam.” “Where are they?” Came my reply as I clenched and unclenched my fists. “Oh Adam, Adam, Adam,” Wood sighed as he moved closer towards me followed by the two men, “Must you always ask that” I have told you your family are perfectly safe and happy.” “I highly doubt that.” “Adam, I am hurt, do you not trust me?” “What do you think?” I said more as a statement than a question. “Well you are just going to have to trust me Adam, if you are to ever see your family again.” “Do you get off on keeping people against their will Wood?” “I am not keeping you against your will Adam.” “Oh yeah so what is with the two thugs behind you then?” I said pointing at the two men. “They are simply associates of mine.” Wood stated. “Yeah right.” “Anyway Adam,” Wood said choosing to ignore my disbelief, “Guess what I have just had faxed over to me from the GW offices?” “What?” “It is the card for the next Pay-Per-View, High Octane.” “So?” “So it looks like you have got quite the match. It seems you are booked in a ladder match for your Cruiserweight Title against Ami Mizuno and Aradia Xiomara. You faced Aradia at last week’s Evolution didn’t you” It was a close match.” “Yeah only because of you.” “And what do you mean by that?” “Well I don’t know if you noticed but at the beginning of the show I was thrown out of a van by some of your stooges onto a concrete floor. It nearly dislocated my shoulder, I am not taking anything away from Aradia but if your stooges hadn’t done what they did then the match wouldn’t have been as close as it was.” “Now Adam, I have apologised for that a million times and told you that I fired those responsible.” “Don’t talk crap Wood! The ugly sisters behind you were driving the damn van and I saw those stooges of yours in the lab yesterday!” Wood just stared at me for a while; I could see him trying to think of an excuse but he simply shook his head. “That is not the point Adam. The point is the match at High Octane. Now a ladder match is a great place to test the suit in a combat situation.” “A ladder match is hardly a combat situation. Besides what do you think I have been doing the past 10 years?” “Yes, but we were not collecting data on the suit back then and a ladder match is a perfect test for the suit.” I sat down on the “bed” again and hung my head. I could feel everyone’s eyes on me and all I wanted to do was scream in fury but I would not give them the satisfaction. I wouldn’t let them know they were getting to me. Wood clapped his hands together as I looked up at him. “Right well we better get you ready for the journey, it is a long way to Detroit.” Wood turns to one of his men, “Bring the suit in will you Charlie.” The man obviously known as Charlie walks out of the room and brings my bio suit in on a trolley. I stare at the suit, knowing that is the reason I am here, that is the reason I am playing Wood’s stupid game! “We will leave you to get changed.” Wood says as the 3 of them begin to walk out. “By the way I wanted to ask you something Wood.” I shouted after Wood as I stood up. “What is that?” Wood asked as he turned around to face me. “Does your daughter know what a twisted little shit you are?” Wood’s expression instantly changed, no longer was he smug but he was furious. “You son of a”!” Wood yelled as he smacked me across the face as hard as he could. “Don’t you dare talk about my daughter, you freak! You think you are so clever, well don’t you forget I own you Adam! I say jump, you ask how high!” “Screw you!” I screamed back at him now directly in his face. “Screw me”!”! Screw me?!”! No, no, screw you Adam! At High Octane, Charlie is going to accompany you so to keep you in line! And you remember, you, you, freak, you remember that I have eyes everywhere and there is nowhere you can run that I can’t find you! Now get ready for High Octane!” With that Wood turns around and walks out through the door followed by his henchmen. The door closes and I sit down on the bed again. I reach over and grab my mask and simply look at it? This is the reason for all my pain, this is the reason for all my suffering. At High Octane I am no longer fighting for my belt but for the survival of my family. How far will a man go who has nothing to lose? You will find out at High Octane!
