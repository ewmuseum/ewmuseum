Checking up, Part 1

9 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/03 Brick has come to visit his friend Aaron and Aaron’s wife Felicia. He’s come to see how his friends are holding up in lieu of the recent edition of Evolution, whereupon Salaok aired footage of the time when the Nevermore held Aaron hostage and subjected him to sadistic torture. Among the images that were shown: Draven continually sticking his lit cigarette into Aaron's arm, Aaron being hosed down with freezing cold water. Aaron going for days without food and having the entire Nevermore eat in front of his face. Salaok breaking his fingers. Aaron having the back of his calf sliced open and hot ash placed inside the wound and many more horrid things. It was a painful time in both men’s lives. It was a time each of them has tried to put behind them. Salaok has refused them that luxury. “I can’t believe what that son of a bitch pulled,” says Aaron. “I’m glad you whooped his ass for it.” “It was my pleasure,” Brick responds. “I’ve tried for months to put that behind me and he just re-opened the wounds. And then he actually brought back that piece of shit Derek Draven?” “That shouldn’t surprise you,” says Brick. “Draven is Salaok Lite. If there’s anybody he would have brought back, it would have been him.” Felicia’s upset too, and it’s not good for her or the baby.” “Don’t worry about it, man. I’ll deal with his ass.” “Good to have you back in new York, man. I know it wasn’t easy being stuck in Dallas after that shit he pulled with the police.” “Yeah, well, it’s good to be home, and Salaok is not gonna ruin that for me.” “But what about next Monday” You gotta face Chris Camero.” On the last Evolution, Brick dropped a bomb when, after Salaok told him that he was suspended, he produced an official document that negated his suspension, signed by none other than Bubba McCoy. In retaliation, Salaok booked him in a match with the returning Chris Camero, who has seemingly sided with the leader of the Nevermore. “Yeah, I do. I don’t know how the hell he pulled that off, or why he’s hooked up with Salaok. And you know what” I don’t really care. Next week, icon or not, Nevermore or not, Camero’s going down. If he’s stupid enough to pal around with that piece of shit, he’s got to pay the price.” “I have to admit, though,” says Aaron with a smile on his face, “I did like it when you dropped the bomb on Salaok, with that contract. Did Bubba really sign it” I thought he was still in a coma.” “Yeah, he was, but he just came out of it. He should be up and about in a few days.” “So the document is legal” Because Salaok might question it.” “Yeah, he might. But fortunately, I’ve got a lawyer for a brother-in-law, so just in case Salaok wants to bitch, I’m covered. Oh, and I got a little surprise for Salaok this coming Monday on Evolution.” Just then, Felicia enters the room. She’s now 6 months pregnant, but looking no less radiant. “Hey, Felicia.” “Hey, Jamal.” Brick kisses Felicia on the cheek. “Came to see how y’all were holding up. After’you know?” “I wasn’t okay that night, but I am now. I’m trying to stay strong for the baby, if no one else.” Brick looks down at her womb and places a hand on it. “Just three more months till I see my little godson or goddaughter, huh?” “Yes, God willing.” “Listen, guys, I think I’m gonna head out and see how Monica and Kyle are doing.” “You worried about them, too?” asks Aaron. “Yeah, especially after what Salaok said to me during the handicap match.” “What did he say?” “He said, “By the way, Brick, I enjoyed your sister's wedding. But the cake was a little dry, I thought.” “He was at Monica’s wedding?” “So he says, but why take chances” I’ll call you just in case.” Brick embraces Aaron and Felicia before he leaves. As Brick goes out into the hall, his cell phone rings. “Hello” Monica? Yeah, I can be there in a little while. I’m just now leaving Aaron’s. Okay, I’ll be there. Okay, bye.” Brick hangs up the phone and heads for the elevator. “Salaok, you son of a bitch”, Brick growls as the elevator doors close.
