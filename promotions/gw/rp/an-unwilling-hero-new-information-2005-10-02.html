An Unwilling Hero: New Information

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 2, 2005 There was an air of anxiousness present in the banged up blue Impala. Today was the day that the four dastardly young men that made up “The Southside Locs” would collect the ransom money. Even though all of their faces were obscured by their blue bandanas it was very obvious that they were all smiling, not even Carlos’s muffled screaming from the trunk could dampen their spirits as they sped towards their payday. Once the car had reached the vicinity of the rendezvous point Stomper broke the silence by telling Lazy to circle the area around the alleyway a few times just to make sure that there weren’t any law enforcement present. When Stomper was certain that everything was clear he instructed Lazy to pull into the alley and park. “Now what vato?” Creeper asked from the backseat. “We wait for that fucking white boy to show up with the money,” Stomper answered. And so they waited...and waited...and waited some more. After half an hour had passed Stomper hit the dashboard in frustration, he tore the bandana off his face revealing a nasty snarl that was pasted on his lips. He then exited the vehicle and removed the cigarette that rested behind his ear and lit it up. Stomper began to pace back and forth as he tried to figure out what to do about the absence of Biddle. An idea finally came to him and he returned to the car to let his amigos know what he was thinking. “That damn gringo doesn’t care enough about his friend to pay up when we tell him to then there’s only one thing to do,” Stomper paused for dramatic effect, “we have to make him care.” “How the hell are we going to do that?” Lazy asked. “Take us back to the casa and I’ll show you,” Stomper replied. ***** Mike had purposely not shown up at the rendezvous point, he figured that after spending a week with Carlos the kidnappers would return him and the situation would be over with. So instead of trying to find the identity of the four men like he had originally planned, Mike instead spent his time out carousing with women of questionable character. Besides, after the events of the last Evolution Mike had a lot to celebrate and there was only one proper way to celebrate his recent success. Mike didn’t even know the name of the blonde woman that he held in his arms and played tonsil hockey with as they made their way up the front steps of his home. He only knew that she was a beautiful woman in skimpy clothing and that Mike’s fame and fortune aroused her. As they neared the door Mike began to fumble around in his pocket for his keys, which was difficult to do with a beautiful woman shoving her tongue down his throat. Once Mike had his keys in his hand he began to back up towards the door, only to trip on a small object and fall to the ground. Luckily for her the young woman wasn’t injured, Mike had broken her fall. She looked over and picked up a videotape that was on the ground. “I think this is what you tripped over,” she softly said as she handed Mike the tape. “What the hell is this doing out here?” Mike asked no one in particular as he looked at the tape the woman had handed him. His dark blue eyes became wide with shock when he saw that his name was scribbled on the side of the tape along with the words: “we want twice as much now gringo!” The woman detected something was wrong. “What’s wrong?” She asked. Mike was silent for a moment. “I...uhhh,” he paused as he searched for the right words, “I need to watch this, it’s part of a business matter. The spa is in the backyard; I’ll meet you out there in a moment.” The woman nodded her head as they both got up. Mike unlocked the door and they both entered. “I’ll only be a moment,” he said as he bent down to kiss her. As the attractive young woman made her way to the backyard she glanced over her shoulder and saw Mike rush upstairs. Mike entered his upstairs study and locked the door behind him. He popped the tape into his VCR and sat down to see what the tape was all about. The tape opened with the leader of the group standing in a dirty bedroom with only a mattress on the floor, his face obscured by a blue bandana. Mike saw the “Stomper” tattoo on the man’s arm and instantly recognized him. “Hey you fucking gringo,” the man on the tape said, “I have someone here that wants to talk you.” Stomper reaches off camera and violently tugs Carlos into the scene, Mike gasps when he sees the state that the fat young man is in. Carlos’s clothes are torn, his face and body scratched and bruised. Stomper smacks Carlos in the back of the head, causing tears to stream down the victim’s face. “Now say what I told you to say you little faggot,” Stomper screamed in Carlos’s ear. Carlos shook with fear as he looked directly into the camera. “P-please M-Mike,” Carlos stuttered, “just give them what they want...I’m begging you. My life depends on it...” Carlos suddenly broke down with a fit of tears. Stomper then reached up and hit Carlos in the back of the head, effectively knocking the kidnap victim out. Stomper then turned his cold gaze to the camera and spoke. “The price is two million now asshole,” he said, “I’ll be nice this time and give you two weeks to come up with the money but if you don’t pay up on time your little bitch friend is going to die.” The tape abruptly ended there and Mike instantly decided that he should never tell William of the tape’s existence. “I guess this means I actually have to do something now,” Mike sighed as he put the tape in one of the drawers of his desk and locked it inside. The business of finding Carlos could wait until tomorrow though for right now there was a beautiful woman outback and Mike needed to relax a little bit before setting off on his quest. ***** The first thing Mike did when he woke up the next day was call a man that had recently became a hit amongst the Beverly Hills social circle, Dr. Clint Ramey. Dr. Ramey was a sociologist that specialized in the study of street gangs. The growing concern over the recent surge in gang violence around Beverly Hills along with Ramey’s great knowledge of gangs had led to his name being added to many guest lists. The socialites from Beverly Hills figured that Ramey knew some secret answer that would prevent them from being the victims of gang violence. Mike hoped that Ramey would be able to tell him what part of town the gang that kidnapped Carlos was from, that way he wouldn’t have to waste time searching every square inch of Los Angeles. Mike had arranged for Ramey to meet him at one of the sidewalk cafes on Rodeo Drive for lunch. Ramey was an older man in his mid-fifties, his hair silver in color but his body physically fit unlike some other men in his age group. The weather was nice as they both sat outside with their food. Unfortunately, the tourists were out in full force. “I must admit,” Dr. Ramey said, “I was surprised by your invitation.” Mike chewed his food thoughtfully before responding. He had only met Ramey once and that had been at a party well over a month ago. “Well Dr. Ramey,” Mike said, “I’ve had a particularly bad problem and I could use your expertise to help solve it.” “What sort of problem?” Dr. Ramey asked, his interest obviously piqued. Mike took a moment to flex his acting muscles before he told the good doctor the little white lie he had come up with. “I’ve had a graffiti problem the past few days,” Mike said. Ramey’s eyes became wide with excitement, new specimens to study! “Is it still there on the property?” He asked. “No,” Biddle shook his head; “I had the gardener pressure wash it off the walls.” “Did you at least get a picture of it?” The doctor asked, slightly disappointed. Mike shook his head; he couldn’t help but notice the frown on the doctor’s face about the lack of physical evidence. “But I did write down what the graffiti said,” Mike said as he reached into his jeans pocket for a piece of paper. On the piece of paper Mike had written down what the tattoos that adorned the bodies of the kidnappers had said. The doctor looked over them with extreme interest. Finally after a few moments of intense study he spoke. “It looks like you’re dealing with the South Side Locs from Compton, they just recently broke off from the 18th Street Gang. It’s a small but very violent gang; they’ve been breaking into a lot of houses in Beverly Hills recently. If I were you I’d make sure to beef up security around your house, it looks they’re planning on hitting you next,” the doctor explained. Ramey handed the piece of paper back to Mike. “Thank you doctor,” Mike said. “Why didn’t you just call the police?” Ramey asked. “Dr. Ramey,” Mike began, “when you’re famous like me every time you call the police the media picks it up. I prefer to keep out of the local tabloids.” “I see.” Mike then turned the conversation to more pleasant topics before the doctor figured out Mike’s little white lie. After they had finished their meal the two men parted ways with Mike promising to donate a sizable sum to Ramey’s research. Now that Mike was armed with the kidnappers general location he could go rescue Carlos. Sadly, that would have to wait for Mike had to leave for Las Vegas this evening so he wouldn’t be late for Evolution. -To Be Continued-
