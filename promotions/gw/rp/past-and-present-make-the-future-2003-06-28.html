Past and Present make the future

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jun 28, 2003 Ely sits at the table across from his old friend Talon Ravenclaw. Talon is dressed casualty in jeans and a plain white T shirt. And several necklaces around his neck, notably a leather strap, with a brown leather bag attached. Talons hair is pulled back and two feathers hang from his black pony tail. His eyes shine with their unusual color of gold. Ely as well is casually dressed in jeans a black T Shirt with the GW logo on the pocket. And his ever present cowboy hat.. In front of him is a set of papers. A steaming mug and a cigar in an ashtray. “Is this all she sent you Talon?” Ely asks running his hand though his hair and replacing his hat. “Yes Cowboy. That is all your friend Christina sent me.” Talon replies in his deep voice. ? This doesn’t really show me much information.. I do know he is a former wrestler in three sixty wrestling. A former champion there, and now a current free agent. Other then that nothing. NO home. No date of birth. Nothing.” Ely says frustrated. “I have also called some old friends of ours. It is like this man does not even exist. I do not like this Ely. I have a bad vibe about this fight.” “Yeah me to. Is there nothing else you can do.” Ely ask almost desperate sounding, “I can do a spirit walk. See what aid the ancients can give us.” Talon says with a slight smile coming across his face. “Thanks my friend.” Ely says downing the rest of his drink and heading to the door. “I have to get back to Vegas. Call me as soon as you know anything.” Ely says and opens the door. Ely stops and turns and takes a deep breath. “I saw Jade the other day.” Ely says in a cold voice. “So the snake still lives. Be careful Ely she is a dangerous woman. ? Talon says/. The look on his face added to the tone of his voice. “I will be. But She gave me her side of the story. I do think she is telling the truth.” Ely says. “HA! Jade telling the truth. ? ? NO that is wrong. All have to have the chance to earn their honor back. If Jade is, I am happy for her. But none the less be cautious.” Talon says as he walks over to a wall long cabinet. “I plan on it.” Ely says and closes the door behind him? Talon looks at the door for a moment and he takes a deep breath Talon Ravenclaw. Former Special Ops in the United States military. After that a member of Ely’s Mercenary group called the Cell. After his Military career ended he returned home. His grandfather thought him life after being a warrior. That being a shaman. Now his life pulls a three sixty of its own. “Grandfather, give me guidance. I wonder if there has ever been a Warrior Shaman?” Talon says to nobody but his self. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “So you have made contact with Braxton. Good my Dragon good.” Says a male voice coming from a shadowed corner Jade Dragon stands in front of this person, she only nods her head in acceptance of the complement. The normal hustle and bustle of the Los Vegas life style going on all around them in the club they are in. “You must make sure Braxton never wins this. I have assured my clients that Tyrant shall win. Do not fail me in this.” Says the mysterious man. “Yes master. I shall not fail.” Says Jade as she knells to a knee placing her head on to of her knee. ? For your sake Jade. I hope not.” Says the man once again as he places an iron gloved hand on Jades head. “It would be a shame to see your daughter have a unfortunate accident.” “Yes. ? it would. ? Master.” Jades says. All emotion drained from her voice. Jade stands and turns, and all her emotions are betrayed. The rage in her face says it all. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Talon sits crossed leg on a large stone striped of all save his pants. He sits in a almost trance like state. “I call upon the sprits of the elements. I cal out to my ancestors. I call out to aid a brother!” Talon says. He looks to the heavens. “Shall my calls go un answered?” He asks. A small black cloud forms over head and Talon’s head drops? “Your calls are answered youngling.” Booms out a voice. “What assistance we can give we shall” “This youngling is grateful. I require only knowledge of a man who by this ages laws does not exist.” “His name?” Booms the voice. “We only know him by one. By the name Tyrant. With the naming of the voice lightning fills the cloud and the winds pick up. “This is a old name. Not many have worn this name since the first. Know this Ravenclaw. This warrior faces a foe with great and dark power. This Tyrant His power is not as it once was. But only due to his lack of knowledge of his power. Should this man remember his past life.. and his past power. Your friend is in dire trouble. ? Says the voice. This time in a calm concerned voice then its demanding one. “Can he be defeated in one on one combat” ? Ask Talon. “Yes he can. Be weary he will kill the Warrior facing him if he is inclined.” Says the voice. “Thank you great spirits. ? Says Talon. Talons eyes snap open to see the daylight of the surrounding area. Still a while before the setting of the sun. though Talon. “I better get in touch with a lot of people right now ? Says Talon. ? He pulls the cell phone out. “Ely.. Look. This is what I learned?” ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
