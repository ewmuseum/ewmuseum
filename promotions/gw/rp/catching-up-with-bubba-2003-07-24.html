Catching Up with Bubba

24 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/24/03 Brick slowly walks down the hallway. He’s still getting over the outcome of his Hard 10 match with Deacon Holmes. Both men were hospitalized after falling fifty feet from the Globaltron straight through the steel stage. As always, Brick has managed to live to tell the tale. Right now, he’s visiting his employer Bubba McCoy, the owner and CEO of Global Wrestling. Bubba is, himself, recuperating from a serious illness and has been here for months. His survival is testament to his legendary toughness. When he gets there, Bubba is in bed, watching TV. Brick knocks on the door. “Can I come in?” “Well, this is a first. You actually knockin” on a door and askin? to come in.” “Ha ha. Can I come in, please?” “Yeah, sure. Come on in.” Brick walks into the room and sits down in a chair next to Bubba’s bed. “Christina told me you had one hell of a night.” “Yeah, you could say that. It was a long time coming, this thing between me and Holmes. He’s been runnin” his mouth about me so much, I thought it was time for him to back it up. And he did.” “Yeah, I saw what happened when you were celebratin” on Evolution. I gotta admit I laughed my ass off when you gave him those diapers.” “Yeah, well, he tried to step to me like he was a man, but he’s still a boy. And I figured he’d try something to ruin my mood that night, so I had that prepared for him.” “Still can’t believe Salaok made him shit himself. Actually, now that I think about it, I probably can.” “Yeah, and we might have gone at it if Christina hadn’t stepped between us. I gotta say, she’s doing a pretty good job running the company, and no, I’m not kissing ass, no matter what Deacon Holmes might think.” “Brick, I known you for four years. You’ve been a lot o” things but you’ve never been an ass kisser. You never kissed my ass, you damn sure never kissed Ayers? or Lansheer’s asses.” “That’s for damn sure.” “Christina’s been keeping me up to date with the whole T.U.E. thing. Just what this company needs. Another bunch o” jackasses tryin? to take over.” “And now Stecchino’s a member. Same as with the Nevermore.” “Damn shame what happened with Salaok an” his family.” “Surprised to hear you say that.” “Don’t get me wrong. He was a sick and evil bastard, and what he did to the company, and to you, your family and friends was disgustin”, but that don’t mean he deserved what happened. Losin? Lisha and the baby. I wouldn’t wish that on even him.” “Me, neither. I still can’t believe it’s over between us. Just like that. No big rematch from Wrestlecade. Man, I figured it wouldn’t be over between us until one of us was dead or both of us were. And now?” “I know, I know.” “By the way, I never did congratulate you on winnin” the World title. I’m proud of ya, Brick. I really am.” “Oh, go on.” “No, seriously, I am. I remember when I once said you weren’t ready to win the World title. Normally, I don’t like bein” proved wrong, but in this case, I’m glad I was.” “Thanks, Bubba. That means a lot coming from you.” “Aside from Holmes, anybody in T.U.E. makin” any real trouble for ya?” “Not yet, unless you count the damn bodyguards, but I expect that’ll change. I know Stecchino and Price are definitely gonna have their eyes on my title. I ain’t worried, though. I wasn’t scared of the Nevermore and I ain’t scared o” T.U.E.” “Well, I don’t plan on stayin” in this damn hospital bed forever. I have a feelin? Price, Stecchino an? the rest are gonna try something big and I’m gonna have to be there to put a proverbial boot in all their asses.” “Look, you just rest. We can handle T.U.E. until you get back.” “Who’s we?” “Me, Christina, Ely, the Youngbloods.” “Oh.” “Look, I better get out of your way. I’ll see you around.” With that, Brick rises out of his seat. “Hold on there. Stay for a while.” “You sure?” “Yeah, I’m sure.” Brick shrugs his shoulders. “OK. What time is it?” Bubba looks at the clock and says, “Almost one o’clock.” “Oh, good! All MY Children is coming on.” “All My Children?” “Don’t start, Bubba.”
