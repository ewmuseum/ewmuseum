Who do you trust?

20 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20/04 It was late afternoon as Brick walked into his apartment. He went over to his answering machine to see if he had any messages. The blinking red light told him that he did. Brick, needless to say, has had a bittersweet 2005, thus far. In January, his sister Monica gave birth to twins, Randall James and Mary Alice. They were obviously a blessing, but at the same time, Brick knew that they would be targets. In February, his friend and tag team partner Donny McNasty was brutally assaulted by an unseen assailant. The prognosis was that it would take Donny a year to return, if he could return at all. The most obvious suspect was Train. Even though Brick didn't have any solid evidence placing Train at the scene of the crime, Train had motive and opportunity and that was good enough for him. When the two men engaged in a huge backstage brawl, Bubba McCoy decided to pair the two men as a tag team, as the new Ruckus Inc., much to both men's chagrin. 'Three new messages,' the answering machine intoned. BEEP! 'Hey, yo, Brick!' It was Train, Brick's reluctant partner and co-tag team champion. Brick wondered how he got his number. He knew he didn't give it to him. He wanted as little to do with Train as possible. The only reason he was having anything to do with him was because Bubba had ordered them to operate as a tag team. 'We need to deal with that punkass janitor who almost cost us the Tag Titles at High Octane!' Brick rolled his eyes and sighed. The janitor in question, Adam Grey, had come down to the ring during the Tag Gauntlet match at High Octane. When Brick tried to get him out of the ring, Grey sprayed Windex in his eyes, blinding him and almost costing Ruckus Inc. the Tag Team Championship. However, Brick had bigger fish to fry. Namely, finding out who was behind Donny's assault. BEEP! 'What, you can't return a nigga's phone calls?! We need to discuss how we gonna fuck this cat Grey up, man! Need to let that mufucka know who he fucked with! Now quit bein' a fuckin' bitch an' call me back!' Who does this nigga think he is? Brick thought to himself. If Bubba hadn't pulled that bullshit about not being able to touch him-- BEEP! 'I know you there, nigga! Pick up tha mu'fuckin' phone!! Nigga, pick up! Man, this is some bullshit!!!' BEEP! 'End of messages.' Brick pressed 'erase' and then went over to his desk and turned on his computer. He immediately logged onto the Internet and went straight to the Global website. Scrolling down to the 'Columns' section, he notices a new link: The GW Rundown, vol. 2 The last time the Rundown had been on the site, it was around the time Brick began playing with Deacon Holmes' head. Brick decided to click on the link and read this new edition of the Rundown. One part of the column, in particular, caught his attention. ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ ~~~~~ The Shadow Realm Alright, here’s the part I really like. The reality check for Global... straight from the mind of The Shadow. My subject this week? Donny McNasty. Or, rather the possible demise of his career. Or, even more accurately, just how that came about. Don’t get me wrong, I don’t so much object to what happened to him, getting his brains beat in, in the dark. Hell, it’s a rough sport folks and life’s a bitch. Brick wasn't quite sure how to feel about that part. Much of what he said about wrestling being a rough sport was true. However, at the same time, this was a friend of his that the GW Shadow was talking about. What I do object to is the rampant dumbassosity that seems to be catching around GW that keeps everyone from being able to figure out who the perpetrator of that attack was. I mean it’s unbelievable. I mean the guy that should be the prime suspect gets a clean pass with no suspicions while guys who pretty much had no reason to commit the crime (if you wanna call it that) have to bear the brunt. First there’s Train. a man who went so far as to call a man on his cell phone to avoid a face to face confrontation when he and Donny were both in the same building at the same time. Why? Train has made it abundantly clear that his only goal in GW at this point os holding the title. Why get himself bogged down in another brutal war with McNasty (or his army of midgets) when he’s got his eye on the top prize? Then there’s Salaok. Ok, I’ll admit it. Salaok Davis is a sick bastard. I don’t believe for a second this man would have the slightest compunction about hurting Donny McNasty the way he was. Morally speaking, there’s no doubt he’s capable.. And it’s obvious what happened to Donny gut under Brick’s skin so that could be a possible motivation. But here’s the thing. The same reason Salaok is a suspect is the same reason he wouldn’t do it... Salaok Davis is a sick bastard. In this ugly fued with Brick, Salaok has committed many an atrocity, including to threatening Brick’s infant niece and nephew. And that proves one thing. Salaok isn’t interested in the mundane. Beating up Brick’s tag partner isnt? depraved enough for Salaok’s sick tastes. When he does something, I assure you it’ll be a lot worse than what happened to Donny. A lot of that statement made sense to Brick. While Salaok was a suspect, it still didn't seem Machiavellian enough. Also, Salaok had often chosen victims who couldn't fight him off, otherwise, terrorizing Brick's friends and family. Like a hyena, he went after the relatively weakest prey. Donny could take care of himself when it counted. So that brings us back to the begining. Who done it? Actually the answer is obvious for anyone willing to pull their head out of the ass long enough to look.... Bubba McCoy Yeah... that’s right... you heard me. Think about it for a second. The second Donny got “hurt” Bubba’s only real objection was that one of his major draws had been snatched in Ruckus Inc. Oh, but he didn’t waste much time finding a replacement for Donny in his brand name tag team, now did he? And let’s be clear folks, for you folks not int he know of the goings ons behind the closed doors of the board rooms of GW. It’s no secret amongst GW officials that Bubba has had a hard on to see the two big, black dudes from the streets of Harlem and Chicago running ruckshot through the ranks of GW. I mean damn... talk about marketable. Especially as GW Tag Team Champions. So, the only issues keeping that from happening basicly get nixed, one after the other, in the grand span of 2 whole weeks. First, of course, there’s McNasty himself. He comes to the ring to celebrate valentines day, to talk about who his heart beats for and somebody winds up nearly tearing it out. Talk about irony. Oh, and then there’s the little fact Brick and Train HATE EACH OTHER’S GUTS!!! Oh, but since nobody can prove anybody else’s guilt or innocence they’re forced to work together lest they be suspended in definitely. All because Brick assumed Train was the one that hit Donny and proceeded to try and take his head off. I mean in that context Bubba McCoy was simply trying to protect his investments. Perfectly reasonable. Not to mention convenient. Rest assured folks, none of this is a coincidence. This was a well crafted, well orchestrated plan. The only real question is not who’s behind it, but who McCoy got to do his dirty work for him. I mean come on folks. I know he maintains that “I’m a real live cowboy” image... GW’s version of President Reagan... but the fact of the matter is cowboy’s don’t wear $2,000 suits, and the ones who do damn sure ain’t gonna get their hands dirty. You know... GW’s version of President Bush. The point is this the mastermind isn’t nearly as interesting as the hitman contracted to take Donny out. But that’s a story for another time... Brick didn't want to think about someone he greatly respected being behind Donny being put out of commission. True, he was still a bit angry with Bubba for forcing him to tag team with Train, but at the same time, he wanted to believe that Bubba was an honorable man. On the other hand, Brick had been betrayed before. ---- Place: Honolulu, Hawaii Bubba McCoy was walking out of his hotel room. He had to deal with all the arrangements for the special edition of Evolution. Special not only because of its location, but because it was the only Evolution in March. Next would be Clash of the Titans in Rome. 'Bring the limo around. I'll be down in a little while.' When Bubba got out to the entrance, he saw Brick, leaning against the limousine, his arms folded. 'We need to talk.' Brick then stepped aside, allowing Bubba to step into the limo before entering himself. 'So, Brick, since you've decided to bum a ride in my limo, what do you want?' 'Have you been reading the Rundown?' Brick asked. 'I've been readin' a bit of it.' 'I take it you read the GW Shadow's ideas as to who might have had Donny taken out.' 'Total bullshit,' Bubba replied. 'Still, a lot of what he said made sense.'' 'Did I have Donny taken out, Brick? Is that what you're askin' me?' 'I dunno. Did you?' 'Oh, so now you're lettin' some gossip-monger affect your judgment? I'da thought better o' you, Brick, especially considerin' he wrote that you're from Harlem, when your bio clearly states you're from Brooklyn.' 'Still, Bubba, you have to admit, you did decide on me an' Train bein' tag team partners pretty damn quickly.' 'I had to make a business decision as hard as it was, an' maybe if you hadn't tried to take Train's damn head off, I might not have made that decision.' 'And for that matter, how the hell is it your decision as to who I tag with?' 'Maybe because I own the damn company?' 'Not Ruckus Inc., you don't. There's a reason they call me the CEO of Ruckus Inc.' 'Actually, Brick, you started that.' 'Regardless, man, you should have left finding a new tag team partner to me!' 'I would have if you hadn't acted like such a damn fool!' 'You know how I get when someone I care about gets hurt!' Brick roared in Bubba's face. 'I already failed Aaron! I ain't gonna fail another friend!' For the longest while, there was silence between the two men. Brick then turned away from Bubba and stared out the window. 'If you want, I can help you find out who attacked Donny. You can trust me. You know me.' 'Yeah,' replied Brick, still looking out the window. 'I do.' 'In the meantime, try to concentrate on your match with Corvus. It's a Platinum Series match and it could be big, so you need to focus.' Brick once again had to face the Dark Prince. The first time they ever faced one another in the ring, Brick lost, tapping out to the Dark Embrace. The second time was the 2-28-05 edition of Evolution in the main event. Brick was strangling Salaok, getting ready to kill him. The only thing stopping him was Corvus' intervention. Eventually, Corvus eliminated Brick after a Descent into Hell. This time, it was personal. Corvus stopped Brick from dealing with his worst enemy once and for all. That wasn't something Brick was willing to let slide.
