After the ambush

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-5-03 Needless to say, it was not a good night for Brick or for Ely Braxton. Brick was to face John Zero of the Upper Echelon and Ely was to be the special guest referee. At first, Brick was dominating the match. Of course, a one-on-one match with any member of T.U.E. inevitably becomes a numbers game. That was certainly the case when Zero started a fight with Ely, giving Sean Price the opportunity to interfere in the match. After knocking Ely out with a steel chair, he then proceeded to give Brick the Appex onto the steel chair, enabling Zero to get the three count. Now, Brick and Ely had to be looked over by the medical staff. To say these two men were unhappy about the turn of events would be a gross understatement. “Goddamn sonsabitches!!” Ely growled as the EMT looked him over. “Tell me about it,” Brick snarled in reply. “I ever find out who in TUE it was that interfered in that match?” “HA! Like you care. You’ll just go after all of “em.” This was true. Brick had recently made fighting TUE a personal crusade, just as he had with the Harvesters. TUE was looking either to dominate Global Wrestling or destroy it. Neither option pleased Brick. He helped build GW for four years into the company it was. As far as he was concerned, no one was going to mess with it. Just then, Christina McCoy walked up to Ely and Brick. “How are they?” she asked the paramedic. “Few bruises, no breaks.” “Oh, Ely,” she said, wrapping her arms around Ely’s neck. Ely then wrapped his left arm around the small of Christina’s back. “Hey, darlin”. Any word on Bubba?” “Nothing. No ransom note, nothing.” “You still think it was TUE?” “It had to be. There’s no one else who it could have been.” “They do have an alibi. I mean, staff said they were in their private box when it happened.” “Well, who else could it have been” How many other power hungry egomaniacs do we know who could have done it?” “Maybe it was?” “No,” Brick replied, bluntly. “It wasn’t him. I’d know if it was.” “When you ran backstage, you didn’t see anyone?” “Nobody, and I ran everywhere.” “Surprised you had the energy for that match,” said Ely. “I’m surprised you could still referee. When I find out who it was in TUE that jumped me?” “I know who it was. It was Price.” “Probably still pissed because I beat his ass last week. I’ll deal with him sooner or later.” “We’ll deal with them all. Especially if they’re behind Dad disappearing.”
