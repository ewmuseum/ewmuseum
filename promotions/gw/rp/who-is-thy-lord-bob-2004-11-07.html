The voice echoed across the large room. The clicking of footsteps bounced off the walls around Vivica J. Valentine as she looked unknowingly around the room. Suddenly the lights came on, and the massive hall of the Zenith Mansion came into view. There were old statues lining the checkered dark and light marble floor and gray stone-like walls. A black rug ran from the entrance of the hall back to a door, which lead to Christian's personal observatory. Where he went to relax, train, study... everything a man could ever need rested in that room. Christian Zenith stood in front of that room, tall in his black custom-fitted 3 piece suit. He adjusted his tie as he approached Vivica J. Valentine, his cadet blue eyes glimmered from the lights above. As did his Global Television Championship, which was around his waist. 'Do you sleep with that thing on?' Vivica asked Christian jokingly. Zenith looked down at his belt and the back up at Vivica J. Valentine and smiled a bit. 'Let's just say I'm very proud of my accomplishments, and I plan to add to my collection very soon,' Christian replied, 'it seems I've been entered in a tournament for their precious GW World Championship. There is nothing I'd like to do more, than to prove to all those pathetic redneck, low class losers, that I'm no 'rookie.'' Vivica looked back at Christian Zenith in confusion as he continued. 'Since the day I stepped foot into Global... it has been nothing more than a hobby to me. Something to pass the time, and as a psychologist might say, a healthy outlet for my built up frustrations that I've accumulated over my life. Trust me, as you know there are quite a bit of those.' Christian paused before going on, 'As much as they push me around and call me a rookie and treat me like I'm not anything, I don't mind... I guess I'll take their petty superiority complex, for now. Of course the less they expect from me, the more surprising it is when they are on their asses and don't remember what happened to them.' 'I made my first move to dominance when I beat their Television and Platinum Champion this past Monday, Ven Pendragon it was. Some kind of raving lunatic they picked up off the street, probably a homeless bum living in a fantasy land. A modern-day Don Quixote... someone needs to wake up to the reality.' Christian looks over at Vivica who smiles a bit in response, 'Which is where I come to where we are now. I've asked you to come here because they're match me up against Pendragon again in the first round of the tournament, and put my Television Championship on the line, but not his coveted Platinum Championship. A complete lack of respect won't be tolerated anymore! It's time we make our move.' 'How are we going to do that Christian?' Vivica replied with a smile. Finally it looked like she was going to get some action. She's never been one to stand on the side and play cheerleader, it wasn't her style. It's ate her up inside sitting in the back or in the seats or at ringside watching Christian get all the fun. 'We've already robbed Pendragon of his title... it's time we take away his pride.' Christian said firmly, 'It seems Pendragon has been receiving orders from some guy that thinks he's 'God' that he calls 'Bob.' I think we all know how I feel about Gods.' Christian looked back at Valentine as she chuckled, remembering several church experiences she's been slightly involved in, 'It's time we find this 'Bob' fellow, and let him know how much of a help he's been to us.' Nothing suffocates you more than The passing of everyday human events Isolation is the oxygen mask you make Your children breath into survive But I'm not a slave to a god That doesn't exist But I'm not a slave to a world That doesn't give a shit The first place to look of course, would be Ven Pendragon's favorite restaurant... or at least that was the word on the street. They journey lead them to a themed restaurant named Medieval Times. Instantly, the structure reminded Christian of Pendragon... old and horribly tacky, it was perfect in every way. Christian looked over at Vivica, who eyed the place as they stepped out of their Black Chrysler Crossfire. A huge fireball erupted out of the middle of the restaurant as they approached. 'Cheesy pyros and all... this place has to get us somewhere.' Christian spoke as the approached the huge drawbridge entrance to the restaurant, which was more like a small stadium. The middle was open, a dirt area... where shows went on and pyros were fired. At this point in time, a duel was taking place between two actors. 'Wait... please tell me this whole thing is a joke and this is just a lame attempt to take me out on a date Christian.' Vivica chuckles as she looked over at Christian. Zenith just looked back at her, making no sort of facial expression at all. Then by walking away, Vivica knew that this couldn't be a joke. She looked baffled as he marches down the stairs towards a beer wench. Zenith stood behind the humble wench as she poured a glass of Guinness to a man, deeply interested in the show. She turned around to resume her job and ran into Zenith. She stumbled backwards in shock, while Christian dusted off his jacket from the head-on wreck. 'Excuse me dear sir, my apologies for my haste. I am but a humble beer wench, if you do not mind I must get back to my duties.' She said with a smile. Christian just looked back at her. 'Um... okay, yeah.' She said before she began to whisper, this time out of character 'I get off in like 30 minutes, I'd really appreciate it if you could move. I'm sorry about the jacket, it's just my mind is in other places right now, I promise not to bother you again.' 'Who is your King?' Christian said looking down at the teenage girl, seconds before the crowd roared in approval as the hero of this particular duel has gained advantage. 'Out kind is King Richard IV, he is seated up in thou balcony. It's right there in front of everyone... he must of course have thy perfect view of all the battles.' She said, hurriedly trying to get rid of Christian Zenith. Zenith turned around and headed up the stairs, where Vivica looked away like she didn't know him. He came to the top of the steps and looked into her eyes. He could tell immediately that she didn't take any of this seriously and she really just wanted to go back to the hotel. 'King RICHARD... and 'Bob.' C'mon Vivica, do you really think that's a coincidence?' Christian questioned. 'Yes.' Vivica said with a little bit of attitude, and without a single bit of hesitation. 'Obviously Ven Pendragon is a former actor here who has gone COMPLETELY crazy. His skewed since of reality must've made all of this real to him. This man, the only man of 'power' he can remember must be his almighty 'Bob'. Once we take this 'Bob' before him, and make him cower down in my presence. Pendragon will bow down to my feet, a defenseless cowardly and confused psychopath, exposed for what he truly is.' Zenith spoke. 'So you're trying to tell me, that this actor... up there in that plastic booth, has been telling Pendragon what to do this entire time in Global?' Vivica questions, 'That doesn't make any sense at all... I think you're the one who has gone crazy.' 'Not necessarily...' Christian pauses before he goes on, 'Pendragon could simply think this man has been giving him orders all along, when in all seriousness they may not have seen each other in years. Hell... this 'King Richard' fellow may not even remember his dear fellow actor. But either way, it doesn't make a difference to me... I just need him.' Zenith says pointing up to the booth where the King claps with delight. 'Or he could just be a whack job...' Vivica paused, expecting a response, 'Whatever, but I get to kick him.' Vivica said, giving up on the debate. 'That's fine, I'd expect nothing less from you.' Christian said, before heading towards the staircase. FIGHT!) But I'm not a slave to a world That doesn't give a shit (FIGHT! FIGHT!) The crowd roars as Christian ascends the staircase with Valentine on his coattails. Chants pursue the duo upstairs as the modern day gladiators battle in the arena. As they approach the balcony two security guards stand in front of them... 'Sorry, this is an employee only zone.' One security guard said, complete in light blue rent-a-cop uniform. Christian looked over at Vivica, who was obviously dying to say something. It was a only a matter of time until she let it fly, she never shut her mouth for long. 'Aren't you suppose to speak thou old English, swine?' Vivica said finally, after a good 3 seconds of biting her tongue. The security guard in question instantly got on the defensive, grabbing his tazer from his right belt loop. The other one looked on, in anticipation. 'What are you going to do? Tazer a girl?' Christian said, as he reached for his .357 Magnum. Little did the rent-a-cops know that he left that particular gun in his car tonight. The security guard saw what Christian was doing and went to tazer him. Suddenly the security guard with the tazer screamed and fell down before rolling down the stairs. Vivica waved as the guard went rolling down the stairs with a smile, she was the one who kicked him in the groin after all. The other guard went to tazer Valentine, who had her back turned but was quickly introduced to Christian's right foot. The shot knocked the security guard into the stone wall head first. He dropped to one knee before getting an enviguri to the back of the head from Zenith. The other guard rolled down the stairs and into his partner, making them both hit the floor again. The two looked up at the top of the staircase where Zenith cracked his neck and looked back down at them. They quickly got to their feet and ran in the other direction. 'Well, that was almost worth the price of admission.' Vivica said out loud, not realizing Christian had already reached the top of the staircase and stood firmly behind King Richard, not realizing that someone was behind him. King Richard stood up to decide the fate of the loser of the duel below. The crowd screamed their answers to him from below as the victor held his sword at the losers neck. King Richard held his fist and thumb out sideways, before Christian grabbed him in a rear choke hold and whispered into his ear. 'Move... scream... do anything, and I break your neck... Bob.' The death of one is a tragedy The death of one is a tragedy The death of one is a tragedy But death of a million is just a statistic The crowd talks amongst themselves in a state of confusion. The actor portraying the defeated knight stood up and looked up at King Richard, who was in the grasp of a strange man. The Queen stood up to run away, but was stopped by a smiling Vivica J. Valentine who waved as she blocked her way out. The Queen snarled before slowly going back to her seat. 'What do you want from me, thief.' The Kind said through his teeth, trying his best not to startle the audience... also trying not to die. 'What do you know about Ven Pendragon?' Zenith said in his ear, tightening his grip. 'I don't have a clue what you're talking about...' The Kind said before Christian tightened his grip even more. Richard struggled to break free, losing breath. 'C'mon Bob. Don't you think it's a bit obvious? I know who you are... I know what you're doing. This is what happens to assholes like you who play God. Using the weak and the retarded to do your dirty work. The difference between people like me and people like you is, I never deceived them, they knew what I was and what they were a part of from day one. For the most part I didn't even want them, they just came, and that's their own business. I'm a lot of things Bob, but a liar isn't one of them. If you want something done, do it yourself like a man.' Zenith said in Richard's ear. 'My... name... is... Gary.' The King said before losing consciousness from a lack of oxygen. Zenith let go and King Richard fell to the floor. The crowd gasped as Christian Zenith stepped into the spotlight in his place. The Queen looked up at the man in black in fear as he stood up on the chair. 'Hear me puppets!' Zenith screamed across the crowd, who quickly hushed in response. They all looked up at him, every man, woman, and child within the call of his voice. The actors down below stared up at Zenith in confusion. 'You see... this man below me, lying on the ground like a baby, is a man you let control you. You look up to him as a king, as a God. That is what makes you all weak, that's what makes you all unsuccessful. Instead of going to the very limit of your existence to be everything that you could achieve, you bow down to another person. Be it your boss, your parents, your God. In fear of empty promises and broken dreams you all bow down to some kind of asshole who think he's the greatest thing to walk the earth.' Zenith observes his surrounding before continuing. 'They say jump and you jump. They say bow and you bow. They say sit and you sit. They say pray, and by your God you pray. Why? Because you couldn't survive on your own. The thought of controlling your own destiny scares the life out of you, so you put it in someone else’s hands. Either someone who doesn't want it, someone who does want it and uses it to drive you into the ground, or someone who doesn't exist. That is the definition of weakness, THAT my puppets is why you were born worthless and why you all will DIE with no purpose.' Zenith preaches over the crowd who scream back inaudible responses in disgust. 'It is in that very principle in which you all cower to my presence. Where you see fear in chance in walk away, I see opportunity and excel. Where you guys see opportunity and turn it down in immorality, I seize the day and take it as my own. A lot of you may not agree with me, but some of you may. But if you don't agree with me, take a look at the facts. I'm standing here in a spotlight preaching to you all, where a man... just like every single one of you.. who had a chance to stand up for what he believed in is here, at my feet. Limp and disgraced, lying on the ground like a dog.' Christian speaks before spitting on the unconscious king at his feet. 'For those of you who know my name... and even for those who do not head my words. You see 'Thy Lord King' is just like every single one of you. Dillusional... weak... unable to escape his destiny of being my bitch for all of eternity. Just like I took his precious gold, in a few days I will take his soul, his pride, and everything take makes him a man. By the end of the night, 'Thy Lord King' will be nothing more than 'Thy Harmless Bitch'. He is just the first of many to fall to my feet. I will lead... or I will not be a part. Worship is for the weak... the lambs of insecurity lead to slaughter at my command.' Zenith screams in defiance to the rowdy crowd below before pointing down to the actor playing the victorious knight in battle. Zenith then makes the thumbs down motion. 'He is not the champion... I AM THE CHAMPION!' Zenith screams holding his fist high in the air above his head. Christian Zenith looks around at the various people staring up at him before he smirks and steps out of the spotlight. Christian Zenith and Vivica J. Vivica looks over at Christian, who is driving away. 'Do you think that was Bob?' Vivica questioned. 'No... his name is Gary.' Zenith responded, 'He didn't seem so kingly when he was lying unconscious in a puddle of his own urine did he?' Vivica giggled a bit at the comment, mentally picturing the look on his face when he wakes up in a puddle of his own urine. 'So why did you do it?' Vivica asked. 'I was merely stating the truth, it's something all these people wanted to hear. On another note, you never know... word might get back to that crazy old coot Pendragon, he might just do the smart thing and not show up at all.' Christian answered, staying focused on the road. The rest of the ride home was rather silent considering the excitement just seconds before. One thing was clear though, whether it be Pendragon, Maverick Reno, Josh, or Jesus Christ himself... Zenith is ready to show the world he bows to no man.
