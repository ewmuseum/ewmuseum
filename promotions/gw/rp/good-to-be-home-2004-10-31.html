Good to be Home

31 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-31-04 The dark street stretches for blocks. Children are visible in the hot spots under the dim street lamps. The jack-o-lanterns spotting the windows on the extravagant homes are burning a dull orange. One of these homes is more than just another house on the street. It is the home of the newest family on the block. Not Tim and Sandy Jones, not John and Martha Blackwell. None of these ordinary families live in this house. The family of this particular house is non other than the returning GW superstar Derek Frost, his beautiful wife Cindy, and their 4 month old son Damon. Derek is seen through a window, looking out at the smiling children in their costumes. He wipes the condensation off the window, then dries his hand on his pants. His eyes are deep in though as he stares at the ghosts, goblins, monsters, superheros, and mythical creatures outside. Before long, his wife gets up from the couch and walks over, putting her hand on the small of his back as she glances out the window with him. Derek jumps a little, then settles down quickly. 'Oh, I didn't mean to startle you,' says Cindy. 'What are you so intent on?' 'Children. Their sheer happiness at such a simple thing as getting to pretend to be somebody they aren't.' Cindy wastes no time with a reply. 'It's a thing you and I take for granted when we get to do it. Then again, we sorta get paid to play dress up. Not that we do, because everything the GW fans see is everything we are.' Derek smiles a little and turns around, putting his hands on his wife's hips. He kisses her on the forehead, then whispers in her ear, 'So, is our son sleeping upstairs?' Cindy smiles and kisses her husband on the cheek right as the doorbell rings. The couple looks at each other, Derek with a playfully disappointed look on his face, but soon they are answering the door. The children yell 'Trick or treat!!' at the top of their lungs, and Derek steps off to the side to rub his ears. Cindy grabs the bowl of candy and lets the children have their pick of the goodies. As soon as she closes the door Derek grabs a few pieces of chocolate out of the bowl. 'Derek, those are for the kids. If you want some you'll have to wait til later,' snaps Mrs. Frost. 'But what if I can't wait til later to 'get some'? What if I want 'some' now? Besides, those were the last kids on this side of the street, everyne on the block has been here. Except for the Nelson family, but they're shoved so far up the Catholic Church's ass that they would never do anything fun,' says Derek, using 'air quotes' as needed. Cindy smiles and sits down in the oversized armchair next to the stairway. Derek gets up and flops down in the chair, his legs draped over his wife's lap. Just as they lean toward each other for a kiss, the doorbell rings again. 'Damn. Stopped by the bell again.' 'Ignore it, its my turn to get my sugar,' replies Derek. 'Besides, it will just be another kid in a rubber mask and blue jeans, or a teenager with facepaint slopped on by his half-drunk mother.' 'Derek, why would you say that about somebody's mother on halloween?' 'Hey, I've seen plenty of parents using their kid's pitchfork as a cane because they were too intoxicated to stand on their own. It's sad that it takes that much to let a kid have a good time. Mmmm, speaking of a good time....' Derek trails off as he goes to kiss Cindy's ear as the doorbell rings once more, prompting Cindy to stand up, dumping her husband like a Mexican out of a pickup after crossing the border. Cindy opens the door to see a male body in the doorway, about 6'0, black jeans and shoes, white hoodie, and a mask resembling that of none other than George W Bush himself. Standing next to the 'president' is a woman, slightly shorter and in blue jeans and a black windbreaker and a John Kerry mask. Cindy laughs as the two masked figures in the doorway yell out 'Vote for me!' simultaneously. Derek hears this and looks up only to tell his wife 'see hun, I told you all the kids were gone, these two goons wouldn't be seen anywhere near middle class children.' 'George' and 'John' pull their masks off to reveal a young man with ear-length black hair, and a cute young lady with shoulder length brown-blonde hair. 'Well well well, I'm not surprised to see you sitting on your ass on Halloween, Derek,' says the guy. 'Yeah, Troy, well I'm sorry for not being as interested in checking out 6 year olds as you are,' replies Frost. 'HEY! I resent that comment!' says the lady in the door. 'No Maggie, you resemble that comment,' quips Derek again. Troy and Maggie make their way inside and sit on the couch. Cindy sits on Derek's lap in the chair and the two couples begin a conversation. Unbeknownst to them, there is more than just a camera focusing intently on this chat between friends. A shadowy figure steps away from the window on the back side of the house and disappears into the dark.
