Out on the Town pt.2 (repost)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***The 'Sharpshooters Bar & Grille' is in the same state as when we last left it. It's just about a packed house with people eating and watching the meriad of television screens and equal number of sporting events. However, attention from those events are waining while notice of the tense gathering in the back corner, in the area of the pool tables, is growing. Train looks to be survey the selection of pool cues, looking down their barrels, determining their worthiness to be his weapon of choice. The crowd seems to be gathering around the middle pool table, a large pile of cash sitting dead center in the table next to it. Travis the cowboy stands, propped up on his cue, his friends still stroking his ego. Train finally picks a stick and calmly chalks it up. Travis grabs the triangle and starts to rack up the balls. But, despite the stakes Train seems more interested to watch the replays of GW matches playing on the tv's around the pool tables.*** Travis- Alright my friend... ***He lines up the cue ball and hunches over the table, readying his stick.*** Travis- Hope you didn't need that money for rent and pampers. Train- Keep talkin' shit I'll be hopin' you didn't need them teeth for nothing. Break 'em chump. Travis- That anxious to lose your money, huh? No problem. ***Travis gets a decent break on the balls, knocking in a solid. Travis gets a decent run of three balls, but misses on the fourth. The crowd continues to gather little by little as the contest goes on. Train and Travis trade shots and misses for about 20 minutes. Ariella, the waitress returns with Train's order as Travis continues shooting.*** Ariella- Here you go. Sorry it took so long. Train- It's all good. *sips his beer* ***Travis is still on the table, on a small role, but Ariella can see Train is only half paying attention, most of his attention locked on to a classic match with GW superstar Brick.*** Ariella- You know, when most guys are looking at losing five grand they usually pay more attention to the competion than some wrestling match on tv. Train- Yeah well... Let's just say I'm a student of the game. Ariella- Mmmmhmmm... Look, don't take this as a pick up line, cuz it's not, but... Haven't I seen you somewhere before? Train- I don't know if you seen me before, but you can see all of me you want later. Ariella- Cute. I'm serious. Train- Shit, me too. Ariella- hahaha... Funny. Look I.... *looks at tv* Wait a second... ***Her hand shoots up to cover her mouth as she looks at the screen. Her reaction draws everyone's attention to the television. The replay of the last GW Evolution rolls.... Sights: What the hell?! Bishop: Hey, this is a family show Brandi! If you're going to kill family values you could at least copy Janet Jackson when you do it. Sights: Shut up! Look there's someone coming down the ring... Hey, I think that's the new signee Train! As Wikkid stalks Savage, to begin the match, the man they call Train speeds out of the entryway and down the aisle! Train gets to the ring and immediately starts digging under the ring! Bishop: Whoa this Train guy comes up with a table from under the ring! Hey, this piece of crap match might just be worth watching after all! Train quickly sets up the table outside the ring. In the ring Savage stops to see what Train is doing. Wikkid, taking advantage of the opportunity, is slowly approaching him from the blind side. Suddenly Train slides in under the bottom rope! Train grabs Wikkid by the back of his mask and tights and tosses him over the far ropes! T! he ref is calling for the bell! Sight: What in the world is this guy doing? Bishop: I don't know, but it's better than that crap we were going to be forced to watch before! BLOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOD!!! Train whips around to Savage. He comes around from behind Savage and hooks him like he's going for an inverted DDT, but instead he lefts up into a vertical suplex! Train holds him there, letting the blood rush to his head. Then, almost out of nowhere, Train snaps Savage down and nails him with a modified Front Face DDT!!! 'The Hollowpoint' has been fired!!! Sights: Good night, what an impact! Bishop: Thank God! It's about time you left. Sights: What? Bishop: You said good night, I thought you were going to do everyone a favor and leave. Sights: Moron. Train leaps back to his feet! Savage is nearly dead weight, but Train starts to lift him up anyway. All of the sudden Wikkid is right on Train, raining down right hands!!! Train is rocked up against the ropes! Wicked lands some big time, clubbing blows right across Train's back, driving him down to one knee! Wikkid has the intruder at a disadvantage... right until Train's right arm swings up between Wikkid's legs and nails him square in the crown jewels!!! Bishop: Ohhhhh! Owwwwie! I hate to see that happen to any man! And I know Brandi does too! For a girl like her that could ruin her plans for just about every night of the week! *SLAP!!!* Bishop: OWWW!!! The damage is done, Kid Wikkid hunched over, barely able to take a breath! Train is back up to his feet and he looks pissed! Train double underhooks Wikkid and lays in 4 nasty kneelifts to the ribs! With one swift jerk Train lifts Wikkid up into a powerbomb type position! Wikkid can't do anything as Train turns around towards the near ropes and... ***CRASH!!!*** Bishop: HOLY CRAP!!!! Train powerbombs Kid Wikkid to the outside, right through that table!!! Sights: Good Lord, what a viscous maneuver!!! Kid Wikkid could be broken in half! Savage rolls on the mat holding his head while Kid Wikkid lies in the debris of the splintered and mangled remains of what used to be a table! Train hops outta the ring and looks down at Wikkid. Train: A little advice chump. When I ain't coming for you? Stay the fuck outta the way! You don't like that? What the hell you gonna do about it? Huh? WHAT!?! 'You Don't Want Dat' by Petey Pablo bumps through the arena as Train heads back up the ramp and disappears behind the curtain.*** Ariella- I knew I had seen you before! Damn, I guess you must've been in a bad mood that night. Train- I was in a taking care of business mood that night. Like I said, student of the game. Just like always. Travis- ahahahahaha... Looky here boys. Spends all his time rolling around with guys in tight pants and now everybody thinks he's a tough guy. Ariella- I know you're a dumbass, but do you really wanna pick a fight that you just saw kicking someone's ass on national tv? Travis- Actually it was just some half ass movie channel and I ain't impressed. Train- Well ain't nobody impressed with you runing your mouth. ***Travis just smirks. He leans over and shoots, missing his next shot.*** Train- Ain't impressed with that shit neither. Ariella- Oh, I am. That was smooth as silk. Travis- Don't you have some drinks to serve or something? Ariella- My shift ended five minutes ago. Travis- Well, you could still make yourself useful and fetch me another beer. Hell, once I win this money I think I'll buy a round for the house. ***Train gets up and finds himself a shot.*** Train- You ain't there yet son. ***Train proceeds to go on a small streak, two in a row, but can't seem to concentrate. He finally misses and Travis takes the table.*** Ariella(to Train)- Look... I never said thank you. You know, for sticking up for me. Train- Hey, I'd be happy to sti... Ariella- Pleeeeease, let's not ruin a nice moment. Train- hahaha... Aight, aight... I can see you ain't a sucker for sparkling conversation. But let me ask you. If you ain't interested, why you still sticking around? Ariella- Cuzzzzzz... Chivalry is rare. Besides that the bus doesn't run for another 20 minutes. Train- The bus? Hell girl, you don't need to be trying to catch a bus at this time of night. Ariella- Well, tell that to my piece of shit car. It broke down on me last week. Train- Oh, for real? Hmmmm.... Travis- Alright my man, here we go. Down to the eight ball. You ready to be broke? Train- You ready not to choke? ***Travis slams the stick into the cue ball and the eight ball slams into the side pocket in a chain reaction.*** Travis- Looks like. ***Travis snatches up all the cash as he and his boys celebrate. Train has an irritated look on his face.*** Travis- It's been good taking your money. (To Ariella) You finally ready to leave with a winner. Ariella- Piss off. Travis- Classy. Train- Naw son, the word you lookin' for is pussy. You gonna try to roll out without giving me a chance to get straight right? Travis- You had your chance. You lost. Why not try a little dignity? Train- Man, you just won 10 grand and them nuts is shrinking up already?! Damn man, I thought you have balls for at least another 20 minutes. Travis- Man... Some guys just don't know when to quit. So you think you're a big man, huh? What do you want? Double or nothing? Train- Didn't I just say I wasn't going for no pussy shit. Fuck double or nothing... Triple or triple chump. Ariella- Ummm... Look Train. ***Train just holds his hands up to quiet her as Travis and his boys laugh.*** Travis- Triple? hahahahaha... Jesus son, if you wanna commit suicide why don't you just find a bridge to jump off of? I got 10 grand in my hand. You lose triple you owe me 30 grand. How do I even know you're good for that kinda cash little man? ***Without even blinking Train takes of his platinum Rolex with the diamonds in the face and tosses it onto the pool table where the money had been.*** Train- Like I said, you can buy your momma a new double wide with that. Hell you'll have 30 and some change if you win it. So now what son? What? Travis- This guys fucking crazy. Train- Damn. I can just hear them muthafuckas shriveling up. Travis- Fuck you. Train- Oh, what's the matter? You was talking all that bullshit a second ago. Travis- You think I carry thirty grand on me everywhere? I'm pretty sure you can't just draw that outta the ATM. Train- What them keys go to? Travis- Keys? Train- The keys hanging outta yo pocket muthafucka. I see Ford on the keychain. What you got, an Explorer? Travis- Ford F-250... King Cab. You wouldn't even fit in a truck that big? Train- I might not, but I can damn sure sell it for 30 grand. Travis- The money and the truck is more than 30 grand. Train- Well then I guess this shit's just winner take all. ***Travis looks around, feeling a little boxed in with all eyes on him.*** Travis- You're a glutton for punishment my friend. *Pause* Rack 'em. ***One of Travis's boys quickly racks up the balls and both men get set for battle. Ariella tries to say something to Train as he walks around the table, but he just winks at her.*** Travis- Hell, I'll even let you break. I mean everyone on death row gets a last meal. ***Train quickly grabs the cue ball and lines up his break.*** Travis- Besides, I seen you play. You can use all the help you can get. hahaha... Train- You know what the say.... ***Train nails a huge break and sends two balls into the pockets.*** Train- Don't believe everything you see. Chump. Solids. ***With that retort Train proceeds to completely run the table. Travis never even gets a shot as Train clears the solid balls one after another. Finally he comes to the 8. Train doesn't even open his mouth to call the shot, simply pointing his cue to the intended pocket and knocks down the shot! Everyone in the bar explodes as the slowly rolls over and falls into the pocket. Needless to say Travis and his boys are pissed!*** Travis- You son of a bitch! You fucking hustled me! Train- Hook line and sinker. Pun intended. ***Train quickly snatches up the keys to Travis' F-250 and tosses them Ariella. He starts to go for the money, but Travis grabs his wrist!*** Travis- I don't think so boy! (to Ariella) You...give me my fucking keys (to Train) and you don't even think about touching my fucking money! Train- You don't wanna go here with me. Now take you God damn hands off me. ***There is a tense staredown, Train's trademark, cold, laser beam stare backs Travis down. The 6'4', 270 pounder seems lets go. Travis turns... but suddenly whips back around and swings down with the heavy end of his pool stick! But he's caught by surprise as Train catches it with both hands! Train doesnt' waste any motion delivering a swift kick to the jewels! Travis loses grip on the pool cue, hunching over, clutch at his groin. Train reaches out as if to hug Travis, but he quickly puts the pool cue horizontally across the back of Travis head... With a viscious blow Train brings his knee up under Travis' jaw! The force of the blow nearly lifts Travis off his feet and sends Travis' head through the pool cue Train was holding, wide armed, behind his head!!! Travis crumples to the ground! One of his boys tries to bum rush Train, but Train ducks and send the heavy end of the broken cue right across his knee! The man cluthes at his knee, screaming in pain! The third man tries the same routine, but doesn't get 5 feet before he nearly gets his head knocked off with a superkick! In a matter of seconds the fight is over and three large men are on the ground either in pain or preparing to be once they wake up. The balding man behind the bar rushes over, as he had been in the process of doing when the fight started and ended just as quickly. Ariella seems stunned, but the look on her face hides her giddiness from no one. Manager- Jesus.... Train- Sorry about the damage dogg. Here... ***Train picks up his cash and breaks off about 5 c-notes. The manager takes with a small grin on his face.*** Manager- Thanks. If it wasn't for the fact that Travis was such a prick I'd be talking about kicking you outta here. But he is a prick, so good job. Look, you better head on out before the cops get here. Train- Naw dogg. I promised myself I'd be an upstanding citzen and shit in Dallas. It was self defense. Manager- That it was. Man, I saw you on Overload last week. If you can kick three cowboys in the ass I know you're gonna be a hell of a player in GW. Train- Big hats don't make them muthafuckas cowboys. Manager- No...but the jerseys do. Train- What? Ariella- Jerseys. You just beat down half the Dallas Cowboys' defensive line. ***Train looks down at Travis and his friends in shock for a moment.*** Train- Damn. Hell, most of them muthafuckas went down with one shot. No wonder they can't keep nobody outta the end zone. I don't know how much they paying these bitches, but it's too damn much. Ariella- ahahahaha... ~~~~~~~1 1/2 Hours Later~~~~~~~ ***The lights on the cop cars are still flashing as Train exits the doors of Sharpshooters, the manager still talking to the cops, backing up Train's version of events. Train hops in the Navigator. As he turns the ignition 'Act a Fool' by Ludacris starts to bump. Train pulls out of his space and pulls up to the street ready to make his turn when a big, red Ford F-250 pulls up next to him. Train rolls down the window as Ariella rolls down hers.*** Train- What you still doing here? Ariella- I didn't wanna get lost on the way to the house. Train- You been drinking? You can't find your way back to yo' house? Ariella- I'm not talking about my house. ***Train pauses for a moment as a grin washes over his face.*** Train- Let's roll. ***The Navigator and the F-250 pull out and roll down the street as the scene fades to black.***
