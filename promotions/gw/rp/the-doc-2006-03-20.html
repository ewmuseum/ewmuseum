The Doc

20 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 20 Dr. Mason’s office is decorated with gold knick-knacks that were strewn about on the few, but fine, pieces of oak furniture. The four white walls were lined with framed awards, and certifications. Dr. Mason was kindly older man, not much older than Dylan, but Dylan guessed him to be about 55 or so. Dr. Mason had full, but completely white head of hair, and deep lines were traced along Benjamin Mason’s sunken cheeks. He was the kind of doctor you actually trusted to tell your most intimate thoughts and feelings, which was a necessity (seeing as how Dylan was indeed paying nearly hundred bucks an hour to tell this guy his thoughts and feelings, might as well feel comfortable doing it). Dr. Mason’s thin nimble fingers were interlaced with one another and clipboard and pen sat across his folded legs. The doc leaned back in his fine black leather wing chair, carefully listening to all that Dylan had to say. The Killer had told Dr. Mason about his dream, the one in which he saw his wife Kaylee having sex with Jake Hix. He told Dr. Mason about his and Jake’s history, which went all the way back to RAWF, then SAWF, and that now he and Jake were again in the same federation together. He also told Dr. Mason about how for the past six months Dylan had been going the most rigorous training in his whole life, just to prepare for his “un-retirement” match. Dylan easily recalled his memories of SAWF, it was the greatest period in his wrestling career, if not his life. It was comforting, almost soothing to remember such great times (so soothing in fact that if Dylan, who was laid out on very nice, and equally expensive black leather couch, hadn’t propped himself onto his elbow the Killer would have fallen asleep). All the while that Dylan relayed this soothing memories and thoughts he had never locked eyes with him, instead he stared up a copy of Van Gogh’s Marigolds, which was positioned just above where the doctor sat in his chair. Dylan wasn’t sure if he could look into the eyes of an older man who was there to deduce his thoughts and dreams. Like a hired father, that’s all he really was, an expensive parent for a very big kid. Anyways, Dylan was sure Dr. Mason had noticed this, every now and again Dylan would catch the doc gazing down at him, analyzing him, his body language, and the words he chose to use. “So, Dylan, you say that this Jake Hix, the same man in your dream, was the man who retired you from professional wrestling?” Dylan glanced from replica painting and stared at his hired dad, caught off guard, all Dylan could do was nod his head. “Well, Dylan, I think you may have a bit of an inferiority complex.” The Killer’s eyes narrowed as he, for the first time, looked into Dr. Mason’s unwavering brown eyes. “I believe that your dream represented something other than your fears for what your is doing when you’re not home. I believe that you were dreaming about your fear for Jake. I believe that you were just envisioning yourself losing yet another thing you love to this Jake Hix fellow.” The Killer sat up, not quite angry, but very bewildered. “Excuse me doc, but are you saying that I think I’m less of a man than Jake Hix” Because I don’t, I’ve beat him before, and I can beat him again. I guarantee I can.” Dylan didn’t mean to sound arrogant, he was just saying what he felt, it was just his competitive attitude coming out, that’s all. “No, no, Dylan, I simply feel that you’re afraid that Jake is going to take away what you love, you’re thinking of him as some sort of a bullying. He retired you before you could ever get the World Title, which I assume was something important to you?” Dylan shrugged his shoulders, but he nodded his head in agreement. “Well, then you saw him in your mind taking away another one of your loves, this just happened to be your wife. Heck I wouldn’t be surprised if you dreamed of Jake playing Frisbee with your son David in the park.” Dr. Mason sneered his thin lips, but that brought another memory, a memory Dylan had forgotten. “David?” Dylan whispered to himself as he flashed back to SAWF. I had been wrestling Jake Hix when David made his wrestling debut as? the Texas Assassin. David wore that day that he? that he attacked me with that kendo stick. That’s right, he helped Jake beat me once, then David went and stayed out at the Hix Family Ranch so Jake could finish training him. Dylan shook his head and shuddered a bit, he realized now that the doc was right about the whole thing, but he didn’t tell Dr. Mason about this recalled memory, he just rolled himself off his side and sat up on the couch. Dr. Mason fixed his blazer and smoothed out his slacks as he continued, “Dylan, I think your mind is just pushing you to achieve your dream of being World Champion, you didn’t accomplish it in SAWF, so your mind feels that if you can dethrone Jake Hix in this new federation you’ll make up for that.” Dylan fully agreed with Dr. Mason now, but the Killer of course new as well as everyone that he would have to pay his dues, when you went to a new federation you had to climb the ranks just like everyone, very rarely did your past achievements matter in a new federation. DYLAN the killer KNIGHT
