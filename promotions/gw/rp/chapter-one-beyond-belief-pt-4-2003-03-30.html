Chapter One: Beyond Belief? (PT. 4)

Chapter One: Beyond Belief? (PT. 4) Leviathan Global Wrestling Promo #46 Date: March 30th, 2003 Location: Blagoveshensk, Russia ﻿The Book Of Judgment Chapter One: Beyond Belief? (Part 4) Date: February 28th, 1980 Location: Blagoveshensk, Russia “No Nikita, you stay here.....it would be with in your best interest. You have been through enough as it is.” Vladimir says to her. “No....I am going, I am not staying here alone.” Nikita fires back. “It’s ok, we’ll have a group of you stay back, there is no reason for everyone to leave. Alright, everyone came from the fourth floor and all women, stay here. Oh yeah, before we go, be sure to close this door securely and don’t open it for anyone, no matter what!” Vladimir says. With that said, a large group of men leave the room, Vladimir the last one out the door. Six men and four women are left in the staff room, Nikita included. One of the men closes the door to the room and barricades it with chairs and the large wooden table from the center of the room, via help of the rest of the staff. The four women sit on the sofa, while the men search through the utensils drawers to find anything that could be possibly used as a weapon. --The Hunters Become The Hunted...-- Vladimir and his clan of hunters slowly pace through the darkened floor, looking for the beast known as Leviathan. Vladimir raises his hand to signal the group to stop, and walks alone towards the elevator. He presses the down button, then the up button. “The elevator is dead...he has shut down the emergency system as well.” Vladimir states to the clan. He walks over to the right of the elevator, approaching a steel door, and pushes on the release bar, however the door does not move. He steps fully behind the door and wildly kicks it, but the door is securely shut. “We are stuck on this floor which means he is as well. This floor is not that big, we will split up.” Vladimir instructs the clan. Vladimir approaches his clan, and begins to count how many members there are to equally make separate hunting groups. He counts out a total of sixteen, then decides to split up the group. “Alright, we have sixteen people, so we will divide into four groups of four people. That should be more than enough in a group to overthrow this big goof.” Vladimir says. Vladimir separates everyone into the four groups, and has them stand together. They all huddle up to discuss their plan of action to take down Leviathan. “Alright, here is how it goes. My group will go to Victors office. Boris, your group will be heading to the bathroom facilities. Sergei, you will be going to the recording studio and Igor, your group will at random re-locate through the halls to give back up to any group which needs it. Lets go!” Vladimir shouts. The four groups separate and head to their locations. Boris’ group enters the male bathroom facilities to check out their designated area. There is six stalls, and the room is very dark. Boris opens the first stall, and it is empty. The other group members each select a stall to check and try to. One is successful, yet the other two say there are locked. Boris and the group huddle around the first locked stall and prepare for the knockdown. Boris kicks the door down with brute strength, and no one is inside. The move towards the next one, and prepare for the same tactic Just before the door is kicked down, the lock is unlocked, and the door creeps open. A figure is seen in the darkness, and steps forward. It is a small timid man. He has blood smeared on his uniform shirt and is shaking uncontrollably. “D...do you know of that beast in this building....” He asks Boris. “Yes...we do and there is no need to worry...he won’t be alive much longer.” Boris states with clear confidence. “Well....he has killed your hall patrol....and viciously murdered the four in the studio....and now, he is coming for you....” the man says as his shaking stops, and laughter begins. The man oddly transforms into a smaller scaley creature and is hunched over. The door to bathroom slowly creeks open and the group huddles together ready to take on Leviathan. The large figure steps into the room, and turns towards the group. He stands still, staring at them as the scaley creature approaches him. Leviathan raises his hands and pulls his hood down revealing his gruesome appearance. With doing so, one of the four men charge towards him but is halted as his face his punched in, literally, and falls to the ground as a puddle of blood forms. Leviathan steps over the corpse and approaches the three men, Boris stand in the center. He sticks his arms out and grabs the two outer most men by their heads, and squeezes as moaning is heard by the two men, as blood formation begins on their faces. Leviathan keeps walking, holding onto the two men, causing Boris to walk backwards in fear of this beast. Leviathan drops the two men and they fall hard and sickly to the ground, but it doesn’t matter as they are already dead. Boris is fully backed up against the wall as Leviathan is chest to chest with him. Leviathan places his hands on Boris shoulders and swings him around in a full 360 and tosses him through the wall as dry wall and bricks from the buildings outer layer fly into the sky with his body. Boris screams as he falls sickly from the seventh floor of the building, but his screams are soon ended as his body crashes into a car parked on the street. Leviathan stares down at the prone body in a complete trance, until the scaley creature breaks that trance. “Leviathan...your not done yet...finish them all off.” It says. Leviathan snaps around staring at the creature, with his fiery red eyes. He raises his hands and wipes them together, smearing the blood together. “Let’s go.” His deep voice booms.
