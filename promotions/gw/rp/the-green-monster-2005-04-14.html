The green monster.

14 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 14th, 2005. The green monster. 'Wake up, wake up!', came my girlfriends excited voice as she pounced on top of me in the bed. 'Mggfhh!', came my sleepy reply as I jerked the covers over my head and retreated, much akin to a turtle into his shell. 'Get up, you loser!', Raki laughed as she punched me through the covers. The punch caught me in the kidney and I suddenly regretted taking her under my wing as an apprentice to the Wrestling industry because she really did pack quite the punch for a girl at the five foot nine, one hundred and twenty-eight pounds mark. 'Go away...', I moaned in protest, as I squeezed my eyelids closed even further, not quite ready to concede defeat in this fight that I was most definitely going to lose. 'Don't make me come in there after you, loser!', Raki said, no doubt smiling, although I couldn't see her smile. Her body was resting on my back and I put my arms over my head trying to cling to the precious remnants of sleep that I had been torn away from. 'Rise and shine!', she declared loudly as she playfully drummed on my kidneys with her fists. 'What bloody time is it anyway?', I moaned, not wanting to get out of my warm and comfortable bed. 'Time to wake up before I drag your butt out of bed!', she said with a laugh, shoving her hands against me in an attempt to wake me up. 'Yeah, try it.', I mumbled in a sleep induced state of consciousness. I realized as I said it that my lovely girlfriend had the temerity to try it and, try she would. 'That's it, slacker, you asked for it!', jerking the covers back she tore the pillow from my grasp and locked a hand onto my left ear. 'Up yet?' 'Owww, owww, owww...', were the most productive words I could manage as I raised my head to relieve pressure on the ear that she seemed to twist even harder by the second. Raki stood on the bed and forced me to my knees, reaching up I grabbed her by her bare legs and pulled them out from under her sending her crashing onto the bed. 'Ahhhh!', she playfully screamed as I grabbed her wrists and leapt on top of her smiling. 'Ok, little lady, what's the big idea of waking me up at...', I paused, glancing over at the white digital clock resting on our nightstand, '...9:06?'. Raki smiled up at me and suddenly I was happy to be awake. I glanced down at her body, clad in a pair of pink daisy dukes and a white tank top she appeared to have just awakened herself, her uncombed hair verified this fact, and leaning down, I smiled as I blew my morning breath into her face. 'Hey jerk, that isn't cool!', she yelled with a laugh as I leaned down and kissed her softly. As I kissed her my shaggy black and blue streaked hair fell across my face, ignoring it I kissed her again before letting her go and leaning back with a satisfied smile. 'Did that taste like morning breath?', I grinned my face still stricken with the dazed picture of sleep. 'I am so going to beat you once you get off of me...', she said, shooting a playful death stare at me. 'Oooh, I'm horrified, please don't hurt me.', I retorted with a sarcastic grin. 'But hurting you is half the fun!', she protested with a laugh. 'Yeah, well you never did answer my question, what am I doing up 21 minutes before the alarm goes off?', I inquired as I released her wrists and rolled off of her. '21 minutes?', she asked, her lovely face seemingly puzzled at the odd number. 'Yes, the alarm was set for 9:27.', I answered seriously. '9:27?', she echoed as she crawled out of bed. 'Of course, 9:25 is too early and I feel guilty if I don't wake up by at least 9:30.', my answer was completely serious as I placed my socked feet onto the floor and stood up yawning widely and wiping the crusty remainder of sleep from my eyes with balled up fists. 'Loser.', she said with a laugh as she kissed me on the cheek. 'Sheesh, first you start with the physical abuse, now the verbal? That's it little lady, I'm calling the fuzz and filing domestic dispute charges against you!', I snapped playfully. 'Oh sure, that'll make headlines everywhere. Internationally renowned star of professional Wrestling gets beaten to a bloody, whining, little pulp by his 128 pound girlfriend.', she laughed, punching me playfully in the ribs. 'Ouch, see what I'm talking about? What do you mean by bloody, whining, little pulp? I'm not a bloody, whining little pulp!', I protested, poking her in the stomach with my right index finger. 'Well I'm not finished beating on you yet, am I?', she asked with a broad grin. 'You are evil...', I exclaimed, opening my eyes wide with an animated expression crossing my face. She laughed, such a cute sound emitting from her beautiful mouth, I couldn't resist another kiss, on the forehead this time as I stepped past her and opened the closet door in our apartment. 'You know, you still haven't told me exactly why you got me out of bed so early.', I said, reaching into the closet and extracting a pair of faded Versace jeans. Stepping into them, left leg first, I pulled them up and buttoned the fly of my low rise jeans before seeking after a shirt. 'No, no, I haven't.', she said with a smile as she turned to walk away. 'Where are you going?', I asked, sorting through my shirts before deciding on a gray t-shirt with the shadowy white outline of a human body on the front and the word: 'ghost' written across the chest. 'To breakfast and if you hurry up then maybe you can see why I woke you up so early.', Raki answered with a chuckle, showing just how much she was enjoying this little game of hers. 'You suck!', I shouted playfully as I dropped onto the bed to tug on my black Hi-top Chuck Taylor's. 'But that makes you a very happy man, doesn't it?', she teased as she stepped through the open door and into the living room of the apartment before disappearing into the kitchen. '...I have no response to that.', I said, blinking in surprise as I laced my Chucks. Standing to my feet, I padded across the gray carpet beneath my feet and pushed through the open door and into the living room that opened into the kitchen. Strolling comfortably to the bar that served as the central eating station of our cozy apartment, I glanced at my girlfriend where she stood with her back to me. Throwing my arms around her waist I squeezed her, kissing her on the back of the neck as I savored the sweet fragrance of her hair that met my nostrils. I love the smell of vanilla and I didn't mind her thick black hair falling across my face. 'Be good or I'll have to beat you up again.', she chided, focusing on the task in front of her: breakfast. My eyes widened as I removed them from her lovely visage and to the stunning feast set before me on the kitchen's wide bar. A plate had already been prepared for me, 4 pancakes stacked high, slathered in blackberry syrup and covered with bananas and nuts with a liberal dosage of powdered sugar on top. Next to the beautiful mountain of pancakes, sat a cup of coffee, the smell of hazelnut drifting through the air to introduce itself to my nasal passages as it brought an even broader smile to my face. 'Wow, you've really gone all out.' I spoke with sincerity, genuinely impressed by her effort. 'Of course.', she responded with a smile, as she motioned for me to grab a barstool and sit down. 'Did you do something wrong?', I asked with a playful smile. 'Because usually when we have a great breakfast around here it's because I screwed up so I'm cooking for you in an attempt to grovel and beg for my forgiveness.' She laughed as she responded. 'No, I didn't do anything. Gee, you're paranoid. Guilty conscience there, champ?' I smiled as I picked up the fork on the right side of my plate. 'Then it's safe to eat this?' She answered my question by picking up a fork of her own and using it to cut into the pancakes. 'I knew I shouldn't have purchased that stupid life insurance policy.', I teased with a mumble. 'Ok, I guess I haven't told you what I've been teasing you about all morning, so perhaps I should tell you now, shouldn't I?', she asked sitting down her fork. 'Yes, you most certainly should, especially after all the hassle I've went through just to hear your, so-called big news!', I said, with a playful whimper. 'Ok, well, I guess, that just maybe I could tell you...', she answered, reaching to the end of the bar where she procured the morning mail. She deposited a brown envelope, shipped certified mail, in front of me and I couldn't help but smile as I saw the initials: 'PTC', on the return address. 'Don't tell me...could it possibly be..?', I stopped midsentence to drop my fork and snatch the envelope up. I tore into it, ripping it open and extracting a perfectly printed letter. I scanned the letter with a broad smile on my face and a pounding heart. 'Well?' Raki asked as she impatiently pursed her lips. 'You do realize what this is, don't you?', I asked as I rubbed my thumb gently over the letter, afraid to crinkle the paper and treating it as gently as I would have handled the declaration of independence. 'Josh, I'm not blonde.', Raki stated in a rather blunt manner. My answer came with a complimentary smile. 'It looks like PTC has accepted my application to participate in their second annual Cruiserweight tournament.' 'Have they listed your opponent?', she asked, firing off the million dollar question. I turned my eyes back to the paper in my hand to find the answer. 'Jeremy Castle.', I said, stroking the morning shadow on my chin in deep thought. 'Who?', she asked, minus the sarcasm. 'Exactly.', I replied, plus sarcasm. 'I've never heard of him, is he good?', Raki inquired. 'He's formerly of WMW, but according to the free agent status listed here it would appear that they've parted ways.', I said thoughtfully, my mind searching for knowledge of Jeremy Castle. 'So, he's good then?', Raki took a sip of her orange juice after the question. 'Good enough to get into the tournament.', I answered with a shrug. 'So, surprised by the good news?', she asked, reaching across the table to squeeze my hand in excitement. 'A little. I was hopeful, I'm dead set on this tournament, I only wish I had known sooner so I could have been a little more prepared.', I raised my palms to signify that it wasn't of great importance. Raki smiled showing that she had other things on her mind. 'Okay, that was part of the surprise.', she said, reaching back into the pile of mail and pulling out another letter from PTC. 'What is that?', I asked in confusion, arching my eyebrows at the sight of a second letter identical to the first. 'Josh, allow me to explain something.', Raki slowly said as she tore open the envelope a serious expression on her face. 'You've been training me since August, and I'm a little disappointed at where I'm at in this stage of my training.' 'Ok, tell me what you have in mind, I'm open to suggestions. In fact I've been thinking of spending more time working on your training.', I commented, taking a sip of my hot coffee at the end of my sentence. 'I'm not talking about training, Josh, I'm talking about competition. I've already been training with you for 9 months and before I started training with you I had been training 3 months prior. You said it yourself Josh, I'm as good of a beginner as you've seen, I think it's time for me to step into the ring.', she stated, flipping the envelope between her fingers. 'No.', I answered, shaking my head. 'You aren't ready to step into the ring, as of yet.' 'Josh! It's been a year!' she persisted. 'If I had stayed back in Dallas and continued my training there, I would already be working independent shows.' 'I know, and you would be a great independent Wrestler.', I said, wrapping both hands around my hot mug of coffee and staring down into the black liquid, my own reflection looking back up at me. 'However; I didn't train you to be an independent Wrestler, I trained you to be a professional Wrestler.' 'But you haven't raised a finger to get me into the big leagues.', she snapped defensively. 'You've bounced from Global to Core and now back to Global without ever even attempting to get me in a match. I know you've got pull Josh, you're a star!', her face was beginning to turn a little red showing signs of anger. 'Yes, and I haven't done anything because you aren't ready yet.', I calmly answered as I took another sip of my coffee, it was just how I like it: 4 sugars and a splash of cream. 'Fine.', she answered with a glare. 'I knew you were going to say that so I decided to do something big, and something that even you can't stop me from doing. I submitted my application to compete in CW2.', she sneered, smiling arrogantly at me. I shrugged. 'Whatever you choose to do, did you get in?', I asked, motioning to the letter in her hand. 'We'll see, won't we?', she spat out, the chip on her shoulder becoming easier to knock off by the moment. She smiled as she pulled out the letter and read it off. 'Dear Raki, we thank you for the interest you expressed in participating in the second annual Cruiserweight tournament hosted by Primetime Central. We have received your application, however, we do apologize but we have already elected 32 superstars to compete in the aforementioned tournament. Feel free to apply again next year...', her voice trailed off as the paragraph came to an end. No words came to mind, and I felt it best to stay silent than to open my mouth and insert my foot, unfortunately she didn't share those sentiments, deciding to speak freely and loudly. 'I cannot believe this!', she shouted, slamming a fist down onto the bar. 'I don't want to say I told you so, but...', as soon as the words had left my mouth I knew they should have stayed there. I couldn't have picked a worse thing to say at any less convenient time. 'You shut up, this is your fault.', she roared, with a glare. 'My fault? How do you see that?', I asked my demeanor becoming a little defensive. 'You're the one who keeps saying I'm not ready! If you had talked to McCoy about getting me an opportunity in Global I would be in this tournament right now!', she answered, her anger rising. 'I'm sorry, Raki, I just don't feel that you're ready as of yet, but hey, you saw the letter, it said to feel free to apply again next year, maybe by that time you'll have the opportunity to step into the ring.', I spoke in a gentle voice trying to calm her down. As she knocked over her orange juice spilling them across my pancakes I discovered that my gentle voice wasn't helping. 'Hey, I was going to eat that!', I protested, looking down at the soggy pancakes with a sad expression. 'Here, you can have mine!', she angrily screamed, shoving her plate hard and sending it across the bar into my lap. 'Raki, please watch what you're doing, these jeans are Versace!', I calmly scolded her, not raising my voice in the least as I remained calm. 'Ugh, sometimes you disgust me, how can you be so...ugh!', she screamed in frustration as she jerked back from her seat sending the bar stool toppling to the floor. Spinning on her bare heel she walked away from me and into our bedroom where she violently slammed the door. 'Honey, are you okay?', I asked, raising my voice so that she could hear me through the closed door. She didn't answer and somehow I knew she wouldn't. Glancing down at my jeans now covered in syrup and sliced fruit I shook my head as I took another sip of my coffee. 'Geez...PMS.'
