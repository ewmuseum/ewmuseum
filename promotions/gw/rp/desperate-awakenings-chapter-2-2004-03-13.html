Desperate Awakenings: Chapter 2

13 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-13-04 Prologue: Desperate to make a difference, so desperate that it hurts. Jesse wants to live on the edge, but at the same time wants to love. His newfound love Nikki and him are happy. For the first time in near 6 years Jesse is happy. Since the day he lost his wife life has been the bitter end of many things. The questions still line up, is he really okay? Does he love her or is he feeling the emptiness? Can love and living on the edge work hand and hand? Will his love become too much to handle and cause him to take a plunge in the ring? Find out. Story Continues: Through the curtains of his dark room Jesse is their sitting and playing his guitar. Moving his head back and forth through each motion, his reaction to the words is so free and yet artistic. Slowly he gets up and starts to sing as he plays the guitar to the tune he has on his stereo. Come one and all and see the broken man, talking to himself He sits and waits for something better, he'll never find it here The people touch his hair and pinch his cheek; he can't even feel it There it goes again, he's listening to someone He hears the bitter laughter And all he wants to know is… Why------ does any of it matter? (I can't take it anymore) You've gotta try------ the inhale that makes the exhale so much better He wipes his hands on anything in reach, he never feels clean He shakes at night because his nerve is gone, every muscle hurts Come one and all and see what happened…that broken man is me There it goes again, I can hear it louder It doesn't feel good anymore All I want to know is… Why------ does any of it matter? (I can't take it anymore) You've gotta try------ the inhale that makes the exhale so much better NOW I KNOW I DISAPPEAR! I CAN'T FIND MY WAY FROM OUT OF HERE! EVERYTHING IS FADING ON ME! SOMEONE TELL ME… someone tell me… Someone - tell me Why------ does any of it matter? (I can't take it anymore) You've gotta try------ the inhale that makes the exhale so much better WHY?! YOU'VE GOTTA TRY!! Despair is in his eyes as he finishes the song. His fingers let go up the guitar and it crashes to the floor as tears overcome him. His eyes begin to flood with tears as if he cannot control them. He drops to his knees and puts his hands to his face crying in them with no reason whatsoever. Then, within a flash Jesse falls over and begins to shake. He is having a seizure or a heart attack, one or the other for his body is having convulsions. The door opens to his room and it is Nikki. She rushes to the phone next to Jesse’s alarm clock and hits 911. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** There lying in a hospital bed with tubes and wires hanging from bags and machines is Jesse Jamester. His eyes a gazed look as if he’d been in a fight after having too much alcohol. He looks over to his left and sees Nikki holding his hand asleep. Her gentle face a smooth apple compared to his rough hands, yet she loves him and he loves her. Jesse touches her face with his other hand and she instantly awakes in a gut reaction sort of way. Her face seems scared at first but then she sees Jesse’s smile and knows he’s okay and so the fear becomes relief. The two hold hands for a few minutes before one speaks. ’What happened to me?’ ‘You had a mental breakdown honey. You were just so stressed and so worried that everything collapsed. Your blood pressure was off the charts, your heartbeat was racing, and you had a fever. The doctor said you’d be okay but your suppose to rest.’ ‘Rest? I have a match tomorrow night with John Zero for the International Title! You know how hard I’ve worked to get this re-shot?’ ‘I understand Jesse, but if you wrestle you could easily have this happen again. Do you really want to risk that?’ ‘I would risk my life for wrestling Nikki… I LOVE IT! I want to die in a wrestling ring! It’s my dream, and it’s my choice! I told you when we got together, things like this would happen, and now you have to be strong for us both.’ ‘Jesse, I love you, and I promised I’d stick with you. And that’s exactly what I’m doing. I will not leave your side unless there is no other choice.’ ‘Good… Then find me some clothes please, I’ve got to get out of here.’ ‘Okay. Be right back.’ Jesse rolls over and tries to stand up off the bed, however his body is too weak and like a fly being hit with a flyswatter, he drops. Jesse grips the side of the bed and climbs back up. Looking down at his body he is paranoid of the wires, and so he begins ripping them out. The doctors start rushing in for the wire for the heartbeat stops and they think he’s dead. Jesse throws the bed across the room finally after gaining his leg strength again and being able to stand. The doctors try to calm him but he pushes through them and slams through the door, going into a full sprint down the hall way. He doesn’t stop however, and goes right through the glass door and rolls down the steps. Lying again on the ground as before he is unconscious, and in the midst of a crisis he cannot overcome. ’Fading to black… I’ll fight. When the lights are out I’ll become the blind… yet continue to fight. This is my heart, this is my desire, and this is my world… In which I do not lose, nor do I fail… I only become better and overcome the odds. Give me light and I will give you hope, give me darkness, and I’ll guide you through the fear that lurks within you. Remember me… Remember the heart of this warrior, for he may be old, but he’s still willing to die for his army.’ (To Be Continued…)
