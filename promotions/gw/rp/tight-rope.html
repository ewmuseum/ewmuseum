Date: various .:: ::: ::. 4 July 2005 The J’dr, Somewhere over the Atlantic 5.30, roughly Another chapter in the tome of my life has been written. Another moment where I may simply close this volume, and set it away on a shelf of its own. Quite literally, I might add; I am an avid journalist, and so by now, the chronicle of my own life is getting to be quite large. But that is aside from the point. The chapter that I speak of is, of course, WMW, and my tenure there. Now that I have walked away from Crossfire II, on the other side of the spectrum, I feel as though my last concerns about Wrestling Midwest, and all the grief that came with it? are over. There are those of my kind who would like to have everyone who comes in contact with them believe that immortality comes with an infallibility clause. Sadly, though both words do indeed start with the letter i, that is essentially where the relationship ends. There is no law, written or unwritten, that states that vampires can never make mistakes, never be confused, never have bad judgment. That, unfortunately, is incorrect. I could only with that I got some sort of written document the moment I was changed that stated such things. Suffice it to say that I was wrong about WMW. It was somewhat of a freak accident that I was led there in the first place, something that is shrouded in the mystery of my mind. As I’ve come to discover, vampires are not wholly immune from freak spasms of human psychology. We were once human, after all, and I can only assume that the basic philosophy in our brains remains the same. I digress. What the human doctors call a psychogenic fugue is what brought me to Wrestling Midwest in search of my ex-husband. A fugue usually results from some trauma, be it environmental or emotional; in my case, I’ve not yet discovered what that trauma was, exactly. The rarest of cases sees sufferers creating an entirely new life for themselves. I’ve always been a rare case. The way I remembered things one year ago, I was an assassin for Clan Assamite. I remembered being from Paris, being sired by a rogue Assassin who sired outside of clan rules, choosing not only a non-Arab, but a woman on top of that’most unusual for the time, I assure you. My mind had me sired a mere 200 years ago, and my mind had me in search of the one man from my past who might possibly provide me a link to my future’my ex-husband. It was not until GTT4 that things began to come clear. In the middle of a particularly demanding assassination assignment, I began to have flashbacks of a very different life. The memories felt almost as though they weren’t mine, but that’s when someone from the outside came in, playing devil’s advocate. That someone was known to me at the time by the name Gideon de Medici, though I soon came to remember him under his real name: Virgil Ambrosi. When he finally got me away from Cleveland for a little while, and into his estate at Napa Valley, I finally started to remember the truth; no sooner had I started to remember than a crack group of Cleveland’s Den, of which my little friend Alexis was a part, burned the mansion to the ground. The rest of the story has been told in detail since I lost my GTT4 Bracket Final bout with Kasper Sky, and it has been told in detail in my own personal journals, so I see no need to recount it here, save to mention fleetingly that it was the trip to Prague that I took with Virgil that showed me that I had no obligation to stay in WMW, title or not, if I didn’t want to be there. I won’t take anything away from Umbrage winning the Ace Superior title from me by coming back now and saying something stupid like I let him win it. Make no mistake that Umbrage won that title on his own, and he won it because I simply walked out to the ring, stood in the middle of the Wrestling Midwest crowd? and felt absolutely nothing. I felt no desire to shine. No desire to compete, if just to shut my opponent’s mouth. Every meaningful moment that I had in Wrestling Midwest suddenly meant nothing. So I competed with half a heart, and I lost with half a heart, and I went backstage to Brad Johnson, broke my contract, and left his company. That all sounds so dire, but in reality, I had one thing on my mind, and one thing only as I walked out of the doors of Wrestling Midwest, a free woman once again: Revenge. Revenge against the man who’d been the sole reason I ended up in that cesspool. I’d say that little plot was carried off quite well, all things considered. I got what I wanted, and a whole lot more. Right now, I’m surrounded by teammates who at the very least can appreciate that I am talented, unlike the teammates I ended up with in Wrestling Midwest. It isn’t just that, though? it runs much deeper. I have someone, in Soma, who recognizes my potential, and wishes to teach me more about the lessons that I am still in existence to learn. Someone in Virgil who quenches my need for connection to my past, reminds me that love can come in many forms. Someone in Dawn who? frustrates me to no end, and yet somehow, I still can’t get her out of my mind? Sigh. I don’t suppose everything has to make sense. In any case? I finally have what I wanted. I’ve shut the door on my Wrestling Midwest past with a victory’it’s not just a victory that puts a tally in the “W” column for my new place of employment. To me, it is a victory that symbolizes the end’my triumph over the past that very nearly ate me alive. Now if only I can make sure that such things don’t reoccur’the first step is finding what caused it in the first place, but that is something I can undertake at leisure. At the moment, I intend to focus on the present and the future, which are far more promising prospects at the moment. WMW is a door that is closed, the locks broken. Enough of this, though; in the end, it’s just a pointless summary. No words can accurately express the things I am trying to express. In any case, I do believe it bothers Rune significantly that I no longer have to retreat below deck when the sun rises over the sea. I would have done it sooner, but I ran on a little longer in this entry than I hoped to, and alas, the light in the cargo hold is rather too abysmal to write by. But, I suppose that I owe him some courtesy, since I am a guest in his plane’to the hold I go, to catch some sleep before touching back down in America. I’ve a bit of business to take care of in Vegas before going back to Toronto. Shadow .:: ::: ::. Undisclosed Date McFarlane Estate 11.45 Her eyes blearing with pain from the sunlight that she still wasn’t very used to, Shadow opened the side door of the palatial McFarlane Estate, planning to enter through the kitchen. She supposed that was the least obtrusive way to get in right now, considering the front door would have made a good deal more noise. Or, if the truth were told, the kitchen was closer to Shadow’s room, and Shadow was trying to avoid her disgruntled teammate as much as possible. The door was still open when Shadow’s eyes first caught on the pair of legs sticking out of the refrigerator. Her mind briefly tried to convince her that perhaps it wasn’t Dawn, but as her eyes trailed upwards, catching on the long shapely pair of legs extending from underneath the cottony white towel that wrapped her torso, there was little doubt that it was indeed the very woman she’d been hoping to avoid. Still, there was some hope that Shadow could bypass her bad luck if she could do it silently. But at the moment, she couldn’t force herself to move’creamy white shoulders beckoned to her, their toned muscle set off by the jagged black tribal markings that formed the shape of wings; of course, Shadow couldn’t see the whole design, just the part that peeked above the towel’s seam. Briefly, she found herself imagining what the rest of it would look like, before she forced her eyes upwards to somewhere more innocuous, up the sensuous curve of shoulder and neck and to the white towel wrapped around Dawn’s hair. Tiny strands of black and strawberry blonde, mottled together, stole down her neck, a typical careless and carefree gesture of not catching up all of her hair after her shower. Circled around her neck was a black lace choker, covering up what most vampires would have viewed as the best part’to Shadow, it was an inviting tease that made her muscles tense slightly. Shadow shook her head, suddenly coming out of it, and remembering her original plan of getting past Dawn before Dawn realized she was there. Of course, it was at that exact moment that her Global Television Title chose to slip from the strap of her back. It crashed down to the floor with a resounding Thud!. Shadow immediately bent to pick it up, which caused her to wince Dawn flinching at the noise. By the time Shadow had clumsily scraped her belt off the floor, Dawn had composed herself, and Shadow found herself staring into a pair of bright green eyes, halfway narrowed, across the island in the middle of the kitchen. Dawn’s fingers idly picked through the fruit tray that she’d just pulled out to munch on in lieu of lunch. She jerked her head towards the door. “You going to close that” I don’t want the flies getting in.” Shadow blinked, then realized what Dawn meant’wow, she was slipping, wasn’t she? She stepped quickly over to the door, shutting it as gently as possible, and clearing her throat. “Er” sorry,” she said, looking up into Dawn’s eyes. Dawn shrugged slightly as she picked up a chunk of fresh-looking pineapple and nibbled on it. Shadow looked away from Dawn’s gaze, which was extremely magnetic at the moment, suddenly finding the pattern on the kitchen tile extremely absorbing. “I’m surprised you came back,” Dawn said, her voice somewhat dry, “I figured you’d stay in Vegas” or perhaps with Soma or Virgil.” Shadow shook her head. “No” Soma doesn’t have any more training for me to do, and Virgil is busy with his business appointments right now?” Shadow trailed off, practically sensing Dawn’s disinterest in the subject matter at hand, and decided to try a different talk. Dawn’s eyebrow arched slightly as she sucked the juice out of the last bite of the pineapple chunk rather innocuously. “Well” good to finally get things settled with WMW, I suppose?” “Guess so.” Shadow cleared her throat again. Thankfully, Dawn wasn’t looking at her at the moment, as she was picking through the tray’s contents, trying to find another piece that suited her. She picked up a strawberry, holding it by its soft green leaves and looking at Shadow as though challenging her to give it another shot. “At least it’s over,” Shadow responded breezily, “I was hoping I’d never have to hear Slayer bring up anything about either of our past relationships, ever again, but I can’t say I didn’t expect it.” Dawn’s response was? well, nil. She simply bit off the end of the strawberry and chewed, as it to clearly say “is that all you’ve got?” Naturally, this left Shadow scrambling for a response? or, rather, a clumsy continuation of the previous train of thought. “So what was with AWS Man, and him?” She made a distasteful face. “Hitting on you?” Dawn stood up to her full height, tossing the end of the strawberry into the trash can. “You know” I really don’t want to talk about it, if you don’t mind.” Shadow’s jaw dropped for a moment, before she forced herself to pick it up. Nothing was worse than looking undignified before a mortal, or so the vampire within her was trained to think. Her eyes narrowed slightly as she looked unblinkingly at her host. “Um” okay.” Smooth, Shadow. That hadn’t been exactly the scathing response she’d been looking for, but she supposed it would have to do. She thought she detected a hint of an eye roll from Dawn, but at the moment, she was too busy scrambling for an out to be offended. She shifted the weight of her bag on her shoulder, repositioning the Television Title so that it wouldn’t fall when she was walking up to her room. “You look awfully tan for” you.” At that, Dawn’s jaw set a little firmer, while Shadow inwardly winced at her extremely poor delivery. “Yeah well” I’ve been spending a lot of time out by the pool. Enjoying my week off.” “Some of us should be so lucky,” Shadow said lightly. The remark landed a little farther left of center than Shadow meant for it to. Of course, she didn’t notice the bitter twist of Dawn’s lips into a half-sneer, because her eyes had once again drifted downwards, avoiding the mortal’s intimidating gaze. “Well hey, some of us should be so lucky to have friends who take a bullet for us during our title shot.” A hollow feeling clipped Shadow suddenly in the stomach, perhaps a bit like being shot. She swallowed hard, her flat-soled boots squeaking against the kitchen tile as she shifted her weight. “I need to call Rune, so” I’ll just let you get back to your week off.” All the anger faded from Dawn’s face, and it was once again just a sense of annoyance that Shadow felt radiating off of her. How deep that ran, it was hard to tell, but talking to Dawn was like trying to reach through a brick wall right now. Impossible. “Leaving so soon?” Dawn quipped emotionlessly. Shadow shrugged. “Virgil has a new estate in New Orleans that he wants me to see.” She figured that the simple answer would work, but evidently, she was wrong. “Oh?” Dawn snapped, “I thought he was busy with business concerns.” Shadow squeezed her eyes shut for a moment, but unfortunately, her genie seemed to be all out of wishes. “Look” I had it prearranged with him. He IS busy, but he wanted me to see the estate anyway, and he probably won’t even be down there when I go.” Gods, if that doesn’t sound like you’re scrambling for an excuse? Why don’t you just tell her “it’s not what it looks like?” “Sure?” but before Dawn could finish the remark, Shadow cut in again, her own voice starting to twist with anger. “Do you know where Alexis is” I’d like to let her know that we’re leaving, so she can get her things together.” “You’re going to take her too?” For the first time, Dawn stepped around the kitchen counter and closer to her stablemate, a few errant drops of water spilling onto the floor. “Alexis and I had plans! The poor girl has practically only the clothes on her back, and I’m going to take her out shopping to get some new ones.” Shadow shook her head. “Plans can wait, Dawn?” “No. They can’t.” Shadow froze once again, Dawn’s voice cutting through her like a steel blade. “Shadow, you can’t just take away every facet of her mortal life, okay” I don’t give a damn what you think you need her for, she needs to be able to have something to hang on to, someone to keep her sane. Like I never did.” Shadow’s eyes widened slightly. She hadn’t expected such a vehement reaction, but despite the anger, Shadow couldn’t deny that Dawn’s point had hit home? or perhaps she just didn’t want to risk driving the wedge deeper between herself and Dawn. Whatever the motive, she simply nodded. Dawn stood poised for confrontation, and seemed to be slightly surprised when it didn’t come. Instead, the normally fiery vampire turned on her heel and walked up to the guest room that she had been occupying for some weeks at the McFarlane Estate. Whether Alexis was coming or not, Shadow still had a few things that she needed to pack up before leaving again. And in the mood Dawn was in right now, leaving was almost a relief. .:: ::: ::. Undisclosed Date Outside of New Orleans, Louisiana 4.30 roughly Shadow didn’t usually fuss too much about her appearance’didn’t have to, really. Being of Tzimisce blood gave her the remarkable ability to sculpt herself into however shockingly beautiful a being that she wanted to be, and once there, nothing else was really necessary. Makeup would have been overkill, and clothes? well, clothes were a necessity in her situation. Perhaps, had she had her own manse in the Carpathians, she could have pranced around clothed in only bone armor, but somehow, she didn’t think that the people she worked with (or the ones she hung around for that matter) would take too kindly to her sense of fashion. She tried not to take too much out of her fussing over looking nice for Virgil. It had been a long time since Shadow had been able to feel the pulse-pounding excitement of seeing a lover, and wanting to make him think you were the most beautiful creature on the planet. She knew with relative certainty that Virgil already thought that. Nevertheless, she had stood in front of a mirror in the airport bathroom picking at her long black waves of hair for almost half an hour, making obsessively certain that each curl lay just right. But now, though her stomach was tied in knots, there was no time for acting silly and childish about the whole thing. Quietly, she opened the front door and stepped in; her senses lead her towards a large room on the left side of the sweeping grand staircase. It wasn’t so much that she scented him out, just that every time Virgil was near, there was a certain dizzy anticipation that directed her movements inexplicably in his direction? the sooner to be in his arms again, or so she hoped’there hadn’t been much of that lately, even despite their ecstatic reunion in Las Vegas a few weeks ago. She would have wondered why, but right now, she couldn’t concentrate on why, only on want. Virgil's eyes snapped up as she walked into the room, and couldn't help smiling slightly, even if he was irritated. He felt his heart pound in his chest, though it wasn't a necessary process, and he stood to meet her, looking her over, completely entranced as usual by her appearance, which tonight consisted of an elegant corset, heeled boots, and a rather short skirt, perhaps one of the only modern looks he had any fondness for. Of course, her supernatural beauty shone through anything she wore, and as usual, he was enthralled, and quite ecstatic to set eyes upon her again. A smile spread over her face; she had seemed rather calm and non-confrontational this week in any case, but right now, she seemed to be positively glowing. “Virgil” Nazdar, I’ve missed you. Europe wasn’t the same without you being on the trip.” Her voice was uncharacteristically warm as she alluded to the obvious important history between the two of them; had it not been for him, she would have had no clue of the stupid mistake that was her entire career in WMW. That set off a slight point of irritation in his mind... not so much with her specifically, but more with what had gone on before she'd actually left for Germany. He wasn't intending to cause a confrontation, but something about being completely passed over pierced his ages-hardened veneer, and actually hurt. 'I noticed you weren't too interested in seeing me beforehand.' Shadow winced. Of all people, she hadn’t expected him to guilt trip her, especially after she’d flown all the way from Toronto to New Orleans to see him. She shook her head, almost disbelievingly. “There wasn’t anything I could do” Soma called me for training, and I had to go. You said you were busy anyway, and I didn’t want to get in the way.” She was trying as hard as she could to keep her temper down right now, but after what had happened just a few hours ago with Dawn, she didn’t know if she was going to be able to keep from exploding. Virgil ran his fingers through his hair, more to get it out of his face than any kind of annoyance. Sitting alone for a few weeks had left him rather irritable, even to perhaps the only one he never expected to disagree with. 'What use would it have been if I wasn't? I'm surprised he hasn't called in the few seconds you've been in the room.' Shadow hadn’t the words at the moment, so she only growled’but the growl was a sign of the storm brewing underneath, and seconds later, it burst forth. “What do you care”! I swear to Jesu Christe, Virgil, if it’s not Dawn breathing down my neck about how I should be acting around Soma, it’s you with your jealousy as regards my relationship to him. He GAVE me something by sharing his power with me, and for me to turn around and spit in his face is? unthinkable!” A noise of disgust found its way out as well. “Are you that petty that you have to resort to jealous guilt trips to try and make me care about you” Is me flying out here at the first second I’m available to see you not enough proof that I do?” He narrowed his eyes slightly, anger coming forth from the pent up annoyance of the past few weeks. 'Of course it is, but I can hardly depend on having any time with you, because by the end of the night, you'll be gone again, and I won't see you for another few days at the least, depending on whether or not he decides to keep his hands on you constantly at the arena or not. Why is it so unthinkable for me to actually prefer seeing you occasionally?' At that, a slight hint of red began to bleed into her irises, a rather unsettling consequence of having imbibed Soma’s blood some weeks back. “How can you be so selfish?” she snapped, “It’s not like you’ve been making time for me! You made such an issue out of having business to take care of that I thought leaving well enough alone would be perfectly fine.” She shook her head, the hurt in her eyes starting to force the colors of her irises to splinter, red and green mixing together in a palette of mixed, confused emotions. “I don’t know what’s happened to you. I wasn’t supposed to fall in love with you, you know, but I did because you were impulsive and romantic, and truthful, before all that. I didn’t come all the way down here to be with some jealous vampire-child who’s crying because he didn’t have the courage to take what he wanted, and all because he was stupid, and let it go once before. If I wanted that, I could have dragged myself back to Seattle to find your brother, since I was supposed to be involved with him before you decided that I’d be better off with you.” There was no “too much” for Shadow at this point’she wanted what they’d had before, not this, and she’d be damned if he was going to try to force her to be happy with anything less, especially when that involved a guilt trip over both her own and Soma’s motives. She folded her arms over her chest, refusing to look at him. Virgil pressed his hands against his temples, trying to calm himself down, since he'd been the one to start all of this in the first place. He wasn't entirely sure what had caused him to bring up feeling left out in the first place, but somehow he knew that it would end up with him being wrong... he was rather talented at that. 'Alright... I'm sorry I brought it up. Staying here while you were in Germany, and tending to bastard Cathayans who constantly threaten me with final death has left me slightly embittered. I don't mean to take it out on you, because it's not your fault that any of it's happening.' He forced a sigh, shaking his head. 'And the business won't be a concern anymore.' The passion slowly continued fading from her eyes as she shook her head, answering him dully. “Why?” He took a step closer to her, attempting to bridge the gulf between them somehow. 'It was a worthless pursuit that only kept me from you. I was tired of seeing you with Soma all the time because I had that ridiculous engagement to deal with, and so I broke the agreement. I've been completely miserable since I found my way back to you, and I refuse to let something so inane tear us apart. Experiencing that once was more than my sanity could stand.' Finally, she turned her gaze back to him, but she had absolutely nothing to say, for once. There was no reason for guile between the two of them, but the earlier events of today, plus feeling like Virgil was simply a shell of who he’d been before? well, they were taking their respective tolls. Wearily, she shook her head. “I don’t know if it’s wise for us to try to talk right now” Why don’t I just get a flight back to Toronto and see you in a few days?” Her already travel-weary body practically tried to throw her into his arms in order to avoid having to spend another few hours cooped up on a plane, but try as she might to keep herself from doing it, she just couldn’t. That last little floodgate in her mind somehow realized how angry she was at Virgil for how he’d just acted, how he was still acting right now, like all the other dispassionate Kindred she’d ever met? if she stayed, she might very well do their relationship more damage than good, and after planning for everything to go so well, and being so sorely disappointed from the moment she set foot on US soil. He couldn't stand what was going on right now. This wasn't supposed to be happening right now, when they were finally face-to-face again. The frustration snapped in his head, and suddenly, he didn't care about Cathayans or Soma anymore. A quick step crossed the distance between them, and he slipped his arms around her stubborn figure, pressing his lips gently to her conveniently presented neck for a few moments, attempting to flush out all of the frustration in a way that would be a bit more enjoyable for both of them. He only paused for a moment. 'I refuse to sleep while you're unhappy.' Finally, the dam broke’finally, he was himself again. She slid her arms around him, pulling herself closer to him by default as she leaned up to kiss him hungrily, having missed the way his lips felt pressed to hers after such an absence. And for the moment, it seemed that time slipped away and all was made right? but with Virgil as protective as he was, the dread still lingered in the back of Shadow’s mind that it wouldn’t be long before Soma’s closeness to her proved to be a problem once again. .:: ::: ::. Undisclosed Date Outside of New Orleans, Louisiana 9.30 Shadow rolled over, vaguely feeling the warmth of the sun blazing away outside. Gradually, she was learning not to fear it, but it would take some time, as she’d spent many decades hiding in darkness whenever it was out. She was also still having to get used to the feeling of not sleeping through an entire day, and not feeling drowsy just because the sun was coming up. Every little noise woke her, and this time, it was the noise of padding, quiet footsteps that awoke her. She tried not to be annoyed. She tried as hard as she possibly could, by pulling herself closer to Virgil’s chest, to ignore that this disturbance was likely someone coming to pull them cruelly apart once again now that they had finally gotten things settled. “Ms. Lia?” Came a whispered voice. Shadow’s forehead creased minutely in irritation. “Ms Lia!” More urgent this time. Using Virgil’s chest for leverage, Shadow sat up, turning her head to the side. ?What?!? she snapped. Her eyes were assailed by Daria, the new, young assistant for Leinad Soma. Shadow had asked that she fly down to New Orleans ASAP, since Soma had hired her to be a guide after all’and if Shadow wanted to go out during the day, she’d need her, since theoretically, she was still quite blinded by the sunlight. Daria’s eyes widened to see a rather nude Shadow, and a rather nude companion, but she didn’t dare say anything. And who would under these circumstances? She backed away slightly, pressing herself almost up against the door, and suddenly stammering. “I” I-uh? I’m sorry Ms. Lia, but um? Mr. Soma called a few minutes ago.” “And?” “And he wants you to come see him! At his club!” She breathed out a heavy breath, her chest still heaving from the effort of being around these creatures and trying to remain calm instead of running away screaming. Shadow rolled her eyes, and Daria immediately started to wonder what she’d done wrong. Instead of getting assaulted, however, Shadow simply threw the covers back, and started dressing. Within a few moments, she was ready to go’but she had passed not a word to her assistant, Daria. She hefted her bag up over her shoulder, but then her eyes caught on Virgil’s motionless form, drifting in the peaceful sleep of the undead. She couldn’t just leave him here, without him knowing where she’d gone, because God knows, if he was jealous now, it would certainly be worse if she disappeared on him. Hastily, she pulled a note pad from her bag and scribbled on it. She left the note lying on the bedside table where he’d be sure to find it when he woke up, but left the room with a quickness in her step. Leinad Soma was not a man to be kept waiting. Virgil-- My apologies- Soma called at the last minute about business, and I fear that the punishment for offending him is greater than that of making you jealous. Listen well, though- I will see you in Las Vegas, and I hope you will be as assured of my feelings then as you are now. I will remember how I felt last night. Let us hope, then, that you will remember what you felt as well. Amiti’s, je reste la tienne, Lia .:: ::: ::. Undisclosed Location It wasn’t ever easy being Lia Vlaszy. You think you have one thing settled, and then something else goes wrong. But there’s no use complaining, is there? What will you say? Will you demand that you be given someone else’s soul so that you no longer have to deal with this confusion, this frustration, this pain? So was it choosing Dawn’s road? And what exactly was Dawn’s road? She seemed to be covert enough in her feelings and issues towards Shadow that the vampiress couldn’t rightly make up her mind whether to harbor like or dislike towards the young woman. At the very least, Dawn’s implication that Shadow wouldn’t have her TV Title had Dawn not taken a bullet for her, in the form of Jake Hix, was very troubling. It was troubling especially when this was a title whose legitimacy she had to defend week after week’but it wasn’t just the title’s legitimacy, it was hers. All eyes were on a champion who had to defend her belt at every show. And with all eyes on her, all eyes would see when she finally slipped and fell. And what would choosing Virgil do for her? Virgil might have thought wrestling silly, but it was something Shadow had grown to love over the year or so that she had been involved in it on the grand PTC stage. Without a doubt, she loved Virgil? but how long would he be willing to return that love before he went off to do something else more interesting? She’d heard all the stories about his kind’the love “em and leave “em sleazy gypsies. She didn’t want to think that’s what Virgil was, but the thought scared her’he’d left her once, who’s to say that he wouldn’t do it again, no matter what promises he made in order to try and set things right” Promises could easily be broken, and for Shadow at least, every broken heart was a little bit harder to heal than the last. As far as Soma went, it seemed the safest option. Soma had given her these newfound powers, Soma was teaching her how to use them, Soma was providing her with everything she needed, including distractions to busy herself with when she was bored. And at the very least, if Soma could not offer her the emotional depth of the feelings she had for Virgil, he could offer her the physical attraction that she desired. Physical attraction, after all, was much safer than emotional strings. The one question that lingered regarding Soma was one of utmost weight: how long would it be until he asked repayment for all his kindnesses? And what if that repayment was too much for her to give? He’d already done enough for her that she couldn’t refuse whatever he asked. Shadow’s life had supposedly changed for the better lately’everything involving WMW was finally over. She could walk in the sunlight as she chose for the first time in centuries, she could win wrestling titles, and she could make people pay attention to her where it was necessary, whether it was by using beauty, brains, or brawn (what little of it she had). But when it came to one single person who actually cared about her for no other reason than her being who she was? she wasn’t so sure that she didn’t outright fail miserably. Every relationship was one of convenience, where she was expected to give some huge chunk of herself in order to continue getting any attention at all’there was no unconditional love. There were conditions everywhere she turned. The frightening part was how often those conditions clashed with one another. Thus far, Shadow had been able to maintain her balancing act, but every day of unlife that continued for her was a walk on a razor’s edge’one day, she might not be so lucky. One day, she might lose her footing. .:: ::: ::.
