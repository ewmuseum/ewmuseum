How it all began (part 4)

'On your wedding day, you die. For that day you lose all personal identity and you are no longer a single person, you become a piece of a couple. In marriage, everything that was you dies and becomes apart of something bigger. Why would anyone want to lose who they are and die?' Salaok Davis. ---- 1999 With his head basically on his knees, he sits on the edge of his bed, eyes focused hard on the carpet below him, taking deep breaths and trying to calm himself down. But today, there would be no calm, merely raipd thoughts of what he was about to do. About to become. Sweat running down his forehead and coming to the bridge of his nose before finally coming to the end and dripping off onto the dark red fabric below. This was the day in which his life would change forever and despite the fact that he wanted this more than anything else in the world right now, he was afraid. Salaok Davis was afraid of what he was about to do, what he was about to become. What he would say and do today would not only change his life forever, but would change the life of someone else who was dear to him and he loved with all of his being. Changing people's lives was something that Salaok had become used to as his role in The Lair had seen him take many a life, or simply destroy them and make them regret the day they stood against the powerful underworld force, but to change the life of someone who loves you into the life of someone who is married to you was a big thing for Salaok. Every time he moved his eyes, it was like the world moved in fast darting motions and he couldn't seem to control it despite the fact that it was in turn making him more dizzy than he was already feeling. Sometimes being giddy and dizzy can be a good thing, but for Salaok, this dizzy feeling was making him feel sick to the core and he wanted nothing more than for everything to just slow down as he leant back onto the bed and lay there looking at the roof as the world spun around him. For some people, this feeling of the world spinning is normally related to the night of heavy drinking at the bucks night, but Salaok didn't have one of those. The day before his wedding was spent down in the basement with Mr Wynveen and then the night was spent cleaning up the mess that it had caused once he had realized that over one hundred people were coming to the house the next day for his wedding. Not only however did he have to arrange the clean up for Mr.Wynveen's body, but also for the clean up of his wife's body which he had had Corvus take care of for him. The arrangement with Corvus hadn't been the first deal that the two men had made and it was Salaok willingness to outsource some of the hits which was making him more of a powerful member within The Lair. Salaok was fast becoming one of The Lair highest ranking members and it had only taken a few years for him to reach the same level that his brother before him had reached. The only difference was that Salaok had no idea of the levels in which Johnathon had gone to as his works had been kept a secret due to something called The Inner Circle. Unless you were apart of The Inner Circle, you had no idea what it was all about, other than the fact that what you do suddenly becomes hidden from the eyes of those outside of it. But today, nothing would be hidden from anyone. Today Salaok would put everything in public view so they could see just how much he cared for Lisha. Just how much he loved Lisha. Sitting up again and then pushing himself off the bed, he brings himself to his feet and looks down at the pile of bloodied clothes beside the bed which were from yesterday's efforts and grins to himself. Grinning about the way in which the day took place and the effort in which he went to to cause more pain and turn his basement into the world of hurt that people had feared him for. Walking over the carpet and past the mirror, he suddenly back-tracks to take one finally look at himself as an unmarried man for in less than an hour the deed would be done and no longer would Salaok be just Salaok, he would be apart of Salaok and Lisha Davis, forever. Everything that he saw in the mirror was perfect, the suit, the hair tied back, the goatee shaved off, no tattoos revealed and a smile that he simply couldn't wipe from his face. If there was anything he could have changed it would have been his eyes of pure white orbs but there was nothing that could be done about it and he knew it didn't matter to Lisha as she had looked into those eyes every day for the past five years. Happy with himself, Salaok walks out of his room and down the stairs towards the entrance of the house where the noise begins to rise and the as he pushes open the front doors, a loud cheer comes from the gathering waiting for the wedding to start. It wasn't the choice of Salaok or Lisha to have the wedding on the doorstep of The Nevermore Mansion as The Lair wanted it to be in a place which was safe enough for so many of their members to being all at the one time which didn't bother Salaok at all. The safety of The Lair was something that had been rumored to be under attack in the near future as a situation was arising from some of it's former members who had so far not only managed to escape the hands of The Lair, but been able to create a force of their own. An event as big as the wedeing of two of The Lair's members was a prime time for this rebel group to attack but while of the grounds of The Nevermore Mansion, the chances of them being able to do anything were very remote. With a grin on his face, trying his best to hide the nerves which had caused him to nearly black out earlier in the day, Salaok stands in front of the crowd and nods to them and with that, the old church organ which had been bought just for the occassion begins to sound out. It many pipes churn the note of the Wedding March out into the warm Spring afternoon air as the horse drawn cart pulls up at the head of the isle. There she was, beautiful like nothing else he had ever laid eyes on. From the first day he met Lisha, his heart had raced and he knew that she was what he needed in his life. Not just a partner in life, but an equal. Someone he could be completely and utterly himself with and today marked that day for the rest of their natural lives. Stepping down out of the cart, she stands there in all her beauty with her bright red hair framing her perfect face which was the idealic frame for those eyes which connected with Salaok's straight away. From one end of the isle, to the other, their eyes locked and refused to look elsewhere as Lisha began to walk up towards the man in whom she would marry. But Lisha walked alone. Her family wanted nothing to do with this and were part of the uprising against The Lair. The only family she had in the crowd was Mephiston who despite the fact he hated Salaok, Lisha had requested that he be there so he had turned up unwillingly. Mephiston stood up as Lisha walks up to his row of seats and she stops to look at him before the two of the embrace in a heart felt hug that sees a small tear run down Lisha's face for this wedding would mean that her family would no longer want anything to do with her. So much was their hatred towards Salaok and The Lair, that today she wasn't taking on a new name, but being disowned of her old family name which hurt her greatly, but it wasn't enough to overpower the love she felt for Salaok. Putting Mephiston back in his seat, Lisha turns and walks towards the bottom of the stairs where she is greeted by Johnathon Davis, Salaok's older brother who escorts her up the steps towards the man in whom she was leaving her family behind for. As Salaok stands at the top of the steps, a massive hand plants itself on Salaok shoulder and the familiar voice of Quartz sounds quietly into the ear of Salaok. 'There is still time to pull out Salaok. You remember what the guides have told you about this marrage and yet you still wish to go through with it?'Quartz voice was one of corcern and question as clearly something was known about this marrage, or at least thought to be. 'Quartz, I must request that you do not question this marrage again, especially not now as I watch her walk towards me.' His voice, emotionless... cold... knowing of what is to come but overpowered by love. 'Have it your way Salaok, but you know that the guide hasn't been wrong so far with your life and if the words are true, Lisha will die within five years of today as will your children. You must marry someone else first if you truely love Lisha!' 'Quartz, the guide has been wrong before and she is wrong again about this. I will protect Lisha with all of my heart from my brother. If the guide is true that my first wife and child with die at the hand of my own blood, then he will be the one I watch closest and protect her from him. I can not marry another.' Salaok's tone is starting to become annoyed and short but quickly silences himself as Lisha reaches the top step leaving just enough time for Quartz to whisper one final thing. 'I will do all I can to protect your family then Salaok, but not even I know how this will take place. You have my word, I will protect you and yours.' Salaok turns to Quartz, taking his eyes of Lisha for the first time since she stepped out of the horse drawn cart, gives him a nod and then turns back to Lisha with a smile on his face, knowing that the monster would be protecting them. Stepping forward, Salaok takes Lisha by the hand as Johnathon moves off to the side and the two smile at each other through Lisha's veil of pure white, which although her soul was far from innocent, she still wanted to get married in white regardless. Staring into each others eyes for the entine duration of the ceremony, they know of the love they share, but deep inside, Salaok also knows of the words from the Guide who had told him of what was to come with the marrage and their first child when that should come about. The future had been fore-told and Salaok knew full well what he was getting himself into, so blinded by love that he believed he could change the future with his power and money. No one could hurt Lisha as long as Salaok was around right? Especially not his own family. The thought of the words of warning were all he could think of throughout the wedding and then came the question which made him think. 'Do you Salaok, take Lisha, to be your lawful wedded wife, to love and cherrish, to protect and honor, till death you do part?' Till death do they part. This marrage would cause Lisha's death should Salaok believe the words of the Guide. No, he could protect her. That much he was sure about. 'I do.' In saying those words, Salaok was defying the future and daring it almost to do it's worst as there was no way in his mind that a blood member would cause the death of Lisha and their first born son. A sigh escaped from the lungs of Quartz as he was the only one person to have heard this warning and he knew now what would come about within the next five years which would see what was now a nearly uncontrollable Salaok, turn into a lonely, uncontrolable animal which could either break or unite a new bread of Lair like the Guide had spoken of. It had been a few years since Salaok's actions had caused a massive rift between The Lair and The Tragik Kingdom, but this marrage would be the thing which would in the end cause Salaok's own personal Tragik Kingdom and engulf him in the world of The Void far more than what he was now. For now he had love, but should the story come true, he would end up with nothing as payment for his crimes. ---- Present Day 'For the years we were together my beloved, I had forgotten about the future that had been foretold to me and thought that it had nothing to do with who I was or what I was destined to become. And yet now I kneel here in front of you like the Guide said would happen with your death at the hands of my own blood kind. A blood kind which I knew nothing of until the year before your death. When you said the two small words that sealed us into holy wed, you in turn sealed your fate into the grave within five years and for that I'm sorry. I should have told you.' Salaok's words are dull and full of hurt as he kneels in front oft he grave of his beloved late wife Lisha who lays next to his late son Darrell. It was only a year ago that Darrell had passed away and the scares which ravage Salaok's mind are still deep and have stopped him from being the man in which he was deemed to be. Just like the day of the wedding, a massive hand rests itself on the shoulder of Salaok which he hadn't felt the presense of for a long time now. Looking up over his shoulder at the monster of a man who stands there with his hand on Salaok's shoulder, a look of knowing looks back at him as Quartz stands there in the dark of the night, as always looking over Salaok. 'She did know Salaok.' Quartz's voice was calm and controlled as it was most of the time when dealing with Salaok as he began to explain the situation in which Lisha had found herself due to the love in which she had for Salaok. 'Before you had even asked her to be your wife, she knew of the words in which The Guide had spoken for I couldn't let her make such a choice without knowing what would become of her. Lisha was a powerful member within The Lair, much like yourself Salaok, and I couldn't sit back and watch as her fate would become doomed as her loss would effect how our organisation went about itself. So I had sat her down and told her of the words of the future and with my words came her tears.' Quartz takes his eyes away from Salaok's and looks square at the gravestones, first at Lisha's and then across to Darrell's as both of them were foretold in the world of the Guide. 'But through her tears and the knowledge of her future, she told me of her love for you. She told me that even should you be together for a week, it would be a week that would be worth the rest of her life and would be happy to have it as the rest of her life. I tried to talk her out of it Salaok, but her feelings for you were as true as your's were for her and in turn I had no choice but to allow the relationship between the two of you go further and in turn you would be married against my wishes.' A look of shock progressively grows over Salaok's face as he listens to the words of his closest friend as realization that the woman in whom he loved knew that by marrying him, she was in turn daring the future that had been told of as well. Lisha had known of the Guides words in which she had herself always turned to to find the answers in life and yet she had gone ahead with the marrage knowing full well it would end her life and that of her first born son. 'The only thing Salaok is that she never wanted to have a child with you as the thought of her child dying because of her choice to marry you was simply too much to handle for her which is why she was so upset when she told you about being pregnant with Darrell. She did everything she could to stop herself from getting pregnant but somehow she still did which in turn made her think that the words spoken by The Guide were unstoppable which is why when your brother shot her, she didn't first it. She knew then and there that the story was about to come to an end and it was needed for you to be able to move on in life. She chose her fate when she decided that she wanted to marry you and she didn't care about it, she loved you. And although I know you still think about them both, it is time to move on Salaok and be the man you were predicted to be. It's time to rebuild The Lair and this time I'm going to stand with you insted of standing back and watching. I'm not asking you to forget Lisha and Darrell, but honor then by not fighting for what you've always believed in Salaok. Today marks the one year since Darrell's death, and so when you walk into that arena tonight, I want you to fight for him against Jamester. You are a champion Salaok, but currently you are a champion with nothing to prove that to those who haven't known your past. Change what people think of you Salaok, become The Harvester of Minds all over again and rewrite the story that made you the feared man you are today.' Salaok turns his attention away from Quartz as he looks over at the gravesite of the young Darrell and with a voice of strength that he hasn't held for some time, he speaks. 'You are right old friend. From now on it will no longer be a matter of letting the memory of their deaths control me, but be my power. I wasn't able to protect them from the future foretold, but in turn I gained two angels to look over me.' The two old friends had united once again, after a long time apart, the duo which had caused so much pain and hurt to those who had stood against them were finally back on the same page. A feeling of knowing between the two had always been, but times had changed, as had they. Regardless, they were still True Evil and together they would bring The Lair back into existance and into the fears of those who stood against it once again.
