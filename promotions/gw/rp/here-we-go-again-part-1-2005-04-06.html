Here we go again.... Part 1

6 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/05 'Yes, I realize what I just said made absolutely no sense to you. I understand that you believe I've gone off the rocker once again, but just trust me on this one. I know what I'm doing. I know who I am dealing with. It's not going to be an issue. You just get that paperwork ready, and to me by Friday. Is that understood?' There is a long silence as the picture fades in to see ICE, with a small silver cellphone pressed up against his right ear. Ice's long black hair is neatly pulled back into a low pony tail, which rests gently upon the back of his neck. Slowly ICE's free left hand raises into view as it grabs a hold of the tie holding his hair in place, slowly pulling it out from around his mane. The long black locks untangle from around each other as ICE gives a small and quick side to side shake of his head. 'As long as you do what I tell you to, we've got no problems. Remember that. Now I've got some things I must do. Keep in contact, and do not disrespect me.... for your own good.' With those cryptic words ICE slowly pulls the cellphone from his ear and flips it shut. ICE's cold, merciless blue eyes begin to glaze over as he begins to stare off into the distance. A few seconds of silence, pure and blissful silence, pass as ICE continues to gaze off into the nothingness of the world before him. Suddenly the silence, and ICE's gaze is abruptly interrupted as the small silver cellphone, still in ICE's hand, begins to eek and ring loudly. Quickly ICE flips the phone open, and once again places it up against his right ear. 'She's here? Already?' ICE glances down at his left wrist, which is covered by a generic silver watch. 'A little early, but that's okay. Tell her I'll be right down.' Once again ICE pulls the phone from his ear and flips it shut. This time, however, instead of dazing off into a trance, ICE toss the phone down behind him. No thump, or thud is heard after the tossing of the cell phone. The picture widens to show the cellphone lying in the middle of a queen sized bed, which is covered in a white and blue bed covering. The picture quickly returns to focusing on ICE as he gently runs his fingers through his long jet black hair. After a couple strokes ICE pulls his hands away from his head, and turns towards the door across the room from the bed he is standing in front of. . . . . . . . **** FADE OUT ******* **** FADE IN ********** . . . . . . . As the camera fades in, the beautiful voice go Global Wrestling, Brandi Sights is shown standing next to the front counter of a hotel lobby. Her flowing, fire red hair is in semi - curls which softly drape just past her sexy tan shoulders. The rest of her tan body is covered by a tight, flowered spring dress, that ends just before her knees. Anxiously Brandi paces in a small circle next to the counter as if waiting for someone, when she is suddenly startled by a familiar voice. 'Good afternoon Brandi.' Brandi quickly turns and is met face to face with ICE, who arrogantly smirks in delight at her. Brandi returns his arrogant smirk with a displeased and mockingly arrogant smirk of her own. 'Hello.' 'You, seem startled. I didn't frighten you did I?' Brandi rolls her eyes at ICE's sarcastic comment before abruptly turning away from him and walking towards the front of the hotel lobby. A larger, more arrogant smirk creeps across ICE's face as he slowly follows suit. Just before Brandi reaches the double glass doors that lead out of the hotel she turns back around, an angered look on her face. 'You know you are an ass! A complete ass.' ICE merely smiles and bows in gratitude of Brandi's remarks. This gesture infuriates the firery red - head even more, causing her to turn back around and burst through the glass doors. Again the ear to ear, arrogant smirk appears on ICE's face as he follows Brandi once again. Once outside the hotel a long black limo pulls up. A bellboy quickly opens the door for Brandi, who enters and is followed in by ICE. The bellboy shuts the two in the limo as it tentatively pulls out into the busy Las Vegas traffic. The inside of the limo fills with silence as Brandi angrily stares out the window, observing all of her surrounds except for the obnoxious nature of the man in the limo with her. 'WHAT!?' Brandi's angered yell breaks the silence as she finally notices ICE just staring at her, like a fifteen year old who is trying to get under his sister's last nerve. ICE shrugs at Brandi's question and then turns away from her. ICE begins to gaze out of the window himself, completely ignoring Sights in the process. This action appears to infuriate Brandi even more as her bottom lip begins to quiver from her anger. Again there is silence in the back of the limo as Brandi and ICE gaze out of their respective windows. The silence, however, does not last for long. 'You know, it's been a long time.' Brandi's attention quickly diverts from the scenary outside the limo as she questionly looks towards ICE. ICE however does not divert his attention from the lights and the buildings passing by as the limo continues to drive through the traffic of Sin City. 'What do you mean it's been a long time?' ICE does not immediately respond to Brandi's question, but just before she can repeat it ICE cuts her off with his answer. 'I mean nothing by it. It's just been a long time since we've sat down and had lunch together. It's been a long time since I've physically been on Evolution. It's been a long time for alot of things Brandi. It was just a general statement that escaped my inner voice, and became audible. I did not mean anything by it.' ICE continues to gaze out the window as Brandi, undeterred by ICE's response, continues to look at him in a questioning manner. Her continuous staring finally gets to ICE as his gaze turns away from the Las Vegas strip, and focuses onto her. 'What?' Brandi mockingly smirks at ICE before replying. 'Nothing.' Her mocking smirk grows even wider as she turns away from ICE and begins to look out the windown once more. ICE shakes his head, but does not stop staring at the beautiful red - head sitting next to him. 'Some things never change.' This comment quickly garners Brandi's attention as her head spins and her eyes devour ICE. 'And what is that supposed to mean?' ICE again shakes his head at Brandi Sights. 'It means that you haven't changed one bit from the last time I saw you. That's what it means Ms. Sights.' Brandi is taken back by ICE's raised tone of voice, but stands her ground, refusing to cower from him. 'I haven't changed you're right about that. But that's not a bad thing you know. You.....you however I can't say the same for.' Suddenly the limo stops, and Brandi visciously pushes her door open, and steps out of it. ICE waits a moment before slowly opening his door, and stepping out of it as well. Brandi makes her way around the back of the limo and meets ICE on his side. The two stand side by side and stare at the Global Arena. 'You know, you would think that ol' Bubba would have at least made an arena that looked oh... I don't know... new, instead of basically just rebuilding the old piece of crap from Texas here in Las Vegas.' Brandi softly chuckles at ICE's comment. ICE notices her reaction and gives a pleased smirk of his own. 'Well, shall we?' Brandi nods at ICE, and the two make their way to the building walking in sync with each other. As they reach the doors, ICE begins to look around with a slightly confused look etched upon his face as Brandi fumbles in her purse for her keys. 'Are we the only ones here?' Brandi pulls out a pair of gold keys and places them into the keyholes of the door. 'We are, but remember, that was then this is now. This is strictly business. Nothing more. Got it?' An amused smirk grows on ICE's face as Brandi pulls the doors open. ICE quickly grabs the door handle from Brandi's grip and offers her the opportunity to enter the building first. 'Strictly business.............'
