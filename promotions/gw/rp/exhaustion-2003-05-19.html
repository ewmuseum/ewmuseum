Exhaustion...

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/19 After narrowly escaping Quartz and the rest of the Nevermore I returned home to find CW sitting in my living room feet propped on my table smoking a cigar as usual. This one’s odor was especially pungent. I dropped my bag on the floor after stepping in the door and immediately inquired as to exactly what the hell he was doing in my house. Knocking his feet off my table and extinguishing his cigar in the cup of coffee sitting next to him came soon afterward. After sitting through his usual lame ass excuses for why he was in my house again, including “I thought someone was breaking in”, “You left the oven on”, and “I was seeing if you were here”, none of which cover up the real which is his undying love for my big screen TV, he informs me of my next match. “Roz Blayze!”” I snort in exasperated dismay. My first thought was filled with wonder. Could it have been possible that all the boxing kangaroos were booked and Roz Blayze, the Nevermore’s perpetual whipping boy, was all they could get for me to pummel on such short notice? Then on second though I almost feel bad for the guy. I mean his wife left him, his son’s in the hospital, and on top of that he’s got to face me. CW begins to gather his so called “training equipment”, which in actuality isn’t really training equipment at all. CW’s “training equipment” consists of a small humidor full of cheap ass, gas station cigars that smell like vinegar soaked brimstone, a newspaper, a stopwatch, and a clipboard with an increasingly ridiculous workout regimen attached to it. I disgustedly watch the glee with which he places all of these things inside a mesh bag for a moment before my thoughts drift back to Blayze. He’s probably hobbled up in a hospital lobby right now thinking about how hard it’s gonna be to face me at Evolution. This is because he’s under the impression that we are somehow friends, when in actuality, if it were up to me I’d have pulled the plug on his little boy long ago. I meant everything I said about him and everyone else on Overload, and in no way intend to take any of it back. CW finishes packing up and motions that it’s time for us to go as he attempts to usher me out the door. After driving for god knows how many hours this old man expects me to turn right back around and go to the gym to perform the latest of his bizarre training techniques. But then again that is just one of the prices I pay to be the epitome of excellence that is Deacon Holmes. He slaps me on the back of the head and pushes his bag into my grasp as we walk out the door. Exhausted and fatigued, but back on the grind... someone’s gonna pay for this on Monday.
