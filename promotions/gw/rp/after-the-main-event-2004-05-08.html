After the main event

8 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/04 'SON OF A BITCH!!' It seemed like things were going to go Brick's way that night. After getting his shots in on both Deacon Holmes and Train earlier in the evening, Brick was ready to face Josh for the World title. When the time came, Brick was ready. he dominated the bulk of the match. Then, at the last minute, Josh pulled a fast one, hitting a jawbreaker on Brick and then rolling him up into a pin, pulling down on his pants for leverage. 'Brick, calm down.' 'How the hell could Wolfe miss Josh pulling down on my pants to get the pin? It was so damn obvious!' 'Even the best make mistakes, once in a while.' 'This is bullshit!' 'It wasn't exactly the best night for me, either. First, Josh insults me, then he threatens me and then he tries to assault me. After all the opportunities I gave him, he pulls this.' 'You're right. I'm sorry.' 'Don't be. If you hadn't gotten out there when you did, he might have actually done something.' 'All I know is tonight was my chance to get at least one of the belts back to GW. Every belt's being held by cats who want to tear the company down. TUE has most of the belts and we know they don't give a fuck about GW.' 'Well, at least part of that could change depending on next week.' 'Why? What's happening next week?' 'Maverick Reno and Derek Draven have a match. The winner will be the sole Tag Team champion.' 'Yeah, Reno or Draven. Hell of a choice.' 'I'd figured you would have put things behind you where Draven was concerned.' 'You figured wrong. Ain't no way in hell I'm putting that behind me. What the hell makes you think I'd put something like that behind me, huh? Tell you what, Christina. I can call Aaron so you can ask him if he's put all the drama with Draven behind him. Oh, wait, you know what? He can't! He's never gonna be able to put it behind him! Every time he has to look in the mirror with his shirt off, he's gonna be reminded of it! He still has the brand on his chest Salaok left! Every time he watches one of my matches, he has to remember what happened! Every time he says good bye to his wife, he's afraid it might be the last time! Just like it might have been the last time two years ago and do you know why? Because of Salaok! Because of Quartz! Because of Davis! And because of that little bitch Draven! And you come to me asking to put it behind me?!' 'To be fair, you did throw him off the Globaltron into a dumpster filled with light bulbs!' 'And he had that coming, too! The son of a bitch tried to hang me from the damn rafters!' 'Well, which would you prefer: that Draven get the title or that Reno get the title?' 'I'd rather you vacated the damn thing.' 'Well, I'm the one who runs this company. So, the match stays as is.' 'Whatever. Just don't ask me to ever show any kind of compassion for Derek Draven.' 'Well, don't do it for him. Do it for yourself. Do you really think he cares whether or not you forgive him?' 'No use bitchin' about Draven now. What's done is done. I had that match won!' 'Brick, if you really want, you could always challenge Josh to a rematch next week.' 'Maybe I will.' 'And if he gives you the rematch, do me a favor?' 'Kick his ass?' 'Yes.' 'Gladly. Of course, I do still have some unfinished business with Deacon and Train.' 'I know. I saw the brawl.' 'Then you know there might just be another one.' 'I don't doubt you could handle yourself if there is. In fact, because this issue between you, Deacon and Train seems to be far from over, I may book a rematch between the three of you at High Noon.' “What do you mean “may?” What’s stopping you?” “This feud with WMW that’s starting.” “You mean that’s been started. Bad enough there are cats in this company looking to tear it down, now we got these punks from the outside looking to cause trouble.” “That’s another thing I wanted to talk to you about. I was kind of hoping you’d set an example for the rest of the locker room.” “Me?” “Well, you’re a veteran. You’re one of the guys that helped build this place. A lot of the guys backstage respect you, whether you know it or not.” “Uh-huh. I’ll think about it. In the meantime, I could use a drink. I’m heading over to the Dark Horse.” “Maybe I’ll head there a little later.” “OK, cool.”
