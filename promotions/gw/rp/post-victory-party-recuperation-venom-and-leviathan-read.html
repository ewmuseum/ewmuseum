Post Victory Party Recuperation(Venom and Leviathan read)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Thursday A ransacked apartment is show. It looks as though it would be a nice place if it weren’t trashed. There are overturned couches, there are several large pictures that have all been toppled to the ground. One sits crooked on a wall. It shows a young man with dreadlocks cut up and bleeding holding up a belt in victory inside of a cage with barbwire ropes. Empty bottles are scattered all across the floor of the apartment along with confetti, party hats, and odd articles of clothing. There is also a disco ball hanging from the middle of the ceiling still spinning, next to it there is a large banner that reads “Deacon Holmes Victory Celebration”. A low roar similar to that of a wounded animal can be heard coming from another room. The noises get louder and begin to sound more human, like that of someone having their soul ripped out as they pray in vain to the porcelain god. Deacon Holmes, a five foot eleven inch dreaded and tattooed muscular mass of arrogance sits with his back to the wall and a towel draped around his shoulders in front of the toilet. His driver/ personal assistant/ manager/ uncle, CW is also in the bathroom. Quite a physical specimen himself, he stands over Holmes dressed in the typical hired help penguin suit, complete with white gloves and a chauffeur’s hat watching in disgust as Holmes struggles to his feet. CW reaches over and flushes the toilet. CW: You ready yet boss? We were “posed to leave half an hour ago. Deacon Holmes: Yeah, soon as I... Holmes falls to the floor on his way up and quickly hangs his head over the toilet as more alcohol is rejected from his system. CW: Must’ve been some party, I hope you don’t expect me to clean this shit up neither. And how tha hell are you sick now anyway” The party was Monday night after the show, it’s Thursday afternoon! Deacon Holmes: Everybody just left bout an hour ago... CW: That’s a damn shame, you’re lucky I don’t tell your aunt bout this shit. Holmes gets a slight grin as he reaches over and pulls the shower curtain open to reveal a bathtub full of ice with a keg sitting in it Deacon Holmes: Go ahead, but how ya gonna explain this? You’re the only one here over 21, besides, I’m 18 now, what can she do? One false move by either of you and that house is mine. You know, the one in my name that you both live in. Holmes? grin turns into a toothy smile showing his platinum capped elongated canine teeth. CW: I doubt it, you don’t wanna see me wit tha heat cuz. Both men begin to laugh CW: Now get yo punk ass up, we need to get you in tha gym. Can’t have you lookin out of shape on TV when Leviathan beats you on Monday. Deacon Holmes: When who does what!” CW: When Leviathan, beats, you! Deacon Holmes: What a joke. Leviathan CAN’T beat me, it’s as simple as that. He just doesn’t have it in him. He can’t beat anyone other than a crew of foreign newscasters utterly terrified by his smoke and mirrors parlor tricks. CW: Did you see him on Monday? Deacon Holmes: All three hundred and twenty two unimpressive pounds of him. I mean let’s be serious, this clown had trouble with Eightball, how tha hell’s he supposed to handle me? My first opponent killed himself for fear of stepping in the ring with me. I’ll be surprised if this walking birth control ad even shows. CW: I guess... Deacon Holmes: You guess!” You know! Deacon suddenly springs from the ground, now appearing to be in perfect health. Deacon Holmes: That guy couldn’t beat me if I was his dick and he was in jail! Oh wait, but then again since he’s such a monster and all he’d probably kill everybody and get out huh? CW: ... Deacon Holmes: Tha guy’s a fake, and to make matters worse he’s illiterate! You think he has his little cloaked helper read that scroll before he comes in for fun!” He doesn’t even know what tha damn thing says! CW: Well what about Venom Alexander? You know he’s gonna be gunning for you too on Monday. Deacon Holmes: Yeah right, he’s about as threatening as a bag full of gummi bears. He’s about the softest champion I’ve ever seen. First he gets punked by me on not just one show but two shows in a row, one of which being the night he won tha damn thing, and then on top of that he let that chump Leviathan get away with the belt! That guy’s dad probably wishes he has wasted him on the shower curtain that morning. CW: What!” He’s no you but Venom Alexander is tight. He’s impressive man. Deacon Holmes: At what? Losing and getting punked? Cause that’s all I’ve see him do for two weeks straight! CW: You look like you’re feeling better all ready. Kinda heated now huh? Deacon Holmes: Hell yeah! CW: Then hurry up and put some clothes on so we can hit tha gym bitch! Holmes? expression changes to show his distaste at the fact he was tricked. As he walks past his uncle CW slaps him on the back up the head while ushering him out of the bathroom CW: And when you get back you cleanin this shit up too! Deacon Holmes mouths the word damn as he makes his way to his room to find some clothes to wear.
