The Journey: Part 5/From the view of “The Connection”

4 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-4-04 Early Afternoon Well cats and kiddies its time once again for the fine folks of “The Connection” to take you on an interesting trip into the real and the at times comical world that we live in. That’s right folks its time for our appointment with Joshua Curtis, Zack Johnson, Tina Davis, Juri Arisugawa and Mizuno and yes I just named all FIVE members of the stable that is known as the “Connection” because well’I’m tired of leaving them out of the spotlight damn it! Now on this particular day we are in the residence of the “Connection??you know what fuck it it’s the “Old School Connection” because that is exactly how you remember the stable and how it got famous with the fans of the various territories that the group has bee in but anyways? The group is at the place that they call home, a house that is modeled after 18th century England with the interior and exterior but with the modern amenities that we use in the 21st Century. You know the laptops, refrigerators, microwaves, digital cable and all that fun stuff. We begin in the living room where we find Mizuno Ami, the younger sister of the leader of the OSC in Joshua Curtis and she’s engaged in one of the less trivial matters with a certain friend of hers in Michiru Kaiou: BATTLESHIP! “I got you now Michi-sama!” The blue-haired genius of the group said to her slightly older lover as she eyed the coordinates on her board to try and figure out where Michiru’s battleship was on the board but of course as is supposed to be the case with the game Michiru wasn’t quite so sure that Ami COULD find it! “Go ahead and try dear but I doubt that you can find the ship amongst my board!” The violin prodigy exclaimed but soon her glee turned into horror as Ami called out B-5, which sunk Michiru’s battleship, and to say that Michiru took losing well would be a SLIGHT overstatement! “Uh oh looks like someone won’t be getting any special prize in bed tonight!” Juri said as she came out from the bedroom at the top of the stairs with her girlfriend in Tina Davis as they were in fact getting some outfits out for the trip that they were going to make with Joshua Curtis to this week’s Evolution show. “Hey no cheap shots guys!” Ami playfully exclaimed at Juri and the orange-haired duelist from Ohtori Academy blushed slightly at the remark from Ami. “Was it THAT bad Ami” You know that you sometimes get a present from Michiru if you lose to her and?” Is all Juri got our before getting elbowed by her girlfriend from Toronto, Ontario, Canada. “Easy Juri we don’t want to make a scene before the big show in Tokyo in two weeks.” The Canadian blonde said which caused Zack to pop his head up from behind the counter that he was tending to. “What show are you talking about Tina?” The man from San Francisco, California asked and when Ami explained that it was “Showdown in Tokyo” Zack started to tense up a little bit. “I heard about this show from Joshua over the last couple of nights and honestly I can’t say that I’m too thrilled with him being involved with this match but if he accepts an invite from Ken then I can’t do anything but pray for him and the individuals in this match with him because I know him better than anyone and when he’s in a situation that he’s forced into “He” comes out more often than not and hurts people’bad!” Zack said to the ladies assembled in the living room as they all wondered where Joshua was and that leads us to upstairs right about? NOW! Now I grant that Joshua and Zack are one of the first gay couples to be competing in the fed known as GW but fact is that Joshua is a deeply religious person and when he gets any kind of time in the residence of the “Old School Connection” and specifically his room that he shares with Zack and he is alone then he turns all the lights out, lights two candles at the foot of his bed, takes his “perched” stance and closes his eyes before praying for his soul’s safe return during the show each week! The prayer goes something like this? In the name of the father, the son and the Holy Ghost. Amen Father, One of your children is asking for your help He is about to go and enter into a situation that the odds Are far more against him than for him One of your children is asking for your cleansing He is about to commit some acts that are against your will But its not entirely his fault For a demon that wakes up to do the bidding of Satan himself Is about to take over his body if only temporarily One of your children is asking for you for protection But not just for himself but for his significant other and A dear friend of his Your children are asking that you help protect their mortal souls And forgive them for any harm that they do to those whom disgrace Your teachings Father, I admit that I am not a perfect human being And that you didn’t intend for me to be the way that I am I live for your bidding and to fight Satan out of my body But I don’t know how much longer I’ll be able to do it For the demon that lives within me knows no boundaries Please protect me when I drift into my “dark side” For it may be the only way that I see Ami and my family again I live for your will despite what people believe But their Judgment I care nothing about For it is you O, Lord that allowed me to live the life that I have right now And for that I am eternally grateful I wish that people could understand the way that I walk and the friends that I keep But it is not their fault For the people that have misread me and my actions and for those whom feel That we need to be punished because of the way that we act Then they really don’t deserve to know you Lord I live for you and you alone Lord Almighty And I live to help Ami and my family live their lives without persecution For that Lord we shall become stronger Tonight Lord I must go out into the world with my family and we will be thinking of you As you died on the cross and then was resurrected 2 days later I love you O Lord and its with that we must close our connection for right now But know that when time comes we will speak again and I hope that nothing changes Over the time that it takes for us to connect again In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost Amen And with that Joshua blew out the candles and walked out of the room that he lived in as a “hermit” and with that for tonight we must leave you. Good Night’love ;)
