Getting Back in the Swing

3 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.03.04 Wardrobe BOSS Hugo Boss: Navy Blue Wool with Red Pinstripe Suit BOSS Hugo Boss: Navy Blue Cotton Shirt Johnston and Murphy: Black Polished Leather PVC Sole Lace-Ups with Nude Soles Lalique: Platinum Starter Watch with Black Mesh Band 'I don't care,' shouted Ken in a very serious tone, 'I have a job to do.' Bill Moresi, standing across from him had a somewhat worried look on his face as Ken walked gingerly to the bar set up in his office within his nightclub, The Elements. 'You should still be in the hospital,' began Bill calmly. 'I mean, look at you, Ken, you're having trouble walking from Point A to Point B. How do you expect to take on TUE?' Ken poured a nicely aged MacAllen scotch over a few cubes of ice and began to sip on it, mostly to take away from the pain. 'I'm fine, Bill,' confirmed Ken sharply. 'Besides, it's not like I've never worked through pain before.' Bill simply watched as Ken grimaced with each swallow of drink, not because the liqour was harsh. No, Ken always had the finest and smoothest cognacs there were, and kept his personal bar stocked with them. The look on Ken's face derived from the pain that shot through him even with the simple task of swallowing liquid. 'You were younger then, Ken,' said Bill as he began walking away from Ken and towards one of the room's two black Italian leather sofas. 'You're injury was fresher... and it was only your back, then. Not a troublesome knee, a bad neck, and a couple of herniated discs.' 'Look,' shouted Ken, slamming his now empty glass down on the bar, 'I'm fine, dammit! I can do this, Bill, and you fucking know that I can. I was off last week and will be looking at nearly two weeks worth of rest come Evolution on Monday. That's more than enough.' 'You were off last week because you were in the fucking hospital until an hour before the show started,' Moresi shouted back. The room became silent for a time, with the only sounds coming from Bill sitting back down on the sofa and the ice rattling in Ken's glass. KPJ's eyes wandered to the wall, where a picture hung of himself being held high by both Bill and Shawn as he displayed the XCWA Grappling God Championship proudly above his head just seconds after he had won it from Osiris. Those were easier times, nothing compared to this. His main concern then was Ace Harding, a bitter foe with more talent than could ever be estimated, and, while Harding wanted nothing more than to defeat Ken, murder wasn't on Ace's mind. 'Bill,' began Ken quietly, 'I have to do this. I have to go on with this tournament. I have to prove that I still have what it takes to be the best in the world. I have to prove that I'm not broken down, that I can still go. KPJ paused for a brief second and looked up at his friend. 'I have to figure out why Silent Wind was trying to kill me and who he was working for,' he continued. 'I have to prove that The Elite is still just that. And, I have to prove to Ice... who the better man is.' Bill simply looked back at Ken and nodded. He knew that there was no way to get hi to change his mind, and that the best thing that he could do for him was to be there for him when the time arose that he would need him. 'Okay,' confirmed Bill, 'I'm sorry. You know your body better than I do... hell, better than anyone does. If you say you're okay to go, then I won't argue anymore.' A smile etched across Ken's face before they were interrupted by a knock at the door. They both turned to look as the door opened, revealing a beautiful Gunner Addison clad in a lovely white dress with black trim by Helmut Lang that fit her body perfectly. She waved at Bill, who smiled as he waved back. Her eyes then met Ken's, whose smile had only gotten deeper and warmer. She smiled sweetly at him and began to walk his way, allowing the door to close behind her. As she made it over to him, she wrapped her arms around him, and the two embraced in a long romantic kiss. 'I guess I'll excuse myself,' said Bill under his breath, picking himself up from the couch. 'No,' said Ken as he pulled away gently from Gunner, who still had a smile on her face. 'Stay.' Ken's attention was redirected to Gun as Bill sat back down. She almost read his mind as she answered his questions without him even asking. 'I just wanted to see how you were doing today,' she began. ' I went to your apartment, and the doorman told me that you had come here with Bill.' 'Yeah,' admitted Ken. 'I guess all that time in the hospital made me realize how little attention I'd been paying to things here.' A somber look lurked across Gunner's face as Ken mentioned the hospital. 'You've been spending to much time in those lately,' she said. 'I worry so much about you.' 'I've been trying to get the same point across to him, Gun, but he's not listening,' said Bill. 'I thought we dropped this,' said a slightly perturbed Ken. 'I'm going through with this tournament and I'm going to meet Silent Wind again.' 'Absolutly,' said Gunner. 'I wouldn't think it would be any other way. I imagine that Ice is going to be in there down the road as well. Not to mention Showdown in Tokyo.' 'What,' asked Ken and Bill in unison with a slight tone of disbelief in their voices. 'What did you think,' Gunner continued, 'I was coming here to ask you to stop wrestling? I was going to demand that you not return this soon after your release from the hospital.' 'Well,' said Bill, 'to be honest... I kind of thought that was what you were implying.' 'Hell, no,' she confirmed as she turned her head to Ken. 'Baby, I think you should get back out there as soon as possible. You know you can do it... Bill knows you can do it... Josh knows you can do it... and I know you can do it.' The three of them stood up with a bit more vigor in their steps. 'Well,' announced Ken, ' I guess we have some business to tend to.'
