Going Hollywood: The Cast

19 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 19, 2005 Mike walked briskly up the Miramax studio lot as he searched for studio 22B. Today was the first cast meeting of the film and naturally he was fashionably late. It wasn’t his fault that he was late though, it had taken him an hour to find his lucky red shirt, and when he was about to leave his maid had seduced him. He finally found the studio and entered the building through the large doors. His footsteps echoed throughout the building as he searched for the group. Within seconds he found the rest of the cast gathered around Peter and Benjamin; the writer/director duo. Benjamin noticed Mike’s presence and began to speak. “Well, now that the rest of the cast is here we can begin,” Benjamin said an annoyed tone. Peter, the fat bald man with a ponytail and beard cleared his throat, “First of all I trust that you’re all familiar with the original show that this movie is based off of.” The four men that represented the cast nodded their heads. Benjamin picked up where Peter left off, “The movie will be identical to the show except for a few character changes. Looking through the script you’ll notice that the Mrs. Howell character has been removed and Mr. Howell has been re-written as a young millionaire playboy that will spend the majority of the movie flirting with Ginger and Mary-Anne. Also the Skipper and Professor characters will be much younger than they were in the show.” “Now,” Benjamin continued with a smug smile, “our cast includes quite a few stars.” Peter pointed out a tall man with a goofy look planted on his face, “I’m sure you’re all familiar with this man’s work on “Seinfeld” and “The Tick”. Patrick Warburton will play the role of the Skipper.” Warburton nodded his head in acknowledgement of the other men. Benjamin then pointed out the small scrawny man that stood beside Patrick, “And our title character, Gilligan, will be played by Jimmy Fallon.” Fallon waved at the rest of the cast. Peter took over again as he pointed to an awkward looking man with thick eyebrows, “The Professor will be played by Jason Lee.” Lee presented a nervous smile as he silently wondered what he had gotten himself into. Both the writer and director smiled broadly as they turned to Mike. “And the role of Mr. Howell goes to the next Global Wrestling World Heavyweight champion, Mike Biddle!” They both said in unison. “Hey,” Jason Lee broke in, “who is going to play Mary-Anne and Ginger” I’m not going to take a dual role here?” This comment caused both Peter and Benjamin to grimace. “Well,” Benjamin said with a wavering nervousness, “we can’t find anyone willing to play them right now?” “What the hell do you mean you can’t find someone to play the parts,” Fallon whined. “We have two actresses in mind but they haven’t returned our calls,” Peter answered. “Well who are they?” Warburton bellowed. “Deanna and Bobby,” Benjamin answered with a small snicker. “The porno stars?” Jason asked with an agitated tone. The three stars continued their argument with the writer and director. Mike listened for a few moments when he had finally had enough and he interrupted the “discussion”. “I know Deanna and Bobby,” Mike said with a smooth confidence, “and I could easily get them to agree to be in the movie by this time tomorrow.” “Can you really?” Benjamin asked with a desperate voice. “Of course I can,” Mike said, “I’m Mike Biddle for crying out loud.” “Okay then,” Peter said with a relieved voice, “we’ll all meet here again tomorrow afternoon and start going over the script.” ***** Mike knew both Deanna and Bobby from his frequent trips to the Playboy Mansion. They were party girls and the three of them had spent many adventurous nights together. Due to his experience with the pornographic duo it was easy for Mike to set up a date with the two. After a few drinks Mike and the girls returned to his mansion in Beverly Hills, or more specifically the large spa in his backyard. Deanna was a tall and lovely redhead, Bobby was a short and beautiful brunette, and the two of them looked perfect for the roles that Benjamin and Peter had wanted them to play. Mike sat between the two girls with his arms around their shoulders and a huge grin on his face. “Tell us more about how you’re going to be the next World Champion Mike,” Deanna said as she laid her head on Mike’s shoulder. Mike’s grin grew even wider; he had spent the entire evening discussing his favorite subject with these two women. “Oh I will in due time,” Mike began, “but now that we’re completely alone I want to discuss a business matter with you.” “You mean you want to be in a porno Mike?” Bobby giggled. Mike winked at her. “That’s actually pretty close to the sort of business that I want to discuss.” “Go ahead and spit it out Mike,” Bobby said as she kissed Mike on the cheek. Mike cleared his throat and began to speak, “You’ve both heard of Benjamin Johansen, right?” He asked. The mention of Benjamin’s name caused both girls to make a disgusted look. “Yeah we know him,” Deanna said, “he’s that greasy little fan boy that keeps asking us to be in some movie that he’s making.” “I bet it’s a fake movie too,” Bobby added. “How do you know him?” Deanna asked Mike. “I’m going to be in that movie that you mentioned,” Mike answered. “Let me guess,” Bobby said, “he asked you to get us to be in the movie huh?” Mike paused a moment to take a breath. “Actually,” he said, “it was my idea to ask you to play those parts. I thought it could lead to quite a bit of fun.” He finished his sentence with a sly smile. “You mean we could do stuff like what we’re doing now every night?” Deanna asked. “Exactly,” Mike replied. The two women looked at each other and then at Mike and then at each other again. It didn’t take long for them to reach a verdict. “We’ll do it just as long as that creepy greasy guy stays away from us,” Bobby told Mike. “Good,” Mike said as he pulled the girls closer to himself, “there’s a cast meeting tomorrow afternoon at the studio. You two can stay the night here and we’ll go to the meeting together.” “I like the sound of that,” Deanna said. The two women scooted in closer to Mike and both lay their heads on his chest. A little bit of dirty talk began, followed by some unmentionable touching. Suddenly a loud shriek was heard accompanied by a sob. The noise interrupted Mike and the two women. “Can you go see what that noise was?” Bobby asked Mike with a frightened voice. Mike carefully stepped out of the spa and into the cool night air. He slipped on his sandals and made his way towards the direction of the loud sobbing. After two minutes what he found brought him great annoyance. On the ground, curled up into the fetal position was Hillary Watson. Mike nudged her with his foot. “What the hell are you doing here?” He asked with a stern voice. Hillary took a few deep breaths and then answered, “I-I came h-here to s-see y-you,” she wailed. “I didn’t invite you,” Mike said as he crossed his arms. “I w-wanted to s-surprise y-you,” Hillary said. Mike knelt down beside her. “Let me make myself clearer then,” he said with an agitated voice, “you’re not welcome here. Now get off my property before I call the police.” Hillary shot up, her tear stained eyes looked directly at Mike. “I-I thought you l-loved me,” she said with a scared voice. Mike laughed at her stupidity. “Hillary if I loved you I wouldn’t have left you at the alter last year. You’re nothing more than a semi-decent piece of ass to me,” he said, “Now get the hell out of here. You’re not worthy of my presence.” Tears streamed down Hillary’s nymph-like face as she ran off into the night. Mike rolled his eyes in annoyance and made his way back to Deanna and Bobby. -To Be Continued-
