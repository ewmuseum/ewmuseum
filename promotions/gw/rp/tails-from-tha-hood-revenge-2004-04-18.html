Tails From Tha Hood: Revenge Jay Quickness Global Wrestling Promo #1184 Date: 4/18/04 Location: Bronx/Roof top Re-Venge (ri-venj') n. 1. The act of returning injury for injury to obtain satisfaction. 2 A means of avenging oneself or others. 3 The desire for vengence. The scene opens up with Jay Quickness chilling ontop of his roof with one of his best friend JBlood. JBlood is leaning against the side of the roof rolling up a blunt. Jay Quickness see's his red and black duffle bag and grabs it from the floor. He opens it and pulls out a brand new 9mm. Jay Quickness looks at JBlood and they both smile. Jay Quickness reaches into the bag again and pulls out a clip. He threws the bag in the corner. Jay Quickness places the Clip into the 9mm. and cocks it back. ...Click...Click... JBlood begins to nod his head head, He lights up the blunt that he just finished rolling. Jay Quickness sit's down on the roof top with his feet hanging and so does JBlood. JBlood passes the blunt to Jay Quickness and they both begin to talk to each other. 'So you just got that peace today?' 'Actually, it belongs to one of my otha homies. He let me hold it, I told him I got some beef wit some MOB cat's. But I got something in store fo' them. Im not gonna let what they did to me go by, they jumped me like 2 litte pussy's, you seen what happen to your boy TRAIN he got his shit cracked, and I left him right in the middle of that set in a pool of his own blood. You dont now how good that made me feel.' 'Yeah, that was a nice shot you gave him, I bet he aint see that coming. He was just lucky that you aint decide to stomp his ass out. Cause if it would of been me Jay it would of went down different. I would of went all out.' 'He got what was coming to him, but at the moment I have someone else who is on my shitty side and that's Micheal Greensino. He is tha one that I really want to get my hands on. Just wait and do time It will happen.' 'Yo, Jay how did you get into this beef with these MOB cat's. I just want you to now that what eva goes down I got your back fo' what eva goes down. I got you.' 'Yea! no doubt, let's just say they tried to give me a offer I couldnt refuse.' 'And what did you do?' ...BANG....BANG......BANG... Jay Quickness let's 3 shot's out of his glock. Jay Quickness ducks and so does JBlood cause they knew that someone was gonna look ova. They decide to chage the way they were sitting so that now one could spot them. The both sit with there back against the wall and begin to speak again. 'I refused! They cant take me down it will take more then a MOB to take HOOD HEFNER down. I been threw worst, this is light right here trust me I am gonna be taking care of all this pretty soon and I will be on top.' 'Yo, can I see that gun? Let me bust off a couple of shot's up in this bitch.' Jay Quickness passes the gun to JBlood, he stands up and looks around carefully to make sure no one is trying to be noisy. JBlood walks close to the edge of the roof top and looks over to the street's. He see's car's stoping at red lights and, cars going while green. but something stands out and it's a black impala with two dressed officers. 'Yo Jay come ova here check this out.' 'Aight.' ...BANG....BANG......BANG... JBlood fires 3 shots at the black impala both men quickly come out of the car with flashlights and gun drawn out looking at the roof tops. Jay and JBlood hide behind a wall until the officers get back into there car and hit the sirens and blare up the street. 'Yo, are you crazy Blood. Give me my damn shit back nigga.' 'Aight my fault. I got excited. 'You is crazy.' 'Thats what my moms say.' 'Im starting to believe her. Yo let's get out of here before they come back. Jay Quickness put's the gun back into the bag while JBlood picks up the shells from the roof top floor. The camera fades to black and goes to a GW commercial. -Scene ends
