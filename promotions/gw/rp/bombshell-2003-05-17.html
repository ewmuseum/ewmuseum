Bombshell

17 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/17/03 Brick rushed out of his apartment and to his car as fast as he could. The phone call he had just gotten from Monica was just that alarming. She sounded frantic and could barely talk. It was just like the call he got the night he helped her leave her ex-boyfriend Cedric. Recently, Brick’s enemy Salaok had taunted Brick during a match by insinuating that he had secretly attended Monica’s wedding on New Year’s. Other than himself, Kyle and Monica were the most worried. Kyle had probably even considered going after the Nevermore himself. Brick had talked him out of it. Finally, Brick arrives. He runs right up to the door and presses the doorbell. Seconds later, Monica opens the door and rushes up to him. “Oh, thank God it’s you.” “I got here as soon as I could. What’s up?” “Come into the living room.” The screen takes a few seconds to warm up and start showing a clear picture, but when it does, it shows what looks to be a home video of a wedding. But this isn't just any old wedding as we see Brick with his family waiting at the front of the church for his sister to arrive. And then she does so. Out of a white old fashion limo she steps and her dress trails along after her. She is truly beautiful and she slowly makes her way up the steps out the front of the entrance to Brick who wraps his arms around her and gives her a huge hug. A voice comes from behind the camera and its tone gives it away straight off. Brick immediately rises to his feet 'Oh Brick, good to see that you can still relax and have fun. Your sister does look nice today. Such a shame that she will not look so......well, it could be put down as a shame that she will not look like this tonight.' The scene cuts to a little later on that day and everyone is in the church and seated, part from the important people up the front. Salaok, who holds the camera, pans it around the room, making sure to get everyone on film before turning it around to look at him. “Stunning day, isn't it, Brick” I went to such trouble to get here today. I brushed my hair, cleaned my teeth, oh, and I had to bribe my way in here. Seems as though you had a security company hired to make sure I don't turn up. Well, Brick my boy, it is always best to make sure that the company you are hiring doesn't actually work for me. If you had, I would have had to pay a lot more than what I did today.” Salaok grins before turning the camera back around to the wedding ramblings at the front. Salaok’s voice comes from behind the camera again. “You see, Brick, it doesn't matter what you do. I can and will always be able to find you. Always be able to get at you. And you know what's so much better than all of that” I now have every single one of your family and friends on tape in order to make my identifying of people easier. I know what they all look like, Brick. The only question is, what on Earth would I do to them?” The camera stays on the wedding events up the front of the church and then suddenly cuts to darkness. Slowly, the camera starts to get light at the stop of the screen as it moves up on what looks to be a window. A bush is moved to the side by a hand behind the camera and we see Brick's sister and her new husband retiring to bed and beginning to take off their clothes. Angered by what he has just seen, Brick storms over to the DVD player and presses Eject. “He was there! The son of a bitch was there!” roared “He was watching us, Brick! All of us!” “I’ll kill him!” Brick growls. “He was watching us get undressed! Who knows what else he was watching us do?” Brick grabs his sister and pulls her close to him. “Listen to me,” he says. “I’m gonna deal with Salaok and the rest of his little bitches. He ain’t getting away with this. Just like I promised Dad, I’m gonna protect this family, whatever it takes.” He says those last words with a grim determination and conviction. Knowing Brick’s mindset, he means it when he says “whatever it takes”.
