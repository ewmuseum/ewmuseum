The request

26 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26/04 Bubba McCoy spent one year of his life in forced captivity. No TV, no music, no newspapers, no windows, no human contact. That whole year, Christina McCoy often worried about what whoever was behind her father's disappearance might have been doing to him. Last month, Bubba made his return to Global Wrestling at High Noon. Although now, their relationship was one with its share of tension. 'Chris, I still say it's a mistake to put Maverick Reno and Josh on the same team. It's gonna explode.' 'Look, Dad, how many times do I have to say it? I'm not taking Maverick out.' 'Those two get into it at Crossfire, we could lose. I'd almost swear that's what you want.' 'What? Don't be...' 'Excuse me, but you can't go in there.' Just then, Brick burst into the office. 'We need to talk.' 'What is it, Brick? Dad and I are in the middle of something.' 'What it is, Christina, is I want to know why the hell I'm still wrestling against Umbrage in just a standard match at Crossfire!' 'Look, Brick,' said Bubba, 'we went over this before. We got enough hardcore matches on the card as it is. We got the Choose Your Own Fate match with Donny and Noah Hanson. We got the main event, the Wargames match between Team GW an' Team WMW, which I'm tryin' to get my daughter to change...' 'Never mind all that! I need to make an impact! I need to send a message! I won't be able to do that in a standard match!' 'I thought we'd settled this. What happened?' 'What happened?' Brick then pulled out a videocassette and placed it in the TV/VCR on Christina's desk. The tape was cued to two recent interviews Umbrage of Wrestling Midwest had made. The first was an appearance he made on the Benny Tyler show. Benny ? Those were some good matches, Umbrage. Quite bloody too. When then leads us to Crossfire, the pay-per-view that is Wrestling Midwest versus Global Wrestling, and one of the matches on the card is Umbrage versus Brick. Umbrage ? That it is, Benny. Benny ? Brick has gone on record as saying that all you have done is run your mouth and that you are a lamb running to the slaughter. Any comments? [Umbrage leans forward in the chair.] Umbrage ? I sure do Benny. Brick says I’ve been running my mouth? Well, that’s fine. I have been. But I make sure that I can back up every word I say. I’ve been watching the tapes, and I see what kind of game he’s bringing. It’s like Candy Land to the NBA finals. No comparison. Yeah, he complained about my match at Blood Factory. That’s fine. So did I. Horrid match. One for the dark matches. But I say Brick, watch the tapes. Go back a little bit farther. I mean, come on. Everyone has a rough spot in their career. Everyone has a bad match. Have you seen your highlight reel? It’s like the Incredible Crash Dummies got a wrestling ring? But with less violence. [Umbrage stares right into the camera, as sparks seem to emit from his eyes.] Umbrage ? Brick, I’ve been asking for a hardcore match the entire time. Bring your ladders, tables and chairs. Bring the plate glass and the beds of nails. Bring the barbed wire baseball bats. Bring whatever you want. I’m ready to step into the ring with you and as of this moment, we really don’t have much longer to wait. Umbrage versus Brick, and it’s all going for the sky. I plan on raising the bar and dragging you crippled black ass the entire way. You want to try and shut me up? Then don’t pussy foot around at Crossfire. Come into the ring with you’re “A” game and prove you have the true skills to get in the ring with me. Because when I’m done with you, you are going to wish you stayed at home in whatever dank corner of the ghetto you crawled out of in the first place. Brick then began to fast forward the tape and cue it to an interview with WMW reporter Steven Squires. Squires - Umbrage, you travelled all this way to come to Crossfire to face GW's hardcore legend, Brick. What do you think about the match? Any thoughts? [Umbrage shakes his head.] Umbrage - You are asking me that? Damn, Sqeaks, couldn't you think up something a bit less generic? BUt fine, I'll answer. What do I think about this match with Brick... Well, Umbrage versus Brick in a standard match? Something tells me Brick isn't pushing too hard for this to become a hardcore match. I've alread ysaid publically and to various other people on my end that this match should become a hardcore match, but for some reason, it hasn't been signed that way... Well, Brick, why the hell not? Maybe it isn't your choice. Maybe Global Wrestling wants to keep their little hardcore guy in one piece for their shows. Maybe this will just be a standard match. Maybe this will stay just a test of standard wrestling moves... [Umbrage whips his head to his right, causing his neck to crack qite loudly.] Umbrage - But I doubt it. You and I both know that it's going to escalate, and I say, good. Great. Let's go for the gusto. Let's make sure our match is the one the fans are truly looking forward to. Let's make sure than when the highlight reel rolls on WMW TV and GW TV, that they won't be talking about the five on five, or the four on two... Just Umbrage versus Brick. [Suires waits for a moment to see if Umbrage is done, then slightly nods as he asks his next question.] Squires - What do you think of Global Wrestling as a whole? [Umbrage laughs.] Umbrage - Ass-clowns. All of them. THey showed some potential when Epitome jumped ship, but really, that guy isn't worth the time. None of their workers are. All the guys who seem to show potential seem to burn out right before they might make a breakthrough. It's pretty sad for an entire fed of losers like GW have to end up that way. Squires - And what comments to you have to say about Brick possibly making a breakthrough with your match at Crossfire? [Squires sinces as if he's about to be hit and takes a step back but Umbrage grabs Squires' mic hand and pulls him back into frame.] Umbrage - Brick make a breakthrough against me? He's going to actually beat me? He must be dreaming. You see, I plan on backing up these claims. I plan on making sue that Brick never gets to hit the Bricklayer, and if by some slim change he manages to, I WILL kick out. What he won't be able to kick out of is the Riot Act, and that will put him to the mat for the one, two, three. Mark. My. Words. [Steven nods, then asks another question.] Squires - Are you expecting some outside involvment in your match at Crossfire? [Umbrage gives a weird look to Squires.] Umbrage - What kind of question is that, Squires? If Brick needs to be a pussy and get outside help to beat me, that's fine. I say bring it. I'll drop anyone who wants to interfere, Global Wrestling or Wrestling Midwest. I don't need any help beating Brick, and if he's a real man, he won't welcome any help either. 'Brick, listen...' 'Brick, listen, my ass, Christina! I won't kick out of the Riot Act? That fuckin' weak-ass spinebuster he does?! You know how many spinebusters I've kicked out of? He thinks I'm not gonna be able to kick out of that? Crash test Dummies in a wrestling ring?! The list of cats I've put out reads like a motherfucking phonebook! ' 'Calm down.' 'No! I'm tired of these punk motherfuckers from WMW runnin' their mouths! And I'm tired of this 'standard match' bullshit! I want Umbrage in a hardcore match at Crossfire! I don't give a damn what you have to do to get it done, but get it done!' 'Look, Brick...' 'Better yet, I want Umbrage...in the Pit!' Bubba and Christina then looked at each other and back at Brick. 'Brick, are you crazy? You already had a match in the Pit this year!' 'That was six months ago.' 'Brick, please reconsider. The Pit is the most brutal match we've got.' Christina wasn't speaking in hyperbole. The Pit truly was an excruciating ordeal. It was a fifteen-foot deep hole with concrete walls and an iron floor. The only way out was two steel doors which could only be opened and closed by a remote computer, which also dropped weapons at random into the Pit. The only way to win the Pit was by submission or knockout. Brick has won each match in the Pit that he's been in, and the first time he competed in the Pit, he won the World title. 'If you won't give me a match in the Pit, at least 'Well, Brick, maybe you should have requested this match in advance instead of, oh, a few days before the actual event!' 'That's enough, Christina! Look, Brick, I'll think it over, but I can't promise ya anything.' Brick glared at Bubba and then glared at Christina. 'Fine,' he growled, and then stormed out of the office. 'That went well,' said Christina. Bubba sighed. 'What's wrong, Dad?' 'I just got a feeling. No matter what type o' match we make it, Brick is determined to hurt somebody.'
