Going backstage

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.01.03 “Punk motherfucker!” Brick growled as he stormed backstage, Christina McCoy walking alongside him and a number of security guards following them both. “Calm down, Brick.” “Calm down my ass, Christina! Who the hell does UK think he is?! And I don’t know what the hell Ice’s problem was! I wasn’t aiming for him! Then he wants to go and slap me like I’m some little bitch?! Oh, hell no!!!” “Look, you’ve had a bad night. I think you just need to relax.” “What I need is to whup UK’s ass some more, especially for that ‘baboon’ shit.” “What you need is to calm down.” Needless to say, it was not one of Brick’s best nights. * * * He had originally planned to go out to the ring and screw Deacon Holmes out of the Primestar title. Unfortunately, as the old saying goes, “the best laid plans, etc.” Trying to lay Holmes out with the World title belt, but hit Ice instead. Already, there was interference in the match from Holmes’ fellow T.U.E. member John Zero and an attempted save by Kid Flash, who got a beating from the TUE bodyguards for his troubles. After Holmes, Zero and the bodyguards took their leave, he tried to make amends with Ice, but he was met with a slap to the face. Of course, Brick is not someone to take disrespect like this lightly. His interpretation of “turn the other cheek” has, more often than not, been to turn the other person’s cheek. And this is what he had planned to do to Ice after he shoved him into the turnbuckle. Unfortunately, a sneak attack from behind stopped his plans short. Someone from the crowd had entered the ring with a steel chair and hit Brick in the back and in the head. Ice had started to kick Brick when he was down, only to receive a chair shot from the mysterious attacker. The assailant turned out to be the returning Mr. UK. Mr. UK is known for his month-long stint in GW during which he became World champion a month after winning the Clash of the Titans battle royal. Mr. UK had gone on to berate the champion, saying that the title was soiled, that it had fallen from grace since Brick won it. This prompted Christina McCoy to offer Mr. UK a contract and the chance to earn a shot at Brick’s world title. Naturally, UK accepted the chance and for his troubles, got a right uppercut and a Bricklayer, leaving him unconscious. * * * “You know,” said Christina, “maybe what you need is to head down to Dark Horse.” “Christina, I don’t need a drink.” “But you do need to simmer down.” “Fine,” Brick sighed reluctantly. “I got nothing else to do.” “I’m glad you see things my way.” “What makes you think I see things your way? I just think I sent UK enough of a message tonight.” “Even if you didn’t get to send Deacon Holmes a message.” Just then, Ely Braxton walked into the office. He had some bandages on his head as a sign of his recent Overload match with Ace Cash. “You wanted to see me, darlin’?” Braxton asked in his deep Texas drawl. “I did, yeah. Brick needs to unwind some. I was hoping you could take down to the saloon for a while.” “I don’t need a babysitter, Christina.” “Don’t think o’ me as a babysitter, Brick. Think o’ me as a confidant. Come on.” Brick and Braxton walked out the door. Afterwards, Christina pressed a button on her intercom. “Roberta, when Mr. UK comes to, have him come to my office.” “Yes, Ms. McCoy.”
