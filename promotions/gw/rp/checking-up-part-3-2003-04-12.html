Checking up Part 3

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/03 It was late in the afternoon when Brick got to his apartment. He had just gotten through checking in with his friends and family, all of which have felt varying degrees of dread after the recent Evolution, because Salaok used them all to taunt Brick during the main event. Aaron and Felicia were upset. For Felicia, this emotional turmoil was dangerous, considering she was 6 months pregnant. Kyle and Monica were upset to the point where Kyle was considering buying a gun and perhaps even going after the Nevermore himself. Brick spent a lot of energy, trying to convince Kyle to stay out of it and let Brick handle the Nevermore. He still wasn’t sure if any of his words got to Kyle. It seemed that ever since Brick saw him coming out of that coffee shop with an old girlfriend, Kyle was determined to prove to Brick that he was committed to Monica. This scared Brick, as he knew, in all likelihood, his baby sister might become widow before her time. Now he was booked in a match with the returning Chris Camero for this week’s upcoming Evolution. Camero was revealed to have allied himself with Salaok and the Nevermore. Considering that the Nevermore numbered at ten, while Brick’s only allies were Ely Braxton and Jesse Briggs, it meant that they would have to fight harder. But Camero was an obstacle. One he was more than ready to overcome, and, if necessary, in the most brutal way possible. He remembered Camero’s words as his image appeared on the Globaltron. “You know, Bubba was an idiot for releasing me. But I found someone who would hire me on my terms and someone who wants the best for this federation and it's fans.” “Best for the federation and its fans, my ass!” Brick growled as he walked over to his answering machine. He checked to see if there were any calls while he was out. The flashing red light told him yes. He then pressed the play button. “Five new messages,” intoned an electronic voice. “Brick, it’s Monica. Give me a call when you get this message.” BEEP! “Would you be interested in subscribing to?” Right away, Brick pressed the erase button. He hated the telemarketers with a passion. BEEP! “Brick, it’s Jesse. I think we got trouble. Call me when you get this.” BEEP! “Brick, it’s Jesse! Where the hell are you”!” This call was just coming in. Brick immediately picked up the phone. “Jesse?” “Brick?” “Yeah.” “Where the hell have you been?” “I had to check up on Aaron and my family. I just got in and heard the message. What’s up?” “There was a fire at the saloon.” “What?” “Yeah, but we got it under control.” “Anybody hurt?” “Donovan got beat up pretty badly. Silent Wind was there.” “What” Did he do it?” “No, but I think we both know who did.” “Salaok.” “Or Mani Shigeru. Anyway, Braxton wants you to meet him at the airport Saturday at ten o’clock A.M.” “Did he say why?” “No, he just said he was going “back to his roots”. “So?” “So, Braxton was a soldier. He was in Delta Force.” “Get out!” “I’m serious. Look, I better go. He wants me to look after the McCoys.” “Yeah, I’ll head right out. I was planning on going hunting, anyway. I just need to get a hold of some gear. See you in Texas.” Brick hung up the phone. He then went to get his coat when the phone began to ring. “Hello?” “Brick, it’s me, Braxton.” “Hey, man. I just talked to Briggs. I was just headed out to the door to meet you.” “Well, you better hurry the hell up. It’s important.” “What is it?” “Get some gear and some protection. You and me, we’re going for a raid on Omni Tec.” “Are you serious?” “Deadly serious. We need to tell them sumbitches if they want a war, we’ll give “em one.” “WLook, I ain’t no soldier, man. I don’t know a damn thing about?” “Look, Brick. I ain’t gonna force ya into this. But I figured you might want to stick it to Salaok, and I need somebody to watch my back, and I damn sure don’t trust Silent Wind.” “Good point. OK, I’m in.” “Good. Hurry up.” Brick then hung up the phone and headed out the door. He then dialed a number on his cell phone. “Mom, it’s Jamal. I’m headed out of town. Yeah, I got some business to take care of. I’ll call you when I can. OK, bye.” Brick hung up the phone and headed for the elevator. In his heart and mind, he knew one unalienable truth. The war was just beginning.
