Date: 100904 The men circled us, at least fifteen of them, their guns clicking, safeties coming off. That’s far enough, gentlemen. Damn. I hate bad information? “especially if it comes from a reliable source. That means one of a few things. Either the source was setting me up” “or they really didn’t have a clue. Drop your weapons. I looked over at Zero and shrugged. He dropped his guns. I dropped mine one at a time, using my left hand only. In my right, I kept the pack firmly in my grasp. Drop the bag. I can’t. Why” Its on a pressure switch now. If I drop it’kaboom. O’riley, go retrieve the bag. A man stepped forward, O’riley I assumed. He came towards me, his assault rifle held casually at his side. O’riley held his free arm out to me. I tried to sound as serious as I could. Whatever you do, don’t drop it or sit it down or anything, okay? He slowly nodded, a small wave of fear passing behind his eyes. Sucker. I grinned under the mask. Catch. I threw the pack at him, his eyes growing wide? “everyone gasped” “a few people looked away” “the pack fumbled around in his arms” “I lurched forward” “the pack steadied in his arms” “O’riley gave a triumphal shout” “right before my hands grabbed his head and jerked hard to the left and then right, snapping his neck” “O’riley’s body began to fall” “his friends started to react” “but I acted quicker, snatching up the assault rifle out of his limp hand. I saw Zero drop to the ground as I did a complete circle, spraying the entire contents of the magazine out, trying to take out as many of the men as I could. I kept the barrel pointed slightly downward, adjusting for the jumping of the gun. Rat-a-tat-tat-tat” ...click? Fuck. Bodies dropped, thudding and thumping about. Men cried? “groaned” “moaned” “bled” “oh my god’d” “pleaded” “begged” “while others” “well, they just got slightly more pissed off. Those that could stand, as well as raise a gun, took aim on a new target” “Me. The noise though” It was something new to the air, to the battle. It sounded more like a light cough. There were several of them. And then more bodies started to fall. Not that there were that many left. I looked down and saw Zero, laying on his back? “silenced pistol in each hand” “spinning on the ground” “shooting at anything that moved. He looked like a breakdancer hooked on death. The last body fell. Zero still lay on his back. He ejected the two clips and loaded fresh ones in, chambering the first round on each gun. Sighing, he laid his head back down on the ground. What did you say earlier” About what? One or two guards at the door. A possible third wondering the halls. Yeah. So? I don’t know about you, but this smells like a trap to me. You get that feeling too, huh? Yeah. It makes me feel all warm and fuzzy and exceptionally pissed off. I think we may have to make another stop tonight. You up for it? Yeah. Let’s go. ***** The evening stood still. The early morning birds were starting to chirp. The sun was turning the horizon into a dusty mixture of reds and oranges. In the distance, the building sat? “Squat, unobtrusive”. “a landmark of the past and a haunted house of the future” “until the reddish-blue flames started erupting out any opening of the building” “the walls started to vibrate until they gave to the pressure” “the metal walls exploded outwards, the material breaking into thousands upon thousands of shards. I looked into the chaos, the destruction, the mayhem” “and felt peace. Beautiful.
