Turning the other cheek...

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/18/04 There are times in a man’s life when things take their toll on you, when situations go beyond your control. It’s almost as if there is some sort of chaos theory when it comes to life’s ordeals. White Rose decided now was time to make me pay back the loan from a long time ago to help save my Mother’s farm. I challenged them to make me pay it back basically. They want one million dollars for a quarter million dollar loan. Even in today’s economy it doesn’t add up. In fact, it’s actually illegal. Technically speaking there was nothing I did wrong by getting the loan, it’s just that I didn’t get it through a real bank. Apparently White Rose is basically the bank. I almost question myself now as to why I said two million dollars if I lose the match. Lets face a fact though, I have confidence in what I do, and how this is all going to turn out. I have a tag partner ready to go, but his identity will not be known until the Overload before Wrestlecade. The rumors are swirling on the message boards on the internet as to who my partner is, but I will give no clue as to who it is. Monday night after my interview with Steven Dinkler, some egotistical assholes from WMW decided now was there time to make themselves known in OUR federation. They came into the ring and attacked me, outnumbering me and making me bleed. They tried to disgrace me, the family name of McNasty and the Irish ancestors of this country by pouring a breakfast cereal on me. If that didn’t make matters worse I was attacked again but this time by White Rose. Why? To give me a response they say. This shit is starting to get deep. My mind was already not there as it was Monday night, I have a lawsuit on my hands that I have to deal with. It’s a shame in this country of great opportunity and freedoms that someone like me can’t have a little bit of good clean fun. I just can’t believe those midgets want to take me on in the court of law, you know what I say, I say fucking bring it. I will continue to have Leprechauns as a part of who I am. Vertically Challenged People are People Too will just have to deal with. My God I am employing these midgets to work for me, for good money too. What helps me through these situations is something my Mother always told me. Turn the other cheek. If you turn the other cheek and they hit you again, it’s time to strike back. I think right now is a situation to do so. White Rose, WMW’s Noah Hanson and Vertically Challenged People are People Too will soon know what it’s like to make me bleed and make me build up a rage. I almost pity who ever gets in my way, my rage isn’t something to be dealt with easily. When I have my rage, I have no more sense of right and wrong, everything seems like a good idea. I have stitches on my head that will heal and I may show no marks of having them, and I may eventually forgive what was done to me, but I will not forget what they have done. I have turned the other cheek one too many times in my life and I will not turn my cheek to those who assault me, and make personal attacks anymore. Call it aggressive, call it psychotic, but I call it protecting my ass. After all aren’t we all out for ourselves? I had a talk with Hardcore Harv earlier tonight about all of these events. He asked me what the hell was this loan business about. I wasn’t going to lie to him, I needed to do what I could to help my Mother out. After saving my own ass, my family comes next in line. I know it sounds selfish, but that’s just the way it is. Harv looked at me as if I was crazy though, two million dollars is an awful lot of money. I had to reassure him that I had it all worked out. He wanted to know who my tag team partner was going to be in order to take care of White Rose at Wrestlecade, as much as Harv is one of my best friends, I couldn’t tell him who it was. I just told that he will know next Thursday night on Overload. I made special arrangements with the Global Wrestling production team to do a live satellite feed from the Drunken Leprechaun. To make things worse for me, I had to deal with the likes of WMW, Noah Hanson and his pussy buddies decided to make a sneak attack on me. I said before they attacked who I was and what I stood for. It’s time for payback. And with me payback truly is a bitch. I have few things planned for their punk asses. If they want to mess with me and try to make an example out of me on how they view Global Wrestling, then I will make an example of them on how I view WMW. I may not get a long with everyone backstage at Global Wrestling, but to me we’re a family even though some of us hate each other, if WMW wants to step forward and start a war, so be it. We’re ready. It’s been such a long night for me. I am sitting here in the Altoona Police Department, Why? You may ask. Well, just as me and Harv were cleaning up for the night there was a loud explosion out front. It was about 2:45am, everyone was gone, it was kind of a slow night and as I said, BOOM! The windows were blown out and the bar just shook. Me and Harv ran out there as fast we could and there it was, it was MY CAR, and I MEAN MY FUCKING CAR was on fire. Someone who is out to get me blew up my car. Harv just so happened to look down on the ground in front of the front door to the bar, and there it was a White fucking Rose. I guess there was no trying to figure out who did it. White Rose was blatant enough to let me know it was them, as if the beating they gave me after I was already weakened and in the not best of minds was enough, they had to destroy my car. As I said, you can only turn your cheek so many times. I have turned my cheek one too many times, and now it’s time to strike back. The time will come when I do so. White Rose, Noah Hanson and his crew and Vertically Challenged People are People Too, are about to see how and why I mean business?
