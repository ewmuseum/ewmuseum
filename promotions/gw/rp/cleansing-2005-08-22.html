Cleansing...

22 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/22/05 In the background, the sound of Danielle's ring music plays loudly the words seem to burn in her mind even more today then they did the first time she ever heard them. Thank-you for the lies, the lies you sold us. Thank-you for this vision of violence. Thank-you for this song you gave me to sing. Thank-you for the capping day of a culture that lay dying. Yes it's us dying. Maybe it won't be long, now that it's all gone on. It's all gone wrong and there's blood on our hands. She lays there, face down on her bed, her face at the foot, thinking. Her journal laid out in front of her as she dips her pen into the inkwell beside her. She takes the quill to the pages again, filling in her thoughts and other things... her sanity check for the days as they go by. We saw them lying there with their arms around each other, staring through their fear and the miles between our heads. You could have told the truth. It was your decision to sacrifice integrity and compassion for greed. Now it's all gone wrong and there's blood on our hands. Geoff Tate, of Queenryche, saying things she would love to be able to say to certain type of people that she has dealt with in the hopefully soon forgotten past. Those from her past that still call her a “Exhibitionist Pocahontas” and such. Those who are jealous of what she is and has become. A beautiful woman who cares for who she is and what she is. She knows she has a body that can sell things, and she's not afraid of others to show it to them. It's almost like she grew up in a different country, if not a different planet then the outside. All these uptight people who think that a woman's body is should be hidden from view and it's shameful. All of these people think that she should be ashamed of herself. For what? Because she cares not what you think? And the equating to Pocahontas is a bit rough. While it should be an honor to be equated to a woman who was a great person for her people, the Powhatan... what she later became was nothing to be proud of. Disregard Disney and it's retelling. In the end, Pocahontas was kidnapped from her people, taken to England and married off as a trophy wife to some minor nobleman. She was forced to become English, loosing her name and her traditions, her true self and all what was left was an Indian Shell with a hollow inside. Her soul, her spirit, was destroyed. If you were to equate Dani with her, you must know this... there isn't anything that you can do to break her of her spirit. She's got a blood line that can't be held down. Pocahontas was a princess, daughter of a great chief. But she was not the daughter that came from a line from... ... A Legend. She stops writing, and then puts the quill down into it's holder. She lifts the book slightly and blows gently across the sheet in a feeble attempt to get the ink to dry quicker. As she does she glances her writing to check it over. Journal Date: July 19, 2005 If you took a word to describe me it would be loser right now. I cant win a match to save my life. I knew I should of never wrestled in Medicine Hat, it was cursed and the curse carries on. That other federation was stupid to hold their event there instead of in the larger city only an hour away. Then again, if you look at it, they're feeling the curse a bit too. In the morning, I go to a sweat lodge, to cleanse my body and mind. My grandfather was nice enough to hook me up with a Sweat Leader to guide me through the cleansing. My fasting begins tonight, at sun down, and I will not eat again until the dinner after the Sweat is finished. I have to fix things and fix them quickly. I invited Gray and Josh to join me. I know Gray most likely will, but Josh being more of a Modern person prefers his sweating done in the local gym. All fine and dandy, but he has no grasp at the traditions involved in this all. But... I have to give it to him. He has been helping build the lodge with Gray. Lately, even small things amaze me about him: he can be the most annoying little boy in the world, then turn around and be the sweetest gentlemen. I don't think I will ever understand him. But he says he has a question for me but is waiting to ask it. Wonder if he needs me to set him up with someone again. Like I use to in school. Although, I don't know if I could listen to one of my friends again, having to listen to them about how he can grate on their nerves. I still think of the night of my dance his voice carried over the drum and the other singers, strong forceful, willing me on to dance harder. I made one mistake in my dance I think. Gray was the only one to notice it, but to his credit he didn't say a word about it. Looking back, the tour with my last fed was good... until Medicine Hat, Canada. The site of a battle that ended up bearing a curse for both sides. One of those sides was my own ancestors. It was bad for me to break their will and engage in a fight there. Now it is time to shed my ill feeling of them and the dirt that stains my soul about them. It is time to wash my body of that other federation. After tomorrow, they will just be a memory. I hold contempt and hatred for them, and that's holding me back. Global is my path now, and here is where my focus needs to be. This week I know I have a match, but with whom yet I am not sure. I am still waiting for Kris to show up with the paper work on that. But he isn't going to be here until tomorrow, after I get out of the sweat ceremony, so I will be focused on what is at this time more important. Danielle puts the book down and then moves to a seated position on the bed. She looks to her side, picking up a small leather bikini, she just smiles. Out of the respect of the Sweat Leader, and the others, she will be wearing it tomorrow, and the men a loin cloth. Normally, tradition would dictate that she would wear nothing. Freeing their bodies of the human confines of clothes to be closer to mother earth. This is a rebirth of the soul, the body is cleansed of impurities, and the mind is set free. This would not be Danielle's first, when she came to live with grandfather at the age of 10, he insisted on her taking part in one to cleanse herself of the hate for the nuns of the orphanage. It worked at least to a small point. There was no secret she still didn't trust the 'white' skins. after the lies she had been told for most of her young life. There wouldn't be enough sweats in the world to cleanse her of that. But she at least learned to tolerate them. After all she is better then they are, she doesn't need to lie about things and hide behind supposed purity that is only used to control people in their world. That's what they feared most about Dani, she was nothing but a free spirit and there was no way they would ever control her. Like the mustangs of the great plains, a few would fall, but there was always that one horse that would never have its spirit broken, no matter how hard you tried. Dani looks out the window of her hotel suite at the Mandalay Bay, the sun is setting and the sky is on fire, it is time for the fasting to begin she will not see nor speak to someone until morning when the ladies come to get her for the sweat. Then there will be no speaking still just relaxing quiet to keep her mind focused on what this is all for. Dani puts the Bikini back down on the bottom of her bed, tomorrow will be a long day as she slides under the blanket, on her pillow is the teddy bear that was given to her by either Kris or Gray, no one has ever said anything to her about it. A small teddy bear wearing a deer skin costume, normally held in a wooden, had woven, basket. Like a papoose pouch of the old pictures. She places her arms around it and hugs it, she then realizes something that she's never noticed before. The smell on the bear is something different, something that she has to think hard to figure out. It's cologne... Drakkar Noir. Kris wears perform. As much of a man he is, he's also in the line to be a lady. Gray wears an aftershave, nothing more, as colognes impair his other senses that most don't know about. But the aftershave covers his musk so that it can't be picked up by others. She knew only one person who wore that scent... Joshua Bodaway Brand As she turns out the lights, a lone tear rolls down her face for no reason what so ever. As the night Creeps fully into Danielle's room with the last lights of the day, Dani closes her amber eyes and drifts off to sleep with a smile. She knows tomorrow will be a new day and a new start on her career as a wrestler, and hopefully as a person ~~Interlude~~ A young girl sitting in a meadow, surrounded by tall grasses and wildflowers. She sits, playing with some things that she's found. She's wearing a traditional, very old style, hide dress. In her long, silky black hair are a few of the wildflowers, put there as she played. The girl looks up at an older woman, a striking prototype to her cloning. Young daughter and mother looking on at them. They look happy, but with a touch of sadness in their eyes. The little girl looks up at her mother and asks her, in Lakota, something. Little Girl: Do you think we're safe out here momma? I don't want to be snatched up and eaten by the Red Eagle! The girls mother looks down and smiles. Mother: We'll be fine my dear. The elders have called for Fallen Star. He is a great hero, son of one of the Maghpia Oyate. He's coming to kill the red eagle before he steals anymore little girls for his feast. Come, let's take these turnips back to the village so we can add them to the stew. The mother takes the little girls hand and picks up her basket. They start to walk down what appears to be a well traveled trail through the grass. Just as they do, a loud sound can be heard, it sounds like the screech of a large bird. They turn their heads, and see it coming in... and then they try to run, the little girl screaming as she does. The attacker, a large raptor with a hooked beak like an eagle, but with a red plumage that made it look like it was almost on fire, swooped down and almost had it's large talons on the little girl... The sound of something whizzing by her head, the wind being cut by something straight and true and then the loud sound of flesh being penetrated and then the breaking of bones by the force of the shot. A lone arrow lands and impales the eagle, causing it to slam down into the ground with a loud ruckus. The little girl falls down, as she was being prepared to be taken and lifted up to be food for the large predatory bird. As her vision clears she sees the one who took down the threat to their village that had taken 7 young girls over the last week. A large, handsome man, almost a giant in stature compared to others, stood there. His red dyed hide clothes showed a pattern that the people of the Black Hills and the Rosebud village had only heard about before. A single star burst design on the back of his cape showed that this was the man who had come and promised to save them. Fallen Star, the son of a Sioux woman and one of the Cloud People had answered their call and had killed the red eagle. He looked down and took her hand, and then pulled her up into his arms. Powerful arms that were stronger then any other man, yet gentle all the same. He put her back down and then started tracing off towards the side of the mountain, all he could do was indicate that he was headed up to find the giant birds nest... to destroy it once and for all. Dani's eyes flickered as she continued the dream in her reduced conscious state inside the sweat lodge. Her analytical mind started to try to piece things together for what she was seeing, but it came up with nothing. At least not yet. ~~Back on track~~ We pick this up in the evening, after the sweat and rituals that have hopefully done what she needed them to do..... Since they made this a full on thing with the local Paiute, and borrowed their spot on the Valley of Fire reservation north of Las Vegas, Dani has taken the time to mingle with the locals. These people are the ones that have produced another wrestler, the person that Dani just calls the Lady, her cousin comes from here. Danielle is seated on one of the large red stone rocks, in a set of tight jean shorts and a baby doll tank top. Her hair pulled back out of her face in a long braid that goes down her back, and her amber eyes reflect the lights of the fire, with all of it's sparkle. She is talking to some of the younger girls who asking a thousand and one questions at once. Dani looks up and sees Josh and Gray talking, Gray is more looking at Danielle then he is Josh which means she is the subject of their conversation. Dani: If you girls will excuse me I need to go talk to my other friends, it looks like they might have information for me on something. The young girls part and allow her to pass but not without a wave of sighs. Dani just smiles and walks over to the two guys. Gray: Hi Dani, looks like you finally got away from your little Fan club over there. Danielle: Any word from Kris yet on my match? As she asks that both Josh and Gray look at each other and then look at her Danielle: Guys? What? Josh: You know we know how much you hate tag matches, but I guess the leaders of Global didn't get that memo. Danielle: A WHAT? I am a single wrestler what gives them the idea I would want to work with someone in the ring as a partner? If I wanted to be a tag team I would of called you, Gray, and told you to leave him home. As she says that she winks at Gray and then smiles. And shakes her head. Danielle: So who am I tagging with? And who is the match against? Gray pulls out a sheet of paper from his jean pocket, unfolding it. Then hands it off to Danielle. A fax from Kris simply reads : MOTM match T'sao Liang/Jesse Jamester v. Kristian Zenith/Danielle Northsky Josh: Basically, you and Zenith, the guy behind the wench that beat you last week, are to fight alongside each other against another team. If you win, then in the next round of the Master of the Mat tourney, you get to face each other. Danielle: Sounds like a fun match, just as long as my partner is focused we should be okay. What do you know about the other side? Gray: Jamester has a weak ankle so he will have to watch that, no need to have it broken again. My only worry is the fact that these guys have a good hundred pounds on you, and as much as a foot in height. Danielle: GREAT! Andy why am I the odd 'man' out on this match then? Is there still that one wrestling school here in town? Gray smiles before answering. Gray: There is one of the best schools here. But you have to know where to find it. Danielle: And I know you do, that means it's run by your bosses. Can you get me to this School and get it's leader to set up a match with three guys that are the same height and weight as these three or as close as he has. I am going to need to work on this. I think he would know someone I am sure. Gray: Already done. You'll need to be there by 8 am in the morning. But he only had an open time in a few days. You've got a few things to do over the next few days as it is. Including finishing this. I'll have them send you the details to your laptop so you have it all. Be ready, Mr. Kogagawa does not like to wait. He has no tolerance for tardiness. Danielle: That name sounds familiar? Gray: It should. But I'll leave that for you. Once you're done here, I'd advice heading back and getting some sleep. Danielle: Yeah, and both of you, thanks for coming to this. Gray: No problem. Dani reaches out and hugs Gray, her nose wrinkling at his choice of aftershave to cover up his other traits. She lets him go quickly and then reaches over and pulls Josh into a hug. Instantly, once out of range of his older brother, the smell of Josh's cologne fills her nose and she remembers the scent on the teddy bear she's been sleeping with for the last few months, not knowing who had gotten her the gift. She fights herself and lets him go, letting them both head back to where they've been staying. Josh lingers a bit, almost as if he was in shock. Dani can make out as Gray moves to grab him by the arm and take him back to their vehicle. Dani looks down at the sheet of paper again with her match assignment on it and thinks to herself. This match is the start of a new dawn for me. I would like to start it off on a good foot with a win. It is time to get serious about Global, the game is at a new level for me. And I now have to raise the bar step up and actually grow up in the ring. This losing streak has to end, as of today I have left my past for my future and that future is Global wrestling. There is more to the future then just winning a match the future is a series of blind corners that you have to take with caution and with bravery. This is also the beginning of the Master of the Mat tourney, apparently. She must do what she can to go as far as possible in this tourney. She has to prove that 6 months were not a fluke. Then again, the curse should be broken now that she has been cleansed. Gray looks at her as he's getting into his car, and nods in her direction. He winks at her, knowing what is going on in her mind. Now a woman on a mission. That mission is lined with gold, and bodies on the ground at her feet. But as all there are bumps just as long as she doesn't allow those bumps become road blocks, she will continue on. A strong force to be reckoned with. And they know this they have seen this side of Danielle before a long time ago, when teacher said that she was too old to begin the training that they themselves had been doing since they where young. Gray by that time had become a teacher and Josh was the oldest in the class even being the same age of Danielle when she joined he was still the oldest in the class. The Teacher at the time pretty much had just told Danielle that she might as well pack up and go home, she was learning to slow and would never be at the same level as the rest of his students. And that is when Josh saw it for the first time the reason she goes by the name Siruis, the wolf-star. She had been biding her time watching and learning. And in a match with josh, Danielle earned the teachers approval not just because she flashed Josh with her breasts, causing him to loose the match. But because she learned that any advantage that you can take, is an advantage that can be used in your favor. She had found Josh's weakness in his lecherous leering of her cleavage and she had used it and thus won the match. As the teacher always said, “Keep your mind on the game excuses are as plentiful as leaves on a tree or the rain in Ireland.” ~~A few days later....~~ Journal Entry .... July 22, 2005 It took more then a day for me to really feel up to doing anything. The sweat made me exhausted but I am good. My mind and body feel better then they have in a long time. I have asked Gray to come to my room today, without Josh I need to talk to him and just him. I think he knows something that I need to know. Too many things to do not enough time to do them all. Practices went well, more bumps and bruises to add to my collection, but life goes on. The three guys did what they were hired to do. Test me and let me practice being with a partner, hard to go from solo to working with someone even if just for a night. And then, once we win in that night, we have to face each other soon after that. I'm going to have to be really ready for that one. She stops writing, sitting at her desk, and then bolts up and to the door. She opens it and reveals a slightly shocked Gray Brand on the other side, his hand extended up as it to be ready to knock on the door. Danielle: Come on in. I've been waiting for you. Gray: I can tell. She walks into the main part of her room, and then takes a seat, Indian Style, on the lone chair. She motions for Gray to take a seat on the love seat or couch as he pleases. Gray: First, let me get some drinks. Gray walks over to where her small wet bar is in the room, and pulls out a couple of bottles. Looks like some American beer for him, and a fruity wine cooler for her. He opens hers and hands it to her. Danielle: You know, it's my room. If I'm supposed to be hostess, I'd be doing that. Gray: But that doesn't matter. You wanted to speak to me about things? Danielle: Yes. I have a few things actually. There are things that usually I'd prefer Kris to you to talk about, but there are things that we've not been able to discuss... our schedules don't match well for that. Since they're things that need to be discussed without either Kris or Josh around. Gray takes a seat and puts his feet up onto the table. Gray: Well, what's up? Danielle: I've been having dreams lately. I need them explained. Gray: Well that's not exactly my forte. I'm a combat instructor. Wrestling, martial arts and the like. Not a dream interpretor. Are you using a catcher? Danielle: Yes, they're not nightmares. They're something else. Usually they entail a young girl, and a man dressed in red buckskin. The girl varies in age, but usually young, but it's the same girl. Gray: The key figure is not the girl, to a point. The man you see if your ancestor. I've told you about him, right? Danielle: Fallen Star. I know who it's supposed to be. But it's almost as if.. Gray: I learned long ago, to not question the spirits when they come to us. In wake or sleep. Danielle: Your kind, it's easy to say that. Gray cocks his head to the left at that, and looks at her as if she had just smacked him. Gray: My kind? Danielle: I've known, old Gray Muzzle. I've known. For a long time. But it doesn't bother me, nor is it a concern. I know who you work for, and with. That's why one thing that happened in the real world recently doesn't equate to anything real that I can see, except for one conclusion. Gray: What's that? Dani motions her head over to one of the small tables in the room, where the teddy bear that she holds so dear sits, inside it's papoose pouch holder at the moment. Gray: What about it? I've seen you with it. Figured something you picked up. Danielle: Well, I didn't buy it. When we were camping on one of the nights in Canada, it appeared in my tent. I thought my 'big cousin' and 'protector' had bought it after the fights I had with his little brother. Either that or Kris had bought it, thinking it was cute. Gray: No, neither of us did it. Kris would have taken the opportunity to have given it to you in front of everyone so you could have both cried together with how cute it was. Danielle: Again, it doesn't add up. Gray: Yes it does. You're only after confirmation of your suspicions. You know he likes you, he's liked you since you were both in school. Even before you beat him in that wrestling match. He's always been afraid to admit it though. He's even gotten into fights with me over if you and I are... Danielle: EWWW! Now that would be sick. You're ancient! Gray: I'm only a little older then you, damnit. But I've had my wife, have my children. I don't need more of the same heart ache when someone leaves you. You're a free spirit. You need someone who's just as free. I'm too much of a bump on the proverbial log, when you have the whole forest to run around in. Not to mention, our 'other' differnces. Danielle sits back, biting on her lower lip for a bit. Gray looks at the teddy bear and focuses his eyes a bit. After he does, he looks over at her. Gray: Yes, I'll confirm that my younger cub of a brother had something to do with that. Only he would wear that stench. Danielle: It's not a stench. Gray: To each their own. I'll leave it at that. But, I don't need to worry your or get your mind unfocused on other things. You have your career to worry about and your upcoming birthday. Don't get too out of line. Worry about birthdays, hearts and other things when you have time. Gray stands up and winks at her. Gray: I know the way out. Although, you do know I'll have to report your dreams to my superiors? Right? Danielle: That's why I called you privately. I expected it. Have a good night. I'll be at the dojo again tomorrow. Gray: Good. I'll bring Josh by. I can't have him getting lazy. And if you'd like, I'll grill him a little for you to sneak some information out... after your match. Ease your mind a bit. Dani just looks up at him. Danielle: Thanks, big brother. Gray: Anything for you Sirius Star. At that he turns and leaves, tossing the bottle from his beer into the waste bin. Dani sits there, looking across the room at the teddy bear... her mind racing, trying to reel it back in. ~~End~~
