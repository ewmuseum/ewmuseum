The Task At Hand

5 May 2003

The Task At Hand Salomane Vation Global Wrestling Promo #171 Date: 05/05/03 Location: Catholic Monastary A single monk walks across the lush green grass outside a monastery that appears to be over hundreds of years old. He walks with his hands tucked inside the opposite sleeve, looking down at the ground as he shuffles along his path, deep in thought and prayer. Another monk walks past him, as he nods at the second monk as the second monk greets him back. The monk continues shuffling across the grass until he arrives at a pond. He stand over the clear blue water, making it look like something from a fairy tale, he then walks to the side of the pond and bends down to smell a red colored flower which is growing from the bank. As he does so a shadow slowly falls over him as he freezes in terror. “My brother, I believe you have been told of my coming…Feed me, water me, and help me prepare for my quest,” came a booming voice from behind him. The monk slowly gets back to feet, his knees almost giving way, as he stands upright again. He turns around slowly to see in front of him a man, covered in white, with a long white robe blowing backwards in the very small breeze blowing through the field. The monk stares into the blue eyes of this man, eyes which are almost the same as the water itself. “Y-Y-You are…Salomane?” The monk asks, still staring into those blue eyes. “Then it is true, you have been forewarned. Have you prepared what was asked of you to gather for me?” The monk nods his head, as he looks back at the face of Salomane something behind him catches the monk’s eye. Another monk approaches, this one is much younger, maybe even in his twenties. He approaches the two, and stands between them looking at Salomane in amazement. “Is everything alright Brother Stewart?” The young monk asks. “Yes, it fine, just a friend of mine…Please leave us child, we must talk in private, I will be fine.” The young monk nods before walking away Salomane turns his head and watches him leave, as the monk puts his hand over his mouth as a small cough comes out. Salomane suddenly turns back and looks at Brother Stewart once more, this time looking at his face as if reading a book. “Is there something wrong Brother Stewart?” asks Salomane in quite an angry tone. “No…No…There is nothing wrong…nothing wrong at all.” Salomane looks at the face of Brother Stewart again, reading him, studying his features as Salomane shakes his head. “Why must you lie to a subject of our Lord? Do you fear me? Or do you simply not believe in me?” “There is one thing…Your task specifically. We do not understand why you have been sent here, the people do not need a savior, times have changed Salomane, the people have changed…The church has changed…We at the monastery believe that you are not wanted here, and I think you shall have that made clear to you wherever you walk.” “That will be what it will be…But they are simply blind to the cause, you will all see the extent of my power, the first step of my task began this Sunday. I have already taken aim at a false idol in GW, Mr. Pailthorpe, and the fear of God has been placed in him.” “But again…Do you think terrorizing the stars that fans like is a good idea to get the people back to the church?” Salomane stares at Brother Stewart again as a very deep laughter comes from him, he stares Brother Stewart in the eye before shaking his head. “Do not bow down to any idol or worship it, because I am the Lord your God and I tolerate no rivals. I bring punishment on those who hate me and on their descendants down to the third and fourth generation. But I show my love to thousands of generations of those who love me and obey my laws…Commandments Exodus 20 4-7…I shall show myself to my room.” With that Salomane turns away from Brother Stewart and briskly walks along the green grass himself. He takes large steps, squashing the grass under the heavy weight of his feet as he walks. Brother Stewart looks on in despair and crosses himself before turning back to the pond again and muttering a silent prayer.
