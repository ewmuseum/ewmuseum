Old Boy

28 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/28/05 There they were; there could be no more of an uncomfortable situation than there was of the one that was taking place. Michael Mercer and the acquaintance that he still refused to call his friend had held up Luis Renaldo Mercer in the confines of his own home. His hands were tied behind his back with rope and his feet were bound by what looked like endless amounts of duct tape that wrapped from his ankles to knee joints. The middle aged man's only defense on the living room couch would have been to curl up into the fetal position and simply pray to God that his punishment would be as quick as humanly possible. The two ladies in the room could only stare breathlessly, even if Arielle had more of a knowing look in her eyes as to what was happening and why it was. Mindy, on the other hand, was simply stunned. Where did her boyfriend and cousin come from? What kind of grudge did they have with her father? Silently, Arielle slinked over to her daughter and put her lips close to Mindy's ear. 'Head with me upstairs,' she whispered plainly. 'We'll talk about your parents while we don't become bothersome to what's going on here.' How she could keep up the angelic streak while her own husband was tied up and could very well be subjected to severe beatings was a mystery. However, it didn't compare to the ongoing question in Mindy's mind of why- and how- Markus and Michael both got there so hastily. But with how angry both men seemed to be, she thought it best that she took her mom's advice. She couldn't hear Michael's screaming at her father, but then again, no one else could. The screaming was only existent in Michael's subconscious; a wave of emotion that was barely suppressed by the want for his cousin not to be brought down by his mood. 'Why did you do this to me? What gives you the right, you son of a bitch? What gives you the fucking right?' The words raced through his mind and were gone before he could even think of bringing them to his lips. Maybe it was for the best; he had a tough time conveying his anger through words, simply because words were so overused. Love. Hate. Sorrow. Joy. They had no meaning anymore. But contrary to popular belief, the one single most overused sentence to Michael was: 'I hate you.' That was indeed Michael's mindset as he stared down a now helpless man that he once would've happily acknowledged as his uncle. He said nothing, but Luis knew what Michael was thinking. And no matter what, he had all the right in the world to think it. Luis, of all people, knew that. The old man was a jerk for what he did, sure, but he wasn't stupid. ---- Cameraman: Is this thing on? Michael Mercer had to forget about his altercation on that night so he didn't seem too upset in front of the camera as it began to roll. Granted, he had no business in the Global Arena that night other than to watch out for his cousin's safety in her dangerous cell match. Michael had been caught completely off guard by the camera, too busy watching the backstage monitor to notice the camera before it was too late. Unfortunately, Michael's overjoyed smile was caught on tape, elated at the sight of his cousin having just won the Gold and Glory tournament. Of course, fame in Global for Michael himself would have assisted in boosting his spirits much more, but he couldn't complain that Mindy was such a success. Mindy had worked her ass off to get to the point where she'd have a shot at a prestigious title like the one Train was carrying, especially after only few people stuck by her from the beginning. 'Well,' Michael thought, 'at least one of us is still living the good life.' He had little time to react to the cameraman behind him, but with such time Michael was able to manage a stoic expression on his face. Turning around, Michael looked directly into the camera lens, blocking the monitor he was looking at. Mercer: If you weren't holding that camera, I swear to God I would have mistaken you for Sloth from The Goonies. The man whose name Michael didn't care to know had merely sighed in distaste of Michael's joke. Lucky for him, no fight would have the opportunity to break out, as something- rather, someone- stepped in between the two men. Tsunami: Michael Mercer, one of Global Wrestling's more... overlooked superstars. I see you're happy with the result of your cousin's win tonight? Mercer: o_O Michael couldn't even take in a breath before Noi's microphone was shoved into his face. Michael slowly blinked his unnaturally dark brown eyes in annoyance. His semi-long spikes were flattened shortly after as one of his hands rested on his head, ruffling his black and red hair with little care in the world. Who knew- maybe he'd make himself look unpresentable enough for an on-camera interview and they would leave him alone. Seconds after, though, the duo continued to gaze unflinchingly at Michael. Mercer sighed out of frustration, and shook his head. Mercer: No, actually, I'm pissed Mindy won. She's my cousin, I took her in when she needed my help, and we've been a fairly successful tag team in Global Wrestling at times, but that doesn't change the fact that her win annoys me very much. Noi took a slight step back, surprised by the response. Obviously, she didn't sense the sarcasm dripping from Michael's every word. Tsunami: Really? Care to explain why? Michael groaned in repulsion. He hated being interviewed. However, expressing any side of himself other than his 'real' side was welcome. There was no way he was going to reveal his emotions in front of the camera; he wasn't that kind of person, and the things on his mind were not the kind of material to be displayed on television. Mercer: ...I'm kidding, Tsunami. Calm your peaches. I'm glad Mindy won, she deserves it with how she's been progressing. Name me one other woman who could have pulled off a win like that. Noi tilted her head slightly from Michael's comment, but kept on firing questions. She was a reporter, after all; trained not to be emotional- or at least, too emotional- by the answers given to her, no matter how shocking or abrupt. Tsunami: Quite a bold statement... well, if I may, I was hoping that you could get me your opinion on Train? Michael shrugged. Mercer: Good means of transport if you're living in an old fashioned Western movie. Tsunami: ...The champion, Tr- Mercer: Holy mashed potatoes, Batman, it's a joke. Not for nothin', though, why would I really care about Train, pray tell? Tsunami's brows furrowed in impatience. Of course, she was responsible for researching the wrestling background of every wrestler on the Global roster. Sure, she found out from doing so that Michael Mercer was a rather outspoken and very cocky man to say the least, but most wrestlers were. She wasn't expecting this. Tsunami: Well... he is the person who Mindy will have to defeat for the World title. Mercer: Wow, that's informing. Is he pregnant with Brick's baby, too? Well, congratulations to them and their child. Maybe they can continue their one-named family and call the kid 'Blanket'. No, wait, someone else did that already. 'And to think, people love this guy,' thought Noi Tsunami, unimpressed with the GW rookie's humor. It may have been infectious in his past companies that knew the man well enough; to Noi, however, he was simply being a jerk. Well, not a jerk as much as just running his mouth more than he should. Tsunami: No offense, Michael, but surely you have an idea of how dangerous Train is? Mercer: Well I'm assuming Train is dangerous if you don't know enough to get out of the way of an oncoming bulk of steel and metal on wheels. No, I mean, well.. no, I don't have any idea how dangerous Train is. Is he as dangerous as Christian Zenith? As dangerous as Mike Biddle? As dangerous as one hundred and eighty-four days? If so, I fail to realize why I should care, because it'd be nothing Mindy can't handle. Tsunami: I hope so. Mercer: You hope so? What do you mean, you hope so? Before Michael can get an answer out of Noi, the 'interview' is interrupted by a passerby. No, not just a passerby. Ryba: What's all this happy horse s[bleep]t? Oh s[bleep]t, I'm on TV. My bad. Oh... well damn, I can't say that, can I? Mercer promptly turned his head in his friend's direction. Mercer: Hey, now, Ryba, get your facts straight. It's not just TV... Ryba looked back at Mercer. They both smiled and finished the sentence together. Mercer and Ryba: It's HBO. Ryba: Man, I completely forgot about that. It's all the same lookin' at it from the ring, though. Mercer: Ain't that the truth? Tsunami simply remained quiet as the two men spoke between one another, trying to regain her patience to get some more info out of Michael. He was possibly one of the most quiet members of Global Wrestling, and she wanted to know exactly why that was; just like any other reporter would. Sadly enough for her, Michael Ryba noticed. Ryba: What is she standin' there for? Michael wavered in response; amidst the joking about, he seemed to forget, himself. Mercer: Uh, I think she was trying to interview me. Ryba grinned a knowing grin. Ryba: Well, after Crossfire when we get the number one contendership to those Tag Team titles, she can interview the both of us. Mercer: Perhaps. Call it irony, though, that she wanted to talk to me about what I thought about one half of those Tag Team champions. Ryba: Let me guess... Train? Mercer: Sup, mister Cleo. Ryba laughed at the remark and shook his head. Ryba: Yeah, had that feeling. Because you know what, no one would want to talk about a man named after a rectangular glob of clay. Mercer: Well with that mindset, you have to wonder who would want to talk about someone named after a method of transportation? Ryba: Heh, well that's true, too. And with that, Noi Tsunami turned away to face her cameraman, whispering as she did so. Tsunami: Turn the camera off... Over the sound of the heavy set man fiddling with the buttons to his camera, Michael Mercer could hear Noi say something else. Her back was to him and his friend, but it didn't change the fact that he was able to hear those words perfectly; so perfectly, that they almost seemed to be spoken solely to mock the brown-eyed Mercer. ---- 'I don't think he cares about how much Mindy is at risk by going up against Train.' That sentence, even though it was never truly spoken, had plagued Michael's mind- so much, that he shot out of bed in defiance. 'I do care...' Michael had awakened in time to catch himself slur the words from his mouth, and he was able to supress them into a collective whisper rather than the scream it threatened to be. In that same moment, it was then that he realized another thing. Michael took note of it with an exasperated sigh. 'A dream. God... it was only a dream.' 'Well so much for words having no meaning, Michael, you old boy.' The truth was that Mindy had been allowed to sleep safe and sound in her own bed that night, despite the brutality she had endured in her match that night. Markus was with her, and although Michael couldn't vouch for his cousin, he was sure that Mindy having her boyfriend around would help ward off any bad dreams on her behalf. He cared about Mindy; shit, Michael cared about his entire family. Mindy was the only one he had keeping him from going insane, and there was no way he could afford to lose her. God knew that his friend Michael Ryba knew all about situations like the one he was in, but he couldn't bring himself to think about that. They both had their problems, yeah, but they were never the kind to actually bring their problems of the past to eachother. Michael Ryba wasn't the problem, though. The problem was doing enough for his cousin that he could look at himself in the mirror every morning and say with a straight face that he truly does care. With a low grunt, Michael slid out of bed and made his way over to the computer across the room. He plopped down into the chair by the desk, and began typing. With a little luck, Michael would be able to get enough research on Mindy's newest challenge to relay to her; but it would take hope for Mindy to accept her cousin's offer for assistance. 'I did sort of flip out on her for getting in my business with Liang... I just hope she understands the difference in the two situations here.' Speaking of T'sao Liang, Michael hadn't seen him in quite some time. There was something wrong with the entire scenario; Liang was 'hired' by Michael to help find Jaden and Gabi. 'And instead, he found my parents.' Not that that was a bad thing, but as much as it pained him to say it, they weren't around for the memories that shaped Michael into the slightly paranoid man that was sitting up late at night trying to do some 'studying' for his cousin. Jaden was. Still, that wasn't taking away from the fact that he was happy to know his parents were okay. ---- 'Let them go,' Michael ordered. Luis sat there shaking in his plush couch, with no way to defend himself from the onslaught that would be upon him if he resisted. However, he had no choice... 'I.. I can't.' The speed which Michael's right arm shot out at his uncle had even scared him, but fear wasn't going to make his hand refrain from gripping Luis' throat as tight as possible. Michael's means of reason were far beyond succumbing to the power of fear. 'You can. You will. You will tell me where they are. You will find a way to let them go, and you will do it now. Right now.' Luis made the overwhelming mistake of looking into those eyes. Those scary, black eyes did indeed tell more than words could ever express. They didn't mean something as atypical as death, surely; but they did mean something undeniably bad. How far that went, however, was never known to anyone other than Michael. However, considering that most people who did see that look in his eyes never wanted to hear the name 'Michael Mercer' again, perhaps that proclaimed enough. They easily overshadowed the calculating menace to his voice, although that in itself wasn't an easy feat, either. 'Can you imagine what I'm thinking right now? I lived the majority of my life without parents. My mother... my father.. they were dead to me for more than half my life, when you kept them alive all along. You...' Michael trailed off. The grip around the other human being's throat grew tighter still. Luis' face began to become more and more red. 'Heh... you can see the look in my eyes right now. I can kill you right now if I want..' A sick grin was soon displayed on Michael's face. 'Look into my eyes... do you think I can kill you?' Michael did not let up on his hold around Luis' neck, thus preventing the man to answer the question; as if he had any kind of answer that wouldn't have resulted in a lot of pain. Desperately, he coughed out what few words he could gather in such lack of breath. 'I.... ca... can't.....' Michael's eyes remained still; he refused to let up. '...can't... bre.. breathe....' There he was, dying right before Michael's eyes, and Michael could not even blink an ounce of sadness; and granted, he had no reason to. In the end, however, he did have a reason to let Luis go. Reluctantly, he did so. 'I want to see them. Where are they?' Needless to say, Michael Mercer got his answer; one that was well over ten years in the making. ---- How could Michael Mercer possibly explain that to his buddy Ryba? He couldn't. He wasn't going to, either. His pain would simply be explained through the torturing and picking apart of two unfortunate tag teams in La Herencia de Herederos and The Enforcers. Michael's wish, however, was not just to win. 'I hope mom and dad don't plan on watching Crossfire.'
