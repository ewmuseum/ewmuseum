'United we stand: Divided we fall.'

11 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 11th, 2004. It was a beautiful day in Dallas and a great day to be alive…for most anyway. The wind was still, the sun was shining down, the Texas sky was blue without a cloud in sight and the sun shone down providing deliverance from the cold. Seventy-five degrees and it was only March. At least a few days out of the year the Dallas weather could be manageable. Of course…only a few days because it seemed around mid April God cranked up the thermostat and the sun glared angrily down on Dallas, heating the ancient city with it's burning rays. However today wasn't like that at all. Beautiful weather, beautiful scenery and a beautiful day. All in all it was the type of day where you take off from work or play hooky from school and have a little fun with friends. Which was exactly the course of action two young men decided to follow. Those two aforementioned individuals were Global Wrestling World Champion, Ice and his partner in crime 'American Youth' Josh, also a fellow champion of the Cruiserweight division. Collectively these two men were known as the Impact Force and they also formed one half of the Elite. They stood face to face their eyes locked on one another less than six feet apart. The cold blue eyes of Ice locked onto the bluish gray orbs of American Youth before Josh looked away. His gaze focused on Ice's spiked blonde hair before traveling to his feet. Despite their storied friendship and loyalty Josh still couldn't bear to look Ice eye to eye…or anyone else for that matter. It was simply due to the self-consciousness that possessed Josh and the overly analytical demons that sit on his shoulder and screamed into his ear about all of his imperfections. Once a bold and cocky kid he had grown up to be a self-loathing, brooding, loner that only allowed his closest friends to dig deep into his soul and know him for whom and what he truly was. Of course having your dreams blow up in your face tends to do that to you. Ice was quite another story entirely. His ego had always been massive and surprisingly he had yet to be humbled for his brash behavior. Ice seemed invincible…the only way he could be hurt was to attack the ones he loved. And perhaps the reason he was deemed a monster was for the pure agony that dripped from his wretched soul. Then again, what man wouldn't have serious difficulties coping with life if everyone he had ever loved had been violently torn away from his possession? These two were cut from the same mold…a mold that was then shattered to save humanity from future generations and second comings of these infidels. Each man had difficulty coping with life; each man had failed miserably in his pursuit of happiness and love. Instead these two seemed to be walking a path of strikingly similar comparison. Where the path would take them was a mystery that no one bearing a single morsel of sanity would care to scrutinize. Instead these two were best left alone. Aside from their few friends and one another they weren't much for social interaction anyway. Instead each man stood there, haunted by his own personal demons. The demons inside that clawed, scratched, ripped and tore at their senses as they tried to break through the skin and unleash themselves on the rest of the innocent world. Neither man could completely control his demons and it showed by their actions. The saying 'actions speak louder than words' is well applied here as each individual has said things with their actions that even the most eloquent of speakers and listeners would have difficulty foretelling and understanding. Trouble seems only to attract even more trouble…thus the hinge of their relationship. A relationship of rocky proportions from adversaries, enemies, bitter rivals, mutual respect, friendly rivals, to finally…great friends. Friends that had spilt the others blood countless times. However today the two friends stood across from one another engaged in battle once again. The battle was both physical and mental. As brutal as this battle was no blood had been shed, no punches thrown and no bones broken…thus far. Of course this battle was quite different than their battles of days gone by. When you travel down memory lane and look at their prior collisions you will see many moments of brutality…but today it's purely about pride. Today there are no punches, no kicks, no suplexes, and no high-flying maneuvers. Instead there is a battle between two men. Two focused men engaged in a friendly rivalry. Locked in a heated confrontation and enjoying the game of basketball. The court was no work of art, a large gray concrete slab with a single basketball goal set against a brick wall covered in graffiti. Lovingly created images of 'Tupac' covered the wall as well as a horde of gang symbols. The wall was covered in this 'ghetto art' that seems to be commonplace in this neighborhood. A silver chain link fence surrounded the court with only one door opposite of the goal itself. This desolate yet strangely serene court played today's host in the confrontation of American Youth and Ice as they stood there, the Cruiserweight champion clutching a basketball in his hands. Ice moved his feet in a slow pattern, as the bluish gray eyes of American Youth stayed focused on the white Nike sneakers, Ice wears. Both men were dressed fairly similar with Ice being in silver Gym shorts whereas American Youth opting for a pair of black Gym shorts and black Nike's as well. 'Are you going to shoot the ball or what?' Ice sneered at his friend as American Youth simply ignored him. American Youth dribbled the orange sphere to his left and Ice immediately took a step over there looking for the steal, instead 'American Youth' Josh quickly changed the direction of the basketball as he sent it to his right hand. 'Not again...' Ice said with a groan as Josh stormed by him with Ice reaching out in a vain attempt to snare the basketball from the hands of American Youth. Instead Josh made a fast break to the basket and grabbed two points for himself with a lay-up. 'Sixteen to sixteen, Icy.' Josh said as a playful smirk danced across his face. To Ice and the rest of Josh's concerned friends the smirk was more than just a braggadocios annoyance…to them it was actually a relief. To see the smirk of days gone by was a good sign. A sign that perhaps he's slowly beginning to overcome his broken heart, a sign that perhaps one day he will return to normalcy. The latter is unlikely…but false hope is better than painful acceptance. Ice shot back a short smile of his own slightly embarrassed by falling for the same move that Josh used on eight of his previous sixteen points, generating half of his offense on one fake. 'Shut up and play defense.' Ice stated sourly although his opponent knew that Ice is having more fun today than he has in ages, even if it's for no reason other than Ice has been able to put business aside briefly. Putting business aside even for just a few minutes is the best thing Ice can do because it's been the repercussions of the Wrestling industry and his in ring actions that have caused him so much emotional and mental trauma. As the ball bounced down from it's trip through the hoop, Josh grabbed it up and tossed it to Ice. The World Champion accepted it and took a step forward, he knew where the weakness of 'American Youth' Josh lied. It wasn't his sanity or lack thereof…at least it wasn't in athletic competition. Instead it was in his size. At five foot ten and just barely tipping the scales at one pound north of two hundred Josh was at a rather large size disadvantage. The six foot three, two hundred and forty pound Ice took another step forward waiting for a chance to overpower and over shoot the smaller albeit faster man. Charging forward he tried running through Josh but American Youth used his quick wits as he reached around and nearly stole the ball. Ice reacted quickly by jerking the ball back and in his haste to do so Josh was all over him, Ice stood there crouched clutching and protecting the ball as Josh hovered about him waiting to dig into the arms of Ice and rip the ball out for a steal. 'I'm faster, smarter and better looking…it's only inevitable that you lose Ice.' Josh sneered at his friend as Ice clung to the ball. 'Well…you are faster.' Ice admitted while dismissing American Youth's latter two claims 'You forgot smarter, better looking and ten times better in the sack.' Josh said as he kept the trash talk flowing. 'Wrong. Your faster little buddy but that's it.' Ice said as he spun away from Josh with his back towards the admittedly faster player. 'Yes but you also have to factor in all of those things. After all…my consistent beating of you in our matches kind of proved that don't you think?' Said Josh as he attempted to annoy Ice into making a mistake. 'You seem to be forgetting something…I beat you in our last two matches.' Ice said with a playful grin as he spun around and launched a shot attempting to send it sailing over the head of American Youth. However it was not destined to score as Josh leapt high into the air in an amazing display of athleticism and with soft hands he plucked the ball from the sky. As soon as the soles of his Nike's hit the ground he was a casa en feugo which to be interpreted from Spanish stands for 'house of fire'. Taking several quick steps Josh took the ball back across the single white line on the otherwise gray concrete court. American Youth eyed Ice once again as the two squared off. Dancing to the left American Youth progressed the ball that way. Resolving to not be faked out for what seems like the hundredth time, Ice stayed put following American Youth from a slight distance. 'Too bad were only going to twenty…I was just getting warmed up.' Said 'American Youth' Josh as he went quickly to the right. This time Ice bit, only to be victimized yet again by the quick spin move as American Youth went scrambling away to the left leaving Ice by himself. With Ice missing an opponent he turned just in time to see American Youth sink another lay up putting this game at and eighteen to sixteen disadvantage for Ice. As the ball slowly fell from its lofty heights Josh scooped it up and bounced the rock to Ice. 'Man, I hope you're not this out of shape when we go to face Souls of the Black Lotus on Monday.' Said 'American Youth' Josh with an obnoxious smile spread over his handsome features. 'Are you kidding, I'll show everyone why I'm the World champion and why you've never even had a shot at that title. Sorry pal but I'm stealing the show.' Ice said as he dribbled the orange sphere towards the basket. This trip towards the hoop was looking more successful for Ice though as he faked American Youth to the left and went charging towards the right. However Josh quickly recovered and came charging back but it was too late as Ice had already thrown up a shot…and it slowly fell through the hoop knotting the game at eighteen apiece. Ice snatched up the rock with his own rebound and sent the ball to American Youth with a bounce pass. American Youth smiled slyly deciding to let his actions speak for him rather than his words. With this in mind he took two quick steps forward as Ice pounced quickly, looking for a steal. However Ice only fell into another of 'American Youth' Josh's traps as Josh quickly stepped back and launched the ball into a vertical pattern from what we could only assume was the three point line as the ball soared majestically towards the hoop. As it sunk into the hoop a resounding swish confirmed Ice's fears without him even turning around. 'Game point. Ice...playing against you is just like dating your mom...I can't help but score.' Said Josh with a bedazzling smile spread over his face.
