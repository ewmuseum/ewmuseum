In Tough

19 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/10/03 “Man, I’ve got it tough, I don’t know if I can face Akiyara this week. I mean we trained so hard for our tag title match, and we fucked it up,” said Jushin. Jushin looked up to his brother; Ichiro was seated in deep thought on the couch. He had been quiet and had gone out little this week, Jushin thought maybe the loss at Maximum Carnage had bothered his older brother just as much as it had bothered himself. He looked around the apartment, he saw the birds outside the window sill, and he saw the television set playing. The woman on the television set was recapping the sports scores for tonight, but Jushin had no interest. He turned back to his brother and awaited a reply. “Yes, but the titles are still with TUE, and for right now that’s all that matters,” answered Ichiro. “Yeah, maybe for you, and for Il Capo, but not for me!” answered Jushin. “Calm down bro, as soon as Deac and Zero drop the ball, we’ll be right behind them to pick it up and run with it,” said Ichiro. “Yeah, but as soon as I get my hands on that ball, I am never going to let it go!” answered Jushin. “That’s the spirit, see you can take on Akiyara, he’s got nothing on you man!” encouraged Ichiro. “Yeah, but this is my first singles match. Not just in GW, ever!! You’ve been a singles fighter before, not me, I’ve always had you right there to back me up,” Jushin was discouraged and upset with himself about Maximum Carnage. “Don’t worry bro, I’ll still have your back. It’s not like you’ll need it out there bro, just get out there and kick his ass!” said Ichiro. “Besides, it’s not like you’ve never been in worse situations, remember that mission when we had to pretend we were part of the mob?” “Yeah, man, that was intense.” answered Jushin. Flashback “Let’s go Jushin get in the fucking car,” Jushin looked at his brother, he was nuts. There was no way he was going on this mission, it was suicide! He could only stare at his brother who was already sitting in the driver’s seat getting ready to go. Jushin shook his head no. “JUSHIN! Get into the fucking car, right now!” “Man, are you crazy”! This is suicide, you can’t just walk into an underground car garage in one of the Mobs vehicles, and hope they don’t notice you’re the enemy until it’s too late. This is international shit, shit we better not get our hands into,” replied Jushin. “Look man, I don’t like the idea too much either. But when the wops, want to come into our country, into our town, and try to take away OUR business, we’ve got to do something,” answered his older brother. “I guess you’re right man, let’s go,” and with that Jushin hopped into the car, and slammed the door. He buckled his seat belt as his brother; Ichiro was already burning out of the parking lot. The black Italian made car sped down the streets of downtown Tokyo. Jushin looked to his brother, he looked intense. Jushin didn’t know how he could do it, his brother never seemed to look worried about anything. They were walking into a mission that could easily spell death for the two of them, but Ichiro looked like he was about to take a walk in the park. Jushin turned his attention from his older brother, to the backseat of the car. The backseat of the Italian car, was made of fine black leather. However, stacked on top of the leather were two fully automatic machine guns. Jushin looked at his brother, “You sure that is going to be enough fire power?” “Yeah, don’t worry about it, they are each loaded with a one hundred round clip,” answered Ichiro. “And how many guys do we have to take out?” asked Jushin. “Dad said to expect about ten to twelve,” answered Ichiro. “Alright, then how much longer until we get there?” Jushin furthered. “What is this twenty questions” Shit man, I don’t know a few minutes, so do me a favor, the windshield glass should be really loose, just make sure it’ll come out when we kick it,” said Ichiro. Jushin touched his finger to the windshield. He pressed lightly, and felt the glass move slightly, as he did so. “Yeah man, she’s good.” “Great,” said Ichiro. “Now check the guns and bring them up to the front we’re almost there!” Jushin reached into the back and checked the guns. Both of them were already loaded, Jushin examined the guns, and it seemed that both were loaded properly. Jushin grabbed the guns and pulled them into the front seat. He placed them in his lap, facing towards the ceiling. Slowly and carefully he removed the safety from both of the guns and got them ready to go. Just as Jushin was finishing up his adjustments on the gun, Ichiro noticed the entrance to the garage. Ichiro began to slow the vehicle and made the right turn into the lot. Jushin began to get nervous, as the vehicle slowly wound its way into the bottom floors of the parking complex. Finally when they reached the bottom floor, Ichiro parked the Italian styled car right in front of the meeting that was taking place. Those who were discussing the business at the hand noticed the car pull up. They stared at the car, but still continued on. The exchange was about to be made, one the Italians went into the black SUV and pulled out a small metal briefcase. He carried it over to a man, and then placed it into his possession. “That’s the briefcase we need, I have a good feeling it will tell us what the wops are doing here,” said Ichiro. “Okay, but how the fuck are we going to get it now?” asked Jushin. Just as he spoke the man in possession of the briefcase, took out a pair of handcuffs and attached the case to his wrist. It was a good anti-theft measure, but it had serious repercussions on the Nakazura brothers? plans. “I got it handled,” Ichiro said smiling. He ducked down and reached under the seat, he pulled out a large knife, which appeared to be a machete. He smiled as he showcased it to his brother. Then they both noticed that some of the Italians were growing curious and were beginning to approach the car. Jushin handed his brother a gun and they both sat back in their seats, they pulled the seats all the way back, so they were sitting almost horizontal. They both positioned the guns across their chests, and breathed in and out. Ichiro watched as the Italians got very close to the car. “On three kick the windshield out,” he said. “Okay,” said Jushin. “One, two, three go?” “No on three,” “Okay,” said Jushin. “One, two, three”.” Ichiro counted. Just after he finished speaking the two brothers kicked the windshield as hard as they good, it went flying and nailed the two approaching Mafioso in the head, and instantly broke their noses. They dropped their guns and grabbed their noses in pain as blood began to pour from their noses. The two brothers pulled up on the lever underneath of their seats and they popped up into normal seated positions staring out the open windshield. All the Italians were now reaching for their guns as the two brothers came into view. However, as the two brothers popped up into view, they also positioned and aimed their guns. The fully automatic weapons were now pointed out the open windshield into the direction of all fifteen standing Mafia members. “There’s more than we thought, gotta make every shot count,” Ichiro instructed his brother. Jushin pulled the trigger on his weapon, and instantly bullets began to spray from the gun. Ichiro followed suit and the Italians began to fall one by one. The two brothers moved across with their fire in unison, making sure to sweep the entire area. Finally, when the two guns clicked as they were out of bullets, there was not a single member of the Mafia left standing. Jushin smiled at his brother, “What did you say before I started shooting, I wasn’t paying attention!” “Smart ass!” answered Ichiro, as he stepped out the car. He looked across the ground and began to scan the area for survivors. He thought he saw one move slightly, and delivered a swift boot to the mans face. The man went limp, he was either dead or out cold. Ichiro then casually walked over to the man with the briefcase attached to his wrist. “Now what are we going to do, genius?” snickered Jushin. “Grab the fucking knife, and bring it here,” instructed Ichiro. Jushin reached over and grabbed the knife, and the towel that was wrapped around it. He pulled it up, and it was surprisingly heavy. He carried the knife to his brother, and his brother gladly accepted it. He took the towel and tossed it to his brother, “Blow up the car!” “What?” asked Jushin. “We can’t leave any evidence, there are no other cars around except there’s,” at that moment Ichiro pointed to the scatter of bodies lying on the concrete. “So, we can’t leave a trail, so blow up the car.” “Alright fine,” said Jushin as he began to walk to the black Italian car. “But how are we going to get out of here?” “There’s a car on the next level up waiting for us,” answered Ichiro. Jushin nodded and he stepped around to the gas tank of the car. He opened the hatch to the small gas tank, and then quickly unscrewed that cap. He then, took the rag and stuck one end into the tank and left the other dangling on the outside. He looked to his brother, to see if he was ready. Ichiro raised the machete high above his head, and with his other hand was pulling the briefcase into the air, raising the dead man’s arm from the ground. He swung down heavily and the force of the large knife’s blade, cut the man’s forearm clear off. Ichiro smiled satisfied and then wiped the handle of the blade clean of any finger prints. He then stuck the knife into the dead man’s back, and walked away, with the briefcase in his hands. Jushin walked pulled out a lighter and immediately touched the flame to the rag. Instantly the rag ignited in flames. Jushin ran to meet his brother, and then together they ran up the ramp towards the awaiting Yakuza vehicle. Just as they got into the car, they heard a loud explosion. They saw a burst of flames, coming from the floor underneath of them, and felt a small shockwave of debris come flying as they were stepping into the vehicle. They stepped into the red Yakuza vehicle, and quickly fastened their seat belts. Ichiro turned the key in the ignition, and the car’s engine immediately roared to life. He shifted the car into gear, and the two brothers began to pull away from the scene. They got onto the road, and began to head for home. End Flashback “You’re right bro, I’ve been in tighter situations than this. But still, I am going to have to go train up a bit,” said Jushin. “Yes, you will,” said Ichiro. “Well then, I’m headed up to the rooftop, care to join me?” asked Jushin. Ichiro shook his head no. “No I think I am going to get some sleep, but go ahead. Just remember, tomorrow night, I’ve got your back. I will always have your back!” “Thanks bro,” said Jushin. With that Jushin stood and began to walk towards the door. He was already dressed to train as before Ichiro had come into his room, he had been doing some push-ups and sit-ups. He closed the door behind him, and began to walk towards the stairs that would take him up to the rooftop of the building.
