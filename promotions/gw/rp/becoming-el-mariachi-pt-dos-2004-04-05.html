Becoming 'El Mariachi' Pt. Dos

5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/04 'Get over here now!' Reminscent of the famous Mortal Kombat warrior Scorpion, I lay on my bed waiting to die. Somehow my heart managed to crawl into my head and proceeds to beat every two seconds, adding to the ache and pain. Apperantly I had been dreaming of the old Sega game. I failed to realize that my boots were still on, that my shirt was torn and that there was a fat hoss laying next to me... 'James! What are you doing in my bed?' The noise apperantly started 'Fat' James, causing the fat hoss to roll over and off the bed into a small coffee table. The table crushed under his immense weight. Thank goodness he still had all his close on, his white cowboy suit was dirty. Apperantly we had had a wild night. 'Whoa compadre! Take it easy, my head feels like a thousand rushing race horses.' 'My does too, what did we do last night?' 'The last thing I remember was making a bet with some girl that I could out drink her.' It was scary having no knowledge of what had happened, especially waking up in the same bed with 'Fat' James. A knock at the door startled both of us. 'Room service, Mr. James you have a call from Luna.' The color ran from 'Fat' James' face. He rushed to the phone and picked it up. All I could do to keep from laughing was bite my lip. 'Yes Miss Luna...I know it's already ten...We had a little problem this morning with our suit cases...No mam, we weren't out late...Yes mam, I can have him there...No man I don't want you foot up my...' 'Who was that hot shot?' 'Hey you leave that to me, get ready we need to be at Lunas house by two, we should have been there two hours ago.' Luna, I had heard the name before but I wasn't sure exactly where from. Goddfrey went into the bathroom making it clear that I wouldnt be going in there for the next few days. I through on a standard t-shirt, and some jeans. I also had to put on the horrid mask. We ended up leaving the hotel room after one, but the limo driver still managed to get us to Lunas by two. As soon as I walked into the mansion I knew who Luna was. She was the famous world class designer. Apperantly GW had hired her to prepare my gig. 'Ah you can take off the oogly mask Senor Gonzalez. Today I will create the new you, today I will create 'El Mariachi.' The first thing she did was take out a pair of clippers and shave off my long hair. I was bald, completely bald. That was followed by her shaving off my eye brows and then every hair on my body, yes every hair! 'Your skin, eets so nice and beautiful. I can't remember the last time I had a man that looked that tanned.' SHe finally dug through seven cases of contacts before handing me the pair that she swore was perfect. I thought it made me look kind of queer. They were red, so apperantly I was supposed to have red eyes. She followed up the contacts by having three teams of men bring out three diffrent racks that had atleast twenty different capes. She finally picked out a red one, it had white trimming and on the back it had 'El Mariachi' written out in solid black cursive letters. She tied it around my neck and soon I was standing in her work area nake, wearing nothing but the red contacts and the red cape. 'Pedro, you're looking better with every second. I knew you had it in you kid, once we took that whole nasty hair off your body and made you look like a true champion!' I wanted to choke him. Finally Luna brought out a pair of wrestling tights, they were solid red briefs, the same shade as the cape. She then through two red knee pads on the floor and told me to slide them on, and then a pair of red elbow pads. I was the living incarnation of the kool-aid man! 'Ah Senor Gonzalez, we are almooost done. In a matter or seconds you will be ready to show your face to the world.' I saw one of Lunas lackeys bring out my red mask, and my red boots. Then finally I stood there in the center, everyone gazing at me and applauding. I have to admit, I felt the role. But I hated the people. 'Look at chu Pedro, you're ready champ. I don't think that Dylan kid is going to stand a chance! Just wearing this outfit, you're going to completely own him in that ring.' Yes Dylan Ford, he had been on my mind as of late. I had reason to believe that the kid would be no challenge. Apperantly nobody in the world knew who the kid was and there was no way of knowing his style and how to work against it. The one thing I had going for me was experiance, fighting in prison and working matches with the guys there really helped me out. I felt the role, I was ready. The only question I could think of was 'Is Dylan Ford ready, Are you ready Dylan?'
