Make me a superstar

16 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/16/05 Pulling into my drivewayonce again after another disappointing pay per view. Another blown title shot for me. However this time I getout of my Escalade with my head held high. Well, somewhat anyway. I did well for being the guy that didn't have a snowball's chance in hell of walking out the new Platinum Champion. From the start of the match I got under KPJ's skin. Sure he got out of tohe way when I fired my super soaker but I hit Gunner Addison which in some ways was even better. Now I have a match with a superstar. A supser star so big he feels the the need to put it infront of his name. KPJ make me a superstar. I stand infront of my shetto ass steps looking down at them 'Please don't break' I mutter to myself. The 1st step creeks a bit. So far so good I think to myself. The second step creeks even more then the first one did. The 3rd step... all or nothing. I step on it the noise doesn't seem to be a creak but a snap. I stand on the moorch looking down at the steps. 'I need to get that fixed.' I say to myself just as I did before I left for England. Unlocking my front door and entering my home I turn on the nearest lamp to get some light in the place. I check the caller ID first... 11 new callers. I start reaching them off outloud to myself. 'Scott... Chill... Owen... Owen... Scott... Karen... Fuck you... Kevin... Sammers... Chill... Scott... Owen... Karen... Bitch...' Finishing off the names I am ready to crash on the coach. However I catch a flashing three on my answering maching and I press the play button. '1st message' the electronic female voice says. 'Yo Grester this is Chill I must have just missed you. Just wanted to wish you the best of liuck in capturing your 1st piece of PTC gold.' '2nd Message' 'Hey... Freddy... I guess this is your chance to prove me wrong. Best of luck I guess... Love you...' '3rd message' 'Karen again... Freddy.. Quit Wrestling. You don't have your title. Why can't I just have you all to myself?' Karen's wordin her last message piece through me like daggers. Chill and heer 1st message words of encouragement. Hope. Something I rarely get unless its from myself. I have almost always been my own worst enemy and my own best friend. Now a days though I have been getting plenty of support for whatever reason. Except from the person a short while ago came back into my life and everything seemed to be fine. Back in the day I held her and Owen in the highest of standard but that was years ago. Better... Happier... days long seemed passed. Suddenly I hear a crack and a snap. Pretty damn sure its the steps. I run to the front door swinging it open I see the steps broen and Chill laying on the front poch trying to get himself back up. 'You okay Chill?' as I offer a hand to pull him up. He accepts my hand and gets back to his feet. 'Yeah I'm fine.' 'Good because if anyone is going to kick your ass it'll be me not the steps' I jokingly reply. 'Don't make me bust a cap in your ass, homeboy.' Chill responds. 'See already resorting to using a gun... thug!' 'That's it Grest we's about to rumble.' Chill says jokingly. 'Okay besides your horrific grammerI know you're joking. Besides I have a match with KPJ so I'll let someone elsle take care of my light work.' 'I ain't no light work foo!' You can see Chill's head move bounce forward when he says that. 'Right' I say giving Chill an odd look. 'Anyway I just dropped by to wish you luck against Ken Partridge. I have to go pick up Sandra.' 'Alright man. See you around.' I shake Chill's hjand before I head back inside. I enter the house once again. The answering machine plays a new message. 'I hear you have a match against KPJ this eek. Don't you think you're a bit out of your league this time? Then again I'm out of your league as well. Maybe that's why you can't play your cards ri...' I had enough and deleted the message before she could continue any further. Karen wanted to be with me but only if I gave up my dream. That is something I can not do. Not even for someone as amazing as I once found Karen. That has long sense past as well. Kentucky or something as taken everything I found amazing about her away. I sigh in disbelief wondering if she is right. If I should just walk away from everything. No, I can't do that. I won't do that. I have tons of people supporting me now. Chill, Scott, Kevin, Samantha, Owen, and my fans. They aren't giving up on me just yet and neither will I. 'Neither will I' I mutter to myeslf. I had KPJ in the Freddy Crab at Reload. One on One he would have tapped out the only thing that saved him was Jay Robson. I do not fear him. I do not fear anyone. KPK you will make me a superstar. KPJ I don't care who the hell you think you are. Nothing is going to get in my way. 'Fuck you Karen.' I flip off the answering maching and then grab it angerily pulling it off the table and throwing it across the room. 'KPJ I may not be a super star but I hope you're ready for a fight.'
