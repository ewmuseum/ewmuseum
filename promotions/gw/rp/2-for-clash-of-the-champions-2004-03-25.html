(# 2 for Clash of the Champions)

25 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 25 The scene opens up to the outside of the gym. I was standing there, still sweating from my workout. I started walking down the street with my gym bag slung over my shoulder. I walked into my front door of my house and threw my gym bag onto the floor by the living room entrance. I walked into the kitchen and saw my mother making some lunch for me and her. “Hey mom.” I said to her. “Hi champ. Still pumped about your match?” she asked me. “Better than ever. And the good thing about it is I just met Larry today.” I said. “Larry, the Larry that you used to goto to practice your wrestling?” she asked me. “Yes. And he is still awesome. But his wrestling gym broke down and now he just goes to the normal gym. He told me he was going to try to be a wrestler.” I told her. “Oh. That man was always a nice guy.” she said. “So, what are you going to do after we eat lunch?” I asked her. “Well. I volunteered to be a teacher helper at the local school. If you want, you can come to. I’m sure the kids would love to meet you.” she said back to me. “Ok. I’ll come for a bit just to see them.” I said. I walked to the brown wooden table that was standing in the middle of the kitchen. I sat down on one of the chairs and waited for my mom to bring the food to the table so we could eat. My mom brought a plate of sandwiches and a bowl of pickles as I started eating instantly. My mom sat down and we prayed and then started. A few minutes later we were done and already putting the dishes into the dishwasher. “I’m going to the school now. Are you going to come?” she asked me. “Yeah. Just give me a second to change my clothes.” I said. I walked out of the kitchen and to my bedroom. I took off my shirt and pants and put on a white tank top and my wrestling pants. The pants were black with a blue belt. It had Roswell down one leg and then some red flames coming up on the other leg. I put on my black boots and walked downstairs and into the kitchen, cause that’s where my mom was. I saw my mom just opening the door so I ran to the door. “I’m ready now.” I said to her. “Ok. We will take your car. You can drive if you want.” she said back to me. Me and my mom walked out of the door and to the driveway where my car was parked. We entered the car and I put the keys into the ignition. I backed up out of the driveway and down the street to the school. I stopped in a large parking lot and stopped the car. Me and my mom got out and started walking towards the school. We pushed the double doors opened and walked into the school. A whole bunch of kids stopped what they were doing and looked in shock. But they weren’t looking at my mom, they were looking at me. “Hello kids.” I said to them. “You, your Max Roswell, from Global Wrestling.” one of the kids said. “Yeah. Why, do you know me?” I asked the kid. “Oh I know you alright. Everyone here knows you.” he said back to me. “What do you mean everyone know’s me here?” I asked again. “We all love you.” a girl walked out of the crowd with a smile on her face. “Ok, something’s not right here. Why do all of you love me?” I asked one more time. “In Global Wrestling. All of us watch Evolution every night it is on. Your are all of are favourite’s. And do you think we could all get some autographs?” the girl said. “Ok. Let me get this straight first. All of you watch Evolution. All of you love me. And all of you want some autographs?” I asked them. “Yeah.” all of them shouted. “Well, I can’t say no to kid’s who like me, so lets get some autographs.” I said. My mom had already been in the staff room where all the teachers would stay when they were not teaching there classes. I walked over to the kids and let them lead me to there classroom so they could get some autographs from me. I walked into a class room, and saw a whole bunch of desks with papers on them. I walked over to the corner of the classroom and grabbed a chair. I carried the chair over to a desk and sat down. The kids lined up in a line waiting to get there autographs. I signed a few and then a girl came up with a really big smile on her face. “Hello sweety. What’s your name?” I asked her. “My name is Sarah Hollowatti. I love you.” she told me. She handed me a picture of me with a big red kiss in the very corner of the picture. I signed it with my signature. I gave it back to her and she hugged and walked over to her desk. She sat down at her seat and put my picture in a frame and just sat there staring at it for a few minutes before going to talk to her friends. I signed the rest of the pictures and got up off my seat. I walked towards the door and stopped at the doorway. “Bye kids. I had lots of fun today. I’m really glad all of you watch me.” I said to them. I walked out of the door and down the hallway. I stopped at the staff room door and knocked on it. My mom answered the door and I walked in. I sat down on the big red couch that was in the corner of the room, and started reading the magazine that was laying on the coffee table. I opened the book as the scene faded.
