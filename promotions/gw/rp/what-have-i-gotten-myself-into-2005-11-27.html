What have I gotten myself into?

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/05 Oh my god, what have I signed up for? I'm a Team Captain going into this event. Showdown in Tokyo. One of Global's biggest shows of the year. Now only do I get to try to lead a team to victory in the first match, but then after that it's me and 8 others, including a man who can't win it, looking for a shot at the World Title, currently held by Christian Zenith. Zenith... My first round opponent coming up for PTC's GTT5, also. ARGH! What did I do to get all of this thrown at me in one time? Blaise is the leader of the KB:NAN, not me. Why didn't he get picked? Why not him? Why me? And then again, there is something else that I have to worry about... If we pass by the team led by Jake Hix, a man I've failed to beat twice now... Blaise is on my team, I asked for him. I wanted him on there. But can I face him in the ring during the finals, when it's every man and woman for themselves? Just when we're finally starting to speak to each other, and build some trust, do I know if I can truly trust him? But that didn't seem to be his worry now. It was nothing more then a side effect, an afterthought. Blaise had come to Dani with a need, and she had taken him up on it. The ride to Tokyo has been as pleasant as it could be. Dani had to tank herself up on tranquilizers quite a bit to get the 14 hour trip out of her mind. She slept through it as much as she could. She could never figure out just why she was so afraid of flying. It made no sense, but she had found out when she had been younger. When she had gone to the academy in Chicago. Yeah, most don't know this. But her time at the wrestling school hadn't been in the Dakotas. That was her home, she wouldn't deny that. But she had met Josh, and Gray in a small place in Chicago that used to be owned by another wrestling federation as it's training center. Today it's called the West Wind Academy and had previously been run by the man she considered her ultimate hero when it came to the wrestling world. That man bore the same last name as Blaise. Now it was coming to light. All the victories in the CCWA didn't matter anymore. Not even that title belt that sat over her fireplace. All that mattered was Global. All that mattered was two things right now. And she was torn between the two of them. On one hand, she had to beat Christian Zenith, the current Global World Champion to advance on PTC's little gathering's first round. If she did that, she'd not only move on, but also... she'd have a victory over the World Champion. How could Global deny that? It's not like the ego and greed of Zenith would allow him to put over anyone, let alone some woman who was still technically in her rookie years. Her second goal was to carry a team of 4 to the final match at Showdown in Tokyo. A team that held her and Blaise. But also held a man she thought was one of the dumbest in the world in Fred Grest, and also a man she didn't know, named “The Ladies Man” Jay Robson. Mr. Robson was supposedly some sort of hall of famer and former world champion. All fine and dandy, but it was a setup for potential failure. Grest had almost no talent as it was, and his attitude was one that he didn't care. Robson was a wildcard, she didn't know how to take him. That would mean that Team Queen had one thing going for it. Two people who would and could act as a team. They had before. Team King had nothing like that. Hix, although good, and had beaten Dani twice... appeared to always be out for himself. A loner, lone wolf if you would. MacFarlane was cool. She and Dani had spoken before. And there was something between her and Gray. That was fine and dandy, but she was not best friends or anything with the others. Josh and Walker? Walker was new, newer then Dani. And Josh had enough ring rust to keep WD40 in business for a year. She and Blaise had to keep things going for their team in the first match. It had to go down that way. As for the match after that... that's where Dani's leadership fell apart. She didn't know. ~~Tokyo, Japan~~ ~~Kushinaga Residence~~ Dani had come at the request of Ukyo. After the events of the morning, Ukyo had been left at her parents house, and Blaise had gone back to the hotel. Tomorrow was going to be a big day for Blaise and Ukyo. Ukyo appeared to still be in shock. Blaise had sideswiped this on her. While they had talked about it before, many, many times, it was still a suprise. Tomorrow, by Japanese custom, as well as that of his side of the equation, Ukyo Kushinaga and Blaise McClancy would be engaged to eventually become husband and wife. Dani was happy for them, and at the same time pissed off at Josh for failing his couple attempts to get up the nerve to ask her recently. Dani sat in a chair behind Ukyo as she was looking though some books at pictures of her past. Her mother, Ayame, had begun to find things and lay them out on the bed while her father started to prepare other things for the family dinner tomorrow. Her mother was very busy, trying to find all of the pieces of her own Kimono for the Tai-On, or Engagement ceremony from over 25 years ago when she and Yukio had done this very thing. Dani was there to talk to Ukyo, and to help Ayame with anything. It was an emotional time, but no male was allowed to be near Ukyo for this night, other then those of blood relation. The problem Ukyo was having, was that there was only her father there. Her brother Haru had died a while back, and her other brother, her adopted one... had been disowned by the family for his actions earlier this year. He'd also been the one that had injured Ukyo in their old federation right before Blaise had asked for their release. Ukyo was looking at pictures from her school years, of her and Haru while Dani tried to keep her head straight while she brushed Ukyo's lavender locks. “He was handsome.” Dani looked over Ukyo's shoulder at the pictures of the two siblings. “Yes, he was. He had all the women all over him. From growing up till his death. But he had many things that he wanted to do in life before he settled down.” Dani thought about that. The same reason she'd originally given Josh before she finally came to her senses. Haru had died, for all intents and purposes... alone. “That's a shame.” “Yes, it was. I think that's part of why I finally gave in to Blaise. Other then the fact that when we met, I caught him looking... but he never pushed. He worked on my self esteem. My confidence. But never once tried to put me down. And he was always a gentleman.” “Yeah, he seems to be a pretty good guy. I can see his cousin's side runs through him.” Ukyo giggled. “Yeah, he's been noted for that. Some say that he and Fey are the oddballs in the family.” “I wasn't talking about the Lady. I was talking about the headmaster of the Academy, Sean. He was always a gentleman to the ladies in the classes. Even if he didn't need to be. I'd heard bad things about him when I got out of Gray's class and moved up to the things that only the headmaster taught. But it was a good experience.” Ukyo nodded. “Sean is good. But he can live up to his namesake in a heartbeat if you cross him. I sometimes have a hard problem in remembering that when I see him though. For me, before I met him, it had been all videos of when he broke Blaise's knee in the ring. That night that he took the Superior Championship from him.” Dani gasped out loud. ?What? Is that how they do the fighting amongst themselves?? Ukyo stopped and realized that Dani hadn't been around for any of that. “No. Blaise hadn't joined the Order at that time. He'd actually sworn against it. He was in denial of his heritage of the courts and saw it all as one big thing to avoid. The others wanted him to join. It was after that, when he got his ego crushed a bit, that he figured out that he wasn't bigger then the Order combined. He was only a player in the games.” Dani was trying to figure it out. She had a question on the tip of her tongue, but couldn't bring herself to ask it. ?He seems pretty good. As I understand it, he was a student of the South Wind, and also passed the final stages at the big academy in Texas, right?? “Yes.” That didn't set Dani too well. ?So if we get passed this first match, he's going to kick my ass, right?? Ukyo stopped in her tracks of flipping the page of pictures. She hadn't been expecting that. Quickly she turned around in her chair and looked at Dani. “Well, in all honesty, by following the Code of the Order: eventually he'd have to try. You see, in the first match this coming week, Team Queen versus Team King, it'll be a team environment. You can count on him.” ?Why am I leader, and not him?? “Probably because you've been in Global longer. Blaise is still a loose cannon to them. He keeps taking down people, and whatnot. Global needs to make sure with him.” “I wish we had time to work out a plan.” Ukyo took Dani by the shoulders. “Oh, please. You do. After tomorrow night, I'm going back to the hotel with Blaise. Don't ask for him then...” That brought up a slight bit of red on Dani's shoulders. ?But the next day, all we need to do is ask. I know the Dojo's around here. I grew up here. I can get you ready to work with Blaise. I've been working with him as a team mate for over a year now. I know him better then his cousins and the trainers at the Academy's do. You'll be ready.? Dani pulled back a tear at the show of support by her newest friends. ?Yeah, but what about the second match. What about when we have to face each other?? Ukyo smiled. “Well that's where it gets complicated. You're team mates. And you're on the same team. The code of the Order and the Brigade would call that both of you work together...” ?What? How can we win then?? “Easy... let me finish. You would work together. Until the ring would be clear. Once the other 7 in that ring are gone, then it's time to come down between you two. Then it's may the best Brigadier win.” “That's what I'm afraid of.” Ukyo smiled. “That's nothing to be afraid of. If you're going to get an honorable match in Global, it'll be with him by far. He'll shake your hands before it, and afterwards. As long as you give it your all. What you should worry about at first, is Hix and the people on his team.” “I'm really worried about Hix. So I figured I'd let Blaise take care of him.” Dani smiled wide at that comment. ?Ask him, and he will. He might prefer you take on Ms. MacFarlane. You seem to have a slight rapport with her. Blaise has never been one to just take a fight to a woman before, if he has no reason to hate her. He can, just doesn't see it as a very good thing to do. Besides, Gray might not like it if he dropped her on that pretty face of hers. The other two, just kick their ass. ? Dani nodded. “That's the plan. I find it funny though. I've lost to Hix twice, I've beat Dawn in a cluster match. I've seen the others. But the one I'm dreading possibly being in the ring with, will be your husband to be as of tomorrow. I don't know why” I don't know if it's his name, since I learned from a McClancy. I don't know if it's because I've seen him fight. Or even if it's because I know him personally, now. Or maybe it's because he has the balls to do what Josh can't. I'm not worried about the first match per se. I'm worried about the final match. And only about one person.? Ukyo then asks the question that Dani expected. ?What about Zenith?? ?What about him?? That's the answer Dani gives. The two girls laugh. “You know, Dani. I think I'm going to call the hotel. How do you think Josh would take it if I asked if you would stay tonight? We can do a girls night here. You'd have to leave before the ceremony though. Maybe you'll catch a shot of Blaise in his dress whites.” She giggled at that. ?I think I'd like that. Why do you ask how Josh would take it?? “Well due to the ceremony, Blaise isn't going to be having company in his bed tonight. If you're here with me, Josh isn't either.” “Good point. But he'll live.” ?Good, let me call then. Do you have clothes?? “Yeah. Old habit. Never go anywhere without at least a change of clothes in my bag. From my days when I'd go hiking on a moments notice.” “That's good.” ~The End~~
