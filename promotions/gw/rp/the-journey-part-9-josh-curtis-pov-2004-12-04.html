The Journey: Part 9?Josh Curtis' POV

4 December 2004

Date: 12/04/04 Early Morning How are YOU doing?! I know its been a little while and the irregularities of our meetings is getting to be worrisome because of how often you don’t hear frome me but fear not my friends for I’m hear for you all in spirit and in body! Now as we last left our favourite group (OK more like one my favourites but hey) we were having some problems as Joshua was struggling within himself as he was preparing for one of the greatest mental struggles of his life in the match at SiT. Now its no secret that Team Elite, which is headed by one of Joshua’s close friends in Ken Partridge Jr., had asked him to be a part of their team and Joshua had agreed to do it. Its when Joshua discovered that there was a second part to this match where if his team wins then they get put into an “Every man for himself” match and that is something that Joshua doesn’t like. Fact is that Joshua is loyal to his friends and that is something that he considers Ken, for Ken was the first ever tag partner that he had in Global Wrestling and the fact that he might have to go and potentially hurt him wouldn’t sit with him well because he has in fact basically said to people in private that if he had a choice of bustying Ken open or knocking himself out he’d d o the latter. Why has Ken affected Joshua so much? Not even Joshua knows because of the fact taht he has a boyfriend in Zack but Zack is ok with Joshua having Ken for a friend and the concern that he has for him because fact is that Ken is worried about Ken also due to Joshua’s really bad outbursts and that sometimes causes him to lose complete control and severely hurt people! Now is it necessarily a bad thing that Joshua is going to have to hurt people? No because its the nature of the business but what does concern him is the fact that he has gotten close to Ken and if he injured him to the point of not being able to work forr a long time....its best not to think about it right now! So here Joshua sits in his room by himself and looking at the emptiness of the space that he and Zack live in when they are at the residence of “The Connection”. Zack is out for the day with some friends of his and the rest of the gang is out as well so he can think about his life and the time that is coming for him. Tpkyo, Japan is like a home to home to him and the fact is that he has wrestled at all of the big venues in Japan when he was just starting out. A name that he earned when he wrestled there was “Golden Child” because of how YOUNG he was when he first started wrestling there and long before he wrestled with his sexuality! Josh needed to learn the art of submission wrestling and that was where he got his education. From Masa Hiro Chono to Mr. Siato and even the high flyer Jushin Liger tutored him on submissions but the fact remained that no matter how hurt you made him he wouldn’t quit! It was impossible for Josh to swallow his pride and actually say “I QUIT!” because he was too proud! So Joshua was going through his closet and finding his karate gi that he ws going to take with him on this trip. Now one thing that I must mention is that Joshua is taking this trip alone! Yep that’s right Joshua isn’t going with any Connection member save maybe Zack but that’s mainly because of the fact that there are things he wants Zack to see when he goes through his training and that even includes a run up Mt. Fiji and yes I said RUN! Is this always a good thing to do? It can be for Joshua because he wants to stay emotionally neutral and with some shit that has gone on in his life he just needs people around him to be able to talk to him. Why do you think Joshua likes walking the streets of New York City? Because its the fans that drive him to be so open with his emotions and say what you may want to about his lifestyle choice but there are very few people that can push their bodies to limits that are sickening like him! Joshua has been known to run 7 miles in 30 mph winds AGAINTS THEM just to learn about resistence, he would do 75 situps hanging upside down from a par, flip down in a kinf a backlip and land in a push up position to do another 75 pushups EACH of standard (on the palms of your hands), knuckles and “claps” (whe you push up and then clap before you come down). Is it normal? No but not much about Joshua is normal! Joshua prides himself on doing exercises that no normal person would dare attempt because of the insanity that it involves! So why do it? Not many people would dare answer that question but Joshua always answers it with the standard “Because the fans wouldn’t expect less.” and he’d be right! The fans night in and night out at hte house shows and the other arenas that he fills in his off time expect nothing but! See people have their expectations of the “Rookie Sensation” and when he goes away to places where people know and respect him its not about how famous he is or was but its about the joy that he sees on the little kids faces when they say “Mr. Curtis could I please have your autograph?” and Joshua could never turn them down, for if he did he’d become what he hated: An egotisticcal manica that beleves he is better than anyone and everyone when in fact he is nothing more that a common man with the gift to enter the ring and please people! After running throught the checklist of his belongings Joshua, clad in a white dress shirt with black tie, Arizona Blue Jeans and a pair of black Nike “Twenty” sneakers. grabbed his bag that already was tagged for JFK Airport in New York and walked out of his bedroom and out of the house. Did he really havea plane to catch? No but he figured if anything he was going to get some sleep before the match so he packed a pillow and a blanket for the plane along with the rest of his wardrobe on the trip and walked out of the residence with a note neatly tucked away in an envelope taped to the front door before getting in his 1996 Dodge Viper and heading off to the JFK Airport. Now Joshua isn’t one for making a reservation on a plane but the people there know him so well that he never needs to make one and they always hold a plane for him OR if he requests sometimes a jet! “SO if Joshua is supposed to be so humble then why the special treatment” you ask...well I’ll tell you! See a couple of years back when Joshua was actually hitting strides in England and Australia one of the managers of the airport needed a loan to keep the place going. Joshua being the good natured person that he is decided to help out the place and sent them the money that they needed and since then they said if Josh need anything from the company to just ask! So as Joshua made his way to his car and threw his suitcase and duffle bag in the back seat he made sure he had absolutely everyrthing and the one thing he made sure to have was his cell phone on him to call Zack which he did so he was all set to go. So as Joshua embarked on his drive to JFK Airport he put the top down (yes its a convertible) and drove comfortably all the way to the airport and when he passed people on the highway or they passed him he would beep and wave at them and the liitle kids always got a good feeling when they saw him because they knew who he really was! When Joshua got to the airport about 30 minutes or so later he reached into the back seat and grabbed his bags before opening the driver’s sidedoor and walking out and handing the keys to the parking attendant whom graciously bowed and stowed the car in a safe place so it wouldn’t get stolen. Now when in public Joshua doesn’t try to hid whom he is and he lets anybody walk up to him to talk to hoim about anything: Life, sports, his relationship and even his family! Why be so accessible when most famous people aren’t? Because his mother back home in England taught hime to be polite to everybody and anyone that couldn’t accept him as a person needed to just be ignored and walk away from it. As Joshua walked through the airport he looked at when flights where leaving for Tokyo so he decided that he’d go with United Airlines today and the person at the counter was more than willing to help out. “Greeting sir and how may I help you today?” The young laday said as she was standing on a box to be able to see eye to eye with Joshua “Is it possible to get me on Flight 291 to Tokyo, Japan? I need to get there because I’m wrestling at a show for a company called ‘Global Wrestling.” Joshua explained and without saying a word the lady produced a ticket for Joshua as she knew already whom he was. “Here you go Joshua and have a good day...oh nad if Zack shows will make sure he gets there for the show too!” The young lady said and Joshua stood there bug eyed for a couple of minutes before regaining his composure and taking a seat while wating for the plane to board. In the interim a woman whom appeared to be in her mid 30s was holding a young boy’s hand and he almost looked the spitting image of Joshua to boot! God how Josh probably wishes for his youth back and not the bumps, brusies and oh yea blown out knees that he has now but he made the career choice so eh lives with the consequences. “Excuse me sir but is this seat taken?” The woman asked and Joshua shook his head no before the lady took her seat and let the young man sit next to Joshua. “Hey mom isn’t this Joshua Curtis from ‘Global Wrestling’?” The young man asked and Joshua turned his head immediately to talk to he young man “You’ve heard of me?” Joshua asked somewhat puzzled but then again “The Connection” has fans of all ages, so it shouldn’t come as too much of a shock. “Of course dude! Mom will only let me watch your matches or segments though because she says that you are the classiest person there.” the young man said and Josh laughed a little at that because he knew that his segments weren’t THAT clean but clean enough for kids to see them. “Well thank you. Its always good to have a fan and I’d haveto tell you that GW fans are the best, the may be a little bloodthirsty but they mean well.” Josh told the kid and his mom decided to join in on the convo. “So how does it feel to have a boyfriend?” The woman asked and Josh was very truthful with her “It feels great actually. The one main drawback is that you get criticized sometimes for sleeping with your gender but Zack and I are good people...matter of fact I think I baby sat your youngest daughter last week if I’m not mistaken coorect?” Joshua asked the woman and she nodded before replying “That’s right Joshua.” The woman, whom is postively identified as Brandi Walker and her youngest son Greg. They were waiting for their father when Josha’s flight got called for boarding he made sure to have time to sign something for them both. “To my best clients and friends havea safe Xmas and an even safter New Year” Joshua L. Curtis And with that he handed the two photos of him and Zack together to the mother and son before walking off to catch his plane.... Who says you can’t go home again?
