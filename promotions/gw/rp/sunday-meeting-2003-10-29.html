Sunday Meeting

29 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/29/03 Millions of men gather together for one day each week for a common purpose. Something drives them to join each other to celebrate an event. Friendships are born by a common love and rivalries collide in a solid hate. But why does this happen over other people’s blood, sweat, and pain. The funny part about it is that the outcome of this event will not change the life of the people watching directly. They just simply walk home almost as if nothing happened. What is this strange event? Sunday football. This Sunday is no different for these two men sitting 3 feet in front of the television, slouched back in their second-hand furniture. Johnie Ringo and his old friend, Trey Reynolds, are enjoying the second half of today’s big game of the Denver Broncos vs. the Minnesota Vikings. So far the luck hasn’t gone so well for the Broncos. A 21-7 deficit is not where you want to be halfway through the 3 rd quarter. “You just can’t deal with it can you?” Johnie blurts out. “What” What can’t I deal with?” Trey fires back. “The fact that the Broncos suck ass. They haven’t been good since they had Elway and you know it.” Trey leans to the right side of his wooly red chair to face and points at his comrade. “Bullshit! Griese did a good job when he took over and they made the playoffs that year.” “HA!” Johnie stays still with beer in hand and merely tilts his head off to Trey’s side. “The only reason why you started following them was because they won the Superbowl. Big whoop! You just hopped on the bandwagon. I was surprised you didn’t leave them for the Rams the next season.” Johnie takes a sips a bit at his Corona and looks back at the television as Denver receives the kickoff. Trey leans back offended, yet still confident. “At least my team’s been to the dance, baby! You’re still following a sorry excuse of a team. Have they even won this season?” “Hey!” Johnie snaps towards Trey. “The Cardinals have some hope yet. They at least play with some heart.” Trey can’t help but laugh at that defense. “What heart” They have nothing. You kept telling me, “Oh, Emmitt’s there this year! He’s gonna shake the team up. He’s not too old. He’ll get the job done.” What has he got them? He’s even out for the rest of the year! At least Denver has some talent.” He turns back towards the TV and sees Jeff Beuerlein throwing a ball right into traffic as it is picked off. Trey jumps up and yells back at the screen. “NO!! You idiots! Stop him! Get him! FUCK!!” Its funny how people scream at the television and think that they can change what’s going on. Johnstone of the Vikings runs the ball back and scores a touchdown from the Denver 33. Trey just collapses back to his chair holding his head up with his palm. Johnie can’t help but grin. “You were saying about talent?” Trey doesn’t move much. He’s probably just glad his skin’s dark enough to not show his red face. But he merely raises his head towards Johnie and goes back to his sulking. “shut up...” Johnie laughs a bit as he rises to head towards the fridge in the next room. Well, not really the next room. Trey’s apartment would be considered a one-room dump with four walls, had it not been for the hole the landlady called a bedroom and bathroom you can barely move in. Of course its got a kitchen that is only divided from the “living room” by the carpet line running from the door to the bathroom. Big change. As Johnie opens the fridge he views nothing more than a case of bottled Coronas and some Tupperware. Johnie lifts up a small square Tupperware box and slowly cracks it open. He sees something that looks like it was topped with cheese, but it had fur on it. Shocked, he burps it back shut and tosses it back to the netherworld of the fridge. Trey’s voice echoes to him from the “entertainment area” “Hey Johnie grab me one too.” He shakes off the horrors he just witnessed and snatches two bottles as he heads back toward the chairs. Johnie hands one over to Trey and he cracks his open as he stands watching Jason Elam kicking a field goal. “3 points closer to a win, baby!” Johnie nods in simple response as his brow bends in thought. “Hey Trey...what would you say if I asked to move in here?” Trey grimaces as he pulls back his swig of corona and gocks at Johnie hesitantly. “I would have to ask why?” Crouching down in the hard blue chair beneath him, Johnie looks down at the beer by his feet. “Because I’d like to move in. Get out of home. Get somewhere.” “Why here, man?” “Its a nice place.” “It’s a dump, dude!” Johnie leans back into his chair staring at the screen again. “Its better than the street.” A smirk peeks across Trey’s face as he glares back at his friend. He already knows the reason why he’s asking. Johnie knows this. Still, he’ll have to play the game. “But you don’t have a job, bro. How are you gonna help pay rent?” Johnie’s eyes droop reluctantly downward. He doesn’t know that though. Maybe there’ll be something? “I’ll figure it out,” he says. Trey just looks back and keeps playing along. “Well, I do have a couch open.” Johnie smiles a bit, “Its a start.” *BEEP EEP EEEEP* That annoying ring can only be a cell phone. The racket is coming from Johnie’s jacket. He reaches to the back of the chair and pulls the phone out of the right pocket. He flips it open, raising it to his ear in contempt as he saw the call ID. “What is it, Jenna?” The static on the other end is deafening, but that doesn’t mean he still can’t hear his sister. “Where are you, Johnie” Mom’s all worried and upset.” “Is this where I’m supposed to care?” He hears Jenna’s grumbling groan and smiles. Good. She’s mad. “Well you should care. She’s your mom too. Anyway, she’s only trying to look out for you and help you find what’s best for you.” Redness flushes over Johnie’s cheeks as he gets up for this one and heads to the back of the room. “What’s best for me”! Bullshit! She wants what’s best for her. Simple as that. She wants another person to leach off of and I’m not gonna be the one to be it. And if you’re just gonna be there to help her then I have nothing more to say to you.” “But Joh...” Johnie didn’t hear that. He had already closed his phone and hung up. Trey looks back at him and smiles. “Is that why you want to move in?” He sighs and shakes his head in impatience. He starts to walk back up to the front of the room again, but the phone rings again. Johnie’s eyes bulge out as he whips the phone back to his ear. “Listen Jenna, I’ve had it with this shit! If you’re just gonna defend her then I’m not talking! Understand”!” “Interesting choice of words.” The voice is different? Its not Jenna, but its not a voice he’s heard before either. “Who is this?” “Well, certainly not Jenna, of course.” The voice is a little contemptuous, but he has a point. “Answer me!” “Settle down, kiddo. You’re gonna pop a vein.” He’s right again. Maybe he should settle down. Johnie starts to control his breathing a bit better, but still sounds firm. “Well then tell me.” “That’s a good boy. I can’t answer that now, I hate doing this over the phone. Come and meet me at the parking lot a couple blocks south. You’ll see a black 2004 Mercedes parked near the back. Just stand beside it. We’ll talk there.” “About what?” Johnie prods. “About a job.” *Click* Johnie lowers the phone from his ear. His face is full of questions, but no answers. He starts walking towards his chair. Trey’s still watching the Broncos trying to drive the field at the start of the 4th quarter. “So what did Jenna have to say?” Like a zombie, Johnie picks his jacket off the chair and starts heading towards the door. “I gotta go.” “Go” Go where?” “I’ll tell ya later.” Johnie turns the knob as he tosses his leather jacket on. Spinning around, he closes it softly as he leaves. “See you in a few.” Trey just sits there in utter silence, other than the announcers babbling on as usual as Jason Elam backs up for another kick. “Well I hope so.” The kick is up, and its good! 28-13, Vikings. “I don’t want you to miss the comeback!” To be continued......
