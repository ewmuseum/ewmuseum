The Max Attack: Chapter 12

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Roswell is seen walking out of the stadium with a Metallica shirt on. Roswell is kind of limping because of him jumping a lot. Roswell starts walking down the really dark street to his hotel. Roswell lifts his arm up and looks at his watch. “12:30. Oh my god! The concert went that long.” says Roswell. Roswell now starts walking faster so he can get to the hotel without getting kicked out of it. Roswell gets inside the hotel right at 12:45. Roswell runs to the stairs instead of taking the elevator. Roswell gets to his room and walks inside it. Roswell sits down on the master size bed and lays down resting from all the noise and jumping at the concert. “Holy, that was the best concert I’ve ever been to. Even though it was my first but still.” says Roswell inside the empty room. Roswell takes his shirt off and sees that his muscles have gotten bigger. Roswell starts posing with his muscles into a large mirror. “Why have my muscles gotten bigger” Roswell asks his self. Roswell gets into his bed and by the time everything is calm he falls asleep. IN THE MORNING Roswell awakes and gets out of the bed and starts stretching. Roswell yawns once and then puts the shirt he was wearing yesterday on. Roswell walks to the curtain and opens it looking into the bright city. The alarm clock goes off and the red numbers read “11:00.” “Already 11 in the morning. I never sleep that long” says Roswell. Roswell walks out of his hotel room and out of the hotel for some fresh air. Roswell starts walking down the sidewalk humming Neverland in his head. Roswell walks by the billboard Metallica was on but some painters are putting white paint on it. Roswell hears a noise in the sky and looks up. He sees something flying around a skyscraper. The thing that is flying drops something onto the building and the top of the building all of a sudden explodes! Windows shatter every on the street and people are seen jumping out their office windows killing them self’s when they hit the ground. “I thought he was in jail!” yells Roswell. Roswell starts running towards the building. Roswell walks behind the building where no one can see him and changes into his Max Attack suit. Roswell jumps up to a fire ladder hanging from the building and starts climbing it to the top. Max reaches the top and kicks the flying thing so it lands on the building. The top of the building where Max and The Blue Bat are is glazing in flames so they jump into the air again. Max and The Blue Bat start punching and kicking each other. The Blue bat throws one of the little balls at Max but it misses him and hits the middle of the same building. The Blue Bat gets a sick smile on his face and starts flying away from Max. The Blue Bat lands on the Sears Tower and grabs a little ball out from his pocket! Max rushes as fast as he can but The Blue Bat drops the ball! Max lands on the Sears Tower and lets the ball hit him straight in the arm! The ball blows up and Max goes flying back into a door. “See you in hell, MAX!” yells The Blue Bat. The Blue Bat flies away and out of sight as Max lays on the ground not moving. Max’s legs starts moving a bit signally that he is not dead. Police Officers start coming to the top of the Sears Tower but they don’t see Max! The police officers leave and Max comes limping out of a room at the top of the building. Max is holding his arm and it is not really moving. “I think my arm is broken” says Roswell is a low voice. Max opens a different door and walks down the stairs inside it. Max is seen inside the Sears Tower with his suit all tore up. His mask is ripped and you can see some of his face. Roswell sits down on a bench, and is still holding his arm. “How did he get out of jail” asks Roswell SCENE FADES
