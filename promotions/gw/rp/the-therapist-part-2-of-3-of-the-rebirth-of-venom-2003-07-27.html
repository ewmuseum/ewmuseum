The Therapist Part 2 of 3 Of the rebirth of Venom

The Therapist Part 2 of 3 Of the rebirth of Venom Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #471 Date: 7/27/03 Location: Toronto In a little office building, Venom is sitting in a comfortable bed chair. Talking to his therapist, about his issue and problems. “Now Venom, what is your problem?” calmly as the therapist is asking Venom. “My problem is that you keep repeating yourself, what is your problem? You must have said like ten times already.” Venom then turn to the therapist, sitting up on the bed chair. And looks her in the eye. “What is it Venom?” Asked the therapist. “It’s everything, my best friend passed away, then I get involved in a wreck when my girlfriend was with me, she didn’t make it. Seems like my life has hit a brick wall, and I can’t seem to pull myself away from it.” Venom stand up looks out the window, starring at the sunrise. Pacing back and fourth. “Something wrong.” Asked the therapist. “Since Vyper returning, all the attention been on him, sure he is a one of the reason why I started wrestling in the first place. He is a legend but, sometimes I wonder what it would be like if we need up crossing paths in GW.” Venom stare at the therapist waiting for a reply, but nothing. The therapist is just writing something on her little notepad, Venom walks over tries to take a peak, but the therapist turns away to prevent Venom from seeing. “Well, Venom it looks like you are still stuck in the past. That something inside of you wants to be released, and you are trying to stop it.” Venom just look at her, wondering the hell she was talking about. How is Venom stuck in the past, and what wants to be released from him? “Doc, I have no clue what the hell you are talking about, how I’m I stuck in the past, the past of what?” Venom heads for the door, and turns around to the therapist to tell this session is over. A few hours later, Venom start to think maybe, the thing that wants to be release is he alter ego. An ego that would cause some of the fans to hate him. However, he starting to like that fans respect him for what he does day in and day out. The respect where the phrase Mr. V.A would come into use. How did Mr. V.A come about, back in Venom’s old days in TCW, he lost it, he felt that he wasn’t being treated fair like the other wrestlers. So he wanted respect from all that he would step into the ring with. Venom demanded all to call him Mr. V.A, that he was a superstar on the raise. However, eventually Venom was fired from TCW for his ego. What would happen if his ego got the best of him in GW, how would Roz react to it when he returns, maybe if Venom’s ego returns, it would help Roz and Venom in the tag title hunt? Venom finally make his way home, to get a good night of rest. “Home sweet home, I swear that therapist she didn’t have no idea want she was talking about.” As Venom talking out loud to himself he makes his way to the answer machine, he finds he has one new message. “This message is for a Mister Alexander, I am from Dell you have a payment past due, please give us a call.”Venom take the answer machine and throw it up to the wall, breaking it in little pieces. His anger building up inside, he runs to the bathroom. And gets out a bottle to claim him down. Venom then heads to bed to relax, after his long week of worries. T.B.C
