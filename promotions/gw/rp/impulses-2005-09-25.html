Impulses

25 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/25/05 The Order had been good to them, and the last few weeks have shown the new, higher morale on the teams of Unite and their 'little sister' (in attitude only, not for real), Dani Northsky. Last week the boys showed up the team of the Enforcers, a long standing tag team in Global to gain some points in the Tag Team Tourney that was now going on in Global. One team down, more to go though. This week they've got a new singing called 'Divine Darlings'. Two women who are becoming very stereotypical in wrestling today. They're both highly trained, and both into each other. You'd figure they'd let Shadow and Dawn McFarlane have some hold on that gimmick in Global, but they'll let more in. But last week's victory for Dani was the one that scored the largest boost in morale. It's no secret that the boys would wish any success on Dani, she's been the one that's been riding the higher paid contracts since her debut last year. And now in Global, she secured her first title run, taking the Platinum title away from a man(?) named Virgil Ambrosi. The 'boyfriend' of one Lia “Shadow” Vlassy, one that the team has already had a few run ins with already. Shadow has a win over Dani, and Josh. And it's known that for Gray showing some interest in the affairs on her paramour of the feminine kind, Dawn MacFarlane. But his interest was not in her for a romantic type. Some know that these people also serve a higher organization in their endeavors. And that is right now the research into just what a woman named Dani Northsky really was. Now that has come to pass, and she's shown her true form. But it only lead to even more questions on top of the answers it granted. So for now, they'll take it easy and get their lives situated after moving into the future here in Las Vegas. One thing that the Order has taken care of, is a certain technicality with Mr. Joshua Bodaway Brand.. His suspended driver's license from Washington State. His shiny new one is for Nevada, even though he never took a driving test here, let alone went to the Department of Motor Vehicles. However, with the connections that the Order has, no one will ever really know the difference. The good thing is that the two states don't actually talk to each other. Now that he can legally drive again, there is only one more thing to take care of? What do you get when you hand one Joshua Brand a blank check for picking out his own car? One pissed off, and bored out of his mind, Gray Brand. Over the last week since returning to Las Vegas, Gray had driven his brother to EVERY car dealership in the Vegas area, especially those that have cars that would fit Josh's personality more. We now find the two brothers sitting in what looks to be a Border's Book Store, in the coffee shop. Located in a little off shoot of Las Vegas, down on the south side, also known as Henderson. Gray has a frazzled look to him, while Josh is sitting with a pile of papers on the table. Both men dressed in jeans and t-shirts. Josh is sporting a bruise on his face, from the impact of one Virgil Ambrosi's fist with his face last week's show, as Josh stopped him from hitting Danielle with a chair for the umpteenth time in a match. Josh sucks down some of the liquid energy from a cup of expensive coffee swill, as Gray starts to give him crap for taking so long. ?Okay, you going to make your decision this year?? Josh looks up at his brother and laughs “What, it isn't like buying a pair of jeans, I am going to have to enjoy driving this thing around for the next few years at least. Gotta pick the right car. Especially when they've basically given me a budget that would put a car in that garage that actually belongs parked next to a house like that.” Gray just shakes his head, as he grabs one of the sheets of paper in front of Josh. “I still don't think this car is a good idea. You are just getting your privileges to drive back. I don't think you need to really get something like that to encourage your previous habits that took them from you. But then again I am sure you will talk to Sirius about things.” Josh looked down at the papers “Not all things. There are some things still remaining. But we've talked about the car, and she supports anything I choose.” Gray raised an eyebrow at his brother. ?You haven't given it to her yet?? Josh hesitates to answer. “What are you waiting for? You've never done anything non-impulsively before.” Josh doesn't bother to recognize that last snipe at him, and just gives a simple answer, but one that shuts Gray up on the subject. “No, I'm waiting until I can talk to her grandfather first.” This being said, Gray knew that Josh, was thinking things though for once in his life. The half-breed that Josh was, he knew that Danielle's grandfather might not approve of their, affections, towards each other. Then again, little did Josh know that based on the old traditions as they were, no matter what Josh was: Joshua and Danielle would already be husband and wife, hand fasted together by their actions. Josh sat fiddling with the stack of papers. As he did his brother just sat and looked at him with a smile. “I would say your scared but I know better. At least your not rushing into this like you have rushed into other things. Although we do need to talk about your actions last week, still.” Josh looked at his brother and tilted his head a little. ?My actions?? “Yes your actions... last time I looked decking the guy wasn't in the code. You were not supposed to get involved in that match. The bell to bell rule states...” Josh put the papers down and folded his hands. ?You need to look at the code again big brother. It was a no DQ match up. The Bell to Bell Rule that we follow states that if it's against the rules, in between bells you don't do it. With no DQ, everything was far game. Besides he used the chair first, I just kept him from using it a second time. Dani tried to keep from using weapons against him. He kept pulling her hair, and trying to hit her with that chair. So I just evened it out.? Gray just shook his head one thing he knew about his brother he followed the letter of the law, and followed it to the letter. There was no loose interpretation with him. He basically did follow their rules, but he didn't want Dani to think that Josh had managed to assist her in some form in getting that title off of Ambrosi. He waited for this brother to continue. “Our code, is set clear the match was a no DQ, you know as well as I do what that means. Hell, our own headmaster at the academy became the true Hardcore Icon for a reason. He used the No-DQ rules to his advantage. He was the hit man for the Keeper and his boys, and while they were in a No-DQ situation, they were targets.” Gray sighed he knew his brother was right. But there was one thing that was gnawing at him. “You do know that Virgil will be looking at a way to call foul. And you might of given him that. He'll demand a rematch, and try to block us from being at ringside.” “How? How can he call foul? And if he wants a rematch, he can abide by the rules and ask for a regular goddamn match. Not some crappy gimmick match. Regular rules.” Gray huffed out loud at his brother. “How can h e call foul? Oh lets see, Distracting him, followed by that kick that almost took his head off. I'll admit, you hit that side thrust kick just like you used to on the kick targets. Right on the money, and right with maximum extension.” “Thanks, but if he allowed himself to be distracted by my words, that's just tough. He should be more focused, didn't you tell me that when Danielle used her assets to distract me in school.” Gray just nodded again, he knew his brother would have his words ready for this talk. He just continued. Part of his brother's training was making him think about what his impulsive actions brought about for him. ?Oh and Calling Lia what you did, won't bring them down on you or Sirius?? Josh still answered him quite matter of factly. “Danielle is a big girl she will handle what ever Lia has to send her way. If Lia wants to take it up with either of us, she'll be all to happy to. I'll smack her right on the ass again, and laugh over it.” Gray just shook his head. One thing about his brother never changed, he was cocky as all hell. “What about that Kick? You know that Virgil isn't a player by the rules like us. We've got a match this week, I'd expect him to get involved in some way now.” Josh just fired off another response. “Hey the man hit me first. Only thing I did at first was grab that damn chair from him. For it, he punched me in the face.” Gray responded. “You're too hot headed for your own good, sometimes. Maybe you need more training on the control of that temper.” Josh just smiled. ?You'd not be the one to give that either. But, remember, I only give what I receive big brother. Trust me, Virgil wasn't exactly happy with the fact he was getting his ass kicked by a mere girl. Remember he is a man's man, if you can call him that. Does the manly equipment even work on a vampire? I know they can't procreate. He's probably been turned down by Lia more times then there are beds in the Mirage. I am sure that effects the mind as well as the equipment. Plus Lia seems attracted more to Dawn. We all know she prefers carpet to the pole, which doesn't help Virgil's ego any. To know that your driving a woman into another woman's arms. Makes me wonder how many Women he has done that to. After so many years, I am sure the list is long.? Gray just chuckled at the comment and turns his head to look out at the traffic moving along Sunset Blvd. “We are going to be needed to get back soon Sirius is going to be wondering where we are.” Josh just shrugs. “At least she is sleeping better. Her dreams are finally settling to more peaceful ones. God knows what Jaymes did to her would of given any one nightmares. She is a tough woman.” Gray took a drink from his Italian soda, sucking the last bit through the straw before moving the conversation on. “That she is. So that drawing your working on...” Josh interrupted him before he could finish. ?What about it?? Josh takes a sip of coffee from the cup sitting next to him, then sets the cup back down before picking up the papers again. Gray moves to finish his thought. ?Your new tattoo?? Josh shrugs at him. “Haven't decided just yet. But had to draw what I had seen. It was too much of a vision burned into my mind.” “I am glad you did, she is beautiful. It's too bad I missed it.” “Yeah, I am sorry you did also. But you had an appointment for swimming lessons with a hot blonde life guard. But, as for Dani, there are no words to describe her like that.” Gray just smiled, there are never words to describe something that is so beautiful. Go ahead and try, and you'll be at a loss for words that will truly do it justice. But, for the first time Gray sees his brother at a loss for words on anything. And, that says something for a smart mouthed kid that always likes to get the last word in... on anything. As Gray thinks about the changes going on in his own life, and those of his brother and the woman he likes to think of as a little sister, Josh suddenly says something that catches him off guard. “I am thinking of getting her a gift. Something that is fitting of someone with wings.” Gray tilted his head. “Come on, I'll show you what i mean. I have done lots of research on this, and I think I know what to get her and where to get it from. Oh By the way, This is the car I want.” Josh throws down one picture, of a 2006 Pontiac Firebird. Josh gloats a bit as the thoughts of such a car. “Fitting name for me. I think Black with Black interior. But for Joshua Bodaway Brand, my middle name is the Salish word for 'fire'.” Gray just shakes his head. “Yeah, fitting name and all. But, a black car in Vegas? You're going to be roasting on the freeways, but just remember keep it under eighty on the freeways. They allow up to 10 over down here.” “Thats why they make them convertible big brother. Put the top down and use the 80 mile per hour air conditioning.” Josh smiled wide at his brother. “Alright, I'll submit my stuff to Annie about what car I want. Other then that, you're going to drive me one more place. I need your opinion on something.” Josh started to get up from his seat. Gray didn't get up right away. ?Josh, that's fine and dandy, but a quick question for you. We have the Divine Darlings this week, they know about your weakness for women.... you're going to be focused and ready, right?? Josh smiled at him. ?I'm a one woman, man. I've got Dani. She's what I want. I don't care about these two carpet munchers. There are so many of them around, it's not like I'd not know if they did anything that it wouldn't be a sincere offer. However, big brother and mentor... I'm going to respectfully request permission to do something in this match this week.? Gray looked inquisitive. ?And that would be?? Josh smiled wide. “When I wind up and get a practice swing in, don't stop me. I want to make sure I'm ready for Shadow when she pops in on our business. I'm expecting it, and when I give her another red turkey, she won't know what hit her.” Gray laughed at him, openly and fully. “Hey, but I'll get a couple practice swings in on the two sushi serving girls.” “Oh, I sure hope so. I do so hope so.” Both men get up from their chairs and head out of the coffee shop.
