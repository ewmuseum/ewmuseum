The Max-Attack: Chapter 3

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The scene opens up to the white and grey air plane that Roswell was in. Roswell is sitting in a red leather chair with no one beside him. Roswell is looking right across from him at the man with the coat on. A lady at the front of the air plane grabs a microphone and says that the air plane will be leaving a 5 minutes. Roswell looks back at the man and gives him a confused look. The man is seen taking a gun out of his suitcase but no one can see it. The man takes out a clothe and starts wiping the gun making it cleaner than before. Suddenly the air plane starts rising from the ground and starts flying from the spot it was in. A couple of minutes later the air plane glides through the air going through each cloud as it moves. Roswell’s eyes start closing and he falls asleep right there on the chair. Suddenly Roswell opens his eyes but it is not in a air plane. He is standing in the middle of a wrestling ring with none other than Deacon Holmes standing beside him. Deacon then turns to Roswell and levels him with a punch. As Roswell hits the ground Deacon locks in The Final Flash. Roswell wakes up and shakes his head. Roswell looks across to see the man also sleeping and snoring. A lady walks by with a clothes pin in her hand. She clutches the clothes pin onto the mans nose as the snoring stops. Roswell starts dozing off again but this time sees a CAR! A car that was parked in the middle of a highway. But it wasn’t a normal car, the car was on fire. Roswell who knows this is already a dream walks into the picture. Roswell walks over to the car but no body is in it. Roswell then turns to the left and sees a ambulance with paramedics beside it. Roswell walks over to the ambulance and sees a stretcher with a man on it. Roswell asks the paramedics what happened and who the man was. The paramedics replied with “A car crash. And the man’s name that was killed is: Doug Roswell! Roswell suddenly wakes up sweat dripping from his forehead, and hands tight together. Roswell wipes the sweat off of his face and turns to the man again and is still out like a light. Roswell whispers to his self that he is not going to fall asleep again and he just going to read the new Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix. Roswell takes the book out of his suitcase and opens the book to Chapter 1. **1 HOUR LATER** Roswell stops reading the book and puts a bookmark on the page he stopped on which was Chapter 20. Roswell puts the book back into his suitcase and looks at the man yet again only to see the man waking up. Roswell turns back and looks out of the window to see the air plane landing. The same lady at the front takes the microphone again and says they will be landing shortly. Suddenly the plane stops and hits the ground with a screech. A stair way is lowered for the people to get off onto the ground. A couple people walk down it and then comes Roswell trudging behind the man. The man calls a different kind of taxi and drives off. Roswell quickly does the same and follows the man yet again. The mans taxi stops at a stripper club. The man get’s out of the taxi and pays the driver. He then walks into the stripper club. Roswell gets out and also pays the driver and runs into the club. Roswell sees the man walk into a door that says “Employees Only.” Roswell walks through the crowds of people even running into a drunk guy passed out on the floor. Roswell makes sure no one sees him and walks into the door where the man went into. “Who’s there?” sputters Max Roswell into the darkness of the room. Roswell cannot see one bit ahead so he calls out again. “Who’s there?” A shuffling noise is made from behind Roswell. Terrified, Roswell turns around toward where the door was. With the door open, part of the light from outside seeps in but as Roswell looks, a large, dark, shadow is covering the light. “MUWAHAHAHAHA!” The figure in the doorway pulls out a ball shaped figure with wings. The mysterious figure takes the ball with wings in his hand and throws it in the direction of Roswell. The ball hits the concrete floor and an explosion rings out through the area. Roswell is lunged backward and hits a wall, falling to the ground.A screaming pain fills the body of Roswell as the figure in the doorway turns around. “See you later, Roswell!” The figure quickly disappears through the doorway and out into the world. Roswell sits motionless against a concrete wall in a room. He looks over to his right where the ball that the mysterious figure threw lies. Using his one good arm that isn’t in pain, Roswell picks up the item and looks at the black, charcoaled ball. The ball looks like a regular nerf ball and because of this, Roswell doesn’t see how it can cause so much pain. Just to be sure however, Roswell takes the ball and places in into his pants pocked struggling to get to his feet. Once Roswell has gained his conscience, he gets to his feet. “You will pay. Whoever you are!” With that, Roswell steps out of the room into the world ahead. Placing his hands in his pocket, he feels the item and walks off, a new enemy in his life...
