The Appeal

18 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18.02.06 The court room is pretty much deserted apart from at the two front desk, and the African-American judge sitting on his chair looking over the proceedings. On the right hand table sits two men, both in very expensive looking suits with chiselled jaws (the kind of men that are models in perfume adverts), and both looking pretty annoyed as they browse through the numerous pieces of paper that they have assorted in some kind of order on their desk. On the left hand table sits another man dressed in a not-so-expensive looking grey polyester pin-striped suit, he is much different to the other two lawyers. At about 5’7 he resembles a child sitting at the large table, his dark hair is dishevelled and he looks like he hasn’t slept for a couple of days. The Judge bangs his hammer on his desk beginning the proceedings. “Order in the court’Right, Let’s get this over with,” the judge said as he looks down at the paper in front of him, “This is the case of Fazz McLovley Vs. the criminal justice system.” The judge looks down over the top of his glasses at the man next to the dishevelled short lawyer, “Mr. Green, can you please wake your client up?” And’indeed’the man that was sitting next to the dishevelled lawyer was in fact sound asleep. This bloated, dirty excuse for a man was face down on the desk, his long blonde, matted hair partially covering his face as his loud snores echoed through the large wooden courtroom. “Of course your honour, I apologize,” replied the lawyer. “Fazz, for the love of God wake your ass up!” Mr. Green shoved the large oath by the shoulder a number of times trying to stir him, but to no avail as the snores continued. Mr. Green, embarrassingly looks at the Judge with a forced smile, before leaning down by the side of his chair and picking up his briefcase. With another quick glance at the judge, and at the prosecution lawyers he raised the briefcase up and slammed it on the table. “Mr. Schwarzenegger, I’ve got your macaroni, just don’t spank me?” shouted the sleeping man as he was suddenly awoken by the large impact on the table, “What the f***? Where am I?” “Fazz, you’re in the appeal meeting, remember?” said Mr. Green. “Appeal what now”...Did you slam something?...I’ve warned you about that’the pacemaker’s only good for another 3 months’Who are those guys?” Fazz asked, seeming very hungover. “They’re the prosecution, Fazz.” “Mr. Green, is your client fit to continue” the judge asked, getting slightly annoyed with the freak show happening in his court room. “Of course, he is. I’ll start with my opening speech if that is ok?” The judge nods at Mr. Green, “Thank you kindly. Your honour, I have a clear reason for a mistrial verdict, in this very briefcase. As we all know my client was imprisoned 6 months ago for a number of incidents, not least including indecent exposure, being drunk and disorderly, illegally handling of an exotic bird, and driving an aeroplane without proper license.” “Damn right,” came a call from the prosecution desk, “He almost killed that poor parrot.” “I was just flying with it’Jeez, can’t a guy steal an aeroplane anymore without getting “punished??Anyone got a drink going?” Fazz slurred, as he opens up his lumberjack jacket and from the inside pocket pulls out a bottle of Jack Daniels, “Never mind?” “Fazz, pack it in” The judge shakes his head at this sight, and then looks down his paper once again. “Mr. Green, you say that you have a clear reason as to why Mr. Mclovley should be released from prison?” Asked the judge. Mr. Green pulls out a piece of paper from his briefcase, and holds it high up in the air for everyone in the court to see. “Yes, sir. If you look please at the original statement that was given by my client, Mr. Mclovley, you will see at the top of the page the name of the officer that took the statement a Mr. Patrick Rogers. However, if you look closely your honour he has actually spelt his name Padrick Robers, and not Patrick Rogers which is and has forever been his name.” ??And this is your case?”” The Judge asked, shaking his head with the pathetic level of the appeal. “Yes it is your honour.” “In all of my twenty years of being a judge I’ve never heard something so ludicrous, something so anal, just in the hope of getting a mistrial. Although I must admit it does have very, very mediocre grounds of actually having a case. So I am very confused as to what to do.” The judge sits there stroking his chin, “I’ll tell you what. I don’t want to stretch this thing out in the major courts, and cost the tax payer thousands of dollars in expenses. And god knows, Mr. Green, your weasel little ass will push this thing until one of us is dead.” “You’ve got that right, sir” replies Mr. Green. “Then it is my decision, as judge of this court to give Mr. Mclovley a little thing us modern judges are calling “Creative sentencing”, I’m sure you all say the thing about the donkey and the students, and other such things happening down south.” ??Nah I couldn’t have stolen a donkey’I mean I tried, I REALLY tried’but I find them to be less co-operative than parrots, move hooves, and tail and stuff.” Pipes up Fazz from the seat where he simply been downing JD while the recent conversation happened. Fazz coughs noticing that everyone is now staring at him after he sudden outburst. ?*coughs*I mean’Please, carry on your’holiness, honour, type thing.” The Judge looks at Fazz with another disapproving head shake. “I remember Fazz, when you were wrestling in the big leagues, everyone loved you’Everyone loved Salomane Vation, and he was just weird. You used to light up the crowds, you were electric’you and Mr. UK, goddamn, my kids used to have your t-shirts and dolls and merchandise crap that I spent a good deal of money on from those thieving GW bastards. So it is my decision, and the decision of the court that Mr. Mclovley will be released’on one condition. He must get back in shape, and get his wrestling career back in line. If he fails to do this then I will be more than happy to slam his ass back in jail. That’s all” The judge banged the hammer on his desk closing the session, as everyone in the courtroom looks on in complete shock, and I mean complete shock, their mouths were wide open and everything. As the Judge stood to leave, Jimmy Green shook the sheer horror from his face, before grabbing the jug of water on his desk and nervously pouring himself a glass before downing it. Fazz sits there, smiling. “You can’t be serious’I mean look at him, he’s a fat drunk!” Mr. Green says, terrified. “Hey! I don’t call you names’You skinny, runt like man.” Pipes up Fazz, annoyed at the attack on his physical being. “I am serious Mr. Green, and as his lawyer I am making it your responsibility to make sure he carries out this order. That is all’good day gentlemen.” The Judge leaves as Mr. Green slumps back into his chair, and the prosecution leave the court room also. Fazz sits there with a massive smile on his face, almost beaming about what he has just heard. He stares into space, possibly reliving his moments in the ring. “I’m gonna be a wrestler again’I’M GONNA BE A WRESTLER AGAIN!!! I’ll drink to that.” Fazz then downs the remainder of his bottle of Jack Daniels, before smashing it on the table. He jumps up from his seat and goes in front of the bench, apparently doing some kind of “I’m gonna be a wrestler again” dance. Mr. Green just stares at this sight, looking almost suicidal. ??I’m gonna be fired again?” He cries, before slamming his head on the desk.
