Happy birthday

9 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/9/04 The date was January 9, 2004. Thirty years ago on this date, a baby boy was born to James and Coretta Randall at precisely 11:04 p.m. They named the boy Jamal James Randall, but Global Wrestling fans know him as Brick. This day, Brick celebrated his 30th birthday, a birthday many would be surprised to see him reach, given the intensity of his chosen profession, particularly the types of matches he’s known for. Many call him “Mr. Nine Lives” for a reason. Everyone was there to celebrate: Aaron, his wife Felicia and their daughter Ashley, Brick’s mother Coretta, his sister Monica and her husband Kyle, and his singing group DMAK. They all sat around the table as the cake came in. The cake had chocolate frosting with “3” and “0” candles in the middle. Written in icing around the candles was “Happy Birthday Jamal”. “OK, somebody get the light,” said Coretta. Kyle reached for a light switch and turned it off as Coretta lit the candles. “OK, everybody. One, two, three.” Happy birthday to you Happy birthday to you Happy birthday, dear Jamal Happy birthday to you Then everyone cheered and applauded as Brick blew out the candles. Just then, DMAK broke into a rendition of Stevie Wonder’s “Happy Birthday”. Happy birthday to you Happy birthday to you Happy birthday Happy birthday to you Happy birthday to you Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday “So, you think you’ll get your wish?” asked Aaron. “Hopefully.” “I wish you didn’t have to get in the match with Mike Page again.” “Mom, I’ve taken that punk’s worst and survived. Hell, I’m celebrating my 30th birthday today. That should be proof enough.” “But you know how crazy he is,” she replied. “Mom, Brick knows what he’s doing,” said Monica. “Besides, I’ve got Jesse watching my back.” “That doesn’t exactly ease my concern, Jamal. You remember he did turn on you during your fight with the Harvesters.” “Yeah, I remember, but he and I are past that. Besides, he’s got just as much reason to want to kick Terminator’s ass as I do. Plus that punk Hardy has an ass-whipping coming to him, too. But hey, why are we ruining the day? Let’s just enjoy the rest of the evening.” “Amen,” said Aaron, as Brick started to cut the cake. “Wait, Jamal,” said Coretta, “shouldn’t you open your presents?” Aaron reached into his pocket and pulled out a small wrapped package, handing it to Brick. Brick shook it a bit before unwrapping it. He opened the box to find…a pair of bifocals. “You know you’re not getting any younger,” Aaron laughed. “Oh, nigga, shut up! You ain’t but two months younger than me, and you know I’m gonna get your ass then!” “Watch your language in front of the baby.” “Oh, OK. Sorry, ALF.” “And stop calling her that!” “This one is from me,” said Monica, handing Brick an envelope. Brick opened the envelope, reached inside and pulled out a certificate, which read: “This certificate hereby states that JAMAL JAMES RANDALL is officially older than dirt.” “Very funny, Monica. Wait till your birthday comes up.” “OK, OK, now for a serious gift,” said Coretta as she handed Brick a small package. It was a collection of contemporary gospel songs. “Thanks, Mom,” Brick said as he kissed Coretta on the cheek. He then went on to open more presents. This was how Brick wanted to celebrate his birthday: with the people he loved, and not a worry in the world. Because in the days to come, he’d really have to concentrate. On the 9th of January, Brick celebrated his 30th birthday. A week ago, Michael “Terminator” Page celebrated his 40th birthday. This year may be the last birthday for one of them.
