In Search of the Talisman Known as Platinum

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-14-2003 Venneman Pendragon, true king of The Land, last of his line, walks with his eyes wide through the burning desolation that was the village of King’s Point. The crackling of burning wood, burning bodies and burning hope fills the air, accompanied by the wails of the newly widowed women. Here and there, small groups of the former goodwives huddle close, taking what comfort that they can from the warmth of one another. Pendragon’s eyes slide past them, taking in the entire grisly scene that has manifested itself upon his beloved subjects. Suddenly, he stops and bends to the ground. He lifts a half charred doll from the soot and holds it in both hands, staring. A solitary tear forms in his right eye, growing and growing, until finally the eye can hold it no longer and the tear spills over onto his cheek, rolling down his noble face. He walks on, still holding the doll. Eventually, he makes it to a small group of armor clad soldiers standing before a smoldering mound. Lord Benjamin is among them, his many wounds wearing white bandages in stark contrast to the battle grime covering the rest of his body? “My Lord King’Every man in the village, killed and either burned or eaten. Your horsemen too adorn this very hill. Not the burial that men expect’None should go to the clearing at the end of the path at the hands of the Fangspawn”? He spits after growling the last word and wipes his mouth upon his arm. Pendragon pats him on the shoulder, a comforting gesture, before he moves on and shares a private word and touch with each man gathered there in turn. Tears do fall, even from these men who are hardened from war. Rage mingles with the sadness and multiplies the intensity of the emotion. Finally, Pendragon stands himself a bit apart from the others, his head down, eyes closed, as he faces the crude mound of man flesh? “By the hand of the divine creator, thee men were brought forth into this land of both beauty and danger. To be taken from the Land by the hands of the Demon lord, is an affront to all things just and right in this world. Fangspawn, born from the darkness, sent to ravage the land by Lucifer himself’Truly this Broadbane is at one with a great evil. And yet, even in this, our seemingly darkest hour, there lies hope. Hope for thy souls still, and hope for all of mine people whom inhabit this wondrous land.” Another tear breaks free of it’s eye prison, and is joined by yet another and another as Pendragon delivers this simple eulogy for the brave men who have fallen in battle against the darkness that sweeps across their land’Some people believe that Pendragon is a hard man, seeking only to glorify his own name and gain honor for himself. This is not entirely true. Few possess the strength of heart as our Lord King, and few would remain committed to causes as strongly as he. The man is truly a king and a warrior’A throwback to when deed would become myth, and myth would form into legend. His type is forgotten in our world, and nearing it’s extinction in his other world. The last of the True’The last of the White is he? ***********FLASH*************** The Wizard Merlin stands atop a great hill, peering down at the spectacle of the burned village below. Out of sight of the army, he weeps a bit himself. He gathers himself as he looks directly into the shot?. “My friends’Down there is a man who will stop at nothing to defend the honor of the weak. Some of you fine folks view him as insane’To your twisted ideals of honor and righteousness, I guess that he is. His honor is to him what your breath is to you. And you would piss upon his name as not belonging. His land needs him, but I daresay that your pathetic domain would need him more. Too few of you have the strength to do what is right, rather than what is easy. Too few of you have the character to step up, when all around you fall like flies. I, too, have been guilty of downing my Lord. He has his reasons for GW, and I believe that I now see them. GW is a stage. A stage watched the world over. Venneman has chosen GW out because it reaches a broad base of people’Children to adults watch the sometimes violent sport. It isn’t right that all they should become accustomed to seeing are those who are the very dregs of society gaining glory, gaining respect. GW too, realizes this, finally. Pendragon, if nothing else, is admired by the fans. A loner by nature, he garners everything on his own. Few have the will to make it in the business alone, and yet he perseveres. There are many in GW who would see him fail. Many who would not relieve themselves upon him were he aflame. Yet still, he is as dedicated as anyone to the glorification of his home. And finally, he is being acknowledged. His quest for gold and glory finally come together. He faces what can only be described as a scholarly thug. With as many enemies as friends, Train is a master manipulator. His greatest strength is his slyness. It will be of no avail against my Lord. Pendragon is single minded in his pursuits, and the wake of destruction he leaves is the proof. And yet, he is torn yet between the worlds. He will fight for his throne in the land, at the same time fighting for good in your world.” Merlin motions towards the farthest reaches of vision with his staff? “He is the Lord of this land, and yet he fights to carve a niche of goodness in your world. Ruler of all he surveys here, but to you he is a herald of better days and better times. Were it a perfect world, those things would once more come to pass’That is his fight. He fights for his people here, and for you he fights also. He fights to allow you to see that there is good yet in your slowly dieing world. The measure of a man is the good he does, and the lives he touches. Were this so, physically Pendragon would bestride the world as a colossus?” Merlin lowers his staff, and lower his head also, in a silent prayer for the two worlds’One young and endangered, the other aging and dieing? **************FLASH**************** Another hill, in another place. Pendragon sits Xerxes, staring down below through his spyglass. Lord Benjamin sits next to him, hands crossed upon the pommel of his saddle. Pendragon gives a grunt and places the spyglass back into one of his saddlebags. Benjamin wears a dark look and his voice matches the picture his face paints? “Why do we wait” Take the cavalry, swoop down and crush the rabble. What is such a decision of that?” Pendragon looks at his companion and shakes his head? “There is an army of ten thousand, made up of the Fangspawn, men and their evil overlords. Our army would swoop down upon that mass of evil, and we would break like water upon rocks?” “But with you at the head’We would?” Pendragon’s eyes blaze? “We would all die! That is all that we would do! Thy emotions have thee in their grasp, and thou needs to thinkest with a more clear head! My heart grieves for those already lost, but must we join them” Yonder army is a juggernaught that was raised with destruction as its soul purpose. Broadbane has no true want for mine throne! He is but a puppet for the Lord of Darkness. Long has he strived to lay hands upon the land, and now it appears that he is getting his chance. Certainly Broadbane has been promised mine kingdom for his troubles, but the will pulling the strings is a far greater bane to the land than he. Swoop down and wipe them out??Twould be a noble and yet utterly useless end to us. After we go, who will stand before the land and eternal darkness?” Pendragon shakes his head and begins to ride off, Benjamin following him. Berated, Benjamin’s next question comes uneasily?. “And what of your magic” Some of your most powerful ancestors were wizards’Or at least sorcerers. I know you have some magic’Why not use it?” He cringes, waiting for the explosion, yet Pendragon only looks at him quizzically? “Would thee like me to tell to thee a tale” An ancient tale, and unhappy for thee? True, some of the more archaic Pendragon’s were magic wielders. Each moment in the Land, my past becomes a tad more clear, and I remember more. These ancient forebears of mine line drew their power from a most unlikely place’Platinum.” Benjamin cocks his head?.“Platinum?” he says questioningly? “Platinum” Pendragon replies. “The magic was inside, but Platinum was the key. It was the catalyst for the magic to be concentrated. Now, though, there do be no platinum in the land?” “And what is this Platinum?” “A precious metal. Hard to find even in mine other existence, and a truly noble metal. Not just any Platinum will do. It must be Platinum garnered in a noble pursuit of just and goodness. One can buy platinum in some places, but Platinum is what you make it. Platinum’GW?” “What is GW sire?” Pendragon’s eyes flash as an idea strikes him?“We have to get to the castle keep’I need a look at some old manuscripts! I think I have a solution”.” ***********FLASH************ Candles illumine what appears to be an archaic library. Minus the cobwebs one might normally associate with medieval booklore, this library is quite clean. Venneman Pendragon sits behind a largish pile of books, his eyes taking in the text of seemingly four at one time. “It has to be here somewhere’Damned story’Where is it?” A smallish man enters the room. Clad in a brown robe, he appears to be a monk. He cocks an eyebrow at Venneman Pendragon?. “What is it that vexes thee so, my Lord” Is there anything I can help you find?” Pendragon looks up from his work and frowns? “Only if thee can direct me to the account of Lord King Xavier Morningstar Pendragon’Preferably his use of Platinum to defeat darkness!” Pendragon leans back and smiles a look that says “Hey! I’ve been all over this damned library, so I doubt you are gonna be able to help me” as the monk smiles and nods. He heads over to a chest, opens it and pulls out a leather-bound book. He brings it over and sets it down in front of a flabbergasted Pendragon. “Here you are, my lord. “A Time of Magic?”.I hope it helps you?” The monk turns to leave and Pendragon shoots him a look before tearing open the book, scanning it for what he wants. He flips page after page, until finally he smiles?. ***********FLASH************** Chapter Thirteen’Noble Platinum and the Morningstar? Roland DeStern, Chronicler? And so it came to pass, that as the darkness and hatred of the Dark Lord began to take a firm hold upon the land, the magician, Merlin, set Xavier Pendragon, the Morningstar, on a quest with dire consequences upon the line. The noble line of Pendragon is one of magic, yet wild magic. Untamable without the help of a special talisman. Platinum so mine lord called it, and nearly unattainable. Merlin spoke of places where Platinum was plentiful, yet impossible to get. Thus it was, that my Lord Pendragon set out upon his quest. IN the year of our lord, twelve hundred and five. He needed to reach what Merlin called another plane, and the doorway to this place was guarded by a great dragon. If my lord Pendragon were to slay this fearsome beast, then the glory of the conquest would give the platinum he retrieved the talismanic powers that he needed to help the land. The Morningstar asked Merlin why he could not use his magic to defeat the Shadow, and Merlin answered, most ruefully’He said “My son, I am but a conjurer, and imperfect as I am, mine magic hold inherent imperfections. Your magic is wild and perfect. Anything done with magic by your hand would be impossible to corrupt. It is for this that you must seek the platinum. The glory of your deed will make it whole, and you shall use your magic to defeat the shadow.” To this, my lord asked Merlin why he could not bring this platinum back on one of his trips into these strange lands. Merlin told him it was because the deed was the key. He would await Pendragon at the other side of the portal with what he needed, and after the dragon slaying, the platinum would be Platinum and would be his to save the people. Thus it was that the Morningstar traveled to the Mountains of Morning, so named because of the view they present of the setting sun. West traveled he to face the dragon, and the dragon’s name was “Trainer”. A truly unstoppable force. With him I rode, as I was the chronicler, and these deeds needed to be recorded. When finally we reached the dragon’s mountain, we saw the littered remains of many warriors. Armor scattered here and there. Bones. Trainer was an unstoppable force, and none were able to best him, until mine lord. Standing brave at the mouth of the cave where other, weaker heats would quail, the Morningstar drew his sword, Excalibur. Holding it forth, he called forth the dragon??TRAINER! I command thee to show thyself! Thy fate awaits! Out thee now! We have business?.” At the end of his command, his blonde hair blew as a warm gust issued forth from the mountain cave. Pendragon raised his shield, and just in time too?.From the mouth came a stream of flame. It deflected off of the shield, and Pendragon held on for dear life. With a snort, the flames stopped and a huge black dragon came forth from the cave’In a rumbling voice, the dragon spoke??None but the lord of the land can defeat me’Go home before I become hungry.” Pendragon lowered the shield and raised his sword, which then glowed with a white fire of it’s own. With a yell, he rushed the dragon, sidestepped to flame shots and plunged the sword in the beast’s breast. Stunned, the dragon fell over dead’Wiping the blade as he pulled it free from the carcass, Pendragon strode into the cave. Moments turned to minutes, and minutes to an hour. After what seemed to me an eternity, my King returned with a dull metallic belt strapped to his waist. With a nod, he pointed at the belt and smiled??Twas then I knew that all would be right in the land’My King defeated the powerful Trainer and came away with the Platinum that he needed?”” **********FLASH******** Pendragon’s eyes blazed as he read the old chronicle. As he finished, he leans back in the chair, a wondering look upon his face’With a start, he comes back to himself? ? “Twas then I knew what needed to be done. To save my land, I needed Platinum. It is odd how two worlds can sometimes come together. GW was second on my agenda, and then it was suddenly pushed to the fore. I needed to defeat Train. I needed the Platinum. I would go to the portal to get back to YOUR world. I would take what would be mine and I would save this land, and make a name in the other’Perhaps, along the way, I could save my own heart and my soul. After all’Anything is possible in fairy tales?”
