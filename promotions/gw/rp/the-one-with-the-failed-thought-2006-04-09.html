'The One With the Failed Thought.'

9 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 9th 'The One With the Failed Thought.' It'd been less than a week following Evolution and another victory had come to Rich Sherwin. His career was rushing from strength to strength, knowing no direction other than up. He'd disappoined a lot of people with an upset loss against Arik Idol in his official debut, but only three short weeks following that loss, he'd transformed from something of a joke to something that was looking solid. 'Captain Retirement' was evolving into something that had 'Potential' written all over it. With the right direction and the right choices, there is no doubt in most fans minds that this man could be a future Champion of the company. How long until such an event would come to pass? Not even they could give such an answer. With all of four days to go until his next 'Triple Threat' challenge, Rich knew that he was going to have to be firing better than ever before if he wanted to extend his win-streak to a rather smooth three. You see, Evolution of this forthcoming week was going to be something a little different in comparision to his other contests with the organization. This week, he'll be up against somebody of the same weight classification... and then somebody else, who was without any doubt, a mighty heavyweight. J.C Diamond; the man from Queens, weighed a rather good 225lbs. He was the lightest going into this bout, Sherwin being heavier by all of 10lbs. Brick though, he tipped the scales at a significantly vasted 280lbs, giving him a major advantage over both the 'Captain' and the ghetto-like person. The scene opened in what appeared to be a gym, 'Captain Retirement' being the only figure in view. He stood around, stroking his chin, trying to figure out a few things internally. It'd probably be a safe bet to go with the information thrown around prior to this but meh, I was told that being a rumour maker was never the greatest thing to be. Sherwin remained still, clad in a pair of loose fitting shorts and a pair of white nike trainers. His facial expression carried that intense look of thought; the rest of his body reflecting the sheer magnitude of tension. 'Brick; 280; Heavyweight... what would be the easiest way to come through and destroy him? Would it be wise to attack the legs and cripple the subject? Would it be wiser to attack the head, forcing him to either tap or be knocked out? No, that'd idea could prove to be very counterproductive, more so because the contest in hand isn't going to be one against one..,' Sherwin said to himself, his voice carrying a distinctive tone of thought. Standing straight for a few more moments, the thoughts and ideas entered his mind, desperately trying to piece themselves together. '... If I have Brick against the ropes, I'm always going to have to contend with Diamond... and if the person against the ropes is vise-versa, I'm going to have to contend with a black man that's both angrier and bigger...' Trying to come up with a solution to the problem that he undoubtedly faced, Sherwin sat down on the long bench which had been conveniently put into place when nobody was looking. Seconds passed, minutes followed although the hour didn't come anywhere close as Sherwin struggled to find an idea, find some form of solution from a prior point in his career that would help him defeat both a legend in the eyes of GW AND and up-and-coming superstar. 'Captain Retirement' thought long and hard, his attention waving when certain people walked past his bench. 'I got it!' Sherwin shouts with delight, jumping up from his conveniently placed bench, striking a muscular pose of gold upon making it to his feet. 'Got what?' screams a voice from afar, the hammering of footsteps getting louder as whoever it was got closer to out intrepid hero. 'Gah!' Sherwin responded, dropping to the floor like a sack of potatoes as the screaming voice KO'ed both his train of thought and determination to succeed on a hypothetical basis. '... Got what?' the voice again exclaimed, this time in a tone that wasn't quite as loud as before. Sherwin rolled over onto his back, looking up and around, his eyes adjusting to the brightness. The poor soul hadn't been knocked over or anything... instead, he was just trying to make things dramatic and have a little sympathy spread over him by the many lonely middle-aged wives that trained at this establishment. After looking at the lights, he looked over to the hovering face of someone... of something. 'Hello, what are you?' he asked, trying to sound as calm and collected as possible. To you and I, this individual was nothing more than an overweight being. This person, was just another fatty that had come through to this gym, looking to burn off about thirty stone with little to no real effort or commitment. Apparently dazed and bewildered though, Rich couldn't quite figure out what he was looking at. '... Are you the angel? Are you some kind of oversized behemoth that has been sent down by God and asked to cart me away to the big ring in the sky?' Silence ensured, the large person being unable to fully comprehend the question that was being thrown in his general direction. Regardless of that though, the tubby guy still decided that it'd be worthwhile to give a dignified response. '... Do you enjoy lying down on the floor or do you find that he helps you get rid of fat a lot easier.' 'Of all the intellectuals we have at this place... the fattest one of the bunch has to come along to try and make it seem as though they're doing something productive.' Rich said to himself, rolling his eyes in sarcastic fashion, the tone of his internal voice complimenting the visual sign perfectly. Too bad this wasn't something that Sherwin actually said to someone. It may have proved worthwhile. '... I didn't think that lying around and doing nothing was really going to be a productive way to lose weight.' the fat person said, Rich looking somewhat disgusted at the way his words were forming. 'You think I'm lying down here for a reason?!' Rich exclaimed, a look of amusement spread across his face. He chuckled somewhat, thinking that the prior statement was nothing more than a childish joke. 'If you think I'm lying down here for a reason, then I'd dread to think what you consider it when you're at home!' The fat person was caught completely off-guard, Sherwin taking the comments of the larger individual as though they were sarcastic and filled with some form of petrol. The silence that followed ensured us that the words had hurt both people. Rich, perhaps better off than the fat person, still harboured resentment at the comment of lazyness, whilst the fat man... well, he was looking worse for wear, having been proverbially ripped by an apt comment. '... You're a meanie. I'm going.' 'Rememeber, don't stop until you feel like a feather.' The fat person, as nameless as the pre-modifier suggested, turned and ran from the GW athlete, his arms waving too and fro, a minor sobbing sound coming from his general direction. Rich picked himself from the floor, looking on as the large individual ran into the distance. 'A-HA! NOW I'VE GOT IT!' Sherwin screamed, his arms raised in the air in triumph, as though he'd managed to win some kind of special award. 'Thanks to that big fellow, I think I have a plan as to how I'm going to get around that 280lb punk! The only problem now, is getting somebody to listen to my plan.' Desperate for the attention of someone, Sherwin dropped himself back onto that ever-so convenient bench. He looked around, left and right, hoping that somebody would catch his attention and absorb his uncanny information. 'Hey there!' Sherwin shouted, noticing a rather dashing fellow walk past where he was sitting. The guy turned around and took a seat next to Rich, looking rather excited and overly interested. Unfortunately for us, he was something of a geeky-looking thing, a pair of glasses and the sort completing his uniform. 'Y-y-y-y-y-y-y-y-yes, Rich?' the geeky guy said, stuttering on almost everything prior to this. 'W-w-w-w-what c-c-c-can I h-h-hel-help with?' he pressed forward with, still stuttering and spluttering as though he were an old man without proper treatment. '... O-k, you're a freak for knowing exactly who I am without my introduction, but I'm really not in the mood to care about that for the time being. Instead, I need you to do me something of a small favor. Is Jimmy ok doing that... or is it going to be a little problem?' Sherwin said in a slow and sarcastic manner. The nerd nodded his head repeatedly, desperate to do something that could benefit someone... especially someone in the position of Sherwin. 'YES! YES! YES!' The nerd screamed, his voice filling the entire gym with delightful screams. The wrong person may have thought that something was going on with the intensity of the screams but with the number of people around, they decided to ignore it... for the larger part of things. 'Right, anyway... I think I have the ultimate plan to rid myself of a big angry, fat black guy. I don't know if it's going to work exactly, so I'm going to ask you for a few hints... and an opinion on the plan that I've decided to come around with for Evolution next week.' Rich began, the nerd nodding excitedly as Rich pressed forth to the actual plan. 'Ok, I have to take on this guy who is like, 280lbs on Evolution and I'm thinking that the only way I'm going to be able to get through to him is by insulting him for his weight.' 'WHAT? YOU MEAN STUFF LIKE, FATTY?!' the nerd screamed again, this time sending shockwaves of hillarious laughter through the establishment. The move sent Rich into a visual state of shock, the athlete moving further and further away from the nerd as his eccentric attitude began to get the better of his behavior. '... Well, no, but because that flows along the general lines that I'm aiming for, I'll just nod my head and agree.' replied the GW athlete, smiling and nodding his head in a slow but respectful manner. Fearing that another outburst would come along, Rich decided that he was going to try and make this meeting as quick as possible. '... Oh, I know what you're talking about! I think you're going to do REALLY well against this person!' they replied, causing Sherwin to remove himself from the bench. 'That's... great.' Turning away from the geek, Sherwin decided that the only way to escape was to run off in the same direction that the fat guy had prior to this. He figured that if something as fat as that could disappear, he could do something of the same thing. Dejected and unimpressed that he'd not been able to get clarification of an idea to distract, defeat and destroy the 280lb Brick, the scene faded deep into the confinements of darkness. : End :
