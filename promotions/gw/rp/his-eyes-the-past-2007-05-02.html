His Eyes: The Past

His Eyes: The Past Nathan Boswell Global Wrestling Promo #3102 Date: 5/2/07 Location: Boswell's mind Nathan Boswell’s eyes close to prevent him from facing reality. He doesn’t want to think about the terrible things that have happened to him recently. He doesn’t want to think about his loss. So he closes his eyes and thinks about the happy times. ---- 9/21/2003 She stands at a corner, her glassy eyes fixed ahead at the intersection where she currently stands, a tattered backpack at her side loosely. The cars steadily passing become quicker and quicker, transforming themselves into a beautiful rainbow pattern, colors seen and just as soon forgotten by the folk making their way through the commute of their meaningless existence. When the cars stop finally, halting the art in motion and becoming just plain vehicles, a few walkers start to cross the street, not thinking as their blank expressions suggest. She also wears a blank expression, but she remains immobile. Her eyes don’t register the free walk, or the people passing her. Her eyes don’t even register when a heavyset man accidentally sends his shoulder into her back, pushing her forward a bit in his quest to pass the street. However, her mind registers and she begins to make her slow but sure pace between traffic. She counts in her head, which makes it from her brain to her vocal chords, which ultimately lead to her whispers of “three, four, five, six,” and so on. Another man, this time slightly skinnier and much taller, looks at her back as he approaches her, his face mangled in what some would call confusion. Upon seeing her face, however, he nods and continues. When she reaches thirteen, she stops and takes a sharp right, one reminiscent of a private in the armed forces only without the buck calls. She restarts her counter, beginning anew as she walks slowly down the crowded street. She hits the shoulder of a young woman, her blond hair pinned up into a bun held fast by a revolting Indian pattern clip. Gasping in disgust, the woman instinctively shouts “Watch it, bitch!” and moves to the side, ready to attack. Upon seeing her, the woman does not sober in expression, but instead rolls her eyes and leans over to the person standing conveniently next to her, whispering, “They shouldn’t let people like that walk down the street.” She could hear her. She was blind, but she wasn’t deaf. However, comments like that were nothing but rain drops that moistened the top of her blouse and within hours faded away, not to be recognized or mulled over. She continues walking, her expression never changing because there is no stimulus to change it, only the constant walking of the same path that she had memorized long ago. “fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seven…” She stops, her mouth popping spittle as she wonders what just happened. She suddenly hears a honk passing and fading and feels her shawl blown up as if by a strong wind, pushing her back slightly. She continues to stand still, waiting for a sign or something else that will help her find her way. “OUT OF THE ROAD, BITCH!” HOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOONK! Again it happens, and she steps back a few feet, shielding her face two seconds two late from the water that splashed up on her face. Still standing in the street, she begins to panic, her mind racing at speeds that she didn’t think were possible on an open freeway, much less inside her head. The woman, she thinks. She threw me off by bumping into me, and now I’m in the street. I can turn around, but then I won’t know how far to go. How long was it since I stopped counting? Fifteen, sixteen? Then, she gasps silently, but out loud this time: “help.” Suddenly she feels the firm grip of a hand around her own, and she feels herself being pulled. It feels like she is being lifted off of the ground, the force of this hand is so strong. Starting to struggle, she sputters and coughs, trying to think of something to do or say. Before she can vocalize her thoughts, a calming baritone voice enters her ears. “Don’t worry, you’re safe.” This is how Arianna Riley and Nathan Boswell met. ---- Nathan’s eyes open, and a tear drops down his face. He knew she was special when he first met her. And he didn’t understand why, but he felt himself go soft around her immediately. At the time, Nathan Boswell was not soft. Nathan Boswell had just chokeslammed a man named Edward Lambert, costing him a title. Nathan continually tried to make BloodAngel feel pain during those few months. But all of that was gone when he was alone with her. Even the first time he had her in his house. ---- She wakes up, and she can feel that this isn’t her bed. What happened? Suddenly the events of the past few days spring into her head, and she sees – ha, she has never been able to get over thinking that – pictures she has created. The man who saved her – he must be tall, strong. Maybe…black hair? She has never been good at guessing, and it wasn’t really important. She didn’t care if his hair was blue, all she cared was that he had helped her, and now she must be sleeping in his bed. “Are you awake?” He’s still here. A giddy feeling that reminds her of younger days shoots through her head, and she feels the smile come onto her face. She couldn’t understand what she was feeling, but it was still a good feeling, and she wanted to talk to him more. “Yes, I’m awake.” “You’re giggling.” She was, actually. She heard him step into the room, and the giggles kept coming. She pulled into a sitting position, and she watched him – ha, again – enter the room. “What’s so funny?” A smile arrives on Nathan’s face as he sees his tittering houseguest rolled up on his bed. He sees her face strain for one second, trying to recognize the voice, and then transforms into mirth once again. “I don’t know, I just can’t stop laughing.” And then she falls forward, still laughing. She looks up where she thinks Nathan is and smiles. “What’s your name?” “I’m Nathan.” She giggles again, getting back to her original position. “Hi, Nathan. I’m Arianna.” What am I doing? This man could be wanting to kill me for all I know. But she couldn’t help it. Whenever she even felt his presence she began to feel happy, calm, complacent. She stuck her foot over the bed, trying to find the floor. She hears him gasp, but she forms a very mean face – at least it felt mean to her. “No you don’t, Nathan. You’re not going to save me this time. I am going to get off of this bed myself, and you’re not even going to move one step.” He answers with a chuckle, and she smiles again, putting her other foot over the bed. Slipping forward, she feels the carpet sink under her left foot, then waits for the second foot to feel carpet. It doesn’t happen. “Ooof!” “Arianna!” Her head would hit the corner dresser if he didn’t act fast. Luckily for Nathan, he wasn’t just a big wrestler, but also a surprisingly agile one as well. He dives forward and grabs her sweeping her up and holding her in front of him, breathing quickly. “Are you…all right…?” No, not at all. “Fine…I’m fine…” She starts to sputter, gasping for breath, and she lets her head fall forward. “Thank you, Nathan…” She feels her head lifted gently, and she smiles, which is replaced by gasping once again. Then, something else replaces the gasping. Something warm. Something wet. Something beautiful. ---- Nathan Boswell touches a finger to his lips slowly. When he had kissed her, after only knowing her a day, he felt his entire earth shift. Nothing like that had ever happened to him before. Before this, besides terrorizing BloodAngel, Nathan Boswell was part of a team to help destroy the NWC. When he started getting involved with her, however, his passion for it died. He slowly faded into the background. And he had been there ever since, until a few months ago. He had tried to juggle his two loves, and one killed the other. And now he is left with nothing. Nathan tries to think of more happy times, but now he can only think of last Fusion. Of the loss. Of the handcuffs. Of the video. Nathan Boswell’s lips curl and his eyes narrow, the tear and touch on the lips forgotten. Now he can’t think of the good times. He can’t think of the happiness. He can only think of Reginald Atkins. And that’s some bad news for BloodAngel.
