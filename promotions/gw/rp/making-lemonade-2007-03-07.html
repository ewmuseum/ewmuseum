Making Lemonade Jenny Callihan Global Wrestling Promo #3034 Date: 03/07/07 Location: Condo Parking/Kitchen/Flo's Sitting in a shade with an array of tools by her right and a Harley Davidson, She works on it feverishly despite warmer temperatures. In a stereotypical world, this pretty blonde would have shiny tools in a bikini as she pretends to tune-up a bad ass motorcycle. However, this wasn’t a stereotypical world. The tools had a dull shine as they have been used before, and she had a greasy white t-shirt on. Last, but never least, she wasn’t pretending to tune up her motorcycle as she has a baseball cap on backwards to keep her luscious blonde hair out of her face. The front screen door of the condo opened gently, with the gesture of someone who had a great deal of respect for other people's possessions- so much, in fact, that he scarcely dared touch them sometimes. Karma stepped out onto the sidewalk, rather enjoying the warming March breeze that ruffled his long blonde hair, which was not yet tied back for the day. He spotted Jenny out working on her bike in the drive, and made a beeline for her, hoping he wasn't interrupting. He watched for only a moment, before he felt like it would have been rude for him not to speak up. 'Good morning...' “Ah goddammit!” she said dropping a rachet wrench as she tried to keep the oil plug in. “Umm...Karma, could you...get that for me please? I have no desire to be covered with more oil then I need to.” Karma ducked down, quickly picking up the wrench and handing it back to her. 'I'm sorry, Jenny... I didn't make you do that, did I?' Jenny shakes her head. “No, my hands are just getting a slippery.” She puts some tugs the nut some more and then places the rachet onto the cloth that held her tools. “There...all I need to do is give it a wax job, and it is all set.” She picks up a cloth, and wipes the grease on her hands before standing up. “Blah…I love my bike, but I need a shower every time I do it.” Karma smiles, nodding slightly. 'It looks like it might be a bit of a messy hobby. Nothing wrong with getting your hands dirty now and again, though.' he quickly added- it was obvious from the way he dressed that he was a bit of an outdoorsman himself. “Man, if my mom could only see me now. She would be screaming ‘Jennifer...quite playing in that dirt!’” she said with a witches face appears on her. “The only sweat was to be from doing floor exercise, and the only possible grim from skidding on the mats and getting paint.” She throws the cloth onto the tools and turns to directly look at Karma. “I didn’t wake you, did I? I mean, I didn’t turn on the engine cause I thought you were still asleep, although it only needed an oil change.” He shook his head, though the fact was that some of the banging around outside had awakened him- he didn't find it bothersome. 'I needed to get out of bed- this is about the usual time that my body is used to, so I woke up, I guess. Then I noticed you were out here, so I decided to say good morning.' “Good morning. Me, I am up before dawn.” She said with a smile. “Just accustomed to it like you I guess. It really puts a damper on late night parties though.” He nodded. 'I'm not much into the party scene myself, I suppose, but I can see where that would have a definite effect. Want me to make some breakfast or something?' He was a passable cook, thus why he offered- his food tended to be a good deal heartier than most women might have preferred, but he'd never claimed to be a gourmet. “Sure thank you, but I cook dinner!” she said with a mischievous grin. He smiled sheepishly. 'It'll probably turn out better if you do... I'm not the best cook in the world when it comes to more complicated meals, but I can fry an egg. Or two?' he asked, by way of determining how many he should make. “I prefer scrambled.” She looks at herself. “Tell you what, you cook breakfast and I will take a quick shower. Thankfully, these clothes are ones I reserve for working on dirty stuff or I would be a fashion nightmare by now.” 'Works for me,' he said, smiling, 'I'll get on it, and have it hot and ready for you when you're done.' She nods. “Cool, thanks.” She said as she walks into condo shower up. *** 10 minutes later *** A lot of men would complain that women take the longest showers, but apparently never met Jenny. She tends to take a long enough shower to clean and get out as quick as she can. Some would proclaim this had to do with her being in gymnastics and early morning practice. Only the truth is she had to take it in five, and her hair was much shorter then. The real reason she is accustom to quick showers is she lived in a desert for so long with water restrictions, and she had it in her mind to conserve water even if there was an abundance. She makes her way into the kitchen/dining room area with her still wet hair layered across her silky short robe that really left the imagination soaring as it bends around her curves. She gracefully sits down at the table in her normal spot. This is where Jenny might be a contradiction. A lot of people see the tough woman imagine so they will think she is abducted and replaced with a fembot if she even shows a more feminine side. The truth was that Jenny is a unique creature who is every bit of a woman without the prissiness and likes to fight and right motorcycles. Karma hadn't looked up when she entered, as he had been distracted by trying to manage the scrambled eggs and bacon at the same time- had it been only his own food, he wouldn't have much bothered, but he wanted the eggs to stay light and fluffy now that he had someone to serve for. He glanced up at her with a half smile, only barely catching what she was (barely) wearing. 'Sorry it's not quite done yet... I've got to get the eggs and bacon done, and then I'm going to pop this toast in the oven...' He trailed off as he finally saw what she was wearing, and tried to find words to say that would not be disrespectful. Finally, he opted for what he felt was the smart option- keeping his mouth shut. Blushing furiously, he looked back down to the skillet. She nods as she cross her leg, and just sits back and relaxes. She looks at Karma from a womanly point of view. Despite being married, she could still look couldn’t she? If it weren’t for the fact she was happily married, she would dragged him into her spidery webs long time before now, but it would be disrespectful to both Jake and Karma to do so at this point. However, the thoughts did rush through her head about the nice Norseman that standing in her kitchen. She often wonders why he didn’t have a girlfriend to make noise with in his bedroom. He was nice, polite, and the fact was he wasn’t hard on the eyes. She could see him giving Speedo’s some justice. He pushed the eggs up against the edge of the skillet with the spatula, turning them so that they wouldn't get too brown on any one side. With the bacon frying happily along, he decided it was time to broil the toast, and turned to slide it in. As he stood up, he noticed Jenny watching him, and he blushed once again. He tried to smile it off, however- being so awkward around people was starting to make him feel like he was crazy. 'So... what would you like to drink? Orange juice? Coffee? Might take a little longer to make, but if you're in the mood for something hot this morning, it'd hit the spot,' he said, wondering if that line sounded like he was hitting on her. Too late now. She innocently giggled and removed herself from the seat. Of course, this might a wiggled the robe a little and open the upper torso bit just a tad, but she went to her refrigerator, and pulled out the orange juice. “Probably best. Never been much of a coffee drinker. Want some?” she goes to reach for the glasses in the cabinet, which allow the bottom half the robe snake up her very tone firm thighs. Karma blinked for a moment. 'Ah... yeah. That would probably get me going just right.' Oh God. How does that foot taste, Karma? She looked at him as he said that, and could see the awkwardness on his face. “Does this bother you?” she asks as she lowers two glasses and gently places them on the shelf. Karma fumbled for a response for a moment, and then sighs, smiling slightly. 'No... but my tongue getting all twisted up does. I'm sorry; I'm not trying to be an idiot.' Jenny giggles again. “I am sorry, I wasn’t thinking when I put on this robe.” She said looking. “I can go get change if you make you more comfortable.” He shook his head. 'No, no, it's not your fault. I'm just not used to being around people so much, particularly... women people. It's sort of like coming out of a long tunnel into the light,' he said, smiling wryly at himself. The smell of the eggs seeming to get a little hot turned him back to his job. Jenny nods with a smile as she fills two glasses with orange juice. “So...like what you saw?” 'Well... um...' he said, fumbling for an answer, 'Of course. You're a very attractive woman.' He cleared his throat, not looking at her for a moment. At least plating up breakfast was a good excuse not to. “It’s ok if you look, I am not going to rip your head off or anything.” She said as she sat back down leaving Karma’s glass at the other seat. “I am quite accustom at strange men looking me on a nightly basis, so for you to look at me in with a little twinkle doesn’t offend me at all. Just want you to be comfortable, is all.” He carried the two plates over to the table after pulling out the toast, and smiled ruefully. 'I'm just a bit clumsy around women, I suppose. At least, that's what Betsy seems to think, and my performance here was certainly no better. It's been a while since I've been in close company with a woman, much less you know... done something like serious dating.' “Who’s Betsy?” she asked curiously. 'Oh...' he said, realizing that he must not have told her the story, 'This girl that is my waitress most of the time that I go to this diner I like in downtown Dallas... I sort of struck up a friendship with her, I guess, and tried to take her out on a date because she seemed like she needed a good time. It was just... strange. Things ended up strange.' She raises her eyebrow. “Not to pry to heavily, but define strange?” 'Oh, she just told me I was weird,' he said, shrugging as he forked the first bite of eggs. He didn't put it into his mouth yet, because that would have been rude, 'I guess I'm not the type of guy she was used to dating, or something. She acted very odd when I gave her a hug at the end of the evening, like I'd done something wrong.' She took a first bite of the eggs as she heard Karma talk, and rolled her eyes as she swallows. “She might have had ...different expectations, but still she shouldn’t have made you feel that way. If I may be bold, and this is just an observation, you just need to be a little confident around women, that all. Of course, Jake does tremble when we tango, but that an entirely different subject. Tell you what, why don’t we go out on a few dates? Nothing fancy, just you taking me out for a good time to build your confidence a little.” His eyes widened as he paused halfway to his mouth with a forkful of eggs. 'A date? I couldn't, what would your husband think?' “Man, if men would say that when they give me even more indecent proposals.” She said with a mischievous bat of her eyes. “It won’t be anything too serious, but something to help you feel comfortable around women. When you feel comfortable around women, you will be the stud of the party.” He chewed his lip for a moment, but nodded slowly. 'Well... I suppose it will be alright, if he's okay with it. I wouldn't want him to think I was trying to take his wife away or anything like that.' She chuckles. “Hmm...oh god, it is too early for dirty thoughts.” She said with a small bit of laughter. “Anyways, with my mind out of the gutter, it will be cool. Jake is cool, as far as he told me anyways, with me going out with men as long as we don’t…well...you know.” Karma nodded, and chewed thoughtfully. He swallowed. 'I suppose my problem is just that... well, I didn't used to be the type of person that I am now. I'm kind of scared to repeat the performance of my younger years' She nods as she took a few more bites of her food. “Well, this is what a wise teacher once told me. Being worried about what you done in the past means you learned the lesson well enough, but to be mortified means you are scared to go into the future. Basically, it is ok to let the things we learn be in our backburners so we don’t forget, but it is not ok to let it cripple us…in your case, socially. That is part of growing up, and learning from your mistakes is going to happen repeatedly, but letting your mistakes haunt you will hinder your growth as a person severely.” He nodded again. 'Very wise advice. I suppose I take it to extremes sometimes, trying to make up for what I did then.' Of course, nobody even knows what Karma has ever done- but he's not making any rush to tell, either. “And that’s ok.” She said with a smile. “Don’t worry, I will make you into the best responsible studmuffin there is, deal?” She was partially joking, although she couldn’t see why a woman wouldn’t want him. So perhaps once he gets out of that shell, he will have babes breaking down the doors. Stranger things have happen. “So which Diner is this?” 'Flo's,' he answered, almost immediately, 'I popped in on my first week here looking for something good to eat... they're 24 hours, and the food isn't all that great, except for the chocolate pie. I guess I go there mostly for the company.' She nods, although not letting him know why she was nodding or even asking which Diner it was. “Sometimes the food doesn’t need to be great when you are on the go. Oh my god, these eggs are good...I can’t believe I haven’t mention it already.” He smiled, his face almost overtaken by the expression. 'I'm glad you like them... I'm only used to cooking for myself, so I wasn't sure how it was going to work out.' Realizing that his own were getting cold, he looked down and forked in another two small mouthfuls. His stomach growled, reminding him how hungry he actually was, but he refused to make a pig of himself in front of a lady. She nods as she continues to eat gracefully. Some might have called her a trucker dyke, or something of that nature, but she did have manners. “So what are your plans for the day?” His brow furrowed for a moment. 'Training, I suppose. I'm not happy about this match I'm in this week. I'd much rather take on Atkins myself, but I guess I have to do what I can. I've got to make sure that I'm ready.' “I’ll join ya…well, when I am not in this flash-worthy robe.” She said looking down to notice that it does object to movements to what would make the shy blush and the slick lust.” Then I gotta head out of town on some business. Nothing major, just certain adjustments needs me to actually be there to do.” His eyes widen. 'Everything's okay, right?' “Oh yea, everything perfect.” She said with a sweet smile. “It’s just in the wrestling world where my name is splashed all over the place, if I am not wrestling and I dealing with business. Thankfully, I learned how to handle it effectively in the BACW, so it won’t be too long of a venture.” What she wasn’t mention was this was a white lie. If she mentions what she was truly going to do, he might try to talk her out of it. No, there is no talking her out of this one. Someone has emotionally tangled Karma, and she doesn’t take that lightly. Someone is going to get a visit from the darker side of the Harley riding blonde. He nodded. 'Ah, okay... anything you need me to do while you're out?' “Sit back and relax may seem like a retarded request, but…” she says with a wink as she finished her plate. “I got a collection of movies you can watch if you like.” A smile broke over his face, bigger than any she'd previously seen him give. 'That's a tall order for somebody like me, I hope you realize, but I'll give it a shot.' You can leave them in the sink; I will get to them later.” She walks into her bedroom, and closes the door to get changed. *** Late that evening *** Flo’s Diner was, as it usually was, quiet as most of the patrons were passersby or it wasn’t superly busy like lunch hour. In the door walk a blonde woman wearing black jeans, white tank top, and a biker jacket. However, this biker jacket spoke experience rather then just another cool accessory for her. Jenny surrenders herself to a booth as she placed her helmet on the table while waiting for a waitress. She was hoping by fate it would be Betsy, but she knew the odds. If Betsy wasn’t in tonight, which is rare of a graveyard Diner worker, she will certainly leave one hell of a message. Betsy had a headache at the moment- of course, that didn't excuse her from having to work. As soon as she saw the blonde in the biker jacket come in, she steeled herself. Bikers were usually difficult types to deal with, and she didn't have any reason to believe that this woman was any different. She rubbed her temples for a moment, and then grabbed her pad and pen out of the front pocket of her apron and walked over in her ugly orthopedic non-skid shoes. 'Hey, welcome to Flo's Diner,' she said with a Texan drawl that let Jenny know this was definitely a home-stater, 'My name's Betsy, and I'll be haulin' your food tonight. Can I get you a cup of coffee while you look over the menu, or are you settled?' “A coffee would be fine.” She said with a smile as she caught the name. 'Cool,' she said, ducking to the back. It wouldn't take her long to pour up a cup and grab the creamer. Sugar was already on the table. At least this was easy, she thought to herself as she aimed the coffee spout into the white porcelain cup. One customer closer to getting the hell out of here, and back into her bed. She returned a few moments later, and took out some packets of creamer from her apron pocket. 'Here you go, miss. You just sit there and enjoy your coffee however long you want. I'll be along to check on you in a little bit to see if you need anything.' She was trying to keep up her chipper voice, but even she could tell that she was drooping when she spoke. “Have a seat Betsy.” Jenny spoke to her. Despite the fact Jenny is an early riser; she definitely can bring authority to her voice. I have to go check on the gentleman down the row there and my break's not for another twenty minutes...' “This won’t take long.” She said. “And if that gentlemen has a problem with that, I am sure we can come to an agreement.” Betsy sat down, a little harder than she meant to, as the plastic booth back rattled a little. She chewed her lip for a moment and stared into this woman's hard blue eyes, but could find nothing there that was even remotely familiar. 'Do I... Do I know you?' It could have been someone from high school that she didn't remember, after all. “No, but you know a friend I know.” Jenny said. “But since it would be rude to know your name without you knowing mine, my name is Jenny Callihan.” 'Betsy Sims,' she said, gulping like she had a rock stuck in her throat. She felt for some reason like she was in trouble. “You went on a date with a guy I know by the name of Karma?” 'Karma...' she said, her face contorting in confusion. The only date she had been on recently was... 'You mean Oyvind?' Suddenly, she felt a little guilty for the way she had treated him, his own odd behavior aside. 'I swear I didn't know he was hitched!' she said, coming out with the only think she could think of. “If he was hitched, we wouldn’t be having this conversation, so count yourself lucky.” She said only hinting at a dangerous consequence. “But let say word of how he felt after your date has reached my attention, and let just say I don’t appreciate people who treat my friends like that.” 'Huh?' she said, her eyes practically popping out of her head, 'Treat him like what?!' He has admirable qualities that damn difficult to find in a man, and to make him feel like he wasn’t worth your time? That just doesn’t fly with me honey. He is a good man, and he deserves better.” 'Because I... is it...' and suddenly her face fell, 'Because I told him he was strange?' “That makes him feeling like a less of a human being to you; yes I think you hit the nail in the coffin.” She blinks. I just... he's so different from anybody I've ever been out on a date with.' “And the problem is?” she looks at her. “Did he mistreat you?” She shakes her head. 'No, of course not... he wouldn't, I just... I don't know how to deal with guys that shy, I guess.' “So that the only hindrance? The fact he is very shy? I know he is shy, he lives with me. But you know, it’s not a problem since beneath all that he is a good man with a good heart. Since he is not like other men you have dated, name a few of the qualities of the other men.” The unfaltering look from Jenny’s eyes display the body language that she was not joking. 'How will it be a blind date if we already know each other?' “I don’t tell him who I am hooking him up with at first?” she chuckles. “Ok, it will be a semi-blind date..or something. Anyways, will you or will you not?” She blinks her eyes for a moment, and nods slowly. 'Yeah... I want to give it another chance. Maybe I was just in a bad mood before.' And I will see what I can about his shyness.” She drinks down the coffee, and pulls out her wall. “How much do I owe you for the coffee.” She shrugs. 'No worries about it... on the house.' “Thanks…maybe sometime we can get together and ..you know…fix you up real nice.” She said smiling. “Make his jaw drop? Anyways, I got to go…I get up earlier then Priest for Choir practice.” Betsy's jaw dropped, and she started giggling uncontrollably as she stood up to go back to the kitchen. 'Thanks for stoppin' by... nice to meet you!' “You too.” Jenny says as she grabs her helmet and starts to walk towards. A wolf call comes in her direction as she heads out the door.
