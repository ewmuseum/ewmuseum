Do as I say, and you won't get hurt (RP #2 PPV)

28 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/28/04 It's the night before Maximum Carnage, Donny and Alexis back together in Chicago decide to have some fun tonight so they go to a bar right down the street from what Donny considers the Mecca of Baseball, Wrigley Field. As the happy couple begin to drink the night away, and beligerent drunk man begins to hit on Alexis, Donny takes notice and keeps his eye on the situation. Donny notices the guy is getting a little too close to his woman and Donny walks up to the drunken idiot... 'Excuse me, can I help you with something' Donny is looking dead straight into his eyes... The beligerent man just ignores Donny.'Listen sweetheart, you should just come home with me tonight and let me make you feel like a real woman' Alexis tries to get away from the drunk but the man grabs her wrist, Alexis screams and Donny runs up and grabs the mans throat. The man lets go of Alexis and she runs behind Donny who is poised to take care of business. 'Alright fuckhead,I didn't mind you talking to my girlfriend, but you overstepped your boundries, and don't act like an asshole, you seen me with her all night. Give me one good reason I shouldn't beat your ass right now.' Donny tightens his grip on the drunk man. The choking drunk responds gaspingly, 'Fuck you man, I can beat your ass if I wanted to.' Donny begins to lift the man into the air 'You really think so huh? I'll tell you what we're gonna do. I'm gonna pick you up a little higher, then I am going to throw you, then if your so tough you'll get back up and try to beat my fucking ass, how does that sound bitch?' The man barely can breath but manages to somewhat shake a yes. Donny picks him up higher and throws him onto a round table where someone had been sitting, the man crashes onto the table spilling leftover beer and food scraps all over him. The Drunk man being numb from the alcohol gets up and charges after Donny and he lands the Final Destination on him onto the floor of the bar. Donny seeing that's not quite enough he picks up the almost knocked out drunk and gives him a massive spinebuster onto the floor. Donny begins to yell at the hurt drunk. 'You thought you could beat my ass huh? Why don't you sober up, go home and sniff your Mothers panties.' Donny begins to walk away, Alexis looks down at the man who tried to pick up on her and kicks him square in his nuts. The man clenches his crotch in pain and begins to puke. As Donny is walking away, he tosses the bartender a wad of hundred dollar bills and tells him 'This is for the mess thanks for a great evening' The happy couple walk out of the bar, with having gone through more than they bargained for the night before Maximum Carnage. Will Donny take out his anger on anyone else? That is yet to be seen...
