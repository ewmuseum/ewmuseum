My Story: Chapter Three

30 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.30.04 MY STORY Chapter Three The Introduction The large arms of 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr were finally starting to get tired from his work-out. The Global Wrestling International Champion wasn't sure as to how many push-ups he'd done, but he'd been doing them for over an hour and a half now without even giving a thought to it. A small pool of sweat was growing on the cold concrete under his face where a fountain of the wetness had been pouring off of him. His biceps trembled slightly under his weight, causing slight muscle spasms in his triceps. Yet, still, he continued. This was nothing unusual for the former UHWF World Champion, as his work-out regimen had been like this for as long as he could remember. He would focus on one exercise, regardless of how advanced or elemntary it may be, and would do it until he simply couldn't anymore. Obviously, the length of time that he puts into now has changed, but only because he can go longer now. Push-ups formerly lasted a mere fifteen or twenty minutes. Now they seemed to be taking up the better part of a full feature length film. There was no doubt that he was in the best shape that he'd ever been in... career-wise and life-wise. However, he would test today just to see how far he could go. He vowed on this day to do a non-stop twenty hour routine. Insane to some, but dedicated to others. He woke up at four in the morning after a full seven hours rest. After a quick, but satisfying breakfast, it was directly to the cross ramp to work on leg and cardiovascular strength. From there to the sticks. From the sticks to the pool... the pool to the boxes and from the boxes to the ropes. At times he would go back to one, or ahead to another, hitting each exercise four or five times in the day. A quick half hour shuttle run to work on side-to-side coordination and movement as well as speed fell in there as well before the pull-ups began. Then the inverted push-ups. Fifteen minutes of one of the streinous activities you could possibly do while all the time your blood is rushing to your head. All on this day, for this one day was special. This day being the final day before the biggest match of his life... a Global Wrestling World Heavyweight Championship shot against the man that knew him better than anyone in this business, past or present. The man that had proven all to many times that he deserved to wear that Title. The man that Ken viewed, not just as his best-friend, but as his brother. Ken looked at the clock on the wall, which read 11:15 pm, and developed a slight calm and peace. If he could continue for forty-five more minutes, not only would he have done push-ups longer than he ever had before, but then his work-out would finally be complete as well. Then... he could go to bed... and he knew that he would have no problems resting on this evening. 'Ken Partridge Jr... I'd like you to meet FLash Lightning,' introduced Damien Payne. 'I'm sure you to will get along great here in neWave, especially seeing as you two are starting at about the same time. The two young men stood staring at one another as the shook hands. 'Nice to meet you, Flash,' said Ken enthusiastically. 'Same to you Ken, and you can call me Josh.' As the two released the grip that their hands had on one another in their greeting, Payne nodded before making his exit. 'Best of luck to both of you,' finished an exiting Damien Payne. The two men simply nodded to the neWave owners back before redirecting their attention at one another. 'So, your Kid Flash, huh,' began KPJ. 'I've heard of you before, but unfortunantly, I've never had the opportunity to see you in action.' 'Ahh,' sighed Josh, 'don't believe everything you've heard. I'm still trying to learn some of the ropes.' The two chuckled a bit before Josh continued. 'But you, on the other hand,' continued Josh with a change of voice towards the excited side, 'You've already learned yours, huh? I remember hearing it backstage at an indy show I was working... there's a new WCW Champion. Y2KPJ--' 'That's not me anymore,' cut-off Ken. 'Y2KPJ no longer exists. Now, it's just me... just the Superstar... Ken Partridge Jr.' Funny how things stick. 'Well, Ken Partridge Jr or Y2KPJ... your success still hasn't changed. You're the same man.' The two continued chatting for what seemed like an hour before thir first NWW show was ready to begin, and they made sure to continue that conversation after their debut matches were over... And they still continue it... To this day. All Ken could think about in the last five or ten minutes of his workout was whether or not this one match would change everything. Could this meeting in the ring really tear apart the bond that they had worked so hard to build. It hurt him to think these things. It pained him immesly to fathom the thought of never being on friendly terms with Josh again... to not be able to talk to him about the things that nobody else could comprehend, let alone understand about him. Was this worth it? Was this Championship really worth the possible forfiture of a brotherhood that had last a good many years to this point? Ken didn't know... but he was more than willing... ...to find out.
