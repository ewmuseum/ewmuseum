An Expensive Taste

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.23.04 'You're thinking about her again,' proclaimed Josh in a subtle underlying tone that shook Ken from his thoughts. 'What,' question KPJ as he shifted his weight in the leather recliner that he had become restful in. He sat adjacent the large flat-widescreen plasma televsion screen that hung on his wall firing off images of himself, Akodo Rokku, and John Zero in various matches. Ken was dressed comfortably, yet a little out of character for him, as he wore a pair of patchwork flare-legged jeans and his signature pearl colored 'Superstar' sleveless, bearing his silver and blue emblem on the front and had 'The Elite' scripted in gold on the back. He was studying today and had no need to dress any other way. 'Gunner,' Josh voiced once again, 'you're thinking about her again.' Ken let his head fall into the back of the recliner, allowing him stare at the ceiling for a bit before closing her eyes and seeing her beautiful face. Her smile lit a fire inside of him and made him feel warmer than he had felt in a very long time. He could smell her sweet fragrance as her beautiful blonde hair rolled in the breeze. 'Snap out of it,' exclaimed Josh. And just like that... she was gone. No longer did Ken feel warm or smell the beauty of her... now he was just... cold... again. Josh sat caddy-cornered from him in a pair of Jean Penne jeans and a traditional blue Superman t-shirt, neither of which were tattered or torn, but neatly pressed and new-looking on the contrary. 'You don't have any holes in your clothes, Josh,' voiced Ken in a very monotonific voice. 'I figured I would take your advice. If I'm the champion, I should at least dress a little for the part,' beamed Josh, enjoying it a touch, but to proud to admit it. He quickly attempted to get back on subject, coming across a little frustrated. 'Ken, why are you thinking about her so much again?' 'Did you see her at WrestleCade, Josh,' asked Ken, drifting off once again into his mind. 'She was radiant. Just as she always was.' 'She's out of your life,' stated Josh matter-of-factly. 'She doesn't have to be,' responded Ken, still a bit airy. 'Dammit, Ken, we've got more to worry about right now. You need to start fucking focusing,' shouted Josh, snapping Ken out of his half-dream and compltly into reality. 'Excuse me, Josh,' came from Ken's mouth in a frustrated and angry tone. 'You think I'm not focusing? You don't think that I'm taking Elite business seriously? The Elite IS MINE! Do you understand that?' Ken actually had a slight look of insanity etched across his face that made Josh feel more than a little uncomfortable. 'It's Mine... and I challenge anyone on any roster to take it away from me, dig! I don't care if you are the World fucking Champion, Josh, this is my brainchild... my offspring, and I've had to watch it suffer since WrestleCade. I disposed of Ace Harding, because he was a cancer that was spreading through my child to a critical point. So I went in, and I cut it out, burning it to ash as I finished to make sure it would never return.' Ken had a look in his eyes that Josh had never before seen. 'Now Ice is gone... neither you nor I can get in touch with him... and we have to be honest with ourselves... we don't know if he's ever coming back. It's you and me... Josh... you and me. We have to carry the load of MY baby. You've done your part, and continue to do so, you're the World Champion... my World Champion. I'm beyond grateful and more than anything proud of you for that fact... but don't for one second think that I, of all people, am not focused on a match to bring my child another title. This is something I need, Josh. This is something I must acheive to survive, I've really proven not much of anything since setting foot in Global Wrestling--' 'What are you talking about,' interrupted Josh. 'You're a former Primestar Champion, and the only reason you lost it was because you were stripped of it. You just defeated BJP, on of GW's best in a ladder match. You made Ace tap-out... you're the only person that will ever be able tos ay that. And, on top of it all, you have a damn good win-loss record.' 'My win-loss level is far below par of what it whould be. Benjamin didn't bring his game to the table, and the Primestar title was a strap that meant nothing.' 'You won it from me,' said Josh solemnly, looking down aty the floor and away from Ken. A silence filled the room, and Ken returned to his normal status quickly thereafter. His hands stopped shaking and he was quick to place his head in them, occasionally pulling them through his uncharacteristly unsculpted hair. 'Josh... I'm sorry,' began Ken, 'I'm just very on edge.' 'I know... no need for apologies,' said Josh in an honest tone. 'The Primestar title meant something while you held it, and it meant something to me because I won it from you... but it went on to become worthless. Think of it as the WCW Televison Championship. It was worth something at one time, but then it just became a piece of trash.' 'I understand, Ken,' retalliated Josh, 'and I know that you're focused. I know that you need this. But, I also know that you'll have no problem acheiving it.' Ken stood up from his chair and began to walk into his kitchen. 'Thanks, Buddy,' said an appreciative Ken. 'Thank you so much... I need your support. You'll finish watching this footage with me so I can study Rokku and Zero, right?' 'Of course,' smiled Josh. 'I'm gonna get us some water real fast first. I'll be right back.' Josh sat back on the couch and felt more at ease. He was glad that Ken had finally gotten things of his chest and was looking for to his comrade... his teammate... his friend winning the International Championship on Monday. He looked up to see Ken fall off of a ladder, richochet off the tope rope and lock in The Epidemic on BJP. 'He's ready,' Josh said quietly to himself. 'Hey, Josh,' voiced Ken from the kicthen. 'Yeah,' answered Josh to a re-entering KPJ. 'You still haven't beat me,' winked Ken with a grin his face. Josh simply smiled and chuckled a bit as he took the water from Ken and rested on the sofa.
