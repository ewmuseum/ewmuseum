Pressure

1 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Various Wednesday, February 1, 2006 They say every dog has its day. If Fusion was my day I find that simply horrifying '30 seconds' I muttered to myself. I sat in the corner of the bowling alley watching everyone having a blast. I rest my feet on another chair. '30 seconds' I mutter to myself again. Why do I always do this to myself? I always isolate myself from everyone else and beat myself up like this. I'm either my own best friend or my own worst enemy. Sunday, April 6, 2002 I was just starting to break out in a local organization in Toledo, Ohio. Every Saturday night for the past two years I was involved one way or another with World Extreme Championship Wrestling but just recently as a wrestler. I always wondered why such a small organization why it had such a big name. Last night I yet again retained my Hardcore Championship. This time against the man who has tought me the most Mindgame. He believes I am ready to take the next step in the industry. However, I am not as confident in my abilities as he is. I have lots to learn luck is not enough to relay on. Walking inside the bowling alley and bowling ball bag over my shoulder. As I walk in and I'm greeted by Aaron the captain of my team and a man odder then even me. 'We're two games out Fred.' Aaron said. 'Nice lets do it' I said wanting to get the show on the road. 'So how did you do last night?' Aaron asked. 'Got lucky' I answered. 'Are you ever confident?' Aaron inquired. 'No... Not really.' I said. 'Aren't you a cocky asshole in the ring though?' Aaron wondered. 'Yeah, but sadly that's not reality.' I responded 'Well, we need him if we want to win.' Aaron demanded. 'I'll do my best.' I said. 10th frame of the last game. Last Frame and I put all the pressure on myself yet again. If we won we're in the championship if we lose we're in 3rd. I used to think I'd be the kind of guy that preformed well under pressure but the truth is I'm not. I walk up to the lane before anyone can tell me its my turn. I need a strike and a spare to win the game. I grab the ball and set myself on the far left lane. Walking up there, bring the ball back, I throw it and manage to get the strike. One more and we win the game. I place my feet on the same spot and throw the ball and end up with a 7 10 split. 'Fuck!' I say rather loudly. I know full well I am not good enough to pick this up just like I know I won't be getting out of the local scene anytime soon. I throw the ball and hit the 10 pin but that's all. I blew it just like I have in the past. Wednesday, February 1, 2006 'Fred' 'Fred' 'Fred!' I wake up from my day dream and look over and see Owen ready to bowl. 'Sorry Man I was day dreaming' I say. 'No problem, I'm late anyway.' Owen responds. I take my feet off the other chair. 'Have a seat, we have to wait till this league is done to get a lane. 'Nice match at Fusion' Owen says. 'Yeah, it was but not as good as it could have been.' I say disappointed. 'Don't worry about it man you proved you can go with top stars of Global.' 'I know I can... but I don't think anyone else does.' I say. 'So destroy Jay Robson and everyone will know you can.' Owen says as he sits down on the other chair. 'Maybe you're right. I just have to get my head on straight.' I can go with anyone in Global can't I? I just need that 1st big win to get me going. The Draw against KPJ did do me a little bit of good. However its not quite the confidence boost I need. The only boost that will do me any good is where I get my hand raised in victory.
