An Eventful Night: Prelude

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/5/05 ~Mandalay Bay Hotel & Casino~ ~Josh & Gray's room~ “You know it, Gray, it's a likely story. What they have done is basically ensure that we're not going to get our chance to shine.” A familiar voice, that of the hot headed and rash Joshua Brand, appears to not be happy about something. It's more then likely a few things. “Come on, Josh. The man was hit by a car. I don't think he planned that.” A calmer, if not forced to be, older sounding voice sounds out. His older brother, mentor and partner in the ring, tries to calm him down a bit. “Josh, think about it... would you want...” “We've had our chance to go win the Global Tag Titles, in our friggin' home town. I grew up in Seattle, and you and I were both born a half hour ferry ride from it. It's pretty stupid. If you ask me, it's all a setup. They knew they were retiring, and they just did this to cost us our match and chance to kick their asses, and take the titles.” “Josh, please. Calm down, okay? He's injured. You'd not want to have the belts just handed to you, would you? I mean, where is the real challenge and honor in that. It's against our code of honor to just accept them like that. I asked for the match to be canceled. In return, they've booked Dani against that blonde bimbette again. She's booked for a Pay Per View, we get the night off.” Josh stood there, in only a pair of sweats. His breath coming heavier then normal. He appears to have just returned from a run around the 'beach' club area down below, and is still drying off with a towel. He came back to the bad news from his brother. Their match, a match for the Global Tag Team Titles, had been canceled. He was glad that they hadn't bothered to try to leave Las Vegas yet. They were going to head up only a day before, and spend the week after up with family. But now there are a few other things in the way, such as they don't even have a match. ?I still don't like it. It's too friggin' convenient. So we don't even need to head up there, huh?? Gray bows his head a bit in resignation. He knows his brother won't ever take it the way it needs to be. His own arrogance would never allow it. “We're invited to join Dani up there. As you know, Card Subject to Change. Global wants all of their stars there, if possible. Always best anyway. I'd not want Dani to be alone for this show as it is. After what you told me about Soma and his offer, let alone the fact that Ms. Vlassy seems to have a beef with me and isn't afraid to take on others close to me... I'd like to be there. As I understand it, your mother could be there too. If we're not used on the show, it'll be a paid vacation. Remember, we're actually paid for every day we travel outside of Vegas. It'll be good.” Josh sighed. “If you say so. It just really adds more onto an already bad week.” Gray knew what he was talking about. Josh was very suspect when it came to a man who had recently come in to help Lady Leanan and her investigation of what was going on with Dani. They didn't know, and he was some sort of Tribal expert. And this man had taken upon himself to step in and gain Dani's trust. He saved her last week from an attack from a Native mythos demonic entity. He's gained her trust enough that tonight, they're going out on a 'date'. Jaymes, the Nakota Wise Man, has asked Dani to join him for a night on the town. A night that he says will help him ask her questions of her, to help him investigate more into what's going on. Jaymes says that it's going to be rather casual; he says that it's easier to ask questions when out and it's not the main pastime of the night. He says that most answer questions without any restraint when it's something in passing, and not a spotlight and uncomfortable chair event. This had Josh hopping mad. And he's not hiding it at all. ?Look, we'll be fine. I was told by Miss Reno herself that we were guaranteed to billing for figuring this stuff out. Everything is going to be fine, okay?? Josh didn't answer. He just looked up at his brother, as he flopped down into one of the chairs there. Before Gray could ask a second time, a knock came upon the door. Gray moved to open the door. Standing there was a vision that Josh didn't need to see. Dani stood there, dressed to go out for the evening. Her raven colored hair was pulled back into a tight French Braid, a string or harness of semi precious stones flowing down the braid to hold it in place. Around her body was a small, silky dress that looked like a Greek version of a toga. The thin shoulder area was covered in beads that matched the sapphire blue color of the silk. It plunged darlingly at her neckline, cinched at the waist and dropped to a ruched1 bottom, a natural drape-like ruffle or pleating. Her long, powerful, legs bare, going down to a pair of heels that would not be too comfortable to walk around the Las Vegas strip in. Around her neck is what looks like a small silver necklace with a trinket on it that shows in a form of a spiral. In her hands were a black leather clutch type purse, and her large set of truck keys. ?Well, don't you look nice?? Gray asked, trying to move to block Josh's view of the woman, to keep his jealousy down. “Thank you. Jaymes will be here in a few moments. He just called me on his cell. I figured I'd drop the keys to my truck off to you, in case you two need it. You might want to both go out or something, who knows. Don't want you two feeling like bumps on the log while I'm out tonight.” Dani almost didn't realize, that as she talked to Gray, she was making it hard for him to hide her from Josh, as she was blatantly trying to look around Gray to spy on his little brother. Josh's face showed his absolute distaste for this arrangement as it was. Gray moved to keep things civil. ?So what's on the plan for tonight?? Dani turned her attention back to the larger of the two men. “Dinner at a place called Picasso, I don't even know where that is. Then he mentioned that he has box seats for the only Cirque show I've not seen since we got here, Zumanity. I've heard it's a bit racy, 18 or over to get in. But I think it might be fun.” Gray nodded to her. “Sounds like a fun night, don't do anything I'd not do.” Dani blushed. “I won't. You heard what he said. Being in comfort is easier to ask questions and get answers then doing a formal interview or interrogation. I'll be alright.” Dani did a half wave with her left hand, as she put her truck keys into Gray's outstretched hand with her other. Turning on her heels, she heads down the hallway, to disappear. Gray puts the keys down on the table next to the door. Gray looked over at his brothers red face. His discomfort, anger, jealousy, whatever you would call it, was obvious. “C'mon, Josh. Get over it. You heard it, a dinner and a show. Nothing more.” Josh glared up at his brother. “Dinner at a 5 star restaurant, and then a show based on erotica? He's got plans. I know it. I can sense it.” “You can sense it? Come on, you're just jealous. He's a business man.” Josh let out a sound that wasn't a sigh, but it was surely not a real word. “The man is doing this to help Dani. You can't be too off on that. Come on.” “The man is a vulture. But no one else will listen to me. He's a snake, and his interests aren't Dani's best. But you, Lady Leanan, Kaylee, no one is going to listen to me.” Gray chuckles. ?What do you want to do? Follow them or something—Want to spy on her and him tonight?? Josh doesn't hesitate. “Yes, damnit.” Gray looks a bit shocked that his brother would answer so quickly. ?And if you got caught?? Josh again doesn't hesitate. “Then I will owe Dani quite a bit to make up for violating her trust. But I know I'm right. I've never been wrong on a hunch, and you damn well know it.” Gray's face didn't change. It still showed the shock. But it also showed some resignation. He knew that to be a fact, Josh had never been wrong on a hunch when it came to people. He couldn't argue that. Gray looked over at the keys in the table. “Get dressed...” Josh responded with confusion. ?What?? ?Get dressed. You and I are going out, while we happen to have her truck. Where is Picasso's?? “The Bellagio...” “Hmm, always wanted to get a close up view of their fountains. Pretty cool when we drive by. But just a note for you...” Josh got up and started picking up shirts from a pile of laundry that filled up most of the couch. ?Yeah?? ?We get caught, you're taking the blame, right?? Josh turned to his brother and smiled. “Yes, it's the least I could do.” Gray picks up the keys and slides them into his jeans pocket. ~A little bit later~ ~Mandalay Bay Porte Cochere~ Gray sits in the area pulled away from the valet and the cars coming in to pick people up. He and his brother had gotten down to the resident's parking rather quickly and then pulled over here to watch. Gray currently was watching Dani as she looked to be waiting to be picked up. He shook his head as she kept asking the valet what time it was. He noticed how out of place she seemed to feel. She was still adjusting to her semi-fame as a model and wrestler. About 40 feet away, his brother was getting ready for something else for tonight. He knew his brothers eyes and ears were much better then his own. He was but a human. But something had caught his eyes and he had begged off. He and his brother both had the cell phones with the walkie talkie function in them, so if Dani's ride got there, he could run back to the truck. Josh walked up behind his intended prey and suddenly stepped out of the bushes near the gates to the Beach, the club and pool area of the Hotel. He gets the intended reaction from the men standing there. ?Holy chit, mon. What are you doin'? Tryin' t' scare us?? A man with dark black skin, and braids in his hair to look like the not so traditional dreadlocks pops his goggles that he's wearing up onto his head. His partner, who looks Hispanic, does the same, as well as dropping a couple of other items form his hand. Josh addresses them. ?Alright, what the hell are you two doing?? Both, not fully seeing Josh yet, thinking that they might be dealing with hotel security. “Nothin' mon, nothin' at-tal.” The man's accent was thick, a tourist who was checking out things that they shouldn't be. “I'm not security, but I could go get them if you don't let me in on it...” He saw a wide white smile come from the darker skinned man. “Hey mon, don't worry. We started by checking out some of da honeys in the pool. It's thong city tonight. But right now, it's a couple apparently on their wedding night who have taken to their consummation up on their balcony.” Josh didn't look up, but he saw these two do so. He noticed that the gear they were using was the same type of 'spy kids' stuff that you can buy for children at a toy store. Basically a shot gun microphone and night vision goggles for kids. Guess they do work, hopefully better then the old x-ray specs did. “Okay guys, show's over. But I'll tell you what. I'll make it up to you for the night. I'll give you $200 bucks for all your gear here.” Both of the guys look at him, and then shrug their shoulders. The other guy answers him. “I don't know about him, but I know you can have mine for that much. We're out of work, so I could use the cash.” The guy takes his goggles off and picks up his other gear. Quickly he hands all of the 'toys' to Josh. Josh whips out his wallet and pulls out some bills and hands them to the man. “Thanks.” Josh heads back to the borrowed truck. Just as he's getting back and sliding into the passenger seat, Gray starts the truck up. ?What's up?? Gray doesn't answer, he just nods his head in the direction they had seen Dani in before. Standing there now is Jaymes, wearing a pinstripe suit in navy blue, with a silver-gray shirt and silver tie. He steps out of a black car, a brand new looking Jaguar XJ 8. The open door shows it's champagne colored interior. He steps out and hands Dani a single red rose, and she blushes with it. He quickly gives her a hug, lingering with his arm around the small of her back, and then directs her around his expensive car. He opens the passenger seat door for her and helps her get the seat belt pulled out before shutting it. Jaymes heads back to his own door, sits into the car and then pulls off. Gray audibly counts to 60 and then pulls out, coming a few cars behind it in the line to exit the hotel's property. Unbeknown to the two boys, another party is watching... them. A blonde haired woman, known as Kaylee; the maid servant of Lady Aine Leanan, puts her cell phone up into it's belt pouch and then pulls a full faced motorcycle helmet over her long blonde hair. She then starts her Ducati Motorcycle and heads out, just a bit behind them. What is coming up for this evening that's destined to be eventful? ~End of Part I~ 1Ruche : A ruffle or pleat of lace, muslin, or other fine fabric used for trimming women's garments. In this case, Silk.
