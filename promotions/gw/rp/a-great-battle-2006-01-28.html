A Great Battle

28 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-28-06 [Jack Duncan kneels before a small wooden table, his katana laying beside him on the floor. The door behind him slides open, and a young woman comes in carrying a tray with a pot of tea and a ceramic cup on it. She kneels before Jack, and places the tray on Jack’s table. She pours Jack a cup of tea, bows to him, and then exits the room. Jack sips at the tea as he contemplates his next match] Jack: Eddie Ross and Ace Cash, what the hell are they worth? Nothing as far as I can tell. They’re just a couple of generic pieces of shit. I haven’t got much information on Ace, and from what I hear there isn’t much to know. Eddie thinks he’s some kind of bad ass because his father wrestled all over the world. Hmph. Let me tell you something boy, this ain’t yo? daddy’s NWA. [Jack chuckles to himself.] Jack: Neither one of them knows who they will be stepping into the ring with this week. When they step into the ring with me, they will come face to face with a true demon. A man who fought the fucking Sub and lived. A man who has gone toe to toe with many a sasquatch and slaughtered them all. A man who has seen the very deepest levels of hell and come back to tell about it. Death cannot stop me! What makes these two think they will do any better! [Jack is interrupted by the sound of his doorbell ringing. He finishes off his tea, then hears someone running towards the door behind him. The door slides open, and a man quickly drops to his knee behind Jack. He bows low, then speaks.] Man: My lord, you must come quickly. [Jack picks up his katana and shoves it through his belt, then follows the man out of the room and through the halls. Just as Jack enters the courtyard, the front gate is busted down and a tremendous beast steps through. The beast is nearly eight foot tall, and very large. Its skin is dark red, and its huge, yellowed fangs jut out from under its upper lip. A curled horn like a ram’s grows from either side of the beast’s head. A tuft of black hair grows between the horns. In its hands, it holds a massive spiked club. Jack eyes the club for a second, then turns to his servant.] Jack: I’m going to need my other sword. Quickly, go and fetch it. Man: Yes, my lord. [The man dashes back into the house and Jack charges towards the beast. The beast raises its club and charges Jack. As they close, the beast swings at Jack, who deftly dodges the club. Jack unsheathes his katana, swinging it at the beast’s wrist, but it barely scratches the thick hide. Jack dodges away from another swing, and tries another attack with nearly identical results. Behind him, Jack hears his servant calling his name. Jack tries to retreat, but the beast presses the attack. Jack sheathes his katana, and pulls a small dagger from the sleeve of his shitagi and throws it at the beast. The dagger hits the beast in the eye, staggering it. Jack’s servant runs up beside him, carrying a massive black sword across his back. The servant struggles to lift the six-foot blade over his head, and nearly drops it presenting it to Jack. Jack grabs the handle of the blade and takes it from his servant. The servant runs back towards the main house as the beast pulls the dagger from its eye. Jack raises his sword and faces the beast once more.] Jack: Now it’s time I sent you back to Hell, demon! [The beast swings its club at Jack again, but this time Jack blocks it with the massive blade of his sword. Jack shoves forwards, knocking the beast of balance. As the beast stumbles backwards, Jack lashes out at its chest, but the beast manages to block the blow. Jack swings again, this time with such ferocity that he knocks the club from the beast’s hands. Jack thrusts his sword through the beast’s gut. As Jack pulls back, the beast falls to its knees clutching its gut and keels over. Jack steps to the side and lets out a mighty bellow as he swings the blade down onto the beast’s neck, severing it’s head with one stroke. Jack grabs the head by its hair and hoists it high into the air.] Jack: I am the mighty Man-Ox! No foe can vanquish me!
