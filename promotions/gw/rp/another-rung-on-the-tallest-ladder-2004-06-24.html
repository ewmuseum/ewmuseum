Another Rung on the Tallest Ladder

24 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.24.04 06.24.04 'Superstar' Ken Partridge, Jr. -Another Rung on the Tallest Ladder- 'But who shall dwell in these worlds if they be inhabited? ... Are we or they Lords of the World?' -Kepler (quoted in The Anatomy of Melancholy) Ken, wearing only an oversized white towel around his waist, sat motionlessly and silent in his poolside sauna. With millions of things jumbled into one's mind and pulling down like only a burden can, the simple task of lifting an arm to douse water on the coals becomes quuite difficult. However, the thing that weighed him down the most wasn't the thoughts of Gunner Addison or their past. Nor was it The Elements, his restauraunt or his nightclub, The Blue Monkey. It wasn't the fact that he hadn't been able to get in touch with Ice since WrestleCade or that he had lost another World Championship opportunity at Evolution. Surprisingly, it wasn't his tortured ribs or his shoulder, which was screaming in agony. No, the burden that seemed the heaviest of all was the meeting of ten Lords of two different Worlds that was to happen inside of a gigantic steel cage housing two rings at Cross Fire. War Games was something that Ken had never experienced. While he had perfected a bevvy of other matches, the one that a group of his idols in the game, the Four Horsemen perfected, he had never even participated in. There was something about near a dozen men locked in a steel jungle that made the hair on the back of your neck stand on end. This was the darkest and most ruthless battleground that our industry had ever seen. And now, Ken was preparing to step into it. At least he would have one friend in there with him. Not just a friend, but a brother... and a champion in the 'American Youth' Josh. Unfortunantly, their match on Monday sprung to mind leading Ken to believe that the two hadn't made the wisest of choices taking each other on in a High Wire Challenge less than a week before they were placed in a ten-man prison. He reached down to grab his ribs and began thinking of the carnage of their World Title Bout. The Youthful Decent through the announce table, The Cure to the cold hard cement outside the ring. Flashes of the crimson mask that covered his face and the internal bleeding that Josh was coughing up made him shake his head, causing his sore neck to hurt even worse. Was it really smart for the two of them to lace into each other in such a way with what may be the biggest match of their lives right around the corner? 'Yes,' whispered Ken in response to his own thoughts. For the Global Wrestling Heavyweight Championship, he would do anything... and so would Josh. KPJ reached back to the crown of his head, which had been split open via a kendo stick thanks to his little buddy. Ken wasn't mad at him for resorting to that, just mad at himself for not doing it first. 'That's smarts,' thought Ken aloud, wincing at his own touch to the still tender spot. He removed his hand and was able to manage to push himself up off of the bench and into a standing position. Sweat was pouring down his body, looking like a river running through the Grand Canyon as it reached his perfectly shaped abdomen. He opened the sauna door, revealing his brilliant in-door pool. As he sat next to it allowing his feet to dip in, he wiped the sweat off of his face and allowed another thought to enter his mind. Maverick Reno. Reno was going to be on Team GW. A man that he had grown to loathe more than any other he'd ever met... including Eddie B. Ken knew he couldn't trust him. However, he was positive that Maverick knew that he couldn't trust Ken either. It was bad enough that Train was on the team. The Platinum Champion had seemed to have let his head grow to unruly proportions as of late, and had even claimed that his strap meant more than Ken International Championship or Josh's World Title. Yes, it was bad enough that had to look over their shoulder's for him, but Josh had chosen him and Ken trusted his friend's judgment. Reno, on the other hand, wasn't chosen by either one of them, but rather by a vengeful, spiteful, power-hungry bitch that went by the name of Christina McCoy. It was almost as if she banking on allowing her father's company to lose top WMW this Sunday. Unfortunantly, Ken didn't plan on letting that happen. He would win for GW, even if it was only to prove her wrong and throw it all back in her face. Ken slipped in the pool, allowing the cool water to comfort his aching muscles, and a smile slid across his face as he thought of his final teammate... The Lord Thy King himself... Venneman Pendragon. Ken knew he could trust VenPen, there wasn't a doubt in his mind. While he might be a touch out of his gourd and living in a time warp, he felt he was nobility nontheless. And noblemen thrived on their honor. The same as Ken, who felt that honor was one of the few things in life worth maintaining. He didn't necessarily agree with Pendragon's choice to make the fans happy, but that was neither her nor there. At Cross Fire, Ken knew that he had a true fight on his hands. Not just against WMW, but GW as well. He only hoped that Train and Reno could hold in their hatred towards him until after the match was over, as he planned to do towards them. This was a defining moment, not just in their careers, but in the industry that they all love so dearly and in the organization that they all call home. They had the chance this Sunday to be Lords amongst men and claim another World in conquest at the end of the battle if they wouldn't let animosity get in the way. Unfortunantly, hatred is a hard virus to kill.
