Party Time #3

21 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-21-03 Where the bad folks go when they die? They don’t go to heaven where angels fly, they go to a like of fire and fry’see em again till the Fouth of July “Yeap?” I said into the phone as I sat back down on the barstool and grabbed my almost empty can of beer “Mr. Reed’whats goin on son?” I said excitedly into phone because it was my good buddy Trey Reed on the other end “Your commin tonight right” Ok great man’ok’cya then bro? I flipped the phone closed and slid it into my pocket at about this time I seen a short Mexican guy walking past me dressed in a white servants uniform “Hey Mexican’come here” I shouted at the guy as I waved to him with my arm “You guys about got them kegs set up?” I questioned to the Mexican guy and he just looked back at me with a confused look “Huh’ya hear me?” I once again questioned and got the same reaction from the guy “What” Are ya fuckin stupid son? Answer me? I once again said to the Mexican now just wanting him to reply to me with any kind of answer “No Habla Es Spaniol” He said back to me really fast, hell I couldn’t understand the little sum bitch “What” Go’get out outta here’Undalay Padro? I said to the little Mexican shooing him off with my hand “Christ where does Ben find these people?” I asked myself as I stood up and went around the bar and grabbed another beer and cracked it open, I was already starting to feel a little tipsy sense I had been drinking most of the day in preparations for this party we were throwing. I proceeded to go check on our kegs myself in the other room. I pushed the door open and was surprised to see 4 mexican guys?.including the one I just talked to drinking our beer’that’s right’our beer. I looked up at two of em? holding up one on the keg as he did a keg stand “Ocho, Nueva, Deace?” I head them chanting as the other was sucking away on the tap “Yo’what the fuck”!” I shouted out to the little fuckers as the dropped the guy from doing the keg stand and they all just stood there looking at me “What the fuck you mother fuckers think yer doin?” I shouted at them with a pissed off look on my face as they all just looked at each other and shrugged their shoulders “Que” Said the one in the middle with greasy black hair and glasses “K”...Mother fuckers get out of here? I shouted to the Mexican guys as I pointed at the door, they all walked by me as I gave each one of the a bad look as the passed, once they left I went over to my kegs for an inspection, I grabbed the first one and checked it. Like I expected it was full and I dropped it back down into the trashcan filled with, I did this with all four of my kegs and they were all relatively full **DING DONG** The door bell rang as I pushed the door open from the keg room, I looked around the room to see the four Mexicans standing side by side near the bar’I pointed at the one on the end to go open the door’he did as I said and went to the big oak door and pulled it open, as he did about ten people walked inside, three guys and the rest girls “Hey Jay whats up man”.” One of the guys I knew shouted and I just nodded my head and took a drink of my beer. At about this time BJP came down the stairs and I almost started laughing “Dude..Ben?” I said to him as I looked at him trying to keep a straight face “What do you think Jay?” Ben asked me sporting his new duds proudly “Uhh’you take a page outta Austin Powers book” I asked him as I looked at his purple velvet pants and his white shirt with ruffles on the chest “Hey mate’Austin Powers has mojo..I have Benjo” Ben exclaimed proudly as I started to laugh “Haha’man Ben’your something else brah” Ben looked over at the four Mexican guys and looked back over to me with a confused look “Who are those guys?” I looked over at the Mexicans and looked back at Ben “I thought you hired them?” Ben cocked one eyebrow and scratched his head “No’I thought you hired them” I looked at Ben confusingly “Man’hell no I didn’t hire no fuckin Mexicans, you know I don’t like those fuckers, but I do know that I caught em” drinking our beer right out of the kegs? Ben got a pissed off look on his face and started over towards the Mexicans “Fuck that’nobody drinks our beer who isn’t allowed” Ben exclaimed as he grabbed one by the collar “What are you doing?” I asked Ben as he started for the door still holding one by the collar “Throwing them out..are you going to help?” Ben shouted to me as a smile crossed my face “Well hell yeah?” I said as I started for one of the Mexicans but they all took off out the door before I could catch them, I stopped at the entrance near Ben “And don’t come back” I shouted at the Mexicans as the took off down the driveway “Yeah!” Exclaimed Ben after me as I saw Trey Reed walking up sidewalk to the mansion as he looked back at the Mexicans “Dude..who were they?” Asked Trey as he made it up to the door “I don’t know?” I said to trey as I shrugged my shoulders and welcomed him inside. Once inside Trey looked at how many people were already here as we headed to the bar “Damn man I thought I was early, there is already a few people here” Trey said to me as I tossed him a cold one from the other side of the bar “You know when we say we are having a party it lasts all day and night” I said to Trey as I cracked open another beer “Jay toss me a beer?” Boomed the word of BJP as he made it up to the bar next to Reed, I did as he said and handed him a cold beer “I think we should have a toast” Exclaimed Ben as he opened his beer “To what” Your clothes?” Joked Reed as I laughed at BJP “No’to the Youngbloods’and Benjo” I nooded my head as Reed looked at BJP with a confused look “Now the YB part I can understand but what the fuck is Benjo?” Questioned Reed as Ben began to open his mouth and tell Reed but I cut him off “It’s a long story Trey..I will tell you later..now to the Youngbloods” I raised my beer and BJP and Reed did the same as we all tosted to the YB name **DING DONG** I glanced up at the clock and saw that it was almost seven o? clock “Ben I think they are here” I exclaimed to Ben excitedly “Who?” Ben replied sheepishly and he turned and looked at the door “Yeah’who?” Then questioned Trey also wondering who I was talking about, I reached out and patted the side of Ben’s cheek “Dude’the chicks I was talking to earlier’wake up Bennay” “Oh..” Said Ben quietly “OH!...Those chicks” Ben shouted as I guess his brain finally started clicking and just nodded my head and look at Trey “Hey bud’will you open that?” I asked Trey and he just shrugged his shoulder and started for the door “So’what should I say?” Ben asked me in a serious manner as I watched Trey open the door “Ok’you know how I have a southern accent” And when girls are around I really have a bad southern accent? I asked Ben as I watched the front door open with my baby blue eyes “Yea I noticed that mate” Ben exclaimed as he to continued to stare at the door “Your English..and you have a bad English accent” I explained to Ben as he shot me a bad look “No no’its not a bad thing’chicks love guys with accents so play it up” I said to Ben as we watched beautiful girl after beautiful girl stroll through our front door as Ben leaned up on the bar as the women came closer and closer “Hey Jay?” Ben whispered to me, but my eyes were locked on the fine girls coming through the door “Yeah Ben” I replied to Ben as I continued to look “Man..Im ready for that hair cut now?”
