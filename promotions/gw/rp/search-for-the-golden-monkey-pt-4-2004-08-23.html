Search for the Golden Monkey pt. 4

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-23-04 “SERPENTI!!!” Talon shouted as he looked at the static filled monitor in front of him. Talon quickly swivels in his chair and checked another monitor in the back of the hummer. Subject: Serpenti --- Vital Signs: Fair. --- ----Diagnosis of current condition? Subject is currently unconscious. --- The word flashed in green.. “Good! He is still alive.” Talon thought to his self quickly hitting buttons and making his way to the driver seat. Moving slowly and ducking down so he did not hit his head. ---- “Wake uppp”. Wake uppp??A dreamy female voice vibrated though Serpenti’s mind. Slowly Serpenti stood up shaking his head. “That’s it”. Wake up!” The sound changed from dreamy to a harsh order. Serpenti opened his eyes and he was aboard his ship? The sky was of the deepest night and the sound and smell of the sea filled his ears as the ship rocked to and fro in the water. Still shaking his head Serpenti stumbles across the ship looking for the bearer of the voice. His quest ended as he headed up the stepladder leading to the upper deck. Standing next to the wheel was a woman dressed in a flowing almost see-through sea foam green and powder blue gauze dress. The wind slightly blowing the dress; causing the dark tanned skin underneath to show though here and there hinting but never revealing. Serpenti stood there drowning into the beauty of the woman.. Slowly her bright azure eyes seemed to hypnotize him. “Where are” are? we? ? Serpenti said in almost a trance like whisper. The female form came to Serpenti almost floating in the air. “We are in a place that is many places but nine. We are in what many would call a void between death and life.” The female form said, her mouth not moving once. Her face completely emotionless all save the eyes... Serpenti staggered away tearing his eyes from those of the female fearing them more now then ever. The action resulted in him feeling drunker then he had ever felt before. Everything started to spin causing Serpenti to stagger and fall to his knees looking up and seeing the night sky swirl around. He felt like puckeing everything he had inside of him ? “It is not your time my serpent. Rise up” FIGHT!!! FIGHT!!!! Everything went into a flash of light and pain? Serpenti let out a howl of pain as his eyes flashed open? Standing above him stood something many would never believe could exist. Floating there; shimming in white and blue, was a female. The female from Serpenti’s mind, from his vision just moments before. Serpenti quickly scrambled to his feet. The ghostly females shimmer giving enough light to see his immediate surroundings. Serpenti reaches into his pocket and pulls out another glow stick as he ignites it the Ghost in front of him smiles and disappears? “Ahead is the one you seek?” The feminine voice sounded though Serpenti’s head once more. Serpenti shook his head trying to clear the cobwebs from it. Looking up he saw the hole he fell though. Nice drop. But not to far down. Serpenti looked around seeing the large rocks and the spot where he laid moments before. Rubbing the back of his head causing pain to shoot though his mind and spots flash in front of his eyes. Shaking his head to clear it again he realized what had happened. But he knew that the vision and the ghost was not something that was caused by the hit he took on the rocks. “Serpenti!” Talons voice shouted down at Serpenti. Serpenti looked up not seeing anything.. “Serpenti!” Talons voice shouted again. “Damn it! I am on my way!” Serpenti felt his head and eyes and realized the head gear was gone.. “NO AMIGO!! I am okay!” Serpenti shouted up. “Ohh thank god!” Talons voice sounded again, this time with a lot of relief present. “But, seams I have lost the head gear, guess I am going to finish this alone.” Serpenti called up while looking down the cave; in the direction the female ghost pointed. “Okay, I will be here. In your pack there is a homing beacon turn it on and Ill be able to track you. ? Talons voice rang out again. “Si Sinor Talon. I’ll see you when this is done, be ready to go in a split second.” Serpenti calls out again this time his voice more somber. “Talon, If things go wrong, Get the hell out of here. DO NOT follow me!” Serpenti says very serious. There is silence for a while? “Sure thing, my captain.” Talon says? but there is none to hear. Serpenti heads off out of distance of the voice coming from the head gear. Glow rod up and sword drawn. “Guns are going to be little help here.” Serpenti thinks as he heads off into the cave. After what seams like a eternity the cave tunnel ends. Set into the rock face is a old stone carved with pictures and glyphs. “Boy they buried this guy deep, some one must have wanted him to stay berried.” Serpenti thinks to his self as he rubs his hand across the surface of the stone. “Yep buried for good” Serpenti whispers, as he pulls a wad of gray clay looking stuff and placing it in the center of the stone. Then he places another small device into the gray matter. Serpenti presses a button causing a beeping sound to fill the tunnel killing the silence. Serpenti runs back down the tunnel always then pulls out a small devise and presses a button on it then drops down covering his head. Shortly a lowed explosion is heard and a flash of light fills the tunnel. A few rocks roll into view as Serpenti climbs to his feet. Serpenti slowly heads back towards the stone door. Approaching he sees the stone blown to bits leaving the way open. “Guess none planed on c4 being invented.” Serpenti says with a smirk as he steps though the hole where the stone used to be. Suddenly the hairs on Serpenti’s neck stand up as the torches ignite upon Serpenti stepping into a small chamber. A casket sits on a raised plat form and numerous treasures and things sit in the corners and hang from the walls. “Magic, never a good sign.” Serpenti says shaking his head, knowing that things just got a bit harder. Serpenti slowly makes his way over to the casket and looks around as he circles it, looking for traps. His head slowly moves over to the stacks of golden coins and plates. “Aztec and Inca gold, this far north of South America. What on earth is it doing here?” Serpenti whispers as he heads over to the treasure. Suddenly Serpenti’s head snaps and looks at a tapestry handing in the center of the small wall. In the center is a sun similar to the one on his arm. Serpenti looks closer? and sees that the sun is not really a part of the tapestry at all. “Cleaver disguise. Many would have over looked this, seeing a cloth tapestry of little value.” Serpenti whispered again. Slowly Serpenti follows the thin golden chain, which is emulated, on the tapestry causing it to look like part of the design. Finding no connections to hidden wires Serpenti slowly pulls the talisman off the tapestry. Serpenti lifts the Talisman up and then places it into a pouch on hi hip. As Serpenti turns around he is greeted by the visage of a large Indian warrior. His face paint looking as if freshly painted. The mans muscles ripple with power. “Who are you to dare steal what is mine?” The warrior says in a booming voice. Serpenti stands there? Trying to think of a way out of this one. To be continued.
