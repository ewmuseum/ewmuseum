Grappling God

1 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.01.04 As Ken sat back in his white leather recliner, his New York City apartment suddenly began feeling more like a home than it had before. His ribs were still hurting from his encounter with Josh in the High Rope Challenge two weeks ago, and the rest of his body was in serious pain from the interfed Hell in a Cell match against WMW at Cross Fire. He hadn't felt this banged up in a long time, and a piece of him was loving it. As much pain as he was in, he knew that he and the rest of Team GW had won the match, which was signifigant proof that Team WMW was in worse pain than him... except, perhaps Dawn MacFarlane, which had really impressed the Superstar in London. 'That was my first match at Wimbley,' thought Ken aloud as his mind rushed to the famous Bret Hart - British Bulldog WWF Intercontinental Championship match held in Wimbeldon and the effect it had on him and his dreams to become a professional wrestler even back on the 29th of August in 1992. That was one of the matches that molded him into what he wanted to become, and what he was today. And, now, not only had he wrestled on that same amazingly sacred ground, but he had competed and came out on the team of victors in the most important match in GW's history in that same arena. Very few things had rivalled that... Ken's first Heavyweight Championship victory, his current International Championship run, and his classic with Ace Harding at WrestleCade. Suddenly, Ken's ailments were all pushed aside as his eyes raced up to one of his many trophy cases. In it, three of Ken's ten Title belts were displayed along with a slew of plaques, photos, and medals. His eyes traced the contours of his old GW Primestar Championship, a belt that had since been retired, and then over to his first ever strap... the RWWF Television Championship. However, when his eyes spotted the next... his mind began a backwards race through time. The Grappling God Championship glimmered beautifully in the track-lighting of Ken's apartment. It was so gorgeaous, it almost looked like it did as the referee held it above his head on the night that Ken won it from Osiris in the now defunct Power House Wrestling Federation. It was something that meant a lot to Ken. Perhaps because it was the first title that he won with Gunner Addison by his side. However, it could have been because of the complexity of the belt. The Grappling God Title could only be won by submission and was a belt designed to be worn around the waist of the true marvels of the squared circle. Ken was the last man to hold that belt, as he even took it to another organization after PHWF closed and was recognized as the official Grappling God Champion in the International Wrestling Association. It and the PHWF Heavyweight Championship of the World were two titles that he had never seen around anyone else's waist after it had garnished his. No matter what, he would always be a Grappling God. Grappling God... Master of the Mat... they fit well together. There was no doubt in Ken's mind that he could win the Master of the Mat award. However, he also knew that the toughest of all the tournament challenges would be the very first round. How was he supposed to trust somebody that was escorted around by two men carrying fully automatic hand cannons? Then again, that's what you would want going into a gun fight. Juan Gonzalez was unique, indeed. Ken wasn't one to deny the fact that he had an amazing talent. Still, though, the trust issue remained. 'Maybe this is a problem I should fix myself.' ____________________________________________________________________________ The black, silver, and gold Elite chopper sat down on the helipad, kicking a thick cloud of dust up into the air making it difficult to see. As the blades were coming to a halt and the wall of brown had descended, the door slid open to reveal KPJ in total business mode. He let his black flat-toed pradas touch down before walking into the small building before him. Ken was dusting off his black Armani suit as he opened the door and adjusting the silver tie that was lining his black shirt as he walked to the front desk. 'May I help you, sir,' asked the man at the counter, failing to look up. That was the type of thing that Ken couldn't stand, as it showed a lack of respect. When one can't even acknowledge another's presence with a little eye contact, Ken wanted nothing more than to grab their faces and force them to look. As Partridge stood there, silent with no reply, the man finally looked up. As his eyes sat upon Ken's face, his jaw dropped and a smile rushed across his lips. 'Ken Partridge Jr,' he began gleefully, 'We've been expecting you. I must say I'm a big fan of yours. We all are around here. We're also all very excited. It's not everyday we have a star of your stature here with us.' Ken rolled his eyes back before letting out a deep sigh and running his hands through his hair. Noticing Ken's sign of frustration, the man, stopped talking and looked at Ken in curiosity. 'Look,' stated Ken bluntly, 'I'd really appreciate it if you'd just get me to my limo and have my driver ready to go.' 'Oh,' stumbled the man, 'absolutly. I'm terribly sorry about that Mr. Partridge. We'll have it ready to go in just a minute. Let me take care of it all now.' As the man scurried off, Ken looked around, but his eyes were paying no attention to anything that struck them. Instead, his mind raced into thoughts about what would happen in the next hour. He was curious as to how J-Gonz was going to react upon seeing him and whether or not it would be a business meeting or gun-fight. It was times like this that Ken wished that he was the type of person to carry a gun, but he wasn't. He was confident enough in his technique and his ability to use his environment not to carry one. Then, there was also the fact that he simply didn't like them. He was only hoping that this meeting wouldn't give him reason to dislike them even more.
