Finding the Tickets

22 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 22 (#1 for PPV) Roswell is seen taping up his wrists with black tape. He bites it to make it snap off. Roswell walks over to Larry and starts showing him some moves that he learned when he wasn’t there. Larry picks up some papers and hands them to Roswell, he starts to read them carefully so they don’t rip. Roswell: Wow! So there is going to be a little tournament here and there will be all these trainees in it. Am I in it? Larry: Of course that’s why I handed you the papers so you can sign them to make sure your in it. Roswell reaches into his pocket and takes out a blue pen and starts to write on the papers. All set! Larry: It is tomorrow, so be prepared. Is it alright if you train today with a guy named Tony Shock? Roswell: That’s why I came here today. Roswell walks over to the Men’s Shower Room door and goes in. Inside are the light orange lockers and blue tile flooring. Roswell walks over to the black bench and sits down. He starts taking off his shirt and pants, he then grabs his gym bag from underneath the bench and lifts his wrestling singlet out. He slips it on with the boots elbow and knee pads. He quickly puts his normal clothes in the bag and runs out the door. Roswell walks behind a red curtain and waits for about 5 seconds and then “Halo” by Soil is heard through the room and Roswell walks out from behind the curtain. Roswell jumps up to the apron and goes over the rope. He puts his arms in the air and waves. “Remedy” by Cold is heard and a man about 25 wearing a black shirt with a thunder bolt on it and blue jean shorts walks out from the curtain. He slides under the ropes and also waves to nothing. Roswell walks over to the guy and he starts backing up afraid of Roswell. But Roswell catches him and puts out his left hand to shake. Roswell and Tony lock up with Tony somehow taking the advantage. Tony hits Roswell with a spinning heel kick, he then hits Roswell with a flying forearm. Tony picks Roswell up and kicks him in the balls. Tony again picks Roswell up and kicks him in the balls for the second time. Roswell comes back and nails Tony with a right hand. Roswell kicks Tony in the gut and gives him a vicious powerbomb. 1...................................... 2...................................... THR!!No! Roswell brings Tony up and hits him with a Crucifix Powerbomb. Roswell brings him up yet again and hits a Vicious Spike Double Arm DDT! Roswell picks him up again and hits him with another Crucifix Powerbomb. 1..................................... 2........................................ 3.................................... DING DING DING Roswell brings Tony up and shakes his hand yet again and raises it showing respect. “Halo” by Soil is heard again and Roswell leaves the ring with Tony limping in the ring. Roswell walks over to Larry and starts speaking. Roswell: You know, I think this kid will have a chance in the tournament. Roswell walks into the shower room again and 5 minutes later he walks out all dressed in his normal street clothes. Roswell walks out of the room and walks down the long narrow hallway and out the door of the building. Roswell: What a nice day out. Roswell digs into his jacket pockets and feels something. He pulls them out and they are 2 tickets with gold stamps at the top. Roswell starts thinking what these could be of. Then he starts running down the street. Roswell: Oh my gushers! These are band tickets, I was supposed to see Soil perform live tomorrow! But the tournament! Roswell runs down the street till he comes to the big blue house, Roswell runs up the steps and opens the door. Inside Roswell walks over to the couch and lays down trying to rest from the running.
