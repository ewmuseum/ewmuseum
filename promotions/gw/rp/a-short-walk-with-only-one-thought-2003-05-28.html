A short walk with only one thought

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/28/03 The dark cover of the night was something that Salaok always did find comforting. The dim blue come coming from the moon overhead if the sky was clear. But if it wasn't clear, there was darkness. Complete and unadulterated darkness which most would feel hesitant to walk out into, but not Salaok. Fear was something he knew, but not as something he felt. The 'Flight or Fight' theory didn't sit in Salaok's subconscious, instead there was only Fight. If something was foolish enough to get in his way then it deserved to be attacked. Or at least so he thought. The days in the office were long, but at least it was money. More money. And more money was always good. But what else could he possibly want. Casinos and strip clubs he already owned. The Global Wrestling Inc was half his and the other half simply didn't matter to him. Cars? He had plenty, although he was still needing to get the old hearse fixed after what Deacon Holmes had done to it. He didn't need to splash his money around to get the attention of women. He all ready had the best one he could find and he had no need for others. Lisha was his love, while Darrell, was his pride. But with one man he knew, his family was almost everything to him. Family and friends was something that could always be used against you. Could always be used to trick and you make you do things you normally wouldn't do. Salaok knew this because he had tormented many a family in his time and had even had his own wife stolen and kidnapped at the start of this year. It was due to that reason along that Salaok now had two security guards patrolling the ground of his mansion that others seemed to refer to as a castle. Just because a building has large stone walls, twenty-something bed rooms, and look like something out of old-England, doesn't always been it is a castle. But it sounded good none-the-less. But there was always one family that he loved to play games with more than any other. Even more than the Welch family these days. No, now it was Jamal's family which he loved to be ever o nice to. Infact, this week along he had sent tickets for them to go and be in the front row at DeadZone to see Brick take the center stage and fight it out for the World title. A title in which Brick still claims to have been cheated out of by Salaok some few years back now. Something that Salaok knows is implanted well into Brick's mind and is exactly why those tickets were sent to his family. The thought of something happening to them would drive him insane. But more importantly, the idea of them watching as the nevermore did the same thing to him this time as they did last time would make Salaok's day. Not only would he have made a sizable amount off the PPV, but he also would have had the pleasure of seeing Brick's momma sitting there as the Harvester's took apart her baby boy. He would smile as Monica saw her brother beaten to near death, only to be allowed to walk free again so they could torment him another day. Or more importantly, being able to see Aaron's face as Salaok likes up a cigarette right near his face and let him smell the same object which had burnt his skin time after time after time while being trapped in a small cage in the basement of Salaok's castle. A grin slowly makes it's way across Salaok's face as his feet scuff up some of the loose dirt along the pathway. The idea of letting Brick go through the same amount of torment was something that made Salaok simply feel all warm and gooy on the inside about. RING RING! RING RING! Salaok had always vowed to change the ring tone of his cell phone but never did get around to doing it. It was Old School and something about that was growing on Salaok. Letting a breath out of his lungs and the cold air turns it to frost in mid air, Salaok reaches into his pocket and pulls out the cell phone. Looking at the screen, he knew the number and can't help but laugh before finally answering it. 'Salaok's escort service, how may we serve you Brick?' Bam, straight away the yelling comes fast and furious down the phone to the point where Salaok has no choice but to hold the phone away from his ear so the yell's are audible. 'Tisk tisk Brick. I do a favor for your family and you ring me up and abuse the crap out of me? Now how is that to treat someone who just splashed out on your family?' Again the verbal abuse continues down the line before the phone cuts dead which Salaok could only take pleasure in thinking that it was Brick throwing the phone across the room out of frustration. He takes a big deep breath in and holds it for a second before letting back out into the night air. Again the breath from his lungs turns into frost and as he moves forward, he walks straight through it. His head turns up wards to sky and looks at the patches of the sky which aren't completely covered by the clouds of what looks to be a fairly nicely building storm. The old star shines here and there but darkness is the major winner of the skies tonight. 'Oh Jamal. When will you learn? You work for me now. I own you.' That thought pauses Salaok for a moment and it is just enough to draw his attention to the fact that he doesn't have a cigarette in his mouth which is soon fixed. The box is pulled from his pocket and flung open faster than a street worker's legs. Within seconds Salaok has lit up the cigarette and the sweet smell of burning tobacco filling the air and trails behind him as he walks. 'It is times like these that you should be happy that your family are around you. This could be your big night. Your big win Jamal and yet all you do is worry about your family. You don't get it do you? I've won already. The mere fact that no matter what, you mind will not fully be on this match and that will destroy you. Not that Sage wouldn't have done so anyways.' A crack of lightning in the distance gives life to the death-like darkness which surrounds Salaok as he walks. The lights of his castle were growing brighter and brighter the more he walked. Asking his driver to drop him at the gate was something that he was glad that he does now. First it was simply to stop the guy from thinking he's welcome to come in and have a drink. but now it was Salaok's chance to gather his thoughts. And tonight's thoughts were extra sweet. 'You see Jamal, I've let you get this far. The Nevermore let you get this far. Do you really think that if I hadn't wanted you to get to the main event that you would be there? Didn't you think it strange that I was not entered in this tournament as one of the eight? Do you really think that Quartz was too slow in stopping that pin last week? No you fool. We let it happen. We are the reason you are in the main event this time.' The idea that Brick thinks he had made it this far on his own seemed to make Salaok laugh on the inside but didn't make any noise on the outside. His lip turning up at the corner was the only indication of this but it was quickly removed as the cigarette was shoved back in his mouth. 'No Brick, I've got plans for you. Plans that not even you could see coming. But then again, you never thought that I would kidnap a member of your family did you? And then again, you never thought I would be able to get to Monica's lovely wedding. And oh how lovely she did look in that stunning white dress.' Again the lightning cracks open the sky and shadows fill the area. Shadows of the trees and shadows of the clouds. His own shadow, although not sen by him, was completely blacked out but in the quick flash of light, it looked like Salaok was wearing a wide brimmed hat, much like his older brother used to wear. This time the thunder rolls in and echoes as it bounces off each of the clouds and makes it's rounds of the sky. It slowly builds up as it makes it's way to Salaok and reaches almost defending levels until it slowly drains off again into the nothing from where it first began. The idea of something so powerful and explosive just forming out of nothing was something that had always struck home with Salaok for it was exactly how he was told The Lair was formed. Out of no-where the alliances were made and the group with such power was formed from basically nothing over night. But unlike the lightning and the thunder, The Lair wasn't about to drain off into the nothing from were it once came. The Lair was going to be around forever, no matter how many people like Brick tried to stand in it's way. Again the cigarette makes it's way up to Salaok's mouth and his lips pressed down around it. Breathing in the thick dark smoke and letting it drift down into his lungs, he keeps it there fore a while to make sure that as much of the nicotine as he could possibly get was absorbed by his body. Finally he breathes out through his nose and two pillars of smoke trail down towards the ground, much like a dragon in some child's cartoon. Only thing was, this wasn't a cartoon, Salaok was real. He was the real thing that had troubled so many people for so long. He had stepped up after his brother had disappeared and led the Lair into a new era. He had done what he was always going to do. There had been a reason for Johnathon and Quartz making sure that Salaok's path led towards The Lair when he was a child. He was the one in their master's eyes who would be the next true leader and now it had been for-filled. He had followed the steps that had been planned out for him and yet never explained. He never knew why the old master had taken to him so much but now he found himself in his master's shoes. The power of The Lair was at his call and the only ones he had to honor still where those who were around before him. But these days there weren't many of those left. Only Quartz, Johnathon and Flare. But Quartz seemed happy to just sit back and let everything happen in it's own time, Johnathon had disappeared off the face of the earth again, and Flare was at The Lair's old castle, trying to rebuild the warriors in that region. Finally Salaok was one of the most powerful men within the underground crime world and within The Lair. 'Oh Jamal, this time, you shall be at the heart of the best game to date. This time will be one of the crowning moments of my time that I have known you. Not because you finally get your ass out of the gutter and over-come all odds. No, but because this time the games will take another step up Brick.' A droplet of rain falls from the ground and splashes into tiny drips upon impact with Salaok's shoulder. The house was only a hundred feet or so away now and there was no point in running to stay out of the rain. A little bit of water hurt anyone thought Salaok. As one drop turns into thousands, Salaok walks at the same speed he always had along the dirt path which was slowly turning into mud under his feet. He raises the cigarette to his mouth again but it's basically useless as the rain has soaked the paper and it was falling apart with every step that he takes. With a flick of the wrist, the cigarette flies through the air and lands on the ground in front of him, only to have Salaok's foot stomp down on it without him falling out of stride. He finally makes it to the front of the house and stands at the front door, brushing some of the water off his jacket. He turns and looks at the grounds of his property just in time to see it light up again as the sky opens with another huge crack of lightning with touches down just beyond the front gates and the explosion of sound which follows rattles the windows as fire spits up into the air and a warm push of air flows onto Salaok's face. As the corner of his lips curl up, he chuckles to himself. 'Yes Brick, this time you will truly see just how powerful we are.'
