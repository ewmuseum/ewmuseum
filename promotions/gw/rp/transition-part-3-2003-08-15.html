Transition, part 3

15 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 15th, 2003 I awoke the next morning to, rather annoyingly, the sound of pounding on my apartment's door. My annoyance was ameliorated somewhat by the naked girl sleeping with her legs and arms wrapped around me, though. It was tough to extricate myself from Satomi without waking her up, and while I worked at it the pounding just kept on. I found myself thinking that not only must Satomi-chan be able to sleep through anything, but that whoever was at my door must be getting a really sore hand. Finally, I managed to slip out of the bed. I quickly threw on my favorite gold kimono, and tugged on a pair of boxers as I dashed for the door. Skidding to a stop on the hardwood floor, I unlocked the door and swung it open. And as repayment for my efforts, I got a hard left to the jaw. I rubbed my jaw, and blinked hard to clear my sleep-blurred vision. I knew that left cross? 'Good morning, Yoshiko-chan,' I muttered, as the lithe young woman with snow-white hair barged past me into the foyer, 'what brings you here so early?' Yoshiko spun to face me, the muscles of her flawless gymnast's body tensing beneath smooth, milky skin. Soft pink lips frowned, and cobalt blue eyes darkened, her noble features twisting into a scowl. 'Why do you THINK I'm here, Lion?' 'Well, uh,' I stuttered, nowhere near awake enough to answer questions from angry women. 'You,' she jabbed my chest with a finger, 'just up and decide to leave IWL, to go across the ocean to AMERICA? and do you tell me? Do you tell ANY of your friends? No! What the hell, Lion? What do you have to say for yourself?' 'Well, uh?' I rubbed my eyes, trying to shake off the last vestiges of sleep, 'I'm sorry 'shiko, I should have told you guys first... but I didn't want word getting out before I met with Genbu. I wanted to see the look on that pig's face when he heard I was leaving him in the dust.' 'Well,' asked Yoshiko, still scowling, 'was it worth it?' 'Are you kidding me? I only wish I'd thought to tape it.' Yoshiko continued to scowl, and hit me in the arm. 'You didn't TAPE it? Come ON Rokku, think of your friends!' At the same time, we both broke up laughing. 'Was it really that good?' she asked, when our laughter subsided. 'Oh, definitely,' I replied, 'he was shaking, red in the face, squealing at the highest pitch his voice could manage? it was classic. Telling him I quit to his face was an experience I'll take to my grave.' 'I'll bet. Still, you should have told the guys in the locker room. You leaving is going to shake up a lot of things. Plus, some of them are your friends.' I smirked. 'Still the Silken Crane, even off the job. Y'know, when I'm gone, I think I might miss how you always tell me how things affect everyone but yourself.' Yoshiko raised a well-groomed eyebrow. 'You were expecting some kind of outpouring of emotion?' 'Well, I had toyed with the idea. I can see it now! Kakita Yoshiko, running down the streets of Tokyo, tears streaming down her face as she rushed to reach her ONE TRUE LOVE in time!' 'Oh, please!' said Yoshiko, rolling her eyes. ''Oh, Rokku!' I exclaimed in a falsetto voice, dropping to my knees and hugging her denim-clad legs, 'don't leave me! I love you!' I looked up at Yoshiko's face, and found her glaring down at me with contempt. I cracked up, falling onto my back and shaking with laughter. Smirking a little, Yoshiko flipped her long white hair over her shoulder, and marched into the adjoining kitchen. 'For that,' she said, as she began rummaging through my fridge, 'you owe me breakfast.' 'Fine, fine,' I said, as I got back on my feet, 'I'll make you something. Any requests?' Yoshiko backed away from the refrigerator, and hoisted herself up to sit on the kitchen counter. 'I'm feeling like eggs today,' she said, 'sunny-side up, if you would.' 'Yes Yoshiko-sama' I replied, affecting a submissive tone and bowing low. Yoshiko laughed. A few minutes later, the eggs were sizzling away as Yoshiko and I chatted about my plans in America. That was when Satomi came striding out of the bedroom, stretching and yawning -- and wearing nothing more than a satisfied smile. 'Good morning Rokku-chaaaaAAAAA!' Satomi immediately turned around, and darted back into the bedroom. 'Hrm,' I said, 'I'd better put on a few more eggs.' Yoshiko quirked an eyebrow at me and smirked. 'What?' I said, before breaking into a broad grin.
