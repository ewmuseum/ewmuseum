Lord of the Ring

4 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.04.05 The injuries which he had endured in Tokyo had taken their toll on the body of Ken Partridge, Jr. With hindsight being twenty-twenty, he had come to the conclusion that diving off of the top of the steel structure and onto a gourney which housed John Zero with a Kenton Bomb may not have been the smartest thing he'd ever done... but he did win the match. His back may ache, his knees may hurt, and his body may suffer... but he did win the match. However, there was now a new battle on the horizon. One for which he had been preparing for over a year now. One more war for the unclaimed Heavyweight Championship of the World. Moreover, just as Lord Aragorn, Ranger of the Dunedain, had claimed the vacated crown of Gondor, so would Ken do with the title of World Champion. Although, Ken would not be facing the Dark Lord Sauron, but two great threats by the name's of Christian Zenith and Jackson-Namenlos Duisternis, the battle awaiting him would surely be trying and testing. However, while Ken did not take these two men lightly, they were simply Nazgul Ring Wraiths to him. The true Dark Lord which he longed to face was Ice. Ice had disappeared before Ken had his opportunity to get his hands on him at Showdown in Tokyo, almost conveniantly. Nobody seemed to know where he was or how to lure him out of his place of dwelling, either. But Ken knew how to... just as the Gollum, Smiegal had tracked Frodo for the One Ring, Ken knew that Ice would forever be drawn to his precious. 'You seem distracted, Champ,' said a concerned Bill Moresi. 'You alright?' 'What,' responded KPJ, finally snapping back into reality. 'Of course I am. Why wouldn't I be?' 'Well,' Bill began, 'for starters, that vein in your forehead was throbbing again. Then, on top of that, you've been on that cross-ramp for about twenty minutes longer than usual.' 'I'm just in the zone, I guess,' defended Ken as he realized that he'd been doing a steady uphill incline for an hour and twenty minutes and hadn't even noticed. 'This match coming up isn't exactly a joke.' Ken got off of the cross-ramp and began his cool down stretches and Yoga techniques as his friend continued. 'Why did you keep saying his name,' asked Bill somberly. 'Whose name?' 'Ice's,' said Bill quietly. Both men became quie tand still for a few moments, allowing only the monitor showing Ken's past matches to make any noise at all. Ken's mind raced, as he didn't remember saying anything aloud while he was training. But, Bill knew... Ken needen't have said a word for Bill to know. 'He's been haunting me, Bill,' Ken broke the silence. 'He betrayed us, stabbed us all in the back and disappeared like a coward. To think that I once called him brother makes me ill. Knowing that he's to scared to take me on so that we can settle things once and for all... that makes me plain sick. I can't sleep at night. I just toss and turn. Gunner's starting to get worried... which means he's indirectly hurting her... which is my final straw.' Ken wiped the beads of sweat from his forehead with a white towel. His black ribbed tank was sticking to his rippling body, revealing him to be in quite possibly the best shape he's ever been in. His muscles were jumping and spasming from the training they had just endured coupled with the pure anger and disdain that was coursing through Ken's veins. 'Bill,' finished Ken, 'I will win this World Title. It's been my dream since coming to Global. I will show everyone that I am the best. But afterwards, that will lure Ice back out of his cave.' Ken turned to look at Bill, his cold blue eyes piercing through Moresi's as he spoke with all of the calmness and seriousness in the world. 'I will break him.'
