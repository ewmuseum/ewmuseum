From the von Doom Archives: An Introduction to the Killer Pirates

2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Early 2005 An Introduction to the Killer Pirates 'Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean' Show Intro [We begin with a typical TV show intro for this one. There's a black bearded Pirate Captain on screen with an eye patch on his left eye.] Captain: Are you ready kids? Kids: Aye aye Captain! Captain: I can't hear you. Kids: Aye aye Captain! ['Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean' them music starts playing.] Captain: OHHHHHHH...Who lives on ship floating on top of the sea? Kids: Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Captain: Big and fat and hungry is he! Kids: Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Captain: If no-brained wrestling nonsense be something you wish! Kids: Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Captain: Then sleep on the deck and smell like a fish! Kids: Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Captain: Ready? Captain and Kids: Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Kil-Ler Klown from Ou- Ter Space! Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Captain: Cap-Tain Mor-GAAAAANNNNNN!!! Captain: HA HA HA HA !!!! [We end the song with a flute playing for a moment before the 'Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean' logo appears on the screen.] [Cut.] * * * The Origin of Captain Jack Morgan [The scene opens outside in New York City with the song “Raind Drops Keep Falling on my Head” by Hal David playing in the background. A young New Yorker in his early twenties by the name of Mike Skinner is skipping along toward his apartment building. A car honks nearby.] #Raindrops keep falling on my head... #And just like the guy whose feet are too big for his bed,# #Nothing seems to fit. #Those raindrops are falling on my head, they keep falling.# [Mike turns and waves at the man.] Mike: “HI!” [The man in the car flips him off.] Man: “F*** YOU!” [But Mike ignores him and keeps skipping along with a smile on his face.] #So I just did me some talking to the sun.# #And I said I didn't like the way, he got things done?# #Sleeping on the job..#. #Those raindrops are falling on my head, they keep falling. # [And then, just as Mike reaches his apartment building, a pot falls from a balcony overhead and hits him in the head. He then falls to the sidewalk in a heap and the scene fades to black.] A FEW HOURS LATER? [Mike wakes up in a hospital room and looks around.] Mike: [looks left] “Where” [pause, looks right] Where am I?” [He holds his bandaged head in pain and then notices that the television is on in his hospital room. He looks up and sees that it’s the Disney flick “Pirates of the Caribbean: Curse of the Black Pearl.”] Mike: “My name is?” [Long pause, followed by him talking like a pirate.] Mike: “Garrr, can’t remember me name.” [He scratches his head and sees the television. He sees Captain Jack Sparrow on there. He then looks puzzled and then turns to see a bottle of Captain Morgan his brother left on his bedside for him. Mike’s face then lights up like a lightbulb.] Mike: “Avast ye landlubbers! Captain Jack Morgan’s my name and plundering’s my game!” [And, with that, the scene fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * The Origin of the 'Killer Klown from Outer Space' [This is the only known footage of the 'Killer Klown from Outer Space's' debut in the James von Doom promo entitled 'A New Beginning.' It was originally broadcast on 6/6/2004 at 3:08 PM on the DWS promo network in the NWC. The footage begins near the middle of the promo as James von Doom is motioning for the camera to leave.] 'Now, if you idiots will excuse me, I must feast.' [James pulls his fork up and is ready to dive in when the doorbell rings. He looks pissed. He gets up and grumbles as he walks over to front door. He unlocks it and pulls it open with authority. He then yells out angrily without remorse.] 'OK! WHAT THE HELL DO YOU WANT?!' [He then looks and sees a whole bunch of the nerdy writers who work for the DWS standing in front of him. There have to be at least 20 of them standing there, glaring at him with much anger and spite. James smiles and laughs. He then points down the street.] 'Oh! I'm sorry. The Revenge of the Nerds Convention is down the street. Good day.' [Just as he is about to slam the door shut, one of the writers blocks it and begins yelling at him.] Nerdy Writer#1: 'We're sick of you pushing us around!' Nerdy Writer#2: 'YEAH! We've had it up to here with you James!' [James gives them a WTF look and then brushes them off.] 'What the hell are you nerds talking about? I mean, don't you have a Star Trek marathon to watch or something?' [The first Nerdy Writer flips him off.] Nerdy Writer#1: 'F(beep) you von Doom! We're sick of you and your bulls(beep)!' [A third Nerdy Writer, this time a fat one, steps in and hands James a script.] Nerdy Writer#3: 'So we've taken the liberty of ruining your career even more than you have already by opening your big mouth.' [James glares at him.] 'And what the f(beep) is that supposed to mean you little s(beep)head?' Nerdy Writer#3: 'That means that we've given you a really stupid sidekick who has one of the worst gimmicks ever created.' 'Wait a minute...' [James looks down at the script and starts flipping through it. He has a deer in headlights look on his face now. He can't believe what he's reading. He then looks up at all the nerds and smiles.] 'This is a joke, right?' Nerdy Writer#1: 'Good day, James.' [The writers start walking off.] 'You can't be serious!' [The writers ignore him and walk off. James then shuts the door and stares down at the script, shaking his head.] 'They really can't be serious.' [James walks over and sets the script down on his coffee table and then walks over to the kitchen table to start eating. The camera, however, pans down onto the script and the words on the cover reveal who his new sidekick is. The words are etched across the screen and are written in pink letters on the cover just to piss James off. The writers know that pink is his least favorite color.] 'KILLER KLOWN FROM OUTER SPACE' [Cut.] 6/8/2004, 5:18 PM [A few days after the previous incident, James received a videotape from DWS Management in the mail of the test scene of his new sidekick. James is sitting on his couch and has just opened up the package he received. The camera zooms in on James as he stares at the unmarked videotape.] 'Now what the f(beep) is this s(beep)? First the Crotch Crusaders and Prime, [pause] and now this.' [James turns on his television and pulls the video out of its case.] 'Well, better see what I'm dealing with.' [James gets up and places the videotape into his VCR. He presses play on his remote when he sits back down You all will see what the angry writers have stuck him with right...NOW.] [Action!] [The scene opens outside, near the woods. There is a rustling noise in the background. The camera pans over to show a teenage girl in a cheerleader's outfit with a Letterman's jacket over it sneaking home after a make out session with her 30 year old boyfriend who works in stocks. As she tip toes along the way, she hears an owl hoot and looks around.] Girl: [acting badly] 'This is SOOOO scary.' [The girl continues along. She looks up at the full moon, which the camera zooms in on, but then looks around when she hears a loud groan.] Girl: [gulps] 'Hello?' [Suddenly, a large hand comes out of nowhere and taps her on the shoulder. Startled, she turns around to see a large man dressed like one of the clowns in the campy '80's horror flick entitled 'Killer Klowns from Outer Space.' He smiles at her with a big, toothy smile. She quietly waves at him.] Girl: 'Hello. My name is Jessica. What is your name?' Killer Klown from Outer Space (KKFOS): 'Kwendo mahi stingo.' [The girl has no idea what he's talking about.] Girl: [being ditzy] 'Oh...that's nice. [looking around cautiously] So, how long have you lived here?' KKFOS: 'Jinxsa mo fudu aki zambaie tay.' [The girl is now beginning to think he's some sort of refugee from a country that is in crisis, such as Yemen or the United States. *shakes head* Kids now days. Haven't a clue as to what country they're living in.] Girl: 'Ummmm...ok. I'm going home now. Later Clownie.' [Just as she turns to leave, KKFOS starts to walk behind her. You can hear he crunching noises from his feet walking over some twigs on the road. The girl turns around and the Klown motions for her to come here with his index finger. The girl turns and runs off, leaving our clowny pal all alone. He shrugs his shoulders and head toward the mothership...which just happens to be shaped like a giant circus tent. AND IT'S LOCATED RIGHT BY A TOWN NEAR YOU!] [The tape ends and the camera switches back to James, who looks to be in shock. He then pauses for a moment and then smiles, shrugging his shoulders.] 'Well, it could be worse. I could be stuck with a Transvestite dressed in drag.' [James gets up, shuts off his VCR and TV, and then walks off screen with a sort of smirk on his face. He's already planning to get his revenge on the writers, Prime, and Paradox, but it will take time. But maybe his new *cough* sidekick could help. Just maybe...] [Scene fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * Introduction to Captain Jack Morgan [The scene opens on a beautiful, sunny day in Port Royal. There is to be a parade in the streets. But, by the docks, something is going on. A ship is headed toward the dock but is sinking as it comes closer. The one mate on-board, I guess he's the Captain, has climbed the flagpole and is up by the waving French flag as the ship nears the dock. The camera scans around to show all of the sailors on the other ships docked there just staring at him. The ship is now right next to the dock as he sticks his boot out and steps onto the dock. The camera pans around and zooms in on him. He begins speaking to the camera ala 'Wayne's World' like Captain Jack Sparrow from the 'Pirates of the Caribbean' movie.] Jack: 'Hello fans. My name is Captain Jack Morgan.' [He has a cheesy little grin on his face.] Jack: 'I am from the port of Tortuga. It's a suburb of Guam. Anyway, I'm here in Port Royal to, shall I say, find a new mode of transportation [motioning toward his sunken ship] considering than mind has, alas, sunken to the Carp depths at the edge of the dock.' [Jack continues walking but the dock master stops him.] Dock Master: 'Say, you there! You're going to have to pay to dock here.' [Jack turns to him and gives him a look.] Jack: 'How much?' Dock Master: 'One shilling and your name, sir.' Jack: [thinking to himself for a moment] 'Hmmmm... [pulling out some coins] How about three shillings and we forget the name.' [Jack lays them out on the Dock Master's logbook and the Dock Master's eyes widen. He suddenly gets a smile on his face.] Dock Master: 'Welcome to Port Royal Mr. Smith.' [Jack smiles and turns the other way to continue walking down the dock. He then notices a bag of shillings on the Dock Master's stand. He picks it up, shakes it, and then puts it in his pocket.] Jack: 'Now, isn't there something I must be doing? [snaps his fingers] Oh yes. The party.' [Jack walks off in the other direction as the scene fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * Meanwhile... [The parade is starting up and all of the guards are on duty. None of them has a clue that there's a pirate in town. There are a couple of notable characters in the crowd. One is dressed in a clown suit. His name is the 'Killer Klown from Outer Space.' He's one of the most popular wrestling personalities to come around in years. He's basically an escaped mental patient who thinks he's a killer clown from the campy '80's horror flick 'Killer Klowns from Outer Space.' He only speaks in a weird, space clown retard dialect that no one can decipher. The man he used to manage, Deimos, is standing next to him. Deimos is wearing a 'Big Trouble in Little China' T-shirt and comfortable lounge shorts. They are watching the parade. The camera zooms in on them as the clown taps James on the shoulder.] KKFOS: [pointing toward the parade] 'Ashti madio ZAPOWA!' [Deimos smiles.] Deimos: 'That's right. The parade is starting.' KKFOS: [giving a thumbs up] 'UNDO!' [Undo, pronounced 'Oon-doe,' is the only word we know the meaning of in the clown's language. It means 'awesome' and he always says it giving a thumbs up. As they are watching the parade, some street toughs from the Wing Kong gang bump into James. James turns and gives them a look.] Deimos: 'HEY!' [The clown grabs him by the arm and James turns to him. The clown shakes his finger.] KKFOS: 'Nou fitianti.' [Deimos rolls his eyes.] Deimos: 'Ok.' [Just then, everybody hears an explosion and looks toward the docks. All they see is a big cloud of dust. As that is going on and everybody is distracted, a Chinese man is waving to his girlfriend who just arrived at the bus stop across the street.] Man: [waving] 'Miao Yin!' [As he does, he sees the Lords of Death going after his friend. One of them trips his friend and another one runs across the street and kidnaps his girlfriend at gunpoint! The two men chase after the Lords of Death. Deimos then notices the man in a wheelchair next to him is laughing. This man is David Lo Pan, the Godfather of Chinatown in San Francisco. Deimos turns to him and taps him on the shoulder.] Deimos: 'Say, what's so funny old man?' [Lo Pan gives him a look.] Lo Pan: [acting like he can't hear him] 'Huh? What? What was that?' Deimos: 'What's so funny?' Lo Pan: [motioning toward the commotion] 'This! Chaos! I love it!' Deimos: 'Good point. At least it ain't boring.' [As he says this, Captain Jack Morgan comes running by and runs right into him and the clown. Deimos looks angry. He picks Jack up and is ready to punch him but stops.] Deimos: 'Nice costume. Just escape from the psycho ward, huh?' [Lo Pan gives him a look.] Jack: 'No, mate. Just running some errands.' Lo Pan: 'What is your problem Mr. Von Doom?' [Jack turns to Lo Pan and smiles.] Jack: 'No problems here, mate.' Deimos: [confused] 'How the hell did he know my name? [acting all serious] And it's Deimos now, by the way. Read the script.' [Lo Pan pulls out his script and reads through it.] Lo Pan: 'Oh yes.' [Lo Pan puts his script away. He then turns his motorized wheelchair around and speeds off but not before leaving with parting words.] Lo Pan: [as he's driving off] 'I saw you in Entertainment Weekly you prick!' [Deimos gets pissed.] Deimos: [angry] 'HEY! [flipping him off] Go eat your baby food you crazy old fool!' [Just then, three giant men dressed like Raiden from Mortal Kombat, who are apparently Lo Pan's bodyguards, point at Deimos, Jack, and the clown. Deimos turns to Jack.] Jack: 'I say we best get out of here, mate.' [Deimos and the clown nod their heads in agreement.] Deimos: Yeah. Good idea.' [The three of them back away and then start running in the other direction only to have to head down the nearest alley when they see the island's soldiers looking for Jack. Then, suddenly, the scene fades to black and we are left with this crap at the end.] STAY TUNED FOR NEXT WEEK'S THRILLING CONCLUSION! [Awww! It's over. Oh well. Part two coming soon. Later kids. 'Later Captain!'] [Cut.] * * * The Very Unexciting Conclusion to Their Introduction 'Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean' Show Intro [We begin with a typical TV show intro for this one. Captain: Cap-Tain Mor-GAAAAANNNNNN!!! Captain: HA HA HA HA !!!! [We end the song with a flute playing for a moment before the 'Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean' logo appears on the screen.] [Cut.] * * * Running through the Back Alleys Filmed: 01/25/05 Narrator: “As we left our daring heroes, here is what happened to them at the conclusion of our first exciting episode!” [Here is a flashback to what happened last time! Enjoy it folks!] [As he does, he sees the Lords of Death going after his friend. One of them trips his friend and another one runs across the street and kidnaps his girlfriend at gunpoint! The two men chase after the Lords of Death. Deimos then notices the man in a wheelchair next to him is laughing. This man is David Lo Pan, the Godfather of Chinatown in San Francisco. Deimos turns to him and taps him on the shoulder.] Deimos: 'Say, what's so funny old man?' [Lo Pan gives him a look.] Lo Pan: [acting like he can't hear him] 'Huh? What? What was that?' But they bump into some old friends from before! Be sure to watch the thrilling conclusion of this tale’RIGHT NOW! ENJOY!” [They run unto the two men they saw running earlier. Jack gets bumped into a nearby garbage can, as does the KKFOS. One is a tall man with a bad '80's haircut, a Chinese design tanktop, jeans, and work boots. The other is a young Chinese guy in his twenties whose wearing a jack, black pants, and a white shirt. The taller guy helps the Chinese guy up, considering he ran right into Jack who got bumped back into the clown. Deimos turns and gives them a look.] Deimos: 'Where the hell are you two idiots running off to?' Tall Man: 'HEY! Don't talk to us like that! [Deimos smiles.] Deimos: 'I wasn't talking to you. [motioning toward the KPFOC] I was talking to them.' Tall Man: 'Oh...' Deimos: 'What the hell were you guys running from?' [They then hear police sirens and hear soldiers stomping in their direction. The KKFOS gets up and helps Jack up to his feet.] Tall Man: 'We'd best get going.' Chinese Guy: 'Yeah. They're after us!' [The Tall Man shakes his head.] Tall Man: 'No! They're after him!' [He points at Jack. Jack shrugs his shoulders.] Jack: 'What?! What did I do?' Tall Guy: 'COME ON!' [The Tall Guy and the Chinese Guy run off down the alley, making a right down the nearest alley. Deimos turns to the KPFOC.] Deimos: 'Don't you think we should get going?' [Deimos runs off after the two men. Jack turns to the KKFOS, who gives him a dirty look as he runs after Deimos.] Jack: 'What was that look for, mate?' [Jack runs after him and the scene fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * The Airport A COUPLE OF MINUTES LATER... [The five of them run past a homeless guy and are at the end of the alleyways. They have reached the island's only airport.] Tall Guy: 'There they are!' [Everyone looks forward and sees some of the Wing Kong martial arts gang dragging Miao Yin, the Chinese Guy's girlfriend, to the airport. The Chinese Guy runs out of the alley and yells at the thugs.] Chinese Guy: 'HEY! That's my girlfriend!' [Just then, the three giant bodyguards of the old Chinese guy named Lo Pan who stole the other guy's girlfriend turn around. They are the Three Storms and they are dressed like Raiden from Mortal Kombat. They turn and glare at the Chinese Guy and his Tall Guy friend, who stupidly runs out to join him whereas Jack, Deimos, and the KKFOS stay hidden in the alleyway. The Three Storms, the Wing Kong, and the Lords of Death street gang, all allies of David Lo Pan, gang up on the two men and beat them into oblivion before taking them away in the direction they took the Chinese Guy's girlfriend. The camera then switches back to Deimos and his pals, the KPFOC.] Deimos: 'I'm sure glad we didn't follow those idiots.' [Jack nods his head in agreement.] Jack: 'Yeah, because we're not idiots.' Deimos: [smiling] 'Right.' [The homeless guy joins to watch the show but then transforms into the old guy in the wheelchair that they saw earlier to their amazement using crappy SFX.] Deimos: 'Ok, who the f*** are you and why won't you leave my promo?' [The old man smiles.] Lo Pan: 'I'm David Lo Pan bitches and I will send you all to the hell where people are skinned alive! It's that simple!' [Deimos gives him a look.] Deimos: 'Are you crazy? Is that your problem?' [Lo Pan smiles.] Lo Pan: [driving away in his wheelchair] 'I'm getting off this god forsaken island so I can go back to San Francisco's China Town so I can become human again! Later you puny mortals.' [Lo Pan speeds off and flips them off in the process. Deimos turns to the KPFOC.] Deimos: 'We'd best get moving too, don't ya think?' [Jack and the clown nod their heads.] Jack: 'Right boss.' [They sneak out of the alley and look out for the soldiers who after Jack. They then run toward the airport and sneak inside as the scene fades out. And that is how Deimos, Captain Jack Morgan, and the 'Killer Klown from Outer Space' became a team.] [Cut.] * * * Change in Plans Filmed: 01/30/05 [As the scene opens inside the airplane, Deimos is looking at his script and shaking his head. Jack turns and looks at him.] Jack: 'What is it?' [The clown is wondering the same thing.] KKFOS: 'Whatiando?' [Deimos turns to them, shaking his head.] Deimos: 'This isn't right. This is not what I had in mind. This is just pointless and stupid.' Jack: 'What boss?' [Deimos throws the script down.] Deimos: 'I...I can't do this anymore.' [Jack looks confused, as does the KKFOS.] Jack: 'Can't do what, mate?' Deimos: [badly acting emotional] 'This gimmick is too stupid even for me. I...I must end this crap now.' Jack: 'What are you talking about?' [Deimos turns to him.] Deimos: 'I can't be this stupid gimmick anymore! I can't copy someone else's footsteps! [starts looking around] Writer! Writer! I'm firing your ass! [acting tough] Understand! I'm firing you for giving me this crappy ass gimmick that nobody cares about! I'm firing you for turning me into some Raven wanna-be with no acting talent and less charisma than a four-assed monkey!' KKFOS: 'Whatiando fatei mondo bosso?' Jack: 'Yeah. What does that mean boss?' [Deimos stands up.] Deimos: 'I am no longer Deimos.' [Jack looks confused.] Jack: 'Then who are you then?' [Deimos turns and looks at him.] Deimos: 'I'm James F***ing von Doom bitch and I'm walking off this set and I'm going to renegotiate my LOW contract so all of these horse f***ers in the audience no longer can see me wrestle! [pause] Or will never see me wrestle because I am their God and they are nothing but my BITCHES!' [Deimos, er, James turns and walks off the set of the plane. You can see him walking off our badly done fake plane. The set then falls over. Everyone is looking around.] Director: 'To hell with this crap! CUT!' [Cut.]
