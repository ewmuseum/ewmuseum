Reload Hype: The Year Of The Big Dawg

3 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/3/2006 The camera opened up in the dark room. A single yellow bulb cast a small circle of illumination where a huge man kneeled on the floor. The long black hair hanging around his head prevented anyone from seeing his face, but the bare chest was covered in a thin layer of sweat over his massive muscles. Wafting across the space, lines of smoke hung in the air. The man spoke in a powerful, bass voice without any hesitation, “And I heard a voice, in the midst of three beasts. And I looked and behold, a pale fighter. And his name that laid atop him for the pin was the Big Dawg. And the championship left with him.” The man looked up, the long black hair falling out of the way. He stared across at the camera with a small sparkle in his eyes. The camera rushed in, the convergence onto Jake’s twinkling eye as the screen exploded into white light. A series of clips was shown on the screen. Jake Hix was sent over the top rope to the floor by Brick during Global Brawl 2005. “And Jake Hix comes in second place in his Pay-Per-View debut ladies and gentlemen! An amazing start to his Global career!” Jake Hix exchanges blows with some of the former stars of Global on the screen before he is shown on the turnbuckle raising his Television Title into the air. “Our new Global Television Champion, Jake Hix!” The picture is frozen, then fades to black and white before it is cracked like glass and blown away by a strong wind. A headline in black and white replaces the picture. JAKE HIX SUSPENDED AND STRIPPED OF TELEVISION TITLE More competition is shown, with Jake’s bloody face shown over and over as he is beaten by opponents. A series of clips plays out. Dawn McFarlane defeats Jake Hix in the Master Of The Mat Finale. “Jake Hix comes in second place!” Dawn McFarlane pins Jake in the PTC Super Series Re-Match. “Jake Hix comes up short again!” Shadow and Dawn McFarlane are shown double-teaming Jake before eliminating him with a double pin at Showdown In Tokyo. “Bishop: No Fair they both pined him!!! McCoy: He is eliminated though!!! And looks to be out cold! We are down to the last two. Shadow or McFarlane will go to Reload to face Emoticon for the World title!!!!!” Hix with a bandage on his head runs down from the back baseball bat in hand?. He quickly circles the Octagon and then sneaks up to the fallen McFarlane and Shadow? Shadow not paying attention other then to Dawn does not see Hix? Hix draws back and brings the bat down across the head of Shadow with such force the wooden bat explodes with a loud crack. “Bishop: Hix is sending a message” He wants his title shot that he earned!!!” The image pulls back out from the twinkle in Jake’s eye to show him back inside the darkened room. He smiled as he spoke into the camera, “No more second place. Everything changes this time?” The scene cut away. Jake Hix was walking down with a red brick wall behind him. He spoke confidently as he looked into the camera, “Zenith is holding on to the last vestige of his power now that the rest of his faction has folded. The Hunt is almost at an end, and your time has passed.” The Big Dawg stopped, picking up a single black brick from the floor. He shoved the brick into a hole in the otherwise pristine wall, and as for the Rottweiler himself, well as it turns out he’s just another Brick in the wall.” Jake stuck the brick into the wall, and then the camera pulled out showing a huge wall as long as a football field and as tall as a skyscraper. The message was in black on the red background. January 11, 2006 Global presents RELOAD World Championship Elimination Match Christian Zenith ? vs Jake Hix vs Brick The Year Of The Big Dawg Begins!
