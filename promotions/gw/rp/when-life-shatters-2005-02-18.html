When Life Shatters...

18 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th February 2005 Fame? I have never considered myself to be famous, I know what I do is considered to many people as entertainment but all I have ever seen it as is a lifestyle. It is a lifestyle that keeps the money flowing into my bank account and it is a lifestyle that I would not change for the world. The first time I ever encountered my so called fame is when I was coming out of the GW arena and a small crowd had formed waiting for me. I thought nothing of it and signed some autographs and recorded a few answer phone messages on people’s mobiles. From then on every time I stepped out of the arena the crowd slowly grew and grew. At the moment I would say the crowds are a medium size compared to someone like Brick’s crowd or Ken Partridge Jr.?s crowd. However I did not get in this lifestyle for the fame, I got in this lifestyle because I love what I do, if fame comes with what I do then so be it. But I will not be one of these superstars who think they are bigger than the game just because a few people know their name. Fighting is not about becoming famous, it is about you and your opponent and who is better. Fame may bring you recognition but it also has a price. Once you are famous people come to expect perfection every time and if you falter once then you lose whatever fame you had. Fame has many prices, one of which I paid recently? Before we get to that however I would like to address the matter of last week’s GW Evolution and this coming Evolution. Last week I had the chance to cement my place in the GW by advancing in the GW’s Platinum Series, now the odds were stacked against me as I would have to defeat 2 Goliaths of GW, Brick and Corvus. I flew all the way over to Australia for this special event and what a night it was. Did I come out the winner? No I did not, it was Corvus who was fortunate enough to come out as the victor and advance on in the series. However it was not the victory that I wanted, what I wanted was to prove to the rest of the GW that even though I am a cruiserweight I can still take the power moves that I can be thrown around that ring like a rag doll yet still keep coming. Yes I will admit like everyone that I have my limit and that is why I lost the match. However I defy anyone who says they can get up after a 280lbs man has been power bombed on top of you. As far as I am concerned I proved in that match that I have no fear and that I will never fear another man again. Of course at next Evolution I defend my Cruiserweight Title against a woman by the name of Aradia Xiomara. I have watched Aradia come up through the ranks and it will be an honour to defend my title against here. I do wish to warn her that this belt is a belt I have come to cherish and you will have to go beyond what you think is your best to take this belt away from me. Aradia, all I say to you is good luck! Now onto my point about how I paid for my fame? “Would you like some more ice sir?” “No I have plenty, thank you.” The air hostess walked away as I placed the ice pack on my right arm. I winced as the cold touched the swelling on my arm, as I leaned back I looked out at the window at the clouds above us. The GW had been kind enough to pay for a private jet to fly me home, obviously a sign that they were pleased with my performance at Evolution. I felt a sense of satisfaction wash over me as I let out a sigh, I had finally put the demons in my mind to rest. All those demons that kept telling me to give up and that no one in GW respected me or what I did. Last night’s match was all I needed to reassure myself, to show myself that I still had something to offer to this game. Sure I got one hell of a battering and that was the reason I had the swelling on my arm, but I was still standing in a sense at the end of the match and that is all I needed. Now I could concentrate on continuing to climb the ladder of GW. I felt a smile creep on my face as my eye lids grew heavy and the pain began to dull. Just as I was about to drift off I saw the air hostess approaching me. “Excuse me sir?” She said with a pearly white smile on her face. “Yes?” I replied groggily. “There is a call for you.” She said as she handed me the portable phone. “Who is it?” I asked but she had already made her way back to the crew cabin, I put the phone against ear, “Hello?” “Hey sweetheart!” Came my wife’s sweet voice from the other end of the line. “Hey darling.” “How you feeling?” “A bit battered and bruised but I am feeling more like myself. How is Emma?” “She is fine; I am just getting her bottle for her feed. We missed you.” “I’ve been missing you too gorgeous, but I’ll be home in 24 hours and we can have a nice quiet family day. How does that sound?” “That sound’s great Adam. In fact better than great, wonderful!” SMASH! “What was that?” I asked concerned. “Probably Emma getting impatient and breaking one of her bottles. I’d better go sweetheart, love you.” “I love you too! I’ll see you soon.” With that the line went dead. Again I smiled at the thought of a loving family waiting for me when I got home. I threw the phone over to the chair facing me and again laid back and stared out of the circular window. As the sun disappeared I drifted off into sleep, for the first time in a long time I was finally relaxed and slept the whole plane ride home. I pushed my baggage trolley through the exit doors at the airport into the bright sunshine of California. I looked around at the people rushing into Taxis all wanting to get home as fast as they could or for some start their holiday. I looked around for a spare Taxi but they all seem occupied. “Hey Adam!” Came a familiar voice from the side. I turned to see Peter Willows waving at me next to his car. “Hey Pete,” I said as reached him and his car, “What brings you here?” “Lunch.” “Really, I thought it was like 5 o’clock.” “That will be the 24 hour flight you have just been on.” Pete laughed as he loaded my luggage into the boot of his car, “Come on hope in.” As we set off Pete handed me an envelope. “What is this?” I asked as I opened it. “Something the GW Head Office sent my way; I think it is the card for next week’s Evolution.” I pulled a piece of paper out of the envelope and it was indeed the card for next week’s Evolution. I looked down and saw my name down for a defence of my Cruiserweight title against Xiomara. I then continued to look down and saw 3 questions marks positioned where the Main Event was. “Hey what is with these question marks?” “Apparently Bubba McCoy is going to announce a mystery event that involves the roster.” “Really” Sounds interesting.” “Yeah but don’t lose focus on your title defence Adam. You wanted to bring that title back into the lime light so this is you chance.” “Don’t worry, Pete, I have heard good things about this Xiomara, should be a good match.” “Yeah, that is all well and good Adam, but you need to come out the champion if you want to get up that GW ladder.” “Relax Pete, everything will be fine,” I looked at Pete who was now looking at me as if I was on drugs, “What?” “You!” “What about me?” “You’ve changed. You seem like the Adam Myers of old, more relaxed, more focused.” “Well Pete, things are on the up!” I looked at him again and he could tell I was smiling as he smiled back at me. The rest of the ride home me and Pete joked about the old times. It had been a long time since me and Pete had just talked being mates. We pulled up into my drive. “Thanks for the ride Pete.” I said as I got out of the car. “No problem Adam, I’ll call you tomorrow.” “Okay speak to you tomorrow.” I closed the car door and waved Pete goodbye as he sped off into the distance. I turned around, looked at my house and sighed, “It’s good to be home.” I walked up to my house and the closer I got I noticed a red light flashing inside. My heart dropped as I dropped my luggage and raced towards my house. “Warning! Warning! Warning!” The alarm system screamed over the speakers in the house as the red lights flashed. “RACHEL!” I screamed at the top of my voice. My house was an absolute bomb site glass was everywhere the phone had been ripped from the wall. “RACHEL!” I screamed again as I raced towards the kitchen. As I reached it I fell to my knees in horror. Milk was splashed over the floor; Emma’s high chair was broken in two, every single piece of equipment was smashed. But worst of all was the blood that dripped down the work surface. Rachel and Emma were nowhere in sight but I feared the worst. I just sat there knelt on my knees, crying, when a voice came from behind me. “Hello Adam!” As I turned around I felt a cold blunt instrument smack me across my head. I began to fade into darkness as two blurry figures stood over me. The last thing I heard was? “Its time to start the programme! Take him in!”
