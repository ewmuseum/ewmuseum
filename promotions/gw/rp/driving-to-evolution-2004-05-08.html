Driving to Evolution.

8 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 8 There we were, sitting in the car. Me and Jamester. He had just gotten out of jail, and I was picking him up. At Evolution, we did not win that tag team title match, but we did stay in longer than I expected, and we raised a little hell that night. I System Crashed Juvian Ramorez onto a chair which held him out of the match. And Jamester was at the other side of the ring kicking some other guys ass. We did pretty good that night, but did not win. “Hey Jesse.” I said weakly. “Sup dawg?” he asked back. I guess he was in a good mood, wait what am I thinking, he just got out of jail, he must be the happiest guy in earth. He must be really happy to actually get some fresh air unlike in jail when you have to breath in smoke and all those deadly gases. He must also be happy because he could actually walk on pure cement, unlike the ripped up bad cement in the jail cell. “Nothing much, you?” I said back. “How do you think I feel” I just got out of jail!” he yelled at me. I started to laugh, it was kind of funny that this guy, after getting out of jail, was a bit crazy. Oh well, he should join my club, when I’m happy, well, you don’t even want to know what I like doing. I felt happy for Jesse, he could actually go and see his family again, he could actually do stuff that he couldn’t do before when he was in jail. “I guess you would be happy.” I said. I smiled at him. He was sitting in the passenger seat right beside me. He must have really like my car because he kept rolling the window down and sticking his head out of the car and looking at it. Well I wouldn’t be surprised, this car was pretty nice. It was a present from my mom when I was 18. I was so happy when I saw it parked in the backyard, and she said it was mine. “Where did you get this awesome car?” he asked me. “It was a present from my mother when I was 18.” I said. “Oh, where is your mother now?” he asked. “Well, she died last week.” I said really weakly. There was silence in the car. I didn’t really want to talk about it, but I felt safe around Jesse, it was like he was more than my friend, he was my teacher. He has been wrestling for over 9 years, and me only 1 year. He is going to teach me everything I need to know about wrestling, and he’s going to teach me how he gets so far in his wrestling career. “I’m really sorry to hear that.” he said. “Its ok, I’m doing my best to keep it out of my head.” I said. I think Jesse knew what I was going through. His head was down in shame, I think one of his siblings or parents had died to and he did know what I was going through. He brought his head up and smiled, I think he had gotten over whoever had died out of his family, but that was too hard for me to do yet. “So, where are we going?” he asked. “I don’t know, don’t we both have matches on Evolution?” I said. “Oh yeah, your in that battle royal for that belt, and I’m against Juvian Ramorez I think.” he said. “Well, Evolution it is then.” I said. I stepped on the gas. We were already on the highway. It was a double lane so I turned into the other lane to pass this old grandpa. I stepped on the gas again and it went up to 180. I was flying down the highway. I passed a few more people as Jesse had his window down. It looked like he was happy. I passed a big patch of trees off the highway and suddenly came a police siren. The police came out of the trees and were now chasing us. They started catching up as the scene started to fade.
