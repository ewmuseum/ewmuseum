The Suffering...........

2 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-2-05 Pain or Pleasure? If pleasure is a state of well being. Than obviously pain would be the opposite of pleasure. But what if someone could derrive pleasure FROM pain? What would that make that someone? A freak? A lunatic? A psycho? Or maybe that someone is a higher state of being than you? some would say pain is man kinds biggest weakness. So what if someone does not have that problem? Would that be your definition of unstoppable? ------------------------------------------------------------- *The scene opens in a black room........All black, No walls can be seen in the room at all except for the one Damian is leaning against......Damian stands in the corner of the room engulfed in blackness....He is watching the center of the room, where one light shines straight down like a spotlight on another person. He's sitting down on his knees, head to the ground as if he would be praying or something of the sort. It is Rafe Corvin sittin in this light. Damian stands watching him, almost studying him. Waiting for any movement. Damian begins to notice people talking. Obviously only in his head because there is noone else around.* One voice, is somewhat whiney, more of a female sounding voice. 'Hes so great......i wish you could be more like him......' Another sounds like man in a twisted tone, as if he is always filled with hatred. 'Fuck that your perfect!. MAKE HIM BLEED!.....He wishes he was you!......Hes just going to stab you in the back because he wants to be you!......stab him first........DO IT DO IT NOW!!!!' *Damian just stands still, not letting the voices bother him to much. Only squinting his eyes a bit as if hes trying to fight them off inside his own head.* female voice:'You could never hurt him.......Hes done so much for you....You have to worship him. He'll make you better!' male voice:'Dont listen to that shit!.....You dont need help! They're just afraid of you because your stronger! You must punish the weaklings!!' *Damian becomes more and more bothered by the voices in his head and begins to notice himself slowly drowning the female voice out of his head and wanting to follow the male voice in his head......'Hes so vulnerable right now....just look at him. Waiting for it.....Hes so cocky. Just finish him now. Fix all his problems for good!' the male voice begins screaming over and over again. Damian realises he cant fight off the voice in his head and he doesnt know why. He takes 3 or 4 steps towards Rafe, hatred pumping threw his viegns. As he gets closer Rafe looks Damian right in the eye and within 3 seconds of there eyes contact Damian falls to floor and blacks out............................* ------------------------------------------------------------- *Damians eyes begin to open slowly, and when they are fully open Damian finds himself starring up at a ceiling wich looks to be made of stone. He feals a cold chill running down his entire back side. He looks around to find himself Laying down on a cold stone slab. Maybe some kind of bed or something. As he looks around all he notices is black. Hes use to this however, most of his life has been just blackness for him. Everything in the room is cold and dank as if it has to be several storys underground. Damian notices a small door in the corner of the room made of very old planks of wood and only a rope hanging off of it for a handle. He makes his way out the door trying to determine where hes at. Most would be scared of a place so dark and mysterious as this but Damian is more frusterated to not know where hes at or what just happened to him or how long hes been out. Damian gets to his feet and makes his way towards the door. He pulls the door to open it, its stuck at first but after a good boot he plows right threw the door. Damian now finds himself in a large stone room, that looks like the whole room was carved from one big stone, its a round room, with old greek style pillars every 5 or 6 feet apart all the way around the outside edge of the room. On the floor is a large red painted symbol of a huge star with 9 points in the middle of a circle. In the center of this star is a pillar that sticks up about 2 foot out of the ground. On this pedistle sits Rafe Corvin, Knelt down on his knees. Damian stands and stares at Rafe as he begins to stand up. Rafe: 'A journey is about to be proved dear Damian. Each point leads you to another challenge...another destiny! Each door is another path to salvation and salution dear Damian. The only way you may be saved is by besting each test and making your way back standing toe to toe with your master. Damian this is the only way you will be saved and in no way is this fail proof, many dangers await...there is no guarentees you will escape fate.' *Damian notices each point of the start points directly at a door behind the pillars.* female voice:'Do what he says Damian........hes going to help you.......hes going to fix all your problems.....Hes so great.....' male voice:'You dont have to listen to him Damian! your better than him! listen to his lame ass talking.....what does he think he is some kind of fucking shakespear! Make him suffer Damian! MAKE HIM BLEED! MAKE HIM FEAL OUR PAIN!!!' female voice:'DO IT DAMIAN! JUST DO WHAT HE SAYS!!' male voice:'KILL HIM DAMIAN! KILLLLLL!!!' *Both voices begin to repeate those lines franticlly over and over, getting louder and louder in his head until Damian cant fight them off anymore* Damian: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!! GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY HEAD!!!!!!!!!! *Damian falls to his knees and grabs his head and starts shaking his head left to right trying to make it go away* Rafe: Listen to me my brother...Do what I say, Follow the first door and suffer no more........ *Damian jumps up and makes his way to the first door of the 9 he notices a white symbol written in some foriegn ancient language on the door. He ignores this and smashes open the door to find a long dark hallway he can see a good ways down it but the farther down it goes it gets darker to the point where all Damian can see is blackness.* ------------------------------------------------------------- *As Damian makes his way down the black hallway things begin running a hundred miles threw his head about what could be at the end of the door. The hallway seems like its never going to end just walking threw eternal blackness. But turning back is not an option. Damian is prepared to do whatever it takes to make the suffering stop. And He has some strange confidence that this is what it is going to take. Confidence being something Damian doesnt experience very often so he can do nothing but focus on it and what this is going to lead to. Damian walks for several minutes, anticipating what could be at the end of this hallway that is going to help him. Finally after all the walking he sees a man aproaching. He's to far to tell who it is but theres a boy standing in front of the man. Damian wants to know who this is so he takes off running towards them. Suddenly when he gets up on them the walls around him disapear and he is just left in a big void of blackness. He stands still looking at the man......It is his father.....He doesnt notice the face at first because he died when Damian was very young. But now he recognizes it. The boy is him......They are froze until Damian walks up on him then the motions carry out.* Dad:WHY DID YOU DO THAT! HUH?! YOUR WORTHLESS! WHY THE FUCK DID I WASTE MY FUCKIN SPERM TO MAKE A WORTHLESS LITTLE PIECE OF SHIT LIKE YOU! Boy: im sorry daddy!!! *Damian is frozen solid starin and slowly remembering this exact day. He got in a fight at school that day. He doesnt remember what for. Just that his dad had to pick him up that day from school and take him home early. Damian keeps watching them as the dad grabs the boy by the back of his neck and drags him into the kitchen.* Dad: So you think your some kind of badass now huh?! you wanta fight huh?? Why dont you fuckin fight me!? *The dad grabs the boy and punches him upside his head a couple times then throws him up against a table* Dad:So your pretty tough now arent you?! *The dad then grabs a iron off of the table still hot from being used recently* Dad:' I bet from now on you'll fuckin think before you do shit like this!! I'll make sure of it!' *The Dad grabs the boy and holds his hand down on the table. Young Damian now screaming so loudly that nothing else can be heard as the dad drops the iron and burns the flesh off of the boys hand a scream so loud Damian cant even hear the voices in his head anymore comes echoing threw the blackness. Damian drops to his knees and grabs his hand and can feal the burn from when he was a child all over again. He lets out a scream so loud and in a fit of rage jumps to his feet and runs after the man and takes one huge swing with his fist to sock the dad in the face but his fist flys right threw the face of the man. Damian lands on the other side noticing nothing happened trying to figure out what is going on. Suddenly he looks back and the dad drops the boy to the floor.* Dad: Now clean up this fucking mess you made in my kitchen and go the fuck to bed or something just get out of my sight. *The boy lays on the floor holding the bleeding burnt hand and crying.....Damian remembers this was the first day he began to learn to numb the things that happen to him in his life. As it comes back to him it hurts all over again but this time Damian cant numb or block it out like he use to. It just hits him harder than it did even when he was a kid. The images disapear into the dark as several more begin to race threw Damians head.....hundreds or memorys of his dad abusing him to the point of near death. More and more of awful times in Damians life he learned to block out. But now hes helpless as all the pain comes back to him. Suddenly Rafe appears in the room..........* Rafe: 'Get up brother.....Dont question the methods, Just press on. You'll understand and thank me in the end for this. The road to salvation lies on.........' *Damian stands up and looks back at Rafe but hes no longer there. Damian has given up trying to figure out what is going on. He just notices now a door in the blackness. He heads towards it and walks threw it. Light pours threw the door as he opens it and when its fully open the Light shines threw so bright that it blinds damian and everything around him...............* ------------------------------------------------------------- Imprisoned in this world of darkness Few have known a monster lusting blood And the hunger grows out on the street, Danger's real, the killer's still at large A trail of death through sewers to the underground I feel their fleeting life draw into my bones Hear the heart that pounds, The blood that flows eternity I damn thee let me rot into the ground as the empty veins collapse, Cast them down to living hell
