The building is very tall, with a huge steeple at the top. A large wooden cross is above the two massive wooden doors, guarding the entrance. We step inside? “inside, he is seen sitting in the back pew. He carefully listens to the man speak in the front of the church behind the podium. He sports a old pair of black jeans and his black Sage Sabbot shirt. He sits with his hands together with his fingers of his hands interlocked with each other. He doesn’t move an inch, he sits and concentrates and studies each word the man ahead is speaking. The man ahead is wearing a long white coat that falls to the floor. He is very old. But with his age, he brings experience, wisdom, and knowledge. He continues to speak to all the people in the room. All of the normal people. The people who work morning to evening to pay for bread on their table. People who don’t get things given to them. People who realize what they have and who don’t envy what they don’t have. They come here to learn from the teacher in front of them. Hopefully to strengthen them spiritually and emotionally. For they have nobody in their lives to comfort them except their savior. They have nothing to look forward to in life’they realize they will one day die. Their bodies will turn cold and decay. They will have worms and maggots feast on their flesh. So when you think of it, they have nothing to look forward to’except paradise. They believe if they do what the book says, that they will live forever in peace in heaven above. They listen to each word the man says” ??for the Lord is with you. Forever and always. It won’t matter what you do or what you didn’t do. The love the Lord gives you will last forever. And you will always be able to give the love back to him. Why? Because he is a forgiving God. And you should never be afraid of him or be intimidated by him. For you are his sheep and he is the Shepard. He will try to lead you to follow him’no matter where you are in your life.” A voice comes from the back of the room? “You say he is forgiving, right?” “Why, yes son. The Lord will forgive you for any sin you have. You just have to repent deep inside your heart and you will be forgiven. You just have to find out the solution.” “There is no way around the Main Event at Evolution. What do you suggest?” “I think that if you search in your heart, that the true answer will reveal itself to you. And then, then you will make the correct decision my son. For you shall do what the Lord wants you to do’even though you may not want to.” “But you said that he will forgive me right?” “Yes, he will forgive you but you shoul?” He interrupts the man once again? “Thank you, that is all I wanted to know.” “But son, I am trying to explain to you. I have been there. There have been times that I wanted to do something else that the Lord has wanted me to do, but I have followed the Lord and I was better off. I speak to you from experience.” The attention of the people is directed towards the discussion. He begins to stand up, for he has already got his answer. “And where did you hear that from Father?” “It is written in the word my son. The Bible has everything you need to know.” “So, you have been through the Bible then” You have read it a couple times?” “It is the guideline and map to my life my son. I live the word and I am only offering helpful advice.” “Did you know that in the Bible it reads that you shall not call anybody “Father” except God above and your birth father?” “Yes son, I’ve read that. And don’t cram religion down these people’s throats. Religion is a job, religion is a career’it is not a chore my friend.” The man stands with his jaw dropped almost touching the floor and he starts to mutter as he stands up and opens the large wooden doors and leaves the church. He walks out and steps down a few steps onto the concrete stairs leading to the entrance. He runs his hands through his hair and takes a deep breath. “The world, oh such a horrible place. A place of sorrow, poverty, and hatred. How can this world change” He shakes his head side to side in disappointment? “It can’t change. Why” Because there are people like Jay Robson walking around on television in front of millions and millions of people watching him. And what does he promote? He promotes premarital sex, good looks, and materialism. He gluttons himself in his fame. He once was a legend. He once was a champion. But once I stepped into the ring against him. Once I told the world that I was going to defeat him, then I did what I said I was going to do. I walked into Evolution last week a nobody. I then left with the GW Cruiserweight Championship Belt across my waist. The world is starting to sense my talent and skill. Until each bone is shattered inside their body. And until each pain is recognized through their souls’for we are the founder of their fears. We are the redeemer of their sins. And we are the deliverers of their pain. For stepping up against us, the price is deadly. And at Evolution, The Nevermore will work our magic right before your very eyes. For I will step into another Main Event. This time with a fellow stablemate, Jay Vyper and we will take apart the other 2 superstars. We will rip a former GW Heavyweight Champion, Silent Wind apart. Then I will conquer another legend here in the GW in Silent Wind. Then the true Legend will stand in front of you, with Silent Wind’s blood on my hand. And I will wipe it on the ground, for his blood doesn’t deserve to be on me. Oh, I wait for the moment my cold hands are cooled by Wind’s warm blood. For my hands haven’t felt the same since. Revenge, that is the reason why Wind. You defeated me a couple of months ago, and now, I will return the favor.
