Gone And Forgotten

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/29/03 He is seen, surrendering his thoughts into the empty part of the night’s sky. Alone, searching for a place to relieve his sighs’breathing one more time. Relief’he’s never felt this way before. He surrenders, his arms up in the air, unclothing his black desire. Replacing them with determination out of desperation. Another breath has been taken, no another word has been spoken. Actions, the ultimate payback for not another lost cause. The water slowly, calmly drifts by him. The sound of fresh water passing by, sooths his mind and eases his sorrow. He lies back, almost into a deep sleep. Dreaming of never letting go of this feeling. Now, now it is with him forever and ever until his last dying breath. He’s never felt this way before. He’ll never let them down. “As I sit back and ponder about the past. Thinking about those still framed memories’the pictures of insanity and pain flash through my eyes. Right before me, each drop of blood bleeds a little more. How do you think it makes me feel?” He takes a deep breath? “The dying and the dead’will once be forgotten and never remembered. Each day passes and we slowly forget those who aren’t among us anymore. As their flesh, consumed by worms and maggots, slowly eats away’depleting their remains. Their skin, growing colder by the minute. Their skeleton, exposed for nothing more then a shrugged shoulder or a decoration for the bearers of the soil. Taking up nothing but space. This my friends, this is what is going to happen to each and every one of us. With all the technology, with all the power we have as humans’nothing can save us from our destinies at becoming dead. Carpe Diem, seize the day. Live each day to its fullest and remember to leave no regrets behind once we’d stepped onto the next word. Is that what our lives consist of” Is this the meaning of this life? Is this the hell we live amongst?” He pauses? “But then you argue, innocent humans buried with nothing at all left in their lives except the cause of heartache and stress among their loved ones. Innocent you say” I consider that greedy and disturbing my friends. But you, as Americans and you as pathetic nomads of this world. Traveling, searching for something better, something greater then what you have now, greed. A deadly sin that eats everybody with the first sight of it. And you call yourself innocent when you can’t even fend off the temptation. You have no will power and no mental strength. Your world is composed of a talent show and a beauty contest. You idolize and admire the ones that are exposed by the media, singing about rape, murder, and hate. Or what about idolizing the man who played the cop in the latest movie who kills his nemesis in the end? You look up to them? What ever happened to looking up to guys such as William Shakespeare. Or guys like Ralph Waldo Emerson? Or guys like Johnathon Edwards? You civilians have fallen, face first into the world’s temptation. You love to see blood, you love to see gore. Sick and egotistical’simply because it is not you that sheds the blood. Step into the world of reality and swallow it. Many of you should.” Especially Brick “Look at what is set ahead of you.” Dead Zone GW Heavyweight Title Match Hell In A Cell Brick VS Sage Sabbot “Don’t forget what you leave behind.” Love Fame Fortune Life “Time, a thing often taken for granted.” Tomorrow Night “Your life, set in the palm of my hands.” Ending It With One Tear From Heaven “Surviving will be harder’so it seems.” I Am Your Worst Nightmare “You just don’t know it yet.” Realize “The end is near my friends.” And There Is Nothing You Can Do About It “Pray for the best.” Pray For Forgiveness “Maybe you’ll walk out alive.” I’ll Be Forgiven. “Your life will be changed.” As will mine. “For the worst.” For The Best. “You’ll become a memory. A still framed picture that will once be forgotten. Your body, will be eaten by maggots and worms and your skin will turn cold.” I’ll Become Champion Champion Of The World Holding My Arm In The Air I’ve Found My Spot In The Heavens
