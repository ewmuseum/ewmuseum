Elite - noun; plural:

10 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.10.04 The camera pans around the room; displaying works of fine art, a few parlor game tables, and extravagant furniture. It is not until it stops on a picture of 'Superstar' Ken Partridge, Jr. and 'Heartbreak Kid' Shawn Michaels celebrating in the ring after Ken's becoming the first UHWF Heavyweight Champion of the World that we realize whose home we're in. The camera pans back around to Ken, sitting on his white Italian leather sofa, book in hand, ready to speak. Ken: Elite... noun... plural... 1.) a group or class of persons or a member of such a group or class, enjoying superior intellectual, social, or economic status. 2.) The best or most skilled members of a group. The White Rose wants to look up names about Mafioso and Gangs in the dictionary, well that's absolutly fine. However, what you, White Rose, should realize is that you're not going up against a gang... you're not going up against an off-shoot of the Mafia. You're in a match with The Elite... the best in this business. Men that have been places and done things that two of you could only dream about. Men that, between them hold more accolades than most organizations can claim. And you plan to counter that with what? Not experience... we have that. Not ring leadership... that's our's as well. I suppose one would claim that you're 'fresher' than us, but we're still young men. We just got into this business early. So here you are, first big time match... and you're destined to fail. ... I can't imagine how that must feel. To know... that you're not good enough. To know that you can prove nothing. But scarier than that, my two young yearlings, is that I believe you two actually think that you have a chance... and have no idea of the dissappointment that awaits you. I'm taking nothing away from you, as I have yet to witness the two of you in the ring and there's nothing there for me to take away from. I'm just warning you... that you're being fed to a couple of hungry lions. Your brother has your back through thick and thin. Bah! That's supposed to worry us? Ace and I know what the other is going to do before he does it. We read each other like books. We know the others every thought and move. Does Ace have my back? Damn right! Do I have his? You'd better fucking believe it. We're not brothers... but we are grown men with respect for one another. We are two of the best Global Wrestling and this busniess have ever seen. And we are measuring sticks. You think you can measure up, boys? Go for it. Go toe-to-toe and hold-for-hold with the Submission Marvel and the Innovator of True Fucking Greatness. But know this... YOU WILL TAP OUT! Neither of you are Elite... neither of you will EVER be Elite. And after Evolution, neither of you will even be walking. The scene fades to black as Ken stares into the camera with a very serious look on his face.
