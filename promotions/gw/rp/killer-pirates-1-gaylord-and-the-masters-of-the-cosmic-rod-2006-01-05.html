Killer Pirates #1: Gaylord and the Masters of the Cosmic Rod

5 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/05/06 A long time ago? On a pirate ship far, far away? *cue Star Wars theme* [The following opening text crawls up the screen as the musical score plays in the background.] EPISODE IV ? The Killer Pirates After their berthing port of the Island of Misfit Wrestlers was attacked, the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean found themselves in a quandary about what to do. After landing on a small, deserted island in the South Pacific, a shuttlecraft containing Matt and Adam landed on the island and the four of them got drunk after hearing the news that Matt and Adam had slain the evil reborn Sith Lord Darth von Doom. Then, after a night of partying and drinking rum, Adam and Matt left in their shuttlecraft to go into hiding. Soon, the Killer Pirates left as well, heading for their original home port of Tortuga on their ship, the Black Nemo. The Killer Pirates then realize that despite the fact that Darth von Doom is dead, the Horny Mathematician and his goons, the Circus of DOOM~!, are still after them and they are now marked men. So then our heroes decide to go back to the Horny Mathematician’s lab to see what kind of evil deeds he is up. But, much to their horror, they see that Darth von Doom is alive and is a cybernetic death machine. They also find out that the Mathematician has built the Cosmic Rod and made a prototype, which they decide to plunder once the Circus of DOOM~! has gone through the portal to another dimension? * * * THE COSMIC ROD In the now, the here and now, that is? [The Horny Mathematician and his goons are all standing around something that he has created. Darth von Doom is looking at it with much interest, almost looking over every inch of whatever it is. It seems to have great value to the Circus. The Mathematician then hold sup his greatest creation. It’s a golden key to the universe shaped like male genitalia. Circus of DOOM~! members Propellerhead, Dumpster Man, Lobster Man, and Igor the Mathematician’s lab assistant look on as the Mathematician holds up his greatest creation.] Mathematician: BEHOLD! THE COSMIC ROD~! Circus: Oooooooo? [Not one for giving out compliments easily, Darth von Doom gives one anyway.] Darth von Doom: Impressive. [The Mathematician giggles with demented delight over his latest creation.] Mathematician: Now, we can use this to catch the universe off balance and make everyone horny! [Darth von Doom contemplates this for a moment but then comes to a conclusion.] Darth von Doom: But aren’t they horny already? [The Mathematician raises an eyebrow.] Mathematician: What do you mean? Darth von Doom: Well, there are a billion people in China, correct? [The Mathematician nods his head.] Mathematician: Yes. Darth von Doom: Well, I know storks didn’t just bring the babies there. [The Mathematician seems intrigued now.] Mathematician: What are you implying, Lord von Doom? Darth von Doom: I am implying that by the mere fact that there are a billion people in China, people must be horny. And horniness leads to sex, which in turn leads to one billion people being in China. Mathematician: ? [The Mathematician thinks about it for a moment and then smiles.] Mathematician: First China, then the universe! [The Mathematician starts punching numbers into the Cosmic Rod. A portal then opens up and all of the Circus of DOOM~! enters the portal, led by the nefarious Lord von Doom. The Mathematician follows and the portal closes behind them.] * * * LOOTING Moments later? [The Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean, Captain Jack Morgan and the “Killer Klown from Outer Space,” come barging into the room with big smiles on their faces. Jack thinks he’s Captain Jack Sparrow from Pirates of the Caribbean and is wearing a Captain Jack Sparrow Halloween costume complete with fake pirate weaponry. The “Killer Klown from Outer Space” is a giant 380 pound monstrosity that may or may not be a “Killer Klown.” Rumor has it he is an escaped mental patient from somewhere in the Midwest, but no one knows for sure. Anyway, they sneak into the Mathematician’s lab and start looking around fro something.] Jack: Clown, look for the prototype Cosmic Rod. [The clown nods his head and they start trashing the joint looking for that and more stuff to plunder.] Jack: We must have it. We must stop their diabolical scheme! KKFOS: Rightiando! [And the KKFOS does not speak English, only a weird alien language that only he knows the meaning too. Yep, these guys are brain surgeons. They continue plundering, breaking beakers and test tubes, pulling papers with scientific jargon all over them out of drawers, and tossing everything all over the place.] Jack: We must find it! We must stop them! KKFOS: Mustiando smello! [Jack giggles.] Jack: I know it smells musty in here, mate, but we still have a job to do. [After they are finished tearing up every drawer in the room, Jack stands up and puts his hands on his hips.] Jack: I don’t get it. [The clown stands back up now, too.] Jack: Where is it? Where could it be? [Jack looks at the table counter and sees that it was lying there the whole time. Jack grabs it.] Jack: Oh? [Jack looks at it.] Jack: Oops? [The KKFOS giggles.] KKFOS: Funnie. [Jack takes a look at the object in his hands. It looks to be some sort of Japanese music synthesizer. Jack and the clown look at it.] Jack: Wow’the Cosmic Rod. (pause) I wonder if we’ll find your home planet with this thing? [The clown smiles deviously.] KKFOS: Clowniando invasioans ov de world! [Jack laughs.] Jack: Be serious, clown. (shaking his head) Clown invasion of the world? [Jack then notices a big red button on the side.] Jack: Hmmmm’I wonder what this does? [Jack pushes the button and a portal opens. Jack and the Clown are quickly sucked inside and are in Eternia now as well.] * * * GAYLORD OF THE UNIVERSE Meanwhile? [The Horny Mathematician, Darth von Doom, and the Circus of DOOM~! end up in the middle of Castle Gayskull as the portal closes behind them. Upon landing, they get up and brush themselves off.] Mathematician: Damn movie spoof. Couldn’t we have picked something easy like Cheaper by the Dozen 2? [Darth von Doom shakes his head.] Darth von Doom: No. [The Mathematician puts his hands on his hips.] Mathematician: And why not? Darth von Doom: Because that would be too easy. [The Mathematician growls with frustration.] Mathematician: Well, this is just great. Now what are we going to do? [Footsteps are heard behind them and the Mathematician turns around to see a big, hunky looking man checking him out. The man has a giant sword, is wearing almost no clothes, and is flanked by a guy dressed as a cop, a scantily clad ballerina, and a guy wearing make-up who is holding a purse.] Mathematician: I’uhh? [Darth von Doom busts out his lightsaber and ignites it.] Darth von Doom: Who are you insolent fools who dare mess with the mighty Horny Mathematician and the Circus of DOOM~? [The man smiles at them.] Gaylord: I am the Gaylord of the Universe and by the Power of Gayskull, I HAVE THE POWER!!!!!! “of Gayness. [The Circus all look at one another, perplexed.] Mathematician: Where the hell did we end up” France? [Gaylord shakes his head.] Gaylord: No, you are inside Castle Gayskull in the realm of Eternia, center of the universe. Darth von Doom: And what powers do you have, (mockingly unimpressed) GAYLORD? [Gaylord smiles at him.] Gaylord: I have the power to destroy all heterosexuality and turn everyone to gayness. My sword can shoot rainbows out of it and I am fighting the evil Straightor and his minions of heterosexuality who are out to destroy me for my beliefs. [The Mathematician looks at Darth von Doom, who finally unignites his lightsaber and puts it away.] Mathematician: (gulps) Any ideas? Darth von Doom: Yes. Avoid conversion at all costs. Mathematician: Oh? (turning back to the smiling Gaylord) Right? * * * WHERE ARE WE, CLOWN? Meanwhile? [Jack and the clown fall through their portal as it closes up behind them. They get up and brush themselves off.] Jack: Where are we clown? [The clown shrugs his shoulders.] KKFOS: Me dunno. [Jack and the clown pull out their weapons as they are surrounded by Straightor and his minion of heterosecuality, the Homophobic Priest, as they are now inside the Castle of Straightor.] Straightor: (in a commanding voice) Who are you?! Jack: We are the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean. Who are you? Straightor: I am Straighor, minion of straightness and you are in my castle. [Jack look around.] Jack: Oh? [Straightor raises an eyebrow.] Straightor: Are you two another bunch of Gaylord’s couples trying to convert us to gayness? [Jack looks at the clown, who looks repulsed. Jack turns and shakes his head.] Jack: No. We are not partners. [Straightor smiles.] Straightor: Good. Then you will not die in the Dungeon of Straightness! Jack: Oh’that’s nice. [Jack and the clown look at each other and shrug their shoulders.] Jack: Oh well’we could be in Newark. [Cut.] [End Killer Pirates, issue#1]
