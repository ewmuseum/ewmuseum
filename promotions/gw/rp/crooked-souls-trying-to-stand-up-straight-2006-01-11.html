Crooked souls trying to stand up straight.

11 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 11th, 2006. Crooked souls trying to stand up straight. ---- 'Open your eyes, Joshua.' The voice is soft, surprisingly soft for a man's voice. 'Huh...wha...where...where am I? I ask as my bluish gray eyes flutter open. No answer is given, as I slowly look around. Above me the sky is blue, the sun bright and warm, soaking into my tanned skin. Looking down I see that I'm naked, completely bare-ass naked. 'Where...where am I?' I ask again as I sit up, my eyes traveling around my strange environment. I'm on a stone floor, the stones are cold and hard, as I raise my head I see a table before me, turning slowly as I pull myself to my knees I see that the table almost completely surrounds me, the only exception being a gap in the semi-circle that allows individuals to step into the center. 'What the hell?' I ask in confusion as my eyes widen with astonishment. At the table is a group of individuals, women and men both. They all look young, strong, and all have a strange glow upon their face. It seems as if the very essence of the sun has burnt into their skin and is shining through in a way that I've never seen. They're dressed strangely, the men wearing red robes with white lapels, the women in the opposite fashion wearing white robes with red lapels. Beneath the table their feet are covered with white leather sandals. They all have pale skin, blonde hair and soft blue eyes...every last one of them. 'What...is...this?' I question, astonishment in my voice, as I now rest upon my knees. They don't answer, they just stare at me, their eyes so bright, so full of life. A gentle breeze is blowing through this strange room, with four stone walls, two windows, a single door leading into another room and most eccentric of all...the lack of a roof. 'Who are you?' I demand an answer this time, anger in my voice as I leap to my feet. My bare feet. My eyes circle the table, there are twelve of them. Six men and six women, all seated side by side around the table. Every single one of them watching me, never taking their eye off of me for a second. 'Who the hell are you?!' I demand with a scream. 'Where the hell am I?' They say nothing. Just sitting there, watching, waiting. 'Answer me, damn it, answer me!' I scream at the top of my lungs. 'How did I get here? Who are you? Where the hell am I?' The silence is broken as one of the men at the table stands and claps his hands together loudly. 'Ishmael!' 'Wha...?' I blink in utter confusion, before I see a young child step through the door. The child at the door looks like he's about 12 years old and just like every other damn person at the table he has long blonde hair and bright blue eyes. His attire is slightly different as he wears a pale blue robe and matching sandals. In his arms he carries a bundle of what appears to be a black robe and sandals. 'Ishmael, please give our guest his clothing.' The man standing in the center of the table commands, his voice gentle but firm. 'Yes, Lord Azariah.' The child answered meekly as he walked towards the tables. Upon reaching the gap at the table he stopped and knelt down, placing the clothing in the gap, smiling at me as he turned and walked away, leaving the room as quickly as he had entered. 'Please, clothe yourself Joshua.' The man I now knew as Lord Azariah said with a warm smile. 'What the hell is this?' I snapped, still angry and completely naked. 'Please, my friend, clothe yourself and we will explain everything for you.' Lord Azariah said his voice gentle yet strangely commanding. Much to my surprise I found myself walking towards the garments that had been left in the gap of the table. Slowly, cautiously I knelt over to pick them up, feeling all twelve pair of eyes in the room locked onto me as I did so. I threw the black robe on and tied it with the sash cast loosely around it, stepping into the sandals I glanced up at the door that had been open, contemplating an escape, the door was now closed. 'You had better start explaining things and explaining things fast before I have to hurt someone.' I warned, as I took a step back from the hardwood table. 'Please, dear friend, no violence is necessary, we don't wish harm upon you.' Lord Azariah said in a friendly tone, as he guided me back to the center of the table with a wave of his left hand. 'I can't say the same for you, jackass, and if you don't tell me who the hell you are, why I'm here and what exactly is going on...things are going to get nasty, very nasty.' I threatened as I curled my hand into a fist. 'Friend, there is no need to threaten violence upon...' He began, but I quickly cut him off as I leapt at him. His open mouth caught a handful of pissed of 'American Youth' as a right cross drilled him straight across the chops, a punch thats knocked out men half his size only made him flinch and I dropped to the floor holding my hand in pain. 'What the hell?!' I screamed as I clutched my right hand within my left. Lord Azariah only smiled at me. 'I warned you. In the future perhaps you'll heed my warnings, Joshua.' 'Please...tell me what you want...' I groaned as I pushed myself up to my knees, my eyes full of pain as they stared into his eyes, gleaming with a passion that I've never seen before. 'The question, Joshua, is not what we want, but rather...what do you want?' Lord Azariah asked. 'I'd like to start by getting out of here.' I retorted, still clutching my hand in agony. 'And you shall.' Lord Azariah promised with a nod. 'Your stay here won't be long, this I promise you, no one ever stays here long.' I was stunned by his words, unsure of exactly how to respond. At this moment I was convinced I had been abducted by a cult and was about to be offered as a human sacrifice, his words had seemed kind but underneath it all, I saw it as a veiled threat. 'What are you going to do, kill me? Sacrifice me? Hell, eat me?' I quipped with a glare. 'No, of course not.' Lord Azariah said with a laugh, the other eleven individuals at the table joining in the mirth...somehow I missed the punchline of the joke. 'Then what?' I asked, my eyes scanning the table as I looked for any signs of weakness. 'Am I free to go?' 'Yes.' Lord Azariah answered with his constant smile. 'I can leave...now?' My voice was full of hope as I asked the question. 'No, not yet, but soon.' Lord Azariah said enthusiastically. 'Exactly what the hell do you want from me?' I snapped, angered by the games of Lord Azariah. 'The answer to the question is; what do we want to give you?' Lord Azariah replied gently. 'What do you want for yourself?' 'Don't talk in riddles!' I screamed, saliva flying from the edges of my mouth. 'Joshua, please...calm down.' Lord Azariah commanded as he took a seat. My eyes widened with anger, the veins in my neck beginning to bulge. 'Sit, all will be explained.' Lord Azariah repeated. Reluctantly I sat on the cold stone floor, the material of the robe providing little cushioning. 'You are here because of the life that you had lived...' Azariah stated, before I cut him off. 'Whoa, wait...had? Past hence?' I asked, my eyes even wider now, blown open with shock. 'Yes. Joshua...you're dead.' Lord Azariah gently stated. 'No...no...no...' I shook my head in disbelief. 'I can't be dead, no...' 'You're dead, thats why you're here.' Azariah said in a low tone. 'I...no...It can't...no.' I shook my head frantically, reaching both hands up to my half black/half white hair and grasping the flowing locks in each hand. 'I'm...not even twenty-five yet, I can't be dead...' 'I'm sorry.' Lord Azariah said in an effort to sound sympathetic. 'No, I can't die this young...this...this can't be Heaven!' I screamed out, my voice cracking with emotion as I leapt to my feet. 'No, this isn't Heaven, I'm afraid.' Lord Azariah said with a bowed head. 'No!' My voice rang out loudly, echoing off of the stone walls in protest. 'I can't be sentenced to eternity in hell, I've been a good person! I've done so much for others, you can't send me to Hell...this isn't fair...' 'Please calm down, Joshua.' Lord Azariah said softly, motioning for me to be seated. 'You haven't been sentenced to hell either.' Then...where...am I?' I gasped in a panic as I slumped to my knees on the hard stone floor. Lord Azariah answered with a single word: 'Purgatory.' 'Purgatory?' I gasped in disbelief. 'This is the home of the hollow souls.' Lord Azariah responded. 'Yes, you have been good to others throughout your life, Joshua, but you've also sinned greatly. You've sinned unto yourself, you've sinned unto God.' 'How? Tell me...please?' I begged, hardly containing the strength to raise my head. 'You have been kind to others, yet you've refused to admit your own mistakes. When did you ever stop to ask for repentance? When did you ever stop to confess your sins to the almighty?' Azariah asked, his voice barely above a whisper. 'I tried...God knows I've tried.' I gasped, tears filling my eyes, blurring my vision of Lord Azariah. 'Have you tried?' A woman seated next to Azariah asked accusingly. 'Yes! Yes, I've tried!' I snapped in retort. 'Had you tried...then you would not be here.' She countered. 'Jachin, please...' Azariah scolded, before turning his attention back to me. 'God is not willing that any should perish, it was written that all have come short of the glory of God.' 'Save me...please...' I begged Azariah with sorrowful eyes. 'But I wish that I could, Joshua.' He replied, punctuating his sentence with a soft sigh. 'Do you know how you died?' 'No, tell me...' I begged, allowing the tears to slide from my eyes and onto my cheeks. 'At your own hands.' The woman known as Jachin said, her voice now filled with compassion. 'You slit your own wrist and bled to death.' 'Wh...Why?' Was the only question I could blurt out amongst heavy sobs. 'I can't...I'm too young to die...I...I...' Lord Azariah clasped his hands together and rested them on the table as he took a seat. 'You misunderstood the purpose of your pain.', he said. 'What is the purpose...what is my purpose?' I asked, closing my eyes in a failed attempt to hide the tears. 'The purpose of your pain is to make you a better person.' Azariah said in his infinite wisdom. 'The purpose of your life...', Jachin spoke up, '...is in your hands.' 'What do you mean?' I asked, as I allowed my hair to fall into my face to cover my shameful tears. 'We as angels cannot tell you the purpose of your life.' A man seated next to Lord Azariah said, his voice low and kind. 'Lord Josedech is right, you must determine your own purpose.' Azariah confirmed. 'How? I'm dead...I'm freakin'...dead.' I gasped as I shook my head in disbelief. 'Are you?' Jachin asked, her voice broadcasting a sly tone in it. I shook my head again, completely lost. 'Am I..? You said...you told me...' 'You are here to decide whether or not you're truly dead.' Lord Josedech said with an intriguing air. Blinking the tears away I locked my blurry eyes onto him. 'Are you giving me another chance to live?' 'Yes.' Lord Azariah nodded. 'If you will accept it.' 'Yes!' I exclaimed leaping to my feet. 'I'll do anything to live!' 'Good.' Lord Azariah said with a smile. 'Because you'll be asked to do a lot of things.' 'What..? I asked, my eyes widening with panic. 'Come.' Azariah waved his hand, directing me towards the window as he stepped up to the window himself. With careful, reluctant footsteps I stepped through the gap of the table and padded across the stone floor until I reached the window. Azariah raised his hand again, this time pointing out of the window. Stepping up to the window, devoid of glass, filled with only the blue of sky and a kind breeze I stared out...and couldn't believe my eyes. There I was, lying on my bedroom floor in the house of Ken Partridge Jr. A crumpled note was in my right hand, a razorblade in my left. The note was bloodstained and crushed within my grasp, my entire right hand and wrist soaked in a dark crimson that had seeped into Ken's white carpet. Lying on my right side my face was visible, my eyes wide open and staring at the ceiling with horrified shock. They were no longer their usual bluish-gray color, instead they had turned an abnormal shade of black. My lips were slightly parted, no breath escaping. I wore only a pair of torn Versace jeans, my chest and feet were bare. My body wasn't moving. I was dead. ...stone cold dead. 'Dear...' I tried to speak but words failed me. 'Sweet moth...' My hands trembled and suddenly I felt cold all over as I stared down at my own corpse through the window. 'This can't...this...it...there is no...this can't...be real.' I stammered, dry-mouthed with shock. 'But it is real, Joshua.' Lord Azariah said gently. 'This is your life...or rather, was your life.' 'I can't believe this...I'm really...' I lifted my hands up in front of my face and stared at them, moving them in an attempt to prove to myself that I was still alive. My fingers moved, but my corpse rested silently, without a single movement. 'I'm...' I shook my head, still blown away by the revelation of my own death. 'I'm...fucking dead...' 'Yes, Joshua, you're dead.' Lord Azariah said, placing a strong, but comforting hand upon my shoulder. 'They always said I'd die like this, every last one of them, and I proved them right...' I staggered back two steps from the window, my knees shaking uncontrollably as the hair on my arms raised with fright. 'That was your life.' Azariah nodded. 'Now, follow me.' Lord Azariah turned on his heel and marched towards the other side of the room, coming to a halt when he reached the window juxtaposing the one that held the view to my fallen corpse. With disbelief I backed away from the window, finding it difficult to peel my eyes away from the scene before me. As I reached Azariah I turned and looked through the window next to him. There I was again, this time with a smile on my face. I was breathing, moving...living. It really was me; crazy hair, tattoos, torn jeans ...the works. I was still on the floor, this time without the blood, without the suicide note. I was fast asleep, a smile the likes of which I had never seen on my face before. Sleepily I opened my eyes, and yawned widely, before trying to get comfortable again, not even making an effort to get up off of the floor, as I closed my eyes once more. 'I...I'm alive?' I gasped, a smile of surprise crossing my face. 'Yes, you're alive.' Azariah said, countering my smile with one of his own. But...' I looked back into the other window, my corpse still lying there, eyes wide open. ...I'm dead.' 'Yes.' Azariah looked back at the window. 'You're dead.' 'No, no...' I pointed back through the window to where I stood smiling. 'I'm...alive.' 'Yes.' Azariah said with a smile. 'You're alive, my friend.' 'But I thought I was...' I turned my head back to the other window. '...I'm dead.' Azariah frowned, wrinkles appearing in his brow. 'Yes...you're dead.' 'No!' I screamed out, my confusion turning to anger. 'I'm alive!' 'Yes!' He exclaimed with a bright smile. 'You are alive!' 'I can't be alive and dead...am I...' I struggled to find the words. 'Am I dead, or am I alive?' 'Yes.' Azariah said softly. 'Which is it?' I asked, my anger beginning to grow inside of me. 'Both.' He answered as he stared off into the distance at my smiling figure. My mind was quickly beginning to cloud with anger. 'How can I be both dead and alive?' 'Ah, the duality of human nature. Tis confusing, is it not?' Azariah asked rhetorically. 'You're confusing...:' I snapped. 'Enough with the riddles already...' 'No Joshua, you're confusing.' Azariah scolded as he turned his blue eyes onto me. 'What you see is the man you are. In one window is the selfish, bitter, angry young kid who refuses to accept responsibility for his actions...he claims the world made him the way he is; and...he's right.' Azariah raised his finger and pointed to my smiling figure. 'And there is the other part of you. Also nothing more than a kid, a young kid, but this one is different. This one is a dreamer.' 'The...duality of human nature...' I whispered softly, slowly beginning to grasp the concept. 'Yes.' He smiled as he spoke. 'You are both men, yet you are but one man.' 'A...good side and a bad side?' I asked slowly, my head swiveling back and forth between the windows. 'Everyone has each side, but one side will rule your life, which side is that going to be?' Azariah asked the question with a sincere tone in his voice, suddenly making things sound as important as they were. 'Will your bad side die?' Lord Azariah asked, pointing to the window on the other side of the room. 'Will your good side live?' His eyes turned slowly to the smiling figure in the window. 'I just...want to live.' I whispered, a tear rolling down my cheek as I stared into my own smiling face. 'We will allow you to live...but the life you choose will decide how long you live. Who are you Joshua? Who do you wish to become?' Azariah asked, his voice nothing more than a whisper. 'What do I have to do to live?' I asked, my voice at the same volume as Azariah's. 'I told you; simply choose. Each life will have its own advantages and disadvantages. The purpose of your life must be determined by your own choices. Choose ye this day; whom ye will serve.' Azariah quoted, taking the latter phrase from the old testament book of Joshua. 'And, what happens If I choose to be the angry child, again?' I asked, testing Azariah. He shrugged as he answered. 'Your fate will be decided by your own hands. We've given you another chance at life, after you took your life away. Each choice will have its own deep repercussions on the world around you.' 'And if I choose to do good with my life?' I tested him again. 'You will be rewarded.' His smile vanished as he spoke again. '...and punished. You will be loved for showing kindness to others, and hated for the same reason.' 'If I choose to do good with my life...it won't be easy? Shouldn't it be easier, logically it seems to be the right choice...' I mused as I shifted my gaze to the corpse in the other window. 'A good life will be a hard life.' Azariah stated soberly. 'But virtue is its own reward.' He added with a smile. 'And if you don't like my choice?' I asked, wiping the tears away from my face as they slowly began to cease. 'Only God will know of your choice.' Azariah said with a bow of his head. 'You'll never know?' I probed. 'No...because good people can do bad things, and bad people can do good things. The duality of human nature is so bittersweet, Joshua, you know this as well as anyone.' He squeezed my shoulder with his hand. Turning away from me he spoke with a loud voice. 'Council, rise!' Without question the eleven angels around the table, 5 men and six women, rose to their feet and walked towards the large door on the other side of the room, Azariah followed them to the door as they made their exit. He stood at the door and once again turned his eyes upon me. 'Joshua, the choice is yours. I won't tell you how to live your life, but I will be watching it...' Azariah's voice bore a grim tone. 'I won't forget this...' I whispered to him. 'Yes,', he said with a smile, 'you will. As soon as you step back into life you won't remember anything that happened here. Choose your destiny now...and choose wisely, dear friend.' One last smile crossed his face as he closed the door behind him. I stared at the smiling figure in the window. He looked so happy, as happy as I've always wanted to be but have never been. Turning my eyes again to my corpse I saw something of intrigue in those dead eyes. I was the tragic kid who would never grow up...the world had done this to me, it wasn't my fault. Those bastards should be held responsible for what they had done. If I'm fucked up, they're to blame. My eyes moved back to the smiling figure, there was something in his eyes that I had never seen in my own. A spark of hope...of joy... I knew it was time now to make my choice. My eyes went back... Forth... Back... And forth again. To the left... And the right. Gritting my teeth I let out a heavy sigh as I walked toward the window, it was now time to step into the window of my choice. I picked a window...and stepped through into the unknown. ---- 'Wake up.' Demanded a laughing Ken Partridge Jr. as he shoved his bare foot into my ribs. 'Mmmm...drgahf...ammnit. Mhmm?', I groaned at Ken as I rolled over onto my other side. 'Rise and shine, Josh, its breakfast time.' Ken chuckled, poking me again with his freezing cold toe. 'Mmmmm...' I protested a little more strongly this time, closing my eyes even tighter. 'What the hell are you doing sleeping on the floor anyway?' Ken asked, as he jabbed me with his toe again. 'Your mom.' I mumbled softly, in a failed effort to make him go away. 'My mother is a Saint.' Ken said with a smile this time putting his entire foot on my back as I jerked upright from the freezing cold skin on the bottom of his foot. Ahhh! Stop...you bastard.' I grumbled as I rolled over onto my knees. ...if your mom is a saint is that why she's always on her knees?' 'Oooh, Josh told a funny joke, are you gonna be okay? Do you need to lie down again?' Ken prodded, poking me in the ribs again with his icy toe, drawing a playful slap from my hand. 'Suck me.' I sneered, as I wiped the sleep from my eyes. 'Oh wait...your mom already did.' 'Enough with the mother jokes, why the hell were you sleeping on the floor? Your previous owners did housetrain you and teach you how to sleep in a bed, didn't they?' Ken asked, as I looked up at him. 'Hell if I know...' I shook my head. 'I don't remember getting on the floor.' 'Yeah...one other question.' Ken started, tilting his head to the side in curiousity. 'Mmmm?' I asked as I glanced up at him. 'When and where did you get that tattoo? I don't remember seeing it before.' Ken stated. I stood to my feet, dizzy and slightly confused. 'What tattoo?' 'The one on your wrist.' He said, pointing. 'Dude, I've had this for like two years...' I began, raising my right arm to expose the Arishikage tattoo on the underside of my forearm. 'Not that one...' Ken shook his head. 'Your other wrist.' 'I don't have...' I started, before I looked down at my left wrist. '...what the hell?' I raised my arm, to discover a new tattoo on the underside of my wrist, directly over the veins... It was a golden halo.
