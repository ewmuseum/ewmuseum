Organized Crime

14 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14/04 The day began as always at Paco Inc, the business offices of the great J-Gonz. From this office the famous GW wrestler runs his chain of organized crime, from this office J-Gonz runs the city of Dallas. On this particular day J entertains the Jewish Mafia leader, Tsion Raghbar. The two organizations have been working a deal to send some of the American grown grass over to the country of Israel where the drug is really getting popular. 'What do you take me for homes? An idiot, a fool?' 'What are you talkinga bout J-Gonz?' 'I know you're itching to stab me in the back, you're wanting to move in on my turf bro...but like a late great legend said, we gonna war!' With that two of J-Gonz men, Spencer K and The Hulk show up dressed in there black suits, and black shades. 'It's a cloudy day homes, what's with the shades esse?' 'Part of the job sir.' The two men pull out a pistol and hold it up to Tsions head as the Jewish Mafia leader begs for his life. 'Pointless pleading homes! Te miro tomorrow...BANG!' The dead body of Tsion falls to the floor and the two goons drag it out of the room leaving a horrid trail of blood. 'Organized pays bro, it pays to be bad and I'm bad! Josh Kosidlo is going to find that out first hand at the next Evolution show. On the side I do organized crime, but in the ring...I do it in the ring too esse! Frosty, bring me my baby~!' A heavy set man walks in carring the world famous oozi. J-Gonz holds it to his chest as and carress it like a woman on the first night. 'I've been called a lot of things in my life, Mexican trash, wet-back, cabron and worse. At Evolution Kosidlo you're going to call me daddy! You're going to beg and plead and cry out hoping that I don't break your neck. But I'm gonna shatter you homes, I'm gonna crush you esse, I'm gonna put you out! You see this oozi here baby, this is a sign of what I'm gonna do to you. We have a match at Evolution, but theres nothing that says that you're gonna make it there alive!' Jeremiah puts the oozi aside and gets up from behind his desk. He walks around his office and many of the beautiful stolen paintings hang on the wall. The carpet is that of a polar bear and the heads of a deer, a lion and a tiger hang over the fireplace. 'Your head belongs there Josh, it belongs next to the lion. Hmmm...I might just arrange it to make sure that I do have a collection of your head. It would look nice next to the deer, with the tongue sticking out, the eyes glazed over...ah yes Josh. I'm not out there to end your career...I'm out there to end your life! You think I'm joking? Frosty bring me the guy.' Frosty disappears for a minute and then returns with a man dressed in a wrestlers attire, the man has the name Josh Kosidlo written on his trunks. Covering his face is a solid black bag as to hide the wrestlers face. J-Gonz attacks the man and throws him to the ground beaing him savagly! The man cries out to no avail, only to have J-Gonz lay it in even harder. 'This is just a message Josh, take it in bro...watch this!' J-Gonz grabs the oozi and lays in a few consecutive rounds on the poor wrestler. The blood oozes down into the carpet as J-Gonz stand in his white suit smiling at his evil duty. 'Theres a certain wrestler that says, that he lies, he cheats and he steals...well to all the GW wrestlers, let it be know...I kill, I rape and I plunder! Eso es todo, cause J-Gonz put it like that!'
