Xavier arrives to Texas

20 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Xavier arrives to Texas Xavier 'Pheonix' Leizer Global Wrestling Promo #1852 Date: 1-20-05 Location: Texas The Dalls-Fortworth Internation Airport is still bustling with activity at 4:00am. People still sit and wait for their flights to either come or go, the intercom system still spews out lifeless calls to various planes. Over the pale horizon comes the red lights of a British Airways commercial jet. It's landing gear extends downward and it smoothly sails towards the flashing lights of the run way. The tires hit the asphalt and bounce as the plane slowly coasts to a stop. On board there are a few passengers, most of them just beginning to wake from a restless sleep. There's one man however, sitting next to a window who has been awake the entire time. He's gaze drifts over the landscape of Texas. The sun is already beginning to come up, casting an orange haze over the buildings in the distance. The man's face turns to disgust as he looks upon the skyscrapers of Dallas. As the flight attendant opens the door and begins directing traffic out, the man continues sitting in his seat staring out the window. The attendant clears everyone out and walks over to him. She bends down and taps him on the shoulder, 'Uh Mr. Leizer, it's time to get off the plane, you can get your carryon bag and leave now.' The man turns to the flight attendant and scowls at her, 'Miss, I would appreciate if you would keep your hands off of my suit, I don't want your filthy fingers all over it. I'm quite capable of getting off a plane, thank you.' The attendant backs up with a look of shock, she quickly regains her composure and backs away from Mr. Leizer, 'Yes sir.' The man stands up and moves into the isle. He reaches over head and pulls down a small black suitcase. He begins walking towards the exit with it by his side. As he comes to the door he tips his red fedora hat down and straightens his blonde hair before stepping into the pale light of the terminal. At the baggage claim he stands patiently as he waits for his luggage to come around. Finally he picks up two larger suitcases with the nametag reading, 'Xavier Leizer.' He carries all three pieces of luggage without much struggle down the corridor to the glass doors leading to the parking area. Outside he sets his luggage down on the side walk and adjusts his suit, looking around for his transportation. A red limo which seems to be based off of a cadillac pulls up. Xavier leaves his luggage sitting on the sidewalk and walks towards the door. The driver of the limo rushes out and runs around the limo to open the door for Xavier. 'Sorry Mr. Leizer for being late.' Xavier ignores the drivers comment and ducks into the back of the limo. The driver closes the door and proceeds to pick up Xavier's luggage, having much more trouble than Xavier did carrying it. He quickly opens the trunk and then piles the luggage in, before running back to the drivers door. Once inside he turns around and looks at Xavier, who's looking out the window in disgust. 'Where to boss?' The driver asks quickly. 'I believe I have to go to the Global Wrestling headquarters, and I hope you know where in Dallas it is. I don't know this filthy city, and I don't care to. So the quicker you could get me through this wretched place and to the Global Headquarters, the happier I will be. Now if you wouldn't mind, turn around and drive. I'd prefer not talking to the likes of you. I've had enough of having to deal with you people on the plane, I would like time to myself, thank you.' With that Xavier presses the button to raise the divider between him and the driver. The limo easily makes it through the streets of Dallas. The morning traffic has yet to come out on the road, so the trip to GW Headquarters is relatively quick for Xavier. Which is good, as the limo passes through Dallas his face gets more and more digusted. Seeing the vile buildings, concrete, and filthy signs of human life makes him sick to his stomach. The limo pulls to the GW headquarters and the driver quickly lets Xavier out. As Xavier stands he looks around and then looks up at the GW headquarters building. 'God, what am I doing here. Bloody filth everywhere. I should have stayed where I was and never accepted his proposition. Oh well, my word is my word and if I must, then I must.' Xavier sighs and walks to the door of the building, waiting for the driver to open it for him. Once inside Xavier walks past the main desk and to the elevator, dissapearing as the doors close behind him.
