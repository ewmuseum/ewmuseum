the beginning of an old war

10 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-10-03 Frost.... A dark growling voice can be heard in the distance. Sitting on a ledge ontop of a large building, Kullypso can be seen crouching just staring into the open vastness of the city. Jay.... Again another word muttered out of the man from the shadows. Kullypso's eyes can be seen with stone-cold glares as he watches hundreds of people interact throughtout the city going on with their pitiful meaningless lives. Y......B...... The once infamous cliq that once consumed the life of one of Kullypso's alter egos, Mark Gunthree. Kullypso stands up on the ledge and seems to be watching this one couple very closely. Step-by-step, Kullypso observes the movements of the two young kids. As the young couple walks further down the street; hand-in-hand. In an alley not too far from the wandering couple, Kullypso can be seen sliding down a fire escape. As the couple passes by the dimly lit alleyway, the sparkle of the chainmail around Kullypso's leather jacket can be seen as he steps out onto the busy streets following the couple. In a low voice that only he can hear, Kullypso begins to talk. Frost remember one thing I messed with you once before but you got the better of me. This time are you sure you want to mess with the shadow man? Kullypso stops walking and stands near a newspaper stand still following the couple. The couple seems to be going to a near-by taxi. Kullypso creeps closer to hear the two talk Dear, you got to go. It's not safe for you to be around hear much longer! The man puts his arm around his wife and gives a small peck onto her forehead as he opens the taxi door. Unwillingly the woman gets into the taxi after giving him a passionate kiss on the lips. As the taxi door closes, the man steps away as the taxi takes off down the road to its next destination. When the taxi can no longer be seen in the distance the man crosses the street and heads back the way he came. With a grin on his face and an evil thought coming across his mind, Kullypso speaks. I'm always watching!!!
