Friends

23 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 23 The scene opens up to my empty house. I was standing in the doorway, tears dripping from my face. I looked around at the open space. I started walking around the house remembering all of the memory’s that me and my mom had. I walked up the stairs and into her room. Big bed in the middle of the room with a brown dresser in the corner. There was a big mirror on the wall with another doorway that lead into her own bathroom. I walked over to her closet and opened. I had forgotten that it was a walk in closet. Suddenly someone came running into the doorway with a smile on. It was my friend, Joey. “Hey man. Sorry to hear about your mom.” he said. “It’s ok. I’m trying to get over it but its really hard to.” I said. “Yeah, I know how you feel. When my mom died I was petrified for a month. I didn’t talk to anyone. I guess your not like me because your talking to me right now.” he said. Me and him smiled at each other. Sometimes I called him my best friend but sometimes he acted like a real jerk and an asshole, so I just call him my friend even though he’s the one who I talk after something bad happens to me like when someone dies or something. “You wanna go for some ice cream?” he asked. “Sure I guess. You go downstairs and I’ll be down in a second. I have to do something.” I said. “Whatever dude.” he said back to me. He walked out of the room and down our hallway. I heard his feet hit the steps as he went downstairs. I walked over to my mothers dresser and opened the drawer on the left. I took those things my mother had left me in the hospital and put them in the dresser underneath some of her clothes. I closed the drawer and ran out of the room and downstairs. Joey was waiting in the doorway. “Ready.” I said. “Alright, let’s go.” he said. He opened the door and held it open for me. Me and him walked out of my house and onto out sidewalk that let down to our driveway. We walked down the little sidewalk and then got into to my car. It was a red convertible. I got into the driver’s seat and Joey got into the passengers seat. I started the engine and backed out of the driveway. I went down the street. *2 Minutes Later* Me and Joey walked into a Dairy Queen. If some of you hadn’t noticed, it was a ice cream place. Best in town I think. We walked up to the counter where there were a whole bunch of flavours of ice cream. I got a chocolate cone and Joey got a smarties blizzard. We started eating it as we walked to a table to sit down. We sat down and started eating our ice cream whiling we started talking. “So, what you going to do since your mother is gone?” he asked me. “I don’t really know. I’m going to try and forget about it but I’m still going think about her in my dreams and all that. I’m also going to get back into that wrestling stuff. I have a match at the PPV called Wrestlecade. It’s a 6 way cruiserweight elimination match. It’s going to be awesome and winner gets a Cruiserweight title shot in May. I’m going to try to win.” I said. “Awesome.” he said. *10 Minutes Later* Joey got up and walked over to a garbage can to put his empty blizzard cup into the garbage. I didn’t have to throw anything away because I just had a cone and I could eat it. Joey came back and got me to leave. “Let’s leave now.” he said. “Ok. Were are we going to go?” I asked him. “I don’t really know. We could go back to my house if you wanted to.” he said. “Ok then.” I said. We walked out of the door and back down to where we parked my car. We got to it and got into it and started driving down the street. Joey’s house was like way out of town. Probably 15 minutes away from right here where we are. *15 Minutes Later* We just got into the driveway of Joey’s house. We parked the car behind his and then got out. We walked up his sidewalk and to his doorway. He took a key out of his pocket and opened the door with it. We walked into it as he put the key back into his pants. We walked up the stairs into his room as the scene started to fade.
