Blowing Up

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 18 (for 21st) The scene opens up to a dark alley just across the street from Roswell’s house. He is walking carrying a bag that says Amy’s Grocery Store. But all of a sudden he hears something, but this isn’t something Roswell will want to hear. Man: Everything set for the kill. Man 2: Yes it is boss. Man: Good. When Roswell heard this he wondered. Roswell: What kind of kill? Man: Wait till that girl on 654 Evergreen Road will suffer. Roswell: 654 Evergreen Road, Oh my god! That’s where my mom lives. So Roswell quickly runs home and goes on the phone. Roswell: Father Clark is the only person that knows about my Long Distance Hearing, I can tell him. Father Clark: Hello, Who is this? Roswell: It’s me Max. Father Clark: Oh hi, what are you doing up so late? Roswell: You will never believe what I just heard from these 2 slum bags. Father Clark: What, what did they say? Roswell: They said they had explosives and were going to kill the girl who lives at 654 Evergreen Road. Father Clark: 654 Evergreen Road, isn’t that where your mom lives? Roswell: Yes and that’s why I’m scared. What should I do? Father Clark: Well the best thing you can do is get your mom and leave somewhere safe. Roswell: But how, I think they are going to do when she is sleeping tonight. Father Clark: So you have to get her fast, use you Super Speed to get there faster. Roswell: Thanks, But I’ll need your help, meet me at the church in 10 minutes. Father Clark: Yes, Ok.. **AT THE CHURCH** Roswell: Hello Father you made it. Father Clark: Yes, indeed I did. Roswell: So this is what we will do, You will distract the guys well I grab my mom, is that ok? Father Clark: Yes, that is a great plan, and then block them off with you Enhanced Strength. Roswell: Yes, hopefully that will work. **AT HIS MOM’S HOUSE** Roswell: Ok, they are here they are just getting there explosives ready. Father Clark: There ready. Roswell: You know Father, you’ve been like a dad to me, and I appreciate that, Thanks. Father Clark: I will help you no matter what. The 2 men start walking to the house and so does Roswell and Father Clark. The door is right in front of them. Roswell: Ok, ready go! Father Clark starts running towards the 2 men and shouting. Father Clark: Hey, come and get me you suckers. As he distracts them Roswell quickly runs into the house and grabs his mom out of the bed and runs back out the door. But the 2 men push Father Clark away and throw the explosives right where Roswell has his mom. Father Clark: Watch out Max! BOOM!!!! ** To Be Continued**
