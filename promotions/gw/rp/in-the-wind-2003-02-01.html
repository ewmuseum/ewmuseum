In the wind...

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/1 Blazing down long dust back-roads and hurtling around side-streets while sitting backwards on the edge of the seat of a Ninja was definitely not how I’d expected to spend my night. The inline four cylinder, dual overhead cam engine of this 900cc super street bike propels us forward with such alacrity that street lights and neon store signs go from marginally vague to utterly indistinguishable as they blur and elongate in our passing to the point that they appear as pennants of fluorescent light wafting in the wind. Dust dirt and various articles of miscellaneous debris are flung into the air in our wake and appear to be sucked into a black hole in our wake. Damn I gotta get one of these things... The fast flowing air whirls through her red hair causing it to whip and strike in a random arrangement of directions like a tribe of snakes bursting from a dark red pit. The wind provides their hiss as well as their strike which repetitively tickles my neck and ears. Aside from animating her hair, the wind stirred up as we slice through the stagnating air goes even further to give me quite a chill. Which isn’t all that hard considering I’m just wearing some Mecca swimming trunks and a pair of Nike Shox “Fire” running shoes... Looking down at my bare and tattooed body I witness the slow yet steady appearance of goose bumps as they bulge from my skin a few at a time like eager club goers until I’m completely covered. The “club” is packed and the moon is full... Reaching across my body with each arm and using it to rub the other, two recurring thoughts continually blare throughout my head in surround sound as I attempt to create enough friction to restore some warmth to my body. The first thought being... The black man was not invented for this sort of weather. And the second... I still don’t even know this chick’s name, or even where we’re goin. The first thought continues to bother me as I continue my expulsion of these goose bumps brought on by the less than tropical temperature due to all this wind. I mean damn, why wouldn’t this chick just drive a car? You know, like normal people. That thought is soon snapped in half cremated and then stuffed inside of a metal urn and left outside in a thunderstorm to be struck my lightning several times as the idea of being stuck holding onto the hood of a car with this chick at the wheel muscles it’s way into my head. The second thought stuck in my head bothers me much less, and in a way... I kinda like it. This is new, this is exciting, this is an adventure. I can honestly say that I have no clue what is going to happen next... and I like it. But as the icy wind continues to bite at my body like a pack of rabid wolves one thing’s for sure... I’ve never missed Florida so bad in my life. Traveling down the a tree lined street as nothing more than a neon green and dazzling silver blur with a full moon looming over our heads a quick look over my shoulder reveals that we are racing toward shipyard. Once there we rocket past the closed office building whose screen door hangs crooked on it’s hinges, and onto the rickety walkways made up of decomposing planks of wood and bent nails driven into them haphazardly. Our speed does nothing to the effect of decrease as we blaze down stretches of wood kicking up splinters as we pass before quickly stopping on a dime, swinging over to the side, and taking off once again tires screeching in protest. Finally we come to a stop in front of a house boat painted blue navy blue and heather gray gently floating atop the calm water. A short slab of reinforced sheet metal acts as a ramp between the ramp and floating abode, affording us a way onto the vessel. At a slow crawl we ease down the ramp repeatedly revving the engine as if to signal our arrival. Once at a complete stop she slides off of the bike as I leapfrog to my feet. As I turn around I find that I’m just in time to watch her remove the helmet from her head, unmasking her beautiful face, and then shake her hair back into form. It’s like watching a centipede burst from it’s cocoon as a delicately decorated butterfly and float away flapping it’s newly formed wings. The cool air and gentle breeze blowing over the water carries a slight chill but seeing her face once again in it’s full beauty somehow gives me a warm sensation throughout my entire body. It’s probably just my raging hormones. But what if it’s something else? She takes the set of keys hanging from one of the belt loops of her shorts and unlocks the door shooting me an inviting look and licking her lips as she slips inside the door. I am putty in her hand... Like a zombie I stumble inside the door, closing it behind me not knowing what is about to happen. Who knows what she’s got in here... This chick could be psycho... How exciting... Inside the house what I find is anything but some hot psycho chick clad in patent leather standing in the middle of a dungeon cracking a whip. But if causes my eyes to widen and my jaw to drop just the same... Inside the house I find her bent over causing her perfectly rounded ass to protrude even further, causing it to peek out slightly from the bottom. “Get your mouth off the floor...” she informs me, popping her head up from the now lit fireplace catching me standing there with my eyes bulging from my head and mouth gaped open. “The floor’s dirty” she continues, offering up a wink in order to let me know it was said in jest as she moves over into the kitchen. Still standing in the door way surveying the surroundings this seems to be a pretty nice place. Leather couch, big window in the front that allows for a view of the ocean that extends all the way out to the horizon, stone fireplace, cocaine white soft shag carpeting, this place is like a bachelor pad, except, well... for a chick. Drawn into the room by my curiosity and will to give everything a closer inspection I rapidly scan back and forth between each of the items in the room. My favorite of which being a seventy two inch projection screen television which immediately draws a grin on my behalf. One that grows even larger as I notice the Bose Lifestyle 28 system attached to it. Hypnotized by the amount of “toys” in her living room I slowly unclinch my nervous hands and begin to reach out for the remote. “I take it you like my place...” she bellows from the kitchen causing me to whirl around, not having realized she was watching me. Her full lips form into a devilish grin before opening once more. “Wait until you see the bedroom...”
