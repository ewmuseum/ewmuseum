Made Men

14 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/14/04 It's time to do business. That's the only thought that was going through Train's head as he saw the headlights slowly emerge out of the dark. They had be a small twinkle a second ago, but now they were glaring at him as the car that owned them traveled quickly in his direction. It had to be him. People hardly ever came down this road anymore. Train figured it was because of that old factory that had closed who knows how long ago. He'd found the place just driving around smoking a little something, thinking about his plans for GW a couple of weeks back. He'd been lost at the time, but he wasnt' really worried then, he needed some space and didn't care where he'd found it at the time. Train had pulled over on the side the bridge and sat on the hood of the Nav, just like he was now, and watched the sunset. In that whole time he hadn't seen one car come by. It was the type of place he used to love being. Quiet, calm... A place you could escape to when shit got too hectic. Back in the Windy City that place used to be the roof of the project he was living in with his mom and little sister. The rocky gravel like surface of the roof crunched under his shoes...he always used to feel like he was climbing some mountain to some undiscovered country when he went to the roof. No doubt the place was still a shithole, but what a view. He could see the whole city...the lights twinkling in the night. He used to sit up there and dream about the day when he'd be out there instead of stuck right where he was. This place kind of reminded Train of that roof. Lights of the city, now Dallas, twinkling on the surface of the river running swiftly beneath concrete structure over it. Train hadn't really thought about this spot again until he needed a quiet meeting place. He wasn't sure how he'd gotten there the first time, but it was an instinct thing that guided him back. Every Thug that's worth a damn has a sixth sense about finding spaces where no one could see... no one could hear... Where there would be no witnesses beyond some old buildings or some grass and a tree or two... and God... The devil too... Those were the only ones allowed to see. Damn. Maybe his sister had been right. This gangsta thing... No matter how fast you run, the instincts always seem to catch up to you. But he couldn't run from them. Despite it all he didnt' really want to. Wasn't any need to. Besides... This MOB thing...it didn't used to be some nifty name to market yourself to wrestling fans. The shit used to life and death and seeing as Train, a black man from the Southside of Chicago, had made it past 20 and wasnt' dead or in jail there was no doubt he knew how to play it. Anyway, it ain't like this was what he used to be doing. He wasn't going to wind up weighing down some chump with chains so he stays on the bottom of Lake Michigan. Naw...nowadays somebody just winds up getting his monkey ass whupped. That makes it all good...right? Good or no, the shit was going down. He'd made the call and now the son of a bitch was on his way. Some things needed to be made clear and ground rules had to layed. The MOB was the opportunity to smash through that wall and hit the big time. Train didn't need to like who he was working with to do the smashing, but he wasn't gonna hide who he was or what he thought for nobody. The bullshit had to be settled so an nderstanding could be met. Now... If he wanted to work it out with words cool. If he wanted to settle with fists, it was all good. Lord knows Train wanted an excuse to put his hands on the bastard. And if the shit got too out of control? The 9mm he had in the holster behind his back, hidden beneath his leather jacket, would end the beef. And judging from the current of this river it'd be a few days before anyone found his monkey ass. He could be back in Chi-town, dump the gun in the lake and be back before anyone even knew he was gone. He'd made the call from a cloned cell so ain't no way to trace the shit on ol' boy's phone records. Chances of them finding hair and fiber on the body would be pretty small so... Damn. Old habits really do die hard. Train hops down from the hood of the Nav and stands, arms hanging down in front of him, hands clutched together. His eyes squint a little as the jet black Lincoln Towncar pulls to a stop a few feet in front of the Lincoln Navigator that had been here waiting. The lights and engine switch off as Train just stands stoneface, staring at the driver, his face obscured by shadows. The driver's door pops open and out steps a dark figure that seems to grow and grow as he emerges into the night. The man exits the car and slams the door closed. Slowly he walks over towards the front of his car a few feet from where Train stood, unmoving and unflinching. As the man emerged, moving cautiously towards him, Train first noticed the shirt. It would have been your average wife beater if not for the large black cross in the middle, black lines drawing the outlines of the white flames burning around it. You'd have expected a better appearance from a man who just hopped out a luxury car than black jeans, boots and biker Jacket. But it's not like this was this cat was trying to win some popularity contest. Not this guy. He was arguably the most hated man in Global Wrestling... and proud of it. The driver of the car moved around the front of his vehicle and leaned back casually on the hood. Train was face to face with his new ally... Mark Makonie. Train half chuckled to himself as Makonie just stared smugly at him, his shaved head reflecting the light of the street lamps. 'Nice shirt,' Train observes after looking from the gorgeous car to the ugliness on top of it. 'You can give a boy classy things...just can't give him class, huh?' 'Oh, on the contrary little buddy.' Makonie responds with a smirk. 'What could be be more classy than knowing who you're superior to?' 'It'd take less time if I told you wasn't classier than the bullshit you poppin' dogg.' 'Oh really? 'Really.' The two men just stare at eachother for a moment, contrive grins almost masking the contempt they have for one another. Almost. 'I got to tell you Anthony... I was kinda intrigued when I got your phone call,' Says Makonie, looking at Train as if he was something to be scraped off his shoe. 'But, I got to tell you also, now that I'm here? The act of sullying myself with your prescense is quickly losing it's novelty. What he fuck do you want?' 'First off...' Train starts, unclenching his hands and stepping forward his stare icing over, controling his instinct to pull the 9 and ending this once and for all. 'Only my friends and family call me Anthony. Don't get this 'La Familia' bullshit confused with reality. You and me we ain't family and we damn sure ain't friends. You can call me Train.' Makonie slowly rises, the front of his car raising as he lifts his massive body off of it. He takes a step forward, looking down at the much shorter Train, taking his own step forward. 'haha... Don't get your panties in a wad... Train.' 'Don't push your luck... Mark.' Hearing this unrepentant man who lacks his 'superiority' breaks through the surface of Makonie's cool and calm demeanor. His smirk quickly turns to a sneer. 'This brings me back to my question little man. What the fuck do you want?' Makonie growls. 'I wanna find out if you know how to be a man.' Train responds plainly. 'Excuse me?' 'The fact that you're saying 'excuse me' like that tells me you heard exactly what I said.' 'Oh I heard, but I'm looking for a little clarification before I decide wether or not to stomp you through the pavement.' 'Well then, before you make the biggest mistake or your life let me clarify. See there's an old saying... A man's gotta do what a man's gotta do. Every day men round the world prove that shit's true. They get up and go to work at job they hate... with people they hate... for pay they hate. And I don't care whether it's in an office or some construction sight or washing dishes... It don't matter what the job is. Bottom line, hate it or not, they get up and they do the shit cuz that's what they gotta do. They do it to provide for them and theirs. That's the definition of a man son. A man makes a way whichever way he has to to provide.' 'That's a very nice speech. What's your point?' 'My point is you and me aint' too much different from them. My point is, all bullshit aside, you and me? We work with people we hate,' Train says staring into a pair of eyes nearly as cold as his own. 'But see here's where the difference is dogg. You and me? We got jobs we love. What could be better than whipping some loud mouth punk's monkey ass? Not a damn thing. And as for providing. Hell, I can see from the car I ain't got to explain to you what kinda money we looking at if the MOB gets big. You may not like me and I know good and God damn well I don't like you... But fact of the matter is, I can be a man and tell you you wanna of the toughest bastards in GW. One of 'em. And, if you're man enough to admit it you already know you looking at another one.' 'I suppose I can give you that.' 'Well then the shit is pretty obvious. You and me...working together? Ain't no doubt the shit is strange, but it's also an ugly combination for anyone that wants to step up. But here's the thing. See there are plenty of cats out there claiming to be men...but can't see past their own bullshit long enough to get the job done. Long enough to see what needs to be done and get the shit handled. They talk like they're men, but they don't know how to take care of business. A real man? He can put the personal shit aside and handle business. So the question is still... Do you... know how to be... a man?' 'Do you?' Makonie inquires. 'I was born one.' Train fires back. There is a long pause, the only sounds being the wind rustling through the trees and the river flowing beneath them. 'Inside the arena we kick ass. Outside... I don't wanna see you, hear you or talk to you.' Says Makonie. 'How's that sound?' 'Perfect.' 'What did you expect from a superior being little man?' Train just snorts at him and climbs inside his truck. Teh engine turns over as Makonie stands unwaivering, smuggly grinning at Train as he pulls off into the night. Inside the truck Train pulls the 9mm from his waist and places it in the glove compartment. Makonie just stands for a moment, watching the Truck pull off. Slowly he reaches behind his back and retrieves the Smith & Wesson .357 magnum. He smirks, believing himself to be the superior intellect as he switches the saftey back into place. He turns and stares out at the city skyline as the scene fades to black.
