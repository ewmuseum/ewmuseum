Beverly Hills: Blackmail

20 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 20, 2005 One Week Ago When Mike awoke he found that the world was a dark, greasy place with a horrific odor that caused eyes to tear. His body ached all over, more specifically his eyes and ribs. The last thing he remembered was being thoroughly tenderized by those two goons when he tried to get into the party at Charles’s house. The party! “Damn it,” Mike thought to himself, “I better not of missed that party.” He sat up and hit his head on the ceiling, causing a loud metallic clang that made his headache worse. His hand then slipped on something that felt like rotten chili and then he finally realized that he was in a garbage bin. With a disgusted look etched on his handsome features he lifted the lid of the bin and stepped into the cool night air. He looked down at his clothes and small disgusted grunt escaped his lips when he saw the many stains and bits of garbage littered all over his expensive threads. “I can’t go to the party like this,” he sadly muttered to himself. He looked around at his surroundings and quickly realized that he was behind the Tommy’s Burger Shack on Hollywood Boulevard. He reached into his pocket for his cell phone, which wasn’t there. He began to search himself frantically for the device and after a few moments remembered that it was shattered into several pieces across his library floor. He cursed silently as he began to look around for a payphone; he quickly found one a few yards away. As he emerged from the shadows of Tommy’s he looked about with a paranoid fear of being seen in his current state. So he hugged himself and hunched over with the hope that no one important would see his face. Despite the heavy amount of traffic he was able to make it to the phone without incident, people just naturally assumed that Biddle was another one of the city’s countless homeless. Mike picked up the receiver and at the same time began to fiddle around his pockets again, this time looking for spare change. After a few seconds Mike remembered that he never carried money with him. He tried to remember what the lower classes did when they didn’t have enough money to make a phone call, he remembered seeing a commercial for it once but for the life of him he couldn’t remember the process. He then happened to glace at the space above the phone’s keypad and saw the number [detail withheld]. “Aha,” he breathed as it all came back to him. All he had to do was dial that number and he’d be able to contact William and get a ride home. He dialed the number and followed the automated instructions. Mike placed the call to the phone in William’s limousine; the old man was always out for a ride at this time of night. Within seconds Mike heard the ringing that confirmed the process had worked. “Hello?” Answered the confused Edinburgh accent of William Bastafar. The line Mike had called was a very private line with a call ID and William couldn’t figure out how an unfamiliar number had gotten his number. Before Mike could answer William’s query the automated voice cut in, “You have a collect call from Mike, will accept the charges?” Mike heard an aggravated sigh followed by a yes. “There bloody well be a good explanation for this lad,” William said, his voice rising with each word. “I just need a ride home,” Mike calmly stated. “Hmph,” William grunted, “you’ve been out drinking martinis again haven’t you lad?” William finished with a fatherly tone. “No,” Mike defensively answered. “Very well then,” William began, “where are you?” “I’m by the Tommy’s Burger Shack on Hollywood. How soon can you be here?” “I’m on Hollywood at the moment so not long lad and you better have a damn good reason for calling me collect!” William hung up. Mike sulked back to the shadows behind Tommy’s and waited for William’s limo to appear. Thankfully the wait wasn’t long; Mike sulked out of the shadows and into the back of the long black vehicle. The interior was warm and smelled of fresh leather. Mike took a seat on one of the plush seats; the chair was so comfortable that he felt as if his problems had temporarily vanished. “What the bloody hell happened to you and what is that horrific smell?” William exclaimed once he laid eyes on Biddle. Mike looked into the mirror that separated the driver from the passengers and got a good look at his face. He cringed when he saw the bluish gray fist sized bruise that covered his left eye and the dried up blood that surrounded his nose. How something this horrible could happen to someone as good-looking as he was he would never be able to figure out. “What the bloody hell happened to you and what is that horrific smell?” William asked once more. Mike turned to William and explained himself; “I just went to a party and got jumped by two well-dressed gorillas. I don’t even know why but they claimed I wasn’t invited to the party and the next thing I knew I woke up in a garbage bin.” William glared at Mike as his face began to turn red; Mike’s occasional mental lapses tended to make him angry. “Have you gone completely daft” You can’t go to any of your little parties when you’ve been blacklisted!” A shocked look overtook Mike’s handsome features as he once again wondered how everyone knew about his situation. “How the hell do you know about that?” Mike asked. “Mr. Rumson was at the club tonight and after a couple of daiquiris the man becomes quite the braggart. In fact he told everyone there how he had single handedly gave Mike Biddle his eviction papers from society,” William said. “That bastard...” “You shouldn’t worry about something so trivial,” William interrupted, “you need to worry about cleaning yourself up and getting rid of that black eye before Evolution.” The conversation went on like that the whole ride home. No matter much Mike tried to turn the conversation back to his predicament and what the two of them could do to fix it, William was more concerned about Mike getting rid of his bruises. Of course the black eye didn’t go away in time for Evolution but to be honest that’s the trivial part of the story. During the ride home Mike realized that for the first time in his life he actually needed Hillary’s help. She was the only one that could back up his story; he just had to convince her to help him out. ***** A Few Days After Evolution Only one week on the blacklist and he was already bored. Evolution had been the highlight of his week, even if he had been delegated to opening match duty it was still better than sitting around his house with nothing to do. Despite the distraction of his distress over his situation he was still able to pull out a win, an important step in his quest to add more gold to his r’sum?. Hillary had yet to return his calls despite his constant pleading with her answering machine. So he decided that the only way he could enlist her aid in his time of need would to be to go to her apartment and talk with her in person. Mike parked his silver Aston Martin by the curb and stepped out into the hot afternoon sun. He wasn’t particularly thrilled about the fact that he was in West Hollywood, the part of town where all the wannabe actors/writers/directors flocked. The residents of this part of town always wandered about the streets trying their best to look trendy while they begged all whom walked by to read whatever drivel they dare call a script. Biddle took a quick look around just to make sure that no such morons were present before he walked up to Hillary’s building. Once he was satisfied that he was safe for the moment he walked up to the front door and pressed the button that was marked H. Watson. One moment after the loud buzzing noise of the button came the pleasant sound of Hillary’s soft voice over the little speaker above the buttons. “Who is it?” She asked. “It’s Mike,” he said, “I’d really like to talk with you.” A sigh and then, “Fine, if you must.” Another buzzer sounded and the front door became unlocked. Mike entered the building and made his way to the elevator, soon he was on Hillary’s floor. As he walked up to her door he found her waiting for him, still clad in her pajama’s: a plain blue t-shirt and pink teddy bear pants. She wasn’t wearing makeup and despite that fact her nymph-like features were still beautiful. Her baby blue eyes were a clear window to her inner pain; it hurt her deeply to see him like this but she tried her best not to show it. “You just can’t take a hint,” Hillary began with a faint smile, “can you?” Mike’s dark blue eyes rolled in annoyance, a common gesture when he was around Hillary. “I just need you to talk to Rumson,” he pleaded, “I know he’ll believe you.” “No he won’t,” she said. “And how do you know that” You haven’t even talked to him.” “And what makes you think I haven’t talked to him?” She asked with an annoyed tone. “The fact that I’m still blacklisted!” He answered with an aggravated shout. An angry looked contorted Hillary’s nymph-like features, it was the first time Mike had ever seen such a look from her and it scared him. “Will you just calm down,” Hillary said through her teeth, “I’ve been trying to help you.” “Then why haven’t you called me back?” Mike asked. “Because I’ve been busy with Charles,” she answered; she paid close attention to his body language to see if there was any hint of jealousy. She got the desired effect when Mike began to squirm slightly; he flat out did not like Charles. The red headed man always annoyed him with his constant attempts at competition, and Mike knew that Charles’s relationship with Hillary was another one of his attempts to compete with Mike. “So you’ve talked to Rumson already then?” Mike asked, trying to turn the conversation away from Hillary’s relationship with Charles. For some reason her association with the other man bothered him greatly. Hillary hugged herself, the hallway was cold and she wasn’t wearing a bra. “Of course I did,” she said, “I talked to him a few nights ago. He told me that his mind was made up and that there was no point in discussing it.” Mike sat on the floor and hung his head in defeat. He silently wished to himself that there were some way for him to get off the blacklist, if only someone or something would give him a sign. Hillary’s heart became heavy with sorrow, as she looked down at Mike; she’d never seen him down like this before. If only she could help him then maybe he’d finally appreciate her and then they could be together. Then it finally dawned on her, she knew the perfect way for Mike to get off the blacklist. “Why don’t you come inside,” she said with a bright smile, “I know a way to get you off the blacklist.” Mike’s dark blue eyes lit up, “Really?” “Really,” she said, “now get in here so I can tell you about it.” With renewed hope Mike followed Hillary into her apartment. He knew from past experience that the majority of her ideas were stupid but at this point he was willing to try just about anything. ***** The Next Day Strangely enough Hillary had a good idea, good enough that Mike waited patiently in the lobby of the Travelodge near the LAX with a very expensive digital camera that hung from a black strap around his neck. A pair of dark sunglasses covered Mike’s dark blue eyes and a LA Dodgers baseball cap was delicately placed over his golden locks, Mike Biddle was incognito. Hillary’s plan was very simple, all Mike had to do sit in the lobby and wait a young brunette with a black purse to pass by. He was then to follow her to a room and take a picture. The picture would then get Mike off the blacklist immediately. Mike was so eager to get it all over with that he had showed up at the hotel bright and early. He took both his breakfast and lunch at the busy Denny’s that was conjoined to the Travelodge. Mike also spent the majority of the day telling the front desk staff that he did not need their help and that he was perfectly fine. Thankfully, according to Mike’s watch it would only be a matter of minutes before the brunette would walk by and Hillary’s plan would fall into place. Mike’s eyes wandered over to the front door, where he saw the exact sort of girl Hillary had described to him in her apartment. A tall elegant brunette with a black purse entered the lobby and stopped at the front desk to request a room number, once she got the number she walked out of the lobby and into the main hallways of the hotel. Mike waited a few seconds before leaving his seat to follow her, the brunette was not to know that she was followed. From a safe distance Mike watched as the brunette stopped at a room by the pool. She knocked on the door and out stepped Nicholas Rumson. As the curly haired bastard gave the woman a passionate kiss on the lips the beautiful moment was ruined by the flash of a camera and a triumphant shout from Biddle’s lips. When Rumson looked over to see what all the commotion was about he went pale and looked as if he had seen a ghost. He motioned for the young woman to go inside the room, once she was safely inside he addressed the photographer. “What the hell is this about,” Rumson said, knowing full well what it was about. Mike walked over to where Rumson was standing and put his arm around the shorter mans shoulders. “How well do you think your wife will take it when I show her the picture I just took?” Mike smugly asked. “You wouldn’t...” Rumson nervously muttered. “Oh I would,” Biddle said with a smile as he tightened his grip on Rumson’s shoulders. “Why I bet she’d divorce you and take away all that money you’ve been so used to all these years, and without her as your wife you’d lose your position in society...why you’d practically be blacklisted.” Mike was enjoying himself for the first time in a long time as he watched Rumson squirm. Rumson slid out of Mike’s grip and began to nervously twiddle his thumbs. “What would it take to get you to keep that picture between the two of us?” He asked with a hopeful voice. Mike’s smile vanished as he became all business. “I think you know what I want Rumson,” Mike said. “Fine,” Rumson said out of desperation, “consider yourself off the blacklist. But how in the hell did you know about the affair, I’ve been so careful to cover my tracks.” Mike smiled once again. “It just so happens that your lady friend is the little sister of the hairdresser that styles the hair of a very good friend of mine.” With his business done Mike turned to leave but stopped when he felt Rumson’s hand grip his shoulder. “I think there’s one thing you should know,” Rumson said with a sigh, “Charles was the one that encouraged me to blacklist you. I wasn’t going to do it but then he threatened me with pretty much the same thing you’re threatening me with.” Mike was surprised that Charles would even have anything to do with this business. “Why would he want me on the blacklist?” Mike asked. “I have no idea,” Rumson answered, “but I just thought you should know who the real culprit is in this whole mess.” “Thank you,” Mike said before he walked away. Mike had hoped that the situation would be done with once the blackmail was complete, but with the new information from Rumson Mike knew that it was far from over. -To Be Continued-
