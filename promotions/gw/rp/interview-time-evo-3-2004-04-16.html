Interview time Evo #3

16 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/16/04 A little over 24 hours before the biggest match in Donny J McNasty’s career, WTAJ TV-10 is doing a one on one with the biggest name in Altoona, PA. Carolyn Donaldson is the interviewer, she’s very much an up beat and positive news anchor, who Donny has had the pleasure of talking with before. Donny is sitting across from her dressed up in a suit and tie, a very different way of dressing than Donny is used to, but he figured since this is a big deal he might as well dress that way. Carolyn begins the interview by introducing the interview and introducing Donny. “Good evening, this is a special presentation of the WTAJ this evening, as we have in our studio Altoona’s very own Donny J McNasty as many of you are aware of he’s a Superstar on Global Wrestling which can be seen on HBO Monday and Thursday nights. Tomorrow night Donny J McNasty has the biggest match of his career as he faces American Youth Josh for the World Championship.” Carolyn looks over in Donny’s direction and begins the interview. “Donny thank you very much for coming on in such short notice, this is a real pleasure to get a chance to interview you. How are you doing today?” Donny smiles and looks at Carolyn. “I couldn’t be better, thank you for having me on here. It’s a real pleasure to get a chance to talk to my fans and the people of my hometown.” Carolyn looks down at her notes. “Ok lets get this started, now Donny, most people remember you from your days in the now defunct SWF, what made you go to Global Wrestling?” Donny looks at her takes a breath and gives his answer. “Well you know some people might think it was for the money or something like that, but with me it’s a different situation, I was bored, I needed some competition in my life again. I kept getting phone calls from other wrestling federations and even from people who said they were going to re-open the SWF and nothing felt right. Then I was flipping through the channels one night and I seen this show called Evolution on HBO, and I told myself, now there’s a place I could work for. There was just something about the atmosphere of the arena, the fans, and the Superstars. So if you want to know what brought me to Global Wrestling I would have to say it was definitely their atmosphere and overall aura.” Carolyn nods and asks another question. “What is it that you feel that you bring to Global Wrestling that no one else does?” Donny looks up to the ceiling in thought. “Hmmm well I would have to say it’s being a person who listens to what fans want. If they want me to make a guy bleed I do it. Not many people get a chance to see it really but I sign the autographs before and after the show. Even after a bad night. If it wasn’t for the fans being supportive of me I wouldn’t have worked so hard to get where I am today.” Carolyn nods and looks at her notes for another question. “Donny tomorrow night has to be the biggest night of your young career, what are your thoughts going into the match and what’s your basic strategy” Donny looks and smiles lightly. “There is no doubt Josh is one heck of an Athlete, he’s quick on his feet and he’s a skilled wrestler. He worked long and hard to become the World Champion, but I also have worked long and hard to get where I am at today. What I can tell you of my strategy is to keep Josh off his feet, use my power, strength and size to my advantage. If I can toss him around like a rag doll well then we’ll have a new champion. As far as my feelings going into this match, I been thinking about it all week. I mean this is the high light of my career, I win the World Championship and the world is at my feet. Right now, the fans love me win or lose. And that right there is almost greater than being a World Champion.” Carolyn looks at Donny with a smile. “Donny I can personally tell you that the whole city of Altoona is cheering for you to win, and whether you win or lose, the people of Altoona will think of you as their champion regardless of what happens tonight.” Donny has a large smile across his face. “Thank you Carolyn, that means a lot to me to know that everyone here is behind me one hundred percent” Carolyn looks at her notes and has one last question for Donny. “One last question Donny, what kind of words of advice could you give all the young boys and girls out there watching you week in and week out in Global Wrestling?” Donny shakes his head in agreement. “Well for one, stay in school, get your high school diploma, go to College, Trade School or join the military. Talk to your parents about everything, let them know what’s happening in your life good or bad. It could very well prevent bad things happening to you later in life. Be yourself, don’t let others tell you how you have to be. Individuality is one of the things I pride my self on. Work hard, and give everything you do one hundred and ten percent” I think that’s about it. I guess I’ll have to do some motivational speeches in the high schools in the area then.” Carolyn acknowledges what Donny has said and is in agreement with him. “Well Donny that’s all the time we have. Again thank you so much for coming on and talking with me. And I wish you all the luck in the world for tomorrow night” Donny smiles and shakes his head. “Thank you Carolyn, lets do this again” Carolyn wraps it up. “Remember tomorrow night on HBO it’s Global Wrestling’s Evolution. Stay tuned for your regularly scheduled programming, and later tonight it’s 10 news at 11, Live Local and Late Breaking?” As the interview goes off the air, they both get up from their chairs and shake hands and Donny walks off the set. Tune into Evolution tomorrow night to see if Donny beats the odds and wins the World Championship?
