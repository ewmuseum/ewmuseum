Sunday dinner with the Fosters

27 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/27/04 It was another Sunday night at Coretta Randall's house. Brick, his brother-in-law Kyle and his friend Aaron were setting the table. Brick's sister Monica was resting on the couch. Her figure told that she was at least four months into her pregnancy. Aaron's wife Felicia sat on the couch next to her, with their daughter Ashley sitting on her lap. 'I wish you two would let me help you set the table.' 'Oh no,' said Kyle. 'As long as you're carrying the twins, you're going to take it easy.' 'Yeah, we ain't takin' no chances with the baby,' Brick added. 'I just don't like feeling useless.' 'Hey, you're carrying my babies. That makes you far from useless.' 'No, but just sitting here doing nothing makes me feel useless.' 'I know how you feel, Monica. When I was pregnant with Ashley, Aaron went into total overprotective mode. I couldn't even go to the bathroom with him hovering over me.' Brick then turned to Aaron. 'That's nasty, man.' 'Hey, she was pregnant with my first-born, okay?' 'Let's shift the subject. So, Brick, Aaron, how's the pay-per-view concert coming along?' 'Pretty good. We still have a few rough edges to iron out, but DMAK should be ready by the night of the concert.' 'How come you guys are doing it at the Apollo? Why not somewhere like the Garden or Nassau Colisseum?' 'It was a bit too pretentious, we thought. We figured the Apollo was just perfect. That's where people got to hear them outside of the subway stations for the first time.' 'Jamal!' Coretta called from the kitchen. 'I need some help with this food!' 'Be right there, Ma! Come on, fellas.' As Brick, Aaron and Kyle went into the kitchen, Monica and Felicia took the opportunity. 'I just have to say, Monica, I'm impressed. Your first pregnancy and you're carrying twins. I don't know if I could have handled it.' 'I'm dealing with it. It's not easy, but I'm dealing with it.' 'So, have you thought of any names yet?' 'Not really. Since the twins are male and female, we might give each a combinations of our names.' 'Just be careful you don't give them names they'll get teased over. Aaron and I are still getting grief from your brother.' 'Well, you have to admit, Ashley Lillian Foster? A-L-F?' 'Et tu, brute?' Just then, Brick, Aaron and Kyle came out, placing food on the dining room table. Felicia helped Monica to her feet as they went into the dining room. Dinner on this particular Sunday evening was roasted chicken, macaroni and cheese, lasagna, collard greens, candied yams, ham, cornbread, potato salad. It wasn't unusual for Coretta to cook a lot of food, but this time, it was somewhat warranted. 'OK, everyone, time to bless the food. Jamal, it's your turn.' 'Father God, we come to you in the name of your son Jesus Christ with thanksgiving and praise. We thank you for this day and for this time together. We thank for our health and for our lives. We thank you for the food we are about to recieve and for the hands that prepared it. We ask that you bless this food to build the body, the mind and the soul. In Jesus' name, we pray.' 'In Jesus' name.' 'Amen.' 'Amen.' After blessing the food, they began to prepare their plates. Naturally, Aaron prepared Ashley's plate for her. 'So, what's up with your match at Maximum Carnage?' 'It's me against John Zero and Ariefev...no, Arfiev...no...' 'Arefiev.' 'Thank you. Anyway, the International title is on the line, and after what happened last month, I intend to win this time.' 'I know. Sabbot really screwed you over.' 'Do not remind me.' 'I'm telling you, man,' said Aaron, 'you need to be careful going into that match.' 'Why?' asked Monica. 'Aaron's worried Zero might want to get payback on me.' 'Weren't Zero and Deacon were tag team partners?' 'Yeah, they called themselves the Midnight Sons. Even held the tag titles together. So?' 'So, you don't think he's gonna be mad at you for getting Deacon suspended from Global Wrestling?' 'Hey, I didn't get Deacon.' 'Deacon got Deacon suspended,' Brick and Aaron said in unison. 'Jamal, I warned you that game you were playing was gonna create problems,' Coretta added, shaking her head. 'Look, it's not a problem, okay? I'll deal with Zero.' 'And what about Arefiev? After what he did to Kitty Mac and Adam Meyers...' 'They didn't see it coming. I will. Plus, I've already beaten Arefiev, remember? Me and Donny had a tag match against the Vergomovs last Evo. Plus, from what I hear, Zero and I ain't the only cats he has to worry about.' 'That was a tag match. This is a Triple Threat. Every man for himself.' 'Look, let me worry about Maximum Carnage. Besides, the food's getting cold, so let's eat.'
