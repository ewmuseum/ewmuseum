Recording the track

29 October 2003

Date: 10-29-03 “OK, let’s go over that again, from top,” Brick said over the intercom as he and Aaron watched from the control booth in the recording studio For weeks, Brick’s singing group DMAK had been working on their third album. Their debut went platinum in a matter of weeks, as had their second. This, their third album, was an album of cover songs. They had done covers before on their previous albums. On their debut, they sang “Shower Me with Your Love” by Surface. It was one of the top wedding songs of the summer. On their second album, they did a cover of the Deele hit “Two Occasions”. This album would be filled completely with covers, ranging from “In the Still of the Night” to “Hotel California”. There was also going to be a cover of the old Lisa Lisa and Cult Jam song “All Cried Out”, a duet with Kelly Rowland of Destiny’s Child. They had been working on one particular track, since early in the morning. It was the very song that got them discovered in the first place. It was “Kiss and Say Goodbye” by the Manhattans. Since the song had such meaning to the group, they wanted to get it just right. They decided to sing it a capella. Marcus, the bass in the group, started off the song with a spoken part, followed by Damien, one of the group’s two tenors, singing lead. This has got to be the saddest day of my life I called you here today for a bit of bad news I won't be able to see you anymore Because of my obligations, and the ties that you have We've been meeting here everyday And since this is our last day together I wanna hold you just one more time When you turn and walk away, don't look back I wanna remember you just like this Let's just kiss and say goodbye I had to meet you here today There's just so many things to say Please don't stop me 'til I'm through This is something I hate to do We've been meeting here so long I guess what we done, oh was wrong Please darlin', don't you cry Let's just kiss and say goodbye Many months have passed us by (I'm gonna miss you) I'm gonna miss you, I can't lie (I'm gonna miss you) I've got ties, and so do you I just think this is the thing to do It's gonna hurt me, I can't lie Maybe you'll meet, you'll meet another guy Understand me, won't you try, try, try, try, try, try, try Let's just kiss and say goodbye Mmmmmm (I'm gonna miss you) I'm gonna miss you, I can't lie (I'm gonna miss you) Understand me, won't you try (I'm gonna miss you) It's gonna hurt me, I can't lie (I'm gonna miss you) Take my handkerchief and wipe your eyes (I'm gonna miss you) Maybe you'll find, you'll find another guy (I'm gonna miss you) Let's kiss and say goodbye, pretty baby (I'm gonna miss you) Please, don't you cry (I'm gonna miss you) Understand me, won't you try (I'm gonna miss you) Let's just kiss And say goodbye “OK, fellas, that was beautiful. We don’t need to do it again. That was just perfect.” “Brick, you sure?” Aaron asked. “Positive. OK, let’s break for lunch.”
