Training Session Part 4

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/10/03 The two brothers stood side by side. Ichiro tired and exhausted from using the Lotus technique only an hour before. Jushin panting heavily from the beating he had just taken as the result of his brother's little test. 'Alright, now this can be sort of complicated, and you will fail several times before you can get it down brother. So do not get angry or frustrated, merely remain calm and keep trying. Also the first time you get it, you will be able to hold it for only a minute or two, you must practise on your own to maintain the state of being. For that is something that I cannot help you with. Do you understand?' Ichiro began. 'Sure do, now let's get onto this,' answered Jushin. Well at least he was eager, though Ichiro. Jushin was very proud and very determined, he would not stop tonight, until he learned this technique. It was this pride, that would fuel Jushin. And it would be that very pride which would end up allowing him to succeed. Ichiro began by relaxing himself. He would need a little bit of energy to perform the technique again, and he would have to do it slowly enough, that Jushin would be able to observe the minor details. Jushin knew his brother was tired, so he allowed him a short time to rest and prepare. He was really excited about this chance, he had never been this excited for something of this small a magnitude before. But strangely now he was. He was excited at the opportunities this would offer, and at the power he and his brother would soon yield. Jushin paced around as his brother laid on the soaking wet mats, trying to regain some strength and energy. Jushin was ready, but could not force his brother, so he instead just paced around and waited. Ichiro laid across the two mats atop the ledge. They were soaking wet and very uncomfortable, but he needed to regain strength, and lying down was the only way he could. No matter how uncomfortable it was. About twenty minutes later, Ichiro awoke from a short power nap. The rain had now completely stopped and Jushin was now sitting near him. Ichiro stood up, feeling refreshed and ready. Jushin then followed suit, not so much refreshed as ready. The two brothers stood up, and Ichiro quickly shook himself to awaken himself fully. Jushin stood waiting as Ichiro finished waking up, and slowly began to calm himself down. Jushin watched as Ichiro's body became completely relaxed, every muscle in his body put to ease. He was surprised that Ichiro was still standing, because of the muscle relaxation in his legs and the rest of his body for that matter. 'Alright, I am ready,' said Ichiro calmly. 'Well that's good to hear, I have been ready for the past thirty minutes,' answered Jushin, teasing his big brother. Ichiro could only smile at his brother. For a guy so intense and usually angry, he did have a pretty good sense of humour. 'Alright then bro. Do nothing right now, except stand and observe. Watch every part of the entire technique, there are no large movements, all the details are minor and easy to miss. And I am currently tired and will only be able to do it once, maybe twice to show you,' instructed Ichiro. 'I understand,' said Jushin. 'Okay, then watch carefully,' said Ichiro. Ichiro stepped away from his brother, and stood directly in line with him. He figured it would give him the best view possible to notice all of the little things. Ichiro closed his eyes, and clenched every muscle in his body. He began to pump his fists, and he concentrated his mind on the Lotus. Clearing his head of everything else, while continuing to relax and tense up his muscles. He then began to feel it's coming, he felt the surge of energy and adrenaline. His body began to shake slightly, he could feel it. His eyes shot open, and they became very dark, before rolling back slightly. When his eyes finally settled and the shaking stopped, he knew it was finished. He felt stronger, and quicker. He knew that he had done it successfully. 'Is it hard?' asked Jushin. 'Not really, just tense your muscles and then relax them. Then next all you have to do is open your mind, I usually just blank out all thoughts except for that of the Lotus petals burning back at the temple,' answered Ichiro. 'And that works?' questionned Jushin. Ichiro could only nod to reply. 'Now give it a shot,' said Ichiro. Jushin nodded, and stepped away from his brother slightly. He wasn't too sure if he could do it, but he was going to try his damnest to do it. He wasn't going to give up, not until he had the technique mastered, just like his broteher. All he wanted was to be able to have the technique down pat, and then they would be untouchable. He clenched his fists, and his jaw. He began to slowly pulsate his muscles in and out. He tried to relax his mind, and focussed on the Lotus as much as he could. But it didn't seem enough, he was trying to hard to keep his muscles going. 'I can't do it, I am paying too much attention to making sure I tense and relax my muscles,' complained Jushin. 'Don't think about your muscles, at first you'll have to. But as you clear your mind, stop thinking about it. It will become second nature and your body will do it on it's own, then just focus on the flames of the Lotus petals, like I said,' answered Ichiro. 'Alright, let me try again,' replied Jushin. Jushin again clenched his fists, and began to slowly flex and then relax his muscles. He felt a slight pain, after a little while and became distracted by it. Ichiro noticed the looks of pain on Jushin's face and tried to encourage him through it. 'Don't be a pussy, you can do this. Don't let your mind be troubled by the pain, the pain helped me get through this the first time, and it's going to help you too, now focus!' encouraged Ichiro. Jushin clenched his jaw, and tried to forget his pain. He spent so much time thinking about getting rid of the pain, that his body began to flex all on it's own. 'You were right bro. My body is flexxing on it's own,' stated Jushin. Ichiro couldn't believe it. The big tough Jushin Nakazura, was acting like a little girl at Christmas time. 'Great, don't inform me about it, get back to work. Focus hard on the Lotus. When you do it right, you should feel a large surge of adrenaline, almost like electricity is being pumped through your veins.' Jushin focussed hard. Thoughts ran through his mind rampidly, he tried to dismiss them as quickly as they came upon. But he couldn't do it. He stopped tensing his muscles and fell to his knees in defeat. 'GOD DAMN IT!!!' screamed Jushin. 'Relax, it's tough. Don't get discouraged or fustrated, you'll never be able to do it if you get down on yourself,' encouraged Ichiro. 'Well apparently, I can't do it now anyways,' snarked Jushin. 'Well then stop being a whiny bitch, and try again!' stated Ichiro. Jushin stood up and glared at his older brother. He was furious, when he saw the smile on his brother's face. It fuelled him, he was going to show his brother he could do it. Anything Ichiro could do, he could do. He wasn't better than him. Jushin closed his eyes, and clenched his fists once again. He bit his teeth down hard, and began to tensen his body. He began to let all thoughts slip from his mind, he began to focus simply on the temple in Mt. Kidake. He wasn't on the Lotus yet, but it was keeping the other thoughts out of his head. He began to focus on the Lotus now. He saw the black and purple flames erupting and so it went for what seemed like an eternity, but was really only a few seconds. That's when it began, he felt a surge of adrenaline. His blood rushed throughout his veins, carrying the rush. His body began to shake violently, and his eyes began to roll up into the back of his head. If Ichiro hadn't known better he would have thought that Jushin was having a seisure, but instead he stepped back and observed. Jushin continued to shake and pulsate, and his eyelids quivered. He was panting and breathing heavily, the same signs as the first time Ichiro performed the technique successfully. Suddenly Jushin's eyes burst open, they were all black, but with a tint of purple where his pupil would have been. He stopped shaking, but continued to breath heavily as he looked around, feeling stronger and better. 'How does it feel?' asked Ichiro. 'Better than anything I've ever experienced. I feel so, powerful.' replied Jushin. But as his thoughts faded away from the Lotus, he began to lose it. The electricity left, and he was left panting for breath. He was now tired and in pain. 'Well, that was pretty good for your first time,' said Ichiro. 'We'll keep practising, and you'll soon be able to fully control it. Then we will test the true strength and power of your new ability.' 'Okay. Is it always like this when it's over?' asked Jushin. 'Yes, you will feel very tired, and very, very out of breath. There isn't any way to deal with it, it's just the way it is. But you'll get used to it, but there is no way to adjust. Except to get some rest, so what do you say?' answered Ichiro. 'Sounds good bro,' answered Jushin. 'It's been a long day, and I just want a shower and some sleep,' said Jushin. It was true all he wanted to do was go to bed. But that night when he fell asleep, it was different. His mind felt relaxed, amused by the new technique he had learned. And his heart and soul felt excited and relieved at the coming of this new technique, he would soon master. Soon enough, he and his brother would be invincible.
