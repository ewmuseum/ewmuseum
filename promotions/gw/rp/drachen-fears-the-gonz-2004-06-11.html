Drachen Fears The Gonz

11 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/11/04 'I like it!' Rasberry stood looking at J-Gonz holding up a poster that read, Drachen fears The Gonz. She smiled as J-Gonz showed his appreciation with a romantic kiss to her cheek. It had taken her two hours to get it to look perfect, the red, green and silver glitter and the drawing of the Mexican flag had. 'You think it'll look good on TV?' 'Babe! It'll look better then the Television Championship around my waste! And that's gonna look good.' 'I'm so excited papi! You as the Television Champion, wow! That's all I can say! Wow! And my mom said I'd never amount to nothing if I married a Mexican.' 'Well you have to remember babe, your mom aint nothing but a honky tonk trailer trash ho, wonder if she's related to Drachen by any chance?' 'No, I don't think so. That's awful trash there, and my momma wouldn't be related to trash like that.' The two love birds shared a laugh as they left the room. In anothe room Paco and Chocolate were shown talking. The two clonies once again dressed in the nice suits that had been purchased by El Mariachi. 'Paco, the problem is he doesn't know that our little friend is back, and he wants to know who took him out.' 'I don't think it'll be good to tell him today. He's so happy about just being with Ras...well he should know though. Our little friend might become a hinderence.' 'He told us to take him out, to take him out for good.' 'You were driving Chocolate...' 'Yeah I know...' Paco and Chocolate hush as J-Gonz enters the roomed. Gonz is adourned in an ivory white suit that shines, his vest is a solid blood red and the top of the shirt has been left unbuttoned. 'Well-well, if it aint Paco and Chocolate! How are you two boys doing.' 'Not so good boss, me and Paco have something we want to tell you.' 'Ah come on boys, enjoy the day with me today. It's a great day to have fun and smile. Sit-sit, join me.' The three men take a seat around a table and J-Gonz pulls out a fresh bottle of Whisky. He pours some into three shot glasses and reclines back into his seat. 'Boys boys. The day is so alive boys. I'm three days away from becoming the first ever Mexican Television Champion. I think after winning the title I'm gonna rename the belt to the Mexican Championship...yeah that sounds like a good idea. So tell me boys, what do you have to say.' 'Well boss, you know how we were supposed to take out your little friend?' 'Yeah, everything went real good didn't it? It sent him to the hospital...' 'Well boss, it seems that this friend of ours has some super human powers. He is back and he's looking to find out who did it.' 'What?! You guys were supposed to put him on the shelf!' 'We did what we could boss.' 'Alright, no problem...I'll take care of that at Evolution. You guys leave that up to me.' J-Gonz stood up from the table and headed to the window. He looked out into his city, the city of Dallas. His penthouse apartment hovered over the city just like he hovered over Global Wrestling. 'What more can a man ask for? I've got money, I've got the woman and soon I'll have the Television Title...make that the Mexican Title! Our little friend thinks he's gonna ruin my plans, but I'll show him on Monday, that you don't mess with El Mariachi! The sign says it all, Drachen fears The Gonz!' J-Gonz holds up his hands and continues to look out of the window. A smile comes over his face, and soon he turns around and walks away from the window.
