Building A Mystery (part 2)

28 March 2005

Date: 03/28/05 Your life slips through your hands Like grains of sand You watch it go There's no time to be lost You'll pay the cost If you say no There's no way out of here When you come in You're in for good You blow your only chance away - Monster Magnet *** Corvus returned home to his manor with extra cargo then he had left with. The gates slowly opened and he drove in and moved on the driveway into the garage. He stopped the car quickly and hit the button so the garage door would close. The neighbors may put up with some of his odd behavior but he knew that some of them were probably sneaks. He didn’t really need anyone seeing what he was up to, especially considering he had a run in with the police earlier. The key to the trunk was placed in the lock and obviously the guy was still alive and boy was he a nervous wreck. Corvus looked at him as he started screaming about the amount of trouble that behemoth was in for kidnapping him. All he did in response was punch him in the face as he said, “Shut the fuck up.” When he was in moods like this, Corvus very seldom said much. It was like he was sent into this world that only he was in and nothing else but what task he was focused on at the current moment. He pulled the unconscious mans body out of the trunk and slung him over his shoulder as if he were a feather. The trunk was then closed and he proceeded to put in the access code to gain entrance into his house. Once inside, he took the man to his basement and looked through his things to make sure that the first thing was he wasn’t a cop. Something odd seemed to come to him as he was looking over the man. The scent the man produced smelled familiar as if he had encountered it before. As he was chaining the poor man (well one who was on the outside looking in would consider it unfortunate), he tried to recollect where he had caught on to the scent that this flesh bag was giving off. Without wasting much time ... it came to him. He had smelled it in his living room when the cop left. Maybe this was the jerk that broke into his house. Everything he wanted to know would find its way out into the open in due time. The first level of torture was to not give his prisoner any food, he was going to not give him anything and if becoming close to being dehydrated didn’t give him any answers then he would take the next step. That step is not something that anyone would want to endure certainly. *** He left the man in the dark, dreary, basement and went upstairs to make a phone call. Corvus had not talked to his old friend Matt Landes (many of you know him as Epitome from WMW/GW fame) and since the topic of Cleveland came up with the detective, he thought he might give him a heads up. With phone in hand, he dialed the number and waited for it to ring a few times. Matt answered the phone but he was pretending to be his own answering machine and told Corvus to leave a message at the beep and then made an atrocious beeping sound. “Matt, it’s me ... Corvus. Quit playing games you tard and listen up. I don’t have much time to talk but I was entertaining a detective this morning and she seemed to have information about the teacher we did in a while back. You remember the one ... yeah the sick pedophile we gave an acid facial. So anyway ... keep your eyes and ears open. If anyone comes to you about it then call me back, I want to make sure that things don’t get ugly. That will make my job of cleaning the mess up a little harder. Regretfully, I have to get going. I’ll try and give you a call another time so we can catch up ... I figured this was too important to waste any more time on without telling you. I have a guest that needs tending to, talk to you later, ok?” He ends the conversation and walks into the kitchen to get a glass of bloodwyne. He was parched from dealing with the particulars of that evening. Corvus looked around for a minute and found what he was looking for: a fine box of Cuban cigars. He smoked them now and then like they were out of style, probably because he could afford them in such high quantity. He brings the box and his glass with him as he walks downstairs to start questioning the schmuck that he threw in his trunk after catching him in the act of stalking. The chair he set up was a good couple feet away even though the guy was shacked by both arms and feet without any means of escaping. Corvus took out a cigar and used a cutter to get it ready as he placed it in his mouth; lighted it with a Zippo lighter. “Are you ready to tell me what I want to know yet or do I have to start getting ugly?” The man tried to struggle a bit until he realized that he wasn’t going anywhere. His eyes set upon his captor sitting across from him smoking a cigar. A small grin appeared upon his lips as he spoke for the first time since he screamed at Corvus as he was about to be removed from the trunk of the car. “If you can’t figure shit out yourself, then you are one stupid ass son of a bitch. If it hasn’t become apparent to you yet, it will soon enough ... you stupid cocksucker!” Smoke came out from Corvus? mouth as he mulled over the words coming from the mouth of the man. At first he considered punching him out again but that would only prolong his ability to get information if the victim was unconscious. “The first thing I would think is that you’re working for the pig fucker Salaok, but you just don’t seem to fit his style. That leaves me with more questions then it does answers. I’m going to give you one more chance to tell me who sent you and what you were doing breaking into my house. I know it was you ... so you might as well tell me what is going on now because I’m not going to hesitate to make things uneasy for you as you can see I haven’t fed you in a day and a half, I don’t plan to either unless you tell me what I want to know.” There was something about this guy that he couldn’t pinpoint as of yet, besides the fact that he wasn’t able to read his thoughts like he could from most people. It pissed him off that he couldn’t get what he wanted by using his dark mojo, but if the guy didn’t want to talk then he would definitely learn that Corvus was not someone who liked to play games. “I already said all I was going to say ... if you don’t like it too fucking bad! Your day of reckoning is coming, vampire. We’ll see who is mighty when you are begging for mercy you ...” That was all that Corvus could take before he stuffed a ball gag into the guys mouth and held it closed as he grabbed some electrical tape he had nearby and taped his mouth shut. He went through the personal effects that he had taken from the man earlier and looked at his wallet to find out a little more. The guys name was Jack Scanlan, who lived in the city from the address on his driver’s license. He tossed it aside as he inched closer to him and his eyes changed from their normal light blue hue to a crimson color. His hand was then on Jack’s throat as he spoke to him. “I would be clich” and ask you if you felt lucky Jack, but I’m sure bow now you realize you are shit out of luck by not telling me what it is that I want to know. You thought I was having fun by just shackling you up and not giving you any bread or water ... ha! You haven’t seen a thing yet.” With that said ... he took the cigar out of his mouth and pressed the end of it right into Jack’s forehead. Even though Jack’s mouth had been gagged and taped, you could still hear his groans of anguish, which were reveled in by the sick, demented, creature of the night. This was only the beginning of his despicable acts. He looked at the cutter and became inspired instantly. He reached for the cutter and grabbed Johnny Boy’s hand and slipped his pinky finger through the hole. A low chuckle comes from his mouth as he snips the digit off with ease. The muffled screams brought him gratification on a grand scale. He reveled in it as he didn’t stop with the pinky. Momentarily he paused to take a drink from the glass he had sitting nearby. He brought the cutter up again and continued to work, first on the ring finger *snip* and then the middle finger, *snip*, followed by the index finger. *snip* The man was in obvious agony given that he didn’t have any medication to prevent the feeling of pain from going through him. Tears began running down his cheeks as Corvus got to the last of the digits on his right hand, the thumb. When he was finished, he lit another cigar without even wiping the blood off of the instrument he used for such cruel and unusual punishment. He stood back a few steps and looked at his handy work impressed but not quite satisfied. He would do more work but it would have to wait until the next night if Jack was still alive. The taste of Jack’s blood was on the cigar and it gave Corvus some indication about why he might have been following him. The blood had traces of vampire blood in it, this much he could tell. The little bastard was a ghoul ... but who was he working for? There had not been any other vampires in the area that he knew about, much less any that wanted to screw with him. Corvus cleaned his hands and his cutter off as he approached the near broken man. He grabbed a hold of his chin and held it up to force him to look into his eyes to give him one more chance to concede the information that he wanted. “A dirty little ghoul eh ... you should just tell me who sent you to do this. I’m not going to be any nicer to you from here on out. I’m probably going to torture you much worse before I finally decide to end your life. All I want for you to do is shake your head yes or no when I ask you this next question. Do you understand what I am saying to you?” Jack nodded his head yes as his eyes started to flicker from the amount of pain he was in. He was seconds away from passing out that much could be discerned. “Are you going to tell me who sent you to break into my house” It was obviously another kindred since you knew that I was a vampire ... only a select few people know what is truth and what is not. It’s your last chance for tomorrow will be more painful than today ... ? Jack vehemently shook his head no which made the decision to leave him there until tomorrow where he would continue to torture him unto his death. Corvus slapped him on the cheek and said, “You better not die on me, I want to have more fun with you.” He then grabbed his glass and retired to the upstairs, leaving the now fingerless Jack to suffer in pain until the next session of torment began ... that is if he survived the night without bleeding to death. *** Corvus felt the light hit his skin as his eyes opened up. Most of the time he was used to sleeping through the whole day but something caused him to stir much before it was time for him too. He was never afraid to get up during the day because he had special glass windows all throughout the house in which he could walk freely without being burned by the harmful rays of sunlight. He sat up in bed and looked around, it seemed like something was amiss but he was still a bit foggy from his slumber to make any easy judgments. Maybe he was just thirsty, which he could feel deep down that he could use a bit of nourishment. As he stepped out of the master bedroom he felt something strange, his skin began to burn along with smoke rising from his flesh. His reaction was to jump to his room but it seemed like something was holding him in place to keep him from getting shelter. The flesh began to peel from his body as the agonizing pain coursed through his body. He tried to struggle and fight with all his might but it seemed to be of no use. His knees gave out as they had completely turned to ash, he was now on his stomach reaching out to try and pull himself into a dark space but it was no use. Just as he was about to fully burn up, he began to try and yell but his voice couldn’t hold up ... *** Corvus? voice suddenly came back out of nowhere as he sat up in bed and looked around him. There was no sign of daylight anywhere as his eyes scrambled around to find his digital clock radio. The time on it said it was about 6:13 at night. He rubbed his forehead a little bit realizing he had completely forgotten about his pal Jack in the basement. He slipped some grubby clothes on so that he wouldn’t get anything dirty that he wouldn’t be able to get stains out of. Once downstairs he could tell that Jack was still alive but very quiet, maybe hoping that he would be forgotten about or figured as dead so Corvus might ditch his body and he would live through this ordeal. Unfortunate for Jack that the devious vampire had inhuman hearing; no matter how light Jack tried to breath he could still hear it, save for his heartbeat as well that was going rapidly. “Quit faking, I know you’re still alive so don’t try and screw with me ... I was considering giving you one more chance to talk before I continued what I started last night but I’m not in a very giving mood right now so back to breaking you down I go.” Jack didn’t have much strength but what little he did have left he tried to shimmy to try and break the bonds that kept him but was of no use. Corvus had walked further back in the basement and when he returned brought a bag that had lord knows what in it. He looked at Jack with a smile as he pulled out a long sledge hammer out of the bag and tapped it against his hand. “Jack, meet one of my good friends ... he’s going to assist me in my next act of malice.” Jack started to mutter but still had the ball gag taped inside his mouth to which he could not be heard. As Corvus approached him he reared back and took a swing, the sickening sound of Jack’s kneecap being crushed and his groaning of absolute pain caused what one would consider a rush of adrenaline for the brute that was assaulting him. “Hello ... I am Jack’s shattered kneecaps!” He swung again and decimated the other knee with a grin as he was making a mocking comment towards the popular film, Fight Club. The movie and book were actually one of Corvus? favorites, as he was a sick individual; it only seemed to go hand in hand with his actions. He now spent time looking through his bag of gadgets to see what else he could do to ensure the last moments of Jack’s life were unbearably excruciating. “Here we go ...” the atrocious vampire said as he found inspiration not in any tool in the bag, but a katana that was mounted on a rack behind him. It had been given to him by master swordsmith, Muramasa, who deemed him worthy after seeing his ferocity during one of his first visits to Japan. The Japanese have always believed that with Muramasa’s works were cursed and that unless blood was shed by the blade after it had been unsheathed that it would not return into it’s covering. This suited him fine as he never used it unless he was proving a “point”. The beautiful piece slid easily out of the Saya (scabbard) as he brought the sword upward, holding it there for several seconds before descending the blade; slicing through the skin of Jack’s shoulder blade. The arm detached cleanly as Corvus definitely knew how to use a sword. Jack began to breathe heavily through his nose as the right side of his body sags a little from the separation. The vampire enjoyed himself so much that he pulled the sword back again and liberated Jack’s other arm from his body as if he were carving a piece of roast beef at Old Country Buffet. Jack’s now armless torso began to lean backward as there was nothing to hold him up as his feet were only shackled now. “Jack ... no slacking going on here my friend. I’m not finished yet, because I’m sure that whoever sent you here to screw with me is really feeling this now being that you’re connected to them you lousy sack of shit!” Corvus grabs Jack by the hair and forces him to look at him, tears steaming from his eyes as his mind begins to fade from the pain coursing through his body. There was not really much more Corvus could do to the man that would make any difference. He’d cut off the fingers on his right hand, smashed his kneecaps and cut both of his arms off. For a brief moment, Corvus thought that the Assamite Clan might have sent the little turd, but it was obvious that they were a bit more controlled and stealthy than this guy had been. They would have been able to do more than break into his house that was for sure. There was a cloth nearby which he grabbed and used to clean off the blade before re-inserting it into the Saya; then returned it back to the wall mount. Jack was pretty much done for but Corvus wanted to put some finality into the man’s death. He rummaged through the bag and found the instrument he was looking for: a power drill. “I think I’m going to give you a chance to say one last thing before I make this your ending.” Corvus yanked off the tape and pulled the ball gag out of Jack’s mouth, he frantically gasped for air as he hadn’t used his lungs in their full capacity for nearly two days. “Do you have anything to say ... like who sent you” I figure if you told me what I wanted to know then you might actually die without a guilty conscience. Maybe then your god will accept you into the pearly gates if that is where you’re headed.” “You will be found ... and you will be dealt with. You’ll die just as painfully as I will you demented son of a bitch.” Jack couldn’t say much more but tried his best to attempt spitting at Corvus but failed. His body was too badly damaged for him to really hang on much longer. “We’ll see about that, Jack, we will definitely see about that ... ? He plugged it in to be positive that it was going to work before approaching Jack, who was bleeding profusely to his death as it was. Corvus attached a long drill bit to the end of the machine and grinned as he put on some goggles. The drill was then pressed against his forehead where the devious demon pressed the trigger, boring the bit into Jack’s skull. His screams were like a symphony to the undead king of GW. Blood, pieces of flesh, and whatever you could possibly think of sprayed Corvus as he continued to push it through until finally it came out the back end. Corvus smiled as he pulled off the goggles, admiring his handy work but he knew that killing him was just the beginning. He couldn’t just leave the body there with a nosey cop snooping around his place. A thought came to him as he looked at the lifeless body that was broken in front of him, legs still chained up; his bloody, severed arms hanging from the shackles above. For the next hour or so, Corvus hacked up the body into as little pieces he could and stuffed them along with the remains of his clothes, wallet, anything else associated with Jack into a duffle bag. When he finished, he did his best to clean everything up and make it as clean as could be ... (as best as it was to start with being that it looks like a dungeon down there) His next agenda was to get a shower before he took care of the duffle bag, there was no point in walking around with blood and brain matter all over, it would scream suspicion obviously. He left the bag in the basement until he finished cleaning up because who knows if anyone would or could walk into his house. *** The parking lot of the Global Arena was sparse, save for the few cars which were probably belonging to the security staff and janitors. It would be highly unlikely that any of the wrestlers would be hanging around there at this time of night. They were probably making travel arrangements to get to Hawaii or were already there for the next Evolution that was going to take place there. One of the concessions Corvus made before signing with Global was that he would be able to have access to the building when he needed to, even if it were late at night. He walked up to the doors and showed his identification card to the security who were familiar with him already as he went in a few times before to work on some things for the audio/video department. This guy has his hand in everything from wrestling, to music, to video authoring ... you name it, he can do it. Corvus moved quickly through the building making sure that he wasn’t being followed around. He found the room that lead downstairs to the boiler room and found the furnace. He opened up the door and looked at the bag for a second with a big grin. “See ya around, Jack ... wait maybe not.” He shoved the bag in there and closed the door back up and let the flames do their job of burning the evidence of our dear departed Jack. Corvus walked around for a bit to not make it seem like something was up. He even went to one of the green rooms to have a clove to kill a little more time. Corvus strolled back through the building and made his way out and the guard says goodnight to him but then stops him for a second out of curiosity. “Hey, didn’t you come in with a bag” I remember seeing you with it when you arrived.” “I put it in my dressing room; I already have the bag that I’m using for Evolution and Clash of the Titans.” “Ahh, gotcha ...” said the guard as Corvus headed to his car. One the way there his cell was making noises to alert him that he had a incoming text message. He walked to the car and got in and sat there, pulling out his phone. The text message read: I hope you enjoyed your little dream of getting a permanent sun tan ... that is only the beginning. Check your email ... you won’t want to miss it. Corvus turned on his car the fastest way he could and peeled out of the parking lot. He did not live too far from the Global Arena but with as cryptic of a message as he received, he didn’t want to waste any time trying to analyze anything. Whoever it was knew about the dream he had, which was enough for him to be suspicious and maybe even a tad worried. He arrived back at the manor and left the car in the driveway, using the front door this time instead of the garage like he usually does. The first thing he did was head towards his room and picked up his laptop computer and turned it on. It booted up and he accessed his email program and waited for all of the mail from the last day or so to download. A message that appeared to be from himself is one of the postings in the list. He examined it as the topic declares the content of the message is “Information You Should Know”. Typically that is something that would be a virus or spam of some kind but he did a little checking and he didn’t know how, but it was actually sent from his own computer. He opened it up and read ... Corvus, As you can realize with the text message I sent you, I am very serious in my intentions. To cut to the quick, I have your wife and if you do not meet with me within the next few hours ... you will be a widow. Meet me at the Spring Center Warehouses by 2 am or else; it’s not a warning, it is a promise! Sincerely, An old friend He reached into his pocket and dialed a number ... it was to the WMW offices. This wasn’t a normal thing he would be doing as everyone knows that Corvus has a long standing rivalry with them. It wasn’t a matter to be joking about so he had to know a few things before he took the email seriously. Thankfully he knew a few people that liked to work late that wouldn’t tell him to piss off when he called looking for information. The other end of the line picked up and he did not hesitate for a second with what he needed. “Hey, can you tell me where Shadow is headed to” I need to know if she was coming anywhere near Las Vegas. She hasn’t called ... I haven’t heard from her.” He listened as he was told that she was no longer actively employed in Wrestling Midwest; it made his jaw drop nearly to the floor as his source told him the details of what went on. “Thanks for your help; I owe you one ... eventually.” Corvus looked over at his clock as he closed the phone and saw that it was almost one thirty. He muttered expletives under his breath and quickly walked through the house to the garage and pulled off the dust cover on his motorcycle. It was the quickest thing to get him where he needed to be in a short amount of time. He got onto the “46 jet black Indian Chief Roadster and hit the button for the garage to lift the door. As soon as it was of height for him to clear, he sped out and through the gate which he had opened at the same time as the garage door with the remote he had inside. *** Corvus made good time getting to the warehouse. He stopped his bike and slipped the keys into his leather coat as he started to walk around. The sky began to pour as if on cue and it was a drenching kind of rain as well. It didn’t take long to soak Corvus” hair amongst other things. Rain never bothered him so much as it seemed to calm him down some as he continued to investigate. He couldn’t see or hear anything, nor did he pick up his wife’s scent in the area. However, something odd struck him and that was a scent he did recognize. It was not something he had encountered for quite a long time but he did remember it. He shook it off not really paying any attention to it. The rain had reminded him of a time not too long ago before he and Lia had been fighting. She had come to live in his estate in Ohio when he was still a major part of Wrestling Midwest. They had gone to take a walk around and discussed some issues that needed to be dealt with; one of them was their daughter. All that was over now as he knew that he couldn’t go back even if he wanted to, there was just no way. In the end they had realized that despite the fact that in ways they were miles apart in the vampire realm, they still cared for and loved one another as they did in the mortal realm. He could never forget how passionate their kiss was as they stood outside the house in the pouring rain. It was something that stayed etched in his mind. It was one of those reasons why he did not hesitate to follow where the email led him, unarmed and unprepared for what may be awaiting him. He could not risk losing the one thing that kept him level in the world. He had begun to give up after not finding anything he was looking for. There was no sign of anyone or anything that he could persuade himself to think that the email was valid. It could have been one of the things Jack did when he broke in without disturbing the slumber he was in. Corvus was about to leave when the scent he had picked up earlier became more prevalent in the area. He spun around but saw nothing. He was being played just as he had done to countless victims of his own over the course of a millennia. His anger began to rise as he clenched his fists and called out to his stalker. ?ENOUGH GAMES ... show yourself!” Something deep inside him now really did not want that to happen; it felt like something within had triggered as he knew what was coming closer but wanted to deny. Then he heard a voice which belonged to a woman who was behind him about twenty feet from him. “Hello Aric ... ? Corvus froze as the voice resounded in his ears and a thousand years and change suddenly smacked him in the very fiber of his immortality. He couldn’t turn around; he didn’t want to because that would be admitting that what he heard was real, which was something he did not want to do. He closed his eyes; his voice filled with a haunted tone as he spoke in response. “It’s been a long time ... Celine.” TO BE CONTINUED ...
