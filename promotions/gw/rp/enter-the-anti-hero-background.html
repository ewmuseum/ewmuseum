Enter The Anti-Hero (Background)

The light is dim. There is movement all around me. I stare, but I still can't see. They are like ghosts, phantoms moving in the cloak of the night. Like soft silhouettes they dance through the darkened streets. I can only hope to myself that I don't look as dark and as shadowed, but as much as I hope, that is all that it is’hope. Hope? because I know that even if not on the outside, inside, I am even darker. The bell tower of the large church has a clock on its front. In perfect time and harmony the bells begin to toll the hour of nine o'clock. Christian walks down the trashy downtown core, dimly lit stands are beginning to be erected as he strolls through a midnight bazaar. He looks up at the church in disgust. His face contorts underneath the shadow of his hood. The stone gargoyles sit on guard, eternally perched watching the city in their stone prisons. The gothic statues stare back at him, leaving him with an uneasy feeling in the pit of his stomach. Christian turns his gaze from the limestone statuettes and turns his cold stare to the ground. His eyes dart back and forth as he watches his feet continue the simplest of motions as he quickens his pace. I am more uneasy than usual, jumpy even. Even the church's eerie presence frightens me. But, a lot has frightened me lately, many things have run through this troubled mind of mine. It seems so hopeless now, all of it, and it's turning me into a monster. I am a ticking time bomb, just waiting to go off? just waiting for someone to push me over the edge? Christian continues his path down the street. He quickly crosses the dark street. It was a rough area of the town and the last thing we wanted was to get in a physical confrontation. He had enough on his plate right now; he didn't need anything to add to the list. His block cotton hoodie emblazoned with the logo of the DWO blows in the cold caress of the April wind. I look at the logo on my shirt as I pull it closer to my body, trying to provide myself with some semblance of warmth. Dominant Wrestling Organization? my home, my domain. Over there, I am a known name, I am the big dog, I am the World Champion. Now, I can't help to look at myself in PTC's Cruiserweight Tournament. I am not the big fish, I'm the small fish in the world's biggest fucking pond. But, I am now in a place that I have grown so accustomed to in my life, I am the underdog. It had only been a few days since his Round 1 victory over Peter Vetra of PRIME; a strong competitor in his own right, but Christian had long since forgotten that win. He was looking into the future, the same way he always did, the same way that he chose to live his life. No fear for consequence, no looking back with regret, only looking forward, living one day at a time. Christian begins to slow his pace, he was nearing his destination. He has never been to this spot before, but it feels so familiar to him. The scene, the situation, the speech, it was all routine. The moon casts a silver gleam across the sky, illuminating the right side of the dark alley. Christian notices the light as it gleams off of his shoulder, he takes a few steps back, retreating into the darkness. The wait is always the worst part. I check my watch, just about 9:15, I'm running right on time. Of course though, he's not. That's the problem with drug dealers these days, they just don't know about customer satisfaction. It's all part of the business though, no matter how late they are, you'll still pay full price happily for their product, and walk away just like any other satisfied customer. Christian looks around, he sees that no other figure is entering the alleyway. He plops himself down quietly on top of a few crates placed behind a warehouse door. He buries his hands into the front of his hoodie, once again pulling the sweater close to his body. He takes one hand out, reaching into his pocket he feels the soft brush of twenty dollar bills against his trembling hand. He smirks as he pulls out the neatly assembled gangster roll and puts it in the front pocket of his hoodie. I begin to twiddle my thumbs, I'm beginning to get anxious. This is why I say the wait is the worst. The paranoia comes first, what if it's a setup? What if I get caught? Then the worst part comes next? the guilt. Do I really need this? Is it right for me to do this? I've asked myself those questions so many times in my life, I couldn't count. But, it's only recently that I have discovered the answer. Yes, yes I do. It's taken a long while, but I realize it now. I do need it, I need it to survive, to get through. The cold, terrible fact is that I am indeed a junkie. There always has been a fix of the day for me, and I've always been the junkie who needed that fix of the day to get by. It's been like that? always, ever since the death of my parents. Lost and confused I had run away then, longing, searching, needing, just that one little something to get me through the next day. That one little something to take my pain away. First it was the drugs; the hard drugs that I found so easily and readily on the cold Toronto streets, then it was the adrenaline; the adrenaline coming from the crimes I committed on those same streets, for those same drugs, then I turned another direction. Not for the better, no not at all. I am no reformed man, I am a man of weakness and submission. I moved onto my next fix, pain and violence; the pain and violence that could only be found in hand to hand combat. And, now after all of these years? look where these fixes have gotten me, caught up in a mix of both those worlds, waiting for my drug dealer in a cold dark alleyway. Crazy how things come into light when you sit by yourself. Its crazy how one innocent little kid could become so messed up. But that's the problem with society nowadays. There isn't any hope, not for you, not for me, not for anybody. Our society is doomed for eternity to be hopeless. Christian hears a noise slightly in the distance. He sees a small shadow beginning to stir at the end of the alleyway, he quickly ducks down, hiding his position. He didn't know whether this was the dealer or a cop, you could never be too careful. Soon he heard the whisper. 'Christian, you here?' Ah? a sound for sore ears. The easy, stress-relieving voice of the neighborhood drug dealer. I sit up and begin to emerge from the shadows. I step over the small crates and make my way towards the man. A man, whose slender face and thick jaw line are all too familiar to me. A man whose shifty brown eyes and twitchy nose have been away from me for too long, a man who possesses the single greatest and most exploiting job in the world today. The local drug dealer, Andy, the one man who feeds off the pain and suffering of others, only to turn a profit and get by. But, I do not hate him for it, in fact I love and respect him for that. For I too have taken the same path. Wallowing in the glory of another man's pain. Watching with a sick satisfaction as the blood of an opponent was spilled. Watching with horrid temperament as the life seems to drain from another's motionless body. I had taken up fighting as a mean's to escape, it was no longer a way to escape, it was the thing I was trying to escape from. - - - - - - - - - - Christian quickly rushes down the street. He has what he needs, and he now just wants to get as far away as possible. There is a sick feeling in the pit of his stomach, a feeling of self-disgust, but is fine with him. He needs no self-respect, nor righteousness. He needs only to get by, only that fix to get going through another day. Christian thinks back to the events that took place only ten minutes ago, as he nears the front door to the hotel that he is staying in. He quickly rushes inside, his hand cupped firmly around the bottle of pills in his pocket. He rushes past the doormen and hotel managers. He has only one thought on his mind the fix. The easy way out. Christian brushes shoulders with several annoyed bystanders as he pushes his way into the lobby elevator. As the elevator begins to ascend, his anxiousness only grows. Inside his pockets, his fingers caress the bottle of capsules. This elevator couldn’t seem any longer, or seem to be going any more slowly. All I want, all I need is to get up into that room, and down a couple and I’ll be set for a while. I can feel my hands starting to tremble, I feel like Ray Charles on a heroine binge, I just need it. Oh shit! The doors have opened, it’s my floor. I rush outside into the hallway, letting go of the bottle of Ecstasy pills for only a second to grab the keycard to my hotel room. Christian fumbles around in his pocket, searching for his key card. In almost no time it all, he grabs a hold of the card, and pulls it out, nearly pulling out the bottle of pills with it. He blushes slightly at his own stupidity, but it didn’t matter to him. He wanted this high so badly he could taste it right now. He stepped to the front of his small, oak door. Here it is? room number 307. My home for the past few weeks, I’ve been staying here ever since I entered Cruiserweight 2. It has been my home away from home, my hideaway. My new place to run to. It’s funny how that works, when I was young I ran to the streets to seek salvation in drugs, now I’ve found it in the convenience and comfort of Best Western’s Room #307. Christian slams the door shut behind him, with a loud thud the door clicks into locked. Christian looks back, the door made a louder noise then he thought it would. He contemplates holding back on his drug trip for a few minutes, to see if anyone would investigate the noise. Fuck it! I can’t wait any more! I’ll be done by the time anyone from downstairs at the front desk gets here anyways, I’ll send them away quickly. It’s escape time? Christian throws his hooded sweatshirt at the bed, it falls short of its mark and lands in a crumpled mess on the floor. It doesn’t matter to him, he holds in his hand the only thing he needs. He cracks the seal on the bottle for the second time, he had already inspected the goods, the last thing he wanted was another shitty trip like the last one he had. Christian smirks as he sticks his fingers into the bottle, he walks to the bathroom and starts running the tap water. He closes the pill capsule and returns it to his pocket, before taking a complimentary glass and filling it near the brim with water. He pops the two pills in his mouth, carefully, then with a flick of his neck swallows and washes them down with a small gulp of water. He dumps the rest of the water out and returns to his bedroom. He plops himself down carelessly on the soft spring mattress. He reaches for the converter and clicks on the television monitor, he doesn’t bother to flick stations, he merely lays his head back and closes his eyes as the wave begins to come over him. The sensation of floating uplifts him from his bed, although physically he never leaves. With his eyelids weighing tones, he begins to relax and fade in and out of consciousness. At least these are better than the last ones he gave me. It’s the first thing that pops into my head, after that, the high sensation is just too much. My mind begins to race, the darkness flowing from my body. I feel as if I’m watching myself, instead of being myself. I close my eyes and I begin to think, the initial wave is starting to wear off. Now, I can do what I do best, contemplate. There is a lot on my plate, and the only way I could ever have prepared to tackle this mess was with a clear mind. A smile forms across my face as I lay there, deep in thought, pondering. Christian’s eyes remain closed, his face begins to change. He goes from the cold-hearted, stone-faced masochist, to a wise man, deep in contemplation. The smile begins to slip from his face, his eyes flutter softly, inside the gears are in motion. The clockworks are moving Christian Darke is preparing for his next battle. One week ago, I beat a man that many thought I could not defeat. I went in the underdog, and I emerged victorious. Peter Vetra was only the first man to feel the true pain and rage that only I can deliver. But, it is not about Peter Vetra anymore. No, now there stands a new man, a new threat, a new favorite to overcome. I got by the first, now comes the second round. El Rayu Azul II. A delusional man, a man more hopeless than myself. A man, who lives by the strict moral principles of hope. But, truly what is hope now. Hope is nothing in this modern society. Hope is a forsaken word in these modern streets. A word with no true meaning. A man cannot carry hope, a man who fights with only hope is destined to lose. For in this new world, of greed, corruption and hatred, hope is nothing. Hope is a word, spat on by the masses. Azul? you are a hero to many. The mask you wear is a sign of hope, the ability to rise above, but it is still a mask. A place to hide, and if there is anyone who knows about hiding it is myself. You are a hero to the modern world. Conveying hope and encouraging the poor to rise above? but I am the anti-hero. A man corrupted by violence, pain, loss, drugs and hatred. A man who conveys the message of hatred and failure, not hope and success. The poor will never rise again Azul? they cannot. The poor, the hungry, the tired, they are all weak. They are blemishes on this world, blemishes’like me. But, I have come to terms with that, the question is can you. For Azul, your downfall will be the proof of my words, the results of my prophecy. The poor are crippled, and soon you shall be too. The light of hope still shines, but its rays will not last forever. El Rayu Azul II, is like a candle, flickering in the rain. Holding onto its last strands of life. The blue mask of hope will soon crumble, rip and tear into a crimson mask of failure? Christian sits up slightly. His body still felt relaxed and numb, it would for a while. There he lays, the anti-hero for the modern world. Soon enough, a great battle would be waged, the Hero versus the Anti-hero, the classic story book tale of good versus evil. This story would not have a fairy tale ending however?
