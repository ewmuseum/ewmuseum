Any Publicity is Good Publicity

2 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/2/06 “Hello everyone,” the loud, obnoxious store manager bellowed from the stand at the centre of attention. His voice cut through Blind Rage like a chainsaw through butter, making wonder for the hundredth time that minute precisely why in hell they were at this new WalMart opening in the first place. “And welcome to our grand opening!” Blind Rage sighed and nudged Warrior with his elbow. Warrior almost fell over, but steadied himself quickly and angrily glared Rage. “What in the hell’s wrong with you?” “Why the hell are we here, man?” Blind Rage questioned. “This is seriously gay.” “Just grin and bear it, Rage,” Warrior told him. “This is just a publicity stunt, nothing more.” “But why?” Blind Rage responded, desperately trying to keep his voice down. He failed miserably. “We already get enough exposure fighting in Global! And we should be preparing for our next match instead of wasting time here!” “Rage, we can’t get enough exposure!” Warrior declared. “The more we get, the more work we get.” “And the more pirates we destroy!” Blind Rage added, an almost sadistic grin crawling across his face. “Exactly,” Warrior declared. “So let’s just get this over and done with, then we can go home and train.” Blind Rage sighed and silently nodded in agreement. Then the store manager piped up again. “Please put your hands together for our special guests for the opening, the superhero duo and Global superstars Justice Pink!” Blind Rage glared at Warrior angrily. “Justice Pink?” Warrior shrugged and he led the way onto the stage, as the crowd greeted them with a mediocre response. Warrior grabbed the mic off the store manager then under his breath muttered “That’s Justice Inc, moron!” “What?” The store manager questioned, obviously confused. “Never mind,” Warrior whispered, ushering him off the stage. “Whatever!” The store manager replied, carefully making his way down the steps to join the general public. “Yes, ladies and gentlemen we are your local superheroes...Justice Incorporated!” Warrior proudly declared. There was another below average response from the crowd to this and few of the onlookers at the very back began wander off to get back to their daily routines. A couple mumbled something about expecting some kind of hot girl band. Nevertheless, Warrior continued undeterred. “And proud to be here today, to open up this fine...erm...” “Walmart!” The store manager quickly shouted out. “...uh...yeah, Walmart store today,” Warrior said, turning to glare at Blind Rage, who already had the large novelty scissors, hovering above the elongated red tape. “Rage...” With that Rage cut the red tape and waited for the cheers to envelope him...but they didn’t. Blind Rage just grunted something offensive under his breath and allowed the novelty scissors to simply drop out of his hands. Then, someone in the crowd shouted. “Do something!” Both Blind Rage and Warrior glared at each other wondering just what in the hell the person wanted. Then another added. “Pick up a car or something!” Someone else. “Throw a bike over the store!” With that the store manager’s eyes widened and he stormed onto the stage, taking the mic out of Warrior’s hand in one smooth movement. “There’ll be none of that! We’ve only just built the damn place, I don’t want one these clowns throwing bike through the window!” “Clowns?” Warrior repeated. “Hey,” Blind Rage began, holding his arms out. “I could through a bike over that damned building!” The store managed rolled his eyes. “Yeah, sure you can...” “Clowns?” Warrior said again, getting closer to the store manager. “Hey, instead of bikes,” Blind Rage began, also edging closer to the store manager, “how’s about I throw you over that damn building!” The store manager developed a terrified expression as the two surround him on the stage and now “FIGHT!” chants had begun to circulate through the crowd. The store manager held his hands up in front of him defensively. “Hey now, there’s not need to get hot about this...” “Clowns?” Warrior repeated for a third time. Blind Rage shot him an angry glance. “Will you stop repeating that! He said it, okay” Let’s kick his ass and get the hell outta here!” “I didn’t mean it in a bad way!” The store manager pleaded. “I like clowns!” “Hey,” someone in the crowd shouted. “Shoot him with your heat vision or something, man!” Blind Rage and Warrior turned around to see the guy in crowd wearing a blue bandana of some kind. Warrior’s eyes widen immediately upon seeing in and jumps down off the stage and charges towards the man, throwing innocent bystanders out of the way as he went. “What the hell are you doin”!” Blind Rage called after him. Warrior didn’t turn around to answer him, he simply continued toward his target with unwavering focus. “Pirate! He’s a pirate!” “What?” Blind Rage asked, glaring at the supposed pirate confused. Warrior finally reached his target and glared at him suspiciously. “What do you want...pirate?” The man with the blue bandana simply glared at him, confused. “I’m not a pirate?” “No?” Warrior questioned. “You have a bandana!” “So?” the man replied. “A lot of people wear ban...” Before the man could finish his sentence, Warrior hurled a fist at him, hitting his jaw sweetly, knocking him out cold instantly. “No more lies, pirate!” Warrior bellowed, giving the guy the boot whilst he was down. Blind Rage shook his head and quickly made for his tag team partner. He had caught him with three large strides and pulled him back with one of his massive arms with the greatest of ease. “Dude,” Rage said. “Calm down!” Warrior still struggled, uselessly, in his arms, desperately attempting to get at the down man. “He’s a pirate! I’ll kill him!” Blind Rage sighed and hauled Warrior over his shoulder. “I’m sorry about all of this! He has, um, pirate problems!” The man in blue bandana regained consciousness and quickly jumped up to his feet, his left arm cradling his ribs. “I got my ass kicked by a superhero! Yeah!” Blind Rage shook his head and made his way over to the Justice Inc mobile (the orange VW camper van), with Warrior still struggling in his arms. “But he’s a pirate, Rage!” Warrior declared. “We have to destroy him!” Blind Rage opened the side door, at the back of the camper van and hurled his tag partner in the back, not particularly caring whether he was hurt or not. “Man,” Blind Rage began, climbing into the driver seat of the van. “We got publicity alright, just the Russell Crowe kind rather than the Superman kind...”
