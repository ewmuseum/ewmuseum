Dividing Two Passions #8: The Visitor

17 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 17th 2004 KNOCK! KNOCK! Zergolov opens his eyes. Did I hear something? He questions in his mind as he lies under the warm covers of his bed. He rolls over unsure of any noise or anything going. KNOCK! KNOCK! He rolls back over to face the door of his bedroom. Yeah, there was definitely noise that time. He strained his eyes upwards to look at his alarm clock. Seven-Thirty! Who was knocking the front door at seven-thirty in the morning? Are they mad? Today is a big day! The video is being filmed today. Zergolov didn’t need bags under his eyes for the video. Zergolov sat up in the bed and rubbed his sleepy eyes. KNOCK! KNOCK! Why hadn’t Igor answered the door? He was closer; surely he would have got up? Probably still hung-over thought Zergolov as he walked towards the apartment door in only a black thong? yeah Josh, you wish? in a pair of black boxers. KNOCK! KNOCK! Zergolov quickly paced across the living room area from his bedroom and opened the front door. “What sort of fucking?”” begin Zergolov, but harsh question soon trailed off. He stood in silence and looked. “What?” came the sweet reply from a female at the door. Zergolov suddenly became aware of what he wasn’t wearing. “Come in!” he muttered like an embarrassed school girl and quickly fled for the safety of the bedroom. Zergolov quickly disappears in the bedroom, his face quickly going a bright shade of red. The apartment stands empty. A foot slowly steps inside, then leg and eventually a whole person is now in the apartment. It is a female too. It’s Anna Arefiev, Igor’s younger sister. She tentatively looks around the whole apartment with nervous eyes. She is wearing a pair of cream trousers and a black low-cut top. She clears her throat with a delicate cough and continues to walk around the plush studio planned apartment. “How come you’re here?” shouts Constantino Zergolov through his bedroom door. “Igor invited me,” she replies. Her soft, gentle voice still sounds gracious even while shouting. “He did?” shrugs Zergolov on Anna’s reply. “What was that?” she asks, not fully hearing what he had said. “Nothing!” bellows Constantino. Anna shrugs and sits at the coffee table reading a magazine. Suddenly Igor Arefiev stumbles through the doors. He is wearing blue faded jeans and a yellow t-shirt. He is wearing a black jacket as well. Arefiev is covered in dirt and cuts on his face and his clothes aren’t the cleanest either. Arefiev groans as he stumbles against the door and moves into the living area. “What happened?” asks Anna as she rushes to her brother’s side. Water begins to gather in her eyes. Arefiev groans, as he holds his ribs. With his spare hand he reaches out towards his bedroom door. “Bed?” he whispers. Anna nods and helps her brother through the door and into the bedroom. The apartment is empty once again. Zergolov re-appears from his room wearing a blank black tracksuit. Where did Anna go? He questions in his mind as he looks around. The door is still open. Zergolov starts out towards the room when he hears a loud thud in Arefiev’s room. His head snaps in the direction of the room almost immediately. It’s probably Anna he shrugs, but decides to investigate anyway. Constantino crosses the room quietly and patiently, step-by-step, unsure of what he’ll see. “Anna, you alright?” he questions. The door is ajar so he doesn’t bother to raise his voice. Zergolov steps closer. “No?” she pants with real stress in her voice, “could you give me a hand with this?” “Sure?” he smiles. I knew she wanted me all along, his smile increases. Zergolov stands back into an upright position. Previously he was creeping at a slant. Confidently and with a little strut he bounds towards his best friend’s room and smugly pushes open the door. “What seems to be the problem pretty lady?” questions Zergolov with a smooth sound to his voice. He looks into the room and at the bed, lovingly. Anna looks up the bed and shrugs. “Do you ever stop messing?” she sighs. Zergolov stops. She didn’t want his body. Lying on the bed is Igor wearing just his faded blue jeans. More cuts and bruises are apparent on his body. “What the fuck?”” panics Zergolov. He rushes to the opposite of the bed and begins to totally freak. “Who did this” What happened? Who were they? I’ll kill the bastards! You just give me there names! I’ll hunt them down?” he screams as his flips in every which way direction. Anna shakes her head. A lot of use you are. “I’m going out!” announces Zergolov in the height of his panic. “What?” questions Anna. “I’m going to find the bastards that did this too him!” Anna shakes her head once more. Always the hero. Zergolov bounds out the bedroom door. SLAM! The closing of the front door echoes throughout the whole building. Anna rolls up the t-shirt she has just taken off her brother and tosses onto a pile of clothes in the corner. At least he’s the same old organised Igor. Anna then moves her hands to his jeans and then looks at his face. Igor peers back, in too much pain to reply. “You’re my brother but I am not going there!” she smiles. “Want a cup of coffee” Or a bowl of soup?” At the second suggestion Arefiev bows his head forward slowly. Anna smiles and leaves the bedroom. As she crosses the apartment she lifts up a piece of red cloth with flames symbolised to be growing up the front. She looks down at Igor’s wrestling mask with some confusion in her face. After a couple of seconds she merely shrugs and walks on into the kitchen.
