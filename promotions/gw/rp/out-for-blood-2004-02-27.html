Out for Blood

27 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/04 The night is still. That's the first thing Harv thinks as he uses his key to snap the deadbolt on the front door of the Drunken Leperechaun shut, locking it for the night. The moon glows bright in the in the sky, only a few, thin clouds floating in the air with it. Perhaps it was the beauty of moon and the stars that made him feel so serene. Of course standing outside in the middle of a huricane could be considered serene compared to what happened not two nights ago. As much as Harv hated to acknowledge it to anyone, he hadn't really gotten a descent night's sleep since...since him. Sociopath. That's who he was. A God damned sociopath. The world's taken a serious nose dive as of late. You try to do a favor for a friend and some crazy bastard you've never met, only heard about secondl-hand, turns up in the middle of the night pointing a fucking gun in your face. He told Donny to turn in that tape when he had a chance. Maybe that would have stopped the whole thing from happening. Perhaps the cast on his left hand where the bat smashed the bone wouldn't be itching so damn much right now if They had just turned this Train character in to Christina McCoy. Maybe his contract would've been terminated and Train would have been out of their hair without incident. Then again maybe he'd be dead. The guy wasn't right. Babling on about how he and McNasty had taken it beyond wrestling. How he wouldn't stand for being punked out. Wrestling was a sport for God's sake. It wasn't supposed to come to this. Some armed thug, jumping you in the middle of the night, threatening to take your life over an 8mm video tape. But Harv gave him the tape though. Because as crazy as the man may have been... Harv looked into those eyes...the cold ones with the 1,000 yard stare and knew. Harv Knew he wasn't bluffing. But again, the night was calm. And for the first time in days, the aching of his hand a steady reminder of the danger that nearly swallowed him a few nights ago, he finally felt safe. Maybe he'd catch up on that sleep he'd been missing. Well, it was closing time and the bar was all locked up, so Harv was certainly going to give it the old college try. The loose gravel crunched under Harv's feet as he walked all the way across the unpaved parking lot to his car. The hint of a light breeze blowing through the trees mixed with the jingling of Harv's keys as he slide the correct one into the door and pops the lock. Harv slides into his car and inces a little as he uses the broken left hand to close the driver's side door. Harv quickly switches keys and slides into the ignition. Just as he's about to turn on the engine a phone rings. Harv goes into his pocket and retrieves his small, black cell phone. He quickly flips the on button and puts it to his ear. Harv- Hello? Voice- Sup dogg? *Pause* Harv- What? Who is this? Voice- Awwww, I'm hurt. You done forgot about me already? And here I am thinking we done bonded and shit. Serenity shatters almost instantly as the reality of who has reached him in the middle of the night, on his personal line as if he were an old friend. As if Harv had not feared for his life the last time he was in the prescence of the voice's owner. The man they called Train had violated his space again. Harv- Son of a.... How the hell did you get this number? Train- Hey... I know some folks, that know some folks that know how to find folks. Damn man, you keep asking questions like that and a brotha's gonna start thinking you're not happy to hear from him. Harv- Look... I didn't tell the cops, ok? If that's what you're wandering. Train- Oh, I know that. If you had we'd be talking at your house. Harv- Then what the hell do you want?! Train- First off, take the bass outta your voice less you looking to get it knocked out. Second... I got a message for you to give McNasty. See guys like your buddy? They easier to read than a damn pop-up book. I know what he's been telling you. What he's been telling himself. Fillin' yo head and his with all this trash about settling the score. Talking about how he's gonna tear me apart and all that bullshit. See cat's like him, they love getting all up in other folks area, but they can't take it when somebody returns the favor. First thing first, you let his ass know he ain't got nobody to blame for the shit that went down the other night, but himself. See he's the little bitch that couldnt' just fight me like a man. Naw, naw... Instead of trying to get his knuckles bloody he wanted to run a street games. Trying to box me in so I can't make a move. Thing is? He ain't got a game to run that I ain't played and perfected! Same goes for you! Don't you muthafuckas know who you dealing with?! Bitch, my name is Train and you can't beat me running street game... Muthafucka I am the streets! It ain't where I'm from, it's who I am! And the streets say when some little punk tries to get all up in yo personal space you get further up in his! He takes a shot with a bee-bee gun, you grab a 12 guage! Every punk can play until you take it farther than they was willing to go. Second thing I want you to tell him? I know what his ass thinks, but he's got it all backwards. See the shit that went down in the bar between you and me? Hell, I ain't gonna lie to you. I been doing that shit for a long time dogg. A long time.... Don't get it twisted. I ain't apologizing for shit. Not to you. Not to anybody else I had to run up on. But see... this shit? This wrestling thing? This was my out son. See way back in the day I didn't never have no problems letting people have it. See they wasn't even really people to me. The was just obstacles. Shit blocking my money. It wasn't nothing putting some punk ass muthafucka in chains and putting his ass on the bottom of the lake. It wasn't nothing to spit a whole clip at somebody and not give a fuck who the hell else might get hit. That was the sreet. Problem is... It ain't like that no more. A brotha gets a little older and he starts to see... See all the shit he's doing. Now don't take me for a bitch. Man's gotta do what a man's gotta do. But see, I was thinking when I got here, I wouldn't have to be doing the same old shit. Like I said, I ain't apologizing for shit...but this ain't the way it was supposed to be. This was supposed to be a damn dream job. Hell, I was supposed to be getting paid for whuppin' muthafuckas asses. Wasn't supposed to be any need for putting the barrel to a muthafuckas head. Oh, but yo boy changed all that. He took me back to place that I went way out on a God damn limb not to have to go back to! He fucked it all up! All because he didn't have the balls to stand straight up on his own two! *Pause* Hahaha... Yeah, he's got it all backwards. See he thinks that he's coming to the pay per view looking for me. He thinks he's the one with the score to settle. Naw muthafucka... You let that son of a bitch know. He ain't the one who's out for blood. He ain't the one looking to roll the cat across the ring out on a stretcher. You let that son of a bitch know that he took me to a place I didn't never want to go, but now that I'm there?! I ain't responsible for what happens. You let him know. He ain't coming for me.... I'm coming for him. Suddenly the air on the other end of the phone goes dead. Harv sits unsettled, wondering what's going to happen to his friend now that he's open the pandora's box as the scene fades to black.
