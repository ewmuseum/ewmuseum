RIP Ray Charles

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/04 It was around 5:00, that Thursday afternoon. Brick heard the TV anchor say, 'Breaking news. America mourns the loss of another icon. Music legend Ray Charles died today of liver failure in his Beverly Hills home. He was 73.' Brick often had fond childhood memories of his dad sitting in the living room, listening to his Ray Charles records, so this announcement had a profound impact on Brick. Brick found himself walking over to his CD collection. The first CD he pulled out was a collection of Charles' greatest hits. He then proceeded to place the CD in the stereo, program a few tracks and press play. The first song to play was 'What I'd Say.'
