Birthday dinner (Reload RP 2)

9 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/9/04 The date: January 9, 2005. On that day, one Jamal James Randall, best known to the world as Brick, turned 31 years old. Because his birthday fell on a Sunday, in fact, the very day before Reload, it was another Sunday dinner at his mother's house. Coretta cooked Brick his favorite meal: lasagna. Everyone was there to celebrate: Aaron, his wife Felicia and their daughter Ashley, Brick’s mother Coretta, his sister Monica and her husband Kyle, and his singing group DMAK. 'Father God, we come to you in the name of your son Jesus Christ with thanksgiving and praise. We thank you for this day and for this time together, where we celebrate my oldest son reaching another birthday. We thank for our health and for our lives. We thank you for the food we are about to recieve and for the hands that prepared it. We ask that you bless this food to build the body, the mind and the soul. In Jesus' name, we pray.' 'In Jesus' name.' 'Amen.' 'Amen.' After blessing the food, everyone began to prepare their plates. 'Speaking of birthdays, Monica, you should be due in a few weeks, right?' 'Yeah,' Monica replied, rubbing her belly. 'Shouldn't be too long, now.' 'So, big match coming up, huh?' asked Felicia. 'Yeah, Donny and I really need to win this match. If we win, we get to face the Tag Team champions for the title. And if we win that match, we'll be the Tag Team champions.' 'You sure there's no other incentive for winning this match?' asked Coretta. 'You mean aside from getting my hands on Salaok? Oh, that's the icing on the cake. I'm just sorry it's not a no holds barred match.' 'Did you ever get GSA back for trying to drown you?' asked Kyle. 'Yeah, a few weeks after, remember?' 'Roght, right,' nodded Kyle, 'when you turned a hose on Zero and beat Wind with it. So, any news on the Luke Cage movie?' 'Aside from the fact that they're polishing the script again before they do any auditions, no. All I know is I'm gonna have to up my game if I'm going up against Tyrese.' 'Tyrese is too scrawny to play Luke Cage,' said Aaron. 'Plus, he's not that great an actor.' 'Maybe not, but he's more known as an actor,' Brick replied. 'I'm really gonna have to give the producers one hell of a screen test.' Minutes later, everyone stood around Brick as the cake came in. The cake had chocolate frosting with “3” and “1” candles in the middle. Written in icing around the candles was “Happy Birthday Jamal”. “OK, somebody get the light,” said Coretta. Kyle reached for a light switch and turned it off as Coretta lit the candles. “OK, everybody. One, two, three.” 'Happy birthday to you Happy birthday to you Happy birthday, dear Jamal Happy birthday to you' Just then, DMAK broke into a rendition of Stevie Wonder’s “Happy Birthday”. Happy birthday to you Happy birthday to you Happy birthday Happy birthday to you Happy birthday to you Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday Happy birthday Then everyone cheered and applauded as Brick blew out the candles. “So, you think you’ll get your wish?” asked Aaron. “Hopefully.'
