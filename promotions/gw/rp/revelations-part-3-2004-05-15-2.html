Revelations Part 3

15 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 15 Ichiro and Jushin sat silently, they were back inside their uncle’s living room, once again. Jushin sat staring off into space, as Ichiro began coming up with some form of story to explain their return. The wait was always long and boring, their uncle seemed to be getting a little bit used to the luxuries of running a Yakuza empire, and they hoped that it was not affecting the way he was running their father’s empire. Ichiro was soon taken away from his thoughts as he heard the soft sound of footsteps coming down the stairs. Soon enough they had to wait no longer. Their uncle stepped into the doorway and smiled when his thoughts were confirmed. His security had told him that Ichiro and Jushin had returned, but they had not called in advance, and was surprised to see them sitting in his living room. He was ecstatic nonetheless as he walked over and hugged the two of them, before sitting down. “My nephews, what business brings this pleasure upon my household?” he said in a soft tone. Jushin stared at Ichiro, awaiting his brother’s explanation. Sure enough, a few seconds later Ichiro finally spoke up. “Uncle we have returned to see how things are going, we wish to help out against these Americans in any way we can, we cannot allow them to take over Tokyo. They have stuck their noses into the wrong place, and they did the wrong thing when they attacked the Nakazuras.” “Alas, it is as I feared. You have returned to check up on me,” said Gohei with a slight frown. “Of course,” started Jushin. “But, why do you think of this as an insult uncle” We come because we care, because we want to make sure you alright. It is a sight for our weary eyes to see you, for you are the only family we have left.” “Your words move me, young nephew,” said Gohei, smiling modestly. “But, alas if it is business you have come here for, then it shall be business which you take care of. What is it that you wish to do” I am sure I can find some outstanding debts or some informants that have been talking about us.” “Uncle, do you think that after such a slap in the face by the Americans last week that we wish to engage in such minor details. We want the Americans, we need to find out more about them, that is the business which we wish to complete here,” said Ichiro. Gohei looked shocked at Ichiro. It was not often that Ichiro had such a strong passion to seek revenge, the only time when Gohei had seen such a rage in his young nephew was the death of Ichiro’s father, of his own brother. But, if the Americans were what the two wanted, then that is what they would get. He definitely needed help dealing with these foreigners; they were increasing their strength, trying to gain a stranglehold in Tokyo. “So be it,” said Gohei. “If that is the mission you wish to take, then such is the path that I shall set the two of you along.” “Thank you uncle,” said Ichiro, bowing his head slightly in thanks to his uncle. “So, if you wish to deal with the Americans, then you must know what has happened in the days since you were last here,” started Gohei. “The Americans are trying to gain a stronger pull in Tokyo, they are trying to take out competition left, right and center. Like lions they act, they find the weakest and the most vulnerable and then strike. Deadly and precisely they strike, killing the weak and each and every time they kill, gaining a bigger stranglehold on Tokyo and a larger share of the underworld here in Tokyo.” “So the Americans no longer lay dormant?” asked Ichiro. “That is correct,” answered Gohei. “Well, that’s just fabulous. Not only are they incredibly good at killing people, but now they want to do it more often. This isn’t looking good, we need to stop this before it gets out of control,” Jushin piped up, finally breaking his silent streak. “There is more,” began Gohei. “This past week, the Americans took another blow at the foundation of the Tokyo underworld. But, it was nothing like the attack on the two of you in the subway. No, indeed this was much more planned, and made much more of an impact.” Gohei paused for a second, the news was definitely close to his heart for some reason. It was hard for him to tell the tale, Ichiro could see it on his face. But, this was not the time for tears, he and Jushin needed to know what was going on, and they needed to know soon. “Go on, uncle,” Ichiro encouraged. “I am very sorry, my nephews,” Gohei started speaking once again. “They have killed a Yakuza boss. An important hit and one which has sent a huge shockwave through our business. It has sent a lot of money in different directions, and it has sent a lot of free-agent, free roaming street warriors out into the streets without purpose.” “Who was the target?” asked Ichiro. “One of our better competitors, one with whom we have had an unspoken alliance with. Not so much an alliance, as an agreement mind you. An agreement to leave one another alone and since our business practices allowed us to stay away from one another, we had not had any violence or bad blood between our empire and his for a long time,” answered Gohei. “Uncle, who was the target”!” demanded Ichiro. “Matsui Li,” answered his uncle. Jushin and Ichiro were both taken back. Matsui Li was one of the oldest bosses still living and working in the Tokyo underground crime syndicate. At the age of nearly seventy it was insane for him to not have retired. But, feeling that his son was not ready to take over, and that he was yet too weak to lead the empire, he stayed in charge. Running things the way they had always been ran, awaiting the time when his son Yoshahiro would be old enough to take over. When Yoshahiro would be ready to take over, mature enough to take over, powerful enough to take over. Now, it appeared that it would never happen, as without his father in charge Yoshahiro had gone into a brief hiding. He was afraid that he was next, but still he had sworn revenge on the Americans for what they had done to his father and to his empire. “What of Yoshahiro?” asked Jushin. “To the public he is also dead, the police have not reported him dead, but they have reported him missing. But, we know better. He was the one who found his father, lying, bleeding on his death bed. Matsui however, did not go down without a fight, he managed to tell his son everything he had heard from the Americans talking when they thought he was dead, and were cleaning up the mess. He also told Yoshahiro that he had only one ally powerful enough to take down the Americans,” answered Gohei. “The Nakazura empire,” Ichiro finished the sentence for his uncle. Gohei nodded his acknowledgement. “He called us once this week, he informed us that he would soon need to meet with someone. But, he insisted that it be only you. We did not want to disturb you in America, so we tried to reason with him. However, he demanded the two of you. We lost contact with him, but he did leave a voice mailbox, in which you can contact him.” “Good, let’s have it,” said Ichiro as he stood up. Gohei stood up and handed Ichiro a slip of paper with a number written on it. Ichiro looked down at the paper and smiled. “Well, I have a few things which I must deal with today, so I must leave you until later on tonight,” said Gohei. “I will be going out for dinner at nine o’clock this evening, at the Samuratarian Club. Meet me there, and we will discuss what you have learned. I will have one of the security personnel get you some transportation from the garage. Jushin, I assume you know how to drive standard?” Jushin only nodded his acknowledgment. Of course he could drive standard; the Mitsubishi they were driving last week was standard. Gohei walked out of the room, and did not look back. When Jushin was sure that his uncle was out of ear-range, he stood up and walked towards Ichiro. Jushin pulled out his cell phone and placed it into his brother’s hand. “Well brother, is all this overwhelming news worth a small tattoo?” said Jushin. Ichiro took the phone and began to dial the number. He looked towards his brother and smiled when he saw the huge smirk on Jushin’s face. He held the phone up to his ear, as he heard the familiar sound of the dial tone. “Of course it is brother, it will all be worth it!”
