Answer To All Your Questions

24 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 24th, 2004 Its time to face your fears. Months have passed since he has. The truth plays hide and seek with your mind. Find it and you shall survive. Deny it, and you shall be sacrificed. Rumors are spoken in a language you’re afraid to understand. Passed around like the proverbial cigarette you smoke. You smoked yourself dry. “Things are about to change”, so they say? Hundreds of voices of men, women, and children echo??Things are about to change.” You’re afraid to admit it. You clinch whatever you possess. You hold dear to whatever you love. Hoping it won’t be taken away. Hoping you won’t be taken away. Greed finds you guilty. If you could, you’d hide. But you know deep in your mind that its no use. There’s no use in hiding because he’ll only find you. He seeks? REVENGE “he seeks something. He has returned. He washed himself in the blood and has returned to this mortal world. You only wish you could send him back to’to wherever he came from. But you can’t. Instead, you swallow that gulp of heartache that just burst down your throat. It thuds at the pit of your stomach. Your palms begin to sweat. You hiccup every chance you get. You know deep down in your heart that things are about to change. But again, you don’t want to admit it’do you” Thousands of miles from everything and everyone is where we are. Nothing seems familiar at all. YOU ARE SCARED You sit and wait. Something is bound to happen. You know this, at least you think you know this. You start to feel confident, but it has no use. This isn’t what you are used to. This isn’t your world. You are the guest at this banquet of fear. You look forward, waving your hand in front of your face. You push the smoke away. Taking a deep breath, you think its going to smell like hamburgers? You’re fucking pathetic. The smoke splits apart. PARTING THE SEA OF SMOKE IF CHRIST CAN DO IT WHY CAN’T HE? Time allows the smoke to fade. Your heartbeat starts to slow down. You see a field of corn in the middle of the night. Your heartbeat starts to beat faster once again. You start to remember movies and films about what happens in these cornfields. You remember Children of the Corn Part 1-32. You remember Joyride. You remember aliens drawing patterns into the fields. Now, you should realize how fucking gullible you are. It’s just a fucking movie. Nothing ever happens in corn fields’or at least you hope not. Again, you swallow that gulp of heartache and wait for something to happen. You begin to hear a rustling through the corn field. You wait for Freddy Krueger to jump out and stab you. You wait for a man with a sickle to come out to hack at your throat. You wonder if you walk into the field, you’ll go back to the days you used to play baseball. You are a fucking joke. Combined with the sound of the wind blowing, the noise gets louder and louder. It’s getting closer and closer to you. You begin to shake inside your boots. You begin to make me fucking sick. You see the stalks of corn start to sway side to side, whatever the hell it is’its getting closer. You take a step back. You actually think that taking one fucking step back is going to stop a serial killer with a chainsaw from cutting you into pieces? You sicken the fuck out of me. The swaying of the stalks gets a couple feet from the front. Seconds pass’you hold your breath. You try not to make a noise. You start to run out of oxygen. Your eyes begin to water. You open your mouth for a breath. You inhale the loudest you ever have. Your heart sinks. You gives mortals such a bad fucking name. Something grabs the back of your head and pushes you onto the ground. You turn your head around. Hoping to catch who is doing this to you. Maybe you’ll whip out your cell phone and explain to your fucking grandmother that you are about to die. Or you’ll take out your digital camera and take a picture to post on the internet. Or maybe you’ll just have a really awesome story to tell your friends back at home. Who never will believe you anyways. You’re so fucking stupid. You are sitting on your ass’looking straight up at him. Things are about to change. He takes a step closer. The motherfucker is wearing black jeans and a black hooded sweatshirt. He has the hood on the sweatshirt over his head. Why? Because its fucking scarier. It’s the only thing you can think of. You only wish you could do something to him’because he is? SAGE SABBOT Just some guy. You wonder if its some guy you know? SOME GUY YOU ONCE KNEW It sends chills down your back. I’ve seen more spine in jellyfish. I’ve seen more guts in 11-year-old kids. You take a deep breath and let it out. You sniffle up wet snot that is about to drip out of your nose. You stand up on your feet and look into his eyes. The man pushes you back down to your ass. He takes a couple steps over your body. You still can’t see his face in the shadow. He chuckles’an evil laugh. It sounds familiar, but you can’t seem to make it out. He grabs you by your collar on your Tommy Hilfiger shirt. He hoists you up to your feet. You are slightly ticked that somebody would place their dirty hands on your $40 shirt. You wipe off the brown dirt, you don’t want it to stain. You are the biggest piece of shit in existence. You miss me? The voice sounds very familiar. You raise your eyebrows, confused. Of course you are. Or don’t you remember me? I’ve only been gone for a little while, a couple months to be exact. You couldn’t have forgotten already. You stand directly in front of him. His hood is still up. You try to think of somebody you haven’t seen in awhile. It would be easier if you could see his face. You have to be kidding me. I’m not your normal, every day average guy. Finally, it clicks in your fucking head. You finally realize who this is’none other then? He throws the hood off his sweatshirt off his face. He reveals himself? Its Sage Sabbot’sporting scars from’from his death. You take a step back, like you’ve just seen a ghost. In your head, you try to explain the unexplainable. What the hell is going on, you ask yourself. But no answer can come to your head. Its impossible? IF CHRIST CAN RISE FROM THE DEAD SO CAN SAGE SABBOT It’s me you bastard, its Sage Sabbot? So I ask you again, do you miss me? You try to speak, but nothing comes out’you’re too damned terrified. You’re fucking retarded. 1,000 things just ran through your fucking head in the past 4 seconds. And you didn’t get any smarter. I’ll take that scared look in your eyes as a yes. I am back m’friend. Back from a place where nobody thought anybody could come back from. The power of revenge’sweetest of revenges has brought me back. Things are about to change. I came back to extract my sweet revenge on The Upper Echelon. Maverick Reno, John Zero, Ice’and whoever else decided to shoot me hundreds of times. It’s going to take more then that to kill me. It’s going to take more then that to kill Sage Sabbot. Things have changed when I look at Global Wrestling. TUE reigns now. They have almost every title in the Global Wrestling. But things are going to change. I look for Salaok and Quartz. I look for Silent Wind. I look for Johnathon Davis. I look for Mani Shigeru. I look for The Nevermore. I look for the Harvesters. Everybody has vanished’proving that I am the true Soul Survivor. Things are definitely about to change now that I am back in town. He walks around you. He slowly circles around you and stops in front of you. He looks you into the eye with his ice blue eyes. You wish you could pat him on the back’just so he’d back off and stop pushing you. I came back to be on top again. To be at the top of the ladder of the Global Wrestling. And don’t take me for granted. You would think I have a little rust from not being in the ring for many months. I am not a nobody. My name is feared in Global Wrestling and always will be. I am a former GW Heavyweight Champion of the World. I am the former GW International Champion, former GW Platinum Champion and former GW Cruiserweight Champion. I am the Harvester of Tears. I have everything you wish for and then some. Credentials don’t mean shit, I know what you are thinking. But wait until I step into the ring once again. I’ll prove to the world that the new Sage Sabbot is back in business’right? You don’t answer’just stare at him. He shakes his head side to side. He takes a couple quick steps towards you and shoves your chest, hard enough to send both of your feet over your head. You soar into the sky and come crashing down to your back. You cringe in pain. Now its starting to get old. But there is nothing you can do about it. A couple rocks stab you right in the back. You roll over on one side and brush them off. Sage stands over you and laughs once again. Instead of getting up, you just lay there. You know what’ll happen if you stand up again. Even you aren’t that fucking stupid? Maybe next time you’ll answer me This is exactly what I’m going to do to my opponents. Every time they step up to the plate. Every time they are on their feet. I’m going to knock them down. And I will sit back and laugh. Laugh at what I am and what you are not. And after I knock them down a couple times, they’ll start to get smart. They’ll stay down. Even the bravest of fighters won’t get up. So stay down, where you won’t get hurt. Stay down’right below me. Right below the championship belt. Once again, I will raise it up towards the heavens and rejoice. Rejoice because I am the soul survivor and I am the motherfucking best in the business. But for now, my business with The Upper Echelon awaits me? He turns around and walks into the corn field, disappearing into nowhere. Instead of getting up, you lie on your back. Helpless. You are filled with rage. You so want to kick his ass. But for now, you can’t even stand up. “You wonder if it can get any worse. Then it starts to rain? It starts to fucking rain? The Tears From Heaven Leave You Lying In A Pool Of Your Own Pain. Sorrow Fills Every Gaping Wound. Nothing Has Ever Hurt This Much
