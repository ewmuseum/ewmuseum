A Rude Awakening (REPOST from Temp)

26 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/26/03 When a woman looks into the mirror and she realizes how much she has aged, she is horrified. When a man looks into the same mirror, he looks and sees how much he’s changed?..and lost. Today is no different for this man. He rises this morning slowly and his life is about to change. He knows it, but he’s not too sure how its going to happen exactly. Nevertheless, he must move onto the next chapter. Good or bad, he’ll have to deal with it. To prepare for it, he does nothing more than lie in bed, face down on his fluffy pillow staring at the crack of the curtains. Why move when its easier to lie there and do nothing? The sunlight shines through and shows nature passing him by in song. Just beyond that is nothing more than a forgetful life. *Knock, knock, knock* “Go away,” he thinks to himself, but it won’t do him any good. He knows who’s...correction...what’s at the door wanting to come in. Despite a null response, the thing enters” “Are you planning on getting up this morning or have you decided to go into hibernation for the winter?” Quiet devil woman. “Ughhh”.” He turns his head only enough to smother himself in his pillow. Why bother himself with the rants and raves of someone he won’t have to deal with much after today. Probably because he can’t stand being disturbed from his nirvana....sleeping in. “That’s not exactly what I was looking for in an answer, son. Its half past nine o” clock. I think its time for you to get up.” she bellows. As she continues to disrupt his tranquility, she begins to collect up the remainders of what he had worn the day before. And the day before that, of course. She begins to loudly hum an upbeat tune of energy. This causes the slumbering lump to act quickly to grab his spare pillow and clench it over his head. “Why must you torment me so?” he mumbles out of his feathery cocoon. “Can’t you see that I’m trying to sleep?” “Trying to sleep and sleeping are two different things. If you’re trying to sleep this late in the day, you might as well get up. Because sleep in not what’s gonna happen.” She lectures like a Sunday school teacher on a preaching spree. Wait...is it Sunday? But he finally turns himself over and pulls himself out of his head shell. As he does that, his black hair puffs out to about twice the size of his head and sticks out in every direction. Holding her hand to her mouth, she giggles in shock. “My good gravy. Johnie, it looks like you got attacked by the bedhead fairies!” “Nice to see that I can amuse you, Mom,” he mutters as he rubs his hands through the joke. Continuing the laugh at his expense she sits herself on a chair full of more scattered crap. “What do you expect” Its been a while since I’ve seen you so...” “What”!” He cuts her off with flare in his eyes. “So adorable” So childlike? So messed up?! Is that it?” “No...just so grumpy that it was cute.” Surprised her a bit, but didn’t shake her at all. “Geez, quit it. I’m tired, I’m cranky, and I haven’t had my wake up juice yet.” “Wake up juice, huh?” She gets up and goes back to picking up more dressy shirts and a couple pairs of boxers. “Well hurry up and get a move on, kiddo. Maybe then you’ll get out of this mood and go get yourself somewhere.” “I’ll get there as soon as I have to.” “That’s not soon enough.” Johnie’s head hangs a bit as his eyes droop towards the thin football comforter around his waist. All ragged and torn as though its lived its own lifetime and is ready to retire. Some emblems on the helmets have faded and worn to the point where you can’t tell what it used to have on there. Luckily they repeat themselves here and there. “How am I supposed to get up if you’re in here scavenging my stuff on my floor?” he blurts out. “I gotta get dressed.” She springs up to her feet in shock with an armful of clothes hanging in all directions. Quickly, she balls them up and tosses them at his head. As they impact, he falls backwards onto the mattress. “Do you think I’m going through your clothes to find spare change” Doing it right in front of your face so you know who takes it? Yeah, that’s smart.” Pulling the leg of jeans off, a thin frown glows on his face as he mutters, “I wouldn’t put it past you.” “Oh that’s trust,” she counters as she paces around the bed corner. He bends over and leans over his face. “At least I’m not looking for drugs and condoms. I should though! You never know with the troop you’re out with these days.” Pulling himself up, he starts sorting through which shirt he should put on and what else. “Troop” Oh that’s a classic! What are they? Some sort of militant gang that plans on taking over western New Mexico?” Raising his fist into the air in triumph, he yells at the ceiling. “First the southwest, soon THE WORLD!” “Real dramatic, kid.” She heads towards the door and slides herself out. “Just make sure you’re ready for dinner. You sister’s coming by today.” The door closes as he pulls a black shirt down over his head and he sits there to glare at the door. “Oh joy. The good one“s gonna be here today.” Johnie throws off the comforter revealing an icepack around his right knee. He leans backwards stretching his body in every direction he can at once. As he unstraps the commercial pack, he grimaces while pulling himself to his feet. Grabbing his ankle, he pulls his leg back. Snap, Crackle, Pop. Just another morning with problems to deal with. What else is new? To be continued....
