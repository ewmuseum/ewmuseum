The Zenith Manifesto (Vol. 1): In The Flesh?

17 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/17/04 Manifesto Man`i*fes'to, n.; pl. Manifestoes. [It. manifesto. See Manifest, n. & a.] A public declaration, usually of a prince, sovereign, or other person claiming large powers, showing his intentions, or proclaiming his opinions and motives in reference to some act done or contemplated by him; as, a manifesto declaring the purpose of a prince to begin war, and explaining his motives. --Bouvier. It was proposed to draw up a manifesto, setting forth the grounds and motives of our taking arms. --Addison. Frederick, in a public manifesto, appealed to the Empire against the insolent pretensions of the pope. --Milman. January 12, 2004 Time: 0:00 Location: Chicago, Illinois The moon hangs high over the night sky in Chicago, Illinois. The clock strikes midnight, the church bells swing back and forth singing their melodic song over the third largest city in the United States. Thunder rumbling could be heard in the distance... but there was not a cloud in the sky. Down below, on the streets, a rumbling could be felt. The rumble grew louder, like an oncoming stampede. Excitement filled the air, the word was out, anticipation was enough to make a grown man bow to his knees and beg. Suddenly, they came from every direction. An Urban stampede, hundreds of young men and women stormed the streets in a mad sprint. 'HE'S BACK! HE'S ALIVE!' screams of the nature could be heard in the midst of the pack. The South side of Chicago could swear they had an earthquake that night, but in fact it was much more like a tsunami. The tidal wave of humanity swarmed a nearby abandoned warehouse like a pack of jackals on a fresh kill. People pushed and shoved to fit through the two entrances on opposite sides of the building. The inside of the warehouse was dark and dank, shattered glass littered the floors like a carpet being crushed under the feet of the South Side Riot. The crowd huddled close to a massive stage-like structure made out of old used crates. The vermin scattered in fear for their lives, some would never get the chance to escape, being crushed under the feet of an unknowing soul. Masses continued to pack the warehouse just to get a glimpse of greatness. It was then it started. The sea of people erupted into a roar that could be heard across Lake Superior that night when they heard the sound of 'O Fortuna' through a series of large impromptu speakers strung about the facility. Although a vast majority of the crowd didn't know a lick of German, chants of 'Au’er Uns! Au’er Uns!' quickly grew contagious. Most of the people didn't have a clue what they were saying, but it really didn't matter. Two spotlights from the back 2nd floor balcony of the warehouse popped on pointing directly at the structure of crates. Suddenly two black banners fell from the ceiling, each sporting a white circle at the bottom, which contained a black 'Z' intertwined with a silver upside down crucifix. The mass of human emotion tried to shove their way up to the front, some of the people in the front began to faint and pass out, as they were pinned up against the crates. So ya Thought ya Might like to go to the show To feel the warm thrill of confusion and That space cadet glow A metal lifting garage door began to open behind the crate structure, much to the delight of the congregation. An abandoned parking lot was on the other side of the door when it opened. The lights from the parking lot silhouetted 4 people as they approached the warehouse. Slowly, the 4 made their way into the warehouse side by side to the deafening roar of their over joyed audience, surrounded only by their visible breath in the cold January air. The light shined down on a man, his black hair partially covered his cadet blue eyes, which burned brilliantly under the heat. His emotions were cold and understated as he acknowledged his crowd with a single right fist high into the air. The crowd repeated, all thrusting their fists into the air in approval. He wore a black three piece custom fitted black suit. On the jacket two gold buttons on each side were laced with a 14k gold chain, underneath the jacket was a black suit vest. A white dress shirt, black tie, and black slacks completed the outfit. Christian Zenith lowered his right arm holding his shoulder with his left arm, in pain... but his face stayed strong, bold, and intense. He looked to his right, where Vivica J. Valentine was by his side. She nodded with a little smile on her face. She pulled a piece of her blonde hair out of her face, you could see the adrenaline rush pumping through her veins. She loved being on stage, to her there was no greater rush than being in front of a crowd. She sported a black leather jacket over a Black tube top, and a pair of black bondage pants from a local Hot Topic. Demitri and Jason looked into the crowd on each side of Christian and Vivica, dawned in all black. Jason LeMaster smiled a little bit back at the crowd whose roars overcame the blasts of 'O Fortuna' over the ghetto system he installed in like two hours earlier in the day. Demitri stood tall and firm, like a brick wall, looking back at the crowd. He slowly looked over to his left back at Christian and Vivica. Zenith put his arms in the air... 'STILLE!' Christian shouted out over the crowd, who grew quiet at the command, 'I HAVE RETURNED!' Tell me is something eluding you sunshine? Is this not what you expected to see? If you'd like to find out what's behind these cold eyes? You'll just have to claw your way through the disguise The crowd pops in approval while Christian stands firm in front of them. Zenith holds his arms in the air, hushing the rowdy crowd in front of him. 'I have looked into the eyes of death, spit in her face! Like a phoenix I have risen from the ashes and came back here, for what? This is not a mission of domination, nor a mission of territory. This is a mission of a brand new kind, I come back with a mission of vengeance!' Zenith spoke to his followers, 'I have been struck by Die Erntemaschine, she has made an attempt on my life!' Christian Zenith slips his suit jacket off to reveal a sleeveless white dress shirt. He turns around backwards to show a bandage covering a gun shot wound. The crowd looks at each other and talks amongst themselves. Some start screaming calls of murder. 'You all have heard the name Die Erntemaschine and just know that it is the name of a brutal assassin. The name Die Erntemaschine is the one thing that has stood between us and complete control of this city. Die Erntemaschine is German, it means 'The Reaper'. This assassin has gotten this name, because everyone who has seen her face has not lived to talk about it, thus the touch of death.' Christian continues, 'but I have seen the face of Die Erntemaschine, and I am not here to talk about my experiences... I am here for a quest of vengeance! This is far more than some petty payback, this is a CALL FOR BLOOD! I will not rest until Die Erntemaschine is brought here with her head on a platter. That little bitch has shot me in the back like the coward she is, and I know that coward by name!' LIGHTS! ROLL THE SOUND EFFECTS! ACTION! The crowd gasped at Christian's escape from the most feared assassin in Chicago, Illinois. Some shouted out battle cries for the hunt and slaughter of Die Erntemaschine. Zenith paced back and forth across the massive crate structure. His head bowed, looking firmly at his shoes when he held up his hand for silence... 'Her name is...' Christian stopped for a moment and looked up at Vivica J. Valentine, who looked back. She knew something was wrong almost immediately, as worry crossed her face. 'Her name...' Christian's sight became blur, his balances was off as he stumbled forward. Suddenly images rushed through his head and the reality of the moment escaped him. His fell deep into a trance for away from the present. One word escaped his mouth seconds before he fell to the floor. 'Tera...' Next Installment: The Zenith Manifesto (Vol. 2): The Thin Ice
