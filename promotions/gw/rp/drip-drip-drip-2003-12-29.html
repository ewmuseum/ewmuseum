drip drip drip

29 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.29 One single source of light in the black abyss.. It lit up the nude body of a man who lay unconscious, bound at his hands and feet.... 'ughhh' The man let out as he slowly came back to reality. 'Where the hell am I?' His simple question echoed in the dark room. Fear crept through him as a single line of green illuminated a steel helmet in the corner. 'Where am I, he dare ask. You are not here to ask questions.' The figure looked up at the man who quickly pulled himself into a sitting position. 'Listen, whatever he's paying. I swear to god I will double it.' The helmet cocked sideways as a slight chuckle escaped it. 'You are trying to bargain with me? Well, let me take that into consideration. No, A. I've seen your bank statements, you don't have enough to double my fee. And B. God, he doesn't exist. You don't believe in him anyway...' The figure walked into the room and squatted next to the man. 'You see Aaron Hathaway, I do my homework. You're not only an atheist, but also a child rapist. Guess you raped the wrong little girl.' The figure stood up and turned around to grab hold of a piece of paper. 'I will however give my employer credit. He has one hell of a twisted idea of revenge.' The figure turned back to the small table behind him. The man suddenly doubled over as if he had been fed some form of poison. 'Oh I get it,' his voice echoed, 'He paid you to poison me and watch me die while you act like fuckin Shakespeare huh?' The figure suddenly turned back his helmet catching the light and reflecting it. 'No no, you have it all wrong dear boy.' The figure suddenly raised a needle and a scalpel. 'See the cut right below you abdominal muscles?' The man suddenly panicked as he realized the fresh sewn wound. 'What the... What the fuck did you do to me!' The figure chuckled again, this time louder. 'I only followed the description for my hit.' The black globe that sat almost dead center in the helmet suddenly changed blue as the figure scanned up and down the man. 'Ahhh, there it is...' Suddenly the figure lunged forward and made a connection between fist and lower abdomen. The man buckled and immediately began to scream. 'Have you ever seen the effect of Cianide on human flesh?' The screams intensified as the man began to roll on the floor. 'See, my instructions...' The man screamed even louder until the figure lifted a leg and stomped down on his throat. 'Quiet, I'm talking. You see my instructions included a vial filled with cianide and the act of placing it in you somewhere. I was supposed to let it dissolve naturally and eat you when it did. I had a problem with that since it would involve following you for another two weeks. I already watched you rape 5 people, I'm not up for anymore of that.' The man writhed in pain as the liquid oozed it's way through finally. The muffled screams abated as his body went limp and the 'drip drip drip ' of liquid hitting the floor took dominance. 'That worked out rather well...'
