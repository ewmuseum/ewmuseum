The Rome incident - Rohypnol.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 23rd, 204. When in Rome, do as the Romans…even if you can't speak their language or understand their customs. I was in a distant land surrounded by strangers that could not comprehend who I was. But in reality…there were no vast differences. After all no one understands the things I say or do in America either. At least here hardly anyone knew who I was so I was allowed to rest in solitude…for the most part. But tonight there would be no solitude, tonight there would be no rest. Tonight my mind was plagued more than ever with the infestation of insecurity and self-loathing. These feelings were accompanied by a feeling of shame, a feeling of disgrace…and the feeling of failure. As beautiful as the city of Rome was I could hardly enjoy it in the deep state of depression that I had sunk into as if it were a mire of quicksand. It all started on Evolution. My loss to Deacon Holmes on Evolution had destroyed everything I had worked for…every single thing. I had paid my dues, I had climbed the proverbial ladder and worked for everything I had. I beat him once and proved that I was the better man…yet the second time around I simply couldn't do it. His arrogance disgusted me…it blatantly slapped me across the face. It spit upon the reputation I had worked so long for. He didn't even know a single Wrestling move, he couldn't tell you the difference between a wristlock and a wrist clutch suplex…much less execute the latter. In fact if you wanted to dig deep into the talents of Deacon Holmes you would discover nothing more than a overrated, narcissistic maniac that couldn't even perform his job correctly and Wrestle. Inside the ring he had no psychology as he hopped about like a Mexican jumping bean. Yet he had somehow found a way to defeat me…a pure Wrestler. I was the same guy that could name every suplex in the book and even a few dozen that aren't in there. I was the same guy that knew every Wrestling move so well I had to start creating my own to keep the excitement alive. Yet I wasn't the one who had been chosen to appear in Sports Illustrated. I wasn't the one receiving a sponsorship and my own personalized shoe from Nike. No…instead I was the one sitting empty and broken in my cheap hotel room. While Deacon no doubt lodged comfortably in some fancy Italian suite I was stretched across my lump bed staring up at the white ceiling that innocently overshadowed me. I'm sure he had everything. Global Wrestling had probably flown him to Rome first class, I'm sure his limousines had been covered, his hotel room and without a doubt his food as well. Yet here I was…no longer a champion and no longer a concern for Global Wrestling. I had been flown to Rome in coach. No limo had waited at the airport for my arrival, not even a taxi. I had to hail and pay for my own cab. My hotel room hadn't even been provided. Once again I was overlooked, once again I was neglected…once again I was shoved far back into the shadows while less talented yet bigger stars stole my spotlight. It was the story of my life. I had gotten used too it, long, long ago. I had accepted a lot of brutal facts in my life…but one I refused to accept was that anyone could or would ever represent the Global Wrestling Cruiserweight division better than I did. Perhaps the title only hugged my lean waist for a meager month and a half but I had been a champion to look up too. My methods may not have always been fair, sane or logical but I had been an honorable champion. I defended my belt with dignity, honor and pride. I wasn't like Deacon Holmes and neglecting the Cruiserweight title because I thought I had bigger fish to fry. I wasn't like Jin Akiyara, the man who ran from me, refused to give me a title shot and even had Christina McCoy book circles around his contenders. It was simple…Jin was a big star, Deacon was a big star and therefore they would be easily handed shots. Perhaps they were nothing more than one hit wonders but they were given their opportunities. I had always paid my dues to earn mine…yet their shots may as well have been gift wrapped they were so undeserved. I wasn't the Deacon Holmes type of Cruiserweight champion; I wasn't the Jin Akiyara type of Cruiserweight champion. Unlike either of those men I had class. I had class and talent yet the front office staff chose to ignore it. Perhaps I didn't sell enough foam fingers or T-shirts…I could only assume that was their reasoning behind the opportunities I had been denied. But not a single person would ever be able to deny that I was a phenomenal Cruiserweight champion. Even amidst my insecurity and self-loathing I had realized that. I had set out on a path to become the greatest Cruiserweight champion of all time…only to run across a detour. This was only a minor setback however because it was only a matter of time before I exercised the rematch clause written into my contract. I would become the Cruiserweight champion once again, and when I did my journey would begin anew. It was time that the Cruiserweight's of Global Wrestling had a figurehead to look up too. A man of integrity…a man that would defend his title against all comers. I felt the great desire to write my name amongst the same history books where it would juxtapose names such as Sage Sabbot, Johnie Ringo and Tyler Pierce, three men of great ability both inside, outside and above the Global Wrestling Octagon. My mind ceased its aimless and painful reminiscence for a moment as I stood from the bed covered in soft green blankets, my weight coming down on the pair of black Converse shoes I wore. I didn't bother to brush the wrinkles from my torn Ralph Lauren jeans with a hole in the right knee. Standing slowly my bluish gray eyes scanned the room in search of my luggage and I quickly spotted a faded and worn Military backpack lying near the door. Making my way towards it I quickly reached inside one of the pockets and pulled out a bottle of pills. 'Rohypnol' was the name written across the bottle. These pills had been given to me by an Italian fan…in his broken English he explained to me that they would help me with my insomnia. The only thing I knew about this apparent wonder drug is the fact that it is illegal within the borders of the United States. I was in Rome however and the last time I checked that far from my native land, therefore this substance was suddenly legal as could be. However I couldn't help but notice the corner of a magazine poking out as I prepared to turn away from the backpack. The magazine caught my eye and I pulled it from the backpack. Suddenly there he was…the ugly face of Deacon Holmes staring back at me arrogantly. I could only shake my head in awe of how an untalented mutant such as that was placed on the cover of Sport's Illustrated. Returning to my bed I towed the magazine along as well, dropping it across my bed before sitting down beside it. With a bottle of pills clutched in one hand and the other hand resting on the magazine I began thumbing my way through the pages searching for Deacon's story. I had read it countless times shocked by the arrogance…the stupidity…the immaturity and the fact that the American public had an infatuation with all of this. Deacon Holmes was the equivalent of a one man, walking Jerry Springer show, a classless, tasteless and even talent-less individual who had been handed the reigns to Global Wrestling's Cruiserweight division. My eyes scanned the pages as I reached up with my left hand and brushed away a lock of my shaggy black hair as it fell across my face. Looking down at the magazine I spotted exactly what I had been looking for. One simple sentence that infuriated me yet crushed my ego at the same time. His thoughts on me… 'Too easy to get inside his head, too slow. He’d do better as a heavyweight.' That one sentence thoroughly disgusted me and mutilated my image amongst the non-Wrestling fans in the American public. I was almost tempted to drag him into court and slap him with a libel charge so expensive it would make his pea sized brain do flip flops. It's odd how one mans ego can try to instigate the downfall of a better man. He had never accepted defeat when I beat him. I was faster than he was the night I defeated him…and I still am. Yet he called me slow. The only question that brings to mind is…Why? Perhaps he was saying all of this to save face. Maybe it was just the fact that he could not accept defeat or humiliation in front of the eyes of society. Unlike him I didn't care what others thought…and I didn't care what others said about me unless it was damaging to my career. But this was more than just damaging to my career. This was a slap across the face, this was a challenge, and this was an example of one bitter man spewing lewd and untrue comments to damage my reputation. Closing the magazine I angrily hurled it across the room where it landed on the floor in a flatter of pages. I quickly twisted off the cap to the Rohypnol bottle. My hands shook angrily as I dumped one pill into my hand…and then two…then three. Surely this would cure my insomnia. Slumping from the bed I dropped to my knees distraught and depressed. My shirtless body quivered with emotion as I kept a tight grip on the open bottle of pills in one hand and a trio of pills in the other. Looking down into my hand my heart seemed to grow cold. I had lost the two things I loved the most…my Cruiserweight title had been taken from me and I could accept that because there was always the possibility of earning back my property. The other missing piece was Alexia…she hadn't been taken away by anyone she simply didn't want me. She refused to talk to me…she refused to return my phone calls. Logic says to forget about her and move on…but my heart says, impossible. She haunted my dreams…and my Cruiserweight title loss haunted my nightmares. Perhaps if sleep only brought upon me dreams and nightmares I may as well end all of those dreams of false hope. My insomnia could be cured…only instead of never sleeping again I would never be able to open my eyes wide-awake again. Turning the bottle over in my hand I stopped counting as they spilled into my hand. Perhaps it was nine or ten that fell into my palm but without hesitation my mouth was agape and my hand dumped the pills inside, I quickly swallowed every last one of them…bidding the cruel world adieu. My heart raced as I waited for the pills to take their effect…yet there was nothing. I stood to my feet in frustration but as I reached a vertical base the room began to spin rapidly. I took a step forward only to feel my limbs cease their work. My legs became as rubber and my arms lost their strength. My knees shook slowly as they began to bend unable to support my two hundred and one pounds. I toppled to the floor in a heap, face down. My eyelids closed as I realized what I had done. I knew that what had been done couldn’t be changed now, this was destiny. It was all for the best. I had been haunted enough in life so now it was time to enter a place where…haunting is eternal.
