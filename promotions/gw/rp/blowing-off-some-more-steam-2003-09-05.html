Blowing off some more steam

5 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/5/03 It was a common practice for Brick. Whenever he had a big match coming up or somebody in Global Wrestling had made the unfortunate mistake of pissing him off, he would drive out to an empty field with an Army duffel bag full of firearms and cardboard cut-outs of the superstar or superstars he was facing or had angered him. The last time he was out in the field was when he was feuding with the Harvesters. In recent months, one individual has done the latter on a number of occasions. That individual is Deacon Holmes. Over the past few months, the young GW superstar has gone out of his way to antagonize the World champion. It all started with his scathing comments on an edition of Overload. Their first physical confrontation was after one of Holmes? matches, where Brick had served as a guest commentator. The Nevermore had ambushed both Holmes and Roz Blayze. Brick saw fit to interfere, cleaning house. Holmes saw this opportunity to get in Brick’s face. He got a vicious beating and a Bricklayer for his troubles. Since then, their rivalry has escalated into epic proportions. Holmes has insulted Brick, interrupted Brick celebrating his first-ever World title win, vandalizing Brick’s car and other stunts. The first time they ever faced each other in the ring was the Hard 10 match, in which to win, you had to knock your opponent unconscious for a ten count. The match ended in a draw, with both men falling off the Globaltron through the steel stage. It did nothing to quench the fires of animosity between them. There were many brawls between the two, including a brawl at an autograph signing, which cut the event short and made a rescheduling necessary. The latest stunt Holmes pulled involved knocking Brick unconscious and having him sent to an alternative lifestyle club as the 'entertainment', all the while framing Ice for the deed. Brick would receive payback when Christina McCoy ordered Homes to defend both his titles in a triple threat match and appointed Brick as the special guest referee. Although ordered not to lay a hand on Holmes, the animosity between the two and the blatant lack of respect from the youth pretty much made a physical confrontation inevitable. A subsequent brawl in Christina McCoy’s office led to her booking the two in a New York Street fight with the World title on the line. This time, Brick decided, rather than use cardboard cut-outs, that he was going to use tin cans with Holmes? picture taped to them. As he polished off a few cans, he could hear another car pull up and see the glow of headlights. He turned to look behind him and saw a pair of headlights. “Brick?” a voice called out. “Aaron?” Brick replied. Aaron walked right up to Brick’s left side. “Figured you’d be out here. Deacon Holmes really pissed you off, didn’t he?” “To say the least.” “Man, the last time you were this pissed was with the Nevermore. And for them, you used cut-outs.” “Which I then burned to ash.” “I can understand. I didn’t appreciate that “friends of Brick” crack he made the night he?” “I know what happened that night,” Brick snarled. Obviously, it was not something Brick liked to talk about. “Right,” Aaron replied before changing the subject. “So, what’s with the tin cans?” “Well, they’re about the same height as Holmes, so?” “Oh, that’s wrong, man,” Aaron chuckled. “It’s the truth.” “I doubt very much Christina McCoy is gonna let you shoot him.” “Don’t be so sure. She don’t like his ass any more than I do, and I can’t stand the little bitch. Besides, the pain I plan putting him through is gonna make him wish I’d shoot him.” Brick then proceeded to pick off a few more cans. “I think you need to focus. Last time you had a match with Holmes, it ended in a draw.” “Well, this is how I focus, mentally. It’s a hunt. I’m the lion and Deacon’s the antelope. I’m the hawk and he’s the rabbit. I’m the cat and he’s the mouse. Come the 8th, Deacon better give his soul to the Lord because his ass belongs to me.” And then Brick picked off one last can.
