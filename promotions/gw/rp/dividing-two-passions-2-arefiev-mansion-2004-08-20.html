Dividing Two Passions #2: Arefiev Mansion

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 20th 2004 Cautiously the Red Ford Mustang of the Flying Vergomovs snakes it’s way down the winding driveway of the Arefiev mansion in the hills of Warsaw. The sun is shining brightly off the sparkling clean car as the pair approach the large grey castle. “Wow!” exclaims Zergolov. Arefiev snaps his head to the left and stares at the driver of the car. Zergolov returns the look at his tag team partner. “What?” questions Constantino. “You’ve been here before,” exclaims a surprised Igor. “I know,” replies Zergolov. “Then why the “Wow!””” Zergolov shrugs his shoulders. “It just always seems huge to me?” Arefiev shakes his head with a little disbelief. Zergolov pulls the car to a stop in front of the five grand pillars that stand above the steps leading up to the front door. The top of steps is slowly filled with people. Clearly members of Igor’s family. Arefiev waves out his window as the car stops on the loose stones. The two doors of the car swing open and Arefiev steps out first. He is wearing a yellow t-shirt and dusty blue jeans. He waves at the five bodies that have taken up positions between the pillars. Zergolov then steps out and waves as well at the five people. Igor bounds up the steps and hugs an elderly woman. She is a little smaller than the 5? 4 Arefiev, has curly grey hair and is wearing a lime green summer dress. “Hey mum!” exclaims Arefiev to this elderly lady. “Welcome home son!” smiles Mrs. Arefiev. Igor then turns to face an elderly man standing close to his mother. The pair shake hands. The man is balding with grey head at the sides of his head. He is wearing a navy jumper and grey trousers. “Welcome home son?” exclaims the old man. Igor Arefiev just nods and smiles back at his dad. Zergolov has now reached the top of the steps and is welcomed by Igor’s parents. “Mr. and Mrs. Arefiev,” nods Constantino. “Hello Constantino,” is the icy reply. Igor turns to face the three other members of the welcoming committee. “Gloria, Stephen” How are you?” Igor nods at his two older siblings. Gloria is a very pretty girl with jet black hair and a muscular build. She is wearing a white summer top and blue jeans. Stephen has messy brown hair. He stands taller than Igor, much taller. “Hello Igor,” exclaim both in unison and smile. Igor hugs both of them and then turns to the final person at the door. “Anna!” smiles Igor. Anna Arefiev is a very small girl with brown hair and blonde highlights. She is wearing a red summer dress. He bounds a couple of steps and lifts his little sister high into the air. “Happy birthday sis!” shouts Igor, as his sister playfully struggles to break free. The rest of the group watch with smiles on their faces as the two youngest Arefiev get reacquainted. “Hello Gloria,” exclaims Constantino Zergolov. Suddenly, the smiles drop from everyone at the door. Igor lets his sister down to her feet. Everyone’s attention is drawn to the former lovers standing face-to-face no more than five feet from everyone. All of the Arefiev’s have icy expressions except for Igor. Zergolov tries to smile. “Hello Con?” is the sharp snappy reply from Gloria Arefiev. “Okay?” interrupts Mr. Arefiev, “Igor and Constantino” Geoffrey will show you to your rooms.” Mrs. Arefiev smiles as Igor and Constantino rush down to their car and remove their bags. The pair then bound back up the stairs and follow the butler, Geoffrey into the house. The Flying Vergomovs step out of the sunlight and into the grand lobby of the mansion. The lobby is very elaborately decorated with a lot of mahogany panels and oak cravings. Geoffrey leads the pair up a “Y” staircase in front of the double doors that lead into the house. “Wow!” exclaims Zergolov. “Would you stop that”!” bites Arefiev. “What?” questions a puzzled Constantino. “The whole pretend “Wow!” It gets really annoying!” “Sorry! This place just takes my breath away?” whispers Zergolov. Arefiev sighs as the two men continue to pace up the steps. -------------------- “Constantino Zergolov” I present to you the Arefiev gym!” Igor Arefiev pushes open the swinging double doors of the gym. The Flying Vergomovs stand side-by-side at the door of a large room. Above their heads is a landing rope for circus performers and above that hand two or three trapeze poles. On the floor are numerous gym apparatuses. Constantino rubs his hands together and a big smile flashes across his face. “How come you never showed me this before?” asks Zergolov as he steps onto a treadmill. “We never needed it before,” begins Arefiev, “when we visited before we were always finished tours and therefore we were winding down. There was no need for us to train hard. But now it’s a different story. This is a big opportunity for us so we better try to get the most out of this trip.” As the pair start to jog on the treadmills, Zergolov strips off his tracksuit top and throws it to the side. In the mirror, Igor can see that Zergolov is wearing a GW Flying Vergomov t-shirt. Igor hops off the back of his treadmill. “WHAT THE FUCK”!!” Zergolov turns around to look at his partner, loses his balance and falls flat-on-his-face on the treadmill and then is slide onto the ground. Zergolov groans and rolls over to look up at Igor. “WHAT ARE YOU WEARING”!!” screams Arefiev. “A t-shirt and tracksuit bottoms,” exclaims Constantino. “NO! You’re wearing a GW t-shirt!” “Shit!” Zergolov looks down at his t-shirt. Constantino jumps to his feet and rips the t-shirt off over his head. “What do I do?” A sense of panic has overcome Zergolov as he looks down at the t-shirt and then back at Igor. Just then, Gloria and Anna steps through the gym doors in tracksuit bottoms and t-shirts. “Hey guys!” exclaims Anna. “Hey!” smile the two secret wrestlers. Igor and Constantino have now turned to face the Arefiev sisters. Behind his back, Constantino clutches the t-shirt. The Flying Vergomovs flash cheesy miles as the girls. Anna and Gloria look at the two men with puzzled expressions, shrug and begin to climb the ladder to the trapeze platforms. “What do I do?” whispers Zergolov. “Wait until they reach the top?” explains Arefiev. “Then what?” “Put it in your suitcase?” sighs Arefiev, “do you have any common sense?” Zergolov shakes his head, still flashing a cheesy smile. “Constantino?” shouts Anna, about three quarters of the way up the ladder. There is silence. “Yes?” replies Zergolov cautiously. “Put it away!” The two girls laugh and Arefiev sniggers. Zergolov seems embarrassed. He grins at the girls and then walks out of the gym. “Where’s he going?” shouts Gloria to her brother. “To get a t-shirt,” explains Arefiev. “But did he not have one in his hands?” Arefiev shakes his head. Gloria shrugs and climbs onto the trapeze platform, high above Arefiev’s head. Zergolov quickly returns wearing a pale blue t-shirt. The Flying Vergomovs begin again on the treadmill. “Igor?” “Yeah?”” nods Igor. “I kinda,” begins Zergolov, “bumped into your mother along the way and she gave me the funniest look, almost she wanted to get me in the” “SHUT IT!” “Sorry,” smiles Constantino. The two men run in silence. “So how are we supposed to train with those two here?” “What do you mean?” asks Arefiev. The volume of the conversation has returned to a normal level now that the two sisters are out of earshot. “They will notice the difference in a circus workout and a wrestling workout. Like how will we explain so much weight work and very little tumbling?” There is silence. Arefiev seems deep in thought. Another wave of panic attack begins to sweep Zergolov. “Sabbot and Soma are huge! If we don’t get the weight training done we will just have to rely on our speed! But if they hit a couple of big moves! That will slow us down! And they we will be just a couple of midgets! Pretending to be tag team title contenders!” Sweat has very quickly run down the forehead of Zergolov. “Calm down!” exclaims Arefiev. Zergolov begins to breath a little easier. “What are we going to do” What are we going to do?” Zergolov seems wired as he bites the questions at Arefiev. “We will go to sleep now and then tonight, we can do some night training?” Arefiev hops off the treadmill and lifts his jacket. Zergolov nods and follows suit. The Flying Vergomovs disappear out the gym doors carrying their tracksuit tops.
