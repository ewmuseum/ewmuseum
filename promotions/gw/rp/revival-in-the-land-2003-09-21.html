Revival in the Land

21 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.21.03 I couldn't believe it. There they were, waiting for me to get out of church so that they can pounce on me with their questions. I don't think that the media has any respect whatsoever anymore. My sister was the one that pointed them out to me as we were standing at the door, speaking with Brother Houston. She asked if I knew the men outside wearing the Global Wrestling Staff shirts. They followed me here, to the town I grew up in, and they can't even wait for me to get back to New York before the barrage me. This is ridiculous. I shook Brother Houston's hand and told him how much I enjoyed the service before we walked outside. As they began approaching me, my first instinct was to run to the car and head directly to my sister's house, but I knew they would only follow me. I kissed my neice and nephew on their foreheads, shook my brother-in-law's hand, and gave my sister a hug. 'I'll get there as soon as I can.' They headed to their Pathfinder and Trever, my nephew, closed his door just as the camera made it's way in front of me. At this point I was incensed, but I was standing in the church parking lot and knew that I needed to be on my best behavior. 'You're kidding me, right? You guys can't even leave me along while I'm in town for my best-friend's funeral?' 'Well, Ken, we really just want to know if you're still going to be facing Kid Flash at Evolution this Tuesday.' 'If you're simply asking whether or not I'll be there, the answer is a certain yes.' The bomb will drop any second now. I know that they didn't come all the way out here simply to ask me that. If so, I was more popular than I thought. I doubt that to be the case, though, as the GW employee standing in front of me was obviously poised to delve deeper into my personal life. 'What about Gunner?' 'She'll be there.' 'But where is she today,' he asked. I can't believe this, it's none of their busniess where she is or what she's doing. It's not like it's even a big deal, she just needed to leave after the funeral to train for her match... the same thing I should be doing. It's so rare that I get to see my family, though, and my neice and nephew are growing up so fast. 'Don't worry about it,' I responded. 'Trouble in paradise, Ken?' I could pound this guy right now. How could one person be so meddlesome? 'Excuse me,' I said with a hint of bad attitude. 'Is everything still okay in the Supestar-Addison camp?' Who was this creep, Super-Idiot? Things had been better than they ever were between us. Hell the truth is, I'm in love with her. I have been forever, I just don't have the guts to tell her. 'Everything's fine between the two of us. Now, if you'll excuse me.' I tried to press forward, but it didn't seem to work. 'What about Ice? I stopped dead in my tracks... looking into aboslute nothingness. The patrons of the church that had been preoccupying themselves around the camera stopped as well. Brother Houston, the pastor that just happened to also be an old friend that's helped me through a lot in life, looked at me questioningly. I shook my head no slightly, and he began distracting everyone again with handshakes and smiles. Although I did notice him glancing up at us when he got the opportunity, simply to make sure everything was okay. 'What about him? He's scum. He's dead to me, now,' I responded truthfully. 'Hardly the way for a church man to speak of an old friend, there, Ken.' That was it. I'd had it. I knew Brother Houston would understand. I'd been through a lot in the past two weeks. He knows because I talked to him about for an hour and a half before service this morning. I knocked the camera off of the cameraman's shoulder, causing it to shatter on the ground. Then I grabbed the Interviewer by the collar as I spoke very calmly. 'You have no decency at all, do you. You're as bad as Flash... as bad as Ice. You're out for yourself, and that's it. Well, I've got some news for you, if you don't leave me and Gunner alone right now, you'll not only be out of a job, but the next thing I knock off of a pair of shoulders will be your head. Understand?' The man just nodded his head quickly as I released him. I straightened my jacket and looked back at Brother Houston with a new smile on my face. 'I'll see you again before I leave, Brother David.' 'You'd better, Ken. Tell your mother I said hello.' 'Will do,' I said, waving goodbye. I got to my car and opened the door, glancing back at the camerman and the staff member. I noticed that he had a wet spot on his pants. That's when I realized that I'd actually made him piss himself. I chuckled a bit and got in, closing the door. It was time for me to go, and I couldn't wait to eat with my neice and nephew.
