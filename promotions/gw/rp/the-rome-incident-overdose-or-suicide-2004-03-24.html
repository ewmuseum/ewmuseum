The Rome Incident - Overdose or Suicide?

24 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 24th, 2004. It's odd how people say they see a white light before they die. For me there was no white light. For me there was no majestic music. For me there were no beautiful visions of grandeur. Instead I had seen the complete opposite. When I hit the floor my life had flashed before my eyes. The women…the money…the broken bones…the torn muscles. The pain…the pleasure…the good times…and the bad times. I had seen my parents who I never speak of, my brother that I haven't seen in ages, my nephews that seem to be growing faster by the day. Then I had seen Amber, Alexia…and my friends and trainer, KPJ, Ice, Tommy Rock, Jose Guzera. I had seen the good things I had done to make everyone happy…and the bad things I had done to crush hearts. Alexia had danced through my mind one last time as my eyelids closed permanently. Her beauty was unmatched…and unattainable. When the vision of Alexia faded, my body was hung in the balance between Heaven and Hell as it threatened to plunge into the yawning black abyss that burned uncontrollably. I could hardly make anything out…flames were all that reached my eyes. But the screams that hit my ears were something I would never forget. Screams of torture, screams of sheer outright agony. Screams unlike I had ever heard before. And the screams weren't just from the weak and cowardly. They came from the voices of men…tough men, some of the toughest to ever walk the earth. They came from women, beautiful women. They came from the under developed lungs of children. The screams came from everyone. It was the screams of souls sentenced to a life of eternal torture. The lake of fire had swallowed their soul and captured it forever. Just as the Bible had predicted there were both the great there as well as the strong. Not the weaklings as I would've expected. Yet as my body was suspended over the ominous and menacing lake of fire below a mighty voice rang out. 'It's not time.' Were the words spoken by this majestic and powerful voice. A voice that sent chills down my spine and almost made me quiver. Then slowly…I resurfaced to reality. In reality things seemed to be going crazy, machines beeping, voices in the background, and babies crying. But anything…and I do mean anything was better than the screams I had just heard. Screams that would haunt me to my untimely demise. Screams that almost welcomed me to what I thought was destined to be my untimely demise. But as the voices around me picked up in volume I began to wonder exactly what had happened. I began to wonder where I was. Slowly I opened my eyes to discover my whereabouts. As my eyelids uncovered my bluish gray eyes my vision scanned the ceiling that was above me. I saw white tiles and bright lights…lights a bit too bright for my personal taste. 'He's waking up!' Came an excited female voice that I didn't recognize. Looking around for the person that the voice had came from I spotted an Italian nurse. A brunette, fairly short but quite pretty with sparkling brown eyes and a low cut top that inspired thoughts of lust. Then someone approached my bed from the other side; I turned my eyes to spot Ken Partridge Jr. smiling down at me. His baby blue eyes bore a beam of genuine happiness as he jubilantly stood over the bed I was lying upon. 'About time you woke up.' He said with his smile never fading as he continued on. 'The doctor said when you woke up you could leave.' He finished. 'Great.' I said, confused as to why I was here. All I could remember were the screams and the voice…I didn't know why I was here myself. 'How are you feeling Josh?' The nurse inquired in her Italian accent. Pausing to contemplate this I realized there was a gnawing pain in my stomach, which I needed to address. 'To be honest with you…I'm starved. I would kill for a strawberry milkshake about right now.' I said completely serious. My companions in this room erupted into laughter as Ken shook his head. 'I take it your feeling pretty good then.' Said my stable mate. 'Yeah…hey Ken, what happened?' I inquisitively asked of him, since he surely had more knowledge of why I was here, since I couldn't remember anything. Turning to the nurse Ken gave her a look, which signaled she should exit the room. She did so immediately leaving Ken and I alone in this hospital room. Turning away from me he walked towards the window looking out briefly before finding the words to explain to me what had happened. 'Everyone is hoping you can tell us that.' He softly said. 'Wait, what do you mean?' I said furrowing my brow in a very confused state of mind. 'Josh, you’re here because you overdosed on Rohypnol.' He said without looking at me. I knew the words had been difficult for him to say but he continued on. 'The question that everyone is asking is; did you overdose, or was it a suicide attempt? My mind went blank I couldn’t remember anything outside of the screams and voices. The ringing of my cell phone had eluded me. The knocks on my door had escaped me. I couldn't recall my friends Ice and Ken Partridge Jr. kicking in the door to my Rome hotel room to discover my nearly lifeless body stretched across the floor. I could bring nothing to mind involving my friends call to 911. I had been unconscious from the time I hit the floor to the time I reached the hospital. Yet even at the hospital I was still asleep until now. This conversation wasn't an easy one to have no matter how close we are. He was like a brother to me and I was a brother to him. Since my own family had turned their back on me Ken and I had become much like family members. I struggled for the words to try to tell Ken I didn't remember anything that had happened. But before I could tell him, we were interrupted once again by the nurse. She stepped through the door a clipboard in her hand, which she promptly laid, on the foot of my bed. Turning to face Ken she spoke in a polite tone. 'Excuse me Mr. Partridge but I've just talked to Dr. Kutner and he's informed me that Josh is ready to go home now.' Ken nodded as he silently stepped outside of my room. The nurse pointed to a chair in which my clothes had been deposited for me, she too then exited the room closing the door behind her. As I wobbly stood from my bed I looked at my whereabouts. The hotel room was no doubt top of the line. An IV machine was next to my bed and looking down at my arm I realized that sometime in my stay I had an IV injected into my veins. Jerking the white hospital gown from my body I threw it onto the floor as I scooped up my clothes. A pair of gray boxers, which I promptly pulled on, wrinkled Versace jeans, which I pulled on next and buckled the black leather belt that was already through the loops. I scooped my shoes off of the floor and my white sleeveless shirt as well that featured a Captain America figure on the front. I pulled the shirt over my head and shoved my arms through their openings. Sitting on the bed I pulled my black ankle socks on and the Red converse sneakers soon followed. I laced each one quickly and then turned anxious to get out of this hospital room. As I swung the door open three figures were waiting for me outside. Ken, the nurse and a short man with a shaved head bearing a nametag reading Dr. Kutner. It didn't take a genius to figure out who the latter was and his brown eyes had a dim look to them as he greeted me with a firm handshake. 'Hello Josh, I'm Dr. Kutner, your physician.' He said as he shook my hand. I only nodded in reply, as I wasn't one for small talk. He continued on apparently reading from the clipboard he held in his left hand. 'Josh you overdosed on a drug called Rohypnol. It's one of the most popular club drugs used by today’s youth and it is gaining widespread attention because of its sedative-hypnotic effects.' I simply nodded again in response. I knew what it was. In fact it is often known as the 'date rape' drug but I've heard it can put someone to sleep hella fast which why I took it, I didn't however feel the need to explain that to this mutant. He continued on in fine English despite his thick Italian accent. 'Rohypnol causes “anterograde amnesia,” whereby you are unable to remember events experienced while under its effects.' He stated as he informed me of the seriousness of the drug I had ingested. 'Now Josh I understand from talking to your friends that you have been suffering from insomnia. Therefore you may have taken this drug to cure that, and that is prescribed in many countries, however I feel as if I must ask you a very important question.' The doctor said with a pause at the end waiting for my approval to ask. 'Go ahead.' I replied with a nod, wondering what his question would be. 'Josh, you have to answer this honestly…and if you can't remember that's fine. But for your own safety we need to know this. Did you intentionally overdose?' He asked in a very serious tone. My eyes stayed focused on him as I took a step back down the hall. 'Josh…were you attempting suicide?' He asked again as my eyes widened. I didn't know… I didn't remember anything… I had just taken a drug that was known on the street as the 'forget-me pill'. I couldn't remember anything…only the screams, only the voices in my head before I came back to consciousness. I couldn’t answer the question because I didn't know the answer myself. 'Josh, were you trying to commit suicide?' He asked again as I backed further and further down the hall with Ken at my side. I didn't know the answer so I quickly turned my back and walked quickly down the hall with Ken matching me step for step. There are very few things that can rattle me…but not knowing whether or not I just tried to kill myself is one. I didn't know what to say because I couldn't remember what I had done…or what I had attempted to do. It's crazy…everyone was asking me if I had just tried to kill myself and I didn't even know the answer. I couldn't remember what had happened, I couldn't remember if I had tried to kill myself. But now only one thought was running through my mind…had I?
