On The Way(Terrelle's Jet)

25 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On The Way(Terrelle's Jet) Terrelle Green Global Wrestling Promo #789 Date: 1/25/04 Location: Detroit,Michigan Inside Terrelle’s Jaguar (Terrelle is sitting talking to Don Giovanni on the speakerphone.) “Joe I am on the way now I just had a long night at the club thats all.” says Terrelle. “Terrelle ever since I have known you I have always seen you running late I am up here waiting at you plane for you.” says Joe. “Why are you not on the jet watching the movie I’ve got Goodfellas set to come on, really I’ve got whatever you want to see. Including an open bar whatever you want I got you are family help yourself to whatever you want. Anything I dont got they will get tell them I am sure they will run to get it no get your ass on the plane and wait.” “Alright just get here.” “I will.” (Terrelle hangs up the phone with Joe Giovanni as the speed towards the airport.) “Petro how long will it be before we reach the airport?” “We should be there any minute sir.” says Petro. “Good make it this minute.” They speed past a highway patrol officer and his motorcycle gets behind them the driver begin to slow down. “What the hell are you doing?” yells Terrelle. “An officer sir has gotten behind us.” “What the fuck did you do?” “Speeding” “Oh yeah I forgot that was against the law.” They come to a stop as the officer gets off the bike and goes to Terrelle’s window and knock on it. Terrelle signals the driver to let it down he begins letting it down with Terrelle sitting in the back seat with his sunglasses on. “What?” asks Terrelle. “Are you aware you were going 15mph. over the speed limit.” says The officer. “Let me think........NO I Wasn’t cause I am not driver you fucking prick! yells Terrelle. Let me give you a tip you see him up there he’s the driver he is the man you need to talk to not me.” Terrelle signals Petro to let up his window in the officers face and he walks up to Petro’s window and stands writing a ticket. “I am going to give you a ticket for 300 dollars and it is to be paid in two weeks.” Out the back seat reaches Terrelle with a check for three hundred dollars. The driver hands the officer the ticket and the check and closes the window and rides off. “See Petro I always have things under control cause you would have been in big trouble had it not been for me.” “Thanks Don Greeniso.” “Just dont let it happen again.” Thirty minutes later they pull up at Terrelle’s private jet were Don Giovanni is standing there waiting. “I thought we weren’t going to make it to Dallas for a minute slowpoke.” yells Joe. “I got stopped by the cops.” “For what this time? Guns, drugs, taxes, trafficing, or maybe kingpinning. Terrelle shakes his head walking up to the door of the jet. “Speeding.” Joe begins laughing as they walk onto the plane and the door slams shut and the plane is getting ready for take off. Joe’s laugh is heard as the men yell for take off of the plane. Inside The Plane (The two men sit back talking as Joe is still snickering at the pety crime that Terrelle was stopped for.) “This show tomorrow Joe we have to set our mark.” “I know what you mean beat these two to a pulp right.” “No, those to are just stepping stones until we get to the Main Event and Championship Matches. Those two are just a couple of hills you see.” “Yeah I see. We need to make everyone know and fear Don Giovanni and Terrelle Green just like in real life.” “But, you know the difference between these wrestlers and the normal people is that they seem not to car about who we are. They care about our performance in that ring and in that ring we must prove ourselves to be the most dangerous.” “I see what you mean we should try to control the ranks and what better way than to beat the hell out of everybody we come across.” “ No, what better way than to send everyone we come across to the fucking hospital.” (Joe nods his head and Terrelle smiles in a sinister way) “I know what you mean cousin I got the drift. These two men we face tomorrow not only will we beat them we will try and focus on hurting them.” “Yes, we need to make our own mark in this wrestling federation we need to make the wrestlers respect us.” “Those who dont respect us will be sentenced to cruel and unusual punishment.” “You are a very sick man Joe.” “Thank you Terrelle thats the nicest thing anyone has ever said to me.” The two men laugh “Now lets sit back and watch this movie Joe we should be hitting Dallas in about two hours.” “What is this movie?” “Scarface.” “You and this fucking movie you and this Tony Montana have similarities you know.” “Like what?” “For instance that scar above your eye.” “You know if you had of been anyone else besides my cousin I would have killed you.” “Yeah thats why i said that there is an upper hand to being familia.” “I know and we will show GW.” “You took the words right out of my mouth.”
