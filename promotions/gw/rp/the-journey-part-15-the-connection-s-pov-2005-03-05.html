The Journey: Part 15/The Connection's POV

5 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-5--05 Early Afternoon Its about 2:00 in the afternoon on a somewhat rainy day in New York City and boy you cna tell it in people’s mmods! Some people are really nasty if you try to say hi to them and some would just flip you off let alone give you directions anywhere! Some people can just be bitches that way can’t they? Today started out as a good day for Joshua Curtis as he found out that he would be allowed to enter the Tag Gauntlet at HO to compete for the tag team titles at which case if he were successful in winning the gauntlet it would be his second time ever being the tag team champion of a federation. The bad news is that his boyfriend , Zack Johnson, has fallen ill so he will not be allowed to compete in the gauntlet with Curtis. Whim can Curtis find at such a short notice to fill in for Zack? Meanwhile back at the residence... Ami Mizuno was just waking up from her late night with Michiru as they got home at about 3 in the morning and while Joshua was a little upset he forgave his younger sister and went on out to Michael’s to get his hair done for the night as it would be his return to the ring andhe wanted people to not forget him. When Ami had gotten up from her late slumber she took a look around to see if anyone other than herself and Michiru were there and fact was that Juri had gone out with Tina early in the morning and she knew that Joshua had errands to run so he wouldn’t be home all day there for it was just a matter of what could she do to satisfy the itch that she had to do something! And then the idea hit her... While Joshua was out it meant that she was allowed to sneak into her brother’s room and take a look at his wardrobe! Now is this unusual? Not really because Ami takes a look at Joshua’s clothes all the time to see what she can fit in that doesn’t him anymore. Josh has given Ami a lot of different outfits that he outgrew or just flat out grew tired of and Ami has been very grateful but at the same time every time he gets a new outfit she tries to spot it just in case so that she doesn’t get surprised as much by him backstage at an event. Now because Zack was sick it meant that he was home so Ami had to be especially careful with sneaking into the closet and taking a peek at the outfits that Joshua had so she very carefully and quietly opened the door, walked in and made her way to the closet that Joshua keeps his major outfits in. How impressive is Joshua’s collection? Very! Joshua has a walk-in closet that houses somewhere in the neighborhood of 2,000 outfits and some were made espeically for him by friends of his! On of the first outfits that Ami took a look at was a grey suit that Joshua had made by someone that knew the legendary Ric Flair whom Joshua idolized and still does to this day! Another outfit that Ami noticed was one that Joshua has been known to wear to a cosplay convention or two in the “Sailor Mercury” outfit that he bought weeks before a convention one year and when everyone saw him in that outfit they both got a good laugh and complimented him on making it work! It isn’t often that a MAN can make an outfit that a woman wears work but then again that is what makes Joshua different from all the other guys out there. The prize outfit that Ami always likes looking at in the closet is the “Zechs Outfit” but she isn’t alone in that view... Zack just happened to be getting up to get some ginger ale when he heard Ami in the closet so he decided to havea look see with her at some of the outfits that Joshua kept in the closet. When he noticed Ami taking a look at the “Zechs Outfit” a smile crossed his face like very few men did! The reason? This outfit was specially made for Joshua by Lucrezia Noin, a good friend of both Joshua’s and Lt. Zechs. Lucrezia knew that Joshua cared a lot about Zechs and wanted to honor him in the ring in some way so when she found out that Joshau was going to return to the ring the first time she cameup with the idea of the outfit to honor his good friend. Joshua took Lucrezia up on the offer and it fit perfectly on his slim body. Ok ok yes you do deserve some answers as it pertains to the friendship of Baronet Lucrezia Noin and Joshua Curtis, do I’ll give you all the answers you need. Her name is Lucrezia Noin, a Baronet in one of the most elite armies in the world and a tought soldier to be sure! Lucrezua is the right hand woman to Zechs Marquis and if anything happened to Zechs she would be eternally devastated which is why she asks for Joshua’s help in a lot of situations but it wasn’t always like this you know... Lucrezia is a woman that very few people can gain the trust of because she is someone whom is VERY picky about her friends and when it comes to telling secrets there are some that not even her closest of friends know. One night when Joshua went on a trip for a wrestling federation to a base in St. Edwards he was witness to some of the dirlls that the soldiers of this army did and was in awe of how they did things. The instructor was Lucrezia and she was barking orders out with a viscousness that Joshua had heard about but never seen and when she saw Joshua that night she told him to get in there and do the exercises with the men to which point he did as asked and did them and then some! Joshua performed every exercise flawlessly and procceeded to put on a show for the men at the base the like of which Lucrezia never saw and as she’ll tell you from the door will never see again because Joshua’s agility was a one of a kind that no one and I mean NO ONE would have on that base! “Very impressive sir” Lucrezia said as she approached Joshua carefully while he was catching his breath “my name is Lucrezia Noin and this is St. Edwards base. What is your name yong man?” Lucrezia asked as Joshua caught his breath and turned to salute the Baronet before speaking. “Ma’am my name is Joshua Curtis. I wrestle for a promotion called the IWF and we are on a tour of some military bases right now and yours was next on the list. I’m sorry if we caused a disturbance to your exercises but you asked me to come into them so I did and I wanted to show my agility to your troops.” Joshua explained and he saw nothing but a big ol’ grin on the face of the Baronet when she heard the name of Joshua Curtis! “Joshua” Lucrezoa started “first off you needn’t apologize for the interruption as I did tell you to get in there and second off I am very pleased with your agility and toughness in the ring as well as what you did here today. Would you please come with me to see someone? I think you two might hit it off rather well.” Lucrezia explained and Joshua saluted before following her to a hangar where a man wearing the outfit that Ami saw in the closet was overseeing the construction of a very impressive machine in the hangar. “Lieutenant Zechs!” Lucrezia called out and a man wearing a red jacket with a black shirt underneath, a pair of white pants with a black belt and black knee high boots came down from the scaffold that he was up on and Joshua noticed something else very much distinguished about this man: he wore a mask! Now this didn’t freak Joshua out but rather have him more curious as to whom was underneath that accessory and when Zechs came face to face with Joshua ,and the young rook at the time swallowed hard so that he didn’t get sick from nervousness to the point where he would end up throwing up! “Good afternoon Joshua” Zechs began “I was watching your little exhibition over with the troops and you seem to have impressed Noin quite a bit.” Zechs told Joshua before realizing the error of his ways “Oh my apologies sir. My name is Lt. Zechs Marquis but you may just call me Zechs. Would you like to come with me for a little walk Joshua? Zechs asked and Joshua ,although a little shaken at first by the presence of the man that is referred to as the “Lightning Count”, decided to follow Zechs to wherever he decided to take him. Boy was Josh in for a surprise... It turns out that Zechs had been watching Joshua close for while during the time Joshua wrestled for the federation and noticed that Joshua’s ring attire was a little...”plain” I guess is how you could put it. Zechs took notice to this and decided that it would be best for Joshua to have an outfit very much similiar to the outfit that he wears in the military(including the mask) made just for him! Joshua of course was blown away by the offer that was made to him by this man whom had just seen him in action but a few seconds ago and now he wanted him to have essentially a duplicate of the outfit that he wears! “Zechs I...” Is all Joshua could say for about 20 minutes before he finally took Zechs up on the offer and a couple of days later Zechs presented the outfit to Joshua personally on a live IWF broadcast that had fans talking for the next two months and beyond! It was rare for someone to present someone with an award or anything on IWF television but Zechs was as genuine as anyone could be when he presented it to Joshua with Lucrezia by his side. That night some people swore they saw all the demons being exorcised from Joshua’s body as he made this new friend and was given such an honor! Back to the present... “You love that outfit don’t you Ami?” Zack asked as he watched his “adopted younger sister” run her hands up and down the sleeves of the outfit in a trance. “Yes Zack I do. I love the fact that Lucrezia and Zechs trust Joshua with the honor of wearing this outfit and that Joshua is the one wearing it. If another man ended up with this outfit in their possession it would probably get burned and therefore disgraced....well ok many not ALL people would do that but I know that a vast majority that would be ignorant to his legacy would disgrace it.” Ami pointed out and that is when Zack had to come to the defense of a certain “Superstar” in GW “What are your thoughts on KPJ? I think if he was the one to carry on the legacy then Josh would have no objections would he?” Zack asked and the younger sister thought long and hard before answering her good friend. “Joshua considers the jacket sacred Zack. If someone is going to wear it he wants that person to honor the originator of the outfit. I don’t think that KPJ is THAT good a friend yet for Joshua to hand the outfit over to bvut considering that he is one of the few people that Joshua confides in outside of the Connection he’d make a good candidate.” Ami explained as the front door opened and then closed with a male voice being the first thing heard from downstairs. “Hello? Anyone here? Joshua called out as he began to walk up the stairs to his room when he saw the light on and he quickened his pace to see Zack and Ami in the closet looking at the outfit that his friend Zechs gave him. Well hello you two.” Joshua said with a smile as he knew he caught two of his confidants with a piece of himself in their possession. “Hey bro!” Ami called out as Joshua walked up to her and kissed her on the cheek and did the same to Zack before walking over to the outfit and became somewhat hypnotized by the sight of the outfit for a little while before he looked at Ami and Zack and began to speak again. “Zechs is a good man...a bit misunderstood but a good man. Zechs cared about Noin and his sister enough that he would rather be killed by his so-called friends than let his relatives die. Zechs told me when I was at the base that he wanted me to end his life one night when I saw him in a never ending sob that scared a lot of people but he confided in me a lot of things the night before I was given this outfit. If Zechs were to die tonight at least his legacy as the “Lightning Count” is being carried on by a friend and the people that truly cared about his happiness will be there to gather in his memory. The difference in people like me and Zechs is that I questioned myself a lot and he didn’t. Zechs knew his ability and was ready to show it to his opponents when they thought that they were going to get the upper hand against him. Zack I know that you are suffering from a flu so I won’t aske you to compete with me in the Gauntlet but Ami how about you?” Joshua asked and Ami readily accepted the inivitation as she went to go find the outfit that Lucrezia made for her days later and it was set...the connection has been re-established! Au Revoir!
