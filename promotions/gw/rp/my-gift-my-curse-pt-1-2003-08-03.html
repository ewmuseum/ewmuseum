My Gift;My Curse pt.1

3 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My Gift;My Curse pt.1 Christian Wright Global Wrestling Promo #511 Date: August 3rd, 2003 Location: Home sweet home I came home early, everyone was tucked into their beds, everyone had fallen deaf to the sound of the earth. I opened the door as the sun shined bright that day. I walked in trying to hold my composure. I felt like I wanted to scream and jump for joy but again I held my composure. As I looked around for any sign of life, there was my dog Skip. Skip sat there looking up at me with this look in his eye, yea he had this look of....hunger. But what did I care, he was the first sign of life and I had to share the news.... 'You won't believe what just happened! I got it! I am the newest member of GW! Thats right GW!' I looked back at Skip and he still had that look on his face. 'And you really don't care. You just want some food.' I stood up disappointed that no one who really cared was up. I looked down at Skip and pet his head. I went into the cabinets and began to look for his food. I found the bag and I began to pour the food into the plate. I knelt down next to him to place the food on the floor for him and he quickly began to chomp away at it with his tail wagging left and right. 'Riiiight.....now your happy.' I sat back down at the kitchen table and kicked my feet up in the air on to the table. I must have dosed off because by the time I came through, I moved my feet off the table and there my father was sitting drinking his coffee and reading the New York Times like any intelligent man would or so he thought. There was my mother, with a smile on her face as she gazed into my eyes. I smiled back at her. 'Your out of college now, where are the jobs!?!' My father quickly snapped at me. I looked at my mother and she just simply rolled her eyes back. 'Dad I just got done.' I snapped back at him. He now put his paper down and looked at me. He never liked to be answered back so quickly. I knew what was coming, my mother knew what was coming. 'Well you sitting on your behind isn't helping the situation. Its time you got out there in the real world and found something for yourself. You don't expect to live off of us for the rest of your life do you?!?' My mom quickly jumped into this. 'My little boy can live off of us until he is damn well and ready to be on his own.' She looked at me and gave me a wink and a smile. He turned to her and gave her this look. The look that basically tells you, you've spoken too much and now it is time to be quiet. My mom was an old fashioned house wife who did as she was told by her husband. One thing I hated about her. She did as he said. She turned around and walked to the sink and began doing the left over dishes from the other night. He continued his ramblings... 'You see me, I am 55 years old, not getting any younger. I am not going to be here forever and when I am gone, I want to know my son is set in life to do as he pleased.' I put my head down, because the thought made me think about just that. Would I be set in GW? Would GW complete the life that my father wanted me to live. I knew I was good enough to be there but how long can a wrestler achieve greatness. Then I thought to myself, in everything in life greatness comes and goes in the end its whether you were proud of what you did in your time is what mattered. Nothing would make me more proud then to go day in and day out putting on the show and hearing those fans cheer for me, me! I was proud of being accepted to GW, but would GW be accepted by those around me? I figured it was time, it was time for me to tell my father, the man who had these out of reach expectations for me, that I would be set, I would reach the highest expectation, I was going to be the man he never was and always wanted to be. 'Well I need to tell both of you something. Mom if you could sit down. I want both of you to hear this. I found a job. I found something that would have me set for life if done correctly.' Their eyes opened wide. I saw the druel running down he side of my father's lips as he knew that I was going to be the success he always dreamed to be. I was going to bring the money and glamour that he always dreamed the family name would have. My mother reached out and grasped my hand as her eyes filled with joy. 'I am the new member of....' Just then the phone began to ring. Ring....Ring...Ring....Ring...Ring One right after the other, usually the answering machine picks up right after the second ring. My family liked to wait and see who it was especially if it was an unavailable on the caller id. Not this time. The phone just rang and rang. I paused. Was this a sign? Should I wait and see how this goes in GW? No this had to be said now. 'I am the new member of........' My father got up still gazing into my eyes and walked over to the caller id as the phone continued to ring. He stared down into the caller id waiting for the number to pop up. 'Shit its work!' He yelled as he quickly picked up the phone. I sat back and wondering why he had to pick it up. Why couldn't it just wait? My mom looked at me knowing that my announcement had been ruined. I just looked at her and she shook her head as she squeezed my hand once more. I got up and walked away from the kitchen and began to make my way up the stairs. 'I have to go, there are going to be inspections today and I have to make sure everything goes well.' That was my dad. He had basically forgotten everything that was said and wiped everything that could have been and put on his sneakers and made his way to the door. He stopped at the door when my mother called out to him. What about your brother?' He stopped and put his head up in the air and his eyes just closed. What about him?' He snarled back. I just stood at the top of the steps wondering what was new with Uncle Lou, the one man who would know and feel the joy that I felt. 'Are you going to lend him the money to start up that wrestling farm fed?' He gave one of those sarcastic laughs of his. 'Wrestling, what a joke. You send him this message for me, get a real job!' And he stormed off slamming the door behind him. Thats where it hit me. He would never accept me or GW. I knew then that this could only be my secret. Not even the only man who could understand how great a chance this was could know. My father and my Uncle Lou had been bumping heads since they were children. If my Uncle Lou would know he would have something over my father and I could not live with myself if I was to enter in their twisted game of top this. I was going to do this on my own. I was going to be the Clark Kent, the Bruce Wayne, of my time. If I was to do this and keep my family I would have to live a double life. I put my hands in my pocket and pulled out my contract. I laid it in my top drawer and walked out. I made my way downstairs and before my mom could even get a word I had walked out. After I left my mom began folding the laundry. She had gotten all my stuff and before I left she was going to ask me to bring up my clothes. She did it for me. She walked up those flight of steps not knowing what was to be waiting for her as she walked into my room. She opened my top drawer to put in my jeans and as she looked down, she saw the letters. The only letters of the alphabet that meant anything in the world to me.... ...GW...
