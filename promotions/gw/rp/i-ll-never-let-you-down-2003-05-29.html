I'll Never Let You Down

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/29/03 He sits with Salaok in Salaok’s office. He wears black jeans and a black The Nevermore shirt and a pair of black boots. Salaok sits at his desk, all clean and dressed up for work. They begin to speak? “I bet you are wondering why I called you here.” “Yeah, just a little bit.” “I just wanted to pat you on the back. You made it. The finals for the Heavyweight title at Dead Zone against Brick. It must be a dream come true for you.” “Dream come true?” He chuckles? “That is an underestimate. This week I have gone through some of the greatest things of my whole entire life. Being in the finals for the Title Competition. And finding something I’ve been searching for’for quite some time.” “And that is?”” “Just something Lisha and I were talking about.” Salaok nods his head? “Heart to heart conversation then, huh?” He nods his head? “Yeah.” “Anyways, I just wanted to give you a few pointers at the Main Event.” He nods? “Sure thing’it can help.” “The first thing is, don’t walk in there afraid. Brick has experience, he has been in the Global Wrestling for many years, he is probably one of the most experienced wrestlers in all of GW. He won’t be afraid at all or intimidated. He is bigger then you and stronger then you. You, you are smarter and more powerful. I have been in the ring with Brick before, he is all tough, he doesn’t think’you take advantage that way. You can’t walk in there with any mercy at all for him, because he will chew it right up and spit it in your face.” He nods his head? “The second thing is to give this match everything you have. This match, it means more to your career then any other match in the world. Brick is a tough opponent, and this is your time to shine Sage. Don’t hold anything back at all, bring all you have, and then some more. You deserved this title belt, and at the end of the match, it is going to be yours. Have some self-esteem, pat yourself on the back, you are the GW International Champion’and don’t forget that. Brick has not held a title in the GW for quite some time. And remember, you have been in the Global Wrestling for only a couple months and you have won the Platinum, Cruiserweight and International title belts, Brick has been in GW for years upon years and he never had the opportunity to have the Heavyweight Title around his waist. And don’t let him ever have that opportunity. This is your match, and in the Hell in a Cell, nobody can stop you from doing anything to Brick. Not Jesse Briggs, not Ely Braxton, not anybody. Hand him his ass’for yourself, for me, and for The Nevermore.” My New Family The thoughts run through his head? I’ll Never Let Them Down “I understand.” “And the last thing is, you have beat Brick already. You have him afraid and shaking in his boots. He has studied every move you make and every breath you breathe. Brick is a veteran and he knows what to do and how to do it. But remember Sage, you beat him already because he has watched those videos and watched what you can do to your opponents. He must be sitting at home right now sitting in a pool of his own sweat worrying about this match. Who wants it more Sage” You or Brick, that is what it comes down to. And when I look into your eyes, I see determination and I see desire. And I know, deep down in my heart, that I am glad’damn glad you are one of us now. A Harvester by the hard work that you have given. The Harvester of Tears is what you are. Now walk into Dead Zone and make Brick shed every tear he has’just to win that damn title. I’ve done something wrong in my career by not holding it, bring it home to The Nevermore Sage.” I’ll Never Let Them Down “This is your time to shine. This is The Nevermore’s time to send a message to everybody by having you be the Heavyweight and International Champion of the World. Sage, don’t give up.” I’ll Never Let Them Down “Because I know’win or lose, you’ll never let us down.” I’ll Never Let Them Down “Thank you’words of wisdom, experience and knowledge’something I needed. Thank you, I won’t let you down.” I’ll Never Let You Down His head raises towards Salaok’s eyes’words of encouragement at the least. Pushing him, further and further to reach his goals. Expanding his dreams and pushing his limits to be something. Dead Zone’a match to remember. A match he will never forget.
