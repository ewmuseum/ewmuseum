Return to Regis and Kelly, pt. 2

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/3/03 The scene fades in on footage from the closing moments of Brick’s match at Whiplash. Brick finally lets go? West: Here is your winner and the NEW GLOBAL WRESTLING WORLD CHAMPION, BRICK! The referee hands Brick the title. Brick looks at it and then cradles the belt close to his body with tears of joy. Sights: After four long years, Brick has finally done it! He’s finally reached the top of the mountain! Bishop: No one said he could do it and he did it! I admit I was one of them, but I got to tip my hat to him. He’s earned it. Brick slowly gets up and is handed the title, Brick looks at it and staggers to the entrance of the Pit, holding the World title in the air. Fans: BRICK!! BRICK!! BRICK!!! The image then freezes and dissolves to Brick, Regis and Kelly. “And we’re back with the new Global Wrestling World champion, Brick. That was a very emotional evening for you as I can imagine, just from looking at that footage.” “Yeah, and those were tears of joy, OK?” “What?” “No, “cause I remember what happened the last time I was here, you were gettin” on me for blushing, so I just wanted to get.” “Well, tears of joy are acceptable,” says Kelly. “OK, now describe the match itself, the Pit.” “The Pit.” “That just says rough match, doesn’t it” The Pit!” “You just wouldn’t get the same feeling if you called it, say, a tickling match.” “No, it’s one of our most brutal matches. The Pit itself is fifteen feet below the arena, surrounded by 8-foot thick concrete walls. The floor is solid iron. Only way in is two four-foot thick iron doors, locked by a remote computer in the GW Arena. The computer also drops weapons into the pit at random.” “And they don’t count to three like in a regular match?” “No, the only way to win is either by submission or knockout.” “Well, with an iron floor and concrete walls, I can imagine somebody’s gonna get knocked out or they’re gonna wanna cry uncle.” “And I imagine it’s pretty hard to get that kind of win off of you.” “Very hard.” “All that for a title?” “Yeah, and it was worth it.” “Well, GW Evolution airs Monday nights on fX. Check your local listings. Brick, always a pleasure. And don’t be a stranger. Drop by, anytime, World title or not.” “OK, I will,” Brick says as he shakes hands with both Regis and Kelly. “Brick, ladies and gentlemen. Cooking tips from Nigella Lawson when we come back.” The audience applauds as the scene dissolves to footage from Beyonc? Knowles? video for “Crazy in Love” and the announcer saying “Friday on Live: R&B sensation Beyonc”.”
