Making New Friends Part 3 of 3

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/18/03 (As the scene fades in we see Sean Price walking down the metal steps towards many of Salamone’s men, all waiting anxiously to be given the order of either welcoming or ending Sean Price. A few feet behind Price lies Don Salamone himself. As the two reach the bottom of the steps Salamone places his arm around Sean’s shoulders and then introduces him to two of his men.) **Here it is, now I will be meeting the men that will hopefully become my friends. The guy’s that I will count on to watch my back when I am out doing a job, and the men who’s back I will be watching when asked too. This is the family that I want to work towards becoming a member of. ** “Sean, I would like to introduce you to some of the members of the family. This here is Frank, he takes care of the books and watches over the money that comes and goes for us. He is my oldest friend, like a brother to me.” (The man standing directly infront of Sean puts his hand out towards Sean for a hand shake. The man looks to be about 60 years of age. He is bald ontop with gray and white hair on the sides of his head. He has small circular glasses on his face that go over what appears to be an above average sized nose.) “Nice to meet you Sean. I have heard some good thing’s about you.” “Oh really” Do tell?” “Maybe later Sean. I am sure in the next coming weeks you and Frank will have plenty of time to talk. This next guy is one of the toughest guy’s I have ever met. His name is Paulie, but around town he is known as the Predator because of the way he shadows his prey before taking them out. This is the guy that you should get to know the best because he and you will be doing a lot of work together I can assure you of that right now.” “How ya doin Paulie?” (Sean extends his hand for Paulie to shake but instead of returning the favor Paulie backs away from Sean. He takes a hard look at Sean before turning around and walking away.) “Don’t mind him Sean, he is like that with everyone new. It’s not just you. So don’t take it personal.” “I don’t care who he is like that with. People will show me respect or I will show them a beating.” (With this Sean takes the Don’s arm off his shoulder and quickly walks towards Paulie who is still walking in the opposite direction. Sean yells out Paulies name repeatedly but Paulie ignores him. Sean then moves faster towards Paulie and shoves him up against the car with his back towards Sean.) “You gonna stop this boss?” “No Frank. I want to see what this kid has. That goes for the rest of you guys. No one interfere.” (Price grabs Paulie’s arm and wrenches it behind him and then with his other free hand Sean places his open hand against Paulie’s head and shoves it into the hood of the car.) “You wanna disrespect me do you” Paulie the Predator. Please, your nothing more then a facsimile of people’s imagination. You are nothing special, no bigger than me or anyone else in this room. You think that because you have put a bullet to some people that makes you better than me? Please, I beat down people like you every day of my life.” (Paulie somehow manages to maneuver his way out of the hold that Sean had on him. He pushes Sean away and then reaches inside his coat pocket and pulls out a Colt 1911 and points it at Sean’s head. Sean doesn’t flinch at all and instead walks into Paulie’s gun.) “Go ahead tough guy, pull the trigger. Your such a bad ass why don’t you pull the trigger and get me over with.” “Ok gentleman that is enough. Paulie put the gun down. Sean back off him.” (Both Paulie and Sean comply with the Don’s requests. Sean then sticks his arm out again to offer Paulie a hand shake.) “Let’s try this again.” (Paulie stands there for a moment and then finally reaches out and shakes Sean’s hand and then smiles.) “That took some ball’s Sean. I like that. I’ll be happy to work with you.” “Good I’m glad, makes my job that much easier.” “That’s nice, you boys are becoming friends. Now cut the mushy bullshit, Sean I have to introduce you to the man that will provide you with any type of heat you need. This here is Vinny DeDucci.” (Vinny is a man with a large build. He has dark black hair, much like Sean’s. His face is a bit stubbly as if he has not shaved in a few days. Vinny has a very noticeable scar above his left eye and another on his right cheek. Vinny extends his hand to Sean, before Sean shakes it he notices that Vinny’s hand is wrapped in a white cloth that is partially red.) “That’s right Sean, anything you ever need you come to me and I will provide you with the best. You can bet your life on it.” “Well let’s hope I never have to bet my life on it, but thanks just the same.” “Alright Sean, that’s enough for right now, lets get you cleaned up, a new suit perhaps.” “What the hell is wrong with the suit I have on?” “I like this kid. I like him already.” (The scene fades out as the Don again places his arm around Sean’s shoulder, the two men begin laughing as Sean, Frank and the Don head towards their car to take a ride.) **Well there you have it. I have been introduced to the family. They know me and I know them. Now the true test begins.**
