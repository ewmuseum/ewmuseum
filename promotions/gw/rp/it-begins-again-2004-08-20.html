It Begins... Again

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/08/04 It Begins... Again The scene slowly began to open in a vast desolate field. Every inch of life that could have been there neglected to exist... every element of plant-like form that should have been growing was nowhere to be seen. Two people filled the picture, standing at either side. One of the figures had their arms crossed... the other had their arms down by their sides. The expression on the faces of these people though; were nowhere near to being as different as the rest of their structure. The fellow on the left of the picture could be identified as the returning GW athlete, Rich Sherwin... the other was an unknown individual. He stood tall and proud, slowly bringing his arms from his sides up to a fighting pose. He stood ready and looked across to Sherwin, who didn't budge an inch at the movement of his enemy. The only thing stopping the deathly silence between the two was a gentle breeze that blew through; picking up little bits of dirt. '... Don't think you're going to be able to toss me away as quickly as your boys did. Don't think you're going to get rid of me with so much ease. This time, the timing is all different... and as you can see, I'm nowhere close to being the idiotic human that I used to be.' Sherwin called aloud, the sound of his voice causing a confused expression to appear on the face of his opponent. What exactly was the former Television Champion talking about? Was it even possible for somebody to throw away their humanity and be something different? Sherwin slowly began to walk the distance toward the other individual; his arms still crossed over his chest. The other person took a couple of paces back, prompting Sherwin to stop moving. 'What the hell are you talking about, Sherwin?' called the other guy in response. He looked around himself a few moments before continuing, '... You're a human and you're going to stay a human until the moment that you die. When we threw you into that fucking cesspool of a sea, you must have got lucky and escaped. You must have been REAL lucky to get out of that situation... but hey, looks like your brain didn't move ahead from that serious predicament. Walking straight out of the jaws of death... looking for another way to pass into otherworld. Are you so desperate to die that you had to seek me out... and then challenge me? Didn't Juan Gonzalez knock some fucking sense into you the moment he made you drive over that cliff? Didn't the whole point of this situation settle in when you LOST to Gonzalez?' Sherwin looked down to the floor for a few moments, the words of his enemy passing through his mind like pieces of a puzzle. He tried to fit them together... but nothing came of the complex spiral of words. Nothing pieced together in the way that they would for a normal human. Everything seemed too far-fetched to make any actual sense. Nothing appeared to be realistic. '... Escaping from your little trick isn't something that I was able to do, Guerrera. No, I died on that night... when you tossed my breathing body into that... fucking cesspool of water. Unfortunately, I seemed to drink a little too much of that water and it got into my lungs. Don't ask me how... perhaps it was the handy little attachments that your boys gave me?' Sherwin joked; his tone of voice still as serious as it could be. Rich began to walk towards Guerrera, his pace beginning to quicken with every other step. As it appeared, Sherwin looked as though he was determined to do something... perhaps for the first time in his entire career. With the human elements of fear and concern thrown aside, Sherwin was able to look toward the goal with nothing in his way. Nobody would stop him from extracting his revenge and when he went back to work, nobody would stop him from becoming the best thing ever. 'You really don't want to be doing this... not when the FAMILY is about!' Guerrera screamed; forcing Sherwin to stop in his tracks and look around the immediate area again. Within seconds, all kinds of vehicles could be heard flooding the area. Out of view appeared one vehicle... and then another followed, with another following that. The sounds of the roaring engines became louder and louder as the vehicles came even closer. Rich looked bewildered as everything poured into the scene, encasing both him and Guerrera in a tight circle. 'I told you that you didn't want to be doing this, Sherwin. Did you honestly think that I would have come to this place alone? Did you really think that I would trust somebody like you? I mean, I'm the guy who had you sent down... and you just wanted to have a nice little chat? No way, buddy. I don't do these pathetic little chats with shit like you. Instead, I make sure that anything that needed to be finished... actually IS finished. Now, with these kind words out of the way, I believe you're going to be very busy with a few things. Farewell, you unfortunate bastard.' Simultaneously, multiple doors began to open on the vehicles and people dressed in black suits began to appear. Eagerly, they removed themselves from the vehicles and encased Sherwin in an even tighter circle. Rich stood his ground, preparing himself for the battle that was to commence. Looking around, he made a mental note of the number of people that had poured forward from the cars to take part in what was supposed to be the extermination of a single being. Overkill perhaps? Nobody knows quite yet... even if the odds are ALL against the almighty former TV Champion. 'Heh... I guess nobody was kidding when they told me that you like to go all out for the most difficult of problems. I appreciate the attention... but not that much.' Sherwin joked, looking to Guerrera and flashing him a quick smile before returning to the circle of impending doom. 'Mr Sherwin, I know how much you desperately wanted to finish this game with me... but you know? I'm not quite finished with you. Should you be fortunate to survive this encounter... note survive and not actually win, do take note not to come into my path again. Next time; I might just make sure these men extinguish your fire completely.' Guerrera said, a smug grin appearing across his face as he turned and began to walk toward one of the vacant vehicles. He got into the back of one of them; one of his men coming to the front and getting inside. The engine started and the motor reversed out of the circle, performing a 180 degree spin before starting to drive away into the distance. Sherwin tried to charge forward but the circle got a little tighter around him; this signalling the intention of all the men involved in this brawl. 'Where do you think you're going?' one of them said with a cocky tone to their voice. The individual stepped forward from the crowd and began to approach Sherwin, walking around him in a circle. The reborn GW athlete remained still; neglecting to move or show any sign of being intimidated. True, this was the first time that Rich had such horrific odds stacked against him... but at the same time, this was a time where Rich wasn't your average Rich. He'd evolved beyond the standard state of a being. He'd been improved, enhanced and generally upgraded with the latest in universal technology. The same suit walked toward Rich from behind and placed his left hand on Sherwin's right shoulder. Immediately following the movement, Sherwin's right hand shot up and took hold of the suits left hand. He squeezed effortlessly, the man letting out a shrill scream as the compressing pressure on his hand started to become too much. 'I don't have time to be dealing with you subordinate scum. Not only do I have a very important job to return to... but at the top of it all, my beef is with your employer. If you want to try your luck against me... then that would be a wish of your own BUT, I'm not going to be held responsible for anything.' Sherwin muttered, the situation clearly becoming boring for him now. Were his words something that contained complete truth? Or, was he simply trying to psyche out everyone around him with a few vile words. Granted, the whole concept of fear through words would be something that may work for the man... after all, he's apparently come back from the dead. Sherwin released his grip on the left hand of the suit immediately after saying his piece, the injuried individual charging back into the circle, clasping his hand the entire way. Three men immediately stood forward, all of them wearing identical clothes... the only difference between the three being their size and weight. The first of the three looked rather slim... the second looked overly muscular and the third was a little larger than the average boy. Rich shrugged his shoulders as each of them cautiously made their way towards him. It was apparent, for now, that they were intimidated by the story of their enemy. '... You can't fuck with us!' screamed the smaller of the three as they dived into the battle; Sherwin spinning around immediately to greet them. Upon making the turn, Sherwin scored with a huge punch to the gut of this fool before attacking the winded enemy with a huge right hook that send him down to the floor... seemingly completely out of it. The other two that were leading the charge looked at each other in disbelief at the ease in which Sherwin had destroyed one of their allies. 'Like I said...' Sherwin began, his voice starting to carry a significantly sterner tone, '... it doesn't have to be this way. You don't have to lose your lives because of the actions of your boss. I'm going to give all of you the chance to walk away from this... but I warn you, if you choose to stay behind; what I did to THIS guy will only be the beginning of what I do to you.' Looking at each other once more, the two other individuals turned and ran from the scene; everyone else following them into their vehicles before driving off into the distance. Sherwin smirked as the threat of his new-found power began to stretch across the entire family of Guerrera. Rich looked down to the individual that he'd slammed down with ease; their body beginning to move as they slowly began to come around from the pasting. Rich walked over and grabbed him by the throat, holding his head up so that eye contact could be established. 'Gah? What the fuck are you doing?! HELP ME! SOMEBODY!' the freaked out suit screamed, desperately hoping that somebody from his side of the field would come about and offer some form of assistance. Unfortunately for him, nobody bothered to walk in such a direction. '... Now, I'm going to extend the same offer to you that I did to the rest of your supposed friends. Although, in this case, it's going to cost you something.' Sherwin began, the scared suit nodding their head furiously as Rich then continued, 'I want to know something about your boss. I want to know where he's based and I want to know exactly how to get to the GW arena from here. As I said previously; I have an important job to return to and I'll be damned if you pricks are the reason why I can't return to active duty. So, you better start talking now... and you best start talking well. You REALLY wouldn't like me... when I'm angry.' With his final words, the scene slowly but surely began to fade into a sea of darkness. Rich Sherwin had made his impression on this crime family and now he was looking to make an impression back home... back in the job that he'd been forced to leave following the most unfortunate of accidents. Would he be ready to score a successful return or would history repeat itself and grant the star a second losing return? There is only one way you're going to be able to find the answer to this question... stay tuned.
