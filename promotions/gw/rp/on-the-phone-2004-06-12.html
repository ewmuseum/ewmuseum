On the phone

12 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/04 'Alexis Steele, Donny?' Brick said over the phone. 'What's wrong with her? You've talked to her. She's not that bright. Yes, it does matter, Donny. I know Christina was kinda embarrassed that she thought you were trying to ask her out. Yes, she was. I think she was a little mad about it, too. Yeah, hold on, man. There's someone on the other line.' Brick then pressed the 'flash' button on his phone. 'Hello?' 'Brick, it's Aaron.' 'Hey, man, what's up?' 'Just wanted to check up with you on Ashley's birthday party.' 'I'll be there, man. You know I will. Man, she's already turning one. Still can't believe it.' 'I just wanted to make sure that there wasn't any kind of entanglements, what with work and all.' 'I'll check with Bubba and Christina if there's anything coming up, but they're usually pretty understanding when I have certain obligations. I shouldn't have any problems.' 'Good, because I know Ashley really wants to see you.' 'I want to see her, too. She is my goddaughter, after all. Listen, I got Donny on the other line. Let me click back over and then I'll get back to you.' Again, Brick pressed the 'flash' button. 'Yeah, Donny? That's Aaron. He wants to talk to me about my goddaughter’s birthday party. I'll talk to you later, okay? Bye.' One more press of the 'flash' button. 'Yeah, Aaron, so what's the plan?' 'Well, I was thinking about hiring a clown.' 'What? Man, don't hire no clown. You'll frighten her.' 'What?' 'Yeah. Ain't no one-year-old gonna wanna see a clown.' 'Well, maybe if we throw it at McDonald's...' 'Hell no, you ain't throwin' it at McDonald's! That's just ghetto! Anyway, she's only one. We can just have a small party with family and a cake.' 'That's what I told Felicia. She's the one that wanted to hire the clown and the magician...' 'Magician? I know this is your first and so far only child, but don't you think y'all are taking it a little too far?' 'I told her that too.' 'Then, man, handle your business!' 'So what did Donny want?' 'Not much. Just asked me about going over to a WMW card with him.' 'To start trouble?' 'Hey, they started this, man. They go attacking Donny and everybody else. Now I hear this little punk in WMW wants to call me out. Calls himself Umbrella, or something like that.' 'Umbrage?' 'Yeah, that's it. How do you know?' 'I...I checked out an episode of WMW Burnout once.' 'What?' 'Just to see who GW was up against. Anyway, this guy is short, but he’s got some power.' “How short are we talking?” “About Deacon’s height. Weight’s about the same, too. He’s their current Heartland champion. Isn't there some big PPV you're having this month where you face their superstars?' 'Yeah, Crossfire. He and I might just meet there. I got just the match for him, too.' 'Did Donny really ask Alexis Steele out?' 'That's what we were talking about when you called.” “Let me guess. You weren’t too receptive to the idea.” “Aaron, you’ve seen her on commentary, man. You know she ain’t that bright. Can you imagine her on a date?” “Now, Brick, you’ve dated a few stupid girls in your time.” “What” Name one.” “What about Mimi?” “Oh damn, I forgot about Mimi. Yeah, she was stupid.” “Listen, I gotta get back to planning this party. I’ll talk to you later.” “OK, bye.”
