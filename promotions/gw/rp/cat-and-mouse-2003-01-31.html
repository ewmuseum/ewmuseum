Cat and Mouse

31 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31 Ya know how sometimes you’ll catch a glimpse of a chick in passing and think she’s pretty hot, but then when you finally get close to her under closer inspection she’s got a lazy eye, split ends, stretch marks, and a dead tooth? Well this is certainly not one of those cases... She sits snaked across the open space in between our stools looking me dead in the face, resting her head against the fist of her arm that’s laying on the bar, batting her eyes repeatedly insinuating a long wait. Up close and personal she’s just as beautiful and striking as she was amidst the sea of alcohol filled bodies on the dance floor if not even more so now each and every one of her lack of imperfections can be even more closely observed. The way her reddish brown hair frames her smiling face and her sparkling green eyes glow in the moonlight could instill courage and bravery in even the weakest and most cowardly of men. It can rob the most daring of those same virtues... My eyes grow wide staring into hers, well, not so much staring as randomly scanning the surrounding area before locking back onto them in a nervous panic. “Don’t worry, I won’t bite... much” she whispers to me in a breathless voice before offering a smile and retreating from me space. A faint smile forms over my face at hearing this, but quickly fades away due to my nervousness. She grabs two of the shots from off of the tray, her fingers deftly maneuvering around the two glasses as the lifts them into the air and hands me one of them. “Here, this should loosen you up” she says, her voice rhythmically lilting as she does so. “Yeah...” I reply chuckling nervously “Bottoms up.” Looking each other dead in the eyes, after knocking our glasses together we both simultaneously down our shots before planting them firmly on the bar face down. She doesn’t even pull a face, but then again... neither do I. Without a pause in between she immediately reaches for the second set of shots and doles out my half of the round which I graciously accept with a nod. Once again we clang our glasses together before slamming our shots and planting the overturned glasses on the table. Those two shots were just what I needed to catch a full on buzz. Now feeling a little more relaxed, the corner of my mouth curls into an uncontrolled smirk brought on my the surging levels of alcohol coursing through my veins. She takes note of this and responds with a smile, knowing that she’s caught me. “So what’s your name cutie?” she asks me while sliding my long island iced tea across the counter. I freeze for a moment, looking up to find that she has turned her stool to face me and is leaning against the bar sipping her drink. It should be against the law for anyone to be that fine. If she were the spokes model for mass genocide, Nazis would rule the world... Finally regaining my wits I slowly begin the daunting task of mustering enough courage to reply to her inquiry. Looking back into her eyes I stammer “Da... duh...” before she sees fit to take me out of my misery. “Deacon?” she interjects. A sigh of relief is expelled from my body with that social yoke lifted from across my neck. Although the temporary break from the stress of this encounter will most likely be a short one. “Ye, yeah, Deacon Holmes, that’s the one... ha... how’d you know?” I sputter out in short controlled bursts. “I’ve heard of you before, and, well... the whole three hundred person “Welcome Back Deacon!” that rang out when you go here kinda made it hard for me not to know who you are.” she replies before taking another sip from her drink. “From what I’ve seen on tv you’re quite the wild one, let’s say you and me get wild on the dance floor.” she continues, closing the sentence with a suggestive wink. With her drink in one hand and my hand gripped firmly in the other she slides from the barstool and onto her feet as she slinks across the deck and onto the dance floor. Once there she begins to wine her hips while grinding her body rhythmically against mine. The friction caused by both of our rapidly gyrating, semi clothed bodies generates enough hate to cause both of use to develop a slight glow brought on by perspiration. As we continue on in this fashion, drinks in hand, and showing no signs of slowing down I can’t help but wonder how much longer before one of us catches fire. “Slow Jamz” by Twista is mixed in through the speakers as the last song is faded out, causing her to adjust to the less hastened pace of the beat. She stands up leaning back against me, gently sucking and licking on my neck while continuing to grind against my pelvis with both my hands deftly exploring her body as I follow her ass as close as humanly possible. The combination of her gently biting my neck and the smell of her hair is driving me crazy... Eventually our cups become empty which is cause for yet another trip to the bar. Once there we set our empty coconuts on the bar and signal for a refill as we slide onto a pair of barstools. “I never caught your name...” I inform this still nameless beauty that I have no other choice but to refer to as “Her” Brushing her reddish brown hair out of her face she replies “I never threw it...” before shooting me a wink while enticingly sipping her newly acquired drink, her soft, full lips enveloping the straw as she draws alcohol into her mouth through it. I now wear a mask of confusion in response to her last response, but it soon changes to one of curiosity. She’s different. Every woman I’ve met since blowing up has been somewhat stars truck, too eager to please me. But not her, it’s like she doesn’t even care. I like it... Growing a little braver as the alcohol begins to kick in I lean closer to her and whispering in her ear inquire “Well then how would I go about catching it?” in my best seductive tone... one that doesn’t get much use due to the relative ease with which I usually pick up women. Her hand planted on my chest she whispers back in a throaty, seductive tone “Catch me.” She sounds almost breathless as she blows the words into my ear while running a finger in and around the curves of my pecs and abs. I am once again dumbfounded by her response. Disoriented and bewildered I blurt out “Catch you!”” not turning, thinking she’s still sitting at my side. Then catching a glimpse of her sliding away from her stool I quickly slide off of mine and onto my feet before quickly whirling around asking “What do you mean catch you?” expecting her to still be near me. As I whirl around to face the pool deck and dance floor instead of finding her standing in front of me smiling I bump right into the man who made all of this possible, and probably the only friend I’ve ever known, John Zero. I’m just not in the mood to talk with him right now... “Hey D, how’s the party going?” he asks me. peering over his shoulder while scanning the myriad of people inhabiting the deck and dance floor in a panic I hurriedly reply “It’s goin great, man.” Hoping that was all he wanted to ask I start to take a step past him but get held in place by yet another inquiry. “You like the place?” he asks, partially noticing that I’m not exactly paying attention. “Yeah...” I quickly inform him before catching a glimpse of some red hair moving through the crowd. Hoping it’s her I lean slightly to the side in order to get a better look without John in the way only to find that it isn’t her. “It’s great” I add, now standing on my toes while scouring the dance floor. John begins “Hey D I’ve been meaning to ask you...” before being cut off mid-sentence by me as I quickly raise my hand into the air in a sweeping motion. 'Listen John, I would love to chat, really, I would, but you see, I've got some business?' I plead with him. Offering up one of his patented blank stares while cocking his head to one side John utters but one word. “Business?” ?'Yeah, some business of the utmost importance that must be handled, post-haste. So, if you will excuse me for one moment...' I explain to him. Then, motioning to the bartender I exclaim “Get the man a drink!” That said I make my way past John and tear off into the crowd. Desperately searching for “her”, I rifle my way through the vast number of people attending my combination welcome home and housewarming party. Finally I make it through the crowd and to the front of the house... and there I see her. Off in the distance I see a chick hopping on a motorcycle, I look a little harder and realize that it’s her. You don’t forget an ass like that... The panic I feel quickly subsides when I realize that I will easily be able to catch her in my car... which just happens to be blocked in by the vehicles of several party goers. “Well ain’t that about a bitch.” I grumble to myself as I prepare to do something extremely stupid... Zero has a hidden driveway which dips into a tunnel and then resurfaces right near the only road that leads to his estate. The driveway is hidden by a wall of densely planted high shrubs that give off the impression that there is nothing there. Behind these shrubs is a brick wall that outlines John’s property, one that I intend to jump in an attempt to head her off at the pass... What can I say? I love a challenge... Looking out over the vast lawn that I’m preparing to tear across as fast as my legs are willing to carry me, I draw in a deep breath, allowing it to fill my lungs, causing my chest to swell before I slowly exhale that very breath knowing I’ll be gasping for it soon. At the very instant at which I expel the final bit of air passes through my pursed lips I shoot across the ground like a bat bursting from the depths of hell. With the moon high overhead shining down to light my way, I wring every ounce of speed I have from my body causing the drops of dew that have formed on the grass to be flung off in a myriad of different directions as I pass. The wall grows closer and closer as I continue to push myself farther past the limit. I don’t think I’ve ever run this fast in my life. And I’ve been shot at before... I’m burning fast twitch muscles I didn’t even know existed as the wall grows closer. Once within range I spring into the air as high as humanly possible using each and every bit of my forty four inch vertical with my momentum still carrying me toward the wall. Just before colliding with the mass I quickly plant a foot on it and push off then do it once more with the opposite foot in order to gain the height needed to reach the top of the wall. With my hands clamoring against the edge of the wall I press both my feet against the wall while bending at the knees then push myself up using the extra strength to get on top. Damn I never realized how high this thing is... Perched atop the wall looking out over the road I see nothing more than uninhabited pavement. No cars no nothing, not even some kid on a punk ass bicycle getting an early start on his paper route. An overwhelming feeling of failure falls over me as my shoulders slump. Maybe I wasn’t up to the challenge... Just as I begin to take a seat on the ledge and prepare to drop back down into the yard I hear the dull roar of an engine growing closer as it echoes. I look over to the opening of John’s driveway and am delighted to find a single headlight breaking free from the darkness followed by the piercing roar of a 900cc Kawasaki ZX-9R Ninja. The neon green and silver abstract designs covering the street bike cause it to stand out against the night like a sore thumb as it eases it’s way up and out from the tunnel of the hidden driveway before roaring onto the street. Now comes the hard part... As I stand to my feet I realize that I’ve forgotten one crucial detail. The shrubs. The dense wall of foliage stands a full foot higher than the brick one it conceals and is pushed away maybe a foot or two. This same wall of foliage I now have to clear with no room in which to gain momentum. Quickly I take a half step backwards, mainly because I don’t have room to do much more, before lunging forward and springing into the air. At the height of my jump I tuck my legs in order to avoid clipping the branches below and being dragged down prematurely. Now comes the even harder part... With my arms flailing wildly in the air I rapidly plummet toward the ground. The impact between my body and it is unforgiving as it knocks the wind from my body and leaves me on my hands and knees heaving. It takes everything I have to lift my head up in order to gauge the distance between me and the fast approaching bike which shows no signs of slowing down. Apparently she wants to make this as hard as possible. And now for my next trick... I muster up my final bit of guts as I climb to my feet and prepare to do something incredibly stupid... With the bike rapidly approaching my position as it rockets down the road I begin moving out closer toward the center of the street and jump into the air with her only a few feet in front of me. Ducking down is all she can do to avoid being knocked off of her bike which allows me to clear her and land behind her sitting back to back. We each look over our shoulders at each other with a smile as she clinches down even harder on the throttle shooting us down the street so fast the sound barrier should be calling any minute now out of breath begging us to let it catch up. Then it hits me... I have no clue where we’re going.
