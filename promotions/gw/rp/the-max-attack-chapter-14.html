The Max-Attack: Chapter 14

Date: Today The scene opens up to the clear sky in the windy city of Chicago. Both Roswell and the Blue Bat are staring at each other. The Blue Bat quickly grabs a little ball from in his pocket and throws it straight at Roswell, but Max dodges it with his feet kicking it down to the ground below. Max dives at The Blue Bat hitting him with a kick to the jaw. The Blue Bat starts bleeding from the lip. The Bat suddenly dives the other way and out of sight leaving Max confused. “I wonder what happened to him” says Max. Roswell jumps back down from the air with Ben and his mom surprised at what they just saw in the air. Max straightens his clothes out from the dust. “You can fly?” asks Ben. “Well its kind of temporary” says Roswell. “What does that mean?” “Well it means that I have no clue where I could fly from and I don’t know when to use it” “Ok” “Well thank you for finding my sweet Ben” says Ben’s mom. “No problem” Ben and his mom walk away into the hotel as Roswell goes and sits down on a brown wooden bench. A couple minutes later Max gets off the bench and starts walking slowly down the sidewalk. Max walks by a dumpster and sees a man sleeping in ragged clothes. “Are you alright?” asks Roswell. “What?” yells the man. “I said are you alright” “What do you think. I live in a dumpster!” yells the man again. “Sorry” Roswell walks away from the dumpster with a weird face. Roswell looks up at the Sears Tower and sees a couple windows shattering. Glass falls everywhere as the people in cars and on the sidewalk start screaming for their life. Max sees a man dangling another man over the top of the building threatening to drop him. The man drops the other but Max jumps into the air catching the guy and putting him safely on the ground. “Are you hurt?” asks Roswell. “I’m alright” says the man. Max goes behind the building and climbs up a fire exit to the very top. Max jumps and kicks the man over the edge of the building making him fly through the clear sky. The man quickly pushes something that he is holding and starts flying back up using nothing. “So The Blue Bat has come back for more” says Max. Max looks away but when he turns back around is kicked in the face by The Blue Bat. Max dives at The Blue Bat but misses him and flies over the edge of the building. The Blue Bat looks over the edge of the building but doesn’t see Max falling to his death. Underneath the edge of the building Max is holding on to a piece of cement to keep him from falling. Max flips up onto the top again and pushes The Blue Bat into a door and locks the door. Max starts kicking the door with his speedy feet making holes in it. The Blue Bat sticks his hand through one of the holes and unlocks the door without Max knowing. “Max, I’m over here” says The Blue Bat. Roswell turns around but doesn’t see The Blue Bat. Max looks back where he was standing and gets punched in the face. The Blue Bat reaches into a little pocket and grabs one of his explosive mysterious balls. He throws it at Max but Max moves and it hits the door but it bounces back and hits the floor. A ring of fire starts around Max and The Blue Bat kick Roswell straight in the throat. Max falls to the ground as some of the fire gets on his suit. Roswell’s suit indulges in flames as The Blue Bat leaves. Suddenly Roswell awakes but doesn’t feel none of the flames. Roswell lays there and suddenly hears some kind of song in the air.
