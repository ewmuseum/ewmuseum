Talk with Christina

12 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/12/03 As Christina McCoy sat in her office, she perused the screen on her computer. Internet wrestling sites were all abuzz about it. Everyone was talking about the brawl between Brick and Deacon Holmes at a recent GW autograph signing. Although both men were placed on opposite ends of the autograph signing area, Holmes still managed to get under the champ's skin when he set a stack of Brick personality photos on fire while encouraging reluctant fans to boo the champ. When Brick commented on the brazen young star's behavior he replied 'If you don't like it, BEAT ME!' Never one to back down from a challenge Brick set out to do just that and the two men began an all out brawl right then and there. The fight ended quickly as both men were soon separated, but the autograph session was none the less rescheduled to eliminate the potential for another confrontation. Needless to say, Christina was less than pleased about it. She had been working hard to keep the company running in Bubba’s absence and this recent blowup was a black eye. Suddenly, the intercom buzzed. “Yes, Roberta?” “Ms. McCoy, Brick is here to see you.” “Send him in.” The door opened and Brick walked in, carrying the World title on his right shoulder. “I know I ain’t got to ask what this meeting is about.” “No, you don’t,” Christina replied, turning the monitor towards him. On the screen is one of the various websites that covered the story about the brawl. “Look, Christina?” “No, Brick, you look. I’m used to the wrestlers fighting in the ring. Hell, I’m even used to them fighting backstage, but what I’m not used to and don’t want to get used is wrestlers getting into fights at publicity events.” “Hey, you want someone to blame for this, blame that little punk ass fire starter, and I don’t mean Ben Pailthorpe!” “Oh, I plan to have a talk with Deacon?” “Good.” “But you’re not totally free of blame here.” “Look, you know how he gets to me.” “So why do you keep letting him?” “I didn’t do anything nobody else would have. You think I’m the only cat in this company that wants to beat his little monkey ass to a bloody pulp” Hell, his boys in T.U.E. probably want to beat his ass every now and then.” “Never mind the rest of T.U.E. You’re supposed to be setting an example for this company.” “I helped build this damn company, Christina! I know what my responsibilities are!” “Then worry about your match with Mr. UK and forget about Holmes.” “What do you think I’ve been trying to do?” “Try harder! Now, I think we’re done here. Now I have to straighten Deacon out.” “Please, do that,” Brick said as he began to leave. “Or I might do it for you.” As Brick left, Christina sank into her chair and sighed.
