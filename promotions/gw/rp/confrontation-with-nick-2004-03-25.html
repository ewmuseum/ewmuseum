Confrontation with Nick

25 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Confrontation with Nick Mark Makonie Global Wrestling Promo #1044 Date: 3-25-04 Location: PPV rp 1 Makonie is sitting down in a room in a steel chair. There is a steel table that is in the middle of him and Geoff. Mark is sitting there looking into the eyes of Geoff as he looks like he is about to croak. His skin is pale white along with his white hair. His teeth are stained yellow from smoking for so long in his life. He just looks like a ghost. He is sitting in his wheelchair as him and Mark are talking about something. “How are things going around in your life?” Geoff asks as he looks at Mark. “Well the wrestling thing is going ok, there are somethings that I could really deal without there.” Mark starts to say. “But I have to stay in the federation to try and pick up some more publicity for us. “Yea, I’ve been hearing that you have been teaming up with a black.” “Unfortunately yes. But it was not my choice.” “Then what are you doing it for?” “I need some protection in the fed and so did the tag team that is in the stable. They asked the fuck to join after I did, so I could not drop out.” “If I were you, I’d be watching my back every step of the way.” “I am.” Geoff coughs hard, as some blood spills out from his mouth. He grabs onto the table as he coughs some more. He spits out onto the table red liquid from his mouth that would be blood. Mark gets up and he goes over to where Geoff is leaning onto the table at. “Are you alright man?” Mark asks as he leans his head down to see if Geoff is alright. “Yea, this stuff happens all the time. No big deal.” Geoff says as he gets himself upright in his wheelchair.” There is one of the members of Mark’s group that runs by the glass window in the room. Mark sees him out of the corner of his eye and he watches the closed door. There is a pound at the door and Mark walks over to the door, as his feet hit the cracked cement laid out on the floor. Mark gets to the door and he puts his hand on the steel door knob. The teenager is standing there in black pants, black leather boots, and a white t-shirt. “What do you want Nick?” Mark asks as he looks into his brown eyes. “What is this I hear you are in a group with a damn black dude?” Nick asks as he looks Mark into the eye. “Why don’t you just stay the hell out of that?” “Well you preach to us that they are bad and we shouldn’t even make contact with them.” “Nick you don’t even know what the wrestling business demands. I’m getting more publicity being in the MOB and then that will get us more members in the group.” “Well how would you like it if I made contact with a black person?” “Well if you smashed his face in, then I would love it, but if anything else then I would be pissed off.” “Exactly” Nick says as he turns his back away from Mark. “If you don’t fucking like what I’m doing then you can kiss my ass.” Mark yells. “You might not ever see me again.” “If I do then your going to fucking regret it.” Mark slams the door as he seems to be pissed off at the situation. He has his mind on the rumble right now and not the people who care about what he is doing since he is the leader. He looks over at Geoff as he is laid face down on the table. Mark rushes over to the table and he puts his head down to see if Geoff is still living. “Geoff are you alright?” Mark asks as he puts his upper body up in his wheelchair. Geoff doesn’t respond as Mark freaks. He bolts out of the room as he goes to his car to get his cell phone. He starts to dial 911 as his brain gets off the rumble subject and on the subject at hand which is keeping Geoff, his leader, alive.
