motm3-fortunate coincidences [of jd lawson].

21 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Wednesday - july 21st, 2003 I’m still breathing, and here’s another evening. Dial up the number, wait. It rings and rings. And then finally an answer. 'Hey, it’s Reed.' I held the nearly destroyed piece of paper in my hand, reading the number over as I listen. The paper’s been sitting in my pants pockets for the past ten days, since I picked it up from Jack a week from last Monday. I didn’t find it until it had already gone through the wash. Luckily the number was readable, or at least readable enough. 'Yeah, I can.' Hearing, but not really listening. It’s just small talk anyway, the same stuff everyone else goes through every conversation. Instead, busy fumbling around my pockets. Pull out a pack of Camels, only three left. That’s good news in one-way, and bad news in another. It means that I haven’t even smoked one pack in the last three days. But, it also means that I’m only going to have two cigarettes left. Pull out a stick and bang it against the table that’s holding my coffee. Pull out a small box and strike a wooden match off the roof of my mouth because it looks cool. Stick the match into the coffee to kill the flame, and take a puff. 'Okay, on my way.' I wonder what happens when you only hear one part of the conversation. Does it intrigue people? Does it make them want to hang around you all day just to find out what he said? Hang up. Finish off the sulfured coffee, leave with cigarette in mouth, keys in a safe place and money in wallet in pocket. Destination is about a mile from here, to Ronny’s apartment. I take another puff of the cigarette and begin my workout-walk, an ironic setting. It’s the right time for a walk because the sun is still up, but on its way to setting. So the air’s fairly cool, and despite the humidity I can dig it. I wonder what Jay is doing right now. I wonder, because I tried to call him yesterday, and do something with him. He said that nothing was wrong, but he wanted to get some stuff done. I’m kind of worried that something’s going on with Robson, something that I’ve never seen before. He was telling me that he needed to get some work done, that he wanted to train for Master of the Mat 4. That’s not him. If anything, the most training Robson would ever do for a match is see how many people he can out drink the night before. It has me worried about myself though, too. I haven’t trained for anything in years. In fact, one day not too long ago, I made a list of things I haven’t done: -lift weights. -do sit-ups. -do push-ups. -run for five miles. -spar. -punch a bag. -anything else involving some strain or effort. Maybe I’m taking Master of the Mat too seriously right now. But what if I’m just not taking it seriously enough right now? Maybe that is what Jay figured out for himself, that this is the big event, and it’s the one to put him back on top of the sport. A world title shot would be nice, as well as a once-a-year-only kind of trophy. Now I’m wondering what would be wrong with giving this tournament an actual shot. I’m trying to remember who all is in it. -people from TUE. -people from the Youngbloods. -couple others. Is that what it’s about? If someone like Deacon takes the Master of the Mat, does that suddenly make TUE the betters? So, if Jay won it would that give YB the advantage? I guess it’s all about the struggle for power, and for bragging rights. Studying Sean Price, I’ve found that he’s heavily into bragging rights. Being around Jay Robson, I know that he’s heavily into bragging rights. Only know a little about Ronnie Long, it would seem that he’s a good fighter, but that’s about all I can imagine. Maybe I’ll go into the arena tomorrow, work out. Not like I want to show Jay Robson up, wink wink, but he can’t go around pulling pranks on me without a little revenge. If he can beat me in mind games, maybe wrestling is the game that I play victor over him in. I don’t want him to start beating me in that now too, nothing against him...but I have to have something on him. He’d be on my twenty four-seven if he picks up a win, and that I know is true. Because it’s not like I’m into bragging rights, I’m just into bragging rights. Understand? 'Trey? Hey...what’s going on?' 'I need a ride, if that’s cool.' 'So...you walked over here to ask me for a ride?' 'Yeah, to the airport.' 'Why couldn’t you just...call?' 'I had to get some stuff off of my mind. And I don’t think a ten mile walk to the airport carries that much thought.' 'Okay...do you need to head out right now?' 'Yeah, let’s head out.' Grabs his jacket, key cars and a cola. He drinks soda like I smoke cigarettes, so it’s not something I can argue with him over. It goes like “your teeth rot” vs. “your teeth rot worse.” It’s like “that stuff messes up your stomach” vs. “that stuff messes up your lungs.” And so on from there, so we don’t bother. Instead, we hop into the 1986 Blue Festiva, and make our way towards the airport. And just think, once I get to the airport, I’ll get to wait two hours for a delayed flight probably. Talk about time to think. Do you think it’s weird to think about thinking? I do it a lot. ---- 'What took so long, mon?' 'Flight was delayed two hours, go figure. What’s going on?' 'Well, Lawson’s gotten a little impatient waiting up for you mon. But he sounded pretty interested when I told him your story.' 'Interested?' 'Yah mon, didn’t you hear?' 'Fill me in.' 'Basically, JD is starting up another corporation since JDTV died out. He’s calling it the Avarice Conglomerate, and it’s going to be an international promotion ring of wrestlers. But, it’s not all about wrestling mon, it’s about doing what it takes to make the Conglomerate succeed. And most importantly, it’s about making money mon.' Well. I’m not a greedy guy. But the fact is I joined GW for the money. I tried 360 for the money. I’m still living off of paycheck to paycheck. I’m back to walking on foot since my Beatle’s been stolen. Think about it, am I really going to say no to a chance for making more money than I owned through my entire FWF run? The answer is no. 'Hey JD.' 'Wow, you come in here without a joint in your mouth, I’m surprised.' 'Yeah, I’ve chilled on the whole smoking marijuana non-stop thing, you could say.' 'Yeah. So what are you doing here?' 'Jack told me to come check you out.' 'Oh yeah he told me how you’re struggling to survive out there.' 'I manage, somewhat.' 'Holding bums at gunpoint for change doesn’t sound like a good life.' 'Uhh,' I look over to Jack. He throws me a thumbs up and I sigh. Go along with it, I guess. 'Yeah, I’d say it isn’t.' 'Well I like a scrappy attitude, how’d you like to represent GW for the Avarice Conglomerate?' 'Yes. Yes I would.' Done deal. Small talk and small business talk follows. Fly out of here tonight, back home. Maybe with the money JD is supplying me, I can think about buying a weight bench. Heh, sounds good.
