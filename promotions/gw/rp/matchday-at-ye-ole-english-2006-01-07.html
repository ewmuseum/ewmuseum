Matchday at Ye Ole' English!

7 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/07/06 Prelude: So here we are, Global Wrestling, we return once again. After years and years of memories on the rollercoaster that has been Shane's life, we have learnt so many things and have witnessed the change from boy to man, from rookie to legend. But legends to stop there in the mind of the Eternal one, legends continue to breathe a different air that makes them different to the rest of us. Shane's path from PWA through the greatest wrestling organisations known to man is still going strong after a long ex-cruciating absence. Shane's love for the wrestling game had never stopped and still grows as time goes by, still driven by the Wethermaniacs across the world that have stood by him through everying. They have stood by him through the Eternal Wrestling days when he was Framed for an expired visa, when he was attacked and planted with a bio-chip from the evil owners of EW towers. The Wethermaniacs continue to feed the force that is Shane Wethers, giving him the drive to succeed like no drug known to man. Time took Wethermania to PCW, SCWF, $W and Prime where injury ruined his chances of reaching his full potential. But now after a lengthy absense, he has now returned in top form and ready to stake his claim once again THE BEST athlete to every be known in professional wrestling. Now as he takes up the wrestling role in GW he is ready to face anyone that is put infront of him, as nothing will ever stop Shane from success..ever..again. Its a cold Saturday morning in early January and nothing but grey clouds cover the gloomy british skies, the sounds car tyres making splashes and squelching sounds as they roll along the wet tarmac can be heard in the distance. In the middle of the small city of leeds lays the very foundations of a public house called 'The Peacock' where Leeds United supporters gather on matchday to either watch the game if its at home, OR to watch it on the big television in the Pub. The place is surrounded by white shirts as they gather in a pre-match atmosphere, talking to one another about the upcoming game. At the end of one bar sits Shane Wethers, sat comfortably on a pub stool gazing into his pint of John Smiths ale. He belches out loud before looking at the barmaid. Shane: Another pint please Dawn, when yer ready. Oh..and a packet of scampi fries aswell. Dawn smiles at Shane, Dawn: Ok my love. She takes a pint glass from underneath the counter and walks over to the beer pump. She grabs the beer pump with her young hand and begins to pour the silky smooth ale into the glass. She is quite young, yet legal to work behind the bar. She stands with her blonde hair tied back and a tattoo of a rose on her right shoulder. Meanwhile, at the side of the bar sits Leachy, a local punter who spends more time at the pub than he does at home(hence the name 'Leech-y') He a scruffy looking fella with a crew cut, an earing and tattoo's up and down his arm. He puffs away on a cigarette like he hasnt had one in years and then takes a huge gulp from his pint. He wears a white Adidas t-shirt and a pair of jeans, nicely unmatched witha pair of mucky black boots. Shane: What's the matter Leachy? yer lookin a bit stressed out. ::Leachy nods his head and takes a huge drag from his cig:: Leachy: Me' grandad died last week. I'm gutted. ::Shane sighs and takes a drink from his new pint of bitter:: Shane: A'm sorry ter' here that matey, but if its any consolation... I lost my Grandad in Macdonalds when i was 12. ::Leachy looks at Shane with interest:: Leachy: He died in Maccy D's? wow that must have been a bastard. ::Shane shakes his head:: Shane: No, i just lost him. He ran off with Ronald Macdonald ter' get an autograph and never came back, never trust a f^cking clown. ::Leachy shakes his head, agreeingly. Dawn listens in as Shane continues:: Shane: But my other Grandad died at the age of 93 making love ter' me' Nan. ::Dawn looks on with a loving smile:: Dawn:: Awww bless Leachy: What a way to go! ::Shane nods his head:: Shane: They used ter' do it on a Sunday morning cos he needed the churchbells ter' keep his pace. ::Shane imitates the churchbells by swinging his hips in time to the chimes:: Shane: 'Ding!....Dong!' ::Leachy and Dawn smile, Leachy picks up his glass for another drink:: Shane: Me' Nan said he'd still be alive today if the ice cream van didn't come round the corner. ::Dawn shakes he head as Leachy almost chokes on his beer whilst laughing. Leachy clears his throat and changes the subject with Shane:: Leechy: So Shane, you looking forward ter' returning to the ring this Sunday? ::A cheshire cat grin appears on Shanes face:: Shane: Oh i am! eighteen nobodies in that ring all tryin ter' match-up ter' me! I hope each and everyone of them realises who they are up against cos A'm bring the whole shipload of Wethermaniacs with me on Sunday and we aren't leaving until every single one of those pickledicks are on their asses! all of a sudden the match begins as the camera's fade. ----------------------- ADVERTISEMENT: Nineteen men....one ring....one winner! Global Wrestling kick starts 2006 with a match that will defy the ages! Shane Wethers faces a tough task as he returns to the ring for the first time in over 12 months to face former legends Carl Ca$h, Tim Walden, Chenzo Stecchino and many many more! Don't miss GW's Reload, this Sunday! live on SKY SPORTS 1! ---------------------------------- (Later) :;The camera's return to Shane who's Narh merrily singing along to the Leeds united anthem:: 'Marching on together! we're gonna see you win! we are so proud! we'll shout it out loud, we love you LEEDS! LEEDS! LEEDS!' ::Shane turns to Dawn behind the bar:: Shane: I'll have a Jack Daniels please Dawn, make it a double..A'm celebrating after a fantastic 1-1 draw at Wigan. ::Leachy turns to Shane:: Leach: Good win Shane, don't yer reckon? ::Shane nods his head:: Shane: Of course it was! Wigan are in the league above us and are doing damn well. Ter' get a draw away against wigan, its a good result! ::Dawn returns to the bar with Shane drink, she puts it infront of him:: Dawn: Sounds like a good win then. It'll be two out of two if you win the gauntlet tomorrow at Reload... ::Shane interrupts:: Shane: WHEN i win Dawn! get it right will yer! ::Dawn looks at Shane with a sheepish expression:: Dawn: Sorry Shane. Shane: It's alright Dawn. Don't you worry... it'll be 2 out of 2 tomorrow...no doubt. Just you watch!
