Mr. Cash, you need a sedative (pt.3)

18 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.18.03 There seems to an endless number of stairs, but Ace doesn't care. They're his only way out of this hospital, and he's grateful he found an exit. He runs quickly down the stairs. What the hell was wrong with that nurse? WAR PAINT!? Ace couldn't wait to get out of that hospital and be back home. He'd just get a cab as soon as he got outside and he'd be gone. The fresh night air feels great on Ace's face as he opens the exit door that goes out into the alley. 'Thank god!' Ace screams as he gets a running start out of the alley and towards the main street. As he breaks out onto the street, a nurse stands, just getting off of her shift. 'AHHH NOOO!!!!' Ace lets out yet another girly scream when he sees the nurse outfit. It takes him a moment to realize that this isn't the same nurse. 'Oh my god, are you okay?' She asks. 'Yeah..I'm good..' Ace says through panting breaths. 'I just thought you were somebody else. Damn near gave me a heart attack.' 'Oh..I see. Hey, you're kind of cute.' She says, inching closer to Ace. 'Your girlfriend is lucky.' Ace takes the time to look this rather attractive nurse up and down a few times, before a smile comes across his face. 'Well, she would be lucky. But she left.' Ace says. 'Ohh...' She replies a smile on her face, 'How unfortunate for her.' 'That's what I tried to tell her.' Ace says, definently liking this nurse. A hell of a lot better than the other one, that's for sure. 'Well..you know, being a nurse doesn't give me much time for my...personal..needs.' She says, provactively rubbing Ace's arm. 'Oh my! You work out, I see.' 'Actually, I'm a pro wrestler.' Ace replies. 'Mmm, a wrestler, huh?' The nurse says, now moving her hand to Ace's chest. Ace is really feeling this situation, but he sees a cab driving and he jumps out to the street and hails it down. Ace jumps in the backseat, and leans his head out to the nurse. 'Call me!' Ace yells, before turning to the cab driver. 'Thank god, man. Take me to 1400 Ma-' 'AYAYAYAYAYAAYAAYAYAY!' Lisa screams as she opens the door of the cab and injects the driver. He passes out immediately, and Ace jumps out of the car, running down the street. The sound of screeching tires as the car peels out after him only makes Ace run faster. He looks back to see Lisa driving the cab with her head hanging out the window, a syringe gritted between her teeth. She takes it out from her mouth and throws the needle in Ace's direction, he barely ducks it. Now, not only does Lisa have a utility belt full of the syringes, she also has a shoulder strap of them like a soldier would have bullets. Syringes break all around Ace's feet and fly past his head as the car speeds towards him. Ace ducks into another alley, where the cab cannot fit and runs down that. 'My god...this just isn't my night' Ace says to himself, continuing his run. 'When I get my hands on the TUE, they're all gonna get seriously messed up! AND I HOPE THEY BRING THEM HERE with Pochahontas...on crack.' Ace continues his jog down the alley, seeing the end of it leads to another street. Right as he's about to exit the alley, Lisa screeches to a stop in front of it with the cab. Ace turns to run again, but it's too late. He hears the syringe as it flies through the air and makes contact with his right ass cheek. 'OWWW!!!....ugh..' Ace screams and grunts, collapsing on the pavement. Lisa gets out of the cab, and stands on the hood, pounding her chest like a caveman. 'OWWGUHH!!! OWWWGUHH!! AYAYAYAYAYAAYAYAYAYAY!'
