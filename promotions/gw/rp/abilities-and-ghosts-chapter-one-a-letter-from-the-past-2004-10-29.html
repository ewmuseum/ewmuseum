:: Abilities and Ghosts Chapter One: A letter from the past ::

29 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/10/04 He was aroused out of his sleepy reverie by the sound of wind, forcing the rudimentary balcony door to rattle loudly against the side of the wall. It took Winters a couple of minutes to get himself together, whereupon he finally came to the realisation that he never even left the balcony door open. Immediately he got to his feet, his senses immediately alerted. He glanced around and saw nothing. If it was those guys with guns he would’ve known about it by now. They wouldn’t have tiptoed around, they would’ve come in guns a blazin?. Cautiously, he made his way to the balcony and attempted to shut the door, but immediately his senses were alerted and instinctively, he threw the top half his body to the right. He felt the arrow cut through the air, just inches away from his neck. Arrow neatly sliced into the back wall. Winters had a good look out of the window, but could see nothing and no one, which automatically meant that he was dealing with some kind of professional. But what kind of professional would just leave his target still breathing? However, that would only be a puzzlement if the perpetrators intentions were kill him. He considered the prospect for a second, whilst quickly shutting the balcony doors behind him. He made his way over to where the arrow stuck delicately out of wall. Winters the letter attached to the base of the arrow and ripped it off without finesse. He opened the piece of paper up, and when finished simply found the sentence of : “I’m back.” in rather large lettering. Who the hell was back? Winters thought to himself. Someone from his past? Perhaps someone from his past had found him again. Perhaps?
