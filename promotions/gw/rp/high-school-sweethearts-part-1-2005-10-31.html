High School Sweethearts Part 1

31 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/2005 Halloween 2005 On a night where little kids run around dress as something they wish they were getting candy. Fred Grest was training for his in ring return. Running on the tread mill sweat running down his face and back wearing his ring gear. He knew he had to be ready for Elia Smith because he knew after the stunt he pulled he'd better be ready for anything. Plus the rumors running wild that Elia Smith just doesn't have both ores in the water anymore. With that in mind he's throwing out her recent losing streak which could possibly have something to do with Elia's mental unstableness. Grest can after all relate to that. He wasn't exactly the pillar of sanity when he 1st came into Global Wrestling. Having nothing to offer to GW but a few memorable in his last lack luster organization. He's coming off a win in against Tessa Windsor a tremendous competitor and Pranksta's Paradise which will be one of his highlights in Global Wrestling regardless of what else he does for here on out. Grest turns off the treadmill trying to catch his breath. The Original Pranksta now more then ever was out for revenge. He was beaten down by Hunting Grounds after his win over Tessa Windsor. He's completely focused on being a megastar in Global Wrestling. The ink on his forearm now means more then ever. Hope. Its all that keeps him going. He vows that his armed will be raised in victory and a title around his waist before he bows out. He steps off the treadmill and grabs a white towel. Wiping the sweat of his face he sighs and heads up stairs. Tossing the sweaty towel in the corner He heads up the basement steps exhausted from the workout he just had completed. Grest here's a knock on the door he looks up at the small round black clock seeing that it's five. 'Damn, its a bit early for trick or treating.' Grest says. Grest walks up to the door and turning the door knob, opening it slowly. He sees a young woman dressed as Rouge. In this yellow and green X-Men spandex uniform showing her curves and topping off the custom her already red hair is pulled back with a dyed white streak going down the middle. 'Surprised to see me Fred?' The mystery woman says. 'To say the least Karen.' Fred says as he smiles standing before a pale skinned woman. 'Its been to long Fred' She says. 'What 4 years? How did you find me?' The Extreme One asks. 'I saw you on TV at Dark Rituals. Figure I’d come track your sorry ass down. I can't believe you're actually living your dream.' Karen says smiling. 'Yeah, Wouldn't trade it for anything.' He says “I see you still have a way with the ladies.” She says before she runs up and hugs Grest and gives him a kiss on the cheek. “Ewww Sweaty!” “I save my charm for the southern bells like you Rouge.” Grest says with a laugh in his voice. Give me about 10 minutes to take a shower and we’ll pass out candy to the little hell mongers.” She smirks and says “Can I join you in the shower?” “Maybe later” Grest says jokingly winking. Karen shakes her head as he heads up the steps to take a shower. “Have a custom?” She asks. “You should know better then that I have tons of customs and I don’t even need a reason to dress up. Any day can be Halloween in my book.” He smirks. “Considering how well long I’ve known you I should that better then anyone.” She smirks. 20 Minutes Later. Fred Grest comes down wearing a long brown trench coat, blue jeans and a black shirt. He’s holding a long staff in one hand and a deck of playing cards in the other. He looks at his friend Karen. “How ya doin there, Cher?” “Fine, Sug.” Karen says as she flashes another white toothy grin. “Hey since you’re Rouge why don’t I jump off the steps and you catch me?” Grest says. “Only if I can anally rape you with that staff!” Karen says. “Kinky”.. Again later? Grest says sarcastically. “Don’t worry some day you’ll find the right guy Freddy” Karen says as she plops on the black leather sofa. Grest sits down next to her sighing. “So you know what I’ve been up to what about you?” Grest asks. “I own a few stores down in Kentucky that are doing pretty well for me.” She states. “Really what kind of store is it?” Grest questions. “Novelity store”..” She says. Fred Grest raises an eyebrow and then smirks. “Gag gifts?” Karen nods. “Yes Ma’am” “I am so endorsing that!” Grest shouts. “Gah! Are you Gambit or Banshee”!” Karen says as she covers her ears. “Okay, before we go any further” You do realize we’re both dorks right?” Grest asks. “Ofcourse I do but we’re hot dorks!” She exclaims. “True especially you.” Grest smiles “Still a flirt I see.” Karen says getting up as she hears the door bell ring. “Shut up, and give the kids some candy.” Grest demands. “Oh you are so not getting laid.” She says. “I didn’t realize it was an option.” He replies. “I said I wanted to take a shower didn’t I?” She responds. “Okay but we’re still up for the staff rape right?” “Planned on it” She winks as she pucks up the bag of candy corn and heads to the door. Grest looked on as she handed the kids there candy. Seeing two young boys one dressed up as a vampire and the other a goblin. Grest looked on seeing his life changing before his eyes and for the better. He’s more determined, more focused, and more hungry then ever. Now he has his 1st love back in his life Karen. She supported him more then anyone in his quest to become a wrestler even more then his best friend Owen. Win, lose or draw though Grest knows one thing will happen at Evolution someone will be being pranked.
