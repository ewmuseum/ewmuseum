Transition, part 1

11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 11th, 2003 'Tell me, Akodo-san... how big of a star do you want to be?' In the end, it all came down to that question. That's what it had always been about. Stardom. Name up in lights, and all that. The Show. And as much as I pretended to struggle with it, I'd known the answer before she'd even asked. I had always known. 'The biggest damn star in the world, McCoy-san.' 'Should I take that as a yes?' I smiled, and switched the phone to my other ear. 'Indeed you should, McCoy-san. Working in your federation would be an excellent opportunity, and the contract you offer is quite generous. I would be honored to join Global Wrestling.' I heard a satisfied sigh from woman on the other end of the line. 'You've made the right decision, Akodo-san. I'll fax you the necessary paperwork tonight -- how soon can you be in the states?' 'I should be able to settle all my business here in about... two weeks, I think.' 'Sounds good. In two weeks, then?' 'Hai,' I said, grinning, 'sayonara, McCoy-san.' After I hung the phone up, I just stood there for a few moments, absorbing everything. This was it. The years of training, of working indy federations, dark matches, and climbing to the top of the IWL -- it was all for this. America. Home of the largest, most famous wrestling promotions on the face of the earth. There, if I played your cards right, I could be more than just a fighter. I could be a genuine superstar. But first, I had business to deal with. *** 'Akodo-san,' said the pretty young secretary, 'Yoritomo-san will see you now.' I looked up from the latest issue of 'IWL Attack!' Magazine, tipped down my sunglasses, and flashed her a billion yen smile. 'Arigato...' my eyes panned down to the nameplate on her desk, and I took in her figure on the way, 'Satomi-chan. I guess I'll have to go in and see him then, won't I? Wish me luck.' 'Ganbatte, Rokku-san,' she said, smiling back at me. I winked at her, and kept my eyes on her as I walked away, making her cheeks burn red. I finally looked away when I reached my destination -- the office of Yoritomo Genbu. Rolling my eyes at the new owner's nameplate, I opened the door and stepped inside. The spacious office behind the door looked only vaguely like it had the last time I had been there -- when Yoritomo Hari had told me about his cousin's hostile takeover of IWL Inc. Hari's pictures and old wrestling trophies were gone -- replaced with overpriced paintings and a bar. But the glass back wall still gave a breathtaking view of downtown Tokyo. The only thing marring the view was the plump, unctuous little man standing in front of it. 'Ah, Rokku-kun,' said Genbu, bowing slightly, 'how nice to see you. Please, take a seat.' I returned the bow, but not the pleasantries, and settled in one of the chairs before his desk. 'Would you like a drink?' Genbu asked, as he poured himself a cup of shochu from the bar. 'No thank you,' I replied, 'I never drink while conducting business.' Genbu laughed, and downed the shochu in one gulp. 'Very well,' he said, setting in the high-backed chair, 'what business brings one of my new company's biggest stars here, then? I hope you aren't planning to wring more money out of me.' 'Not at all, Genbu-san. In fact, I plan on taking quite a bit less of your money in the near future.' Genbu raised an eyebrow, looking at me curiously. 'Don't tell me you want stock. I had that talk with Hida Hideo yesterday.' 'Not at all, Genbu-san. I plan to take less of your money because I plan to not be working for you.' Genbu laughed -- a thick, gurgling sound. 'I had no idea the IWL champion had such a sense of humor! With my acquisition of IWL, I now own the largest professional wrestling company in Japan, Rokku-kun. No other company in the country can pay you as well as I do. So I suggest you get to the punchline, before I stop finding your joke funny.' My lips curled into a feral smile. 'I'm deadly serious, Genbu-san. I've had the good fortune of being offered employment with Global Wrestling -- you may have heard of them -- in America. I'll be signing a contract with GW within the week, and I leave for the States a week after that.' Genbu blanched visibly, clenching his jaw. 'You can't do that, Rokku-kun. You're under contract with IWL -- under contract with ME. If you break contract, I'll have you so mired in litigation you'll never work again.' My smile widened, and I know Genbu could see his own paling reflection in my sunglasses. 'It's very fortunate for me, then, that when I negotiated my last contract, Hari gave me an escape clause -- I can void the contract at will if IWL changes ownership. I imagine he added that clause to my contract because he feared you would be stealing his company from him in the near future.' Genbu glared at me over the desk, and his face began to go from white to red. 'Contract or no, Rokku-kun I own you. I would reconsider your decision, if I were you.' Scowling, I rose from my chair, towering over Genbu. 'First of all, Genbu-san, drop the -kun -- there's no affection between us, and you know it. Second of all, you AREN'T me, and that's a good thing, because I would hate to be a gluttonous little bastard with no personality. And finally, nobody owns Akodo Rokku. NOBODY. Now Sayonara, little man. I hope I never have the misfortune of crossing your slimy trail again.' I turned and walked towards the door, leaving Genbu stunned into silence. But as I opened the door, I felt a clammy hand grab my arm. I looked back over my shoulder, and saw Genbu, his pig-like face flushed red with anger. 'Don't do this, Akodo' he growled, 'or you're going to regret it. Greatly.' I snapped off my sunglasses, and locked eyes with Genbu. 'If you don't remove your hand from my arm,' I said, my voice calm and level, 'you'll find out why I'm called 'The Steel Lion'. And you'll regret THAT greatly, Genbu. I guarantee it.' Genbu blanched again, and jerked his hand away from my arm. Smiling, I stepped out into the hallway, and slammed the door behind me. Whistling happily, I put my sunglasses back on, and walked back over to Satomi's desk. 'Satomi-chan,' I said, leaning on the desk and looking into her eyes, 'it doesn't look like I'm going to be around here much any more. And I just couldn't pass up what might be my only opportunity to ask you something.' 'Hai, Rokku-san,' she said, her eyes glittering, 'what would you like to ask?' 'I was wondering, Satomi-chan... what are you doing after work tonight?' The pretty young secretary blushed furiously, and smiled at me. I returned her smile, and knew that this day could only get better.
