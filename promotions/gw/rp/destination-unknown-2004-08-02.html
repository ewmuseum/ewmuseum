And I thought Texas was hot. “Night or not, it is hot as Hades here.” It wouldn’t be this bad if I wasn’t wearing black jeans, a short sleeved black Sage Sabbot shirt, and a heavy black leather trench coat. You are probably asking yourself, “why the hell are you wearing all of that?” The answer is simple? “Stop being such a baby?” “The blasted vampire doesn’t mind the heat. He gave me these clothes to wear, he told me that it was necessary to wear them instead of my normal clothing. He actually gave me a black, wool shirt, but I traded that in for the new shirt that can be ordered on the GW merchandise webpage on the website. He wasn’t very pleased with me. “We’re almost there.” “How much longer?” “Depends on how fast you walk.” I sink my head down and we both pick up the pace. Destination: Unknown? <hp> By the time we get there, it is difficult to see my very hand in front of me. I kneel down, face to face with the little girl? “Can I help you with something?” She doesn’t say anything, she just smiles at me. “Or are you just going to smile at me.” I say jokingly to her with a smirk? She tugs on my sleeve? I look around me and there are about 5 children surrounding me. They are all scrawny and thin. I search through my pockets and pull out a half eaten Snickers bar. I hand it to the little girl and she turns around and runs off to her mother in the group of women. Her mother sees her gift and looks as if she just won the lottery. She lifts her up over her head and smiles. She sets the little girl down and the girl points at me. The mother smiles at me and nods her head, thank you. I might have just changed somebody’s life? I look around me, still children surrounding me. I search through my pockets and I pull out a lint ball. I show the children that I don’t have anything else and they bow their heads and frown. Soma looks on? They turn around and walk away? “Wait! You children come back here for a second.” The children come running back with their smiles on their face again. I give it to him. His jaw drops to the ground. He runs back to show his mother his gift. I reach inside my shirt? “Chi salubrete, forbast crete flache eche frishe.” I close my eyes and whisper to myself? I pull out a box of goldfish and hand it to the rest of the children. Soma throws back the curtain, which I suppose is the door and steps inside, I follow him. We stand in front of this giant of a man. He is probably around 6’8 and weighs over 300 pounds of pure muscle. He reminds me of Brick, but larger. He has long black hair that is braided into a ponytail. He sits in a luxurious chair that looks to be fit for a king? “Welcome Leinad. I’ve been expecting you.” “It has been a long time Nasir.” “Indeed it has.” I stand back and watch, perhaps Nasir is one of Leinad’s friends? “I welcome you to my village.” “I am honored. It looks a lot better since the last time I’ve visited.” I look at Soma in shock? “Better?” He hushes me with his finger. “What can I help you with Leinad?” “Preparation.” “So, it’s time.” Leinad nods his head? “Make sure that you are ready. Keep your guard up.” “Do you know when?” “Soon, that is all I can state at this moment. I will fill you in on details once I know them.” “Of course, of course. A beam of light from the ceiling bursts down into the hut and shines on Nasir’his arms and shoulders begin to smoke and burn. He sucks it up, into his mouth? “And Sage said, let their be light.” “And he shouldn’t be fucked with.” Nasir takes a step back from me and extends his hand around the light. I’ll see you once again.” “we turn around and walk out the hut. <hp> Walking past the children for the last time, I make buckets of fruit and vegetables suddenly appear in front of their huts.
