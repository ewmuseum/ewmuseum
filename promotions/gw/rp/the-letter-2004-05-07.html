The Letter.

7 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 7th, 2004. Stepping from my gray Camaro I slammed the door behind me and nonchalantly hopped to the sidewalk with a spring in my step as I threw open the door to this Dallas post office. My pace quickened as I reached P.O. Box 1986. Procuring a set of keys from the pocket of my tattered jeans I inserted the key into the lock and opened it. Without even glancing at the mail I had procured, I shut the box and pulled my key out before spinning on the heel of my scuffed black Chippewa ankle boots. I left the post office as quickly as I had entered trying to stay as unnoticed as possible which isn't easy for a man such as I. Everything about me seemed to draw attention in some way, shape or form. Not only was I a celebrity throughout the Wrestling world but I was also strikingly handsome with impeccable taste in clothes, albeit the latter had been disputed. Looking down at the jeans that bore a hole in each knee, my black threadbare sleeveless shirt, which was held, together by mere clothes pins, a black leather cuff about each wrist and of course scuffed Chippewa boots, I smiled. And it was then I forced myself to admit I probably did look like something out of a Nirvana pin up magazine. Humming the theme to 'Lake of Fire' by my aforementioned favorite band I was thankful that my shaggy black hair fell over my bluish gray eyes and partially hid my face from public view as I threw open the post office door and escaped from the public eye. Well…almost. It was then that a very attractive young woman in her mid twenties took a step toward me her soft blonde hair swaying in the gentle Dallas breeze as she appeared dressed for summer in flip-flops, khaki shorts and a yellow belly shirt. Her dreamy green eyes mesmerized me as I noticed her walking my way. 'Hey…Josh?' She asked in shock, as she attempted to make sure she had correctly identified me. Looking up at her I forced the corners of my lips up into a very small smile as I returned her gaze. 'Hi.' Was my simple response as she now stood directly in front of me. 'I'm Nadia.' She said with a flirtatious smile as she dug into her thin black leather purse. 'I guess you already know who I am.' I said almost sarcastically. Returning from her purse with a pen and a blank sheet of paper she presented them forward before leaning in and speaking in a low, seductive tone. 'Would you mind doing me a favor…and signing this?' She said as my eyes danced from her face down to her cleavage before finally taking the pen out of her hand. Without so much as a word I simply nodded in response as I quickly scribbled my signature on this paper. 'American Youth' Josh, Global Wrestling World Champion. Oderint dum metaunt. Handing the paper back to her she readily accepted it and smiled as she slowly began to back away, nearly ten feet away from me now she slowly expressed her gratitude. 'Thanks, my boyfriend will love this.' She told me with a cheery smile as I felt my heart sink in depression. 'Go to hell…' I mumbled under my breath as I turned away from her, lowered my head and made haste to reach my Camaro. Finding my parking spot I threw the door open and dropped inside slamming the door once I had been seated on the black leather interior. I turned the ignition and the beautiful piece of machinery roared to life as I smiled proudly. Immediately upon starting my 'ride' for lack of a better word, music hit my ears as I heard the sweet sounds of the acoustic guitar playing as 'Further' by Pillar filled my speakers. Dropping my mail onto the passenger seat I carelessly sorted through it. Junk mail, junk mail, junk mail, bill, bill…and a letter? I looked down at the letter in confusion as I twisted my lips thoughtfully. Gazing down at the white envelope I was perplexed. After all…who writes me? Quickly scooping up the letter I read the title to see the name 'Dean Busker' written perfectly in the upper left-hand corner, yet no return address was listed below. Ripping the letter open I pulled out a single piece of handwritten sheet paper and studied it thoughtfully. My bluish gray eyes quickly scanned this paper as I began to read and absorb the blue ink scrawled neatly across the page. Dear Josh, A longtime fan of Global Wrestling and a longtime fan of Wrestling in general I was thrilled to hear that you would be joining Global Wrestling, yet almost a full year later as I reflect back upon your entry into the illustrious dynasty I am quite frankly a bit disappointed. When you first joined I applauded you, as did every other fan in the arena, proud to see a man of such obvious talent competing on a worldwide circuit. It was said that you would evolve and become something in this business…something special. Everyone proclaimed that within a year you would be the greatest Cruiserweight Champion of all time and a man that it would be nearly impossible to tear the title away from. But you first struck Gold in the form of the PrimeStar title and then it seemed as if you as a person began to deteriorate. Your Wrestling skills were still as sharp as ever if not more so than usual yet as a person you began to falter. You worried about the approval of the fans and since not everyone gave you the respect you thought you deserved you decided to make them hate you. You grew braids, you danced, and you made an ass out of yourself every week. And what did it garner you? Laughter, mere laughter and you soon became the laughing stock of the entire corporation. Every week people tuned in to Evolution to watch the man known at that time as Kid Flash make a fool of himself. It's quite amusing to see others humiliated but when someone humiliates themselves in such a degrading manner it becomes a bit embarrassing. Yet every time you listened to rap music…I cheered you on. Every time you 'got jiggy with it'…I clapped away. Every single time you made the fans laugh at you…I sympathetically chanted your name in support. But then one day something happened. The amusing wannabe that was known as Kid Flash died. It was with great remorse that his career died because even though everyone hated him they knew he had potential to turn things around. So when Kid Flash died and a monster named American Youth replaced him…I could cheer no longer. Gone were the boyish ways and goofy charm that Kid Flash once had and the man that now controlled his body was a sick, depraved lunatic. I tried to cheer for you, I really tried but no matter what I couldn't bring myself to do so. I couldn't cheer for a man who went out of his way to inflict harm onto others in an attempt to end their career. I couldn't chant the name of a man who tried to commit suicide because of his emotional and possible mental instability as well. I could no longer continue to be a fan of the star once known as Kid Flash. 'American Youth' Josh was the newest superstar in Wrestling, he had broken through the glass ceiling, he had shot into the stars and his career was hotter than Gunner Addison in a thong. Yet nonetheless…I had no use for him. Sure you won the World title and accomplished your dream yet it was your arrogance after becoming the Champion that only furthered my blinding hatred for you. You screamed about how you defied the odds, you told anyone and everyone that would listen how you had done it all, you had beaten them all. You were the man…oh, bow down, for you were the man. Yet all of this only seemed to fuel your social problems even further. On your path to success you threw your girlfriend to the side, briefly turned your back on your trainer and longtime friend, Tommy Rock. And to put the cherry on top…you made fun of and almost assaulted Christina McCoy, the woman who made your career what it is today! Instead of flirting with Christina as you had in the past you proceeded to berate her, you made fun of her and then you did the one thing I knew Kid Flash would have never done…you actually superkicked a woman that you hired to imitate Christina McCoy. From gentlemen to jackass it seems you have came full circle, and unfortunately it is the loyal fans of Global Wrestling that are forced to watch your shameful escapades week in and week out. You now believe you are no longer humiliating yourself but you must look in the mirror every day and ask, 'What have I became?' Instead of thanking the fans for allowing you to make a living in this business you referred to them as mutants. Instead of thanking Christina McCoy for making all of this possible you began a personal vendetta against her. As I look at your roots to where you stand today I feel as if I have been stabbed directly in the heart. A man I once looked up to has abandoned his fans, his friends and those who made everything possible, and you have done this all in the name of Gold and Glory. My six-year-old son was once a fan of yours yet now every time you appear in front of the audience at the Global Arena my wife and I are forced to cover his ears. We are forced to do so to keep you from polluting his young mind with the 'Youth of a nation' garbage that you continually spout. Perhaps it's time to leave the youth behind and be a man. You've already become the Champion so now its time to be a man as well. Take responsibility for your own actions, stop blaming your parents for the hardships you have been forced to endure. Step up to the plate and take your just deserts. Oh, and while you're at it…comb your hair. I'm disappointed Kid…truly disappointed, and now I loathe you…oh, how I loathe you. A disgruntled former fan, Dean Busker. Looking down at this letter I stared blankly for a moment not quite sure what to make of this writing. Pausing momentarily I sighed as I let my head rest against the back of the seat with my eyelids tightly closed…and that's when it hit me…he hated American Youth. 'Oderint dum metaunt.' In Latin that simply means, 'Let them hate so long as they fear.' Let him hate me…let everyone hate me, for after I defeat the Legion this week and their heroes fall they will have no choice but to hate… …and to fear. With my eyes still closed I slowly whispered my beloved Latin phrase once more… 'Oderint dum metaunt.'
