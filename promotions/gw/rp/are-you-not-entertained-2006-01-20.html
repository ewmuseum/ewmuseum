Are you not entertained?

20 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Week Of 1-20-06 Here he was, in front of the mirror with only the black silk boxers covering him. Time had ravaged his body very little, mainly thanks to him fighting against the effects. So here he stood, looking himself over as he waited on the man to come out. For years he had struggled to gain the respect he had long felt he deserved. Other men were made into stars before the masses, other men were made into celebrities the world over and other men became champions. Jake had always occupied the background, simply a monster to help put over the superstars. Finally, years before when he was sick and tired of the politics, being passed over and being considered a man living off the name his brothers made famous he began dedicating himself to the business once again. Training, practicing and refining his on-camera skills all came to the forefront of his life. The Big Dawg was born in those months of rededication. He became a star, and quickly rose to become a four-time world champion in a long defunct league where giants had walked among mere mortals. Then, he had gracefully stepped aside and let the next generation of superstars step forward for their time in the spotlight. Now, a new challenge was needed. After years of rest, he had stepped forward into this new world. He was what his brother affectionately called a Pit Fighter. He was selected to bash and brutalize other men for the masses, prove his mettle against the best in the world. Are you not entertained? He smiled, the toothy grin looking dangerous as he saw himself in the mirror. Yes, the line from the movie seemed so appropriate to his current lifestyle. He was the modern day equivalent along with everyone else in the league, but even they were something more. They were also entertainers more expansive than a simple arena and dinner party of Rome could ever have envisioned. They were rock and roll stars? They were icons? They were giants among men once again? And now he had found his place at the top of the mountain. Through all the pain, blood and sacrifice he had found himself once again. No matter the darkness spreading all around him in his personal life, he had become the standard of excellence for the moment. The small man came in, carrying the clothes carefully on the hangars. He set them on the notches in the wall, and handed over the pants first. The black pants, with smooth silk woven inside, slid on easily gracing his skin softly. He buttoned the waist and then took the dark gray knit shirt. He pulled it over his head, enjoying the smooth cotton without a collar. He unbuttoned his shorts and tucked the shirt bottom in before refastening the button and zipping them up. Next, the black belt with silver clasp came on to help hold them up. Jake looked himself over. The hand made outfit was cut to fit his body perfectly, allowing his huge bulk to show while not being restrictive. He nodded at the small tailor, and then slipped his arms back into the black suit coat. He brought it over with a simple roll of his shoulders. There it was, finished and perfect. He raised his leg, and the tailor slipped a sock on followed by the black dress shoes. He repeated the process for his other leg, and then he stepped away. Jake walked up to the three-way mirror. He carefully checked himself over, looking for any flaw or speck of dirt. There was nothing out of place. He was ready. “Bring it to me.” The man nodded, and then stepped over to the silver Halliburton case. He opened it, and delivered the Global World Championship. He presented the belt to the behemoth before him. Jake unstrapped the title, and laid it over his shoulder. ?Does everything meet with your approval sir?? “Yes.” Jake turned around. “This is the suit I wanted. This is the suit that says to the world I am the champion.” Jake walked back to the case, and put the title away. He closed and locked the case and then left the room. He had a show to make it to, and he wasn’t about to be late.
