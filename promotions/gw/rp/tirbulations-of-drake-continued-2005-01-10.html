Tirbulations of Drake, Continued...

10 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/10/05 Tribulations of Drake, Continued? -Cold Blooded- We open right outside Drakes apartment building at ground level, this is clearly a rundown section of LA, and look up to the top floor. Pulling back through a tedious window and looking around its actually kind of sad to see how Drake lives. He has nearly nothing on the walls, no electronics, only a small kitchen table and chair. A recliner sits in the middle of his living room facing a clock that sits high up on the wall. This place is empty probably resembling how he feels inside. Drake enters the kitchen and grabs a bottle of water from inside the fridge. He makes his way to the window. Across the alley, in another apartment, a man is beating his girl friend. Drake watches on. “He’s beating her again. I could stop it, but I’m not going to get involved. Ever since Katie has been taken from me I no longer have an urge to make someone else’s problems my own. I used to jump at an opportunity, such as this one, to help someone in need, now I look at this as nothing more than, “they have their problems and I have mine.” People have said I’ve turned “cold blooded” and maybe they’re right? A part of me denies it while another embraces?” He takes a drink and steps away from the window leaving the beating to continue. In his living room, he takes a seat on the recliner and stares at the clock. ??But I can no longer get involved?” Closes his eyes. “Even as I sit here, one can only imagine what she’s going through and how much pain she’s in. I wish I could go out and find her but I’ve done everything I can do for now. Or have I” Either way my body is growing tired and I must rest. Tonight I have to make a flight; my first match in Global Wrestling is tomorrow. But after that I will return to continue my search?” We now journey into his dream? A field’an endless field of graves. That is wear this journey has taken us. The sky is red, the air is red, and it’s raining blood. Red lightning melts into this bloody rain directly after striking, making this one hellish scene. Drake walks amongst these graves not bothering to read the names. It doesn’t appear he knows wear he’s going, just keeps walking. The tombstones begin melting simultaneously into puddles of blood that dissolve into the soil beneath. Drake keeps moving for a little while longer. He stops and turns to find thousands of dead trees that also appear ahead of him as he continues onward. The trees moan as if in pain but he blocks them out. Finally he stops coming to a large void. Bloody vines dangle from the trees; it now looks like a swamp from hell. He stares to the bottom of this void as long slender arms slowly rise, the slithery black razor type nails cutting through the thick soil like scissors through paper. Bloody, black-cloaked figures begin pushing through violently and, in an instant, infest the air. Their screeching evil laughs bring Drake to his knees frantically trying to cover his ears. Suddenly, one swoops in from behind and knocks him into the massive hole. He tries to get up but an unknown force holds him down. The cloaked figures dance above him chanting in an evil manner. “AHHH!” Drake screams in pain as his wrists and ankles begin to bleed as if being cut by a blade. Out his veins and into the ground it rushes and the hole begins filling with his own blood. Faster it rises and faster he begins growing pale. He tries breaking free but he’s become too weak! Within a matter of seconds the hole is now an enormous pond but the blood doesn’t stop. Trees begin limping over, as the blood rushes by like a tsunami. It continues to flood and the trees gradually begin melting, like the tombstones, and a second later everything is silent. The blood is perfectly still and stretches out as far as the eye can see’like an ocean. Lightning has stopped and now things seem peaceful. Until? Drake rises quickly catching his breath and splashing violently trying to keep from going back under. In front of him now stand the cloaked figures watching on silently and calmly. Without warning they fly into the air and dive straight down, single file, onto Drake taking him back under? Drake wakes up in a panic. The open bottle of water bounces off his lap and spills all over the carpet. He looks around trying to catch his breath realizing it was only a nightmare. But what’s this? He looks down to see blood streaming from his wrists’and his ankles are bleeding as well! He’s speechless. Obviously he doesn’t know what to think of all this, would you? He stands and rushes out of the room as we focus on the blood stains left on the arms of his recliner? To be continued?
