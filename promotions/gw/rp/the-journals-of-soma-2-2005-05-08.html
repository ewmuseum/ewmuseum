The journals of Soma #2

8 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/08/05 Weapons can be used or caressed. The caress of death itself. The new Venus emerges from the mud while she caresses death. Teotihuac’n, the city of the gods. “Why did we have to come here Mr. Soma?” Soma looked at her with distasteful look. “I told you, call me Leinad. We are here because this your true home. It is a shame you have no memory of all the things you did here. I thought a vampyre of your power would have been able to fall into slumber and awaken with memories in tact, but like all the others, you have been found lacking.” “Look, and I told you I’m not some ancient vampire named Sasha. I’m a vampire from this time period only. I have only been one for a short time too. So please stop telling me that.” “The word is vampyre”.not vampire. The words have different meanings. Vampire means undead. Vampyres never died. They also are stronger than the vampires. Vam is an ancient word for child and pyre the ancient for light bringer. So the word vampyre means child of the light bringer?.the fallen one. We are also closer to nature our blood is pure and we are closer related to the first werewolf than the vampires. The same kind of blood in me flows through you even if you believe it or not.” She opened her mouth for a retort then suddenly closed it as she realized it would have made no difference what she said, she knew he would not listen. “Then tell me Leinad. Tell me about this great sorceress vampyre named Sasha Sadim.” Soma looked down on her once again annoyed with her disbelief. This was going to be harder than he thought. It reminded him of Shadow. This lowly vampire had caught his attention for reasons unknown to him. He could smell the humanity, even though unlike most of her kind, it was a very miniscule amount. But still, her scent was still in his nostrils as strong as the night he kissed her. The more he watched her the more he wanted her. He realized that she could be a problem if his interest in her continued. But right now there was no time to think of her. “The first time I met Sasha, I had already given up on those that served me. I had all but destroyed them because they were not perfect as we are. I came to Teotihuac’n as a wanderer who fed on the people on the outskirts of the city”..there are other ways you can learn this story Sasha.” The sudden stop of the story confused her but she wondered what he was talking about. What other way could she learn without him just telling her? “How” Why don’t you just tell me.” Her voice becoming irritated. “You only have to taste my blood and you will travel back to when you and I first met. You see all of us have special abilities that the others don’t. My special abilities are that my blood can turn vampires into one of our kind, and it can restore a vampyre’s memory. The experience however is very painful in the beginning. Do you really think you can live through it?” At the thought of pain, Soma’s mouth turned to a devilish grin. “If this is the fastest way to see if I’m this person you speak of then I’m gonna have to be. Let’s go.” Soma begins to walk a path the lead to an ancient pyramid. He looks back at the girl and begins to walk again. “Follow me. We must take the avenue of the dead to its northern end. There we will prepare you for your true awakening.” They walked in silence down the avenue of the dead, their silence a sign of respect to the days of old. Those days where they ruled supreme as gods and deities. The days when the pyramid that stood before them, the pyramid of the moon. Sasha’s pyramid. They walk into the entrance, there awaiting them, a stone table long enough to fit one body. A sacrificial table to what they saw as their moon goddess. Soma had Sasha lay on the table as he pulled a small dagger from his boot. He slit one of his wrist and rested it in front of her mouth. “Feed from my wrist and you will remember. Do you accept this” Do you accept the blood of the keeper of oblivion?” “Yes. Yes I do.” She took his wrist and begin to feed, the pinch from her bite making Soma smile as he used his other hand to hold her down. The blood began to burn within her body. She stopped feeding as she felt like she was on fire. The room began to spin very fast, her eyes not being able to focus on the room anymore. Her body began to shake violently but Soma held her to the table. She could not see anymore, at least not the room in which they presently stood. The pain almost unbearable, her screams could be heard echoing through the pyramid. Suddenly, she heard voices in her head, but she did not recognize them. They seemed to be coming from deep within her subconscious. Finally, there was silence. All the universe of illustration in one single point, a dominant color, a drop of blood that looks us right in the eye. Games of symmetry, symbols, whispers of breeze, and, in front of the background in motion, the quiet, challenging figure “Goddess, there have been reports of a creature attacking farmers on the outskirts of the city. Please aid us” The sacrifice has been set. We await your answer.” It was always her pleasure to help the priest who served her. She stood from her throne, smiling at the man who was bowing before. Her beauty could not be compared by any other of their gods. Her long black hair, her crimson eyes, and the pale skin of the moon itself radiating from her. Her monks tried never to gaze upon her for to long for fear that they would be struck down. She walked out of the throne room and into the sacrificial room which was had be decorated in blood. Blood smeared the walls and stained the floors. She smiled as her bath seemed ready. She removed her gown as she looked down on a pool filled with blood. She slowly stepped into the pool and looked up to the ceiling of the room which was open to the night sky. The moon’s rays lighting the pool and its goddess. Her priest followed her awaiting her emergence from the pool. “Will you be handling this problem yourself, Goddess” “Yes I will. What is the creature?” “The only thing we know it is stronger than all of our men. Even you elite guard could stand no chance against him.” “Him, so it is a man of some sorts?” “From what we are told, we think so. Most are not sure if it is man or beast. Some say he may even challenge you. Please goddess, kill this foul creature and protect us.” She sat in her bath contemplating the news she had just heard. Could there be others like her out there? If there was she knew she would have dispose of them. She was, of course, the moon goddess. She stood and walked out of the pool, her priest having readied her armor, started putting it on her as she stood there. Her armor was detailed unlike any other armor. Her helmet crafted in the shape of the Feathered Serpent. Her breastplate had ancient inscriptions to protect her from the evil spirits. Once ready, she walked out of the temple, mounted her horse, and made her way to the outskirts of the city. The outskirts were the darkest parts of the city at night. Many creatures have come and been destroyed at her hand, this one would be no different. The closer she got to the outskirts the more on edge her horse seem to become. Finally, she came to a remote part of the outskirts where few people lived; and she could see why. The land was not as fertile. Everything looked dead, and it was from a small hut that she heard one lonely scream. The jumped from the horse and landed almost in a dead run for the hut. Her staff ready in one hand she entered the hut only to be kicked harder than ever before. The kick knocked her outside the hut. Slowly, a very tall creature with crimson eyes just like hers walked out of the hut and stood there before her. He seemed to be part man part an animal she had never scene before. The creature had long red hair that covered his face, only his eyes could be seen through it. He wore an animal skin cape that was golden brown with a mane of longer brown hair around the neck, he wore a breastplate across his dark brown skin, sandals that laced up to his knees, and a belt of a design she had never seen before. As she looked about the creature she realized her held a long bow in one hand, which is how he must have deflected all her staff attacks. But what could his bow be made of to withstand such an attack? This creature stood with the stature of a god or a king. She, for the first time felt fear; Fear that could only be felt at the time of one’s demise. But even in her fear, she attacked him without thought of her own wellbeing. With the speed and grace of a rattlesnake, her attacks were swift and direct. This man however seemed to be that much faster. It was like fighting a whirlwind, who at any time could unleash the brutal force of nature. Every one of her attacks failed her as this creature’s style of fighting took many forms. The more she fought, the more the creature seem to adapt till finally for no reason the creature stopped. He spoke to her in a language that almost sounded like her own, but a very obscure dialect. She listened as she only made out certain words. “You”.I’same.” She stopped and looked at him strangely peering into the same crimson eyes that she herself was known for throughout her kingdom. Could he be like she was? But even if he was, why was he so much more powerful. She knew if she continued to fight him she would come to her end faster than her life expectancy gave since she aged and would know death. She gave a bow to show she meant no harm and in turn it did the same. She tried to ask him his name through pointing to herself and then giving her title. He nodded, getting her point faster than she thought it would take. “Ade..” Out of the all the creature muttered it was the only thing she caught. In her own language they also used the word “Ade”, and the meaning brought awe to her face. Eternal. She did not know what to think of this traveler who said his name was Eternal. But she did know one thing. He was more powerful than her and that meant he had to be like her. She slowly began to walk back and she offered a gesture than he would understand meant to follow. From a distance he followed her all the way back to Teotihuac’n. The people stood in awe of the rather large man followed their moon goddess back to the temple. Realizing he was not like they are, the people bowed in reverence as Sasha and the traveler proceeded? That was the first time they met. After a time he learned the language of the people in the city of the gods and they found his name to be Kukumuo Ade. They saw him as a distant sibling to their goddess from another land. They saw him as a darker version of their moon goddess. His ways were not theirs. He did not simply bath in blood. He consumed it. He did not only consume it but he craved mortal blood. She did not understand and then realized that Ade was more of a title..he lives through consuming blood, the essence of life. She herself never tasted blood, as she never thought to, the bathing in the blood let her live for hundreds of years. And even though blinded, if she needed to she could walk in the sun. And to hurt her followers, she could never do that. But to live for eternity all she needed was to feed from those mortals. Temptation. mortal and immortal both have be tried And both have fallen short in the eyes of the most high She awoke screaming violently, with Soma still holding her down and when she finally calmed down he helped her from the table. She stood there in the ruins of her now ancient temple, her thoughts restored, she could not help but weep. She had succumb to the temptation of immortality and it was all because of this creature. This vampyre. Before she could say anything, Soma grabbed her by the hair, pulling her to him, he bit into her. She tried to fight him off but he was too strong. She remembered how strong as his arms squeezed her tightly. He did not stop feeding till she could hardly stand, then, as quickly as he had grabbed her he plunged his hand into her chest and pulling out her heart. She stood in horror as she realized, as soon as she remembered who she was, that life would be taken from her. “Why?”She asked Soma with her dying words. “I must feed from your heart to obtain the power you held within. They could not be taken as long as you had no recollection of them. Now you remember and now I have your heart. Thank you as this will help me a lot. Your blood held the secret to walking in the day. Now I will have that. Granted I also know, I will be blind when I attempt the “trip”, I will have the inner sight. It’s a shame I won’t have to bath in blood each time I do it as my blood works in special ways as well you know. Goodbye moon goddess.” Without even turning back, Soma began devouring the heart, feeling the new power coursing through his veins, like an addictive drug. Soma walked out of the temple as soon as the sun started its ascent “Here me now! I shall come for the rest of you! Your power will be mine!” (Quotes by Luis Royo)
