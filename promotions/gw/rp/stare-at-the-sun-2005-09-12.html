Stare At The Sun

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.12.05 09.02.05 | An undisclosed location 'For a jewelry shop, this place is pretty damn cheap looking.' The brown eyes of Markus Riley scanned the glimmers of gold and silver, walking up and down the line in deep thought. He stroked his chin in debate, as the pawn man on the opposite side of the glass simply stared back at him with little patience. He'd seen people like Markus before; so interested in looking around, but never so eager to open the wallet. Yep, that was Markus- in fact, that was Markus for what was nearly three weeks. 'For some raggedy fuck like yourself, you have some nerve calling my merchandise 'cheap'.' Markus smirked; it was almost like conversing with Gideon, the fat pawn shop owner from The Crow; hell, the man sure did have the look for it. 'I'm not raggedy,' Markus responded, actually finding it easy to chuckle at the man's heated insult. 'I just figured it'd make you feel better if someone came in here looking worse than you.' 'Yeah. Really funny. You trying to act straight-edge amuses me more, though.' Markus pouted. It wasn't his fault he was a druggie, as he made sure to point out in reply. 'Hey.. can't help it if I'm sensitive to peer pressure, as you can't help the tempation of the angus burger, you fat ooglar.' 'o_O' At that point, the man behind the counter was beet red as he stared across at the innocent look on Markus' face. Sighing, he thought it best to get things back on track. 'So, are you going to buy something or what?' 'Well dammit,' Markus shot back, 'maybe I would if you had anything worth buying.' Markus leaned in over the spotless glass, keeping a close eye on a certain ring in his sights. It wasn't pure beauty, but being the best one of the lot Markus could find, his expectations would have to be lowered. Unless... 'Don't you have anything else I may be interested in?' The pudgy man threw his hands behind his back, and smiled. 'Perhaps I do.' 'Well Jesus H,' Markus exclaimed, 'let me see it!' There was nothing more pleasant in the world to Herald the pawn shop owner than to get people into thinking they wouldn't find anything they liked until he showed off his one finest piece of merchandise. It was one that not even the wealthiest-looking people (the ones that came into the store, which come to think of it, wasn't a great standard) seemed to be able to buy, and for good reason. It was a fine masterpiece, one that should only be owned by the most deserving of people. The thought of Markus Riley being that person made Herald cackle on the inside. 'Let's see if I'm a fat bastard to him after he blows his load at this.' As he tried hard to stifle one of his loud guffaws, the morbidly obese pawn shop owner disappeared from Markus' sight. He walked with a bit of a saunter to the back where all of the merchandise he'd bought or traded had been kept. The item had used to be out under the glass with the rest of the 'beauties', but it had grown tiresome to Herald to have to constantly clean drool from the glass. Markus waited impatiently as Herald took his time finding the object in question. Finally, however, Markus saw the chubby man waddle out into sight, carrying a relatively small lockbox. 'What is it?' Markus inquired. The fat guy grinned, and placed the box on the counter. 'My meal ticket.' The box was opened, and inside was revealed to be.... Wow. There were no words to describe it. 'Hoooooly mashed potatoes, Batman,' Markus breathed out from between quivering lips. The light that poured out from the box was breathtakingly beautiful.. a sight Markus had never experienced before. 'That is the most bestest thing I have ever seended,' he babbled. Just the reaction Herald wanted. 'How... how much is that?' Herald's grin stretched from ear to ear. 'More than you want to spend, I reckon,' he winked. Markus soon understood how he was led into the trap of buying the item, and frowned. 'Bastard.. just tell me how much it is.' Triumphantly, Herald remarked the magic number; one that would make Bill Gates' nuts shrivel up at the sound of it. 'One million dollars.' '...' '...' Markus exhaled. 'Faglips.' Defeated, Markus left the pawn shop. And with that.. perhaps a new game plan was in order. 'No...' he boldly thought to himself, 'that ring is perfect... I need to get it somehow.' 09.02.05 | The Mercer Household | 9:18 PM 'Hey cuz, have you seen Markus?' asked the twenty year old World Champion. Every now and then for the past few weeks, Mindy found her boyfriend Markus leaving the house without a trace. Needless to say, along with that and the recent attacks made by the Hunting Grounds on her and her cousin Michael, she was beginning to worry. Unfortunately, Michael seemed to know just as little about Markus' whereabouts. 'Nah, thought he was sleeping already or somethin'.' With a pout, Mindy walked out into the kitchen to get a late night snack- preferably something sweet. 'What the hell could he be doing now?' she cried softly, opening up the refridgerator to pull out a bottle of soda. 'I don't get it..' she continued aloud to herself, 'what could he possibly be doing?' At that moment, she was provided a change to find out. 'I'm back,' Markus exclaimed, just over the sound of the front door swinging open and shut. He still seemed rather distressed over the overexpensive piece of jewelry he had looked at, but with the shadow his hat provided over his face, his mood was not revealed to anyone other than himself. The first thing he did was start to look for Mindy, but that wasn't too hard; she had made herself perfectly.. er... findable. 'Aieeee!' A few steps later, Mindy had leapt into her boyfriend's arms; that was all Markus needed to smile again. 'Hey there, beautiful.' No matter how much Markus said it, that remark never failed in making Mindy blush. 'Hiii..' Mindy replied quickly, gleefully. 'How are you, sweetie?' 'Eh.. I'll manage. And you?' Mindy shrugged and flashed a smile. 'Fine now.' Satisfied his girlfriend was okay, Markus gave her a kiss on the cheek. 'Where've you been, huuuh?' Mindy questioned swiftly, poking Markus in the ribs. That question proved to be tougher to answer than Markus wanted it to be. He dreamt his whole life of finding someone worth marrying; never once did he think such a thing would truly be possible. The world seemed to generate little more than the same cloned women from Hell over and over again, but Mindy... she was so innocent, and didn't fake it. Most people who watched Mindy in Global and didn't know her in real life figured her only to play a ditz on TV, but that was really her. Markus appreciated that. What he didn't appreciate was the thought of not being able to afford the one ring that Mindy deserved. And there was no way he could simply tell her something like that. 'Uh...' Markus stammered, 'I was just.. checking Best Buy out to see if they had any Global DVDs. That's all.' Mindy giggled; the look in her eyes hinted that she didn't necessarily buy Markus' story, but she wasn't going to bring it up. With so much going on at the moment, she didn't have the heart to start another argument. 'Ooh.. okies. Well, at least you're here!' Mindy wrapped her thin, frail arms around her large boyfriend; the equivalent of a man trying to hug a tree. Markus chuckled in relief, and gently hugged back. His massive arms held her close, as protective as any boyfriend would be of their partner. 'Sorry if I worried you and all,' he said solemnly, 'I don't want to be away from you more than I have to, I promise.' Mindy smiled, comforted by the honest-sounding words that Markus whispered to her. 'I know... just don't be away from me too long. Please..' Mindy trailed off for a moment to hop up and place a kiss on Markus' forehead. 'Okie dokie?' With a wide grin, Markus calmly stroked his girlfriend's face, and his nodded his agreement. 'You have my word.' At that, Mindy scampered back into the kitchen to pour her soda. As she did so, Markus slowly followed. 'Where's Michael?' he asked. Without turning away from her drink, Mindy pointed in the direction of the bedrooms. 'Prolly in his room.' 'Gotcha.' Markus turned his attention on the hallway in the distance, over to the door at the very end that remained out of the lightly overhead. 'Right...' It was no big secret that Michael's mental stability was growing steadily worse as the days passed, and for good reason. Markus tried to put things in perspective, try and judge how heartbroken he would be if someone had taken Mindy away. Alas, the thought was much more tame than the reality. The experience had to be felt for Markus to understand what his friend was going through, and that wasn't something Markus was ready for. Slowly, still, the tall man approached Michael's bedroom door, and out of Mindy's sight. From the kitchen, Mindy could hear the door open, and for a moment, the sound of music screamed back out. Oh, that I could scream And the world would stop and listen And these scars could speak in volumes But who has ears to hear? Or eyes to see? Again, I scream And then the door closed again. Mindy sighed. Once again, she was by herself. With a small pout, the sugarholic sipped her caffienated beverage before putting the bottle of Pepsi back in the fridge. 'Bugh...' once again, Mindy found herself doubting. 'What if I can't beat Dawn next week, after all? Maybe I'm just kidding myself by trying to act big and bad and stuff.' It was a legitimate thought, considering the amount of trouble Dawn had given Mindy in their prior meeting. Mindy is on dream street, as Dawn measures the Television Champion. Mindy staggers over to Dawn, who hits her with a kick to the gut... The Black Rose in the center of the ring! McFarlane quickly follows with the pin. One.... Two... DING DING DING! And in that moment, Mindy was saved. She hadn't realized what had happened until long afterwards when she noticed the Television title remained over her shoulder as she had gone back to the trainer's office, but on the inside, Mindy was grateful she had survived those ten minutes. As naive as she was, she truly thought she would have little trouble with Dawn that time around, and it took those ten grueling minutes for her to think differently. 'But you're much stronger than that now, girly... you have to be!' Arguing with herself wasn't going to help much- it only slightly took away from the sting of failure. What Mindy needed, however, was an outside opinion. Someone to tell her that everything would be okay; she would retain her championship, her cousin would get revenge on Jake Hix, and they could all be one big happy family... well, maybe that wouldn't happen even in a perfect world. If only Markus wasn't so damned busy with.... whatever was keeping him busy other than staying with her. 'What could he possibly be doing that is so important?' she asked herself. Maybe if she had the courage to ask him, she wouldn't have been beating herself up over it. Then again, either way, her thoughts would have been the same. 'He's probably cheating on you again.. I hope you realize that.' Her first reaction to the distressful thought was the only thing that came to mind on top of all the other harrowing things that plagued her brain: she sank to her knees on the kitchen floor, and against the cabinets she began to weep. The words she stated through her choked sobs were wrought with sadness and desperation; words she never thought once she would have to cry out, but in her haste to find something to scream out into the air, it was the only sentence she could come up with. 'I want my momma...' Little did Mindy know of her boyfriend who stood there, watching, listening... only helpless to Mindy's suffering as she cried openly on the tiled floor. He knew how much she needed support, and Markus wanted to give her that support no matter what... ...but in his nervousness and fear of complicating things further, he backed away. He stayed away from Mindy until she had literally cried herself to sleep in the kitchen, to which then he finally carried her practically weightless body into her room and placed her gently in bed. A kiss on the cheek later, Markus smiled. 'Tomorrow, I'm going to make things all better.' 09.03.05 | The Mercer Household | 6:04 PM Things were not better... not yet, at least. Mindy silently picked at her dinner, as her cousin stared blankly back at her. Michael cared, yes, but as Mindy knew too well, he had other things on his mind. 'That doesn't mean my feelings shouldn't be forgotten, though..' Mindy thought to herself bitterly, unable to enjoy her food laid out in front of her. 'Hmph.. I guess I shouldn't be surprised Markus still isn't around...' Almost as if on cue, that front door opened, and Mindy's heart suddenly began to race. Seconds later, Markus was standing before his girlfriend, a wide smile on his face as he looked her in the eye. He could obviously see the sadness in Mindy's eyes; and the caffiene fiend took no effort in trying to hide it. 'Hi..' he waved slowly, causing Mindy to rise to her feet to greet him. 'Hello...' For a second, Markus forgot to breathe. The tension in the air was almost too much for him. 'I... I need to talk to you alone, if that's okay.' The only thing that popped into his girlfriend's head then was the fact that there was no way in Hell this could be about something good. 'Uh... what about?' Instead of getting a verbal reply, Markus took Mindy by the hand and led her to her bedroom. 'Huh?' The moment they both entered Mindy's room, Markus slammed that door shut and placed a passionate kiss on Mindy's lips- one that spoke of only the deepest love and passion. Maybe this wouldn't be so bad after all. 'I know what you're thinking,' Markus spoke calmly, trying to restrain his inner happiness. Hopefully, his plan will have been worth it. His hands rested behind his back, not allowing Mindy to see his fingers crossed behind his back in childish giddiness. 'Crazy stoner guy keeps going missing, he's prolly either selling drugs or picking up chicks behind your back.' Markus inhaled deeply, and slid one hand into his pants pocket. Not long after, he pulled out a tiny ring-shaped box. 'But nope, not this long-haired piece of white trash.' Mindy's eyes suddenly widened, as tears of joy threatened to escape. Mindy's feelings had gone from the bottom of the food chain to the top of the mountain in just seconds. Only someone she was madly in love with could possibly have such an effect on her. 'Markus...' she breathed softly. Quickly capitalizing on Mindy's dumbfounded happiness, Markus dropped to one knee and opened the box. It was then that Mindy witnessed what was quite possibly the most astonishing golden ring she had ever seen. Her jaw dropped. 'I'm sorry if I caused any pain from you worrying about me, but I was hoping this would be more than enough to make up for it.' Markus smiled, offering the gorgeous ring to his girlfriend. Nervously, Mindy took it and examined it more closely. However, she nearly dropped it in shock from what Markus said next. 'Mindy... will you marry me?' September 11th, 2005 Well, diary, soon I will have to overcome my biggest and toughest challenge yet: Dawn McFarlane. After all the times she's attempted to ridicule me, I'm finally gonna get the chance to kick her teeth in! Isn't that great?! And what's better, is that this time there will be no time limit to save her if I decide that beating her isn't enough punishment! Joy! To think, I was once worried about having to face that girly again... blech, shame on me. But now I know I can beat her.. I have it in me to do it, I have to be strong and not let the odds get to me at all. I'm an underdog, and nothing's ever been in my favor.. so why should that change now? I'm on top of the world, but that doesn't make me the shoe in to win every single match... even if I have pretty much done that so far already, teehee! ^_^ Anyways, I've learned that sometimes yeh, life can really kick people in the boobs, I gotta keep fighting. I didn't get to where I am now by crying over everything... I need to show my strength to the world, and show them that I do deserve what I've been given, bad or good. This is my life, and what happens... well, it happens for a reason, as always... and I've figured out the reason why I'm going to win at Whiplash. It's because now... I have more than myself to win for. My cousin.. his wife and child... my family... and of course, my hunny bunny of a fiance, Markus Riley. I hope he forgives me for any doubt I've shown in him.. I'll know never to do that again! So, diary, thus concludes another sugar-inspired entry. Win or lose in a couple of days, I'll still be happy. I'm getting married!!! Shiggity shiggity shwa! <3 Oh, note to self: don't write while wearing this blasted ring. It weighs a friggin' ton, it does. x0x0 ~ Mindy ~ x0x0
