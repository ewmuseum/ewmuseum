Payback is a bitch

19 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/19/04 (Scene begins in the Drunken Leperchaun where we see the Nomad Donny J McNasty sitting at the bar with a decent size crowd in the bar tonight, Hardcore Harv is bartending of course, the two are talking about things, the Nomad is on the pissed off side about his match on Monday night, when Train pinned him for the win, but there is a look of optimism in his eyes as he knows tomorrow night he has his chance at him 1 on 1.) Nomad: Harv, you know I am still pissed off about Monday night, I mean I had a chance for the win, but I didn't see that fucking blind tag and the next thing you know, I'm the one who takes the loss. I mean it was my DEBUT for Global Wrestling. Here is the situation Harv, tomorrow night I have another chance to show the fans of GW who don't know who I am yet what I am made of. This time it's against train, but the idea remains the same. Harv: You know something Donny, here's something to consider. One way you KNOW you can beat him is to pull him into an all out brawl, toughness v.s. toughness. When it comes to something like that I don't think he stacks up against you. I seen you when you were fighting with your toughness, and you were unbeatable. I know who you are Donny even if you don't. Deep down inside you ARE a CHAMPION waiting to break out. You have won a totalk of five total championships during your days in SWF. You can do it Donny, it's time to put your mind to it and get out there and beat the shit out of this chump. Nomad: Harv I think you have sparked something inside of me, even if I do lose tomorrow night, I want to make Train an example for everyone else. I want to show my critics and fans that I am for real. That I will be a mover and a shaker. Harv: You're on the right track Donny, you should go and get ready you have a flight to catch... Nomad: You're right, I'll give you a call after the match, while your at it, make tomorrow night an all you can drink night, five dollar cover you know the drill... Harv: Alrighty chief, I'll talk to you then! (The Nomad gets up from his stool and walks towards the back and dissapears, and then the scene fades to black)
