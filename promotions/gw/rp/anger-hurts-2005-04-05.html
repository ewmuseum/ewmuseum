Anger Hurts

5 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-5-05 The storm water came blowing through the air from the lake that was just beyond the tree’s. Splashing up on to the dirt that laid beneath my feet. The pregnant clouds that hung over hear in the light blue covered sky whistled through the air, bringing the thoughts of rain that would splash down on me. The feel of the green crisp cut grass that flung on the ground twinkled between my toes as I took baby steps, gliding through the filled up earth that was in front of me. I had returned from Rome. Coming home to what I thought would be a happy reunion but that wasn’t to come. The PPV was over and the fight still raged on even in my head. Even though I did everything right I still found my faults. Walking along the misty waves that crackled against the rough rocky side of the lake, I could hear the birds passing by above me. They seemed at peace. Lie a free man that was just let out of prison. I couldn’t help but wonder what was next. Even thinking that I was two and zero in my wrestling career here in Global, I still wondered if I was good enough. Thinking back to the days that I arrived as I stood at the edge of the lake, watching the waves track upward against my bare feet. But my thinking wasn’t going to work, at least not for awhile. “Hey girl.” Turning around, I noticed it was my good friend Karla. She was an old friend that I hadn’t talk to since my father passed away. She was smart. A college student that even after she went to college, she never forgot me. “What are you doing all the way out here?” She asked away, walking towards me with her mini skirt on, waving in the wind. “Just thinking.” Turning away from her with those sad feeling words. “You seem to always think.” She answered. “I think that’s your downfall.” Karla was always a straight shooter. There was one time she was caught drinking at an underage with a few friends and me. Well, the cops came by and she kept drinking away, not really carrying for them. Karla stepped beside me, looking towards the lake that began to become a calm storm in the night. “So what are you thinking about?” She continued, tossing her hair out of her face before looking at me with her pretty green eyes. ******** ~Flashback~ “FATHER!” Running towards the window that was nearest to the front door. Back inside our old farm house before we moved to Texas. My father opened the truck door to his ancient old ford truck before slamming it shut. “FATHER?” My eyes poured out the tears as I ran for the door. “Don’t go.” It was to late. Starting the motor up, my father pulled away, leaving me in a cloud of dirty dust that tore up like an open gas line on under his tires. ~End~ ******** I turned my head toward Karla’s eyes, not showing my emotions that I was hiding deep inside. I wanted to say something to her but I couldn’t. I turned and walked away from her, walking towards the tree’s and woods that were behind us. “Come on Elia. You got to talk to someone. If you don’t then whatever you are thinking of will eat away at you forever.” She said, grabbing my shoulder for support. “It’s nothing Karla.” Shrugging her hand off my shoulder. “Why do you have to be so damn hard to please?” “What do you mean?” I could tell by her voice she wasn’t happy. But she knew or she wouldn’t be so angry. I twisted my body around to were she was facing me. The wind began to blow a little harder, making for the storm that was expected to hit us in a few hours. “I mean this. You butt in when you’re not welcome and get everyone angry.” Getting closer to her, almost right in her face. She stood her ground as my words made me even stop. What did I say? Why did I say it? God how I felt bad inside. I could see the feelings and friendship coming apart deep inside my heart. Her eyes began to change with the breezy weather of words. I wanted to say something else but couldn’t. Karla pressed against my shoulder, knocking me out of the way towards the tree’s. “Karla” Twisting around, Karla stopped before reaching the tree’s. She didn’t turn around. She didn’t even raise her head up from the ground. “I’m sorry.” Whole heartedly and passionately as she began to turn around. “You know something Elia,” Karla said, wiping away a small tear. ? Your problem is, you try to make everyone else happy rather then yourself. Maybe you should try for once in making your dreams come true first.” Karla turned around, walking away into the soon to be night as the air blew the tree’s around. Maybe she was right. Maybe I did try to hard to please others rather then myself. I just stood there, watching her fade away in the woods before I turned around and walked away.
