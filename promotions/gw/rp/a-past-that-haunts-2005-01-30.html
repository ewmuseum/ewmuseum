A Past That Haunts...

30 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-30-05 Screaming...feed me here. Fill me up again. Temporarily pacifying this hungering. ? “The Hollow” A Perfect Circle 10 p.m. I just returned home from getting something to eat. Tonight wasn’t as tough as it has been lately. A good meal is getting harder to find here in this cursed city. At least when I was working in Cleveland, I was able to not have to worry about selection. There was always something that suited my tastes. After making sure that I’ve had something good, I make sure that I have a good workout. I guess you could say that I make sure that my body is in peak condition because with the business I’m in if you become complacent and lazy then you might as well enjoy being relegated to the loser’s bracket. Where all you get is dark matches against up and coming talent that they want you to make look good so they can get on television. I’m never going to do that and I will tell anyone who asks me to do so to fuck off. I’ve been one of the most dominant men in wrestling for over five years and counting. I’ve battered and bruised and in some cases left opponents broken in the ring without even blinking. I like my ruthless manner of conduct and I intend to keep it and having the body of Adonis certainly helps bring on a whole world of hurt. First sit-ups, then bare knuckle push-ups, a little jump rope and then some weights. After that then I’ll do some martial arts. I have an old friend of mine coming to spar with me in a bit. Kendo sticks are just one of many ways to punish someone enough to make them piss themselves. You would think that I might be a little weary about using something made of wood because I am a creature of the night. That someone could break it and jab me with it and I turn to dust and am no more...well it doesn’t work like that. That’s just Hollywood’s way of making vampires look like pussies. People always come up to me with shit like that and I tell them that they watch too much of that Buffy show...morons. I could go on and on about that but I’m not. I’ll just say that they have no clue on how to portray real vampires. Then again they have no idea that we are, that we truly exist. At least from what I know is that the show finally ended so I don’t have to hear about it so much. Good riddance to short, blonde, and slutty. I believe that my sparring victim...err partner has arrived. Let the games begin...hahahahahahaha. Corvus walks to door wearing a black and red karate gi. He lets the man in who is roughly about six foot in height and is rather small compared to the massive size of his soon to be opponent. He wears Victorian clothing and has a top hat as well as rounded black shades. The man takes off his hat and cloak and puts them on the rack by the door. He surveys the place a little bit and seems to be pleased with what his eyes take in. This man was refined and enjoyed some of the best things that life or in his case the after life had to offer.* “You’re still wearing that old getup” Come on D, I would have thought by now you would have at least decided to get with the times. It’s not like you are trying to bring this back into fashion...you’re just too damn lazy to adapt.” The man chuckled as he looked around some more. It was the first time that he had been by this particular dwelling of Corvus?. It was even a surprise that he has showed up to this one since the two haven’t spoken in nearly two years...give or take. “They say if it is not broke then why fix it” I enjoy this mode of dress it is comfortable and I still make it look good. You’re just jealous that you cannot anymore. So where is your equipment?” He spoke with a cheeky Brit accent. His eyes continued to roam the room and made mental notes about the d’cor to himself. Of course he wouldn’t say anything to Corvus out loud and would more likely find a way to break in to fix the things he felt needed changing. “I have the basement rigged up real nice. There’s probably enough space down there to put in one of those octagons like those wannabe fighters play around in. I’m happy though with just the weights and the martial arts weapons. Are you sure you want to spar in that ugly outfit” Corvus thought it was an awful idea that his spar partner, who you will come to know as Deacon Black was planning on fighting in the awful and gaudy Victorian suit that he was wearing. The fact that Corvus was cut bigger than Deacon made it impossible for him to borrow anything. “What’s wrong with the outfit” All I need to do is remove the jacket and we’ll be ready to get this thing going. Now let’s move along because you’re wasting precious time. Not like we don’t have it but I really hate to be wasteful.” Deacon slipped the jacket off and tossed it around his shoulders and took it upon himself to lead the way towards the basement. It was lucky that he had a good sense of where he was going otherwise he would probably wander off someplace he didn’t want to go. His walk was confident yet had a cocky swagger to it as well. Corvus follows behind him and mutters something about Deacon being a “cocky little snot” as he went down the stairs. The basement was rather large and had many types of weapons mounted on the wall and racks of them as well both practice and live varieties. There was also weight equipment, a tread mill, and an exercise bike there for his fitness use as well. The floor was not carpeted and had a nice wood finish. The walls were painted white and there was no visible sign of windows at all in this area of the place. It was bare bones compared to all the other rooms of the residence. Corvus walks over to the wall and grabs two kendo sticks and tosses one of them to Deacon. “I hope you’ve kept in practice...because I don’t want this to be easy for me. I need a challenge. That is why I moved and began working for Global Wrestling...I needed a change of atmosphere and people who weren’t going to jerk me around.” Corvus sets himself in a defending stance giving Deacon the opportunity to try and take the first offensive knowing that it wouldn’t be easy for him to get an advantage. The two men circle one another for a brief moment and Deacon takes that opportunity to interject another comment. “You certainly did make your impression on those lads in that last place you were wrestling for. They were scared of you so much that they did whatever they could to ride you out on a rail. Such a people person you are...really you should work on your etiquette.” Corvus flips off Deacon and motions for him to come get a piece of the action. Deacon obliges coming after the huge monstrosity. The two of them battle back and forth for what seems nearly like eternity until Deacon manages to get a stiff shot to Corvus side. The big man doesn’t even seem to flinch at the hit. “Nice...keep working at it because it didn’t even sting. You have a long way to go before you get me to...” Before Corvus can finish, Deacon is already at him again but this time Corvus manages to keep on the defensive seemingly reading all of strategies that his opponent has and dodges and blocks all of them. The moment of opportunity comes for Corvus and he wraps Black in the right knee and then follows by hitting him in the left. Now that Deacon is unbalanced he spins and cracks him right in the back of the head causing Black to drop the weapon he had in his hand and fall to the floor. “Looks like you should practice a little more... You know what” You’re still a punk.” Corvus says with a laugh as he takes the wooden sword and presses it against Deacon’s chest. “You know if I didn’t even remotely like you, I’d put a gap in your chest cavity.” Deacon would gulp if his physiology would allow him too. He looks into Corvus? eyes not being able to tell if he were bluffing or if he was really going to take him out during a simple little spar session. “You can relax that thing anytime you’d like.” The man who looked older than Corvus in appearance was not even half of his age but they were both of the same blood as far as vampires go, they were of the same house. “I think I’m going to keep my workout light tonight...plus you don’t look like you are having any fun. You’re welcome to stay in one of the guest rooms if you like otherwise just come by whenever you want. You are always welcome here, D. I’m going to take a shower.” Corvus pulls back and offers his hand, helping his friend up. He gathers the two wooden swords that they were using and returned them back to the wall. A smile came over his face as he started to walk for the door that led back upstairs. He flexed his right arm and commented to Deacon. “These are some awesome weapons...too bad you never decided to work out more.” Deacon straightened his clothes and slipped his jacket back on and shook his head. He had not wanted that for himself. A fighter was not really what he was interested in. Deacon enjoyed the arts and only because he owed Corvus a favor did he ever get involve in professional wrestling. “I enjoy my body the way it is. There is no need to look like a behemoth and I don’t understand why you feel that you do. I liked you better before you started cavorting with those savage killers. They really did a number on your cerebral workings.” “Sorry, I get into what I do...because I’ve fully accepted what I am. It’s just like when I completely decimated Chris Gothic. Did I care I was injuring someone” Did it bother me that I didn’t win the match? NO, because I broke him; now he will never wrestle in GW again. His first match and I took him out JUST LIKE THAT!” Corvus? eyes widen with joy as he snaps his fingers and recalls what it was like when he destroyed Chris Gothic’s knee. It was a turning point in his career because it told everyone who was watching that he was out there and he could do massive damage. It wasn’t the first time and it would not be the last. “I think I’ll let myself out while you muse about how you play with your food. I’ll be seeing you again...don’t think I haven’t been watching. Stay sharp good man.” With that Deacon made his move and walked past Corvus up the stairs and the sound of the door closing was heard moments later. Corvus continued to grin a little bit longer. He made his way upstairs and then up to the second story where his master suite was. It was his routine to always shower after he worked out. He didn’t have any sweat glands and really didn’t have body odor but he liked to keep himself groomed anyway. The radio was turned on and then cranked up as Corvus turned on the water to the shower. It wasn’t set at any particular spot in the hot or cold position because his body didn’t feel any of it. He just slipped out of his clothes and stepped right in to do his business. 4 a.m. Even as time goes on, I can still say that being a vampire is not easy. You would think that once you got passed the routine that everything would just be like one of those 9-5 jobs you hear your victims complain about while you stalk them. Sure I have it down pat but that doesn’t make it any easier from when I first transitioned from being just a man to becoming a monster. Most of my kind if they even make it that long, lose all the humanity they once had. One of the unfortunate things of my hereafter is that I’ve kept most of mine. It’s almost like having a soul, a constant nagging. The kind one might get if they’ve neglected taking out the trash and their spouse gets pissed at them for it. I can’t say that it always gets to me...just around certain parts of the year. The first part of a new year always seems to even send a chill down my cold spine. It’s all memories. Memories have a way of cutting through even the most solid objects. For those with hearts of stone, you’re not safe either. In the end they always get you...coming or going. You are of noble blood, you are better than everyone else. This shouldn’t even faze you. Hah, such is true...you see before I became damned I had promise. I had the opportunity to become one of the greatest leaders of all time...that is until fate decided to deal me a double edge sword. I’ll get into that later...but first more about my roots. I’m not going to make this into a history lesson since I don’t have to go through all of it seeing as how I lived it. I guess I just need a reminder every now and then why I am here and what I’m going to do with my longevity. Without this reminder of my past, I would not be who I am today. I was the son of a King back when there were such things. He was one of the greatest Kings of probably all time. Needless to say because I was born out of wedlock, the asshole never married my mother yet bore two sons from her that made people look upon my brother and I as specks of lint on the Swiffer of society. As time passed my father began to see potential in us, my brother and I. He wanted us to one day rule his Kingdom after he was gone if something happened to his children born to those who were his Queens. Though like one of my half brothers, who had a deformity and was looked down upon...my height also brought upon me much heartache. Back then being seven foot tall was something that made you unique but also at the same time made people wonder if you were the work of something sinister. This was much before they considered the Thirty First day of October to be All Hollow’s Eve. Despite that fact, my father still tried to do what he could so that I’d be seen as a possible heir to his thrown much to the chagrin of some of the other family members. My in-laws were what you could say power hungry and encouraged me more than I would have liked to stick my nose into things to make sure that I was given my due. Things just didn’t happen that way and without his knowing....my father actually outlived me in mortality but I still have the upper hand on the man. I met my mortal demise six years before him and was half his age as well. But what I lost as far as power...I gained it all back and more. I had received good amounts of money from my father to see to it that I was properly taken care of. After I “disappeared” and became one of the undead, I went back to my family’s estate...made off with a good deal bit more wealth and had a hell of a good meal off some of the servants. I can’t say that after I found out that my half brother’s squandered away the empire that I was too heartbroken...I should have eaten the son of a bitch’s, HA! Some good times those were...truly. Corvus now sits in a largely antiquated room filled with books and other things that look to possibly be important, a fireplace roaring in front of him as he is seated in a posh leather chair. His shadow can be seen on the wall in what seems like at first a black bird perched upon the chair and then shifts to a sort of horrifying dance until it seems to stop suddenly as if it was demanded its attention of its master. It was obvious that he was telling this tale to his shadow as it always listened no matter how torturous it was. He sits with a glass of reddish liquid in his hand. It was that which kept him going, his essence, and his power. It did for him what mortal food could no longer...sustain him in his re-animated state. In the other hand was one of the black clove cigarettes he seemed to enjoy. It’s not like they did any damage to him so he figured he might as well enjoy something other than killing. I haven’t always been this sadistic or harbored grudges or vendettas. I was once a man as well who enjoyed nothing more than breathing and living out his days to grow old with the woman he loved and two wonderful children. Fate had to step in on that one as well. They say sometimes you can never love your second child quite like your first and sadly for me this is also true. I had a beautiful daughter, Angela. She was due almost close to my birthday and I had welcomed that fact but she came just a few weeks earlier. Back then you really couldn’t predict those things as well they can now with all the modern machines and things they have. You always seem to torture yourself this way. You don’t have to do this...you could let it rest. That was then...a time long forgotten except by you and the one who calls herself Shadow. She had the fairest golden hair you ever did see from a child. She was a near spitting image of her mother with her father’s eyes. If you had been near death and saw this child you thought you might be going to heaven...she was an angel and that is what we named her after. Angela could have made such a difference...though she never got the chance. A plague spread through our lands and she was the only one who came down with it. Maybe her immune system wasn’t as strong as the rest of us...but her last days were nothing short of agony and that was the beginning of the end....yet it also came with a new beginning. I know for a fact that if my daughter had not lost her life...I would not be sitting here going over this one more time for the gazillionth time. I would have met my end due to illness or old age which was not necessarily as long as today’s normal life span. It’s always a mental mind fuck to try and weigh out which would have been better...to get older and watch your children grow and raise some of their own or watch as one of them dies and throw you into a maelstrom of despair, loneliness, and not to mention drunkenness. In which you make the ultimate choice that becomes a sacrifice of everything you have ever known. I’m sure people would say if they ever managed to hear this story would be, “How could you have chosen to drink blood and murder innocent people?” To be honest after a while you never even recognize the taste it just seems like water to me. As far as my meals go, I wouldn’t be so quick to jump on the innocent bandwagon. I am rather choosy when it comes to who I devour. I choose those who live poorly and do not cherish life very much. They take themselves and others for granted. It’s a shame that mankind hasn’t learned anything in over a thousand years. It’s a tragedy how evolution has taken a u-turn and instead of improving it is rapidly getting worse. But who cares about mortals anyway? They squander their existences on whims and are no better than garden slugs at best. There are very few of them that can be trusted, I’ve learned this several times. Those who are loyal are rare these days. When you let people in and they see what you are and are capable of they just want it for themselves. Very greedy mortals are but I don’t need a reminder of that. All I need to do is focus on what is here before me, not on my losses. Everyone loses something but it takes a strong individual to overcome it. Mortals lose something every day. A job, a family member or loved one, their self confidence...the list runs on and on. I don’t have those faults. I don’t have to worry about tomorrow because for me there will always be one. If only my father could see me now. He’d have realized how I would have been the logical choice to take over in the end. I’m powerful, perfect in body and mind, and aggressive when it comes to the things I want for myself. When something I want comes to mind I don’t waste any time. I make it mine...and that is what I’m going to do in Global Wrestling. It might not be right now but it will be. Charlemagne was considered to be a great King, but my goal is to become much greater than my father. I’ve already had a good start...and I’m not nearly even finished. Corvus takes another sip of his glass as he continues to stare into the fire. It was getting closer to daybreak so it meant that he should go rest soon. With the remaining time that he has he spends plotting his next course of action which can never bold well for anyone.
