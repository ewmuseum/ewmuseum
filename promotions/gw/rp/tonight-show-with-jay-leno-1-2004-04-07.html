Tonight Show With Jay Leno #1

7 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tonight Show With Jay Leno #1 J. C. Diamond Global Wrestling Promo #1102 Date: 4/7/04 Location: Los Angeles,CA Jay Leno: “Welcome back to the Tonight Show, I’m your host Jay Leno, duh.” Audience: Brief giggle. Jay Leno: “Tonight we have a special guest, who makes his living beating up people. Ladies and Gentlemen please welcome, Jayson Christopher. Diamond, AKA J.C Diamond!” Jay Leno stand and applaud along with the crowd. J.C: J.C steps out from the side curtain. J.C is dressed in a pair of blue jeans made by FuBu, a black/yellow Laker's Jersey with matching headband. Around the neck of the sweater is a gold chain with a charm that says Diamond. On his left arm a gold watch is hugged around his wrist with a black face and gold numbers. On his feet, J.C has a pair of black and yellow air forces with laker's sign stitched onto the sides of the sneakers. Jayson waves at the crowd and walks over and shakes Jays’ hand. Audience: The live audience is applauding, and the women are “Wooing”. J.C: J.C set on the sofa next to Jays’ desk. Jay Leno: Jay sits and grabs a pencil and flips it around. “Welcome to the show, J.C.” J.C: J.C nods. 'Thank you, Jay, and thanks for inviting me.' Woman of the crowd: “You are so fine! I love you J.C!” The rest of the women are “Wooing!” Jay Leno: “What do you have to say to that, J.C?” J.C: “What can I say, I’m J.C Diamond, and you got to love me.” Audience: Giggles and brief Wooing. Jay Leno: “So Jayson, you are a professional wrestler, correct?” J.C: “Yes, that’s correct. Well, it is if I still have a job, I have been gone for so long do to this trail. GW probably got all my stuff on the curves.” Jay Leno: “That’s right; you were in a little trouble weren’t you. I was going to slip my way into that, but glad you brought that out.” Audience: Brief giggles. J.C: “You know, you can’t trust anyone these days, not even a talk show host. But anyway, yea, I was testifying in court for the past two months.” Jay Leno: “Oh, please tell us why Mr. Diamond.” Jay put on a big greasy smile like an evil, greedy scientist or something, and folds his fingers together. Audience: Brief giggles. J.C: J.C smirks but explains. “Well, on Saturday of October, around 3:00am. I heard someone pounding on my door. Now me, being the sexy creature I am, thought it was a bootie call.” Audience: Briefly laughs. Jay Leno: “Did you really think it was bootie call though?” J.C: “Nah, I was just joking. Well, anyway. I got up out of the bed, and went to the door. No house coat, or pajamas, just in my boxers.” Female Audience: “Woo!” Jay Leno: “Oh, were they silk?!” Audience: “Brief laughs.” J.C: “Did you just ask me were they silk? …Nah they weren’t, sorry Jay.” Jay Leno: “Aw, darn.” Audience: Audience giggles. J.C: “Yea, well I went to the door, and I opened it. Standing outside my home were about fifteen police cars, 30 police with these bright ass flashlights aiming at me, and a guy with this loud bull horn. He must have had it on max. J.C, get down on your knees and put both hands behind you head, you are under arrest. Ya know, treating me like I was a criminal or something.” Jay Leno: “So, what did they arrest you for?” J.C: “…Fifty Five Thousand dollars worth of credit card fraud.” Jay Leno: “Whoa! Good gracious. Were you guilty of the charge? I mean, did you do that?” J.C: “Of course not… I was innocent…Until proven guilty.” Audience: The live audience laughs hysterically. They also applaud because it is true. Jay Leno: “Well that’s true. So, go ahead and continue.” J.C: “Alright, so they had me in court testifying and of course I pleaded not guilty. They were throwing all this evidence at me and everything they could use. It was going back and forth for two months and I was like f**k this. I was tired and I just wanted it all to end. So I was like, give me a lie detector test and I will prove my innocents. So they took me to the back the last day of the trail, hooked me up to this machine. I took a deep breath, and he asks me questions. Questions like, “J.C, have you ever stolen any objects from a store, or someone’s house?” I answered no, and then they ask have I ever heard or remember the name Franklin White, he was the owner of the card. I answered no. Then they ask have I ever use anyone’s credit card illegally, I answered no. Then they ask have I ever use Franklin Whites credit card illegally. I answered no. About 30 minutes later, the test was over, they took a recess. The judge walked in and set down at the bench. He said he had the results of the test.” Jay Leno: “So, what were the results?” J.C: “Well, I knew I had it all won ya know. So I set back and folded my arms. Then the judge was like, “J.C, the results show that you told the truth 0.1% of the time and lien 99.9 percent of the time.” Audience: “The crowd laughs hysterically.” J.C: “Man I was so pissed, because, something had to be wrong.” Jay Leno: “Wait, you really didn’t do it?” J.C: “…No I did it, it was just f**ked up they found out about it.” Audience: The crowd laughs hysterically. Jay Leno: “What do you mean; you offered to take a lie detector test. What did you think you could trick it?” J.C: “Yes, because like, I read a page online, about how to pass the lie detector test and…” J.C paused until the crowd calms from laughter. “It picks up whether you telling the truth or not by your heart rate. So I figure you know if I slowed my heart rate down I could get passed it. That is why I took that deep breath I told you about in the story.” Audience: Crowd laugh even more. Jay Leno: “You said it said you told 0.01% of the truth. What was it that you actually told the truth about?” J.C: “…My name and age.” Audience: The crowd laughs. J.C: “And the whole thing…The whole credit card thing, happen back when I was a junior in high school. And that was about, four or five years ago. So why bring it up now?” Audience and Jay Leno: The audience and Jay are both laughing at this wild story. Jay Leno: “So, the credit card theft happen back when you were in high school? It wasn’t a recent thing or recent incident?” J.C: “No no, I was still young then. Well, I’m still young, I’m only twenty-two. I was about 16 maybe 17 then.” Jay Leno: “That’s good.” Jay turned and looks towards the camera guy. “Hey Ken, it’s okay, you can give me my wallet and check book back, I don’t think he’ll steal it.” Ken: Ken reaches in his shirt pocket and takes out the check book, and then in his back pocket and takes out the wallet and walks onto the stage and gives it to Jay. Audience: The audience is laughing and stands to applaud; the band plays a little tune. J.C: J.C is shaking his head. “That’s cold Jay, that’s really cold.” Jay Leno: “No, J.C that was before me new the real you.” Jay paused while the crowd briefly giggles. “Jayson we are running out of time. So why don’t u tell everybody when you are expected to return back to Global Wrestling Action.” J.C: “Okay. I’m not gonna tell you when, that’s a surprise, but it is soon.” Jay Leno: “What can we expected from J.C Diamond.” J.C: “I can tell you, this time around is going to be a lot different, a lot more exciting. I have finally found my place in GW and I’m not going to be the same wrestler from before.” Jay Leno: “Sorry but we got to get out of here. J.C Diamond I enjoyed the hell out of you and I’m sure everybody else here did. Make sure you tune into Global Wrestling, where you can see J.C kick ass every week. That’s it, I'm Jay Leno, and we are out of here!” The Tonight Show: The Tonight Show came to an end showing credits and J.C shaking Jay Leno hand.
