Lies

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/19/2003 The scene fades in with a close up of Yoshiko, who dangles from the shower head. Her hair dripping wet, and her skin a sickening mixture of red, pink, and white. Her breathing is heavy as her eyes slightly open. The dark circles that surround her eyes, barely tell the story of what she has been through, and the exhaustion, pain, and torture she is feeling. Her slightly opened eyes open wider as the bathroom door creeks. Ice, with a smug expression on his face walks into the room with a paper plate of food in his hands. With his left hand he reaches over and puts the toilet lid down, setting the plate on top of it. He looks over at Yoshiko and removes her bound hands from the shower head. 'Eat.' Yoshiko looks at Ice then lunges towards the food, eating with a mass fury, like she has not eaten in weeks. Ice chuckles at her animal like instincts towards the food, and watches at she eats and licks the plate clean. He steps out of the room briefly, only to return moments later with a white paper cup. Yoshiko looks up at him, while on her knees, and Ice offers the cup to her. 'Drink.' Without hesitation Yoshiko grabs the cup and chugs down its contents. A white creamy liquid begins to drip down the sides of her face. She finishes the cup, and sets it ontop of the empty plate. Ice reaches forward and takes both the plate and the cup and tosses them into the nearby trashcan. Yoshiko looks up at Ice with a pleading in her eyes, while stretching out as much as she can, for as long as she is allowed to. 'I'm going to ask you this, just as I did the other day. Are you ready to cooperate? Because if you are, I will let you stay the way you are right now. But should you pull another stunt, like last time, what happened to you before, won't even register compared to what will happen to you this time.' Yoshiko nods in compliance. Ice nods back, leaves the room, and closes the door behind him. Ice walks over to a small dresser a few feet away from the bathroom door, and firmly places his hands upon its edges. He stares down at the dusty brown dresser top, before lifting his left hand, reaching over, and pressing a button on the black radio that sits on a small table next to the dresser. Ice lifts his head and stares at his own reflection, which is radiating from the circular mirror hanging on the wall above the dresser, as Lies by Evanescence plays in the background. As the song ends Ice closes his eyes, and the scene fades to black.
