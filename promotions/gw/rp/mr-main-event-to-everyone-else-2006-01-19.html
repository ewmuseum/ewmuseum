Mr. Main Event to Everyone Else

19 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/19/2006 Day one The first day inside the ring again. It was spectacular. One of a kind. The crowd screaming. The food flying. The sweat pouring but’there was just one thing that didn’t need to be there. The other crappy talent. Come on, tell me you don’t agree? Tell me you don’t like young talented men like myself? My charm? My spirit? My perfect body? Oh it’s what god intended it to be. And you didn’t get to see it because of the lower class no talent wrestlers that Global hired. The Main Event is here? --------------------------------------------- Thursday January the 19th “Jamie, where’s my necklace?” Holler’s David from the other room. The room was covered in white. White curtains, white sheets, white TV, and even the most shocking, a white bra that Jamie was wearing as she steps out from the bathroom. “Maybe on me?” Jamie giggles out loud from her sudden reply. “Get some cloths on. Were going to be late.” Responding. Jamie’s eyes rolled away, right to the dress that she had laid out earlier. Long and silky, just enough to cover her legs from the bottom to the top. She was elegant in it. Like a supermodel in some ways. A goddess. David turned away from the sight of Jamie. Giving a little grunt before pulling out every drawer on his desk. “Dammit! I need that necklace.” Slamming the top draw. Jamie laughs a little before responding to his grunting and childish ways. “Why is it so damn important for you to have the necklace rather then looking at me?” Angry somewhat. Jamie tosses her hands up in the air, frustrated in his utter ways. “Because it was my fathers. You know that?”, glancing an eye at her. Jamie gives up almost. Pulling the dress up and over her curved body. It was magic. Like a glass of water getting water poured in. She dazzled in the dim light of the room. Almost as if she was a light of her own. Walking over to the bathroom door again, a buzzing sound was here over off the corner of the room. David looked up, looking over at his new Sprint phone going off. “Hello?” Responding. David’s eyes grew wide with whatever he got on the other end. “Listen here you over load of human trash, you get me those papers and you get me them NOW!” David screams, tossing all his anger through the phone. Jamie ran back into the bedroom, wondering what was going on. David chunks the phone down on the bed as he grabs his black Dockers t-shirt that was laying on the chair beside the bedroom door. “What’s wrong honey?” Jamie responds. “What do you think. I need those dang papers so I can keep track of all these so called “Players” in Global.” David’s hands come up to make the so called quote signal. “Players?” Jamie asked. “Yeah. Like are so called International Champion. You see him talking all that crap at reload. Oh how I’m going to be a double champion?”, David says like he’s some girly girl. “Oh how he thinks he’s The Main Event or something.” Jamie giggled in a soft and utter silent way before responding. “I think both your packages are getting a little to big.” David looks at Jamie with a hard displeasuring look, “Well at least we know your not making them get big.” David presses his button’s together, pressing the shirt down into the bed of his blue Dockers pants. He grabbed his brush, pressing it through his think black long hair, David gave a sudden turn, watching a picture that was on the wall. “You remember that?” Pointing to the picture that held the shot of a man holding a title belt. “Yeah. I can still remember the blood that was shed from that. Why you ask?” Jamie responds. “That’s the night that our so called “champion” decided he wanted to make a fool out of me. That night, all the blood, all of it that was spilt, I proved I was Main Event caliber.” David says as he wonders over to the picture. “I guess that’s how I’m going to have to treat everyone else in Global if they get in my way.” Jamie, taking a small sigh before walking over to the center of the room between David and her. Her lips, puckering up to the ceiling almost. “David?”, she says as she rubs her hand through her head. “I sure miss the innocent guy I found.” Jamie says. David twist his head around, looking at Jamie who was just standing there from her responds. “Well I’m sorry sugar bear but that man is just simply?”, pausing with his answer. ??dead.” With that David walks out of the room, leaving Jamie to stand there alone. Her hands went to his hips with a stance that almost seemed like she was done asking.
