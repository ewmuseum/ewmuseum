Date: 1-19-06 The scene reopens in the backroom of Jun’s Herbal Store that was somewhere in Okinawa, Japan. “Julia Kushinaga, age 54 was brutally murdered in her hospital room in Central Tokyo Hospital. Her reason for being admitted is still unknown.” It was revealed that the voice was coming from a newswoman on small portable television set that sat on a flat wooden table. Kyo leaned on the back wall with a lit cigarette in his mouth as he watched this. His friend, Jun was sitting in a chair backwards as he too was watching too. “Police says that this man fitting this sketch was responsible for the death of the elderly Kushinaga woman.” The news broadcast shown a sketch of man that looked exactly like Kyo. Jun has concern in his eyes as he looked at Kyo. Kyo sharpens his eyes in a serious glare. He can’t believe that they were pinning this one him. Kyo had no reason to kill his own mother but the police didn’t think so. He was relieved though that the police didn’t know that she was his mother because if they did then the police would be able to make up some lie about him getting into some kind of argument that led to murder as that would instantly land in jail for a long time or worst... “Julia Kushinaga survived by her daughter, Ukyo Kushinaga, age 24. It’s a sad day when a monster like that kills a defenseless elderly person. I and everyone here in the news station send our condolences the Kushinaga family.” Jun turned off the TV with the use of the remote. “I see that they still think you’re dead since they didn’t mention you.” Kyo breathes warm smoke through both nostrils. “The Organization probably told them that I was dead. Mother knew I wasn’t dead since I was the survivor in the family.” He sighed. “It’s better that my sister don’t know that I’m alive. The Organization might just let her be.” “Do you know why your mother was in the hospital?” Kyo shook his head. “The only thing I know that she thrust her hand deep inside of her and pulled out a long sword that somehow found its way inside her.” Jun begins thinking. “It sounds like that she was being used as a vessel.” Kyo blinked and looks at Jun with puzzlement. “Didn’t you tell me that you’re mother was visited by what was possibly a higher being?” Kyo nodded. “Yeah, this being told her everything about me and what The Organization was planning on doing. The being did that two years before I was awakened from my crystallized tomb.” “Your mother was gifted with high Spiritual Power at birth. This would later enable her to see into her future.” Jun looked with seriousness on his face. “The being chose your mother to be this vessel due to her powers. I’m guessing what sent her to the hospital was that the sword within her had finally completely drained every once of her power.” He looked at Kyo. “Even if she didn’t pull the sword out of her she would’ve been dead anyways.” Kyo starts pacing in the room. “Your mother was a sacrifice?” Jun looked down with disbelief in his eyes. “She sacrificed herself and her power so the sword could be fully charged once it left her body. Once you grabbed hold of the sword it went inside of you and started supercharge you.” He looked at Kyo. “I bet you for a split second you were at full power, didn’t you?” Kyo stopped and sighs in frustration. “This still doesn’t make any sense!” He snapped his head at Jun with a glare in his brown colored eyes. “Just what the hell is going on!”” Jun still sat in the chair in calmness. “Someone has great interest in you perhaps a heavenly or hellish faction.” “Well, if I don’t start getting any answers from both factions I’m going to start killing every single devil or angel that gets in my way.” Kyo took another drag from his cigarette and breathes the smoke pass his lips. Jun sighs. “Listen, I’ll do some research on some prophecies and the sword that you described to me. I’ll call you when I found something.” “Thanks, Jun.” Jun smirked. “Hey, I owe you one for saving my ass that time.” “Oh.” Kyo slipped his hand within his long trench coat. “I was wondering if you have any rounds for this.” Jun looks at Kyo with puzzlement. His puzzlement look turned to shock when he saw that Kyo has pulled out a Spell Binder. “That’s a Spell Binder! Do you know that thing has been outlawed here in this world!”” “I’m aware.” Jun blinked as he was still in shock. “How were you able to get your hands on one of these!”” Kyo has a puzzled look on his face. “Come to think of it I don’t remember how I came into possession of this gun.” He handed the gun to Jun. Jun examined the brass gun. “This is no doubt a Spell Binder but I never seen one as unique as this one.” “Why do you say that?” Jun scrolls his fingers over the engravings on the gun’s wide and long barrel. “It has an unknown spell engraved on it.” Kyo blinked. “I didn’t know that.” He laughed as he scratches his raven colored hair. “I thought it was markings that made the gun look cool.” “Uh-huh?” Jun handed Kyo back his gun. “I’m afraid that I don’t have illegal God Shells here.” Kyo holstered the gun behind his pants. “Great?” Jun gets up from his seat and walked over to a bookshelf as he was preparing to do research. “If you go to Shinjuku then you might find something there.” Jun was probably right since Shinjuku is full of crime and corruption. It was also the place where devils feel right at home. Can say it was a devil hangout when they can’t go back to their lairs. Kyo put on his shades along with a black hat wanting to keep his identity unknown since there was probably manhunt for him going on. He walks to the backroom’s door? “You’re heading there right now?” Jun looked at Kyo as he places a stack of books on a desk. “I have thing to do before I go there.” Kyo exited the room closing the door behind him. Jun has a look of puzzlement on his face but he realized what Kyo was planning on doing. He nodded his head in what appear to be a gesture of respect. Jun soon begins his research on prophecies. ==|Part II: Saying Goodbye|== The scene changes to the outside of Jun’s Herbal Store. Kyo left the store as he pulls up the collars of his trench coat giving him more of an innuendo look. He begins walking among the natives. It has been a week since Kyo’s mother died. Today was her funeral. Kyo didn’t want to go at first since he didn’t want to say goodbye to yet another person that he cared for but he knew that would it be insensitive of him after this woman took him in and raised him to be part of her family. He made it his sworn duty to go to the funeral before he leaves Japan for the states but he was unaware that an obstacle was about to stand of his way of accomplishing this duty? The scene switches to the rooftop of a tall building that wasn’t too far from the Herbal Store. A man walked on the edge of the rooftop with a familiar advanced sniper rifle in hand. He was watching Kyo’s every move as he walked. The man stopped as he drops down on one knee positioning his gun at Kyo. It was revealed that the man was none other then Sniper. Sniper grins. “Gotcha you, Kushinaga?” His guided his finger over to the trigger. Gunshot was fired! The natives screamed! Kyo was kneeling down on one knee with a look of pain on his face. He has been shot but the wound wasn’t fatal as the bullet went in and out of his left shoulder. Kyo started to look around as he was searching from where the sniper shot him from. It was hard to see due to all the people fleeing in great panic. The scene changes back to the rooftop. Sniper was having the same problem too but he soon came to a conclusion to this. Multiple gunshots were fired! People began falling dead left and right. Sniper didn’t care who he shot as long he got Kyo in the process. They continued fleeing from this area as their screams of fear filled the air. Sniper stopped when noticing that there was no sign of Kyo. “Damn it!” He realized that Kyo must have fled during the commotion. Sniper blurs away? He soon reappeared at the site that was full of blood and corpses. He started walking among the dead until he noticed a single blood leading away from her. Sniper grins knowing that this was Kyo’s blood as he begins following the trail. The scene changes to the inside of a supermarket that was a couple of blocks away from a street should be now called Death Street. Kyo walked in the market as he was trying to conceal his wound from the public’s eye. He looks up and saw a sign that read florist in Japanese. Kyo makes a turn around the corner and entered the florist aisle. Why would go to the florist aisle? Some of the costumers were giving him suspicious looks especially the security guard of the store. Kyo heads for the exit with a bouquet of some random flowers in his blood covered hand. The security guard was about to approach him. Kyo pulled out one of his handguns and aim at the guard. “Down!” He watched the guard hold up his hands for instant surrender. “Down now!” The guard did what Kyo told him to do. Kyo walks to the guard and removed the gun from his waist as he toss it away. He looked around the market seeing that the customers had frightened looks on their faces. Who wouldn’t be frightened when seeing a person waving a gun around? He soon left the store? Sniper has entered the market in the same way that Kyo did as he was still following his blood trail. He soon sees that the people were in a state of confusion and fear giving him more proof that Kyo was here. The scene switches to outside of the market in the parking area. Kyo walked up to a park car as he was about to break into it. Police siren was heard! Police car drove up to Kyo. One police officer exit from the driver’s side as another did from the passenger’s side. They approach him. Kyo stood motionless beside the driver’s side of the car? One officer touched Kyo on the shoulder as he was about to turn him around. Kyo turned around himself and strike the officer down with a strong elbow to the rib then knocked him down with a sick headbutt bursting the officer’s nose. He sees that the other officer was going for his gun. Kyo stopped him with a swift kick to the groin then thrown the officer back with a strong thrust kick to the chest. Both officers were down favoring their injuries. Kyo smashes the car’s window with a stiff punch as he entered after unlocking it. He closes the door and was about to hotwire the car. Kyo stopped and pulls down the overheard compartment as the car keys fell out. He starts the car and drove off. Sniper finally exited the market as he was able to catch of glimpse of Kyo driving off. He soon takes notice of the fallen officers. Sniper approached them as he slip behind him pulling out a handgun. Two shots were fired! Sniper killed both officers with shots to their heads. He places his rifle in the passenger’s seat of the police car and soon entered. Sniper started the car as he drives in pursuit after Kyo. “Meanwhile” The scene changes back to Okinawa. A woman dressed in a dark colored cloak walked among the people. It was revealed the woman was Chi as she was searching for Kyo. “Where did that man get to?” She stopped allowing the people to pass her. Chi looked around the area with her red colored eyes. “I could’ve sworn I sensed him here.” She soon noticed a commotion going down a few blocks away. Chi blinks as she had a puzzled look on her face. “What happened over there?” She walked over there. The scene switches to where the commotion was at. Chi made her way through the crowd of people. She soon sees that yellow tape surrounded the area but she was also seeing many white sheets that were obviously covering dead bodies. “What in the hell happened here?” “It was a sniper attack.” Chi looked at the civilian. “A sniper attack?” “Yeah! Some mad shooting that went on here! Killing everyone in sight!” Chi stands there and listened to the crowd’s banters. “I think that sniper was just aiming for one person and he just killed everyone in the crossfire.” “Aiming for one person?” Chi began thinking but soon realize who the sniper’s target was. She left the crowd shoving and pushing. “Can’t that man stay out of trouble!”” “Meanwhile” The scene switches to one of Japan’s many busy highways. Sniper was chasing Kyo down the highway but they weren’t alone as there were a few police cars along with a police helicopter were pursuing them. Their reckless driving caused some of the officers to crash hard into other cars while others simply ran off the highway that led to a wicked crash. The scene changes inside of the car that Kyo was in. Kyo had the car lighter on his bullet wound as he was stopping the bleeding. It may have been twisted idea but it did work. He feels that his wound had burn closed as he put the lighter back where he found and resumed driving. Kyo noticed a highway sign through his windshield as the sign read that he was only a few miles away from the Silent Wind Cemetery. He suddenly sees that his vision started to get blurry as it felt that his body becoming heavy. Kyo’s vision returned normal after he rubbed his eyes but he wondered why he felt that way he did a minute ago. What was the caused of this? Glass shatters! “Damn!” Kyo had ducked down avoiding what was obviously a gunshot that came through the back window. The scene switches to outside of the car that Sniper was in. Sniper was halfway sticking out from the front window shooting at Kyo’s car with a twisted look on his face. “Die! Die! Die! Die Kushinaga!” He laughed as he continued shooting. Can tell that this guy was extremely obsessed on killing Kyo. Guess missing him made some screws loose. Kyo makes a hard turn as he bumped into another car causing that car to swirl into Sniper’s car. Sniper got back in his car and managed to maneuver around the incoming car. Crash! The police weren’t so lucky. One police car crashed into that car then a few other police cars crashed into that police car making a nasty pile of twisted metal and glass. Sniper grins. “What’s wrong, Kushinaga” Are you feeling the effects of that curse bullet I shot you with?” He glared. “I want you suffer! Suffer before I shoot you to death! Suffer for the pain that you put me through!” Sniper tightened his grip on the steering wheel. “You put me through hell ever since I missed you. I don’t miss and I don’t lose a target. Die, Kushinaga, die!” Can anyone say coo-coo? The effects of the curse bullet were getting worst on Kyo. His vision was getting with minute as his body was getting heavier. He still drove as he was determined on getting to his destination. Will he make it or will the effects of the curse bullet fully consume him? Sniper was sticking out from the window again and resumed shooting at Kyo. Kyo ducked down as he avoided the gunfire. He knew that it was someone from The Organization that was pursuing him. Kyo also knew that he was being tailed since he left Jun’s place. He looks over to the outside mirror as he saw Sniper was still firing at him. “Who are you?” Kyo soon watched the outside mirror shatter apart after Sniper shot it. “You really hate me, don’t you?” He smirked. Sniper was still shooting. “YOU IN THE POLICE CAR AND YOU IN THE GRAY CAR!” Sniper stopped as he looked behind him. Kyo did too. It appeared that a police officer was speaking from an intercom that was installed in the police car. “STOP AND PULL OVER AT ONCE ARE WE WILL BE FORCED TO USE LETHAL FORCE!” Kyo blinked as he has a puzzled look on his face. “Why are they threatening to shoot me!” I’m not even the one shooting!” Sniper goes in his car as it looked like that he was going to do what the police told him to do. He soon opened the car as he was sticking out of the car with his sniper rifle in hand. Sniper kept the car steady with one hand on the steering wheel. Sniper places the rifle on his shoulder and took aim at the five remaining police cars. “Lethal this!” He turned his aim to a nearby trailer truck. Sniper shoots a couple of the truck’s front tires. The truck jackknifed! Crash! All the police cars crashed and exploded into the trailer truck that now blocked part of the highway as every officer died instantly. The police helicopter still tailed both cars. “Get away! Kushinaga is mine! My target!” He glared up at the police helicopter. “You want me!” “What!”” Sniper snapped his head saw that Kyo was driving backwards with a handgun aimed at him. “You got me!” Kyo panted heavily as the effects of the curse bullet worsen. Sniper grins. “Do you seriously think you can shoot me in your condition, Kushinaga!”” Kyo positioned his gun for a better aim. His vision was becoming more blurry but he shook his head as he tried to regain his vision that way but it was to no prevail. Sniper still grinned. He aimed his sniper rifle for Kyo’s head. “I gotcha now...” Sniper inched his finger over to the trigger. “Farwell, Kushinaga!” Gunshot was fired! Kyo had tilted his head to the side avoiding Sniper’s shot as he felt his cheek cut open from the graze of the bullet. “I missed you again! Damn you, Kushinaga!” Sniper gone for another shot. Another gunshot was fired! Sniper’s yell of pain was heard! Kyo managed to shoot Sniper out of the moving police car. He turns the car around in the right way but soon saw a stopped car through the windshield. “Damn!” Kyo swirled away from that car. Another stopped car was in his way! Kyo slammed on the brakes! Crash! Kyo crashed into the car so hard that his car flipped in the air and landed hard on the concrete. It skidded across the road a few miles until it finally stopped. The traffic has stopped moving. Civilians exited out of their cars as were looking at the crash site. The front door of Kyo’s car swung open? Kyo was crawling out of the car. One side of his face was covered in blood as some blood dripped out of his mouth. He didn’t hear anything except for his heart beating. The impact of the crash has rendered his senses but he wouldn’t have to worry since they will be returning soon. Kyo soon takes a seat on the ground as he swirled his tongue around in his bleeding mouth before he spit the blood out. He favored his head as he was looking around his surroundings. Kyo saw that he was being watched by civilians. He decides that it was time to make his disappearing act as he went in the turned over car and pull out the bouquet of flowers that was covered in glass. Kyo started to walk away? It appeared that the chase was finally over? Gunshot was fired! Kyo dropped down on one knee as he just got shot in his leg. The civilians got back in their cars in result of seeing Sniper limp his way in between their cars with sniper rifle in his hand. Sniper was covered in cuts and bruises. It appeared that Kyo had shot him in the chest but he was a few inches off him hitting the heart. Kyo slips his hand in his trench coat and pulled out his handguns. He was about to turn and shoot Sniper. Sniper approached Kyo. “I don’t think so!” He kicked the guns out of Kyo’s hands as he soon kicked kick in the face knocking him down. “You don’t know how much trouble you put me through these last past weeks.” Sniper glared at Kyo. “I was in hell!” He kicked Kyo in the side. Kyo grunts and favored his side feeling that some of his ribs have been possibly broken with that kick alone. “I was known a great sniper who has never missed a target.” Kyo crawled over to his twin guns. Sniper stepped on Kyo’s gun wound that was on his leg as he stopped Kyo. “Until you and that stupid Spirit Beast came along!” Sniper glared. “Spirit Beast?” Kyo soon realized that this guy was. This guy wasn’t only a person who worked with The Organization but he was also the same guy who murdered Kairi. “Your Spirit Beast isn’t here to take another bullet for you.” Sniper guided a hand up to the bullet wound on his chest and pulled up the bullet. “A Dark Spirit Shell” This would’ve been more effective if you aimed for my heart. I surprised that you were even managed to shoot me so close in your condition. You must be suffering in pain now.” He grinned. “Don’t worry though I’ll end it all in a second.” Sniper aimed the rifle at the back of Kyo’s head. Kyo lied there motionless? “And to think that Aion said that you would get stronger by fighting guys in tights. What a joke?” “Stronger, yes?” Kyo clutched his right hand in a tight fist. Sniper looked at him with puzzlement. “But unpredictable too!” Kyo swung around on his back as a long blade slid out from right gauntlet. Sniper yelled out in pain! Sniper saw that his rifle was cut apart but he also saw both hands were cut off from the waist. “My hands!” Blood gushed everywhere. Kyo was standing in front of Sniper as his body pulse crimson in response to the bitter rage that he was feeling. “You killed my friend!” He struck a hard punch in Sniper’s jaw. Blood spew out from Sniper’s mouth! Kyo grabbed hold Sniper by the collar and pulls him back. He glared in Sniper’s dull colored eyes. Kyo roared out as he started punching Sniper in the face. Blood gushing out with each punch! Kyo didn’t have any thought of letting Sniper go as he continued beating the living hell out of him. He begins having visions? The visions started when he first met Kairi a long time ago? Vision soon ended with her death? Kyo released Sniper from his grasp as his fist was covered in Sniper’s blood. He glared down at Sniper and saw that his face has been beaten beyond recognition. Sniper coughed. Kyo has limp over to his guns. He picked them up and limp to the fallen Sniper. Sniper soon saw with one eye that he was looking up at the barrels of Kyo’s handguns. He laughs with a grin on his face. “You think’killing me’will solve’anything?” “No?” He glared as his eyes flared crimson. “But it’s a start.” Multiple gunshots were fired! Sniper lied dead in large pool of blood full of bullet wounds. Kyo holstered his guns on both legs. He looks over at the cars and saw that the civilians were looking at him. Kyo turned away from them and started to leave like nothing happen. Kyo had avenged Kairi’s death but it wasn’t over yet. He still had a promise to keep? “Later” The scene changes to Silent Wind Cemetery that was located a few miles away from downtown Tokyo. Kyo stood in front of the large headstone that read Kushinaga in Japanese as this was the Kushinaga’s family tombstone. The headstone had his name, his brother’s name, and now his mother’s name engraved on it. It was surrounded my reefs of flowers. “Sorry I was late, mother. Traffic was a bitch.” Kyo laughed. He holds out a bouquet of flowers but most of the flowers were missing. “Uh, these were supposed to be for you.” He threw them over his shoulder. “They weren’t your favorite kind anyways.” The scene switches to the back of the graveyard. A suspicious man in a suit and an earpiece as this attire made him look like a rip-off agent from The Matrix series. He was looking at Kyo through his thick black sunglasses. The man soon started to speak into his earpiece. His face was soon full with pain as he vanished in a black mist. Chi had vanquished the man with a stab to the throat. “Damn Ghost Men?” Chi soon saw Kyo. “So the devil is a sensible.” She smirked. The scene changes back to the Kushinaga’s family headstone. “I don’t what to really say. I’m confused about what you want me to do.” He scratched his cheek. “You gave me a sword that you have been nursing since I was awakened. Then you tell me that I will do the right. What is the right thing to do?” He sighed. “I know you’ll probably tell me that I’ll figure it out and you’re right I will.” Kyo looked at the headstone. “I just want to say think you having a hand in raising me and making part of your family. So thank you, Julia Kushinaga for everything.” Kyo soon kneels down as he gone in his pocket and took out the strands of Kairi’s hair. Some of green mist started to seep out from out of the reefs. The mist was heading for Kyo from the behind. Kyo stopped at the sight of fireball heading straight for him but he soon saw that the fireball went pass him. He looks behind him and saw that the fireball struck the misting reef of flowers. The reef was covered in flames. “And here I thought you might need my help to deal with that little old sniper.” Kyo turned around and saw that Chi was standing a few feet in front of him. “It appears that your skills are coming back.” Kyo started walking again. “What’s wrong” Don’t like that I’m putting you out of the job?” Chi pretended to laugh. “Very funny... But if I didn’t have thrown that fireball then you would’ve been killed by mere Possessed Flowers. What a way for the mighty Outlaw to go out. Death by flowers.” She smirked. Kyo still walked away from her. “You need to go the rejuvenation center for your wounds.” “I only a need a good night sleep and I will be good as new.” Kyo left the graveyard. Chi laughed as she walks in the opposite direction of Kyo. “Meanwhile” The scene switches to the open-spaced room that was at an unknown locale. Aion sat alone at the long table as he was drinking a glass of white wine while listening to some Mozart on an entertainment center. A cold wind blows?
