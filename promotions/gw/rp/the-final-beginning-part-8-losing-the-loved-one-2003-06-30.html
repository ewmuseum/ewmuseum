The Final Beginning. Part 8 - Losing the loved one

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/6/03 White All of the wall were white and it was starting to shit him off. It was his wife and his unborn child in the room with a bunch of doctors who he doesn’t know, doing god knows what. Everywhere around him, coughing, crying, sneezing. All he wanted to do was to tell them all just to fuck off. This was his waiting bay now. No one else would be allowed anywhere near it. He had lost one special person in his life so far, and damned if he was about to loose another one. The day his father died had been hard on him, everyone knew that, butt he day his wife passes away would be the day that hell breaks loose on the world and Salaok would never be the same again. Knowing that this was a planned attack only drew Salaok closer to insanity. Lisha had never hurt anyone in her life and the only reason she was on the wrong end of the bullet was because of him. Because of the love that they shared and because she was carrying his child. It was his fault that she now lay in the hospital bed with metal being thrust into her body, trying to fix the wounds that the single bullet had caused. A doctor walks out from one of the doors in the hallway and instantly Salaok pounces on him wanting all the answers that he could possibly give. His hands gripping the doctors shirt and lifting him high up into the air, Salaok gains the doctors attention rather quickly. ”Where’s my wife? Where is Lisha?” Trying to keep his calm while suspended in air, the doctor speaks. ”I’m sorry Mr. Davis, but I am not the one you need to be talking to. I don’t have anything to do with her case.” Snapping back, Salaok presses the man against the wall and eases him down to the ground. ”Then who do I need to see?” ”They will come out when they have some news. That’s the way things work around here sir. I’m sorry I can not help any further.” A heavy sigh bursts from Salaok’s lungs and his head drops, looking straight at the ground while he thinks. ”You couldn’t go in their seeing as though you’re a doctor could you and find out for me?” ”Sorry, sir, I am not allowed to do that.” ”I’ll pay you money. Lots of money. I just want to know how it’s all going?” Salaok replies in a state of desperation. ”Sir, I’m not allowed for your wife’s health sake. Look, here comes one of her doctors now.” Salaok spins around quickly on his heel to see a doctor walking towards him, with no expression at all on his face, which clearly must have been something that he had been taught. Any sign of bad or good news would make the recipient of the information guess what they were going to be told before hearing it for themselves. That was not what the doctors wanted to happen as it could either get their hopes up, or destroy them before getting the official news. The doctor, decked out in his white coat over his blue pants and blue top walks up to Salaok and puts his hand out as a welcome. ”Mr. Davis, correct?” ”I am” Replies Salaok as he begins to guess where this is going. The doctor indicates for Salaok to sit down and instantly, Salaok begins to think the worst. He wouldn’t need to sit down unless it was bad news. Anyone can take good news standing up, but bad news always needs people to be sitting. Regretfully, Salaok sits down and the doctors does the same as all eyes turn to Salaok. Grendel and Kullypso stand there at either end of the small section of the waiting bay, preventing anyone from walking through the area. ”Mr. Davis, I’m not going to lie to you. The bullet that entered your wife’s body has caused major internal bleeding and she has lost a lot of blood and her body isn’t accepting the blood we give her.” The sound the followed next would have shaken the windows and been heard all throughout the hospital as a two letter word roars to life from Salaok’s lungs at a deafening level. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO.” Salaok head falls into hi hands, which are propped up by rests his elbows in his knees. But the doctor hasn’t finished with the news. ”There is something else you should know Mr. Davis. The child that your wife was carrying was not yet matured enough to survive without your wife’s body.” The news was devastating and for the first time in years, Salaok felt a tear run down the side of his face. Salaok had lost his beloved wife and unborn child and there was nothing he could do about it. Everything else in his life he had been able to control or pay people off in order to fix, but this, this was different and Salaok knew it. The doctor, although not wanting to say a word, speaks up. ”Mr. Davis, you will be able to go in and see your wife in a minute and be with her for her passing if you so wish.” ”I do.” That same word that he had spoken on their wedding day to start their life together, would be the same word that would allow him to see his wife for the last time. The doctor gets up and goes back into the room as Salaok sits there in silence. No one says a word as that tear finally reaches his chin and then with no where else to go, drops to the floor. The small splash can easily be heard in the silent deathly atmosphere. At that exact time, Jackson Diasporo walks into the waiting bay, knowing full well what has happened and knowing that Salaok was going to give the order. Salaok slowly turns his head to look up at Jackson and through his tear blurred eyes gives Jackson a look, which he understands. With a voice, wrecked with emotion, Salaok speaks the words of the order. ”Mani must die. I will double your contracted amount for what he has done to this family.” That was all. The contract had just been doubled and for that amount of money, Jackson would be able to assemble a team that would hunt down the forgotten Davis brother and destroy him for his crimes against the family. Jackson nods and replies. “It will be my honor Salaok. It will be my honor.” Salaok nods before standing up and walking towards the door of the operation room where Lisha lies. As his hand reaches out and presses on the door, he stops and turns to Grendel. ”Tell Sage to bring Darrell to see his mother.” Grendel doesn’t say a word, only a nod and a quick movement away down the hall as Salaok pushes the door open. He steps into the room and there she lay, awake now and changed into clean hospital clothes as all of the external bleeding had been contained. There was nothing to show that Lisha wasn’t going to pull through except for the knowledge that the internal bleeding wouldn’t stop and her body wouldn’t take the blood on. Her face was that of an angel. Although on death’s bed, her face was always strong and beautiful. She would never let anyone other than Salaok know that she was hurting, but even now, she stays strong for her husband. He moves over to the side of the bed and grabs a hold of her hand as she smiles. It wasn’t a forced smile, but one that was heart felt and one that she was glad she could give him. ”I’ve excepted it Salaok and you must now do the same.” Her voice of calm reason was one thing that Salaok could not bare to do without. He brings her hand up to his lips and plants a soft kiss on her tender and now pale skin. ”I will never accept what they have done to you my dear.” ”In the end Sal, you will see that everything that has ever happened, happened for a reason. Every action has it’s purpose whether you like it or not Salaok.” Salaok lowers his eyes, not worthy of being in the presence of such a woman. Although not a lot of strength is left in her body, she nudges Salaok’s chin with her hand to make him look at her. ”Make sure that Darrell remains safe at all times. And in doing so, I will make sure you are the same. I will watch over you Salaok and be there when you ask for guidance. When you need me, just call out my name and I will be there.” ”You didn’t fight this did you? You gave up back at the house.” Taking Salaok’s hand in both of hers, she holds them tight. ”I didn’t give up Sal, I accepted that which had been given to me.” As soon as Lisha finishes the door opens and Sage walks into the room, behind Darrell who still has tears streaming down his face. ”Mumma!” Darrell runs across the room and throws his upper body over the bed. Lisha’s hands let go of Salaok as she wraps her arms around Darrell’s body and squeezes him with everything she’s got. She looks up at Sage and smiles.”Thank you Sage. Now make sure you look after my boys now.” Sage stands there silent as a tear runs down his cheek before opening his mouth. ”I will Lisha. And thank you.” Lisha nods in understanding of what Sage meant and then turns her attention to Darrell in her arms. In the calmest voice she could possibly muster, she begins to explain things to Darrell and Salaok can do nothing but stand there in silence and in awe of this beautiful person who he is about to lose. ”Darrell my dear I have something to tell you. I’m going to be going away to a place you will never be able to go. My life is coming to an end, but you life is only starting. Remember everything I have told you and you will grow up to be a great man.” Darrell, now completely overwhelmed by the hurt inside his little heart, talking through his cries. ”I don’t want you to go Mumma. Why do you have to go?” ”I’m dying, Darrell. The doctors can’t stop the bleeding inside of me. But don’t worry. I will be all right. Wipe away those tears and be strong for me Darrell. I need you to be strong.” Darrell’s cries don’t stop, however they do reduce in volume as he tries to be strong. But for a child losing his mother, there is nothing in the world that is harder to try and be strong through. ”I don’t want to be strong Mum. I just want to be with you.” ”I know dear, but you can’t be with me. One day we will be together again, I give you my word on that.” Suddenly Lisha visibly shivers as her body begins to go cold and she grimaces with the pain which luckily Darrell doesn’t see, but Salaok and Sage certainly do. ”I will forever be with you in your hearts as long as you remember that I love you.” With that said, Lisha closes her eyes and let one last breath exhale from her lungs as Salaok drops to his knees next to the bed. ”I will never stop loving you Lisha. Never.”
