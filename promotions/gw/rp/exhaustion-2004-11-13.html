Exhaustion

13 November 2004

Date: 11.13.04 Ken's head landed hard on the white silk pillows lacing the head of his bed. The thick five o'clock shadow on his unshaven face showed the exhaustion that his body had been taking and his eyes showed the wear and tear that his life was finally taking on him before they closed. The bed felt so good beneath him as he lay down, allowing his body to rest for the first time in weeks and giving himself some time, even if it was only a few minutes to rejuvinate. He stretched his legs out until he felt his hips pop and allowed a dull roar to emit from his mouth as he did so before pulling them back in and drifting off. It didn't take long, it rarely did as of late, once he got the opportunity to lay down... or even sit down and relax, for that matter. His rest had been in effect for less than five minutes when Gunner entered and looked down at her still fully clothed lover as he looked to be in peace for the first time since the Red Sox had won the World Series. She knew that his world was too stressful at the moment. It seemed to be one thing on top of another for him that, good or bad, was running him rampid. After Silent Wind, there was Ice turning his back on them and the rest of The Elite along with the return of Maverick Reno. It would seem that him getting placed in the Tournament of the Worthy after his hospital stay following Dark Rituals would have been a brighter note and, while it actually was, it's done nothing but make him work harder. Now, he'd realized that John Zero was involved with his attempted murder, whcih wasn't making things any easier on the man she was proud to say she loved. You factor in his restauraunt and bar, and it's a wonder that he can even stand at times. Gunner smiled a sorrowful smile onto her Superstar. She couldn't bear to wake him, nor would she dream of it. Their date could wait for another time but Ken needed to rest. She sttempted not to wake him up as she removed his black flat-toed tuxedo shoes by Doc Martin and then his dark slacks from the plaid collection by Ralph Lauren's Purple Label. The tugging on his pants awoke Ken as he lay groggily on his back in the bed, allowing his eyes to flitter to life. Gunner noticed his stirring and quickly stopped, looking up with an apologetic look in her eyes. 'I'm sorry,' she proclaimed softly in the voice that Ken had come to attatch to the word beauty. ' didn't want to wake you, Sweetheart. I was just going to try and make you more comfortable.' A smile spread across Ken's face as her vision filled his sight. 'It's okay, I needed to get up and get ready for the show anyways,' he said as he started to sit up in the bed. 'I can see Phantom anytime, Ken,' she announced as she gently pushed him back onto his back. 'You need to sleep, and don't bother arguing, because it will get you aboslutly nowhere, undertstand.' His smile became warmer, 'I understand. Thank you.' 'No need for thanks,' she whispered as she continued pulling off his slacks and socks. 'You need to be well rested for Evolution. Because, Hun, at this rate, you're going to end up killing yourself,' she said with a worried tone in her voice as she moved to Ken's chest and rested her hand upon the black button-up that adorned his torso. 'If I have you,' he said, his smile conceding to the worry he'd caused her, 'I'm not afraid of dying.' She gazed into his eyes as tears welled up in her own. 'But I am afraid of you dying, Ken. I love you too much for you to leave me.' He reached up and pulled her down onto him, allowing her head to bury into his chest, and embraced her firmly and lovingly. 'I'm not going to leave you, Gun. I love you too much to lose you again. It killed me the first time, I'm not putting either of us through it again.' She sat back up and looked at him again, drying her tears as she did so. A smile slowly formed across her faceas she leaned in and kissed him softly on the lips. She pulled away after only a few seconds and began unbuttoning his shirt, allowing his chest and abdomen to breath up and down unrestricted before she removed it from his body. The two kissed again, this time longer and more passionate. As they released on another, Ken's hands moved to her face and then down her neck to her shoulders, where he removed the straps of her dress and watched it fall to the floor. He pulled her back to him and the two embraced romantically. Neither wanted anything paricularly sexual to happen and instead, allowed the romance and emotions and their heart to make love to each other as the two lay holding one another. 'You know that John Zero's going to come back for you,' she said quietly with fear in her voice. He kissed her again, 'I'm counting on it.' ... 'I love you.' 'I love you.' ... Those were the last words spoken as the two fell asleep in one another's arms. Ken slept well that night, as it was the one night that nightmares weren't haunting him. The one night that a smile remained across his face. The one night he could have died... and been happy.
