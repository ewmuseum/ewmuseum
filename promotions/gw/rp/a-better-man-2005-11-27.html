A better man.

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/05 On my way back to Tokyo... It's been a little over a year since I was last there. And it changed my life at that time. At the moment I had just unmasked myself from another persona in the ring, and taken on a tag team partner that seemed to have been forgotten in the shuffle of the regular wrestling world. She had the looks, at attitude, but she wasn't like the others. She didn't flaunt her body around for them to see... thus she was more of an afterthought when it came to the so called 'wrestling divas' that were predominant. And what did we do? We took the tag team titles. Tokyo, actually Japan has always been good to me. A few nights before we took those titles, we took something else too: Each other. And, we're still together. A street tough girl from Tokyo, and a whacked out boy from the Las Vegas area. I'm more messed up then a jigsaw puzzle, and she's my anchor into the real world. As much real as it gets for us anymore. Now what do I get by coming to Tokyo again? A chance at redeeming myself in the glory that as a man I always crave. I won't lie, winning Showdown in Tokyo for Global would mean a fast track to the top. It would mean bypassing all the niceties and preliminary stuff. It would mean instant glory and success, and the third, nagging side of me would be sated for a bit. It would have what it feels it needs. I would feed one of those seven vices that humans have a tendency to want to break, no matter what holy books they follow. In my case, I don't have that burden, but others will still try to chastise me for it. I want to win, I need to win. I desire it. If you've not noticed my time in Global has been about making waves, about taking down the competition to a new level for them, and using them to boost myself. Virgil Ambrosi and Shadow... Two title holders right now, platinum and International. I beat them. They beat me, but I beat them with the help of my new stablemate. She and I are on the same team going into this thing. We're a team. It doesn't matter about Grest and his pranks here. He's part of the team for a few short moments. As for this hall of famer guy who I don't even know... just pull your part Mr. Rusty. Once we've taken down the other team, then it's time to shine. Dani will have to realize that once we make it to the finals, it's time to be a team still, we're teammates till the end. We're part of the Order, we're part of the Brigade. But when it comes down to us, only one can win. That's where the honorable thing really needs to happen. One of use has to win. We can't afford for the others to do that. Jake Hix is already the number one contender by his last win over Dani. Dani has a shot at Zenith in the big PTC Tournament going on. She can beat him, I know it. If she does, she should basically earn a shot at Zenith's title also. For me, I would need to win this event. I would need to take things by the horn and win it. It needs to be about me. It needs to be about my pride, my greed, my lust for reclaiming what I should rightfully have. It's been a while. A long while. I know that others will be gunning for me. If they're smart they will be. They'll look at all I've done over the handful of weeks I've been here and they'll know who the target should be. Ambrosi: Beaten in a way. It wasn't a final battle, not by a long shot. And I've claimed a second contendership spot behind KPJ for his belt. Shadow: I was a surprise to her. One she couldn't handle. We both fell to the referee's hands slapping the mat, but it should have shown the others what they were getting into. Elia Smith: Former TV champion, one who wasn't ready for that title. But one that has so much potential. Sometimes you have to learn from the losses too. Glad I could oblige. Mike “Marvelous” Biddle: Supposedly the Number One Contender for the International Title held by Shadow. If that's the case, the way I mopped the octagon up with him, that should mean I could claim that title. I really should, but then again he was probably not on his game that night. He was facing a McClancy, now wasn't he? And the last one... Brick. Former everything, grand slam champion. He may be built like a brick shit-house, but he sure as hell fell down like one too, after a demolition ball had hit it. I don't play favorites. Fans had like or hate the person in the ring with me. In the end, they'll have either been through one hell of a fight, or they would have fallen down to someone who is undeniably... Superior. Superior, a word that will follow me for life. I was the longest ever reigning Superior Champion. But it's a word that many can't deal with. They fail to realize their own shortcomings until they're laying at my feet. Showdown in Tokyo is my time to prove that to them. I can't have things going down half-assed, not by a longshot. But, that's talking about what I need to do while I'm in the ring. What I need to do to put my name down in Global History. What I need to do to claim that which I see as my destiny to eventually claim. Truth be told: If my exploits were to ever be written down, they would speak of the way that I did just that. I took what I wanted. And, I did it in a way that the person I took it from couldn't argue with. I proved my superiority. I am the better wrestler. I am the better fighter. But am I the better person? Does it make me a better father? Does it make me a better lover? Does it make me a better... man? That's still up in the air. Those are things that take place, outside the ring. Clark County Regional Justice Center Las Vegas, Nevada Early Last Week Blaise sat in the judges chambers. This wasn't a regular visit, not even one that was needed every few weeks for him to show up with his daughters to be interviewed over how his 90 days with them was going. This was something he needed though. He had plans for his trip to Tokyo, and those plans needed things to be straight and narrow, setup and approved. The judge that had granted him custody of his daughters while their biological mother and stepfather were pending trial for their other infractions. Blaise was wearing the equivalent of a suit, with no tie on a collarless shirt underneath the jacket. His now growing out hair was pulled back into a tight pony tail to keep it out of his face, and his usual five o'clock stubble was neatly shaven. The judge sat there, looking tiny compared to her large desk, let alone the large leather chair they gave her to sit in. It was intended to be imposing, so that the smaller woman could come off with the authority she needed in the case of looking down someone the size of Blaise, or bigger. Not saying that Blaise was a big man; but he sure wasn't a slouch in the gym. “So, Mr. McClancy, what brings you into my chambers this day” Your appointment for your 60 day checkup isn't until next week.” Blaise swallowed a load of spit down. He was actually getting.. nervous. But if he could get through the other things he had going on over in Tokyo, he could get through anything. Even those two matches that he'd potentially be in. “Yes, well I need your permission for something that has come up. This weekend we're going to Tokyo, Japan.” The Judge leaned back in her chair, her hands folded in her lap. “Ah, yes. Your job with that federation, Global. I'll have to order the Pay Per View for this show. I understand there is a big prize instore for the winner. Well, as you know I've approved all of your trips out of the state. You have that nice girl, Kaylee, as a nanny for the days you're gone. And I've approved your parents to act as long term babysitters if it's only a few days. What more do you need?” His nerves were quite a bit off. “Kaylee won't be available this coming weekend. And my parents both wish to be in Tokyo to see me at the event. It's hopefully going to be a big day. Although that's their reasons. I also kneed them in Tokyo for the Saturday before that...” He didn't fill her in fully. He knew she'd ask more questions. “You need them there” What for?” Blaise stumbled. “Uhm... din... dinner.” She cocked her head a bit askew and looked at him. He felt his palms starting to sweat. He could sense that she was picking this up. “Dinner?” He held it back for a moment, trying to figure out a way to word it. “Yes, dinner. Dinner between two families. The Kushinagas and the McClancys of Nevada. It's a traditional thing, almost a ceremony. I can't really fill you in on more. They'll be arriving a few days behind the rest of us. Staying for Saturday, Sunday and then the event on Monday. And well... I'd like my daughters there for this, too.” She smiled at him, wide and full teethed. It was a politician's smile. But one that was actually coming across with sincerity. “I understand Mr. McClancy, fully.” He exhaled loudly. “You do?” She smiled even wider at him. “Yes. My husband is from Okinawa. We met while I was in the Air Force. I know all too well what you are saying. I'll sign the release for you. With the weather around places, and since you've come to me first, I'll add an extra day on either side. For a period of five days the girls will be allowed to travel between the states and Japan. They must stay within a fifty mile radius of the city of Tokyo's outer limits. That gives you some leeway to sight see with them. If they are not back in the states by Wednesday next week, a warrant will be issued. You do understand this” Right?” Blaise nodded. “I'll not be taking more time then that. I don't need it. I've got to be back in the states for this tournament that I'm in for PrimeTime Central, too.” She reached out and pulled the papers in front of her closer, as she pulled out a large Dr. Grip style pen. “I don't doubt that Mr. McClancy. I just need to make sure you know the rules. Coming up in January, you'll have hit your ninety days. I don't think you want to ruin anything. So far you're doing quite well. Don't mess it up so close to the end.” With that she edited some of the information on it, and signed her name... All was setup on the legal standpoint. Lying here awake again. Minutes before dawn I hear your breathing, your heart beat like a song. Lately I’ve been feeling a little less then good but seeing things for the first time like I never could. A few days later: Saturday Morning local time. Tokyo, Japan. They had flown on a commercial flight, which has really taken it wrong with Dani. She hated to fly so much, that they had to suggest tranquilizers like you would an animal. After they had got off the plane though, that's when things went very different. They were staying at the Imperial Hotel, the premier hotel in Tokyo. It was near the Palace itself, and was named that way for a reason. It was early Friday morning, and they had just enough time to get settled in and get everything unpacked, when Blaise had hit Ukyo with the other plans. As she stood there, finishing the unpacking on one of her bags, Blaise spoke out as he looked at the clock. “Well, it's early in the morning, just right time. They should be awake by now.” Ukyo didn't look at him as she answered. She'd gotten used to him being around to that point. “They” I was hoping to get some rest before we headed out.” He smiled as he was working behind her, packing a small bag with some of her clothing that she had already put into the dresser, into his backpack he'd brought along. “It was a fourteen hour flight, and we've been here a bit more then an hour. You slept plenty on the airplane.” She giggled. “Not like the choice of movies didn't dictate that to be the case. Chick flicks suck so bad.” Blaise laughed. “At least they didn't show that movie about the plane crashing like they did up to Alaska. Dani really didn't need that.” Ukyo almost burst out laughing, but pulled it back. She didn't see it right to laugh at her friends phobias. “Well, where are you planning on going?” Blaise tossed the backpack over his shoulder. “There are some things that need to be handled as soon as possible. I arranged for lunch with some special people here. And I know there is something you need to do. They kinda run into each other... we're expected at your parents house by ten. The car should already be waiting.” Ukyo dropped the clothes that she was holding back onto the bed. He'd gone behind her back for something and that wasn't too much like him. The last time she had seen her parents in person was a few months ago when they had visited Seattle to see her. Right before the had to announce their disowning of her adopted brother, Kyo. “Where are we meeting them?” Blaise looked down at the floor. “At their place. Your house where you grew up in. I know that you also have need to do something else. The shrine for him is in their garden.” Ukyo went silent. She knew he was right, and was more then likely trying to put it off. There were things that just felt heavy on the heart. Things that she hadn't had time in the last year to take care of. “You coming?” Blaise asked her, as he looked up. “Yeah. Something tells me that I have to thank you for taking care of this.” She turned and grabbed Blaise's old jean jacket that she'd taken to borrowing. It would only be up to the sixties today, but the mornings were a bit chilly. The jacket would do her well. I’ve had my head down against the door, trying to get to something I couldn’t find before. That special something, that feeds my soul, is a rhythm to live by, it’s a rhythm of hope. About an hour later They found themselves walking up to the front door of the large house, together. Blaise dropped the backpack down on the porch, and then took off the tennis shoes he'd worn out. Ukyo slipped out of her shoes as an older woman answered the door. The woman looked rather pretty, even for her age and having two kids in her life. She looked just like an older version of Ukyo, except with a normal shade of very dark brown, almost black for a hair color. She started to greet the two in Japanese, when she suddenly yelled out and slammed the door open to embrace her daughter in a hug. An older man walked up behind her after that. He looked to Blaise and nodded his head. “Yukio-Sama.” Blaise said as he addressed her father, Yukio. He knew it had the same 'verbal sound' as his daughers name, but it was far from it. Yukio Kushinaga, or Kushinaga Yukio to be proper, was the only person in Japan to ever tell Blaise that his Japanese was atrocious, and that he preferred him speaking English. He'd been a business man who had done dealings in California for many years, and spoke it well enough. However, hearing Blaise say his name with the proper address to it made him smile. “I see you two made it. And from the tear in my daughters eye, I can see that you followed my dare and didn't tell her.” Blaise nodded at that. Then over lunch we can speak of good things.” He father spoke those words and nodded to his wife of many years. She took Ukyo by the arm and led her to the back door of the large house. Yukio turned to Blaise. “Now, it's time for you and I to talk, yes?” Blaise nodded. “Shall we have some tea?” “Hai... yes” Blaise slipped out the Japanese and then corrected himself. “Ah, that's one word you don't mess up with your strong accent. I know what you plan to ask me Mr. McClancy. Fortunately for you, I've been prepared for it. Everything but her preparation is ready.” Blaise looked at him strangely. “All you have to do is ask.” Blaise's nerves were coming back. “You know?” “Why else would you ask to have dinner here the day after you get in town” But you also book through your Order the flight of your parents and children here. A lucky day to have a ceremony.” Something drives us onwards. Something gives us strength. What makes our judgment falter is the questions we think. Who am I to fly so high? But it had served it's purpose for something even more special. Have you been down like this before? I guarantee there’s something waiting for you just open that door. A rhythm to live by, that moves your soul. A rhythm that holds you. A rhythm to live to. Afternoon, the next day. The Kushinaga Residence. A motley crew stood outside the door to the place where the family of Ukyo Kushinaga lived. Two young girls, dressed in nice dresses, their fire-red hair done up in pigtails. Their grandmother stood there, proud as could be in a dress that did her darker skin good. Maria McClancy, a full blooded Moapa Paiute Indian looked the part, even if she did dress in a modern style. Her raven hair was pulled back out of her face, tied by proper, traditional means. The two that really stood out from the others were wearing white. White with black and gold trim. The sabers at their side showed that it was some form of military uniform. It was the one that not many people saw in their lives. The dress uniform of the officers of the Order of the Hammer. Standing in front of the door were two officers. Both of different branches, but still officers all the same. Their high collars were emblazoned with their rank insignia, one a Commander, the other a Lieutenant. Both with hair that matched the little girls, they showed that the girls carried their genes. Not only were they wearing the ranks granted them by the Order, but their shoulders held other insignia that only they were allowed to wear in the Order, them and their ilk. They were representatives of the Royal Family of Tir Na Nog, the Faery Realm of Ireland. Both princes of the royal line. One Conner McClancy, the bearer of the Mantle of the old King Elatha, but currently reduced to the status of Prince while King Oberon sat on the throne. And his son, the bearer of the mantle of Prince Turren, son of Elatha. Blaise McClancy... The door opened, after everyone had removed their boots and shoes, and the family started to walk into the house. It had been decorated for the event at hand. In the main living room was a table setup, large, and low to the ground. The cushions around were provided for everyone to eat the dinner properly, in the right manner. Along the back wall, another, taller table had been setup. Laid out upon it were assorted thing that appeared to have been delivered this morning, including a cask of what looked like Sake. Blaise looked over and sighed seeing that the delivery had been made. Yukio Kushinaga walked over to Conner and greeted him with a very western style hand shake. The two talked as Ukyo's mother, Ayame, greeted Maria and the two younger McClancy's. They made light of everything, helping the westerners get to their seats for their proper placement. As soon as it looked like everyone was ready, Ayame bowed out and then headed towards the back of the house for a moment. Blaise was the only one standing, next to the two open cushions provided in between everyone. And then he saw her, as her mother brought her in. Dressed in a traditional kimono, Ukyo walked in with her escort. Her lavender dyed hair was done up in a traditional style, pinned back out of her face. Her face had been done up with the proper types of makeup for the event. Blaise felt his heart jump to his throat as she walked towards him. The nerves where there again, just like when he had been thinking about this moment every time he got to over the last few weeks. Now it was here. He had his part to do, the Western part of the tradition before the dinner could be served. He forced the feelings of nervousness away as she stepped towards him. When this was done, other arrangements would need to be made, but it would be official to both families that soon they would become one. She walked up to stand next to him, a nervous smile on her face also. It had been a long time since the self professed tomboy from Tokyo had dressed so... feminine in her own country. But for now, she appeared to lavish in it. Especially with Blaise standing in front of her, in his uniform. The last time she'd seen him in that uniform, he had been taking the place of the best man in his cousin's marriage. Only this time it wasn't for someone else. It was for her. She choked back a tear as one Blaise William Todd McClancy turned to her and took her hand in his left. From his right hand, he pulled a small box, covered in the same emerald green velvet that it's case had been in, out of his pocket. Sometimes fate gives you only one hand of cards to deal with. This week Global went to Tokyo, the home town of his lover and girlfriend. The country and area that they had decided about a year ago to join together as lovers. You don't look a give horse in the mouth. You put the reigns on it, and ride it around, to see what it'll give to you. Today it gave Blaise the chance to finally do things the right away, and move his future in the path he wants it to. All he had to do was reach out and grasp them. And that's what he was going to do as he opened the case to reveal a special item. A ring, a ring of gold, with a large diamond in the center, surrounded by emeralds, the official stone of the Seelie Courts of Tir Na Nog. Ukyo had seen it before, and she knew the story behind it. It was the same ring that Conner had used to propose to Maria. One of the few rings of the royal family in the line of Ogma, Blaise's grandfather by rights in the courts. For Ukyo, things were just beginning to settle in, and make sense. It was a good thing as she saw Blaise drop down onto one knee before her. Lying here awake again. To be continued, always.
