Blood calls to blood Prt2

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/9/06 1300 Hours?. Jaques Martel listened intently to the monotone voice coming out of the receiver he’d placed in the small burned out building he’d set up as his base. “We don’t know what he’s here for, this story about his brother could be a lie, we’ve invested too much in this project to have it brought to light just cause you had some issue with your conscience. Go back there Colonel and kill him, take his gear and burn his body with the rest of the diseased.” Then there was static. Jacques sighed and rubbed a hand over his bald head for several moments taking it all in. His company had been storming quietly along this border attempting to erase any evidence of the experiments that had been going on. Until now they hadn’t encountered anyone who wasn’t covered in those grotesque pustules. Now that they had things would be a little different. He didn’t enjoy killing, and he most certainly didn’t enjoy killing a man who was unarmed. Walking over the make shift desk he’d set up, Jacques picks up his side arm, flicking off the safety he walks out of the doorway, the gun resting behind his leg as he steals up on his men guarding Cervantes who had spent the day rummaging through hundreds of dog tags. The air outside was dry, as the moisture is eaten up by the smoldering pile of bodies at the edge of the camp. The smell of burnt flesh hangs in the air thick enough to choke on it and the afternoon sky is dark with clouds churning for the coming of thunder. As the foreboding weather loomed overhead, Micheal Cervantes suddenly looked up and saw Jacques. “It’s not here.” he says. Jacques does not reply only keeps walking forward. “I’m leaving.” Cervantes says, his voice cold. Jacques stops, his gun now hanging visibly at his side, the two other men step back slightly there hands resting more firmly on there own guns. Cervantes stares daggers into Jacques. “I have gambled all my life Mr. Cervantes, mostly with my own life. But I have always played by the rules, I’ve never turned a card nor shot a man in the back and I won’t change those rules today.” Jacques lets the gun drop and kicks it to the side, with two snaps at his waist he removes his tactical vest and opens his arms wide. “I will fight you Mr. Cervantes” man to man. The winner gets to leave this forest alive. The loser on the other hand, well he’ll be dead.” Jacques strokes his mustache, a large pearly smile on his face. ? Michael Cervantes wasn’t going to bother trying to sort this out. He had an opportunity that his instincts were screaming at him to snatch up and Michael Cervantes was never a man to disobey his instincts. Darting forward like a rattle snake he moves in low before suddenly lashing out with his right foot, but before he could reach his target he was met with an almost bone shattering boot to the side of the leg dropping him his shoulder. Jacques laughs from deep within his chest and stands over Cervantes, his body is relaxed, his lead foot raised and his hands in a classic boxers position. “Did I mention I have been the top Savatte fighter three years running now” it’s a private passion of mine.” Bringing his foot verticle Jacques pauses before dropping the heel but Cervantes is no long there, scrambling to the side and forward he drives his foot into the side of Martel’s knee. To avoid having the knee broken Martel pivots with it falling to the ground but rolling back to his feet. Both men stand several feet apart from each other in there respective stances. Martel comes forward, feints high with his lead foot but before he can drive forward with his other foot Cervantes is in his face. Driving his head into Martel’s mouth and then dropping low with an elbow driving it into the socket of his hip. On instinct Martel brings his knee to meet his elbow in a clinching maneuver connecting with Cervantes? back and chest sending him staggering back to catch his breath. “Pankration?” Martel asks. Cervantes nods, his face set in grim determination, his head bleeds from a cut given to him by Martel’s teeth when he shoved his head in his mouth. He inches forward then darts left but changes right and instead of dropping to the legs he takes to the air coming forward and driving his elbow into the bridge of Martel’s nose rewarding him with the crunch of bone and the snap of cartilage as blood bursts out in a torrent of red. Not stopping Cervantes presses the advantage stepping to the side he drives his fist into Martel’s kidney over and over until the man drops to a knee. Gripping Martel’s head he drives his knee forward but his legs are suddenly wrapped up in Martel’s large arms and the man powers forward hurling Cervantes into the dirt and proceeds to drive swift and powerful kicks into Cervantes? side. Using his arms and legs to protect his torso and head Cervantes attempts to roll out of the way but Martel moves quickly to cut him off with a swift stomp of his heel. As Cervantes feels his body convulse and blood begins to drip form his mouth. Lashing out with his arms in a last ditch effort, Cervantes grabs both of Martel’s legs and ties himself into them keeping him from kicking. Following with a quick strike to the groin he topples and Martel and rolls on top gripping his right leg as he goes and wrenching back and to the side in a classic submission half crab. As thunder breaks over head and lightning begins to streak across the sky, A fog begins to rise. Martel powers himself out of the submission and scrambles to his feet, he’s met with a strong cross to the face followed by a short and compact uppercut to the ribs. Cervantes cuts into Jacques with brutal efficiency collapsing as he does, a punch leads to an elbow leads to a shoulder butt leads to a back fist. As Martel begins to stagger under the relentless assault of a man who seems possessed with an otherworldly fury; not the anger shown in some, but a cold fury of a shark who smells his next meal. Martel attempt to riposte but his hand is slapped aside and he is answered with a sudden surge forward from Michael who drives his knee into the solar plexus of his adversary. As rain begins to fall, slow at first then with compounding frenzy, Jacques soldiers seem to fade into the ever thinking fog that has now become the spectator of this marathon of brutality. Jacques takes the next hit to the head but with a renewed vitality answers with the quick roundhouse to the stomach followed by an axe kick to the back of the head anger seething from his face, his head now red from rage he leaps into the air driving both heels into the back of Cervantes? skull. The rain came in torrents through the fog, soaking the ground and churning it into mud. All Cervantes could see was stars, it felt like his head had swollen and he could feel nothing but a dull numb pain. He tried to move, to get up and crush that son of a bitch but try as he might his body wouldn’t respond. All he could do was lie face first in the sinking mud in puddles of rain and his own blood. Jacques Martel smiled a bloody smile, he stood regally in the rain, his chest heaving from the exertion. As he prepared to finish the job he heard the sound of whistling cutting clear through the thunder. It wasn’t a song, it wasn’t exactly a tune, it was like the absent minded whistling of a man at work. Pausing as he prepares to strike his eyes search futile through the thick fog. “Pier! Jean!” he shouts to his two subordinates. Minutes pass and the whistling continues, there is no answer from Pier or Jean. Michael Cervantes is as still as a dead man and Martel perches over him like a bird of prey? but something is coming and Martel can feel it, every step it takes as the whistling gets louder and as the rain falls harder? He strikes without warning, like a wraith from the darkness the knife enters Jacques side and is out before he can react. In the fog is a silhouette? something from his past, something from his nightmares. Even through the thick fog he sees the outline of the hat? the length of the coat he can almost see those blue eyes peeling back his flesh and staring at his soul. His blood mixes with the earth, he staggers losing his balance he drops to his knee’s his chest pounding as the silhouette grows larger and a voice rings out like a razor. “Colonel Jacques Martel, born a bastard of a French man and a German woman, not very smart and lacked any real skill in sports. It wasn’t till joining the military that you found yourself I believe” Infact if I remember correctly. Until you finished boot camp I remember you were rather scrawny and pathetic, you were almost afraid of your own shadow.” Jacques shuddered, whether from the rain, the loss of blood or the voice he didn’t know. Cervantes had begun to stir but he couldn’t move, couldn’t tare his eyes away from the man in the fog. “But look how you have changed!” the voice raised in pitch, almost ecstatic. “Strong, fast and tough you’re an exemplary soldier aren’t you?” “W-who is that” I-I don’t know you!” the scream comes without control, anger boiling to the surface, fear sweating out of him. He begins to droop, his loss of blood causing him to lose his strength just as Cervantes regains his. “In spite of all that..” the voice drops, venom oozes from unseen lips as he continues as if he couldn’t even here Jaques. ??I still see the shrimp, the little boy who was beat by his father and unloved by his mother. I see the same boy, illegitimate and anabaptized looked down upon by the community? too poor for formal schooling you would hang around the yards and pick fights you knew you couldn’t win; attempting to show how tough and brave you really were? but in the end you were beaten and soundly if I remember. All this has caused you to be an overly brash man, although confident on the outside you leap aggressively at any chance to prove yourself to others using excuses as conscience and honor to back up your machismo attitude.” Cervantes had rolled away, into the fog, giving wide berth to Martel and the man. As the silhouette began to part the man in the hat stepped as if out of a dream or rather, a nightmare, a bloody knife hanging in his left hand. With a wide smile that has caused blood to curdle, the man behind the low hanging wide brimmed hat spit words of contempt. “I’ve ran into you before Jacques Martel! I know who you are and where you came from! I told you then to never interfere in my affairs but here we are, almost seven years later with your grubby French fingers in my business!” spit flies from his mouth with each punctuated word. “You could have ruined everything! Over a decade of planning, over a decade of sweat, blood and patience!” Stepping ever forward, he seems to glide over the mud like and ice skater on ice, the knife hanging loosely like a wolf circling for a kill. “I-I k-know you! S-Sc.” as the words escape from his lips the man darts with amazing speed bringing the long wicked knife to bare drive it in Martel’s neck and tearing it across turning his head into a pez dispenser spraying blood into the air with tremendous force. The man that had once been a powerhouse in the French military and Global Wrestling now lay dead at the feet of one of the most hated men in the world. “.. Michael Cervantes watches in a detached haze while thunder and lighting tore the sky apart, as The Scarecrow stands over the dead body of the French colonel with a smile on his face that Michael had grown to both hate and respect. Covered in blood his mentor turns those terrible blue eyes towards Cervantes that same wicked smile playing across his lips. “I have been looking for you” he says almost with a whisper. Cervantes staggers to his feet, his head pounding his face and ears bleeding, “Is it time?” he replies. The Scarecrow’s words spoke of finality “It’s time” he says and The Scarecrow with his prot’g? stand in the fog in veritable lake of blood and mud. That night they would be in Las Vegas and the world would be reunited with the man the Scarecrow had begun grooming as the new Harvester of Fear and the world will tremble and bleed.
