Excerpt From Jin's Journal Jin Akiyara Global Wrestling Promo #532 Date: 08/20/2003 Location: Jin's Journal August 20, 2003 People say I am undeserving They say I am being handed success on a silver platter A mere child being silver spoon fed The people say I have been lucky all my life. To be a nineteen year old hired by one of the best federations in the world with such little experience meant that someone up there must love me. How else could they explain it? How else could I explain it? I never won a title in another federation. I never made it to the wrestling magazine’s front cover. I never even a match. Hell…I was never in another federation period. All there was, was just the training gyms in Hawaii. And even there I was nothing. So what am I doing here? Is this God’s way of mending the pains of my past? Of my sister’s past? Is this some retribution to make up for the alcoholic parents we had? What am I doing here? I’ll tell them what I’m doing here. I’m making the best of it. I’m fighting because I choose to fight. I fight for the people, to entertain them. I fight to make sure they are happy, because at least one of us will be. I fight to be the best, not in terms of wins, but in terms of memory. With each passing day I feel more and more at home. Maybe that’s the real reason I belong here. To feel at home…to be in a place where my coworkers share the same cause. Maybe deep down what I really want is just to be part of the show. I don’t care if it’s a small part of a big part. I just want to be able to say yes, I am a part of GW. I want to say yes, I live in Dallas. I want to say yes, this is my home. So now I’m here, fighting, hurting, loving it. I still got a long way to go, but things seem to be working out fine. I won my first match, and then won the Cruiserweight Championship. People doubted me, and even I doubted myself. I didn’t expect to win, because quite frankly I was concerned with doing the right thing. I wanted to make sure I didn’t do anything wrong. I was worried about messing up in the ring, messing up my career, messing up people’s expectations. But somewhere along those lines, I won. I won more than just a match. There was more than just a pin to win the contender’s spot before Master of the Mat. There was more than hitting Ice with the Epiphany and pulling down the title. There was more than soaring high above with the Price of Glory. There was much more than just a random referee counting to three. I won the approval of the people. I won the approval of the fans. And if I could take all those things, I would only keep the approval of the fans. They liked me. They, dare I say it, loved me. Without them, I would have no need to compete. Without them I would know no motivation. Without them, I would not even be employed. They keep me here. And as long as they want to see me, GW will always have me. And this is more than just a few wins, and a measly title. Because deep down inside me, that’s how I truly feel. Any man can win a match, be it by good deeds or evil methods. Any man can wear a gold strap around his waist if he so chooses to. And most people…they choose so for all the wrong reasons. But it takes a better man to know that the business we run is more than just self-glory. The title I have? It’s just strap. An indication that reminds me I won a certain match because I worked hard for it. It doesn’t say I am the best. It does not say I am deserving of it. All it says is that one night, one match; I managed to outperform another individual long enough for three seconds of their life. That’s all it is. You want to know what I cherish more than the Cruiserweight title? Letters. The mail I get from people congratulating me. The mail I get from friends aboard, and fans I never even knew I had. To open each one and read some stranger congratulating me…that means everything. I owe it to everyone for this chance. I owe it to God. All I can do is continue doing what I am doing right now. Continue to fight, and to perform. Continue to do all this, so that each day will be better day. And it won’t stop there; I’ll have to rely more than just motives. I’ll have to rely on the right cause. Because the difference between people like Ice and Kid Flash, and myself, is that I fight for everyone else. They fight for themselves. They fight to feed their greed, their ego, and their lust. And now they want to fight me, and put and end to me. They feel as if I should not even be here, as if I stole something from them. I must apologize to them. I am sorry that my success is overriding theirs. I am sorry that to me, wins mean a lot less than they do to them. Finally, I must apologize that for one moment I was better than them, and it stuck on from there. But now those two have a chance to take their frustrations out on me. This Friday on Evolution, Ice, and Kid Flash with a mystery partner, take on Angus Da Bull, Pete Hardy, and myself. Three on two seems unfair, I hope they find someone. I will not partake in such an unfair match, but maybe their hatred of me will be the X-factor. Kid Flash says he hates me. He hated me from the moment he laid eyes on me. Why that was, I will never know. If I had to guess, it would be because I did not suffer as much as he did growing up. We all suffer our own battles and torture, no one should feel worse than anyone else. So I beat Kid Flash once, big deal. It was only once. I went on to win the Cruiserweight title, great. Kid Flash talks about hate. He talks about hating me. Maybe what he really hates is himself. I’m sure Ice feels bitter. He feels cheated. He thinks the title should have been his. I don’t mock everything he’s ever accomplished, but this time he came up short. All it took was one mistake. One mistake and Ice found himself knocked out cold and came through to see me win the titles. But now, after what happened on Evolution, it is more than just this measly title. Ice will stop at nothing to get to me. He will say anything to get to me. He insulted my pride He insulted my manhood He threatened my Boss He insulted my sister And that was his biggest mistake I don’t take too kindly to someone insulting my family. I don’t take too kindly to anyone insulting me period. Ice is only bitter. Bitter because I bested him, and there is nothing he can do about it. He knows it. So he does the only thing there is left to do. That is annoy me until I submit to his pleas and grant him his wish. I apologize Ice, but you are not ready for the Cruiserweight Title. Ice and Kid Flash are two men who hate me for my success. They hate me because they feel I stole something from them. I’m sorry they feel that way. As for my teammates, I know nothing about them. I saw Pete Hardy protect Christina McCoy, so I know that he is a decent person at best. He protects the innocent, he protects woman. So this Friday night, I face off against a team that shares a common hatred for me. Why is it? Is it because I was hired at such an early age? Is it because I won a match or two? Or is it because I bested them both and made my success off them? We all play the hand that life hands us. Sometimes we get royal flush and we are set for life. Other times we have to settle for a jack high. Those are the times when we have to pull ourselves together to work harder, and take life more enjoyably. Other times we are dealt a couple of pairs, and those are the times when we know life is slowly working our way. We just have to be working harder. We can’t always get what we want. We can’t always have the world see things our way. Life is hilarious like that. You never know what each day has in store for you. That is why you enjoy each day, and live each day to the fullest. We will never have the full course meal every time. We will never have the expensive jewelry and wine all the time. That’s why we make the best of things. That’s why we find something to live for other than ourselves. I do this business not for myself, but to help my family remain economically independent. I fight so that the people can enjoy the show. Ice and Kid Flash both live their lives with a one-track mind. They feel the world rotates around them, and if it ever switches off course, someone messed up. Until they realize that they are fighting for a worthless cause, they will lose every time. There is more to life than just winning. There is more to life than wearing a strap of gold. Life is what you make of it. That’s what makes us stronger. That’s what makes a champion. Someone who can play the hand life gives him and takes opportunity by the horns. Someone who knows that you can go a lot farther by fighting for someone else, than for your own selfish needs. Someone who won’t give up or give in to adversary. Someone who will always stick to his beliefs to the bitter end. And if that is what makes a Champion, then maybe I am a Champion in my own right. Maybe we are all champions in our own right. We don’t need a belt of gold to remind us that. We don’t need a ring to remind us we are champions. We don’t need a trophy. As long as we live our lives and do the right thing, we will be champions. As long as we fight for the right reasons and for what we believe in, we are champions. As long as we don’t give up, we are champions… …that’s what I think… ”The Silent Angel” Jin Akiyara
