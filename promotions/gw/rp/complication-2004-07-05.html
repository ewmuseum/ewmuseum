Complication

5 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/05/04 Following this past week's edition of GW Evolution, a new chapter had begun in the books of our daring hero, Rich Sherwin. Despite biting off a little more than he can chew when given the opportunity to fight for Deacon Holmes' Cruiser Championship, the hero... battered and bloodied, had to give his two cents to the fiends that carried out such an attack. However, whilst this was going on, a stranger jumped the audience rail and confronted Sherwin, revealing themself to be his former friend and companion, Ryan Moore. Demanding that the powers that be grant a specific match in the near future, GW fans were introduced to what appeared to be the united Tag Team of the 'Pretty Cool Guys'. Before these issues and complications are brought though, PCG and the world about them have to look forward to next week's Evolution. In what is bound to be one of the most exciting contests of this month, 10 men have been given the opportunity to fight for the bragging rights of being a first Champion. Amazingly, a 'new' Championship is going to be introduced to the wrestling world and Rich Sherwin is going to be one of the men vying for this opportunity. Despite being considered the best equipped for matches of this size, does Sherwin have a chance at making it three good showings out of three... or will it be third time unlucky for the ever-impressive athlete?! Stay Tuned. Location: Home Date: Thursday 6th May 2004 It had been two days since Evolution had been both aired across America and displayed to a live audience. Rich, the victim of quite an attack, had since had his wound bandaged and stiched up with bandage wrapping 'wrapped' around his head to protect the wound from infection or any breakage. Ryan, the friend who jumped the rail, had tended to the needs of his wrestling friend, doing whatever he could to ensure the following few days were as easy as possible. After all, Rich didn't only suffer a wound to the head in this attack. The scene slowly began to open in what appeared to be the home of Rich Sherwin. It was the same old location that it had been before, an empty apartment with little to no furnishings. The walls were still white and the floor will still the plan old laminated wood that it'd been prior to his last visit. Entering the front door of the home, Rich walked into the picture first, clad in a pair of black trainers, blue jeans and a white vest. For the state that the athlete was in, he didn't look all that bad in a completely unbiased opinion. Ryan entered the scene a few paces behind the 'Battle Royal' entrant, himself clad in a pair of white trainers, blue jogging pants and a zipped up puffy waistcoat. To complete his apparently 'stylish' look, Ryan had took it upon himself to wear a pair of rectangular framed shades. The duo walked through the hallway of this establishment, an eerie silence seemingly seperated the two. '... Everyone was right about you, amigo. You really have changed since the time we ran around Primetime Championship Wrestling as the 'Pretty Cool Guys'.' Ryan commented, breaking the ice that had inserted itself between him and Sherwin. Neglecting to answer immediately, Sherwin continued to lead the way through the hallway, eventually stopping when he walked into what appeared to be the kitchen. Plain white cupboards, drawers and tables lay on the inside of the walls, empty and clean... apparently unused. In the middle of this placid room was a rather comfortable table; able to sit a maximum of four people. Sherwin walked over to the table and took a seat before looking up to Ryan and motioning for him to take the seat that was directly across from himself. Happy to oblige with the request of his friend, Moore walked across and took the said seat, his eyes looking for an answer to his prior question. '... Whether you like to admit that people change after time or not, unfortunately... many of those people are very right about both myself and my changes.' Rich began, his voice flat and lacking energy. He had not wished for tales of his exploits to reach his former singing buddy but unfortunately for him, this situation had long been out of his control. 'I had not anticipated for any of my career to have been sent along to you following our contract cancellation in PCW... and then our subsequent decision to seperate and fall back into our seperate lines of work. My line being the art of professional wrestling... your line being the recording studio.' he continued, his voice maintaining the same lack of excitement and interest. Ryan looked somewhat disappointed at his former partners hope that he'd not have found out about his wrestling history after PCW. 'Why did you not want me to find out how you were doing? I thought we were a couple of guys who really were friends? I mean, I would have never been ashamed or anything of you finding out about my career. I mean, it was rough work breaking back into the music scene... but at the time, I'd been able to rush through everything and get to the top of the mountain. Hell, last year alone I found myself releasing three platinum singles and a triple platinum album. I know you didn't do too badly for yourself either...' Ryan responded, trying to sympathise and work with Sherwin. The GW athlete, despite not damaging his reputation with his chosen destination following PCW, really hadn't done all that much following the subsequent closure of that organization. 'You didn't need to know what was happening in my career because to be completely honest with you, I really didn't do much with my career following that PCW departure. The first time we left, you know that I was able to find my way through a number of smaller feds to win a little recognition... but after the second run, I had so much money riding in my account that I didn't even need to worry about professional wrestling half as much. Hell, I'm so not bothered about the real stuff that I've decided to hang about a couple of places that actually specialise in pure fighting.' Rich responded, his voice slowly beginning to sound a little more upbeat. For one reason or another, it appeared as though Rich was beginning to feel a little prouder knowing that GW was his second place of employment with regards to fighting organizations. '... I really don't understand what happened to you following that day we decided to walk our own ways, Rich. I can't say I really know you all that well anymore.' Ryan answered, getting up from his seat at the table before walking over to the far corner of the kitchen. Seemingly, he'd expected to have caught wind of Sherwin and his fighting exploits the second they happened and realising that this didn't happen, is a little upset. Rich though, maintains his cool and calm appearance, refusing to spring to life with a defensive plan... or even an offensive one. Ryan turns back from the corner and walks back to the table, thrusting his hands down onto the wooden surface, his eyes piercing through Sherwin like a stake through the heart of a vampire. It seemed that despite his 'joy' at the arrival of his former-friend on show, he wasn't all that impressed off it. 'Do you even care that I've walked out of a recording contract just to come down here to help you out?' he asked, refusing to sound subtle with his line of questioning. 'To be totally honest with you Ryan...' Sherwin began, looking up to his 'friend' from his seated position at the table, '... I didn't even ask for you to jump into the ring and provide some kind of cover for me. I may have been bashed out of my mind and not have know exactly what was happening, but I could have taken both of those guys out without any help. In fact, this coming week on Evolution, I'm supposed to be involved in a ten man 'Battle Royal'. I didn't need your help back when I was against five men on Pay-Per-View... and I'm NOT going to need your help when I fight nine men on Monday night. I'm going to win that contest in the same way that I won my shot at the Cruiserweight Championship. The only difference between then and now coming from the fact that after Monday, I'll be taking a Championship belt home with me.' he finished, sounding confident and proud. It was a shame to see that Rich had not honestly felt he needed the help of his former partner this past week on Evolution. In the past, the two had some great chemistry before... but it appeared as though time changed all of that. It appeared as though the bridge that they once walked as a united force had burnt down and fallen into the stream. 'This is the way I'm shown appreciation for coming out and saving your ass on Evolution? This is the way you thank your buddies for throwing away their own careers to come on down and help you? Sometimes, I don't even know why I ever bothered teaming with you in the first place. The guys told me that you were a self-serving moron but they didn't tell me that you were an absolute prick too. You know what, don't bother looking for a corner-man when Evolution comes about because the way things are going now, I wouldn't even expect me to be there. I'll see you later.' Ryan said in a stern voice, picking his hands up off the table before storming off, barging out the front door and slamming it behind him. Rich remained at the table, his facial expression changing no more than it had for the entire duration of their conversation. '... That went well.' Rich said with a sarcastic tone as the scene slowly faded out into the confinements of darkness. One can only wonder how the duo are going to be in the days to come... even more so when Evolution rolls around the corner. Will the two be able to come to an agreement on what Ryan is really doing here in Global Wrestling? Will Rich be able to look past his own stubborn nature and accept help from a former friend? Will the Justice League be able to save the world from the hands of J Shit Diamond's mother?! ... No, that's another show. Regardless, stay tuned~!
