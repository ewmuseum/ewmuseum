The Tragedy of Barry Bluebird

12 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Tragedy of Barry Bluebird Psycho Bunny Global Wrestling Promo #2128 Date: 07/12/05 Location: Toon Town The scene opens in the woods. Psycho Bunny has his assault rifle loaded and is waiting for someone to come into his sight. He's been waiting to get to kill someone for days now. Psycho Bunny: Bollocks... He says this to himself when he realizes that there may not be anyone left to kill. That is his pleasure. That is how he gets Bunny Pleasure to his carrot. Somewhere, the females of GW faint at the thought of his carrot. Psycho Bunny: So, who am I gonna kill now? The trees? He still cannot fathom the fact that a zombie invasion has destroyed humanity in the demented world of von Doom. No. Now, he is alone. But he soon comes across something that makes his day. Psycho Bunny: Jackpot... He peers through the forest and sees a Toon Town or a city of toons. Toons are immune to the zombie invasion. He smiles as he looks out at the town. Psycho Bunny: Fucking A. Now I finally get to kill somebody. Meanwhile, in Toon Town, Barry Bluebird is working in Casey Crab's Food Shop. Barry looks out and sees several customers milling about and sighs. Barry Bluebird: Same thing every day. Same old boring people buying the same old boring things. He rests his arm on the checkstand he is at and sighs again. Barry Bluebird: I wonder if anything exciting will ever happen here? Just then, screams and gunshots are heard outside. Everyone scatters but Barry runs toward the window. He peers out and sees a Bunny cackling and murdering everybody. Psycho Bunny: You must all feel the wrath of the bloodthirsty carrot! Barry is in awe as he watches Psycho Bunny mow everybody down. Barry Bluebird: It's him. The one who killed the Trix Rabbit! Monty Moose, who works at the store, is hiding behind the checkstand. He hears this and starts to shake. Monty Moose: Don't let him kill us! Barry is still in a state of wonder as he watches the Bunny's rampage. Barry Bluebird: Wow... Monty Moose: Will you get down! He'll see you! Barry turns to Monty. Barry Bluebird: He's so cool... Monty begins to plead with Barry. Monty Moose: PLEASE BARRY! GET DOWN HERE! Psycho Bunny then turns and eyes the store. He sees Barry looking through the window and smiles as he reloads his assault rifle. Psycho Bunny: Easy kill. Psycho Bunny finishes reloading as Barry watches on with glee. Barry turns to Monty Moose. Barry Bluebird: He sees me! He sees me! Monty Moose: And he's going to kill you dumbass! Barry pays him no mind as he turns back to see Psycho Bunny cock his rifle and point it in his direction. Barry about craps on the floor when he sees this. Psycho Bunny smiles. Psycho Bunny: Good bye, birdie. Psycho Bunny then fires several shots, killing the bird. And that is the tragic tale of how Barry Bluebird died at the hands of a demented character by the name of Psycho Bunny. Cut.
