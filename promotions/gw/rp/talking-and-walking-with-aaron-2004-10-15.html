Talking and walking with Aaron

15 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/15/04 The temperature was 70. It was rather warm for autumn. Brick and Aaron were walking down the street with Aaron's daughter Ashley. Aaron had just picked her up from daycare. Brick was holding a clipboard and pen. A woman walked towards them Brick walked over to her. 'Hello, how are you doing? My name is Jamal Randall. I'm running for Mayor in 2005.' 'Are you now?' 'Yes, and I was wondering, if you have the time to spare, would you sign this petition to get my name onto the ballot?' 'Well, I don't see why not.' She took the clipboard and signed her name to the petition. 'You're awfully young to be running for mayor, aren't you?' 'Well, I do turn 31 next year. It's not like I'm a teenager. Thank you very much.' 'You're welcome, and good luck,' the woman said as she walked away. 'So, you're really considering running for Mayor next year?' 'Isn't that what I said that Friday afternoon?' 'Why?' 'Why?' 'Yes. Why? You don't think you have enough on your plate as it is without launching a mayoral campaign?' 'You know me. I like to play the odds.' 'But isn't it a bit early to start a campaign?' 'Actually, I'm late. Charles Barron and Gifford Miller already started their campaigns. If anything, I'm coming into this late. Good thing is, there are some people in GW who want to help out with my campaign. 'I thought Donny was a Republican.' 'He is. But if there could be Reagan Democrats, there can be Randall Republicans.' 'Good point. So, I saw Evolution a couple weeks back. That was kinda messed up what happened between Ely and Gunner.' 'I actually thought they'd go the distance. I was rooting for them. You should have seen him after the show. He was down at the Dark Horse, getting wasted. Me and Bubba tried to help him out, but he wouldn't take it. He and I almost got into it when he slapped me.' 'He slapped you?' 'Twice,' Brick replied, holding up two fingers. 'Why'd he slap you?' 'When Gunner turned him down, he asked her if there was someone else. She didn't say yes or no, from what I understand. She just said 'I'm sorry' and ran away. He asked if I was the 'someone else'.' 'And you just stood there and took it?' 'He was drunk. What could I do? Of course, the second time he slapped me, Bubba did have to hold me back.' 'Yeah, well, drunk or not, I think he was an ass. I mean, accusing you of trying to steal Gunner from him? He knows you wouldn't do that.' 'He wasn't thinking clearly. The love of his life turned down his marriage proposal. He was drunk off his ass. This isn't the first time I've seen him drunk. Trust me, it's never pretty. I remember the time he was upset about Christina eating him out over Gunner.' 'Yeah, I remember you telling me about it.' 'Yeah, well, he was worse this time.' 'Well, losing the love of your life will do that to a man. You should know that better than anyone.' Right about then, Brick spun towards Aaron with an intense look in his eyes. 'Do not go there. Ever.' 'Brick, come on, it's been nine years. I figured you'd be over her by now.' 'Yeah, well, I thought I was, until just now.' 'I'm sorry. I shouldn't have even mentioned it.' 'It's okay. You're right. It's been almost a decade. I need to get over it.' 'I mean, that's part of why you and Gunner never really hooked up in the first place.' 'Yeah. How are you dealing with this?' 'I don't know what to think. I mean, both Ely and Gunner are my friends and I don't want to have to choose between them.' 'So, you're not that 'someone else', are you?' 'No!' 'Okay, just asking.' 'Well, I'm not. But I think I have a good idea who is.'
