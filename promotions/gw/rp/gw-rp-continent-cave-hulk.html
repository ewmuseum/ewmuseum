Continent Cave Hulk?

24 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Continent Cave Hulk? The Atlantic Ocean has been rumbling for weeks now and suddenly, a continent emerged from the depths of the ocean. The continent has been deemed Devonia by the scientific community, which is currently at odds over the solar system and what planets should be a part of it. The name comes from the Devonian Period of geologic time. The continent has many features and animals not seen anywhere else on Earth. The native species, the Hulks, are all being relocated back to this continent, including GLOBAL's own Cave Hulk and all of his family members. The Hulk Family has been on the endangered species list for years and now they finally have a home after centuries of displacement. They are natives to the Hills of Satan region of Devonia. A hilly section of Slovakia was rennamed this after the family settled there many centuries ago. A giant mountain range called the Devonian Mountain Range is the most prominent physical feature of this continent. It takes up 20% of the land mass and the Hulk family has been known to live in this region. There is also the Silurian Desert, home of the native Desert Nomads, named for the Silurian Period of geologic time, just to the west of it, and and a Carboniferous Forest on the southwestern coast near the Square Tower Ruins. A Petrified Forest, and an Ancient Forest can be located on the central eastern coastal region of the continent. The Great Salt Desert, Salt Flats, and a Crystal Forest can be located on the central western coastal region of this continent. Also in this region are the Narrows, the Gulch, Ice Cavem Crystal Peak, Echo Point, Hidden Peak, Hidden Canyon, Tower Ruin, Blue Lagoon, Desolation Lake, Earth Shrine, and the West Temple. The settlements in this area are Monoceratus, Lagoon, Cygnus, Titan, Triton, and Andromeda. There is also a small kingdom in the southeastern section of the continent called Rodinia, named for a past ancient continent. The people who inhabit the Rodinian nation are somewhat like humans, but they are a bit more primitive and medieval than modern man. The kingdom is protected by the Great Wall and some of its geologic features include the expansive Valley of the Gods, the Trapezium, the Castle, the Great White Throne, Golden Throne, the Golden Stairs, the Maze, the Labyrinth, the Water Shrine, and the King's Palace. The major settlements in this area are Proxima Centauri, Omega Centauri, Eta Carinae, Oberon, Hyperion, and Rodinia. On the northeastern coast is a land that no one dares venture into. It's dark, hot, and almost like Hell on Earth. The creatures and peoples that live there are just like the region in which they inhabit. Such places in this region include Hell's Backbone, Hades Pass, Brimstone Canyon, Hell's Canyon, Point of No Return, Burning Hills, Fire Shrine, Black Mountain, Fiery Furnace, Tower of Death, Burnt Mountain, Devil's Garden, the Sea of Sorrow, and the previously mentionned Hills of Satan. Despite all of this, there are some settlements in the region. These settlements include Dark Angel, Lillith, Samael, and Nephilim. The northwest region of the continent is sparsely inhabited and is a hotbed for animal life. The Temple of the Ancients and the Tomb of the Unknown King are located in this region as well as the Temple of the Sun, Temple of the Moon, the Grotto, Jupiter Temple, and Lost Lake. The only settlements are Lost City, Forgotten City, and Mammatus. The animal life life on the continent is what the scientists are excited about. There are dinosaurs and other primitive creatures not seen by the human eye. The animal life also stays in their native areas on the continent, mostly in the Northwest, Southwest, Southeast, Ancient Forest, in the area near the Blue Lagoon, and in the Carboniferous Forest. Now, the question is, how will the scientists lure the Cave Hulk back to his native habitat? And, once there, how will he adjust to his new environment and how will he interact with the peoples and creatures of this new environment? Note: And yes, I did actually make a map of Devonia. Came up with the idea while reading location names in Utah. A lot of the names from here are places in Utah National Parks. :)
