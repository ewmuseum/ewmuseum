A dark union #2

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/01/03 Silence. The two men stand there amazed that I continue to get up. I finally get to my feet once again, my eyes glowing a dark blue and my fangs showing. I look at Holmes as my body heals itself around the bullets. I hiss rather coldly. “I rather despise guns. Especially being shot up. I mean come on. It’s a little over done. IT’S THE BAD GUY! SHOOT HIM! SHOOT HIM! You should take time to realize what your shooting sometimes…Some of us may have to save your life one day isn’t that right Debra.” I look to her as she looks between myself and Holmes. “What?” Holmes says. “So why are you here?” Zero says looking a little puzzled but also keeping his very stern look. “I’m here cause I was sent by the man you know as Il Capo. He and I have had some talks and I’m here to show ya some things that you never thought exsisted as well as letting you guys get some brand new state of the art equipment brought to you by Soma Corp. “So what the fuck are you. I mean I just shot you a couple of times and your ass keeps getting up. I mean damn are you like super negro. Bullets just bounce off your ass or somethin? Holmes says smirking at the comment he just made. “Yes what exactly are you if you don’t mind us asking seeing that your in my house and technically uninvited….”Zero says calmly looking at Holmes and shaking his head at Holmes’s comment. I look at the men and smile as I sit back down on the brown leather sofa. My hair covering my left side of my face. The stinging from the bullets subsided and I nod to the men. I look over to Debra as she is still on the table. I stare into her eyes and now she has no choice to look back. “You need to leave…now…” I say in a cool snake like voice. She stands from the table and walks out of the room. “Damn” “Fuck” Both men reply to her uncontrollable will to listen to me. “Now that she has left…You want to know what I am…Fair enough. I’ll give you two the basis for what I am cause there is no possible way to explain in depth and you two understand. “ “Man you tryin to say we are stupid? I mean motherfucker you came up in here and got shot down and now you wanna talk shit…” Holmes begin to rant but I catch his eye doing the same thing I did to Debra. “Silence.” I say to him before he can mutter another word and he stops. “As I was saying…in so many words…I’m a vampire, a mage, a businessman, and a very wealthy man. Most weaponry doesn’t kill me as you can see…and I’m a little higher on the vampire chain. What I have come to show you is that in this world everything you thought you knew has two sides to it. Your world’s side and mine. I’m here to show you my side of it…” The room stays quiet for a few minutes. “Ok…let me get this straight…you’re a…” “Vampire, yes this is true…” “Ok…” Holmes says quite shocked. I look to Zero who’s face never falters or changes. He looks to me trying to make since out of all this. He clears his throat… “What does this mean for us? You are going to show us your world.” Zero says calmly. “That is what I’m here to show you…My world the world that you are taught as children that doesn’t exists. You are taught this so that we may keep a veil over your eyes and we can work our plan within plans…since the dawn of time we have carefully constructed things that will keep us safe. Making sure some people became wealthy, making some have lots of power, etc. This of course will make life, and for some of us our unlives even more harder than they usually are. “Naturally” Zero answers. “My offer to you guys is we go on a mission. A mission of my choosing to “acquire” something that I have been waiting on for a while. It is to be well guarded by things which you have never faced before and gives ya the chance to try out weaponry that far exceeds anything you have in your arsenal now…. What do you say? “So what you are saying is we would get to shoot up all new kinds of creatures? With new weapons that I’m sure your giving us” Holmes says looking intrigued. “What’s the catch?” Zero asks. “The only real catch is you may not survive the fight…. But I’m sure you fellas go through that sort of thing all the time.” I look at the men as they look at each other and ponder what they will say. I have just given them the opportunity to be apart of a world that most don’t even know about. The chance to make themselves known amoung the denizens of the world. Even the black market has an even blacker more dangerous market, which I myself am apart of. “Whatcha think Zero?” Holmes says calmly as he is beginning to understand the situation. “I think we shall talk to Il Capo, see if Mr. Soma’s story checks out, and then and only then will I give an answer. Is that okay with you Mr. Soma?” Zero says in his usual calm manner. I smile thinking to myself “These men are pretty damn smart. If I were them I wouldn’t trust me either. But that’s what I like about them. They realize things most don’t.</> “Its fine with me Mr. Zero. I would have it no other way.” I say still giving my usual sadistic smile. “Fine then…Lets contact Il Capo and get all this cleared up.” Zero says. “Hell yeah…but before we do that can I ask you a question Soma? Holmes asks. “Sure…go ahead” “You’re an old vampire right? Like really old…Around during the time of Jesus?” “Yeah” “What was it like growin' up with Jesus? Was he cool? I bet he was stuck up…. He was stuck up wasn't he?” Zero smirks and walks off. I sit there for a minute and start laughing…..
