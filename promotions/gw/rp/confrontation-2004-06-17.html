Confrontation

17 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/17/04 It was a hot sunny day on the McCoy Ranch. A black Jaguar pulled up to the ranch. The driver's side door opened and out stepped Brick. The intensity and speed with which he stepped out of the car suggested that he was not in a good mood. The GW legend stormed right up the path to the front door of the house. 'Bubba!' Brick shouted, banging on the door. 'Bubba!' The door then swung open and Bubba McCoy stuck out his head. 'Brick, are you outta your damn mind?!' 'I could ask you the same damn question. What the hell is this I hear about you cutting a deal with Train?' 'Look, Brick, Christina's already given me hell about this...' 'Yeah, and now I am.' Bubba brushed past Brick and headed for the stables. Brick was close behind. 'Hey don't walk away from me, Bubba! You can't just brush me off, man!' 'Look, Brick, I know you and he have had some personal dealings with Train while I was gone...' 'I just think it's a mistake getting involved with him. He's a troublemaker.' 'Funny. Ayers an' Lansheer used to say the same about you.' 'Hey! Do not compare me to Train! He and I are nothing alike!' 'Oh, really? You're both ornery sonsabitches.' 'Besides, how do you know you can trust him? I mean, he did turn on MOB. Somehow, I don't think loyalty is high on his list of priorities.' 'Let me deal with that, when the time comes.' Just then, a silver Lexus drove up to the car. 'Aw, hell,' said Bubba. Out of the driver's side stepped Christina McCoy. She slammed the door shut and headed right up the walkway. 'Look, Christina, I just about don't wanna hear it.' 'Well, that's just too damn tough, Dad! What the hell is this I hear about you cutting some kind of deal with Train?!' 'Just what I asked him. He ain't really give me an answer yet.' 'Well, now he can give us both an answer.' 'Look, you two. I can handle Train, if I have to.' 'You don't know what I've had to put up with since he first came here. He's disrupted matches, he's nearly gotten this company sued after he beat up those Dallas Cowboys...' 'I had a long talk with Train. He and I came to an understanding.' 'An understanding?!' 'Yeah, an understanding.' 'Dad, with all due respect...' 'Quick reminder: who still owns the company?' 'You do, but that's not the point...' 'That is the point! I think I know what the hell I'm doin'! So, if anything happens with Train, I'll handle it. Understand?' 'Yes, Daddy.' 'Brick?' Brick was silent for a minute and then reluctantly growled, 'Yeah.' 'Good.' Bubba then mounted his horse and rode off. 'Think he knows what he's doing?' 'I hope so.'
