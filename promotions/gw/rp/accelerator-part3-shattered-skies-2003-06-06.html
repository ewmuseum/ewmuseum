Accelerator(Part3) - Shattered Skies

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-6 -03 “Now Wally stands tough, notice that this man did not have his hands up’this Free World’s got you gassed up, now whose afraid of the big bad wolf?” Eminem ? 8 Mile The search for Valerie Cruse was becoming an obsession in my life, I was going to find this girl and her son if it is the last thing I ever do, this quest has led me around the world and back and still nothing, I haven’t talked to Johnny Cruse in what seems like years, but in all actuality its only been several weeks, but now I think I have finally found a break in the puzzle, London England was my destination, Ben Palithope is who I am looking for, but I have no clue as to where I could find the Cocky Cockney’this whole fucked situation has me confused inside. My eyes were close as I reclined back in my soft comfy chair thirty thousand feet up, I was in a deep state of sleep when suddenly a I felt a nudge on my shoulder’I opened my eyes to see a blonde headed stewardess standing above me, I looked up at her still drowsy, my eye lids fluttered a bit as I finally focused on her face. “Yea” I said as I looked into her eyes. “Im sorry for interrupting you, but is your name Jay Robson” She said in a sweet voice as she looked back at me “Heh’Why yes I am?” I said back to this lovely girl in an arrogant tone of voice, because by this time I was about to throw a mack on her “Ok..well someone is looking for you?” She said back to me as she flipped her strawberry blonde hair to one side “Oh really?” I questioned as I looked her up and down, hell yea, im about to join the mile high club again I thought to myself as I continued to look at her “Yes, there is a man in the coach cabin looking for you” Well fuck, all the dirty visions I had running in my head were just trashed by that one sentence, I said to myself as I listened to her reply back to me “Oh..” I said back to her in a disappointed tone of voice and kind of hung my head a little but, but not so much that she would notice that my intentions were only to fuck her brains out “He wants you to come back and talk to him” I shrugged my shoulders a bit and stood up from my chair, I stretched a bit and straightened up my orange Abercrombie shirt and stepped into the aisle as I did the shorter stewardess grabbed me by my collar and kissed my passionately with her tongue. Woah! I thought to myself as I let her kiss me, after a few seconds she stopped and glared at me passionately with a smile on her face, as I looked back at her with a confused look “Your going to die Jay Robson!” She shouted at me, as I took a step back from her and looked around the first class cabin and found that all the people were just sitting there looking straight forward like they were in some kind of trance, I sttod there and shook my head a bit a glanced back towards the crazy bitch. “What The Fuck?” I Said as I fell back onto the seat I was at, her eyes were turned from a glamorous green to a blood shot red’I picked myself up quickly and pushed her out of my was as she stared to scream “AHHHHHH” “AHHHHHH” “Robson your dead” “AHHHHHH” “After that shit I turned and darted out of the first class cabin through a red curtain and stopped dead in my tracks, Ehric Aldante’it was him I knew it, he was standing there in the aisle decked in a black suit holding a bloody knife in one hand’and in the other the decapitated head of Valerie Cruse’dripping blood formed a small pool on the blue floor” “What the Fuck?” “I said in my own words as fear was starting to build in my body, I was racking my brain trying to figure out what in the world was going on when suddenly the light went out’Blackness, Nothing, a Void. After a few seconds the light started to strobe, I tried to focus on Aldante but I couldn’t, each time the lights flickered on and off he was closer and closer to me, panic engulfed my body, I was frozen I couldn’t move, like a deer caught in the headlights of a truck. Me and Aldante were face to face’my knees shook, my body trembled I closed my eyes hoping it would stop and at that moment I fell, no not to the ground’I was in a free fall, my body twisting and turning. Was I dead” I thought to myself, I don’t know’at about this time I heard a voice “Do it Jay’do it” It was Derek Frosts voice but from where? *BOOM* The loud noise hurt my ears’as I looked around to see that I was in a smoke filled arena, I was elevated on the top turnbuckle of a wrestling ring, sweat poured from my face as I couched down positioning my feet, I glanced over to Frost who was leaned up with his arms stretched across the ropes, I stood up on the turnbuckle as the crowd erupted, looked at my target on the laid out on the mat’it was Salaok in a bloody mess. I leaped off the turnbuckle and watched as everything began to spin and executed a Phoenix Splash better known as The Next Element, I began to go down and at the moment of impact my eyes shot open? I was in the air plane’my heart beating out of my chest, sweat poured from my face as I looked around at the people with my baby blue eyes. *DING* *Crackle* “Attention passengers’this is your Captain speaking, we are now approaching London International Airport, please buckle your seat belts and thank you for flying Trans Atlantic” That was it? It was only a dream’and thank God it was only a dream
