Wrestle at Wrestlecade: Episode III

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/04/04 Wrestle at Wrestlecade Episode III: Chojin Power~! Chojin... Superman. Power... Self-explanatory. In the last episode of preparation toward Wrestlecade's 6-Man affair, Wang Kenobi admitted that Rich Sherwin had got further than he would have ever dreamed of. Wang Kenobi admitted that Rich Sherwin had the opportunity to rise up and snatch everything from what was likely to be the jaws of defeat. Having been made the favourite to win the contest by various people around the world, strategy isn't something that these two are going to have the benefit of. After all, more times than many would care to think, the favourite is often the first to be targeted by everyone else. With this vital piece of information buried deep in his mind, Wang thought was time he passed on some his own greatest techniques to his student... none of which recorded as being used in the real-world? What on earth could these be? Keep view to find out... The scene opens inside an old warehouse, a wrestling ring set-up in the middle it. The rest of the picture is only lit by dim lights suspended from the ceiling. Three figures stand inside the ring itself, two of them being Wang Kenobi and his student Rich Sherwin. The other character though, remained something of a mystery at this point in time. They were clad in various elements of body armour so it would probably be safe to assume that this being was to be a live tackling dummy... or something along those lines. With Wang and Sherwin dressed in their usual attire, the two men began to discuss their plans for training... or in this case, teachings of devestation. 'Before I show to you any of these moves, it is absolutely essential that you promise me they will never be taught to another living being.' Wang said, opening the entire picture on something of a sour note. The expression on his face said it all though. He didn't want his student to be trying to sell these secrets should the hardship of life really get to him. '... Kenobi, like I said time and time again on the way here, I won't teach these moves to anyone or anything. I might be a clown a lot of the time and you may not truely appreciate my humourous manner... but if there's one thing that I'll keep to, it's the word that I give.' Sherwin replied, the expression on his face showing a level of intensity that was fierce but sincere. He wanted his tutor to know that these moves would remain in his position and not be shared. Nodding his head in response to the given statement, Wang Kenobi brought the armour-clad human closer toward he and Rich Sherwin. 'Ok, before you learn the skills I am going to show you, it is absolutely essential that you know something about your opponent. With that in mind, I wish for you to meet your opponent for the day... Norman Briggs from a hospital called 'Community General'. He was brought here against his own will... but it's not like we're going to be telling anyone that, are we?' Wang stated, his voice now carrying a cheery and delightful tune. Was he actually going to enjoy everything that this poor, little average person was going to be bit through over the course of the next... however long. 'Hey Norm, sorry if I hurt you.' Rich replied, raising a hand to signify that he was saying 'Hi'. Norm attempted to reply in the same way but the combination of tightness and thickness only allowed the man to raise his arm about half of the way up. Once completed, Wang violently grabbed hold of 'Norman' and placed him in a front facelock. Upon doing this, he began to pull the opponent up, almost ready to execute a suplex... but, instead of just lifting him vertically, Wang's hands slid down the sides of his opponent, taking their positions closer to the ankles of the person. It looked as though Norman was simply sitting on an upside-down ceiling, his back almost vertical and his legs outstretched as though he were just being lazy. With all the protective gear though, Norman was weighing down on the Korean tutor and the strain on his face showed. 'Rich, this move is the first that I am going to teach you. It is known to us as the Kinniku-Buster and was used by a very powerful family during times of close combat. As you can see, I have got hold of the opponent in a variation of the hold prior to a vertical suplex. It is essential that you clamp hold of the leg, just above the ankles for the ENTIRE duration of the move. Failure to do so can result in an easy escape and counter... and believe me, THAT is the absolute last thing that you want.' Wang informed his student, placing emphasis where required to make sure the essential elements of the technique are the things that are remembered. 'Following this..' he continued, '... you have to jump as high as you can into the sky before landing on your buttocks, the shockwave sent up being potentially traumatising. Of course, the intensity of damage caused depends on exactly how high into the air you get!' Rich nodded his head in agreement and watched as his tutor jumped up in a vertical line before crashing down onto the canvas, his ass landing first. The body of Norman Brigg's jolted violently upon impact and within sheer seconds, his body was allowed to fall to the floor. Rich clasped his mouth and looked on in awe at the kind of devestation that could be done with this one move alone. For the first time in a while, he was absolutely speachless. Wang Kenobi picked himself up from the floor and dusted himself off, looking down at the body of Norman Briggs before letting out a hearty chuckle. '.... It looks like I hit the poor guy a little harder than I thought I was going to. However, Rich, you have to keep in mind that this man was just another average being. The people that you are most likely going to be facing will be probably be able to absorb this move if you perform it TOO early in the contest. This is one of three finishing techniques that you will learn here.' Again, Sherwin nodded his head, this one simply in response to the information that was being thrown in his direction. Wang strolled over to Norman Briggs and picked his lifeless body from the floor, supporting it as best as he could whilst walking over to one of the rings four cornerposts. Sherwin looked at him with a puzzled expression on his face wondering what kind of moves could be executed whilst an opponent was there. 'Wang, exactly what kind of damage are you expecting to cause that hasn't been done already? I mean, hasn't the charging spear into the corner been a move used by people for many, many decades?' he asked, his question legit and with serious concern. Exactly what was Wang hoping to do with the body of his opponent burrowed nicely in the corner. 'Unfortunately, due to the unforetold circumstance of Mr Briggs being unable to take the brunt of one Kinniku-Buster, I have been forced to modify the following move for demonstration purposes only. When it comes to the real thing, it would be better to have the opponent wallowing around the ropes... not too close to them and most certainly, not too far away from them either. At that point, they'll also be a little easier to work with than an unconcious body clad in armour.' Wang stated, walking away from the slumped body of Norman Briggs. Along with Rich, he walked to the opposing corner, looking back at Norman Briggs every so often to make sure that his body hadn't moved anywhere. Heh, like it really would go anywhere in the condition that it was currently in. 'Ok..' Wang began, jumping around on the spot in preparation for his next technique, '... this move, is a variation of the usual 'spear' that so many people love to perform in this day and age. You see, unlike the conventional method where somebody would ram into the gut of his opponent, we aim to ram into the back of the person. Also, unlike convention where you'd use your shoulder to attack the opponent... we are going to use our head.' '... Wouldn't that do us a lot more damage then it would them? I mean, if I'm having to do this move into the ropes, isn't the lack of real support going to just snap my neck or something on the rebound? I mean, it's not as though I can use everything to my distinct advantage, is it?' Sherwin said, throwing his arms into the air following his made point. How could this move be pulled off without due injury to the neck and/or spinal portion of a mans body? 'Ok... second thoughts about the demo that I was going to give you. Instead, take Mr Briggs close to the ring ropes and when I give the signal, let go of him.' Wang told his student, Sherwin nodding again in response. 'Captain Retirement' moved over to the other side of the ring and pulled Mr Briggs out of the corner. He stood him close to the ring ropes, keeping hold of him to ensure the body didn't just fall down to the floor in a big heap. As both athletes got into position, Wang Kenobi crouched down and then began his ascent, rising from the ground before sprinting toward his enemy. 'NOW!' he screamed, Rich letting go of Briggs just as he appeared to be coming to. Putting his head down, Wang continued running until his head connected with Briggs' back, his own arms shooting out instantly to grab those of his opponent. Outstreched and completely unprotected, Briggs found himself going into the ropes front-first... the top rope catching his mouth, the middle rope catching his waist and the bottom rope catching his thighs. Wang had let himself lift from the floor momentarily so that his force would act as an arrow being fired from a bow. As the ropes reached their furthest point of extension, Wang dropped to the floor and rolled out the way, allowing his opponent to fly back... the burns of the ropes showing in the aforementioned areas. Panting and looking for breath, Wang looked down to his opponent before looking up to Sherwin. '... That move... was called the Muscle Millennium. Unlike in theory when the intensity of the move was derived from a slingshot-effect at the other end of the ring, we are unable to defy gravity in such an abusive way. This move, whilst not as traumatic as the original Millennium is good enough to total almost any living man. The whole equation of this feature is force times mass...' 'Force times mass... Force times mass... Force times mass.' Rich said to himself a few times, looking to capture the entire equation in his head. He looked down at the floor for a few seconds before looking back up to Wang, '... Is this dude going to be Ok after we've finished using him as a punchbag?' 'I don't see why he wouldn't be alright? If anything, the only thing he's going to have is a couple of bruises and the sort. The level of protection that he's currently equipped with will ensure that none of his bones are broken... and that no blood is actually spilt. He'll feel the pain that goes behind every move, but he's supported enough not to break anything during the move.' Wang replied, confidently and effectively. In all truth though, Wang didn't quite know how the protection was holding up... considering that he'd already nailed him twice, both times seemingly a lot harder than he had actually intended to. Either way, he had just one more technique to offer his student... one last wheel that could be the difference between victory and loss at Wrestlecade this year. 'Ok, now we're onto the last of the three techniques. Rich, this is not only the hardest of the three to pull off successfully... but it is the most devestating of the three also. The amount of damage this can cause to the arms is without equal. The name for it is something that escapes me... but to keep in tune with it's founder, we'll call it 'Tainin'... the Japanese word for retirement.' 'Japanese word for retirement, eh? Doesn't sound too bad.' Rich replied, nodding his head in satisfaction at the selection of name. Wang walked over to Norman Briggs' battered body and kicked him a few times, the broken individual stirring a little before getting to his knees. '... Now Rich,' Wang began before continuing, '... this move is going to be the most devestating of the three that I have taught you. It could be the difference between life and death in the wrong situation... that my friend, is no lie. To begin the move, you must make sure that you are back to back against the opponent. In this case, it doesn't matter... as long as you are potentially looking opposing ways. After this, you step back, your legs moving back between those of the opponent and hooked around their thighs. For small opponents, this kind of move whilst they are standing won't work because the chances are, they won't be able to support your weight. In that event, you can just stand about... but make sure your feet are in the same position. Finally, you want to reach back and grab both their arms, preferably at the wrist again.' Wang informed his student, doing each instruction as stated, reaching back to hold onto the wrists of his opponent. '... Once complete, pull foward...' his cold and caluculating voice stated before Wang began to pull ahead with all his might. Leaning back to contradict the way he was pulling, the level of pain being sent through the body of Norman Briggs forced him to begin screaming in agony. Having his arms pulled behind him... and then having his body pushed ahead because Wang was leaning back... it was sheer agony. Rich could not contain his own delight at the way that such a simple move was causing more pain than anyone had thought possible. '.... and finally Rich, when used on a big person this move can be the only thing that saves you from a defeat. Hook around their thighs, pull the arms forward and lean back as much as you can. The contradicting forces will cause extreme pain and in the most extreme of circumstances... wrench their arms straight from their sockets!' Wang shouted, releasing his hold on Norman Briggs. Walking toward his student, Wang placed a friendly hand on his shoulder. 'There is little more than a day until you will have to choose whether you take this moves with you to the big show... or you leave them at the door and use them in better circumstances. I have faith in your ability to win regardless of what happens. Remember that...' With his final words, Wang left the ring and walked away... almost leaving as mysteriously as he had appeared. Despite originally being the apparent roomie of Sherwin, it was now clear that this only objective during the course of the last week had been to prepare out hero for a battle like none other. As the scene faded deep into the confinement of darkness, Rich could only wave goodbye to his friend. :: END :: Next time... Episode IV: Final Preparations
