Date: 1/31 Alright, so here it is... Two hours ago I was in the process of being debriefed by Il Capo while simultaneously receiving the bitching out of a lifetime for the so called “sloppiness” of my report, and the “unprofessional nature” of not only my diction, but my dress as well. Afterwards I spent the next hour riding “home”, as it were, to Zero Estate... Quite possibly the most boring ride ever experienced in my life. I killed time by writing in the diary, err, I mean... journal, I was given. Diaries are for sissies... At any rate, I came to Zero’s house to find that some things never change, the fact that he still hadn’t installed a lock on the front door capable of keeping me out, and that some things do... namely, the fact that my entire room had been whitewashed and that all of my personal belongings had been relocated. But to where? Some serious furniture movin? was in order and I wanted answers post haste. Finally Zero showed up, probably after hearing my screams of “I’ma kill that motherfucker!” after realizing that all my stuff was gone. After explaining to me that everything was fine and thus preventing fourteen hand cannon propelled bullets from piercing his flesh and vital organs... he revealed to me that I now had my own wing of the house. Well... naw not really, more like my own little guest house out by the pool. But at any rate, he springs this whole guest house deal on me and then when I go to check it out a fuckin wave of people all come bursting out of there screaming “Welcome Home”. Who would’ve guessed, shit, certainly not me. I’d never gone from pissed off to happy so fast in my life. So now here I am... Sitting in a jacuzzi Girl under each arm And a drink in each hand... now this is the life. Nineteen, not a care in the world, and no one to answer to. As I take another sip from one of my two cups I can actually feel my pupils dilate even further, so much so that I may actually be absorbing light from other countries. My eyes roll into the back of my head while their lids drop an extra centimeter or two. Suddenly my head feels heavy, forcing me to rest it on the chest of the girl sitting next to me. Cindy I think her name is... Or is it Candace? Cameron maybe? No matter, not like I’ll be using it anyway... Just as I feel myself beginning to pass out a wave of hot water splashes across my face, quickly breaking me out of my semi-unconscious state. I immediately sit straight up with both my eyes wide open as the sound of both the girls in the jacuzzi with me giggling. With a devilish grin on my face I rise to a standing position and begin moving over to the girl sitting in front of me who is still laughing uncontrollably. “Now that’s gonna cost ya!” I inform her as I lunge forward scooping her up in my arms. As I lifted her up to my chest while stepping up onto the wall that hangs over the pool her friend jumps onto my back, causing me to lose my balance and sending the three of us tumbling off of the ledge. We’re still in the air and already I know this is gonna turn out bad... At the very instant our bodies collide with the water I can feel every inch of my skin pull and tighten so much so that I don’t doubt having the look of an emaciated corpse right about now. The temperature change from the jacuzzi to the pool was so drastic that it has made the pool feel more like it’s filled with liquid nitrogen rather than water. The three of us almost simultaneously burst from the depths of the pool half laughing hysterically, half gasping for air to fill our hollow lungs. Apparently we made a bigger splash than anticipated, as everyone on the deck along with those in the pool, is now standing at the edge of the pool, drinks in hand, cheering for our acrobatic performance... and laughing at the fact that we’re all freezing our asses off in the water. The three of us look at each other with a brief smirk before darting through the water over to the shallow end of the pool and up the stairs onto the deck. Once there... It was time for revenge. As soon as we were all up on the deck the tables quickly turned. Any and everyone we could get our hands on was immediately carried away and thrust into the pool. With revenge exacted, I begin laugh maniacally as I gaze down over everyone that we tossed into the pool... some of which are fully clothed. I strike a pose similar to that of a proud and charismatic kind looking down on his loyal subjects as everyone continues to drink on the deck while splash fights break out in the pool. Everybody’s drinking, the music’s pumping, laughter is in the air, and everybody is having a good time. Then I turned around... Across the deck, amidst a sea of people, stands what is quite possibly the finest thang on two legs dancing languidly while seductively sipping a mixed drink from a hollowed out coconut. And at that very moment... everything stopped. And I mean everything. The dancing, the laughing, the drinking, the music, everything, I’m not even cold anymore. Once glance at her and boom... instant sensory deprivation. Nothing else even exists... Except her... I can’t help but stare, it’s like I’m wearing blinders and she’s the only thing in front of me. She stands somewhere in the neighborhood of six feet tall, bow legged, eyes shinin? like four karats a piece just blindin? me. Tight, muscular thighs attached to the silkiest, smoothest legs ever made to stand on slide down from the frayed openings of a pair of daisy dukes, which barely contain a sweet lil tail that peeks out ever so slightly from the back of her shorts. Nothing more than a minuscule piece of multicolored fabric tied around her upper body conceals two perky, perfectly rounded mounds of what I’m sure is nothing short of heaven, which her reddish brown hair almost comes down to. Suddenly she stops dancing and looks up with her piercing green eyes falling on me. Her face can be described as nothing other than angelic, and for a moment I find myself locked in a gaze, blatantly staring directly into her big green eyes. You know when you wake up with a wicked hangover and spend the first part of the day between the bed and the toilet because you’re puking your brains out? Then once you finally decide to go outside the sun is shining, the birds are singing, a light breeze is in the air, the sky is without a cloud and even bluer than usual, the grass is evenly trimmed and just as bright green as ever? Well that’s what her eyes look like, and that whole sun shining down, birds singing, not a cloud in the sky feeling is how I feel right now staring into them. Perfect... Until I realize what I’m doing... Without warning I’m zapped back to reality and everything comes back along with it. Suddenly it’s no longer just me and her, we’re once again swimming in the middle of a sea of gyrating bodies filled with more alcohol than plasma, with music blasting from every angle. One thing hasn’t changed though, I’m still staring... Wait, I’m still staring!” A nervous chill falls over my body as my eyes quickly dart down to the floor. Hoping she hasn’t taken notice of me, my eyes remain glued to the floor until I am able to angle myself toward the bar. Once there I notice someone rather familiar sitting at the counter and pull up a chair next to him. Beside me sits Ice, who is surprisingly doing rather well with the ladies. Whether or not he cares though is a completely different story all together... He sits at the bar hunched over a menagerie of shot glasses, each of which has been discarded in a different manner. Some overturned onto their sides, some standing right side up, some upside down, all of them empty. I had no clue he drank like that... What’s even more surprising is the fact that he’s flanked on each side by a very attractive member of the opposite sex, both of which are bikini clad and posses what I like to call very impressive “chest talent”. The two girls? combined four hands roam all over Ice’s rather ripped upper body, caressing his bulging muscles as they glide over them. Not that he seems to even notice. He’s not even cracking a smile, or looking at them for that matter. It’s like they’re not even there, or maybe it’s him who’s absent. I mean, it’s hard to shrug off two girls at the same time... This guy’s like a ninja warrior, his kung-fu is strong... and apparently, so is his liver. I watch patiently as the bartender pours him yet another shot, which he downs immediately, before giving him a nudge on the arm and a nod as he turns to me. “What up man?” He replies with a simple nod and returns his eyes to the bartender, whom he signals for another shot two as the two girls are handed their drinks in hollowed coconut shells. After pouring Ice yet another shot from a large, and now half empty, handle bottle of Canadian Mist, the bartender makes his way across the counter and stands in front of me. “What’ll ya have?” he asks, although I’m too busy watching Ice in action to catch it. I turn around catching the end of what he just said and blurt out a disoriented “Huh?” Just as he goes to repeat himself I feel the smooth caress of a hand beginning at the back of my neck that then moves across my shoulder and down onto my chest. Still looking forward I can feel the warmth of another human body pressed up against mine and a certain softness on the side of my face that could be nothing other than a pair of breasts. The scent of cucumber melon fills the surrounding air, and although it could be just about any of the feminines attending this party... I know it’s her. “He’ll have two kamikazes, two liquid cocaines, and... two long island iced teas... no coke.” proclaims a feminine voice, one whom I’m still to nervous to look up and see whom it belongs too. “That’s alotta booze for one person, you sure you can handle all that?” inquires the bartender as he begins preparing the various drinks. The woman’s hand gives my chest a firm grope as her arm slithers across my shoulders and neck like a snake maneuvering across a system of tree branches. I can feel the warmth of her body fade as she moves away from me, the unmistakable screech of the legs of a chair sliding across the floor can momentarily be heard and all I can do is assume she’s pulling up the stool next to me. I’d look over and find out but I’m still a bit too nervous for such a bold gesture. Apparently I was correct in my assumption because the same hand is once again felt upon my body. This time slowly running up and down between my knee and upper thigh, gently massaging the tense muscles as it slides across them. “Oh, don’t worry, he’ll have plenty of help.” the same female voice replies to the bartender, who looks up with a quick smile before returning to the drinks he’s making. Her voice is deceptively seductive, subtly growing more throaty with each syllable pronounced and pushed between two full lips. Or at least that’s what I remember being when I got stuck in a trance ogling her on the dance floor. Yet I can’t seem to build up the courage to look at her while she’s right under my nose... “Here ya go!” informs the bartender as he slides a tray with six drinks on it onto the bar, four shots and two hollowed out coconuts. I look up from the ground to grab a shot off of the tray and all of a sudden I’m face to face with what I’ve been trying to avoid this whole while. Her.
