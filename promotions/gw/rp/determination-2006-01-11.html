Determination

11 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-11-06 El Serpenti sat in the room watching his friend El Lobo get stretched as the nurses worked on helping him recover the use of his leg? Months had passed since the accident? No scratch that, the brutal unprovoked assault those giant buffoons; The Hammer Brothers did. There time would come Serpenti thought to his self as the nurses wrapped up the exercises and sat El Lobo back into his wheel chair. Lobo smiles and thanked the nurses and then pinched one as she turned to leave getting a small whimper and then a laugh from the fiery red headed nurse. The two nurses walked away looking back and smiling? Serpenti let out a deep sigh and hoped off the table. Lobo pressed the control stick on his chair and pulled forward. “I don’t understand amigo. You smile and laugh yet?” Serpenti started. “Yet I no longer can walk” Come on Gaspar. I am injured not dead. I’ll get my legs back? IT is just going to take some time.” Lobo said. Thankfully the hospital understood that they wished to remain in a mask do to tradition and not wanting to reveal there identities? Serpenti picked this hospital in Mexico just for that reason. However the two wore plain black masks that just covered their faces allowing their hair to flow free. Serpenti made sure of that as well as paid for the finest doctors. But still his friend could not walk. But Lobos optimism always made Serpenti smile regardless. “I know what your thinking Gaspar.” Lobo said as he stopped and wheeled around to be in front of Serpenti. “Really. Did you develop mutant powers like that of the movie we watched yesterday.” Serpenti said with a slight chuckle. ?:No. I don’t need those to read your eyes. YOU been here thanking this is all your fault. You have not left my side.. I thank you for that and for all this?. Help you have pervaded me with. But Gaspar.. What happened was not your fault. And as soon as you realize that.. The better your heart will be.” Lobo said looking up into Serpenti’s eyes. “But” I was the one who brought you to Global?” Serpenti said. “Ohh Yes” You did a lot in that. You stopped a man from being a drunken fool. You helped that same man realize he was not washed up as he had though. You open the door to Global for that Man? I thank you mi amigo. The time I was in that ring again and having the crowd cheer as I flew again. I will never forget it. If I never walk again, I will never forget it.” Lobo said with a tear running from his eye into his mask. Serpenti opened his mouth to say something then shut it again as nothing came into his head. “What I am saying Gaspar is you have done all you can here. It is time for you tto return” I know about your Match that you were given.” Lobo said with a nod of his head. Serpenti sighed and looked away. “I am not going to England, They can fire me I do not care.” Serpenti said hanging his head. “What.. I cannot believe my ears” Serpenti. Staying here you will wither away.. I know you love those fans I know you have waited for this opportunity, If you do not go. Then my situation will be for nothing.” Lobo said as he drove around to face Serpenti again. “What are you talking about?” Serpenti asked. “I will not have you go the route I did. I fell into drink and depression Serpenti. I will be dammed if I let you fall. You who pulled me back out. Serpenti please go back. Go to England, and win tat title you have wanted for such a long time.” Lobo pleaded to Serpenti Serpenti looked into his friends eyes.. He was right? Serpenti knew it. Serpenti nodded in agreement. “You are right Amigo. I will return here after I face the others in the Match.” Serpenti said. “Good. But you will not be going alone.” Lobo said with a smile. “What do you mean?” Serpenti asked with a tilt of his head. “A friend of ours gave me a call” I asked a favor and he has agreed. Jago Tamura will be waiting for you in England.” “Jago” I have not seen him since our days in Japan Lobo. It will be good to see him once again.” --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- That was three days ago. Serpenti now walked though the streets of London England. Dressed in a hooded trench coat. Covering his green and red mask. He knew of the men he would be facing tonight. And he needed to prepare for a serious fight. Serpenti turned down the street and saw the building he was looking for. A strange building for London; not many building of Asian architecture. How ever this was the place, The Red Dragon Dojo. Serpenti walked past the two twin red dragons and entered the sliding doors. Four men stood in the center of the room. As Serpenti entered one with black hair and strange face paint and a white robe with Red Dragons on it bowed. The other three white clad and masked men stood as still as statues. “Welcome Serpenti. It has been a long time, old ally of mine.” The Man said. Serpenti removed his coat revealing that he was wearing his red and green serpent mask and a red gi with green snakes covering it in places. “Indeed it has been Amigo, to long. Looks like your doing well for yourself.” “Yes. I am doing quite well. And It looks as if your have come prepared.” The Man said approaching Serpenti. The two shook hands then embraced in a quick hug. “Shall we begin training right now?” The Man asked with a slight tilt of his head . Serpenti nodded and stretched out a little. “Better now then later. I got to be ready for tonight Jago” Serpenti said looking up.. “Good. Then defend your self, Serpenti. Ninja Attack!” Jago shouted and the three men finally moved and rushed Serpenti.
