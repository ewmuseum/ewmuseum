Don't be sorry, just win

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/03 (As the scene fades in we see a sharply dressed Sean Price again sitting in the office of TUE head, il Capo. However at the present moment Capo is not in his office, it is just Sean and the TUE lawyer Preston Banks. Suddenly the door opens up and Capo walks inside the room. Due to the poor lighting in the room his face can not be identified. Capo walks over to his Italian leather chair and sits down in it. He takes a cigar out of his cigar box which is neatly placed on the desk. Preston takes a lighter out of his pocket and lights it for his boss. Capo slowly leans in just enough so that the tip of the cigar is joined with the flames. Capo puffs in a few times and then pulls back revealing that the cigar has been successfully lit. After completion of this Preston hands il Capo a contract. Capo takes a look at it, slowly places it on his desk and then looks up at Sean.) “Well Sean, seems like you have one hell of a test in front of you. What do you propose we do about this?” remarks il Capo as he puffs his cigar. “Well boss I am not sure. Tyler was rebooked in the Cruiserweight title match and I don’t under any circumstances want to give him double duty. Same goes for Deacon who is opening the show in a table match.” Replies a somewhat clueless Price. “Ok Sean, you have told me what you don’t want to do but not what you want to do. You have not given me any solutions to this problem.” the anger words of Reno echo through the room “Well boss I just found out about this rebooking 30 minutes ago. I have not really had time to think over what exactly it is I would like to do. I am sure I will think of something. So in the mean time how about you and Preston take care of other matters and leave my match to me. I am more than capable of dealing with Dynamite X and Venom Alexander myself.” Replies a confident Price. “Lets hope it doesn’t come to that.” “Don’t worry Sean, Capo and I will figure something out.” Remarks Preston. “Oh im not worried, at all. Infact the idea of a mystery partner is somewhat energizing. So many possibilities, we could hire someone for just one night. Hell I could use Bruno my body guard if I wanted. This is not a problem for me, so its not a problem for you either.” (With this Price gets up from his chair, shakes the hands of Preston and il Capo and then heads out of the office. After he closes the door to Capo’s office he reaches into his jacket pocket and pulls out his cell phone. He dials up a number and then puts the phone to his ear. He waits a moment and than the man on the other line picks up.) “Hey Sean, what is going on?” remarks the man on the phone “Not much Ty, just leaving Capo’s office, he wanted to talk to me about the rebooking of Evo and how I am left without a partner.” “Hey man sorry about that, I really didn’t mean for it to go down like that.” Remarks a somewhat upset Pierce “Sorry” Your sorry? Sorry for what? Sorry that you are getting a chance to win the cruiserweight title that you should of won at the PPV? Don’t be sorry, just win.” Replies a happy Sean Price. “Thanks man, you sure you don’t mind?” questions Pierce “What is there to mind” It’s not like I am facing Salaok and Sage Sabbot, its Venom and Dynamite X. Might as well be Logan Creed and Ely Braxton. I will win the match, partner or no partner. I have had harder challenges in my life, this is just a tune up for things to come. You go, and you win your title, I will take care of my business you can count on that.” “Oh there is no doubt in my mind that you will walk out of that match the victor, like you said, its not like you are facing the cream of the crop. So we still on for the gym at 2?” “As far as I know we are. Dec is supposed to meet us there, he had some other business to take care of first.” “Ok sounds good to me, you want me to swing by your place and pick you up or are you just gonna meet me there?” “Actually Ty I need to go and pick up a few suits so it will probably just be easier if I meet you there as opposed to going back home and then back to the gym, seeing as how the suit place is a few blocks away from the gym.” “That works for me. I will talk to you later Sean.” “Take it easy.” (Sean flips the sides of the phone together thus ending the conversation between he and Tyler. He slides the phone into his coat pocket just in time to get into the door of the limp which is being held open by one of the TUE body guards. Sean gets in the car, sits down in his seat and then picks up the telephone that is linked to the phone upfront with the driver.) “Driver, could you please take me to Versaci suits I need to pick up a few orders.” “Right away sir.” (Price hangs up the phone, reaches in the small cooler and pulls out a bottle of Corona. One of the body guards in the back of the limo reaches out and takes the cap off with a bottle opener. Sean thanks the man and then proceeds to take a sip of the beer as the scene fades out.)
