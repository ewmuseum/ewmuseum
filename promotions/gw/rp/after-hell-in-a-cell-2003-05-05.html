After Hell in a Cell

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/05/03 The bell had already rung and it was official. Brick had won. He finally got revenge on the man who had determined to make his life hell when he pinned Salaok and got the 1-2-3. A spent and bloodied Brick struggled to his feet. He stared down at the fallen Salaok, satisfied that he had finally gotten his retribution. Salaok was unconscious and bleeding profusely from his face, chest and legs. A massive cut was over his left eye. “Got you, bitch!” Brick spat at him, before exiting the ring. He then staggered out of the ring and stared at the lifeless body of Mark Kullypso, who along with Quartz, had attempted to screw him out of the match. What he got for his troubles was a suplex to the outside of the ring. Brick staggered out of the cell to a standing ovation. The fans were on the feet, showing Brick their respect and congratulating him on his victory. Brick looked out to them and managed a smile and slowly raised one fist in the air before making his way back to his Lincoln Navigator, which he drove into the Astrodome to make his entrance. “Where are those damn keys?” he asked himself, patting himself down and then finding them in his lower pants pocket. He deactivates the alarm and gets in. Before he puts the key in the ignition, he takes a deep breath. He then sticks the key in the ignition and turns on the car. He waits a little while and then shifts the SUV into reverse, backing out of the arena. He checked his rear view mirror to see GW officials running and jumping out of the way, so as to avoid getting hit. As he backed up through the entrance, he shifted into drive and turned around, slowly driving through the backstage area of the Houston Astrodome. Many crewmembers and GW superstars moved out of the way of the Navigator. Finally, Brick saw what looked like a small dot in the distance. He started to come to a stop. Getting out of the Navigator, he saw Logan Creed. “Hey, Logan. Almost didn’t see you.” “Just wanted to congratulate you on winnin” your match. But since you’re headed out anyway, I was hopin? you could drive me to the nearest hospital.” “Hospital” Why?” “I wanna check on Gunner.” “What happened to Gunner?” “That’s right. You weren’t here. Help me get in an” I’ll tell you on the way.” Brick walked over Creed to the passenger side and opened the door. “Now you know I don’t have a baby seat, right?” Brick quipped as he helped Logan up into the Navigator. “Ah, go fuck yerself!” Creed shot back. Brick then got back into the car and drove off. “Y” know, maybe you oughtta get that looked at,” Creed remarked, concerning the open cuts on Brick’s forehead. “Never mind me. What happened with Gunner?” “It all started after Ely’s match with Eight Ball and Jesse.” “Who won?” “Jesse, but Ely was a little off considering the cheap shot Jesse took before the match.” “What cheap shot?” “The one he gave Ely after Ely got in his face for callin” you a chicken shit.” “That motherfucker called me a what” Who walked out on us like a little bitch that night?!” “Anyway, after the match, Ely an” Eight Ball double-teamed him. Then, next thing you know, the damn Nevermore comes down to the ring and starts jumpin? Ely. Gunner, bein? the girl she is, goes down to the ring an? tries to help out. She even tries to get Jesse to help. An? for a while, it looks like he is gonna help. But then the sonnuvabitch whacks Gunner from behind with a bat and gives her the Kentucky Mash.” “What”!” Brick roared. “That motherfucker! You know, I owed him an ass whipping before but now?” A smile then began to form on the Hardcore Dwarf’s face. “Well, I may be able to help you there.” “You’re gonna book us in a match?” “Not just any match. A match for the World title.” “Uh, Logan, what the hell are you talking about” Camero’s the World champion.” “Not anymore. Right before the International title match, Dynasty jumped him backstage. Tossed him into a window. Anyway, that leaves the World title vacant, so I’m holdin” a tournament for the title and your match with Jesse is gonna be a first round match.” “This made Brick especially happy. Not only was he getting another chance at the World title that continued to elude him, but now he was getting another chance at payback, this time against someone who stabbed him in the back. And in Brick’s mind, the recent development with Gunner, someone with whom he’s had a close relationship, was going to make the beating all the more painful. Thoughts of glory and vengeance raced through Brick’s mind as he drove out of the Astrodome.
