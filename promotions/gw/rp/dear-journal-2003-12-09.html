Dear Journal...

9 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/9 Barely visible in the darkness of the night, a pitch black Cadillac Escalade complete with mob tinted windows and twenty four inch chrome rims glides effortlessly across the pavement. The vehicle also has a rather nice aftermarket stereo system capable of setting off car alarms a considerable distance down the road, but tonight it is not in use, which is quite odd considering that the passenger inside is none other than “The Poster Child for Don’t Try This At Home” himself, Deacon Holmes. He sits slumped down in the final row of seats in the vehicle wearing nothing more than a white tanktop, a baggy pair of AND1 basketball shorts with graffiti designs all over them, and a pair of matching Jordan XI’s. Normally when riding in TUE transportation Deacon immediately usurps control over the radio and proceeds to attempt to blow every speaker inside the vehicle. Tonight this is not the case, tonight he sits quietly in the back seat looking somewhat more thoughtful than usual. Beside him sits a thin notebook filled with paper. Wrapped up in the metal coils that hold it together is a rather nice looking pen. Although it’s hard for a solid platinum pen with custom engraving given to you by Il Cape himself not to look nice. Deacon continues to sit there staring blankly at the back of the driver’s head before drawing in and then exhaling a deep breath as he reaches over toward the notebook. He places his hand flat on the front of it as if he reconsiders picking it up. Deacon sits like this for only a moment before sliding his hand across the top and gripping the spine of the notebook as he picks it up, using his other hand to catch the pen as it slides down the tunnel created by the coils. He then opens the notebook to reveal a bright white sheet of paper that is completely blank. Holmes rests the open notebook on his lap and begins stroking the measley bit of hair that he has accumulated on his chin as of late and is trying to pass off as a goatee. A thoughtful look forms on Deacon’s face as he stares forward as though he were looking right through everything in front of him. “Hmm, you’re supposed to name these things aren’t ya?” he mumbles to himself before his eyes light up and he once again grasps the pen in his hand in order to put it to the paper. ?Dear momma, Demetria, err, mom. Ah fuck it... Ms. Demetria Elizabeth Holmes... whatever you wanna be called. We never met, but this is your son, Deacon... I know that’s not what you named me, but then again, you never really got a chance to. Hemorrhaging during labor and bleeding to death as your child takes it’s first breath tends to steal away life’s little chances like that. Since you didn’t get to give me a name they just gave me your father’s... never met him though. Hopefully I’m doing it justice, if not... too late to change it now. Not to say that if I could I would. I’m here, he isn’t... and for that matter, neither are you. But I am. I’m walkin? today, I’m talkin? today, I’m feelin? today, but most importantly I’m livin today. Why couldn’t you? Maybe then I would’ve had some sort of chance at a normal life. But at any rate... I’m nineteen now, I made it without you... maybe even in spite of you, but I made it. Seeing as how you died before I even got a chance to take a proper breath and all... What a way to come into the world, thanx a lot mom... No matter though... Your death couldn’t stop me and neither could anything else. At the time of our separation I was unconscious, supposedly I wasn’t to make it out of that hospital either. When I was finally awakened it was by the dull buzz of a machine as it breathed air into my lungs. From that point on I was nothing more than a statisitc. One of them babies with a momma who didn’t love “em that would have to grow up fast. And grow up fast I did... Having an alcoholic with a real passion for beating small children and overall cheating them of any quality of life they may be able to attain for a foster parent tends to do that do you. She used money the government sent to her for alcohol instead of food and clothes for me. Often times when I’d come home from school she’d be passed out on the couch drooling out of the corner of his mouth with Wheel Of Fortune blaring from the TV. Then around four or five after she’d sweated out enough alcohol while sleeping in that hot ass living room she’d find somethin” to beat me for whether I’d done something wrong or not. I wasn’t allowed out past the front gate so I wasn’t exactly hard for her to find. Switches, extension cords, wet leather belts, the water hose... you name it I got whipped with it. It sucked, but eventually you get used to it... it helps if you close your eyes and fantasize about a life that less closely resembles hell. Once I hit High School things got a little easier, I started playing basketball, football too. Found out I was pretty good although I’d never played. Being able to run like the wind and jump through the roof made up for my lack of fundamentals in each sport. Come to think of it I probably gained my four flat speed and forty three inch vertical from tryin? to dodge all those drunken ass whoopin’s from “Mother” I was an all american in both sports, even ranked in the nation in football. I was the fourth best running back in the country rushing for almost two thousand yards and twenty seven touchdowns my junior year. I was always praised for my so called “skill” at the position, even though I had none... I ran out of instinct, fear, it wasn’t skill, I didn’t see three and four steps ahead of what was going to happen on the field, I was just scared out of my mind. Running for my life. Just like the day I ran away... Except on the football field after a big run I’d just come back and do it all over again. This time I never came back, as a matter of fact, I just kept running...? Holmes? pen stops for a moment as he pauses to look up and finds that they are moments away from Zero Estate. With this realization, Deacon quickly closes his journal, once again fixing the pen within the coils, and reaches down onto the floor, from which he pulls a black leather duffle bag. Holmes slowly unzips the bag which when fully opened is revealed to be filled with nothing more than clothes... two custom made .50 caliber pistols with ivory grips and lion designs down the handles and barrels, and... a razor edged dagger with a handle similar to that of the two guns. These three items sit on top of a stack of neatly folded dress clothes in a custom holster belt which situates both guns on the back pointing downward with the dagger sitting in between them. After stashing the journal Deacon quickly reseals it as the pitch black behemoth of a sport utility vehicle shifts into a downward angle while adjusting to the steep slope of the hidden driveway leading to Zero Estate. Once at the bottom of the slope the driveway flattens out into a sort of tunnel created by concrete walls on each side and somewhat of a ceiling formed by overlapping foliage. As they vehicle further traverses the driveway it once again goes at an angle, but this time it’s up. The Escalade roars uphill and back onto level ground right in front of Zero Estate. Holmes begins to gather all his belongings as the SUV glides down the rest of the driveway past the sprawling and immaculately kept font lawn which has a rather large fountain situated in the middle of it. Once at the front of the house Holmes? escort eases to a stop right behind Zero’s black Mitsubishi Eclipse GT, which just happens to be parked right behind Deacon’s pride and joy. Lucille, a black Porsche Carerra GT Turbo Concept with a few modifications... metallic flakes, nineteen inch Enkei Shogun rims, low profile tires, mob tinted windows, heated leather seats, and a custom stereo system made to fit inside of such a small car yet still powerful enough to shatter windows and wake the neighbors up at night thirty minutes before he’s even in the neighborhood. “She” also just barely clears the ground at a clearance of only two inches... hitting a speed bump at speed would probably send “her” into orbit. After coming to a complete halt the air bags in the Escalade deflate, causing the vehicle’s suspension to lower to the point that it is only a few inches off the ground, making it easier to exit. Deacon climbs out of the back door, leather duffle bag in hand, with his eyes locked on his car. A mischievous grin forms on his face as he walks past “her” and up the three or four steps leading to the two large doors situated at the front of Zero’s house. He fumbles through his pockets seemingly looking for a key but after a few moments of exasperated searching Deacon removes a lock pick. He inserts the pick inside the keyhole and with a few quick adjustments successfully jimmy’s John’s door open. As the door creaks open Deacon whispers “I still got it.” to himself while picking up his bag and entering the house, this is his first time in the house in quite some time. On the way to his room he takes a quick look around to see if things are still the same and finds a few small changes, a few vases moved, a new couch, nothing compared to what he finds when he opens the door to his room. Nothing... The walls which he painted black before doing graffiti all over them had been whitewashed, his posters were gone, as well as his stereo, computer, and everything else he had in the room. He drops his travel bag out of shock as the vein in his neck begins to pulsate with rage. Holmes? entire face contorts into one that makes him appear as though he could go toe to toe with a rabid grizzly bear and win as his breathing becomes slow and deep while veins begin to pop out all over his body. He is practically fuming with anger. “I’ma kill that muthafucker!”
