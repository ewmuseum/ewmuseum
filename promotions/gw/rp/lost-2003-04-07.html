Lost Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #95 Date: 4-7-03 Location: Beach ((You feel the cool night air blowing throught your hair. You love the senrinity on knowing that you are alone but are you alone, Are you really alone ever? There is alaways someone watchign you. You may not know it but fiftteen feet away in the darkness there is some one. This person may be been seen by the naked eye but he's there. Oh yes. He's there. Your eyes are scanning the ocean front as you walk down it. The sand in your toes and the sound of seagulls beggning their daily animal routine. Hunt,Eat,Sleep and Die. The sound of teh waves crashing agnist the shore is the very same as when you heard it the first time your dad took you to the beach. When you were small. Yes. When you Dreamed of being a doctor. When you dreamed of being a fire fighter. Those days have long since passed for Roz Blayze. This is when you see him. walking to you on the beach. His hair is down. Thge wind blowing it this way and that. Every few seconed he takes a hand to the left or the right and pulls a hand full back. His body is cloked in his normal drap. a T shirt that can not be seen through the leather jacket he has on and a pair of black baggy pants. Not un-like other time you can see that his face shows nothing. His body shows all the emotion of what he feels. His movement tell a story.)) Roz: Again and Again. People tell me i'm not good enough. They tell me that i can last. That i'm no good. Or my Star is Not as Bright as some peoples. Well damn it. Thats why i'm here. I'm here to prove all you rat basturds wrong and I'm going to start with you Sage. Sage Sabbot. The CruiserWeight Champion of the world. Best on the Globe. Well. I'm here to tell ya. I dont get pushed around at all. You dont scare me with your horse shit talk about being an apple in Salaoks eye. You want to stand looking at the Mid-night sky to tell me that your better then me. Thats fine. Hell. Havent we all picked out a star from time to time? ((Roz's face is still showing no emotion as these words spill out of his mouth like water from a faucet)) Roz: 'wise through reflection and experience' is the definition of Sage. Are you? Sage. You've been here longer then me. You've accomplished something that i've wanted to do for a long time. That title you have around your waist. Is something that i have wanted for years now and now i look at the holder and think. This is going to be a hell've a Job to get it away from you and not becuse you are a great champion. You have Great Champions backing you up. See the nevermore is the beat of the GW. I have to crawl into the belly of the best to win the title. Can i accomplish that? Could i ever get my chance. Sage i'm not asking for a title shot. I'm going to earn it just as i did with the International champion. Yiu know what? You were right. With Frost i couldent get the job done. I couldent beat him. Now i see that Kully Draven and Silent wind all have a chance of leaving Evo with the plat title. So what's left for me. You tell me Sage. Your suspossed to be the wise one. You are the one that is going to beat me. Why am I a stepping stone. Honestly. You went from calling me Low-Down dirty Trash to a stepping stone, To be a stepping stoen you have had to accomplish something. Maybe the International Number one contendership in the first three weeks was something. Something you diddent do and if you did. Ya diddent win the title. Yeah you beat Jay. I beat Brick. So your in the Nevermore. Really doesnt bother me. See Sage. The worlds been agnist me since the day i was born. I'll be damned if i'mgoign to let a thing that runs out of your petty mouth hurt me in any way. So if you think that if you saying that My star was a little more dull then yours. That diddent hurt. You knpw what that means. Mines further up then yours and that means my star is higher up on the totem pole. So guess what? ((Roz lowers his head and chuckles a small bit)) Roz: That means I'm better then you. So you saying that your goiing to beat me. It'll only be becuse Salaok,Quartz and Jay Vyper help you. Now that I understand that the CW title thats around your waist. You think your hot shit. Well i'll tell ya what. You are. That means your one of the best and when i beat you. Thats going to make me one of teh best. Alaready you have been Plat champion. You are the current CW champion. You beat 'TLM' For the championship. That dont make a fuck to me. See You and me. We dont have a history. After this match. I hope that you never will be dumb enough to face me again. Unless it's for thet pretty little title you hold around your waist. ((Roz then puts a hand on his head. felling the bandage that wasent really seen untill this moment. Blayze fee;s the bandage)) Roz: If your wondring. I was in the hospitle all night last night getting stiched up. guess who i saw there. Jason D. The man who gave me this thing. You know how bad it was that i couldent move untill this morning. I saw Jason there. He walked right by my room. As i laid there and looked at the 6'6 man. I really honstly thought he'd be trouble. The only one i fear in GW right now is my self. Jason you. I respect you. You diddent talk shit. You did what you did. I respect that. Sage. When i beat you and all that shit you talked about my star not being as bright as yours. When i beat you. I'm going to have a star and thats going to be the death star. ((Roz begins walking again. He walks to the dunes. Then stops looking out at the ocean)) Roz: GW is a game. I live and Die by that game. If i die by it tehn so be it but as of this moment Liveing is my main conserne. ((A Voice is heard and the camera jumps to it. The Sadistic smile of Draven is seen.)) Draven: Are we goign to make it the party tonight? Roz: Sure. Draven: You really beat Brick? ((Roz walks to the car and opens teh door)) Roz: Sure enough ((This is teh last word heard. Then the sound of a door slams. The car starts and Roz as well as Draven leave the beach parking lot. A note is seen falling form a hand in the car. thge camera runs to the note. reading)) Forevermore Fin
