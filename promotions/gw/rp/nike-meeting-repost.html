NIKE Meeting(Repost)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ... Inside a rather plain looking single window room a forty two inch plasma screen mounted against the wall hangs over a black component shelf stand. Atop the stand sits a DVD player along with a small remote, other than these few items the room is empty save three large black leather rotating chairs. Talking and footsteps can be heard getting closer and closer until the door finally opens and two well dressed men slide into the room, the first of whom is wearing a black pinstriped Versace suit, a box tucked under his arm, and the second wearing a heather gray Versace suit with a blue shirt and gray and blue tie. The first of the two men walks in and goes directly to the chair furthest from the door while the second, carrying a folder, walks over to the DVD player. While the DVD player is being set up, into the room steps Deacon Holmes dressed casually in a pair of Avirex Jeans, plain white t-shirt, and a pair of all white Jordan XI’s. A portion of Deacon’s shirt is tucked behind a gaudy platinum belt buckle in the shape of a crown that rests in the center of his waist and upon closer inspection the seemingly abstract red stitching on the left pant leg of the jeans is in the form of a roaring lion’s head. As Holmes shuffles into the room with his hands shoved deep in his pockets a glistening watch can be seen on his lower left forearm while a matching bracelet adorns the right. An intricately linked chain hangs from around his neck and extends to the middle of his torso with a platinum roaring lion’s head dangling from it. Looking up with a smile from the DVD player the man carrying the file folder sets it on tope of the component shelf and moves over to Deacon with his right hand extended. Holmes? hand slips from his pocket and into the executive’s hand, receiving a firm handshake. “Deacon! Glad you could make it. I’m Ethan Rodgers, Vice President of ad production.” The man informs Deacon as they shake hands. Then motioning to the man in the chair he continues “And that’s Andrew Miller, president of product development.” Holmes moves past Ethan and moves over toward Andrew, who stands to his feet and offers a firm handshake. “Nice to meet you, hopefully what we’ve come up with for you is to your satisfaction.” states Andrew while shaking Deacon’s hand. He and Holmes then take seats and are soon joined by Ethan who is holding the remote to the DVD player. A nod from Ethan acts as a signal to Andrew, who then immediately reaches down beside him onto the floor and pulls up the box he came in with. Handing it to Holmes he informs him “And these are for you.” With the box sitting in his lap Deacon anxiously removes the top from the box like a child opening a Christmas present. Inside the box is a pair of low top red white and black tennis shoes that have their heels slip into two levels with a set of four “shox” separating them. The sides of the shoe’s white soles have segmented air bubbles that are colored red and run all the way to the front of the shoe. The bottom of the soles are black and have rubber bumps popping out in order to provide improved grip and better response. The shoes also have a small arched “A” groove that runs across at the middle of the foot, which would make balancing while standing on a set of ring ropes a breeze. The icing on the cake is a red silhouette insignia of Deacon Holmes performing one of his many suicidal maneuvers. His eyes grow wide staring into the box at the pair of shoes in amazement. Slowly he lifts one of the shoes out of the box, cradling it in his hand as he stares at it jaw dropped. When it seems as though he’s on the verge of salivating Ethan breaks Deacon’s gave by handing him the remote control to the DVD player, completely derailing his train of thought. “Just wait till you see what we’ve cooked up for your commercial, I think you’ll be pleasantly surprised.” He informs Deacon who seems torn between the shoes and the added prospect of watching the video. Slowly, Holmes gently places the shoe back inside the box fitting it in perfectly next to it’s duplicate. To further draw his attention away from the contents of the box Deacon fixes the lid over it and places the box on the ground next to his chair before aiming the remote and pressing play. The plasma screen flickers on with it’s screen flooded by a dense rolling fog and the sound of a howling wind blowing through the PA system. Suddenly the center of the fog begins to whirl around itself giving way to a plain black void of a background behind it. As the pace of the swirling fog begins to quicken to that of a tornado six extremely slim square pieces of highly polished metal enter the fray and are sucked down into the center of the twister amongst various other debris. The vantage point smoothly transitions from inside the tornado to one zoomed out from the side. As the twister rages on swelling and constricting at random, flashing bolts of shimmering red and silver lightning strike the spinning mass at it’s core before being absorbed and causing the storm to grow larger. Suddenly the tornado apparently grows out of control as it abruptly stops before violently dispersing it’s contents sending debris zooming into the foreground. As the fog clears once more giving way to the plain black background six silver tally marks sit embedded in the black void each evenly spaced from one another with red electricity surging through them. Long deep breaths can be heard as the screen becomes semi-transparent and a heaving chest with beads of sweat rolling down it becomes somewhat visible as though the previous storm were man made. The heavy breathing pattern grinds to a halt and the background once again becomes opaque as the leftmost of the vertical slashes flips over onto it’s side to reveal itself as a “D”. Without warning the camera view is quickly sucked into the opening in the center of the “D” just as “Where You At” by Lil Wayne begins to blare. Once inside the hole a black and white scene of a large arena packed with a roaring crowd is shown. In the center of the arena is a wrestling ring with two men going blow for blow, one of whom has a huge size advantage and also appears to have the upper hand as he has his opponent in a rear waist lock. He pops his hips and in one huge heave sends the smaller of the two men flying overhead with a German suplex. Red and silver trails follow the body of the smaller man as it hurtles through the air when out of nowhere he performs a miraculous feat of amazing balance and agility as he manages to flip out of the suplex and land standing in the center of the top rope on shaky footing. His muscle bound opponent quickly rolls over and climbs to his feet in order to continue his assault only to find no one lying on the mat. His eyes widen as he realizes what has just happened. The roar of the crowd becomes almost deafening as he looks up to find his opponent standing on the ropes gesturing to the crowd before leaping off. The scene freezes and zooms in on the smaller man, who’s shoulder length dreadlocks are frozen in place jutting out in a myriad of different directions. He hangs in mid air with a set of flashing red and silver trails behind him wearing nothing more than a pair of basketball tennis shoes and some baggy jeans that expose the top part of his boxers. Although small, the man’s repeatedly tattooed body is defined so much so that it almost appears cartoonish in nature as the scene begins to roll again, causing his one hundred and eighty four pound frame to be instantaneously dropped out of mid air like a brick off the top of the building. During his descent, with trails closely following him as the only color in the black and white reel, he wraps his right arm tightly around his stunned opponents neck before kicking both of his legs out in front of him while exclaiming “Lights out bitch!” causing the very top of the larger man’s head to be violently driven into the canvas which in turn produces a small explosion due to C4 being planted in the mat. At the precise moment of impact the scene bursts into full color as every member of the audience stands to their feet going crazy after what they’ve just seen. Amidst all the madness a commentator can be heard repeatedly shouting “Holy Shit!” as the smaller man bounces back first off of the mat before rolling over onto his stomach in one of the corners of the octagon writhing in pain as his opponent lies face down on the canvas... unconscious. The clearing of the small cloud of smoke caused by the smoldering patch of canvas where the impact occurred allows for closer inspection which reveals the smaller man to be none other than Deacon Holmes. As he begins to climb to his feet his dreadlocks fall out of his face and show that he can’t be much more than eighteen or nineteen years old. Various tattoos cover his small frame. His left arm is covered by a full sleeve tattoo depicting guardian angels splitting the clouds as they fly down from heaven to combat demons bursting from the depths of hell. His upper right arm is covered by a tattoo of a massive roaring lion’s head with a jewel encrusted crown floating over it. “Holmes” is written in arching old english characters across his stomach, and a pair of skeleton wings adorn his back with a few odd feathers appearing to have fallen off of them and something written in small script letters is written under them but it’s too small to make out. With his opponent still sprawled out on the mat a mangled bloody mess the referee comes to Deacon’s side and raises his arm as he bell is wrung. The quickly begins to zoom out as the crowd’s applause reaches a fever pitch to show that the crowd is largely Asian. The last thing heard as the scene is sucked out further and further is “Deacon Holmes wins!”. The scene is finally pulled all the way out and the blank black background with the “D” followed by five tally marks is once again shown. Suddenly the vertical slash to the right of the “D” is overturned as well to reveal itself as an “E”. The camera view is then sucked in between two of the arms protruding from the wall of the “E” and the previous scene is revisited. The same Asian crowd is still cheering as a worn out and bloodied Deacon Holmes attempts to gather his breath inside the ring with an unconscious opponent sprawled out on the canvas. Intermittently objects inside the arena begin to blur and shake before being completely ripped out of the picture and replaced. The all Asian crowd is infused with black, white, and hispanic fans. The lighting arrangement changes, along with the guard rails, and ramp. The large screen hanging over the entrance ramp suddenly becomes the Globaltron, the logo in the center of the octagon changes from a set of Japanese characters to “GW”, and the unknown Japanese wrestler shifts and morphs into none other than Brick. Suddenly the official holding Deacon’s arm up as a signal of victory becomes Ely Braxton, who with an enraged scowl goes for an irish whip on Holmes sending him racing toward the ropes. With Deacon bouncing off, Ely bends down and prepares for a back body drop. Holmes spots this on his return trip, and as he approaches Braxton places a hand on each of his shoulders then rolls forward, flipping over Ely and landing in a crouching position. Braxton quickly stands and turns, whirling around with his arm cocked back as far as it could possibly go without bursting from it’s socket. Ely swings a monstrous clothesline... unfortunately for him it’s target is now nowhere to be found. Completing the circle, Braxton turns another one hundred eighty degrees and much to his chagrin finds none other than Deacon Holmes standing behind him arms crossed. Standing still as a statue Deacon peers up at Ely who’s eyes are currently somewhere around the size of quarters after finding him standing there. Suddenly, a million dollar smile, reminiscent of the one plastered on the Cheshire cat from Alice in Wonderland, is flashed by Holmes before instantly bursting to life and delivering a jaw jacking super kick. Just as Deacon’s foot is about to collide with Ely’s face everything in the arena stops and begins to revolve. Fans stand doubled over as they prepare to burst from their seats in awe, popcorn spilled in the midst of everyone’s enthusiasm hangs in mid air along with droplets of sodas and beers with only the light of camera flashes illuminating the silent scene. Slowly, the scene zooms in on the halted action in the center of the ring where Ely Braxton stands staring into a Deacon Holmes “No Contest” super kick like a deer in the headlights. The zoom becomes even tighter to show only the shoe on Deacon’s foot, the same one that sits in the box next to him in the room. With the shoe the only thing it sight now the red patent leather on it begins to glow before suddenly colliding with Braxton’s face full force causing his eyes to glaze and roll into the back of his head as he collapses on the canvas in a heap. The audience bursts back to life as well spilling drinks and popcorn in their excitement. A hail of camera flashes and the deafening roar of their cheers fill the arena as the scene seems to fall away until the view is once again of the same black void with a “D” an “E” and four vertical tally marks. The slash situated next to the “E” turns onto it’s side, revealing itself as an “A” before the view is then vacuumed into it’s steeple. Inside the ring, bordered on all sides by a crowd of people standing from their seats cheering their lungs out, are Kid Flash and Deacon Holmes. Holmes stands on spaghetti legs wobbling back and forth dizzily in the ring as Kid Flash prepares to springboard off the ropes. At the very instant Flash sets himself to bounce off the top rope Deacon rushes toward the ropes before diving to slap them with his outstretched hands. Coming up successful in his last ditch attempt to avoid a big time move from Flash, Holmes causes the ropes to shake to cause Kid to lose his footing and fall down on the top rope... crotching himself. Without a moment’s hesitation Deacon quickly hops to his feet signaling to his tag team partner John Zero. Each man immediately moves into action, Zero grabs a steel chair from the outside and climbs up onto the apron pressing it firmly against the face of Kid Flash as Deacon Holmes leaps onto the top rope in a single bound then springboards off with a tremendous somersault front dropkick that smashes the chair into Flash’s face. A deafening roar erupts inside the arena as a result of what’s just happened as the scene once again slips through the same space it was introduced through. The tally to the right of the “A” then quickly flips over into a “C” just in time for the vantage point to once again be drawn through it like coke through a straw, swirling as it passes though. The rotating vantage point eventually falls on the Global Arena where a tag match between the team of Akodo Rokku and Jin Akiyara and The Midnight Sons is taking place. Inside the ring Deacon Holmes stands at the mercy of Akodo Rokku who currently has him in a standing head scissors. He looks out over the sea of rowdy fans with a gleam in his eye before lifting him up over his head into a powerbomb position with relative ease. The crowd’s reaction becomes even stronger as Rokku then sets off running across the ring preparing to deliver an earth shattering running powerbomb. Unfortunately his victim somehow manges to slip free from his grasp and slide down behind him. Deacon then quickly snatches out the ankles of Akodo Rokku, who’s back is still to him, causing him to go crashing down with nothing but his face to break the fall. Holmes then plucks a chair out of the smoky air, thrown from the apron by his tag partner John Zero, and performs a somersault with the chair held tight against his legs as he flips the entire length of Rokku’s body. Deacon lands in a sitting position on the back of Rokku’s head, the chair smashing it into the canvas just as soon as he began lifting himself off of the ground. The crowd erupts as Holmes goes for a pin, rolling over Rokku’s now limp body to reveal his profusely bleeding nose and glazed over eyes. One! Two! Thr, no! Out of nowhere comes Jin Akiyara flying in to make the save with a well places front dropkick. John goes to step through the ropes but is immediately stopped by the referee, whom he begins arguing with. Jin then lifts Deacon to his feet and begins riddling him with a series of kicks and punches in rapid succession before whipping him to the ropes and giving chase. As he approaches the ropes Holmes regains a bit of control and manages to leap up to the top turnbuckle in a single bound and immediately bound backwards with a long, looping backflip. Akiyara slams into the turnbuckle and quickly turns around to find Deacon just now landing between him and Rokku, who is still laid out on the mat. Holmes looks over at Rokku and then shiftily back over at Jin before flashing a mischievous grin and tearing off toward Akodo. Akiyara chases after him and it would seem that he might just be able to make yet another save... until John Zero flashes in out of nowhere to blind side him with a wicked chairshot that he’d cut all the way across the ring to deliver. Jin is turned inside out by the magnificent blow, and now in the clear Deacon is left to do what he does best... showtime. Reaching the turnbuckle Holmes hops over Rokku’s lifeless body, landing on the middle rope on the right side of the turnbuckle, he then quickly springboards off, landing on the top rope to the left of the turnbuckle this time before bouncing off of that one. In the air Deacon crosses both of his arms in front of his chest and begins spinning like a centrifuge working double time while also performing a moonsault. The double jump skytwister press connects with great impact, sending the frothing crowd into a chant of “Revolverlution” so loud the three count is barely audible. When the victory is official John Zero comes over and drapes half of the tag team titles over his obviously drained partner’s shoulder as the scene is once again jerked away. After being funneled through the shape of the “C”, the same black void is once again reintroduced with just two slashes remaining to be revealed. Suddenly a bolt of red lightning strikes the figure adjacent to the “C” and as the surging electricity flows through the metallic conductor it turns over, showing itself to be an “O” which quickly sucks in the vantage point like a black hole devouring space debris. The view plummets into the “O” traveling faster and faster until it is expelled overlooking the wrestling ring where Deacon Holmes is facing off against Ice, who currently finds himself in a bad way. Ice sits in one corner of the ring, all of his limbs limp, his head laying lifelessly on the middle turnbuckle. A steel chair has been positioned in front of him and rests against his bloody face, who’s glassy eyes lay half slit. The view then swings across the ring while staying in the same spot, to show Deacon Holmes racing away to the opposite end and leaping to the top turnbuckle in a single bound. Once atop the ropes, Deacon stands to his feet gesturing for the crowd to make even more noise. In response to Deacon’s request just about every man woman and child in the Global Arena stands to their feet hootin? and hollerin? at the top of their lungs. Holmes continues to posture just a bit longer before bending his legs then shooting off of the turnbuckle as though he were some sort of spring loaded action figure. Soaring from one end of the ring to the other, Deacon tucks into a crunch position with both his knees and elbows bent as he begins to angle himself in order to properly execute the maneuver, cameras flashing all the while. Mere feet away from connecting with an obviously out of it Ice, Holmes explodes from crunch position, violently thrusting both his feet forward into a vicious front dropkick. For a moment everything in the arena is silent as each and every jaw now resides on the floor after witnessing such a thing. A cross ring front missile dropkick into Ice’s chair covered face has stolen the tongue of not only every member of the audience, but the announce team as well. The crash of the chair against Ice’s face still echoing throughout the arena and ringing in everybody’s ears. Deafening silence is all that’s heard, even “Where You At” by Lil Wayne stops momentarily as even Holmes lies seemingly unconscious on the mat. Then out of nowhere his legs fold backwards toward his head and he springs to his feet causing the crowd to erupt in a chant of “HOLY SHIT!” as the music once again picks up. Deacon staggers about the ring hands raised high above his head as the image begins to fish eye and blur before being sucked back into the same void, this time leaving only one tally to be turned over. Finally the last vertical line is turned over and just as expected it’s an “N”, which then envelopes the scene in darkness as it’s sucked under the arch before returning to the wrestling ring where Holmes is now facing off against Ace Cash, who currently rests in a position of power sitting atop the turnbuckle. Deacon staggers over toward Cash, who then leaps off of the turnbuckle and in mid air latches onto Holmes? neck. Once Ace has his arm secured around Deacon’s neck he begins to whip around into a tornado DDT. As the two go whirling across the ring Cash seems to have a little trouble bringing Holmes down as he seems to have somehow managed to maintain his balance. Rather than going down Ace begins to go up as Deacon uses his hands to push Cash up as the continue to twirl, and in an unexpected show up strength Deacon actually manages to reverse... into a miraculous spinning northern lights suplex! Holmes immediately bridges but only gets a two count as Ace kicks out at the last possible second. Deacon rolls over as Cash kicks up, causing them both to get to their feet at the same time. Ace gets on the offensive first though with a couple of overhand rights before going for an irish whip. Holmes reverses and sends Cash to the ropes, who then quickly reverses once he gets there by jumping up to the top rope and going for a springboard maneuver... which unfortunately for him is cut short. Unbeknownst to him, Deacon was running right behind him and was able to follow up by pushing him off of the top rope sending him out of the ring and crashing into the guard rail. Fans of all ages begin reaching over the guard rail patting on Ace Cash until Holmes slides out of the ring to continue the fight. Cash delivers a quick blow to Holmes? mid-section in order to buy enough time to scramble to his feet. The two then begin trading punches back and forth until Deacon manages to black one and delivers two direct hits that stagger Ace against the guard rail. Holmes doesn’t stop there though as he snatches a drink from the hands of an unsuspecting onlooker, rips the lid off casting it aside on the ground, guzzles the remainder of it, then smashes the cup of ice into Cash’s face sending him toppling over the guard rail. A mischievous grin forms on Deacon’s face as he, seemingly without reason, turns his back and slides back into the ring. A confused sigh comes from the crowd and echoes through the arena as everyone’s brows furrow at the sight of Holmes seemingly taking it easy on Ace Cash. Once in the center of the ring the mischievous grin on Deacon’s face turns into a full blown, ear to ear, cheesy smile as he looks to left and right into the crowd before tearing off across the ring. The entire audience bursts from their seats as Holmes bounces off the far ropes and comes rocketing back toward the ones he slid under running full speed. As he approaches the ropes he leaps into the air without even breaking stride. While Deacon is in the air the entire scene begins to slow down and rotate as the red patent leather of the shoes pick up a light glow. The view slowly zooms in on the glowing NIKE swoosh logo on the side of the shoe as it makes contact with the top rope, pushing it down gently. The view continues to zoom in closer and closer on the swoosh as the second foot slams down next to it so much so that the shot is so compact it only contains the shoe, and section of Deacon’s leg. The rapidly slowing scene begins to taper away in the same shape as the NIKE swoosh as the muscles in Holmes? legs begin to tense and veins start bulging out of them. Red electricity starts surging around the edges of the swoosh as it constricts to the point that only contains the shoes and some random objects beyond them. Then suddenly the image is thrown back into full motion and the bent rope is quickly pulled tight once again, seemingly ejecting what’s left of Deacon into the air. Although he can no longer be seen, a loud crash can be heard soon after he is thrown from the ropes along with a gigantic uproar from the crowd and a commentator screaming “OH MY GOD!!!”. The scene is then brought back to the same black screen which now reads “DEACON” in razor blade letters with red electricity surging through them along with a NIKE swoosh centered under which is also being surged through. “HOLMES” slides in under the swoosh and a red lightning bolt strikes the image causing a small “2004' to appear under it. The screen then goes to static before promptly being turned off. The lights inside the boardroom begin to ascend to their maximum brightness as both Ethan and Andrew lean forward in their chairs and look over at Deacon Holmes who’s jaw has officially taken a vacation from the rest of his face and currently resides on the floor. “I take it you enjoyed the video” Ethan spouts proudly. “Hell muthafu... I mean, it was aight...” Deacon replies, attempting to mask his enthusiasm. “Well, why don’t you just look over the terms of the contract we’ve come up with for you.” interjects Andrew, handing Deacon a file folder with a few sheets of paper stapled together inside of it. Holmes flips through the papers, skimming each one much less than thoroughly before flipping to the next. Apparently something toward the end catches his eye though as they widen to the point that they’re at risk of popping out of the socket and rolling right down to the floor. “FIFTY MILLION DOLLARS!”” exclaims Deacon hopping to his feet while still in the chair. “Well yes, but the money would be...” Andrew tries to add before being cut off. “Ya’ll wanna pay me fifty million dollars!”” continues Holmes. “Get me a pen!” With a smile on his face Ethan begins “Well, we can’t sign the contract today, but if you do indeed agree to the terms of this contract we could schedule an official signing as early as tomorrow.” “Shit, I’m there!” adds Deacon, still obviously excited. “Well then, although it’s not official yet, I’m glad to have you aboard” says Andrew, offering Holmes a hand in congratulation... which Deacon takes immediately. Handing Holmes the file folder again to further review on his own time Ethan adds “Oh, and if you don’t mind, wear a suit to the meeting.” “A suit?” Holmes asks, a bewildered look on his face. “Well certainly you have a suit...” Begins Andrew, the older of the two, ? A man needs two suits in his lifetime, a gray one for business and a black one to be buried in? “How’s about I just buy a black one and wear it twice?”
