Consequences and repercussions: Good cop - Bad cop.

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 30th, 2005. 'Consequences and repercussions: Good cop - Bad cop.' ---- 9:37 AM. July 29th. SoHo, NY. ---- Using the towel from my recent shower I cleared the steam from the mirror of Ken's bathroom, staring back at myself through the brilliant glass. My black hair with additions of royal blue and silver streaks, glistened with water beneath the light, the hair itself still being slightly damp, my bluish grays eyes were sharp and keen as they studied my reflection, showing no evidence of the fact that I had been awake for nothing short of 30 minutes. My clothing had been carefully selected from my expansive and costly wardrobe tucked inside one of Ken's many plus-sized walk in closets. Todays attire consisted of light wash faded A&F jeans, the flare barely extending over my black hi-top Chuck's, a white ringer; boldly proclaiming: 'MxPx' on the front in a royal blue font, that matched the trim of the soft tee. I had selected both earrings - one in each ear - and my eyebrow ring as accessories, topped off by a rather unusual selection, that being a trio of white beads; braided into my hair on the left side of my forehead. I had shown precision in my grooming on this beautiful July morning, choosing to shave my stubble which made for a slightly less deranged effect, although shocks of multi-colored hair, shooting off in every direction, worked in direct contradiction to the former effect. I smiled, blew myself a kiss and gazed back at the hottie in the mirror. 'Such a sexy devil.' I said, my reflection mocking me all along and adding a wink at the tail-end of the sentence. Flashing a brilliant smile, complete with stainless teeth I couldn't help but admire the figure before me in the mirror. Yes, I was taking a moment to be narcissistic, and after the way things have been lately: I needed it; by God. 'Josh?', asked the woman's voice, through the oaken door, preceded by the sound of knuckles rapping on the wood. The voice belonged to Gunner Addison, longtime friend of yours truly and longtime lover of Ken Partridge Jr. 'Yeah Gun, I'll be out in a second.' I sighed, as I looked away from the mirror and took a step towards the door. 'There are some men here to see you.' Gunner stated in a matter of fact tone, showing a cosmic cookie dough mix of emotions the most predominant being fear, or perhaps concern. 'Of course, tell them I'll just be a moment.' I responded, opening the door and stepping out into the hall where I met face to face with Gunner. 'Josh.' Gunner stared at me, her eyes locked on mine. 'They're cops.' 'Okay...' I answered with a shrug and an eyebrow raised from intrigue. 'What's going on, Josh?' Gunner asked, staring directly at me, expecting me to tell her the truth, the whole truth and nothing but the truth, so help me God. I sighed, before answering. 'It's about Branner, about a month ago someone attacked him and decided to rearrange his face, he was in a coma and once he snapped out of it, he seemed to think it was me.' 'Was it?' Her question was point blank and to the point, it required a straight answer...but that was too much for me to provide. 'What do you think, Gun?' I scowled as I asked this question, glancing down the hall and towards the staircase where the men were no doubt awaiting my presence downstairs. 'I don't know Josh, you haven't been yourself, that girl really messed you up. The whole world found out your secret about Rosa Padilla and Branner threw that in your face, I know that you tend to react rather violently to things sometimes.' Gunner stated, shaking her eyes focused on the floor in deep concentration, locks of blonde hair falling over her lovely face as she stood there still dressed in a pair of blue silk pajamas. 'I know...' Was the most I could manage half-heatedly with a heavy sigh. 'You've tried to commit suicide, pulled a gun on Ken and for once in your life you haven't acknowledged that you made a mistake and tried to rectify it. I don't know anymore Josh, you're impossible to read...' Sighed a deeply concerned Gunner Addison. 'I know and I'm sorry. I'm sorry for all of the trouble I've caused you, I'm sorry for all of the trouble I've caused Ken, you know that my intentions have always been for the best. Gun...I've never meant to hurt either of you, or Bill, Ace either.' I said in my best attempt to console the worried bombshell. 'I'm sorry for doubting you Josh, everything is just so crazy right now...we're really worried about you.' Gunner stated as she rose her blue eyes, filled with tears to meet mine. 'I'm sorry, I've been freaking out lately, I've been an idiot.' I whispered as I leaned in, gathered her in my arms and hugged her. 'I'll make it up to both of you.' 'I know you will.' She whispered back as I released her. Turning my back to descend the staircase I didn't even need to look at her to know that she was smiling, I had somehow managed to put her worries to rest and now I just hoped that I could do the same for Ken and especially myself. I took my time down the stairs, taking them one by one instead of the normal two-step routine that delivered me to the bottom of the staircase much faster and more efficiently. Taking one last deep breath I stepped out into the living room where I saw Ken standing with two plain clothes detectives. Ken was hardly dressed himself, wearing only a purple silk robe and slippers his hair was mussed, his face unshaven and his eyes bloodshot. He stared at me and tried to force a weak smile, failing miserably in his attempt, I ushered an equally pathetic return as I glanced over at the proverbial boys in blue. 'Josh, these two gentlemen have requested a moment of your time.' Ken stated softly, his voice still choked with sleep. 'Yeah.' I nodded a greeting at the officers. 'Gunner told me.' Only one of the officers responded with an introduction. 'Josh, I'm Sergeant Derek Kolbeski of the NYPD and this is Sergeant Joe Richter, we'd like to ask you to come downtown and answer a few questions.' 'I'm assuming this really isn't optional?' I asked, with a pair of raised eyebrows. 'Let's just say, it'll make both of our lives a lot easier if you come down to the station and give us just a few moments of your time.' Sergeant Kolbeski answered. 'I understand, and I've already stated to others involved in this case that I'm completely innocent, but I would be more than willing to cooperate.' I said with a boyish smile, before tacking on: 'After all, what could I possibly have to hide?' 'Good.' Sergeant Kolbeski smiled, it looked as if I had made things easier than he thought they would be. 'We'll take up as little of your time as possible.' As Sergeant Kolbeski headed towards the door I followed in his steps, the second detective falling in behind me, I was halted by the sound of Ken's voice. 'Josh, are you going to be okay?' Ken asked, his baby blue eyes emitting their deep concern for my well-being. 'Just peachy.' I responded with a smile and a lie, my heart pounding a mile a minute between the two sergeants. Ken knew I was lying but he nodded his head and allowed me to continue on my way. 'Good luck, I hope this gets resolved.' Those were the last words I heard from my best friend before walking out the door, hopping into a black sedan and speeding towards downtown for an interview unlike any I've ever had before. ---- 10:04 AM. July 29th. SoHo, NY. ---- 'Coffee Josh?', Kolbeski asked, holding a white mug out towards me. I accepted it with a smile and a nod of appreciation as he took a seat across from me at the wooden table. The room we were in seemed to be your standard interrogation room found in every cop-shop across America. Rough wooden table and a trio of chairs, full pot of coffee and mugs resting on top of the hard wood, a carpeted floor, the brown shade hardly showing any deep thought. A mirror, which no doubt served as a one-way window hung across one entire portion of the wall, the portion which Sergeant Richter leaned against, unbuttoning his blue suit jacket and supervising his hands carefully as they performed the mundane task. Richter was a slob, coffee stains on his white shirt and striped tie, bread crumbs in his brown goatee, if he had any hair it would have probably been messier than mine; without that as his intention. Kolbeski on the other hand was trim and clean, looking rather fashionable in a gray Christian Dior suit and black shirt, minus a tie, and topping off the outfit with a pair of neat leather dress shoes. He was dressed a little too nice for a cop it seemed. Come to think of it, with baby-blue eyes, spiked black hair and the face of a man in his late 20's he hardly struck me as your typical dime-a-dozen smokey. He seemed to display character and class, two characteristics that are generally unheard of on the police force. Kolbeski looked like a fresh-faced, zealous rookie, straight out of law school and ready to do his job, whereas Richter had been paired with him to kill the kids enthusiasm and turn him into your average pig. A fat, beer-bellied mick who went home and took out his frustrations by beating his wife every night and getting away with it by being a member of societies evil empire. Nothing more than a government endorsed gangster that could shatter every law in the book and get away with it; all in the name of justice. Then again, looks can be deceiving and I do tend to over-analyze the situation at times. 'I know that you've already spoken with two men on this case, however we do have some additional questions that we would like to address.' Kolbeski said in his pleasant manner, making this interrogation seem like an interview. Then again, as a professional Wrestler I've had some pretty bizarre interviews. 'Yeah, thats fine.' I nodded in response. 'I know you've already been asked this question, but where were you between the hours of ten and twelve PM on the night of June 26th?' Kolbeski asked, folding his hands on top of the table. 'In bed.' My answer was short and to the point. 'Alone?' Kolbeski asked, a devilish smile on his lips. 'Hardly.' I answered with a grin. 'I was with a woman.' 'This question has also been posed on a previous occasion but I feel the need to verify your answer. Who was she?' Kolbeski's feet shuffled beneath him on the floor as he crossed his ankles under the table, the rustling sound breaking the brief silence before my answer. 'Casey Branner.' The truth was too much to bear without a chuckle of irony. 'And that would be Mr's. Casey Branner, the wife of Howie Branner, the victim, is that correct?' Kolbeski looked deep into my eyes, trying to read me. 'Absolutely.' I stared back into his orbs, he found it too hard to read the face of a man who stands in front of thousands of people every week and bluffs his way through many encounters, so he chose to glance down at his hands for the next question. 'One would naturally assume that Mr. Branner was oblivious to her affair, was he?' Kolbeski asked, pretending to study his nails while all along I knew he was gauging the sound of my voice. 'Of course, he had no idea of what was going on.' I replied. 'Was this the first time you had engaged in... Kolbeski paused, looking for the right words, once he found them he continued. ...sexual relations?' 'Second time actually.' I said, smiling proudly. 'Josh, how would you describe your relationship with Mr's. Branner?' Detective Kolbeski asked, seeming more like a therapist than a hard-boiled cop. 'We met the day I was in the studio and we both wanted the same thing out of it: sex.' My statement was blunt, honest. 'She was in a relationship with a man that couldn't please her and I had just broken up with my girlfriend, we both wanted sex...needed sex.' 'Did Branner publicize the fact that your girlfriend had dumped you?' The question was cold and cruel, no surprise as it was Richter posing the question instead of Kolbeski. '...he did.' I glanced over at Richter, my eyes sparking with anger, he scowled back in return. 'That angered you, didn't it?' Richter asked, walking to the table and pouring himself a cup of coffee into the lone mug in the center. 'Of course, what do you expect?' I snapped back. 'I expected as much, but that wasn't all.' Richter took a seat on the edge of the table, not even bothering to look at me but staring into his coffee instead. 'Branner, being the ballsy and crazy guy that he is, decided to bring up your most tender subject; knocking up another mans wife.' Richter's remark was cold, biting, I chose not to reply, biting my tongue for fear of what I may divulge. Kolbeski's eyes were locked on me, studying my reaction to Richter's taunting, reading me...or trying. 'But that wasn't the half of it, not only did Branner tease you about poking your peter into places it didn't belong, he also let the world know that you hadn't even taken responsibility for it.' Richter took a sip of his coffee as he finished. 'Am I right?' 'Yeah, something like that.' It was growing increasingly difficult not to tear into Richter, his attitude and cutting comments bringing my temper to a boiling point. 'Man, you knocked up a friends wife and then won't even take care of your kid when the parents buy the farm.' Richter's lips curled into a snarl, mine matched his snarl, my anger beginning to show. 'Branner, being the brash soul that he is called your bluff. He let the world know about your status as a poor father, he let the world know that 'American Youth' Josh, wasn't quite the role model he was cracked up to be.' Richter swished the coffee around in his cup before taking another drink. 'But no, you couldn't have Branner tear you down, especially in front of the public eye. Aside from your overwhelming ego, you had to take in consideration the impact this news could have on your career.' Richter stared at me, his pale blue eyes cold and accusing. 'So there was only one thing to do, and that was silence him.' 'Look, I've already told you, I didn't do it!' I snapped in protest. 'I was with Casey Branner that night, we were in bed, ask her!' Kolbeski nodded. 'We're planning on bringing Mr's. Branner in for questioning.' 'Are you incapable of keeping your hands off of another mans wife, Josh?' Richter asked,his question point blank and abrupt. 'Incapable? No, I can conquer my temptations, however I wanted to prove a point to Branner. He humiliated me by showing the world I had an affair with another man's wife, I was going to humiliate him by turning the tables and having an affair with his wife.' I smiled arrogantly at Richter. 'It makes it a lot more difficult to tease a guy about keeping it in his pants, when it's been in your wife's pants instead.' 'So, the entire affair was meant as retribution for the incident on Branner's show?' Kolbeski arched an intrigued eyebrow as he posed the question. 'Yes. It was all about revenge. Branner wanted to crucify me for sleeping with other men's wives, I wanted to add him to my list of men who's wife had left them for a little play time with someone far superior.' I kept my eyes locked on Richter, he looked away in disgust. 'That's low man.' Richter spat out. 'Vindictive.' 'Branner is low, he never deserved her, she's an angel.' I growled in response, my hands balling into fists as they remained in my lap. 'An angel with devilish tendencies.' Richter spat back, Kolbeski shooting him a glare of admonishment. 'Now Josh, we would like too...' Kolbeski began before being interrupted by Richter. 'We would like to know the truth.' Richter said in his blunt manner. 'No more games, no more lies. I'm not asking you if you were the one who assaulted Branner, I'm telling you...you were the one who assaulted Branner.' 'Prove it.' I said in a low tone, my voice coming from behind hot breath and clinched teeth as I raised a pair of middle fingers in Richter's direction. 'Don't worry, I will.' Richter nodded back in return. 'And when I do Josh, you'll go to jail and discover what it's like to be on the other end of a relationship, because this time...you'll be the wife.' The lewd and disgusting Richter took a sip of his coffee, before I suddenly bolted upright, my chair slamming to the floor behind me. Kolbeski reacted quickly, leaping to his feet one hand tucked into his coat pocket and resting on the butt of a gun. Richter's response was unexpected as he spilled coffee down his chin and yelped as the liquid burnt his tender skin. I turned my eyes to Kolbeski. 'Do I have to be here?' Kolbeski frowned, glared at Richter, then turned his gaze back to meet mine before answering. 'No. Legally we have no right to detain you, you're here on your own voluntary status.' 'Good, then unlock the door.' I snapped back, my eyes focused on the heavy metal door to the interrogation room. 'Okay.' Kolbeski nodded, jerking a thumb towards the door as a signal to open it, it was quickly opened and I walked abruptly to the door, brushing past Richter on my way out. 'Josh.' The voice stopped me dead in my tracks, it was Richter again. I didn't bother to turn, choosing instead to face away from him. 'What?!' I demanded, my nerves grinding. 'I expect to see you again...very soon.' Richter warned. Stomping away I left him in the background as I made my way through the insurgent mass of humanity in the police station, before pushing open the dual glass doors in the front of the building and stepping out onto the stairs. At the top of the stairs I glanced around New York, my eyes picking apart the people walking by. This mass wave of human flesh continued to float down their concrete rivers, oblivious to my presence, yet there I stood watching them. There are no doubt many people in this world that are envious of the things that I possess, yet little do they realize that it's truly I that envies them. Why can't I lead a normal life? Settle down? Meet a nice girl? Start a family the right way? A real family. I shook my head, they were truly the lucky ones, the blessed ones. They would never know what it was like to be standing here rich, famous and successful yet willing to throw it all away for the one thing that seems to evade me to no end, that being; happiness. I've already been stripped of so many of life's simple pleasures, I refuse to be stripped of my freedom, and with that thought in mind I made a move. Reaching into my pocket I extracted my cell phone and quickly thumbed through the phonebook before reaching the name 'Casey', with a jab of the send button the phone came to life as I pressed it to my ear. One ring, a second ring, then a third before the sweet voice of a beautiful woman came across the airwaves on the other line. 'Hello?' Casey seemed surprised to hear from me. 'Casey, have the cops called you?' My tone was low, hushed. 'No...' She seemed rather confused as opposed to my prior misconception of surprise. 'Good.' I said, breathing a sigh of relief. 'We need to talk.' 'Josh, is something wrong?' Asked Casey. 'Very wrong, meet me in twenty minutes at the cellar door coffee shop, take a cab.' My tone was straight, serious and to the point, that was the last thing I said before ending our conversation and walking quickly down the stairs, raising my hand for a cab. ---- From behind the dual doors of the Police Station Detective Richter watched Josh hail a cab and step inside, there was a brief conversation before the yellow taxi began to roll down the street. Stepping out into the heat of a July morning Richter was ready to follow him, because he knew that if he searched long enough he would find the truth and that's all he needed to put Josh away. The truth wouldn't set him free, it would soon incarcerate him. Richter smiled. 'American Youth', how fitting when you consider the social decline of America and their youth today. A true representation of stupid mistakes and childish games...he would be made an example.
