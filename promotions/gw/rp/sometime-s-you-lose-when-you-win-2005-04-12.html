Sometime's you lose when you win

12 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/2005 Flashback from Evolution: *************** Fred Grest fuming with frustration pulls Smith back up to her feet and whips her into the far side ropes. Quickly she hits the ropes and bounces back before being hit with a powerful spinebuster, that is instantaneously followed by Grest turning her over and into the Freddy Crab. Sights: THE FREDDY CRAB!!! Bishop: It's got to be over now Creed: It looks that way. Sights: I wouldn't be so sure of that guys! Just as the words eek out of Brandi Sights' mouth Elia Smith some how wriggles herself out from under Fred Grest and the Freddy Crab, countering it in the process with a small package type manuver. Bishop: NO! KICK OUT! Creed: 1..........2..............3!!! Sights: She did it! Elia Smith with the upset! **************** “Are you not trying?” I looked up at the white ceiling that held the wall of the training room up. My mind gone for some reason. Not even in the same house or place as my body. But for some reason, I was glad. It was strange to me that even though I had won, my body and mind still always made me think I was a loser, and today was no different. But the day was different. Not in many ways different then the last three years that I had lived. No, it was different as a sense to yesterday. But the only thing that could make it different wasn’t the way I felt or the way I dressed. Not even the weather or the president of the united States. No, the only thing that made it different was the fact that Uncle Johnson wasn’t there. “I said, are you even trying Elia?”, A sharp voice rattled within my ears. “Yes?”, turning my head towards Karla with my lack of a response. “Well it doesn’t seem like that to me and I know nothing about this sport.” Karla said, giving her little two cents of her mind. She was right. I didn’t seem like I was trying but after that lucky win over Fred Grest, I was just glad to be able to walk away not hurt from tapping out. Tossing my brownish blonde hair out of my sweat covered face, I pressed up to a standing stance. Grabbing at the lower back, feeling what felt like a little pop, I gave a slight face of agony that made Karla worry, and me as well. “What’s wrong?” Karla asked, walking up the steel steps that were near by. “The back?”, still grabbing at it as I tried to get to the corner. “It just doesn’t feel the same for some reason.” “Well maybe you shouldn’t fight at Evolution this Friday?” Karla said, grabbing my hand and helping me down. Maybe it was the words, maybe it was the way she said it, but the pain went away. Not fight? In the biggest match of my life. How could my best friend say that to me? But she wasn’t the only one. “She’s right,” A very familiar voice said, walking out from the corner of my eye from the closed door in the corner. Something wasn’t right. Uncle Johnson wasn’t looking the same. His blue eyes seemed to be a bright red that glowed right to mine. His anger showed with ever step he took and I got that butterfly gut feeling that I wasn’t going to like what he said. He didn’t look away nor did I as he approached me all the way from across the room. “If you feel this much pain from someone who is nothing important in that ring, wait till you face a real champion.” he said, not giving an inch of feeling away from the question. “I’m not hurt Uncle. Besides, it’s my dream to do this whether I’m hurt or not.” Walking away from Karla and towards the woman’s locker room door for a long shower. I knew I wouldn’t get far. You see, once you start a conversation with my uncle, you will never be able to leave. My head was drenched with sweat from the workout in the ring. I was in no mood to battle with my uncle or anyone else for that matter, especially about my dream of holding a title. No one was going to take that dream from me. “Don’t walk away from me young lady. You will listen to me whether you like it or not.” His fire came out and he was angry for sure. I turned around and look at him like a woman that was fixing to die on death row. You could hear a pin drop in the room with the way me and him were standing. I didn’t hear Karla or the other people that were in the room still sweating it out. But I was a Smith and I didn’t listen to what anyone said, not even the man I looked to as a mentor. “I won’t listen to you. Whether I am broken, beat down, or worst I’m going to fight for what I believe in?” Pausing to embrace what was lingering in my mind. “You know what Uncle, if you think I shouldn’t fight then maybe I shouldn’t be here.” A single tear wanted to go down my face from the words that came out. Maybe I meant it or not but the damage was said. Walking away and to the locker room, trying to forget everything that was said and done, though I couldn’t. The pain was still there and I was going to feel it forever. A scar had been brought to my heart and it wasn’t heeling. Going through the door and seeing a different set of walls, it really hit me hard. Dropping down to my knees with a bang, I felt a hand being placed on my shoulder. It was Karla. “I’m here for you?”, she said before leaning down and placing a kiss on my forehead. My world was falling apart and I couldn’t do nothing. I wasn’t welcome in this world if I was wrestling and I wasn’t welcome if I was feeling pain. Either way I’m losing. But I needed to be strong. I needed to be brave for my dream was ready to evolve to something greater then I could ever imagine. Looking up at Karla, a small part of me deep down inside knew what was needed to be said but I just couldn’t. “I’m here?”
