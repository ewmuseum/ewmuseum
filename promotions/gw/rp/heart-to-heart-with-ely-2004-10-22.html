Heart to heart with Ely

22 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/22/04 It had been a couple of weeks since that night. Ely Braxton and Gunner Addison had been seeing each other since before Wrestlecade. They suffered Christina McCoy's jealousy and anger. They suffered the constant lewd insults from Deacon Holmes. They suffered the Consiliu sending Ely to the emergency room. Ely had decided that Gunner was the woman he wanted to spend the rest of his life with. It was all so perfect. Bubba allowed Ely to use his office so that he could pop the question. Everything should have gone Ely's way. But it didn't. Now, Ely was alone. The woman he loved was now with another man. All he could do was sit in the Dark Horse Saloon, downing Texas mudslides, one after the other. On the jukebox played 'Free Bird' by Lynyrd Skynyrd. Then the door swung open and in walked Brick. A waitress with strawberry blonde hair and blue eyes walked up to him. 'Hey, Brick, how you doin', darlin'? 'Hey, Daisy. I'm good, I'm good. Ely here?' Daisy sighed. 'Yeah, he's here. He's been sittin' at the bar for about a half hour now, downin' mudslides.' 'Again?' 'Again.' 'I knew he was messed up about losing Gunner, but...' 'It's awful. Gunner ought to be ashamed of herself.' 'Now, we don't know the whole story.' 'What's there to know? Ely loved her and she just dumped him for Ken Partridge. Look, Brick, I know she's your friend, but that's just the way I see it.' 'They're both my friends. I don't like the idea o' havin' to choose between them.' 'I can understand that, but right now, you need to deal with the friend who's hurtin' the most. I don't see Gunner in here, drownin' her sorrows in mudslides.' 'Lemme go talk to him.' As Brick walked up to the bar, he pondered the tone in Daisy's voice when she talked about Ely. It may have been pity, but then again... 'Ely, let me holler at you for a minute.' Ely looked back at Brick as he approached. 'Come to beat the hell outta me?' 'Nah, I ain't come down to beat you down,' Brick replied, taking a seat on the stool next to him. 'Yeah, well, you'd be within your rights after what happened a few weeks back.' 'Yeah, and I wanted to kick your ass that night, but you were drunk an' in pain, so I cooled down. Hey, did you ever get the ring back?' 'No.' 'Well, what'd you expect? You threw away a diamond ring.' 'I know. You think it's true about Josh Curtis an' his boyfriend finding the ring?' 'Who knows?' Brick replied, shrugging his shoulders. 'Let's not think about it.' Ely then stared into his glass. 'I loved her, Brick.' 'I know, man. I know.' 'I guess I should have seen it comin'. But to do it when a man proposes marriage...can you think o' anything more coldblooded?' 'Actually, I can. I'm gonna share something with you that you might not have known about me. You know why I told you that you should have waited a while before thinking about marriage with Gunner?' 'Yeah?' 'Well...I was married once.' 'What? Get outta here!' 'No, seriously, I was.' 'How come you never told anybody this?' 'Nobody ever asked,' Brick replied, shrugging his shoulders. He then sighed wistfully before resuming his story. 'I don't like talking about it, anyway. It was before I actually became a wrestler. Her name was Vida. We got married right out of college, both of us young and in love. Or at least I thought so.' 'So, what happened?' 'One night, I came home from work. I drive up to the house and I see somebody's car parked out front. I walk up the walkway to the door. When I get into the house, I hear voices coming out of the bedroom. Right away, I rush to the room and I see Vida in bed with some other dude, buck ass naked.' 'Wow.' 'Right away, I saw red. I lunged onto the bed, tryin' to strangle this dude. The minute he saw me comin', he scrambled off, tryin' to get his clothes an' get the fuck outta there. Then I turned to her, and asked her why. She actually said I drove her to it. She said I wasn't there for her. Then she filed for divorce.' 'Damn.' 'So, Gunner leaving you may have hurt, but at least she didn't wait until after you were married.' For at least a minute, there was silence between the two men. 'So, this Vida, is she why you never hooked up with Gunner?' 'Part of the reason, yeah. By the way, I think Daisy likes you.' 'What?' 'No, I'm serious. I think she really likes you. You should have heard the way she was talking to me about you, earlier. 'Ah, she probably just feels sorry for me.' 'No, I think it's more than that.' 'I dunno. Wounds are still kinda fresh. I don't wanna try to get with anyone on the rebound. Only result in one more person gettin' hurt. When the time is right, I'll get out there an' move on.' 'Okay, you take your time. But you could do worse than Daisy.' 'Like?' 'Well, you could get back together with Christina.' Both men shuddered at the thought.
