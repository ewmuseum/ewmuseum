Tirbulations of Drake, Continued...

9 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/09/05 Tribulations of Drake, Continued? -The Abduction- December 18, 2004 “Before we get into details, I’d just like to re-live my last night I had with Katie’before everything went to hell!” We now find ourselves inside Drakes home on a dark stormy night where Katie awaits to surprise him with a little celebration. Balloons are scattered all round, and ornaments on the Christmas tree have been arranged to spell “Happy Birthday”. Wesley, his best friend, has joined Katie in celebrating Drake’s twentieth birthday. They wait patiently until finally? “HAPPY BIRTHDAY!” they shout as Drake walks through the door. He looks around seeing everything his cousin and best friend have done for him in pure delight and gratitude. “Wow” he says with gleam as Katie gives him a hug. “Happy Birthday man” Wesley throws in shaking his hand. “Are you surprised?” Asks Katie. “With out question” he responds still looking around the house. “You guys didn’t have to do all this!” They just smile, “Of course we did! It’s your birthday silly!” Katie tells him with a loving smile. “I was so lucky to have the two of them. Katie most of all! Truly my source of peace and happiness. Her long dark hair glistened in the light and her eyes’her beautiful green eyes’she looked like an Angle. Then, of course, there was Wesley. An old friend and the only man I trust like a brother. No matter how down I may be at times it was always the two of them that would make me laugh and enlighten my spirits.” “This is from me,” says Katie handing him a gift. Drake can’t help but smile, “oh Katie” he laughs aloud. He tears open the wrapping paper and digs into the box. “Whoa” he says with much surprise and satisfaction. He pulls out a black trench coat. “You like it?” She asks, “Hell yeah!” He shoots back. She helps him put it on. “Perfect fit!” Wesley chimes in. Drake is very pleased with this gift but the fun isn’t over yet. Wesley now hands him a gift, “now open mine. I’m sure you’ll make use of this, plus it goes along with your new coat too!” As before, Drake tears through the paper only this time he’s left speechless. “So?” Wesley asks with a smile on his face. “It’s incredible” Drake responds pulling out his new sword. “It’s so light' letting it slightly bounce in his hand. He swings, “and it glides through the air like a bird,” and swings again. “Nicely sharpened too,” he says feeling the blade. “This is the most amazing Samurai Sword I’ve ever seen!” “I thought you’d like that,” Wesley says obviously quite proud of himself. Finally Drake puts the sword away. “But wait, it’s not over yet!” Katie tells him, as she exits the room. Moments later? 'Happy Birthday to you, happy birthday to you? the two of them start to sing as Katie enters with the cake bringing a smile to Drakes face once again. “Happy birthday Jonathan, happy birthday to you!” The song ends as Katie sets the cake down on the dinner table. They clap and cheer actually causing Drake to blush a little. “Well make a wish” Katie encourages him. Seconds later the once lit candles now smoke bringing on cheer and celebration once more? “They made this the best birthday yet. Though they didn’t have to do any of this for me, I was still grateful they had. But as I said before’something so great is not meant to last! I had just finished making a single slice into my cake when we heard a knock?” “I’ll get it” Wesley says quickly as he heads for the door. So far everything was going just the way it was planned’until some unexpected guests showed up. Wesley turned the handle and moments later multiple men in all black with black ski masks forced their way in. The first one tackled Wesley to the ground while the rest headed for Drake. “Katie get out of here!” He shouts and she wastes no time listening. The first man throws a punch but Drake easily blocks and delivers a punch of his own. Seconds later he was blocking and countering everything he could but he knew he’d have to do something before they overwhelmed him. “Everything happened so fast. Fists and feet of furry were coming at me in all directions and I blocked to the best of my abilities. I had to find an opportunity to turn this fight around. The whole time all I could think about was Katie but I knew that if I were to help her I’d have to end this fight quickly. But it seemed like no matter what I did they just coming, these guys were relentless?” It finally became too much and they knocked him to the floor. Drake knew he had little time to act but luckily he was near the dinner table. He wasted no time rolling underneath and kicking one foe’s shins dropping him mouth first on the table. Another dove in after him only to have a collision with Drake’s foot. Rolling back out, Drake was on his feet once again with a few less opponents. “I dodged one bullet but I knew I may not get lucky twice. One of these geniuses decided to throw my cake, like that would do any good. The thing that really pisses me off is the fact I didn’t even get a damn bite! I wasn’t sure how long I had been fighting but I knew that I had to get Katie?” The fight continued on for some time? Drake ducks in the niche of time as a broken chair leg darts over his head hitting one of his opponents in the face knocking him out cold. The other foe can’t believe he has done this, “feel any smarter?” Drake asks sarcastically. This angers the man, just as intended, causing him to swing angrily without thinking. Drake easily kicks the weapon out of his hand. So he throws a punch only to get caught half way and gets reversed into an arm bar. “You owe me a new cake asshole!” Drake says just before kicking the man in the face sending him flying back onto the floor. He turns to see his Christmas tree has been knocked over. “Damn it!” Another foe can’t help but laugh at the sight. “What are you laughing at?” An angry punch sends the man down. “The fight was finally over but I couldn’t rest for long. I had to find Katie! I quickly searched the house but there was no sign of her?” Drake makes it back into his living room and rushes over to Wesley lying on the ground. Wesley?” He calls shaking him, ??Wesley are you alright?” He’s barely conscious but Drake can sense he’s ok. “Katie!” He stands back up shouting. He doesn’t think twice before rushing out into the monstrous storm. “The rain was pouring heavily and clouds made it a little difficult to see but I couldn’t let a little thing like that stop me. I looked all around but I couldn’t see anything. No movement. “Katie” I kept yelling, but no response. Suddenly a truck pealed out from the trees across the street and what I saw horrified me’they had her! I could see one of the men waving to me, as if to mock, and I gave chase. Faster and faster I ran but it was no use. One of the mans comrades pulled the door shut and I stopped running. All I could do now was watch helplessly as they drove off into the night. My legs suddenly gave out and I dropped to all fours closing my eyes hoping it was all a nightmare’unfortunately I knew this was not the case. When I opened my eyes I saw something on the ground. Katie’s necklace. It must have come off when she was struggling to break free. This was the necklace I gave her not too long after her parents were killed, a silver chain connected to a beautiful crucifix. I held it up and gazed into it with great sorrow?” Focusing on the necklace in Drakes hand, we pull back to find ourselves back on the skyscraper where he still sits, now gazing upon the necklace as he did that very night. ??And that was the last I saw of her. This necklace is all I have left?” Tears cascade down his face as he places the necklace into his coat and rises to his feet. “What frightens me most is I know they were the same men who murdered our family’does that mean she’s dead too” With that question raises another. How can I focus on my match when all I can think about is her? I suppose I'll just have to do the best that I can. The one thing I do know is that has gone on long enough! It's time for me to bring my families war to an end...one way or the other... Drake turns and slowly disappears behind a pillar. To be continued?
