Focused Daily

8 September 2003

Date: September 8th, 2003 There he sat. No longer indifferent. No longer careless to the outside world. For the past three weeks, since Master of the Mat, Trey Reed has been out of sink with the rest of the world. Sure, he was the first International champion in awhile, winning the whole tournament, but only due to a mere technicality. In reality, he did not defeat Sean Price in the finals for the title. Despite the fact that he had a chance for the three count, he did not pull it off. No excuses, Reed thought. Trey Reed defeated Sean Price by disqualification, and Sean Price later went on to defeat Trey Reed for the three count. But no longer is he sulking. He is no longer caught on the wrong side of the tracks. On Evolution, this upcoming Monday, he will get his revenge. He has the chance to prove the Upper Echelon wrong. To once again gain the edge in this vicious battle between the Young Bloods and TUE. This is what will make him right with the world once again, to place the smile back on his face. Just when he thought that he would never see another opportunity to gain a clean victory over Sean Price, Christina makes the match. And now the signature grin has returned. Trey Reed sits upon the curb outside of his downtown Dallas apartment. The sun beats down on the back of his black mesh jersey, while he leans forward looking into the asphalt of the road. He sees ants march on by in a single file line. Such organization, he thinks, I wonder if that’s how you would classify the young bloods, or the TUE. Most likely, people would say that both groups have no order in them. The Young Bloods do not even define a leader, nor do they walk in one path. While the Upper Echelon’s “leader” is Sean Price, members do whatever they want, whenever they feel like it. “Maybe GW just has no boundaries.” And that was what he had been trying to figure out for so long. Pondering since he first arrived on the 21st of June, just what was the secret to this place? The master key to open all doors could lie in anyone’s hand. Perhaps it was what he was trying to block out from his train of thought. The fact that Sean Price is working for the mafia, the idea that Silent Wind, heralded as one of GW’s greatest, is a cold-blooded contract killer, isn’t easy to get used to. GW is from the wrong side of the tracks. So this is what I chose. It makes him wonder what type of position he has held in this company. Clearly he is not the gunslinger that Tony Stechinno has proven himself to be, or an amazing martial artist like the Steel Lion is. He is not an incredible brawler like Ronnie Long, or a beer-bong champion like the Ladies Man. He has no ties into London crime lords like Pailthorpe, and was never a soldier for hire or cowboy. He’s from another world, the world he thought everyone else lived in, where rules were meant to be kept. All he knows is wrestling, and a thing or two about the ladies, which makes him a total oddity here. “Maybe I should clear my head,” the excuse to smoke. Only his hands move as he fumbles into the left breast pocket of his jersey and pulls out a pack of Reds, then a match from his right jean pocket. To give off an impressive effect, he decides to strike the match against Sweat droplets begin to drop from the brim of his hair, and drip down his forehead onto the tip of his nose. Heavier and thicker, until one last droplet sent their momentum over the edge and flying towards the street. The rain-like drop crashed on to the line of ants, splashing high like a tsunami to the tiny insects. The strength of the drop sent three of the ants out of the line, and washed them down into the gutter. Amazing, was the response by Trey, as despite loosing three of its own, the line just continued as nothing had happened. To stay focused on the subject at hand, to not let his mind race through ideas. He feels like he is online running through a forum connected to DSL, the way he changes subjects so fast. If he could keep the thought to what is most important, no doubt it would make things easier. Instead of running between having to beat Sean Price, having to fit in, having to party, having to fight in the Universal Title Tournament, having to worry about YB and TUE, and having to figure out what Rokku’s stand is, he could find what is most significant and deal with that first. A car’s horn sounds. Trey is leaning too far into the road, right into the vehicle’s path. Car swerves, honks and then gives an unkind gesture to Reed. His focus was kept on the ants, which kept their focus on their task. This was Trey’s ultimate problem however, unable to control his mind from within. His thoughts quickly change. When will he get here? How am I supposed to beat EDH? Is Monday Night going to end in another Price victory, or another DQ? Another tie? Was Rokku there to help me or for his own purposes? Is Brick going to become a Youngblood? This and that and this and that. But in the end, the answers were there. GW was more important than that tournament, and Sean Price was the most important thing in GW to him. There was the whole feud for who was the top group in Global Wrestling, but that didn’t matter. Dave Hurst wasn’t even on the list for worries, win or lose it’s less that Reed has to deal with. It was Sean. It was the International Title. This was a different Trey Reed than that of the past. The man who would run from federation to federation, losing interest and moving on is no more. He wouldn’t scurry back to 360 version fourteen, or hop on the bandwagon of the newest interest around. He would stay in GW, defend his title and return to the legend he once was. He would beat the number one contender for the world title, the master of the mat, all with a smile upon his face. This time when I car was driving by; it did not swerve to avoid Reed’s head. The car honked once from about 100 feet away, it was running the speed limit up to about 70 miles per hour. Trey figured that the person would not be heartless enough to decapitate him, and kept his focus on the ants. The ants were headed towards Sean Price, they were headed into battle for a victory. The car came closer, closer before screeching on its brakes. The passenger side turn signal bumped Trey in his head. “What the hell, Jay” You could’ve just stopped man.” He knew it from the second the horn sounded, it had to be TLM. The 50 Cent music blared in the background, and the Ladies Man wore a bling-blinging gold watch that shined onto Trey’s eyes from far back. “I wanted to see if you’d flinch, haha.” “Nope, just doesn’t happen like that.” “Well, are you ready?” “What are we doing tonight” Drinking, rolling...smoking?” The demeanor in Reed’s eyes, he would be happy to go party, to get his thoughts off of Evolution. “Weight lifting biatch.” “What?” “I just got a truckload of steroids pumped into me man, let’s do this!” Trey looked at Jay, furrow browed. Jay replied with a quick laugh, then slapped Reed on the back. “The party is five minutes away, and about thirty minutes from us getting there. You ready?” Trey smiles. “Yeah,” standing up “I’ve got everything I need.” He takes the International title belt from his waste and sets it into the back of the car, then hops in...Careful not to step on the intent ants. Evolution was his, the title was his, Sean Price will be his, and the night is still young.
