Fear (Character Development)

15 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-15-2005 Going out first in the huge battle royal was the hardest part of it all. The hard work. The dedication. The mind set that I had drawn to the ring, all for nothing? Was coming to wrestling, all for nothing? Or should I be asking, was coming to Global all for nothing? As sad as that question really is, I had been thinking about. Though it wasn’t the only thing. Uncle Johnson? PrimeTime Central? The GW World Title? My life? I could go on and on but that just wouldn’t be fair to even myself. But the fact of the matter is, my life, the one I have tried to build for myself was a big pile of nothingness. But yet at the same time, other’s had lesser stuff then me. Take the people in Iran. The women for that matter. They can be killed for less profiting stuff then my problems. Like if a woman can not have a baby and keep the blood line going for her husband then he can slit her throat from ear to ear. Or if she isn’t submissive enough then he is more then in his right to burn her from body to ash. And my problem’s as a girl here in the United States is hard? So is my problems worth fighting over anymore? “Yes..” says the lady in front of me doing my long finger nails. It was babying time for me. Lips stick and make up, made over with the feminine feeling of getting a manicure on your feet and nails. The rushing feeling of her crossing my fingers made all my time of thinking up my complaints feel’well, less important. “But why?” Simply asking her in a girlish voice as I began to pull my hair away from my eyes. “Because women are more superior then any man. That holds weight, height, or even judgment of anything you want to in life.” She says as she presses down with the scrubber toy for the outsides of the nail. But in my world, men and woman are the same. At least they should be. Growing up, all I heard in school, in my family, or anywhere else is that men are the most dominate female in anything. Bigger, stronger, more smart then any female could ever be. But then what about the females I use to watch. Girls like Chryste who ruled the square circle like she was a goddess from above. Drawn down to the world that I walk ever day to celebrate to use on how hard, how smart, and how much we have to offer. “But it’s not that. It’s the fact that I just can’t live this stupid dream anymore. It tears away the lining of my stomach every time someone pins me in that ring.” Lowering what pride I held deep in the blood covering heart that was beating through out the chest that I once was pressing high in the air. The nail salon wasn’t full at all. No one other then me and this beautiful woman that was surly sent from above down to me. Her eyes, dancing with ever little smile she came with. Like Cinderella but without the blond hair. Her lips, coving with red and black liner Gothic like personality but just so compassionate. I felt at ease with her and that was perfect with me. “It sounds to me like you are just not wanting to get hurt.” The gothic girl replies. “Not wanting?” Asking with a weird look across the perky red cheeks that I carry with me. “But I believe in the crowd of people that come out to see me. I’m not scared of getting hurt.” Pressing up from the hard oak chair that my pretty little female ass had been sitting in, walking towards the only little table that was closest to me, pausing in my foot steps, I couldn’t help but remember. Thinking back, maybe I am. You see, before my TV Title match, before my Cruiserweight Title match, even before my first in round four for the GTT4, I remember praying ever day, every minute, of every hour to god and asking him to watch over me. To watch over the people that I love so much. Asking for the guidance that I need. Grabbing the drink that I had sat down there from earlier, taking a drink of before turning around. “Ok?”, seeing the small grin coving the face of the gothic chick. “Maybe I am a little but so what. That can’t be the problem.” Shaking her head, “Yes.” Shaking my head no with a little attitude towards it, “No!” Not against her but her idea’s it seems but if I was truly afraid of it all then why would I continue? That was a question I keep on asking. “Yes you are and the simple reason being, you see all the old people fight and don’t want to be like them do you” The broken fingers, the hurt necks’the deaths?” Trying to grab my attention it seemed as I sat back down in the oak chair. “Just look at how you are reacting around me with it. Imagine how you are with someone you love.” “I’m not against them,” surly yes I was right, at least with me I knew it. I was a pretty little girl growing up. Always getting my way with everything. Now it’s fully opposite. Just sitting there as she almost got done with my nails. “Well you could of fooled me?” giving a laugh from the bottom to the right with her small eyes. “Anyways, that will be thirty-three dollars.” “Here you go,” handing him a little money that I had in my pocket. I feel bad but this was my idea. Going men evil on his butt as I did. “Just remember. Losing takes place every day. It’s how well you deal with it.” The girl says, giving a simple laugh from his ear to ear. Maybe she was right. Fear is in the eye of the beholder and sooner or later all that fear will fade away’or will it? Looking away and walking towards the outside door, pressing open to a world that I knew all to well. The sun, shining right in my eyes as I thought, “What next?”
