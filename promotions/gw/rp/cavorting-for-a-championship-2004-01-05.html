Cavorting for a Championship?

5 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/05/04 Cavorting for a Championship? Part II: Lonely Soul Most athletes in the trade of wrestling often find themselves friends, allies and/or associaties within a few weeks of beginning their new careers. Most athletes will have some knowledge of the people currently working and they'll be able to talk to a few people based on specific likes/dislikes or perhaps even weight division. Whilst the many faces of the GW locker-room associate with whomever fits into their specific categories, one man sits on the side of the bench... alone. He doesn't sit there to grab attention or promote a life-style that could be deemed anti-social... instead, he bides his time, recollecting on the face and looking far into the future. Rich Sherwin was a man of many faces... and a man of many trades. Happiness, Sadness, Anxiety, Joy, Anger... just a few of the masks that would be worn by the self-proclaimed 'Captain of Retirement'. More times than not, few would be unable to distinguish whether Rich would be displaying real emotion or whether he'd just be trying to play along to the people again with sarcastic wit. Either way, with the events immediatly following the 'Wrestlecade' show, Rich was given judgement that even he would have not been able to fake reaction to. Cast aside by his blood relations, he wasn't sure where his place really was on this earth. The fun-loving, witty guy had been struck down by a vicious piece of work. Let's just hope this 'something' won't damage his psychological thinking prior to Evolution's Title fight with Deacon Holmes! The scene slowly begins to open in what appears to be the safe, solitary confinements of a bland, plain bedroom. Seemingly, Rich's own room, we find our hero sitting on the edge of his bed clad in nothing more than a pair of boxer shorts. He gazes around his room momentarily, sitting in complete and utter silence. Ever since he'd been informed of his removal from the family, Rich had not slept right... nor had he had any reason to eat properly. The combination of horror, devestation and shock had been more than enough to send this grown man spiralling into a puddle of self-pity. A puddle that would eventually become a pond... and then a pond that would drown the life of yet another. 'Of all the times they could have chosen to tell me of their decision... of all the moments in life that they could have said that I was no longer welcome to their family, why did they have to go out of their way to give the worst possible news when I was almost at a point of experiening my current high?' Rich asked himself, suddenly throwing his body back onto the bed, his arms sprawled out and outstretched. When you decided to leave your sister in that hotel to pursue your dream of being a champion, you effectively cut yourself off from this family for good... When you decided to leave your sister in that hotel to pursue your dream of being a champion, you effectively cut yourself off from this family for good... The words, as horrific as they were, continued to play in Rich's mind... running over and over and over again. He still didn't understand why his family had cut him from the tree completely just because he chose a profession that he'd been dreaming about from day one. Perhaps one day everything will come to light? Perhaps one day, everything will slowly move back to the way they were before Sherwin decided to step back into the ring? Would history allow the damaging effects of his decision to be resevered? '... Have you people always had something against me? Are you jealous of the way that I've been given attempt after attempt to grab success by the neck and throttle it until I got something? Are you just disgusted that your son is the only member of the family that found something he enjoyed doing?' Rich asked himself, imagining that he was communicating with his ex-family in some way, shape or form. After a few more minutes of talking to himself, trying to answer the questions that weighed heavily on his mind, Rich found himself drifting away from his lost sense, jumping up from the bed, eyes darting around the room for something to wear. '... I'm going to need something that's both sharp and smooth. Something that isn't too loud but isn't going to ensure that I'm the one walking in the shadows.' Rich said to himself, his eyes scanning the floor of the room for something that could be worn and matched the description that he'd given himself. After searching like a 'man on a mission' for a good twenty minutes, Rich managed to come out with a clean suit, strangely enough... pulled freshly from his wardrobe. As another few minutes passed, Rich entered a small room to get changed, coming back into his bedroom and the scene to show off in his smooth new attire. Sherwin, despite not feeling his best, proceeded to walk out of his room, opening the door with little to no effort before descending down a flight of circular stairs, passing through a room with walls that were completely red... crimson red to be precise. This area of the house was as bare as it always had been, granted there were the occasional item here and there from the time that Wang had spent training our hero... but aside from that, nothing existed here. 'I need to clear my head... I have to get into some frame of mind for Evolution, otherwise Holmes is going to rip me a new one and the whole world is going to think that victory at Wrestlecade was nothing more than a fluke...' Sherwin said to himself as he came to the front-door of his home. Turning the doorknob with his right hand, the GW athlete opened the door and began to walk into the curtain of the night. Hours passed as the athlete wandered down streets and around neighbours. With every step, he seemed to get further and further from the answer to his question. His mind continued to fill with the anguish of his former-family and the desperation to get his head straight for Evolution. 'Why? Why can't I get over it all?!' he screamed, dropping onto his knees, looking up at the sky. 'Why?! What have I done to deserve everything? Nothing good ever seems to happen without something bad to cancel it out! Have I done something so bad that you can't give me just a few hours of joy, a few moments where I can be happy that something has happened and NOT suffer any kind of emotional torture? What do YOU have against me?!' Sherwin dropped to the floor completely, his hands spread far and wide infront of his head, the silence of the night giving no answer to his current problems... and no hope for a future solution. Despite making a name for himself within the walls of Global Wrestling, nothing good had truely come to pass without a payment of some other kind. He remained there for a few minutes, his body filling with anger and frustration. Pushing himself up from the floor like an undefeatable warrior, Sherwin looked up to the sky, tears beginning to well in his eyes. The torment had gone on for too long... The punishment was becoming too much... The payback for this hardship was long overdue... '... You know what, big guy? One of these days you're going to wish I wasn't the person you messed with. You're going to scan the world looking for some big assed problem and you're going to find me, the man who never was... and then suddenly became! You can take my friends and my family... hell, you can make a mockery of everything I stand for! This week though, even Evolution hits the screens of EVERY television across the land... people will watch Deacon Holmes rubbished for everything that he can only dream of being! YOU will watch as a new era unfolds on your pathetic planet!' Sherwin screamed aloud, raising a fist to the air in order to pass his threat through to anything that was high above the sky. As the scene slowly began to fade into the confinements of darkness, one could only begin to wonder if this was some kind of permanent change or whether this was going to fade into the history books before too long. Sherwin didn't look impressed and he was now a man with a mission... a man with a goal. :: End ::
