The Myers Family

6 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th January 2005 Beginnings... I know it is a strange thing to talk about in the middle of my career but I have always found how people began interesting. I am not talking about how they came into the world because well I don't need to tell you all about that do I? No, the beginnings I am talking about are how people started their journey into becoming what they are today. Even when I was younger and I read my comics, I always preferred the making of a superhero instead of their journeys’ afterwards. It is a great moment the beginning of a journey, it is a time when you decide to take that risk, to start the journey, to begin a different life. Recently I have come across one of these beginnings, a new beginning of my own, the beginning of being a father... Just to bring you up to date, just before Showdown In Tokyo, GW's last PPV of the year, I became a father. However my wife Rachel began giving birth to our child on Tuesday 30th November. Now what is the problem with that I hear you ask? The problem was that at the time I was 5 states over from my wife, training with Brick who I fought alongside at Showdown In Tokyo. This was only the beginning of my problems as for the first time in my life I had forgotten to organise transport to take me back home. I had expected to stay over at a hotel organised by my friend Pete but obviously I needed to get home as soon as possible. Therefore I raced over to the airport in hopes of catching a flight back home. However nothing seemed to be working in my favour that day as all planes to my home were booked to fly the next day. It seemed like there was no hope for me and I sat down in that airport and wept as the thought of my child being born without me present flooded my mind. But someone was obviously smiling on me that day as thanks to a little girl and her father, whose name sounded incredibly familiar to me, were kind enough to fly me back in their private jet. I arrived home just in time to hear the first cries of life from my baby daughter, Emma Catherine Myers. Now I face what some people believe will be the hardest title defence I will ever have against self-proclaimed 'Golden Boy' Joshua Curtis. I have respect for Curtis as he is a talented fighter but whether he can take my Cruiserweight Belt is another matter... 'She is so tiny,' I said as I held baby Emma in my right arm and my left arm wrapped around Rachel with a huge smile on my face. 'I can't believe I am a father.' 'Well believe it mister, we are a family.' Rachel yawned after only getting 3 hours sleep thanks to Emma. 'You alright sweetheart? Do you want to go grab a few more hours, I can take care of Emma.' 'No no I am alright, besides I can doze right here as long as you don't mind the drool.' She looked up at me and smiled and placed her head back on my chest. 'Not at all.' I replied kissing her on the top of her head. 'She is perfect isn't she?' Rachel said now looking at Emma asleep in my arms. 'Yeah,' I sighed, 'But...' I stopped and bit my lip hoping that Rachel was too tired to hear the 'but', no such luck. She lifted my arm off her and sat up on the couch and looked at me. 'But what?' 'Nothing.' I said not wanting to say the thing that for over the past few weeks had flooded my mind. 'Come on Adam, don't close up on me again.' I sighed again... Emma is perfect; the doctors said themselves that she was perfectly healthy. That had been my biggest worry during Rachel's pregnancy, as I would never want anyone else to have my condition. However now that Emma had been given the all clear my biggest worry became my lifestyle. I mean how I live is not the ordinary, my house, my home is not the ordinary. It worried me that Emma would grow up and realised what a freak her father was. It worried me that she would become resentful towards me, as her friends would lead normal lives when she had to come back to this freak house. I know I am over analysing but it is something I would have to face up to as Emma grew up and I didn't want her to resent me. 'It's your lifestyle isn't it?' Rachel asked reading my face like a book. 'Yes.' I admitted 'Why does it bother you?' 'It bothers me because when Emma grows up she will realise that my lifestyle is not the normal. She will realise what a freak I am and will resent me because everyone else is normal except me.' 'Do you really think our child could be that vicious? Because if you do then I think you have underestimated Emma. Emma is going to grow up being so loved that your lifestyle will not matter. She is going to love her 'strange' father because he loves her and your condition and lifestyle won't matter because Emma Myers knows that her father is not ordinary but extraordinary!' 'But...' 'No buts Adam,' Rachel interrupted, ' look at your daughter, look how comfortable she is in your arms. She is your daughter and you are her father and we are a family. Whatever comes in the future we will face as a family, together.' Rachel kissed me and I smiled and put my arm around her again as she snuggled up to me and we lay there and dozed off...as a family. The Next Day... 'Richard Woods?' Peter asked running the name through his head. Peter and I were in my specialised gym again. Peter had come to catch up on how Emma was doing but the conversation soon turned to the man I had met back in November. 'Yeah does the name sound familiar to you?' I asked as I landed another roundhouse kick onto the kicking bag. 'Kind of but I am not sure why.' 'That is just how I felt when I met him. Also he seemed to look at me like he knew me already.' 'Well most people know you by now Adam, they know you from GW.' 'No, no this was a different type of knowing me.' 'How do you mean?' Peter asked confused. 'Well it was like he knew me before GW and he seemed to know a lot about the suit.' 'In what way?' 'Well while I was talking to him on the plane and I was explaining my condition he often jumped in explaining how certain parts of my suit worked and when I asked how he knew these things he said 'just a guess.' It was weird.' 'Did you ask him what he did?' 'Yeah, he is some sort of executive at a new technology company.' 'Well I will look into it Adam, it certainly is strange that he knows so much about the suit. But maybe he just knows so much about it because he works for a technology company.' 'Yeah but the designs of this suit were lost years ago. I remember you telling me of the company's closure a couple years back.' 'That is true,' Pete looked off into the distance before shrugging his shoulders and continuing, 'I wouldn't concern yourself with it now though Adam, you've got a big match coming up to start off 2005.' 'I know it has been a while since I defended my Cruiserweight Championship, I am looking forward to it.' 'You've faced Curtis before though haven't you?' 'Yeah back at Dark Rituals when I reclaimed my title. Although he was attacked backstage so he wasn't at his best. Hopefully this time round he gives me all that he has got.' 'Well I have seen some of his television spots and he seems more than ready to take the belt from you.' 'Good.' 'Good?' Came Pete's astonished reply. 'Yeah good. I don't want to go into Reload knowing that this match is going to be a walk in the park. This is my first match for 2005 and I want it to be a top of the range match. I want to start 2005 with a bang not a whimper. If Curtis wants the Crusierweight belt as much as I do then it is going to be one hell of a match. I want him to throw his best punches, I want him to pull out every manoeuvre in his book. I will bring my best and if he doesn't bring his then it will be a walk in the park. This is a New Year Pete and I am going to make it the year of Adam Myers!' 'Who could doubt a speech like that? Anyway I better get off, good luck at Reload.' 'Luck has nothing to do with it!' Pete smiled at me and I smiled back behind my mask. He simply nodded and continued to smile as he turned his back and walked out of the gym. I watched him go and turned towards the kick bag. A stern look crossed my face as I struck the bag with my hardest kick causing the chain to snap. I picked up my towel and smirked. 'Happy New Year GW. Welcome to the year of Myers!'
