One down, one to go...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday Deacon Holmes is sitting at the edge of his hotel room bed eyes glued to the small television set no more than three feet in front of him. The dark room is partially illuminated by the cathode ray tube of the television, giving everything seen a bluish tint. Even more so when lightning strikes from the storm keeping most everyone in this evening. Following a commercial for the new Lexus RX 330 “Action News 4' returns to the air about to report their next story, on the death of a wrestler. When Holmes hears the name David West he flashes a smile much like that of a crocodile’s profile or a shark sinking it’s teeth into an unsuspecting beach goer. He begins to chuckle, softly at first, then loud and triumphant. Just then there is a knock at the door. Holmes stops mid laugh and quietly makes his way toward the door before peering through the eyehole. Deacon Holmes: Who is it” “Alexis Steel” is heard from the other side of the door and with that Deacon Holmes turns the lights on and opens the creaking door. Steel steps inside the room leaving her umbrella and rainsoaked coat at the door. Holmes watches her walk past him as he closes and locks the door behind her. “What do you want” he asks as the makes herself comfortable taking a seat on the side of his bed. “Well seeing as how it looks like you already know about West, I’ll just get right to the point” she answers back. Alexis Steel: Did you know anything about this? Deacon Holmes: Oh of course, I always stay in close personal contact with crazy people. Are you kidding me? I never had anything to do with that clown, he wasn’t even worth my time. I was seriously considering paying my driver CW to handle him for me. In all seriousness though I do feel bad about what happened to him. Alexis Steel: Really? Deacon Holmes: Yeah, I just wish I would’ve been there. I could’ve done something. Alexis Steel: You mean like talk him down? Deacon Holmes: Yeah right, like I have any of my oh so important personal interest in the personal safety or well being of any of the clowns in this federation. I wish I could’ve been there to cheer him on! Alexis Steel: Well that’s not very nice. Deacon Holmes: Neither is having a clown perform at West’s funeral but that’s what I intend to do. Good thing my name’s Deacon and not nice. Alexis Steel: So you don’t feel bad about what happened to him at all? Deacon Holmes: I gotta give it to ya Lexi, nothin gets past you! I gotta give to him though, blowing his brains out was probably the best bit of advice those voices in his head ever gave him. I mean think about it, he had to step in the ring with me. It was either instant death or an agonizingly painful public display of why you aren’t fit to step in the ring with me ending with you being carted out of the ring on a stretcher and living the rest of your miserable life in a wheelchair. If you ask me he made the right choice. Alexis Steel: I guess... but that’s wrong. Deacon Holmes: No, wrong again Lexi, that’s me, that’s Deacon! Alexis Steel: Well what about Venom Alexander? What was that all about last week? Deacon Holmes: Oh, well that was just me sending him a little message. One that ended with his face being smashed into the belt I helped him win. If anything he should be thanking me rather than slamming me on national TV. I mean if you think about it he owes me. Here it is Sunday night and I’ve yet to receive a thank you for saving his boring ass match mind you. What an ingrate. Alexis Steel: Well from what he said during that interview I don’t think he appreciated it too much. Deacon Holmes: Oh I know, and if I cared I’d probably be bothered in some way. No matter though, all he’s succeeded in doing is turning this whole thing into a me and him situation which will soon turn into a him in ICU situation. Alexis Steel: What are you gonna do? Deacon Holmes: That’s for me to know and you to find out. I’ve told you enough, feel free to leave anytime. With that said Holmes walks out the door leaving Steel sitting in his room dumfounded
