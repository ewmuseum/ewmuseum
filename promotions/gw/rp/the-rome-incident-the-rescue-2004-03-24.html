The Rome Incident - The Rescue

24 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/04 'Where the hell is he?' Ice's face contorted with anger as he and ELITE running mate Ken Partridge Jr. walked down the rather dark and long hallway of a small two story motel. The lights flickered, the walls were cracked, and the paint was chipping and peeling off in large chunks as the two men walked, trying to get ahold of their third amigo, 'American Youth' Josh via Ken Partridge Jr.'s cell phone attempts. 'I know the little bastard is a fuckin' pretty boy, and takes more time to get ready than any female I've ever met, but JESUS CHRIST! This is getting a bit ridiculous!' 'I know man.....I know. But you know Josh, he's Mr. Punctuality. I mean the kid is never late. I'm kind of worried something is up.' 'Yeah, well something had better be keeping him busy and making us late, otherwise something will be up.....and that will be my foot up his scrawny little ass.' Ice and Ken Partridge Jr. took a few more steps before reaching room number 21, and stopping. Ken pulled his cell phone away from his left ear and shoved it back into his back pocket as Ice pounded on the the door with force. 'Josh, finish putting your make-up on, and fixing your pretty little hair and let's get going.' Ice took a step back from the door and looked over at Partridge Jr., who gave a unsure shrug in reply to the look from Ice. Ice rolled his eyes, and let out a frusterated sigh as he turned his glare away from Ken Partridge Jr., and stared angrily at the closed hotel room door. The two stood there for a few moments, waiting for the door to open and Josh to appear. That however, was not to be the case. After a few moments the door still was not opened, and Josh had not appeared ready to go. As a matter of fact there was no type of reaction what so ever. Ice again turned towards Partridge Jr., and gave him a frusterated look as he extended his arm, offering Ken the opportunity to see if he could get a response from Josh. Ken took a step forward towards the door and casually knocked three times. He waited for a moment then but still received no response from within the room. Ken worrily looked back at Ice and reached for the doorknob, only to find it locked. Ken wrestled with the doorknob with one hand, and vigorously pounded on the door with the other, all while yelling for Josh to answer the door. However there was still no response. 'Ken.' Ken looked back as Ice grabbed his shoulder and slowly pulled him away from the door. Ice took a step back and then lunged forward, shoulder first, barrelling into the door, and knocking it off of its hinges and crashing to the floor. As the door fell, Ken and Ice stepped into the room to see Josh sprawled out, face first on the floor next to his bed with an empty bottle of Rohypnol next to him. Ken and Ice briefly looked at each other before rushing over to their fallen friend. Immediately they turned him over onto his back, and checked for a pulse. His face had begun to turn a light shade of blue as his body quivered and convulsed, gargling up a thick foamy liquid every few moments. 'Ken, he's got a pulse, we need some help.' Ken's face, stricken with horror, told the story of the situation as he quickly reached into his back pocket and pulled out his cell phone and called 911. Ice did all he could, straddling his friend, in attempt to keep his body from convulsing and causing any more damage. Ice then parted Josh's lips with his fingers, and stuck them into his mouth and begin to remove some of the thick foamy liquid in a scooping motion, to try and keep Josh from suffocating on it. Ken put down his phone, and looked on, wide eyed. His face began to turn pale, and he did not move. 'KEN! KEN! GET OVER HERE! I NEED YOU TO HELP ME HOLD HIM TO KEEP FROM CONVULSING ANY MORE!' Ken shook his head, as if escaping the trance he had be placed in from the shock and rushed over to help Ice keep Josh from convulsing by holding down his upper body, and cushioning the back of his head. The two men fought hard to keep Josh's convulsions to a mininum as he struggled between life and death inside. Finally, after what seemed like forever, the EMTs arrived and took over the situation as Ice and Ken Partridge Jr. stood back and looked on unable to do anything to help the situation. A look of shock, and sincere worried expression still resided over Ken Partridge Jr.'s face, while an almost emotionless expression was on Ice's face. The EMTs loaded Josh onto a stretcher and began to roll him out of the room. Ice and Partridge Jr. followed them out of the room, down the long dark hallway, and to the parking lot where the ambulance was parked. Ice grabbed Ken's right arm, right across the Superman tatoo Josh and he had gotten together, and spun him around. 'You go ahead and go with him. I'm going to make some calls, to Ace, and Christina, and let everyone know what's going. I'll catch up with you at the hospital.' Ken simply nodded in response, and headed off into the ambulance. The doors slammed shut, the sirens sounded, and the ambulance sped off out of the parking lot. Ice watched for a moment, then slowly walked out of the motel lobby, and into the parking lot. Ice then made his way over to the small rental car they had gotten. He opened the driver side door, and took a seat. He sat there for a moment, staring out in front of him before reaching back and grabing his small silver cell phone. Ice flipped the phone open and began dialing. Shortly there after he placed the phone next to his ear. 'Hello, I need to speak with Christina McCoy as soon as possible.................No, I can't hold on, it's an emergency.' Ice let out a small sigh, and leaned back against the seat. 'Christina, it's Ice. Josh overdosed, and was just taken to the hospital..............I don't know. Ken and I came over to pick him up for dinner, and he didn't respond to our knocking, or calling of his phone. We busted in and found him on the floor with an empty bottle of pills next to him.................I don't know. I'll let you know more when I find out...........Yeah, Ken went with him in the ambulance...........I'm heading over there now. Alright.' Ice pulled the phone away from his ear, and stared at it briefly before dialing again. 'Ace, it's Ice. When you get this give Ken or myself a call. Josh was just taken to the hospital. I'd talk more but I don't know much more, and want to get over there. Give us a call when you get this.' Two more times Ice pulled the phone away from his ear, dialed, and left a messege about Josh being taken to the hospital before he flipped the phone close, tossed it onto the passenger seat, and slid into the car fully. Ice pulled the driver side door closed, and stuck the key in the ignition, but did not turn it over to start the car. Instead his hand slid off the keys and reached back over to he cell phone. Ice flipped open the cell phone once again, and began to dial. Slowly he placed the phone to his ear, and waited. His wait was not long however. 'Hi. It's Brock. I know long time since we've spoken...........umm.........I called because I know you probably still care, and would probably want to know...............ummmmm Josh OD'ed, and has been rushed to the hospital.' Ice paused, and an eerie silence filled the car. 'I am heading over to the hospital now. If you need something, let me know......I'll call back when I know more...............Yeah, sure. Bye.' Ice flipped the phone close, and tossed it back over onto the passenger seat next to him. His hand gently slid over the keys, still in the ignition, and Ice started the car. Slowly he back out of his parking space, and turned the car a complete 180, and pulled out of the parking lot. Down the road the car sped, as the eerie silence within was broke. 'I don't do this very often, and I know you and I aren't on very good terms right now. In the last six months you have taken my unborn child and the woman I loved. Before that you took my parents. Just recently you have begun to take the second woman to enter my life. So now you see fit to take the man I consider my best friend?' Ice's eyes begin to well up with tears. Angrily he intakes a long deep breath, and fights back the tears. The car begins to slow, as a large brown building appears. A small sign at the corner reads 'Hospital', and Ice pulls into the next opening leading into its parking lot. He pulls into the first available parking space, and shuts the car off. The inside of the vehicle once again becomes eeriely silent. 'Alright..............you win.' Ice's defeated tone is followed by his head dropping. His head remains lowered for a few moments before it shoots back up and all the back as he stares straight above. 'YOU HEAR ME! YOU FUCKING WON!' The vains in Ice's neck buldge out as his faces turns a bright shade of red. His breath becomes faster and faster, and then begins to slow back down. As his breathing slows, Ice's face begins to lose its red tint, and fade back to its natural pale color. A calm expression glides across Ice's face, and his eyes gloss over, as if he is somewhere else. 'You've won, you've beaten me, you have made me do the one thing I swore I'd never do. Here it goes. I need you........I need you to do something for me. I need you to make sure he lives. I don't care if you do it for me. I don't care if you do it him. I don't care who you do it for. Just please do it, some how, some way. I'm not going to make some promise I won't keep. I'm not going to beg for your mercy, or any crap like that. All I ask is that you spare him, unlike you did Star. She didn't deserve to be taken. She deserved more of a life than what she had. Just like Josh deserves more. You ripped Star away from this world too soon. Now you have a chance, in a situation just like hers, to right the wrong you made before, by doing the right thing this time.' Ice's head lowers once again. Ice then, leans in and rests his head on the top of the steering wheel. His head is there for only a matter of seconds before he raises and sees Ken Partridge Jr. briskly walking side by side with Josh. A slight smirk crawls across Ice's face as he looks up above in a triumphant and pleased manner. 'Thank you.'
