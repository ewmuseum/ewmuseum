So is Deacon Holmes...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday From between the trees a black Cadillac Eldorado with mob tinted windows and twenty two inch chrome rims is slowly rolling to a halt. The high beam lights turn off along with the knocking from the trunk. The vehicle’s two long doors open simultaneously revealing Deacon Holmes and CW. The two men hop out of the car wearing all black, complete with leather coats and ski masks. CW turns back toward the car and digs around in the back seat as Holmes makes his way to the now open trunk and holds a flashlight over it as he searches for something. CW joins Holmes at the rear of the car holding a flashlight and a red gas can. “You mind telling me exactly what tha hell we’re doing following some guy around Detroit” he asks. “Not to mention in the middle of nowhere in Detroit” he adds. Deacon Holmes replies “Relax, I’ve just got a loose end to tie up and then we’ll be on our way.” as he pulls a gun from the trunk, cocks it and then places it in the waistband of his pants. He then slams the trunk closed and begins to walk into the trees. CW: Are you sure this is even the right spot? Everything looks the same around here. Deacon Holmes looks around and finds a sign. “Yeah, this is tha spot, Seventy Fifth and Rocky Creek. There should be an old warehouse right around here.” “Damn I miss Florida already” moans CW as he digs a flask out of his coat before taking a sip. “I’m freezing my balls off out here.” Holmes turns to face CW who is falling behind “Less talk more walk unc, soon as we pay lizard man a little visit we’ll be on our way. And put that shit away! CW tucks the flash back into his coat and catches back up to Holmes who is standing in front of a chain link fence. The two men make their way down the fence to an open hole in it. After ducking through the small hole Holmes and CW walk through the yard of the warehouse scanning the ground with their flashlights for anything suspicious on their way to the entrance. Upon reaching the large steel door both men press against the wall next to it and gather their wits before making their next move. Deacon pulls the gun from his waist and readies himself to go in. “Alright, one... two... THREE! Holmes kicks the door open and quickly aims his pistol into the darkness of the empty warehouse while scanning with his flashlight. After a few moments of silence the two make their way in to find the place empty. They survey the area and find a lantern which they immediately light in order to escape the darkness of the room. “Alright, he’s not here” CW says, as he begins to make his way toward the door only to be stopped by Holmes. “I didn’t expect him to be.” “What!”” roars CW, “then why tha hell are we here?” Holmes looks around and then walks with rats dodging his every step to a wooden hatch in the floor with a rusted handle. “This is why.” Holmes opens the hatch and hops in. CW follows him down the short flight of stairs cursing all the way. When he reaches the small room at the bottom of the stairs Holmes? facial expression says it all. “Damn it stinks down here” is heard from CW while only the beam from his flashlight can be seen before he get all the way down. He continues “What died down here?” Holmes and CW begin to walk around the small room studying the dilapidated objects within. CW: Alright now, he’s not down here either. Can we go now? Deacon Holmes: You still don’t get it do you? I didn’t expect him to be here at all. CW: They why come all this way? Deacon Holmes: To make things clear. For some reason this clown thinks I’m afraid of him. He attacks the ambassador of the land of warm beer and crap weather while his back is turned, and then barely, just barely beats eightball, one of the biggest wastes of human flesh I’ve ever seen. On top of that he needed Brick’s help to do it! Then he has the nerve to say I haven’t proven anything!” Last week I had no match because my opponent blew his brains out to avoid meeting me in the ring! CW: Yeah, yeah, yeah, can we go now! Deacon Holmes: Hold on, did you grab those matches? “Yeah, right here” CW replies as he holds them up. “Alright then let’s get to work” says Holmes. Both men begin to pour gasoline all around the small room. Holmes stops for a moment and removes a red sharpie from his coat pocket. He then walks over to a short list and scribbles “Leviathan” underneath Lawrence Stanley and Eightball
