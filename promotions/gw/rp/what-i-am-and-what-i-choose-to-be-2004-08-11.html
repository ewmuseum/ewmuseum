What I am and what I choose to be

11 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/11/04 In the darkest of dungeons sits a creature in silence. This creature.....over the decades has decimated towns, killed millions, and still he persues....still he hunts. This creature is what I have become. I, Leinad Soma have become a monster of legend. The great beast that will reign for one thousand years. I began to vomit thinking of what for weeks I have felt happening to me, and yet I did nothing to stop it, nothing to keep the one thing holding me from becoming a true monster with some ounce of remorse. But why? I am an immortal that feeds off an animal that says its at the top of the food chain. I have powers unlike any other. A god in my own right, equal or higher to HIM. I stalk the nights being open with what I am and humanity thinks its some sort of game. That I am the animal that must amuse them. And for this....they must be tormented. Killed. Destroyed. Lambs to the slaughter. My slaughter. But humanity will not feel pain alone. Those not born as vampires, those in the ranks of merely been bitten. A disease to our race. A virus. Each of us could 'infect' others, well all but me. My children, born what they were. There are others born as well and they will stand behind me and destroy the masses. Or be struck down....Deep in this pit a single 'soul' lingers, and has intwined within my own. My dark Cassandra, a creature unlike any i've ever known. So enchanting yet disturbing. Deep within her eyes tell a tale of horrible events and with that has created a match for me. This seemingly queen to my kingdom. As she is in this pit with me, I smile. For this pit was created to inflict pain on the many who have caused me problems. On the walls lay knives, swords, crossbows, anything to inflict pain. This pit...this marvel. I show no fear as she takes a whip to my back. The small whip cutting into my flesh, my back soaked in blood. Tied to two post one on one side of the pit and one on the other, using rope to striaghten me out, she whipes me for my 'transgressions'. She speaks some language even unknown to me as the leather begins to burn leaving the marks on my back. I close my eyes as each time it touches my skin I seem to feel stronger.....I begin to notice the true power of these unknown vampires to me. She walks over to me....strongly gazing into decades of memories, her lips curved into a small smile, knowing I have given her the only chance anyone has been able to have in centuries to destroy the first of its kind. She walks around me wearing her ruffled black skirt and black leather bodice. Across her right shoulder my International Title. She rubs her hand down my back on each scar she has given me. I look on... feeling the sensations, the sensation of lust....lust for her...lust for what should be and will be mine...lust that will be obtained. Once again she walks around me taking one hand rubbing it against the scarred part of my face. She whispers into my ear. The voice soothing but dark...I listen on. 'We are two parts of the same creature you and I. This creature destroys what it needs to so it can survive to acheive its goal.....its Destiny. Together, we shall not be forsaken. None will be able to stop our destiny. It was destiny that brought us together Leinad. It will be destiny that brings others to our cause.' She stands on her toes lightly licking over the scarred as it begins to lightly heal itself as she licks it. By the end, we are kissing in the heart of darkness. I pull back looking at my destiny as blood covers most of her face. I tell her to begin to untie me and she listens well. She gives a large grin as I begin to tie her up. I look on the walls at the thought of what to use. 'I have a better idea Leinad.' 'What is that?' I put down a rather large knife and untie her. No sooner than when I untie her she grabs the large knife and swings at me, quickly I try and dodge it but it skims my stomach. She stands there smiling, knife ready. I pick up a tai chi blade and unsheath it. I look at my beauty as she rushes me, the large knife whirling around with such grace and accuracy, she makes an attempt at my heart. As I block I grab her wristand throw her. She hits a wall on the far right corner of the pit. I begin to make my way over to her throwing down the weapon and when close enough, kicking the knife away from her. I lift her up, she breaks free of my grasp sending her palm straight into my face, the hit sending me across the room. None have hit me with such force before. I stagger and get up and as soon as I shake off the hit she stands before me. A flurry of punches connecting with parts all over my body. I go to grab her and she ducks and uses her great balance to stand on her right leg and kick me with her left. I take the hit and stagger a little, as I begin to fall I grab her left leg grabbing it from under her sending her to the hard dirt floor. I smile and pull my hair back out of my dirt filled, bloody face. Cassandra stands. 'You are not trying Leinad. You, who is called the king of vampires. Use the powers at your disposal. Try and kill me.' 'If I wanted you dead you would be.' 'Is that so?' Cassandra stands there a moment wiping blood and sweat from her face. I stand there, posed in a beginning stance of muay thai. She smiles as she takes a the beginning stance of shoalin kung fu. We rush each other like we know exactly when to start. Each attack moving at lightning speed and not a one hitting either of us. 'You wanted me to try Cassandra.....but it looks like you aren't trying your hardest. Stop trying to hit me and kill me.' Her eyes begin to glow red, her style changes. She rushes me once more, this time her attacks getting the better of me. This time showing she could possibly be my equal. Each lash, scratch, kick, punch all showing her worth. I smile as i'm bleeding even more. I will put her in her place for as much power she holds, I am stronger. I am all powerful. I and I alone. I change styles on her, blocking everything she throws at me. She tries combinations of different martial arts styles, wrestling styles, all types of fighting styles all to no avail. She begins to tire out and I smile even harder. 'Your attempts were worthy Cassandra, but as you said....I am king of vampires, even those I know nothing about. You should go rest now. We have had a worthy battle. Or if you choose you can go out and hunt for the night is young.' She nods and kisses me softly on the lips. She walks up the steps to the mansion. I stand there alone in the pit I have created. My masterpeice that shall soon be used again. I think of the time where screams were heard all night long coming from this pit. Oh how I wish I could get Josh in my pit. I could show him pain and despair like no other. The world champion who thinks he knows pain. The world champion who thinks he knows sacrifice.....he is only human....where I.....I am unlike anything he has ever faced. A creature that is bent on destruction....on death...a creature who has killed countless times and now has his sights on Josh. Do I want his championship belt? Of course. To be a champion is to be a type of king. Josh right now is a high king...highest of all the kings of GW. He represents many....a creature that must fight everyday to keep what he has...this is where we are the same....the only thing we share....we fight.....and soon we shall fight each other...I smile at the thought. I hear someone coming down the stairs and as I turn I see Sage standing there. 'Leinad, what the fuck happen to you?' 'I was sparring with Cassandra.' 'I see...anyway we need to have a talk' 'About what?' 'About what? Leinad you flipped out and kicked the crap out of Josh.' 'You are right. I beat him, but not as bad as I could have.' 'You have changed. You're different than you use to be.' I smile at him. He thinks i've changed. But I am merely what I have always been. 'Sage do you remember back when I, and the other members of TUE shot you?' 'What?' 'I mean really Sage, what all do you remember?' 'Stop it Leinad.' 'Do you remember the first bullet hitting you?' 'Stop.' 'Do you remember the faces of each of us?' 'Why are you doing this?' 'Do you remember the smile on my face, or the bullets saying 'From us to you'?' 'STOP THIS!!' He rushes me and slams me against the wall, right on to a spiked mace. As he lets me go, his eyes flaming at the rememberance. The smile never leaves my face as I remove the mace from my back. 'Yes. That is the Sage I remember. That is the Sage I brought back. This is the Sage that will win titles. Remember what you were and look at what you have become. You are an animal for their amusement. You, like I, have the power to make those ordinary mortals bow down to your will. Become a prince of this world and not a slave to it.' He begins to calm down and look at me, seeing the fire in my eyes. He turns away from me not knowing what to make of all this. I put my hand on his shoulder. 'I am still the creature you befirended, who welcomed you into his house, who helped you in your hour of need. But I am also the monster that kills and destroys those in his path to reach his goals. You've known what I was since the begining and you took my hand of friendship. One of the men who killed you brought you back for this task. To become what is rightfully yours. You are called the soul survivor. Show them why you are. Reclaim the status of champion. You know what you are. Don't fool yourself into believing anything else.' I turn him to look at his reflection in a battle axe. 'Look at yourself. What do you see? Do you even know anymore. Does Cherish know what you are?' 'Does she know what you are Leinad?' 'Do you think she doesn't? I mean really....anyone who really knows me....has lived with me...she knew what she was getting herself into when she met me. I don't hide what I am. Don't hide what you are.' He turns around and looks at me. Without saying a word he walks up the stairs and out of this pit.....this pit that has shown him the creature I really am. He has always known just has never chosen to care. As does every other person to come in and out of my life. What better way to live with evil by not acknowledging its exsistance? I begin to laugh and then begin to walk up the stairs in silence.
