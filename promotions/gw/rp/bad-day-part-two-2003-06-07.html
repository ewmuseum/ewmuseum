Bad Day (part two)

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-7-03 'You say you're happy and content when every fight's the main event, but now I'm in the corner, spitting blood and spent' --------------- Where did I leave off when I was last telling you about the worst day to ever happen to me? Oh, yes. Here we go. --------------------- My cell phone rings. My hand pulls it up to my ear, flipping it open. 'Hello?' ..... ..... ..... 'Holy shit, are you serious? God damnit, where is she now? You don't know? FUCK! Ok, I'll be there as soon as I can.' I slammed my phone closed on my thigh, then jammed it back into my pocket while running across the semi-slick floor and jumping over the door over my Corvette, starting it, and slamming it into gear and burning out on the street. Back in the garage, Troy was yelling for me to stop, but I ignored him. He jumped in the Stratus and turned the engine over, backed into the street, then spun the tires making the 17 inch Konig wheels a blur of silver surrounded in white smoke. That simple phone call changed my whole day, and made it unforgettable as a repeat of the worst day ever in my life. The day my arch enemy, Mark Kullypso, kidnapped my beautiful wife, Cynthia Nicole Cruse Frost. Two hours later, outside the airport in Lansing, Michigan I pull into the parking lot, trying to figure out where the police are. I can't see shit tho, because the stupid shuttle was parked right in my way. After a few minutes and even more profane words, I managed to find the detective in charge of the investigation. 'Mr. Frost. I'm so glad you made it.' 'Where is she? What has that maniac done this time?' 'I'll tell you all I know. Many fans were walking up behind your wife asking for an autograph. As soon as she saw them, she turned around because she didn't want the attention, they say. Well, as she exited the front doors, a man in a black leather trench coat grabbed her and shoved her in a car before speeding off.' 'DAMNIT! Of all places, an airport!. So much for the increased security you all are putting in place. BULLSHIT! That's what all this.' 'Mr. Frost, please calm down. We have a videotape from the security station if you wish to see it.' I step right in the detective's face, looking down a few inches at his stocky frame. 'Do you have any idea what you just asked me?' 'Yes, I asked if you wanted to review the security footage.' 'You ignorant asshole! You just asked if I wanted to watch my wife get kidnapped. Now WHY IN THE HELL WOULD I WANT TO WATCH MY WIFE GET KIDNAPPED?? Can you cops get any lazier?' The detective gets upset with me for that remark, and honestly I don't blame him. I know I get pissed when people call me a fake because I'm a professional wrestler. 'Sir, I'm going to have to ask you to kindly remove yourself from my crime scene, and stop yelling. You're only doing two things, and neither will benefit you. You are drawing even more attention to the event, and you are insulting the officer in charge of the investigation. Now, will you leave on your own, or will I have to have officer McCluskey escort you out of here?' I look around and see three other police, as well as two airport security guards. 'Which of these is 'officer' McCluskey?' My guess was right. The fatter of the two rent-a-cops steps forward and proudly announces that he is, in fact, Security Guard McCluskey. I laugh a little, forced at a time like this. 'Fine, I'll leave peacefully. The less time I have to spend around this mass incompetence, the better.' And with that, I walk away from the detective, whose name I still don't know. Before I cross the police line, I pick up one of the day glow orange signs with a blinking light on it, and throw it against the nearest car I see, which happens to be a tan Honda Civic. The closest uniformed police officer yells, 'You're going to pay for that!' I glare at him, then sneer. 'Oh, come on. I did that piece of shit a favor. Want me to do youa favor?' 'N..n..no thanks.' I open the door of my Corvette, twist the key so hard I was afraid I'd break it in half, then once again scorch the tires on the pavement. Five minutes, 145MPH, and 5 blocks later. My cell phone rings again. Without looking at the display, I flip it open and raise it to my ear. 'What do you want now, detective? Going to threaten to send your overgrown doughnut, I mean Rent-A-Cop McCluskey after me?' 'Dude, what crawled up your ass? And did the blue hair dye seep into its brain yet?' Troy, calling to see if everything is under control. 'Troy, if you knew what was best for you right now, you'd shut up. Where are you?' 'About a block behind you. I can see your ass end right now.' I almost drop my cell phone as I'm looking over my shoulder. 'You mean you're keeping up with me? I'm doing around 130 right now.' 'Yeah, you'd be surprised how fast this little cloud will go. Although, I knew it would. I thought you'd be the first to have your custom Stratus out running. But I guess I had to get in something that would have even had a chance of keeping up with you in your Vette. Fucking rich boy.' 'Not so much rich, but hard working and violent. Very, very violent. Especially right now becuase of somebody involved with my work.' 'Frosty, you know I'll help in any way I can, right bro?' I let a slight smile come across my lips. I guess my lightbulb just went on, because I had a great idea. 'Maybe there is a way you can help me. Let's go back to the garage.' I can hear the question in Troy's voice. 'What are you thinking, D? You know I hate when you get these crazy ideas.' I simply smile my almost sadistic smile as I hang up the phone and let the 5.7 liter engine eat the highway laid before it.
