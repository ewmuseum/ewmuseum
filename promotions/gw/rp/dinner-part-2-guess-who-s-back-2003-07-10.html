Dinner Part 2 GUESS WHO'S BACK!!!

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/10/03 The scene fades back in as Brandi Sights, Evolution commentator, and her cameraman are exiting Pizza Hut, after their brief chat with Star. Brandi storms through the door, and appears to be upset. She stops just outside the glass doors and opens up her black leather purse. She pulls out a pack of cigarettes and lights one up. She takes a deep drag and blows out the smoke before turning to the cameraman. 'Why that little bitch! How dare she tell me to leave! She has the nerve to act that way to me, after all we have done for her, and her little boy toy Ice! I swear if she doesn't start treating me with the ty.....' Brandi stops in mid sentence, her eyes grow wide, and her jaw drops. She stands stunned for a second and her cigarette drops to the ground. Brandi takes off running past the camera and heads towards the parking lot. 'ICE! ICE! Excuse me! Can I get a word with you?' Ice, just stepping out of his black Ford Explorer turns to see Brandi dashing towards him and his vehicle. Ice leans inside the vehicle for a few moments, and just as Brandi reaches him he slams the door shut and stands in front of it. Ice stands wearing a pair of black work out pants, a black trench coat, and a t-shirt. However he is wearing a new t-shirt. This shirt is all blue, with bold white block letters that says ' ICEMAN COMETH'. 'I just finished talking with Star inside and said she was waiting for someone other than you. Who might that other person be?' Ice crosses his arms in front of his chest and smirks. 'Wouldn't you like to know? Maybe it is a family member, or maybe a personal friend that doesn't want to be on camera? Maybe it's neither of th forementioned. Frankly no matter who it is, it is not any of your business.' Ice smirks and tilts his head from side to side cracking his neck. Ice walks away from the camera and enters the PIZZA HUT. He returns shortly with Star at his side and they enter the black Ford Expedition Ice arrived in, Ice enters in the driver's side door, and Star enters in the door directly behind him. The engine starts up and the vehicle begins to back out of its parking spot. The camera watches as the black Ford Expedition turns around so much that the side opposite of them, the passenger side is now facing Brandi. The passenger side window slowly rolls down, and Kid Flash is shown with a cocky, arrogant smirk on his face. Ice reaches across Kid Flash and at the same time they both give Brandi, the camera, and the viewing audience the one finger salute. The engine revs up and the cameraman and Brandi are left standing in the parking lot watching it drive away. Scene fades to black.
