Dividing Two Passions #7: Diversification 2004 #3

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 12th 2004 “Hey Stevie! What’s happening?” chirps Igor Arefiev as he steps into the Darkhorse Saloon. The barman, Stevie is standing with a glass and towel in his hands. “Good evening Mr. Igor! How are you?” smiles the barman as he rests the glass on the counter. Arefiev slowly steps across the bar. He is wearing blue faded jeans and a yellow t-shirt. He is wearing a black jacket as well. He nods at a couple of Global Wrestling workers sitting at the tables, and then continues towards the bar. He pulls up a stool and looks at the barman. “Yeah. Things are good!” smiles Arefiev, “Just a beer please!” Stevie nods and turns to a fridge below him. He pulls out a bottle of beer, opens the top and then places it in front of the GW superstar. “Where is Mr. Constantino tonight?” questions the Mexican sounding barman. Arefiev shrugs. “Who cares?” sighs Arefiev as he sips at his bottle of beer. “Is something wrong Mr. Igor?” questions the clearly concerned barman. Arefiev shrugs. “He is being really weird?” “Queer weird” Because I always had my suspicions about that boy!” “No!” smiles Arefiev, “just weird weird! Like he is trying to be himself weird?” Stevie seems confused. “Is he not supposed to be himself?” “He is?” explains Igor, “but he just” It seems like he is trying too hard to be different from me! I think he is getting jealous of all the singles opportunities being offered my way.” “Why would he be jealous” He should be very proud?” “Yeah, I know!” slams Arefiev, “That’s what I thought” He should be backing me up, because he’ll get his chance sometime, but lately he has been trying really hard to make the focus of the team him and his heritage?” “Maybe he feels left out” Mr. Constantino has yet to compete at a Pay-Per-View?” Arefiev shrugs. “Maybe” But we haven’t been doing enough to get us matches at Pay-Per-Views?” protests Igor, “My two appearances were both gauntlet matches.” Stevie nods. “I understand,” says Stevie, “but you have had two appearances. He has had none.” “But that shouldn’t matter. He will get his chance, why can’t he just wait” It’s not like this whole thing will be over soon?” Arefiev sighs, as Stevie nods. “Hey” Can I get two beers please?” shouts a voice at the other end of the bar. “I’ll be right back,” motions Stevie. Arefiev nods. Igor sits in silence at the bar as Stevie attends to another client. Igor’s attention is suddenly captured by the small television set in the corner at the roof. Zergolov is standing in the middle of the screen, in full ring attire with his hands on his hips. The screen is muted as Arefiev continues to watch. Suddenly, a video cover spirals from the centre of the screen and stops covering the whole screen. “Zergolov’s Diversification Workout 2004” reads across the title of the video. The picture then fades to black. Arefiev grumbles as Stevie. “Did you see that?” grumbles Igor. “See what?” Stevie is confused. “That commercial!”” screeches Arefiev. Stevie shakes his head. “No sir. I’m sorry. I was busy.” “I don’t believe it?” begins Igor, “I knew he was serious about the workout but I didn’t think he would do it without me!” “A workout” I am sorry Mr. Igor but I am greatly confused,” pleads the barman. “My so-called tag team partner has decided to release a workout video for Christmas?” “You are annoyed by this?” “Yes,” nods Arefiev, “because firstly it takes away from wrestling. We have only been wrestlers for two months but already he wants to pursue other projects. It really annoys me. He won’t just devote his time to the wrestling and realise the rest will fall into place eventually.” Arefiev downs a large mouthful of his beer. “And now it seems like he has already recorded the video without asking me! I’m his tag team partner! I’m his best friend!” Stevie shrugs. “I think you may be over-reacting a little,” argues Stevie. “Oh fuck you! Stupid barman!” screams Arefiev. He slides off the stool with a dirty look for Stevie and proceeds to the door of the Darkhorse Saloon. “Fucking wanker!” mumbles the GW superstar. Arefiev steps out onto the street. He shoves his hands into his jacket and buries his head into his collar. Arefiev marches down the streets of Dallas. Suddenly, Igor shoulders a man walking the other way. Igor is knocked off balance. “Watch where you going jackass!” exclaims the unknown man. “Up yours!” snaps Igor. Igor marches on, but is suddenly attacked from behind. He is clubbed in the head. Igor stumbles forward and turns to face his attacker. The attacker is instantly recognisable to Arefiev as the man he shouldered a second ago. The man is much taller than Igor. He has dark hair and a sparse beard. He is wearing all leather. Igor backs off a couple of steps, and bumps into another body. The second body grabs Igor’s arms. Igor tries to struggle free but the attacker is much stronger. “You’re not much of a wrestler, little man?” sneers the second attacker as he comfortably holds Arefiev in an immobile position. Arefiev struggles some more. The first man steps in a couple of steps and grabs Igor’s feet. They drag him down an alleyway. Igor is tossed further down the alleyway. He springs to his feet and runs at his attackers but is soon levelled with a right hand. Igor folds over on the ground. The two men step forward and lay kicks into Arefiev. After five minutes of beating the helpless superstar the two men run off. Igor rolls around in pain in the alleyway.
