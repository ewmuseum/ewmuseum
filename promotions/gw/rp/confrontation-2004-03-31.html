Confrontation

31 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-31-04 Ace can be seen kneeling down lacing up his boots, when suddenly another pair of boots steps right up in front of him. He looks at the boots, then stands up to come face-to-face with Ken Partridge, Jr. The two stare at each other with a stern look on their faces. Ace: What? Ken: I just have to tell you right now, man to man, face to face, that what happened out there Monday night was NOT what you probably think it was. It was simply an error, a mistake, a miscalculation if you will. Do not take it as anything more than that. Ace: Really? Are you 100 percent of that, Ken? Are you positive that it was just a mistake? Ken: I’m dead serious about it. Ace: Or is this just some sort of grand scheme you’ve concocted, Ken? Because I know how to play mind games, I’m one of the BEST at it, in the ring and out. And I can see a head trip coming a mile away. You know when I first came here, I decided to put my own ego aside and believe the things you told me. I was ready to believe that you actually meant what you said, when you said that I would finally be somebody. After 10 years of bullshit, I would finally make a name for myself. But what has happened since then, Ken? Do you know where I am? I’m in the EXACT fucking place I was, from the BEGINNING. I haven’t moved a SINGLE inch, not ONE fucking RUNG!! You told me that doing this would finally prove to people that I deserve respect. All I’ve gotten since I came here, was embarrassed and SHIT on! Maybe this isn’t the greener pastures you’ve made me believe it is, Ken. Maybe, just maybe, you’re trying to FUCK WITH ME? Ace is directly in Ken’s face now, practically spitting venom. Ace: Maybe this whole thing is a set up, a plan, a rouse, maybe you’re just trying to FUCK with me Ken. Congratulations, you REALLY had me convinced you lousy FUCK!!! But this all stems from a long time ago, doesn’t it Partridge? Do you know why, back in the XCWA, I drove your girlfriend headfirst into that mat? Do you REALLY know why? I wanted to see the look on your face, the helplessness in your eyes, and the ANGER in your HEART! I wanted you to get a small glimpse of what I feel every SECOND of every DAY, that sense of nothingness, that cold, hollow grave that I’ve been forced to live in my whole?.fucking’LIFE!!! Ace is breathing deeply now as Ken stands firm. Ace steps back slowly. Ace: And just who the hell do you think I am, Partridge? You come complaining to be about this Josh guy, when you know DAMN well why I joined this thing in the first place. I could care less if the guy is six feet under before the end of the month cuz he can’t handle his personal shit, I don’t give a shit about him, Ice, and ESPECIALLY not YOU!!! I came here because we had a deal, and so far, I’m at the rough end of it. You should know damn well that I could care less about anybody else here, I learned a LONG time ago that that was a weakness I could do WITHOUT. So if you get in my face with your pissy little whining about your stupid friends, I will NOT neglect to fuck your WORLD over, and walk out of this goddamn hellhole you call a federation! Do NOT fuck with me, Ken Partridge?.do NOT’FUCK?.WITH? Ken suddenly punches Ace directly in the mouth, sending him backward, stopping his fall by grabbing a nearby wall. Ace wipes blood from his mouth, then lunges at Ken, tackling him to the ground. The two tussle for a few seconds until Ice and Josh show up and break up the fight. Ace breaks free of the others and screams at the top of his lungs. Ace: FUCK YOU PARTRIDGE!!! FUCK YOU YOU PIECE OF SHIT!!! Ace then storms out of the room wiping his bloody face as Ken breathes heavily, still restrained by Josh.
