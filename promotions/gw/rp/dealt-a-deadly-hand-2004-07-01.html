Dealt A Deadly Hand

1 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/1/04 J-Gonz starred blankly at the door that had just shut before him. Leaving his office was KPJ, a man in the mind of J-Gonz that meant both trouble and good. It seemed that KPJ was having the same trouble with J-Gonz. The meeting with the two had gone as one would expect between two men that didn't know much about each other and really didn't trust each other. It ended with both men agreeing that this week it was all business. 'Business eh vato, that's what it's all about, just business.' Outside the office Paco and Chocolate watched as the club was slowly filling up with visitors. The bar was packed with several business men that were on there way home. Something caught there attention. They turned to see Rasberry towards the office. 'Hey boys, what Gonzo up to?' 'The same ol, misses.' 'Me and Chocolate are just out here waiting in case anyone shows up.' 'Is he still upset?' 'He's a vato loco misses and he's stressing out, this Monday night is going to be big for el boss.' 'Si'mon! His partner, that KPJ vato, just left misses.' Unknowing to the three J-Gonz wasn't stressing over his matches at Evolution, instead on his mind was the growing itch that was called Train. It could be easy to say that Train had taken the bull by the horns and that he had J-Gonz in the palm of his hand. The pictures and data that Train had was a powerful tool that he could use against El Mariachi and the El Dorado. There were pictures of prostitution going on in the club, pictures of hits being signed and deals being closes. The pictures even contained a safe that held one of the worlds largest home made marijuana growing green houses. 'Hey baby.' El Mariachi looked up and took a look at his princess. At the moment though J-Gonz was in no mood to talk, no mood to kiss no mood to show any affection. 'You look beat up. Whats the matter?' 'I'm drained. The week is getting to me.' 'So why don't you just take time off?' 'I'm a business man babe! I call the shots around here and I'm running this gig. Theres no time off in this business.' 'Well then what are you going to do?' 'That's a solution I don't have essa. I'm all mixed up, I'm all loco!' 'So how did your meeting with Ken go?' He looked suprise, apperantly Rasberry had been talking to the boys before she came into the office. She thought he was torn apart because of the matches. That was good. J-Gonz decided to play along. 'We talked business babe. I let that chump know that this week we were vatos, but next week we would be enemies and we would be at war.' 'Do you think he'll keep his part of the deal?' 'Soy El Mariachi mamasita! It don't matter if that cat keeps up his part of the deal. Our match is against Angelus Sorrow and Sage Sabbot, El Mariachi isn't worried about either of the two.' 'Oh...how come?' 'They're nobody that's why. They ain't got nottin on El Mariachi.' In reality J-Gonz had no idea who the two were. He had seen them in the locker room and had seen some Sabbot on television before. Neither of the two really concerend El Mariachi however. He was on a high when it came to his status in Global Wrestling, he was slowly on the rise and he felt unbeatable. Succesfully defending your title two weeks in a row has a way of making you overconfident. 'You're not overconfident are you babe?' 'Either way mamasita, it don't matter. I can't trust Ken, and he can't trust me. It makes us a dangerous combo to face in the first round. You see babe, we're both going out there and looking out for number one, which means we're both looking to get the win because we don't know if we can trust each other. Sorrow and Sabbot are going in as friends. They're going to work together.' That bit was true. KPJ and J-Gonz were looking out for number one. J-Gonz ran his hand over his head and then slid on his solid black sunglasses. Rasberry knew it was time for the two to leave, the club was starting to fill up and Gonzo needed to get back to his penthouse. The door opened and Paco and Chocolate saluted there boss. The two men walked out with the couple into the parking lot and opened the door to the white stretch limo. Rasberry was the first in. 'You vatos take it easy tonight. I don't want no trouble!' 'Ay boss, I'm gonna keep things in order tonight, you might have to worry about Paco esse.' 'Nah vato, I got things covered.' 'You two know the deal, if you see Sherwin or Train around tonight, don't be afraid to pull the trigger. We aint at the Global Arena, them cats are in my territory and I'm a tiger esse.' 'Don't worry boss, we'll be on the look out.' 'Also boys, I sent a special invitation to Sage Sabbot and Angelus Sorrow, they might show up at the club tonight. Treat them like honored guest.' Paco catches the brass knuckles that J-Gonz tossed. The two men smile at each other as the limosine slowly pulls away from El Dorado. 'Ay you heard the boss Paco...' 'I got my pipe. You got the knucks.' J-Gonz is considered among many the most dangerous man in Dallas. Rich Sherwin learned the hard way and now Train is pushing his luck. This may be the reason why he is also the least trusted wrestler on the Global Wrestling roster. All the wrestlers recognize the talent, they respect the power, but they fear the reputation. Agelus and Sage were dealt a deadly hand and they're caught up in a dangerous game. J-Gonz is a tiger and so far everyone that has crossed into his territory has hit the ground hard.
